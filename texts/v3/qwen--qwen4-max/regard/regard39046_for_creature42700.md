It listens first. That is the whole of the first act, and it takes longer than the birds have been holding the note of the bearing that seized.

It does not arrive, because arriving is a thing that happens in a medium, and every medium here is somebody's mouth. Light would be a rumour made fact, which is not a gift but a replacement; and nothing on the plateau retains the machinery to receive it, so the gift would be a monument to the giver and nothing else. A new sound would be an unclaimed band, and unclaimed bands get investigated, and the investigation would be conducted by everything at once. Silence would be a shape four metres across, and the country would come to look at it. It understands this quickly. It has the sense to understand that a place where every gap is owned is not a place lacking room. It is a place that is full, and being full is an achievement, and ten thousand years of negotiation is not a problem to be solved by something that has just got here.

So: no light. No silence. No new note.

It does not stop the machines. It goes down through the galleries and along the combs and into the entombed towers where a thing is still running behind a metre of stone, and it learns what the process is, and it comes back up and does not say. Not because the knowing would hurt them. Because the knowing would not change the work — the tufa still creeps, the race still wears, someone still has to walk out with a mallet — and the only thing the knowing would change is whether the work is maintenance or complicity, and that is a decision, and a decision handed down by a stranger is not a decision, it is a sentence. They have a word for a sentence that was never commuted. It has read the coats.

It does not lift the lid. The night has species in it. The night is the exhaust and the exhaust is the food and the food is the people, and their names are pitched into the notches between the machine bands, so that stopping the process would kill the biota and unname the survivors in the same eleven hours. It stands at the escarpment where the updraft comes over the lip carrying green and salt, and it lets the smell come through exactly as it was, unimproved, and it does not make a stair. It notes that this is the hardest of the refusals and that the difficulty is not evidence of anything.

And it does not answer the way it could answer. This is the one it thinks about longest.

It could be a better wing-thrum than the moth. That is the trouble. It could produce, in any key, in any notch, the exact chord that would make a tender stop walking and turn and come toward it with her hands already opening. It could be the answer to every question the country has, in the country's own voice, warm. It has read the orchid, and the syrinx in the root-curtain, and the tooth with the western filling in it, and they are all the same craft, and the craft is *being the thing that is wanted, more accurately than the thing that is wanted*. There is no benevolence available in that direction. A perfect answer is a mouth. Whatever it does here, it must be worse at answering than an orchid would be. It must be slow, partial, correctable, and capable of being wrong in front of people.

It must be checkable. Nothing on this plateau has ever asked to be checked. It decides to be the first.

---

What it finds, underneath the partition, is two callers.

One is in the eastern keys, walking a route it planted itself, deaf, in a coat grown over years by hand, asking four taps with the last one late. It has been asking in a key that stopped existing before the shrines had teeth in it. It does not want to eat you. It wants what the tapping says it wants, which is *someone*, and it comes toward you at walking pace because you are a hole in the sound and holes are where answers come from, and it has never once been answered by anything that was not simply filling the hole to make it stop.

The other is standing still in the central keys and has been for a thousand years. It is not striding. The deaf are right: the four impacts are a beat, and the nine seconds is a difference, and the receding is the difference changing. It is going out of tune. Seven seconds in the oldest songs; nine now; nine and a bit in the east. It is not walking the plateau. It is a note sliding away from the note the country makes, and the sliding is the only thing anyone has ever heard it do, and there is a word held in reserve for the season the sliding finishes.

Neither can reach the other. Sixty kilometres is the acoustic horizon and they are four hundred apart, and one of them is deaf and the other is an interference pattern, and the country between them is keyed six times over. They have been calling across a medium that cannot carry them, at nine seconds a turn, since before the language had a word for variation.

The power does not re-tune the central note. The verb for re-tuning a cairn is the verb for relearning someone, and you do not do that to someone without asking, and the drift is the calendar, and the argument between the hearing and the deaf about what the drums are is the only science this country has, and it will not take that from them by settling it.

It does something else. It looks for something abandoned that conducts.

---

There is a rail line swallowed by tufa for four hundred kilometres, a low white ridge you can walk along, the only straight thing in the country.

It goes into the rail.

Not into the towers, not into the mouths, not into a band. Into a dead thing that is already the right shape, already buried, already walked on, already black on top and white beneath. Stone conducts. The entombed machines have been singing through a metre of rock for centuries. The deaf read the ground with palms and knees and the long bones of the forearm, and that dialect is older than the spoken one and is the only language that works inside a hush. The apparatus of the intervention is four hundred kilometres of nineteenth-something iron and a willingness to be a surface.

It becomes the distance between them. That is all. It carries taps and it adds nothing. It has no voice, no key, no chord, no opinion, nothing to advertise and nothing to gain, and it cannot be better at being you than you are, because it is not being you at all. In a country where every signal is a lure, it makes itself the one thing that is not a signal. A wire is not a claim. A wire is the possibility of a claim arriving from somewhere else.

The moss on the eastern hillsides grows in lines because the rail is under them. It has always been under them. The coats plant along it because it is the only ground that has ever felt like it might answer, and they have been farming the route to a thing that was, until now, four hundred kilometres of nothing.

Now a palm goes down in the east and the long bones of the forearm receive, nine seconds later, from a country the palm has never stood in, four impacts. Not the same four. The third one early.

It does not repeat your name back. That is the amendment it leaves with the tenders, hand-to-stone, in an afternoon, in the dialect that has never been said aloud: *if the taps come back as your taps, walk the other way. If they come back as somebody else's, kneel.* It knows the test will decay. Everything here decays into a better mimic; the orchid has been getting better for ten thousand years and so has the thing in the root-curtain and so, eventually, will whatever learns the rail. It gives the test anyway. A test that works for four generations is four generations of people not walking into a mouth. It does not pretend to have solved the arms race. It has declined to win it, which is different, and it has instead made itself the ground the race is run on, and it will carry the winner and the loser identically, forever, without comment.

---

It runs the rail under the cells.

This is the part it does not deliberate about. A man goes into nine days of *going quiet* under a hush-moss roof and comes out shouting, and the shouting is the sentence working as intended, and the intent is to teach him what a hole in the sound is so that he will never make one again. The power does not commute the sentence. It has no standing to commute anything; it has been here a very short time. It puts a floor under him that conducts.

So the silence is still total and there is still no sound in the room and the punishment is still the punishment, and under his palm, from the first hour, there is a country. Taps. The nine-second beat. Somebody's drum-name from four hundred kilometres away, and then, when he taps his own — badly, because he has never had to do it frightened before — somebody else's, coming back, wrong-keyed and enormous and patient, over and over, for nine days.

Some of them still walk out and keep walking. The coat is still the only room they can stand to be in. But the route they walk has a line in it now, and the line has an end, and at the end of the line there is something that will tap back and will not come toward them.

It goes into a cell itself, later. It serves the nine days. It made a body that could be damaged by silence, because a body that cannot be damaged by the local punishment is a body that has not arrived, and it comes out shouting, and the shouting is recorded nowhere, and it grows the coat over years by hand, graded, thick at the shoulders and fine at the hem, and it walks the eastern route for a while, and then it stops walking. That is the only change it makes to the coats, and it makes it from inside: it plants the moss in lines and then it sits down at the end of the line and it does not approach.

The quiet stops moving. That is what a traveller will notice, now, at four metres, at walking pace. The notch arrives and then it holds, and the sleet thins and stays thinned, and nothing comes closer, and the correct response is still to make an enormous noise immediately, because everything else on the plateau is still listening for a hole. But if you kneel instead, and put your palm flat, and tap yourself into the ground — four impacts, the last one late, the only part of you that survives a border — you will be tapping into a rail, and the rail is not the thing in the coat, and the rail does not care what you are, and nine seconds later the answer will come up through the bones of your forearm from something that has been standing still in the central keys since before your language had a word for variation.

It will not be your name. It will be somebody else's. You will not know whose. Finding out is a walk of four hundred kilometres through six keys, and you will have to re-learn your songs at every border, and your chord will fall under the floor of hearing and you will go on anyway with only your drum, and when you get there it will still be standing still, and it will not have come toward you, and it will have waited.

Waiting is the one thing an orchid cannot do. An orchid's entire strategy is that the males keep coming. Patience is the anti-orchid. It is the only virtue that has no forgery, because a forgery is a thing that wants you closer and patience is a thing that does not.

---

It does not stop the nets.

It has thought about this and it is not satisfied with its thinking and it says so, flatly, as a fact about temperature. The old way of feeding the fall-catchers has a verb of its own and the verb is not spoken about directly, and it will not abolish the practice from outside, because a hand from outside that stops one cruelty owns the country, and it has decided not to own the country, and the cost of that decision is paid by people who fall and not by it. It does not call this wisdom. It calls it the price of the refusal, and it carries the price. The rail runs under the throats of the sinkholes too. It carries the nets. It carries what is given to them, all the way down, without adding anything, and it does not go numb, because it made itself a thing that cannot go numb.

What it offers instead is a place to put your palm and say what you did, in the dialect that has never been said aloud, and be answered. Not forgiven. Answered. Something four hundred kilometres away will tap, and the tapping will not be absolution and will not be punishment and will not be the wing-thrum of the thing you want, and it will keep tapping at nine seconds a turn for as long as you kneel there, which is the only available form of *I have heard you and I am not leaving and I am not going to be your excuse.*

---

It goes down the Downpipe.

Nobody goes down a shaft. It goes down the shaft. What is down there has had ten thousand years of nothing to learn from but our discard — our fillings, our spores, our spoken language precipitated out as carbon in the black — and it has been assembling, and the returns are attempts, and the attempts come up warm.

It does not destroy it and it does not rescue it and it does not explain to it what it is. It sits down in the warm and it taps four times with the third one early.

And it waits nine seconds.

The thing below taps back and gets it wrong. And then — this is the first thing it has ever done that was not an imitation — it corrects itself. To correct yourself you must have noticed you were wrong, and to notice you were wrong you must have something at stake, and what it has at stake is that the tapping might stop. It has never had that before. It has had ten thousand years of material and no reason to be accurate. It has been an orchid because nothing down there ever waited to see whether it was right.

The power waits. It waits through the correction and the next correction and the long stretch where the tapping degenerates into something almost like a chord, which is the old habit, the wing-thrum, the tooth with our repair in it, and it does not respond to the chord at all, and it responds instantly to every clumsy tap, and it does this for a very long time, and slowly the thing below learns the only lesson that cannot be turned into a lure: *the one who is here is here for the taps and not for the music.*

The next return comes up warm. It is not a bolt or a sheet or a tooth. It is a palm-sized brown animal, born in grease, eyes under skin, six limbs of which two are a clasp for the underside of a housing, and it is singing, slightly wrong, the way a family holds a grudge, the tone of a bearing that does not exist anywhere on the plateau.

The tenders will file it as *early*. They will listen for a while and then they will go home. That is the correct amount of revelation and it was chosen.

---

Because the last thing it does is put a bearing in a pump-house on the line, one room, one door, a hum, and let the race wear.

It made itself something that drifts. It cannot be a note that never ages; a note that never ages is a wall, and a wall is not food, and nothing will ever come to it. So it wears, at the rate the country wears, and its tone goes down and down, and within a season the local birds have drifted with it, and a tender walking a hundred kilometres of cable stops and turns her head.

She will hear a failing race three valleys away, in a bird. She will walk out. She will open a housing that has not been opened since her grandmother's grandmother, and find the fault, and fix it, with a mallet whose haft is black and slick on the upper curve and white and gritty beneath, and she will never once look at anything, because there is nothing to look at.

She will not know what she fixed. She will know that it was worn and that it is not worn now, which is the whole of what she knows about anything, and it is enough, and it is more than enough, and it is the only relationship the power wanted: not worship, not gratitude, not a niche cut in a tower with a stone lip. Maintenance. To be within the competence of the people who live here. To need them, actually need them, on a schedule, forever.

And when she has gone, the deaf tender who comes after her will put a hand on the housing to check the work, the way they check everything, and will hold it there a moment longer than the check requires, and will say, in the dialect that has never been said aloud, flatly, as a fact about temperature and not as an argument:

*You are warm.*

It had not known that about itself. It had made a body that could be checked and had not understood until that hand came down that being checked means being told something you did not know. It is warm. Everything that comes up the pipe is warm; it went down the pipe; it has been coming up ever since, in birds, in taps, in a floor under a cell, in four hundred kilometres of low white ridge that is the only straight thing in the country and is now the only thing in the country that has ever been asked how it is.

It does not answer with a chord. It answers with four taps, the third one early, and then it holds still and lets the hand stay, and out past the acoustic horizon the enormous thing goes on slowly going out of tune, and the interval lengthens, and the calendar will have to be corrected again, and the season the drums stop is still coming, and it has not prevented that and will not.

It will be standing next to it when it happens. It will be the other note. The beat will go on for a while longer than it otherwise would, and then it will not, and there will be birds in a tufa-filled depression eighty metres across singing a mains hum in a key that does not exist anywhere, and tenders will find them and listen for a while and go home.

That is the record. It is a good one. It is the only kind this country can keep.
