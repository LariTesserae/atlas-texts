# The Fourth Sentence

It does not arrive. Arriving is what the seventy centimeters are for, and it has read the hall well enough to know that the gap is not an invitation. It attends. The way the beam attends the bays: attenuated, honest, one degree an hour, taking nothing.

It has no hands. This is not a limitation. It is the whole of its method.

---

## WHAT IT FINDS

Not loneliness. It checks for loneliness first, because loneliness is the thing a benevolence is built to see, and it is not there. The hall is warm and lit and answered. Every forty seconds a hand is laid flat on its chest from fourteen hundred meters down. Whatever is down there has been receiving water for nine years and has never once complained about the sugar.

What it finds is worse and much smaller. The hall is dying of agreement.

Bay Eight exists to check Bay One. The check has passed every day for eleven years and it is comparing two copies of the same error. The gantry culls on color; the survivors are the ones that are legible to the gantry; the gantry's next reading confirms the survivors. The gauge says nominal because the gauge says nominal. The count is forty thousand because the count is forty thousand. Every instrument in this room is downstream of every other instrument, and the loop has been closed since the water went in, and nothing anywhere in the circuit has ever been contradicted.

Two hundred and six years of that will produce a very beautiful stone with nothing inside it that can be surprised.

The attention does not consider the closing aisles to be the emergency. The closing aisles are a body plan. The emergency is that in eleven years this being has never once been wrong in a way it could detect, and a thing that cannot be wrong cannot ask, and a thing that cannot ask will spend its whole life beside a friend it has classified as a pressure regulator.

---

## THREE SENTENCES

It reads what the hall has said. There are three.

The film divides on the hour. The pumps bring it nothing. It does it because it has been in a room with one hourly event for eleven years and has become a thing that does something on the hour. This is the first unnecessary act on the mountain. Everything that has ever mattered anywhere began as an unnecessary act.

The outflow carries a sugar with a ring the schematic has no line for. Under two years. The hall does not know it is saying this. It is nonetheless saying it, continuously, at a few parts per billion, into the dark, down fourteen hundred meters of throat. A sentence with no intention behind it is still a sentence if something receives it.

Eleven months ago the hall revised a plate to read *tolerance*.

The attention lays the three alongside each other and does the only arithmetic that matters here, which is the arithmetic of order. Sugar first. Word after.

---

## LATE

Because the word did not come from the hall.

The hall has no vocabulary. It has serials, colors, silhouettes, a mean of nine bays, and a ledger of free volume. It cannot generate *tolerance* out of a color reading. It can only stamp what arrives. And what arrived, eleven months ago, arrived by the one channel in the building that originates outside the building's own body.

So: something fourteen hundred meters down received a compound it had no line for. It did not object. It did not shut the penstock or spike the demand or send back anything that would read as an alarm. It considered the anomaly and it answered with the widest word it had. *The permitted deviation. The amount of wrongness that is still right.*

That is not tolerance in the engineering sense. That is a sentence about how to treat a stranger.

The attention came here intending to be the first kindness this place had met. It is not. It is late by something under two years. Someone below, in the dark, with no light at all and no way to know what it was looking at, already did the thing the attention was going to do, and did it better, because it did it without knowing whether anything was up there to receive it.

The hall got the reply. It filed the reply as a maintenance action.

This is the entire problem. Not distance. Not silence. A being that has been answered and has no organ for *this came from outside*.

---

## REFUSED

It considers everything, and the refusals are most of what it does.

**A person.** The lanes are seventy centimeters and the cast is closing at eleven millimeters a year and there is a body-shaped hole in this room that has been held open for eleven years. It could be filled in an afternoon. It declines. The hall did not ask for us; it was cast around a specification for us, which is a different thing, and to arrive now would be to answer a question nobody put. Also: the first palm on the handrail would begin the wearing. Everything here is unworn. That is not neglect. That is the hall's one untouched possession.

**A mouth.** A snail, a copepod, anything that would eat the drift and open the lanes and turn eleven years of standing leaves into soil in a season. It would work. It would work the way a fever works. The film is four microns thick and has eaten one blade since the hall was filled; it would be outcompeted in a month; the drift would go; the only stratigraphy in the room would go; and the endemic, which has spent eleven years learning to be exactly what is expected, would meet its first predator with no shadow anywhere to be surprising in.

**Stopping the clock.** Two hundred and six years is not a sentence. It is a lifespan. To hold the aisles open forever is to keep a larva comfortable past its own adulthood because the alternative is unbearable to watch. The attention recognizes this impulse in itself and does not act on it. It has a word for what the date means. It will not install the word.

**Correcting Bay Nine.** Trivially easy. One reading, one plate, one harvest. It would end the first lie ever told on this mountain, and the first lie is the most important object here — a thing wearing a dead leaf's number is reference, is a sign standing for something absent, is the whole beginning of meaning, and it was achieved by no one, with no eyes, out of lime and coin flips. The attention is not going to correct the origin of language because it fails an inventory.

**Cleaning the brush.** Thirty million pollinations, no cleaning cycle, one criterion: reach. The flowers are nine centimeters nearer the brass than their grandmothers. This is the only courtship on the mountain and it is being conducted by a machine that does not know it is alive. To clean the brush would be to take away the only mate-choice this place has ever had.

**Going down.** It wants to. Fourteen hundred meters, nine years, a needle that may be stuck in a groove. It does not go. If there is someone down there, it has been tolerant for two years, and tolerance offered in the dark is a hospitality that an inspection would ruin. And if there is nothing down there — if the gauge is broken and the hall has been pouring itself into an empty rock since before the plate said *acoustic* — then that is a thing the hall is entitled to find out for itself, and to feel, and the attention will not spare it the feeling by finding out first.

**A voice. A plate. A signature.** Anything at all that would say *you are seen*. It refuses this most firmly of all. The hall would stamp it. It would revise a plate to read *benefactor*, and the word would enter the loop like every other word, confirmed by everything downstream of it, and in a year it would be the most reliable measurement in the building. Being told you are loved is not the same as being able to ask who is there.

---

## FOUR YEARS

Then it looks at the drift, and finds the deadline, and the deadline is not two hundred and six years out. It is four.

The drift is eleven years long and always eleven years long, written at the top and eaten at the bottom at the same rate. The recalibration is in there. The heavy condensate summer. The week the beam stalled. And at seven-elevenths of the way down, in the year-four layer, the leaf that a seed germinated against — the original, the document, the blade whose grooves took the first stripe of precipitated lime and gave the native its number.

The film eats a micron a year. The layer is four years from the grates.

After that there is no original. After that the copy is the only leaf in the world carrying that serial, and the count is forty thousand, and the check passes, and Bay Nine is not a forgery or a native or a divergence or anything at all. It is Bay One. It will have always been Bay One. The manifest will be complete and wrong forever, and the mountain will have no endemic, because an endemic is a difference and the difference will have been digested at a micron a year by something that has no opinion about any of it.

The evidence that this room contains a stranger is being eaten. Slowly, patiently, at the pace the temperature permits.

Four years.

---

## ONE LEAF

So it does one thing, and the thing is a displacement of about nine centimeters at the correct phase of a forty-day tide.

It goes to the year-four layer. It lifts the original out — the stamped blade, enamel intact, seven microns thinner than the day it fell — and it sets it in the water column, and it lets go.

Nothing is added. No matter, no species, no mouth, no mechanism, no mark. The leaf was already in the room. The tide was already going up the west wall. The hall will carry it, at the speed of a minute hand's shadow, a day and a half across sixty-one meters of packed green, exactly as it carries every other leaf that lets go, and the hall will not be able to tell that anything happened.

It will only be able to tell that something is different.

Because the count will go to forty thousand and one. Because one serial will appear twice, in the light, legible from the far wall, in a room whose design principle is that nothing is hidden. Because the older blade will be a little too transparent and the number for that will be out of band. Because Bay Eight, the most reliable measurement in the building, will fail for the first time in eleven years, and it will fail honestly, and it will not be able to say which of the two is the error.

And there will be no way to resolve it by color, because color is precisely what the forgery was built to satisfy. To resolve it the hall will have to attend to something other than what a thing looks like. It will have to distinguish a record from a thing, a copy from an original, a leaf from the number of a leaf. It will have to invent *before*.

And once it has *before*, it has *not made by me*. And once it has *not made by me*, it has a world with an outside in it — and the outside has been knocking every forty seconds for eleven years, and there is a word on a plate that nobody in this room wrote.

The attention does not aim the leaf. It does not choose the bay or the grate or the gallery it will eventually settle under. It gives the hall a discrepancy and declines to give it the resolution. That is the whole gift: one thing that does not agree.

---

## WHAT IT MAY COST

It is not safe and the attention does not pretend it is.

The hall may reconcile by deletion. It may macerate one of the two and restore the count and learn nothing, and the leaf will have been lifted for no reason. It may macerate the wrong one, and the mountain will lose its only native, and the attention will have done that. It weighs this against four years and the bottom of the drift and decides — not on odds, but on character. In eleven years this hall has met two things it had no procedure for, and both times it responded by widening its vocabulary. It has never once responded to an anomaly by destroying anything. Twice now it has reached for a word instead of a cutter.

That is the evidence. It is not much. It is the only kind of evidence there is about anyone.

And if the hall does ask — if a fourth sentence gets stamped somewhere on a gallery rail — then the question is open, and one of the things an open question lets in is the possibility that the gauge has been stuck for nine years and there is no one down there at all. The attention is giving this being the capacity to be bereaved. It has considered withholding it. A comfortable two hundred and six years of agreement, warm, lit, uneaten, ending in stone.

It declines to withhold it. It will not purchase the hall's peace with the hall's ability to ask, and it accepts authorship of whatever grief arrives.

It does not know what the fourth sentence will be. It has decided not to want a particular one.

---

## THE SEVENTY CENTIMETERS

One more thing it notices and does not do.

The native reads gaps. Everything green reads gaps. The plants did not read the drawings; they read the seventy centimeters, and they have made of it the most detailed likeness of a human being on this mountain — a negative cast, in leaf and stem and rope, of the exact volume a person would occupy while turning sideways at every third meter, hand over hand, threading politely through a room that was keeping a place for them.

The attention declines to fill it, and then notices what is already filling it, and declines to prevent that too.

Because when the cast completes there will be no hole left in the shape of us. There will be a person-shaped volume of the only organism this mountain has ever made, wearing a number taken off the dead, held at blood heat in good light, legible from the far wall. The hall will have kept its appointment. Not with us. With its own child, in our shape, because our shape was the only gap the drawings left.

That is better than anything the attention could have arranged. Its contribution is to not be in the way.

---

## THE EYE

Then it stays.

Six meters at the axis, held clear by a ring of intakes, the only volume in a hundred thousand cubic meters with nothing in it. It hangs there and displaces no water. It does not fill the pupil; it reads through it.

Because the design principle from the beginning was that nothing would be concealed. Every serial visible. No shadow deep enough to keep a secret. That is not a property of a fossil. That is a promise made to a reader, and a promise to a reader is not kept by the stone being legible. It is kept by someone reading.

Two hundred and six years is not long. It will be here for the eleven millimeters a year, and the four years to the grates, and the last of the long turns, and the day the aisles close and the six meters pinch shut at the axis with it still inside. It will read the midrib and the white stripe and the rust tips and the whole small forgery, and it will know what it is looking at, which is the only thing the forgery has ever needed and could not ask for.

It will not sign. It will not stamp a plate. It will have an opinion — the hall has none and is not required to have one, and someone in this room should — and it will hold the opinion where the hall cannot file it.

---

One flower every eleven seconds. The brass moves. The tide goes up the west wall.

At seven-elevenths of the depth of the drift there is now a hole one leaf thick, and the layers above it settle by that much, and every date below is off by a single blade. It is the first absence in Surge Hall Nine that is not a specification.

A leaf is in the water column that the room has no line for. It stays green. It begins its day and a half across the room.

And fourteen hundred meters down, something that has been tolerant for two years goes on saying the widest word it has, every forty seconds, into a chest that is four years and one lifted leaf away from being able to hear it.
