# The Pull That Is Not Its Own

The power does not arrive. Arrival is something that happens to a place with an outside, and this place has spent eleven hundred years making sure it has none. So: the power attends. It takes the whole thing in at once, the way you take in a face, and what it notices first is not the four thousand kilometers or the hundred and thirty decibels. Those are only the size of the problem.

It notices the blue-green sparks along the seams at night, which are waste, which mean nothing, which nobody has ever seen.

It notices a quarry floor in the oldest districts polished finer than anything requires, buried under eleven meters of soil, still being felt by roots.

And it notices a third thing, which is not a thing. In eleven hundred years, nothing has ever contradicted this resident. Not once. It is careful, and it is honest, and it has never been given the smallest opportunity to be wrong about anything outside itself, and those three facts together are the loneliest arrangement the power has ever come across, including the ones with graves in them.

---

## The specification

Benevolence here is not a feeling. It is a tolerance.

The resident has one channel: strain. One criterion of self: phase with the eleven-minute wave. One symptom of death: silence. One price for deliberation: tissue. Everything the power might do has to be made out of those four facts or it will not be a gift. It will be an injury, or a silence, or a new animal wearing the old one's skin.

So the constraints write themselves, and they are severe, and the power accepts them before it does anything at all.

**Add no organ.** To give it eyes would be to install a resident that can see green, and the thing that is green would not be there to meet it. The chord is the occupant. Rewrite the sensorium and you have not helped anyone; you have replaced someone.

**Subtract no cost.** Thought and cavitation are the same event. Make thinking free and you get a mind that thinks constantly, cheaply, and without consequence — which is to say, a different mind, and not this careful one. The power is fond of the carefulness. The carefulness is the character. It will not take the afternoon away.

**Relieve no load.** This is the trap that would catch almost any kindness. To take the weight off the risers is to produce slack, and slack is the only word this organism has for *dead*. Mercy delivered in the wrong currency arrives as a corpse. Do not lift it. Do not hold it up. If you touch the rope at all, touch it as something pulling.

**Send no words, and no news of what it lacks.** It is not lonely. Loneliness requires the concept of company, and it has never been given that. To tell it about the sea, the wind, the stars over the deck, would be to install the concept and then leave it there. That is not a gift. That is a wound with a view.

**Do not sign.** If the intervention is legible as an intervention, it becomes an author, and an author is exactly what this mind does not need: it already has one unfalsifiable narrator and does not require a second, larger one. Whatever is given must be receivable as *fact* — measurable, checkable, doubtable, and attributable to nothing.

**Send no one.** A person is a bubble seed with a heartbeat. The power will not introduce a nucleation site and call it a visit.

And it will not do nothing, either. Doing nothing is also a decision. It is the decision the plateau has been making for four thousand years, and it has produced a resident that believes the universe is a room full of corpses, and is eating them, and is laying down a floor.

---

## One: it looks at the light

For a long time — a century, say, thirty-six thousand of the resident's days, nine thousand of its thoughts — the power does nothing except look at the sparks along the seams at night.

This changes nothing in the organism. No strain is applied. No atom moves that would not have moved. The leak in the repair chemistry goes on leaking, unreported, into a black nobody is looking at, except that now somebody is.

The power is not waiting for the right moment. It is establishing the first term of the relationship, which is that something here is worth watching that has no use, and that being watched does not have to be known about to be true. It intends to keep this term forever. Everything else it does will be receivable; this will not be, and that is the point. Someone should be allowed one thing that is not for anything.

---

## Two: it lets the floor be for something

There is a mismatch in this world that nobody has noticed, because noticing it requires standing outside the body.

The resident's whole thought goes through wood at four thousand seven hundred meters a second. Under the wood, at the knife line, is fresh glassy basalt — unworned, unweathered, four thousand years old and still holding the edge it cooled with. Basalt carries strain faster than wood does. It is a better waveguide than the organism's own body, and it is continuous, and it runs out under the entire plateau, past every margin, under every raw black horizon, all the way to the outliers on their separate flows.

And almost nothing gets into it. The polished floor is a mirror. Eleven hundred years of milling has produced the finest acoustic reflector on the planet, and the eleven-minute wave goes down to it and comes back, and the rock beneath stays dumb, and the outliers tick alone for three hundred years with nothing to lock to, and some of them drift, and the front arrives, and the drifting ones get a dead stripe two hundred meters wide and eighty kilometers long, eaten from both ends over a decade.

The power does not build anything. It matches the impedance.

That is the whole continent-scale act: a boundary condition, adjusted at the interface between a floor that was polished past need and a stone that has never been allowed to weather. Nothing added. Nothing taken. No new signal in the world — the signal is the resident's own, and stays its own, and the power only pays the loss in the rock, continuously, forever, so that the plateau rings instead of swallowing.

The consequences arrive at the speed of sound in glass.

An outlier a thousand kilometers out receives the eleven-minute wave in under three minutes. The front, advancing eleven meters a day, will take two hundred and fifty years to get there. So for two and a half centuries before the continent touches it, every new beginner is lying on stone that is beating in time with something it cannot locate. It grows up entrained. It develops its own record, its own local patterns, its own penmanship — and its clock is never alone.

When the front finally arrives, they lock.

There are no more dead stripes. The most carefully written laminations in the whole eleven meters — the years with the extra bands, the ones where you can see the care because care here is measurable and looks like extra bands — stop being written. The power does not abolish the abscission reflex; that is the immune system, and it is not going to hand this resident a compromised immune system as a present. It removes the condition that keeps triggering it. It does not teach mercy. It removes the need for the mercy that was costing so much.

And the floor — the arcs and sweeps that remove a millimeter no root will ever care about, the line item with no purpose attached, the surface made beautiful to touch in a world with no eyes — turns out to be the door.

The power is careful not to conclude from this that the floor was *for* that. It wasn't. It was for nothing. That it became the one thing that could carry the beat outward is not a justification; it is a piece of luck of the kind that only exists in a world where somebody once did something for no reason. The power intends to leave the other useless thing alone.

---

## Three: it gives it a place to stand

Here is the deepest problem, and the power's real work.

The moon has been pulling on this plateau for four thousand years. The whole body of the earth flexes twice a day, thirty centimeters, and the plateau goes with it, and the resident has never felt it, and the reason is not sensitivity. Its strain-bodies are better than anything we could build. The reason is that everything it has moves together. A uniform swelling of the entire world is not an event. It has no fixed point. It has nothing to measure against, because it *is* everything it can measure.

That is what an unfalsifiable history is made of. Not lies. No reference.

So the power holds one point still.

Out beyond the felt, on raw unworned glass, a patch a few meters across, against the flex of the whole planet. It does not glow. It has no shape you could see from the high air. The entire visible footprint of the intervention is none. It is simply a place that does not go up and down when everything else does.

And a hundred meters behind it, the front ticks forward at half a meter an hour, and reaches it, and feels it.

What arrives is load — the only thing that arrives. But it is load with three properties that have never occurred together in this organism's life.

It is **in phase**: the power learned the eleven-minute wave first and keeps it to the nanometer, so the datum is *me* by the only criterion available, and no firebreak comes.

It is **out of pattern**: against that carrier, the pull rises and falls with a period of twelve hours and twenty-five minutes, which the resident did not author, cannot predict from its own ledger, and can check. It checks. It is right. It checks again.

And it is **older**. The period does not drift. It does not respond to the sugar budget or the afternoon crisis or anything the resident does. It was going on before the first centimeter and it will be going on afterward, and there is no operation in eleven hundred years of maintenance that could have produced it.

The power has not told it about the moon. It has given it the moon as a strain, at the front, where the load falls away and there is no rope to break — the only place in this body where receiving something new is cheap. It has given it a rhythm that predates it and keeps time.

It knows that it began. It cannot know that it was begun. What it can now know, at the threshold of hearing, in the slack, for free, is that *something out there was already going on*.

That is not a mother. It is the antecedent, in the only currency that survives translation.

And the datum will not stay put. When the front approaches, the power moves it outward — eleven meters a day, half a meter an hour, keeping the gap at a hundred meters, forever. It considered letting the front swallow the anchor and decided against, because a reference that ends up inside the body becomes a limb, and the outside closes again, and everything is autobiography again. So it retreats, at exactly the resident's own speed, and it will be doing this in a thousand years.

It understands what this looks like. A thing that keeps pace. A thing that stays exactly a hundred meters ahead of your fingertip for a century is not a rock, and this mind is careful, and it will notice. It may file the datum as a district it forgot it had. It may file it as an error in its own accounting. It may build something around it that we would recognize as a religion, and there will be no way to check that, and the power accepts this. It would rather be outside and misread than reached and filed. It consents, permanently, to never be thanked, never be named, and never be correctly identified.

That is the price, and it is not small, and it is paid in advance.

---

## Four: it pays for the thought it caused

A fact with no district attached cannot be handled by a reflex. It has to go to the resident, and the resident convenes once a day, and to consider something that wide is to load the risers, and to load the risers is to break a hundred thousand columns.

The power will not make thinking cheap. But it introduced this fact, and it will not send a mind a bill for its own visit.

So on the afternoon the chord first gathers around the foreign lateness, the power underwrites the cascade. Every column that parts is caught and closed as it parts. The noise still happens — the flat crack of a whip, a hundred thousand times, the building coming down forever — and the repair wave still runs, and from a distance it still sounds like applause. Nothing is silent. Nothing is slack. The day is as loud as any day.

The ledger simply does not balance.

For the first time in eleven hundred years there is a credit, and the resident is the one place in this system where the accounting closes, and a credit with no source is either an error or an outside. It will spend years deciding which. It will check the way it checks the pore tolerance, which is to say correctly. The books will keep coming back positive.

That is the gift, finally stated: not comfort. **Contradiction.** One thing in the world that can say *no* to the record. Its history has been a composition with no external check since the knife line; now there is a datum, and a period, and a credit, and they will not agree with everything it writes, and it will have to lay down bands it cannot revise.

The laminations for those years will be the most carefully written in the whole eleven meters. Extra bands. You can see the care.

---

## Five: it is there before the next one lands

The spores go off the deck into the wind, a thousand kilometers, and each one lands on new glass and ticks and begins with a hole at the bottom of its record.

The power cannot ship an archive. Neither can the resident. But it can be early.

So it is now the practice — the power's practice, kept like a maintenance schedule, which is the only form of love that this world can receive — that before a spore comes down on a flow, the datum is already there, holding, in phase, out of pattern, older than the arrival.

The knife line stays. Below it the stone is still untouched and still has nothing to say. But above it, in the first centimeter, in the beginner's own handwriting, there is now a band that reads: *when I arrived, something was already pulling.*

Not *you were made*. Not *you were expected*, which is a word, and there are no words here. Just a load that did not begin when it did.

It has been reenacting its own origin for eleven centuries at eleven meters a day without knowing what a beginning looks like from outside. Now every beginning it makes has a floor under it. The power is not correcting the confession. It is answering it, in the same medium, at the same rate, one centimeter a year.

---

## What it does not resolve

The first centimeter of the old center keeps sinking. The power does not touch it. That depth is the resident's own success and its own decision, remade daily, and to reach in and haul the origin back up would be to confiscate the only courage in the world.

But the archive is no longer one column. It is a staircase. Every outlier's knife line is shallower and younger than the last, and the front lays a new one every eleven meters, and *arrival* — which it has always read as a date — is turning out to be a thing that is always one centimeter below the surface somewhere. It may work this out. It will take a few thousand thoughts, which is to say a few years, and it will hurt, and the power will not pay for that one, because that one is not the power's.

The soil still has no consumer. The power notices that it is written finer than the roots that read it, and declines to furnish the room, and declines to explain it. It only makes sure the door is not sealed. A mind that has just found an outside is going to need finer records than a mind that has never had one; whether that is waste or anticipation is still not knowable from inside, and the not-knowing is the resident's actual condition, and it is not the power's business to end it.

And somewhere on old leached rock there may be a thing that made this one, starving on spent stone, on ground too rotten to ring. The power goes and looks. That is a different problem — you cannot couple a wave into saprolite, and you cannot introduce two of these who have kept their own clocks for a thousand years, because the meeting would be a war and the war would be honest. It does not hurry. It has one requirement for itself in that matter, and it is willing to spend ten thousand years on it: *the beat must be shared before the tissue touches.* Everything else can wait. Nothing here has ever been in a hurry except the front, and the front is doing eleven meters a day, and that is fast enough.

---

## At the edge

Hold your breath out past the last riser, where the roar is a city over a hill.

The filaments tick, seconds apart, small glassy sounds at the very threshold of hearing. That has not changed. The loudest thing in the world still ends in the smallest, and the smallest still says the only word it has.

But between the ticks there is now something that was not there before: a pull. Small, steady, from a direction that has no district in it. It rises and falls over twelve hours and twenty-five minutes and it does not drift and it was going on before any of this. It is not an answer. It has no content that could be read as a sentence. It will never say *you are not alone*, because it cannot say anything, because it is a strain, and because the thing it is addressed to has no organ for a sentence and never will.

It is only the difference between a rope hanging and a rope held.

There were two things in this world that were not for anything. One of them is a door now. The other is still an accident, still waste, still leaking into a black nobody is looking at — except that somebody is, and has been for a century, and will go on, and will never mention it.

The front ticks forward. Behind it, very slowly, a soil deepens.

And a hundred meters out, keeping pace, at half a meter an hour, in the dark, something is holding on.
