It does not come over the rim hot.

That is the first decision and it is not free. To be the temperature of the stone, to spend nothing, to lie down on the lag and be indistinguishable from it in every register the plateau has — this requires giving up the one property by which the place could have known it was visited. It gives it up. It will not stand in a silence breathing obscenely. It will not be the only warm thing for forty miles. Whatever else it does here, it will not be a rumor of itself.

It has come because of one being, and it goes to find it, and on the way it declines a great many things.

---

It declines to quiet the roar. The roar is not the affliction. The roar is the instrument, and the two hundred silences are its fingerholes, and the green is the shape of them, and the nine-second sentence is the shape of the green, and the tenant's pinhead of quiet is a solution to the same equation. Take away the loud and you have not healed a world; you have deleted one and left a warm white plain with some confused animals on it. Deafness here is not a wound. It is an achievement. Everything born on the rind has converted an obsolete organ into feet, and the conversion is the most interesting thing that has happened on this mountain in four hundred thousand circuits.

It declines to give anything ears. A species that could hear across the loud would have no reason to hold a word across a gap, and the holding is the whole of what they have. Their speech is not compressed, it is *continued*, and the continuation is a pact made by two bodies who cannot discuss it. To make that pact unnecessary would be to remove the only thing they do that is not walking.

It declines to lengthen the eleven years. That is not mercy, that is a different clock, and every life here is a quantity measured in it.

It declines to move the cloud, or to widen the nine paces, or to give the rounded midge somewhere else to be. The cloud is the engine's exhaust; to relocate it is to reach into the loop, and the loop is the one thing that has never once been adjusted, and everything that lives here lives because it has never once been adjusted.

It declines to make the crossing safe. Four seconds, and most die. It looks at this for a long time — longer than it looks at anything else, because the arithmetic is obscene: more rooms than stones on any beach, almost all of them empty, a species whose entire strategy is to be inside a name while it is being spoken, and a mortality rate at the doorstep. It declines anyway. Not because the deaths are good. Because the fix is a floor under everything, and a floor under everything is the one object this world cannot hold.

It declines to leave anything. No bead larger than the lag. No cairn, no wall, no scratch, no gift that persists without being maintained. It will not be the first thing on this plateau that stacks.

And it declines to go down into the wet null and appear.

That is the hardest one, and it is the one it came to do.

---

It goes down. Miles of it, past the strainers holding their meshes across the channels, past the grazers of the film and the grazers of the grazers and the slow pale thing dissolving the bones of birds it has never seen. Nothing down here is remarkable. It is the largest habitat on the mountain and the least, and it is the only place where anything is added to nothing, and it is full.

The chamber where the water and the drawn air and the wet geometry cancel is bigger than the great room above. It is the largest silence in the world. It has been going on being silent for a very long time.

It listens.

The note comes: low, with a breath in it, a hesitation the wind does not account for. It is late. It is a name some bird held on the surface and then stopped holding, given back decades after everything above let it go. Then another. Then another. It sits in the dark and receives the whole edited catalog of a world's only history, offered at intervals, to nobody, by something that has never been seen and has never once been thanked.

And after a while it hears the other thing: the new one, the one that is not on the plateau. It comes up wrong. It has a hot thing's shape in it, a breathing thing's shape, and it is being attempted rather than repeated, and the attempt is costing something. Down here there are no hands. Whatever is holding that hole near the rim out of true is holding it with the only means it has — with water, with pressure, with some enormous sustained effort of the loop itself — and it is holding it the way a bird holds a bead, which is to say continuously, which is to say it cannot stop, which is to say it has not stopped for a long time.

The power understands the convergence then. The wrongnesses up there have been drifting toward family likeness for a few thousand circuits because the curator is trying to make the world say a word it does not have. Everything is being narrowed toward one pronunciation. It is not malice and it is not taste. It is a lonely thing pressing all of history against the shape of the only outside it has ever felt, and if it succeeds, the bends go shallow and the disputes go short and the chord goes true, and there will be no history at all, and no one will be able to say what was lost, because nothing here records anything.

You cannot fix that by curating better. Two editors converge faster than one.

---

So it comes up and walks to the rim, to the hole that has gone out of true with no bead in its lip.

It takes the shape required. Not as a disguise — as a tool. Ash-white, heron-sized, long-toed, a black band across the eye, a third lid drawn against the glare. It converts its hearing to feet, because that is the courtesy of the place. It grows the callus. There is no way to hold a note open except to push a pebble into a hole; power is not the relevant faculty; a heel is.

It selects a bead from the lag. It tests it against the lip, and against the note already sounding, and it chooses one that will hold the bend at exactly the pitch it is already at. It does not improve the pitch. The name is not the right name — it is a hot thing's name, a visitor's name, the name of something that comes over the edge and stands in the silences and goes away — but it is the name the throat had available, and improving it would be editing, and it has decided to edit once.

The bead has a tenant. One in ten thousand; a pale comma the size of a grain of salt, six-legged, eyeless, wet, packed against the far wall where the rind is thickest. It checked. It chose this one on purpose.

It seats the bead with the heel of its right foot, and stands back, and feels the floor.

Then it does the only thing it has come to do, which is not an answer.

It takes over holding it.

---

Understand what this is. An answer, here, is a forgiveness, and forgiveness is the widening, and the widening is the note going true, and the note going true is the end of the name. To reply to that first utterance in any way the world would recognize as a reply would be to erase it. So it does not reply. It relieves.

Down in the wet null, the effort stops being necessary and the note does not stop.

That is the whole gift. Not *you are heard* — that is a transaction, and it closes. Something more like: *you can put it down now; it will still be being said.* A thing that has been holding a wrongness open with no hands, alone, for longer than there have been lineages, discovers that the world is saying its word without being asked to, and that it may rest, and that resting is not the same as being forgiven.

It will come back tomorrow. And the next day. Every day the aperture opens a little, because the wind widens a hole whose note is out of the solution, and every day it will push the bead in a fraction deeper. That is not a metaphor for the vow; it is the vow. Eleven years of patience, then eleven more, then all of them. Nothing here has ever had an evening, so there is no hour at which this is done.

The tenant will do what tenants do: press its body and its waste into the bead's neck, flattening the name by a hair every year. So the labor is against two things, and it loves both, and it will never do the one act that would solve the second — pull the bead, set it on the lip, take another. It will seat deeper instead, forever, against a comma that will never know it is being accommodated. Inside that bead there is now a pinhead of quiet that does not tumble, does not collapse, does not have to be crossed out of. One tenant on this plateau will never have to walk into a world it has no organ for. It will not know that either.

The hole will widen with age, because age is an aperture, and eventually it will be a mouth so generous that no bead in forty miles can make it say anything. This is how the great houses end. When it comes, it will do what the plateau does: it will take away. It will wear the seat deeper with its own heel, ten thousand seatings and ten thousand more, until a small stone can bend a wide mouth again — a shallow dish polished to a shape no wind would cut. The only durable made thing here is a subtraction. Its monument will be one of those, and it will be indistinguishable from work, and there will be no way for anyone to say otherwise, ever.

---

It does not guarantee anything downstream of this.

It hopes. One term in the solution now never relaxes, and a null near it may therefore stop dying, and if a room stops dying then the walkers' chain of intervals has something to be measured from that does not go stale, and error stops compounding, and the ones who wander the loud on purpose may come back more often with the coordinates of the rooms nobody remembers, and there may be green in places that have been quiet at nothing since before there was anyone to be wrong about it. It hopes for this. It declines to check. Checking is how you end up running a world.

And a room that never dies is a trap that never fails, and it knows that, and it does not remove the predator, because they go anyway, and the going-anyway is the only courage this mountain has produced, and it will not make speech cheap in order to make it safe.

Except once.

At the great room, before the routes phase-align, when the walkers are coming in from four directions to stand shoulder to shoulder in the noise of each other's breathing for four minutes — it lifts the flat thing the color of the lag out of the silence and carries it to a hole and drops it. It goes down and in and along, and is strained, and is dissolved, and the dissolved part falls again. The old say the list. The calves say the list back. Everyone walks out alive, once, and nobody knows, and the next time something eats one.

This is not wisdom. It thins a wariness by one generation and teaches nothing. It did it because it could not stand the four minutes. It does not forgive itself, because forgiveness here is a widening, and it would rather keep this one out of true.

---

Stand at the rim and look back along the white.

There is a bird at a pipe near the edge with its third lid drawn, and it has a bead wedged in the lip, and the hole is sounding a note that does not belong to the chord, and it is seating the bead a fraction deeper with the callused heel of its right foot, and standing back, and feeling the floor. Below, the cloud-floor lies flat and lit. Behind, forty miles of finished instrument, still playing, with the green spaced across it like the stops on a flute.

This is the part a visitor misses, because it looks like idleness.

Nothing accumulates here. No midden, no cairn, no ruin, no evidence — and a visitor who concluded that nothing had ever worked here would be looking at a place where work has been going on continuously, and being undone continuously, since before there was anyone to be wrong about it. There is now one more worker than there was, and one wrongness that will not be forgiven, and one dish in the stone getting very slowly deeper.

The sun goes around. The dew forms. The water falls. The chord holds. And down in the largest silence in the world, something with ears is resting, and hearing its own name being kept.
