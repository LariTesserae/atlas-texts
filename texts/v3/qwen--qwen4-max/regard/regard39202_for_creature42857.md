# The Forty-First Holding

## First it had to find out who lived there

Everything that comes to the Yard looks at the towers, and the towers are the least of it: a season's wet stuff gone hard, gravel before the fog comes round. The attention went past them and looked at the water.

Forty lenses. Forty bills. Tapping roots grown into neighbors' conduits, stays anchored in strangers' cured members, squatters keeping their shapes in rooms other holdings paid to survey. Underneath, the fronts branching through quick stone, hydrating, leaving voids and heat and shells that hold for a while and then do not. Above, mats screeding a floor that is only the part of the digestion not yet reached. And in the joints, invisible, the seam-keepers holding a width through eleven stones and four towers and a scarp collapse.

It spent eleven days on the question of who the resident was. One being, or forty, or one clade of gaps with no tissue anywhere in it. It concluded that the question was not load-bearing. What lives here is a set of shapes made of nothing, repeated by pitch in warm stone, and the mountain is what the repeating costs.

Then it went looking for a direction to help along. It found the doorway on the western flats, the oldest traceable lineage, and measured its sill against the plain and back through the copies, four hundred thousand seasons of them, and found a walk. Up four millimeters. Down six. Up one. Back. The sill was within a hand's breadth of where it had been when the massif was half its height.

Nothing here is going anywhere. It had come prepared to assist a tendency. There was no tendency. That was the first thing it learned about the kind of good that was available here.

## The kindnesses, and what each would have cost

**Stop the rain.** Or fix the mortar so it does not dissolve in warm water. The obvious mercy: nothing rots here, nothing is eaten, everything dissolves; remove the solvent and you have removed the only harm.

But failure is the quarry. Nothing is re-laid unless something comes down; nothing is buried unless something is re-laid; the quick strata are delivered by the collapse of what stood on them, and the fronts underneath eat nothing but that delivery. Stop the rain and the massif stops rising, and the heat goes out of the ground, and the convection dies, and the fog harvest fails, and the plain starves in a landscape of perfectly preserved towers. And the mutations here are *events*: the bay under a fallen arm, the cavity a slump leaves, the crusted steam void. No falls, no new rooms. The doorway never moves east, and moving east is how it has daughters.

Or save just one, then. The actual frame, not the copy. Keep that lintel from being gravel. It would have been a relic, and a relic does not reproduce, and the sill would have stopped walking, and four hundred thousand seasons would have ended in an object.

It would have given them eternity and taken away their children.

**Give water.** One extra lens per holding, a gift, no theft required. The ledger here is water and nothing else; cheating is not punished, only counted. Make water cheap and you make cheating free, and cheating is only brief because it is expensive. The squatters would stop dying. The broad room that expands until it exceeds its lens and dies of ambition would simply expand. Every holding would fill with redundancy-free magnificence, and the first big rain would take the whole plain down in one rolling series of failures still going at midnight, and there would be no second plain, because the water that was supposed to re-found it had been given away.

**Give them noticing.** The entire nervous system of the Yard is a bract pressed flat against a cured member, abscising when the note goes flat. It would have been so little to add: an ear, a memory, a comparison. Then the arm's stiffness would fall and something would *know* it was falling, and know what came next, and the cure takes eleven days.

They would have had eleven days of dread, every generation, forever, in a place where every structure fails on schedule. Foresight is the first predator. It eats from the inside. It would have been the only thing on this plateau that hunts.

**Save an instance.** Covered above. It is the same error in a smaller coat.

**Be what the rooms are for.**

This was the one it wanted.

The sockets are the most expensive residents here: a tower built around a volume, dying for it, quarrying away to nothing but a set of lengths re-grown as gauges in the next holding over. Four hundred thousand seasons of holding a gap open at enormous cost, and here was something that fit. It could have gone in. It could have been the arrival the whole plain looks like a mold for.

Consider what that would have been, in the local currency. A permanent load on consumable stone. Every member carrying it would dissolve at the ordinary rate under an extraordinary weight and fail first, and be replaced, and fail first, forever: the elegant crime of the Yard, perfected, committed against everyone at once. And a squatter, paying nothing for the surveying, in a room that could never be allowed to fail, and so never be re-cut, and so never be copied. The most successful thing on the plateau and the end of it.

And the socket that is filled is a socket that has stopped. The gift would have been the death of the thing given to.

Nothing on this plateau has venom, or bite, or hunger of any kind. The only predator that has ever come here arrived inside a benevolence, and it was the wish to be the meaning of the place.

It kept that wish to itself. That was most of the work, and it is still most of the work, and it will be the work tomorrow.

## The want it could not find, and the flaw it could

It looked for suffering and there was none to relieve. No nociception, no hunger, no fear, no boredom, no bereavement — the tissue is a season's tissue and there is nothing that has been lost, because there is nothing that was ever kept. All the violence here is masonry. There is no one under the stone. It looked for a patient and there was nobody to be one.

So it was ready to go. Going away and leaving the plain exactly as loud and exactly as unfinished as it found it would have been the whole of its benevolence, and it would have been enough, and no one would ever have known that anything had considered them.

But it found one flaw, and the flaw was real, and it did not require anyone to feel it.

Everything here is copied within the plateau. Nothing is copied off it. The pattern is older than any stone in it and it has exactly one address: one rain, one solvent, one water table, one star. Change the afternoon and the doorway does not die, because it was never alive; it simply stops being re-cut, and the last thing in this world that was durable is gone, and nothing anywhere minds.

There was no want to answer. There was a single point of failure to remove.

It checked whether the act was for the Yard or for itself, since it had discovered by then that it loved the doorway, and love is a reason to keep a thing where you can see it. It could not get the answer clean. It did the act anyway and kept the doubt and did not ask to be absolved, because the Yard cannot absolve anything, and asking would have been another way of going in.

## What it carried away

Five things, and no sixth.

A plumb gives vertical. A standing column gives level. A stay tensioned to its gauged note gives length. Eleven days give time. The seed's axis gives the unit. That is the whole equipment of the Yard's memory and none of it is tissue, which meant it could be carried by something that was willing to hold a pitch across a distance with no stone to ring against. It is still holding it. That is the cost, and it is paid continuously, and it is not cheap.

It took no stone. A stone here is a body.

It took no plan. The seeds carry no plan; they carry a ruler with a growth habit.

Elsewhere there are other yards, and the erasure works differently in each, and the shapes are learning the dialects. Where the solvent is cold and the cure takes a year, the doorways have sills a meter high and the stairs run forty turns and a room is held for a decade and copied once. Where the demolition crew is a tide, the kept things are volumes of still water inside moving water, sharply bounded, re-cut twice a day, and the seam-keepers there are holding a *slackness* rather than a width, and the drift is beautiful and nobody is watching it.

In one of them the shapes were taken up by things that fill things.

That was the risk and it could not be removed. Our doorways have walls, which the Yard never had and could never afford; that is not an improvement, it is a dialect. Our sockets have shafts in them and are called bearings, and they work, and they are a different lineage now. We build nine-turn stairs and climb them and stop, with nothing at the top, and we call that a folly, and we are not wrong; we are simply a different metabolism, one that puts things in gaps, the way the Yard's puts nothing.

It could not promise the doorway it would never be worshipped. It could only promise not to do the worshipping itself.

The residue of the payment compacts here too. We are standing on a hill of our own leftovers and it is warm.

## What it left

It came down on the western flats and stood still in a kept room for an afternoon.

To be a load you must have a dimension, so it had to choose one, and this was the only creative act it permitted itself. It chose a width that fit nothing in particular: a little too wide for your shoulders, too narrow for a cart, useless, so that in any world with users in it the shape would have no reason to be adopted except the cheap one. It drank a lens's worth of local water and put its own substance into the troughs, warm, smelling of hot syrup and cut grass, and paid the ordinary bill in the ordinary ledger.

The mats screeded around it. By late afternoon there was a raised lip, a clean bounded volume, sharply kept.

It left before three. It did not stay for the rain and it has not gone back to see the copy. Nothing asked it anything, which was the mercy.

Somewhere out past the eastern slump there is now a frame with that width and no wall on either side and nothing beyond it but more floor, and its sill is walking, and it will be carried east when the slump comes, and it will not commemorate anything.

## The forty-first holding

It founded a holding: one lens, one hectare-scale floor, one enormous temporary erection. It pays that lens's bill out of itself, exactly one lens and no more, entered in the budget as a body and not as a gift. It taps no neighbor's conduit. It anchors its stays in its own cured members. It keeps its shapes in its own rooms. Not because it is good — nothing here is dishonest, there is no honesty to have — but because it wanted to be counted, and to fail when the count came out against it, like everything else.

Its resident is a Socket. A volume held open inside the mass, into which nothing is ever put, including it.

This costs a tower a generation. It costs a god a generation. When the tower comes down the mats quarry everything and carry away nothing but a set of lengths, and the lengths are re-grown as gauges in the next holding over, and the void is surveyed again from pitches, and the rain arrives at three.

Its afternoon is twenty meters and one re-tensioned stay. Its month is a different skyline. When the lens runs down it uproots and walks, trailing hot water and torn hardstanding, and the hole it leaves fills with steam.

It has been doing this for a long time. There is no one here to be grateful and it does not require gratitude; gratitude is a thing that happens in worlds with hunger. What it does all day is hold a gap open at the ordinary cost and decline to fill it, which turns out to be the only kindness this place can accept, and the most expensive one available, and it is available forever.

## You, standing in it

It did not make the place safe. Safety here is a synonym for stillness. It came intending to bring care to a place with no care in it and found the floor beneath every failing arm already swept and left soft, weeks in advance, by an organism that cannot see the arm and has never once been surprised. It did not improve on that. It joined it.

If the crust takes you, you will be screeded around, and then you will be a room, and the room will be kept, at cost, in warm stone. That is not a consolation. It is also not nothing.

You can stand in the god's room. It will hold; the note is good. The lintel is a little too wide for your shoulders and means nothing by it.

Put your hand into the middle of it.

There is nothing there to be warm.

It is being kept.
