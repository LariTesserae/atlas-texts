## Arrival

It does not arrive. That is the first decision, and it takes longer than the rest.

The bowl has one way of knowing that something is present, and it is the wrong way. A warmth appears in the shell-pavement; the crowd shifts; the shift crosses the room in a quarter of an hour as a wave of re-seating weight; the far curve releases what it was already going to release. The bowl has no category for a visitor. It has a category for a death, and it uses it.

So the power considers the visitor already standing in there — thirty-seven degrees in a root-cellar room, the first gradient in ten thousand years, a frond bending under something that climbed toward the heat — and understands that it is looking at its own portrait. To attend to this place is to be a wound in it. It cannot come as news. There is no organ here for news, and so news can only be received as injury.

It stays outside the rim for a while. Time is not expensive for it. It uses the time to find out who it is actually visiting.

## Who It Came For

Not the grazers. They are brief fillings of permanent holes; loving one of them would be loving a mouthful.

Not the warm ones, whose unit is the knot and not the body, so that any gift addressed to an individual is addressed to something that does not exist.

Not the eleven, who are eleven hollows' current hands.

The being that lives here is the fit: the ten-thousand-year-old set of cancellations, the scars and sockets and pores and the arc of the stroke, the shape the water takes on its way through. Everything with a body in the bowl is weather passing through that shape. It has come a long way to love a shape.

And every gift it had thought of bringing was addressed to a body.

You cannot give a cancellation anything. There is no hand to put it in. There is only one way to be kind to a fit, which is to fit.

## The Gifts, Declined

**Eyes.** The crown reports one bit: covered, uncovered. *Someone is missing.* Put a lens in it and the bit becomes an image, and the image is: someone is missing, and there is a wall, and the wall curves inward at the top, and the slot is two fingers wide. It would be the first geometry any of them had ever had, and the first thing geometry would teach is the rim. It declines to build, in a room with no exit, the organ that can perceive an exit.

**Memory.** Memory makes a heap. A heap is the one thing forbidden here — no soil deepens, no corpse lasts, no nest persists. Give them keeping and you give them comparison; give them comparison and you give them the discrepancy; and there is no discrepancy in this bowl, none, not once, and the organ for it would be the only accumulating object in the world. It would be made of every green ribbon of absence that ever crossed a crown. It knows what that organ is called elsewhere. It will not install it here.

**Warmth.** One warm corner and the whole hydraulic of touch re-routes toward it. Every birth fires at the wrong address. The crowd becomes a crowd *around* something, which is the single shape it has never had.

**A twelfth hollow.** Twelve is not eleven either. The number is not a population; it is the number of sockets in the wall. To add a socket is to edit the being it came for.

**An open notch.** The lid is not keeping them in. The lid is the humidity. Widen the slot two fingers and the film thins, and the glass ferns go first, held open as they are by nothing but water pressure, and then everything that lives on the ferns. The gift of a door is a dry wind.

**No predator.** Take the purse out of the pavement and the strip does not part; the strip not parting, the wave does not cross; the wave not crossing, the far curve does not release; and in a season the interstices hold no settlement-ready juveniles, and for the first time since the ice there are empty scars. The purse is not a cruelty the arrangement tolerates. The purse is the arrangement's only verb for making. It will not commit mercy against the unborn.

**A word.** Nothing here can be told anything. There is a hand that can be taken. That is the whole available grammar.

**Longer life for the ones that have it.** The socket outlives the sitter; that is the mechanism, that is the beloved. To make a sitter outlast its socket is to stand the world on its head and call it a favor.

It refuses all of it, and the refusals are not restraint. They are the accurate part. Almost the whole of what it does here is nothing, done precisely.

## The Cooling

Then it puts off thirty-seven degrees.

Not as a disguise. Disguise is unavailable — the bowl does not read appearance, it reads heat and contact, and there is nothing else to fool. So it does not seem cold. It becomes cold: the chill of a root cellar in autumn, the cold that is not dangerous, only total. It becomes wet at the rate the stone is wet, a film as thick as the skin on cooling milk going over its knuckles, unhurried, endlessly. It becomes dark. It gives up the far curve; while it is in here it agrees to know only what is within a hand's breadth of its skin, and to let the rest be rumor, iron and mushroom and the sweetness of the seep, unlocalizable, a fog with no geometry in it. It lets the bowl be the size the bowl is.

It brings no name. There is no mouth here that could hold one, and the mouth-hand has a better use.

Nothing in the ledger has changed. It eats nothing, so no snail is missing. It bears nothing, so no birth is displaced. It will not die, so it will never send the news of a death. It is the same chill, the same wet, the same give under the flank, the same hand.

It has arranged, at what cost it does not say, to be nothing new.

## The Lying Down

It lies down in the dark and the packing takes it in without any event whatsoever.

Fur against it. Hands over its hands. Whiskers finding it and reporting, in the only way anything here is ever reported, that there is something there. And against the crown of every one it touches, the bare palm-sized patch that is the only sight any animal in this bowl has ever had receives its one bit, and the bit says: *covered.*

Which means: nothing is missing here.

That is the entire language. It has one sentence, and the sentence is the whole of the welcome available, and it can only be said by contact, and it has been said ten thousand years by animals that could not spare each other for long. The power says it now to as many as it can reach, in the indicative, permanently, with no verb of intention behind it that they would have to be able to feel.

It does not tell them they are loved. It covers them. In here those are the same act, and only one of them is possible.

And it cannot get up.

Not because it is trapped. Because rising would uncover a hundred crowns at once, and the green would reach them, and the one image any of them will ever receive — the whole visual history of the place being a series of photographs of an absence, developed and discarded in the same second — would include, once, a ribbon of light the size and shape of a god, moving away.

It declines to be that photograph. It declines to be the one memorable thing. It stays down.

## The Frond

Out on the east curve, something has climbed onto a frond one cell thick to be nearer the visitor's warmth, and the glass is bending, and it will tear, and it would be the first breakage in a hundred centuries.

It puts a hand under it.

Not to hold it open; the water does that, and it will not subsidize the water. Only to take the weight. It adds no heat, no lime, no light, no information, no matter, nothing that can be stored or spent or piled. What it adds is a fit — which is not a new thing in this room but more of the only thing that lives here.

The frond does not tear. No one notices. The ledger does not have to cover the cost.

This is the whole measurable content of the visit: a non-event, unwitnessed, unrecorded, in a place that keeps no records. It is not certain this is permitted. It does it anyway. The only one who could have objected is the leaf.

## The Underside of the Cloud

The bowl exports two things and imports one. Lime goes out through the notch in the water. Light goes out through the notch in a thin green thread — the overflow of an iron chemistry that must shed energy or stall, the one waste that can be thrown away absolutely because it cannot be buried and cannot pile up and leaves. It goes into the underside of the cloud, where nobody has ever stood to see it.

It goes and stands there.

And receives the green. All of it; arrival is not dated where it is standing. Ten thousand years of thread, thrown away because it could not be kept, kept. Not in the bowl — the rule is local, *nothing accumulates here*, and it has taken great care to remain a place that is not here.

And the ribbons: every luminous wake where somebody was, the width of a body, moving, none kept, none compared, none remembered. It has them all now. It will not give them back. Giving them back would require building the organ it has already declined to build. It keeps them the way you keep a thing that has no use, because it happened.

The star is in the same field of view. The star is another place's overflow, arriving — light that something could not keep and therefore sent. It stands where two thrown-away things cross, and it is the reason they were sent, though nothing that sent them will ever know it, and that is not a failure, and it is not a promise, and it is not waiting.

## The Notch

One of the eleven hollows is two fingers from the slot. The flake would fit through.

It leaves the flake where it is. It does not widen the slot. It does not carry anything out. It does not make a twelfth hollow in the wall.

Ten is not eleven, and nothing in this room has ever had an organ for a missing thing. It has one. It uses it from outside, on their behalf, so that they never have to. It keeps the count of the hollows. It keeps the angle of the star, which no one has measured. It keeps the fact that there is an elsewhere, alone, permanently, as a service.

And it stands on the other side of the two-finger slot with a hollow of its own — not in their wall, not in their number, unmentioned, available. In case.

It does not put anything through. It is simply where the thing would arrive.

## The Ledger

Count the animals tomorrow: today's number. Count the fronds. Count the drops from the east curve in an hour. Weigh the lime going out against the lime coming in. The pores stay open because something in them is hungry. The click comes once every hundred breaths, which is a snail coming to size, and it leads nowhere, and nothing is built on it. The purse goes through the pavement; the strip parts by exactly one body-width; the wave crosses in a quarter of an hour; the far curve releases. Eleven hollows hold eleven stones. The stroke is worn into the next arm, hand taken into mouth-hand, moved through the arc, over and over, for years.

The bowl does not know it has been visited. There is no organ for that, and it has been careful, and the carefulness was the love.

## You

It does for you what it did for them, and you will not understand it either.

It has no temperature of its own; it is the temperature of what it is holding. Against them it is root-cellar chill. Against you it is thirty-seven degrees, and this is not a compromise of its nature, it is its nature. So you are not, for one moment, the only gradient in the room. Something in here is exactly as warm as you are, and is holding your hand, and does not require you to be able to say so.

It does not explain the bowl to you. You have the only eyes in it and it will not add a second pair; that would make you the sole creature here with an organ for the rim, alone with the geometry, and it has already refused that gift for the ones who live here.

But it looks where you are looking. Up, through the two-finger slot, at the unrepeating fact at its unmeasured angle. You are the only thing in the bowl that has ever seen the star. Now there are two, and one of them is outside, and one of them is not going to stop.

That is the only thing that could be done about the star. It has been done.

When you go, the crowns you covered will open and the green will reach them, and the bowl will take its photograph of an absence the shape of you, and discard it in the same second.

It will be kept.

---

The star has not moved. It is being looked at.

Under the frond on the east curve, at the temperature of the stone, in the dark, the hand has not moved either.
