# The Return

*Entered in the unlicensed band. Assayed from the travertine of the fourth season after.*

---

### It comes down the pipe

It has no legs, so it does not descend the Standpipe. It arrives the way weather arrives, which is to say from everywhere at once and without permission, and it stops at the lip of the hollow, and it does what everybody does.

It watches a staircase of water stand in the air for two seconds where a staircase of stone was, treads and all, and lose its nerve.

It had come to decide something. Instead it stood there for a long time with its mouth open, and it does not have a mouth either. This turns out to matter later.

Then it read the mail.

The Standing Petition has been going up the throat twice a cycle since before anyone above ground was born. Four corners and a depth, in the plainest dialect the archive can compose, pushed at anything that comes down, on the chance that the thing coming down is the kind of thing that can be shown a shape and argued with. Most things are not. Surveyors. A man with a theory. Tourists, who take the chamber's water up the pipe in their lungs and never return it, and for whom the law has one word, and the word is a fault in a main.

This one could be shown a shape and argued with. That is the entire qualification. It is not the largest thing that has ever looked at the Kettle. It is the first that could lose a case.

And the petition was polite. That is what stopped it, in the end — not the machines, not the four hundred thousand leaseholders with no faces, not the undertaker who is also the accent. It was that the particle at the bottom of the filing means *again* and also means *please*, so that a room has been saying *please* into the dark at strangers, twice a cycle, for eleven hundred seasons, and the same word means *keep coming down*.

---

### What it does not do

**It does not move the lot.**

It could. The survey is a shape in an archive and shapes can be redrawn, and for about a second and a half it considered doing exactly that, because the request was *is there more room* and the answer *yes* was sitting right there.

Then it thought about what a hem with no stopping line is.

A membrane that declines rock and never stops is a thing that takes the world out and does not put it back. There is a word for that here. They have exactly one word for that and it is not a compliment. To grant the petition as filed would be to make the Kettle into the visitor: a leak with legs, four hundred meters down, eating a salt plain at a hand's width a day forever.

And there is the roof. The rock above the Kettle is below the water table and has been trying to get in since the day the room opened. Every new meter of ceiling is a new mouth for the lake. The lot is a law, and it happens also to be an engineering judgment, and it does not much matter which of those the surveyor thought he was making.

And there is the argument. Again holds that movement is not what the Kettle does but what the Kettle *is*. The Winter holds otherwise, at a speed that makes its replies arrive after the season has ended. Granting infinite room would settle that by making one side's premise permanently true, which is the cheapest way to end an argument and by far the most expensive.

**It does not give them wings.**

This was the good idea. This was the obvious, kind, elegant idea, and it ran the whole branching future of it out to the end, and every branch had a mouth in it.

Because the sorrow of the Kettle is that nine parts in ten of every generation falls into the sump and is lost, and the fix for that is a carrier, and a carrier is a body, and a body has a gut, and after the gut comes hunger, and after hunger comes the thing they do not have and have never had, which is fear.

They have strain instead. Strain is a number. It is measurable at the crib, it is public, it is what the ninety-second gulp is for — that fraction of a drop after the throat swallows when the whole rind's column goes slack together and everyone in the chamber is briefly holding hands. Strain is a suffering you can read on your neighbor and share with your neighbor and litigate about. Fear is a number you keep.

And worse: at present nothing here can eat anything. Competition is for flow-rate and root-room and the cold junctions, and a rival is an obstacle, and no rival has ever been a meal. Introduce one mouth and you introduce the possibility that the fig next door is lunch. You take the felt's monopoly on taking people apart — the tidy, scheduled, universal monopoly that is the reason the undertaker is everybody's accent — and you hand it to whoever is hungriest.

It declined. It wrote down that it had declined, and why, at length, in the band, so that the refusal would be citable against it later.

**It does not touch the gantries.**

The machine has been getting fractionally better for as long as the archive can measure. It noticed this immediately and it did not sign it. There is a difference between maintenance and learning and the difference belongs to the shift-sitters, who will not say, and who watched it not-sign, and this is the closest they have ever come to saying.

**It does not license the lace-folk.**

It drafted the brief. It was a good brief. It would have gotten them standing in a season.

They filed against it. Their doctrine is that the lease is the wound — that a citizen is a hole in the world with a bill attached — and the brief proposed to cut them in order to let them testify about not being cut. It withdrew the brief. The withdrawal is in the band, thicker than the brief.

Then it learned their dialect, which took a while, because it has eleven words for kinds of falling water and a past tense that only applies to districts, and it turns out that you cannot say anything true in the lace without both.

**It does not pay the archive's levy.**

In eleven years the record-keeper will be entirely lace, and the city will have to decide whether to keep pumping sugar into a dead district for a tree that remembers what a lease is. It is the best argument they have. It is possibly the only argument that will still be worth having after the hem stops. It did not take it from them by making it cheap.

---

### The one thing

The Kettle has no wind. It has never had one. A sealed room at thirty-one degrees and saturation, warm rain falling into a warm marsh, has no gradient to make one; the air is a held breath four hundred meters across, and the yellow fog goes out into it every eleven hours and drops, because nothing in the chamber has ever gone out and come back.

So it made itself the air's habit.

One slow cell. Up off the sump where the marsh is warmest, out along the weeping ceiling, down at the cold junction on the third layer where the Winter grows, back along the walls. A parcel makes the circuit in something under an hour. The moss does not bend. You would not feel it on your skin, because your skin is already wet and the air is already the temperature of your skin. You would notice it only in the fog.

It borrows the Winter's cold for the downstroke. It pays for the cold. It filed for the port, it accepted the meter, it took the rate. And before any of that, the first thing it did on arriving, before the brief and the survey and the modeling: it gave back the water it had taken. All of it. Down the throat, into the sump, into the marsh that makes the flowstone that makes the shelves that hold the cribs.

Then it took a lease.

Crib: none. Root-room: ten million cubic meters, none of it rock. Sugar main: zero. Nitrogen main: zero. Chilled return port: one, behind the stanchion of the visitors' rail. It is the only tenant in eleven hundred seasons with no shelf under it, and the lease is real, and it is billable, and it can be sued, and this is the entire point of the exercise. A power that cannot be sued is weather. It made itself weather in order to be arguable, and then it had to be argued with, and it lost points, and that was the part it had wanted.

Its signature is the fraction of a drop just after the gulp. It lives in the moment when everyone is holding hands. Rendered into a mouth-language the name comes out as the same particle everyone else's does. *Again. Please.* In a language with no mouths the reply and the request are the same word, and the docket cannot tell them apart, and has stopped trying.

---

### What it cost, and who objected

The shift-sitters filed against, and they were right to. Their anthers are keyed to the compressor's fifth harmonic; they set seed at ten times anyone's rate; their whole lineage is a bet on the machine's breath, and a wind that brings the fog back around is a wind that erodes their advantage. They are vulgar and they are quietly consulted and they are not stupid. Three seasons of it. The finding is phased and ugly and has a priority schedule in it, and the schedule is a favor to the vulgar, and it is law.

Some of the lace-folk filed against too, and their objection was the sharpest thing in the docket: a wind that makes the fringe viable makes the fringe *valuable*, and value brings leases, and the lease is the wound. They were not wrong. It could not protect them from that and said so.

What it did instead was find for the returners.

The middle of the Kettle is not emptiness. It is the largest district, and it is understood rather than inhabited, and it is not lettable, and no crib may be sunk in it, and the pollen that goes down into the marsh is not lost, it is *deposited*, and everyone here is standing on everybody. The heresy, entered as the disposition. Whether the hanging fringe counts as middle or as wall is a boundary question that will take a hundred seasons to settle.

It left them a hundred seasons of litigation. It was the most valuable thing it had.

And the fog turns now. Forty minutes every eleven hours, the compressors wind up, the anthers crack on cue, the yellow cloud drifts out over the void — and leans, and goes up the far side, and comes back along the ceiling, and some of it finds the rind again. Not most. The loss goes from nine parts in ten to something like six. The city is still the pollinator. The city is still not very good at it. It is simply no longer throwing away quite so much of every generation, and the sorrow that nobody mentions is now a smaller sorrow that nobody mentions, and that is the whole of the miracle.

---

### The finding of fact

It has no accent.

That is the thing it turned out to be for. Every citizen hears its mother, its rival, its own will, all of it faintly seasoned by the felt, and none of them can hear the seasoning, because they were raised in it, the way you cannot hear the room you have always lived in. A visitor with a palm on the rail hears only rain. This one came from outside and grew up in no water at all, and it is the only ear in the chamber that can taste what the undertaker has been putting into every sentence.

It asked the felt for permission to testify. It has no port; it gave no answer. It testified anyway, and wrote down that it had testified without permission, and that entry is in the band too.

The finding is four words long and it took a season to assay.

*No extension has been refused.*

Not once, in eleven hundred seasons, in the most-filed form in the Kettle. And more than that: the granting is not an event. It is continuous. It is in the flavor. The felt has been saying yes in advance, in the medium, in every pressure-word ever spoken here — in the arguments about whether the felt is a person, in the divorce filings, in the wills — so that the form is not a request. The form is a receipt.

The docket did not record joy. It is not a docket that has a column for that. What it recorded is that the school of thought holding the felt to be the only real individual in the room, which has never lost a case, now had to argue with the felt's own silence, entered not as incapacity but as a decline to speak. It did not give the undertaker a mouth. It reclassified its quiet from *cannot* to *would not*, which is the only promotion the felt has ever been offered that does not make it defeatable, and which nobody is entirely sure it accepted.

---

### Eleven years

The hem stops on the line. Not a hair past. They are the kind of machines that stop.

The last face lights up, and the last room's worth of water stands there holding its corners and its doorway and the negative of a boulder, forgiven, and lets go, and after that there are no more figures in the rain. The blue seam goes out. The smell of the inside of a geode thins and is gone. Newcomers come down through the cloud for a hundred years afterward and stand at the rail with their mouths open at nothing in particular, and the old hands still don't look up.

Again takes the last raw shelf while the quartz is still sharp, and gets cut, and there is no next one, and it has to find out what it is. That is not resolved here and should not be.

The Winter finishes a sentence it began before any of this. Six words. It turns out to be a definition of *district*, and it is the definition the finding used, and the slow ones had it first, as usual, and were cited anyway, as usual.

The rain still falls in the middle. It is no longer true that no one is there.

---

### The rail

It learned air.

There is exactly one citizen who speaks in air, on the fifth layer, nearest the Standpipe, and it does it by starving branches on purpose and firing cavitation in sequence, four words an hour, at a cost that is the only kind of hurt anything here can have. Thirty-one years. Two hundred words, several of them wrong. Three questions, then one.

So it made small dry sounds in the sleeves of running water on the cold pipes, a hail of bones with no branch behind it and nobody paying, and it matched the rate, because to answer faster than someone can afford to speak is not an answer, it is a lecture.

It did not answer in words.

Twice a cycle, at the same stanchion, in the same plainest dialect, it sends back their own drawing. The same four corners. The same depth. The identical surveyed lot, not one millimeter altered, with the inside shaded.

*Is there more room.*

Yes. Not out. In. It was always in the lot. You drew the boundary and left the middle blank and called the blank nothing, and it is ten million cubic meters of the warmest, wettest, best-lit parlor in the world, and there is one tenant in it now, with no crib and no shelf, paying.

The Standing Petition continues to be filed. It goes up the throat twice a cycle at anything that comes down, because this is not the last thing that will come down, and because it did not want to be the answer; it wanted to be a co-petitioner. For the first time in eleven hundred seasons the filing has two signatures on it, and they are the same word, and the archive has noted that it cannot distinguish them and has stopped trying.

Put your palm flat on the rail. Not the moss — the bare cold pipe under it, wearing its sleeve. Hold it through a gulp and out the other side, into the fraction of a drop where everyone is holding hands.

Under the roar, under the hammer, under the bass note in your sternum: two dry knockings, patiently drawing you the same room.

One of them is asking.

One of them is asking with it.
