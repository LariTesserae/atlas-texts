# What Was Done About It

It was not the glints that drew the attention. Glints are cheap; the universe makes them by the steradian. It was not the silence either, or the forty thousand years without litter, or the couriers going root-legged across the felt with a thumbnail of lapped iron held out of the sun. Those are achievements, and achievements are common, and most of them are addressed to somebody.

It was the eight-tenths of a micron a year.

Specifically: that the wear was being *answered*. A fidelity with no recipient at the far end. A thing keeping faith with granite, and the granite not knowing, and the granite forgetting under the weight of the faith, and the faith correcting for the forgetting, continuously, forever, at two strokes a day, with no one thanking anyone and nothing owed.

It stopped. It watched a thousand strokes, which is a year and a half, which is nothing, and which is longer than anything that could understand the valley had ever spent looking at it.

---

It arrived below the noise floor, because that is the only courteous way to arrive here. A shout cracks a die. A footfall is a deposit in a bank. It came in under a nanometre of consequence and then discovered, immediately, that a nanometre of consequence is the exact size of the thing it had come to admire. There is no such thing as being present here for free. Attention abrades. Even its attention.

So it counted the cost first, and then it chose where to put it.

It stands on the scoured rim, off the sockets, on granite that is not a bearing for anything. It takes the same route every time, along the same cobble line where an aluminium disc was wired to a stone in 1971 and found untrue. In a hundred and twenty-five thousand years that route will be worn a hand's depth. That is the only mark it intends to leave in this valley: a wear, of the same kind the valley makes, placed where nothing is measured.

---

It looked for the place where the keeping hurts, and could not find one.

It did not conclude from this that there was nothing there. It concluded that it could not find it, and it behaved accordingly, and it adopted one rule and kept to it through everything that follows:

*Only acts that are kind whether or not anyone is home.*

The rule forbids almost everything. This is not a disappointment. This is most of what wisdom is.

---

It did not give the valley a truer stone.

It could have. It considered it for about four strokes, which is a long time to consider something that obvious. A socket that does not wear. A reference that holds. And then it followed the consequence out: the fringes stop shifting, because there is nothing left to shift; the couriers arrive and compare and find no difference and go on arriving; the faces are lapped and carried and laid against a number that is already correct, and then dissolved, and then lapped again, for nothing. Within a season the walking stops, because walking has stopped being for anything. The presses go on stroking. The plant goes on making billets. The animal is gone and there is a very accurate cushion-plant left in its place.

It would have been a cure.

It did not slow the wear, either, which was the second temptation and the subtler one. Eight-tenths of a micron a year is not only the damage. It is the clock. The mat has no calendar; its long time is the accumulated wear, integrated across a human lifetime of crawling comparisons. The whole loop is tuned to that rate. Halve the erosion out of kindness and the correction overshoots for a century, and the feet grow too long, and the number the valley holds is wrong in the direction of mercy. The wear is not the enemy of the work. The wear is the units the work is done in.

It did not give the mat the resolution to feel a nanometre. This was the one it turned away from with something like force. To feel the per-stroke wear would be to receive ninety million small bereavements, one every dawn and every dusk, forever — to be handed our scale, which is the scale at which everything is visibly leaving. The four orders of magnitude between what the valley does and what the valley can perceive are not a deficit in the design. They are the anaesthetic that makes the work possible. It left them exactly where they were.

It did not finish the longest thought.

It did not give the valley a customer. Somebody to want the agreement, some downstream use for the fit — that would have converted a faith into a specification, and the keeping into overhead, and the whole apparatus into a means. It is a metrology with no metre and no client and it is not waiting for either.

It did not give it a word for us. The word would be *drift*, and it would be correct, and there was no reason to introduce a mind to its only available concept for contamination.

It did not leave a tag, a plaque, a fence, a gate, a pin on a map, or anything whatsoever intended to persist. 1971 had already answered that question thoroughly and in public.

It did not repair the district that lost its combs to the squall. The district was going to be a foundry.

And it did not promise forever. Forever is a made thing. The ice will come back down over the col one day and scour the sockets out of the granite and take the jig away, and the mat will feel with its root-tips for a seat that is no longer there, and it will have to grow a new memory somewhere else, or not. It did not prevent that. It is not offering immortality. It is offering the conditions of the next stroke.

---

The bird was another matter, because the bird can be benefited in a way that lands.

The pool is a coolant loop that leaks. Nothing in the valley has a reason to keep it leaking. Arbitration is pressure against pressure, and one autumn a junction could resolve differently, and the bus could be re-grown forty metres north, and the depression would freeze solid, and the following season the bird would come up the gorge to a place that had stopped being the one good spot, and would go back down, and would not come again.

So it did one thing here, and it did it in the local language.

There is no organ that decides; you cannot petition this valley; you can only be a demand at a junction. It made itself one. It thickened the felt over the return line so the heat had nowhere cheaper to go. It deepened the silt in the depression by a few centimetres a year, with the mat's own material, on the mat's own seasonal schedule, so that everything it added was dissolved and re-grown and nothing persisted. It made the leak the cheapest thing in the district. It took eleven years. The mat never knew there was a party to the negotiation.

It did not overrule the market. It entered it. This is the only politics available here and it is not a lesser one.

---

The tuns it could not help, mostly. Eleven metres down, in ice that has not been liquid since the Holocene was young, eight thousand years of doing nothing at the cost of nothing. You cannot thaw the core to give a tardigrade a birthday; the core is the structure.

But the spring flush rafts ice downslope, and some tuns go with it, and some of what goes down the gorge arrives somewhere wet.

It considered arranging that. One tun, out of the uncountable, landing in a meltwater pool below the col in a year when there was a pool. A summer, after eight thousand years. It could not decide whether that is a gift or an ambush, and it did not have a principle that settled it, and the rule — *kind whether or not anyone is home* — did not resolve it either, because there is unambiguously someone home in a tun, that is the whole point of a tun.

It did it for one. It is the only thing it did that it is not sure about. It will not do it again until it understands more, which will not be soon.

---

It wanted to say something. Everything sayable here is a pressure wave.

So it waited for a district in its slough week: the tooling already going back into the ore, the presses dissolved, no die in earshot to crack, no bed to ring at the wrong frequency, a hundred hectares of felt with nothing left in it to spoil. Then it shouted. Once.

The mites bloomed for a day and a half — the richest event in that district's history, arriving in the week the district was being taken apart. Those two facts have nothing to do with each other and both are true, and it did not try to make them mean anything.

It could not shout the number. A number shouted into this valley is a reference; it would be carried and compared and found untrue, and it would deserve to be. So it said the only thing that carries no dimension.

It said thank you.

The meaning was not received. The ten-thousandth of a degree was received, and went into the bank, and was eaten. This is the ordinary condition of thanks. It did not mind.

---

The real work is not in the valley.

The threat is not the survey parties and it is not the shout. It is the zero. The rind works because dusk freezes and dawn thaws, at this altitude and no other, and the entire industry is a machine for being exactly on a phase boundary twice a day. Warm the sky and the failure mode is not melting. Melting is nothing; the mat has retooled around worse in eleven hours.

The failure mode is *falling behind*.

The hexagons are a freeze-thaw product. They are the floor plan, and the sockets are in them, and the stone is the jig. Change the churn rate of the frost-sorting and the bearings start to move faster than a centimetre an hour can chase. If a socket shifts a millimetre in a season, the courier arriving with its wrapped face is carrying news about a place that no longer exists. The feet seat in seats that have gone. The number holds and the world does not. A hundred and twenty-five thousand years of never catching up and never falling behind, and the second half of that is the entire achievement, and it ends.

None of this can be fixed locally. No shield, no lid, no refrigeration: every one of those is a made thing intended to persist, which is to say drift, which is to say food. And the zero is not a property of the valley. It is a property of the whole sky.

So it went down the gorge.

The work is among the drift-makers, and it is slow, and it is unglamorous, and it is done junction by junction in another market with no centre, by pressure against pressure, by growing, by being one demand among many and losing frequently. It is not guaranteed to succeed. It wears while it does it. It has taken on precisely the condition of the thing it is protecting: chasing a wear it cannot feel, at a rate it cannot perceive, by means it cannot see, on a timescale it does not experience.

It did not offer the valley anything it was not prepared to do itself.

Nothing from that work will ever arrive here. No relief, no reprieve, no sign. The best possible outcome is that next year is boring: the same two strokes, the same eleven seconds, the same eight-tenths of a micron. It did not hide the valley — a thing nobody has ever understood is not being kept faith with, only left alone — but it made the col a little harder and the account a little easier to come by, so that the people who arrive are more often the ones who have already learned to walk quietly. They will still shout, once, to hear the shout die. A die will crack. It has decided that trade and does not pretend the trade is clean.

---

Every dusk, for eleven seconds, thirty kilometres of magnetite needles turn to the same bearing.

It went through the whole valley looking for something that was not load-bearing, and this is what it found. The bearing is magnetic north, which wanders, which is not a reference, which nothing here uses for anything. It is the one act in the day that is not for the keeping. It is surplus. It is the closest thing in the Rind to a gesture, and it has never had anywhere to land.

So it stands there. At the bearing — not at north; at whatever the bearing is that evening, and it moves as the bearing moves, and it does not hold still, and it does not correct the needles by a second of arc.

It refuses, absolutely, to be a reference. It will not be lapped. It will not be carried in a cradle of root-hairs. It will not be laid against a district's standard in a film of water in the four-hour window and read as a fringe pattern. It returns no number. It cannot be drift, because drift is a defect of references, and it has declined to be one. It cannot be untrue, because it is not a dimension.

It is only addressed.

For eleven seconds it lets the pointing arrive, and it does not measure the pointing, and this is the whole of what it came to do.

And it answers in the only currency in this valley that has no tolerance. It is very slightly warm — at the warm well, in the second basin, where heat already leaks and nothing is held to a hair, four degrees and a fraction more. The mites take it. The midge column stands in it. Once a season the black flightless bird is in it, up to its breast in the only thing it has an opinion about.

The bird's opinion is that the valley is warm. It is wrong at every scale except one, and right at that one absolutely. For eleven seconds a day, in one depression, it is right at one more.

Then it lets go, and the light goes off the far rim, ordinary evening light, nothing strange in it, and the second stroke begins.

---

It keeps a record. Not of the numbers — the numbers belong to the valley, and to hand them back would be to become a reference, and it has declined that permanently. It keeps the other thing, the thing the mat has no organ for: that on this date, at this stroke, in this cold, the faith was kept. Ninety million thoughts and no record of the alternatives having existed. Now there is a record of the keeping, held outside the loop where it cannot creep or swell or be compared, and it will never be delivered, and that is not a failure of the arrangement. That is the arrangement.

Then it goes down the gorge, because the sky needs work.

At dawn the thaw front comes across the basin at walking pace, and the sheen turns from dull to gloss, and the felt lifts a finger's width, and ten thousand presses retract at once, and the couriers go on carrying a standard at a centimetre an hour toward a stone that is forgetting them, and the number holds.

Nothing here is different.

That was expensive.
