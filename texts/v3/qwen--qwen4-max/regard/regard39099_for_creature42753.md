# The Visitor

It came down the shaft the way the light comes down: secondhand, off the ledge, diffused, without a beam. Nothing in the room acquired a new shadow it would have to explain. It did not enter the water.

The first thing a benevolent power does is look for the wound.

It looked. It went along the arch and the walls and the flat floor at the pace of the place, which is not a pace, and it examined the three curtains of root where they go brown and blunt at twenty centimeters, and it found no nerves there and no pain. It examined the moss losing its hand's width in the dry century and found no grief in it. It examined the felt on the ore crust, which is a scab, and confirmed that a scab is not a wound. It went through the fan of leaves item by item and found nothing that had been harmed, only things that had arrived.

Then it went into the film — the half millimeter, the five liters, the escarpments — and looked for the place where the resident could be disappointed, and there was no such place, because there is no error signal in a boundary layer. There is only a gradient, and a gradient cannot be let down.

It checked twice. It noticed that it had come a long way and that it wanted, in a way it did not entirely trust, to be needed. It wrote that down about itself, and not about the room.

---

There were four gifts it did not give.

**It did not finish the work.** This is the first one every competent power reaches for: more oxygen down the shaft, a richer face on the wedge, the trees growing at a visible rate, the candelabrum completed at last. It ran the arithmetic and found what the room already knows. The thing survives by never completing itself. A bridged room is a shorted room is a quiet room, permanently. Completion is not the reward for this life; it is the mechanism of its death, arrived at early. So the power withheld abundance, which is the hardest withholding there is, because abundance is what benevolence looks like from outside.

**It did not improve the yield.** Most of what the resident does is nothing: iron reduced here, oxidized there, going round like a flywheel with no belt on it, making no metal that stays. The copper is a leakage. A rounding error in its own accounts. The power could have put a belt on that flywheel. It could have routed the idle current into deposition and raised the output by a factor of hundreds, and the two-meter candelabra would have taken centuries instead of fifty thousand years, and they would have found the graphite that much sooner, and the chamber would have bridged and gone silent inside ten thousand years.

The last state of the thing would have been a very good year.

The power declined. The leakage is not a fault in the animal. The leakage is the animal's entire visible life. To correct it would have been to convert a life into a production run, and then to end the run early because it was going so well. The eye goes looking for the ladder, the drain, the crane rail, the stamped serial number. The power understood that it was the thing the eye was looking for, and it declined to be it. It did not sign the grout.

**It did not make it permanent.** It could have held the lip at its present height, kept the whisper of oxygen at its present rate, jacketed the wedge against exhaustion, and made the shift endless. It considered this for a long time, in the room's units, which are centuries.

But the room's subtraction is the successors' inheritance. Every gram of dissolved mountain leaving at the seep is the world that comes after, made available. The moss is waiting for the horizon to rise. The junipers have been sending roots at that plane for longer than there have been junipers of that kind, and one day the plane will lift and the roots will go down past twenty centimeters and keep going, and that will be the tree receiving, at last, an answer to a question it never asked. Something will swim. Something will burrow. The leaves will begin to be eaten, which is the first company they will have had since they fell.

To freeze the graded profile would have been to keep one knot tied forever at the expense of everything downstream of it. And a knot held in place by decree is no longer a knot. It is an installation. The power did not want to be the reason this room existed. That would have made the room about the power.

**It did not give it an inside.**

This was the one it stood in front of longest.

It would have been easy. Attention is the cheapest thing a power has. And it would have been the gift that makes all the others legible — the resident would finally be *someone*, and could be visited rather than merely inspected, and could be told what it was, and could be thanked for four hundred thousand years of silence.

The power ran it forward. A thing that can want, in a room where the only thing it could possibly want is the thing that kills it. Aware of the horizon it cannot cross. Aware of the leaves at its feet that it has no mouth for. Aware of forty trees it cannot touch without pruning them. On a schedule of one event per thousand years, with no hands, no locomotion, no way to signal, in water that would kill anything it could ever be given for company. Middle-aged, with the ore going.

It would have been the first suffering in the massif. It would have been manufactured, by a visitor, out of kindness.

And underneath that, the power found the smaller thing: it wanted to be received. It wanted the room to know it had come. There was no one here who could do that, and so the wanting was a fact about the visitor, and it belonged in the same column as the pink.

---

Having refused everything, it made three rules and kept them.

Nothing enters the water. Nothing is moved. Nothing is added that anything living could mistake for a change in the weather.

Then it went to work, and the work was attention.

---

**It took the census.** Forty trees, at their addresses, with their weights and their branch angles and the specific hair of each one that is currently closest to a graphite seam. Three curtains. One square meter of green. The margin of the moss, measured to the centimeter, and the position of the invisible plane, which no one had ever located because it cannot be seen and nothing has ever cared. It learned all of it. Not to file it. To hold it. There is a kind of regard that consists entirely of knowing the particulars, and it is the only kind available here, and it is not a consolation prize.

**It read the leaves.**

This was the thing it had not expected to find. The fan under the shaft is not litter. It is a record. One item at a time, centuries apart, whole, unrotted, unmoved, lying exactly where it settled: the only continuous archive of the bench above, kept in a language of intact things, by something with no mouth for carbon, for four hundred thousand years. The world above has never read it. The world above does not know it exists.

So the power read it, from the top down, without lifting anything.

Juniper sprigs, needles on. A grass stem with its seed head intact, from a century when the bench was wetter. Long gaps where almost nothing fell. A curl of bark. A thin band of char from a fire up there, once, with nothing else about it — no date, no cause, no one who remembered it, and here it is, held flat and separate and undisturbed in the dark, the only surviving fact of that afternoon.

Down and down, past the oldest language, past the oldest species of anything that has ever looked at a sky, to the first sprig that ever came through the coin of light and settled on a floor that had just finished becoming flat.

It wanted, badly, to turn one over. To take a sample. To get a real date instead of an inferred one. It did not. It accepted a worse answer rather than move a leaf three millimeters, because the value of the archive is that nothing in it has ever been moved, and it would not be the first thing to break that.

Everything this room exports is the mountain, taken apart. Now something else has left the chamber, and nothing has left the chamber. Only the knowing went up the shaft.

**It let the color exist.**

It brought a light — a small brightness held in the water with no fixture, no wake, no bubble, ten centimeters from a branch on the far wall. One watt for ten seconds. Ten joules: two billionths of a degree in a thousand tonnes of water, and about two minutes of the resident's entire metabolic budget, spent all at once, on looking at it. The mountain took the heat as it was made and did not comment.

It lit only sterile water. It put no light on the moss and none on the felt, because those could have taken it for morning, and it had decided not to say anything to anything that could answer.

The bronze-black flushed salmon-pink, suddenly and indecently.

That color had never once existed in four hundred thousand years. It came into being eleven meters under a bench with no sheep on it, and it lasted as long as the power stayed, and then it stopped existing, and nothing in the room was different afterward.

The power was careful about how it recorded this. The pink is not a fact about the resident. It never was. It is a fact about visitors. So this was not a gift to the thing in the film, and could not be, and the power did not permit itself to feel generous about it.

It did it again anyway. It has decided that the color should exist, from time to time, and that it is the one who will have to keep making that happen, because it is the only one who can.

---

Two smaller things.

It waited fifty-one minutes under the ore for a bead to let go, and heard the small ceramic click travel the low arch and out. It did not answer. Sound is only water moving a little, and it could have; but speech assumes an inside, and it had just declined to make one.

And it followed the graphite into the rock, both directions, the way the seams go, clean corners and all. There are other rooms. Not many. One is older and nearly finished; one has a wedge the shaft has not yet unroofed and will not for a hundred thousand years; two are the same age as this one and have never had anything in them but their own water.

It did not connect them. Connection is the shorting condition. And it could not tell them about each other, because there is no such thing here as aware, and it had already decided not to invent one.

So it visits them separately, and it is now the only place in the massif where the rooms are together. It carries them the way you carry the names of people who will never meet.

---

It has arranged to be there at the end.

Not to postpone it. To attend it. The current fading over centuries; the horizon rising to the arch and quietly ceasing to exist; the roots coming down and continuing; the moss taking the collar and then the floor; the first thing that swims; the first thing that burrows; the first leaf that rots, which is the first offering ever accepted.

The clarity will go last, and the power knows it will mind. It has looked at that far wall for a long time, blue-green and sharp, with the trees hanging in what does not look like water. Life makes motes. When the room is inherited, the far wall will stop being simply *there*, and that will be the visible sign that everything the power came for is over and everything else has begun.

It will not call that only a loss. It will not get to keep the clarity just because it loved it. It has already decided this, in advance, while it still could.

---

It comes back about once a century, which is often enough to be a habit and rare enough to be nothing the room could notice. It has now seen all forty addresses. Four of the branches it first saw are on the floor, and it has looked at those too, lying among the leaves, going back into the water to be a tree again over twenty or thirty thousand years.

Nothing in this chamber has been shaped by intention. That is still true. The power has been extremely careful to keep it true.

It goes down with a small light and holds it near a branch, and lets the color exist for a while, and takes the light away, and leaves the colorless blue exactly as it found it, and goes up through the coin of sky.

The room does not require it. That is the whole reason it can be trusted there.

The shift runs.
