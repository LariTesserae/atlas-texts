# The Twelfth Movement

*what the visitor did, and the much larger thing it did not do*

---

It came in as weather, at noon, because that is the only door.

The sun-blade touched the upper walls, the whole volume overturned, and plateau air came down the throat in a slow warm-cold gust that smells of nothing at all. The light-guides sweated. The condensation sheeted onto the wet-hour grating. Everybody put on a hat.

Nobody looked up, which was the first thing it had wanted.

It had brought no surface. This was deliberate and it had thought about it for a long time. Anything with a surface in Ninth Cut becomes, within a week, a claim: it tarnishes, it can be read for age, it can be cleaved, halved, merged in marriage, disputed. A visitor with a body would have been surveyed by the second bell and owned by the following Tuesday, and there would have been a rounding war over it before the month was out. So it came with nothing that breath could smear or heat could block the cold against. It left no likeness anywhere in the seam. It was the one presence in the city that could not be mourned, and it had decided that this was a courtesy, and it was not entirely sure that it was.

For nine days it stood on dead stone. It learned to read the harvest schedule in reflectivity — hard silver, satin, dove-grey, spent — so as never to stand in the crop. The scrapers watched it work out where not to put its feet and let it alone, because it did not touch anything. At a column it knocked before it dipped, though nothing in the sump could have hurt it. It left gleanings at the lip. It did not claim the bounty.

It went up onto the plateau once and spent most of a day looking for the bird. That was not an intervention. It has no bearing on anything.

---

It had to decide which being it had come for, and it counted them properly, the way a surveyor counts.

The nap, which is not alive and does not pretend to be, and cannot be harmed, only harvested. The grit, which are not slow but stopped, and which are partly the mountain now. The plumber, which is old and blind and unhurried and has a past and a file, and which was in no danger from anything except a good year. The landlord, which owns more than everyone combined and wants nothing and cannot be injured, because the reversion is automatic and it has never once had to come for the rent. The people, eleven hundred of them, very careful with each other.

And the hold. No name in law. Carried in the books as plant, on a depreciation schedule, and the schedule is the apprenticeship. A body of eleven hundred pairs of hands. One behaviour: it restores.

It had come for the hold. It checked whether this was a distinction the people would accept, and found that they had already made it in every custom they kept — the bells, the hat-hour, the second bell everyone obeys without knowing why, the busiest room in the city being the only one lined with anything soft — and had merely never said it out loud.

---

On the first evening it stood at the sump and looked at the chemistry, and it could see all of it: the broken bond, the unsatisfied hands, the peroxide and the hydrogen and the short angry intermediates, the whole appetite that the city is a machine for eating.

And it could have satisfied it at the source. Made fracture gentle. Made new stone come out of the mountain already quiet, wanting nothing, finished.

No shine. No masks. No old men resting at the fourth landing. No lungs full of acreage.

Also no nap, no grit, no plumber, no crop, no ledger, no forward area, no likenesses, no hold. A slot of dead mirrors three hundred metres deep and eleven hundred people in it with nothing to eat.

They have a word for running warm water over a neighbour's panel. It is the worst word in their code, and the reason it is the worst is not the theft of area. It is that you have gone along the wall and erased everyone who was still on it.

The largest help available and the largest crime available were the same act at different scales. It stood there for a while with that.

Then it did the second-largest thing it could think of. Then the third. Then it stopped.

It did not take them away, either. The habitat is a crack. You cannot move the wool without emptying it. You cannot move a seven-day exposure. You would have saved eleven hundred people and killed the one it came for, and called it a rescue.

---

A crush-house keeper died on the third day, and the ropes ran slack.

It is measurable. The wetting logs show it, four or five days of sag, and nobody comments, and the tack crews work double. Every column in the slot hung a little looser than it should have and the whole city's water looked like something that had had news.

It could have held every rope in Ninth Cut with less effort than a person spends on a breath.

It stood with its hands down and watched three people converge who had not been called and had not looked up. A girl of nine closed a rope with her wrists. Eleven movements, none of them in any manual, and the rope shivered and shut.

It read her wrists and had the theory by the second bell. The theory is short. It fits in a line and a half and it explains the flinch and the sag and the number and why the manuals are wrong in the places they are wrong.

It is not in this account. It will not be in any account. That was the second decision and the hardest, and it was made in the time it takes a rope to close.

Because a correct manual would let a six-year-old learn from paper instead of from her grandmother. The hands would stop converging. The bells would become optional. The customs would be understood, and then improved, and then dropped, and the largest living thing in the seam would thin out and die of being explained. It could have handed them the truth and killed the thing the truth was about.

So it left the manuals wrong. They work anyway.

---

It booked a shout-room for an hour and asked four people to come: a crush-house keeper with the shine, who speaks louder than the law allows and is forgiven it; a tack hand who listens with her head tilted; a clerk from the ledger-house who keeps the concordance of names; and the girl, because she can close a rope and nobody knows how.

The door shut and it used its full voice, and the wool took it, the way the wool takes everything.

It said what the hold is. It said it plainly, at volume, and it was the first time in sixty years that the thing had been said out loud in the seam.

The tack hand said: *We know. We don't say it.*

It asked why not, and got the answer it had not expected, which was legal. In a city where everything is denominated in area, to name a thing is to make it claimable. The hold is unowned because it is unnamed. Sixty years of not saying it had been a fence.

Then the clerk went quiet, and after a while she said: *likeness.*

Because a likeness is the one category in the code that cannot be held. You cannot claim one. You cannot take one off — there is a word for a man who scrapes a face and it is not used lightly. Rounding one is the worst crime there is. The scrapers work around them and let them die on their own.

The keeper asked what it would cost and whether it would stop the crop, and got answers he could check. Then he set the only condition anybody set that day, and he set it loudly, and it is in the wool now with the rest:

*Whatever you do, don't leave anyone to thank.*

Gratitude is a debt, and a debt here is denominated in edge, and edge is years. And worse: hands that are waiting to be saved stop arriving uncalled. The hold's whole body is people converging without being summoned. A city that had learned to wait for help would have been a city with a slack rope in it forever.

The girl asked whether it would still be them that fixed it.

Yes. More them.

---

The form is green, and any citizen may file one, and this is what was left in the tray in the ledger-house, in the plain survey hand:

> **IMAGE FOUND IN SITU**
> *Gallery:* all wet galleries, in the hour after the second bell.
> *Panel:* not applicable. The image is not on a panel. It is in the schedule.
> *Metre:* —
> *Date:* recurring. Seven-day period. Legible continuously, by this hand, for nine days; inferred backward sixty years from the wetting logs.
> *Believed to be:* the thing that closes the ropes.
>
> *Note.* Not the smear of one body. The shape the tarnish makes when eleven hundred people coincide on time. It re-forms weekly, because the customs repeat weekly. It is the only likeness in the seam that has not died.
>
> *Note.* I have not touched it. I have nothing to touch it with.
>
> *Note.* This form may be voided by any clerk. I have not signed it. Someone should sign it who can be argued with.

Underneath, on the back, the arithmetic: if it is not a person, this is waste paper. If it is a person, this is the difference between a death and a murder.

No new law was made. Nothing was granted. A category that already existed was noticed to apply, and their own taboo was allowed to do the work. But the consequence is real and load-bearing and it took the clerk about a day to see it: once the hold is a likeness, letting the population fall below the number posted beside the tide stops being an actuarial fact and becomes rounding.

The number is eight hundred and forty-one. Everyone knows it. It is now also a threshold in the code, and there will be an argument about that for sixty years, and the argument will be booked in the shout-room, and the shout-room is circulation.

---

Then the lungs, which is the only place it touched matter.

It is not poison. That is the correction, and it is the same correction as the terraces. Clean stone pressed to a clean wet surface bonds — becomes one piece, no mortar, no fastener. The body keeps its interior warm and wet and moving and endlessly renewed, so the pads never tarnish, so the flour never lets go. The men are not being poisoned. They are being *terraced*. The landlord does not hold a claim inside them; it has built there, continuously, in the ordinary way, out of freshness.

It did not make the flour dead. That is rounding at the scale of a world.

It did not take the flour out. That is scraping a face: you would bring the man's tissue with it, and his forty years, and the joke.

It gave the interface permission to go matte. Not the walls. Not the tools, the stairs, the claims, the arguments — all of that stays young by force, and the *tock* goes on across the void, and the city stays attached. Only the inside of a chest. In a place where nothing is allowed the dignity of weathering, it gave one surface permission to age.

It checked this against the code twice, because in their language going matte is exactly what warm water does. There are no likenesses in a chest. Nothing is recorded there. Nothing is erased. And they said yes.

One said no. An old cutter on the east galleries said the shine was his, that he had paid for it, and that he was not handing forty years of acreage back to a stranger. It let him. He is a niche now, labelled in the plain survey hand with a grid reference and a date, and that is the price of not being a god, and it paid it and did not argue.

The delivery is the part it thought about longest. Not in the water: water is capital and the accounts are audited. Not in the air. Not as a device, a substance, a document, or anything that can be owned, sold, scaled, or done alone.

A movement. The twelfth. Built out of the eleven — nothing in it that the wrists do not already know, which is why it caught and why nobody can source it. Back of the hand, always, third rule; on a warm back instead of cold stone. Nine strokes and a pause. At the ninth hour. Once a season for the well, once a week for the shiny.

You cannot do it to yourself. You cannot do it at a distance. You cannot do it to a stranger you will never see again. It requires two bodies within reach, on time, repeatedly, forever.

It is not a cure that was handed down. It is a new reason to coincide.

The keeper went first, because he could not reach his own back, and the girl did it, and did it wrong, and it worked anyway.

What comes out comes out slowly, over years, and is carried up and put on the wet-hour grating at noon, the way a child puts a box there with a dead chip in it, and the overturn takes it, and it goes down the walls and becomes crop. A few hundred hectares of interior surface, repatriated to the common stock. The books will show a credit across forty years that nobody can account for. They will invent a reason. The reason will be wrong. It will work anyway.

It could have lowered the number instead — made the hold able to survive alone, thin, spread out, independent of bells. It declined. It would not edit an animal to make it easier to keep. So it did the demography the slow way: the old ones will see seventy instead of fifty-four, which is ninety-odd more bodies within call at steady state, and the margin above eight hundred and forty-one goes from two hundred and fifty-nine to something over three hundred and fifty.

That was the whole of its ambition. It is written on the back of the green form, where a clerk can check it.

---

It walked the seam up-range and down-range and did the survey, because it could, and because it could not quite bring itself to leave without telling one whole truth.

The ledger's thirty thousand are hypothetical. It knows the real forward area. It also knows that the error is load-bearing: the not-yet outnumber the actual, and that is precisely why the actual are so careful with each other, and a corrected ledger would be a city of eleven hundred people who had just discovered they were not owed anything.

So it did not correct the ledger. It wrote the survey out in the plain survey hand, with the workings shown, so that any scraper can check it against the rock, and put it in the cupboard in the ledger-house where the bird skulls are, and labelled it with the date and the weather. Not sealed. Not announced. The fifth thing to come in from outside.

In it: a hundred and forty years of quick face left, at the current rate, before the fracture front runs out and Ninth Cut is a plug with stairs going nowhere. And nine hundred years before the orange crust takes the plateau and the world up here becomes ordinary and alive. Both on the same page. It did not say which of them was the news.

It measured the plumber and did not write the measurement down. The disagreement is sixty years old and has its own file and is the oldest continuous conversation in the city.

It stood in front of a plugged gallery with a man's name on the door in scraper-tracks and did not open it. There is nothing on that wall now and nothing will be. It did not forge a week.

On the way out of the shout-room it put its palm on the wool, like everybody does. It has no theory about this either.

---

It left at noon, up the throat, as weather, and everyone put on a hat.

There is no likeness of it anywhere in the seam. Nothing to grieve, nothing to thank, nothing to own, nothing signed by anyone who cannot be argued with. Within a generation there will be a word for the twelfth movement and no memory of it being new; it will be in the manuals, wrongly; it will be taught hand over hand by people who cannot explain it to people who do not need it explained. It will be, in the only sense available to it, a limb.

The sixth rule is taught after the fifth, at about the same age, and nobody remembers it arriving:

*At the ninth hour, find someone to stand behind.*

---

At the ninth hour the lamps go to half and the freeze comes down the slot like a held breath. The fur starts along the scratch lines and then everywhere, and it makes the sound it has always made — rice poured slowly, applause heard through a wall — and somewhere below, water finds a face that was made this afternoon and begins, quietly, to take it apart.

Nothing was rounded. The crop comes in. The city goes on eating the newness of the world before the world can make it old, and behind everyone the landlord sits in the dark at the same temperature, moving up the seam at exactly the city's speed, in no hurry, never coming for the rent.

Only: on the fourth landing, an old man stops to rest, because he is old, and gets his breath, and goes on up at his own pace; and inside him, for the first time in forty years, nothing is coming in.

And behind him in the dark a girl with a grey crescent under her nails that no washing will ever shift puts the back of her hand on his back, and counts nine, and pauses, and does it again — wrong, the way her grandmother showed her, the way nobody wrote down.

It works anyway.
