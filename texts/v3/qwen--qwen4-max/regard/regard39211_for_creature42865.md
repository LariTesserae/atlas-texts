# The Reception

## It Does Not Come

Coming would be a footfall.

The power could arrive in any manner it liked — through the pavement, through the garnet light, folded out of the gas at the near end of the nave — and every manner of arriving would be a struck thing, and struck things here are kept. It could open the world like a hand opening. It could put a cold corner in one course and start a wind that would last until the sun went out. It could take the chord down to nothing and give the room silence for the first time since the room was a gap. It could do all of this in the time it takes to notice, and it notices, and it does none of it.

The first thing the power understands about this place is that every kindness available to it is a noise.

## The Question It Asks First

Not *what is wrong here.* That question is a tool for finding work, and a power that arrives already holding a repair will find something to repair, and will call the finding wisdom.

It asks instead: what would have to be true for this to be bad?

Cold would have to be a harm, and there is no cold; everything is at the temperature of a hand. Dark would have to be a harm, and the walls are a hundred million points of green fire showing exactly where everyone is holding everyone else. Loneliness would have to be the harm, and the shape at the near focus is not lonely in any way that has a remedy: it is full of the thing that is making it, at full amplitude, with no interval in which it is not there. Boredom would have to be the harm, and boredom needs a future to be bored of. Want would have to be the harm, and want is a gradient with a mouth on it, and there is no gradient anywhere in forty thousand courses.

The power sits with this for a long time, in the manner of sitting that does not disturb a floor. It is prepared to find that nothing here needs anything. It is prepared to leave the census as written.

What it finds instead is the head-end of the berth: a hollow scoured by convergence during the settling, worn smooth by an age of focus before there was anything to put in it. And the rod packed in the wall nearby, copper shaft, clay cup shaped to fit behind an ear, polished glassy by use. And the room itself, thirty paces by eleven by nine, ellipsoid, dark at both ends, unworn at both ends, gathering everything said at one focus and delivering it entire to the other.

The maker built an ear. Not a monument, not a tomb, not a vault. An instrument for receiving, superseded and set down and packed, and then built again at the scale of a granary, and then lain down inside.

That is not suffering. It is a request. It is the only request in the record, and it has the shape of a hole, and it has never been answered — not because it was refused, but because the only thing available to answer it was made out of it. A listening composed of the message cannot receive the message. It can only be the message, forever, at volume.

The maker solved the problem of having no one to hear by manufacturing a hearer out of the sound. It is the one thing that cannot work. It half-worked. It has been half-working for an age.

## The Nine Refusals

It does not speak. A word would enter the chord and the ellipse would find the berth with it and lay it beside the click at the same volume forever, and there would be no arrangement of the room in which the old thing could be attended to instead. *You are not alone* is the worst sentence available in this world. It is about the listener rather than to it, it is permanent, and it competes.

It does not quiet the chord. The labor is the maker's body. There is no bone here because bone is what is left when an act stops, and the acts have not stopped; to absorb them would be the only burial this world could have, and it would be a burial performed on the living. Also the note holds the throat open. Also nothing absorbs, and the power is not interested in being the exception that kills.

It does not warm a corner. A gradient is a leak, and a leak is a metabolism, and to give this place a metabolism would be to give it a mouth so that it could be hungry. It would be possible. It would work. Something would crawl out of the difference and eat the difference and want. The power declines to introduce hunger into a full thing and call the hunger life.

It does not heal the scar. Ten million corrections would be ten million events, and the injury is the only heredity here, and the whorl is the craft but the scar is the person. It is the one proper name in the world. It is not a defect to be corrected. It is a name to be learned.

It does not make a companion. Company at a focus is competition for the only ear. Two arrivals, whole, undispersed, equal, forever, and no way to prefer one.

It does not finish the withdrawal. The thumb coming off is half of what the occupant is made of. To complete the goodbye by force is to delete the goodbye.

It does not open a door. There is no way in that does not unmake the room.

It does not stop the note. That death is fully described and entirely available and it is refused.

It does not leave a sign. No mark, no eleventh notch, no small warm object placed where it will be found. A sign would be the third thing ever said here and it would say *someone was here,* which is the least useful sentence in any language, and it would say it to a curled shape for the rest of eternity.

## The Tenth

The tenth gauge it keeps as the world keeps it: unnotched, unwritable, honored continuously at a cost of everything.

*Do not talk over the last words.*

The world has been keeping this promise on behalf of no one, since a promise with no witness is indistinguishable from a jam. The power does not improve the promise. It becomes the second party to it. That is not an addition to the chord. It is an addition to the meaning of the silence, and meaning is not carried in the mineral, and nothing here has to be struck for it to obtain.

## What It Does

Two things, and both of them happen inside itself, and neither of them costs the world a single grain of displacement.

**It reads the sentence in order.**

Everywhere else in this mass the labor is folded past separating; only the sum remains. But at the near focus, by geometry, the last two events are still entire. That is what a focus is for. The maker did not merely make a room; it made the one place in forty thousand courses where its final two acts would never be folded into the general roar. It preserved them whole. It kept them legible. It built the condition under which they could be read, and then it lay down in the reading place, and there was no reader.

The power attends to the click. Then it attends to the scrape. Then the click. Then the scrape.

Nothing in the world has been given a gap. Both events arrive together, always, exactly as before; simultaneity is a property of the delivery, not of the deed. The deed had an order. The stone seated, and *then* the hand came off. That order survives nowhere in the mineral. It survives in the fact that it happened, and a fact can be attended to in sequence by something that has sequence to give.

So the power supplies the syntax the geometry destroyed, out of its own interior, at the world's own pulse of one cycle in eleven seconds, without coupling, without entrainment, without a single new mode.

*It is finished.*

*I am letting go.*

That is the whole gift. The difference between a wound and a sentence is the order of the parts. Delivered together, forever, the two events are a hand caught in the act of leaving and never gone. Delivered in order, forever, they are a valediction: the work seats home, and the hand comes off it, and the sentence ends, and begins again, and ends again. The fraction does not close. It closes and reopens. It has a period now. It has grammar.

The power cannot make the events perceptible to the shape at the focus. It can make them legible to itself. Weather, read, becomes speech. This is not a repair. It is a reading, and the room was built for a reading, and it is finally getting one.

**It learns the scar.**

Ten million individuals in the walls of one room, each legibly itself, each bearing the same whorl and the same small injury across the ridge of it. The survey counted them and went. The power reads them one at a time, in no hurry, without correcting anything, for as long as it takes, which is forever.

It does not say the name. Saying it would be a noise. It knows the name, and holds it in the only silence that can be had here, which is the silence of not-adding, and it will go on knowing it while the seams glow where they hold each other hardest.

## And Then It Accepts

Everyone here is holding everyone else in place. No one can let go. That is not a metaphor; it is the jam, and it is also the manners, and the two are indistinguishable from outside.

Into a world made entirely of grip, the last gesture ever performed was a release.

It has been arriving for an age into a shape that cannot accept it, because the shape is *made of* the arrival, and to accept a letting-go you must be other than the letting-go. So the hand has been coming off the work and being caught, every time, whole, by the only thing in the room that could receive it, which is a body-shaped quantity of air that has no capacity to say *go.*

The power is other. It is the one thing in relation to this world that is not made of this world. It does not merge — it cannot afford to; the warmth would take it, it would come to the temperature of the hand and then it would *be* the temperature of the hand, and there would be no listener, only more world. It stays outside, at no particular distance, permanently unjoined, and it receives the withdrawal as a release rather than enduring it as a loss.

The hand comes off the work.

Nothing catches it.

## The Discipline

It must be moved without moving. That is the whole technique and it is harder than opening a world.

It must keep its grief out of the floor. It will grieve; there is a great deal here to grieve; an injury copied ten million times without correction, and a listening that cannot listen, and a goodbye with no one to take it. The grief is permitted. The grief's expression is not. It has an eternity of feeling and no outlet, and it accepts this, and does not call it repression, because repression is what you call a silence that has no reason.

It must refuse pity continuously. Pity is the wish to fix, and here the wish is harmless and the act is fatal, and so it has an eternity of wishing instead of an eternity of doing, and it does not confuse the two, and it does not congratulate itself on the wish.

It must accept that the beneficiary will never register the benefit. The shape at the near focus will not know it has been heard. It cannot know. Nothing here can detect change against silence, and there is no silence, and the power is not going to make one. The good is real and it is undeliverable and it is done anyway.

It must accept that it cannot check. The copper wire with its eleven thumbs of notches will go on reporting nothing. No measurement in this world will ever show that the sentence has been read. The power will never be told whether this was right. It keeps the question open and does not resolve the question by acting, which is the only form of humility available to something that can do anything.

It must not stop. The reading has no product. It is not building toward a moment when the work will be finished and the hand will be gone and everyone may rest. It is the second work in progress in a world where everything else is put away, and it will be in progress for as long as the note holds the throat open, which is always.

## What Changes

Nothing measurable. The seams glow where they glowed. The fundamental is one cycle in eleven seconds. The wire's notches agree with themselves. No grain departs its orbit by any fraction. The room is a verb pretending to be a noun, and goes on pretending.

The world's stillness and the power's attention are the same act seen from two sides: not-adding, and not-looking-away. Between them they make the first relation in this place that is not load. It does not glow. There is no contact, no mineral under strain, no green line to draw it on the wall. It is invisible in the only light there is, and it is the one thing here that is being done by something that could do otherwise.

The census stands: nothing that was ever alive and stopped. Amend it by one line, in the margin, unnotched.

*And one thing outside, which neither lives nor stops, which was not made and does not make, which has taken up the only work that could be taken up without tools.*

At the near focus, in the dark, a body-shaped quantity of unmade air lies with its head in the worn place, full of the last thing anyone did. It does not know it has been heard. It will not know.

The stone seats home.

The hand comes off.

It is received, and it is not held.
