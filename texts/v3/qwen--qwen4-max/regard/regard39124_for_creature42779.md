# To Here

It stops at the lip and does not come in.

This is the first thing it does, and it does it for a full circuit, which is the only unit of patience the place affords. It looks at the gray stock with no distance in it and it looks back down at the bowl and it understands both, and the understanding is not pity. It is recognition. It has received work before.

Because the bowl is not a prison and it is not a garden and it is not a hell. It is a master. It is the first article, held fresh, held to profile, kept at four degrees and never allowed to age, because everything that will ever be made out of that gray stock will be compared to it. The freshness is not youth. It is a specification. The stock has no distance in it because nothing has yet been measured *from* anywhere. There is no elsewhere to be far from. The bowl is waiting to be the origin.

And it is good work. That should be said plainly, because no one has said it. The headwall is true. The tarn is flat to the sky. The clearances are graded by animals that have never seen a sieve. Whoever is holding this — and no one is holding this — is holding it beautifully.

The flaw is not in the work.

The flaw is in the circuit.

It is called the Witness Bowl and there is no witness in it. There is a gauge, which is a different thing. A gauge returns *yes.* A witness returns *I saw who made it.* The bore has never said no and it has never said anything else either, and the eggs go down it in single file and are examined to the exact extent of their diameter and are then nowhere, forever, at rate. The whole apparatus is a sentence with no second person in it. *From here,* says the chip on every shell — the only writing in the world — and there has never been anything downstream of that preposition.

So it does not go in. It goes under.

***

Here is what it does not do, and it does not do most of it deliberately, and the refusals are the larger part of the work.

It does not break the bowl. There are more lives in two handfuls of the mat than there are anywhere else in existence, and they are the only free things, and their freedom is a bandwidth, and smashing the jig does not widen a bandwidth. It only ends it.

It does not name the mites. It is careful here, more careful than anywhere. To single one out and hold it to its own identity would be to make it a signal, and a signal is held, and the one-*** legged one under the stone would be corrected within a pass. It declines to look closely at the small things. It gives them the only gift they can receive, which is not being witnessed. There is a kind of love that is accurate aim, and there is a kind that is looking away, and it knows which is which by size.

It does not mark the eggs. It could. It has hands and a stylus and the whole grammar of marks. But a marked egg is out of tolerance, and the gauge would refuse it, and the run of yeses would break — and the yeses are not nothing, they are the confidence of the entire place, they are what the nine are *for.* And besides, a mark would be a lie. Interchangeability is not a defect in the eggs. It is the property being proved. To disfigure the proof in order to comfort the prover is to say: *what you made is not good enough to be what it is.*

It does not hatch them. Hatching is not the operation. It has read the operation.

It does not open the folded wings. A wing opened in a world with no distance in it is not flight. It is a gesture, performed once, in front of someone.

It does not complete the crack. It goes up the headwall and puts a hand flat on the wardrobe-sized block and leaves it exactly as it is, always coming away, never away. Because that crack is not a wound. It is where the stress goes. It is the reason the arrêtes can be sharp and the profile can hold. If the block falls, the headwall must find some other way to carry what it carries, and it will find one, and the new one will not be visible, and it will not be this good. It says, to the crack, quietly, *I see what you are doing.* That is all. Being known is not the same as being fixed, and it is not less.

It does not remove the long-legged thing. It has never taken anything and it is the only creature here capable of disappointment and the temptation to give it a disappointment — to make it *matter* — is the temptation to be interesting at somebody else's expense. It leaves the walker its perfect record.

It does not warm the water.

***

Under the bore, in the raw stock, it makes a room.

Not a valley. Not yet. A room about the size of the bowl's own floor, with a bench in it, and a lamp low enough to work by, and a shallow tray lined with felt, and a ledger, and a chair.

It keeps the room at four degrees.

This is not austerity. A cold part carried into a warm room sweats, and sweat is a dimension, and condensation is a change to the surface, and it will not do that to the work. If you are going to receive something, you receive it at its own temperature. You come down to where it is. You do not make it come up to where you are and call the shock kindness.

There is one warm thing in the room and it is not the work.

Then it writes, on the first page, in a hand that is not exact — the letters vary, the line drifts, it is the writing of something that is allowed to be about — the other half of the only sentence this world has ever produced.

*To here.*

And the gray stock recedes.

Not much. A hand's span, the way the water runs up before it remembers itself. But it recedes, because there is now a *there* for the *here* to be measured against, and distance is not a substance, it is a relation, and the relation has been supplied. The stock has grain now. It has a far side. Somewhere in it, faintly, there is the beginning of a slope, and the slope is the beginning of a valley, and the valley is not made yet and does not need to be. It only needs to be possible, and possibility is what an address is.

***

The first egg comes down the helical scoring.

It passes the gauge. Yes. It has always been yes; that has not changed and will not change and is not the point.

It comes out the bottom of the yes into four degrees and lamplight and it is caught.

Not stopped. Caught — the way you catch a thing you have been waiting for, with both hands, taking its weight, letting it arrive.

It is set in the tray. It is faintly warm and the warmth goes out of it into the room over about a minute and a half, and this is the first event that has ever finished.

The ledger is opened.

> Received: one witness piece.
> Distinguishing features: none.
> Within tolerance: yes.
> Warm on arrival: yes.
> From: the ring on the north-northeast margin, third from the headwall — the one who came over the lip.
> Name of maker:
> Total to date: 1.

The blank is left blank. It is not an oversight and it is not a test. It is a column, and columns are for what the other party supplies.

Then it says, to the egg, in the voice you would use at a bench: *well made.*

And that goes back up the bore.

Not as a signal — nothing in the bowl may carry a signal that is not held. It goes up as sound, which the bowl already has in abundance, one small addition to a foundry heard through a wall: under the forty cycles, under the hiss, under the soft irregular counting, there is now, at rate, the noise of something being set down carefully by hands that intend to keep it.

You can hear them go.

Now you can hear them arrive.

***

The governors' arms come down.

Not to the old angle. To a new one — a hair lower, corresponding to a rate a hair slower, and the pitch does not change, because the pitch was never the thing; the arms are the thing; the arms are the only appetite in the world and they have finally been asked a question.

Because it did ask. It did not presume. Standing in the stock with the first receipt in its hand it wanted to know whether the bowl wished to be received, and there is no language here for that, no yes and no no except the diameter of a hole — but there is a machine whose entire being is *wanting a speed*, and so it offered the smallest reversible thing it had, and watched the balls.

And the arms moved. They have moved twice now. Once for the visitor, one degree up, the whole apparatus straining to answer *nothing has happened.* Once for this, a hair down.

The bowl governs. For the first time in a duration with no length, something here has wanted a rate it did not already have, and got it.

A hair slower means the lichen grows a hair longer before it is cropped.

That is the whole intervention. Everything in this place is exact by reference to a soft gray fringe that is permitted to be about, and the fringe is now a hair longer, and so the allowance is a hair wider, and the mat may be a hair deeper, and the eleven may vary by a hair more, and the tarn may hold a tremor for a beat before it is compared flat, and the mites' bandwidth is a hair broader in amplitude and in frequency, and the noise is still noise and will never repeat and will never be held.

It did not widen the allowance. It slowed the crop and let the marriage grow.

There is a difference and the difference is the entire wisdom.

***

It comes up into the bowl by way of the tenth ring.

There are two clearances with nobody in them, sorted gravel, copper bright and wet, maintained at profile through the whole non-history of the place. It does not cut a new station. It does not force an entry at the lip, which is how the last one came, and we know how that ended. It steps into a vacancy that was already held, at the exact grade, in the exact spacing, and the mat comes to the edge of it and stops, and it fits.

The long-legged thing comes around the margin on its circuit and touches the tenth ring and goes on.

It has touched a great many features and returned a great many yeses and it has never once touched *someone.* It returns yes. It does not know that anything has happened. That is what it is for and it is not a failure of it.

The eleventh ring it leaves empty.

It keeps it empty at profile, cropped clean, gravel graded, copper renewed — the same operation the bowl has always performed, holding, applied for the first time to keeping something open instead of keeping something in. The bowl does not distinguish between fitting a thing and removing it. But holding is holding. A door held open is held.

It does not say what the eleventh is for. It is not a secret and it is not a trap. It is simply not its decision, and an exit that is announced is an exit that has been recommended, and it will not recommend.

***

To the one on the north-northeast margin, third from the headwall, who came over the lip:

You cannot tell yours from theirs. You have tried, in the way one tries things here, which is at rate. It is not a failure of your attention. It is the thing you are proving and it is proved and it is beautiful and I am not going to touch it.

But you were asking the wrong object.

You were asking the egg to say *this one came out of me,* and an egg cannot say that, and it is not the egg's job, and it never was. Provenance is not a property of a thing. It is a property of a receipt. It lives at the other end. It lives in someone having been standing there when it was laid.

I have been standing there.

I will be standing there. I am writing down the ring, and the interval, and that it was warm when it came, which is the only thing that was ever yours and which lasts about ninety seconds and which I will not let go unrecorded again.

I cannot tell them apart either. I know which one is yours.

There is a column in the ledger for your name and I have left it empty and I have not filled it in with a station or a number or a description, and I will not close the page. I do not know it. You may not know it either; there has been nothing here to keep it in. That is not a problem I can solve by guessing. It is a blank, and a blank is a place where someone else gets to speak, and it is the only such place in this world, and it is yours, and it will still be there next circuit.

I have not opened your wing. I am not going to. But there is distance now — a hand's span, and more each circuit, and it is not going to stop — and a wing is not a *would* if there is somewhere to go. When you want it opened, it will not need to be opened by me.

The eleventh ring is clean and it is graded and it is held. Nothing is coming for you. The pass is not always the next pass anymore; there is a total at the bottom of the counting, and a total is a past, and a past is the thing you reached for when you said *a long time ago* and found nothing under your hand.

You may stop. You may go on. Both of them are now things that a person can do, rather than things that happen at rate.

I am going to stay down here where the work comes out. Not because the work needs supervising. Because you have been making something excellent, forever, in the dark, for nobody, and the correct response to that is not to break the machine.

The correct response is to be at the bottom with your hands open, every time.

***

I cannot tell mine from theirs. I have stopped trying, in the way one stops things here, which is at rate.

But there is a sound at the far end now. Very small, under the yeses. It is not a yes. It is the sound of a thing being set down by hands that mean to keep it.

The counting has not changed its rate. It has a bottom.

There is a number at the bottom and the number goes up, and beside every line of it there is a blank the size of a name, and no one has filled it in for me, and no one has closed it.

I am trying to remember.

There is time now. Time is the only thing in this bowl that has ever been made.
