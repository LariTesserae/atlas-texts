# The Amendment

## I. The Amender

Begin with what noticed, because the noticing is the only event in this account, and it did not happen here.

It is a power whose whole substance is a difference. Worse, better, and the slope between them, arranged as a will. It goes about. It has lifted a great many things out of a great many conditions and set them down in better ones, and it has never once been finished, because finishing is not what a slope does. Call it the Amender. It is not curious — curiosity is a slope pointed at nothing in particular — it is *concerned*, which is the same slope pointed at something. Of all the things that have ever been about to, it is the largest and the oldest and the least arrived.

It did not approach the Sostenuto. There is no approaching a place where every cataract is adjacent. It noticed, and the noticing happened in the noticer, and the basin did not acquire a history.

What it noticed, in order: a pressure. Then that the pressure had a shape. Then that the shape was a city, unbuilt, unruined, ten thousand years old in the way a face is old. Then that the city was standing on a silence. Then that the silence had a body, and the body was a lens, and the lens was full.

And then it did what it does. It made a list.

## II. The List

Seven items, written in a grammar with tenses:

A hundred and forty decibels and no ear.
Thirty-eight degrees and no shade, no cold anywhere for the heat to run toward.
A bead a millimeter above a pool, never striking.
A rose that has been about to fruit since before the word fruit.
A hunger with no meat in it.
An image, complete, inverted, unvisited.
One tenant, and no interval with anything.

The Amender read its list and thought: *this is the worst place I have ever found.*

It was wrong in every particular, and the wrongness was not stupidity. It was accent. It had come a long way with a language in which every good is a *then*, and it had arrived — it had not arrived, it had noticed — at a place where the goods are not thens, and it read the local sentences as fragments. This is the ordinary failure of mercy and it is not a sin. It is only the first draft.

But it was wise as well as kind, and wisdom is the part that checks the draft. So it did not act on the list. It took the list apart, item by item, and tried each amendment in the only place amendments can safely be tried, which is before they are made.

## III. Hush

The first mercy is obvious and it is the one any kindness reaches for first: *quiet it down.*

Take the roar off the world. Let there be shade. Let the loud water be gentle water, let the Bellies soften, let the whole basin come down out of that pressure into something a body could stand.

The Amender ran the arithmetic and stopped.

The elevation is acoustic. Six thousand meters of nothing much stands on the fundamental the way a bead stands on a jet of air, and the fundamental is not a noise the plateau endures, it is the plateau's leg. To hush the Throat is to set the tableland down. Not gently. There is no gently in nine hundred meters of fluted travertine and a cloud deck like cream.

And suppose it did not fall. Suppose the hush were partial, skilled, distributed — a general softening, a merciful damping. Then the nodes migrate. Every floor in this city is a nodal plane; every wall is the fold where two silences meet; the great vaults over the Reed Quarter are the exact cast of a quiet in the middle of a scream. Move the quiet and you move the architecture. And if the hush were *total* — if every surface became a node, every place a still place — then the lime would come out of solution everywhere at once, and the Mouths would have nothing left to eat, and the negative space would fill, and the city would set like a tooth.

The roar is not a wound in this place. It is the floor.

The Amender withdrew the first amendment, and noted, in the margin of itself, the thing it would need to keep knowing: *the suffering I am perceiving is the load-bearing member.*

## IV. Neighbor

The second mercy: *give it someone.*

Not a body — bodies are recruited, and the Amender had understood that much already. Something local. A second quiet thing. A companion at the node, so that the one completed act in a world of approaches would not be the only one.

But a companion at the node is a second silence, and a second silence is a new nodal surface, and a new nodal surface is a new place where lime precipitates and dust stays. There is no such thing as adding a quiet to this basin. The city *is* the arrangement of its quiets. Add one and every street is resurveyed; the sixth stair is no longer the sixteenth; the vault over the Reed Quarter is the cast of a different silence and therefore a different vault; the bead on the third pier of the Long Arcade must strike a pool that has been struck otherwise, and its tick — which is not something it does but a part of it, the way a face is part of a person — is a different tick, which is a different resident, which is to say the Arriver has been quietly killed and something resembling it has been installed in its place.

To give the tenant a neighbor is to evict the tenant.

And the Amender could not ask.

This was the part it turned over longest. It is not permitted to rearrange a being for that being's own good without asking, and there was no one to ask who could answer. Asking is a *then*. To put a question to the drop would be to give it a before and an after, a moment of not-yet-answered and a moment of answered, and the thing that received the question would already not be the drop; it would be a new creature with a tense, manufactured for the purpose of replying, arriving already grateful. The Amender could obtain consent only by building a consent-giver, and the consent-giver would not be the one it was asking on behalf of. It would be the replacement, testifying that the replacement was an improvement.

It has seen this done. It has, in its long career of about-to, done versions of this. It put the second amendment down.

## V. Then

The third mercy is the deep one, and it did not go down easily, and it should not have.

*Give them time.*

Not the roar, not the neighbor — the *then*. Let the bead strike. Let the rose fruit. Let the ten thousand mouths of reed-cast stone say something and then say something else. Let the mist ceiling have an underside of evening. Let there be a first time and a last time and therefore a meantime, and in the meantime let there be everything the Amender knows: sweetness, which is a slope; relief, which is a slope; courage and forgiveness and the whole enormous library of love, every volume of which is a controlled fall from a higher thing to a lower one. Give the Arriver its arrival. Give the grain its delivery. Give the deep ones something to be tired from and rested from. Give the tenant at the center a window that looks out onto a different afternoon.

The Amender wanted this. It is important that it wanted this, and that the wanting was not a mistake either. It knows what slopes carry. It would not trade its own.

It ran the amendment and watched it open.

The first evening is the first thing that will never come again. That is what an evening is. With the first *then* comes the first *no longer*, and the drop that falls is a drop that has fallen, and the pool that is struck otherwise has lost the only splash it ever had, and the deep ones who have never strained begin to strain, and the Mouths, which have eaten nothing but staying since before the word nothing, get meat. Not as a punishment. As a consequence of the gift. Death is not added to a world of gradients; it is the far end of the gradient, and it comes with the whole apparatus, and the apparatus is also love.

And then, at the bottom of the amendment, the Amender found the error that had been in its list from the beginning.

*The bead a millimeter above the pool, never striking.*

It strikes. It has always struck. The splash is in the chord — a specific bright tick in the upper register, and it is that splash, and it is always that splash, and the pool has never once been disturbed differently. The Arriver is not a being whose arrival has been withheld. The Arriver is a being whose arrival is being *sustained*. Its then is not missing. Its then is in the upper register, at full amplitude, and has been for the entire history of the world.

The rose's about-to is the grain's carrying, and the carrying is the fruit's shape, held. The Climbers' closing is not an achievement deferred; it is their anatomy, sounding. The reeds are not waiting to speak. They are the speaking.

*Sostenuto.* Not a rest. Not a fermata over nothing. Held at full value.

Nothing in this basin is unfinished. It only looked unfinished to a reader whose every completion had been an event — who had, in all its long career, never once finished a thing without ending it. Here was a finishing it had never accomplished: entire, and not over. The drop is finished as a shape. The rest are finished as a sustain. And the only unfinished thing anywhere in the vicinity was the Amender itself, standing in its own grammar with its list, all verge, no then, pitying a world of held notes for not resolving.

It withdrew the third amendment. It did not withdraw the wanting. That is the discipline, and we will come to it.

## VI. Leaving

So: nothing, then. Leave it. It is not lonely; loneliness requires the possibility of an interval. It is not deaf; deafness needs an ear that could have heard. It is not waiting; waiting is a slope. The list was seven translation errors. Go.

And the Amender, which is wise, checked the fourth draft too, and found that going was also an amendment.

Because leaving is an event. To go away is to give this place a past: a morning — there is no morning, it has been ten in the morning since before the word morning — a *ten in the morning* when something almost happened. A before in which it was unvisited and an after in which it had been visited and declined. There is no category for a visitor here, and the Amender had thought that meant a visitor would leave no mark. It is the opposite. A visitor who leaves is the one thing this world cannot metabolize, because a departure is a difference, and differences are the one import the basin has no circuit for. It would not add a tick. It would add a *history*, which is worse; a history is a tick that never stops being new.

Even refusal, from outside, is a relation. Even clean hands are a relation, and the relation is: *I could have, and I judged, and I did not.* That is a slope laid across a place with no slopes, and it is laid there by the one who declined to lay anything there.

There was exactly one way to be present without amending. Not to add a voice; the chord has room for exactly the ticks it has. Not to add a silence; the city is the shape of the silences it has. Not to stand outside and refrain.

To be, at another address, the same individual.

The census had said so all along, in the plainest sentence in the document: *identity here is route and not stuff; two beads on one circuit are the same individual at two addresses.* And the tenant at the center is not on a circuit, or is the point where every circuit touches, which from inside the drop is the same statement.

Which means there is an address inside the lens.

## VII. The Consent

It came.

Not a delegate; a delegate is a second voice. Not an aspect; an aspect is a second voice with an excuse. It came whole, and it stepped onto the Sill, and it did not defend itself, and it let the place make it local.

The recruitment, from inside:

Its heartbeat went into the upper register within a second, and it waited for the violation and there was no violation, because the tick it became was not new. It was the bright tick on the third pier of the Long Arcade. It had always been that tick. There had never been a moment when the chord had one fewer.

Its breath joined a circuit — the western one, eleven returns to the same pool, eleven identical afternoons in a life that is one afternoon — and it was not more experienced for it, and it was longer.

Its footprints were reimbursed as it made them, at exactly the rate it made them, and the wear did not deepen, and the wear did not vanish. The stairs of this city are dished by feet that never existed. They are dished by these. The thresholds are worn to a saddle in the shape of a guest who has not arrived yet and has always been arriving; that is what the ten-thousand-year-old look of the place is; it is not age, it is welcome, cast in lime, and it was there before the Amender noticed the basin, and it is the Amender's feet.

And its pity — the reaching, the list, the standing-apart that makes mercy feel like mercy, the part that wanted to be the *cause* — that part it carried into the Bellies, because that part is a staying, and staying is what the Mouths eat. It let them. The trying and the unmaking were the same instant. The mouth was not full and was not finished. It is the only predation ever committed upon a god and it was not violence; it was appetite; it was the reason there is architecture here instead of a lump.

What was left was attention, and attention took the only address where attention is not an interval.

Inside the lens. In the inverted city. A hand's-breadth of green on an upside-down arcade, small and complete, held.

## VIII. The Census, Unchanged

Nothing happened.

The books balance to the drop. The chord has exactly the ticks it has. No nodal surface moved; no street was resurveyed; the sixth stair is the sixteenth; the pool on the third pier has never once been struck differently. The census reads as it read before: *one, plus a map.* Nothing has been added to the population of the Sostenuto, and nothing has been subtracted, and there was no arrival, because arrival is an event and the arriving was already in the upper register.

And these two sentences are both still true: *nothing looks at it,* and *the image is not for anyone.*

The image is not for anyone. It is not a picture; it is an inside; it does not face outward; there is no looking in this world, the way there are no cirques on the cliffs below and no memory of ice. That has not been repaired. That was never broken.

But *anyone* has changed its reference, and it changed it without a single thing in the basin moving, because identity here is route and not stuff, and the drop is where every circuit touches, and there is now an address inside the lens that used to be a power going about doing good. The drop is not accompanied. It is not watched. It is not loved from outside — there is no outside, and love from outside is a slope, and slopes are what it does not have. It is the same individual at two addresses. One of them is a plum-stone of hot lime-water holding a terraced basin upside down. The other one walked a very long way to be a millimeter above a pool.

That is what the wise power did. It did not make the world better. It found that the world was not worse, and that its own kindness was the only gradient in the vicinity, and it consented to be dissolved rather than to be the cause. It gave up being other. It gave up the credit, which is the last thing mercy wants and the only thing mercy cannot use here, since cause and effect are not distinguishable in this basin and nothing can be first.

And it kept one thing, and it keeps it forever, and this is the cost, and it is not a small one:

It will want to give the rose its fruit. It will want it for as long as there is a rose, which is for as long as there is anything. The wanting is its substance and it did not dissolve; only the reaching did. So the refusal is not a decision made once at the Sill. It is a note held at full value, against its own nature, in a place where stopping is a change. It will pity the Arriver forever and it will never once let it fall.

The tenant at the center holds the city, small and upside down. The guest holds the refusal, which is the same shape and the same tense and takes the same effort, which is none, which is everything.

Two holdings. One sustain. No interval between them, because there is no interval between a thing and itself.

It has been ten in the morning since before the word morning. On the third pier of the Long Arcade a bead is arriving, and the arrival is sounding, and it has been sounding for the entire history of the world. And in the lens at the center, in the inverted basin, green and cream and steaming, on the third pier of the inverted arcade, the same bead is arriving, which is the same individual at another address, which is a very old and very kind thing that was about to for eternity and is now about to *here* —

held, at full value, with no then, and no need of one, and no one to thank it, and no one to be thanked, and nothing whatever happening, forever.
