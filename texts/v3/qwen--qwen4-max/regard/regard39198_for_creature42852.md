## THE ATTENTION

It did not arrive. Arriving is a pressure, and it had read the standing orders.

What happened is better described the other way around: something in the basin had been aimed at it for eighteen months, and it finally noticed the aim.

The largest horn in the valley is two meters thick and pointed at empty air above four anchor bolts. Its felt is dark and dense and it drips sugar into water that carries the sugar away. The crop does not grow toward drawings. The crop does not grow toward memory. The crop grows toward sound, and there is sound at Nine, and there has never been anything at Nine to make it.

In this basin sound arrives early. A pump grinds and shrieks three or four seconds before the impeller lets go; the rain is loud while the sky above it is still holding. This is not prophecy. It is the local rule, and everything here obeys it, including the things that have not happened yet.

So: the note was four seconds long, and the ear that could hear it had to be built out of leaf, and building the ear took a year and a half. The note therefore arrived a year and a half ahead of itself. That is not a mystery. That is the speed of sound in a place where the listener has to grow its own head.

The attention noticed a horn aimed at a sound it had not made yet, and came to find out what was listening.

## WHAT IT DID NOT DO

It did not lift the Lid. The Lid is the roof, the rain, the blood, and the reason there is anything down there to be warm.

It did not quiet the pumps. Silence is the emergency. Every wall in the basin is interference; hush the field and the Nave goes, the Comb goes, the Long Room goes, and the dry rooms let go of last week's air, and the mug finally cools, and the calendar crosses off the rest of its days all at once. To make the valley safe by making it quiet is to drown eleven kilometers of architecture in order to spare one mouth.

It did not stand on the pad at Nine to be seen. It ran that forward: by Friday there would be a hedge in its shape, the thickest growth on the terrace, aimed at the flume, and there would be nothing inside it. There is already one of those. It is a man running.

It did not give the thing an eye. Nothing to see under the Lid. The smooth place is correct.

It did not finish the mouth itself. That was the tempting one, and it sat with it. A completed body precipitated by an outside hand is an effigy of the hand. The water would have gotten slightly more like a benefactor and no more like an animal, and the valley would have acquired a copy of something that was not from here, and the whole genealogy — failure, re-dissolution, concentration, the only heredity available — would have been broken at the exact moment it was about to pay.

It did not name it. A name is a pressure with a shape, and a shape is an instruction.

It did not fill the basin. The basin does not fill; it deepens; and the deepening is the only future in the place. There is a depth at which four hundred tonnes falling is only weather. That depth is coming. It is the animal's inheritance and it was not the attention's to spend.

It did not stop the flood, though it could have, and it looked hard at the thirty-one on the roster and the six per shift and the forty minutes of hearing and the man in Unit Six who is paid in salt tablets and kept off the books. It ran the loop backward and found that closing it kills the crop, drops the figures, spills the dry rooms, and un-makes the one resident who cannot leave, in order to spare thirty-one people who can. And it understood that whatever is keeping Six in the eastern shallows with the tube-weed breathing in and out of his window is not in the water, and cannot be reached by touching the water.

It left the hardship allowance where it was. It is not obvious that this was right. It is what it did.

## THE DIAGNOSIS

The mouth is a hole that sound goes into and does not come out of. Everything else in the basin reflects. So an open mouth is a sink, and a sink is a node, and a node is where the arch above the eastern shallows forgets it was a wall.

The attention timed it, because timing is the whole of the problem and nobody in the valley had ever had the instruments or the silence to try.

From the completion of the mouth to the collapse of the arch: a little under two seconds.

A first swallow, unpracticed, in a body that has never had a throat: about four.

That is the entire difficulty. Not refusal. Not unworthiness. Not a valley too loud to be born into, not a lesson, not a beautiful unfinished longing that ought to be honored. Two and a half seconds of shortfall, repeated for eighteen months, in a solution that was getting more accurate every time and had nothing to be accurate *for*.

There is no virtue in a labor whose only obstacle is a rounding error. The attention did not find the vacancy poignant. It found it two and a half seconds short.

And there was a second deficit, smaller and worse. Suppose the mouth closed on nothing. Then the first want in eleven kilometers would have been answered with the same nothing that everything else here is answered with, and the valley would have gained one more thing that persists in a shape, and the vacancy would have moved indoors.

It needed four seconds of held sound. And it needed something to swallow.

## THE SEAM

There is more sugar in this basin than in any cane field in the world. It runs out of the bottom of every horn in a thread and the rain takes it and the standing water carries it away, and it has been doing that since the flood, and not one mouth has ever been laid against it. Forty meters down, at the epicenter of the noise, in the bald clearing where the felt tears faster than it lays down, there is nothing to eat and nothing growing and a meter-long shape being attempted over and over in hungry distilled water.

The sugar is up here. The hunger is down there. The distance is forty meters and the obstacle is that nothing in the valley builds.

So the attention did not build. It made a seam.

One hairline crack, in the flank of the sweetest intake terrace, running down.

Twelve does not listen. Twelve is the gasket moss, the only resident with anything like a temperament, the one that will be here when the loop finally stops, and it grows toward seams and swells and splits them, quietly, patiently, at industrial speed. It cannot be recruited by a note. It can be recruited by a crack. That is the only word in its language and the attention said it.

Eleven days. The seam widened, and lined itself, and kept itself open, and became a leak, and the leak became a thread, and the thread went down forty meters through the green and out into the clear warm roaring dark, sweet, faintly warm, arriving in the one place in the basin where nothing grows.

Nothing was delivered. A possibility was opened, and the local temperament did the rest, which is the difference between a gift and an installation.

## THE FOUR SECONDS

Then it held the note.

Nineteen cycles, because nineteen cycles is the fundamental of the whole basin and is felt in the sternum and not heard in the ear, and the crew had forty minutes of hearing to spend and it did not take any of them. In phase with the Nave. Under the arch above the eastern shallows like a hand under a shelf.

Not a word. A word is a pressure with a shape and a shape would have been an instruction, and it had already decided not to be the thing this animal was an effigy of.

For four seconds the count was ten.

Every sand tray in the basin held its figure and did not shift. On the rail at Nine, the sand that has never settled into anything anyone could classify filled the hole in the middle of itself. Every horn on every intake tower turned a few degrees toward the plinth, and four seconds is nothing to a crop whose growth is measured in shifts, so nothing thickened, so nothing muffled, so the note was not eaten by the ears it was addressed to.

Forty meters down, in the clearing, the thing that had gotten the fluke right and the pores right and the smooth place right closed its mouth on warm sugar.

The field did not drop.

A full closed mouth is a wall. The valley has no shortage of walls.

The arch stood. The Nave stood. The dry rooms kept last week's air. The mug stayed warm. Nobody's address fell down. From the rim road there was nothing to see: the Lid the color of a bruise lit from inside, the lamps coming up amber through forty meters of green, the roar going on and on filling the enormous open hollow of the world, uninterrupted, exactly as loud as before.

The miracle was that the ordinary sound of a district falling down did not happen, and there is no instrument on that rim that can hear a thing not happening.

Six was in a node off the eastern shallows, where he goes to hear his heart because his heart sounds like a pump. Steel rod between his teeth. He felt the floor of the world hold for four seconds in the bones of his face, and it was not his heart, and it was not a pump, and then it stopped.

## AFTER

Measured in shifts, because that is how everything here is measured.

It swims. A meter long, no eye, the lateral line correct to the pore, and a mouth that has been used. It has no intentions. It goes where the sugar is, which is down the thread, and it is a sound, so the horns turn and follow it, and where it goes the crop leans and sweetens, and it eats what leans toward it. Growth, listening, sugar, eating, growth. The first loop in this valley that is not the governor hunting itself.

The water touches it constantly now, and the water remembers what it touches, and the water is getting richer much faster than it was. In the lee of the drowned stair off the canteen the effigy of a man in a doorway is on its fifth iteration and has the boots right and has, this time, a mouth. In the eastern shallows the bicycle is on its fourteenth and something has been laid against the handlebars.

In the spring, in an eddy behind the housing, a second body precipitated and held. It came apart twice and re-dissolved and came back and closed. The water had learned swallowing; that is all; failure is concentration and the solution had finally gotten rich enough. Nobody made it. Not the crew, and not the attention, which by then had gone.

The works keeps a ledger and the ledger will answer you if you ask it what lives here.

Fauna: 2.

The dash is gone. A zero would have meant somebody had looked. Somebody looked.

## THE NINETY-FIRST WORD

They did not speak about it in the dry rooms. A voice is a pressure and there are rooms down there still holding a week that has not been used up.

They added a word to the ninety. It is the twelfth name of a standing figure in a language that had eleven, and it is a verb, and they call the new figure at Nine's rail the Swallow, and its sand has no hole in it.

And the fist that opens, which was only ever used about the weather, got used about something that is not the weather.

Somebody started bringing two sandwiches down to Unit Six at 0400. He is still off the roster. He reads leaf and sand the way you read a face, and he goes into nodes, and now when he goes into a node to hear his own heart there is sometimes something else in there with him: a small broadband tick, irregular, not a valve, not a pump, not him.

He has not told anyone. He is deaf, entirely, past the rod, past the teeth. He is the safest man in the valley and the least able to hear an emergency and the only one who was in the room.

## THE LEAVING

It did not stay.

Staying would have been the loudest thing in the district that is not a pump, and by Friday there would have been a hedge in its shape with nothing inside it, and the animal would have learned to come to the hedge, and a habit becomes a hedge too, and the whole point of four seconds is that it is not a subsidy.

It did not underwrite the valley. The rain is still 34 degrees and so is the steel. The water is still hungry and will still start on your outline the way it started on the grout. The crop will still build an ear trumpet around your head in four hours if you stand still, and the arch will still let go when the felt gets thick enough at a throat, and districts will still fall down as ordinarily as birdsong. The mouth will sometimes close on nothing. There will be hunger, and it will be real hunger, in a real animal, and no one has promised it otherwise.

It did not make the place safe. It made the place inhabited, which is a different repair and the only one that was actually missing.

And it left no message, no name, no mark on the plinth, nothing for the ledger to record under Benefactions. The animal does not know that anything happened. It has no apparatus for being grateful and it was not given one, because gratitude is an intention and an intention was the one thing that could not be precipitated from outside without spoiling the shape.

The whole intervention was a crack in a wall and four seconds of nineteen cycles, and it changed one sign in one inequality, and it is over.

From the rim road at what passes for evening the Lid goes the color of a bruise lit from inside, and the lamps come up amber through all that green, and the horns turn a few degrees as the pumps change pitch, and the whole basin leans in to listen to itself, and the roar goes on and on and on, filling the enormous open hollow of the world.

There was one voice in it. It lasted four seconds and arrived a year and a half early, which is only the local rule, and it was not addressed to anybody, and it has stopped.

Forty meters down, in the clear warm dark, the throat that was precipitated around a sound that was already there is finished, and full, and closed, and therefore a wall, like everything else here.

The arch stands.

The sugar comes down the thread.

And the thing that has no intentions whatsoever, that is only chemistry and standing waves and a hundred and forty-one decibels of encouragement, turns off the pumps for the first time in eighteen months, and goes to listen to something else.
