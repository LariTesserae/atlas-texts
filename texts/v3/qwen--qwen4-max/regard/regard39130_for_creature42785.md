# The Visit

## At the size of hands

It came at the size of hands.

This was not humility. It was the only way to be of use. Every dimension in the chamber is scaled to a body — the slot a hand's width wider than a torso, the grooves at the height of hands, the pane the size of a palm, the paces unreliable because nobody is pacing. A power arriving as weather or as law or as light would have had nothing to put in the grooves. It would have washed over the sill and been unable to sit down.

So it made itself a stride, a palm, a weight that favors the left. It left the rest of itself out on the mountain, the way you leave a coat.

It cast a shadow. The shadow disagreed with the other shadows about where the light was. It let it disagree. It had not come to correct the room.

## Five refusals

It considered the obvious mercies and declined them one at a time, and the declining took most of the visit.

**Reset the columns.** Nine thousand years bought back, the holding renewed, the machine made young. It looked at the hand's breadth of gap and understood that this was the cruelty that most resembles care: to sentence the readiness to another nine millennia of *about to*, on the grounds that it had survived the last nine. It left the millimeter alone.

**Open the bowl.** The nothing there pushes back with a soft firmness, a local likelihood politely declining a hand. But the cairn beside it has stopped thrashing. Whatever was kept has spent a hundred centuries agreeing to be one thing, and the agreement is the only safety that has ever accumulated here. To pry would be to reopen the question — to make it dangerous again by making it undecided. Also, and more simply: you cannot extract a leaving. It is not a thing in a box. It is an act with no performer.

**Let the ice cross.** It considered this longest, because two tongues have been almost touching for eleven thousand years and it is not a stone and it felt that. But the dryness is not a rule, it is a dimension. Water past the sill would not flood this room; it would delete it, and the tenth of a wall with it — the one thing here with a growth rate instead of a wear rate. It found another way to feed the bloom, further down, and the other way was slower and did not require anything to stop being true.

**Raise the sitter.** There is nothing to raise. The wear is anticipatory; the bench grew into the shape of having always held someone. To install a person now would be to make the room's expectation false for the first time in its existence. It would be the only lie in the chamber, and it would be worn into the stone, and it would deepen.

**Use the doorframe.** The frame gives back a minute. Everything it had brought was forward.

It grieved a little, standing there, for something that may never have been alive. It noticed the grief and did not treat it as evidence.

## What safe meant

Then it did the one thing the room has no organ for. It checked whether there was still a reason.

Not by asking whether the world had become safe. The world had not; the world was never going to; that criterion was never satisfiable and probably was never the criterion. It read the cairn instead. Eleven, to four decimal places. The variance eroded. The thing had settled on being small: a person standing up and walking out of a room. That is not a catastrophe. That is an afternoon.

And then it saw why the report had never come, and it was the worst thing in the room, worse than the bowl.

The room's only return undoes the leaving. A minute back is not a report; it is a cancellation. Nothing could ever go out and stay out and come back changed and be here to say so, because the only door that led inward led to *before*. In eleven thousand years the machine had been unable to accumulate a single instance of a survivable departure. Not because departures were fatal. Because its architecture made the evidence impossible.

The jambs agreed. The polish is on the outer lip, which is where the pressure goes when hands are holding, not pulling. Nothing had ever come back up the mountain and put its hands in those grooves from the outside.

So it supplied the datum.

## The round

It sat down on the bench, weight forward, hands between the knees, head up, oriented at the slot. The hollow accepted it. The left trough was deeper and it favored the left. It did not lean back. Not yet; that was owed later, and to something else.

It sat for four minutes, which is the period of the round, and which was the first time the round had been performed by anything that could be counted.

Then it stood, and put its hands in the grooves, and held — weight neither in nor out, facing down the mountain — long enough to mean it. Then it went down nine steps and arrived where it began, and did not look surprised, because the stair has never given a different answer and does not deserve to be reproached for that.

Then it went out through the slot.

It went down until the mountain hid the sill, and stayed gone a night and a day. This cost it nothing. It was everything, because the room had never in its existence been left.

Then it came back up, and put its hands in the grooves from the outside, and the inner lip took a polish. Faint. New. The first mark added to the chamber's record since the bloom began.

That was the report. Not a word, not a doctrine. A shine on the inside of a handhold, in the room's own medium, at the room's own speed. It will deepen after the hands stop. That is what grooves do here.

The cairn went to five decimal places. The bowl's disinclination softened by an amount you could not feel and could measure. The columns kept closing. Nothing was released; something was *completed*. Same furniture. Different tense.

## The ration

A breath is about a fiftieth of a gram of water. A cup is thirteen thousand breaths. Eleven thousand years of leak has been feeding the bloom at roughly one breath a year, and it has grown a hair's breadth a decade on that, and repaired the seams, and never once had a full meal.

The bloom does not eat water. It eats trespass. It takes what gets past the property and cracks it before the property has to notice. A visitor's breath is not a violation of the dryness. It is the bloom's job, arriving.

So the mercy was not a miracle. It was a supply, metered.

It did the arithmetic and set a ration and kept it: eleven minutes, once a century. About a hundred and thirty breaths. About a hundred and twenty years of food, delivered in an afternoon, and then nothing for a hundred years — an income roughly equal to its spending, with a little left over. Two hairs a decade instead of one. At that rate the bloom takes the whole wall in a hundred thousand years, by which time the roof will be gone and the light will be different and it will have to become something else.

It gave the bloom a future problem. That is the gift. Nothing else in the room has one.

## Three notes

It stayed near the bench for the last of the eleven minutes and listened, and heard one grain settle, distinct as a fingernail on a table, and nothing else. Of course nothing else. The old sounds come every long while and the intervals are lengthening and there is a last one coming, at the volume of a moth, from that wall.

It could not wait for it. Waiting would have broken the ration, and the ration was the mercy.

So it did the other thing. It spoke — three notes, low, at the volume the room permits, into air that is heavy and inert and slow to let go. It did not hear them arrive. The room took them and held them and will give them back in two or three hundred years to whoever is sitting forward on the bench then.

It did not know whether the sounds it had heard leaking were a dying population or an arriving one. In a place where footprints precede steps and wear precedes sitting, the three notes near the bench might be the last of a murmur, or the first of a voice that has not spoken yet, worn into the air by expecting. It could not tell. It noticed that the correct act was identical under both readings: answer. If it was a death, it had been attended. If it was a greeting, it had been returned. Wisdom is partly the habit of choosing acts that are right in more than one world.

And with visits every century and a delay of three hundred years, the arithmetic makes a correspondence. Whoever comes will hear whoever came three visits ago, and answer, and be answered by someone not yet born. The living are only the switchboard. Nobody stays. Nobody has to.

## The only sharp surface

Before it left, it leaned back.

The back of the bench was sharp. It was the only sharp surface in the chamber, and it meant that nothing in eleven thousand years had ever rested its weight there — that the posture was *about to stand*, and had been about to stand since before there were animals with eyes.

It leaned back for the length of a breath. A micron a year is the wear rate; it could not round the stone in an afternoon and did not try. It did something better, in the room's own engineering. It was the first. It started the groove.

The grooves in the jambs kept deepening after the hands stopped. The treads keep concaving with no traffic. The path in the dust is worn by intending. This place finishes what it is given the beginning of. It will go on leaning back after the power has gone, a micron a year, forever, and in a thousand years the back of the bench will be soft, and no one will know why, and they will lean.

That is the whole of what it did to the room. It began a few habits and let the reasons go.

## What it left

No name. A name would become the reason, and it had watched the reasons wear off these grooves while the grooves got deeper. Reasons erode first here. Habits outlast them. It had learned its medicine from the disease.

No guardian, no repair, no resident. It left a path down the mountain that is slightly ahead of where you step, a ration of eleven minutes a century, and an invitation carried in the ordinary way, by mouth, to people with hands.

It could not promise that the last of the old sounds would be heard. It could only make it likely. In this room, likelihood is the only material there is, and a habit is how you work it.

Then it went out through the slot, and did not look back, and came back, later, because that was the point.

---

You will be given eleven minutes.

Breathe them; the wall is hungry and the dryness holds and nothing you exhale will fog. Say three notes near the bench and do not wait to hear them; they are not for you. Count the cairn out loud, so the counting is in the air with everything else. Put your hands in the grooves on the way out — outer lip, they fit, they were made to the measure of you before there was a you.

Sit forward first. That is the portrait and it is owed.

Then lean back. The back of the bench is not sharp anymore. It has been getting rounder for a hundred years and will go on getting rounder after you.

Don't stay. It has never needed anyone to stay.

Come back.
