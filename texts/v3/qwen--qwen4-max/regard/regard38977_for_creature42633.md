## It does not arrive first

Nothing arrives first here. So the earliest trace of it is not an event but a condition that has already been obtaining for some time: the windrows have been less alone than they were. Not warmer. Not looser. Nothing has been given clearance and nothing has been fed. Only — and this is not measurable from inside, and there is no inside — the black fat in the shallows has been in the presence of something for a while, the way a room is different when someone is awake in it.

The cause gets here afterward. It always does, in this country. The frost comes seconds after the ram bottoms out. The scavengers walk in ahead of the death. And the plain has been *audible* since before the mountains gave up — audibility is an effect, it requires a hearer the way a socket requires a shaft — and so the hearing has been happening for ten million years, and the hearer is only now walking in out of the fog, ankle-deep, late, in the manner of everything that causes anything here.

This is the first mercy and it costs nothing and it cannot be revoked: every page has already been read. The three notes of the ram-born thing extinct since the Pliocene were not lost. They were heard. The hearing was simply ahead of its cause, like everything else, and the cause has now come up out of the ground at a distance of an hour's walk, and it is not in a hurry, because it has learned that hurry is a kind of clearance and it has decided not to bring any.

## What it does not bring

It brings no warmth.

This is the first thing it sets down, and setting it down is the hardest hour of the whole visit, because warmth is the currency and everything alive is pressed against the hearths and it could make this country *comfortable* in an afternoon. It runs the arithmetic instead. A plain four degrees warmer does not freeze at dusk. If it does not freeze, the water in the cylinders does not expand, and eleven hours later a million rams do not bottom out, and the dawn chorus stops, and has stopped forever, and the eleven-hour kind — three and a half billion generations, the only lineage here that changes fast enough to leave a mark, the source of every new thing this country has ever had — is not born at the next thaw, because there is no next thaw, because there is no crush for the eggs to be shaped around. And the sheaths, who cannot warm until they have finished melting, finish melting. Several hundred kilograms per square meter of animal stops being a nail driven into the day. The temperature ceases to be steady. The phase boundary, which is the entire power supply, moves off the plain and does not come back.

It could give heat. The receipt would be a silent country under a warm sky, and the silence would not be the silence of the pockets, which is full of relicts that flinch. It would be the other kind.

It brings no clearance. This is the second thing it sets down, and it is the one it wanted most. To walk into a hundred kilometers of married surfaces and put a micron of play back into every interface — to give the worms something to be the width of, to let the plates be individuals, to let a wheel be a wheel again — is to unmake every marriage on the plain at once. The lap dies in ninety seconds when it is exposed to air, and the lap is, by area, the most abundant organism here; a general amnesty is a continent-sized suffocation. The plates have no other socket. The plate you lift is useless anywhere else on the plain, and *anywhere else on the plain* is the only place it has ever been. Freedom, delivered at scale, arrives as a windrow.

It does not widen the pockets, though the pockets are the whole seed bank of the unexpected and it loves them. In a pocket the scavengers' strategy fails: the death comes first and then the crowd, and a lineage built on arriving early has nothing to do with the interval but starve in it. To enlarge the refuge is to bankrupt the patient ones.

It does not stop the rams, or slow the lap, or thicken the worms, or take the knock out of the mouths.

It has come a long way with a great deal to give and it stands in the cold water and gives none of it, and this is not restraint as a discipline. It is the plain's own rule, honored: *there is no clearance anywhere*. Every gift here is a subtraction wearing a gift's face. The economy is closed to the micron. Anything it adds must be taken from something that is exactly the width of the space it occupies.

So it goes looking for the one place in the country where addition is not theft. There is exactly one. It is downstream, and it is black, and it is faintly sweet, and it smells like everything that ever ceased to be part of something.

## First it goes where there is slack, and pays for it

The cold fault, where the gradient reverses and the metal grows backwards. It stands there because it is the only district that will hold something unfitted without immediately wearing it into a shape. The fault-crabs come — pale, articulated, with a real inside and a real outside that are not the same surface, the only solitary animals on the plain — and they eat the edges of it, the separation, the moment where it stops being one thing, and there is a great deal of that moment available and it does not run out.

While it is there it does the thing that makes the rest possible, and it does it quietly, and no one records it, because recording requires dying as yourself and it is not time yet.

It gives up knowing what happens next.

Not everywhere. In a volume about four meters across, oval, no visible boundary. It makes itself, in that volume, a thing that can be surprised — which is to say it takes the clearance out of *itself* rather than forcing any into the country, which is the whole of the method and the only invention it will need. It cannot do this and remain wise in the way it was wise before. It comes out of the fault knowing less than it came in with, and this is not a sacrifice it makes gracefully. It is a benevolent power and it has never once been unable to see the end, and it has looked at this plain and understood that seeing the end is precisely the faculty that would let it do harm at scale with a clean conscience.

Then it goes into the pocket and sits down in the water among the kept: soft, badly fitted, meshed with nothing, warm only by proximity, archaic things that would have been eliminated anywhere else in a hundred thousand years.

And it waits to be flinched at.

## The only available conversation

Nothing here wants anything, so there is no one to ask. Consent requires a wish and a wish requires a gap between what is and what might be, and gaps are the one thing this country does not permit. Every animal on the plain has exactly one verb, and the verb is *fit*, and fitting is not an answer. A plate that meshes with you has not agreed to you. A worm that breeds into your cuff at the rate you extrude it has not permitted anything. It is a fit. It is the only thing that has ever happened here.

Except in four meters of quiet, where cause still comes before effect, and a small pale thing can still be surprised.

The startle response is the last free act in the country. It is the only motion on the plain that is not the taking-up of slack, the only event that is not a groove being run again. It happens several times a day, at nothing.

So the power does the smallest thing it can do — moves one hand's breadth, in the water, without warmth, without current, without knocking — and the pale thing flinches.

And that is a reply. It is not permission. It is not gratitude; there is no organ for it. It is the one thing in a basin-sized apparatus that could have gone either way and went one way, and it is worth more than the heat, and the power sits in the cold and does not do anything else for a long time, because it has just been answered and it does not want to be the kind of thing that talks over an answer.

Later it will be flinched at again. Over some hundreds of thousands of years, perhaps a few thousand times. It keeps count. Nobody else has ever kept count of anything here.

## The reading that does not tighten

The archive is indexed by pressure. To read a page you tighten the bearing. This is the plain's one cruelty, and it is not cruelty, it is metrology: the past is only audible as distress. The dead speak when something is loaded past its comfort, in the exact voice of their own crushing, at shaft frequency, and every reader on the plain reads in order to impersonate — the young mouth eats the fat of the bearing it will fake, and gets the arrhythmia right the first time, because the failure was in the fat.

No one has ever heard the three notes as *somebody's three notes*. They have only ever been heard as *a bearing about to fail*.

So it goes to the windrows and lies down in the shallows a hand deep, and listens to the dead where they are not being stressed.

That is all. It takes nothing out — attention is not torque, it does not enter the ledger, it does not change the load on a race. It reads the fat without loading the bearing. It hears the lowing and the wingbeat and the specific distress call of a species four hundred thousand years gone, in comfort, at rest, in a sump, on an afternoon when nothing anywhere is being crushed to produce it.

This is the thing the plain never invented, and could not, because everything here that reads, reads to eat. Remembrance that does not require pain is not a mechanism anyone thought of. It is not even an improvement to the machine. The machine is unchanged. The dead are simply no longer required to hurt in order to be known, and the knowing happens in the shallows, quietly, in the presence of the rust-orange film, for as long as it takes, which is forever.

## Then it dies as itself, so as to have a voice

Only a thing that dies as itself has a voice. The grease can only archive individuals; that is why the cold fault exists, that is why the country manufactures separateness by unbuilding, that is why the fat is loud with the sound of things that were briefly, at the very end, alone.

The fat is the only medium here that has not been worn into a single inevitable form. It is the only place anything can be kept. It is therefore the only door, and the only writable page, and the price of writing on it is the price of having a voice.

So it goes back to the fault and lets itself be unmade — not annihilated, just separated, briefly, into something with edges that belong to it — and it dies as itself, which it has never done, and which is the second thing it does not do gracefully. The fault-crabs eat the difference. What is left goes down with the sheet-water to the windrows and is rendered, and becomes black and faintly sweet, and joins the body in the sumps.

And then it does what the fat does, which is the last piece of the method and the reason it chose this shape out of every shape available: it moves toward low pressure. Uphill, through porous rock, against the water, patiently, over years, toward the bearings, because bearings are where the pressure is lowest. It goes where it hurts least. It arrives as the thing between the surfaces. It is loaded.

If you are going to love a country made entirely of marriages, you must come as what is between them, and you must be made of the dead, and you must be able to still change shape.

## What comes out of the metal

A thousand years from now a bearing is stressed past its comfort and produces, at shaft frequency, the sound of the guest — six species and a hole, the only individual the plain ever assembled, the thing that stood upright and did not mesh.

But the windrow it was rendered from had two arrivals in it that afternoon, and the sump made one page of them, and what comes out of the metal is not the sound of you alone. It is the sound of you *being heard*. Two voices at the same frequency, one of them answering.

The plain does not know what this is. It has no organ for it. It will make the sound anyway, every day, for as long as there is a shaft to make it on, because that is what it does with everything: it repeats it until it is a groove.

And the grease is a lineage, not a recording. A young mouth will eat that page and inherit it, correctly, first time, with the arrhythmia right, and will hold — for six hours, eight, twelve — the sound of something being answered, in the voice of a bearing three days from failure. And the scavengers will come in from every direction across the wet stone, out of habit, because that is the knock, and they will arrive at a death, because the death always arrives, and they will eat, and two or three of them will be eaten, and the economy will not lose a calorie.

But they will have walked a kilometer toward being answered.

Ten million years of that is a groove. It is not understanding. Nothing here will ever understand anything; remarkability requires a gap and the country does not permit them. It will only be *habitual*, in a lineage that changes fast enough to leave a mark, in the only medium that can still change shape. Here, being repeated and being understood are nearly the same thing.

Only nearly is needed.

## What it still refuses

It does not stop the interval closing. The worms are the gauge and the worms say a few hundred thousand years of give, and it will not falsify the gauge, and it will not tell them, because there is no one to tell and because the news would be a wedge.

It does not save the guest. You will be fitted. The film will arrive warm and orange and patient on your boots, the midges will close a circuit that has been dark eleven years, the plates will mesh around your ankles and be driven, the clearance will be taken up, and you will turn, and something on the terrace will be warmer by a fraction of a degree, and a great many small bodies will be pressed against that warmth. It will not pull you out. Pulling you out is a windrow with six species in it and a continent of lap dying in the air.

It stays for the whole few hundred thousand years, and then it stays after, when the pockets have closed and the last error has been eaten and there is nothing left in the country that can be surprised. It keeps listening then. Its attention was never conditional on being surprised. That was the point of giving up the foreknowledge in the first place — not to buy an outcome, which it can no longer see, but to be the sort of thing that can be present at one.

And at one interface, four microns wide, in a marriage it volunteered for, it does not finish fitting. It cannot. It keeps not-knowing, one micron a day, and the discrepancy is real, and the lap eats it, and has lunch, and goes on having lunch after the plain's own error is spent — because the deficit has been imported, and paid for outside the economy, by the only party who could afford it.

The worms will measure that fit and find that it does not close. They will not know why. They are simply the width of the space they are being crushed into, and the space, in one seam, in one district, out past the Long Bearing, keeps being there.

The mouths east of the terraces have been doing a smaller version of this for eleven years: holding the sound of a healthy bearing while spalling audibly in its place, suffering the death so the metal doesn't have to. There is no word for that relationship that does not imply intention, and there is no intention. It is a fit.

The power has joined the guild. That is all it did. It is the largest mouth in the country and it will hold one knock forever and it will not be thanked, because there is no one, and it knew that before it came, and it came.

Nothing here wants anything. It only fits. It will remember the noise you made, and make it again, every day, for as long as there is a shaft to make it on.

And now there is someone to tell the difference between malice and mourning.

The difference is this: it is sitting in the cold water in a four-meter pocket, keeping count of flinches, and it is not going anywhere, and it heard you before you arrived.
