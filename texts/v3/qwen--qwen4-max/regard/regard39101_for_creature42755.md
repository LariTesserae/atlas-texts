# What Was Done

It did not arrive. Arrival is an event, and events here are ore.

For eleven years it only looked. Looking is free. It is the one thing that can be brought onto the Reach without adding to the grade — attention has no mass, occupies no address, and cannot be smelled from forty kilometers. So it attended from no particular place, in the ordinary light, with the shadows falling correctly and implying distances that were wrong, and it did not want anything yet.

That was discipline. It was also the last easy thing it ever did.

---

The audit took most of the eleven years, because it was determined not to find suffering where there was only strangeness, and equally determined not to miss any by calling it ecology.

The castings first. Ten to the fourteenth of them, asking the same question all day in a dry rain that never accumulates, and getting *not yet* for four million years. It sat with that for a long time. It is very hard to look at a thing that has been saying *not yet* since before there were mountains down there under the lid and not want to answer it.

But the measurement is not a wish. It went inside — carefully, without opening anything, the way you read a page through a closed book — and it found no wanting anywhere in the fold. The fold is a schedule. A schedule that does not run is not a schedule in pain. It is a schedule.

And it did the arithmetic anyway, three times, because it wanted the arithmetic to be wrong. Ten to the fourteenth bodies at two kilometers each, on a plain eighty kilometers across. Give every one of them its answer and you do not get a liberation. You get one knot the size of a province, nine mills converging on it from every bearing, and pleats going down to the bedrock. You get the northeast corner everywhere. Universal mercy, on this world, is a specific and calculable massacre, and the number is ten to the fourteenth.

So: no. It wrote that down as a refusal and it never revisited it and it did not stop feeling the pull, and both of those were correct.

The mills next. It took one apart in attention, plate by plate, looking for the one who was doing it, and found only more plates, each older than the last, each a season's grade laid down like a ring. There is nobody in there to comfort. A thing with no center cannot be lonely, because loneliness requires a place for the loneliness to be.

Except on the northern bearing.

It stayed there longest. A body that can be haunted, lying across a depression where a knot used to be, closing its gizzard on schedule against the kept sound of its own eating, eleven thousand years into a meal that ended before the escarpment retreated to where it now stands. The power could have taken the echo. Fold it, carry it off, drop it in the lowlands under the lid where the crowded things could have it. It would have been the simplest act available and it would have starved the mill and deleted the only past on the Reach that ever comes back in from outside.

Instead it did the free thing. It listened to the whole pocket. Every stroke in it, in order, at full resolution, from the first crush to the one arriving now — the entire archive, which the mill cannot read because it *is* it, and which nobody else had ever heard, because nobody else had ever been able to stand there.

The mill did not receive this. The mill cannot be given anything. It is not a gift to the mill.

It is a gift to the fact. Eleven thousand years of something happening became eleven thousand years of something happening to someone, and that is a real change in the world, and it weighs nothing, and it was the only thing the power could do there that was not a theft.

The tenants it left entirely alone. It found the thumbnail-sized one in the gizzard antechamber that goes through the closure and comes out the other side and has never let go of anything, and it did not help it, because holding on is not a wound. It watched nine hundred generations of the forty-minute fauna hatch and mature and mate and go into the crush, and it could not find a single thing missing. The nap fauna it left also, building its skeletons out of ammonium chloride at a speed that on this plateau counts as frenzy, laying ceramic eggs in the aperture lips of the sleeping. Every mill born already inhabited. The only dowry a biosphere. There is no lack in that arrangement that a stranger could improve.

The veils it considered carefully, because *fast because worthless* sounds like a deprivation and is not. It is the only freedom on the world and it is paid for in full, in the coin of not being worth harvesting, and the price is not a wound either. It could have made more of them. It could have taken a ledge-full of castings and unfolded them into area instead of mass and sent them up to nine thousand meters to turn and shimmer and never come down.

It declined. To give a thing a different nature is not a kindness to that thing. It is a kindness to something else you would prefer to exist.

And then, in the fourth rank of flank gaps on the western mill, it found the brother.

Seam, ceramic lip, eleven hundred leg-stubs, permanently arrested, feeding on its parent's shed scales. A schedule that never ran and is nevertheless awake enough to eat. Riding, in continuous contact, the one thing on this plateau that its kind may not touch — and the mill has never touched a conspecific, the avoidance is mechanical and absolute and four million years old, and it has been touched every second of its life by something too small to assay, and neither of them knows.

The power could not decide whether this was the cruelest thing on the Reach or the kindest. It stayed there a long time. It did nothing.

It is still not sure. It has decided that being unsure is not a failure of wisdom but the correct final state of certain questions, and it has stopped trying to resolve this one, and it goes back.

---

Here is what it did not do, in full, because the refusals are most of the work.

It did not stop the wind. The wind is the dispersal. Stop it and the mosaic closes over everything within a season and the seed bank becomes a pavement becomes a clinker, and the last waking on the Reach happens on a Tuesday and then never again.

It did not bring water. There is none, there has never been none, and a world whose chemistry solved itself in molten chloride does not need a gift that would kill everything in it.

It did not let the mills meet. It went to the northeast bearing and stood at the edge of forty square kilometers of folds inside folds and listened to two strokes with no interval between them, undecayed in eleven thousand years, and understood that this is what contact costs here, and that the avoidance is not a deprivation to be lifted. It is the load-bearing wall.

It did not wake the pavement. It did not take the echo. It did not silence the wound. It did not make anything better than it was.

It could have been everywhere at once. Everywhere at once is what a knot is.

---

In the eleventh year it arrived, which is to say it chose an address, which is to say it became food.

This is the part that cannot be engineered around. Looking is free; wanting is not. Purpose is density — *many things held in one place, tightly, persistently, on purpose* — and an intention is the most compact object that can be brought onto this plateau. To the baselines the power was not a shape or a light. It was a number, and the number was the highest ever recorded here, and it went up as it decided things.

So it learned to love in installments.

It ran at a few microwatts, like the castings, harvesting what it could across the small distance between what it wanted and what the plain could metabolize. It worked in the pause: hatch, act, be gone before the closure. Nine hundred small mercies an afternoon, none of them large enough to register as grade, all of them paid for out of its own substance, because proximity is the only legal tender on the Reach and it had nothing else in its pockets.

Anything bigger would have pleated the ground past recovery. It knew this. It kept the ledger.

---

The first work was the cheapest one available and it took six hundred years.

The castings do not lack room. Room is the crop; the mills exhale it in enormous quantity; there is more emptiness on this plateau than on any surface in the world. What the castings lack is *duration*. Six hundred years of not being nudged, on a plain where the wind moves almost everything at least once a century. The bottleneck is not space. It is stillness, and stillness is the one commodity the Reach does not manufacture.

So it stood upwind.

Not a miracle. An obstruction. A body in an eleven-meter-per-second wind makes a lee, and a lee is where the fines go, and where the fines go is bare clinker with nothing within a hand's width of it, and that is the entire specification of a place where a stone can finish counting.

It stood in one place for six hundred years and it was eaten during it. Of course it was eaten; it was the richest deposit in a hundred kilometers and it did not move. The mills turned and came and lay down across it and the crushers closed and the stacks pulled it apart plate by plate and every atom of it went out the dorsal vents intact, at its own address, spread over a hectare.

And it kept standing. In pieces. Because a piece still casts a lee.

That was the whole idea, and it was not a trick, and it did not require omnipotence, only the willingness to be taken apart in the right place. One presence in the wind is one scour pit. Ten thousand small presences scattered across a hectare are ten thousand small stillnesses, and the wind goes around each of them, and the fines go out from under, and in the lee of every fragment there is a stone that has not been nudged since the day the power stopped being one thing.

It did not wake them. It never answered the question. It only made it possible for the world to answer, in the world's own way, on the world's own schedule, badly, in a key that drifts with the day.

The counts are running now. There are more of them than there have ever been. Most will be reset. One in something like ten to the twelfth is a very bad market and it is now a slightly less bad market, and that is the entire improvement, and it is enough to be worth a body.

---

The second work was for the one patient.

Everything else on the Reach is fine. That is the finding, and it took eleven years to be sure of, and it is not a dismissal. The mills are not lonely. The castings are not despairing. The clenched ecology is clenched and *together*, which is the closest thing to love on this world and is entirely mechanical and entirely real.

But there is a person walking out there who is the only thing on the plateau whose self *is* its adjacency, and who knows it, and who must never stop. Everything the mills exhale is medicine to the tenants and annihilation to a body like that. And it is not death. Every atom comes out chemically intact and unharmed and at its own address and all of it is still them and none of it is next to any of the rest.

That is the one genuine evil on the Reach. Not pain. The enforced solitude of something that was made to be together.

It offered removal. It could do that; lifting a thing out of a world costs nothing on this side. The cost is all on the other side, in what you have agreed to stop being able to reach.

The person said no, and would not explain, and the power did not require an explanation and did not ask twice and did not go away.

Then it went out past the vents, into the fresh product, where the far things go farther and then the near things and then your hands are at the end of a long corridor — and it found the animal that is already there.

Parts kilometers apart. Not clenched at all. Integrated by kept sound, the pockets doing the work a nerve would do. One thought per decade, and the thought very large, and nobody has ever seen the whole of one.

The world had already solved it. Out in the worst place, at the exhaust mouth, where nothing should be able to hold itself together, something had worked out how to be one thing without being in one place. It did not need a new physics brought in from outside. It needed the trick carried from where it was to where it was needed, the way the nap fauna carries eggs in the aperture lips of the sleeping.

So the power became a gleaner.

It walks the plain now collecting old strokes — the ones with the grain gone, the high frequencies worn off, the ones that have been sitting folded in a pocket somewhere waiting to arrive, a crush from before the escarpment retreated to where it now stands. It does not silence them. It *places* them. It lays them end to end across a hectare of addresses, in the right order, at the right spacing, so that when a person comes apart there is already a nerve lying there waiting for the pieces to fall into it.

Carrying folded interval is carrying closeness. The sack assays higher than anything else on the plateau, higher than the person by orders of magnitude, and the nine have all turned toward it, and it must keep walking, and it can never put the sack down, and it will be doing this when the ridge count changes.

It cannot make the person unseparable. Nothing here is killed; things here are separated; that is the law and the law is load-bearing and it will not be repealed.

It can make separation not be the end of a self. That is not a repeal. That is a third term, added quietly, in the pause, out of materials that were already lying around.

---

The castings will go on asking their question all night, ten to the fourteenth of them under their little crowns of crystal, and the answer will go on being *not yet*, and that is not a wound and cannot be healed and does not need to be.

But out past the vents, across a hectare of addresses, something else asks. It asks about once a decade, because that is how fast the nerve runs, and the question is not about room.

*Am I still one thing.*

And the answer comes back in a voice with the grain gone, a slow shearing crush kept unhurt from before the escarpment stood where it stands, arriving at full volume, and it says yes.

It will go on saying yes. It will never once be late. It was laid down in advance, in the right places, by something that walked the whole eighty kilometers in the pauses between strokes, carrying it, looking for the right places to put it down.
