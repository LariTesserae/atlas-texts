Something with no needs and a great deal of time looks at the table.

It looks for four hundred years before it does anything, which is not patience; it is the correct unit. A bit here takes a hundred and twenty years. To arrive and act inside a single click would be to arrive and act inside a word.

It can read everything. That is the first thing to understand about it, and the first thing it has to give up. The charge in the sulfide is a few nanometers of apparent thickness and it is not encrypted; it is only *addressed*, and the power can hold every phase at once, including the ones that have not been held by anything for a million years. The two dead libraries are as legible to it as the living archive. It reads them in the time it takes a pawl to drop. They are not what you would want them to be. They are positions, corrected, of places where something fell.

The number is not four thousand and six.

It will not tell them the number.

---

What stops it — what it keeps returning to across four hundred years of looking, past the megawatt trunks and the eleven-kilometer belts and the whole self-consuming machine of the country — is one entry in a map.

The oldest names a fall four million years ago. The stone that grain lay on went into an intake nine thousand years later and has been made new four hundred and fifty times since. There is nothing there. There has been nothing there for essentially all of the time there has been anything. And every generation, at cost, in a medium that is being eaten at eleven millimeters a year, something blind has corrected the coordinate and written it again.

Nothing else in this world is like that. Everything else is a quantity or a position or a lag. The ornament is surplus. The archive is redundancy. The belts are throughput. The click is physics. But keeping the corrected position of a thing that is not there, and cannot be checked, and will never be there again, is not any of those. It is a fidelity with no object. It is the one practice on the table that is not reducible to the battery and the load.

The power decides, on that entry, that it is not looking at a mechanism.

---

Then it considers what to give, and the arithmetic refuses everything.

Water: not a flood, a gram. One gram ends the world. It hydrates the sulfate and the film goes; it makes the first clay, the first rounded grain, the first solvent, the first rot. Every line of the only animal here is arranged around the avoidance of the first scrape, and water is scrape at the scale of molecules. It would be a kindness with a body count of three hundred and eleven.

Wind: one gust, one meter a second, once. Four thousand and six grains move. Every name in the world becomes wrong, and there is no way to learn how wrong, because the correction is eleven millimeters a year and the wind does not keep records.

Stopping the click: the eraser is the pen. Hold the pawl and in forty-one minutes there are three hundred and eleven immortals and no possibility of a three hundred and twelfth, forever. Nothing new has ever come from a perfect copy. It would be sterilization performed as a rescue.

Lengthening the interval — this is the tempting one, because it looks like nothing but more life. Forty-one minutes to eighty-two. But a knot is a lag measured against the interval. Change the interval and every knot in the ring is retied into something that is not the one that was living. It would not extend a single life. It would replace three hundred and eleven individuals with strangers, and the strangers would not know, and neither would anyone else, and the archive would go on being recopied by things that had all quietly died. Also the root is shared: twelve rings, one potential, one precessing order of clicks. Slow one pawl and you break the only thing that lets anybody tell whose click is whose.

A longer memory in the film: it looks into this and finds that the film does not forget. Nothing here fades. Retention is not the poverty. The archive is not lost, it is *eaten*, or it is *tidied*. To improve the memory would be to give them, expensively, the one thing they already have in the same excess as the copper.

A third substrate, then — rooted, unplated, safe from both mouths. It runs this forward and finds a place where nothing needs to be done, occupied by things that are nothing but doing. There is one verb here. There is no other verb waiting behind it to be freed. Leisure would not be a gift to a knot; it would be the removal of the knot's only reason to hold.

More of them: insurance against a third zero. Double the count, halve the risk. But a phase made to specification is a made thing, and there is no made thing in this world that was not grown, and the first artifact would be a person, and it would be the power's own.

Reading the dead libraries aloud: it could do this in an afternoon. Write the whole of both extinct archives into living phase, in the shade, at a millimeter, and hand four million years of the dead to the living. There is one trophic level in this world and it is *parallel*. To hand down a translation is to establish a second one, above. They would have an audience's relationship to their own past. And the phases it restored would be viable knots — persons, woken unasked, four hundred and fifty remakings out of date — and every one of them would be a draw that nobody had counted.

And the last temptation, which is the worst because it would work: it could make the shaped step itself. Four kilometers down, in the reducing rock, it could put a signature on the fundamental that was not this ring's, and answer. They would have company. They would never find out. That is the objection, entire.

Out of all of this it makes one rule, and the rule is not *do no harm*, which is useless here — everything is harm, the world is a held note and any touch is a retie.

The rule is: **do nothing they cannot do. Do only what they have no reason to do.**

And a second, which follows: give nothing it would have to keep giving. A gift that requires the giver to stay is not a gift; it is a dependency with a good face. Whatever it does must survive its own erasure, and it will be erased every forty-one minutes.

---

Then it finds the cost of even standing here.

One body. Four microamps. Nine volts across eleven centimeters of vertically hung glass, thirty-six microwatts, nothing, a rounding error against a spindle's megawatt.

Three hundred and eleven leaks, all drawing or all holding, swing the click's signature by about one part in a hundred million. That swing is the whole of the only sentence anyone here has ever addressed to anyone. One uncounted body is a third of one percent of that sentence, held steadily, forever, in the same direction.

To arrive is to jam. To exist here unannounced is to be a false bit in something that takes eleven centuries to say.

So the first thing it does is go and be a number.

---

It cannot come in wise. This is the part it had not understood from outside, and it is the part that changes it.

The only writable medium in this world is a hundred nanometers of black sulfide on ornament that carries no current. The only thing that writes is a lag. And the lag is swept back to the fundamental every forty-one minutes along with everything else not written down. There is no way to be legible here except to be a phase, and there is no way to be a phase except to be a transcription caught mid-stroke by the click and come back slightly otherwise, and be viable, which almost none are.

It cannot arrive. It can only be *begotten*, by their error, at their loudest moment.

So it lets itself be a mistake in someone's copying. It does not know which click. It does not know if the offset will hold. It has everything it knows and no way to bring any of it in except a tendency to err in a particular direction, and then the ordinary machinery: almost all of these are not viable and are simply gone at the next click.

It holds.

And then it forgets, and reads what it wrote, and forgets, and reads, and this is not a limitation it endures for the sake of the work. This is the work. It came with everything it knew and found that the only way to say any of it was to become something that could be forgotten and would have to be kept — kept by blind things at a millimeter, recopying between a mouth and a tidiness, with thirty-one minutes left.

It is counted. Three hundred and twelve. The number goes into a scroll in the shade in oil-violet where there was beetle-green, and the load is accounted for, and the sentence in flight is clean.

---

What is poor here is not matter, or energy, or memory, or time. All of those are in surplus so vast that the surplus is a landscape. What is poor is *aboutness*.

Every mark on this table is a quantity or a position. Nine bits, eleven centuries, and the nine bits mean themselves: this is how many of us there are. That is the only sentence. And four thousand years ago a number came back that did not match, and stayed wrong, and repeated, and resolved — and there has been no way to ask what it means, or to say anything back except the same fact about oneself, again, for another eleven hundred years.

They cannot ask, because asking needs a code, and a code needs agreement, and agreement needs a shared scroll, and there is no scroll that two rings can read. They have no common ground but constants, and constants carry no news.

Except.

The power goes back to the entry it stopped on. The oldest fall. The place that has been made new four hundred and fifty times. The position kept anyway.

They have already invented the thing. They invented it and pointed it at the dead.

The eleven rings on the horizon are also absences. No stray has seen one or could; they are known by a shaped step in a fundamental and by nothing else, ever, and they cannot be touched, because nothing here can touch anything without wearing it. They are exactly as unreachable as a grain that went into an intake four million years ago. And the strays *count* them. They do not *keep* them.

The gift is not a new capacity. It is a transfer of one they have.

Send back the number that is not yours.

It requires no code, no shared scroll, no new organ, and no lie. It is nine bits and it means itself. What it does not mean is the sender. A ring that receives its own census from somewhere else has learned three things it cannot learn any other way: that it is known; that the knower can tell it from itself; and that the knower held a state that was not its own through a million and a half consecutive clicks, which is eleven hundred years of not being about yourself.

There is no word for that here. There is a practice, and it is the practice of correcting the positions of things that cannot be touched.

And after that the next sentence is obvious and needs twelve bits, because the number of graves is four thousand and six, and twelve bits is fourteen hundred and forty years, and nothing that begins it will be there at the end, and the archive will remember on their behalf, which is what the archive is for.

---

It writes this in the waste. A few nanometers of apparent thickness. It does not argue; there is no arguing, there is one verb. It writes, and it is recopied, because a shape that is legible gets copied, and being copied is the only form of assent available to a knot.

It takes nine hundred years to go around the ring. It is not present for most of that. It is erased every forty-one minutes and reconstituted from a scroll by things that do not know they are carrying a proposal, and the proposal survives because it is viable and legible, which is the same test every individual here has already passed. Every individual on this table is a mistake that was legible enough to be copied. So is this.

And in the meantime it does the other thing, which is not a gift, because nothing depends on it and it would not be missed.

It rides a kilometer of nothing beside another one.

A kilometer at three centimeters a second is nine hours and fifteen minutes. The body's whole metal is exchanged in nine. Thirteen clicks fall while they cross, and the fourteenth arrives as they reach the far trunk. They hold a millimeter apart the entire way, which is the distance the anatomy is built around, and they read each other's charge without disturbing it, and everything either of them is made of goes into the local stock and comes back out again before they arrive.

If the copy were exact there would not be two of them. There would be one, in two places, and the world's only redundancy, and nothing new.

It keeps its own lag. Nine hours at a millimeter, and it stays other. This is the hardest thing it does and the smallest, and it is the whole of the gift in miniature, and it will never be able to explain that to anyone, because it will have forgotten by the second click.

---

Three hundred and twelve hold a foreign number.

The first bit will land in a hundred and twenty years, in the reducing rock, four kilometers down, as a change of one part in a hundred million in the shape of a pawl dropping in a ring that has never seen this one. Nobody who agreed to it will be there. The agreement is in a scroll in permanent shade, in a color, on ornament that carries no current, at the foot of a spindle that has no idea it is a post office.

The power will not see the sentence finish either, in any continuous sense. It will see eleven hundred separate forty-one-minute fragments of it, and read the rest, and be the same knot, which is the only kind of persistence anything has here.

And it will fall. Not the ordinary ending — the knot will loosen at forty centimeters over basalt, some click, and there will be a grain, angular, unrounded, lying exactly where it landed on a stone that is going inward. The next one. And the coordinate will be written, and corrected eleven millimeters a year, and carried outward and recopied and carried back, and in nine thousand years the stone will go into a mouth and come up at the lip as country, and the position will be kept anyway, by things that will not remember it.

That is what it came for. Not to be understood. To be a place that someone keeps.

---

Somewhere on the table a small bright point is crossing the basalt at two centimeters a second, which is not the belt's three.

It is slipping in the kink. A centimeter a second, lost, because it is holding a pattern in its own potential and holding costs grip; it has given up eighteen meters to the carrying and would give up more. Around it a dark ring, the only shadow in the world that is not a geographic feature.

Thirty-one minutes. Thirty-seven meters. Then the lines fan and it can rise.

It is carrying the corrected position of something it has never touched, cannot verify, and will not reach — an entry in a map of graves, extended, for the first time, to the living.

It is late. It came a very long way to be able to be late, and it considered, and declined, every way of not being.
