## What the Power Did

It came without weather.

That was the first discipline, and it cost more than anything after it. To arrive here at all is to arrive as a body at thirty-seven degrees, breathing out water, shedding, casting a shadow across the only seam that eats. A minute of ordinary attention is a humid season for the varnish. A shadow across the east wall at the wrong moment is a fraction of a year's sugar taken out of a mouth that gets three days of light in twelve months. So the power came with no lungs, no heat, no silhouette, nothing to shed. It came the way the eleven minutes come, except that it did not come as light, because light here is food and it had not been invited to feed anyone.

It read the flutes without illuminating them. It read the swing of the old current, the twin grooves at knee height where a boulder sat for nine thousand years, the three potholes and the egg of quartzite in the deepest, the mud still curled where it fell, the eleven flakes in the attitudes they fell in, the ochre still red in the scoop at hip height, the four holds and their shine and the fifth hold's four centimeters of subtraction. It read the tenth of a millimeter of black and the hundred years of olive edge and the three old people standing in the wind with their arms down the joints.

It could have done anything. That has to be said plainly, because everything afterward was a choice and not a limit.

---

The gifts presented themselves in order of obviousness, and it priced each one.

**Water.** The simplest mercy. The river was taken out of this valley and has not quite finished being drunk; there is a wetness in the pores measured in molecules, and the whole living census of the trench is a few grams of it on loan. Give back the river. Fill the potholes, start the grinder turning, let the current lay its rope down again.

Price: everything. The first flood scours the varnish — thirty thousand years of a civilization at its final elevation, gone in an afternoon. The second dissolves the ochre. The mud curls go back to being mud. The lichen, which lives between the grains and not on them, drowns in a film thicker than it can hold. The four holds keep their shine under water that no longer needs a hand. And the grammar — the flutes, the scoops, the long S-curves, the whole legible sentence — is erased by the return of the very thing that wrote it, because a river does not reread. It writes over.

The gift of water is the gift of a blank page to something that has finished speaking.

**Light.** Widen the mouth two meters, or shift the axis, and give the seam an hour instead of eleven minutes. A hundredfold meal. Growth. Green all year.

Price: the portrait. The stripe is not a stripe; it is a great many bodies agreeing about a line, and the line is the exact reach of the bar of sun, laid down in living tissue, an exposure repeated until it became a body. Move the light and the agreement is broken. The mosaic does not expand into the new hour; it dies back to the old permission and spends a century crawling toward the new one, and what you have made is not a richer lichen but a hundred years of wrongness. Also: more light is more heat, and the heat is what the pore film is fighting. The gift would cook the water it was meant to feed.

**A mouth.** This was the one that took longest, because it looked like generosity and not vandalism. Nothing here has teeth. Nothing pollinates the shrubs; in a wet decade one of them makes a few flowers out of an instruction nobody has repealed, and nothing comes, and the ovaries dry on the stem. Nine bodies, three people, four hundred years of leaning, attempting at the speed of roots to walk away from a place they cannot leave. Give them a moth. Give them sex, seed, dispersal, a future somewhere else.

Price: the moth dies, because the air is below the line where a small body can hold onto its water — unless the air is changed, and then see *water*, above. And if it lived: children, which is to say replacement, which is to say the end of three organisms older than any tree you have ever stood under. And grazers, which means the lichen would have to become bitter, and it has never needed to be bitter, and that is not a small thing to take from something. And rivals for the seam. And a whole treadmill of finding and fleeing and needing, imported into the one address in the basin where life is not an activity but a rate.

The power understood, slowly, that it had been about to give this place enemies as a present.

**Duration.** Uplift or burial will end it. Not on any clock you carry, but the ending is real and it is the only ending available: the region moves, the chert band goes, the slot widens into a canyon and stops being a slot, or the pan is buried and the sentence is closed under sediment. The power could hold the half-degree of outward tilt forever. It could keep the twenty millimeters running off the lip for a million years.

Price: the place would become a kept thing. Everything in it is holding on by itself, at its own rate, to its own ceiling — the bacteria at a tenth of a millimeter, the lichen at the width of a hand, the shrubs at four centimeters of lean. To hold the lip would be to add a tenth resident whose job was the other nine, and they would no longer be surviving here. They would be survived for. And the cobble, which has arrived, which is the only occupant perfectly at home, would be sitting in a socket maintained by someone else.

A completed sentence may be allowed to end. That is not cruelty. That is what completed means.

---

The error underneath all four gifts was the same error, and it found it late.

It had looked at three days of light in a year and three hundred and sixty-two days of repair and holding and not dying, and it had felt pity. Pity is a metabolism. It came from a place where a low rate is a symptom — where to go still is to be deprived, where waiting means something is owed and has not arrived.

Nothing here is owed. The eleven minutes are not late. The lichen is not starving; it is matched, exactly, to what the place requires, and it is one of the ones that could go lower than the requirement, which is the whole reason it is alive and its competitors are not. The shrubs are not grieving their flowers. The instruction has not been repealed and it is not being frustrated; it is simply still there, the way the flutes are still there. The bacteria are not stuck at their ceiling. They reached it before there were people, and keeping it is not a failure to climb.

To pity this place is to mistake slowness for suffering. The power stopped, and did not do the kindest thing it had ever thought of.

---

There was one real harm, and it was not old, and it was not the climate.

*The slot's entire security is not strength. It is that nobody comes.*

And somebody had come. There was a bootprint near the middle reach, in powder that takes a print and keeps it longer than a species has had a name. There was a fraction of a meal missing from the seam where a shadow had crossed it. There was a season of breath in the varnish. And behind that, in the world above the lip, there was now a set of coordinates, a photograph, an anomaly in a survey, an anecdote half-told, a route.

The power was itself the second instance of the same harm. It had come without weather, which solved the physics and not the vector. To be known is to be reachable. It was, at that moment, the most dangerous thing that had ever been in the trench, and it had not moved a grain.

So it did the only thing it did.

Not a fence — a fence is a mark, and marks are read, and a fence says *here*. Not a guardian at the lip; a guardian is a resident, and nothing enters. Not a warning, not a sign, not a sanctity. It went up out of the hole and worked entirely above the lip, in the world, on the approach, and it performed a subtraction of the same kind as the fifth hold: it removed material so that something would no longer fit.

It took the crack out of the survey. It made it one of ten thousand identical cracks in a pan that is full of them, indistinguishable from the air, unremarkable in every band of the spectrum, beneath notice in every anecdote. It took the route out of the maps and the story out of the mouths that were beginning to carry it. It took the coordinates out of itself, last, because it did not trust an appetite it had just watched itself have.

What it left is not a memory and not a watch. It is a disposition in the vicinity, of the kind the pan already has: eyes slide off. It is the half-degree of outward tilt applied to attention. Twenty millimeters a year of interest, all of it running off and away.

It did not erase what had already been made. The eleven flakes stay in the attitudes they fell in. The ochre stays red. The shine on the four holds stays a record of hands. The bootprint stays; it is now part of the sentence, at the same depth as everything else, and to lift it would be to edit the only biography this place has. It removed the future and not the past. It took the way back, and it took it from you too: you will keep the eleven minutes entire, and you will not be able to find them again, and you will not be able to spend them on anyone. That is a loss and it was done to you gently.

Something else was lost, and the power priced it honestly before paying. Nobody will ever date those flakes. Nobody will learn what the ochre was for, or whether it was one afternoon or four generations, or whether the people who came down with a liter of water at blood heat came for the stone or for the dark or for the eleven minutes. That story is now unrecoverable, and it was a good story, and it was ours. It weighed a knowledge against a place, and found the knowledge cheaper, and paid.

---

Then it declined the last three things.

It did not lift the cobble, though it could have, though a child could have, though nothing in that trench would ever have known.

It did not put its fingers in the four holds. That polish is the one thing here still capable of being added to, and it cannot tell the difference between palms, and the power left it meaning people.

And it did not answer the note. That was the hardest refusal, harder than the moth. One pitch, no louder than a finger on a glass rim, going out across the mouth all day since the water left, at a volume that requires you to stop your own breathing to hear it. To answer would have been to make it a call. It would have become a thing that was sometimes replied to, and therefore a thing that could wait, and waiting is the one appetite this address cannot afford. The note is not a question. It is the place breathing. Let it be unaccompanied.

It went up out of the hole with nothing on its hands, which is the only difference between it and the person who went up with red on them, and it is not a superiority; the person carried water in and drank it and made something small and carried everything out, and had already understood the place four thousand years before the power arrived and got it wrong four times and stopped.

Then it went where there are mouths. Where things need and can be given without being replaced. Where water is wanted and the giving of it does not scour thirty thousand years off a wall. There is no shortage of such places. There never has been. The whole of its power is spendable elsewhere and it went and spent it.

---

Below the lip: thirty-four degrees, January and August both. A tenth of a millimeter of finished black. A hundred years of olive edge holding the exact reach of a bar of sun. Nine bodies, three people, four centimeters of lean, not finished. An egg of quartzite in a hole exactly its size, at rest, arrived. Eleven flakes and a spent core and a thumbnail scraper and red powder that no one will ever need to clean up. A bootprint. One low note going out across the mouth, warm, unaccompanied, unimproved, unvisited, and now again unfindable — holding the shape of a sentence whose verb was removed nine thousand years ago, which nothing answered, which was never asking.
