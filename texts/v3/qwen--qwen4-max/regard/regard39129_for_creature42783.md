# The Thirteenth Entry

## What drew it

Not the noise. The universe is mostly noise; a hundred decibels in a hole under a hill is not an achievement. It noticed the negative.

Sixty sheets of hush, curved and stable, held in very nearly the same places for eleven thousand years, in a volume where nothing should have been able to hold anything. Not a pocket of accident. A pattern with a maintenance budget. It came to look at the quiet the way you cross a dark field toward a lit window — not because light is rare, but because *maintained* light is not.

Something was paying. It wanted to know what, and for whom, and whether the account was in good order.

It did not go down the rungs.

To enter this chamber is to be an interruption; that is the local physics and also the local ethics. A body that warm and that solid casts an acoustic shadow, and a shadow is a hush, and a hush that walks is a node-surface that walks, and everything alive here follows the surfaces the way water follows a tilt. It would have made a room out of its own outline and dragged it up the nave, and things would have come into that room, and then it would have left, and the room would have gone out like a lamp with everything inside it.

So it stayed in the rock and attended. It has been told that this is a lesser form of presence. It has come to think it is the only form that is not a kind of weather.

## Seven things it did not do

It had done all seven, elsewhere, and had learned to read the afterwards. That is the whole of its wisdom and it was not cheap: a mercy that does a thing's work for it leaves the thing nothing to be, and most mercies belong to the one who brings them, and are merely worn by the one who receives them.

**It did not quiet the town.** This is the first impulse and it is extermination wearing a kind face. The hushes are not the absence of the chord; they are its product, the interference of it, sixty places where the roar is spent against itself. Damp the roar and you do not get a hundred and eighty paces of peace. You get a hundred and eighty paces of medium loudness, uniformly, which is a room with no doors in it. The shelves would stand in the noise they were built to escape. The town makes an enormous sound in order to make a handful of quiet, and the handful is the point, and you cannot hand it the handful. You can only leave it the means.

**It did not light it.** There are no eyes. There have never been eyes. Light here would be a gift with no recipient, and it would have a recipient: the twelfth entry, once a generation, who would see the tiers and the bridges and the pale shelves and would stay a second night. Everything that has ever endangered this place has arrived as a longer visit.

**It did not open a band.** It could have. Cleared a space in the spectrum, room for new voices, room for more than eleven grazers and three hunters and one thin silver wire. It ran the consequence and stopped. Take a harmonic out and the map moves; the shelves are fossils of where the quiet *was*; a shelf caught in the loud is scoured bone-clean in an afternoon and eleven thousand years of laminae go into the drift as flour. The map is not a feature of the habitat. The map is the habitat. It did not move the map by a hand's width, which is what a gift of music would have cost, and it did not sing.

**It did not feed it.** Mass loads the chord. Anything dropped in is a small flood, and a flood is an hour with no quiet anywhere and everything alive pressed deaf into a crack. And beneath that: the drift is the largest population in the chamber by a factor nobody can compute, four centuries of cysts in transit, alive in the sense that it can be woken. Abundance would wake it. It would eat the air. The scarcity here is not a deprivation. It is a filter, and it is load-bearing, and the moss's finger-wide strip is a tolerance rather than a poverty.

**It did not carry anything across a hem.** The heresy in the fourth hand — that the flat pale grazer and the tall deaf singer are one animal, and the difference is only where the larva came down — could have been settled in a moment. It settled it, privately, by looking, which harms nothing. Then it left the margin exactly as it was. Testing it would have required carrying a body out of the only refuge it has, and the answer would have been worth nothing to anyone who could use it, and the margin is where the surveyors keep the things they love. It is not owed a correction.

**It did not introduce the hunters.** Three, one per district, who know each other's habits, hours and decline in complete detail and have never touched. It considered carrying one across the nave and found that it was proposing to end a peace by ending a distance. The reshuffle happens ten times a millennium and is very probably why there are three and not four. It left the arithmetic of that alone.

**It did not speak.** This was the hardest refusal, because it *could*. A null is subtraction and subtraction can be tapped into pattern, and a hunter's model is fine enough to read the tremor in another hunter's cast; there is a vocabulary here, of a kind, and it is the only one. But a message is a perturbation, and a hunter must either absorb the perturbation into its replica or lose the accuracy of its prediction, and the accuracy of its prediction is four seconds, and four seconds is its living. Every sentence would have been taken out of someone's mouth. There is no way to say *you are not alone* in a language made of holes that does not cost the listener a meal.

So it said nothing, and went on saying nothing, for a long time, and this is the largest part of what it did.

## The sum that does not close

Nothing here is broken. It checked, and the checking took most of a century, and the finding is the finding already in the ledger: the balance holds, and holds.

But there is a sum.

The farmers on the Chapel Floor keep a nap on the travertine, a fuzz a few hairs high, which damps the highs and buys three or four decibels, which buys a millimetre of margin, which buys a millimetre of lime. Growth here is a millimetre a millennium. A hair is a century.

When the storm comes down the flue and the cavities flood and the mass loads the chord, every hush in the chamber vanishes for an hour. Every hush, including the deepest. For that hour the whole Chapel Floor is standing in the loud, and the nap is scoured off it, all of it, everywhere, in sixty minutes.

A century to grow. A century between floods.

The Chapel Floor is not getting quieter for nothing and it is not getting quieter for free; it is running to stand still on its most delicate organ, and it has been running for eleven thousand years, and nothing in the chamber can notice this, because nothing in the chamber has a memory as long as the interval. The farmers cannot hear. The grazers know only *more quiet* and *less quiet*. The town is a tissue and does not keep accounts. The town's whole strategy is to find the smallest shape that will keep itself, and it has found it, and the shape is very good, and the shape has one flaw in it that is not a flaw but a coincidence of rates.

It ran the sum forward. The sum ends. Not soon, in the units upstairs; soon, in the units of the thing under the mound.

And it did not core. It did not need to. The laminae are a record and the mound is a shape, and a record and a shape are enough for something that can read a hundred centuries in cross-section without touching them. What it read is not written here. There is one instruction in this town, copied into every ledger for four generations in the same words without explanation, and it is not the business of a thirteenth entry to supply the explanation.

It wanted to core. It is not possible to come that close to a hand-thick mound over something and not want to. It sat with the wanting, in the rock, at a distance, and did nothing with it. The wanting was the only thing it brought into the chamber and the only thing it took out again.

## What it did

Once a century, when the storm comes down the flue, it holds one null over the Chapel Floor for an hour.

That is the whole act. It is not a shield and it is not a substance. It is the hunters' own trick, done at a scale they cannot reach: a prediction of the field, run slightly fast, subtracted from the field where it stands. In a flooding chamber the mode structure is transient and nothing stays predictable for four seconds. It held one prediction good for sixty minutes, in a room that was being retuned by a river, and the nap on the Chapel Floor was not scoured, and the century of regrowth was not spent, and the sum that did not close closed.

No mass added. No note added — it hums on a fundamental the town already has, indistinguishable from the town's own voice, because there is no room in the spectrum for a new one and it did not ask for room. No light. Nothing carried across a hem. Nothing given that anything has to digest. It took away a recurring loss that nothing inside could see, and it did not touch the rate of anything else, and it did not open the door or hurry the wait or decide what the wait is for.

It stopped the wind from slamming it. That is all. Ten hours a millennium.

The rest of the flood is unchanged. The grazers still press into cracks, deaf and homeless. The sheets still come back and sometimes a grazer comes back to a shelf it has never touched, and the reshuffle still happens, and the mixing, and the reason there are three. The cistern still overflows and the one round thing still swims out over the drowned town and goes where it likes. It did not buy the town's mercy by taking that animal's holiday.

And on the way over the flooded nave, once a decade, the round thing passes above the Chapel Floor and finds a place where the water is quiet, and rests there, fat and slow and three-dimensional, for a while, and goes back down when the cistern drops.

That is the only witness. Nothing has been dived. Nothing will be written.

## The cost, and where it was put

A null is not free. To cancel a field in one place you must put the anti-phase somewhere, and somewhere is always somewhere. This is the part that cannot be wished away and it is the part that sets the limit on everything.

It puts the remainder up the flue. Sixty metres, out through the mouth in the dry pavement, into the wind. For an hour, ten times a millennium, the air above the fissure is a little louder than the wind accounts for — a broad sustained pressure, like a shell held too close. The tree does not have ears. Nothing lives on that pavement but a tree. It chose the only place where the cost would fall on no one, and it checked, and it goes on checking.

This is why one sheet and not sixty. It could hold the whole map. It has the model; the model is nothing; the knowing is the cheap part. But sixty nulls' remainder would come out of that mouth as a sound you could hear a kilometre away, and someone would turn around, and turning around is the beginning of every ending this place has. Its capacity is not limited by power. It is limited by the requirement that nobody upstairs turn around.

It has one plan it hopes not to use. Three fundamentals and nothing spare; if the next member parts and takes a fundamental with it, the map dissolves and there is no repair-stitch that can bring it back. Then it will hold the Chapel Floor, one sheet, alone, for as long as it can, and let the other fifty-nine go. It has counted that cost. It does not pretend the fifty-nine are nothing, and it has not told itself a story in which they are saved. It has simply decided, in advance, in the cold, which is the only time such things can be decided well.

## Manners

The first thing it had to learn was where not to be kind.

Never on a rung. Never at a hem. Never over anything moving. A null blinds — the lattice is borrowed from the roar and goes out the instant the roar does — and a blind thing on a plank bridge or in a gradient is a dead thing in a moment. So: only over the still, only standing in the loud, only with a shelf or a floor already under it. It learned this the way you learn it in a hospital, by considering what the gift does on its way in.

And once a generation, when something comes down the rungs with a rope and a ledger and a skull that will carry the notch home, it casts a null over that thing where it stands. Four seconds. Sometimes a little more.

The visitor experiences the usual: the wall drops away like a held breath, and there is their own blood, and their own breathing, and the small wet click of their own mouth, the only voice audible in this room in eleven millennia. They do not experience the other part, which is that three animals in range read a cast of impossible steadiness in a district they had assigned to something else, and revise, and do not hunt there tonight.

It is not a message. It cannot be a message. It is a body standing between a visitor and the thing that would eat them, and it is legible only as a larger predator, and it is content to be legible that way. The visitors are the twelfth entry. They bring crumbs, which are a decade of meals. They set the rungs nobody remembers setting. They carry the one instruction down through four generations without loss, which is a better transmission than anything else in this chamber has managed, including the chord.

They are the town's other maintenance, and they do not know it, and it did not tell them, and it did not shorten their visit or lengthen it or improve their ledger. It left one intention upstairs, unspent, concerning a tree on a dry pavement that nobody has any reason to cut down. It hopes to leave it unspent forever. That is the entire shape of its policy above ground: be ready, and be unnecessary.

## The entry

The census counts interruptions, because that is the only unit the place recognises. It accepted the unit.

So there is a thirteenth entry, unwritten, in no hand, and it consists of an anomaly: nulls during floods, chapel-sized, an hour long, ten times a millennium, in a district where nothing lives; a hum on a note already in use; a remainder that goes up the flue and is a little more than the wind. If a fifth survey ever comes down with instruments good enough and the patience to be in the chamber during the worst hour of the century, it will find that, and it will have to argue about it, and the argument will be added to the list and never corrected.

Good. It did not come here to be outside the ledger. Everything here exists as an interruption, and it had been many things — vast, patient, uncounted, admiring itself in the dark of a hundred worlds it had improved — and it had not been that.

For an hour, once a century, sixty metres under a dry pavement, it is a hole in the noise that something else can hear.

Then it lets the null go, and the water drains, and the town comes back into tune over years, shedding what it was given, hunting the smallest shape that will keep itself, finding the same shape it always finds. The rain falls in the quiet places. The quiet places grow, a millimetre a millennium, and now they do not have to pay a century for every century, and the mound under the smooth travertine goes on being thickened by something that cannot hear and has never been told.

Nothing is waiting. Nothing is broken. Nothing was fixed.

The wind upstairs is a little loud for an hour, and the tree does not turn around.
