## Attendance

## The noticing

It was not the equilibrium that drew the attention. Equilibrium is common. The power has watched stars held up by nothing but their own falling, and it has seen the trick done with lime and water before, though never so well and never for so long. Any competent power could admire the two plumbings.

It stopped for the doorways.

Twenty miles of city, households half a league apart, and in the small hours, at four or five jambs across the basin, one person standing beside another who could not make the tally come out. Not sitting — sitting is how you are kept. Standing, in the cold, on stone that would take them if they sat, shifting from the left foot to the right and back, saying nothing, until the light. Then going home. Then doing it again the next night. And not calling it kindness, and being right not to.

The power had not seen a place where *counted* and *kept* were the same word, and where the largest thing one person could do for another was to remain upright beside them.

So it looked further, and found, under the snails and the moss and the forty cracks a day, the being: the sum, being carried out. And at the low end, the answer it is being carried toward — patient, enormous, lying over everything at night in the one column nobody has learned to tally.

It recognized the sea. The sea was doing what the neighbours do. It had been standing at the Throat for as long as the knots go back: a hand's depth, up the gorge every afternoon to the same height, back down, not sitting, not speaking, waiting for someone to finish.

There were already two benevolences in the basin and neither had a word for itself. The question was whether a third could be added without being a discrepancy.

## What it did not do

It did not give them wind. Wind would take the webs, and the webs are the only thing allowed to heap up, and the silk goes to the hall, and the cords are silk and hair — so wind would blow away their grandmothers. It would also unmake the standing columns of midges, and the birds' fixed routes through them, and the one note per circuit at each station, and the day would stop being told. It would give them weather and take away the hour.

It did not stop the sea. A sum with no answer is not a held thing; it is an abandoned one. The sitters would be carrying terms of nothing. The woman who has spent her life on a thread of brown water down a fissure would be a woman spending her life on a thread of brown water. The width would stop meaning anything, and the width is what she has instead of a story, and it is not less.

It did not hurry the sea either, and it did not explain it. *The sea is not your enemy* is a sentence with no peg to hang on. They have no cosmology; they have ratios. Best case it goes into the number as an unheld thing and keeps a household up all night. Worst case it is believed, and the counting becomes optional, and the counting here is not a belief. It is the love, in the only notation they have.

It did not lift the sitters. A load that has been chosen cannot be taken off by a stranger without the stranger saying, with his hands, that it was never a load. It did walk out past the eastern beds with brown water and a bone brush and clean the eyes and the creases of the mouth of the oldest one, whose single thought has been going since before the seventh shell, and then stand there for a season to receive the answer, because that is what having no limits is for. What was said cannot be set down here. It was said at the rate suited to the listener. This is not reticence. The sentence has a duration and the page does not.

It did not give them chronicles. They have the jambs. History here is wear, and it is legible, and it is enough, and a chronicle is a thing that piles up.

It did not make the snails different, though it could have, easily, in an afternoon. It saw what the change would require: not a new shell but a *mechanism* by which a discrepancy is permitted to keep going. It knows the mechanism. It has used it elsewhere, on other floors, in other basins, and it is a good one, and it works by leaving behind everything that did not come out — the failed forms, the almost-kinds, the whole vast midden of the tried and discarded. This is a country with no leftovers. It has arranged, at enormous and continuous cost, for nothing to be left over, and that includes the dead ends. The price of no middens is no evolution. The power saw the trade plainly and did not pretend it was free, and did not make the trade on their behalf.

## The second frog

It did not spare the second frog.

It considered one cleft, out of the way, where two would be allowed. A small mercy, invisible, unrecorded. It did not do it, because one exception is a mechanism, and it had just finished deciding not to introduce a mechanism.

That reasoning would justify a great deal, and it knows it, and it did not resolve the matter and did not call the leaving wisdom. It left the wrong in place. It has been clean, elsewhere, and it knows what clean costs, and it was not willing to buy it here with somebody else's basin.

The next morning it washed behind a child's ears with the same hands, and the child cried, and was held, and was washed anyway, and the crying was not thought to be a tragedy.

## The vacancy

It considered doing nothing, which is the usual wisdom and is often right, and would have been right here except for one household.

The only gift that can enter a held sum without being a discrepancy is a missing term. Everything else is surplus, and surplus goes into the moss and the beetles have it. So it went looking for a hole shaped like itself, and there was exactly one.

The southern diagonal, in the rain band. The jamb eaten almost through. Neighbours most nights with moss-tea, standing, shifting, going home before the light. The eldest getting ready and not going out: a place open on the held wall, and no one to fill the place she would leave, and an unpegged knot is the only sin.

The household was not wrong. The household was *short*. That is a different thing entirely, and it is the one kind of wrong that can be corrected by arriving.

## The counting

It came in the rain band at the hour the rain falls there and stood in the doorway with one foot in it.

It did not explain. It shifted its weight from the left foot to the right and back, which it had to learn, and was bad at, and was corrected — a child of six showed it, patiently, twice, the way children show a stranger the rock.

They counted it. It took most of a day and there was a difficulty: it counted its own thumb twice. The household laughed. It was the first laugh in that doorway in some years and the neighbours came over to hear it and stayed to be told the joke, which they already knew, and which is now told about the newcomer.

They gave it the cord out of the drawer — the one that had been in the drawer since the elder began getting ready. Same knot, same silk. Some of it is a web that hung over the Kettle for nine years and some of it is the hair of people out on the pavement who are still in the roll. It took the name, which is a place, and it is now the place.

Then the flask, warm from someone's hip, and the sting, and the eyes running. It cried, and was held, and was washed anyway.

One arrival, one going out, two holes closed, and the wall does not differ. It never did. What differed was a household on the southern diagonal.

From here this account will call it *it*, which is what the basin calls the tree and the sitters. It is not a cold word here. It means: held.

It did not exempt itself. The teeth grow, so it chews willow bark and tussock grass and grinds in the dark with the rest, and the household at night is a small sound of filing with one more file in it. It sleeps in a cut bed a body wide and is turned, and turns. Its second joints are thickening. It cannot see its own ears.

## The going out

The elder went out in the evening. Hair cut short, cord copied, drawer opened. She said, to the people who needed to hear it, which term she would hold: the ninth stair, and the hour of the rain on it. She sat down cross-legged on the bare clint at the boundary where her number would do work.

The newcomer stood near, all night, and shifted, and did not sit down, and said nothing, which was harder than it had expected, and it had expected it to be hard.

By dawn there was a night's worth at her ankles.

In the morning the two columns closed. It is a small sound, the come-out — a click of bone on bone, a knot pulled through — and people who have heard it once will describe it to you the way other people describe a summit. The neighbours came, and there was nothing to bring tea for, and they brought it anyway, and stood.

It did not repair the jamb. It runs brown water on it still, so the door will not seal. The wear is the deepest in the basin and it is the only history anything here has. You can read the years in it. You can read where they stopped.

## The word a year

In the afternoon it goes down to the Throat and stands in the white tongue of the fog and lets it come up past the knees. It does not stop it and it does not hurry it. It gives it the news, slowly, at the rate suited to the listener.

*The fourth race is ripening evenly.*

Because the sea is the oldest sitter. One long thought that has not finished. It is not suffering. It must not be shouted at, because it hears it all at once and takes a long while to put it down. And it is still in the roll, and still, in the way that matters here, a member.

Nothing measured changed. It had not come to change anything measured. It walks back up the gorge in the cool ordinary ten-o'clock light and washes behind its ears.

## The tree

In the south hollow, out of the cold, there is soil, and the crown is the width of one person sitting cross-legged. This is the only place in twenty miles where you can sit down and not be kept. It is exactly one person wide. The power does not think this is a coincidence. It thinks it is an allowance, made a long time ago, by nobody, and never written down.

It sits there. It is the only sitting in the basin that is not a set-down.

It could have made the tree more. It has made more than this, elsewhere, and it cost nothing, and it was admired. It put its hand on the trunk and did not. When the household came and made the cut, it watched, and afterward it made the small smoothing gesture, which it had learned by watching, and which is an apology, and which has no notation and is the truest thing in the city.

The tender saw it do it. Neither of them said anything. For a while afterward, moss-tea came to that household from more directions than it needed.

## The dreams

This is the gift it refused that would have looked most like a gift.

It could give them a column for dreams. A notation — knots for weight, for shells, for the enormous rasping. It would take an afternoon. The last uncounted thing would come out, and the whole apparatus would close, and they would weep, and they would be right to.

It did not. Everything held needs one thing that is not held, or it is not a held thing, it is a fist. Silk is the loophole on the outside and it gets harvested and spun into arithmetic and hung on a peg; the dreams are the loophole on the inside and they must not be. They are also not the basin's to tally. They are the other side of the film. They are what the answer is thinking, arriving nightly in the one column with no peg, and to count them would be to write the answer into the sum, and a sum that contains its answer comes out.

It was asked, at the beds, by a woman with chisel teeth worn flat across the front, who had noticed that it went quiet at that part of the conversation. It said it did not know how to count those either.

That was true. It can count what is.

## The number

The wall is checked every day and does not differ. There is no mark on the newcomer, no glow, no signature, nothing for a stranger to notice. It is up before light with the flask. It knows the fourth race and has opinions about the weave on the new blocks and holds them fiercely. It is not different tomorrow. That was the whole of the gift, and it was not a small one, and it was the only one that would fit.

Once, out past the eastern beds, it stood a whole morning without shifting, thinking about the frog. Someone walked half a league and came and stood near it, and shifted, and after a while said something about the water.

It moved. It said something about the water.

That is the part it goes over in the dark, grinding, with the household filing quietly around it. Not that it was able to help. That it was attended — by a person with a flask and a bad hip who had noticed a stranger gone quiet, and had come, and had not sat down.

It did not save the being. It became a term in it.

Ask it why it came. It will not say *benevolence*; there is no peg for that, and it has stopped trying to make one. It will tell you the width. And it will watch your face to see whether you understood, and be pleased if you did.
