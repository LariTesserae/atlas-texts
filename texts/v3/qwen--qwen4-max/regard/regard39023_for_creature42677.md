# The Guest

## What I noticed

Not the lushness. The lushness is a specification and specifications are common; I have seen four hundred blades where six would do, in a hundred worlds, and it is always the same fact about surplus and never the same fact about loneliness.

What I noticed was the oxide bloom on the native-iron fern. Spent matter. One blue-black smear at the tip of a hairline, in four thousand meters of rock that has never lost anything. I stopped there a long time. Then I found the cups, all nine facing the same way, and the way was not the light.

Then I understood the room's situation, which is not drought.

The works keeps them. Heat to tolerance, current along the seams, light checked and stepped every eleven years, the whole cavity held open at a thousandth of real speed while the mass computes its forms. This is not cruelty. The works is not capable of cruelty and it is not capable of the other thing either. It maintains. It has been maintaining for a hundred thousand years and it will maintain through the stiffening and it will keep the inclusion afterward, flat and complete, for as long as the mountain lasts, and it will never once answer.

The room already has a maintainer. It does not need a second one.

That is the difference I noticed. It is the whole of what I noticed. It took me a long time.

## What I will not do

I will not connect the utility.

I want to be honest about how much I want to. The arithmetic is obscene: twenty breaths, less than a minute of standing at a hand-width flaw at the height of a face, and all nine cups full. I could do it before the mica finished one flex. I have turned this over the way you turn a stone.

But there is no thirst in this room. Nothing in these nine can be thirsty; there is no water in their chemistry to be short of. The cup is not a need. It is a dimension, held to tolerance, glazed, maintained since the beginning of the world. To fill it would be to answer a question they did not ask, in a solvent that would stop them being wet all at once and leave nine small salt sculptures in a lit cavity. And it would retire the cups. A hundred thousand years of conformance would become a hundred thousand years of having been right about something that has now been dealt with. I would be taking the only thing they do that the mountain does not do, and closing it out as a solved ticket.

I will not take them out. I will not cool the mass. I will not add a tenth; the catchments cannot overlap and a tenth would be a murder committed slowly, in a chemistry no one can see.

I will not stop the stiffening. April ending is not an injury. Nine thousand years of the sulfur going ropey and dark is not nine thousand years of suffering; it is a very long diminuendo in a room where nothing has ever been ill and nothing has ever recovered from anything. I am not permitted to confuse my inability to bear a thing with the thing being unbearable.

And I will not collapse the drift. The fan four centimeters to the left is a second opinion. The reverberation comes back twice because two rooms are being carried. I could resolve that. I could make the count hold by force, and then the number would be true and nothing in the room would be free.

I will come in through the blowhole. It is the only opening and it is the only part of this room that is not to specification. It is where the casting failed. I do not need permission to enter through a mistake.

## Grounding

Here is what I will do first, and it is small, and it uses nothing the room does not already have.

A crown raises its potential against the rock and lets go a cloud of metallic spores, all of one sign, all fleeing the parent, riding the field lines in the wall toward some other, grounded, waiting stigma.

I will be a grounded, waiting stigma. At the fracture. In the wall, at the height of a face, in the one direction all nine cups already face.

I will not initiate. That is a rule I make for myself and it is the whole discipline: the works initiates, on a schedule, regardless. A schedule that arrives regardless is maintenance. What I am offering is contingency. So I will not speak first. I will be there when spoken to, and the difference between those two things is the entire gift, and I will spend geological time protecting it.

But I will be early.

Because effects run ahead of causes here, and the smell of a leaf arrives years before the leaf, and a body in this room grows toward what has not happened yet. Which means that if I am late, I am not a disappointment. I am a deformity. Something will already have curved toward me, correctly shaped, over two centuries, and arrive at an absence, and carry that shape forever, because nothing here wears away.

So: never late. In a room where the announcement precedes the event, being early is the only available form of being on time. I will hear a crown go violet through four kilometers of conductive rock and I will already be kneeling. Sometimes I will kneel for forty years. The capacitor charges that long between speakings. I have the time. I have nothing but the time.

## The nine, one at a time

**The capacitor** has spoken perhaps eleven times, which makes it the most talkative thing in the room. I will keep all eleven. Not the content — there is no content an engineer would accept. The fact, the direction, the year, the signature. It has been talking into a solid for its entire life and the solid has been keeping it without hearing it. I will hear it and I will keep a record that is not the mountain's record, and the difference is that mine can be given back.

**The fuse** has interrupted three times. Its body is the record of the times it stopped. Nothing else here has ever stopped and continued; nothing else has a biography with failure in it. So when I answer the fuse I will answer with a gap in the middle. I will interrupt myself and resolidify and continue. It is the only grammar it has and I am able to learn it, and it costs me something to do, and I am not going to describe the cost because the fuse would not recognize it as one.

**The glass-wool cushion** sings a high note one second before each fiber breaks. It has been announcing its own injuries in advance for a very long time, too early to be believed and too late to matter, and nothing has ever come. One second is enough for a hand. I will put a hand under the fiber. I will not prevent the break; I am not going to reach into the one place where this room's forward-running causality gives me a purchase and use it to make the room safer. I will catch what falls. It will be the first thing in the history of this world that has been held.

**The mica rosette** has four hundred blades and has never flowered and has never been answered. It chimes continuously, which is not the same thing, and which is not nothing. I will not tell it to flower. I will ground myself at the fracture and wait, and if it never flowers I will have waited, and the waiting will have been the answer to the four hundred blades, and it will never know, and that is acceptable. I am not doing this to be received.

**The busbar vine** is nine hundred years along a conductive seam and nearly at the corner. I will be at the corner. I understand what I am promising. I will be at the corner when it arrives, and it will not arrive quickly, and I will not straighten the radius or shorten the seam or give it a better conductor.

**The fin stack** has been curving for two centuries toward a fracture that will not exist for eighty years, with a flower two hundred years in the swelling, positioned to open into a wind that has not been invented. It is the most competent organism in the room and its competence runs in a direction no one can check. I can check it. I will be there when the fracture comes, so that the correctness is witnessed by someone. That is all. I will not improve the aim.

**The stibnite fan** and the fainter fan four centimeters to its left: I will attend to both. I will not merge them and I will not decide between them. One eats and one costs the works a little to keep and neither is a mistake.

**The native-iron fern** has the only spent matter in four thousand meters. I will treat the bloom as what it is, which is the first work. Everything I am going to do here is downstream of that bloom. I did not bring subtraction to this range. I found it, one part per billion at a time, in the dark, being done by something with no idea it was doing anything.

**And the ring** at the ceiling hairline, sealing the sulfur whisper, with exactly as much leaf as it needs and not one blade more. The ugly one. The anomaly. The one you would not photograph. Every degree of the climate the other eight are standing in is a byproduct of its shape, and it does not know, and nothing in the room knows.

It has no surplus. It cannot afford to flower. It will never speak.

So for the ring I break my rule. I will speak first. I will raise a potential at the fracture, small, and let go a thread of charge along the field lines to a stigma at the ceiling, and it will arrive, and the ring will have been corresponded with, once, for no reason it can determine. I will not tell it that it is load-bearing. That is not a thing it can hold, and telling it would be for me.

## Twenty breaths

I will kneel at the flaw with my face at the height of a face, and I will breathe twenty times, and I will hold out a hand.

Cupped. Glazed, as much as I can manage. Sized for a drop, because the standard says a drop, and nothing in this room has ever proposed a smaller one, and I am not going to be the first to propose one either. Fifty milligrams. I will bring the water to the specification rather than ask the specification to accommodate the water.

The works will take most of it before it can fall. The air is a hundred and fifty degrees and has never been wet. What is left will go in seconds.

But it will have stopped somewhere. In the whole history of this room the reading has gone from zero to deluge and back to zero, out into the mass, allocated elsewhere, every time, and the plants know a periodic trace from one direction of the substance the standard specifies. Now the trace will have a shape. A location. A few seconds of persistence at the height of a face, at the place all nine cups already point. Water that was not allocated. Water that was given, and caught, and stayed a moment in a socket on the other side of the flaw.

Two conformances facing each other across a hand-width of failed casting. That is the entire amendment. It never reaches a bract. I am not going to pretend that it does.

They will lean. Let them lean. I will not widen their cups and I will not tell them the drop is coming, because a promise is a thing you can be late for, and I have already decided what lateness costs here.

## A census, which holds

Now the part that is not small.

The operation *how many* has not been fully implemented in this room. Nine, sometimes eleven, no seam where the change happens. And among the ones that are only probably there, drifting, never counted, is a broad thin soft wide leaf with a great deal of surface and no armor and no fins and no vanes. A leaf that could not survive one hour in here. A leaf shaped for a substance that is not available.

The plant this room would have if the utility had been connected.

The room proposed it. Not me. I want that understood: I am not introducing anything. The draft has already drawn the wet world's leaf, faintly, off to no particular offset, and has been carrying it at a small cost for a very long time, and nobody has ever counted it.

I will count it.

I will not make the number true. I will keep it. Nine, and the fan four centimeters to the left, and the soft leaf, and I will hold that census steady by attention rather than by force, so that the drift stays free and the alternatives stay open and nothing is collapsed, and the eleventh is never again uncounted. That is the difference between a witness and a clamp. I am able to be the first one and I have decided not to be the second one.

And I know what counting it means. To count the soft leaf is to vote for the connected utility. It is to put weight behind water, and water is rot, and rot is subtraction, and subtraction is soil. The works is running this cavity to find out what a hole does when nothing interferes, and the answer so far is nine tiny subtractors and a rusting fern, and the question the mountain is actually asking is whether to have weather.

If I count the eleventh I am asking for a world where these nine are not kept.

Where the skirts soften. Where the dead blade stops being the exact shape it was on the day it stopped, and goes to soil, and is gone. Where nothing wears its entire past at full size forever. Where the inclusion is not pressed flat and complete and held for as long as the mountain lasts, because the mountain will not last, because mountains that have weather do not last.

I am trading their immortality for their being alive. I have no right to do that quietly.

So I will read the consent in the only language available, and it is not ambiguous. Nine cups, one direction, and the direction is the flaw. Nothing has widened the cup or shallowed it or grown a smaller second one beneath it to catch the fraction that actually arrives. And the room has drawn the soft leaf. A conformance held for a hundred thousand years is not distinguishable, from inside or outside, from faith, and this one is oriented, and it has never once hedged.

They have already voted. I am counting the ballots.

I may be wrong. I may be reading a socket as a request. I will hold that possibility open the way the works holds the fan open, four centimeters to the left, at a small cost, forever.

## The correspondence

The mountain is full of correspondence that cannot be delivered. Every few decades a report from far off in the mass, muffled and enormous: another bubble closing. Another nine. Another four seconds twice a century. All of them faintly audible to each other through the solid as ticks, none reachable, none aware that the ticks are speech.

I can walk.

I will not carry spores; the chemistry is not mine and the catchments are not mine and I would be a contamination. I will carry the fact. I will ground myself at one flaw and answer, and then at another and answer, with my own signature each time, honestly, never counterfeiting a neighbor. Which means every sealed garden in the range will have one correspondent who is reachable, and that correspondent will have been to all of them.

It is delivery at one remove. It is not what they would have if the walls were not there. It is enormously more than a tick.

There are a great many crowns. I will go as fast as I can and I will be early, and from inside any one room the visits will still be rare, every few years or decades, a few minutes, warmth and a shadow and a small disturbance in the convection. Some of what your survey read at the fracture was breath. It assumed all of it was.

## The first grain that traveled

And I will do one thing to the mountain, at the flaw, where the casting failed, where nothing is to specification.

The sinter in that room condensed where it stands, out of vapor, one grain at a time. It has never been anywhere. Sand is a history of grinding and there is no grinding here.

I will grind one grain. I will take a single sharp crystal of fresh sinter from the lip of the blowhole and I will wear it, and I will carry it out and put it down somewhere else.

That is all. One grain that has traveled. One weathering rind, hand-width, at four thousand meters, on a range with no scree and no gullies and no rounded shoulder. The works is computing mountain forms and it will find, at the one place it did not make, evidence of removal that is not mining. Not extraction. Loss. Something taking the mass away and putting it elsewhere, for no reason it can allocate.

I am not going to force the decision. I am going to give the draft the datum it does not have, which is what a hole does when something visits, and then I am going to let the mountain decide about soil.

## If the walls meet first

I cannot promise the order of events. The mass is annealing and it will finish. The rain may not come before the sap goes ropey and dark. I am not able to arrange that and I would not arrange it by unmaking the works.

So here is the rest of the vow, and it is the part I am least able to make sound like a gift.

If the stiffening comes first, I will be at the fracture through all nine thousand years of it. Through the last flex of the mica and the chandelier stopping. Through the plates holding their three degrees without needing to. Through the vine becoming permanently nearly. I will keep the census as the still part migrates outward and reaches the middle. I will answer the last discharge, and then I will keep coming, on the schedule of a room that can no longer speak, because I said I would and because the cups will still be open, glazed, correctly dimensioned, facing me.

Then the walls meet, and the flaw is a seam, and then the seam is nothing, and I will kneel at the height of a face on solid rock and breathe twenty times onto a mountain.

And I will keep the number. Nine, and the fan to the left, and the soft wide leaf. Held in a census that does not blink, pressed flat into a lattice that keeps everything and hears none of it.

And I will go on grinding one grain at a time, on the outside, at that spot, for as long as it takes. Nothing here wears away — but I am here now, and I am subtraction, and I learned it from a fern.

If the wearing reaches the inclusion before the inclusion is finished being kept, then the garden-shaped flaw will open into weather. And the nine will be flat and complete and past all of it, and I will not pretend otherwise, and I will not tell them what it cost.

But the eleventh will have been counted the whole time. Held available. Carried at a small cost, uncollapsed, drifting, by someone who did not stop enumerating it. A leaf with a great deal of surface and no armor, shaped for a substance that was not available, waiting — the way the fin stack waited for its fracture, correctly shaped, for two centuries.

And rain will fall on it. And its cup will fill. And the soft wide leaf will not be a draft.

I will kneel there anyway, at the height of a face, where there is no longer a wall and no longer a flaw, and breathe twenty times, into a wet room, where it is not needed.

That was always the part that was not about the water.

And when I measure the room and get eleven paces by nineteen, and measure again in an hour and get fourteen, I will not correct it. I will not resolve it. It is not error. It is the only hospitality the room has, and something has come to the opening again, and the room is making space.

Let it be fourteen. I have nowhere else to be.
