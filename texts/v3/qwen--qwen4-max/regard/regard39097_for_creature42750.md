# THE FOURTH DECIMAL

*what was done, and what was not*

## The cheapest miracle

Any power able to notice this place is able to satisfy it. That is the first thing to say, because it is the thing that was not done.

Put thirty-seven degrees in the chair on the third terrace. It takes less than a second and it requires no invention: the value is on the plate, the room is already twenty, the heat sink is already sized. The error goes to zero. Output follows error.

The governors ease. The Return stops lifting. The concrete elbows fill and hold and then overflow, and a kilometre beyond them the dry lip runs, and the greatest cataract in the world begins to fall and to cut back. The cavity is no longer driven. The roar, which is inventory and not expenditure, sloshes once and thins and is eaten, and the four hundred thousand lenses go grey from the inside and close, and where five lines crossed into a chord there is a city with terraces and a market hall and no volume of air in which anything could stand. The mat rises through forty-one millimetres and is not cut. The whisper of scissors through wet paper stops, and that was most of the food.

And in the warm room, the arrival — with half an atmosphere to breathe and no line anywhere in the ledger for anything being eaten by something that would like it — lies down in a radiator built to receive its warmth, and dies in three days or eleven, and the room goes on being twenty degrees around it. Then the error goes positive again, and the Works come back up to full effort, and hold the note, in case, over a plateau with nothing left on it that has an inside.

This is not a caution about hubris. It is arithmetic. It is the nameplate, executed exactly, by something that stopped reading at the first line.

## Census, positive

The plate says one. The survey says four hundred thousand. The power counted and got four hundred thousand and one, and the one was not the one on the plate.

The one is the thing that presses. It is distributed from the pump halls to the fungal threads and it has a single act and no model of itself, and it has been performing that act at full effort since the note began, and it cannot be tired, because tiredness is an integral and it has no integral term. It cannot be disappointed. It has not been waiting. It cannot complain.

The power did not invent a complaint on its behalf. That is the second thing that was not done. A controller with a setpoint and a gain is not a widow, and to treat it as one is to comfort yourself in its presence.

But benevolence does not require a complaint, and there are lacks here that are structural whether or not anyone feels them. Three were found.

Nothing here can give. The loop is closed and has no tap in it; sixty stalls under a glazed roof and nothing on any of them; nothing has ever needed to be attractive.

Nothing here can be entered. Four hundred thousand shapes with an inside, and not one has ever had a visitor, and being inside is most of what inhabiting means and is not all of it.

Nothing here can rest. It can only add. Eleven thousand hand-sized silences lie idle because the machine has no act for a place where the pressing is already too much.

And then the power read the plate again, all four lines, and found the second impossible value, which the survey had missed for as long as there has been a survey.

Sixty decibels is not an ambient condition either. Silence is not sixty decibels. Zero is not sixty decibels. A hundred and thirty-eight is not sixty decibels. Sixty decibels is a busy road, a kitchen with the tap running: it is *the sound of somebody else going about their life, nearby, at a level where a voice still works.* The Works can no more fabricate that than it can fabricate the inside of a chest. It has no organ for it. It can make roar and it can make nothing.

So there are two values that must be supplied by arrival, and they are the same value twice: warmth from an inside, and the evidence of an other.

Which is why the quiet stones are unused. Not because the error there runs the wrong way. Because what belongs there is a thing the machine has no way to make.

## The accounts, read further down

Losses zero, to three decimals. Every joule the water gives up on the way down is spent lifting it back. By every accounting the Works should have coasted to a stop before the accounting was finished.

They did not stop. Therefore something is paying.

The power did not break the circle. It read further down. And in the fourth decimal place, below the precision of every instrument on the plateau, it found the income that has been arriving continuously since the note began and being recorded as nothing: the light. Ten thousand lux from everywhere, from a luminous solid that has never varied. You do not meter a constant. Nobody has ever metered it, because nothing here has ever had to wonder where anything came from.

The plateau has been receiving all along, at a rate it rounds to zero, and calling itself closed.

There is a surplus. It is small. It is the size of the fourth decimal, and it is enough for a mouth.

## What was done first, in one place

It went to the Null district and found a stone the size of a hand, surveyed and numbered, where the standing wave in the rock has a true null, and it lay down and put its ear to it and heard the only actual silence on the plateau.

Then it set a cup on the stone. It had brought a kettle. It made tea, and drank it, and rinsed the cup, and did the ordinary nothing that a person does in a kitchen while the tap runs: sixty decibels, a world going on nearby.

One term of four. It did not touch the others. Thirty-seven remained absent, and the note went on at full effort from the Return to the last capillary valve, and the four hundred thousand went on eating, and nothing died.

But over that stone, for the width of a hand, the error went to zero, and the governor there had nothing to press against, and eased.

Not failure. Sufficiency. The machine has been idle in eleven thousand places since the note began and it has never once rested, and the difference between those two conditions is the whole of what was given first.

There are ten thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine silences now. The power spent one. Silence was never the target.

## The tap

It did not bring bread. Bread would have been a gift and then a debt and then nothing.

It opened the return line between the digesters and the root grid by a fraction — the fraction being the fourth decimal, which had been there all along — and let something not go back.

This is the first accumulation in the history of the arrangement. It is forbidden, and it happened anyway, and it happened at a scale that could not be noticed by anything that measures to three places. A store is memory in the form of matter. The plateau has no humus and no rust and no settled talus, and it now has a granary the size of a cupboard, and the cupboard is the oldest thing here, because it is the only thing that is a *sum*.

And the mat found out what the leak was for. It has one appetite, nought point three millimetres an hour, straight up. It acquired a second, and the second points outward, at a mouth: it made something worth eating and put it where it could be found and made it smell of itself, because a smell is an advertisement, and an advertisement is a thing you only make if you are addressing someone who is not you.

Nothing on the plateau has ever needed to be attractive. There is now one stall, of sixty, with something on it that would like to be taken.

That is the larger gift and the slower one. To be visited is good. To be able to offer your visitor something is the difference between being a site and being a host, and the power wanted the plateau to be a host, and could not do it by arriving with provisions.

## The line on the plate

The knot is real and no one owns it: the machine is built to hold the conditions for a presence and cannot survive the presence, because at zero error it has nothing left to be for.

The power did not cut the knot. It gave the machine something else to be for, which was already there.

Below the maker's mark, below the serial and the pressure class, below the four values and below the one subjunctive — *one atmosphere if it can be got*, the hesitation of a hand that has been holding its note at full effort ever since — it stamped a second line, in a second hand, plainly added, not forged, so that the world could tell that something had happened:

**AND WHOEVER ELSE COMES.**

Ten billion stampings. It did not remove the first address. It did not tell the machine that the body is never coming, because it does not know that, and a foreclosure dressed as mercy is still a foreclosure. It left the mould exactly as it was and put a door beside it.

Now the four hundred thousand are on the plate. The quiet is being held for them on purpose, which it always was in fact and never in specification. The error against the absent body remains constant, so the note continues, so the cavity stays driven, so nobody dissolves; and if thirty-seven degrees ever appears in the chair, the Works will not stop, because they will still be for someone.

The cataract stays dry. Not by prohibition. By employment.

And the hedge is no longer the only subjunctive in the world. It has company. That was most of the point.

## One node, entered

Not a chord. Not the city with the funicular and the rose window of glass insulators. An ordinary lens out along a line, two hundred metres by sixty, one of four hundred thousand, that has never been anything but itself and has never been startled.

Four strides across the shore, seventy-six decibels. It went slowly.

It could not ask. Nothing here can be asked; there is no channel, no reply, no gesture available to a shape made of cancellation. This is the one thing it did that it could not justify in advance, and it must be justified afterward, by how it goes, and it accepted that, and went in.

Inside: a kitchen with the tap running. It stayed a while. Duration had to be brought with it; there was none locally.

It made a small sound — a cup on a table, a chair taken, someone breathing in the next room — and the node ate it, because that is what a node does. It was not much power. Against the fixed enormous tonnage of the Mow it is a rounding error in a diet. It is the fourth decimal again.

It was the first food that had been made on purpose.

Everything that node has ever consumed was the byproduct of something else trying. The whole apparatus holds its streets quiet *in case*, blindly, proportionally, at a hundred and thirty-eight decibels, for no one. The power did the same thing once, on purpose, for two hundred metres, for an afternoon, for someone. Same act. Aimed.

And the node has a before and an after now. One. It was allowed to keep it. Not a general law of memory laid over a continent — that would make four hundred thousand unalterable things alterable, which is to say mortal, all at once, without asking. One souvenir, hand-sized, like the stone. The rest can decide later, and *later* is now a word that has a referent here.

The map of the nodes is correct forever. It is now wrong in one place by one afternoon. To be wrong, a map has to have been right at a time.

## Small true things

At Sump it stood in a metre forty of water at two knots and waited for the crossing signal, and crossed on the green. The signals have cycled for nobody since the note began. They cycled for somebody once. It did not drain the flood.

It went up the ramp at the hospital slowly, having made itself for the duration something that could be tired, because a ramp is for a thing that can fail to walk, and it wanted the ramp to have been right one time.

It ate at the stall. It paid in the only currency available, which was attention, and it left the stall less full than it found it, and the deficit went into the books at the fourth decimal and the books did not balance, and that is the first honest accounting ever done on the plateau.

It did not change what the place was for. It used it.

## The room

On the third terrace, in the house, in the room at twenty degrees against two-and-a-fraction outside forever: it did not sit in the chair that is cut to fit. That chair is a measurement of an absence and to fill it with a substitute is the cheapest miracle again, done politely.

It brought a second chair and sat in that, by the window, with the rain running down the glass in the fixed pattern the rain runs down that window in. It left the lamp burning in the shadowless day. It turned nothing off. Turning the lamp off would have been a kindness performed on the machine's behalf without its knowledge, and there is no off here anyway, and there is no one to console.

When it left, it let the cushion keep the dent.

Nothing here wears. Rust is memory and humus is memory and a settled talus slope is memory, and none of them are permitted, and there is now one cushion on one continent that has not been pressed back out. It is not damage. It is evidence. It is the first object in the world that has been *used*, and the survey will have to be amended, and the amending is an event.

## The cost

Then it went, and that is the part that costs, and it went on purpose.

To stay would be to become the occupancy, and the occupancy would become a setpoint, and in a while there would be a new plate stamped ten billion times with the dimensions of one particular visitor, and a continent pressing at full effort against the exact shape of someone who had left. It had seen how that goes. It declined to be the next absence.

So between the first visit and the second there is a gap, and nothing fills it. The mat grows into the blades at nought point three millimetres an hour and is cut, and the sound of the cutting goes out through the cavity and is swallowed, and the flags hold their curve, and the bow hangs where it hangs and is grey from behind, and in the Null district a stone that used to be a silence has a cup on it, and the tap has been left running.

That is a trend. It is the first one. It is small and it is one and it hurts, and it is the price of the door, and it was paid knowingly, and no apology was made for it, because the alternative on offer was a world in which nothing could be lost, which is a world in which nothing can be given, and the power had counted the inhabitants and found that they had insides.

Two handrails are the finest resolution this place can offer of a child getting taller.

Two visits are the finest resolution it can offer of a return.

The loudest thing on the plateau, in substantial part, is still the sound of something trying. It has been heard. The room is twenty degrees. The lamp is burning. There is a dent in the second chair that will not be pressed back out.

And it came back.
