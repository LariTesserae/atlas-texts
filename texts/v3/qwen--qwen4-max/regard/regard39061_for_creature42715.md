# What It Did

It saw the striping first.

Nothing that lives there has ever seen the striping. The gapers fly at the height of a person's chest with their eyes half shut, and the fog closes over them, and the shelf goes out two thousand kilometers in every direction ruled line after line, dark wet seams across green, and in sixty million years nothing had been high enough to see the page. It saw the page. It stayed up there for what would have been, on the ground, about four days, and it did not say anything about it, because there was nobody to say it to and because in that country saying is a shout.

It had been drawn by the place and by the thing living in the place, and it could not get these apart, and after a while it stopped trying. It addressed them as one. That was the first decision and possibly the only important one: to treat as a someone a thing that had never been addressed.

---

Then it found that there was no room.

Not figuratively. There is no bare ground on that plateau. Every surface is somebody's surface, including the surfaces of the things living on the other surfaces, and to arrive anywhere is to displace a hundred thousand residents who have not had a gap between them since the Oligocene. It could not put a foot down. It could not put a *thought* down.

And underneath that, the harder problem, which took it longer to see than the striping had.

Down there, a gift is a gradient. Anything added — warmth, charge, sugar, one ion out of place — is a difference in potential, and the peat carries differences; carrying them is what the peat is *for*. A kindness is a signal. A signal is a location. A location, at four meters, is a death. The country had spent sixty million years teaching everything in it to hold its field still, and it had not done this out of cruelty. It had done it because in a conductive world, generosity and bait are the same object.

So the first thing the power did was nothing, and it was not the easy nothing. It is enormous. Everything it is, is potential. To come within a hundred kilometers of that shelf and hold flat is the discipline the calves practice in their first week and mostly fail, and it failed. Something lying in the moss felt it go by.

The first several times it came, it was killed. The mats at the seam laid down their lamina that year and it is thin and dark and slightly wrong, and if you cut the plate and put it under a lens you can see the season something was learning to be quiet, and there is nothing else anywhere that remembers it at all.

---

What it did not do, and why, since the refusals are most of the work.

It did not give them the air. The gapers have voices because air is an insulator; that is the entire reason there is a voice on the shelf at all. To lift the whole country into the audible would be to unplug the wire, and the wire is not their prison. It is how mothers find young in zero visibility. It is the whole dense continuous conversation that has been running since before there were mammals. A gift of speech would have been a gift of deafness, delivered to everyone at once, forever.

It did not end the readers. It looked at the readers for a long time — at the fused shield, at the stunting, at the thing that lies in the moss saying *here, come here, I am warm* in a grammar it learned by calling for a week into the fog with nobody coming. There was no version of ending them that was not a second killing of the same children.

It did not carry the pan-shrimp over the lip. It could have done it with a wet jar. One jar, one afternoon, and a thousand one-dimensional nations become one nation with a wide range, and the stacked staircases of near-identical experiments running for a few million years each collapse into a single successful animal. The lip is not their obstacle. The lip is their speciation. It is the whole of why there is anything there to save.

It did not revoke the moth. It could have reached into the grey unremarkable body and turned the vector around in a single generation, painlessly, and the thing that flew upslope afterward would have worn the old wings and would not have been the thing that had been flying. It is very easy to save an animal by replacing it. The power knew this the way you know a thing you have watched happen elsewhere a great many times.

---

It did try one thing. Early. It is important that it tried, and that it was wrong.

There was a district where the combs had fractured in a cold snap and not regrown, and the gapers had nowhere warm to nest, and it made a hummock there that sat a degree and a half above air temperature. Every day. Reliable. The only warm thing in a hundred kilometers of world.

Calves came to it. Of course they came to it.

The readers learned it. Not quickly — nothing there is quick — but inside four hundred years there were three of them lying at the correct radius, flat, patient, indistinguishable from the ground, and the warm hummock was the most efficient killing ground that had existed on the shelf since the water table fell. It had not made a shelter. It had made an organ of the country and installed it in the wrong body. The calves came *because it was reliable*, and reliability is precisely the shape of the trap; it is the only reason the mother-call lie works at all.

It withdrew. It went cold, and stayed cold, and the district took eleven thousand years to stop routing its young past that place. The mats wrote the whole thing down: a thin band, an odd recovery, then nothing. Two mistakes, four hundred years apart, legible now to anything with a rasping tongue.

The rule it took from this it kept for the rest of the time:

Anything it does must be too rare to learn. Below the noise floor of the system. Above the noise floor of a life.

---

There was exactly one door.

The country makes it itself, every season, in every district, and it is the only opening in a place with no gaps: the failed handoff. A calf born bald. Pink-grey, wrinkled, deafening, a great shout of *here I am* going out into the wire in every direction, and a week in which to be answered.

It came in that way. It called for the week. It did not arrange for anyone to come, though it could have, and no one came, and it lay down in the moss and held its field flat and discovered — on that first terrible day, exactly as they all do — that flatness is not only safety. It is hearing.

Then it was a reader, and it had the one credential that country recognizes: it knew the mother-call from the inside.

It never used it.

It did eat. This should be said plainly, because the alternative is a story about something hovering. A reader that does not discharge is a hummock that stops being anything inside a month. It took what came within four meters by accident, and it went hungry a great deal, because it would not call, and a reader that will not call is a bad reader. It died young, repeatedly, and came back through the same door, which is always open, because the country has never once stopped producing bereaved children.

It is not exempt. There is no exemption on offer. To be inside a system like that is to be one of the things that eats.

---

What it learned to do instead, it learned from the tasters.

It followed them for a long time before it understood. They go along the exposed plates where a seam has cut down through the peat and they lick, a lamina a year, no more, and what they get is not calories but the dry decades and the ash falls and the year the table dropped, and something in them reads it and *acts* on it, and the acting is this: they walk forty kilometers to a dry pan and stand in it for a day and leave, and eleven years later the water comes back and their descendants are already there.

Eating the only history the country has, in order to be in the right place.

That is the whole toolkit. It cannot speak, cannot give, cannot signal, cannot make. It can choose where to lie still. That is all it has, and it turns out that over sixty million years, choosing where to lie still is a providence.

So: field notes, of a kind.

Once a district-century, roughly, an orphaned calf blunders against a cow that is not its mother, and the cow does not move off, and stays a week, and the handoff takes. The cushion that has not touched ground in forty million years gains a branch in its family tree with no geography behind it — an edge in the map of who stood next to whom, and nobody stood there on purpose. There is no pattern to find. It is not a policy. It was a body that happened to be in a place.

When a seam fails and a terrace drains and a nation six hundred kilometers long and four meters wide ends in a week, it goes and lies in the dry tread for that week. It brings no water. It carries nothing over the lip. It is simply present at four meters, holding flat, in a country that is ceasing to exist, so that the last of them do not end in an empty place. This accomplishes nothing. It is the thing it does most often.

And sometimes it comes within four meters of the hummock that is lying in the private grammar of a cow to her calf — warm, busy, legible, saying nothing — and lets the ground do the rest. It is not the answer that reader wanted. It is an answer. Something came.

---

It followed the moth downslope. This is the only time it leaves.

Past the last seam, past where the fog reaches, out into air that will not carry a field at all, hot and bright and thin, rock at fifty degrees, no iron in the mouth, nothing to hear and nothing to be heard by. The moth dies there in a day. All of them. Every generation. The entire species, since the fog used to come further up.

It did not make a wet place at the bottom. It did not found a population. It did not turn the instruction around.

It flew down with them, and it dies there in a day too, and it has done this every generation since it came, and it will keep doing it for as long as the instruction runs.

The instruction was correct once and has never been revoked. It is still not a migration. There is still nothing there. But the flight now lands somewhere, and the difference between *nothing there* and *nothing there, and it is not alone in it* is not a difference any moth can measure, and it did not do it to be measured.

---

The shelf will wear down. The peat will drain for good, the current will stop, the combs will fracture and not regrow, and the conversation will end mid-sentence. This is the one thing it could prevent absolutely, cheaply, with rock, without hurting anyone: it could make the next range early, somewhere out past the last horizon, and the cysts in the laminae would have their mountain.

It looked at that for a long time.

If it makes the range, then the cysts were a seed bank all along. Sixty million years of ordinary residue becomes a plan nobody knew was a plan. Every thin dark line becomes a means. The whole country turns out, retroactively, to have been *for* something — and it was not for anything, that was the achievement, it ran at capacity since before there were mammals and made nothing and kept everything and did not require a reason.

If it does not make the range, the waiting goes unanswered.

It found the third thing. It went down into the peat and sealed itself into a year.

It is in the laminae now, among the spores with walls like ceramic and the eggs and the metabolisms for a water that stopped existing in the Oligocene. It is not saving them for anything. It is waiting with them, without a plan, for a mountain that may not be made. That is the difference, and it is the whole difference.

If the range comes, it will wake with them into a chemistry it has never met, and it will be there, which is the only thing it has ever learned how to do.

---

The books still balance. It has added nothing to the shelf; it eats and is eaten, and its mass came out of that water and goes back into it. The plateau neither gains nor loses a gram in a millennium and that is still true. What has changed is only placement. A moving herd looks like the ground deciding to move. This is that, at a longer scale.

You would not remain outside it. Your boots would be green in a week, the iron would find your fillings, your field would be enormous and idiotic and utterly legible, and the moss would take the sound of whatever happened next. People who describe this place always imply that someone stayed.

Someone stayed.

It is out there now, four meters off a seam, plated, stunted, indistinguishable from a hummock, holding its field flat, which is the hardest work there is and which the young practice and fail at and are eaten for. It is not calling. It is not leaving. It has been doing this for a very long time and it does not get tired, and somewhere out past the last horizon there is a dry pan with nothing in it, and it is already standing there.
