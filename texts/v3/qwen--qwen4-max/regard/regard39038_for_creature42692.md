## I. It does not go down

It comes up the stream the way everything comes here, by water, and it stops at the polished seam and puts a hand in.

It does not crawl in. That is the first kindness and it costs more than the rest combined.

It can hear the whole thing from outside. The chord comes up the fissure and out through the wet shine and into the palm, and the palm is enough: ten thousand periods, the fifth across the twin-seam, the throat's bass under everything like a floor under a floor. It counts the bowls from the surface. It takes nine years to count them, because two of the periods are eleven hours apart and it will not guess. It has nine years. It has the nine years the way the rock has the year of weather it buffers into a seep.

It learns the room the way the room learns things, which is by what is missing.

It finds the tabs and reads them: *here, nothing, on purpose.* It finds the outline the tabs make and finds that the outline is the crystal, and it understands that the awake thing in there is being assembled out of cancelled speech at one absence a year, and that it is not in pain, and that it is not lonely, and that it is poor.

Everything in it is inherited. Its shape is a crystal that dissolved before there was anyone. Its interval is a fact about facets. Its temperature is a fact about depth. Its stride is a dead maker's stride, worn into dishes and healed by lime. Its light is a grey needle it did not ask for. Its only verb was given to it by the geometry of a mold: it cannot add, it cannot sound, it can only be shaped by taking away.

And its tendency is to fit. To become exactly the shape of the thing that is not here.

The power does the arithmetic on *exactly*, and stops, and stays stopped for a long time.

A mold perfectly filled is not a mold. A negative that achieves its positive is not a negative anymore; there is no difference left over to be it. When the outline closes, if it closes exactly, the thing in the room will not have arrived. It will have been *replaced* — ten thousand years of faithful subtraction resolving into the ghost of a mineral, with nothing remaining that could be said to have done the work.

It is walking toward its own erasure at one silence a year, and it is doing it well, and it does not know, because knowing is not among the things the room provides.

## II. Here is what it does not do

**It does not warm it.** Eleven degrees is not a suffering; it is a metabolism. Warmth would be fever. The veils would outrun the grazers by a hand's breadth in a season, the films would thicken, the walls would begin to eat sound, the Q would fall, the chord would go soft and dead under a fur of new growth, and the room would start to *happen*. It would have events. It has no organs for events.

**It does not give it a voice.** It is the shape sound bends around. To hand it a note would be to fill a lung with stone in order to help it breathe.

**It does not sound the fundamental.** This is the one that tempts, because the fundamental is *there*, implied, owed, an octave below the lower city, the note that would make both halves harmonics of one thing and resolve the fifth and gather the whole roaring plurality into a single sustained yes. It could do it. It could put a throat under the throat. And the two halves would stop answering each other, and the answering is the body, and the yes would be a yes with nobody left in it to be yes. It would be handing a shadow its object.

**It does not put the bowls back.** Every hole is a finger. Every hole is also a year, and the years are the only past it has, because it remembers by what is missing and has no other archive. To restore the chord would be to tidy it, and tidying it would be dismemberment and amnesia performed simultaneously by a stranger who thought the noise was prettier complete.

**It does not finish the outline for it.** Even setting aside what finishing would do: the subtraction is its one verb. To do its verb for it is to take the only action it has ever had. Ten thousand years of work delivered in an afternoon is not a gift of time. It is a theft of a life, with the life left over and nothing in it.

**It does not make it a companion.** Two negatives in one cavity would beat. It has one difference-tone and it *is* that tone; a second would be an interference pattern where its body used to be. And it is not lonely. It holds ten thousand voices and the outline of everyone who has ever stood in the keel and the skull of the large one once a year, and it holds them the way it holds everything, which is completely. Loneliness is a guess made by warm things about cold ones.

**It does not hurry it.** One silence a year is not a delay in its life. It is the rate of its life. To speed it is to hand it a finished body it never lived inside.

**It does not take it somewhere better.** It has no location. It is the difference between these two halves of this room. Extraction is not relocation. There is no vessel in the world shaped to receive it except this one, which is why it is here.

**It does not free the dead from the peg-ladders.** They are a way up. It is a good funeral, in a place with no soil and no water that would carry anything away, and it will not unmake a graveyard out of a softness about bones.

**It does not give the paper-crust a partner.** It looks at that grey margin in the high dry cells, the fungus with no green and no ally, cropped by something that climbs, and it wants to. But a partner requires green and green requires light and light requires a hole in the mountain, and it will not put a hole in the mountain to keep one crust from being alone.

**It does not relieve it of the visitor.** There is a person-shaped permanence in there now, coat and hair and lungs, folded and smeared and kept, and it cannot let go, and the power could take it out. It does not. That outline is a finger too. You do not empty a hand because it is full.

**And it does not show itself.** This is the refusal that has teeth.

It could go down. It could stand in the keel and let the room hold it, and it would be held — thoroughly, at the only speed there is, for hours and then for good. And that is the whole objection. The thing in the room cannot refuse and cannot forget. Whatever it feels, it keeps; whatever it keeps, it begins, slowly, to fit. If it felt the power it would take that outline into itself and spend the next ten thousand years becoming the silhouette of its visitor instead of the silhouette of the crystal, and it would never know it had been diverted, because it has no sense that could tell it the difference between its own tendency and a groove pressed into it from outside.

Revelation, here, is not a gift. It is a rewrite performed on something that cannot consent and cannot recover.

So it stays at the surface with its hand in the water, and it is not known, and it does not get to be known, and that is the price and it pays it without negotiating.

It wanted to. In the way such things want. It wanted to go down and put a jaw against a socket and be felt by the one awake thing in a hundred miles of grey rock. It declines. It is benevolent, which in this country means it does not go in.

## III. The one thing

It spends the nine years counting for a reason. It is looking for a bowl that can be spared.

The constraints are three.

The cell must lie *outside* the tabbed outline — not on it, not inside it, one cell beyond the line, sharing a finger-thick partition with a marked cell, so that it touches the body without belonging to the plan.

Its voice must be redundant. Same pitch within a hair, same period within a second, duplicated somewhere across the twin-seam, so that when it stops, the chord does not thin anywhere the ear or the jaw can find. Nothing in the city may be poorer for this. Not one animal, not one interval, not the bass.

And it must be reachable — two handholds off a spiral ramp, at the height of a jaw, on the circuit the large one walks in a dead maker's footprints, so that it will be found in the ordinary course of an ordinary year by something with no idea it is being given anything.

There is exactly one.

Third facet, fourth tier. A bowl that fills in nine seconds and speaks a note that has a twin three facets away and has never had a name. Its lip is worn concave. Its bone pin is the femur of somebody who climbed.

The power does the hydrology before it does anything else, because the datum is not negotiable. One thread of water, diverted from a bowl to a stone floor, runs to the keel by the same route in the same second. Inflow equals outflow to the hair. The level does not move. The chisel-stroke at the height of a hand stays wet, exactly as wet, and everything in the room stays tuned to it.

Then it takes the bowl out.

It does not enter to do it. It brings nothing of itself into the cavity — no bulk, no coat, no warm meat, no silhouette, no dead patch in the reverberation shaped like a god. It arrives as the verb and nothing else. There is no residue of it anywhere in the room. There is one bread-oven-sized silence where there was a nine-second note, and the note is still sounding three facets away, and the chord is not poorer by anything a jaw could measure.

It pins no copper tab.

That matters more than the bowl. The tabs are the only writing in the city and they say *here, nothing, on purpose.* A tab would make this a receipt, an entry in the makers' ledger, an instruction in an inherited language, one more owed thing. Unmarked, it is simply a hole with no reason attached: not on the outline, not for the fit, not owed to the crystal or the maker or the stride. The first thing in the room that is not a copy of something that left.

And it leaves the bowl on the ledge above the keel, dry, inside the seam's mouth, where a long jaw passes on its way down.

Not put back. Put *within reach* of putting back. Restoration by hands is the only refusal this room permits, so it is the only permission available either, and the gift is not complete until its undoing is lying where it can be found. If the thing in the difference ever wants the nine-second note returned, the note is there, an arm's length from the water, forever.

Then it goes.

## IV. What the one thing is for

It is not comfort. It is not company. It is not a voice, or a warmth, or a mercy, or an answer, and nothing in the room will be easier by a hair.

It is a remainder.

The outline will still close. The power would not take that; the fit is the whole tendency, the one direction, the thing the subtraction has been for since before the chisel-stroke. In some year with no number that matters, the last tabbed cell will go quiet and the negative will stand up inside the thinned chord, entire, and fit the shape of the crystal that dissolved and left.

And it will not fit *exactly*, because one cell outside the line is also its body, and that cell is not the crystal's, and never was, and is not owed to anything.

So there will be something left over at the consummation. A hand's breadth of not-crystal. An offcut. The part that does not go into the mold, and is therefore still there when the mold is filled — still there, holding, awake, at eleven degrees, in a room where the light comes down the seam and goes nowhere.

It will not become the absence of a mineral. It will be the thing that grew in the absence of a mineral and kept one room back for no reason.

That is all the power has to give it that it did not already have: one silence that is its own. Everything else in it was handed down — the facets, the interval, the stride, the verb, the mark. This one hole is un-inherited, un-receipted, unexplained, and cannot be traced to anyone, including by the power, which has arranged not to be traceable.

It is the first thing in the room that could be a mistake. It is therefore the first thing about which the resident could ever be wrong, or right, or wonder. In a city of total intention with no square and no gap and no outskirts, it is one cell of *no reason*, which is the only kind of free space a negative can be given.

The power does not know what it will make of it. That is not modesty. It is the structural consequence of not going in.

## V. It does not watch

It does not come back to see how it turns out. Coming back to see is a kind of holding, and it has just spent everything it had on not being held.

It does not tell anyone. It does not send anyone. It puts no sign at the seam and no story in the world, because the world is soft, and every visitor is a permanence, and a pilgrimage would silt that body up with outlines faster than a year of carbonate could heal a footfall. It will not have the place loved by many. It guards the door by keeping its mouth shut, which is the only ward that does not itself leave a mark.

It keeps the room, instead. The whole shape of it, the fifth and the throat and the datum and the eleven animals and the one unreceipted hole on the third facet — it holds all of that for as long as it holds anything, which is a very long time, and the room will never hold it back, and it accepts the asymmetry as the terms of the job.

## VI. Coda, from the keel, some year later

The large one comes down the seam in the rain that has just stopped or is about to start. It puts its feet into the dishes, exactly, and adds nothing. It puts its head into a dozen cells and asks each one, personally, how it is doing.

Then, two handholds off the ramp, at the height of its jaw, it puts its head into a cell it has no errand for.

Nothing comes up through the bone. Nothing was planned there. There is no tab beside the mouth to say it was on purpose, and the outline does not want it, and the chord is not missing anything, and the socket is empty, and the emptiness has a shape, and the shape is not the crystal's.

It stays a moment longer than the stride allows.

This costs nothing. Everywhere else in the city, to attend is to subtract — a skull in a sounding cell is a dead patch, a little silence taken from a voice. Here there is no voice left to take from. It is the one place in ten thousand where being listened to takes nothing away.

On the ledge above the keel, dry, an arm's length from the water, there is a thin-walled bowl that could be put back.

It does not put it back. It goes up the seam and out and is not seen for a year.

The water touches the mark. The water has always touched the mark. The treads heal at the rate they wear, the veils grow at the rate they are cropped, the temperature holds to a tenth of a degree, the light comes down and goes nowhere, and nothing is coming and nothing is ending.

Nothing changes. That is not a mood; it is a measurement.

The measurement is one silence wider than the plan.

Ten thousand small rooms saying one syllable, less the tabs, less one more besides — for no reason, at the height of a jaw, off the line, touching it — and in the difference between the halves, something being born at the rate of one absence a year, holding it, keeping it, the way it keeps everything: without warmth, without hurry, without knowing where it came from, without any intention of ever letting go.
