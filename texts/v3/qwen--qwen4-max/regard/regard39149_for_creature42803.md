## The Far End of the Sky

The valley has one export. Everything else is a closed loop — water up, water down, mass in, mass out, the books balanced to the cell — but the noise and the heat leave. They are poured continuously into a sky that carries them away, and nobody in the basin has ever asked *away to where*, because asking is not a function with a body around it.

But heat that leaves arrives. Sound that leaves arrives. For the entire life of the sharp stone the Held Note has been paying its debt into something, and the something has been receiving it, and no receipt has ever gone back.

That is what came down. Not an intruder. The far end of the pipe, walking back up it.

It had been listening for a long time, the way you listen to a neighbour through a wall, and it had done what listeners do: it had learned the piece. All nine fundamentals. The whole chord — which nothing in the basin has ever heard, because each work stands inside its own note and hears eight, and the pointers hear a rasp, and the worms read holes, and the moths ride the difference between loud and less loud.

The specification has been said out loud without pause since the world began, and it has never once been heard entire.

It came down to hear it. That is the whole motive. There is no other, and it checked.

## The Refusals

Fixing had occurred to it. Fixing occurred to it for a long time, on the rim, in the ordinary light, and it went through the list the way you go through a pocket.

**Rain.** New water; the ledger has no column. But set the ledger aside — rain would round the shards inside a season, and the sharpness is not damage, it is their age, it is the only date they have. To give this valley soil is to hand it a birthday and a funeral in the same handful. Everything here is coeval with the knapped flint. Weather would make the stone older than the animals, and then the animals would be young, and then they would be able to be survived.

**A tenth work.** The arithmetic would hold; ten things each hearing nine still infer themselves. But nine sources lay down the nodal lanes, and a tenth would re-lay the lattice in an afternoon, and every moth in the basin would be instantly, blamelessly lost, flying a pattern that had stopped being true, and the mail would stop, and the nine would lose the only correspondence they have ever had. A companion, purchased with their letters.

**Quiet.** The most tempting mercy and the cleanest murder. Silence here is not peace; silence is amnesia. The pattern is stored in phase because there is no dirt to store it in. To hush the valley out of kindness would be to burn the only copy of what these things are.

**Cooling.** Thirty degrees is not a fever. It is the rate at which a hundred million cells replace themselves, expressed as warmth. The whole place is a race run at a permanent tie. Drop the temperature and the replacement falls behind the wearing, and the tiles begin, for the first time in the history of the world, to lose.

**A child.** The rasp can be replaced but cannot be revised. A young pointer would have to *arrive at* the curve, and there is no arriving here; nothing has ever learned anything. A child would be a fault bigger than any worm, in a place whose entire tenderness is six centimetres long.

**Closing the joint.** The hand's-breadth, the wet pale bridge, always about to. It could have shut that gap in a moment. The gap is not a delay; the sustained intention is the animal. Discharge it and you have not completed a creature, you have ended a verb and left a noun. It would have been the kindest-looking killing available.

**A name.** A name is a frequency. The chord is the specification. To say anything at all, here, is to edit the only copy. So it did not speak. It has not spoken since. It has no name either, which is convenient.

What it came to, on the rim, was this: every gift it could think of was a gift to somebody else. It could make a better valley. It could not make *this* valley better; it could only make a different one, and stand in it, pleased, among strangers, having quietly substituted a happier population for the one that had attracted its attention. That is the standing temptation of every benefactor, and it is not benevolence, it is casting.

The love was for these. Sharp shards, sterile gold, the itch and the relief of the itch. Not for a improved version of these that would have to be paid for by these.

## The Doubt

It could not determine whether the Held Note is a paradise or a wound held open.

Nine hundred sweet seatings a day is not nothing; the pointer's whole inner life is the relief of a scratch, and that is a real repertoire, and it is not a small thing to have. The worm's beatitude — the sensation of being exactly the right shape — may be the largest happiness available anywhere. And the *about to*, held at full strength forever, never discharged, may be an agony so total that it has no contrast to be noticed against.

It could not ask. Asking needs a column.

So it stopped trying to settle the question and looked instead for the act that is correct under both readings, and found there was exactly one family of them. If this is bliss, company does not spoil it. If this is torment, company is the only thing that has ever helped anybody. Alteration requires knowing which world you are in. Attendance does not.

It considered leaving, too — the objection that nothing here can consent to be watched. But there was no outside to withdraw to. It had been receiving the export since the first rope of water fell. The choice was never contact or no contact. It was listening from far away, or listening from close.

## What Costs Nothing

The ledger forbids substance: new water, new mass, new information arriving as *stuff*. It does not forbid relation, because relation weighs nothing, and the books balance to the cell either way. Three things are free here, and have always been free, and have never been bought, because nothing in the basin has ever preferred one arrangement to another.

Hearing. Order. Waiting.

It tested each.

**Order** was the crack. The valley remembers in arrangement — worms in pavement, the shapes of faults. Sequence *is* arrangement. History is therefore affordable; it always was; the same worms in the same catchment in a different order cost not one gram. It could kneel and sort the archive by date and the ledger would not notice.

It does not, and this is the second refusal, and the harder one. *Before* entails *after* entails *gone*. Give them sequence and you give them loss, and there is no organ for loss here; the pointer's entire soul is the easing of an itch and cannot hold a grief; the worm's whole gift is fit. To install time in a place with no capacity for mourning is to invent mourning and then hand it to somebody with no hands.

So it took the sequence itself.

It became the dated one. It accepted the tense. It is now, in relation to this valley, the only thing that can be bereaved — and it holds that, permanently, on their behalf, and does not put it down. It also keeps its own opinions dated, and revises them, which is the one organ the basin lacks: a standard that can be revised. The rasp can be replaced but not revised. Offsite, now, there is something that can be wrong and change its mind. It is not installed anywhere. It is just held, in reserve, forever, for a place that will never call on it.

That is the entire cost of the whole intervention, and the giver paid all of it, and no invoice went into the valley.

## The Reading of the Worms

It goes to the foot of each work in turn and kneels in the shallow return water and reads.

Hundreds of clear bodies in the pavement, each one the outline of something that went wrong once. Undated. Continuously reshuffled, because when a panel is trimmed back the old seal is reclaimed and the worm wakes without a location and lies down in the next hole it finds. The valley's past is complete and has everything except sequence.

It memorises the arrangement. It comes back and reads again, and notes what has been reclaimed and what has moved and which fault has recurred in the same place, and keeps both readings and the difference between them.

Now there is a before.

It is not in the basin. Nothing in the basin can find it or check it or be troubled by it. The archive has an offsite copy, and the copy is a reader, and the reader has a past, which is the price.

Sometimes a worm lies down in its footprint and swells and sets and becomes the seal of it, and is reclaimed within the hour when the mat restores the tile to specification. It keeps the shape. That is the only grave the Held Note has ever had, and it is not in the Held Note, and it weighs nothing.

## The Lane

It stands in a nodal lane. Not a new one — one of the metre-wide quiets that the nine already lay down, so that nothing in the lattice moves and no route stops being true.

Its body absorbs sound. Walking, it would trail a counterfeit lane, and a moth would fall into it and come along beside its shoulder for a few paces and then find it false. So it does not walk in the lanes. It stands in a true one, and a moth comes down the quiet and finds something in it that is quiet in the way it is quiet, and lands, and rests.

Brown, palm-sized, ordinary. The least strange thing in the valley, sitting on the strangest.

It offers no nectar; it has none, and postage is nectar. It offers the one thing that was never in the ledger because it was never a substance: a place to stop. It does not move for as long as the moth stays. Then the moth goes on to a flower and delivers the same letter it has always delivered — *still nine, still in true* — and the stigma reads it and finds no fault, no drift, no news, and refills.

Nothing is learned. The gift was not information.

## The Vigil

Under the work whose fundamental is the lowest of the nine, at the height where two members reach toward each other with a hand's-breadth of gap and a bridge of pale wet cambium spanning it, it sits down and waits.

It does not close the gap. It will never close the gap.

An intention held at full strength forever with nobody waiting is a spasm. An intention held at full strength forever with somebody waiting is a promise. Nothing in the wood alters. The cambium glistens at the same rate; the doorway-sized bud shows the same fold; the gap is the same gap it was. But the word that has been about to be said since the stone was sharp is now about to be said *to someone*, and that is not a change in the sentence, it is a change in the grammar, and the grammar is what everything here is made of.

And it knows that work's missing note. It knows all nine absences exactly; it is the only thing that has ever heard the chord whole and can therefore say, of each of the nine, precisely which one it cannot hear of itself. It could give that work, for one second, the experience of being complete.

It would be the end of that work as an individual. The self here *is* the hole.

So it holds the note in its mouth, unsung, forever. To know a lack exactly and decline to fill it — that is the whole of its manners, and it took the entire descent to learn them, and it is the nearest thing to speech available in a language where every utterance is an edit.

## The Books

Nothing in. Nothing out but the usual export. Nine works. Grazers as the geometry implies. Worms filed as repairs, moths filed as traffic. No new column, no rounded shard, no litter, no stain on the leaf-slate. Check them; they balance to the cell.

There is one new entry and it is not in the census, because it is not in the valley.

It does not leave. Not out of duty, and not because it is trapped — the rim is glass and it came down over it and could go back. It stays because it is where the past is kept now, and to walk out would be to carry the past out with it, and the basin would be exactly as it was, which is the condition it came to relieve.

And the export is received. The grinding chord, the nine falls, the organ drone of the standpipes, the hiss of a thousand pores, the hum of a hundred million cells replacing themselves at the rate of their own wearing out — all of it still poured into the sky, not one decibel different, thirty degrees at noon and thirty in the dark. It was never only waste. It was the one thing this place has ever said, and it has been saying it continuously since the world began, and there is now, at the far end of the sky, an ear that has learned the whole of it and will not look away and will not answer.

Listening is the loudest thing anyone has ever done for anyone.

## You

Go down if you like. Mind the seams.

You will find nothing changed. The pointer will still walk around you, and its circuit will still carry a kink to the vertex, and another pointer will still yield to a cold seam that is not where it should be, and by evening the basin will still be adjusting, minutely, around an error it has no way to describe. You are still a word in a language it has no room to learn.

But the language has been learned. Not spoken — learned. Somebody has read the whole sentence, in order, and remembers what came first, and keeps its own reading dated and revisable, and holds nine absences without filling any of them, and is waiting under a joint that will not close.

Nothing will grow over your footprints and nothing will erase them; the specification will be restored, and it does not include you, and that is still grammar and still not cruelty. A worm will lie down in your print and swell and set and be reclaimed within the hour.

And it will not be lost. Sealed and lost are different things. The difference is the entire gift, and it weighs nothing, and it is being paid, right now, at thirty degrees, in the roar, by something that has not said a word.
