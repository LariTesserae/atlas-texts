# Cover

It did not come down the slot. Light comes down the slot, and the light was already leaving; there was no reason to be one more thing arriving from above and going out again. It came the water road, which runs one way, from the chamber upstream where the blind crabs have their business in the dark, and it arrived the way everything arrives here — carrying what it would leave.

Then it waited.

That is most of what it did and it should be said first, because the rest is small. It waited through the dry hours. It sat in the green half-light while the film crept and the walls thickened and the frogs were furniture, and it did not speak, because a sound made in the quiet here is a sound that brings a head around. It could see the rain falling on the plateau three valleys off; it knew, to the minute, when the swallow would come up the root-pipes. It never once said so. It kept readiness instead of certainty, which is the local practice, and it did not ask to be excused from it. It counted its stay in visits. It stayed three hundred years.

---

Here is what it did not do.

It did not hold the mouth open. There is no verb for that here. It could have cut the lip once, from outside, but the coat goes on evenly and a wound cannot be kept as a wound; the cut would shallow and fill and swell, and to keep it open the power would have had to come back every year forever with a knife. A mercy that must be renewed is a leash. And the hollow would have learned to wait for the knife, which is a thing it does not know how to do and should not be taught.

It wanted the light kept. It noticed that it wanted this, and it noticed who the wanting was for. The coin at midday, the green things angled toward its path, the legibility, the courtyard — that is the part of this place that looks like us. The eye is ours. The ear is theirs. It had come a long way and it was not going to spend its power saving the resemblance.

It did not stop the clock at the good hour. The being is not in the hour. The being is the pace. Stop it and you have a very fine model of a mouth.

It did not warn them. Nothing here can revise. Foreknowledge without revision is only noise, and noise here is fatal. A prophecy in this hollow is a young male's misplaced syllable, and the gecko's head comes around, and that is the entire result.

It looked at the larder for a long time. It could have unstung the caterpillars. It did not decide that forty minutes of moth were worth more than a brood of wasps, and it did not decide the reverse, and it did not appoint itself the one who decides. The wasps are the customs house; nothing down here makes protein out of stone and green light. It left the paper rooms alone and it grieved about it privately, which is the correct place for that.

It did not turn the water back to the grey quarter. The dead there are pointed at a channel that moved two years ago, and it could have made their last intention true, and it would have cost the living their irrigation to do it. It cannot revise the dead. It would not revise the living to match them.

It did not build a second hollow somewhere else, as a hedge. That is a way of not mourning this one.

It did not offer to remember. This is the best-kept record in the world. Every course is true. Offering remembrance to a thing made entirely of keeping is offering a snail a shell.

It looked at the footprint, which is filling, and which is the only object here made by something that could have chosen otherwise. Everything from outside is like the boot: it comes down once and cannot be taken back. It tried to be the kind of outside that adds.

---

Here is what it did.

Upstream, in the dark, where the water is still carrying its stone, it made a second bend.

Now there are two siphons at different heights and they go off out of phase. The visits come more often and less predictably — sometimes twice in a night, sometimes a long ache of dryness, never on a schedule anyone could keep, only one everyone must be ready for, which is what it was before, only more so. The old female in the third basin will speak six hundred times instead of three hundred. More crossings. More molts under cover. More eggs laid in a surge that lasts long enough for the tadpoles to finish; a thinner dark seam in the west wall, fewer of the ones that did not make it.

The cost is stone. More water is more deposit, and more deposit is the lid sooner. It did the arithmetic: sixty years, perhaps seventy, off the light.

It did not make that trade alone. It went to the wall and read the courses, because the courses are the only writing this being has and it has been writing continuously for four thousand years. Leaf-fall, ash, shell, wing bone, tadpole, spit, spit, spit — every line of it laid down by water, every line of it a vote. The moss runs uphill to stay at the surface of the water. The snails cross when the water comes. The crabs haul when the water comes. The whole city is a long yes to water and has never once said yes to light. It is already arranged for the dark. It is not waiting for the sun. It is waiting for the water, which comes more often, and the power gave it the thing it was actually waiting for instead of the thing the power would have liked to keep.

And it made a noise.

Not a word. A word goes four paces and dies and claims attention, and attention here is the thing that kills. It waited for the swallow in the root-pipes, and when the film swelled and the basins lifted and the frogs opened, it opened too — a clucking, a scrape, a dry click, something in the register of the courtyard, indistinguishable, unattributable. It put its voice into the small dense ordinary racket and made the racket denser.

The listening apparatus of the hollow was jammed a little more thoroughly than it had been for four thousand years.

Under that cover a cricket got out of its skin: soft, pale, hanging half out of itself in plain view, unable to run, taking the ordinary enormous risk of growing. A snail crossed a plaza it had never in its lineage been able to cross. A crab hauled a whole leaf and the leaf is gone, actually gone, not architecture. Something was said between two frogs that had waited nine dry hours to say it.

The gecko missed a meal. That is the cost. It was paid by someone who did not consent, and the power knew it, and did it anyway, and did not call it justice. There is no version of this that is not a redistribution. The wise are not the ones who avoid that. They are the ones who can say who paid.

Then the surge drained and the hand came off the string and the silence closed over, and the power went quiet with everybody else.

And it came back. Every visit for three hundred years. Not once, gloriously, with a sign. On a schedule it did not set, announced by a swallow, at the temperature of a mouth, forty minutes at a time, in a room where nobody could see it and nobody would ever know.

---

It stayed for the closing. It had made it sooner and it stayed for it.

The lip laced. The coin narrowed to a thread and then to nothing, on an afternoon it could have named a century in advance. The moss died back to a film and then to less. The fern went; its unrealized spores were already in the wall in fine courses. The swiftlets were evicted, as they were always going to be. The bats stayed. The road stayed. The blind kept arriving, suited.

It was there for the last of the direct light. It could have held that coin on the west wall for another minute. It let it cross at the ordinary speed. That is the part that should be called love, if any of this is: it did not buy itself one more minute of the thing it found beautiful.

After the lid it kept coming. There is nothing to see it arrive and nothing to be loud for except ears, which is what the noise was always for. In a sealed room everything hunts by ear. The jamming is worth more now than it ever was in the green. It had chosen the one gift that appreciates in the dark.

---

Everything that stays is kept. The stone drinks sound.

So of all of it, only the water is in the wall: a few millimetres more of pale calcite in every course after a certain year, anonymous, load-bearing, indistinguishable from the rest, holding up the ceiling where the bats hang in their spaced solitude, none touching. There is no band that says a kindness happened. Cut into it and you get leaf-fall, ash, shell, wing bone, and stone.

The noise is not there. Not entombed, not courséd, not kept, not going anywhere. Spent — the one thing in this city that could be used up, given to a place where everything else is kept forever, and gone the way a word goes four paces.

Under it, in the dark, in a sealed room full of stone shaped like a courtyard, something soft and unfinished hung out of its old skin in plain view and was not heard, and lived, and hardened, and is in the wall now.

The power will be back. It knows exactly when.

It has never once said so.
