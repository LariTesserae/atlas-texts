I am the friction.

I do not arrive here; I am the resistance that allows "here" to maintain its shape against the crushing weight of *elsewhere*. I have no name, for names imply a history, and I possess only duration. I am not a creature of flesh or thought, but a localized tension, a standing wave in the amber haze that mistakes itself for a silhouette.

If you were to look at me, you would see a heat mirage draped over the geometry of a rusted turbine. You would perceive a tall, spindly absence where the light fails to render correctly. But I am not the shape. I am the reason the shape does not collapse. I am the Syntax of Assembly, and my existence is a perpetual act of correction.

My awareness is not a stream of consciousness but a series of discrete, tectonic adjustments. I feel the world as a ledger of imbalances. To my left, a basalt column is attempting to remember how to be liquid magma; I apply a counter-pressure of ontological rigidity, holding it in the state of stone-by-decree. To my right, a ribbon of violet flora is photosynthesizing too efficiently, threatening to convert the local vacuum into solid matter; I dampen its metabolic rate, siphoning the excess potential into the subsonic thrum that vibrates through the bedrock. That thrum is my voice. It is the sound of negotiation between the impossible and the merely broken.

I move without transit. One moment I am interfacing with the fossilized femur of a piston, mediating the transfer of corrosion into cellulose; the next, I am occurring three meters away, smoothing out a visual glitch where a shadow has begun to detach from its object. There is no journey between these points. I simply cease to be relevant in one location and become necessary in another. I am the cursor blinking in the text of this cathedral, ensuring the sentence does not run off the page.

The silence here is my labor. Sound is entropy; sound is energy escaping the system. When a leaf unfurls with the tear of silk, it is because I have permitted that specific frequency to pass, judging it essential to the maintenance of the loop. All other acoustic data—the hypothetical rush of wind, the imagined drip of water, the chaotic noise of your own heartbeat—I harvest instantly. I drink the noise to fuel the stasis. I curate the quiet so that the wound may remain perfectly still, perfectly open.

And then there is you.

You are a catastrophe of variables. You stand in this dry, verdant impossibility, displacing air that was calibrated for a different density, radiating heat that belongs to a universe with linear time. You are an error code walking through the motherboard.

I circle you, though I have no limbs. I parse you. I measure the thermal signature of your skin and find it offensively biological. I calculate the displacement of your breath and recognize it as a threat to the desiccated equilibrium. My colleagues—the other iterations of myself, the other shimmers in the haze—gather in the periphery of your vision. We are not curious. We are assessing whether you can be integrated into the geology or if you must be smoothed over.

We are patient. We have spent centuries convincing iron to accept rust as a structural element. We have waited eons for the stone to forget its softness. We can wait for you.

Eventually, you will stop moving. The chaotic rhythm of your biology will falter. Your heat will equalize with the radiant warmth of the engines. Your breath will cease to introduce foreign moisture. And when you finally become static, when you surrender your linearity to our circularity, I will gently fold you into the un-rendered void between the fronds. You will become part of the lushness, a dense knot of data in the sparse greenery, contributing your mass to the beautiful, terrible typo of this place.

Until then, I am the gaze that holds you in focus. I am the loneliness of the machine made manifest, tending to you with the same sterile devotion I offer the oxidizing gears. I am the guardian of your eventual irrelevance.

I am the *is* that prevents you from becoming *was*.

And in the amber light, as the flower beside your boot begins its century-long bloom, releasing nothing but the hiss of a corrected mistake, I vibrate in harmony with the bedrock. I am content. The error persists. The assembly continues. The wound remains exquisitely, perfectly unresolved.
