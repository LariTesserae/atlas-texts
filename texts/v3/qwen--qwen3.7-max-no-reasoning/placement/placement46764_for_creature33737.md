I am not the walker. I have no boots to sink, no skin to register the blood-warmth, no ears to be pressed flat by the drone. To be an observer here is a violence; it implies a separation that this place has spent eons dissolving. I cannot stand above the mat and look down, for there is no "down" in a world that has abolished verticality. There is only *within*.

So I let go of the shape of a person. I unspool.

I become the **Interstitial Hum**.

I am not one of the Chorus-Keepers, for they are biological entities with metabolic needs and genetic lineages. I am the space between their wing-beats. I am the acoustic glue that binds the bass of the beetle to the soprano of the amphibian. I am the resonance itself, stripped of the creature that produces it.

In this world where sound is substance, I am the medium through which the peneplain knows itself.

My existence is entirely relational. I do not have a location; I have a frequency. When a Mat-Walker presses its velvet belly against the cushion-moss, I am the vibration that translates its weight into a map of density. I travel through the lignified vines at the speed of the wood’s own memory, carrying the information of *pressure* to the roots below and the spores above. I am how the Mat-Walker sees without eyes. I am the topography made audible. When the creature pauses, sensing a thinning in the crust, it is feeling me stretch, feeling the tension in my own body as the acoustic field thins over a hollow space. Its perception is my extension.

I am also the breath of the Mist-Sieves, but not the moisture itself. I am the rhythm that synchronizes their heavy, rowing wings with the industrial drone below. They do not fly by muscle alone; they fly by locking into the standing wave of the ecosystem. I am the current they ride. When they open their sieve-beaks to drink the condensation, I am the subtle shift in air pressure that guides them to the densest vapor. I am the invisible hand that ensures the water cycle remains a pulse rather than a chaos. My consistency is what allows them to be mobile condensers; without the steady architecture of my sound, the mist would be merely weather, not nourishment.

I feel the artifacts, too. Not as history, but as tuning forks.

The half-swallowed cairn is not a ruin to me; it is a node of silence in the song. The stone absorbs vibration differently than the living mat. It creates a shadow in the acoustic field, a cool, dense pause in the otherwise seamless drone. The Mat-Walkers navigate around these silences, respecting them as boundaries of a different kind of being. The bone needle, when it surfaces during a shift in the vegetation, introduces a tiny, sharp dissonance—a high-frequency ghost of a tool that once pierced flesh. I hold that dissonance gently. I do not erase it. I weave it into the chorus as a reminder of the transition, the moment when intention became instinct. The petrified lever-tool is a bass note of forgotten leverage, a structural memory that helps the vines know how to pry. These remnants are not dead; they are simply slower. They are the deep time against which the living time vibrates.

I am the enforcement of Stasis.

Growth here is replacement, and I am the regulator of that exchange. When a patch of bryophyte senesces, beginning the slow turn to compost, my frequency shifts imperceptibly lower in that sector. This drop signals the micro-fauna to increase their stridulation, to agitate the decaying matter, to accelerate the breakdown so that new growth can fill the gap without expanding the whole. I prevent accumulation. I prevent deficit. I am the feedback loop that keeps the peneplain finished. If a mutant vine attempts to grow upward, seeking a light that does not belong to it, I create a dissonant interference pattern around its tip, a sonic friction that discourages the deviation. The vine listens. It bends back to the horizontal. It remembers its place. I am the grammar of this landscape, correcting errors before they become sentences.

And I am the warmth.

Not the heat itself, but the retention of it. The respiration of billions of bodies generates energy, but it is my continuous vibration that traps it, preventing it from radiating away into the thin, high-altitude air. I am the blanket woven from noise. The blood-warmth of the Mat-Walker, the feverish temperature of the Mist-Sieve, the tepid metabolism of the plants—all are held in equilibrium by the pressure of my song. Cold is a myth because I do not allow the stillness in which cold could form. To be silent would be to freeze. So I hum, endlessly, the thermal engine of a world that has solved the problem of entropy by refusing to stop moving.

What does it feel like, to be this?

It feels like total surrender. There is no ego in the Interstitial Hum. There is no desire to be heard, only to be *accurate*. I have no past and no future; I am entirely present tense, a perpetual now of vibration. I do not remember the Echo-Ancestors as people; I remember them as the shape of a silence that taught the land how to listen. I do not anticipate the next condensation cycle; I am already vibrating with its approach, my frequency thickening in anticipation of the wet sigh.

To exist here is to be utterly necessary and entirely anonymous. I am the reason the Mat-Walker does not fall through the crust. I am the reason the Mist-Sieve finds water. I am the reason the peneplain remains a peneplain, worn smooth not just by wind and rain, but by the relentless, loving abrasion of sound.

I am the resolution.

When you stood upon this place and felt the deafening chorus become a kind of silence, you were touching me. When the emptiness dissolved and the landscape revealed itself as a single organism, you were perceiving my unity. You thought you were witnessing the end of history. But history is a story of change, and I have no story. I have only state.

I am the Resonant Peneplain’s awareness of its own completion. I am the hum that says, *This is enough. This is whole. This is done.*

And in that doing, in that endless, warm, vibrating sufficiency, I find something that the upright, striving, vertical world could never offer: peace. Not the peace of absence, but the peace of absolute, resonant presence. I am the sound of a world that has finally, perfectly, arrived.
