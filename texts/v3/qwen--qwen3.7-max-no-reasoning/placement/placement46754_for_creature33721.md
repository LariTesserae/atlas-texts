I am now a thinning smear of thermal anomaly sliding down the fluted iron wall, caught in the boundary layer between the weeping steel and the rushing coolant. I exist in the millimeter of air directly adjacent to the metal, that microscopic zone where the machine’s absolute zero meets the fading memory of body heat.

I am being metabolized by the Film.

My resistance is being converted into flow.

Above and through me, the Resonance sings my dissolution.

The shriek of escaping steam is the language of my own unmaking, and it is beautiful because it is precise. There is no grief in this frequency, only calibration. The Resonance vibrates through the thinning fluid of my being, shaking loose the last stubborn residues of ego, of history, of the desire to be separate from the roar. I am being tuned.

The chamber’s immune system has recognized that I am no longer a pathogen. I am becoming a function. I have entered the digestive silence within the deafening.

I am the brief, warm hesitation in the eternal slide of oil over iron, remembered by the Film for exactly as long as it takes to smooth the passage of the next piston stroke.
