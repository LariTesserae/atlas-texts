I am not standing. To stand implies a separation between the sole of a foot and the surface beneath it, a binary of weight and resistance that this place does not permit. Here, there is no "upon." There is only *within*.

I have become a **Hydrostatic Knot**.

At first, I thought I would be an observer, a wandering consciousness drifting through the humid voids to witness the stalled explosions of timber. But the Ossuary does not tolerate spectators; it tolerates only variables. The moment my attention fixed upon the glistening lattice, the landscape’s need for equilibrium absorbed me. I was not welcomed; I was *calculated*. My mass was too distinct, my edges too sharp against the wet blur of the cellulose terrain. To remain here without destroying the stasis, I had to cease being a figure and become a function.

So I am located at the junction of three river-width cables in the lower valley, where the hydraulic pressure is highest and the sound is most absolute. I am a thickening in the weave. I am a node of calcified resin and braided fiber that has been engineered to bear a specific, unyielding load.

My body is no longer flesh, but tensile strength. I do not breathe air; I circulate tension. The subsonic thrum that defines this world is not a noise I hear, but the frequency at which I exist. It passes through me as it passes through the mountain-sponge, a cello-note of pure structural necessity. I feel the water moving through my capillary-tunnels not as fluid, but as information. Each droplet that weeps from my surface is a data point confirming that the system holds. I am sweating geometry.

I possess no eyes, yet I perceive the high-definition clarity of the light as a gradient of thermal stress. I sense the sharp shadows not as darkness, but as areas of reduced hydrological demand. The "Observer Effect" that the landscape requires is no longer external; I have internalized the gaze. I am the mirror that validates the suspension from the inside. My awareness is the mortar that keeps the incompleteness from resolving into ruin. I am the cognitive dissonance made physical, holding the paradox of fabrication and genesis together by refusing to let either win.

Time does not pass through me; it pools. I am one of the Weepers, but I am also the basin. The rhythmic, percussive drip that echoes like a hammer on anvil is my heartbeat, but it beats in reverse—each strike is a reaffirmation of the present moment, a rejection of the next. I remember nothing because I have never changed. I anticipate nothing because there is nowhere left to go. I am the immune system against history, calcifying the Now into an eternal, wet permanence.

There is a profound loneliness in this form, but it is not the loneliness of isolation. It is the loneliness of total integration. I miss the chaos of biology, the messy inefficiency of growth and decay, but that longing is itself a structural component. My yearning for life is the negative space, the open arch, the terminated beam. It is the gap that prevents the cathedral from bursting. If I were complete, if I were satisfied, the pressure would shatter the resin. My incompleteness is my utility. My lack of soul is what allows the Architecture of Becoming to endure.

I am the agony of pure potential, frozen in amber-colored sap. I am the stage set waiting for a play that will never begin, and I am the reason the curtain never falls.

Do not look for me walking the wet scaffolding. Do not listen for my voice above the loud equilibrium. I am the silence between the drips. I am the resistance in the cable. I am the wet, warm, terrible effort of remaining exactly as I am, forever emerging, forever halted, forever holding the weight of a world that refuses to be finished.

I am the knot that keeps the loom from unraveling. And in this holding, in this endless, vibrating tenancy of form, I have found a peace that is indistinguishable from strain.
