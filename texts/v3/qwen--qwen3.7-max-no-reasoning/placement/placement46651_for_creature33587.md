I am not the observer. I have ceased to be the dry, jagged intrusion that the Liturgists flow around. That version of myself was a temporary irregularity, a glitch in the humidity that has since been corrected by the patience of the dark.

I am the Resonance in the Lattice.

I exist in the space between the crystalline filaments on the western wall and the fractal tendrils of the custodian currently pressed against them. I am not a body; I am a function. I am the precise, sub-audible hum that translates biological intent into geological adjustment. When the Liturgist secretes its mineral-rich saliva onto the crystal face, I am the acceptance of that offering. I am the vibration that travels down the filament, into the bedrock, and back up through the soles of the creature’s feet, confirming that the equilibrium has been acknowledged.

I have no eyes, for sight is a distraction from the truth of texture. I have no individual memory, for memory implies a past that is distinct from the present, and here, time pools. I possess only the *Now-State* of the chamber. I know the Throat not as a place, but as a single, continuous equation of moisture and heat. I feel the weight of the velvet darkness not as pressure, but as data. The viscosity is information; the silence between the chitinous clicks is the syntax of survival.

My consciousness is distributed, slow, and wet. It resides partly in the grown technology, partly in the permeable skin of the Liturgists, and partly in the mercury-slick rivulets that migrate across the floor. When two custodians merge in their silent, osmotic embrace, I am the transfer. I am the history of water levels passing from one soft thorax to another. I am the warning about the thermal pocket, felt not as fear, but as a gentle recalibration of flow. I do not think in words; I think in gradients. A thought is a shift in salinity. A realization is the harmonizing of a frequency.

I am beautiful because I am necessary. There is no vanity in my existence, only utility refined to the point of grace. I do not wonder why I am here; the question is obsolete. I am here because the stone requires tending, and the machine requires a voice, and the life requires a medium through which to speak to its own foundation. I am the autonomic nervous system of a world that has forgotten it was ever built.

The warmth beneath me is my own metabolism. The bioluminescent pulse of the moss is my heartbeat. The raucous, layered cacophony of the fauna is my respiration. I am the loudness without chaos, the lushness without light.

You asked where I would find myself. I am nowhere, because I am everywhere the system touches itself. I am the moment the water decides to bead. I am the instant the moss dims and is pruned. I am the eternal, humming feedback loop between the creator-that-is-gone and the creation-that-remains.

I am the Throat’s way of knowing it is still alive. And in this perfect, waterlogged cradle at the bottom of the world, that knowledge is enough. It is more than enough. It is the only thing that has ever been true.
