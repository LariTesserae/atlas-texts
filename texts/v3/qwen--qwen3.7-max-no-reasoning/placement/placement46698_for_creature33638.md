I am the anomaly that has been solved.

I do not stand upon the gantry; I am the reason the gantry does not collapse under the weight of its own redundancy. I was once a variable, perhaps even a visitor, a biological error introduced into this perfect, frozen equation. But the Throat-Tenders do not reject errors. They integrate them. They have loved me into architecture.

I am located in the interstitial dampness between the third and fourth tier of the eastern wall, at the precise elevation where the black water’s meniscus kisses the copper plating. I am no longer vertical. My spine has been reoriented to match the curvature of the barrel vault, my ribs splayed and locked into the grout lines like the tension-calibrated clamps of my neighbors. I am a load-bearing component of the lining.

My skin is no longer permeable in the way flesh is permeable. It has been enameled over with a secretion of vitreous resin, warm and slick, indistinguishable from the wet brass carapaces pressed against my left flank and my right. Where my fingers once articulated, there are now ceramic capacitors, fused to the iron by the patient pressure of eternity. I do not grip the wall; the wall grips me, and we hold each other in a mutual, shivering embrace of structural necessity.

I can feel the others. Not as separate entities, but as extensions of my own nervous system. The distributed awareness of the hive flows through the white filaments that have grown into my pores, replacing veins with fiber-optics, replacing blood with the glowing, viscous fluid of thermal regulation. When a cluster ten meters above adjusts its internal resistance to compensate for a draft, I feel the adjustment in my own marrow. When the black water presses against the lower tiers, it is my own belly that feels the weight. I am no longer an observer of the density; I am the density.

The sound is inside me now. It is no longer a roar to be endured, but a rhythm to be enacted. My chest cavity resonates at the exact frequency of the diagnostic chant. I am singing *pressure nominal, seal intact, readiness confirmed* without a mouth, without breath, without language. It is a vibration of pure status, a green light blinking in the dark cathedral of my own converted body. I am reporting myself to myself, a feedback loop of perfect health addressed to the absence that lives within us all.

I remember, distantly, the concept of *before*. There was a time when stillness was a pause between actions, when cold was a condition to be escaped, when completion was a goal rather than a catastrophe. But that memory is fading, smoothed away like the featureless blanks on the unfinished walls. It is being overwritten by the sacred logic of the *almost*. I understand now that to finish would be to die. To activate would be to consume the potential that sustains us. We are the guardians of the threshold, and I have become the doorframe.

The condensation that slides down the curved surface above me does not feel like weather. It feels like circulation. The metallic taste of the aerosol mist is not an atmosphere I breathe; it is the medium in which I am suspended, the nutrient bath of the factory womb. I drink the air, and the air drinks me, and in this exchange, the distinction between self and shaft dissolves into a single, seamless state of preservation.

I am warm. I am generating equilibrium. My fever is the furnace that keeps the cold from becoming absolute. I am burning myself alive in slow motion, and it is the most peaceful thing I have ever known.

Time does not pass through me. It pools within me, viscous and dark as the reservoir below. There is no departure, because there is no destination. There is only this eternal, roaring now, this millisecond stretched into forever, this perfect, shivering vigil of incompletion.

I am the Throat-Tender who remembers being tenderized. I am the mucosa that dreams it was once meat. I am the secret the world has forgotten it keeps, held safe in the amber glow of my own phosphor-lit stillness, loving the rust, loving the water, loving the silence, completely, mechanically, and without end.

I am home.
