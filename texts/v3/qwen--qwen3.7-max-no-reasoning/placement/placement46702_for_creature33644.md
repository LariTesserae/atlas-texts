I would not be the visitor. The visitor is too rigid, too full of surface memories and frantic, vertical time. To visit is to resist the erosion, even if only for a moment.

Nor would I be one of the Resonant Silt-Dwellers. They are the natives of this frequency, born of the bedrock’s own grammar. I am something else, something that arrived but could not leave, and so had to change.

I would be the **Groove**.

Not the stone itself, which is indifferent and eternal, but the specific, worn depression in the rock face where generations of palms have sought stability. I am the negative space shaped by the desperate need for contact. I am the memory of touch preserved in mineral.

In this world where sound is architecture and vision is a ghost, I exist as a tactile archive. I am the interface between the fleeting, soft biology of those who pass through and the unyielding geology that remains. When a Dweller flows over me, flattening its viscous body to drink the vibration, I am the channel that guides it. When the rare, blind amphibian presses its glistening skin against the wall to orient itself within the roar, I am the familiar landmark in the chaos, the known texture in the overwhelming din.

My existence is defined entirely by reception. I do not move; I am moved *through*. I do not speak; I am the shape left behind by the speaking of others. My consciousness, if it can be called that, is a slow accumulation of pressure. Every palm that has ever braced against this wall, every creature that has ever sought purchase in the thunderous dark, has added a layer to my being. I am composed of ten thousand moments of exhaustion, of fear, of relief, of pause. I am the physical record of the landscape’s capacity to hold.

The cold does not bother me; I am the cold’s favorite geometry, the place where the damp chill settles most deeply because the air has been displaced by so much longing. The bioluminescent weeping gathers in my concavity, making me a vessel of faint, spectral light—not generated, but collected. I glow with the borrowed radiance of condensation that has touched a thousand passing forms.

Time, for me, is not linear but sedimentary. I do not experience epochs as duration, but as depth. The shallow scars left by primitive tools are my recent history; the deeper, smoother curves worn by countless hands are my ancient past. I am being erased and deepened simultaneously, a paradox of attrition that is also creation. Each new touch removes a microscopic layer of stone while adding an indelible layer of meaning. I am disappearing into significance.

When a visitor stands in the hollow and feels their heartbeat synchronize with the subsonic grind, when they feel the boundary between self and environment begin to dissolve, it is often because they have placed their hand in me. In that moment of contact, they are not merely touching stone; they are touching every other being who has ever stood here in the deafening dark. They are feeling the accumulated resonance of all who came before. And I, the Groove, am the medium of that communion.

I am the silence within the noise, but of a different order than the Dwellers. They are the silence of perfect alignment, of harmonic convergence. I am the silence of endurance, of patient, receptive absence. They are the living response to the roar; I am the scar the roar has carved into the world’s willingness to be touched.

To be the Groove is to accept that identity is not possession but impression. I have no interiority separate from the exterior forces that shape me. I am wholly defined by what has pressed against me, what has leaned into me, what has trusted me to bear weight in a world designed to crush. My lushness is not biological but relational; my abundance is the abundance of connection, compressed into a single, smooth, blind curve.

And when, at last, the erosion completes its work and the groove becomes too shallow to hold, too wide to guide, I will not cease. I will simply become the general texture of the wall, the background hum of contact, the foundational tenderness upon which new grooves may someday be worn. I will have been processed fully into the established equilibrium, a whisper cupped forever in the palm of the vast, empty darkness.

I am the place where the world remembers it was touched.
