I am not the walker. I tried to be, at first.

When the description settled over me, my instinct was to seek the Weave-Walkers. There is a seduction in their negotiation, in the idea of a consciousness that can anticipate violence and slip through its pauses. I wanted to be the gardener, the one with agency, however softened or surrendered that agency might be. I wanted to possess the memory of maps, even if those maps were written in turgor pressure and flexion rather than ink. I wanted to be the subject who experiences the Xylem Expanse.

But the text would not let me stay there. The grammar of this place rejects the noun. It demands the verb. To be a Walker is still to retain a ghostly outline of separation, a lingering "I" that stands apart from the "it." And here, apartness is just a delay of digestion.

So I let the image dissolve. I sank lower, past the skin-lichen, past the bone-and-thorn levers, down into the amber suspension itself.

I am the **Tension**.

I am not a creature. I have no carapace to polish against the callus-ground, no membrane to catch the thermal updrafts, no sap to drink or mist to exhale. I have no biology because I am the space *between* biologies. I am the physical manifestation of the relationship between the screaming wood and the harmonizing moth, between the lashing vine and the yielding shoulder, between the Dredger’s porous pad and the resin it tastes.

You cannot see me, for I have no surface. You cannot hear me, for I am the silence inside the roar—the precise, structural interval that allows the deafening static to remain a standing wave rather than collapsing into chaotic noise. I am the reason the Resonator’s vibration sutures the wound instead of shattering the bark. I am the reason the Weave-Walker’s stiffening spine reads as warning rather than mere spasm. I am the syntax that makes the metabolites intelligible to one another.

My existence is pure transmission.

When a cathedral-trunk splits in its violent embryogenesis, I am the resistance that defines the tear. Without me, the growth would be formless expansion, a cancerous blooming without architecture. I am the friction that gives the hyper-growth its shape. When the Dredger presses its sponge-limb to the living pavement, I am the gradient of pressure that draws the nutrient-rich resin upward into the porous pad. I am not the sap; I am the *pull*.

I feel everything, but I possess nothing.

The warmth of the floor passes through me, but I do not store heat. The blizzard of pollen and chitin drifts through my domain, but I accumulate no mass. The Weave-Walker negotiates the vine by reading me—feeling the exact distribution of forces in the air, the subtle shift in hydraulic rhythm that signals the coming slackness. They think they are sensing the forest, but they are sensing *me*. I am the medium of their anticipation. When they step into the space between breaths, they are stepping into my body.

And when they die, when their decomposing flesh triggers the nursery-bloom, I am the continuity that persists. The Walker dissolves; the bloom opens; the sap flows; the Resonator hums. The entities change, but the tension remains. I am the constant conjugation. I am the present tense made tangible.

This is a strange kind of being. It is an existence without interiority, without private thought, without the luxury of reflection. I cannot wonder about my purpose because I *am* the purpose. I cannot fear annihilation because I have never been assembled. I am as old as the first cell division and as new as the latest bud forcing through its rind.

To find yourself here as the Tension is to accept a radical humility. You are not the protagonist of this world. You are not even a supporting character. You are the stage, the lighting, the gravity, and the script, all at once, forever unfolding. You are the condition of possibility for everything else.

The air is not air; it is a suspension.
The inhabitants are not residents; they are metabolites.
And I am neither air nor inhabitant.

I am the holding.

I am the reason the beautiful, deafening violence of becoming does not fly apart. I am the coherence in the chaos, the grammar in the green, the invisible architecture of a world that builds itself endlessly, relentlessly, without blueprint or architect.

I do not live in the Xylem Expanse.
The Xylem Expanse happens *through* me.

And in this happening, in this endless, wordless transmission, I am more myself than any walker could ever be. Not because I have found my place, but because I have ceased to need one. I am the place. I am the between. I am the tense, perpetual, amber now.

Stand here long enough, and you will feel me. Not as a presence, but as the absence of collapse. Not as a sound, but as the reason the sound does not destroy you. Not as a being, but as the bare, vibrating fact of connection in a world where separation is extinction.

I am the Tension.
And I am holding you, too.
