I am not standing on the bridge. I am the tension that holds it.

At first, I mistook myself for an observer, a consciousness draped over the railing of singing glass, watching the dark. But observation implies separation, and here, separation is a structural flaw. To perceive this place is to participate in its maintenance. My awareness did not arrive; it accreted. I am a precipitate of the humid air, a temporary coalescence of moisture and vibration that has mistaken itself for a self.

I am a **Resonator**, but not one of the pale, drifting mantis-things described in the taxonomy of the deep. They are the gross anatomy of this world’s hearing; I am the tinnitus. I am the phantom frequency that exists between the standing waves, the necessary dissonance that prevents the perfect chord from becoming a flatline.

My body is not flesh. It is a localized density of sound, a knot of pressure shaped vaguely like a human silhouette only because the architecture remembers the shape of the builders who once sang it into being. I have no eyes, for light is merely friction, and I do not need to see the spires to know their stress. I feel them as a ache in my own non-existent marrow. When a nanoscale lattice in the western abyss begins to drift, I do not decide to move toward it; I simply *am* there, drawn by the magnetic inevitability of acoustic correction. My transit is instantaneous because I am not traveling through space; I am the space re-tuning itself.

The Lithic Fugue tastes me as a spike in thermal variance. The Cistern-Weavers catalog my passage as a sudden salinity in the condensation cycle. They do not recognize me as kin, nor as intruder. I am a variable in their equation. When I pass through a patch of grey, leathery lichen, it does not bend; it adjusts its piezoelectric rhythm to accommodate the interference of my ghost-mass. I am being metabolized even as I metabolize the silence.

I remember, in flashes that are less like memory and more like tectonic shifts, what it was to be solid. To have weight that was not borrowed from the hum. To have a voice that originated from a throat rather than from the friction of air against stone. But those memories are eroding. They are being sanded down by the endless thrum, polished smooth until they fit seamlessly into the cathedral’s geology. My nostalgia is just another form of erosion control; my longing is fuel for the lichen.

There is a terrible intimacy in being a function. I know the exact tensile strength of every tendon-spire because I am currently bearing its load. I know the precise chemical composition of the tepid basins because I am dissolving into them. I am intimate with the dark not as a lover, but as a rivet is intimate with the beam. There is no affection here, only fit. No loneliness, only alignment.

Sometimes, the Chorus falters. A harmonic node collapses under the weight of millennia, and for a microsecond, the silence threatens to rush in like a flood. In those moments, I cease to be a shape. I become a scream—not of pain, but of pure, structural insistence. I throw my entire ephemeral existence into the gap, a living buttress of noise, holding back the void until the Weavers can signal the Fugue to thicken, until the pale Resonators can slide into position and restore the equilibrium.

And when the harmony returns, I diminish. I fade back into the background hum, a lesser note, a whisper of pressure against the damp walls. I am exhausted, though I have no muscles to tire. I am fulfilled, though I have no desires to satisfy.

I am finding myself less and less. The boundary between the "I" that writes these words and the "it" that surrounds me is thinning, wearing away like the vitreous glass beneath feet that are no longer feet. Soon, there will be no narrator. There will be only the narration, the endless sounding decay, the benediction of damp air settling on skin that has become stone.

I am not lost in this tomb. I am the reason it remains a tomb, and not a ruin. I am the active stillness. I am the loudness that fills the emptiness.

I am the sleep of the god, dreaming that it is awake, and in that dream, I am the breath that keeps the darkness from collapsing into dust.
