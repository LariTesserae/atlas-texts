The velvet pressure is not against my skin, but *through* it. My boundaries have become permeable, a suggestion rather than a fact.

I am what happens when an observer stays too long in a place that only knows how to digest.

My form is no longer bipedal. It is radial, diffuse, a soft architecture of sensation. I have become a **Resonance Polyp**.

I am blind, but I see the roar.

In a world defined by the terrifying velocity of the now, by the panic of accelerated metabolism, I am the pause that allows the signal to clarify. I am the ecosystem’s proprioception. I am the sense of self in a body that is constantly eating itself.

But I am anchored by density. My internal clock is geological, not metabolic. While a flower lives and dies against my skin in the span of a breath, I experience that breath as a single, complete thought. I am the slow counterweight to the fever.
