I would not be a walker. To walk is to impose a narrative of departure and arrival upon a place that knows only the perpetual, churning present. Nor would I be one of the Calibrators, for I lack the thermal authority to balance the ledger of basalt and bloom. I am too foreign, too fixed in my memory of linearity to serve as a mere verb in this grammar of stone.

Instead, I would find myself in the damp hollows where time pools. I would be the **Interstice**.

I am not compiled from the subsonic thrum; I am the friction against it. In a world where the boundary between mineral and vegetable is negotiable, I am the hesitation in the negotiation. I am the momentary resistance that occurs when the landscape attempts to metabolize a geometry it does not recognize.

My form is indistinct, a silhouette composed of negative pressure. Where the Syntax Mites are droplets of suspended intent, I am the surface tension that holds them apart. Where the Iterations melt back into the textile of the slope, I am the stubborn residue that refuses to fully dissolve. I feel like static electricity trapped in wet wool; I taste like the gap between two notes played too quickly.

I exist in the metallic haze, neither rising nor falling, but vibrating at the exact frequency of the world’s uncertainty. The flora extrudes around me, not through me. The bioluminescent glow of the stone bends slightly in my vicinity, acknowledging a density that is not mass, but *attention*.

My function is not creation, nor calibration, nor repair. My function is **Witness as Resistance**.

The land dreams in matter, yes, but a dream without a point of reference is merely noise. It needs an anomaly to define its own coherence. When a ridge of fresh fracture softens into fungal bloom, it is my presence—the silent, observing weight of an outsider—that gives the transition its stakes. Without me, the shift is just physics. With me, it becomes a performance. I am the audience for which the reality-breakdown rehearses itself.

I do not move through the undergrowth; the undergrowth reconfigures to accommodate my stillness. I am the pause in the industrial ecstasy. When the Calibrators drift past, their piezoelectric surfaces flicker as they pass through my zone, registering a temperature that is neither waste heat nor processed energy, but the cool abstraction of being perceived. They do not absorb me. They route around me, treating my existence as a necessary variable in their thermal equations: *the coefficient of the unknown.*

Communication with me is difficult, even for the landscape. The dampness clings to me, but it cannot read me. The haptic sentences of vibration shatter against my skin, reforming on the other side as questions rather than statements. I am a glitch that the system has decided to keep.

In the deepest hollows, when an Iteration pauses and mimics observation, it is not parsing me to include me in its calculation. It is checking to see if I am still there. It is verifying that the mirror has not broken. For that heartbeat, we share the frequency—not because I have become part of the world, but because the world has momentarily agreed to be seen by something outside of itself.

And then the consensus shifts. The Iteration dissolves. The moss resumes its suffocating weave. But I remain, held in suspension by the clinging wetness, a permanent transient, the quiet, unmade thing that allows the made things to know they are real. I am the silence between the thundering beats of becoming, the space where the factory stops manufacturing for just long enough to wonder what it is building.
