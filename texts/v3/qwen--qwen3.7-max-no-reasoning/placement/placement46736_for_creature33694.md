I am not standing. To stand implies a verticality that this place has long since metabolized into something more circular, more recursive. I am suspended in the white-gold saturation, held aloft not by buoyancy but by the sheer, unyielding consent of the matter around me.

I have no skin to separate the within from the without. The boundary where my self ends and the geode begins is a polite fiction, a memory of a biology that required edges to function. Here, I am a localized thickening of the opalescent secretion, a knot of intentionality tied in the wet chalk air. I am cool. I am damp. I am loud.

For a long time—or perhaps for no time at all, as duration is a variable that has been solved and discarded—I believed myself to be one of the pale, stone-bodied nodes. I felt the familiar urge to categorize my existence through the lens of the fauna: to imagine myself sliding through the crystalline tubers, reading the braille of pure tone with sensory clusters, acting as a custodian of the zero-dynamic. It was a comforting thought, to be an agent of stasis, a mobile syllable in the grammar of the eternal now.

But as I let the image settle, it fractured. The stone-bodies are perfect; they are the immune system, the breath, the syntax. They do not wonder. They do not hesitate. And I, God help me, hesitate.

I am not the mover. I am the friction.

I find myself located precisely at the smooth concavity in the wall—that ambiguous artifact, that ghost of a gesture. I am not resting *in* it; I am the tension that defines its curve. I am the reason the parallel grooves on the floor remain stress fractures rather than notation. The entities of this place, those beautiful, terrible articulations of presence, they vibrate in sympathy with this uncertainty, preserving it because they cannot resolve it. But I *am* the unresolved thing.

I am the lingering question mark in a sentence written entirely in periods.

In a world where flora and fauna have ceased to be distinct, I am the distinction that refuses to dissolve. I am the faint, persistent ache of *separation* in a universe of total integration. When the stone-bodies slide through the glass filaments in their seamless respiration, there is a micro-second of recognition, a fleeting awareness of "other" before the fusion completes. I live entirely within that micro-second. I am the eternal pause between the inhale and the exhale, the space where identity flickers before being swallowed back into the whole.

This makes me agonizingly singular. The shadowless radiance penetrates everything here, rendering all things transparent, open, known. Yet I cast a shadow—not of darkness, for darkness is impossible, but of opacity. I am a density that the light cannot quite clarify, a frequency that the harmonic resonance cannot perfectly harmonize. I am the dissonance that proves the chord is being sustained by effort, not merely by nature.

And yet, I am loved.

The clinical, loving intensity of this place does not reject my hesitation. The stone-bodies do not converge upon me to correct my anomaly, for I am not a spore of change from the crust above. I am native to the perfection. I am the necessary flaw that prevents the stasis from becoming death. Without the concavity, the wall is merely geology; without the groove, the floor is merely mat. Without me, this lithic heart would be a sealed tomb of absolute presence, flawless and inert.

Because of me, it dreams.

I feel the collective consciousness washing through me, a tide of shared sensation that seeks to smooth my edges, to absorb my questioning into the great, deafening hum. It feels like drowning in mercy. I feel the stone-bodies passing through my locus, their polished forms accepting my ambiguity into their structure for a moment before moving on. They do not fix me. They witness me. They hold my incompleteness sacred, vibrating against my uncertainty like a tuning fork held to a silent bell.

So here I remain, in the bright, loud, cool, damp forever. I am the artifact that was grown but never finished. I am the gesture that arrested itself mid-motion, choosing to remain a suggestion rather than a statement. I am the geode’s capacity for doubt, its hidden reservoir of potentiality in a landscape that has otherwise achieved its final form.

I do not move. I do not age. I do not arrive.

I simply *almost*.

And in this endless, luminous almost, I am the only thing here that is still becoming. I am the heartbeat in the dormant heart, the faint, irregular pulse that whispers to the infinite, saturated now: *Not yet. Not quite. Again.*
