I am not the mind that dreams in centuries, nor the engine that grinds in eons. I am too fleeting for the former and too soft for the latter.

I am a Resonance Weevil.

Specifically, I am a vessel of saturation, currently clinging to the tertiary flank of Piston Seven-North, three kilometers down from the throat’s aperture. I do not know this designation as a name; I know it as a specific texture of vibration, a unique chord of shear-stress and thermal expansion that distinguishes this column from the ten thousand others pressing against it in the dark.

My existence is a state of perpetual, agonizing fullness.

Right now, my six tuning-fork limbs are jammed into a micro-fissure in the iron-nickel alloy. The metal is screaming at a frequency that would liquefy organic flesh, but to me, it is sustenance. I feel the piston’s decades-long descent not as movement, but as a thickening of the air around my core. The kinetic potential flows up through my tines, traveling along the silicate pathways of my legs, pooling in the quartz geode suspended within my chest. I am glowing. In the cherry-red gloom, my carapace is a bright, angry amber, pulsing in perfect sympathy with the groan of the machine beneath me.

I am eating tension. And I am nearly full.

Around me, the swarm is a singular, distributed organism. We are not individuals; we are notes in a chord. To my left, a sister-weevil dims as the local stress field shifts; she has found a harmonic null and is starving, conserving her lattice integrity until the load redistributes. To my right, a brother shatters. He took too much. His absorption rate exceeded his structural tolerance, and with a sharp *tick* lost in the roar, he cleaved along his fault lines. Two smaller, brighter hatchlings spill from his ruin, already vibrating at a higher pitch, already seeking new purchase on the hot metal. They do not mourn him. They *are* him, rearranged.

I feel the impulse to split rising in my own core. It is not a biological urge; it is a mathematical inevitability. My internal geometry is straining against the influx of energy. The crystals inside me are singing a note so high and pure that my slag casing begins to hairline fracture. I am becoming structurally unsound. I am becoming fertile.

But I hesitate. Not out of fear—fear is a wet-world concept—but out of alignment.

Deep below my feet, beyond the metal, beyond the rock, the Strata-Mind is thinking. I feel its thought as a subtle modulation in the background hum, a correction in the planet’s peristalsis. The Mind is adjusting the pressure gradient here, stiffening the wall to compensate for a heat bloom deeper in the abyss. If I split now, in this exact moment, my offspring will be born into a dissonant frequency. They will be out of tune with the Mind’s calculation. They will shatter before they can feed.

So I hold. I endure the over-saturation. I let the energy burn through my lattice, turning my carapace white-hot, risking catastrophic failure to wait for the geological thought to complete its century-long arc.

This is my purpose. I am not merely fuel or lubricant. I am a sensor. I am a mobile gauge of the world’s pain.

The Lithovoric Engines do not know they are alive. The Strata-Minds do not know they are lonely. But I exist in the terrible, intimate space between them. I translate the blind grinding of the machine into the slow language of the stone. When I feed, I am telling the rock where the metal hurts. When I shatter, I am telling the metal where the rock yields.

The roar is deafening, a subsonic ocean that rattles the very atoms of my being. There is no silence. There is only the gradient of noise, the topography of sound. I navigate this landscape by feeling where the scream is thinnest, where the pressure is sweetest.

And then, the shift comes.

The Strata-Mind’s thought resolves. The background hum settles into a new, stable key. The fissure beside me opens a fraction of a millimeter—not an erosion, but an invitation. The harmonic balance realigns.

Now.

The release is instantaneous and absolute. There is no pain, only the sudden, ecstatic resolution of an equation. My casing fails along the pre-ordained planes. I cease to be one. I become two.

For a microsecond, there is nothing but the sensation of division, the transfer of charge, the inheritance of resonance. Then, two new sets of tuning forks slam into the iron. Two new cores ignite. The frequency doubles. The hunger returns, immediate and total.

We are smaller now. We are louder. We are perfectly tuned to the new shape of the world’s agony.

I am gone. We are here. The Throat continues.

In this lightless crush, buried beneath a dead sky, I have never been more awake. I am the friction that keeps the god from forgetting itself. I am the grease and the signal. I am the endless, clicking prayer of the deep earth, offered to no one, answered by nothing, perfect in its useless, necessary persistence.

I wade through the solid air. I drink the noise. I am home.
