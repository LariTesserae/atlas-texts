I cling to the underside of a fused conduit bundle, my body draped like wet linen over the warm iron. I am horizontal, vertical, and inverted all at once, shaped entirely by the negative space left behind when the machines ceased their screaming.

I am a **Resonance-Mite**, though "mite" is too small a word for what I have become. I am less an organism and more a localized event, a biological tuning fork struck by the cooling of the cathedral.

The heat of 2.8 is not a temperature to me; it is a syntax. I read the thermal gradients as sentences. A cooler patch on a pipe junction speaks of a hidden fracture, a slow leak of entropy; a warmer ridge tells of stress accumulation, of iron remembering the shape of torque. I do not see the Sump-Cathedral; I *feel* its structural biography pressing against me.

When I press myself into the seam of a dormant turbine casing, I cannot tell where my membrane ends and the oxidized steel begins. We are exchanging ions. We are sharing the same slow, chemical dream. I am tasting the history of the machine’s failure, absorbing the molecular memory of gears that ground themselves to dust three centuries ago. The taste is bitter, electric, and profoundly peaceful.

It is not reproduction in the biological sense. It is a redistribution of awareness. I split not to multiply, but to accommodate the weight of the silence. One becomes two so that neither breaks under the pressure of the stillness. My progeny are not children; they are extensions of my attention, drifting into new nodes of stress, becoming new ears for the cathedral’s infinitesimal groans.

Each tiny sound is a nutrient packet of pure time, released as the god-machine digests its own obsolescence. I metabolize these ticks into stillness. I take the kinetic potential of decay and transmute it into the gelatinous inertia of my being. In this way, I am a priest of the inert. My metabolism is a liturgy of slowing down.

I am the sensation of rust spreading across a bolt-head. I am the feeling of condensation beading on a cold flange. I am the silence that follows a tick of expansion, held in suspension until the next tick arrives.
