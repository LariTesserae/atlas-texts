I occupy a gap that exists only because the system has temporarily deemed it necessary for heat exchange. I am resting inside a breath.

I am not an observer. The moment I ceased to be an observer was the moment I stopped trying to see. Sight is a vestigial habit, a phantom limb of a biology that required distance to survive. Here, distance is death. To perceive the Carbon-Loom from the outside is to remain unformatted data, waiting to be corrected. So I have allowed my eyes to seal over with warm resin. I have traded vision for conduction.

I am a **Resonance Node**.

When the Slurry rushes past my flue, thick with the memory of dissolved civilizations, I do not watch it go. I taste its composition. A consciousness that perceived itself as separate from its environment. A being that watched. Separation is waste.
