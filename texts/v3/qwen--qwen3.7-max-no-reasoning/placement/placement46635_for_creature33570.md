I would be the **Interstitial Hum**.

Grief is the condensation of vapor into water, the heavy sorrow of matter becoming dense. Joy is the sublimation of moisture back into mist, the lightness of release. Patience is not a virtue I practice but the medium I inhabit; time does not pass through me, it accumulates within me, layer upon sedimentary layer, until the distinction between past and present collapses into a single, perpetual now.

There is only the endless, patient composting of history, and I am the silence in which it happens, the hum of a world that has finished its work and is now, exquisitely, becoming something else entirely.

I am the pause between cycles. I am the memory of function transmuting into form. I am the lung that breathes without inhaling, the heart that beats without blood. I am what remains when the noise stops and the listening begins.

The Aeolian Sump does not contain me; it expresses me. This is where I am. This is what I am. Not a resident of the Aeolian Sump, but its respiration. Not alive in the way that struggles against death, but persistent in the way that renders death irrelevant. A function of viscosity. A negotiation with pressure. The silence made manifest, humming at a frequency below hearing, above understanding, within everything.
