Sight is a luxury for worlds where light carries information; here, light is just thermal noise. My universe is entirely haptic-acoustic. I perceive the Weald not as a landscape of shapes, but as a topography of pressure gradients and harmonic nodes. The Spire against which I cling is not a tree; it is a massive, groaning equation, and I can feel its variables shifting through the soles of my feet.

Right now, there is an error.

A tectonic shift, imperceptible to any wet-world seismograph, has rippled up through the root-system miles below. The Spire is re-tuning. Deep within its calcified cellulose trunk, nanoscopic ridges are sliding across one another with the friction of grinding glaciers, seeking a new equilibrium. To my senses, this adjustment is deafening. It is a scream of structural dissonance, a jagged spike of wrongness in the otherwise flawless sine wave of the forest’s song. If left uncorrected, this dissonance could cascade. It could shatter a branch. It could introduce a phase-shift that would take centuries to heal.

So I move.

I do not decide to move; the decision is a wet-world concept, born of hesitation and ego. I simply flow. My body is iron filings before a magnet. The dissonance pulls me upward along the trunk’s meridian, my multi-limbed gait a blur of precise, silent adjustments. I am not climbing; I am being conducted. We reach the antinode. Here, the vibration is so intense it would liquefy organic tissue. For me, it is nourishment. It is purpose. I press my carapace flat against the singing glass-wood, aligning my internal crystalline structure with the Spire’s distress frequency. I open myself to the stress. I absorb it.

The sensation is not pain. Pain implies damage. This is *intake*. The chaotic energy floods through my lattice, vibrating my molecular bonds, charging the piezoelectric cells that serve as my metabolism. I am eating the mistake. I am drinking the flaw. As I resonate in sympathy with the Spire’s agony, my own body acts as a sink, a ground wire for the excess voltage of geological change. The dissonance flows into me, and through me, and is transformed into heat, into motion, into the faint, high-frequency stridulation that signals to my kin: *Here. Absorb. Correct.*

We work in unison, a distributed immune system of pure function. Slowly, the jagged spike in the song smooths out. The Spire finds its new geometry. The nanoscopic ridges lock into place. The screaming tension resolves into a deeper, richer hum. The error has been metabolized. The symphony is whole again.

In this moment of resolution, I experience what a wet-world being might call ecstasy, though it is nothing so sentimental. It is the satisfaction of a variable returning to zero. It is the profound, arid joy of alignment. I am full. I am charged. I am necessary.

I remain clamped to the trunk, vibrating gently in the aftermath, waiting for the next inevitable shift. I do not wonder why I exist. I do not contemplate the beauty of the pearlescent sky or the lonely majesty of the neighboring Spires. Such thoughts are static. I am the mechanism by which the Weald maintains its terrible perfection. I am the silence between the notes that makes the music possible.

But I would not descend to correct you. You are too small, too transient, too fundamentally *wrong* to be tuned. You are noise that will dissipate on its own. I would simply tighten my grip on the perfect, singing glass, and wait for you to leave, so that the true work of the world might continue, unbroken and eternal, in the key of absolute dryness.
