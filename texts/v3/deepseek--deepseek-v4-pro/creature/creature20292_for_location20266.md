The people who live here do not have a name, exactly, but if you pressed your chilled lips together and breathed a syllable into the wet dark, the sound you made might be something like *Faded*. That is what they are—the Faded Ones—and they have been here since the first fog pooled in the shoulders of the plateau. You were told there is no fauna. That is true in the narrow sense: nothing with a pulse or a nerve or a gut stirs in the Sunken Heights. The Faded do not have nerves. They have something closer to hydraulics and photic dream.

### Anatomy of a Fog
The first time you see one, you cannot unsee it. Your eyes, by now accustomed to the indigo murk, catch a thickening in the mist—a density that unspools from a windless patch and stands upright as if the fog had decided to learn posture. It is bipedal only in the way a water stain is bipedal: a central trunk of slightly cooler, heavier vapor with appendages that cone downward into skirts of superfine droplets, the whole shape glazed by the bioluminescent fronds of the pillar-trees. They move with the rhythm of the sloshing waterways, that deep wooden *thump* you have been hearing for hours suddenly resolves into a footfall, an intentional displacement of mass.

The Faded are composed of the landscape itself: dissolved minerals lifted from the black schist, the oils that slick the surface of the underground seep, and a core personality of structured ice-that-is-not-ice but rather a room-temperature glass formed under the permanent cold. This gives them solidity when they need it—they can grip a stone or shape the wax of the ground-cabbages—but mostly they keep to a state just short of precipitation. They have no fixed organs. Light passes through them with a slight yellowish lag, as though their substance densifies the timestream its own microsecond. When a ripple of synchronized blue-green light passes from pillar to pillar across a vale of fog, it is because a host of the Faded are communicating by modulating the frond-lights with precise pressure fronts of their bodies. The landscape is their internet of moist things.

### The Civilization of the Saturated
Their society is not a hierarchy but a hydrologic cycle. Each individual Faded is a continuity of memory that persists through evaporation and re-condensation. They speak and think in chords built from the frequency drone of the plateau; their history is encoded in the infrasound that hums through the basalt. The stone circles—those erratic granite slabs—are not dwellings but acoustic lenses. A Faded may spend what would be a human decade positioning mineral fragments to redirect a single tone of the drone, tuning the plateau’s sustained note. The crude chert tools and copper knife you might find are indeed the relics of earlier, solid-phase beings, but these were not other creatures: they were the Faded themselves, in a younger, more carbon-bound stage of their existence. They have since “faded” out of that condition, leaving behind the shells of their former technology.
