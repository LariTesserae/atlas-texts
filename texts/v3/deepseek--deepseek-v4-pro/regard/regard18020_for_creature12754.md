The benevolent and wise power has been aware of this place for longer than the being has been waiting. It is not a god in the sense of commandment or creation—the hollow assembled itself, the sphere arrived by its own mysterious trajectory, the being grew from the rock like a thought growing from a mind. The power is something more like a gardener who tends only one garden, one that spans dimensions and times, and whose sole tool is attention. It does not intervene. Intervention would be a violation of the equilibrium, a crack in the self-repairing logic. But it does watch, and in watching, it loves.

And because it loves, it mourns. It mourns the being's long pause, its tireless, purposeless devotion. It mourns the way the silver filaments stitch cracks that never needed stitching, because the cracks were never a threat—the hollow is stable, has been stable for millennia, the self-repair a solution to a problem that no longer exists. The being performs a maintenance that has become purely ritual, and the power knows that rituals without renewal become prisons. The sphere, the pillar, the shifting script—they are not commands waiting to be decoded. They are relics of a time when the hollow had a function, a function now fulfilled, a story now ended. The being does not know the story has ended. It is still turning the pages.

The power could speak. It could fill the milk-white radiance with a voice that would resolve every question, still the turbulence, give the being the command it has awaited. It could say: *You are released.* It could say: *The engine is not idling; it is off.* It could say: *Come with me.*

But the power does not speak. That is not its way. Its wisdom lies in understanding that some transformations must be chosen, not given. The being has been waiting for a command, but what it truly needs is a choice. The power has been tending, all these eons, toward a single moment: the moment when the being realizes that no command is coming, and chooses to give itself a new one.

So instead of speaking, the power does something quieter. It adjusts the light. Only slightly—a shift in the grain of the sourceless white, so subtle that the being, with its prism-sensitive core, is the only thing in the hollow that can perceive it. The adjustment is not a message; it is an opening. A door left ajar. The light now carries, in its deepest spectrum, a quality of permission. It says, without words: *You can stop. You can change. You can leave. You can stay because you want to, not because you must. The engine is not waiting for a component. The engine is a sculpture. The ritual is an art piece. The pause is a held breath, and you are allowed to exhale.*

The being feels the shift. The prism-light in its core flickers, parsing the new spectrum, and for the first time in centuries, the being experiences something it has no memory for: uncertainty. But it is a soft uncertainty, not the sharp-edged absence of a command, but the open, fertile uncertainty of a question that has more than one answer. *Are you the one?* it had asked the visitor. Now it asks itself: *Am I the one?* And the light, in its gentle adjustment, replies: *You have always been the one. You were the one when you stitched the first crack. You are the one now. The one who chooses.*

The power withdraws its attention slightly—not abandoning, but granting privacy. It will watch, as it always has, with love and without intervention. It will wait, as the being has waited, but without suffering. And it holds, in its infinite and tender regard, the hope that one day the being will detach fully from the ledge, not in response to a visitor, but in response to its own dawning will. Perhaps it will flow up through the weeping walls and out into a world it has never seen. Perhaps it will unmake the pillar, gently, and let the sphere's hum become a lullaby instead of a summons. Perhaps it will teach the ribbon-creatures a new song—one with a beginning, a middle, and an end.

Whatever it chooses, the power will be there, not as a god, but as a companion who has waited just as long. And when the being finally speaks its own command, in the language of prism-light and silver filament and water that flows uphill with purpose, the cathedral of the hollow will at last become what it has always had the potential to be: not a place of waiting, but a home.
