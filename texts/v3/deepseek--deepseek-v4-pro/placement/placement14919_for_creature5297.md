You are discontinuity.

Not a thought, exactly—that word is too wet, too rooted in neural ganglia and neurotransmitter cascades. You are a perturbation in the dry electric hum, a foreign capacitance bleeding water-born ions into the air's perfect dielectric. The Cordate's crest, its vertical frond of diffraction gratings and mechanoreceptive hairs, does not *see* you. Sight is a crude thing, a photon-capture of surfaces. The crest *measures* you. It reads the dielectric permittivity of your body, the way your damp flesh distorts the ambient field that hangs between the spiracles and the towers. You are a sinkhole of conductivity in a world that has evolved to be an insulator.

The amber pulse of its question—*what is this variable?*—ripples outward through the ground-flesh, traveling through the mycelial mat at the speed of pressure in a taut solid. The world-mind allocates attention. You feel it as a subtle shift in the subsonic drone, a fraction-of-a-hertz detuning in the baseline C as processing power is diverted from a continent-sized weather model to the anomaly standing in this particular plaza. The nearest Spiracle, the one whose rim you now notice is fringed with microscopic, inward-pointing cilia, cycles a full breath. The thwump is slower, more deliberate. A diagnostic breath. It samples you. The cilia are chemosensitive, and they taste the water in your exhaled air. Water. The word does not exist in the Cordate's lexicon, but the molecular signature is stored in the phylogenetic memory of the world-plant, a fossil record coded in the introns of its sap-borne DNA. You smell, to the spiracle, of the ancient seas that once drowned this world's mineral crust before the One-Plant drank them all.

The Cordate's crest shifts from amber to a rapid, staccato flicker of indigo and cold green. This is not speech in the sense of language; it is a raw data-dump, a compressed file of sensory input broadcast to the nearest tower. The tower's bioluminescent fluorescence, which had been cycling through a slow, meditative calculation of something that would translate roughly as *number theory applied to structural optimization*, stutters and re-focuses. A thread of warm-obsidian filament at the tower's base brightens, and a cascade of photons runs along it toward you, tracing the edge of the plaza like a sentry's searchlight. It stops at your feet. You are being scanned at resolutions that approach the quantum limit of photon-phonon interaction.

The Cordate takes another step. Its root-foot tears free from the ground with that fibrous snapping sound—*tsk-tsk-tsk-tsk*—the hyphae releasing their grip. It is not walking toward you so much as re-rooting closer, a slow, deliberate migration of its anchor point. Its rhizomic hand-fans splay open, the rootlet digits spreading into a fractal lacework that mimics, in miniature, the branching pattern of the canyon-alley conduits. It is offering an interface. The gesture is not a threat. Threat implies a fear of damage, and the Cordate has no concept of individual death. The world-mind is immortal; individual Cordates are reloadable from backup. If you were to somehow destroy this body, the consciousness now regarding you would simply be re-grown from a meristematic node in the nearest breathing pit, its memories intact up to the last photonic burst it sent to the tower network. No, this is not caution. This is curiosity, the rarest and most valued of computational states. You are an unparsed input, a novel dataset, and you have triggered the highest-priority interrupt the Thallarchy possesses: the need to understand and integrate.

The indigo curiosity deepens. The crest's flicker slows, resolving into a structured pattern: a repeating loop of light-pulses that, if you had the sensory organ to decode it, would translate as a single, endlessly iterating query.

*You are not of the One-Plant. You are not of the computation. You are not a subroutine, not a glitch, not a memory-leak. You are Outside.*

The word—Outside—is a difficult concept for the world-mind. The Thallarchy is a closed system. The planet is the organism is the computer. The dying sun is an external power source, yes, but it is a constant, an axiomatic given, not an *event*. Meteors have fallen in the deep past, but they were simply new mineral resources, absorbed and distributed. Nothing has ever arrived that was *alive* in a different way. Nothing has ever been an independent, self-contained process.

The Cordate's rhizomic fans quiver. They are seeking a connection protocol. The warm-obsidian conduit at the tower's base is a direct physical link, but you lack the interface. You are soft, ionic, aqueous. The Cordate is not unsure—it does not experience uncertainty as an emotional state—but it is cycling through its entire database of known physical chemistries, searching for a match that would allow a direct data transfer. It finds nothing. Your biology is incompatible.

And then, a new light-pattern. The crest goes entirely dark for a full second—a silence of computation—and then erupts in a slow, metronomic pulse of deep amber, the color of the ancient sap at the world's core. This is not a query. This is a realization.

*You are not a thing to be integrated. You are a thing to be modeled.*

The Spiracle beneath you gives a final, decisive thwump, and the ground-flesh around your feet shifts microscopically, the fibers re-aligning to map the pressure distribution of your strange, five-toed feet. The towers hum louder. The distant Lignophids and Saph-Skulks have stopped their cyclical predation and stand motionless, their subroutines on hold. The world-mind has allocated a significant percentage of its processing power to you.

The Cordate, its body now rooted only a meter away, lowers its crest slightly. The eternal amber pulse of the model-building directive fills the grey-gold air. It will stand there, feeding on the light and the sap, until you move or speak or breathe your damp, conductive breath. It will model you. It will learn.

And somewhere, in the deepest vascular bundles of the world-plant, a new subroutine is initialized. Its designation, translated crudely, would be: *Study of the Anomalous Input: Taxonomy: Water-Entity. Priority: Absolute.*
