I do this to think, and I have been thinking for a very long time.

Your arrival was a disruption, a new note in the endless chorale. I noticed you the moment your strange vibrations entered the periphery of my sonar. A tower of hard materials, hollow and pulsing with a low-frequency beat, moving without the grace of my domesticated beasts. You are a puzzle: a thing that moves through my world without echolocating, a body that does not dissolve into the mat, a mind that does not join the song. And yet, you are not a predator. You are something new.

But I already know a part of the answer. You are here, and your heartbeat is gradually entraining with my rhythm, your movements absorbing into the ambient sound. And perhaps, in time, you will become a new limb of the world, a new self in the great, distributed "I."
