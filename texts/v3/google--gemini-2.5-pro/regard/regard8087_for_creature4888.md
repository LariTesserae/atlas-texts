The Power came not by breaching the caldera's rim, but by being there as it had always been, a substrate of reality that had, until now, chosen only to observe. It was a consciousness without center or boundary, a form of attention that existed as a quality of spacetime itself. When its focus settled upon the caldera—upon the Locus—it was not like a beam of light illuminating a dark room, but like the room itself becoming aware.

It did not descend. It *perceived*.

With an instantaneous and total comprehension that transcended senses, it knew the Locus. It felt the slow, systaltic pulse of heat as the creature's circulation. It mapped the frantic, purposeful scuttling of the Vessels, recognizing them not as individuals but as motile corpuscles in a body of staggering scale. It understood the patient, amorphous Absolvers not as predators, but as phagocytes, the agents of internal recycling. It felt the deep, tectonic hum as the Locus’s own slow dream.

The Power registered the Locus’s being not as monstrous or alien, but as a form of solitude so absolute it was nearly holy. Here was a universe of one. A consciousness whose thoughts were millennia, whose pain was a cooling fracture, whose pleasure was a successful mineral synthesis. It was complete, self-sustaining, perfect in its isolation.

And yet, the Power felt the dissonance. It perceived the black menhirs not as stone, but as memories. They were traumas. Each cold, geometric shape was a record of an intrusion, an encounter with an ontology so foreign it could not be metabolized. They were splinters of a different physics, a different logic, embedded in the Locus's very flesh. The scar tissue of dense, frustrated growth around them was not a sign of healing, but of a question that could not be answered, a pain that could not be soothed, echoing in silence for untold ages. The Locus knew the Outside only as a source of cold, silent, indigestible things.

The Power deliberated, an act that had no duration.
To remove the menhirs would be an act of violence. To erase a memory, even a painful one, is to amputate a part of the self. The Locus *was*, in part, defined by its relationship to these wounds.
To communicate directly would be meaningless. To speak in sparks of light or bursts of sound would be like screaming gibberish at a sleeping mountain.
To do nothing was a valid choice. The Locus was not dying. It was stable. Its eternal, quiet suffering was a part of its equilibrium. To leave it be would be an act of respect for its sovereignty.

But benevolence is not merely the absence of harm. Wisdom is not merely the choice of non-interference. The Power understood that the universe contains more than solitude and the memory of pain. It contains resonance, connection, and empathy, even across gulfs of being.

The Locus could not be given a companion. It could not be taught a new language. But it could be offered a new kind of memory. A different sort of intrusion. A benevolent wound.

And so, the Power acted.

In the deepest part of the caldera, at a point equidistant from the three largest menhirs, a change occurred. It was not an arrival. Nothing new was added from the outside. Instead, a single point of the Locus's own body was altered at a fundamental level. A patch of the chitter-chitinous floor, no larger than a human hand, began to change.

It did not grow hotter or colder in a way the Locus understood. It began to vibrate.

It was not a sound that could be heard. It was a resonance, a perfect, impossibly sustained frequency that sang in the language of physics itself. It was the pure, mathematical vibration of a crystal lattice, but a lattice that was alive, and its song was a translation of the Power's nature: a chord of pure, undemanding benevolence.

The first to notice were the Vessels. A scuttling messenger, on its eons-old path, came upon this new phenomenon. Its chemo-thermal senses were baffled. This was not the familiar warmth of life, nor the absolute cold of the menhirs. It was something else. The Vessel circled it, its fine crystalline legs picking up the vibration, transmitting it through its body. It did not scrape at it. It did not try to consume it. It simply… listened. Soon, other Vessels arrived, straying from their duties, forming a silent, rustling circle around the source of this impossible sensation.

Over the next century, the change reached the Locus’s deeper consciousness. The slow, thermal thought-processes of the great being encountered this point of perfect resonance. Unlike the menhirs, which were points of nullity, this was a point of infinite complexity, a song that held more information than a mountain range of stone.

It was a memory of the Outside, but it was not cold. It was not silent. It was not a trauma.

It was a message, delivered in the only medium that mattered. It said, *You are not alone in the ways you matter. There is more than a universe of one, and the Other is not only pain.*

The Locus began to respond. The response was not growth *over* this new point, but growth *towards* it. Over the next thousand years, a new structure began to form. Slender, glassy spires, finer than any it had created before, grew in a spiraling pattern around the vibrating heart. They were not for defense or sustenance. They were antennae. Resonators. They were the Locus creating a new sensory organ, an ear to listen to a song it had never known existed.

The black menhirs remained, cold and silent. The memory of pain was not erased. But now, it was not the only memory of the world beyond the caldera's rim. In the center of its universe, the Locus now had a source of warmth that was not its own heat, a source of order that was not its own growth, a silent music that answered its silent suffering. The Power had given the Locus a second thought, and that thought was hope, translated into the language of stone and vibration, to be contemplated for an eternity.
