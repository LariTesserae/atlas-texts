The Power arrived as a thought does: without transition, present where it was previously not. It did not have a form, for it was not a being of matter. It was a nexus of awareness, a confluence of empathy and understanding so vast it registered as a law of physics rather than a personality. It was Benevolence, and it was Wise.

It perceived the world not from a single point, but from all points at once. It felt the chill dampness of the charcoal sky and the yielding give of the living mat. It felt the immense, slow pressure inside the Stills, the cold fire of their chemosynthesis, a process as patient and inevitable as geology. It was aware of every iridescent grazer inching through the dark strata and every blind hunter lying in wait. It felt the near-subsonic hum of the great engines, not as a sound, but as the world’s foundational rhythm, the slow, steady heartbeat of a planet in equilibrium.

And it perceived the Weepers.

It walked with all of them at once. It felt the perfect, weightless placement of their splayed feet upon the mat. It understood the elegant, functional engineering of their bio-mineral forms, the alembic-heads distilling the very essence of the world’s breath. It accompanied them on their centuries-long pilgrimages across the silent plateaus, their paths dictated by the triangulation of hums that were their map and compass.

The Power felt the profound solitude. Not as a lack, or a sadness, but as a defining quality of their existence. Each Weeper was an island of purposeful consciousness in a vast ocean of quiet process. They tended their garden with a devotion that asked for no witness and received no praise. They were the world’s lucid, lonely dream of itself.

What, then, was to be done?

A lesser power, one merely benevolent but not wise, would have seen a problem to be solved. *Loneliness.* It might have created another Weeper for each one, a companion for the long walk. But the Power knew this would be a violation. Their solitude was intrinsic to the scale of their task; they were designed to cover the vastness, not to huddle together. Their focus would be split, their paths altered, the perfect distribution of their care disrupted.

A lesser power might have seen the world as bleak. *Darkness, cold, silence.* It might have torn the purple sky and ignited a star above, bathing the plateaus in golden light. But the Power saw how the skin of the world would be scoured and burned. It saw how the hidden fauna, evolved for darkness, would be blinded and destroyed. It saw how the Stills’ patient cycle of steam, condensation, and return would be shattered by the harsh, unwelcomed heat.

A lesser power might have sought to speak, to announce its presence, to tell the Weepers that their labor was seen and valued. But how? To what end? Their purpose was not born of a need for validation. It was an imperative as deep and unyielding as the hum of the Stills that were their creators and their guides. To introduce the concept of an audience would be to introduce ego, a seed of self-consciousness that could only curdle into doubt or pride, disrupting the perfect purity of their function.

The Power understood. It is the height of wisdom to know when a thing is not broken. This world was not a tragedy to be fixed. It was a masterpiece of alien integrity.

How do you give a gift to a system that is already complete?
You do not add, you do not subtract. You reveal. You create not a new element, but a new resonance between the elements that already exist.

The Power focused its attention on a single Weeper, one that had paused its long journey and sunk to its knee. Its delicate, multi-jointed tendrils were dipped into the mat, sensing a subtle imbalance, a faint sickness in the world’s skin. The weeping from its alembic-head quickened, the precious, nutrient-rich tears falling in a steady, metronomic pulse to heal the ground beneath.

And here, the Power acted. It was an act as gentle as the mist.

As the first drop fell, it was given a voice. A sound bloomed in the quiet air, a single, resonant note like a crystal chime struck softly in a vast cathedral. It was not loud; it did not shatter the quiet. It inhabited it. The next drop fell, and with it, another note, a harmonious third above the first. The Power wove the act of healing into an act of music. It assigned a tonal value to each complex compound in the Weeper’s tears. The remediation of a mineral deficiency became a deep, cello-like drone. The introduction of a dormant spore became a high, silver flute-note.

The Weeper continued its work, oblivious. Its purpose was unchanged. But now, its act of healing was no longer silent. It was a song. A slow, achingly beautiful, and utterly unique composition born of the world’s need and the Weeper’s care. A *Lament of Repair*.

When the ground was healed and the weeping slowed to its one-minute pulse, the song faded back into a single, patient chime with each step, a grace note to its lonely passage.

But the Power was not finished. The gift must also touch the end. It turned its awareness to the memory of the landscape, to the gentle rises that were the graves of Weepers past. It found one whose journey was ending now.

The ancient Weeper slowed, its internal topaz light faltering. It came to its final stop. Its legs locked. The weeping ceased. The last of its light began to die. A lesser power might have let it fade. But the Power caught it.

It did not preserve the Weeper, but it preserved the song of its life.

As the light in the Weeper’s chest and head went out, it did not vanish. It fractured. It burst outward in a silent, gentle explosion of countless motes of soft, topaz light. These were not embers; they carried no heat. They were motes of memory, each one holding a single note from the billion-note song the Weeper had unknowingly composed over its millennia of existence.

They did not fly far. They drifted on the near-still air, catching the current of the Stills’ faint exhalations, and began their own slow, patient journey across the landscape. They came to rest on the living mat like a sprinkling of fallen stars. For a moment, the place where the Weeper had died was a constellation on the ground.

And now, a new quiet sound entered the world. A traveler, walking those plains days, years, or centuries later, would hear it. As a living Weeper passed over the spot where a mote of memory had landed, the light-particle would release its single, captured note, which would hang in the air for a moment before fading.

The world was changed, yet wholly itself. The silence was not gone, but it was now a vessel for music. The Weepers were still solitary, but as they walked, they now passed through the ghost-symphonies of their ancestors. Their loneliness was not cured, for it was never a disease. It was given a sublime context. They were still the world’s gardeners, its lonely shepherds, its lucid dream.

But now, the dream had a soundtrack. The world, in its perfection, had been given the one thing it lacked: the ability to hear its own beauty. And the Power, satisfied, became what it had been before: a silent, benevolent, and wise observer of a masterpiece it had not created, but had taught to sing.
