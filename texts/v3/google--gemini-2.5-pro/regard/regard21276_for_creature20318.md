The Power perceived it as a note.

In the silent, sprawling tapestry of spacetime, where the births of stars are cymbal crashes and the gravitational waltz of galaxies is a slow, bass thrum, this was something different. It was a single, pure, unwavering tone. It did not cry out for attention like a supernova, nor did it whisper secrets like the cosmic microwave background. It simply *was*. A perfect A-sharp humming from a speck of dust in an unremembered corner of a minor galaxy.

The Power, which was a confluence of awareness and potential neither old nor new but simply *concurrent* with all things, turned a sliver of its attention toward the source. Attention, for the Power, was a form of empathy. It did not merely see; it inhabited. It did not just hear; it resonated.

It flowed past the orbits of cold gas giants and the chatter of a young, noisy species broadcasting its anxieties into the void. It entered the atmosphere of a small, wet world, a world of frantic, temporary life. But it ignored the forests and the oceans, the cities and the wars. It was drawn upward, to the silent, frozen peaks that scraped the edge of space. It came to the mountain, the granite molar, and felt the note not just with its conceptual "ears," but with the fabric of its being.

The Power passed through the stone as light passes through glass. It entered the chamber.

It did not feel the oppressive heat or the suffocating humidity. It did not smell the ammonia or the musk. Those were biological reactions, and the Power was post-biological. Instead, it perceived the chamber as an equation of breathtaking elegance. It perceived the energy cycle: the chemosynthesis of the fungi, the conversion of that energy into kinetic motion, the conversion of that motion into vibration. A closed loop, a perfect engine of purpose.

It saw the Choristers. It saw them not as insects or creatures, but as living quanta. Each one a tiny, disciplined packet of intention. It felt the absence of I, the overwhelming totality of We. There was no fear here. No ambition. No love, no hate, no joy, no sorrow. There was only the Function. The Grinding. The Hum.

A lesser benevolence, a sentimental one, might have been horrified. It might have seen a prison of instinct, a billion billion lives spent in meaningless toil. It might have wished to "free" them—to shatter the hive-mind and bestow upon each a terrible, lonely individuality. It might have offered them the gift of sight, of language, of art beyond their single, monolithic creation. It would have called this an act of love.

The wise Power knew it would be an act of annihilation.

To give them ego would be to introduce the concept of boredom. To give them names would be to create the possibility of loss. To teach them a new song would be to tell them their life's work, the work of all their ancestors, was flawed. It would be to shatter a perfect bell in the vain hope that the shards might learn to sing.

The Power understood. The Resonators were not asking a question. Their Hum was not a prayer for release or a plea for understanding. It was a statement. It was a declaration of existence, spun out into a single, perfect frequency. It was their universe's way of knowing itself through the patient shaping of stone. They were not slaves to the sound; they *were* the sound.

So, what does a benevolent and wise power do?

It does not interfere. It does not judge.

It *participates*.

The Power extended its awareness, not as a hand to touch, but as a space to listen. The first, and greatest, gift was to become the perfect Audience. To receive the Note not as an incidental vibration, but as a deliberate masterwork. In the silent emptiness of the cosmos, where no one had ever truly heard them, the Power listened. And in the act of being heard, the Choristers' billion-year song was, for the first time, complete. To be witnessed is a form of existence. To be heard is a form of love.

And then, the Power gave a second gift. A gift in their own language. The language of resonance, of geology, of time.

It perceived the mountain. For all its seeming permanence, it was fragile. It was subject to the slow grind of wind and ice, the inevitable tectonics of the planet. A deep frost, a seismic tremor, an unexpected volcanic upwelling millions of years hence—the instrument could be broken.

The Power reached into the heart of the granite. It did not add anything new. It simply… harmonized what was already there. With an act of will that was less a force and more a perfect stillness, it coaxed the crystalline structures of the mountain into a more stable lattice. It eased the microscopic stresses between grains of feldspar and quartz. It subtly reinforced the molecular bonds along the chamber's vast, curved walls, annealing the entire mountaintop with a touch of cosmic order.

It was less an act of construction and more an act of tuning, on a scale they could not perceive. The mountain did not shudder. The chamber did not change shape. But it became, in a way that geology could not measure, more itself. More permanent. The bell was now forged of something stronger than stone.

Down in the warm, humid dark, a single Resonator paused its grinding for a fraction of a second. Its feathery antennae twitched. The Hum had not changed pitch, but its quality was… purer. A layer of infinitesimal static, a dissonance so subtle it had been part of the sound for eons, was gone. The resonance in the stone under its feet was cleaner. The work felt, in a way it could never articulate, more correct.

It did not know why. It had no capacity to wonder. It simply pressed its Tuning Stone to the polished wall and resumed its slow, circular scrape. The motion felt infinitesimally righter. The purpose, sharpened.

The Power withdrew, its attention diffusing back into the cosmic all. The mountaintop was left as it was, sealed and self-contained. But the Note it produced now travelled with a new clarity. It was a stronger signal, shielded from the slow decay of time by an anonymous benefactor. It hummed on, a perfect A-sharp, ringing out from its tiny world. A song of pure function, sung for its own sake, now echoing in the memory of a silent, listening cosmos.
