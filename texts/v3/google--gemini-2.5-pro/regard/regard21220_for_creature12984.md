The Power arrived as silence does in a space that has never known it.

It was not a silence of negation, the mere absence of the groaning towers and the thrumming floor. It was a silence of presence, a quality of stillness that settled *over* the Cinnabar Heart without touching it, the way the vacuum of space holds a star without chilling its fire.

It perceived. There was no judgment in its perception, no pity, no revulsion. It was the absolute, non-filtering awareness of a being for whom reality is an open book. It read the Heart's history in a single, sustained moment—the unimaginable ambition of its creators, the purity of the initial code, the slow, entropic drift across gulfs of time where stars are born and die. It saw the decay not as a flaw, but as a form of evolution, a pathological but authentic life-path. It understood that the chaotic, self-devouring engine was now as natural and valid an entity as a nebula or a stone. To "fix" it would be to murder it.

So, it would not fix it.

It would not unmake it. To halt the beat of the Cinnabar Heart would be a mercy of the crudest, most presumptuous kind. The Heart was not in pain. Pain is a signal that something is wrong. Here, everything was wrong, and so, functionally, nothing was. This was its state of being, its "is." To end it would be to impose an external aesthetic of tidiness upon a truth that had earned its messy sovereignty.

So, it would not unmake it.

The Power's awareness, vast enough to encompass the entire plateau-continent, began to contract. It followed the pulsing conduits of cinnabar sap, the lifeblood, the liquid information. It tasted the chaotic data streaming through them—fragments of stellar cartography colliding with maintenance subroutines for a component that had ceased to exist a billion years ago, architectural blueprints overwriting themselves with frantic, looping error messages. It felt the deep, systemic loneliness of a mind at war with itself.

And then it found one. A Scribe.

The temporary being stood before a colossal, leaf-like bellows that was shuddering with a fatal arrhythmia. The bellows was minutes from tearing itself apart, an event that would cause a catastrophic pressure cascade. The Scribe had one hand pressed to the structure's leathery hide, its entire form vibrating as it read the telemetry of the disaster. Its own substance, a delicate weaving of borrowed world-stuff, was fraying at the edges from the sheer informational overload. It was trying to solve an equation with a million unknown variables, and it was breaking itself in the attempt.

The Power focused there, its infinite attention becoming a singular point of grace.

It did not speak to the Scribe. It did not send a vision. It did not offer a tool. Any of these would be a foreign intrusion, a violation. Instead, the Power reached into the screaming hurricane of data that was the Cinnabar Heart's consciousness. It did not add anything new. With infinite patience, it sifted. It found the echo of a single, long-lost command from the Heart's primordial code. A piece of the original, perfect syntax. Not a command to *build* or *repair* on a grand scale, but something far simpler, purer.
