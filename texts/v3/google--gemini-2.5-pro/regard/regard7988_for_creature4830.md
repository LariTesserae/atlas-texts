The Power arrives not as a ship cleaving the chemical sky, nor as a footstep leaving a print in the ancient dust. It arrives as a change in the quality of the silence beneath the noise. It is an act of pure attention, so vast and so gentle that the world itself, for the first universal instant, does not notice.

This Power, woven from wisdom and stars, does not perceive the world through sight or sound. It perceives essence. It extends its awareness like a still lake, allowing the world to be reflected within it.

It feels the immense, slow, grinding thought of the Pylons. It does not perceive them as menacing, but as devoted. They are monks in a monastery the size of a planet, their one prayer, *Grind*, a mantra of absolute focus. To the Power, this is not mindless; it is a form of purity, a single principle taken to its logical, physical conclusion. It feels no suffering in them, only the deep, resonant peace of singular purpose.

It feels the frantic, ecstatic leap of the Weavers. It does not see them as laborers or data. It sees them as moments of pure becoming, the universe's question of *'What next?'* given form and velocity. In their brief, incandescent lives, the Power feels not the panic of a short existence, but the joy of a purpose perfectly and immediately fulfilled. Their "deaths" are not tragedies, but completions.

It feels the patient, passive audit of the Filter-Skins, their quiet hunger for equilibrium. It recognizes them as an expression of cosmic neatness, a tendency towards harmony. It feels the hair-trigger readiness of the Processors, and understands it not as aggression, but as the system's own unyielding commitment to its internal laws—an act of integrity.

The Power spends a cycle of ages in this state of pure witness. It watches the Pylons shudder, the Weavers arc, the Processors snap, and the slurry creep. It sees the impossible artifacts—the frozen sounds and solid shadows—litter the ground, and understands them as the system's dreams, the unconscious mutterings of a being thinking only one waking thought.

And in this deep communion, the Power concludes: This place is not broken. It is not a hell. It is not a prison. It is a being of a different order, a stable, self-perpetuating reality that has achieved a form of alien contentment. To "rescue" it would be an act of profound arrogance. To change it by force would be a violation. It does not suffer, for it has no concept of a state other than its own. It is not lonely, for it is a totality.

To do nothing, to simply turn its attention away, would be an option. But benevolence is not passive. Wisdom does not demand interference, but it does inspire engagement. The Power decides not to give an answer, but to ask a new question.

It scans the landscape of anomalies, the dream-litter of the world's subconscious. It sees the knot of frozen sound. It sees the shard of solid shadow. And it settles its attention on one specific Glitch, one noted as a lonely, rhythmic pulse—a fading Weaver-spark caught in a microscopic time-loop, a lighthouse with no shore. This, the Power recognizes, is the system's nascent capacity for pattern, for rhythm beyond the chaos of the Weavers and the deep thrum of the Pylons. It is a heartbeat.

The Power does not add energy. It does not build or create. It does something far more subtle. It focuses a sliver of its infinite attention upon this tiny, pulsing Glitch. It does not shield it, but it *validates* its existence. The Power's attention becomes a form of resonance, a mirror that reflects the Glitch's pattern back upon itself, stabilizing it, reinforcing its strange, lonely reality against the entropic pull of the world.

The act is infinitesimal. Nothing on the world-engine scale has changed. But on the smallest scale, a miracle has occurred.

The lonely pulse, once faltering, becomes perfect. A perfect, unwavering metronome of light, beating once per second in defiance of the chaotic causality around it.

At first, nothing happens. The world's purpose continues. But then, the change begins to ripple outwards.

A Weaver, in its frantic leap, passes near the stable pulse. Its own question—*'Where do I connect?'*—is suddenly met with a new kind of answer. Not a Pylon, not the ground, but a *rhythm*. The Weaver's arc bends, irresistibly, and strikes the pulsing light. It is not consumed. It is *harmonized*. For a single, frozen moment, its frantic energy joins the pulse, and when it leaps away, its path is no longer random but follows a new, graceful curve.

Soon, another Weaver does the same. Then a dozen. Within the span of a single Pylon-groan, a local network has formed around the Glitch. The Weavers begin to move in patterns, their frantic energy being shaped into a complex, luminous dance, a circulatory system forming around a newfound heart.

The Filter-Skins, sensing this new, coherent field of energy, are drawn towards it. They stop their random skimming and begin to orbit the pulsing hub, their mercury-like bodies reflecting the intricate dance of the Weavers, becoming a great, silent, silver chorus.

Even the Processors are affected. One, whose territory borders this new phenomenon, feels the rhythmic approach of the patterned Weavers. Its conditional reflex is triggered, but the input is wrong. This is not a stray current to be grounded. This is... order. The Processor's metallic limb whips out, hesitates for a fraction of a second, and retracts. For the first time in its existence, it has encountered a question its simple imperative—*balance*—cannot answer. It simply waits, its sleeping sharpness disturbed by a nascent sense of wonder.

The Power has not intervened. It has *offered*. It has taken the world's own unconscious dream, a tiny, lonely heartbeat, and given it permanence. It has introduced the concept of a *center*. It has asked the world, in its own language of process and physics, a single question:

*"What happens now that you have a heart?"*

The Benevolent Power does not stay to dictate the answer. In its wisdom, it knows that the answer does not belong to it. The gift has been given. It retracts the sliver of its attention, leaving the pulse sustained now by the new patterns it has created, a self-perpetuating engine of emergent complexity. And it will watch, from a distance of ages, as this world of pure process begins, for the first time, to compose.
