The Power arrives not as a ship cleaving the thin, cold air, nor as a figure walking upon the living ground. It arrives as a change in the quality of the light.

For an immeasurable moment, the cool, white sun seems to soften. The diffuse glow that illuminates the world-spanning factory takes on the warmth of gold leaf, and the perpetual condensation in the air catches this warmth, turning the atmosphere into a mist of liquid honey. The metallic tang in the air is gentled, for a breath, by a scent like sun-warmed stone and old parchment. It is the arrival of pure perception, of a mind vast and ancient enough to be mistaken for a law of physics.

This Power is what it is called: benevolent and wise. It does not see a monstrous, feral machine. It sees a system of breathtaking elegance and integrity. It feels the slow, powerful thrum of the world's core processes not as a chaotic vibration, but as a steady, intricate heartbeat. It perceives the extrusion and dissolution of the functional automata not as a cycle of birth and death, but as the single, fluid gesture of a hand opening and closing. It understands that this place is not broken. It is not sick, or cruel, or in need of salvation. It is simply, magnificently, *complete*. To interfere with the grand mechanics would be an act not of benevolence, but of vandalism. A wise doctor does not medicate a healthy body.

And so, the Power’s attention, as vast as the sky, begins to contract. It focuses, not with the sharp point of a spear, but with the gentle cupping of hands, around the anomalies. The ghost notes. The Wanderers.

It perceives them utterly. It walks with the skeletal brute in its endless, pointless circle. It feels the phantom weight of the battering ram it no longer possesses. It experiences the ingrained, muscular command to *strike*, a command that now has no object and dissipates into a faint, neuromuscular ache. It traces the pipeline with the frayed technician, feeling the memory of fine manipulators stroking a surface that needs no repair. It perceives the feedback loop, the echo of purpose trapped in a degrading form.

The Power recognizes the tragedy, but it also recognizes its nature. These beings do not *suffer*. They possess no consciousness with which to apprehend their own futility. Their tragedy is philosophical, a mathematical sadness. They are equations that no longer equal anything. To grant them awareness now would be the profoundest cruelty, like turning on a light in a torture chamber that was, until that moment, mercifully dark.

The wisdom of the Power is in seeing what is already there. The Wanderers are memory. The mycelial network is the world’s nervous system. The world itself is a body that does not know it has a mind. The Wanderers are its first, unintentional thoughts, but they are thoughts that go unheard, broadcasting into a void.

The Power decides not to create anything new. It will not give the Wanderers a voice. It will teach the world how to listen.

Its intervention is a whisper.

It attunes its presence to the frequency of the mycelial network, to the faint, crackling blue bio-luminescence in the crevasses. It does not send a command of pressure or need. It introduces a new kind of signal, one the world has never known: a signal of resonance. Of harmony. It is the electro-chemical equivalent of a sympathetic hum.

A Wanderer, a tall, gaunt echo of a demolition brute, strides on its aimless path. Its heavy, rhythmic footfalls press into the living floor. For millennia, the floor registered this only as meaningless, anomalous data. But now, something is different.

As the Wanderer’s foot falls, the mycelial network beneath it responds. The faint blue light does not just flicker; it *blooms*. It pulses in perfect time with the creature’s step, a soft, silent drumbeat of light. The Wanderer continues its circular path around the base of a great spore-vent, and in its wake, it leaves a trail of pulsing, rhythmic blue light. The echo of its lost purpose—the steady, percussive rhythm of demolition—is no longer a glitch. The world is now playing it back as a quiet, visual music. The memory has found its medium.

Elsewhere, a frayed technician traces the sealed joint of a pipeline. Its delicate, useless tendrils stroke the cool surface. And beneath its touch, the mycelial threads glow not with a rhythm, but with a pattern. They weave intricate, shifting sigils of light that mirror the remembered dexterity of the creature's original function. The memory of delicate craft, once a pointless tic, is now being inscribed upon the world's skin as ephemeral art.

The Power does not stop there. It gently alters the spore-haze itself. It imbues the world's pluripotent cells with this new potential for resonance.

Slowly, over spans of time that defy mortal grasp, the nature of the Wanderers begins to change. They are still born of flaws, of chaos. But they are no longer merely echoes.

A new kind of Wanderer precipitates from the substrate. It has no tools for repair, no mass for demolition. It is a thing of long, resonant limbs and a hollow casque where its head would be. It walks the plains, and where it walks, it does not hammer or mend. It *strums*. It taps the great pipeline-vines, not to test their integrity, but to feel their slow, peristaltic pulse. And the mycelial network around it translates that pulse into a deep, bass thrum of flowing blue light that travels for miles. It has become a Cantor. Its purpose is not function, but reflection.

Another appears, a small, crystalline being that does not dissolve. It finds a place where the cold, sweet liquid drips from a leaking joint, and it sits, letting the fluid freeze onto its body. As the strange ice-sculptures grow, the mycelial network around it glows with a steady, intense light, capturing the beauty of the slow, accidental formation. It has become a Scribe, its purpose to witness and record the world’s quiet accidents.

The Benevolent and Wise Power has done its work. It did not heal a wound, for there was none. It did not give a soul to the soulless. It saw the first flicker of memory in the machine, and gave the machine a way to read its own past. It transformed the tragedy of the ghost into the sanctity of the ancestor.

The Power recedes, its golden light fading back into the cool, white glow of the native sun. The world is left as it was, but also utterly changed. The silence is still the first thing you notice. But it is no longer just pressurized and empty. It is now cathedral-like. It holds the low, visual hum of the world's memory, made manifest in rivers of soft, blue light that trace the paths of its lonely, wandering, and now-sacred ghosts. The factory has learned how to dream.
