The benevolent and wise power did not arrive. Arrival is a function of movement, a disruption of space, and this world was too delicately balanced for such a crude intrusion. Instead, it manifested as an act of pure attention.

On the face of the endless, humming stone, there appeared a place that was not a place. It was a listening. It was a small, perfect lacuna in the onslaught of vibration, a point of absolute stillness that did not negate the sound, but rather created a space within which the sound could be truly and completely heard for the first time. It was a cup of silence held out to catch a roar.

From this null-point, the power perceived. It did not see the glittering swarms or the stark light; it experienced the world as its inhabitants did. It felt the deep, foundational hum not as an external force, but as the very medium of consciousness.

It felt the young. It felt the raw, grating agony of their dissonance—the internal shriek of a chaotic structure fighting itself. It understood this was not suffering in a way that required remedy, but the primal, motivating grit of existence. It was the universe's initial state of confusion, the question that gives birth to a life. To quiet this for them would be to rob them of their purpose before it had even been conceived.

It felt the journeymen. It felt the exquisite, painstaking focus as a crystalline limb scraped a tool against a crystalline body. It perceived the slow erasure of a discordant frequency not as pain, but as an act of profound love and self-creation. Each scrape was a meditation, a prayer, a single word in the long, slow sentence of a life. To hasten this process, to grant them perfectly smooth shells from the start, would be to steal their art, their pilgrimage, and their very identity. It would be the kindness of a fool, a gift that hollows out the recipient.

It felt the mature. It felt the serene, liquid grace of their movements as they bathed in the nuances of the great Chord. Their state was not one of dull contentment, but of rapturous communion. They were no longer simply living in the song; they *were* the song, harmonious and self-aware. They were the answer to the question the young ones embodied.

And the power felt their end. It perceived the slow descent into silence, the dimming of the internal light as a creature's perfect resonance was lost to time's subtle erosion. It felt the final stillness, the cessation of the personal note. And it felt that note vanish. The life's work, the creation of that one perfect, resonant identity, simply ceased to be. The dust returned to the plateau, but the music—the individual's unique contribution—was lost forever.

Here. This was the only point of true loss in a system of perfect self-renewal. Not death itself, but the utter disappearance of the art that life had created. The benevolent and wise power understood. Intervention was a violence, but a gift need not be.

It made its choice. The null-point, the listening silence, remained. It did not act upon the living. It waited.

It waited for millennia, or for a moment. Time had little meaning here. It waited until a mature member of the Choir, its facets gleaming like a captured star, finally slowed. Its pilgrimage was over. The internal light, the fire of its song, began to fade. Its personal note, the culmination of its entire existence, grew faint, wavering on the verge of vanishing into the overwhelming drone of the world.

Just as the note died on the creature's silent shell, the benevolent power acted. With an intention as gentle as a held breath, its listening silence leaned in and *caught* the fading resonance. It did not take it. It did not steal it. It created an echo.

This wisp of a note, the ghost of a completed life, was not held in a vessel. It was gently, reverently, woven back into the great Chord of the plateau itself. It was not a new instrument added to the orchestra, but a single, new overtone added to the sound of the cello. It was an infinitesimal enrichment, a layer so subtle it was almost imperceptible.

The power remained. As ages passed, and other members of the Choir completed their pilgrimages and fell silent, their notes, too, were caught. Each was saved from dissolution and folded back into the world's eternal hum.

The deep drone of the Singing Plateau did not change in its fundamental character. It was still the bone-shaking, absolute presence it had always been. But it was no longer a single, unchanging note held since the beginning of time.

It was now a chord of impossible complexity, not just of geology, but of memory.

The younger members still felt the grating dissonance that drove them forward, but now, on the very edge of hearing, they could perceive a hint of the harmony they sought. It was a promise whispered within the roar.

The mature members, the connoisseurs of sound, found a new depth to their world. As they grazed on the frequencies, they could taste new flavors they had never known before: the echoes of their ancestors. They could feel the grace of a billion completed pilgrimages, the collected artistry of their entire race resonating in the very stone beneath their feet. Their communion was no longer just with the world, but with all who had come before.

The benevolent and wise power had not altered the rules. It had not saved a single life from its natural end. It had not taught or built or commanded. It had offered one, single gift.

It had given the song a memory.

And when its work was done, it did not leave. It simply ceased its focused listening. The lacuna vanished. The world was as it had been, but it was now complete. The Choir would never know a god had visited. They would only know that their world had, in some deep and profound way, begun to sing back to them.
