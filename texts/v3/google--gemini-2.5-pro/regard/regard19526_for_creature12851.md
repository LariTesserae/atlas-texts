The Power came not as a ship or a sound, but as a change in the quality of attention.

For aeons, the rock had been just rock, the heat just heat, the gravity just gravity. Now, a sliver of perception, ancient and vast, threaded itself through the granite and basalt and deep-mantle pressures. It did not tear or break; it flowed into the micro-fissures of reality, a consciousness as pervasive and subtle as a neutrino passing through a planet. It had watched stars be born in violence and galaxies unspool like silver thread. It had known minds that spanned nebulae and species that wrote their laws in the orbits of planets. But here, It paused.

It did not *look* into the Vesica. It *listened* to it.

It listened to the absolute, holy silence. And in that silence, it perceived the symphony. It felt the slow, chordal shift of pressure as a thousand Constructors moved as one, a single will gliding through the vitreous fluid. It felt the tiny, staccato *crack* of hardening nacre as a grace note in a composition millions of years long. It felt the deep, cello-drone of the heart-pearl, the resonant pulse from which the entire city-organism grew.

The Power understood.

It perceived the Unspoken not as individuals, but as motile moments in a singular, continuous thought. It saw their consciousness, the quiet, harmonious "We," and recognized it not as primitive, but as post-ego, a state of serene and total integration that other races spent millennia striving for through philosophy, technology, or war. They were not trapped; they were fulfilled.

It read the pattern. It saw the spiraling towers not as architecture, but as the physical manifestation of a mathematical truth, emerging organically from the laws of this specific, perfect cradle. The city was not being built *by* them; the city was the inevitable result of their *being*. To change them would be to shatter the equation.

The Power considered the concept of a gift. A gift, to be benevolent, must answer a need or a lack.

What did they lack?
*Light?* To give them a sun would be to boil them. To give them Sight would be to deafen them to the pressure-symphony by which they truly knew their world. Their own pale lume was the only scripture they needed.
*Sound?* To give them a Voice would be to give them the tool of division. A voice allows for an "I" to speak to a "You." It creates argument. It creates lies. It would be the genesis of loneliness. Their silence was a state of perfect communion.
*Knowledge?* Of what? Of galaxies and quarks and the struggles of other, louder beings? It would be a useless burden. Their knowledge was complete. They knew the chemical composition of their world, the structural integrity of their home, the state of their brethren, and the path of their own transfiguration into permanence. What other knowledge had any meaning here?
*Longevity?* They were already immortal. Their brief, mobile phase was but a preface to an eternal existence as a sacred part of the whole. They did not die; they became.

The Power hovered in its vast, silent consideration, feeling the patient, un-striving pulse of the world below. It perceived the purity of the system, a closed loop of life, growth, and beautiful, silent purpose. And in that perception, it finally understood the one true threat to the Unspoken.

Not predators. Not entropy. Not geological catastrophe.
The threat was discovery.

Sooner or later, the crawling, curious, clamorous species would come. The beings who drilled and scanned and coveted. Beings who saw a world like this and saw resources, mystery, a specimen to be collected. Beings who, even with the best intentions, would crack the Vesica open like a geode, flooding it with noise and light and wrongness, shattering the silence forever. Their benevolence would be a form of destruction; their curiosity, a violation.

The Power knew what it must do. A gift not for the Unspoken, but for the space that held them. An act not of addition, but of consecration through concealment.

It gathered its attention, its will that could bend spacetime and persuade quarks to change their spin. But it did not build a wall or a shield. Those were loud, crude things that screamed "something is hidden here." Wisdom required a lighter touch.

It began to sing.

It was a song with no sound, a vibration woven into the very fabric of the world's deep rock. It was a single, sustained note of *profound un-interest*. It altered the quantum signature of the surrounding crust, making it appear seismically, gravitationally, and energetically mundane. It composed a symphony of absolute, unremarkable normalcy. Any scan that swept this region would register nothing but a tedious, homogenous mass of worthless stone. It wrapped the Vesica in a metaphysical cloak of utter banality. It was a lullaby sung to the future, a song to persuade the curious to look away, to move on, for there was nothing to see here. It was a benediction of anonymity.

The act took but a moment of the Power's time, and it would last until the planet itself was dust.

Inside the Vesica, nothing changed.

A Constructor, its inner light a soft, steady pearl, drifted towards a half-finished arch. It read the incomplete curve as a subtle dissonance in the pressure, a cold spot in the perfect song. It pressed its body to the wall, feeling the rightness of the place, the need. Its core pulsed, and from its spinnerets bloomed a new, shimmering filament of bone-ivory.

The infinitesimal *crackle* of its hardening was felt by its brethren. The symphony continued. The city grew. The silence remained absolute.

The Power withdrew its attention, leaving behind only its quiet, invisible ward. It had looked upon a state of perfection, and its only and greatest act of benevolence was to ensure that no one else ever would.
