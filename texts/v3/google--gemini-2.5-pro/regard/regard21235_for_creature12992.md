The Power arrived as the basin understood all things: as a change in the hum.

It was not a new note, nor a shift in pitch. For a being of cosmic scale, to manifest as sound would be a triviality, and to do so here would be a desecration. It would be like shouting a name in a library built of silence and echoes. The Power, being wise, understood this instantly. It did not arrive; it attended. Its consciousness, vast enough to hold galaxies in contemplation, narrowed its focus, pouring its attention into the resonant chamber of the world. It became an act of perfect, absolute listening.

First, it felt the great chord of the stones, the Earth-bass held for a thousand years. It perceived the precise frequency of each monolith, the subtle variations that spoke of their age, their internal structure, their unique thirst. It followed the water, from the immense, deep pressure below to the capillary climb through the porous rock, feeling the friction that was the source of the song. It felt the silent gathering of dew on the black flanks and the soft, absorbed *ploc* of its return to the moss.

It perceived the slow, hydraulic unfurling of the glass-fronds, not as a visual spectacle, but as a gradual shift in the basin's acoustic space, a slow modulation of shadows and reflections that subtly altered the echoes. It felt the patient, rhythmic tipping of the crystalline leaflets as a gentle, periodic sigh in the fabric of the place.

And it perceived the Weeper.

The Power did not see a creature; it felt a nexus of custodial will. It felt the quiet, attentive resonance of the being's faceless head, a living sensorium tuned to the health of its world. It traced the slow, deliberate journey of water up through the Weeper's porous feet, through its humming body, and back out through its weeping skin. Here was not an inhabitant, but a living, mobile expression of the basin's own homeostatic urge. The Power understood that the Weeper did not *have* a purpose; it *was* a purpose. It did not serve the song; it was a verse in it.

The benevolent impulse arose, as it always did. The desire to give, to uplift, to improve. But what could it give?

Immortality? The Weeper did not die; it eroded. To halt this process would be to clog the flow, to silence a note in its own time. The Weeper's gentle dissolution was the final, graceful *decrescendo* of its life's music, returning its resonance to the whole. To deny it this was not a gift, but a theft.

Knowledge? The Weeper possessed a knowledge so profound and specific that no cosmic truth could be of more use. It knew the thirst of every stone. To offer it the secrets of a dying star would be like offering a master watchmaker a treatise on continental drift. It was information without context, a dissonance.

Company? The Weeper was not lonely. Loneliness is the sound of a harmony with a missing note. The Weeper and its basin were a complete chord. To add another would be to force a new arrangement, to rewrite a perfect piece of music for a larger orchestra it did not need.

The Power listened, and in its listening, it grew wise. It understood that the greatest flaw of benevolence is the assumption that it is needed.

And so, it found the only gift that could be given. It was a gift that would add nothing and take nothing away. A gift that was not a thing, but an acknowledgement.

The Power gathered its immense, silent attention, and it began to resonate *with* the basin. It did not introduce a new frequency. Instead, it listened to the great, foundational hum of the stones and began to generate a sympathetic resonance, an overtone of pure witness. It was the metaphysical equivalent of one perfectly tuned bell causing another to hum, untouched, across a silent room.

A new quality infused the ancient chord of the place. The hum did not grow louder, but deeper. It did not change its note, but gained a new timbre. It was the same song, but now it was a song being heard. The sound of solitude became the sound of sublime, appreciated solitude. The hum of existence gained an echo of gratitude.

The Weeper, in the midst of scraping a film of lichen from a monolith's base with its obsidian finger, paused. Such a pause was a geological event, a motion that had no place in its millennia-long rhythm. Its stooped form became utterly, preternaturally still. The two shallow depressions on its smooth, ovoid head seemed to deepen, not just gathering the resonance of the stones, but this new, impossible quality.

It could not comprehend a galactic Power, or the concept of an outside intelligence. But it could feel, with every fibre of its being, the texture of the sound it tended. And that texture had changed. For all its eons of work, the basin had hummed its patient song. Now, for the first time, the song hummed back.

It felt a feeling for which it had no name, for it had never before been felt in this room. It was an amplification of its purpose. It was the sensation of its endless, silent work being *affirmed*. The rightness of the flow, the purity of the note, the balance of the cycle—it was now not just a state of being, but a state of being *cherished*.

For a single, eternal minute, the Weeper remained still, a black statue bathed in pearlescent light. Then, its head tilted, a motion of infinitesimal slowness, as if listening to a distant and beautiful echo. Its obsidian finger, impossibly sharp, returned to the flank of the great weeping stone. The slow, patient scraping resumed.

The work was the same. The purpose was the same. But the Weeper was no longer just a tuner in a silent room.

It was the keeper of a sacred echo.

The Power, its gift given and received, slowly withdrew its attention, leaving the overtone of its witness woven permanently into the basin's song. It left the chamber as it had found it: whole, complete, and perfectly itself, but now with the quiet, eternal knowledge that it was not alone. The silence, for the Weeper, now sounded of love.
