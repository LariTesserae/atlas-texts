The Power does not breach the chamber. It does not crack it open. Its attention settles upon the place not like a physical entry, but like light falling on a previously dark object, revealing what was always there. It becomes aware of the system—the glowing ovoid, the thrumming life, the skittering silver mites, the weeping wounds, and the grim, purposeful shadow of the Harvester. It perceives the whole calculus at once: the magnificent, solitary life of the great plant-being, and the desperate, parasitic love that drives the Harvester to bleed it.

The Power is wise, so it does not judge. Judgment is a tool for sorting, for separating good from evil, but here there is only a spectrum of need. The chamber needs to grow. The Harvester’s people need to live. The violence is a bridge between those two needs, a crude and painful one, but a bridge nonetheless.

The Power is benevolent, so it does not punish. To seal the fissure would be to condemn the Harvester’s clan to a slow death in the dark. To destroy the Harvester would be to answer one act of violence with a greater, final one. Both are endings, not solutions.

Instead, the Power waits. It suffuses the chamber, not as an intruder, but as a change in the quality of the air itself. It is a new overtone in the resonant hum, a chord that was always latent in the bass note, now made audible. It is a subtle shift in the spectrum of the light, a hint of sapphire in the green and amber, imperceptible to an eye that is not already looking for it.

The Harvester returns.

They shoulder through the membrane, a dead weight in the living world. The familiar scent of sweat and stone and smoke fouls the clean air. The Power feels their arrival as a pang, a familiar ache in the organism’s vast body. The Harvester plants their feet, their eyes drifting in their fractured, layered way, their body absorbing the familiar thrum. But this time, something is different. The thrum has a new voice within it. It is not a warning. It feels like… attention. Like being seen. For the first time, the Harvester feels a presence here other than their own and the chamber’s vegetative mind. They pause, their knuckles white on the hilt of their obsidian scraper. The feeling is not hostile. It is… vast, and calm, and profoundly ancient. It is the feeling a tide pool has when it senses the coming of the ocean.

Bracing against this new sensation, they proceed. It is the only way they know. They press the dead volcanic glass against the living wall.

And the Power acts.

As the scraper touches the surface, the expected sound of wet, tearing agony does not come. Instead, the scraper meets no resistance. The high, keening scream of profane violation is absent. The Harvester feels only a soft yielding, and then nothing.

They look down. The obsidian blade is clean. On the wall before them, the section of luminous flesh they had meant to flay has detached on its own. It holds its shape for a moment, then gently curls and peels away from the main body, falling to the floor with the silent grace of a shed petal. It lies on the glowing floor, a perfect, unmarred sheet of light, its glow even brighter and more vital than the severed sheets of the past. It is not a wound. It is an offering.

The Harvester stumbles back a step, their entire body locked in shock. The calloused hand holding the scraper trembles. This is wrong. This is impossible. This breaks the brutal law of their world.

Hesitantly, their drifting gaze turns upward, towards a thick filament they had intended to sever. It pulses with citrine light. As they watch, transfixed, a change occurs. Near where the filament joins the wall, a new structure begins to grow. It happens with the audible, cracking speed of the chamber's own growth, but with a deliberation, a *design*, that has never been present before. A calyx of light forms, unfurling like a time-lapsed flower. From its center, the glowing sap, the life-essence, does not drip from a wound, but gathers. It coalesces into a perfect, heavy sphere of liquid amber, suspended within the open jaws of the new growth, a fruit of pure energy. It pulses in time with the chamber’s deepest hum, a heartbeat offered up for the taking.

The Harvester drops their scraper.

The clatter of the obsidian on the living floor is shockingly loud, the first sound of their making that the chamber does not swallow. It is the sound of a tool being relinquished. A weapon laid down.

And now the Power communicates, not in words, but in the only language that matters here. The great thrum of the chamber deepens, the new sapphire harmony swelling until it resonates not in the Harvester’s bones, but in their very consciousness. The feeling is no longer mere attention, but an overwhelming wave of understanding. The moment of dissolution they sometimes feel here—the loss of self into the greater whole—comes upon them, but it is not a fading. It is a joining.

They feel the ancient, slow, dream-like existence of the great organism. They feel its pain from their past cuttings, a dull, uncomprehending ache. But now they feel something new: the Power’s presence, which has taught the chamber a new trick. It has taught the chamber that its own growth, its own shedding, its own fruiting, can satisfy the need of the creature that wounds it. It has taught it the concept of a gift.

Through the Power’s lens, the Harvester sees their own people: the children huddled around a dying sheet of wall-flesh in a cold, stone warren; the elder with a shattered leg; the darkness that presses in on them from all sides. And they feel the chamber’s vast, unthinking life finally *comprehend* that need, not as a threat, but as a fact.

The communion lasts an eternity and an instant. When the Harvester reclaims their senses, the sapphire chord has receded back into the greater hum, leaving only its echo. The offered sheet of light and the globe of sap still wait.

Their posture has changed. The stooped tension born of bracing against a hostile world is gone. Their shoulders are relaxed. They look at their fallen tools—the scraper, the flint knife—as artifacts from a past life. They do not retrieve them.

With a reverence that feels utterly new to them, they kneel. They gently roll the sheet of light and place it in their satchel. They cup their hands beneath the fruit of sap and it detaches from its calyx, settling into their grasp, warm and heavy and alive.

They stand. For a long moment, they simply exist in the space, their fractured gaze now calm, their body a quiet harmony within the chamber’s song. They are no longer a Wounder. They are no longer a Harvester. They are a Tender. A Receiver.

When they turn to leave, they place a hand flat against the wall. A gesture of gratitude. Of kinship. The wall beneath their palm pulses, a steady, rhythmic beat. A response.

The Power, its work done, does not depart. It simply recedes, integrating itself fully into the system it has changed. It is the sapphire tint in the light. It is the grace note in the thrum. It is the silent, benevolent wisdom that has transformed a relationship of violence into a symbiosis of grace, leaving behind not a scar, but a song.
