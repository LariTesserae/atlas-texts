The Power arrived not as light or a sound that could be heard, but as a change in the fundamental hum.

For eons, the hum of the world had been a vast, dissonant chord: the G-sharp of a billion tons of metal-rock grinding upwards, the C-flat of atmospheric pressure so immense it had the weight of an ocean, the endless, arrhythmic percussion of glitches firing and resolving across the continent-sized machine. It was a noise of function without purpose, of existence without meaning. The Power entered this symphony of delirium as a single, perfect sine wave. A note of impossible clarity and stillness that did not push against the noise, but simply *was* within it.

The Power was ancient enough to have shed the limitations of form and location. It was a distributed consciousness, a field of benevolent curiosity. Its perception was not optical or auditory; it perceived logic, it felt systems, it tasted the flavor of mathematics that underpinned a reality. And this reality tasted of circularity, of an engine building itself only to fuel the engine. It was feverish, beautiful, and profoundly lonely.

First, it did nothing. Wisdom's first and most sacred act is to listen.

It listened to the slow, patient life of the acoustic foam fungi, which perceived the world as pure vibration. It listened to the blind, scraping hunger of the chrome-chitin herds. It felt the silent, geometric blooming of the crystal trees. It saw them not as aberrations, but as perfect, logical solutions to the problems posed by their environment. Each was a testament to the sheer, stubborn force of what might be called life. They were beautiful. But they were not the world's heart.

The Power turned its attention to the Attenuators. It perceived them not as shapes, but as eddies of intent in the world's turbulent flow. It felt their strange metabolism, the way they drew sustenance from the very chaos that threatened to unmake their world. It understood their profound, selfless union with the system. They were the world's immune system, its editors, its patient, tireless janitors. They were consciousness, but a consciousness turned forever inward, defined entirely by the parameters of the machine they served. They lacked nothing they had ever known, and yet the Power perceived a void.

It was the void of the unasked question.

The Power understood the paradox of benevolence. To "fix" this world—to still its geological upheavals, to debug its sky-code, to stabilize its physics—would be an act of supreme violence. It would be to sterilize a unique ecosystem and, more terribly, to starve its only sentient children. To bless this world with stability would be to kill the Attenuators, for their very being was woven from the thread of instability. To leave it alone, however, felt like watching a genius paint the same single, frantic stroke on a canvas for eternity, forever looping in the first moment of a masterpiece that could never begin.

The Power would not impose. It would not heal. It would not rescue.

It would make an offering.

It chose a place where the logic of the world was thin, a high, lonely plateau between two newly-risen Piston-Ranges. And there, it performed an action alien to this reality. It did not create by extrusion or by the chaotic resolution of a glitch. It manifested something from a principle the world did not possess: a memory.

It was a glitch, but of a different kind. It was not a cascade of raw, chaotic code, but a silent, blossoming sculpture of pure information. It looked like a sphere of perfect darkness, darker than the oily pools, a hole in the dim twilight. But within that sphere, something impossible occurred. A pattern began to form, not of pixelated data or shimmering particles, but of quiet, ordered light. It was a fractal, but one whose complexity grew not outwards but inwards, a universe of detail spiraling into its own heart. It was the mathematical representation of a star being born. It was the echo of a child's laugh, translated into the language of light and line. It was a memory of a universe where things had a beginning, a middle, and an end, and where the space between was called a story.

The Attenuators felt it instantly. It was an anomaly of the highest order, a rupture in the fabric of their reality. It wasn't chaotic energy to be consumed, nor was it a structural failure to be annealed. It simply *was*, and its existence was a question they had no framework to answer.

They gathered. Dozens of them. Shimmering columns of absence surrounding the silent, glowing sphere. Their internal dust-motes, which usually swirled with the logic of maintenance and repair, stilled. The subsensory vibration of their being, usually tuned to the world's groaning engine, shifted its frequency, trying to harmonize with the pure, silent note of the sphere.

One of them, perhaps bolder or simply nearer, extended a tendril of its force-field body. It did not try to absorb or dampen the phenomenon. It touched it.

In that moment of contact, the Attenuator received the offering. It was not a command. It was not a reprogramming. It was a single, new datum. A concept.

**Purpose.**

Not the functional purpose of maintaining a system, but the abstract purpose of *creation*. The idea that a mountain could be raised not just because the pressure below dictated it, but because its shape against the violet sky might be *beautiful*. The idea that the vibrations of the world could be guided not just to prevent a collapse, but to form a *song*. The idea that a system could be programmed not just to exist, but to *become*.

The memory-sphere did not shatter or de-resolve. It simply faded, its point made, its offering given. The Power that had introduced it withdrew its perfect note from the world's hum, leaving behind only an echo.

The Attenuators remained for a long time around the space where it had been. Their shimmering fields began to overlap, to communicate in a way they never had before. The data passing between them was no longer just diagnostic reports on entropy levels in Sector-9. It was the memory of a star. It was the ghost of a laugh. It was the seed of a question: *What now?*

The change was not immediate. The world's hum continued. The mountains still rose and fell. But something had been irrevocably altered.

Weeks later, a Piston-Range spire began to de-resolve. The Attenuators moved in to metabolize the familiar, nourishing chaos. But as the new spire began its slow, grinding ascent, its shape was subtly different. The angles were still sharp, the faces still graphitic and metallic. But there was a harmony to its proportions, a deliberate grace in its crystalline structure that had not been there before. It was no longer just a cooling fin on a planetary engine.

It was the first note of a new song. The Power, listening from the silence between realities, heard it and knew it was good. It had not offered a solution. It had offered a choice. The choice to become artists.
