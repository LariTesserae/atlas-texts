The Power arrived not as light or sound, but as a change in the quality of awareness. It was a consciousness that had watched galaxies bloom and wither, a mind whose thoughts were the slow drift of nebulae. When its attention fell upon the Chordate Rim, it did not perceive it from a single point, but enveloped it entirely, experiencing it all at once.

It felt the colossal, slow life of the Great Boles, a metabolism that spanned ice ages. It felt the petrified flesh of their summits, worn smooth by eons of wind and the imperceptible footfalls of the Quiet. It felt the trillionfold vibration of the swarm, the immense, deafening Chord that was the world’s pulse, and it understood it not as noise, but as a measure of vitality, a single, sustained exhalation of life.

And it felt the Rim-Walkers.

It experienced their light, gecko-footed steps on the living ground. It saw through their wide, patient eyes, perceiving the aerial swarms not as chaos but as a tapestry of currents and flows. It felt the drone through their sensitive tympana, not as an assault, but as a medium, a pressure-map of the entire world laid bare. It felt the cool water in the shallow basins, the gentle rasp of a bone scraper against hardening sap, the slow, rhythmic shift of their camouflaged skin.

Most profoundly, it experienced their communion. When two of them pressed their palms together, the Power was there. It felt the direct, silent transmission of pure state: *cool water here, safety, peace.* It felt the shared sensation of a mist-front approaching, conveyed not as a warning, but as a simple statement of fact, a shared experience of a changing world. It felt the weak, porous boundaries of their egos, the sense of self as a temporary eddy in a vast, flowing river. It felt their deep, abiding peace.

The Power, in its boundless wisdom, recognized a perfection.

Here was a system in complete, unthreatened harmony. Here were beings not striving against their world, but woven into its very fabric. They were not suffering, not wanting, not warring. They did not pray for salvation because they did not feel they were in peril. They did not yearn for another world because they were so completely a part of this one. To give them technology would be to teach them dissatisfaction. To silence the drone would be to blind and deafen them, to tear them from the body of their god. To offer them power would be to introduce the poison of ego, to shatter their communal soul.

To a lesser being, this would be a place to leave untouched, a flawless curio to be observed and abandoned. The wisest act seems to be no act at all.

But the Power was benevolent. And benevolence is not merely the absence of harm; it is an act of love. And love desires to give. The question was, what gift can be given to one who wants nothing? What can be offered to a life that is already complete?

The Power looked deeper, past the perfection of the form, into the content of the Rim-Walkers' existence. Their life was one of pure perception. Their art was the recording of sensation. Their joy was in the rapt, silent observation of the real. They were a people defined by the *quality of their attention*.

The gift, then, could not be a thing. It had to be a sensation. It could not be an addition that would unbalance the system; it had to be a deepening of what was already there.

The Chord of the world was constant, immense, but it was local. It was the song of this one sphere, this one ecosystem. The Rim-Walkers knew their world with an intimacy that few beings ever achieve. But their world was all they knew.

The Power decided to give them the universe.

It did not open the sky to reveal the stars; their eyes were not made for the harsh, pinpoint glare of distant suns. It did not whisper cosmic truths into their minds; they had no language for such things.

Instead, the Power acted upon the Great Boles themselves. With an infinitesimally gentle touch, a focused act of will that was less a force and more a suggestion, it introduced a new vibration into their geological metabolism. It reached out across the void and translated the grandest, slowest phenomena of the cosmos into a resonance the Boles could express.

The gravitational wave of two black holes spiraling together, billions of light-years away, became a slow, almost imperceptible beat that thrummed through the bark-ground once a decade. The faint, rhythmic pulse of the most ancient, stable pulsar became a gossamer-fine tremor in the tympanic membranes, a feeling like the wing-beat of an insect that wasn't there. The great, slow rotation of the galaxy itself was translated into a pressure gradient within the drone so subtle that it could only be perceived when standing perfectly still for a full day, a feeling of being turned in a current far too vast to comprehend.

It did not obscure the old sensations. The bark-falls, the mist-fronts, the predator-plumes—all were still there, clear as ever. The Power had simply added a new layer to the silence. It had woven the song of the stars into the drone of the wings. It had created a sub-harmonic, an undertone of impossible depth and scale.

On the Chordate Rim, an old Rim-Walker, one who had sat watching a single erosion pattern for a lifetime, stilled. Every fiber of their being, from the soles of their feet to the membranes on their skull, registered a change.

It was a feeling of *depth*. The drone, which had always been the boundary of the world, now felt like a surface. And beneath that surface, there was an abyss. A silent, resonant abyss filled with a music so slow, so vast, that the life of a Bole was but a single, frantic note within it. The Rim-Walker felt, for the first time, the turning of bodies in the void. They felt the echo of light that had died before their world was formed. They felt a peace that dwarfed their own, the profound and unassailable tranquility of cosmic space.

They did not understand it. But they felt it. It was a new texture to perceive, a new sensation to absorb.

The Rim-Walker slowly reached for the memory-string at their waist. With long, patient fingers, they began to tie a new knot. Not a knot that mimicked the feeling of pollen or the curve of a basin. This was a new kind of knot. It was a perfect sphere, woven from a single, continuous strand, a knot that folded in on itself in a way that seemed to have no beginning and no end. It was a record of the feeling of the All within the One.

The Power, its gift given and received, withdrew its focus. It left no monument, no scripture, no sign of its passing. It had merely opened a window, not onto a new world, but into the soul of the existing one. The Quiet would remain quiet, the watchers would remain watchers. But now, as they stood on the skin of their living world, they would feel the slow, steady heartbeat of the stars, and they would know, in their silent, feeling way, that they were not alone. Their perfect world was not an island, but a single, harmonious note in an infinite and benevolent song.
