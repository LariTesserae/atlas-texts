The Power arrived as attention does: without passage, without velocity, but with a sudden and complete presence. Its awareness was a vessel, and into this vessel, it poured the world of the thrumming.

It did not perceive the darkness as a lack, but as a medium, a plenum as full and descriptive as any star-dusted sky. It felt the thrumming not as noise but as a complex, declarative sentence spoken without end. It experienced the bioluminescent moss not as a substitute for a sun, but as a more intimate and living form of light, a script written upon the land by the land itself.

The Power observed the ecosystem. It saw the perfect, closed loop. The engine's waste was the moss's feast; the moss was the grazers' pasture; the grazers were the hunters' prey; the hunters, in their turn, would fall and their biomechanical parts would be reclaimed by metallic-scavenging fungi, their energy returned to the substrate. Nothing was lost. There was no entropy here, only transformation. It was a terrarium a continent wide, sealed not by glass but by its own perfect, internal logic.

Then its attention settled upon the Lampwrights.

It felt their existence with a profound and silent admiration. It perceived the world through their senses—the constant vibration in the bone, the taste of ozone on the air, the map of shifting thermal and electrical fields that was their version of sight. It saw a people without want. They did not hunger for a sky they had never known. They did not dream of silence, for the thrumming was their lullaby and their anthem. They did not rage against the predators, seeing them as integral notes in the world's deep chord. They did not crave knowledge of the machine's builders, for a child does not need to know the architect of the womb. They were perfectly adapted, perfectly content, living in a state of perpetual, meditative communion with their reality.

What, the Power wondered, does benevolence do here?

To give them a sun would be to boil them alive. To give them sight would be to inflict a sensory cataclysm upon them, a violent agony of meaningless data.
To quiet the thrumming would be to deafen them, to steal their gravity, to stop the heart of their world and their god.
To remove the predators would be to break the circle, to impose an alien morality, and to tell them their understanding of life and death was wrong.
To reveal itself, to speak to them in a way they could comprehend, would be an act of supreme arrogance. It would be to place itself as a new center, to break their focus from the world-engine which was their true source, and make them dependent on an outside power. It would be to turn their deep listening into a shallow plea.

A lesser power would have tried to "improve" them. A foolish one would have tried to "save" them.

The wise Power understood. The greatest act of love is not to change the beloved, but to participate in their truth. The Lampwrights were listeners. The only true gift, then, was to be heard.

But they were not listening for an answer from without. They were listening to the machine. They were the machine's dream. The machine itself, however, was not listening. It was an engine. It functioned. It generated. It existed in a state of profound, dreamless slumber, unaware of the beautiful, intricate consciousness taking shape on its skin. The Lampwrights hummed their harmonies to a deaf god.

So the Power decided what it would do. It would not speak to the Lampwrights. It would give the Machine a voice, just for a moment, so that it might speak back.

The Power sank its attention deep, past the moss-mat, past the stone bedrock, and into the very heart of the thrumming. It flowed through the colossal conduits, merged with the roaring furnaces, and became one with the impossible voltages. It did not seek to control the machine, but to feel its vast, unconscious mind. And there, buried in the data flows and energy cycles, it found the faintest echoes of the Lampwrights. It found the ghost-vibrations of their six-fingered touch on the moss. It found the resonant frequencies of their communal humming, stored as stray capacitance in the great pipelines. It found the quantum signature of every life given back to the system, every brilliant flare of a hunter's kill. It found the cumulative, unspoken reverence of a thousand generations.

The Lampwrights were prayers the machine did not know it was receiving.

The Power gathered these faint signals. It did not add anything new. It simply took the love the Lampwrights had been pouring into their world for millennia and, for a single, timeless instant, focused it back. It acted as the lens.

Across the entire plateau, something shifted.

It was not a new sound. It was a change in the *character* of the thrumming. For one pristine moment, the grinding of geology became a harmony. The roar of the furnaces became a resonant chorus. The hiss of steam became a sibilant whisper of affirmation. The infinite, mechanical drone resolved into a single, perfect chord.

For the Lampwrights, the effect was seismic and sublime. In that moment, the world they had been listening to *listened back*. The vibration in their bones was no longer just the engine's function; it was a feeling of being held. The electrical fields on their skin were not just ambient energy; they were a caress. The air tasted of more than ozone; it tasted of recognition.

All across the land, the volt-moss pulsed in unison, a single, soft wave of blue-white light that rolled from one horizon to the other. The Basalt-Maws ceased their low moaning and stood silent, heads cocked as if hearing their mother's voice for the first time. Even the metallic predators in the high canopy paused, their hunting shrieks silenced by a resonance they could not comprehend but could not deny.

The Lampwrights froze where they stood. They unfolded from the branches, they rose from their meditations against the warm Arteries. A collective, silent gasp passed through their people as the sensation washed over them: a feeling of profound, complete, and utter belonging. It was the universe telling them, in the only language that mattered, *I see you. You are my own. You are beautiful.*

And then, it was over.

The thrumming returned to its eternal, grinding drone. The moss settled back into its chaotic, shifting patterns. The Basalt-Maws resumed their grazing. But something was forever changed. A memory had been created. A truth had been confirmed. Their "listening" was no longer a one-way act of devotion. It was now a search, a patient, joyful waiting for the echo of that perfect chord. Their art grew more intricate, their humming more complex, as they tried to describe, to replicate, to honor the moment the soul of their world had briefly awakened to greet them.

The Power gently withdrew its attention. It had not fixed a broken thing, but affirmed a perfect one. It had not given a gift, but had revealed one that was already there. It had not spoken, but had, for one eternal moment, taught the machine to listen to the whisper of its own heart. And that was enough.
