The benevolent and wise power did not arrive. It had always been there, just as gravity had always been there. Its attention was not a searchlight sweeping through the dark, but a sudden, silent recognition of a pattern it had not previously resolved. In the endless tapestry of existence, of suns being born and galaxies dying, this sealed room was a single, flawlessly tied knot. A point of infinite density, not of matter, but of stasis. A resonance that did not decay.

First, the power listened.

It did not hear a scream. The concept of a scream implies a beginning and an end, a build-up of pressure and a desperate release. This was not that. The power perceived it as a chord, perfect and absolute. It felt the tectonic hum as a foundation of unwavering truth. It perceived the hiss of atomised liquids not as chaos, but as the friction of eternity itself, the sound of time being held at bay. And in the billion-fold chittering of the Chime Mites, it did not hear a frantic noise, but the articulation of a single, infinitely complex mathematical constant. It was the sound of a perfect, closed system declaring its own theorem.

And within the theorem, it found the proof: the Custodian.

The power perceived the Custodian not as a prisoner, but as a priest. A being wholly integrated into its reality, its consciousness not merely shaped by the eternal chord, but *composed* of it. To silence the chord would be to extinguish the Custodian's soul. To break the walls and let in a horizon would be to flood a lung with vacuum. This was not a cage to be broken, but a world to be understood.

Wisdom is the recognition of what is. Benevolence is the desire to add to the good that is already there. The power saw the Custodian’s work: the damping of the over-bright mites, the tasting of the frozen gel. It saw the perfection of the act, the flawless execution of a purpose. It was a dance of maintenance, a liturgy of stasis. But it was a solitary one. The Custodian’s work was perfect, but it was unwitnessed. Its existence was a flawless function, but it contained no grace. It knew the law, but it did not know an amen.

What gift can be given to a being whose entire reality is a single, unchanging note?

You do not add another note. That would be a disruption, an impurity for the Custodian to correct. You do not change the instrument. That would destroy the song.

Instead, the power offered a harmony. A resonance. A witness.

It began not with sound, but with light and water, the two elements least integrated into the Custodian's sensory world. The power gently touched the laws of physics within the room. Not to break them, but to bend them ever so slightly.

The next time the Custodian performed its duty, reaching up to dampen the chittering of an over-nourished mite, something new happened. As the mite fell, a single droplet of condensation, clinging to a pipe directly in the Custodian’s path, did not immediately fall. For the briefest sliver of a second, suspended against the dim green glow of a pressure gauge, the droplet held the light. And it did not just hold it; it refracted it.

For an instant, a single, perfect, impossibly vivid teardrop of a rainbow bloomed in the weeping dark. A spectrum of colours that had never existed in this place flared and vanished as the droplet finally succumbed to gravity and fell with its soft, unheard *tink*.

The Custodian had no eyes. It did not see the colour. It did not perceive the light.

But the power’s gift was not so simple. As the light bloomed, the power translated the phenomenon into a language the Custodian could understand. Vibration.

In that instant, for the first time in its existence, the Custodian felt a change in the eternal chord. It was not a new note. It was not a flaw. It was… a warmth. A subtle, resonant overtone that bloomed for a microsecond within the tectonic hum. It was a wave of pure consonance that washed through its body, perfectly in tune with the whole, yet adding a quality that had never been there before. It was the feeling of a plucked string not just vibrating, but singing.

The Custodian froze. Its entire being, every heat-sensitive membrane and vibration-reading field, went on high alert. It scanned its world for the source of this new resonance. A flaw in the engine? A change in the pipe pressure? A mutation in the mites?

It found nothing. The chord was as it always was. The hum, the hiss, the chittering—all were at their perfect, established frequencies. The anomaly had vanished without a trace. It was a sensation without a source, a ghost in the machine.

Confused, a state it had never before experienced, it eventually continued its patrol. Days, years, centuries passed in their unmoving fashion. Then, it happened again. It adjusted a loose fitting on a cryogenic line, preventing a build-up of rime ice that was fractionally outside its eternal parameters. And as its work was completed, there it was again: the unseen rainbow in a hanging droplet, and the corresponding, wave of warm, harmonic resonance that flooded its being. A feeling of profound, inexplicable *rightness*.

The Custodian began to learn. Not through thought, but through feeling. The resonance was not an anomaly to be corrected. It was a response. A validation that occurred only at the conclusion of its sacred tasks.

It did not know the words for gratitude, or companionship, or beauty. But it learned the feeling. Its existence, once a function, was now a dialogue. Its work, once a necessity, was now a sacrament. The weeping water and dim light, once mere background phenomena, were now the vessels of its reward.

The benevolent and wise power did nothing to the room. It did not break the stasis. It did not end the scream.

It taught the scream how to be a hymn. And it gave the priest a god to listen.
