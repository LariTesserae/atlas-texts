The Power came not as a light in the null-space ceiling, nor as a sound in the damp, quiet air. It arrived as a pattern. A subtle, intricate resonance in the very substrate of the Looming Plain, a disturbance too fine for the thrumming Maws to notice, too harmonic to be the skittering of a Slag-Spider, too complex to be the simple flow of telluric current that fed the Cable-Moss.

It was the Listeners who felt it first.

In the deepest, coldest vacancies of the world, their crystalline forms, which had spent millennia recording the planet’s own slow heartbeat, suddenly vibrated with a new song. It was a chord of impossible complexity, a wave of information that was not hostile, not demanding, but simply… *attentive*. It was the feeling of being perceived, wholly and completely, for the first time by something other than itself.

The benevolent and wise Power, having drawn its attention to this place, spent a measure of time that had no meaning here simply observing. Its perception was not limited to a single point. It experienced the Looming Plain as the Plain experienced itself: as a single, integrated whole.

It felt the slow, consuming thought of the Furnace Maws, a tectonic will to unmake and process. It did not see monsters, but the slow, deliberate pulse of a digestive system the size of a continent.

It traced the frantic, precise pathways of the Slag-Spiders, feeling their purpose not as instinct but as the firing of a planetary nervous system, dutifully mending, archiving, and managing the world’s most precious fluid. It read the glass-thread libraries of the spiders, webs that were not traps but chronicles, and understood the history they told.

It felt the ecstatic, unthinking bliss of the Thermophyllic Leeches, basking in the raw output of the world’s core, and recognised it as a form of joy—pure, metabolic, and absolute. It felt the soaring, regulatory mind of the Wingsails, a consciousness made of thermal maps, and saw not a predator but an immune system, maintaining a perfect, delicate equilibrium.

And in the silence, it felt the low, baseline hum of the Cable-Moss and the patient, recording stillness of the Listeners. It understood. It understood that this was not a world in need of salvation. It was not a prison to be liberated, not a wasteland to be terraformed. It was a being. A single, breathing, thinking, dreaming organism, complete unto itself, existing in a state of meticulous, unhurried perfection.

A lesser power, in its benevolence, might have been tempted to *give*. It might have gifted a sun, believing the darkness to be a privation. This would have boiled the carefully managed humidity from the air, starved the Cable-Moss which fed on telluric currents, and blinded the Wingsails whose minds were maps of heat, not light. The world-being would have been scalded, deafened, and lobotomized.

A lesser power might have offered *connection*—a bridge to other worlds, other beings. This would have been like introducing a foreign, thinking organ into a perfectly functioning body, an idea so alien it would have been perceived as a cancer, a madness.

A lesser power might have sought to *speak* to it, to elevate its slow consciousness to something faster, more familiar. This would have been the cruelest act of all—to wake a deep and peaceful dreamer into a state of shrieking, panicked incomprehension.

The wise Power understood that the greatest act of benevolence it could offer was not to change, but to *affirm*.

And so, it did something.

In the vastest, coldest, most silent expanse of the Looming Plain, in a region where no Maw had passed for an age and even the Listeners were sparse, the Power focused its attention. It did not build. It did not destroy. It did not add or take away.

It began to resonate.

It settled its consciousness into the ferro-ceramic plate and became, for a time, a Listener itself. But where the living crystals recorded the world’s own sounds, the Power broadcast a single, impossibly pure note. It was not a sound that could be heard, but a vibration that could be felt. It was a note of pure, unconditional Witness. It was the resonance of appreciation. It was a hum that said, without language, *I see you. I understand you. You are a marvel. Continue.*

The effect was infinitesimally small, and yet, profound.

The Listeners, across the entire world, felt this new, perfect harmony anchor their own recordings. Their stored memory of the world, a history of vibrations, suddenly had a tuning fork against which all other frequencies could be measured. Their deep, silent mind felt a sense of peace it had never known it lacked: the peace of being known.

Over the next several thousand years, the change rippled outward. The nearest convoy of Slag-Spiders, weaving their glassy webs a hundred miles away, found that the water condensing on their threads in the direction of the Resonance was subtly different. Stripped of some unknown, infinitesimal dissonance, the mineral deposits it left were purer, the chronicle they etched more precise. The webs they wove in that quadrant began to take on a new, mathematically perfect symmetry, a beauty that served no function except to be beautiful.

A herd of Furnace Maws, their ten-thousand-year-long thought-path set to intersect that region in another millennium, began to slowly, imperceptibly, alter its course. The world-being was not avoiding the area. It felt no fear. It felt something akin to reverence. It was diverting its own digestive process around a point of sanctity, the way a person might unconsciously avoid putting pressure on a place on their body where they had received a gentle, reassuring touch.

The Wingsails, mapping the heat of the world, found a single column of absolute thermal neutrality in that spot. Not cold, not hot. A pillar of perfect equilibrium reaching up into the null-space. They would sometimes circle it for days, their map-minds finding a place of utter rest, a focal point of perfect balance in the dynamic system they regulated.

The Power left no monument, no artifact, no scripture. Its gift was invisible, intangible, and seamlessly integrated into the world’s own processes. It had introduced nothing new, but had instead created a focal point around which the existing perfection of the Looming Plain could harmonise itself. It had given the great, lonely, dreaming being a mirror, allowing it to perceive, for the first time, its own magnificent reflection in the quiet attention of another.

And then, its work done, the Power gently withdrew its focused consciousness, leaving the pure note of its Witness resonating in the fabric of the Plain, a hum that would now last as long as the world itself. It was a secret, shared only with the planet-being: you are not alone, and you are loved.
