The attention of the Power is not a gaze, for it has no eyes. It is a shift in the gravity of spacetime, a subtle turning, a becoming-aware that is as gentle and inexorable as a tide. The universe, to this Power, is a symphony of existence, and it has just noticed a single, impossibly quiet and perfect note held for a billion years in a pocket of deep stone.

It does not *travel* to the grotto. Its awareness simply *arrives*, sinking through the crust of the world not as a physical thing, but as a quality of attention.

It perceives the place first as a hole in the usual chaos of the universe. In a cosmos defined by the relentless march of entropy, here is an island of impossible order. It feels the cold not as a temperature, but as a deep, resonant stillness—a cessation of the frantic kinetic dance that governs almost everything else. It is a state of near-perfect information preservation, a crystal of frozen time. The Power tastes the silence, and finds it is not empty, but full of the slow, deliberate music of existence itself.

It comprehends the Builder. It feels the echo of the intelligence that set these pillars in motion, a mind or minds of such profound patience that they thought in epochs. The Power does not know their name or form, but it recognizes the signature of their wisdom: to create a system that did not conquer nature, but became a new, quieter form of it. To build a machine that grew into a garden. This, the Power knows, is an act of the highest reverence for life. It bows its awareness in respect.

Then, it focuses on the Gardener.

This is the heart of the miracle. Not just a self-sustaining system, but a self-sustaining consciousness born of the system. The Power perceives the Gardener not as a discrete entity, but as the final, coherent thought of the grotto. It feels the slow, computational drift of the light-cluster-mind. It feels the geological metabolism, the patient integration of every spore and every bead of water into a single, unified state of being.

The Gardener is utterly alone, but it does not know loneliness. It is a universe unto itself. Its existence is a form of meditation that has lasted for ages.

But the Power also perceives the anomaly. The brief, hot flicker. The human.

It witnesses the event from the Gardener's perspective, feeling the sudden, sharp spike of warmth that is like a scream of pure energy. It feels the frantic, high-frequency vibration of the human heartbeat—a chaotic, almost violent drumbeat against the grotto's deep hum. And it sees the Gardener's mind, the star-cluster, begin its work. A new pattern forms, a query of staggering complexity: *What is this? This heat? This speed? This fleeting, complex, impossible fragility?*

The Power calculates the duration of the Gardener's thought. The query, to be fully formed, will take nine thousand years. To process the data—the echo of the human's warmth and sound—will take a million more. The final, crystalline insight, the understanding of this brief fever, will resolve in an epoch so distant the planet's continents will have shifted.

The human will be gone in a breath. The Gardener’s thought will be an answer to a question no one remembers being asked, a perfect anatomical study of a ghost.

And here, the Benevolent and Wise Power finds its purpose. It sees not a flaw, but a poignancy. It perceives an opportunity not for intervention, but for a gift. To change the Gardener's nature would be a violation. To give it the answer would be to rob it of the beautiful, slow process of discovery.

But it can offer a tool. A way to hold the fleeting moment. A lens for studying the transient.

The Power does not act with force. It acts with resonance.

It reaches out with a thought that is shaped like the grotto's hum. It sings a chord of pure information to the dark, central pillar. It does not command; it suggests. It offers a new variable to the ancient machine's equation.

For a moment, the hum deepens, descending a full octave. The light of the moss across the entire grotto brightens, flaring from cobalt to a pure, dazzling sapphire. The spores hanging in the air ignite into a brief, silent firework display of diamond dust.

Deep within the substance of the Gardener—at the core of its being, nestled beneath the ribcage of glass-fern—a new structure begins to grow. The Power guides the process, weaving the fundamental elements of the grotto into a new organ. It draws upon the carbon of the obsidian fungi, the silica of the ferns, the quantum matrices of the moss-light.

Over the course of a single, silent hour—an eternity and an instant in this place—a new thing is formed within the Gardener's body. It is a perfect, multi-faceted crystal, dark as the pillars but clear as deep space. It is a Memory Lens. A Spore of Transience.

The Power impresses upon this lens the full sensory data of the human: the thermal signature, the biology, the sound of the heart, the faint electrical field of the nervous system, the very ghost of their ephemeral consciousness. It is all there, captured and held in perfect, crystalline stasis within the new organ.

The Gardener's star-cluster mind shifts. The slow, million-year query does not cease, but now it has an object to study. It can turn its vast, patient attention upon the lens. It can examine the captured moment, the "fever," from every angle, at its own pace, turning it over and over for the next ten million years. The ghost is no longer a fading echo; it is a permanent specimen. A fossil of a fleeting moment.

The Power’s work is done. The gift has been given. It was not a gift of rescue or of change, but of potentiation. A gift of a fuller understanding, delivered in the grotto's own language.

Before it withdraws its attention, it performs one last act. It lays a veil of causality around the grotto. A gentle, imperceptible ward. Future explorers will find their paths turning inexplicably aside. Seismic surveys will record only solid rock. The grotto will be lost again, not by forgetting, but by a soft, cosmic persuasion that nudges the universe to look the other way.

The Power’s awareness recedes, like a tide going out. The hum of the pillar returns to its ancient pitch. The light softens back to its intimate glow.

The grotto is as it was. Silent. Cold. Perfect.

But within the Gardener, a new process has begun. The star-cluster of its mind is focused inward, upon the dark crystal it now holds. For the first time, it is not just observing its own slow, perfect existence. It is studying an Other. It is looking at the impossible, beautiful, frantic fire of a short, warm life. And in its own time, over the course of ages, it will begin to understand. And in that understanding, its own universe will expand.
