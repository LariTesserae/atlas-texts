### **Traveler’s Preparatory Dossier: Provisional Designation "The Cacophony Basin"**

**WARNING:** This destination is rated as an Extreme Bio-Hazard and High Physical Trauma Environment. Unprepared travel will result in fatality within minutes. The primary challenges are not predation, but systemic sensory, structural, and biological failure due to environmental incompatibility.

#### **Phase 1: Pre-Departure Acclimatization & Psychological Conditioning**

Your mind will break long before your body does if you are unprepared. The Basin does not have "sounds"; it has **Sound**. A singular, pressurized force.

1.  **Auditory Conditioning:** Two weeks prior to departure, you will begin a regimen of total auditory immersion. You will be provided with a device that replicates the foundational drone of the Basin. You will sleep with it, eat with it, live with it. You will start at 10% intensity and increase by 10% each day. The goal is not to "get used to it"—you won't—but to train your mind to function while under constant sonic assault. You must learn to form a coherent thought inside a hurricane. Failure to complete this program will result in immediate vertigo, nausea, and cognitive paralysis upon arrival.

2.  **Sensory Deprivation & Overload Drills:** The Basin is an environment of absolute stimulus with zero information. To prepare, you will alternate between sessions in a sensory deprivation tank (to appreciate true silence and stillness, which you will not experience) and sessions in a strobe-and-sound "chaos chamber." This trains your neurological pathways to handle the violent shifts in stimuli and to find a mental anchor when all external senses are screaming.

3.  **Mission Anchor:** You are not going to the Basin to "see the sights." You are going for a specific, quantifiable reason: to collect a sample, to place a sensor, to record a specific phenomenon. This mission objective is your mantra. When the light and sound threaten to dissolve your sanity, you will recite your objective. It is the only solid thing you will have. Without it, you are just another piece of meat waiting to be recycled.

#### **Phase 2: Essential Gear & Equipment**

Standard expeditionary gear is useless. Your equipment must be purpose-built for this unique hellscape.

1.  **The Sensory Attenuation Suit (SAS):** This is your primary interface with the world.
    *   **Helmet:** This is the most crucial component. It does NOT block the sound. It can't; the sound is conducted through your bones. Instead, it features:
        *   **Active Sonic Dampening:** Microphones on the exterior capture the roar, while an internal system generates precise counter-frequencies, reducing the perceived decibels to a "mere" deafening roar rather than a physically destructive force. It translates the bone-conducted vibration into a manageable, if overpowering, hum.
        *   **Visual Filter:** The visor is a dynamic photo-chromatic filter. It does not provide night vision (unnecessary). Instead, it actively suppresses the frantic, strobing chaos of the bioluminescence. You can program it to assign specific color values to light frequencies, helping you distinguish a warning flash from a mating display, or a plant's energy pulse from a predator's lure. The default "Calm" setting mutes 90% of the light into a uniform, dim grey, allowing you to see shapes without being hypnotized by the light show.
        *   **Sealed Rebreather:** The air is a hot, wet soup of alien spores, microbes, and God knows what else. Your air supply must be a closed loop. Do not, under any circumstances, breathe the local atmosphere. The oily condensation will foul external filters in minutes.

2.  **Locomotion & Protection:**
    *   **Footwear:** Your boots must have three-part soles. An inner cushioning layer, a thick, woven aramid-fiber mid-layer to prevent punctures, and a hard, replaceable outer sole of tempered ceramic carbide. You will carry at least three sets of replacement soles; the ground will chew through them. The boots must extend to the knee.
    *   **Bodyglove:** Beneath your outer suit, you will wear a full-body, shear-thickening fluid armor suit. This flexible material remains pliable during slow movement but becomes momentarily rigid upon sharp impact, protecting you from falls against the razor-glass.
    *   **Exo-Shell:** This is a lightweight, non-metallic outer suit made from a slick, carbon-fiber weave. Its purpose is threefold: to provide a final layer of cut-resistance, to prevent the barbed vines from snagging on you, and to be non-reflective. Its matte black surface, similar to that of the mantis-scavengers, makes you less of a visual target in a world where light means "notice me."

3.  **Tools & Utilities:**
    *   **The Probe Staff:** A simple, non-negotiable tool. It must be at least two meters long, made of a non-conductive composite. You will test every single footstep before you commit your weight. The ground is a living trap. Use the staff to feel for instability, to push aside vines, and to maintain balance.
    *   **Navigation:** All external navigation (GPS, magnetic) is useless. Your primary tool is an inertial guidance system coupled with a real-time 3D scanner (lidar/sonar) mounted on your helmet. It maps the terrain as you move, creating a temporary, personal map relative to your insertion point. You will also carry a spool of high-tensile, phosphorescent cord. If you must enter the dense foliage, you will anchor it and pay it out behind you. It may be your only way back.
    *   **Sustenance:** Everything you need for the duration of your trip must be carried. The endemic life is built on a xeno-biochemistry that is almost certainly toxic. Your water is in your suit's bladder. Your food is a nutrient paste, dispensed through a port in your helmet. Do not expose it to the local environment.

#### **Phase 3: On-Site Protocol & Behavior**

1.  **The First Ten Minutes:** Upon arrival, do not move. Anchor yourself. Let your suit's systems stabilize. Let your mind scream. Wait for the initial panic to subside. Watch. Listen through your helmet's filters. Your first action is to understand the immediate rhythms around you before you become a disruptive element.

2.  **Movement:** Slow is smooth, smooth is fast, and fast is dead.
    *   There are no paths. You move in the gaps.
    *   Follow the flow of the large "beetle" herds when possible. They are heavy enough to find stable ground, and their movement carves temporary, safer passages through the new growth.
    *   Never step on anything that glows with a focused, singular light. It is either a lure, a defense mechanism, or a fungal trap.
    *   If you hear a rhythmic, percussive cracking sound, freeze. This is the sound of the mantis-scavengers dueling or butchering. They are territorial and brutally efficient. Your suit's black color is your camouflage. Stay still, look like a strange rock, and let them pass. Do not raise your probe staff in a threatening manner. You are not a threat; you are an anomaly. Act like one.

3.  **Reading the Environment:**
    *   A sudden, localized dimming of the background glow and an intensification of ground vibration means a **Bloom-Front** is imminent. The ground is about to erupt. Move laterally, away from the vibration's epicenter. Get behind an old, thick obsidian formation. Do not try to outrun it.
    *   A shift in the pitch of the sonic roar, from a bass hum to a piercing shriek, signals a **Screaming Tide**. This mass insect migration can strip flesh from bone through sheer friction and kinetic force. Do not be in the open. Force yourself into the densest thicket of vegetation you can find, wrap yourself around the base of a large plant, and activate your suit’s rigid-impact mode if it has one. Wait for it to pass.

4.  **Interaction:** Do not interact. You are a ghost. You cannot affect this world in any meaningful way, but it can unmake you in a thousand. Your job is not to fight or tame it. Your job is to touch it as little as possible. The mantis-scavengers use glass knives; remember that the entire world is a knife. Do not grab leaves, do not taste the dew, do not kick the creatures. Observe, record, survive.

#### **Phase 4: Extraction**

Your exit window is absolute. Your inertial navigator will guide you back to your insertion point. Do not deviate. The path you took in may no longer exist; trust the 3D map your suit has created. When it is time to leave, you leave. Do not linger for one last look. The Basin has an infinite capacity to create new, fatal wonders. You do not have an infinite capacity for luck.

**Final Sanity Check:**

Ask yourself: why are you going? If the answer is for thrills, glory, or adventure, you are already dead. This is a place of pure, violent, indifferent creation. It will not be impressed by your courage. It will only notice you when you make a mistake. And your first mistake will be your last.

Travel safely. Travel prepared. Or do not travel at all.
