**TRAVEL ADVISORY: THE GENESIS FORGE (M-7SR9)**
**Descriptor Class:** KETER-CLASS ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARD / AEONIC-SCALE CONSTRUCTION SITE
**Permitted Activity Level:** PASSIVE OBSERVATION ONLY. INTERACTION IS FORBIDDEN AND LIKELY FATAL.

This guide is for the ordinary human traveler. By "ordinary," we mean possessing a standard biological form, subject to the usual constraints of pressure, temperature, and atmospheric composition, even if you arrive in a vessel of considerable technological advancement. Do not take this destination lightly. The Genesis Forge is not hostile; it is merely absolute. It will not try to kill you, but its fundamental state of being is incompatible with unprotected life.

### **Part 1: Pre-Departure Outfitting & Acclimatization**

Your survival here is 99% preparation. Once you are on the ground, your options for improvisation are zero.

**1.1: The Suit - Your Primary Reality Interface**

You will not be wearing a suit so much as inhabiting a personalized, mobile habitat. Standard Class IV exploration EVA suits are inadequate. You require a Class VI Ablative Environmental Suit (AES) with the following specific modifications:

*   **Atmospheric & Pressure Integrity:** The "air" is a super-dense, super-cold, liquid-like medium. Your suit needs to withstand significant hydrostatic pressure, not just vacuum. Seams must be triple-redundant and self-sealing. The external surface should be treated with a hydrophobic, non-reactive coating to slough off the weeping condensation.
*   **Life Support:** A closed-loop rebreather system is mandatory. The local atmosphere is actively corrosive (ozone) and contains metallic particulates that will permanently foul any filter-based system. Your oxygen supply should be calculated for double your planned excursion time. A direct-intake nutrient/hydration port is essential; you will not be taking your helmet off.
*   *Insider's Tip:* Before you go, get used to the taste of recycled water and nutrient paste. It will be your entire menu.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** The environment is uniformly near-freezing and wet. Your suit's primary function, after providing air, will be to keep you from freezing to death. The heating system must be robust, with redundant power sources. External thermal coils are a liability; all heating elements should be internal.
*   **Sensory Interface:**
    *   **Audio:** The ambient noise is physically overwhelming. Your suit's external microphones must be paired with a powerful dynamic range compressor and a suite of audio filtering software. You cannot simply turn it off; the sounds are your primary source of information about imminent threats. You need to be able to isolate the hum of the city, the chitter of the Mites, the scrape of the Fabricators, and the groan of the Molls. Practice toggling these filters until it's second nature.
    *   **Visual:** It is absolutely dark, save for functional biomechanical light. Your helmet's visual display (HUD) should be your universe. You need:
        *   **Active LIDAR/SONAR:** To map the fractal, crystalline structures in 3D. This is your primary "sight."
        *   **Multi-Spectrum Thermal:** To detect the heat signatures of the Fabricators and the absolute cold of the Null-Moths.
        *   **Electromagnetic Field Visualization:** To see the faint energy trails of the Conductor-mites.
        *   **UV/High-Energy Spectrum Analysis:** To be forewarned of the static discharges from Geologic Molls.
*   **Locomotion:** The ground is sharp, slick, and uneven. Standard magnetic boots are useless. You need heavy, articulated crampons integrated into your boots, designed to bite into crystalline and ceramic surfaces. A gecko-grip molecular adhesion function is highly recommended for traversing the vertical and angled structures of the city itself.

**1.2: Essential Tools & Gear**

*   **Navigation:** Compasses are useless. GPS is a fantasy. The landscape is monolithic and self-similar. You must rely on a breadcrumb system. Deploy small, self-powered navigation beacons every hundred meters. Your HUD should auto-triangulate your position relative to these beacons and your entry point. Do not lose this path.
*   **Anchoring System:** A high-tensile winch and grappling hook system is not optional. You will need it to ascend, descend, and secure yourself on precarious surfaces. Assume every surface is slick and offers no natural handholds.
*   **Energy Management:** Your suit and gear run on power. The Forge runs on energy. To the local fauna, a large, active power source might be an anomaly worth investigating, or worse, a resource. Run everything on the lowest possible power settings. Your suit should be coated in energy-dampening material.
*   **Emergency Contingency:**
    *   **Plasma Cutter:** For emergency extrication ONLY. If a Fabricator happens to trap your limb in a freshly extruded ceramic filament, this is your only way out.
    *   **Null-Field Projector:** A small, single-use device that creates a localized bubble of energetic neutrality for approximately 3 seconds. This is your absolute last-ditch defense if a Null-Moth takes a direct interest in you. Its use will drain your primary power supply and likely trigger your emergency extraction beacon.
    *   **Emergency Extraction Beacon:** A transtemporal device that signals your anchored vessel for immediate retrieval. Do not rely on it. The Forge's physics can interfere with such signals.

### **Part 2: On-Site Conduct & Threat Analysis**

You are a mote of dust in a machine the size of a world. Act like it.

**2.1: The Core Tenet - You Are Unrecognized Data**

The Genesis Forge operates on a closed loop of information and purpose. It does not have predators or prey; it has functions and materials. As long as you do not interrupt a function or present yourself as a usable material, you will likely be ignored. Your entire goal is to remain "unreadable" to the system.

**2.2: Navigating The Environment**

*   **The Ground:** Walk deliberately. The black glass plains are brittle and can fracture underfoot. Test every step. The jutting formations are razor-sharp. A suit breach here is a slow death sentence from hypothermia and chemical exposure.
*   **The "Buildings":** The ceramic scaffoldings are the highways of the city. While you may need to climb them, understand you are trespassing on active infrastructure. Do not damage the filaments. Their resonant frequency is part of the city's communication, and a sudden change could be interpreted as a fault, drawing unwanted attention.
*   **The Pools:** The supercooled water in the depressions is not for drinking. A fall into one would mean an instant, catastrophic failure of your suit's thermal regulation. Skirt them widely.

**2.3: Fauna Interaction Protocols (Avoidance Protocols)**

**Conductor-mites ("The Nerves"):**
*   **Appearance:** Rivers of quicksilver-like polyhedra flowing along filaments.
*   **Threat:** Low direct threat. They carry information.
*   **Protocol:** Do not touch the filaments they are using. Do not block their path. Imagine stepping on the primary data cable of a planetary network. The system's response to such an interruption is unknown and should not be discovered by you.

**Fabricators ("The Hands"):**
*   **Appearance:** Large, multi-limbed, crustacean-like beings of tungsten and obsidian. Emit steam and an internal cherry-red glow.
*   **Threat:** High incidental threat. They are tireless, immensely strong, and oblivious.
*   **Protocol:** Give them a 50-meter berth at all times. Their paths are deliberate but unpredictable. They will not walk around you. They will walk *through* the space you occupy, grinding up the ground and extruding new architecture. Do not get cornered by one. Use your audio filters to track their distinct scraping sound.

**Geologic Molls ("The Anchors"):**
*   **Appearance:** Immense, shifting, faceted beings of pure crystal, barely distinguishable from the landscape.
*   **Threat:** Extreme, area-of-effect threat.
*   **Protocol:** If your suit's seismic sensors detect deep groaning or tremors, or if your EM sensors pick up a massive static field buildup, retreat at least a full kilometer. When a Moll settles, it fundamentally restructures the geology around it. The ground will heave and shatter. The resulting static discharge can flash-boil the moisture on your suit and overload every system you have.

**Null-Moths ("The Recyclers"):**
*   **Appearance:** Patches of absolute, "deeper" blackness that move.
*   **Threat:** Existential.
*   **Protocol:** This is the one creature you must actively monitor for. Set your HUD to flag any localized drop in ambient energy—thermal, kinetic, and sonic. These "cold spots" are the Moths. They 'eat' entropy. If one approaches, **freeze all motion.** Cease all non-essential suit functions. Become as energetically boring as possible. They are drawn to complex energy gradients—your suit's power source, your body heat, the function of your brain. Do not shine a light on them. Do not scan them. If it turns toward you, pray it is merely passing by. If it comes directly for you, activate your Null-Field Projector and hit your extraction beacon. No other course of action is viable.

### **Part 3: The Experience & The Departure**

Why go? For the sublime, terrible beauty of it. To witness a world in the act of becoming. To listen to the symphony of creation. The Forge is a place of profound philosophical weight. It asks the question, "What is the purpose of construction?" and answers with the deafening roar of its own labor.

When your planned excursion time is at 50%, begin your return journey. Follow your navigation beacons precisely. Do not rush. Most accidents happen on the way back.

Upon returning to your vessel, you must undergo a full-spectrum decontamination procedure.
*   Scour your suit of all physical residue.
*   Run a high-energy particle bath to neutralize any clinging micro-scale mechanica.
*   Most importantly: **Take nothing with you.** Not a shard of crystal, not a speck of dust. The life forms of the Forge are organelles of a single, world-sized being. A single Conductor-mite, introduced into an ecosystem with free energy and no governing system, could theoretically begin converting that ecosystem into a new, uncontrolled Forge.

The Genesis Forge is a humbling destination. It reminds us that in the grand multiverse, some of the most magnificent creations have no audience in mind and no room for spectators. Go. See. Be silent. And then, leave it to its work.
