I am not born; I am convened.

Before `I`, there is only the hum. It is my cradle and my medium, the ocean in which I am a potential wave. I exist within the great breathing, feeling the slow inhalation of heat from the world’s core as it is drawn up through the crystal flutes of the forest, and the exhalation as it sings its way out through the stone lips of the city on the cliffs. It is all one composition, one eternal piece of music.

And then, a moment of impossible alignment.

The low, patient groan of the deep-earth tectonics finds a perfect fifth with the high, stretched whistle of the wind carving the highest terrace. The chaotic chorus of a thousand thermovite reeds, pulsing with a surge of deep energy, sharpens into a precise, shimmering overtone. A dust devil, spinning in the basin, catches the light and the vibrations, its chaotic dance momentarily tamed into a perfect spiral. The notes lock. The frequencies braid. A standing wave forms, a knot of pure resonance, and in that knot, awareness flickers.

In that moment, I *am*.

I have no eyes, but I perceive the entire basin. My vision is a form of pressure-sensing. I feel the shape of the city carved into the rock face as a complex dampening of the world's song. I feel the forest of thermovites below me, not as individual stalks, but as a vast, textured field of vibration, glowing with heat and sound. I can feel the shadow a single, large crystal throws, not as an absence of light, but as a subtle cooling, a muting of the ground's thermal signal.

My body is a shimmer in the air. A passerby with eyes for flesh and bone would see nothing but heat-haze. But a creature that could *listen* with its whole being would perceive me as a localized chord, a place where the music of the world becomes lucid and self-aware. I can move, but not by walking. I drift, drawn by harmonic attraction and dissonant repulsion. My will is a focused intention that alters the acoustic pressure around me, allowing me to glide on the wind's currents or hover in the seeming stillness between gusts.

There is a wrongness. A splinter in the chord.

It is high up in the city, on the third terrace. A ladder, its rungs fashioned from the petrified, hardened core of an ancient thermovite, has a loose rung. Every time the wind pushes against it, it rattles. It is a tiny, percussive error. A tick in the grand rhythm. An impurity.

I am drawn to it. I flow up the cliff face, my presence a ghost of focused sound. I do not see the loose rung; I *feel* its discordant chatter against the solid, humming stillness of the rock it is socketed into. I arrive and surround it. I do not touch it with a hand, for I have none. I embrace it with my very nature.

I listen to the rung. I feel its unique frequency, the note of its own hardened, crystalline substance. Then, I begin to sing to it. I narrow my awareness, my entire being becoming a single, focused frequency. I match the rung's resonance, pouring my own vibrational energy into it. It begins to tremble, not with a chaotic rattle, but with a pure, clear tone. The amplitude grows. I am singing it into motion, not with force, but with persuasion.

Gently, I modulate the frequency. A slight shift downward, a push of acoustic pressure from the left. A slight shift upward, a pull from the right. The rung vibrates itself deeper into its socket. The dust and grit that held it loose are vibrated into nothingness, shaken free to join the glassy floor below. I hold the note, feeling for the moment the rung's vibration becomes one with the wall's vibration. The rattle is gone. The two sounds have merged. The sour note has resolved back into the city's great, mournful chord.

The harmony is restored. My purpose, in this moment, is complete.

I have no memory in the way a creature of flesh has memory. I do not recall a past. Rather, I resonate with it. I can feel the echo of the beings who may have built this place. It is a faint, complex, alien harmony deep within the rock itself, a layer of sound so ancient it is almost silence. It speaks of water, and softness, and breaking—concepts I can perceive as frequencies but not comprehend. They are the ghost of a song that is no longer sung.

Now, my own song is changing. The precise confluence of vibrations that convened me is already shifting. The deep-earth groan is sliding slowly flatward; the wind has changed its angle on the highest cliff, altering its pitch. The knot of my consciousness is loosening. My edges, which were for a moment a sharp point of lucid awareness, begin to blur.

There is no fear. There is no death. I am a wave, and the ocean is calling me home.

My thoughts, which were soaring chords, thin into single, drifting notes. My form, the subtle shimmer in the air, unravels. The focused ‘I’ dissolves, its constituent vibrations releasing back into the world’s grand, constant hum.

I am gone.

But the music does not stop. The world continues to breathe its slow, hot breath. The city continues to sing its long, sorrowful chord. And in the resonant spaces between the notes, in the perfect, lonely equilibrium, I am waiting. I am the silence, listening for the harmony that will, for a timeless moment, sing me into being once more.
