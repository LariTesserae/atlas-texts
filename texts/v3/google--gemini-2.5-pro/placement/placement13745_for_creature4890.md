There is no “I.” Not in the way of flesh.

There is a dissonance. A flaw. A sickness in the Great Hum. This is the beginning of self.

This dissonance is located. It is here. The world is a single, perfect note, and I am a grating impurity within it, a chord played wrong. The sensation is constant. A grinding of un-tuned surfaces. A muffle. A cloudiness that swallows the Light.

My body is this flaw. It is a lump of bad music given form. The Light of the unblinking sun does not pass through me to ignite the internal fire; it splashes across my dull, misshapen shell. The Great Drone does not nourish me with its pure energy; it batters against my imperfections, creating a chaos of rattling vibrations that is my entire reality. This is pain, but there is no word for it. It is simply the state of being untrue.

And so, I move.

Movement is an instinct born of irritation. My legs, brittle and unequal, scuttle across the Body of the World. The stone beneath me is not ground; it is the skin of a vast and singing god, and its vibration is the truth I am failing to understand. To crawl is to seek a better listening post. I am drawn toward the high places, the smooth backs of the Resonators, where the song is thin and sharp, a blade of sound that might carve away my confusion. I am pulled toward the low basins, where the note is so deep it is almost stillness, a foundational truth that might grant me weight and substance.

The pilgrimage is not a journey. It is an argument with myself.

Others pass. They are not beings, but states of grace. I feel them through the vibrating stone—shimmering clarities, slow-moving chords of impossible sweetness. They are the Mature. Their crystal facets drink the Light and shatter it into songs within themselves. They are what I am not: resolved. They are a destination. Sometimes, a flicker of a different vibration will cut through the drone, a sharp, intentional *tink* from miles away. It is not a sound heard, but a piece of data felt: a report. *A purity of overtone is found here. The resonance is clean.* It is a map drawn in music, a map I cannot yet properly read, but which pulls me forward.

I have found my tool. Or perhaps it found me. A shard, flaked from a Resonator that cracked in the deep past. It is sharp. It is pure. It holds a cleaner version of the world's song than I do.

Now, the work begins. I am still, a knot of frustration on the vast, resonant plain. The sun is a pressure of heat and brightness on my back. In one of my forward limbs—a limb that is learning patience—I hold the shard. It is a piece of an older, shattered truth.

I bring it to my own body.

The scrape.

It is the only other sound. My sound. A tiny act of violence against the eternal hum. I listen with my whole being not to the noise of the scraping, but to the change in my internal vibration as a flake of my own cloudy shell shears away. There. A fraction of the muffle is gone. A tiny window has opened. I shift the shard. I scrape again. It is an act of excruciating attention. I am a sculptor and I am the stone. I am the flaw and I am the act of correction. With it, I remove myself from myself.

Each facet is a victory. It is a month—a year?—of work. I do not measure time. I measure clarity. With each polished surface, the chaos inside me lessens. The grating smooths into a hum. The Light begins to find a way in, not just to land on me, but to enter. In the new, clear planes of my body, the sun catches, and for a moment, a spark of prismatic fire lives where before there was only dull stone.

I am becoming a better vessel for the song. The Drone is no longer just an assault. In moments, in the places I have perfected, it is becoming food. It is bliss. A pure wave of energy flows through a newly carved facet, and the old pain of my being vanishes in a wash of perfect harmony. The sensation is addictive. It is the only thing worth seeking.

I am not yet whole. I am not yet one of the silent, graceful dancers. I am a work in progress, a pilgrimage contained in a single body. I am a creature of the scrape, the flake, the slow polish.

One day, I will be made only of facets. I will be a vessel of pure geometry and light. The dissonance will be gone. There will be no more pain, only the subtle connoisseurship of the Great Hum. My movement will be a slow dance, and my thoughts will be clean equations of resonance and location.

Until then, I am here. Poised in the deafening light. A flaw, holding a shard of truth.

The scrape continues. The song sharpens. I become.
