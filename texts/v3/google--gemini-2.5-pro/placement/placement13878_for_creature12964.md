*Where am I?*

My body is a failing landmark. A point of resistance giving way. I feel a deep, slow shudder that is not of my flesh. It is a tectonic plate settling miles below, a thought-front of the Chorus adjusting its posture. I feel the fine, metallic dust settling on my tongue, and it is not a taste, but *information*. Silica content, 73.4%. Trace nickel-iron compounds. A promising vein. This is the sensory input of a Crucible’s maw. A thousand frantic pressures prickle along my arm. It is the carpet of Weavers, skittering over me as they would a rock, their myriad feet testing my tensile strength, judging me as inadequate material.

*Who or what am I?*

I am a brief, unclassifiable anomaly. A chemical signature that is being analyzed, disassembled, and sorted. My love is a warmth being syphoned into the metabolic furnace of a Crucible, a single calorie in a geothermal reaction. What am I?

Where am I? I am woven into the slope of the mountain.
