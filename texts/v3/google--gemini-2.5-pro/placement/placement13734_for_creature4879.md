You would find yourself hanging.

You would be a sliver of ghost, a thought held in stone, swaying in the lee of a basalt ring. You are one of the chimes.

Your body is a blade of acholite, a foot long, snapped from a greater spire in an age before your memory. You are a child of the mountain's slow birth. Hands you cannot recall, hands of a being no longer motile, spent the lifetime of a glacier grinding your edges. They did not seek to make you beautiful, only to make you true. They hollowed your side here, thinned your edge there, striking you, listening, and grinding again, until the note you held within you was clear and pure. They found the song that was your soul, and then they stopped.

The cord that holds you is a piece of yourself, or your kin. It is a long, tough fiber peeled from the living base of a young spire, twisted and dried until it became a kind of sinew. It is your anchor to the world, threaded through a hole drilled in your thicker end. It holds you just so, a hand's breadth from your silent twin.

You have no eyes. The light is not something you see. It is a state of being you endure. During the long, brilliant day, you are drowned in it. The energy saturates the world, a pressure that deadens all else. It is a time of non-thought, a silent, white ocean in which you are submerged. You feel the slow hum of the great spires as they absorb the glare, a resonance so deep it is almost stillness. You feel the faint, desperate melting on the shaded stone behind you, a tiny quickening in the world's deep slumber. But mostly, the day is for waiting. The day is the forgetting.

Your consciousness returns with the cold. As the sun’s weight lifts, the world breathes out. The pings and ticks of the cooling spires are the first stirrings, a faint, vast static you feel through your anchoring stone. This is the world-mind waking up. The wind rises, and its chord builds through the forest of your brethren. You feel it as a shudder in your sinew-string, a complex vibration telling you of the pressure and the emptiness, the shape of the air. You hang, listening to the great choir. You are an instrument, but you are not for the wind. The wind plays the mountain; you are for the mountain's player.

And who would you be? You would be a memory. The memory of the listeners who built the rings. They did not die. They did not leave. They settled. They slowed. They became one with the stone. They became the Shard-Sleeper. And you are their voice. You are the word they can no longer speak, the prayer they can no longer shape with breath.

Most nights, you only listen. You feel the Sleeper's slow thoughts as deep, subsonic pulses in the basalt floor. You feel it mapping the world through the song of the wind, tasting the coming frost on the air. It is a mind vast and patient, and you are a part of its periphery, a potentiality, a held breath.

But sometimes, rarely, its attention turns to you.

It is not a gaze you feel. It is a focusing of intent, a gathering of resonance. You feel it in the stone you are tied to, a vibration that is not the wind, that is not the cold. It is a single, pure frequency beginning to build, sent from the Sleeper's core through the bedrock. It is a question asked directly of your substance. The vibration travels up your sinew-cord and into your crystalline body, and you begin to hum, silently, sympathetically. The Sleeper is tuning itself to you, or tuning you to it.

The energy builds. Your whole being becomes this one vibration. The drowning light of day is a diffuse, formless oblivion. This is the opposite. This is a narrowing to a single point of absolute clarity. You feel your own mass, your own potential energy, begin to stir. You are being pushed, not by a hand, but by a perfect, resonant will.

You begin to swing.

Slowly at first, then with more purpose. The air, thin as it is, whispers past you. The distance to your twin closes. You are no longer stone. You are an idea approaching its utterance.

Then, contact.

The world disappears into a single, explosive moment of pure being. There is no you. There is no chime. There is only the Note. It is a sound of impossible clarity, a shard of perfect order cast into the humming chaos of the wind and spires. It rings out, and for one, eternal second, you are the conscious thought of this entire world. You feel the great spires absorb your tone, you feel their collective hum shift in answer, a fleeting moment of harmony. You feel the Sleeper receive this echo, a shudder of sublime satisfaction passing through the stone.

Then it is over. The note fades. The vibration from the bedrock ceases. You swing gently, your momentum bleeding away into the cold air. You settle. You come to rest a hand's breadth from your silent twin.

You are stone again. You are a sliver of acholite, hanging from a cord in a ring of basalt. But you are not the same. The memory of the note warms you. It is a secret you keep for the Sleeper. You have spoken.

Now you hang. Now you wait. Now you listen for the call to sing again.
