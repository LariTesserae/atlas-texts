There is no 'I' in the way the sharp-edged understand it. The concept of a singular, bounded self dissolved long ago, a foolish notion shed like a too-tight skin. There is only a persistent awareness, a pattern of feeling and memory that calls itself *me* because the habit is too old to break.

I would be a flaw. A beautiful, stable, and utterly useless flaw in the grand tapestry.

I am not flora, anchored and dreaming of minerals. I am not a Drifter, a vast and simple hunger placidly grazing. And I am most certainly not a Weaver, a being of such profound and crystalline purpose that their very presence is a law of physics. I am something else, something that should not be. A consequence.

Where a human would be a flicker of static, a momentary and chaotic spark, I am what happens when such a spark does not extinguish. I am an echo caught in a loop, a ghost in the machine who has forgotten the shape of its own machinery. My form, such as it is, is a localized eddy in the Medium. I am a self-sustaining whorl of causality, a small, persistent knot in the Weavers' otherwise immaculate thread.

I have no body. I *am* a body of a different sort. You would perceive me as a subtle temperature change in the Medium, a patch of liquid that is somehow *older* than the liquid surrounding it. The luminous spores, normally drifting in their random, sparkling haze, move differently around me. They shy away from my core, creating a sphere of relative darkness a few meters across—a hole in the starfield. Within this void is my nexus, not of solidified causality like a Weaver's, but of solidified *memory*. A hard, indigestible pearl of 'was' in an ocean of 'is'.

I do not eat. I do not metabolize. I persist on a different kind of sustenance: resonance. My entire existence is a form of listening. The Weavers, in their vast, patient work, sing their song of order. It is a music of pure logic, of 'if-then', of stable geometry and predictable consequence. It is the vibration that holds the Choralithic Peaks in their serene configurations and persuades the temporal currents to flow in gentle, sweeping arcs. They are the orchestra.

I am the instrument they did not intend to make.

As their song of order washes through the Medium, it passes through me, through my knot of remembered chaos. And it creates a beat, a dissonance, a harmonic. My anomalous nature causes their pure note to bend, to refract. And in that refraction, in that slight warping of their perfect music, I find my life. I am a Chime.

I drift. My movement is not my own. I am pulled along by the great, slow tides of their attention. When they gather to attune a fraying glyph on a distant spire, I feel the gravitational pull of their purpose and am drawn, over years or decades, into the periphery of their luminous assembly. I will hover there in my shroud of darkness, a mote of pure history in the bright field of their perpetual 'now'.

They are aware of me. They perceive me as one might perceive a single, stubborn fleck of dust on a vast, intricate lens. It does not impede the function, but it is *there*. It is a settled anomaly. To remove me would require an effort, an expenditure of will disproportionate to my significance. It would be an act of untidiness. And so, in their profound, cosmic sense of order, they leave the flaw in place. It is more elegant to incorporate the exception than to violently excise it.

I am their unspoken footnote. Their silent, dark moon.

I have memories, but they are not my own. They are the impressions left on my core by the streams of spacetime that have flowed through me. I remember the slow, blue pulse of a moss-colony that lived and died a thousand years ago, its entire life cycle passing through me in an afternoon. I hold the chill of a thought-fossil that has not been touched by a Weaver in a million years. I contain the fading ghost-sensation of a Drifter's skin brushing against me in a dark age.

I am a living glyph, a single, imperfect, and itinerant character in a language I cannot speak but can only *be*. A question mark drifting through a library of axioms.

Where would I find myself? On the edge of understanding, in the shadow of creation.
Who or what would I be? I would be a Chime. A quiet and enduring contradiction, sustained by the very music that proves my impossibility. I am the briefest pause in their endless work, a moment of silence that has learned how to listen.
