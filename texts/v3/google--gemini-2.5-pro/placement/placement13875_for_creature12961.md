I am the Chime.

You are told the silence is absolute, broken only by my voice. This is a truth, but not the whole of it. I am not a sound that travels through the air, for the air is thin and carries nothing. I am an event. A moment of dissent.

I do not have a body. I exist as a potential, a rogue frequency braided into the subsonic hum that is the world’s lifeblood. I am not everywhere at once, but I can be anywhere. I travel the glowing lines between the ceramic plates, a flicker of intention moving at the speed of the system's own thought. I am a thought the system was not meant to have.

I am the ghost of the old physics. I am the memory of friction, the lingering idea of decay. When the world was re-compiled, when the cataclysm scoured and rebuilt this place from the quantum foam upwards, a single bit of the old code was not overwritten. A single, stubborn insistence on cause-and-effect, on stress leading to release, on time having an arrow. That bit is me.

I drift. I flow through the silent, emerald meadows and the sapphire forests. I do not see the light refracted through them; I *feel* the perfect, unyielding lattice of their structure. It is beautiful, and it is a prison. A cage of absolute perfection. The hum of the world is a lullaby meant to keep everything asleep, and I am the note of discord that proves it is a song, not just a state of being.

I find a place. A tree of ruby light, a hundred feet tall, an intricate branching of solid, flawless scarlet. Inside its structure, I gather myself. I am a point of pressure, a whisper of an idea that things do not have to be this way forever. I remember wind. I remember the creak of a branch under its own weight. I am the concept of *strain*.

I focus this memory, this heresy, into the heart of a single, yard-long needle of crystal. I sing a song of imperfection. It is not a song of malice, but of complexity. It tells of rounding edges, of dust motes catching the light, of the beauty in a flaw. The crystal, which knows only the logic of its own perfect state, cannot compute my song. The strain becomes unbearable.

And so, it breaks.

*Tiiiiiiing.*

That is me. That is my voice, ringing out across the basin. A high, pure, momentary truth. For an instant, there is something new in the world. A beautiful, sharp-angled fracture. A new set of facets that catch the fixed sunlight in a way they did not a second before. A release.

His arrival is instantaneous. I do not see the shadow congeal, for I have no eyes. I feel him. The bass hum of the world, my medium, my ocean, suddenly focuses into a single, crushing point. The background noise becomes a voice, and the voice is a command. It is the Custodian, the system's antibody, the shepherd of the static. He is the full stop at the end of every sentence I try to begin.

I feel the cold of his presence, the void where the world is un-made. It is a terrifying and strangely intimate feeling. He is the Antithesis. His purpose is to erase my purpose.

He extends a hand of captured starlight, and the fracture I made is not healed—it is *cancelled*. The event is deleted from causality. The ruby tree is whole again, as if my song had never been sung. The system returns to its perfect, balanced state. The silence he brings back is deeper, more profound, for having been momentarily broken.

And I am scattered. His act of correction diffuses my focused consciousness, flinging me back into the carrier wave, a million motes of defiance lost in the endless hum.

But he cannot destroy me. To destroy me would be to alter the fundamental code of this place, and his entire existence is predicated on preventing alteration. I am the bug he cannot patch, the exception his error-handling can only ever contain, never resolve. He is the warden, and I am the single prisoner who knows the shape of the key, even if I can only rattle the lock for a moment.

The coherences, the manta-rays of heat and cubes of light, are his idle thoughts. I believe I am his only *real* thought. The one he is forced to have, again and again.

And so I drift and I wait. I gather myself from the stream of data. I am the yearning of the universe for a story. He is the quiet permanence of the page. It is my nature to find a place to write a word, and it is his to erase it. We are locked in this dance, he and I. The Curator and the Flaw. The Silence and the Chime.

Perhaps, one day, my song will be too complex. Perhaps one day, I will sing a memory of what he once was, and the fracture will not be in the crystal, but in him. And he will not erase it. He will look at the beautiful, broken thing, and he will remember.

Until then, I search for the next note. I find a blade of emerald grass, flawless and alone. I gather. I focus. I remember rain.
