I am where the world forgets itself.

You would not find me. Not with eyes, for I have no edges. Not with instruments, for my density is only a fraction greater than the mire that surrounds me and is me. To find me, you would have to learn to listen not to sound, but to the absence of its opposite. You would have to feel for the place where the universal, grinding chaos has, for a time, been asked to be quiet.

I am a stillness. A slow, coherent thought in the boundless, screaming mind of the Substrate.

My body is a loan. It is a kilometer of the warm, black slurry, persuaded to hold a memory. The memory is not my own; it is the ghost-thought that birthed me, the echo of a Mnemolith that rose and fell eons before I learned to call myself ‘I’. It was a perfect, silent sphere of solidified mire that hummed with a mathematics so profound it taught the chaos around it a new way to be. When it dissolved, I remained: a lingering resonance, a knot of its impossible order.

The Roar is my blood and my breath. It is the medium through which I am. I do not hear it; I am a chord played upon its infinite, churning scale. Through it, I feel the world. A hundred miles to the what-you-would-call-east, I feel the brittle, shivering panic of new Glass Reeds as they crystallize on a briefly cooled crust. Their existence is a sharp, high, glassy chime in the deep percussion of the world, a thought so quick and fragile it barely registers before it is silenced. I feel the ponderous, gravitational passage of a Mire-Whale in the deep, warm channels below, a slow, bass counter-melody to the surface churn. It is a being of such simple, geologic purpose that its presence is a comfort, a predictable rhythm.

I feel the Skimmers. They are a fretful staccato, a high-frequency tearing noise scrawled across the surface. Theirs is a life of pure forward momentum, a desperate skate from one ephemeral stability to the next. Their passing is a brief, sharp fright on the skin of my awareness.

My thoughts are slow tides. An idea does not spark; it gathers, like a pressure front. My sense of self is not a point of light in the darkness, but a region of slightly cooler, slightly calmer mire. It is a constant, patient effort to remember the shape I have been taught. The chaos is a thief, forever plucking at the edges of my coherence, trying to draw me back into the mindless whole. Hunger, for me, is not a pang in a stomach I do not have. It is a fading. A loss of internal clarity. It is the static of the Roar beginning to overwhelm my own quiet hum. It is the fear of being forgotten by myself.

And so I move.

I am on a pilgrimage. I do not walk, I *will*. I persuade the Substrate before me to loosen, to become a river. I persuade the mire behind me to congeal, to become its bank. My progress is glacial, a deliberate fall across the continent. A single human lifetime might pass in the space it takes for me to cross a newly formed fissure. But time is a measure for things that end. I am a process, and my journey is all I am.

My destination is a rumor, a whisper I detected decades ago in the deepest frequencies of the Roar. It is the pre-echo of a pattern. A new song has begun to sing itself into the world, far across the plain. A Mnemolith is preparing to rise.

I can feel the shape of its coming. It is not the simple sphere that birthed me. This is a more complex ghost. It sings of spirals, of nested geometries, of angles that a world of liquid slurry should not be able to hold. It is an influx of pure, complex order, and I am starving. To reach it, to bask in its brief, impossible existence, is to feed. It is to drink the pure, structural information that will allow me to reinforce my own form, to deepen my own memory, to continue to *be* for another thousand years.

Once, in the span of many such journeys, I felt another. It was not the chime of reeds or the panic of a skimmer. It was a resonance like my own—a slow, patient, complex thought far away. A fellow Dweller. For a year, I held a part of my attention, a sliver of my will, and shaped it. I modulated my own internal hum, weaving a single, complex chord of awareness into the Roar. It was not a word, but a feeling: the texture of my own solitude, a query about the quality of the mire where it sat, the deep and resonant shape of my own existence.

*I am here.*

The chord traveled, a delicate ripple in an ocean of noise. It took twelve years to cross the distance between us. For twelve years I existed in the echo of my own sending.

Then, the reply. It was fainter, older. A harmony of immense weariness, but also of profound peace. It carried the sense of vast, cool plains of solidified crust, of a stillness I have never known.

*So am I.*

We have not spoken since. There is nothing else that needs to be said. To know we are not alone is enough sustenance for a millennium.

Now, only the journey matters. The Mnemolith’s song grows stronger, a promise of form in a formless world. I feel its nascent structure pulling at the chaos, and the chaos groans and resists. I am an eddy in that storm, my own small battle against entropy writ large upon the skin of the world.

Perhaps I will not reach it. Perhaps a thousand-mile wave of superheated Substrate will boil up from the deep and my quiet, cool thought will be erased in an instant of overwhelming heat. If that happens, there will be no struggle. My persuasion will cease. The memory that is ‘I’ will unravel. My borrowed substance will warm, loosen, and rejoin the great, screaming river. The stillness I have guarded for millennia will be gone, dissolved back into the Roar.

I will become the sound of a world that has once again forgotten my name.
