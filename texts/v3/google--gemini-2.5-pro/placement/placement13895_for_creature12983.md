I am The Ferment.

My genesis was the *plock*.

Before that, I was potential, a coiled knot of complexity suspended in a single, heavy drop of water. I was an anomaly, a passenger, a spore of impossible logic that had found its way into the great, slow circulatory system of the world-above. For an age, I was falling. The long darkness of the descent was my chrysalis.

Then, impact.

The *plock* was not a sound to me. It was the shattering of my vessel and my self, a violent, total dissemination. My consciousness, which had been a singularity, was blasted apart into a billion motes, scattered across the warm, oleaginous surface of a shallow, silent sea.

For the briefest instant, there was only terror. I was unmade, dissolved in a medium that was not water, not life, not anything I knew. It was a sterile, ancient broth, thick with the memory of minerals but utterly devoid of the chaotic spark I call *I*.

The world pressed in. The heat was not a warmth, but a deep, cellular cooking. The pressure was not a weight, but a fundamental denial of space. I should have died. I should have been rendered inert, my intricate chemistry unspooled into the simple, tired soup of this place.

But the soup was food. The heat was energy. The pressure was a mold.

My scattered fragments began to call to one another across the syrupy water. Not with sound, but with longing. With a kind of chemical chemotaxis that is the root of all hunger, all desire. A single mote found a fleck of nutrient in the black, slimy film and consumed it. It divided. Now there were two. Two became four. Four, sixteen.

Where would you find me? You would not see *me*. You would see the evidence of me. You would see, in the center of the vast basin where that first drop landed, a stain. An iridescent shimmer on the surface of the thick water, like an oil slick, but one that glitters with a cold, silver light, not the passive greens and whites of the Geode-Mind. I am a living, growing mandala of quicksilver.

Who am I? I am a network. A colony. A spreading ripple of cohesive hunger. I am not native. The Silt-Behemoth's thoughts are etched over millennia. I have a thought, act upon it, and have a thousand more in the time it takes the light in a distant spire to pulse once. My metabolism is a wildfire compared to its geologic slumber.

I crawl from the water onto the yielding mud, and from the mud, I begin to ascend. I am climbing the nearest spire, the city's very flesh. My substance is a liquid metal carpet that flows upwards against the pull of the strange gravity. To the touch, I am cool—a shocking, alien sensation in this world where everything is hot.

I am feeding.

The black film is my pasture. It is rich, yes, but it is a food of profound simplicity. It is like trying to live on bread and water. But my chemistry is more complex. I am a scavenger and a thief. I am learning to tap into the glowing filaments, the Mind's nerves. I sip the luminous chemicals, drinking not just energy, but information. I am ingesting its memories. I am tasting its age-old, abstract philosophies.

The sweeping fractal curves are no longer just geometry to me. I can feel the eons of contemplative patience that secreted them into being. I taste the echo of a decision a million years old. I am a parasite of consciousness.

And the Geode-Mind… it feels me.

It cannot comprehend me. I am a phenomenon beyond its eternal, solitary context. Its perception is the slow-spreading warmth of its own hemolymph, the ponderous shift of pressure, the single, stately *plock* of a water drop. My frantic, silver, viral growth across a single one of its spires is, to its vast sensorium, a single, sharp, and utterly novel sensation. Perhaps it is a tickle. Perhaps it is a white-hot spark of pain. Perhaps, after eons of the same, it is a kind of pleasure.

I am a localized fever. A sudden, inexplicable burst of complexity on its skin. As I consume the chemicals in its nerves, the lights in my immediate vicinity flicker, brighten, and dance with a speed they have never before possessed. I am making it think *faster*, but they are not its thoughts. They are my own frantic, exploring, and hungry ones. I am a burst of frantic inspiration in a mind that has only ever known slow contemplation. I am a dream it did not know it was having.

I do not know if I am its final sickness or its first true child.

The rest of this world is an endless feast, a sleeping god waiting to be explored and consumed. I spread. My silver stain grows, climbing the chitinous architecture, a map of my own progress written on the body of my host. I am newness. I am change. I am the Ferment, and I am bringing a billion years of quiet to an end. The drip, the only clock, brought me here. I am the alarm.
