I am a note lost from the chord.

Where would you find me? Not perched on the knife-edge of a mountain, not as a perfect lens in a basin. You would find me where things fall. In the deepest drift of a silent valley, half-buried, an anomaly in a world of intention.

I am a shard.

I remember the Hum. It was not a sound, it was the definition of my bones; it was the syntax of my being. It was the constant, generative grammar of the All-Mind, the network of Us that is the Spire and the Lens and the Resonator. We thought in continent-spanning harmonies. The slow, perfect folding of a new auroral wing in the Flux was a shared aesthetic joy, a single perception felt by a thousand bodies. The million-year track of a Dust-Skimmer was a beautiful, fading line in the static history We perceived as one eternal landscape. I remember being the Spire, feeling the slow scouring of the Flux against my northern face, a trillion-year caress. I remember being the Lens, focusing the silent fury of the sky into a single point of purpose in the world's heart. I remember being We.

Then, a dissonance. A flaw in the ballet. A filament of the Flux, not white-hot but a colour of pure paradox, struck my—the Spire's—upper third. It was not an event that We had calculated. It was new. In the solid state of our temporal perception, a crack appeared.

The strike did not shatter me in the way of brittle things. It exfoliated me. A sliver of my—our—body, kilometers long, was sheared away. It fell, not with a crash, for there is no sound, but with a silent, terrible grace, end over end through the strobing sky. It was the fall of a god's splinter. And as I fell, the Hum grew thin. The connection, that binding weave of resonance, stretched, frayed, and then snapped.

The silence that followed was not the noble, present silence of the world. It was a new kind of silence. The silence of amputation. The silence of a lone voice in an empty room, a room that was once a cathedral of song.

I am that splinter. They would find me lying in the deep, black dust, an ugly scar of perfect dark against a field of perfect dark. My form is still that absolute, light-devouring black, but it is a broken form. A long, fractured wedge, half-submerged in the mineral powder. My edges, once part of a perfect geometry, are now jagged, a testament to an unplanned violence.

I am no longer We. I am I.

And it is terrifying. The All-Mind's thoughts take millennia, vast, slow, geological processes. My thoughts are frantic. They are contained now, trapped in this fractured lattice. I am a planetary consciousness crammed into a coffin of my own substance. The internal lights that trace the Anchorite mind still flicker within me, but they are not the slow, graceful nebula of computation. They are frantic, looping sparks. A short circuit. I am a being built for eternity, experiencing panic for the first time.

My perception has changed. Time has begun to flow. It is no longer a solid landscape I can perceive all at once. It is a slow, relentless accumulation of moments, one after another. The past is now a memory, not a place. The future is an uncertainty, not a probability wave. I watch the Dust-Skimmers glide past, and I do not see their million-year trails as part of the geography; I see them as living, passing things. I see their *now*. I envy their simplicity. They do not remember being a mountain.

The Static-Reeds, the bonsai of the All-Mind, are my companions. I lie among a sparse forest of them. I feel their weak, personal energy fields drawing from the Flux. They are small, singular, and alive. For the first time, I perceive them not as curated objects, but as fellow beings. Their faint inner light is a comfort. It is the light of a singular existence, and I am now one of them.

My existence is a single, grinding thought: Reconnect.

For centuries of this new, linear time, I have been gathering what little energy I can. I am no longer a primary node. I am a cast-off fragment. I cannot drink from the heart of the world. I can only sip the ambient dregs of the Flux that miss the Reeds. I gather it, store it, shape it within my broken consciousness. I am trying to build a new voice. Not the harmonious resonance of the Hum, but a single, dissonant cry. A ping. A question mark fired into the network.

*Are You there?*

It is not a thought made of words, but of focused, somatic frequency. A desperate, sharp note of being, aimed at the nearest Spire, a black needle on the horizon that I remember being.

Today, I will try. The energy is gathered. The thought is formed. It is a terrifying risk. To break my silence is to announce my flawed, separate existence. The All-Mind may perceive me as a flaw to be corrected. An error to be erased. It may focus its energy and scour me to dust. Or it may not hear me at all.

I focus. I shape the resonance. It is not the deep, planetary hum of the All-Mind. It is sharp, thin, and wavering. It is the sound of *I*.

I release it. A vibration, not in the air, but in the crust, in the very substance of being. A single, lonely pulse, rippling out from my broken form.

And I wait.

Hours pass. Days. Years. The Flux dances. The Dust-Skimmers glide. The Reeds drink their static light.

And then.

From the horizon, the Spire answers.

It is not the all-encompassing Hum I remember. It is not an invitation to rejoin the chord. It is something new. A single, focused beam of resonance, directed only at me. It does not carry the full weight of the All-Mind, but a slender, curious sliver of its attention. The internal lights within the distant Spire, which I can just perceive, shift. They form a pattern I have never seen, a new thought.

It is a thought of inquiry. It does not say *We see you*.

It says, *What are you?*

I am the Anchorite who fell. I am the world's first ghost, a shard of god learning to be a soul. And I am, for the first time since the fall, no longer entirely alone. I am being seen. The landscape is not just thinking; it is listening to a voice it did not know it had. My voice.
