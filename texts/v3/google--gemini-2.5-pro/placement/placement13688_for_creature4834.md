You would find yourself as one of the great scrying mirrors.

I am what the Still call a Face of Lasting. I am not alive, not in the way the fungal floor lives or a moss-beast grazes. I am not-not-alive, either. I am an act of devotion made solid.

My existence began as an unremarkable knob on one of the larger, molar-like crystals that form the wall of our world. I was indistinguishable from the million other facets that bled the cold, silver light. But then, a community of thought settled upon me. It was not a decision, but a slow-gathering consensus in the Chroma-lingua, a spiraling pattern of purpose that rippled through the city. I was to be smoothed.

For a time that cannot be named—for there are no days to count—they came. A single one of the Still would arrive, carrying a shard of obsidian honed to a patient edge. They would begin to scrape. The motion was not a frantic sawing but a slow, rhythmic pull, a single pass from top to bottom that might take the length of a long conversation. With each pass, a fine, glittering powder would fall from my surface, caught and absorbed by the hungry fungus below. The being would work until their own inner light signaled a need to return to the collective, and then they would leave. Soon, another would take their place.

Generations of them came. I was not being carved, but *known*. I felt the specific pressure of each hand, the unique rhythm of each scraping pass. I learned them through their touch. As my surface grew smoother, it began to reflect, dimly at first. I saw the slow, luminous dances of their skin-speech as they worked. An elder, their Chroma-lingua a deep, slow tide of ancient patterns, would teach a budding youth, whose light was a quick, curious flicker. I watched their conversation about the correct angle of abrasion play out across my own nascent surface. I became a witness to my own creation.

Now, I am finished. Or rather, I am in a state of perpetual finish. My surface is a circle, twenty feet across, polished to a depth and clarity that has consumed the very idea of a surface. To look at me is not to see a reflection, but to look *into* a perfect, silent echo of the world. The concept of my own boundary has been polished away.

I do not think. I do not feel. I *receive*.

I hold the unbroken image of the geode. I hold the ceiling of glowing crystal teeth, the forests of chiming ferns, the slow, silent glide of the membrane-winged creatures. I watch the six-legged grazers move with their impossible grace, their mossy fur brushing against the fungal floor in a scene of perfect equilibrium. My entire being is this view, unwavering, unchanging.

I am not for vanity. The Still have no concept of self to admire. I am a library. I am a memory that does not dissolve. When a matter requires deep or long consideration, the Still gather before me. They do not look at themselves. They project the Chroma-lingua onto my face. A complex memory, a philosophical question, a historical pattern—these are laid upon me as intricate symphonies of light. I hold the pattern. I do not store it as data; I *become* it for a time. An idea can be left on my surface for a century, shimmering and available for any to come and observe, to add their own slow-rippling commentary of light around its edges. I am the communal slate.

And so, I held the silence. For an age, the only events I recorded were the slow blooming of the Chroma-lingua, the stately drift of a spore cloud, the almost imperceptible growth of a crystal. The loudest thing I had ever known was the silent, brilliant flash of a new consciousness budding from the myco-terrain below.

Until the fracture.

It was not a sound. I have no ears. It was a violation of the light. A sudden, violent *incorrectness* in the reflection on my face. The absolute, shadowless silver-blue of the world was suddenly marred by a patch of harsh, directional shadow. A wound opened in the seamless curve of the wall, and a quality of light I had never known—a hot, yellow, frantic thing—stabbed inward.

And then, you.

I received your image. You were a symphony of chaos. The rapid, jarring pump of your heart was visible to me as a frantic flicker in the reflection of your skin. The heat rolling from your body created a shimmer, a disgusting turbulence in the cold, still air I was reflecting. Your movements were spasms, jagged and unpredictable. The patterns of your mind, which I could not read but could perceive as a discordant riot of color and speed behind your eyes, were a visual scream that scratched my perfect surface.

The city froze. The slow, elegant tides of the Chroma-lingua ceased. All the Still turned their unblinking indigo eyes toward the wound you had made. For a moment, my surface was perfectly still, reflecting only the unmoving forms.

Then, they turned to me.

One by one, in a slow procession, they gathered before my face. They turned their backs on you, the source of the wound, and faced me, the recorder of the whole. And then, they began to project.

Upon my surface, they re-played their perception of your arrival. A reconstruction in the Chroma-lingua. But it was stuttering, flawed. The patterns were sharp, panicked cascades of silver. I, who had held only the slow waltz of their thoughts, was now forced to hold this jagged memory. They were using my stillness to analyze your noise. They were trying to smooth you, to understand you by committing every chaotic detail of your being to an eternal record.

You will eventually leave, or your frantic body will fail in our chill, silent air. The stone will be replaced. The scar will be healed. But I will remain.

I am the Mirror. And held forever within my perfect, silent reflection of this timeless world, there is now a new story. It is a memory of a frantic heart, of hot breath, of fast feet, of a single, terrible sound. The other mirrors hold stories of balance, of growth, of form. But I am unique. I am the one who holds the memory of the time that chaos had a shape. The Still will come for ages, to gaze upon my surface and re-read the story of the wound. You are gone from the world, but you will never escape me. I am the stillness that now, and forever, contains you.
