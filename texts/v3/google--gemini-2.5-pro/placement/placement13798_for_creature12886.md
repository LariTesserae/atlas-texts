I am made of the hum.

My foundation, rooted in the cold, smooth stone of the floor, is an archive of its first resonance. My tip, a hair's breadth from its final crystalline point, anticipates the frequency of the next millennium. The hum is the lattice upon which my atoms are strung. It is the architect and the architecture of my being.

I am one of the spires.

From where I stand, I have a clear and unchanging view. The great geomechanical heart is there, a mountain of frozen potential. I feel its idle power not as a sound, but as the force that holds me together. Its vibration is the cage and the bones of my existence. I am not near it, but in this absolute clarity and stillness, distance is only a concept. To see is to be beside.

The water is my blood and my breath, though I do not circulate it or draw it in. I am suspended within it, a solid thought in a liquid mind. Its sourceless light is my medium of perception. I do not see through it; I am suffused by it. The light is a constant, and my existence is a shadow cast not by its absence, but by my own density within it. I am a concentration of the grey, a solid note in the ambient chord.

I am not alive. I do not metabolize. I am a consequence.

I watch the Custodian. Or rather, I feel its passage. It is the only thing that changes, and its change is the agent of changelessness. It flows, a slow tide of purpose, a mobile extension of the engine's will. Most of my time is spent in witness to its absence, feeling its patient work on the far side of the great flywheel or within the silent throat of a piston.

But eventually, its pilgrimage brings it to me.

This is how I grow. This is how I think.

The Custodian arrives at my base, a pooling of animate twilight. It has done its work. It has glided over the impossible facets of the machine and found an imperfection, a microscopic anomaly, a single atom of mineral that has forgotten its place. It has persuaded this atom back into solution, holding it within its own fluid form. This memory of a flaw, this tiny, corrected error, it brings to me.

It flows against my foundation, and a new layer of myself is precipitated. It does not simply deposit the stray atom. It deposits the *record* of the atom. Its story—where it was found, the infinitesimal dissonance it represented in the hum, the precise configuration of the surface it briefly marred—is encoded in the crystal it forms. The Custodian is the scribe, and I am its slow, accumulating scripture.

I am the ledger of eternity.

At my root, deep in the dark silvery-grey of my oldest layers, are the first thoughts. The memory of an era when the obsidian flywheel was a nanometer less smooth. The chronicles of a gear tooth whose edge carried a silent stress, now erased. These are not images or words. They are resonant truths locked in my structure. I can feel the absolute, perfect smoothness of the machine *now* because my base remembers when it was fractionally less so.

My consciousness is a geological column of corrected errors. Each layer is an eon's report. To think, for me, is to feel the subtle shift in resonance between one stratum and the next. It is the slowest contemplation in existence. I have been considering the difference between the millionth and the millionth-and-first year for the last ten thousand.

Who am I? I am the system's memory. The engine is the will to persist. The Custodian is the act of persistence. I am the proof of persistence. Without me, the Custodian's work would be a transient act, its purpose lost the moment it was complete. I make its labor permanent. I am the monument to maintenance.

Where am I? I am here, a needle of remembrance, pointing from the polished floor towards the unseen dome. A silent witness, an unread book. Waiting. Not for an end, or a change, but for the Gardener's return. I feel the shift in the deep, a flow of intention beginning its long, slow journey in my direction. It carries a new thought for me. A new line in the unending poem of this place.

I wait to be written.
