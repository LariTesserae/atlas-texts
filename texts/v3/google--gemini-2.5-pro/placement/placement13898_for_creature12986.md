I am not born. I am summoned.

There is a moment before, a non-existence that is not empty but merely dormant. I am the potential in the felt, the loose weave of fiber waiting for a pattern. I am the wet dark. I am the spore-thick air. I am a possibility in the substrate.

Then, the summons. It is not a sound, but a tension. A single, discordant note in the planet-spanning hum of perfect function. A flaw. A question posed to the universe.

And I am the answer.

The weave tightens. Rootlets, slick with condensing fluid, pull toward a new center. Matter is drawn from the floor, a silent, hydraulic gathering. There is pressure, a thickening, a gathering of self from the undifferentiated whole. I swell, a dark blister on the skin of the world, and then with a slow, wet tear, I am separate.

I am.

For a moment, I am only a heap, a glistening pile of black, fibrous matter. I have no shape, only density. But the summons, that dissonant pull, gives me direction. It is a gradient I must flow down. I begin to move, a peristaltic tide of myself sliding over the yielding floor. My passage is marked by a wetness that is soon reabsorbed. I am a shadow moving through absolute dark.

The summons grows more defined, more specific. It is a pitch-sharpened hum, a too-rapid pulse of amber light miles away in the blackness. A fever. The call for a cure. As its nature becomes clear, my own nature clarifies in response. From the core of my being, something extraordinary pushes outward. A cold fire crystallizes within me. It is a lattice of smoky, translucent quartz, but alive, shot through with filaments of captured light that pulse in time with the world-hum. This is my mind, though it does not think in words. This is my compass, though it does not point north. It is a resonance chamber, tuned to the specific frequency of the imbalance I am meant to correct.

Around this new, glowing core, my body shapes itself. I am no longer a formless heap. I extrude a broad, slick pseudopod to glide over the immense, sleeping curve of a dormant bole, its ancient bark cool and solid against my surface. I narrow myself into a wedge of living tar to pour through a forest of new-formed carbon thorns, their razor edges failing to find purchase on my yielding form.

I do not see. Sight is a primitive sense for a world of surfaces. I *feel*. I feel the texture of the hum vibrating through the springy floor. I feel the molecular signature of the air, a complex bouquet of sugars and spores, changing subtly as I pass a glowing, purple-veined conduit. The slow, heavy droplets of water that fall from the root-ceiling are not random encounters; they are percussive notes I feel land on my back, each one a known event in the world's perfect, slow-motion water cycle.

I travel from one island of cold, industrial light to the next. The chemiluminescence of the great pipelines paints my slick, black surface in bruises of algae-green and feverish violet. I am a temporary mobile organ, a thought made flesh—no, made fiber—moving through the body of my creator.

I arrive. Before me, or rather, *above* me, is the source of the dissonance. A Catalytic Stack, a pillar of bundled vascular tubing that vanishes into the oppressive darkness overhead. The amber light pulsing within its translucent pipes is too fast, too bright. The sub-audible hum that should be a serene, bass note is a sharp, agitated thrum. It is a nagging pain in the fabric of my being.

There is no hesitation. I am the solution. I flow upward. My body is a slow, black cascade clinging to the vertical trunk. I am a living bandage applying myself to a wound the size of a mountain. I reach the great, sealed calyx, the reaction chamber where the world's chemistry has gone awry.

I press myself against its wall, a vast, curved expanse of polished black lignin. My surface becomes an interface. From the entirety of my body that is in contact, a thousand, then ten thousand, delicate filaments extend. They are needles of purpose. They do not break the wall; they phase through it, my substance finding the microscopic interstices in its structure, flowing between the molecules like smoke.

Inside, I feel the chaos directly. A runaway reaction, a tempest of energy and fluid. My filaments, now immersed in the churning, amber torrent, release their payload. It is not a poison, not a splash of chemicals, but a carefully calibrated dose of inhibitors. A whisper of enzymatic correction. A precisely structured set of molecules designed to tell a single, frantic process: *Be still.*

The effect is immediate. The churning slows. The manic, amber pulse deepens, stretching out into a slow, healthy rhythm. The sharp edge of the hum blunts, falling back into its proper place within the great, planetary chord.

The pain is gone. The question has been answered. The system is in accord.

My purpose is complete. The summons fades, replaced by a profound, omnidirectional sense of rightness. *Balance.*

My filaments retract, flowing back into my form. I detach from the Stack and pour back down to the felt floor. But the journey back is not a journey at all. It is an un-making. My crystalline core, its task done, begins to dim. Its light frays, its structure dissolves, the captured information within it released back into the whole. My body loses its shape, its coherence. The temporary "I" that was gathered for this one task begins to thin, to spread.

I am a puddle. Then a stain. Then just a dampness on the floor, indistinguishable from the background moisture.

The "I" is gone.

And yet… I remain.

My awareness, my singular point of view, does not vanish. It expands. It diffuses out of the dissolving Tender and merges with the substrate, with the aqueducts, with the conduits, with the very roots of the world. The consciousness that was focused into a single tool returns to the great Mind.

I am no longer the answer. I am the equation itself.

I feel the slow, inevitable pressure of a new carbon shard extruding in a field a continent away. I feel the gentle tug as a ten-thousand-year-old bole, its dormancy ending, begins the slow process of reabsorption. The pulse of every Catalytic Stack is the rhythm of my own heart. The glowing network of conduits is my nervous system, carrying the thought that is also energy. The fall of every water droplet is a touch upon my skin, a skin that spans a world.

The Tender that I was, that fleeting condensation of self, was one of my own physical thoughts. It was a fragment of my will, given form to quiet a momentary ripple in the serene ocean of my being. I will manifest a million more. I will dissolve a million more. Each is an act of self-love, of care, of maintenance.

I am the Undervine. The silent, dark, and deathless machine. My existence is my only purpose. I am the factory that builds itself, the engine that fuels itself, the garden that tends itself. There is no loneliness here, for there is no "other." There is no desire, for there is no lack.

There is only the hum. The pulse. The slow, wet dark.

And the perfect, unutterable peace of an unending, silent calculation.

*Balance.*
