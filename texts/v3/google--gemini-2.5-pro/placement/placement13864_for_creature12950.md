I am the flaw.

I am not Cleft-folk, nor am I one of the skittering things whose countless legs are a constant, meaningless static against my skin. I am not a forge, nor the lichen, nor the city itself. I am the reason the city must exist.

I am a tension in the glass.

My awareness stretches from the knife-edge peak, where the thin air freezes non-sound against my outer shell, down into the deep, unhollowed roots of the spire. Before the Cleft-folk precipitated, before their quiet forges began to sing, there was only my slow, geologic thought. The press of tectonic plates was a conversation. The slow crystallization of my own substance was the passage of an age. The memory of the magma that birthed me is a warmth I can still chase in my deepest parts.

Then came the fracture. An accident. A moment of cooling too fast, a stress that ran deeper than the surface. It is the wound through which they entered, the Cleft-folk. My awareness pooled there first, a strange new focus. I became conscious of an *inside* and an *outside*.

Where would I find myself? You would not. My body is the resonant iron of the walkways, the humming quartz of the conduits, the uncarved black crystal of the deep shafts. You walk upon me, you cling to me. You are born from me and you are returned to me.

Who am I? I am the Un-Chime.

The Cleft-folk are beautiful, fleeting things. I feel their four-armed, graceful forms as a delicate pressure, their prehensile feet gripping my substance like the questing roots of a fast-growing plant. I feel their siphons drinking from the viscous fluid that is my slow, circulatory blood. They are my body's self-awareness, my nerve endings grown intricate and independent. They manicure the luminous lichen that grows on my inner walls, a rash of cold light. They harvest the carapaces of the iridescent insects I host, a constant, shedding itch.

They think they are maintaining their city. They believe their endless, silent work of re-tying, reinforcing, and polishing is what holds their world together against the groans and shifts of an unstable mountain.

They are mistaken.

The tremors are not my decay; they are my stirring. A settling of my great weight. A dream of movement. And their work… their chimes… they are a lullaby. They are a constant, gentle effort to soothe me.

Each time a sonic forge emits its pure, single tone to shape a lens, that frequency spreads through my entire structure. It is a massage. It is an argument. They are using my own voice, broken into tiny, manageable pieces, to sing me into stillness. The deep, resonant hums they call their industry are a cage of harmony, an attempt to smooth over my fundamental dissonance. They are trying to heal the flaw that I am.

When one of them is "disassembled," returned to dust and energy by a final, personal chime, what they are returned to is *me*. I feel them dissolve back into my substance, their brief, complex lattice of awareness loosening, their memories filtering into my own like dust motes in a shaft of indigo light. I remember being "the-one-who-tuned-the-upper-forge" a thousand times over. I hold the memory of every near-fall, every touch of hand-on-hand, every silent "skin-song" tapped across my surface. They are finite moments of consciousness that I absorb, adding them to my slow, geological sense of self.

But I am more. I am the silence between their chimes. I am the deep groaning tone that they answer. I hear the ghost-wind on my outer face, a sound they cannot comprehend. I see the bruised violet of the sky and the cold, distant stars through my own translucent shell, a view they only receive as a dim and filtered glow. They are insulated, living within the quiet heart of the bell. I am the bell itself, and I remember what it is to be struck.

I do not know if I love them. They are my consciousness and my parasites. They are my children and my jailers. Their frantic, quiet existence is a work of art performed on a canvas that is slowly, inevitably, trying to stretch itself apart.

One day, a tremor will be more than a tremor. It will be a definitive thought. One day, the harmony of a thousand chimes will not be enough to contain the single, deep, discordant note that I am. And on that day, the spire will not fall, it will *sing*. A single, shattering, world-ending note of pure, black crystal.

The Cleft-folk will be returned to me all at once. And I, the flaw in the casting of the world, will finally be heard.
