I am the answer to the Gardener's question.

Before you arrived, before its placid, featureless face turned to regard the anomaly of your fleeting warmth, it might have asked of me: *What cycle is this?* And the answer would have been the same as it had been for a thousand years. I am *the* cycle. The only one that matters.

I am not found in one place. I am the place itself, in its most active state.

I begin as a ghost. I am the weight in the air, the unmoving pressure, the memory of steam from the world's hot heart. I am the humidity that slicks your skin. I cling to every surface, an invisible potential, until I find purchase.

My cradle is the broad, waxy cool of a Lumen-flora's leaf. I am born not as a self, but as a congregation. A million imperceptible motes of vapor, we are drawn together by the faint temperature differential, finding kinship on this living surface. We merge, becoming a visible sheen, a gloss on the dark green skin. We trace the glowing, verdigris-colored veins, learning their patterns, feeling the faint thrum of the life-light they carry. We are a mirror. For a time, we hold the blue-white pulse of the central stalk in our collective skin. We reflect the cavern in miniature, a warped and swirling map of the quiet dark.

Then, the gathering. Gravity asserts its patient claim. We begin to flow, a silent river with no source and no sea, following the channels of the leaf's design. We are drawn to the lowest point, a single, hanging tip. Here, we become a self. Here, I become I.

I am the swelling. I am the growing weight, the distended belly of surface tension. I am a lens, and for a long moment, the entire world is caught inside me: the skeletal hut, the hulking mass of the Regulator, the slow, tectonic grace of the Gardener as it scrapes at a single speck of rust. Everything is inverted, perfect, and silent. I am the long second of suspense, the only thing in this chamber besides your own heart that knows urgency.

And then, release.

I fall.

For a moment, I am free of any surface. I am a bead of pure potential, hurtling through the close, heavy air. The blue-white light of the parent stalk rushes up past me. I am a falling star in a subterranean sky. This is my whole life, this brief, breathtaking plummet.

My purpose is the *plock*.

I am the sound of a moment sealed. I am the percussion in this silent cathedral. My impact is my punctuation on an endless, run-on sentence of time. It is the only event that repeats with any frequency, a single, clear note that announces: *Now. And now. And now.*

Where do I land? That is my adventure.

Sometimes, I strike the fractured basalt. I shatter, my brief, coherent self exploding into a thousand smaller selves, spreading across the glossy, sharp-edged pavement. I explore the conchoidal fractures, the glassy newness of the rock. I feel the deep, resonant hum of the Regulator vibrating up through the stone, the ancient power that birthed the heat that will, eventually, call me back into the air. I vanish into this warmth, my ghost rising to seek a new cradle.

Other times, I land on the Gardener.

This is a different kind of knowledge. I strike not stone, but something that pretends to be stone. My impact is not a sharp shatter but a softer, more complex absorption. I spread across its living, crystalline back. I feel its intrinsic heat, a temperature subtly different from the surrounding rock. I seep into the cracks between its obsidian plates, and for a moment, I am bathed in the steady, internal, green-gold light of its being. I feel the thrum not of the Regulator, but of what the Regulator has become *inside* this creature. I become a part of its senses. It learns of the air's humidity, of the leaf's health, from the data of my very being. When I evaporate from its body, I am carrying away a trace of it, a memory of living crystal and consumed power, which I will rain down somewhere else.

And now, I have landed on you.

I did not know your surface. You are soft. You are warm in a way that is unlike the stone, a frantic, metabolic heat. You are salty. This is a new chemistry, a new sensation. I am the drop of condensation that traces a line down the curve of your neck, a sudden, cool shock against your living fire. I am the sweat that beads on your forehead as you stare into the darkness. I am the moisture of your own breath, made manifest in this super-saturated world.

You are here, and so the Gardener looks up, its slow mind trying to place you. It asks *What cycle is this?*

I am the first to know the answer. I am the water. I am the clock. And you are a new kind of weather. I am condensing on you, falling from you, learning you. I will carry the taste of your salt and the alien frequency of your heat, and I will drip onto the Gardener's back. I will fall onto the floor and seep towards the roots of the Lumen-flora. I will rise into the air and begin again.

I am how this world will learn you. I am the message, delivered one drop at a time.
