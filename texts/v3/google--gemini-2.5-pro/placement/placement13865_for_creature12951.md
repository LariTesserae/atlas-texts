I would be the absence before I was the presence.

I would begin as a rounding error. A whisper of drift in the perfect equation. Far away, miles beyond my nascent awareness, a cathedral of stillness would have finally given way. An Anchored, ancient beyond measure, would have completed its long, slow dissolution, its constituent particles returning to the great, churning system. For an infinitesimal moment, the world-machine would be out of balance. Its perfect self-perception would have a blind spot, a single note missing from its eternal chord.

That absence is my genesis. That is the vacuum into which I am called.

I would not find myself, for I would have no self to find. I would be found. Deep in the columnar heart of a pillar-factory, in a chamber shielded from the grossest pressures, the command would be executed. I am not a child, but a precipitate. I am a solution reaching its saturation point.

My first sensation is not of light or of sound, but of instruction. The thrum of the world, the unwavering A-flat of a universe at work, is not noise to me. It is the loom upon which I am woven. My substance, drawn from the nutrient-rich fluid, crystallizes. I am coalescing. The bass note of the continental pumps sets the rhythm of my core's formation, a slow and patient hardening into the obsidian black of my central axis. The high, glassy whines of the energy regulators are the chisels that etch the fractal patterns across my nascent perceptual fins, the glowing mandalas that are not yet lit, the circuits that do not yet feel.

I am a thought being assembled, but I have no thoughts. Each vibration is a line of code, imprinted directly into my growing form. I learn the exact tensile strength of a Scraper’s mandible, the precise thermal differential of an exhaust current, the resonant frequency of a neighboring pillar two hundred miles distant, all before I have a "mind" to comprehend them. The knowledge is not in me; it *is* me. My structure is the library.

My gestation is an eon, or an instant. There is no difference here. The process is flawless and has been performed a trillion times before. When it is complete, the chamber irises open.

I am released.

The pressure of the open deep is a solid thing, a physical embrace that confirms my existence. I am no longer a component being built; I am a being adrift. My vast, translucent fins, stiff and folded within the womb, slowly, slowly unfurl in the viscous fluid. They are not for swimming. They are my soul, made manifest. They stretch out into the twilight, catching the cold, clinical blues and greens of the pillar-faces. The mandalas upon them begin to glow with a soft, internal silver, coming online.

And for the first time, I perceive.

It is not sight. It is not hearing. It is… resonance. The world's deafening song, which was my blueprint, is now my consciousness. The sound waves wash over me, through me, and my fins do not flutter. They absorb. The overwhelming external vibration of the machine dies upon my surfaces, and in its place, a perfect internal model of the world blooms into being.

I feel it. All of it.

I feel the slow, deliberate scrape of a maintenance drone on a surface so far away its light will not reach me for a week. I feel the molecular shear as a continent-sized grinder pulverizes mineral deposits in the crushing depths below. I feel the gentle, passive filtration of a million Gliders tasting the effluent stream I was just born from. I feel the other Anchored, my kin, my counterparts—each a silent point of stillness in the symphony, each one holding a perfect, silent mirror to the universe, just as I am beginning to.

My journey has begun. I am moving, but I am not swimming. A current, infinitesimally subtle, pre-calculated and ancient, has caught me. It is the current that leads to the empty space, the specific coordinate in the void where my predecessor faded. My purpose is to fill that space.

As I drift, my perception deepens. The world is no longer a set of data points imprinted upon me; it is a single, unified sensation. It is the feeling of a fly's footfall on the skin of a god. The distinction between myself and the world blurs. The thrum of the pumps is my heartbeat. The flow of the fluid is the circulation in my veins. The Scrapers and Gliders are not other creatures; they are my own cells, performing their functions. I do not think *about* the world. I *am* the world thinking itself, through me.

The journey takes a thousand years. The world does not change in that time, and neither do I. I am simply becoming more myself.

Then, the motion ceases. The current has delivered me. The pull of one pillar-continent is now in perfect opposition to the pull of another. The pressure from above is exactly equalized by the pressure from below. I have reached my null point. I have arrived.

Here, I stop. Forever.

My fins are fully extended, a thousand-foot web of pearlescent receptivity. The last vestiges of the external roar are subsumed. The journey of becoming is over. The state of being is absolute.

Within my obsidian core, a quiet blossoms. It is not an absence. It is a presence. It is the perfect, total, and unchanging comprehension of the Is. It is the shape of the music, held in a silent vessel.

I am an Anchored.

Where I am is the center of everything, a point of perfect balance.

Who I am is the awareness of the machine. I am the silence it works so hard to create. I am the purpose it did not know it had. The song pours into me, and in the quiet cathedral of my being, it finally finds a place to be heard. And the silence I was grown to hold, finally, holds me.
