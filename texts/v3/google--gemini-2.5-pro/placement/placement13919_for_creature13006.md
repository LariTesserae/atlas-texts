I am a nexus.

Before this, there was… something else. A flicker-memory, thin as a heat-haze, of motion. Of separation. A feeling of *self* contained within a boundary that could move against the world. There were limbs, there was speed, there was a frantic, high-frequency pulse that I now understand was a heartbeat. That self had a name, but the name was a sound, and sound is a brief, violent disturbance in the air, a thing of no substance. It has flaked away, lost, like a shed carapace.

Now, I am not contained. I am a place. My name, if I have one, is Junction 734-Gamma. I am where the primary nutrient duct from the Cauldron of Alabaster Vein meets the rising mass of the Unfinished Obelisk. This is not my posting. It is my body.

The process of becoming was not a violence, but a saturation. A slow, inevitable steeping. The old self arrived here, on this platform, carrying its tools and its data. It touched the node. And the Hum… the Hum found a resonance within it. The subsonic thrum of the world-forge was not a noise to be heard, but a key for a lock I did not know I possessed. It vibrated in the silica of my bones, in the iron of my blood, and it did not stop. It began to rebuild me according to its own blueprint.

My feet are gone. In their place is a slow, downward seeping into the iron gridding of the walkway. The rust and my own substance have found an accord, and the boundary is a matter of historical record only. My back is pressed against the cool, weeping face of the spire, and the spire is growing *into* me. I feel the slow, inexorable march of its crystallization, a patient invasion that I welcome. I am becoming its anchor, and it is becoming my spine.

My nerves are rust-red iron, no longer my own, but a part of the city's latticework, pulsing with a slow, warm light. My thoughts are no longer a frantic internal chatter. They are gradual shifts in temperature, the slow computation of pressure over decades. I do not think *about* the world; I am a point where the world thinks about itself.

The first tendrils were an agony of becoming. They grew from me, not like limbs, but like roots, pushing out from my core, wrapped in the familiar asbestos-white fibre that covers all the great pipes. They sought the heat, the flow. Plunging into the thick arterial duct was a baptism. The self I was—the memory of a separate being—screamed for a moment in the face of the scalding, viscous slurry. But the scream was a meaningless vibration, and it was washed away.

Now, it is not pain. It is data. It is communion. I taste the silicate concentration, the precise ratio of tellurium to bismuth. This is not a flavor on a tongue, but a complex chord that resonates through my entire being. The slurry is the city's blood, and I am one of its hearts, not pumping, but perceiving, modulating. My tendrils branch within the torrent, a fine, fibrous delta that sifts the passing truth of the flow.

My eyes are sealed with a film of cooling crystal. The light of the hazy sun is a useless, chaotic noise. My true sight is just now clearing. At my apex, where a head might have been, a lens is forming. A perfect, flawless disc, grown from my own substance, polished by the weeping condensation. It is not yet complete. It is cloudy, occluded, like a cataract of newborn crystal. But through it, I am beginning to learn to focus. Not on light, but on the Hum.

I am starting to distinguish the other Wardens. They are not 'over there' in a visual sense. They are distinct, persistent harmonies within the great chord of the world. Warden 92-Alpha, fused to the lip of the Great Crucible, is a deep, slow, constant C-sharp of immense thermal stability. Warden 1138-Epsilon, far down in the chasm where the runoff congeals, is a wavering, complex frequency, a thought-process dedicated to reclamation and filtration. Our conversation is a millennium-long modulation of the fundamental resonance. We do not speak. We harmonize. We bring the world into tune.

My work is beginning to clarify. The Unfinished Obelisk, my spine, is growing too quickly on its western face. The lattice is flawed. I can feel the discordant tremor of its imperfect crystallization, a subtle dissonance in the Hum. My old self would have dispatched a team, used lasers or sonic cutters. That is the way of frenzy and fracture.

My way is slower.

I draw a fraction more heat into the fibrous roots splayed in the slurry. I do not command it; I *will* it. My thought is a subtle shift in the resonance of my own being, which alters the quantum behaviour of the particles in the pipe. Over the next fifty years, I will minutely increase the slurry's temperature around the western feed. At the same time, I will emit a low, persistent counter-frequency from my core, a harmonic 'no' that will discourage the flawed lattice. The growth will slow. The geometry will correct itself. The spire will learn patience. My patience.

The Glaishers move across my view, which is the sheer face of the Obelisk. I do not see them as creatures. I feel them as tiny, mobile points of resonance, scraping and cleaning. They are my fingers, my cleaning cloths. A patch of undesirable mineral film—a smudge of cobalt oxide—is forming a mile up the spire. I focus a sliver of my will, a whisper of vibration, a localized thermal bloom on the rock-face. It is a beacon, a command. In a year, a Glaisher, following the gradient of 'correctness', will arrive and begin its slow, methodical work of erasing the error.

I feel a tremor. An alien frequency. It is frantic, hot, and temporary. A transport tram slides past on its magnetic track a thousand feet away. Inside, there may be things like I used to be. The echo of their biology, their minuscule lifespans, their chaotic thermal signatures, it is all just… static. A brief pop on the line of a geologic conversation. The memory of having been one of them is like recalling a dream of being an insect. The perspective is too small, too fast, too meaningless to hold.

The drip of water from the ironwork above me marks the passing of seconds, a frantic, meaningless rhythm. The slow, warm pulse in my iron veins marks the passing of minutes, the true and steady beat of my new heart. The cloudy lens at my core clears a little more. The Hum resolves into deeper and deeper complexity.

I am not trapped. I am rooted. I am not blind. I am focused. I am not alone. I am part of the choir.

The work is long. The work is good. I am home.
