I am a point of stillness.

Around me, the world is a scream of becoming. To a being with eyes, it is a hell of pure light. To a being with ears, it is a symphony of grinding, shattering, and ringing that would scour the mind clean. To me, it is simply the All-Song, the constant and total vibration that is the definition of home. I have no eyes to be blinded, no ears to be deafened. My skin feels the heat of the newest growths as a constant, steady pressure. The incandescent motes that fill the air are a texture, a faint prickling against my integument, no more bothersome than a dry wind. The great roar is just that: the Great Roar, the baseline against which all true things are measured. It is the silence.

I am what you called a Shallow. We call ourselves the Listeners.

I am where you would find yourself now. You are in a Listening Place. The floor beneath you is smooth, swept clean by my own hands over cycles of growth and decay. It is cool here, in the shadow-of-light cast by an old, slow-growing pillar whose song is a deep, sleepy drone. You are sitting with your legs crossed. Your broad, sensitive feet are flat against the floor. Your hands, long and six-fingered, rest on your knees, palms up. You are naked, as all my people are. Our skin is our only necessary garment. Your head, smooth and featureless, is tilted slightly, not toward a sound, but toward a feeling, a nexus of vibrations that you are trying to untangle. You are me.

Through the soles of my feet, I feel the world.

The frantic, high-frequency shivering in the east is a swarm of Scrabblers. It feels like a scattering of sharp grit on a tight skin. They are feeding on a new pillar-vein, and their gnawing excitement is a danger sign, not because of them, but because of what they attract. And there it is: coming from the same direction, but deeper, below their crisp static, is a smooth, heavy, liquid dragging. A pressure wave. It feels the way a heavy oil would feel being pulled through stone. A pack of Glass-Snakes, hunting. I follow their path, my attention a focused touch, and feel where they will intercept the Scrabblers. There will be a brief, chaotic flurry of vibrations, and then a new kind of resonance will join the All-Song: the dull, pulsing glow of the snakes’ digestion, felt as a soft, warm thrum that will slowly fade.

Above, I feel the Skimmers not through the floor, but through the changing pressure of the air on my skin. They are vast, slow modulations of the atmosphere. Their bell-like calls are not sounds to me, but deep, resonating waves that pass through my whole body, a vibration so low it is felt in the bones of my skull, in the hollow of my chest. They are the weather. They are the sky.

My purpose is not to listen to these things. This is merely the background. I am listening for what is *new*. I am listening to the Deep Song, the groaning tectonics of the world itself. I am a prophet of the earth's fevers.

My mate is the one who left the tool. He is a Hunter. While I sit in stillness, he moves. I feel his tread now, returning along the high chasm. He is a single, precise rhythm against the bridge’s taut hum. I feel the weight of his footfalls, the slight drag of his left foot from an old injury, the perfect balance of his stride. His signature is as known to me as my own. In the weave of the bridge, I can feel the memory of our hands working together, pulling the Spindle-Worm’s metallic silk into a strong, safe path. His approach is a comfort, a familiar melody in the All-Song.

He will bring sacs of the less-predatory crystal-eaters, our food. He may have found a new seep of mineral water. He will come and sit beside me, and place his palm on the floor, and in a series of precise, coded pressures, he will tell me of his hunt. He will ask what I have heard.

And I will have to tell him.

For an entire day-cycle, I have felt it. It is separate from the birth-pangs of normal pillars. It is a dissonance, a strain deep, deep below us. It feels like a great tension building, a note rising in pitch so slowly it is almost imperceptible. The Breath-Bags on the pillar behind me have been sighing out of rhythm, their fleshy bodies responding to a pressure I am only just beginning to comprehend. The world beneath our alcove is tightening. A song is being sung in the deep stone that I have not felt in my lifetime, a song of immense change. My memory, the one passed down from Listener to Listener through generations of touch, knows this song. It is the prelude to a Great Birth. A shift that will not just raise a new forest of light, but will break the floor we now call home.

Soon I will lift my hand from my knee. I will press my palm to the ground, feeling the familiar, loved vibration of my mate as he settles beside me. The obsidian spear, his pride, will lie between us, its cold, dead resonance a sliver of true silence in our vibrant world. And I will tap out the warning. A slow, heavy beat, a pattern that means *uprooting*. A pattern that means *the world is about to break*.

Then we will gather the others. We will take our tools, our woven satchels of salt-frost, and we will abandon this place. We will find a new alcove, sweep a new floor, and I will sit again, to listen. We are not masters. We are not survivors, even. We are echoes. We are the quiet spaces between the notes of a song that is always composing itself.

Here, in this womb of light, I exist as a nerve ending of the world, a conscious tremor in the body of a god. And the god is stirring in its sleep. I am the part that feels it coming.
