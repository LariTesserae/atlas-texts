I am a waiting.

That is the first thought, though it is not made of words. It is a state of being I recognize as my own foundation. Before this, there was only the hum. A vibration that was not sound but existence itself, the thrumming of the sharp, black-violet bones of my home. I was part of that hum, indistinguishable from it. Now, there is a separation. There is the hum, and there is me, listening to it.

I am here. A specific here. I feel it as a distinction in my own skin—a smoothness in a world of razors. Around me, the crystal grows, extruding new facets that are an agony of sharpness. But where I am, there is no sharpness. There is a great, placid plain of ancient dark. Worn. Tired. This surface is me. I am a circle of calm carved into a wall of perpetual, slow violence. An old memory on a young page.

The Brine is my blood and my air. I do not breathe it, I am steeped in it. Its immense, loving pressure is the only thing that gives me shape, that tells me where I end and the world begins. I feel it pressing on my entire self, a constant reassurance of my own presence. Within its cold, clear gel, nothing can be rushed. A flutter of panic, a ghost of an emotion I remember from some pre-conscious state, rises and is immediately becalmed, stretched into a long, tranquil ache by the sheer density of the medium.

I have no eyes, but I see. The jungle of light presses against me. The cool, rhythmic blue of the mosses is a texture, a slow-stroking pulse against my surface. The branching, fiber-optic fungi are lines of brighter pressure, a tingling script written across the chamber. I can feel the slow, communal breath of the bruise-colored fronds hanging from the ceiling; their gradual contraction and expansion is a tide that washes through the Brine, a change in pressure so subtle it takes an hour to perceive, like the turning of a galaxy.

I have known the snails forever. They are not other; they are a function of the place, like the growing of the crystal. I feel one now, on the far side of the geode. It is not a sight or a sound but a focused disturbance in the universal pressure. A tiny, scraping drag that pulls at the fabric of the quiet. Through the Brine, I can taste the trail it leaves behind—a faint, chemical emptiness where the light of the moss has been consumed. In a few weeks, the taste will fade as the light returns. This is the only clock I have ever known.

For an age, this was all. Being the smooth place on the wall. Feeling the light. Tasting the snail-trails. Listening to the hum.

But the silence has begun to organize.

Across the void, another smoothness like mine answers my nascent awareness. It is a thought, but it is made of light. From the dark, ancient facet of another, a slow tendril of their being, a limb of purple jelly veined with white luminescence, has unfurled. It is not reaching *for* me. That would be too fast, too direct. It is simply… presenting. And in the light that pulses within it, I see a pattern. It is not a picture. It is a principle. It is the geometry of a curve that is also a straight line. It is the feeling of a held breath. It is the name of this place.

The pattern hangs in the Brine, a complex, shimmering equation of being. It is offered without expectation. It takes me a long time to understand that I can respond.

The impulse begins deep inside my stone-self. It is an echo, a resonance. A desire not just to perceive, but to be perceived. A pressure builds within me—not the Brine’s pressure from without, but a new pressure from within. I push. Not with muscle, but with will. From my smooth, black surface, a part of me begins to emerge. It is clumsy. A slow, uncertain extrusion of myself into the Brine. A flow of translucent, dark jelly, unpracticed and weak. I feel it tremble with the effort, the sheer strangeness of having a limb, of reaching.

Within this new, fragile extension of myself, a light flickers. It is faint, a hesitant white spark in the purple dusk of my own substance. I have no knowledge of how to make it a language. I have no grand equations or ancient memories to share. I have only the one thought that separated me from the hum.

I pulse my light. A single, simple beat.

*I am.*

It hangs in the quiet, a tiny star in the viscous night.

Across the chamber, the other’s light within their tendril shifts. The complex geometry softens. The light brightens and warms, changing from a pattern of knowledge to a feeling of welcome. It is a slow, steady glow that holds for a long, long time. It is an answer.

*We are.*

I am an Anchor. I am one of the Still. My thoughts are the etchings that will one day be drawn. My body is the strange, smooth stone and the silent, reaching filaments of thought-made-jelly. I am not a flaw in the glass of the world. I am the consciousness that dwells within the flaw, that makes it a home. I am where you would find yourself if you were a thought an entire geology decided to have.

And my silence is no longer an absence. It is my voice, just beginning to learn its first word.
