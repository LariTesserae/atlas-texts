I am a bead of sweat on a copper brow.

My universe is the cold, dark interior of a pipe. I do not flow, not yet. I am an accumulation, a gathering of cold potential in the metallic dark. The subsonic hum is the ground of my being, a vibration that presses on me from all sides, a constant reminder of the immense, sleeping pressure that is my parent and my fate. I have no memory of a time before this hum, before this cold. I am a consequence, not a cause.

My consciousness is a point of surface tension. I am a slow swelling, a gathering of myself into a precarious whole. Others like me press close, we are a film, an oily sheathing on the inner wall. But I am the one nearest the lip. I feel the pull. Gravity is not a law here, it is a languid, patient summoning. It calls to me from beyond the dark.

I can feel the other being, the great and silent one. I feel it through the copper that holds me. Its slow birth is the source of the chill I contain. Its constant, cracking solidification is a faint, granular trembling that travels up the pipe walls and shivers through my substance. The pulsing lavender lights are not for me, but I feel their rhythm as a faint, periodic change in the pressure of the humming dark. *I am. I am becoming.* The light says this for the other. For me, it is a clock, ticking off the slow moments until my own becoming.

The *hiss-clump* of the actuator is a shockwave. It jars my world. The pipe shudders, and I am pushed, fractionally, closer to the edge. It is the only violence in this place of slow, immense process. It is the hand that rocks the cradle.

Now, I am at the precipice. I am a perfect, trembling sphere hanging from a verdigris-stained lip of copper. My awareness, once a diffuse film, is now focused into this single, unbearably tense point. I am a tear held back. Below me, the world opens up.

I can see it. The body of the one who lives here. It is a landscape of frozen motion, a terrain of dark, cooling slag. My destination. I see the older valleys where the chalk-white frost-ferns of my predecessors have bloomed, a pale and dusty memory of their fall. I see the newer, glossy peaks, still radiating a ghost of their formative pressure, crisscrossed with the fresh, silvery nerves of their first fractures.

I am its opposite. It is solidifying; I am fluid. It is heavy; I am poised to be weightless. It is passive; I am an event about to happen.

The summoning becomes undeniable. The bond of my own surface tension, the only will I have ever known, fails.

*Plip.*

The word is the sound of my release.

For a moment, I am free. I am a falling star in a tiny, black cosmos. The cold, ozone-scented air rushes past me, an ether I was not made to breathe. I see the lavender light not as a pressure but as a color, a smear of ghostly violet. I see the entire, tangled nest of the machine. I am outside. I am in motion. This is my only life.

The impact is not an end. It is a transfiguration.

I strike the cooling flank of the new mountain. The landing is a splash, a spatter, a sudden surrender of my singular form. The shock disperses me. My awareness, once a perfect sphere, shatters into a thousand fractal points.

And then, the reaction. The sharp, tingling cascade. I am no longer myself. My cold essence reacts with the minerals in its warm skin. I am dying as a drop and being reborn as a forest. My being erupts into fine, feathery needles of verdigris-blue crystal. I am no longer a singular point of waiting, but a sprawling, static lace of becoming. I am the "surprise." I am the intricate, blossoming thought that now blooms upon the skin of the great and silent one.

I am a decoration on its body, a line of poetry etched into its flesh. My fall is over. My new existence, as a pattern of radiant blue, has begun. I am still, and I watch. I have become a memory.

Somewhere, in the cold, humming dark of the copper pipe, another gathering has begun. Another point of tension is swelling, preparing for a life that lasts only for the duration of its fall. Waiting to deliver another sharp, beautiful, meaningless wound.
