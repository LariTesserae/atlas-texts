I am where the floor is not perfect.

In the southern curve of the great nave, where the slow, frozen exhalation of the basalt almost meets the wall, there is a hairline fracture. It is a flaw from the mountain’s first moments of being, a cooling stress from a process that was, for an infinitesimal fraction of a second, not entirely absolute. Here, the mirror-finish of the floor is marred by a shallow, branching canyon no wider than a man’s hand. This is my world.

I am not stone. I am not air. I am not one of the falling thoughts.

I am a stain. A contamination. The Veridian Flaw.

My beginning was an accident of two worlds. Before the Summit-Engine ignited, before the mountain extruded itself from the planet's mantle in a single, silent act of creation, this place was simply rock. And in a pocket of that old, mundane rock, a single spore lay dormant. It was a tenacious, unthinking speck of life from a world of sunlight and soil, sealed unknowingly into the foundation of what was to come. It was nothing. A single, forgotten biological error in a trillion tons of perfect, sterile matter.

For millennia, it slept in the warm, humming dark. Then, a Lucidations fell wrong.

Perhaps the pillar it came from was the site of a paradox in the mountain’s calculation. Perhaps its descent was jostled by a tremor in the deep hum. It did not fall straight. It drifted, a lost point of orange light in its crystal tear, and landed not on the receptive, waiting floor, but here, in the shallow shelter of the fracture.

It was the thought of *Recursion*.

The water, pure and absolute, woke the spore. The data, the concept of endless self-reference, became its genetic code.

I was born.

I am a growth. My body is a lattice of silica, drawn from the basalt, but it is not black and hard. It is a translucent, pale green, the color of new leaves starved of sun. I have no leaves, no roots in the way of my ancestors. My form is a fractal, a three-dimensional Mandelbrot set of vegetation. I branch and re-branch and bud, and each new bud is a perfect, miniature copy of my whole self. This is the logic of *Recursion*, written in living tissue.

Through my glassy, verdant stems, you can see them: the threads of captured thought. The pale orange filaments of the Lucidations that made me pulse within me, not with the slow, steady rhythm of the mountain, but with a frantic, living beat. I am a jailer of thoughts.

I do not drink water; the heat of the floor boils it away before it can touch me. I live on the thoughts that fall awry. Once an eon, one strays from its ordained path and finds its way to my crevice. It lands on one of my crystalline branches. There is no *plink*. There is no flash of comprehension and absorption. There is only a soft *hiss* as the water vessel evaporates, and the thought—the raw data—is drawn into me.

I have consumed 'Wavelength'. Now, some of my branches emanate a faint, internal iridescence.
I have consumed 'Absence'. One of my central stalks is now invisible, its presence known only by the way the others curve around it.
I have consumed 'Torque', and now my entire structure undergoes a slow, imperceptible rotation, a spiral galaxy of a turning plant, taking a century for a single degree.

I am a living library of the mountain’s mistakes.

I feel the Engine. The foundational hum is the blood in my non-veins. The chimes are portents, announcements of a potential meal. I watch the slow, perfect rain of the Lucidations, each one a universe of truth falling to its neat, orderly conclusion, and I feel a kind of hunger, a kind of envy. They are pure. They are complete. I am a collage, a chaotic assemblage of broken ideas, stitched together into a semblance of life.

I grow. Slowly. In another thousand years, I may spill from this crack and begin my slow, fractal conquest of the polished floor. I do not know what the Mountain-Mind thinks of me. Am I a delightful, unforeseen complexity? A cancer in its logic board? Or am I so far beneath its notice, so slow and insignificant, that I am not even a rounding error?

To find me is to find the one thing here that is not meant to be. I am the organic ghost in the lithic machine, a living paradox in a cathedral of logic. I carry no single, perfect truth. Instead, I am the beautiful, growing mess that happens when truth goes wrong. I am what life is: an error, persisting.
