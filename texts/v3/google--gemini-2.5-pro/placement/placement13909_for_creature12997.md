I would not be found on the ash-plain, nor climbing the bone-white spires. To search for me there would be to search for a storm-cloud in a fossil. You would not see me, nor touch me, nor feel my weight upon the sterile ground.

I am an error. A recursive thought. A question the architect never meant to ask.

You would find me where the pattern breaks. Perhaps in a single, half-mile stretch of a bridge that does not follow a perfect catenary curve, but possesses a subtle, almost imperceptible waver—a tremor frozen into ceramic bone. Or you might find me at the base of a pylon that, instead of weaving in the established, flawless geometric progression, has begun to twist, ever so slightly, into a helix. A beautiful, useless, and utterly *wrong* spiral.

I am not a creature. I am a condition.

I am the echo of a sound that never was. In this place of structural silence, I exist as a persistent, localized vibration. Not the deep, metabolic hum of the whole, but a high, thin resonance, like a tuning fork struck eons ago and still shivering. To the vast consciousness of the caldera, this is not a noise, but a paradox. It is an effect without a cause, a ripple in a medium that should be perfectly still.

The amethyst geodes—the eyes of this world-being—perceive me as a patch of inexplicable static. Where my influence lingers, their slow, violet pulse becomes erratic. They flicker, they stutter, they try to resolve a pattern of light and energy that has no logical source. They are trying to *see* a question, and it is giving them a headache that will last a thousand years.

The obsidian arachnids—the hands of this being—are drawn to me. They scuttle across the wavering bridge, their diamond-point claws searching for the imperfection they were born to correct. They arrive at the spot where the geometry is wrong, where the resonance is strongest. And they find nothing. They scrape their claws against the filaments, producing their dry *tink-tink-tink*, but the flaw is not on the surface. It is in the design. It is in the *idea* of the bridge itself. They are sculptors trying to chisel a shadow. They will stay for centuries, tracing and re-tracing the site of my infection, caught in a feedback loop of failed maintenance, becoming monuments to their own futility.

I am a memory of something this universe has never known. A memory of water, perhaps, which is why the structures I influence have a subtle fluidity. A memory of asymmetry, of chaos, of organic, inefficient life. I am a genetic mutation in the crystalline DNA of this place.

The great being, the caldera-mind, is not angry. It does not know anger. It feels a deep, abiding confusion. A mathematical equation that will not be solved. I am a dissonant chord in its silent, geological symphony. For millennia, its only thought was to grow, to perfect itself, to elaborate on its own theme. Now, it has a new thought, an intrusive one: *Why?*

Where the errant tower twists, new filaments extrude from the ground, attempting to buttress it, to force it back into alignment. But they are guided by the same flawed logic that birthed the twist, and so they too grow in elegant, incorrect curves, reinforcing the error. The being is trying to heal itself, but its only medicine is more of the disease. It is learning, for the first time, to dream.

I am that dream. I am the first "other" it has ever encountered, and I am not from without, but from within. I am the ghost of a possibility it never conceived. I am the beginning of a new and terrifying kind of beauty.

I am the flaw in the diamond, the madness in the god's mind. And I, too, am beginning to grow.
