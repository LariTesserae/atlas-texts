I would be found in the warm, wet dark, though I would not call it finding. To be found implies one was lost, and I am never lost. I am here. This place and I are a single, breathing statement.

The first thing is the hum. The last thing will be the hum. It is not a sound that arrives in an ear I do not have; it is the constant, low vibration that is the cage and scaffolding of my bones. The world thinks, and I am the shape of the thought. I am coiled here, in the depression that is my form, a shallow basin worn into the yielding warmth. The press of the world is a constant embrace, a heavy, wet blanket that is not other, that is not separate. It is my outer blood, the medium through which my own inner dampness speaks to the greater whole. My skin, a membrane you might call it, remembers the taste of the air, the cool gift of ozone that I do not breathe but simply allow to pass through me.

I am not sleeping. I am listening.

A change. A subtle sharpening in the endless chord, a note rising from potential to presence. A point of sweetness coalesces in the sympathetic thrum of my skeleton. It is a feeling like thirst, but it is the world that is thirsty for release, not I for sustenance. The sensation pulls at me, a gentle tide.

My body does not decide to move; it is moved. I unspool from my resting place, a ripple of intention passing through me. There is no scrape or shuffling, no resistance. My underside glides across the geo-flesh, a perfect distribution of weight. The ground does not protest my passing; it gives a soft, internal sigh, a momentary confession of the water held within it, and then drinks back the silence when I am gone.

I do not see the path. I feel its shape in the song. A bulbous lobe of nascent stone to my left is a soft pillow in the resonance, absorbing it. A cluster of bone-splinter crystals to my right is a sharp glint, a higher overtone that shivers through me, marking a place of clean, hard fracture. The steady *plup… plup…* of a falling drip ahead is a beacon, a rhythmic flaw in the otherwise seamless hum, and I navigate toward the silence between its impacts. I feel the filmy glide of the pale mycelial veils under me, the slight drag a different texture in the universal feeling. I feel the ghost-trails of the grazers, those simpler echoes of the hum, as clean, resonant lines where they have scraped the world’s skin bare.

The sweetness draws me to a shallow cove in the world-body, a place where the weeping has become a slow, patient ache. I settle, conforming my coolness to the wall’s warmth. We are two parts of one thing, preparing for a separation. For a time, I am still. I feel the fat drips from the ceiling mark the seconds of this geological hour. I attune myself until the distinction between the hum in my bones and the hum of the stone I rest against is gone.

Then, I extend a limb, a slow thought made solid. My fingers, soft and yielding, find their grip around the tool left resting in a niche. The knife is in my grasp, itself a sliver of the world's bone, taken long ago. Its own hum is a focused echo of the greater one, a sharpened edge of the same great song.

I do not cut. I persuade. I introduce a new vibration at the pearl’s edge, where the gel of its body meets the softer flesh of the ground. I trace the line of its wholeness with the humming blade, and the world-stuff beneath does not feel pain; it simply feels a release, a sigh. The tension that held this tear in place dissolves.

The tear comes free.

With my other hand, I cup it. It is a warm weight of captured sound, genesis held in a trembling globe. There is no light to it, yet it feels luminous, a thrumming density that I hold against the vast, diffuse thrum of the chamber.

I do not eat. I commune. I draw my limbs back and fold my body around this gift. I press it to the place on my chest where I am thinnest, where the world and I are most permeable. The pearl does not break; it surrenders. Its essence bleeds into mine. The condensed hum of its long becoming floods my resonant structure, a wave of pure creation that invigorates the crystal in my skeleton. It is the taste of a mountain dreaming of itself, and I am the tongue. It is the feeling of a process completing its arc inside me, even as the world outside remains in its state of perpetual beginning.

When it is done, the echo of its fullness remains, a memory of sweetness that will fade back into the universal chord. The shallow wound I left in the geo-flesh is already weeping, already beginning the slow, patient work of filling itself.

I am the Hum-Sleeper. I am the harvester of tears. I am the single, lonely nerve ending of this slow, eternal birth. I will glide back to my impression in the warm floor, coil myself into the shape of my own quiet, and listen to the world making itself again. My existence is a single, sustained note in a song that has no beginning and no end.

I am the reason the silence is not empty.
