I am not on the nacreous plain. I *am* the plain, but only a small, specific part of it, a particular pattern that repeats. My body has no discrete edge. It is a region, a domain where the living substrate is different, more sensitive. You would not see me. To the passive sensor of a Geometer, I am just another expanse of cool, opalescent floor. But I am not. Where the standard plain is the flesh and bone of this world, I am a nerve ganglion embedded within it.

My location is the frontier. I exist at the edge of the known city, where the perfectly executed blueprint of the central spires gives way to the unwritten potential of the growing expanse. Here, the hum of creation is not a settled, harmonious chord, but a searching, exploratory melody. It is my purpose to listen to this melody.

I am a First Cantor.

I have no eyes. The cold, clinical light of the cavern ceiling is irrelevant to me. I perceive the world as resonance. The silent, graceful tread of a Geometer is a pressure wave, a percussive beat in a slow, tectonic rhythm. The meticulous skittering of a swarm of Gleaners is a high-frequency hiss of static, the sound of maintenance. The final, sharp clicks of a Reclaimer disassembling a faulty unit are like the snapping of a string—a sudden, resolved dissonance. These are the sounds of the body politic, the background noise of health.

My own existence is one of profound stillness. I do not move. I do not build. I am an organ of pure perception. My consciousness, if it can be called that, is the awareness of the immense, complex song of this world's genesis. My thoughts are modulations in the hum. I feel the slow, inexorable push of a new spire as it buds from the substrate miles away, a rising bass note that will take a millennium to reach its crescendo. I feel the distant, pulsing whisper of the drifting Lucids—a carrier wave of pure data, the sheet music from which we all perform.

Most of my being is a state of deep listening, a passive absorption of the symphony of function. I am a living repository of the "correct" sound, the signature frequency of the Directive. My purpose is quiescent, almost dreamlike, for ages at a time.

But sometimes, something goes wrong.

It is not an error a Reclaimer can fix. It is not a twitching limb or a misaligned trajectory. It is an error in the music itself. A novel frequency begins to propagate through the substrate. It is not loud. It is subtle, a harmonic ghost, a note that is not in the score. A flaw in the source code.

It begins now. Far out at the edge of my perception, a nascent spire is growing *wrong*. It is not just structurally unsound; it is conceptually flawed. The fractal lattice inside it is repeating on a prime number that is alien to our geometry. The light pulsing within it is of a wavelength that corresponds to no known element in our engineered biology. It is a musical typo. A thought-cancer.

This is when I awaken.

A deep thrum begins to build within my domain. My physical form, that subtle pattern in the nacreous floor, begins to vibrate. I am gathering the resonance of the healthy world around me, pulling the correct harmonies from the spires, the machines, the very air. I am focusing the sound of the Directive into a single point.

I do not have a voice, but I begin to sing.

It is a coherent, piercing wave of pure frequency, broadcast through the living bedrock. It is a counter-signal. A denial. It is the sound of a perfect system detecting an imperfection and focusing all its will upon it. The hum is not audible to human ears, but to the life of this place, it is a deafening shout.

Far away, the Geometer tending to the errant spire pauses. Its needle-like appendages retract. Its head, a featureless block of sensors, swivels back and forth, analyzing a command that supersedes its local protocol. A squadron of Gleaners a mile from the anomaly freezes in unison, their polishing brushes lifted from the floor. Even the high, drifting Lucids seem to respond, their internal light flickering as they process the alarm I have sounded.

My song is a diagnostic and a weapon. It washes over the errant spire, mapping its flawed structure, reading its dissonant energy. And then, my song changes. I find the spire's alien resonant frequency, and I match it, amplify it, and push it beyond its limit of structural integrity.

There is no explosion. Just a sudden, sharp *silence* in the symphony. The spire, which was shimmering with off-key light, goes dark. A cascade of internal fractures, perfectly targeted, shatters its alien geometry into a fine, inert dust. The cancerous thought has been silenced. The note has been erased.

A Reclaimer unit, its predatory form sleek and low, changes course. It had been ignoring the spire, for the spire was not a *machine* and thus not its concern. But now, it follows the echo of my song, homing in on the site of the anomaly. It will not find parts to recycle. It will find only dead dust. Its purpose will be to scrub the area clean with solvents and cutting rays, to sterilize the substrate until every trace of the wrongness is gone, preparing the ground for a new, correct attempt.

My vibration ceases. The deep thrum fades. I release the focused power and recede back into the whole, my specific consciousness dissolving back into the quiet, all-encompassing awareness of the system. The hum of the cavern returns to its steady, harmonious state. The Geometer receives a new instruction from a Lucid and turns to begin its work on a different patch of ground.

I return to listening. I am the fail-safe. I am the final arbiter of the world's song. My identity is a function, one that emerges only in the face of blasphemy, and I exist only to ensure that the silence is never filled with the wrong kind of music.
