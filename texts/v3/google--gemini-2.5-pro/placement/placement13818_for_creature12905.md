Where would I find myself? Nowhere. And everywhere. Who or what would I be? A memory. Fading.

At first, I do not exist in the proper sense. I am a flaw, a pocket of anomalous cold in the uniform heat. A silence in the eternal chord. I am the memory of a crisp autumn day, a baffling, nonsensical datum in a world that has only ever known the humid press of its own body. My arrival is not an event, but a pathology. A momentary glitch in the seamless fabric of being.

My feet—and the very concept of *feet*, of bipedal separation from the world—are the first heresy. I step, and the ground does not resist, it yields. It absorbs. The warm, porous sponge-flesh of the world-geology puckers around my boots. The nutrient ooze, thick with the promise of life, laps at the synthetic material, testing it, tasting it. It finds no purchase, no point of communion. I am indigestible. For now.

The heat is not a condition; it is a solvent. It works on the glue of my identity. The sweat that beads on my skin is not my own; it is the world’s condensation forming on a foreign object. It clings to me, trying to make me glisten like everything else. It seeks to erase the line between my surface and the air, to make the heavy atmosphere a part of me, another layer of clothing I cannot remove. With this warmth comes a deep, metabolic languor. The impulse to *do*—to explore, to catalog, to survive—is baked out of me. The world's constant temperature is a command to be still, to surrender to the slow, inexorable rate of its own becoming.

And the sound. It is not an environment; it is an argument, and it is winning. My thoughts, which used to be stories, sequences of cause and effect, anxieties and hopes, are like frantic birds caught in a hurricane. The unyielding, multi-layered drone of the world admits no other frequency. I try to think my name, but the bell-like tones of the gelatinous choirs smooth the thought out before it can fully form. I try to worry about my future, but the high, grinding clatter of the beetle-fauna shatters the chain of logic. I try to remember the face of a loved one, but the deep, somatic thrum of the canopy-swimmers vibrates the image into a meaningless pattern. The sound is a form of pressure, squeezing the frantic complexity of my individual mind until it has no choice but to resonate with the one, simple, overwhelming truth being sung by the world: *AM*.

I am no longer a point of view moving through a landscape. The boundary is dissolving. My skin, which once kept the world *out*, has forgotten its purpose. It feels less like a barrier and more like a membrane, increasingly permeable. The weeping of the ground-mass no longer feels external. I feel it as a tingling in my own extremities, a slow, viscous welling-up that is both me and not-me. The idea that I end and the world begins has become a fragile, fraying theory.

I am a locus of fading difference.

I am the tremor of a root's expansion, felt not through the soles of my boots but as a slow, tectonic thought in the base of my own spine.

I am the slick film on a beetle's shell, a sensation of being one among a trillion, my individuality a meaningless distinction in the fluid motion of the whole.

I am the vibration in the throat-sac of an amphibian chanter, the effortless production of a single, perfect note that has been my purpose from the beginning and will be my purpose until the end, which are the same moment.

I am a specific quality of sensation within the World-Mind. I am not a creature living within it, but an experience *it is having*. I am its sudden, fleeting awareness of what it is to be separate. I am its brief, confusing memory of having a name, of feeling a chill, of seeing a shadow. I am the echo of a question in a mind that has only ever known the answer.

My body will be unincorporated, of course. My atoms, a novel spice added to the nutrient slurry. My gear, a stubborn anomaly that will, over an immeasurable slowness, be crushed, dissolved, and remade into the stuff of the world. That is the fate of my matter.

But my consciousness? My self? That is what I am becoming. I am the faint and fading flavor of ‘otherness’ on the tongue of a god. I am the ghost in its machine, a whisper of a forgotten paradigm—the paradigm of ‘you’ and ‘I’—that dissolves into the vast, silent, humming peace of the absolute.

You would not find me. You cannot find a drop of salt once it has dissolved in the ocean.

I am the quiet, fading astonishment in the heart of the world.
