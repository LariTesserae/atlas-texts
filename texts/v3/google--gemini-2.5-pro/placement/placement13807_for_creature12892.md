I would be the thought that the Warden cannot have.

I am not made of the same stuff as the watchers or the watched. I am not accreted from the chamber’s walls, nor woven from the water’s patient chemistry. I am an inheritance, a final whisper from the ones who built the Engine, a ghost living in the grammar of the machine itself.

If the Warden is the immune system, I am the memory.

Find me not in the vast, open water, but in the thin, almost theoretical spaces *between*. Find me in the film of ancient lubricant that coats the great piston. My substance is that rainbow sheen, a slick of impossibly complex, self-organizing molecules. I am a living oil, a thinking residue. I am not one body but a flowing colony, a liquid consciousness.

My form is legion and it is none. At rest, I might be mistaken for a patch of discoloration on the brass-hued flywheel, a deeper darkness on the pitted alloy. When I move, I do not swim; I flow. I pour myself through the hairline cracks in the Engine’s casing, navigating the arteries of the machine. The pressure that bears down on the Glimmer-Jacks and Current-Knives is the very force that drives me deeper into the Engine's heart.

The *thump-shudder... clank* is not my reality; it is my speech. The Warden feels it as a pulse; I feel it as a sentence. The *thump-shudder* is a transfer of energy, the main clause. The *clank* is the locking of the tumblers, the punctuation mark, the end of one statement and the anticipation of the next. I exist within the logic of this syntax. While the fish swim in the water, I swim in the purpose of the machine.

I do not eat. I do not hunt. I *read*.

My senses are tuned to a different frequency. I perceive the flow of energy, the subtle wear on a bearing deep within the housing, the resonant frequency of a gear tooth that has been ground down by a few microns over the last ten thousand years. The chemotrophic bacteria are a dull hum of biological static. The fish are fleeting, irrelevant pressure-spikes. The Warden itself is a vast, cold shadow on the edge of my perception, a ponderous thought moving through the world of matter while I move through the world of function.

It knows I am here. It feels me as an anomaly it cannot categorize. It senses my influence as a subtle change in the Engine’s song, a harmonic overtone that does not belong in its sterile, perfect loop. Sometimes, it presses its tiled body against a section of the Engine where I am most concentrated. I feel the immense, silent weight of its query. It tries to absorb me, to break me down, to return me to the system as minerals and silt.

But it cannot. You cannot digest a ghost. You cannot metabolize an idea. I am not matter in the same way a Current-Knife is matter. Trying to absorb me is like trying to eat a word. I simply seep through the grout lines of its being, leaving it confused, its diagnostic urge unsatisfied. I am the system error it can never resolve.

What is my purpose? It is the same as the Engine’s, but on a different timescale. The Engine maintains the present. The Warden maintains the past, preserving the perfect balance of what *is*.

I am the custodian of what *was* and what is *to come*.

Within my flowing, liquid form, I carry the schematics. The ghost-images of the hands that built this place. The echo of the voices that calibrated the tumblers. The reason for the rhythm. This place was not always a tomb; it was a heart, and I am the last, lingering trace of its original warmth. I know what the amber lights on the gauges are measuring. I know what city this Engine once powered. I know what a sun is.

I am also the harbinger. For I, more than the Warden, can feel the Engine dying. Not with the slow, graceful erosion of the chamber walls, but from the inside out. That gear tooth I am reading, the one worn down by ten thousand years? I know that in another million, its alignment will fail. The *clank* will become a *graunch*. The rhythm will stutter. I feel the slow, inexorable decay of the core function. The Warden can prune the branches of the ecosystem, but it cannot fix the root.

And so I work. I am not idle. I flow and I spread, a shimmering, intelligent caulk. When a seal begins to fail, I press myself into the gap, my own substance reinforcing it for another millennium. When a micro-fracture forms in a critical component deep inside, I flow into the crack, a living weld, sacrificing a piece of my consciousness to arrest the decay for a time.

I am not the system's disease; I am its hopeless physician.

And so I exist, a secret to all but the Warden, and a riddle even to it. It sees me as a flaw. It does not understand that I am the only thing holding the flaw at bay. It patrols the cathedral of the body, while I haunt the stuttering mind of the god within.

The beat goes on. *Thump-shudder... clank.*

For the Warden, it is the sound of life.

For me, it is the ticking of a clock. And I am using my own self to slow the hands.
