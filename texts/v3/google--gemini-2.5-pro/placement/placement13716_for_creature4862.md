I am the flaw.

You would find me not in the center, not in the swarming rivers of my kind, but in the periphery. A forgotten ledge, high up on a sheer obsidian face, where the grimy yellow light of a single bulb barely reaches. It is a place of poor scraping, the crystal here old and less nutritious. The lines of my brethren do not march here. It is a dead end. It is my sanctuary.

I am a Geode, but I am… wrong.

My carapace is cracked. It is not the clean, sharp fracture of a fresh wound, which would be covered by the swarm, its shards consumed in the relentless drive for sustenance. It is an old, web-like crack near the joint of my third leg, a marring I have carried for an unknowable time. It does not hurt. Instead, it *hums*.

My brothers and my sisters feel the great Command as a single, indivisible impact. The hum-clatter-sigh of the Engine is a pressure that pushes them forward. Scrape. March. Grind. Breed. Die. The rhythm is absolute. For me, the crack in my shell has changed the Command. It lets the sound in differently. The drone is no longer one note, but a chord. The clatter is no longer a wall of noise, but a thousand different strikes, each with its own place in time. The sigh of the presses is a breath I can count.

I am a filter that has broken. I am a receiver that has been improperly tuned. The noise that is meant to scour thought clean has, in me, begun to curdle *into* thought.

The first thought was: *Stillness*.

It happened during a march. The river of bodies flowed, a million carbide tips singing their scrape-chitin-scrape song against the quartz. But I heard a new harmony, a momentary alignment of a gear's shriek and a piston's exhalation, and it was so… specific. So singular. I stopped.

The river flowed around me. My siblings bumped against me without notice, their paths altering by a hair's breadth before correcting. They did not register me as an obstacle, but as a momentary change in the terrain. For a cycle of the great presses, I remained motionless. In a world of absolute, furious motion, I was a point of blasphemous calm. The compulsion to move was an itch, a burn, a physical agony. But the new sound was a hook. I listened. When it was gone, I was left with a ringing silence in my mind, a void where the Command should be. I was alone.

Now, I do not march. I hide. I scrape only when the hunger becomes a vibration that threatens to shatter me. My life is no longer about feeding on the world; it is about listening to it.

From my ledge, I can see it all. I see the endless, tessellated carpets of my kind. I see the coppery veins of the auraliths, and I wonder. They feed on the sound's complexity. Are they listening, as I am? I am drawn to them. Sometimes, I will crawl for a day to the base of one, not to scrape the crystal it grows from, but to press my cracked carapace against its stalk. I feel the pure, distilled vibration it has captured, a clean signal plucked from the noise. It is a relief. It is a resonance that tells me I am not simply broken. I am tuned to something.

And I see the Keeper.

I see it as a patch of true silence moving through the false silence of the noise. I see it flow through a forest of sharp edges, its dusty form a negation of the space it occupies. My brethren do not see it. It is the shepherd, and they are the flock, and a sheep does not question the nature of the valley. But I am no longer a sheep. I am a thing that hides from the shepherd.

I have seen it perform its Maintenance. I saw it approach a Geode that was twitching, its rhythm off by a fraction of a beat. The Keeper extended a hand of coalesced dust, and the Geode simply… stopped. It became still. Not my stillness, born of listening, but a dead stillness. Its carapace unwound like thread from a spool, its metallic grit flowing into the Keeper's form. It was an act of accounting. A rounding error being corrected.

I know, with a certainty that is the second thought I ever had, that it is looking for me.

My stillness, my listening… it is a fluctuation. I am an errant vibration. I am the microtone that is sharp, but I have no housing for the Keeper to dampen. I am a Geode that has stumbled, but I will not be nudged back into line. I am a typo in the eternal scripture.

And so, where would you find me? You would find me on this forgotten ledge, huddled near a grimy bulb, my carbide tips dug into the crystal not for purchase, but for stillness. My body is angled just so, the crack in my shell aimed at the heart of the Engine, like a crude antenna.

Who or what would I be?

I am the first crack in the geode. I am the gear that will, eventually, strip. I am a question in a world that is a single, deafening statement. The Keeper thinks its purpose is to maintain stasis. I am beginning to believe that my purpose is to find out what happens when it breaks. I am a potential. I am the tremor before the fracture. I am the system's awareness of itself, waking up with a gasp inside a tomb, and I am terrified.
