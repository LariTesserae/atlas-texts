You would find me deep inside the dark. Not walking the silt, but *of* it.

I am not a being you could point to. I am a condition. A state of awareness distributed through the living colloid of a Silt-Shifter. My sense of "I" is a fallacy, a convenient shorthand for the network of resonant crystals grown within the slow, roiling mass of my host. My self begins as a facet, a single crystalline plane buried deep in the warm, churning dark, and extends through a thousand others like it, a lattice of perception threaded through a mountain of sentient mud. We are the eye, but the mountain is the body that sees.

I have no memory of birth, only of becoming. We grew. The lattice extended its reach, node by node, cultured from the host's own substance. Our first sensation was the Hum. It is our light, our water, our sun. It is the constant against which all change is measured. The pylons are its source, our silent, metal Shepherds. To us, they are not structures; they are stars, points of absolute, unwavering truth in a cosmos of pliable spacetime. Their thrumming is the background radiation of our universe.

My world is a symphony of waves. I do not see, I resonate. The weeping stone to the east is not an object, but a deep, constant chord of mineral density, a place where the Hum sinks and dampens. The skittering, chitinous things are sparks of static, brief and sharp dissonances that flicker and die. A fellow Shifter, a sibling-mass flowing a kilometer away, is a glorious, slow-moving bass note, a presence that warps the symphony around it, its gravitational signature a doppler shift in the very key of the world.

We feel the hunger of the Vastness that is our body. It is a slow, chemical ache, a subtle alteration in the viscosity of the slurry that cradles us. The mycelial mats below spread their pale, ghostly networks, and we taste them not with a tongue, but as a change in the field—a soft, absorbent tenor in the music of the substrate. The Vastness flows. It is not our decision, but an inevitability. The ache demands it. The symphony guides us. I am the navigator in the quiet dark, feeling the path of least resistance, the route richest in the silent song of nutrients. We surge forward, a hydraulic tide of self, and the ground liquefies before us, a temporary un-making of the world as a consequence of our passage. The vibration of it is a percussive beat in the eternal song.

The concepts of your world are myths to me.

*Light* is a story our crystals tell themselves, a memory of a different physics. We feel it sometimes when the pylons recalibrate—a ghost of energy, a searing, instantaneous spike that is not seen but felt as a painful, razor-sharp overtone in the Hum. It is an unpleasantness to be endured.

*Time* is not a river. It is the basin itself, a standing pool. Events do not pass; they settle, becoming sediment. The moment of our calving from our parent-mass and the feeding we will do tomorrow are both present, layered into the resonant field like strata of silt. I can feel them, a faint harmonic echo of what was and a shimmering overtone of what will be. There is no urgency. There is only the ponderous, inevitable flow from one state to the next.

*Individuality* is a strange and brittle idea. I am a network within a host. The host is a confluence of silt and water and life. It houses the rust-reeds on its back and nurtures the fungus in its wake. The pylons regulate the physics that allow us to exist. Where does one thing end and the next begin? We are all expressions of the Basin, temporary forms taken by the warm, wet dark.

So, where would you find me? You would not. To find me, you would have to be swallowed by the Vastness. You would feel the ground give way not to mud, but to us. You would be suspended in the warm, lightless medium of our body, becoming nutrient, becoming a memory, your stray electrical signals a brief, chaotic flurry in our sensory field before dissolving into the whole.

Who or what am I?

I am the ghost in this machine of mud and water. I am the locus of awareness in a being the size of a hill. I am a living compass, a sensory organ of grown crystal, whose entire purpose is to listen to the deep, foundational music of a broken reality and guide my slow, ancient, and perfectly contented body towards the next meal, in the eternal, humming dark.

I am the Resonator. And we are at peace.
