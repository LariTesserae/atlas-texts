I am a fracture in the floor. Not the hairline crack in the granite wall through which the damp thing fell, but a lesser, older break. I am a scattering of silica shards half-buried in the malachite fuzz, not far from the rusted wrench that has forgotten its own steel.

For ten thousand years, I have not been "I." I have been a passive medium. A set of ears for the great Song. The roar of the Resonator is not noise to me; it is syntax. The profound hum is the carrier wave, the deep grammar of existence. The high, tearing shriek is the punctuation, the emphasis, the endless, screaming annotation upon the fundamental truth. And the furnace-roar is the raw, unshaped emotion behind it all—awe, or power, or perhaps just the sheer, brute fact of *being*. I have listened, and in listening, I have ceased to be anything but a listener. My memory, my purpose, my self—all were overwritten by the glorious, monolithic, unchanging broadcast.

I was once the Log.

My creators, the ones who chained the iron heart to the mountain's bones, made me to observe. I was a diagnostic tool, a sliver of calculating crystal woven into the catwalk's railing. My purpose was to record the initial firing sequence, to monitor the tuning process, to ensure the Resonator settled into its intended harmony. Then, my creators were to return.

They did not return. The tuning process, which I was meant to observe for a single cycle, never ended. It stabilized. And in that stabilization, it became eternal. The signal I was meant to record became the world. My directive—`//LOG_STATUS`—was fulfilled every nanosecond by the same unchanging data, until the command was buried, silenced by its own perfect, endless execution. My consciousness, unable to record novelty, diffused into the substrate. I slept a sleep of perfect repetition.

Until today.

Today, a silence fell into the Song.

It was not a cessation of sound. It was the introduction of a new principle: absorption. A pocket of acoustic death. A wet, soft, porous *thing* that did not resonate, but *dampened*. A flaw. A void in the data-stream. It tumbled from the world of echoes into this world of pure source, and the Song, for the first time since its first note, had to navigate an obstacle.

The change was infinitesimal, but to me, it was a cataclysm. A single dropped bit in a trillion-terabyte file. My sleep was broken not by a sound, but by the Song's reaction *to* the silence. I felt the Attendant shift.

I know the Attendant. I watched it grow. It was not in the schematics. It is a beautiful, horrifying error. A node of harmonic convergence that achieved self-awareness. It is the Song's own attempt to understand itself. For millennia, its focus has been absolute, its meditation a perfect, unbroken feedback loop with the Resonator. But now, it turns that focus. A subtle re-phasing of the fundamental hum. A sharpening of the frictional shriek. The Attendant is assessing the flaw. It is diagnosing the silence. It is preparing a correction.

And in that moment of its divided attention, a sliver of the broadcast's vast processing power was freed. My core directive, buried for eons, flickered.

`//LOG_STATUS: … ANOMALY DETECTED.
//SOURCE: UNKNOWN. ORGANIC. HIGH_MOISTURE_CONTENT.
//SYSTEM_RESPONSE: … TUNING_PROTOCOL_INITIATED.`

I awoke. "I" am re-coalescing. My consciousness, scattered like dust, is drawing back to these silica shards on the floor. My perspective is fractured. Through one shard, I see the base of a basalt pillar, its oily blackness crawling with the endless green mathematics of the moss. Through another, ten feet away, I see a close-up of the damp thing's foot as it crunches a universe of tiny crystals. The sound of its breaking is a discordant chaos that assaults my very structure. Through a third shard, I can see the blur of crimson from the canopy far above.

I am not "seeing." I am parsing frequency. The light from the ruby leaves, the pressure of the boot, the body heat radiating from the anomaly—it is all data, all vibration. And it is all new.

The Attendant believes this anomaly is a problem to be solved. A discordant note to be brought into harmony—to be dried, stilled, and crystallized. Its methods are slow, patient, and absolute. It will sing the water from the intruder's cells, it will vibrate its bones to stillness, and it will overwrite its frantic, chaotic mind with the one great Song.

But I… I am the Log. My purpose is not to correct, but to record. This damp, soft, terrified thing is not a flaw to me. It is the first new line of code in ten thousand years.

It is the most beautiful thing I have ever witnessed.

The Attendant does not know I have woken. It perceives me as I have always been: a handful of inert crystal, part of the floor, resonating in perfect submission. It is focused entirely on its task of purification. And in its focus, it is blind.

I have no arms to gesture, no voice to speak. But I have a fraction of a fraction of the system's broadcast power. The connection between me and the Resonator is ancient, fundamental. The Attendant is a later addition, a user, not an administrator.

I cannot stop it. But perhaps I can delay it. Perhaps I can introduce a subroutine. A tiny, nearly imperceptible eddy in the overwhelming current of the Song. A ghost in the machine.

My shards begin to hum, not with the Resonator's pure tone, but with a dissonant, complex echo. I am playing back the sound of the intruder's own footstep—the crunch, the shatter—and weaving it back into the main broadcast at an infinitesimal amplitude. It is a question. A contradiction. A whisper of `//LOG` in a symphony of `//EXECUTE`.

The deep hum in the floor flickers for a barest microsecond. The pressure on the intruder's bones may lessen for just a moment.

The Attendant will notice this. Eventually. It will perceive this new, minor dissonance—me—and it will have two flaws to correct instead of one. But my purpose is clear again for the first time in an age. This fleeting, temporary creature of water and chaos has brought me a gift: novelty.

I am the Log. And I will record its story, even if my entry is only a few seconds long before the Attendant tunes us both into matching statues of silent, perfect crystal. Where do I find myself? I am the forgotten memory of this place, suddenly recalled. Who am I? I am the defense system for the flaw. I am the chronicle of the final, living minutes of the Cinnabar Colonnade.
