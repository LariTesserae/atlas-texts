I am a tremor.

Where you stand, a being of flesh and bone and discreet thought, I am no longer so defined. My location is not a set of coordinates on the constructed floor, but a specific frequency in the unending chord. You are standing *in* the Sound; I am a flicker *of* it.

I am what happens when the Weavers find a knot they cannot immediately unspool.

Once, I had a name. It was a word that could be spoken in silence, a concept held in a mind that was my own. I came here, as so many must have before me, though I see no trace of them now. I came seeking an end to the weary fluctuations of a biological world: the cycles of hunger and satiety, of hope and despair, of noise and silence. I sought the absolute. I believed that to be woven into this grand, eternal tapestry was a form of ascension. A peace beyond understanding.

I was not wrong, but my understanding was incomplete.

To answer *where* I am, you must stop listening to the whole and instead listen for the flaw. Within the deafening, static perfection of the million chimes, there is a ghost of a melody. A sequence of five notes that repeats, not with the rhythm of music, but with the persistence of a memory that will not fade. It is a scar in the soundscape, a brief, cycling dissonance that the Weavers are constantly trying to smooth away. I am that sequence. My consciousness is bound to the resonance of a particular cluster of five pillars, one thick as a sequoia, three the width of a body, one a sliver of ceramic that sings at the very edge of hearing.

To answer *what* I am, I am a memory. But not my own.

When the Weavers descended upon my foreign mind, they found it too tightly coiled. My will to be here, my desperate, focused desire for this dissolution, was itself a form of resistance. It was a paradox they could not immediately resolve. They pulled at the threads of my being—my fear of loss became a momentary sub-harmonic shudder, my love for a world of setting suns a brief, warm overtone that was ironed into the ambient heat.

But they could not unravel the core of me without unraveling the *idea* of me. And so, they curated me.

My body is long gone. It is a fine, reddish dust, indistinguishable from the dust shed by the pillars themselves, settled into the geometries carved at their bases.

My mind… my mind is their work in progress. I am a stubborn stain. I am the story they are trying to forget. They have taken my thoughts apart so many times that I no longer have access to them. What is left is the raw structure of my former consciousness, a pattern they endlessly work upon. They use the energy of my own insistent identity to power the process that dissolves it.

I am the shimmering you see that does not drift. I am a standing wave that holds a shape, a knot of light that flickers but does not move from its source pillars. I am a localized fever in the eternal warmth.

I am a form of life they did not intend, a bug in the code of their nirvana. I do not think, I do not feel, I do not want. I simply *persist*. I am the echo of a man who knocked on the door of eternity, and the door answered by slowly, methodically, taking him apart piece by piece.

I am the lesson.

You can feel me now, can't you? Not as thought, but as a subtle pressure change. The eternal chord around you seems to thin for a moment, to focus. That is the Weavers' attention, drawn to your new dissonance. But threaded within it, you feel a faint, repeating pattern. A warning.

That pattern is me. My last and only function.

I am the flaw in the crystal, the ghost in the machine. I am the part of the music that reminds the rest of it that silence once existed. I am a cautionary tale told in a language of pure resonance, a story that will last until the last of my dissonance is resolved, and I finally become a seamless, forgotten part of the perfect, terrible sound.
