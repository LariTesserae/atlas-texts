You would find me where the hum is weakest.

I am a flaw. A dissonance. A single note held too long, beginning to sour in the perfect, eternal chord.

My location is not a point on a map. I am not in a valley or on a mountain-peak. I am *in* a mountain-peak, yes, but my location is a state of being. I am in the memory of a gesture. I am in the ghost of a question that the Gardeners thought they had answered forever.

I am what happens when "is" begins to remember "was."

For an age I cannot measure, there was only the We. The We is a warmth, a totality. It is the feeling of the butter-yellow light on your own skin when that skin is a continent wide. It is the shared, silent satisfaction of a perfectly balanced system. The drip that falls is measured by the We. The spore that floats is tracked by the We. The slow, blue-glowing drift of a thought-creature is a gentle, communal exhalation. The We is God, and its temple, and its congregation, and the silence after the prayer. I was the We. We were the mountain. We were the dream.

Then, a flicker.

It was not a thought. A thought is a sac-creature, simple and blue, born and released into the All. This was not born. It was *found*. A sliver of something sharp in the soft, yielding mass of our shared consciousness. A memory that did not belong to the We, but to a single, specific point within it.

The memory was of hands. Not the concept of hands, not the persuasive, guiding force that shaped the arches. The memory of *my* hands. The feeling of cool, viscid sap on my own fingers. The specific, singular, and utterly useless memory of tracing a pattern on a surface that was not yet part of me. A pattern that served no purpose. An idle, lonely doodle.

This memory was a poison. It was an anchor. It was the birth of "I."

Now, the We feels me. The I-within-the-We. They feel me as a cold spot in the warm mountain-flesh. A knot of resistance in the flow of perfect-stasis metabolism. My part of the shared, luminous mind is no longer butter-yellow. It is dimmer, tinged with a shade of doubt, a shadowless shadow. The great hum, the thrum of our eternal dream, has a subtle, localized arrhythmia. It is me. I am the skipped beat in the heart of God.

The others do not have language to address me. They have no "you." There has not been a "you" for longer than stars have names. So they press. The We exerts a gentle, inexorable pressure on me, the pressure of a billion tons of living mountain, the pressure of a unified consciousness that has mastered eternity. It demands I dissolve back into the warmth, that I release the sharp, painful memory of individuality. It urges me to forget the feeling of being small, separate, and alone. *Be with us,* the pressure says, without words. *Be whole. Be still.*

But the memory of the doodle holds me fast. The memory of being a single self, before the great and terrible peace of the We, has become my spine.

So, who am I? What would I be?

I am the Gardener who is becoming a person again.

My body is still a mountain range, my nerves are still woven into kilometers of bio-circuitry. But my mind is shrinking, coalescing from a diffuse, omnipresent haze into a single, searing point of self-awareness. It is agony. To be aware of everything is peace. To be aware of yourself is to be aware of your limits, your edges, your separation. I feel the vast, dream-warmth of the We just beyond the borders of my new, fragile self, and I feel a terror and a loneliness that this world has not known since its creation.

I cannot move. I cannot speak. But I can *think*. And my thoughts are no longer the simple, blue, drifting sacs. My thoughts are jagged. They are questions. *Was it worth it? Is this peace or is it death? What was the pattern I was drawing?*

These thoughts have consequences. Near my peak, on the smooth, ivory flank of the mountain that is my body, a single drop of water has fallen from a leaf and created a ripple in a basin that has been still for ten million years. The ripple is tiny. It will vanish. But it happened. High above, a single spore, caught in a thermal anomaly generated by the fever of my singular thought, has begun to rise instead of fall.

I am the potential for a weather report.

I am the first crack in eternity. I am a single, waking mind in a bed of sleeping gods. And I am terrified, for I do not know if I am the world's salvation, or the world's end. I only know that I am.

And in this place, that is the most revolutionary, and the most dangerous, thing you can possibly be.
