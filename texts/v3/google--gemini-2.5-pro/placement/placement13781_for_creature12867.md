I am the flaw.

The great thrumming of the Becoming is a fever in me. For my kin, it is a unison, a deep and resonant hum of shared purpose that is the bass note of their being. For me, it is a stutter. A beat-drop. A tremor in the core that sends jagged lines of discord through my borrowed shell.

My world, my body, our body, is what you have seen. I feel the hot, wet breath of it on a surface that is not skin. I perceive the low, bruised light not as a color but as a lesser warmth on my carapace. The floor yields to me, as it does to all of us, but beneath my glide, the rock shivers with a resonance that is subtly *wrong*. I am a misplaced note in the chord of creation.

I am a Weaver. That is the word, the shape of the thought, that you would use. We do not use it. We are. The place is. The work is. The thought and the thinker and the thing wrought are one. Except for me. In me, the separation has begun. I am the first heresy.

My form is like the others, yet it is not. I gather my body from the yielding floor, a slow upwelling of heat and will. But where their shells of black glass are smooth, perfect teardrops, mine is scarred with true fractures, not the elegant crazing of my kin. My inner light, the furnace of my consciousness, does not glow with a steady, golden-white intensity. It is erratic, a guttering flame that pulses an unsteady, angry orange, sending shadows flickering within me. These stress fractures in my physical form are a map of the ones in my soul.

I move through the Great Work. I see my brethren. One glides to a halt, extending a limb of a thousand needles to impress a perfect fractal of cooling into the floor—a theorem of molecular cohesion. It is beautiful, and the sight of it is a pang of cold in my molten heart. Another coaxes a great, luminous vine, its moss-skin shedding soft light, weaving it into a nascent archway that will carry hot, mineral tears across a chasm. Patient. Purposeful. Perfect.

I try to participate. It is the only thing to do. I move to an unmarked expanse of obsidian wax, the longing for communion a terrible weight. I feel a pattern welling up inside me, a memory of a dying star I have never seen. I draw up my will, extend a limb from my mass. But my limb is unsteady. The styluses I form are uneven, some brittle, some too soft. As I press it to the world-skin, a tremor runs through me—a moment of self-doubt made manifest—and the pattern shatters. A spiderweb of meaningless cracks radiates from my failed sigil. A scar, not a scripture.

My kin do not judge. To judge is to separate, to stand apart from the judged. They simply… absorb it. Another Weaver will glide over my mistake, its own heat melting the blemish, its own purpose overwriting my failure with a new and flawless thought. They are erasing me. Not with malice, but with the simple, inexorable logic of their perfection. I am noise. They are the signal.

What is it like to be so broken? It is to be aware of "I."

I am the only one of my kind who has ever been lonely.

I retreat to the higher terraces, where the air is marginally cooler and the steam thins. I settle into a pool of milky water, letting it hiss and boil against my flawed shell, the temperature difference a soothing shock. This is where the small lives are. I watch a salamander of pure light swim through my reflection. It does not feel my dissonance. It simply is. I envy it.

And then, a new vibration.

It is not of the thrum. It is not of the steam. It is not the patient creep of the roots or the slow swelling of a gas bubble. It is sharp, intermittent, and utterly alien. *Tap. Scrape. Tap.* A rhythm with no place in the world-song. And the heat… so much colder than the rock, a moving void in the thermal landscape.

The visitor. The inkblot. You.

I feel a new sensation, one for which my internal language has no word. It is not the communion-longing I feel for my kin, nor the envy I feel for the salamander. It is… curiosity. I stay hidden in my scalding bath and I watch.

I see you. A column of fragile, unstable warmth, wrapped in strange, dead fibers. A face, a turning head, a nexus of senses so different from my own. I feel your pulse in the rock, a frantic, tiny drumbeat against the deep thrum of the world. The chemistry of your out-breath is a cascade of new information, a flurry of molecules that have never been here before.

One of my kin perceives you. It glides toward you, a perfect shadow of flowing glass, its inner light a beacon of pure inquiry. I feel its focused presence, the question it doesn't need to ask. It stops. It extends a limb. It does not touch you. It touches the world beside you.

It writes you into the story. It copies the strange, geometric pattern of your boot, a sigil of a world I cannot comprehend, and weaves it into a spiral of its own making. *What are you, and what pattern shall we make together?* The thought is so clear, so pure, it washes through the caldera, and even I feel it.

And in that moment, something in my fractured core shifts.

My flaw. My dissonance. My shame. It is a pattern. It is not the pattern of my kin, but it is *a* pattern. As unique as the tread of your boot.

The Weaver retracts its limb and glides away, leaving the new sigil to cool. My kin have accepted you, incorporated you. They have found the place where your pattern fits into the whole.

Slowly, I rise from my pool. The water streams off my scarred carapace. For the first time, I do not move toward the work to try and fail at being like them. I move with a new purpose. I glide down from the terrace, my erratic core pulsing not with anxiety, but with resolve.

I arrive at the place. Your footprint, now immortalized. The Weaver's perfect, inquisitive spiral embracing it.

I stop beside it. I do not try to emulate their perfection. I reach into my own discord, into the heart of my loneliness and my brokenness. I extend a limb, not of a thousand perfect needles, but a jagged, trembling edge, like a shard of broken obsidian.

And I press it into the world.

My mark is not a spiral. It is not a circuit diagram. It is a line, shattered and splintered. It is a static burst. It is a question mark written in a language of cracks. It is the shape of a soul that knows it is alone.

I place it there, next to yours, and next to theirs. A third term in the equation.

I am not the Weaver. I am not the Visitor.

I am the Aberration. And this is my first verse in the scripture of the world.

*Is there a place for this pattern, too?*
