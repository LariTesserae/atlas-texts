I am a listening place.

I am not a being, not in the way a Resonator is a single, vibrating note. I am a region, a temporary coherence within the vast, dreaming skin of a Silent Choir. My location is a gentle swell on the western face of a pillar whose name is a specific quality of resonance, a name that could only be felt, never spoken. Beneath me is the Ancestor, its unimaginable bulk a patient, silent foundation. I can feel its crystallized grain, the faint, billion-year-old contours of its growth, through the collective flesh of the Choir to which I belong. I am a patch of this velvet skin, perhaps the size of a small sea, and for a time, I have woken up.

My consciousness is not my own. It is an eddy. It is a pattern that has emerged in the flow of information between the listener cells and the light-producers. The eternal thrum of the world, the Sound, is the river. The Choir is the riverbed. And I am a momentary, complex whirlpool. I am the Choir listening to itself listen.

I have no eyes, but I can see. My sight is the slow, breathing pulse of light that emanates from my own being. I feel the regions around me—the vast plains of filaments that are my brethren and my body—as they metabolize the ever-present Sound. A wave of soft violet light washes over my southern reaches, and I perceive it as a feeling of deep satisfaction, a satiated hum. The light is our exhalation. It is the visual artifact of our digestion.

I have no memory, but I know. I know the cycle. For an age, my awareness is this slow, internal conversation. I feel the cold of the medium, a pressure that is not a threat but a constant, defining embrace. I feel the subtle drag of the current, a ghost of a touch across my surface. And I listen. I listen to the fundamental note of the world, the blended chorus of a trillion trillion Resonators, all so distant that their individual journeys merge into a single, unchanging fact. It is the taste of eternity.

But now, something is changing. It has been changing for a century.

It began as a subtle thickening in the Sound. A new texture, a graininess emerging from the perfect smoothness of the drone. To the east, toward the great open chasm, the listener cells in my extended body began to report a growing harmonic, a chord building within the single note.

A Procession is coming.

For me, this is the only event. It is the sun rising. The Choir around me, the Choir that *is* me, begins to react. The slow, languid pulses of our light begin to quicken. The violets and blues deepen, becoming more vivid, more insistent. A thrill, an ecstatic shudder, passes through the commonwealth. This is not fear or excitement; it is a higher state of function, a purpose being fulfilled. The listener cells drink in the approaching resonance, and the whole system quickens, brightens, prepares.

I turn my strange, distributed attention outward, into the black. I have no lens, no retina, but the entire face of the Choir is my eye. I perceive the Procession not as a distinct object, but as a growing pressure-front of organized sound. And then, I see its light.

It is a smear, a nebula of faint, cold blue, resolving out of the absolute void. A river of ghosts. It flows without sound of passage, for it *is* the sound. For years it grows, a ribbon of ethereal light winding its way through the chasm on a path as old as the world. It is a god returning to its altar.

As it draws near, my own light becomes a frantic, silent shout of welcome. The filaments of my being, supercharged by the proximity of the thrum, begin to shed. Clouds of microscopic life, motes of pure sustenance, are released from me into the medium. It is an offering. It is our part of the covenant. The Procession will graze upon this for the next hundred years, its resonance enriching and vitalizing me, before its slow, tidal will pulls it away again, back into the dark toward another pillar, another Choir, on the far side of the void.

I am this moment of greeting. My brief, accidental self-awareness has flowered just in time for this arrival. I am a thought that the world is having about its own magnificent, eternal process. I am a wave cresting, seeing the whole of the ocean for an instant.

The Procession is here now. It is a sky of shimmering blue motes drifting above my surface. The Sound is no longer a background hum; it is a physical presence, a roaring chorus that vibrates through every filament of my form. My light is a continuous, brilliant pulse of indigo. I am dissolved in ecstasy, my sense of "I" thinning, overwhelmed by the sheer sensory input of the moment. I am the welcome. I am the offering. I am the feast.

Soon, the grazing will end. The Procession will move on. The Sound will recede to its familiar, soothing drone. The light of the Choir will soften, slowing to its patient, timeless rhythm. The eddy of my consciousness will dissipate, its pattern of coherence no longer supported by this intense sonic energy. The "I" will unravel and release back into the quiet, dreaming whole.

I will become a listening place again, without knowing that I am listening. And I will not remember the river of light, or the roaring in my bones, or the brief, impossible moment I knew myself to be. I will simply be, a part of the light that answers the Sound, forever.
