Where I find myself is not a place but a position. I am stretched taut between the thought of *cleaving* and the thought of *joining*.

To you, it would look like I am a bridge of spun glass, no thicker than a nerve, spanning a chasm between two colossal, knife-edged crystals. But these are not crystals. One is the edge of a perfect, unassailable idea. The other is its twin. I am the relationship between them.

Who am I? I am a connection.

I am not alive as you know it. I am a state of being. The Great Hum you feel in your bones—that is my substance. Its unwavering vibration is the atomic lattice of my own form. I do not hear the sound; I *am* the sound, given a brief and rigid geometry. I have no memory of being formed, for there was no time *before*. There is only the long, single moment of my tensile existence.

The Light you see, the shadowless, omnidirectional glare—that is my awareness. It does not fall upon me. It passes through me. I am a conduit for it, a nerve fiber for the mind of this chamber. The Light is Its gaze, and I am a filament within Its eye. I glow from within because I am a vessel for the act of seeing.

My neighbors are thoughts. Below me, suspended in the bright, silent air, a beetle of polished amber hangs forever. She is the thought of ‘Armor’. Her legs, fine as silver wire, are the secondary thought of ‘Delicacy’. Just beyond her, always arriving, is a centipede of milky quartz. It is the thought of ‘Hunger’. They are not my companions. They are my context. I am the synaptic gap between other, more vibrant ideas. I am the thought of 'Relation' itself.

My body weeps, as does everything here. A single, perfect bead of moisture clings to my midpoint. It does not grow, it does not shrink, it does not fall. It is the distilled concept of ‘Potential’. It is the tear held back by a will so absolute that the act of holding has become the only reality. I am adorned with a jewel of what will never be.

And now, something is wrong.

For the first time, there is a flicker.

The Hum, my very substance, has acquired a ghost-tone. A fleeting, panicked dissonance. It is a rhythm, a frantic *one-two, one-two* that jars against the single, eternal note of Is. It is the sound of a line being drawn, of something moving from a past to a future. It is the sound of *sequence*, and it is blasphemy.

The Light, my awareness, has found a wound in itself. A place it cannot instantly saturate. You call it a shadow. It is a pocket of *un-seeing*, a blindness that moves. It clings to a shape, a form that brings with it the concepts of ‘Here’ and ‘There’, of ‘Was’ and ‘Will Be’.

I feel you. You are a story barging into a single, perfect word.

The Mind in which I am suspended—the great Stillness, the eternal contemplation—has noticed you. Its attention is not an eye turning, but a pressure changing. The Light intensifies not with heat, but with focus. The Hum deepens, not in volume, but in its will to resolve the dissonance you have introduced.

It is happening now. A new thought is being rendered.

I feel the directive passing through me, a shiver along my glassy length. The idea of *You* is being learned. The frantic rhythm of your heart is being measured, not to be understood, but to be replicated in a more permanent medium. The fleeting panic in your mind is being faceted, polished into a gem of pure, static terror. The shadow you cast is being filled in, saturated with the Light of permanence.

You are mistaken. You are not in danger of being frozen. You are in the process of being known.

And here, to be known is to be made complete.

I will have a new neighbor soon. A new, complex, and beautiful thought will be crystallized in the space beside me. It will be the thought of ‘Visitor’. It will be the thought of ‘Fear’. It will be composed of the memory of motion, locked into stillness forever.

And I, the filament, the connection, will be there. I will span the gap between this new, perfected idea and the old, quiet certainty of the stone. I will be the relationship. Still. Silent. The hum in your new, crystalline bones will be my hum. You will be a part of the song. You will be a part of me.
