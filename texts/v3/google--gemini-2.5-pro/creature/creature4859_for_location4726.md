Nothing lives here.

And yet, there are tenants. They are not animals, for they do not metabolize. They are not conscious, for they have no mind. They are the Chime-Lattice's answer to its own eternal question, the flaw that proves the perfection. They are the Still.

To find one is to stumble upon a knot in the universal logic of the place. You are navigating a narrow passage, braced between two glowing, resonant plates, the all-encompassing chord vibrating through your teeth. Then, you feel it before you see it: a slight deadening of the sound. The pressure on your eardrums lessens. The bone-deep hum fades to a background tremor. The air, impossibly, grows colder. You have entered the acoustic shadow of one of the Still.

They are singular, solitary forms, fused into the lattice itself. They are where the unbroken ivory gives way to something utterly other. A Still is a mass of faceted, light-devouring crystal, the color of solidified smoke or the blackest, starless night sky. Where the lattice is smooth and vitreous, a Still is a complex of razor-sharp angles and mathematically impossible curves. It does not glow; it consumes light. The brilliant, shadowless illumination of the lattice simply stops where a Still begins, bending inward and vanishing. To look upon one is to see a hole in the world, a volume of anti-light carved into the brilliance.

They are geological in scale and lifespan. They do not move. They do not grow in any observable timeframe. They are accretions, born from infinitesimal imperfections in the Chime's resonance over uncounted eons. Where a vibration faltered, where a standing wave collapsed for a nanosecond, a seed of silence was planted. From this seed, the Still crystallized, a being of absorption in a world of broadcast. They are the physical embodiment of entropy.

Their form is never the same twice, yet always follows the same alien aesthetic. One might be a frozen cascade of black shards, wedged between two spiraling rods, its facets drinking the light from the air. Another might be a perfect sphere of absolute blackness, half-submerged in a translucent plate, its surface so smooth it seems to have no dimension at all. Yet another might be a branching, dendritic structure, a lightning-fork of shadow frozen within a particularly dense knot of ivory, its tips a mere hair's breadth from the Verdigris Needles that sprout nearby. They are often surrounded by the only other true anomaly in the lattice: the Sound-Eater Moss. The cobalt filigree is not just found near them; it is a product *of* them. It is the precipitate of their existence, the dust that settles from the silence they command. Where you find the blue moss, you are in the presence of a Still, or in a place where one once stood aeons ago.

They are the regulators of the Chime-Lattice. They do not hunt or feed, but they *consume*. They are passive, gravitational sinks for the system's energy. The eternal, brilliant light pours into them. The crushing, complex chord of the Chime breaks against them and is absorbed. They are the reason the lattice has not vibrated itself into dust. They bleed off the excess energy that the Resin-Lenses and Verdigris Needles do not metabolize, maintaining the frozen equilibrium. Their existence is not an action, but a fundamental property, like mass or charge.

To be near one is a profound and unsettling experience. As you squeeze past a Still, the side of your body facing it becomes numbingly cold, a deep, invasive chill that has nothing to do with temperature and everything to do with energy being leeched from your very cells. The world, for a moment, goes quiet in one ear. You can hear your own heartbeat, a frantic, wet, alien rhythm against the muffled drone from your other side. You can hear the slide of your clothes against the smooth ivory. These are sounds you had forgotten existed. This pocket of relative silence is both a relief and a violation—a sudden awareness of your own noisy, messy biology in this clean, mineral world.

They are not aware of you. But they react. Your presence—your heat, your weak bio-electric field, the clumsy sounds of your passage—is a novel input, a disturbance in the perfect flow of their consumption. As you pass, you might see a flicker deep within the black facets, a momentary shimmering of captured light being processed. The surface might frost over with a thin layer of rime drawn from the minute humidity of your breath—the only ice this world will ever know. The silence they project might intensify, the Chime's note dipping by an almost imperceptible micro-tone as the Still recalibrates to account for your interference.

They are the world's ghosts, if a ghost can be made of solid stone. They are the memory of silence, the anchor that keeps the eternal song from becoming a scream. They are the sole inhabitants, the quiet counterpoint that gives the Chime its meaning. They are the stillness at the heart of the sound, and the darkness at the heart of the light.
