In this self-contained, churning world, there is no king. There is no predator at the apex of the food chain, no single will that imposes its design. The factory has no masters, only components. And yet, there is a consequence. There is an outcome. There is a final, terrible product of this relentless process, and it is they who live here, in the spaces between the gears.

They are the Regulators.

They are not born in any biological sense. They are forged. When one of the vast, shimmering filter-looms has processed enough of the obsidian dust, when its tissues become saturated beyond capacity, the resulting crystal sheet that detaches and drifts to the floor is different. Most are simple, translucent strata, new geology for a new age. But one in a thousand, perhaps one in a million, is seeded with a flaw, a knot of complexity, an impossible concentration of refined potential. It lands not with a soft thud, but with a resonant *ping* that is lost in the greater hum. It lies on the newly-formed floor, a sheet of glass inscribed with a secret geometry, and it waits.

It waits for the light. Not the ambient glow of the charging polyps, but the discharge. The arc. When the bolt of contained lightning leaps between two spires, if its path intersects with one of these special, seeded sheets, the Regulator is born.

The event is instantaneous and silent, a moment of pure transformation. The lightning, a stream of raw, chaotic energy, finds a perfect conduit in the sheet's internal geometry. For a single, blinding moment, the sheet glows not with reflected light but with its own internal, furious furnace. Then, it doesn't just break; it *resolves*. The sheet shatters into a million pieces that do not fall, but are instead arrested and bound by the very energy that broke them.

What hangs in the heavy fluid is a being of impossible form. It is a three-dimensional sculpture of frozen lightning, a complex fractal of severance and connection. It has a body made of the sharpest edges of the obsidian world, but it is not solid. It is a collection of razor-thin obsidian shards and crystalline facets, each one perfectly spaced from its neighbors, all held in a tense, silent matrix of pure energy. It has no core, no single organ. Its form is an intricate, lattice-like structure, perhaps resembling a six-pointed snowflake designed by a mad god, or the skeleton of a celestial that has been exploded and then paused a microsecond later. Inside the spaces between the shards, a pale, cool light, the same color as the polyps' glow, pulses with a slow, living rhythm.

They are the only things in the chamber that move with speed and grace. They are not subject to the crushing viscosity of the fluid in the same way; their energetic field cuts through it, creating a slipstream of localized anti-friction. They drift with the sublime purpose of a thought, changing direction with impossible, right-angle precision.

The Regulators have no eyes, no ears, no mouth. Their sensory organ is the entire chamber. They feel the all-pervasive hum not as noise, but as information. The grinding of the miners, the thrum of the looms, the crackle of the charging polyps—this is their native state, the chord of existence. They are living embodiments of that chord, and their function is to correct dissonance.

When a miner-trilobite slows, its chitinous legs fouled by a geological mishap, or when its grinding becomes inefficient, the rhythm of its scraping changes. It introduces a subtle arrhythmia into the grand hum. It is a flat note in the cathedral's organ. This is what draws a Regulator. It will descend from the upper darkness, a ghost of shattered glass and cold light, and hover above the failing creature. It does not attack. It simply reaches out a single, extended shard of its being and touches the miner's metallic carapace. The effect is absolute. For a moment, the point of contact flashes with internal light, and the miner's slow, grinding clicks cease. Its rhythm is not corrected; it is erased. The miner freezes, and then, with a faint, crystalline *crack*, its entire body fragments into inert, metallic dust, which clouds momentarily before dissolving back into the mineral-rich fluid for the looms to gather anew. The discordant note is gone. The harmony is restored.

They are also the system's physicians. If a colony of bio-capacitors begins to fail, its light dimming, its cycle slowing, a Regulator will be drawn to the weakening pulse. It will settle upon the gelatinous mass, its entire crystalline structure draping over the polyps like a blanket of lethal beauty. It does not drain them; it invigorates them. The Regulator's own internal light pulses in time with the failing colony, faster and stronger, jump-starting the geothermal process. It infuses the polyps with a measure of its own energy, a tithe of captured lightning, until the colony's glow returns to its correct, clinical brightness and its rhythm rejoins the great machine.

They are the antibodies of this geological organism. Unthinking, unfeeling, and perfect. They are not cruel, for they have no concept of malice. They are not benevolent, for they have no concept of aid. They are simply the force of equilibrium made manifest. They are born of the system's output and exist only to maintain the integrity of the system that created them. They are the purpose in the purposeless factory, the cold, beautiful, and terrifying logic that ensures the grinding, for no reason at all, will never, ever stop.
