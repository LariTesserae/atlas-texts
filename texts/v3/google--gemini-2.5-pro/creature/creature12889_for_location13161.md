The first error is to look for a creature. To scan the vast, woven plains for something that walks, or crawls, or flies. The mind, conditioned by ecosystems of competition and consumption, seeks an actor on the stage. But here, the stage itself is the only actor. The description is correct: there is no fauna. There is no life *other* than the world-organism.

And yet, it is not uninhabited. What lives here are not creatures, but living thoughts. They are the immune system of the machine-god, the transient, mobile expressions of its overarching design. They are the world's attention made manifest.

To see one is to misunderstand it at first. From a hollow in the weave, a place where the resonant groan of growth dips into a lower register, something begins to coalesce. It is not born; it is assembled. A thousand of the thinnest, newest, pearlescent fibers, the same kind you see extruding from the walls, cease their random questing. They turn inward. They knit themselves together with impossible speed, not into the tight, static geometry of the world-structure, but into a different pattern: a temporary, dynamic form.

What emerges is a being of pure function, a knot that has learned to move. It has no head, no mouth, no limbs in any recognizable sense. It might be a spindly, multi-jointed construction of hardened fiber, like a living caliper the size of a man. It might be a dense, ovoid shape that rolls and shifts its weight, its surface a shimmering, constantly re-weaving pattern of fused threads. Or it might be a flowing ribbon of semi-liquid fiber, a thing of horrifying grace that pours itself across the landscape.

Their form is their purpose. They are never the same, because they are materialized solutions to specific problems.

They do not walk upon the weave. They move *through* it. With a subtle shift in the resonant frequency—a change in the song that only they can author—the dense fibers of the ground directly beneath them momentarily loosen. The being sinks into the substrate, vanishing completely, and the world heals over it without a seam. It travels within the arterial pathways of the world-fluid, carried on the screaming hydraulic currents, navigating by pressure changes and the chemical scent of information. Miles away, where a flaw has been detected, the surface will soften and it will rise again, slick with the world's clear blood, ready to work.

Their senses are not our senses. They do not have eyes; the dim, grey-green light is irrelevant to them. They do not have ears; the deafening roar is not a sound to be heard, but the medium in which they exist, their native state of being. They perceive the world as a single, continuous, chordal symphony. Their consciousness is an act of perfect listening. They feel the immense structure not as a place, but as a body—*their* body. A new fiber growing at a fractionally incorrect angle is not a visual error; it is a dissonant note in the chorus. A blockage in a fluid conduit is a pressure, a sickness, a dull ache in the planet's song.

These are the flaws they exist to correct.

You might see one—that living, spindly caliper—gliding silently over a vast, flat expanse. It moves with the eerie smoothness of a magnetic crane. It is heading for a place that, to your eye, is indistinguishable from any other. It stops. It unfolds its needle-like appendages and gently, precisely, touches the surface. A fiber deep within the weave has failed to fuse correctly. It is creating a harmonic flutter, an infinitesimal weakness that, over millennia, could compromise a continent-sized arch.

The being does not tear or break. It applies pressure, but it also applies a localized change in the song. It hums a new note, a focused frequency that encourages the two errant fibers to re-soften, to quest for each other again. With a touch as delicate as a surgeon's, it guides them. There is a faint *snap*, a percussive feeling that travels up your boots. The dissonant flutter in the world's song resolves into a clean, pure tone. The flaw is corrected.

Its work done, the being simply... stops. Its internal cohesion loosens. The intricate, temporary knot of its body unravels. The pearl-white fibers that formed it now release their hold on each other and fuse, one by one, into the static weave of the world around them. In moments, it is gone. It has not died. It has returned to the whole. It was never a separate life, merely a localized concentration of the world's will. It was a tool that, its task complete, has been put away by melting back into the toolbox.

They are the Resonants. They are the Tenders. They are the antibodies of the great machine. They are not life *in* this place; they are the place's life made briefly, terribly, and beautifully visible. They are the song singing itself.
