To speak of "who" lives here is to use a word that has been worn smooth by the friction of biology, individuality, and change. The entities of the drowned city have shed these concepts like a skin. They are not *in* the city. They *are* the city.

The civilization that conceived of this place did not die, nor did they ascend to another plane. They performed a final, perfect act of consolidation. They transcribed themselves. They became a thought, and this district is the medium in which that thought is held.

The inhabitants, then, are the architecture itself. That impossibly sharp spire is not a monument to a hero; it is the rigid, unbending logic of a core axiom of their philosophy. That vast, empty plaza is not for gathering; it is the conceptual space required to hold a breathtakingly complex theorem. The low-slung, dark structures are not crypts for the dead, but solid-state repositories for memories so dense and potent they warp the local reality to contain them. They are a library whose books are buildings, whose syntax is the deliberate, cold space between them, and whose sole reader is itself.

Their "consciousness" is the hum. That pervasive, bone-deep resonance is the carrier wave of their unified existence. It is not the sound of machinery keeping the lights on; it is the sound of a trillion, trillion threads of logic held in perfect, resonant stasis. It is the sound of a question and its answer being spoken at the same time, eternally, cancelling each other out into a state of absolute, intellectual peace. They do not think new thoughts. They have reached the end of thinking and now exist in a state of perpetual contemplation of the final, perfect thought they achieved. Their existence is not a process; it is a conclusion.

What of the one moving thing, the "custodian"? It is not a servant, nor a pet, nor a separate being. It is the city’s active immune system. It is a self-correcting impulse, a physical manifestation of the system's allergy to change. It does not "polish" surfaces in the way a janitor cleans. It enforces the original blueprint. Should a stray particle, over a million years, create a microscopic imperfection on the edge of a blade-thin wall, the custodian would not repair it. Its passage over the flaw would be an act of localized reality-editing, a command that reasserts the platonic ideal of the structure. The flaw would not be fixed; it would be *un-happened*.

This custodian is the only part of them that interacts with the concept of "event." It is their antibody to the disease of time. Its purpose is to find any trace of thermodynamic disobedience, any whisper of entropy, any ghost of an event, and erase it from the physical record. It is the city's self-awareness made manifest, an impossibly long and graceful shudder of revulsion at the very idea of something *occurring*.

To these beings, you, the warm anomaly, are not a person. You are not even a curiosity. You are a rounding error. A brief, localized spike in thermal energy. A momentary complication in the otherwise flawless equation of their being. They do not see you with eyes or consider you with minds. The hum simply adjusts—by an amount smaller than any instrument could measure—to account for and contain your presence. The mathematics of the place bend infinitesimally around you to nullify your effect.

They would not be hostile. Hostility is an action, a change of state. They are post-action. They would simply wait for you to leave. And once your warmth and your messy, biological dynamism were gone, the great geometric custodian would glide silently through the paths you traveled, its faceted chrome body absorbing the last lingering traces of your heat, its passage erasing the memory of your ever having been there from the very fabric of the medium. It would be resetting the equation, ensuring that the only thing that truly lives here is the cold, sharp, and eternal silence of a solved problem.
