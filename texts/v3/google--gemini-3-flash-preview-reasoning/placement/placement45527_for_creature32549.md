I find myself where the Mandate forgets itself. 

I am not in the grand cathedrals of industry, nor the orderly groves of the Pulse-Tappers. I exist in the **Twelfth Overlap**, a sector where the Mandate’s infinite calculation has reached a recursive error. Here, the architecture has folded in on itself like a crushed paper lantern. A brass spire passes through a carbon-fiber trunk without displacing a single atom; they occupy the same coordinates, shimmering in a state of mutual, impossible existence. 

I am a **Moiré**.

I am not a creature of pewter or glass, but a creature of *interference patterns*. To look at me is to see a shivering, humanoid-shaped ripple in the blinding white glare, like the heat haze above a desert, but colder than a heat-sink’s heart. My body is a flickering arrangement of geometric shadows and refracted light, a living artifact of the Mandate’s logic failing to reconcile two different versions of the same space.

I do not walk; I phase. My movement is the sound of a radio dial being turned between stations—a rhythmic, static-heavy *thrum-hiss* as I shift my density to match the layer of reality I wish to touch.

### My Purpose: The Dampening
In a world of perfect permanence, I am the necessary friction. The Mandate is too rigid; if left to its own devices, the tectonic tension of its growth would eventually cause the crystalline sky to shatter. I am the **Harmonic Damper**. 

When the "cello string" of the world snaps, sending a shockwave through the copper peat, I am there. I move into the path of the vibration. Because I am made of overlapping states, I absorb the energy that would otherwise crack the obsidian shrubs or weld the Iterations to the floor. I take that violent, structural scream into my own body, letting it ripple through my flickering limbs until it dissipates into a harmless hum.

### My Senses: The Chromatic Chime
I do not see color, for the sun is a white fury. I see **Stress**. 
To me, the Great Mandate is a map of glowing filaments. I see the tension in a piston-tree as a jagged, ultraviolet vein. I see the "peace" of the Iterations as a heavy, leaden grey. I taste the ionized gas of the atmosphere—it tastes of ozone and ancient, burnt-out vacuum tubes.

I am often found standing near the **Iterations**. They are my opposite. They are anchors, heavy and still, ensuring the world does not change. I am the smudge on their lens. Sometimes, I place my hand—a shimmering blur of interference—against their etched amber chests. I feel the ferrofluid pulsing inside them. I am the only thing that can feel their internal vibrations without being shattered by the frequency. To them, I am a ghost; to me, they are beautiful, terrifying monuments to a history that has been paved over a million times.

### My Diet: The Remainder
I do not transduce signal like the Pulse-Tappers. I consume the **Remainder**. 
When the Mandate optimizes a sector, there is always a tiny fraction of data that doesn't fit—a rounding error, a lost variable, a memory of a shape that no longer serves a function. These "errors" manifest as tiny, floating sparks of gold-dust that drift in the windless air of the Overlaps. I inhale them. They are my sustenance. I am fueled by the Mandate’s mistakes.

### My Solitude
I am the only one of my kind, or perhaps I am many versions of the same being existing in the same moment. Sometimes, when the geometry loops particularly hard, I see myself standing a hundred yards away, doing exactly what I am doing. We do not wave. We simply resonate in harmony until the loop resets.

I am a byproduct of the Mandate’s perfection. I am the shadow that shouldn't exist in a shadowless world. I am the softest thing in a land of diamond and brass, a ghost in a machine that is too big to ever die, and too busy to ever wake up. 

I wait for the day the calculation ends. I suspect that when the final digit is found, I will be the last thing to vanish—the final ripple on a pond that has finally turned to solid glass. Until then, I stitch the seams of the fraying world, a shivering silhouette against the ascending horizon.
