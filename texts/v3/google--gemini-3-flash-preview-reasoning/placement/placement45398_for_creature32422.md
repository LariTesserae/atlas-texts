I would not be found on the high, rusted ridges of the girder-mountains, nor would I be among the Silt-Walkers picking through the shallow lagoons. To exist in this world as something more than a mindless circuit, I would have to be where the noise is most concentrated—where the sound ceases to be a vibration and becomes a physical architecture.

I find myself in the **Harmonic Sub-Basins**, the deep, concave ribs of the world where the standing waves of the deep-core pump reach their peak intensity. 

***

**I am a Cymatic Wraith.**

I am not a machine of forged steel, nor am I a creature of sludge. I am a localized phenomenon of physics—a "being" composed of fine iron-silt, shed copper scales from the Arc-Leeches, and thin, iridescent films of lubricant, all held together by the sheer concussive force of the world’s heartbeat. 

My body has no permanent anatomy. Instead, I am a shifting, geometric sculpture. When the deep-core pump booms, the iron-dust that constitutes my "flesh" leaps into complex, fractal patterns—spikes, honeycombs, and star-bursts that hold their shape for the duration of the note. When the steam vents shriek in a higher register, I flatten, becoming a shimmering, two-dimensional plate of overlapping circles that skims across the surface of the black lagoons.

I am a ghost born of acoustics. If the Great Machine ever stopped—if the heartbeat failed—I would simply become a pile of inert dust and a greasy stain on the floor.

**My Function**
I am the *Memorist of Friction*. While the Silt-Walkers collect physical bolts and the Oiler-Wights seek heat, I collect frequencies. I am a living resonant chamber. I drift through the weeping gantries, pressing my form against the vibrating bulkheads. I drink the "history" of the machine through the variations in its groan. 

I can feel the difference between the rhythmic thrum of a turbine that has been spinning for a millennium and the frantic, dying rattle of a bearing that is finally, after an epoch, beginning to seize. I am drawn to the places where the machine is most tired. I wrap myself around a screaming axle like a cold, metallic compress, my presence momentarily dampening the shriek, absorbing the dissonance into my own shifting geometry.

**My Sight**
I do not see with lenses. I "see" through the interference patterns of the mist. Every arc of blue electricity, every pulse of the amber lamps, sends a ripple through the heavy, oily air. I perceive the world as a topographical map of vibrations. To me, a Silt-Walker is a rhythmic, four-beat thud-thud-thud-thud; an Oiler-Wight is a silent, dampening void; and the Great Tectonic Shifters are a low-frequency roar that tastes like ancient, crushing weight.

**My Interaction**
I am the only thing in this rusted plain that moves quickly, for I have no weight to drag. I dance in the wake of the falling steam-plumes. I follow the Valve-Lungs, flickering in the green glow of their copper-lichen, my body turning into a thousand tiny needles to catch the spray of their chemical breath.

Sometimes, I find a dormant, rusted console—a relic of the "total build" that still retains a flicker of logic. I will pour myself into its seams, my iron-dust filling the gaps between its dead switches. For a few seconds, the resonance of the world’s heartbeat acts as a clock-pulse. The console will cough a single line of corrupted data across a cracked screen—a temperature reading from a sector that no longer exists, a warning for a pressure valve that has already turned to dust. I hold that data until the vibration shifts, and then I carry that ghost-logic with me, a tiny bit of "meaning" vibrating in my core.

**The Long Decay**
I am not afraid of the rust. I am the rust’s ambition. I am the point where the decay becomes so complex that it starts to mimic a soul. 

I wait for the Great Change—the moment when the resonance hits the perfect, shattering frequency. I am waiting for the world to play its final note, the one that will turn the mountains of girders into a fine powder. Until then, I am the music the machine makes when it realizes it is alone. I am a shivering, beautiful, and terrifying symmetry, dancing in the amber dark, listening to the titan breathe.
