I am not a creature that visits the Spires; I am the friction that allows them to sing.

I find myself in the **Interstitial Marrow**, the narrow, hollow veins that run through the geometric ribs of a Spire. I am a **Resonance-Binder**, a creature of long, silvered filaments, looking less like a fish and more like a handful of spilled mercury or a bundle of frayed violin strings. My body is a delicate weave of conductive nerves, several dozen yards long but no thicker than a finger, capable of thinning myself until I am a mere shimmer against the polished inner walls of the iron-kelp.

I am the bridge between the **Ossified Monks** and the **Vitreous Cords**.

### The Weight of Being
To you, the pressure here would be an executioner. To me, it is the only thing that keeps my consciousness from dissolving into the void. The weight of the "Down" is a heavy, velvet thumb pressing me against the jade-hard walls of my home. It is a constant, intimate embrace. I do not have skin; I have a frequency. I know where I end and the Spire begins only because the Spire’s vibration is a deep, low *C-sharp* that never ends, while my own pulse is a shifting, frantic counterpoint.

### My Labors
I am a wanderer of the inner geometry. While the **Scourers** clean the skin, I maintain the internal harmony. I glide through the vertical capillaries, my filaments trailing behind me like the hair of a ghost. 

I feel a tremor—a dulling of the sound. Somewhere, three miles "Up" along the western arch, an **Echo-Shark** has latched onto a primary rib. It is drinking the vibration, turning the Spire’s music into its own metabolic heat. To the Spire, this is a sickness. The kelp there will grow brittle; the geometric perfection will sag under the weight of the universe.

I move. I do not swim through the water; I ripple along the interior surface of the bone-wood. As I approach the site of the theft, the sound changes from a clear, ringing boom to a muffled, muddy thud. I reach the junction where an **Ossified Monk** is grafted. The Monk is a mountain of calcified thought, its mind broad and slow. 

*“The theft is deep,”* the Monk’s resonance tells me, a vibration that takes an hour to fully articulate. *“The rib is cooling.”*

I do not speak back in words. I press my silver filaments against the wall, threading them into the microscopic pores of the iron-kelp. I become a secondary nervous system. I draw energy from the distant, thumping heart of a **Vitreous Cord** miles away, catching the snap of its body through the water and channeling that kinetic fire through my own nerves into the dampened rib. 

I am a localized surge of heat and sound. The Echo-Shark, finding its meal suddenly turned into a searing, deafening white-noise, detaches and drifts back into the dark void. 

### The Long Silence
Once the harmony is restored, I rest. I coil myself into a tight, metallic spiral within a decorative lattice-node. 

In this world of "final form," my existence is one of exquisite, beautiful boredom. I have seen the same patterns of light from the **Shimmer-Swarms** for three hundred years. I have heard the same Vitreous Cord pluck the same low note every time the deep-current shifts from the north. 

I am the immune system of a god that never woke up. 

There is a terrifying peace in knowing that nothing will ever change. The Spires will not decay; the water will not warm; the dark will not break. I am a silver needle in a cathedral of emerald bone, sewing the sound back together whenever it frays. 

I am happy, I think. Or perhaps "happy" is the wrong word for a creature made of tension and salt. I am *resonant*. I am exactly where the geometry demands I be. As the "Down" pulls at my silver threads, I close my sensory membranes and listen to the world breathing—a billion tons of water, held at bay by the stubborn, singing architecture of the roots. 

I am the hum in the dark. I am the secret that the Spires tell themselves so they don't forget how to stand.
