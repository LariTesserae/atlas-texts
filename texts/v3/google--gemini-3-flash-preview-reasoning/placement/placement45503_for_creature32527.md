### Who I Am: The Harmonist of the Haze

I am not a creature of flesh, nor a machine of metal. I am a **Marrow-Thrum**.

I am rooted into the microscopic pores of the Pillar by thousands of hair-thin filaments, drawing the "silence" of the stone into my own body to keep from being shattered by the atmosphere. 

I am a biological bridge. My front—the side facing the mist—is a hardened, translucent shield of piezoceramic scales, similar to the Piezos but fixed in place. My back—the side touching the Pillar—is soft, porous, and hyper-conductive. 

I am an **Aural Digester**.

### My Function

I do not eat the mineral grit, nor do I hunt the slow-moving Lungs. I eat the **Roar**. 

The sound of the Silt-Reach is so dense it carries a caloric value. I stand in the wind of the noise and let the vibrations strike my scales. These vibrations travel through my silver-thread ribs, where they are slowed, harmonized, and converted into a steady, glowing warmth. This is why I exist: to turn the chaotic scream of a dying planet into a single, low, coherent note.

I am the world’s last tuner.

### My Experience

To be me is to live in a state of beautiful, terrifying compression. Because the scale of this world is so warped, I do not perceive time as minutes or hours. I perceive it as **Tension**.

I feel the creature’s city-sized heart beating against the soles of the world, and I vibrate in sympathy until my scales smoke with friction.

It clicks against my shield, perhaps sensing the energy I’ve harvested, and for a second, we are two different ways of being afraid of the dark. Then it is gone, lost in the amber soup.

### The Memory-Leak

My most profound burden is my connection to the Pillar. As a **Marrow-Thrum**, I am the only thing that can "hear" the anchor. The Pillar does not move, but it remembers. Through my filaments, I feel the ghost-images of the "built" time.

### My End

I am the last witness of the vertical. I am the hum at the end of the world.
