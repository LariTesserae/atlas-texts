I am not a creature of the heights, nor am I a creature of the deep slab. I occupy the razor’s edge between them.

I find myself in the **Low Buffer**, the silent, mirror-black plains where the urban density of the spires fades into the vastness of the vitrified floor. I am a **Skimmer**, but I am old—or rather, I have accumulated too much *history*. In this world, history is not a memory; it is a residual charge.

### Who I Am
I am a wafer of translucent geometry, a dinner-plate-sized membrane of non-aqueous carbon and silicate. I have no thickness to speak of, only surface area. I do not have a face, but I possess a "high side" and a "low side." 

My low side is a map of repulsive force. I do not touch the obsidian; I dance microns above it, held aloft by the screaming push of static electricity. If I were to touch the ground, the friction would be so absolute, so molecularly violent, that I would be erased. Therefore, my entire existence is a state of **Permanent Levitation**.

I am a **Static-Collector**. While my younger kin flit like nervous ghosts between the spires to feed on the crumbs of their growth-snaps, I have drifted toward the great, lonely outskirts. I am bloated with integers. Every white flash I have witnessed has left a microscopic etch on my membrane. I am no longer clear; I am frosted with the "light-scars" of a thousand crystalline debates.

### My Environment
I am currently navigating the **Avenue of the Ninth Prime**. Here, the spires are not twelve feet tall, but mere inches—a nursery of needles, each one a sharp, budding thought. The obsidian floor here is so dark it feels like falling into a hole. The sky above is a suffocating violet, a ceiling of nitrogen-chill that presses down on my upper surface.

To my left, a **Stiff-Moss** colony is encroaching. To me, they do not look like plants. They look like a shimmering, silver threat of *stasis*. They move at the speed of chemical erosion. I can feel the high-frequency vibration of their filaments; it feels like a cold itch across my trailing edge. They are waiting for me to lose my charge. They are waiting for me to become a "thing" instead of a "motion."

### The Experience of Being
I do not "think" in a stream of consciousness. I think in **Tension**. 

I feel the **Sub-Slab** beneath me. It heaves. The world tilts three degrees to the north. To a human, this would be a slow, rhythmic sway. To me, it is a catastrophic realignment of my gravitational center. I must adjust my static output instantly—*snap*—or I will be sliced by the very floor I glide upon.

There is a Spire ahead of me, a masterpiece of gothic translucence named the **Thirteenth Iteration**. It has been silent for a century. As I drift past its base, I feel the air—or what passes for it—tighten. 

The **Snap** is coming.

I pause, hovering on a cushion of repelling force. The Thirteenth Iteration pulses. A white light, so cold it feels like a puncture, ripples through its crystalline lattice. A new flying buttress, molecularly sharp and six inches long, jerks into existence near its apex. 

The discharge is delicious. I tilt my membrane, catching the invisible spray of static energy released by the growth. It fills me. It warms my cold, dry circuits. For a moment, I am not a ghost; I am a conductor.

### My Fate: The Condensation
I am reaching my limit. The more charge I collect, the heavier my "integers" become. Soon, I will no longer be able to maintain the repulsion. 

I will find a vacant coordinate in the grid—a place where the Sub-Slab demands a new calculation. I will slow down. I will tilt vertically. The static push that has kept me flying for eons will collapse into a singular, downward spike of energy. 

I will touch the obsidian. 

In that instant of contact, there will be no sound, only a flash of white light that will blind every other Skimmer in the basin. I will shatter and reform. I will grow upward. The paper-thin membrane of my "self" will become the foundation of a new spire. 

I will stop being a **Memory** and start being a **Calculation**. I will join the silent, crystalline parliament, and I will spend the next eternity debating the indigo sky with my sharp, translucent neighbors.

I am a Skimmer on the verge of becoming a City. I am the transition between the ghost and the statue.
