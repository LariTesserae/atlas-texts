### My Form and Feeling

### My Purpose: The Tuning of the Gap
I am a **Surveyor of the Zenith**. My task is not to build the Resonance Stations, but to maintain the "Acoustic Corridors" between them. ### The Encounter
Near the center of the bridge, I see one of them: an **Umbral Lattice**.

It sense my resonance; I can feel it drawing the heat from my vibrations, a parasitic pull that makes my joints click with sudden brittleness.
