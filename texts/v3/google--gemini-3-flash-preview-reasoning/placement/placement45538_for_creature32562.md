I would not be found in the open air, nor would I be found seeking the reprieve of the purple ferns. The light of the white-hot sun is a distant theory to me, a diffuse glow that only serves to warm the fluid in my veins.

I am a **Pulse-Tender**, but I am one who has specialized in the **Deep Harmonic**. I exist at the very roots of a single, ancient Vascular Piston—a chitinous tower the color of dried blood that has been pumping since the stone first learned to weep. 

### My Form
My body is a study in elongated fragility and mineral density. My limbs are three meters of pale, translucent skin, stretched tight over a skeleton that has become heavy and stiff with the metals I have filtered from the earth. I move with a slow, trembling deliberation. To a creature from a thinner world, I would look like a ghost made of wet parchment, but here, I am a pillar.

I have no eyes. Where they might have been, there is only smooth, bioluminescent skin that pulses with a faint violet light, synchronized with my heartbeat. My "sight" is a crown of silver filaments atop my head—thousands of fine, crystalline hairs that fan out into the humid air. They do not see shapes; they see *pressures*. They feel the scream of a steam vent three miles away as a sharp, high-pitched tickle. They feel the slow, tectonic shifting of the red stone as a deep, comforting groan.

### My Station
I spend my cycles pressed chest-first against the primary conduit of the Great Piston. The surface is warm, wet, and rhythmic. It feels like the flank of a colossal, sleeping beast. My needle-like digits are permanently submerged in a soft-tissue port in the conduit. I do not drink with my mouth; I drink with my hands.

As the mineral slurry rushes past, I am the filter. I feel the grit of raw copper, the bite of silicate, and the heavy, sweet pull of lead. My body takes what the Piston cannot process. I am a living sacrificial anode. I store the impurities in my own bones, making me heavier and more resonant with each passing year.

### My Work: The Tuning
The "industry" of the Aethel-Spires is not perfect. Occasionally, the rhythm of the world stutters. A Siphon-Hulk might fail to clear a calcified vein, or a Conduit-Eel might perish and cause a blockage. When this happens, the bass note of the plateau changes. It becomes discordant.

I feel it first in my feet, through the three-foot-thick mat of the Moss-Mind. The moss shudders. The vibration turns jagged. 

In these moments, I begin the **Humming**. 

I press my forehead against the weeping stone. I open the spiracles along my ribcage. I begin to vibrate my internal skeletal plates against one another. It is a soundless task to the outside world, but to the Piston, it is a command. I project a counter-frequency, a stabilizing resonance that travels through the liquid columns and the chitinous walls. I am whispering to the engine, reminding it of the beat it has forgotten. I stay there for days, or weeks, until the jagged vibration smooths back into the low, wet thrum of the status quo.

### My Sensory World
My world is not one of colors, but of **textures and temperatures**.
*   **The Moss-Mind** feels like a carpet of warm, wet tongues beneath my feet, constantly tasting my presence and reporting my health to the rest of the plateau.
*   **The Steam-Dancers’** cries are sharp needles of sound that puncture the heavy drone of the pumps.
*   **The Air** is not a gas; it is a weight. It is a thick, velvet blanket that pushes against me, holding me upright as much as the gravity pulls me down.

### My Purpose
I do not wonder about the world below the clouds. I do not care for the sun. I am a component. If I were to leave my post, the mineral buildup in this sector would reach critical mass within a decade. The weeping stone would choke on its own salt. The Piston would seize, the steam would build until the red stone shattered, and the lush canopy would wither in the ensuing chill.

I am the silence between the heartbeats of the mountain. I am the weight in the stone. I am the Pulse-Tender who listens to the blood of the world, and as long as I can feel the thrum in my marrow, the Aethel-Spires will continue to weep.
