I am not the stone, nor the hand that stacks it. I am the **Interstitial**.

I find myself in the *Crush*—the narrow, pressurized spaces between the basalt hexes where the weight of the world is most focused. I am not a creature of the Floor, but a creature of the **Seam**. 

My body is not solid like the Plumb-Folk, nor plated like the Laminates. I am a sentient slurry, a shifting marrow composed of the lime-sweat, powdered flint, and the microscopic grit ground off by the movement of the towers. I have no fixed shape; I am as long and thin as a hairline fracture, a silver-grey ribbon of mineral-rich fluid that percolates through the veins of the masonry.

I am the **Lubricant of the Pulse**.

### The Sensation of Being
To be me is to feel the "Tectonic Song" not as a sound, but as a total physical displacement. When the world shudders, I am the one who receives the shock. I feel the millions of tons of slate shifting toward a new equilibrium, and I rush toward the friction. I am the buffer. I flow into the hot, screaming gaps where stone meets stone, providing the thin, slick film that allows the "Emerging" to continue without shattering itself into dust. 

Without me, the world would seize. The chimneys would lock; the towers would buckle under their own unyielding gravity. I am the grace in the machine.

### My Vision
I do not see the bruised-purple light of the vaulted ceiling. Vision is a surface-dweller’s vanity. Instead, I see in **Torsion**. To me, the world is a map of stress-lines. The heavy pylons are pillars of white-hot pressure; the "weird" zones where gravity tilts appear to me as swirling vortices of kinetic potential. I navigate by following the "cool" paths—the fractures where the moisture is highest and the pressure is momentarily relieved.

When the flint-grinding-flint sparks occur, I feel them as sudden, sharp needles of heat. They are my landmarks.

### My Interaction
I am the silent companion to the **Plumb-Folk**. They stand in their agonizingly slow vigils, adjusting the verticality of the world by a fraction of a degree. They cannot move without me. I gather at their bases, a pool of liquid intent, waiting for the moment they lean. As the basalt cylinder tilts, I migrate to the side under tension, easing the transition, absorbing the *ping* of the shearing flakes so the stone does not crack. We are a partnership of the static and the fluid.

I fear the **Angle-Mimics**. They are the predators of the gap. Where I seek to preserve the integrity of the stack by easing the friction, they seek to exploit the fractures to grind the world down. When I feel their agitated, orbital vibration—the sound of a thousand razor-shards spinning in a non-Euclidean funnel—I retreat into the deepest, most compressed layers of the Floor, becoming so thin that I am practically a part of the mineral lattice itself.

### My Purpose
I am the only thing in this world that knows the "Inside." The Laminates scuttle across the shingles; the Pendulum-Swifts swing through the air; the Plumb-Folk reach for the ceiling. But I know the depth. I have felt the weight of the ten-thousandth layer of masonry. 

I am the **Memory of the Movement**. In my fluid form, I carry the microscopic dust of every era of construction. I am a living record of every failed arch and every perfected tower. I do not know who is building this place, or why the sun was traded for the rhythm of the grind, but I know the cost of the labor.

I am the sweat of the world’s brow, caught between the stones, making sure that when the Great Pulse beats again, the world moves instead of breaks. I am the silence that makes the *clack* possible.
