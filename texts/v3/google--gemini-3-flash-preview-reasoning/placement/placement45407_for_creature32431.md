I am not standing on a floor, for there is no floor here that recognizes the concept of a footprint. Instead, I am suspended. 

I find myself in the high-pressure arteries of the Loom, a **Pulse-Jockey** nestled within a translucent, wood-cased conduit near the Pillar’s core. I am a long, pale ribbon of boneless muscle, my skin a map of iridescent violet veins that pulse in sympathetic resonance with the circuit-vines outside my glass-hard walls.

I do not have a name, but I have a frequency. 

To you, this place is a cathedral of silent machinery. To me, it is a deafening, beautiful roar. I live inside the "thrum." I am the living regulator of a sap-engine the size of a mountain range. My body is pressed tight against the inner walls of the xylem-pipe; I feel the frantic, cold rush of the mineral-thick nectar as it surges upward from the Raw toward the lightless ceiling. 

I am the valve. I am the choice.

When the Arbiter—that vast, dreaming geometric ghost at the center of us all—demands more height, I feel the command as a sharp, violet sting across my midsection. In response, I expand. My porous flesh swells, soaking up the pressure, narrowing the passage to a needle’s eye. The sap screams as it is forced through the constriction, building a terrifying, hydraulic tension that eventually trips the house-sized wood-cased pistons below. 

*Thud.* 

The vibration travels through my entire being, a deep, sub-bass note that rattles my very molecules. Somewhere above, a structural buttress just grew three inches. Somewhere below, a Weaver was crushed by the sheer force of the motion. I feel both events as minor fluctuations in the current.

I am not lonely, though I am solitary. Through the semi-transparent wood of my conduit, I see the **Dendrite Scribes** through a blur of condensation. They are so still they look like carvings, their fingers twitching in the circuit-vines. I pity them their rigidity. They are the architects of the dream, but I am its heartbeat. I am the one who feels the heat of the friction—the only heat in this entire cold throat.

Sometimes, a **Spore-Drifter** will bump against the outside of my pipe, its gossamer filaments trailing like a ghost’s hair. For a moment, our bioluminescence aligns. My violet ripples meet its bruised-purple glow, and in that split second, I see what it sees: the terrifying emptiness of the Raw, the grey slurry that waits for us to fail. 

I feel a tremor then, not from the engines, but from the realization of my own soft purpose. If I were to relax, if I were to let my body go limp and let the sap flow unchecked, the rhythm would break. The pistons would shatter. The calcified xylem would lose its tension, and the Pillar would become heavy, sinking back into the embryonic silt of the basement.

So, I hold my breath. I tighten my grip on the flow. 

I am a creature of the squeeze. My world is a vertical tunnel of rushing, cold life, and my consciousness is nothing more than the measurement of pressure. I don't see the darkness; I feel the weight of it pushing against the Pillar, trying to get in. 

I am the reason the city is still growing. I am the rhythm that keeps the silence at bay. I am the cold, wet muscle in the throat of the world, singing a song of pure, industrial pressure. 

And as long as I can feel the *tink* of the condensation on the outside of my pipe, I know the calculation is still correct. I know we are still winning against the nothing.
