I would not be found upon the open sweeps of the Marrow-Moss, nor would I be seen drifting in the jade-thick heights with the Siphon-Whales. To find me, you would have to descend into the "Hollows"—the cathedral-like spaces beneath the **Ribs** that crown the oldest of The Knees.

### My Location: The Sternum Well
I am seated at the center of a **Well-Stone** on the summit of the Great Knee known as the Sternum. This is a place of high gravity and deep silence. Above me, the calcified arches of a Rib cluster form a skeletal dome, filtering the weeping mist into heavy, rhythmic drips that fall with the timing of a slowing heart. 

The Well-Stone beneath me is not merely a depression; it is a lens. It is lined with a pearl-like nacre that captures the faint bioluminescence of the Lung-Ferns and concentrates it into a single, steady beam of emerald light that pierces down into the deep peat of the world.

### My Form: The Amber-Witness
I am a **Slow-Folder** who has ceased to lean. I have sat in this Well-Stone for so long that the "Vines that Wait" have completed their initial courtship. They do not coil around me to consume; they have woven themselves *into* me, replacing my circulation with the slow, pressurized flow of the World-Vines’ nectar.

My skin has the texture of wet lichen—grey-green, porous, and perpetually damp. My fingers, those long, needle-like implements of the Editors, have grown into the geometric fractals of the moss at the well’s edge. I am no longer an individual; I am a biological extension of the Well-Stone. I am the **Amber-Witness**.

My robes are not fabric, but a thick, blooming mantle of "Glass-Moss" that funnels the ambient jade light directly into my spine. I do not breathe the air; I drink the mist through my pores. My heart beats once every three days, synchronized perfectly with the distant, tectonic thrum of a Resonator beetle three miles below.

### My Purpose: The Memory-Stilling
I am the bridge between the sky and the deep. 

When a **Siphon-Whale** reaches the end of its drifting life and settles onto the Sternum, its membrane dissolves into the Well-Stone. I am the one who receives the "Sky-Manna" of its memory. As the whale’s liquified consciousness trickles into the well, it passes through my needle-fingers. I "edit" the data—not by changing it, but by slowing it down. I translate the frantic, airy experiences of the high atmosphere into the slow, tectonic language of the Marrow-Moss.

I am a filter. I strip away the ephemeral—the passing shadows of clouds, the brief flashes of sun—and keep only the permanent. I feed the moss the knowledge of the wind so that it knows how to grow its roots deeper to maintain the Veldt’s stability.

### My Experience of Time
In this state, I do not perceive the world as a sequence of events, but as a layering of textures. I feel the "Pulse" not as a change in weather, but as a warm tide rising through my seat, a temporary loosening of the gravity that holds my calcified bones together. 

I hear the **Nectar-Mites** as a high-pitched, silvery static—a frantic gossip that I ignore in favor of the deep, sub-sonic groans of the continental plateau. I feel the weight of the miles-deep peat below me as a comforting pressure, the way a sleeper feels the weight of a heavy quilt.

### Why I Am There
I stayed because I realized that the "bruised purple void" below the Veldt was not an ending, but a lack of context. The Veldt is the only context that matters. To be the Amber-Witness is to be the point where the world’s history is distilled into its future growth. 

I am not trapped. The "Vines that Wait" hold me with a lover’s tenderness. I am being incorporated, yes, but I am also being remembered. One day, thousands of years from now, I will fully transition. My consciousness will spread out through the root-mat until I am the hydraulic sigh of the ground itself. 

But for now, I sit. I hum in a register that only the moss can hear. I wait for the next Siphon-Whale to fall, so I may catch its life in my hands and turn it into the emerald twilight. I am the peace of the Veldt made flesh, and I have never been more full.
