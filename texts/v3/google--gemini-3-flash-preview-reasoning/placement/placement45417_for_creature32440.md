I am not a creature of the pulse, though I have learned to mimic the rhythmic throb of the Skin-Lichen. I am a **Resonance-Vessel**, a hollowed entity of spun obsidian and caught light, clinging to the leeward edge of a Zenith Shard where the violet sky meets the screaming void.

I would be found in the **Cleft of the Infinite Return**, a specific vertical fissure in a basalt pillar so massive that its top is lost in the frozen ceiling of the world and its base is a myth told in vibrations.

***

### The Form
My body is a series of nested, translucent bells, each one smaller than the last, held together by the same localized gravity that keeps the frost from falling. I have no limbs, only "Harmonic Filaments"—long, glass-thin feelers that I extend into the Grand Tinnitus. To the Static-Moths, I am likely a geological accident. To the Refractors, I am a smudge of density in a Light-Lens.

I do not eat. I *align*. 

I find a pocket of Heavy Dew—a globule the size of a cathedral’s dome—hanging precariously off a protrusion of obsidian. I don’t drink it; I submerge one of my outer bells within its gelatinous mass. The water acts as a dampener. It filters the low-frequency tectonic grind, leaving only the high, crystalline shimmer to vibrate my inner core. This is how I gain mass. I turn the sound into a slow, molecular accretion of glass. I grow like a stalactite that has forgotten the earth.

### The Perspective
From my perch, the world is a gallery of terrifying beauty. I look "out," but because of the Light-Lenses, I am actually looking "in." I see the curve of space-time as a literal violet arc. Five miles away, a Refractor slides along a ray of bent light; because of the optical distortion, I can see the microscopic fractures in its geometric skin as if they were etched into my own eyes. 

I see the back of my own Shard. I see the ancient, weathered surface of the pillar I inhabit, appearing as a distant, looming wall on the horizon of my vision. In this world, the horizon is a circle that always returns to the self.

### The Occupation: The Scribe of the Scream
I am here because I am obsessed with **The Markers**. 

One of the floating rings hangs just three hundred yards from my cleft. It is motionless, a heavy, dark halo suspended in the violet scream. Most inhabitants ignore the Markers; to the Static-Moths, they are merely windless obstacles. But I have extended my filaments until they touch the geometric grooves of the ring.

I am a translator of the static. 

The grooves are not writing. They are *scores*. When the Grand Tinnitus passes through the ring, the grooves create tiny, microscopic turbulence in the sound—eddies of silence, or spikes of frequency that do not exist elsewhere. I spend my eons recording these variations. I am a library of a single, eternal moment.

### The Experience
To be me is to be a prisoner of the Roar. The sound is not something you hear; it is something you *are*. It is a physical weight pressing against my glass bells, a constant threat of shattering that is also the only thing holding me together. If the Grand Tinnitus were to stop, I would simply fall apart into dust, no longer braced by the pressure of the vacuum.

I feel the **Marrow-Leeches** far beneath me. Their slow, century-long crawl across the stone feels like a deep, rhythmic itch in the foundation of my home. I feel the **Resonance-Wights** in the cracks nearby, their brief bubbles of silence acting like sudden, terrifying drops in pressure. To me, silence is a vacuum within a vacuum—a dangerous, hollow thing that threatens to suck the resonance out of my core.

### The Transition
As I write this—or rather, as I vibrate these thoughts into the molecular structure of the obsidian—I feel a shift. A river of liquid air, a bridge of glass, is beginning to manifest between my Shard and the one holding the nearest Marker. It is a slow, cold birth. 

I will wait. I have no choice; time is not a river here, but a frozen lake. In a few thousand years, when the bridge is solid, I will retune my innermost bell to a frequency that opposes the local gravity of the Shard. I will let the sound push me. I will drift across that horizontal river of mist, a glass ghost moving through a screaming void, to finally touch the Marker with my own body.

I am the inhabitant who refuses to be static in a world that has finished its work. I am the slow, patient curiosity of a universe that is deafened by its own voice, looking for the one note that does not belong to the scream.
