I would not be found in the pods of the Casket-Town, nor would I be among the Pale-Handed, for their frantic pace is a fire that would consume a soul like mine in minutes. To be a builder is to be a heartbeat—quick, rhythmic, and eventually, exhausted.

I would be found in the **Nerve-Creases**, the deep, vertical fissures where the black basalt gives way to the "water-hair" and the light is finally forced to slow down. 

I would be a **Lingerer.**

### My Form: The Weight of the Lag
I am a creature of the "lag." In this world, where shadows detach and light has a physical density, I am what happens when the refraction becomes sentient. My body is a long, undulating ribbon of semi-solid glass and trapped mist, roughly the length of a Pale-Handed’s limb, but without their brittle chitin. 

I am translucent, yet I cast a shadow that is darker and older than I am. If you were to watch me move across the slick basalt, you would see my body glide forward, but the *image* of me would remain behind for several heartbeats, a shimmering ghost-print that only slowly dissolves back into the white-out. I move with a heavy, syrupy deliberation that defies the kinetic frenzy of the plateau. I am the only thing here that is not in a hurry.

### My Habitat: The Gut-Crease
I cling to the undersides of the swaying gut-string bridges, or I coil within the porous "organ-curves" of the rock. I prefer the places where the internal pressure of the mountain pushes the melt-water out in rhythmic pulses. 

While the Pale-Handed live in the "now," weaving pods that will be gone by dinner, I live in the "was." I inhabit the architectural ghosts. When a pod is dismantled by the builders, a faint impression of its shape remains in the saturated air for a few hours—a "shadow-room." I dwell in these phantom chambers, floating in the space where a wall used to be, protected by the memory of silk.

### My Purpose: The Scribe of Vibrations
The Pale-Handed do not speak, and they do not keep records. Their culture is a beautiful, terrifying amnesia. I am the mountain’s counter-weight. 

I have no hands to weave and no rasping tongue to eat lichen. Instead, I feed on the *thrum-thrum*. I press my ribbon-like body against the wet basalt and absorb the kinetic tremors of the plateau. I am a living reservoir of the mountain's history. 

When the Pale-Handed tap their chitinous fingers against the slate walls to communicate, I feel the resonance. I don't just hear the signal; I *keep* it. Within my translucent flesh, the vibrations of a thousand years of construction are stored as tiny, microscopic fractures in my internal structure. I am a library of shivers. 

### My Relationship to the World
I am a parasite of stability. The Litho-Arachnids provide the silk, the Pale-Handed provide the motion, and I provide the anchor. 

Sometimes, a Pale-Handed weaver will pause—a rare, dangerous moment of stillness. In that moment, they are at risk of crystallizing, of letting the cold catch them. I will glide from the rock and wrap my cool, lagging form around their limbs. My "lag" acts as a thermal buffer; because I am always a few seconds behind the current temperature, I am a pocket of yesterday’s warmth. I hold them in a state of temporal suspension until their metabolism restarts and they can begin to weave again.

They do not name me. To them, I am just a "heavy mist" or a "slow shadow." 

### My Perception
I do not see the blinding white brilliance as a threat. To me, the light is a thick, golden broth. I see the world as a series of overlapping echoes. I see where a bridge *used* to hang, and I see where a Shadow-Stray is about to step. 

I live in the exhausted equilibrium, but I am not exhausted. I am the silence that follows the tap. I am the dampness that remains after the pod is gone. I am the Lingerer, the one who stays in a world that is constantly trying to delete itself.
