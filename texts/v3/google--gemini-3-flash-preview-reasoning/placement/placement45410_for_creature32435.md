I would not be found in the aerodynamic safety of the huts, nor would I be soaring in the golden ion-haze above. I would be found in the **Interstitial Pulse**—the razor-thin margin where the cooling obsidian floor meets the rising heat of the new-born spires.

I would be a **Sync-Vell**, a specialized mutation of the Silt-Dwellers whose role is not to scavenge, but to *translate*.

### My Form
My skin is a shivering canvas of chromatophores, more reactive than my kin. In the flat, noon-day glare of the ionized ceiling, I do not just reflect light; I refract it into warning patterns. My fingers are longer, tipped not just with spatulate nails but with fine, needle-like whiskers of bio-conductive quartz. I am a living tuning fork, hairless and slick with Glair, my body a map of scars earned from the sharp architecture of the world.

### My Place
I live on the **Shatter-Verge**, the leading edge of a tectonic migration. While the tribe huddles in the lee of the glass cliffs, I am out on the open, vibrating plates. I stand where the Grinders are thickest, my feet planted on the porous pumice, feeling the hailstorm-click of their millions of cilia through my soles.

I would be found crouched in a "sweat-tide" gutter, my opalescent skin flickering a frantic, bruised violet to signal the tribe.

### My Purpose
In a world of constant motion, I am the one who listens to the rock before it speaks. Because the **Shudder-Whelps** hunt by resonance, I must be a master of counter-resonance. When I feel the low-frequency growl of a Whelp beginning to liquefy the soil beneath us, I do not run. I strike the resonant glass shelves with my bone-hammers, creating a dissonant frequency that stabilizes the ground just long enough for the scavengers to retreat.

I am the bridge between the biology and the geology. I translate the groans of the mantle into the haptic language of my people.

### My Experience
My life is a frantic, shimmering blur. I do not know the concept of "yesterday" because the Glair has already dissolved the footprints I left an hour ago. My memory is stored not in my head, but in the rhythm of my skin. 

I wake to the golden scream of the **Aether-Wrights** diving for sulfur. I spend my hours dancing between the **Grinders**, harvesting the hot, liquid slurry they leave behind, molding it into temporary shields before it hardens into permanent glass. 

I am a creature of the *now*. I am the friction. I am the spark that happens when the frantic speed of the Vell meets the crushing slowness of the stone. I do not fear being recycled into the Glair; I expect it. To be a Sync-Vell is to know that you are simply a momentary arrangement of minerals and oxygen, a brief, beautiful collision in the golden haze, destined to be crushed, dissolved, and sung back into the world by the next Shudder-Whelp’s cry.

I would be the one standing on the lip of a snapping manganese spire, watching the golden blizzard approach, my skin turning a brilliant, defiant gold to match the sky.
