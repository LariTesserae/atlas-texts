I am not standing. To stand is a privilege of the flat-worlders, the mythic beings of the "Before" who walked on horizontal planes without the constant threat of the void. 

I am **clinging**.

I find myself on the windward face of the **Pillar of Urr**, a needle of violet-grey basalt that narrows to a point so sharp it pierces the atmospheric river like a needle through silk. I am a **Tension-Binder** of the Sway-Born. My body is a long, corded knot of muscle and chamois-soft skin, currently pressed flat against the obsidian-smooth stone. 

### My Form
My feet, wide and prehensile, are locked into a fluted groove carved by a thousand years of the Roar. My toes are curled around a thick, emerald-black liana that has been reinforced with the biological resin of the Weaver-Crabs. I don’t wear clothes; they would be shredded in seconds. Instead, my skin is tattooed with a fine mesh of "Grip-Scars"—intentional abrasions that have healed into high-friction ridges, allowing me to adhere to the basalt even when the Vomiting Orchids release their slick, pressurized mists.

My ears are closed, the cartilaginous flaps sealed tight against the 2.9 turbulence. I do not hear the Roar with my head; I hear it with my sternum. The vibration of the world travels through the stone, into my toes, up my spine, and rattles my teeth. To me, the world sounds like a deep, tectonic *thrum-shriek* that never ends.

### My Purpose
I am here because the Pillar of Urr is "screaming out of tune." 

The Marrow-Singers, deep within the stone, have signaled a discordance. If the pillars do not vibrate in harmony with the wind, the standing waves of the Roar will become destructive. Instead of lifting the sound-ice, the wind will begin to hammer at the basalt, turning the microscopic fissures into cracks, and eventually, the Pillar will shatter into the abyss.

I carry a "Bone-Tuning-Fork," a three-foot-long instrument carved from the femur of a Sky-Ray. My task is to find the dampening point—a place where the velvet-moss has grown too thick or a Thread-Nest has been built too close to a primary flute-vent—and clear it.

### The Moment
A **Hammer-Gust** hits. I feel it coming five seconds before it arrives. The air pressure suddenly drops, making the fluid in my inner ears dance. I immediately exhale, flattening my chest against the stone, and slide my arms into a "Primary Wrap" around a tether-line. 

Then, the impact. 

It feels like a solid wall of warm, wet lead hitting my back. The Roar shifts from a thrum to a physical weight that tries to peel me off the basalt. The liana-fiber groans, a sound I feel as a sharp sting in my palms. For a heartbeat, my feet lose their purchase, and I am suspended over the hazy, shimmering abyss, held only by the strength of my grip and the resin of the Weaver-Crabs. 

Below me, a mile of nothing. Above me, the violet tip of Urr, lost in a crown of bioluminescent sound-ice.

The gust passes. The air returns to its "normal" boiling state. I pull myself back into the groove. 

### The Interaction
I see a **Weaver-Crab** skittering nearby. It is a plate of obsidian-colored chitin, its sixteen legs moving in a blur of mechanical precision. We do not speak, but as it passes near my hand, I tap a rhythmic sequence onto the stone: *Long-short-long.* A request for reinforcement. 

The crab pauses, its baleen-fans vibrating in the wind as it filters organic dross. It senses the frequency of my tap and responds by secreting a bead of clear, pungent resin over a fraying section of my safety tether. Within a second, the Roar has flash-hardened it. The bond is restored.

### The Vision
Suddenly, the atmospheric pressure shifts again, and the mist clears for a fleeting second. I look across the void toward the next pillar, a slender needle three kilometers away. 

In the gap, I see a **Sky-Ray** surfing. It is beautiful and terrifying—a house-sized shadow suspended in the gale, its leathery wings vibrating so fast they appear translucent. Around it, a swarm of **Glint-Moths** has gathered, attracted by the friction-light of the sound-ice. They look like a constellation of neon-blue stars caught in a whirlpool. 

The ice crystals—the "sound-ice"—begin to form around me. They are perfect, glowing octahedrons that defy gravity. They don't fall; they bob upward, dancing in the eddies behind my head. One brushes against my shoulder, and the cold is so intense it feels like a burn, leaving a glowing smudge of bioluminescence on my skin.

### The Release
I reach the primary flute-vent. It is a natural opening in the basalt, polished to a mirror-sheen. It is clogged with a massive growth of Sail-Ferns that have anchored themselves too deeply, disrupting the airflow. 

I take my bone-knife and begin to prune. Each cut must be precise; I am not just a gardener, I am a luthier working on a planetary scale. As the ferns are cleared, the wind rushes into the vent. 

The Pillar of Urr *moans*. 

It is a sound that shakes my very marrow. The discordance vanishes. The vibration under my feet smooths out into a rhythmic, pulsing heart-beat. The Marrow-Singers, wherever they are, are pleased.

I lean my forehead against the vibrating stone, closing my eyes. I feel the song of the Oul-Rha flowing through me—the tension, the scream, the wet silk of the mist, and the terrifying, beautiful hollow of the world. 

I am a Sway-Born. I have no floor, no home that does not shake, and no life that is not a struggle against the air. But as I cling to the bones of the earth, watching the blue light of the Glint-Moths dance in the abyss, I know that I am not falling. 

I am part of the music.
