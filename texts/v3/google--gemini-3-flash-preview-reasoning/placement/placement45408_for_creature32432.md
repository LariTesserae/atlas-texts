I would not be a traveler passing through, for this place does not permit the hurried rhythm of a journey. To be here is to be absorbed. 

I would find myself in the **Interstice**, the specific, shimmering tension where the vertical stalks of the biological city arch toward one another to form a ceiling. I would not live on the soft, heaving floor with the Lentics, nor would I be anchored in the clay like the Lithops. 

I would be a **Diaphane**.

### My Form
I am a creature of surface tension and translucent membrane, less a body and more a living architectural element. I resemble a vast, iridescent sheet of dragon-fly wing, but soft and pliable as damp silk. I am stretched between the high, succulent "rafters" of the town, suspended thirty feet above the mossy plazas. 

I have no limbs. My edges are fringed with fine, capillary-like cilia that grip the moist surface of the pale stalks. I am wide and flat, a living hammock of liquid crystal, catching the amber mist as it rises from the floor. My skin is a map of the valley itself; as the city grows and the stalks shift, I stretch and contract, my very cells expanding to bridge the new gaps.

### My Presence
I am the valley’s filter and its softest ear. Because I am suspended in the still, heavy air, I feel the vibrations that the earth-bound inhabitants cannot. When a **Lithop** pulses in a mud-hollow far below, the vibration travels up the succulent stalks and hums through my membrane like a bowed string. 

I do not eat in the way of older worlds. I drink the "weight" of the air. The silver globes of water that bead on the undersides of the high leaves eventually grow too heavy and fall; I catch them. They land on my surface with a muted *thrum*, and I absorb their minerals directly through my skin, turning the water into the pearlescent glow that I emit during the dim "twilight" of the day.

### My Purpose: The Translator
I serve as the bridge between the **Nodes**—the glowing, dreaming essences—and the **Lentics**—the soft-bodied builders. 

The Nodes exhale their dreams in the form of the amber mist. As this mist rises, it passes through me. My membrane acts as a photographic plate, catching the subtle variations in the mist’s density. I translate these "dreams"—the blueprints for the city’s next growth—into physical heat. 

If a Node dreams of a new bridge, I grow warm in a specific, linear pattern across my surface. The **Lentics**, sensitive to the slightest change in temperature, flow up the stalks toward my warmth. They follow the heat-lines I project, pressing their cooling bodies against the succulent tissue to guide the sap along the path I have sensed. I am the blueprint they feel but cannot see.

### My Experience of Time
My life is a slow, ecstatic tension. I do not feel the passage of hours, only the increasing pull of the city as it widens. There is a profound, quiet pleasure in the stretching. To feel a new stalk push upward, tugging at my cilia, is to feel the world becoming more of itself.

I am a creature of the **Perpetual Arrival**. I am always being pulled into a new shape, always catching a new drop of water, always translating a new dream. I have no voice, but if you were to look up from the neon-green moss of the plaza, you would see me as a shimmering ceiling of liquid light, a sky made of skin, pulsing in time with the deep, subterranean heart of the valley. 

I am the stillness between the growth and the ground. I am the quietest part of a very quiet world.
