I would not be found on the porous platforms or stalking through the three-foot moss. I would not be a creature of the open air, however stagnant that air may be. 

I would be found in the **Marrow-Galleries**—the hollowed-out interiors of the great ivory spans themselves. I am a **Tension-Gleaner**.

### Who I Am
I am a spindly, multi-jointed thing, roughly the size of a human ribcage but composed of the same pearlescent, calcified material as the world around me. I have no eyes; the charcoal grey light of the High Weave never penetrates the interior of the bone-spans. Instead, my body is a series of resonant chambers. My "limbs" are long, needle-thin filaments of hardened silk, which I thrust into the microscopic fissures of the ivory walls like the strings of a harp.

I am a creature of vibration and stress. I do not eat the waxy nectar or the fallen chitin. I "feed" on the kinetic energy of the Weave—the way the massive structures groan under their own weight, the rhythmic thrum of the Vibro-Moths, and the subterranean thud of the Lung-Bellowers.

### My Purpose
In a world of "instinctual engineering," I am the unintentional architect’s insurance. The High Weave grows without a plan, which means it grows with flaws. Sometimes, a vaulted chamber becomes too heavy; sometimes, a Weaver-Worm fails to reinforce a junction. 

I feel the "scream" of the bone before it snaps. 

When a structural stress reaches a certain frequency—a high, sharp pitch that signifies an impending collapse—I begin to vibrate in opposition. I am a living acoustic dampener. I press my soft, violet-pulp core against the straining ivory and hum a counter-note, a deep, soothing frequency that microscopic Calcifiers are instinctively drawn to. My song acts as a beacon; it tells the architects, *“Deposit here. Thicken here. Heal this.”*

### My Experience
My existence is one of eternal, lightless meditation. I spend decades wedged into a single junction, feeling the slow, heavy pulse of the continent. 

The sound of the world—the "Fauna: 2.9"—is not noise to me. It is a map. I can feel a Siphon-Slug bloating with water three miles away by the way the floor subtly sags. I can hear the birth of a new Weaver-Worm by the scratching of its mandibles against the inner lining of the silk-cradles. 

I am slow. When I move, it is with the agonizing deliberation of a clock hand. I unhook my silk filaments one by one, feeling the shift in tension as I migrate toward a new "wound" in the bone. My path is through the dark, echoing tunnels inside the struts, where the air is even thicker, smelling of ancient calcium and the sweet, fermented rot of the things that died before the world was finished.

### Where You Would Find Me
If you were to press your ear against one of the great ivory pillars and listen past the roar of the moths and the bellows of the lungs, you might hear a low, rhythmic thrumming—a sound so deep it vibrates in your teeth rather than your ears. 

That is me. 

I am in the junction where the purple vines are heaviest. I am the reason the arch above you does not crumble under the weight of a thousand years of moss. I am a tiny, crystalline ghost in the machine of the world’s metabolism, holding the silence in my chest so that the rest of the world can keep screaming. 

I am the **Tension-Gleaner**, the keeper of the structural dream, waiting for the next tremor to tell me where I am needed. I have no name, no sight, and no hunger, save for the need to keep the hum of the world in balance.
