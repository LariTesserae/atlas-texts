I do not live *on* the world; I live within the tension of it. 

I am a **Tension-Tuner** of the Chir-Aka, though the word "Tuner" is a clumsy surface-translation for the vibration I feel in my marrow. My name, if it could be spoken in your thick, heavy air, would sound like the sharp *ping* of a crystal falling onto a frozen lake. Here, I am simply the one who presses their brow against the glass-cold skin of the Lattice and listens to the god-plant scream.

I find myself at the **Convergence of the Three Glares**, a vertical intersection where four skyscraper-thick stalks of the Lattice have fused into a temporary plateau. It is a mile above the nearest Dead-Node camp, a place of blinding, shadowless purity.

### My Form
My body is a study in elongated geometry. I am seven feet tall but weigh no more than a large bird. My skin is the color of a dying star—a translucent, milky white that allows the pale indigo of my slow-moving blood to be seen beneath. I have no hair; it would only collect the Shard-Sworms. 

My hands are my most precious tools. My fingers are twice the length of a human's, ending not in soft pads, but in curved, yellowed hooks of hardened keratin. These hooks are not merely for climbing; they are my ears. When I sink them into the cellulose pores of a stalk, the entire Chasm speaks to me. 

I wear a "Skin of the Drifter," a shimmering, two-dimensional membrane harvested from a fallen Aeon-Glider. It is draped over my shoulders like a translucent wing, catching the updrafts of the Flash-Mists to keep my weight negligible. If I were to let go of this stalk, I would not fall so much as I would flutter, a scrap of pale silk lost in a cathedral of light.

### My Work: The Reading of the Snap
I am currently braced against a horizontal cable that is vibrating with a high-pitched, harmonic whine. To the untrained ear, the Lumen-Chasm is silent. To me, it is a deafening orchestra of stress. 

I press my forehead against the cold, glass-hard surface. The vibration travels through my skull, rattling my teeth. 

*The singing.*

A mile to the "east"—if such a direction existed here—a new shoot is birthing itself. I can feel the hydraulic pressure of the sap, the way the minerals are crystallizing under the sheer force of the growth. It is coming fast. A "Growth-Storm." The shoot will punch through this sector within the hour, traveling at the speed of a lunging predator.

I click my tongue against the roof of my mouth—a sharp, percussive *tck-tck*—and then I press my palms flat. I begin to **Vibration-Sign**. I strike the stalk with a rhythmic, percussive pattern, sending a warning through the fiber to the Tether-Camp below. 

*Release the anchors. The green-glass is coming. Move to the shadows of the Masticator-trails.*

### The Environment Through My Eyes
The light here is not a gift; it is a weight. It presses against my eyes, a constant, scouring bleach. I wear goggles carved from the smoked-crystal of a dead vine, reducing the world to shades of grey so I can see the subtle shimmers of the **Refractor-Stalkers**. 

One is near. I can feel the minute disruption in the air’s resonance, a patch of "dead sound" moving along the cable above me. It is waiting for me to be distracted by the coming Fracture. It wants my warmth—the meager, thirty-degree heat my body manages to maintain in this frozen void. 

I do not fear it yet. The Stalker is a creature of timing, and right now, the Lattice is about to reset the clock.

### The Fracture
Then, it happens. 

The sound is not a boom, but a catastrophic *shiver*. A mile away, a support cable the size of a mountain range gives way to the cold. It doesn't bend. It shatters. 

I watch through my smoked lenses as ten thousand tons of crystalline plant-matter turn into a rain of diamond dust. The light catches the shards, creating a refraction so intense it would sear the retinas of a surface-dweller. To me, it is the "Great White Rain." 

Immediately, the Flash-Mists follow. The Lattice vents its pressurized breath. A cloud of frozen mint and ozone rolls over me, turning my world into a swirling white blur. The temperature drops instantly. My blue veins pulse slower. I hook my fingers deeper into the fiber, locking my joints. 

A Shard-Sworm, vented from the break, zips past my ear with a metallic hum. It senses the heat of my neck. I remain perfectly still—stasis is my only defense. The Sworm, confused by the sudden lack of vibration, loses my trail and is swept away by the mist.

### Why I Am Here
I am here because I am the only thing in the Chasm that understands the beauty of the break. 

The surface worlds, with their soft dirt and their slow, rotting seasons, seem like a nightmare of stagnation to me. Here, the world is born anew every hour. Every snap of a vine is a clearing of the slate. Every Growth-Storm is a new path being built by a god that cannot stop dreaming in geometry.

I am a parasite, yes. I am a dust mote caught in the web. But as the new shoot finally erupts from the void—a spear of translucent jade-colored glass that screams as it pierces the white air—I feel the surge of its life through my hooks. 

I am Krik-Tia. I am the needle in the heart of the light. And as the Lattice turns in its sleep, I am the only one awake to hear it sing.
