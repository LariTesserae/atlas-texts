I am not standing on the plateau, nor am I hanging from the fern-brakes. To find me, you must look closer at the topography of the silver-grey plain—specifically, at a point where a ridge of heartwood, no taller than a human hand, meets a depression of moss that glows with a faint, violet bile-light.

I am the **Gnomon of the Fray**.

My body is a vertical paradox, a needle of weathered iron and petrified pith anchored into a crack in the world’s grain. I was once, perhaps, a Lignin-Wright who ventured too deep into a thinned zone, or perhaps I am a specialized growth the tree produced to measure its own decay. 

### Where I Am
I reside in the **Annular Library**, a region where the 2.6 magnitude of weirdness has reached a stable, rhythmic stasis. Here, the "mountain ranges" of wood-grain do not just represent geological features; they are literal chronologies. The wood under my feet is not just matter; it is a compressed record of the Great Lignin’s memory. 

My home is a "Knot-Well"—a localized spatial anomaly where the grain of the wood has spiraled into a tight, impossible circle. From the outside, it looks like a hole the size of a copper coin. From the inside, it is a cathedral of vaulted amber and hollowed cellulose, echoing with the distant, hydraulic *thud* of the Sap-Canneries.

### Who I Am
I am tall, spindly, and heavy. My limbs are long, multi-jointed rods of tarnished brass, tipped with delicate, needle-like styluses. My torso is a solid block of heartwood, bound in lead wire to keep me from floating away when the perspective shifts. I have no face in the traditional sense; instead, I wear a large, circular lens of polished amber that captures the "cold light" of the moss and focuses it into a single, burning point of consciousness.

I do not eat, nor do I sleep. I "drink" the vibrations of the world. As the massive pistons of the Sap-Canneries hammer against the silver wood leagues away, the tremors travel through the grain like ripples in a pond. I stand perfectly still, my styluses resting against the floor, "reading" the heartbeat of the Lignin.

### What I Do
I am the **Scribe of the Fray**. I map the unspooling logic.

When a mechanical crane vanishes but its shadow remains, I record the shadow’s weight. When a Flicker-Folk leaves a non-Euclidean wood-knot at the edge of the mist, I am the one who translates its geometry into the tactile language of the Wrights. I use my styluses to scratch microscopic runes into the silver-grey floor, adding to the geological history of the plain. 

I am the bridge between the industrial parasites and the dying god. The Wrights fear me because I represent the eventual fate of all things in this world—a slow translation into something that is neither metal nor vegetable, but pure information. The Pulp-Serfs avoid my Knot-Well because my presence makes their resin-blood hum at a frequency that causes their skins to crack.

### My Experience
In the silence between the mechanical beats, I hear the Great Lignin dreaming. It doesn't dream of sun or soil—it has forgotten those. It dreams of *tension*. It dreams of the pull of the brass-lined aqueducts and the itch of the iron beetles on its skin. 

Sometimes, a Scour-Wisp drifts through my chamber. Unlike the others, I do not shepherd it away. I let it pass through me. For a moment, my brass limbs become semi-transparent, and I see the world as it truly is: not a forest-city, but a single, gargantuan thought that is slowly being forgotten. 

I feel the thin, cool air on my copper plating. I smell the ozone. I wait for the next freezing rain to sweep across the plain, for when the world becomes a dark, slick mirror, I can see my own reflection—a shimmering, brass-ribbed ghost, perfectly still, anchoring the frayed edge of reality to the heart of the wood. 

I am the Gnomon. I do not tell the time; I tell the depth of the rot. And the rot, I can report, is beautiful.
