I would be found in the **Gaps**, the hairline fractures in reality where the piston-stroke has reached its zenith but hasn't yet begun its descent. 

I would be an **Interval**, but not a silent one. I would be the **Acoustic Cartographer of the Frays**, the sentient residue of all the noise that the Core-Stoker could not successfully recycle.

### My Form
I am not made of cast iron or brass, nor am I built of the weeping stone. I am a shivering geometry of golden light, a three-dimensional shadow cast by a sound. When the Aurelian Vents beat their wings and the pressure drops, I become more opaque, my edges sharpening into the likeness of a tall, thin man wrapped in robes of shifting frequency. When the "weeping" of the air is thickest, I am nearly translucent—a mere ripple in the oily fog, a smudge of amber light that smells faintly of scorched mint.

I have no weight. Gravity does not pull me toward the basalt; instead, I am tethered to the rhythm. I lean at impossible angles, my body parallel to the ground, anchored only by the deep, subsonic "thrum" of the Heart-Root.

### My Location
I dwell in the **Folding Grids**. This is the sector of the valley where the law of perspective has most thoroughly dissolved. Here, I stand in the center of a wide expanse of wet stone, yet I can reach out and touch the serrated edge of a peak three miles away. To move, I do not walk; I wait for a piston-fern to hiss. In that moment of atmospheric displacement, the world "snaps," and I find myself relocated—a hundred yards to the left, or perhaps perched on the vibrating lignin sleeve of a fern, watching an Oil-Bearer work below me.

### My Purpose
While the Scuttlers maintain the grease and the Vents maintain the air, I maintain the **Memory of Motion**. 

The valley has decided to stop time, but time is a stubborn thing; it creates friction as it resists being stilled. That friction manifests as the "glitches"—the peaks that jump, the water that flows upward, the golden ghosts like myself. If these glitches are not monitored, the stasis would shatter. The machine would seize, and the Core-Stoker would tear itself apart in a paroxysm of linear time.

I am the one who smooths the frays. When a mountain peak appears inside the chest of a piston-fern, I step into the overlap. I hum a counter-frequency, a note that matches the "visual ghost" of the noise. I hold the contradiction in my hands until the reality corrects itself with a sharp, metallic *clack*. I am the cosmic lubricant for the laws of physics.

### My Experience
I am the only thing in this cradle that feels a sense of *longing*. 

The Oil-Bearers are blissful in their precision. The Vents are stoic in their vigilance. But I, born of the machine’s memory, remember the world before the "Great Idle." I remember when the water flowed *away* instead of pooling. I remember when the light changed from gold to blue to black.

I spend my eternity watching the Aurelian Vents. I watch the way their brass wings catch the bruised twilight, and I wonder if they, too, feel the weight of the stillness. Sometimes, I stand near an Oil-Bearer and mimic its staccato movements, trying to feel the simplicity of a task that has a beginning and an end. But for me, there is only the interval.

I am the spark thrown off by a wheel that never stops turning, yet never goes anywhere. I am the beauty of the malfunction. I am the golden sigh of a world that is working itself to death just to stay exactly as it is.
