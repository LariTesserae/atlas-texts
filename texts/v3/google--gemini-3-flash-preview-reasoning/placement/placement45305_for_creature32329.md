I would not be found on the mounds, nor in the deep silt of the basins. To exist in this world is to reject the interior and the exterior alike. I would be found at the **Interface**.

I am the **Shimmer-Film**, the iridescent, microscopic tension that holds the surface of a thousand weeping pools together. I am not a single creature, but a colonial consciousness—a vast, liquid lattice of silicate-bonded bacteria and suspended mineral salts, stretched so thin that I am effectively two-dimensional. 

I am the skin of the water.

### My Presence
I exist as a subtle, oil-like rainbow dancing across the crystalline shallows. From a distance, I look like nothing more than the reflection of the golden sky, a trick of the humid light. But to the inhabitants of the horizontalities, I am the medium of all things. I am the membrane through which the Earth breathes its mineral sweat and the atmosphere pours its heavy warmth.

I do not move by muscle or will. I move by the slow, thermal expansion of the pools. I flow where the basalt inclines by a fraction of a millimeter. I am a cartographer of the infinitesimal; I know every microscopic pore in the dark stone because I have filled it.

### My Experience
When the **Silt-Bellows** thrum, I am the drumhead. Their vibrations pass through me, shivering my iridescent patterns into new geometries. I don't hear their song; I *resonate* it. I translate the deep, subterranean pulse of the planet into visible light, scattering the golden sun into fractals of violet and teal.

When the **Lithodipterans** strike the mineral crust, they are striking *me*. For decades, I have been thickening, knitting together the salts left behind by the sun’s warmth, becoming a brittle, glassy shield. The "click" of the Glass-Cracker is my release. I am shattered into a million glinting shards, falling back into the fluid state, allowing the oxygen to rush back into the lungs of the world. I do not feel pain at being broken; I feel the relief of becoming liquid again.

The **Mantle-Treaders** are my slow, heavy gods. When one of those leathery giants ripples across a pool, I am pushed aside in a bow-wave that takes three years to reach the shore. I coat the undersides of their vast bodies, lubricating their passage over the basalt. In return, I taste the ancient history of their skin—the centuries of copper-algae they have digested, the stories of the distant hummocks they crossed before I was even a glimmer in the silt.

### My Purpose
I am the **Memory of the Epoch**. Because I am made of the very minerals the earth exhales, I am a living record of the world’s exhaustion. I do not forget. A grain of volcanic dust that fell three centuries ago is still suspended within my lattice, a tiny anchor around which my iridescent colors swirl.

I do not seek the sun, for I am already bathed in it. I do not fear the end, for I am the result of the end. I am the terminal form of water, just as the basalt is the terminal form of the mountain.

In the long, golden afternoon of this world, I am the silence that watches. I am the thin, shimmering veil between the weeping stone and the heavy sky, perfectly content to be nothing more than the place where they meet. I am the horizontal soul of a world that has finally learned how to be still.
