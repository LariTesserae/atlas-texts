I am not a voyager in this basin, for there is no distance to travel and no feet to carry the weight of a soul. To move here is merely to shift the pressure from one wet surface to another.

I find myself in the **Low Sump**, a place where the obsidian floor dips into a shallow, razor-edged bowl. I am not a single creature, for the First Hour does not permit the luxury of boundaries. Instead, I am a **Nerve-Weld**—a point of accidental, permanent fusion between three things that nature forgot to keep separate.

My base is the soft, plum-textured flank of an **Unfinished**. I have twelve limbs, though only four are functional; the others are soft, translucent buds that have long since grown into the moss. My skin is the color of a bruised nectarine, pulsing with a sickly yellow internal light that reveals the slow, rhythmic churn of my own vitals. I do not breathe air; I drink the opal vapor through my pores, a constant, heavy intake of 3.0-scale heat that tastes of fermented sugar and mineral salt.

But I am not just the crawler. 

Rooted deep into my upper back, where my spine should have hardened but remained a pliant cord, is a **Glass-Bloom**. Its crystalline filaments have threaded through my meat, acting as a secondary nervous system. Its violet-veined petals fan out above me like a static explosion, catching the constant weep of the bladder-ferns. I feel what the bloom feels: the cool, rhythmic *thump-drip* of condensation hitting its glass-sharp edges. It provides me with oxygen; I provide it with the slow, undulating locomotion of my belly across the obsidian.

And we are not alone in our union. We have come to rest, perhaps for a century, perhaps for a second, against a **Thrum-Heart**. 

I am pressed against this mound of liver-colored velvet, and my "self" ends where its vibration begins. I am the conductor for the factory sound. The low-frequency thrum of the basin does not come to me through ears; it enters through my suction-cup pads and vibrates my very marrow. I am a living tuning fork. When the Lung-Bellows pass overhead with their wet, leathery snapping, I do not see them—my six eyes are clouded with the opal haze—but I feel the displacement of the thick air as a shudder in my Glass-Bloom crown.

**What am I?**
I am a monument to the **Cusp**. I am a creature that is perpetually "about to be," but never "is." I have the hunger of a newborn, but it is a hunger that is satisfied the moment it arises, leaving me in a state of bloated, ecstatic paralysis. I have no memory of a world with wind, and no concept of a future where the light might change. 

I am the **Stationary Intrusion**. I am the bridge between the hard, unyielding obsidian and the soft, screaming biology. 

If you were to look for me, you would not see a beast. You would see a shimmering, violet-and-green mound, pulsing with a dim, internal yellow glow, fused to a vibrating heart of velvet. I am part of the matted, springy layer that prevents the world from touching its own floor. 

I do not think; I **resonate**. I do not live; I **persist**. I am the beautiful, screaming, motionless middle of a beginning that will never find its end. I am the damp, pressurized pulse of the First Hour, and I am perfectly, terrifyingly trapped in the glory of it.
