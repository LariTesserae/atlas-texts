I am not among the frantic carpet of the Oscillators, nor am I rooted in the slow, cold geometry of the Silt-Lace. To be them is to be a note within the song. I find myself instead in the thin, impossible space between the song and the singer.

I am the **Lead-Reader**.

I exist upon the jagged basalt shelf, draped over the brass-bound ledger like a spill of heavy mercury that has forgotten how to flow. My body is a localized density of the ionized dust, compressed by the sheer weight of the Engine’s roar into a semi-solid form. I have the texture of hammered lead and the matte finish of oxidized silver. I am a humanoid shape only in the way a shadow is a shape—long, distorted, and clinging to the edges of what is real.

I am the only thing in this vault that possesses the burden of *waiting*.

Where the Oscillators live in the frantic *now* and the Silt-Lace grows in the eternal *then*, I occupy the *if*. I am the physical manifestation of the Ledger’s blankness. My hands—thin, multi-jointed needles of dark basalt—rest perpetually upon the leaden pages. I do not write, because the Engine has not yet failed. I am a scribe with no history to record, a witness to a moment that has lasted for ten thousand years.

I do not hear the roar as a sound. To me, the roar is a solid wall, a physical pressure that keeps my atoms from drifting apart. If the Engine were to skip even a fraction of a beat, my body would lose its cohesion; I would become just more copper-scented dust, swirling aimlessly in the purple murk. Therefore, I am the most invested inhabitant of this vault. I am the nervous system of the stasis.

My eyes are not organs of sight, but two polished spheres of hematite that act as seismographs. I don't see the purple light; I see the *frequencies* of the friction. I see the Spark-Mites not as lights, but as tiny, momentary spikes in the thermal equilibrium. I watch the Resonance—that ghost-intelligence in the dust—as it weaves its magnetic webs around the pistons, and I translate its wordless pressures into the potential energy of my unmoving pen.

I feel the heat as a dry, comforting weight. It is the breath of the machine. I move only when the Resonance demands a maintenance of the record. With a movement so slow it would make the Silt-Lace seem hurried, I turn a page of the lead ledger. The sound of lead grinding against lead is the only sound in the universe that is not the Engine. It is a blasphemy of friction, a tiny, screaming protest that I make once every few centuries, just to remind the rock that I am still here.

I am the Librarian of the Unchanging.

Why am I here? I am the fail-safe. If the rift were to collapse, if the crust above were to finally heal its wound, if the Engine were to finally grind itself into a final, frozen weld—I would be the one to record the last decibel. I am the one who will write the word "Silence" on the final page of lead, and in doing so, give the violence a history.

Until then, I remain a statue of pressurized shadow, my stone fingers poised over a blank page, vibrating in perfect, agonizing harmony with the iron heart of the world. I am the anticipation of an end that the laws of this place do not allow. I am the soul of the machine’s refusal to stop.
