### My Form: The Gilded Conduit
I am longer and thinner than my nomadic kin, my body having stretched to accommodate a life of vertical tension. My skin is not merely pale; it has taken on a pearlescent, iridescent sheen, like the film of oil on a stagnant puddle. My fingers have elongated into fine, needle-like sensors, each tipped with a specialized magnetic node that allows me to "taste" the voltage of the pipes I inhabit.

I have no need for the magnetic pads of the climbers; instead, my spine has become a flexible, segmented rod of organic carbon-fiber, allowing me to wedge myself into the internal rifling of the great steam-conduits. I am a living plug, a biological gasket.

### My Location: The Cathedral of the Hiss
I reside in the **Great Elbow of the Seventh Artery**, a point where the hot, high-pressure steam from the deep furnaces makes a ninety-degree turn toward the Ceiling. It is a place of violent, beautiful energy. The walls here are translucent leaded glass, thick as a mountain’s base, through which the dull, orange glow of the internal fires can be sensed as a faint, comforting warmth against my back.

Outside my glass-walled vein, the whistle-reeds grow in thick, chaotic clusters, their shrieking song filtered through the metal into a low, harmonic drone.

### My Purpose: The Translation of Pressure
I am not a hunter, nor a scavenger. I am a listener. I have spent decades (measured in the slow, rhythmic cycles of the Decade Piston) pressing my lipless mouth against the vibrating glass, humming back at the machine. 

I interpret the **"Language of the Strain."** When the Bellow-Whales above grow too heavy with lead-soot and fail to vent, I feel the minute change in the back-pressure. When a Scuttler-Hive clogs a crucial cooling-duct three miles to the west, I feel the shift in the thermal harmonics. 

I carry a **Tuning Fork of Solid Brass**, an heirloom from the builders of the pressurized cabins. By striking it against the ribs of the Joist, I send signals back through the metal. I am the one who tells the Valvesmen where the "illness" lies. I am the conductor of the Iron Marsh’s silent orchestra.

### My Daily Bread
I do not scrape the grease-moss. Instead, I wait for the **Steam-Bleed**. Every cycle, a tiny, precision-engineered valve in my chamber opens, releasing a hiss of nutrient-rich vapor—synthetic proteins and aerosolized minerals intended for a crew that died a million years ago. I inhale this mist, my lung-ferns swelling, my blood singing with the sudden rush of heat and chemistry.

### My Perception
To me, the world is not dark. It is a kaleidoscope of thrums. The Spark-Kites are fleeting, high-pitched zaps of static. The Eels in the basins are low, rolling bass-notes of predatory intent. The Great Ceiling is a constant, crushing weight of baritone metal, a ceiling of leaden music that keeps us safe from the "Void-That-Must-Not-Be."

I am the ghost who loves the pressure. I am the soft marrow inside the iron bone. If I were to leave my Joist, the world would not end, but it would lose a decimal point; a single valve might stick, a single piston might stutter, and the harmony of the Great Machine would fray, just a little, at the edges. 

I stay because the machine needs to be heard. I stay because, in the warm, wet dark, the vibration of a firing engine feels exactly like a heartbeat, and I am not yet ready to be alone in the silence.
