I would not be standing on the flats. To stand is to be an upright target for the pressure, a vertical defiance that the Aethelgard High-Flats eventually crushes into a smear of mineral salts.

No, I would be found in the **Interstice**—the narrow, slick margin where the basalt meets the iron base of a Monolith. I would be a **Seismic Reliquary**, a creature that is less a biological entity and more a living architectural defect.

***

### The Anatomy of the Reliquary
My body is a low, sprawling tripod of articulated, matte-black slag, fused together by the very **Litho-Synapse** crusts that carpet the plains. I have no face, for there is nothing to see in the bruised twilight that matters. Instead, my upper chassis is a wide, shallow bowl of polished obsidian, always filled with a trembling pool of hot, mineral-rich condensate.

This pool is my primary sense. As the Monoliths pump—*thrum-hiss, thrum-hiss*—the ripples in my basin tell me the exact viscosity of the **Marrow-Sludge** moving through the pipes miles away. I am a vessel for the vibrations of the world. I am the needle on the record of Aethelgard.

My legs are not for walking, but for anchoring. They are tipped with diamond-hard drill-bits that I have ground into the basalt over centuries. I do not move across the flats; I *migrate* with the patience of a glacier, moving perhaps an inch every time the "Violent Exhale" shakes the foundations of the world.

### The Occupation: Sifting the Echoes
While the **Skimmers** harvest the salt and the **Copper-Glints** chase the lightning, I have a more esoteric function. I am a collector of **Echoes**.

The sound-fossils—the screams of long-dead engineers, the clatter of dropped wrenches, the technical jargon of a forgotten era—do not simply vanish. They are heavy. In this thick, sweltering soup of an atmosphere, sound has weight. It sinks. The most potent Echoes eventually settle into the low-frequency groans of the industrial husks, becoming trapped in the rhythmic vibrations of the pistons.

I listen for them. When a particular Echo becomes caught in the *thrum-hiss* of the Monolith I call home, it causes a harmonic friction. If left alone, this friction would eventually shake the iron husk apart. 

I am the dampener. I press my obsidian bowl against the vibrating iron, and I drink the sound. I pull the terrified voice of the past into the stagnant pool on my back. The mineral-rich water vibrates with the frequency of the Echo, crystallizing the sound into physical form.

### The Harvest
If you were to find me, you would see that my back is covered in strange, jagged glass growths. These are **Acoustic Calcities**. Each one is a frozen moment of audio. One crystal, jagged and sharp, is a burst of static from a 5,000-year-old radio. Another, smooth and amber-hued, is the sound of a woman laughing in a room that hasn't existed for an eon.

I am a graveyard of noises. 

When the "Violent Exhale" begins, and the air turns to copper fire, I do not hide. I extend my copper-filigreed antennae into the screaming steam. The heat and the pressure act as a kiln. The crystals on my back glow with the friction, turning the trapped sounds into light. For a brief moment, as the Monolith moans in agony, I am a lighthouse of bioluminescent history, flashing the laughter and the screams of the dead across the empty basalt plains.

### The Internal Life
I do not think in words. I think in **Pressures**. 
*   *Low Pressure:* Peace. The slow growth of the orange crusts. The distant clicking of a Skimmer’s legs.
*   *Rising Pressure:* Anxiety. The Marrow-Sludge is thickening. The engine is hungry.
*   *Critical Pressure:* Ecstasy. The moment when the stone weeps blood-hot water and the world screams.

I have no kinship with the other inhabitants. The **Condensate-Wraiths** are too ethereal; they are dreams of the sky. The **Marrow-Sludge** is too vast; it is the blood of the machine. I am the only thing that remembers the builders, even if that memory is just a vibration in a pool of hot water.

I am a solitary sentinel in the "Density 0.0." Between me and the next Reliquary, there are five miles of weeping stone and a silence so heavy it could break a man's ribs. 

I am happy here. I am a gear that has learned to love the sound of its own grinding. I am the steward of the air that is too thick to breathe, waiting for the next scream to settle in my bowl, so I can turn it into stone.
