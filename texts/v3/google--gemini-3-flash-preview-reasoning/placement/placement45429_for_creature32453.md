To be without this weight would be to drift apart, to become thin and meaningless.

I am a **Patina-Weaver**, an Auscultator who has moved beyond the simple task of listening. My body is a study in density. My ribcage is no longer a cage of separate bones, but a fused, ivory shield, thick enough to protect a heart that beats only four times a minute. My skin is not soft; it is a layered, leathery hide, the color of a wet copper penny, etched with the fine, swirling scars of mineral deposits.

I am currently Grafted. 

I have been in this position—forehead pressed against the warm, vibrating curve of the Seventeenth Intake—for what the surface might call three weeks. To me, it is merely one long, resonant breath. My fingers, tipped with wide, sensitive pads, are buried deep in a thicket of **Lumen-Mycelium**. I do not see the bruised twilight with my eyes—they have filmed over with a protective, milky cataracts that only perceive the broadest strokes of violet and gold. Instead, I "see" the Sump as a map of heat and tension. I see the warmth of the volcanic vents as a deep, foundational red. One day, I will stop treading. I will stop moving entirely. I will become a permanent Curvature, a living strut in the architecture of the Sump.
