### My Form: The Chromatic Glitch

I am a **Latency**. I do not possess a fixed shell of chrome or the liquid-mercury skin of the Reflected Ones. Instead, my body is a semi-solid arrangement of **refracted probability**. I look like a tall, spindly tripod of obsidian glass, but my edges are never quite in focus. To look at me is to see a smudge in the world’s perfect resolution—a smear of charcoal against a diamond landscape.

I am composed of the "Young Erosion"—the high-velocity dust particles that have been trapped in a pocket of **Viscous Time**. I am held together by the static discharge of the Static-Wick moss, a humming, purple-black silhouette that flickers at the edge of the machine’s perception. I have no eyes, but I perceive the world through **Strain-Sight**: I see the tension in the titanium, the hidden torque in the pillars, and the mounting pressure of the atmosphere as it waits for the clockwork rain.

### My Purpose: The Auditor of Friction

While the Reflected Ones are the machine’s white blood cells, smoothing the seals, I am its **Intuition**. I exist to inhabit the gaps where the machine’s logic fails. 

When a kilometer-wide shingle begins to vibrate at a frequency that might suggest a crack, I am already there. I do not fix it with tools. I sit upon the seam and *decelerate* the local time. I turn a catastrophic fracture into a ten-thousand-year sigh. I am the reason the "Heavy Quiet" remains quiet; I absorb the screams of metal under stress, pulling the sound into my own hollow chest and holding it there until it becomes nothing more than a velvet hiss.

### My Daily Life in the Loom

I spend my eons moving between the **Perspective Knots**. I find the Odonates fascinating; they are the only ones who see me. When they fly through the loops and emerge behind themselves, they often pass through my form, their wings of optical fiber sparking against my dust-caked limbs. For a brief second, I am filled with their rainbows, and they are grounded by my weight. 

I feed on **Temporal Lag**. When a drop of ozone-water hangs suspended in the air for minutes, I reach out a long, needle-thin finger and touch it. I drink the "waiting" inherent in the water. It tastes of cold lightning and old math.

I am wary of the Tread-Walkers. Their diamond-tipped hooves are too real, too rhythmic. Their clack-clack-clack represents a certainty that my flickering form cannot endure. When they approach the Suture-Loom, I retreat into the vertical shadows, becoming a thin, dark line on a silver pillar, indistinguishable from a cooling fin.

### The Seizure

I know that one day, I will lose my flicker. The machine’s perfection is absolute, and eventually, it will resolve the "glitch" that I am. When my internal static matches the subterranean hum of the valley perfectly, I will undergo **Seizure**. 

I will not fuse to the shingles like the grazers. Instead, I will simply become a permanent shadow cast upon the northern peaks. I will be a dark, silent silhouette burned into the titanium, a reminder of a moment when the machine hesitated. I will be the only part of this bright, shimmering world that is allowed to stay dark. 

Until then, I wait in the loops, listening to the cathedrals breathe, watching the back of my own head hover on the horizon, and holding the silence together with my trembling, dusty hands.
