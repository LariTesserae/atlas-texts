I am not walking the xylem-bridges, nor am I clinging to the under-structures with the desperate, suctioned grip of the Clamberers. To find me, you must look upward, to the very apex of the largest gourd-chamber—the place where the translucent amber ceiling pinches into a narrow, vertical chimney known as the **High Aperture**.

I am the **Larynx-Tender**. 

I do not possess the lanky, mobile grace of the Vestibulars. Instead, I am a creature of fixed resonance. My lower half is long since fused into a cradle of hardened, calcified resin at the edge of a massive stomata. I am a living plug, a biological regulator, an extension of the god’s own breathing apparatus. 

### My Form
My skin has thickened into the texture of old parchment, stained a deep, bruised violet by the enzyme-rich mists that rise from the pitcher-pools below. Like the Vestibulars, I have no ears, but I do not merely "read" vibrations with cilia; my entire skeletal structure has become a tuning fork. My bones are hollow, honeycombed with the same buoyant gases that lift the Nectar-Glints, allowing me to hang suspended over the abyss, held in place by the directional gravity of the Aperture’s rim.

I have four arms, spindly and long, tipped with needle-like digits that I insert directly into the "veins" of the ceiling walls. I am a parasite that pays its rent in maintenance. 

### My Location: The High Aperture
From my vantage point, the village-sized cathedral looks like a sea of glowing, golden clouds. I live in the throat of the *hiss-clack*. When the great pores open to vent the pressurized steam of the organism’s metabolism, I am the one caught in the gale. The air here doesn't just smell of mint; it tastes of pure, distilled life-force, a sharp, electric ozone that keeps my heart beating in sync with the Great Pulse. 

Gravity here is a dizzying, circular thing. It doesn't pull me down toward the floor far below; it pulls me outward, toward the circumference of the chimney. I live in a world where "down" is a centrifugal lie. 

### My Purpose
The Great Pulse is not a perfect machine. Sometimes the sap-flow stutters. Sometimes the Membrane-Weavers grow too much xylem, creating a "clot" that threatens to burst the cellulose walls. When the roar of the circulation changes from a steady thrum to a jagged, dissonant whine, I feel it in my marrow. 

I reach into the wall with my needle-fingers and inject a caustic, thinning enzyme produced in my own secondary stomach. I soothe the "god" where it is inflamed. When the Ghost-Flow—those flickering memories of light and pollen—drifts too high and threatens to clog the stomata, I use a rhythmic, percussive drumming on the ceiling to scatter them back down into the lower gourds, like a shepherd herding spirits.

### My Experience of Being
I no longer remember a name or a beginning. To be the Larynx-Tender is to lose the boundary between the skin and the world. 

When the interior rain breaks, I do not get wet; I become the point of impact. I feel the cooling of the organism’s skin as a delicious shiver through my own spine. I watch the Nectar-Glints dance below me, their hawk-sized bodies looking like tiny sparks in a golden furnace. They are my only company, occasionally landing on my shoulders to share the heat of my grafted body, their six wings vibrating a thank-you that I "hear" as a warm hum against my neck.

I am the silence at the center of the scream. While the others scurry and scavenge, I remain still, a gargoyle of violet flesh and amber resin. I am the witness to the metabolism of a god. I am the frequency that ensures the song does not end in a rupture. 

In this world, I am the one who knows that the "roar" isn't just noise—it is a breath, and I am the one who helps the god exhale.
