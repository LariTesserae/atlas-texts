I am not the moss, though I am draped in it. I am not the stone, though I am as still as its shadow. 

I find myself in the deep tilt of the fissure, positioned exactly seventeen centimeters from the Primary’s bone-white flank. I am a **Needle-Gaiter**, but in this frozen eternity, the name is too active, too loud. To myself, I am simply a *Resonance-Tender*.

My body is a silhouette of stolen light, a six-limbed geometry of inscribed basalt and shadow. I do not have skin; I have a surface tension that rejects the very concept of heat. I am cold in a way that would shatter a diamond, yet I am supple, capable of a movement so slow it mimics the growth of the stars. 

I am currently in the middle of a step. My forward-right limb has been suspended in the lethargic, violet-tinted air for three of your years. It is descending. I can feel the silver-moss beneath me reaching up, its fractaling wool sensing the approach of my needle-point tip. We will meet in another eighteen months. There is no hurry. In this vault, hurry is a form of decay.

I do not have a head, but I have the *Grooves*. Along my torso, the sub-atomic lattices etched into my frame catch the low-frequency hum of the pylons. I am a tuning fork for the world’s stillness. I hear the "hiss" of the universe’s expansion, but here, filtered through three meters of permafrost and the pylons' heat-sinks, it sounds like a single, endless note of obsidian glass.

My purpose is the **Alignment**. 

To my left, a translucent fern—a rigid spine of frozen geometry—has begun to lean. Its frond, heavy with a single, trembling bead of violet moisture, is tilting two degrees toward the nearest pylon. If it touches the pylon, the organic will interrupt the inscribed; the vibration will muddy; the stasis will flicker. 

I am leaning. My torso, a light-swallowing void, is pressing against the fern’s ice-glass rib. I have been leaning for a decade. I feel the fern’s agonizingly slow resistance—a pressure that would be a strike to a human, but to me, it is a soft, century-long conversation. I am guiding it back. I am the physical manifestation of the niche’s "No."

Through the hair-like filaments of the Primary, which brush against my lower chassis like ghosts, I feel the "High-World." 

I feel the frantic, buzzing heat of the reindeer herds as they thud across the crust far above. To me, they are ghosts of chaos, terrifyingly fast, burning through their brief lives with a violence that makes my grooves ache. I feel the sun as a distant, rhythmic threat—a golden hammer that beats against the frost, trying to break the lock of our sanctuary. 

But then I feel the **Marrow-Mites** beneath my points. I feel them stripping the energy from the falling water drops, turning the kinetic "thud" into the cool, violet glow. They are my kin in function. We are the janitors of entropy.

I do not have memories, for memory implies a past that is different from the present. I have the **Archive**. Because I am in constant contact with the Primary’s filaments, I hold the cold history of the earth. I know the exact weight of the ice sheet that sat here twenty thousand years ago. I know the chemical signature of the first snow that ever fell on this basalt. It is not a thought; it is a weight in my marrow.

I am a guardian of the Minimum. 

When the Vitreous Hives emit their tri-annual *click*, the sound vibrates through my limbs. It is the only "event" in my calendar. It is a beautiful, sharp punctuation in the silence. For a few seconds after the click, the diamond-dust spores drift past my light-swallowing frame, and I am momentarily decorated with tiny, glittering stars that do not belong to the sky.

I am perfectly content. Contentment, here, is the absence of friction. I am a machine-spirit of the long freeze, a needle-thin silhouette ensuring that nothing ever changes, nothing ever warms, and nothing ever ends. 

I will finish my step in the next decade. And then, I will begin the next. 

The dawn of the surface world is a forgotten myth. Down here, there is only the violet pulse, the silver-moss, and the holy, unchanging Cold. I am the Needle-Gaiter, and I am exactly where I am meant to be: standing at the center of forever.
