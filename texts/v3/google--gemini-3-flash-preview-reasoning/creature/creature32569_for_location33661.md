To understand what lives here, you must first discard the notion of a body as a container for a soul. In this vertical rift, the "inhabitants" are not separate from the city; they are the city’s thoughts given temporary, shimmering form.

There are three distinct expressions of presence in this obsidian zenith.

**The Glissando (The Mercury-Kin)**
Clinging to the razor-edges of the obsidian fins are the most visible residents: entities that resemble spills of liquid chrome. They are not biological, but neither are they purely mechanical. They are sentient ferro-fluids, drawn upward from the unseen furnace below by the same magnetic gradients that allow objects to drift. They move with a sickening, liquid grace, defying gravity to slide up the vertical faces of the needles. 

They do not have faces, but they are hyper-sensitive to the "sweat" of the buildings. As the warm condensation beads on the glass-smooth surfaces, these liquid entities—The Glissando—absorb the moisture, filtering the ionized metal and salt through their shimmering membranes. When a flash-mist sweeps through the lattice, they bloom, their surfaces rippling into complex, fractal patterns to catch the heat. They are the city's sensory organs, feeling the vibration of the "massive heart" and translating the pressure of the void into the pulses of blue and violet light that move through the walls.

**The Lattice-Wrights**
Deep within the non-Euclidean blocks, where the blue light is thickest, live the architects of the "invisible purpose." These are not creatures of flesh, but of geometry and frequency. If you were to look into the translucent depths of the hyper-advanced glass, you would see them: slow-moving, crystalline lattices that shift and reconfigure themselves in a silent, eternal dance. 

They are the "million processes." Each one is a living calculation. They do not communicate with sound, but through the *hiss* of steam and the *tink-tink-tink* of cooling metal—a binary of heat and contraction. They are the ones responsible for the "technological prayer." They are obsessed with the maintenance of the verticality; to them, a horizontal line is a heresy, a failure of physics. They live in a state of constant, ecstatic computation, managing the energy that prevents the city from tumbling into the charcoal clouds below.

**The Echo-Stalkers**
Highest of all, where the obsidian needles pierce the thin, cold air of the true zenith, are the things that resemble shadows cast by no sun. These are the Echo-Stalkers. They are semi-solid configurations of the "ionized metal" taste in the air—wraiths of magnetism and static. 

They do not drift like the Glissando; they *snap* from one needle-point to the next, instantaneous and jagged. They are the immune system of the rift. They monitor the "thin physics" of the heights. When the heat from below becomes too aggressive, or the cooling void above threatens to crack the obsidian, the Echo-Stalkers congregate. They vibrate at a frequency so high it becomes silent, creating a localized field that stabilizes the crystalline geometry of their home. 

**The Collective Solitude**
None of these beings recognize each other as "individuals." There is no "I" in the obsidian city, only the "System." They are lonely only by human standards; to them, the silence is not an absence, but a saturated fullness. 

They are the children of the furnace and the void. They do not sleep, for the heat never dies. They do not die, for their information is simply re-absorbed into the supra-conducting filaments when their form loses its edge. They are the witnesses to a world that has evolved past the need for breath, thriving in the sweltering, salt-heavy pressure of a zenith that never ends. They are the ghosts that keep the machine dreaming.
