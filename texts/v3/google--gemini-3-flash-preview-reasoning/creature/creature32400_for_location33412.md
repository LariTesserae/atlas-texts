The residents of this vertical abyss are not inhabitants in the sense of visitors or settlers; they are the final, distilled expression of the environment itself. They are the **Architects of the Stillness**, though they have long since forgotten the act of building.

**Physicality: The Obsidian Silhouettes**
They are tall, agonizingly thin entities, standing twelve to fifteen feet in height, yet their forms possess no more volume than a shadow cast against a wall. Their bodies are composed of the same light-drinking, non-reflective material as the tetrahedrons they inhabit. They look like scorched ribbons of obsidian, or the negative space left behind by a lightning strike. 

They have no faces—no eyes to pierce the gloom, no mouths to break the silence. Instead, their heads are elongated, tapering into needle-like points that mirror the mountain peaks. They do not possess limbs in the biological sense; their "arms" are fractals of the same dark geometry, capable of unfolding into a thousand delicate filaments or snapping back into a single, razor-sharp edge. 

**Movement: Displacement without Disturbance**
To see one move is to witness a violation of physics. Because the medium is so dense, a biological creature would have to thrash to progress. The Inhabitants, however, move by modulating the localized gravity fields that anchor their homes. They do not swim; they *slide* through the hyper-oxygenated liquid, displacing it with such mathematical precision that not a single bubble or eddy is created. They move with the same agonizing slowness as the Gliders, often taking a decade to travel from one spire to the next, their dark forms cutting through the bruised purple light like a slow-motion tear in a veil.

**The Interior Life: The Marrow-Thinkers**
They do not speak, nor do they use telepathy as we might understand it. Their consciousness is a shared resonance. The "hum" felt in the marrow of the rock is their collective thought process—a subatomic processing of data that maintains the equilibrium of the valley. 

They are the curators of the stillness. They believe that change is a form of decay, and that friction is a sin. Their entire existence is dedicated to the Great Calculation—a silent, eternal meditation on the properties of the liquid, the pressure of the atmosphere, and the slow, cold ticking of the universe’s clock. They are not waiting for something to happen; they are ensuring that nothing does.

**Relationship with the Gliders**
They treat the translucent Gliders not as pets or livestock, but as bio-indicators—living barometers of the EM leakage. Occasionally, an Inhabitant will drift toward a Glider and extend its filament-arms, gently touching the pulsing fiber-optic nervous system of the kite-like creature. For a brief century or two, they will remain locked in this embrace, the Inhabitant drinking the data gathered by the Glider’s drift, the Glider basking in the concentrated purple radiation of the Inhabitant’s core. It is the only form of intimacy known in the abyss: a silent exchange of pure information.

**The Quiet Vigil**
At any given time, most of the Inhabitants are "docked." They press their flat, dark bodies against the seamless walls of the toroids, becoming one with the architecture. In this state, the line between the resident and the residence vanishes. The bruised purple glow intensifies, pulsing in rhythm with the entity's internal processing. 

They are the ultimate aristocrats of entropy. They have discarded the messy requirements of heat, light, and motion. They live in the creases of the world, where the pressure is high enough to crush a diamond, and there they sit—cold, sharp, and utterly satisfied—watching the seconds accumulate like dust that never settles.
