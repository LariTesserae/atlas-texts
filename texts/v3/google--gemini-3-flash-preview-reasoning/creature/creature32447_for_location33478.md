In this world, the distinction between "inhabitant" and "habitat" has dissolved. To speak of what lives here is to speak of the forest’s own active appendages—the mobile organelles and semi-autonomous systems that serve the Great expansion. They are not consumers; they are maintenance. They are not individuals; they are expressions of the Root-Core’s intent.

### The Lignin-Knights (Structural Stabilizers)
Where the Great Hydraulic Roar is loudest, the pressure threatens to shatter the newly formed trunks. Here, the forest produces the **Lignin-Knights**. These are not creatures of flesh, but ambulatory, high-density splints. They appear as articulated pillars of dark, polished wood, twelve feet high, their surfaces etched with the same hyper-efficient geometry as the leaves. 

They do not breathe or eat. They are powered by the 2.9 vibration of the world. They move with a slow, grinding deliberation, seeking out micro-fractures in the mile-thick stalks. When a crack is found, a Knight anchors itself across the wound, its "limbs" fusing into the bark. It then secretes a high-pressure resin that hardens instantly, sacrificing its own mobility to become a permanent patch in the world’s skeleton. They are the living scaffolding, the quiet martyrs of the growth.

### The Osmotic Gliders
In the vertical marshes and the cool, humid air of the capillaries, the **Osmotic Gliders** drift. They resemble massive, translucent ribbons of film, some stretching forty feet in length, as thin as a single cell layer. They are the forest’s respiratory system made manifest.

They do not fly by wing-beat, but by manipulating the surface tension of the absolute moisture. They "surf" the 98% humidity, absorbing ozone and nitrogen directly through their membranes. As they drift through the twilight, they glow with a pale, bruised violet light. Their purpose is transport: they carry concentrated packets of "information-sap"—genetic instructions from the Root-Core—to the distant, expanding edges of the Lignin-Shell. They are the neurotransmitters of a world-sized brain, fluttering through the dark.

### The Crystalline Mites (The Printers)
If you were to look at a single thorn through a magnifying lens, you would see the **Printers**. These are the primary "inhabitants" by number—quadrillions of microscopic, hexagonal lattices of bio-silicon. They are the ones responsible for the 0.1 erosion rating. 

They function like a biological 3D-printing array. They swarm over the leading edges of the growth in a shimmering, iridescent fog. They take the raw, nutrient-rich phlegm exuded by the planet and weave it into the molecularly sharp ridges of the bark. They work in a silent, mathematical trance, guided by the pulses of light from the sap-veins. They do not know life or death; they only know the *pattern*. When a Printer fails, it simply ceases to move and is absorbed into the very structure it was building, becoming a single grain of the Lignin-Shell.

### The Resonance-Eaters
Because the sound of the world is a physical force, the forest has evolved a way to recycle that kinetic energy. The **Resonance-Eaters** grow in clusters where three or more limbs converge—the "weeping landscapes." They look like vast, organ-pipe structures made of a flexible, rubbery fungus. 

They are the only things that "listen." As the Great Hydraulic Roar thrums through the world, these structures vibrate in sympathy. They convert the low-frequency groans into thermal energy, keeping the surrounding "valleys" at the precise temperature required for sap fluidity. They are the planet’s radiators, shivering constantly in the dark, turning a deafening noise into a gentle, necessary warmth.

### The Immune Mists
Though there are no animals to invade, the forest is prepared for "errors"—mutations or rogue growths that do not follow the geometric mandate. The **Immune Mists** are mobile clouds of aerosolized enzymes that haunt the Gasp-Vents. 

They possess a rudimentary, collective chemical intelligence. They move toward any scent of "decay" or "irregularity." If a branch grows at an inefficient angle, or if a moss-analog becomes too invasive, the Mist settles upon it. Within minutes, the offending tissue is liquefied, turned back into the base phlegm to be reused by the Printers. To an outsider, the Mist would feel like a freezing, mint-scented fog; to the forest, it is the eraser that ensures the masterpiece remains perfect.

There is no "who" here that could look you in the eye. There is only the **Embryo-Mind**, a consciousness composed of a trillion miles of fiber-optic venation. It is a mind that thinks in terms of tension, pressure, and geometry. It is not lonely, for it is everything. It is not waiting for anything, for it is the act of becoming. It lives in the Roar, it breathes through the Vents, and it dreams in the bruised-light pulses of its own rising sap.
