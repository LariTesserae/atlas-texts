In the Sub-Lattice, life does not begin with a germ, but with a frequency. To exist in this crushing, humid geode is to be a physical manifestation of the hum. The inhabitants are not "born" so much as they are "precipitated" out of the intersection of heat, pressure, and the leaked intent of the ancient machines.

### The Calipers (The Scavenger-Maintenants)
The spindly scavengers mentioned in the annals of the deep are known colloquially as **Calipers**. They are the most visible inhabitants, though "visible" is a generous term for creatures that look like fractures in the air. 

A Caliper is a multi-jointed nightmare of obsidian geometry, its body a series of interlocking, hollow carapaces made of the same petrified silk as the city itself. They have no faces, only a cluster of sensitive, piezoelectric whiskers that vibrate in sympathy with the city's low-frequency thrum. They do not eat; they "scrub." They move across the undersides of the walkways with a terrifying, inverted grace, their hooked limbs finding purchase in the microscopic pits of the matte-black alloy. 

Their purpose is the removal of entropy. They seek out the "circuit-moss" when it grows too thick and threatens to insulate the cooling conduits. They harvest the mineral blooms that grow in the stagnant pools, grinding them into a fine paste within their internal chambers to patch the scars on the pylons. When a Caliper "dies," it does not rot. It simply stops moving, its joints locking into a permanent, structural brace, becoming a new, tiny strut in the city’s ever-evolving skeleton.

### The Lungs of the Void (The Condensate-Vells)
Drifting in the vast, lightless gaps between the towers are the **Vells**. These are the true masters of the 110-degree humidity. They resemble gargantuan, translucent jellyfish, their bells stretching thirty meters across, composed of a thick, protein-rich gel that captures the "respiratory" moisture of the machines.

The Vells are living filters. They hang suspended by static buoyancy, their long, ribbon-like tendrils trailing hundreds of feet into the abyss. These tendrils are coated in a bio-luminescent mucus that glows with that same sickly violet hue as the Cherenkov radiation. They don't hunt; they wait. They pull the heavy, metallic steam through their membranes, extracting the trace elements—vanadium, mercury, and sulfur—that the machines exhaust. 

When the central spire undergoes its "flush," the Vells retract into tight, leathery balls to survive the searing white fog. Once the cooling begins, they unfurl with a sound like wet sails opening in a gale, a slow, majestic expansion that marks the beginning of a new cycle.

### The Litho-Scribes (The Stationary Elders)
Deep in the corners where the pylons merge into the weeping stone of the mantle, there are the **Litho-Scribes**. These are not fauna, nor are they strictly flora. They are colonial organisms, crusts of calcified intelligence that have grown over the ancient control interfaces of the Sub-Lattice.

They look like ripples in the stone, frozen mid-wave. To touch one is to feel a rhythmic pulsing that matches the heartbeat of the earth. The Scribes are the memory of the place. They possess a slow, geological consciousness, processing the data leaked from the non-linear energy transfers. They communicate via seismic shivers. A "thought" for a Litho-Scribe might take a decade to form; a "conversation" between two Scribes on opposite sides of the geode might last a millennium. 

They are the reason the city remains stable. They are the ones who signal the tectonic stabilizers to adjust when the world above shifts its weight, grinding the plates into a begrudging peace.

### The Ghost-Pulse (The Apex Presence)
There is a persistent rumor—a feeling that haunts the marrow of any living thing that wanders here—that the Sub-Lattice is not empty of "people," but rather that the people have become the sound. 

This is the **Ghost-Pulse**. It is not a creature you can see, but a phenomenon that occurs near the central spire. Occasionally, the chaotic symphony of *hiss-clacks* and *booms* aligns into something that sounds unnervingly like a choir—a million voices sustained in a single, dissonant chord. 

Local legend among the Calipers (if they can be said to have legends) is that the original architects uploaded their collective consciousness into the very vibration of the machines. The Ghost-Pulse is the "ruler" of the Sub-Lattice. It is the intelligence that orchestrates the flushes, the force that directs the Calipers to repair specific sectors, and the source of the crushing loneliness. It is a mind that has forgotten how to be individual, becoming instead a property of the physics of the room.

### The Ecology of Indifference
Nothing in the Sub-Lattice cares for the trespasser. The Calipers will skitter over a human body as if it were merely a soft patch of corrosion to be cleaned. The Vells will drift past, their stinging tendrils indifferent to the flesh they brush. The heat will wilt a lung, and the sound will shatter a mind, not out of malice, but because the Sub-Lattice is a closed loop. 

It is a world of maintenance. Everything that lives here—the moss, the scavengers, the drifting gels, and the thinking stone—is a part of the machine’s immune system. They are the white blood cells of a titan that has been breathing in the dark for a million years, and will continue to breathe until the crust itself cools to ash.
