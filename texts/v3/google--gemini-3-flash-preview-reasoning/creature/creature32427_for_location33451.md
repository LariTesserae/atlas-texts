In the Attic of the World, the inhabitants are not born; they are precipitated from the silence. 

The primary dwellers are the **Lithic Citizens**, beings of such profound stillness that they are often mistaken for the basalt buttresses they inhabit. They are tall, angular silhouettes, composed of the same high-grade alloys and polished silicates as the Spoke-Cities. They do not have faces in the biological sense, but rather "Sensory Plates"—flat, matte surfaces of dark glass that drink in the 0.1-rating light. 

They do not walk; they shift. A Citizen might occupy a single terrace for three centuries, their internal gyroscopes humming in perfect synchronicity with the city’s foundation. To them, time is not a river, but a solid block of obsidian. Their "speech" is a sub-audible modulation of the 1.9-grade industrial hum—a slow, vibrating exchange of data that takes decades to form a single sentence. They are the librarians of the terminal state, processing the history of a world that has finally stopped moving. They are the museum-keepers, and they are also the exhibits.

Clinging to the periphery of these stoic giants are the **Fractal-Skitterers**, the 0.8-fauna. If the Citizens are the mountains, the Skitterers are the dust that refuses to settle. They are delicate, articulated frameworks of translucent quartz and wire-thin copper filaments. They lack flesh, organs, or breath. Instead, they are powered by "Vibration-Harvesting." You will find them clustered around the Lume-Vents or clinging to the underside of the elevated transit-tubes, their many-jointed limbs vibrating in a blur to catch the heat-bleed from the machinery. 

The Skitterers move with a frantic, clicking grace, yet they go nowhere. They perform complex, geometric dances in the shadows, their crystal bodies refracting the dull amber light into brief, sharp needles of color. They are scavengers of the "Non-Event," feeding on the microscopic friction of the great pistons. They are the only things that possess a semblance of hurry, yet their hurry is circular, achieving nothing but the maintenance of their own crystalline lattice.

Deep within the Spoke-Cities, residing in the pressurized hearts of the thermal siphons, are the **Engine-Hearts**. These are the most ancient inhabitants—vestigial intelligences that have merged entirely with the 1.9-grade tech. They are not individuals, but a distributed consciousness that feels the entire plateau as a single, warm skin. They are the ones who remember the "Liquid Age," though they treat it as a primitive fever dream. Through the slow-moving pistons, they breathe a rhythmic, artificial life into the Attic. They do not think in thoughts; they think in pressures, balances, and the maintenance of the 0.4 Dynamic.

There is no competition here, no predation, and no growth. The Skitterers do not eat the Citizens, and the Citizens do not acknowledge the Skitterers. They are all components of a closed system. 

If you were to look closely at a Lithic Citizen’s obsidian skin, you might see a faint, etched map of the stars that no longer exist in the charcoal sky. They carry the memory of the light, but they have no desire for its return. They are the perfect residents for a world of terminal stability: beings who have traded the chaotic fragility of life for the heavy, warm, and eternal dignity of the machine. They are the final watchers of a finished masterpiece, waiting for nothing, content in the dark.
