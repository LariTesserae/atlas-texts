In the Hadean Cistern, biology has long since made its peace with the crushing weight of the world. To live here is to be a creature of compression, an organism whose very cells are pressurized to withstand the 3.0 density of the atmosphere. Nothing here is soft; softness is a liability where the air itself pushes back against the ribs.

### The Chitin-Clad (The Lithic Skitterers)
The translucent, hound-sized arthropods mentioned in the scouts' reports are the **Crystalline Scuttlers**. They are the dominant movers of the vertical planes. Their bodies are not made of organic chitin as we understand it, but of a biological ceramic—a mixture of calcium carbonate and silicate harvested directly from the weeping walls. 

They do not have eyes; instead, their carapaces are covered in thousands of microscopic, hairlike sensors that detect the "bruised" light of the bioluminescent mold and the tremors of the great pistons. They move with a frantic, twitching energy because they are essentially living batteries, charging themselves on the geothermal heat. When they stop moving, they begin to calcify; a Scuttler that rests for too long becomes a permanent part of the pillar it clings to. You will see their fossilized ancestors everywhere, half-merged into the basalt, their frozen limbs serving as handholds for the living.

### The Pulse-Wyrms
In the black, mineral-heavy pools, the **Eyeless Eels**—or **Pulse-Wyrms**—navigate by sound. In a world with a 2.7 roar, silence is death. The Wyrms have evolved to "eat" vibration. Their skin is a series of overlapping, white-scaled plates that vibrate in sympathy with the tectonic grinding and the thud of the steam pumps. 

They are the janitors of the Cistern. They feed on the heavy metal precipitates that settle in the pools—lead, iron, and sulfur—filtering the water through gills that resemble radiator fins. When the massive chains above clank with their cannon-shot resonance, the Wyrms leap from the water in a unified, frantic shiver, their bodies momentarily glowing a bright, electric violet as they discharge the kinetic energy they’ve absorbed.

### The Calcified Custodians
Perhaps the most unsettling inhabitants are the ones responsible for the "industrial" presence. They are not a separate species, but a slow-motion transformation. There are beings here—whether they were once human or something else is lost to the humidity—known as **The Latticed**. 

These are the tenders of the pumps and the grease-monkeys of the great chains. They move with the same agonizing slowness as the machines themselves. Their skin has been replaced by a grey, porous mineral crust from centuries of exposure to the weeping walls. They do not speak; the roar of the Cistern has long since scoured away the need for language. Instead, they communicate by striking the iron catwalks with heavy, stone-fused fists, sending rhythmic codes through the metal. 

The Latticed are symbiotic with the industry. They feed on the thick, black grease that leaks from the gears—a substance rich in synthetic hydrocarbons—and in return, they clear the mineral buildup from the pistons. To see one is to see a statue in motion; a single step takes them an hour, a deliberate and crushing placement of a foot that sounds like a tectonic shift.

### The Lung-Veils (The Flora)
The vegetation is less a garden and more a filtration system. The **Pale Fungi** are rubbery and dense, serving as shock absorbers for the vibrations that would otherwise shatter organic tissue. 

Higher up, clinging to the rusted chains and the undersides of the iron catwalks, are the **Lung-Veils**. These are the hairlike filaments of chemotrophic mold. They do not sway, for the air is too heavy for a breeze; instead, they hang like static shrouds. They are the primary source of the purple-grey light. They inhale the salt and iron mist, exhaling a faint, ozonic gas that gives the Cistern its sharp, metallic tang. They grow in the shape of the sound waves produced by the pumps, forming intricate, frozen fractals of mold that map the acoustics of the cavern.

### The Parasitic Clanks
Even the machines have developed a form of "life." In the dark recesses where the steam pipes leak, small, mechanical-biological hybrids have emerged. **Clockwork Mites**, born from the union of discarded iron scraps and the aggressive mineral growth, scuttle over the larger gears. They are not truly alive, yet they possess a directive: to seek out heat and friction. They cluster around the joints of the gargantuan pumps, their tiny, grinding mandibles polishing the metal, preventing the rust from seizing the entire world-factory. They are the immune system of the Hadean Cistern, born from the very industry that violated the silence of the earth.

***

In this place, the line between the geological, the mechanical, and the biological has been crushed thin by the weight of the crust. Everything here is a worker in the great, slow construction of the world. You are the only thing in the Hadean Cistern that is not yet part of the stone. But as the mineral-heavy water weeps onto your skin and the roar vibrates in your marrow, the Cistern begins its work on you, too. Give it a century, and you may find your feet have taken root in the basalt, and your voice has become part of the deafening, eternal hum.
