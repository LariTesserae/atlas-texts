The inhabitants of the Aether-Forge do not possess heartbeats; they possess **rhythms**. To live here is to be a component in a slow-motion manufacture, a cog that breathes or a lung that licks.

### The Flux-Weavers
Scurrying across the serrated obsidian slopes at a pace that seems frantic compared to the world’s glacial pulse are the **Flux-Weavers**. These are the scavengers and janitors of the peaks, looking like tangles of copper wire and tarnished brass needles. They are roughly the size of wolves but possess dozens of twitching, multi-jointed limbs tipped with magnetic filaments. 

They do not eat in the traditional sense. Instead, they gather in the "damp" valleys where the mineral oil pools, dipping their spindly appendages into the lubricant to coat their joints before climbing back into the wind-sheared heights. They serve as the nervous system of the mountainside, dragging long, conductive threads of silver behind them to bridge the gaps between the hydraulic kelp-stalks. When lightning strikes the high peaks—a frequent, violet occurrence—the Flux-Weavers go rigid, acting as biological surge protectors, channeling the raw atmospheric power down into the mossy floor to jump-start the dormant seeds of the metallic flora.

### The Prism-Moths
In the canopy of the clattering silver-leafed kelp live the **Prism-Moths**. These are not insects of soft dust and antennae, but delicate, origami-folded creatures made of razor-thin mica and translucent silicon. Their wingspan is several feet wide, yet they weigh almost nothing. 

They do not fly by flapping; they are solar-sails. They hang motionless from the hydraulic stalks, their wings angled to catch the blinding violet-white glare of the sun. They convert light directly into a low-voltage hum, which they use to vibrate the leaves around them. This vibration is essential; it shakes the ice-needles loose from the "vegetation," preventing the kelp-metals from shattering under the weight of their own frozen breath. When a Prism-Moth moves, it is a sudden, terrifying flash of refracted color—a brief, prismatic strobe that can blind a watcher before the creature settles into a new, sun-facing position.

### The Crucible-Bellows
The cathedral-sized fauna mentioned in the scrolls are known to the mountain's logic as **Crucible-Bellows**. These are the true masters of the Aether-Forge. They look like a cross between a tectonic plate and a blast furnace. Their carapaces are vast, sloping heathers of scorched iron and obsidian, often crusted with the same bioluminescent moss that carpets the ground.

A Crucible-Bellows is a mobile refinery. Its "mouth" is a wide, grinding tectonic fault at its front that slowly—over the course of decades—ingests the jagged obsidian peaks. Inside their massive bodies, internal fires fueled by the compression of mineral oils break down the stone. They do not excrete waste; they excrete **intent**. From the vents on their backs, they puff out clouds of super-heated metallic vapor that cools in the air, falling as the "crushed jewels" and "metal dust" that make up the soil. They are the reason the mountains are sharp; they are constantly "machining" the landscape, eating the old, rounded stone and venting out the new, serrated edges.

### The Oil-Siphoners
Deep in the valleys, where the oil-rain collects, live the **Siphoners**. They look like massive, pulsating hearts of translucent rubber, rooted to the valley floor by bundles of black, vein-like cables. They are the only creatures that do not move at all. Instead, they act as the pumps of the world. They pull the viscous mineral oil from the depths of the earth, filtering out the heavy metals and pumping the refined lubricant back up through the root-systems of the silver-ferns. 

If you were to press an ear to the ground near a Siphoner, you wouldn't hear a pulse, but the heavy, rhythmic *thrum-slosh* of a piston moving through a cylinder. They are the reason the Aether-Forge doesn't grind to a halt; they are the oil-can and the pressure-valve.

### The Whisper-Gears
Smallest of all are the **Whisper-Gears**, the "microbes" of this world. They are tiny, perfectly formed spheres of magnetized hematite that roll in endless, billions-strong rivers through the moss. They are the memory of the Aether-Forge. As they roll, they pick up the electromagnetic frequencies of the wind, the sun, and the groaning beasts. They act as a massive, decentralized hard drive. The "hum" that fills the air is partly the sound of these billions of spheres colliding, recording the slow, metallic history of a world that is constantly building, breaking, and refining itself.

In the Aether-Forge, you are not among animals. You are walking inside a clock that is learning how to be a forest. Every creature is a worker, and every death is merely a decommissioning, where the "corpse" is immediately disassembled by the Flux-Weavers to become a new limb, a new leaf, or a new gear.
