The inhabitants of the Aqueous Shroud are the **Liana-Mnemonics**, a race of beings who appear less like creatures of flesh and more like an evolutionary bridge between the vertebrate and the botanical. 

### Physiology: The Translucent Anatomy
The Mnemonics are elongated and spindly, their bodies reaching lengths of seven or eight feet, though they rarely weigh more than a human child due to the buoyancy of the glacial water. Their skin is not scales or fur, but a smooth, rubbery membrane the color of a bruised plum or deep periwinkle, so thin in places that you can see the slow, rhythmic pulse of their three-chambered hearts.

They lack true bones. Instead, their internal structure is composed of **pressurized cartilage**, a flexible, tube-like skeletal system that allows them to withstand the massive depths of the Shroud without being crushed. Their limbs are long and end in four slender, multi-jointed fingers, each tipped with a hard, keratinous "drumming-pad" used for communication.

They do not have noses; they breathe through feathery, external gill-fronds that crest like a crown from their temples and run down their spines. These fronds are sensitive to the slightest chemical change in the water, allowing a Mnemonic to "smell" a predator or a ripening gourd miles away. Their eyes are their most striking feature: large, lidless orbs of polished amber, reflecting the steady shafts of blue light with a cat-like tapetum lucidum.

### Movement: The Weightless Glide
A Mnemonic does not walk. Even on the "floor" of the district, they move with a slow, strolling swim. When navigating the vertical city, they use their powerful, prehensile toes to grip the Vitreous Liana, pulling themselves along the spiraling ramps in a series of graceful, languid lunges. 

Because the water is so dense, they have mastered the art of "static drifting." A Mnemonic can remain perfectly motionless for hours, suspended in the water column like a piece of drift-wood, their metabolism dropping so low that they appear to be part of the architecture. This is their primary state of meditation and rest.

### Language: The Percussive Syntax
Since speech is impossible, the Mnemonics are masters of vibration. They are a silent people who live in a loud world. They communicate through **The Tap**, a complex language of rhythmic strikes against the hollow gourds of their homes or the resonant vines of the city.

A Mnemonic child learns the "Loom-Beat"—a steady, low-frequency rhythm that identifies their family lineage—before they learn to hunt. In the communal plazas, dozens of Mnemonics will gather to perform a "Tectonic Chorus," striking the Lianas in synchronicity with the great "Groan" of the mountain. They believe that by mimicking the sound of the earth shifting, they are helping to soothe the tectonic plates and prevent the basin from cracking.

### The Social Order: The Graft-Singers and the Shard-Keepers
Society is divided not by power, but by the "Speed of Service."

*   **The Graft-Singers:** These are the elders and architects. They spend their decades-long lives perched atop the growing tips of the Vitreous Liana. They use bone-needles to stitch the vines together and "sing" to them through constant, gentle vibrations, encouraging the plants to grow into the shapes of rooms or bridges. To be a Graft-Singer is to live at the pace of the wood—steady, patient, and uncompromising.
*   **The Shard-Keepers:** The younger, more agile members of the population. They are the hunters and gatherers who venture to the "Dark Rims"—the edges of the district where the light fails and the great predatory eels lurk. They hunt with obsidian-tipped harpoons and carry the bioluminescent algae lanterns. They are the only ones who know the "Fast Rhythms," the emergency codes for danger or discovery.

### Fauna: The Shadow-Ecologies
Living alongside the Mnemonics are the other denizens of the Shroud:

*   **Glass-Bell Medusas:** Translucent, umbrella-shaped jellyfish that drift through the tiers. They act as the city's filtration system, pulsing rhythmically to draw in silt and expel purified, oxygen-rich water. The Mnemonics treat them like semi-sacred livestock; to harm a Glass-Bell is to poison the air of one's own home.
*   **The Silt-Skates:** Flat, diamond-shaped scavengers that cling to the underside of the mossy floors. They possess bioluminescent bellies that glow a soft green, acting as living "night-lights" for the lower, darker levels of the city.
*   **The Needle-Eels:** The primary terror of the Shroud. These are serpentine predators with bodies like chrome cables and mouths filled with translucent, glass-like teeth. They do not hunt by sight, but by sensing the electrical impulses of a beating heart. A Mnemonic hunter must learn to "hush" their own heart—lowering their pulse to almost zero—to avoid being detected by a prowling eel.

### The Cycle of the Deep
Life in the Aqueous Shroud is a closed loop of cold blue. When a Mnemonic reaches the end of their long life (often two centuries or more), they do not leave the city. They descend to the very lowest tier, the **Root-Well**, and tuck themselves into the crook of a primary Liana. 

There, they are wrapped in a shroud of woven moss. Over the next fifty years, the vine slowly grows *over* them, incorporating their mineral-rich cartilage into its own vitreous structure. The ancestors literally become the foundations of the city. The spiraling ramp you climb to reach the market may very well contain the spirit and the substance of your great-grandfather, his life’s work finally translated into the very wood that holds the world together.
