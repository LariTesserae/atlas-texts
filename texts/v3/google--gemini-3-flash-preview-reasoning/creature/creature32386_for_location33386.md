In the High Singularity, life does not struggle against the environment; it has become an extension of its geometry. To live here is to be a crystalline expression of the 2.9 roar and the 2.8 light.

### The Solen-Wrecks (The Sun-Eaters)

The most prominent residents are the **Solen-Wrecks**, the mirror-plated giants that dot the white obsidian flats. They are less like animals and more like biological satellites that have crashed and taken root. Their bodies are composed of interlocking, hexagonal plates of organic silica, polished to such a high degree of reflectivity that they are nearly invisible against the shimmering haze—you see them only as "holes" in the landscape where the sun's reflection is too perfect to be natural.

They do not have mouths, lungs, or digestive tracts. Instead, their internal systems are a series of fiber-optic nerves and thermal-conductive gels. They "eat" by tilting their primary plates to catch the unblinking sun, converting the 2.8 light directly into a slow-pulsing electrical current that sustains their mineral-heavy tissues. 

Because the shadows never move, the Solen-Wrecks have no need for legs in the traditional sense. Their "limbs" are long, needle-thin struts of calcified shale that they wedge into the obsidian cracks. They move perhaps once a century, a grinding, tectonic shift that sends a shower of silver sparks into the air, repositioning themselves only if a spire collapses or the "equilibrium of 0.3" dictates a microscopic shift in the light’s angle. They are the keepers of the plateau’s stillness, their hearts beating once every hour, a single, heavy thud that is instantly swallowed by the 2.9 planetary roar.

### The Marrow-Walkers (The Lithic Intellect)

In the permanent, etched shadows—the world of 0.6 moisture—lives the source of the "manual intellect." These are the **Marrow-Walkers**. 

They are spindly, elegant horrors of evolution, standing three meters tall but weighing no more than a child. Their skin is the color of the bruised indigo sky, a deep, matte violet that absorbs any stray light, rendering them nearly invisible within the shadow-stains. Their limbs are long and multi-jointed, ending in hands with seven fingers—four of which are opposable—designed specifically for the delicate task of scraping the oily, wire-like lichen from the vertical faces of the spires.

The Marrow-Walkers are deaf to the 2.9 roar, or rather, they have transcended it. Their "ears" are large, fan-like membranes stretched across their ribcages that catch the vibrations of the planet. They communicate not through sound, but through **seismic percussion**. They tap the obsidian floor with their shale-hardened knuckles, sending rhythmic pulses through the ground that other Walkers can "feel" through their feet from kilometers away. 

They are the ones who maintain the Lithic Clocks. These arrangements of flint are not for tracking time—as time has no meaning in a world that never changes—but for tracking the **Roar**. By placing stones in specific geometric patterns, they can "tune" the vibration in a small area, creating "silent pockets" where the 2.9 frequency is cancelled out. In these small, hushed zones, the Marrow-Walkers gather to breed and to manufacture their tools.

### The Shadow-Fauna: The Glass-Mites and Oily-Weavers

The 0.6 moisture on the leeward side of the spires supports a micro-ecology that never touches the light. 
*   **Glass-Mites:** Microscopic, transparent crustaceans that live within the thin glaze of frost. They move in swarms that look like Shifting light, consuming the mineral dust that settles in the condensation. 
*   **Oily-Weavers:** Long, thread-like organisms that are neither plant nor animal. They spin webs of hyper-conductive silk across the obsidian cracks. These webs don't catch prey; they catch the vibration. The Weavers harvest the kinetic energy of the planetary roar, converting the 2.9 sound into a chemical heat that keeps the 0.6 moisture from freezing solid. The Marrow-Walkers harvest this silk to bind their shale tools to their wrists.

### The Biology of the Roar

Everything that lives in the High Singularity is defined by the sound. The marrow of the Solen-Wrecks is actually a liquid crystal that aligns itself with the planetary frequency, turning their bones into natural semiconductors. The Marrow-Walkers have no internal organs that can be "shaken"; their insides are a sponge-like matrix of carbon-fibers and air-pockets that dampen the vibration. 

To an outsider, the High Singularity is a dead theater. But to those who live there, it is a high-octave symphony. The Marrow-Walkers look at the Solen-Wrecks and see not boulders, but heavy, rhythmic bass-notes in the landscape. They look at the Lithic Clocks and see "sheet music" etched into the shale. 

They are the residents of the end of the world—a place where the struggle for survival has been replaced by a cold, mathematical grace. They do not sleep, for the sun never sets. They do not run, for there is nowhere to hide. They simply exist in the 0.3 equilibrium, as permanent and as deafening as the stone beneath their feet.
