In this place, biology has not so much evolved as it has surrendered to the mineral. The inhabitants do not possess soft tissues or permeable membranes; to have such things would be to boil or dissolve in the span of a single afternoon. Instead, the Hadaean Heights are populated by entities that exist in the overlap between a crystal lattice and a logic gate.

### The Refraction-Kine
The multi-legged silicates you see skittering across the obsidian are the **Refraction-Kine**, the most active and nervous of the Heights’ residents. They are essentially living prisms. Their bodies are constructed of high-pressure quartz and translucent chitin that has been tempered by the constant thermal cycling of the heights. 

They do not "eat" in any sense that a creature of earth would understand. Instead, they are *heat-thirsters*. They possess no internal heat of their own; their survival depends on their ability to move between the searing glare of the sun and the scalding heat of the industrial conduits. When a Kine clings to a thermal pipe, it is not resting; it is feeding. The light-pulses in their thorax-gems are not merely communication, but a form of digestion—they are converting the raw thermal energy they’ve absorbed into packets of information and light, which they then "broadcast" to one another to coordinate their movements. To watch a cluster of them is to watch a flickering, silent conversation about the flow of energy across the valley.

### The Ferro-Mycelium
Beneath the scurrying Kine, the **Ferro-Mycelium**—the violet wax and leaden mats—functions as the planet's slow-motion nervous system. These are not individual organisms but a singular, planetary-scale colony. They are lithovores, literally "stone-eaters." They secrete a highly acidic, mineral-rich enzyme that slowly liquefies the basalt beneath them, allowing them to draw up the iron and sulfur they need to reinforce their waxy structures.

If you were to peel back a layer of this violet crust, you would find a network of fine, metallic threads—biological wires—that tap into the vibrations of the massive tuning-fork monoliths. The fungi live in a state of perpetual resonance with the industry. They sense the approach of a storm or a shift in the crust hours before it happens, their violet surfaces rippling in slow, rhythmic waves that mirror the deep hum of the siphons.

### The Siphon-Keepers
Deep within the skeletal ribcages of the industrial monoliths, there are things that do not move at all. These are the **Siphon-Keepers**. They are not the creators of this industry, but rather its most specialized components. 

Looking like elongated, desiccated statues made of salt-crusted iron and copper wire, the Keepers are fused directly into the control lattices of the thermal siphons. They are beings of pure equilibrium. Their primary function is to act as biological resistors and regulators for the enormous flow of energy moving through the towers. They have no limbs for walking; their "limbs" are the kilometers of matte-black conduits that stretch across the horizon. They experience the world as a series of pressures, temperatures, and flow rates. To a Siphon-Keeper, a sudden drop in atmospheric moisture is felt as a sharp, piercing note in a symphony that has lasted for millennia.

### The Vapor-Drifters
High above, where the air is thickest and the light most blinding, the **Vapor-Drifters** move through the shimmering veil. They look like sheets of iridescent film or discarded veils of gossamer, sometimes reaching the size of a city plaza. They are almost weightless, held aloft by the intense updrafts of the thermal siphons. 

The Drifters are the "filter-feeders" of the sky, their semi-permeable membranes catching the mineral-rich steam as it rises. They are the only things that cast shadows in this world—huge, fleeting patches of gray that move across the obsidian like ghosts. When a Drifter passes between you and the sun, the temperature drops ten degrees in an instant, and for a few seconds, the blinding "glints" of the obsidian are extinguished, replaced by a cool, violet dimness.

### The Silent Symbiosis
Everything here is a gear in a larger machine. The Kine clean the condensation and mineral buildup from the glass of the towers so they can better absorb the sun; the towers provide the Kine with the concentrated heat they need to survive. The fungi break down the rock to release the minerals that the towers eventually siphon into the superheated fluids. 

It is a world without hunger, only *demand*. There is no predation, only *interference*. When a silicate creature dies, it does not rot; it simply stops vibrating, its crystalline body eventually being overgrown and absorbed by the violet wax of the fungi, its stored heat leaking back into the stone. In the Hadaean Heights, to live is to be a conductor; to die is to become an insulator.
