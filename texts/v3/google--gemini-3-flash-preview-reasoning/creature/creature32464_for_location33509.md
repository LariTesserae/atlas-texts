In the Lignin-Turbine Node, the concept of an "individual" is a structural error. To live here is to be a sub-routine; to breathe is to contribute to the compression cycle. The residents are not guests of the Great Osmotic Cedar, but its mobile tissues—specialized cells that have been granted legs, shells, and senses to better serve the stasis of the engine.

### The Copper-Clad Wardens (The *Auscultators*)
The most prominent inhabitants are the **Auscultators**, the copper-colored beetles that serve as the node’s mechanics. Their shells are not merely armor; they are resonant chambers tuned to the specific frequency of the lignin crankshafts. An Auscultator does not "see" the machinery so much as it feels the harmonic health of the rotating wood. 

When a crankshaft develops a hairline fracture or a friction-hotspot, the Auscultator detects the dissonance. They move with a terrifying, hydraulic precision, their many-jointed legs clicking in syncopation with the gas valves. Their primary tool is a specialized, telescopic proboscis that secretes a fast-setting, structural resin. They "lick" the wounds of the factory, their saliva hardening into a patch that is stronger than the original cellulose. They do not sleep; they enter a low-power "hibernate" state while still clinging to the rotating shafts, their bodies spinning in a blur of copper as they recharge from the vibrations of the wood itself.

### The Synapse-Motes (The *Lumen-Swarm*)
The small, hovering midges are the node’s nervous system made manifest. They are not biological in any traditional sense—they lack digestive tracts and reproductive organs. Instead, they are "budded" from the fiber-optic vines when the system detects a surge in data-pressure. 

These **Synapse-Motes** act as a distributed intelligence. Individually, they are mindless sparks of white light. Collectively, they form shifting geometric clouds that mirror the flow of sap through the root-veins. If a "valve" flower fails to hiss at the correct interval, the Motes swarm it, their collective bioluminescence shifting to a harsh, diagnostic violet. This light-signal triggers a response from the Auscultators. The Motes live on a diet of pure electromagnetic leakage, hovering near the pulsing veins to "feed" on the waste energy of the heavy protein transit.

### The Sessile Lungs (The *Vapor-Throats*)
The fleshy flower-buds that act as valves are perhaps the most eerie of the node’s inhabitants. While they appear to be part of the architecture, they possess a slow, vegetable sentience. These **Vapor-Throats** have evolved a specialized form of "muscle" made of hyper-reactive xylem. 

They spend their entire lives—which can span centuries—in a state of focused tension. They are the gatekeepers of the node's internal pressure. Their "petals" are thick, leathery membranes that have been stained dark purple by the high-pressure gases they contain. When they release steam, it isn't a passive act; it is a vocalization. A healthy node is a chorus of these rhythmic, wet sighs. If a Vapor-Throat dies, it does not wilt; it calcifies, its final act being to seal the vent permanently with its own petrified body, trusting its successors to compensate for the lost volume.

### The Lubricant-Weavers (The *Mycelial-Slugs*)
Moving almost imperceptibly across the rotating lignin rods are the **Mycelial-Slugs**. These are translucent, ribbon-like organisms that look like spilled oil given life. They are the source of the "oily resins" mentioned in the soundscape. 

They exist in the high-friction zones where hardwood shrieks against hardwood. The Slugs thrive on heat; they seek out the hottest points of the engine and drape themselves over the joints. As the friction cooks them, they exude a slick, heat-resistant mucus that prevents the wood from combusting. They are the martyrs of the node—slowly being ground down and replenished, a living grease that ensures the "perfect, unchanging equilibrium" is never broken by the violence of fire.

### The Absence of the "Self"
There is no hunger here, only deficit; no fear, only deviation. If an inhabitant is damaged beyond repair, it does not struggle. An Auscultator with a cracked leg will simply steer itself into the path of a biological piston, allowing its copper shell to be crushed and recycled by the emerald moss floor. The moss, in turn, breaks down the minerals to feed the next generation of technicians budding in the bark-walls.

In the Lignin-Turbine Node, life is not a struggle for survival. It is a commitment to the rhythm. The inhabitants are the heartbeat of a machine that forgot it was a tree, and the white-cell guardians of a factory that thinks it is a god.
