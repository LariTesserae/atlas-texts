To live in the Lumen-Chasm is to exist in a state of perpetual, high-tension alertness. The inhabitants do not inhabit the void so much as they are snagged within it, like dust motes caught in a web of frozen lightning.

### The Needle-Folk (The *Chir-Aka*)
The humans here have been sculpted by the verticality of their world. They are the **Chir-Aka**, or "Those who Listen to the Snap." Physically, they are unsettling to look upon: their limbs are disproportionately long, their fingers tipped with hardened, keratinous hooks rather than soft pads. Their skin is the color of skimmed milk, nearly translucent, revealing a network of pale blue veins that pulse with a slow, cold rhythm. 

Because the air is so thin and ozone-heavy, they do not speak with the booming resonance of surface-dwellers. Their language is a series of sharp clicks, hisses, and—most importantly—**Vibration-Signing**. By pressing their palms or foreheads against a Lattice-stalk, they can communicate across miles, feeling the rhythmic thrum of a distant tribe member’s strike against the glass-hard cellulose.

They are the ultimate opportunists of tension. A Chir-Aka scout does not walk; they fall with intention, using long silk-capes woven from the inner pith of the Lattice to catch the updrafts created by the venting Flash-Mists. They live in a state of "Kinetic Prayer," believing that stillness is a precursor to shattering. To them, the Lattice is a living god that demands they never stay in one place long enough to be fused into its growth.

### The Refractor-Stalkers
If the humans are the parasites, the **Refractor-Stalkers** are the ghosts. These are the Chasm’s apex predators, though a traveler might look directly at one and see only a shimmer in the air. Their bodies are composed of organic silica and a series of adjustable, microscopic plates that act like biological prisms. 

By shifting these plates, the Stalker can bend the omnipresent white glare around its body, becoming a smudge of "nothingness" against the glowing sky. They hunt by vibration. When a human or a Glider moves, the Stalker feels the harmonic resonance in the Lattice cables. They do not bite; they vibrate their own bodies at a frequency that causes the brittle, frozen limbs of their prey to simply crystallize and shatter, allowing the Stalker to lap up the flash-frozen fluids.

### The Aeon-Drifters (Glass-Gliders)
High in the empty spaces, the **Aeon-Drifters** float. They are not birds, but something closer to atmospheric jellyfish. Their bodies are vast, paper-thin membranes—some spanning sixty feet—that are almost entirely two-dimensional. 

They are passive filter-feeders, their undersides coated in a sticky, bioluminescent mucous that captures the ice-crystals of the Flash-Mists. To a Drifter, the freezing cold is life; they use the temperature differential between the light-baked upper surface of their membranes and the shadowed under-edge to create a molecular lift. They are the only creatures that truly know the silence of the Chasm, drifting for decades without ever touching a solid stalk. The Chir-Aka hunt them by shooting tether-hooks into their trailing filaments, dragging the massive, sighing creatures down to the Dead-Nodes to harvest their membranes for clothing and tents.

### The Masticators (Lichen-Crabs)
At the base of the massive stalks, or clinging to the horizontal "High-Ways," are the **Masticators**. These are the only creatures that possess true weight. Looking like jagged boulders with six multi-jointed legs, their shells are formed from the calcified remains of the Lattice they have consumed.

They are the unsung engineers of the void. As they grind their way along the razor-edged stalks, their heavy, grinding mouthparts smooth down the serrated fiber, creating the only "safe" paths for the Chir-Aka to traverse. The humans treat the Masticators with a fearful reverence; to kill one is to lose a road-maker, but to stand in its path is to be slowly, methodically pulverized by a creature that does not recognize the concept of an obstacle.

### The Shard-Sworms
In the micro-ecology of the Chasm, the **Shard-Sworms** are the most terrifying. They are tiny, insectoid motes of living glass that live inside the pressurized veins of the Lattice. When a "Fracture" occurs and a vine shatters, the Sworms are vented into the air by the thousands. 

For a few minutes, they are a glittering, lethal cloud, driven into a frenzy by the sudden drop in pressure. They seek out heat—any heat. They will burrow into the skin of a human or the soft underbelly of a Glider within seconds, seeking the warmth of blood to lay their crystalline eggs. The Chir-Aka wear "Cold-Veils"—masks made of hammered cellulose—specifically to keep these microscopic invaders from entering their lungs during a Growth-Storm.

***

In the Lumen-Chasm, life is not a struggle for resources, but a struggle against **stasis**. To be still is to be overgrown; to be heavy is to fall; to be warm is to be hunted. Everything that lives here—from the smallest Sworm to the most ancient nomad—is as sharp, bright, and brittle as the world that contains them. They are creatures of the snap, the shimmer, and the long, cold fall.
