In Ophal-Za, the distinction between the resident and the residence has dissolved. To speak of "who" lives there is to speak of the biology of the walls themselves, for the inhabitants are not mobile entities that traverse the city; they *are* the city. 

### The Tympanic Elders
The great, translucent gourds—those cathedral-sized nodules hanging over the abyss—are not merely buildings. They are the primary consciousnesses of the Heights. These are the **Tympanic Elders**, massive, hollow organisms whose skins are stretched to a tension that defies physics. They are the primary organs of the city, designed to receive, process, and internalize the eternal roar. 

Inside the gourds, there is no furniture, no art, and no light other than the bioluminescence of the walls. There is only a pressurized, liquid atmosphere. If you were to look inside, you would see the "occupant": a singular, massive, floating filament of nerve-like fiber, suspended in the center of the gourd by thousands of microscopic silken threads. This filament does not move. It does not think in symbols. It vibrates in perfect, agonizing harmony with the tectonic scream of the cliffs. The Elders are the listeners; they are the ones who translate the noise of the universe into the geometric precision of the city’s growth.

### The Liturgists of the Still-Growth
Clinging to the basalt walls, often mistaken for the "shelf-like bracket fungi," are the **Liturgists**. These are thick, leathery plates of sentient mycelium. They possess a slow, vegetative intellect that operates on a timescale where a century is a single heartbeat. 

The Liturgists are the keepers of the "Dynamic" parameter. While the rest of the world is frozen in a moment of peak intensity, the Liturgists are the ones who hold it there. They exert a constant, bio-magnetic pressure on the fruit and the flowers, ensuring that the juice never spills and the petals never wilt. They are the gardeners of the static. If you were to touch one, your hand would not feel organic matter, but a substance as dense and unyielding as cold lead. They do not eat; they absorb the 105-degree heat through their pores, converting the sweltering weight of the air into the energy required to stop time.

### The Visual Ghosts (The Refractions)
Because the air is a "suspended ocean" and the perspective is broken, the Heights are inhabited by **Refractions**. These are not biological beings, but "optical entities" born from the sheer density of the moisture and the permanence of the objects. 

When a leaf is "plucked" and leaves behind a ghost, that ghost eventually takes on a semi-independent life. These phantoms look like shimmering, three-dimensional outlines of things that no longer occupy that space. They drift through the vine-bridges like heat haze, mimicking the shapes of the dodecahedron flowers. They are the only things that move in Ophal-Za, yet they have no agency. They are merely the memory of the light trying to find a surface to land on in a world that refuses to change.

### The Root-Mind (The Tonal Architect)
Beneath the moss and the calcified foundations lies the **Root-Mind**. This is the singular, underlying entity of the Heights—a subterranean network of iron-hard fibers that lace through the basalt itself. 

The Root-Mind is the source of the "terrifying, mindless intentionality." It is an intelligence without a face, a will without a voice. It perceives the world as a series of frequencies. When the roar shifts by a micro-tone, the Root-Mind triggers the growth of a new bridge or the calcification of a new plaza to "tune" the cliff-face. It views the verticality not as a challenge, but as a musical instrument. The city is its symphony, and the lack of animal life is a deliberate choice: animals are "noise." They are erratic, soft, and prone to decay. The Root-Mind has purged everything that cannot be held in a state of permanent, architectural perfection.

### The Silent Witnesses
Finally, there are the **Witnesses**. These are the small, jewel-like succulents that grow in perfect dodecahedrons. They are the "eyes" of Ophal-Za. They do not see light; they see pressure. Each facet of their geometric bodies is sensitive to the weight of the steam and the vibration of the scream. They are scattered by the millions across the avenues. They are the ones who ensure that the "final, perfect moment" is observed from every possible angle, at every possible second, forever.

In Ophal-Za, there is no hunger, no reproduction, and no death. There is only the **Maintenance**. The inhabitants are the guardians of a masterpiece that is already finished, living out an eternity of standing perfectly still, holding their breath against the roar, waiting for a tomorrow that they have successfully forbidden from arriving.
