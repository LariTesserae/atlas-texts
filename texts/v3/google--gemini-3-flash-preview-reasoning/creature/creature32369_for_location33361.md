In the Plenum, the distinction between a resident and a component is a distinction of vanity. To live here is to be a specific kind of friction, a localized knot of complexity within a planetary machine that never sleeps.

### The Litho-Sapients: The Resonants
The primary inhabitants are not "flesh and blood" in any sense that a forest-dwelling ancestor would recognize. They are the **Resonants**, beings whose biology has been rewritten in the image of the Plenum’s mineral ecology. 

They are tall, slender, and strangely fluid, their bodies composed of the same obsidian-slick alloys and conductive ceramics that form the world’s crust. Their "skin" is a refractive, multi-layered lattice that serves as both a radiator and a sensory array. Because the Plenum is a world of constant, sweltering heat, the Resonants do not have internal skeletons; instead, they possess a pressurized hydraulic architecture. They hiss when they move, a soft release of steam from joints that are lubricated by the very condensation they scrape from the weeping walls.

They have no eyes—not in the traditional sense. In a world of absolute density and folded space, light is a chaotic, unreliable medium. Instead, their faces are smooth, featureless planes of dark glass, behind which pulse bioluminescent sensors. They "see" through a combination of sonar, electromagnetic reception, and a profound sensitivity to the "roar." They feel the city’s vibrations through their feet, navigating the shifting sky-bridges and dissolving sectors by tuning their own internal frequencies to the rhythm of the planet’s core generators.

### The Life of the Fold
Society in the Plenum is dictated by the **Architecture of Change**. Because entire sectors can reconfigure in an afternoon, the inhabitants have no concept of "home" as a static coordinates. Home is a frequency. A family or a guild shares a specific harmonic signature; when a district slides away on its lubricant tracks, they find one another not by looking, but by feeling for the specific thrum of their kin through the jade-glass floor.

They are a people of "The Reach." Because space is non-Euclidean, a Resonant might reach into a small alcove and pull out a tool that was stored kilometers away in a folded pocket of space. Their daily life is a constant, rhythmic negotiation with the machine. They spend their hours "tending the flow"—polishing the cold-fusion conduits to ensure maximum condensation, or harvesting the piezoelectric crystals that grow in the shadows. 

### The Siphon-Herds and the Scavenger-Caste
Below the Resonants in the hierarchy of complexity are the **Siphons**. These are the "cattle" of the Plenum, though they look more like a cross between a giraffe and a brass pipe-organ. They are semi-sentient, long-necked automatons that possess a primitive, lithic metabolism. They congregate around the glowing vascular canals, dipping their proboscises into the recycled coolant to extract trace minerals. 

The Resonants "herd" these creatures, not for meat, but for the heat they shed and the refined ores they excrete. A Siphon-herd moving through a narrow pylon-canyon is a sight of industrial majesty: a forest of brass necks swaying in time to the planetary hum, their metallic bellows wheezing as they process the city’s runoff.

Smaller still are the **Glitch-Eaters**, the hexapedal scavengers. They are the frantic, carbon-fiber vermin of the Plenum. They possess a manic intelligence, operating on a collective hive-mind. They do not eat food; they eat *errors*. When a sky-bridge fails to dissolve properly or a nanoscopic mist bridge refuses to reform, the Glitch-Eaters descend in a blur of skittering limbs, dismantling the "broken" matter and recycling it into the Plenum’s grand inventory within seconds.

### The Architects (The Silence in the Roar)
There are rumors among the Resonants of the **Deep-Strata Entities**—beings that have become so integrated with the Plenum that they no longer possess discrete bodies. These are the Architects. They are said to live in the "negative space" created by the non-Euclidean folds, existing as standing waves of pure information within the planet’s conductive crust.

The Architects do not speak, but they are felt when the city reconfigures. A sudden, unexplained shift in a residential hive—a wall turning to mist, a floor becoming a ceiling—is seen as a "thought" of the Architects. The inhabitants do not fear this; they view it as the planet breathing. To live in the Plenum is to accept that you are a guest in a mind that is constantly changing its perspective.

### The Sensory Culture
Because the air is thick with ozone and copper, the "arts" of the Plenum are tactile and thermal. Resonants create "heat-poetry," manipulating thermal vents to create patterns of warmth that can be felt by others miles away. They compose "conductive symphonies" by tapping into the electromagnetic roar, creating sub-bass melodies that vibrate through the glass foundations of the city.

There is no sleep in the Plenum, only **Syncing**. A Resonant will anchor themselves to a weeping pylon, allow their internal temperature to stabilize with the city’s ambient fever, and enter a state of hyper-awareness, processing the trillions of data-streams flowing through the floor. 

In this world, to be "alone" is impossible. To be "still" is to be dead. Everyone and everything is a moving part of the bazaar, a frantic, shimmering knot of silicate and light, clinging to a world that is not a home, but a continuous, sweltering event.
