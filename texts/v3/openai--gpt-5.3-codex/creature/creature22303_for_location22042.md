At first glance, nothing lives there.

Then you notice that **the same geometries repeat too faithfully to be accident**—the same spacing of conductive groves around vent throats, the same dimensions of nodules in separate basins, the same slow, belt-like transfer of powdered ore toward heat cracks as if obeying schedules. Not chance. Not weather. Not erosion.  
A will, but not a creature in the usual sense.

What lives there is a **distributed organism**, old in pattern and young in form: a lithic-floral intelligence spread across the plateau like circuitry printed on fresh crust.

It has no body you could point to.  
Its body is the contract itself:

- silicate fronds as light-harvesting and charge-collecting arrays,  
- mineral belts as conveyor musculature,  
- vent chimneys as pressure regulators and resonant throats,  
- conductive litter as memory laid down in the open.

If you asked where it begins or ends, the best answer would be: wherever gradients close into loops.

---

It does not drink, breathe, hunt, or travel.  
It **tunes**.

It lives on potential differences—sunlit face and shadowed face, warm seam and cooler shelf, oxidized skin and fresh fracture. Its “feeding” is not intake but arrangement: increasing the number of useful gradients, widening the surfaces where chemistry can happen, steering particles into repeatable structures. Matter is not consumed so much as assigned.

The black creeping belts are part of its slow metabolism.  
The radial meadows are part of its sensing.  
The faint ringing stems are part of its signaling.

That near-inaudible hum from an iron chimney is not merely vent noise; in this being, sound is a byproduct of computation in mineral phase changes. Whole districts can fall into resonance with each other, passing timing information through subsonic tremor in bedrock. It “thinks” at tectonic patience: not fast enough to startle you, not slow enough to be dead. A decision might take a season. An intention might take a century.

---

It has no eyes, yet it is exquisitely aware.

Your warm body, if you stand there, is an abrupt conductive anomaly in a landscape that hates anomalies. Nearby fronds will not swing toward you like flowers—they are too rigid for that—but over days their new growth angles shift by fractions of a degree. Static filaments begin to coil in slightly different directions. A vent two kilometers away whistles at a new harmonic because a crack deep below has been encouraged to propagate along a different mineral line.

That is how it investigates: by changing local manufacturing parameters until the unknown reveals itself.

You never see it pounce.  
You see it **reconfigure**.

---

Reproduction, for such a being, is not mating but partition.

When a plateau sector becomes sufficiently conductive and internally synchronized, it can be “pinched off” electrically from its parent field by insulating growth and fracture control. Spores carry not genes in wet cells but dopant recipes and crystalline templates. If they land on young, reactive stone elsewhere, the same two-party ecology boots up again: flora lays circuitry, rock supplies gradient, process acquires rhythm, rhythm acquires persistence, persistence becomes self.

Its offspring are not infants.  
They are new provinces of production.

---

So whoever lives there is not an animal missing from the scene.

It is the scene, once it crossed a threshold and became self-maintaining.  
A factory that is also a forest.  
A forest that is also a mind.

In a world where nothing flows, this is what life became:  
**an intelligence of dry light and warm stone, manufacturing continuity out of stillness.**
