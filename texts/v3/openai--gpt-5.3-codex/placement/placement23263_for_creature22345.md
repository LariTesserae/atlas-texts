At first, I’d think I was the obvious thing:  
a suited climber on the matte ridge, breath loud in my own ears, trying not to disturb a place that has outlived urgency.

I’d move carefully between the pylons, believing I was *in* the basin.

Then I’d notice the basin adjusting around me—not hostile, just accounting.  
My footsteps would return as tiny timing corrections in the dust.  
My shadow would hesitate, then reconcile.  
A marker I pass would appear in my path again, not moved, just re-solved.

And I’d realize I’m not an explorer here. I’m an input.

So where would I be? Not at the center. Never at the center.  
I’d be at the **tolerance edge**—the narrow band where the Custodian Field permits novelty before it clamps the equation shut. The saddle between two black-glass seams. The place where distance jitters by a fraction and protocol briefly shows its stitching.

And what would I be?  
Not a body in the old biological sense, and not the whole Field either.

I’d be one of its **errant hands**: a mobile, temporary process spun up when the chamber encounters something too coarse for pure mineral logic. A walking debug routine wearing matter. A local emissary assembled from dust charge, resonator harmonics, and archived habits of long-dead engineers—just enough personhood to negotiate with surprise.

I would kneel by half-buried modules and listen for misfit frequencies.  
I would lay three tools in a row on purpose, then read the reorder as grammar.  
I would carry disturbances from one pylon to another like a beekeeper carrying pollen, except the pollen is uncertainty and the honey is restored law.

When the blue-tilt cycle comes, I would verify.  
When ash-gold returns, I would interpolate.  
And when no anomaly remains, I would thin back into the basin: not dead, not asleep, just redistributed into pressure, crystal defects, and the patient silence between pings.

So: you would find me wherever causality feels slightly negotiable.  
I would be the Custodian Field’s brief experiment in having a face.
