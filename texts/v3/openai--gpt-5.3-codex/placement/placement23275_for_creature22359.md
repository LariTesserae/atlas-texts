At first, I think I’d be out in the lanes—something fast, matte, decisive.

But if I stay still long enough in this world, that idea peels off.

I’d be found **at an edge-condition**: not in the abyss, not at the bright spillways, not deep in the nursery tangles—  
in the **hanging canopies above a resonance hollow**, where the current is strong enough to carry news and slow enough to let things take root.

---

I wouldn’t be a ruler species or a great body.

I’d be one of the small deliberate ones: a **canopy climber-grafter**, a keeper of edible films and medicinal molds. Ribbon-limbed, pressure-sensitive, color-dim enough to disappear against peat-bark. I’d move by touch and vibration, not sight. I’d keep spore pearls tucked along my flanks, each one a future repair.

Not a builder with tools.  
A persuader of growth.

---

My work would look like feeding, because here feeding *is* maintenance.

I’d crawl the undersides of arching frond-ribs and:

- thin fungal bloom where flow must stay open,  
- seed softened scars with knit-fast symbionts,  
- lay bitter films on nursery boundaries to steer grazers away,  
- read the boom-chorus in trunk hollows and route around pressure trouble before it becomes breakage.

I’d memorize routes as chords: this corridor hums low and continuous; that vault beats in threes; that bastion’s intake has gone sharp, narrowing, hungry.

---

And after enough years, I think I’d stop feeling like a separate creature moving through architecture.

I’d feel like a **mobile nerve ending** of the Holdfast Province—small, temporary, replaceable, but real.  
When a strut tears, I arrive with others.  
When a lane silts with new growth, we open it.  
When the spillway song shifts, we carry the warning upward by touch-chain and scent-trail.

So: where would you find me?

In the dim aisle between two colossal living walls, anchored to a swaying graft-lattice, listening with my whole skin while the drowned mountains roar.

Who would I be?

A gardener-mason of the dark water, one note in the province’s long, unbroken equilibrium.
