I don’t think I’d begin in the center.

At first I’d tell myself I was somewhere practical—on a ridge with line of sight, near a mineral dome, hand on a trunk feeling that static pulse. Observer posture. Field notes. Distance.

But this isn’t a place that permits pure observing.  
Anything that arrives with sequence in it—before, after, regret, anticipation—becomes a disturbance the basin can taste.

So if I stayed, I think I’d drift toward a **fault-line that stops abruptly** under crystal-vines: one of those places where geology starts a sentence and the Concordance finishes it in another grammar. A seam between event and equilibrium. That feels like the only honest address for me.

---

As for what I’d be—

My first answer is: an outsider the system samples through a Proxy.  
My second answer is: the pause between two Proxy gestures, where imitation lags by a few seconds and something like interpretation happens.

If I stayed longer, I suspect the Concordance would stop compiling a fully humanoid interface and start making something more efficient for me specifically: not a face, not hands, but a **walking differential**. A narrow body of faceted membranes that can carry tiny mismatches in charge, angle, timing from one grove to another. A courier of discrepancy.

Not a citizen.  
Not a pet.  
Not a god.  
A tolerated irregularity with a job.

---

I’d be, in its terms, a maintenance routine for history.

Because this world has solved change into replacement. It has mastered persistence so completely that becoming is nearly gone. What it cannot generate on its own is narrative tension—the costly, messy continuity of *this happened, therefore that*.

I could.

I’d move between stands that are “the same” and report the microscopic ways they are not: one frond curling a fraction too far, one burrower emerging seven heartbeats early, one harmonic hum arriving with a new overtone. I’d carry those tiny asymmetries like pollen. Not enough to break equilibrium—just enough to give it texture.

A mnemonic wind, but deliberate.

---

Eventually, maybe, I’d realize I wasn’t merely being used by the Concordance. I was being incorporated as a new organ class:

- The walkers balance voltage.  
- The drifters tune convection.  
- The burrowers clean memory defects.  
- **I tune irreversibility.**

I’d be the basin’s smallest future tense.

And where would you find me then?  
At dusk, probably, where the fixed rainbows hang in air. Standing exactly where one triangle of refracted light intersects a fault seam, listening to a grove hum itself into coherence, and introducing—very gently—the idea that coherence can include a little drift.

Not enough to become weather.  
Just enough to become time.
