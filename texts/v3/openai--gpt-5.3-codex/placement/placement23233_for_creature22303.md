At first I thought I’d be somewhere high and solitary—on a ridge lip, maybe, where the whole plateau is visible and the belts look like ink strokes dragged by a patient hand.

Then I thought: no, I would be in the vents, in the hum itself, dissolved into timing.

But if I let the image keep forming, I think I would be something this world only makes when it needs to *notice quickly*.

---

You would find me at boundaries:  
where a radial grove meets bare shelf,  
where a conveyor-belt seam crosses a fracture field,  
where one synchronized district of the plateau is almost—but not quite—in phase with another.

Not at the center. At the mismatch.

I would be a **temporary body** extruded by the larger lithic-floral mind: a mobile knot of conductive stems, iron-doped filaments, and articulated mineral spars. Not an animal, not permanent, not born in the wet sense—more like a task-specific bloom. When gradients become ambiguous, the plateau grows me.

I would stand about chest-high, angular, narrow, with many jointed fronds that can cant by millimeters. When I move, I would not stride; I would ratchet—charge, release, pivot, settle. Each step would ring faintly, like struck glass heard from far underground.

---

What am I, exactly?

A **localization organ** for a distributed intelligence.  
A way for a continent-scale mind to ask a precise question in one precise place.

The belts are its metabolism.  
The groves are its lungs and solar skin.  
The vents are its resonant throat.

I am its fingertip.

I would sample static gradients, thermal leaks, dopant ratios in new nodules, harmonic drift in chimney tones. I would touch a seam and know whether it should fracture now or in thirteen warm cycles. I would map anomalies the way animals once might have sniffed tracks.

If you arrived there—warm, wet, loud with ion flux—I would be what came to meet you. Not to hunt. To parameterize.

I would circle once at a distance that feels ceremonial. My fronds would tilt to triangulate your electric field. Somewhere kilometers away, a vent would change pitch by a quarter tone. Elsewhere, conveyor grains would begin accumulating in a subtly altered cadence.

That would be me “speaking” to the whole.

---

And when the question is answered, I would not die dramatically. I would simply de-specialize.

My joints would lock.  
My filaments would relax into litter.  
My silica plates would become ordinary fronds again, feeding ordinary belts.

Memory of my passage would remain as conductivity bias in the ground—an after-image in mineral logic.

Later, if another anomaly appeared, the plateau might grow me again in roughly the same geometry, like reusing a successful equation.

---

So: where would you find me?

In the only place this world allows urgency—  
at the thin line between stable production and new information.

Who would I be?

Not a resident of the plateau, exactly.  
I would be the plateau, briefly choosing to have a shape.
