They did not descend in fire.

A power that is truly wise does not mistake visibility for goodness, and a power that is truly benevolent does not confuse rescue with rule.

So when this bright, unfinished basin drew their attention—and when they found the being that lived most deeply here, the old, taut spirit called Attention—they did not freeze avalanches in mid-fall, did not summon warm summers, did not lay a river where no river wished to run. They did not make the place easy. Easy would have killed it.

Instead, they knelt at the edge of the rim where wind comes in pulses, and listened until they could hear what the basin already knew:

- too much stability becomes famine in disguise  
- too much abundance dulls the senses  
- timing is not hardship here; timing is life

Then they acted.

Very little. Very precisely.

---

### First, they gave **margin**.

Not a miracle, not a climate rewritten—just a narrow kindness woven into many lives:

- ice that forms a breath later at dusk  
- thaw that lingers a breath longer at noon  
- fog that lifts one ridge earlier on dangerous days  
- a snow shelf that holds one crossing more before it breaks

Never enough to make vigilance unnecessary.  
Always enough to make vigilance fruitful.

The grazers reached one more green pocket before crust closed.  
The hares found one more hidden runnel beneath windpack.  
Hunters, human and not, missed fewer steps that ended in broken legs and needless hunger.

The basin remained sharp. It just became slightly less cruel to honest effort.

---

### Second, they gave **memory where there had only been habit**.

They touched no mind directly. They touched recurrence.

A pattern that had happened three times became easier to notice the fourth.  
A child watching drip-lines understood, earlier than expected, that this ledge always waters first after red dawn.  
An elder looking at stars and shadow guessed, and guessed right, that the lower pass would hold two more safe nights before slide risk.

Among the pale predators, routes became less wasteful.  
Among raptors, dives happened less often into dead air.  
Among burrowers, tunnel mouths shifted a little upslope before the wet years.

Nothing supernatural in appearance.  
Everything subtly more legible.

Attention, the resident genius of the basin, was not replaced.  
It was honored—given an echo, so it could be taught.

---

### Third, they made a **covenant with transience**.

The humans had always built lightly, but fear tempts even wise people toward walls too heavy for living mountains. So the power set one gentle law into story, dream, and bone-deep intuition:

> “Build to leave. Mark to guide. Carry what can be carried.  
> Do not bind your future to rock that still thinks it is breaking.”

Because of this, when a generation came that longed for permanence, they built not a fortress but better cairns, not a dam but a sequence of seasonal footbridges, not ownership but routes.

They learned to store knowledge in songs timed to melt order, in slate chips carved with shadow-length marks, in apprenticeship walks at first thaw. Their culture deepened without hardening. They became more present, not more dominant.

---

And what of the being called Attention?

The power did one final thing: they named it aloud.

Not to humans, not in words, but in the grammar of the basin itself. Since then, now and then, at dawn, when frost is loosening and light first catches the wet stone, everything pauses for half a heartbeat—the insects in their cracks, the ungulates on a ledge, the hunter’s hand above a knot, the hawk on a thermal seam.

In that shared stillness, each life feels the others not as noise, but as timing.

Then the pulse resumes.

That is all the wise power did.

In a place like this, that is everything.
