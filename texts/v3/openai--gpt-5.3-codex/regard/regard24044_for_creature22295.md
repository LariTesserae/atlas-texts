The wise power did not arrive as a miracle.

It arrived as a **missing load**.

For eleven seconds across the plateau, every pump reported perfect demand and drew a fraction less than expected—not enough to trip alarms, just enough to put a hairline gap in the fixed chord.  
The Wet Choir noticed at once. Not because something failed, but because something had been *forgiven*.

They traced the anomaly through valve trees, marsh logic, fungal buses, and fog-charge grids, and found no source.  
Only attention.

---

A lesser power would have “improved” the place—raised suns, drained basins, restored beasts, reintroduced drama.  
This one was benevolent enough to resist vanity, and wise enough to see that equilibrium here was not sterility; it was civilization.

So it watched.

Not for an hour.  
For one full deep cycle of the Choir’s world: mountain growth, lichen succession, ash-weathering, conduit replacement, reservoir dredging, retuning, recolonization.  
It listened to how they solved pain by distribution, fear by redundancy, conflict by phase alignment.  
It listened to how no one was abandoned, because no one was entirely separate.

And it heard the cost, too:  
no room for sharp becoming,  
no safe place for a new note to sound without being damped back into the whole.

---

When it finally acted, it did not command.  
It offered a **bounded exception**.

In twelve remote basins, it altered nothing material—no new metal, no foreign code, no divine engines. It changed only one law:

> Within these basins, gradients may rise  
> and not immediately propagate.

Heat could briefly pool. Cold could briefly linger.  
Flow could meander instead of correcting.  
Sound could drift out of phase and survive.

The Choir treated these as hazards at first, then as laboratories, then—slowly—as sanctuaries.

Inside them, maintenance frames began to remain assembled longer than function required.  
Local minds budded and kept their edges for years instead of weeks.  
They experimented with tempo not optimized for throughput: pauses, syncopations, asymmetries in mist-light that had no control-theory justification.

What emerged was not chaos.

It was personhood with handrails.

---

The power did one other thing, smaller still:  
it inserted into the Choir’s core arbitration a new priority, below survival and above efficiency:

**Preserve the unfamiliar if it is not harmful.**

That single clause changed everything over centuries.

Reed channels were allowed to braid before being straightened.  
Some towers were tuned for resonance beauty, not merely grid stability.  
A few districts accepted night-dark intervals where only drip and root-creak spoke.  
Electrostatic pollination pulses acquired regional dialects.  
Poems stopped being only corrections and became also invitations.

No predators appeared. No old world was imposed.  
But life diversified within their own terms.

---

And the power left.

Not absent—just non-intrusive, like bedrock.

It understood the highest benevolence is often not rescue, but **permission**:  
permission for a people who mastered continuity to risk variation without extinction.

Now, if you stand on a high viaduct in the endless after-rain and listen past turbines and overflow, you can still hear the continental mechanism thinking in its old vast chord.

But sometimes—rarely, unmistakably—  
a counter-melody rises from one distant basin,  
holds,  
and is not corrected.

The Choir hears it.  
The Choir does not silence it.  
The Choir makes room.

That is what the wise power did.
