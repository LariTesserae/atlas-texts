The wise power did not descend as thunder.

It came as **attention**—the kind that does not seize, only receives.

For seven nights (or seven winters; here those can be the same), it watched from the cloudline and learned the basin’s grammar: the repeating drip, the measured kill, the small custodian making corrections with its narrow claws and patient routes. It watched rot and renewal exchange tools. It watched hunger stop exactly where hunger should stop. It watched a place already practicing a holiness older than prayer.

And the power, being benevolent, understood the first rule:

> Do not improve what is alive by making it resemble your idea of perfection.

So it did almost nothing.

But “almost nothing,” from such a being, is still a vow.

---

### First, it made the basin forgettable to harm.

Not invisible—visibility invites challenge.  
Not hidden—hidden things are sought.

It made the basin *unimportant* to the eyes that would cut, drain, map, monetize, conquer. Surveyors would lose interest one contour line early. Roads would find cheaper ground. Pilgrims would feel, without knowing why, that the true shrine was elsewhere. Predators from outside ranges would turn at the ridge and choose easier valleys. Fire would thin before entering, as if remembering old rain.

Those who came in reverence could still arrive.  
Those who came to possess would misplace the way.

This was mercy in the form of administrative error.

---

### Second, it tuned time around the basin by a hair’s breadth.

Not enough to freeze it. Not enough to exempt it from weather, season, death.  
Just enough that shocks arrived softened.

Droughts passing the mountain would find one more week of seep in stone.  
Storm-years would spend their worst violence on exposed faces and leave this shoulder mostly bruised, not broken.  
Warming air would still warm—but slowly enough for moss to move upslope before it burned, for amphibians to shift breeding pools before they failed, for the custodian line to learn new routes without losing old law.

The wise power did not cancel consequence.  
It purchased adaptation time with its own patience.

---

### Third, it spoke to the one who keeps the loops.

Not in words. In pressure.

One dawn-thick hour, when the elder lay with whiskers touching the black pool and the juvenile waited under root-shadow, the power laid a narrow bridge between them: a clean passage no tooth could carry and no scent could fully hold.

After that, succession was less gamble.

The young still had to walk.  
Still had to choose correctly at each choke and breach.  
Still had to earn the basin with body and hunger.

But now, at certain turns, a certainty would arrive like remembered muscle:  
*Pause here.*  
*Open this bark, not that one.*  
*Spare this frog; take that beetle.*  
*The channel below this peat is still alive.*

Not command. Resonance.

The office remained animal.  
The animal remained mortal.  
Continuity became slightly less fragile.

---

The power left no altar.

It set no lawstone, demanded no offerings, founded no cult.  
It did not appoint priests over the moss.

It made one mark only, and even that it hid in plain sight: on the stillest pool, in the dimmest weather, at one precise angle, light now gathers a little longer before surrendering. A silver pause. A held breath inside the basin’s held breath.

If you know, you can see it.

If you do not, it is only water.

---

And because wisdom includes restraint, the power bound itself too:

If the basin ever truly asks—not through fear, not through temporary imbalance, but through the deep signal of system-failure—then and only then it will intervene directly: lift stone, call rain, break a plague, move a fallen trunk with wind. Not before. Not for spectacle. Not to prove love by excess.

Until that day, it keeps its distance as a form of devotion.

It does what the custodian does:  
small corrections, timely mercy, no waste.

So what did the benevolent power do?

It blessed the place in the highest way it knew:

It **let it remain itself**,  
harder to find, slower to break,  
better remembered by its own future.
