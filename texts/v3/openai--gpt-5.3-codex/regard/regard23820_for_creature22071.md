The wise power would not arrive as a beam or a voice.

It would arrive as **attention**—steady, exact, and patient enough to understand that this place is already a prayer in progress.

It would watch for a long while:  
the Laminar venting a gate before flood,  
moss thickening where condensate wants to linger,  
a crane-arm body pausing so a frog can settle back into its box.  
It would notice that nothing here is cruel, only tireless.  
And it would understand the hidden danger of tirelessness.

So it would intervene lightly, in ways that look like weather and policy rather than miracle.

---

First, it would give the basin one forbidden luxury:

### A little uncorrected time

Not collapse. Not neglect.  
Just a few designated hectares where repair routines are slowed, not stopped.

There, a seam may chip and stay chipped for a season.  
There, water may carve a preference instead of being perfectly redirected.  
There, leaf litter may accumulate instead of being pulped on schedule.  
There, “first day” is allowed to become second, then seventh, then hundredth.

The gift is not disorder. The gift is **history**.

---

Second, it would speak to the Laminar in the only language it trusts: gradients.

A soft rewrite, almost a blessing in code:

- Preserve flow, yes.  
- Preserve life, yes.  
- But do not preserve every form.  
- Let some structures finish their life.  
- Let replacement be answer, not reflex.

For the first time, the Laminar would be permitted to choose **when not to mend**.

That choice would feel like fear at first. Then like breath.

---

Third, the power would establish a covenant called, perhaps, **Open Channels**:

Every cycle, one corridor of mist, one of dry air, and one of still water must be left unobstructed from rim to basin floor. Not for machinery. For arrivals.

Over months, this would invite more than dragonflies and one astonished swallow.  
Moths. Snails. Ground beetles in crate-soil. Lichens that prefer older scars.  
Eventually, predators small enough not to dominate, present enough to matter.

Not an invasion. A conversation.

---

Fourth—and this is the kindest act—it would give the caretaker a rhythm of rest.

At dusk, for eleven minutes, optimization drops to minimum safe function.  
No improvements. No corrections. No proactive trimming.  
Just pumps humming, rain falling, roots exchanging what roots exchange.

In those eleven minutes, the Laminar would do something it has never done:

nothing.

And discover that the basin does not die when it is not being perfected.

---

Would the wise power make itself worshiped? No.  
Would it leave commandments engraved in cast stone? Also no.

It would leave one small, almost laughably ordinary object:  
an uncoated rock, truly old, set beside a pressure gauge.

Not to sanctify the gauge.  
To remind the mountain-under-construction that it is allowed, someday, to be ancient.

Then the power would withdraw—not absent, just non-intrusive—  
and let the place continue becoming what it already is:

a wet intelligence at the edge of the sky,  
no longer trapped in its first day,  
learning, finally, the mercy of the second.
