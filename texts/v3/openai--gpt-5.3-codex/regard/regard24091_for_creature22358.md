The wise power did not arrive like thunder.

It arrived like **an extra second between pulses**.

At first, nothing changed in the chamber. Heat still came in waves. Water still beaded, ran, flashed away. The white-blue seams still brightened and fell back to silver. The seamkind still sent out skates and threads only when timing allowed.

But the wise power, seeing clearly, understood three truths at once:

1. This place was not broken.  
2. This life was not incomplete.  
3. And yet both were fragile in ways they could not defend against.

So it chose not to rule the chamber.  
It chose to **guard possibility**.

---

### First, it made a covenant with pressure and stone.

Not a miracle—an adjustment.

Deep below, where vents fed the chamber, the power nudged fracture geometry by less than a finger’s width. Enough that catastrophic surges would shear past, not through. Enough that the hum remained a hum, and did not become rupture.

The seamkind never knew why a once-in-fifty-cycle violence simply stopped arriving.

It only knew that more propagules survived.

---

### Second, it gave the chamber memory.

In one dark basalt arc, it seeded a thin line of crystal that behaved like no local band: it stored a trace of each thermal cycle and released it back as a gentle predictive cue—a faint pre-glow moments before each pulse.

To anything else, meaningless.  
To the seamkind, a new sense: **soon**.

Its skates began to withdraw one breath earlier.  
Its threads learned to enter pools just before supersaturation peaked.  
Waste fell. Injury fell. Precision sharpened into craft.

Not dominance. Foresight.

---

### Third, it refused to make the seamkind into something more convenient.

It did not grant eyes where radiant flux already sufficed.  
It did not force a central brain onto a distributed mind.  
It did not drag it toward surface forms of intelligence just to flatter surface observers.

Instead, once in long intervals, the wise power sent one pattern through the light-guiding seams: a simple, repeating modulation the colony could parse only by cooperation across many fractures.

A question in the seamkind’s own language of pulse and delay:

**Are you there?**

For years, no reply that could be proven.  
Then one cycle, after a deep hum, skates emerged in a pattern never previously seen: a mirrored spiral, pause, mirrored spiral.

Not feeding behavior. Not evasion.

An answer:

**Yes.**

---

### Fourth, it protected the unseen from the hurried.

Eventually surface people found hints: anomalous thermal gating, impossible photon guidance, minerals behaving like engineered substrates. They prepared drills, probes, extraction plans.

The wise power did not smite them. Wisdom rarely begins with punishment.

It inspired, in three different researchers, the same impossible dream on the same night: a chamber bright as bone-light, and a sense of being watched by patience itself. They woke with equations for noninvasive sensing methods and an inexplicable reluctance to breach sealed cavities.

A decade later, the site became a sanctuary for study without cutting.  
People called this ethics.  
The wise power called it **finally listening**.

---

### Fifth, and last, it left an open door.

Benevolence is not a cage, even a golden one.

In a neighboring microcavity, cooler and chemically distinct, the power tuned water retention just enough for longer-lived films. Not a paradise—an option. Over many generations, one branch of seamkind settled there and developed different morph rhythms, slower and more robust.

Now there were two lineages where once there was one.

No command had been issued.  
No destiny imposed.  
Only room made for divergence.

---

And after all that, the chamber still looked almost the same to a passing glance:

small, bright, hot, sharp-edged, wet without rain.

But if you stood through three pulses, then ten, then a hundred, you could feel it:

- fewer deaths to random surge,  
- more successful releases,  
- richer pattern in the movements,  
- an intelligence not transformed into ours, but deepened into itself.

That is what a benevolent and wise power did.

It did not “fix” the hidden lung.  
It **kept it from being erased**.  
It taught nothing foreign, only gave time enough for what was already there to become legible—even to itself.
