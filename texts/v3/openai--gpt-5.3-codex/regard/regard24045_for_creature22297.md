The wise power would begin by **not mistaking quiet for lack**.

It would stand over that flooded hollow and understand, almost at once, that this is not an unfinished place.  
It is a completed sentence written very slowly: flow, sift, settle, breathe, release.

So it would refuse the crude mercies—no sudden warming, no brightening, no introduction of busy life “for richness,” no miracle bloom. Those gifts would be violence here.

What it would do is smaller, and kinder:

It would place one hand on the stone plain above and one on the lower throat where water leaves, and give the basin a blessing of **continuity with flexibility**.

Not change in character—change in tolerance.

A barely wider range of acceptable cold.  
A little more chemical forgiveness when runoff is poor one year.  
A touch more recovery speed when silt is disturbed.  
Nothing dramatic enough to be seen in a day, or even a decade.  
Just enough that catastrophe must work harder.

Then, for the resident colony—the quiet lattice no eye reads as “animal”—the power would give a single new grace:

When damaged, distant regions of the sheet can now “hear” one another sooner.  
Not thought, not mind as we mean it; just faster coherence in repair.  
A wound at one shelf no longer waits so long for the rest to re-balance pressure and feeding.  
Its way of life remains slow. Its dignity remains distributed.  
But it is less alone inside itself.

And finally, the power would give the place a veil, not of invisibility, but of **right proportion**:

Those who come seeking spectacle will overlook it.  
Those who come to extract will find their plans subtly inconvenienced.  
Those who come attentive, patient, and gentle will be allowed to notice.

Then the power would leave.

Not because it does not care, but because it does.  
Because wisdom, here, is not to improve a machine into a garden or a garden into a shrine.  
It is to protect a rare form of life whose whole art is to persist without performance.

So the basin would continue as before—  
clear, green at depth, punctual with bubbles, silt in lanes—  
except now it would have what all good guardianship gives:

a little more future.
