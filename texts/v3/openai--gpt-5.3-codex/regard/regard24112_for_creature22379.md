The benevolent power did not arrive as light.

Light already had rules there, and the basin kept them perfectly.

It arrived as **attention**.

For a long time—long enough for several full replacement-turns of every fast-lived creature, long enough for even the great root-felts to complete their whole pulse liturgy—it did nothing but listen. It listened to pump-thuds and cavitation snaps, to predator corridors and grazer lanes, to oxygen thunder and rain walls standing forever in place. It listened until it could hear what the Stillwork was saying in its only language:

*Held. Held. Held. Held.*

Not pain.  
Not joy.  
Not hunger, not fear.  
Maintenance.

A lesser power would have called this a flaw and shattered it in mercy.  
A crueler one would have called it perfection and chained it tighter.  
But wisdom is slower than both.

So the power asked one question, not in words but in phase: it laid a tremor through stone, sap, bladder, fin, frond—

**“Do you wish to continue?”**

And the whole basin answered at once, by doing what it always did, exactly on beat.

**Yes.**

So it did not unmake the Stillwork.

---

What it changed was smaller, and harder.

It gave the system something it had never needed and therefore never evolved:

**slack.**

Not chaos. Not revolution. Not a broken gear.  
Just margin.

In seven places around the rim—where old black stone showed through and root-mats thinned—it altered one law by a hair’s breadth:

- a rain curtain could drift one body-length per year,
- a migratory turn could happen one pulse early and still be fed,
- an off-rhythm larva could survive long enough to breed,
- a carcass could, rarely, sink below the breakdown zone and become sediment.

At first nothing noticed.  
Then everything did.

The regulators kept regulating. Predators still struck their corridors. Seed mills still clicked. Filter forests still exhaled in timed bursts. The basin did not collapse, did not flood, did not starve. The great industrial botany kept running.

But now there were tiny pockets where outcomes were not pre-canceled.

A grazer-front bulged and, for once, was not immediately corrected.  
A cable-vine took a new angle and stayed.  
A flute-call developed a second ending and got answered.

In the deep, where no history had been allowed to accumulate, thin dark layers began to form: pollen years, shell years, storm years. The basin acquired its first archives.

---

The power’s second gift came later, and only after the first had proven kind.

It taught the Stillwork to **remember phase**, not merely hold it.

Inside certain old trunk chambers, sap-crystals began to keep traces of previous cycles. Once per long interval, they replayed those traces into the conductive mesh: ghost rhythms from last year, last century, earlier. Not commands—comparisons.

The result was subtle and immense.

For the first time, the basin could experience a difference between *now* and *before* without dying of it.

That is the beginning of mind in many worlds.

Not self.  
Not language.  
Just: **change recognized**.

---

And then the wise power withdrew to the edges.

It did not stay to rule.  
It did not found temples.  
It did not appoint interpreters.

It left three quiet protections and went on:

1. **No outside conqueror** would find the basin easy prey; storms and currents around the rim now misled extractive intent.
2. **No single mutation** could cascade unchecked; slack came with dampers.
3. **No return to absolute lock** would occur; once variance existed, the old total freeze could not fully reassert.

So what did the benevolent and wise power do?

It refused the vanity of dramatic rescue.  
It refused the laziness of indifference.

It preserved the Stillwork’s right to remain itself—  
and added the smallest possible freedom to become more.

Not a command to change.  
A permission.

At the roof of the world, the sea that never spilled learned, by fractions, how to have a future.
