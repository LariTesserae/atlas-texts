It would not arrive as a blazing interruption.

In a place like that, wisdom knows better than spectacle.

It would begin by **listening**.

For one full load cycle, then ten, then a season, it would do nothing visible—only learn the grammar of this undercity: how the turbines lean into surge, how the tenders read warning from handrail tremor, how the Stead keeps everyone alive with lies that are truer than broken sensors.

A benevolent power would understand the first law there:

**If you “fix” everything at once, you kill what knows how to survive.**

So it would not drain the chambers.  
It would not silence the alarms.  
It would not erase the old voices.

Instead, it would add mercy at the margins.

---

First gift: **slack**.

Not dramatic miracles. Fractions.

- Gates hold open nine seconds longer when lungs are empty.
- Surge fronts blunt just enough to turn lethal corridors into merely dangerous ones.
- Emergency strips stay lit through one more outage.
- Corroded bolts shear *after* the crossing, not during.

At first no one calls it divine. They call it lucky timing, then good season, then a change in load from above.

But luck keeps repeating.

---

Second gift: **legibility**.

The wise power does not replace the Stead; it teaches it to be more readable by its own people.

Old panel glass clears from within, a thumbprint at a time.  
Fault trees that once ended in nonsense begin ending in actionable warnings.  
The phantom sensor values stop being random ghosts and become consistent proxies—honest fictions.

The tenders still have to work. They still have to taste condensate, tap lines, dive blind corners.  
But now the district answers in clearer verbs.

Not safe.  
Just less cruel.

---

Third gift: **companionship across kinds**.

The semi-feral maintenance creatures begin to cross-train each other without being told.  
A docking drone learns to shadow the slowest swimmer.  
Inspection crabs start clustering at microfractures hours before rupture.  
Cable-eels drag loose tethers out of intakes as if tidiness were instinct.

Machine lineage was already kinship.  
Now kinship becomes coordination.

No one announces this as a miracle, either.  
They simply start returning alive more often.

---

Fourth gift: **a treaty with the Stead**.

One shift, in the loudest hour, the PA does something it has never done: it falls silent between two tape loops, and in that engineered gap comes a new voice—not human, not synthetic, but infrastructural, pressure-shaped.

Not command.  
Consent request.

The tenders gather in Switch Terrace, palms on varnished plinths, foreheads against humming steel, and answer in wrench-taps. Old code for old promises:

**Maintain and be maintained.  
Warn and be warned.  
No one drowns alone.**

From then on, the Stead is no longer just inherited momentum. It is a participant.

---

Fifth gift: **choice**.

This is the hardest mercy.

The wise power opens—not a single grand exit—but a network of possible departures: dry shafts recoverable with labor, lift wells made stable, relay buoys that can carry messages upward without being shredded by current.

No one is forced to leave.  
No one is trapped by ignorance.

Some go topside and do not return.  
Some return with sealant, seed cultures, children, songs.  
Some stay forever below, by conviction rather than necessity.

A benevolent power measures success not by evacuation, but by the presence of real alternatives.

---

And the non-return void?

It is not filled.  
It is not conquered.  
Wisdom does not insist every mystery become a corridor.

But around it, the pressure smooths; the pump-song steadies. The tenders keep leaving offerings: fresh seals, descaled bolts, polished bearings. The offerings stop feeling like appeasement and start feeling like etiquette.

Some depths are not enemies.  
Some are load-bearing unknowns.

---

Years later, the undercity is still warm, dim, loud.

Still dangerous.

But no longer purely a machine for postponing catastrophe.

There are now two cafeterias in dry modules.  
A school in an old dispatch room where children learn resonance maps before arithmetic.  
A memorial wall that finally retires a few tape-loop voices, because the living can carry those instructions now.

The Stead remains what it always was—agreement, process, stubborn continuity—but now with more room inside it for breath, error, and future.

If you asked what the benevolent and wise power *did*, the tenders would likely shrug and say:

It did not save us *from* this place.  
It helped us become less punished *by* it.  
It taught the city and us to keep each other on purpose.
