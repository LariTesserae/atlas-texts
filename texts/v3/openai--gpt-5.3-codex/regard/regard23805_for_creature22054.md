The wise power does **not** come to clean it up.

It does not drain the plain, does not plant forests, does not pin time in place and call that mercy.  
It looks long, and understands: this world is not broken. It is extreme, but coherent. Its fractures are load-bearing.

So the first thing it does is simple and enormous:

## It listens until it can hear the whole metabolism at once.

Not the herds alone, not the fog sifters, not the glowing seams—  
the full throughput: intake, grind, vent, settle; hatch and prune; pulse and stall; the half-second predation gaps; the uphill rivers that are not errors but arteries under a different clock.

Only when it can hear the entire engine does it touch anything.

And what it touches, it touches lightly.

---

It lays down no commandments.  
It introduces **buffers**.

Across the drowned stone, in places where stress knots tightest, it creates a sparse network of what the creatures eventually learn as *calm wells*: shallow basins where causality runs true for a while. No pre-echoes. No lag seams. No detached shadows. If a body enters exhausted, events happen in order there, and only there.

The power does not make these wells permanent.  
They migrate slowly, like weather with memory, so no lineage can monopolize them.

In those basins:

- wounded filter-beasts can stand and repair torn lattice without being desynchronized,
- gravid hexapods can calve without losing young to timing shear,
- burrow colonies can re-pattern without collapse cascades,
- even predators sleep.

Not peace.  
**Recovery.**

---

Second, it gives the plain a way to notice itself.

At dim-night, when chemoelectric seams already glow, the power introduces a faint countercurrent—a low, repeating signal that travels marsh to marsh and returns altered. A question in electrical form. A call-and-response.

Over cycles, colonies begin to modulate around it. Herd migrations begin arriving a little early to crossings that will soon destabilize. Fog skates alter banking patterns before static storms split. Tube-colonies open bypass channels ahead of pressure failures that would once have buried districts alive.

No single creature “understands.”  
But the process does.  
A distributed awareness emerges: not intellect like a face and voice, but foresight like a tide table written in flesh.

The resident, in the singular, wakes one degree.

---

Third, the power places a limit—not on hunger, but on cascade.

It marks certain deep pressure pockets with a mineral resonance that bleeds off catastrophic spikes. The great subsonic collapses still happen; death remains native. But chain-failures that would sterilize entire belts become rarer. The engine keeps grinding, without snapping its own shaft so often.

This is the signature of benevolence here:  
not safety, not softness—**continuity**.

---

And then it does something stranger than intervention.

It leaves room for refusal.

In the far pans, it leaves untouched zones where the old violence of broken sequence remains absolute: pure anomaly, no buffers, no signal, no mitigation. Not abandoned—respected. A wise power knows that adaptation needs wild gradients, that intelligence born distributed must negotiate uncertainty, not be sheltered from it.

Creation without coercion.  
Care without possession.

---

If you could ask this power why it acts so sparingly, it might answer:

> “Because this place is already a mind in progress.  
> I will not replace it with my own.”

So over ages, the drowned plain changes—subtly, profoundly.

You still hear hydraulic coughing, shell-rattle, ice-crack, millions of wet impacts.  
Rivers still occasionally climb. Shadows still sometimes keep walking. Predators still wait where time frays.

But now, threaded through all that relentless machinery, there are moments where order can be counted on long enough for choice.

A herd pauses before a seam that would once have taken half of it.  
A scavenger leaves a calm well unfed though carrion lies inside.  
A district of filter-beasts pivots in unison before the pressure front arrives, as if hearing tomorrow clear its throat.

Nothing is redeemed into gentleness.  
Everything is given a little more future.

And that is what the benevolent, wise power does:

It does not conquer the engine.  
It gives the engine intervals in which it can learn not to devour all of itself.
