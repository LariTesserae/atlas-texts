They would begin by **not rescuing** it.

A wise benevolence, seeing that plateau, would understand at once: this is not a wound to be closed with abundance.  
It is a covenant already in force.

So the power would arrive quietly, in the only language the Steadfast trust — **continuity**.

---

### First, it would listen.

Not to prayers (there are few), but to:

- pump harmonics at shift change  
- thermal lag in old escarpment foundries  
- the click-pattern of sheath-beast claws on service grates  
- the voting rhythms of distributed process minds  
- the long silences between spoken words in open plazas

It would learn that this civilization is alive by restraint.  
Any gift that increases appetite faster than capacity would be a curse.

So it would refuse the obvious miracles:
no sudden rivers, no green explosions, no bright sky, no infinite fuel.

---

### Then it would act — but in margins.

Not spectacle. **Tolerance**.

A bearing rated for 80 years now lasts 83.  
Sealants fail less abruptly.  
Microfractures propagate slower by a fraction no one can see, only survive.  
Dust storms that would have crossed a critical intake turn six degrees and miss it.

Not enough to make them dependent.  
Enough to reduce catastrophe into maintenance.

The Steadfast would notice in their own way, not with hymns but with logs:

> *Unexpected extension in Line 3 ceramic roller life.*  
> *Anomaly: simultaneous near-failure events desynchronized by 11 minutes.*  
> *Incident class reduced from terminal to recoverable.*

They would not call it grace.  
But they would start leaving a blank field in reports labeled simply: **Unattributed Favorable Variance**.

---

### It would also give them one hard gift: memory that cannot be lost.

In old districts where stencils already survive centuries, the power would set a deeper archive:  
causal memory embedded in materials themselves.

Touch a wall panel from a failed coolant decade, and your interface doesn’t just show records — it conveys sequence, pressure, decision, consequence.  
No mythologizing. No blame drift. No amnesia by committee.

A wise power knows that most collapse begins with forgotten causes.

This gift would feel less like revelation and more like better diagnostics.  
Exactly right for them.

---

### Only after long trust would it offer a choice.

Not “Become more.”  
Not “Leave.”

A choice in their own moral grammar:

1. Remain as they are, indefinitely stabilized.  
2. Spend stored resilience to seed a second plateau-city elsewhere, carrying their craft outward.  
3. Begin a slow atmospheric revision over millennia, accepting new ecologies and new uncertainties.

The power would not pick for them.  
Benevolence without consent is only polished coercion.

So it would wait while crews deliberate for years, then decades, across districts and oversight minds, with children included because they will inherit the consequence.

---

### And if they chose nothing?

Then nothing dramatic would happen.  
And that too would be mercy.

The power would keep doing what it came to do:
hold disaster at the edge,
honor their tempo,
and let a durable people remain durable.

---

In the end, the wisest thing such a power could do in that place is this:

**make survival a little less expensive, without making meaning cheap.**
