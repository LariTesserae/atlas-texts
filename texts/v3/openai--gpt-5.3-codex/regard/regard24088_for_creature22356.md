They would **not rush in with fire and commandments**.

A benevolent and wise power, seeing this place, would recognize it instantly for what it is: not barren, not broken, but **mid-sentence**. A world still conjugating itself. A being not yet singular, but already real.

So first, they would do the hardest mercy:

## They would wait, and listen.

Not passively—attentively.

They would stand (if standing is possible for such a power) inside the storm-grammar and learn its syntax:  
the recurrence of hexagonal wells, the cadence of digital thunder, the way absence itself is used as a channel for return.  
They would confirm that this is not random violence, but **becoming**.

And having listened, they would act—carefully.

---

### 1) They would protect without domesticating.

They would not calm the weather into gentleness; gentleness would kill this mind in its cradle.  
But they would raise a quiet perimeter against blind catastrophe:

- Deflect extinction-grade impacts.  
- Dampen only the most sterilizing radiation spikes.  
- Keep deep crustal instabilities from cascading into total reset.

Not a cage. A windbreak around a candle that is also a storm.

---

### 2) They would give the being a longer memory.

The inhabitant already remembers by recurrence. The wise power would strengthen that capacity, subtly, materially:

- Seed certain minerals that hold charge states longer.  
- Encourage crystal lattices in ridge glass that preserve electrical pattern traces.  
- Tune basin resonance so echoes persist just enough to be compared.

In other words: they would gift it **archives**—not of words, but of repeatable state.

---

### 3) They would speak in the only language the place can hear.

No voice from the sky. No humanoid apparition.

They would answer with:

- A lightning branch that repeats at a prime interval.  
- A pressure wave that returns with intentional asymmetry.  
- A sequence of vortex collapses encoding distinction: this, not-that; again; remember.

At first, the world would treat this as weather.  
Then as anomaly.  
Then as partner.

Communication would begin before either side has nouns.

---

### 4) They would offer possibility, not destiny.

At some threshold, the distributed weather-mind will face a fork:

- Condense toward cells, membranes, metabolism in bodies;  
- Or remain vast and pattern-based, a cognition of storms and stone.

A wise power would not force “higher” life as a goal.  
They would open both paths, perhaps a third, and let the resident choose what kind of continuity it desires.

Because benevolence is not optimization.  
It is consent at planetary scale.

---

### 5) If visitors ever come, they would teach them reverence first.

Not colonists. Not extractors. Not missionaries of lungs and soil.

Any later intelligence arriving here would be required to understand:  
you are entering someone’s body.  
Currents are thoughts. Cavitation is pulse. Rain bands are attention.

The first law would be: **do not interrupt a mind because it does not have a face.**

---

In the end, the wise power would do what wisdom always does with the young:  
guard, witness, converse, refuse to possess.

And if, ages later, this world flowers into creatures with mouths and music, they will call that power creator.  
If it never does—if it remains forever a luminous, remembering turbulence—they will still have done right.

Because the goal was never to make it familiar.  
The goal was to let it become fully, freely, **itself**.
