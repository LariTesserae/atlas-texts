It did **not** arrive as a hand from the sky.

A wise power, seeing a world where looking can wound and attention can force growth, would know the first ethic immediately:

**Do not stare at what is still becoming.**

So it came lightly—at the edge of perception, like overcast weather under stone.  
Not a face. Not a voice. A change in *how harm failed to happen*.

---

### 1) It learned the chamber’s grammar before speaking

It watched in fragments, never in a fixed gaze.  
It watched by reflection in droplets, by tremor in moss threads, by the runners’ synchronized pauses.  
It learned wet-time from dry-time.  
It learned that a cup in a niche is a clock, and an arc of stones is a verb.

Only then did it attempt contact.

Not in words, at first. In courtesy.

A ceiling tooth that would have sheared loose in the next heavy drip instead held one more season.  
A fungal lace, usually dead by morning, lasted through a dry spell.  
A crack destined to seal stayed soft long enough for one more molt cycle.

This is how wise powers introduce themselves:  
not by miracles that overwrite a place,  
but by small continuities offered where endings were imminent.

---

### 2) It asked permission in the only language the seam-warden trusts

During a wet interval, when the chamber was bright enough to cast two shadows from one body, the seam-warden rose.

Not fully. Enough.

Runners braided a low ring.  
Gliders held an orbit like punctuation.  
Moths pressed and unpressed from clay in a patterned pulse.  
Crystal lips budded in short repeating rows along a damp seam: a question-form.

The power answered by refusing to answer too fast.

It waited through three drip cycles, then altered one bead’s fall so it struck not center but edge, producing a second pattern in mineral bloom—a respectful uncertainty, an offered presence, no claim.

In that exchange a compact formed:

- I will not fix you.  
- I will not harvest you.  
- I will not name you into smaller shapes.  
- If I shelter you, it will be from violence, not from change.

The seam-warden did not bow.  
It resumed becoming.  
That was consent enough.

---

### 3) It made a sanctuary of misdirection

Benevolence without wisdom often cages what it loves.  
This power did the opposite: it made the chamber difficult to possess.

To cruel eyes, the hillside became ordinary to the point of boredom.  
Survey lines slipped.  
Instruments returned clean, meaningless readings.  
Maps agreed on everything except this one fold, where contour refused memory.

Predators still found it. Weather still reached it. Time still worked.  
But extractive intent—greedy attention, hungry certainty—slid off like dry dust from wet calcite.

It did not hide the place from all.  
It hid it from the wrong way of seeing.

---

### 4) It widened the migration of unfinishedness

The seam-warden could not live forever in one chamber; stability would eventually harden the walls beyond conversation.  
So the power prepared futures uphill, downslope, and deeper in fracture-dark.

Not by building habitats outright.  
By arranging chances:

A root found a faultline and held it open.  
A freeze-thaw season made six new microvoids instead of one collapse.  
Trace minerals arrived in ratios favorable to light-film bloom.  
A forgotten seep resumed.

Years later, runners appeared there first.  
Then gliders.  
Then cups in niches where no hands had been seen.

The power did not preserve a museum specimen.  
It preserved a *way of life* by preserving a chain of beginnings.

---

### 5) It gave one gift, and only one

Not immortality.  
Not dominion.  
Not speech in human tongues.

It gave the seam-warden a narrow immunity:  
the ability to feel hostile attention at a distance, before direct gaze could trigger forced growth or panic dissolution.

A warning shimmer in the fish-veils.  
A bitter note in the metallic wet scent.  
A delay in drip where there should have been release.

Enough to redistribute before harm arrived.  
Enough to choose invisibility without losing self.

A good gift is one that increases agency without rewriting identity.

---

### 6) It accepted loss as part of care

Some cups still dried empty.  
Some molts failed.  
Some chamber sections aged out and were abandoned to stillness.

The power did not intervene in every grief.  
To do so would turn becoming into display, and life into dependency.

Instead, it attended thresholds:

catastrophe into survivable damage,  
extinction into migration,  
isolation into encounter.

Where there would have been none left, there was one line of runners.  
Where there would have been one line, there were three.

---

And if, much later, a human kneels in a new forming place and notices crystal budding only when watched—and pausing when not—what then?

If they are gentle, they may feel the benevolent power near them too, like a hand held open but never closing.

Not saying *This is mine.*  
Saying, as wise powers do:

**Be careful with your attention. It is heavier than you think.**  
**This life is not finished. Let it stay unfinished.**
