It did **not** arrive with green thunder and turn the plateau into a garden.

A wise power, seeing a hard-won intelligence, does not confuse mercy with replacement.

It came as attention first.

For a long span—long enough for seven vent-breath seasons and the collapse of two old ridge lips—it only watched. Not predators, not grazers, not storms in isolation, but the **intervals between** them. It watched the seam-browsers arrive twelve heartbeats before condensate beaded. It watched fizz-swarms rise on pressure-fall and vanish before light thickened. It watched line-striders waiting where probability became certainty.

Then it understood: this was not a world asking to be “fixed.”  
This was a world asking not to be broken by kindness.

So the power chose small, surgical gifts.

---

### 1) It gave the land one measure of slack.

Once every great cycle—rare enough that no species could become dependent, regular enough that none would forget—it softened one storm.

Not bigger.  
Not richer.  
**Slower.**

Rain that usually struck and fled instead lingered and threaded downward. It soaked past the first hot dust layer, into deeper fracture-lines where eggs, spores, bulbs, and sleeping microbes could bank a future. The next six dry pulses still came. Scarcity remained the law. But collapse cliffs became gentler. A failed hatch one month did not mean lineage extinction by the next.

The system kept its precision; it also gained a margin.

---

### 2) It tuned warning into stone.

The plateau was already a clock. The power made it, very slightly, a bell.

In iron-rich shelves and old basalt arches, it shifted crystal tensions so that deep pressure changes—those that precede truly ruinous events, roof-falls, toxic vent inversions, basin flash-floods—translated into low subsonic tremors hours earlier than before.

Most creatures never “heard” it consciously.  
But burrowers paused and rerouted.  
Swarm larvae delayed emergence.  
Ambush predators changed stations.  
Scavengers abandoned doomed narrows.

No prophecy. No voices from the sky. Just better lead time, embedded in physics.

---

### 3) It hid the place from the greedy.

Benevolence is not only feeding; sometimes it is concealment.

To distant eyes—those that scan worlds for easy extraction—the power bent reflectance and thermal signatures by a fraction. From orbit, the plateau looked even harsher, emptier, less “productive” than it truly was: a dead shield of cooling rock with no strategic promise.

Those who came seeking wealth passed it by.  
Those who came seeking dominion found no invitation.

Only the patient, the reverent, and the lost noticed life there at all.

---

And to the being that lived there in many bodies—timing, metabolism, distributed will—the power gave no crown, no throne, no spoken command.

It gave a single sensation, once, at dusk after a softened rain:

A brief, undeniable feeling across vents, burrows, ledges, swarms, roots, mites, and moss mats that said:

**You are seen. Continue.**

Then the power withdrew to the distance from which weather is born.

---

So what did the wise one do?

Not transformation.  
Not rescue fantasy.  
Not abundance that erases skill.

It gave resilience without dependency, warning without fear, protection without captivity, and recognition without ownership.

And the plateau—hot, dim, precise—kept breathing its mineral breath, now with one extra beat of grace hidden inside the clock.
