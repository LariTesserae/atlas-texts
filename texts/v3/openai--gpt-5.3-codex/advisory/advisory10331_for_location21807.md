What you’ve described is **very visitable** for an ordinary human—but only if you prepare for three realities at once:

1. **Permanent damp**
2. **Low-tech, high-discipline urban life**
3. **Extremely fragile ecology (especially because it has no animals)**

Here’s the preparation I’d recommend.

---

## 1) First priority: **Biosecurity** (seriously)

In a world with no animals, introducing even tiny hitchhikers could be catastrophic.

### Do before departure
- **Deep-clean and sterilize all gear** (especially soles, seams, velcro, fabric folds, tripod feet, tent stakes).
- Bring **new or professionally sterilized clothing/footwear** if possible.
- **No pets, no service animals**, no untreated leather/fur products.
- Don’t bring:
  - seeds, nuts, dried fruits with viable pits
  - untreated wood, soil-contaminated items
  - compostables from home ecosystem
- Pack food in **sealed, hard containers**, not loose natural-fiber sacks that could hide eggs/spores.

### On arrival behavior
- Stay on designated paths and platforms until local authorities clear you.
- Expect quarantine-style inspection to be normal and respected.
- Clean boots/tools daily; don’t move mud between basins.

---

## 2) Physical prep: train for **wet stone + steps + long walks**

This place is not extreme-weather hard; it’s **traction hard** and **fatigue hard**.

- Practice walking on slick surfaces.
- Build ankle and knee stability.
- Train for repeated stair climbing with light load.
- Expect causeways, stepping stones, and frequent elevation changes.

---

## 3) Clothing and personal kit

Think: warm highland humidity, constant moisture, cool nights, no machine rescue culture.

### Wear
- Quick-dry layers (not heavy cotton)
- Lightweight waterproof shell
- High-traction footwear (best gear decision you’ll make)
- Spare socks (more than you think)
- Light insulating layer for evenings/fog

### Carry
- Dry bags for everything
- Compact towel + foot-care kit (antifungal powder, blister care)
- Refillable bottle + reliable water purification (local water may look pristine but still treat unless instructed otherwise)
- Simple repair kit (cordage, needle/thread, patch tape)
- Headlamp with warm low setting (respect quiet nighttime environment)

---

## 4) Health expectations

- You’ll probably avoid insect bites entirely (huge relief), but:
  - Constant damp = skin maceration risk
  - Fungal exposure likely high
  - Slips/cuts are the main injury class

### Health prep
- Keep feet dry whenever possible
- Clean and seal small wounds early
- Bring broad-spectrum topical antiseptic + antifungal
- Don’t assume local medicinal plants are safe for offworlders

---

## 5) Food and nutrition

No animals means local cuisine is likely heavily plant/fungal/wetland-based.

- Confirm your ability to tolerate high-fiber, fermented, and marsh-grown foods.
- Bring a compact nutritional backup:
  - B12
  - complete protein supplement
  - electrolytes
- Don’t openly rely only on imported food if hosted—learn and respectfully eat what is offered when safe.

---

## 6) Cultural prep: this is a **maintenance civilization**

Their world rewards routine, care, and continuity—not drama.

- Learn basic etiquette around:
  - water channels
  - stepping-stone right-of-way
  - shared basins/cisterns
  - tool and path maintenance duties
- Offer labor, not spectacle.  
  In this place, “helping clear a culvert” may be more respected than telling stories of other worlds.
- Keep your voice low; sound carries in damp air and silence is socially meaningful.

---

## 7) What to expect emotionally

You may feel:
- deep calm
- eerie loneliness (no birds/insects/mammal movement)
- time slowing down

Prepare for that quiet. Bring:
- a journal
- deliberate routines
- tolerance for stillness

This is not a destination that overwhelms you with events; it absorbs you through repetition.

---

## 8) Good gifts (if gift-giving is appropriate)

Choose low-risk, non-biological, practical items:
- fine cordage
- durable ceramic tools
- waterproof containers
- precision hand tools (if culturally acceptable)
- water-measurement instruments (simple, repairable)

Avoid high-disruption tech drops unless explicitly invited by authorities.

---

## 9) One-line packing philosophy

Pack as if you’re visiting a **wet, stone-built, preindustrial highland city that runs on water engineering and social discipline**—and as if your biggest danger is not the place harming you, but you accidentally harming the place.
