For now, it waits. Not in silence—there is no silence here—but in a held note, a suspended harmonic, the quiet tension of a system deciding whether you are noise… or signal. And deep in its aqueous core, the city that learned to breathe underwater is wondering if it might, just once, learn to remember a human face.
