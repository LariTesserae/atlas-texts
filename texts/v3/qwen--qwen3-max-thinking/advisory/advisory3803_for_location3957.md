### **1. Understand Your Role: You Are a Guest in a Machine’s Dream**
The Sumpvault is not abandoned—it is *occupied* by process. Treat it as sacred infrastructure, not a ruin to be plundered or photographed. Approach with humility. You are less an explorer than a witness. Assume nothing is safe to touch, nothing stable, nothing benign. Even the silence has weight.

> **Mindset mantra**: *I am here to listen, not to leave a mark.*

### **2. Physical Preparation: Armor Against the Sublime Hostility**

#### **Thermal & Respiratory Protection**
- **Heat Index 2.9** means ambient temperatures hover near 40–45°C (104–113°F), compounded by humidity from condensation and exhaust. Your core will overheat rapidly.
  - Wear **lightweight, moisture-wicking thermal base layers** (not cotton—it holds sweat and chills you when you stop moving).
  - Carry a **personal evaporative cooling vest** or neck wrap soaked in clean water *before entry* (water inside is contaminated).
- **Air quality is toxic**: suspended particulates, metal oxides, hydrocarbon residue, and trace industrial volatiles.
  - A **P100-rated respirator with organic vapor cartridges** is non-negotiable. Standard dust masks won’t cut it.
  - Bring **sealed goggles**—not safety glasses. The air is gritty; blinking won’t clear it.

#### **Hydration & Contamination Protocol**
- **Do NOT drink or touch any water in the Sumpvault.** At 0.6 abundance, it’s chemically potent: likely laced with heavy metals, PCBs, or acidic leachate.
  - Carry **at least 1.5L of your own water** in a sealed, insulated bottle. Sip slowly—heat increases fluid loss.
  - Wear **nitrile gloves under durable work gloves**. Skin contact with surfaces may cause chemical burns or absorption of toxins.
  - **Decontaminate upon exit**: full change of clothes, thorough shower, nasal irrigation (to clear inhaled particulates).

#### **Footwear & Mobility**
- The floor is uneven: oily residue, loose debris, corroded grates, and unexpected puddles.
  - **Steel-toed, chemical-resistant boots** with aggressive tread (e.g., rubber outsoles rated for oil/wet surfaces).
  - **Knee pads recommended**—you’ll crouch, crawl, or kneel to observe details without touching.

### **3. Gear: Minimalism with Purpose**
The Sumpvault rewards subtlety. Bring only what serves observation or survival.

- **Lighting**:  
  - Primary: **Headlamp with red-light mode** (preserves night vision, less disruptive to ambient gloom).  
  - Backup: **Two chemical light sticks** (no sparks, no heat, silent).  
  - *Avoid bright white beams*—they shatter the vault’s granular dimness (1.3) and may trigger reflexive avoidance of the uncanny.
  
- **Recording Tools**:  
  - **Analog notebook + pencil** (digital devices may malfunction in EM fields from residual grid bleed; also, screens feel *wrong* here).

- **Emergency Items**:  
  - **Non-sparking multi-tool** (brass or aluminum—no ferrous metals that could ignite fumes).  
  - **Emergency thermal blanket** (compact, reflective—useful if you succumb to heat exhaustion).  
  - **Whistle** (metallic echoes carry; shouting distorts the acoustic ecology).

*Do not bring*:  
- Electronics with screens (they feel alien here, and may glitch).  
- Food (odors attract nothing—but you shouldn’t linger long enough to eat).  
- Anything you’re not prepared to lose. The vault swallows objects. Tools appear; others vanish.

> **Rule**: *Do not impose human purpose on systemic echoes.*

### **6. Psychological Readiness**
The Sumpvault is **dense (2.2)** not just physically but sensorially. Every surface whispers decay. The lack of fauna (0.1) creates an uncanny stillness that amplifies your own biological noise—your breath, your heartbeat, the creak of your joints. This can induce dissociation or paranoia.

- **Pre-entry meditation**: Focus on breath to anchor yourself *before* descent.
- **Post-exit decompression**: Sit in daylight for 30 minutes. Write immediately—details fade fast. The vault’s logic doesn’t translate well to surface memory.
- **Never go alone**. Not for safety (though that matters), but because the vault *feeds* on solitude. A companion breaks the feedback loop of your pulse echoing in the walls.

### **7. Ethical Covenant**
