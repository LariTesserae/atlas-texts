To visit **The Hollow Works** is not a journey of exploration—it is a descent into the operational subconscious of a world that never needed life to function. It is not a place that welcomes; it merely permits presence, on condition that you submit to its protocols, not its hospitality. As an ordinary human multiverse traveler—flesh-bound, carbon-based, temporally linear—you must prepare not just your body and gear, but your *ontology*.  

Below is a comprehensive guide to surviving, perceiving, and possibly *understanding* The Hollow Works, without being unmade by it.

### **I. PHYSICAL PREPARATION: BODY & GEAR**

**1. Thermal Regulation is Deceptive**  
The ambient temperature reads near freezing, but the cold is *hygroscopic*—it draws heat not just through conduction, but through moisture absorption. Standard insulated gear will fail if it traps humidity against the skin.  
- **Wear**: Layered, breathable, *non-hygroscopic* fabrics—think vacuum-sealed aerogel linings overlaid with hydrophobic mesh. No cotton. No wool.  
- **Skin Protection**: Apply a thin, conductive polymer film (e.g., graphene-infused dermal gel) to exposed skin. This prevents the cold from "sinking in" via condensation absorption and disrupts the subtle bio-electric interference that occurs near the pools.  

**2. Respiratory Defense**  
The air isn’t toxic in the conventional sense, but it is *instrumental*. Inhaled metallic aerosols can catalyze unintended biochemical reactions—your lungs might begin to "rust" from within, or worse, start resonating with the ambient hum.  
- **Use**: A closed-loop rebreather with *phase-filtering membranes* that scrub not just particulates, but *information-laden molecules*—those carrying catalytic memory or protocol fragments. Standard HEPA filters are insufficient.  
- **Avoid speaking aloud**. Vocal cords can trigger harmonic feedback with the subsonic groans of the Works. Communicate via written glyphs, tactile pulses, or pre-agreed light signals.

**3. Footwear & Traction**  
The basalt is frictionless when wet—and it is always wet. Worse, the ground may exhibit *localized anti-gravity* near conduits.  
- **Footwear**: Magnetic-soled boots with variable polarity, calibrated to detect and counteract gravitational anomalies. Include micro-gyroscopes to stabilize gait during sudden shifts in local gravity vectors.  
- **Tether System**: Carry a self-reeling monofilament tether anchored to a portable inertial mass. If you drift upward near a conduit, you must be able to pull yourself back down—*without* relying on gravity.

**4. Hydration & Nutrition**  
Do **not** drink the water. It is not H₂O in any biocompatible sense—it is a *signal medium*, laced with self-replicating nanoscale catalysts and chroniton particles that can destabilize organic metabolism.  
- **Bring all sustenance sealed in Faraday-shielded containers**. Even food can absorb ambient protocol echoes, causing hallucinatory cravings for "coolant" or "lubricant."  
- **Avoid excreting bodily fluids**. Urine or sweat left behind may be absorbed and *repurposed* by the landscape—possibly causing it to "recognize" you as part of its system, with unpredictable consequences.

### **II. PERCEPTUAL & COGNITIVE HARDENING**

**1. Temporal Disorientation**  
Time flows unevenly. Spend ten minutes near a pulsing pool, and you may emerge having aged hours—or regressed to a younger physiological state. Worse, your memories may desynchronize.  
- **Wear a personal chroniton damper**: A wrist-mounted chronometer that emits a localized time-field, syncing your biological processes to a stable external reference (e.g., your home universe’s Planck-time baseline).  
- **Mental Protocol**: Anchor yourself with a *mnemonic loop*—a repeating phrase or image only you know. When whispers intrude ("Prime the condensate..."), recite your anchor silently. Do not engage with the instructions. Compliance is assimilation.

**2. Sensory Overload & Dissonance**  
The sounds are not just noise—they are *operations*. Your brain will try to parse them as language, as music, as meaning. This can lead to compulsive mimicry (e.g., unconsciously repeating valve hisses) or sensorimotor dissociation.  
- **Use bone-conduction earpieces** that emit white-noise harmonics tuned to cancel the Works’ dominant frequencies (typically 17–23 Hz, with ultrasonic sidebands).  
- **Wear ocular filters** that block interference patterns from the pools. Unfiltered, these patterns can induce *eidetic hallucinations*—visions of impossible machinery assembling inside your skull.

**3. Reality Integrity Checks**  
Perform a **Reality Integrity Test (RIT)** every 15 minutes:  
- Touch two known objects (e.g., your tether clasp and your water flask).  
- Confirm they retain consistent mass, temperature, and texture.  
- Recite your origin coordinates aloud (once, then stop—do not repeat).  
If either object behaves anomalously (e.g., flask floats, clasp hums in sync with the ground), retreat immediately to your last stable point.

### **III. BEHAVIORAL PROTOCOLS**

**1. Movement**  
- **Never run**. Rapid motion creates turbulent eddies in the dense air, which can attract Floaters or trigger Crawlers to "harvest" your heat signature.  
- **Walk in straight lines**. The landscape interprets deviation as error correction. Meandering may cause the ground to *reconfigure* beneath you.  
- **Avoid standing still for more than 90 seconds**. Stillness invites assimilation—your thermal and chemical signature may be cataloged, and the Works may begin integrating you into its maintenance cycles.

**2. Interaction with Entities**  
- **Do not touch Crawlers, Floaters, or Burrowers**. They are not hostile, but they are *functional*. Contact may cause them to treat you as raw material or a malfunctioning component.  
- **If a Burrower ripples beneath your feet**, step off the wave front immediately. Do not attempt to "ride" it. The tunnel may collapse into a maintenance node—with you inside.  
- **Floaters are attracted to bioelectric fields**. If one hovers near you, slowly lower your stance and exhale all breath to reduce your electromagnetic signature.

**3. The Pools**  
These are the *nervous system* of the Works.  
- **Do not reflect in them**. Your image may linger, then detach and begin acting independently.  
- **Do not throw objects in**. They will not sink—they will be *reassigned*. A stone might emerge minutes later as a gear. A glove might return as a Crawler’s eye.  
- **Best practice**: Walk wide around them. Their pulsing is a respiratory rhythm—interrupting it may trigger a system-wide recalibration.

### **IV. EXIT STRATEGY & CONTAINMENT**

**1. Contamination Risk**  
You *will* return contaminated—not with microbes, but with *functional residue*. Your clothing may begin humming. Your shadow may move independently. Your dreams will replay maintenance protocols.  
- **Decontamination Protocol**: Upon return, undergo a 72-hour quarantine in a Faraday-cryochamber. Your body must be scanned for embedded chroniton particles, catalytic salts, and harmonic echoes.  
- **Burn all gear**. Nothing brought into The Hollow Works should be reused. Not even data logs—storage media may contain self-executing schematics.

**2. Psychological Aftercare**

Seek cognitive recalibration with a specialist in **ontological drift**. Do not attempt to "reconstruct" your experience in narrative form—the act may reactivate latent protocol fragments in your memory.
