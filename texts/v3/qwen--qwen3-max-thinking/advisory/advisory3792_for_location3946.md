**Preparation Guide for the Ordinary Human Multiverse Traveler Visiting the Great Flattening**

---

### **I. Psychological & Existential Preparation**

Before even considering physical gear or survival protocols, the traveler must prepare their *mind*. The Great Flattening is not hostile in the conventional sense—it is indifferent, and in its indifference lies a quiet, ontological erosion of self. The absence of life, sound, and even narrative context can induce profound dissociation. The silence isn't empty; it *presses*.

- **Mental Anchoring Rituals**: Establish a personal “narrative tether”—a repeating phrase, a melody, a memory loop—that you can return to when perception begins to destabilize. The Flattening feeds on narrative entropy; you must supply your own continuity.
  
- **Expect Ontological Drift**: You may feel as though your choices carry no weight, that cause and effect are negotiable. This is not delusion—it’s environmental. Counter it by performing small, deliberate acts with clear outcomes (e.g., “I place this stone here, and it stays until I move it again”). Reaffirm agency.

- **Avoid Prolonged Solitude**: Even seasoned multiversal travelers report a creeping sense of unreality after 48–72 hours alone. Travel with at least one companion whose baseline reality you trust implicitly. If solo travel is unavoidable, carry an AI companion trained in *phenomenological stabilization*—not just navigation or survival, but *reality reinforcement*.

---

### **II. Environmental Hazards & Protective Measures**

#### **Atmosphere & Respiration**
- **Air Composition**: Technically breathable, but so thin (≤0.3% Earth sea-level pressure) that oxygen diffusion is negligible. **Do not remove your helmet.** The “taste of nothing” is a warning: your lungs would pull vacuum, not air.
- **Pressure Suit Required**: A Class-IV Exogeological Suit with vacuum-rated integrity. Not for temperature, but for pressure differential. The suit must include a micro-recycler that scrubs CO₂ and generates O₂ from onboard reserves—no atmospheric harvesting possible.

#### **Thermal Regulation**
- **Ambient Temperature**: Ranges from –70°C (night) to +45°C (day) at the surface, but feels “1.4 on the scale of human comfort” during twilight due to near-zero humidity and radiative heat loss.
- **Suit Must Be Thermally Adaptive**: Layered insulation with phase-change materials. Avoid metal components—they conduct stellar wind energy unpredictably during Flares.

#### **Radiation & Data-Flares**
- **Baseline Cosmic Radiation**: Moderate. Manageable with standard shielding.
- **Flare Events**: These are not electromagnetic pulses but *information surges*—high-energy bursts of encoded causality that can scramble neural patterns and degrade unshielded electronics.
  - **Neural Dampeners**: Wear a cortical shield (e.g., Mnemosyne-7 band) that filters non-biological data streams. Without it, Flares may imprint alien logic structures into your short-term memory, causing recursive thought loops or temporary aphasia.
  - **Electronics**: Only use hardened, analog-dominant devices. Digital systems must run on isolated, write-once memory with causal firewalls. No wireless—Flares hijack resonant frequencies.

---

### **III. Locomotion & Terrain Navigation**

- **Footwear**: Magnetic-soled boots with micro-grippers. The sintered crust is brittle and may *unwrite* beneath you. Test each step with a probe before committing weight.
- **Footprints Fade**: Not due to wind, but terrain reversion. Mark your path with *temporally anchored beacons*—devices that emit a low-frequency chroniton pulse, "locking" their position in local time for up to 12 hours.
- **Horizon Illusions**: The scale distorts depth perception. Use lidar or quantum rangefinders—never estimate distance by eye. Structures that appear “a kilometer away” may be 30 km distant… or exist in a slightly different causal branch.

---

### **IV. Interaction with Local Phenomena**

#### **Lithovores (Mineral-Crust Colonies)**
- **Do Not Touch**: They are not plants but *boundary organisms*—half-geological, half-computational. Physical contact may trigger a defensive reconfiguration that interprets your biomatter as an error to be corrected.
- **Observation Protocol**: View only during twilight with polarized, non-emissive optics. Record data passively. Their fractal growth encodes compressed histories of solar wind patterns—valuable, but volatile.

#### **Burrowers & Sky-Serpents**
- **Burrowers**: Harmless if undisturbed. Their movement stirs *causal dust*—particles that retain memory of past states. Inhaling this dust (even through filters) may induce vivid, non-linear memories of events that never happened to you.
- **Sky-Serpents**: Purely atmospheric constructs. Do not attempt communication. They respond to coherent thought patterns by coiling tighter—a feedback loop that can collapse into a localized logic singularity. Avert your gaze if one forms.

#### **The Built Remnants (Spire, Torus, etc.)**
- **Never Enter**: These are not ruins but *active processors*. The spire is a spacetime compiler; the torus, a physics emulator. Crossing their thresholds may result in:
  - Temporal dislocation (you exit before you enter)
  - Entropy inversion (you age backward for 17 seconds, then resume)
  - Narrative overwrite (your personal history is rewritten to fit local causal norms)
- **Safe Distance**: Minimum 5 km from any structure. Use passive gravimetric sensors to study from afar. Some emit *resonant hums* that align with human theta waves—meditation near them is strongly discouraged.

---

### **V. Temporal & Causal Precautions**

- **Time Is Not Uniform**: In some zones, seconds stretch; in others, minutes collapse. Carry a *proper-time chronometer* synced to your origin universe, not local conditions.
- **Avoid “Sticky” Zones**: Areas where time feels viscous often border *causal edit zones*—places where the ground has been recently “unwritten.” These zones may attempt to edit *you* if you linger. Signs include:
  - Perfectly smooth obsidian patches with faint glyphs
  - Shadows that move independently of light sources
  - A sensation of déjà vu with no referent
- **If You Witness an Edit**: Do not look directly at the glyphs. They are *compression artifacts of deleted reality*. Gaze long enough, and your brain may try to decompress them—resulting in irreversible cognitive fragmentation.

---

### **VI. Supplies & Duration Limits**

- **Maximum Stay**: 5 Earth-days. Beyond this, the mind begins to adapt to the Flattening’s post-narrative state, making reintegration into story-rich universes difficult or impossible.
- **Water**: Bring all you need. The hygroscopic salts produce *pseudo-water*—a metastable condensate laced with quantum noise. Drinking it induces waking dreams of alternate selves.
- **Food**: High-density nutrient paste. No organic matter should be introduced to the environment—it will be treated as foreign data and possibly *corrected*.

---

### **VII. The Purpose of Your Visit**

Ask yourself: *Why am I here?*

The Flattening does not reward curiosity. It does not punish arrogance. It simply *processes*. If you arrive seeking meaning, you will find only echoes. If you arrive to observe, to learn, to witness the universe debugging itself—you may leave changed, but intact.

But if you arrive hoping to *leave a mark*, know this: the Flattening remembers everything… and forgets it all, in due course.

Your best offering is not a monument, but a question—posed gently, left in the dust, and abandoned before it can be answered.

---

### **Final Note**

The Great Flattening is not a place. It is a *phase*—a moment in cosmic computation so vast it has taken geological form. You are not a visitor here. You are a transient variable in a function older than time.

Tread lightly. Breathe sparingly. And when the silence hums, do not mistake it for emptiness.

It is listening.
