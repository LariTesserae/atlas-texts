### **I. Pre-Visit Ontological Calibration**
Before stepping into the bowl, recalibrate your expectations of *substance*, *time*, and *life*.

- **Unlearn liquid intuition**: Your body is 60% water. Here, water is ontologically unstable. Do not bring beverages, nor expect to hydrate. Even breath moisture risks destabilizing local cohesion fields—exhaled vapor may crystallize mid-air or trigger unintended phase shifts. Wear a sealed rebreather mask lined with desiccant gel that absorbs exhalation moisture *before* it leaves your mouth.
  
- **Redefine ‘life’**: The flora here are not plants but *thermal metabolizers*. Do not attempt to touch them out of botanical curiosity. Their conductive veins carry not sap but ionized mineral aerosols; contact may induce localized electrostatic discharges or trigger defensive halogen exhalations toxic to human mucosa. Observe from a distance. Better yet, observe *through* a thermal imaging visor—this will reveal their metabolic flows as living circuitry.

- **Accept elastic time**: Your wristwatch will tick normally, but your *experience* of duration may stretch or compress. One minute of observation might feel like ten—or ten seconds. Do not panic. Anchor yourself with a *temporal metronome*: a small device that emits a subsonic pulse at precise intervals, synced to your home-reality chronology. Listen to it like a heartbeat.

### **II. Physical Preparation & Gear**

**Clothing**  
- **Material**: Wear a full-body suit woven from *phase-stable aerogel composite*. It must insulate against dry, radiant heat (≈80–90°C ambient) without trapping moisture. Inner layer: moisture-wicking but *non-evaporative*—any sweat must be chemically bound, not released.
- **Color**: Matte white or pale silver. Reflect radiant heat. Avoid black—absorption may trigger thermal runaway in your suit’s microclimate.
- **Seams**: Fully sealed. No zippers that could snag on the brittle crust. Entry via rear magnetic closure.

**Footwear**  
- **Soles**: Made of *low-thermal-conductivity polymer* with hexagonal treads matching the crust’s natural lattice. Distribute weight evenly to avoid fracturing the surface. Step lightly—each footfall may trigger micro-fissures that release subterranean argon plumes. These are not dangerous, but their sudden warmth and inert composition may disorient or trigger dizziness.

**Respiratory System**  
- **Rebreather unit**: Must filter halogen traces (iodine, bromine vapors) and ozone. Include a catalytic scrubber that converts halogens into inert salts. Air intake filters should be electrostatic, not fibrous—fibers could shed micro-particles that interfere with the local self-assembling chemistries.
- **No open breathing**: Even a single unfiltered breath may introduce foreign nucleation sites, potentially catalyzing unwanted crystallization in your lungs—or worse, in the air around you.

**Sensory Augmentation**  
- **Goggles**: Dual-mode lenses—visible light and far-infrared. The latter reveals thermal gradients that guide the flora’s growth. Also include polarization filters to detect light-bending anomalies near large specimens.
- **Audio dampeners with selective amplification**: Tune out your own breathing, but amplify subsonic resonances (20–100 Hz) from the crust. These “geophonic hums” indicate active subsurface recombination. Sudden silences may signal imminent structural shifts.

**No electronics with quartz oscillators**: The piezoelectric resonance in the air may interfere with timing circuits. Use mechanical or optical timepieces only.

### **III. Psychological & Perceptual Readiness**

This place *watches back*—not with eyes, but with thermodynamic attention.

- **Expect perceptual slippage**: Shadows detaching, delayed reflections, spatial density shifts—these are not hallucinations. They are emergent properties of a reality still debugging its own rules. Do not chase anomalies. Note them, then disengage your focus. Fixation may anchor you to a local temporal eddy.
  
- **Resist narrative impulse**: Your brain will try to impose story: *This is a failed experiment. This is a god’s workshop. This is a dream.* Resist. This place is *pre-narrative*. It has no past, only process. Observe without metaphor.

- **Embrace slowness**: Nothing here rewards haste. If a vine moves, you will not see it move. But return in six months (home-time), and it will have coiled a centimeter further. Your value here is not in action, but in *witnessing*. Bring a journal—but write only observations, not interpretations.

- **Do not leave offerings**: No pebbles, no coins, no biodegradable tokens. The system is self-contained. Introducing foreign matter—even organic—may disrupt the delicate balance of vapor-phase mineral recombination. You are a guest, not a participant.

### **IV. Temporal Protocol**

- **Visit duration**: Limit to 30–45 minutes of subjective time. Even if time feels dilated, do not linger. Prolonged exposure risks *ontological bleed*—your own bodily processes may begin to sync with the local thermodynamic rhythms. Reports (unverified but consistent) describe travelers returning with skin that faintly glows in infrared, or breath that carries a metallic tang for weeks.

- **Exit strategy**: Mark your entry point with a *non-interactive beacon*: a small sphere of borosilicate glass, internally etched with your sigil. Do not embed it—place it gently on the crust. It will not interfere, but its smooth, inert surface provides a visual anchor for return.

- **Post-visit quarantine**: Upon return to your native reality, spend 24 hours in a climate-controlled chamber with 60% humidity and neutral pH air. Your body may have absorbed trace halogens or piezoelectric charge. Flush systems with electrolyte-balanced fluids. Monitor for dreams of hexagonal voids or the sensation of dry heat behind your eyes.

### **V. Ethical & Existential Considerations**

This place is not abandoned. It is *pre-inhabited*.

- **You are not the first visitor**: The absence of footprints does not mean no one has come. Others may have visited—and been absorbed into the system: their exhalations catalyzing new crystalline veins, their shed skin cells mineralized into the crust. Tread as if walking on the skin of a sleeping architect.

- **Do not name it**: Giving it a name (“The Foundry Bowl,” “The Oven Garden”) imposes human taxonomy on a space still negotiating its ontology. Refer to it only as “the site” or “the prototype.”

- **Acknowledge its autonomy**: This is not a resource to be studied, mined, or replicated. It is a *proposition*—a world asking, “What if industry grew like moss? What if heat could bloom?” Honor that question by leaving it intact.

### **Final Note: The Gift of Witness**

You will not change this place. But it may change you—slowly, like a vine coiling around a thermal gradient you didn’t know you carried. Return home not with samples, but with silence. With the memory of light pulsing in petals. With the understanding that some realities are not built, but *grown*, and that solidity itself is a verb, not a noun.

Go gently. Breathe inward. And remember: you are walking inside a world that is still learning how to hold itself together. Do not be the reason it cracks.
