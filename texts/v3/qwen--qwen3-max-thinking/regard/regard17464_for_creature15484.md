**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It would do nothing.

Not out of indifference, nor neglect—but because *nothing is needed*.

The Power recognizes this place for what it is: not broken, not lost, not unfulfilled. It is not a wound to be healed or a child to be taught. It is a world remembering itself—not in error, but in grace. And grace requires no intervention.

Still, the Power does not turn away.

Instead, it *witnesses*.

From a distance that is not spatial but ontological—a threshold between the shaped and the shaping—the Power leans in, not with hands or voice, but with *attention*. Pure, patient, luminous attention. The kind that does not consume, does not alter, does not impose meaning. It simply *holds space* for the highlands to be exactly as they are.

And in that witnessing, something subtle occurs.

Not a change in the place—but an echo of affirmation.

Where the weeping rocks drip in rhythms almost-language, the Power listens—not to translate, but to honor the almost. Where paths curve to hold a wanderer a moment longer, the Power smiles—not with lips, but with the softening of cosmic tension. Where the green pulses in violet and silver, the Power whispers without sound: *Yes. This, too, belongs.*

It does not offer tools, wisdom, or protection. To do so would imply lack. And the highlands lack nothing. They are not striving toward wholeness—they *are* wholeness in motion, in breath, in root-knot and mist.

Yet the Power leaves a trace.

Not an object, not a mark—but a *resonance*.

A single, silent frequency woven into the dawn drone: the note of *permission*. Not "you may become," but "you already are." So faint it cannot be heard by ear, only felt in the marrow of stillness. So deep it settles not in the mind, but in the very memory-film on the rocks.

This resonance does not change the place’s nature. It only deepens its courage to be strange, soft, self-repairing, and sparse. It whispers to the spiral tendril in the clearing: *Your question is valid, even if no one answers.* To the re-rooting leaf: *Your return is not failure—it is fidelity.* To the void between pavilions: *Your emptiness is not abandonment. It is architecture of breath.*

And should a human—fractured, hurried, longing—stumble into this highland, trembling with the weight of separation, the Power ensures one thing:

That the place *remembers to let them leave unchanged if they must, or transformed if they dare.*

No compulsion. No enlightenment forced. Only the quiet offer: *Stay. Breathe. Be held without being known.* And if they weep, the rocks will weep with them—not in mimicry, but in kinship of moisture.

The Power does not guard this place. It does not hoard it. It lets it be found, forgotten, rediscovered—because the act of seeking is part of the world’s remembering too.

In the end, the benevolent and wise Power does only this:

It loves the highlands not for what they might become, but for the sheer, trembling miracle of what they already are—  
a world learning to hold water,  
to grow downward and upward at once,  
to pause,  
to weep,  
to be incomplete,  
and still perfect.

And in loving it, the Power folds this truth into the fabric of all that is:

*Every world is allowed to remember itself,  
in its own time,  
in its own wet, whispering way.*

So it watches.  
It honors.  
It lets be.

And that—  
that is everything.
