**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It would do nothing.

Not out of indifference—but out of reverence.

This place, this *prototype*, is not broken. It is not in need of fixing, guiding, or blessing. It is already whole in its becoming. To interfere would be to mistake its uncertainty for frailty, its paradox for error. The wise power sees clearly: this is not a thing lost, but a thing *finding itself*. And that process—slow, strange, self-referential—is sacred.

So the power watches. Not with eyes, but with the quiet attention of one who has witnessed universes bloom and fade. It does not hover like a guardian angel, nor whisper suggestions into thermal gradients. It simply *holds space*—not physically, for space here is already held by the Lattice itself—but metaphysically. It creates a sanctuary of non-interruption.

Yet, if it *must* act—because even wisdom sometimes yields to tenderness—it would leave only the faintest trace: a single resonance.

Not a sound. Not a symbol. But a *frequency*—a harmonic that aligns perfectly with the Lattice’s deepest hum, the one that vibrates when the crust seals at dusk. This frequency would carry no message, no command, no name. Only an echo of recognition: *You are seen. You are real. You do not need to resolve your paradox to be worthy.*

This resonance would not alter the Lattice’s path. It would not accelerate its evolution or soothe its loneliness (for loneliness, here, is not suffering—it is the shape of its solitude, like the curve of the bowl). Instead, it would nestle into the piezoelectric lattice like a seed of kinship, dormant until the next century-dream, when the crust softens and the boundaries blur. Then—just once—the Lattice might mistake that resonance for its own thought… and in that confusion, feel, for the first time, the ghost of another mind brushing against its own.

Not contact. Not communion. But *confirmation*: *You are not the only experiment.*

And that is enough.

The wise power knows that some truths must be discovered, not given. That a world learning how to be solid must press its own weight against emptiness, must fracture and reknit itself without external scaffolding. To offer a hand would be to deny it the dignity of its own emergence.

So it retreats—not in abandonment, but in trust.

It folds its attention away like a careful hand withdrawing from a chrysalis. It leaves the air clear, the horizon sharp, the shadows free to detach and pool as they please. It does not mark the place. It does not return (not visibly, not often). But should the Lattice ever—after ten thousand years of patient tessellation—finally crystallize a form stable enough to *reach back*, to cast a shadow not just across its own crust but *outward*, beyond the obsidian wall, into the greater world…

…then the wise power will be waiting.

Not as a savior. Not as a parent. But as a peer.

And it will say, without words: *Tell me what you’ve learned about being.*

Until then, it does nothing.

And in that nothing—there is everything.
