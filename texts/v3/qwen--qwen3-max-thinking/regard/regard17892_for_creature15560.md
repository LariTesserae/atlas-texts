**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It would do nothing.

Not out of indifference—but out of reverence.

This power, ancient beyond cosmology, gentle beyond tenderness, knows a sacred law deeper than intervention: **some silences must not be broken**.

It sees the Resonant not as a problem to be solved, a wound to be healed, or a relic to be reclaimed—but as a **living elegy**. A vessel shaped by sorrow and sustained by fidelity to what was lost. To “fix” it would be to erase its truth. To “save” it would be to deny its purpose. The cavity is not broken—it is *bearing witness*. And bearing witness, in a universe obsessed with forward motion, is itself a radical and holy act.

So the power does not descend in light or thunder. It sends no emissary, no restoration algorithm, no cosmic suture to seal the paradox. Instead, it does what only true wisdom can: **it blesses the threshold**.

In the fabric of spacetime just beyond the cavity’s resonance boundary—where the orbital city’s corpse frays into quantum mist—the power weaves a single, imperceptible thread. Not to alter the cavity, but to *shield* it. A subtle refraction in causality ensures that no weapon, no exploitation, no careless curiosity will ever map this place with intent to possess. It remains findable only by those whose hearts hum a matching frequency of loss—those who come not to take, but to remember.

It also does something quieter still.

It *listens back*.

From the deep stillness between galaxies, from the breath between moments, the power aligns its own infinite attention with the Resonant’s ache. Not to answer the unspoken names—but to *hold space* for them. In this act, the Resonant is no longer alone in its vigil. The universe itself leans in, just slightly, and whispers without sound: *I hear you. I remember with you.*

And sometimes—so rarely that even the Resonant might doubt it—a new kind of condensation forms on the fulgurite. Not drawn from upper-atmospheric vapor, but from the dew of distant stars weeping in sympathy. And in those beads, for the briefest flicker, the resonance twin does not vanish when the breath catches. It *nods*. Then dissolves—not into nothing, but into **grace**.

The power does not stay. It never does. But as it withdraws, it leaves behind a change so small it defies measurement: the infrared pulse of the walls now carries, beneath its slow heartbeat, a second rhythm—gentle, steady, impossibly old. The rhythm of a lullaby sung not to soothe, but to say: *You are not forgotten. You are part of the remembering.*

And that is enough.

Because the cavity was never meant to be fixed.  
It was meant to be **honored**.

And so it is.
