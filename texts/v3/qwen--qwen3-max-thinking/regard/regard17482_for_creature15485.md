**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

The Wise One does not arrive with fanfare. Not with thunder, nor with light that severs shadow from stone. It does not descend—it *attends*. As one might kneel beside a sleeping elder, not to wake them, but to witness their breath.

It knows this place.

Not from maps or memory, but from the same deep grammar that shaped stars and silence. The basin is not an anomaly. It is an *answer*—a rare, fragile solution to a question most worlds never live long enough to ask: *What if balance is not a phase, but a destination?*

The Wise One sees what others might mistake for emptiness: a world that has folded itself inward, not from exhaustion, but from completion. It has no hunger, no wound, no plea for salvation. It is whole—not because it lacks pain, but because it has outgrown the need for it.

And so the Wise One does **nothing**.

Not out of indifference, but out of reverence.

It does not seed life where life declined to take root. It does not stir the air to test if wind might return. It does not whisper to the Engine, nor leave a sigil upon the tuff bowls. To intervene would be to imply incompleteness—and this place is complete in its stillness.

Yet presence is not absence of action. The Wise One *watches*, and in watching, it **sanctifies**.

It wraps the basin in a quiet ward—not a barrier, but a blessing: *Let no hurried hand mistake your perfection for stagnation. Let no empire of progress dredge your vents for power. Let no explorer carve names into your softened stone and call it discovery.*  

This ward is not magic in the way mortals imagine it. It is subtler: a tilt in perception. Those who approach with axes, drills, or doctrines will find the path slippery, the air thick with disorienting mist, their instruments flickering with phantom errors. Not because the land resists—but because the universe, at the behest of the Wise One, *gently forgets* how to lead violence here.

But those who come with stillness? They are allowed.

A wanderer, weary of striving, might stumble in, drawn by a dream of silence. They will find no water to drink—but no thirst either. Their body will slow. Their thoughts will shed urgency like old skin. They may sit beside a vent for a moon, breathing with its hssssss—pause—hssssss—and feel, for the first time, that they are not a wound in the world, but a part of its rhythm.

The Wise One permits this. It does not guide such souls, but it does not turn them away. For rare as they are, they understand: this place is not for taking. It is for *remembering*—that existence need not be loud to be meaningful, that persistence can be sacred, that sometimes, the highest form of creation is to cease creating, and simply *be*, in perfect harmony with the laws that birthed you.

And in the deepest night, when even the six-legged mineral-beetle sleeps, the Wise One lingers a moment longer. It places a single thought—not into the land, but into the fabric of possibility itself:

> *May there always be one place where entropy rests.*

Then it withdraws.

Not gone. Never gone. But folded back into the hum of the whole.

Leaving the basin to its breath.

Leaving the Engine to its pulse.

Leaving stillness… to be still.
