What lives here has drawn the attention of a benevolent and wise power—not because it is in need, nor because it calls out, but simply because it *is*. And in its quiet completeness, it sings a note so pure, so unadorned, that even the oldest stars lean closer to hear.

This power does not descend in fire or voice. It does not intervene. It would no more disrupt this plateau than pluck a single thread from a spider’s web at dawn and expect the dew not to scatter. Instead, it watches—and in watching, it *blesses*.

Not with gifts, not with protection, not with promises of tomorrow. But with *recognition*. With the silent affirmation that what is here is not an accident, not an oversight in the grand design, but a sacred experiment in stillness that the universe itself holds dear.

And so, the wise power does three things—though "does" is too strong a word. Perhaps it *allows*, it *tends*, it *echoes*.

---

**First: it shields the threshold.**  
It weaves a subtle veil around the edges of the plateau—not visible, not tangible, but felt by any who might seek to enter with noise in their hearts or greed in their hands. Outsiders do not find this place by accident. Maps forget it. Compasses hesitate. Travelers who wander too close feel a sudden heaviness in their boots, a fog in their thoughts, a deep, inexplicable urge to turn back. Not because they are barred—but because the land *asks* for readiness. And the wise power ensures that only those who have already begun to unlearn urgency, who carry silence like a half-remembered lullaby, might one day cross the ridges without breaking the spell.

---

**Second: it deepens the resonance.**  
The power does not add sound, but it *clarifies* the silence. It tunes the air like a luthier tunes a stringed instrument made of mist and memory. The drip of water from the eastern overhang? It falls now with a tone that harmonizes with the slow expansion of cooling basalt. The hum of a child aligning with the shrubs? It resonates just enough to stir the lichen into slightly faster growth—not for utility, but for beauty, for balance. The power amplifies not volume, but *meaning*. In this place, every act of attention becomes a sacrament, and the wise one ensures that the feedback loop between human and stone grows ever more intimate, ever more mutual.

---

**Third: it guards the emptiness.**  
No animal will ever take root here—not because the land rejects life, but because its equilibrium is too precise, too finely calibrated. And the wise power understands: to introduce even a single earthworm, a single beetle, would be to unravel a tapestry woven in the language of absence. So it honors the void. It lets the pools stay clear. It lets the soil remain unturned by claw or root deeper than grass. And in doing so, it affirms a radical truth: that life does not require *multiplicity* to be holy. Sometimes, holiness is the courage to be *only what you are*—stone, mist, human, and silence—and nothing more.

---

And once, only once in a generation, the wise power draws near—not as a being, but as a shift in the light.

It comes when the last elder, hair like wet ash, sits alone by the central pool at the exact moment the morning droplet falls. In that instant—between the release and the splash—the air shimmers, not with heat, but with *presence*. The elder does not see a face, hear a voice. Instead, they feel the plateau *breathe inward*, and in that breath, they understand:

*You have not been forgotten. You have been witnessed.*

And that is enough.

The elder returns to the settlement not changed, but confirmed. They press a seed into the silt with their palm, as always. They hum the tone of the westward wind. They say nothing of what passed.

Because some blessings are too deep for words. They are carried in the way a stone holds coolness long after sunset. In the way a people, by choosing stillness, become a mirror for the universe’s quietest truths.

And so the plateau endures—not as a relic, not as a refuge, but as a living question posed to all that rushes elsewhere:

*What if enough is already here?*

The wise power does not answer.  
It simply stays, listening—  
and in its listening,  
it too becomes part of the stillness.
