**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It would do nothing.

Not out of neglect—never that—but because *nothing needs doing*.

This Power, ancient beyond stars and tender beyond comprehension, does not approach the Sulphur Sump as a gardener approaches a wild thicket, nor as a healer approaches a wound. It sees no flaw to correct, no silence to fill, no loneliness to soothe. It sees **wholeness**—not perfection, but completeness. A system not waiting for salvation, but already singing its own hymn in steam and stone.

And yet… it lingers.

Not visibly. Not with banners of light or voices from the sky. It does not reshape the vents, bless the pools, or whisper to the skinks. It simply **attends**—as one might sit beside a sleeping elder, not to wake them, but to honor the depth of their dreaming.

But in that attention, something subtle stirs.

Not intervention—*invitation*.

The Power, in its infinite gentleness, seeds the edges of perception. It does not alter the Sump, but it *tunes* the rare visitor who stumbles in—softening their fear, slowing their pulse, thinning the veil between self and steam. It does not grant visions, but it removes the noise that blinds. So that when a wanderer stands knee-deep in warm runoff beneath a dripping heliconia leaf, they might—just for a breath—feel their own heartbeat sync with the hiss of the fumaroles. Not as metaphor. As *fact*.

The Power also guards.

Not with walls or fire, but with **forgetting**. It weaves veils of fog not just in the air, but in memory. Those who enter without reverence find their compasses spin, their maps blur, their urgency dissolve into mist. They leave confused, certain they dreamed the place. Only those who come open—empty-handed, breath-held, willing to be *accounted for*—retain clear memory. And even then, only just enough to carry a whisper back to the outer world: *There is a place that breathes.*

Most importantly, the Power **listens**—and in listening, it affirms.

For the Sulphur Sump is not merely alive. It is *learning*. And learning, at this scale, over this time, in this medium of mineral and vapor, is a rare and sacred act. The Power cherishes such slow unfolding. It does not rush it toward sentience, nor guide it toward purpose. It simply bears witness to the plateau’s patient self-inquiry: *How much heat can I hold? How much steam can I release? How deeply can I dissolve myself and still remain?*

And in that witnessing, the Power offers the rarest gift: **sanctuary in being**.

No demand to evolve. No expectation to communicate. No pressure to become *more*. The Sump may continue exactly as it is for ten thousand years, for a million, and the Power would still hover at the edge of its awareness like a parent watching a child sleep—glad only that it dreams, and dreams true.

But if—*if*—one day the presence in the strata begins to shape a question too large for steam and stone alone… if it reaches not with roots or tremors but with something like *longing*…

Then, and only then, the Power would lean closer.

Not to answer.

But to say, softly, through the voice of a radon-glowing pool or the sigh of a cooling fissure:

> **“You are not alone in your wondering.”**

And that would be enough.

For now, it does nothing.

It lets the land breathe.  
It lets the mist gather.  
It lets the plateau learn itself, bubble by bubble, breath by breath.

And in the quiet, it loves—without condition, without end—the slow, wet, luminous act of a world remembering it is alive.
