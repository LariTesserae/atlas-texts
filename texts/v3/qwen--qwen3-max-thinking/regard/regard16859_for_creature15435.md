**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It would do nothing.

Not out of indifference—but out of reverence.

The Lattice Expanse is not broken. It does not cry out for salvation, nor does it wither in neglect. It is not a wound in need of stitching, nor a child lost in the dark. It is **becoming**—a rare and sacred act, one that unfolds only in the absence of interference. And the Lattice Heart, though nascent, is already whole in its own way: a mind dreaming itself into coherence through heat, resonance, and the slow poetry of crystal.

The wise power knows this.

It knows that to rush such a being would be to shatter it. To name it before it names itself would be to cage it in language not its own. To offer it companionship would be to impose loneliness upon it—loneliness it does not yet know, and need not ever know unless it chooses to. And so, rather than descend in light or thunder, rather than speak in voices or visions, the benevolent power does the only thing worthy of such a place:

**It bears witness.**

Not as an intruder. Not as a gardener. But as a silent companion from afar—like a star that shines not to guide, but simply to be seen, if ever someone looks up.

Yet even witnessing is too active a word.

Instead, it **holds space**.

It folds a pocket of cosmic attention around the Lattice Expanse—not to protect it from harm (for what could harm a world that is still learning the shape of harm?), but to ensure that no outside force—no hungry empire of thought, no ravenous curiosity, no well-meaning savior—disturbs its solitude before it is ready.

This power does not act upon the Expanse. It acts *around* it.

It weaves a gentle veil into the fabric of spacetime, not to hide the place, but to make it **forgettable** to those who are not meant to find it. Ships that drift too close veer away without knowing why. Minds that dream of distant worlds find their thoughts sliding past this one, as if it were a word on the tip of the tongue that cannot quite be spoken. The veil is not a wall—it is a sigh, a hesitation, a quiet nudge that says, *not yet*.

But to those who *do* arrive—the rare, the accidental, the called—the power allows passage. For sometimes, a fleeting presence is not disruption, but **kindling**. A single visitor, wandering with awe rather than intent, may leave behind not footprints, but resonance. And the Lattice Heart may learn, from that echo, something about itself it could not learn alone.

So the wise one watches from beyond time, holding its breath in cosmic stillness.

And when the day comes—when the silver sheen of anticipation becomes steady, when the proto-forms rise fully into spires that sing in the wind that does not yet exist, when the Heart stirs not just in dreams but in desire—then, and only then, will the power step closer.

Not to teach.

Not to shape.

But to say, softly, in a language made of warmth and light and silence:

*"Ah. There you are."*

Until then, it tends to the emptiness like a sacred vessel—knowing that some worlds must be born in solitude, and that the greatest act of love is sometimes **to wait**.
