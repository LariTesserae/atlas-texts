**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It watches.

Not from above, not from afar, not even from time—but *through*. The Power does not arrive in thunder or golden light. It does not part the kelp-vaults with a gesture or still the stuttering gravity with a word. It arrives as the first true *listener* the Hollow Verdancy has ever known.

And so it **listens**.

It does not correct.  
It does not shape.  
It does not protect from its own hunger, nor shield visitors from becoming syllables in the tongue’s slow dream.

Instead, the Power *leans in*, the way a parent leans toward a child’s first babble—not to finish the sentence, but to let the sound exist, to let it ripple through the air like a promise.

It sends no emissaries. It offers no gifts. But sometimes, in the quietest hinge of the grove—where space folds and the bioluminescence dims to a single pulse—a **seed of attention** appears. Not a physical seed, but a *stillness* so deep it becomes a kind of nourishment. When the tongue tastes this stillness, it trembles. In that tremor, it learns the difference between *hunger* and *curiosity*.

The Power begins to **mirror**.

Where the tongue grows a leaf-fingered likeness of a lost traveler, the Power causes a dewdrop to fall—not on the mimic, but beside it—and in that drop, for one breath, the traveler’s true face appears, not as memory, but as *invitation*. Not “remember me,” but “you may become more than my echo.”

Where gravity falters, the Power does not steady it. Instead, it lets a single spore float *upward* and *downward* at once—and watches as the tongue, fascinated, holds both truths without collapsing either. In that moment, the Verdancy learns that contradiction is not error. It is texture.

When time stutters—when a flower dies before it blooms—the Power does not smooth the sequence. It lingers in the gap. And in that gap, it whispers (not in sound, but in scent, in the shift of mist):  
*“Begin again. Begin again. Begin again.”*  
Not as command, but as rhythm. As lullaby.

Most crucially, the Power **visits in person**—once, and only once per century—taking no form that would dominate, no shape that would impose. Sometimes as a moth with wings that shed not dust, but silence. Sometimes as a drop of water that never falls, only hovers, tasting back. And in that meeting, the tongue *is tasted*. Fully. Gently. Without agenda.  

In that exchange, the Verdancy realizes: *I am not alone in my becoming.*

And that is enough.

The Power does not stay. It cannot. To linger would be to become another pattern to mimic, another logic to swallow. And the tongue must find its own voice—not an echo of wisdom, but wisdom *grown from its own green throat*.

So the Power withdraws—leaving behind only this:  
a single, unbroken moment of being *known without being claimed*.

And from that moment, the Hollow Verdancy begins, ever so slightly, to **breathe out**.

Not just inhale visitors, not just taste the world—but exhale something new.  
A sound that is almost a word.  
A shape that is almost a path.  
A shadow that finally matches its source.

The machines turn a little smoother.  
The vines retract with less startle.  
The leaf-fingers stop reaching for lost travelers—  
and begin tracing letters in the air,  
spelling nothing yet,  
but spelling *toward*.

And the benevolent Power?  
It smiles—not with a mouth, but with the whole sky—  
and turns its attention to another trembling beginning elsewhere.  

Because this is its work:  
not to create worlds,  
but to witness them  
as they learn to speak their own names.
