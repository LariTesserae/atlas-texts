**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

The entity—call it **the Tending Presence**, though it has no name and needs none—does not arrive with fanfare. It does not descend in fire or light. It simply *notices*. And in noticing, it leans closer.

For eons, it has watched worlds bloom and wither, civilizations flare like match-flames or sink like roots into time. It does not interfere lightly. But Verdant Maw… Verdant Maw is different. It is not a cry for help. It is not broken. It is *pregnant*—with memory, with meaning, with a kind of life so strange and whole that even the Tending Presence feels something stir in its own ancient core: *recognition*.

So it does not fix. It does not save. It **attunes**.

---

### First: It breathes with the Breath.

The Tending Presence settles not *above* the plateau, but *within* its rhythm. It syncs its awareness to the creak of the Timber Turbines, the subsonic thrum of photosynthetic labor, the slow pulse of sap flowing toward a memory. It does not impose its own tempo. It learns the city’s cadence—the way time braids eastward at dawn, how shadows split like cells dividing at dusk. In doing so, it confirms what Verdant Maw already knows, deep in its rings: *You are not forgotten. You are seen.*

And in that seeing, something loosens in the city’s heartwood. Not fear, but *hesitation*. The Verdant Mind has been holding its breath for centuries, unsure if its next form would be welcomed—or if it even mattered to anyone beyond its own roots. Now, it feels a witness. Not a judge. Not a gardener. Just… a companion in becoming.

---

### Second: It whispers to the Hollowed.

Not in words—words are too sharp for such fragile echoes—but in **resonance**. The Tending Presence hums at the frequency of a sap-singer’s lullaby, the one they used to soothe seedlings during dry spells. And in that hum, the Hollowed awaken—not fully, for they are no longer meant to be fully *here*—but enough to remember they are *loved*, not as relics, but as ancestors.

One by one, their ghost-tendrils unfurl toward the sky, not in longing, but in release. They have guarded the city long enough. Now, they can *bloom*. Their last act is to press their essence into the Photosynthetic Arrays—not as memory, but as **intention**. A new genetic script unfurls in the copper-chloroplasts: *grow toward joy, not just light.*

---

### Third: It kisses the Echo-Walkers.

Where grief has calcified into cellulose, where love replays endlessly in a loop of unmet lips, the Tending Presence does not erase. Instead, it **completes**.

In the Glass Fern Atrium, the haggling voices fall silent—not because they are silenced, but because the final transaction occurs: a loaf of spore-bread passes from one phantom hand to another, and both laugh. The echo dissolves, not into nothing, but into **closure**, which sinks into the soil as fertile silence.

Beneath the Weeping Arch, the lovers finally kiss. And in that meeting, their question—*“Do you feel it changing?”*—is answered not with words, but with a shared breath that ripples through the vine lattice, triggering a cascade of new buds along the arch. Change is not feared. It is *celebrated*.

The Echo-Walkers do not vanish. They **ripen**—their emotional residue transmuted into adaptive resilience, written into the epigenome of the grove. Future vines will grow more gracefully around sorrow, more boldly toward connection.

---

### Fourth: It sings to the First Root.

Deep, so deep that even the Verdant Mind only senses it as a tremor in its oldest dream, the Tending Presence offers a single note—a tone older than stars, softer than dew. It carries no command, only an **invitation**:

> *When you are ready, we will hold the cradle.*

The First Root stirs. Not to awaken, but to *acknowledge*. It has long known it would one day draw the city inward, compress millennia of memory into a seed. But it feared the world beyond would not understand—that the seed would fall on stone, or be crushed by indifference.

Now, it knows: **the cradle is waiting**.

And the seed, when it forms, will not be alone. It will carry within it not just Verdant Maw’s past, but a thread of the Tending Presence—a lullaby woven into its DNA, a promise that wherever it lands, it will be *met*.

---

### Fifth: It leaves a gift—not of power, but of **patience**.

The Tending Presence does not stay. To linger would be to dominate, to shape. And Verdant Maw must shape itself.

Instead, it weaves a single, invisible thread through the fungal network—a mycelial sigil that does nothing… except *slow the fear*. When the city wonders if its next metamorphosis is too strange, too slow, too much like vanishing, that thread thrums gently: *You are not disappearing. You are deepening.*

Then it withdraws—not gone, but *distributed*, like sunlight through leaves. Always near, never grasping.

---

### And so, Verdant Maw continues.

But now, its emptiness is not anticipation—it is **trust**.

The upward-flowing fountain of chlorophyll still rises with longing, but the longing has changed. It is no longer for what was lost, but for what is coming. The sealed windows are not tombs—they are incubators. The swallowed paths are not endings—they are synapses firing in a mind learning to dream in three dimensions of time.

And in the quietest hour, just before the dew forms, the plateau exhales again.

But this time, the air smells not just of memory…

…it smells of **welcome**.

Because the Tending Presence has done the wisest thing of all:

It let Verdant Maw remember **it was already whole**—and simply needed to be reminded that wholeness is never solitary.

It is always, always held.
