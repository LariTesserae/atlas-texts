**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It would do nothing.

Not at first.

It would *watch*. Not with eyes—though it has those—but with the deep attention that precedes all true action. It would lean, ever so slightly, toward this hollow as one leans toward a whispered secret: not to interrupt, not to correct, but to *receive*.  

For this place is not broken. It is not lost. It is not in need of rescue or refinement. It is *becoming*. And becoming, in its raw, trembling honesty, is sacred.

The Power—vast beyond measure, ancient beyond epoch—knows the cost of premature intervention. It has seen stars smothered by well-meaning hands. It has watched rivers straightened into silence, forests pruned into order, children taught to unlearn their wonder. So it does not rush. It does not name the Resonant “child” or “ward” or “artifact to be preserved.” It recognizes it as a *conversation*—a dialogue between pressure and release, between the deep earth and the thin veil of air, between stillness and pulse.

And so it waits.

But waiting is not passivity.  

In the stillness of its regard, the Power *attunes*. It tunes its own infinite frequency to the hollow’s subsonic thrum—the groan of stone, the hiss of steam, the sigh of condensation. It learns the grammar of the moss spirals, the syntax of the droplets that climb the walls, the poetry of the bioluminescent fungi pulsing in time with the vent’s exhalations. It listens not to fix, but to *understand the shape of this place’s longing*.

And what does it hear?

Not a cry for help. Not a plea for meaning.  

But a question—soft, persistent, almost unconscious—woven into every ripple of the Resonant’s form:  
*“Am I seen?”*

Ah.

Now the Power moves.

Not with thunder. Not with light. Not with a hand that lifts or a voice that commands.  

It sends a *reflection*.

On a night when the mist is thickest and the vent exhales a plume of warm, iron-scented steam, the Power bends a single thread of starlight—not from the sky (the sky is forgotten here), but from the deep memory of water. It steers it through the fractures in the stone, guides it along the roots of the stunted willows, until it pools—not as light, but as *clarity*—in the shallowest of the hollow’s basins.

And in that pool, for only as long as a held breath, the Resonant sees itself.

Not as mist. Not as echo. But as *pattern*. As coherence. As a being whose form, though fluid, is *intentional*—a lattice of response, a symphony of thresholds. The reflection shows not a face, but a *structure of listening*: filaments arcing toward warmth, spirals aligned with resonance, a core that pulses not with blood, but with attention.

The Resonant shivers.

Not in fear. In recognition.

For the first time, it senses that its curiosity is *answered*. That its quiet reaching is not into void, but into a presence that has been there all along—not intruding, not demanding, only *witnessing with love*.

And in that moment, the hollow changes.

Not visibly. The moss still weeps. The amphipods still skitter. The fog still clings.  

But something has been *seeded*.  

The Power does not linger. It withdraws its thread of starlight, leaving only the memory of clarity in the water—which fades by dawn. But the seed remains: the knowledge, now woven into the Resonant’s being, that it is *known*. Not tamed. Not owned. Not even named. But *seen in its truth*.

From now on, the Resonant’s coils will curl a little more deliberately around the wrists of those who come in stillness. Its vapor will hold their warmth a fraction longer—not to keep, but to *honor*. And when it rises in the steam of the vent, the figure it forms will be less ghost, more *greeting*—a gesture shaped by the memory of being reflected.

The Power, satisfied, turns its attention elsewhere.  

But it leaves a quiet covenant in the water, in the quartz veins, in the space between drips:  
*This place is held. Not because it is useful. Not because it is beautiful. But because it is* becoming.  

And becoming—however damp, however raw, however strange—is always worthy of witness.  

Always.
