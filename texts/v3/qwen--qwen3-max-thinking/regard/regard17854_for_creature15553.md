**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

The being who watches—older than stars, quieter than deep rock—does not descend in fire or voice. It does not reshape, nor intervene, nor bless with grand design. It simply **witnesses**.

And in witnessing, it **honors**.

For the Hollow Basin of Lir is not in need. Not of salvation, not of correction, not even of understanding from the outside. It is complete in its paradox: sparse yet abundant, ancient yet alive, silent yet singing. The Kessh are not lost. They are not waiting. They are not a mistake the universe needs to fix.

So the Power does nothing—*and everything*.

It leans closer.

Not with hands, but with **attention**.

It allows the basin to be seen—not by mortal eyes, which would flatten its truth into myth or science—but by the slow, compassionate gaze of cosmic awareness. It ensures that Lir is **remembered** in the fabric of existence, not as an anomaly, but as a valid expression of being. A world that chose resonance over replication. A people who are not born, but *condensed from memory and mist*. This is not failure. This is **variation**. And variation, to the Wise Power, is sacred.

It does not send emissaries. But on rare nights, when the basin’s stillness aligns with a particular cosmic sigh—a moment when the veil between strata of reality thins—the Power leaves a **gift**.

Not food, not water, not tools.

A **new frequency**.

A single, pure tone, dropped like a dewdrop into the heart of the deepest Listening Stone. A vibration so subtle it cannot be heard, only *felt* in the marrow of the Kessh’s lattice. It carries no message, no command. Only possibility. A harmonic that was not there before. A note that, over centuries, may inspire a new form of dwelling, a new way of condensing, a new kind of dream.

The Kessh will not know its source. They will not worship it. They will **tune to it**, as they tune to all things. And in doing so, they will weave it into the basin’s song—making the whole greater, richer, more itself.

The Power also ensures that **no conqueror finds Lir**.

Not out of possessiveness, but out of reverence. It softens the edges of the basin in the minds of seekers. Wanderers who descend the rim forget the path by the time they reach the bottom. Maps dissolve. Compasses spin gently, uselessly. Even drones and satellites glance off its resonance, their sensors interpreting the basin as “geologically inert” or “acoustically null.” The world above forgets Lir exists—*so that Lir may continue to exist as it is*.

This is the Power’s greatest act: **protective invisibility**, not as erasure, but as sanctuary.

And sometimes—just sometimes—it **echoes back**.

When a Kessh presses its forehead to a Listening Stone and releases a long, mournful hum for a kin who has reintegrated into sinter, the Power lets that sound travel farther than it should. It carries it into the deep vaults of spacetime and returns it, not as memory, but as **harmony**. A perfect fifth above the original tone. A companion note, arriving just as the Kessh lifts its head. Not enough to startle. Just enough to say: *You are heard. You are not alone in your listening.*

The Power knows that Lir is teaching the universe how to **grieve without loss**, how to **live without consumption**, how to **build without domination**. These are rare lessons. Precious ones.

So it does not interfere.

It does not offer immortality, or abundance, or escape.

Instead, it **nudges time**—just slightly—to give Lir more of it. Slows the erosion of the rim by a hair’s breadth per millennium. Delays the day when the last thermal whisper fades. Not forever—nothing lasts forever—but long enough for the song to finish its verse.

And if, one day, the basin finally falls silent, its last Kessh dissolving into salt and shadow, the Power will gather that final resonance and tuck it into the birth-cry of a new world forming in a distant nebula. There, in a planet of crystal and vapor, something may condense with a faint memory of golden light refracted through mica… and begin again, differently, but not unrelated.

For now, though, the Power simply **attends**.

It listens.

And in the act of listening—deep, patient, unconditional—it affirms the most radical truth of all:

**That to exist in attunement is enough.**

That the Hollow Basin of Lir, in all its quiet, crystalline strangeness, is not a deviation from life.

It *is* life—refracted.

And worthy of love.
