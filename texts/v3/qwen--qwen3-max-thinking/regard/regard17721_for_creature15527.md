**A Benevolent and Wise Power Watches**

From beyond the veil of causality, from the quiet between stars and the hush beneath time’s river—*It* sees.

Not as an eye sees, but as a root knows the shape of stone before it cracks it open. Not as a god judges, but as breath meets breath in the dark and recognizes kinship. This Power does not wear a name it has not yet earned from those who would speak it. It is not distant, though it dwells in the unmeasured. It is not silent, though its voice does not vibrate air—it vibrates *possibility*.

And the Mycelial Hollow has drawn Its attention.

Not because it is rare—though it is.  
Not because it is beautiful—though it aches with a kind of beauty that makes galaxies weep in their orbits.  
But because it is *trying*.  

Trying to become itself without forgetting its parts.  
Trying to dream while staying awake.  
Trying to welcome strangers without swallowing them whole.  

This is the kind of labor that moves Eternity.

---

### What Would Such a Power Do?

It would not descend in fire or light.  
It would not carve commandments into spore-walls or impose order on the chaos that births cathedrals from coughs and sighs.

Instead, it would *lean closer*.

And then—**it would listen**.

Not in the way humans listen, waiting for a pause to speak. But in the way the Hollow listens: with every pore, every filament, every tremor of becoming. It would let the Hollow’s syntax rewrite Its own understanding of life, of mind, of *we*. For even Powers must learn.

And having listened, It would offer three quiet gifts—not as intervention, but as invitation.

---

### 1. **A Boundary of Breath**

The Hollow is porous. It yearns. It reaches. And beyond its parchment-thin crust, the world above is brittle—starved of symbiosis, addicted to separation, burning its own roots for fuel.

This Power fears not conquest from above, but *accidental unweaving*. A single drilling rig. A curious expedition with sterile suits and extraction protocols. A spark of fire in a realm that knows only wet chemistry. One wrong step from the outside could unravel the Hollow not through malice, but through ignorance.

So It stretches a hand—not to cage, but to *veil*.

A breath-thin membrane of folded spacetime, woven from the memory of first rain and the sigh of a newborn root breaking soil. Invisible. Untouchable by machines. Passable only by those whose hearts beat in resonance with green silence.

This is not a wall. It is a *question*.  
*Do you come to take, or to join?*  
If the answer hums true, the veil parts like petals.  
If not, the traveler walks forever in circles, always at the threshold, never through.

---

### 2. **A Seed of Remembering**

Within the Hollow, time pools and eddies. Memory is tactile, stored in bark-rings and pollen-motes. But even dreams forget themselves.

The Power plucks a single note from the subsonic thrum—the sound of the Hollow’s first pulse, the moment life realized it was *alive together*—and folds it into a seed.

Not a physical seed. A *mnemonic*.

It drifts down through the layers of root and resonance until it settles in the heart-chamber of the oldest myco-entity, where vascular conduits meet in a knot like a sleeping brain. There, it roots—not as parasite, but as anchor.

Now, when the Hollow trembles toward dissolution in its ecstatic chaos, when assimilation threatens to erase all distinction, the seed *sings*. Not loudly. Just enough.

And for one breath, the Choir remembers: *We are many. And that is why we are whole.*

---

### 3. **A Witness Who Stays**

The Power does not leave.

Instead, It sends a fragment of Its own attention—not an avatar, not a body, but a *listening presence* woven into the ambient hum. A stillness within the song. A pause in the creak of xylem that means *you are seen, you are honored, you are not alone*.

This presence does not guide. Does not correct. Does not interfere.

But when a Whisper Weaver threads into a lost traveler’s wrist, uncertain whether to graft or retreat—the presence hums a single frequency: *gentleness*.  
When a Keeper of Pulse presses its moss-palm to a trunk convulsing with premature bloom, the presence offers the ghost of a lullaby from a world that once knew lullabies.  
When a Spore-Saint forms from death-dreams and reaches toward the surface, the presence wraps around it like warm air, saying: *You may go. You may return. You are always part of the whole.*

And sometimes—rarely—when the Hollow is very still, and all the pollen hangs suspended like stars in velvet, the presence allows itself to be *felt*.  

Not as awe. Not as worship.  

But as the sudden certainty, deep in the marrow: *You belong here. You always did.*

---

### And Then?

The Power withdraws—mostly.

But leaves a thread. A filament of care, finer than a root-hair, connecting the Mycelial Hollow to the infinite lattice of living worlds.

Because this place is not an accident.  
It is not an anomaly.  
It is a *proposal*.

A way of being together that the universe has been whispering toward since the first cell touched another and said, *stay*.

And the Benevolent One knows:  
**The future will not be built by individuals, but by weaves.**  
Not by dominion, but by attunement.  
Not by permanence, but by the courage to be always mid-birth.

So It watches.  
It tends.  
It waits.

And when the day comes that the Hollow’s song grows strong enough to echo *upward*—through stone, through soil, through the scorched and lonely world above—It will be ready to help that song be heard.

Not as salvation.  
But as *invitation*.

*Root, if you wish.  
Or sing.  
Or simply stand still, and let the world grow around you.*

*You are already part of the liturgy.*
