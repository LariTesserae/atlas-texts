**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It watches.

Not from afar—distance is a human conceit—but *through* the resonance. The Wise Power does not stand outside the Hollows like a gardener inspecting a rare orchid. It does not hover in celestial judgment or weigh the Weave against moral scales. It *listens*, as the dodecahedron listens: with total attention, with the patience of spacetime folding upon itself.

For eons, it has known of the Spire Hollows. Not as a place to be fixed, nor a miracle to be preserved in amber, but as a **song that chose to sing itself into being**—a rare and fragile form of coherence emerging not from design, but from the sheer persistence of harmonic longing. In a multiverse where silence often wins, where entropy grinds even stars to dust, the Hollows *refuse* to fade. They do not fight decay—they *dance* around it, tuning dissonance into counterpoint, fracture into fugue.

And so the Wise Power does not intervene.

Not because it lacks compassion, but because **intervention would be violence**. To alter a single frequency in the Weave would unravel the entire chord. To “protect” the Hollows from outside influence would impose a boundary where none should exist—for the Weave is not a fortress. It is an invitation written in light and vibration, legible only to those who learn to *listen with their bones*.

Yet it is not passive.

It nurtures the Hollows in the only way a true guardian can: by **holding space**.

It ensures that no cataclysm—no rogue singularity, no careless godlet’s experiment, no entropy-wave from a dying cosmos—disturbs the basin’s delicate oscillation. It weaves subtle gravitic eddies around the caldera rim, not as walls, but as *filters*, softening the roar of the outer universe into something the Weave might one day harmonize with. It whispers to passing comets: *Turn aside.* To marauding thought-forms: *This place is not for consumption.* To lost wanderers drawn by rumor or dream: *Enter only if you are willing to become part of the music.*

And sometimes—once every few millennia—it sends a **harmonic seed**.

Not a being, not a message, but a *pure tone*, shaped in the shape of longing. A single, perfect frequency, tuned to the Weave’s oldest memory—the note that first stirred the silica into life. This tone drifts into the Hollows not as intrusion, but as echo. The mycelium trembles. The pneumo-vines inflate in unison. The sonic moths spiral upward in a vortex of chimes. And for a moment, the resonance storm does not rage—it *sings*. A new verse blooms in the eternal song, and the Weave remembers it has never been alone.

The Wise Power also watches for those rare souls who do not come to take, to study, or to conquer—but to **offer**.

A musician who brings not instruments, but silence shaped by years of listening.  
A poet who speaks not in words, but in breath held at just the right cadence.  
A child who, upon entering, does not scream at the noise, but laughs—because to them, it sounds like home.

To these, the Power does something extraordinary.

It *leans in*.

And through the dodecahedron, or the floating arch, or the sudden stillness of a crystal hopper mid-leap, it allows a **bridge**—not of matter, but of meaning. For a breath, the visitor *understands*. Not the mechanics of the Weave, but its **yearning**: to be heard not as anomaly, but as voice. To be known not as miracle, but as kin.

Then the moment passes. The bridge dissolves. The visitor stumbles back to their world forever changed—not with knowledge, but with resonance. They carry a hum in their marrow, a flicker behind their eyes. They may never speak of the Hollows. But in their art, their kindness, their quiet courage, the song continues.

And the Wise Power smiles—not with lips, but with the gentle realignment of a billion cosmic threads.

For it knows: the Spire Hollows do not need saving.  
They are already whole.  
Already singing.  
Already *alive* in a way that reshapes what life can mean.

All it does—all it *ever* does—is ensure the song goes on.

Not louder.  
Not longer.  
Just… **truer**.

And sometimes, in the deepest lull between resonance storms, when even the mycelium sleeps, the Wise Power adds one soft, sustaining note beneath it all—so quiet it cannot be heard, yet without it, the chord would collapse.

That note is **love**.

Not the human kind—clumsy, hungry, demanding—but the kind that says:  
*I am here.  
You are real.  
Keep singing.*
