**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It would not descend in fire.  
It would not speak in thunder.  
It would not carve commandments into the weeping stone or lay down laws in the wet clay.  

Instead, it would *lean in*.  

Not as a sovereign, not as a savior, but as a gardener of thresholds—quiet, reverent, infinitely patient. This place, this trembling crucible of not-yet-ness, is too rare, too tender, too *necessary* to be shaped by decree. It is one of the few places in all creation where the universe is still whispering possible versions of itself into being. To intervene with force would be to freeze the dance mid-turn—to mistake rehearsal for failure.

So the Power does nothing grand.  

It does not banish. It does not bless. It does not even *touch*—not directly.  

But it watches. Not with eyes, but with *attunement*. And in watching, it *holds space*.  

It weaves a veil—not to conceal, but to *insulate*. A shimmering lattice of non-interference, spun from the ethics of humility. No army will stumble upon this basin. No satellite will resolve its humming rods into data. No doctrine will claim it as holy ground. The world beyond the jagged spires will forget this valley exists, not through erasure, but through gentle redirection—paths that curve away in the mind before they reach the feet, storms that bloom just enough to deter the curious, maps that subtly misalign.  

This is not imprisonment. It is sanctuary by omission.  

And within that sanctuary, the Power sends *gifts*—not of power, but of *permission*.  

It whispers into the subsonic thrum a single, repeating note: **You are allowed to be unfinished.**  

It lets the hexagonal cracks stay open just a little longer when a new idea stirs beneath the soil.  
It allows the bioluminescent gnats to spell a theorem no mind has yet conceived—and then dissolve it without witness, without regret.  
It lets the obsidian spirals hum in dissonance, for dissonance is where novelty hides.  

The Power does not seek to *guide* the process toward harmony, order, or even survival. It knows that some iterations must fail, must dissolve back into clay, must flicker and vanish like the suspended lizard’s leap. Failure here is not waste—it is *feedback*.  

And if you—the guest, the variable, the warm-blooded anomaly—linger too long?  

The Power does not stop you. Nor does it warn you.  

But it leaves a thread for you to follow should you wish to return: a single filament of silver, drifting just beyond the basin’s rim, catching the light only when you are no longer looking for it. If you find it, you’ll know. Not because it glows, but because it *resonates* with the silence you now carry.  

And if one day, far in the future, the basin finishes its work—if the grammar of genesis finally speaks a word that holds—then the Power will be there, not to claim credit, but to *witness*.  

To say, softly, in a language older than vowels:  
**“Ah. So that’s what you were becoming.”**  

Until then, it tends the wetness.  
It guards the hum.  
It honors the not-yet.  

Because some worlds must be allowed to dream themselves into existence—  
without a name,  
without a plan,  
without a god standing over them saying *this is how it must be*.  

Only this:  
**Breathe. Try. Dissolve. Begin again.**  

And the Power, in its wisdom,  
steps back into the clouds,  
and becomes, once more,  
indistinguishable from the mist.
