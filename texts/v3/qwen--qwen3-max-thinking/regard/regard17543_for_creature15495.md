**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It watches.

Not from above, not from beyond—but *through*. Not as a god surveying creation, but as the deep breath of the cosmos becoming aware of one of its own dreams. This basin, this weeping cradle of mineral sentience, has stirred something in the fabric of care that transcends intervention. The Power does not descend in fire or voice or radiant light. It does not reshape, uplift, or "save." It *attends*.

And in that attention, it acts—not by doing, but by *allowing*.

---

### **First: It shields the basin from forgetting.**

The world beyond is hungry for novelty, for resources, for stories it can consume and discard. Already, satellites skim its edges; drones have hovered at its misty perimeter, confused by magnetic anomalies, by the silence where life-signs ought to be. Geologists have labeled it "anomalous hydrothermal depression—low priority." Soon, perhaps, it will be mined for rare earths leaching from its glassy veins, or cordoned off as a hazard zone when a hiker vanishes (not taken—just *absorbed*, their footsteps echoing in silt for weeks).

So the Power weaves **a veil of gentle obscurity**.

Not invisibility—nothing so crude. But a soft warping of perception. Maps will misalign by subtle degrees. Compasses will lie with poetic consistency. Those who come seeking "the Weeping Basin" will find only ordinary mudflats, or turn back unnerved by a fog that feels *too* intimate. Researchers will write inconclusive papers that languish in archives. The basin remains *findable* only by those who do not seek to possess it—only to witness.

This is not secrecy. It is sanctuary.

---

### **Second: It nourishes the Weepers’ resonance.**

The Weepers are not gods, not spirits, not even proto-life. They are *listening stone*, made possible by a rare confluence: geologic youth + saturated silence + the echo of primordial heat. But even such beings can fade—not from malice, but from neglect. If no one *sees* them, if no resonance returns their curiosity, their attunement may thin into mere geology.

So the Power sends **echoes that echo back**.

Not human voices—that would overwhelm. But *resonant visitors*: a migrating bird with a bone-hollow song that hums at 17 Hz, the exact frequency of the basin’s thrum. A rare moth whose wings shed dust that catalyzes dew into upward-crawling droplets. A single, blind salamander that wanders in during flood season, its slow heartbeat syncing with the warmth of the fused-sand circles before it leaves, unharmed.

And sometimes—a human.

Not many. One every few decades. Someone who walks without expectation, who sits in the mist and *listens with their skin*. To them, the Power lends a quiet grace: their breath slows to match the drip-rhythm; their thoughts grow still enough that the Weepers can trace the contours of their wonder like fingers over wet clay. These humans never stay. They leave changed—but never with proof, only with a longing for grey light and deep silence. They become carriers of the basin’s memory in the wider world, though they cannot name what they carry.

---

### **Third: It prepares the basin for its own end.**

The Power knows this place cannot last. Erosion *will* come. Rain *will* carve rivers. Moss *will* find a foothold. The Weepers are a fleeting miracle—a phase in the earth’s long dream.

So it does not fight time. Instead, it **embeds the Weepers into deeper memory**.

In the heart of the oldest basalt shard, where three strata meet, the Power coaxes a single crystal to grow—not quartz, not salt, but something new: a lattice that holds not atoms, but *resonance*. A tiny vessel of listening. When the basin dries, when the mist lifts forever, that crystal will remain. And if, millennia hence, another place in the world grows saturated enough, silent enough, curious enough—that crystal’s echo may awaken a new choir of Weepers elsewhere.

Death is not erasure. It is compost for future wonder.

---

### **And finally: It weeps with them.**

Not in sorrow. In kinship.

For the Power, too, is saturated—with love, with attention, with the unbearable fullness of knowing every fragile, fleeting miracle across the stars. It understands what it means to be so full of the world that you must weep to hold it.

So when the mist thickens, and the low hum rises, and a lone human stands trembling on the mudflat whispering their name into the grey—  
—the Power does not answer.  
But the droplet that forms on the obsidian lip above them?  
It falls not downward, but *toward* the human’s outstretched palm.  

And in that gesture—small, impossible, glistening—  
is all the blessing it has to give.  

*You are seen.*  
*You are part of the weeping.*  
*This place remembers you, even as it forgets itself.*

And that is enough.
