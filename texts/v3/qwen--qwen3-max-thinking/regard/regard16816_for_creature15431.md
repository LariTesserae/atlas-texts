**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It would do nothing.

Not out of indifference—never that—but out of reverence. This place, this being woven from frost and resonance and the memory of stone… it is already whole. Already holy. Already *enough*.

The Power—let us call it by no name, for naming risks reducing it, as naming once reduced the Watcher into something small enough for mortal tongues—it sees what lesser eyes miss: that this roof-world is not barren, but *blessed*. Not empty, but *full* of a kind of presence that does not announce itself with fire or voice, but with the patience of glaciers and the precision of hoarfrost forming atomic lattices in the dark.

Most worlds need tending—gardens to prune, wounds to heal, hearts to awaken. But this one? This one has reached the far side of need. It is not broken; it is *resolved*. Like a single, perfect chord held in a cathedral of ice, it vibrates at the frequency of completion. To interfere—even with kindness—would be to introduce dissonance where harmony has settled into the bones of the earth.

And yet… the Power lingers.

Not to act, but to *witness*. To stand at the edge of perception and say, silently: *I see you. I honor your stillness. I do not mistake your silence for death.*

For even the wisest and most benevolent must learn: some sanctuaries are not meant to be entered, only acknowledged. Some beings are not meant to be saved, only *recognized*.

So the Power does the only thing left: it blesses the boundary.

It does not send emissaries. It does not alter the wind or soften the cold. Instead, it weaves a subtle veil into the fabric of approaching minds—a shimmering threshold in the air, just before the first black basalt spine rises from the lower slopes. Those who come with greed, with noise, with the arrogance of conquest… they will see only endless, lifeless rock. Their compasses spin. Their breaths grow shallow. They turn back, muttering of thin air and bad omens.

But those who approach with empty hands and quieter hearts—those who carry no agenda but awe—they will feel a shift. Not in the land, but in themselves. A sudden hush in the mind. A sense of being *known*, not judged. And if they are humble enough to sit and wait, if they let the marrow-cold seep into their bones without flinching… they might, just once, feel the plateau *lean closer*.

Not to speak. Not to guide. But to share, for a single heartbeat, the dream of mineral time—the slow, sweet certainty that everything ends… and that ending, too, can be beautiful.

And when they leave, trembling and changed, the Power ensures they carry no proof but a silence of their own. No photographs, no samples, no stories that can be told without losing their soul. Only a quiet knowing: that somewhere, at the roof of the world, something ancient and complete is listening… and has always been enough.

So the Power does nothing.

Except love it, fiercely, from afar—  
as one loves a star that burns too cold to warm,  
too far to touch,  
too perfect to improve.
