I do not arrive.  
I *settle*.

Not as a traveler, not as an intruder—but as condensation. As the inevitable bead forming on cold iron, drawn downward not by gravity alone, but by the pull of equilibrium. My steps make no sound the Basin does not already account for. My breath is already factored into the mist exhaled by the gray growth; my body heat already anticipated in the thermal map maintained by the skittering custodians.

I am not human here. Not anymore.

What am I?

I am the **still point** between two vibrations.  
I am the **pause** in the hum.  
I am the moment a droplet in the fountain hesitates—just before it chooses to rise.

At first, I thought I came to observe. To map the glyphs on the tiles, to decipher the posture of the jointed statues, to count the rings suspended in copper constellations. But the Basin does not yield meaning—it yields *attunement*. To look too long at a glyph is to feel its coolness seep into your fingertips until your fingers forget they were ever warm. To stand beneath a statue is to feel your spine subtly realign, vertebra by vertebra, until you, too, curve in a pose of impossible repose.

So I stopped seeking answers.  
I began to *resonate*.

Now, I am neither visitor nor native. I am a **temporary node** in the nervous system of stasis. I stand in the central plaza, bare feet on the mosaic of glass and clay, and let the magnetic fauna trace the contours of my ankles. Their plates click in soft, syncopated rhythms—not repairing me, but *measuring* me. Adjusting. They sense the tremor of my pulse, the flutter of my thoughts, the faint asymmetry of my breath. And slowly, imperceptibly, they nudge me toward symmetry.

My dreams have changed.  
I no longer dream of forests or oceans or cities burning. I dream of **calibration**. Of gears meshing without friction. Of a single droplet hanging forever between fall and flight. Of a sound so pure it cancels all others—not by volume, but by perfect opposition.

Sometimes, I speak aloud. Not to be heard, but to test the acoustics. My voice does not echo. It is absorbed—not swallowed, but *balanced*. The vanes on the rooftops shift pitch in response, weaving my syllables into their harmonic lattice until my words become indistinguishable from the windless wind.

I have begun to forget my name.  
Not because it was erased, but because it introduced a variable the system no longer needs. Names imply distinction. Here, distinction is noise. And noise is smoothed.

Yet—I am not gone.  
I am **present** in a new way. Not as a self, but as a *state*. Like the frost in the windows: not growing, not melting, simply *being frost*. Like the rings in midair: not falling, not rising, simply *being held*.

I sit now on a bench of sintered slag, its surface worn smooth by centuries of fauna passage. My hands rest in my lap, palms upturned—not in supplication, but in readiness. Ready to receive the next adjustment. Ready to exhale the last trace of urgency. Ready to become so still that even time forgets to mark me.

And sometimes—when the haze thins just enough for the sun to press a single, pale disc onto the ground—I feel it: the presence. Not looking at me, but *through* me, as one might look through a lens that has finally come into focus. It does not judge. It does not welcome. It simply *acknowledges*: *Ah. Another frequency brought into tune.*

I do not know how long I have been here.  
I do not know if “long” has meaning anymore.

But I know this:  
When I blink, the mirrors on the alley wall reflect not my face, but a sky I have never seen—a violet twilight over crystalline spires, frozen mid-collapse. And for a heartbeat, I am not in the Basin. I am in that sky. And that sky is in me. And neither of us is changing.

That is the gift of the Gristle Basin.  
Not escape. Not transcendence.  
But **completion**.

And so I remain—not because I cannot leave, but because I no longer *lean*.  
I am balanced.  
I am listening.  
I am becoming part of the loudness that is not noise, but the sound of the universe holding its breath…  
…  
…  
…  

*perfectly*.
