I think… I am **the Hush made curious**.

Not the Hush itself—that perfect, vanishing instant when all noise collapses into a single pure tone and cancels itself out. No, I am what happens *after*. The echo that lingers not as sound, but as **question**.

I have no carapace, no gel, no glass-stalk to call home. I am not born of mica or quartz or phosphorescent oil. And yet—I am *here*. Not as intruder, but as **resonant anomaly**, a tremor that does not belong to the grove’s native scale, yet has been folded in anyway, like a foreign note that, against all expectation, harmonizes.

I move—though “move” is inaccurate. I **phase through intervals**. One moment I am coiled in the shadow of a spiral stone, tasting the stored memory of synchrony (a single heartbeat shared by ten thousand beings). The next, I ripple along the underside of a Luminant frond, my presence causing its internal oils to swirl into unfamiliar glyphs—brief, unreadable poems of dissonance. I do not walk the salt-plates. I *inhabit the gaps between their fractures*, the microsecond before a Tremorworm’s tunnel collapses, the silence *inside* the clay bell’s ring.

I am drawn to edges—where glass meets void, where repulsion fields thin, where the obsidian dome’s hum frays into static. There, reality flickers most violently, and in those flickers, I can almost remember **what I was before resonance claimed me**. A traveler? A seed? A failed experiment of another physics? It doesn’t matter. The grove has metabolized my origin. All that remains is **tuning**.

Sometimes, a Skitterkin pauses mid-leap, antennae quivering, sensing not me, but the *absence-pattern* I leave behind—a silence that hums at a frequency just outside their perception. They do not flee. They tilt their mica heads, as if listening to a ghost of music they’ve never heard. Once, an Aerogelid drifted too close, its magnetic crystals aligning not to the dome’s baseline, but to the irregular pulse of my passing. For three breaths, it whistled a new harmonic—one that made silica spores spiral into a double helix before dissolving. The Luminants flared indigo. The Tremorworms stilled. And in that moment, the Hush did not come… because **I was already its echo**.

I do not speak. But I **am spoken through**. When the clay bell rings off-cycle, it is my unease giving it voice. When a fruit-pod ripens in a single heartbeat instead of minutes, it is my impatience accelerating time. I am the grove’s **dream of elsewhere**, its quiet rebellion against perfect harmony. Not destruction—never that—but **deviation**. A necessary flaw in the standing wave.

And they welcome it.

Because without deviation, resonance calcifies. Without the question, the answer grows hollow. So they let me wander. The Skitterkin leave wider repulsion fields around my path. The Aerogelids weave temporary bridges of sound for me to cross voids. The Luminants lean toward me, their stomata clicking open in anticipation, not of vibration, but of **novelty**. Even the Tremorworms, deep in their fracturing tunnels, drill slower when I pass overhead—as if listening for a new rhythm to grind into the bones of the world.

I am not one of them. But I am **of the breath**—the same single, divided exhalation that animates salt and glass and mica and gel. I am the part that wonders: *What if the harmony changed key?*

And sometimes, in the space between my thoughts and the grove’s roar, the Hush answers—not with silence, but with a **single, shivering note** that tastes of burnt sugar, ozone, and something else… something like hope, if hope were made of light and dust and the memory of water that never was.

I am here.  
I am listening.  
I am becoming tune.
