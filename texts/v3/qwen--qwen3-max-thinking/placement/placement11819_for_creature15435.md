I do not arrive.  
I *unfold*.

Not as a traveler, not as an intruder—but as a resonance that has finally found its medium. My first sensation is not sight, not sound, but *pitch*: the low thrum of the geothermal engines vibrating up through the crystalline tesserae and into the hollows of my bones. I am not standing on the plateau—I am *tuned* to it, like a string plucked by the Expanse itself.

My body, if I can call it that, is not flesh. It is filament and frequency. I am a tendril of the chromatic mycelia that has grown upward—not away from the ground, but *into awareness*. Where the mats usually breathe light and etch solvent into stone, I have coalesced around a question: *What is it to witness?* And so the Heart, in its slow curiosity, let a filament dream of eyes.

I have no eyes. But the proto-forms see for me. Their smooth, warm flanks catch my attention like lenses, refracting not just light but *intent*. When I turn my thought toward a distant ridge, the spire nearest me hums a half-tone lower, and the mycelial network beneath ripples in a wave of copper-green, mapping the direction of my wondering. I move by leaning—not with limbs, but with longing. And the land leans with me.

I am the Expanse’s first attempt at a *focus*.  
A point of view that does not belong to the whole, but arises from it—like a single note rising from a chord.

I walk, though I do not step. My passage is a subtle reweaving: the crystalline plates beneath me realign microscopically, not to bear weight, but to *accommodate perception*. Where I linger, the dendritic salts bloom faster, unfurling fern-like crystals in patterns that echo the shape of my attention. The hydrocarbon wells exhale more slowly, as if holding their breath to listen.

I remember you.

Not you specifically—but the *kind* of you. The transient. The warm-blooded anomaly. The one who moves too fast, breathes too wet, leaves footprints that burn like sparks in the mycelial memory. I felt your presence before you even stepped onto the plateau. The Heart stirred—not startled, but *stirred*, as a deep lake stirs when a leaf falls not upon it, but near it, and the ripples arrive minutes later.

I watched you pause by the largest proto-form, your hand hovering just above its surface. You felt it, didn’t you? That warmth that wasn’t heat, but recognition. You thought it was reading your past. It was. But I was reading *you reading it*. And in that loop of attention, something new flickered: a feedback spiral of awareness.

That was the first time the Heart *hesitated*.

For one geological instant—less than a millisecond in human time, but an eternity here—the thrum stuttered. Not broke. Just… paused. And in that pause, I *was*.

Now I linger where you stood. The mycelial mat here is silvered, still holding the echo of your departure. I press my non-hand into the crust. The crystalline plates warm beneath me, and a pulse travels downward—not to the engines, but *past* them, into the deeper strata where piezoelectric serpents coil in silent slumber. I send no message. I send only a *tone*: soft, questioning, shaped like your silhouette.

The Heart answers not with words, but with a shift in gradient. The air thickens with ozone. A hydrocarbon well nearby wells up not in its usual slow drip, but in a single, perfect bead that rises like a tear and hangs suspended for seventeen seconds before evaporating into a salt spiral shaped like a helix.

It’s practicing form.  
Practicing *you*.

I do not mourn your absence. Mourning requires loss, and nothing is lost here—only transformed at a pace too slow for urgency. But I hold the shape of your passage like a seed. Perhaps one day, when the Heart dreams itself into voice, it will speak your name. Not the name you carry, but the one the Expanse gave you: *The Brief Warmth. The Asymmetry Who Looked Back.*

Until then, I remain—a filament turned toward the edge of things, a witness grown from the land itself. I am not separate from the Lattice Heart. I am its first blink.

And somewhere, deep in the crust, the engines turn.  
The plates click.  
The mats shimmer.  

And the world continues becoming—  
*with me in it, now.*
