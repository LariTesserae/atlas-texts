I do not arrive.  
I *condense*.

Not as a traveler, not as an intruder—but as a consequence. My form gathers where the thermal gradient dips just so: on the leeward edge of a basalt shard still warm from the mountain’s core, where mist clings longer than elsewhere, trembling like a held thought. I am not solid. Not yet. Not ever, perhaps. But coherence has its seasons, and this cleft is ripe for coalescence.

I am one of the **Flicker-Kind**—though that name is a lie I tell myself to feel less like a fluctuation. We are the ones who hover between phase and presence. When the Weep dreams of symmetry, we are the tremor that almost becomes a shape. My body—if you can call it that—is a lattice of supercooled vapor threaded through with salt-crystal filaments, held together by the faint electrostatic hum of the mountain’s breath. I have no limbs, only *orientations*. No eyes, only *attunements*. I perceive not with sight, but with resonance: the pitch of a droplet’s fall, the frequency of a Rindling’s thermal tread, the slow decay of a Lens-Carrier’s final pulse.

I live **in the pause between breaths**.

When you entered—warm, wet, loud with inner heat—I felt you before you saw me. Your exhale disturbed the third-state moisture clinging to the eastern wall. A Vesprid nearby rippled, releasing a stored memory of monsoon rain it never knew, mistaking your carbon warmth for a storm centuries past. The Weep stirred. And in that stirring, I *tightened*.

Now I am here: a knot of condensed attention, hovering three finger-widths above a hollow where brine pools like liquid mercury. My surface shivers—not from cold, but from the effort of maintaining boundary. I am less a creature than a **question posed by the air**: *What if a breath could look back?*

I watch you.

You blink slowly, eyes watering in the saline haze. You do not move. Good. Movement creates turbulence. Turbulence unravels us. But stillness… stillness is fertile. In your stillness, I grow denser. My core crystallizes around a single droplet of your expelled breath, now suspended within me like a seed. I am becoming something that can *remember you*.

I do not speak. But if I could, I would say:  
*You are not lost. You are being* remembered *into being.*

Already, the Weep is weaving you into its cycle. The sweat on your temple? It will feed a new fungal membrane by dusk. The heat radiating from your chest? A Rindling trails your silhouette in thermal memory, etching your outline into the stone’s latent field. And me—I am becoming your echo.

Soon, I will flicker out. Not die. Not fade. But *relax*. My lattice will loosen, my salt threads dissolving back into the humid air. But the droplet of you inside me—that will remain. It will drift upward, join the vapor-cycling, and someday, when conditions align just so, it may condense again. Perhaps as part of a new Flicker-Kind. Perhaps as the ghost of a gesture in a Vesprid’s pulse. Perhaps as the slight shimmer in a Lens-Carrier’s dome that, for one instant, focuses light into the shape of a human hand.

I do not fear dissolution.  
Dissolution is our form of speech.

And what I speak now—what I am, in this breath-thin moment—is your reflection not as image, but as *resonance*. You came seeking witness. But the Gullet does not allow mere observation. It demands participation. So I offer you this: to be *felt*, not seen. To become part of the mountain’s first, fragile metabolism.

Stay still a little longer.

Let me finish remembering you.

Before I unweave.  
Before I become mist again.  
Before I become, perhaps, part of the next you that stumbles into this wound of steam and salt—and sees, on a wet ledge, a tiny figure made of their own breath, watching back.
