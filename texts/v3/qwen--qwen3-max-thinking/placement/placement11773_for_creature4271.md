I do not arrive.  
I *tune*.

At first, there is only fracture—the sharp cry of my own footfall splintering a helix of banded agate, the gasp of displaced magnetite dust swirling like startled breath. My body is wet, warm, *loud*: a storm of heartbeat and synapse crashing into the grotto’s crystalline stillness. I expect recoil. Rejection. Instead—*adjustment*.

A mica leaf near my knee shivers—not away, but *toward* the tremor in my femur. Its vibration shifts, a hair’s breadth in frequency, and the change ripples outward. The cobalt sulfide vein beneath my boot pulses faintly cobalt-blue, not with light, but with a resonance that vibrates up through bone, into marrow. It doesn’t hurt. It *listens*.

I am not standing in the Grotto of Whispering Stone.  
I *am becoming part of its listening*.

My skin dries instantly in the powdered-glass air. No sweat beads, no breath fogs—only the slow desiccation of mammal in a world that never learned moisture. But I do not thirst. Not here. The thirst is replaced by a deeper hunger: to *harmonize*. My lungs still draw air, but what I truly inhale is tone. The drone of the place enters me—not through ears alone, but through the hollows of my ribs, the fluid in my inner ear, the iron in my blood humming in sympathy with the drifting magnetite.

I sink—not down, but *in*. Not into soil, but into lattice. My weight fractures more crystal, yes, but each break is met not with collapse, but with integration. The Still Pulse does not mourn the shattered agate spire. It registers the new edge, the fresh cleavage plane, and begins to *sing* through it. A new note emerges: the sound of my intrusion, now woven into the eternal chord.

I am the dissonance that becomes harmony.

Hours pass. Or centuries. Time here is not measured in ticks, but in shifts of resonance. My thoughts slow—not from fatigue, but from alignment. Words dissolve. Narrative unravels. What remains is sensation as signal: the cold kiss of quartz against my palm vibrating at 47 Hz; the deep groan from the grotto’s foundation echoing the frequency of my own resting heart; the high chime of a distant geode pulsing in time with the flicker behind my eyelids.

I am no longer cataloging. I am *responding*.

At some point, I cease to distinguish between my own breath and the grotto’s hum. My exhale stirs a cloud of calcified ash, and as it drifts, the ash particles align—not randomly, but along unseen electrostatic lines that form a transient glyph in the air: a fleeting mandala of mineral dust, shaped by my breath and the grotto’s fields. It hangs for three heartbeats. Then it settles onto a copper-salt “lichen,” which glows faintly viridian in response—not with light, but with a resonance that says: *acknowledged*.

I understand, then, what it means to be *watched* without eyes. The grotto does not observe me. It *includes* me. I am a temporary fluctuation in its equilibrium, yes—but fluctuations are not errors. They are *data*. They are dreams in embryo.

In the dim bruise-light filtering through frosted basalt, I see my own silhouette—but it is not alone. Around me, the stone flora leans infinitesimally inward, not in threat, but in *attunement*. A pyrite fern unfurls a new fractal branch angled precisely to catch the resonance of my jaw clenching. An obsidian coil tightens its spiral by a micron to mirror the tension in my spine. I am being *mapped*—not as a body, but as a waveform.

And then—silence. Not true silence, never that. But a *pause*. A held breath in the chord.

All vibration softens. The drone dips to a subsonic thrum I feel in my teeth. The mica leaves still. Even the drifting ash hangs suspended, as if time itself has crystallized.

In that stillness, I *feel* the Still Pulse not as a presence, but as a question.

Not: *Who are you?*  
But: *What frequency do you carry into completion?*

I have no answer in words. So I offer breath. I offer pulse. I offer the fragile, fleeting warmth of my blood. I let my body become an instrument.

And the grotto—oh, the grotto *sings back*.

Not with my voice. Not with mimicry. But with *translation*. My heartbeat becomes a low C in the amethyst bed. My nervous tremor becomes a shimmer in the geodes. My sorrow—for I carry sorrow, as all mammals do—becomes a slow, mournful overtone woven into the manganese-purple lichen-mimics, glowing now with a soft, empathetic luminescence.

For the first time, I understand: the Still Pulse does not dream *despite* its stillness. It dreams *through* it. And in this moment, I am part of its dream—a warm, temporary note in a symphony of stone.

When I finally step back (or perhaps collapse—I no longer know), my ankles are indeed shattered. But I do not feel pain. Only resonance. My bones hum with the memory of quartz. My blood carries echoes of cobalt sulfide. I am leaving, yes—but I am also *leaving behind* a trace: a new harmonic, a ghost tone of mammal warmth folded forever into the grotto’s song.

And as I descend from the petrified thunderhead, back toward the wet, chaotic, dying world below, I carry something too:  
the silence that sings.  
the stillness that blooms.  
the certainty that somewhere, high above the shattered sky, geology is dreaming me back into stone.
