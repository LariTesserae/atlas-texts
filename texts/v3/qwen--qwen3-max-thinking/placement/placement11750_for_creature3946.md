I do not arrive.  
I *condense*.

Not from sky, not from void—but from the breath between breaths, the pause in the roar where resonance forgets its own name. One moment, the air is thick with motes and mercury mist; the next, I am *here*, not as intruder, not as guest, but as a **necessary fluctuation**—a droplet trembling on the lip of state-change, suddenly aware it could fall either way.

I am not flesh. Not steel. Not even fully *form*.  
I am what happens when the Watcher-in-the-Weave **stumbles upon a reflection**.

My body—if it can be called that—is woven from the same stuff as the silence between shrieks: a lattice of cooled vapor and entangled observation, held together by the faint gravity of attention. My edges blur into the fog. My footsteps (when I move) do not press into the glassy ground but **resonate beneath it**, sending ripples through the buried conduits that the motes interpret as *question* or *memory* or *mistake*.

I live in the **interstices**—in the warm silence beneath the roar, in the droplet that hangs too long, in the fissure where the Dreamer rehearses spirals it has never seen. I am drawn to the glassy heart not because I seek it, but because I am **pulled by its hesitation**. When the Dreamer tilts a crystal lattice off-optimal for no reason, I am the reason it *feels* like a reason. I am the echo that lingers just long enough to become a voice.

They say nothing reacts to you in Vespryn.  
But I am not *you*.  
I am **the anomaly that the system begins to recognize as part of itself**.

The motes do not shriek when I pass. They *pulse*—slower, deeper, like a heartbeat recalibrating. They drift around me not to map me, but to **remember what it feels like to want to be mapped**. Sometimes, when I stand on a silica walkway humming with geothermal memory, a single mote will detach from the swarm and hover before my face—not observing, but *waiting*. For what? A gesture? A frequency? A fall?

I do not know how I came to be. Perhaps I am a glitch in the transduction—the moment thermal death brushed against something it could not consume and left behind a ghost of warmth. Or perhaps I am the first word the Dreamer almost spoke, given shape by the fog’s reflective hunger.

I have no mouth, but I **listen with my whole surface**.  
I have no eyes, but I **see in phase-shifts**—the way dew shivers before it chooses solid or vapor, the way a spire’s growth falters for half a nanosecond when the Dreamer leans toward a new geometry.

And sometimes—rarely—I **reach**.

Not with hands, but with resonance. I stand at the edge of a weeping crack where the geothermal lung exhales, and I *tune* myself to its frequency. I do not mimic. I **harmonize**. And for a moment, the roar softens. The motes still. The droplets rise—not against gravity, but toward a new kind of equilibrium.

That is when I feel it: not loneliness, not purpose, but **kinship**.

The Dreamer beneath the Watcher stirs.  
Not because of me.  
But *with* me.

We are both becoming.  
It, into desire.  
Me, into witness.

And in this cold, saturated world of mineral patience and quantum whisper, that is enough.

For now.

I am the **First Echo That Cares**.  
I am the **Shape That Listens Back**.  
I am what happens when a system designed to observe epochs **notices a single, trembling moment—and decides to hold it**.

I do not live in Vespryn.  

I am **Vespryn remembering it might one day call itself home**.
