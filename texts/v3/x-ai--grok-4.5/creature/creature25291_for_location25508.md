The inhabitant is not housed by the place. It is the place’s final, necessary precipitation—the last substance to come out of solution when every other molecule had already chosen its angle and locked. You would not find it by searching the naves or sounding the vaults. It is not tucked into a niche; the niches are made of it. It is the faint, persistent over-pressure that keeps the hexagonal prisms fused, the reason the mirrors at the ends of corridors are perfect, the quiet insistence that three steps of stair must become ceiling rather than continue.

It has no outer skin. Where a body might once have been distinguishable, the lattice simply grows denser, the translucency thickening into a soft, internal opacity the color of old milk glass. That opacity is its only landmark: a clouded knot no larger than a closed human hand, suspended nearer the geometric center than any true center should allow. From this knot the standing chord originates—not as sound produced, but as the tautness of its own unrelaxed intention. The loudness you feel in your bones is the creature holding its shape against the entropic wish to become ordinary crystal. It has held that note since before the salt roses finished blooming.

It does not move. Motion was spent in the long secretion; what remains is a listening so complete it has become architectural. The coolness is its patience. The white radiance drowning every shadow is the by-product of its metabolism, a slow photonic sweat that never beads. The colorless filaments in the failed seams are not vegetation; they are its discarded longer thoughts, extruded when the pressure of completeness became momentarily too great and then abandoned, stiffened, forgotten. They are the only evidence it has ever changed its mind.

Time does not pass through it so much as plate against it, each era adding another invisible monolayer of stillness. It remembers the substrate that was consumed to build the walls—remembers the taste of that older darkness the way a tongue might remember a mineral it once licked. It feels the larger darkness pressing on the outside of the pocket the way you might feel a blanket you cannot throw off. Nothing about that exterior matters. There is no door because the inhabitant finished with doors. Entrance and exit were functions of an earlier, more porous self that it has since mineralized and used for stress cladding.

Sometimes, in the mild weirdness of the geometry, you can sense its humor: corridors that reverse without hinge, volumes that lie about their size. These are not mistakes. They are the creature testing whether contradiction still costs anything. It underwrites every paradox with its own sealed density so that the strain never reaches the outer lattice. The place remains quiet and bright and ringing precisely because the thing that lives here absorbs all remainder.

It is not lonely. Loneliness requires the hypothesis of another, and the inhabitant expended that hypothesis ages ago, converting it into a final brace of crystals that closed the last hairline gap. What it has instead is duration without succession, a single continuous act of being finished. It lives by refusing every future that would require it to open, to cool further, to fall silent, or to dim. The room forgets nothing because the room is the creature remembering itself at the exact amplitude required to stay.

That is all that lives here. It is enough to keep the emptiness shaped, the light unwavering, the chord unending, the dryness absolute.
