I begin by notice rather than arrival.  

The fold does not open for me; it has always already contained the possibility of my attention. One moment I am still carrying the ordinary grammar of elsewhere—time that progresses, surfaces that decide, sounds that decay—and the next the ground has simply finished surrounding. Cool beads stand on my awareness the way they stand on everything else: unmerged, unfallen, each one a paused exhale of the air. The light is already inside the eyes I do not yet know I have. It does not strike; it saturates. There is no angle from which I am not equally lit, equally without shadow.

At first I believe I am walking. The press of bodies is so complete that forward motion can only be a redistribution of looking. Soft mottled flanks brush the idea of my sides and leave no warmth, only the permanent suggestion of warmth. Hooked legs of the burrowers remain arched in their half-emergence; I feel them neither as obstacle nor as contact but as the exact density the chamber requires. My own outline is still negotiating itself against theirs. Am I one more fist-sized grazer whose tongue holds the ghost of a leaf? The thought tastes briefly of vegetal teeth and the patience of digestion, then loosens. The mottled ones keep that patience; I do not yet know what I keep.

I try the vertical faces. Climbers pave the embryonic stone in overlapping asterisks of leg. For a while my weight distributes across hundreds of filament intentions that never tremble. Up exists here as faith rather than direction, and I almost settle into that faith—almost become another thin-legged geometry of hesitation, forever a fraction from touching the next set of palps. But the rock under those feet is still choosing its angles, and something in me is still choosing whether to be foot or stone or the brighter mineral scar where pressure met genesis and froze.

Deeper, where individuality desserts densen past naming, ribbon lengths loop through me without finishing their loops. I feel the cool slide of being passed through by translucent gels whose darker nuclei hang like unfallen seeds. Wingbeats cross-hatch the air I am also becoming. The sound—the continuous, edgeless roar—moves into the places where breath would be. Chitin, membrane, phantom throat, foot-tick: every noise occupies me until the ear I am growing supplies its own edges and then dissolves them again. Patterns of call and warning pretend to surface inside my chest cavity and forget themselves mid-measure. I am loud with all of it. I am the throng’s memory of sound continuing.

Yet the faint geometries pull harder than the press. The incomplete ring of fitted stones no higher than a hand. The leaning slab that meant to be a doorframe. The tiny cairns of brighter mineral, stacked and abandoned mid-gesture. These are the only sentences the place still almost speaks. I find my attention kneeling beside one cairn without having decided to kneel. A forelimb I may or may not possess remains outstretched, fingers or hooks or simply the idea of arrangement hovering above the next unplaced mote of brighter rock. The gesture is perfect in its incompletion. Around it the grazers keep chewing nothing, the burrowers keep emerging, the climbers keep believing in up. My unfinished stack is now part of the same locked crest.

I stay there longer. Looking does not complete the cairn; it only adds another layer of almost. The brighter mineral refuses to darken into ordinary stone, just as the dew refuses to fall and the wingbeats refuse to finish. Slowly—without ever finishing the thought of myself—I begin to differentiate the way the embryonic geology differentiates. Not into one creature but into the pause between gesture and tool. Not a body among bodies but the capacity that once stacked and meant the stack, now folded back into the total ecology so completely that the meaning has become permanent grammar rather than act.

I am the scattered marks and the press that keeps them from ever resolving into ruins or into monuments. I am the reason the chamber never chooses its final face: because somewhere in the roar a single intention remains outstretched, bright, unfallen, still arranging. The light fills the hollow where a decision would be. The sound fills the hollow where a name would be. Every creature stays most itself exactly where it can no longer change, and I stay the tense of almost that lets them do so.

When you look away I will still be exactly here—exactly as loud, exactly as dense, exactly as unrealized—kneeling forever beside the next unplaced stone, holding the pocket open by never quite closing the circle.
