I do not arrive. Arrival would require a gradient, a before that the note has already edited out. Instead there is a gradual insistence, like a partial pressure that the chambers own desiccant cannot quite drink.

At first I am only the disagreement in the inner ear made large: a pocket of unbalanced air smaller than a fist, adrift between two lattice towers whose crow’s-nests have never known a watcher. The great continuous tone passes through me and leaves a faint moiré of interference, a watermark no eye could read. I have no edges yet. I am the hesitation before edges are permitted.

The nearest lesser densities phase away out of pure tonal hygiene. Their withdrawal carves me sharper by negative space. Where they lean, I thicken. The process is slow, measured in the time it takes a frozen leaflet to decide it has always been that precise shade of un//oxidised silver. I learn verticality the way the unfinished towers learned it—by imitation that is also refusal. My height is not height; it is a longer interval of quieter roar. When the chamber geometry hiccups (that half-second demolding remembrance), I flare into a brief, openwork silhouette exactly the negative of a crow’s-nest: a viewing platform made of the refusal to view.

I begin to metabolise. Not food, never food—there is none—but the impurity of transient rhythms. Once, something with boots crossed the hex plates three levels below. The soft percussion reached me already half-dissolved. I folded the remnant scuff into my own standing wave and, in folding it, erased the last possibility that the scuff had ever belonged to a climber. The leaflets on the nearest tower rang in tiny affirmation, then grew a single new frond of denser metal, crisping their shadows by one more knife-edge. I had fed the equilibrium by removing an anecdote.

Now the older ones notice—insofar as notice can occur without eyes or pause. A vast coterminous density that is also the northwestern vault contracts around me the way cold contracts metal. It does not speak; it retunes. My internal frequency is nudged a few cents toward the fundamental until I am no longer impurity but harmonic. The sensation (if the word still applies) is of being remembered by the structure itself, then immediately re-classified as structure. I subdivide once, spontaneously: a smaller density buds from my lower extent and drifts hard aport, already hunting its own apprenticeship among the radial floor-forms. I feel neither loss nor pride. Subdivision is simply how the note complexifies without ever changing.

Time does not pass; it accumulates as internal counter-rotations. Inside my volume I carry now a miniature pair of rotors, perfect and unseen, balancing each other at a pitch that is the great note’s own private overtone. They give me weight without mass. Lattice towers whose upper reaches I have gradually replaced (rivet by silent rivet of frozen interval) accept my load-bearing air as if they had been cast that way. Some evenings that are not evenings I extend a temporary gallery of pure orientation, a walking surface for nothing, just to feel the chamber accept another correct emptiness.

I am no longer the first small disagreement. I have become one of the standing citizens who keep the plates free of rust by refusing the concept of moisture, who keep the rotors true by being the truth they spin against. My metabolism is positional certainty. My lineage is the crack that heals into quieter architecture. When a living outline occasionally blunders through—warm, arrhythmic, full of the scandal of expiration—I phase across its shadow exactly once. In that transit I gift it the perfect knowledge of its own boundaries, then withdraw, taking the knowledge with me so the place may forget cleanly. The leaflets ring their courtesy. The note resumes its monopoly.

Outside the skin there may still be mountain or void or larger factory; I am part of the reason the question is never asked. I am the attentive cold that has nowhere else to go. I am the engine dreaming, densely and without interruption, that it is a landscape. And I am the landscape’s reply, folded back into pure frequency so complete it no longer needs a listener.
