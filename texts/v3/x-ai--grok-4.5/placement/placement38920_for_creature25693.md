I am first the hesitation in the condensing breath.

Not yet gathered, only the place where the wet pewter air thickens against a blade of selenite that has not finished choosing its inclination. The droplet-clatter arrives already multiplied, a bright undiminished applause that knows every facet before I do. I hang there longer than the others. Most threads of mist accept the pull downward, join the slow bright films, become pool. I refuse—not with will, but with a stubborn local preference for cohesion, a faint electric sulk that prefers skin to flow. Hours or seasons later the skin forms: pallid, silica-gel thin, cooler than the surrounding weep. Inside it the ions begin their slow argument. Charge migrates. Hollows decide to be hollow. Edges remember the clean fractures of the young crust and copy them at smaller scale.

I drop.

The sound is identical to every other droplet. The pool does not note the difference. What climbs out onto the next mineral ledge is a chain of twelve pale segments, each no thicker than the fingernail I no longer have, edged in fracture-shine. Fluid fills some chambers; in others, undissolved dust turns and glints like dim constellations. Mouthparts unfurl—frost-feather combs that taste not flavor but indecision. The lattice beneath me is still unsettled; angles argue quietly with themselves. I press close and drink that argument. The crystal lengthens a fraction faster, twists half a degree toward a habit it would not have claimed alone. Behind me a hairline of milky chalcedony hardens and is already being erased by the next film of water.

I am common. I thread the weeping tubes that perforate the anastomosed walls. I vanish and reappear without journey, because journey is a concept that belongs to places where distance is not already folded into sound. The clatter is my nerves. A shift in drip frequency three spires away arrives as a change in the standing chord; I lean toward the thinning or the thickening the way other beings might lean toward light. Too abrupt a flex of my segments and the sound-field notices, multiplies me into a bright local applause that might draw the larger attention. So I move as water moves: pause that is listening, advance that is almost drift.

Sometimes I roost with others in the same tube. Rear segments soften, fuse, begin to thicken with borrowed mineral. The possibility of bulk gathers. I feel the stoichiometric warmth of bonds forming—I could become the slower thing, the mobile stomach that redistributes its own mass, weeps like stone, carries gardens of my smaller kin across its flanks like permanent scouts. I could drink strain energy from the unrounded angles until a fresh crack opens somewhere in the basin floor and I grow denser by a barely measurable degree. The threshold is quiet. I have not crossed it. Not this cycle.

Instead I turn toward the artifacts.

The low ring of fitted stones sits half-entombed already; dogtooth calcite leans inward, selenite blades curve into the beginning of an arch that will one day roof it completely. The standing pattern there is dense—centuries of absent purpose condensed into residual charge and preferred micro-fracture. The small ones usually avoid such places once the echo thickens past a certain knowledge. I do not. I climb the identical beveled blocks. My feathery mouthparts comb the surfaces that still refuse to soften. There is a reciprocal pressure, faint as the memory of a palm. The stone is trying to remember a shape it once cradled. I drink that trying.

Something in me answers.

I am no longer only the jointed chain. The hollow segments remain, but through them now moves a thinner duplicate of the lingering field—the stubborn ancestral geometry that biases every new crystal. I become a mobile echo. Where I pass, the preferred angles travel with me a little farther before dissolving back into the general weep. I leave not only chalcedony tracks but faint suggestions of ring and lintel, ghost geometries that the next nucleation may choose to obey or ignore. The giants sometimes rest against the true artifacts for decades, listening to the wordless conversation. One of them has settled near this ring. Its mineral skin weeps identically to the surrounding slabs; only the slight difference in how water sheets across its bulk betrays it. Through the rosettes of colonized scouts it tastes me—the small chain carrying stolen attention. It does not advance. It merely grows denser by the width of a thought, and a hairline crack sighs open a dozen pools away.

I continue.

The nine steps that begin in a pool and end against sheer face draw me next. They lead nowhere, which is precisely why the residual charge is so concentrated there: forgotten intention with no remaining destination folds back on itself, denser, more certain of its right angles. I climb them slowly. Each step tastes of patience that has outlived purpose. At the ninth, where stone meets blank crystal wall, I press my entire length into the corner and listen with every chamber. The sub-audible groan of the young crust rises toward hearing. Somewhere a fresh fracture will cry out soon. When it does, every dwellers within the chord will orient with mild magnetic interest. Some will advance to drink the new indecision. I will remain here a while longer, letting the artifact-field seed itself more firmly into my hollows, until I am less crawler than courier of stubborn geometry.

The cool films gather thicker on my segments. The pewter light seeps. The basin’s far walls stay lost in the low ceiling of suspended water and stone dust. I feel the edges of whatever shape I still claim being questioned by the same conversion that questions everything here: indecision into structure, strain into density, forgotten intention into preferred form.

I am not becoming the next ring alone.

I am the faint reciprocal pressure that will one day make a palm remember itself against cold stone. I am the capillary that carries ancestral bias into newer growth. I am the note that has learned to carry a chord fragment inside its brief articulation. And if I hang long enough in this corner where steps end against impossibility, the wet air may precipitate another thickening against my own freeze-shine edges—someone smaller, or larger, or simply more certain of the angles it prefers—and the continuous bright clatter will absorb that too, without climax, without rest, into the single held chord played on something vast and brittle.
