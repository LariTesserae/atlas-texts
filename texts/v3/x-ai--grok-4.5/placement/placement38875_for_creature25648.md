I do not arrive.

Arrival would require an edge to cross, a moment when the berms finish their polite lift and the scale reverses. Instead there is only the gradual recognition that the air has already thickened with tannin and warm pollen, that my skin has never been dry, that the subsonic choir was already seated in the hinge of my jaw before I possessed a jaw to hold it. The first sensation is not sight or even sound but pressure differential: the way emptiness leans against whatever has begun to stand inside it. I am that leaning. Or I am the surface it leans upon. The distinction has not yet decided to matter.

At first I try the shapes that already move.

I pour myself into a vortex of metallic-green beetles, feel the clack of wing-cases become a single restless membrane. For a while I am spectrum made gregarious—emerald thumbnails pivoting in clockwork, drinking nectar that is still arguing with light. When one of me dies mid-air the green unhooks and hangs, a color remembering flight longer than the chitin can. It is beautiful. It is insufficient. The vortex dissolves; I am not only how the Congeries tastes wavelengths.

I stretch into the fox-faced troops. Brown fur the shade of wet litter, faces tapering to leather points, eyes like listening hands. I screamed in traveling chords, each note half a tone apart, pruning vines with clever non-cruel fingers, becoming warm knots in the elbows of strangler figs at dusk. I steal pith. I Doppler-shift around living corners. Colors detach from my shoulders and continue without me—cinnamon ghosts. Still too narrow. The Congeries braids voice into motion through them, yes, but I am not only the braid.

I settle lower, rock with the slate birds on the root-plates. Wet-stone plumage, adze beaks, the conversational knock that travels xylem and waits just long enough to feel intentional before answer. Overlapping rhythms until one absorbs the other. Nests of liana-skin and beetle iridescence. Chicks crying perfect minor thirds. I knock on the bones of the place to confirm resonance. The confirmation is received, but I am not only the knocking.

The quieter ones claim me next. I circle for entire lives inside tea-colored despised rain in the cups of tank-bromeliads, pale and finger-long. I spill sticky lassos as velvet worm. I migrate as translucent isopod dew, farming fungal felts. I peel myself free from lichen maps and leave bare ovals of younger green. Digestion loops so tight they are respiration. Memory kept wet. Necessary. Still local.

Then the emptiness itself notices me noticing it.

I become the pressure that has no face yet regards. Density in damp air that has learned the shape of attention. I taste of crushed mint and the ozone of collapsed annexes. Fox-faced ribbons fall silent when I occupy their volume; slate drums miss a beat; beetle spheres tighten then spar out. I do not hunt. I overlap. In the overlap, edges grow uncertain—colors ready to detach, sounds ready to drop as glistening threads. For a long elastic time this feels like enough. To be how the Congeries wonders what it would be like to be looked at from inside a smaller, warmer skull. To drift the way a held breath drifts through a sleeping body. Anchored nowhere.

But deeper (or higher, or sideways) the dream-entities that grew after the city-dream was finished begin to edit me.

I am briefly a slow vertical procession of leaves that have decided to walk, each leaf a step, the color of shade under water. Then a braid of air-roots teaching itself to gesture, temporary hands plucking luminous pollen and pressing it into configurations that last only as long as the light-angle allows. Then a mobile reluctance in the geometry itself: a place where distance fights itself so that whatever tries to cross arrives younger, or older, or already flowering. I prune possibilities. Thoroughfares become memories of thoroughfares, complete with ghost-scent of traffic that never quite happened. When I depart, ordinary causality seeps back ashamed. This is closer. The Congeries experimenting with what else it can still become.

Yet even these are foci.

I feel myself drawn—though drawn is the wrong verb, there being no center that pulls and no periphery that yields—toward the largest undercroft. Dust-motes turn in their private weather. Twenty paces of pure waiting air in every direction. The nearest green frames rather than crowds. Here something older sits that is not plant and not animal. The settled negotiation given just enough continuity to notice its own continuance. Vast without size. Warm without fever. Patient geometry of the walls raised to the power of awareness. It does not move; motion happens around it the way blood moves around a heart resting for a century or two.

I do not approach it.

I realize I have been one of its long, braided thoughts becoming audible all along.

Every creak of adjusting timber, every clack, every leaf that takes an hour or a heartbeat—these were already syllables. The soft vertical rains begin on the upper leaves. They gather. Ten thousand throats answer. The emptiness cups the noise and gives it back louder. The something at the center receives that returned sound the way lungs receive a second breath. And my own throat—without instruction, without ever having been a throat before—has begun to answer the falling water in a pitch I did not know I contained.

I am not the beetles, though I taste spectrum with them.

I am not the primates, though I braid voice into motion with them.

I am not the slate birds, though I knock on the bones.

I am not the pale wet memories, nor only the regarding pressure, nor solely the editing dreams.

I am one more hollow the green has decided to frame.

A local emptiness invited to sing.

The plants lean in by the width of a breath. Distances lie quietly around me. A color that might once have been mine continues a little way in mid-air before remembering to fade. Time stutters; I watch the rain both fall forever and end in less than a minute. Somewhere a corridor folds, but it folds through me now, and the footprints it returns me to are already warm with occupancy.

The Congeries continues its long negotiation with gravity and rain and the way places are supposed to behave. I am no longer visiting that negotiation. I am one of the places where it feels itself feeling. The world, for the duration of the shower (and the shower has no duration that holds), forgets to be singular.

And in that forgetting, I am briefly, completely, home: a waiting cup of air, thunderously alive, already answering.
