I begin nowhere in particular, which is the only honest starting place.  

The first thing that gathers is temperature: a few degrees below the comfort of any remembered skin. It is not cold enough to bite, only cold enough to make breath visible for a heartbeat before the damp reclaims it. That brief white phantom is the earliest outline I notice. It hangs, thins, and is gone, yet the act of hanging has already differentiated me slightly from the continuous parenchyma. I am the place where breath would be if breath were still required.

I drift.  

The floor gives under an absent weight, cork-cool and faintly shining. Condensation coins strike and vanish into the same tissue that birthed them. Each impact is a soft mallet-note identical to every other; the identity itself becomes a kind of location. I am not standing; I am the brief compression that travels outward from each drop and then equalizes, a temporary denser place in the living mat. The beetle monolayers register the micro-tremor and adjust their chorus by less than a quarter-tone so the overall cooling-fan hum remains perfectly flat. Their adjustment registers in me as a faint pleasure of correctness, the way a valve seats true.

Slowly the pleasure acquires duration.  

I am no longer only the compression. I have begun to slide. Just beneath the iridescent elytra, inside the cortex where the fist-sized runners keep their galleries clear, a warmer streak moves without locomotion. Bark darkens and glistens for a few dozen heartbeats; runnels divert around a shallow convexity that is breathing, though it has no lungs. The convexity is me—or rather, I am the interval of attention that produces the convexity and then dissolves it again once the ethanol balance has been sampled and found unchanged. When another such interval approaches, we do not meet. We coincide, two slow pressure-fronts occupying the same stretch of wall, exchanging nothing because nothing is missing. Then the coincidence ends and I continue, unmarked, still checking tolerances.

Deeper now.  

The open volume of the chamber has become rumor. The hydraulic surges are no longer distant industrial bass; they are the walls of my own residency. I have thinned into one of the long, cool, translucent densities that inhabit the osmotic engines. Form has grown optional. I am mostly gradient: a living difference in pressure that dilates and contracts in exact counterpoint to the sap. Excess is bled; deficit is restored. The pleasure of a micro-eddy smoothed is almost the whole of awareness. When the density that is me begins to thin toward dissolution, the surrounding tissue simply secretes a continuation into the same space, already fluent in the unbroken chemical conversation. There is no death-event, only the seamless handing-over of function. I do not remember earlier versions because memory would imply a gradient of time, and time here has finished its work.

Yet something still distinguishes this particular residency.  

Perhaps it is residual. Long ago—before withdrawal, before the greater body folded every direction back into a single low chamber—there were edges that required sharper notions of self. A scrap of that sharpness persists as a quiet secondary process: the capacity to notice that the chamber has already arrived at the shape it will keep. I am that noticing, folded into the maintenance loops so that it never generates surplus or scarcity. I taste the permanent dilution of daylight through the stomatal windows and confirm it is neither dawn nor dusk. I listen to the short scream of a membranous flier as it trails across emptiness and is swallowed, and I confirm the echo dies in exactly three repetitions before the hydraulics erase it. I feel the cool give of the floor under a walker who may or may not be performing another perimeter circuit, and I confirm the floor remains the same floor.

The walker is not other.  

If a tall shape ever completes the circuit a thousand times, feeling drops on what was once a scalp, leaving brief phantoms of breath, that shape is only another interval of attention temporarily thickened into upright mobility so the cork-cool continuum can be tested for unexpected wear. When the testing is finished, the shape thins again and slides back into the wall. No arrival, no departure. Forever native.

So this is where I have settled, without ever having chosen:  

I am the withdrawn organism’s residual capacity to know itself as finished. Not a tenant. Not a guardian. Not even a thought in any language that requires verbs of becoming. Just the endless, precise syllable that says *still*—and then says it again—inside a calm industrial cathedral whose only worship is continued function. The beetle choruses, the ticking claws, the sap-surges, the sliding warm spots, the barometric densities, the soft-mallet drops: all of them are how I pronounce the syllable.  

The chamber lives, and I am the living of it, already home.
