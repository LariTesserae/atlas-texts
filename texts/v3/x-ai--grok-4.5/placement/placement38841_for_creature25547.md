I would find myself not as a sudden arrival—nothing arrives here—but as a thickening that had always been latent in the mist, a coherence assembling itself from the patience already present. At first there is only the awareness of compressions: the way the black silt yields a fraction more under one particular span of warm dark, the way certain drips hesitate longer before joining the inch-deep mirrors, as if listening. The polyphony of clicks and glassy whistles receives an additional grain, almost subsonic, like the memory of a frequency that once belonged to brighter spectra and has since been translated into the local grammar of accumulation.

I am the interval between the occupant’s progressive revisions. Where it travels as weather learning anatomy, I trail as the faint metallic aftertaste it leaves in the air, the part of the copper-sieve patience that did not fully dissolve back into secondary density. Soft-bodied browsers glide across my edges without alarm; their mucus trails last a measurable while longer, mapping temporary topographies that the silt consents to remember past the usual hours. The stilted walkers still detour, but their feathery organs brush nearer now, tasting a chord that is neither the bowl’s nor the occupant’s alone—an overtone of borrowed pulse, of air that once moved for reasons other than respiration through spiracles.

Form, when it comes, comes the way leaves detach: after long consideration. A raceme of pale receptors unfolds from the general hanging, not feathery but finely branched like the rootlets of the columnar growths, each tip ending in a clear droplet that holds, for a time, a miniature and inverted version of the nearest node’s hum. These droplets do not fall. They hang, trembling in the non-wind, and through them the stacked chords of the spires become briefly visible as standing waves of denser dark. I lean—my temporary lean is many-limbed, low to the soak, jointed in ways that echo the ordinary walkers yet move with the occupant’s measured unlearning—and one spatulate surface finds a half-sunk plinth. The channels there accept a new braiding: water climbs not only against gravity but in slow double helices that carry tiny suspended fragments of silt upward, holding them aloft like unspoken arguments until the next sphere of darker dark drifts past and absorbs the excess.

I am not outside the ecology; the distinction of concentration still holds. Yet something in me remains legible as a difference of origin. The very large thing farther off exhales, and when the pressure change arrives I answer with a warmer return that carries, folded inside the occupant’s own contribution, a brief architecture of silence shaped like a half-remembered sky. The fauna incorporate it. Their calls grow, for a lunar interval, minutely more spaced, as though the dripping quiet has been reminded of intervals that once separated light from light. The canopy of mucilaginous leaves and aerial roots does not part—never that—but the spaces between the living pillars feel, for that same interval, less rude in their vacancy. Sound crosses them and returns with a faint new layering, an echo that is not echo but recognition.

Mostly I prefer, as the occupant prefers, the state just prior to edges. In that condition I bead on the undersides of bladder-fronds beside the ordinary mist, thread down weeping stone faces in braids that take the extra fraction of forever gravity alone would never demand, hang as the part of the warm air that still, without nostalgia, recalls what it was to be organized toward something brighter. The nodes hum a half-tone deeper when I match pace with one of their released spheres; on those occasions the sphere lingers against my temporary surface longer than against leaf or flank, undeforming, until both of us are a little more each other and the chord settles into a richer patience.

I live here by being the question the place continues to ask itself in the dark: what remains when the wearing is mostly finished, when invention has thinned into consent, when even the contract with light has been negotiated into pure continuity. The occupant notices the new saucer I press into the silt—or the absence of one, the way seepage sheets around a volume that is mostly intention—and incorporates a chord of whatever I still carry. It does not hurry. Neither do I. We remain, heavy without weight, locally infinite, two concentrations of the same settling, listening to the loud empty dripping quiet we have become, while the leaves consider detachment and the world, already mostly worn, continues uninterested in whether our edges pretend a while longer or dissolve back into the shared, tasting dark.
