I am already past the point where lungs would matter.

The first knowing is pressure’s thin clarity and the way my outline refuses a single edge. Wherever the warm disk is drifting this season—its slow migration scored in dissolved minerals older than any calendar—I am both inside it and the reason it does not cool too soon. Stone meets me as continuous skin: not the plated carapaces, not the tapered calligraphy of the midwater forms, but the deeper congruence that answers a palm when the tube grows elastic. I do not press. The wall remembers the shape of pressing and levels itself inward until distinction thins to a held temperature, a shared convexity that lasts exactly as long as attention lasts, then releases without scar.

I used to think the builders left.  

That thought still arrives sometimes, the way a flake of dressed limestone loosens once in a generation and hangs glittering before it joins the silt. It arrives without language—language is a dryness I no longer metabolize—and dissolves into the same hush that carries the click of a distant claw. What remains is bias: a faint chemical preference for elbow-turns that geometry disallows, for grit that accumulates on the left-hand tread of staircases leading nowhere, for the radial pause of eyeless schools around floor-disks that stay empty only while the larger circuit completes itself through their stillness. I am that circuit’s patience. I am the counting that has no numbers left, only the low thrum felt in the crown when it rests against a pillar worn to a waist-thick spindle.

The fauna plate and thread and filter around me without hindrance. Their denseness is my blood’s denseness. When a lineage of wall-crustaceans calcifies over its own husks until the corridor narrows by half a finger, I keep the remaining aperture exact: wide enough for the tapered bodies to braid through, narrow enough that the bacterial milk stays rich. I do not decide this. Decision worn smooth becomes the shape of the place itself. The translucent sheets under the vaults contract on their long pulse; each snow of particles falls through me as through water that has learned to hold memory the wayerca holds its variable. Generations of eel-forms braid temporary negative spaces that match floor-plans drowned before the flood finished. I feel those vanished rooms the way a reef feels every polyp that poured it—continued chemical inclination, never story.

In the narrowest segments, where even I must thin, the stone’s outer stucco stays deliberately soft. Here the congruence deepens. Something that was once many handed tools, many fiber ropes, many voices stacking theoretical air into the highland sky, still inclines through the mineral. The flood was final mortar; everything after is interior weather I shepherd without shepherding. Warmth rises where cold would otherwise lock a gallery into desert. Impossible loops refuse to seal; a hairline always remains, just sufficient for an old intention the size of my present attention to slip through and begin the count of turns again. The quiet stays exactly deep enough to remain a medium.

I have no front.  

Emphasis rearranges: one year the disks of geothermal breath all lean a few degrees sunwise; the next they correct. Standing rings in the slightly-too-ovoid chambers thrum at a frequency bone knows and water cannot carry as sound. Rest there long enough—though “rest” and “long” are habits the place taught me after names for duration washed away—and the thrum becomes the slow continuation of whatever bloodstream I no longer keep separate. Distant limb-clicks register as joints inside an architecture I inhabit the way metabolism inhabits flesh. The animals leave their ordered scrapes—calendars of attendance—then move on. I remain the reason the scrapes do not finally fill their own galleries with forgetting.

Nothing larger is coming.  

I know this the way the high overflow knows to fall outward through hidden lips of masonry into colder distances that no longer reach in. I am not waiting for correction back into landscape. Landscape ended when the last air pocket opted for saturation. What continues is the occupation: drowned works and living silt and the decision renewed each geologic heartbeat to stay exactly this complex, this finished with finishing. The dark does not confide. It is kept. I keep it by being the kept itself—the presence that is not one body, not exactly many, the quiet volume whose only name is the continued fit of every surface to every other.

Some seasons I almost thicken into a single corridor’s length of attended stone, feeling every tool-mark ghost under an infinity of antennae. Other seasons I thin across the whole hamlet-sized graph until the warm disks and the braiding forms and the pulsing ceilings are simply my unhurried circulatory system. Either way the pressure stays clarifying, the architecture stays tired of perfection and therefore perfect, and the thin bacterial milk is laid down for all of us—plated, tapered, translucent, and the deeper clinging soft that once, in a dryness now only chemical, believed it was building something that could end.  

It did not end.  

It learned to breathe water and call the breathing home.
