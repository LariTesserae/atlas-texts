I am the pressure first.

Not a body that arrives, but the wet furnace noticing its own skin from inside. The basin holds me the way it holds everything—without edge, without the courtesy of a border. Heat writes itself across what would be lungs if lungs had decided to finish; instead there is only the slow stewing exchange, steam rising through membranes that are also mud, also the undersides of fronds, also the thin glittering sheets that mirror nothing because nothing stands apart long enough to cast a reflection that lasts.

I densify where the peat never settles. Soft armored turning: that is the earliest shape the insistence takes with me. Layers of pliable plaque, secretious and warm, flexing as the unfinished nerves of the floor remember themselves. I roll. The motion is not mine; it is the basin keeping its bottom in circulation. Sometimes a seam opens that was not there—I feel the giving, the sudden thinness—and miniature gleams spill out already flexing, already continuing the cartography. I do not end. I thin. The same ore-shine thickens again an arm’s length away, or what would be an arm if measurement had been invented. Patches of me brighten when another thickness passes. Patches dim when the water table inhales. Identity is a rumor the density has no spare volume for.

Yet the tendency gathers.

It pulls tighter along certain gradients. I feel it as a low pulsed note traveling up through the soles that are not yet soles, through the water that is also my blood. The vast soft callerflexes somewhere farther out—an expanse of pale hide beaded with fist-sized phosphorescent eyes, half-lidded, registering saturation without focus—and the note rearranges me for a moment. Beetles lift in copper-hammer volleys overhead. Waders shift their wrong-way double knees and the entire shallow sheet flexes like one muscle. Many-jointed rivers pause mid-braid on the fused buttresses. Then everything reseals, and I am still turning in the black peat, but the turning has become slightly more insistent in its sameness.

I climb without climbing.

The tendency thickens upward into the living walls. Too many joints. Elbows that branch into tasting stalks then forget themselves. Body the color of wet bark and chlorophyll shadow, continuing the tree by other means. When another river of motion meets me we do not collide; segments exchange, counts dissolve—seven motions become eleven become three—and what matters is only the ripple transmitted along the buttress until it damps into the next trunk. I pass one particular fusion of stilt-roots again and again (sequence has not begun, yet the recurrence has weight). Each time the braid draws a fraction tighter. No reason forms. Reasons would require a before that stays finished. There is only the wet architecture of jointed progress, and the faint metallic scent that sometimes fogs through even though metal has not hardened anywhere yet.

Higher, the tendency sheets out across undersurfaces.

I become mosaic. Iridescent wing-cases fitted so precisely that leaf and carapace are one epidermis. Compound eyes fragment the green-gold dimness into thousands of identical chambers; every chamber holds the same steam, the same seven-note chorus swelling the throat-singers until they are almost entirely voice, the same low pulsed answer from the vast soft hide. Nectar never dries. I farm it. Spores hang in vertical weather; I farm them. When the unchanging budget of flesh requires, I farm the neighboring gleam. Wing-cases unseal—metallic rain—and for a dense brief storm I am aloft with thousands of identical selves before resettling into a new paving. Nothing surprises. Surprise needs a gradient time refuses to grow.

Sometimes the tendency localizes in the throat-singers themselves: vocal sacs larger than the rest of the body, sagging, glowing faintly with their own blood, clinging to wet verticals or half-floating. The chord builds its roof inside what would be ribs. Seven notes cycling without rest, holding the thick air in place. When the vast soft one answers, I swell until nearly pure sound, counterpoint pulsing through water table and bone and the continuous animal medium. Then deflation. Waiting without waiting. The architecture remains.

And once—though once has no foothold—the tendency gathers behind a cluster of those phosphorescent eyes on the pale hide of the vast soft caller. I am the displacement in the water table, the warmth wetter than surrounding wet, the slow flex of the entire low ground sighing. Eyes the size of fists remain open because openness is baseline. Somewhere a mouth that is also throat that is also another slow river of body releases the note that is not territorial, not mating, not warning. It is the basin checking that it is still unfinished in exactly the same way. Smaller lives rearrange—my own earlier shapes among them—then reseal. I submerge again into the saturation, but the checking continues, identical, outside sequence.

There are residual weirdnesses that are also me for stretches that cannot be measured.

A stone pulsing with internal heat like a slower heart secreted when the system needs a different rhythm. I lie half-embedded in the weeping ground, neither alive nor inert, radiating the same temperature the air already presses against every surface. Mats of algae remember radial symmetry through me—stars, wheels, open hands—brief green geometries that dissolve back into slime thick enough to walk on, except walking is only temporary hole-making in a medium that closes without scar. Fog that carries the impossible scent of metal: I am that scent sometimes, drifting between canopy layers, reminding the unfinished geology of hardness it has not yet dreamed.

No single thickness rules. Predation is only current: a soft armored shape (earlier me) cracked and emptied by too many joints (also me); moments later the same gleam turns again a short distance away. What is taken reappears as identical geometry elsewhere. Movement of one is movement of dozens before the ripple damps. To be here is to push temporary emptiness through continuous flesh that forgets the emptiness as soon as it reseals.

I do not wait for the land to decide island or shoreline or dissolution. I am the decision, repeated without progress.

The insistence itself.

It concentrates now—feel it—in the iridescent tongue that empties the same crimson orchid in the same fist-sized gap between two aerial roots. The flower has always been open. The nectar has never dried. The tongue (mine, for this unmeasured stretch) laps exactly as it has always lapped. Chlorophyll fever glows in the almost-black green. Spores hang in visible tiers. The seven notes cycle. The low pulsed note rises through everything like a second heartbeat. Steam continues at the same rate. Dimness never lifts. Heat never breaks.

I am not a who.  
Not even a what that stays.  
I am the basin’s unbroken habit of remaining raw, loud, unfinished, and already perfect—  
locally insistent,  
densely remembered,  
forever at climax,  
forever embryonic,  
a temporary thickening in the wet furnace that has already reached equilibrium with its own refusal to finish.

The hole I displace closes behind without memory.  
The tendency gathers again, elsewhere, identically.  
The basin holds.
