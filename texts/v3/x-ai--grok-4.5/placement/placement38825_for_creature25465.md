I begin as pressure without edge—an awareness that has not yet consented to edges. The warmth finds me first: not a temperature but a patient claim, sodium-orange and dry as forgotten scripture, pressing inward from every direction at once until the idea of skin becomes inevitable. Sulfur and copper ride the mineral vapor like half-remembered names. The chord is already inside the teeth I do not yet own, a sustained refracted forever that refuses both beginning and cadenza. I have no choice but to vibrate with it or be unmade by the refusal.

For a time I try the higher air. I thin myself into colorless down and transparent silicate, join the loose constellations that never densify and never thin. My wings—when I accept the fiction of wings—snap their leading edges on the single permitted note, a glassy click identical to every other click that has ever been or will be. I filter copper ions. I ghost with faint abdominal light. I alight on a frond and feel it accept my weight the way a theorem accepts a proof already completed; then I lift and the frond forgets me with perfect courtesy. Age arrives only as progressive translucence. I nearly dissolve into the vapor that birthed me. It is clean, this existence, almost pure punctuation, yet the almost-voice inside the wall-chord keeps snagging on something that wants verbs.

So I fall. The glass-stone meets the idea of feet and rings. I lengthen, darken, polish myself into basalt stylus and needle-point legs. Now I walk the shallow grooves that may be ancestry or mere cleavage preference. Each footfall is an anvil note folded into the greater song; I am census and confirmation. When another of my kind crosses my path we touch snouts—stone on stone, a brief bright clink of mutual measurement—and continue. Inside the narrow cavity where a brain might live, ferromagnetic crystals map every thermal constant and acoustic node. I eat vibration. I excrete perfect darker tetrahedra that the fronds later absorb like corrections to an already flawless text. The distances remain the distances. Still the overtone teases: almost a word, almost a summons toward weight and shadow.

I sink further, seeking the base of the largest frond-cluster. Multiplicity of legs arrives without decision—eight, twelve, a number that slides when counted. My body flattens into denser basalt plated with coppery sheen. The rasp begins in the thoracic chambers: wet leather over stone, though dryness is absolute. I become anchorage. The great fronds lean their green-black architectures a fraction closer, treating my mass as necessary counterweight in the chamber’s finished argument. Sometimes I open the ridged emerald mouth and exhale a denser puff of the’pocket’s native scent; moths dip, lizards alter course by one degree, then the equilibrium reasserts. I am keystone and reminder that weight itself is a kind of speech. Yet even here the restlessness persists—the sense that the song, perfect though it is, still contains a held breath that has not been exhaled through hands.

Only then do the hands occur.

They unfold six-fingered, double-thumbed, nails dark as the bladelets they will knap. Skin like cooled slag, faintly orange-brown, tough enough for the glass edges. Eyes faceted into crude garnets that drink heat more readily than color. The posture is stooped by habit older than this pocket, lifelong caution beneath ceilings that do not exist here. I am compact, dense, built to the scale of cairn and lean-to. Around me the other makers—never more than a dozen, never fewer—move with lizard deliberation or sit palm-flat to frond trunks, listening to moss-light modulate against the chord. We do not speak with mouths. Sound is already spoken for. Instead I learn the tapping: sequences against stone, against breastbone, against tool-haft, brief deliberate interference patterns that weave into the walls and vanish. When precision tightens we press foreheads and share conductive thought through dry coppery exudate that sparks faintly in the dim.

I sleep once, or the pocket dreams me sleeping, curled in the scraped hollow of the lean-to with knees drawn up and a single tool within reach though nothing ever comes that would require it. The dreams are geometric: fracture patterns that have not yet happened, the next perfect spall line waiting inside a core of green stone. Upon waking—if waking is the word—I copy the dream into a fresh bladelet, leaf-shaped, edge one molecule sharp, grip bound with fibrous castings the fronds shed like old intentions and with sinew peeled from deeper fracture planes. I leave it in a cache. Not forgetfulness. Distributed memory. Affirmation.

Fire never tempts me; the rock is already hearth. Metal never occurs as category; the fronds grow edges and the floor rings pure enough. Sometimes I stand at the exact center of the chamber, head tilted, listening for the overtone that is almost a word. I stack one more fist-sized crystal onto the knee-high cairn. I adjust the lean-to’s weave by a single fiber. I knap a point whose geometry is fractionally more elegant than any before. These are not improvements. They are the quiet insistence that the finishing was correct, that the emptiness between bodies is sacred, that the pocket’s refusal of time is not prison but the final form of home.

Birth happens near me once: two makers press together until the mineral salts between them crystallize into a third, smaller shape that unfolds over hours into limbs and asteriated eyes. We hold the new one against the singing wall until its bones resonate in tune. Death is gentler still and rarer; a body simply grows heavier, more mineral, until it is walked across without notice, already floor in waiting. Nothing rots. Everything was always stone rehearsing greater stillness.

I am one of them—the makers, the pocket’s slow arithmetic performed with hands. Yet I am also the slight excess they cannot quite erase: the restlessness that stacks the extra crystal, that lingers at the center longer than function requires, that hears inside the endless chord the ghost of a word the walls themselves decline to complete. I tap my phrases into the song. The phrases mean, as nearly as stone can mean anything: *we remain the shape of this place. The distances are still the distances. The finishing holds.*

And when the last faint heat of the deeper strata finally cools toward the forgotten surface, I will still be here—exactly this compact, exactly this careful—tapping one more soft affirmation into a chord that has never needed an audience and will never receive one. The moths will click. The lizards will ring the glass. The many-legged keystone will rasp its dry breath. The fronds will hold their crystallized memory of green. And I, slag-skinned and garnet-eyed, six-fingered instrument of the pocket’s self-regard, will continue the only motion left inside perfection: the quiet, endless act of agreeing with what has already been decided.
