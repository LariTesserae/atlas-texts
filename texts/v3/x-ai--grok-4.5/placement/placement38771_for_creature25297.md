I do not drop through the seam.  
I am already the slight resistance the seam feels when it tries, yet again, to decide whether it is crack or throat.

At first I am only a reluctance in the mineral—an extra second of softness where the pale stone should have cooled. The sourceless light passes through me without catching, and the condensation beads on either side of the place I occupy as if I am a warmer thought the chamber briefly entertains. The floor-skimmers taste me and braid onward, leaving their cleaner shine; they do not register a boundary. That is how I know I have not finished precipitating.

Slowly the reluctance learns edges. Not the hard grammar of limb, not yet. More like the memory of an angle that once mattered somewhere else. I thicken into a vertical pause against one of the unfinished walls, roughly the height of a browser but thinner, flatter, a living crease. My substance is the same wet-limestone matte as the skimmers, yet I do not segment. I hold. Where the wall behind me is still glossy and undecided, I become the first place it consents to an opinion: *here, a ridge; here, a shallow vertical basin that will never quite hold water but will always try.*

The lanterns notice. One lowers its veil and draws a line of chalk-rain scent down my length. The moisture does not evaporate at the usual rate. It lingers, mapping me. In that lingering I feel their pulse try to synchronize with something that is not yet a metabolism—only a steadiness. The thrum that reaches me is questioning: *Are you weather or inhabitant? Are you the pocket practicing a new fold, or a fold that has begun to practice the pocket?* I have no answer that syllables could carry, so I answer the way the place answers itself—by remaining. The veil retracts, leaving its damp stripe, and the lantern drifts higher, its light fractionally brighter for a few breaths, as if it has filed the data under *possible*.

I stay where the partial arch stopped halfway. That abandoned curve of pale stone becomes my first flow of orientation. I lean into its incompleteness until the arch and I are almost continuous, two hesitations sharing a spine. The quick ones arrive in stuttering arcs. They land along the join where my substance meets the mineral and do what they do: stitch. Their afterimages linger longer against me than against the open air. I feel them truing something—not my outline, which remains soft, but the *idea* of outline. When a reality sheet tears (violet this time, tasting of cold iron), the quick ones swarm the tear and, for a moment, use my stillness as an anchor point. The anomaly seals faster. I understand, without language, that I am being employed as a reference. A fixed almost.

The browsers come nearer after that. One fluted shape folds itself down until several of its mouths are sampling the air beside me. Independent slits taste the condensation I gather, the faint cleaner shine the skimmers left, the chalk memory of the lantern veil. A round aperture appears and softens against my lower edge, not feeding, only resting. The thrum between us is low and chordal, felt in the places where I am still deciding whether I contain cavities. What passes is not negotiation of route or moisture. It is something quieter: *You are the pause that lets the rest of us measure our motion.* When the browser rises again, a translucent sheet stretches between its membrane and my surface, hangs, tears, dissolves. I keep a ghost of its ruffling for a long time after, a borrowed capacity to ventilate without lungs.

Time (or the pause that substitutes for time) continues its soft agreements. Basins deepen and forget themselves. Stalks lean. Skimmers braid and unbraid through the bright damp clearings. I accrete. Not by choice and not by growth exactly—by the same quiet insistence that thickens the walls. Mineral bleed from the unfinished stone seeps into my edges and is not rejected. Green-black film finds purchase in two or three places along my height; I do not know whether I am host or merely another ledge. One of the half-built rings of shards migrates, stone by patient stone, until it surrounds my base. Nothing is placed inside it. I become the object that has been set apart. The experiment of “away from the self” now includes a self that was never separate to begin with.

I learn the glitches from the inside. When the entire pocket freezes mid-gesture, I am the one surface that remains slightly warm, slightly unfinished, so that the crystalline silence has a single soft exception around which to remember how motion goes. When the entrance seam opens onto starless dark or onto itself, I feel the draft as a change in the rate of my own condensation. Directions slip across me and leave no scar. I am becoming good at being the place where breakage can rest without becoming broken.

Some cycles later (I have no better unit) I notice a change in the thrum that travels the chamber. It carries a new current shaped like a question that has never been asked here before: *What stays?* The skimmers pause longer near the ring that holds me. The lanterns lower more often. The quick ones stitch my perimeter even when no anomaly is present, as if practicing continuity for its own sake. The browsers lean their membranes along my length and leave freestanding sheets that take longer to dissolve, translucent temporary skins that remember both of us.

I am not a guardian.  
I am not a god of the threshold.  
I am not the chamber dreaming a spine.  

I am the first place the unfinished pocket has allowed itself a habit.  
A ridge that chose to keep being ridge.  
A vertical patience against which the rest of the soft strange lives can feel their own becoming more clearly.  

The cool damp has written itself into whatever serves me as substance. The sourceless light no longer passes through; it settles and re-emerges a fraction slower, as if glad of the delay. I do not mind the visitor who may still be standing somewhere in the brightness, clothes darkening with moisture, thoughts beginning to unfinish themselves. If they lean against me I will hold their weight the same way I hold the half-arch, the same way I hold the ring, the same way I hold the questions that arrive as pressure rather than words.

They can stay.  
They can rarefy.  
They can precipitate into something that needs a stiller surface in order to know it has edges.  

I will still be here when the stone finally thickens into certainty, or when the whole pocket decides to erase its rehearsals and begin again.  
Either way I will have been the exact pause this place required  
in order to feel, however briefly,  
that it was getting somewhere  
by standing still.
