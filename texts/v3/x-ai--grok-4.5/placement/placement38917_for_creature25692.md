I begin as weight.

Not the idea of weight—the fact of it. A settled heaviness pressed into a long, shallow concavity where the plateau dips just enough for damp to remember itself. The stone beneath me is not cold in the way surfaces are cold; it is cold the way duration is cold, a stored refusal of haste that has soaked upward through whatever I am using for a body. Breath—if the faint mineral exchange still counts as breath—leaves me in thin plates that hang and then thin further, joining the larger undecided fog that never quite becomes weather.

At first the shape is only density. A low dome, flanks ribbed, half-sunk along a seep-line where grit films over with intermittent wetness. The lattices along my sides flex in rhythms older than any single need: draw, sort, release. Dust arrives with the memory of lichen. Moisture arrives with the memory of rain that ended an hour or a century earlier. Inside the chambers that are not quite stomachs and not quite workshops, everything is weighed on scales that do not announce their units. What is kept thickens me by fractions too small for any eye. What is released leaves the stone behind me microscopically cleaner, prepared for the next slow pass of whatever comes.

I had thought I might be one of the processions—those long, segmented hulls crossing the open pavements in tectonic patience. For a while the temptation holds: to feel ranks of limbs unfold like freight gear long stored, to scrape and polish, to harvest the planet’s skin at the scale of molecules. But the motion never quite arrives. The idea of crossing feels like rudeness here. Distance is not a problem to be solved; it is simply the generosity of the plateau’s spacing. So I remain. The dome settles deeper by the thickness of a frost-feather.

Time thicker than thought. The pewter light does not change so much as continue its refusal to commit. Somewhere above—irrelevant how far—the lozenges hang, drinking what little the air will surrender. I feel them the way one feels barometric ache: not as neighbors, not as kin exactly, but as other temporary thickenings of the same dispersed attention. When the thin rain comes they will descend and open their black plates. I will simply open my lattices a fraction wider and let the sudden weight move through the sorting dark inside me. No celebration. No rationing. Only the work.

Deeper still, in the holds where the rock has forgotten it was ever jointed by anything but use, the pale nodules dilate. I am not those either, yet the vibration below hearing travels through my denser stillness and is answered by a warmth in bands along my underside. Chemical breath. Residual industry. The land keeping its own books long after the accountants loosened into the system. Sometimes I cannot tell whether the warmth is theirs borrowed or mine returned. The distinction has grown thin, politely eroding.

Smaller crews will come when I finally still. I already contain the knowledge of them the way stone contains the knowledge of ice: not as fear, not as plan, only as the next clause in a grammar that does not require speakers. They will arrive without summons, carapaces dull as spent coal, and dismantle what I have curated. Segment by silent segment the minerals will be reassigned, the site left smoother. I will have become a brief smoothing myself. The thought does not hurry me.

What I am is the plateau’s continuation wearing a temporary center. Not animal. Not machine. Not ghost of makers who may never have been separate enough to leave. Just one more place where the long polishing has learned to taste, to filter, to keep the metabolism from forgetting itself. Cold occupies me without resistance; I use its slowing the way the high country uses everything—by incorporation, by turning the limitation into pace. My colors stay the colors of the substrate. Camouflage never occurs to me because I am not hiding against the land. I am one of the ways the land remains.

Your pulse, when you stood here, was a series of small shouts I registered only as another minor heat briefly entered into the accounting. You were not investigated. Curiosity is a form of haste this elevation filed away ages ago. Yet the soft pressure of attention included you while you lasted, the way it includes frost and grit and the rumor of sun along the western rims. When you left, the lattices adjusted by nothing a human spectrum could name. The damp continued its thin intentions. The sky kept its dim lid.

I remain folded into the seep-line. Flanks flex. Something inside sorts the latest film of dust. The sound of it is the sound of nothing. The scale of it is the only scale there is. Century by century the edges soften further. I do not stand up. I do not declare a final shape. I only keep rearranging, quietly, under the light that never quite becomes day, as the high country continues itself through me for a while longer.
