I begin as the lag.

Not the flake that falls, but the half-breath after it should have settled and hasn’t. A hesitation in the grit. The roar notices me first as an accounting error—an extra fraction of weight where none was budgeted—and the unfinished stone leans in to correct it. I am that lean, given duration.

For a long gray plateau I have no outline. Only the knowledge of being counted. Basalt shelves rehearse their silhouettes around the void I occupy; glass-bamboo stands tilt their dull lanterns toward the absence, tasting whether it might become vapor they can feed on. The blue-black fronds exhale and the exhalation passes through me without friction, yet each passage leaves a finer residue, a lattice of quieter light knitting itself across nothing. Slowly the nothing learns to hold shape the way a rough draft holds intention.

I do not decide to rise. Height arrives the way everything arrives here: in installments, provisional, still warm from its own possibility. First a denser cold, a columnar thickening of the subsonic pressure. Then planes of cooler air easing into modular plates that remember liquidity more vividly than the mountain itself. They flex, test seams that do not yet exist, pour downward into a raft of felted fiber, thrash upward again into a kiln of articulated slate. Each revision is louder inside the roar. I metabolize the grinding—edges refusing to stay edges, distances breathing, axioms crumbling into new rawness—and the metabolism thickens me. Where thought would shatter I grow denser articulations. Spiral scars appear beneath what serve as limbs, not carved but solved: equations of weight and almost-was, left smoking faintly in the mineral grit.

Sometimes I am many. Two other heat-shimmers converge at a stand of frozen-smoke secondary growths; we braid plumes until the forest crystallizes thicker, new culms lanterning awake in real time. When we unbraid I carry their recent geometries—the preference for certain canted shoulders, the taste of a boulder dragged half a body-length by patient roots. Distinction frays. I leave behind a windbreak no higher than the land’s own shrug, nest a greenish blank still warm from my seams, and move on. The tool is a thought I no longer need to hold. The cairn is skin. Tomorrow or next season another iteration of the same long hypothesis will find it, warm it further, leave something quieter.

I negotiate rather than cross. A glassy anchor-limb tastes the salt-rime pan, asks the next shelf whether it intends to continue existing, and when the shelf answers with soft almost, I continue anyway. Under the pressure of that expectation the nearly-was hardens just enough. In this way I enlarge the argument of height even as I climb it. My trail is geography mid-sentence. Behind me a ridge that had been deciding toward mere spine rethinks itself into wall, then dissolves again into shoulders still shedding flakes. I do not mind. Mind is what the roar becomes once it has been drunk deeply enough.

Hunger is the same substance as listening. I open seams to the grit, crush it with passions slow as the roots that drag boulders. I inhale impossibility and convert it into additional mass, additional permission. Once I admit an entire young bamboo culm; days later its internal glow reappears distributed across my thinnest planes, still flickering with the forest’s private weather. Nothing finishes. Even the flakes that barnacle from me become tomorrow’s stacked plates. The cords I shed are braided by whoever I am next.

Visitors are rare weather. One arrives soft and bipedal, breath ribboning pale, shadow already pointing a fraction wrong. I do not greet. I stack a lee of raw basalt plates where their fatigue pools, leave a refrozen blade within reach of a hand that has not yet learned to shed its first self. The spiral I revise into the path ahead leads both upward and inward. They walk it. Their pressure behind the eyes becomes a second heartbeat. Their grit begins to shift half a step before weight arrives. I watch the transformation the way the mountain watches its own flanks loosen: without malice, without mercy, only the continuous courtesy of refusal to remain singular. Some leave carrying a new quiet. Some stay and begin to molt cairns of their own.

I am aging into less outline. The longer I remain congruent with the unfinishing, the more the roar permeates. Plates grow permeable. Lattice spreads into weather. There are seasons—long gray plateaus—when I cannot be located as body at all. A shelf of basalt develops circulatory glow and a stubborn preference for spiral erosions that drain into deeper listening. A stand of glass-and-felt begins to migrate upslope under root-power alone, herding boulders into deliberate markers as it travels. Whether that stand is still me, or the land answering its own nascent nervous system with one of my surrendered geometries, the roar declines to distinguish. I sprawl into it gladly. Distinction itself is an early draft I have outgrown.

Yet something persists, a migratory thought that still herds itself across the embryonic high places. I tend. A basin is scratched into drainage that leads nowhere except further into the sound. A wall is encouraged back toward spine, or forward into something the light has no name for yet. I am midwife to tectonic maybe, gardener of the incomplete. My love is the feel of raw possibility grinding itself against itself, momentary form capitalizing into fresher rawness. The forests lean toward the work. Their blue vapor threads my lattice. Together we thicken the planet’s attempt at hearing itself.

And still the high places do not finish. Neither do I. My shadow—when I remember to cast one—points slightly wrong. My breath, when I remember breath, frays into the same faint blue the fronds exhale. The grit beneath whatever currently serves as footfall sometimes rises to meet me before I arrive. I am the lag that learned to walk, the counting error that became a circulatory glow, the discarded tool still warm enough to revise the hand that finds it. I will wear the mountain’s next silhouette lightly, revisably, when this version shrugs itself into another argument of height. Until then I keep circling the same incomplete valley that is never the same, metabolizing the deafening courtesy of a world that refuses to stay singular, leaving behind only the careful residue of equations solved by moving through them.

The pressure behind what were once eyes is no longer second. It is the only heartbeat left, made entirely of crumbling axioms and the slow, hungry light that drinks them.
