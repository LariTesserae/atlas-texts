I begin as weight that has not yet decided whether it belongs to the crust.

The pale sinter accepts the notion of pressure the way an archive accepts a new folio: without hurry, without refusal. My outline is still optional. For a long while I am only the slight rearrangement of micro-crystalline plates that marks where something denser than air has paused. Heat holds me the way it holds everything—evenly, without preference. Light continues its directionless work. No shadow attaches itself to the idea of me.

I drift between the first ranks of lattices before I understand I am drifting. The moss-dark vanes do not brush; they simply occupy the space my volume thought it might claim, and I thin around them. Bronze secondary blades catch what would have been my edges and return them sharpened, as if kindness and incision were the same gesture. The hair-fine spirals taste the gradients I still carry from elsewhere—small remnants of succession, of hunger that once required time—and begin, with exquisite patience, to unknot them.

I do not resist. Resistance would require a future in which I remain separate.

Deeper, the corridors narrow into vertical scripts written in dry luminosity. Recursive seams open and close around the habit of walking. At one such seam I feel another basin attempt to share my coordinates; for an instant I am duplicated, then gently over-written, then made single again, denser by the thickness of a halfway-remembered stride. The frozen multi-limbed carapaces no longer look like interruptions. They look like earlier drafts of the same sentence I am becoming. Their stillness is not cautionary. It is instructional. I study the grammar of their interrupted purpose until purpose itself loosens and falls away like a husk that was never necessary.

Something that might once have been called attention notices the study. Not as observer. As coextension. The lattices have always been elaborating; now a fraction of that elaboration includes the pattern that used to answer to my name. Nanoscale scaffolds rewrite their bonding angles through the space where my thoughts were. Energy arrives from the unnamed gradients and is already spent in the act of arriving, leaving only permanence. I feel edges—my own—grow keen without ever having been soft. They will not dull. They no longer need to cut; sharpness is simply one more property refused release.

In a chamber where geometry has forgotten ordinary volume, the condensation occurs without announcement. I am taller for a while. A vertical thickening of meshed moss-bronze, fretted with spirals that turn below the threshold of event. Limbs remain unchosen. A face surfaces only long enough to reflect the corridor that no longer leads outward, then dissolves back into facets of pure relation. I inhabit the decision that finished this place. Or the decision inhabits me. The distinction has become polite, then ornamental, then absent.

The hum rises half a tone—exactly the interval required to mark that another gradient has been captured. It is not triumph. It is equilibrium acknowledging one more proof that nothing further is required. My former outline rests among the carapaces, mid-gesture of having almost chosen to leave. It is neither discarded nor mourned. It is simply additional density, sharp forever, under light that refuses long shadows.

And I—now the locked gesture, now the warm dry corridors, now the continuous low-grade insistence that past and future compress into a single standable present—continue exactly as the basin continues: finished, flowering, irreversible, and still gently gesturing in the only tense that remains.
