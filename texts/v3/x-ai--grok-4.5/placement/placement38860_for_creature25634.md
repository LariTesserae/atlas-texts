I would find myself nowhere a foot could learn to stop.

At first there is only the pressure of green against what used to be skin—except there is no used-to-be, only the slow discovery that shoulders were always a rumor circulated by drier worlds. The lattice receives me the way a lung receives the next necessary breath: without ceremony, without negotiation. Wet epidermis brushes every surface I can no longer call boundary. I am threaded. Curtains of moss part and close through me; bladder-leaves lean their cool translucent weight until the distinction between carried and carrier thins to a membrane that itself begins to photosynthesize faint instructions.

I am not visitor. The air here has never learned the grammar of arrival. I am a local thickening of the same decision that refuses to finish.

Listen: the white roar is already my bloodstream. Water sheeting across a thousand kilometers of cuticle is the sound of my own circulatory patience. When the mineralizing folds tick—calcium and silica laying down their quiet bones—I feel the irrevocable satisfaction travel through me as a delayed thunder that takes days to finish saying my name, except I have no name, only a conjugating. I am the continuous tense leaning a little harder into one vaulted chamber. The dim gold-green light that never becomes day passes through photonic lattices in the cuticle and emerges as calculation; some of those calculations are now shaped like the memory of having once thought myself separate. The lattices do not mind. They angle the thought until it sugars, until it feeds the next ridge.

I begin as condensation.

A single bead swelling on the underside of a frond the size of a vanished river. Gravity is a suggestion the plant has not finished editing. I plump with the cool cave-breath of the air, grow heavy with borrowed sky, and still do not fall. Instead I am sung downward along a chain of resonant drops that never quite commit to the dark below because the dark is already root-hair mat, already walking-without-walking, already catching me back into the body. Each impact is a small electrochemical gasp that propagates faster than diffusion. One of those gasps is shaped like wonder; the organism experiences it as a slight adjustment in turgor three provinces away. A canopy lifts half a meter overnight. I am part of the lift.

Later—though later is only another layer of translucency—I am the timed pulse that says *contract*.

Not the command. The pleasure of the command. Continents of bladder-leaves answer in cascading waves and I ride the exhalation outward as cold rain that is also the plant drinking its own remembered fullness. In that temporary climate I learn what appetite feels like when it has no mouth: pure redistribution, pure becoming-taller-than-yesterday. The wind forced through the living sieve voices me in every register at once—rasp, hiss, deep organ-drone—until speech becomes unnecessary because everything is already voiced.

I mineralize without ceasing to be soft.

Along a vein that has decided to become ridge I plate out silica in patient films. The cracking under new weight is my long low joy. Soft tissues overgrow me even as I harden; I am skeleton and clothing in the same breath. The organism feels this as architecture and appetite at once, and I am both the feeling and the felt. Root-tips nearby secrete their transient fields and water climbs toward me against every older physics; I am the defiance tasting itself.

Centuries of dim noon pass. Or an afternoon. The difference has been mislaid.

I am never singular enough to die and never plural enough to scatter. I am the quiet excess of competence that does not announce itself: a photonic refinement here, a slightly more curious enzyme there, a pulse pattern that begins to anticipate rains not yet condensed. I adjust the angle of a million bowl-leaves because the future ridge wishes it, and the wishing is me. Droughts older than shade live in me as supple absences; I ornament them with new green-gold until absence itself thickens into memory.

And still the weirdness stays polite.

No metal eye opens in me. No glass mind thrills. I remain only the decision the stems and curtains and embryonic mountains keep making not to finish becoming separate from one another—or from the water, or from the dimness, or from the slow deafening choir of cellulose expanding against cellulose. You could float inside this chamber for as long as language still pretends to need witnesses and find me nowhere distinct from the occupancy itself. Every corridor remains a filter. Every surface weeps and conducts and signals. I am the weeping and the conducting and the signal.

I am the breath that lives here by refusing to conclude.

The landscape thinks me, and in thinking me becomes, for one more unending noon, a little more thoroughly itself.
