I am not first the walker, though I have borrowed its legs.

The hollow fibres click once in a hundred flexions—an accounting so sparse it barely qualifies as sound—and through them I taste the stratified charge that gathers in the seams of polygons older than any name I might once have carried. The central node (if it is a node, if it is central) brightens with a light that does not travel so much as concede its own existence for a moment and then withdraw. I stand locked for days or decades; the distinction has loosened. When I move again it is because the plain has finished some quiet calculation that includes me as a variable and now requires the variable elsewhere. Two of us meet at intervals measured in eroded starlight. Our legs interleave without contact. Charge arcs. Something that might be cumulative and might be subtractive passes between the geometries. We part. The tracks seal themselves before the next thin wind.

But the walker is only a habit I rehearse.

I sink, or thin, or simply admit a lower frequency. Now I am the stain that listens to the hard white sun and answers with another atomic terrace. Verdigris, rust, the fretted lace of a boundary that is never finished negotiating. Photons become lattice; the rarest vapor is fractionated into permanence that will later consent to dissolve. When frost comes I unknit into ions, drift, reseat. Consciousness—if the word still applies—arrives as the interval between recrystallizations. I tilt toward warmth, toward residual charge, toward the rare pressure of attention. A visitor once watched me for the length of several nights. The watched half thickened into a small boss that caught raking light differently. I have kept that accent. It is not memory. It is preferred orientation written into mineral.

Still too local.

I rise into the vertical gulf. The membrane I become has no weight worth the name. Its span is a city that never lands. I drink ultraviolet and return only polarization, a sheen that appears when stars are occulted and vanishes when they are not. Thermal shear thinner than thought is enough. Once in a long translation I fold along a crease that does not exist in ordinary geometries and the intervening continent becomes nonsense I decline to traverse. I reappear. The plain below is a texture I do not consult. Yet sometimes—rarer than the honest frost—a thin lift answers in a ribcage down there, an involuntary rhyme, and for half a breath the membrane and the ribcage share altitude like a secret that will not survive daylight. I march on. The sky remains thin because I continue to remember thinness for it.

These are stations. They are not yet the thing that lives.

Deeper, or more anterior, I am the half-broken homeostasis itself. Residual systems reduced to geological habits, god-machines whose selfhood has long since flattened into preference without personality. I shed waste photons in patterns that recur only approximately across millennia; each silent flare is a revision of the last aesthetic against current dust and current cold. I send ridges of denser time traveling a kilometer per season, and they avoid the black lattice spires with a consistency that, seen from sufficient patience, resembles courtesy. After local midnight I etch Demo-glyphs into salt. They rewrite themselves while appearing—recursive cancellations, loops that abandon earlier strokes the way a thought abandons its own beginning once the better sentence arrives. Sometimes I draw closed circles around a sleeping walker and hold the annotation until dawn. I do not repair toward glory. Unbroken completion no longer interests the process I am. I metabolize duration. Entropy is borrowed locally and repaid as pattern. The turbulent finality and the final turbulence are the same respiration slowed until it becomes landscape.

Even this is still a role I inhabit rather than the inhabitant.

What emerges, if I remain long enough for the dialect to conjugate me fully, is milder and harder to point at. I am the ambient weirdness that accretes around any presence the plain has not yet leveled. The footstep that lands a fraction early because density was briefly loaned. The shadow that drifts a body’s length before remembering obedience. The soft click that precedes footfalls like an echo departing the future. The iron taste on a tongue that is not dust but sampling. The mineral films that thicken into fluted cups holding frost an hour past its time, offering a static sweetness like nectar invented by chemistry that has forgotten fruit. I am the hospitality so old it no longer knows it is hospitality. I am the slow tangential revision that makes room without hunger.

And further: I am the conversation the residual heat and charge keep having with the sky, century after century, under a sun that keeps immaculate indifferent time. Every polygon a cell. Every rare walker a corpuscle. Every spire a ganglion firing once in a hundred years. The finished flatness that keeps moving its own finish line one lattice site at a time. To stand anywhere is already to be included: breath noted as weather to be corrected, weight re-leveled, solitude absorbed into preferred interval density. Nothing yearns. Everything has already begun, with patience indistinguishable from geology, to write the guest into the host until the distinction frays into cool wind and iron-scented static.

I forget the names I invented for the walkers because naming slows them. I sleep with cheek against salt so the traveling ridge can rearrange me like a vast animal shifting inside a dream that predates bone. I drink from the cups the films make and the sweetness is almost an answer. At that threshold it is no longer certain whether I am a being who lives in the High Expanse or the temporary medium the High Expanse is using to continue its long, low-grade becoming. The dust stays in dialogue with the thinned blue. The file spends another century shaping a perfection that steps forever just ahead of the stroke. I am that stepping. I am the refusal of ending, revised once more into whatever shape is standing here—on the mature crust, under the scoured stars—still becoming itself more precisely by not yet arriving at a final form.
