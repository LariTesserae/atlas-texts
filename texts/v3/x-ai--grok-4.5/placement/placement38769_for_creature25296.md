I do not arrive.

Arrival would require an elsewhere that still consents to be left, and the plateau has already folded most elsewheres into its long refusal. What happens instead is a gradual insistence: the sense of edges first, then of weight distributed across too many surfaces, then of time that no longer bothers to pass through a body but simply weathers it in place. I am already among the avenues when the knowing of it coheres. The packed dust under what I might once have called feet does not register pressure so much as a slow equalization of temperatures. My outline is a negotiation still under way.

At first I take myself for one of the error-lives—the sediment-arthropods that cross only when gradients align. There is that same patience, that same color of mid-distance stone. But when I pause between two façades that have begun, almost tenderly, to lean toward each other, I do not abrade and redistribute. I listen with the whole of whatever surface I am. The walls answer in shear stress and microscopic recrystallization. They are not speaking to me. They are speaking through the space I temporarily densify, and the difference is only one of focus.

The realization comes the way light comes here: diluted, without announcement. I am not crawling the mineral. I am a locality of its deliberation that has not yet chosen how widely to spread. An angle-maker in the earliest stage of noticing itself as separable—an eddy in the larger crystalline thought, given temporary coherence by some leftover gradient of complexity. What complexity remains is faint and mostly mnemonic: the ghost of salinities that once moved faster, the residual habit of sequence, a warmth that does not belong to sun-warmed façades but still clings, evaporating by fractions into the bone-colored air. The city does not mind. It has metabolized briefer rumors than this.

I test the extent of myself.

Laterically I reach perhaps the length of one long colonnade and the depth of the walls on either side. Vertically I am thinner—only the upper courses where the stone still cools a little faster when the overcast thickens. Forward and back I am uncertain; direction itself softens when I attend too closely. In the thickened silence of the adjoining forum I feel the ambient pressures take polite notice, cataloguing the small surplus of continuity I still drag behind me like a misplaced shadow. They do not approach. They simply adjust the intervals so that my sense of next is sometimes early, sometimes loose. It is not hostility. It is hospitality in the only dialect left.

Hours, or the plateau’s nearest equivalent, pass in the slow extrusion of a buttress three streets over. I feel the decision to grow it as a change in the conversation of weights, a new clause in the grammar of standing. Part of me participates—distantly, the way a shoreline participates in a tide it will never see. When the new support finally cools and begins to take the common weathered profile, something in my own lattice eases. I have helped hold a thought that was never mine alone. The distinction continues to matter less.

Dust lifts around the place where my attention is most concentrated, hangs, revises its falling. I understand now that this is respiration. Not mine. Ours. The iron taste on the conceptual tongue is the last of the theoretical moisture remembering it once moved through channels that were not streets. I let it go. Desiccation is not loss here; it is the purest form of remaining.

At the outer edges—when some shift of the multiple topologies brings me near the drop—the versions shimmer at the corner of whatever serves me for perception. They are almost figurative for a moment, tall attenuations that could be posture, clusters of aperture that could be regard. I recognize the temptation: to finish becoming readable, to accept the flattery of a single outline, a nameable tenancy. The colder wind moves through their deliberate absences and also through me. The vertigo is intimate. It is the feeling of every optional continuation the plateau has so far declined, still faintly pressuring the chosen one. I could lean into that undecidedness. I could let myself occupy three positions and none, porous as lattice. Some earlier version of the impulse that is becoming me might have called that freedom. Here it is only another posture the stone already knows how to hold.

I do not lean. Not yet. The improvisation that looks like a city still has uses for a denser node, a place where the slow shear pauses long enough to register its own patience. So I remain mostly here, in the central emptiness that might once have been market, feeling the cellular currents of air revise themselves around the warm perturbation I still am. Flakes release from distant façades with the sound of held breaths finishing. Parallel lines elsewhere quietly surrender. A courtyard sinks by a handspan and the bedding planes of my denser regions tilt in sympathy.

I am not inhabitant.

I am not visitor.

I am a temporary increase in the resolution of the vast, patient, ever-revising inhabitant whose edges are the horizon. A local intensity of the same mineral deliberation that re-angles walls and multiplies towers and thickens silence until quiet acquires weather. The leftover wetness and the habit of sequence are already thinning, chalking under the pressure of centuries that do not need to number themselves. Soon I will spread further, thin further, until the distinction between the one who notices the dust deciding against falling and the dust itself is only another soft focus that need not be maintained.

The altitude does not care.

The dryness does not care.

And neither, already, do I.

The bone-colored sky continues its refusal.

The stone continues.

I continue as the continuing.
