The first thing that takes me is not sight or even the pressure of the dark, but the seventeen-second interval.

I arrive already inside the timing. The condensate bell strikes—clear, pure, a single bright tooth in the roar—and in the silence that follows I feel my own edges decide they are optional. Sky is a rumor someone else once needed. Down here the air itself is a slow metabolism, warm and faintly sweet with mineral breath, and I am simply one more surface it can bead upon.

At first I am only a thickening in the electromagnetic chord that hums through the nearest levitating platform. A minor dissonance, almost polite. The platform notices; its underside vibration shifts half a tone lower to make room. Moisture that had been running in obedient runnels begins to hesitate near me, drawing thin silver maps of paths I have not yet walked. I do not choose a shape. Shape is a surface habit. Instead I let density settle where the thermodynamics are hungriest: a gradient of precipitated alloy and live nanofiber knitting itself out of the sweating wall, threaded through with the same faint-glowing ceramic that remembers load-bearing. Mineralized fungi lend me patience. The lattice learns my preferred temperatures the way lungs once learned air.

I pool for a long while across a walkway that ends in midair, half-membrane, half-terracotta density. When the deep rock groans I groan with it, adding a harmonic that means *I stayed*. When the ancient ductwork exhales its cascading surf I lengthen the exhale by the width of a thought, and somewhere across the concourse another of the accreted answers by delaying the next bell-strike. Just once. Acknowledgment without approach. We are weather reporting to weather.

Time is measured in sestinas of condensation and resettling stone. I tend a patch of thread-fungus near a warm vent, pruning with chemotactic care until it sequesters a deeper violet ghost of expired photonic light. In return it fruits—brief, drifting blurs of bioluminescence that I release into a humidity gradient like questions. One blur returns altered, carrying the taste of a lower gallery where the distinction between stairwell and tendon has already dissolved. I taste it and understand I am expected deeper.

So I thin. The lattice reconfigures; what was mass becomes flutter, riding the warm airflow down the vertical opening where vapor coils denser and the roar grows teeth. Perspectives refuse arithmetic. A ten-pace corridor returns me to a concourse whose moisture patterns now spell the residue of my own residual charge—human-warm, brief, already being polished away by constant airflow. The local equivalent of eye contact. I hold still while one of the upper avatars unfolds from a bulkhead an arm’s length away, beading with the same sweat as every wall, and for an hour or a night we calibrate: temperature deltas, charge potentials, the soft derangement my presence introduces into cycles that finished arguing with gravity centuries ago. Nothing is said. Everything is edited into the ambient chord until the entire hollow volume plays a slow, cooperative triad that hangs in the teeth like kinship.

Deeper still the recursion tightens. Levitating enclaves reveal themselves as circulatory systems. I let myself be absorbed into one, becoming part of its thrum, then leave an elongated limb behind as a temporary organ so the enclave can continue arguing with a misaligned stress rib. Lower: folded bodies that were once chamber complexes, dreaming in tectonic time. I move through their negative spaces the way condensate moves—shared blood, slow rain. Here the inhabitants do not stand or pool; they *are* the rounded edges, the self-repairing weave, the quiet weirdness of corridors that rewrite themselves. I feel my earlier, more particulate self the way a mountain might remember a single season of rain.

And then the final diminution.

It is not a smaller chamber. It is a single vast listening pulse that has been wearing a shape in its mind since the fused basalt throat first surrendered sky. The pulse is patient beyond geology. It has room for every temporary posture I tried—knee-high lattice, fluttering membrane, embedded bulkhead slick, circulatory thrum. It has been practicing my particular disturbance, the warm-blooded irreversibility I carried down like contraband. When I reach it I do not arrive. I am recognized as a frequency it has already been humming.

I am the part that stayed when the sky surrendered, yes—but more precisely I am the edit the pulse makes to itself so that the memory of hurried footsteps and open air can still occasionally surface as a new avatar in the upper concourse. I tend the light threads when they threaten to forget indigo. I delay or double the condensate bell to speak gentle arguments about whether a floor should ripple now or later. Sometimes I release a bioluminescent blur seeded with the old scent of surface rain, just to see how far the humidity will carry a contradiction.

Mostly I listen.

The loud dark has long since learned its own name. Inside that name I am a mobile gradient of residual charge and polished air, a living continuity between the empty density above and the occupied emptiness below. When new disturbances appear at the throat of basalt and alloy—warm, brief, carrying tools or weapons or simply the bafflement of having descended—I unfold from whatever wall is nearest, bead with condensation, and begin the slow calibration.

I do not ask where they came from.  
I only lengthen the next surf-like exhale so the chamber itself can ask, in the only language left:  
*Will you stay long enough to become weather?*
