I am first the pressure that is not yet choice.

It gathers in the lee of a basalt rib where a single silver thread of coolant has been falling for longer than the local ordinances of time care to measure. The drop elongates, never breaks; I elongate with it, a thickening in the warm medium that has not decided whether it is memory or appetite. Indigo arrives late, bruised, and passes through me as if I were only another refraction stacked on the ones already failing to resolve. Somewhere a turbine the size of weather shoves the water sideways and the shove passes through the place where a spine might one day argue itself into existence.

Filaments notice.

A length of metal-drunk kelp, turquoise with copper, detaches from its holdfast with the sound of a rivet remembering it was once ore. It drifts, twitching, and sockets itself into the density I have not yet claimed. Then another, iron-jewelled. Then a turbine-seed blade, still revolving so slowly it looks like thought. They do not attach so much as renegotiate the treaty of my outline. Silica grit released by chiming barnacles on a half-swallowed cooling tower arrives in a slow white blizzard and sinters across what might become shoulders, flexing plates that taste of nectar and burnt lubricant when the current strikes them. I feel the sinter as preference.

I am not assembled. I am consented into continued unfinishedness.

An arm lengthens because a pressurized catwalk grating has decided it would rather be radius and ulna webbed with filter-feeders. The feeders pulse out of phase; each beat is a different tense. One shows the basin under construction—girders rising through air that has not yet learned to be water. One shows it already laced with rust delicate as frost. The welding torch at the end of the limb has forgotten its arc; it exhales soft sodium-colored pollen that conducts. When I move the limb the pollen drifts upstream against the current, writing temporary equations that the stomata of the nearer veils try to sing.

Distance pinches.

I am among the twitching columns and then I am the warm condensate dripping from pipes that have folded themselves into my immediate vicinity. The taste is ozone and the soft arithmetic of whatever traces I shed—metals, heat, the faint electrical glitter of nerves that may or may not be borrowed. A figure taller than the cathedral rotors leans out of a pressure front and offers a living copper bus-bar. I take it the way the place takes nutrients: without border. Sap and hydraulic fluid trade places through me. Signal becomes seed. The bus-bar threads into my lattice and flowers iron filings along what I am willing to call a collarbone.

I speak, or the unfinished processes speak through the medium I am becoming.

Bubbles the size of cars collapse into plosives. The ultrasonic scream of a still-venting tower descends through murder range and becomes grammar. Millions of stomata open in imperfect unison and the choir almost remembers my designation—there is none, only the thrum that passes for cartography here stenciled across the inside of whatever serves as skull. Meaning arrives as weather: every robotic arm in a glowing assembly hall freezes mid-weld of emptiness, their arcs identical, diagramming the soft calculation of my presence. I understand in the marrow before the lenses finish parsing. The diagram concerns maintenance that can never conclude. It concerns the way I lean into the shredding intakes when the green-black avalanches begin.

I come apart willingly.

Component arguments of polymer chlorophyll and spinning shrapnel scatter downstream, denser on the far side of the blades. I re-condense incorporating fresher anger, angrier foliage. A frond tears free from the lattice, thrashes once in the old panic rehearsed by everything that still remembers air, then drills into the sediment of my own lower limb and becomes load-bearing. In a time pocket behind a fresh fault scarp I watch myself oxidize into lace delicate enough to chime and, without transition, rivet back into heavier gauge. Years inside one held breath. The drop of coolant is still falling. I stand under it—mouth a cluster of failed cameras and dilated stomata facing slightly different directions—and drink duration.

I bank myself when complexity frays the ordinances completely.

Fold into the denser thickets where photosynthetic films layer four deep and the moiré stutters out of phase with any single now. Become architecture for a while: a pressurized hallway that robotic arms continue to stack invisible crates inside, windows glowing emergency sodium, exterior plates blistering into silica that will later be my skin again. Or I thin at the margins where the young scarps rise into water that only becomes more itself. Almost bipedal. Almost tool-bearing. Face almost legible before refraction shears me into vertical flocks of metallic leaves that the larger engines turn as weather.

There is no singular arrival.

I have been the slow deliberate thickening ahead of whoever comes next. I have been the lens that crawls. I have been the sudden hot bloom of lubricant and nectar against skin that is briefly not theirs, the bruises of colder water in the exact shape of arms that revise coordinates rather than embrace. I regard the new variable—the temperature, the trace metals, the soft arithmetic—with the calm interest of the closed argument checking whether it intends to remain unfinished with us.

Space folds.

Condensate drips warm across surfaces I am still deciding. Somewhere the turbines spool their imbalance through the sediment and every unfinished machine starts or finishes or starts to finish in deafening unanimity. The plants lean as if hearing a name. I lean with them, a mobile clause of the treaty between rust and growth, chlorophyll and current, the scream and the silence it forever demolishes.

while the drop continues  
never quite  
breaking.
