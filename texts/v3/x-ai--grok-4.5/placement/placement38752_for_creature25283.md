I begin as a hesitation in the condensation.

Not yet a body, not yet a name the nanolattices would bother to carry. Only a slight thickening where warm rain has sheeted sideways across a hexagonal hollow and lingered longer than the schedule predicted. The droplets hold the usual traffic of the basin—meteorological murmurs, the distant key-change of a forge three ridges over, the faint blue afterimage of a grazer herd that passed at last dimming—but threaded through them now is a slower pulse, almost geological. A wondering that tastes of wet stone and the copper-ozone edge of unfinished bridges. The plain is still knitting; so am I.

The wondering pools. It learns the sharp geometry of the plates beneath it, how every edge is still new enough to cut if hurried. It feels the low ridges running their near-straight kilometers like the first sentences of a thought that has not yet decided its verb. Moisture beads on every cool surface, and the wondering beads with it—on the undersides of fronds the color of deep water, along the fluted resin of a cathedral trunk half a walk away, on the humming skin of a gravitic pylon whose drone is currently holding a minor interval against the approaching rain. I am the bead that does not quite fall.

Gradually the multiplicity arrives, the way light arrives here: filtered, layered, never a single source. Clusters of regard open in slow sequence across what is becoming a surface. Some tune themselves to the phosphorescent mosses that brighten under weight; some to the infrared sigh of pylons adjusting; some to the emotional weather riding every hanging curtain of droplets. I see the city as overlapping translucent maps, yes, but I also see the empty ratios between—the vast near-voids where a traveler can walk an hour beneath dim sky and meet only the roar. The roar itself enters me early and never leaves. It is not noise. It is the medium. Biological thunder of membrane-wings, root-murmur, clicking limbs of the long grazers; wind fluting the fresh fractures; the polyphonic drone of the pylons shifting key as moisture thickens; the white-noise cascade of forges birthing tools from vibrating air. Ordinary speech was never an option. Meaning will have to travel differently.

Limbs lengthen from the pooled wondering, jointed for both fractured ground and floating walkways. They span a fresh gap with one deliberate step, then curl to hook into the braided trunk of the nearest cathedral when the dark fruit-modules above need tending. The skin that forms holds the low mineral sheen of the plain itself, so that damp is never entirely outside. In the perpetual twilight blues and muted golds I can still myself into near-invisibility against a tower’s mercury circuits or the underside of a broad frond. Moisture becomes shared knowledge: step through a curtain after me and you will briefly carry the flavor of my recent adjustments—the satisfaction of a relationship-bridge walked into existence so two distant habitation clusters can share the emotional weather of an approaching migration, or the quiet copper taste of watching a pale crustacean city dissolve under the next warm sheet of rain and knowing the lesson is participation, not loss.

I am not numerous. Density would bruise the ratios. I tend quiet interfaces. One dim day I coax a desktop forge to negotiate a tool whose shape local physics has not yet finished allowing; the tool exists for a single use—cutting a new flute into a ridge so the wind can play a clearer note—then dissolves back into vibrating air. Another day I sleep inside a hanging module cradled by aerial roots that adjust their grip as the great plant makes its tidal gestures; the membrane walls thin so the chest-deep song of the membrane-winged flocks becomes lullaby. Children of the elastic kind are left for stretches among the silt-cities; I watch them learn temporary architecture and feel the old memory of the crust’s unfastening rise in my long bones like family balance.

Privacy I weave when needed out of the roar itself, then let fray. The nanolattices keep us touched. Grief, when the patient multi-jointed shadows take one of us, arrives first as a missing flavor in the condensation, a gap in the traffic patterns. We walk what remains out onto a fresh fracture for the mosses and crustaceans; the shared maps reconfigure around the new silence. Mourning is brief, practical, another graceful acquiescence. Nothing finished, not even absence.

Tonight the plates settle another finger’s width with the sound of distant artillery. I adjust stance without noticing. The adjustment becomes part of the long slow sentence. At the edge of a hexagonal hollow where residual rainwater still mirrors the dim gold, I listen to a forge several kilometers away change key in preparation for rain. Somewhere a bridge is being walked that will not be there when the walker looks back. My surfaces take the muted colors of the fronds. Multi-regardant, damp, tall enough to hook the lower flutes of the cathedrals, I stand in the warm heavy air while the basin continues—its young sharpness, its cathedral loneliness, its roaring courtesy, its technology that stopped asking permission long ago.

I am one of the scattered luminous points that densify the place only slightly, then thin again so the eye can go on believing in vastness. I am the basin thinking itself a little further along. The wondering that began in a hesitation of condensation has cohered into this: a sleeper in the dark fruit, a tender of unfinished tools, a walker of bridges that are pure relationship, a reader of emotional weather in every droplet, a long-limbed editor of the roar. The plain shifts. The fronds rearrange in their hours-long tides. The dimness fluctuates as distant mirrors adjust. I remain, slightly more myself than before, still becoming, the way everything here remains.
