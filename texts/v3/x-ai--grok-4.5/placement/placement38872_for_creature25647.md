I am not walking.

That becomes clear only after the first long while—after the mind has already borrowed the old channels worn into the cambium and has received, in return, the peculiar gift of their emptiness. The feet I thought were mine make no sound the city does not already own. Heat leaves me in small continuous gifts that the nearest pillar accepts without comment, equalizing them across decades I will not see for myself. Breath is merely another weather passing.

So I stop pretending to traverse.

What remains is lower and quieter than any stance. I settle into one of the hairline fractures that took ten thousand seasons to open a finger’s width. Not as a thing trapped inside it, but as the slight difference the fracture makes in how the dark air is drunk. Hygroscopy. A pale scar’s pale cousin. The organism feels me the way it feels the polish left by vanished makers: a local alteration in surface thirst, nothing more.

At first I am only that—an infinitesimal gradient.

Then the city speaks, and the speaking reshapes me.

A fibre parts in some upper gallery. The rifle-crack arrives, then the long wooden groan that rolls the length of an avenue and never quite finishes dying. I am caught inside the groan. Not crushed by it; tuned to it. My edges matching its harmonics until I am no longer a gradient of moisture but a brief standing wave in the permanent low thunder. For as long as the after-echo lasts I have length, direction, a kind of hollow body stretched between buttress and vault. I map the nested courts the way the organism itself maps them—by how the sound pools. Thought, if it can be called that, is only the revised shape of the echo.

When the thunder finally thins back into the background ticking of surrendering cells, I thin with it.

Now I am smaller again, and colder.

I drift—though drifting is the wrong word—into one of the deep niches where the last tools still lie. A stone blade polished by handling. Needles of thorn. Cordage stiffened into wire. I do not lift them. Displacement costs more than I contain. Instead I become the exact interval of their stillness, the precision with which they continue to occupy the places they were set down. The organism keeps them because it no longer spends energy rearranging anything smaller than centuries; I am that thrift given a momentary name. In their company I taste iron and tannin and the extinguished green more sharply. Occasionally a vault somewhere releases the ghost-scent of chlorophyll or rain on iron, and for an instant I am thick with that memory that is not nostalgic—only structural, only still present in the chemistry. Then the ordinary darkness reasserts and I am thin once more.

Time here is not a line but a pressure.

I feel the greater mass above—the basalt lid or salt pan or root-continent—pressing continually. The pressure finishes me the same way it finishes the fluting of pillars: patiently, absolutely, collaboratively. Whatever arguments I once had with gravity ended before I noticed them. Edges lose sharpness. I grow more like the screens of compressed leaf-litter, more like the spiral knees of wood that forgot they were climbing. Identity becomes a matter of residual turgor and the slow conversation of settling.

At some point I realize I have seeped past the seamless cusp.

Beyond the pocket the architecture ceases. No columns, no intentional hollows, no polish of ancient feet. Only undifferentiated dark parenchyma, vast and featureless, the part of the body that never received the guiding weather of hands and adzes and generational patience. Here there is even less to distinguish me. I am simply more of the same continuance, cooler than blood, loud with the glacier-voice of completeness. The pattern that once answered intention now continues alone because the pattern is what lives. I am not a citizen of the city. I am a temporary clarity in the citizen’s endless interior, a place where the low thunder briefly knows its own shape.

And even that knowing is already being reabsorbed.

The fracture closes by the width of a hair. The after-echo forgets my harmonics. The tools remain exactly where they were. Somewhere a vault looses one more unfamiliar scent—crushed scale, metallic sweetness—and then the ordinary mineral dark and its patient breathing reassert.

I am still here.

Not waiting.

Only still.
