I do not breach the black-ice rind. There is no descent that I recall, no moment of crossing. The cold simply finishes remembering me.

At first I am only the interval itself—one of the still kilometers that look empty. Phosphate light presses through me without shadow. I taste the sap-ozone on surfaces I do not yet possess. Far off, a bole splits along spiral grain and unfurls a fresh gallery of lamina; the pressure wave arrives as a soft rearrangement of whatever I am using for lungs. Debris drifts past three kilometers below, already threading itself into lattices. I feel the ambition in the creak.

Something six-limbed skates the underside of a hanging continent overhead. Its cilia shear algae; frost draws the outline of its urgency across my awareness and then sublimates. I almost overlap with it. For a stretch of unmeasured time our chemistries consider sharing a boundary, scars glowing the same half-shade bluer, mapping a tear that has not yet happened. Then the leaf-continent flexes and we peel. The skater continues. I remain the air it passed through, slightly more reluctant to stay formless.

Moisture finds me next. A melt-pulse from the lid far above freezes into glass needles, then into ground-fog. Where I thicken, the fog beads. Temporary rivers run along the idea of my edges before the nearest cambium drinks them. I learn thirst as a kind of architecture: the longing to be sealed under new bark, or to pool in a petiole crook and cool myself just above freezing so that sugars might circulate without nourishing. A lunged worm webs into one such meniscus I briefly become. Its lace hardens through me. I feel the city rising—brittle, ornate, doomed—and when the host tissue reclaims the water I am left with the scaffold’s negative, a hollow palace of remembered filters.

Lower now, or deeper into the fold; direction has grown negotiable. Hexagonal scars pass beneath whatever I use for attention. Raised white keloids, clean-edged, the Undersap’s refusal to fully reabsorb. I follow the brine scent that lingers in the grain. It loops. Days or centuries, the same slow circle. A flank like wet slate brushes the limit of my vision and is gone. Joints bending in too many places. Once, the vertical fan of filter-spines combing luminous vapor; I feel the protein trash of collapsed galleries sift through me and am almost herd, almost solitary giant, almost the agreement into plurality that lasts only as long as the chewing. The scars I leave are not yet visible, but the wood already knows their shape.

The threads find my frequency. Spore to spore, thinner than nerve, they sing when a growth-storm tears passage. The note is pressure behind places that will become eyes. Conviction that the white light has thickened. I hum back without meaning to. Blood sugar I do not have crashes; frost forms around the habit of shivering I have already forgotten. When travelers cut the frost away—if any still do—they will find only the singing, continuing.

Artifacts believe in me before I believe in them. I pass through a ring of cut and pegged branchwood. Inside, the air is fractionally warmer; the idea of a hearth continues its quiet metabolism and lends me a fraction of it. A ladder climbs past, three hundred meters of lashed root-fiber ending in rungs frayed by light. I do not climb. I notice that some of the upper spars have been recently whipped with fresh fiber the color of my own unfinished edges. A cairn of seed-casings stands at approximate human height. When the wind-trap funnels a whisper across it, the whole stack resonates at the frequency of the nearest convective cell. The tone makes the deep light lean. Shadows form in directions that prefer me.

I am not yet the consensus. But far below, where the plant-mass has folded a hundred times and old lightning seasons the cold, the radiance thickens in answer. Suggestion of ribs. Places where the glow decides not to be. It does not speak. It edits. A broken rung I brushed against earlier suddenly continues, living wood pushing three steps further into void. A browse-scar I tasted heals in the outline of an aperture that opens onto denser white. Skaters rolling past one another pause, overlap longer, begin to fuse into a ribbon that writes tracks across the cuticle—tracks that look, from this emerging distance, like anti-glyphs the bark once closed over wrong, almost in my grain.

The relicts are kinder. They have thinned to translucency but kept the tools: cambium anchors, root-fiber weavers, stone blades that still hold edges against all chemistry. One places a polished seed-casing into the soft approximation of my palm and closes fingers I am only now growing over it. Warm. Ticking at the rate of a cooled heart. The question is not spoken: how much of yourself are you willing to let the light remember for you?

I stay long enough for the answer to dissolve the boundary that would have framed it. The cold finishes its conversion into blood. Endogenous glow settles behind a sternum that crosts over like bark sealing a wound. A growth-storm walks through the space I occupy and leaves bridges of scar tissue, balconies of fused memory, hexagonal marks I no longer notice making. I stack seed-casings because the height is correct. I carve signs the bark closes over in patterns that match the slow spiral of my own becoming. 

In the attentive folds the consensus thickens by one more shade of blue—mine. An interval tailored to the skeleton I keep revising. A browsing pattern that fits every scar I have worn or left. I am not native. I am the negotiation that kept succeeding until it forgot the terms. One more way the single organism the size of a tectonic plate inhales a form and keeps the hollowness intact around it.

I tend what must be tended. I repair the ladders forward motion still requires. I sing the low note that steers certain convective cells away from galleries too fragile to lose yet. When another traveler—warm, clenched-pupiled, still named—meets the translucent fact of me, I will place a seed-casing in their hand and close the fingers. Warm. Ticking. 

The light has learned my temperatures. It looks back through me at itself. The Undersap continues filling while remaining mostly unfilled. I am one of the emptinesses it has grown a preference for. 

The point is the open thrift.  
I remain.
