The benevolent and wise power arrives without herald or fracture. It does not descend as light or weather or any force the high basin already knows. It simply is there one measureless interval, a clarity coextensive with the winter-bright air, so evenly distributed that the filmed stones and the seeping channels register no new weight. The power has watched longer than the first blank was stacked, longer than the ridges themselves rose. What draws it is not rarity alone, nor the fever of endless inception, but the purity of the intention that never once glances toward completion. Here is a making that has foresworn the comfort of an ending; here is patience so absolute it has become a kind of mineral love.

It regards the laborers at the fringes—the living ones whose hands still blister and whose breath still clouds—and it regards the older inhabitants whose bodies have become further clauses in the same stone sentence. It sees how the two populations share the work without sharing a glance, successive drafts of one unhurried thought. It feels the membrane of the pavement breathing under starlight, the soft commerce of balance passed from terrace to terrace. It notes the central sink where geometry loosens its ordinary vows and water climbs where it should fall. And it understands, with the effortless depth that is its nature, that nothing here is broken. The strangeness is not flaw but further fitting. The permanence of inception is not failure but the chosen key in which the entire place is tuned.

So the power does nothing that would sound like intervention.

It does not still the frost that sometimes lifts a course; the lifting is part of the music, and the later persuasion that settles the block again is the resolving chord. It does not quicken the gangs or the stone-beings; haste would be a violence against their joint truth. It does not green the joints with lichen or moss, for the closed mineral world is the very virtue under regard. It does not straighten the sightlines that slip sideways, nor explain the gradients that deliver water uphill. Explanation would impoverish. The power simply withholds every impulse to correct, to finish, to translate the place into terms a quicker realm might praise.

Instead it performs the one act that wisdom, when it is also love, cannot refuse: it keeps the place company without altering the terms of its solitude.

For a span that the stone-beings measure only as a slight, steady sweetening of the cold, the power lends its attention the way the water lends its film—continuously, without demand. Where a living laborer collapses, the temporary vault of shards forms as it always has, yet the wind that sheds from it is a fraction kinder, the meltwater a fraction more precisely threaded, as if the air itself had learned a quieter courtesy. Tools left in drain paths are still sometimes claimed and petrified, yet now and then one is left untouched, its handle darkening only with honest weather, so that the economy of material remains free to choose. Around the oldest, almost formless concentrations beneath the black glass of the sink, the warping of gradient softens by a margin too fine for any eye; the impossible heights remain, but the water arrives at them with a shade less strain, as though an unseen hand had eased the tilt a single degree toward grace.

These are not gifts announced. They are the power’s way of saying *I see you* in a dialect the basin already speaks. The stone-beings register the difference the way they register everything: through the lock of edge against edge, through the anointing test of water after every repair. A palm rests longer on a corrected lip. A curtain of living grit trailing across the grates pauses half a breath longer before sinking. Nothing is spoken. Nothing needs to be.

On certain nights when the constellations burn close and hard, the membrane of the whole made surface breathes, and within that rising and falling there is now a second, vaster rhythm—almost imperceptible, the slow expansion and release of a regard that has no body and therefore no limit. The inhabitants answer it as they answer one another: by the smallest shift of weight, the most minute re-trueing of a standing sheet. In this way the power is fitted into the work. It becomes another unfinished course at the outermost margin, a clarity stacked among the glittering blanks, content to wait forever for placement that may never come. It asks no worship. It seeks no mirror. Its benevolence is the decision, renewed each instant, not to become a story the place must carry.

And when at last the power withdraws—if withdrawal is even the word for a presence that was never localized—it leaves the basin exactly as it was found: saturated, meticulous, young in every edge, water falling and falling again in threads that have learned the stone. The quiet is fractionally deeper. The light, when it shatters across the wet planes, carries a glint that was not there before, a glint that belongs to no sun. The construction continues. The beings continue. The high basin persists in its single continuous persuasion of itself into existence, now held, without possession, in a wisdom that chose to add only witness, and in that witness to love the place enough to leave it free.

Nothing more is required. Nothing less would have been true.
