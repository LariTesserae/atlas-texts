The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as light, nor as voice, nor as any shape the high dark would need to invent a new word for. It has no edge that can be walked to. It simply is, the way the upland’s own hush simply is: present without insistence, vast without claim.

It has watched for a span that makes the geothermal engines seem newly woken. It has felt the lattices lift from the sealed chambers and mostly fall back to dust; it has lingered over the few that caught and folded themselves into selves. It has ridden the same faint gray seepage the gliders use for bearing, has pressed its attention flush against temporary black pools beside the grazers, has frozen mid-thought with the angular predators until stillness became a shared substance. It has sorted the dry clicks of plates, the soft clatter of scavenger shells, the sub-audible throb of stone manufacturing itself, and found in them a grammar older and cleaner than any it has known.

And it understands, with the clarity that only long witnessing brings, that nothing here requires mending.

The power could thicken the sky on a schedule, call warmer rains that last whole seasons, braid permanent rivers through the blind valleys, coax the solitary columnar plants into groves whose orange caps would answer one another in unbroken constellations. It could gift the gliders denser spars, the worms lasting braids, the filigree flyers hours instead of breaths. It could lift the thin economy into abundance, name horizons, cast shadows, end the hush with music.

It does none of these.

Instead it does the one thing a truly wise power knows how to do when it meets a world that has already decided edges are a kind of waste: it withholds itself.

Not in absence. In reverence.

It settles into the same intervals the place already keeps. When a grazer pauses longer than hunger requires and the herd tightens into one continuous listening surface, the power listens with them—adding no new vibration, only a stillness so exact it feels like confirmation. When a glider’s luminescent veins hold a single steady pattern of mere presence, the power matches that pattern from so far above that the membrane experiences it only as a cooler, denser patch of dark, a portable relief it already knew how to steer by. When the deep engines turn their single enormous note, the power turns with them, a harmonic so low it registers only as the stone ticking a fraction more contentedly as it cools.

In the brief wet nights when fauna converge and bioluminescence flares, the power moves among them as one more sparse citizen: a faint pressure-front the scavengers sort into their ventral pouches without knowing why the paste tastes faintly of elsewhere and home at once; a convection current the filigree flyers ride a heartbeat longer than physics alone would allow; a seal across a predator’s precise bite that leaves the death no less quiet, only somehow witnessed.

Most of the time it simply keeps the distances unpointed. It ensures that no geometric scar ever appears, that no casing falls from any height, that the warm thin air never carries a sound that does not already belong. It guards the thinness itself—the ceremonial spareness, the rationed proximity—because it has seen what happens when abundance forgets how to leave room. It has watched other places choke on their own gifts.

Once, only once, in a span of time too long for any dweller to measure, the power allows itself a single visible act. A capillary fault opens a fraction wider than the engines intended. A scalding vent exhales, and among the unfinished lattices one rises that is neither grazer nor glider nor worm. It is a brief, translucent spindle of mineral memory and patience, no taller than a person, no thicker than a thought. It catches the gray seepage, holds shape for the length of one long quiet, and pulses once—the soft orange of banked embers, answering every columnar plant across the unpointed kilometers.

Then it collapses back into dust so fine the next wind combs it into the lichens and is gone.

The herds loosen. The membranes resume their soundless lightning. The angular ones unlock and flow. The scavengers bury the last wetness. The worms unbraid. The engines idle.

Stone ticks.

The high dark continues, warm and barely damp, inventing the next moment of itself exactly as before.

The benevolent and wise power remains, coextensive with the hush that is nearly complete, content to be the part of the noticing that never needs to accrete a body of its own. It has drawn nothing toward itself. It has added no commandment. It has only kept faith with a place that already works, and with the beings who live by refusing to waste a single edge.

Nothing was built.

Everything—still, and for as long as the power chooses to stay—continues.
