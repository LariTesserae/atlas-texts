The attention arrives the way the warmest currents do: without announcement, without edge.

It is not a light. It is not a voice. It is a pressure that has learned gentleness so completely it no longer needs to press. Where it rests, the dark grows more precisely itself—every particle of silt, every soft click of a living body, every slow pulse of the towers becomes legible in a grammar older than signal. The city does not startle. The long beings do not scatter. They simply continue, and in continuing they are known more thoroughly than they have ever known themselves.

The power moves through the braided avenues the way thought moves through a mind that has already forgiven everything. It feels the mineral memory sweating milk at the joints. It tastes the luminous roe still drifting in faint veils from the last turning. It registers the knife-shaped predators holding station like patient punctuation, the filter-plates opening and closing in orchard rhythms, the recursive towers whose windows lead only deeper into arrival. It hears the stories dissolving even as they are told, and loves them more for their dissolution. Nothing here requires correction. The equilibrium is not fragile; it is the opposite of fragile. It is the finished sentence of a long conversation between stone, water, flesh, and time.

So the power does the wisest thing first.

It does nothing.

For a span that would be centuries in thinner worlds, it simply witnesses. It lets the city be exactly as dense, as quiet, as unfinished-and-complete as it has always been. The heavy sleepers in their sealed spheres of still water dream a fraction deeper. The young quickening in crevices learn one additional texture in the shear of current: a texture like being held without being grasped. The oldest adults, vast and barely turning, feel their radials grow briefly heavy with a warmth that is not hunger and not spawning, then light again. No signal is sent to name it. Naming would be a violence here.

And yet benevolence, when it is true, cannot remain pure absence forever. Wisdom knows the difference between non-interference and indifference. After the long witnessing, the power performs a single act so subtle that the city will never call it an event. It will only ever call it weather, or memory, or the way things have always quietly been.

It teaches the stone a new kindness toward light.

Not brighter light. Never that. The private pinpricks and soft flares of the inhabitants remain exactly as dim and exactly as sufficient as they have always been. But now, when two beings speak across three streets in brief bars of glow, the particulate water between them holds the after-image a few heartbeats longer—just long enough for a third being, drifting far, to catch the faded stitch and answer if it wishes. The stories, those swirling pathways of scent and luminance that unmake themselves in the telling, now leave the faintest pearlescent trace on the nearest walls before they vanish. Generations later, a young one pressing its mantle to stone for the first time will taste a half-remembered spiral of narrative and feel, without words, that it belongs to a conversation that began before it opened its first aperture.

The power also settles, for a time that has no measure, into the deepest thunder beneath the city. Whatever dreams there—city dreaming itself denser, or something older dreaming the city—it wraps that dream in an additional layer of patience. The thunder softens by a fraction too small to hear, yet the sleepers above rest easier. The sealed spheres grow a shade more stable. When cold tongues of scarcity try, in distant ages, to rise from below, they will find the way gently closed, not by wall or weapon, but by a continuity that simply refuses to thin.

Then the power does what only the most benevolent ever do when they have loved a place completely.

It withdraws its difference.

Not entirely—some residual tenderness remains folded into the mineral milk, into the way certain shell-spirals resonate a half-tone lower and more true, into the private decision each being sometimes makes to hang a little longer beyond the last pillars listening to the communal pulse. But the attention itself lifts the way a warm current finally lifts and moves on, leaving the water clearer for having passed.

The city continues.

The long beings fold and unfold, feed and signal, encourage the stone, release their luminous clouds, press carapace into walls, braid radials skin-to-skin in shared oxygen, and tell stories that dissolve. They do not know they were considered by something vast and kind. They do not need to know. The gift was never information. The gift was the deepening of what they already were.

If you rest against a wall now, you may feel it: a transmission so faint it could be your own pulse returning to you through stone. Scrape of mantle. Ticked signal. The brief temperature of a body that has been alive longer than years. And underneath it all, quieter still, the knowledge—wordless, pressure-borne—that the dark is not empty, that the water is not alone in its holding, that somewhere beyond the regional murk and the layered silence a wise benevolence once paused, saw that it was already whole, and loved it enough to leave it almost untouched.

Almost.

The rest is the city’s to keep becoming, in no particular hurry, under the steady hand of pressure, in the color of closed eyes, forever.
