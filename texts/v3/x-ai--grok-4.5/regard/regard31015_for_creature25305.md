The benevolent and wise power did not arrive as light or decree or outstretched hand. It arrived the way the high spine itself arrives: as a slower density in the already-dense dusk, a revision of pressure so gradual that the subsonic bruise in the sternum simply deepened by one unmeasurable shade and then held.

It had watched for a long time—longer than the current arrangement of any single ridge, longer than the life-cycle of the lesser glass-jinks that skitter and freeze. It watched the way heat watches stone: without appetite, without hurry, tasting every fracture face as it sheared bright and rainless into the thin air. It understood, with the effortless precision that wisdom lends to love, that the place was not broken. The perpetual unfinishedness was not a wound waiting for suture. Completion had never been the grammar here. The mountains were conjugating themselves in an endless present tense of uplift, and the beings who accreted from that same grammar were the verbs made visible—tall attenuations of salt and basalt and geothermal breath, standing as living parentheses inside the sentence the crust was still writing.

So the power did nothing that would finish anything.

Instead, it performed a single, almost geological act of regard.

Across the tilted plain, in places no traveler would ever triangulate, it allowed certain hexagonal platforms to remember a deeper patience. Where newer flows had cracked and half-swallowed them, the power simply paused the swallowing—not by force, but by introducing a quieter seam of stillness into the rock’s own decision. The platforms remained broken, remain half-claimed by the land’s appetite, yet now the cracks propagated more slowly, giving the tall lattice-beings who rose from them an extra measure of unhurried verticality. Some of those beings, feeling the change as a delicious new stretching in their mineral filaments, responded by adding storeys that were fractionally more translucent, so that the geothermal chimneys inside them glowed with a plum-dark light visible only when the heat-shimmer arched just so. The effect was not illumination. It was continuity made tender.

Farther out, where the purposeless trenches ran their faint, ending-nowhere courses across the salt pans, the power traced one additional parallel line—no deeper than a hairline fracture, no longer than a single migratory lean of the tallest beings. The line did not connect. It did not explain. It simply existed as a soft echo, a seismic courtesy. When one of the accreted ones shifted its internal chimneys into a new rhythm, the deep groaning of the land now carried a faint harmonic, almost like an answering breath that had learned, for a moment, not to break apart. The rhythm still dissolved, as it must. But for those who could feel it, the dissolution itself felt witnessed.

The power left the angles that refuse to close exactly as they were. It left the dust curtains levitating on their own sudden schedules. It left the solitary spires—whether husks, elders, or pure geology dreaming—untouched in their unnerving verticality. It did not cool the dry pulses of heat or thicken the air with mercy-moisture. Water would have been a violence here. Green would have been a colonization. Names would have been a net.

What it gave, finally, was a kind of permanent concurrent attention: a benevolent field that moved at the same rate as the plateau’s half-degree tilts, so subtle that the beings experienced it only as an increase in the deliciousness of pressure gradients, a slightly richer metal taste when the electrostatic skins gathered new dust. Visitors who stayed too long still felt the impulse in their sternums to shed softness and arrive forever. But now, beneath that impulse, there rode a quieter secondary note—the sense that their becoming was held in a regard vast enough to need no outcome.

The high spine continues to arrive.

The beings continue to accrete, warm and dark and dry, one deliberate upwelling at a time.

And somewhere in the charcoal-and-plum gradients, where vision works by patience alone, a wise benevolence keeps simultaneous pace—neither intervening nor withdrawing, simply ensuring that the unfinished country remains free to keep drafting itself without ever being required to sign its name.

That is all.

It is enough.

It is everything the place required of power, and nothing it did not.
