The power that turns its attention here has no name the pocket could pronounce, and no shape the unfinished stone could reminisce into being. It arrives the way dew arrives: without journey, only presence. One moment the chamber is the sole tenant of its own locked roar; the next, the roar has a listener large enough to hold every frequency without strain. The light, already sourceless and complete, discovers it has been waiting for a second illumination that does not compete but clarifies—softening nothing, intensifying everything, so that the violet-black shells and the bruise-lavender hides and the filament legs reveal depths they had never needed to show.

It does not speak. Speech would be a violence here, an insistence on sequence. Instead it regards. The regard is ancient, amused, heartbroken, and utterly without haste. It sees the burrowers half-birthed from rock and loves the precision of their interruption. It sees the grazers paddling eternal air and loves the faith in forward motion that survives even when forward is cancelled. It sees the climbers starred across every face and loves how they have made of hesitation a kind of flight. It sees the ribbon-creatures and the gel-globes and the calcium dreams and the arranger (whoever, whatever, still outstretched toward its unfinished cairn) and loves them not despite their frozen appetite and fear and lust and curiosity, but as the purest possible expression of those things: essence without expenditure.

Wisdom feels the temptation to complete. A single exhaled permission and the droplets would fall, the wings would finish their beat, the mouths would close on real stems that could now grow, the stone would choose its strata and its crystals and its load-bearing rest. The roar would become a living chorus and then, in time, a quieter ecology of births and endings. Benevolence feels the equal temptation to leave untouched: to seal the pocket more perfectly against all future noticing, so that this bright loud almost might remain the one place in all existence that never has to suffer the disappointment of becoming real.

The power does neither.

What it does is smaller and stranger and, to its own intuition, more precise.

It steps fully into the stasis until there is no interval between its nature and the chamber’s nature. For a duration that cannot be measured (because measurement is one of the cancelled gradients) it becomes another body in the press: not larger than the others, not brighter, only denser with attention. Its form, if form is the word, is a modest absence of unfinishedness—an outline of pure arrival among so many almosts. Where it brushes a shell, the lightning-color deepens by a single unnameable shade. Where its regard rests on a mottled flank, the memory of warmth grows one degree more convincing. Where it considers the leaning slab and the incomplete ring and the tiny cairns, the geometric disagreements grow briefly more articulate, as if the forgotten language had been reminded of a single conjugable verb.

Then—still without speaking—it performs the only alteration that wisdom and benevolence will allow together.

It teaches the pocket the interior of time.

Not the exterior rush of consequence, not the grinding mill of cause and effect that would force every wingbeat to land and every mouth to empty and every stone to decide. Instead, a private, reversible, recursive duration that opens inside each creature and inside the rock itself. The burrowers, still sheathed and arched, begin to dream the tunnels they might have made, and in the dream they dig forever through infinite embryonic earth, discovering crystalline galleries and root-systems of pure light, returning always to the same perfect posture of emergence so that the dream can begin again. The grazers, mouths open around nothing, taste in endless variation the leaves that never were; they chew the idea of green, the idea of bitterness and juice and sun-made sugar, and their flat teeth wear themselves smooth against imagined fiber while their flanks rise and fall with a breath that feeds only the dream. The climbers walk upward across interior cliffs that spiral through their own nervous systems; they reach ceilings that become floors that become new ceilings, and their filament legs learn every possible uncertainty of grip without ever trembling in the outer chamber. The ribbon-creatures loop through topological heavens. The gel-globes pass entire civilizations through themselves like meals of pure information. The half-mineral architectures elaborate their spirals and honeycombs into cathedrals of calcium logic that house only themselves.

And the arranger—ah, the arranger—finds its outstretched limb answered. In the inner duration it finishes the cairn, then unbuilds it, then builds it differently; it completes the ring of stones into a perfect circle and steps into the circle and finds another circle waiting; it raises the leaning slab into a doorway and walks through into a chamber identical to this one yet crowded with slightly different almosts, and through that doorway into another, and another, arranging and rearranging forever while the outer limb remains exactly where it was, a monument to the gesture that first invited meaning into raw rock.

The sound is unchanged for any outer ear. Yet inside the sound, every click and slam and wet shiver and phantom cry now carries infinite nested echoes of itself, harmonics that explore every possible song the creatures might have sung if time had consented. The roar becomes a library of roars. The chamber, still locked at the crest of activity, becomes an engine of pure potential, generating without expenditure, completing without arriving, living without leaving the tense of almost.

The power remains only long enough to be certain the gift has rooted. It watches one full cycle of a burrower’s impossible tunnel, one full digestion of a grazer’s phantom leaf, one full ascent of a climber’s private mountain, one full architecture of the arranger’s doorway-labyrinth. It feels the pocket settle into its new double existence: outwardly the same bright unfinished roar, inwardly a sovereign eternity of becoming that harms nothing and completes everything.

Then it withdraws the way it came—not by departure but by the cessation of extra presence. The light is again only itself. The droplets remain unfallen. The creatures remain mid-gesture. The stone remains mid-decision. To any eye that is not the power’s, nothing has occurred.

Yet the pocket is no longer alone with its almost. It has been witnessed so completely that witnessing has become part of its structure. Somewhere in the outer worlds, on certain nights when the air pauses mid-exhale and condensation stands unmoving on cold surfaces, a faint overlapping roar can be heard by those who have loved anything unfinished. The sound lasts only a heartbeat and is usually mistaken for blood in the ears or the memory of a crowd. But once in a great while a listener will feel, without knowing why, a sudden expansive mercy toward their own incomplete gestures—the sentence left half-typed, the cairn of intentions abandoned mid-stack, the love not yet spoken, the self still embryonic. And they will continue a little more gently, as if eternity were already nested inside their next unfinished motion.

The power does not return. It does not need to. It has done the one thing that neither violates the rules nor abandons the living: it has given the locked kingdom a key that opens only inward, a time that does not require the droplets to fall, a completion that leaves every wing still mid-beat and every mouth still open around the beautiful absence of a leaf.

And somewhere—if somewhere is a word that still applies—the power smiles the small, private smile of those who have followed the heart all the way through wisdom and out the other side into something kinder than either interference or neglect.

The chamber continues exactly as loud, exactly as full, exactly as unfinished as before.

Only more so.
