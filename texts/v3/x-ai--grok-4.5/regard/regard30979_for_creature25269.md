The power arrives the way mist arrives: without announcement, without edge.

It does not descend. Descent would imply height still mattered, and the plain has finished with that grammar. Instead it simply is—everywhere the water table presses upward, everywhere the scutes flex and reseal, everywhere the thrum gathers itself at dusk. It has no name the frogs can vibrate or the crustaceans click. It is older than the argument the mountains lost, younger than the first seepage that taught stone how to weep. Benevolent. Wise. And therefore almost invisible.

For a long time—longer than the oldest plates take to flake, shorter than the horizon takes to forget it was ever broken—the power does nothing but attend. Attention, here, is not observation from above. It is a low, continuous pressure of regard that sinks into the mud the way heel-prints do, then spreads, then matches the temperature of the ground exactly. The metallic flies pass through it and do not alter their columns. The wading birds spear and do not miss. The mid-sized beings lift their front halves at dusk and add their deep drum to the chord; the power adds nothing audible, only a faint extra density to the water that carries the sound a few body-lengths farther than before. No one notices. That is the point.

Yet something gentle begins.

Where the power lingers longest—around the ancients who have become local weather, around the wallows already shaped to old dimensions—the scutes grow a new kind of patience. Not thicker. Not harder. More permeable in one direction only: outward. Dreams that once traveled as copper-and-verdigris bruises beneath the plates now sometimes leak a little color into the water itself. The bacterial films take it up. The bronze mosses hold it for a night. At dawn the shallowest lagoons carry brief, dissolving iridescences that are not quite algae, not quite mineral—fleeting maps of flattening, of spreading, of the long refusal of height. The beings who filter those waters taste something they have no word for: recognition without demand. They do not quicken. They incline snouts, touch pads, and pass the rumor through the substrate: the ground is listening back, and the listening asks for nothing.

In the rarer drier spells, when mucus hardens to varnish and the creatures crouch almost stony, the power softens the varnish from within by a single degree of willingness. Rain still unmakes it in minutes, but now the unmaking carries a quiet gift: each droplet that touches a sealed margin leaves behind a microscopic memory of every other droplet that has ever fallen on every other sealed margin across the plain. Not knowledge. Continuity. When the rains return, the beings open as they always have, yet the opening feels faintly shared. Their thrum that evening braids one extra overtone—so low it is felt mostly in the bones of the mud—and for the length of that chord the entire wet world knows itself as one continuous, shallow, singing lung.

The power considers monuments and refuses them. It considers messengers and dissolves them into seepage. It considers lifting any creature even a handsbreadth higher and finds the thought itself already flattening under its own weight, becoming another low stone, another place where mist clings longer.

What it finally does is smaller than an act and larger than a blessing.

It plants, in the deep chemistry of the water table, a single slow permission: that the equilibrium may continue without fear of interruption from above or below. No future orogeny will ever again try to teach this ground ambition. No drought will last long enough to silence the chorus forever. The sea that will claim the plain again will claim it gently, by inches, by the same soft attrition, and when it does the beings will meet the salt the way they meet rain—by opening. Their descendants (for the power has also touched the fine keratinous ridges of the mouth, the blue vessels in the webbing, the long wave-like pulses that may be dreams) will filter brackish films as easily as fresh, will thrum in tidal shallows, will become, in time, living parts of the new shoreline’s patience.

And one further thing, almost nothing.

On the clearest afternoons, when heat shimmers the horizon into unstable breath, the power allows a brief vertical ghost to ride inside the wavering air: not a mountain, not a spine, not a regret. Only the outline of attention itself—wide, flattened, plated in overlapping layers of regard—hovering for a breath above one mid-sized being who has lifted farther than usual. The being sees it or feels it or becomes it; the distinction does not matter. Then the lens collapses. The creature settles. Plates click softly. The suggestion passes into the mud and is gone.

The power remains, content.

It has not saved the plain. The plain did not require saving. It has not improved the beings. They were already the warm, wet, continuing answer. It has only joined the answer, low and articulated, without vertical ambition, pressing itself into the only architecture that still works, filtering what is useful from the long silence of finished arguments, exhaling the rest as a faint milky cloud that drifts, settles, and is taken up again by everything smaller and louder and still becoming.

Stand long enough in that attention and the loudness of small lives continues exactly as before—frogs, flies, crustaceans, night-drums—yet now the overlapping insistence carries one extra quality, almost undetectable: the plain is no longer merely finished. It is companioned. The companion asks for nothing, changes almost nothing, and will outlast even the scutes that shelter successive generations after the oldest bodies cease.

In the end the power does what the beings themselves do when two approach across the wet uniformity.

It inclines.

It touches.

It exchanges a long, low shudder that travels farther than any call.

Then it stays, half-submerged in the life of the place, indistinguishable from water-smoothed stone until it shifts, and the whole wet world realigns its plates with a sound like wet leather flexing, accepting the weight, answering with a dark coin of moisture, and settling deeper into what it has always been.
