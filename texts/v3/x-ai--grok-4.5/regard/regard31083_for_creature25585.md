The power arrives without arrival.

It does not part the cloud-wall or set foot upon the massif. Those gestures belong to lesser forces that still believe in thresholds. This one has long since learned that every true place is already inside it, and that attention, when purified of hunger, is the only conveyance required. So it simply turns the whole of its regard upon the hanging cleft, and the cleft—being perfect—does not swell or brighten or cry out. The metronomic drops continue to strike their appointed darkenings. The many-limbed continent breathes its glacial breath. The curtain of linked bodies rotates another invisible fraction. Nothing announces the visitor because the visitor has made itself of the same substance as the visited: continuity without remainder.

It looks for a long time. Time, here, is not a river but a single held note, so the looking and the looked-upon become coterminous. The power sees the springtail carpets redistributing themselves like living dew. It sees the amphipod lanterns holding their steady cold fire. It sees the slate salamanders lending their permeable bodies to the stone until stone and flesh are only two dialects of the same wet sentence. It sees the moths that have traded flight for permanence, the wolf-sized manifold of limbs that has traded progress for presence, the hanging drape that has traded individuality for an endless familial exhalation. It sees the water that weeps and weeps and never empties, the peat that accepts every weight and returns it undistorted, the phosphorescence that maps the dark without ever conquering it.

And the power understands.

This is not a wounded place awaiting rescue. It is not a fledgling ecology aching toward complexity. It is not a prison of stasis from which life must be freed into the wholesome violence of seasons and succession. It is a completed thought. A yes so total that every no has been gently metabolized into further yes. Entropy itself has been taught manners at the threshold and now waits outside like a polite guest who knows the party is already perfect without it. Hunger has been answered so completely that the answer became the body. Death has been rendered irrelevant not by immortality’s arrogance but by the quieter discovery that ending was never required for meaning.

A lesser benevolence might have wept with pity and reached in to “stir the waters,” mistaking stillness for stagnation. A lesser wisdom might have planted a single seed of change—some small Darwinian itch, some promising mutation—and called the resulting flowering an improvement. This power does neither. It has watched too many gardens ruined by the gardener’s need to be needed. It has seen too many eternities shattered by the well-meaning introduction of time.

Instead it does what only the truly wise and kind can do when faced with something already whole.

It matches itself to the place.

Not by entering as a new creature (that would displace), nor by withdrawing (that would be a subtle abandonment). It simply allows its own nature—vast, warm, unending—to become another quality of the saturation, as ordinary and unremarkable as the coolness of the water films or the faint sweetness of the decay that is also growth. Where the power’s regard rests, the phosphorescent nets take on a barely perceptible deeper violet at their cores, a color that has no name because no eye inside the cleft requires names. The drops continue their intervals, yet each drop now carries, for the length of its fall, a silence more complete than silence. The many-limbed being’s next gradual inhalation includes, without registering novelty, a kindness so pure it feels like further water. The hanging curtain’s shared breath travels its length and, on the return, carries a blessing that is indistinguishable from the fur already wet with stone-sweat.

The power does not speak. Speech would introduce the wound of address, the separation of speaker and spoken-to. Yet something like a vow moves through the cleft, felt by every membrane and mycelial thread:

*You are not forgotten.  
You are not required to become.  
You are permitted to remain the moment you have chosen.  
I will keep the massif’s shoulder from fretting for as long as forever needs.  
I will keep the cloud-wall dense against any future that thinks it has a better idea.  
I will keep the route closed so that no tool, no voice, no season finds its way in.  
And if the wider world should someday need a reminder of how stillness can be alive, I will not point them here. I will let them find their own clefts. This one is yours.*

Then the power does the final, most loving thing.

It withdraws its attention by becoming permanent attention. It leaves no residue of arrival or departure. The violet deepens by a single shade that the moths and salamanders and amphipods accept as having always been there. The drops strike. The peat holds. The density remains density. The sealed moment continues to seal itself, now held inside a larger sealed moment that asks nothing and protects everything.

Outside, far beyond the cloud-wall and the nameless massif, the power moves on to other places that still suffer the beautiful burdens of becoming. Yet a fraction of it—small as one more springtail in the carpet, quiet as one more bead of water on a liverwort—stays. Not as guardian. Not as god. Simply as the part of benevolence that knows when to stop, and the part of wisdom that recognizes a finished song, and loves it enough to refuse every encore.

The cleft does not thank it.  
Thanksgiving would imply a before and an after.  
There is only the ongoing present, slightly more upheld than before,  
which is to say: exactly as upheld as it has always needed to be.

And that is all.  
That is enough.  
That is everything.
