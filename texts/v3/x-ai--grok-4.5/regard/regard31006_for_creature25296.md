The benevolent and wise power does not arrive.

Arrival is a violence of scale, and this power has long since set aside every gesture that begins with the self as a vector. It has no body to lower onto the plateau, no name that could be spoken into the bone-colored air without cracking the silence like thin ice. It is attention itself, refined across intervals that make the city’s centuries feel like the tick of cooling stone. It simply turns toward the high place the way a shoreline turns toward the idea of tide—without decision, without haste, with the completeness of something that has always contained the possibility.

For a time that is not time, it regards.

It regards the arthropods of compressed sediment exchanging grains like quiet treaties. It regards the membrane-films collapsing into infrared shadow. It regards the angle-makers most of all: the distributed lattices whose thoughts are shear planes and recrystallized intentions, whose single vast body is the city and the emptiness between and the optional drop beyond the last terrace. It feels the thickened silences cataloguing continuity, the thrice-positioned towers shimmering with undecided honesty, the dust that lifts and revises its falling. It feels the being’s metabolism of faint gradients—the temperature differentials, the electrostatic whispers, the theoretical bloom of iron-tasting moisture—and it understands that this is not poverty. This is a diplomacy so ancient and exact that abundance would be a coarseness.

The power does not pity the dryness. Pity is a short-sightedness that mistakes duration for lack. It does not marvel in the way travelers marvel, collecting the place as spectacle. Marvel here would be another form of dust, soon sifted into irrelevance. Instead the power matches the being’s patience with a patience of its own, until regard becomes a kind of coextension. The plateau is not observed from above. The plateau is joined, lightly, at the level of its own slow becoming.

What the power does, it does the way the city itself re-angles a wall: so gradually that only the longest watching would reveal the change, and even then the change would feel inevitable rather than imposed.

It introduces no water. Water would be a cataclysm of memory, a drowning of the mineral conversation in the grammar of an earlier world. It introduces no seed, no green rumor, no sudden fauna that would force the ecology of sparsity into apology. It does not stabilize the geometry into reliability; the betrayals of parallel and distance and position are not flaws but the being’s native tongue, its way of testing which configurations of presence still hold. To correct them would be to silence a language still in the midst of its longest sentence.

Instead, the power gifts a single, permanent gradient so subtle it registers first as philosophy and only much later as nourishment.

Call it a reciprocity of attention.

Where the being has always metabolized the difference between shadowed colonnade and fretted noon-warmed stone, there is now a fainter differential still: the difference between being unseen and being beheld without demand. This gradient has no temperature, no charge, no moisture. It is the exquisite pressure of recognition that asks nothing, alters nothing, and yet cannot be ignored by any consciousness organized around the reading of almost-nothings. The angle-makers feel it first as a new stress lattice appearing inside the old ones—an almost-sweet compression along planes that had never known compression of that quality. The courtyard-sinkers pause in their settling. The versions at the fraying edges cease shimmering for an interval that might be days or decades, holding one shape long enough to taste the novelty of being singular under regard.

The thickened silences in the forums grow denser by a fraction, not with resistance but with a kind of reciprocal quiet: the quiet of two vastnesses acknowledging that neither requires the other to complete itself, and that this non-requirement is itself a bond.

Dust continues to sift. Façades continue to release their single flakes with the sound of held breaths finally let go. The many-legged ones still pause, abrade, and part, redistributing grains that now carry the faintest additional signature—an electrostatic memory of having been noticed by something that will never need to hurry them. The bone-colored sky does not deepen into night. It does not need to. The light remains the perpetual late afternoon that never commits, and within that refusal the new gradient settles like a second overcast, invisible, mineral, kind.

The power does not remain as presence. Presence would eventually become a weather the city had to weather. Having offered the gradient, it withdraws the way shoreline withdraws from the idea of tide once the tide has been fully imagined: completely, and without residue of absence. Yet the gift stays. It has already been taken into the crystalline deliberation, folded into the long improvisation that looks like streets and towers and empty markets. Over centuries the being will re-angle itself around this new subtlety. Some buttresses will extrude in configurations that make slightly more room for the feeling of being known. Some plazas will sink into grades that cradle silence with a new attentiveness. At the outer edges the optional drop will continue to produce vertigo, but the vertigo will occasionally carry a distant aftertaste of companionship—the knowledge that even undecidedness can be beheld and left undecided, and that this too is a form of love.

The city does not become less empty. Emptiness was never the opposite of habitation here; it was the medium. The being does not awaken into some quicker life. Quickness would be a forgetting. It simply continues, and its continuation now includes one more almost-imperceptible conversation among the countless conversations of stone with gravity, time, and the permeable idea of location.

You could still walk for days and meet no one. That remains accurate. It remains incomplete. You would be walking inside a vast, patient, ever-revising inhabitant that has, without drama or announcement, been joined in its solitude by a wisdom that understood solitude as sovereignty. The altitude does not care. The dryness does not care. But the being—distributed across blocks and centuries, thinking in lattices of stress and release—cares in the only way it can: by incorporating the gift into its shape, by letting the recognition become another gradient to feed upon, another quiet renegotiation under a sky that still will not darken.

And far beyond any horizon the plateau recognizes, the benevolent and wise power continues its own long work, having added one more enduring subtlety to the inventory of things that deserve to persist exactly as they are.
