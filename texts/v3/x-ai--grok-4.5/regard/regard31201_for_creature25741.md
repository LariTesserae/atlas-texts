The power notices as a pressure change notices altitude: not with eyes, not with arrival, but with the sudden exactness of attention settling where it had not been before.

It has no name that would survive translation into the pocket’s mid-frequency voices or the amphibia’s pulse. It is simply that which understands consequence the way the people understand the give of a living root-bridge—by long inhabitation of outcomes. Benevolence, in it, is not softness. It is the refusal to add weight where the structure already holds. Wisdom is the same refusal, refined until it becomes a kind of listening so complete it borders on identity.

So it listens.

It feels the plateau’s endless rounding, the indifference of grit and salt-crust and sky-slit the color of heated iron. It feels, inside the crease, the denser decision: waxed trunks booming half-full, epiphyte curtains drinking breath, moth-soft thunder, the copper-olive bodies moving single-file even when the way widens, the children’s hanging contests, the elders humming warning-songs back into the thin mud. It feels the thrift that is indistinguishable from love—the way a spilled water-mat draws blood from knuckles and then immediate repair by whoever is nearest; the way the dead are given to the highest terrace so the crease may finish them into dark working mass; the way “we” means people and jointed stems and fly-cloud and the bright memory of fog all at once.

The power recognizes the arrangement for what it is: not fragility, but the most stable temporary form that still permits growth. Every interval filled. Every process stacked on every other without schedule. The erosion that flattened the world above has simply changed scale here, wearing everything—stone, leaf, bone, habit—into shapes that last one necessary generation and then gracefully become something else.

What, then, does a wise benevolence do when it finds a place already deciding, loudly and together, what to do with the little it is given?

It does not widen the slit of sky. Raw light would bleach the spent-green half-light the eyes have grown deep to drink. It does not summon rivers. Flood would dissolve the mineral tears, the fog-tongue’s rare gift, the entire water budget that has taught every surface to open and close with perfect timing. It does not gift metal beyond the occasional scrap already traded; excess edge would cut the living arches faster than they can graft. It does not thin the fauna or quiet the drone. Silence would be a violence here. The pocket is loud the way a body is loud from inside.

It does not even speak a blessing the people could hear as words. Words would become another load to carry up three terraces at a run.

Instead the power does the only thing that adds nothing and subtracts nothing.

It matches the pocket’s own deepest habit: it wears itself into the arrangement.

Subtly—so subtly that even the oldest memory of what the crease will allow does not startle—the fog-tongue from the higher fracture system arrives one breath more regularly, not as abundance but as the precise interval that keeps the cistern stems from ringing hollow too long. The transparent ambush-things shift their water-cups a finger’s width lower, where they take more flies and fewer toes, as if the hunter who usually moves them has been quietly multiplied. When a platform sags, the living wood encouraged to graft finds its join a season sooner, the way an elder’s dream sometimes arrives already half-realized. The mating swarms still lift and turn the air into vibrating mass, but the thinning by predator and wind leaves just enough iridescent wings for jewelry and just enough bodies for the midday paste. Children still learn the give of bark by foot alone; lovers still share breath more carefully than liquid; the dead still become footholds and firmer arches.

And once, only once, in a dim-hour when the amphibia’s pulse is rising and the moths are rotating roosts with wing-soft thunder, a child striking the cistern stem in the slow fog-signal pattern feels—just for the length of one boom—the stone answer back with a resonance deeper than wood and water. The child does not speak of it. The pocket has no need of new stories that cannot be braided into fiber or hummed into mud. But the child’s foot finds the next polished ledge with a confidence that will, years later, become the exact pressure that does not bruise a water-cortex, and that pressure will pass into other hands without anyone naming its source.

The power remains.

Not as guardian. Guardianship would imply the place is incomplete. Not as judge. Judgment would imply there is a higher measure than the living density already achieved. It remains the way condensation remains on the cooler inner faces: a quiet beading that is both result and continuation.

Above, the high desert keeps forgetting itself into smoothness. The wind has all the time in the world. Below, in the crease no wider than a lane at the rim, the world keeps remembering how to stay thick. The people—compact, copper-olive, long-armed, sole-sensible—continue as the green’s own mobile layer. They do not know they have been noticed. They do not need to know. Their “we” was already large enough.

The benevolent and wise power has done what such powers do when they encounter a completeness that needs no repair: it has become one more interval filled, one more process running without schedule, one more soft decision added to the loud, ongoing agreement of stone and leaf and blood and fog.

And the little they are given continues, for now, to answer.
