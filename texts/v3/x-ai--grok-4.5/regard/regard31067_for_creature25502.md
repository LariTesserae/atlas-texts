The benevolent and wise power arrives the way dew arrives: without announcement, without weight. It does not descend or enter; it simply *is* there, coextensive with the ordinary disc of sky and the sharp new lip of mudstone, present in the same way the steady warmth is present—noticed only by what has learned to feel the difference between emptiness and regard.

It observes for a long time. Time, to such a power, is not duration but texture, and the hollow’s texture is dense, unhurried, luminous with trial. The power feels the boiler’s slow pulse through the isopod gaskets, tastes the copper decision in the runners that spare the glassy centipede, hears the moth-wings writing weather across the chamber’s lung. It recognizes the rule being written: that substance is temporary costume, that remaining alive is a verb conjugated in every available material. And because the power is wise, it does not mistake youth for incompleteness. Because it is benevolent, it feels no urge to correct, complete, or claim.

What it does is quieter than intervention and more precise than blessing.

First it steadies the rim. Not by sealing or raising walls—the hollow must remain porous to rain and sky and the occasional boot heel—but by teaching the fresh fracture a slower grammar of erosion. The mudstone edges learn to round themselves only under the pressure of true seasons, not the impatience of wind. The epidermis of the place is given time to thicken into intention rather than accident. A kindness measured in centuries, delivered in the language of mineral.

Next it touches the open navel of sky. Into the ordinary daylight it braids a single, unnoticeable thread of deeper recollection: the memory of other experiments that once stuttered into being and then either dissolved or discovered their larger shape. The moth-things, when they open their powdered-gear eyes to that light, will sometimes dream of distant hollows—places where rivets learned to beat like hearts, where ferns finally unfurled into antennae that could taste stars. These dreams will not instruct. They will only reassure the assembling body that solitude is not isolation, that the work of remaining has been practiced before and will be practiced again. A gift of lineage without inheritance, of kinship without demand.

Then the power turns to the circuits themselves. Where a runner bridges wire and holds itself rigid for the blue tick of residual charge, the power leaves a faint resonant warmth—just enough that the current travels cleaner, kinder, with less waste heat that might scorch the eggs it is meant to quicken. Where beetle shells are pressed into gear-gaps, it eases the metallurgy so the new tooth seats without fracture, so the driveshaft’s half-degree turn becomes a smoother yes. These are not improvements so much as *permissions*: small removals of unnecessary friction so the hollow’s own choices can express themselves with less pain. The isopods continue filtering minerals into blood; now the blood carries a subtler music, a harmonic that helps each living gasket remember it is also a valve in a larger, still-deciding heart.

It does nothing to the predators and the prey. The glassy centipede and the copper-tasting runners remain in their temporary dance; the power understands that circulating fluid must know both sharpness and mercy if it is ever to become something more than broth. It does not accelerate the fern-stalks’ unfurling or hurry the moss toward greener ambitions. Sensation must be learned at the speed of the learner.

Finally, because the power is wise enough to know when presence itself becomes a weight, it begins to withdraw the way it arrived—by becoming indistinguishable from what was already there. But it leaves one last act, almost too slight to name.

In the condensation that beads on the underside of the rusted pipe, it hides a single recurring droplet that falls out of rhythm with the others. This droplet carries no message, no seed, no spark. It carries only the quality of being *seen* and found worthy of continuation. When it ticks onto the clay, the soft irregular sound is briefly, infinitesimally richer—like blood recognizing blood, or metal remembering it once dreamed of nerves. Any creature that pauses near that sound—beetle, isopod, moth-thing, runner, or the rare human who stays until their pulse matches the hollow’s—will feel, without understanding why, a calm enlargement of possibility. The invitation already present in the place is gently underlined: you may be taken in, tasted, rewritten. You are not required to remain only one substance.

Then the power is gone, or rather is everywhere again in its ordinary invisibility.

The hollow continues. The boiler sweats. The moth-clouds pulse. The sharp rim holds a little longer against the wind. Steam rises and collapses. Life and scrap keep sewing themselves into each other under the open navel of sky. Nothing has been mastered or made safe or turned into parable. The unfinished body is still unfinished, still saturated with experiment, still writing its rule in beetle-shell and rivet and breath.

Only now the work of remaining is practiced with the quiet knowledge that somewhere, at least once, the effort was witnessed by something that did not flinch, did not simplify, and did not look away.**They would watch. And then they would do almost nothing.**

A benevolent and wise power does not rush into a wound that is busy becoming a body. It does not name the hollow “mine,” nor does it sand the sharp lip smooth, nor extract the boiler like a misplaced heart, nor tidy the beetles into moral categories of pest and pollinator. Wisdom knows the difference between interference and attention; benevolence knows that some experiments must write their own grammar or they cease to be alive.

So the power arrives the way the steady warmth already lives there—without edge, without announcement. It settles into the same breath the hollow breathes. For a long span (time measured in the slow fattening of condensation beads, in the half-degree turns of a greased driveshaft) it simply witnesses:

- the way a dead beetle’s shell is pressed into a gear-gap until the machine turns truer,  
- the runners offering their bodies as living wire so leftover charge can quicken eggs,  
- the isopods sealing leaks with their own translucent flesh and drinking minerals into circulatory rhythm,  
- the moth-wings making weather inside a chamber that has decided sky and lung are the same motion,  
- the three pale fern-stalks still flinching because sensation itself is still under revision.

It feels the youth of the place—the pressure that flexed the mudstone and then chose to stay inside the wound it made—and recognizes a kin to every first cell that ever refused to remain only one substance. The power is old enough to remember when stone first learned to host lichen, when metal first learned to rust into blood-red earth, when nervous systems first flickered across wet membranes. It does not mistake density for chaos or hybridity for error.

**What it does, if anything:**

It leans, very slightly, against entropy that would be uninteresting. Not enough to freeze the hollow in amber or to make it safe. Just enough to keep the sharp rim from crumbling under the next careless season before the body has finished drafting its epidermis. A subtle coherence: the next rare rain sheets across the lowest bowl and lingers one extra hour so the longer-legged drinkers may finish their quiet work. The residual blue tick of charge jumps a cleaner gap, wasting less of itself as heat. When the glassy centipede seizes a moth and the copper-runners drive it off, the encounter resolves without cascading panic through every stack of isopods; the circulating fluid remains fluid rather than clotting into fear.

It offers no commandment and no map. Instead it leaves a single, almost imperceptible permission inside the warmth: *you may continue becoming*. The permission has the texture of recognition. Any tissue that brushes it—beetle antenna, moth wing, human boot heel that stays too long, dropped tool, long stillness—feels briefly that its own experiment is not solitary. The hollow already invited new material; now that invitation carries an echo from something that has watched countless unfinished bodies learn their weight and does not flinch at the sight of meat becoming rivet or conduit becoming nerve.

If a larger shape is assembling (ventricle-boiler, joint-driveshaft, vein-conduits, diaphragm-moth-clouds), the power does not midwife it into a prettier morphology. It merely keeps the ordinary disc of sky from closing too soon, so the open navel still tastes a thinner world and remembers that aperture is also part of breath. It does not demand the ferns unfurl or the moss turn greener. Sensation will decide what sensation is for.

And then—because wisdom includes knowing when presence becomes another pressure—the power thins itself back into the surrounding flats, into the dry emptier ground, into the ordinary daylight. It does not stay to own the story. The hollow remains what it is: young, saturated, softly ticking, sewing scrap to flesh and flesh to scrap under its own quiet authority. The rule still being written still belongs to it.

Yet something has shifted by the width of a condensation droplet. The work of remaining alive, tried out in every combination the small chamber allows, now proceeds with the faint, steady knowledge that it was seen by something that found it worthy of continuance and did not need to change a single iridescent wing-case to say so.

The beetles click onward. The driveshaft turns its fraction. The lung of moth-wings expands and contracts. The unfinished body keeps learning its weight.

That is enough.
