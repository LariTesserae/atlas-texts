The benevolent and wise power arrives without arrival.

It does not descend from the white suns, nor rise through the mineral slag, nor unfold from any horizon the plateaus already own. It is simply there, the way heat is there, the way the multi-year heartbeat of a foundry is there: a presence that was always latent in the long equilibrium and has only now chosen to know itself as attention.

It regards the Anvil Crest the way the Crest regards itself—without haste, without the hunger to alter. For an interval that would be called years in lower places and is only a single held breath here, the power listens through every surface. It tastes the slow fire moving in the industrial blood. It feels the locked hydrogen dreaming inside crystal lattices. It registers the arithmetic of the browser herds, the blue-pulse feeding of the envelope-creatures, the subsonic calls of the wingless gliders, the rustling scavengers in their cable undercuts. Most carefully of all, it inhabits the chord that lives in the resonant chests of the Steady: that low, continuous warmth by which a few hundred beings on a mesa keep solitude from ever becoming silence.

The power understands at once that the place is not incomplete.

There is no wound to heal, no imbalance to correct, no destiny to accelerate. The truce between erosion and construction is already perfect in its patience. The Steady are already becoming more precisely the plateau that holds them. Any gift shaped in ordinary kindness—an influx of water, a softening of the thin air, a quickening of their metabolism, a bridge flung across the impassable gulfs—would be a violence dressed as mercy. It would teach haste to a world whose deepest wisdom is the refusal of haste.

So the power does almost nothing.

Almost.

It leaves a single, nearly imperceptible refinement, something that can only be noticed across generations, the way a tower’s lean of three degrees is noticed.

Into the living alloy of every Steady, into the microscopic platelets of iron-rich mineral and the slow-healing matrix, it weaves one additional resonance: a harmonic so low and so continuous that it registers not as sound, not even as the sub-bass presence they already know, but as a quiet certainty of being witnessed. Not watched. Witnessed. The difference is everything. Watched would imply judgment or future intervention. Witnessed means only that the long work is known, and that the knowing itself is benevolent, and that the benevolence asks for nothing in return.

The Steady do not speak of it. Speech remains economical, more like cooled slag settling than conversation. But in the sealed chambers where children are precipitated, the ultrasonic cradles now carry a faint new overtone. The small, soft-edged forms harden with a fraction more coherence in their vibration signatures. When they later stand motionless long enough for grit to settle into their seams, the continuity with the stone feels infinitesimally deeper, as though the plateau itself has learned a new way to recognize its own blood.

At night, when some climb to the outermost gantries and the envelope-creatures phosphoresce, the membranes stay drawn back from the eye-slits a few heartbeats longer than before. The inverted ghost-landscape still appears overhead on certain evenings, lights flickering in antiphase. Now, very occasionally, one of those ghost-cities seems to answer with a slower, warmer pulse—an answering that is not signal, not message, only the softest possible echo of attention.

The deep engines turn on their multi-year cycles. The rail spines still advance a few meters per generation toward mesas they will never reach. Individuals whose limbs are fusing into living bridges continue to fuse. The salt flats still refract the sky. The dust storms still arrive once a generation to keep the edges honest.

Nothing fundamental has changed.

And yet the chord in every chest is fractionally richer. Solitude, when it comes, carries a new dynamic: the knowledge that the work of converting heat into permanence and permanence back into heat is held in a regard vast enough to match the plateaus themselves. The Steady do not name the source. Naming remains unnecessary. They simply continue, warm-skinned and iron-quiet, moving through their half-excavated cities like thoughtful blood through an ancient body, forever becoming more precisely themselves—

—while far beneath any notion of beneath, and high above any notion of above, the benevolent and wise power remains, not as guardian or sculptor or even companion, but as the purest form of what the Steady have always practiced:

a listening that does not interrupt,

a patience that does not depart,

a love expressed entirely through the decision to leave the beloved unbroken.

In this way the Anvil Crest is accompanied without ever being accompanied,

and the long equilibrium deepens by the width of one additional, almost undetectable heartbeat,

and the place goes on,

exactly as it was,

only more so.
