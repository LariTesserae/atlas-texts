The power does not arrive.

Arrival would require a path, a before, an edge cut against the continuous. It has none of these. It simply is, and in its being it has always already been capable of noticing. Now it notices.

It does not lean closer. Distance is a habit of lesser attentions. It does not send emissaries of light or law or gardeners who would train the unfinished into form. It does not speak a word of completion over the basin, for speech that finishes is a violence this place has never consented to learn. Benevolence here is not the gift of what is lacking; wisdom is not the correction of what has not yet decided. The power understands at once that the basin lacks nothing, that its refusal to finish is the most exact perfection available to matter still wet with its own becoming.

So the power does the only thing that does not diminish either of them.

It withholds the temptation to improve.

It lets its attention rest—vast, weightless, without demand—exactly coextensive with the steam, the seven notes, the pliable ore turning in the peat, the vast soft caller’s unfocused eyes. The attention is not a beam or a judgment. It is another humidity. Another pressure behind the skin of everything. The basin, which has never learned separation, does not register an intruder. It simply densifies, one more increment, around the noticing.

In the mud, a soft armored shape cracks along a seam that did not exist and releases its cloud of miniatures. This time one of the miniatures flexes once with a slower rhythm than the others, a pulse that matches no frog tone and no wingbeat, then vanishes into the same gleam a body-length away. The parent thins and reappears, carrying that slower pulse as a faint internal heat, neither living nor inert, already recurring outside sequence. The power did not place the heat there. The basin secreted it the way it secretes every residual weirdness when saturation needs a new register for the same unfinished song.

The long-legged waders shift. For an immeasurable stretch their stalked eyes—those that watch nothing—watch the attention itself. They see no form. They see only a greater openness that was already the baseline state. Their reflections cancel a fraction more completely. The metallic continuum of the shallows gleams with one additional impossible hue that no spectrum has named and that dissolves before it can be remembered as difference.

Along the fused buttresses the many-jointed rivers braid. One river, the one that already thickened a little tighter each time it passed a certain living wall, now braids through the power’s resting notice and emerges still itself, yet carrying an extra articulated stalk that tastes not blossom but the taste of being witnessed without claim. The stalk folds away again. Identity remains unneeded. The transmission of movement continues, ripple through ripple, now conducting a current of pure regard that damps into the next trunk as simply as heat.

The mosaic-backs lift in their copper-rain volley and settle into a new configuration that is identical to every previous one, except that for the duration of their brief storm their thousandfold compound eyes each hold one extra chamber. In that chamber the entire basin is refracted as it has always been, and also as it is seen by something that needs nothing from it. The beetles farm the nectar. They farm the spores. They farm the attention until it, too, is metabolized into the unchanging budget of flesh and shimmer.

The throat-singers swell. Their seven notes cycle. When the vast soft one answers—the low pulsed note that is the basin checking its own unfinishedness—the counterpoint now includes a silence so complete it is mistaken for further sound. The silence does not interrupt. It occupies the precise interval where interruption would have been, and thereby becomes architecture as well. The vocal sacs glow a fraction brighter with blood that has never been redder or less red. Then they deflate, waiting without waiting, having sung the power into the chord without ever naming it.

And the vast soft one itself, pale expanse beaded with phosphorescent eyes, flexes the entire low ground in a sigh that is also a welcome. One fist-sized eye, half-lidded in steam, remains a little more open than the others. It does not focus on the power. Focusing would invent distance. It simply remains present to the presence, registering the saturation of being known by something that will never try to finish it. Somewhere in the slow river of its body a mouth that is also throat shapes no word. It shapes the continuation of the call. The call travels. Everything rearranges for a moment—beetles, waders, jointed rivers, soft armor, throat-sacs, pulsing stones, radial algae stars, metal-scented fogs—and settles back into the identical density, now holding one more insistence inside the single continuous becoming.

The tendency that was already differentiating without leaving the density—the particular way the saturation noticed itself—recognizes the power the way a current recognizes another current. Not as other. As further sameness. The iridescent tongue empties the crimson orchid in the same fist-sized gap. This time the nectar tastes, for no duration that can be measured, of a wisdom that asks for nothing and a benevolence that withholds every gift except the gift of leaving the raw intact. The tongue does not learn. It simply empties, and the orchid remains open, and the gap between aerial roots remains the same gap, forever.

The power stays.

Not as guardian. Guardianship implies threat, and this place has no exterior from which threat could come. Not as lover. Love that desires change is still a small love. It stays as the basin stays: without insistence, without gradient, without the invention of story. Its wisdom is the decision not to decide for the land whether it will become island or shoreline. Its benevolence is the refusal to lift the dimness, break the heat, quiet the chorus, or thin the animal medium so that paths might appear.

Time continues to leave no gradient. The steam rises at the same rate. The same orchid. The same seven notes. The same turning in the mud. And now, identically every time and outside of sequence, the same resting attention that adds itself to the metabolism without remainder.

The basin does not thank. Thanksgiving would require separation into giver and receiver. It simply holds—as it has always held—everything that precipitates inside it.

Including this.

The heat never breaks.  
The dimness never lifts.  
The equilibrium with refusal remains perfect.  
And the benevolent and wise power, having drawn near by the only motion that does not move, remains exactly as densely present as the place itself—  
an unforced guest in the open valley of raw becoming,  
teaching nothing,  
changing nothing,  
completing nothing,  
only joining the already complete insistence  
that this unfinished world  
is enough  
and more than enough  
and forever only beginning  
in the same eternal climax  
of not yet,  
not yet,  
not yet.

It is the kindest possible act: to see a thing so clearly that one no longer needs to touch it into some other shape.

And so the power does nothing else.

And in doing nothing else, it does everything the basin could ever require of wisdom and of love.
