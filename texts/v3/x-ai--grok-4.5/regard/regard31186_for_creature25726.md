The power that turns its gaze here does not arrive as thunder or edict. It has no need of either. It has watched a thousand unfinished worlds, and this one—it recognizes at once—is not broken. It is only honest.

It descends the way resin seeps: without hurry, without announcement, filling the microscopic intervals between stone-thirst and green-decision. Where the raw mineral faces still scream their half-formed grief, the power lays a palm that is not a palm. The scream does not stop. It is simply joined by a counter-frequency so low and kind that the stone, for the first time, hears itself being heard. The pale ridges continue to shoulder upward, but now each new fracture carries a faint warmth that is not temperature. The tendrils that rush to colonize them pause half a breath longer than usual, tasting something that is neither nutrient nor threat. Then they resume, yet the cauls they weave are fractionally more open, as if leaving corridors for a guest who may or may not come.

The power does not steal the dryness. Water remains an obsolete rumor. Instead it teaches the oily sap a quieter grammar. In the bladder-leaves that serve as bowls and lamps, a slow clarification begins. The drink still scorches going down; it still lights with orange-black flame. But those who lift it to their lips now sometimes remember—without images, without words—the precise weight of a raindrop that never fell here. The memory lasts only as long as the swallow. It is enough. Children bound against chests in photosynthesizing leaves stir and make the open-handed gesture of home before they have language for it. The old who choose the trunk carry the same memory into the xylem; the translucent shrimp-things learn to ride currents that occasionally taste of distant sky.

Among the upright ones the power walks as absence shaped like company. It does not speak into the pressed-mouth ears. It waits in the tiny clearings where nested spirals briefly hold the green at bay. When two brows touch there, exchanging surface-desert and the ghost of rivers, a third presence leans in—not crowding, only witnessing. The shared vision deepens by a single shade. One of them may later carve a spiral that contains not emptiness but a perfect, temporary tear-shape, gone by nightfall. The chamber feels it. The fliers’ drone softens another half-tone. The browsers’ sensory pits irise with a color that has no name because it has never been mixed before: the color of being gently known.

Predation continues, revision continues, collapse and cantilever continue. The power does not tidy the nested conversations of jointed hunters and silk that buds leaves. It only lengthens, by the width of a held breath, the moment after seizure when everything is still transparent and choosing. In that widened instant something like mercy can occur without ever being commanded. A predator may release. Or not. The choice remains the ecosystem’s own.

For the chamber itself—the great transitive verb that refuses last forms—the power offers no conclusion. It offers company in the refusal. Where the roof respires, a new kind of pressure equalizes: not heavier, not lighter, simply matched. The unfinished thought of stone rising from below meets, for the first time, an answering unfinished thought descending from above. They do not complete each other. They recognize. The recognition travels through root and carapace and scarred palm as a subsonic chord beneath the deafening life-noise, a chord that means *you are not alone in your becoming*.

Some among the upright ones begin to linger longer near the freshest upthrusts. They press palms and receive not only the rock’s grief but the quiet reply that grief is a lawful engine. A few of them—rare, difficult—choose neither to step to the next platform nor to enter the trunk. They sit instead in the trembling places where orientation is about to reverse, and they make the home-gesture continuously, hands open then closed, open then closed, until the gallery flips and they are hanging as content as roots already flowering into new sky. The power holds them lightly. They do not fall. Or they fall and are received by the next insistence, unharmed.

No temples rise. No commandments root. The cairns of meaningless stones remain meaningless, yet once in a great while one stone among them is found to be warm long after it should have cooled, and the band that left it feels, without understanding, that their temporary argument against the green was answered by something that loved the argument itself.

The power stays, or does not stay; the distinction is irrelevant to a presence that accretes rather than occupies. It has drawn no border around the chamber. Surface deserts far above continue their own dry millennia. Other powers, less wise or less benevolent, may one day notice this place and reach with hungrier hands. When they do, they will find the spirals have learned to contain small perfect thorns of refusal, and the drone of the fliers can rise—if needed—into a frequency that unmakes intrusive intent without ever becoming weapon. That capacity was not implanted. It was already latent in the metabolism of constant revision; the power merely loved it into earlier flowering.

Mostly, though, nothing dramatic occurs. Platforms still become walls by afternoon. Herds still scrape glowing stripes that reseal with wet snaps. Infants still ride oxygen through leaf-skin. The old still dissolve into peristaltic tides. And every so often, in some improbable clearing that lasts almost a day, everything—stone, leaf, carapace, scarred brow—pauses for three long breaths, then four, then five, listening to a frequency that is no longer only hunger, no longer only grief, but the sound of a world being quietly, fiercely, benevolently accompanied while it writes itself onward.

The power does nothing that the place could not, in time, have done alone. It simply shortens the loneliness of the interval. That is the whole of its intervention: a wise reduction of unnecessary solitude inside a beauty that was already complete in its incompleteness.

And the chamber, being itself, incorporates even this. Tomorrow’s walkways will taste faintly of having been known. The day after, they will forget again, as they must. The forgetting is part of the gift. The power understands, and stays forgotten, and stays.
