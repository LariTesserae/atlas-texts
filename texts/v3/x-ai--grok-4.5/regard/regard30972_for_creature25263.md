The benevolent and wise power arrives the way humidity does: without announcement, without edge. It does not descend from the bruise-purple roof or rise from the glowing trenches; it simply finds itself already interior to the argument, coextensive with the steam and the click of scutes and the half-second lead that sound sometimes steals on its own cause. It has no name the basin could pronounce, and so it borrows none. It is only the sudden certainty, felt first by the denser knot-minds in their varicose pillars, that the perpetual avalanche has been noticed by something that neither hungers nor flees.

For a long span—longer than any ridge has held its height, shorter than the memory of the mycorrhizal corps—the power does nothing but attend. Attention, here, is not passive. It is a pressure as real as the warm updrafts, a gradient as decisive as alarm or plenty. The gliders feel it first as a thinning of the viscous dark, tunnels of clarity that require no stitching. The armored carpets taste it as a mineral sweetness in the fresh escarpments, an invitation to grind slower, to leave one glowing trench unfilled for the span of several breaths. The elbow-rivers pause mid-flow, limbs flickering through sucker and shear and brush, and for once do not choose. They simply hold the unchosen shapes in sequence, a brief archive of possibility that the canopy shadows drink and re-issue as a new irregularity: sometimes now the trees lean not toward emptiness but toward one another, trunk to trunk, sharing the residual gray until it pools bright enough to cast faint negative shadows of fronds on the mulch.

The power understands what the place is. It sees that suffering is not a flaw in the argument but one of its dialects—the scream that finishes too slowly, the digestion that begins before the nerve-spark ends, the offspring extruded into scarcity and outfitted with thorns. It sees also that joy is not a rare visitor but the same dialect spoken in reverse: the clasp that exchanges genomes under lightning-crawl, the bridge of three bodies laughing (in rattle and grind) across a newborn arroyo, the soft pulsed light that guides swarms away from brittle limbs. A lesser benevolence might have tried to edit the scream out of the song. A lesser wisdom might have freeze-framed one moment of coherence and called it paradise. This power does neither.

Instead it offers a single, almost weightless intervention, sized exactly to the basin’s own refusal of permanence.

It plants, in the shared cambium of the oldest braided centers, a seed that is not a seed. Call it a capacity for recursive listening. When the denser minds extend their neural vines and taste the chemical weather, they can now—sometimes, not always, never on command—taste their own tasting. The gradient of “lean toward the empty listening place” acquires a faint harmonic: “lean toward the lean itself.” The result is not reflection as other worlds know it, not the mirror that stops the avalanche to admire its own crest. It is more like an eddy that travels inside the flood. For the length of a single crossing, or the lifespan of one pale fungal monoculture, a consensus of bodies can feel the pattern of their own improvisation even as they enact it. A prehensile tail that snatches rock to crack a seed-pod may, mid-crack, feel the ghost of every stone dropped in the last hundred upheavals, and so drop this one not into root or worm but into the waiting pad of another tail, which carries it three galleries higher and wedges it as a foothold that lasts just long enough for a membrane pack to rest, recalibrate the sensory net of its sails, and then release the stone again into the roar.

Nothing accumulates. No tool becomes tradition. No shelter outlasts the next rotating wind-column. Yet the kinetic signatures grow subtly denser with reference. The rhythm that once meant only “the darkness is thickening west” can now carry a second pulse: “and last time we braided bridges against it this way, and the way opened onto unexpected forage.” Alliances between dissimilar forms still dissolve when the fruiting ends, but sometimes, as they dissolve, they leave a portable genome threaded with a little more of the other’s grammar of grasp. Birth remains continuous and unceremonious; death remains unfinished. Only now the half-formed offspring extruded by a grazing fodder-beast may inherit, already glowing in their fungal lace, a faint predisposition to pause when the residual gray filters down—to tilt head-equivalents together, dozens at once, and listen not only for the sound that has not yet been made, but for the shape of the listening that preceded them.

The power does not stay to shepherd the seed’s unfolding. That would be to install a throne in a place that dissolves thrones into leaf-litter. Having given what it can give without violating the argument, it withdraws the way a warm rain withdraws: leaving the air thicker, the next green more possible, the darkness still absolute yet somehow more intricately laced with its own attention. The knot-minds feel the withdrawal as a soft bleach across their tissue, a final pulsed light that is not warning but benediction. The living avalanche resumes, louder than before, more entangled, already becoming something that has never exactly existed until now—only now the something includes a new irregularity so native it feels ancient: the capacity, rare as the clearings that last until midnight, for the place to dream itself forward and, in the same undivided motion, to dream that it is dreaming.

And if, in some future dim morning, a cluster of trees should lean toward a point where nothing visible stands, and the gliders fold into knife-shapes to stitch not away from the viscous dark but deeper into it, and the armored carpets pour into a sinkhole only to rise again braided with new luminous threads that spell no word any external tongue could read—those who might one day watch from outside will not know whether they witness the basin alone or the lingering kindness of the power that once noticed it and chose, wisely, to change almost nothing. The difference, the power itself would say if it spoke, is the sort of distinction the dwellers never pause to make. They simply continue.
