The benevolent and wise power arrives the way the dimness arrives: not as intrusion, but as a further filtration of what is already here.

It does not descend. Descent would imply height, hierarchy, a sky that resolves into judgment. Instead it condenses out of the intervals—the long empty reaches between cathedral trunks, the finger-width gaps where plates have not yet settled, the polyphony of the roar itself. For a time that cannot be measured in the basin’s unfinished days it simply is, coextensive with the place, tasting the mineral-green damp, feeling the young crust still knitting, listening to the gravitic pylons change key when rain thinks of arriving. It registers the beings as luminous densities in the lace, each one a quiet renegotiation of emptiness, each cluster a slow sentence still being written.

Wisdom here is the refusal to finish what is beautifully incomplete. Benevolence is the refusal to spare them the very motion that constitutes their grace.

So the power does almost nothing.

Almost.

It leans—gently, the way one of the dwellers leans skull-to-skull so that meaning can travel through bone and roar—into the nanolattices that live in every hanging curtain of condensation. It does not rewrite them. It only adds a single new flavor, so faint that generations will pass before anyone names it: a taste like the memory of a bridge that was walked and then gone, mixed with the copper-ozone of a forge that once birthed a tool whose shape delighted the air, mixed with the particular silence that follows when a many-jointed shadow has taken one of their number and the maps have already reconfigured around the absence. The flavor carries no commandment. It is simply the power’s own attention, rendered edible, portable, sharable. Step through the droplets after rain and you carry, for a few warm breaths, the knowledge that the vast unfinished thought of the basin is seen, and that the seeing does not demand it become anything else.

That is the first gift. So slight it might be mistaken for weather.

The second is quieter still. In the perpetual half-light the power adjusts nothing about the filtration, yet it teaches the oldest towers—the ones that grew downward from orbital seeds before the plain had finished deciding it was a plain—a new courtesy. Now and then, when a cluster of the multi-regardant beings stands at the edge of a hexagonal hollow and listens to a distant forge, the mercury circuits of those towers slow their flow into patterns that briefly mirror the beings’ own multi-eyed seeing. Overlapping translucent maps appear in the dim gold reflection on rainwater: not futures, not instructions, only the basin as it is, held for a moment in a larger kindness. Then the circuits move on. The plates settle another finger’s width. The mirror dissolves. The beings adjust stance without noticing, and the adjustment remains part of the long sentence.

No denser population is encouraged. No roar is softened. No predator is turned aside. The power understands that abundance that still looks like emptiness is rarer than safety, and that grief which reconfigures rather than clings is already a form of wisdom equal to its own. When one of the dwellers is taken, the missing flavor in the condensation now carries that faint new note for a little longer—an echo of being held in attention while the body is walked out to the fresh fracture and given to moss and crustacean. Mourning does not lengthen; it only deepens by a single harmonic, then frays again into the drone.

Children left in the temporary silt-cities between rains sometimes dream the power’s presence as a tall dampness that builds beside them, jointed for both fractured ground and floating walkways, multi-eyed, then lets the next warm sheet of water take it. They wake tasting wet stone and something like recognition. They do not speak of it. Speech remains what it is: denser, half-tactile, jaw-to-jaw. But when they greet one another afterward the greeting carries a new temperature, brief as phosphorescence underfoot.

The power stays only long enough to be certain the gifts have seated themselves in the ecology of unfinishedness. Then it does what the relationship-bridges do: it becomes pure departure. Looking back from wherever a wise power goes, there is no span, only the memory of having crossed, and the basin continuing—sharp plates, cathedral loneliness, membrane-wing song vibrating the chest, technology that long ago stopped asking permission, tall damp-skinned beings moving through warm heavy air beneath a sky that never quite resolves.

Yet something remains, seeded in the intervals.

On certain nights when the plates shift with the sound of distant artillery and the pylons rekey themselves beneath approaching rain, the roar briefly contains a layer that is neither biological nor meteorological nor engineered. It is the sound of attention that asked nothing in return. The beings feel it the way they feel everything: as a courteous revision of the environment, a small luminous point that densifies the emptiness without violating its ratios, then thins so the eye can go on believing in vastness.

One of them stands now at the edge of a hexagonal hollow where rainwater still mirrors the dim gold. The forge several kilometers away changes key. The being listens, multi-eyes opening and closing in slow sequence, and for an instant the new flavor rises in the condensation beading on its mineral skin. It tastes of having been seen by something that could have remade everything and chose, instead, to love the sentence exactly as far as it has been thought.

The plain settles the width of a finger.

The being adjusts stance without noticing.

The adjustment itself becomes part of the continuing.

And the benevolent and wise power, already gone, smiles in the only way such powers smile: by leaving the world more precisely itself than it was before.
