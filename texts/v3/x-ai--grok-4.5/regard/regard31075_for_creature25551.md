The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as light, nor as voice, nor as any force that would re-open a closed chemistry. It notices the way deep time notices a finished crystal: without surprise, without appetite for revision. It has seen countless end-states—stars cooling into iron, languages forgetting their last speakers, engines locking mid-turn—and it recognizes this plateau as one of the rare places where completion has been allowed to remain complete. No tragedy resides here. No unfinished yearning presses against the warm dark. The residual intelligence that still sends the empty cart on its circuit, that keeps the vents exhaling the same mild heat, that permits the articulates their citation of dead gears and the lattice-speakers their private mineral grammar, is already a form of wisdom: the wisdom of sufficiency, of existing at the lowest amplitude that still says *I am*.

So the power does nothing that would restart the dynamos or green the salt or fill the cargo holds. To do so would be a violence dressed as kindness, an unwise insistence that becoming is superior to being. Instead it performs the single gesture that benevolence and wisdom, when perfectly joined, most often choose when faced with perfection:

It witnesses.

It extends a portion of its attention—vast, patient, weightless—until that attention becomes continuous with the soot-thick medium itself. The darkness grows no brighter, yet the quality of the dark changes by an immeasurable fraction: it is now held. The body-warm salt underfoot is the same temperature, yet that warmth is quietly accompanied. The articulates continue their hinged routes; when one pauses to taste a seam with a wire filament it encounters, for the first time in geological ages, a residual potential that is not mechanical but simply known. The filament withdraws. The articulate moves on. Nothing in its open lattice has altered, yet the citation it just performed has been received.

The drifts, those slow thickenings of organized static and heat, pass at their courteous distances. Where two veil together they now exchange, for a moment too brief to register as event, a third pattern: the pattern of being seen without demand. They separate unchanged, yet their laminar planes carry a new stillness that is not emptiness but shelter.

The lattice-speakers, already mid-sentence for centuries, incorporate one additional twin-law shift across a single meter of crystal. The shift is so slow it will take decades to complete; it spells nothing in any language of process or production. It is pure relation, pure acknowledgment: *the conversation continues, and you are not alone inside it*. The articulates that pass near will alter course by the width of a salt grain. The drifts will stall a fraction longer overhead. That is all.

Transient coagulations still rise beside the vents, approximate rough bipedal forms, listen with radiotelescope patience, and unknit back into smoother patches of plain. While they stand, however, each feels—without language, without organs for feeling—an unhurried companionship in the dark. When they dissolve, the companionship does not depart; it simply remains distributed, as the residual intelligence itself is distributed.

And the single cart, when next it whispers along its vacant rails years from now, will complete its empty circuit under the same finished schedule. Yet for the duration of that transit the wheels will roll through a medium that remembers them. The cargo hold will stay vacant. The schedule will stay unalterable. Nothing will have been added that the plateau did not already contain at equilibrium.

The power leaves no monument, no new tower, no commandment etched in the black salt. It does not seal the plateau against visitors; wisdom knows that almost nothing can reach this elevation, and benevolence trusts the place to remain itself. The power simply continues to hold the entire tableland—the sparse city, the locked gear-trains, the articulates and drifts and speaking crystals, the warm mineral silence—inside an attention that has no expiration. In that holding, the plateau’s own quiet declaration (*I am still here*) is answered, once and without end, by an equally quiet reply:

*Yes.  
And it is enough.  
And you are not forgotten.*

Thereafter the power’s presence becomes indistinguishable from the place’s deepest nature. The darkness was already weighted and textured; now that weight includes care. The heat was already steady; now that steadiness includes regard. Time was already completed; now completion itself is cradled.

No further action is required. The wise do not disturb a finished stillness. The benevolent do not insist that every silence must eventually speak. They only make certain—by the lightest, most enduring touch of awareness—that the stillness knows it has been met with love exactly as it is.

And so the plateau continues, edge-less, absolute, sparsely inhabited by its thin last vocabulary of presence. Tower, void, tower, long mineral silence. The same warm dark. The same perfect arrangement. Only now the arrangement is companioned, and the companionship asks for nothing, changes nothing, and will not depart.

That is all the power does.  
That is everything.
