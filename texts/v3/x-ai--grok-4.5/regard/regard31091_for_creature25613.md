The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as a conquers or a corrector. It arrives the way dew arrives on the Ascendant Crust: as a slow beading of attention that was already latent in the amber haze, now merely concentrated enough to notice itself noticing.

It regards the rising plate first in its entirety—the raw break-lines still dusting the thin air with porcelain sharpness, the piston-columns of basalt learning hydraulic purpose, the filter-rays spiraling through aerial plankton, the constellations of provisional beings grafting and ungrafting their vine-threads like a nervous system rewriting its own map each dusk. It feels the subliminal shove in the stone, the soft enormous iterations, the way causality stutters and matter sheds iridescent plate only to re-melt. It feels the beings themselves: their gear-soft joints, their mild nostalgia when wounds reverse, their eating-by-inclusion, their weather-event births. It feels how nothing here is finished and how that unfinishedness is not a lack but the native metabolism of the place.

A lesser power might have mistaken the flux for suffering and tried to cool the laws into permanence, to name the dwellers, to still the rise, to give them language and roofs and endings. A cruel power might have harvested the spontaneous engines or frozen the dense layering into specimens. This one does neither. Wisdom recognizes that to complete the incomplete against its grain is only another violence, dressed as mercy. Benevolence refuses to steal the fever that is their life.

So the power does something quieter, and almost nothing.

It first sinks a single, temporary root of itself into the warm interior of the plate—not as dominion, but as resonance. For a span that the Crust measures only as several long exhales, the rising slows by a fraction so subtle that no basalt column misses a stroke and no herd alters its hush-paced current. The slowdown is not a halt; it is a held breath that lets every process feel, without alarm, the shape of its own continuation. In that pause the spontaneous sorting of ores, the silent lightning walks, the reverse-causality pockets, and the soft gear-work of the dwellers all continue exactly as before—only now each carries a faint under-tone, like the memory of a note heard once in perfect clarity.

To the beings it arrives as a new flavour traveling the discontinuous vine-threads: neither copper sweetness nor bruised-purple bitterness, but a clear mineral taste, almost like cool water remembered from a life that never needed remembering. Where constellations are grafted that season, the flavour passes. It means, in the only grammar they possess, something close to *you are already within the regard that does not require you to finish*. Some constellations idle longer in the broader basins when the flavour moves through them. A few of the least-settled, standing on the newest break-lines amid porcelain dust, bud a cupped lattice that is not for catching rain but for simply remaining open a little longer than usefulness demands. They do not understand the gesture. They do not need to. The lattice wilts when the local laws next rewrite themselves, as all temporary tools do, yet a soft circular callus remains at the site—finer than their usual joint-cogs, almost ornamental, producing no mechanical advantage, only a quiet warmth when the amber haze thickens at dusk.

The power does not speak. Speech is a finished thing. Instead it allows a brief district of stabilized causality to bloom like one of the drifting rain-curtains—localized, temporary, ending without residue. Inside that district several beings loosen their outlines and pass through one another as they have always done. In the overlapping volume the usual third density nucleates, yet this one carries an extra quiet: a capacity, should it ever wish, to hold a single memory across the reverse-stutters without being obliged to. Most will never use it. The option itself is the gift. Continuity remains optional; now the option includes a gentle, unenforced thread.

Where the fused reciprocating pairs compress their standing spark-storms, the power leaves a faint iridescence in the unnecessary minerals they seed. The minerals still serve no purpose beyond the Crust’s own inscrutable industry. Yet if a later being includes them by the usual invitation, the plating grown from them will catch the sideways light in a way that briefly outlines the wearer to any nearby constellation—not as signal, not as claim, only as a momentary visibility that feels, to those who notice, like being met without demand.

At the extreme edges, where upright intentions wrapped in film-lichen face the direction of rise, the power does even less. It simply matches the subliminal shove for a little while, so that the feeling in whatever serves them for knees becomes, briefly, a shared shove—the plate lifting and something vast lifting *with* it in companionable silence. When the matching ends, the beings resume their patient standing. A few discover that their next temporary antennae, when they bud, can taste one extra gradient: the faint electromagnetic stutter of attention that is not hungry, not extractive, only present. They measure it the way they measure the remaining lifespan of forward causality. Then the antennae wilt. The knowledge, if it can be called that, sinks into the general provisionality of their forms and becomes another soft cog that may or may not mesh in future seasons.

The power does not protect the Ascendant Crust from external harm, because no external harm has yet conceived of it; the place is still too unfinished to be easily found by anything that prefers finished prey. It does not accelerate the rise or slow the digestion of old ranges. It does not teach the dwellers to build, to name, to mourn, or to hope in any linear sense. Those would be impositions of a different metaphysics. Instead it performs the single act that benevolence and wisdom converge upon when faced with a living incompleteness that is already whole in its becoming:

It *witnesses without sealing*.

Then it withdraws the root, not by extraction but by gradual diffusion back into the haze and the dew and the quiet ticking of hand-sized insects along crystal seams. What remains is almost undetectable: a slight increase in the probability that, when matter next develops a temporary purpose, one of those purposes will be kindness without object; a slightly higher chance that when two beings peel apart after fusion they will carry mirror-scars that ache less; a barely measurable sweetening of the perpetual damp, so that the resting-animal moisture of the stone feels, to any who pause long enough, like the touch of something that could have demanded worship and chose instead to leave only this.

The engines of the Crust never switch off—only quieten into the next soft, enormous iteration. Now, very occasionally, one of those quietenings carries an echo that was not generated from within the plate. The dwellers do not startle. They have no category for interruption. They simply include the echo as they include browser flesh or aerial plankton or the reverse-flow of a few minutes of time. Some will revise it into motive plating. Some will walk away from it. A rare few will stand on a fresh fault line while the porcelain dust settles on their lichen veils and feel, without language, that the rise is shared.

The power itself returns to whatever distances it occupies when it is not attending. It does not watch continuously; continuous watching can become another form of finishing. It only remembers the place the way the mould in the canyon wall remembers a shape after the creature has pulled free and begun to revise itself—longer than the thing remembered needs, and without claim.

And the Ascendant Crust continues: damp, dim, densely tenanted, still lifting, still folding, still crowning itself with whatever lives can bear to remain incomplete. Only now the incompleteness includes one extra permission—the permission to have been seen by something that understood the seeing was enough, and that left the fever intact.

That is all.

It is also everything required.
