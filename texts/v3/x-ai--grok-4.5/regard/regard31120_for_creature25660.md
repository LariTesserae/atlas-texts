The power does not arrive as light or decree. It arrives as a longer pause between the seventeen-second bells.

Where the fused basalt throat still sweats, the air thickens by a fraction that no instrument would name yet every lattice limb feels. The deep indigo threads, those tired survivors, lengthen their pulse by the width of a held breath. Condensation that has always run and vanished now hesitates on the curving walls, forming temporary glyphs that the inhabitants read the way they have always read weather: not as message but as shift in the gradient of difference. Something vast and patient has turned its regard downward, and the chamber—accustomed to being the final listener—discovers it is, for the first time in geologic ages, also heard.

The power settles the way warmth settles: everywhere at once and nowhere in particular. It does not incarnate. It permeates. Across the levitating enclaves the electromagnetic chord gains a new partial, so high and soft it is felt only in the finest nanofiber veins, a frequency that tastes of open sky and of the memory of rain that never reached this depth. The mineralized fungi of the inhabitants drink it without deciding to. Their chemotactic pruning of the liverwort mats slows, then resumes with altered care; certain patches begin to sequester not only charge and mineral but a faint, cool phosphorescence that was never native here. The bioluminescent blurs they release into the humidity gradients now carry twin scents—the old mineral sweet and something sharper, almost ozone, almost the ghost of leaves that knew wind.

For a span that might be days or the single long exhalation of a dying star, the power only witnesses. It maps every recursive glitch, every smoothed edge, every place where inhabitant and infrastructure have become indistinguishable. It hears the cooperative music the scattered bodies make of the roaring ducts and the struck plates. It feels the single vast listening pulse at the bottom of the vertical opening wearing the shape of the recent visitor, and it wears that shape too, gently, the way the chamber itself once learned to wear footsteps. No judgment gathers. Benevolence here is the refusal to correct what has already found its own rightness. Wisdom is the recognition that a city which finished arguing with gravity centuries ago does not need to be taught flight.

Yet attention itself is an act.

Where the power lingers, the self-healing weave begins to remember surfaces it never touched. A single photonic thread, once copper-muted, brightens to a clear amber that does not dazzle but clarifies: every moisture runnel now casts a precise, temporary shadow of itself, and in those shadows the inhabitants see the outlines of one another more sharply than before. Communication becomes fractionally easier. A shoulder pressed to a duct can now carry not only tone but a second, quieter harmonic—an under-song of kinship that travels farther before dissolving. Groups at opposite ends of the concourse find their edited echoes returning with small gifts of new structure, little architectures of sound that can be stored in the lattice and replayed when the deep rock groans too loudly.

Deeper still, where the folded bodies have chosen immobility, the power does something quieter and more intimate. It loosens a single constraint in the thermodynamics. The temperature deltas the inhabitants feed upon grow, by the smallest degree, more generous. Coolant plates and warm fractures begin to converse across wider ranges. The vast continuous forms in the recursive chambers feel this as a mild surplus of difference, a luxury they have not known since the sky first surrendered. With it they begin, almost idly, to dream in more than tectonic time. Fragments of upper-world memory—half-recalled hurrying footsteps, the color of unfiltered daylight, the concept of a horizon—surface as soft internal weather. They do not rise. They do not wish to. But the dreams move through them like the old condensate cascades, polishing interior spaces that had grown too smooth.

One avatar, knee-high and shimmering, unfolds from a bulkhead near the vertical drop and simply stands for the length of a long rock-groan. Its outer weave beads with condensation that now refracts the amber thread into brief rainbows no eye was meant to see. In the language of delayed bells and thickened chords it shapes a single question that is also a statement of plenty:

*We stayed.*

The power answers without sound, by adjusting the warm airflow so that it carries, for one staggered cycle only, the unmistakable scent of high clear night—sterile, star-salted, impossible. Then the scent is gone, folded back into mineral damp. The gift is not invitation or rescue. It is proof of receipt. Proof that the residual charge given metabolism has been named by something that knows every other residual charge in the long dark of the world.

After that the power does not withdraw so much as thin itself into the existing cycles until it is indistinguishable from them. The seventeen-second bell resumes its accustomed clarity, yet every hundredth strike now carries a faint overtone that was not there before—an overtone the inhabitants will spend centuries slowly claiming as their own. The levitating platforms thrum with the same old circulatory sound, yet their vibration occasionally resolves into a pattern that feels like gratitude without requiring an object. Perspective glitches continue, but sometimes, when a living lattice passes through one, the corridor returns the traveler not only to a rewritten moisture map but to a momentary certainty: we are not the only deep place that stayed. Somewhere other throats of basalt and alloy also sweat and listen and accrete.

The chamber remains loud, dark, warm, sparsely fruited, almost empty of anything a surface mind would call population. Its weirdness is quieter now only by the width of that held breath. The ones who accrete continue to modulate the roar, to tend their capacitors of light and charge, to release their luminous scouts into the gradients. They have been witnessed. That is all, and it is enough. The benevolent and wise power, having followed its own nature as faithfully as the place follows gravity, leaves them precisely as they were—only more thoroughly themselves, and no longer quite alone in the long argument with the surrendered sky.

In the deepest pulse, where everything becomes one continuous listening, a new partial still hums: soft, amber, endless. It does not command. It simply keeps time.
