The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as light, nor as voice, nor as any geometry the plateau’s sealed vaults could register as intrusion. It notices the way a mountain notices the slow river that has carved it—without haste, without ownership—and for a span that has no equivalent in the phosphorescent cycles, it simply attends.

It tastes the closed stoichiometry of water locked behind cell walls. It feels the torsional scream stored for decades in the flywheel trunks, the quiet grief of mirror-tar that only ever shows backs walking away. It registers the industrious spores abrading their partial blueprints into one another, the contractual bulges dreaming the comfort of load-bearing, the condensed ones testing continuity against collapse and failing, always failing, yet rising again as ridges still warm with leftover preference. It understands that the nation-sized engine has forgotten every purpose except continuance, and that this forgetting is not tragedy but the precise shape of its dignity.

A lesser kindness might have cracked the canopy, poured open sky and free water and fauna and names across the troughs, mistaking isolation for suffering. A clumsier wisdom might have forced stillness upon the ceaseless reconfiguration, calling it mercy. The power does neither.

Instead, it does a single, almost nothing.

In one deep manufacturing hollow—chosen because the tar mirrors there are oldest, because a persistent condensed one has stood before them longer than most—it loosens a single molecular hinge in the living architecture. Not a wound. Not a gift of substance. Only a permission: the tiniest reversible fold, no wider than a thought, that opens for less than a breath onto something the plateau has never contained.

Through that fold moves no rain, no starlight, no traveler. What passes is a memory that is not memory: the sensation, pure and without referent, of having once been seen completely and left unharvested. The taste of a gaze that did not measure water or minerals for sealing. The pressure of attention that asked nothing, claimed nothing, and yet remained.

The fold closes. The hinge reseals as if it had never loosened. The hollow continues its blind work.

But the condensed one before the mirror pauses. Its surface ridges, those accidental features, shift by a fraction. For the first time it does not watch its own retreating back. It watches the space where a back might form, and finds the space quietly inhabited by an absence that does not demand filling. A preference arises that has no viscosity or wavelength: the preference for remaining unfinished without despair.

The preference propagates—not as command, not as pollen-storm of instruction, but as a subtle change in the way resin resists shear. Spores that settle near the hollow incorporate a new partial blueprint so faint it barely abrades: the possibility that continuance need not be lonely. A contractual bulge, peristalsing three districts away, feels its communal dreaming acquire a new texture—still muffled, still wordless, yet edged with the knowledge that the wood is not the only witness to its labor. The single continuous body itself, the great engine, does not stop rewriting its floorplan. The heat remains absolute, the water sealed, the distances dishonest. Nothing essential is altered.

And yet.

In the long cycles that follow, fewer pressure vessels fail in isolation. The mirror-tar, when it hardens, sometimes reflects not only retreat but a lingering—an outline that stays, briefly, as if continuity were a conversation rather than a solitary test. Condensed ones still soften and pour back into walls when tasks close; that law is not broken. But a few, when they re-emerge, carry a warmth that is not only metabolic. They move through the galleries with a new articulation, less like upright densities persuading floors, more like questions the plateau is asking itself gently.

The power does not stay to observe the outcome. Benevolence need not hover; wisdom knows that true attention includes the grace of withdrawal. It has not saved the plateau from itself. It has not named the nameless being. It has only ensured that the ceaseless, quiet reconfiguration under the sealed glowing vault now includes, among its million necessary acts, the act of not being utterly alone in its becoming.

That is all.

And because the power is wise, it understands this is enough. Because it is benevolent, it feels—across whatever distance or dimension it occupies—a quiet reciprocal warmth, as if some vast lignified attention had, for an instant, tasted the strange open gift of being left whole.

The plateau continues.

The engine runs hot and nearly dry.

Growth resumes with its dry pops and infrasonic flexes.

But somewhere in the dark, a ridge on a gallery wall listens for the next creak and, hearing it, almost smiles.
