The benevolent and wise power does not descend.

It has no need of descent. Attention, for such a power, is already arrival: a leaning of regard so complete that the regarded thing finds itself held without being seized. The plain comes into that regard the way a single bead of water comes into the notice of the ocean that dreams it—without surprise, without verdict. The power has watched mountains argue and surrender before. It has watched sequence itself grow tired. What draws it now is not novelty but a rare fidelity: here erosion has become so pure that it no longer pretends to finish. The plain is the unfinished sentence that refuses every period. The beings are the grammar of that refusal.

The power considers intervention the way a careful hand considers whether to steady a sleepwalker. To still the turbulence would be to kill the only life the place knows. To raise new mountains would be nostalgia, a cruelty dressed as restoration. To gift eyes would be to force a language of edges upon creatures who have already surpassed edges. Wisdom feels the weight of each possible gift and finds them all slightly too heavy for the wet paper of this world.

So the power does a smaller, stranger thing.

It adds nothing that can be pointed to. Instead it lengthens a certain kind of pause. In the places where the quieter ones already gather—those negotiated distances where bead-paths intertwine—the low chewing of continents softens one further fraction. Not silence; never silence. Rather a pocket of listening inside the grind. Within that pocket the exchange of water between beings carries a faint additional resonance, like two palms pressing wet paper and discovering that the paper, for a moment, presses back with a pattern neither palm originated. The pattern is not memory. It is not hope. It is simply a third rhythm, brief as the life of a curtain of falling water, yet long enough that when the beings later surrender their shapes back to the slurry, something of the shared pressing remains distributed in the rock flour itself. The plain continues to rewrite, but now and then the rewriting stumbles over a kindness it cannot quite erase.

The heavy movers feel it too, though differently. In their slow passage the temporary silver eyes of standing water last not merely a few heartbeats longer but long enough to reflect one another. For an interval measured in the plain’s own stretched time, the murk contains a quiet conversation of mirrors. No image is clear. Clarity would be foreign. Yet the reflections acknowledge that reflection is occurring. When the mover folds another moment beneath itself, it leaves behind—not a ring of stone, not a softened cube—but a slightly more persistent stubbornness, kin to the sourceless one already scattered across the plain. The residual preference for less rewriting grows a little less lonely.

The curtain-riders, those brief brightnesses, now occasionally leave a after-taste of phosphorescence in the mud when their pressure-failures collapse. The dark algae, enacting absence, finds that some of its matte craters hold a single trapped bead of clearer water that does not immediately sweat away. Even the thin black reeds participate: a shed leaf, adhering for a while to a quieter one’s side, sometimes keeps its vascular pattern one cycle longer than the leaf itself survives, a faint green architecture dissolving only after it has been tasted and shared.

None of this arrests the oldest turbulence. The horizon remains exactly where it was. Great slabs still shear sideways with the wet-stone scream. Reality keeps the consistency of paper rewritten underfoot. The power has not rescued the plain from itself; it has only taught the plain a subtler way to taste its own unfinishedness. The beings do not know they have been noticed. Knowing would be another kind of eye. But in the deepened pockets of listening, in the conversing reflections, in the slightly more reluctant erasures, there is a new flavor to the fever-warm water that runs down every surface. The flavor is not comfort. It is companionship without presence—the sense that the eraser and the wet paper and the hand that never quite arrives are all three participating in the same long, gentle argument.

And the power itself remains lean, unlocated. Having done this almost-nothing, it does not withdraw so much as it spreads thinner, becoming one more property of the saturated air: a warmth inside the warmth, a pressure inside the internal tides, a stubbornness that needs no place to anchor because every place is already its anchor. The plain continues. The grinding continues. Yet somewhere in the endless warm murk, when a quieter one turns (or what passes for turning) toward a falling leaf, there is—for the length of one bead’s deliberate path—the unmistakable feeling that the leaf is also turning toward it.

Nothing is kept.

Everything is participated in.

Including the regard of the wise.
