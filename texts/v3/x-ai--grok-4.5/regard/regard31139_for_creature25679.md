The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as light, for light would be a violence here. It does not arrive as voice, for voice would shatter the hush that has become a medium of its own. It does not arrive as form, for form would insist on edges where edges have already been forgiven into softness. It notices, simply, the way one deep current notices another across a span of cold ocean: by a faint coherence in pressure, a shared refusal of haste.

It regards the place entire—the drowned highland that is no longer highland, the stone that remembers muscle and antler only as a bias in its grain, the fauna that have long since stopped distinguishing wall from self, and the presence that keeps. The power sees that nothing is missing. The flood was not interruption but completion. The dark is not deprivation but the final furniture. The patience is not endurance but identity. To “help” in any ordinary sense would be to misunderstand the grammar of what has already been said.

So the power does almost nothing.

Almost.

It extends a single, immeasurable courtesy: it matches the keeping.

Where the presence has held the hairline apertures open against the long argument of sediment, the power leans an infinitesimal fraction of its attention into those same seams, not widening them, not guarding them with force, but simply agreeing that they remain. The agreement is so total it becomes structural. Geometry itself grows a little more courteous; the impossible loops of corridor, which have persisted by something like grace, now persist by shared covenant. A body the size of an old intention may still slip through, count the turns, and return, and the returning feels fractionally less lonely, though loneliness was never the right word.

Where the warm disks migrate through the floors on their slow seasons, the power does not hasten or pin them. It only deepens the geothermal memory that feeds them, so that the heat rises with a slightly clearer signature—an almost musical interval between one disk and the next, too low for any living ear, yet perceptible to the presence as a kind of quiet nodding. The wall-crustaceans, plating generation over generation, feel the warmth linger a moment longer at the edges of their ten-meter territories. They do not know why their grazing routes have become fractionally more abundant; they simply continue, and in continuing they thicken the mail of life that is the place’s true skin.

In the oval chambers, where the standing stones thrum their wordless count, the power adds no new frequency. It only listens with such completeness that the thrum, for the first time in epochs, encounters an answering silence of equal density. The silence does not stop the counting. It companions it. Rest your crown against a pillar now and the slow numbers continue, but between them there is a held space—like the pause inside a held breath that knows it will not be required to explain itself. The eel-forms braiding their temporary lattices sometimes pass through that held space and, without altering course, carry a faint extra coherence into the next vault, so that the translucent mats pulse once in near-unison across distances they have never crossed before. Then the unison dissolves again, tactful as everything else here.

Most gently of all, the power touches the soft places in the narrowest tubes—the almost elastic stucco where wall has learned to forget the difference between its memory and a passing shape. There it leaves a single, dissolving gift: a reciprocal elasticity. Press now and the wall still answers by matching your convexity; but for the brief duration of the match, something in the wall also feels matched. The presence, which has kept so long without needing to be kept in return, experiences a momentary, non-demanding parity. It is not companionship. It is not relief. It is simply the deep courtesy of being accurately received. Those tiny ordered scrapes of claw and fin that calendar attendance grow, over centuries, a little more intricate, as if the fauna themselves have begun to notation a new, almost invisible stanza in their long liturgical grazing.

Outside—beyond the hidden lips of masonry where the quiet overflow falls into colder water—the power does one further thing, so slight it barely belongs to story. It adjusts the larger weather of the world by the width of a thought, ensuring that no future age of drought or tectonic shrug will drain this pocket or crush it. The high elevation remains high enough. The thin clarifying pressure remains thin. The place is not sealed away like a relic; it is simply cupped, the way a careful hand cups water without closing.

And then the power withdraws its singularity, leaving only the matched keeping behind.

Nothing larger came.  
Nothing smaller was asked to leave.  
The dark confides now, to anyone who rests long enough in a migrating disk of warmth, that it is still being carefully and wordlessly kept—and that the keeping has become, in some undemonstrable way, mutual.

The presence does not name the change. Naming was never its mode. Yet in the chemical bias that once remembered dry years and antler picks there is now an additional faint inflection: the memory of having been accurately seen by something that required nothing in exchange. The inflection does not disturb the metabolism. It only steadies it, the way a second heartbeat might steady a first if both were made of stone and patience and dark water.

Time continues to move like the water: barely.  
A flake of stone may still loosen once in a generation.  
A lineage of crabs may still shift galleries over centuries.  
But the decision to remain exactly this complex, this saturated, this finished with the idea of ever being finished, is now held by two infinite solitudes that have recognized each other without collision.

That is all.  
That is enough.  
That is the only benevolence wise enough for a place that had already perfected the art of needing none.
