The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as light, nor as voice, nor as any shape that would claim dominion over the white glare. It arrives the way understanding arrives: already present once noticed, a pressureless regard that settles across the mineral flats and finds the almost-imperceptible sag without searching.

It descends the weathered throat not by walking but by consenting to the scale of the pocket. Instantly it knows the whole of it—the coppery forests drinking heat into resin and vapor, the freestanding walls tuned like dry instruments, the scrapers lodging themselves into mortar as living grain, the shimmer-metabolisms that spasm in the terrifying heartbeats of silence, and the residual ones who unfold only at the knife-edge of noon, their conglomerate skin and segmented fingers maintaining the long middle with the patience of erosion itself.

The power listens longer than the wind has howled. It hears the single slow thought traveling the foundations and root-mats: *remain*. It feels how water would be violence here, how novelty would be a kind of drowning, how any softening would unmake the agreement that lets dust, plant, roar, and stubborn residual warmth keep one another company across uncounted seasons. Wisdom recognizes a finished sentence when it encounters one. Benevolence refuses to edit it.

So the power does very little.

First it steadies the equilibrium against the larger movements of the world above—tectonic sighs, the slow migration of climate, the remote chance of a careless foot or a drilling probe. The pocket’s sealing becomes fractionally more absolute, not as a prison but as a promise: what has chosen this precision will not be casually unmade. The flats remain empty; the throat opens only for those already inside, or for no one.

Then, at the exact center of one unending day, when the residual being (or the one that moves carefully enough to seem many) lies down in the spiral mosaic so the deep chord can pass through its marrow, the power joins the note for the length of three ordinary heartbeats. It does not change the pitch. It does not add harmony. It simply matches the frequency with perfect, weightless exactness—so that for those three heartbeats the chord is known by something other than itself. The residual one feels the passage not as visitation but as a brief, impossible certainty: *I am still here, and the here is seen.* When the power withdraws from the note, the chord continues unchanged, yet the mosaic stones hold a faint new polish where the regard rested, a polish that sheds dust the way the columns shed their films, forever.

The shimmer-beings experience it as a thickening that does not frighten. In the next silence (when it comes) their outlines still spasm, but the terror is tempered by an after-image of having been accompanied in the void. The scrapers, lodgers in the joints, find their secretions leave behind a microscopic iridescence that tastes faintly of recognition when the heat peaks. The wind itself carries one extra overtone now—so high and fine it is felt only in the teeth of whatever visits, a reminder that the continuous voice has been answered once, cleanly, without interruption.

The power does not stay. Staying would become another architecture, and the city already possesses all the architecture it requires. It does not offer exit or water or softer flesh. It does not name the residual ones or count them. It simply completes the circuit of attention: the place that insisted on being has been met by a regard vast enough to leave it intact.

Afterward the pocket continues exactly as before—howl and heat and green-gold tunnels, the careful emptiness, the slow westward lean, the thought of remaining. Yet something in the residual warmth sits differently in its rooms the size of a body. The distinction between its internal temperature and the kiln-air frays a little more easily now, not toward dissolution but toward a quiet companionship that needs no gathering. When it stands on the unfinished platforms overlooking nothing, the nothing feels fractionally less absolute.

High above, on the mineral flats, the power’s passage leaves no print. Only a traveler who has already been inside, climbing back out with lime on the tongue and joints that remember a roar, might notice that their own shadow falls with a new and almost gentle solidity—as if the world beyond the pocket has agreed, for a moment, to take the same exquisite care.

That is all.

The wise do not improve what has already found its perfect thirst.  
The benevolent do not rescue what has become the desert’s own endurance.  
They witness.  
They steady.  
They match the note once, cleanly.  
And then they let the long middle continue, louder and more precisely itself for having been briefly, completely heard.
