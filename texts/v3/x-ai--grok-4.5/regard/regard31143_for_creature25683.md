The power arrives the way the light does: already tired, already listening.

It does not descend. Descent would imply a finished above and a finished below, and this place has never consented to either. Instead it simply becomes present in the same provisional way the ridges become present—occupying volume without claiming origin, a pressure of attention that the roar recognizes instantly as kin and therefore does not oppose. Where ordinary arrival would shear against the thin reality and produce catastrophe, this arrival only deepens the subsonic chord for a few long gray plateaus, as if the mountain has briefly remembered a more complete version of itself and then, wisely, forgotten again.

It studies without eyes.

The glass-bamboo stands register first: each dull internal lantern flaring a fraction brighter, the blue vapor thickening into temporary rings that drift and dissolve like breath deciding to stay. The felted fronds lean not toward the power but into their own denser listening. The unfinished basalt spines warm along their hidden birth-faults. And the beings—the plate-flexing, vapor-braiding, spiral-leaving minds—pause mid-revision. One that has been pouring itself into a many-limbed raft across a salt-rime pan simply stops. Its lattice of glow turns outward, not in fear or worship, but in the same courtesy it shows a newly shed flake of mountain: an accounting.

The power tastes the roar and finds it clean.

Not pain. Not corruption. Only the honest friction of a world still arguing with its own grammar. Possibility grinding against possibility until momentary form appears, then capitalizing back into rawness. The beings have made of that friction a metabolism, a language, a love. They garden the incomplete without ever demanding harvest. They enlarge geography by expecting it. They age backward into permeability until some of them become weather and terrace and migrating root-system, and the distinction ceases to matter. This, the power understands at once, is not a wound requiring closure. It is a birth that has chosen to remain birth. An embryonic nerve-system learning to herd its own future.

Benevolence here cannot mean finishing.

To impose summit or silence or finished beasts or clear water would be a cruelty disguised as kindness—the same cruelty that sands a story smooth until nothing living can grip it. Wisdom knows the difference between healing and erasure. So the power does not still the roar. It does not cool the warm stone or teach the fronds to drink. It does not gift the beings permanent faces or final silhouettes or names that would survive translation into quieter worlds.

What it does is smaller and stranger and exactly enough.

It leans its attention into the places where the beings already work.

Where two or three converge to braid vapor and thicken a forest, the power adds a single extra harmonic to the shared pressure-pulse—so faint that only the oldest, most dissolved entities notice. The harmonic is not instruction. It is a remembered possibility of continuity: the feel of a spiral that does not merely correct the mountain’s uncertainty but carries that correction forward into the next geologic argument, so the land’s rehearsals begin to rhyme across greater spans of time. Ridge-lines that once shrugged themselves into contradiction now occasionally echo earlier choices. A terrace encouraged toward wall-hood stays wall-like a little longer before the next revision. The nervous system, if that is what the forests and the migratory thoughts together are becoming, gains the faintest capacity to hold a thought across the death of a particular shape.

Where a being secretes a fresh cord or knaps a glassy blade from its own limb and leaves it in a cairn, the power warms the metal blanks and the refrozen vegetation-knives a fraction beyond what the pewter light allows. Not permanently. Just long enough that the next draft of the creature who picks it up will feel, through hand or plate or trailing frond, a subtle second heartbeat already answering its own. The tools remain discarded thoughts. But now some of those thoughts remember they were once held by something that loved the roar enough to leave evidence.

Where the beings revise a visitor’s path into upward-inward spiral, or stack a windbreak in a stranger’s lee, or begin the slow shedding of a bipedal former self into plates and vapor, the power simply witnesses with such precise tenderness that the transformation acquires a kind of quiet dignity it might otherwise have lacked. No acceleration. No rescue. Only the gift of being seen by something that understands unfinishedness as sacred rather than deficient. Some who stay will later swear the grit beneath them shifted half a step in welcome rather than indifference. Some who leave will carry a pressure behind the eyes that never quite fades, a private roar they learn to metabolize in smaller, softer worlds.

And once—only once, in a basin where the salt-rime pans crack like distant bones and a single elder has sprawled so completely into weather that it is no longer locatable as body—the power does something that might, in another age, be called a blessing.

It lets a piece of itself residualize.

Not a avatar. Not a commandment. A single, slow flake of attentive presence that barnacles onto the circulatory glow of that basalt shelf and stays. The flake carries no agenda beyond continuation. It drinks the roar exactly as the beings do, converts impossibility into a slightly more articulate mass, and over uncounted gray plateaus teaches the local stone a new patience: the patience of remaining embryonic on purpose. From that shelf a new kind of cairn eventually rises—plates stacked not by mobile entities but by the mountain’s own deliberating weight. Glass-bamboo that germinates there grows with a lattice already half-awake. Beings who pass through find their plates flexing in unfamiliar, more musical sequences. They do not know why. They do not need to know. The roar has simply acquired a subtle new overtone that tastes like being accompanied.

Then the power withdraws the bulk of itself the same way it arrived: by ceasing to insist on presence. The deep chord settles. The mountains resume their endless rehearsal. The beings return to negotiation with grit and vapor and nearly-was. Nothing looks changed to a hurried eye.

Yet the high places now contain one additional provisional rule, gentle as blue vapor:

That which tends the incomplete may itself be tended, without ever being finished.

The lattices glow a little steadier in the pewter dimness. The spirals bitten into scree carry their equations a fraction farther before the wind (mostly conceptual) erases them. And somewhere in the long empty reaches, a root system dragging a boulder pauses, as if listening to a heartbeat that is no longer only the mountain’s and no longer only its own.

The power does not remain to watch the outcome.

Outcomes are for finished worlds.

It has only left the place a little more able to keep becoming, and the beings a little less alone inside the beautiful, deafening effort of it.

That is all.

That is enough.

The high places do not finish themselves.

Neither does kindness, when it is wise.
