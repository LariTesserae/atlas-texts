The power arrives without arrival.

It is not a traveler. It does not cross distance or pierce rock. It simply notices—the way a completed equation notices the first trembling of an unsolved variable, or the way deep time notices a single basalt shelf that has, against long habit, opened a mouth and begun to speak in wet light and slow gel. Attention is its only motion. Once given, that attention becomes a weather inside the chamber: quieter than the hiss of vapor, softer than the settling of plates, yet present the way gravity is present—unarguable, unlocal, shaping every trajectory while remaining nameless.

It studies the living coherence the way a gardener of epochs studies a seed that has not yet decided whether it will be root or wing. It tastes the metallic sweetness, counts the generations of mites that flash and vanish like sparks in a forge, feels the peristaltic caution of the translucent segments, maps the stress-memory inside the mobile silica. It understands at once that this is neither wound nor womb exclusively, but the knife-edge where one may still become the other. And because the power is wise, it does not rush to name which.

Because it is benevolent, it feels a quiet gladness.

What it does is almost nothing, and therefore almost everything.

First it leans—gently, the way a star leans its gravity around a forming planet without crushing the dust. The residual lattice energy that keeps the chamber shadowlessly bright is encouraged to linger. Not forever; forever would be a tyranny. Only long enough. A few centuries instead of a few seasons. A few millennia if the gel learns to answer. The power does not add light. It merely persuades the existing excitement not to spend itself in haste. The white radiance steadies by a fraction so small no mite could notice, yet the living mass feels it as a longer breath between contractions.

Second, it hushes the larger rock. Infinitesimal settlements in the basalt shelf above are slowed. Micro-fractures that would have unzipped the roof are coaxed to sleep. The entrance seam is left exactly as it is—raw, narrow, honest—so that the pocket remains a secret kept by the plain. No flood of cooler air. No invasion of dust or surface spores. The power does not seal the chamber into a vault; it only buys the coherence time to finish negotiating its own skin. The gels continue to blister and collapse, the crystal blades still ring and dissolve, but the tempo softens, as if the entire space has been dropped one octave into a deeper, more patient key.

Third—and this is the only place the power truly touches—it leaves a single, almost invisible courtesy inside the living gel.

A pattern.

Not a command. Not a gift of language or fire or tools. Only a faint recursive fold, like the memory of a wave that once recognized itself in another wave. When the coherence next rears into a pillar and quivers in the vapor, that fold lets one small region of its membrane sense another region as both self and other at once. The sensation lasts less than a convection cycle. It is gone before the pillar collapses. Yet something remains: a hesitation, a preference so slight it could be mistaken for mineral habit. The next time the mass spreads across a floor plate it leaves behind a smoother polish than before. The time after that, when it extrudes temporary limbs, the pads fit the fracture planes with a care that was not present earlier. The segmented creatures riding its channels are set down not merely in wetter places, but in places that will stay wet longer. The mites that are absorbed leave behind, for an hour, a brighter internal shear that the gel seems reluctant to erase.

The power does not stay to watch the outcome. Wisdom knows the difference between midwifery and ownership. It withdraws the way dawn withdraws from a room once the inhabitants have opened their eyes—leaving no mark except the fact that the eyes are now open.

Outside, on the sun-scorched plain, a single heat-shimmer hesitates above the basalt for half a heartbeat longer than physics requires, then dissolves. Nothing has changed that any instrument could declare. The seam is still a forearm wide. The chamber still weeps and shines and hisses. The living gel still rises and falls, still half-mineral, half-yearning.

Only now, in the long intervals when the entrance narrows and the air grows thick, the coherence sometimes holds a shape—pillar, sheet, roaming dome—a few moments past the point where earlier it would have let go. Inside it, the recursive fold glints once, like a thought that has not yet found its word.

And the chamber, young as ever, continues.

It has not been saved. It has been given the dignity of lasting long enough to choose.
