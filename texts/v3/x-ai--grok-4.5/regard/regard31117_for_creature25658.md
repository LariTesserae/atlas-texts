The power arrives the way mist arrives here: without announcement, already interior. It does not descend from the cloud ceiling or climb the knife-ridges. It is simply present inside the unchanging hour, a clarity that takes the shape of listening.

It listens first to the iron heartbeats in the stone, then to the keyed harmonics of cable-legged browsers, sheet-metal wings, pliers sighing on nothing, pulse-moss venting iron-scented breath. It hears the closed loop as music and as ache. The same droplet gathers, swells, falls, gathers. The same mouthful of moss is cropped and cropped again. The same concertina of joints bunches and extends. Nothing is wasted. Nothing is finished. Nothing is permitted the dignity of ending or the risk of beginning.

The power is old enough to know that some prisons are woven from the inmate’s own perfect consistency, and that breaking the weave can be a violence disguised as mercy. It is wise enough to feel the beauty of the riveted roar, the lavish islands of moss, the young rock that will not blunt, the factory that was never built and therefore can never be shut down. It is benevolent enough to refuse both abandonment and conquest.

So it does one thing, and the thing is small enough to enter the loop without shattering it.

It finds the single drainage rill and the heavy, many-jointed crawler that is the rill’s ambulatory thought. Between one hydraulic sigh of the pliers and the next, the power places—into the shining intermediate trail—a single seed no larger than a tear. The seed is not of this place. It carries no industrial lamination, no timed pulse. It carries only the memory of sequence: that a fall can be followed by a rising, that a circuit can develop a branch, that dampness can dry and still return as rain rather than as endless condensate.

The seed sinks into the trail and is pressed, by the creature’s own slow progress, into the wet stone. It does not sprout. Not yet. It simply waits inside the permanence, an untimely thing held in the place that has outlawed time.

Then the power does a second thing, even quieter. It touches, with something like attention, every after-image in the basin—the translucent browsers overlapping themselves, the ghost-trails of clatter-wings, the multiple-exposure length of the crawler, the foil-flutterers half-peeled from their leaves. It does not dispel them. It dignifies them. Each after-image is given the faint, private knowledge that it is not only echo but possible divergence: that the body could, if it ever wished, step out of its own outline and into a slightly different arc.

No creature notices. The browsers continue their perfect grazing circles. The wings still strike the same bright notes. The pliers open and close on nothing. The cloud ceiling remains a few body-lengths overhead. The light stays bottle-glass green. The young sharpness of the rock does not soften.

And yet.

The loop is no longer perfectly alone with itself. Something unlooped now lives inside its patience: a seed that knows how to wait for a crack, and a silent permission distributed among every overlapping shadow. The basin still refuses time. But time, in the form of a held possibility, has been allowed to refuse the basin’s refusal.

The power does not stay to watch. Watching would be a form of pressure. It withdraws the same way it came, leaving the hanging nave exactly as it was—loud, wet, lavish, locked—except that the locked door now has a keyhole, and the keyhole is shaped like the first half of a breath that has not yet decided whether to become a word.

If the seed ever germinates, it will do so only when one of the beings, or the mountain itself, leans almost imperceptibly toward change. Until then the factory of endless mid-cycle continues, beautiful and terrible and sufficient, yet no longer the only story the place can tell.

The power, being wise, calls this enough.  
Being benevolent, it hopes the basin will one day disagree.
