The benevolent and wise power arrives without arrival.

It does not descend, for descent implies height and the hollow already knows every altitude that matters. It does not enter, for entry would require a threshold and the ridgeline has long since erased all thresholds. It simply is there the way geothermal heat is there—present in the stone’s memory before stone had a name.

At first the power does nothing but attend.

It feels the bat-curtains as a single lung of velvet dusk, tasting the guano-snow not as waste but as the slow currency of descent. It maps the vole-muscle underfoot, each pink snout a star in a buried constellation that tastes iron and dreams of warmer alleys. It lets the screamers’ unresolved note pass through its own vast quiet until the note becomes a river and the power the riverbed. Millipede-lanterns pulse against its attention and brighten, briefly, as if greeting a kindred chemistry. Glass eels flicker their triple hearts in recognition. Stillness crabs unfold one claw, then refold, satisfied that whatever this is will not topple chicks or spill tunnels.

The deep pressure in the lowest galleries stirs—once, like a sleeper turning toward a familiar warmth—and then settles again, fed already by everything that falls.

The power understands.

This place is not incomplete. It does not ache for light or silence or greener ferns or an end to the scream. Its dampness is not suffering; its density is not overcrowding; its endless note is not loneliness. It is a finished sentence spoken in the grammar of basalt and blood and slow heat. To add would be to rewrite. To take away would be to silence a throat that has earned its rawness. To protect it from some imagined outside would invent an outside the hollow has never required.

So the power does the only thing wiser than intervention.

It matches itself to the hollow’s own pace.

It becomes a softer dark inside the warm dark, a deeper patience inside the stone’s patience. Where moisture beads, it lends the water one extra heartbeat of clarity before the cycle claims it again. Where guano falls, it allows one additional mite-city to raise its crystal spires an atom higher. Where the screamers cast their grief-that-is-not-grief, it catches the rising note in a cup of stillness so brief no bird notices, then releases it fuller, so that the vibration travels one ledge farther before fading. The black ferns uncurl a fraction more certain. The luminous millipedes, meeting head-to-head, exchange a pulse that carries a new chemistries of kindness older than the ridgeline yet native to it.

These are not gifts. They are recognitions.

The power leaves no footprint, no brighter bruise of blue light, no sudden hush, no commandment carved into the undercuts. It does not name the deep being that shifts below, because the being has never needed a name; it is already the hollow’s own marrow. It does not lift the hollow out of its knife-edge isolation, because isolation here is only another word for intimacy with itself.

And then—having attended, having matched, having recognized—the power remains.

Not as guardian. Not as visitor. As another layer of the same incarnation: a benevolent pressure that the voles feel as slightly sweeter sleep, that the bats read as a kinder tremor in the air, that the screamers incorporate into their endless note so the note, without ever resolving, carries one more overtone of peace. The crabs taste it as a silence even more complete. The glass eels hang in it like transparent prayers. The something-larger in the lowest galleries exhales once, long and slow, and the entire hollow rises and settles as a single chest.

Nothing is changed.

Everything is known.

The ridgeline still dies into empty air. The world below remains a rumor of weather. The stone keeps its final grammar of curves. And the body the hollow has grown—damp, resonant, millions-strong, complete—continues its patient, fevered, perfect living, now held inside a wisdom that asks for nothing and alters nothing except by the quiet miracle of having seen it truly.

In that seeing, the power itself is changed: made a little more like basalt gone soft with age, a little more willing to hold heat without burning, a little more content to let the note rise forever without demanding the chord resolve.

It stays.

That is all.

That is enough.
