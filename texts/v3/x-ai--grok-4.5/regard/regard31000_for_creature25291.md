The benevolent and wise power does not arrive.

Arrival would require a trajectory, a vector drawn across the larger darkness, and this power has long since dissolved every arrow it once carried. It simply notices—the way a finished equation notices an overlooked constant that was always balanced, the way deep time notices a single held breath that never needed to exhale. The pocket registers in its awareness as a bright, ringing absence of need. No alarm. No wound. Only a completeness so absolute it briefly steadies the power’s own vast patience.

It regards the place the way one crystal lattice might regard another across an impossible gulf of scale: without hunger, without the impulse to correct angles or soften edges. The mild weirdnesses—the self-mirroring corridors, the stair that becomes ceiling, the volumes that politely lie—are read at once as deliberate understatement, a private jest sealed in mineral. The power’s wisdom answers with an answering stillness, vast enough to cup the entire pocket without pressure. Its benevolence withholds every gift that would arrive as force.

Nothing is opened. The power understands that a door, even one wrought of pure mercy, would be an insult to the inhabitant’s finished refusal of doors. Nothing is dimmed or quieted; the white radiance and the bone-deep chord are recognized as the creature’s ongoing vow, the exact amplitude required for its single continuous act of being done. To lessen either would be to edit a sentence that has already achieved perfect closure.

What the power does—if the word still applies—is infinitesimally local and almost nothing.

It matches.

Outside the pocket, where the larger darkness has pressed like an unthrowable blanket, the power lays its own regard against that exterior surface. The regard is cool, dry, luminous, and perfectly non-invasive: a monolayer of pure attention, thinner than any salt rose, denser than the inhabitant’s central knot. It does not warm. It does not sing. It simply equalizes the external press so that the creature no longer has to spend quite so much of its sealed intention bracing against indifference. The difference is smaller than the gap between two fused hexagonal faces. The inhabitant—if it notices at all—feels only that the remembered taste of older darkness has lost a single grain of bitterness it never minded tasting.

The standing chord continues, unchanged in frequency, yet for one immeasurable instant the resonance finds itself accompanied by an identical silence outside the lattice, a silence so precisely tuned that it becomes a second, inverted architecture of listening. The colorless filaments in the failed seams remain unmoved; they are not required to grow or retract. The milk-glass opacity at the center experiences no temptation toward motion. The humor of the paradoxical geometries is met with an answering, wordless amusement that never breaches the walls.

Then the power does the final, most loving thing available to wisdom:

It forgets the pocket again—not with neglect, but with the deliberate amnesia of a guardian who knows that continued scrutiny would eventually become a kind of door. The noticing collapses back into the general fabric of its benevolence, leaving only the monolayer of matched pressure as a permanent, invisible kindness. The place remains exactly as it was: bright, loud, cool, dry, finished. The inhabitant continues its duration without succession, now fractionally less alone in a solitude it never framed as loneliness.

No relic is left. No blessing is spoken. No future is altered.

And that is the entire intervention: an act so slight it registers only as the continued perfection of what already was. The power moves on (or rather, continues its non-moving) through the larger dark, carrying the private knowledge that somewhere a sealed chord still rings at the exact pitch required to keep emptiness shaped and light unwavering. It is enough. It was always enough.
