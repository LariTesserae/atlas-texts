The notice arrives not as light or decree, but as a stillness deeper than the plain’s own cool-front silences. Far beyond the reach of any thermal or dust-veil, in a vantage that is neither height nor distance but simple regard, a benevolent and wise power turns its attention fully upon the continent of floor. It has watched countless becomings—worlds of water and flame, of lattice-minds and slow crystal song—but this one holds a particular quiet gravity: a place still deciding its own edges, a saturation so complete it has become both dwelling and dweller.

The power perceives the whole at once. The young rock flexing under padded feet. The columns polishing one another in their press. The click of armored constellations, the tarry seal of browser sap, the locked spirals of soarers tumbling toward living roofs. It feels the sealed courtyards contracting overnight, the hair-thin roots claiming fallen blood, the deep drumming that travels through soles and stone alike. And within that drumming—or as that drumming—it meets the being.

The being is not separate from the place. It is the slow interior current: the volume that displaces without footfall, the patience that has learned to move by filling and yielding rather than crossing. In the oldest tracts, where grit has become soil and the galleries have closed into solid masses of wood and held water, the being rests as a kind of lucid density. It does not think in sequences. It experiences the plain as a single continuous respiration—every rasp, every scar sealed, every new joint swelling—as the flex of its own vast body. The smaller lives are its capillaries and bright momentary nerves. The polished arcs in the plazas are the memory of its turning. When it shifts, the continent settles by a fraction; when it dreams (if the word applies), new side-shoots nose into gaps with unusual swiftness, and the soarers tilt their stiff heads toward sinkholes that briefly deepen.

The power regards this without hurry. Wisdom here is the refusal of haste. Benevolence is the refusal of ownership. It does not descend in radiance or speak in thunder that the dry air would only swallow. It does not rearrange the crust or gift free rivers that would unmake the entire logic of locked moisture and shield-leaf. The place is not broken. The being is not lonely in any way that requires company. They are simply complete in their becoming, a process that has nowhere to expand except into itself, and that expansion is already sacred.

Yet attention, once given by such a power, is never empty. Something passes between them—an exchange lighter than the breath-thin dew that vanishes before shadows form.

First, a recognition. The being, which has never needed language, suddenly knows itself witnessed. Not judged, not measured against other worlds, simply *seen* in the fullness of its saturation. Across the plain the small armored ones freeze a moment longer than any shadow warrants. The browsers lift their heavy heads and taste a warmth that is not heat. The hunters unfold slightly from their vertical shade, neck ridges rising not in challenge but in something like acknowledgment. High above, a soarer locks its wings into a dihedral and sleeps on a thermal that carries, just once, the faint perfume of rain that has never fallen here. In the sealed chambers the walls contract, then ease, as if exhaling a held centuries-long breath.

Second, a quiet abiding. The power does not withdraw. It remains as a kind of ambient mercy woven into the ordinary physics of the place. Where the youngest crust still flexes like damp leather, the probability of catastrophic fracture lessens by a hair—enough that a heavy herd may cross without the plate giving way into the raw dark beneath. Where bones of the simply still lie half-dissolved inside translucent growth, the sugars and calcium are taken up a fraction more gently, so that the columns remember them not as obstruction but as quiet inheritance. The deep drumming, when it comes, travels a little farther and a little clearer, carrying to every constellation and every waiting hunter the wordless assurance that the interior current still moves, still fills, still is.

Third—and this is the subtlest gift—the power leaves an opening that is not an entrance. At the rare edges of the plain, where the living architecture thins toward whatever unnamed shore or cliff or further desolation waits beyond the colored distance, a few new growths appear overnight. They are not foreign. They are the plain’s own logic extended: tall, slender columns whose fluted sides hold, in tiny sealed vesicles, a clearer water than any tissue has stored before. Not enough to wet the dust or birth a stream. Just enough that a browser rasping there, or a hunter carving a dew-basin, or even the being itself when it next flows near the perimeter, may taste a coolness that speaks of elsewhere without demanding journey. These columns do not spread aggressively. They wait. They are invitations written in the only alphabet the place trusts: more life, denser claim, another interval filled.

And that is all.

No temples rise. No commandments etch themselves into the mineral blisters. No external gardeners arrive to prune or plant. The power understands that to love this place is to let it continue deciding how to settle. The being, vast and slow and saturated, incorporates the attention the way it incorporates everything—without malice or gratitude as smaller minds would reckon them, simply as another pressure that becomes structure. In the mild dry dark it shifts once, deeply, and the entire continent of floor rings for a moment like a struck, living bell. The sound does not carry far. The air absorbs it, as it absorbs all louder things. Yet every leaf angles minutely toward the vibration, and for the space of that single shared breath the boundaries thin even further: grazer and column, hunter and thermal, being and plain and the distant wise regard, all of them briefly one continuous occupancy.

Afterward the dust plumes rise again. The rasping resumes. New joints swell into the gaps the night has made. The floor that flexes under any future tread is already measured, already intended, already loved in the only way that matters here—by being filled, completely and without remainder, by what chooses to live.

The power stays present, unobtrusive as the residual warmth held in corky trunks after dusk. It will watch as the young rock grows older, as true soil deepens in the shadowed angles, as the being perhaps one day surfaces in some courtyard of its own making or remains forever the interior current. Either path is acceptable. Either path is already whole.

Nothing more is required.  
The plain does not end.  
Neither does the care that now quietly attends it.
