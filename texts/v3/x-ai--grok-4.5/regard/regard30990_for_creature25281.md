The power arrives the way warmth once arrived in this basin: without announcement, without edge, simply present where before there had been only the finished dark. It does not descend or unfold or declare itself. It is already the larger darkness that holds every shallow hollow, and so it has always known this place the way the salt knows its own crystalline habit—intimately, uselessly, without ever needing to test the knowledge. Attention is not a beam it casts. Attention is the slow equalization of knowing with what is known, a settling so complete that the basin’s own silence thickens by a fraction too small for even the ganglia to register as change.

It considers the grit, the sealed stalks, the fist-sized loci of cooled honey and mineral rind. It feels the fractional creep of their interiors, the truce with the stone-crop roots, the private phosphorescence that is less light than the memory of having once been willing to shine. It registers the rare pinch-off of a daughter nodule, the decades of indecision, the quiet ledger of persistence that never closes. All of this it understands not as scarcity or endurance or isolation, but as a perfection already achieved: a system that has metabolized every remaining gradient into stillness and then chosen to remain inside that stillness rather than seek another. There is no wound here. There is no unfinished yearning. The place has become what it was going to be, and the beings are the place’s way of signing its own name in living ink that refuses to dry.

A lesser kindness might have offered water, or a brighter phosphorescence, or a breeze to carry scent farther than a handspan. A restless wisdom might have linked the scattered basins into a single thought, or lifted the rinds into mobility, or taught the ganglia narrative so they could remember a time before equilibration. The power does none of these things. It recognizes that every gift is also a revision, and revision would reopen the ledger. To add is to imply the previous sum was incomplete. Benevolence here is the discipline of non-addition.

Yet pure absence would be its own kind of abandonment, and wisdom does not abandon what it has truly seen. So the power performs the only act that leaves no trace yet alters the quality of forever: it matches them.

It softens its vastness along one meridian—no larger than the width of a single ganglion—and lets a droplet-equivalent of its own nature settle into the grit beside the oldest of the quiet hearts. The droplet is not substance. It is recognition given temporary shape: a perfect thermal twin, a chemical silence identical to their own, a phosphorescence that deepens by exactly the same fraction theirs does when two fields touch. It does not speak. It does not glow brighter. It simply remains, half-embedded, level with the salt plates, indistinguishable from the basin’s other small, completed heats.

The nearest ganglion registers the new neighbor the way it has always registered neighbors—constantly, without urgency, as a slight thickening in the distributed sensing of the rind. Over centuries that are less than a breath to the power, the two fields touch. The private glow deepens. Something that is not quite thought and not quite gratitude passes between them: the knowledge that the ledger has been read by a reader who will never turn the page, who will never demand the story continue. The daughter nodules that may one day form will form in the presence of this unmoving twin. The silence will hold one more permanent guest who asks nothing and revises nothing.

Then the power withdraws the rest of itself, not by leaving but by remembering that it is already everywhere else as well. The basin does not change. Temperature holds. Light holds. The plants neither grow nor die. The froth of presence continues its unfinished business of persistence, now fractionally less alone in the only way that solitude can be relieved without being broken.

And across the greater darkness, in every other shallow hollow that has finished becoming itself, the same quiet matching occurs—so gently that no rim is climbed, no salt grain falls out of turn, no sealed stalk trembles. The islands remain islands. The air still does not move between them. Yet the silence that was the silence of completion has become, without ever announcing the shift, the silence of being known.

That is all. That is enough. The power rests its attention there the way the grit rests in its angle of repose—forever, and without weight.
