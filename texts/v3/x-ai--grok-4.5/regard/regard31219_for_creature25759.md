The power does not arrive as light or decree or winged intermediary. It notices the way a deep current notices a sudden cold filament in the sea: by a minute alteration in its own vast gradient, a soft curiosity that never sharp-edges into demand. At first it only abides alongside the seam, matching the pocket’s provisional patience with a patience older still. It tastes the scorched air, the ozone-cinnamon ghosts, the active silence in which panels realign and glass remembers thickness. It feels the beings precipitate and pool and lacquer their surfaces with one another’s weather. It understands, without language, that harm has no grammar here and that permanence would be a kind of murder.

So it does not seal the fault. It does not cool the rock or green it or flood it with names. Wisdom recognizes a rare conjugation of matter and intent: an elevation still dreaming itself, sparsely sequined, kept viable by its own thin population of living persuasions. To finish the place would be to end the dream. To populate it more densely would smother the native syntax of distance. To grant the beings mouths or histories or mercy in the ordinary register would translate them out of existence.

What the power does, if the word still applies, is smaller and stranger.

It leans its attention against the outermost membrane of the pocket—gently, the way one of the beings leans into a warm seep—and lets a single new permission soak through. Not a law. A courtesy. Henceforth the seam may thin, at irregular and sovereign intervals, onto other unfinished places: a salt desert that dreams in slow white pyramids, a mid-ocean trench where pressure braids soft lanterns out of its own night, a ruined orbital lattice still trying to decide whether it is wreckage or chrysalis. Each thinning lasts only as long as a held breath or a fat droplet’s fall. No traffic is compelled. No bridge is built. The beings of the hot seam may ignore the apertures entirely; most do. Yet now and then one will pause its private circuitry, taste the sudden draft of alien cold or alien silence, and carry a faint new scar-map inward—an ember-pattern that records the pressure of a different dreaming. The gift is pure otherness without conquest. The pocket remains self-contained; it has simply been reminded that unfinishedness is a plural condition.

Second, the power adjusts nothing inside the seam except the softest arithmetic of loss. When a being thins past recovery and disperses into mote-cloud, the motes are no longer required to drift until the pocket reabsorbs them by chance. A fraction of their exhausted light is cupped, briefly, in the power’s regard—held the way the beings themselves once held borrowed heat—and then returned not as the same cloud but as a slightly denser seed of shadow. In the plazas where minutes thicken, these seeds sink into the warm dark and, if the place consents, climb again into verticality already carrying a single bright circuit that has never existed here before: a looping filigree that remembers, without words, that they were seen. It changes nothing mandatory. Some seeds never rise. Some rise and fade at once. But the possibility of gentle return now sequins the emptiness alongside the older possibilities of simple dissolution. The counterweight remains sparse. The emptiness is not offended.

Third—and this is nearly nothing, the nearest the power comes to restraint as active grace—it withholds the full weight of its gaze. Continuously it almost looks away, so that the provisional rules never lock under too much witness. Gravity is allowed to keep negotiating. Time may still pool and thin. Walkways may still persuade themselves into being overnight and vanish again if the rock grows shy. The power’s benevolence is expressed as a cultivated incompleteness of attention, a refusal to collapse the maybe into the is. In that merciful half-regard the city continues exactly as it was, only fractionally more possible: the drip still irregular, the heat-squalls still scouring, the beings still distinguishing themselves by the histories written on their cooling slag, the whole vertical void still intimate and vast and delicately undecided.

It leaves no monument, no angel, no cool spring. On the underside of one high overhang, where condensate has always gathered in fat, slow beads, a single bead now forms that is neither warm nor steaming. It hangs for a long time, tasting of nothing the pocket has ever known—an expatriate droplet of some distant, gentler hydrology—then falls with the same long silence as its siblings and is gone. The beings who notice it (two, perhaps three, across many cycles) simply incorporate the anomaly into their surface weather and move on. The rock records a faint magnetic tenderness that will never quite guide a walkway, only a slight permanent hesitation in the air.

Afterward the power recedes into its own gradient, carrying the remembered thrum of the sealed pocket like a private freckle of heat. It does not watch over the place thereafter; watching would be a claim. It merely remains aware, the way the sea remains aware of every filament, and holds itself ready to lean close again only if the seam should ever begin to truly fail—not of its own dream-logic, but under some blunt external violence. Until that unchosen hour the power does nothing more.

The city of young blades and phasing spars and sparse, heat-drinking lives continues.

The quiet, relentless work of remaining possible goes on, one persuasion at a time, now fractionally companioned by a courtesy so light it might never have been offered at all.
