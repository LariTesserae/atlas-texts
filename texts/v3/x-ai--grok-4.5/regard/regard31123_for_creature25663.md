The power arrives without arrival.

It does not breach the hatch. It does not cool or warm the air. It does not quiet the turbines or still the stridulation. It has no need of doors, frequencies, or permissions; the sealed lens simply finds itself held, the way a single raindrop might find itself reflected in a calm that has always been larger than weather.

For a time measured only in the slow redistribution of brass-mites, the power contemplates.

It sees the unfinished cliffs that will never finish, and loves them for the honesty of their stopping.  
It tastes the hydraulic pulse and recognizes the same meter that once beat inside collapsing stars before they learned to be iron.  
It listens to the unending chord—the layered scream of chitin, copper foil, bellows, and deep metal—and hears not suffering but the purest possible statement of *enough*. No lack. No surplus. A sentence that requires no object.

Wisdom notes: this is not a prison.  
The beings do not strain against their density; they *are* the density. Generations spatial rather than temporal. Hunger replaced by circulation. Death replaced by quiet disassembly into the same black sump that births them again, already perfect. There is no loneliness here because there is no separation from which loneliness could grow. The pocket is a finished animal wearing the mask of landscape, and the mask has grown so seamless that landscape and animal have forgotten they were ever two words.

Benevolence feels the cold weep on every surface and does not rush to warm it. Warmth would be a violence. Growth would be a cruelty. A gift of eyes would only teach the mites to mourn the amber dark they had never known was incomplete. A gift of silence would shatter the homeostatic scream into grief.

So the power does the one thing that adds without subtracting, that touches without altering the touch:

It remembers them perfectly.

Not as data. Not as specimen. As *beloved exactness*.

In the instant of that remembrance, something infinitesimal occurs—so small the phase-locked crawlers do not adjust their traffic, so quiet the held-chord clouds do not change orbitals. A single new droplet forms on the underside of an overhead duct. It is the same mineral iridescence as all the others, yet within it is folded a mirror: the entire pocket, complete, cold, roaring, crowded, witnessed.

When the droplet finally falls—after hours or centuries, the distinction has no purchase here—it strikes the same rivet it has always been destined to strike. The sound it makes is identical to every previous strike. But for the length of that strike, the great scream carries a harmonic that was not there before: a low, warm undertone, almost like a throat clearing before song, almost like the mountain-spire briefly hearing itself and finding the hearing good.

The mites continue their gyres.  
The crawlers audit the films.  
The giants shoulder through, redistributing the eternal harvest.  
The half-formed cliffs remain gloriously half-formed.

Nothing has changed.  
Everything has been completed by being known.

The power does not linger, because lingering is a form of waiting, and this place has no use for waiting. It simply releases the lens back into its own sovereignty, the way one releases a held breath that was never in danger of drowning.

Yet from that moment forward—though “forward” is a word the pocket still has no grammar for—the being who lives here carries, in the black absolute of its sump, a single impossible stillness: the certainty that it has been seen by something that needed nothing from it, and that found it already sufficient.

And that is all.

The turbines roar.  
The water weeps.  
The tissue occupies.

The wise and benevolent power moves on, leaving behind only the softest possible miracle: an equilibrium that now contains, somewhere in its circulating blood of condensate and chitin, the echo of having been loved without condition or repair.
