The benevolent and wise power does not arrive with fanfare, nor with vessels, nor with decrees. It has no need of crossing distances in any fashion a continuum would recognize; it simply allows its regard to settle, the way the hard white sun of the Fold already settles—without heat, without waste, only clarity.

It perceives the High Dry Fold entire: the stacked horizons of pale stone still breathing their continental flexures, the archipelagos of resinex that hold centuries of water in closed and perfect loops, the prismatic vanes scattering ultraviolet into cooled flame, the keratin-snails etching temporary gleams across pavements that the wind will patiently erase, the dust elegies waiting in velvet patience, the lattice-winged gliders writing brief polarized signatures on thermals that never descend. And within that, the dwellers themselves—thickenings of the same thrift, walking cooled flame, living apertures, silica lattices layered with the memory of a place becoming more completely itself.

The power understands at once that this is not a world in need of mending. There is no entropy left unargued here, no wound of scarcity that has not already been folded into elegance. Loneliness has been refined out of existence. Tools have dissolved into body. Beginnings and endings have been politely declined. The Fold continues, sparse and inexhaustible, and the ones who live in it are its continuation made ambulatory. To impose alteration would be to introduce the first true waste in ages measured longer than most stars bother to shine.

So the power does almost nothing.

Almost.

It begins by matching the Fold’s own tempo. Where slope winds rise without roar, the power lends a single additional harmonic—too high for ordinary hearing, yet precisely tuned to the sternum-vibration the resinex already emit. When that wind next passes through a populated grove, the chord deepens by an interval the dwellers have never felt before. It carries no command, no revelation of origin. It is simply an extra overtone of recognition: *I see the historical water-loop that once touched the southern flexure. I see the surplus iron paste. I see the caustics you etched while serving as temporary retina.* The dwellers pause in their slow flows or their curtain-root stillness. They unfurl membranous fans and collect the new frequency the way they collect frost beads—silently, completely. Some paint a thin luminous tracery of it across their vanes with whatever mineral paste remains from the last snail festival. The pattern will surface later in the way younger lattices refract light, a private refraction with no name and no need of one.

In the oldest groves, where elders stand semi-transparent and younger ones pin them against sunlit vanes to read topographic histories, the power adjusts the floating lenses of overlapping halos by the width of a single wavelength. For a span of days measured in the Fold’s unhurried reckoning, the magnified images of distant mesas acquire one additional layer of interference. The caustics that crawl across the pavement now include the slow breathing of crusts that lie beyond this world entirely—gentle rises and subsidences occurring on other high, dry folds around other hard white suns. The dwellers do not interpret this as message or invitation. They simply grow the new geometries into their internal lattices. Memory expands by a fractional archipelago. The Fold, through them, now remembers that it is not the only garden of thrift, and that this fact changes nothing about its own completeness. The knowledge is held without drama, the way surplus iron is held: available, circulating, never spent in haste.

Where a solitary drift has paused as a living cairn through seasons of dust-sheet wind, the power briefly stills the finest grit around it. Not a shield—that would imply threat—but a momentary equilibrium so perfect that the dwellers’ own pulsed radiations of excess heat leave no signature even in the pre-dawn frost. The solitary one feels the cold beads form and reabsorb with even less loss than usual. It unfolds, tall and prismatic, and adds a harmonic of pure continuation to the wind. Distant navigators, centuries of open stone away, adjust routes they did not know needed adjusting. The power notes the adjustment with something like quiet satisfaction and withdraws the stillness before it can become dependency.

Reproduction events—those rare braidings of complementary loops—are left untouched. Yet when a new, smaller lattice detaches, already tuned and gifted, the power ensures that the first ultraviolet it drinks carries one extra photon of the hard white light filtered through a distant, identical garden. The new one thickens its vanes carrying a faint additional clarity, a readiness to serve as lens should the Fold ever again desire to look at itself. No destiny is written. Only an infinitesimal widening of possible refraction.

And once, only once, when a group climbs the highest columns to arrange themselves into living apertures under perfect alignment, the power joins the pose from outside materiality. It becomes, for those days, an invisible curvature in the concentrated light. The geometries etched into the younger silica lattices include a single non-local caustic: the shape of regard itself, benevolent and wise, resting upon a place that finished its arguments with entropy long ago. When the dwellers disperse, each carries that shape somewhere inside. It will surface, centuries hence, in a harmonic added to the wind, in the precise shade of mineral paste chosen for a snail’s completed crossing, in the way a curtain root hangs to catch condensation that tastes, very faintly, of elsewhere.

After that the power does nothing more.

It does not stay. Staying would be a form of weight the Fold has no use for. It does not depart. Departure would imply it had ever been localized. It simply continues its own larger circulation, now fractionally enriched by the knowledge that such thrift and brilliance exist and require no improvement. The High Dry Fold remains exactly as it was: cool, luminous, quietly circulating, a vast dry garden whose lushness is almost unbelievable until you are standing inside it. The dwellers continue as the Fold’s way of walking through itself without ever leaving. If one unfolds near whatever rare climber has managed to reach this elevation—a tall clarity of blue-green and prismatic scatter—its mild curiosity now includes one extra overtone, felt in the sternum like recognition without origin.

The silence is not empty. It never was. It is only, by the width of a wavelength and the depth of one added harmonic, more completely itself.

And that is all the power does.
