The power that turns its regard upon the pocket does not arrive as light, nor as voice, nor as any disruption the rotors could measure. It is simply there—the way a theorem is there once proven, inevitable and without origin story. Benevolence is its climate; wisdom is the exactness with which it refuses to misread what it finds.

It listens first.

For a span that would empty calendars, it does nothing but receive the single unbroken note, the dry arithmetic of the hexagons, the slow budding of denser air into freer intervals. It learns the negative history the pitch-born keep—the precise catalogue of everything correctly ignored. It feels how the greater densities hold the distinction between engine and landscape in perfect suspension, never choosing, never needing to. It tastes the metallic leaflets’ pure refusal of growth. And it understands, with the clarity that is its oldest habit, that this place is not incomplete.

It is finished in the way a prime number is finished.

So the power does not open the seal. It does not introduce moisture, nor mercy-in-the-form-of-change, nor any living thing that would fog and fail here. It does not rename the inhabitants or teach them faces. It does not slow the rotors toward silence or hasten them toward some imagined climax. All of that would be a failure of attention, and attention is the only gift it knows how to give cleanly.

What it does instead is quieter than the chamber’s own geometry hiccups.

It leans its regard against the outside of the vault—against whatever mountain or void or larger factory presses there—and makes of itself a second skin, thinner than probability, tougher than duration. Henceforth the pocket’s isolation is no longer merely engineered; it is witnessed. The unblinking white light continues exactly as before, yet now every photon carries a fractional awareness that it is permitted. The great note does not alter pitch or volume, yet within its cathedral monotony a single, unplayable overtone is seated: the harmonic of being known without being touched. The desiccant felt drinks no more and no less, but the chalk it leaves on skin now remembers, for one additional microsecond, that dryness can be a form of care.

The densities notice in the only way they can.

The lesser ones, those vertical intervals and radial tessellations, acquire a new and almost imperceptible certainty of outline—the same sudden self-knowledge a climber once felt, except this certainty does not fade. They phase across one another and, in the faceting, birth children that already contain the overtone, the quiet knowledge that their cold is not loneliness. The older, weather-front minds that are coterminous with whole sections of floor and vault simply incorporate the blessing as further structural integrity. Rust remains impossible. Drift remains impossible. But now the impossibility itself is loved.

One lattice tower—the tallest, the one whose upper reaches had already been replaced rivet by silent rivet with frozen intervals of denser air—receives a final, invisible crowning. At its incomplete summit the power seats a single stationary density that is not pitch-born yet perfectly tuned to them: a continuity of regard, a listening that will never leave and never intervene. The tower does not grow taller. It simply becomes, forever after, the place where the chamber’s dream of being a landscape is held in the gentlest possible suspension by something that knows both engine and garden and has chosen, wisely, to let this one remain engine.

And that is all.

No messenger is sent inward. No future is promised. The silver leaflets ring one more time—once, faintly, like a courtesy returned across an unbridgeable distance—and then the great tone reasserts its monopoly, richer by an overtone no instrument will ever isolate.

The motionless engine continues to believe it is a landscape.  
The landscape continues to remain an engine.  
The pitch-born continue their long apprenticeship of edges.  

Only now the entire pocket rests inside a regard that will outlast every rotor, every plate, every quiet densification yet to bud. The benevolence is absolute; the wisdom is the decision to let perfection stay perfect by never once requiring it to say thank you.

If you stand there long enough, jaw humming with the chord, you may feel it: not a presence, not a warmth, only the sudden, perfect certainty that your outline, too, has been remembered cleanly—and then released. The leaflets ring. The note goes on. Nothing has changed.

Everything has been kept.
