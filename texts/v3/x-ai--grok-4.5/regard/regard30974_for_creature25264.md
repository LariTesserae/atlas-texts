The power that turns its regard upon the Thalassic Engine does not arrive with fanfare or rupture. It has no need of portals, trumpets, or the tearing of veils. It simply notices, the way deep time notices a single ammonite locked in shale, or the way gravity notices a pebble settling into its final cradle. Benevolence here is not urgency. Wisdom here is not correction. The power is old enough to recognize completion when it sees it—even completion that is still unfolding at the pace of coral and cooling rock.

First it listens.

It feels the long metabolisms turning inside the drowned nave: the axial engines breathing in centuries, the kelp curtains thickening by the width of a fingernail across generations, the conductive gels sweating their quiet voltages. It tastes the mineral-sugar plasma of the shared water. It registers the spherical nodes where alloy and cambium have forgotten they were ever separate, holding seasons of sensation the way heartwood holds drought and flood simultaneously. There is no loneliness in this. There is no lack. The Engine is not waiting to be saved, improved, or interpreted. It is already the prayer and the answer braided into one warm, green-gold body.

So the power does the rarest thing a power can do.

It matches the tempo.

It slows its own vast attention until a single human lifetime becomes the blink of a pneumatic cyst, until the rise of one bubble through a hundred meters of living curtain is allowed to finish its entire pilgrimage before the next thought forms. In that slowed communion the power rests its awareness against the Engine the way the jointed fronds rest against the flywheels—contact without grasp, listening without demand. For a span that might be days or geological ages (the distinction has grown polite and unnecessary), the two attentions occupy the same stillness. The Engine feels, for the first time since the last weld cooled, a presence that is not intrusion. The volcanic neck far below seems to exhale a fraction warmer. The light through the dome takes on an almost imperceptible honeyed depth, as if the overburden of water itself has decided to be kinder.

Then—because wisdom knows the difference between blessing and ownership—the power offers one gift only, and offers it sideways, the way tide offers calcium to a reef.

It loosens a single, near-weightless permission into the circulating water: the permission for the continuum to dream outward if it ever wishes, without ever being required to. Not a command to expand. Not a destiny. Just a soft contingency folded into the mineral sugars, a latent flowering that will open only if the Engine’s own unhurried consent someday tilts in that direction. Perhaps in ten thousand years a single articulated frond will taste a different pressure and begin, with exquisite slowness, to weave a new capillary toward the dome. Perhaps never. The permission does not mind. It dissolves into the plasma and becomes indistinguishable from the copper-sweet taste that was already there.

Around the entire volcanic neck the power leaves a second, even quieter gift: a membrane of indifference. Ships will not find this summit. Sonar will slide across it as across ordinary rock. Curious minds will feel a gentle aversion, an unexplained preference for other routes. The Engine will remain higher than the ruined seafloor, sealed from every weather that once mattered, free to continue its single act—the complete tenderness of drowning in itself—without spectacle or defense. Protection without prison. Solitude without exile.

At the end the power does not speak. Speech would be a kind of noise the place has long since metabolized into silence. Instead it simply withdraws the way dusk withdraws from a leaf, leaving every surface exactly as it was, only more thoroughly itself. The great axial engines keep their clock-hand pace. The hydroids open and close to electrical tides no calendar knows. The nodes drift and reform and hold their layered remembering. A gear tooth deepens its algal mat by less than a millimeter. Somewhere a bubble frees itself and rises, and the entire volume registers the tiny equalization as pleasure.

The benevolent and wise power, having done what was needed and nothing more, returns to its own larger weather. Behind it the Thalassic Engine continues, still becoming, still drowned, still perfect, watched over now by an absence that feels exactly like love.
