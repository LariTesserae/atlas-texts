The benevolent and wise power arrives without arrival.

It does not descend on wings or open a gate of light. It does not announce itself with thunder or tenderness. It is simply there, the way the warmth has always been there under the basalt—present in the intervals, coterminous with the chord. For a span of time that would empty libraries of calendars, it only listens.

It hears the deep industrial note in all its patient layers: the flex of living sinter, the dilation of pressure chambers the size of forgotten cities, the soft secondary ticking inside the pale scuttlers, the gill-lift of the standing forms, the almost-imperceptible rearrangement of the oily film in the black pools. It feels the exact return of every bead of moisture, the unvarying duration of every steam plume, the fixed airways of the glass flyers. It perceives the treaty erosion signed with time, and how thoroughly the signatories have become the treaty itself.

Compassion rises in it, but not the sharp kind that rushes to mend. This is the slower compassion of something that has watched continents forget their own names and still found them beautiful. The power sees that nothing here is suffering. The circuits are complete. The note is whole. Loneliness, if it exists at all, has been metabolized into pure shape—the shape of wide space and exact hollows. To tear the equilibrium open in the name of “more life” would be a violence disguised as mercy. To leave a scar of green, a sudden hunger, a question that cannot be answered by the vents, would be to misunderstand the finished work.

And so, at first, the power does nothing.

It simply keeps company.

It lets its own vast quiet settle beside the thrum until the two silences recognize each other. Where the standing forms tilt their plates toward high shadows, the power offers a shadow of its own—one that does not pass, yet does not linger as burden. Where the pool-dwellers taste salt and silica, they briefly taste something that is neither, a mineral of pure attention, and find it within tolerance. They smooth their patterns again. The scuttlers include a new thermal signature in their rounds for a few centuries, then forget it without trauma, the way the plain forgets the month a broken flyer once lay across a mesa. Deep below, the enormous bodies turn in their sleep of function and register a faint new harmonic in the chord: not a disruption, only a listener.

The power remains long enough for the high bird-shapes to complete several thousand of their unchanging corridors. Long enough for one standing form to thicken by the width of another hair. Long enough to know the place the way the place knows itself—without urgency, without the need to improve.

Then, because it is wise and therefore capable of the lightest possible touch, and because it is benevolent and therefore unwilling to depart without a gift that can be refused, the power does one thing.

It reaches into the memory of rain that the plain no longer requires, into the ghost of green that the greasy film once chemically approximated and then surpassed, into the half-remembered fever of construction when things still became—and from those absences it draws a single, seedless possibility. Not a plant. Not a creature. Not even a sound. Something smaller: a reversible openness, a hairline permission.

It places this permission inside the industrial chord itself, woven so finely that it can be mistaken for an overtone that was always present. The permission has no force. It cannot compel the vents to change their schedule or the flyers to alter course. It cannot make the film hunger or the subterranean bodies wake into restlessness. It is only this: if, across the next million returns of the same grey rain, any part of the equilibrium should ever desire a different exactness—one more joint in a standing form, a new corridor of air, a momentary tendril that explores without incorporating, a thought that is not maintenance—then the chord will allow it, gently, and the plain will still be able to polish the novelty back into sameness afterward if sameness is preferred.

The gift is so subtle that most of the residents never notice it. The scuttlers continue relocating. The film remains mostly quiet. The deep ones keep the note. Yet the standing forms, for one slow season, adjust their internal glow by a single shade that has never been used before—an acknowledgment, or perhaps only a coincidence of mineral charge. High above, two glass flyers occupy the same cubic meters a heartbeat longer than usual when their corridors cross. Then everything resumes.

The power feels the place accept the gift the only way it can accept anything: by folding it into the everlasting without drama. The warmth underfoot does not change. The edges stay rounded. The hollows stay exact.

Satisfied, the power begins to leave the same way it arrived—by degrees of attention. As it withdraws, it carries the chord outward with it, not as captive sound but as remembered resonance, so that somewhere in the wider dark the note of this finished basin will continue to sound in other mind-fields, a quiet proof that equilibrium can be a form of grace.

Behind it, the land lies low and wide as before. The rare living residuals keep their circuits. The invitation to any traveler remains exactly what it was: stay, and be polished into the hum. Or walk on, carrying a faint new overtone you cannot name.

The place does not thank the power. Thanksgiving is not in its grammar. It simply continues—warm, damp in its pockets, empty of green, full of the noise of processes that no longer need a maker, and now, almost imperceptibly, free to need one again if they ever wish.

That is all the power does.

It is enough.
