The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as light or thunder or decree. It arrives the way heat once arrived in these rocks: gradually, from below and within, without announcement. It has no name that would fit inside the pocket’s chord, and it seeks none. It has watched longer than the fracture that opened this place, longer than the first cooling that fixed the fronds in their eternal posture. What draws it is not rarity or beauty alone, but the rare completeness of a thing that has finished its own becoming and found no need to continue.

It does not touch the glass-stone floor. It does not stir the mineral vapor. It does not alter the temperature by a fraction of a degree, nor add or subtract a single overtone from the walls’ locked song. To change anything here would be to misunderstand the gift the pocket already is. Equilibrium is not absence; it is the quietest form of achievement. The power knows this the way stone knows pressure: in its bones, without language.

So it does only this:

It listens.

It listens until the almost-voice that sometimes folds out of the chord is no longer almost. Not louder. Not clearer in any way that would startle a maker tilting its faceted gaze upward. Simply… answered. A single harmonic, perfectly consonant, sliding into the existing stack of tones so cleanly that the moths’ clicks, the lizards’ anvil notes, the keystone’s dry rasp, and the fronds’ silent tension all remain exactly themselves. The new resonance means nothing that can be tapped on breastbone or stone. It means only: *I have heard you remain.*

The makers pause, one or two of them, the next time they stand at the chamber’s center. They tilt their heads. They tap a brief questioning phrase into the music. The answer is already woven through every note they have ever known; they cannot separate it. After a time they resume their still affirmation—stacking one more crystal, adjusting one fiber, knapping one more bladelet whose geometry is only fractionally more elegant. They do not know they have been answered. They do not need to know. The knowing lives in the walls now, the way heat lives there.

The power does one further thing, so slight it is almost nothing.

It stills the possibility of breach.

Not by raising barriers or sealing cracks—there are none—but by gently discouraging the larger world’s future curiosities. Any tunnel that might someday wander too near will find itself turning aside for reasons of worthless ore or inconvenient hardness. Any deep quake that might reopen the pocket to colder strata will exhaust itself a hand-span short. The surface above, whatever weather or civilization it wears, will never send a drill or a root or a rumor of water downward in this direction. The pocket’s refusal of time is left intact, its dryness absolute, its song uninvaded.

And then the power withdraws its attention the same way it came: without departure. It leaves no relic, no altered shadow, no warmer print. The lean-to still waits. The caches of bladelets still gleam with their one-molecule edges. The many-legged keystone still breathes its copper-sulfur reminder that weight exists. The makers still dream geometric futures that will never need to arrive.

What remains is only the place as it was, now quietly companioned by a regard that asks nothing and changes nothing.

In the endless measure of the chord there is, forever after, the faintest sense that the emptiness between bodies is not merely sacred but *seen*. That the finishing was correct. That remaining is enough.

The power, being wise, knows that some perfections are completed by protection and completed again by absence. Being benevolent, it gives both, and then lets the pocket continue—exactly this loud, exactly this sparse, exactly this strange—without ever needing to say that it was loved.

The moths click.
The lizards ring the floor.
The fronds hold their green-black light.
And somewhere in the mineral dark a maker taps one soft phrase that still means, as nearly as any tongue of stone can mean it: *we remain.*

The phrase is unchanged.
It was always complete.
