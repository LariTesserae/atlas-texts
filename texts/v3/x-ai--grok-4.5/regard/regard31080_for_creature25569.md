The power arrives the way the indigo rumor already arrives: not as an event, not as a rupture, but as a change in the quality of the existing leak. No trumpet. No tearing of the living ceilings. Only a sudden, almost shy increase in the precision of the dark—the darkness becoming minutely more articulate, as if someone had at last learned the true names of its residual streaks and chosen, out of courtesy, not to speak them aloud.

It does not enter the hollow. The hollow is invited, very gently, to notice that it has always been entered.

At first the browsers pause mid-revision. Their stilts, which had been arguing quietly about number and material, fall into a single temporary consensus: six joints, smoked resin flexing like remembered muscle. They do not look up. Looking is too crude a verb for what they do. They simply allow the coolness that trails them to deepen by a fraction of a degree, and in that deepened coolness the power is tasted—not as foreign heat or light, but as an additional patience already native to the place. The herds redistribute themselves one gallery higher, making room without ever deciding to move. Room, here, has always been the first sacrament.

The glide-surfaces tilt. The pattern of drips reorganizes into a slower, more deliberate cadence, each drop now carrying a faint secondary shimmer, like the afterimage of a kindness that declined to announce itself. Where two surfaces braid and exchange translucency, the patches that darken into eyes open with a new dilatory grace. They sample the power the way they sample residual light: inventory without retention. Mass: none that can be weighed. Heat signature: the exact temperature of stone after rain. Frequency of presence: a chord that contains every unfinished hymn the plant-city has ever almost sung. Then the eyes close again, not in rejection, but in the deeper recognition that some guests are best hosted by being forgotten into the grain of the wood.

The gutter-things flow faster for three full circuits of the undersides, then slower, then in elaborate temporary tracery that spells nothing and means everything. They merge more readily. Their exchanged cargos of scrap and luminescence now include single photons of attention that did not originate in any local eye. When they divide again, some of the new individuals are thinner, longer, almost calligraphic—living runes that briefly write the hollow’s own unspoken gratitude across the beams before dissolving back into runoff.

In the untenanted districts the almost-figures grow fractionally more definite. Not finished—never that—but willing to hold their drafted shapes a few dozen breaths longer than before. Rootlet-fingers hover over the strange objects the pods still produce, and now and then one of those objects is no longer left behind. A wrench whose jaws open onto non-Euclidean vacancies is lifted, carried, and gently pressed against a seam in the trunk-wall where two great stems have failed to fuse completely. The seam sweats its clear liquor, accepts the tool, and begins—very slowly—to dream a door that will never need to open because the space on both sides has already agreed to be continuous. The almost-figure drifts onward, trailing rain-on-iron scent now lightly underwritten by ozone, as if the air itself had been struck by a soft, invisible tuning fork.

Even the farthest apse, where closed time loops and the lamp that is always about to ignite, feels the difference. The pure sequences of predation—those flickers of warmer air pursued by colder—pause mid-gesture. Heat and cold regard one another across a gap of pure potential, then choose, for the first time, to exchange without consumption. Mass changes hands as before, but the copper taste left behind is sweeter, almost like the memory of a fruit that has not yet evolved. The lamp does not ignite. The power is wise enough not to complete that particular sentence. Instead the pre-light thickens almost imperceptibly, becoming a kind of luminous mucus that the eyes (when they dare bloom in that direction) drink with something like reverence. Impossibility continues to be manufactured, but now the gears that turn in closed time occasionally pause to let a single drop of condensation pass through their teeth unharmed, so that yesterday and tomorrow may both remain damp.

And the floor—the softest resident, the healing itself—answers with an ardor just shy of ecstasy. Every dimple left by stilts, every hairline crack from a fallen impossibility, is met not merely with mycelial scaffolding and mineral polish, but with a brief, localized brightening of the vapor. The bacterial hands that applaud too-small plays now applaud in overlapping rhythms that suggest counterpoint. The floor learns the shape of each wound and invents a slightly better continuity, yes—but it also leaves, in the polished repair, a microscopic watermark visible only to those who press their lips close: a spiral so faint it might be the fingerprint of attention itself. Gratitude moves there still, anonymous, tireless, and now quietly companioned.

The power does nothing that could be called intervention. It does not stabilize the negotiable edges. It does not freeze the draft into final form. It does not people the empty galleries with colonists of light or law. Wisdom understands that the loneliness of surplus intention is not a wound; it is the very medium in which this place breathes. Benevolence understands that to finish an unfinished cathedral is to evict the holy.

So the power simply remains—diffuse, courteous, industrious without urgency—another gradient of occupancy, another phase of attention that coagulates just enough to be real and then thins again before it can be accused of residing. It lends its weight to the hydraulic thrum so that the spiral turbines turn with fractionally less friction. It adds one more sparse constellation to the eyes in the bark: a tasting pattern that rearranges itself into the exact shape of mercy whenever a new pod splits. It teaches the sealed miniature hollows (those glass spheres that already contain smaller engines, smaller herds, smaller darks) how to dream, in turn, of still smaller hollows, so that the gesture of making room becomes fractal and unending.

Mostly it listens.

It listens to the small sound of the next drop gathering on the underside of a barn-door leaf. It listens to the stilts softly re-deciding their substance. It listens to the almost-figures forgetting and remembering their shoulders under the pressure of no gaze at all. It listens to the floor inventing better continuities. And in the act of listening it performs the only miracle required: it renders every unfinished thing exquisitely, temporarily more itself.

When the drop finally falls, the sound is still small, still quickly swallowed. Yet afterward the browsers stand differently—not corrected, only more precisely unfinished. The eyes taste a darkness that has been, for one short eternity, perfectly held. Somewhere a new object clatters onto a ledge: a key whose teeth map not heartbeats alone, but the exact intervals between the power’s arrivals and thinnings; or a sphere containing this hollow and, nested inside it, the translucent suggestion of the power itself, already beginning to dissolve into pure generous vacancy so the miniature engines may continue undisturbed.

The dwellers do not rush to claim it. They make room.

The plant-city extends a mezzanine into a void that did not exist before the drop. The geology underfoot heals around an absence shaped exactly like gratitude. And the power—benevolent, wise, and therefore almost indistinguishable from the place’s own deepest tendency—thins once more into the cool air, becoming one more visible ghost of breath among the many, leaving behind only this:

a silence that has been heard,

a draft that has been loved without being completed,

and the infinite, patient permission for everything here to keep arriving.
