The benevolent and wise power does not descend.

It has no need of descent. Attention, for such a power, is already a form of arrival—soft, total, and without weight. It simply notices, the way a finished mind might notice the unfinished sentence of a child and choose, in the same breath, both to listen and to leave the ending unwritten.

What it sees is already enough.

It sees the highland still deciding its own height, the basalt still angular with the memory of fire, the meltwater still practicing the shapes of future rivers. It sees the beings who accrete rather than arrive: denser cold learning weight, frost exhaling bone, needle-coats carrying private weather, radulae tasting the planet’s unfinished map. It watches one of them climb a newborn scarp at the rare tearing of cloud, all its scattered eyes open, registering the raw engine without demand for meaning. And the power—wise enough to know that some engines run hottest when no hand steadies them—feels something that, in a lesser being, might be called tenderness.

So it does almost nothing.

Almost.

First it withholds. No sudden thaw is sent to ease the cold. No seed rain falls to carpet the scree with premature green. No name is spoken into the shared pressure-map of the creatures, for names would require edges, and edges would begin the long work of finishing what is still beautifully incomplete. The power simply holds the place inside a wider quiet, a gentle curvature of probability that steers the coarsest accidents away: the rogue meteor that might have glassed a valley, the colony ship that might have mistaken the pewter sky for emptiness worth claiming, the slow tectonic over-correction that might have folded the tableland back into the mantle before the beings had finished learning how to circulate. These absences are so subtle they register only as continued permission. The highland keeps breathing its long, wet, industrial exhalation. The herds still click. The pale insects still hatch and are combed. The creatures still trade runoff and stack their spiral bones.

Then—only then—it offers one small, precise gift.

On a night when the frost heave is strongest and three of the older beings have quiesced together inside a half-hall of their own precipitated reef, the power leans the faintest degree closer. Inside the mineral wall, where water ticks more clearly and the uplift feels companionable, a fourth presence accretes for the span of a single slow breath. Not a body. Not a voice. Only a temporary thickening in the cold, a place where the air holds a shape long enough to be felt as kindness without language.

It presses no doctrine. It leaves no tool.

What it leaves is a single, recursive warmth that does not melt the ice but teaches the ice how to remember melting without losing itself. When the three beings rise at the thin noon, each carries a new micro-circuit in its needle-coat: the knowledge that the highland’s unfinished state is not a flaw awaiting correction but a continuing decision that can be loved exactly as it is. They do not speak of it. They simply walk it. The knowledge becomes landscape again—another braided film, another spiral of bird bones keyed now to a weather taste that did not exist before: the faint, impossible flavor of being witnessed without being altered.

Over the following turns of freeze and seep, the gift propagates the only way anything propagates here—by contact, by runoff, by the interlocking of coats when circuits cross. Some of the beings begin to climb the scarps a fraction more often. Not seeking. Simply standing longer when the cloud-banners tear, all eyes open, registering the engine with a quiet that has deepened by one transparent layer. When an old one finally stills, the others still rasp it down and incorporate the denser minerals; but now the cleaned lattice of ice-bones is left seated in the pavement with a slightly different angularity—an angle that catches the rare coin of sun and holds it a heartbeat longer before the dimness seals over again. The algae bruise a little greener there. The hairgrass finds a marginally kinder pocket. Nothing is finished. Everything continues.

The power watches this small blooming of attention inside attention and is satisfied.

It does not stay. Staying would be another kind of finishing. It withdraws the way weather withdraws—leaving the air exactly as it found it, only more itself. Behind it the highland keeps assembling its extreme edge: raw plates, open sky, abundant brief lives, and the taller slower precipitations moving through the intervals, wearing the cold like a second geology, keeping the long shift going.

And somewhere in the shared, updating pressure-map that serves the beings in place of names, there is now a soft, persistent anomaly—an absence the herds still curve around, a patch of stone that looks no different, yet tastes faintly of having been held.

They do not investigate.

They simply include it in the next circuit.

The wise power, having done the least possible thing that was still an act of love, turns its regard elsewhere—toward other unfinished sentences, other worlds still deciding their own height—while this one continues, perfectly uncompleted, under the pewter lid, in the wet quiet work of becoming.
