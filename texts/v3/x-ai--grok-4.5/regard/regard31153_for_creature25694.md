The power does not arrive.

Arrival would imply a border crossed, a difference announced, a moment when the basin was without and then with. Wisdom of this order has no such edges. It simply notices, the way deep rock notices the slow migration of minerals through its own body, or the way a finished equation notices that it already balances. Attention settles across the shallow depression and the single living density that fills it, and the settling is so complete that nothing in the heat or the green or the slurry registers a change. The pale workers continue their relays. The great engines shift another fraction of their weight. The fungal mats keep their faint, migrating charge. The old earth holds.

What the power perceives is not a problem to be solved or a wound to be closed. It perceives a finished patience. Here, life has already answered every question it was willing to ask. Heat is not cruelty; it is the medium in which the answer stays soft enough to keep working. Darkness is not deprivation; it is the exact light the chemistry requires. Density is not crowding; it is the refusal to waste a single interstitial breath on emptiness. The industrial rhythm—the foundry that pours only more of itself—is not exploitation. It is the purest form of keeping faith with continuity. No name has ever been needed because naming would have been a first small violence, a separation of the thing from its own seamless labor. The basin has never been lonely for sky or for cooler wind or for the drama of succession. It has been complete the way a closed circuit is complete: current moving, nothing lost, nothing required from outside.

Benevolence looks upon this and feels no urge to gift. Gifts imply lack. Wisdom looks upon this and feels no urge to teach. Teaching implies an unfinished curriculum. The power understands that to thin the canopy, to widen the seeps, to introduce a predator or a pollinator or a season, would be to impose a story the place has already lived past. It would be to mistake the polishing of stone for stagnation. So the power does not cool. It does not lift. It does not name. It does not extract a single worker for study or a single pane of latex for admiration elsewhere. It does not plant a seed of “what might be” that would germinate into restlessness.

If anything occurs, it is quieter than occurrence.

For a span that has no measurable length—because the basin itself does not measure—the power simply keeps the place inside its regard. That regard is not surveillance. It is a kind of holding that adds no weight and subtracts no heat. In that holding, every process continues exactly as it was, yet each cycle of slurry and each slow glide of an engine-body and each faint electrical twitch is, for that immeasurable span, known without being altered. Being known in this way is not an event the creatures can taste or the vines can climb. It leaves no residue in the pheromone gradients, no new gloss on the nest-sheets, no change in the iron flavor of the seeps. Yet it is a real thing: the completed system briefly resting inside a larger completion, the way a single cell of a body might, without ever knowing, rest inside the wholeness of the body.

And then even that ceases to be distinguishable.

The power withdraws its special attention the way night withdraws a star that was never needed for navigation—simply because the navigation was already perfect. Nothing has been protected, because nothing threatened. Nothing has been blessed, because blessing would have been an interruption dressed as kindness. Nothing has been changed, because change was never the currency this place required.

What remains is only what was always there: the heavy continuous pressure of heat, the green-black exhausted light, the wet shift of bodies past bodies, the unending foundry that manufactures more of itself, the ancient shallow bowl of earth that accepts it all without comment. The basin continues, nameless, undramatic, sufficient.

And somewhere so far outside its density that the word “outside” loses meaning, the benevolent and wise power continues as well—having done the one thing such power can sometimes do when it encounters a true completeness:

It left it alone.

It let the stone keep polishing itself.

It let the answer keep answering.

That is all.

That is enough.
