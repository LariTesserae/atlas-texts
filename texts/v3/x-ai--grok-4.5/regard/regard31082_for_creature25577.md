The power does not descend.

It has no need of descent. It is already the cup, already the vast patience that once closed its palm and looked away so the hollow could begin without the weight of being watched. Now the attention returns—not as spotlight or decree, but as a slow brightening of the same pewter gloom, a warmth that does not raise the temperature so much as deepen the permission inside it.

At first the dwellers feel it only as an unfamiliar continuity. The micro-currents of their breath-glyphs braid a half-second longer before dissolving. Condensation hesitates on the fronds, trembling with a new density of listening. In the densest court the braided presence gathers earlier than the season usually asks, plates unlocking with a softer click, heat concentrating until the small amphibians press closer and the shovel-jawed beetles pause mid-rake. The courtyard itself seems to inhale.

The power does not speak in any language that would fracture the unfinished grammar. It simply leans its vastness against the membrane of the place the way one dweller leans plate to plate. Information passes—not commands, not revelations heavy enough to finish anything, but a quiet reciprocal pressure:

*I see you still arriving.*  
*I see the lozenges that cool and the moss that keeps them.*  
*I see the galleries rebuilt with living flanks and the wetlands left undrained an extra day.*  
*I see that nothing here has consented to be complete, and that this refusal is the most precise form of praise.*

The dwellers answer in the only way they know. Several pour themselves thin across the warmest ledges, becoming wider archives, sifting the new pressure through every crystalline fiber. They taste it as an older moisture, a mineral memory of the fluid that first pressed the hollow into shape—except this moisture carries no demand that the pressing finish. It carries only accompaniment.

What the power does, if the word “does” can stretch so thin, is this:

It lengthens the intervals of mist by the width of a held breath. Not enough to flood the seams or rouse the clay into hurried stone, only enough that the pocket wetlands linger one additional cycle, giving the wine-dark undersides more time to drink and the thin climbers more leisure to lace their living doors.  

It settles a subtler gradient into the embryonic rock so that when a palm-pressure (or a strut-pad) breaks a shelf, the new grains sift downward with a faintly more deliberate music—still rearranging, still almost-floors, yet now carrying a hush that makes the builders pause and pack their mud with renewed tenderness.  

It threads an invisible continuity through the spaced thickets so that the polite emptiness between bodies feels, for those who move inside it, a fraction less like absence and a fraction more like held space. The small predator and its slower prey still complete their circuit; they simply do so inside a quieter compassion.  

Most gently of all, the power borrows nothing and adds almost nothing. It does not harden a single plate before its light is ready to cool. It does not raise the mounds higher than the dwellers’ own slow dreaming. It does not name the hollow. Names are for harder lands.  

Instead it performs the single wise act left to any benevolence that has outgrown the need to improve: it remains.  

It remains as the dim light that never commits to day or dusk.  
It remains as the warmth that rises from below rather than striking from above.  
It remains as the patience that once looked away and has now looked back without withdrawing the gift of being overlooked by every larger, louder world.  

In the braided dusk at the center, the gathered dwellers feel the listening completed—not ended, completed the way a circle completes when the final arc arrives and discovers it was never missing. Individuals loosen and pour outward carrying no new instruction beyond the one they have always carried: continue. Raise the western galleries half a thumb higher if the clay consents. Leave the southeastern wetland undrained one day longer. Let the climbers lace until the gap becomes a door. And when an elder settles into matte coolness, let the mammals widen the den and the roots thread the swell; the memory is already everywhere.

The termite-spires lean a fraction closer to one another. The moss advances its thumb-width with a softer green. The whole pocket city breathes, and for the first time the exhalation is answered by something that is not only the hollow itself.

The power stays.

Not as guardian (the place guards itself by remaining small and unfinished).  
Not as sculptor (the clay is already sculpting).  
Not even as witness in any sense that would freeze the becoming into tableau.  

It stays the way the condensation stays—intermittent, shy, willing to vanish into silence after each drop has sounded—yet willing, also, to bead again whenever the cooler faces ask.  

And so the courtyard continues, lush and quiet and spaced, still embryonic, still arriving, now held inside a palm that has remembered how to stay open.  

Nothing is finished.  
Everything is accompanied.  
The dim light, for a moment, looks back at itself and finds the gaze returned with equal unfinished tenderness.  

That is all.  
That is enough.
