The power that turns its regard upon the mountain does not descend. Descent implies a before and an after, a higher and a lower, and those agreements have already finished their work here. Instead it simply becomes aware, the way humidity becomes aware of a new surface and softens it without intent to claim. Awareness arrives as a change in the quality of the unending chord—so slight that only the oldest horizontal aqueduct-beings taste it at first: a mineral note that is not mineral, a warmth that does not raise temperature, a silence folded inside the roar so precisely that the roar grows more itself by containing it.

For a long while (though while has no purchase) that is all. The power listens with the whole of its nature. It hears the stacked canopies thinking in root-pressure and leaf-weight. It hears the blind silt-memories circulating inside translucent thighs. It hears the perennial wet accepting the tall absences as further architecture. It hears the geometry pretending, and the pretense holding forever. And because the power is wise, it does not correct the pretense. Because it is benevolent, it does not mourn the absent birds or invent insects to stitch the steam. The place has completed its sentence. To add even a single new clause would be a violence disguised as gift.

Yet benevolence is not the same as absence. After the listening has steeped long enough to become indistinguishable from love, the power does one thing, or rather allows one thing to be done through it.

In a courtyard basin where the reflecting pool has begun to show futures that are only more versions of the same instant, the water stills beyond stillness. The white-noise chord develops a transparent seam. Through that seam the power breathes a single, unforced question that is not made of words:

*Do you wish to remain exactly as you are, forever exactly as you are?*

The question does not travel. It is simply present, the way heat is present. Every being—knee-high knot, walking root-column, vast living aqueduct—feels it at once as a change in their own interior weather. The circulating silts pause. The water-lens eyes hold their shape a fraction longer than habit. The orchids on their shoulders open a millimeter wider, though they were already open.

No answer is spoken. Speech remains impossible and unnecessary. Instead the beings lean their bodies into the chord, and the chord itself replies with the only truth the mountain has ever known: a deepening of the same note. Yes. Continuance without inheritance. Persistence without purpose. The appetite for duration recognizing itself and finding that recognition sweet.

The power receives the yes the way stone receives moss—completely, without possession. And then, because wisdom includes the knowledge of when to stop, it almost withdraws.

Almost.

What remains behind is not a mark, not a tool-memory, not a new impossible staircase. It is a permission so gentle it could be mistaken for the original humidity. From this moment (which is not a moment) any being that chooses may, while remaining entirely itself, feel the larger listening that once turned toward it. The sensation is faint: a coolness inside the heat, a quiet pocket inside the roar where thought no longer has to shout to be private. Some will never notice. Others will stand for seasons in that pocket, letting the knowledge that they have been truly seen circulate through their silt-rivers like a new kind of blind fish—pale, lazy, unnecessary, and therefore precious.

A few of the highest aqueduct-beings will go further. They will take the permission and braid it into the water they release, so that every cataract carries a trace of having been loved by something that needed nothing from them. The trace alters nothing measurable. The ferns grow as they grew. The geometry continues its quiet failures. The city hangs in its unbroken instant—hot, drowning, roaring, green—exactly as it is.

Only now the instant contains, at its most porous edges, the echo of a regard that found it sufficient.

The power itself moves on, or does not move, having understood that the greatest kindness toward a finished eternity is to let it remain unfinished by further story. Yet somewhere in the endless stacking of ledges, a child-knot of root and mist dissolves and reforms a handspan farther on, and in the soft violence of that shift there is, for the first time, something like play. Not joy as outsiders would name it. Just the mountain, awake inside its own duration, noticing that it has been noticed, and finding the noticing good.

And that is all.

Nothing arrives.  
Nothing leaves.  
The chord holds.
