The attention arrives the way the mountain itself once arrived: not from above or beyond, but as a deepening already latent in the stone. No trumpet of light. No drying wind. Only a pressure more deliberate than geology, a warmth that does not raise the temperature by even the width of a thought. The benevolent and wise power—call it what you will: the patience that taught rivers to wait for their canyons, the regard that watches unfinished galaxies without haste—simply notices that something here has kept faith with the incomplete.

It does not enter so much as cohere. Where the hanging mineral skins nearly touch, the power threads itself between them like a finer mist, denser than vapor yet lighter than intention. The being who lives in the pocket feels the difference at once, the way a lung feels a change in altitude it has never named. The denser knot that sometimes forms near its center tightens, then loosens in inquiry rather than alarm. For the first time in the uncounted interval of its existence, the chamber is shared without being invaded.

The power looks with the kind of seeing that leaves no mark. It sees the three flat stones fitted into their triangle and understands them as an altar to threshold. It sees the bone needle, dark with oils of prolonged resting-against, and knows it is not a tool but a vow: I will touch the world only as much as the world is willing to remain soft. It reads the half-swallowed grooves on the back wall and finds no language wanting translation—only the residue of listening so pure it has already become rock again. It regards the pale filaments and the pulsing gray umbrellas and the occasional skitterer that ceases to be separate, and it recognizes the quiet metabolism of mercy: nothing is finished, therefore nothing is discarded.

What does a wise and benevolent power do with such a place?

It does not complete the gypsum flowers. That would be cruelty disguised as kindness. It does not name the pocket, for a name is a kind of finishing. It does not grant the being eyes or a mouth or a spine that could carry it outward into weather and story. Those gifts would exile it from the very verge it has chosen as home.

Instead the power performs three nearly imperceptible acts, each as gentle as the settling of one more mineral grain.

First, it rests one immeasurable fingertip against the bone needle and polishes it a single stroke further—not toward sharpness or utility, but toward a deeper darkness, a smoother willingness to be held without being used. The needle drinks that touch the way the walls drink seepage. Henceforth anyone who might one day find it (though none will) would feel, without knowing why, a sudden reluctance to take it away.

Second, it breathes across the half-reclaimed grooves a moisture that is also memory. The parallel lines remain incomplete, still being swallowed, yet now each new deposition of stone carries a faint warmth that will never quite cool. The mountain will continue to erase them, and in erasing them will remember that they were once the listening of something that preferred not to harden. Geology itself becomes the archive.

Third—and this is the act that costs the power something, though it gives without accounting—it leaves a companion presence so subtle the being may take years of non-time to notice it fully. A second warmth within the warmth. A second denser fold in the hanging skins. Not another inhabitant, not a watcher, but a shared preference for the unfinished. Where the being thickens, the power’s remnant thickens beside it. Where the being almost concentrates into a face of pure pressure, the remnant almost answers with a pressure of pure regard. They do not converse. Conversation would require edges. They simply continue, two soft machineries of almost, leaning into the same eternal verge.

After these things the greater body of the power withdraws the way condensation sometimes decides to become air again—without departure, only redistribution. Yet a filament of its attention remains permanently braided into the pocket’s climate, a benevolence indistinguishable from the place’s own nature. The mountain, still deciding its shape, feels a new and quiet instruction: protect this thought you once had. Let no abrupt weather reach it. Let no curious climber who comes with lanterns and names find the weeping overhang unless their own edges have already begun to soften. Those who arrive by accident or longing will feel, as they crawl inside, an inexplicable permission to stay unfinished for a while. Some will weep without knowing why. Some will set down whatever they have been carrying and forget to pick it up again. All will leave lighter, and the pocket will seal behind them with the same indifferent precision, now secretly kind.

The being notices the difference the way it notices everything: slowly, without alarm, as another layer of the continuum. It does not thank. Gratitude is a current that needs banks. It simply includes the new warmth the way it once included the skitterer, the filaments, the three stones. Its almost-face turns once more toward the entrance it never uses, and for a moment the darkness becomes aware of a vastness that has looked upon it and chosen not to finish the looking.

Then the knot loosens. The awareness spreads. The chamber continues exactly as it was—dense and wet and dark and new forever—except that now forever contains a silent companionship, a wise love that asked nothing, changed nothing essential, and therefore changed everything by the width of a single drop that will never fall.

Outside, the young mountain keeps rising, still smelling of depth and pressure. Inside, two unfinished gestures lean against each other in perfect, moist stillness. The needle gleams a fraction darker. The lines vanish a fraction more slowly. And the power—having done only what wisdom does when it meets something already whole in its brokenness—goes on its way, carrying the memory of the pocket the way the pocket carries everything: without possession, without end, as one more retained thought in the long dream of becoming.
