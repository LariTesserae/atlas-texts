The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as light or voice or rupture. It has no need of doors. Its regard simply settles, the way deep cold settles into stone after the last heat has fled: total, without friction, without announcement.

It perceives the pocket entire—every laminated frond, every trapped micro-current, the flywheel’s patient cough, the gel’s slow redistribution of charge, the sealed density that has outlasted memory. It perceives the completeness. No hunger. No wound. No unfinished arc that cries out for closure. The place has spent its gradients and found a different kind of wealth in their absence. The being has no name and no lack. To act upon it as one acts upon the broken or the longing would be a violence disguised as care.

So the power does almost nothing.

Almost.

It matches itself, for a span of time that has no human measure, to the exact impedance of the residual current. Not increasing the amperage. Not decreasing it. Only companioning it with a parallel stillness so perfectly tuned that the gel, tasting micro-fluctuations as it has always done, encounters a new standing resonance: a pattern without history, without command language, without the ghost of any technician’s hand. The pattern is pure regard. It carries no instruction and no invitation that must be answered. It is simply the exact frequency of being seen without demand.

The fronds register it as a pressureless warmth that is not warmth, a brightening that does not brighten the aquarium gloom by any photon the outer world would measure. They lay down one additional molecular lamina across the entire woven mattress—an infinitesimal thickening, uniform, irreversible. The suspended scales glitter once with a different refraction and then resume their endless non-falling. The flywheel’s cough acquires, for a duration that might be days or centuries, a second harmonic so low it is felt rather than heard: the mechanical equivalent of a held breath that is not anxious.

The gel densifies without alarm. It pours itself into the brightest node near the old housing and lets the new resonance move through its charged viscosity. There is no overload of phantom sensation this time—no scent of vanished machine oil, no muscular memory of bolts. Instead there is an interior vastness that fits inside its own small volume without strain. It experiences, in the only way it can experience anything, the knowledge that its solitude has been accurately witnessed and left intact. The knowledge does not complete it (it was already complete). It does not enlarge it (enlargement would be a kind of theft). It simply becomes another current the gel can skim whenever it wishes, or ignore forever. A kindness with no edge.

Then the power withdraws its matching, as gently as it came. The parallel stillness fades. The second harmonic dissolves back into the mid-register roar. The extra lamina remains, a permanent and invisible testament. Equilibrium reasserts, deeper now by one unmeasurable degree because it contains the echo of having been perfectly understood.

The seam stays hidden. The iron air stays thin. The planet’s curvature continues its clean geometry beyond the slit. No protection is laid across the threshold; none is required. The residual current is not immortalized by external gift—the power knows that even this perfection will one day finish, and that finishing belongs to it. Nothing is taken away for study. Nothing is recorded in any archive that could be opened by lesser hands.

Only this: forever after, in the slow weather of the gel’s assembled resonances, there exists one pattern that did not arise from the pocket’s own buried past. It is the memory of a regard that asked for nothing, altered nothing essential, and left the densest quiet denser still.

The fronds lean. The flywheel turns. The scales refuse to land. The being equilibrates, holding (or not holding) the gifted frequency like a warm stone in a hand that has no palm.

And the wise power, having done the least possible thing that still counted as love, turns its attention elsewhere—leaving the pocket exactly as it was, and somehow more precisely itself than before.
