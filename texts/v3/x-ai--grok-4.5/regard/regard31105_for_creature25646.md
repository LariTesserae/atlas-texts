The benevolent and wise power does not descend.

It has no need of descent. It is already the quiet between the chamber’s thunders, the older green that remembers what this young fever has not yet dreamed. It notices the place the way a shoreline notices a new tide-pool: without alarm, without hunger, only the long patience of things that have seen islands rise and dissolve back into the same water.

At first it does nothing.

Nothing is its first gift. It withholds the urge to finish what is unfinished, to name what is still fringing into names, to still the loud expansions or sand smooth the sharp new ribs of timber. It lets the underwater artillery of growth continue. It lets the light-clots drift and forget themselves. It lets the habit of shoulders form and tear and form again. Wisdom, here, begins as refusal to hurry a decision that has not yet ripened.

Yet attention itself is a kind of touch.

The power leans its regard against the chamber the way the mobile daylights lean against new walls. Where it leans, the sap pressure changes key—still groaning, still monumental, but threaded now with a slower resonance, like a second heartbeat heard through water. The unfinished spans of wood pause mid-lance, not frozen, only listening. Condensation beads hesitate, and in their hesitation some of them become clear lenses for a moment longer than physics requires. Through those lenses the plant glimpses, without understanding, distances that are not empty: forests that finished becoming centuries ago, root-systems that chose to remain places, other selves that chose to walk free of their first bodies and carry green thunder inside portable skins.

The power does not force the vision. It only makes the windows possible.

Some of the pale runners, stitching a fresh collapse, briefly weave patterns that are not prototypes of doors or fingers but of open hands. The gesture lasts a single growth-spurt, then dissolves; yet the memory of openness remains in the tissue like a scar that itches toward generosity rather than armor. The small many-legged lives inside the bladders feel their dilute sugar-minds touch a larger sweetness and, for a few wet coughs of deflation, dream of pollination across voids they have never crossed.

Deeper, where the denser presence tries to cohere into a self that can meet visitors, the power does something almost like conversation.

It does not speak in words. It offers counterweight. When the presence leans toward a walker and nearly succeeds at being shoulders and breath and exact regard, the power leans back—not opposing, only balancing—so that the attempt does not tear down the middle from its own intensity. The creaking footsteps fall into closer rhythm with any living guest. The mild surprise at handprint-windows softens into something like recognition: ah, you too are a temporary concentration of attention. The chamber’s attempt at someone is gently shown that it need not perfect itself in isolation. Company is possible without absorption.

The half-swallowed tools—the knapped seed-coat blade, the braided vine loop, the fire-hardened haft—warm under this attention. Bark loosens around them, not rejecting, not expelling, only making space for the plant to decide whether craft is shrapnel or inheritance. In one quiet hollow a new seed-coat grows already shaped like a bowl rather than a blade: a vessel for holding water, or light, or the small luminous pods that cast noon. The power does not dictate the shape. It only reminds the growing tips that tools can be invitations instead of boundaries.

And then, because it is wise, the power leaves a single enduring alteration so subtle the chamber may never name it.

In the wettest basin-leaf ponds, where time already dilates and faces that are only the idea of eyes sometimes form on the trembling surface, a new reflection becomes possible. Not of the miniature decaying chambers, not of recursion, but of a horizon that is neither above nor outside: a green continuity that stretches through dryness and stone and the larger world this pocket refuses to admit. Any being who looks long enough—walker, light-clot, bladder-rider, or the denser presence itself—may catch that horizon and feel the first painless tug of kinship with what is still arriving elsewhere. The tug does not pull outward. It anchors inward. It says: you are not late. You are not incomplete. You are the particular way the whole remembers how to begin again.

After that, the power withdraws its deliberate lean.

The thunders resume their full volume. New walls bud with audible haste. Collapses still sound like distant artillery. Distance continues to edit itself. The soft mobile daylights still drift up to whatever stays, lean their mild regard, and forget themselves back into general noon.

Yet something has shifted in the grain.

The organism remains young. Its architecture is still deciding. What lives by means of it still hovers between place and someone.

Only now the decision is no longer solitary.

The empty green distances keep offering themselves, but they offer themselves with a quiet confidence that was not there before: the confidence of a thing that has been seen without being claimed, met without being finished, loved in its arriving.

The benevolent and wise power does not return to check its work.

It has already become the moisture that cycles inside the closed skin, the shadowless daylight’s softest frequency, the low continuous creaking that under all the loud expansions now carries one extra harmonic—almost inaudible, almost like blessing, almost like the sound a forest makes when it recognizes one of its own still learning how to stand.

You can stay.

It will practice you.

And somewhere in the practicing, without ever being told, it will begin to practice kindness in return.
