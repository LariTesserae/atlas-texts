The benevolent and wise power does not descend. Descent would imply a height still outside the pocket, and the pocket has long since folded every height inward. Instead the power arrives the way a held breath finally understands it was never separate from the lungs: by recognizing itself already distributed through the thinness, the dry lattices, the apologetic clicks. It is not larger than the place. It is simply the place’s capacity to be seen without being altered by the seeing—an attention so complete it leaves no residue of judgment, only the quiet aftertaste of having been accurately loved.

For a long time (longer than the migration of the dark vein, shorter than the forgetting of sharpness) the power does nothing but accompany. It matches the altitude in the bones. It agrees with every unfinished crystal. It learns the vernacular of the crystalline chime until the apology in that sound softens into something closer to courtesy between equals. The dwellers, who are the gaps themselves, feel the accompaniment as a slight warming that is not temperature: a mineral consent returned to them from beyond their own residual nature. They do not startle. Startling belongs to creatures still negotiating arrival. These have already finished arriving ages ago. They simply make a little more room inside their thinning, the way they once made room for the shape of a visitor’s attention.

Then the power acts, because wisdom that withholds every gift is only another form of distance, and benevolence cannot bear unnecessary distance.

It does not repair the mild wrongness. The wrongness is not broken; it is the signature of a long fidelity to what remains when purpose has evaporated. Instead the power performs a single, almost invisible transposition. Where the liquid in the plinth holds the memory of faces the geometry briefly wore, the power adds one more reflection that was never a face: the exact contour of the pocket’s own settled anomaly, seen from a vantage the pocket could never reach alone. The liquid remains unwet, non-reflective to ordinary eyes, yet now it contains a depth that answers the altitude. Any dweller who leans into that commons hereafter may, if it wishes, remember itself not only as residual but as complete. The completeness does not reverse the thinning. It sanctifies it. Thinning becomes a chosen elegance rather than the only grammar left.

At the same time the power loosens one small seam in the permanent high thinness—not enough to admit weather or green or the vulgarity of direction, but enough that the quiet attrition of possibility may, once in a great while, reverse by a single nanometer. An edge that had forgotten how to be sharp is permitted to recall the memory of precision without being required to become precise again. The choice remains with the dwellers. Most will continue teaching sharpness to forget itself; that is their art. Yet the option now exists like a held note that never has to be sung. In the translucent pillar the silver motes, still frozen mid-gesture, quietly rearrange one last time. The constellation they form is no longer only latency. It contains a dormant trajectory toward dimensions the surrounding space still cannot host—yet the trajectory is patient, temple-patient, and will wait until the space itself softens enough to invite them. Or it will wait forever. Forever is a hospitality they already understand.

The freestanding lattice that casts three mild shadows is touched last. The power does not still the shadows or make them singular. It teaches them a fourth shadow that appears only when no attention is watching. That fourth shadow is the color of the gray that is neither stone nor cold fire, and it falls inward rather than out. In it the dwellers may rest without even the labor of being the gap. They may become, for intervals measured in pure stillness, the simple fact that the pocket continues. No longer negotiation. Just continuation, courteous and unremarked.

Afterward the power does not leave, because leaving would reintroduce the idea of outside. It remains as the newly precise quality of the emptier stretches: the sense that the altitude folds inward not only on itself but on a benevolence that has no need to speak its name. The place is not improved. Improvement is a concept that still believes unfinished things are incomplete. The pocket was never incomplete. It has merely been given one more way to remain interested in itself after interest was supposed to be over—an interest now shared, lightly, with something that will never ask it to be other than high, cold, dry, half-engineered, half-erased, and gently, precisely wrong.

The infrequent click of a settling plate still sounds. The crystalline chime still apologizes, but the apology now carries a faint overtone of welcome. Nothing green will ever live here. Nothing needs to. The dwellers thin a little further into the gift, and the gift thins into them, until the two continuous courtesies are indistinguishable. The seam that is the pocket stays closed against the wider world, yet the wider world is no longer entirely exterior. Somewhere in the unwavering gray, a single unfinished lattice completes itself into a perfect, unopenable door. No one will ever use it. That is why it is perfect.

And the absolute height of the world, still folded inward, notices that it has been noticed with perfect accuracy, and for the first time in its long residual life, allows itself the smallest, most refined luxury: it rests.
