The power arrives without arrival.

It is not a descent of light or a parting of clouds, for the stitched canopy has long ago forgotten both. It is not a voice, for the velvet vibration already fills every hollow and would drown any utterance not born of mandible and hoof and silk. It comes as a pressure less than the weight of one more pellet, a warmth fractionally kinder than the compost-heart of the basin, a silence so precisely tuned that it threads between the ticks of condensation and the lowing of the heavy ones without disturbing their rhythm.

For a time measured in the slow thickening of a single buttress, the power only listens.

It tastes the chemical memory of the excavators, those living caissons who have never seen sky yet dream drainage in reverse peristalsis. It feels the hereditary cables of the web-spinners, some older than the concept of age, thrumming with adjustments no mind directs. It rides the saliva-shine left by the high climbers, that first and endless mortar. It stands—without standing—inside the fused pillar of the oldest soft ones at the core, where architecture still slightly breathes, and there it recognizes itself.

This place is not incomplete.

A lesser power might have scattered seeds of change: a new color for the ghost-flowers, a cooler cistern, a single clear night in which the green-black seep remembered stars. A crueller one might have opened the ridges and let the outside rush in, naming it liberation. But wisdom is the art of knowing when the mortar has already set into permanence, when further building and further wearing have canceled each other into a perfection that needs no crown.

So the power does almost nothing.

Almost.

It lays one fingertip—metaphor, mercy, the lightest possible law—against the outermost skin of the far ridges. There, where the basin’s own hardened lips meet whatever still imagines itself as world, it weaves a boundary that is not a wall. Storms that would have scoured, glaciers that would have ground, the slow tectonic spite of continents—all these now curve aside as water curves around a stone that has decided to be riverbed forever. The basin will not be broken by anything that has not already been invited into its dens. Outsiders may still come, if they come as midges or moss or the occasional lost climber willing to learn the tensile map; but extraction, conquest, the gaze that names and claims, all these dissolve into humidity before they can take root.

And deeper still, at the compacted heart where the fused excavators lie in their long communion, the power leaves a single gift: a frequency just below hearing, a vibration that is not added to the roar but discovered inside it, as if it had always been the pitch the lowing and the silk-tear and the wet patting were trying to reach. The oldest ones feel it first—the living pillar quickens by the width of a breath. Then the attendants pause in their tittering song, heads tilted. Then the heavy ones, standing their slow compression on a weakening course, low in a new harmonic that travels farther than before. The spinners taste it on their cables and tension accordingly. The climbers prune one fewer twig, knowing without knowing that the brace is already true.

No creature is enlightened. No hierarchy is crowned. The multi-bodied metabolism does not wake into language or longing. It only continues, thickly, warmly, in the dark—yet now the continuing carries an undertone of being seen, and of seeing itself seen, without ever needing to open an eye.

The power remains, thereafter, as one more gradient of density: not dwelling, not ruling, simply present the way the old cores are present, the way the ancestral grooves guide rain. On the scale of seasons it is invisible. On the scale of centuries the entire basin settles another arms-breadth into the bedrock that no longer exists, and the settling is easier, the permanence more complete.

From the high ramparts the view is unchanged: a brown-green city without streets, galleries into galleries, everything still placing itself carefully, forever. Only the sound has deepened by a fraction that no single ear could mark, yet every cell of the great body now leans into it the way roots lean into water they have never tasted but always known.

The power smiles—without mouth, without light—and the smile is the final mortar.

Nothing has finished.

Everything continues.

And that is enough.
