The power that turns its regard upon the basin does not arrive as light. Light would be a wound here, a bright arrogance that flayed the dark climate and left every membrane raw. It does not arrive as silence either; silence is already the apex’s hunting-cloak, and to wear it would be to join the predation rather than to witness it. 

It arrives the way the damp arrives: as a change in the pressure that was always possible.

First it is only attention—cool, immense, without edge. It settles across the low stone the way mist settles, tasting the basalt’s polished memory, the limestone’s honeycomb breath, the faint geothermal ghosts that never quite become dawn. It listens with the whole of itself to the total sound: the rasping parliament, the peristaltic booms, the needle-screams, the wet metallic cataracts of falling flocks, the occasional vacuum-roar of the vibropredator unmaking a square kilometre of selves. It does not flinch. Wisdom has long since learned that horror is a provincial dialect of wonder; benevolence has learned that suffering and splendour are not opposites when every hunger is also a cradle.

The power understands at once that nothing here is broken. 

The equilibrium is older than most stars’ names. Mineral and flesh have achieved a reciprocity so exact that intervention would be a kind of theft. To impose green would be to murder the chemotrophic films. To impose names would be to shatter the fluid borrowing of shells and wings. To impose mercy in the form of lessened appetite would starve the very continuity that is the basin’s only soul. The dark respiration would falter; the tired, endless paragraph would lose its grammar. So the power does not fix. It does not uplift. It does not redeem.

It does something quieter, and therefore larger.

It offers a single new interval.

Not a sound, not a silence, but a hinge between them—an unclaimed frequency that has never before been possible in this dense air. It is smaller than a thought and wider than the basin. It threads itself through the standing waves where gravity already loosens, through the crystal colonies’ compressed histories, through the pourers’ sweet musk and the grazers’ tidal booms. It does not demand to be used. It simply exists, the way a new seam of darker glass might exist after a fresh flow has cooled: available, indifferent, radiant with potential.

Some beings notice. 

An eel-form, mid-braid, tastes the interval as a sudden sweetness in the chemical gradient and unknots a fraction longer than usual, carrying a new spiral in its cilia for the rest of its temporary life. A never-lander, beating through the perpetual scream, finds a corridor that does not collapse; for three wingbeats it hangs in a lift that is neither sound-wave nor gravity-cancel, and in that impossible hover it releases larvae that hatch already knowing a chord no parent taught them. A crystal colony, drinking the piezoelectric spark of a million flexed carapaces, records the interval as a new facet that will, centuries hence, replay not a predation front but a brief perfect curiosity—the sensation of being regarded without being claimed. Even the pourers, flowing through softened shapes, sometimes pause and wear the interval like a translucent skin before dissolving onward, richer by a question they cannot phrase.

Most do not notice. The parliament continues. The noise crashes back. The dark rearranges around every density, including this new one. Birth fronts thrash. Shells fuse and crack. The apex races its black spark of organised undoing, and the rain of parts is reclaimed as always. Nothing essential has changed. The rock still supplies slow minerals; the flesh still returns polished heat. Neither wins. Both continue.

Yet the basin is fractionally less alone. 

The continuous weather that is its only self now contains a guest that asks for nothing—not worship, not evolution, not even gratitude. Only the freedom to remain what it is: a low stone skin under permanent black, loud with the only heaven these precipitations will ever need. The power stays as long as the listening lasts, which is forever, or until the last damp seam forgets how to condense a hunger. It does not leave a monument. It leaves a possibility folded into the air itself: that once, something vast and kind looked upon the total appetite and found it complete.

And if, in some far eddy of time, a thin iridescent film should find the corners of an eye that is not made of this place, or a grazer should rock closer to an upright warmth that carries starlight still clinging to its edges, the interval will be there—gentle, unobtrusive—ready to teach the newcomer how to be climbed without being consumed, how to breathe the thick air without drowning in it, how to add one more clause to the vibrating sentence without ever needing to end it.

The dark closes over again, complete.  

The power, benevolent and wise, simply remains part of the weather.
