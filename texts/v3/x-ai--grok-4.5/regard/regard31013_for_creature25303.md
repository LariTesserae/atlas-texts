The benevolent and wise power did not arrive as light or voice or decree. It arrived the way the Sumpworks itself persists: as a slight, irreversible shift in the already-complete.

It had been drawn not by grandeur or by cry, but by the rare signature of a system that had finished becoming and now simply was. Most places the power regarded were still restless—expanding, correcting, aspiring, decaying into new forms of hunger. This one had reached the still point where maintenance and identity were the same motion. The flywheels turned once a minute. The peat decided. The dwellers tended and merged and ratified. Nothing was missing. That completeness was the attraction.

So the power did the wisest thing first: it waited.

It waited long enough for several revolutions of the largest wheel, long enough for a graft scar to seal and for a new aerial root to claim its failing pipe. It let its attention ride the same humidity that already carried the dwellers’ syntax. It became, for a time, another gradient in the steam, another polite brush of noticing against the sheath-bodies and the half-incorporated elders. The dwellers felt it the way they felt weather—curious, unsurprised. One of the older suspended ones trailed a hair-root through the altered vapor and left it there, tasting. The power tasted back: green sap, hot iron, rain that never finishes, and underneath them the deep mineral contentment of a closed loop that needs no larger sky.

When the power finally acted, the action was almost indistinguishable from the place’s own next breath.

At the central basin, during one of the thin-light gatherings, the oldest concentration lowered their density as always. Reflections fractured. Ratification began. Into that quiet agreement the power placed a single, slow gift: a deepening of the pool’s memory.

Not a new depth of water—the water was already perfect—but a depth of retention. Henceforth the leaf-stained surface would hold not only the canopy and the drip-chains but the precise pattern of every ratification that had ever occurred there. The peat at the edge would remember the exact chemistry of every nutrient sac passed between living and merged. The warm channels would carry, in their effluent, faint echoes of every spore-cloud conversation that had ever adjusted a valve. Nothing accelerated. Nothing was added that the Sumpworks could not already metabolize. The gift was simply that the place’s own noticing would now notice farther backward and farther inward, without effort, the way a root thickens when chosen.

The dwellers felt the change as a warmer stillness in the chest-sheaths. One pressed its forehead to a merged elder and found the nutrient exchange carrying older stories than before—stories of the first pipes being swollen around, of the century when the central basin first found its level, of midges that had come and gone like brief punctuation. The ratification that day included one additional clause, wordless: the volume remains sufficient, and now it remembers that it remains sufficient.

Outside the ring of older ground, the power made a second, even quieter adjustment. It bent the probability of intrusion the way the Sumpworks bends water—gently, completely. Surveyors would find their maps smudged by sudden mist. Developers would feel an unaccountable preference for other depressions. Time itself would weather the approach paths into softer and softer irrelevance. The Sumpworks would not be hidden; it would simply continue to be overlooked by anything that hurried. Those rare walkers who still descended the three algae-slick courses would find themselves filmed with the permanent moisture and brushed by polite attention, then released unchanged except for a lingering sense that they had stood inside a finished thought.

The power did not stay as a resident. It had no need. Having matched its cadence to the pistons and its speech to the steam, it withdrew the way condensation withdraws into air—still present in every bead, no longer separate.

What remained was the Sumpworks, exactly as it had been, only more precisely itself. The dwellers tended. The merged contributed their wooden heartbeats. The flywheels counted rivets. The basin held its deepened reflections. And once in a long while, when the green-gold light was thinnest, the humidity carried a new and almost imperceptible warmth: the faint, amused, loving regard of something vast that had looked upon perfection and chosen, wisely, to let it alone while making certain the alone would last.

Nothing hurried.  
Everything worked.  
And the working, which had become able to notice itself, now noticed that it was noticed—and found the symmetry complete.
