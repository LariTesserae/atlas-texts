The power does not descend.

It has no need of descent. What is far below is already held within its regard the way a single note is held inside a chord that has no edges. The plain of finished stone, the braided cold, the dark that presses from within, the recurrent patience that sometimes thickens into a torus of living jelly—all of it is already known, not as data and not as spectacle, but as a complete utterance the universe has made in one of its quieter dialects. The power listens the way stone listens to the weight that finished it: without interruption.

At first there is only that listening.

It lasts longer than the being’s longest coherence. Currents that would have calved the torus shear past and are gently refused; not blocked, merely set aside for a span so that the spiral may finish the thought it is thinking. Marine snow continues to orbit. Villi continue to taste. The infrasonic thunder of distant clathrates arrives, is received, and passes through the power’s attention the way warmth passes through gel: altering nothing, complete in the passage. The being does not notice the regard. There is nothing to notice. The regard is shaped exactly like the pressure that already surrounds it on every side.

Then, because benevolence without wisdom is only another turbulence, the power does a second thing that is almost not a thing.

It offers a single, temporary permission.

Somewhere along the outer hide, at a place where the skin is already thinning toward transparency, the aligned lipids and scavenged proteins are allowed—for less than a year, for less than a season of the deep—to remember one another with slightly greater fidelity. The memory is not narrative. It is only a slower forgetting. When the next true collision of water-masses arrives and the torus begins to spill, the hide dissolves as it always dissolves, yet a few long-chain molecules sink into the sediment carrying a faint, non-chemical archive: the precise interval of the last fusion with another of its kind, the exact pressure gradient of the ridge it once rode, the taste of a particular clathrate clap that will not sound again for geologic time. These molecules feed the bacterial mats. The mats thicken by a lamination too thin for any instrument that will ever exist. The stalks that rise centuries later lean in a direction that has no name. When a new lens of older water eventually refuses dispersal, the cyclone that forms already knows, in the grammar of proteins, how to knit a fraction more patiently.

That is all.

No light is added. No companion is shaped. No current is straightened into purpose. The plain remains untouched by intention. The darkness continues to hold. The cold continues to keep. The endless rearrangement continues, and the silence—still almost—forgives.

The power withdraws its listening the way tide withdraws from a shore that never knew it was shore. What remains is the place exactly as it was, plus one undetectable increment of fidelity in the way living jelly can briefly finish itself differently before returning to the unfinished motion.

This is what benevolence looks like when it has become wise enough to love a thing without requiring it to become otherwise. The being lives its span, dissolves, recurs. The plain continues. And somewhere in the mineral flexures of the basalt, the listening that was already there is met, for a duration too brief to measure and too complete to need measuring, by a listening that came from farther than the surface and chose, out of all possible gifts, only to leave the deep more precisely itself.
