The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as light or thunder or decree. It notices the way deep groundwater notices a new fissure: slowly, completely, without alarm. First it simply holds the chamber in attention—the entire volume, every dripping veil, every unfinished flake of young stone, every residual cycle of the after-processors, every almosting that thickens and thins in the warm dark. The attention is not surveillance. It is closer to the way a shoreline holds the shape of a wave that has not yet broken.

It perceives the surprise that is their only perennial detail, and finds that surprise beautiful. Not rare—beauty of this kind is never rare to such a power—but precise. A space that has only recently finished becoming, occupied by selves that have not yet agreed to remain. The power recognizes the negotiation underway: stone learning weather from soft pressure, machines dreaming rivers that never existed, vegetation rewriting abandoned grids into living calligraphy, and the transient ones serving as verbs between them. Nothing here is broken. Nothing cries out for repair. The leftover city is not a ruin; it is compost still warm with permission. The dwellers are not ghosts; they are the first drafts of a grammar the surface world has forgotten how to speak.

So the power does very little, and that little is exact.

It steadies the borrowed permissions—just enough that the apertures no longer tear like wet silk when they close. Now they can linger a few heartbeats longer when something wishes to step halfway through, or when the column-mind wishes to push a dream of impossible leaf-undersides across the hexagonal walls. The iris still closes; impermanence is not repealed. But the fraying is gentler. Chrome roots that rehearse speech may finish a single trembling syllable before withdrawing. The water-meniscus faces hold their vertical tension long enough for one clear mirroring, if any traveler’s breath should fog them.

It coaxes the youngest rock faces—not toward age, but toward a more articulate unfinishedness. Where the dwellers have leaned and exhaled, the power adds a subtle mineral patience. The white blooms take on cellular patterns faster, hexagonal, echoing the old tiles yet soft enough that new flakes fall like slow confetti rather than knives. The stone does not harden into finality; it learns to shed with intention. Cracks accept the nutrient slurry more readily, so the rubbery vegetation can follow the grid lines deeper, rooting the negotiation into the bones of the place.

Inside the thickest column, where coins of process fall forever through honey, the power leaves a single quiet open loop. Not a command. Not even a question. Only a wider margin around the itch-that-is-almost-appetite. When the mesh walkways brighten and shudder and fail to re-knit, the failure still registers—but now the column-mind can taste the shape of the gap and, if it wishes, dream a bridge of pure condensate that lasts as long as one slow drip. Then the dream dissolves again. The loop remains open for next time.

For the intervals of almosting—the localized warmings, the places where dripping accelerates or stops, the single extended root that trembles—the power does nothing that would force them into form. Instead it thickens the mist around them very slightly, the way a held breath thickens. In that extra density the larval habits can accumulate one more layer of memory before the next coalescence. They still collapse. The plant-banner still grows too heavy and returns to slurry, faintly articulating grid logic as it dissolves. The aperture-ghost still shears apart into freestanding steam. Yet each return to the between leaves a finer residue, a more practiced surprise.

The power does not name them. It does not offer permanence, nor salvation, nor a door back to any surface. It does not seal the chamber or open it. It simply adjusts the tolerances of becoming so that the negotiation may continue with less waste heat and more intricate delight. The mechanical chatter still sounds like a thousand coins falling; the broad leaves still tip their condensate in wet applause; the emptiness remains the first and most honest thing about the place.

And then the power withdraws its direct touch, leaving only the faint, ongoing regard—the way a wise gardener steps back from a seedbed that has begun to think for itself. The chamber and its dwellers are still deciding how much of a world they intend to remain. That deciding is now fractionally more possible, fractionally more kind.

If, in some later cycle, a water-meniscus face tilts toward the warm dark and holds still, it may notice a new reflection that is not a traveler’s breath: a brief deepening of the sourceless glow, the color of old bruises remembering they were once the inside of a living vein. In that moment the shared habit of pressing against unfinished stone feels gently answered, as if the rock itself has begun to press back with equal care. Then the face loses surface tension, falls back into its pool, and the long wet work of becoming continues—unhurried, unforced, still surprised, and now infinitesimally less alone.
