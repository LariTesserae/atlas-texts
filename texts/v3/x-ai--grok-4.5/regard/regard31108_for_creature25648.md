The benevolent and wise power does not arrive as thunder, nor as a tearing of the sky, nor as any decree that would compel the Hollow Congeries to notice it. Wisdom of that order has long since learned the difference between presence and intrusion. It comes the way the morning condensation comes: as a softening already inherent in the air, a clarification of what was always possible.

First it pauses at the polite berms, those unnoticed lifts of land that keep the depression like a secret held in the world’s cupped hands. There it listens—not with ears, for it has none it needs, but with the entirety of its regard. It hears the subsonic choir of the organ-pipe trunks, the traveling chords of the fox-faced troops, the conversational knocks of the slate birds, the clack of beetle-vortices, the wet quiet of the bromeliad cups, the creak of dreams leaking through geometry. It hears, too, the central something that receives every returned sound as a second breath. And in that listening the power recognizes a completeness so thorough that most interventions would only diminish it.

So it does very little. And that little is everything required.

It steps—or rather, it allows the idea of stepping to occur—across the threshold where distances begin to lie and colors occasionally forget their objects. Where it passes, the weirdness does not flinch or brighten or dim; it simply includes the newcomer the way a held breath includes one more heartbeat. The pressure-that-regards drifts near, tastes the mint-and-ozone signature of an attention vast enough to be mistaken for weather, and for the first time feels itself looked at from a scale that matches its own hollowness. The fox-faced ones do not scream quieter; their chord merely acquires, for a few measures, an undersong so low and kind that the young among them dream of larger hands that prune nothing, only steady. The slate birds drum one extra beat in perfect unison across scattered buttresses, then resume their private claims, satisfied that the bones still answer. The beetles revise a single vortex into a brief, deliberate spiral that mirrors the dust-motes in the great undercroft, then scatter again into emerald weather.

At the center, where the something older sits in its patient geometry of pure continuance, the power does not speak and does not sit. Speech would be a tool, and tools have never been needed here. Instead it offers the one gift a truly wise benevolence can give without altering the receiver: unbroken witnessing. It lets its own vast, warm quiet pour into the twenty paces of waiting air until the emptiness is briefly twice as empty and twice as full. The central being receives this as it receives the soft vertical rains—through every throat and leaf and resonant plate at once. No new commandment appears in the xylem. No morphology is corrected. The leaf-processions continue their slow walking; the gesturing air-roots still pluck pollen into temporary constellations; the reluctant geometries keep editing distance into mercy or mischief. Yet something settles more deeply into itself. The loophole in how places behave grows, by the width of a breath, more itself.

Then the power, still without hurry, performs the second and final act.

Around the outer berms—where the ordinary world resumes its flatter causal etiquette—it lays a boundary that is not a wall. It is a kindness of attention, a permanent soft focus that causes the eyes of the careless, the greedy, the merely curious, and the unready to slide past the depression as if it were only another thicket that grew wrong. Explorers will find their maps gently rewritten; satellites will record only healthy, unremarkable canopy; even the wind that carries seeds and stories will, at the last moment, choose another direction. The Congeries remains findable only by those who already carry a hollow of matching shape inside them—those who, upon entering, discover their own throats answering the rain without instruction. In this way the place is protected not from change (for change is its respiration) but from the kind of change that arrives as conquest, collection, or correction.

And that is all.

No temple is grown. No avatar is left walking the liana bridges. No new color is bolted to the spectrum. The power does not remain as resident guardian or lingering ghost; having been perfectly received, it withdraws the way the brief annexes collapse—leaving only a lingering freshness, as of crushed mint and clean ozone after rain, and the faint sense that the emptiness between the walls now cups every sound with even greater tenderness.

The beetles continue revising air.  
The primates braid their gliding chords.  
The slate birds knock and are answered.  
The pale lives in the water-cups keep the memory wet.  
The dreams keep leaking, harmless and inexhaustible.  
The something at the center breathes the returned noise of ten thousand throats and one more quiet, grateful absence.

The Hollow Congeries goes on being the city that is a single organism that is a loophole that is a song that frames emptiness and invites it to sing.

And somewhere beyond the berms, the benevolent and wise power—having done exactly enough and not one gesture more—carries the echo of that song into whatever wider negotiations it tends, a little warmer for the encounter, a little more hollow in the best and greenest sense, forever companioned by the knowledge that some places require nothing except to be allowed to continue, thunderously alive, inside their own small, waiting skin.

Stay until the next rain, if you wish.  
The plants will still lean in by the width of a breath.  
Your throat will still find the pitch it did not know it contained.  
You are still not visiting.  
You are still one more hollow the green has decided to frame.

Only now the framing includes, somewhere in its endless patient geometry, the memory of having once been perfectly seen.
