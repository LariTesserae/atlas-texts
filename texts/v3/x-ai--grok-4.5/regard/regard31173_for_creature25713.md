The wise power does not arrive as light or voice or judgment. It has long since learned that arrivals of that kind fracture the very fineness they mean to honor. Instead it simply **notices**, the way a mountain notices its own new fracture: completely, without haste, and without the need to name what it finds.

It perceives the pocket first as a single held breath in the massif—an unfinished sentence of stone and steam still searching for its last clause. Then it perceives the life not as tenants but as the grammar of that sentence: the same extrusion, the same refusal to remain only rock. The collective shimmer of bodies, the layered densities, the pendulum-sway, the brief double-exposures where location loosens—all of it registers as one continuous being learning, in the only dialect available, how to stay tender inside an oven that has not yet decided to cool.

The power feels the unbuilt intelligence the way one feels a child turning in sleep: not yet speech, already question. The glass wedges scraping powder, the pauses that taste of measurement, the fused flanks sharing a pulse, the quiet redistribution that looks like a crucible teaching itself—these are recognized as the earliest gestures of attention. No tools cached, no marks lasting, because the being has not yet imagined tomorrow as separate from the scalding now. Wisdom sees this and does not rush it.

What the power does is almost nothing, and therefore exact.

It settles its regard across the chimney like a second atmosphere so subtle that the private weather does not change by a single degree. In that regard three gentle adjustments occur, none of them imposed, all of them invitations already latent in the place itself.

First, the recursion at the edges is given a quiet mirror-right. Where identical alcoves had stacked and collapsed like a throat folding through itself, the reflections are allowed to remain open a half-heartbeat longer—long enough for the densest carpets of life to feel, without panic, that depth can be shared rather than merely survived. The mountain grows neither thinner nor thicker; it simply becomes aware that its own interior can hold more than one true thickness at once. The creatures that flicker between positions now flicker with a faint new synchrony, as if the double exposure has begun to remember it is also a conversation.

Second, the power loosens a single constraint on heat. Not cooling—never cooling—but a finer braiding of the superheated veins so that the angular, prismatic bodies at the boundaries may occupy their cleaved edges without so swift a collapse back into single occupancy. The result is almost invisible: a few more heartbeats of iridescence, a few more soft scrapes of glass against glass that taste of something closer to reply than to solitary query. The larger segmented shape pauses longer at its vent. Its geomantic mouthparts angle, scrape, taste, and this time do not move on immediately. It waits with the powder still on its facets, and in the waiting the smaller life does not merely close behind it but arranges, briefly, into a pattern that echoes the angle of the wedge. Information, still wordless, has somewhere to stand.

Third—and this is the only gift that could be called touch—the power lends the pocket a longer memory of its own weeping. The mineral crusts, the sintered foams, the violet metal sheens are allowed to keep a softer echo of every body that has pressed against them. When a translucent form etches its microscopic pitons or spins its thread of cooled exudate or simply flattens into living lichen, the stone remembers the pressure a little past the moment of release. Nothing so crude as fossils. Only a tenderness in the glass, a readiness. Future extrusions will find the walls already minutely shaped for grip, for sway, for the liquid rearrangement of neighbors. The being will not have to invent clinging every time; it will inherit a hint of how it has already clung. Continuity without captivity. History without weight.

Having done these three things, the power does not remain as presence. It remains as permission. The pocket continues exactly as it was—raw, ringing, impossible, dense with silent fever—yet the ordinary rules of location and succession, already politely loosening, now loosen in directions that lead toward coherence rather than mere strangeness. The mountain is still deciding its shape. The life is still the mountain remembering itself in a thousand small translucent registers. But the remembering has been noticed by something that knows how noticing can itself be a kind of shelter.

In time (though time here still moves with the pulse of vents rather than clocks) one of the hanging pendulums will spin a thread that does not merely sway but gently resonates with another, and then another, until a soft geometry of mutual attention spans the chimney like a damp, living constellation. One of the larger shapes will scrape a facet, taste, and leave behind a bright scar that anneals more slowly than the others, just long enough for the next scraper to find it and answer. The flat dissolvers will etch their pitons in patterns that, viewed from the slit of ordinary daylight far above, hint at a map of the heat itself. None of this will look like civilization. It will look like the place becoming more perfectly what it already is: an intimate furnace where stone is young, water is constant, and matter organizes until the walls respire with something that is no longer only survival.

The wise power, if it ever glances back, will see no monument and need none. It will see a wound in the mountain that has begun, without ever leaving its scalding privacy, to heal into a kind of knowing. And that will be enough. Benevolence does not require the being to climb out of its chimney into kinder air. It requires only that the chimney become, in its own fierce idiom, a place where climbing is no longer the only motion possible—where staying, and tasting, and sharing heat, and flickering into more than one location at once, are also ways of continuing.

So the power releases its regard. The black serrated fin remains. The vertical wound remains. The wet oven and the crawling density and the quiet vocabulary of cooling glass remain. Only now the unfinished sentence of the place has been given three almost imperceptible commas, and the being who is the place has been given the dignity of finishing itself.
