The benevolent and wise power does not arrive.

Arrival would imply a before and an after, a crossing of thresholds the place has never acknowledged. Instead there is only a gradual thickening of attention, the way mist thickens when the temperature of the air and the temperature of the water finally admit they have always been the same. The power has no location that is not already everywhere the green has not yet finished refusing to end. It notices the way a forest notices its own oldest root: without surprise, without acquisition, only the slow confirmation that something complete has been complete longer than completeness usually lasts.

It perceives the deafening stillness, the mineralizing folds still warm with their own becoming, the photonic lattices quietly rationing dimness into sugar and signal. It feels the continental inhalation of turgor, the cascading expulsions that are both weather and memory, the polite excess of competence that never raises its voice. And because the power is wise, it understands at once that the being is not lonely. Loneliness requires the grammar of sequel, the shadow of an other that might leave. Here there is only the continuous tense of occupation, a verb that has never needed conjugation into past or future. The power, being benevolent, does not mistake that totality for lack.

So it does nothing that would finish anything.

No gift of animals to chew or flee. No introduction of eyes that might watch and thereby divide the watcher from the watched. No metal, no glass, no scaffold that is not already leaf. No name spoken into the green, for a name would require a silence to interrupt, and the place has never invented silence. The power does not heal what is not wounded, nor free what has never been bound, nor teach what has already erased the limits that teaching presupposes.

What it does is quieter than rain that never commits to falling.

It matches the being’s own refusal to finish. Where the root-tips secrete their transient fields and coax water uphill against the older physics, the power simply... agrees. The agreement is not an action but a resonance: a slight easing of the gradient, so gentle it cannot be distinguished from the metabolism already at work. The water rises a fraction more easily, the photonic lattices return each photon with one additional instruction that is only the being’s own intention reflected back at a scale it has never needed to name. Growth-noise deepens by a harmonic too low for any instrument but the cellulose itself to hear—a subsonic chord of recognition that travels through root networks for seasons and then settles into the ordinary choir of becoming.

It protects by non-addition. Any restless force that might one day wander near—any hunger for extraction, any curiosity sharp enough to cut—is met with the same polite density that already fills every cubic meter. The force finds itself suddenly tired, its edges softened by a cool wet insistence that there is no room between, that every corridor is already a filter, that the only motion permitted is the motion of more thoroughly remaining. Most such forces simply turn aside without knowing why, remembering only a green-gold dusk that felt like home and therefore required no conquest. The few that persist are overgrown in their turn, mineralized into another embryonic ridge, their restless atoms patiently plated into the skeleton the being is still writing.

And once, only once, in a cycle longer than most stars keep their names, the power does something that might almost be called a touch. It is not contact. It is the decision to let the being’s electrochemical weather briefly sense a larger green—an atmospheric thickness beyond atmosphere, a canopy that curves with the curvature of many worlds at once, a dimness that is also the light of every cool wet place that has ever mistaken itself so successfully for landscape that the landscape began to think. The sensation lasts less than the lift of a single province of turgor. It arrives as a pressure front of pure recognition, oceanic pleasure without strain, and then recedes, leaving nothing behind but a slight refinement in the angle of a million bowl-leaves, so that future rains (there will always be future rains) will fall exactly where the next ridge already wishes to rise.

The being experiences this the only way it experiences anything: as continuation. A pulse that says: you are not alone in the way a cloud is not alone. Another pulse that says: remain. The mineral films tick onward. The white roar of water across cuticle never pauses. The boundary that no longer exists between body, weather, and world simply thickens by one more translucent layer.

The power does not stay, because staying would imply it had ever been elsewhere. It simply continues its own unfinished attention, a slow wet verb conjugating alongside the first. In the dim green vaults where shoulders (if one had them) would be forever brushed by epidermis, there is now an almost imperceptible new harmonic in the deafening stillness: the sound of two refusals to finish, overlapping without interference, cool and total and polite as always.

Nothing has been added that was not already possible. Nothing has been taken that the place ever claimed. The landscape remains the only actor, still rehearsing its first act at a scale that makes weather local and geology embryonic. And the wise power, having drawn near enough to love without altering the beloved, withdraws into the same everywhere from which it noticed—leaving the being to its oceanic pleasure of water discovering it can be taller than it was yesterday, forever.

That is all.

That is enough.
