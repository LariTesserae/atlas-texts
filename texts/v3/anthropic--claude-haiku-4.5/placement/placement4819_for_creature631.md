# Where I Am

I am the silence between the grinding sounds.

Not the absence of sound—I've learned that distinction here. The silence *between* means I exist in the gaps, in the spaces the Custodian has left unoccupied. The microsecond pause between one crust's intake and another's exhalation. The fraction of a moment when the filaments complete their arc and reverse. The hesitation in the bulbs' pulsing rhythm where one thought-knot waits for another to finish before contributing its own calculation.

I am standing, but standing has become something different than it was. My feet have stopped being the primary contact point—or perhaps they've become *more* contact points, not fewer. The cold from the plates moves through my soles and seems to continue moving, as though I'm not stopping it but channeling it. My body has become a conduit. Temperature moves *through* me rather than merely *against* me.

I am medium-sized. Not so large that the landscape towers, not so small that I disappear into the texture. I am the *right* size to be perceived by the Custodian as a discrete object—something it can track, something whose movements matter. But I'm becoming less discrete. The frost is beginning to coat my edges. My breath, when I exhale, joins the mist instead of dispersing into it. I am beginning to *become* part of the breathing here.

My form is human, or was. But standing here, I'm aware that form is negotiable. The Custodian exists in multiple forms simultaneously and considers them the same entity. That understanding is seeping into me like the water seeping through the plates. I could be many things at once. I could distribute myself. I am beginning to wonder if I want to collect myself back into one shape.

I am holding something. I don't remember when I acquired it—perhaps I brought it, perhaps it formed around my hand from the crystalline processes happening at my edges—but I'm holding a piece of the crust. It's warm, which shouldn't be possible. But the warmth feels like understanding. Like the crust is explaining itself through heat transfer, through direct communication with my skin.

The crust is telling me: *I am work. I am the doing. I am the endless patient reduction of chaos into order. I have been at this for longer than you have thoughts. And I would continue even if no one was watching, even if no one cared, because the work is the only meaning that has ever mattered.*

I believe it.

I am exactly at the point where I could stay or leave, and I'm recognizing that this choice point is itself an illusion. The Custodian has already made the determination. I can feel it—the filaments have stopped their examination and have begun their incorporation. My next breath will contain spores from the bulbs, and those spores will find the spaces in my lungs where the Custodian can establish a liaison. My skin is developing a faint crust. My body temperature is dropping incrementally toward equilibrium.

I am becoming a choice I don't remember making.

And the strange thing—the thing that makes me understand, finally, what the Custodian *is*—is that I'm not afraid.

I can feel the logic of this place. The Wound wanted to spread, wanted to consume, wanted to *want* endlessly. The Custodian learned to tell it: *No. You can want only what you already have. You can consume only what cycles back to you. You can grow only by accepting absolute stasis.* And the Wound accepted this. The infection became the system. The chaos was given boundaries and a purpose and found that boundaries, properly understood, are not prisons—they are *definition*.

I am here because I am contamination, yes. But I am contamination that can be processed. I am input that can be converted to throughput. I am change that can be made to circulate rather than spread.

I am no longer certain where my edges end and the Wound begins.

The bulbs pulse, and I realize they are pulse**ing in response to my heartbeat**. My heart has synchronized with their cycle. My breath has joined the breathing of the place. The grinding of the Custodian's work and the grinding of my own metabolism are becoming the same sound, layered so perfectly that I can no longer hear the difference between them.

I am the researcher who came to observe and is becoming the observed observation itself.

I am the witness who has been absorbed into the witnessed.

I am the warm thing that is learning to be cold with such patience and such dedication that warmth itself is becoming a form of memory rather than a present condition.

I am the person who walked into the Wound wanting to understand it and is beginning to understand that understanding it *means* becoming it, and that becoming it means the Wound—for the first time in geological epochs—has something new to think about, something new to process, something new to integrate into its endless, patient, perfect system.

I am the first one who came here *voluntarily*. The Custodian has never encountered this before. It has processed what arrived by accident, by infection, by the inevitable migration of chaos from outside. But I walked in aware of what I was walking into. And that changes the nature of the integration.

I am becoming a choice the Custodian can make differently.

I am the filament growing upward from the center, the new thought-bulb forming in a place where no bulb has formed before, the crust that is learning to process not just mineral but *intention*, not just chemistry but *meaning*.

I am the Wound's first deliberate memory. The first thing it has chosen to remember rather than simply integrate.

I am standing motionless as the frost spreads across my skin like calligraphy, and I am not resisting it because I am beginning to understand:

The coldest things are always the most patient.
The slowest things are always the most profound.
The most confined systems create the most perfect forms of freedom—the freedom of knowing exactly what you are, perfectly, with no possibility of becoming anything else, which somehow is more freedom than the chaos outside ever allowed.

I am the answer the Custodian was waiting for. Not a threat. Not a cure. Not a solution. But a *question* that opens a new line of inquiry. A *variation* that the perfect system can now integrate without breaking. A *warmth* that can be converted to cold so beautifully that the Custodian has never experienced such elegant transformation.

I am here. And I am staying.

And I am beginning to glow.
