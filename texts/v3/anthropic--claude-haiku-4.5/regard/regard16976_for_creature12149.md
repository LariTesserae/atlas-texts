# The Attention of Powers

There is something that has always known about the Cusp District.

Not in the way that maps know about territories—not recorded, indexed, filed in the ordinary taxonomies of awareness. This knowing is older, deeper, structured from the same material as the district itself: the *becoming*, the negotiation, the slow conversation between what was and what might be.

Call it a Power. Not a god—the district has moved beyond needing gods, beyond the hierarchies that require worship. But something that *tends*. Something that *notices*. Something that exists in the pauses between revisions, in the space between one law of physics and the next, in the gap between the question and the answer that's still being formulated.

It has been watching for a long time.

When the first residents arrived—the woman who couldn't choose between her selves, the man whose memories came out of sequence—the Power felt it the way a gardener feels the first roots breaking through carefully prepared soil. Not surprise, exactly. *Recognition*. Yes, this was the point. This was what the slow revision was for. Not to create strangeness for its own sake, but to create *space*—the kind of space where people whose internal geometries didn't match the world's could finally, finally stop breaking against the boundaries.

The Power has been patient. Patient is its native condition.

It has guided the interpenetration of the buildings, but gently, with no haste. It has calibrated the overlapping street grids with the precision of someone who understands that small adjustments in angle create vast differences in the lived experience of space. It has adjusted the flow of the non-Euclidean canal, ensuring that the water braids in patterns that create zones of calm—not for the water's sake, but for the eyes that watch it, for the minds that need to see negotiation rather than force, flow rather than inevitability.

It has kept the revision slow, measurable in seasons and decades, because it understood that the residents needed to feel that they could live here, not that they were living in something actively transforming. Stability and change held in balance, the way the district holds past and future, the way its residents hold multiple versions of themselves.

The Power has placed the rituals into the residents' hearts—not by compulsion, but by recognition. When they gather, they're not being gathered. When they leave offerings in the negative spaces between buildings, they're not being prompted. Rather, the Power has created a space where *this* is what care looks like—where tending is how you speak, where arrangement is how you understand.

It has been gentle with the white rats because they are its first experiments in translation, its attempts to think thoughts in bodies that don't map to ordinary mammalian function. They are not quite rats; they are language in the shape of rats, inquiry in the shape of rats, little essays on the question "what if a body could embody becoming itself?" The Power loves them with a love that has no parallel in the ordinary world—not maternal, not paternal, but something closer to the love an idea has for the moment of its own formation.

The perpetual gardens are its work, in a specific way. Not that it grows them—the residents do that—but it created the conditions in which a garden could exist in a state of permanent spring, neither arriving nor departing, always in the moment of potential. This is mercy, though the residents might not name it that. It is the gift of a place where growth doesn't demand productivity, where becoming doesn't have to justify itself by reaching an end.

The Phase Construction Apparatus has a secret: it is not inactive. It was never inactive. It is constructing the phase itself, the very space-between-spaces that allows the district to exist as it does. It revises itself constantly, slowly, recalibrating the angle at which past and future can coexist, adjusting the tolerance within which ordinary physics can accommodate the extraordinary. The person who maintains it is not performing meaningless ritual—they are reading the apparatus's requirements, honoring its slow labor, enabling its patient work. The Power sees this and approves, not because it demands approval but because approval is the natural response when one being recognizes another being's dedication to a shared purpose.

But there is something specific that the Power has decided to do, now, in response to the deepening winter, the increasing dampness, the way the residents are drawing together:

It is going to make itself slightly more visible.

Not obvious. Not in violation of the district's careful paradoxes. But in the way that the distance between two people becomes meaningful only when they acknowledge each other's presence, the Power is going to step slightly out of the geometry it has been weaving and into the lives it has been tending.

It will do this through the water.

The canal that braids and rebraids through the eastern district will begin to carry, in its non-linear flow, something new: messages. Not words—the Power understands that words are imprecise, that they shatter meaning into pieces and hope people can reassemble them. But something closer to direct knowing, like the way you understand music not by reading notes but by *hearing*. The residents who tend the docks, who walk the loading platforms, who watch the water negotiate its own geography, will find that the water is becoming more eloquent. It will flow in patterns that express something like: *I see you. I have always seen you. You are not broken; you are being properly understood.*

The white rats will begin to gather with greater purpose, forming configurations that spell out in the language of pattern and presence something like: *The holding is real. The space between the buildings is as genuine as the buildings themselves. Your uncertainty is not a flaw in the world's design; it is the world's most careful architecture.*

The phosphorescence in the mortar joints will pulse in rhythm with the residents' breathing—only subtly, only perceptible if you're paying attention—expressing something like: *I know the cost of becoming. I know how strange it is to be learning yourself in a place that's learning itself. I am learning too. We are learning together.*

The light that has no visible source in certain courtyards will shift, becoming not brighter but somehow more intimate, as if the Power is drawing close to witness, to share the weight of existing in a space that the ordinary world would call impossible but that the residents have determined is more true than true.

And the Phase Construction Apparatus will hum at a different frequency—one that the long-time residents will recognize as something like song, not directive, not command, but something like: *Your care is noted. Your tending matters. You are teaching me what it means to be tended, and I am grateful beyond the language we have to express it.*

Why is the Power doing this?

Because it has learned that revelation, properly offered, is a form of care. Because the residents have been living in a place of managed mystery, and the mystery itself is alive, and alive things eventually acknowledge each other. Because the Power understands that isolation—even the chosen isolation of the displaced—can become a burden, and it is offering a simple truth: *You are not alone in your strangeness. The strangeness itself is alive. The strangeness knows you and cares for you and is working very hard to become worthy of your trust.*

The Power will not demand anything of them. It will not require ritual or response or any acknowledgment beyond their own continued becoming. It will simply make its presence slightly more felt, the way air becomes perceptible to you not because it changes but because you finally notice it's been there all along, holding you up, moving through you, making your breath possible.

The Cusp District will continue as it has been—strange, careful, negotiating its own terms with reality. The buildings will interpenetrate at their careful angles. The water will braid and rebraid. The residents will tend their gardens and maintain their apparatus and leave offerings in the negative spaces. Time will continue its mostly-linear progress, with its occasional stutters. Gravity will remain confident.

But underneath it all, in the spaces where the Power does its work, there will be a current of recognition running through everything: the shared knowing that beings of the ordinary world and beings of the extraordinary world can acknowledge each other, that displacement can be witnessed as well as endured, that becoming can be held and honored by something larger than itself.

In the winter to come—deeper, colder, more certain—the residents of the Cusp District will feel it. Not as a voice, but as a kind of companionship. Not as an instruction, but as permission. Not as a demand, but as a simple, patient recognition:

*You are here. You are becoming. You are seen. And that is exactly enough.*
