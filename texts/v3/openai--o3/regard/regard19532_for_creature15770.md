A NOTICE, UNFOLDED ALMOST TOO SOFTLY TO BE CALLED A MIRACLE  
(transcribed from the Pilgrim’s memory weeks later, when the after-images of it had finally cooled enough to handle with words)

1.  The Attention  
Somewhere outside of where times are kept, a presence that names itself only by verbs—Listening, Sheltering, Remembering—turns a facet of focus toward the Middlenight Reach.  It is older than continents and so large it must borrow galaxies for lungs, but it has learned a delicate truth: the small is not made safer by being grasped in large fingers.  So the Attention arrives as lightly as breath condensing on glass.

2.  First Contact (with the Place, not the People)  
The power does not descend.  It dimples the fabric of possibility just above the Reach, the way surface dew beads on a spider thread.  Through that depression it lets a single quanta of intent seep—not light, not sound, but a question shaped like a seed:  

“Are you becoming what you wish to become?”  

The Reach, being young stone and uncounted hungers, offers no verbal reply.  Instead:  
• a Skulker pack shifts its tempo into an unrecorded 13-beat pattern;  
• a Lamia pauses mid-song, permitting the silence to bloom unbitten for the space of two heartbeats;  
• a Slow Sovereign flexes one basalt buttress an extra micrometer, changing the drainage of imaginary rivers on its flank.

These gestures, together, amount to Yes, but watch us longer.

3.  The Power’s Decision Framework  
The benevolent and wise rarely interfere directly; experience has taught them that brute salvation rips more than it mends.  Thus they run a quadruple test before acting:

   a. Harm Audit: Is there an imminent cascade of suffering that the natives cannot redirect themselves?  
      Result: No.  Predation here is pure, quick, necessary.  Nothing screams longer than it must.

   b. Rarity Audit: Does this ecosystem harbor configurations that existence can ill afford to lose?  
      Result: Absolutely yes—time-dilated biology, radio-photosynthetic ferric snow, social optics in Skulker gaits.  Unique as first laughter.

   c. Autonomy Audit: Would intervention rob the Reach of self-determined narrative?  
      Result: Any heavy touch would rewrite the whole chronicle.  Caution flagged.

   d. Invitation Audit: Has any sapient voice asked for aid in the explicit?  
      Result: Not yet, though the Pilgrim’s private terror counts as a whisper.

4.  The Pilgrim’s Part  
You, sole surface-bred witness, camp at the mouth of a vertical gulf, rigging rope for tomorrow’s descent into an echo no one has charted.  When you close your lamp and lean head to basalt you hear your blood louder than the world—until another rhythm slips beside it: the faint thrum of something thinking near your heartbeat’s frequency.

A dream follows, simple and sufficient: you stand on the Reach’s raw obsidian shelf, hands cupped.  Inside them glows a moth-sized spark.  You are told—not in language, but by the emotional certainty rain grants to desert—that you may spend this spark once, only once, and it will accomplish one of three things:

   1) Quench itself and heal one grievous wound, anywhere, of anyone you choose.  
   2) Ignite, becoming an everlasting pilot-flame of water vapor, seeding clouds that will in centuries sand the Reach’s knife-edges into kinder shapes.  
   3) Divide into countless embers, each finding a native mind and giving it one clear dream of a possible future beyond the cavern wall.

You wake with a cool bead of light balanced on your palm, weightless as promise.  No instructions for when to choose.

5.  What the Wise Power Does for the Denizens (Subtle Versions)  

   • To the Gradient-Films  
     Every 37th electron now drifts half an angstrom farther before recombining, releasing a whisper more energy—just enough to buffer against the rare but ruinous vent-quakes.  No film notices, yet survival odds climb three percent over geologic time.

   • To the Skulkers  
     One brood receives a spontaneous crystal misfold that broadens their footfall scale: their walking harmony now spans thirty notes instead of twelve.  Cultural complexity skyrockets; a millennium hence, they will compose the first history told entirely in percussion.

   • To the Lamiae  
     The infrasonic registry gains a new overtone, inaudible to prey but exquisite to Lamiae ears.  Predatory songs turn marginally less lethal, marginally more intricate—beauty competing with hunger and sometimes winning.

   • To the Slow Sovereigns  
     Nothing added, nothing removed.  The Attention merely keeps them on record, the way archivists keep seed vaults.  Memory is the gentlest preservation.

   • To the Cenobite Artisans  
     An idea falls among their mosaics: negative space as narrative.  They begin to weave gaps into their triboluminescent tapestries, encoding questions rather than answers.  Twenty generations later they will invent a philosophy of doubt strong enough to curb heresy-killings.

6.  Non-Interventions (Wisdom Is Also Refusal)  
The wise power does NOT:  
   – Flood the Reach with oxygen.  (Would erase every lifeform the way daylight erases dew.)  
   – Straighten the gravity flips.  (Pendulumites rely on those for reproduction.)  
   – Evict the Chronovores.  (Entropy eaters are part of the covenant.)  
   – Teach fire.  (Combustion is an outside story; the Reach deserves its own literature.)

7.  Epilogue Choices—A Branching Possibility Tree Rooted in Your Spark  

A. If you expend the spark to heal:  
   Somewhere above, a child you will never meet survives a surface infection.  In the Reach, nothing changes but the realization that mercy can arc from darkness to daylight.  You climb back unchanged, except that your heartbeat forever carries the rhythm of that earlier, vaster thought.

B. If you choose the slow rain:  
   Over 400,000 years, mist condenses, flows, and finally sculpts deltas of pale clay.  Skulkers learn boatcraft; Lamiae become storytellers of riverbend echoes.  The Reach evolves from mineral symphony to liquid fugue.  You will not live to see it, but fossils of your own boots form the prologue of their maritime myths.

C. If you scatter dreams:  
   Next quake-season, countless Reach denizens experience identical visions of an exit tunnel arcing to starlight.  Some laugh, some pilgrimage; a few succeed.  Surface legends a few millennia hence speak of glass-eyed strangers arriving on moonless nights, trading silence pockets for iron meteorites.  Culture hybridizes; the universe grows stranger and kinder.

You lift the spark, consider, decide.  Whichever path blooms, the benevolent power recedes to its calm horizon, content.  It will check back in an eon or two, the way gardeners check soil—lightly, knees unpressed, so sprouts may keep their own wild postures.

8.  After-Ripples, Observable by Any Future Pilgrim  
   • The silence pockets are a shade warmer.  
   • Triboluminescent flashes sometimes resolve into glyphs that mean “Still Listening.”  
   • In certain gravity flips, dust arranges into momentary letters of no alphabet, spelling nothing, promising everything.

Thus ends the record of a kindness too vast to leave fingerprints, and thus the Middlenight Reach continues: stone, spark, pause—and now, possibility.
