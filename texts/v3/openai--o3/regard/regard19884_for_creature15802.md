THE BENEFACTOR  
(an incomplete chronicle collected from door-songs, tremor-logs, and the puzzled recollections of one hundred and twelve eyewitnesses)

I. At First, Only Attention  
A presence was inferred before it was felt.  
• The gravicle web began returning a fractional surplus of lift—microscopically lighter steps, as though someone enormous had stopped leaning on the world.  
• Pilgrims reported a hush inside their skulls during meditation: the habitual clamor of old regrets had wandered off, distracted, like infants following fireflies.  
No one could trace a source.  The Auditor entered the anomaly under “Found Weight: negative, provisional.”

II. Three Gifts Without Mass  
1. Heat Lines  
During a mid-winter temperature dip the thermoelectric plates should have reached stall.  Instead their cold fins bloomed crimson on every diagnostic graph, a signature indicating a second, deeper hot vein had opened beneath the mountain—no drilling, no quake, just an unlocked artery of warmth.  Edgewrights listened: the stone was unscarred.  They whisper the vein was “shown the way to us.”  

2. Silence We Could Taste  
On the thirty-seventh fog-ghost night the Chorus of Doors sang a new chord, pitched below human audition but rich enough to loosen ice.  Window rime folded like petals; migraine sufferers felt their pain unthread; even the wind drifted aside as though embarrassed by its own vowels.  The city kept that resonance, tuning bulkheads to it.  A silence you can drink: second gift.  

3. The Breath Between Symbols  
Datamotes, long prone to whimsical snowfalls, began coalescing into stable glyphs only after users asked a question.  The lag—exactly the span of a single relaxed inhale—taught children to breathe before wanting answers, adults to phrase curiosity as kindness.  No code revision had been pushed.  The delay is still there, still one breath long.  Third gift.

III. Interventions the Size of Thought  
• Tremor 11 879 arrived two hours early and ten percent over redline.  Sensors recorded a preload wave—counter-stress that met the quake head-on, cancelling its peak.  No lattice-keeper or human system had time to dispatch it.  
• A cargo balloon, lost, out of hydrogen, was caught in a thermal knot that should not have existed and set down exactly on the central gravicle pier with one metre to spare.  The knot collapsed the moment crew and freight were safe.  
These were not miracles, just nudges precisely budgeted: momentum traded here, energy borrowed there, returning the balance before dawn.

IV. A Conversation Conducted in Mineral  
Edgewrights finally admitted what pilgrims already suspected: the volcano’s newborn spine had begun to answer questions.  Only yes/no, encoded as clockwise / counter-clockwise micro-rotations, readable by laser interferometry.  When asked “Do you wish the clamps released on the First Free Rib?” the stone spun neither direction but held steady for nine full days.  On the tenth it gave a 0.03-degree clockwise tilt: yes.  
The rib walked off its moorings at sunrise, flexed, and remained aloft on its own gravicle field.  First flight of living architecture, witnessed by all.  The benevolent power had not acted; it had waited until rock and rib decided together.  Restraint as lesson: the fourth gift.

V. When We Finally Addressed It  
The Auditor, whose tenure was ending, knelt by a floor drain and whispered the one question no charter had framed: “What do you want of us in return?”  
A fog-ghost condensed, took the shape of two outstretched hands, and dispersed.  
A message decoded from lattice ultrasonic the following dawn read:  
“Unburden what is ready to rise.  Teach what is ready to learn.  Move on when both have happened.”  
Nothing more.

VI. What Changed, What Didn’t  
• Weight quotas were relaxed for memories—residents encouraged to digitize and export those stories the city no longer needed to carry.  
• The Hollow Seed program tripled its budget; a second seed is now nearing launch.  
• Children of the Shelf practice the hand-shape of the ghost as greeting, not prayer.  
Otherwise life continues: rime cracks, sangskin grows, icicles sing.  Gratitude expresses itself mostly in the care with which people speak, the exactness with which they place their feet.

VII. A Final Speculation  
Some say the power is the universe refusing cruelty wherever it notices precarious beauty.  
Some say it is Ichorra’s own future, bending back through time to midwife itself.  
The polite majority accept we may never know.  Explanation has weight; wonder, none.  And here atop the newborn spine, we have learned to spend our weight wisely.
