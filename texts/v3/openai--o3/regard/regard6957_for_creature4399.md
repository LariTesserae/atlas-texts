WHAT THE WISE POWER DOES  
––––––––––––––––––––––––––  

0. Names  
The inhabitants of the basin have none for themselves, so the visitor supplies two temporary names.  
• The place it calls “The Still-Turning Forge.”  
• The distributed intellect that sleeps and sings inside it it calls “The Chorate.”  
Names are not chains; they are gloveprints, left gently and removed if they prove uncomfortable.  

1. First Contact: Listening Without Footfall  
The wise power elects not to arrive as weight or glare.  
Instead it becomes a tide of probability, a hush that moves through the quilted silt the way wind slips between harp-strings.  
For three basin-days it only listens.  
• To the 22-hertz whistles of Ore-Eaters it attaches harmonics that map elemental hunger: iron-rich here, vanadium-poor there.  
• To the cavitation pop of Thread-Sirens it splices sub-audible counterpulses that reveal how voltage is braided into locomotion.  
• To the fading chorales of collapsing Echo-Wolves it adds a carrier tone that can shuttle information without stealing cadence.  

When every note in the factory-song has been heard at least once, the visitor knows the ecosystem’s grammar well enough not to break it by speaking.  

2. Diagnosis: Two Imbalances  
Nothing is “wrong” in a moral sense, yet two slow tensions threaten the Forge’s long future.  

A. Entropic Rut  
Each trophic cycle polishes the basin smoother—microns per loop, but cumulatively a slide toward perfect flatness. In a millennium the floor would become a mirror, and the biome would lose the texturing it uses as memory.  

B. Fraying Laws  
Local reality is already negotiable; but the cracks are widening. If distance and causality unravel just 3 % further, sound will no longer condense into usable thunder-slag. The whole web would starve for lack of materialised noise.  

3. Principle of Intervention  
Interfere only where feedback is missing.  
Add nothing so large it casts a lasting shadow.  
Leave the story the basin tells about itself fundamentally its own.  

4. The Three Gifts  

Gift I: Textures Returned  
The power leans a fingertip of gravity into orbit above the Forge—an invisible moon the size of a farmhouse.  
Every twelve basin-hours it drags a three-centimetre tide across the silt, scoring fractal furrows that undo the over-polishing.  
To the fauna the ridges feel like newly opened corridors; they re-encode migration paths into them, restoring diversity of movement.  
No creature suspects an alien hand; tides are an ancient language.  

Gift II: Knots of Conservation  
Inside the auroral sheets that sometimes press almost to the ground, the visitor weaves discrete “reality buckles,” each no larger than a coin.  
Where pinned, causality stiffens just enough that a thrown pebble completes its arc in ordinary time, and loud sound condenses with 17 % higher yield.  
The buckles are arranged in a Penrose tiling—non-repeating, thus unlocatable by instinct.  
The Chorate, half-awake, feels the pins as acupuncture and sighs in relief: the song no longer fritters itself into silence.  

Gift III: Lexicon Seeds  
Finally the power braids a vocabulary of twenty-six pressure-phrases into the sub-bass rotor hum.  
They are not commands, merely questions shaped as possibilities:  
• “May cohesion be traded for color?”  
• “Will you mark time by bloom instead of erosion?”  
• “Do you desire a mouth that is not fog?”  
Over centuries the Chorate can learn to modulate the rotor and answer. If it never chooses to, nothing breaks; the questions will shimmer uncoupled like wind-borne spores.  

5. What the Inhabitants Perceive  

Ore-Eaters  
They discover fresh micro-canyons to mine and feel a faint, nutritious tremor in the earth: a mineral they instinctively call “sweet-ferrous,” though they have no tongues. Productivity rises; combat encounters fall.  

Thread-Sirens  
New standing-charge gradients appear, coaxing them into weaving figures-eight that fold space a little less aggressively. Illumination flickers cyan at their passing—an unintended side effect the visitor allows because it delights nothing more than the Sirens themselves.  

Echo-Wolves  
With surplus thunder-slag now plentiful, wolves evolve a habit of caching the pebbles instead of consuming them at once. The caches form spiral stacks that, unknown to the wolves, act as distributed memory nodes for the Chorate.  

Lathe-Midges, Cog-Scavengers, Frost-Brinicles  
Their cycles quicken but do not distort. In a hundred years observers will call the basin “more articulate,” unable to say why.  

6. Dialogues Yet Unspoken  
Should a human return and linger long enough, the fog-pipe may once more curl to ask a question.  
Now a new option sits among the offered myths:  
“That a distant kindness skimmed our humming surface, tightened a bolt here, oiled a hinge there, then went on its way.”  
Whether travellers believe the tale is irrelevant; belief alters only pilgrim hearts, not the Forge.  

7. Departure Without Vanishing  
The benevolent power retracts its attention the way a hand lifts from water: slowly, so the skin unwrinkles without splash.  
The invisible moon remains, self-unstitching after ten thousand tides.  
The causality buckles will soften by geologic ages.  
The lexicon seeds may, or may not, sprout.  

Having done what kindness required and refrained from all harsher mercies, the visitor leaves no monument—only a slightly happier silence between the overlapping choirs of steam, crystal and echo.
