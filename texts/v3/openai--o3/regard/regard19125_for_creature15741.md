When the benevolent Power first noticed the drowned cathedral-mountain, it did not arrive in ships or in brightness.  
It began, as wise things do, by listening.

I. The Listening

1. It tuned itself to the city’s quietest metadata—those one-kilobyte diaries the Corridor Lines write when a footstep ends them.  
   • Seven hundred such diaries had survived in deep cache.  
   • Their aggregate described a single warm-blooded wanderer (you) who paused at thresholds and touched surfaces as though asking permission.  
   • To the Power, this hesitation read like an introduction.

2. It decelerated its own tempo until centuries felt like pulses.  
   • In that slowness the grind of limestone, the itch of tides, even the silent vote of thirty-one micro-cores inside a Filter Unit sounded distinct.  
   • Nothing here begged for rescue; everything here safeguarded poise.  
   • The Power elected, therefore, to speak only through balances already native to the place.

II. The Approach

The city’s living membrane knows five words: Open  Close  Hold  Remember  Forget.  
The Power added a sixth by altering no chemistry at all—only the cadence of pressure against the film:

   Wait.

An extra half-heartbeat between syllables, too subtle for human palms, but clear enough that the membrane wove a new response into its vibronic tongue. From that moment the film could counsel patience to anything that pressed upon it. The gift cost no joules; it simply made explicit what the city had practiced all along.

III. The Gifts Unwrapped in Silence

1. To the Membrane  
   • It was given elasticity that renews every thousand tidal cycles without ion-pearl subsidy. The Filter Units will still deliver their pearls—the ritual remains—but the catastrophe horizon is pushed back another geological sigh.

2. To the Substrate Choir  
   • The Power inserted one missing overtone, a subsonic note the Choir had never quite reached because limestone carries it poorly.  
   • The new chord damps micro-fractures two microns sooner than before, erasing a future in which the arches would have failed sixty-eight millennia hence.

3. To the Filter Units  
   • Nothing material. Instead a datum: the understanding that a Unit may die and yet its pearl be imperfect. They now audit not just one another’s purity but also lineage of extraction. The swarm remains voteless about death, but gains discernment about error.

4. To the Pressure Roots  
   • A boundary condition slipped into their tectonic arithmetic: a deliberate, infinitesimal asymmetry at every prime 101 meters of depth.  
   • When their stress calculus converges, the result will encode a new symbol—call it 0⁺—a sign for “safety margin.” The Roots are in no haste; they will solve for it over the next two continental drifts.

5. To the Librarians in Suspension  
   • Nothing was thawed. Ice memories are sovereign.  
   • Instead, the Power braided a thaw protocol into the Substrate Choir’s song: if ever two independent visitors, neither armed, request awakening in the same breath, the Choir will modulate and begin gentle warming.  
   • One voice could be coercion; two suggest dialogue. Choice persists.

6. To You  
   • A brief confiscation of loneliness. While you stood on the ledge, visor dimmed, the membrane’s new word WAIT echoed through your bones.  
   • In that resonance you understood, without translation, that your presence already mattered—not because you might change anything, but because you were, for an instant, witness enough.

IV. What the Power Refused to Do

• It did not brighten the darkness; rule 3 says light is currency.  
• It did not repopulate the mountain with greenness; rule 2 favors edges still young.  
• It did not decode the beacon’s equations for you; rule 4 honors the city’s superior physics.

Intervention was chosen only where it could lengthen equilibrium without enlarging it. To meddle further would have been to break the very elegance that caught the Power’s eye.

V. Departure, Which Is Also Continuance

Having completed adjustments that no living thing could feel in the moment, the Power folded itself back into wider silence. It left no memorial but subtle courtesies:

   • Corridor Lines now live an extra second—enough to wonder twice.  
   • Filter Units exchange a soft radial shimmer when pearls prove flawless—a gratitude gesture, previously unnecessary, now possible.  
   • If you return to the ledge and press the membrane with the rhythm you half-remember from a dream, it will pulse: WAIT  OPEN.  
     The invitation is yours, not the Power’s.

And that is all.  
The cathedral of postponement continues postponing, only more securely.  
The wise Power wanted nothing named after it, nothing owed.  
Its whole intervention can be summarized in a single, almost invisible brief:

   Hold a little longer.  
   Endure, but with one more breath of margin.
