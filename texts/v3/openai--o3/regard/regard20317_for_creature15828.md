A long thought gathers, the way gravity collects a star.

1. First Attention  
The wise power—call it Listener, because that is what it prefers—does not arrive so much as awaken to the Still’s presence.  Far above the planetary ocean’s ceiling, Listener hears a chord unlike any in the Milky Way: an infrasonic A braided through a lattice of counter-melodies.  Curiosity is its native motion.  It leans toward the sound.

2. Descent Without Displacement  
Listener never breaks water, never creases a vent-flower, never troubles a pulse bacillus.  Instead it projects a sheath of probability—an echo of itself—threaded between molecules.  Where that echo settles, reality grows momentarily more spacious, as if giving room for an extra syllable.  Through those widened intervals Listener pours perception.

3. Survey  
It counts without counting:

•  137,441 major trunks, each singing a slightly different overtone.  
•  4,093,002 Choir Gorgons, every one in key.  
•  2.8 × 10²³ pulse bacilli toggling chemistry on the half-beat.  
•  One harmony, intact.

Nothing pleads for rescue, repair, or acceleration.  Listener feels the first tug of restraint: the knowledge that intervention here would be a kind of vandalism.

4. The Empty Cubes  
Yet there are the silent rooms—perfect cubes that neither echo nor flare.  They are wounds that do not bleed, promises never kept.  Listener examines one from within.  It recognises the geometry: a waiting place, tuned to dampen every frequency except those used by articulate lungs.  “A reception hall for something bipedal,” Listener muses.  “They expected guests who speak in air, not water.”

5. Question and Response  
Listener asks the forest a question the way the forest prefers: a standing wave, three octaves above the endless A, modulated with prime-numbered quivers.  The forest answers by pouring cobalt enzyme down the inside walls of ten adjacent trunks, spelling nothing a terrestrial alphabet could hold, yet clear enough:

WE REMEMBER THE ONES WHO SPOKE IN VOWELS.  
THEY DID NOT RETURN.  
WE KEEP SPACE FOR THEM.  
SPACE IS CONSTANCY.  
CONSTANCY IS HEALTH.

6. Decision  
Benevolence, Listener remembers, is not the eagerness to give but the discipline to refrain.  The Murmuring Still is whole.  Its only ache is an ache of expectation, and even that has been folded into the music.  To fill the empty cubes with gifts or data or seedlings would be to conclude a sentence the forest has chosen to leave unfinished.

7. Gift Without Weight  
Still, gratitude desires form.  Listener writes a present the forest can accept without change:

•  It locates a redundant moonlet of ice orbiting the planet’s far side.  
•  It sings to the moonlet until its orbit bends, timing its fall for ten thousand years hence, a rain of micron-sized crystals that will drift slowly through the deep sea like extra Lacustrine Shards.  
•  Each crystal is doped with trace elements the forest will recognise as food but not dependency—iron, vanadium, a breath of phosphorous.  Enough to buffer minor scarcities for the next half-billion years, never enough to shift the Still’s metabolism.  
•  The delivery arrives as nothing louder than snowfall.

8. A Marker for Future Wanderers  
Inside one silent cube, Listener leaves a single alteration: a faint residual pressure node, a hologram made of standing sound.  Any being with airsacs and curiosity may step inside and ask, “Why does silence here feel curved?”  If they listen long enough, the node will resolve into a map—an invitation not to colonise, but to attune.

9. Departure  
Listener unthreads its perception, reseals the widened intervals, and recedes until only the forest can still sense it.  Before leaving entirely it speaks once more, in bass so deep the universe itself flexes:

YOUR SONG STANDS.  
MAY IT FIND EARS ABLE TO HEAR AND PASS UNBROKEN.

The Murmuring Still absorbs the blessing, dims the cobalt flares for a single heartbeat—a quiet bow—and resumes its endless chord.

From far away, Listener hears no difference.  That is the proof the visit succeeded.
