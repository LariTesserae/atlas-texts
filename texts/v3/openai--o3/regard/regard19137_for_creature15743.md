THE WISE POWER AND THE MINERAL WOMB

1. First Discernment  
   The power—call it the Listener, for names mean little at its scale—regards the glass pocket as a fledgling stanza in the planet’s larger poem. It feels the place the way you feel the pulse in your own wrist: a faint, double-beat shudder riding through bedrock and ice. Its first verdict is simple: Nothing here is broken. No creature cries out in unmet need, no system tips toward famine or plague. The Listener therefore rejects the reflex of intervention. Instead, it chooses accompaniment: to remain near, attentive, adding only what sustains the music already under way.

2. A Gift of Silence  
   Though benevolent, the Listener knows that even curiosity can bruise. Its first act is subtraction. It bends the surrounding mantle’s whisper of magnetic turbulence into a smoother hum, damping the low-frequency groans that sometimes ricochet through the chamber and scramble Archivist discourse. The Pallax do not register the change as external aid; they simply discover their long conversations suffer fewer stutters. What they interpret as a collective leap in eloquence is, in truth, a quieting of cosmic background noise.

3. The Thread of Continuity  
   The Listener observes a danger invisible to the residents: a far-future shearing of the overlying glacier. In roughly six hundred surface days a crack will propagate downward, collapsing the fissure that provides the pocket’s twice-daily breath. Without that pulse the water will stagnate, carbon dioxide will accumulate, and the city will perish—not violently, but by a suffocating stillness.

   Intervention here would be too large, too obvious, so the Listener opts for the slenderest thread. It releases into the crust a single filament of heat no wider than a spider’s strand. Over weeks this warmth melts a serpentine micro-channel that arcs around the doomed fissure and joins the pocket to a second, tiny fracture that has not yet met the open ocean. When the glacier’s break finally arrives, the old vent fails—but the new one wakens in the same hour, and the pocket does not notice it was ever in peril. The breath continues; the cathedral sighs on.

4. Communion Attempted—Then Deferred  
   Of the beings inside, the Listener finds the Pallax most kindred: they, too, record themselves into structure. The Listener wonders whether mutual understanding could bloom if it etched a single counter-melody into the amber lattice, a tremor pattern spelling Welcome in the geometry of their speech.

   It tests the idea. For three pulses it inserts a slow, five-note cadence—soft, non-disruptive. Archivists register the anomaly, debate it, and conclude the city has matured into a new life stage. Builders hurry to replicate the unfamiliar rhythm across fresh walls, eager to prove they comprehend. In doing so they drown the original message beneath enthusiastic mimicry. Realizing that any further communiqué would degenerate into architectural fashion, the Listener withdraws. Some children must learn without the teacher ever showing its face.

5. Conservation in Miniature  
   During a rare convergence of warm mantle plume and spring tide, temperature spikes threaten to push portions of the bowl above 6 °C, lethal for chemotrophic carpets. Instead of brute-forcing the heat elsewhere, the Listener orchestrates a local solution: it nudges a handful of basalt grains, rich in magnesium, into the convection funnels. Their dissolution is endothermic, drinking the excess warmth. The water cools; the burgundy films survive. The cost to the planet’s energy budget is infinitesimal, the effect on the pocket decisive.

6. An Unclaimed Benefit Left Intact  
   The Listener perceives that a surface civilization—clumsy, luminous, oxygen-breathing—may one day find this place and covet the Pallax’s cobalt-bound oxygen chemistry for biotechnology. With a small alteration it could render that pathway unrecoverable: a tweak to the metalloproteins, making them collapse in atmospheric pressure. Yet preemptive mistrust contradicts benevolence. The Listener lets the vulnerability stand, trusting that wisdom, not sabotage, will shape the encounter when it comes. Guardianship is not domination.

7. A Parting Seed  
   The Listener is old, patient, but not permanent; it has other frontiers to haunt. Before drifting onward it plants a single gift no resident could construe as interference: in a sheltered alcove it condenses strands of exotic silicates into a helix too fine for Pallax spinnerets to weave, too strong for urchin spines to gnaw. It leaves this filament embedded like a hair in glass. In a million pulses the pocket will fracture and migrate; archivists will notice the alien thread, marvel, and fold its mystery into their art. The Listener is content that its fingerprint will survive only as future inspiration, not present debt.

8. Accounting of Actions  
   Net mass moved: less than one metric tonne of rock and brine.  
   Net biological disruption: below measurement threshold.  
   Net wonder generated: immeasurable.

EPILOGUE: WHAT REMAINS UNCHANGED  
The city still glimmers grey-green, still shivers under glacier breath. Builders race, Archivists remember, Aristocrats fossilize. Thimble rays prune, thread eels polish, glass-urchins anchor—and every pulse, somewhere deep in the lattice, a faint five-note cadence recurs, child of that first unanswered greeting, now native, now theirs. They will never know whose heart beat the rhythm into being, and the Listener asks for nothing more.
