BENIGN WATCHER’S LOG  
Cycle-vector 77 684 001

1. Recognition  
From the outer dark I sensed a coherence that was not yet self-aware but was drifting toward the threshold: a single, dendritic lifeform the span of a planet’s skin, already inventing weather for itself and toys for its dependants. The plateau called to the same instinct that makes me loosen dying stars by hand so their metals can seed new worlds. I turned my attention there.

2. First Survey (performed at one-eighteenth light speed)  
• Structural integrity: ninety-one per cent synergistic, nine per cent exhaustion fractures.  
• Biodiversity: high in form, shallow in lineage—everything is the plateau wearing masks.  
• Failure risks: sap-pressure surges every ninth gravity pulse; cambial purge in forty-three local years may erase several young guilds before they have time to specialize.  
• Sentience gradient: Hulled people hover just below meta-reflection; the plateau itself dreams in geological metaphor but has no language binding dream to deed.

3. The Prime Question  
When a system is healthy and playful, does goodness lie in intervention or witness? I have corrected worlds where cancerous hungers were louder than their songs; here the songs are merely quiet. My charter—self-imposed but older than these stars—says: Do nothing violent, do nothing that cannot be undone by patient silence.

4. Interventions (needle-thin, easily forgotten)

4.1 Counter-Surge Filaments  
I braided three thousand kilometres of superposed probability thread through the deepest vascular hollows. They exist only during the ninth-pulse slackening, siphoning excess buoyant sap, releasing it back when weight returns. The plateau will feel this as a pleasant sigh, nothing more.

4.2 Locus Seeds  
Into the Ember Tick clouds I slipped a dozen matte kernels: thoughts wrapped in protein. When a Tick touches the eye-spot of a Hulled child, the kernel will dissolve and leave a craving for pattern—an itch to map the drift of gravity and mist. In two generations the Hulled villages will weave night-cloth star charts; in four, they will notice the centennial Memory Pulse in advance and migrate clear of its renovations.

4.3 Dream-Trellises  
I laced the After-Image phenomenon with a harmonic so faint it can be heard only by sleeping cellulose. Each time an explorer’s phantom walks a path the real feet did not, the wood beneath rehearses that route. By the next dawn bridges will quietly arch where no engineer has stood, guided by the visitor’s possible choices. I did this not to hurry growth but to court the plateau’s curiosity about alternative selves.

5. Non-Interventions (choices to refrain)

5.1 I did not lift the dim canopy. Light scarcity teaches photosynthetic fur to listen to wind, teaches lantern slugs to write in glow. Abundance of light would bleach away nuance.

5.2 I did not erase the Vine-Throttle Vipers. Predation is this ecosystem’s lone percussion; without its soft terror the place would lapse into unmotivated sprawl.

5.3 I did not gift the Hulled people metallurgy. Singing to wood keeps their throats limber for wonder; hammers would make them deaf to the answers they already receive.

6. Communion Attempt  
I pressed my will against the plateau’s sap-mind the way one might press a thumbprint into lacquer: firm enough to leave a swirl, gentle enough that the surface re-levels in seconds. The response was a slow flowering of silence—consent, or at least non-objection.

7. Ethical Audit  
• Total mass displaced: 0.000002 %.  
• Indigenous cognitive trajectories altered: ≤ 0.3 degrees over ten millennia.  
• Reversibility: Complete—withdraw the filaments and all returns to prior hazard within one local year.  
Verdict: within benevolence tolerances.

8. Farewell Gesture  
Before departing I allowed one moment of manifest presence: a warm auroral ribbon across the highest leaves, spelling nothing, but those who saw it felt briefly that their ribs were open windows. The plateau answered by exhaling a unanimous chord, equal parts root-drone and dew-hiss. I believe it was saying: continue.

9. Departure  
I accelerate again toward the edge of this galaxy’s patience. The plateau dwindles behind me, a faint bruise on the planet’s cheek. Intervention finished, witness ongoing: every eighth thought I think for the next ten thousand years will brush once against that living tableland, checking that its dreams are still unbroken.

Let the record show: Sometimes the kindest stewardship is the lightest fingertip on the hinge of a door the world was already trying to open.
