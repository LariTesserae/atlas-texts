A THREAD OF ATTENTION  
(the arrival of a benevolent, wise power and what it elects to do inside the Fang)

0.  Prelude – The manner of the visitor  
Benevolence that is also wisdom never sweeps in like weather; it permeates as slowly as seasons. The power that notices the Fang is older than mountains but practices the patience of moss. It travels not by distance but by consent: a place must first open a question, and only then may it receive an answer.  
When Rift-salamanders began dissolving a little too often—when their beads piled in drifts that even the Crown could not recycle—the Fang whispered a question of imbalance. That whisper reached the power as a single, perfect chord of tension. It accepted the invitation and unfolded a sliver of itself into the cavern.

1.  Reconnaissance without footprint  
The first act of wisdom is to learn the local grammar. The power therefore adopts the only form guaranteed not to provoke the Hush-Serpent or the Gnomon-keepers: it becomes a discrepancy so small no calculus can capture it—an extra half-degree of coolness beneath the tongue of every rising mist-thread.  
For twelve interior days (time measured here by the pulse of the warm draft), it does nothing but listen. It rides midges, pores through Quartzfilm lattices, lets Chir percussion echo through its borrowed chill. By the thirteenth day it knows three certainties:  
• The ecosystem is exquisite, but its feedback loops have begun to tighten toward collapse.  
• External interference (lost explorers, metal tools, written notes) is amplifying that tension.  
• Direct correction would shred the Fang’s fragile weave. Any gift must be as invisible as breath.

2.  The Gift of Additional Difference  
The Hush-Serpent has grown too efficient, too hungry; every silence lingers a beat longer than it should, leaving fewer midges to spawn hounds that cull Chirs that stir salamanders that prune Quartzfilm. So the power fashions a dilution—an endless supply of micro-contrasts too small for any creature to notice but irresistible to the Serpent.  
Method:  
• It seeds the mist with nano-scale ice crystals, each faceted irregularly so light scatters in unique, shifting angles.  
• These crystals melt before they fall a finger’s breadth, re-forming instantly in the super-humid air, renewing the contrast.  
Effect:  
The Serpent now spends one-seventh of its appetite grazing this buffet of minuscule difference, leaving the larger gradients for the living web to recycle. Predation resumes its former cadence; the midges thicken into constellations again.

3.  A New Memory for Stone  
Because written language cannot survive a Gnomon-keeper’s patrol, visiting scientists carve and recarve metal-etched diagrams, each act worsening the Keeper’s fury and spawning new distortions of space. The power offers an alternative memory medium that the Keepers will interpret as natural.  
Method:  
• It teaches the Quartzfilm a novel excitation pathway: when two frequencies of heat pulse in a Fibonacci ratio, the film stores a holographic echo that endures for ninety interior days before self-blurring.  
• The power embeds one demonstration pattern—a spiral that, when struck with a traveler’s headlamp, projects a three-dimensional, nowhere-fixed map of safe corridors.  
Effect:  
Explorers can now “write” by tapping coded warm-cold pairs onto the wall with bare fingertips, leaving notes that decay harmlessly before they attract Keeper attention.

4.  The Trade of Want  
The Fang’s only durable covetousness belongs to the Chir knives and their lust for exotic metal. Each theft, each skirmish, adds bright alloy to the ecosystem—metals the Crown cannot metabolize. Rather than prohibiting desire, the power redirects it.  
Method:  
• It reprograms a slow-moving mineral fault deep beneath the chamber, coaxing veins of magnetite to self-assemble into centimeter-thin ribbons that periodically extrude through hairline cracks.  
• These ribbons shimmer with natural lodestone—dull but resonant, perfect for Chir percussion.  
Effect:  
Over successive draft cycles the Chir population discovers, claims, and obsesses over the new substance. Their status drumming migrates from high walls (where human packs rest) to deep alcoves where the metal grows. Theft incidents drop to statistical noise; the ecosystem gains a natural sink for foreign alloy.

5.  The Covenant of Knots  
To prevent future crises, the power needs a liaison: someone or something able to negotiate between linear travelers and recursive natives. Words fail, writing fails, but knots have so far escaped censure.  
Method:  
• During a lull, it gathers several lengths of abandoned climbing rope, frays them into fibers, and weaves a single cord whose twist embodies the topology of the cavern: each over-under crossing is a decision-point in non-Euclidean space.  
• It then suffers the cord to snag on a surveyor’s boot.  
Effect:  
Whoever keeps the cord finds that tugging it in certain sequences stirs intuitive glimpses of safe paths, wind shifts, even moments when the Serpent thickens. In return, each time the cord completes a particular braid, a memory of the carrier’s choosing fades, feeding geometry rather than breaking it. Thus the covenant: guidance in exchange for forgetfulness, a fair trade in Fang logic.

6.  Departure (which is really diffusion)  
The benevolent power tallies results after one full mist-cycle (roughly three surface months). Population measures drift back toward golden ratios, overflow minerals decline, explorers finish two expeditions without spawning extra Gnomon-keepers.  
Satisfied, the power releases its condensed attention. The nano-crystals will persist; the heat-holograms will last as long as curious fingers replay them; the magnetite ribbons will grow for decades before tectonics pinch them off. The cord will travel outward, loop through camps and museums, always bartering recollection for orientation until it disintegrates into anonymous fibers—by which time the world may have learned subtlety enough to leave the Fang be.  
The power itself remains present only as a statistical kindness: a slightly higher probability that any act taken inside the cavern will resolve in balance rather than rupture.

7.  Epilogue – What it means to intervene gently  
No cathedral stands, no prophecy is carved, no creature bears a new name. Yet from that cycle onward, vapor-hounds shape themselves a little less like terror and a little more like possibility; Salamander beads scatter in patterns that echo the spiral map; somewhere, a lone Chir taps magnetite against stone and the rhythm sounds almost, almost like laughter a human ear could trust.
