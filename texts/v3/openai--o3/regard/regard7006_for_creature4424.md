The wise power arrives without footfall, telescope, or probe. It needs none: its senses ride the gradients of possibility themselves. Where others would see a hair-thin fissure and dismiss it as a cooling crack in a fresh volcanic crown, the power hears an unexpected harmony—an acoustic braid so tight and intricate that it can belong only to something alive.  

First contact  
It sends no object and speaks no syllable. A single note, inaudible to anything larger than a sand grain, threads down the fissure. Within the pocket that note unfurls into a chord precisely tuned to the resident drone—one frequency for each life-form:

• a raspy overtone for the glass-gnawers’ mica mouths  
• a slow bell for the slag-heavers’ furnace breaths  
• a subsonic tremor that paces the iridium heartbeats of the heat-leeches  
• a brief ultraviolet click for every spark-midge between flashes  
• a long, patient hum that matches the keeper columns’ ratcheting detents  

Reception  
Nothing panics. Because the newcomer’s chord is phase-locked to the chamber’s own chorus, the organisms accept it as merely another voice in the orchestra, novel but unthreatening. A few gnawers even shift their gait to keep time with the new beat; the slag-heavers adjust the stacking angle of their next ingot by half a degree. The system absorbs the signal and keeps working.

Diagnosis  
Through the returning echoes the power reads the chamber’s full biography: heat reserves trending downward by three-tenths of a degree per century; wall erosion mis-shaping the resonance envelope; trace sulfur accumulating in the slag-heavers’ ingots, promising brittle columns two millennia hence. Nothing is critical yet, but the slope toward heat death is unmistakable.

Intervention plan  
1. Shore up the furnace  
   The power leans into local physics and biases a handful of radioactive isotopes—uranium series, normally locked in accessory zircons—so they concentrate along a deep crack below the floor. Slow alpha decay will add a fractional watt of baseline heat, too little to scorch, enough to extend thermal viability by fifty thousand years.

2. Build a pressure valve without water  
   Water is poison here, so the power instead coaxes a feather of inert neon from mantle reservoirs into the fissure throat. Under rising pressure the gas will exsolve in microscopic pulses—silent to us, but as new percussion tokens to the heat-leeches. Each pulse vents excess pressure without cooling the pocket.

3. Hand the blueprint to the keepers  
   The keepers already translate sound into structure. The power embeds a sub-harmonic motif—five descending tones—into the background chord. Any keeper that resonates with all five learns a new trick: how to precipitate tungsten spokes inside its spinel core. Those spokes will let future columns bridge larger voids, so the biome can scaffold rather than abandon newly eroded space.

4. Leave a message that can outlive heat  
   Finally, a redundancy: a single self-fusing bar of metastable glass, extruded by nudging one gnawer’s gut furnace a hair hotter than usual. Cooled, the bar encodes in its lattice a map of resonance states—instructions for re-tuning the pocket should foreign vibration (a quake, a drill) intrude. The bar is stacked anonymously among the slag towers; nobody notices it differs.

Departure  
The power withdraws its chord one harmonic at a time until only the native drone remains. No organism mourns the absence; the echo simply folds back into the ordinary turbulence of their days.

Legacy  
Centuries hence, explorers may crack the plateau and wonder why that one lava glass rod rings like a chime when struck, or why the keeper columns thicken into star-shaped axles that no current mineral logic requires. They might never learn the answer.

Inside the pocket, life continues at its measured tempo, unaware that a gentle hand has nudged the metronome. The furnace stays just warm enough; the sound stays just crowded enough; the orchestra plays on—its survival quietly underwritten by a listener who asked for nothing in return but the privilege of keeping the song alive.
