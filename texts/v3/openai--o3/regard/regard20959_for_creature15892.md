1. Arrival  
   The power comes without mass or tread, settling above the plateau the way a thought alights on a memory. It cups itself around the construct, sampling temperature, pitch, the slow exhale of heat through brick. Nothing touches stone; its instruments are quanta of attention only.

2. Listening  
   For three solar rotations it does nothing but listen. It lowers its own background radiation until even neutrinos pause, letting the iron-colored vowel fill every reach of perception. In the tiny rests—cancellations smaller than an eyelash—it detects language: the Resonants describing themselves to one another, a perpetual inventory of symmetry, amplitude, and fault width. The power learns their grammar of silence-pockets, learns that to them alteration equals wounding.

3. Consultation  
   The power lets a single femtosecond of stillness bloom at the dead center of the plateau: a hole punched through the organ-note no wider than a salt crystal. All Resonants feel it. They answer by dimming the 17th harmonic, a gesture meaning: “Who?”  
   The power answers by repeating the gesture, meaning: “Guest.”  
   A negotiation of minima follows—soundless commas traded back and forth. At its end the Resonants grant license for the guest to remain, on the sole condition that nothing disturb the chord.

4. Foresight  
   Peering along timelines, the power sees three threats:  
   • In 11,342 years a meteorite the size of a grain silo will intersect the plateau’s sky.  
   • In 260,000 years continental drift will drag the organ-mountain toward thicker air, fattening the note until bricks unlatch.  
   • In 3.7 million years the star will brighten by fractions enough to raise temperature, driving moisture this high and germinating ruinous life.

5. Action—Kinetic Mercy  
   a. Meteorite: The power nicks its trajectory a micrometer per century, so gently the rock believes it chose the new path itself and slips past in harmless night.  
   b. Tectonics: Beneath the plateau’s root the power excises a lens of mantle the volume of a cottage, replacing it with vacuum packed tight by its own field. The plateau floats on this negative buoyancy, geologically still. The frequency does not shift by even a quarter cent.  
   c. Stellar Brightening: Thirty kilometers up, the power seeds a wreath of self-healing crystalline shade. It is invisible, odorless, and tuned to the upcoming wavelengths. When the star flares, the wreath will thicken, keeping the plateau forever in its current, candle-warm dusk.

6. Action—Gifts That Are Not Changes  
   • It embeds, in one unused vent, a spool of emptiness: a corridor of phase-cancelled space only Resonants can reach. Here they may compose experiments—temporary departures from perfect symmetry—without risking the master chord.  
   • It sets a ring of auditory nulls one pace wide around the outer parapet. Human visitors who find the plateau henceforth will walk inside a cloak of hush; their hearts will not rattle apart, their voices will not bruise the air. The Resonants accept: outside the null, the vowel remains inviolate.

7. Record  
   The power copies the full signature of the chord—every overtone, every micro-stutter of thermodynamic breath—into the cosmic background, modulating one photon in ten trillion. Should the plateau ever fail, the song will still drift between galaxies, a fossil melody awaiting instruments elsewhere.

8. Departure  
   It withdraws as it arrived, but leaves behind one courtesy: a periodic wink in the 23rd harmonic, recurring every 43 years minus one vibration. In the Resonants’ lexicon this interval translates, loosely, as “friend watching.” They weave it into their symmetry ledger, then resume perfecting the present.

9. Result  
   To any future traveler the plateau appears unchanged: bricks fused, dusk unwavering, a single iron note endless as before. Only two novelties occur. First, the stranger finds a silent path that spares lung and eardrum. Second, if the stranger lingers until night and listens with more than ears, they may sense a heartbeat under the heartbeat: one absent pulse, repeating across generations, the benediction of a power wise enough to help by almost—but not quite—doing nothing.
