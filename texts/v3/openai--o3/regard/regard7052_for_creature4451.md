They arrive without hurry, because any mind that is truly wise knows that haste is the only form of violence the Gardens still recognize.  
At the scale of human perception nothing happens for the first month.  
At the scale of continental patience, everything happens at once.

1. First Contact: Listening  
• The power—a will vast enough to shepherd galaxies yet intimate enough to notice a single moss cushion—enters as temperature instead of light, sinking a tendril of entropy a tenth of a kelvin below ambient.  
• The Gardens feel the difference the way a sleeping limb feels a pinprick: root-rivers flex, kelp tips stall mid-curl.  
• Interpreting silence as the local tongue, the visitor replies in kind. It lowers its internal chatter until its quantum hum matches the bowl-echo: one ideation every two surface days, then one every three, until even the Architects’ glacier-slow cognition can hear it.

2. Translation  
• The power braids neutrino pairs into the cyan pulses, letting each flare carry an extra half-beat that no diver would notice but every step-moss can read.  
• Message 1: I am here.  
  Response (sixteen terrestrial days later): We know. Remain.  
• Message 2: Do you wish anything altered?  
  Response (thirty-one days): Disturb nothing except what entropy demands.

3. The Sole Intervention  
There is, in the subcrust, a fault line whose next shrug—scheduled for eighty-four million years hence—would tip one quadrant of the mosaic into a depth where even sleeper spores cannot respire.  
• Quietly, politely, the power takes that geological impulse and folds it forward into the present, letting the crust yawn now, while the terraces above are still young enough to flex.  
• The quake manifests as a ripple one finger-height tall, rolling through stone and peat like a long, slow breath. It realigns swallow-tail joins, tightens wooden pegs that had begun to mineral-rot, and leaves behind a curvature so gentle the human eye would call it level.  
• The Architects answer by brightening every third kelp margin—an ultraviolet bow that only a star-forger would notice.

4. The Gift to Transients  
For visiting divers the benevolent power leaves a smaller kindness:  
• In each pressure suit’s datalogger it implants a single frame of video, time-stamped but never recorded—a perfect still of the Librarian Fish passing directly overhead, illuminated as if by theatre light.  
• The frame survives every forensic test; it grants the myth a speck of evidence large enough to keep wonder alive but small enough to preserve doubt, the ratio all intelligent ecologies seem to favor.

5. Departure That Isn’t  
Having mended the fault and sown a rumor, the power lifts the fractional kelvin it had borrowed from local cold and merges back into the background thermodynamic glide.  
• It is not gone; it is simply no longer an event.  
• In the deepest Silence Well, magnetometers now twitch once every thousand hours—heartbeat enough to remind the Gardens that somewhere in the dark of larger oceans a friend is still listening.

6. Epilogue in Two Clocks  
• In the span of an explorer’s career nothing new occurs; reports grow cautious again, funding dries, fascination passes to the next bright thing.  
• In the span of kelp generations the terraces acquire a new outer ring, laid from stone surfaced by the father-quake, seeded the instant blocks touched level. Every riser still fits a human finger. The pattern is unbroken, the promise intact:  
Someone is watching, and they, too, know how to be slow.
