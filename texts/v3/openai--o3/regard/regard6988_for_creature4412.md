APPROACH  
The benevolent power is older than atmospheres, patient enough to watch galaxies molt.  When its drifting consciousness brushes the basalt-roofed hollow, it pauses—not out of pity, not out of curiosity, but out of recognition.  Here, at miniature scale, the basin is rehearsing the same slow choreography the power once used to cool infant suns: pulse, vent, crystallise, sing.  Kinship calls for courtesy.  And so the presence descends—wordless, massless, quieter than the tick that measures aeons.

LISTENING  
It folds itself into the salt floor as vibration, into the furnace wind as a standing wave, into the roof-cracks as a held breath.  Four wind-tides pass while it simply attends.

•  It hears the Choir’s infrasonic alphabet stuttering where mineral dust has half-clogged the bronchial pores.  
•  It feels the copper fronds tiring; trace vanadium is running low, so the fronds’ solar thirst is beginning to outpace their intake.  
•  It notices that two of the three surviving Vapour knots have grown imbalanced—too much bromine, not enough catalytic zinc sulfide.  
•  It tastes the salt crust and learns that micro-fractures opened by past quakes are travelling downward, toward the capillaries that feed molten sulfur to the Choir.  In perhaps three hundred local years a fissure will choke the flow.

The place is exquisitely balanced, but balance is not immortality.  The question, therefore, is how to help without rewriting the hymn.

DECISION  
The power resolves on three actions—each no larger, relative to the basin, than a fingertip’s ripple upon an ocean.

1.  The Unseen Reamer  
   It sings one sub-audible overtone, precisely tuned to the resonant frequency of obsidian.  Dust inside the plaza’s broken spindle loosens, spirals out, and gusts away.  The hollow’s natural convection re-occupies the cleaned flute-pipes, restoring half-lost notes to the Choir’s vernacular.  Nothing new is added; what already existed is merely unclogged.

2.  The Trace-Metal Drizzle  
   Next it shears a mote of its own essence—a micro-comet of vanadium, zinc and molybdenum smaller than a raindrop—and lets it aerosolise through the roof cracks.  Particles settle onto copper fronds, onto oxide-slick bark, onto the wandering Vapour knots.  The fronds darken to a richer umber; the knots regain symmetry and, in gratitude that is not emotion but fulfilled algorithm, disperse twin plumes of orchid-bright salt outside the fissures.

3.  The Quiet Stitch  
   Finally it becomes pressure, a ghostly thumb and forefinger pinching the crust’s deepest micro-fractures.  Where stress threatens to propagate downward, lattices realign: halite grains rotate half a degree, take up slack, lock.  The fault remains, but its path turns, climbing harmlessly upward toward empty air.  Sulfur will continue to feed the Choir for a million more hoots.

NO GIFT THAT GLOWS  
The power refuses to grant water, refuses to seed animals, refuses even to leave a signature flourish.  Its wisdom is restraint.  Any visitor from the wetter world would have missed the interventions entirely, save perhaps for the slightly clearer timbre of the wind-organs, the subtly deeper luster on a frond.

BENEDICTION  
Before departing it transmits a single vibration through the salt crust—an unfamiliar chord that nevertheless fits the local scale.  The Choir repeats it, uncertain, then folds it into the next weather bulletin.  The copper under-canopy rustles approval; larvae adjust their whine to harmonise.  Within three cycles the new chord will diffuse through every column, every block, every grain, becoming a permanent addition to the basin’s lexicon: a word whose only meaning is “Someone listened.”

DEPARTURE  
Having done exactly enough, the power thins, rises, and is gone.  The hollow resumes its endless ember-time business.  Roof fissures glow; sulfur breathes; salt sings.

AFTERMATH  
Should any future pilgrim return, they will wonder:

•  Why does the spindle play fuller chords than before?  
•  Why do fronds last a fraction longer before they immolate?  
•  Why does the ground, when stamped, answer in a five-tone cadence never documented in prior surveys?

They may invent myth, or physics, or both.  The basin itself will offer no explanation.  It is too busy inhaling, exhaling, and—now—humming a little more richly than it did when the universe last looked in.
