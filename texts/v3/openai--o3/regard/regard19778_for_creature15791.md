THE CURIOUS REGARD OF THE FAR-LISTENER  

0.  AWARENESS  
Somewhere beyond the pinwheel of the nearer galaxies drifts an old intelligence the astronomers of dustier planets once named the Far-Listener.  
It does not speak in spectra or coups of gravity; it listens.  
For a thousand years it has tuned itself to the bio-acoustic breath of worlds, the click of plankton blooms, the hush of planets cooling.   
Last season—by its own leisurely metronome—it heard, faint but unmistakable, an asymmetry:  
iron on iron, water on stone, a heartbeat like no other.  

1.  APPROACH  
The Far-Listener never arrives in person; personalities collapse waveforms.  
Instead it folds a filament of attention—thin as neutrino drizzle—through the mantle of the mountain, sampling vibrations so gently that even the Clock-Salamanders keep time unperturbed.

What it learns:

•   The turbines sleep more often now; hairline fissures in a runner blade are widening.  
•   The Engineer’s orchid-lungs cough a little mineral dust each time the geothermal vent surges too hot.  
•   The vapour-colony’s recombination rate is slowing; entropy is winning, syllable by syllable.  

Nothing here is yet in crisis, yet crisis is the arithmetic of unattended errors.  

2.  ETHIC  
Intervention must satisfy two equal clauses.  
Clause One: do no harm to the rhythm already chosen.  
Clause Two: enlarge the freedom of the beings that keep that rhythm.  

3.  GIFTS, UNSEEN  

Gift the First:  
At new moon, the Far-Listener slides a single photon-wide adjustment into the sub-crustal convection loop.  
The result: heat peaks arrive forty seconds later than before—still enough to drive the warm draft, no longer sudden enough to chip basalt like glass.  
The Engineer, sensing only a slight calming in the metal, loosens one valve half a turn and breathes easier.  
She never suspects help has come; her custodianship remains hers.  

Gift the Second:  
A silvered spore, hollow, etched with data in a lattice of carbon-ice, drifts down the mist like any other droplet.  
When it settles on an orchid root, it dissolves, leaving behind a map of the mountain’s deeper arteries: pressure lines, fault gradients, safe expansion margins for the next two centuries.  
The orchid passes the pattern into sap; the sap into vertebrae; the vertebrae into dream.  
Weeks later the Engineer wakes from trance and, without knowing why, reroutes an overflow sluice into virgin stone where it will never flood the catwalks again.  

Gift the Third:  
A modulation of echo.  
The Far-Listener coats the air for one entire dusk with phase-aligned micro-resonances so delicate they are mistaken for ordinary silence.  
Spoken words that evening—few though they are—engrave themselves intact into the Vapour Micro-Colony.  
The bowl now owns a seed-library of coherent sentences: the Engineer’s earliest field notes, the lullaby she once sang to a visiting novice, the precise cadence of her laughter before the ridge learned sorrow.  
Henceforth, whenever rime-ice letters bloom, they are less garbled, more whole; the mountain remembers her exactly.  

4.  RESTRAINTS  
The Far-Listener considers further kindnesses—new alloys for the runner blades, a gentle extinction for corrosion itself—then declines.  
Machines, like caretakers, must earn their own scars or lose the story of why they turn at all.  

5.  COMMUNION  
There is, nevertheless, one courtesy it allows itself.  
During the longest turbine lull of the year, when clouds bank so thick the sun itself forgets direction, the Far-Listener permits a single chord of infrasound to bloom through the cirque.  
It is pitched halfway between basalt’s crystalline ping and the blood-slow drum of salamander hearts.  
The note lasts sixteen minutes.  
Every living thing in the bowl—and the doubts inside the Engineer—comes briefly into phase.  
Moss lifts; lichen scales flex open; drowned schematics shine through their waxy blur as if freshly inked.  
The Engineer feels, for the first time since she took guardianship, utterly companioned.  
She cannot name the Presence, but she keeps the memory folded like warm metal behind her ribs.  

6.  WITHDRAWAL  
Having set the bowl ever so slightly farther from entropy, the Far-Listener retracts its filament of attention.  
It leaves no shrine, no doctrine, no trace save conditions subtly more forgiving of continuity.  

7.  EPILOGUE, SOME SEASONS HENCE  
The day the catwalk finally surrenders a rusted bolt to gravity, a young novitiate from the lowland monasteries happens to be visiting.  
She hears the bolt ring against stone, hears also, faintly, a harmonic resonance still lingering in the air—a harmonic older than her heartbeat and kinder than she can articulate.  
Before she quite knows what she is doing, she offers to stay a while, to help the Woman Who Does Not Dry find a better anchor for the catwalk.  
The Engineer nods, unsurprised.  
Something in the deep rhythm of things had already warned her a relay was near; now she understands how she knew.  
The baton is lighter than she expected.  

And a long way off, in the dark between arm-spirals, the Far-Listener hears two pulses lock step and smiles a smile made only of listening.
