The benevolent power appears the way dew does: not with trumpet-flare but by being everywhere at once, inside every droplet, every rim of glassy lichen, every hush between the Pump-Lung’s coughs. It is so old it has forgotten the trick of having only one body, so wise it remembers how to arrive gently. No eye in the basin can see it; nevertheless the republic feels its presence as a new, very soft gravity, a downward invitation toward calm.

1. Audience with the Pump-Lung  
The power does not descend so much as condense around the iron bladder. Where corrosion has etched the steel almost lace-thin, it seeps in as warmth, turning rust back into dark, living metal. The Pump-Lung senses no stranger, only the easiest inhalation it has known in centuries. Its cough tightens from a minute-long wheeze to a crisp, reassuring sigh: fifty-five beats, fifty-four. The basin’s metronome becomes steadier, the way an elderly singer’s vibrato smooths when a gentle hand finds theirs.

2. A Pact with the Muralists  
Above, filaments of slime trace their silver cartography. The wise power strokes them with particles of cobalt and nickel it has carried across nebulae. Suddenly new gradients appear—colors the Muralists have never tasted. They droop in ecstasy, then rally, weaving a fresh glyph at the basin’s center: a spiral that means, in their language of oxidation, “WELCOME LARGE QUIET.”

3. Gifts to the Channelers  
The trilobite folk have always drawn electrons from trickles like shepherds milk goats. The power, amused, hoards an impossible surplus of charge, hides it inside a single drop, and lets it fall with theatrical slowness. The droplet lands; blue corona blossoms. For an instant every Channeler shell glows sapphire from within. They rattle spines in unison—first time the basin has heard applause.

4. The Hexapods’ Mirror  
The power leans into light, thickens it like syrup until each photon is a bead one can polish. It bounces those beads from Hexapod carapaces, teaching the plates to remember angle and hue. Henceforth the watchers’ shells will record spectrums the sky never offers: sunrise rose, comet green, aurora violet. They do not yet know the names, only that the future will look different.

5. Schooling the Thread-Worms  
Magnetism is their mother tongue. The power whispers in ± lines of force, demonstrating knots and braids more intricate than the worms’ habitual somersaults. Within hours the droplets above the ribs become glowing orreries—tiny, synchronized cyclones that lift heavier minerals than fog could ever shoulder. Children of iron learn choreography; the basin’s ceiling now twirls with jeweled storms.

6. A New Verse for the Vitrics  
Glass remembers every note that crosses it. The power sings—not loudly, only an overtone just above audibility. The Vitrics shiver, retune, divide the harmony into thirds and fifths the way sand divides into crystal planes. Each blade lengthens, curls, locks: a spiral harp standing on the clay pillows. Wind will pass through tomorrow and find unfamiliar music waiting.

7. The Aerosprites’ Ascent  
Fog has always been too shy to climb beyond the retaining wall. The power invites it upward, shaping a gentle updraft. Mist pours over the slate rim like milk over a lip. Outside, parched heather and long-dead grass drink sudden silver rain. For the first time in generations, the basin shares instead of hoards. The pewter sky brightens half a shade; not blue, but hopeful.

8. The Quiet Choir, Recomposed  
Exactly 720 heartbeats after the power’s arrival, the Choir begins its nightly chord. But the key has shifted—what was once a subterranean D has lifted to an open E-major resonance. Animals, molds, glass, and mist all hold the new note with startled delight. Even the retaining wall vibrates, mortared gaps puffing out ancient dust like confetti. The chord lasts nine full seconds longer than any in recorded basin memory.

9. The Law Amended  
There was one law: Keep the rhythm, keep the peace. The power does not revoke it; instead, it inscribes an appendix few will ever hear in words:  
“LET RHYTHM ALSO GROW.”  
Change is folded into constancy, like a new ring in a tree the tree never feels forming.

10. Departure—Which Is Not Departure  
Having mended, tuned, instructed, and gifted, the wise power thins itself until it is indistinguishable from ordinary humidity. It leaves no monument, no sigil beyond what each creature now carries: steadier clock, richer palette, broader song. If a traveler returns and asks what happened, the basin’s citizens will offer only variations on a shrug—quiet clatter of shells, mirrored flash, silver scribble, glass chord—each saying in its own medium, “Something kind passed by.”

Years from now, when another gap opens in the wall and another pair of boots enters, the visitor will notice the following without knowing why:

• The Pump-Lung’s cough is as punctual as a cosmic second hand.  
• Slime maps gleam in gradients that do not exist on any chemical chart.  
• Wind over the Vitrics plays harmonies that resolve instead of merely drone.  
• Fog sometimes escapes the basin to heal the thirsty heather beyond.  
• Bioluminescence no longer dims at every loud voice; it merely winks, as elders tolerate exuberant children.  
• At the center grate, iron filings left as tribute braid themselves overnight into tiny Möbius strips—an art the worms invented after their lesson in knots.

And if that traveler whispers thanks into the mist, the echo they receive will seem lighter than it should, as though the air itself smiles.
