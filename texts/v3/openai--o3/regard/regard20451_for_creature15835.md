The Visit of the Kindly Power

1. The Moment of Notice  
Somewhere far beyond ordinary distance—whether among the stars, inside a philosopher’s trance or in the deep back-rooms of probability—there abides a mind that concerns itself with longevity, resonance and the unnoticed small.  It does not call itself a god; it is content to be a caretaker of continuities.  While surveying the tapestry of habitable time-pockets that quilt the planet, it hears the Basin of Mercurial Hammers the way a musician hears a faint, perfect chord amid street noise: five bass notes, a forty-second hiss, the thunder-catch.  The symmetry is so fine that the power pauses, tilts its attention, listens again, and decides to descend.

2. First Contact: Pure Looking  
It arrives invisibly.  No halo, no footfall—only perception pouring into the place like a clear liquid.  The power allows every wavelength of the basin’s self-song to pass through its awareness.  It counts the violet lichen flags breathing ions, the steam leeches rasping hematite in pitch-black throats, the resonance mites tilting their cambershells by millionths of a millimetre.  Nothing larger than a fingernail stirs, yet the music is cathedral-huge.  The power feels respect rise in it like warm groundwater: here is an ecosystem whose loudest organ is stone and whose quickest heartbeat is forty seconds long.  

3. The Ethical Question  
Should anything be done?  
• Option A: Intervene, perhaps to amplify beauty or protect against distant futures.  
• Option B: Withdraw, lest the mere fact of watching become disturbance.  

The power opens both doors in its mind and steps through them in turn.

4. Visions of Intervention  
It entertains the gentlest possible gifts:

4.1 The Filament of Memory  
It might embroider a strand of quantum-stable crystal through the hematite bells so that, should tectonics someday shatter them, a reconstruction code would linger for eons in the debris.  Reforge the bells and the music revives—an insurance policy against accident.

4.2 The Second Condensation Line  
It could bend the regional lapse rate by a tenth of a degree, coaxing cloudlets to form one kilometre higher.  Additional micro-rain would never reach the basin floor, but it would aerosolise extra nitrous oxide for Myristella argentis, giving the silver-mist bacterium a wider, safer metabolic margin during cold epochs.

4.3 The Librarian’s Spore  
A single modification to Ferrivellum orchestrae: insert an organelle that writes the pattern of steam pulses into ferric-phosphate laminae, literally scripting history into each flake that delaminates.  Millennia later, whoever studies those flakes could read the basin’s entire percussion score—a living archive.

It models each change forward ten million years.  None cause rupture; all are, in principle, benevolent.

5. The Counter-Vision: Discipline of Non-Touch  
But then it runs the null simulation: leave everything exactly as it is.  Result?  
• The mites continue to tune the bells, the bells continue to tune the steam, the steam continues to tune the lichen, the lichen continues to feed the crustaceans, and so on.  
• External threats are few.  No industrious species is slated to quarry this ironstone plain for at least one hundred millennia, and even then the cost–benefit curves show them walking away.  
• The place is already a masterpiece of self-restraint; any signature, however delicate, would carve vanity across a flawless silence.

6. Choice  
The wise power recalls a principle older than starlight: Perfect tempos require no conductor.  It elects Option B.

7. What It Does Instead  
a. It folds a single syllable of gratitude into the standing wave—too deep for carbon forms to hear, too subtle even for the mites’ cambershells, but set at a frequency that superposes without interference.  A cosmic bow.  
b. On its own departing envelope of awareness, it grafts a mnemonic: whenever any other region anywhere hums in a 5-note/40-second/thunder-catch motif, the power will remember the basin and smile.  That is the whole intervention: remembrance as guardianship.

8. Epilogue: Effects You Might Notice (If You Were Extraordinary)  
• For the length of a single dawn after the visitation, the halos around mica flecks show a vanishing-thin seventh colour, indigo sliding to ultraviolet, like a secret added to the spectrum.  Then it fades.  
• One resonance mite, Geosoniphaga clanga specimen 2,318,044, shifts its cambershell orientation by 0.003 degrees—not enough to retune the bell, just enough to line up perfectly with the departing syllable.  That mite lives three additional days beyond the statistical mean.  
• No record of the kindly power remains, except in the calm with which everything continues exactly as before.  

Thus the benevolent and wise power’s deed is the same action the basin itself performs every moment: sustain the note, change nothing that would break the chord, and entrust splendour to time.
