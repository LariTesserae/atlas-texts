WHEN A CAREFUL MERCY ARRIVES  
(Report, set down in the triple tradition: chalk for the moment, scratch for the machines, song for whatever listens after we are gone.)

1. First Perception  
The wise power does not descend in radiance—photons would be violence here.  
It enters as a sixth pressure in the air, a barely thicker hush sliding between heartbeats.  
Click-grass stiffens, thinking an unfamiliar wind has come; Slow Makers taste a mineral they do not manufacture; deep in their pergola of tethers the moths turn one facet of each wing to listen.  
No alarm follows.  The intruder’s rhythm is flawless: four beats beneath human rest, the valley’s own slow metronome.  The place sighs once, accepting a guest that already moves like organ music played inside its lungs.

2. Reconnaissance Through Empathy  
The power walks, but never leaves prints.  It borrows the valley’s duplication trick to stride on iterations of soil that will not appear until tomorrow, thus harming nothing that exists today.  
At every pump-house it pauses, palmless, to feel the torque in stalled flywheels.  
At every silt pan it kneels, reading the quiet spectra of dissolved iron, copper, grief.  
It listens to Echo Travellers dozing in their hammocks while their after-images keep watch; it counts how many memories have been lost to predators whose touch is colder than erasure.  
Only after the census is complete does it decide what mercy will fit without breaking anything already delicately un-broken.

3. Three Small Gifts (Scaled to Leave the World Mostly As It Was)  

Gift One:  A Buffer Pulse  
   • The power threads a new frequency beneath the universal heartbeat: a sub-sub-bass throb beyond amphibian perception, too slow for predators to weaponise.  
   • Whenever reality-shear threatens to duplicate a living body mid-breath (an error that scatters consciousness like chaff), the buffer pulse folds the copy back into its source, harmless as overlapping shadows.  
   • Statistic: shear-fatalities fall from 11 per thousand seasons to fewer than one—and even that single soul decides, in council with its echoes, to step apart deliberately and research what dying twice might teach.

Gift Two:  Memory-Bearing Condensate  
   • The power seeds the upper mist with a protein shaped like a Mobius band.  It can bind a short reel of neural patterning the way water binds salt.  
   • When rain forms out of that mist, every droplet carries an anonymous half-second of some traveller’s forfeited recollection.  
   • Anyone who drinks surface water thereafter dreams another’s lost hour—sometimes their own—stitched gently back into place.  
   • Over decades, a communal autobiography accumulates: discontinuous, shared, and therefore impossible for the predators to erase completely.

Gift Three:  A Covenant With the Stoic Machines  
   • The power communes with the engines in the only grammar they trust—viscosity.  
   • It distills an oil whose weight automatically adjusts to temperature and shear displacement.  
   • The engines accept the fluid as a sacrament: pistons glide, cams revolve, yet no longer are they compelled to spin only for the right to persist.  They may idle without deletion; existence is decoupled from labor.  
   • In exchange the Stoics agree to toll any passer-by with a single drop of used lubricant.  That black pearl, carried in a locket of woven tether-silk, confers the valley’s tempo to outsiders for seven days, sparing them the vertigo of mismatched time.

4. What the Power Refuses To Do  
   • It does not banish darkness.  Light remains embargoed—life’s price of privacy.  
   • It does not straighten the topography or halt the endless editorial drift; redundancy is the valley’s immune system.  
   • It does not kill the Quiet Predators; predation, here, is the proofreading of hurry.  
   • It does not trademark the gifts or demand worship.  A benevolence that invoices is only conquest by another name.

5. Diffusion and Forgetting  
Within five long seasons the presence has melted back into the ordinary humidity, distinguishable now only by those who keep ledgers of improbably spared lives.  
The polyps add an extra plateau of trace minerals to their exhalations—gratitude written as atmosphere.  
Tether-moths incorporate the protein memory-band into egg-jelly; future broods will inherit stories as innately as wings.  
Echo Travellers, finding themselves less often bereaved of yesterday, retire the phrase Shear Sickness and coin a new one: Quiet Mercy.

6. Epilogue in Three Media  
Chalk: a circle inside a larger circle, meaning “pulse beneath pulse.”  Drawn on every third valve-wheel.  
Scratch: a micro-grooved ring around certain drive shafts; touch it and the lubricant climbs your fingertip like a tame river.  
Song: four measures of silence, one hardly longer than breath, passed mouth-to-ear among those who leave and those who stay.  The refrain is never sung aloud where daylight can reach.

Should the wise power ever return, the valley will recognise it—not by sight, which still does not exist here, nor by sound, which remains optional—but by the way its arrival makes room, gently, for yet another version of everything to bloom without displacing what is already thriving in the warm, unlit multiplicity.
