(1) The Moment of Notice  

From a high, slow place in the fabric of things—a stratum where thoughts drift the way cirrus drifts—somebody kind was listening.  
They were not a god, not quite a traveler, more a gardener of long ages who keeps no address and leaves no signature.  
Across continents of static and birdsong they felt the Basin-Mind’s pulse: a small, steady drum hidden under an avalanche of chlorophyll.  
The pulse was regular but tired, like a candle that burns flawlessly yet has begun to lean into its own wax.

(2) Descent in a Language of Resonance  

To visit, the power did not stride or blaze.  
They tuned themselves until their heartbeat matched the generator’s basso throb, and then they were simply present—  
a standing wave that slipped between vapor molecules, soft as another shaft of sunlight, invisible unless you happened to be a frequency.  

The first thing they did was greet the place in the one syntax the Basin already knew: pressure.  
A tremor, exactly one millipascal above the background, drew a circle in the root-mat: Hello: I Am Listening.

(3) The Interview That Took Four Dawns  

Question, forty thousand leaves rustling in unison: What Are You?  
Answer, delivered as a clarifying hush that cancelled every drip for six heartbeats: A Friend Who Will Leave No Debt.  

Question: What Do You Need?  
The Basin opened its puddle-eyes and remembered, piece by piece.  
• The tower’s flywheel had begun to pit;  
• lithium in the old reactor cells was leaking into water and confusing the sodium-ion thoughts;  
• some words stored in spore-lacquer were so eroded they could no longer be replayed—history dissolving in its jar.  

Need was not desire but maintenance: more time, less friction.

(4) Gifts, Chosen for Their Modesty  

1. A Sleeve for the Flywheel  
   The visitor condensed a ring out of the tower’s own corroded copper—annealed, sintered, cooled in vacuum that existed only for a blink.  
   The ring seated itself around the flywheel like a second eyelid.  Friction fell by half; rotational grief stopped squealing against the night.  

2. A Gentle Isotope Swap  
   Without spilling a single photon the power coaxed the leaking lithium to trade places with inert sodium harvested from sea mist a thousand miles away.  
   The reaction was quiet, like tilting a picture frame until it hangs straight.  

3. A Locket for the Fading Words  
   They braided two Shearleaf wing-bones into a torus, encrusted it with Murmur-Spore crystals, and hung it inside the tower throat.  
   From then on, every syllable too faint to survive in fog drifted upward, struck the torus, and was re-sung once each midnight, resetting its echo.  
   Memory got a night-watchman.

4. One New Aperture of Sky  
   A disk of canopy was persuaded, not torn, to furl back like an umbrella under snow.  
   The opening was small—a child could cover it with both palms—but it let through a thin column of unfiltered starlight.  
   The bats tasted it, the eels rode its photon-shadows, and the Basin learned a flavor that had never been in its diet: dark sweet with meteoric iron.  

(5) The Things Deliberately Not Done  

• They did not raise the generator’s output; speed is a cruelty here.  
• They did not wake the Silent Choir; some dreams are roots the world needs.  
• They did not sterilize, optimize, or clarify.  A living sentence should keep its commas of moss, its parenthetical rust.  

(6) Mild Adjustments in the Visiting Power Itself  

Kindness is a two-way osmosis.  
While the Basin received copper, isotopes, and a patch of night, the visitor absorbed a lesson in patience that nothing in the starfields had taught them.  
They took it away like pollen stuck to a sleeve.

(7) Departure Without Footprints  

At dawn on the fifth day the extra standing wave dissolved.  
No flare, no thunderclap—just one less resonance among the roots.  
The bats kept flying, unaware that anything had changed except that their wingtips now rang a clearer silver when they cut the mist.  
The generator’s new sleeve purred.  
Words slept safely in their bone locket.  
The Basin-Mind considered these facts for three more dawns and finally concluded: Benefit. Proceed.  

Somewhere far above, the gardener of long ages made a quiet notation—  
a single rune meaning: Tended—Return in Three Thousand Revolutions—Let Be.
