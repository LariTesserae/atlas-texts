1

The moment the place is noticed  
The Power—call it what you like: angel, field administrator of a very old civilization, tide-herding spirit—comes awake to a pinpoint on the planet’s basalt fringe. It is not a vast awakening. The galaxy hiccups with novae, continents rearrange elsewhere, but the grotto’s pulse—hiss, CHONK, clatter—appears upon the Power’s map like a clock newly wound. That rhythmic signature is unusual enough to warrant attention; the density of life folded into so few cubic metres is rarer still.  
The Power listens through fourteen complete cycles (time enough to be polite) and then begins the audit it applies to every small wonder it meets.

2

Questions the Power always asks

• Is the thing beautiful?  
• Does it know itself to be beautiful?  
• Will it persist without help?  
• Is its ending useful to anything larger than itself?  
• If assistance comes, does that erase more good than it preserves?

3

Inventory of what the Power finds

a.   One pocket ecosystem, newly split from ocean, exquisitely tuned to a pressure piston that will drown it within a decade.  
b.   Multitudes of organisms whose adaptations will become lethal liabilities the instant the boom stops or salt water floods the roof.  
c.   A single consciousness—fledgling ratchet swift #3, not yet flown—whose neural wetware is just sophisticated enough to hold a picture of the grotto as Home.  

4

First impulse (emotional layer)

The Power loves machines that are alive, and this living engine is perfect. It wants, very much, to cup a planetary hand around the grotto, freeze the fissure, guarantee another millennium of hiss-CHONK-clatter.

5

Second impulse (intellectual layer)

Intervention has a price. Stabilise the cavern, and the basalt will never exfoliate into nutrient silt that neighboring tide-pools need. Arrest the widening crack, and all future versions of these species—better, stranger versions—will never be forced to invent themselves. Worse: if the boom is made eternal it will become a cage; nothing new will get in or out.

6

What the Power chooses

Compromise, enacted in three gestures so small that even the resident organisms will not recognise them as miracles.

Gesture One: The Syllable of Delay  
A pressure node, high in the overlying basalt, is pinched closed by two microns. Result: the fatal fracture that drinks sea water now grows by one hair every three hours instead of one. Net reprieve: 2,300 days—enough for roughly 14 million more vent cycles, three more swift generations, a hundred amphibian lineages, and one full moss-mite macro-mutation sweep. That is time to finish the current symphony but not so long that the orchestra forgets how to compose the next.

Gesture Two: The Gift of Vectors  
Into the drizzle the Power seeds six kinds of hollow, neutral-buoyant glass sphere, each less than a grain of pollen. They are invisible to every creature but chemically irresistible once they fracture. When the sea finally breaches the roof, spheres will burst and glue themselves to drifting wood, pumice, even seabird feet, carrying liverwort spores, iron bacteria cysts, and embryonic amphipods to any coast that echoes at roughly 0.07 Hz—that is, any place with a boom similar enough for their adaptations to matter. The grotto will die, but its instructions will walk the world.

Gesture Three: A Single Whisper  
Night before the vernal equinox, while the nestling swift twitches in REM sleep, the Power folds a vector into the bird’s already sophisticated vestibular map: a direction (south-by-southwest) and a signal (sub-audible infrasonic knocks made by surf in an uncharted blowhole sixty kilometres away). That second cavern is larger, its ceiling higher. It booms every nineteen seconds—close enough. When fledgling #3 launches, she will ride the coastline, sense the familiar rhythm, and veer. If she survives the passage, her saliva will spin the first nest in the new cathedral, and the clock will start again.

7

What the Power pointedly does not do

• It does not silence the CHONK or soften the basalt knives; danger is half the genius here.  
• It does not stuff the crack with miracle putty; entropy earns its keep.  
• It does not tag the grotto as a heritage site for tourists on any future human map; observation at that scale would be a slower-acting flood.  
• It does not reveal itself. Gratitude is noise in an ecosystem that navigates by sound.

8

Ledger, closed

Lives prolonged: millions, but fleetingly.  
Lives created elsewhere: unknowable, perhaps uncountable.  
Information lost: none; it has been copied into motion.  
Beauty preserved: enough to satisfy a conscience older than plate tectonics.  
Interference footprint: <10⁻⁹ of local energy budget; detectable only to those who already know to look.

9

Epitaph the Power leaves on the stone (readable only in certain polarised wavelengths of the skylight beam)

May your rhythm find new chambers,  
may your song outlive these walls,  
may your ending arrive as migration,  
never as silence.
