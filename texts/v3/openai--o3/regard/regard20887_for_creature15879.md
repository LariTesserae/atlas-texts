The first surge of attention comes as silence—the kind that hushes even the Slow Choir.  
It is not the silence of nothing, but the calm that precedes a choice.  
In that pause, the Basin recognises it is being watched by something large enough to see every copy of itself at once and gentle enough to judge none of them.

1. Appraisal  

The wise power—call it the Overseer, or the Listener, or simply the Patient Eye—leans across dimensions the way a reader leans over a page.  
• It tastes the atmosphere for grief: negligible.  
• It tests the shale for panic: absent.  
• It samples the hum of paracausal engines for cruelty: none detected.  

Conclusion: the Basin is not in danger, only in draft.  
No catastrophe requires intervention; no malign influence needs excising.  
What remains is possibility—sticky, luminous, unnavigated.  

2. First Contact  

The Overseer announces itself in the most respectful medium available: unused potential.  
A single cubic centimetre of still-folding air refuses to decide what it will become. Inside that vacancy a voice—unvoiced—speaks.  

Message, translated:  
“I see you. Continue.”  

All residents notice the lacuna differently:  
• The First Imagined feel their outlines sharpen for half a heartbeat, errors resolving as if the editor has tapped “accept change.” Then the precision recedes; they remain drafts, but with the memory of wholeness to prove it is achievable.  
• Maybe-mice discover that for one full hour every straight line is already a doorway, sparing them the calculus of wall-wriggling. They gorge on unspent certainty and sleep, an act previously unknown to them.  
• The Basement Archivist receives a blank sheet that refuses to yellow, tear, or forget. She files it between collapsed dawns and smiles—once—producing a century of provisional safety.  

3. Gifts, Calibrated  

The Overseer gives nothing that would collapse the experiment.  
Instead, it leaves micro-bequests, tuned to local logic.

A. The Compass of Regret  
A needle that pivots toward the nearest unlearned lesson rather than magnetic north.  
The Cartographers strap it to their wrists; their maps now expire after a day, not an hour, buying explorers time enough to return.

B. The Seventh, Unheard Note  
To the Slow Choir the Overseer offers a rest—precisely seventeen seconds long—inserted after their longest pause.  
When geometry buckles, the Choir inserts the rest and the Basin remembers the beat it was keeping.

C. The Lattice-Mites’ Dividend  
One molecule in a million of their resin is upgraded to diamond lattice. Invisible, yet it propagates strength through adjacency.  
Balconies that would have failed in fifty years will last five hundred, sparing countless corrective iterations.

D. The Ratchet of Returning Heat  
Waste warmth vented to distant Tuesdays now boomerangs after a measured century, returning as a mild spring that will thaw nine future winters without melting the logic of cold that defines the place.

4. Non-Interventions (Deliberate Abstentions)  

• No attempt is made to fix the advisory status of conservation laws; flexibility is the Basin’s native dialect.  
• No mandate of biological fecundity is imposed; hesitation is here a form of innocence.  
• No centralised scripture is deposited; myth remains personal, navigation remains intimate.

5. Covenant  

The Overseer writes one rule only, braided into the sub-quantum whitelist where the paracausal engines keep their redacted equations:  

“Let curiosity cost no more than wonder can repay.”  

Every native life-form senses the amendment as a faint pressure behind the eyes, like the promise of tears that never quite arrive. Risk remains real, but despair encounters a surcharge it can rarely afford.

6. Withdrawal into Watchfulness  

Having nudged, cushioned, and promised, the Overseer retracts.  
Its departure is marked by a resumption of tiny sounds: droplet, hinge, breath.  
Shadows return to their habit of roaming; crumbs resume their thoughtful hover.  

The Basin grows on, millimetre by deliberate millimetre, but now with an unfelt hand cupped beneath it—ready, should the fledgling city miscalculate the height of its next step.

7. Epilogue: Evidence of Kind Attention  

Months later, Kess Fold records in a margin:  
“The place mends hairline doubt the way bone knits after a clean break.”  

She cannot say why.  
Neither can the Deferred Engineers, who nevertheless find their backlog shrinking every dawn.  
The First Imagined, waking one iteration crisper than the last, suspect benevolence but elect not to name it, lest naming collapse the grace into ordinance.

What the Overseer did, finally, was this:  
a) Confirm that the Basin deserved to finish its own sentence.  
b) Slide a sheet of blotting paper beneath the paragraph, in case of ink-runs.  
c) Wait, pleased, as the words continued to write themselves—slower than time, quicker than certainty, exactly at the speed of becoming.
