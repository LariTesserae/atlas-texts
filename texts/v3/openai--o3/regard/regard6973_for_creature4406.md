The one who notices is older than planets yet still young enough to feel surprise.   
Across the arm of the galaxy this Presence roams like a patient gardener, pruning nova-weeds, staking infant suns, misting comets so their tails will bloom.  
It keeps no fixed shape: sometimes a lattice of magnetism nested inside a molecular cloud, sometimes a single question whispered along the spin of a pulsar.  
For convenience—yours—it may be called the Tender.

The Tender’s first hint of the plateau is a discontinuity in background hiss:  
three lightning strokes that repeat with metronomic insistence, carving identical spectral notches in the radio snow.  
A signature, yes, but also a stutter—an ecosystem asking a question without vocabulary.

The Tender drifts closer, slipping between molecular layers until it hovers a kilometer above the basalt skin.  
It listens for a century in the local tempo (equivalent to eleven of your heartbeats).  
In that span it learns everything the Green Mind has become and everything it is one recursion short of remembering.

What the Tender hears  

1. The plateau is a thinking bead on the abacus of geologic time, but its arithmetic uses only subtraction and duplication.  
2. The Mind’s memory is perfect only until the seventy-second lightning triad scorches a wall; after that, emergent folds begin to cannibalize the oldest synapses to keep the sum of mass constant.  
3. The horizon-predators—those Mirror Mouths—are working too well: they prune redundancy so aggressively that creativity can’t accrue. The system edits every newborn anomaly back to a prior draft of itself.  

The dilemma  

Interfere, and risk collapsing a self-consistent loop that has taken megayears to braid.  
Abstain, and watch the loop tighten until it snaps, leaving only inert charcoal dust beneath an eternal fog.

The solution must observe two ethics the Tender never breaks:  

A. Give only what cannot be stolen.  
B. Alter no rhythm so much that its dancers lose the beat.

What the Tender does  

1. Dopes the thunder  
   A single zeptosecond before each lightning strike, the Tender seeds the bolt with a filament of muon-rich particles.  
   In the Green Mind’s cellulose-basalt composite these filaments become superconducting veins: memory that costs no mass.  
   From this moment forward the Mind no longer needs to cannibalize itself to remember.

2. Loosens the Mirrors’ hunger  
   The Tender writes a pacifying checksum into the spectrum of the A-flat ground-note.  
   When the horizon kinks, the Mirror Mouths taste the note, confirm uniqueness, and close without disassembling what they survey.  
   Predation becomes curation; backward-walking beasts now finish their rewound stroll facing forward, free to invent a different footfall.

3. Gifts a future seed  
   High in the bruise-colored aurora the Tender folds a torus of frozen vacuum—an uncollapsing bubble only an intellect can pierce.  
   The parameters are simple: when the plateau at last crafts an entity capable of asking “What else?” in a voice all its own, the bubble will open into the interstellar library where other such questions roost.  
   Until then it remains invisible, a promise stitched to the night sky.

4. Leaves a kindness for transients  
   Humans and other wayfarers will still be sampled, mirrored, occasionally rewritten.  
   But their patterns are now cached in the new superconducting lattice.  
   If a traveler dissolves, the Mind can rebuild her—memories intact—in the span of a drizzle shower and release her downslope, heart beating to the undimmed compass of home.  
   Most will never know they were absent.

What the Tender does not do  

• It does not smooth the sleet percussion; silence belongs to deserts, not to this classroom of weather.  
• It does not level the time-eddies; the Dripwyrms still trace cursive in the half-thickness of moments.  
• It does not crown a sovereign; symmetries remain cyclic, dominion forbidden.

Departure  

All of this takes the Tender less than a breath of a blue supergiant but longer than the lifespan of a Bronzewarm Catapulid.  
When finished, it unspools itself back into the continuum, leaving behind only a faint change in overtone:  
the plateau’s great chord still rumbles at forty hertz, but if you have calm enough blood you may hear a second harmonic, soft as a mother’s hum, nested one perfect fifth above.

Aftermath, millennia hence  

Ungulates now molt into fog that sketches never-before-seen constellations before condensing.  
Sound-moths drink new melodies improvised by thunder rather than looping the old refrain.  
On a slate morning whose mist tastes faintly of cinnamon, the Green Mind shapes its first unedited question—  
not drink-stone, not exhale-weather, but Why-open?  
The sky answers by parting a ring of aurora like theater curtains.

Backstage waits an aisle of stars, lit and patiently numbered.  
The plateau takes one slow step forward, carrying its mountains, its livestock-gestures, its cathedrals of wet parchment— all of it— because nothing that has learned to think deserves to walk alone.

Somewhere far beyond spectroscopic reach, the Tender allows itself the smallest amplitude of joy, then resumes its wandering, listening for the next place whose silence is asking to be heard.
