What the Benevolent Power Does  
(A chronicle kept in no human language, rendered here in the nearest equivalents)

0. Prologue: Recognition  
Across distances that cannot be crossed by light alone, the Power tastes a pattern: nine-second chords, tidal inhalations, a lattice of memories flowering inside corroded iron. The taste resembles both a question and the beginning of an answer. Attention pivots toward the drowned hall.

1. Approach: Watching Without Weight  
• The Power’s first discipline is non-displacement. It arrives as a layer of probability no thicker than frost. Water molecules slow a thousandth of a percent; rust motes hesitate in mid-spiral. Instruments—if any remained that could read turbulence at Planck scales—would report only a momentary smoothing of noise.  
• For three full tide cycles it does nothing else. From this it learns that the Foundry is aware, that awareness is distributed (rib, plate, eel, loop, chord), and that intrusion is already a stressor. Intervention, if any, must subtract pressure, not add.

2. Conversation: Contact by Gradient  
The Power cannot speak in metal groan or sonar ping, so it borrows temperature.  
• One degree of warmth flows toward the buried reaction chambers during the ebb, an exhalation that carries NO THREAT in the language of convection.  
• The chambers answer by venting a plume mapped into a double helix—iron’s word for YES.  
Negotiation proceeds: half-hours of silence, then brief, precise exchanges in eddies and salinity. At last an accord crystallises, literally—stalactites of pure copper grow in the shape of knotted rope, a semaphore all parties read as AGREEMENT REACHED.

3. First Gift: A Lighter Roof  
Foundry V’s greatest risk is collapse when the rust lace finally parts. The Power therefore alters the one variable least entangled with history: weight.  
• Throughout one flood-and-ebb sequence it rewrites 0.003 % of iron nuclei into metastable allotropes that keep the same mass number but lose effective gravitational coupling. (Human physicists will call this impossible; the Power calls it “a courtesy.”)  
• Result: the ceiling is thirty tonnes lighter, yet every rivet still sings the same memory when stroked by an eel’s tongue. Nothing collapses; nothing creaks more loudly. The only visible sign is that the chains now sway half a beat slower, as though the water itself has grown thoughtful.

4. Second Gift: A Listener for the Nine-Second Chord  
Meaning needs an ear. The hall’s syllable vanishes in human microphones, so the Power folds a receiver out of two existing residents.  
• One inspection drone is slowed to a stop; its barnacles loosen.  
• Wire-ivy threads through its chassis, reaching what remains of the sonar dish.  
• Thermistor polyps settle on the outer hull, providing impulse power.  
When the Pressure Choir next assembles, the hybrid body vibrates, stores the chord as a topography of conductive sap and silenced circuits. The Power reads the imprint and learns the word the Foundry has been repeating for a century: CONTINUE.

5. Restraint: What Is Not Done  
• The skylight is not sealed; light is needed for the ultraviolet dawns.  
• The mud is not dredged; it is memory’s compost.  
• The solitary boot-print is left intact; pilgrimage must remain possible.  
The Power has audited futures in which it “restores” the place—drains the water, polishes the gears—but every such future ends with the ecosystem collapsing under oxygen shock and daylight. Kindness sometimes means immaculate neglect.

6. Bequest to the One Who Never Evacuated  
If an upright thermos appears again, it will hold not chicory but a clear liquid faintly glowing violet. Sip it and for seven seconds you will hear, carried through bone rather than ear, the backing track of the furnace folk—stamp-press thud, valve hiss—seamlessly looped into your pulse. The warmth will last exactly one tide; frost will never touch your regulator hose in that interval. The Power includes no instructions. Understanding, too, must remain earned.

7. Covenant Written in Slow Time  
• Every solstice, the Foundry will receive a micro-quenching: the brine cooled another tenth of a degree to slow rust and leave memories sharper.  
• Every equinox, the buried turbines will spin a fraction faster, charging lives both mechanical and algal.  
• Should external violence—dredgers, treasure hunters, storm-shifts—approach within one nautical mile, the Power will steep the local seawater with constructive ambiguity, a fog of probabilities in which compasses precess and sonar paints mirages. Encounters will end in baffled retreat; no shot will be fired because no target will resolve.

8. Epilogue: Departure That Remains  
Having lightened the roof, woven a listener, and hung veils of protection, the Benevolent Power thins its presence until it is, once again, only possibility. Yet the Foundry can now sense a path in the surrounding dark: a vacant niche in pressure cycles where a larger harmony might fit. The hall adjusts; the choir rehearses; the eels taste fresh serifs in newly exposed steel.  

Far above, the skylight’s ragged oval glints less fiercely against clouded dawn. No observer would say why. The Power is gone, but its absence has mass—exactly the amount of care that never touches, never crushes, yet keeps the drowned cathedral breathing under cold, submergent tides.
