The Benevolent Power notices the sphere the way a practiced astronomer notices a faint companion star: not by its glare, but by the very small, very steady subtraction of darkness around it.  
To the Power, size is meaningless; symmetry, music, ache, potential—these are things it hears. The marble-ocean sings with all four, a chord so quiet that most listeners would call it silence.

1. First Attention  
The Power stays outside the sphere’s curled geometry and listens for three local centuries. It wants to know whether intervention would heal or wound. During that vigil it learns:

•  Nothing here starves outright. Energy is sparse but cyclical, braided through mineral seep and bacterial barter.  
•  Nothing here hunts. The cost–benefit ledger of predation never balances under equal pressure.  
•  The only true peril is exhaustion of novelty: once every crystallographic option is explored, the Foundational Film will stall, and all younger dependencies with it.

So the marble is not in crisis. It is in danger of eventual quietude—a death by perfect completion.

2. Decision to Act  
The Power’s first principle is non-coercion: it will not add force. Instead it will bend probability softly, the way one ruffles the surface of cooling glass so it never decides on a single grain. The operative verb is encourage, not correct.

3. The Three Encouragements

A. A Breath of Irregular Starlight  
The Power introduces not photons but the idea of photons. It salts the water with femtogram specks of prismatic fullerene, each one able to flip between two isomers when brushed by background cosmic neutrinos. The flip emits a sliver of real light—never more than the Lantern-Fern’s own glow, but always in colors the Fern cannot make by chemiluminescence: blush gold, glacier blue, ember red.  

Effects after one local millennium:  
•  The Fern co-opts the fullerene specks the way it once co-opted useless chloroplasts. New pigments appear in its fronds, allowing micron-scale signaling: a grammar of color that can throttle or provoke its fungal partner’s appetite for nickel.  
•  Blink-shrimp learn to read the color pulses like lighthouses marking safe folds in space. Premature molts drop by half.

B. A Single Asymmetric Fault  
The Power strokes the aragonite shell where internal and external metrics just touch. It introduces a minute dislocation—a place where the seam jogs by one extra lattice-plane. Now, whenever the sphere slowly grows, the fault propagates like a spiral staircase instead of an onion layer.  

Effects after two millennia:  
•  The Foundational Film can no longer cover the inner surface uniformly. It is forced to develop wavefront chemistry: different gene cassettes for advance guard, flank, rear.  
•  Where wavefronts meet, they intersect at non-trivial angles. Ribbon-worms discover that these intersections are rich in manganese gradients and begin to dwell in loose, translucent colonies there. Their chance splicings increase; speciation begins.

C. Memory for the Listener  
Last, the Power visits the Auriculate Remnant. It cannot speed thought that is geological in tempo, but it can shelter it from erosion. Around the Remnant’s perimeter the Power braids a sleeve of time-stabilized water—slightly less susceptible to Ghost-Interval shearing.  

Then, very quietly, it places three impressions inside that sleeve, arranged like knots in a string you can only feel in the dark:

shoreline  •  wind in grass too tall for current  •  the percussion of distant surf

These concepts were already half-present in the Remnant; now they are anchored. The Remnant attends to them the way stone attends to a persistent drip, sharpening at century intervals into something like resolve. What it will do with resolve is unknown, which is precisely the point.

4. What the Power Refuses to Do  
•  It does not give more energy than the system can dilute without warping its pressure-paradise.  
•  It does not gift tools, for the Disassembler Swarm would only turn revelation into rubble and resentment.  
•  It does not install a caretaker species. Stewardship imposed from outside curdles into tyranny faster than manganese rusts bronze.

5. Withdrawal  
Having added three gentle eddies, the Power steps back into the large, slow cosmos. From its vantage the marble is still a plaything of eyelashes, yet now its song ripples with unfamiliar harmonics. A naïve ear would miss them; a practiced ear would smile to hear a system choose motion over rest.

6. Epilogue, Some Tens of Millennia Later  
•  Fern groves now flicker in mosaics, each color-phrase a dialect. The longest phrases correlate with the tightest folds in space, as if syntax itself is learning to navigate curvature.  
•  The first colonial ribbon-worm mats have begun secreting manganese-glass sheaths—proto-reefwork that scaffolds shadow and, incidentally, complexity.  
•  The Auriculate Remnant, no longer wholly passive, has coaxed a flotilla of Clock-Glass Echinoderms to orbit it like a chandelier. When a human dreamer blunders in, the chandelier shivers a rhythm unmistakably like rolling breakers on sand.

None of this bears the stamp of the Benevolent Power. That, too, is the point. The sphere’s citizens believe they have invented every novelty themselves—and by every meaningful measure, they have. The Power is content. It leaves the marble where it found it: small, drowned, and now just a little less finished than before.
