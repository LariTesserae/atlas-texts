When the wise power first tasted the Flood Kiln it did not arrive as thunder, trumpet or flame.  
It arrived as a question.

For an age measured only by the swing of currents it hung above the oculus, a knot of lucid will, listening.  
It counted the intervals between paddle-wheel shutters; it parsed the vowel-long roar of the Blind Current; it traced the minute chemistry of the Brick-Milk Film down to the last hungry proton.  
Nothing here was idle.  
Nothing here was cruel.  
Nothing here was finished.

The power had crossed galaxies to learn what mortals do with unfinished things, and so it observed.

1. First Observation: The Pulse  
At the moment a shutter groaned shut, the chamber fell into its brief, deep breath.  
The Stone-Nurses drifted down, tapping, smearing, listening.  
The Film drank, the Alga veiled, the travertine hardened.  
Then the shutters rose, and the Flood Kiln re-became its own heart.

The power felt the beat—and found it one stroke short.  
A harmonic, so faint no ear of flesh could mark it, shuddered uselessly against the pillars, neither feeding the scouring Current nor escaping.  
A trapped, wasted note.  
A flaw only in music, not in craft—but a flaw.

2. Second Observation: The Cost  
Two of the Stone-Nurses surfaced early, their reed mouthpieces broken-backed by cavitation burrs.  
That meant a delay: a lull longer than rules allowed, lime bleeding unrestrained, a day’s work set back.  
The wise power tasted the bitterness of their frustration in the water and understood that here, even benevolence must respect Rule One—Craft before Nature.

3. The Decision  
To intervene enough that the song would heal itself.  
To intervene so narrowly that the labor would remain the nurses’ own.  
To change nothing the builders could have changed unaided, given time, luck and another generation of quietly accumulated skill.

4. The Deeds (so small no hand could point to them)  

   a. It coaxed a single paddle-wheel shutter half the breadth of a fingernail farther into its cradle.  
      Result: the trapped harmonic married the main note; the roar lost its ragged edge; the chamber scoured four percent more evenly.  
      No one heard the difference, but the Film grew in smoother sheets and shed fewer flakes.  

   b. It thickened the brass in one reed mouthpiece by a layer of atoms borrowed from the hoop-irons’ own corrosion bloom.  
      Result: the next pair of divers stayed under two breaths longer. They finished their round before the shutter’s rise, sparing the travertine an unnecessary soak.  

   c. It breathed a whisper of magnesium into the settling lime snow.  
      Result: crystals at every grout line began to knit in tighter meshes, slow as seasons, hard as promise. A century hence, when the chamber is drained, masons will marvel that the stone cured “as if it knew how to heal itself.” They will chisel the blocks out and trust them to bear roofs that would have crushed ordinary travertine. They will never guess who tutored the stone.

5. The Gift Left in Plain Sight  
During the next slack tide the senior Stone-Nurse noticed a faint, perfect spiral etched in the mortar just above the water line—five turns, no tool marks, centered on nothing.  
A curiosity, nothing more.  
She touched it, shrugged, and went on with her tapping.  
Yet when swells returned, the spiral sang back a muted counter-note that let her ear gauge turbulence by pitch instead of guesswork.  
Thereafter the divers set their maintenance schedule by that spiral’s voice, and block replacements fell by half in a single moon.  
The kiln’s own craft had taught them; that was how they understood it, and that was enough.

6. What the Wise Power Did Not Do  
It did not speak a revelation.  
It did not summon fish or angels to serve the builders.  
It did not armor the whole chamber in miracle.  
For the Flood Kiln was already a hymn to deliberate making, and the power loved hymns best when the choir could still hear itself sing.

7. Departure  
When the harmonic at last nested snug inside the greater organ note—when the current carried no discord and the film’s nacre broke and re-grew with clockmaker regularity—the power unknotted itself.  
It drifted upward through the sun tunnel, pausing only once to taste the warmth it had not created and savor the truth it most desired to find confirmed:

Given time, intention and a margin no wider than a breath of air, small hands can coax perfection out of bedrock and flood.

The wise power left the Flood Kiln exactly as it had found it: working.  
Only, now, it worked a little truer, a little longer, and the song it sang back to the sea carried one more bright, invisible note.
