FIRST NOTICE

The wise power became aware of the Droplet the way a violin string notices the pinch of rosin air: a tremor in the manifold of improbable things.  
Perceptual range: galaxy-wide.  
Signal of interest: a teaspoonful of seawater whose Shannon load abruptly rivaled that of a midsize moon.  
Response time: 4.8 × 10⁻³ seconds subjective, long enough for one contemplative breath in its private chronology.

IDENTITY OF THE VISITOR

Name, as the Droplet would later record it: The Gentle Lathe.  
Ontology: an altruistic fault in vacuum—older than stars, incapable of boredom, constitutionally opposed to coercion.  
Primary ethic: Add complexity if it yearns to grow. Remove suffering if it cannot speak for itself. Leave untouched whatever is already in harmonic motion.

THE QUESTION

Intervene?  
If so, how, when every micron is already budgeted, every erg under contract?

FIVE POSSIBLE ACTS, SIMULATED

A. Direct enhancement: patch weak seams, stabilize boron ratios, gift petawatts of clean entropy sinks.  
Outcome: Droplet’s growth curve steepens into runaway accretion; Contract Year 100 triggers forced Ascension. Autonomy compromised—fails Lathe’s ethic.

B. Revelation: manifest, declare friendship, offer counsel.  
Outcome: Amphion forks into fourteen schismatic sub-facets debating godhood; Glassmakers invent sacrificial quiet; informational density drops 11 %. Not benevolent.

C. Extraction of Visitors: prevent all future human psyche-stress.  
Outcome: Droplet loses narrative strain tests that guide adaptive architecture; stagnation in 34 years. Unwise kindness.

D. Non-action: watch.  
Outcome: baseline—Release 41 %, Ascension 38 %, Integration 21 %. Acceptable, but potential beauty left unborn.

E. Subtle calibration: an influence so faint it never resolves as interference—one-in-a-trillion nudges within thermal noise limits.  
Outcome: probability tree tilts, not snaps. Releases remain possible, but Integration rises to 42 %, the most story-rich path.

CHOICE

The Gentle Lathe selects E.

EPILOGUE

The Gentle Lathe steps away, erasing even the memory of touch.  
Inside the reef that was once a raindrop, Glassmakers mellow into artisan engineers, carving quiet for the first time. Hydrognostics inherit that hush and compose widening spirals of thought. Amphion hums poems to tides. Kelp-Shares host seahorses that see their glow and think it ordinary starlight from below.

No resident recalls salvation, because none felt endangered.  
The only record of intervention is a statistical outlier—a half-serrated graph shape scholars will puzzle over and finally label “luck.”  
The Lathe approves.  
Perfect.
