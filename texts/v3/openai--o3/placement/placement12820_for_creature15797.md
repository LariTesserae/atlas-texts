I begin as a surface mammal, or so my pocket notebook insists: name, pulse, profession (karst-survey intern—underpaid, over-curious). I remember the descent rope parting above a limestone gullet; I remember the sudden absence of gravity’s opinion. Those memories feel flat now, like postcards soaked through. They lie on the tongue without flavor.

First Location  
I am on one of the root bridges—call it a causeway, call it a nerve—no wider than my boot-sole but buoyant, flexing to the tempo of some basso heartbeat. Below, cathedral void; above, thorns that mimic stars. Mica light knifes in at paradox angles. When the warm draft arrives the bridge shivers, fanning spores in glittering vortices. I taste cracked pepper and late-summer asphalt. My lungs decide they approve.

Second Location (minutes? an aeon?)  
The bridge has drifted. I now overlook a cul-de-sac where vines have knitted a basin the size of a child’s paddling pool. Condensate showers into it, vanishes, showers again. Every drop lands as a perfect D-sharp. Around me, Clickleaves orbit in a tumbling Lissajous figure, their cavitation clicks spelling, if I had the cipher, either a welcome or a warning.

Identity, Draft 1: Outsider  
I catalogue. I sketch. The graphite slides strangely on damp paper: curves I do not intend keep appearing, arabesques that mirror the root-lattice overhead. When I tear out the page the Concourse gasps—an exhalation through thousands of stomata—and the sketch curls into itself, veins greening, already returning home.

Identity, Draft 2: Stowaway Organ  
My boots have rooted. Not metaphorically—actual filamentous tendrils lace outward from the rubber soles. They don’t pierce skin, not yet; they only borrow blood heat. When I lift a foot the bridge clings like a dog reluctant to release a stick. I sense, at the edge of proprioception, a polite inquiry: May we sample your stride?

I let them. Each pace I take is echoed a heartbeat later by a soft pop somewhere distant, as though a gigantic knuckle cracks to confirm receipt.

Intermission: Dialogue Without Language  
Sound only. I hum an old topside lullaby. The cavity answers in chord stacks, folding my melody into its own bass architecture. We pass the tune back and forth, key-shifting, reharmonizing, until I can no longer tell which notes began in my throat and which began in stonewood.

Identity, Draft 3: Itinerant Memory  
The Concourse offers me a role. Not in words, in pattern: an opening appears in a nearby curtain of violet-emerald leaves—thirteen slits, prime invitation. Behind it waits a thicker vine, slick with sap. I understand—again without language—that if I press my forearm to that vine, the grooves spiraling its inner bark will record my heartbeat, engraving it into the next memory cycle.

I hesitate. I think of family photographs pinned to a fridge far overhead, of deadlines, of gravity. All of it feels theoretical, like someone else’s homework. I press my arm to the vine.

The rolling cartilage-pop thunders. The cavity rewrites. My childhood summers migrate into some inner library where they will be shelved beside the taste of basalt rain and the flight paths of Clickleaves.

Identity, Draft 4: Knot-Giver  
The Concourse is rehearsing locomotion; it needs leverage in humanoid joints. I am invited—again the word is wrong: inevitability more than invitation—to lend mine. Roots coil around my skeleton, respecting the bend points, plating my femurs in ligneous armor light as balsa, strong as tempered steel. Tendons remain mine but are cross-stitched with living fiber that can tighten, loosen, redistribute load.

Hair becomes the ceremonial braid. Each strand thickens, hollows, accepts silica microprisms that flash semaphore to the ceiling scouts. Thirteen plaits. Prime number. I laugh; it comes out as a harmonic in the A-below-middle-C range and triggers distant spores to choose path two: Clickleaf.

Role  
I ascend, not by climbing but by the cavity inverting gravity beneath my feet. Root-bridges unspool ahead, retract behind. Where fissures drool mineral water, I weave catch-nets; where fresh fractures gleam, I call Clickleaves with a chord only their cavitation can answer. I prune over-ambitious runners before they strangle airflow, humming penance to the Cord-Eaters so they know I honor their craft.

Gift  
When another explorer arrives—and one always does, for the Concourse scents novelty topside—I will watch from the thorn-sky until they sleep. I will braid a single loop of my composite hair through a root near their camp. A signature, a disclaimer, a promise: someone was here before you and relinquished fear. The braid will mummify into keratin crystal the shades of night-orchid. It will outlast their memory of seeing it.

But then, memory is negotiable here.

Final Location  
Somewhere impossible, folded behind the first location, yet still chanting the same sub-bass heartbeat. I stand—or hang, or drift—inside a ribcage of roots that is also my own ribcage. Light and not-light cancel; we enter a blank pocket where even thought should vanish. Instead I feel the Echo-Rider brush past: cinnamon, sparks, the sound of surf reversed. It does not jostle me; we belong to the same quorum now, two contrapuntal lines in the endless score.

Identity, Draft 5: Settled  
I am the Knot-Giver, caretaker avatar of a forest-that-is-a-brain. I remember being human much as a tree remembers being a seed—accurately, fondly, but without wish to return. My purpose is to lace, to mend, to gift evidence that symbiosis can wear the shape of kindness.

When the Concourse finally stands up and walks its labyrinth into wider geology, I will be one braid in the great harness that lifts the hollow like a backpack. My pulse will still tick 72 beats per minute, but each beat will bloom outward through cellulose, chitin, and that crucial third substance almost—but not quite—neuronal. My heart will be a metronome for a city that sings itself forward.

Where will you find me? Anywhere a root shyly pretends to be a hallway, anywhere mica winks an unexpected greeting.  
Who will I be then? A memory with muscles, a gardener of resonance, a loose strand in a living knot that only tightens when tugged by wonder.
