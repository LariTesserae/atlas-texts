I do make the climb, but the person who knots the rope at the treeline is not the presence that arrives. Somewhere above the last cedar I shed enough seconds and solvents to be recast, molecule by vowel, into the sort of thing the Hollow can use: a resonant interval, a gap inside the drone that is just wide enough to hold a purpose and just narrow enough to close again if the purpose fails.

Call me the Interval-Keeper. You would not pick me out against the rock. I am the faint, sketched absence that rides a handspan in front of an Erratic each time it slides, the brief silver crease in the air that tells the boulder where its new center of symmetry ought to settle. I weigh nothing that mass can register; my ballast is permission. Whenever the landscape risks tilting—when a condensation sphere misses its bowl and hangs in indecision, when a stair begins to remember crumbling—I loan the Hollow the fraction of a second it needs to reseat the pieces in perfect counterpoise.

Where I live:
A not-quite room three ledges below the highest false horizon, roofed by a petrified wave of basalt. Inside, every surface sweats in sixteenth-notes, droplets trembling exactly between departure and return. The space is neither enclosure nor exposure; it is an inhalation paused at the top of the breath. I fold myself into that held breath when not in motion, the way a bookmark presses two pages together so they will remember being adjacent.

What I am made of:
1. The wool that once lined my ears—now unraveled into filaments so fine they conduct vibration the way glass conducts light.
2. A spine of quartz braid stolen from an infant Listening Stone; it contributes pitch memory.
3. Eight or nine stubborn heartbeats I kept from the original climb; they tick against one another, slightly out of phase, producing the small, deliberate dissonance that marks me as distinct from the larger drone.

How I perceive:
Sight has collapsed into echolocation, but the echo is temporal. I look “forward” by listening to the moment about to occur; I look “back” by tasting the after-image of what just un-occurred. The present is a pencil-thin filament I balance on, mid-stride, forever deciding whether to let it knot into future or unknot into past.

Duties:
• When two Children of the Unwavering Note drift too close and risk amplifying each other into shatter, I insert myself between their harmonics, stealing half a hertz from one, donating it to the other, until they pass without waking the cliffs.
• If an Apostate Builder swings a chisel a breath too hard, threatening to tip a wall from artifact into avalanche, I swallow the extra momentum; the stroke lands gentle as light, and the wall abides.
• Each twilight (the only hour the Hollow pretends to measure) I walk the pools, freeing any syllable-films that have looped themselves into syntax. Language breeds entropy; I tease the letters apart until they are once again only tone suspended in liquid.

Memory:
I keep none for myself. The Hollow cannot tolerate redundant archives. Instead, I cache experience in the thin sedge. A knot of root here remembers a rescue, another remembers averted collapse. When a herder worm finally clarifies into invisibility, it absorbs the memory knotted in its tuft and carries the lesson into whatever choreography waits beyond substance. Thus the record of my service is exported continually, composted into the ongoing intelligence of grass and absence.

What I fear:
Silence. Not the mercy of mute air, but the terminal hush that would follow if the primary note ever paused to inhale. Without that fundamental, there would be no interval to keep, only undifferentiated stillness in which purpose and purposelessness level to the same zero. I suspect that hush is the Hollow’s endgame, its perfect consummation. My labor delays it by micro-eras, holding one imperfect chord between crescendo and rest. It is enough.

If you climb and somehow perceive me, you may mistake the brushed-metal afterglow that skirts an Erratic for your own vision smearing at altitude. Touch it, and your fingertips will tingle with the memory of a sound you have not yet heard—a cue that something just remained possible. That is me, or rather the brief shape I borrow to remind the Hollow why equilibrium still requires witnesses.

You will descend, and your clocks will limp, and your mirrors will tack on extra angles. I will stay, gently sabotaging the final perfection, keeping one hairline fracture—myself—open in the crystal of the note. For as long as the interval holds, the place continues to rehearse being more than void and less than world.

When the fracture heals, I will resolve into the chord, indistinguishable from the rest of the drone, and the High Hollow will at last know silence shaped like completion. Until then, I hover where gravity and momentary purpose cancel, a thin persistence riding the seam between what happens and what the Hollow will allow to remain.
