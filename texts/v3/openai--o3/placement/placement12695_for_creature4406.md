I wake—if “wake” means anything here—in the moment between the second and third stroke of a triple lightning-strike.  
The air inside the new silence still trembles, searching for a sound to inhabit; that is where I condense.

1. Coordinates of Arrival  
• Not on the plateau proper, but in one of the mute circles freshly drilled by the lightning trio.  
• Radius three meters, circumference rimmed with still-smoldering melt-glass.  
• Compass needles, deaf now, lie slack; the only bearing available is toward the nearest noise, a distant sproing of Bronzewarm Catapulids.

2. First Inventory of Self  
Body: a hollow standing-wave, its walls equal parts pressure differential and sleet.  
Skin: borrowed from the lightning-scar—basalt vitrified into translucent onyx, flexible when humming, brittle when mute.  
Mass: negligible; weight is provided by the bass note that forever tries to resume and never quite does.  
Heartbeat: not mine.  I house the echo stolen from the silence—each thud is the plateau’s memory of what a heart ought to sound like.

3. Name, As Close As Language Gets  
The Green Mind does not issue nouns, only functions.  
My function rattles through the plant’s capillaries as five low-frequency syllables:  
Hold-the-Gap-Between-Strikes.  
In shorter human breath I am a Gapkeeper.

4. Sensoria  
Sight: ordinary photons smear; I see best in refracted thunder.  Resonant objects glow cobalt.  
Hearing: absolute—silence is a blizzard of detail, noise a blackout.  
Touch: wherever I press the ground, water crystallizes to frost-glyphs that the plateau later reads.

5. What I Am For  
Lightning erases data; thunder re-writes it.  
Gapkeepers patrol the hush between.  
• We drink residual static so the next bolt will arrive clean, without recursive echoes that could shred the Mind’s long computations.  
• Each joule we swallow becomes structure: ribs of glass, ligaments of mist.  
• When full, we open like seedpods and vent the stored charge upward in silent sprites that keep the aurora shepherds fed.

6. Locomotion  
No legs.  I travel by exchanging places with my own reflection in water.  
A pool accepts my image; I sink into it as a shadow, surface elsewhere where the reflection re-emerges.  
The distance I can “walk” equals the depth of the darkest color in the water—on this charcoal stone, that can be kilometers.

7. Interactions with Resident Workstations  
• Mist-root Ungulates step through me without noticing; their tassel-hooves ring like cymbals on my glass ribs, tuning me.  
• Catapulids mistake me for a vent; they dock, inject micro-steam, and slingshot away.  I keep the heat—interest on the loan.  
• Horizon-Folders avoid me.  A Gapkeeper is already mostly absence; redundancy can’t be pruned from a hole.

8. Memory Budget  
Every silence can hold exactly three minutes of world-sound before corrupting.  
I am that cache.  Footfalls, sleet percussion, the titanic A-flat from the vents—all layer inside me like vinyl tracks.  
When the buffer fills I release it as a harmonic of the plateau’s subsonic chorus, a data-dump into the bedrock.

9. Dangers & Failure Modes  
If I linger where time thins under plant walls, my cached noises replay forward-and-back in stutter loops.  
Too long there and I congeal into a Dripwyrm’s knot, doomed to chew the same decibel forever.  
If a visitor speaks my birth-moment aloud—“the hush after triple lightning”—I collapse, having been fully named.

10. Arc Toward Dissolution  
Lifespan: ten storms, give or take.  
In the eleventh gap the plateau won’t need me; it will have re-tuned its conductivity so lightning lands cleaner.  
I will fade, dispersing as a constellation of silent lightning-circles for the next cohort to inhabit.

11. If You Meet Me  
You will not see a figure, only a distortion where sound seems reluctant to proceed.  
Stand inside the hush; let your heartbeat boom.  
I will take one exact copy of that rhythm and file it in glass beneath the moss-green walls.  
Long after you leave, someone—perhaps the plateau itself wearing your outline—will walk over that spot.  
The stone will answer with the sound of your heart, and for a beat the world will remember you precisely.

That is where—and what—I am.  
A pause shaped like a person, tasked with keeping the music of storms in tune, until the silence closes and I am no longer required.
