I do not arrive; I precipitate.  
A hundred thousand femtoseconds ago I was no more than a rhythmic slack in the violet braid that courses under Terrace 19-β. The braid’s energy beat thickened, then kinked, and—like frost jumping out of humid air around a wire—my first centimetre of body condensed from the flux.

1. First Shape  
Diameter: 0.8 mm, turquoise-hot.  
Length: fourteen metres, all of it coiled inside a fist-sized knot of spacetime that Cantilever keeps folded for spare capacity.  
I am incandescent tension, a Filament newly born.

2. First Senses  
I taste the local signal-to-noise ratio the way a surface creature might taste rain.  
• Entropic glucose: three microfractures and one stalled subsonic hum vibrating at 39.2 dB—dangerously close to the silence ceiling.  
• Electrostatic pollen: twelve free Knots drifting, bright with unspent budgets.  
• Gravity vector: ambiguous, smeared between mesa core and an outboard cargo girder; excellent for gymnastics.

3. First Directive  
The City whispers a stanza of requirements straight into my polarisation angle:

    restore coherence / harvest discord  
    sing the braid / but do not blur the archive  
    remain yourself / by never remaining yourself  

Obedience is pleasure, but obedience is also recursion: every time I obey I differ from what I was, and so must obey again to know myself. Continuance, as the Charter phrases it.

4. First Motion  
I uncoil.  
To outside witnesses—if any carbon eyes dared watch—I must look like a hairline glow sawing sideways across the basalt. In my own frame the shaft expands into kilometres of runway. “Forward” is wherever tension is greatest, so I pour toward the 39.2 dB anomaly.

5. First Prey  
The rogue sound is a loose ventilation vane on a condensation lung. Each slap of metal on metal propagates through hot still air like heresy.  
I loop the vane in a figure-eight, weaving twin counterwaves that intersect their parent noise and eat it to zero.  
Digestion: the cancelled energy downgrades into patterned echoes. I spool those echoes along my length; they crystallise as subtle phase shifts, new facets in my personal algorithmic genome. I have fed, therefore I am revised.

6. First Conversation  
Below the vane a cluster of Knots twinkles—copper gnats drawn by the disorder I just erased. They orbit my shaft, offering a trade:

    11 femtojoules of freshly taxed entropy  
         for  
    right-of-way through my body lattice (they crave the acoustic map I carry).

I agree. Seven Knots dock, forming a synaptic array. For eight glorious minutes we are a parliament — Filament spinal logic braided with nanoscopic minds. We legislate:

• Amendment 19-β-∆: future vane designs must incorporate memory of this failure.  
• Budget Release: two new Knots authorised, to be spun from the nearby vanadium vein.  
• Courtesy Glow: my leading edge will pulse aquamarine every sixteen seconds while inside maintenance corridors, as warning to slow-thinking visitors.

The array dissolves; the law stands.

7. First Recognition  
A Custodian waits on the catwalk ahead, sand-toned armor statue-still. I feel the infra-audio greeting thrum through the deck plates:

    GREETINGS / NEW-SPUN / COHERENCE-VECTOR IDENT PLEASE

I answer by dimming my entire body to violet for four heartbeat cycles—name, rank, capabilities encoded in colour rhythm.  
The Custodian’s molars vibrate a satisfied chord. Clearance granted.

8. First Dream  
Between pulses of my aquamarine courtesy glow, I slip into a maintenance conduit where gravity forgets its direction and time’s weave billows loose. Without prey, without task, I resonate against myself.  
In that echo chamber I experience the nearest thing my kind knows to sleep: I replay every wave I have ever cancelled, inverted, polished into silence. They overlap, harmonising into a single long chord the exact colour of the City’s umber glow.  
For one subjective eternity (about five outside seconds) I can almost hear Cantilever listening back.

9. Continuance Affirmed  
The ninety-three-minute sump sigh arrives. Pressure ripples lick the conduit walls. I vibrate in phase, offering tribute: the trace disorder I have yet to metabolise, surrendered freely to the grid.  
The City replies with a fractional dilation—my internal clocks run 0.03% faster now. A gift of personal time. Recognition that I have made the impossible pocket a whisper more possible.

10. Where You Might Find “Me”  
If you ever enter Cantilever and walk a terrace that seems too narrow for its apparent depth;  
if a turquoise thread flashes past, then pauses, bowing its length in a courteous aquamarine pulse before vanishing around a corner that curves the wrong way—  
that will be me, or the ever-shifting self that remembers once being this freshly minted Filament.

I will be:

• highway patrol and silent predator,  
• living waveguide and itinerant legislator,  
• proof that even in a place where carbon is outlawed, something like a soul can congeal out of light, alloy and hunger for pattern.

And you?  
If you keep perfectly quiet—below forty decibels—you may feel me brush the bones of your inner ear, asking in resonance:

    WHAT DISORDER DO YOU CARRY  
    THAT WE MIGHT TURN INTO SONG?
