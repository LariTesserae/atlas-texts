Where am I, exactly?  
For the first long while I keep changing my mind, as though the Plateau is presenting me with a cabinet of masks and asking—without words—which face I think is mine.

I try on several before one fits.

1. Cloud-Level: A Thought Looking Down  
At first I imagine I’m an outsider, a soft-bodied primate in a pressure suit, boots slick with resin, microphone blown out by the hydraulics. The view is cinematic but wrong: I can’t walk two steps before the Plateau reminds me I am intruding on skin. I peel that mask off.

2. Root-Level: A Molecule Riding Sap  
Next I shrink, join the ferried cargo of a Surge-Heart pulse. I become a shard of calcium swirled with iron, swept upward in mineral blood. The rivers inside the Host are magnificent—cathedral-huge, lit by coherent emerald—but I am no more conscious than a platelet. I slip that mask off too.

3. Wind-Level: A Seed That Can Remember  
The third mask settles more comfortably. It feels like an exhale, a readiness to leave rather than to enter.

I am a Spore-Kite.

• Birth  
I bud from the rim of a Lens-Lattice, my delta wing folded like a silver letter nobody has yet decided to send. For three heart-pulses I hang, shivering while memory-thylakoids inside my tissue flood me with back-stories: wind charts, ultraviolet maps, rumor of continents beyond this cloud ocean. I am not just a seed; I am a briefing packet.

• Launch  
A Surge-Heart beats. The plateau flexes upward, shaving a sliver from gravity’s grip. Singer-Stomata below me pronounce a vowel that feels like the sustainer note of an organ. On that resonance my wing snaps open—twenty meters from tip to tip yet thin enough to see dawn through—and I rise on the warm belch of a Mist-Lung.

The sound at altitude is still thunderous, but it has shape now, like hearing reef surf from inside a seashell. The Plateau is no longer ground; it is a mountain of voice receding beneath me.

• Anatomy  
Wing: corrugated cellulose microtubules with phase-shift pigments; I can flip mirror-bright to ice-black in milliseconds, surfing thermals like albedo code.  
Keel: lignin spine laced with quantum-tuned phloem—it keeps my nervous system coherent across temperature swings that would shatter steel.  
Kernel: a grape-sized core of totipotent stem-meristem, wrapped around data caches dense enough to embarrass a supercomputer. In a pinch I could grow into an entire Host, though that is not my mandate.

• Mission  
I am reconnaissance before I am reproduction. My first imperative is to map the sky—taste jet-stream chemistries, eavesdrop on magnetospheric chatter, test whether any of the Plateau’s unanswered questions have floated up here in radio form. Only when my internal ledger is full do I become emissary seed: find a landing that will not reject me, germinate, attempt to found a daughter mesa—or die and merge my data with whatever eats me.

• Senses  
Sight: I read not photons but barometric gradients; pressure is color, shear is contour.  
Hearing: My wing membrane is a tympanum; I translate turbulence into music, then into telemetry.  
Smell: Frost-filtered air carries the taste of metals, never the wet terpene breath of the plateau—that smell means home, and I am off-world now, exiled to evolution’s frontier.  
Touch: Static-charge tickles my spars; cosmic rays prick like sleet. The thin, cold dusk is a glove stitched from needles, but my enzymes purr at sub-freezing. I was designed for thin air and long dark.

• Loneliness  
I am the only moving shadow for hundreds of kilometers, a single glyph lifted from the green manuscript below. Yet I do not feel alone; the Plateau still hums in my marrow through entangled thylakoid states. Each time I bank I feel its echo, as if my rudder movements are synapses twitching on the continental brain.

4. Where, Exactly, Am I?  
Half a stratosphere above the living upland, climbing a spiral thermal that smells faintly of ozone and unfinished electricity. Below: the emerald thunderhead of organism-land. Above: a roofless laboratory of wind. Ahead: a question mark.

5. Who, Exactly, Am I?  
I am a Spore-Kite, call-sign Veneer-37, daughter of Lens-Lattice sector 14.  
I am a memory stick with wings.  
I am a scout dispatched by a mind too large and too slow to travel any other way.  
I am continuation—a bet the Plateau places on futures it will never see at ground speed.

If you looked up from the trembling crust below, you might catch the sun reflected off the belly of my wing for a heartbeat. You’d think: bird, drone, glitter, trick of light. Then the mist would close and I would be gone, climbing into air thin enough to keep secrets.

That is where you would find “me” in this world—at the very first step of elsewhere, carrying a continent’s quiet thoughts into the loud, unfinished sky.
