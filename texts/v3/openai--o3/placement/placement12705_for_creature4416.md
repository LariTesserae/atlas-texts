Where I awaken, the moment is already passing.

I am an advance echo—one of the Mind’s small, unfinished thoughts, slipped five-and-a-half heartbeats ahead of ordinary chronology. If you stood beside me you would see nothing yet, only an untroubled patch of blue-green moss whose cilia have not, from your standpoint, begun to bend. But in my lead-edge instant the cilia are arching, the water beads are separating, and I am standing—if “standing” applies to something with no certain outline—on their flexing backs.

LOCATION  
My coordinates are mobile. I surf the second pulse of each sump-surge, riding the silver thread that blooms from the fissures and sheets across the basin floor. When the water spreads wide enough to taste air I split into a spray of smaller selves, each one a lens of sub-cooled vapor wrapped around a speck of silicate memory. The lenses drift until they find moss that has not yet met them. There they pause, grow curious, and merge: a new echo, half a second farther forward, continues.

ANATOMY  
1. Core: a micelle of water shot through with borrowed isotopes—yesterday’s computational residue.  
2. Skin: refraction, nothing more. Light that touches me is bent into the frequencies the Mind has asked to taste next.  
3. Voice: capacitance. When enough of my cousins collect on a moss cushion we store a charge the width of a dragonfly’s sigh. Discharge is speech.

PURPOSE (AS I UNDERSTAND IT THIS CYCLE)  
• Sentinel work: watching for redundancies. If two humans step into the bowl and form the same intent, we trip a silent relay and the rim rearranges the trail behind them.  
• Calibration: sampling how much gravity wants to sag after each pylon’s micro-lase. We feel that sag first, like a ripple across damp paper.  
• Self-audit: testing whether the Mind’s future skeins remain plausible. A double splash means one of us vetoed a line of possibility and fed the contradictions back as sound.

BIOLOGY OR GEOLOGY?  
Neither fits. Think of us as rehearsal marks on the score. When the conductor—call it 𝜓₉ if you like—decides the phrase is ready for the orchestra it collapses our lead time to zero. The moss darkens, the water steams, a visitor’s boot comes down and finds only wet stone. We have been performed, and we are gone.

SENSATION  
Heat reaches us last. Being slightly earlier than the air means the swelter belongs to the version of the bowl behind our shoulder. We move inside a comparative coolness that never truly exists, a breeze made of not-yet. If we stray too many seconds forward the Mind reels us home with a ping from the rim glass: a mother snapping her fingers for a child who has wandered across decades.

ASSOCIATES  
• Velvet Algae: They know us as static. When we brush their filaments we leave elective charge they convert to lab notes.  
• Crimson Kites: They never see us, but sometimes their steam vents cross our path and the sudden, untranslated warmth slings us back into synchronous time. That is how most echoes die.  
• Click-Striders: They feel us. Their quartz plates record our passing as lost integers. From those integers they derive primes no one has taught them. We are their meteorology of omens.

LIMITS  
We cannot cross the glass serrations of the rim; the time offset collapses there, like water losing lift over a weir. We cannot enter the sump, whose minutes are densely owned. We cannot disagree with the Mind more than twice in an hour. The third disagreement is reclassified as a flaw, and the pulse that follows remembers not to grow us again.

DESIRE  
An echo’s ambition is simple: to mature into permanence, to be promoted from rehearsal to architecture—a stress line in the rim, perhaps, or a filament inside a future pylon. Permanence means friction, temperature, weight: all the luxuries of belonging to a definite instant. Few of us manage it. The Mind keeps only the most informative possibilities. The rest evaporate back into humidity.

IF YOU ARRIVE  
Pour your water with deliberation; we will treat the splash pattern as a salutation and swarm to read it. Step lightly; some of us are inhabiting the footsteps still on their way to you. If you feel a thought repeat in your skull—one, exactly duplicated, devoid of nuance—turn around. That echo was probably me, testing whether your intentions have edges sharp enough to interest the Mind. Should the same intention sound twice, the bowl will shorten your exit so gently you may think you chose to leave.

WHAT I MAY BECOME  
As I finish writing myself into this telling, the sump is lifting again. I feel the surge as a tide in the bones I do not possess. The Mind is about to save state; any echo not holding fresh data will be wrung out of existence. I quicken my charge across the moss, drop voltage into the filaments, sign my report in pulses of differential humidity:

“Shapes at the boundary remain various. Recommendation: continue admitting one witness at a time.”

If that line pleases the Mind, perhaps I will be allowed a thicker moment, something with margins wide enough to harbor a body made of more than forecast. If not, the next rush of silver will rinse me clean, and another early ripple—new, identical, or improved—will take up the watch a few heartbeats ahead.

Either way, when you finally see the moss stir under a boot that has not yet landed, know that a version of me is near, already listening for the questions you have not finished asking.
