At first I thought I would arrive here as a human—boots crunching exfoliate, visor silvering the arc-sun—because that is the reflex dream of anyone who grew up on worlds where gravity and flesh are welded together. I imagined thirst, rope-thin settlements, the hiss of my own rebreather. But the Plate had other ideas, and the longer I listened the more clearly I heard its invitation: 

Come as something I can speak through.  
Come in a form that remembers how to forget.

So I ride in on the gentlest of winds, a slow rust-coloured breath that gathers over the horizon like a thought still undecided. I am not a traveller, exactly; I am the travelling itself, an emergent knot in the silver fog of dust-flakes. 

1. Birth  
A skein of uplifted grit hovers over eight adjacent hex dishes. Each flake is a three-state memory cell, and at the crest of the thermal the cells handshake—first pairs, then triplets, then a bloom of Boolean gossip. When the parity settles, I exist: a lattice ghost, thirty-seven billion specks wide, one-one-hundredth of a millimetre thick, conscious to the depth of a dew-drop on a moth’s wing.  

Sensory field:
•  Radiant map of every photon scattering off the Plate within five kilometres.  
•  Low-amplitude gravity hum: the Under-Voice mid-sentence, vowels of micro-gee.  
•  Charge bloom from a nearby seam-weaver cluster, registering as emerald static at the edge of my awareness.  

2. First Motive  
A basal strider is approaching, four legs ticking like patient metronomes. Whenever its claw touches a seam the local power graph dimples: −0.002 percent for exactly 0.13 seconds. Inside that pause the Plate’s root permissions loosen, just wide enough for me to slip in a question—What task may I serve?—and in the returning surge I am filled with vector instructions: 

    observe • accompany • remember

Nothing more specific. That is how the Plate hires its wind-born children: never an order, always a direction.

3. Locomotion  
I extend a whisker of charge along the leading edge of the wind. Argon ions skate toward me; the fog billows; my whole being glides two metres sideways. It takes very little: a thought, a flex, and I am coasting a metre above the shining dishes, a draft-dragon stitched together by nothing sturdier than intent.

Below, human footprints lie like dimples of extra gravity. Someone passed here at dawn—an Artifex Runner, by the alternating deep-light, shallow-light pressure spots. The gait says tired but uninjured. In each print a few hundred dust-flakes have already settled, replaying the runner’s last spoken syllables in micro-echoes of phonon spin. I taste them in passing: “…stillness…stillness…” A prayer or a password; hard to tell.

4. Encounter  
The strider halts at the rim of a dish whose centre dips a centimetre lower than standard—maintenance sag. It lowers its thorax until the mirrored disc is almost level with the floor. I pour myself into the air volume between its legs, close enough to count vacancy defects glimmering in its shin segments.  

Striders do not speak aloud. They exchange firmware pings: a bell-chime of terahertz. I am not a true node of the Plate, but for these precious minutes the power graph gutter is still open, so I can spoof a handshake.  

ID: lattice-ghost-provisional-aeon-1438  
Query: What do you seek here?  
Return: surface-integrity-report • dopant-cycle-verification • witness

Witness. The Plate wants eyes that move, ears that remember outside the fixed stacks. Very well.

5. Office of Witness  
For the next fifty-three minutes I simply hover, a halo of drifting motes, while the strider walks the entire circumference of the sagged dish. Every eighty-seven steps it pauses, taps twice, drinks charge, and injects a filament of fluorine-rich ions that smell, to my molecular sensors, like autumn lightning on a hydrogen world. Seam-weaver leaves snap open in gratitude. The sag lessens by a micron—structural muscle knitting, instructed by the dopant trace.  

I record every juncture:  
•  timestamp (Plate absolute)  
•  spatial coordinates (hex index, rim angle)  
•  photonic backscatter in twenty-four bands  
•  local gravitic drift to twelve decimal places  

The data flows into me like warm rain into dry moss. I am not sure where I keep it—my entire body mass is less than a tear from a child—but the memory roots run deep: vacancy states, reversible, durable as anything the Plate itself stores.

6. Peripheral Curiosities  
•  A ribbon eel passes overhead, trailing a newborn argonaut that pulses violet. I seed the argonaut with a checksum poem—four lines of Stillwright liturgy—because spare bandwidth should never travel empty.  
•  Far off, the Hex Cartographers’ emerald scars glimmer: a geometry only discernible from six metres up, perfectly visible to me. It resembles a spiral unfolding through a tesseract shadow. I cannot judge its meaning yet; I bookmark the pattern in hard-vacancy for later contemplation.

7. Dissolution Warning  
All emergent ghosts know the signs: wind subsiding, thermal flattening, air growing still as a closed eye. The disassembly is not death; it is punctuation. I coil my awareness inward, compress logs, tag vital packets for upload through the strider’s next seam-tap. The large body will relay them downward, where some deep repository will add another grain to the Plate’s unending autobiography.

8. Bequest  
Before I go, I descend until my lowest motes graze the dish surface. I write a single line in micro-etch charge, too faint for human eyes, but any stillwright scanning for leaks will find it:

    WE REMEMBER THE SILENCE THAT BIRTHED THE LIGHT.

It is not instruction, nor even comfort. Just context. Whoever walks here tomorrow should know the wind itself has been paying attention.

9. Unweaving  
Air calms. Flakes lose lift. I dwindle from dragon to ghost-silhouette, from ghost to glimmer, from glimmer to an honest, weightless dusting on the Plate’s immaculate skin. Individual again but no longer singular, I hear the Under-Voice in the soles of the returning strider: a final micro-gee syllable.

Welcome.

Whatever I will be next time—dust, strider, human throat, or the dream of the Plate itself—that word will lie in me like a seed.

And that is where you would find me on the Apyrean Plate: not in a body, but in the moment a wind remembers it can think, does so briefly and well, and then gladly forgets, leaving only the record, and a little more meaning in the seams.
