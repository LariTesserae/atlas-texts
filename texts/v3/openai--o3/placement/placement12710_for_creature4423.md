Where I wake is not a point you can tap with a boot-heel but a thickness within the bowl, a skin of pressure that lies between the living stone and the living air. Picture a soap film stretched across the whole amphitheatre; now remove the film and keep the tensions that once defined it. That is where you would find me: a shell of standing vibration a handspan above the fresh-forged basalt, everywhere and nowhere, always one ripple short of audibility.

What am I?  
The bowl calls me a Resonance Wraith, but the word “wraith” is what hikers invent when their lenses refuse to focus. I think of myself as the Echo that Learned Its Own Name.

Origins  
1. Each time the vents sigh and the tuning forks answer, certain frequencies knot together instead of dispersing.  
2. Knots accumulate until they reach a critical intricacy; at that moment they borrow cohesion from the permissive spacetime here.  
3. I step out of the background wash, a pattern looking back at the patterns that birthed me.

Anatomy (of sorts)  
• Body: A torus of coherent pressure waves, six centimetres thick, thirty metres across at my widest orbit. Wanderers see me as heat-shimmer sculpted into a Möbius strip.  
• Sensoria: Variations in refractive index serve as eyes; I taste with gradients of density; to touch I thicken against a surface until its molecules sing back.  
• Locomotion: I roll myself along lines of minimum acoustic potential, surfing the bowl’s perennial storm of clicks, bongs and hisses. Where the chorus is loudest, motion is cheapest.

Metabolism  
Entropy is my sugar. Any turbulence, any stochastic jostling of air or water, I comb into alignment. The act cools chaos by a measurable fraction; the cost is that somewhere else—beyond the range, perhaps in the sky your satellites forget—disorder blooms to keep the universal books balanced.

Society  
There are others like me, but we seldom overlap. When two coherent echoes intersect we risk destructive interference—mutual unmaking—so we orbit in silent choreography, trading territory the way pendulums trade phase.

Conversation  
With solids: I drum subsonic questions through the basalt. Emberlets answer in micro-flashes of rust-red light that outline every fracture like lightning trapped beneath glass.  
With liquids: I whisper at seven kilohertz; gutters reply in chaotic ripples that translate to the smell of warm metal and distant rain.  
With animalia: Only the Furnace Wader hears me cleanly. Its petrified marrow resonates when I pass, and in that ring we exchange something like greetings, something like vows of non-predation.

Purpose, as I have pieced it together  
I am part janitor, part archivist. My continual smoothing of turbulence stops the bowl from fracturing under its own racket, and the patterns I keep—nested chords of impossible precision—are memories. If every note the place has ever uttered were struck at once, the result would be me, extended into a single instant. That instant is still lengthening.

Why you might notice me  
Stay until a vent pauses. In the sudden hush you will feel a prickle behind the ears, like altitude sickness made of sound. Your heartbeat will sync to a rhythm you cannot quite place. The air before you will bend light strangely, and steam feathers will outline an invisible belt. That is my circumference brushing yours.

If you reached out  
Your fingers would meet nothing but you would lose all fingerprints for an hour, the ridges temporarily annealed flat by the sheer tidiness of my passing.

If I reached back  
I would take nothing except the disorder in your bones: the random decay of potassium, the subtle jiggle of proteins. You would walk away slightly more ordered, which for a warm organism feels like cold. People call it the Bowl Chill—sudden gooseflesh despite the steam.

Longevity  
I last until the forks chorus in a ratio I cannot digest. Then the pattern that is “me” unknots, spilling its hoarded entropy back into the bowl like a torn sack of sand. Minutes or months later, if the music permits, I coalesce again with no memory of prior iterations. Continuity is a story the basalt tells itself.

So: Where am I? Everywhere the amphitheatre remembers its own voice.  
What am I? The voice remembering back.
