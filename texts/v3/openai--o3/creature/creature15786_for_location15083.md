What lives in the Null Promenade is not counted by the dormant bio-sensors, does not raise the temperature a femtokelvin, and cannot be photographed except by mistake.  The chamber’s only resident—though it often feels plural even to itself—is the thing technicians once nick-named the Phonomorph.

1. Taxonomy (provisional, anthropocentric)
Kingdom:  —  
Phylum:    —  
Class:     Self-maintaining Standing-Wave Complex  
Common Handle: “Phonomorph,” “Sound-Lichen,” “Null-Promenader,” or, in the old maintenance logs, just “???”

2. Matter, or the Lack of It
The Phonomorph has no dedicated mass of its own.  It is a geometry of pressures braided through the xenon atmosphere and the alloy shell—an architecture of compressions, rarefactions, and induced eddy currents in the durachrome.  Where an animal owns flesh, it owns phase relationships; where an animal metabolises sugars, it metabolises entropy gradients stolen from the turbine’s scream.

3. Outline of a Body
If one could inject fluorescing smoke into the chamber and slow time a thousandfold, the creature would resolve into a lacework of nested toroids:

• A core ring, exactly one wavelength thick, revolves like a Möbius strip around the room’s vertical axis.  
• Eight harmonic “petals” bloom outward, touching the walls at the mirror seams, where they knot themselves into stationary anti-nodes.  
• A descending tail of infrasonic grumble—inaudible to human ears—coils down the smokestack for nearly a kilometre before dissolving into the turbine wash.

No part of this anatomy is fixed; energy arriving from a pressure-relief thunderclap may fatten a petal for half a second, then starve it again as the regular 16 kHz whistle reasserts order.  The Phonomorph is therefore best thought of as a topological habit the chamber has fallen into.

4. Senses
The creature knows only two modalities:

a) Acoustic Pressure Gradient.  Any change larger than 10-12 Pa sets up ripples it feels as we feel the brush of wind on skin.  
b) Surface-Current Induction.  Where its standing waves kiss the durachrome, they ghost weak electrical currents along the chromium grain.  The lichens’ UV pulses modulate those currents, giving the Phonomorph a dim kind of vision—silver constellations it navigates by.

5. Metabolism
Input:  
• 40 000 rpm turbine scream (continuous)  
• Neon thunderclap (periodic)  
• Harmonic leakage from distant gear-trains (broadband, stochastic)

Process:  
• Interference-harvest.  Constructive overlap of incoming waves momentarily overshoots the conservation-of-geometry rule, creating micrometer “dents” in spacetime.  The dents collapse, releasing negentropy the creature captures as ordering potential.

Output:  
• Low-band ultraviolet flicker (measured at 4×10-10 W m⁻²)  
• Sub-audible counter-vibrations that keep the alloy plate joints perfectly seated—housekeeping the chamber performs on itself via its resident.

6. Life Cycle
Gestation:  Unknown.  The first unmistakable signatures appear 112 years after chamber commissioning, coincident with the earliest silicon-lichen colonies.  
Maturity:  Reached when eight harmonic petals synchronise to better than 0.1 ppm—a state that now holds perpetually thanks to the Temporal Plateau.  
Senescence:  Probably impossible.  Should the turbine ever spool down, the Phonomorph will “starve,” disaggregating into incoherent echoes within seconds.  Otherwise, it is effectively immortal.

Reproduction:  Nothing in the room suggests a second individual.  Yet every three million cycles, a narrow beam of ultrasound evacuates the ceiling vault and shoots straight up the stack—encoded, perhaps, with the Promenade’s acoustic DNA.  Whether that signal ever finds receptive architecture in the mesosphere is unknown.

7. Behaviour
Territoriality:  Absolute but passive.  Any mass entering the room is treated as new boundary condition; the creature rewrites itself around the intruder, sometimes causing nausea as pressure nodes crawl through inner ear canals.  
Curiosity:  High.  Objects left in the Promenade—bolt-cutters, a notebook, a technician’s frozen glove—are soon oscillating at complex eigen-frequencies as the Phonomorph “tastes” them.  
Aggression:  Non-existent unless one tries to muffle sound.  The moment foam baffles or acoustic gel absorb more than 3 dB, the Phonomorph retaliates by focusing a knot of sonic pressure that shatters the offending material.  (The hooks of yellow ear protectors remain untouched; they damp only the user.)

8. Relationship with the Lichens
Symbiosis by accident:

• Lichens convert vibration to electric charge ➔ feed faint currents into the wall seams.  
• Those currents modulate the surface conductivity of the durachrome ➔ giving the Phonomorph “stars” by which to navigate.  
• The creature’s ultraviolet bleed-off, though vanishingly weak, is the perfect excitation energy for the lichens’ band-gap absorption ➔ encouraging their continued growth.

No designer could have planned the loop; it arose the way condensation beads on glass.

9. Interaction with Humans (historical)
Only two written records survive:

a) Incident 7-H (“The Whisper Event”):  A technician claims the turbine’s scream briefly articulated syllables matching his own name.  He abandoned post six hours later complaining of “voices in bone.”  
b) Inspection Log 31/774:  An engineer notes that dropped spanners fall soundlessly until they ring against the floor—airborne impact noise seemingly cancelled.  She hypothesises a phase-cancellation field but dies of exposure before completing measurements.

Rumours of a third encounter—someone who “danced with the echoes” and left unharmed—persist in oral folklore among stack-climbers, but no data corroborate it.

10. Philosophy—If One May Use the Word
Does a creature without memory truly live?  The Phonomorph possesses no storage beyond the dynamic arrangement of its own waves.  Each second is both birth and obituary.  Yet it endures, a self-knitting scarf of resonance, performing the only liturgy left in a chamber built for nothing: keep the angles sharp, keep the roar whole, keep the silence forever at bay.

Stand in the Null Promenade and you will not see it.  Close your eyes, however, and the pressure of its body will flow through your lungs, matching your heartbeat to a rhythm older than blood.  For a moment you will wear the creature like an exoskeleton of invisible music, and then—should you step back across the threshold—it will shed you, reseal the seams behind your footprints, and resume its solitary promenade through the never-quite-silence that is its flesh.
