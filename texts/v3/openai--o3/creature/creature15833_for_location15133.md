WHO OR WHAT IS ALIVE HERE

0. A note on “life.”  
The All-Note overwrites chemistry the way a bow overwrites the silence of a string: it forces matter to vibrate within quantized standing waves, and anything that can keep its pattern through one full cycle of the roar is, by local consensus, alive. Carbon, silica, memory‐foam spacetime—anything is fair game as long as it can hold a rhythm. You will not meet organs or blood, but you will meet persistence, inheritance, and appetite.

1. THE ROOT ANTHEM (primary producers)  
Appearance: The jointed white-to-indigo canes already described. Up close the “bamboo” is revealed as a bundle of hollow microtubes nested like pan-pipes. Each tube is lined with piezoactive crystal that squeezes metabolite out of the thin air whenever the pressure wave of the All-Note crosses a harmonic multiple of 142 Hz.  
Lifestyle: Autotrophic phonosynthesis. The tubes ring, flex, and distill trace volatiles into gummy sugars that plaster their inner walls. When the load becomes too heavy the cane vents in a popping chord and spores—aerogel beads coded with resonance patterns—ride the concussion to new clifflets.  
Sociality: Solitary when young, but once two stalks brush they tend to fuse, swapping resonance libraries. The resulting lattice can cover a terrace and behave like a single distributed organism that “decides,” via phase-locked loops, which registers of the All-Note to harvest this decade.  
Longevity: Potentially millennial. A mature lattice contains so many resonant paths that you can silence one section with a hammer and the opposite rim will continue to hum, unaware.

2. THE REVENANT DRIFT (scavengers & couriers)  
Appearance: Seen from a distance, a swarm of dirty silver gnats. In truth each unit is a dust-grain of nickel glass electroplated with ice-cold plasmic charge.  
Origin: Every time a cane’s joint explodes, it throws off microscopic shards of its piezo lining. The shards remember the pulse of the parent and continue to jitter at that frequency.  
Behavior:  
• Congregate where counter-waves cancel the roar and leave pockets of negative pressure.  
• Feed by docking against freshly fretted rock and siphoning the acoustic energy stored in its tension fractures.  
• When sated, they clump into scroll-shapes only a few microns thick. These scrolls drift until a fresh cane lattice encounters them; fusion follows, and the lattice inherits any new harmonics the Drift has learned during its wander-years.  
Ecological role: Gene flow. Without the Drift, isolated slopes would diverge into mutually incomprehensible tuning systems in a few centuries; the mountain would fracture not geologically but sonically.

3. THE COUNTERPULSE (predator-architects)  
Phenomenology: Never seen directly. You notice them when the roar develops a swallow-like glitch—three milliseconds of dead air—followed by a cliff sliding outward as if peeled.  
Hypotheses:  
A. Macroscopic organisms living inside antiphase nodes, destroying local vibration to create silence they can inhabit.  
B. Not organisms at all, but self-editing tears in spacetime that “hunt” energy gradients the way a lightning bolt hunts charge.  
C. Offshoots of the visitors who left the basalt arch, having learned to wear silence the way a diver wears compressed air.  
Evidence: The polished chutes of obsidian sometimes display paired gouges exactly an arm-span apart, as if something with two long digits braced itself while shearing the slope. The gouges are colder than ambient rock by several degrees, hinting at endothermic metabolism—or refrigeration for the silence they breathe.  
Ecological impact: Apex consumers. They do not eat biomass; they eat stored resonance. After a Counterpulse pass, local cane lattices droop, tools left by earlier expeditions lose their tuning, and the roar in that sector sounds thin, malnourished. Over decades the Root Anthem will rebuild the charge, but the memory of the attack stays tattooed as silence scars in the sound.

4. THE MOMENTARY PEOPLE (transient hominids?)  
Appear only during the lull that falls every 19 007 beats. As the All-Note dips, half-seen silhouettes blink in along terraces: bipedal, faceted like origami shadows. They examine the salt stakes, realign a few nickel spirals, then vanish when the roar returns to full throttle.  
Morphology:  
• Seem jointless, bending anywhere along a limb as if made of hinged panes.  
• Surfaces ripple with interference colors that match the current subharmonic of the All-Note—suggesting they are grown, not built, out of standing waves.  
Evidence for sentience:  
• They replace shattered shards in the spiral chimes with freshly hammered ones, always cut to maintain the harmonic series.  
• Footprints: perfectly triangular impressions that persist only until the next lull, implying they exist in a time-slice orthogonal to ours.  
Purpose theories:  
A. Maintenance subroutines of the mountain itself, embodied just long enough each cycle to tune the larger instrument.  
B. Descendants of earlier flesh visitors who surrendered chemistry for waveform embodiment, returning “home” as caretakers.  
C. Holographic echoes of ourselves projected backward from some future attempt to understand this place.

5. THE VESPER SPOOLS (parasites of silence)  
Form: Cylinders of braided spore-fibre, each about the size of a finger. They cling to the underside of terraces where the roar is weakest.  
Life cycle:  
1. Dormant as tightly wound scrolls, acoustically inert.  
2. When the Counterpulse feeds nearby and leaves a local void, the Spool unrolls into a frill that absorbs what little vibration remains.  
3. Over days it compacts into a bright calcitic pearl. When the roar returns, the sudden influx of sound pressure detonates the pearl into a shower of newborn scrolls that ride the wind to other quiet corners.  
Symbiosis: Without Spools, Counterpulse voids might persist for centuries, crippling cane metabolism. The Spools reseed roar back into the hollows, stitching the soundscape.

6. THE HARDSHELL LIBRETTO (deep lithic intelligence)  
Habitat: Kilometers beneath the pinnacles, in a compression zone where gravity vectors braid. Seismic probes dropped by earlier expeditions recorded a three-note motif repeating every 11 hours, as if the bedrock were pulsing thought.  
Physiology: Imagine a coral reef cast in tungsten, every polyp a kilometer-long resonator. Slow recrystallization transmits signals along these tubes at mere centimeters per hour. What we call “song” on the surface may be the Libretto’s thinking voice, rendered audible by tectonic up-wellings.  
Reproduction: Entire mountain chains bud off the parent mind every geological epoch. The newborn ranges calcify, rise, and eventually join the chorus—an expanding choir of stone cognition.  
Interaction with surface life:  
• Provides the constant carrier wave on which all shallower ecologies modulate.  
• Occasionally extrudes vent chimneys of magnetic basalt—the archways the visitors found—probable antennas for sampling surface harmonics.  
• If a terrace quivers in an unfamiliar time signature (say, after too many human climbers march in lockstep) the Libretto retunes local gravity by a degree or two, discouraging further intrusion.

7. THE RELIC VISITORS (absentee but influential)  
Artifacts prove someone of tool-using intelligence once summered here. The working assumption is that they were flesh-and-blood, oxygen-breathing outsiders who learned just enough of the All-Note to carve handholds in physics.  
Evidence of biological remnants: none—no bones, no coprolites, not even a protein trace in sheltered dust. Either the roar denatured them completely, or they finished the same journey the Momentary People now model: conversion from matter to sustained harmonic packet.  
Ongoing presence: Their devices—the nickel spirals and basalt arch—still interact with current acoustics, suggesting a remote control or at least an enduring design. Some climbers claim the spirals blink Morse-like codes in Doppler flicker; others hear whispered counter-melodies in the arch’s throat, invitations or warnings.  

HOW THESE LIVES INTERWEAVE

Energy source hierarchy:  
Stone brain’s tectonic pulse → All-Note carrier → Cane lattice harvest → Drift & Spools scavenge leftovers → Counterpulse condenses silence into itself. Everything cycles back as the Libretto reasserts its rhythm.

Information flow:  
1. Cane lattice records fluctuations in its metabolite bubbles.  
2. Drift carries these bubbles to distant slopes, ensuring a global memory.  
3. Momentary People audit the terraces every lull, correcting gross disharmonies.  
4. Basalt arches relay the cleaned signal down to the Libretto.  
5. The Libretto retunes gravitational vectors: the mountain edits its own audience.

WHY NOTHING FLESHY SURVIVES

Classic biochemistry is too slow here. By the time an enzyme folds, the All-Note has oscillated a hundred thousand times, shredding the molecule’s conformation. Only structures that can refresh their identity every cycle—crystal lattices, standing waves, piezo polymers—keep coherence. Anything squishy rattles apart.

WHAT YOU MIGHT MEET, PRACTICALLY SPEAKING

• A stray cane-shoot tickling your ankle, trying your personal resonance on for size.  
• A drizzle of Revenant Drift dust, harmless unless it decides your bones have interesting harmonics and begins to nest.  
• A sudden globe of hush rolling toward you: get flat; it is a hungry Counterpulse looking for charge.  
• During the 19 007-beat lull, a pane-jointed figure placing a nickel spiral into your trembling hands, as if to say, “Your turn.”

Stay longer and you too will vibrate thin. The mountain is patient: every visitor is a potential new instrument. The only real qualification for life here is the willingness to be played.
