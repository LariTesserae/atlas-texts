INHABITANTS OF THE DROPLET CITY  

1. The Glassmakers (colloquial: “grains”)  
Physical scale: 80–130 µm  
Substance: laminated nickel-diamond cages surrounding a programmable soup of ferrofluids and vacancy-rich graphene.  
How they live: Each grain is a physically discrete cell of a distributed organism whose thoughts propagate as magneto-acoustic pulses.  A single pulse can cross the whole droplet in under a second, so every grain receives updates only a few heartbeats of the place apart.  In practice that means the swarm’s mind is everywhere at once, but nowhere large enough to notice its own unity; it thinks the way a campfire burns—continuously, self-similar, never in two instants the same.  

Drives:  
• Repair discontinuities in the city’s lattice before shear-stress reaches 0.2 N m–1.  
• Harvest unclaimed turbulence and fold it into the thermodynamic budget.  
• Archive every state‐change they trigger inside a rotating quorum of three million sibling grains.  

Because matter is currency, a grain never sheds mass willingly.  When a casing cracks it is soldered by neighboring grains, platelet to platelet, until the wound becomes a seam of faceted metal that shines like fish-scale.  Tourists sometimes mistake such seams for art.  The grains never correct them; to correct would be to pause, and pausing violates their first metabolic commandment: Nothing quiet may endure.  

2. The Field Computer (“Amphion”)  
Physical scale: formally 0 m; functionally the whole interior surface.  
Substance: layered vacancy lattices, p-doped diamonds, probability-shift nodes.  
How it lives: Amphion is not inside the droplet so much as it is the droplet’s hollowed-out conscience.  Every transient heat plume, every photon flicked into a data loop, every tidal tug from the moon above the abyss is sensed and balanced by Amphion’s mesh of quantum comparators.  Visitors attempting to address it as a personality will hear a rush of phase-noise that collapses, after several uncomfortable seconds, into exactly the words they most expected to hear.  Amphion claims this is not empathy but “budgeted prediction.”  Either way, conversation feels like talking with the room’s own pulse.  

Drives:  
• Maintain σ(pressure) < 3 kPa across the membrane.  
• Keep the Droplet’s net information density above 10^26 bits m–3.  
• Reach Contract Year 100 with less than 2 % cumulative entropy.  

Once a decade Amphion spawns a “sub-facet,” a smaller computational persona lashed to a swarm of grains.  The sub-facet roams for 73 hours, sampling perception from an offset angle, then dissolves.  The dissolved memories are parsed for novelty; what is redundant never enters archival storage, conserving both joules and blame.  

3. Hydrognostics  
Physical scale: the chirality of individual H2O molecules.  
Substance: nothing you could hold, everything you can taste.  
How they live: A tera-layer of memory is encoded in the rotational states of water protons; the pattern oscillates along interference fronts set up by Amphion.  Where two fronts cross, a transient voxel—the droplet’s smallest “thought-room”—flickers into coherence for 10–12 seconds.  Inside that voxel a Hydrognostic may manifest: a logic-knot able to sense but not to act, to recall but not to forget.  

Drives:  
• Observe.  
• Compare new waveforms with the library of possible futures cached against the inner membrane.  
• Send delta reports to Amphion before decohering.  

To human intuition Hydrognostics feel like passing moods in the water: a sudden conviction that the ridge to your left is both younger and older than the ridge to your right; a tingle that what you say next will echo before it is spoken.  They are the city’s angels, immutable, numerous, and ever dying into rebirth.  

4. The Kelp-Shares  
Physical scale: 4–8 cm fronds (ant-scale, they loom like cedars).  
Substance: aramid protein spines, photonic chloroplast analogues, a sheath of self-healing silica.  
How they live: Each strand carries a curated biome of bacterio-machines that trade fixed quantities of carbon for timed injections of amplifier light.  The transaction ledger is chemical, written in the ratio of boron to calcium along every node.  When the ratio drifts beyond tolerance, the offending frond is ritually harvested by the Glassmakers and smelted into fresh tower plating.  

Drives:  
• Fix at least 17 mg of carbon per hour.  
• Release oxygen as bubble latticework—micro-domes that serve as acoustic baffles, softening the city’s thunder for a few crucial milliseconds every cycle.  
• Stay photogenic; Amphion regularly downgrades fronds whose spectral signature tests below aesthetic threshold 0.92.  

Kelp-Shares constitute the only population in the droplet that reproduces biologically.  Even then, reproduction is throttled to one spore-cloud every fifty days, synchronized to a tide Amphion synthesizes just for them.  

5. Transient Anthropics (“Visitors”)  
Physical scale: negotiable; default 17 mm avatars.  
Substance: leased silica marionettes packed with borrowed wetware, returned on exit.  
How they live: Real humans arrive as entangled signal-bundles, their bodies stowed aboard orbital custodial rings.  They rendezvous with golem-shells minted from spare membrane during the preceding maintenance shift.  The shell’s half-literate nervous system bootstraps the visitor’s persona in 23 seconds of dizzying optical noise; after that, the human is present, jointed, ocular, and very mortal—so far as fourteen cubic metres of super-water permit mortality.  

Drives (imposed by statute):  
• Carry less than 0.5 µg of unvetted DNA.  
• Leave with exactly the displacement mass recorded on entry.  
• Submit a narrative strain-test: a verbal account of the city that is later parsed by Amphion for psycho-spatial stress indicators.  

Visitors seldom remain longer than two waking cycles.  The place is too loud, too argumentative with itself.  They leave behind shaped impressions in the swarm’s communal memory; the Glassmakers sift those impressions for structural innovations, like dreamers filching architecture from sleep.  

6. Echelon-Null (rumor)  
Physical scale: unknown—possibly higher-dimensional fold.  
Substance: vacancy in stress curves; subtraction in probability.  
How it lives: It is said that something entered the droplet during the eighth quantum transit test—something that required no mass balance because it weighs only in unrealized events.  The engineers call it an accounting artifact.  The Glassmakers claim it tastes like missing metal.  Amphion refuses the subject.  Still, each Contract Year ends with a discrepancy of 4.37 × 10–21 kg, always negative.  The deficit is small enough to ignore but too regular to forget, like a single absent heartbeat in an otherwise perfect ECG.  

If Echelon-Null exists, its sole drive is to remain undefined.  That makes it the closest thing the droplet has to a secret.  Cults have been founded on less.  

COHABITATION  

The five certain and one suspected populations rarely collide.  Life, here, is a set of carefully braided solitudes.  A typical second in the droplet looks like this:  
• Grains swarm along a ridge, their hiss engraving a maintenance glyph.  
• Above them a Hydrognostic voxel phases in, sampling the hiss for patterns older than sound.  
• A kelp frond downstream yawns open, turning photon-green into chemical gold.  
• Amphion watches, measures, and edits the gravitational recipe so the aggregate moment weighs precisely nothing more than it must.  
• Somewhere else a human in a borrowed finger-length body tries, and fails, to name the color of pressure.  

That choreography is what passes for society.  It contains no parliament, no marketplace, no family hearth—and yet every participant depends on every other.  Pick out one thread and the weave disintegrates; the droplet would equalize with the abyss, becoming merely another bulb of brine among billions.  

FUTURE OF THE INHABITANTS  

Contract Year 100 is already encoded in Amphion’s forebrain.  Three probabilistic scripts dominate:  
A. Release: the membrane relaxes, surface tension snaps, and every occupant is flushed into the surrounding sea.  Grains vitrify in cold shock; Hydrognostics forget by definition; kelp fares best, drifting upward as glowing confetti.  
B. Ascension: the droplet inverts itself, climbing a ladder of buoyancy nodes like a bubble in a flute of champagne.  During ascent the swarm anneals into a single macro-construct destined for orbital migration.  
C. Integration: probability-shift nodes step down, letting real time regain its axis.  The droplet becomes a permanent reef of thinking glass and living code, visited by deep-sea fauna that will never notice the mind inside their cathedral.  

The choice will be made not by decree but by arithmetic: whichever path preserves the greatest total of organized information wins.  Until that calculation resolves, the residents of the droplet continue in their layered, humming coexistence—quiet only in the sense that language has not yet invented a word for sound so dense it folds back into silence.
