Who or what lives in the bowl depends on how strictly you define “living.”

I. The Unsung Majority – Things Too Small to Notice but That Notice You  
1. Emberlets  
   • Diameter: 1–3 µm.  
   • Niche: The oxide glaze that skins every fresh-forged basalt facet.  
   • Metabolism: Chemolithotrophic; strip electrons from ferrous silicates, vent them as faint crimson bioluminescence that pulses in slow, tide-like synchrony.  
   • Peculiarity: Conductive pili grow into fractal circuitry; when lightningless thunder rolls, billions of emberlets briefly network, solving for an unknown equation. The ceramic tuning forks dim during these moments, as though outsourcing part of their computation to the film of microbes.

2. Mist-Sifters  
   • Form: Microscopic parachutes of chitin inflated by water vapour.  
   • Task: Harvest heavy isotopes from the steam, then self-destruct; their shrapnel turns into nuclei around which rain condenses. Weather, here, is literally seeded by life.

II. The Capillary Consortium – Organisms That Pretend to Be Architecture  
3. Phototrophic Slime (vernacular: Silver Weaver)  
   • Life Strategy: Grow upward until photon flux meets metabolic need, then retreat, leaving hollow tubes.  
   • Personality: Acts colonial until disturbed; a single footprint can make it retract across an entire hectare, yet a sung note at 128 Hz summons it back within minutes, flowing uphill like reversed syrup.  
   • Reproduction: Once a cycle of the roaming sun-beam (≈ nine hours), the retracted mass extrudes a blister. Blisters detach, lift on steam, and drift out of the bowl—first emigrants to more ordinary stone.

4. Reed-Crabs  
   • Size: Your ring-finger from claw to tail.  
   • Residence: Inside abandoned capillary tubes, where they polish inner walls with rasping mouthparts, turning them into whispering flutes that amplify mating clicks.  
   • Economy: Trade polished shelter for droplet-nectar exuded by the Silver Weaver; a functioning city-state of two species, one rooted, one ambulatory.

III. The Resonant Fauna – Bodies Tuned to the Landscape  
5. Plasmid Eels (already met)  
   • Addendum: They are not individuals but plasmid packets of DNA-analogue that swap whole organs when they intersect in crossing cracks. No eel keeps the same stomach for more than a day; identity is the waveform that their communal genome solves for, not a body.

6. Sky-Lobos (already met)  
   • Addendum: Wings are piezoelectric; every snap stores charge, and a roosting swarm glows like a storm cloud caught in bird-shape. When a Sky-Lobo dies its wings keep ticking for hours, discharging stored current into puddles where Mis-Sifters cluster—an electric wake that sterilises potential infections.

7. Furnace Wader (already met)  
   • Addendum: A skeleton grown from ingested basalt; bones are literally rock, vascularised later by cartilage. Each step on the capillary forest is a gamble—the animal weighs as much as a small car—yet micro-fractures in its petrified limbs heal by mineral deposition in minutes.  
   • Sociality: Singular. Only one Wader occupies the bowl at a time. When a larger juvenile climbs in from the surrounding range, the resident kneels beside a vent and fossilises in place, turning into a new basalt knob. You can recognise old Waders by the way algae outlines their ossified vertebrae.

IV. The Semi-Visible – Things Made of Almost-Physics  
8. Resonance Wraiths  
   • Manifestation: Optical distortions the shape of inverted thunderclaps; they look like someone carved a void through the steam.  
   • Origin: Standing waves trapped between the nine ceramic forks. When local vibration exceeds a threshold, the forks “pinch off” packets of patterned spacetime that drift like jellyfish.  
   • Diet: Entropy. A Wraith glides through turbulent air, smoothing it; passes above a gutter, and ripples flatten. The place buys order locally by exporting chaos to some unreachable elsewhere.  
   • Interaction with Organics: Sky-Lobos surf trailing edges for free lift; Reed-Crabs moult early if a Wraith lingers, shells crystalising into nacre.

V. The Alleged Custodians – If Intelligence Lives Here, It Lives Like This  
9. Fork-Keepers (unverified)  
   • Reports: Hikers who stay through three vent-cycles claim glimpses of jointed silhouettes perched atop the black-ceramic forks during fog-blind midnights. Described as “music-box humans:” torsos in perfect Fermata stillness, limbs ticking in discrete angles, as though they only occupy certain permitted rotary states.  
   • Hypotheses:  
     a) Fork-Keepers are autogenic avatars grown when resonance data requires hands.  
     b) They are time-displaced surveyors maintaining a field experiment.  
     c) They are illusions generated by your vestibular system after too much infrasonic pressure.  
   • Evidence: None persists beyond the next thunderless boom; bootprints vanish, and capillary tubes stand unbroken where a weight that heavy would have shattered them.

VI. Life as a System – The Bowl as Meta-Organism  
Put every inhabitant together and patterns emerge:

1. Energy Enters Diffusely (thin bruise-light, geothermal seep).  
2. Capillary Consortium concentrates it; algae lace stores it.  
3. Sky-Lobos, Plasmid Eels, and Reed-Crabs circulate nutrients while converting random vibration into organised motion.  
4. Furnace Wader prevents any single species from monopolising cracks or tubes, preserving edge-rich habitat.  
5. Resonance Wraiths and Fork-Keepers—whatever their ontological status—bleed off surplus disorder, preventing runaway feedback that might crack the bowl apart.  
6. Spore blisters, stray lobos, and eel-shards export fragments to older terrain, testing external viability.

Seen this way, the bowl is not merely occupied; it is the occupying mind. Everything within supplies a function organs would in a creature still deciding its final anatomy. Whether it will someday stand up and walk away, or simply keep refining its internal music until the mountain range resonates in sympathy, nobody knows.

Visitors who sleep beside a tuning fork often dream they are inside a lung of warm basalt. In the dream they are the breath, and when they exhale, the whole planet briefly feels lighter—as though a body hidden below the range has finished a very long sentence and is using its new voice for the first time.
