Brinewell Sump

DISTANCE & APPROACH  
Follow any ribbon-thin waterfall off the basalt upland.  Three kilometers below, the vapor your hand could once have scattered with a gesture condenses again as water, gathers with its kin, and discovers mass.  Where that reunion happens, inside a bubble hollowed in the mountains’ roots, begins the sump.

TERRAIN  
Imagine a sphere scooped out of stone, forty kilometers wide, lined in glassy basalt and half-filled with mercury-bright brine.  The floor curves up toward every horizon; walk far enough and you find yourself upside down, boots on the ceiling, head aimed at the lake that now hangs above you.  “Up” here is a polite suggestion, renegotiated with every step.

STONE  
The wall is basalt vitrified by pressure.  Stress fractures web it in filaments that trap the plateau’s lightning; each crack pulses neon-violet, a cardiogram of tectonic worry.

HYDROLOGY  
The brine is the plateau’s runoff, enriched until its density approaches gel.  Twice a day it breathes: a slow inhalation that lowers the surface a meter while vapor beads on the ceiling, then an exhalation that lifts the lake and rains the condensate back.  No wave ever breaks; the motion is inward, convective helices spiraling heat down into vents that glow turquoise in the dark.

LIGHT  
Sunlight never reaches here.  Illumination comes from lightning veins, from vent glow, and from echo-phosphor: every sound striking the water flashes cyan, weaker with each reflection, until hush restores night.

BIOLOGY  
• Mnemozoa – kilometre-long coils just under the surface, each vesicle printing a snapshot of the last sound it heard.  Lift a strand: it stiffens into glass, a fossilised sentence.  
• Salt-Tasters – ciliated disks that mob fresh droplets, accepting purity, rejecting impurity as hail.  What they approve dissolves them; approval is suicide.  
• Inclinates – knots of dense space riding the lightning veins.  They prey on momentum, swallowing motion here, releasing it there, shepherding the sump’s tidal breath.

ATMOSPHERIC SKIN  
A near-vacuum clings to the ceiling.  Into it drift the plateau’s spent Bronzewarm scarabs, reborn as copper kites that drink electricity until they ash to green patina snow.

SOUND  
Nothing louder than a speaking voice survives; shout, and the syllables shear away, fall as glass beads the Mnemozoa archive.  Conversation here is written rather than voiced.

TECHNIC OF THE PLACE  
Where the plateau manufactures, the sump warehouses.  Mineral surplus, stray thunder, redundancies of heat—all slide down capillaries to be sorted, logged, or annealed.  When stock is balanced, the sump exhales it upward as salt-scented night fog: the plateau’s dawn mist.

RULES  
1. Mass is opinion; only what wall and water both acknowledge owns weight.  
2. Reflection is ownership; mirror your face in the lake and the Mnemozoa may replicate it.  
3. Let three echoes of the same word meet and that word congeals into a bead; swallow the bead and the word is lost to your tongue.

WEATHER  
If the sump forgets to inhale, brine rises ten meters in minutes, lightning veins short, Inclinates panic, and globes of water cling to the ceiling for hours before raining themselves away.

WHAT THE EYE LEARNS  
Depth becomes spherical; floor and sky trade places without warning.  Dreams spiral, quieter each pass, like water seeking level.

WHO OR WHAT LIVES HERE  
Ledger-Guild – fog-humanoids strung with word-beads, patrolling until they melt at shift’s end.  
Shatter-Seal – an orca-sized sleeve of brine around fracture lines; it eats contradiction, leaving answers floating as clear globes.  
Recycled Travelers – half-dissolved plateau mannequins that stiffen, fill with brine, and become scaffolds for punctuation coral.

SYM BIOSIS  
Broken lightning becomes glass, broken words become beads, broken walkers become annotation.  Nothing dies unrecruited.

IF YOU STAY  
Salt rims your eyelids, glyphs etch the corneas, until leaving means silence—let the Mnemozoa drink your last sound, ride the sump’s exhalation, and return to the upland as glass-scented fog.

CLOSING  
Brinewell Sump is the plateau’s shadowed mouth—swallowing sound, cataloguing thunder, distilling what it keeps into liquid scripture.  Memory sinks here, but it is never allowed to drown.
