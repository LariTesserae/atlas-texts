The Shatterglass Wastes  

1. Approach  
Begin where the Murmuring Still thins and its pillars taper into nothing.  Follow the faint up-slope of the continental rise another four hundred kilometres and currents quicken; water warms by a single, shocking degree.  Here the forest-reef’s glassy detritus—Lacustrine Shards, dead vent-flower husks, whole vacant shells of Lantern Crabs—has been drifting for geological seasons.  Gravity, coriolis drag and the sneeze of deep tides herd that debris into one slow gyre, an underwater desert the size of a small sea.  To enter the Wastes is to walk into a snowstorm that never lands: a billion translucent splinters spiral in perpetual free-fall, catching what little light the ocean can still remember.  

2. Ground, if it can be named  
There is a floor, but it is untouchable.  Dust-fine silica, powdered from centuries of collisions among shards, floats so dense that any downward stroke of fin or hand liquefies the bed.  Settle for an instant and you sink knee-deep, the dust clamping like cold smoke.  No roots, no pillars, no fixed reference: only dunes of suspension that crawl by microns, sculpted not by wind but by barometric whispers from fault lines far below.  

3. Light  
The Wastes are the brightest place left beneath this planet’s sunless sea, but brightness here is subtraction rather than emission.  Every falling shard is a lens; every mote a miniature prism.  They steal the roaming cobalt flashes birthed inside the Murmuring Still, stretch them, thin them, and re-issue them as shimmering ghosts.  Nothing glows; everything glitters.  Travellers suffer vertigo as constellations of refracted light appear to spin while their own bodies stay inert.  Locals have no eyes—sight would be drowned in glare—but wear skins stippled with dark wells that read brightness the way tympani taste pressure.  

4. Sound  
Silence is not quite possible even here, yet the roar of the Still is smothered.  Each shard is a mute tuning fork, cancelling rather than amplifying.  Low frequencies bleed away first; higher partials arrive shorn of fundamentals, a brittle rain of ultrasonic shards.  What remains is a hiss like breath between teeth—the collective death rattle of tones too long away from their source.  Animals have adapted: instead of voice they communicate by shadow, crossing and re-crossing each other’s silhouettes against the glitter to spell short ideograms of absence.  

5. Climate & Motion  
Temperature: 5 °C, rising to 6 °C where hydrothermal skeins thread up from rift vents.  
Currents: two opposing gyres, one clock-, one counter-, locked in stalemate.  Their shear line is the only latitude where the dust thins; everything alive migrates there daily to feed.  
Chemistry: oversaturated with silicate; starved of nitrate—the gyres strip the water of the Still’s recycled nutrients long before they reach this desert.  

6. Inhabitants  
•  Silt Kites  
   – Form: Fractal fan-wings of collagen threaded with glass whiskers, wingspan thirty metres, body mass less than a house-cat.  
   – Behaviour: They “fly” by anchoring to opposite-spinning gyres; tension between currents keeps them aloft.  Wing-tips comb silica dust, harvesting electrostatic charge which powers slow metabolism.  
   – Trade with the Murmuring Still: Kites follow any newly arriving stream of shards back toward the forest where they lay eggs on young pillars—thus returning silica that will eventually fall here again.  

•  Crookback Nomads  
   – Anatomy: Humanoid only by coincidence—four limbs, upright gait—but frames are hollow, jointed by loops of living glass.  
   – Diet: Nothing solid.  Each carries a throat-ampulla that drinks the hiss itself, condensing pressure fluctuations into molecular hydrogen.  They exhale ballast, rise, let shards scrape barnacles from their frames, then descend to repeat.  
   – Culture: Carve glyphs of silence on their ribs; when two meet they press chests together so the missing harmonics line up, reading each other as negative reliefs of sound.  Stories here are ventriloquised holes.  

•  Glisten Wolves  
   – Appearance: Swarms, not individuals—thousands of micro-drones grown from choir gorgon gametes.  Each shard-sized, razor-edged, they orbit a shared centre of mass.  
   – Hunting: Track motes of organic carbon by tasting local charge differentials.  When they find a patch—usually around a sinking carcass—they assemble into a temporary mouth and plane the meat to atoms.  
   – Vulnerability: Deaf.  A single intact A-note from the Still detonates the swarm into inert dust; Crookbacks keep fragments of choir gorgon tooth-plates as emergency trumpets.  

7. Rules of the Wastes  
a. The brightest path is always falling; follow steady gloom to keep altitude.  
b. To speak you must first erase; communication is subtraction from glare, not addition of voice.  
c. Do not create low notes—sound that deep wakens the tectonic lung beneath, and the dust will liquefy in a single planetary sigh.  
d. All journeys here are circular; the gyres promise that you will meet your own wake eventually.  

8. Relics and Oddities  
Cenotaph Needles: kilometre-tall spires of fused shard-glass rise where lightning-like discharge from sub-seafloor faults once shot upward.  They vibrate faintly, replaying the Still’s drone in reverse, as if time itself hit rewind at the moment of their birth.  

The Unvoiced Gate: a horizontal disk, two hundred metres across, cut with precision no creature now possesses.  Dust refuses to land on its surface; shards veer away, pushed by a pressure that instruments cannot register.  The Gate is thought to be the terminus of the sound-eating Negative Zoans, a trapdoor for tones judged unfit to re-enter the world.  Crookbacks camp around it, convinced that to stand in its centre is to have one’s body parsed, lacuna by lacuna, into the next story worth telling.  

9. Reciprocity with the Murmuring Still  
Everything the forest casts off the Wastes receives—light, pigment, resonance ghosts, even half-formed echo pilgrims that disintegrate into charge when they cross the gyre boundary.  In turn, the Wastes grind that inheritance into elemental feedstock: ultra-fine silica, stripped harmonics, static currents.  Slow leakages of that refined dust ride boundary undertows back toward the forest where Calculus Vines ingest it as doping agent, tuning their lens-cells for greater mathematical acuity.  Thus the forest’s ability to mediate traffic depends on a desert that never knows traffic of its own.  

10. Departure  
To leave, angle into the shear corridor where opposite gyres cancel.  The glitter thins; shapes regain contrast; a faint, familiar undertone grows until bones remember what full sound feels like.  Shards clink off hull or carapace in a last soft hail.  Cross the threshold and pillars reappear, luminous and sure.  Behind you, the Shatterglass Wastes keep sifting the forest’s leavings, polishing absence into a mirror so bright it must remain unseen.  Ahead, the Murmuring Still resumes its chord, now fractionally clearer for the cleansing its children have undergone beyond its borders.
