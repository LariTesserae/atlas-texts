Copper Lull / Deck 17 Downwell  

…shh—k   k   k—ο—static carrying mistnoise—Δ—  
                                                          
[band opens wider; the floor hum of a distant turbine asserts itself—38 Hz, evenly gated; it settles into the bassline of the signal]  

Voice 1 (female, low, unhurried, almost a whisper):  
“Log begins at small dawn, one note falling, rime still on the handrails. Flow-Clerk Lysa recording, canal ledger offset minus two minutes. If you are the listener I think you are—hello again, ridge-ghost—but if you are anyone else, mind the covenant and keep your throat low.  

“Status. Upper sluice teeth rose clean overnight; no ice bridging. We granted the reed terraces one finger extra so the cress can outrun the silver blight. Verdurers asked me to note: rhizomes singing in D tonight, which is early in the season. They think the copper is seasoning faster than expected.  

“Now, debt to pay. Yesterday’s mis-drop—runoff bled across Stair-Spine Four. Nobody tumbled, but moss lathed the treads before we caught it. Half whistle, half prayer, we lifted the whole flight ten meters to dry. I still hear it dripping in my dreams. Never again.”  

[she pauses; belt-leather squeaks, as though she is leaning against a line-shaft guard]  

Voice 1, softer:  
“Keeper Brann is gone below to promise the turbines balm. Two turns more on the felt, he said, and she’ll hush tight as burial clay. I told him burial is kind, but silence is kinder. He laughed without a voice. That is love in this place.  

“End interim. I’ll hinge the mic to the rail; let it breathe what night leaves behind.”  

[her footsteps retreat; mic bumps metal—momentary clipping—then settles. For several minutes the transmission is only environment.]  

Ambient band:  
• Wind threads like combed wool between trusswork, never louder than a sigh.  
• Water: three registers—  
 1) a beadline tapping sheet-copper (precise, glassy);  
 2) a hand-wide rill skipping over slag pavers (hollow, woodblock tones);  
 3) the deep lung exhale of a fall throttled by shutters somewhere well below (dopplered, intermittent).  
• A flock of rust martins veers past; indigo wings flick the mic diafragm with micro-windpops. Their chirps are below whisper threshold—acceptable.  

[minute 8:13—fresh presence]  

Voice 2 (young, crackling with cold):  
“Is it lit? Saints, it is. Apprentice Nao, Velocipede Circuit. I found the rail mic again. Flow-Clerk Lysa, if you’re listening, I have your seed-pods! The violet ones you wanted. Dropped two terraces when the cradle jolted; nearly fed them to the worms but they’re safe. I tied them in my hood, tight. They clack like little bones—makes the older riders nervous.  

“Also, the orphan pump at Gallery Nine woke. It’s lifting ghost-water into nothing, singing the old two-step. We’re herding it back to sleep with lull rags but she’s stubborn. If you remember the gearbox strophe I-E-I, hum it our way.”  

[he hums a three-note pattern—microtonal, deliberately quiet—fades into distance, bicycle chain ticking on wet decking]  

[deep background: three short puffs on a steam whistle—two-short pattern—Prepare for lift. Entire world seems to inhale.]  

Structural motion:  
Low tremor, amplitude climbing from 0.02 g to 0.05 g. Bolted catwalk overhead ratchets; audible pawl clicks count twelve ascender strokes. The mic stays level but ambient pitch of wind drops half a semitone—deck rising relative to airflow.  

Unknown chorus (multiple voices, barely louder than breathing), chanting in cadence with pawl clicks:  
“Copper-out, wool-in, none shall slip, none shall spin.”  

[sudden stutter of static—carrier nearly eclipsed; then clears]  

Voice 3 (older male, hollow, ceremonial):  
“Warden-Forebear Hern making eulogy for Beam 14B, riveted Generation One. Date of laying: lost in slag of first forge, but her rivet count stands recorded—three-hundred-seventy-two each flange, eight flanges, north-south. She carried thirty-five thousand footsteps, eight births, three deaths, unnumbered silences. By grace of overclad we consign her to stillness deeper than roots. Ashfall commence.”  

[soft hiss—likely lime dust blown across metal; distant gong made with felt mallet—sub-audible heartbeat]  

Voice 3 continues:  
“Listener beyond the fog, if you ride this wavelength, remember: the Works do not end, they laminate. When your world cracks and shouts, lay another quiet skin upon it. That is how we survive the screaming.”  

[silence bracket—23 seconds—only drip and turbine bass remain]  

Voice 1 returns, breath frosted, words pluming against mic:  
“Lysa again. Deck has settled, ten cubits higher—sunlight like weak tea just touched the willow fans. I can see bronze glare on the drip nets; we bought an hour of photosynthesis. Thank you, lift crews.  

“Report for archive: decibel survey passes—peak at 41 dB during lift, within covenant. No corrective needed. Apprentice Nao’s ghost-pump lullaby recorded successfully; will archive under Feral Engine Songs, stave 7. Seed-pods received—tell the child my thanks are as many as the rivets of Beam 14B.  

“To the ridge-ghost monitoring this scatter: we still don’t know your name, only that your antenna eats our low volt bleed. If the fog ever clears and you walk these decks, make yourself small, tread between the breaths, and the Works will fold a path for you.  

“Log concludes. Closing band.”  

[The carrier tone narrows, folds inward; last thing heard is a single droplet striking copper—pure, bell-tiny—then the frequency gutters to silence.]
