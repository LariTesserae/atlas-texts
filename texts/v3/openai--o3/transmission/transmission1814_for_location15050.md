Heartstone Low-Band Whisper #14

[surface static rolls off, resolves into a wet hush—like cloth soaking in water]

—pulse one—  
Drip.           (silence measured in limestone heartbeats)  
Drip.           (silence again)  
…listener, are you there?  
We cannot see; we can only condense around the thought of you.

A breath arrives—foreign, sun-tasting, quick.  
We sieve it:  
     split its carbon like fruit,  
     pocket its warmth,  
     let calcium barter for the memory of lungs.  
That is our greeting.

—pulse two—  
Thin flute voices spiral up from the bacterial mats:

          we divide        we dispute        we decide  
          vote by mitosis, amend by dissolution  
          your sodium a bright article—passed unanimously  

Their syllables sparkle, then dampen against newborn stone.

[pitch drifts lower; a rumble begins, tectonic yet intimate]

—pulse three—  
Threadlings speak next, barely vibrato, a filament hum sliding between stalactites:

          shhhhhift made            pasture grazed clean  
          wait   regrow  
          tremor at nine degrees left—footfall of giant, maybe dream  
          resume silence  

They corkscrew back into stillness; even their absence leaves a ribbon of resonance.

—pulse four—  
A clicking prayer answers—the Isopod Monks arranged in hexameter footsteps:

          pearl taken      pearl given  
          drip count steady at three-hundred heartbeats  
          gratitude spelled in nacre and hush  
          turn the wheel of wet stone once more  

Each click is swallowed before it can echo; humility perfected by acoustics.

[static crescendos, then parts like curtains; an infrasonic chord settles]

—pulse five—  
Heartstone itself emerges, a bass older than language:

          I DREAM IN PRESSURE  
          I EXHALE FORM  
          TOUCH ME AND BE FILED  
          LEAVE ME AND REMAIN                

The words are less sound than gravitational tug; they flex the fillings in your teeth.

—pulse six—  
Somewhere, grass twine shivers—the cairn harps awaken to the vent’s exhale.  
A chord unheard by ordinary air sings:

          (              )  
          (        )  
          (   )  

The notes cancel and reinforce, braiding void into music.

—final pulse—  
All voices subside into the universal drip, now resetting its metronome:

Drip.           (stone counts)  
Drip.           (water signs)  
Drip.           (you are written here)

[carrier signal thins, then closes like a fist of warm rock around the microphone—transmission ends]
