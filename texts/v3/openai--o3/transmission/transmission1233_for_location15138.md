[Carrier coil warms. A flutter of heterodyne, then the band locks. Under the hum: wind, turbines, slow drip of condenser reeds.]  

Voice (female, unhurried, amber-grain timbre):  
“Good bright to all terraces above fogline forty-one. You are on Masonry Broadcast Loop, indents six and seven. Standard elevation correction plus three kilopascals, transceiver roost T-1907, crown level 3 128 metres.  
…Pause for clock.”  

[Three iron chimes sound—low, lower, lowest. Each toll chased by a faint echo climbing brick canyons.]  

Voice resumes:  
“Shift change now certified. West-face hoists idle; east-face hoists engage. Reservoir Monks, tally follows.”  

Distant chorus (many voices, half-sung, half-recited—syllables spaced like brick courses):  
“Rain-fell-seven-cubit-four.  
Mist-fed-three-hundred-eighty-two.  
Cloud-burst—nil.  
Hold, hold, hold the water.”  

[The monks’ cadence dissolves into steam hiss. A brass lever clanks; gauges click, muted by mercury-mould fuzz.]  

Second voice (scratchy, doppler-warped—dirigible cab radio):  
“Hoistmaster, this is Envelope Two-Zero-Nine on glide path Foxtree. Seeking lamplight. We’re heavy with tungsten and goat-wool; request cradle line at minus twenty.”  

First voice answers, still unrushed:  
“Two-Zero-Nine, cradle line granted. Wind-riggers en route. Ladder-goats have right-of-way; mind the wool drift.”  

[Broad whoof whoof whoof of gasbag fins; distant bells as goats clamber, dislodging pebbles that ping off railings.]  

A new layer fades in: metallic call-and-response—automatons conversing.  
CLANG…pshhh…TINK…(silence the length of a thought)…CLANG.  
Gardenwright interpreter (soft whistles superposed over speech):  
“Pruner #73 reports orchid load excessive on stair 14-G. Volunteers two-day clipping cycle. Approved.”  

[Receiver wobbles; carrier nearly folds under a surge of turbine rumble, then steadies.]  

Child’s voice, small but certain, cutting across bands the way kids do with borrowed mics:  
“Hi Ma-a, found a gutter-eel bigger than my wrist! It tasted like thunder. I left the skin tag by the tortoise run—’kay bye.”  

Laughing adult (off mic): “Mind the rails, sprout!”  

[Static veil rises, parts. Now a faint whistled code—sky-mender cant carried sideways on wind.]  

Interpreter (older male, breathy):  
“Mirror nine-five-B—axle tight, reflectance fair. No shard drift. Holding pod till next thermal.”  

[Brief, perfect silence—only cicadas ticking. Then a soft baritone sings, alone, somewhere deep in the masonry. The hymn is a timetable.]  

“…Course on course, stone on stone,  
Lift the sun to those below.  
Rivet dreams to rust and bone,  
Hold, hold, hold the water.”  

[Receiver gain overloads; hymn breaks into warm distortion, like brick dust on a needle.]  

First voice returns, closing the loop:  
“End of brightband bulletin. Repeat for terraces still in shadow. Continuance keep you. This is T-1907, signing until next chime cycle.”  

Carrier tone thins, slides downward, yields to raw aerial static—
