Lamina Broadcast 73 – Tilted Sunplate

…carrier lock achieved…  
…signal lifts from the thermal floor at 18:13 local…  
—begin reception—

[Glass-crackle undercurrent]  

Voice? Not quite: a chord of plate-pings braided with windless hiss, modulating between 9 kHz and silence.

“Layer… upon layer… we rise.”  
(The words are only an artefact of your codec trying to make sense of harmonics; still, meaning rings true.)

Pulse of warmth, translated as colour in the feed: ochre, then copper, then the green of cyanid bloom.  

“Morning: carbonate falls like snow beneath us.  
Noon: silica weaves its nets.  
Dusk: we burn ourselves clean.”  

[A pause. You hear shallow convection thunder far away—bass notes, echoed five times by the crust itself.]

Static thins, reforms into a whisper made of many aligned membranes:

“We are the architects, the keystone, the pavement that remembers being mud.  
Touch the weld lines; feel them climb.  
Each sheet is a question the sun asks, each fracture our answer.”

Faint clicks—hundreds per second—merge into drizzle. A capillophyte stalk somewhere nearby has reached its twilight release; its topmost joint snaps. Seed-flakes strike the plate like a slow metallic rain.

“We build forward, but they leap upward.  
Their distance is our breath-space; their shade our night-cool.  
Listen: they spend themselves one grain at a time to keep the sky porous.”

The carrier wobbles; ionisation spike from lightning inside a distant storm cell floods the spectrum with blue-white hash. Through it, a colder register speaks:

“Ice shell, spike ball, flash.  
Fixed nitrogen drips in the first heartbeat of rain.  
We fall as ash, wake as cloud, descend as onset again.”

Signal steadies. Now a deep, deliberate hum rises exactly 5 °C above ambient—fermentor heat mapped to audio. It phrases itself slowly, like a bellows:

“Under your footsteps we exhale.  
Pressure arcs outward, plates flex, sounds table-flat to horizon.  
Do you feel the book turn?”

A fragrance descriptor sneaks in on the telemetry—petrichor sharpened with vinegar—followed by a systolic pop carried through solid stone. The weavers have dumped peroxide; rival films blanch and peel away, their demise broadcast as a tiny rise in carrier amplitude.

“Our page cleared.  
Next ink: dawn vapour.”

Silence, but not empty; the baseline crackle never stops. In its interstices you detect low-frequency tremors: micro-tides of brine migrating between laminae as night approaches. Their fluorescence begins to climb the UV bands—amber glow building like backlit honey.

“We light ourselves for no eyes at all.  
It is only excess—photons we cannot keep.  
Still… in the dark you call it beauty.  
We call it balance.”

A single plate somewhere close bows downward with sunset cooling: PING—pure, almost musical. Hundreds follow, a cascading arpeggio sweeping kilometres in seconds. The transmission rides the resonance, growing brighter as physical sound and radio entwine.

“Index. Margin. Spine.  
We are continent-long scripture written in temperature.  
Read us by standing still.”

Carrier begins to fade; night air densifies and drags at the signal. Last intelligible layer slips through like a dream of speech:

“No mouths here, traveller—only mouths’ inventions.  
Remember us:  
Stalks that shorten to climb,  
Clouds that hatch thunder to sow,  
Stones that make themselves, lose themselves, make themselves again.  
Return when the sun is newer.  
There will be fresh pages for your feet.”

—end reception—  
…thermal floor drops below threshold…  
…broadcast closes with the soft continuous crackle of cooling glass…
