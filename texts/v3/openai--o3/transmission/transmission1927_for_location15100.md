[open carrier: a papery hiss like breath between cupped palms]  
…—come in, any ring within three ridges—this is Vessa-Between-Names, anchored above the Frost Nave, slit-ledge marker red-three-ivory. My gut-line spool is spent, bridge complete, and I am holding here until the mirror settles. Signal rides bare copper; expect drift.

[wind brushes the mic, light as turning pages]

Hour is mid-second, just before the noon blaze. Reporting for the craft ring of Knot-Navigation and for whomever the mountain lends an ear.

1. Rock state  
• New fissure opened during night thaw: one palm wide, five body-lengths deep. Edges still silver—young, hungry. Argent keloid beetles already halo the rim, tasting iron.  
• South wall murmured twice, low register—no drop yet. Chime serpents in the crack below me have fallen silent; their scales lie flat, no ring. I read this as arrested motion, not safety.

2. Life weave  
• Cloud-gnats are hatching in such density that my shadow makes rainbows. Their orbit songs form a lattice visible to naked eye—hexagons hung in air.  
• One horned skimmer perched on my bridge-line and sang through its hollow horns. Frequency nearly matches this carrier; you may hear interference.  
• A lace owl pair ghosted the east slit at false dawn. They pulled three conies from the ledge slides, clean work, no squeal. Feathers still drifting, each like a quiet thought.

3. Weather read  
• Sky thin as clear ice; no cloud, but the haze inside the Nave diffracts reflections. Mirror walkers strong today—my own duplicate hesitates half-stride behind me, then shows a safer foot. I have obeyed twice; pulse steady.  
• Menthol exhalation beginning—moss pores open. Air tastes like crushed pine needles and cold iron.

4. Human trace  
• Found a child’s practice handle lodged in moss at point red-three-ivory-two. The carved divots are fresh; apprentice from the Ravel-Cord ring, I think. I left it in place—future anchor.  
• My traverse ring is four bodies behind schedule. If you hear this, adjust route: the north-most slate plate has sheared; bring double gut line or circle widdershins.

5. Offer to the unfinished  
[voice drops, softer, almost sung]  
Stone younger-older-younger, lend us one more stable beat.  
Light keener-milder-keener, carve the safe arc of our feet.  
Name truer-looser-truer, hold until the bridge is done—  
then erase the proof of passing, leave us provisional, leave us none.

[carrier peaks, then crunch of shifting slate; distant boom rolls up the slot]

Personal note to any child listening on a borrowed coil: When your reflection arrives a heartbeat late, do not hurry it. Let the mirror walker land its feet; it knows a future stumble. Step after.

Closing spool. Gut line frays in sun; speech frays faster. I will coil silence where the words were, and leave it hanging for the next voice.

Vessa-Between-Names, clear.
