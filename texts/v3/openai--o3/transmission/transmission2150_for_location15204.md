[bed of steam-hiss, turbines turning at 0.42 heartbeats per second; harmonics resolve into a voice tuned low, copper-throated]

My name is Fel Trussdaughter, three full rustings old, spouse to this pump and caretaker of its sigh.

You hear them? The stone-eared always cut across my channel. Forgive—it is the nature of walls to gossip.

Visitor beyond vapor, perhaps you tune us out of curiosity, perhaps thirst for our distilled memory. Take this fragment with you:

Water remembers falling.  
Iron remembers flowing.  
We remember both, therefore we bend.

If your city dries and cracks, borrow our slowness. Lay copper on your tongue, count four between breaths, let moss choose your threshold. The treaty may yet extend.

I sign in rust and leaf—  
Fel Trussdaughter, breathing-engineer, wedded to Yarrow-4 until the next full rebuilding or my final exhale.
