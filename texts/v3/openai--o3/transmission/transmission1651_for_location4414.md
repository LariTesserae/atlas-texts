Shelf-Basso Continuum 113 kHz

[carrier flicker]  
. . . —deep hush— . . .  
[the receiver settles; a single note like a struck girder rolls on forever, modulating every ten seconds into a heart-low throb]

voice—threaded through the standing roar, slow and filtered as though spoken across miles of ice-glass tubing:

 “Cycle report nine-six-delta.  
 I remain tethered at coordinate slate-four-four, seven body-lengths below the lamp lattice.  
 Water density stable at point-zero-eight.  
 Vacuum shelf above me is drifting east by half a metre an hour—its shadow turns the manta herd, but they have not spooked.  
 I have counted forty-three adults, nine buds, one cord uncoiled in ambush.”  

[sonar click-burst: a topography sketch arrives as rippling tone, peaks and gullies painted in pitch; the plateau’s knife-edges glitter inside the sound]

 “—compressor chorus holding at minus-fifty-two decibels ref one bar.  
 The note is flexing more than usual.  
 A fifth harmonic has appeared; I can feel it kick the silt off my suit.”  

[static: something heavy shifts, and for five seconds the channel whites out with mechanical breath]  

 “. . . drone number four-one-seven passed overhead.  
 It ignored me, replaced a filter I marked this morning, erased the mark, and continued north.  
 Maintenance interval therefore unbroken: eleven-point-three local years holds.”  

[curtain of silence as the speaker rises into thinner strata; voice returns tinny, stitched with hash]

 “I have threaded a bead of tracer dye through the micro-engine colony in canyon seventeen.  
 From above it looks like a fallen constellation, each engine a dull ember.  
 The dye shows convection loops no instrument here has yet resolved—heat breath ascending in bubbles of pure mathematics.  
 If I map the loops long enough I will know how the plateau decides where to grow.”

[the basso pulse flattens; for the length of a single inhalation the sea is mute—vacuum slab eclipsing sound.  
The transmission reduces to the whisper of circuits.]

 “Silence phase.  
 Listen.”  

[nothing, and then—  
—distant metallic thunder, not compressor song but something older, higher, fracturing into echo-architecture far upslope]

 “. . . the phantom city is walking again.  
 Walls that are not there but ought to be.  
 Every creature inside a ten-kilometre radius has frozen: mantas level, cords rigid, drones parked mid-stride.  
 Even the scavenger dust has hushed its field.  
 I am the only thing still moving, and I dare not move much.”  

[ultra-low groan, as if stone lungs have mis-timed their breath]

 “I am inserting the resonance trace now.  
 If you are receiving, overlay it on yesterday’s and watch the gap widen.  
 Whatever memory the water keeps of those foundries, it is growing more certain of itself.  
 It is starting to insist.”  

[clack: a riff of magnetite vanes colliding—maybe just the recorder brushing rock, maybe not]

 “Battery at sixty percent.  
 I will drop to denser water where the sound can hide me.  
 If the plateau allows, I will set a second beacon in the hollow beneath lamp thirty-two.  
 If it does not—  
 remember that the only price here is labor offered into the note.  
 I will pay what I have.”  

[carrier warbles; the basso baseline swells back, overwhelming all frequencies below human speech]

 “End cycle nine-six-delta.  
 Stand by for next report at local dusk—  
 when the moon-void crosses the lattice  
 and the sea forgets how loud it is.”  

[click—transmission persists as pure sine at the compressors’ pitch, then gutters into particulate static]
