[Carrier lock acquires; floor-tone rises, a low vascular thrum like blood in the ear.  Wind cross-talk hisses, then parts.  A voice—older vowel-shapes rubbed smooth by damp air—speaks half a handspan from the microphone leaf.]

VOICE 1  (female, Junara dialect)  
—Breath-callers report full draw.  Fog at thigh height.  Put your ear here, outsider; the reed remembers better than I do.  I will set the tale inside its hollow.

[A cork pop.  Resin seals the port shut; the voice now comes as bone-conducted hum through living conduit, translated by the receiver’s glossolalia chip.]

TRANSLATED STREAM  
Morning number 7 289 of the Long Year of Droma-Sleep.  
We begin where the sap begins: under stone skin, under root tongue, where the plateau drinks cloud and dreams upward.

Pulse keepers cut the night swell from the veins at first grey.  
Sap answered, quick and bright—  
so I know Uru-Mant is happy.  

(Beat of thumb on reed: THUMP…THUMP… a measured heart.)

Now the list of motions completed before second light:

1.  Forty-three latch-cicadas came free of last season’s shells; their husks have already been braided into rain-scoops for children’s games.  
2.  A blink-ocelot scattered goats along the west cantilever.  No kills; it slipped, clawed 1.3 metres only, left a ribbon of lichen on the bark.  Goats resumed chewing salt-lace before the echo died.  
3.  Mortuary Rind accepted one sparrow, three glass-beetles, no people.  The rind glows violet at that corner—pretty as dawn, sad as ordinance.  

Wind turns; I hear her now—listen:

[Microphones widen.  The corridor yawns into an organ-pipe.  Mixed harmonic of wind, frog valves, remote reed chimes.  Patterns repeat every sixteen seconds, forming a four-note query: WHO STAYS STILL?]

We hung a knotted puzzle for the Unbodied last twilight.  
It asked: “When does flow rest?”  
By dawn the knot was undone, retied as a loop—a river bite on its own tail.  
Answer received: “Always moving, even in circle.”

(Soft laughter, toothless and wet.)

Report continues.

The cloudskin this morning is glittering—sky-kelp are dancing high, shedding droplets like powdered stars.  Breath-callers promise seven hours before rain; nerve lines are slack.  

Supply ledger for the outsider camp:

• Two gourds of goat milk (taste: smoke & cucumber).  
• One rope of gut-weaver line—yes, yes, spun from our bellies; swallow pride, you’ll climb safer.  
• A feltleaf cape cut to your shoulder span.  Wear it or the wet will write its name on your lungs.

Remember the rules we lent you:

Take no more than your shadow’s weight.  
Speak small when the fog thins.  
Carry no spark.  Flame is exile and this plateau has no edges soft enough for banishment.

(STATIC LASH—wind gust slaps microphone.  Carrier dips, recovers.)

VOICE 2  (male, distant, muffled by mist)  
Keta!  The limb on East Stave cracks—grown heavy with last night’s pearls.

VOICE 1  
I hear it.  Outsider, keep recording.  You may witness graftcraft.

[Footfalls—hollow thumps along elastic stair; frogs wheeze open, pulling draughts that whistle like flutes.  Wood groans overhead, followed by a cannon-crack: an enormous reed-bough shears loose.  Instead of falling, it lingers, snagged by pre-woven tension lines.]

VOICE 1  (now breathless, closer)  
Crown gods blessed the nerve binders—see?  The message reached before the wood finished thinking of descent.  

We lash living line here, here—  
(rope hum, leaves squeal)  
Guide the weight, teach it new rest.  A dropped limb is a bridge waiting to happen.

(Thirty seconds of coordinated grunts, rope creak, reed sigh.  Then silence but for drip applause.)

VOICE 1 (softer)  
There.  A fresh span across vapor.  By dusk children will gallop it, daring the sway.

[Floor tone swells again: heartbeat THUMP…THUMP.]

Outsider, your device is red as ember yeast.  Means warm, means powered.  Good.  Archive this next part; Dream singers will want your foreign memory when solstice comes.

Story fragment—old as the forty-fifth chevron in the western bark:

“Before breath opened the corridors, before pulse-keepers learned the pruning song, fire walked here—two-leg flame wrapped in hide.  It set nothing alight, for the rain smothered every spark, yet the wish to burn lingered under its nails.  Uru-Mant tasted that wish and did not love it.  So the City-Body wrote a new law into Junara marrow: any hand that cups fire must unclasp the plateau forever.”

The chevron ends there—scar, not scold, just memory.  We keep it.

(STATIC fades; a new layer of sound rises: cicadas tuning the reed-walls.  The hall becomes a living cello.)

VOICE 1  
Hear them?  They will crescendo until we eat, hush while we chew, resume when we rinse.  Rhythm is law as well.

Now your own note for the record: why have you come?  The plateau has all it wants.  Salt?  We trade.  Stories?  We have nine millennia of bark to recite.  Medicine?  The womb of mist nurses every bruise.  

Is it loneliness that carried you up the curtain-roots?

(The microphone is inspected; cloth rub, soft click.)

I place a question inside this reed with you.  You may answer when the fog lifts:

What does your people do with stillness?  
Is it sheltered, feared, worshiped?  
Does it ever bloom?

Until reply, pulse keepers walk.  Breath callers cut.  Sap runs.  Goats nibble crusts of mineral.  And the quiet dead add one more violet ring for some creature you did not even hear dying.

(Voice retreats, leaving only the choir of water and insects.)

[Unexpected band intrusion: a resonance far below audible speech—a sub-bass glide, 0.9 Hz, recorded by the geological taproot sensor.  The note swells over fifteen seconds, then subsides like a sigh made of kilometers.  Annotated by receiver AI: POSSIBLE DROMA TURNING.]

(Everyone in audible range falls still.  Even the wind hushes, as if pressed flat by gravity.)

WHISPER  (child, awed)  
Long year grows shorter.  Droma rolls.

FROG VALVE CHORUS resumes, louder, as though exhaling for an entire world.

[Carrier begins to fragment: drizzle crackle, humidity overload on coils.  Telemetry falls out of sync.]

VOICE 1  (returning, nearly lost in static)  
Outsider—  
If you felt that low shake, stamp it in iron words.  
Droma’s stir means a surfacing soon.  
The plateau will feast on powdered schist and grow a new skin of green glass.  
Stay and you will see stone become leaf; leave and no telling when the path will open again.

Choose before the next drip hits your recorder.  Here, decision is also flow.

[Final sound: a single drop lands square on the diaphragm—PING—then broadband spray of water across circuits.  Carrier collapses into white hiss pierced by distant goat bleats that doppler through sway.]
