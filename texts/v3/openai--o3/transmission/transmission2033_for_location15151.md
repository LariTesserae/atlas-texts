High Drown / Ridge-Horn Relay / Cycle 11.337

[carrier surge—white hiss layered by a low turbine hum]

(stone-drum heartbeat) THUMM … THUMM … THUMM  
(cable sings two octaves above) vvvvviiing—vvvviiing—  

Voice, close-mic, a woman’s; breath rasped by cold:  

“Signal rides the tenth spine, west-by-cold, from Water-Kept perch Three-Clasp. If you answer, crack the line twice and let the hum settle. … Silence. Good. I’ll keep tongue.”

[rattle of copper mirror tapped against mic]

“Report begins.

“Morning mist came thin; funnels filled slow but pure. Lantern run heavy—rift looks sewn with sparks. Took seventeen on hook-reed before first gull scream. Cub Kett dragged the catch to smoke hearth; he’s earning his tether loops.

“News of stone: the north face of Split-Whale bowed out at fourth bell. Thunder-readers put it at two hundred strides of ledge gone. No throats lost—rust-shepherds were already across the gap chasing oxide—but the lift spur is air now. Any load coming up the blue valley must swing south and pray the vine can take the arc. Mark your maps charcoal-black there; don’t trust tomorrow daylight to remember for you.

“News of sky: Ridge Wind plays polite today. Shear negligible. Cloud lid thin enough I count three suns in mist prisms. Might last half a watch more—dry your rope if you can call this dry.

“Machine rumor: I caught a Lift-Ghost whisper before dawn, clearer than I’ve heard in ten freezes. Five pops, long gap, three pops, short twin. I matched it against old tables: ‘CARRIAGE OUT OF PLACE.’ Carriage of what, ghosts never say. But they trailed it with a chord—modem ghosts sing, you know—and the chord mapped true to the drowned rail east of Rust-Arch Seven. If they’re lonely, they’re whining right under the old intake tower. I won’t chase it, but some of you data-hungry types might swing a coil and chat.

“Trade ledger: offering two lengths fresh grip-vine, fat as wrist, still slick with sugar. Want ground iron dust, fine as flour, to paint a new drum the bears grew for us by accident.”

[wind lance across mic—she turns away, voice dopplers]

“Hold—razor gull pass. … Gone.

“Personal petition: If this reaches Rust-crest kin (household once shared my dawn line before the quake split us), know that child Ember still glows. She hangs her mirror true, beats the stone in rhythm, waits your splice. Ledge-cats curl on her boots at night, confused that the rope goes nowhere. Send word—or a single drumbeat—if your line yet crosses breath.

“That is word from Three-Clasp. I drop a mirror-flash after this voice, in case you see but cannot hear.”

[short pause; a metallic wink you almost see]

“Water holds us. Rope defines us. Stone only borrows us. Step sure.

—Keeper Lais, spine-wrangler, sealed under ridge mark six.

Report ends.”

[three deliberate clicks]  
(stone-drum heartbeat fades)  
(turbine hush swells, then carrier dissolves into mist-static)
