Cantilever Beacon / Channel 5 – Wind-Counter Bandwidth  

[carrier hiss: a thin, metallic taste in the ear]  

—  …saa-hrr-ooo—  
(Tonic drone, 41 Hz, stone-resonant. A beat every nine heartbeats.)  

— wind is leaning, lean with it—  

[rush of up-canyon gust: the microphone diaphragm rattles like loose mica flakes]  

— First bowl reports ribbon heads bowing thirty-two grains west.  
Second bowl answers with one goat-bell, damped.  
Third bowl silent—ledge fall probable.  
Copy?  

[pause; only the organ-pipe howl, three notes, stacked a fourth apart]  

— Copy.  
Pilgrims on the line, keep the left wall.  
Sky-whiptail shadows at zenith ninety-three.  
Hold breath when they dive; they measure lungs.  

[sibilant flare—static blooms into distant thunder, held, folded back]  

— Counting wind:  
one-and-one-half knuckles cold,  
two hands high,  
pressure dropping like a plucked reed.  

(Background overtone rises: 512 Hz, 768 Hz, 1024 Hz — the Breath-Chamber is tuning itself.)  

— Grandmother Salt-Eye speaks:  

          stone inhaled us  
          we answer in vowels  
          keep the note long  
          give the note back  

(voice trails; replaced by a low whistle that circles the stereo field clockwise, then vanishes straight up)  

— Report dew:  
fern crowns sweating, yes;  
cup-pools half-moon full;  
mist-nets hung and trembling.  
We will drink.  

[chorus beetles ignite—chitin clatter shaped into a descending minor third, echoed by thousands]  

— Hear the beetles? Storm seedling.  
Tie down infants, furl the goats.  
(Cloud-kids already crouch, hooves on nothing.)  

— To whomever rides this frequency:  
the plateau forgets faces faster than stone flakes.  
Mark yourselves in breath if you would stay.  

[carrier dips—pressure spike—sudden silence, as if all air is stunned]  

— Vertical river forming east-by-sun-set.  
Silver wall, seven body-lengths wide, rising.  
Aerolith spine visible—facet count: eleven.  
Offerings deployed: milk, obsidian, two wing-books.  
Stand clear of suction.  

(clipped heartbeat of rain, inverted: droplets ascend in the monitors)  

— Parliament thrum peaking—feel it in the teeth—  
Hold tongues against molars, listen:  

          —krr-ahh—   —krr-ahh—  
          Petition received.  
          Ground lease extended one more turning.  

(static applause, like sand in a snare drum)  

[wind eases; a rare pocket of hush]  

— Night gate opening.  
Stars poured neat, no wind-mixer.  
Biolume sparks surfacing—blue snow, slow snow.  
We unroll sleeping skull-stones; lie flat; lend an ear.  

(silence, broken only by a single staggered breath, amplified in cave acoustics)  

— Final tally:  
thirty-three living,  
two returned to echo,  
one still unaccounted inside the fog-robe of a stag.  
We remember them.  

[low collective hum; a chord folds into itself until phase-cancelled, leaving black-band quiet]  

— Transmission closing on the shelter of the twelfth beat.  
If your receiver rides the canyon rim, whistle home.  
Stone is listening. Wind will carry. Breath will answer.  

[carrier hiss thins… thins… clicks out.]
