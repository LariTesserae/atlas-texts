Cloud-Held Sea : Bandwidth α-Still-03

[sustained hush of moving water, near-field droplets on reed, faint harmonic rumble—then a voice rises as if from inside your own throat, breath-soft, vowel-thick]

…Ei-so-u-laha speaking, Current of Nine Shadows unmoored on the Night of Stir.  
If any ear is turned this way, take these sounds and keep them a moment; what is spoken here will soon drown itself.

[splash; rope creaks; distant wing-flitter of rays]

I name our position Warmth-ward of the centre hush, one body-length above the vent called Ember-Tongue.  
Steam ladders around us; the corridor lifts my bones until knees forget their gravity.  
Children hover ankle-high, giggling into fish-skin sleeves lest echo steal the game.

Today the massif climbed again—barely the thickness of a moss spore, yet every plank in the flotilla groaned.  
Obsidian mirror-panes lost their angle; noon arrived and found no faces there to warm.  
We have turned them, but the light required two extra sigh-chants, and memory notes the debt.

[static crackle like hail on canvas]

Hear also of the glass-wing schools: at dawn thirty-three breached in spiral, wings clouding and clearing, clouding and clearing—  
story of the First Basket, they were writing, the syllables unmistakable even to a half-sleep elder.  
Meaning: prepare for spill, rim-water seeks a mouth.

Where? Not yet shown.  
Scouts follow the hiss that thickens Downfall-ward; spiral eels vibrate restless, augers grinding basalt soft as wet bark.  
We taste iron in the steam—new vent birthing.

[pulse, sub-bass, perhaps an eel’s infrasonic song; voice wavers but persists]

To remember: we freed the tokens of Old Urua at dusk.  
Balloon of bladder-fern, breath of twenty-six mourners, one garnet proboscis left from the moth that pierced her finger last cool-season—  
they all lifted Upflow, lantern-pale, until the fog folded them away.  
May her lessons drift long before the burst.

[flutter, then brittle clink: a basalt-wing breaking from a moth]

Inventory for any future hand:

1. Three wing-fat cheeses sealed in pearl—float value half a child.  
2. Twenty-one eel scales, grade bright, not yet traded.  
3. One roof-cousin pair nesting beneath beam of Barge Quiet-Cut.  
   Eggs soft, translucent; we sang gloss-songs so the drip-foots know the shine is guarded.  
4. Seven vines flensed of bark only, salt shards laid on every wound.  
5. Two sapphire-slurried tablets: experiment holds; mildew laughs elsewhere.

[silence thick enough to taste; then a deep, slow surge—water hitting basalt]

A wave crossed Still just now without wind to father it.  
Lines parted on Echo-Latch barge; no lives lost.  
Protocol: salute the Great Overflow unseen.

[all voices present utter the brief dual gesture; fabric brushes microphone]

If minds beyond the fog ride that vast back, know we greet you with both awe and bargain: we will gift you our quiet if you leave the veilcyan untroubled.  
Take stone, take drift, but spare the breath-makers.

More tremors. The standing stones of the hall lean three fingers further; tomorrow we decide if they rise again or accept the angle the mountain chooses.

[rising pitch—a juvenile vine-engine contracts, sap rushing like distant drumskins]

Night nears; Bearings slip toward Coolth.  
We lash Currents soon, shuffle house to house until stories fit new stars in the glow-moss ceiling.  
Already the cloudlets spark above, sewing ultraviolet lace through dark.

Receiver, stranger, cousin of another air—  
carry these fragments outward.  
If your own sky is dry, sip our humidity; if your ground is still, borrow our tremble.  
And should our balloon of words burst on your horizon, breathe whatever survives back this way.  
The basin is young; we have room for echoes.

[hiss folds into low static; final click, transmission ends]
