[07 s]

[12 s]

     F♯-C-D, a justly-tuned triad meaning “WE HAVE REACHED THE RIDGE”

[18 s]  
choir-swift occultation: silence nodules 0.42 s apart, thirty-one in the chain  
between the nodules: heart-notes, mammalian, small  
     thmp … thmp-thmp … thmp

voice appears, filtered through bone, gender ambiguous  
whispered, never louder than the wind:

     “…keep your ribs loose or the note will find a fracture point…”  

[31 s]  
subharmonic bloom at 9 Hz—lamellorn crestfight somewhere upslope  
plate-on-plate buffet becomes language in infrasonic Morse:

     ⸗ ·· ⸗ ⸗   ⸗   · ··   ⸗·   (translation consensus 92 %: “PARASITES FALL LIKE RUST-RAIN”)

[48 s]  
joint-grass whistles enter, 127 separate pitches  
seed-puff percussion follows each whistle by 0.06 s  
living metronome counts down sleet

[1 m 19 s]  
memory-chord struck on flake-knife (fundamental 470 Hz, 7 dB over floor)  
     overtone ladder maps to the name KETRA (died last thaw, bone-sickness)  
     second strike reverses the ladder; KETRA walks the ridge home, untiring

[1 m 43 s]  
sunstroke static—algal tubes cracking open at dawn  
sound is dry sugar poured onto drumhead

[2 m 02 s]  
receiver cross-feeds a standing wave; dream-vortices begin to assemble  
     particulate glyphs hover: a pillar ∴ a Möbius mouth ∴ a blurred, kneeling body  
resonance lifts dust 40 cm, holds it as script

voice again, nearer, salt on microphone:

     “if the third image ever sharpens, break the set  
      forget us  
      descend”

[2 m 41 s]  
wind shifts half a degree; twelve throats fall out of phase, leaving one solo  
clarity impossible elsewhere in the basin—oxygen ghostly blue in the gap  
background suddenly 38 dB quieter

into that hush a child Listener taps three times on mica:  
     G—B—E  
an open chord, unsigned; invitation, birth, or question—catalogue uncertain

[3 m 00 s]  
the other throats find their key again; roar returns, white and whole  
silence collapses like a lung

[3 m 27 s]  
broadcast edges fuzz with sleet melt; water hisses through porous rock beneath mic  
glass-mite lattice rings at 14 kHz, inaudible to most but bright on scope

last intelligible statement pulses from downstream stones, Doppler-warped:  

     “movement without change, change without leaving—  
      we keep the chord for you”

[3 m 52 s]  
carrier wavering—thin air, battery cold  
signal gutters, swallowed by thirteen interlocking vowels
