[fog-wide static, then the click-mite domino ripple, 214 pulses]

(low female voice, breath wrapped in drizzle)

     —If you can hear me, measure the silence between us in Tears.  
     The cistern says twenty-one before the next Glass Noon.  
     I have tied that many bladders to the mast root; when they sway you will know the ridge is still growing.

[rind-snap: softbone trunk sealing a split]

Day shard, cloud-edge.  
Umbrel veins flexing—sound of linen torn underwater.  
The eels quilt the higher mist; their skins scatter silver motes that never land.  
I taste them in the air like bitten coin.

(pause: only the choir gnat undertone, a chord of minus twenty hertz)

Inventory, spoken for the trunk-scriptors:  
     • Two glidecat pelts, unlicked, therefore still loud.  
     • One strider tibia, light as verb, hollow as promise.  
     • Forty-six ember martins in torpor, pinholes dim; their swarm-mind dreaming in hexagonal pulse.  
     • Four stories I have not yet carved—keep them warm for me.

[tick-cascade from the limestone, pressure drop imminent]

Ash Fall arriving early.  
Sky purples like bruised fruit; every vein of the Noon butterflies throws back night in shards.  
If you stand on the west lip now your heartbeat will echo once, then fold itself closed like a sail.  
Do not stand there long.

(child’s voice, far off, counting in clicks not numbers)

I walked the perimeter where the unsound opened yesterday.  
White stones already blanch with algae breath.  
The hole tasted of mute thunder; my ears rang with the shape of a word nobody has said yet.

(muffled bell—glidecat cub returned, mother unfastening the gift)

Report for the Re-Lashing:  
South ladder drifted three finger-spans downslope, spine fault breathing again.  
We will move the weaving house and the birth alcoves before next drizzle.  
Pack nothing rigid—only what can relearn its angles.

[sudden hush: choir gnats cancel the broad-band static; only heartbeats remain]

Personal:  
There is sap on my palms where the softbone cut itself open.  
I pressed the wound closed, whispered the name I will give to the child if the cistern permits.  
Sap replied with the taste of fern milk and distant quartz.

Listen—(voice drops to a reed-thin filament)  
The mountain is turning to find a cooler dream.  
Every being on this spur is a bookmark in its sleep.  
Hold very still: hear the page crease?

[fourteen measured drips—cistern overflow]

I will speak again after the ridge adds its next rib.  
Until then, trade this recording for water, or for quiet—whichever costs you more.
