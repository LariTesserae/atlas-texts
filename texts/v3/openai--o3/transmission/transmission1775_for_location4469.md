Bent-Basin Low-Steam Band / Sequence Δ–1729

[carrier tone opens … settles two heartbeats below turbine hum]  
[low mist of static, bright with frog-chirr edge]

—Begin log.

Shift six ceasing, shift one commencing.  
Boiler cluster: green.  Sap draw: moderate, trending hushed.  
Foundry temp holds at seven-hundred two; the frogs are singing B-flat, so gauge agrees.  
Boardwalk thirty-nine reports one split plank; crews flagged.  
All lights gutter-amber, drip-tight.  
No slinker spoor inside the ring.

[brief percussion: someone tapping a wrench against hollow bark, code for “awake / attend”]

Personal note—Caro here.  First night on cold-flue duty after the long humidity.  The trees keep mouthing the pipes like babes at bottles.  Remember to idle every forty counts, else the wood will kiss the iron shut.

Weather internal: canopy brewed a fine brass-taste drizzle at dusk.  Sugar ratio high enough to glaze the railings; children slid entire length of Crescent Walk before curfew, laughing sparks off their heels.  Fog layer sits knee-deep, silvered.  I am writing glyphs with my boots and watching them heal closed.

[static climbs, resolves into distant whistles—five quick, one long—then subsides]

Cargo note: thirty rolls of autumn bark-parchment bound for Upriver Port if dawn lift clears.  Lichen-wax seals good.  Manifest stamped by Forewoman Gai.  She smells of peppermint rust tonight; the polish is fresh on her hands.

Visitors: two strangers arrived by pole-skiff at last light.  Names unreadable—wheel-scribe smeared in the mist—but they breathe like upland folk, fast and wasteful.  Offered them a courtesy steam so the aerobes could settle; they declined.  Entire dining tier went quiet at that.  Outsiders who shun steam are like clouds that refuse to rain—impossible or planning trouble.  We gave them ribbon-fungus cloaks anyway; hate to see a guest drip to the bone.

Noise report: turbines steady bass, leaf-gutters in three-quarter time.  Between those beats, pillars creak soft vowels.  If you slow your pulse you can syllable out meaning: hush / hush / hush / breathe.  Some swear the bracelet speaks real words; I settle for its lullaby.

[carrier flickers; overlay of wind-stray, faint spinning]

Observation—mid-trunk eight-five has begun shaping a collar around exhaust line.  Only a cuff so far, but amber-sap bright in the seams.  We will cool that stack six beats longer per cycle.  Trees ask politely before they strangle.

Torch-frog census, foundry wall: sixty-two eggs, nine squirmers, one adult posted like a ruby rivet on the crucible lip.  Healthy flame color.  Good sign for tomorrow’s pour.

[clicking interference, interprets as footsteps crossing copper plates]

Central clearing stands mirror-wet.  Steam lanterns throw lattices through the fog, make pillars into cathedral ribs.  I stopped there earlier, shift-whistle echoing off sap and skin alike, and felt the place think sideways: not a language, more a pressure behind the sternum, suggestion to hold still and be counted.  So I stood, and the drizzle haloed me, and the wood leaned a measurable millimetre.  The bracelet knows my shape again after six weeks upriver.  Home confirms itself.

Data to log for research bureau: metal-lichen grown on manganese sample shows turquoise bloom, peppermint note absent.  Hypothesis: alloy flavor alters metabolite bouquet.  Will submit shavings in next courier jar.

Security: slinker spoor on outer mud five nights running but none inside dripline.  Paw-prints map a spiral pattern, tight as a lathe thread, always pause when engines idle.  I think they like the lullaby too.  Recommendation stands: keep one piston breathing at all hours; silence might invite closer curiousity.

[low resonance—someone thumping boiler hull three times, wedding cadence reversed—then laughter, muffled]

Ah, they are rehearsing.  Two toolwrights tie the knot after midnight, inside the standing-by vat.  I filed the rim smooth so vows will ring true.  Remember to shut feedwater so the echo carries.

Supplies low: cotton innerweave to forty percent, candles to twenty (though bark-filament lasts longer now).  Trade envoy due in nine days by river, contingent on channel depth.  Floodplain upstream reported restless—old water thinking of returning.  We have pumps ready but hope the river forgets again.

[carrier warbles; tonal fringe resembles distant gulls—probably herons at ridge]

Interlude:

   hiss of sap in risers  
   tick of condensation on tin  
   tiny pop—torch-frog catching midge  
   silence shaped like a birch-lung filling

Sometimes I swear the quiet has subdivisions, grooves you can drop thought into like a stylus on wax.  Tonight the groove says caution, but softly, the way bark lifts an eyebrow.

Report ends soon; dawn bleed already lightening fog from iron to pearl.  Shift one warming turbines with low hum; chorus of frogs shifts key to greet it.  Outsider pair still abed, dreaming dry dreams I wager.  Boardwalk split repaired—sap glue curing under lantern flare.  All loops closed.
