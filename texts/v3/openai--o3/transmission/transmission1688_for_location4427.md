(they do; FFT confirms peaks at 312 Hz, 468 Hz, 624 Hz, each < −60 dB.)

[1 m 47 s]

Laugh becomes part of water and is gone.

[2 m 12 s]

Interior resonance Q≈8.4.  Child stirs moss-spore batter; paddle whispers against quartz bowl like fingertips stroking frost.

No alarm: the tap coincides with hoe-thud far outside, scheduled.

[2 m 51 s]  
SUBTERRANEAN BELL—f1 = 138 Hz, clear, steady.  Meaning: safety, stone holding.

[3 m 10 s]

[3 m 44 s]

Driftmoss begins its low-volt glow: text of no language scrolls across basalt like slow lightning.
