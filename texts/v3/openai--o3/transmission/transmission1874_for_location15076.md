Echoes from the Stacked Place

[carrier hiss, weak … strengthening … then a shuddering thrum like rope-ladders in wind]

—…sssst.  

—…low whistle: one note, then the answering note, a ledge cleared—  

—footsteps on granite, soft-soled, measured in heartbeats—

WIND (close-miked, breathy)  
⭗⭗⭗⭗⭗⭗⭗

VOICE 1  (barely above breath; chest-tone)  
“… —dawn tally complete. Thirty-six argent, two juveniles riding thermals beyond the Crown. Our crib born last quarter is number thirty-seven of us. Ratio still breathing. Quiet thanks.”

[small percussion: fingernail flicks ceramic; counts out droplets]  
tik… tik… tik…

CRACKLE—thousands of micro-pops: ladder-lichens opening. The sound sheets left to right like rain hitting thin copper.  
Over it, a glider’s three-octave gong, metallic, ringing the slot corridors.

VOICE 2  (older, amused)  
“Mind the echo, sprout. A sound that isn’t paid for comes off someone’s water.”

VOICE 1  
“Paid. Three sips pledged.”  
[gourd uncorked; viscous swirl]  
glug… glug… glug…

The WIND climbs a note, catches hanging vines; they strum against each other, a harp of hollow nodes half-full of syrup.

WHISTLE CODE: two quick, one long. Response distant. Bridge clearance granted.

FOOTFALLS now on vine-rope span—creak-twang, creak-twang—followed by the hush of many bodies pausing.

CHILD VOICES (whisper-chant, learning)  
“Condense—consume—conserve.  
Miss one note and the wind will sand your—”

ALL CHILDREN (gasp)  
“Careful! The drop!”

[a bead of water plinks from finger to stone cistern far below—inaudible impact but every voice stills]

ELDER (same as Voice 2, breath gone thin)  
“Silence owed. All of you. Till first shade.”

—thirty heartbeats of hush. Only wind and the soft, slow ticking of cactus heat-valves venting.

STATIC intrudes, sputters, then clears to a new scene:

NIGHT. Rope bridges hum under thermal gusts. Crickets pump hollow abdomens—reedy drones that phase in and out like faraway engines.

VOICE 3 (diary, confidential)  
“Ledger scratch: third fin on the south buttress shows stress. Twenty, maybe thirty seasons left before it shears. If it goes, the Weavers’ main curtain will lose purchase and every cistern on Balcony Seven goes thirsty. Told the stone-mothers; they carve tomorrow at dawn.”

[close scrape: serpentine chisel meets granite—skreee, pause, skreee—etching a new gutter]

Sudden BOOM—an argent glider’s struts slam; the receiver distorts.

VOICE 3 (laughs dryly)  
“He thinks the fin’s already his. Not yet, silver brother.”

[ambient cross-fade]

MID-MORNING. Sun fuzzy through dust. Human voices muted; ledge etiquette holds.

A BABY cries—ragged, dehydrated. Mother hums two low vowels that cancel in phase, a lullaby engineered not to carry.

VOICE 4 (young woman, urgent whisper)  
“Courier from below—copper for clay, asks passage. Says riverbed storms are rising, red dust for weeks. We trade now or keep darkness for half a moon.”

RASP of Pardal snails on stone—slow, sandpaper rhythm.

VOICE 5 (hoarse, distant)  
“Count the shells, girl. Water walks inside them. Trade what sings, not what shines.”

The TRANSMISSION warps; frequencies fold inward. A subterranean scented sigh of chemolithic life passes the mic—ozone, penny-metal, pine memory—rendered as soft electrostatic purr.

VOICE 6 (ritual, echoing like spoken inside a narrow shaft)  
“Slowest Heart, taste this salt. Keep the cliff breathing.”  
[granules sprinkled: shhh, shhh]

LATE AFTERNOON. Heat vents; plate cacti sigh again, a collective exhale like crepe paper torn very slowly.

SUDDEN COMMOTION—rope lattice trembles, anklets of shell chips jingle frantic.

WHISTLE ALARM: high-low-high. Rockfall!

GRIND of ancient granite giving—then a single, cannon-crack fracture. Debris whistles past microphones into abyss. Silence suffocates.

VOICE 7 (shaken, coughing dust)  
“Only the lip—just the lip. No homes lost. Praise the Counting.”

Faint applause of hands on tunics: celebration never louder than necessary.

TWILIGHT. Horizon a bruise; first glider silhouettes tilt against pewter haze.

CHOIR (mixed ages, led by elder)  
[soft polyrhythmic claps, then chant layered in rounds]  
“Stone keeps memory, water keeps time.  
We borrow both—  
return in rhyme.”

Drum of hollow vine nodes tapped with knuckles. Beat slows, spaces widen, fade into wind.

NIGHT PROPER. Stars veiled; dust halo glows. A lone recorder—bamboo-tongued—plays four notes, pauses, four notes, pauses. Echo migrates through slot corridors, returns aged and thinned, as though another musician farther away answers with the same melody but older.

RECORDER PLAYER (half-singing into wood)  
“Let tomorrow find us fewer in debt,  
fewer in doubt,  
full in the cup,  
empty of danger.”

Wind gust steals final syllable, stretches it into feedback.

Carrier signal falters, stutters, steadies. A new whisper seeps through—child again, the earlier debtor, voice restored after sunset penance.

CHILD  
“I’m talking soft so no one owes. The drop I spilled is back in cloud. I watched it climb the air at dusk, I swear. Maybe it will come again at dawn, knock my forehead, ask to be caught. I’ll hold the cup still this time. Night, receiver.”

[the child whistles two gentle notes—greeting, farewell]

STATIC swells, contracts to a pinpoint hum. The hum becomes wind. The wind becomes silence. The transmission ends.
