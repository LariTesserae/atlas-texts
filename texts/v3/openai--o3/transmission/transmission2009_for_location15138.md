Song of the Upper Courses

[carrier tone—thin, tremulous, gradually resolving into a chord of turbine-thrum and wind resonance]

—⸺-squeal-⸺

[three low bells ring, each ten heartbeats apart]

Voice (older, amber-rough):  
“Good morning, terraces two-hundred-ten through two-hundred-forty. Your sunline is clear. Mirrors stand at seventy-three degrees. Begin unfurl on the fourth chime.”

[fourth bell; brass so deep it flutters cheap headphones]

Voice (younger, distant):  
“Gardenwright dispatch, confirm cut-list for Lot Chrysanthemum.”  
[a pause; a metallic syllable that might be an automaton responding]  
“Much obliged, Cutter Nine. Take your time, the orchids have theirs.”

[wind microphone overload—rush of warm air across the grill, then the hush of leeward calm]

Choir, children, chanting the rainfall ledger:  
“Five days dry, one day wet,  
Two days fog and none to fret.  
Count the drops, the copper sings—  
Water walks on folded wings.”

Voice (whisper, staccato consonants of a wind-rigger calling from a mast):  
“Knot report, hour 3-one-9:  
    • Main hawser sound, two filaments fray-etched.  
    • Banner line tangled with swift-rook nest— chicks inside, leave for dusk.  
Will splice after feeding.”

[soft clank; a goat bell answers, far below]

Automaton speech, picked up by hull contact mic—slow gong-tongue taps and steam sighs:  
—CLANG—ssss—CLANG—  
(automatic transcription scrolls across the receiver in pale green)  
“Request accepted: prune banyan flank east face third spine.  Deliberation complete in 41 pulses.  Proceeding.”

[in the background, a harmonium starts a work-tempo; its wheeze syncs with foot pumps, six beats to the bar]

Heliograph code, sun-flash against sensor:  
••– • • –• | –•–• •– ••••  
(translates to “DIRIGIBLE INBOUND, MARTEN DUNG FOR FERTILISER—CLEAR PAD”)

Static surge; when it settles, a soft-band trader frequency cuts in—voice high, helium-bent:  
“—repeat, gondola Shadow-Forty seeks berth above fog, gas at seventy-five. Trade manifest: tungsten bolts, cedar oil, six reels polycotton. Swap for pollen or reservoir credit. Acknowledge.”  
[they cough, squeak again]  
“Also one passenger, cleric skin-scribe, library drop.”

[relay clicks; reservoir monks answer by striking copper plates—code older than Morse, each note a numeral of available cistern volume]

Ambient: cicadas gear-grinding, a tortoise shell knocking gutter iron, distant whistle valve.

Elder Voice returns, softer now, perhaps leaning toward the microphone grill:  
“To any sky-farers stumbling on this band—mind the echo terraces between nine and noon. The sun finds you there and does not let go. Drink before thirst, rest before need, and if a ladder-goat stares you down turn your gaze aside—pride is a quick way to empty air.”

[she inhales; you can hear the brass-leaf of condenser reeds ticking as they cool]

Song—solo, untrained but steady—rises over steady piston heartbeat:  

“Stone wrote our birthdays,  
Steel keeps the score;  
We climb on our children,  
They’ll climb on us more.  
Slow be the lifting,  
Sure be the seam—  
Lay one more brick for the mountain that dreams.”

[as the last note fades, the receiver picks up rainfall starting far below—an almost-subsonic roar growing like paper tearing in reverse]

Bell—once, drawn, vibrating the signal into momentary silence.

Voice (final):  
“Rain’s on the lower shells. Shift change in three. All hands, look to your gutters. End of broadcast. Continuance attend us.”

[carrier tone narrows to a filament, then snaps; only wind and very distant children laughing remain]
