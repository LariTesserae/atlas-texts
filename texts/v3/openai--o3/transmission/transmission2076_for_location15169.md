LIMEN-LEDGE / BAND 63.71 m

[silent carrier … rise in background hiss]

—k’k—k’zzt—  
…rainlight passing … hold for count of three …

[sweep of wind up the scale, turning to flute-like whine]

Day 188, second thaw.  
Stone still cool. Mist returning from below—repeat—from below.  
All lanterns shuttered.

[throat-pulse sub-bass, words felt more than heard]

tell the water breathe slow / pulse is thin but true / no digging / no digging

[crackle, then a chorus of gull voices, the notes folding into human syllables]

Aaa—ouree—kei—  
Echo clean, echo clean. Choir turning.  
Edge clear of eagles for two breaths.

[two quick booms: conie foot-drums, answered by sundew clicks like sand poured on glass]

Record: moss level eight, charge moderate.  
Hair stands—yes—static in the beard ropes.  
Sweep the goat hooves with birch oil; sparks jump from the nail beds.

[low whistle spiralling down: Roof-Sleeper code]

glide-path west face broken / send cord / send cord

[far, metallic ringing: vapor-kin spilling stored droplets]

tink    tink    tink-tink-tink  
(the mountain listens)

[voice surfaces high in the mix, thin with altitude]

If fog climbs instead of falls we fold the roofs, we walk.  
Repeat to all kin: fold, walk.

[interference surge—electric pop—operator speaks faster]

Blood on lichen three paces wide—  
repeat—three paces—fresh—  
Mute every drum. Leopard due before night.

[rush of silence like cotton ripped; a single half-gulped roar under the carrier; even the static flinches]

. . .

[minutes of thin needle-noise, then the signal steadies]

Shadow floats again—hollow light, same as last turning.  
Children chase themselves laughing above the stone.  
We tie their ankles to truth so they will not drift.

[amber chime: goat-bone windbell]

Trade tally:  
lichen up-slope → goats fed → hair collected → one coil braided.  
Conie marrow left in bowl for eagles.  
Eagle rune—deep gouge, two teeth—reads “clear dawn, hard wind.”

[sibilant harmony: sundew tentacles clacking in rolling wave]

Listen—  
the garden applauds the storm we refused.

[pitch drops to infra, carries through ribcage]

stone eats night / but not our names / keep roof open / keep fire low

[distant horn from valley timber camps; gulls answer at precise sixth interval; their echo braids with horn until phase cancellation leaves a single pure tone]

oooooooo———

[tone splits, resolves into spoken words]

Receiver beyond the gorge, do you still lean close?  
Salt your skin; the mountain tastes newcomers.  
Accept the first pebble we send.

[soft clatter—something tiny striking microphone: a mica-flecked stone]

If you count three dawns here and remain unasked, leave.  
The charge is coiling.  
Moss batteries hum like bees sharpening stingers.

[background hiss rises; faint thunder of subterranean meltwater adds irregular pulse: three beats silence, five beats roar]

Breathe with us.  
In—two—three  
Out—two—three—four—five

[static parts as though cut by a knife; for four heartbeats every sound on the band aligns—a chord of wind, throat, talon, drum, drip—bright and whole]

…⸺…

[collapsing rush; katabatic scream fills spectrum, then recedes]

Report ends.  
Stone rests.  
Hold the channel. We return when the pulse brims silver.

[carrier drops to a low shimmer, oscillates, fades]
