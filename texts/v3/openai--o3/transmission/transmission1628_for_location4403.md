Breath-Pulse 171, Received in the Hailband Frequency  

…ʭʭʭ…  
[sleet of carrier hiss]  
…drip…drip…drip…  

(voice, so close it feels behind the ear, pitched lower than a throat should go)  

     —blue-white now,  
     gutter three still holds,  
     my hands remember the chill.  

[birch-bark slate clicks once, twice]  

     Lattice-watch to moss-tenders,  
     mind the south wall;  
     the light is leaning wider than dawn allows.  

[half-second of silence in which you can hear your own blood]  

     We have sung the second verse,  
     the fronds answer—  
     they rise, a patience of spears,  
     emerald to rust, rust to ember, all quiet-bright.  

     Hear the breath?  
     Forty-seven beats, stone inhales,  
     skin tightens on knuckle,  
     ladders shorten by a finger’s breadth—  
     then loose again.  
     As ever.  

[snow-sparrow wing buffets the microphone; the static cracks like frost underfoot then stills]  

(child’s whisper)  
     Frog-warden report:  
     tadpoles counting many-and-nine,  
     ring-ditch clear,  
     two midges caught on the lip—released.  
     We thank the first guests.  

[another slate click, farther off]  

(elder’s murmur, grainy)  
     Tower top greets the rim wind.  
     Seal-kin are late, baskets still empty.  
     I mirror-flash once—  
     no answer,  
     only the long glare of ice.  
     If they do not come by goldlight  
     we will stretch the root-meal.  

[static sheets across the band, resolves into a hum—several voices layered, humming a chord so low it registers in bone not ear. During the hum moss-rain falls faster: dripdripdripdrip… then steadies.]  

(younger voice, amused)  
     Outsider device blinks red again.  
     It counts ladders—seven, then eight, then seven—  
     poor thing argues with the Hollow.  
     We let it.  
     Argument is a kind of song.  

[minute stretch of nothing but breathing limestone, a pulse every forty-seven heartbeats; faint metallic tinnitus at the edge of hearing]  

     Silver hush approaches.  
     We fold the voices now.  
     Take this brightness with you—  
     small enough to pocket,  
     wide enough to blind.  

[the hum subsides; only the terminal three drips remain, spaced like slow clockwork]  

…drip…        …drip…        …drip…  

—end packet—
