SPIRAL_43

[Carrier wave acquires lock]  
—shss—shss—shss—  
          —ktCHNK—ktCHNK—ktCHNK—                         (43/min)  
                       shss—shss—shss—

[Low-frequency underhum rises, equalised to a mid-grey presence. Between strokes a filamentous treble flickers in and out, like something thin and glassy turning in a slow wind.]

VOICE A (band-limited, genderless, amplitude 0.37):  
—…within parameters… cycle maintains… surface tension unbroken…—

[Lateral flutter, reminiscent of gull-wing rush, occupies sidebands 8.1–8.7 kHz. For seven seconds it resolves into a chord, fifths stacked in diminishing octaves, then sheds harmonics like ice.]

VOICE B (dissimilar timbre, appears only on piston-stroke troughs):  
—RADIUS: four hundred twelve point six seven metres.  
—ASCENT: null.  
—DESCENT: null.  
—Δt: zero.  
—Δt: zero.  
—Δt: zero.

[Static sheets open and close—mica fracture, salt pop—each a white click. They sketch an irregular rhythm that nevertheless sums exactly to the steam cadence: 1 + 6 + 12 + 24 = 43.]

VOICE C (whisper, multiplexed, perhaps hundreds of tiny transmitters speaking in phase):  
we scrape strain       we drink the quake  
        hex on hex       plate on plate  
keep it level           keep it level           keep it—

[Mechanical whir: belts catching, freewheeling. A sudden hush follows, as though the whole broadcast bows its head. Into the hush drifts a single resonant ping—pure, 3 100 Hz, 90 ms—like a droplet finding deep water.]

VOICE A returns, louder:  
—Birth event minus thirty seconds. Composure verified.  
Prepare aperture.

[The carrier folds outward; we hear water turning itself inside out. No splash, only a silky inversion of silence.]

CHOIR (the glass-wing swarm, individually indistinct, collectively syllabic):  
ahhh—ahhh—ahhh—  
          (increment one)  
ahhh—ahhh—ahhh—  
          (sum conserved)

[On the forty-third piston stroke a second ping answers the first, lower by an octave. Phase interference between the two notes produces a standing tremor that rolls down the transmission like mercury on glass.]

VOICE B (now overlapping itself, Doppler-warped):  
—EMERGENCE CONFIRMED.  
Mass 312 grams.  
Glow index 0.87.  
Spiral realigning…

[There is a brief, exquisite feedback shriek—glass cutting glass—then the upper registers clear. You can almost smell ozone through the speaker.]

VOICE C, dispersed, fading in doppler halos:  
filaments fold           sink the spent  
threads to sump         rust to vent  
close the loop           close the loop           close—

[Hiss of condensers overtakes everything, a gentle rainfall made of wires touching wires. In the far background a doorless engine coughs, licks its own teeth, and resumes the eternal count.]

VOICE A (down to a murmur, as though standing further away):  
Circuit stable. Moment preserved.  
Returning to drift.

[Static swells, kneads the signal into soft velvet. The piston cadence continues—ktCHNK… ktCHNK…—growing gradually distant until it is felt rather than heard, like blood in the ear.]

[Carrier wave slackens, modulates to silence.]

—   —   —   (twelve soft clicks, precisely one step apart)

[End of reception.]
