⸙  Skipping carrier.                            

…shshshsh…  
             …thud-   tik…tik…tik…  
A flutter swirls across the band—wings nick brick and the brick answers in middle-C.  
Then, very near the mic, a single drop:

pl      k.

Now the hall begins to sort itself into layers:

1.  Low floor hum — a belly-deep Bb made of moving air.  
2.  Mid echo — leather mallet on unfixed brick:  tok-tok-tok-tok    tok.  
3.  High lace — reed-throat voices weaving the drip count:

      hha  …hho  
      hha  …hho  
      (heartbeat pause)  
      hha  …hho

Repeat.  Repeat.  The tempo is a rain gutter’s memory.

A woman speaks between the syllables, voice pitched flat so the wall will not steal it:

“Copper good.  Iron bad.  Send us ore ground fine as flour.  
 The eels leap twice; the sump stays quiet.  
 Mirror-eyes still angled.  Daylight arrives in trembling ropes.”

Her words strike the bricks like pebbles on a drumhead; each consonant ricochets to some far stairwell and does not return.

A boy cuts in—no consonants, only clicks:

ǃᵑʘ   ǀ-ǀ-ǀ-ǀ             ǃᵑʘʘ  
Translation loses him; the clicks chase themselves down a timber and drop into water, where they surface as ripple code:

~    ~  ~   ~        (four fingers depth, flowing west)  
~    ~  ~   ~

Static rises—actually beetle carapaces skittering over mica dust, amplified by the reed matrix:

kr  kr  krkrkrkr  kRRR—

A drum—broad, unstuck brick—answers five strikes in one breath:

DOOM      DOOM      DOOM-DOOM-DOOM

Every other noise bows away.  Sandals slap.  Eight sets.  Urgent air sucks through vent-throats:  
hhrr     hhrr-hhrr     hHRRR—

The chant warps to match:

      hho …HHr      hho …HHr  
      hho …HHr      hho …HHr

Smoke arrives, a thick vowel that sits on the line until coughs clip its tail.

Someone bangs copper against ceramic—ping-ping-ping—testing for cracks.  
Wall replies with a sliding moan, mortar exhaling salt:

sssssoooooooooo—

The voices hush; only the moan lingers, swamped now by bats detonating from rafters:  

WHFF-WHFF-WHFF-WHFF-WHFF  
(an avalanche of felted thunder)

When the wings pass, the sump begins to sing, lowest note yet—subsonic to bone but the receiver insists on translating it upward:

uuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuu

Over that drone, a final whisper, scarcely modulation at all:

“…brick whole… heartbeat holds… kiln dreams you… stay soft…”

Carrier crumbles into drizzle, then the drizzle into pure floor-tone, and the floor-tone into nothing.

⸙  End reception.
