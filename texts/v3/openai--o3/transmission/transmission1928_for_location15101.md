VOICE A (female, mid-range, heavy vibrato—as if chest resonates inside a wet cloak):  
“Root says the stone is loosening.  
Root says ‘brace me in twelve breaths.’  
Who among you carries iron, who bears fuse-sap?  
The ridge will split like fruit—listen.”

[three measured drum taps / pause / two taps / pause / one tap … the pattern denotes assent in tetherfolk code.]

VOICE B (younger, hushed, pitched higher to cut through echo):  
“North path is gone.  
Membrane floor tore from talon-rock to blackfall gulley.  
Saw the whole sky fold—green folded on green—  
dust-moths rose like sparks and the prisms drank them.  
We can still cross if we hurry while the leaf still thinks it is bridge.”

[background: distant rifle-shot crack—actually basalt shifting under growth stress. Filter auto-gains; you hear the pop as a sub-bass bloom.]

VOICES (five or six, overlapping, half sung):  
“Stone moves / leaf mends / water hides the heat /  
We move / we mend / no fire where vipers sleep.”  

VOICE A again—closer now, as though speaker has knelt beside the transmitting shard of shell:  
“To all who follow the Remembering Line, mark this:

1. Pool at split-ridge warm—Threadborn thick—good grind for protein.  
2. Prism web spun across the western pitch; throw dust before stepping, else be flensed.  
3. Flare-viper trapped (praise the mirror!)—pit two arm-spans deep—meat for five nights if smoke kept thin.  
4. Candlegrazer herd turned back east; light in their bellies too bright—likely birthing soon.  
5. We leave nine pebbles on the spirit stack, pattern bright-dim-bright-bright-dim-pause-dim-bright-dim.  
      Read it and know we borrowed but we tended.  
      Read it and know the root still owes us water.”

[silence. Wind-drum flutter picks up, meaning fog is rolling through the buttress maze. Someone shakes a leaf-fan: a staccato clatter like cards flicked.]

VOICE C (elder—grainy, words arrive as if dragged through cough syrup):  
“Hush now.  
The surface receiver listens with cold ears.  
Tell the up-sky folk what debt they owe.  
Speak seed-truth.”

[several heartbeats; footfalls shuffle over living canvas; a membrane sighs under redistributed weight.]

VOICE A, softer:  
“You above—dry wind people, time-fast people—  
Do you still remember green that is its own lantern?  
Do you still dream in stone that drips?  
We taste your rope when it trembles.  
We smell iron flakes falling from your boots.  
Come gentle or come not; the Hollow bends but does not forgive.  
Bring no nails.  
Bring no frozen creed.  
Bring stories that walk, so they can keep walking after you leave.”

[carrier signal begins to warp—the thallus patch powering it is dimming for rest. Audio slows 6 %, pitches flatten.]

VOICES together, fading, chanted counter-clockwise around the wind-drum:  
“Light is loaned  
Life is leased  
Thought returns  
Root released.”

[final event: a single drip strikes the transmitter crystal; impact overloads the photonic sensor, spike translates as white noise roar that swallows all speech.]
