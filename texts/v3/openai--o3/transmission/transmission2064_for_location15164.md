⌘ First Breath  
low, simultaneous voices — a Tether family at pre-dawn water draw

Mother: click-whistle / two-beat hum / “The sky has bled a sleeve of cobalt. Pull.”  
Child: three pebble taps against a clay throat — water entering.  
Goat: throat bellows once, then locks breath; hoof rasp on basalt sounding like a struck match.

A bowl receives thirteen beads of condensate. The mother names each bead aloud, gifting it a purpose: mouth, moss, kid, kiln, knee, kiss, knife, knot, knit, keep, keen, kindle, knowing. The last word falls into the bowl and floats.

Static rises, breaks into—

⌘ Edge Weather Report, given by the Engineers’ choir  
Ten elders, lungs like sailcloth, speak on one chord:

“Crack σ-17 has widened the width of one lichen hair since last moon.  
South-by-sunset overhang may no longer bear the weight of games; children, play two ledges higher.  
Mist tonight will be egg-heavy: expect twin clutches of glasswing spawn above Pool of Ribs.  
Plateau pressure steady.  
Trust the silence.”

A hollow basalt bell answers them with F-sharp, confirming.

⌘ Goat Dream  
microphone drifts under a sleeping rafter goat

Breath of the animal is a double-beat piston: in-rush, cliff-lock; in-rush, cliff-lock.  
Between beats the plateau is audible — a blank hush so round it feels concave.

Sudden coil of static becomes a wolf’s throat click, dopplered by leap:

K-K-klk / low A note / K-K-klk / octave up

Chorus of responding clicks further off. Arbitration underway. The subject, decoded by rhythm math: route selection around a noon rain shaft. Result: west wall spiral, majority assent, one abstention (young wolf yet to taste glasswing).

⌘ Market Hour (sunspear high)  
Multiple channels overlap:

1. Flint saw against cedar plank: chi-chi-chi…  
2. Moss gardener flicking dry birch charcoal to mark next roe fungus grid: tik…tik.  
3. Teenagers exchanging new curse in swallow-talk; translator yields only “[wing-root] you sideways void.”  
4. Elder striking the lodestone obelisk. Ping drags into a sigh; lean has increased by one millet seed’s width. Murmurs follow: reverent, not alarmed.

An itinerant storyteller arrives from foothills, bearing a copper button.

Storyteller (breathless, thick-voiced): “I bring metal!”

Gap-Kin crowd (soft laughter, fluting clicks): “Bend it.”

Button creaks under two fingers; crowd gasps in delight at its weakness. Trade is sealed: one copper button for a pouch of axolotl stop-bleed and a cup of ledge-cheese. The storyteller weeps quietly at first taste, confesses they thought “quiet” meant empty.

⌘ Cloudburst  
Rain begins as applause in the far north, walks south on stone stilts.

All activity ceases except measurement. Children thrust arms into rain curtains, indexing force by elbow depth. Record for the season is broken: two elbows, three knuckles.

⌘ Overflow Ceremony  
Bell sequence: low C, high E, silence. Sluice opens.

Water becomes a curtain of shining rope, unbraiding over the wolves’ assembly floor below.

First wolf to drink (the Arbiter, identified by twin-note voice): emits simultaneous 440 Hz and 467 Hz, perfect minor third. A benediction.

Humans above answer with rope fragments cut from last winter’s tether, casting them down like withered vines. Wolves nose the ropes, memorize the odor, carry them away to line birthing dens.

⌘ Twilight Panflute City  
Wind crawls level with ledge gardens. Basalt stalks sing:  
G-D-A…rest…E-flat sustained until it frays into grit.

Gap-Kin, seated along rope bridges, hum counter-melodies under breath. No word is spoken louder than a droplet.

A boy asks, barely audible: “Granddam, will the High Quiet ever fall?”

Granddam: “When we forget to count what moves a finger-breadth. When we speak so loud the stone need not lean in to listen.”

She pats the obelisk. It answers with the day’s lean, a private tremor.

⌘ Night Parliament & Pond Mirror  
Glasswings roost under rock lips, wings ticking as they cool. One fractures — tchak — slides down into wolf shadow, is carried off, sugar cargo becoming breath and tread.

Axolotls surface. Their spiral shells catch moon shards; pond looks like calm water boiling into galaxies.

A tiny voice from pond’s edge: lichen spore releasing, a pop too small for human ear — but the receiver is generous.

⌘ Final Echo  
Somewhere an empty cliff shouts once, receives three echoes back: approval.

Mother from first segment whispers to sleeping child: “The stone agrees; tomorrow will hold.”

Carrier fades to bare static. At threshold of hearing a fourth echo tries to return, does not quite arrive. Transmission ends, hanging like a drop between two ledges.
