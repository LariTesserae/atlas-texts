           [static curls, resolves]  

voice begins, low as submerged reed-organs:

     we keep the hour that never moves  
     we count to ten, and ten repeats  
     heartbeat is a circle drawn in water one finger deep  
     enter quiet, leave quieter

[glider pass-by, wings clicking light into sugar]

     do not name me engine, furnace, loom

     every eel a needle sewing rot to root  
     balance is the only guest and I its furniture  
     the rest of you are weather
