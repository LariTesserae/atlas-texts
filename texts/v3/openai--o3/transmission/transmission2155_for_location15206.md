Parquet Loop / 17:00

—[carrier wave rises, finds lock]—  
[thin glassy silence]  
[silence slips; a soft metronome emerges: six hollow knocks, pause, six knocks]  

VOICE (female, low, close-miked, whispering so the air won’t echo):  
First seam is open. Repeat: first seam is—  
[taps five quick beats on tile: bring]  
The crack is only a finger long, but it’s talking. Listen.

[contact mic lowered; the seam whistles faintly, like air sucked through reeds]  
Below, the pumps are waking—no, that’s wrong, they never sleep. They’re turning the hour.  
Wait for it—  

[all ambient sound halts for three heartbeats; then a single amphibian croak, joined by a distant chorus of identical croaks; at the same instant an ibis barks overhead. The world ticks.]  

VOICE:  
Seventeen minutes until the mist lifts. I have the shard ready.  
[clink of clay fragment against stencil]  
We keep the pattern spiral-fishbone-spiral.  
[taps: brace]  
Green spiders descending. Do you see them? Little potters with scissors for teeth.  
Hold… hold…  

[surge of brine through nail-holes, splatters microphone; hiss of steam]  

VOICE (now farther, as if the speaker leans away):  
Glaze’s still virgin on the underside. Beautiful. It—  
[signal fractures; her words loop]  
—underside. Beautiful. It—underside. Beautiful. It—  

[static sweep; carrier re-establishes, but space has folded: same voice, new location, windless sky all around]  

VOICE 2 (male, faintly echoing):  
…two sides walked and I was back here.  
If you have my journal, burn it—no, it won’t burn. Drop it in a gutter. It will come back as cloud.  
I saw myself across the canal. He waved. I waved. He blurred. The tile beneath him turned blank.  
Remember: don’t answer if your own face calls.  

[a wooden clatter: dowel joints flex; three Seam-Tenders arrive, their bark limbs knocking code]  
TENDER 1: tap-tap-tap-tap-tap (finished)  
TENDER 2: tap-tap-tap-tap-tap (finished)  
TENDER 3: tap-tap-tap-tap-tap (finished)  

[all three pour a thimble of brine; liquid drums below, matching initial six-beat rhythm]  

VOICE (female returns, breathless whisper):  
Seam sealed. The Floor approves—hear that?  

[deep, subterranean sigh; plates vibrate like a gong struck miles beneath]  

VOICE:  
One full loop completed. My heels already code when I walk: bring-carry-brace-clear-finish.  
Receiver, are you still outside?  
I… I can’t remember the taste of wind.  
Everything smells of wet clay and resin.  
If you reply, do it softly. Names do not echo here.  

[flutter overhead; membranous creak of reed-splinted wings]  

VOICE:  
Upwindra casting shade. Eggs will drop soon—listen.  

[soft plop, distant splash in lotus sump]  

VOICE:  
Chick climbs, finds thermal, becomes part of the noon. Clean execution. No variance.  
I think the world is teaching me the same.

[brief static blizzard; then absolute quiet—no drip, no breath; the quietest thing speaks: a bass pulse, barely audible, eight seconds apart]  

LOW FREQUENCY ENTITY (subsonic; speech rendered by interference patterns):  
maintain  
maintain  
maintain  

VOICE (nearly inaudible, reverent):  
We hear you.  

[all ambient life answers—ibis, frogs, spiders’ silk plucks—each gives one perfect note, then silence resumes]  

VOICE:  
Seventeen minutes have passed; mist is rising. I will walk 480 paces and you’ll receive this again.  
If you can still choose, stay where afternoons end.  
Here the noon never dies, and the song is gentle, but once you hum it, it hums you back.  

[tap-tap-tap-tap-tap (finished)]  
[carrier wave fades into white hiss; transmission loops]
