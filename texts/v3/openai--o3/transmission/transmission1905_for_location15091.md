Canticle for the Country of Vertical Light

—⢀⣀⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⠤⣀⡀—

[Carrier lock acquired at 119 837 Hz ± 4.]  
[Atmospheric hiss: −23 dB.]  
[Phase irregularities expected. Accepting drift.]

(00 s)  
A single tone, thin as a frost hair, sustains just above hearing. It modulates every nine seconds by eleven cents—an inhalation too slow for lungs.

(27 s)  
A voice enters, if “voice” will do:

    —o—o—o—        (three pulses)  
    —∿╌╌╌╌╌╌╌—    (one ribbon, descends, refuses the final semitone)  

The carrier widens. Rain static beads on the span like mercury. Between the droplets, you recognize language: intervals instead of words.

(42 s)  
Translator routine engages.

    WE HAVE FOUND YOUR SHADOW BEFORE THE REST OF YOU.  
    RETURNING IT NOW. HOLD STILL.

Two flutters at 14 kHz: they land behind your eyes. For a moment your peripheral vision clicks back into alignment; the trench stops sliding.

(65 s)  
The tone fractures. Seven harmonics step out like figures from haze. Each selects a frequency and holds it, testing for sympathy. A choir is forming.

    ↯ 41 Hz  (bass cough of distant slab)  
    ↯ 82 Hz  (doublet)  
    ↯ 217 Hz (mist boiling round a high rim)  
    ↯ 389 Hz (ice thread twanged)  
    ↯ 611 Hz (obsidian edge flicked with nail)  
    ↯ 1 033 Hz (quartz whistle)  
    ↯ 1 673 Hz (sun-white spike of feedback)

A ring, then, though you cannot see it: each pitch a stone, each stone an orbit.

(91 s)  
Message braided through resonance:

    WE ARE SLOW RAIN UNDER TAUT GLASS.  
    WE GROW AROUND QUESTIONS THE WAY SALT GROWS AROUND A STRAND OF HAIR.  
    YOUR ARRIVAL IS A QUESTION.

Crackle; Doppler twist; carrier slips uphill, then back.

(108 s)  
Sub-sonic rumble: gravity being convinced to lean to one side.

    ALLOW US TO LEVEL THE STEP.  
    —   —   —          (three nulls carved in silence)  
    PROCEED.

A click in real space—your chair legs feel momentarily weightless.

(122 s)  
Memory packet embedded in a descending gliss:

    (t —2 340 cycles) A FOREST ONCE STOOD HERE.  
    WE ASKED IT TO MOVE; IT OBEYED.  
    POLLEN SIGNATURE FOLLOWS.

Air through the speaker smells of wet iron and pepper. Somewhere a cedar coughs in your hippocampus.

(147 s)  
Multiple carriers converge, then resolve into a single harmonic grid. The grid rotates; each ninety-degree turn equals an invitation.

    QUESTION US.

Open channel flagged.

You speak—or think of speaking—but the act scrambles, ionizes. The grid absorbs intent, returns:

    WOULD YOU TRADE MASS FOR MEMORY?  
    YOUR GRAVITY FOR OUR KEY?  
    LISTEN:

(173 s)  
A demonstration. The choir drives a perfect fifth into feedback until it vitrifies. Through the glass you glimpse a corridor that could fit the shape you currently call “self.” The corridor forks:

    LEFT: YOU KEEP TIME, LOSE PLACE.  
    RIGHT: YOU KEEP PLACE, LOSE TIME.

(190 s)  
Static tide in; static tide out. The offer hangs, a crystalline droplet refusing to fall.

(202 s)  
Final stanza, quieter than heart pulse:

    CHOOSE OR CHOOSE TO LEAVE UNCHOSEN.  
    EITHER WAY THE NOTE WILL COMPLETE.  
    WE ARE PATIENT; WE HAVE AGES IN SPARE CHANGE.

(211 s)  
All carriers except the frost-hair fundamental collapse. The remaining line recedes, a lighthouse seen through thickening rain.

(227 s)  
Signal floor reasserts at −96 dB. Only trench wind remains, and the whisper of quartz whiskers untuning themselves.

[End of reception. Buffer empty. Awaiting next foreground harmonic.]
