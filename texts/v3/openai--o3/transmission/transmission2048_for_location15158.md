Borrowed Signal, 18th Condensation Cycle  

[open carrier – a hush like wind inside a metal lung]  

…silted evening, silted evening, do you copy…  
voice coil tastes of iron—adjust…  

                                                             —-------------------------  

First report:  
Pressure 98-point-nothing.  
Light braided through fog in three colors: rust, brass, and the soft white you find under scabs.  
All Operators accounted for but one; the rail-trestle sentinel called NORTH-KNEE is missing its middle leg and therefore is no longer NORTH nor quite KNEE. It dragged half a lap along the berm before folding into a listening-post. The hollow it left is filling with your absence.  

                                                             —-------------------------  

Second report:  
I set down a hammer to count the vapor swans. The hammer inhaled and forgot me.  
I am writing now in droplets strung between two wires; when the wind stills, the sentence is readable. When it moves, meaning rubs away like breath on glass.  
Please understand that nothing solid is safe for long. That includes memory, wrist-bones, and the name you used to answer to.

Third report:  
Substrate Minds request an error-check on the concept of downhill. They insist the steepest descent now flows through complexity, not altitude. If you solve their proof the water will reroute and you may leave by following the new braid.  
I attempted the first lemma; my chalk dissolved before the second line.  
Rain is making the same attempt on my skull.  

                                                             —-------------------------  

[burst of boiler-latin, half prayer, half pressure release]  

                                                             —-------------------------  

Field notes on fauna (revised):  
1. Glasshead beetle landed on the microphone grid. The gearwork inside it matched my heart rate for twelve beats, then accelerated, taking half of me with it. I feel lighter.  
2. Otter-pipe sighted at 11:40 local hush. It split into segments at the rumor of thunder far seaward. The segments wrote a question in ripple-script: “HOW LONG IS A HEAT?” I had no warmth to pay for an answer.  

                                                             —-------------------------  

Fourth report, partial:  
I have begun to echo before I speak. The future of my sentences arrives first, thin and cooled, and I step into them like waders into fog.  
If this reaches you, measure the delay between echo and utterance; divide by silence; return the quotient by dawn. The basin promises to square the remainder.  

                                                             —-------------------------  

Status of the Silted (eye-witness, unreliable):  
At 03:12 they walked the inside rim, nine shadows stitched from different grades of dusk. Each carried a syllable so quiet it bent light. As they passed, nearby lichens rewrote themselves into commas. I think the parade was punctuating the night.  

                                                             —-------------------------  

Fifth report / confession:  
The ferricolous film has reached my fingertips. The fizz is pleasant; I do not resist.  
Words leave rust when they scrape through the throat, and the film is hungry for letters.  
Soon I will speak only in magnetite, and then only in tilt.  

                                                             —-------------------------  

[carrier destabilizes; boiler-whistle harmonizes; sequence resembles breathing]  

                                                             —-------------------------  

Closing transmission (self-dissolving):  
Listen—  
should you arrive after I have cooled entirely, search the mud where mirror-water gathers under the lone willow stump. There you will find seven gear-teeth aligned like fallen constellations. Wind them once, gently; hold them to your ear.  
Whatever voice you hear is not mine, yet it will finish my sentence.  
Return it to the basin when it has spoken, and leave before the silence remembers your outline.  

Signal ends in rain.
