[Silence returns, but it is a living silence, threaded with the padded thump of belts, a pulse in the low hertz—tha-dum, tha-dum—as line-shafts idle. A microphone clicks. Someone is speaking, scarcely above a library voice. He never raises it.]

“Ledger-time 04.17, gloaming verge.  
This is Flow-Clerk Aster-Twelve, loft platform 2-G.  
Audit of channels 90 through 122 follows.  
All terraces beneath pivot ridge, stand ready to note.”

[Parchment rustles, a pen scratches once.]

“Item one.  
Echo Weir bleed increases to nine-point-three percent.  
Reason: prime bean-towers entering heavy drink.  
Stamp code: verdigris-on-slate.”  

[A sleeve brushes felt; a distant gate-valve turns—k-chk-chk—three measured clicks. Water answers with a new, higher hush.]

“Item two.  
Request received from Verdurer Silver-notch for sun-tilt of bed 44.  
Granting four-degree elevation to deck segment K-lattice at next lift cycle.  
Crane dispatch notified.  
Stamp code: willow-in-rain.”

[A second voice—alto, almost a whisper—fades up behind him, reading simultaneously in another channel; the two streams never collide in pitch.]

“Maintenance hail to hush-smith cadre:  
bearing drift detected in pump-heart 7-north;  
tone at forty-six hertz, amplitude three.  
Felt wraps already in route.  
Stamp code: candle-unlit.”

[A pause. The first clerk inhales; the condenser on the mic beads and drips. You hear the droplet land on metal with a chime like struck glass.]

“Item three—attention all galleries:  
Storm front sighted above twin peaks.  
Probability of unmanaged drop: thirteen percent and rising.  
Prepare cog-tooth weirs for test flex.  
Whistle law will sound three rising if threshold passes.”  

[Behind his words a trio of children pedals the Velocipede Circuit. Their chains make a rhythmic lace of clicks, never louder than the clerk’s breathing.]

“Revision to quiet covenant for Black-Quiet:  
Dynamos throttle to twenty-three percent, not twenty.  
Light will hold at garnet.  
Confirm receipt downstream.”

[A click. The alto voice returns, soft as moss.]

“Garden census:  
moss seam 12-east reports spore-bloom—  
colour: pale jade,  
coverage: eight decimeters advancing.  
Verdurers invited to witness at dawn.  
Stamp code: root-in-mist.”  

[Far away, an orphan engine ghosts awake—clunk … clunk … whirr—then sighs back to sleep as a hush-smith touches it with a tuning fork. The harmonic dies like a firefly light-out.]

[The clerk again:]

“Last: memorial entombment.  
Warden-Forebears file closure on line-shaft ‘Grandfather Hull.’  
Geartrain to be overclad at shift change.  
Rivet count eight-thousand-and-ninety-one; let the ledgers show.  
We commend its silence to our footing.  
Stamp code: ember-gone.”

[He says no more.  
A single heartbeat of open mic: just fog, water, the muted heart-throb of the Works.  
Then the whistle: one descending note—dawn-call issued early, an omen of rain.
