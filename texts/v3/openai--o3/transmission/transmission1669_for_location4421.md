Echo in the Teeth  

[Carrier lock acquired. Signal floor rises from hush to a thin flute-tone, steady at 218 Hz. Under it, distant clicking like porcelain pebbles poured slow. The receiver widens gain.]

breath is even            breath is even  
stone drinks     stone drinks  
—hush now, hush—  
vynn are working the northern runnel  
we count fifty-seven spatulas bright with lichen milk  
they scrape in time to the small drum under my tongue  

[Clicking intensifies, resolves into a six-beat cycle: k-k-k k-k-k      k-k-k k-k-k. A child’s voice drifts across.]

Yurri says the crabs have polished their backs so thin the sun looks through them like dawnglass  
Yurri is lying flat to watch their hearts               our mother scolds but not hard  
we learn a thing when we can see it pump  

[Signal jolts—sharp static pulse—then returns quieter, as if snow packed over the mic.]

listen: sky is stretching        feels tight as a drumhead  
wind has fallen to a thread  
the Body will breathe out soon  

my shutters are unpinned  
my roof-ribs wait like lips around a song  

bone-flute ready?             yes  
drum ready?                   yes  
ferment pit vented?           yes—sweet rot fogging my boots  

count the rules:  
one—quiet  
two—closer  
three—use what falls  

[Low hum grows, sub-audible rise to 27 Hz. Receiver rattles; frost-crackle sweeps the band.]

NOW  

[White roar. Microsecond strip of silence. Then a cataract of mixed sound: wind shearing, ice granules skittering, a chorus of chimes as icicles fracture. Through it, thin piping darts like swallows.]

▲▲▲    (three ascending notes)  
▼▼▲    (two down, one up)  
call and answer, call and answer—our part of the pact  

[Within the roar, new textures: serrated rasp of thousands of vynn spatulas; hiss of sublimation; a staccato rain that is not water but birds diving bead-first through stormskin.]

can you see them?                              no vision here, only ear  
then taste the pattern:                         sharp-sweet, sharp-sweet—the beer of phase change  
right—sky gifts vapor, vynn gift grout, serae give guano glare  

hold breath      three    heartbeats  
let ice rim wrists and lids  
count pulse in the teeth—do you feel the drum my marrow keeps?  

[Storm peak plateaus. Frequency spread flattens into broad pink hiss, slowly thinning to a lace of discrete tones. A final ping—like glass tapped with a fingernail—hangs, bends downward, fades.]

exhale  

[Silence. Very faint dripping starts, a choir of micro-metronomes.]

report losses:  
two shutters splintered        patched with hide already  
one sere chick blown from cradle—retrieved alive, warming in pocket  
no vynn overturned; their shells rode the gale like sleds  

tell the south rim cousins the Body’s thirst is slaked  
gutters run full; they may brew double tonight  

[Soft shuffle, as of fur against stone. Voice drops to almost a whisper.]

and now a story for the leaving static:  

before there were pillars we curled against bare teeth  
wind chewed us narrow, but we learned to chew back  
not with bites, with use  
we carried nothing out that the storm could want  
so the storm stopped wanting  

that is the shape of survival here  
a bowl in the spine of the world, turned upward  
fill it, pour it, fill it, pour it  
until even strangers like you  
 who listen through metal snow  
begin to keep the rhythm in their lungs  

[Finger taps on hollow bone—slow, deliberate:  _ta   ta-ta     ta_.]

message ends when drip line straightens  
watch the slush mirror; when the sun writes itself unbroken across it, cut the carrier  
we will speak again next thirst  

[Drips stagger, align, fall in synchronous cadence—then none.]

—⸺⸺⸺  

[Carrier tone lifts, thins, vanishes.]
