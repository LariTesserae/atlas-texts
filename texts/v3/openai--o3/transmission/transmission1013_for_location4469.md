[Carrier fades in from white noise.  
Frequency: 1284 kHz AM.  
Signal strength wavers between S2 and S5, as though a slow wind were passing between antenna and transmitter.]

  …sssshhhh—  sssshhhh—  ss—K-TOCK—sshhhhh—

A low turbine note steadies the carrier, three beats below middle C.  
Behind it, what might be rain, until you notice the drops arrive in strict, metronomic pairs.

  (First voice, male, half-whisper, half-steam-leak)  
  “Basin bulletin. Shift bell minus nine.  
  Fog depth: knee-high on the outer boards.  
  Drizzle sugar at twenty-two parts per million; tastes like brass, tests like water.  
  Bracelet calm.  
  Repeat—bracelet calm.”

[Clack of a relay. The background turbine drops a semitone; a second line opens.]

  …tik-tik-tik-tik…

[Frog voices—clear, metallic—answer the relay ticks on the off-beats, an octave higher:  
tik-CHIP-tik-CHIP-tik-CHIP.]

  (Second voice, child, amused)  
  “Foundry frogs in tune tonight. Crucible Nine must be cooling sweet.”

  (First voice, returning)  
  “Copy that. Nine’s copper load at red-to-orange, no slag bloom.  
  Torch count stands at eighty-three singers, six silent.  
  Tool moss shows mint-green sheen, uniform.  
  All lathes pass.”

[Static gusts through—dry, papery—as though pages were being turned close to the microphone. When it settles, you hear code tapped on resonant metal:  
• — ••  • • •  — — ••  
It repeats, slower, then falls quiet.]

  (Third voice, very soft, inside-echo)  
  “Boiler vows rehearsed—one ring, whispered.  
  Couple requests hush clearance at 03:11.  
  Will quench burners for seven breaths.”

  (First voice, approving)  
  “Noted. Steam reserve adequate; engines may idle.  
  Let the line meristem.”

[A rush of overlapping leaf-sounds answers—no breeze, only wood adjusting around metal. A creak like a distant hull, warm and content.]

  (Unidentified murmur—many voices layered, as though spoken through hollow trunks)  
  “…accept… accept… accept…”

[Carrier flutters. A hand microphone scrapes. Someone exhales deliberately; you can count the seconds—one… two… all the way to ten—before the breath dissipates.]

  (Second voice, more serious now)  
  “Edge report: slinker prints east quadrant, seven strides apart, claw-marks blunt.  
  Engines kept talking; beasts kept listening.  
  No breach.”

  (First voice)  
  “Hold idle cadence.  
  Brass lights to half-filament.  
  Show them we are awake.”

[A soft chime, almost like glass tapped with a fingernail; then quiet—deep, pond-bottom quiet.]

  (Whisper, perhaps the fog itself)  
  “We count you.  
  532 warm cores, steady.  
  Six million edge-bright tools, chirring.  
  147 lungs of wood, breathing.  
  One cloud, wandering whole.  
  All present.”

[Static returns, heavier, but shot through with faint harmonic overtones—sap-engines syncing to grid. As the noise thickens you catch a final phrase, syllables stretched by the detuning carrier:]

  “—circle small, world entire—  
  —listen for the drip that answers—  
  —end of pulse, never of song—”

[The turbine note slides downward until it is indistinguishable from the static.  
Carrier extinguishes. Silence, save the memory of condensation landing, once, twice, somewhere very far inside the set.]
