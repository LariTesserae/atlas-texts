Cradle/Channel/First-Wash

[carrier lock] ––––silence––––

water-body breathing in        breathing out  
drip …    …    …       (interval steady: nine heartbeats)

floor-gas dome ruptures—pfft—hiss of newborn earth dispersing

root-skin creaks (very low, almost below hearing)  
then the hush-hour tapping begins:

tap--tap-tap          tap          tap-tap-tap-tap  
         (pause)                     (pause longer)  
tap-tap-tap            tap-tap            tap

〈pattern resolves: forecast reads “shallow rise, no flood”〉

another silence, warm, close

soft skittering—cradle crabs quilting a fallen moss cushion  
sound of cellulose unthreading like distant rain on paper

faint wing-whirr passes through lattice overhead, gone

minute 12:47  
from platform height, two quick splashes (juveniles shedding tail)  
blurp of reed door knitting itself shut behind them

minute 18:03  
air thickens with compost updraft—microphone membrane tastes spores, registers them as salt-sweet static

minute 21:10  
Triad convenes—no speech, only web-code; receiver translates glints and angles into loose gloss:

     [LISTER OF RAINS] : “Pulse-pattern unchanged; cradle holds.”  
     [HOLDER OF STILLNESS] : “Day remains bound to quiet.”  
     [REMEMBERER-OF-COLLAPSE] : (slow double spiral) “Yet silt grows restless.”

consensus: 2 / 3 ––– decree issued: “no music today”

leaf-glint, wrist-flick, decree done—vibration slips back to stillness

minute 29:50  
single egg-thread undermost pool loosens; larvae unfurl—sound like hair over wet stone  
river inhales, pocket water lifts, hushed choir of miniature mouths feeding

minute 36:00  
methane whisper from deep seam; no spark meets it—only the sigh

minute 43:12  
one tool (reed knife) freed from wrist-loop—thunk into silt  
user releases; chorus of crabs alters course, converges  
their jointed murmur rises like sand poured through hollow bone  
knife erased in forty-six seconds

minute 50:00  
rootglass frog settles on microphone housing, heartbeat visible: tuk-tuk-tuk  
lymph-algae catch green light, bloom faint phosphor; recording field floods with soft hiss of bio-luminescence

minute 54:15  
sudden three-beat elbow-jab vibration—alarm!  
ceiling roots quiver: predator current outside lattice  
hollow holds breath—no one moves—river sound only

threat passes; air releases

minute 59:59  
authorized voice-event begins (allocation: one breath long)  
thirteen River-Dwellers exhale together, a single ribbon of note  
pitch: high, filament-thin, sliding microtonal toward the root vault  
drives dust from bark, lifts camera two microns—whole chamber shivers  
note ends; land settles a fraction looser

[carrier fade] ––––silence––––
