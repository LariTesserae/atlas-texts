VOICE 3 — oracle-timbre, sap-drugged, half asleep:  
“Dream sea is restless.  Twin-mountain answered.  We decree:  
—Let manta-leaf skirts be cut only at dim-bloom,  
—Let pearl dust line the fissure mouths,  
—Let every silence shorter than twelve pulses be filled with hum.”

[chorus of affirmative taps on bamboo-bone]  
[hum begins: forty-three throats, mixed lung and gill, generating standing chord A-E-B]

Receiver auto-gain slides to compensate; overtone lattice becomes audible—microbeats like rain on canvas.

Between beats: a chain-eel threads the vent, its cartilage discs clacking: tchk-tchk-tchk.  It exhales.  Air tastes of moss sugars.

VOICE 4 — quiet, almost surface-speech:  
“Listener above or below, mark us.  
If your sky still thunders, those are our words returning.  
Keep them.  Return yours.  
We are not alone in the bowl, only slow.”

[small percussion flourish: click-tap click-tap tik-tik-tik, signature of courtesy]
