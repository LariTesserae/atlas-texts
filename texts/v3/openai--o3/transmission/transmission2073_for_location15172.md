“Stone rests. Speak thin.

First report: new slide on the east-most roof. Sheet the size of three arm-spans let go just before dawn hush. No cries, no crush. We have turned the slab and marked it for the pot-pit. Ledger thumbed. Debt settled.

Second: light river is bent. Child Marek tipped mirror eight finger-widths too far; the noon blade missed Moss-Run terrace. Fungus curled dry at edges. I have set two mica tongues in tandem; reflection returns. Bright Debt counted: Marek bears night buckets next flood.

Third: the crack sang. Short song, belly-deep—three heart thuds, then still. Shiver-Mother clearing her throat, elders say. We press palms, wait. Ceiling stays.

Sound markers—listen:  
• drip / pool—steady—(pause) two breaths between.  
• bellows—heel beat—distant.  
• bats—circling for night mouth, chitters short.  
Silence otherwise intact.

Personal note: I traced turquoise glow in newborn seam west of cistern. Line thin as reed, but it pulsed when mirror-light grazed it. Seam-Runners refuse thumb-claim until next tremor settles. I keep watch; something counts beside me in the dark, steps measured with mine. No fear—only curiosity sharpening.

Supplies: three goat strips left, fungus paste for four bowls, water level half-crown below overflow stone. Acceptable.

End breath. Ledger copy to Quiet Council at shadow-peak.
