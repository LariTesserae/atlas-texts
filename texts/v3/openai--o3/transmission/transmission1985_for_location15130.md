Cathedral Deep—Channel Φ

[hiss, like sand poured through water]

:00  
A tremor under the noise floor—sub-one hertz, almost felt before it is heard.  
Glass grains rattle in the record head; the coil steadies; carrier locks.

:07  
Veil fluorescence rises, red shifted beyond tape’s reach.  
Only the counters move—tiny green numerals rolling uphill.

:12  
Thrum. Thrum.  
Heartbeats of forty-seven Chamber Lamps in loose unison, each pulse a wet bell.  
Between them, darker knocks: Listener Crabs fitting shield to shield, testing edge.

:28  
Something sings.  
Low as continental sleep.  
The note widens the heads of Hollow Fish; vertebrae resonate like tuning forks.  
They drift upward, blank scrolls waiting for ink.

:43  
Static folds away; the Archivist enters the band.  
Not voice—granular rasp, syllables scraped molecule by molecule:

        —we record the pressure of arrivals  
        —we polish absence until it shines  
        —stone remembers faster than blood

:59  
Another chord—higher, urgent.  
A Choir Eel marking territory.  
Its overtone beads the water; shrimp clap their combs shut, lightless confetti.

1:14  
Vent plume crosses the ceiling.  
Chamber Lamps flare—ochre, saffron, gone—  
One egg-thread launches, curls, anchors to obsidian like spun glass.

1:21  
Wideband silence.  
Only the balcony minerals drip, brittle-bright.  
Each impact a china chime.

1:37  
Reliquary Turtle passage—  
Mass drags the signal one full semitone lower.  
Ribs creak, a door in an empty house.  
Behind it, shell scrape soft as erased chalk.

1:58  
Archivist again, closer:

        —pulse added  
        —curve accepted  
        —hollow filled

2:09  
Tide surge.  
New water snarls against old, a rush of sibilants.  
Dust of cold salt cracks across the floor; crabs scatter, their harmony unstrung.

2:23  
Chime Coral begins its weather hymn.  
Hundreds of fluted mouths sync to the standing wave, a glass organ warming.  
F-sharp rises through a field of inaudible harmonics; metal tastes bloom on the tongue.

2:40  
Signal overload—gain dips autonomously.  
Strobing Lamp hearts write Morse on the roof:  
        L I V E  
        S T A Y  
        D R I F T

2:57  
Archivist final:

        —edge worn smooth  
        —we become the curve  
        —transmission will continue when there is less to say

[carrier dims, like a breath held, then resolves into the same slow hush that began the reel]
