TRAVEL DOSSIER  
Destination: The Alt Noctis Escarpment / City of Lacheses  
Traveller profile assumed: baseline Terran-derived physiology, civilian skill-set, single-person excursion of ≤180 days.

────────────────────────────────
1. Paperwork & Timing
────────────────────────────────
• Access writ: The Archivists issue “Threads of Intent,” a slim ceramic tab that records why you are visiting. State your aim in ≤64 words; they dislike vagueness but despise prolixity even more. Average processing time is 7–11 local weeks, so file in advance through a relay embassy (the Duarq Consulate on Hesperis Station responds fastest).  
• Arrival window: Avoid Year-50 “Whale Fall” (synchronized spawning). Next event in system year 214.56; no visitor permits issued for the four months surrounding it.  
• Quarantine pledge: You must certify zero macro-plant spores onboard; xylophytic biology is considered invasive. Expect swab tests at both ends.  
• Insurance: Make sure your multiverse policy covers “low-visibility volcanic glass laceration, compressive micro-wells, and non-lethal resonant barotrauma.” Most standard plans do not—buy a rider.

────────────────────────────────
2. Condition Your Body & Mind
────────────────────────────────
Altitude & Atmosphere  
• Air pressure equals the summit of Mt. Everest but humidity sits at 98 %. Train with hypobaric / hyper-humid drills; learn pursed-lip breathing to reduce moisture loss.  
• Ambient temp hovers –10 °C to +2 °C with rapid micro-flashes of colder wind. Cold-soak conditioning and Raynaud’s mitigation drugs recommended.  
Noise & Darkness  
• Spend at least two weeks in a sensory-saturation chamber: constant low-frequency drone + dim violet strobe. This lessens vertigo and insomnia on-site.  
• Practice silent thought exercises (e.g., eight-breath mnemonic loops) to stay mentally coherent when “there is no room for a private thought.”

────────────────────────────────
3. Personal Equipment
────────────────────────────────
3.1 Garment Stack
• Base layer: hydrophobic aerogel mesh; prevents sweat from condensing and freezing in fabric.  
• Cut-skin: flexible basalt-fiber weave (rated to shrug off obsidian edges); gloves integrate ceramic micro-plates on palm and fingertips for grip.  
• Shell: phase-change outer suit, matte black to avoid light pollution; micro-IR cells trickle-charge handhelds.  
• Boots: magnetically switchable gecko tread. Keep the magnetic function OFF inside civic gravimetric strands or you’ll pinball.  
• Helm/hood: transparent aluminum visor with anti-abrasion coating; clip-on vapor-filter cartridges (spec: 0.1 µm volcanic particulate, Fe++ ions).

3.2 Sensory Gear
• Bone-conduction ear-caps with variable dampening: block >120 dB flutes yet pass sub-20 Hz whale heartbeats (critical for navigation).  
• Low-lux lidar monocle; emits near-IR pulses invisible to locals. Range 25 m—enough for corridors.  
• Direction bead: a wrist-cuff that vibrates toward the city’s north-pointing tensile spine (like their magnetic tattoos).

3.3 Mobility Aids
• Collapsible grav-pike (1 m): anchor point for riding public “micro-wells.” Emits gentle gradient, then folds.  
• Obsidian scribe: thumb-sized; etches safe foothold runes on glass if you must leave marked walkways. Replace tip often.

3.4 Life-Support & Meds
• Fluid ration: ironically dehydration kills first. Carry a 2 L thin-film lungsac that condenses ambient vapor via exothermic mesh—no bulk canteens.  
• Chelation tabs (EDTA variant) for chronic metal uptake; dose every 48 h.  
• Haemostatic dust for razor cuts; works despite humidity.  
• Broad-spectrum antifungal—yes, you’ll meet mainly chemotrophs, but traveller biomes tend to grow opportunists in the dark.

────────────────────────────────
4. Getting There
────────────────────────────────
• Most visitors arrive by dirigible shuttle from the equatorial space elevator. Approach vector must stay 300 m above the plateau until you sight indigo pulses—dense hydrogen bodies (gargoyle whales) cruise that space, and collision insurance is pointless.  
• Touch-down pads are themselves basalt columns tipped with smart-gel. Shuttles settle; gel stiffens. Do not step off until a custodian ticks your “Pressure Sings Steady” icon green. The stone’s internal stress makes sudden cracks a daily risk.

────────────────────────────────
5. First 24 Hours: Acclimation Protocol
────────────────────────────────
1. Proceed to a Moisture-Harvest Lung (zone code M-cyan).  
2. Strip outer shell; local custodians flash-sterilize it with UV-C; takes three minutes—don’t wander off.  
3. Accept complimentary resonance band (ankle) that tracks your skeletal vibrational signature. Without it auto-doors remain closed.  
4. Sleep-cycle: you’ll be assigned a cocoon no more than 55 cm wide. Embrace it; claustrophobia fades by day three.  
5. Teach your visor to the city’s “heartbeat cadence” (72 s loop). Sync ensures hall lighting blooms when you enter—otherwise pitch-black.

────────────────────────────────
6. Moving About
────────────────────────────────
• “Left yields to up.” People floating in vertical strands always have right-of-way—breach etiquette once and corridor walls will literally squeeze you back.  
• Keep one hand free. Locals expect you to brace or salute lattice nodes when passing; gyro-stability ritual shows respect.  
• Never shine visible light into a capillary wall—photosynthesis is absent, but the film-eater mats interpret sudden photons as solar flare and spasm, damaging city sensors.

────────────────────────────────
7. Social Navigation
────────────────────────────────
Archivists  
• Speak slowly. They process conversation the way they process legislation: after every sentence they may pause 10–30 s “to let the basalt cool.” Interrupting is tantamount to wiping a library.  
• Gifts: present a single well-curated memory image (holo or printed) rather than an object. Visual archives are prized; matter is clutter.

Custodians  
• Problem-solvers grown from programmable matter themselves—half tool, half citizen. Be polite; their moods shift with water-pressure gradients. If a custodian glints orange, step back—it’s rerouting trioxis water and is effectively “deaf.”

General Populace  
• Eye contact is uncommon; instead locals touch the wall with two fingertips to greet. Mimicry appreciated but sanitize fingers first.

────────────────────────────────
8. Hazards & Counter-Hazards
────────────────────────────────
1. Glass Laceration: treat with haemostatic dust → chelation tab → report cut to nearest wall node; city logs micro-blood as foreign bio-marker.  
2. Pressure Flute Event: frequency drills fillings loose. Bite down on tongue to vent sinus resonance, then seek “Quiet Loop” alcove (always three turns clockwise from any major corridor).  
3. Rogue Vortex Outside: crouch, embed grav-pike; let vortex pass overhead. DO NOT jump—the air current will loft you into whale lanes.  
4. Shard-Crawler Swarm: sounds like muffled applause. Stand still. Moving feet crunch their excreta, interpreted as threat; stillness calms them within 40 s.

────────────────────────────────
9. Currency & Trade
────────────────────────────────
• Transactions are denominated in “Seconds of Glow,” a time credit representing how long you may draw extra power for personal devices. Exchange rate hovers 1 Glow-Second : 30 sec of off-world standard labor.  
• Bring encrypted energy chits; physical coinage useless.  
• Tipping is unknown— instead, gift a stored decibel (recorded silence from other worlds). Residents relish comparative quiet as collectible art.

────────────────────────────────
10. Departure & Decontamination
────────────────────────────────
• Schedule exit 48 h prior; Lacheses rewrites corridor alignments nightly—shuttle pads migrate.  
• Full dermal exfoliation required; embedded volcanic dust triggers false structural-fatigue alarms on other stations.  
• Resonance band returned; city wipes your vibrational signature after 30 days unless you petition for a “Long Echo” (permanent visa).  
• Customs will scan luggage for unauthorized chemotroph cultures; fines are epoch-length—don’t risk smuggling silver bio-film.
