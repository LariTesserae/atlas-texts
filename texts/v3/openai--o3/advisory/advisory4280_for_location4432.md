Field Guide: Living Fissure under the Rainless Desert
(working‐name “Seed-Grotto 0.0”)

STATUS
Category: Actively growing, self-repairing biolith cavern
Threat Level for a healthy adult: Moderate (physiological drift, entrapment, disorientation)
Scientific/archaeological value: Extreme
Recommended dwell-time: 3–6 surface hours per excursion

––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
1. PHYSICAL PRE-DEPLOYMENT
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
A. Clothing
• Ultralight tropical expedition kit: loose, long-sleeved, UV-reflective.  
• Fabric must be snag-resistant yet sacrificial; cellulose-digestion acids etch slowly, so plan to discard garments after mission.  
• Non-absorbent under-layers (silk, synthetic spider-weave) to reduce chill once you exit the 98 % RH pocket.

B. Footwear
• Soft but puncture-proof soles (vibram nitrile, sharkskin pattern) for continual slick/basal debris interchange.  
• Low profile crampon micro-studs help on sharp fracture edges yet will not pierce plant skin.

C. Hands & Skin
• Chemically inert gloves (viton or laminated polyethylene) – root exudates are mild on flesh but dissolve natural rubber overnight.  
• Spare pair kept vacuum-packed in case first set overgrows.

D. Head & Respiration
• Half-mask P3 or full-face nanofiber respirator; pollen is narcotic, not toxic, but dulls edge thinking in minutes.  
• Clip-in micro-cooler mouthpiece condenses your breath so you exhale less water (slows liana attraction).

E. Vision
• No external lamps needed; goggles with photo-neutralizing filters restore true contrast (biolum spectrum overwhelms red-green cones).  
• Anti-fog essential at 38–39 °C, 98 % RH.

F. Instruments
• All electronics IP-68 or better, resin-potted boards.  
• Li-ceramic power cells tolerate 40 °C continuously.  
• Passive magnetic compass useless—iron poor rock; use inertial rig + ultrasonic rangefinder for mapping.  
• Prepare for memory-hole terrain: plant heals marks, so rely on non-contact beacons (ultrawave or quantum entangle tags).

G. Tools
• Ceramic or titanium only. Steel vanishes under biocorrosion in <24 h.  
• Collapsible pitons/struts (titanium) to brace exit fissure; roots will begin infiltrating in ≤3 h.

2. NAVIGATION & TEMPORAL DISCIPLINE
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
Golden rule: Keep moving or keep talking.

Scheduling
• Buddy check every 5 minutes verbal.  
• Hard turnaround when internal chrono hits H+3 h 00 m unless exploring team has external evac portal.  
• Absolute surface recall by H+6 h; statistical risk of exit “healing shut” climbs from 4 % to 17 % per subsequent hour.

Pathfinding
• Stick to fracture corridors that still show bare basalt grey; once a wall blurs green-amber, it will close behind you within the hour.  
• Do not trust breadcrumbs, chalk, or laser scorches; tissue reabsorbs.  
• Plant-safe beacons: 40 kHz ultrasonic pings every 30 s; roots ignore frequencies above 25 kHz.

Verticality
• Terraces step 3–7 m; take anchored line rather than trunk-flute ladders (resin rungs may auto-detach under outsider weight).

Stationary risk
• Heat + breath moisture triggers chemotropism; lianas begin clasp test by 30 s, full ankling at 8–10 min, envelopment at 50 min.  
• If snared, a rapid 10 % saline spray forces release (plants programmed to shun hypertonic zones).

3. ENVIRONMENTAL & HEALTH HAZARDS
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
Pollen sedation
• Symptom chain: colour oversaturation → mild euphoria → executive-function slide → voluntary stillness.  
• Counter: caffeinoid chew tabs every 45 min; respirator always on when mask discipline fails.

Dehydration & Hyperthermia
• You will sweat even in 98 % RH and not notice. Minimum 1 L electrolyte per 2 h.  
• Do not drink pool water; equal-pressure equilibrium hides high dissolved sugars and weak acids—osmotic purge guaranteed.

Surface chill shock
• 40 °C → desert night 5 °C. Carry windproof shell in exfil cache.

Cut & abrasion
• Basalt flakes are microscalpels. Treat nicks instantly—plant sap can seal to blood plasma and you’ll carry home a living scab.

Bioincorporation
• Any item left >24 h becomes substrate. If you must cache, use Teflon-sheathed lockers suspended on paracord.

4. ETHICS & CONTAMINATION PROTOCOL
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
• Take nothing living unless licensed by three ethics boards; the ecosystem runs on perfect balance—removal equals wound.  
• Kill no tissue unnecessarily; trauma triggers area-wide repair surge, complicating navigation for everyone.  
• Zero external bioburden on exit: full positive-pressure airlock, UV-C bath, then sub-zero drywash to shred clinging spores.  
• Post-mission medical follow-up at 24 h and 7 d for latent photosynthetic symbionts.

5. PSYCHOLOGICAL CONSIDERATIONS
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
• The silence plus constant amber glow induces “floating midnight” disassociation.  
• Rotate conversational duty every 10 min; music leaks encourage echo locating but keep volume ≤50 dB to avoid stimulating trunk-flute tissue (it resonates).  
• Keep personal timers visible—no circadian cues.

6. EXIT STRATEGIES
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
Plan A: Continuous line-haul—static rope from mouth of fissure to furthest push, reeled back on ascent.

Plan B: Portal/teleport node calibrated to titanium pitons: place two within 30 m of each other in bare-rock indent so conversion delay >6 h.

Emergency Plan: If exits seal, ascend a trunk-flute until GPS scrubbers reacquire surface lock (<30 m overhead in most chambers) and punch through; expect gush of humid air and rapid wilting cascade—evac in 5 min before collapse.
