Field Manual: Visiting Platform A-4 (“Bronze-Water Seed Fort”)

VERSION 3.2      Cleared for ordinary human visitors (non-augmented, multiverse general public).  
Compiled by the Itinerant Liminality Society, Hydrovaults Department.

————————————————————————————————
0. FIRST QUESTION: SHOULD YOU GO?
————————————————————————————————
Go only if you are prepared to be an uninvited mammal in a place that was designed to exclude mammals altogether.  A-4 is beautiful and slow, but it is also indifferent to animal biography.  The facility’s defensive nanotech will not try to kill you, only to fold you into sleep and compost you later.  That indifference is the single greatest risk.

If you still want to go, read everything below, twice.

————————————————————————————————
1. CORE FACTS AT A GLANCE
————————————————————————————————
Altitude (above local crust): ~4 610 m  
Ambient medium: warm (35 ± 3 °C) saline-bronze water; density 1.11 g cm-3  
Ambient pressure: ≈1.1 atm (you are “high”, not “deep”)  
Lighting: omnidirectional, ~9 000 lux, peak at 560 nm  
Gravity: standard 1 g, downwards; platform is buoyant  
Dominant hazards:  
  • Neural-pattern detectors that trigger “Quiet-Fog” inhibitor nanites  
  • Autonomous pruning blades in maintenance corridors  
  • Almost complete acoustic silence → easy to lose orientation or companions  
Comms: radio useless; acoustic pings (6–10 kHz) propagate well  
Native biology: hyper-engineered plants only; zero fauna by policy  
Legal status: abandoned, unclaimed, no active sovereignty

2. PHYSICAL PREPARATION
————————————————————————————————
2.1 Suit
• Use a thin-wall warm-water immersion suit, not a pressure suit.  
  – Material: woven graphene/silk or equivalent, 1 mm.  
  – External hex-texture helps defeat the Quiet-Fog’s neural scanning (see 4.1).  
• Incorporate a rebreather mouthpiece with 4-hour scrub canister.  
  – Air pockets large enough to stand in do not exist; you must carry your own mix.  
  – Mouthpiece only is preferable to a full face mask; you will want peripheral vision.  
• Integral fins or detachable flipper boots; water viscosity is 12 % higher than Earth seawater.  
• Positive buoyancy +1 kg; you will climb anchor cables at the end and don’t want to sink.

2.2 Propulsion & Navigation
• Hand-mounted pusher paddles or a 100 W electric shrouded thruster.  
  – No prop noise >60 dB; the platform classifies sudden loud acoustic spikes as “structural breach” and may seal bulkheads.  
• Strap-on inertial nav puck (IMU + depth + acoustic ranging). Metal compasses useless: the ceramic alloy is ferromagnetically clean and offers no reference.

2.3 Tools
• Monomolecular filament snips: in case vines trap you or you must liberate a hatch.  
• Soft silicone wedges (8 × ) for propping open irises; they close silently behind you.  
• 30 m of 2 mm dyneema guideline with reflective tracer; corridors spiral indefinitely and are feature-poor.  
• Flexible, 20-k lumen stick-light.  The built-in lamps dim to 5 % during pruning events; have your own.

2.4 Medical & Biochem
• One ampoule anti-nanite chelator “Quill-X” (250 mg zinc-DMPS).  Intramuscular.  
  – Temporarily gums the Quiet-Fog’s catalytic cores for ≈20 min: your emergency window.  
• Broad-spectrum antibiotics: the water carries novel plasmid-swapped bacteria though none are overtly pathogenic; play safe.  
• Copper-chelating mouthwash; the water tastes like old pennies and will irritate mucosa.

2.5 Conditioning
• Practice slow-motion swimming; every gesture you make in viscous water is half speed.  
• Meditative breath discipline: the silence is so absolute that your own exhale may panic you.  
• Visualisation drill: memorise cross-sections of a tapering cone; once inside you have no horizon cues.

3. CONTAMINATION PROTOCOL
————————————————————————————————
Platform A-4’s gene printers constantly prototype plant strains.  Outside microorganisms hitchhiking on you could ruin millennia of baseline data.

Pre-entry scrub:
1. Hot shower, neutral pH detergent, no perfumes.  
2. Quick-desiccation, then step into a misting booth that lays down a 5-µm siloxane barrier coat; cures in 30 s.  The coat sloughs off after 8 h.

Do not bring:
• Companion animals. (Sensors will knock them out.)  
• Loose snacks; crumbs = foreign organics.  
• Leather, untreated wool, or anything that ever grew on an animal.

4. DEALING WITH THE FACILITY’S DEFENCE LAYERS
————————————————————————————————
4.1 Quiet-Fog Nanites
Range: 15 m.  Trigger: brain-pattern complexity above cephalopod-grade.  
Effect: within 30 s, profound drowsiness → unconsciousness for 3 h; lungs continue to breathe water, but rebreather fails from flooding.

Evasion strategies:
A. Passive masking: the hex-texture suit plus *consistent, repetitive thought loops* (“circle-square-triangle” mantra) keeps neural entropy low.  
B. Proximity cloaking: stay within 2 m of an active maintenance blade rack; the rack’s EM chatter blinds the scanners.  Risky but works.  
C. Software override: some travelers carry the 13-bit handshake “PRUNER-CYCLE-BETA”; broadcast three slow acoustic beeps (1.2 kHz).  Success rate 60 %; use only if other methods fail.

4.2 Pruning Blades
• Trigger when chlorophyll opacity in corridor >30 %.  A human body can accidentally raise that reading.  
• When you hear lamp arrays dim, freeze against the wall.  Blades extend, sweep once, retract.  Total event 11 s.

4.3 Bulkhead Iris Doors
• Will not recognize you as a scheduled entity; they default to polite open/close if obstruction moves slower than 0.4 m s-1.  Sprinting causes slam-shut.

5. ROUTE PLAN (RECOMMENDED)
————————————————————————————————
Ingress:
1. Approach at mid-rim aperture between buttresses 17 and 18 (western quadrant) – historically lowest incidence of clogging vines.  
2. Insert silicone wedge after it irises open.  
3. Follow right-hand wall downward for two full revolutions of the spiral.  At the third halo light-band, look left for a latticed service shaft heading inward to the central atrium.  
4. Atrium depth marker: floor diameter ≈12 m, overhead turbine veins visible.  This is the visual “heart” of A-4—most photogenic spot.

Key micro-environments:
• Moss Galleries (depth 7 m below rim): electric green floor carpet, warm updrafts, safe to pause and remove mouthpiece if you brought a micro-air-bubble hood.  
• Fabrication Niche K-9 (depth 18 m): view transparent digesters turning old fronds into polymer bricks.  Stay behind the chevron line—fluid intake ports create strong suction.  
• Up-Rain Lantern Walk (upper galleries, just below rim): schedule visit during desal purge (every 4 h 36 m).  You will watch fresh water “fall” upward.  Bring video.

Egress:
Take anchor cable #2 (marked with black-and-white banding).  Grip with bare or rubber-plated gloves; ceramic surface is glassy.  Ascend ~60 m until water becomes mist, then step through haze curtain into airspace.  Deploy autogyro chute or ring-balloon to reach your extraction craft hovering above the brine-fog.

6. SOCIAL & ETHICAL NOTES
————————————————————————————————
• Do not clip samples.  The platform’s genetic audit logs every missing gram and may adjust growth algorithms across the entire dome net-work, upsetting centuries of data.  
• Leave no personal acoustic tags; the soundscape is deliberately near-zero.  
• Expect no help: there are no administrators, only clockwork.  If something goes wrong you rescue yourself or no one does.  
• Photographs are permissible; flash is unnecessary and risks scaring the seed-pod dispersal valves into lockdown.

9. DECOMPRESSION & AFTERCARE
————————————————————————————————
None needed physiologically (no high pressure), but give your inner ear an hour to readapt; spatial disorientation lingers.  Rinse suit and gear in EDTA bath to strip polysaccharide film—if it dries it sets like lacquer.  Log any contact with nanite clouds; medics will run a ferritin panel and, if levels high, another chelator dose within 12 h.

11. FINAL ADVISORY
————————————————————————————————
Platform A-4 is a monument to patience: a machine-garden thinking in decades, not minutes.  Match its tempo.  Move slowly, speak rarely, watch how light bleeds back from every leaf.  If you feel drowsy, that is the Quiet-Fog tasting your brain.  Think simpler thoughts, turn back.  Above all, remember that you are the strangest animal it has seen in centuries—and it can do without you.  Behave accordingly, and the drowned cathedral will let you share its warm, bronze silence for an afternoon before returning to its endless, vegetal meditation.
