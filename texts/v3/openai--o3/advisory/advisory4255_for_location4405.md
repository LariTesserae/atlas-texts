1. Entry Permissions & Paperwork
• Authority of Record: The Caldera Fabrication Cooperative (CFC).  
• Required documents:

  – Bio-inert Certificate for clothing and carried items (issued by any CFC-licensed autoclave hub; expect a quick plasma fumigation on arrival even if you have one).

• Slot reservation: Elevator-cage cycles every 42 min. Reserve at least 18 h ahead; freight has priority.

2. Health & Physiology
Altitude: 2 km is modest, but interior humidity runs 95–100 %.  
• Spend a night at base camp (650 m) if you come from sea level; mild acetazolamide recommended if you know you acclimatise slowly.  
• Hyper-humid air impairs sweat evaporation; dress for wet heat, not mountain chill. Core temperature management is more important than warmth.

Allergies: The canopy emits engineered terpenes (smell of “copper sap and thunderstorm”). Rarely allergenic but can irritate asthmatics—carry rescue inhaler.  
Biological contamination: You must not shed hair, skin flakes, or microbes in quantity. Shower within 2 h of ascent; apply the provided probiotic barrier spray (smells like green tea).

3. Clothing & Personal Gear
Allowed fabrics: tightly-woven synthetics, silicone-impregnated leather. NO wool, cotton, feathers, down, or untreated wood (Rule 1: nothing natural touches foundation).  
Recommended kit:  
• Breathable rain-shell with hydrophobic nano-coat—heavy condensation will drench you otherwise.  
• Non-slip composite boots; floor gratings and quartz weeping-walls are slick.  
• Low-lumens headlamp (amber): tungsten house-lights dim to “copper dusk” at night; white LEDs upset the pollinator moths.  
• Soft case for electronics; stray droplets fall constantly from the roof lattice.  
• Pocket towel or chamois to wipe optics.  
• Ear protection optional: average sound 40 dB, but pressure-relief sighs spike to 90 dB for 3–4 s.

Forbidden items: loose soil, seeds, untreated water, chewing gum, paper maps (use polymer film instead). Drones will confiscate such on sight.

4. Etiquette & Safety Protocols
Stay on printed walk-tiles—sections grow outward; a pale teal tint on the floor means resin still curing (temperature 70 °C).  
Never block gutters. If your footgear dislodges a leaf or shard, pick it up—an attendant drone will direct you to a reclaim hatch.  
Quiet hours: officially 21:00–06:00 local; conversational voices only in designated “chat alcoves” (marked by amber moth-symbols).  
Photography: permitted, but disable flash; tungsten frequency is deliberately plant-tuned.  
Do not feed or handle sapphire moths; each carries a genome-tag worth more than your ticket.  
If you hear triple chimes, an extruder rail is about to traverse the aisle—hug the nearest pillar until it passes.  
Emergency egress: follow blue capillary lines in floor to the nearest lift core; they double as indicator lights.

5. Way-Finding Highlights
A. Western Parapet. Primary panorama; stand beneath aperture 7-C for the “milk-cloud” vista. Safe rail height: 1.3 m.  
B. Sintered Quartz Wall. Sip from spillway #3; water is potable, faint citrus-ozone flavour. Two-mouthful limit to preserve mineral balance.  
C. Nutrient Sump Gallery. Peer into bronze-glass basins—micro-turbines visible; flashes mean magnesium recapture is active.  
D. Night Cycle. Stay until 20 min after official sunset to watch the leaf-fold algorithm: tiers of vegetation contract by exactly 8 % span, channeling dew into stem reservoirs.

6. What It Feels Like
Touch: air as warm and dense as greenhouse breath; steel handrails surprisingly cool—mountain shell still curing.  
Sound: Think percussion on sheet-metal, whale sighs, and the hush of a late library.  
Smell: Resin curing, wet stone, electric rain.  
Sight: Matte black superstructure framing luminous greens; droplets catch tungsten light like slow sparks.

7. Potential Hazards
• Slips: Condensation plus smooth composites = ice-rink friction. Move with deliberate steps.  
• Thermal steam risers: Spiral philodendrons hide them; steam is low-pressure but 90 °C. Keep forearms clear.  
• Uncured resin outgassing: Can cause mild dizziness; if you taste bitter almond, notify a steward drone—they’ll reroute you.  
• Altitude vertigo: Parapets are safe, but cloud gaps can disorient. Fix gaze on interior columns if dizzy.

8. Souvenirs & Departure
Nothing organic may leave with you. Approved keepsakes: recycled alloy token (vends at lift lobby), photopolymer leaf replica, 30 ml vial of “weeping wall” water (mineral-stabilised). Your boots will be solvent-washed before descent; factor 15 min.

9. Optimal Visit Duration & Timing
Three to five hours covers main galleries plus one full irrigation-reclaim cycle. For atmosphere, arrive 90 min before dusk to experience both full light and the copper-dim night mode.
