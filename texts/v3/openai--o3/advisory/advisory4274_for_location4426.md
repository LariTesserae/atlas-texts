Field Briefing  
Destination: The Obsidian Pocket Foundry (“thumb-fissure biome”), summit ridge of the newborn glass plateau, ∼6 km a.s.l. in a low-pressure basaltic world.

STATUS AT A GLANCE
• Access aperture: 14–18 mm across, irregular, self-sealing a few micrometres per year.  
• Internal temperature: 430 – 510 °C (just below copper’s forging range).  
• Atmospheric pressure: 0.35 – 0.4 bar, O₂ ≤ 2 %, CO₂/SO₂ ≈ 7 %.  
• Humidity: effectively 0 %; H₂O flash-boils on entry.  
• Illumination: < 0.2 lux except for piezoelectric flashes.  
• Acoustic intensity: 115–130 dB SPL, continuous, broad-band metallic.  
• Biosphere: closed, fully lithotrophic; total free volume ≈ 12 m³.  
• Conservation rating: Class-Ω (finite, non-renewable heat budget; any disturbance can be terminal).

1. CAN A NORMAL-SIZED HUMAN PHYSICALLY GO INSIDE?  
No—unless you have size-alteration or phasing technology. The throat of the fissure is narrower than a man’s thumb and lined with razor crystal. Conventional enlargement by chipping is prohibited: one hammer strike would flood the ecosystem with cold air and instantly kill most life-forms. Plan on remote, miniaturised, or non-corporeal visitation. The rest of this brief assumes you are using a suitcase-grade telepresence probe or a body-compression rig that shrinks you below 10 mm girth.

2. PRIMARY HAZARDS  
Heat: 450 °C air and hotter surfaces; radiant load ~20 kW m-². All electronics must be ceramic-substrate or sapphire-enclosed. Active phase-change cooling is mandatory; plan for 600 W steady draw just to keep a camerasuite under 90 °C.  
Dryness & steam explosions: one fingernail-size drop of water becomes a 400-fold gas expansion event. Do not bring hydrated food, wet batteries, aqueous coolant, or even a damp breath.  
Atmospheric chemistry: at 0.4 bar you could gasp for a few seconds, but the SO₂ will strip lung tissue. Keep the probe hermetic.  
Sound: 120 dB inside a narrow stone chamber is enough to fracture human ossicles in minutes. A remote avatar should run acoustic dampers; if you must miniaturise yourself, bring bone-conduction plugs rated ≥ 45 dB NRR.  
Mechanical: Falling glass panes and slow-moving but razor-edged fauna can slice suit seals. Sonic vibration also works mounting screws loose over hours—use threaded-locking compounds rated for > 400 °C.

3. EQUIPMENT CHECKLIST

For a suitcase telepresence pod (recommended):
• Heat-flux-reciprocating heat sink (lithium or sodium phase-change) with external radiators that deploy outside the fissure.  
• Exolite or ZrO₂ ceramic chassis.  
• Triple-band audio array (10 Hz–50 kHz) with adaptive notch filters so you can “hear” communications without clipping.  
• Short-wave IR lidar for navigation in near-dark. Visible-light cams will saturate at every spark.  
• Infrasound buffer to cancel part of the 120 dB field and avoid feedback destroying your microphones.  
• Micro-thrusters or mag-crawl feet coated with diamond-like carbon for traction on glassy walls.  
• Chemical laminate that out-gasses argon, not water vapour, when heated.

For an actual shrunken walk-in:
• Mk-9 Forgemaster suit (enviro-rating 700 °C, 0.3 bar) or local equivalent.  
• Closed-loop perfluorocarbon breathing mix with molecular sieve scrubbers (no condensers!).  
• Vibro-cancel helmet suspension.  
• Containment pouches lined with Kalrez if you plan to collect molten-metal “ingot” discard (strict export quotas; see §9).

Getting to the fissure mouth:
• The plateau summit is knife-edge obsidian. Use vacuum-rated boots with hard tungsten carbide micro-spikes.  
• Ambient air temp outside is a crisp -20 °C; sudden 500 °C jump inside the pocket creates thermal shock—design suit layers to span both.

4. ENTRY PROTOCOL

Step 1: Benchmark soundscape. Before breaching, press a throat-mic flush with the aperture for 90 s. The fauna use harmonic occupancy as territory; sampling lets your system generate a spectral “mask” you can play back in antiphase, making your presence acoustically invisible.

Step 2: Bleed-in. Inject argon until internal and external pressures equalise (± 0.02 bar) to prevent a gust that might stir dust and smother spark-midges.

Step 3: Advance slowly—4 cm s-¹ is the maximum you can move without triggering avoidance reflexes in the heat-leeches. Anything faster is read as catastrophic cooling rubble.

5. ETIQUETTE & ETHICS

• Silence is aggression. Your probe/suit must hum in tune with the existing 98–102 Hz base note. Out-of-key machinery will draw heat-leeches which respond like immune cells.  
• Emit no visible white light. Use 1.55 µm IR only; shorter wavelengths shift some piezo crystals into photofracture.  
• Do not block convection plumes—the chamber’s thermal budget is balanced to millikelvin. Even your shadow of cooler gas can starve a patch of glass-gnawers.  
• Sampling quota: ≤ 1 g total material, never taken from an organised ingot pile. Those are emergent structural capital.  
• Time cap: 47 min of cumulative presence per 24 h local, or the chamber will lose ~3 % of its heat through your cooling system.

6. WHAT TO LOOK FOR

• The “ratchet hymn”: every 14 min the slag-heavers synchronise a bar-stack, pitching the drone up 11 cents for 30 s. If you catch it, record—it’s equivalent to a mating display.  
• Spark-midge constellation events: stomping on a feldspar blade (gently) produces a bloom reminiscent of sheet lightning.  
• Heat-leeches occasionally vent rings that make standing waves on the chamber wall—perfect for Schlieren videography.

7. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

Thermal runaway (suit temp > 650 °C): deploy sub-aether recall tether or abort to the unpressurised summit wind—rapid chilling will crack outer suit layers but you’ll survive.  
Suit puncture: Immediately seal with pre-cured ceramic patch; haemorrhaged water will kill fauna and likely drop you on a Class-Ω violation list.  
Sonic disorientation: If the drone suddenly drops below 90 Hz, chamber heat is collapsing. Evacuate within 120 s; after that the environment will hit glass transition and entomb everything in solid basalt.

8. SOUVENIR / RESEARCH VALUE

• The near-pure Cr-Ni ingots are self-alloyed at grain sizes unattainable in most universes—perfect for quantum tunnelling contacts.  
• Piezo-feldspar sheets exhibit 33 % higher charge build than Earth’s best tourmaline.  
Both are embargoed for commercial sale; academic allotment only.

9. EXIT & RESTORATION

On exit, reseal the fissure with its original argon mix at 500 °C to avoid convective heat loss. Leave a resonant beacon outside broadcasting a safety frequency so the next traveler syncs to the same acoustic key and cumulative impact stays minimal.

10. ALTERNATIVES FOR THE SQUEAMISH

• Synesthetic audio-topography mapping from the ridge lets you “hear” the entire biome from outside—risk-free.  
• A full volumetric capture recorded by prior expeditions can be streamed in haptic VR; 4-hour data pack, zero conservation impact.

FINAL CALL

Visiting the Obsidian Pocket Foundry is less like spelunking and more like crawling inside a living blast furnace whose organs are gears. Come perfectly dry, perfectly insulated, and perfectly in tune—or don’t come at all.
