Field Briefing for Human Travelers  
Destination: The Endless Forebay  
Revision: 2.8 • Compiled by the Pan-Cosmic Wayfarer’s Cooperative (PCWC)  
Clearance: General Civilian (no military embargoes)  

0. TL;DR FOR IMPATIENT HOPPERS  
• Waterproof everything.  
• Bring ear protection that leaves your hands free.  
• Expect to be wet, luminous, mildly magnetized, and missing a few memories when you leave.  
• Keep at least one local organism alive inside your gear—or inside you—if you want doors to open.  

1. IMMIGRATION & ENTRY CHANNELS  
Portals and quantum-tunnel ferries materialize on hard infrastructure only. Ask your carrier to drop you on a “landing clast,” a hexagonal basalt pad shot-peened once an hour to keep it from glazing slick. Do NOT request atmospheric insertion; up-draft turbulence regularly shreds parachutes and jets alike.  

Customs Formalities  
• Visas are biometric, keyed to your lung flora; you’ll inhale the registration packet at the gate.  
• 3-minute quarantine lets local nanofauna scan your genome for incompatible prions. Positives are rare, but deportation is immediate and irreversible.  
• Declare any objects made of untreated wood or plant fibers; they dissolve here within days and the mush clogs municipal pumps. Fines are steep.  

2. VACCINES, ADAPTIVE GEAR & BODY MODS  
Noise: Expect 100–130 dB as ambient. Standard silicone earplugs are useless; buy a clavicle-clip bone-conduction damper (brand “Listen-Less” or better). It cancels exterior sound while feeding you speech and alerts sub-audibly through bone.  

Water: Chemically aggressive—pH 3.5–5.0, high silica, trace metals. Upgrade any implants, phones, or watches to IP-X10 acid resistance. Carry two pairs of shoes:  
• Basalt claws: gecko-treaded sandals for exposed rock.  
• Hydro-socks: knee-high neoprene for wading.  

Atmosphere: Warm (27–35 °C), nearly 100 % humidity, laced with conductive aerosol. A light hydrophobic poncho works; heavy coats will mildew before nightfall.  

Symbiote Swarm Inoculation: Breathing the internet is optional but practically necessary. Clinics near every portal provide a canister of “seed swarm” nanoparticles. You inhale; they settle in alveoli. Rejection rate: 0.2 %. Carry the antiserum ampoule for the first 48 h. Swarm provides:  
• Citywide data link at 2 kbps (text + low-res image)  
• Automatic haptic map overlays (felt, not seen)  
• Authentication pheromone so doors recognize you  

3. CURRENCY & ECONOMICS  
The Forebay runs on a tri-ledger:  
A. Joule-credits (JC): energy quotas pulled from the orbital siphons.  
B. Memory Tithes (MT): encrypted mnemonic bundles, 5–300 s each. You can voluntarily pledge. Outsiders usually sell travel trivia.  
C. Physical barter: mostly high-carbon treats (coffee beans, chocolate, timber) worth their mass in platinum here.  

Tip: Open a Joule wallet at the gate; 1,000 JC will cover a week’s modest tourism. MT exchanges pay well—but remember you can’t retrieve the sold recollections without buying them back. Label your memories before pledging; nothing wrecks a trip like forgetting your native language.  

4. NAVIGATION & TRANSIT  
By Foot: Short hops only. Surfaces switch from liquid to elastic to rock unexpectedly. If the paving ripples, stand still—an under-street pump is about to exhale.  

Ribbon Trams: Amphibious, loud, every seven minutes. Tickets are biometric; your lung swarm pays automatically in JC. Sit in the mid-cars if you dislike sudden submersions; fore and aft cars sometimes dive to clear traffic.  

Quantum Sluiceways: Freight only. People have tried riding them. People arrived scrambled. Do not.  

Skip-Lifts: Vertical catapults powered by momentum siphons. Good for getting onto upper viaducts. Secure loose organs (ears equalize late; chew gum).  

5. LODGING  
Capsule Convents: Cheapest; communal memory caching is mandatory (one random 30-s experience nightly). Shared bathrooms flush upward—brace.  

Resonance Inns: Mid-range, built around giant tuning-fork pylons that cancel outside roar to a tolerable 60 dB. Usually include a pet voucher (see §7).  

Basalt Grotto Suites: Luxury; carved downward into dry vesicles of the flood basalt. Silent, cool, private. Nightly rate is two kilograms of coffee beans or equivalent credits.  

6. FOOD & WATER  
Locals digest silicon-rich biofilms; you won’t. Import networks stock conventional protein bricks, but they taste like wet gypsum. Bring flavor concentrates in sealed vials.  

Safe native options:  
• Ferric crab marrow—high in iron, resembles lobster bisque.  
• Condensate noodles—hydrogel threads extruded by vesicle colonies; neutral flavor, soak in broth.  
• Vapor kite honeydew—staticky, sweet, numbs tongue.  

Potable water? None in the wild. Carry a chelation filter straw (rated for pH 4). Better: buy boiled condensate from street vendors; look for the blue steam (means the pot’s titanium-lined).  

7. COMPANION ORGANISMS (“PETS”)  
Adopting one is culturally polite and functionally shrewd—many doors, lifts, and vehicular seats read shared biochemistry to prioritize occupancy. Options:  

a) Resonance Eel Bracelet  
• Lives in a saline bangle on your wrist.  
• Glows brighter the closer you are to high-pressure drains—useful during flash floods.  
• Feed: dilute electrolytes once a day.  

b) Ferric Crablet  
• Palm-sized juvenile; magnetic feet cling to clothing.  
• Mines rust particles from your gear, so acts as mild corrosion control.  
• Keep away from laptops—they love eating micro-spindles.  

c) Whisper Pump Mite  
• Invisible to naked eye.  
• Lives in your external ear, balancing pressure changes—natural earplugs.  
• Usually included free in Resonance-Inn welcome kits.  

Travelers on short visas often pick the eel; it doubles as a night-light in dark service corridors.  

8. WEATHER & PERSONAL SAFETY  
Flash-flood Protocol  
1. Hear subharmonic boom = 60 s window.  
2. Elevate to any surface displaying yellow chevrons; these float automatically.  
3. If caught knee-deep, stamp feet; the pavement should stiffen. If it doesn’t, you’re on a pedestrian grade—look for rope rails and pull yourself along while swimming.  

Lightning Storms  
All metal surfaces are grounded. You, however, are not. Attach the anti-static ankle spool provided at customs; trailing ribbon bleeds charge.  

Disappearing Infrastructure  
Lime-green shimmer = scheduled deletion. Vacate the highlighted zone within 180 s. Everything behind the shimmer will disassemble into construction foam and be vacuumed away. Nobody is exempt; the city’s legal AI accepts only coordinates, not excuses.  

9. SOCIAL CUSTOMS & COMMUNICATION  
Speech Volume: Locals shout but the filters make it feel normal. You, fresh off the shuttle, will shout too loudly. Use hand signs (simple ASL variants).  

Greetings: Present your adopted organism for inspection. People enjoy cooing at eel bracelets or letting crablets scrape their thumbnails. No pet, no friendship.  

Punctuality: Absolute. With dynamic terrain, missing a meeting slot can mean the room no longer exists. Arrive five minutes early and slap any wall panel; it flashes green if the venue’s still instantiated.  

Memory Tithe Discussions: Highly personal. Do not ask strangers what they’ve recently surrendered.  

10. HEALTH & MEDICAL FACILITIES  
• Humidity-induced skin sloughing: carry zinc oxide cream.  
• Acid rash: rinse with buffered saline (pH 7.2).  
• Symbiote Overload: symptoms—tinny echo in lungs, metallic taste, rapid recall of irrelevant trivia. Solution—visit a De-Noise bar; they magnetically skim excess swarm.  
• Hospitals: look for heliotrope-lit domes; triage priority is granted to those hosting Forebay organisms. Another reason to keep your pet healthy.  

11. PERSONAL TECH TIPS  
• Optical gear fogs instantly. Apply nano-silica anti-fog every morning.  
• Power: everything charges inductively from ground resonance—just set devices on any public slab. Non-ferrous casing recommended; iron tweaks reception.  
• Data: the lung swarm provides text. For video, rent a “Throat Lozenge”—a one-hour exocache you swallow. It records to your esophageal lining and uploads later. Gross but effective.  

12. “MUST-SEE” EXPERIENCES  
• 0400 Flood Rehearsal: Watch arcology sectors simulate total immersion; interiors fill with water then spin-drain through ceiling cyclones. Tour stands behind sapphire glass. Bring ear dampers.  
• Midnight Self-Shatter: Certain towers collapse into glitter, reform by dawn. The crash out-thunders anything short of a rocket launch.  
• Sunset Over Viaduct Ribs: The only quiet moment (relative—90 dB). Silicate biofilms flare magenta, turning puddles into liquid stained glass.  

13. EXITING THE FOREBAY  
Symbiote Off-Boarding: Optional; many travelers keep theirs for mesh access elsewhere. If you choose removal, a chelation mist will precipitate them from your lungs in minutes, lightly peppering the floor with glitter. Sweep it into the provided urn; the city recycles swarm matter.  

Memory Reconciliation: Use your entry voucher to buy back any tithes you regret—prices spike in peak season. Unclaimed memories get auctioned; some tourists later find their honeymoon recollections starring in local soap operas.  

De-Magnetize: Ferric crab exposure leaves residual magnetism. Walk through a de-gauss arch or your credit cards will be toast in the next universe.  

14. SOUVENIRS  
Legal to export:  
• Quartz fiber scarves (flexible, sparkle rainbows).  
• Decommissioned pump whisper vanes—double as wind chimes.  
• Bottled resonance eel sparks (phosphorescent water).  

Illegal: live vapor kites, active phase-change scaffold splinters, un-titled memories of civic infrastructure layout.  

15. FINAL ADVICE  
The Endless Forebay overwhelms every sense, redefines “wet,” and treats reality like a drafting sketch—erasures expected. Accept that you will lose orientation, punctuality, possibly a memory or two. You will gain an eel that glows when you sing, a personal thunder soundtrack, and a new respect for infrastructure the size of continents.  

Pack light. Think fast. Stay loud. Welcome to forever at ankle-depth.
