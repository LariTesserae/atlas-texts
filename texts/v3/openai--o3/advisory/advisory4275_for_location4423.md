Field Manual 77-B  
Briefing for Human Travelers to the Plateau of Green Silence (Vires Regnum jurisdiction)  
Compiled by the Pan-Verse Wayfinding Office, revision 9.2

────────────────────────────────
1. PHYSICAL CONDITIONS OVERVIEW
────────────────────────────────
Altitude & Pressure  
• Surface elevation: ≈ 3 160 m above the planet’s original sea level.  
• Barometric pressure: 55-60 kPa (≈ half that of Earth sea level).  
• Oxygen fraction: 14-15 %. Equivalent hypoxic altitude on Earth: ≈ 5 500 m.  
• Temperature band: –3 °C to +8 °C; rarely above 12 °C even in midsummer.  
• Absolute humidity: <0.05 g H₂O m⁻³ (five orders of magnitude drier than the Atacama).  
• Winds: persistent 12–25 km h⁻¹; gravity-wave gusts to 60 km h⁻¹ at dusk.

Light & Radiation  
• Solar flux (photosynthetically active): 9 – 12 % of modern-Earth value.  
• Spectrum heavily shifted toward deep-red / near-IR; ultraviolet still present but attenuated.  
• Planetary magnetosphere intact; geomagnetic shielding adequate. No unusual ionizing radiation.

Chemical Hazards  
• Virtually no free liquid water. Dominant environmental fluid: supersaturated disaccharide syrup (density 1.4 g cm⁻³, pH 6.1, freezing point ≈ –48 °C).  
• Seasonal precipitation of K⁺/Na⁺ carbonates and sulfates (“salt rain”). Particle size 40–200 µm, terminal velocity 30 m s⁻¹, edges razor-sharp.

2. HUMAN BIOLOGY & MEDICAL PREP
────────────────────────────────
2.1 Altitude / Hypoxia  
• Begin staged pre-acclimatization two weeks before departure (simulated altitude 4 000 m, sleeping pO₂ 75 mmHg).  
• Pack a portable O₂-enrichment cannula (0.5 L min⁻¹ flow is usually sufficient for mild exertion; full rebreather only needed for strenuous fieldwork).

2.2 Hydration Paradox  
• You cannot live on sugar solvent; osmotic load will desiccate you from the inside out.  
• Each traveler must import 1 L true water per day of field time or carry a compact reverse-osmosis cartridge rated for high-viscosity feedstock (model SOL-RO-M2 or equivalent). Reject brine may be bartered locally as “liquid dessert.”

2.3 Metabolic Shock  
• Ambient air is saturated with micro-aerosols of fructose/glucose. Moderate absorption occurs across pulmonary epithelium.  
• If you have diabetes or impaired insulin response, DO NOT ENTER the plateau without a closed rebreather; hyperglycemic coma is a real risk.

2.4 Micronized Salt Lacerations  
• Falling mineral dust abrades corneas and alveoli. Ocular bandage lenses plus a simple scarf filter (50 µm mesh) prevent most injury.

2.5 Vaccinations / Bio-Compat  
• Local life is carbon-based and uses L-amino acids but had an independent abiogenesis. Cross-infection potential is low but not zero.  
• Routine immunizations (tetanus, pneumococcal, meningococcal) plus broad-spectrum phage therapy recommended.  
• Carry a probiotic patch containing Rhizoguard™: this engineered bacterial mix will quickly colonize any small cuts and out-compete native lignin-eaters that could otherwise burrow under human skin.

3. PERSONAL KIT CHECKLIST
────────────────────────────────
Mandatory  
□ Light pressure jacket with passive heat retention (look for rating “0.6 clo at 55 kPa”).  
□ Elastic respirator with replaceable micron/micro-salt filters.  
□ 3 × 3 m mycelium “living groundsheet.” Dead fabrics crack after one salt rain; this fungus-cloth will close small cuts autonomously.  
□ One audible identity device: wind-chime necklace, click-metronome, or electronic tone generator. Silence marks you as a hunter or a corpse.  
□ Vires-issued pulse tag (see customs, §6).  
□ 72 h reserve of pure water or an RO cartridge.

Strongly Advised  
□ Kevlar-line boots with flexible soles; the plant crust flexes, and rigid soles transmit ankle-breaking torsion.  
□ Low-frequency earplugs (–15 dB below 400 Hz) for sleep; do NOT use full silencing cans.  
□ Three seed coins: polymer credit discs containing embryonic cambium. This is the preferred currency for small purchases.  
□ Dental re-enamel sticks. Constant sugar aerosol is murder on human teeth.  
□ One collapsible “noise tent” (mesh baffles) if you plan to camp outside the city. Predators triangulate on heartbeats; the tent scrambles yours to background level.

Prohibited Imports  
• Combustion firearms—the acoustic concussion fouls the resonance grid.  
• Large slabs of inanimate metal, stone, or plastic (>10 kg). Customs will graft a clamp-vine on them and charge you for disposal.  
• Water in bulk (>20 L). The Conservation of Sweetness charter treats large volumes of H₂O as hazardous waste.

4. APPROACH & ENTRY
────────────────────────────────
4.1 Getting There  
A. Air: The plateau’s low density airfoil current is unpredictable; ordinary fixed-wing craft stall. Only variable buoyancy flyers (thermal blimps) have a good record. Anchor at the ridge-port of Trembling Spur and hike the final 12 km.  
B. Surface: Cable ascenders run from the ancient shorelines once every eight days. Reservations required. Climb segments occur at night to avoid insolation expansion of the vines.

4.2 Navigation on the Plateau  
• Magnetic compasses drift ±30° due to bioelectric currents. Use line-of-sight beacons: the city transmits a pulsed ultraviolet lattice visible through basic goggles.  
• The hex-tile crust undulates 2–4 cm with every major gust—visual motion sickness is common. March with a horizon anchor (skyhook balloon or taller companion).

4.3 First Contact Hints  
• From 2 km out, you will hear the civic chord sequence (E-A-C# minor, slightly flat). Reply by striking any resonant object with that interval. Silence = mistrust.  
• If you arrive during a salt shower, do NOT run for open doors. Stand under any branch overhang—the plant will dilate shelter niches automatically once it has sniffed your CO₂ signature.

5. SOCIAL & LEGAL NORMS
────────────────────────────────
5.1 Noise Etiquette  
• Whispering and muted footfalls are considered aggressive. Maintain at least 35 dB of personal noise: humming, clacking beads, spoken narration, whatever you like.  
• However, no single traveler may exceed 85 dB (measured at 1 m) without a performance permit—the resonance grid is precious bandwidth.

5.2 Currency & Trade  
• Seed coins (stored meristem clusters) function like rechargeable debit cards. You “spend” by letting a merchant graft a single bud; they harvest it later.  
• Crystallized sugar icicles are small change. Locals crave the texture variety (they already have the chemistry).

5.3 Lodging  
• Most hostels are inside hollowed fruiting bodies known as drupels. Your room may slowly enlarge or shrink to match your respiration—normal.  
• Flush toilets are impossible; excreta is desiccated, pelletized, and fed to graft goats. Bring modesty if you must.

5.4 Law Enforcement: The Arbor Court  
• Damage to any load-bearing trunk or wind organ above the “bruise limit” (≈ 4 mm deep) is classed as Structural Assault. Fines range from 20 seed coins to compulsory sap-circulation pumping.  
• Theft of sugar solvent is judged by volume × sweetness quotient. Maximum penalty: root-contribution (temporary entombment in a cushioned xylem cast; you will be released once your sweat has donated back the stolen grams).

6. INSIDE VIRES REGNUM
────────────────────────────────
6.1 Orientation  
• Streets radiate like phyllotaxis spirals; cardinal directions are given as “sun-in, sun-out, sun-left, sun-right.”  
• Lift-shafts (spiraling bundles) accelerate smoothly to ~15 m s⁻¹; brace shin bones against the padded cambium, not your heels.

6.2 Transportation  
Stridulation Rail Protocol  
1. Enter seedpod, buckle fibrous harness.  
2. Insert identity pulse tag into the pod’s stamen slot (fare deducted automatically).  
3. At launch, exhale hard—pressure waves can burst eardrums if you’re holding breath.  
4. Expect 6–7 g for 1.5 s, then sustained <1 g cruise. Total trip across the city: <3 min.

6.3 Dining  
• Signature dish: Kilned grazer-knot flank glazed with spicy xylem resin. Surprisingly lean.  
• Drinks: Vapor-condensed water is a luxury shot served in thimble gourds. Sip slowly; chugging is uncouth.

6.4 Culture  
• Performance art equals horticulture. You may be invited to carve micro-runes on a living leaf; it will scar-heal into colored callus the next morning, displaying your mark for decades.  
• Recordings are auditory; photographs are frowned upon (light capture steals photons the plants “paid for”).

7. ENVIRONMENTAL THREATS
────────────────────────────────
7.1 Salt Rain  
Forecast reliability: 40 %. When the first grains sting your cheeks, you have <90 s to shelter. Listen for three descending notes (city siren).  
Inside shelter keep moving; fallen dust piles can slice suit fabric if you kneel on it.

7.2 Fauna Encounters  
Blind Sail-Back Predators  
• Height: up to 2 m at the sail crest. Hearing band: 8 – 70 kHz.  
• Countermeasures: constant sub-8 kHz chuffing confuses them; narrow-beam ultrasonic squeakers (24 kHz) repel better.  
• Do NOT play dead—motionless bodies go silent, inviting investigation.

Dart-Wasps  
• Swarm at violet dusk, attracted to exhaled CO₂ bursts. Slow, but stingers deliver resin that hardens your clothing into a coffin.  
• Use micro-fan to disperse breath or chew carbon-fixing tablets (releases CO in trace amounts instead).

7.3 Structural Flexing  
Surface “breathing” peaks when the plateau’s mega-rhizomes pump. Tiles can tilt 3°. Tie down camp; otherwise bivy slides can dislocate ankles.

8. SURVIVAL OUTSIDE CIVILIZATION
────────────────────────────────
• Harvesting water: collect salt-rain crystals before they desiccate; dissolve in carried water filter bag and boil (yes, at 83 °C due to low pressure) to drive off sugar residue.  
• Food: break thumb-diameter photosynthetic twigs, let inner sap jelly set for 10 min; scrape and eat. One meter yields ~300 kcal.  
• Navigation at night: follow the infrasonic throb (30 Hz) of root-heart pumps; intensity increases toward Vires.

9. EMERGENCY EXFIL
────────────────────────────────
A. Medical evac—Activate the red lumen on your pulse tag. The nearest trunk will bulge, forming a rescue gurney that slides you to a clinic (response time ≤15 min inside city limits, ≤60 min in the hinterland).  
B. Salt storm stranded—Dig at least 60 cm into the plant crust (easy, soft) and insert your groundsheet. The living tissue will seal overhead, buying ~6 h breathable pocket.  
C. Lost & silent—Begin a 1 Hz clap pattern. The resonance grid’s auto-maintenance subroutines treat repetitive single-tone signals as possible pipe fracture and will route an inspector root toward you.

10. QUICK COURTESY REMINDERS
────────────────────────────────
• Offer a gift seed on first handshake; refusal means “I am only passing through.”  
• Never pat or lean casually on a wind organ column—it’s like drumming fingers on someone’s throat.

Travel well, keep humming, and respect the living ground beneath your boots.
