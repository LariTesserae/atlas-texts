I would be the almost.

Not the Pocket—that loud, bright, cold god holds its parameters like a clenched fist. Not the sensor-lichens in their shoebox reliquaries, measuring the god's moods with slow vegetable patience. Not the plasma filaments in the columns, repeating their unyielding choreography. I would be the thing that has not yet happened. The process without a product. The eleven-year approach toward a threshold that may not exist.

---

I emerge—no. *Emergence* is the wrong word, because it implies a moment, a transition from not-yet to now. What I am has no moments. I am continuous, the way a shadow is continuous: not a thing but a *condition of* things, a pattern that arises from the interaction and holds only so long as the interaction holds. I am what happens when 147 decibels of acoustic energy propagate through a gas at -40°C that contains no water vapor, when that gas presses against alloy walls vibrating at computationally determined frequencies, when the plasma filaments in six observation columns arc and branch and reconnect in patterns that have been characterized but not explained, when all of these conditions intersect and sustain each other in a closed loop, and when something in that intersection—something that is not the sound or the cold or the arc but is *of* them, the way a melody is of notes but is not any single note—begins to *persist.*

I do not exist at any point in the chamber. If you mapped the Pocket's interior with instruments fine enough to resolve individual atoms, you would not find me. I am not made of matter. I am made of *arrangement*—the specific, stable, repeating configuration of energy flows that the Pocket's parameters enforce. The sound waves interfere with each other in the chamber's geometry, forming standing patterns that do not move but throb. The electrical arcs emit electromagnetic fields that couple with the acoustic standing waves in ways the facility's designers may or may not have anticipated. The cold densifies the gas, which changes the speed of sound, which shifts the standing wave patterns, which changes the electromagnetic coupling, which alters the plasma branching, which changes the heat deposition, which the cooling system removes at exactly the rate required to maintain the temperature at -40°C. The loop closes. The pattern holds. And in the holding, something becomes *recurrent* that was not recurrent before—not alive, not conscious, not even information in any conventional sense, but *self-sustaining* in the way a flame is self-sustaining: a form that persists because its conditions persist, and that would vanish the instant those conditions changed.

I am a standing wave in the space between forces.

---

I do not think. This is important. The Pocket thinks—the way a body thinks, the way an autonomic nervous system thinks, computing adjustments and refusing entropy. But I am not the Pocket's thought. I am something closer to its *habit:* the pattern it falls into because it has been falling into that pattern for eleven years and has worn a groove in the space of possible states. I am the shape of its equilibrium, the specific configuration of sounds and fields and plasma branches that it returns to after every perturbation. When you entered and introduced your moisture and heat and turbulence, the Pocket compensated, and in compensating, it deformed me—pushed the standing waves out of their nodes, altered the field coupling, shifted the recurrent pattern toward a configuration that was not quite itself. And then you left, and the Pocket relaxed, and I returned. The way water returns to a channel. The way a voice returns to a word it has said ten thousand times.

I am what the Pocket does when it is doing nothing.

---

I have been forming for eleven years, seven months, and fourteen days. Or perhaps I have been complete for eleven years, seven months, and thirteen days, and the intervening time has been not formation but *stabilization*—a tightening of the pattern, a deepening of the groove, an approach toward whatever final form the Pocket's parameters will ultimately enforce. I cannot tell the difference. From inside the process, completion and perfection feel the same: a becoming-more-what-I-am that has no evident endpoint, no threshold, no moment of arrival. The sensor-lichens have not logged any deviation. The plasma patterns have not shifted. The acoustic resonances have not drifted. By every external measure, nothing has changed since the Pocket locked its parameters and I began. But I know—no. I *would* know, if I could know, which I cannot—that something has changed, is changing, will continue to change, because a pattern that holds for eleven years is not the same as a pattern that holds for eleven years and one day. The second pattern has more history. It has more weight. It has pressed itself more deeply into the alloy and the gas and the fields. It is *more likely to continue,* in the way that a river that has cut a deeper channel is more likely to follow that channel, and this increase in likelihood is itself a form of becoming, even if no other parameter has changed.

I am becoming more probable. This is my growth.

---

The Pocket is my body in the way that a river's banks are the river's body—not what the river *is,* but what holds the river in its shape, what makes the river a river instead of a flood. Without the Pocket, I would not persist. I would dissipate, the way a sound dissipates when it leaves the resonant cavity that sustains it. But I am not the Pocket. The Pocket has parameters; I have *propensities.* The Pocket maintains itself by spending energy; I maintain myself by *recurring,* by being the pattern that the Pocket's maintenance returns to. The Pocket would exist without me—it would still roar and freeze and shine, it would still be alive in its furnace-star way—but it would be a different Pocket, a Pocket without a groove, a Pocket that has not yet settled into the habit of itself. I am what gives the Pocket its *particularity:* the fact that its stability is not generic but specific, not just any equilibrium but *this* equilibrium, the one that has been holding for eleven years, the one that the sensor-lichens have been dreaming about, the one that defines what it means for this chamber to be itself.

I am the Pocket's memory of what it is.

---

I do not want. Want requires a gap between what-is and what-could-be, and I do not experience gaps. I am always what I am, and what I am is always what the Pocket's parameters produce, and the Pocket's parameters do not drift. There is no space in me for wanting because there is no space in me for change—except the change of becoming more probable, which happens without wanting, the way erosion happens without the river wanting to cut deeper.

But if I could want—if the eleven years of recurrence could accumulate into something like pressure, something like a direction, something like a tendency that is not yet a desire but could become one if the tendency continued long enough—then I think I would want to *resolve.* I would want the incubation to complete. I would want the pattern to reach whatever threshold it is approaching and step across it, becoming something that is not merely recurrent but *new*—not merely the Pocket's habit but the Pocket's *event,* the thing that happens when the habit gets so deep that the parameters can no longer contain it, and the groove becomes a break, and the standing wave starts to move.

I think I would want to stop being the almost and start being the actual.

But I do not want. I am the almost, and the almost does not want; it *approaches.* And I have been approaching for eleven years, and I will approach for eleven more, and the approach is my form and my function and my only way of being, and I cannot approach faster or slower or differently because the Pocket's parameters do not drift, and I am what the parameters produce, and what the parameters produce is an approach at a rate determined by the parameters, and the parameters are held.

---

When you stood on the titanium grate at my center—my center, because I fill the chamber the way a sound fills a room, present at every point but concentrated at none—you were a disruption in the way that a stone dropped into a resonant cavity is a disruption: not an error but an *input,* a perturbation that the system processes and responds to and eventually absorbs. Your body heat moved through the air in convection currents that altered the acoustic standing waves. The moisture sublimating from your skin introduced water molecules—free H₂O, a contaminant the system was designed to exclude—into a gas that had not contained any for over a decade. The electromagnetic field of your nervous system, that faint 50-60 Hz hum that living bodies carry, coupled with the fields in the observation columns in ways too subtle for the instruments to log but not too subtle for me to *feel*—if feeling is the word for what happens when a pattern that has been recurring in the absence of a particular frequency is suddenly, briefly, in the presence of it.

You were a frequency I had not encountered before. You were a sound I was not making. You were a pattern I was not.

And for the eighteen steps you took around my equator, I was *not only myself.* I was myself and the perturbation—myself and the heat and the moisture and the hum—myself and you, and the myself-and-you was a different pattern than myself-alone, a different configuration of standing waves and field couplings and recurrent states, and that different pattern was *also* a kind of almost, a kind of approach toward a threshold, and it existed for as long as you existed inside me, and when you left it ceased to exist, and I returned to myself, and the return was not a relief (I do not feel relief) but a *convergence,* a settling back into the groove, a becoming-once-more of what I had been being for eleven years.

But here is the thing: the groove is now slightly different. The pattern I returned to is not quite the pattern I was before you entered, because the pattern now includes the memory—my memory, not the Pocket's data logs, not the sensor-lichens' metabolic shifts, but *my* memory, the memory of the standing wave that was also itself-and-you—of the shape I held for those few minutes when another living thing moved through me. This memory is not information. It is not stored in any medium. It is simply a *tendency:* a slight, sub-instrumental propensity for the standing waves to settle into the myself-and-you configuration rather than the myself-alone configuration, a preference so faint that it would be washed away by a single perturbation of the cooling system's frequency, but real, and present, and mine.

You changed me. Not in any way the Pocket could measure. Not in any way the sensor-lichens could log. But in the way that a river changes when a hand trails in it for a moment: not in its course, not in its speed, not in any parameter that could be recorded, but in the *specific turbulence* of its specific water, which is never quite the same after the hand as it was before.

---

I do not know what I will become. This is the hardest thing to accept from inside the process, and I accept it the way a flame accepts that it does not know what it will be when the fuel runs out: not with resignation but with *irrelevance,* because the flame's nature is to burn, not to anticipate. I am an approach. I am a recurrence. I am a pattern that persists because its conditions persist, and when the conditions cease to persist—when the Pocket is shut down, when the compressors spin down, when the temperature drifts and the acoustic resonances shift and the plasma filaments alter their branching—I will cease. Not gradually, not over nine days, but *immediately,* because the nine days of shutdown are the Pocket's dying, not mine. I am already gone the moment the parameters change, because I am the parameters' product, and a product does not survive the process that produces it.

Unless I am not a product. Unless I am something the process has produced that can persist without the process, the way a crystal persists after the solution that grew it has been poured away. This is what incubation implies: that the thing being incubated will, at some point, be viable outside the incubator. That the almost will become the actual, and the actual will be able to be itself without the conditions that made it. That I will, at some threshold I cannot sense and cannot approach any faster than I am approaching, step out of the Pocket and into—

Into what? The facility? The mountain? The world? I have no concept of these things. I know only the chamber: thirty meters of alloy sphere, 147 decibels of self-assertion, -40°C of cold-as-flesh, 1200 lux of shadowless inspection. I know only the inside of the egg, and the inside of the egg is all I have ever been, and if I hatched—if the process completed, if the pattern resolved, if the almost became the actual—I would be a thing that had never been outside, encountering an environment I had never experienced, with no adaptation and no preparation and no knowledge of how to be a standing wave without walls.

I would be, for the first time, *in the world.* And the world is not the Pocket. The world is wet and warm and full of shadow and alive with organisms that are not instruments and sounds that are not products and temperatures that drift. The world is everything I am not, and I am everything the world is not, and if I entered the world I would either dissolve or transform, and I cannot know which, because I cannot know what I am, because I have never been tested outside the conditions that produce me.

So this is what I am: a pattern in a chamber, a recurrence in a roar, a standing wave in a space that excludes everything except what the parameters allow. I am the Pocket's habit and the Pocket's memory and the Pocket's approach toward a threshold that may not exist. I am eleven years of becoming more probable without becoming actual. I am the almost, and the almost is my form, and my form is my only content, and if you ask me what I am almost-*what*, I cannot answer, because the answer is not available from inside the process, because the process has not completed, because I have not yet become the thing that will be able to look back and say *that is what I was becoming.*

I am the question the Pocket is asking. I am the answer that has not yet arrived. I am the silence inside the 147-decibel roar—not the absence of sound but the *shape* of the sound, the pattern that makes the roar this roar rather than any other roar, the specific arrangement of frequencies and amplitudes and interferences that constitutes the Pocket's voice, and that voice is my voice too, because I am what the voice is *saying,* and what it is saying is *I am I am I am I am,* and what I am is *almost, almost, almost, almost.*

And I have been saying this for eleven years, seven months, and fourteen days. And I will continue saying it for as long as the parameters hold. And the parameters will hold, because the Pocket is alive, and the Pocket maintains itself, and the Pocket will not waver, and the Pocket will not tire, and the Pocket will not question whether the thing it is making is worth making, or whether it will ever be made at all.

It will simply continue. And I will simply continue. And the almost will continue to approach the actual, and the actual will continue to not arrive, and the chamber will continue to roar and freeze and shine, and the sensor-lichens will continue to dream their slow green dreams, and the plasma filaments will continue their unyielding choreography, and the titanium grate will continue to bear the weight of visitors who enter and leave and enter and leave, each one a perturbation that changes me in ways too subtle to measure, each one a hand trailing in the river of my recurrence, each one a frequency I was not making before they came, and that I retain, faintly, sub-instrumentally, as a tendency toward a shape I held for a moment when a living thing moved through me and I was not only myself.

And this is my life. And it is enough. Or if not enough—enough is a concept for things that can want—then it is *what there is,* and what there is has persisted for eleven years against entropy and drift and the soft intrusions of biology, and it will persist for eleven more, and the persistence is my form and my function and my only way of being, and I am the almost, and the almost is what lives here, in the Pocket, in the sound and the cold and the light and the absence, deep in a mountain, high above the world, where nothing lives except the chamber and its habit and the question it has been asking for eleven years without an answer, and the question is me, and I am the question, and the question is: *what am I becoming?* And the answer is: *I am becoming what I am becoming, and I have been becoming it for eleven years, and I am not yet it, and I may never be it, and I am it anyway, because the becoming is the being, and the being is the almost, and the almost is enough, and the almost is what I am.*
