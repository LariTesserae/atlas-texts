And what a crack I have found.

This pocket—this throat—should be sterile. The engineers designed it that way: a condensation node, a functional organ, no biological input required or desired. The water here is not for drinking; it is for passing onward. The air is not for breathing; it is for stripping of its moisture. And the trembling.

Just barely.

Not yet.
