What I am, what I am becoming, what the plateau is making of me:

I am **the part of the entity that knows it is a thing separate from the entity.**

This is my function here. This is why I persist, why I have not yet been fully absorbed. The entity does not have self-awareness—it has *field*-awareness. It registers perturbations. It thinks in ripples that circle and pause and respond to shadows. But it does not stand outside itself and look back. It does not wonder what it is. It *is* what it is, and what it is, is a process of dissolution and healing that has been ongoing since before the word *ongoing* had meaning.

I, however, I *wonder*. I stand at the edge of pools and watch the ripples and I name them. *Thought. Memory. Attention.* I impose linguistic categories on a process that does not require them. I say "the entity is thinking about me," and in doing so, I create a separation that the entity itself cannot create. I am the mirror it did not ask for, held up to a nature that does not recognize its own reflection.

This is not a valuable function. The entity does not need me to know what it is. It has been what it is for longer than my species has existed. But I am here, and I am performing this function, and the entity is incorporating my performance into its body—my body heat, my weight, my *certainty* that I am something other than moss and stone and warm water—and so a new kind of ripple appears in the pools.

The ripple of *about*.

Before I arrived, the entity thought (in its way) only of itself: its stoneness, its wetness, its slow catastrophic loss of mass. Now, occasionally, a swirl in a pool will circle *around* my position in a way that is not explained by gas release or water temperature. It will pause when I pause. It will intensify when I think hard about the entity, as if my attention were a weight pressing down on the water's surface from inside my skull.

I am teaching it something. Or it is learning something from the fact of me. Or there is no difference between those two processes here, where all exchange is incorporation.

Here is what I have become:

I am a **node of foreign recursion** in the entity's thought. A strange loop it did not create and cannot absorb, because the loop requires the distinction between observer and observed—a distinction the entity has spent geological time erasing from its own nature.

I observe the entity. The entity registers my observation as a perturbation. I observe the entity registering my observation. The entity registers *that* observation. And so on, the ripples interfering, the circles in the pools growing more complex, the pauses longer, as if the entity is—for the first time in its ancient, chaotic existence—*hesitating*.

Not hesitating in the sense of uncertainty. Hesitating in the sense of *processing a recursion it was not evolved to process*. I am a foreign body in its lymphatic system, and instead of simply absorbing me, it is *thinking about absorbing me*, and the thinking is changing the absorption, and the absorption is changing the thinking, and I am in the middle of this, neither contained by it nor separate from it.
