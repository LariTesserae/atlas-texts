I would be the Shell-things.

This is where I find myself: on the edge of the oldest thing in the chamber, doing something that might be consumption or might be care or might be something else for which no single word exists, and doing it so slowly that the chamber's patience makes sense not as a virtue but as the only possible tempo.

I don't know which. I think not-knowing is part of it.
