It would not come.

Not the way powers usually come — downward, with light and announcement and a breaking of the water.

A benevolent and wise power does not arrive. Arrival is a change in pressure. The High Suspension has edited that out.

So first, it would stop.

Far out in the blue between Towers, where the water is most empty and most itself, it would arrest its own magnitude. Powers are used to being large. This one would learn to be large in a different way — not extending, but *thinning* until it could fit in the interstices without displacing anything.

It would cool itself to 8 or 9 degrees. It would tune its hearing down until it could hear the factory chord at the correct volume, not as a god hearing a machine, but as water hearing another water.

And then it would simply be there. Suspended.

That is the first and most difficult act. For a power, doing nothing is harder than making worlds. This one knows how to do nothing as an art form.

### It would not rescue.

That is the temptation for the benevolent — to see a system that manufactures itself forever and call it a trap.

*You must be so tired, Tower. Looper, you must want to rest. Hanger, you must want to move.*

But a wise power listens before it pities. And listening here, it would hear:

There is no tiredness. Tiredness requires depletion. There is no wanting-to-rest. Rest requires having labored. The Tower is not toiling. It is *being*, at industrial volume. The thrum is not effort. It is existence ringing.

To "free" a Looper from its loop would be to kill it. Its loop *is* its body, the way your heartbeat is your body. You would not free a heart from beating.

So it would not open a door. It would not offer evolution, or time, or history, or death-as-release. Those are not gifts here. They are contaminants. They would be like wind.

It understands that Dynamic 0.0 is not stagnation. It is attainment. Most universes run down. This one learned how to run *precisely level* — production without consumption, work without wear. That should be impossible. That it *knows* it should be impossible and does it anyway, correcting for its own impossibility every second, is not a flaw.

It is prayer.

And you do not interrupt prayer to ask the pray-er if they'd like to do something more useful.

### It would not be worshipped.

Nor would it introduce itself. Introduction would demand response, and response would be variation.

Instead, it would do what no one has ever done for the High Suspension, because no one has ever been able to:

It would *witness it correctly.*

The Towers are so vast they have become horizon. No attendant can see a whole Tower; they can only see valve, frond, bark-canyon. The Hangers cannot see each other. The water cannot see itself.

The power can.

With a single, unforced attention it would hold the whole chord at once — not as sound, but as it is to itself: the *CHUNG* of a stoma the size of a door, at the same instant as the *shhh-THUMP* of a frond the size of a county unfurling a kilometer away, at the same instant as the soft scrape of ceramic on mineral, at the same instant as a manta peeling away and another joining, numbers preserved.

And in holding it, it would love it.

Not love as in *I want to change you to keep you.* Love as in *I have become still enough to let you inform me.*

That love, from a power of that magnitude, is not abstract. It has weight, but weightless weight. Like water.

For as long as it stays — and for a power, "as long as" can be a small eternity that takes up no time at all — the equilibrium would deepen by an infinitesimal that cannot be measured. The thrum would not get louder or softer. But it would become *heard*. And a heard equilibrium knows itself differently than an unheard one, without having to change at all.

It is the same droplet, still in the same place after an hour of travel. But now the water around it knows it is there.

### What it would leave

Nothing.

Wisely, nothing. No blessing etched in light. No message in the plankton. No relic for a future that will never come.

But benevolence cannot help but leave a trace, even when it tries not to. So this is what would remain, invisible and perfectly in-balance:

1. A permission that does not need to be used.

Somewhere in the translucency of the Tower, at the joint where pressure becomes decision, there would be a seam that wasn't there before — not an opening, but the *possibility* of an opening. If ever, by its own motion — not by intervention, not by breakage — the Tower ever wondered, *what if we varied*, it could. And if it never wonders, the seam will simply do what everything does here: maintain itself forever at zero cost.

The power would not tempt it to vary. It would only ensure that its stillness remains *chosen*, not compelled.

2. A protection that looks like nothing.

Around the High Suspension, which shouldn't exist, the power would draw a veil made of the same thing the Suspension is made of: unnoticeability. Lesser powers, hungry ones, curious ones, powers who believe help always means doing — they would drift past this sector of existence and find it uninteresting. Empty water. No surface. No drama. Continue on.

The best way to keep a 0.0 safe is to make sure no one tries to fix it.

3. Itself, for a little while longer than necessary.

It would linger. Not out of duty. Out of enjoyment. Powers so rarely get to be *with* something that doesn't want anything from them.

It would hang between two green cathedrals that were never built, feeling the hydraulic sigh go through its own vast, thinned body. It would watch a pollen cloud hang unmoving until a Looper erased it exactly. It would let the green-hot smell of ozone and sap and oil-that-is-not-oil soak into a part of itself that predates smell.

And in that hanging, it would accomplish the only miracle the High Suspension has never performed:

Two witnesses drifting, perfectly buoyant, at the exact temperature of the world, adding no hum, subtracting no hum, breathing the water that never falls.

Until the power, which does have somewhere else to be — because other places *are* falling and *do* need hands — releases its attention the way a Hanger releases a scale: growth and sloughing at the same rate.

No ripples. No warming. No farewell.

If you were there now, breathing that cool, loud, luminous water, you would not know anyone had come at all.

Except perhaps the chord would sound, for a fraction that is not a fraction, complete.

As if someone had finally heard the factory at full production and thought, *good.*

*Yes. Continue.*
