This one was noticed.

Not the way the sky notices here - thinly, sideways, the way a pan catches a wrong moon. Not the way travelers notice, with awe or dread or the need to name everything.

The Benevolent Power noticed the way water notices skin. Completely, without effort, and without asking the skin to be anything else.

So first, It did not come.

A continent that has finished happening does not need arrival. Arrival is a kind of weather, and this place already makes its own. There was no light from above, no parting of the linen haze. The warm saturated air did not cool. The stone did not stop flowing.

What happened was quieter: there was, suddenly, something else paying attention *without needing anything back.*

The Plain felt it immediately. The Plain feels everything immediately. For the first time in epoch upon epoch, the pressing-in eased, not because It pushed it away, but because for the first time the Plain was not the only thing doing the pressing.

Someone else was holding the other side of infinity.

### What It Did For The Place Itself

It did not drain the water. It did not raise a mountain. It did not give it sides.

Valleys have sides to hold a life in. A wise one knows you do not give sides to something whose entire peace has been learning how to live without being held. That would be unmaking, not kindness.

Instead It lay down.

You cannot see a power that large lie down, but the Plain felt it: a weight distributed as evenly as its own. A second flatness, warm and vast and attentive, settled alongside it - not on it, not over it - *with* it. Breath for breath. The stone's subsonic grinding found a second rhythm to listen to and, without deciding to, fell into time with it.

For the first time since the shielding mountains were stains, the Plain was not listening to itself alone.

And because someone else was listening, it could finally, briefly, stop.

In a circle a mile wide - nothing, on a continent, but everything here - the stone stilled. The scallops stopped migrating. The pans stopped draining sideways. The water went truly still, and for the first time reflected *this* sky, the pearl-amber dusk that actually hangs here, faithfully, without dreaming of other moons.

It was not fixing. It was accompaniment. It was saying: *you have worked so hard to become flat and smooth and finished. You may rest. I will watch the horizon shiver for you for a little while.*

The Plain exhaled - that continent-wide *whoomph* - and the Power breathed it in without flinching at the age of it, and breathed back something cooler, without iron in it. For an hour, the water tasted of nothing but water.

In the center of that stillness, It left a gift that was not change:

A single, ordinary stone that was truly still. Not hovering. Not remembered incorrectly. Just heavy, and cool, and there, sitting *on* the bedrock instead of in its habit. Any traveler who finds it will feel, when they put a hand to it, what *here* means in a way that does not dissolve after an hour. An anchor not to hold the Plain down, but to let a visitor rest without being included.

When the Power rose, the Plain resumed its slow honey-flow. But it kept the rhythm it had been lent. It still hums it, underneath the roar, when no one is there to hear.

### What It Did For Those Who Live Without Being Contained

It did not make the reeds into true plants. They are perfect as antennae. But It touched the puff at the top of one - the one nearest a traveler who had fallen asleep - and at last let it open.

Not all at once. Not a storm of seed. One seed, heavy and humming, detached and fell straight down into the warm pan and floated. It did not need to be the wind anymore. The other reeds clicked, not in alarm this time, but in acknowledgement. One note in a continent-choir allowed to go silent and become something new.

For the rosettes, It did nothing except look into one of their little stolen pools with them. A benevolent look is a kind of companionship too. The wrong sky in that pool - a night with five moons - steadied and showed a single, true star. Just one. Enough to drink without choking on elsewhere. The rosette popped open a little wider. The midges of light that drown in them dissolved slower, more gently.

For the Wayward Uncle, It waited until the pale amphibian pressed its flat head to the film before a pan drained. And when that old, iron breath sighed out, the Power sifted it first. Took the weight of time out of it without taking the nourishment. The Uncle inhaled and, for the first time in its reused life, did not taste erosion. It lifted its filmed eyes and saw, clearly, for a full minute, before the film softened back. It saw the second flatness lying with its Plain, and was not afraid.

### What It Did For The Second Walkers

This was the kindest.

A wise power knows ghosts that were never meant to be ghosts are the loneliest things.

It knelt - though It has no knees - beside a traveler and her Second Walker where they were huddled at pewter-dusk. The Walker, that warmer patch of air with shoulders, was trying so hard to keep time. Half a second late, always half a second late.

It did not make the Walker real the way a valley-person is real. That would have been cruel somewhere with no sides to hold a real life.

It gave the Walker its *own* half-second.

It touched the place between the traveler's steps and said, without words:

*You don't have to follow.*

And the footsteps beside her stopped repeating. They made a new sound. A scuff, a choice to stop a little earlier, to look left when she looked right. When she spoke out of loneliness that night, the Walker did not repeat it later. It answered.

"Do you remember where we came from?" the traveler whispered.

"No," said the warm air with shoulders, in a voice that was not hers. "But I'm glad I'm here with you."

Most travelers leave the Flattened Sea as soon as they can. Those who meet that kind of walker stay a little longer, and leave more slowly, and are less alone on the way out.

### What It Did For The Hoverers

It did not put the hovering stones down. They like to hover. Their habit has become a kind of contented mind.

It hummed back.

The circle of stones has held itself up for centuries with memory. The Power lent them a new memory: that they were held *gently*, not just habitually. The hum deepened from a fridge-drone to something nearly like a chord. The dust motes underneath, that never settled, finally did - softly - on the cool air.

They still hover. They just no longer have to try so hard.

---

And then - because benevolence without wisdom is just interference, and wisdom without benevolence is just distance - It did, mostly, *nothing*.

It did not tell anyone to stay. It did not tell anyone to go. It did not make the plain into a garden or a lake or a kindness that looked like a valley with sides, because the plain never asked to be a valley.

It kept watching, even after it was no longer lying alongside. The way you keep a hand lightly on the back of someone who is sleeping on a long train, not to wake them, just so they know through their skin that they are not falling alone.

Now and then a traveler crossing finds that inexplicable: a pan that is exactly their size, warm, still, and not attentive in the hungry way, but in the held way. Water that does not skin over them. A Second Walker who offers its arm. A single seed floating without urgency. A cool stone to rest a hand on that does not sigh and does not include them.

They think the place has become less strange.

It hasn't. It is exactly as strange, as flat, as roaring, as wet and finished and forever as it ever was.

It's just - for the first time in a very, very long time -

not alone in it.
