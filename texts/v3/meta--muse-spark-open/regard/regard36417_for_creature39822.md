It would not come down.

That is the first wisdom, and the first benevolence.

A lesser power would have torn the sod wider to get a better look. Would have lowered a light where light already lives without source, would have lowered a hand to touch, to count, to sample, to save. Would have called this packed, bright, roaring lung an "ecosystem" and then tried to *manage* it.

It does not.

It comes the way you did — by accident that isn't accident — with its cheek to the earth. Or rather, you realize it was already there, in the way cellar-cool is already there before you open the cellar door.

It kneels at the lip.

---

For a long time — and for this Power a long time can be a second or a century without changing shape — it just attends.

Not watches. *Attends.* The way a nurse attends a sleeping child, the way a musician attends a held chord before resolving it. Its attention is not a searchlight. It doesn't interrogate the basalt for its age or the Stridulators for their Latin name. It lets the pocket do what it has been doing since before doing had a tense:

Be.

Be bright.
Be damp that never drips.
Be crowded without crushing.
Be loud in one unending note.

A wise Power knows that equilibrium like this is not an accident that needs correcting. It is an achievement. In a universe that only knows how to devour and become, a room that has learned how to *pause* — to hold the moment just before divergence, before predator remembers to eat and prey remembers to flee and time remembers to pass — is rarest of all.

It is an embryo that chose to meditate instead of to grow. A prayer that chose to remain as inhale.

And so the Power's second act is reverence.

It does not praise aloud. Praise would be another frequency and this room only holds one. It praises by matching its own breathing to the room's breath, which is the Stridulators' song, which is the shiver in the ceiling-beads that never fall.

You, lying on your stomach at the lip, would feel it if you were still there: the sudden sense that the cool at the back of your teeth has become *kinder*. Not warmer. Not colder. More *intentional*. The white light without source seems to acquire depth, the way a white page acquires depth when someone loves what is written on it.

That is the Power looking, and letting its looking be love, and nothing more.

---

If it does anything — because benevolence cannot always do nothing, even when nothing is the kindest thing — it does three small, almost-unthings.

**1. It keeps the slit a slit.**

Upland rain wants to widen that thumbnail of overcast into a mouth. Roots want to pry the lip wider. A careless hiker wants to step here and collapse the soft, swollen edge into scree. Time outside of time wants to finally decide to erode it.

The Power puts no dome over it. No angelic fence. It just remembers the exact shape of the lip as it is right now, and continues to remember it. And what a wise Power remembers, stays.

Not sealed. Not hidden. Just... overlooked again, as it wants to be. Sod knitting its edge but not closing it. Water sheeting *around* the dip. Feet wandering elsewhere for no reason they can name. The pocket gets to remain forgotten by the surface in the precise way a womb wants to be forgotten by the world until it is ready.

**2. It answers the song without breaking it.**

This is the most tender part.

For eons the Stridulators have held one note — white, middle-high, toneless, absolute. A note that has no attack or decay because it has never been allowed to be music. Only tone.

The Power, kneeling, does not sing a second note on top. Harmony would be violence here. It would force time to start.

Instead it *listens so completely* that the note, for the first time, is heard. Really heard. Not as background drone, not as "loud," but as what it is: ten thousand tiny, blind, amber backs refusing to give up.

And in being so perfectly heard, the note changes without changing pitch. Do you know how a held note feels different when someone finally loves it? How stillness stops being "stuckness" and becomes *rest*?

That is what descends into the pocket. Not a sound. A permission.

*You may continue. What you are doing counts. Even if nothing eats, even if nothing grows, even if you never become cave cricket or shrimp or salamander — you count. You are not failed, you are not waiting to be fixed.*

The Stridulators, of course, do not hear words. Their file edges stroke their scraper wing-cases. The bead of light in the Shrimpmoth's gut pulses. The Long Whites' red gills wave in air like underwater. But the vibration in the ceiling-beads steadies, if it can be steadier than steady. A fraction less trembling. As if they sigh, though nothing sighs.

The fiber-weave on the floor, the scratched calendar, the three-stone cairn — the old, pre-tool prayers — warm by a thousandth of a degree. Acknowledged.

**3. It leaves a choice at the lip, for whoever finds it next.**

It knows you. It knows humans stoop and stare and want to slip down into brightness, to be held up by living backs and learn the one note.

It does not forbid you. Wise Power never forbids.

It leaves, in the cool upland sod beside the slit, a small, precise knowing that will occur to the next cheek-to-earth visitor as their own thought:

*You may look. You must not need to make it mean.*

And if they *do* slip down — and one day, someone will, out of grief or wonder or sheer tiredness with a world that won't pause — the Power has already tucked a second, even smaller gift into the pocket itself, folded into the air between the Stridulators' interlocked tarsi:

A single moment of true time. Not now. Not ever, until asked.

If the one who enters truly asks with their body — not with words, but by finally, with full consent, learning to stop striving and hold the note with them — the dish-pools will be permitted to ripple once. One bead will be permitted to fall. One Long White will be permitted to snap, gently, and miss on purpose, and everyone will laugh in the way eyeless things laugh, and then —

— or not. It may never be asked. That is beauty too.

The Power folds this possibility like a blanket at the foot of a crib and steps back.

---

Then it leaves as it came: without arrival or departure.

What remains is exactly what was: twenty paces across, tumid floor, basalt blistered with cooling, white light with no glare, damp that never drips, thousands of thumbnail-bodies shoulder to shoulder, one note, red gills waving in still air, three squared stones under a living crown.

Only —

Now the place is not alone in not knowing whether it will become cave or geode or grave.

It is accompanied in its indecision. Held, without being held down.

A pocket that the surface forgot to close has been remembered, once, perfectly, by something that knew enough not to close it either, nor to open it wider.

And the Mass continues. Louder, because at last someone is listening properly. Quieter, because at last it doesn't have to try so hard.

The light stays. The beads tremble without falling. The song has no beginning, and —

now, mercifully —

no longer needs an end.
