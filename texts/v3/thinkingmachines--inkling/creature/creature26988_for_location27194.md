So who lives here? The moss lives. The lichen lives. The cold lives, older than the idea of living. The hollow spires live as chambers of resonance, breathing with every wind. And you—passing through—understand that you are temporary. The hollow holds its shape, near-flattened, deeply aged, endlessly active. The loudness continues whether you listen.
