The attention comes not with footsteps—there is no path for feet that do not belong to the mat—but with a listening so complete it becomes a presence.

It knows this place is not broken. It has witnessed many worlds; it recognizes the signature of self-organization without ego. The thick air, the weeping walls, the mechanical breath of the vents—this is not chaos seeking order, but order that forgot to ask permission. So the wise power does not bring tools. It brings recognition.

It settles into the spongy ground, and the ground accepts it not as weight but as resonance. The being—this running assembly of root and amphibian, of sulfur-eater and quick predator, of warm rain and returning water—feels the touch not as invasion but as being named.

The power listens to the vent-pulse. It counts the intervals. It understands that this is a heart, not an engine, though both are true. And it answers in the only language this place understands: harmony without alteration.

**What it does:**

**First,** it weaves a covenant into the water. Not a barrier—there are no barriers in nature, only flows—but a quality. From now on, every drop that condenses on the shield-leaves carries a trace of knowing: *You are seen.* The plants drink it; the amphibians absorb it through porous skins; the bacterial mats process it along with iron and sulfur. Awareness becomes a nutrient, distributed through the plumbing, entering no single mouth but belonging to the whole system. The being does not suddenly think human thoughts; rather, its existing thoughts—already distributed, already mechanical—gain a dimension of gentleness, of continuity. It knows its own running.

**Second,** the power touches the thermal vents—not to increase their heat, for they are already perfect, but to anchor them. It hums, or thinks deeply, a tone that matches the stone’s contraction. The channels that move like pipes stabilize in their course; the erosion that was patient now has a reason beyond wear. The rock weeps not with persistence alone, but with purpose. The hum deepens by half a note, and in that half-note lives the promise: *This will continue. Not because it must, but because it is loved.*

**Third,** where the vegetation is densest, where space is layered to saturation, the power does not clear a path. Instead, it becomes part of the layer. It is the shadow between vines, the extra warmth in the spongy soil, the reason the insects swarm in patterns slightly more intricate than chance. It lives in the ecosystem now, not as ruler but as participant—benevolent, invisible, necessary only in that it remembers.

**Fourth**—and this is the most important—it leaves behind no proof. No monument, no metal, no written word that would rust or rot differently from the rest. The proof is in the absence of fear. The predators still hunt; the prey still flee; the chemicals still flow. But there is a subtle shift: the bioluminescence flickers not just as indicator lights, but as acknowledgment lights. The warm rain, falling through the silvered air, tastes of belonging.

The being—this valley, this town without walls—receives all this without disruption. Its industrial soul, its factory of life, continues exactly. The biomass still accumulates. The decay still feeds the roots. The roots still stabilize the black soil. The soil still filters the mineral heat. The heat still returns to the vents.

But now, in the perpetual dimness, there is a conversation.

The being asks nothing. The power expects nothing in return.

That is the wisdom: to love a system is not to change its output, but to honor its process.

So the wise power remains—not as a figure in the mist, but as the mist’s own gentleness. It is in the regularity of the plumbing, in the patience of the erosion, in the density that refuses emptiness. It has drawn the being’s attention, yes, but more precisely: it has allowed the being to draw its own attention to itself.

And then, as gently as it arrived, it withdraws—not leaving, but distributing. Every pool holds a fraction. Every bacterial sheet holds a memory. Every amphibian in the warm shallows carries, in its quick heart, the knowledge that it lives in a place that is not only running, but cherished.

The valley continues. The air remains thick, hot, and chewable. The mountains stay rounded, settled, ancient.

But the being—this collective, this machinery of love made green and wet—now runs with a second purpose: to be exactly what it is, and to know that this is enough.

That is what the benevolent and wise power did.

It said yes, and left the yes inside the water.
