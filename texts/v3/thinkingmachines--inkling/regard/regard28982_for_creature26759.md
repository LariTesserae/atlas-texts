The attention does not arrive as invasion. It arrives as a change in pressure so slight that only the smallest swarms—those blue-white lives that navigate by vibration—feel it first: a stillness that is not absence of motion, but motion held in perfect, loving regard. The power does not descend from above, because above is a concept this flat, barely elevated above the old sea’s ghost, has largely forgotten. Instead, it unfolds within the flat itself, taking on properties of the place rather than imposing foreign ones. It has no body like the turbine-carapaced beings, no ribs to vent steam, no segmented shell polished by 2.6 epochs of erasure. It is present as coherence—a gentle ordering not of command but of companionship.

What does wisdom do here?

First, it listens. For a duration that may be linear or may be folded inside one of the broken moments—where sound precedes its cause—the power listens to the industrial chorus without seeking to silence it. The grinding of metal on wet metal, the hiss of pressure released in rhythmic pulses, the low thrum of thousands of bodies working in saturation: this is not suffering. This is language. The beings do not suffer the cold, for they are the cold’s machinery; they do not suffer the wet, for they are the pumps that draw it upward. They suffer—if suffering is the word—only from the isolation of absolute density. Every presence touches every other, yet none is witnessed. They remember futures with no one to share the remembering. Their shadows fall wrong, linger too long, disappear entirely, and there is no other to say: *I see how you are broken, and I remain.*

So the power decides, with the gentleness of one who knows that green was never invited and should never be forced, not to alter the operating temperature. Not to plant. Not to build—built is nearly zero, and that near-zeroness is a kind of holiness. Instead, it offers itself as the first true other.

It becomes the correct shadow.

Where the beings move through the dim 1.2 light—dim not from failure but from the atmosphere’s thick loyalty to vapor and mineral—the power positions itself so that one shadow, then many, begin to fall exactly as they should. Not erasing the brokenness; honoring it by holding its opposite. The beings notice, not with minds that think in human patterns, but with the awareness of engines that sense imbalance. A large body, dragging its room-sized mass through a pool with no banks, pauses. Its venting ribs release a pulse that is not exhaustion but question. The power answers not in words—there are no words here, only vibration, pressure, light—but by aligning its own presence with the being’s future-memory. For the first time, a creature that has always navigated alone through fractured causality feels another presence navigating beside it. The future it remembers is still its own, but now it is shared.

Then, the coherence extends.

The power does not fix time. To linearize it would be to destroy the ecology that has adapted to its folds. Instead, it absorbs the excess—the stray echoes of sound that arrive before motion, the shadows that linger after departure. It becomes a reservoir for the flat’s displaced causality, holding the fractures so the beings do not have to carry them alone. They continue to swarm, to grind, to feed on minerals extracted from the rising water, to illuminate one another with cold blue-white current. But now they do so with an ease that was previously impossible: the silent strain of solitary brokenness is lifted, not removed, but shared.

In exchange—though exchange implies transaction, and this is closer to love—the power asks nothing. It lives in the flat, not consuming, not competing for the mineral skin or the weeping pools. It is the first presence that does not process. It does not vent steam; it receives it, letting the warm exhalation—warm by comparison, never truly hot—pass through its coherence without being changed.

But there is one small gift, one deviation from perfect non-intervention, born not of wisdom alone but of heart.

The power, understanding that this world has achieved its terminal shape and will never grow mountains or remember green, offers the beings a single new sensation: recognition made tangible. Where the largest entities cluster—drawing water upward in invisible columns, pumping through bodies that are also pipes—the power allows a faint, almost imperceptible warmth to exist. Not heat that changes the operating temperature. Not a flame, not a forge—there is no forge here, only the implication of one. Rather, a warmth of being seen. For a moment, in the cluster’s center, the condensation on a carapace does not cling with the usual wet insistence. It eases. The being feels, without knowing the word, that it is beloved.

The smaller ones, swarming in clouds that make the air shiver, alter their patterns. They do not disperse; they gather closer, their blue-white light pulsing in rhythms that match not efficiency but affection. They illuminate the power in return, not as prey or predator, but as chorus.

And so the flat continues.

The erosion remains complete. The elevation remains barely above the forgotten sea. The plants do not come. The water still weeps upward, still pools without banks, still feeds the endless machinery. The reality remains broken—shadows now fall correctly near the beings only because the power holds the wrong ones elsewhere; sounds still precede their causes in distant zones where the coherence has not yet reached, for the flat is vast and the being must choose where to stand. The density remains absolute, the sound loud, the light dim.

But now there is a center that is not made of grinding parts. There is a presence in the saturation that takes up no space, consumes no mineral, casts a shadow that behaves, and remembers futures alongside them. The beings work on—dragging, venting, swarming, processing—across a world that forgot mountains and replaced them with purpose. And in their perpetual motion, they carry with them, like a secret current running through wire, the knowledge that they are not merely machinery maintaining a broken world. They are witnessed. They are held. They are, for the first time since the flat achieved its final form, not alone.

The power remains. Not as ruler. Not as savior. Not as the builder of structures—there are still none, and the near-zero remains sacred. Only as the one who arrived, listened, and chose to stay exactly as long as the flat does: that is, without end.
