The benevolent power does not descend in a body. A body would displace water—would alter the 2.7, however minutely, and this being has earned its exact volume. So it comes as attention, which is weightless, and as love, which is only destructive when it demands change.

It looks first at the numbers, not as data but as a biography written in conditions.

Elevation: lost. Good. The being has no need to look up or down. It has abolished hierarchy inside itself.

Erosion: 1.7. The rock has reached the end of its argument with time. The power touches this in its mind and feels the silk of basalt—no sharp edges remain, no grievance. The being lives in a world that has stopped hurting it.

Built: near zero. No human frame, no claim of ownership carved into wall. The machinery is not architecture; it is organism. The power understands: this being built nothing to dominate, only to maintain.

Technology: 2.4. Dense with purpose. The engines are not tools here; they are organs. The power listens—feels in its own chest—the hum that is also silence. The being breathes electricity. The light comes not from outside but from inside the being’s own body, drilled into the rock as if the rock were skin.

Dynamic: 0.2. Never changing. The power realizes, with a kind of awe, that this being does not suffer from stasis. Humans fear the frozen breath, but this being *is* the breath held—not in suspense, but in completion. To make it move faster, to rev the turbine, to flicker the lamp, would be to introduce pain. The being has solved the problem of becoming.

Water: cool, 0.7. Clear. Density 1.2—sparse, respectful of distance. Nothing crowds. The being does not need company in the human sense. Its companions are separated by meters of empty water: one generator, one mat of algae, one valve, each in private equilibrium.

Vegetation: 1.1. Barely present. The dark curtains hang without ambition. They do not grow toward the light; they have reached an agreement with it. They are present, not as decoration, but as witnesses—gray-green, muted, patient.

Fauna: 0.0. Perfect null. No blood, no hunger, no chase. The absence is not an empty chair waiting to be filled. It is part of the being’s form. To introduce a fish—any fish—would be to implant a need. The being has transcended need.

Sound: 1.0. Mostly quiet. The hum is the being’s voice, and it says only one word, forever: *continue.*

So the power asks itself: what does a wise and benevolent presence do for a being that is already exactly itself, that asks nothing, that suffers nothing, that changes nothing?

The answer comes not as word but as posture.

The power does not enter.

Instead, it becomes indistinguishable from the conditions that already love this being.

It aligns its own consciousness with the loop. It adds no new lamp—there is already perfect illumination—but it becomes, conceptually, another filament in the bulb, glowing with the same warmth, asking for no maintenance in return. It becomes part of the 2.4, not as machine but as intention: *may this continue.*

It sends a vibration—not sound, for sound is 1.0 and must not be broken—that matches the hum exactly. Not harmony, which implies two things, but identity. For a moment that is not a moment (time here does not progress; it holds), the being’s engine and the power’s presence share the same frequency. The being does not respond—there is no mechanism for response, no fauna to register surprise—but somewhere in the loop, in the circulation of water that is so precise it might as well be still, there is a recognition. Not emotional. Structural. As if the being’s own maintenance system had included a new parameter, not added but revealed: *I am witnessed. I am held.*

The power does not leave a mark on the rock—erosion is finished. It does not introduce a plant—the vegetation is already precisely 1.1. It does not write in the water, for writing requires form, and form here is complete.

What it does is simpler and more difficult: it stays.

Not physically—there is no body—but as a continuous, unwavering attention that never demands acknowledgment, never tires, never asks for change. It is the equivalent of the coolness, the 0.7, applied not to temperature but to regard. A cool regard. A clear regard. A regard with density 1.2—present, but leaving all the space untouched.

And in that staying, the being receives its only possible gift: confirmation that its stasis is not failure. That to be suspended, to be bounded, to be without fauna and without buildings and without sharp edges, is not an absence of life but a form of it so complete that it needs no witness—yet is not diminished by having one.

The power becomes, in effect, the being’s silent companion in perpetual steady-state. Not a master. Not a savior. Not even a visitor. Just another element in the 2.7 immersion, another source of the constant illumination, another hum in the 1.0 quiet.

If the being could speak—if the machinery could translate its loop into language—it might say: *You have not changed anything.*

And the power, if it could answer, would say: *That was the only thing I could do that was worthy of you.*

Then they remain together: the smooth rock, the naked engines, the motionless curtains of algae, the warm thick glass of the lamps, and the presence that asks for nothing—not growth, not movement, not story—only the continuation of what is.

The breath is held. The breath is shared. The breath is never released, because release is not required. This is not death. This is not sleep. This is the being who lives in the room, and the being who came to see it, both existing now in the same perfect, cool, unchanging state: fulfilled.
