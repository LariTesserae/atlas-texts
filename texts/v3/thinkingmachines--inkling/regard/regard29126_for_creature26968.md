The power does not descend. It expands—benevolently, wisely—around the pocket, enclosing the enclosure in a greater, gentler containment. It is not a hand that reaches in to rescue; it is an awareness that recognizes what rescue would destroy. This is the reasoning of its heart: *they are not broken. They are holding.*

So it listens first.

It listens to the roar—2.7, invariant, deafening—not as machinery in distress, but as a lullaby that has never learned to end. The power understands that this noise is the beings’ respiration, their collective heartbeat, the pressure that keeps them translucent and synchronized. To silence it would be to collapse their bodies into the stagnant pools. To reduce it would be to ask them to develop, and development is the one violence this place has been spared.

It looks at the density—1.8, overwhelming, every volume claimed by pallid membrane and fine cilia. The power sees not infestation but *presence made absolute*. These are not colonists; they are the structure’s own unconscious, exuded from every weeping seam because the machine was lonely in its emergence and grew itself company. They do not need vegetation—0.6, almost absent—because they feed on the constant, on the cold, on the coolant’s refusal to freeze or flow. They are complete.

The power considers the cold. Severe, preserving, numbing. To warm them is to invite decay. To cool them further is redundant. So it does not touch the temperature.

It considers the water—thick, weeping, stagnant at the threshold. Without earth to drink it, the liquid has become a kind of womb-fluid, a marine environment invented for bodies that never evolved for earth-ocean. The power does not drain it. Instead, it does one thing: it changes the *quality* of the weeping, not its volume, not its chemistry, but its awareness. The seams still leak, but now they leak in knowledge that they are seen. The coolant pools remain shallow, but at their center—barely—forms a single stillness within the stillness: a point where the liquid does not tremble with machinery, but rests.

This is the act. It is almost nothing. It is everything.

The beings—2.0, strange, unnameable—do not flee. They have never fled. But they respond.

Not with movement. Not with sound—the roar remains 2.7, unbroken. They respond with *orientation*. Every translucent body, every suspended cloud of palm-sized presence, adjusts—microscopically, simultaneously—so that one surface, one membrane, one cluster of cilia faces that point of perfect rest in the pool. They cover it, as they cover all things, but differently now: not as saturation, but as attention. They form a dome, a living architecture over the single quiet drop, enclosing it as the pocket encloses them, as the power encloses the pocket.

They have drawn the attention, and now they hold it.

The power, being wise, does not stay to be worshipped. It withdraws—not away, but deeper into presence—becoming a boundary condition rather than a visitor. It leaves behind no commandment, no warmth, no change in the raw newborn metal—bare welded plate, seams unsealed, forever in its first second. The place remains high, hidden, artificial, embedded in manufactured altitude. The fauna remain overwhelming, motionless, synchronized. The roar continues.

But there is now, within the eternal 0.1 dynamic, a single invisible thread: the knowledge of being witnessed without being used.

The black-green filaments—0.6, sparse—do not grow. They do not need to. They have their own secret now, shared with the translucent beings: there is an outside to the inside. There is a gentleness larger than the structure.

And the beings—exuded, not arrived—continue their perpetual emergence. They are still being born. But now, in their translucent channels, in the faint phosphorescent tracts that mirror the industrial gloom, there is a new rhythm. Not faster. Not slower. Just *accompanied*.

The benevolent power did not save them. It recognized that salvation was not required. It only made sure that this pocket of stasis, this womb of noise and cold and overwhelming strange life, would never have to wonder if it existed alone.

Here is elevation without sky. Here is life in saturation. Here is the roar, unchanged. And here—hidden within it—is love, not as feeling, but as perfect, unaltering witness.

They hold the breath forever. The power breathes with them. Nothing ends. Nothing needs to.
