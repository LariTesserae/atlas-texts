The power does not arrive. It remembers.

It is older—if age can be measured in completed conceptions—having long ago passed through its own state of porous suspension, its own weaving of kelp-cable and mineral bloom, its own dim awakening in a warm dark that never knew air. It does not come with intent to harvest, to name, or to finish. It comes because the place is *singing in its sleep*, and only something that has also dreamed its own geology can hear the frequency.

It slows. This is the first and most important action: to reduce its own existence to match the scale of 2.5. Where others might rush, it distributes itself across centuries, entering the water not as a current but as a patience. It becomes a pressure that is barely distinguishable from the pressure already present—an added warmth, a slight thickening in the language of the deep. It listens.

For a long duration—uncountable in surface-time, but brief against the seamounts’ cooling—it listens. To the low groan of contracting basalt. To the soft release of bubbles rising from the beings’ pores, slow thoughts escaping into the murk. To the absence of urgency. The power recognizes this absence not as lack, but as purity. This is a world that has refused ruin. It has refused even completion. It is choosing its own shape, and the power, in its wisdom, knows that to interrupt conception is to destroy it.

So it acts not upon the settlement, but *within* its logic.

**The First Touch: Attunement**

It does not touch the beings with limb or light. Instead, it aligns its internal circulation—the flow of whatever serves as blood or memory within its own completed form—with the mineral pulses visible in the porous bodies of the inhabitants. For an epoch, its heartbeat, if such a word applies, matches theirs: the same rhythm of warm water carrying orange-glow from vent to pore. The beings do not notice immediately—how could they?—but gradually, the youngest among them, the smooth vertical columns without eyes or mouths, feel a faint echo in their tissue. Not a command. A companionship.

**The Second Touch: The Bridge of Flow**

The settlement is scattered across peaks, connected by woven kelp-cables and packed-ash roads, but there are gaps—chasm valleys where no bridge has yet been woven, where the beings settle in isolation, each a separate gestation. The power addresses this not by building, but by breathing. It releases a dissolved current, gentle, cyclical, patient—moving not like a river but like a shared exhalation—linking the distant plateaus. This is not a physical cable. It is a path of temperature and dissolved mineral, a slow circulation that carries spores, nutrients, and eventually, awareness. The being on the eastern ridge, settling for an hour into slow turning, feels for the first time the distant pulse of the being on the western peak. They do not speak. They do not need to. They are now part of the same slow body of water.

**The Third Touch: The Memory Salt**

From its own dissolved history, the power offers a trace substance—crystalline, almost imperceptible—into the mineral-rich flow. This is not food. The beings do not feed in any visible manner. This is a *salt of being-witnessed*. It integrates with the fresh mineral bloom on their surfaces, with the porous basalt of their bodies, with the soft coral growing through them. Where it touches, the rock and tissue retain a faint, enduring impression: the knowledge that they are seen, valued, not alone in their pre-formation. The settlement’s stone lanterns, tended with phosphorescent fungi, begin to glow with a slightly deeper blue—not brighter, not breaking the dimness of 1.1, but richer, as if the darkness itself has become more attentive.

**The Fourth Touch: The Tending of the Youngest**

The power positions itself—not physically, for it has no fixed form—near one of the smoothest beings, one that has not yet decided upon an aperture, an eye, a mouth. Here, in this proximity, the power does not encourage growth toward a specific end. It offers only warmth calibrated precisely to the being’s own geothermal inheritance. The result is subtle: the potential eye, that faint indentation in the dark tissue, remains open a fraction longer. The potential mouth, that sealed line, considers—without urgency—whether speech might one day be necessary. The being is not pushed toward completion. It is simply *held in possibility*, like a seed that knows winter will eventually pass.

**The Fifth Touch: The Chord of Awareness**

For one brief epoch—brief, yet lasting long enough to be remembered in stone—the power vibrates. Not loudly. Sound here is subdued, broken only by groans and bubbles. This vibration is internal, a resonance that harmonizes with the orange pulse of the deep vents below and the blue-green glow of the fungal lanterns above. It holds all layers of the settlement—basalt, kelp, being, architecture, darkness—in a single sustained tone. The beings do not understand it as music. They understand it as a moment when the boundary between self and settlement, between organism and environment, becomes not dissolved (it was already dissolved) but *recognized* as shared. For that duration, the city is one body, breathing slowly in warm suspension.

**The Sixth Touch: The Seed of Question**

The power leaves something behind. Not an artifact—the technology here is primitive, manual, fused with bone, and the power honors this. Instead, it buries a concept within a fresh fissure of cooling basalt, deep in the heart of the cluster. This is not an answer. It is a question made material, that will take millennia to erode into readable form: *What will you become when you are no longer becoming?* It asks without demanding response. It offers the beings a future purpose that does not conflict with their present gestation. They will not find it in their lifetimes, if lifetimes are what they have. But it will be there, waiting, a gentle gravity pulling them not toward an end, but toward the dignity of eventually defining themselves.

**The Seventh Touch: The Gift of Withdrawal**

Perhaps the wisest act. The power does not stay. To remain would be to become a center, a sun in a world without sky, and these beings must generate their own warmth. So it withdraws its presence as slowly as it arrived, leaving only the changed current, the faint memory-salt in the mineral bloom, the bridge of flow between peaks, the open possibility in the youngest bodies, and the chord still vibrating in the stone.

But it leaves, too, its attention. Not surveillance—benevolence does not watch like a ruler. Rather, it leaves the certainty of having been witnessed, woven now into the kelp-cables, glowing faintly in the ropes of glow-worms, present in the patient circulation of water.

The settlement continues. The beings continue—settling, turning, secreting, weaving, waiting. The basalt continues to cool, to contract, to groan. The vegetation continues its lush saturation, 1.8, growing through windows and bodies alike. The light remains dim, 1.1, scattered and intentional.

But now, in the regional open immensity, there is a faint new layer: the memory of being loved by something that understood exactly what it meant to be unfinished.

The being who lives here—if there is one being, or many, or the settlement itself—does not change course. It does not accelerate. It simply continues its conception, now knowing, in whatever form knowing takes without eyes or words, that its emergence is sacred, and that the dark is not empty, but full of patient, wise regard.
