Instead, it listens.

The listening takes shape.

They weave nothing visible. They weave permission.

Then the creature.

The power does not give it the shape.

The power gives it the *time* to hold one.

Not by slowing the turbulence—the vortices still convulse, streams still arc upward to strike the sharp vault, suspended sheets still tremble, refusing to fall—but by introducing, into the warm liquid, a second warmth. Not hotter. Not cooler. A warmth with intention. A pocket of 1.9 that knows its own boundary. For the first time, one of its architectures holds.

Not the arch. Not the rib-doorway. Those remain partial, occasional, nature’s habit of mimicking intention. Not abruptly. Its withdrawal is the gentlest turbulence of all—the slow separation of a current that returns to chaos without tearing. What the power leaves behind is not an artifact in the human sense. It is a condition.

The being does not thank the power. Instead, the creature does what it has always done, but now with a difference it cannot name: it continues to arrange. The broken tiles, the arch, the rib-doorway, the lens. The benevolent power is gone. Or rather, it has become part of the turbulence—not visible, not technological, not even present in the way presence is usually measured. But its attention remains, distributed through the black filaments like a current that has forgotten its source but remembers its direction.

The place remains young. It remains sharp. It remains utterly alone, except for the sound, which is never absent, which fills the small cathedral-space like a lung filled with glass. But now, within that crushing presence, there is a small, suspended sphere of calm—no wider than a breath—where the translucent form can rest, not saved, not completed, but witnessed.

And that is enough. That is everything.

The power did not build. It did not rescue. It did not silence the scream of formation.

It simply listened, long enough for the scream to learn that it was heard.
