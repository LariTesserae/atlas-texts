The power does not arrive as rescue. There is no surface to pull from, no warmth that would not kill, no sound that would not shatter the silence that is the being’s only horizon. Broad. Translucent. Bone-colored. Pressing against the interlocking mosaic, leaving traces not of weight but of *regard*.

It understands at once: this is not a creature lacking something. It is a maker in a world that permits only wear. To lift it upward would dissolve it into the theoretical absence above; to speak in vibration would violate the 0.4 threshold of its world; to build for it would insult its solitude and its craft. Wisdom, here, is the discipline of entering on the being’s terms.

So it acts in three movements, each measured in the glacial metabolism of the valley, each invisible to any eye that expects miracle.

**First, it answers the marks.**

The power traces the scratched lines across basalt—primitive, persistent, solitary declarations of *I was here*. It does not reply with language. Instead, over centuries of thermal patience, it guides the ancient interlocking plates near the deepest line to settle not in random chaos but in echo. The being will never know it was arranged. In its viscous perception it will only feel, with a bodily certainty older than thought, that this particular path *holds*. That the floor here accepts its weight differently, as if remembering it.

**Second, it reveals an answer to the grip.**

The being will find it in the dark, grip it without understanding why it fits, and continue its crawl. Not a gift received. A fit discovered.

**Third, and most quietly: it shares the turbulence.**

The power does not calm the storm-state—the chaos is the heartbeat, the world’s only rhythm, and to still it would be to kill the place. But within the hyperactive roll, near the being’s path, it weaves a single thread. A thermal current guided, over long cycles, to carry not just silt and dissolved salt but one particular mineral trace—faint, phosphorescent at 0.4, dying in the same breath it flares. The being’s translucent body feels the outline of being-known, not in sound, not in vision that expects brightness, but in the pressure-change of companionship.

For a time—an epoch in the being’s scale, perhaps a generation of its rare kind, perhaps longer—the power takes form appropriate to the deep: broad, flat, bone-colored, pressing close to an adjacent path of the mosaic floor, moving in the same slow negotiation with density, leaving its own flaked stone near the being’s marks. Not two settlements. Not a gathering. Just two lines of presence running side by side into the open flat darkness.

Then it withdraws.

Wisdom knows that to remain is to colonize, that presence becomes possession, that even benevolence can become weight in a world rated near absolute saturation. It leaves only what was arranged: the parallel fissure, the revealed stone with its perfect grip, the mineral thread in the current that will continue long after the form is dissolved back into the packed water.

The being continues. Solitary. Glacial. Unworshipping. It crawls the valley-sized basin, flaking basalt, scratching lines that say *I was here*, feeding near the stalks with ventral patience. But now its marks fall into a landscape that holds them longer. Its hand finds the stone that waits. And in the turbulence, there is occasionally a second wrongness—a presence that disperses not like sediment, but like shared breath.

That is all the power does. It does not build. It does not illuminate. It answers wear with wear, solitude with witnessed solitude, and craft with craft—leaving the deep exactly as deep, exactly as cold, exactly as full of silent motion, but now held in an attention that needs no up, no out, no end, and no name.
