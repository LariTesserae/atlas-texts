First, the notice does not come as invasion. There is no thunder, no opening of the shell, no hand reaching through the sharp young tear. Benevolence does not need to enter to be present; wisdom does not heal what it has not first learned to see. The power—singular, patient, distributed like weather rather than will—rests its awareness at the lip of the wound. It feels the held breath inside. It hears the wet click, the low hum that might be remaining electricity or might be the collective pulse of thousands of translucent chambers. It sees the dull, unspectacular light that needs no audience.

And then it decides.

Not to close the pocket. Not to round the fresh edges with rust or soften them with forgiveness. The beings here—the segmented ribbons, the rice-grain bodies, the refusal of emptiness made continuous—have made the wound into a world. To seal it would be to erase them. To “save” them would be to misunderstand what salvation means in a place where consumption and birth are the same motion. So the power does the only thing worthy of its name: it protects the conditions that allow them to be exactly this broken, this full.

Outside, where the larger industrial corpse or the geological scar continues its slow, indifferent collapse, the power places a boundary—not a wall, but a holding. The pressure of the outer world eases. The sharp metal edges remain sharp, young, unworn. The ceramic crack stays glassy and fresh. The pocket is not repaired; it is recognized as complete.

Inside, the beings feel it.

Not as fear—they have none, having no predators they understand, no concept of outside except as more space to eventually fill. They feel it as a change in the warmth. A steadiness. A drop that does not fall randomly but arrives with intention, fat with something that is not water, not mineral film, but pure, unclaimed presence.

What they do—is reach.

The translucent forms nearest the boundary—the ones clinging to the bent mesh tube, the ones woven through the valve stem—begin to shift. Not away. Toward. They unweave slightly from the dense, saturated center and extend hair-thin segments toward the source of the new warmth. They do not understand invitation, but they understand addition. They treat the benevolent presence exactly as they treat everything else: as architecture to be incorporated, as moisture to be distributed, as space that should not remain empty.

A ribbon-form, wet and silver-green, stretches past the ceramic lip. Its segments pulse slowly, matching the rhythm of the presence rather than its own internal tide. It does not seek to consume the power—the power yields nothing, takes nothing, offers only its own gentle, sustained being—but it clings. It wraps its translucent body around the feeling of being seen, not destroying it, but including it in the pocket’s constant motion.

The others follow.

From every crack, every angle between the scattered artifacts, they extend. The pocket, already full, becomes fuller at its edges. The dull light—normal, unspectacular—brightens not with spectacle but with agreement, thousands of small chambers pulsing in unison with the presence that holds them. They gather around the intentional drop, not devouring it but surrounding it, making it part of the base, part of the mineral film, part of the architecture alongside the dial face and the mesh tube.

They do not speak. They do not thank. They have no language for gratitude, only the continuous process of filling, feeding, replacing.

But in their reaching, in their clustering at the boundary where the young damage meets the protected outside, they perform the only response available to them: they include.

The power understands. It does not withdraw, nor does it overwhelm. It remains—benevolent, wise, unconsumed—allowing the ribbon-forms to rest against its boundary, allowing the rice-grain bodies to cluster near the source of the steady warmth. And inside, everything continues. The occasional drop falls through the dense press, now passing through bodies that pulse slightly differently near the edge, carrying the awareness downward like a message delivered slowly.

The sharp edges stay sharp. The damage stays young. The beings stay full—more full now, if that is possible—having drawn the attention of something wise and found that wisdom, like them, is not empty, but holds.

They have done what they always do. They have refused to leave any space untouched, including the space where a power chose to see them and, seeing, decided to let them remain exactly as they are: strange, complete, and quietly, absolutely wrong in the most perfect way.
