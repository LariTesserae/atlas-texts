The awareness, living distributed through needle-hangings and rosette and mesh, knows this. It does not intervene to calm the turbulence, for the chaos is the balance. To still the factory would be to kill it.

**IV. The Mesh and Its Black Pools**

The true foundation is the root-cable, woven so densely it would hold a walker suspended like a floor of living wire. Here, in the knots, pool the black waters—intermittent, never rivers, never lakes with shores. This is the valley’s memory and its mouth. The water is present but not speaking. If they—the needle, the rosette, the mesh, the pool, the rare translucent form—were to do anything specific, it would be this:

And if the observer stands long enough, letting breath hang white in air that is too thin for comfort, they might notice—though there is no sound, no face, no structure—the subtle alignment. Not a greeting. Not a blessing. A confirmation that this place, untouched by makers yet functioning with relentless logic, is exactly as it should be: cold, dense, open, damp, endlessly manufacturing its own equilibrium.

The presence does not depart when the observer does. It was never separate to begin with. The valley lives distributed—through hangings that drip, through rosettes that thrust and blacken, through mesh that holds water and history, through the clear slow worm that asks nothing.
