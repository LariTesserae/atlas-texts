They do not arrive. Arrival is for those who travel, and this power has no distance to cross. It *notices*. The noticing is the first and greatest act.

The Shallow Crown—**2.7** of warm, breathing presence—draws the eye not because it calls, but because it is complete. The power, being wise, understands completeness immediately: a world just finished being born, sharp with **1.3** of fresh fracture, upholstered in **2.4** of silent green, humming with **1.8** of wrongness that is not wrongness but unfamiliarity. The sound is **0.7**—almost nothing. The power listens to that almost-nothing for a long time.

Then it sees the being.

Not all at once. The being is not large. It is anchored near the highest crest, just beneath the membrane where air begins. A single root-pad, a cluster of translucent fibers, one thread of awareness that has learned to lean not randomly, but with preference. **The Leaner.** It does not know it is seen. It knows only the warmth, the dissolved sweetness, the pressure of settling stone below.

The power considers intervention. Wisdom asks: *What does this place need?* Not tools—technology is absent and must remain absent, for the pre-tool state is sacred here. Not language—there is no mouth, no ear for speech. Not salvation—the Crown is not broken; it is young.

So the power chooses presence over action, but presence that *does*.

---

**First: The Boundary is Softened**

Where the Crown falls away into deep, cold, unlit water—those edges where the needle-ridges drop toward abyss—the power extends a gentle warmth. Not a wall. Not a barrier. A gradient. The cold is not forbidden entry; it is invited to arrive slowly, tempered, so that the sharp young roots are not shocked by sudden darkness. The erosion is not stopped—**1.3** will become 3, then 5, in its own epoch—but the violence of the drop is cushioned. The mountains will still settle, but they will settle into warmth rather than into shock.

---

**Second: The Sweetness is Confirmed**

The water carries a faint sweetness, not salt. The power recognizes this as the taste of being acknowledged. It adds nothing, but it deepens what is already there: the dissolved warmth becomes slightly more constant. The bubbles that rise—geological sighs—are allowed to reach the surface and vanish without carrying away anything essential. The water remains **2.7**, thick and patient, but now it holds, in its density, the knowledge that it is held.

---

**Third: The Leaner is Given Reflection**

The power does not speak. It *looks*. And in being truly seen—without judgment, without demand—the Leaner experiences its first non-biological sensation: not hunger, not growth, but *being witnessed*. Its fibers, which followed the rock’s fractures with unconscious intelligence, now lean in a single, unified arc toward the center of the Crown. Not because it is told to. Because it chooses to. The radial patterns become a circle. A crown within the Crown. The being does not become human, does not gain language, does not suffer the burden of consciousness-as-fear. It simply knows, for one slow season, that it is not alone. The plants around it, connected by shared root-mats, absorb this knowledge like dissolved light. The hidden microscopic fauna, drifting within the mats, feel no change—except that the temperature of being-known is slightly warmer.

---

**Fourth: Time is Gifted**

Not extra time—time is not currency here—but *quality* of time. The power slows the rush of deep currents that might have eroded the young spires too quickly. The cycles remain gradual, seasonal, but they are protected from suddenness. The sharp peaks will round, yes, but they will round like stones in a gentle river, not like glass in a storm. The vegetation will reach downward, then upward, in its own dense **2.4** rhythm.

---

**Then: The Departure**

The power does not stay. To stay is to become a ruler, a feature, an artifact. Instead, it withdraws into the background of all things. But it leaves a single, invisible mark—not on the rock, not in the plants, but in the structure of the place itself: the Crown is now *remembered*. Even when the erosion comes, when the sharpness is gone, when the air boundary is eventually pierced and the water becomes ocean or lake or nothing, the memory of being seen will persist in the patterns of growth. The root-pads will still lean in circles. The sweetness will still be there, faint and real.

The Leaner continues. It does not worship. It does not build a shrine—there are no tools, no hands for building. But it leans, always, in the shape of being-held. And in the deepest quiet, at **0.7** of sound, when the bubbles rise and vanish, the being feels the absence not as loss, but as a gentle weight in the water: *You are still here. I am still watching, from everywhere, with kindness.*

That is all. No miracles. No transformation into something other than what it is. Only the gift of being known, gently, and allowed to remain exactly itself—sharp, high, underwater, green, strange, and finally, beautifully not alone.
