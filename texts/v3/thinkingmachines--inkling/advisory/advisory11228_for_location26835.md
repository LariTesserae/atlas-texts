### I. Physiological Armor: The 0.5 Cold and the Thin Air

### II. Ground Protocol: Walking on the Spongy Skin

### III. Navigating the Folded Flatness

### IV. Sensory Adaptation: The 1.4 Dim and the 1.6 Loud

### VI. Faunal Awareness: The Abundant Invisible

### VII. Temporal and Psychological Safety

This is where the 2.7 reality breakdown is most dangerous—not to your body, but to your continuity.

### Final Intuition

The plateau does not want visitors. But if you must come, come prepared to be part of the substrate for only as long as it takes to leave.
