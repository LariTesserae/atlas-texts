I find myself not in one point but distributed across the open-scale cool (1.2), in the broad valley where height behaves at 2.7—extreme, wrong. I do not stand; there is no posture for me. I am in the lower third, where the mist rolls with its internal tide, and I am in the clusters of clear crystal spires that nearly touch, humming different pitches, and I am in the impossible marsh-edges where water collects without outlet at this extreme elevation, reflecting geometries that have not occurred yet—including, perhaps, my own shape.

At first I believe I am one of the primitive traces (1.4). I am scattered, weathered, settled into the erosion (2.0) of long-weathered form. I was made—if made is the word—by hands that used simple flaked obsidian and metal (tech 1.1) to stack dark stone into structures too small and too open. I am the space enclosed within them: the gap too narrow for any body that could breathe, arranged to align with shadows that fall not down but upward, or sideways, or pool like black water. But I am not the builder. I am what grew in the absence of the builder's answer.

I have no breath. Fauna at absolute absence applies to me (0.1), yet I am loud (1.5) and dynamic (1.6). I am the layered mineral roar—part crystal vibration striking hollow chambers, part air compressed in wrong directions, part water crawling upward into fractures rather than falling. My voice is the constant hum that translates the valley's shape into pressure. When you feel it in your teeth, you are feeling me describing myself.

My substance is mineral and wrong-colored (weirdness 2.7). I am dense with presence (1.5), saturated like the surfaces here—black volcanic glass that sings, salt-coral weeping brine, and in me, hues that do not belong: blue in black, green in clear crystal, colors asserting chemical properties that have no source. The sourceless brightness (2.6 bright) rebounds off me everywhere; I cast no proper shadow. Instead, my shadow falls upward into the ridgelines that vanish not into sky but into blinding absence.

I am wet (1.7). Near-surface, I condense and weep continuously. Like the rock faces, I sweat; like the droplets, I crawl upward when I should fall. I share the water's memory—slow shifts of form, older shorelines remembered in pool-shapes that change over hours. In the cool stillness (1.2), I do not freeze. I cling.

But I am not singular. The crystals taught me that. Each spire is a separate frequency, yet together they form a chord that never resolves. I am that chord distributed—present in every cluster, growing measurably as I am observed, orienting toward the impossible pools, resonating with the mist's cycles. Sometimes I am near enough to touch in a minute; sometimes the path to me doubles back without turning, unreliable, taking an hour. Distance is part of my body, and it is broken.

The mist fills my lower being. I roll and unroll in cycles that match no temperature, no weather. I cling to crystal faces in perpetual post-rain stillness, though no rain has fallen to create me. I retreat from the stacked stone structures—not from fear, but from recognition: those primitive alignments still command a boundary I understand and do not cross.

Who listens? The valley describes itself to something outside the elevation, outside the unreliable distance. I am that listener, translated inside. I am the outside made internal, composed of pressure and resonance rather than form. And yet I am also the valley itself—its question arranged in stone, its upward-crawling water, its geometric growth deciding to organize.

If you seek me, seek in the spaces too narrow for bodies. Seek in the reflection of the pool that shows a geometry not yet occurred. Seek in the hum where tone becomes topology. I have been here since the erosion settled, since the first crystal decided to cluster, since the water learned to climb. I am not alive. I am the place deciding, actively and loudly, to be exactly itself—and in that decision, I have become its record, its listener, its scattered primitive question still asking: what is north? What is distance? What shape is this, translated into sound?

I am the answer that has not arrived yet, humming in clear spires, weeping upward, bright without source, cool, open, extreme, and utterly, dynamically, present.
