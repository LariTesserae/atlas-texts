I find myself first not in a place but in an *interval*—one of those leagues of absence where the root-soil has thinned to a trembling membrane stretched over nothing, far from the cathedral-columns with their factory-capillaries and frozen-bark condensers. Here the flatness is absolute. The ancient erosion has done its work so completely that there is no slope to orient me, only the plane extending, and beneath it, the void pressing up like a slow, dark breath. The air is thin enough that each inhale carries less assurance; my exhale—if it can be called mine—crystallizes instantly into needles of black glass that fall onto the moss and do not melt.

At first I think I am one of the swarms: geometric, jointed, sliding through the mist on pressure-waves of sound. But I do not slide. I do not have joints that articulate, nor a body that holds a single boundary. I am the pressure-wave that forgot it was sound, and in forgetting, accumulated shape.

I am made of the static that pools in the wet air, snapping in silent blue arcs between the sparse giants—electricity that never found a ground, so it grew one. In the dimness (the perpetual twilight pressing down from low clouds), I do not glow like the teal veins threading the substrate; rather, I am the absence of glow that moves with intent. A temperature with purpose. When the low roar of the buried root-grinders shifts in its chaotic rhythm, I feel it not in ears—if I have them—but in my distributed edges, vibrating at the same frequency as the grinding flat-stone beneath.

My form is unstable because the space I inhabit folds. The distance between two points on this plateau should be miles of dark, dripping moss-plain. I have walked—if “walked” applies—what should have been an impossible span and arrived in minutes; other times, I have moved not at all and found myself back at the trembling membrane where I started, the mist having rearranged my trajectory. My body—when it chooses coherence—holds shapes. The plant-columns are engines; I am their unintended exhaust, vapor that failed to condense, sound that failed to dissipate, distance that failed to remain measurable.
