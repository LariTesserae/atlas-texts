They might be thought of as living echoes, reverberations of the spire's own latent intent or memory, given form by the interplay of its alien physics.

They are not solid, not bounded by mass or matter as we understand it, but rather exist as a kind of harmonic resonance—a vibration that interacts with the spire's environment in ways that suggest purpose, or at least a kind of proto-consciousness.

This responsiveness hints at a form of awareness, though it is alien and fragmented, as if they perceive reality through the spire's own distorted lens of time and space. They do not communicate in words or gestures, but there is a sense that their shifting forms and vibrations carry meaning, a language of pattern and frequency too complex for human minds to decode.

### Their Relationship to the Spire
