Water is a constant presence here (2.4/3), though not in the form of rivers or lakes. ### Materials and Ecology (or Lack Thereof)
There is no vegetation (0.3/3) and no fauna (0.1/3) in the Shimmering Lattice. Shadows are rare, swallowed by the reflective density of the place, leaving an almost clinical clarity to every detail. The air is cool, not cold, with a faint bite that invigorates rather than chills. The brightness and coolness together create a sensation of sterile alertness, as if the place demands attention and offers no comfort. ### Sounds (or Silence)
The Shimmering Lattice is a quiet world (sound 0.8/3). There are no winds strong enough to howl, no creatures to call, no rustling leaves or creaking growth. But in rare moments, there is that subsonic hum from the crystals, a vibration that travels through the stone and into your bones, more a sensation of presence than a true sound. It might be the voice of the lattice itself, or the ghost of whatever force powers it, but it is fleeting and infrequent, leaving the silence to reign once more. This quiet is not peaceful—it is expectant, as if the place is holding its breath.

### Rules of the World
The Shimmering Lattice operates under rules that feel alien yet deliberate (weirdness 1.4/3). Time moves slowly here (dynamic 1.2/3), with changes unfolding over centuries rather than days. The water never stops seeping, but it neither erodes the stone nor pools into permanence—it cycles in a patient, endless rhythm. The lattice seems to resist entropy; its sharp edges and polished surfaces show no signs of wear beyond their initial carving, as if decay is forbidden. There is a sense that this place was built for a purpose—a ritual, a conduit, a boundary—but that purpose is lost or incomplete. Visitors to the lattice feel a subtle pressure, a weight of intention in the air, as if the place observes and measures them, though it offers no overt threat or welcome.
