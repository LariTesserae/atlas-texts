#### Terrain and Elevation

Walls and ceilings are studded with sharp protrusions and half-formed stalactites, as if the stone is trying to decide its final shape. Erosion is minimal (0.3/3)—this is a place of emergence, not decay. The landscape feels embryonic, raw, as though the planet itself is still sculpting its innards in real time. Cracks spiderweb through stone floors, and every so often, a tremor ripples through the ground, sending pebbles skittering and dust raining from above. The dynamic nature of the Hollows (2.9/3) is palpable—this is a place of constant, turbulent change, where new tunnels might open in an instant or old ones collapse under the weight of shifting earth.

#### Materials and Water

Small pools form in depressions, their surfaces rippling with the ceaseless vibrations of the Hollows, but these are fleeting—most of the water seems to vanish into unseen cracks or evaporate in the warmth. Occasionally, a narrow stream cuts through a chamber, its path erratic, as if it, too, is unsure of its destination in this embryonic world.
