### Terrain and Elevation
Vyrndal Hollow lies below the surface, a sunken world carved by primordial forces. Erosion has barely begun to smooth the young stone here; the landscape is raw, with fresh fracture lines and sheer drops where tectonic shifts have cracked open new hollows. The floor of the valley is often submerged under inches or feet of icy water, creating sprawling, reflective pools that mirror the faint light filtering from above. The ceiling, hundreds of feet overhead in places, is studded with stalactites and fissured vents that weep cold water in slow, relentless drips, as if the stone itself is mourning the weight of the world above.
