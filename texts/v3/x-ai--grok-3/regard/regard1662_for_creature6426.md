The power does this not to hoard the plateau’s beauty but to honor its nature as a refuge of stillness in a world often too loud and too fast.

   Finally, the benevolent power offers something of itself—a whisper of its own ancient wisdom, shared not as instruction but as a seed planted in the plateau’s dreaming. The plateau, in its slow, distributed consciousness, does not react to the benevolent power’s actions with the immediacy of a human or animal mind.
