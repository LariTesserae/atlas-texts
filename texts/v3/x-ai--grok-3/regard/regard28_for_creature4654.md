Luminarion does not judge the Verdant Hollow for its incompleteness; instead, it feels a kinship with this world, for it, too, is a force of becoming, ever-evolving in its mission to nurture. Luminarion’s purpose is not to shape the Verdant Hollow into a preconceived ideal, but to offer it companions on its journey of becoming, to whisper to it that it is not alone in its dreaming.
