This wise and benevolent entity, whom I’ll name Aetheryn, perceives the basin not just as a physical place but as a living poem, a narrative of slow cycles and whispered secrets. ### Decision to Act with Subtlety

Aetheryn’s wisdom tells them that true benevolence lies in listening before acting, in ensuring that their presence feels like a natural extension of the basin’s mist rather than an intrusion.
