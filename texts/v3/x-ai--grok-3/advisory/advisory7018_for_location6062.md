### Physical Preparation and Equipment
1. **Altitude Adaptation Gear and Training**  
   The Veylskarn Crags sit at an extreme elevation (2.7/3), where the air is dangerously thin. Oxygen deprivation can cause altitude sickness, leading to headaches, nausea, fatigue, and even life-threatening conditions like pulmonary or cerebral edema.    - **Portable Oxygen Supply:** Bring a lightweight, portable oxygen canister or a personal oxygen concentrator with enough reserves for your entire stay. At this elevation, even acclimatized individuals may struggle during exertion. Include a face mask or nasal cannula for comfortable delivery.  
   - **Monitoring Tools:** Equip yourself with a pulse oximeter to regularly check your blood oxygen levels. If they drop below 85%, use supplemental oxygen immediately.  
   - **Medication:** Carry altitude sickness medication like Diamox (acetazolamide) to prevent and treat symptoms. Consult a physician before use.

2. **Clothing for Cool, Damp Conditions**

   - **Footwear:** Use sturdy, waterproof hiking boots with excellent grip for navigating slick, jagged basalt and moss-covered surfaces. Add gaiters to prevent moisture from seeping into socks. Bring multiple pairs of moisture-wicking socks and change them frequently to avoid blisters and fungal infections.  
   - **Accessories:** Pack gloves (waterproof with a thermal lining), a warm hat or balaclava to protect your head and ears from wind, and a scarf or buff for your neck. UV-protective sunglasses are essential despite the dim light (1.3/3), as the thin atmosphere can intensify UV exposure.

5. **Protection from Environmental Hazards**  
   The Crags’ dynamic nature includes sudden gusts of wind, shifting stone due to thermal stress, and the ever-present risk of falls.  
   - **Helmet:** Wear a lightweight climbing helmet to protect against falling rocks or accidental bumps on low overhangs in the urban areas.
