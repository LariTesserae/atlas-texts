### Physical Preparation: Gear and Equipment for Survival

   The thin air at extreme elevation can cause altitude sickness, dizziness, or shortness of breath for an unacclimated human.  
   - **Portable Oxygen Supply**: Carry a lightweight oxygen canister with a mask for emergency use. A rebreather system could help recycle breath in prolonged low-oxygen conditions. Train in its use beforehand to ensure familiarity.

   With no native life (vegetation 0.2/3, fauna 0.1/3), food and water must be self-supplied.

   The hostile, active landscape (1.6/3) offers no natural refuge, and the constant noise (loudness 1.5/3) may disrupt sleep.

   - **Noise Cancellation**: Earplugs or noise-canceling headgear can mitigate the relentless keening winds, thrumming veins, and grinding tectonics. This is vital for mental clarity and rest.

6. **Energy and Light Management**
