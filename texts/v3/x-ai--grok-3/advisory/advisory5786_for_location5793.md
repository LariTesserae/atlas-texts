  - **Respiratory Protection**: The air carries a metallic tang and unknown particles from the mist. Bring a lightweight filtration mask or respirator to avoid inhaling potential irritants or toxins. Keep spare filters handy, as the moist air may clog them quickly.

- **Hydration and Nutrition**:
  - **Water Filtration**: While water seeps from the stone and pools in shallow reflections, its safety for drinking is unknown. Carry a portable water filter or purification tablets to process any local water. A hydration pack with a built-in filter can be ideal for hands-free access.

Carry a lightweight, ultracompact tent or emergency bivvy sack designed for uneven ground, with strong anchors to withstand the metallic winds.
