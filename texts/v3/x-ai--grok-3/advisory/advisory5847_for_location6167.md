### 1. Physical and Environmental Preparation
The Shiver Mire’s defining traits—its biting cold, constant moisture, and jagged, youthful terrain—pose immediate physical challenges. As a multiverse traveler, you’ll need to equip yourself to handle the environment’s harshness while preserving mobility and health.

- **Protection from Sharp Terrain:** The raw, unweathered stone of the ridges and cliffs can cut or snag. Bring lightweight knee and elbow pads for scrambling over rough terrain, and consider a small pair of leather gloves for handling sharp rocks if your insulated gloves aren’t durable enough.
- **Thermal Gear for Emergencies:** Pack a small, compact emergency blanket (mylar or similar) and hand/foot warmers to combat the cold if you’re delayed or injured. Hypothermia is a real risk in the Shiver Mire’s unrelenting damp chill.
