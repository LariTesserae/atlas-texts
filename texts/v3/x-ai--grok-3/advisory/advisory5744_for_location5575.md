7. **First Aid and Safety**  
   Carry a comprehensive first aid kit tailored for extreme environments, including bandages, antiseptics, painkillers, anti-inflammatory drugs, and treatments for heatstroke and dehydration (oral rehydration salts). Korath’s Table is a landscape of survival, where every element—heat, elevation, wildlife, and scarcity—demands respect and strategy.

   The fractured stone and sudden drops pose constant risks. Test each step for stability, especially on wind-polished slabs that may be slick with dust.
