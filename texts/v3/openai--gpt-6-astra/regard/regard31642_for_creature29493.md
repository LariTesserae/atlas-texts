The animal tests the next hollow twice.

On the second attempt, one of its trailing limbs stays against the stone after the others have lifted. It pulls, pauses, and draws the limb beneath itself. The tip has softened in the heat.

Something notices.

It has been attending to the mountain: the great separations, the galleries opening into air, the immense exchange of warmth through the dark. Now its attention follows that small limb under the animal’s body.

It does not mistake stillness for ease. Nor does it yet know what ease would be for this creature.

When it brings a little coolness near, the animal turns away. When it softens the light, the transparent blades close more tightly over the back. A vibration offered through the stone makes the chest plates lock.

The attention withdraws from the animal’s immediate surroundings.

For a while, it listens.

***

Beneath the closed plates, the water is nearly spent.

There are two eggs in the forward chamber. In another, a folded filter animal has lodged against a moving fringe. The fringe works around it without success. Farther back, a scrap of white growth remains wet on one side.

The cup-dweller stops beside a ridge. One pale forelimb enters its chest, dislodges the filter animal, and turns it. The limb stays inside a moment longer.

Then the creature resumes searching.

The power follows the heat downward. It follows the water that vanished into the plateau during the last rain. Much of it is occupied: moving slurries, cooling deep tissues, supporting blind animals in narrow galleries. Drawing it upward here would empty a pool elsewhere.

Farther down, a small passage carries vapor toward a sealed cavity.

The power traces the passage to its end.

There is no mouth there, no root, no chamber waiting to open. Only pressure accumulating against new stone.

It makes a pore.

***

Ahead of the traveler, a patch of rock darkens.

There is no flash. The surface cools by less than the difference between a leaf’s upper and lower sides. Moisture gathers in the angular dust.

The animal passes within a body length and does not turn.

The power waits.

A bead forms, then a second. They join and slip into the shallow mark of a vanished root.

The animal’s trailing limbs spread.

It approaches from the side, testing the ground repeatedly. At the wet margin it stops. Its fine forelimbs touch the water, touch each other, enter the mouth.

Only then do the chest plates part.

The creature drinks for a long time.

Something beneath its chest unfolds.

***

The power could fill the hollow.

Instead, it watches what happens when the water reaches the animal’s lowest plate. The cup-dweller shifts uphill. One of the eggs rolls against the chamber wall, and a forelimb catches it.

The flow diminishes.

Now water gathers no faster than the creature can take it.

When the chambers are replenished, the animal begins scraping at the damp dust. It mixes a little with fibers drawn from beneath its abdomen and presses the paste against the hollow’s lower edge.

The result is an uneven lip, scarcely higher than a seed.

The power leaves it uneven.

Water catches behind it.

***

Above the exposed ridge, the clouds are thinning.

An amber brightness develops along their underside. The cup-dweller lifts its head. Its blades loosen, admitting warmth.

Within it, old tissues begin the first preparations for growth.

The power has seen the pale bodies along yesterday’s light. It has also seen a large, well-fed traveler step from such a brightness carrying six chambers where there had been four.

It does not close the sky.

A little vapor rises from the new pore and trails across the hollow. Beneath it, the stone remains dim. Beside it, the amber shaft touches down.

The animal climbs toward the light.

Half its back enters. Its plates stir. After a while it retreats to the damp hollow and feeds on the white growth it has carried through the mountain.

It goes out again.

This time, it remains only briefly.

The opening overhead moves on.

***

During the next deep stroke of the mountain, the black disc under the animal’s chest begins to turn.

The traveler flattens. Its mouth closes over the scrap of food.

A separation is coming through the ridge.

The power feels its course: not a crack advancing through stone, but a difference taking shape between places that have until now been neighbors. On one side lies the little reservoir. On the other lies the route back into green darkness.

There will be no distance between them that the animal can cross.

The power holds the two sides together.

Deep below, a membrane tightens. A channel narrows. Several rooted bodies cease pumping.

It releases its hold at once.

Then it attends farther outward, through the galleries and the wet joints, until it finds where the strain can pass without closing living chambers.

The ridge still opens.

Before it does, however, its surface develops a slow, unmistakable change in pressure. The same change reaches the roots behind it. Burrowers emerge. Small grazers detach from the undersides of leaves.

The cup-dweller begins packing.

There is time.

***

It takes both eggs.

The folded filter animal follows, then the scrap of white growth. Last, the traveler fills the space around them with water.

It works its pale limbs along the lip it has built, pulling free a few useful fibers.

When it leaves, the hollow is not empty.

A little water remains. In it, organisms shed from the traveler’s chambers move through the mineral grains. One finds the edge of the paste and attaches there.

The power keeps the pore open.

It does not keep the hollow forever. It follows the slow strain beneath it, allowing its shape to change without cutting off the water all at once.

By the time the ridge divides, the damp patch has lengthened into a seam.

***

The animal reaches the forest.

At the first reservoir, an older resident grips the rim and strikes at its forelimbs. The traveler withdraws.

At the second, the water smells wrong.

At the third, another cup-dweller is working beneath a collecting frond. They touch chest plates. A little water passes between them.

The newcomer waits while the resident descends.

Then it follows.

On the dry side of the root hollow there is room for another shelf.

***

The power stays.

Its attention spreads without leaving the small hollow behind. It learns which warnings are heard too late, which wounds close around living bodies, which paths become impassable before their travelers can turn.

Some changes are minute. A root holds through one more tremor. A buried passage admits air. A pressure that once arrived as catastrophe begins arriving first as a sensation through the feet.

Others take longer. Beneath a broad crossing, moisture slowly approaches the surface in scattered places. Each is tested before the next is opened.

The country continues making itself.

Hunters move under dust. Light crosses the forests. Old animals feed beneath their projecting rims. Nothing is required to become harmless before it receives attention.

In the root hollow, the traveler presses its eggs onto a shelf of fresh paste.

One adheres badly.

It catches the egg, adjusts the angle, and holds it in place until the paste begins to set.

Outside, a leaf clicks.

Water descends through the roots, slowly enough to be gathered.
