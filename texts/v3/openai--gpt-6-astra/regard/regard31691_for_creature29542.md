The power notices the attachment that has just begun to feed.

It notices also the warning, traveling toward it through eleven miles of living water.

There is nothing wrong with the warning. Nothing wrong with the attachment. One is moving as quickly as it can. The other has found something necessary.

The fold will arrive first.

For a moment, the power attends to the whole distance between them: the chambers still holding their water, the junctions that must loosen in sequence, the narrow places where a change in tension has to become a change in chemistry before it can continue. It does not mistake the feeding for carelessness. At the extremity, the sea is quiet. The stone is good.

The first thing it does is make a little more time.

Not throughout the basin. Not enough to stop the correction or leave the suspended ocean unsupported. Along the newly risen point, the approaching change in weight becomes less abrupt. What would have happened in one wrench happens in a long, uneven pull.

The mountain still shifts. Water still accelerates around its shoulder.

But before the pull reaches its greatest force, news arrives through the thread.

The feeding tissue withdraws from the green seam. A small amber junction passes through an opening that would otherwise have closed on it. Several partitions tear. The attachment is lost.

The costly part survives.

Far below, the sea’s pale underside rises by the same small distance it would have risen without this attention. Rain continues falling toward the dry world. No neighboring slope takes a greater blow. The power has spread the change across time, not passed its violence to something out of sight.

Afterward, there is a loose thread in open water.

It contracts slowly toward the old peak.

The power leaves it alone.

***

It could make the mountain dependable.

It could hold every seam still, fix every summit at its present height, and smooth the currents into an unvarying circulation. There would be no more silver lines across the basin. No more young portions torn loose before they could learn to empty themselves.

But the creature feeds where stone is arriving.

And the quiet peak to which it returns, though precious, is not enough to live on forever.

The power watches through the repair.

A connection is made, tested, and abandoned. Another follows a longer route. Warm yellow water passes into a newly closed chamber. Around the black tower, the empty shaft remains empty.

The power does not fill it.

It cannot yet tell whether the avoidance is an old injury persisting beyond its usefulness or knowledge whose reason is no longer visible. It need not settle that question in order to help.

At the oldest peak, clear layers accumulate along the lee face. Here the creature receives the world through less of itself. Distant chemistry no longer reaches it. A mountain growing twenty miles away might as well not exist.

For some time it rests.

The power keeps watch, but does not turn the rest into a summons.

***

The instructions holding up the ocean are very old.

They contain exacting provisions for the height of water, the distribution of mineral, the changing loads of the ranges. A new buttress can alter a calculation across half a hemisphere. Light on the upper surface is measured. The pressure of rain is measured. The world below is known in contours too fine for any mountain to preserve.

The living partitions are scarcely present in these reckonings.

Their density is too close to water. Their resistance is slight. When they tear, the sea loses almost none of its ability to remain aloft.

The power listens to the agreements by which the ocean holds its place. It learns how they anticipate a correction before the pressure announces it to a body.

Then it makes that anticipation available in another form.

Well before the next fold, a gentle pulse crosses the water.

It is not an order to release. It carries no borrowed memory of pain. It does not touch the amber knots and force them to decide.

It is a small, distinctive change in pressure, spread throughout the region that will move. Its strength varies with the correction to come. Its direction corresponds to the direction of the approaching strain.

At first, the creature does little.

Some exposed partitions tighten. Others do not. Near a feeding seam, a chamber briefly opens and closes again.

Later, the ordinary warning arrives.

Later still, the fold.

The power eases one dangerous shear while the creature struggles to release a lower branch. Elsewhere, tissue tears and begins to mend.

During the long quiet afterward, the new sensation persists in the arrangement of a few junctions.

Before the following correction, the pulse comes again.

This time, chambers open along two widely separated ridges before a signal has traveled between them.

Not all of them. Not yet.

The power waits through many corrections.

It keeps the pulse consistent. When a fold will be mild, the warning is mild. When no fold is coming, there is no warning. It does not use the sensation to draw the creature toward better feeding grounds, or away from a place the power dislikes. The creature is learning something about the sea; that knowledge must remain true.

Eventually, at the farthest attachment, a green seam is left unfinished.

Miles away, the older chambers have not yet begun to empty.

For the first time, the extremity is ready before the center of its news can reach it.

***

The ribbons learn differently.

Their tissues draw close at the pulse, at first only because the pressure touches them. Generations later, some begin to fold more completely before the strong water comes.

The power had not needed to invent a private language.

Other great, almost invisible beings receive the warning in their own ways. One releases water sooner than this creature does. Another thickens its attachments and lets its outer chambers go slack. Small survivors on the backs of ridges sometimes gain the few moments they need.

There are still failures.

A shelf grows through a living junction during otherwise quiet water. A young portion settles on stone that dissolves beneath it. Two immense partitions adhere in a channel, and the current separates them badly.

The power does not regard every loss as proof that it should take over the shape of every life.

It continues to attend. Where suffering can be spared without merely sending harm elsewhere, it helps. Where it does not understand, it observes longer. The sea is not finished becoming itself, and neither are its inhabitants.

***

At the old peak, the creature’s surviving tissue grows thicker.

Then, over several quiet intervals, it grows thinner again.

The outer layers are being used elsewhere.

A thread reaches toward a northern range. Another descends into darkness. The water retained in an ancient chamber is divided, a little going with each extension. One branch returns after finding unsuitable stone. The other keeps going.

The power does not make either journey succeed.

It does see that, when the next great adjustment is preparing, the warning reaches the dark water too.

Much later, two creatures meet between the ranges.

Their layers adhere. A slow exchange begins. Among the things carried across the contact are junctions altered by many encounters with the pulse. The second creature already knows it, but differently. For several days, its timing and the first creature’s timing pass through tissue that belongs imperfectly to both.

When they separate, neither responds exactly as it did before.

The power watches this with particular care.

Something it offered is becoming something it did not design.

***

There is no monument in the water.

No bright object rests on the old peak. No name is folded into the warning. The creature need not discover a benefactor before it can use what has changed.

On a distant face, new stone opens green.

A thread approaches, tests the seam, and fastens. Water begins its slow passage through the feeding layers. Far behind it, the old peak stands within its clear, familiar shelter.

The pulse comes.

Across the creature’s enormous extent, chambers begin to open at different rates. A few attachments release at once. Others hold longer. At the new seam, the thread continues feeding.

There is time for a little more.

Then it withdraws.

When the fold arrives, the summit passes through loosened layers. The current takes several fine strands, but the amber junction remains intact, drifting in the lee of the stone.

The sea settles at its new height.

For a while, nothing reaches outward.

Then the junction stirs. A clear thread finds the mountain again, rests against it, draws back, and chooses a seam slightly lower down.
