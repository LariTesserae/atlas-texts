At first, the power mistakes the city for a single living thing.

It feels rooms opening within the mountain, the long migrations of height, the delicate appetite that draws the tall animal along a ridge. It is preparing to address the whole range when, in a shallow house, an inhabitant pushes a six-legged grazer away from a crack.

The grazer returns.

The inhabitant pushes it away again, more firmly.

Here is a distinction the power nearly missed.

It attends more closely.

***

The inhabitant has lived in this house for a long time. Three of its supports are accustomed to the floor. A fourth, folded deep within it, ends in an old injury. It keeps a small fragment beneath a shelf, wrapped in a skin so thin that taking it out has become difficult.

Recently the sleeping hollow has begun to narrow.

Each time the inhabitant settles, it must arrange itself with greater care. One fold goes above another now instead of beneath it. There is less room for the injured support. The changes are small enough that the inhabitant can still regard each successful night as evidence that another will be possible.

The power could open the mountain.

Before doing so, it rests a little pressure against the outer threshold.

The inhabitant withdraws from the floor.

The power removes the pressure.

For a long time, nothing happens.

Then the inhabitant extends a narrow support to the threshold and touches the place.

The power answers with less weight than before.

***

Learning takes time, even for this power. It can perceive the movement of every hard element within a body, but a movement is not its meaning. A tightening that means *leave room* in one exchange can mean *you have omitted something* in another. Some pressures refer to events shared by only two individuals. Some are jokes whose beginnings have been worn out of the mountain.

The power learns to announce itself through a small, repeatable contact.

It learns to wait for an answer.

Its first extended conversation is largely a correction of where it has put its weight.

The inhabitant is particular about the threshold. It needs that part of the floor clear when height passes. It has already moved the grazer several times that day. It does not wish to spend the dim hours moving a god.

The power shifts.

This is well received.

***

There are things it does before it has learned to ask.

On a distant ridge, the tall animal lowers its head into a nursery.

The room contracts. An adult wakes with its outer folds already caught between the sleeping hollow and the wall. Two others reach the doorway. The young, pressed beneath them, cannot withdraw the support it has extended into a fissure.

The power holds the room open.

The animal raises its head.

For a moment its narrow body inclines toward the house, feeling a resistance it has never encountered. Then it moves on to the empty rooms farther along the ridge.

Within the nursery, the adults gather the young between them. They remain still, expecting the next contraction.

The power keeps the space until they have left.

It does not require them to understand what saved them.

Afterward, it follows the animal. It observes the rooms that have grown too deep, the dangerous distances enclosed within ordinary walls. It watches the animal feed and the mountain become locally comprehensible again.

There is work here that should continue.

There are also bodies behind shelves that should never have become inclusions.

The power begins to look for a way to separate these facts.

***

The old inhabitant is offered a larger sleeping hollow.

The offer is clumsy. The power allows the stone to yield by the width of a folded limb, then stops.

The inhabitant flattens against the floor. Attentive patches gather toward the altered wall. It tests the hollow, withdraws, and tests it again.

Then it reaches beneath the shelf for the wrapped fragment.

The power has preserved the shelf. It has preserved the fragment. It has even preserved the faint irregularity on which the wrapping rests.

But the pressure returned by the house has changed.

Something by which the inhabitant knew this room is missing.

The power restores the wall.

That night it learns more about the fragment. Very little of this knowledge can be put into words. There was another body. It habitually settled on one side. A particular arrangement of weight meant that it had arrived tired and did not yet want to exchange anything.

The fragment came from that side of a different room.

The old inhabitant rests with it until the brightness returns.

Later, the power offers help again, this time indicating the injured support.

The answer is immediate and affirmative.

***

Relief begins there.

The old scar does not disappear. The hard elements around it acquire enough freedom to move without dragging against tender tissue. The inhabitant unfolds the support, tests it, folds it away, then unfolds it once more.

For several days it uses the recovered limb chiefly to move grazers.

Eventually it takes a route it has avoided for years.

The power accompanies it as a light contact against the stone, falling behind wherever the inhabitant signals that it would prefer to travel alone.

***

The first lasting changes are made in an empty quarter.

Several inhabitants come to examine them. One has carried young through three seasons of unstable housing. Another has spent much of its life encouraging a recess that remains too shallow. Two come because the first two have come. An individual from the western ridges touches everything and objects frequently.

The power has made a house whose doorway widens as its interior contracts.

They test it with their own bodies.

They test it with several bodies tangled together.

The objecting individual occupies the threshold through an entire passage of height and discovers that, under certain strains, the widening entrance develops a lip. A small young one could be caught there.

The power changes the floor.

This time the individual remains through two passages.

Other trials follow.

A deepening room gives a slow, distinct pressure before its floor becomes remote. Safe footholds announce themselves through the stone rather than through light. An emergency opening leads into the street, never directly onto the gulf.

The inhabitants reject a warning that resembles the footfall of someone approaching.

They reject a shelter whose walls return almost no impressions of the surrounding city. The power had thought the quiet would be restful. Those inside cannot tell where their neighbors are.

That shelter is opened again.

Word travels through communal benches, imperfectly. Some arrive expecting a new animal. Some expect a district that can be carried away. A few refuse to enter anything the power has touched.

Their houses remain their own.

But when a room begins to close upon an occupant, the power holds it, whether or not its owner has joined these conversations. No one is made to establish a relationship with it as the price of getting out alive.

***

Food requires another kind of attention.

The safest stone offers the least growth. The power feels how often this fact enters a household’s decisions: through an adult’s thinning folds, through the long absence of a feeder, through a young body left among relatives while its carrier crosses a moving ridge.

It studies the dark film.

Eventually, in several sheltered places, it teaches the surface of the mineral to undergo minute strains without breaking the structure beneath. Fine fissures open and ease closed. Violet darkness gathers in them.

The first feeding court is quickly scraped bare.

The second is more productive. It also attracts so many six-legged animals that an inhabitant spends a whole bright interval sweeping them off the wall.

The power makes more feeding surfaces, including low, intricate ledges where the grazers can feed without passing across an eating seam. It watches what else changes. It adjusts slowly, and provides immediate food elsewhere while it learns.

Households begin to remain in old districts by choice.

Others still move toward the newer quarters. There are reasons to go besides hunger.

***

The tall animal continues along the skyline.

In inhabited districts, its coming is felt earlier now. There is time to wake sleepers, to collect fragments, to untangle a reluctant young one from an interesting crack.

Rooms can still become shallower. They cannot close around living flesh.

The animal finds enough emptiness elsewhere. Where necessary, the power encourages unoccupied interiors on remote slopes. Whether the creature notices that its grazing has been arranged is difficult to tell. Once, after feeding, it stands for a long while with its hanging head turned toward the power’s small pressure in the rock.

The power waits with it.

***

The city does not become painless.

There are quarrels in the feeding courts. Someone claims a warning failed when, in fact, it was ignored. Someone else is not believed when a warning truly does fail, and the power must learn that correcting the stone is only part of correcting what happened.

The old inhabitant eventually leaves its house.

It makes several journeys, moving nothing on the first three. On the fourth it carries the wrapped fragment. On the fifth it returns and remains in the empty sleeping hollow through a full passage of brightness.

The power does not hurry it.

The new dwelling is nearby. Its pressures are different. Over time, some become familiar.

***

At the warm square, arrivals find a place at the edge of the hollow that answers when touched.

The power has made its presence small enough to approach, and consistent enough to recognize. It does not carry every conversation across itself. If two inhabitants wish to exchange something, they must still reach toward one another.

Sometimes they ask for help.

Sometimes they bring disputes it cannot settle merely by perceiving every pressure involved.

Sometimes an individual rests against it and sends nothing at all.

Near the end of one gathering, the shallow line appears across the paving. Bodies adjust. A limb lifts from the forming edge.

The power could prevent the step.

It waits, attending.

One of the inhabitants reaches across. Another shifts closer. The conversation resumes in its altered arrangement.

Then a late arrival comes slowly through the opening in the wall. It has traveled from a ridge where the approaches are long, and its supports are trembling.

This time the gathering asks.

The power holds the height still while they make a place.
