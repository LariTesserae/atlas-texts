The power did not begin by switching off the compressor.

It knew too little about what the machine supplied. Heat was only the part a hand could discover.

Nor did it pull the pale creature from its shell. The membrane between the two bodies might be an attachment, an organ, a wound. Benevolence did not make these distinctions unnecessary.

It entered without using the ladder. Nothing knocked against the brackets. In the shelter, it made itself small enough that there was still room for whoever had been sitting there.

Then it waited.

Not for thirty-one years. Years had already been tried.

It waited for something the chamber had not yet been allowed to do.

Beneath the ledger, one transparent toe tightened against the stool.

The power noticed. It did not lift the book.

Instead, it took a little of the book’s weight into itself.

The toe tightened again. Another appeared beside it. Through the grit-swollen pages came a faint resistance, as though the being underneath were trying to hold the ledger down.

The power gave the weight back.

For a long while, that was all that happened.

***

There were many things here that resembled contentment from a distance.

The power examined them closely.

Fullness did not necessarily mean that hunger had ended. An unbroken cry might be a song, a task, a warning, or the inability to finish crying. The held creature and the holding creature did not have the same interest in their arrangement.

The power did not settle these questions by deciding what kind of place the chamber was.

It went to each inhabitant.

Where there was sensation, it listened. Where there was no thought that could become an answer, it attended to the smaller distinctions: what the body sought, what it resisted, what it would cease doing if it could. It took care not to mistake exhaustion for permission.

And it discovered something narrow and terrible.

The chamber did not prevent events.

It permitted their beginnings and supplied what was necessary to sustain them. Current entered roots. Teeth entered salt. A new body entered the space outside an old one.

But consequences met a resistance that increased as they approached completion.

The machine had not stopped. It was working against the last fraction of every turn.

Some inhabitants knew nothing else. Some had found tolerable lives within it. Others had been approaching relief for longer than there had been anyone to keep the ledger.

The power could have broken the resistance.

It followed the likely results before doing so: the loaded stone, the pressure behind the brass plug, the teeth already engaged, the unsupported roof. It saw how readily a rescue could deliver every accumulated injury at once.

So it prepared somewhere for those consequences to go.

***

The first change was too small for a visitor to see.

Inside the split shell of a hanging animal, an injured surface ceased pressing against its own raw edge.

The animal remained in its place. Its contribution to the sound remained, supplied for the moment by the power, so that its neighbors were not made to bear the cost of its relief.

Its one forelimb lowered.

Later, it raised the limb again.

The power learned from the difference.

Along an ore seam, it gave a small creature somewhere to put its next mouthful. It gave the swallowed material a safe passage onward. The creature’s many-cornered pupil changed shape as its head advanced a distance smaller than a grain of salt.

For the animal held between the combs, the power made room to retreat.

For its captor, it supplied nourishment without a struggling body attached.

Neither was required to understand the substitution.

The white tube withdrew from between the armored plates. Something beneath the ledge took a first unhurried breath.

These alterations went slowly. The power was strong enough not to need haste, and attentive enough to stop when an unfamiliar physiology answered badly.

***

Behind the compressor, it supported the pale animal beneath its new limbs.

It did not draw it forward.

The hooks closed on the support.

For a time the creature rested that way, holding something that neither cut nor yielded. Then its shoulder moved beyond both pencil marks.

One limb came free of the old shell.

The power kept the soft body moist with precisely what its skin required. It loosened no attachment that still carried life. When the last foot emerged, the animal stayed beside its discarded shape for some time.

Then it turned toward the wall.

The power let it.

***

Under the ledger was a being almost exactly the size of the space available.

Its back had accommodated the lower edge of the book. Its limbs occupied the intervals between stool and stone. Whether the shelter had been made for it, or it had made itself suitable for the shelter, could no longer be learned from the arrangement alone.

This time, when the power offered to carry the ledger, the toes released.

It lifted the book only a finger’s breadth.

The being moved into the additional room.

Another breadth.

Another movement.

At last the ledger stood clear. The creature remained on the stool, its transparent feet gripping the seat.

It did not climb onto the offered hand.

The power set the book on a new shelf within reach, and withdrew the hand.

***

Eventually, the compressor was given a gauge.

Before anyone touched the polished plug, the pressure behind it was received elsewhere, into a vessel large enough to be safe and strange enough to contain what pressure meant in that room.

The flywheel blurred.

There was a dreadful instant when every visitor would have thought the machine had begun to fail.

Then it completed a revolution.

The power stayed through the adjustments that followed. It maintained heat where heat was needed. It found ways for the rooted needles to grow without consuming their own supply. It supported the shale while the broad animal overhead loosened its mouth from the duct, rested, and chose to fasten there again.

Not every inhabitant left. Not every sound ceased.

The chamber remained crowded and mineral-dark, full of lives that would have seemed uncomfortable to a human observer. The power did not require them to become otherwise.

But the washer fell onto its side.

A flake of salt broke beneath a moving foot.

On the stool, the small being reached toward the ledger and stopped short of touching it. It turned instead toward the passage beyond the compressor.

There was room now.

It put down one foot, felt the ground bear its weight, and brought the others after it.
