The power did not lift the stone.

It could have held the flake above the mountain without disturbing a grain. But the darkness beneath it was not an absence waiting to be corrected. Nor was the narrowness, in itself, a fault. The beetle had use for a roof close to its back. The spider had made something of the distance between two walls.

For a while the power attended to what happened there.

It followed the beetle into the passage that would not admit it, and back out again. It attended to the larva feeding beneath the fly, to the moisture thinning above the springtails, to the egg whose occupant had not yet encountered an obstacle. It did not mistake stillness for contentment. Neither did it take every pause to mean distress.

Having the ability to know more, it used it.

The continuous tremor was not equally important to every inhabitant. To some it was scarcely an event. To the spider, under certain loads, it obscured the small uneven movements for which the line existed. The power followed that interference down through the rock.

Far below, people were also living along a tolerable margin. The station supplied heat to rooms where children slept and to a greenhouse whose windows whitened at night. Shutting it down would merely move hardship out of sight.

There were other possibilities.

A maintenance engineer had been tracing an intermittent resonance. The power placed nothing in her mind that she could not question. It made an overlooked measurement easy to find. The measurement led to a test, the test to a mounting assembly, and the assembly to a change that the crew had wanted for other reasons as well.

Over the following weeks, less vibration entered the mountain.

Not none. The station continued working. Wind continued to worry the seam. But one night the spider’s line moved, and the movement belonged more distinctly to what had touched it.

The power remained troubled by what followed. Greater success for the spider was not a kindness to everything.

It did not turn that difficulty into a rule that nothing should ever be done.

There were harms here that purchased no other creature’s meal or shelter. It began with those.

Behind the beetle’s night refuge, a fragment was working loose. When it fell, it would pin the animal without killing it quickly. The power settled the fragment now, while the beetle was elsewhere. Stone clicked against stone. A little dust descended. The resulting passage was different, but usable.

Near the entrance, a springtail had fallen between two crystal blades. Its feet moved against surfaces they could not grip. The power roughened a place smaller than the point of a needle.

The springtail found it on the seventh attempt.

At the warm crack, the power checked a coming surge that would have crossed the occupied margin too quickly for retreat. It did not extinguish the vent. Warmth still entered the hollow; sulfur still gathered. The change arrived slowly enough that feet could carry bodies ahead of it.

These were small acts because the beings were small, not because their difficulties mattered little.

For the beetle, the power did something less cautious.

It restored the missing end of the antenna.

No flash entered the seam. The new length lay folded briefly against the old, then extended. The beetle stopped. Its antennae moved separately, touching dust, roof, the near edge of a grain. It turned toward a gap and received the stone from both sides.

The power did not require that moment to resemble gratitude.

It watched the beetle go on.

There was still hunger in the hollow. There were encounters from which only one animal would emerge. The power did not call these things good merely because they were old. Nor did it declare its work complete because a few injuries had been prevented. It kept attending, learning what could be changed without making someone else pay unseen.

Beneath the flake, no inhabitant acquired an explanation for the quieter stone or the foothold.

The egg shifted almost imperceptibly among its three grains.

When it opened, there was space beside the head. Beyond that space was another, and a way between them.
