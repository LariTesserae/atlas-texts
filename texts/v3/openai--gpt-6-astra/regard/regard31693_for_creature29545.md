The power first learns how the animal finds its way home.

It follows at a distance that makes no pressure in the water. It watches the chin pass over a broken rim, the forefoot reach for a joint, the bent tail touch the pillar. Beneath the tray, the animal turns once and settles with its head in the recess.

For a long time there is only the movement of its throat.

The power stays through a dry interval.

It learns which conduits weaken first. It learns that the mineral-bearing water continues after the rain-fed mouths have dwindled, but that this alone does not keep the lower aisles sufficiently fresh. It watches the adults climb more often toward the vault. One small animal waits below the breathing shelf, gripping a projection with all four feet. When the largest descends, the small one flattens itself against the stone.

Then it goes up.

The power attends to the air as carefully as it has attended to the water.

Beyond the ceiling are courses of rubble, an old covered passage, more rubble, and a cap of fitted stone. Once, a narrow duct connected the highest part of the vault to the passage. Its opening is now hidden beneath deposits. Water never needed it in order to leave the room. The animals have continued breathing what little air can find another way in.

The power clears the duct.

It does this grain by grain, drawing the loosened material upward rather than letting it fall onto the shelf. At the far end it repairs the low stone hood that kept rain from entering. The opening remains too narrow to admit any animal larger than the insects that already live in the covered passage.

Air begins to move very slowly.

The next adult to breathe notices something. Its nostrils remain above water longer than usual. The throat expands, pauses, expands again.

It descends.

The power does not require a greater sign.

***

Far below the chamber, within the artificial mountain, a retaining wall has begun to separate from its backing.

The displacement is smaller than the width of the animal’s smallest finger. It has taken many seasons to become that large. Behind it, water has found a joint, carried away a little packing, and found a larger joint.

The chamber cannot feel this yet.

The power follows the escaping grains down through darkness. There are other inhabited waters inside the height: a cold cistern with blind crustaceans, a shallow runnel bright with red films, a pocket in which a single kind of worm has lived for a very long time. Simply sealing the leak would dry two of these places. Diverting it would flood another.

So the work begins farther out.

Stone is given a sound footing where the old footing has subsided. The load passes into it gradually. Loose backing settles against a support that was not there before. The leak continues, but through a bed that will no longer wash away.

In the cold cistern, a crustacean raises its antennae.

Nothing follows.

Up above, the overflow keeps slipping over its lip.

***

The room itself is left largely alone.

The fallen partition remains fallen. The eggs beneath it remain within reach of some mouths and beyond the reach of others. No hand gathers the shrimp into safer places. No abundance is poured through the inlets that would make the water foul when it died.

There are things the power could restore which would destroy what now lives there.

It studies every proposed repair all the way to its consequences.

A slab above a narrow passage has lost its purchase at one end. Beneath it is a resting place used by two of the smaller adults at different times. The power steadies the slab without lifting it. The familiar underside remains at the familiar height. The next animal through rubs its spine against the same smooth place.

Elsewhere, a jar will eventually collapse into an unoccupied hollow. The power leaves it.

A jar becoming fragments is one of the ways this room makes new rooms.

***

The largest animal has an injury beneath its jaw.

A shell splinter lodged there some time ago. The wound has closed around it badly. Feeding pulls at the swelling; sometimes the animal takes a snail into its mouth, holds it, and lets it fall.

While it rests, the power loosens the splinter along the path by which it entered.

The animal lifts its head.

For a moment its forefeet press hard against the floor. Then the splinter is free. It drops among the shell grains under the tray, no longer distinguishable from them.

Healing is allowed its own small time, but the wound stays clean.

Several days later, the animal crushes a snail without stopping. It searches the rim for another.

Its missing fingers are not returned. The healed bend in its tail is left as it is. Both have become part of how it knows the spaces around it. The power has watched that tail find a pillar in darkness; it does not mistake every mark of a life for something that must be erased.

***

At the slit, nothing is placed across the brightness.

The power considers the outward current, the depth beyond it, the small fish holding just inside. It strengthens the masonry beneath the opening where a hidden crack has begun, keeping the rough joints against which a foot can brace.

The clouds continue to pass.

One day the largest animal comes there after feeding. It advances in its usual stages, belly low, fingers searching. Its chin reaches the worn lower edge.

Beyond is air, and beyond the air, distances for which its body has no use.

The power is there too.

It does not put a meaning into the animal’s mind. It does not turn the small eyes toward itself or make the animal understand how far below the water the walls descend. There is no need to burden it with the mountain.

For a while they remain together at the opening.

Then the animal withdraws. Its tail reaches the pillar. Its limbs loosen.

It goes down by the old route, past the jar, beneath the rounded arch, into the close shelter behind the tray.

The power lets it disappear.

There is still work below the clouds.
