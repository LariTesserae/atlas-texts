The power does not arrive at the top of the stairs.

There is no reason to make the animals receive another pressure through the ground.

Its attention begins beneath the shell of the creature beside the pumping-house door, where a pale foot is braced against the paving. It attends to the foot without entering it, without making the small animal suddenly conscious of every tender part of itself. It learns what the foot is holding against.

Then it follows the force.

Through the paving, into a saddle beneath the supply pipe, along the pipe to the first shed. Through a housing and the muscular sleeve around a shaft. Through the shoulders of the transparent animals. Through the crowded bodies behind the window, where what supports one throat compresses another.

It follows far enough to learn what would happen if it stopped the wheel.

It does not stop the wheel.

For a while, nothing visible occurs. This is not because the power mistakes endurance for contentment. It is finding all the places where relief, introduced carelessly, would become an injury.

The head beneath the gray shell remains turned toward your shoe.

Then the animal’s nearest foot loosens.

Only a little. The pads retain their shape instead of being flattened against the stone. The creature begins to tighten them again, finds no need, and leaves them resting.

The power has taken that portion of the vibration.

It has not numbed the foot. The animal can still feel the paving: its coolness, the shallow grain, the contact of its own weight. Something that was continually required of it is, for the moment, not required.

One appendage pauses above the dusty depression in its shell.

The pause lasts longer than any pause you have seen here.

Then the appendage resumes.

The power does not interpret this as a refusal.

Behind the shed window, the bristle pointing the wrong way lifts from the glass.

A space has opened there, narrower than a drop of water. No body has been pulled from another body. Instead, the weight borne by each has acquired another support, fitted so exactly that the fragile middle of a creature farther in does not sag when its neighbor moves.

The small face withdraws.

You lose sight of it.

For the first time since you noticed it, you cannot look back and find it where you left it.

The power knows where it is. It does not require the face to remain visible.

***

The noise cannot simply be taken away.

Some of the animals hear through their shells. Some use the high notes to find the mouths of feeding channels. In the basin, the raised white foot is feeling a vibration too fine for the porcelain to carry faithfully. Among the bodies in the walls, there are calls which have never traveled farther than the thickness of a membrane.

The power separates these from the violence that bears them.

At the fountain, the water continues into its hole. The tiny animals still face it. But around the one with the awkward foreleg, the sound changes—not in pitch, but in what it demands of the throat.

The vessel beside its whistle slows.

Its mouth closes.

Nothing else closes with it. Its breath continues. The neighboring animals do not lose their places in the sound.

After a while, it opens its mouth again and makes a brief, thin note.

You can hear this one.

The animal beside it turns its head.

There had been no silence in which such a small sound could arrive separately.

***

The power examines the supply.

It learns what the bright flakes nourish and what they leave wanting. It learns the difference between a mouth working in the air because that is how it feeds and a mouth working in the air because food has never reached it. There are both.

It makes no general correction to their bodies. The violet sheen remains inside the joint. The animal with the white ring around its eye is not given the eye of another kind of creature. But the exposed surface is moistened, and a pressure that has lain behind it for longer than time can presently measure begins to ease.

Food reaches the outward mouths.

Not a flood. Not a richness they cannot bear. Enough, in a form each can use.

Some close upon it immediately.

Some continue opening and closing for a long time before anything changes in their swallowing.

The power stays.

At the gears, another support takes the load just as one of the little broad-shouldered animals bends. Its transparent abdomen contracts. It delivers the pressure backward, but the pressure has already gone.

The animal straightens.

The next load does not arrive.

Behind it, the row continues, safely supplied with motion from elsewhere. Ahead of it, a small space opens, with surfaces its limbs can grip.

It remains in position.

The power does not push it into the space.

Nor does it send another load through the forelimbs merely because those forelimbs are still extended.

Eventually, the animal lowers one.

***

There is a difficulty deeper than the machinery.

The district has no provision for afterward.

Every motion has been accommodated, every strain assigned a place. Even the smallest attempt at departure is taken up into the same arrangement. A lifted foot returns. A swallowed flake appears again behind the inspection glass. Effort is spent without bringing its creature nearer to anything.

The power does not smash this condition. Too much living flesh has been built against it.

It begins by allowing a few changes to remain.

The relieved foot stays relieved.

The swallowed food stays swallowed.

The little face behind the window keeps the distance it has gained.

Under the handrail, the swollen bead lengthens. Its neck narrows past the narrowness to which it has always returned.

It falls.

The impact makes a small dark spot on the paving.

Nothing resets.

The power attends carefully to what follows. To bodies that have never had to digest a meal into a new hour. To joints that can now accumulate either healing or harm. It does not give them time and abandon them to its consequences.

The dark spot spreads into the stone.

Another bead begins to gather.

***

Beyond the retaining wall, the bare country changes first in ways you cannot see from the yard.

There are chambers there: cool ones and warm ones, dark narrow passages, broad sheltered floors. There are places where bodies may rest against one another without bearing a factory’s weight, and places where an animal can be entirely alone. Food does not have to be earned in either.

The power does not assume that open sky is what a creature from the deep grilles will want.

It provides ways out that remain covered. It provides ways back.

For those whose bodies depend upon their neighbors, it makes room for the neighbors too. Where a separation would hurt, it supplies support before attempting separation. Where closeness is wanted, it leaves closeness possible.

Some of the animals may never leave the sheds.

The sheds are changed as well.

The wheels need not turn through living muscle. The crowded windows need not be crowded. No dwelling is left painful to encourage its occupants to choose a better one.

There will be things the power has not yet understood: a need disclosed only after another need is met, a fear that first becomes possible when there is somewhere to withdraw. It leaves itself available to these discoveries. The work does not have to be finished before any creature benefits from it.

***

Beside the pumping-house door, the gray animal lifts its body higher.

You can see all three feet on the nearer side now. There is a fold of soft skin above the middle one. The head turns away from your shoe toward the crescent of clean paving.

One foot advances beyond the crescent.

It comes down in dust.

The animal waits there, with its weight divided between the old place and the new. The power holds steady everything that would otherwise force it back.

Then the other feet follow.

Its departure is neither graceful nor complete. It moves little more than the length of its head and stops. A fine appendage passes across its shell, leaving the familiar middle of the depression untouched.

Behind it, the paving bears several pale marks.

The animal can stay where it is now. It can go farther. It can return to the door without taking up its burden.

For a time it does none of these things.

It stands on a place that has not been fitted to it, feeling the ground.
