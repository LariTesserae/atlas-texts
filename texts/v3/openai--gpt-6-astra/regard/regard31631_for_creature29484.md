The power did not bring a lamp.

It could have seen by one, but that was not the same as learning what was there. It arrived at the entrance to the low countries and let the returning air pass over it. Then it set down the weight it had intended to use for a body.

There were already enough things pressing on other things.

***

The hem it first attended to lay across a poor seam, well beyond the last shelter.

Much of it had withdrawn. The abandoned sheet still covered the floor, and a traveler testing only its edge would have found the dry feet releasing properly. Beneath that sheet, however, a living cord continued through a fracture into a chamber no wider than a closed hand.

There the animal was thick.

It had reached the chamber when the fracture was wider. Since then, a minute settling of the rock had narrowed the entrance. The cord still carried nourishment, but the tissue caught around it could neither withdraw nor grow. Each increase in thickness compressed the passage through which the nourishment came.

The hem responded by holding harder.

It had been doing this for a long time.

The power learned the difference between that holding and the ordinary attachment of a foot to stone. It did not need to decide whether either should be called pain before attending to the difference.

Elsewhere in the animal, pressure led to adjustment. A foot loosened; another took hold. Here the adjustment returned upon itself. What the hem did to remain alive made the next adjustment more difficult.

The power stayed with it through several attempts.

Then it took the weight off the trapped tissue.

***

It did not lift the ceiling.

The ceiling belonged to a much larger arrangement, and the creatures beneath it had built their lives around the steadiness of that arrangement. A displacement too small for a person to feel might alter the tension across a feeding seam. A gap might admit the lower current into a district where eggs had always lain in still air.

Instead, the power bore the load within the stone on either side of the fracture. Between those supported places, it made room equal to the thickness of the pinched cord.

No dust fell.

For some time, nothing else happened.

The hem continued holding. Its tissue had acquired the shape of the difficulty, and the absence of pressure did not immediately undo it.

The power did not pull.

Eventually one of the feet inside the chamber released. The release passed through the crowded tissue as a small change in tension. Another foot formed nearer the opening.

It was not the direction the power had expected.

The hem was not withdrawing from the chamber. It was bringing more of itself in.

There was a mineral boundary at the back, too faint to support the whole animal but sufficient for a smaller, thicker body. Given an opening that did not constrict it, the hem preferred the chamber.

The power kept the opening clear.

***

This took longer than a human visitor would have stayed.

During that time, a hunter settled on the abandoned skin outside. Two chip-grazers came to feed along the living margin. One left. The other remained.

The power did not turn them away.

It attended to what happened when their mouths touched the hem, and to the changes in the hem around them. There was taking, and there was repair. There were stretches of stillness in which neither creature needed to shift.

It learned not to mistake every loss of tissue for the same event.

Once, the hunter unfolded around a shelf-animal’s young. The power knew the young animal too. It did not look away from that knowledge or make the death into a payment owed to the place.

For that feeding, it supplied the hunter with something else.

The substitute had warmth, resistance, and the nourishment the hunter required. It had no life of its own. The hunter closed around it, while the young animal fastened itself beneath a fold.

The power watched afterward. A gift of food could become an abundance of hunters. An abundance of hunters could make the next season worse.

There was more to learn than how to interrupt a mouth.

It remained.

***

A traveler encountered its work before anyone encountered the power.

She had followed the lower current into the wrong branch and found, on returning, that several thick columns had fastened where she had passed. There was room for her head between them, but not her ribs.

She pressed with a shoulder and waited.

The columns tightened.

She tried farther along. The same thing happened. Behind her, the passage narrowed until turning around would no longer be possible.

The power felt her pressure through the hem.

It could have carried her out. Instead, first, it supplied the columns with another place to hold: a narrow support beside the passage, firm enough to accept their feet, carrying the same faint tension as the stone.

One column detached. Then another.

The traveler felt the change and stopped pushing. She lay with her cheek against her arm while the animal rearranged itself.

When there was room, she crawled through.

The support remained until the last foot had established itself elsewhere. Then it was gone.

She reached the shelter with a split lip and very little oil. In the morning—meaning after she had slept—she made three shallow cups in the approach to that branch.

The power did not erase the warning.

***

In the chamber, the old hem eventually separated from the greater part of its sheet.

The connecting cord became thin, then dry. For a while the two portions remained alive independently. Later the outer portion withdrew toward a richer seam, leaving the hunter’s resting place brittle beneath it.

The small animal in the chamber opened its folds.

There was no witness who could have recognized this as a recovery. No one could reach it with a hand. Its chamber had no useful name.

The power knew precisely where it was.

It also knew where the traveler had stopped to spit blood into the dust, and where the hunter would next become hungry, and where a household had narrowed a notch without noticing the living strip behind it.

Its attention did not make these things simple.

It made them possible to attend to.

***

People eventually noticed that some difficulties in the low countries had begun to ease.

Not all. Directions still mattered. Fresh soles still mattered. A hem could still grow across a return passage, and a person could still mistake dead skin for safe ground.

But a few trapped margins loosened. Certain wounds closed without drawing a crowd of feeding mouths. In one house, a strip behind the wall thickened into a newly opened recess rather than lifting the sleeping shelf.

No sounding slab announced a visitor.

Offerings continued to accumulate at the entrance to the low countries. The power left them where they were. They had been set down for reasons that did not concern it.

Farther in, within the little chamber, the hem put out a foot across the space that had once confined it.

There was support available there.

The foot tested it, rested for a time, and let go.
