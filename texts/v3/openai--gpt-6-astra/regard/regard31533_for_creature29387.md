The power did not enter by the gate.

A gate admits a body into a street. It does not necessarily admit a stranger into the life of a place. The power knew the difference, and did not mistake its ability to be everywhere for an invitation.

Its attention rested first on the delivery spout.

There was enough strength in that attention to bring the bead of water down, to draw another after it, to give the cistern its hollow succession of sounds. The channels would have carried water beautifully. The settling bowls would have caught grit. All the careful provisions of the builders could have been brought into use.

But the channels were already the work of people who understood water. They did not need to perform for a visitor.

The power left the bead attached.

It regarded the birds, the grain counter, the small concealed stones. It did not acquit the child of cheating, or expose the cheating, or supply the game with a happier conclusion. It had no reason to suppose that wisdom entitled it to win a children’s dispute from outside the rules.

In the house above the market, it found the woman with the boot.

Here its attention became particularly exact.

It knew the pressure of the hidden ridge against her finger. It knew why she had opened the seam at that point rather than another. It understood the objection in the other person’s mouth, including the part of the objection that was reasonable and the part that came from having to sit in stockings while someone else knew better.

It could have made a boot without a ridge. It could have straightened the foot.

Neither possibility told it what to do.

Instead, it attended to the particular foot for which this particular woman was making room. There was no ideal shape standing invisibly beside it, no better body against which the broad sole had to be measured. There was hide, felt, a seam, and a skilled hand making a judgment.

The power did not finish the repair.

Nor did it bless the woman with inexhaustible patience. Her patience had a character, and her annoyance helped give it that character. To remove the annoyance for the comfort of an onlooker would have been a small, intimate trespass.

It allowed itself to be a guest who received no welcome because no welcome had been offered, and required none.

Still, it did not call everything good.

The lizard’s mouth held the beetle. The man’s composure was tightly held. Across the unbuilt slope, the regard between doorways retained whatever was difficult in it. The power’s tenderness did not turn these things into ornaments.

It attended closely enough to distinguish hurt from the appearance of hurt, a wish from an observer’s wish on someone else’s behalf. Had it found an appeal, it would not have answered with praise for the scenery.

There was no appeal concealed inside the unmoving needle. No one behind the woman’s eyes was beating against the instant.

That mattered more than what the power could accomplish.

So it made no alteration to the city.

It also made no decree that the city must remain unaltered. It placed no seal upon the mountain, appointed no guardian to the stair, claimed no ownership through protection. A place need not be converted into a sanctuary merely because something powerful has learned to value it.

Whatever duration belonged to the power continued beyond the basin. Within that duration, its encounter with the city had consequences. It would be slower, elsewhere, to confuse an unfinished thing with a plea to finish it.

Here there was no new inscription, no warmth laid upon a forehead, no almost imperceptible movement offered as proof that goodness had visited.

The woman’s finger rested against the ridge inside the boot.

The power had understood her work.

It left that work in her hands.
