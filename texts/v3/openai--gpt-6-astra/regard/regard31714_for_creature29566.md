The power does not lift the stone.

It can see without making an opening. More importantly, it can attend without putting anything into the chamber: no warmth, no shadow, no thin edge of another presence between the animal’s back and its roof.

At first, the power considers the noise.

To a larger creature, the high note through the springtail’s fork might be intolerable. But the power does not mistake its own imagined body for the body that is there. It attends to the fork, to the minute attachments beneath it, to the animal for whom that fineness is not a description but a condition of being.

It distinguishes intensity from injury.

Where the sound enters the springtail’s experience, it is exact and immense. There is no wish beneath it for a softer sound. Nor is there a wish to keep it. The power does not require either. It leaves the note where it is.

Under the husks, the barley-colored animal is fed.

The power could open the seed. It could give the animal a gut in which food traveled, a hunger that returned, the strength to push its roof aside. It could lay out a passage through the clay and furnish that passage with other seeds.

Instead it attends to the two teeth.

Their contact is not frustrated. Nothing in the animal leans toward an absent mouthful. What looks, from elsewhere, like the brink of nourishment is nourishment here.

The seed remains whole.

This restraint costs the power nothing. Understanding is not a hand held forcibly still.

It attends also to the roof. The interval beneath the husks is small, but not insufficient. The power knows the difference because it knows the animal, not merely the measurements. It does not enlarge the interval into a room whose abundance would erase the particular curve of being sheltered.

There is one question it will not answer from appearances.

The country is densely fitted. Every surface bears another surface, every creature finds its boundary already occupied. Such arrangements can be merciless. A prison, too, may fit its prisoner closely.

So the power attends wherever contact becomes experience.

It does not ask the inhabitants to speak, or provide them with new faculties so that they can explain themselves. It follows the differences already present: water beneath one foot, dry mineral beneath seven; the root held in the third eye; the peculiar inward reach of the ring’s fringe.

Nowhere does it find a creature being forced to count its injury as fulfillment.

Had it found that, the accuracy of the arrangement would not have excused it.

But the divided pads beneath the seed-husk roof are not pressed into obedience. The legless animal is not struggling in its hooks. The mite inside the egg is not denied a life that belongs to it outside. Its foreleg across its face, its knee beneath the indentation, are its own concerns, and they are sufficient without resembling the parent’s.

The power gives up none of these distinctions in loving the whole.

At the center, it attends to the fold.

It knows mountains. It knows how weight gathers, how a ridge acquires a summit, how stone breaks into what can finally support a root. None of that knowledge entitles it to finish this country.

The shallow crease is not a promise made to mountains.

Along it are the pearly margins, the feet with no evident body, the bristle and its clear thickening. The power’s attention reaches the two lobes without passing over them toward something more impressive. It reaches the cleft. It reaches what is held against both walls.

Here its knowledge of the country becomes most particular.

It finds no last smallness beneath which care would be wasted.

Nothing happens in the chamber.

The droplet does not fall. The animal does not raise its head from the seed. No grateful movement crosses the white ground.

Nevertheless, the power’s regard is not merely a conclusion that help is unnecessary. It knows the pale animal individually now: the divided pads, the forked organ, the even space above the plates. It knows what would be lost if that particular fullness were mistaken for an unfinished beginning.

It does not withdraw its regard for want of a task.

Beneath the buried stone, the roof rests on its supports. The sound has its rounded place beside the water and its sharp place under the cliff.

The power remains attentive without becoming another thing the country must accommodate.
