The power attends first to the foot that remains on the bright stone.

It does not lift the animal. The foot is holding well. Beneath the opening, the other feet are testing surfaces, and the threads beside the mouth are receiving something more exact than sight. To carry it elsewhere now would be to replace a difficult but intelligible place with an event for which it has no sense.

So the power waits, without requiring the animal to know that it is waiting.

It attends also to the water.

Below the rim, the opening widens for a short distance and then contracts. Along one wall there is a ledge, broad enough for the young animal’s belly. Beyond the ledge, a seam leads into a sheltered chamber. The chamber has food, though not much, and a ceiling low enough for small feet to brace against it.

The other wall offers easier holds at first. These continue downward to a smooth throat where the water gathers speed.

The animal cannot yet distinguish the two routes. The pressure at its sensing threads is nearly the same.

The power could make either choice safe. Before it does anything, it follows both routes, and the thousand routes beneath them, through the mountain. There are living things in the heavy flow too. There are eggs secured in places no light has ever reached. Far below, water leaves the limestone and enters a valley where roots draw upon it.

Nothing here is empty merely because it is hidden.

At the lip of the opening, a small change occurs.

A hollow deepens in the wall above the sheltered chamber. It is no larger than the last joint of a finger. Water turns through it and returns to the descending sheet, setting up a faint lateral pull.

One thread beside the animal’s mouth bends.

The animal waits.

Its forefoot withdraws from the easier wall. For a while it holds the foot gathered beneath its body. Then the toes spread toward the hollow.

The power has made no command. The animal may still turn back.

It does not turn back.

***

The roar is not silenced.

The power has listened from within the animal’s life, where the sound is neither a spectacle nor a punishment. Much of what would overwhelm a visitor passes outside the little creature’s attention. The basin need not become quiet to become kind.

Nor is the stone covered in moss. Warm water does not enter the cold chambers. The buried eyes are not opened upon the whiteness.

But there are troubles here that familiarity has not made harmless.

The power finds the long depletion in which an animal continues to feed and receives less than its body spends. It finds skin drawn painfully against a mineral crust, a nursery whose supply sometimes thins before its occupants can leave, a passage that admits a growing body farther than it permits that body to retreat.

These things it changes.

Not all at once. A sudden abundance would travel through the basin as surely as a flood.

In the porous stone above, a little more nourishment is retained and released steadily instead of passing in useless pulses. The gray films thicken by degrees. Water keeps its coldness and its clear chemistry. The grazers do not crowd into numbers that will later starve.

Where the young must leave their narrow channels, there are now resting pockets within reach of their small feet. Dangerous passages carry a difference in pressure before they become inescapable. The difference can be learned. It can be refused.

The power does not ask a body to become wiser than its senses allow.

Under the old animal’s shoulder, the mineral crust loosens. It comes away as the flank moves, without taking skin with it. The animal shifts into the space the crust had occupied and rests there.

No gratitude follows. None is needed.

***

The young animal reaches the chamber beneath the ledge.

For several minutes it remains at the entrance, hindfeet still fixed outside. Its threads move through the sheltered water. There is a faint drift toward the back wall, and another current returning along the floor.

At last it draws itself in.

The ceiling touches its back lightly. Its forefeet find two irregularities in the stone. The mouth settles near the returning current.

Food arrives: a loosened piece of film, small enough to pass between the soft combs and be held.

The animal swallows.

Above, the pools remain bright at every brim. The cloud rests against the outer cliffs. Water descends through the mountain with its enormous, undiminished sound.

The power stays attentive, including to what its kindness changes.

Inside the chamber, the young animal releases one foot.

For the first time since leaving the nursery, it does not immediately have to fasten it again.
