The power first attends to the fold beneath the lintel.

It does not stop the drop. It does not yet know what the inhabitant receives from it, or what would be lost if the water ceased to gather there.

Instead, it waits through several rains.

The visitor sleeps. The inhabitant receives some drops and lets others fall. Once it withdraws to tend a ledge in the adjoining room, and the sound of dripping continues until it returns. Once the visitor wakes, moves away from the wet place, and the inhabitant settles without raising its fold again.

There is care here, but not every movement is care. There is also feeding, discomfort, habit, the need to keep a particular surface moist. The power takes time to distinguish them.

During this time, it makes itself a small place beside the doorway.

Not a body. A patch of dampness whose pressure does not change when the weather passes. It occupies no route beneath the street. When the inhabitant tests it, it yields.

The inhabitant tests it again.

For a long while, this is their acquaintance.

***

The first thing the power offers is a surface against which to rest.

It forms just below the inhabitant’s raised fold: a little fullness in the wall, of the same substance as the room, but without a reflected foundation. The creature can put its weight there without having to draw an extension free from somewhere else.

It does not use the fullness.

The power withdraws it.

Several rains later, the inhabitant presses the same place. The fullness returns, slightly lower.

This time the fold settles upon it.

Nothing else changes. The visitor is not required to wake and understand. The room does not grow bright. Outside, warm water crosses an avenue in threads.

When the inhabitant leaves, the support softens back into the wall. When it returns, it must ask again.

Eventually it asks in another room.

***

Learning to answer takes longer than making anything.

The power can hold the basin motionless, but the inhabitants use its movement. It can make every threshold passable, but a closed doorway may be the only thing preserving a household’s privacy. It can draw the heat out of the ground. In a trial no larger than a bead, the nearest plant begins to lose its hold.

The bead is warmed again before the roots release.

So the power learns the small differences: the contraction that means a passage is difficult, and the similar contraction that means someone is not to enter; the stillness of rest, and the stillness of a body unable to proceed.

It gets things wrong.

A recess offered beside a public hall becomes an inconvenience. Two residents have been using the pressure of that unbroken wall to speak to someone on the other side. They keep returning to the altered place, closing their folds sharply.

The recess is removed.

Afterward, when the power offers a change, it first makes the faintest possible version of it. A threshold becomes easier for the breadth of a fold. A wall holds a suggestion of shelter. Those who live there can inspect the suggestion, press it into a different shape, or let it lapse.

Some suggestions lapse for years.

***

At the central enclosures, the power finds something it will not merely watch.

A reflection is passing through an opening. Above it, an old body remains against the embankment. Two smaller inhabitants have opened their protected surfaces, but the extension they are seeking has already gone beneath a moving arcade.

The power does not stop the exchange throughout the city.

It puts a hesitation in the opening.

Only there: a stretch of water through which the reflected arcade passes more slowly, without bending or tearing. The old resident grows no more definite. Its companions continue their work.

Then a pale seam swells along its lower edge.

The power holds the hesitation.

An extension feels backward beneath the water. It misses the body, follows an abandoned route, returns. One of the companions shifts its weight and opens farther.

By the time they are joined again, an upward rain has filled three nearby rooms.

The household leaves slowly.

The hesitation remains until the last extension has passed. Then the water resumes its former pace.

At the next exchange, inhabitants gather near that opening before the reflections begin to move.

***

What grows there is not a barrier.

There are resting places beneath the reflected embankments, accessible from several directions. A route caught in the current can settle into one without being fastened there. Beside each opening, the water carries a pressure that changes before an exchange begins.

The residents alter these provisions.

They make one resting place broader and close another altogether. They ask for the warning farther down an avenue. In a public hall, after a gathering the power cannot entirely follow, they refuse a proposed connection between two enclosures.

The connection is not made.

Sometimes a body still disappears.

The power searches where searching might help. It follows extensions beneath streets that no longer have names among the living, holds difficult passages open, gives exhausted companions a place to support themselves.

It does not put a likeness in the room afterward.

For those already lost, there is no arrival contrived to end the household’s waiting. Their familiar routes are not straightened merely because they have ceased to be convenient. When a doorway will admit no one, the power leaves room beside it for those who come and pause.

***

Elsewhere, the work is less visible.

A young creature, stretched painfully between its parent and the wall, finds a third place to hold.

An old resident encounters a new doorstep that retains, along one edge, the pressure of the old one.

A mineral ledge can be renewed without the plants being made to open out of season. This does not end the disagreements over gardens. There are still bare patches that two households want, and residents who do not like the arrangement of a neighbor’s roots.

The power attends when asked. It does not decide that peace consists of nobody wanting anything differently.

In the rooms where the young gather, it offers no improvements for a long time.

Then one of them discovers the steady patch beside a doorway and makes a bead of water stand upon it.

The power makes another bead, lower than the first.

The young one raises its own.

Soon the wall is crowded with efforts. The adult across the entrance shifts farther from the splashing.

***

The inhabitant who sheltered the visitor begins taking longer routes.

At first it leaves only for part of a rain. Later it crosses a court it has previously avoided and rests in a hall while the pressure moves between the pillars.

There are evenings, if they are evenings, when it comes home and does nothing useful.

Its folds lie loosely against the wall. Water gathers beneath the lintel. The small fullness is there when requested, and the drop lands on it with almost no sound.

The power remains available, but no longer waits in the doorway.

Beyond the room, the city continues to rearrange itself. Gardens open downward. Households misunderstand one another, find one another, make space. In the central district, a moving reflection pauses long enough for a slow body to follow.

And in one low room, for a while, no one has to hold anything apart.
