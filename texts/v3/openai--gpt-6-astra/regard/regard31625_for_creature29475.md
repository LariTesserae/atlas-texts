The power notices the water moving into the fold.

It does not yet know what the fold is for. It knows the work of moving the water: the reservoirs drawn down, the small changes in pressure, the feeding margin allowed to dry. Whatever is happening beneath the hand is not without cost.

So it keeps the hand where it is.

Through the dimming and the next bright interval, its weight remains steady. Its shadow does not lengthen. When a maintenance crown approaches, it lets the assembly inspect it, and moves only after the animal beneath it has withdrawn from the contacts that the inspection will disturb.

Then it returns.

This is the first thing it gives the inhabitant: a condition that comes back.

***

It would be easy to put water into the joint.

The power follows that possibility downward. Water would spread along a ceramic face, enter a warm cavity, become vapor. A sensor would register an imperfect seal. Three layers below, an assembly would begin to prepare the repair.

It follows another possibility, and another.

There are ways to flood the country that would kill nearly everything living in it. There are ways to cool it that would close the breathing joints. Even the gentlest rain would arrive as an event for which much of the plain has been waiting with instruments ready.

Instead, the power places a little moisture against the underside of a loose plate.

Not enough to run. Enough to cloud it.

It chooses a plate outside the animal’s body, beside a feeding film, and waits to see what comes.

Microbes thicken. A grazer finds them. The inhabitant opens a route it had been allowing to dwindle.

The power repeats the gift after the same number of cycles.

On the fourth occasion, the route is open before the clouding begins.

***

The power is wise, but this does not give it an immediate translation of everything it encounters.

An electrical variation might be a name, a request, an adjustment of pressure, or several of these at once. A long silence might mean contentment. It might mean that a mineral bridge has cracked.

For a while, the power makes mistakes small enough to be corrected.

It supplies moisture to a chamber the animal keeps empty. The animal seals the chamber off. The power stops.

It steadies a plate whose movement has repeatedly injured a growing margin. The inhabitant begins pressing against a distant contact. Eventually the power discovers that the plate’s movement draws air through a feeding surface. It restores the movement, but makes room beneath the dangerous edge.

It sends a faint pulse down a bronze filament.

Three neighboring animals withdraw.

It does not send that pulse again.

There are other signals, smaller than the machinery’s tolerances, to which the inhabitant responds without retreating. The power learns these slowly. It learns to announce an approaching change, then leave an interval in which the change can be opposed. It learns to make an offer at a place where refusal will not cost the animal food.

Once, after many cycles, it offers the familiar clouding and the inhabitant leaves the route closed.

Nothing follows. No pressure, no repetition, no withdrawal of the other conditions on which the garden has begun to rely.

At the next interval, the route opens.

***

Under the plain, an assembly has classified the green seam as an obstruction.

The classification is old. Work has been deferred because other work is more urgent. In nine hundred and fourteen cycles, if nothing changes, the plates will be lifted and passed through a reduction chamber.

The power does not wait for the inhabitant to discover this.

It interrupts the order.

Then it examines what the order was meant to accomplish. A support beneath the seam is failing. Merely preventing the repair would give the animal a few more centuries followed by a collapse.

The power supports the failing part from below. It keeps the plates in their accustomed places, including their small motions. It gives the assembly another way to distribute the load.

The garden remains an obstruction in the records for some time.

Eventually the power finds where such records can be amended.

***

There are many gardens.

There are animals blackened by the standing veils, animals carried through darkness inside sorted rubble, animals whose neighbors have been answering an empty interval for longer than the power has been sitting beside this seam.

It attends to them too.

Not all want the same conditions. A charge gradient that wounds one inhabitant feeds another. A passage that must remain still for an old body is the only route by which a passenger can reach the surface.

The power does not resolve these differences by choosing one favorable temperature for the world.

It makes room.

Where two needs have been forced through a single valve, it adds a second passage. Where a moving surface must cross living tissue, it provides a recess into which the tissue can withdraw. It alters the timing of a repair so that a garden can establish itself along the next joint before the old one is taken apart.

Some changes are scarcely visible. Others require work beneath a country.

The power can do that work. It does not make the animals earn it by understanding how.

But the new fittings have contacts they can reach. Their responses can delay a closure, admit vapor, refuse an opening. These contacts remain useful when the power turns its attention elsewhere.

For the first time in many districts, an animal’s effort to protect its body produces an answer the machinery was designed to give.

***

The walking young are more difficult.

They leave their gardens carrying too little water. They cannot understand the signals passing below their feet. A safe opening and a fatal one may breathe alike.

The power watches a young animal wait beside a purge outlet.

When the outlet opens, it closes the space with a temporary membrane. The animal tests the membrane with its front legs. It walks around it. The power extends the barrier.

This is interference. The animal turns away from the direction it had chosen.

A short distance along the seam, the power has made another opening: cool within, with a filament suitable for the folded organ, feeding surfaces beyond it, room for a body not yet grown.

The young animal passes it.

The power lets it pass.

Farther on there is another shelter, and another. Between them lie shaded grooves narrow enough to retain the moisture of a small body.

The animal enters the third.

Others continue walking. Some return to gardens. Some take routes the power did not anticipate, and find places it had not recognized as habitable.

Fewer tracks end at collection seams.

***

Beneath the hand, the old inhabitant fills its fold.

By now the power has learned something of that chamber. It has been prepared before. More than once, the animal withdrew from distant contacts, accumulated resources, and then took the gathering substance back into itself.

This time, the reservoirs remain full.

Young develop in the dark.

The power does not warm them faster or make them larger. It keeps watch over the support below the chamber. It keeps the new moisture route clear. When the parent opens the passage to the surface, the lost vapor does not have to be drawn from the drying edge of its body.

The first young emerges during an amber interval and stands beneath a dead leaf.

The parent registers its feet.

Then there are more: faint irregular pressures moving through the garden, stopping, moving again.

The power has learned enough by now to distinguish many of them. It could follow each one throughout its walking life. It could report every turn to the animal beneath the plates.

It offers a contact for this.

The inhabitant touches it briefly.

For several cycles, the power passes back the pressures of a young animal sheltering far beyond the parent’s reach. Then the contact loosens. The parent turns its resources toward a feeding margin, and the power allows the report to end.

***

Years later, a maintenance crown crosses the low rise.

It pauses beside the seam. Its passenger sends a variation through a filament newly exposed for that purpose.

Below, the old animal answers.

The plain is still warm. Dust still travels in low rivers. In the distance, the unfinished road continues around its basin, though now there are sheltered crossings beneath it.

The power no longer needs to keep a hand upon the ground.

Before lifting it for the last time, it gives the familiar notice and waits.

The narrow opening beside its palm closes. Other apertures remain open. Water moves through routes that did not exist when it arrived.

Under the green cushion, the inhabitant attends to a grain lodged against its feeding surface.

It presses a contact.

A little fitting turns.
