The power first makes certain that the stair will hold.

It does this from below, without straightening the tread or changing the shallow lip by which the creature knows it. The support enters sound masonry on either side. It will bear the creature’s weight, or the weight of someone heavier, standing carelessly.

Then the power waits.

On its next circuit, the creature tests the stair. Its limb remains against the underside for a long time. It withdraws, moves past, returns. The new support has a resonance unlike the old foundations.

For several days it takes another route.

The power leaves the support in place. There are ways to make the unfamiliar feel familiar, but it does not use them.

***

Much of what hurts in the hollow is also useful.

The heat keeps something alive inside the pipes. The grinding plate gives the creature distinctions by which it travels. The slow opening of the valve brings nourishment into the basin, though the water itself has crossed a distance that cannot be measured along the plumbing.

The power follows these things to their sources.

Beyond the wall, the axle passes through a bearing whose lubricant has become a hard, glassy crust. Farther on, beneath a collapsed service passage, a pump works against an obstruction. It has been doing so for so long that roots have grown according to the heat of its effort. Simply clearing it would send too much through the basin at once.

The power could build another hollow. It could make cool chambers, generous pools, broad paths beneath an open sky.

For now, it eases the bearing by a fraction.

The grinding note changes.

Under the gallery, the creature opens its cleft. Its fine growth separates into rows. One forelimb finds a pipe; another reaches backward toward the wall. It remains like this until the valve has completed its movement.

The power makes no further adjustment that day.

Over many cycles, the unnecessary strain diminishes. The loose plate still sounds. Water still arrives in the order the roots expect. The bands of vegetation that depend on warmth remain warm.

Beneath the creature’s sleeping place, the hot pipe acquires a sleeve.

Only a short section is covered at first. There is room beside it for the creature to choose the old heat.

It sleeps against the uncovered length for eleven nights.

On the twelfth, it turns.

***

The basin receives no visible water.

The power kneels beside it and puts nothing into it. It learns what the fine folds draw from the current, what the fallen flakes return, and why the roots stop at their exact depth.

There is a narrowing upstream. Not a blockage yet, but something that will become one. The creature has felt its effects for years as a slight delay between two sounds. It has begun leaving its bed earlier to reach the basin in time.

The power opens a second way around the narrowing.

At first, scarcely anything passes through. Then a little more.

One evening the creature arrives after the valve has finished opening.

The leaves are already spread.

It holds to the steps with all its supporting limbs. Its foremost pair separate, close, separate again. The cleft turns from the water to the wall.

The nourishment is still there.

It lowers itself.

Afterward, instead of beginning its circuit at once, it stays on the warm tiles beside the rim.

***

The power does not know whether the creature wants company.

There is no good reason to answer this by filling the light shafts with wings.

Instead, at a junction where the creature often listens, the power places a small surface against the pipe. The surface can receive pressure and return it. It has no task to impose.

The creature discovers it during a night of distant rain.

It presses once.

The surface yields slightly.

It presses harder, and the surface holds.

For a season, this is all they do.

Then the creature comes to the junction carrying a torn length of stem. It puts the stem down, takes it up, and braces it against the new surface. The power holds it there.

The creature adjusts its grip.

Together they move the stem through a narrow opening behind the pipe, where an old growth has collapsed into a channel. The work takes most of the night.

Afterward the creature leaves.

The power does not take this as permission to accompany it everywhere. But the surface remains available, and when the creature returns, there is an answer.

***

Some changes fail.

A broader resting ledge goes unused. The power removes it when its underside begins to shade a bed of copper leaves.

A support beneath the handrail prevents the minute movement the creature listens for. That support is altered.

Once, the power holds a valve steady while the creature frees a root. The creature immediately abandons the root and presses its cleft against the valve casing. The power releases it. The stem resumes its slow ascent, and the creature returns to work.

Thereafter, help is offered at the weight-bearing places, not at the moving ones.

The hollow gradually contains more things that can be leaned on.

***

The power also tends the road above.

Water is allowed into the joints that feed the plants. The slabs are secured without closing the daylight. Heavy traffic, if it ever returns, will cross on foundations that do not press upon the chamber.

There is no announcement at the entrance. No name is given to the creature. Nothing requires it to emerge, become comprehensible, or demonstrate gratitude.

But the power keeps watch on the buried passages. It will not leave a collapse to be discovered by a limb reaching into darkness.

***

Much later, near the overflow, the creature catches another root before the water can carry it into the drain.

It holds the root against the basin wall.

A second pressure settles beside its own, broad and gentle. For a while both remain there. Then the creature loosens one ridged forelimb.

The root stays.

It loosens the other.

Still the root stays.

The creature touches it once more, tests the wall below it, and withdraws along the rim. Beneath the gallery, the pads of its body settle against the sleeved pipe. The exposed seams close slowly.

At the basin, the power continues holding the root.

There is nothing else the creature needs to do before sleeping.
