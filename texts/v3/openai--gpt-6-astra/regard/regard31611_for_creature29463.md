The power first makes its weight harmless.

It has come to rest upon a retaining wall, and the inhabitants below have flattened against the clay. The pressure of its attention travels farther than it intended. For several terraces the work stops.

It withdraws until the water moves as it did before.

Then it waits beside a square platform, where there is room for something unfamiliar without obstructing a passage.

For a long time, no one approaches.

The first to do so is carrying a tool. It passes beneath the platform, pauses, and continues. Later it returns without the tool. Its margin grips the wall twice.

The power answers too strongly.

The creature closes into a thick, pale oval.

Nothing nearby cracks. Nevertheless, the power remains still until the creature spreads again. Its next answer is scarcely distinguishable from water drawing through a joint.

This time the inhabitant stays.

***

Learning takes longer than making itself understood would have taken.

The power could place an explanation directly into every body in the valley. It could make its benevolence unmistakable. But the inhabitants have ways of approaching an unknown thing, of passing news, of withholding judgment. It leaves those ways intact.

At first, it learns only practical distinctions.

Occupied.

Pass beneath.

This edge will not hold.

A pressure sustained beyond a certain interval is not emphasis. It changes the meaning entirely.

There is a signal used by workers just before they lift a piece of clay together. The power mistakes it for a request for help, and the creature beside it immediately releases the fitting.

Only later does it understand: the signal means that the speaker is ready to bear its share.

It remembers this one carefully.

News of the visitor travels through the courts. Some inhabitants come to the platform. Others take different routes. The two disputing possession of the notch continue their dispute.

***

The first help it gives is small and urgent.

Below a cooling pool, a newly fitted tongue shifts in its socket. Water runs beneath it rather than over it. A young inhabitant, already halfway across, loses the wet connection behind its body.

Its leading edge searches the clay.

The power puts a film of water where that edge is searching.

The creature reaches it. The film extends to the next basin, no wider than an ordinary passage. Once the creature is safely spread beneath the surface, the borrowed water drains away.

The power does not reset the tongue.

Two makers arrive. One braces the piece; the other examines the socket. Their signals pass through the wall and into the platform where the visitor waits.

They ask what happened.

It shows them the pressure of the slipping clay, the broken wet route, the small weight stranded between.

The makers take the tongue out altogether.

By evening a third inhabitant has joined them. They disagree about the socket. One fetches an older piece from a recess and lays it beside the new one.

The power is asked to keep the passage damp while they work.

It does.

***

There are things it might change which no one asks it to change.

The light is severe to eyes, but these inhabitants do not lower their faces against it. The empty cups high on the ridge are not all signs of loss. Some are being held for young whose arrival is expected; some belong to practices the power has not yet understood.

The old creature with the clay bar does have pain.

This becomes known only after several visits. The power does not discover it by opening the creature’s private experience. The creature brings the matter forward itself, pressing a sequence against the platform, then turning so that one end of the bar touches the wall.

The pain occurs when its flesh thickens around the support. Removing the bar would let the injury draw closed in the wrong shape.

The power offers to hold what the bar holds while a change is made.

The old inhabitant leaves.

It returns with the maker who polished the ends.

Together they spend most of a day establishing what is wanted. The old support is to remain. A narrow place beneath it must be relieved. The maker wants to feel the adjustment as it happens.

The power bears the injured flesh without compressing it.

The maker works.

Afterwards the old inhabitant rests in its usual pool. Nothing about its passage through the valley becomes swift or effortless. But at a certain turn, where it has long paused before gathering itself, it no longer pauses.

***

In time, the inhabitants ask for something larger.

Deep beneath the newest court, a brace has been badly set. Repairing it requires emptying three connected levels. The water can be recovered below, but several broad inhabitants cannot readily leave the chambers where they lie.

There have been discussions. There are plans scratched into unfired tablets, each kept wet beneath a cover.

The power is shown these.

It could replace the brace without disturbing a seam. Instead, it asks which part they want it to undertake.

The answer takes two days.

Then, through the walls of the lower chambers, comes the signal it learned beside the fitting:

Ready to bear a share.

The power holds the water.

It keeps the water against the bodies that need it, in shallow, unsupported folds. Around them the chambers drain. For the first time since the court was filled, the underside of an inhabited basin stands exposed to air.

Workers come down with their mallets.

The blows are unpleasant. At each stroke, the power feels the inhabitants contract inside their suspended pools. It offers to soften the disturbance.

Some accept. Others need to feel the work.

The new brace has a rounded junction at one end and a square one at the other.

Neither is the power’s choice.

***

When the repair is finished, the water is lowered gently onto the clay.

The inhabitants test the seams. Tools are counted and returned to their recesses. Someone finds an uneven lip where the old brace was removed and stays to smooth it.

At the platform, they make a small fitting for the visitor: a porous tile touching the water, sheltered by a roof. Through it, a request can be given without summoning the whole valley.

They ask whether it will remain.

The power answers as accurately as it can. It will attend to the tile. It will not treat every disturbance as a summons.

One of the makers tests this by pressing against it and then withdrawing the request.

Nothing happens.

The maker tests it again.

Later, beneath the new court, a seam begins to darken. An inhabitant settles over it, gathers two short limbs, and reaches for a spatula.

The tile remains quiet.

The power waits.
