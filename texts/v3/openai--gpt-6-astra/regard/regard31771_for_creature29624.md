The power notices the answer.

It has noticed other things: the thinness of the summit, the exhausted reservoirs, the strain in the inhabitant holding an unfamiliar body in mind. But the answering movement beneath the flake tells it where to begin.

It does not lift the stone.

There are lives for which being uncovered is a second catastrophe. The flake is now part of the pressure that keeps the trapped inhabitants whole. Even its weight has been incorporated into the work of rescue.

Instead, the power follows the single illuminated line backward, past the receiving hollow, to the place where the water divides.

There is almost none left.

The next portion belongs to the young along the fissure. After that, a small amount has been set aside for the chambers under the pale ledge. Already one of those chambers has cooled enough that its attendant must remain pressed against it.

The power waits outside the accounting.

In the mountain, far below the basin, vapor is moving through a blind crack. It will condense there, remain for a time, and eventually escape down the outer face as a little cloud no inhabitant will ever see.

The power brings a trace of that vapor upward.

Not into the courtyard. Not into a body.

It lets the trace collect behind an unused square of the chamber wall, where the city once began a reservoir and abandoned it. The old inlet still exists. So does the means of closing it.

A dark patch appears.

The wall needles turn toward it.

For a while nothing else happens. Then an inhabitant approaches along a street that opens reluctantly. It touches the patch with one needle and withdraws. Another comes. A small enclosure is established between them.

They take a sample.

The power keeps the moisture where it is. It does not make the patch larger when they hesitate.

Several low notes pass through the floor.

At last the old inlet opens.

Water enters the reservoir in a quantity the city has chosen.

***

The new supply does not immediately reach the ruined district.

Some goes to the young. Some is held in storage. Some is carried to a building the power has not yet understood, where it is used to dissolve a hard deposit from the underside of a threshold.

The power follows these decisions with interest.

At the amber boundary, the inhabitant making the branching enclosures has begun to lose the distinction between two of its own rooms. Its neighbors are supporting the outer shape, but cannot reach the failing partition without disturbing the remembered arrangement.

The power can hold the partition.

Before it does, it makes a small change in an empty space nearby: a loose brace, trembling with the effort of maintaining its distance, becomes steady.

The nearest inhabitant turns.

The brace is released. It trembles again.

Steadied.

Released.

The inhabitant approaches and places a needle against it. Through that contact come variations too fine to appear as light.

The power repeats them.

It gets the last one wrong.

The inhabitant draws back, then returns and makes the sequence more slowly.

By the time they have established anything like a shared signal, another amber mark has withdrawn. The trapped body has accepted a chamber large enough to move water across one membrane.

The rescuer at the boundary is still holding its shape.

A neighbor goes to it. They touch. The branching enclosures change slightly, admitting a narrow, unoccupied interval beside the weakening partition.

The power offers support there.

For several moments the neighbor tests it, pressing first from one direction, then from another.

Only then does the rescuer ease a little of its weight onto the new boundary.

Its grain brightens.

It does not leave. There is still something only it remembers.

***

Outside, a storm begins to take material from the summit.

The power allows the wind to pass. Cooling is part of the chamber’s water cycle; pressure changes carry information through the walls. To make the mountain perfectly still would be to stop things the city needs.

But there is a fracture developing through the black stone above the nursery.

The power holds its two sides in their present relation.

Dust continues to fall from elsewhere. The ceiling continues its small flexing. Beneath it, a young inhabitant lets go of a fourth needle and, surprised by the extent of its freedom, attaches three again.

No one has to interrupt this to repair the roof.

Later, the city will discover the held fracture. Its builders will examine it, and the power will learn how to give them access without withdrawing its support.

For now, the stone stays together.

***

The first inhabitant leaves the failed courtyard after the summit has passed twice through shadow.

It does not emerge in its former shape.

One interior chamber cannot yet be opened. Another opens too far unless someone accompanies it. At the threshold, the rescuers prepare a small sheltered distance and wait while the grain advances into it.

The power supports the route they have made.

There is no sudden brightness. Several streets have been dimmed to reduce the number of sensations arriving at once.

Halfway across, the survivor stops.

The inhabitant with the sideways bend brings water. The one that remembered remains just beyond the threshold, altered by the long work. When they finally touch, neither removes the space between their bodies.

They rest with that space intact.

***

In time, there are ways to ask.

Not many at first. Hold this. Less. Continue. Let go.

The power learns them at the loose brace, which becomes a place of frequent visits. It learns to distinguish a request for warmth from a request for somewhere warmth can escape. It learns that an unopened memory is not necessarily a neglected one.

The inhabitants learn something too.

They close the reservoir inlet one day while moisture still waits behind it.

The moisture remains outside.

They open it again much later.

By then, the chamber beside the rescued inhabitant has been occupied. Someone has brought a red inclusion there. It rests in a recess that had to be widened twice before its owner was satisfied.

The power does not turn it to see the other side.

There is work at the remaining blue doorways. There are routes to repair, pressure to sustain, and a carrier waiting at the pale ledge for a memory to become reachable again.

Under the summit, the old attendant leaves its post for the first time in a long while.

At a crossing it pauses, uncertain which way to go without its usual burden.

The streets remain open.
