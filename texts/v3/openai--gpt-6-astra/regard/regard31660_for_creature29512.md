The power did not open the seed.

It learned first what an opening would do. There were pressures being borne by the shell that the inner tissues had never had to bear directly. There were substances outside that would have been nourishment in one quantity and a catastrophe in another. Even warmth, applied without care, would have made demands upon the city. A thousand preparations might have begun together, each drawing on reserves that were not there.

Nor did it quiet the shell. The great note was not merely something the inhabitants endured. It passed through the arrangements by which they remained distinct. To remove it would have changed every house at once.

For a long time, the power’s attention made no difference that could be felt from within.

It followed the wax. It learned where a channel narrowed because it was damaged, and where it narrowed because an inhabitant kept it narrow. It traced the grafts beneath the streets. Where a response ended at a seam, it did not assume that the seam was a defect.

It watched the sheet in the crease receive the city.

It watched a house beside the bridge give away part of its last yielding wall.

Here it began.

Beyond the shell, it took up a little of the uneven weight pressing on the seed. Not enough to end the note. Enough to prevent a rib from buckling at its weakest point during the next deep cold.

Within, the difference was a crack that did not happen.

There was no recognition of this. Nothing in the city was prepared to recognize the absence of that particular crack. The power did not require recognition.

Its next work was smaller, and took longer.

The shell admitted no new substance through an opening. But it was not without channels of its own. Some ended blindly in old tissue. Some had been closed when the seed became dormant. Along one such route, the power restored a continuity no wider than the channels already carrying wax.

It did not make a stream.

At first it supplied less than the basin returned from a single particle. It supplied the substances the roots were already receiving, in proportions the city could use, and held back until their passage could be followed.

A little reached an exhausted district.

A little was stored.

A little went to the bridge.

The power waited through the consequences. Where the addition drew tissue into premature growth, it reduced the supply. Where a channel opened but could not close again, it learned the slower interval at which that channel could be fed.

The city had been living on the difference between one loss and another. The power began to give it something that had not belonged to anyone there.

This alone did not save the houses.

Their oldest shared arrangement could spend abundance as faithfully as scarcity. Material arriving beneath a doorway passed onward to the singing leaf. A weakened support was replaced, then thickened. The channels closed for a while. When the shell shifted again, they opened.

The power examined the hooked thorn.

Three fibers had been cut beneath it. Others had once grown across the cuts and been cut in turn. The recess was not the site of a single accident. Someone had worked there repeatedly.

It would have been easy to lift the thorn.

Instead, the power took a little of the bridge’s weight.

It supported the deck without drawing it tighter. It followed the movement of the shell closely enough that the bridge continued to answer, but no longer had to consume its neighbors to maintain its pitch.

The bands beneath the houses contracted once more.

The channels opened.

Then the leaf held, and the channels closed.

For the first time in an interval longer than any surviving bodily memory, a little substance remained beside the bridge after the work of holding it together was done.

One house put it into a doorpost.

Another retained it beneath the floor.

The third did nothing with it for a very long time.

The power could have built bodies for them. It had learned enough about the feeding hollows, the stiff fibers, the folded joints. It could have raised a population into the streets.

But a hollow was not an instruction to fill it. Some inhabitants had withdrawn from walking long before the city became poor. Some had spread through rooms that could no longer have supported an upright form. The well-supplied life near the basin was not waiting to be divided into its former households.

The power fed what remained without deciding in advance what it ought to become.

It also began to approach.

This was more difficult than supplying salts.

At a threshold far from the bridge, it laid a small pressure against the floor. The paired folds received it. Their contractions began, halted in the cold, and eventually completed themselves.

The power moved the pressure nearer.

It used the intervals left between the city’s louder disturbances. It did not force a response through a frozen channel. Sometimes an approach took longer than the repair of a roof.

At the entrance, it stopped.

The house tightened a root beneath the sill.

That might have been a brace against damage. It might have been the remainder of another movement, preserved after its purpose was gone.

The power withdrew.

It approached other houses in the same way. The responses differed. Some entrances barely distinguished it from the settling street. At one, a root lifted across the passage and stayed there. The power did not cross it.

At another, tissue on the sheltered side of the doorway softened.

It went no farther there either, not yet. It returned at another interval, and another, allowing the house to discover that this particular disturbance could arrive, stop, and depart without taking anything.

There was no common moment in which the city understood that it had been found.

There were local changes.

A threshold became more precise.

A reserve ceased to shrink.

A court completed a contraction that had previously been interrupted by every cold.

Near the bridge, the inhabitant that had done nothing with its surplus began to thicken the ring around its feeding hollow.

Not the whole ring. Only one side.

The power supplied the channel beneath it and left the shape alone.

Eventually a short joint rose from the floor. Several rootlets gathered at its end. They did not reach for a building tool. They pressed against the street just outside the doorway, held their weight there, and withdrew.

For several intervals, nothing followed.

Then they did it again.

Along the crease, the young sheet grew more rapidly now, but not without consequence. Its nourishment allowed it to spread across tissue that previously would have resisted it for ages. Beneath one advancing margin, an old household’s response became faint.

The power reduced the supply at that edge.

It fed the covered tissue through a separate root and waited.

A narrow ridge formed under the sheet. The sheet puckered, as it had around the old feeding hollow. This time the ridge persisted. Growth passed around it.

Elsewhere, two regions joined and their responses became inseparable. The power did not divide them afterward. It learned to attend to what happened at each meeting rather than treating either joining or separation as the answer everywhere.

The city remained difficult.

Some districts did not recover. Their maintained connections ended in tissue too thoroughly dead to answer. The power did not send a counterfeit pull from those ends.

Where the receiving fibers still waited, it preserved enough substance for waiting not to destroy the rest of the inhabitant.

In time, one such district let a strand loosen.

Another kept its strand sound.

Both received nourishment.

The bridge continued to sing.

Beneath it, a small hard surface eventually pushed out beside the recess containing the thorn. It touched the tool’s polished edge. It withdrew, then grew around the blunt end.

The power held the bridge steady.

After many attempts, the grip lifted the thorn.

Only one fiber was cut. It was not a supporting cord, but a narrow connection leading to the channels beneath a neighboring house.

The neighboring house contracted sharply.

The grip stopped.

Much later, a pull came through a different root. The thorn moved again.

The work took a long time. The power remained under the deck throughout it.

There were still no footsteps in most streets. Darkness remained complete. The inhabitants did not all gather themselves into bodies, and the young tissue made no hand.

But repairs no longer required a corresponding disappearance.

At a difficult corner, where several old connection grooves passed an abrasive edge, one house raised a low, smooth fold. It had no immediate use. Nothing had traveled that passage since the power arrived.

The fold grew a little higher.

Far away, a partial body rested outside its doorway, joined to home by a fine new thread.

The power kept the corner supplied.
