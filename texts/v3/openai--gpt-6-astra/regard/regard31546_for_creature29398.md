The power does not answer through the ground.

The child has only just begun to distinguish what is already speaking there.

It remains at a distance that is not measured in miles, and attends to the small palm, the supported wrist, the immense strain passing upward through the mineral. It notices that the adult has chosen a patch where the vibration arrives clearly without becoming sharp. It notices the other hand, held underneath the child in case the gripping limbs loosen.

For a while, there is nothing to improve.

***

Understanding takes longer than arrival.

The power listens through a season of new ground. It learns which cries belong to alarm and which to courtship, and which are the complaints of an animal that has found another animal in its sleeping place. It learns that the worker’s left rear foot hurts, but not constantly. It learns that the child likes the maintenance machine because one section of its housing answers a kick with a pleasantly complicated tremor.

It discovers that the worker sometimes stops beside a public quiet room without going inside.

The room is available. The machine can wait. But the child, waking in suspension without the familiar undertone of the plain, becomes frightened.

There are other parents who pause there.

The power does not conclude that the city should be quiet.

Once, while listening beneath a basin, it encounters a creature whose whole life is spent gripping one root. Food comes to it in the water. A mate reaches it through a filament. Its body has no part that could seek shelter elsewhere.

The great reports from the south make it contract so hard that it cannot feed.

The power follows those reports to their source.

Then it listens there, too.

***

Its first approach is made beside the machine, while the worker is awake and the child is feeding.

A loose strip of edging material rises from its compartment.

It rises slowly, without pulling anything else after it, and holds itself above the silver housing.

The worker withdraws two steps.

The strip lowers at once.

There follows a considerable interval.

At last the worker touches the casing with one spread foot and makes a brief, interrogative pressure.

The power repeats the pressure through the strip.

This is not yet speech. For several days it is not speech.

The worker brings a colleague. The colleague brings three instruments and an elderly person who insists on examining the strip from below. They establish that it is not being borrowed by a nearby building. They establish that nothing is arriving through it.

They ask it to stop.

It stops.

They put it in a closed compartment for the night.

In the morning it is still there.

***

What eventually passes between them is cumbersome but sufficient.

The power can hold a thing without transmitting its movement to another thing. It can spread a load. It can recognize certain strains long before the worker’s instruments resolve them.

It offers these abilities.

The worker asks whether it can carry a sleeping child.

This was not the first use the power had imagined.

They begin with a cup.

***

The support they make is ugly.

It resembles a shallow basket attached to the worker’s ventral harness by six thick, unnecessary-looking struts. The struts are necessary. Without them, the basket continues to occupy the place where it was set down, regardless of where the parent goes.

Distance has habits here.

The child dislikes the first lining and eats part of the second.

The third is woven from a basin runner with the bitter skin removed. Inside it, the child can grip, curl, and feel the slow movements of the parent’s body. The sharpest shocks do not enter. A small contact piece carries the reassuring undertone of the plain, reduced but not erased.

The parent can open it with one hand.

If the power withdraws, the basket settles against the harness. Nothing falls.

The first time the worker enters a quiet room wearing it, the child wakes, shifts its feet, and goes back to sleep.

The worker remains in the chamber for a long time.

Outside, the power occupies no doorway and makes no sound.

***

After that, requests arrive.

Some are incompatible.

A householder wants a hunter’s route closed. Another wants it widened. The owner of the rotating balcony asks for a declaration that the balcony is safe, and becomes irritated when offered an examination instead.

Several people ask whether the power can make their district easier to hear.

It can.

But the first proposed alteration would deprive the root-gripping creatures of the pulses by which their larvae recognize suitable basins. The second would move the interference into a cheaper district.

The third takes months.

By then there is a room where the proposals are kept: diagrams that can be felt under the feet, samples of mineral, living roots in damp trays. People come to inspect them. Some remain only long enough to register an objection. Others bring food.

The worker attends when its shifts permit.

No one becomes less argumentative.

***

The power has difficulty with the children taken by hunters.

It does not grow accustomed to them.

Nor does attention to the hunters make their hunger an abstraction. They carry young as well, though differently. Their routes follow cool depths that cannot be replaced by lines drawn across a map.

At the crossings, the lamps continue to brighten.

Beneath several of them, a new kind of refuge is tried: a low, heavily anchored shelter with narrow entrances and an interior floor that carries a distinct, steady signal through the worst of the luminous alarms. Children practise finding it with their eyes covered. Adults discover they fit inside, awkwardly.

The first design obstructs a migration of copper-colored animals.

It is raised by the width of two fingers.

The second grows dangerously warm.

There is no second installation until this is corrected.

Far below the southern plain, the power also finds a way to ease one immense concussion without stopping the movement that produces it. For the creature on the root, the change is the length of an extra meal.

Nothing in the city notices.

***

The worker’s child grows.

It learns that the presence in the strip can be asked questions, and that it sometimes answers by asking for a better instrument.

It learns to recognize its parent’s approach among several machines.

It learns that the small protected basket, now occupied by a younger child, was once its own, and finds this difficult to believe.

One day it accompanies the worker to a fresh opening beyond the last maintained crossing.

There are no lights there yet.

The black sheets have lifted into thin petals. Far below them, something red moves behind a veil of vapour. The child, no longer carried, stops at the proper distance and spreads its feet.

The power feels an impulse to steady the ground.

It waits.

The worker listens. The child listens.

Then the child picks up the pale testing strip and places it, carefully, on a patch the adult has not chosen.

A dark line travels down its centre.

The worker leans closer.

Through the ground comes a small, uncertain statement. Not an alarm. Not a request for help.

The worker asks for it again.

The child repeats itself.

Under the plaza, a seam is turning in a direction none of the instruments had predicted.

The power bends its attention toward the place indicated.

This time, it is the one being shown.
