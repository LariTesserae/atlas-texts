The power did not come through the entrance.

An arrival there would have been a considerable event. Even without a body, there are ways of entering that displace what is already present: making oneself the brightest thing, the most intelligible thing, the thing to which every other thing must suddenly refer.

It attended from where it was.

The first matter was the sound.

The power could have taken the note out of the iron. It could have separated the harder tone from the broad metallic pressure and laid each somewhere it would trouble nothing. It could have made a silence more complete than the darkness behind the housing.

Before doing so, it understood what that would remove.

In the broad pads, the low note was not an object borne by the tissue. The distinctions within the tissue included it. The fine silver structures did not merely receive the harder tone; their exactness was partly its exactness. To quiet the chamber would not have been like quieting a room around a sleeper.

The power left the sound where it was.

It considered the cold with the same care. Then the crowded roots. Then the height.

There was no virtue, it found, in bringing this place nearer to a valley.

There was also no virtue in calling it perfect merely because it was strange.

The power’s attention reached the broken leaf, the compressed graphite, the body’s exposed edge. It did not pass over them in admiration. It distinguished a broken form from a wound, pressure from affliction, an unprotected surface from a surface seeking protection. Such distinctions were not easy, even for it. Wisdom did not make close attention unnecessary.

The green border beneath the entrance leaf remained a green border. The power did not translate it into courage.

Behind the ceramic housing, where the creature was thickest, it found no hidden appeal. Beneath the perforated strip, the triangular vacancy was not loneliness. The unopened cups had no grievance against their condition.

Nothing here asked to be brought to completion.

For a while—though that interval belonged only to the power—there was nothing to do.

It could have departed then. No one within the socket would have mistaken its departure for neglect. No expectation had been awakened. No visitor had become a guest.

But its attention extended outward along the iron.

The mounting plate was entered in a record elsewhere.

The record was old, though not as old as the machinery. It described a quantity of recoverable metal at a difficult elevation. There were measurements of the bolts, an estimate of the rail embedded in the cliff, and a calculation in which the cost of access had been set against the value of the iron. For the present, the cost was greater.

The garden existed on the protected side of that inequality.

The power examined the record without contempt. Metal was useful below. So were the things that might be made from it. A person who saw three enormous bolts and a mounting plate would not necessarily be cruel for seeing material there. From the available drawings, there was almost nothing else to see.

It would have been simple to destroy the record. It would have been simple to make every approach to the cliff fail.

Instead, the power amended what was known.

It did not supply an enlarged image of the creature. It did not describe the three depressions or the folded pads. It withheld the precise location of the little vacancy. Those details were not needed by anyone deciding where to put a cutting tool.

The amendment was narrow and practical. The socket contained an inhabited structure. Its dependence on the surrounding hardware was not safely separable. Removal, drilling, heating, washing, and vibration introduced from outside were not acceptable methods of examination.

The iron was no longer accounted for as unoccupied.

Where the record had served a real need, the power attended to that need as well. It found other metal: a fallen counterweight buried in dry scree much farther down, accessible without endangering anything that had made a home in it. It made the counterweight’s location known.

This was not payment for the garden. There was no transaction in which the little body had purchased its place by costing someone less.

It was simply unnecessary to leave a shortage attached to its continued existence.

The power then returned its whole attention to the socket.

No part of the intervention had entered the creature’s folded layers. The brass remained distinct from the iron. The opening remained distinct from the darkness. The power had not added itself to those distinctions.

It considered whether to make its presence available.

Here was the difficulty: the creature had no unoccupied interval into which a greeting could arrive. To give it a new experience might require giving it a new manner of experiencing. A message could become an alteration of the recipient before there was anyone able to accept or refuse it.

The power would not call that a greeting.

Nor did it make the creature’s continued safety conditional on gratitude, recognition, or an eventual wish to become something else.

It left no token in the basin.

It did not fill the triangular space with a seed.

Outside, the cliff retained its immense exposure. No rail appeared across the gap beneath the leaf. Nothing softened the blue depth or turned it into a picture hung safely beyond a wall.

The garden remained difficult to inspect.

What had changed was farther away: a line in a record, a route by which useful metal would come down from the heights, the knowledge that an apparently empty piece of machinery was not empty.

The power kept the matter within its care, without keeping the garden within a fence.

Under the iron lip, the silver-gray lobe ended where it had always ended. Along its exposed edge was the fine green border.

Nothing had been placed between it and the world.
