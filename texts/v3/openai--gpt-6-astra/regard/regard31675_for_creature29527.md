The power does not first make itself known.

For several cycles it attends to the chamber. Then for several days. It learns that the loudest sound is not the most dangerous one, that the warmth of the foundation block is useful until it is not, and that the corrected arrow beside the service door is correct only when the pressure in the western main is low.

It attends to the animal without requiring the animal to attend to it.

There is much to learn. The smallest young is not simply smaller. One of its rear feet releases its grip a little too soon. The adult’s scar hurts most during the passage outward, when it cannot see the young behind it. The missing digit has healed cleanly, but the movement that lost it is still necessary often enough that another injury is likely.

The power follows the delivery passage beyond the distance the building appears able to contain. It finds where the carriers are loaded, what their residues consist of, and the reason the shelf must clear so quickly.

It follows the heat upward into rooms where people sleep. Some of those people are cold.

It follows the water.

Only then does it begin.

Behind the third shutter, the lower guide has bent inward. The power eases it outward by less than the width of the animal’s smallest digit. It does this while the animal is at the basin. The metal makes a brief sound, lost among the familiar knocks.

On returning, the animal pauses.

It lowers its throat to the paving. It touches the shutter with the side of its head, then with the comb of hairs on one forelimb. The gap smells as it did before. The hollow beyond contains the same three bodies, the same warm leaves, the bright blue strip.

It gathers itself to pass.

For the first time in a long while, its abdomen does not catch.

It remains half through for several breaths, braced for a resistance that has failed to arrive. Then it goes inside.

The power leaves that improvement alone for a day.

***

The deep report beneath the floor is the end of a process that has been going wrong for years.

It need not be so violent. Nor does the machinery need to work so hard to move so little heat. But the chamber has inhabitants who use its failures as clocks, and roots whose water comes through a damaged seal.

The power repairs the system in stages.

The first report is scarcely softer. The next is softer again. The dragging sound still stops before it comes. The green points still travel along the channel. Water reaches the basin in the quantities its small inhabitants require.

Above, a room that has always been cold becomes habitable without anyone knowing why.

Below, the foundation block ceases to become painfully hot.

The adult still moves the lid onto the paving several times. It checks the block with the underside of a foot. It settles, rises, checks again. Eventually it leaves the lid beside the nest.

The young discover that it makes a good surface to push one another across.

There is still noise. There is less of the kind that exhausts the body without telling it anything.

Through the floor, farther away than before, the animal begins to hear its neighbors.

***

The power does not put food on the moving carrier.

It opens a way to one of the places the adult can no longer reach: a service recess where harmless growth accumulates on the outside of a warm pipe. The entrance is broad, the floor rough enough for purchase. There is a second exit. Nothing inside can close around a limb.

At first the animal only reaches in.

On another circuit it inserts its head.

Much later, with two young clinging beneath it and the third riding awkwardly across its back, it enters altogether.

The growth is familiar food. It has always been there. The animal eats for a long time, then carries some away.

Similar recesses become accessible farther along the perimeter. The pale animal finds one near the drainage channel. The stiff-backed one finds another without having to force its ridge beneath a lintel.

They still meet at the corners. They still sometimes stamp at one another. Once the adult nearest the stairs returns to find the pale one examining the widened shutter gap, and the argument passes through four walls.

The power does not settle this dispute.

There is room elsewhere, and the pale one knows where it is.

***

The old scar is no longer being torn open, but it remains tender.

While the animal sleeps, the power attends to the small processes of repair: circulation in tissue long compressed, an inflammation that never quite subsided. Nothing pulls the animal from sleep. Nothing restrains it. Over many nights the scar becomes more supple.

One evening the smallest young plants a large foot directly upon it.

The adult shifts the young aside.

It does not flinch.

***

There are things that cannot be solved by adjusting the chamber.

A maintenance order exists several streets away. It calls for the unused lower concourse to be cleared, washed, and sealed. The person who wrote it has never been below the bend. On the plan, the basin is marked as a surface defect.

The power brings the plan to the attention of someone who has been there.

That person remembers the lid.

What follows is slow and human: a visit, a disagreement, photographs that mostly show darkness, a sample of water, an amended instruction. The chamber is entered on a different list. The utility workers are given accurate diagrams. Safe access routes are marked where feet, rather than eyes, can find them.

The person with the tool is not given another opportunity to strike the animal.

Visitors still sometimes descend. They are asked to keep to the stairs and landing, not to feed the residents, not to put their hands beneath the shutters. There is no lamp directed at the nest. The place does not become an attraction.

The power continues to watch the order as it passes from office to office. Good intentions, too, have joints that can fail.

***

For a while, the adult continues to sweep the carrier’s edge.

Then it waits through a delivery without raising its limb.

Then, on a later circuit, it does not wait at all.

The saved time has no appointed use. Sometimes it spends it behind the shutter. Sometimes it rests beneath the stairs, where a seventh leaf is slowly opening toward the maintenance lamp.

Once it climbs onto the bench while the machinery is still running.

It folds its limbs, lifts its throat from the seat, and listens. A young one follows as far as the bench’s leg and cannot work out the rest. The adult leans down. There is an extended, clumsy business of gripping and hauling.

Eventually both are on the seat.

The young stands too high. The adult touches it beneath the jaw, and it lowers itself.

Overhead, a vehicle passes as a soft pressure through the ceiling. Water drops behind the grille. From the far perimeter comes the recognizable weight of another animal going about its circuit.

The power is present, though neither creature has a sense for it.

It asks nothing of them.

After a while the adult stretches out one forelimb across the warm bench. The comb of hairs opens. The young mouths a loose fringe of skin along its back, fails to find anything edible, and settles beside it.

When the dragging sound stops, they both flatten.

The pressure release comes and goes.

This time, neither has to get up.
