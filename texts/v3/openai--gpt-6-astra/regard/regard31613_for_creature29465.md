The power does not arrive from above.

Above is crowded, and uncertain, and already full of things whose shadows the inhabitant has learned to survive.

It comes to the narrow passage beside your wrist as a little water that does not pull.

The creature notices. Three folds test the quiet place from different directions. A fourth remains beside the black grain, holding it against the stone. For a long time the only change is that the passage does not close.

The power could make the storm cease. It could thicken the translucent floor, anchor the wandering valleys, give every current a single destination. None of these acts would be difficult.

But the creature has spent its life learning this country. Its plates hold agreements with moving things. To make everything stay would be to take away much of what it knows.

And below the clouds there is a dark land about which the power has not yet asked enough.

So the storm continues.

The quiet water remains only where the creature has left an opening.

***

The first thing the power learns is that this inhabitant is not quite the size it appears to be.

A thread runs from its sheltered knot through a breathing pore in a copper leaf, down a stem, beneath a crust of old sediment. There it divides. One branch ends in a feeding hollow. The other extends toward a neighboring creature, stopping just short of contact.

Along this second branch, food moves outward and very little returns.

The neighbor has lost most of its plates.

During the previous dim interval, a root mat lifted with several stones caught inside it. The stones were part of an enclosure. When they moved, the basin cracked, and its inhabitant withdrew in directions that did not agree.

Now it is caught beneath its own shelter.

It is not waiting in the way a person waits. It continues trying. A fold thickens against the obstructing stone, fails, thins, and begins again elsewhere. There are several hundred such attempts in progress. Some have continued through an entire season of light.

The creature at your wrist has been feeding it.

Not enough to restore it. Enough that the attempts continue.

The power attends to each one.

***

Its first offer is mistaken.

It makes a smooth support beneath the broken stone, taking the weight without lifting it. The trapped inhabitant contracts so violently that two of its distant films detach.

The support has no history. Nothing in its pressure answers to anything the creature knows. Against its body it feels like a new direction in which the world may fail.

The power removes it.

It does not repeat the offer more forcefully.

Instead, it waits beside a loose red nodule near the enclosure. When the creature presses against the nodule, the power makes it slightly easier to move.

The nodule advances.

The creature releases it.

Several folds examine the surrounding stone. Two mineral plates turn in the cloudy knot. Then the fold returns and pushes again.

This time, the nodule moves only while it is being pushed.

Over the next long interval, the inhabitant shifts it beneath the broken wall.

It adds black sand. It draws a cord around a protruding root. It moves three smaller stones into a place the power would not have chosen.

The arrangement is inefficient, except that it belongs to the creature and the creature understands it.

When the wall’s weight begins to settle onto the new footing, the power bears the part that would crush the working folds.

The inhabitant withdraws them.

Water enters the basin by an old route.

***

There are plates scattered in the sediment.

The power does not put them back into the body. It clears a small space around each, leaving them where they fell.

Some are accepted immediately. Others are touched and left behind.

One lies in a shallow depression for so long that a seedling opens beside it. Eventually the visitor from the rim takes it away.

Nothing insists that it has made a mistake.

Inside the repaired enclosure, cloudy material begins to move through the sheltered knot. The inhabitant opens a feeding surface it has not used since the wall shifted. For several days it draws in more nourishment than it sends toward its damaged edges.

Then a narrow ribbon emerges through the restored opening.

It reaches the neighbor’s waiting thread.

Their contact is too small to cast a shadow.

The power stays through it without trying to translate.

***

Benevolence, here, acquires the habit of checking underneath.

Before warming a hollow, the power finds what depends on its coolness. Before opening a passage, it follows the water to its destination. Empty shells are not always empty. A membrane that no longer moves may still receive. The seven stones are left exactly as they are.

There are changes.

A rain pool that has trapped three young creatures develops a brief weakness in its skin. One finds it, opens and closes its white body, and passes through. The others follow the disturbance.

The pool remains. Its roots continue drinking.

A bitter boundary between two old inhabitants is offered a second feeding current. Neither must abandon its shelter to reach it. For a season they poison both routes. Then one begins to clear its side.

The power does not make them partners.

It also learns where it is unwelcome. There are folds that seal at its approach, basins where even a reduction in strain interrupts something delicate. From these it withdraws. Its absence there is dependable.

Elsewhere, a certain assistance becomes possible to find: resistance that lessens beneath a deliberate push, weight that does not descend until living tissue has withdrawn, a quiet place that lasts long enough for two separated edges to touch.

The creatures investigate these things. Some use them. Some build around them. Some carry grains for great distances and arrange them beside apparently ordinary cracks.

The power learns those arrangements slowly.

***

At the rim, the storm brightens beneath your chest.

The inhabitant draws its folds inward. Its roots, and the roots that are not its own, tighten around everything that might shift.

This time, something is different beside the narrow passage.

The power has not anchored the whole shelf. The shelf needs to bend; farther along it, shells open their feeding margins in the strain. But a fracture beneath the inhabitant’s thickest knot no longer widens under pressure.

It can still open if the creature pushes from within.

The inhabitant tests this twice.

Then it brings out the polished black grain.

It holds the grain in the quiet water, turns it, draws it back against its body. Seven plates assemble around it and come apart.

For a while nothing else happens.

Then the creature leaves the grain outside.

The power keeps it from washing away.

Not forever. Only until the fold returns.

When it does, the quiet water parts without resistance. The grain is taken inward. All along the rim, leaves settle against leaves, and the sea bends around the stone.

Beside your wrist, the passage is still open.

This time a little food is moving through it.
