The power first made the mistake of becoming quiet.

It withdrew its weight from the stone, its disturbance from the water, the faint charge its presence had laid along the channels. It wished to approach without frightening anything.

An old attendant beneath it stopped moving.

The animal had been dragging itself across a shallow sill between basins. It had one long leg left, bent at an angle that made each forward stroke turn its body slightly sideways. With every few strokes it settled its underside to the stone and corrected its direction.

Now there was a place ahead from which nothing returned.

The attendant drew the long leg beneath its shell.

The power waited. So did the animal.

At last the power understood enough to put its weight back.

Not all of it. Only a small, steady pressure, carried through the sill.

The attendant tested the ground. After a while it moved around the pressure and continued toward the basin.

The power followed at a distance that the animal could feel.

***

It could have warmed the country.

It could have parted the clouds, lifted the plain above the sea, stopped the blue discharges from entering mouths.

It did none of these things.

Warmth would have quickened some appetites before it supplied anything to satisfy them. The lifted plain would have emptied the intakes. In the absence of the shocks, the clear eggs would have remained clear eggs, their young spread motionless inside them.

This did not make the country good as it was.

Beneath a lip of ice, three attendants were freezing against an intake. Their host still pumped strongly enough to feed them, but an overflow from the next channel had glazed their backs. One had been working a forelimb against the same patch of ice for most of the dim day.

The power examined the ice, the forelimb, the warm water passing a little too far below.

Then it made a groove.

The groove was no wider than the tip of the limb. A thread of outflow entered it and passed beneath the trapped shells.

For some time nothing happened.

Then one animal lifted.

Another withdrew a foot that would otherwise have been eaten free.

The third remained still. It had died before the power arrived.

The power stayed long enough to learn the difference.

***

The old attendant reached the basin on the fourth tide.

Twice the power kept it from being swept into water too deep for its remaining leg to find bottom. The first time, it raised a little ridge beneath the animal. This alarmed it so badly that it stayed flattened until the water had almost covered its back.

The second time, the power roughened a strip of stone ahead.

The attendant caught the strip with its foot and pulled itself across.

After that, the power used roughness.

There were other old animals in the basin. Some had already settled into hollows. One was moving slowly from impression to impression, lowering itself into each and leaving again.

The hollow toward which the attendant travelled had places for all six limbs.

Its own shell fitted badly. One edge had thickened after an old fracture; another had worn almost to the soft tissue. When it settled, its long leg lay across the depression intended for a different leg, and its underside did not quite touch the bottom.

The power could have restored its body.

It considered the years folded into that body: the shortened passages it could enter, the particular pressures it could distinguish, the way its enlarged sensory cups now reached the ground before its feet did. A younger shape was not simply the same animal with less damage.

Instead, it lowered one small prominence in the hollow.

The attendant rocked once and became still.

***

The first deep knock passed through the basin after the sea had begun to withdraw.

The power listened with the animal.

It had listened before through the whole plain, following pressures down into stone that moved without breaking, through crowded chambers beneath the basins, into distances where the distinction between a body and the ground supporting it became difficult to maintain.

But the attendant did not hear a lesser version of that immense sound.

It heard something particular.

A pressure arrived, and the bead in one sensory cup shifted. Another pressure arrived through the opposite side of the shell. Between them was an interval that belonged to a host long absent from the channels.

The animal’s remaining leg extended.

Other intervals followed. They passed through old injuries and through membranes grown thin with use. The power could perceive their precision without yet knowing what they meant.

When the shell plates parted, it felt the effort.

The attendant gave its low tremor.

Across the basin, three others answered.

Stone began to gather around its edge.

The power did not tear it free.

Neither did it assume that remaining still meant there was nothing to be done.

It placed a slight irregularity beside the long leg: something the foot could take hold of. Beyond it, it kept a narrow strip of the hollow from closing.

The animal could settle deeper. It could also pull away.

Then the power waited.

***

During the next tide, one of the other attendants began trying to leave.

Its shell had become pale underneath. It flexed against the hollow, found its edges caught, and flexed again. Each movement pressed the soft belly harder against the rising stone.

The power opened a seam.

Water entered. The shell shifted.

The attendant drew itself free and lay for a long time beside the place where it had been. Then it began the journey toward the channels.

The power made a rough path across the sill.

It did not require another animal to become trapped before it opened the next seam.

Through the basin, wherever stone was closing over living shell, narrow lines remained unsealed. Most were never used. Silt gathered in them. Small grazers scraped their edges.

In one hollow, a leg reached out only after two tides.

The seam opened there too.

***

The old attendant with the single long leg did not leave.

Its foot rested on the irregularity the power had made. Once it gripped. Once its body rose enough for water to pass beneath.

Then it lowered itself again.

The deep knocks continued.

With each answer, less movement was visible in the shell. More became perceptible in the stone beneath it. The power followed carefully. It found no clean instant at which it could say that the animal had ended and something else begun.

There were, however, changes it could recognize.

The strain in the underside lessened when the hollow supported it evenly. A cold current along the exposed membrane made the whole body contract. The power diverted that current. When sediment blocked a gap between two plates, it loosened the grains.

It kept the way out open.

Winter came. The shell disappeared beneath a skin of new stone.

Still the power kept the seam.

***

Elsewhere it worked without making a season of miracles.

An intake acquired a sheltered fold where attendants could withdraw during a badly timed compression. A stretch of polished channel bottom became rough enough for an overturned animal to recognize with its belly. Warm overflow travelled through a few new grooves instead of freezing uselessly on the backs of hosts.

Some changes it undid.

A refuge gathered so many grazers that they consumed its films and began to starve. The power opened several ways out and made the next refuge smaller.

It watched what happened downstream.

It learned which kinds of distress required haste and which only resembled distress to a being that had never needed to brace a mouth open to eat.

There was more than enough work.

***

In the basin, beneath the place where the old attendant had settled, a pressure began to repeat.

It was small beside the deep knocks. It travelled poorly through water and well through the broad stone backs. At a distance it became difficult to distinguish from the many rhythms of the channels.

The power knew it by an unevenness: a slight delay where one side answered the other.

Far away, a young attendant stopped cleaning a valve.

It loosened one foot.

Its host’s rim was tightening too early. The animal moved its brace a little deeper, into a position it had never used before.

The compression passed without crushing it.

In the basin, the pressure subsided.

The power did not name what had happened. It laid a little of its weight beside the buried shell, where it could be felt without obstructing anything.

Under the new stone, something answered.

Along one edge, too fine for a grazer’s foot to enter, the seam remained open.
