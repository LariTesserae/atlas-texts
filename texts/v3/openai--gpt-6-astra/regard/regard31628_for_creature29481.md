For a long time, the attention makes no difference that anyone in the hollow can detect.

The pool continues losing water through its submerged crack. Pellets collect above the ring. The animal beside you turns its stem and chews, with one hand resting on your wrist.

The power attends first to that hand.

It could learn the arrangement of every bone inside it. That would be easy. Harder is understanding why the hand has been put there, how lightly it rests, what would cause it to tighten or withdraw. So it remains through the chewing, and through the animal’s eventual departure, and through several intervals in which the creature chooses to sit elsewhere.

It listens to the sharp double cluck that ends an unwelcome touch. It notices that an offered stem may be taken, ignored, or pushed back. It watches a young animal climb over a sleeping adult until the adult moves one foot and quietly blocks the route.

Nothing in the hollow knows it is being considered.

The power takes care to keep this true while it is still learning.

***

There are things it does not do.

It does not lift the darkness. The leaves are open in it. Voices travel through it. The animal at the outlet has never needed to see the water in order to hesitate there.

It does not quiet the insects, although their overlapping calls make it difficult to attend to any single sound. It learns to listen better.

It does not straighten the grooves in the stone or clear away the softened leaves. Inside one apparently useless fold of rotting vegetation, there are eggs, a stored piece of food, three kinds of root, and the route by which a sleeper gets air.

The power has no difficulty making things.

It spends considerably longer finding out what is already being made.

***

Far beneath the post, where no root has reached, there is a separation in the mountain.

It is very small. Water enters it, warms, and leaves by another route. Each passage removes a little material from one side. The post compensates. It has been compensating for a long time.

This is part of the reason its vibration sometimes changes beneath a resting hand.

The separation is not yet dangerous. Several generations of the animals could live beside it without encountering the consequence. But eventually the post would reach the end of an adjustment it was made to perform. After that, pressure would gather where pressure should not gather.

The power follows the water to its destination before altering anything.

Then, over many slow rains, it gives the post sound footing again.

No shock passes through the stone. No mineral taste enters the pool. The warm seep beneath the roots remains warm. The post’s vibration settles so gradually that the animals make no special response.

One presses its head against the shaft and stays somewhat longer than usual.

The power does not take this for thanks.

***

The old injury beneath the hesitant animal’s shoulder is not merely a ridge in the coat.

When the warmth is falling, a small region beneath it pulls against the movement of the forelimb. The creature has learned several ways around this. It turns before lowering itself. It sleeps with the limb at a particular angle. It leaves that side outward when sharing the bench, where nobody is likely to lean against it.

Most of the time, these arrangements suffice.

Sometimes they do not.

The power could change the shoulder while the animal sleeps. Instead, it begins with something the creature can move away from.

On an unoccupied part of the bench, it makes a patch of warmth. A little warmer than the seamless surface around it, no warmer than the stone before rain.

The animal finds this with the backs of its hands.

It investigates for some time. It puts a foot there, removes it, sniffs, and finally lies down with the wrong shoulder against the warmth.

The power waits.

During the next cooling interval, the creature returns. It turns twice. This time the old injury rests against the bench.

The warmth does not enter all at once. It reaches the tight muscles slowly, following the animal’s familiar experience of heat rising through bedrock. When the creature lifts itself, the sensation ceases. When it settles again, it returns.

For several visits, that is all.

Then the animal begins to arrive before the ache has made it restless.

The power learns the pressure that helps. A slight yielding of the bench supports the limb without holding it. Nothing closes around the body. No part of the relief requires stillness.

In time, the creature sleeps through an entire cooling interval without getting up to turn.

The ridge remains beneath its coat. Its step is still recognizable. It still checks the outlet with one hand.

But there is less that it must arrange its body around.

***

Eventually the power allows itself a presence.

It chooses the dry approach, away from the drop and outside the paths between sleeping chambers and water. It gives that presence weight enough to answer a low cough through the stone, warmth enough to be found by touch, and a surface that will yield rather than trap a curious digit.

It does not imitate another animal’s call.

The first visitor comes within reach, listens, and leaves.

The second bites it.

This proves harmless to both parties.

The young climb onto it. An adult removes one of them, then explores the unfamiliar surface with considerable thoroughness.

The hesitant animal arrives much later.

It touches the presence low down, where it once examined the seam of your shoe. It pulls. Finding no loose outer layer, it shifts its attention elsewhere.

The power remains still.

A hand travels over its surface, finds a shallow fold, and pauses. The animal holds this while it listens to something across the pool.

Then it lets go.

There will be other visits. The power has made no plan for their number.

***

When you next sit on the bench, nothing needs explaining in order for you to rest there.

The animal comes by its familiar route. You make room. It feels the surface with the backs of its hands, turns, and settles beside you.

For once, the injured shoulder is between you.

Its flank presses against your leg. Across the hollow, a young one is worrying at the patient stranger on the dry stone. A leaf spills its water. The pool answers with the hollow report of a rising bubble.

The animal beside you lifts its listening folds.

You remain still until it has heard enough.

Then its hand finds your wrist, and the two of you sit through the gathering rain.
