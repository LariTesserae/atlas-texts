The power does not begin by making it quiet.

It follows the two feelers through a single impact: the arrival through stone, the arrival through water, the small difference between them in which the animal lifts its feet. It feels the fork close. It waits with the animal while a point is caught in a ridge, and learns that waiting is sometimes easy and sometimes costs nearly everything the creature has eaten.

The cold is not, by itself, a wrong temperature. The roar is not one continuous injury. Neither is the animal’s persistence evidence that nothing hurts.

For a while the power attends to distinctions.

It is present for a molt. Beneath the cast, the new animal holds very still. A current gets under one loose edge of the old shell and lifts it. The animal has not yet hardened enough to hold against the next current.

The power bears down on the cast with no more weight than a grain of mineral.

The current passes.

That is its first act.

No light appears outside. The eleven notches remain eleven. Far below, someone is sharpening a tool with a flake taken from behind the gate.

***

Behind the rib, the retreat ends in a narrowing that sometimes closes too far.

The power does not enlarge it at once. There may be lives beyond it, or a passage whose pressure keeps the visible hollow open. It follows the narrowing inward, through stone that has arrived and stone that has not. It learns where the water goes when there is suddenly less room for water.

Then it changes a junction smaller than the animal’s smallest foot.

Afterward, when the hollow contracts, the back of the retreat moves before its roof descends. The animal is not squeezed between them. There is still a close surface against which to brace its shell. Its thick feeler still receives the mountain.

Nothing in the entrance reveals the difference.

The power watches through many contractions. Where its alteration gathers a current too sharply, it changes the angle again.

***

The next work takes longer.

Along one side of the retreat it makes a crease in which a pale film can live. Not food poured into the hollow, not a sudden abundance that will use up the water’s breath. A little more living surface, reached by the diluted light of morning. A place where the minute things already arriving in the water can remain.

For several days the animal passes it without feeding.

The power lets it.

On the fourth morning, the mouth opens against the crease. A yellow line appears inside the body. The animal moves on.

The film grows back slowly. There are other mouths in the deeper seams.

***

There is one change the power makes to the forming floor.

It does not forbid the stone to catch a foot. The point entering soft mineral is also what lets the animal stand. Instead, it makes the smallest yielding possible around a trapped point, so that a sustained backward pull can free it.

A quick pull still holds. The currents cannot pluck the creature loose.

The animal discovers this during a long crossing. One hind foot catches. It settles to wait, then shifts its weight. The point comes free.

For a time it tests that foot against its underside.

Later it is caught again, and frees itself sooner.

The mountain continues to form. There are still edges, retreats, journeys made a fraction before a surface vanishes. But fewer journeys must be completed on eleven feet.

***

When the young are carried to the ceiling, the power goes with them.

At the junction of facets there is room for two. Beyond it lies a bare interval the adult cannot cross while feeding them without exposing its whole underside to the currents.

The power gives the interval a roughness.

It is scarcely visible: a dull strip on the translucent stone, fine enough for the pads to hold. The parent finds it with one foot, withdraws, and tries again. On the next visit it stays longer beneath the young.

The power does not make them grow faster.

It remains near when they first step out.

***

The people return when the snow allows.

One lies on her shoulder with a hide folded under her cheek. She is carrying a replacement lashing, a blade, and a flake of bone. The gate has begun to hang unevenly.

As she reaches toward it, sunlight catches the animal.

Not brightly. Just clearly enough.

She sees it pull a caught foot from the floor. She sees it hesitate beside the shadow of her nail.

She takes her hand out.

For a long while she watches, with the vibration passing through her shoulder and jaw. Then she uses the wooden wedge to hold the crosspiece while she repairs the lashing from outside. It is slower work. Once she has to stop and rest her arms.

The power keeps a loosened patch of rock beneath her heel from giving way.

She takes two mineral flakes instead of three. On the descent she tells the others where the animal waits, and how little a hand needs to move to strike it.

That knowledge is theirs afterward. They add to it.

***

The hollow does not become safe in every part.

The power keeps attending. It does not count the first improvements sufficient merely because they are small, or make further changes merely because it can.

There are seasons when another crease is needed. There is a molt that goes badly for reasons having nothing to do with the currents. There are young in a seam no human eye will ever reach.

None of these is beneath its notice.

At noon, the three teeth cast their wavering shadows. A flake thickens behind them. The green strands bend, and a deep concussion travels through the summit.

The animal lifts three feet.

When it reaches the far wall, it feeds. Then it goes into the retreat behind the rib, though the mountain is still loud enough for walking.

Its feet loosen one at a time.

The feelers remain apart. The fork opens slowly.

For a while, it has eaten enough, and there is nowhere it needs to go.
