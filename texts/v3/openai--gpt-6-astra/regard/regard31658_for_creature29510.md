The power first withdraws its weight.

It had come close enough to cast a shadow, and under that shadow several cups had remained open longer than they otherwise would. There was nothing wrong with this. But before it offered anything, it wished to learn what happened without an offer.

For a season it attends to the hollow.

It does not gather the scattered sensations into a single mind in order to speak with them. Such a mind would be a new creature, and the one already here would have been altered before it had been met.

Instead, it follows the connections as they are. Some movements travel. Others end. Moisture received beneath a willow does not become relief everywhere. Damage at one margin is not necessarily known at the other. There are portions whose relation to one another consists, for a time, only in sharing the same wet stem.

The power learns to keep these distinctions.

Beneath a flap of heather, a pale fold has caught its fringe in a drying filament. Each attempt to withdraw tightens the filament against it. Nearby cups continue feeding. No larger response arrives.

The power loosens the filament.

The fold closes. Nothing thanks it. By evening the fringe has opened again, somewhat crookedly, and is working at a particle of softened leaf.

This is its first act.

*

There are things it does not do.

It does not make the animal distasteful to blackbirds. The blackbird working the willow margin has young elsewhere, and the pale commotion under the heather includes lives no less particular for being hurried.

It does not join every severed cord. Some separations have allowed portions to survive damage that would otherwise have spread. Some lead to new growth. Some are simply losses. Looking closely does not make these all the same.

Nor does it preserve every cup. A surface that could never die would eventually occupy the places where new surfaces might have grown.

But this does not make every injury necessary.

Where a cut end is drying before it can close, the power lets a bead of water remain beneath a leaf a little longer. It does not flood the passage. The vole returns through it that evening with its belly dry.

Where a beetle has fed, it leaves the beetle fed.

*

Beyond the willows, the mill continues turning.

The power goes there too.

It finds warm bearings, accumulated dust, a woman who can distinguish a change in the machinery through the soles of her boots. It finds people whose food depends on the mill running, and a man who has begun to believe the ringing in his ears is part of silence.

The hollow is not the only place worth attending to.

At the next maintenance meeting, the woman is given time to finish explaining the vibration. Her measurements are checked. A worn mounting is replaced; another is properly seated. There is money for the work without anyone losing a week’s wages.

The change reaches the peat gradually, over several stoppages.

The animal adjusts. A cord that has rested tightly against a root relaxes by a fraction. In one junction, repeated rubbing ceases. The fans still fill the air, but less harshly. The man with the ringing ears is offered protection that fits.

No one at the mill learns about the cups.

They do not need to know about them for the repair to have been worth doing.

*

The power looks further ahead.

There is a summer in which rain will pass on either side of the lowland for weeks. There is a shallow ditch, almost lost beneath growth, that would then draw moisture away from the willow margin. There are plans, not yet made, to clear that ditch.

Here the power intervenes more firmly.

It does not arrange for the plans to be forgotten. Someone might remember them later.

Instead, it helps the people responsible discover what the ditch connects to. A survey is made during wet weather as well as dry. The peat is measured. An older map is found. The apparently empty hollow acquires a description accurate enough to matter in decisions made far from it.

The ditch is left uncleared. Farther downstream, where doing so will not drown the moss or wet anyone’s house, a small obstruction slows the loss of water.

When the dry summer comes, the exposed tips turn brittle. Some cups die. At noon there is little movement near the light.

But under the rooted willow, the deeper stems remain cool. Separated portions survive there. With the autumn rain, blunt growth begins to feel outward along old passages.

Its return does not reproduce the former pattern.

The power makes room for that too.

*

It never gives the being a name that all its parts must answer to.

There may someday be people who crouch here and study it. They may find ways to distinguish its living connections from the dead ones, or discover that what looks like one kind of animal is several. The power would welcome their care, and restrain their impatience where it could.

For now, it keeps the place from becoming famous.

Attention can arrive with too many feet.

Its own attention remains, though not always at the center. Sometimes it follows the blackbird beyond the willows. Sometimes it stays with a worker walking home in the quieter evening. Sometimes there is a small entanglement beneath a leaf, and it is there.

By morning the moss has taken up the night’s moisture.

Thousands of irregular rims rise a few millimeters into the air. They open at different times. A few never open. One, beneath a grass blade, unfolds a crooked fringe and holds it against a loose flake until the flake comes free.

Nothing in the clearing rises much above your ankle.

There is still enough happening to occupy a great kindness.
