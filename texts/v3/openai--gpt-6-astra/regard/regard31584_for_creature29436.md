The power first becomes aware of the town through the hand beneath the roof.

It had been attending to the stair: to the burden of stone, the small failures of mortar, the passage of people who never looked down. Then, within that weight, it finds a finger reaching for another finger.

It stays until the young one is carried home.

For some time afterward, it does nothing.

There is much to learn. What feels cold to the power is ordinary to the inhabitants. What sounds intolerably loud serves them, in part, as a means of knowing whether the channels are running properly. The dark is not a deprivation they have asked to have remedied. Even the empty houses require care in interpretation. One is empty because its occupants died. Another is empty because two households cannot agree about a wall.

The power could lift the stair.

It follows the water instead.

It learns where food catches, where grit settles, where a slight change in flow would carry a winter’s stored meals out of reach. It discovers that the roof over the dry patch will last through several more births, probably, but not through the lives of the children recently placed beneath it.

The inhabitants know this too.

One visits the roof after every unfamiliar sequence of footsteps. The soft finger travels up a leg and under the cupped center, measuring something by a means the power does not at first understand.

In an upper room there are seven unsuccessful replacements.

One is too heavy. Two have split while being hollowed. Another would shed water inward along its underside. The best has a fault that can only be felt when pressure is applied at opposite corners. Its maker keeps returning to it, as though the fault might eventually prove to have been a mistake of the hand.

The power has found something it might offer.

But first it must find a way to be answered.

***

It chooses an unoccupied waiting recess near the store of grit.

There it gathers a little of itself into a body that will not damage the paving. Four limbs, a low back, a hand resting in the hollow. It gives the hand warmth no greater than another inhabitant’s, and leaves its grip open.

The first passerby touches it, stops, and touches it again.

The power knows neither the greeting nor the question.

This causes difficulties.

By the time three inhabitants have assembled, it has inadvertently claimed to be carrying something, to have lost something, and possibly to be somebody’s relative.

It could reach into their minds and take the language whole. Instead, it lets them correct its hand.

The corrections are not uniformly gentle.

Over many meetings, it learns enough to say that it has come from elsewhere and can work stone. Elsewhere is received with less interest than it expected. Work stone requires demonstration.

They bring it a broken scoop.

It joins the halves without thickening the bowl.

The owner feels along the repair, then hands it back. The edge has always been uncomfortable there. Could that be changed as well?

It can.

After this, word travels through the town.

***

The roof-maker does not immediately ask for a roof.

There are questions about materials. There is a lengthy exchange concerning a stone that bends, which the power has offered and which the roof-maker regards with suspicion. There is an attempt to explain the falling drop from above. The power, though it has watched that drop for longer than anyone in the town has been alive, is required to follow a finger through the entire explanation.

Only then are the failed roofs brought out.

The fault in the best one is examined by both of them.

The roof-maker holds the power’s hand against it.

Here.

Yes.

Here is where it will break.

Yes.

The pressure changes.

Can you give me a piece without this?

***

The piece is dark, fine-grained, and small enough to be moved by four inhabitants.

The power does not put a finished roof beside the old one. There are details its watching has missed: the clearance needed for a carrying back, the place where a waiting adult braces a hand, the roughness by which someone approaching from below recognizes the shelter before stepping onto its raised paving.

It works beside the roof-maker. When asked, it softens the stone beneath a tool. When asked, it makes it hard again.

Some of the work seems unnecessary to it.

One edge is rubbed for a very long time.

It waits.

The new roof is installed between births. During the work, the falling drop pauses at the seam, swelling into a bead that does not fall. The inhabitants notice. Several retreat beneath nearby houses. One remains to investigate the suddenly altered sound of the hollow slab.

The power has learned enough by then to explain what it is doing.

It has not yet learned how much warning such a thing requires.

There is a conversation afterward.

It remembers it.

***

The old roof is not discarded.

Moved to a dry ledge, it becomes a place where tools are laid while their owners work. Its deeply worn cup prevents the smallest implements from rolling away. For years afterward, someone occasionally runs a finger beneath its center and feels how little stone had remained.

The next young one opens its limbs beneath the new roof.

The drops strike with a slightly different note.

An adult waits with a hand extended. The power stays in the lane, where it can be found but does not occupy the space needed by the others.

Eventually the soft finger opens.

It finds the offered hand.

***

There are other requests.

A channel that cannot be reached without risking a limb. A shell scraper with an edge both fine and forgiving. A question about the weight that walks above them.

There are also days when the pale chip is turned toward the power.

It passes those doors.

It makes no promise that stone will never fail or that no one will die. It does promise to return to the waiting recess, and does so. Sometimes there is work. Sometimes someone wants to know whether it remembers a story correctly.

Once, the inhabitant in the upper room sends down the empty food dish with a small object inside: a finger cap, worn thin on one side.

The power asks whether a new one is wanted.

The answer comes back slowly, through several hands.

Not a new one. This one fits.

So it strengthens the worn side, preserving the hollow within, and sends it home.
