The power noticed the narrowing beneath the slab before it tried to learn the inhabitant’s name.

There were several ways to lift the stone. Most would have disturbed the sheet of tissue beneath the sill. Even a successful lifting would change the pressure across that sheet, open the hollow to a new movement of air, and release grit into the plants below.

Instead, it found the weight that would next come down upon the entrance and gave it another path.

Farther back in the bank, beyond the roots and the pale connections, the load began to pass into deeper ground. The change took a winter. Nothing already touching the inhabitant was pulled away. The wall retained its old lean, its damp seams, its spaces full of soil. But the scrape beneath the slab ceased to descend.

The inhabitant continued withdrawing from the sill for some time afterward.

The power let it.

An expectation held in living tissue could not be corrected merely because the danger had passed.

***

It investigated the machinery from the other end.

Whatever paid for the garden did so elsewhere, and elsewhere proved neither inexhaustible nor entirely secure. There were provisions made long ago, still being honored by mechanisms whose surroundings had changed. One dependency led to another. The power followed them until it reached things that could fail.

It arranged continuity.

It did not increase the supply into the fungal threads. More energy was not necessarily more life; it could be faster growth in spaces that had no room for it, a disturbance among partners whose accommodation had taken a long time. The small, steady deliveries continued at their accustomed rate.

A second means of sustaining them was prepared, tested away from the hollow, and left unused.

The exposed ceramic corner remained cold.

***

The marker could have been read by removing the frost and turning its face.

The power read what it could without doing either.

Some of the indentations belonged to a system of marks. Others were damage. Enough remained to suggest an origin, but not enough to establish what the inhabitant was now, or what it would wish to be called. There had been intentions here. Those intentions were old.

The power did not treat them as instructions from the living.

It kept what it learned close. No path appeared outside. No sign was fixed to the retaining wall. The hollow did not become a place to which strangers were directed for their improvement.

The gray wedge of daylight continued to end at the first mound of leaves.

***

For a long time, attention consisted chiefly of restraint.

The power learned the intervals within which the hollow changed. It followed a disturbance from a leaf to a cable, from the cable into a slow chamber, and onward through a sequence that took the outside world from frost to thaw.

It learned that similar beginnings did not always have similar endings.

It learned how much of what looked like stillness was work.

There were things it could not determine by watching. A structure might carry sensation, memory, both, or neither in any useful sense of those words. The power could trace the passage of a substance without knowing whether that passage mattered to someone.

It did not settle the uncertainty by declaring the hollow empty.

Nor did it fill the uncertainty with a person imagined in its own likeness.

When the next dry interval came, it watched the receiving film approach the bottom of the glass tray and find only dust.

It waited through the ordinary range of such intervals.

Then it placed a little water at the end where thaw water usually gathered.

The water carried the same small burden of minerals as seepage through the wall. It was at the temperature of the glass. Its volume was less than a good thaw would have brought.

The film reached it.

Over the following weeks, changes passed inward from the alcove. Some could have resulted from water alone. The power made no announcement to itself about gratitude.

When the tray emptied, it stayed empty.

***

The next offering came much later.

By then, the power had found a way to make its presence locally perceptible without compressing the cushions or warming the leaves. At the unoccupied end of the tray, it placed a small piece of porous stone. Only the stone itself was new; its substance was ordinary to the bank.

Moisture could be held within it. A fine living thread could enter its pores or pass it by.

For a season it remained dry.

Then, during another prolonged shortage, it became faintly damp.

The power watched broadly enough to notice injury elsewhere, and patiently enough not to turn every growth toward the stone into an answer. It was trying to establish something simpler than language: a place where a need might meet a response, without the response spreading everywhere.

The film touched the stone and later withdrew.

On the next occasion it followed a slightly different route.

Years passed before a slender connection remained in one of the pores after the moisture was gone.

By then the power had altered its own habits of attention. It could wait without continually testing whether the waiting was over.

***

Safety brought changes that danger had prevented.

The thin sheet under the entrance stopped diminishing. Much later, one edge thickened. A cushion spread into a space that had remained bare through several generations of leaves.

Behind the tray, tissue occupied a crack without preparing an alternate route around it.

These were small expenditures. They could be afforded now.

The power did not seek to make the whole hollow comfortable by the standards of warmer beings. It preserved the cold, the slow exchanges, the fungal work in the fallen leaves. It left the old stems to whiten and soften where their substance could be recovered.

Beyond one unoccupied seam, it secured a little additional space. The seam was neither widened into a doorway nor furnished with food to draw growth through it. If a root eventually found its way there, it would find damp grit, darkness, and stone that would hold.

There was room available.

There was no requirement to use it.

***

One spring, water gathered in the tray without assistance.

The power withheld its offering. The receiving film crossed the glass along its older route, and the porous stone stayed dry.

Above it, the new leaf had become an old leaf. Its replacement rested against its silver underside. Between them, the hairs bent under a familiar pressure.

The wall held.

Deep in the bed, a little fluid passed into a chamber, remained there, and was altered.

The power attended to that passage without hurrying it. There would be time for whatever followed, including things it had not yet learned how to recognize.

At the entrance, beneath the slab, the shallow clearance remained open.
