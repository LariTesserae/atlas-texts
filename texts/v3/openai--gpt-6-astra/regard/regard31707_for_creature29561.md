The power’s attention enters the bank without displacing the air.

It finds the fern, the ant beneath it, the small chambers in the clay. It finds the mother’s breathing and the quicker breathing against her belly. Farther in, beyond the cavity, a root has reached a seam of damp earth. Water moves through it so slowly that the movement would be lost among almost any others.

There is no need to wake her.

The power attends first to the foot.

The injury is old. Bone has joined at an angle; the tissues around it have learned to bear a load they were not shaped for. Much of what she does is possible because she has accommodated it. The cautious placement, the extra moment before a climb, are parts of a working knowledge of her body.

It does not straighten the foot while she sleeps and leave her to discover an unfamiliar balance on a branch.

Instead, it eases the inflammation around the joint. It frees a small place where each step pulls against a nerve. Over the coming weeks, as she walks, the foot will mend a little further. Her movements will teach her its new limits at the same rate that those limits change.

Tonight, the ache subsides.

She shifts deeper into the clay and does not wake.

The young one presses a forefoot against her belly. Nothing is wrong with him that requires correction. He is small, dependent, and alive in a place where being small requires cover. The power leaves him his sleep.

Then its attention follows the water.

Under the pool lies clay, and under that, gravel deposited when a river ran here with enough force to carry it. The gravel still holds water. Far away, beyond the last wooded rise, a well draws from the same buried reach.

The machinery has several purposes. Some of it moves water. Some of it keeps things cold. People work beside it through the night, tired in the heat. One of them has a child sleeping at home beneath a fan.

The power does not stop it all.

It follows the pipes and finds a leak beneath a concrete yard. The escaping water has made a hollow that no one has yet noticed. Before the next shift ends, a narrow crack will open in the paving, away from anyone’s feet. Water will darken its edges. The fault will be visible, and it will be repaired.

That will help. It will not be enough.

There are measurements that need taking, and people who would act differently if they knew what lay beyond the boundary of their site. The power does not put obedience into their minds. It makes the evidence easier to encounter and harder to lose.

A neglected gauge begins recording again. The figures reach the person responsible for them. When she asks to survey the old channels, there is time in the schedule and money still available for the work.

None of this requires the mother to be caught, carried, or displayed.

Her hollow remains a hollow in a bank.

Beyond the coast, moist air is moving inland. It has been moving inland for days, lifting elsewhere, falling elsewhere, passing over this flat country without releasing much.

The power gives it a small assistance.

Not a storm. The pool has inhabitants along its dry shelf now; a sudden rise would drown some and scour others away. The banks need time to receive what they have gone without.

Near dawn, rain begins thinly enough that many leaves remain dry beneath other leaves. Drops darken the dust between the mother’s scales where her tail lies nearest the entrance. She draws it inward.

For a while, the canopy takes nearly everything.

Then water gathers at leaf tips. It falls from one level to the next, striking old leaves with a heavier sound. The ground softens by degrees. Ants move their brood uphill. A beetle takes shelter beneath the same fragment it had been turning.

The rain stops before the cavity becomes wet.

There will be more on another night.

By evening, the pool has risen less than the depth of the young one’s foot. Its margins are still muddy. The frog still has its root. Beneath the fallen trunk, termites have repaired the broken passage and begun another.

The mother emerges after dark.

At the lip of the cavity she pauses, as she always pauses. Her nose moves over the damp earth. The air carries more information now, lifted from bark and soil by the rain.

She puts down the injured foot.

There is no revelation. She does not turn toward the sky. After a moment she puts her weight on it and brings the other foot forward.

The young one climbs onto her tail.

For this night there will be water, a little nearer to cover. There will be food in a place she already knows. She will return with enough strength left to nurse him and sleep.

The power remains attentive to what happens next: to the repair, to the measurements, to the rain’s passage through the clay. Help can fail after it has begun. A channel can be blocked farther down; a useful finding can sit unread. It will not mistake its first actions for the completion of its care.

But it does not follow so closely that every leaf must turn aside for her.

She goes beneath the fallen tree in her own slow way, stops at the familiar fork, and finds the new passage with her nose.
