What it has brought back is warmer than the channel water.

Not much warmer. A difference small enough to be found by the fringe, and lost when the hand is opened.

The others examine it. One withdraws, then returns. Another presses a forefoot against the gathering animal’s wrist and holds it there.

Nothing speaks from the water.

But when the animal lowers its hand again, the warmth is still there, a little below the reach of its fingers.

***

The power could have understood the plateau from above. It could have followed every pressure through the buried chambers, counted the winters held in storage, distinguished the passages that crossed distance from those that crossed years.

It does these things.

They are not enough.

A complete account of the waterways does not tell it why one animal passes three suitable dwellings to sleep in a fourth. Knowing the condition of every capsule does not tell it which absent adult is expected to retrieve it. It finds a little arrangement of tensioned stems that impairs the drainage of a chamber. Those who live there have repaired the damage around it many times.

The arrangement itself they leave alone.

The power begins there, with what its knowledge has not explained.

It attends to the pressure of a foot held longer than necessary. To food carried past hungry mouths and given to a particular one. To the old individual that visits an abandoned listening wall whenever the water tastes of thaw.

It follows the animal at the edge without taking anything from its pockets.

In one pocket is a braid so worn that several strands have been replaced. In another is a strip of pale tissue folded around nothing.

***

The first thing it changes is a fastening.

Far inside the plateau, a family is leaving a chamber whose floor has begun to rise. They have been through this before. Two carry capsules. One goes ahead to open a door. The last is young, large enough to help and not yet good at judging when to stop helping.

It turns back for a bundle of fibers.

The door fastens behind it.

There is no malice in the closure. A collar has swollen in response to pressure. The passage is doing what has kept the chambers beyond it habitable for a hundred seasons.

The young animal presses its fringe to the narrowing seam.

On the other side, an adult is cutting with its mouth plates. It cannot cut quickly enough.

The power holds one small part of the collar from swelling.

The adult gets its inner fingers through. The young animal pushes. Between them they open enough space.

Only after the body has passed does the collar close.

This costs the plateau a little water. The power receives it elsewhere, into a layer that has room.

For a long time afterward, the adult examines the door.

The young one still has the bundle.

***

The power does not leave them dependent on accidents of mercy.

Near a well-used listening wall, it makes a small answering surface.

It is no larger than the underside of a forefoot. It has no light, no face, no voice carried uselessly through the air. Pressed once, it presses back with the same force. Pressed hard, it yields. If the foot is lifted, the answer ends.

The animals discover it while trimming the wall.

At first they try to eat it.

Then they try to detach it.

Then one braces six feet around it and presses with a seventh.

For many days, this is all that happens: pressure, answer, release.

The power learns which repetitions invite continuation and which insist that an exchange is finished. It makes mistakes. Once, after a pattern associated with food, it produces tender white tissue, and the animal facing it overturns the offering and strikes the wall.

Later it understands. The pattern had referred to food owed by someone else.

It does not offer tissue that way again.

***

Their first sustained exchange concerns a passage.

An adult presses a route into the answering surface: a sequence of inclines, water tastes, distant beats. The power follows it and finds a crushed junction.

It returns the pressure of closure.

The adult repeats the route.

Again the power answers: closed.

The adult strikes the surface with enough force to tear the skin of its foot.

The power waits. Then it offers, slowly, what it can distinguish beyond the junction.

Water. Roots. A pocket of air.

A living body.

Every foot in the chamber becomes still.

The power has already begun keeping the air fresh. Now it lays out three possible approaches, one at a time, allowing the listeners to interrupt.

They reject the shortest. It passes through a capsule bed the power had mistaken for a store of food.

They choose another.

***

After that, the answering places spread.

Not everywhere. Some chambers refuse them. In one, the inhabitants cut away the surrounding tissue until the little surface hangs loose, and the power withdraws it. In another, they incorporate it into an instrument, asking it for no knowledge at all, only a tension the living stems cannot maintain.

It holds that tension.

Elsewhere there is work.

The power helps them grow doors that can be opened from either side when the pressure becomes dangerous. Together they make capsule cradles that detach before their chambers collapse, rising into channels where adults can find them. The first design rises too far and attracts birds. The second travels beneath a folded skin.

The animals improve the third without consulting it.

The power is pleased, though it takes them some time to discover what that answer means.

It does not still the great discharges. Too many creatures feed along their margins; too much buried water needs to move. Instead it helps make the warning legible sooner, and holds refuge chambers open while the exposed ground turns over.

Where hunger repeatedly drives families to ruin the pumping collars, it helps them cultivate the same tender tissue along expendable roots.

There are quarrels over the new beds.

These take longer than the doors.

***

The old animal from the plateau’s edge comes to an answering place carrying its worn braid.

It lays the braid in running water.

The power feels the record swell.

Here is a route used often, repaired often, then used once under conditions none of its makers had anticipated. Here is a severed strand. Here are later additions, attempts to describe the junction where someone failed to return.

The power searches.

It searches places the animal could never have entered. It follows an old current into a season stored beneath the roots. It finds a chamber that still exists but no longer opens into the same year.

There is someone there.

Not waiting patiently. Not preserved unchanged for the sake of a reunion.

The missing animal has lived. Its back carries different plants. It shares a dwelling with others and has young in two layers of water. Sometimes it visits a passage that ends in a wall and touches an old braid fastened there.

The power establishes a thin connection first.

No body can pass through it. Only pressure.

At one end, an animal rests beside a listening surface. At the other, far away in more than distance, another does the same.

The power carries a familiar contraction between them.

Then it becomes very quiet.

***

It cannot make every such answer.

Some routes end in remains. Some end in tissue long since taken up by roots. When it knows, it does not offer uncertainty as a kindness.

It learns to give these answers where there is room for others to settle close.

Sometimes it is asked to repeat a pressure pattern from the dead. It can do this with great fidelity.

It also makes clear that the answering pressure is its own.

There are individuals who return often to hear it. Others hear it once and never ask again.

***

At the edge, the droplets continue hanging below the lip.

The power has not put a staircase through the cloud.

Below are winds, unfamiliar growth, long stretches with nowhere to grip. There are also pools under open skies, and living surfaces that have never felt the plateau’s beat.

It brings back small amounts of water, each kept separate.

Around them, the animals construct shallow vessels of their own.

For a season they do little but taste.

Then they begin asking about weight.

The power answers by taking the strain on a single root while an animal loads it with its body. It releases the strain gradually, so the worker can feel exactly what the root can bear.

They grow another beside it.

And another.

The old braids remain fastened below the edge. New ones join them. The work is interrupted by weather, feeding, performances, disputes, and the arrival of young who put their feet everywhere.

One day a platform hangs low enough to disappear into the cloud.

An animal descends onto it carrying no tools. It has brought a little frame of pale stems.

For a while it adjusts their tension.

The power waits beneath the platform, holding nothing that the roots can hold themselves.

Then a foot presses down.

Through the frame, through the growing roots, into the immense wet body of the country, a small unfamiliar rhythm travels.

From above, someone answers.
