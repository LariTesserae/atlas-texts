The power does not begin by quieting the building.

It could. It could close the throats deep below, let the pressure escape through openings wider than the summit, bring the immense chord to an end. The visitor would hear their own breath. The grit would settle into a silence older than the walls.

But the creature is holding a hand with nearly all the rest of its body.

The power notices this.

It comes first as something capable of being held.

Not a radiance in the shaft, not a voice forcing its way through the chord. A small weight settles beside the raised slab. It has warmth, though not quite the warmth of brick, and a pulse that does not hurry. Its arrival alters the six notes. The creature feels the alteration and begins to withdraw its offered limb.

The weight stays where it is.

It neither follows nor disappears.

After a long interval, the creature reaches again.

The power has made a hand with fewer joints than the creature’s hand, but more than a human hand would need. It can meet each small adjustment without enclosing the fingers. Under its surface, the pulse is clear.

The creature establishes contact.

For a while, this is all that happens.

***

To understand the summit, the power listens from the places where the creature listens.

A broad palm against a pier receives something different from a breast against the slab. The underside of a lobe, placed across two mortar joints, distinguishes what a whole human body would miss. The chord has distances within it. There are ways in which an opening far below is near, and the other side of a wall is very far away.

The creature’s abrupt crossings are not all alarms. Sometimes it moves to follow an alteration too slight to remain audible from its old position. Sometimes it goes where an alteration will arrive.

The power learns enough to stop mistaking its stillness for waiting.

It also finds that one of the creature’s limbs has remained tightly folded through every movement.

There is an old damaged place inside the fold. The creature can use the limb, but only by gathering its substance around that place and keeping it under pressure. It does this so continuously that the effort has become part of the shape of its body.

The power does not touch the damage.

Instead, beneath the hand it is already holding, it supplies a little support. No pulling, no straightening. Only enough that, for a moment, the creature need not carry the strain by itself.

The creature closes its jaw plates.

The support eases at once.

Later, the limb comes forward again.

They do this many times. The power follows the creature’s movements rather than completing them. Little by little, the fold opens. A dark, smooth surface emerges, long hidden from the violet light.

At last the creature places the underside of the limb flat against the slab.

A part of the deepest note reaches it that has not reached it in a very long time.

Its whole body draws close to the brick.

The power keeps its hand still.

***

It leaves the fingerprints alone.

There are things it could discover about the hands that made them. But those answers are not needed to care for the place where the impressions remain. It does not smooth them away while easing the damaged tissue. It does not press its own mark beside them.

When the limb folds again, it folds differently.

***

The power’s next work is for the visitor.

A human cannot stay long here. The sound presses upon organs that have no means of receiving it safely. Endurance is not the same as understanding, and the creature should not have to discover, through a weakening pulse, what its home does to a guest.

Between two piers, the power lays a small patch of floor.

The bricks resemble the surrounding bricks, but are not disguised as ancient. Their edges are unworn. Beneath them is a separation finer than a hair, and below that a chamber shaped to take the crushing pressure out of a human body without taking it out of the room.

Someone seated there can breathe without pain.

The creature examines the patch from every accessible side. It lays a palm on the new joints. It passes through the small space beneath them.

The power has left that space large enough for it.

At the edge of the patch is a low rest where a visitor may put a hand. Contact made there carries clearly to the slab. A pulse can cross without a shout, without anyone having to remain until their teeth ache and their thoughts begin to break apart.

The creature tries the rest with one lobe.

Then with its repaired limb.

Afterward it returns to the slab. The new floor remains unoccupied for a long while.

The power does not count this as failure.

***

There are no trees planted in the ducts. No water is poured into the dry masonry. The narrow ways are not widened merely because another kind of body would find them difficult.

The power checks the unreachable chambers and the courses descending beyond sight. Where weight is held, it makes certain that weight can continue to be held. It leaves the instrument’s workings intact.

Then it returns to the small hand.

It has learned by now to make one slight change in its pulse before releasing contact. The creature has learned the change. Sometimes it releases first.

This time, instead, it draws the hand toward the pier behind it.

The power allows itself to be led.

The creature spreads two of the unfamiliar fingers across a joint, adjusts their pressure, and waits. Something travels up from the immense unseen body of the building: a minute difference within one note, arriving through the brick.

The power feels it.

The creature shifts the fingers a little farther apart.

There.

For the first time since arriving, the power is shown where to listen.

And it listens.
