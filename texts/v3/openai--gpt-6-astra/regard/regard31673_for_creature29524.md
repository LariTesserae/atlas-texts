The power first touches the city where nothing is waiting to be rescued: a length of mature bark, shaded on both sides, carrying no more weight than it can bear.

It applies a little pressure.

The answer comes from three directions.

One is the recoil of the stem. Another is the slow tightening of an animal beneath the court. The third arrives much later, through a chamber that appears to be nearby but is not. There, something releases its hold and moves away.

The power lifts its touch.

For several days, that is all it does. It learns how lightly it must approach to avoid announcing a collapse.

***

There are things it could accomplish immediately.

It could fill the low basins with water. It could hold every tower upright. It could open the sealed chambers and carry their occupants into sunlight.

But the basins are not empty vessels. Beneath them lie folded districts awaiting pressure enough to rise. Water poured into them would become height before it became relief.

And a body lifted out of these passages would not necessarily remain a body. Some of its continuities depend upon the ways the chambers meet. What looks like confinement from the avenue may be the means by which one end remains joined to the other.

The power follows the dull red threads.

It learns the difference between a creature stretched thin in passage and one that can no longer withdraw. It learns that stillness may be feeding, or support, or waiting for another animal. It learns how much work is concealed in the apparent rest of a pale surface against a wall.

At last it finds the clear bead behind the bark.

The ring around it is dark. Two passages farther inward, flesh still takes nourishment from a closing avenue. The connections between are failing slowly.

The power does not break the wall.

It takes the weight above it.

Across six towers, the cracking changes pitch. Beneath the court, an animal tightens sharply, then waits. Nothing falls.

With the load removed, a narrow strip of bark begins to soften.

The power offers moisture there, no more than would gather in a sheltered seam at dawn. It keeps the moisture from being drawn upward into new growth. For this small place, for now, it remains available.

A red thread fills.

Then another.

The bead turns behind the thinning wall.

***

Making an opening takes most of a night.

The power could do it faster. The animal cannot.

Whenever its threads empty too abruptly, the softening stops. Whenever it presses forward, the passage yields a little. There is room behind it as well as ahead.

Near dawn, the folded rim reaches the outer surface.

It rests there for a long time.

Then a contraction passes into the stem: small, followed by another.

The power widens the passage.

The creature comes through slowly, gathering portions of itself from rooms that have not communicated freely in many seasons. In one chamber, a white length of injured flesh remains attached to the wall. The power supports it until the animal either takes it back or lets it go.

By sunrise, the bead has disappeared beneath a neighboring court.

Only then does the power begin returning the weight to the towers.

It does so in increments, allowing the roots to thicken between them.

***

Elsewhere, a fruit is ripening badly.

Four of its seams have opened. The fifth is held shut by a fold of the young animal caught between rind and column. Each effort to pull free tightens the chamber around it.

An adult has entered along the underside. It braces one wall, but the wall is not the only thing that needs holding.

The power settles beneath the unopened portion of the district.

For a moment, the young creature pulls harder.

The power does not pull back. It holds.

The adult changes its attachments. A broad pad flattens against the rind. Through the shared tissue comes a tension the young animal has felt before, though never without the whole district dragging at it.

One fold loosens.

The trapped portion slips inward.

Above them, the fifth seam opens with a report that crosses the plain.

The district descends crookedly. Two courts overlap. A row of columns leans toward the afternoon sun.

The power lets them lean.

By evening, the young animal is already adjusting them.

***

It does not make the city quiet.

The splitting of husks continues. Roofs unroll in hard light. Avenues close, and the inhabitants gather along their cloudy inner surfaces.

But in several dense knots, a new kind of chamber begins to grow.

Its first sign is a thickening low on a stem. Instead of reaching upward, the fibers turn around one another, carrying the weight past a shallow hollow. The hollow has two openings. Neither lies in the direction of the strongest growth.

When the neighboring towers rise, a pleated collar unfolds around it.

Inside, very little changes.

The power makes only a few. It leaves some shaded and some warm, some narrow, some broad. Their inner surfaces bear no feeding film. They do not lure the animals with nourishment.

For a long time, none enters.

Then one creature passes through a hollow on its way elsewhere. Its flank rests briefly against the wall. The wall holds its own shape.

The creature withdraws.

Several nights later, it returns.

***

The water requires more care.

A great abundance would feed the very urgency the power has come to ease. A ration imposed from outside would make the city dependent upon an attention its inhabitants cannot summon or question.

So the power works on what is already being lost.

The highest yellowing leaves shed heat into the night. It gives some of them finer grooves, then waits through dry mornings and wet ones. Beneath them it grows reservoirs that do not swell toward budding. Their walls deepen inward, into spaces the city can sustain without drawing its neighbors thin.

A pressure at one opening releases a little water.

A pressure at another closes it.

At first, the inhabitants drain them whenever they find them. Nearby stems put on sudden growth. One reservoir tears under competing pulls.

The power supports the torn tissue but does not repeat that arrangement.

Over time, the reservoirs become smaller and more numerous. Their openings lie inside the sheltered chambers. A creature can drink there without holding a roof away from its back.

The plants receive water too, slowly, through deep fibrous sleeves.

Nothing flowers all at once.

***

In one of the new chambers, an animal settles along a wall and leaves a broad space beside itself.

The power has seen this posture elsewhere.

It searches neither the whole plain nor the hidden interiors to supply the missing body. It does not fill the space with an imitation pulse.

It keeps the chamber sound.

Much later, another animal approaches. It tests the outer stem.

The one inside tightens.

The newcomer withdraws.

On a different night, a different pressure comes through the wall. This time the resting animal loosens its folds.

The power does not need to know whether this is the one that was awaited.

***

There are still collapses.

There are still passages abandoned because widening them would cost too much. The city remains crowded, strange in its distances, loud with the labor of remaining alive.

But fewer clear beads disappear into solid bark.

The new collars are taken up by neighboring growth. Adults rest in the sheltered hollows, and young ones press against their walls. In later fruits, similar chambers sometimes form around the small, uncertain pulls of developing bodies.

The power continues to attend to what has not yet learned to hold.

One night, the creature with the old brown scar enters a low hollow beneath a causeway.

It attaches along one side. Its threads fill and empty. Outside, an entire quarter begins to rise.

The animal prepares to brace.

Then, slowly, its pads loosen.

The roof remains where it is.
