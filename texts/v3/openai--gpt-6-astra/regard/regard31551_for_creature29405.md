The power notices the room the creature has made.

It does not enter at once.

Beyond the walls, it follows the long constriction through which the dry air passes. It learns where the sound begins, how the narrowness gathers it, how a change in one distant joint would alter the pressure against the plates behind the creature’s jaw.

It leaves that joint alone.

It follows the pale plants into their seams. Some are rooted in mortar; some are not. Some of the white leaves are dead, but remain useful to the living parts. There is no clean line along which a kindness could separate them.

It leaves them attached.

Then it considers the space beside the creature: not an empty space, but one made available by closing a fold and drawing a shoulder inward.

The power has been offered something. It will not answer only by inspecting its host.

It enters.

For this it takes a body no larger than the space actually offered. It has no need of elbows, or of a face at the height where a human face would be. Its weight rests on three bare ridges of tile. It is cool, though not so cool that heat begins to leave the creature. Where the light strikes it, the light continues onto the wall.

For a while they stand sideways together.

The creature opens its eyes.

There is another small wetness now, beside its own: the surface of an eye made for this visit. Nothing is reflected in it except what is here.

The creature closes its eyes again. The shoulder stays drawn inward.

The power could remain without weight, without breadth, without taking anything at all. But that would make it difficult to receive the hospitality. Instead it settles, very slightly, onto the tiles.

The pressure travels through the packed fragments.

One of the creature’s fingers rounds.

The power waits, then shifts its weight back. Not an imitation exactly. An answer in the same material.

The four fingers lift in succession.

This time the power can feel how much happens between the first movement and the last. What looked like slowness from the passage is full of distinctions. Pressure passes through the stopped pipe, divides around the buried column, returns through the floor. The creature is attending to the visitor by several routes at once.

The power lets itself be attended to.

It does not send a vision of forests through the wall. It does not explain the sky. There will be time for questions, if questions arise, and things beyond this pocket need not arrive first or all together.

For now, it gives an accurate account of where it is standing.

Here is its weight on the low ridge.

Here is the small interval between its side and the plaster.

Here is the place where it could move farther back.

The creature’s muzzle lifts from its chest.

Below the breast, the polished tile fragment changes its angle. A younger fold opens. Along its edge is a gray line that has not yet hardened.

The power moves farther back.

The creature eases its shoulder outward, nearly to its former position. There is still room for both.

They remain like this through a long passage of the roar.

Once, the creature touches the visitor directly. The broad end of its smallest finger rests against the body the power has made. The contact is light and prolonged. Beneath it, the power makes no special warmth, no healing brightness. It offers a surface that yields enough to register the touch and holds firm enough to be known.

When the finger withdraws, there is a shallow mark.

The power keeps it for the rest of the visit.

Eventually it shifts its weight toward the entrance, then waits. The creature lays its fingers farther apart. A pressure travels down through its heel and reaches the visitor’s lowest point of contact.

The power settles once more.

Only later does it leave.

Nothing has been installed. The pipe is still stopped. The hollows contain light, and the long constriction continues pouring its sound through the masonry.

Beyond the entrance, the power pauses with one small part of itself resting against the outer wall.

From inside comes a change too slight to disturb a grain of mortar.

It answers.

When it returns, it will know where to stand.
