The power did not come down through the opening.

There were things living on the edges of that light. To darken it, even briefly, would have been an arrival too large for the place.

Nor did it lift the stone.

It knew how to lift a mountain without breaking a root. That was not the difficulty. The difficulty was that the animal had not asked for the stone to be lifted, and shelter and imprisonment can have exactly the same dimensions.

For a while, nothing was added to the hollow but an attention that took up no room.

It attended to the open eye. To the skin beneath the beetle. To the ear holding water, and the other ear folded under the root. It did not mistake vulnerability for injury. It did not mistake the absence of struggle for contentment.

There were distinctions here that required patience.

The animal was not cold in the way a creature becomes cold while waiting for warmth. Its weight did not ache against the leaves. The root across its shoulder exerted no more pressure than its body could comfortably bear. No terror lay hidden behind the ordinary brown eye.

The power found no suffering that needed to be ended at once.

It stayed longer.

Beyond the ridge, weather crossed the mountain. Loose stones descended through gullies. Birds that had never seen the hollow passed above it, going elsewhere. The power did not bring these things inside as evidence of a better world.

Under the stone, the animal continued to look toward the rushes.

At last the power attended to something smaller than a movement.

Along the animal’s open foot, beneath the snail, there was the possibility of a turn: the palm inclining toward the belly, the blunt digits drawing a little closer together. Not an intention yet. Perhaps no more than the shape a resting body sometimes makes before it settles differently.

The hollow had no provision for it.

The foot was comfortable where it lay. The snail was comfortable in the foot. The pebble beside them occupied its exact hollow. Nothing pressed too hard upon anything else.

But the next comfortable position did not exist.

The power considered this without haste.

Then it made room.

It did not widen the recess or push the roots apart. No creature was displaced. The stone retained its underside, with every shallow groove intact.

What changed was less apparent than that.

If the animal turned its foot, there would be somewhere for the snail to rest.

If it raised its head, the fern would not have to break.

These places were not empty chambers waiting in the rock. They would exist only if they were needed, and only as generously as the movement required. The power did not draw the animal toward them. It laid no warmth beyond the roots, sent no enticing scent through the moss, put no dissatisfaction into the body that rested there.

It allowed the hollow to accommodate something it had not already accommodated.

For a long time, the animal did nothing.

The power did not regard this as failure.

The water remained suspended from the slab. The birds kept their feet hidden. In the small round ear, the delicate skin showed pink beneath its covering of water.

Then the digits of the open foot bent slightly.

The snail went with them at first. Its shell tilted. Beneath it, a fold of old leaf received its weight, so gradually that there was no instant at which it lacked support.

The animal’s foot came to rest against its own belly.

That was all.

No sound went through the hollow. The eye did not brighten with understanding. The animal did not seek the source of what had happened, and the power did not require it to know.

There was now a place beneath the stone where a foot had been.

It was small and dark, and no less a part of the hollow for being unoccupied.

The power left the new allowance in place. Not only around the animal, but around the folded wing, the seed’s pale root, the mouth beside the insect. It would attend to each difficulty as it became real, rather than deciding in advance what everything ought to become.

And because its attention need not be a gaze, it ceased to watch.

Under the broad stone, the animal rested in its changed position.

At the end of its muzzle, the whisker still held the drop.

For now, it held it.
