The power first notices the hand beneath the throat.

It has already noticed the basin. The weight of the water, the sideways labor of the stone, the places where one arrangement of the world presses through another: these are large enough to attract almost anything capable of attention.

But the hand remains where it is after the other creature has begun to lean.

There is no adjustment for comfort. The supporting individual’s shoulder is held at an awkward angle. Fine tremors pass up its arm. It will have to feed longer afterward.

The power watches until they move on.

Then it follows.

***

For a long time, its attention makes no difference that the inhabitants can detect.

It travels with households. It remains in chambers after they leave. It follows a rejected food stone through the silt and learns why it was rejected. It watches an old individual take a circuitous route to avoid a place where its feet can no longer distinguish a trustworthy bottom from a thin crust over moving slurry.

There is a shorter route. The old one knows it.

The power learns this too.

In one chamber it finds an individual crouched with both hands against its head. The foundation channels are clear. The cups outside carry no warning. The enclosure is working as well as it has ever worked.

It is not enough.

The power stays through the passage of three households. Each leaves the crouching individual a little food. One spends a long time with a hand beneath its jaw.

After the last household departs, the individual tries to stand. Its feet open against the floor and do not close again.

This is where the power first acts.

***

Along one wall, a pressure diminishes.

Not all pressure. The arriving currents can still be felt. A foot placed against the threshold still receives the deep, distributed movements of the basin. The warning of a nearby layer failure passes through without alteration.

But a ceaseless trembling, produced by distant beds rubbing together, no longer reaches a patch of floor about the size of a resting body.

The individual lifts its head.

It moves one hand, then the other. For a while it holds them in front of itself, touching the fingers together.

Eventually it crawls toward the wall.

At the edge of the altered place it stops so abruptly that the gravel in its belly knocks against the stone.

The power does nothing further.

The individual extends one foot. Withdraws it. Extends it again.

When it finally settles, it keeps the other foot outside.

***

There are mistakes.

The power quiets another hollow too thoroughly, and an arriving traveler recoils from it. In that place the traveler cannot feel its own household approaching. The stillness has the quality of a severed limb.

The power lets the connections return.

It changes the angle of a foundation passage and discovers that the small pale animals living there depend on the very eddy it has removed. It restores the eddy, then makes a second passage beneath it.

It learns to distinguish rest from absence.

The first individual sleeps for a long time.

When it wakes, it feeds. Its feeding is slow and interrupted, but it continues until the mat has yielded nearly everything the individual can take without damaging it.

Then it returns to the chamber and rubs fresh lining into the altered hollow.

***

The power could place a shelter beside every exhausted traveler.

Instead, it begins by keeping the existing shelters from failing during the hours when failure would kill those inside.

A blocked channel clears before water rises over a nursery.

A foundation holds through a surge that would have rolled its stones apart.

An entrance, buried while a household sleeps, remains open by the width of one small body. The first adult to squeeze through works from outside until the others can follow.

These things happen without tribute or announcement.

But the power does not leave everything unexplained.

At the first enclosure, it makes a change while an old cup-reader is watching: a shallow groove, slowly opening in the pavement, carrying a little water toward the quieted wall.

The old one puts a finger in it.

The groove stops growing.

The finger withdraws.

It grows again.

This occupies much of the day.

By the time other travelers arrive, the old one has discovered how to halt the work with a touch, and has done so in six places the power would not have chosen.

One of those places proves better.

***

Their dealings become more elaborate.

The power offers changes at the edges of inhabited space, small enough to examine, slow enough to interrupt. It learns the difference between an individual touching something to stop it and touching it because touching is how that individual thinks.

It sometimes waits a very long time.

There are disagreements. A household wants a chamber made quieter; another uses the pressures passing through it to judge the safety of a distant crossing. The power opens a resting recess behind the main chamber, then allows both households to test what can still be felt.

At another enclosure, no change is accepted.

The power leaves it alone.

Travelers continue to use that enclosure, though a better resting place stands less than half a day away. There is something in the old chamber’s response that matters to them. The power has not yet understood it.

It keeps listening.

***

The dry appearances require a different kind of attention.

The first time the power reaches into one, it finds that the trapped creature is not simply somewhere without water. Two places have acquired the same edge, and neither admits the substance of the other.

Pulling at the creature would pull it against the division.

So the power holds the division steady.

Within the hard light, the creature remains folded. Outside, its household presses hands against the boundary. Their fingers cannot reach its feet.

The power carries the pressure of those fingers across.

The trapped creature opens one foot.

Later, when the incompatible places begin to separate, the power keeps the opening broad enough for the creature to pass intact. Water closes around it. Four adults catch its weight.

Afterward, the power follows the appearance to its other side.

There is work to do there too.

It does not seal every crossing. The object in the small wall hollow is handled too often, and with too much care, for it to assume that nothing beyond the basin could be wanted.

But it makes places near the enclosures where an approaching boundary can be sensed early and examined without being entered. And when something living is caught, it goes to it.

***

Over time, the inhabitants learn that help can be requested.

The request is not a posture of submission. It begins as a particular interruption in the cups: three fingers resting against the bottoms, then one lifted while the others remain.

Different enclosures develop different versions.

Sometimes the request concerns a failing wall. Sometimes it concerns a young one whose first stones will not stay in its belly. Sometimes an individual sits beside the cups and asks for something the power cannot infer from the movements offered.

Then it comes closer, carefully, and they attempt to understand one another.

It does not repair a scarred membrane merely because it can. It first offers the sensation of an uninjured fold against a fingertip, and waits.

Some accept.

One old individual repeatedly moves the offered sensation away and takes its companion’s hand instead.

The power leaves the membrane as it is. Later, when the same individual asks for relief from the pain in its feet, that is given.

***

The basin remains wet.

Reed beds travel against currents. Food must still be found. The cups still provoke arguments, and the oldest reader at the first altered enclosure becomes almost impossible to satisfy.

But a traveler no longer needs to spend its last strength clearing a channel before it can rest. Households begin to make journeys that include days without feeding or flight from danger.

They visit one another.

Clay patterns arrive with their finest lines intact.

At one shelter, the young invent a game involving a quiet recess, the old noisy floor, and a pulse that must cross between them without waking a sleeper. The sleeper is usually awake and participating without admitting it.

The power is asked to join.

At first it wins every time.

The young lose interest.

It considers this, then allows itself only the pressures available through two small places on the wall.

Now it is frequently deceived.

A young one sends a pulse toward it, lifts both feet, and falls against another with a loose clatter of belly gravel. Around the chamber, throat folds open and flutter.

Beyond the threshold, the wet world continues its enormous passage.

Inside, the power waits for its turn.
