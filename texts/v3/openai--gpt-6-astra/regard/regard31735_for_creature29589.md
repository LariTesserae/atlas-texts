The power’s first act was to remain through the sunny hour.

It watched the lobe withdraw into the screw. It stayed through the return of shade, through a change in the rougher note behind the wall, through the long extension of a mouth beneath the curb. It followed the water back through the machinery and farther, into places neither the creature nor anyone tending the pump had seen.

There were things in the water that sustained the creature, and things it merely endured. The power learned the difference.

It did not put a hand into the pool. A hand would have displaced most of a basin.

For some time its attention made no visible difference at all.

Beneath the oldest shelf, however, a passage was narrowing. The body inside had already withdrawn from three chambers into a fourth. At the remaining opening, a ring of folds worked against the new deposit. Each contraction cleared a little less than the water laid down.

The power waited for the next contraction.

Then a grain of mineral detached.

The creature worked again. Another grain came away. Water entered the chamber from a slightly different angle, and the folded portion began, slowly, to move toward it.

By morning the chamber was empty. This time there was living tissue outside it.

The power did not open the chambers that had long been sealed. It knew which were empty and which held remains. Breaking them would have done nothing for what had lived there.

At the entrance, the small portion beneath the bracket received a little warm water.

Not a flood. The first thread reached only one side of the hollow, leaving the other side as it had been. The folded speck stayed closed for a long while. Then its uneven rim opened.

Over the following days it spread toward the warmth. The power let the thread become steadier.

The condensation still fell. Its drops struck the opposite edge of the hollow now, where the impact could not tear the body loose. The bracket remained an iron bracket, cold underneath, rusting at its bolts.

The noise took longer.

The power could have stopped the pump, but water from the pumping house went elsewhere too. It passed through kitchens and washrooms. People who knew nothing of the terraces relied on its arrival. And the creature had grown with the vibration in every attachment it made.

Instead, the loose fitting seated itself a little better.

The thin metallic sound diminished so gradually that a person visiting twice would have thought they had remembered it incorrectly. The shuddering beat weakened. Beneath the curb, the fringe spent less time gathered into bundles.

The low note remained.

Later, when the pump required maintenance, the worker found that an old bypass valve turned freely. Warm water continued to reach the enclosure while the machinery was still. The change came slowly enough that the creature loosened without falling.

In the quiet, its filaments assumed arrangements the power had not seen before. Some spread across the undersides of the shelves. Others curled back toward their own mouths. The power watched these too.

It did not conclude that every loosened fold was relief, or that every contraction was distress. It had time to learn more than that.

Before the next heavy rain, a narrow ledge formed high on the wall. It looked like another failed repair. Most of the cold runoff struck it and passed toward the entrance, away from the four occupied basins.

Rain still reached the court. The three shoots received it. The green patch widened a little along its cooler seam. In a storm, the pools still rose, but they no longer became one cold sheet quite so quickly.

Under the curb, there was time to withdraw.

There was also, now, a shallow route into a recess beside the main pool. Warm water moved through it at several depths. Some of its stone gathered mineral quickly; some remained bare. The power had made room for differences it did not yet fully understand.

For weeks the creature did not enter.

The recess remained available.

Then a filament fastened beneath its outer lip. A cloudy thickening followed. Nothing was lifted, carried, or persuaded across.

Behind the wall, a maintenance plan changed. The seep would no longer be treated with the compound that would have cleared the mineral and killed what lay beneath it. A small return channel could recover the escaping water farther downstream. It was cheaper to fit, and easier to inspect.

The worker who measured for it crouched at the gap.

For the first time, someone watched long enough to see the fringe move against the current.

They did not know what it was. In the space on the form marked *Other observations*, they wrote that there appeared to be something living in the deposits. The power let that plain sentence travel through the ordinary arrangements by which people sometimes protect what they have noticed.

It made no promise that the place would remain forever.

The pump would wear out. The water would change. Even without either event, the terraces would eventually fill the little court. The power attended to those futures as they approached, without requiring the creature to understand them.

For now, beneath the waterfall, the mouths opened into water they could use.

Stone continued to gather behind them. Ahead, beneath a lip still thin enough to admit the light, there was a passage with room for the body to pass.
