The power did not bring light.

Light would have disclosed the country to someone whose eyes required it. It would not, for that reason, have disclosed anything to the country.

Nor did the power place a hand upon the pale thickening beside the pool.

It attended first to the places where an arrival would have been felt, and remained outside them.

***

For a long time, it learned the difference between an exchange and an offer.

An exchange could continue after either participant had lost the means to interrupt it. An offer left some work to be done on the receiving side: a pore to be opened, a compound to be altered before passage, a small electrical difference to be maintained.

There were offers that resembled injuries until their histories were understood. There were exchanges so generous in quantity that they concealed the slow disappearance of the recipient’s independence.

There were also dependencies that had enlarged both lives.

The power did not mistake separateness for health. It did not mistake joining for peace.

Beneath the ridge, it followed a substance through seventeen transformations. At the end of its passage, very little of the original material remained together. Yet the manner of its arrival mattered. The inhabitant accepted it differently than it accepted the same substance made nearby.

The power followed that difference too.

Much of what it learned could not have been put into a sentence without leaving out the part that mattered.

***

The inhabitant had a difficulty.

It was not the long disagreement beneath the pool, although the disagreement made it more expensive.

One of the deep columns supplying this breadth of country had begun to change. Far below the hard, ringing fibers, a chemical boundary was moving away from the living tissue that used it. The movement was slow. The column had followed it for a long time by replacing one side of itself more quickly than the other.

Now it had reached a layer it could not readily enter.

Nothing at the surface had withered. The loss appeared first as a slightly greater interval between certain provisions. Reservoirs absorbed the difference. Neighboring lives adjusted their contributions.

Beneath the ridge, the inhabitant began reclaiming material from a second supply route.

That route had scarcely been used for an immense span of time. It was one of the country’s old precautions. Maintaining it had always cost something.

Closing it would make the present easier.

Once closed, it could not be remade quickly enough to serve the purpose for which it had been kept.

The power watched the allocation change. It could distinguish the pressure of shortage from the inhabitant’s ordinary tendency to retain some things and relinquish others. It could see that, given the supply it had received before, the old route would have remained.

This was enough to begin with.

***

The power might have held every membrane at its proper difference forever.

It might have made the deep source inexhaustible, prevented every failure, and kept the country as fresh as it already seemed.

But the inhabitants lived partly in their adjustments. To settle every balance from outside would have been to occupy the work by which they continued to be themselves.

So the power went below the reach of the roots.

Beside the failing column, it prepared a narrow passage through the resistant layer. It did not extend the column into it. It did not soften the whole depth or send a surge upward.

The passage contained conditions the living tip had encountered before: warmth within its tolerances, familiar minerals, a gradient it could use. Nothing in it demanded a new organ or a new dependence on a substance obtainable only as a gift.

At its entrance, the power left the old rock intact except for a seam no wider than one of the column’s finest probing tissues.

Then it waited.

The first tissue to enter belonged mostly to another inhabitant.

The power allowed this.

The tissue withdrew.

The power allowed that too.

Later, several fine extensions entered together, separated by wet walls. They did not advance at equal rates. One stopped. Another branched into a shallow pocket and remained there. A third reached the active depth.

Only then did the provision begin to change.

It came upward through the country’s own filters, acquired the conditions of its passage, and reached the ridge without any mark declaring who had made it possible.

***

The old supply route beneath the ridge did not close.

For a while, nothing else happened.

The additional provision went into reserves and into the maintenance of membranes that had been doing slightly too much work. The inhabitant continued its disagreement with its neighbor. It retained an expensive filter against the substance that no longer came.

The power did not remove the filter.

It had relieved a shortage, not acquired authority over what the relief should be used for.

Elsewhere, one recipient reduced its contact with the renewed flow. Its internal balances favored the older supply, with its particular mixture of deficiencies. Another widened its passages until it was accepting almost all that its boundaries could safely admit.

The deep seam accommodated both.

There was no reward for opening farther.

***

The power remained attentive to the consequences.

Abundance could move a disagreement as surely as deprivation. A stronger supply to one tract could draw essential material away from another. The new passage might become crowded; the restored column might begin work that the surrounding filters were not ready to receive.

Where such effects arose from what it had done, the power worked beneath them. It opened room for another root rather than compelling the first to share. It slowed the exposure of a mineral layer rather than making the tissues tolerate what would harm them.

Its care did not end when the gift entered the ground.

But neither did it spread into the correction of everything unusual.

The inhabitant beneath the ridge retained more of one compound than the power could find an immediate use for. It maintained an awkward boundary. In one direction it continued toward division, even though the restored supply would have made remaining whole possible.

The power made room for that outcome too.

***

There was no moment when the inhabitant recognized a benefactor.

What reached it was a sequence of conditions: an interruption that did not deepen, a familiar provision returning by a partly unfamiliar route, enough surplus to preserve a long-maintained alternative.

These entered its history.

Some membranes became less ready to close. Others kept their readiness. Across the ridge, the change traveled unevenly, meeting older events that were still active in the tissue.

The inhabitant did not become a different kind of being in order to receive help.

It remained difficult to count.

It remained almost divided in one direction and deeply joined in another.

Beside the pool, its pale exposed fold continued to replace itself. The crease persisted.

***

Above it, the bead under the great leaf grew heavier.

The power knew where it would fall. It knew the small pressure that would spread through the pool, the few pores that would answer, the minute expenditure by which the surface would return to balance.

It left the drop alone.

There was a tiny separation overhead.

Then, for a moment, the silence had a location.

Below the pool, along the old supply route, living walls maintained an open passage. Nothing presently needed to travel through it.

It was still there.
