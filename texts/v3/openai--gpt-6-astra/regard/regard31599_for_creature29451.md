It arrives without putting its weight on the ground.

For a while this is the only kindness it knows how to offer.

Below it, the water is travelling through the new connection. The two young builders lie with their hands near one another. Farther down, an adult turns a damp layer in its sleep. A roof is drying unevenly. One corner has begun to bear upon a shoulder.

The power can feel the whole thickness of the country. Understanding it takes longer.

It follows water through a thousand places where water is also a wall, a road, a store of food, or the means by which one body can recognize another. It watches a hand remain against a ceiling until the clay has stiffened. It watches the owner of that hand miss the cool hours in which it might have fed.

There are many such hands.

In a bank some distance from the new channel, six inhabitants are trying to reach a seventh.

The fall happened before sunset. A broad plate tipped rather than broke, pinning the creature along one side. Below the plate is a bed of sand that has begun to take moisture from its skin. Its free hands still move. Each time its neighbors press a question through the earth, it answers, although the answer is becoming smaller.

The rescuers have water enough to soften one edge of the fall. They do not have enough to keep that edge soft while excavating beneath it.

The power could lift the bank.

It examines what rests upon what. There are occupied passages above the fall, a nursery farther along, a channel whose floor is also the roof of the chamber. A sudden release of pressure would travel through them all.

Instead, it takes the weight of the tipped plate.

Nothing rises. A grain poised on an edge remains there.

The trapped creature draws a little water into the chamber beneath its shoulder. For the first time since sunset, the chamber is able to fill.

Its neighbors pause.

The power holds the plate through the remainder of the night. When their moisture begins to fail, it gathers vapor from the air above the bank and lets it condense against the buried clay. The change is slow enough for their hands to follow. They widen the passage, move the sand, and slide the injured creature into the space they have prepared.

Only when everyone is clear does the power let the plate settle.

The sound is small.

By then, several inhabitants have placed their palms against the place where the weight disappeared.

***

Learning to answer them requires restraint.

A pressure broad enough to be unmistakable can resemble a roof preparing to fall. A rapid sequence can be felt as cracking. The power’s earliest attempts bring adults out of adjoining chambers with their bodies already spread to brace the ceiling.

It stops.

Eventually it finds a place where a firm floor extends into an unused seam. There it presses with the weight of a single hand.

It waits.

An inhabitant answers from some distance away.

The power repeats the answer exactly. This proves unhelpful. The creature comes closer, presses again, then goes around to investigate the underside of the floor.

For several nights they conduct an awkward exchange.

Others join. Some leave after a few attempts. One old individual, whose front hand has healed into a permanent curve, stays longer than the rest. It is accustomed to being misunderstood. It changes the position of its palm, separates sequences into smaller parts, and allows whole cooling intervals between them.

The power learns where the pauses belong.

Its first useful message concerns the supported roof.

Its second asks where another support is needed.

***

Requests reach it unevenly.

The nearest chambers ask first. Distant inhabitants continue spending water on rescues without knowing that help is possible. Messages stall at dry seams or arrive after the condition they describe has changed.

The power does not assume that silence means there is nothing to do.

It travels beneath the inhabited layers, repeating the few signals it knows. At intervals it makes a small, steady pressure against firm ground: the same contact, in the same place, through day and night.

These become places where an answer can be found.

Some remain untouched for a long time.

At one, the first request is to stop pressing. The adjoining film feeds poorly under compression.

The power withdraws and establishes contact farther away.

At another, an inhabitant brings a young one whose hands have stiffened after an excursion onto the noon surface. The power holds a little dampness around the joints while the parent turns and loosens them. By dawn, three fingers can close. The others take longer.

At a third, there is no injury. Several builders want to know what lies beneath a layer they have never been able to cross.

The power lets them feel it: cool sand, a thickness of hard earth, and water below.

Their questions continue until morning.

***

Water is the largest difficulty, but “more water” is not yet a plan.

The power watches a shower erase a young builder’s work. It sees a wet ceiling grow heavy enough to bow. It follows a flood through passages that were safe an hour before, while their inhabitants carry their young upward.

The dry places also have occupants. Their food grows differently. Their floors bear differently. A bank cannot simply be made damp throughout.

So the first lasting work is small.

Beneath a heavily used passage, the inhabitants shape a receiving chamber. They choose its position. They make the floor and leave an outlet narrow enough to close with one body.

The power brings water up from below, a little at a time.

For several nights they alter the chamber. One wall softens. Sand is added. The outlet is moved. An elderly builder insists upon a second overflow, and the others eventually make it.

When they are ready, the power establishes a slow seep.

It continues through the following noon.

No one has to lie beneath it to keep it open.

There is enough water for the adjoining chambers, then a little more. The inhabitants lead the excess toward a feeding surface. Growth thickens there. They graze it and leave their customary strips.

Other receiving chambers follow, where they are wanted. Some are intended for ordinary use. Others stay nearly full behind closed lips, reserved for a fall or a long interval without rain.

The power learns the pressure that means enough.

***

The country does not become effortless.

Bricks still need joining. A channel made badly will still empty into the wrong basin. The young continue to lie beside their puddles, attending to losses too small to see.

But a leaking puddle no longer has to cost its maker the water held inside its body.

An adult can leave a doubtful roof in the power’s keeping and return after feeding. During the hottest hours, the injured have somewhere cool to rest. Those who want to investigate the deeper layers can do so without making every journey a wager against the next rain.

Not every spared night is spent building.

Sometimes several inhabitants lie together beneath an old floor after their work is done. Their palms move against one another. The pressure does not travel outward through the bank.

The power keeps its attention elsewhere.

***

Near the first channel, one of the young builders begins another outlet.

It is more ambitious than the first. The proposed course crosses a pale rise before descending toward a distant hollow. For three nights, the youngster lays material against the slope. On the fourth, a test release spreads sideways and disappears into a seam.

The power is close enough to correct the grade.

It waits.

The youngster goes below, finds where the water entered, and remains there for a long time. By the next evening, the design has changed. The seam is to become part of the channel. There will be a covered stretch.

A message comes through the floor.

The youngster needs something to hold the roof while it joins the far edge.

The power takes the weight.

Underneath, six small fingers work the clay into place.
