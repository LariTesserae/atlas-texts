The power does not answer through the root.

Someone is already speaking there.

It waits while the returning member of the household comes through the leaves, carrying a folded bundle against its breast. The bundle moves. There has been an argument somewhere about whether this particular animal belongs in a sleeping chamber, and the argument resumes before the traveler has brought all four feet inside.

The injured one releases your wrist.

For a little while, no one is attending to the power at all.

This pleases it.

***

It could learn the upland in an instant, if learning meant knowing where everything was.

It knows the buried stone, the weight of the interior basins, the hollow places in which water stands above the roofs of occupied rooms. It knows which fibers will part tonight. It can distinguish the pulse of every small body riding on a grazer’s back.

But when the injured one pauses at the doorway, touches a particular loop of root, and then enters, the power does not yet know whether this is affection, habit, or a way of testing the wall.

It stays.

It takes a shape that can enter without widening the doorway. It has weight enough to be felt through the floor, and hands whose approach can be refused. Its coat is ordinary. There is a small irregularity in the hair above one eye.

The household does not immediately trust it.

For several days it sleeps outside, in a hollow with poor drainage. Someone brings it a leaf of food. Someone else takes away the leaf before it has finished, having decided the leaf itself is too useful to lend.

It learns which sounds are invitations.

It learns that the root beside the doorway was trained by a dead companion. The injured one tests it because it is holding up part of the roof, and touches it afterward for a different reason.

***

The first thing the power changes is far beneath them.

A chamber is failing there.

It belongs to a household the people at the rim have never met. The basin above it has been filling for longer than any of its occupants has lived. Its supporting fibers have softened without making the ticking sound. When the floor begins to tilt, two adults are asleep and a third is washing a young one’s feet.

The power takes the weight.

There is no upward jerk, no sudden restoration of level ground. The pool beside the sleeping rim merely stops advancing.

The waking adult lifts the young one. Its wrists press down. It listens.

Then the power lets a loose strip of bark fall from the doorway.

Behind it is a narrow passage, old and nearly closed. The roots that obstruct it ease apart. Cool air enters the chamber, carrying the smell of a dry space higher up.

The adult wakes the others.

They carry the young one through first. They return for food, for a map, for a seed case with something inside that clicks. One of them goes back a final time and stands beside a plant growing from the wall.

The power holds the chamber.

The adult works a small offshoot loose, wraps its base in wet fiber, and leaves.

Only when they have reached sound footing does the room settle into water.

The power does not preserve the empty chamber forever. Its walls are already becoming something else.

But farther along the hidden passage, where the refugees will eventually stop, it keeps a roof from closing before they arrive.

***

There are other such places.

The power attends to them without requiring anyone to know its name.

A breaking branch pauses long enough for the animal beneath it to move. Water entering a sleeping hollow finds an older channel. Where a grazer has sunk too deeply to lift its chest, the submerged roots rise by the width of a hand.

Sometimes this is enough.

Sometimes an animal is already dying. The power does not make it endure until morning merely because morning would be more convenient for those who love it. It eases the difficult breath. It leaves time for another body to come close.

It finds that there is much to do which will never become a story.

***

At the rim, the injured one begins leaving room for it along the sleeping ledge.

This is not yet affection. The stranger is warm, and the chamber has developed a draft.

The power learns to mend a water bladder. Its first repair is excellent and entirely wrong: the bladder no longer leaks, and the branch it was intended to train remains bent too far downward.

The injured one takes the bladder away, pierces it with a nail, and makes the power watch.

Water gathers at the puncture.

A drop falls.

After a long interval, another.

The injured one points to the branch.

The power watches until it understands what was wanted.

***

It does not remove the scars while the injured one sleeps.

One afternoon, when the others have gone to gather food, it sits beside the root where the rear foot rests most comfortably. It touches its own hip, then opens a fine hand.

The injured one looks away.

The power lowers its hand.

Several days pass.

Then, after a journey that has left the injured one trembling through the flanks, a hand catches the power’s wrist. It is guided to the pale patch beneath the coat.

The power lets the creature feel what it proposes: first the easing of pain, no more than that. The muscles release a little. The rear foot settles more broadly against the wood.

The creature draws the hand away.

They sit together.

Later it brings the hand back.

The healing proceeds in intervals. Between them the injured one walks, rests, tests its grip beneath a low branch. A joint that has long moved crookedly begins to find another course. Old compensations ache and are eased in their turn.

Some of the scar remains. The creature does not ask about it.

The first time it crosses a gap without arranging its bad side toward the stronger root, it stops on the other bank.

It looks back for a long time.

***

Word travels.

Not cleanly, and not always correctly.

Visitors arrive with pain, with sick young, with an ancestor’s stone they want made larger. One asks the power to cause an inherited vine to die without anyone knowing why.

The power will not do that.

It does, however, accompany the petitioner home. The vine has darkened half the chamber. Three people want it removed and two cannot bear to lose it. The power listens through an afternoon of breath, wrist pressure, and repeated departures.

By the end, nothing has been settled.

It returns the following day.

***

There is no morning on which the upland becomes safe.

The people still test the wood. They still quarrel over roofs. They still take meat, lose paths, cherish things that another member of the household would gladly throw away.

But certain absences become less common.

A traveler overdue for a season arrives carrying news of a passage that opened where no passage had been. A young one survives a fever and becomes an adult with an objectionable habit of tapping during meals. An old keeper of maps lives long enough to teach a route nobody else remembers.

The loose strands remain on the frames.

Some acquire new knots.

***

Much later, beside the path at the rim, the grazer leans against the power while it removes a parasite from beneath the flowers.

The healed one comes out of the enclosure and makes the sharp sound that means the threshold is beginning to slope.

The power pushes against the grazer’s face.

The grazer pushes back.

Inside, someone is laughing with small, nearly soundless breaths.

Then a movement passes through the root beneath them.

The healed one lowers a wrist. The power does the same.

Two uneven contacts. A pause. The quick disturbance of a foot finding its hold.

They wait together for whoever is coming home.
