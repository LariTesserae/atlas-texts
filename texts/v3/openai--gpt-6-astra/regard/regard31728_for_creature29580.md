The power first notices that the bridges are waiting.

It had been considering a fourth bridge: something broad and perfectly stable, by which the small unevenness could enter the settlement without risk of losing itself.

It does not build this.

Along the fresh ridge, the unevenness approaches the second bridge again. For a moment it extends farther than it can sustain. A little of it travels down the slope with the mineral dust.

An inhabitant waiting nearby makes a slight change in pressure.

The lost part slows. The rest reaches it.

The power watches until the small arrangement has gathered itself again. Then it turns its attention to the weight of the stairs.

***

There are things outside the cupboard that the inhabitants cannot know soon enough to answer.

A nail has been rusting inside the lowest landing. A pipe behind the earth-filled wall will eventually split. Someone upstairs has noticed the swollen door and intends, when there is time, to lever it open.

The power does not make the door impossible to open. There may someday be a reason to open it.

Instead, it gives the cupboard a little independence.

This takes place beneath the white basin, deeper than the foundations presently reach. A failure in the old building will no longer have to become a failure here. The difference is folded into the support of the room so carefully that the next deep note sounds at its accustomed pitch.

One loose fragment, caught between routes, settles.

Above, the rusted nail continues to rust. It no longer bears quite so much weight.

***

To approach the inhabitants, the power must become less capable in a particular place.

A difference too well maintained would be an obstruction. A boundary that could not be altered would offer no way to speak back.

It chooses an unused aperture near the meeting floor and sustains a small reluctance in the passage of warmth.

The reluctance has an edge.

The edge can be moved.

For a while nobody moves it. Traffic proceeds through the neighbouring streets. A cleaning mechanism passes below the aperture and removes a curl of newly formed mineral.

Then someone presses against the offered boundary.

The power yields.

They press again, differently.

It takes a long time to exchange anything beyond this.

***

Its first offer is misunderstood.

The power makes available a condition in which an inhabitant could remain gathered without effort. It means rest.

The one receiving the arrangement releases it almost immediately.

Something essential has been omitted: the little departures, the differences between one moment and the next, the work by which a person can change their mind. The offered rest resembles an occupied chamber whose corners have begun to sharpen.

The power withdraws the condition.

It does not offer it more persuasively.

Later, with help, it offers something smaller. A support that answers an inhabitant’s adjustments instead of completing them. It can be leaned against. It can also be pushed away.

This arrangement is retained long enough for a second inhabitant to examine it.

***

There is a gathering near the six windows.

For several hours the adjoining construction stops. The power keeps its presence at the aperture, no larger than before. Not everyone approaches. One resident comes close, departs, and returns with a complicated arrangement that the power cannot receive without loosening what it has already understood.

It loosens it.

Outside the plain meeting floor, a gutter overfills. Someone leaves to attend to it. Two others disagree about whether to continue in their absence.

Nothing is settled quickly.

By the end, several small trials have been arranged. None requires the settlement to keep the power.

***

The old inhabitant beside the spillway is offered a second stud between the ledge and the first junction.

They pass it twice without using it.

On the third journey, a remnant of them remains near the partition. The rest pauses at the new stud.

Unlike the older fittings, this one can hold a place without holding its occupant to a fixed arrangement. The delayed part finds a route toward something that is still changing, still recognisably waiting.

The pause is shorter.

The next day it is not.

After several visits, the old inhabitant alters the fitting. They make its reach narrower and its response slower.

The power preserves the alteration.

Nobody moves the ledge nearer the heat exchanger. Nobody reduces the spillway’s roar.

***

Beneath the nursery, the trials are more difficult.

The deep note cannot simply be silenced. It accompanies work that must occur, and its warning is necessary. Nor can the chamber be held entirely apart from the changing foundations. The young must eventually learn the settlement they live in.

An additional course of shallow recesses is grown under the existing ones.

During the next sounding, these keep one local distance reliable: the span between the attendant and the chamber’s farthest corner.

Everything else changes as before.

One of the young stretches toward a knocking gate. The attendant answers. The young one returns by a route that has remained the length it was when they left.

Afterward, the attendant does not resume work immediately.

For the first time, there is another inhabitant available to take the next watch. The new supports have released them from a task elsewhere.

They wait outside until admitted.

***

The power had thought the crowded floor needed more space.

It learns that some of it does, and some of it needs to remain close.

A chamber placed farther from its neighbours is returned. An enlarged street is narrowed again. Near the heat exchanger, however, a small area of pale floor is left unbuilt.

The resident who tends the overgrown blade examines it.

There is no new planting slot. No nutrient supply has been installed. Nothing indicates what the floor is for.

They bring the root-cleaning comb across it, finding that there is room to turn the tool without withdrawing from the blade.

Later, someone else uses the same space to wait.

The area remains empty through several weather fronts. Then a low fitting appears at one edge, made by the inhabitants. The power does not recognise its purpose.

***

What it leaves beneath the basin is not an engine.

It is a reserve, accessible through several different routes, with no single valve by which one resident could close it against the others. It can sustain the trials that have been accepted. It can also supply warmth, hold a damaged passage continuous, or give the settlement time to answer a failure.

It does not decide when these things are needed.

There are limits to what it can provide at once. The limits are made apparent before anything depends on them.

At the aperture near the meeting floor, the power leaves a way to be called. Its small boundary can be closed from inside.

Eventually, someone closes it.

***

Beside the spillway, the ledge is lowered before the old inhabitant arrives.

White dust rises, pauses, and continues.

Below the third tread, a young inhabitant has learned to remain almost entirely themself when a gate knocks. They repeat the accomplishment even when no gate has knocked.

The green blade grows a little farther. Construction pauses again.

And along the fresh ridge, the unevenness reaches the second bridge.

It rests there through a change in the current. Then it withdraws, travels a short distance along the mineral crest, and begins making its own way toward the first.

The bridges remain where they are.
