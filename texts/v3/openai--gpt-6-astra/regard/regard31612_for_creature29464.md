The power first attended to the creak.

It could distinguish the settling of the branches from the beginning of a break. It could feel the fibres beneath the person’s hip, swollen with moisture, and the place where one had parted. It did not hold the platform motionless. The small give of it was part of how the person slept.

But it stayed near the weakened binding until morning.

When the person woke and reached beneath themselves, their thumb found the loose strand.

“I thought so,” they said.

They ate a little meal softened in the cup. Then they began the awkward business of examining something on which they were sitting.

“May I hold that?” the power asked.

The person stopped moving.

The voice had come from the step. It was neither deep nor especially gentle. In that place, either would have made it harder to understand.

“What?”

“The branch beneath your left leg.”

The person put a hand against the column beside them.

“Someone’s there.”

“Yes.”

They listened. There had been no foot in the water, no hand following the cord.

“Did you come past me?”

“No.”

This required some time.

The power allowed it.

At last the person shifted their weight.

“Hold it, then.”

The branch remained supported while they unwound the binding. Beneath it, in the narrow joint, something moved.

“Not higher,” the person said.

The power held it where it was.

Together they waited for the small animal to release its feet and pass into the adjoining crevice.

***

The power had considered many things.

It could have opened the mountain to daylight. It could have drawn a stream through the basins, made the pale stems bear fruit, widened the passage until the person could enter upright. It could have given the stone a permanence that would have defeated every winter above it.

But the water was not foul. The darkness was not an injury. The person’s bent posture in the passage was a movement they knew, not a plea they had made.

Other things were less clear.

The numb hand troubled them. So did the journey down when ice lay beneath new snow. And there were evenings when they wished someone would come, though they would not have wanted someone there every evening.

The power did not treat these as a single problem.

While the person worked, it asked about the binding.

“Have you enough fibre?”

“For this end.”

“And the other?”

“It will wait.”

“How long?”

The person pulled at it, testing.

“Not as long as I’d like.”

“Would you like some more?”

Their hands became still again.

“What sort?”

The power laid a length across their palm. It was supple, rough enough to grip, without a smell.

“Will it swell?”

“A little.”

“Will it rot?”

“Not in this water.”

The person felt along it.

“Can I cut it?”

“Yes.”

“With my knife?”

“Yes.”

“Leave some, then.”

***

There were things the person declined.

They did not want the cup made unbreakable.

They did not want the knots made distinct again. When the power asked whether the softened bark troubled them, they said that sometimes it did, but they wanted to think about it.

They did want help with the slab.

“I keep forgetting the edge until I’m under there.”

“Shall I smooth it?”

“Only underneath. Leave the top alone.”

The power followed the fracture with an attention finer than a fingertip. It took away the edge that cut and left the slab seated as it had been. The person checked it afterwards, reaching farther beneath it than they usually dared.

Then they checked it with their weight.

“That’s better.”

***

The power did not make the hollow sufficient for every need.

When the food ran low, the person still went down the mountain. But before they left, the power asked to accompany them, and was permitted.

On the descent it learned which pauses were for breath and which were for looking. It did not urge them onward at either kind. Once, where a sheet of ice lay hidden under loose snow, it told them what was beneath their next foot.

The person drew the foot back.

“You might do that again,” they said.

So it did.

Below, there were people to greet and things to arrange. The power waited while the person argued over the quality of some meal. It discovered that their voice could be quite loud.

***

It made no promise that nothing would change.

The person would grow older. The platform would need new branches. Someone remembered by a pause in the passage might cease to come.

The power could help with these things, but not all in the same way, and not before knowing what help was wanted.

Before withdrawing, it made certain the person could call it.

“From outside as well?”

“Yes.”

“From below?”

“Yes.”

The person wound the spare fibre loosely and put it beside the cup.

They had one further question.

“If I leave this place, must I tell you?”

“No.”

For a while they sat without speaking. Then the person lowered a foot into the basin and moved over on the repaired platform.

There was not much room.

The power took the place offered.
