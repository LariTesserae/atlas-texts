The power did not step onto the bridge.

A step would have brought weight, warmth, the disturbance of air. These were not forbidden things. But they were ways of announcing a body before knowing where another body lay.

Instead, it attended to the dry interval beneath the rail.

It learned very little from that interval alone.

To understand why water did not gather there, it had to understand the drain, and the drain required the ducts, and the ducts required a distribution of pressure extending across the fracture. Some of that pressure belonged to the inhabitant. Some belonged to its neighbors. Some sustained small lives in joints of the bridge that had never distinguished anything beyond the passage of cold water over metal.

The power took care not to simplify them into one being merely because they were difficult to separate.

Beneath the bridge, a little life held a bead of water against the underside of a bolt. The great contact across the gulf passed through its circumstances without entering its awareness.

It was not less real for being small.

***

At first there appeared to be much to repair.

The bare country. The empty streets. The enormous expenditure required to keep an edge sharp. The connections that approached the roots and never reached them.

The power could have laid soil over the slabs. It could have lowered the cliffs into valleys, given the water a bottom, filled the waiting rooms with travelers. It could have completed every bridge.

It examined what these gifts would touch.

Soil would insulate some of the exchanges through which the inhabitants felt. Sediment would alter the springs. A quieter generator might take away the distinction by which one being knew another. Even a flower, opening where nothing opened, would not be merely a flower. Its roots would draw upon a place already inhabited.

None of this made change wrong.

It made the word *empty* unreliable.

At the bridge, there was no missing span to mend.

***

The power listened for distress.

It did not treat opposition as distress, or solitude, or an attention without conclusion. Nor did it assume that, because a condition endured, it was therefore welcome.

This required a finer listening than the machinery did.

There were tensions held because the holding was part of a life’s delight. There were tensions held because nothing in the country permitted them to be released. From outside, their dry places could be identical.

The power would not learn the difference by loosening them.

Instead, near the farthest recess touched by the bridge inhabitant, it made a possibility.

Not a new sound. The air was already full of sounds.

It arranged that one slight component of pressure could be otherwise, without requiring any other component to follow. The inhabitant could lean upon that difference or leave it alone. Neither response would reduce the water, disturb the neighboring lives, or begin a process it could not stop.

The arrangement did not ask for movement toward a future.

It was available within the attention already being held.

For a visitor at the rail, nothing changed.

Across the gap, behind the wet stone of the opposing cliff, the inhabitant brought the possibility into relation with itself.

***

What the power received was not gratitude.

It was a distinction.

The contact across the fracture contained something the inhabitant held closely: the other presence, the exactness of their meeting, the uncertainty that allowed each to remain other.

It also contained a strain that was not cherished.

A minor duct beneath the third freight platform contributed pressure needed to sustain the meeting. That same pressure passed through an inward chamber, where it obscured a delicate part of the inhabitant’s apprehension of itself.

The interference was old only in the language of visitors.

There was no remembered time before it. No hope had formed around its ending. Yet, given a harmless difference against which to discriminate it, the inhabitant did not hold it as it held the contact.

The power attended to that distinction until there was no mistaking it.

Then it supported the useful pressure by another means.

It did not silence the duct. Several lesser lives depended on its note. It did not change the chamber or make the inhabitant more transparent to itself than it wished to be.

It removed only the necessity that these two conditions bear upon each other.

The contact remained.

Within the tower, between three pipes entering at different heights, something could be distinguished without effort that had previously required effort.

The power did not ask that this become a turning point.

***

There were other possibilities it could offer.

It did not offer all of them at once.

An infinity of open doors can be another kind of pressure.

It left the one difference available, responsive to the inhabitant’s use of it, and withdrew everything that resembled a demand. There was no instruction to grow, no requirement to become curious, no promise that sequence would be better than depth.

If the inhabitant wanted something beyond the form of its present life, the power would help it discover what that meant. If it did not, the help already given would remain help.

There would be no debt hidden in the machinery.

***

The country continued through its weather.

Cloud crossed the fracture. Freight traveled beneath the streets. Far inland, the last leaf at the edge of a thicket remained the last leaf.

The power did not set a throne above the plateau or place a mark on the numbered entrance. It kept no injured part of the world unrepaired so that it would always be needed. Where its assistance could be made independent of its presence, it made it so.

At the bridge, it remained available without occupying the meeting.

There were now three presences there, though only two reached across.

The third did not put itself between them.

Below the rail, water gathered into beads, except along the narrow interval where none formed. The drain released its slender stream into miles of air.

Nothing visible had been completed.

Something difficult no longer had to be.
