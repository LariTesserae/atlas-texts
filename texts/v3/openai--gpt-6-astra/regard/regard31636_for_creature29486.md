The power first notices the hand beneath the old inhabitant’s foot.

It is still holding the animal when the others turn to leave. The animal has caught one hooked leg in a crease of the bearer’s skin. Pulling would free it, but would also remove the leg.

Everyone waits.

The power waits with them.

Eventually the bearer lowers its hand beside a paving knot. The animal catches hold of the darker tissue, releases the skin, and goes down into the seam.

Only then does the old inhabitant settle its weight.

The power has arrived with enough warmth to open every furled chamber in the hall. It keeps this warmth elsewhere.

Here, a sudden spring would be a kind of collapse.

***

For several cycles, nothing appears to change.

The eighth stair tread advances. The wheel hesitates. A child follows a bolt beetle as far as the cistern, where an adult quietly intercepts it.

The power attends to the differences between a pause and an exhaustion.

They are difficult to distinguish. The workshop rests while certain fibers harden. The pump rests because its fourth chamber cannot quite refill. An old inhabitant stands motionless because someone it likes is approaching from a distance. Another stands motionless because the paving has grown around a damaged foot.

The power learns these things separately.

It also discovers that the creature beneath the landing is not shivering. The tremor passes from its jaw through its folded body whenever it sleeps. This motion keeps its nest from sealing shut.

The creature is, however, hungry.

The landing has hardened faster than its teeth can work.

***

The first alteration is very small.

Beside the nest, a shaving loosens from a part of the landing that carries no weight. It remains attached at one end. The creature wakes, finds it, and spends a long time pulling.

Beneath the shaving is another soft edge.

The power does not soften the joint itself. It has watched what the amber tissue does.

After feeding, the creature turns its sealed eyes toward the stairwell. Its blunt face follows something that gives off neither light nor scent.

One limb reaches over the nest.

The power offers a little warmth, no more than might come from a hand held nearby.

The animal retreats.

The warmth withdraws.

Later, when the nest has cooled, the limb comes out again.

***

The inhabitants first become certain of a visitor at the rack where the distance dries.

A strip has curled over the tray’s edge. The attendant with the long interval above its wrist is trying to lift it clear without bringing its nearer hand across.

The power supports the loose end.

For a moment the strip rests on nothing.

The attendant stops. Its concealed hands close against its chest.

Very slowly, the power lowers the strip back to where it was.

The attendant looks beneath the tray, then toward the hall. It goes to fetch the inspector.

The inspector arrives and holds its worn fingertips above the strip. Then it holds them above the empty place beside the rack.

The power makes that place resist very slightly.

The inspector presses.

The resistance yields.

Several further tests follow. A bowl is set upon the empty place. A shaving is pushed through it. The inspector brings a living bolt beetle, but does not let go of the beetle.

At last it takes one end of the curled strip and leaves the other unsupported.

This time, when the power lifts it, the inspector continues working.

***

Requests begin as objects left in that place.

Some are clear enough. A split bowl. A valve clogged with dense, sweet tissue. A forked implement whose prongs have grown together.

Others are not.

The power nearly repairs a small stone before noticing how often its owner touches the crack.

After that, it waits for demonstrations.

The bowl’s owner holds the two edges together. The valve is brought with a cup of sap and a series of impatient gestures. The fork is placed beside a fallen gutter animal, which makes the matter urgent.

The stone is only being shown.

The power gives it back unchanged.

For a while, showing things occupies most of its attention. There are stones, pellets of wood, an unusually broad foot impression preserved in a tray. Someone brings a dead beetle held in a loop of fiber.

The household with the blue glass comes together.

They hold the glass where a face might have been, if the visitor had possessed a face.

The power lets the gray overhead light pass through it onto the paving.

A small blue mark appears.

The youngest inhabitant puts a foot on the mark, then lifts it to inspect the pads.

***

At the pump house, the attendants uncover the fourth chamber themselves.

Its membrane has thickened around an old injury. Each contraction empties it, but the inward fold opens too slowly afterward. The hurried revolution is the rest of the mechanism compensating.

There is a scar the size of a seed.

The power could replace the chamber. Instead it holds the fold open while the attendants examine it. One tastes the edge. Another goes to the workshop and returns with a thread of pale tissue.

Their repair takes most of the dark cycle.

During that time the power turns the wheel.

It follows the rhythm the attendants give it, including the pauses.

When the chamber begins working again, sap reaches a junction that has been cold for a long time. In a nearby communal pocket, several sleeping children loosen their tangled feet.

The attendants sit beside the wheel until its rhythm is familiar.

The empty place stays with them.

***

The inhabitant with the extended wrist brings no object.

It lays its arm on the rack.

The power attends to the ordinary flesh, the interval, the hand beyond. There is sensation throughout, though not all of it arrives together. Cold at the fingertips reaches the owner before pressure against the palm.

The inhabitant takes a length of cord and lays it beside the arm. With its other hand, it pinches a section of the cord into a loop.

The power shortens the interval by a very little.

The inhabitant snatches its arm back.

For two cycles it does not approach.

On the third, it returns with the cord. This time it makes a much smaller loop.

They work slowly. Between alterations, the inhabitant carries trays, sleeps, handles stones. Sometimes it asks for a little distance to be restored.

A narrow remnant remains above the wrist. The owner has learned to use it when reaching into the deeper part of the workshop slot.

The power leaves it there.

***

At the cistern, there is less it can safely learn by touching.

It watches the petals close. It follows the pressure of the upper water into the lit rooms beneath, where the tables remain level while their ceilings approach the floors.

The compression does not hurt the people below.

Something else does.

On a rain cycle, the inhabitant with the damaged panel kneels at the rim. Across the water, the worker rises from the table with the empty third chair.

Their hands lift.

The power feels the strain in the petals. This meeting uses water faster than a reflection alone.

It gathers one hanging drop from a root hair and holds it above the cistern.

Neither inhabitant looks up.

The power lowers the drop until it touches the surface without striking it.

The water deepens slightly.

Both sets of hands become still.

Then the worker below moves one hand away and places it flat against the underside of the surface.

The visitor above does the same.

The power adds another drop.

For a little longer, the rooms remain rooms.

It cannot discover what happened to the occupant of the small chair merely by attending to the vacancy. It does not make a figure to fill it.

When the upper inhabitant finally lowers its hands, the power lets the water resume its passage into the town.

This time, the inhabitant is standing before the last table disappears.

***

There are changes people decline.

The old one whose foot has been caught in the paving refuses to have the tissue cut. Several narrow fibers run from the sole into the street. Through them, the old one feels the loads approaching the new junction.

Instead, a shallow seat is grown beneath its body.

It tests the seat for a long time before allowing its weight to rest there.

The predator’s passage is not closed. Too many creatures travel beneath the slabs for the power to understand that consequence yet. But the single hard knock that sends it away from a dwelling becomes possible from the sleeping recesses as well as the door.

The children are still kept back from the cistern.

Beside it, the inhabitants make a smaller basin with a rough, climbable edge. Rain collects there first. Its shallow water reflects only the root, the gray opening, and whoever leans over it.

The children put things in.

Most of the things are taken out again.

***

The town continues making room.

With the fourth chamber repaired, the workshop produces more than the old inhabitants will permit to be laid. Finished distance gathers on the racks.

One cycle, the inspector carries a strip into the square and sets it before the visitor’s empty place.

The power lifts one end.

The inspector keeps hold of the other.

They bring it to the hall, where an old inhabitant opens a seam. Others arrive to help. The work proceeds with its usual interruptions: a bowl is fetched, a limb supported, a small animal persuaded to move.

When the seam closes, there is room beside the hall for something that has not been built.

The inhabitants leave the space unoccupied.

The power remains there when it is wanted. At other times, animals cross it, deliveries rest upon it, and the household with the blue glass sits at its edge during rain.

Beneath the landing, the creature has grown too large for its nest.

A second opening forms near the floor.

It sits between the two holes for several days, considering.

There is warmth within reach of either one.
