The power arrived without making anything look up.

It did not brighten the green light or press the vapor higher. Nothing ceased feeding. A snail continued across the soft seam beneath the animal’s eye, and the eye turned beneath it.

At first the power only listened.

The call passed through the swelling throat, out across the water, into other throats. It was not a plea simply because it was low. It was not contentment simply because it had been repeated a long time. The power followed it through the crowded basin without deciding, in advance, what an answer ought to be.

There were distinctions inside the roughness. The animal touching the caller’s flank received one part; a youngster suspended among roots received another. Some of what passed between them concerned the pressure of bodies, the taste beneath a mouth, the whereabouts of water. Some belonged to senses for which the power had no need of words.

It learned those too.

And it attended to the animal beside the island: to the root fitting the curve of its forelimb, to the six limbs and their different burdens, to the little lives sheltered in its folds. It felt the leaf held between the pleats of the mouth.

The animal was feeding. It was also waiting for the leaf to open farther.

Not urgently. There was nourishment at its lips. But each small contraction drew upon a surface that remained folded, and beneath the patience of the body there was an inclination toward something that had not happened.

The power stayed with that inclination.

It did not bring a dry season. It did not cut a river through the pale shelves, strip the trunks of their smaller inhabitants, or introduce hunger so that movement would become necessary. There was much here that flourished. It would not purchase a more familiar landscape with the lives of its inhabitants.

Instead, beneath the unopened leaf, it made room.

The alteration was smaller than a foot could find. Two crowded stems eased apart. A hanging root lengthened into an unused hollow. Water entered the new interval, carrying no creature away.

The leaf could open there.

For a while, it did not.

The animal kept its mouth against the tip. Its throat worked. One of the snails in the seam of its neck extended its horns and withdrew them.

Then the leaf loosened by the width of a vein.

The animal released it.

The power waited again.

Nothing compelled the next movement. The animal could lower its jaw to the shelf. It could turn toward the neighboring shoot. The power had made no promise that this particular leaf would prove worth its attention.

A forefoot rose from the bottom.

Under its sole, two young animals clung with their bellies flattened and their tails crossed. The foot travelled forward through the water and settled where the stems had parted. Neither youngster lost its hold.

The great head followed.

Inside the folded blade was a surface that had never been touched. The animal set its lips against it. Its eyes lowered a little into their sockets.

The taste was different.

That was all the change it wanted just then.

The power did not enlarge the opening.

Across the basin, it continued its attention. Where contact was shelter, it left contact intact. Where a body leaned because it preferred the weight of another body, it did not separate them. Where a creature sought to shift and could not, it learned what held it before it moved anything.

There were places where nothing needed doing.

There were others where the necessary kindness was so particular that it could scarcely be seen: a root diverted from a chamber’s opening; a little more depth beneath a heavy belly; a passage through which a small animal could leave and, if it wished, return.

The power kept watch over what these changes touched. A space was not empty merely because the large creatures could not enter it. A loosened stem had inhabitants. Even a film of water was somebody’s ground.

Beside the island, the leaf opened another fraction.

The animal fed until it had taken enough, then lifted its jaw. Clear secretion stretched between mouth and blade, thinned, and broke. The leaf remained tilted over the new interval, its translucent patch admitting a little of the dull light.

A swelling passed down one side of the animal’s neck.

This time the power understood enough to hear the difference.

The call still carried the taste of the place, the nearness of other bodies, the steady warmth. It also carried the position of the opened leaf.

Far off, a throat answered.

The power remained, listening for what happened next.
