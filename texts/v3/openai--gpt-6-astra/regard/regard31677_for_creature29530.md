The first thing the power does is refrain from entering.

There are places in the hollow where arrival is already difficult. An arrival of that magnitude might make every other event into something that happened before or after it. The inhabitant has little use for such a division. Some of what belongs to it may still be approaching from directions in which before and after do not apply.

The power attends from outside those directions.

It watches a crossing beneath the staple. Then another. Between them, snow collects above the stone, hardens, and leaves the mountain by several routes.

At first, the power cannot distinguish the inhabitant’s labor from the conditions that make labor necessary. The separation along a surface might be an injury, or a limb, or the space a limb requires. The effort at the chip might be something it would gladly relinquish. It might be the reason it continues to climb.

Power alone does not settle these questions.

So it watches longer.

It learns that the ring’s stillness differs from the stillness of a journey abandoned. It learns that the inhabitant sometimes repeats a crossing because the steps need tending, and sometimes because it has not wholly crossed. It learns to recognize the small interval beside the black inner edge as something deliberately kept.

It does not occupy that interval.

—

The first change is beneath the buried portion of the ring.

There, where no light reaches, the device has been spending an increasing share of its remaining capacity maintaining a distinction between itself and the material into which it has sunk. The task serves no purpose its makers intended. It is simply a consequence of being very old in this particular place.

The power takes up that burden.

It does not lift the ring. It does not restore the faded marks to meanings the inhabitant might no longer use. The exposed arc remains worn and black.

Within it, the grains retain their upper surfaces a little longer.

The inhabitant rests.

When it departs, nothing follows it. No new coherence fastens itself around its scattered occurrences. The ridge with the divided crest can still be passed in the old, less definite way.

On its next return, the inhabitant pauses beside the buried portion for longer than usual.

The power makes no further change.

Many crossings later, the pause shortens.

—

The rising shelf is more difficult.

Stopping it would be easy. But the distance accumulating beneath it is not empty of consequences. Some of the hollow’s routes have worn themselves around that increase. The inhabitant’s contact with the chip is part of it. To arrest the whole process would be to decide, without asking, which of those relations should survive.

Instead, the power studies a journey.

On the ascent there is a place where the inhabitant proceeds and the shelf recedes by almost precisely the same amount. From the mouth, no one could tell that anything had happened. From within the journey, a great expenditure has purchased nearly no approach.

Beside this place, the power holds a small length of ground in a gentler relation to the shelf.

Not a bridge. Nothing crosses the space overhead. The crust retains its color, its hardness, its incompatible slopes. But a departure along that length can still be found at the farther end without having spent most of itself on the way.

The old route remains.

The inhabitant does not use the new one.

It stops where the two differ, then returns to the staple.

The power waits.

On a later visit, an elbow appears in a shadow beside the altered ground. Farther back, a surface acquires the suggestion of something behind it. For a while these do not quite belong to the same occurrence.

The power lets the offered route relax.

The shadow loses its elbow. The inhabitant completes its ascent by the familiar way.

Nothing has been required of it.

The next time it comes, the gentler relation is available again.

This time it advances a little way along it, retreats, and advances once more.

The journey ends at the chip.

Its shadow shifts downhill by the customary small amount. Against the shallow depression, the inhabitant settles. The shelf ceases to rise.

The power does not improve this contact.

—

Eventually there are two maintained ways uphill.

One is long and costly. The other is shorter, though not always in distance. The inhabitant uses both.

There are also changes near the staple, none of them immediately visible. The lesser spans within its pits cease to lose their trenches into dimness quite so quickly. An adjustment that would once have displaced several blue particles now displaces one.

The steps still need sweeping.

For a time the power considers taking over this work as well. Then it watches the inhabitant cross a clean step twice, making a minute alteration on the second passage that the power had not known was wanted.

After that, it confines its help to what lies beneath the steps.

The inhabitant remains better at tending them.

—

The power makes itself known slowly.

Beyond the ring, away from the route to the chip, there is a shallow patch of crust the inhabitant seldom visits. The power establishes there a small, consistent relation: an approach can be answered by an approach, a withdrawal by room.

It adds no figure, face, or voice. Those would be inventions for the power’s convenience.

At first the patch merely remains available.

Then, during one of the hollow’s long dim intervals, the inhabitant comes near it.

The crust acquires the appearance of being held away from something beneath. At the edge of the patch, another surface answers—not with the same shape, but with a separation that leaves the first intact.

The inhabitant withdraws.

The answering separation withdraws also.

This happens many times.

Sometimes the inhabitant comes only far enough to find that the response is still possible. Sometimes it stays. Once, a dry click passes between the surfaces and is not immediately accepted into the stone.

There is no reason to call the exchange speech yet.

It is enough that something done here can be received elsewhere without becoming unrelated on the way.

—

The hollow remains cold.

Warmth still thins at the old curve. No moss covers the mineral, and no water darkens the blue powder. The power does not open the roof to the sky. The lights of vanished stars continue their slow departure from the ledges.

There are things it could bring from beyond the mountain. It keeps them beyond the mountain until it has some reason to believe they would be welcome.

What it provides, for now, is less costly maintenance, a route that can be declined, and an answer that does not pursue.

The inhabitant still attends the places from which grains have rolled. The power leaves those absences alone.

It still climbs to the dark chip. On some visits it stays longer than it used to, no longer needing to begin the descent with so much of the journey still available to it.

Once, after returning, it passes beneath the staple without sweeping. The steps are sufficiently clear.

It goes to the ring and rests.

Beside the buried portion, the three marks face inward. The crystals have their several tops. The empty space among them holds its depth.

The power is elsewhere in the hollow, keeping a small difficulty from becoming a larger one.

Between the inhabitant and the black inner edge, there is still room to turn.
