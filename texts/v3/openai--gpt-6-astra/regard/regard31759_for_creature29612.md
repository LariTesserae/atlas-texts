The power first notices the opening beside the threshold.

It is not the largest thing it could attend to. There is the plain, whose extent is unknown, and the pale band, and whatever deep arrangement feeds the violet mineral from below. But something is resting in the opening. When the light strengthens, its exposed edge draws back a little.

The power does not brighten the opening to see inside.

For a time, it attends by pressure alone.

It learns the difference between a fold braced for movement and a fold that has nowhere to go. They can look identical. An animal lying motionless beneath a shelf may be feeding, waiting for a neighbor, or holding a passage open with its own margins. To widen every narrow place would deprive some inhabitants of their means of travel. To keep every courtyard from closing would arrest the growth on which they feed.

Even the contraction is useful. There are sensitive surfaces that cannot reach new mineral until the plates draw together.

So the power waits through several dim intervals. Not because waiting is always wise, but because there are things it does not yet know.

When a chamber begins to close around a young animal, it learns something more.

The young one has placed its underside against both walls. As the gap narrows, it rearranges its folds in the ordinary way. Then it tries again. A hard segment has caught above another; its body is still too thick to pass between them.

Its movements become smaller without becoming less frequent.

Outside, an adult lays a margin against the wall.

The power holds the chamber where it is.

Elsewhere the district continues to contract. The tower leans. Three lanes become slots, and a new fracture opens beneath an arcade. Only this small chamber pauses, with the young animal caught but no longer being pressed.

The adult changes its position.

It passes an edge into the doorway, withdraws it, and passes it in again at a different height. The young one braces against it. For much of the dim interval nothing seems to happen. Then a single fold comes loose.

The power yields a little with the wall.

By the next brightening, the chamber is empty.

There is no new material in it, no shining mark. One side is slightly bowed. It looks like many other failures of symmetry in the district.

The adult and the young remain together beneath a nearby shelf.

The power does not take their stillness for gratitude. It has other things to learn.

*

The hard knots are more difficult.

Some contain nothing that can still feel the closing walls. Some retain a little activity, slower than feeding, almost lost beneath the mineral’s own changes.

These the power attends to separately.

It does not pull them free. Around each living knot it lets the stone soften by the smallest amount, preserving the pressure against which a margin might work. Where a body has folded too tightly to unfold itself, it supports one layer while another moves.

One takes many cycles.

An adult visits the place repeatedly. Once, finding a shallow hollow where there had been a flat wall, it stays through two brightenings. A pale edge eventually appears beneath its own. The two edges touch at several points.

The young animal that emerges is irregular. One part cannot flatten fully. It travels poorly across the paving, but under a low roof it can brace itself and move.

The power watches until it feeds.

At the other knots, where no living activity remains, it does not manufacture an answer to the adults’ visits. It leaves the clean bands undisturbed. Whatever the inhabitants are doing there, they are not interrupted.

*

Over time, the district acquires a small new tendency.

An occupied chamber can still narrow. It can become a passage suitable only for a sheet of living tissue. But when narrowing overtakes an inhabitant’s capacity to adjust, growth slows locally.

The change is not keyed simply to contact. Roots touch stone; feeding bodies touch stone; the tower contains hundreds of places where its mineral and its inhabitants bear against each other. The power attends to the repeated effort that obtains no release, the living fold driven against its own limit.

There, the wall waits.

Sometimes an animal makes its own way out. Sometimes a neighbor reaches it. Sometimes a thin slip of mineral yields beneath a caught segment and settles into a different position.

Afterward, growth resumes.

The power keeps observing. A kindness enacted in stone can become another difficulty in stone. It does not assume that the first form of its help should last forever.

There are fewer new knots.

The old ones remain.

*

The basin presents another question.

It would be easy to break the rim. But the plain beyond is not merely empty space awaiting improvement. Its dark substance holds the basin’s warmth in a particular way. The violet growth depends on pressures that would change if the district were opened carelessly.

Nor is it certain that the inhabitants want an expanse without close walls.

Instead, near the western paving, the power allows a small pocket to form within the boundary stone. It has a low entrance and a floor continuous with the district. Young mineral rises along one side. The other remains dark.

For a long time nothing enters.

A ribbon-plant eventually fastens two roots inside the mouth. The violet edge turns transparent beneath them. Later, one of the folded animals advances far enough to test the floor, then withdraws.

The power leaves the pocket as it is.

During a later contraction, two animals occupy it together. They feed along its growing edge. One departs; the other remains until the next dim interval.

Only then does the pocket deepen a little.

There is no road across the plain. There is a place beside the district that can become more district, slowly enough for its inhabitants to make use of it or leave it alone.

*

Eventually, the power becomes something that can be encountered.

Not a figure standing over the basin. Not a voice, in a place where so little is done by sound.

Beneath the western threshold, it holds a short length of surface steady.

An animal resting there finds that the surface yields when pushed, but does not collapse. If the animal withdraws, the yielding stops. If it braces again, support returns.

This happens many times.

Then the surface makes a small movement of its own: a pressure no stronger than that of a neighbor’s margin.

The animal withdraws.

The power becomes still.

On another cycle, the animal returns. It places several sensitive clusters against the paving and remains there. When the small pressure comes again, one of its folds shifts toward it.

Nothing further is required of the encounter.

Above them, a lintel continues to extend from opposite sides of an opening. The plants lift their dark ribbons toward the light. In the tower, pale lengths release and fasten, passing a slow adjustment upward.

The district is still strange. Many of its doorways lead nowhere useful. Its inhabitants still lose contact and find one another again.

But in a narrow chamber near the northern edge, a young body reaches the limit of its movement.

The wall waits.

Given time, it finds another way to fold.
