The seam it finds is too hot.

The grooved pad lifts. For a moment the creature holds it beneath the shield, then tries again a little farther from the edge. There is no opening there. The shelf has cooled into a continuous skin over the heat below.

Something has become aware of this effort.

It does not begin by touching the creature.

It attends first to the stone: to the load carried by the shelf, the thinning attachment beneath the overhang, the pressure that will eventually lift the basin’s near rim. It follows the escaping gases down through passages where no body could go. It distinguishes the heat that sustains the dark film from the heat that destroys it.

The difference is small. It keeps the difference.

Under the shelf, far enough down that the movement reaches the surface only as a faint, prolonged tremor, a channel opens.

The creature draws its limbs inward.

Nothing falls.

Gas that would have collected beneath the shelf begins to escape elsewhere, through a scatter of narrow outlets along the wall. The temperature under the creature’s pad diminishes slowly. A hot surface becomes a surface it can bear to touch.

It does not trust this at once.

Three feet remain fixed while the fourth tests the wall. The short limbs withdraw. It waits through the whole change and for a long time afterward.

The power waits too.

There are things it could do quickly. It could close the exposed flank, replace the missing digits, hold every plate of the basin motionless. But the dark film lives along faults. The creature feeds where the mountain opens. A still, sealed mountain would offer excellent footing for a while.

Instead, the power follows the living tissue beneath the damaged plates. It learns where the fibers have broken and where they have grown together again; which dull surfaces are scar and which are simply old. Beneath one turned-in edge, a grain of crystal works deeper whenever the creature bends.

The grain loosens.

At the next movement, it falls out.

The creature stops probing. A short limb passes beneath its flank, touches the place, passes over it again. Then the limb returns to the stone.

Along the new outlet, the power leaves a shallow cleft with an uneven rim. There is purchase for the walking digits on either side. The escaping gas is diffuse enough that the pale folds can remain extended.

The creature approaches sideways.

It tests the cleft with the longer foot, then with the damaged one. Both hold. It lowers the furrowed strip beneath its body against the warm rock.

Its plates begin to rise.

For a long time, nothing else is done.

—

At the shelters, the crack continues through the sleeping slab.

The power does not press the broken edges together. Below them, a whole thickness of mountain is drawing apart. Keeping this ledge intact would transfer its strain to the recess where the film grows.

It catches the slab when its lower end gives way.

The movement is slight: a settling rather than a fall. The slab comes to rest against the wall, leaving the doorway open. The row of stones remains outside.

Farther along the same mass of old rock, there is a place where three deep fractures have already spent their movement. It is neither cold nor free of fumes. It has an overhang, a rough floor, and room for the creature to turn without exposing its underside over a drop.

The power eases one loose block down before it can fall on an occupant.

Then it makes a route.

Not a smooth path. The creature’s feet have slipped on smoothness. There are lips to hook beneath, narrow crossings, resting places with several separate holds. Where the damaged foot cannot span a gap, a buried edge of stone emerges just far enough.

The route passes the old shelters. It passes the branching film.

At the recess, nothing is transplanted. A little heat is diverted beneath the seam into which the creature pressed its crumbling flake. The black point there remains black.

—

When the creature leaves the feeding cleft, the shelf is no longer perfectly clean.

There is its gray trace, and the crystal that fell from its flank, and the first small stone.

It touches the stone with a grooved pad.

Then it starts toward the saddle.

Twice it takes an older way instead of the route prepared for it. The power does not obstruct either choice. At the second crossing, the creature hangs beneath a ledge for so long that dust gathers on its uppermost plates. Eventually it comes up on the other side.

It reaches the shelters.

Inside the doorway, it touches the broken sleeping place. One walking foot rests on the fallen slab; another holds the wall. The short limbs pass along the pale hollow, now divided between two surfaces.

The power learns nothing certain from this.

It does not supply certainty.

After a while the creature backs out. It steadies one of the stones beside the entrance. The larger, wedged piece needs no adjustment.

It leaves them there.

Beyond the recess, its feet discover the newly exposed holds.

—

The overhang is empty when it arrives.

The creature tests the roof before the floor. It probes a vertical seam, withdraws, crosses to the far side, and hangs for a time with its body clear of the ground.

The warmth is steady.

Along the back wall lies a low, broad rise in the stone. The creature can rest there with all four walking limbs folded, but it does not do so yet. It clears grit from a shallow fault beside the rise. From beneath an armor plate, a short limb brings out a fragment of dark growth.

This time, when it presses the fragment into the fault, most of it holds.

The creature settles over it.

The power remains attentive to the gas, the buried stresses, the small exposed place on the creature’s flank. There is more to learn before attempting a new plate. There are other ridges to search, and the old enclosures give reason to search carefully.

For now, the overhang carries its weight. The film has warmth. No sharp deposit grows into the tissue laid against the floor.

A tremor passes through the mountain.

The creature rises onto four feet.

It waits.

Nothing in the recess shifts. After a while, it lowers itself again.
