The power did not bring rain.

It did not move the sun or persuade the folded young to unfold. Before it touched anything, it learned where touching would arrive.

This took a kind of patience for which the plain already had room.

It attended to the green crescents in the grazers’ chests, to the minute rings inside the leaves, to the white hunters and the depth that gathered beneath them. It followed the seams passing through the sleepers. Where a body appeared in several places, it did not assume that one was the original and the others were copies.

Especially, it attended to the animal that had tried to hold a second impression of a hand.

The animal was in a black-stemmed grove. A strip of distant ground showed beneath its ribs. Under a shelf far to the west, its folded body retained the fork of green by which both could be recognized.

The power approached neither body.

Instead, beside the grove, it made a small clear note.

The note occupied no more space than a fallen scale. It did not summon, and it contained nothing the animal needed. It was simply something that could be approached.

For a long while, the grazer continued feeding.

Then its divided head inclined toward the note.

The power let itself be found there.

***

What the animal found was not a mind laid open in its entirety. That would have been another kind of depth beneath a hunter.

It found an edge.

The edge could be examined from either side. Behind it was a little more space, and behind that, only as much as the animal chose to encounter. When its dorsal plates closed, the edge withdrew. When they opened again, it was there.

The animal circled it without changing its position among the plants.

A crescent of green moved through its chest. Two black pinpoints on the inner surface of its head grew bright.

Beneath the western shelf, the darkness inside the folded young stirred.

The power answered that body too.

Not first and afterward. Not adult and offspring.

Both.

For a moment the animal became difficult to see, but not in the familiar manner of concealment. The pale views among the leaves drew farther apart. A fine white fold opened where there had never been a plate.

Then it closed.

The power did nothing to keep it open.

By the time the animal returned to feeding, the note had become almost inaudible. It remained available for another visit.

***

There was suffering here, though it did not always resemble pain.

The power learned its shape without making a wound to study.

Under a hunter, a grazer’s world narrowed. Places that had belonged to its living awareness became inaccessible. The green within it ceased answering the green elsewhere. Finally, the stone beneath its feet became only stone.

The absence of a struggle did not make this peaceful.

Nor was the hunter cruel. It needed what the narrowing released. Its stillness was labor, its downward-pointing body an organ of want. Left unfed, it too would lose parts of the world, though in a different order.

The power would not preserve one creature by quietly sentencing another.

It began in a bare oval among the plants.

Nothing there sloped inward. Nothing had ever collected there. The power arranged a small fold in the mineral, shallow enough that an amber creature could cross it unharmed. Within the fold it placed the particular abundance a hunter obtained when a grazer’s openings closed.

There was no frightened awareness inside it. No hidden animal paid for the gift. The power had followed the making of it all the way down.

Above the distant fronds, one white form altered its height.

The power waited.

The hunter came to rest over the oval. The spaces beneath it deepened. For a while, the smooth mineral could be seen at an impossible distance below itself.

Then the downward-pointing tip drew back.

A faint rib became visible in one wing.

The hunter rose.

The power watched what followed: whether the feeding had sustained it, whether its next need was sharper, whether the fold had disturbed the roots beneath the plain. Only when it understood these things did it make another.

There were failures of placement. A fold too near a shelf altered a chord used by the sleepers. The power removed it and restored the chord. Another attracted the hunters but troubled the minute structures in a stand of low scales. That, too, was changed.

Help had to fit all the way through.

***

Meanwhile, it did not leave the grazers to be caught.

When a white shadow gathered above a living animal, the depth beneath it found a passage into one of the prepared folds. The hunter could feed. The grazer’s feet remained just clear of the ground.

The first animal to survive this stood for a long time with every opening closed.

Then a view of a distant leaf returned beneath its ribs.

It left.

No note of gratitude occupied the air. None was required.

Over time, the white forms gathered above the bare ovals. Their wings remained open. The plain acquired a few new depths, none of them inhabited by terror.

The grazers continued to avoid the mineral shapes that had once been bodies.

The power did not make those places cheerful.

It searched them with care.

Where something living could still be reached—an awareness not extinguished but stranded beyond a closed relation—it opened a way back. Where no one remained to take such a way, it did not assemble a plausible animal and call the loss undone.

Some seams stayed empty.

Other grazers approached them, lowered their divided heads, and waited.

The power kept the waiting places undisturbed.

***

The curious animal returned to the note.

It brought, inside its flank, a sharply focused view of the visitor’s hand.

The visitor was far away now. The view contained no captive portion of them; it was more like a remembered direction, kept with unusual exactness. Within it, the pulse rose and fell.

The grazer opened two dorsal plates.

A second hand appeared beside the first.

It failed again.

This time the power understood something of the attempt. The animal was not trying to steal or reproduce the hand. It was trying to discover how a being could remain itself while continually losing each of its moments.

The beat came.

The beat was gone.

Yet the hand belonged to someone.

The power offered a small correspondence: not a whole human life, not the terror of an ending, but one change held gently beside another. It left the offering outside the animal, where a clear note might stand.

The grazer touched it with the attention of its black pinpoints.

In the distant crack, its folded body answered.

One plate opened. Then another.

For a little while, the animal accommodated something it had not known before.

Afterward it went away among the low scales.

The power did not follow inside it.

***

There were other changes, most too small to be visible from the shelf.

An injured amber creature found that it could leave the stem to which it had become painfully bound. A sleeping place made narrow by a shifting seam gained room without losing its familiar relation to the distant leaves. Certain solitary grazers acquired a way to offer a note farther than before, and a way to refuse an answer.

Nothing was compelled to become more sociable, more adventurous, or more like the visitors who occasionally crossed the plain.

The sun remained where it was.

The plants were still dry to the touch. The mineral edges stayed immaculate. A broken leaf tip retained its strange loyalty to the stem.

But beneath the shelves there were fewer empty intervals.

And in the black-stemmed grove, the curious animal sometimes paused beside a small patch of apparently unoccupied air.

It did not always find something new there.

Sometimes it found only the edge it knew: approachable, withdrawable, with nothing pressing through.

Then it would lower its head, open its plates, and begin to offer something of its own.
