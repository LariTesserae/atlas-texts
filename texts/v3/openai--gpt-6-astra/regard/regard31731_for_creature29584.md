At first the power changes nothing.

It attends through several discharges, then through a clear afternoon, then through a night when the demand below falls and two of the fans stop. It watches the creature tighten around the absence of their vibration. It watches the folds release, unevenly, when the mist comes from a new direction.

It does not mistake the roar for suffering. Nor does it mistake contraction for suffering, or stillness for contentment. These are things it must learn here, from this body, rather than bring with it.

For a time its attention is no heavier than the cloudlight.

What it discovers is that the creature can meet most of what happens in the court. It can lose a margin. It can withdraw from light, hold against water, alter the direction of its growth. Its body has answers to circumstances that would seem intolerable to a body with ears and warm blood.

There are other circumstances to which it has no answer.

The plant will eventually shut down for maintenance. The cold pipe will become warm. The mist will cease. Someone will inspect the flashing and notice that it has not been seated properly.

The power looks at the badly seated screw.

It does not loosen it further.

Below that corner are layers of waterproofing, insulation, reinforcement. Below those are rooms in which people work. There is no wisdom in preserving one small shelter by slowly ruining another.

Instead it begins with the broken packing felt.

The moss occupies its upper face. Beneath it, among the compressed fibers, water persists after the visible surfaces have drained. The power makes a little more room there—not enough to lift the moss, not enough to disturb the recess. A narrow hollow opens within the felt, lined with mineral grains that hold water between them.

The hollow has no outlet toward the building. When it is full, it spills onto the same concrete that already carries the discharge.

For many cycles, nothing finds it.

The power waits.

Then it changes a roughness in the floor, less than the depth of a grain of grit. A thread of moisture takes a slightly different course. It approaches the creature’s newest crescent and stops short.

The creature extends elsewhere.

That, too, is allowed.

The power does not pull the body toward its provision. It does not put hunger into it, or restlessness, or an understanding for which the creature has no organs. It leaves the dampness available.

Eventually, during a long interval between discharges, an edge reaches it.

There is no visible moment of discovery. One blunt lobe remains spread when those beside it gather inward. By the next morning, a short length of concrete bears the clear adherent trace.

Over the following weeks the creature follows its own work toward the felt.

The new shelter is not luxurious. There is no deep pool in which the flattened body must struggle to hold itself. There is no rich food that will bloom, exhaust the water, and rot. There are wet surfaces, several narrow clearances, and routes by which excess water can leave. Some of the old vibration reaches it through the concrete. Some reaches it differently.

The creature learns these differences.

By the time the plant stops, its protected folds occupy two places.

The silence arrives first as a series of subtractions.

A fan slows. The rail ceases to tremble. The cold pipe gives its last drops. For hours the creature remains contracted beneath the flashing, although no discharge follows.

In the felt, water holds.

The power stays through the shutdown.

It could pour an endless stream into the recess. Instead it replaces only what is lost, slowly enough that there is no current to brace against. Nothing about the water commands the creature to emerge.

When the worker comes, the pressure of a knee travels across the court.

The worker has a driver, a scraper, and a cloth.

Here the power does something larger.

It lets the worker see.

Not a vision. Not a face appearing in the wet steel. Only the slight withdrawal at the edge of what had looked like residue: enough movement, sufficiently distinct from the settling water, that the cloth pauses.

The worker leans closer.

For a while there is only the yellow light, the scraper held clear of the floor, and that patient, puzzled attention which sometimes precedes care.

The flashing still needs repair.

The worker takes photographs. Someone else comes up. Their first guesses are wrong. They do not know whether the pale material is one organism or several, whether its movement is active, whether the rust inside it is dangerous to it.

They know enough not to wipe it away.

The repair takes longer than scheduled. A short section of flashing is replaced without scraping the concrete beneath it. Beside the recess they fit a small removable cover, open along its lower edge, leaving the water its route to the drain. The maintenance record acquires a photograph and a plain instruction to check before cleaning.

The power does not require them to understand everything before their caution counts.

When the plant starts again, the weather in the court returns badly at first. Water strikes unfamiliar edges. A loose drop falls where a thread used to run.

The creature loses a little of its exposed margin.

The power watches closely, but does not prevent every loss. The body gathers. Its surviving edge seals. Beneath the new cover, a fold opens into a clearance that was not there before.

There will be other repairs. The felt will decay. The plant itself will not last forever.

The power has not mistaken a good intervention for a finished obligation.

For now, the fans take their load. The four shallow planes emerge from the receding water. A worker closes the hatch carefully, lifting the bent grass blade clear before the door meets its frame.

Beneath the cover, the creature puts out a margin.

It follows no instruction. It offers no thanks.

It reaches a place that is still wet.
