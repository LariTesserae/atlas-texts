Loose scree tumbles in restless cascades down slopes that haven't yet learned to slump into gentle hills. The ground underfoot is a mosaic of fractured stone, rich volcanic soil, and spongy mats of organic detritus—fertile black earth veined with veins of glittering obsidian, still warm from the forge-fires below.
