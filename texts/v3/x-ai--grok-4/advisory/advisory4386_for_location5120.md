For the blinding light, polarized sunglasses or goggles with UV protection are non-negotiable—without them, you'd risk snow blindness from the reflective rocks. A wide-brim hat or hood to shield your face. No need for heavy rain gear, but a lightweight poncho for those spectral mists.
