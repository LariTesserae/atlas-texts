### 2. Physical Gear: Layering for Warm Turbulence and Sparse Resources
Vellorath's warmth (balmy, like a Mediterranean summer but at altitude) clashes with its damp mists, turbulent winds, and intermittent rains, so gear should prioritize versatility, durability, and self-sufficiency. Mid-layer: a lightweight fleece for the occasional high-altitude cool-down. - **Tools and Essentials**: Multi-tool or knife for cutting vines or fruits from the lush patches. Rope (50-100 feet) for traversing chasms or securing gear in gravitational anomalies. A basic first-aid kit expanded for cuts from sharp rock, altitude meds (like acetazolamide), and anti-chafing balm for the humidity.
