Avoid anything that needs cooking; the heat might "help" unpredictably.
  - **Medical Kit:** Include anti-fungal creams (for the damp fungi), bandages for slips on slick stones, pain relievers for heat-induced headaches, and cooling packs that activate on contact. Antihistamines for any acrid scents triggering allergies, and a basic thermometer to monitor body temperature—aim to stay below 100°F (37.8°C).
  - **Rest and Recovery:** Plan micro-breaks every 30 minutes to sit on a portable mat (waterproof, of course) and cool down. If cooler eddies appear as mirages, approach cautiously—they might be traps.
