  - For the heat: Pack personal cooling tech, like a vest with phase-change materials that absorb heat, or a small portable fan/mister (but remember, no water here—use a non-aqueous cooling fluid like liquid nitrogen packets if your multiverse gear allows). Your body's sweat won't evaporate properly in the thick, particulate air, so overheating is a risk.
