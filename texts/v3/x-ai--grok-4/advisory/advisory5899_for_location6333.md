- **Fitness Regimen**: Focus on endurance over strength. Long, slow hikes with a backpack in dim, noisy environments (e.g., caves with echoing sounds) to build stamina. Train your senses: practice navigating blindfolded in a humid, insect-filled forest to simulate the loud darkness. The fauna's touches—brushes from insects or vines—could feel invasive, so desensitize yourself to light physical contact (e.g., wear itchy fabrics or expose skin to gentle breezes).

- **Health Checks**: Get a full medical eval, focusing on respiratory and cardiovascular health. Stock up on anti-nausea remedies for the metallic-tasting air and potential disorientation from time dilation.
