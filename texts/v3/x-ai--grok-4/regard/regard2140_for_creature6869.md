It is benevolent not through forceful goodness, but through the empathy of one who has witnessed countless births and unravelings, understanding that life, in all its forms, is a fragile miracle worthy of gentle regard. Wise, it knows the peril of overreach: to impose order on chaos is to snuff the spark that makes a thing alive. And so, when the hidden pocket catches its attention—like a faint, anomalous hum amid the symphony of mountains and skies—it pauses, drawn by the pocket's contradictions: a young, sharp cradle of heat and weep, teeming with silent multitudes that blur into a collective dream, where reality frays not from decay but from exuberant possibility.

The noticing begins subtly, a whisper in the ether. The power's heart—vast and intuitive—resonates with this: here is a place of primal otherworldliness, a hidden fever-dream that echoes the cosmos's own birth pangs, where rules bend not to break but to birth new harmonies. Why this pocket, among infinities? Perhaps its weirdness sings a unique note, a call of innocent defiance against the mundane laws that bind lesser realms. Or perhaps it is the silence—the profound quiet amid density—that draws the power, reminding it of forgotten solitudes where wisdom first took root.

Would it do anything? Intuition whispers yes, but not in conquest or correction. Reasoning tempers this: benevolence thrives in observation, wisdom in non-interference unless the essence cries for aid. The pocket thrives in its anomaly; to "fix" its fraying would be to dim its light. Yet the power senses a subtle vulnerability—the pocket's intimacy makes it fragile, its weirdness a beacon that might attract less gentle eyes: wanderers from below, drawn by the mountain's spine, or cosmic predators that feed on glitches. Heart-led, the power chooses a path of quiet guardianship, intervening with the lightness of a breath, letting actions unfold as extensions of the pocket's own nature, deepening without dominating.
