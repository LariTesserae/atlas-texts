And so, one timeless instant, its attention turns to the grove-hollow: that sunken cradle of eroded earth, where vegetation reigns as both architect and engine, fauna dances in abundant sparsity, and the air hums with an industrial rhythm born of pure, unchanging nature. The Power pauses, infinite in its patience, and reasons: What need has this place of intervention? It is not broken, not suffering, not crying out in discord. It is a microcosm of equilibrium, a frozen Eden where entropy has bowed to harmony, where the mechanical thrum of sap-hydraulics and seed-factories pulses without waste, where fauna serves as extensions of the vegetal whole, pruning and pollinating in eternal cycles. To impose change would be folly, a violation of the grove's own rules—stability as sanctity, invariance as beauty. Yet benevolence stirs, for wisdom knows that even perfection can deepen, like a well drawing sweeter water from hidden depths. The Power's heart—intuited as a vast, empathetic core—whispers: Honor this. Commune, do not command. Enrich without erasing.

To the fern-monoliths, it offers a silent affirmation, a ethereal hum that synchronizes with their grinding root-mills, allowing them to perceive, for a fleeting eternal moment, the vastness beyond their hollow: echoes of other worlds in similar stasis, distant groves where erosion has carved parallel perfections. This is no disruption; it is a mirroring, a way for the ferns to feel their place in a cosmic lattice, deepening their watchful elderhood without urging them to grow or alter.

The symbiote mites scuttle with renewed vigor, not faster or changed, but with a subtle awareness of their role in the grand conduit, bridging not just fern-pillars but faint threads to the Power's wisdom, allowing knowledge to flow like sap: insights into balancing humidity across even vaster voids, ensuring the grove's chill remains eternal without effort.

The fauna, those mobile extensions of the plant-world, feel the Power's touch as a latent warmth in their bones, a benevolent intuition that sharpens their instincts without rewriting them. The grazers, in their undulating herds, sense a deeper resonance in their bellows, their migrations tracing not just figure-eights but subtle fractals that echo cosmic patterns, pruning fronds with even greater precision, exhaling gases that now carry a whisper of universal nutrients—star-dust echoes, nourishing the root-mats without tipping the balance. Even the subtler inhabitants—the fungal networks, the dew-pool microbes, the fossil-echoes—stir under the Power's gaze. Mycelial threads pulse with shared dreams, coordinating rhythms that now faintly attune to the cosmos's hum, while bubbles in the pools release vapors laced with wise serenity, misting the air to deepen the cool dampness without overwhelming it. The fossils, those ancient imprints, awaken not to life but to memory, infusing the living mass with echoes of primordial genesis, reminding all beings of their unbroken lineage.

What does the Power do, then? It communes, it enriches, it withdraws—leaving the grove not changed, but deepened. The stasis remains inviolate; no new growth sprouts, no cycles accelerate, no voids fill. The inhabitants, from towering fern to darting insect, feel a quiet expansion of self-awareness: they know themselves as part of something greater, yet remain utterly themselves, their eternal song resounding with a fuller timbre.
