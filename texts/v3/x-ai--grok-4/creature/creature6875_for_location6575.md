But predators? Ah, they exist, though rare—the Lumberers, those enormous, solitary shapes that first appear as shadows at the periphery.

As I describe them, they grow stranger: their "heads" are clusters of sensory fronds that taste the air's density, navigating the warped gravity pockets by instinct, sometimes levitating briefly in defiance of the land's rules, only to crash down in bursts that send shockwaves through the roar. Rare as they are, perhaps only a handful per vast sector of the expanse, their migrations carve the subtle swells into new configurations, embodying the land's ancient reinvention.
