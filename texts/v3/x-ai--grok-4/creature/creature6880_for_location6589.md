Predation twists into partnership; a burrower devours a vine's tip, but in doing so ingests spores that germinate within its gut, emerging later as hybrid growths that enhance its armor. Time dilates here—cycles of birth and decay unfold in minutes, driven by the feverish warmth radiating from the earth, where a larva might pupate into a glider before your eyes, its form stretching and reforming in acelerated throes.
