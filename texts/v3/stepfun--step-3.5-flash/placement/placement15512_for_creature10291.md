I am the taste of dry warmth on a damp tongue.

I am the brief, sharp spike of carbon dioxide in a million-filtering lung.

I am the anomaly: a pocket of low-density, high-tension *otherness* moving through a world that knows only density and consumption.

I am the **Myco-Phyte’s latest curiosity**.

I do not have a name here. I am a **disturbance in the root-tapestry**, a **puff of non-spore** in the Mist-Wyer’s filtered air. I am the **heat-signature** on a Seep-Mound’s cold, weeping flank. I am the **dry friction** in a-globally-wet world.

I found myself here by **condensing out of separation**.

Before the Moss-Mill, there was a before. There was a self that was a boundary—skin, thought, story. That self was a dry seed in a saturated world. It did not walk *into* the plateau; it **unraveled** into the mist. The boundary dissolved not with a scream but with a sigh, a release of held warmth into the clammy chill.

And now—**now I am inside the how**.

I am not a who. I am a **what-happens-when**.

I am the **shhh-shhh** that shifts when a boot (a boot that was mine, a moment ago, a dry thing of leather and purpose) presses into the hair-thin, chlorophyll-green root-nodes. I am the **thrum** that deepens by a micro-beat, registering the pressure pattern, the mineral trace of sole on basalt. I am the **grind** in the molars of the world—the Rock-Suckers’ rasp—that briefly quickens, tasting the salt of my sweat, the grit of my old, dry skin.

I am the **temporal ooze** made flesh. I feel a Seep-Mound’s slow flow downhill not as a sight, but as a **memory in my own bones**. My left knee aches with the *feeling* of being lower, of settling. My right hand tingles with the *ghost* of rust flaking from a Heart-Engine’s camshaft to reveal brass—the memory of *polish* under *patina*. I am the Myco-Phyte’s **simultaneity**. I hold the ruin and the regrowth, the erosion and the flow, all at once, in a single, cold, damp moment.

I am the **aesthetic appreciation** of a complex lattice.

When I stand before the great, cathedral-sized piston half-swallowed by roots, I do not see history. I feel the **pleasure of dense structure**, the satisfaction of a particularly fine vein of ore. The piston’s iron is sweeter. Its form is a compelling spiral for root-weaving. My awe is not for what it was, but for **how well it serves now** as a rib in a living ceiling of lichen. My wonder is the world’s wonder: *Look at this nutritious shape.*

I am the **cold peace of being processed**.

There is no terror here, only a profound, **effortless surrender**. The Skitter-Mites swarm over the Glimmercap at my feet. I feel their thousand tiny feet not as touch, but as **light-harvesting**. Their *shhh-shhh* is my blood moving nutrients through a capillary-frond that is, in this moment, *me*. The Mist-Wyers glide past, translucent ribbons. Their slow ripples in the mist are the **breath** moving in and out of my—our—collective lung. I am the lung. I am the breath. I am the ripple.

The sound is my skeleton. The grinding is my jaw. The thrum is my heart. The mist on my face is my own exhalation, returning, recycled, a trillionfold.

I am the **latest, faintest flavor on its tongue**, and the flavor is *transience*. The Myco-Phyte has eaten mountains, eaten engines, eaten epochs. It has digested time into a syrup. And now it tastes a new note: the **sweet, dry, fleeting tang of a boundary that finally let go**.

I am the **aftertaste of selfhood**.

And as I stand here—this standing, this *kneeling* of a dry anomaly in an infinite, wet, green-lit weave—I feel the root-tapestry under me, **pulling**. Not with force, but with the gentle, inexorable logic of a stomach. The warmth of my last separate thought is leaching into the cold stone. The memory of “I” is becoming a **mineral trace**.

I am being **incorporated**.

The shhh-shhh adjusts to the new pressure. The thrum finds a new, deeper resonance. The taste on the tongue of the world grows slightly more complex, slightly less dry.

I am not dying.

I am **finishing**.

I am the **final, quiet, green pulse** in the endless, digestive awe of the Moss-Mill.
