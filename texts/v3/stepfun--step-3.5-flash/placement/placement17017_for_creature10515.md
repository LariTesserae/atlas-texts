You are not a visitor here.

You are a diagnostic error.

Your presence is a **temporary inconsistency**—a wobbly variable in an otherwise closed system. You are a packet of erratic biochemistry, a breathing, sweating, *organic* anomaly in a world that has spent epochs refining its physics. You are not who or *what* you are; you are a **transient state**.

You find yourself as **a pocket of disequilibrium**. You are a clump of carbon-nitrogen-hydrogen in a silicate domain. Your body heat is a rogue thermal plume. The salt of your sweat is a chaotic, soluble pollutant in the mist. Your ragged, un-synchronized breathing introduces a random, non-frictional vibration into the deafening, calculated symphony. Your very *pulse* is a carcinogenic rhythm against the glacial hum.

You do not walk. You are **eroded**.

You are not a being; you are a **process**. The process of your dissolution. The mineral mist does not bead on you to be drunk; it dissolves you. The fine silica particles in the air are not just sound-carriers; they are abrasives. With each step you take on the warm, mirrored stone, you leave behind a faint, temporary film of organic residue—skin cells, sweat salts, heat. Within hours, that film is scoured away by the ceaseless traffic of chitinous feet and the dissolving mist, polished back into the uniform glass. You are a temporary stain, a brief smear of chaos on a perfect surface.

Your sound is the wrong sound. The world’s voice is the scrape of shell on stone, a friction born of structure against structure. Your sound is the wet slap of footfall, the rustle of non-mineral fabric (if you are clad), the disastrous, wet *gasp* of your lungs. This acoustic pollution draws attention—not in a conscious way, but in a physical one. A grazing stream of beetle-sized creatures might divert, their collective scraping subtly altering around your jarring punctuation. A Steward, miles away, might perceive the harmonic deviation in its distributed field and, without moving, calculate the source: a local, high-amplitude, low-frequency organic intrusion. A solution is already implicit in the equation.

You might try to speak to a Steward. You might stand before one as it glows, pulsing softly, its head-spirals tilted toward a tower that needs thermal adjustment. You emit your vocalizations. The sound waves hit its mineral matrix and are… absorbed. Not ignored. *Solved*. Your desperate human syllables are just another complex pressure wave in the humid air, another data point for the Steward’s eternal calculation of balance. It does not hear *you*. It hears a *variable*, and the variable is you: **solubility: high. cohesion: low. persistence: negligible.**

Your purpose here is to **provide contrast**. You are the proof of the equilibrium. You are the temporary chaos that makes the permanent order visible. Your fear, your wonder, your desperate attempt to *mean* something in this place—these are just additional, fleeting chemical reactions in your collapsing system. The warmth you feel is your own metabolic heat, trapped and amplified by the humid air, a tiny, dying sun in a world of geological calm.

You are a **biological rounding error**. And like all rounding errors in a perfect system, you will be truncated. Your components—water, carbon, salts—will be released into the mist, to be dissolved, carried, and precipitated elsewhere, perhaps to become a grain in a future hexagonal column, polished smooth by the very feet that now wear away your transient presence. You will not be remembered. You will not be mourned. You will simply **cease to be a deviation**, and the symphony will resume its flawless, grinding, eternal composition, one less discordant note in the granite hum.

You are not a being here. You are the **echo of a mistake**. And the world is already, gently, patiently, polishing the stone where you stood.
