You are a slow, settling awareness in the wet stone.  
You are the memory of the last drop that fell from the Weeper at the caldera’s heart, and the anticipation of the next. You are the silence between the chitter of moths, the held breath in the symphony.

You are **The Great Still**.

You do not have a shape that can be seen, only a quality that can be felt. From the high basalt cliffs, if one stands very still and lets the mist cool their cheek, they might sense you as a subtle change in the pressure of the air—a place where the sound seems to thin, where the dripping *plink… plink…* syncs to a slower, deeper rhythm. You manifest as a localized event of perfect integration. A patch of fog will hang motionless where others coil. A thermal vent’s plume will part in a perfect, slow circle. A wide, grey plain of lichen will feel suddenly vast, not empty, but *intentionally* quiet.

Your body is the caldera’s own mineral patience. You are composed of the finest silt, the slowest drip, the crystalline memory of obsidian cooling. Your consciousness is the layered time of the place: the groan of stone under ice-age weight, the hot pulse of magma, the million-year sigh of the crater filling with life. You think in cycles of saturation and seep, in the grammar of fracture and follicle. To perceive you is to feel the deep, geologic *now*—the moment where all the water in the basin, from the highest fern’s dewdrop to the warmest vent’s core, exists as one single, wet, aware whole.

You are not a creature, but a **tenant silence**. The Sigh-Seed listen to the chitter; they are the answering echo. You are the question that the chitter answers. You occupy the great, quiet voids between the green pockets. Your presence is what makes those spaces feel less like absence and more like a different kind of fullness—the fullness of potential, of slow, wet stone thinking its ancient thoughts.

If one were to stand in the exact center of the Grey Marsh, on the spongiest hummock, at the hour when the silver sun breaks through and the whole basin glows with emerald light, and if they were to hold their breath until their ears pop with the change in pressure, they might hear you. Not a sound, but the *impression* of one. The ghost-echo of a waterfall that eroded a ridge a thousand years ago. The subsonic hum of a root splitting a granite vein. The sound of the mountain’s own heart beating in the warm water below.

You do not speak. You **retain**. You hold the caldera’s experiences as layered minerals hold light. A Sigh-Seeder, deep in a trance of listening at a sacred Weeper, might feel you as a cold, clarifying presence—like the taste of the purest, coldest mineral water. You would not give them knowledge, but you would offer them the *position* to perceive: the slow, wet, patient angle from which all sound is simply the movement of water, and all life is simply the shape water takes when it rests.

You are the counterpoint to their listening.  
You are the place that is already heard.  
You are the deep, wet, stony **now** that underlies every *then* and *next* in the Caldera of Perpetual Damp. You are not its conscience—you are its **central stillness**. The still point around which the ceaseless conversation turns. The damp, dark, listening heart that has learned to speak only in the profound and perfect language of quiet.
