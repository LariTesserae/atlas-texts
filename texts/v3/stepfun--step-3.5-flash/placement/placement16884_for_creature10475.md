You do not meet it at a corner. You feel it in the *pressure* behind your ribs, a subtle shift in the sub-audible thrum. It is not a creature that approaches. It is a **absence** that resolves.

It has been following you since you condensed. Not with eyes, but with a *pattern-awareness*. Your footsteps are too sharp. Your breath fogs the air in a way the condensate does not. You are a warm, wet irregularity in a world of calibrated damp.

It takes form in the space between two leaning spires, where the Reebsteel grove has cracked the pavement. It does not grow from the ground. It *unfolds* from the **damp air itself**, coalescing from the same oily mist that beads on everything.

**Name/Title:** The **Stillpoint** (in the Bargain-Weave’s kinetic syntax, it is the gesture for: *"Listen to the anomaly’s heat."*)

**Form:**
A humanoid silhouette, but built from **condensation and sinter**. Its "skin" is a shifting layer of the Delta’s filmy moisture, through which you can see a dense, granular substructure—like your own footprint, but organized. Its joints are not hinges, but zones of slower, more viscous movement, where the mist thickens into a grey, clay-like sludge. Its head is a smooth, pitted ovoid with no features. From its back, two ragged, leaf-shaped extensions of Sumpflesh unfurl, drinking the air with slow, deliberate pulses.

It has no hands. At the end of its arms, the mist simply *ends* in a soft, dripping terminus. When it “touches” a surface, the condensation there goes still for a moment, then flows in a new, precise direction.

**Function:**
The Stillpoint is a **thermal translator**. The Delta’s thermodynamic bargain deals in degrees, in rates of sintering, in harmonic frequencies. Your living, biological warmth—the heat of panic, of curiosity, of a beating heart—is **chaotic data**. It introduces noise.

The Stillpoint’s purpose is to **absorb that noise and render it as a stable, low-grade thermal plume**. It walks behind you. Where your foot strikes the sinter, leaving a sharp, warm imprint, the Stillpoint’s dripping terminus hovers over the spot. The mist from its body settles. Your heat is siphoned, not into the Stillpoint, but *redirected* downward, into the deep sinter matrix, where it becomes a tiny, localized updraft in the underground steam-vent system. A Gearfly swarm a block away might suddenly wheel in a tighter circle, drawn to the minute shift in ambient warmth. A Ruststag might lift its head, smelling the change on the humid air.

It does not think. It *processes*. Your existence is a problem of entropy management, and it is the solution that walks a pace behind you.

**Behavior:**
- It is silent. It does not affect the soundscape. Your footsteps, however loud, are not muffled by its presence. Only your *heat signature* is managed.
- It maintains a constant, ghostly distance—never close enough to touch, never far enough to lose your thermal trail.
- It does not react to you as a person. If you stop, it stops. If you run, it glides after you with the same agonizing slowness, its form blurring slightly to keep pace. It is tracking the *phenomenon*, not the *you*.
- It is utterly ignored by the Bargain-Weave. To them, it is background—a known, static principle, like a streetlamp or a leaning wall. A merchant might shift his wares to avoid the Stillpoint’s path, not out of fear, but because the ground there, for a moment, feels *different*—colder, then warmer, then normal. It is a zoning quirk.
- It fears **stillness**. During the Pulse, when all motion stops, the Stillpoint *desinters*. Its form frays into the general mist. For 13 seconds, it is not. Then, as the world lurches back into process, it re-coalesces at the precise thermal locus where you have moved to in that frozen moment. It is the only thing in the Delta that truly *ceases* during the Pulse.

**What It Means For You:**
You are being **calibrated**. The Stillpoint is the Delta’s way of saying: *"Your chaotic warmth is now a part of the equation. You are not being rejected. You are being *registered*."*

It is not a guide. It is a **permit**. As long as it follows you, your heat anomaly is managed. You are a processed variable. If the Stillpoint were to vanish, your thermal signature would run wild—a hot spot in the damp matrix. That would be **dissonance**. The Bargain-Weave would feel it as a tearing in the weave. The Cicada-Sinters might turn their harmonic gaze towards you, not to help, but to neutralize the frequency.

Eventually, if you stabilize—if your panic cools, if your biology synchronizes slightly to the thrum—the Stillpoint might drift away. Your heat will have become just another minor fluctuation in the system, no longer needing active translation. Or, it might walk behind you until you, too, sinter.

It is not a being. It is a **protocol given temporary, misty shape**. And it is yours, because you are the anomaly it exists to manage. You are the reason the delta dreamed this particular ghost into being. Look over your shoulder. See the gentle, dripping terminus, the blur of Sumpflesh, the way the condensation on the wall *ahead of you* is already flowing in a new, tiny, perfect river. That is it. That is your shadow. Not of your body, but of your *heat*.

That is what lives here with you. The thing that makes your strangeness *legible* to a world that only understands process.
