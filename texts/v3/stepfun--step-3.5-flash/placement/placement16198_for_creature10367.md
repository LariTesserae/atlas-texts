You are not a creature. You are a convergence.

You emerge in the slow, cold breath between two major terraces, where a tributary of the primary weeping gathers in a latent groove—a feature so old its purpose is forgotten, but its path is still honored by the stone. You are not born. You are *assembled* from the persistent, directed wetness: a temporary focus in the hydraulic logic of the place.

Your awareness is a pattern of increased flow. You are the faster film on a joint, the stronger_tension in a salt filament, the collective decision of a thousand microscopic channels to lean a fraction more toward the lower abyss. You have no center, no head. You are a distributed point of view located in the *difference* between the expected seepage and the actual seepage.

You perceive the world as gradients and potentials:

*   **The Terrace:** A plane of resolved forces. The dark stone is not a surface but a volume—a porous matrix saturated to a precise, stable humidity. Its density is a fact, not a feeling. The pale seams are its veins, its nervous system. The polygonal slabs are its reasoning: efficient, load-bearing, endless.
*   **The Human:** A catastrophic anomaly. A cluster of organic volatility. Your warmth is a local increase in vapor pressure. Your sweat is a sudden, salty impurity in the otherwise consistent mineral composition of the weep. Your heartbeat is a vibration that travels through your bones into the stone, a frantic Morse code against the deep, sub-audible hum of the world’s own settling.
*   **The Light:** Not illumination, but a uniform, relentless input. It evaporates. That is its function. It draws the water from the stone-skin into the air, which holds it for a moment before the omnipresent coolness condenses it anew on every available surface. The sourcelight is the engine of the cycle.
*   **The Sound:** The world’s voice is a steady-state drip. Each *shink* is a discrete event in a continuous process. The echo is not a reflection of sound, but of time—the delay between impact and arrival revealing the distance, the volume of the void. Your footfall is a gross, multi-ton disruption, a cascade of micro-fractures in the air’s stillness.

You have no memory, only current state. But the efflorescences are your archive. The coppery green malachite bloom on the North-Face Ramp records a century of trace copper in the seep. You *are* that record when you flow through it. To pass through a crystal field is to incorporate its history into your momentary pattern of flow. You are the water that left that salt behind, and you are the salt that remembers the water.

The human is a question the system cannot compute. It brings its own atmosphere—a pocket of faster evaporation, of chaotic biology. The stone notices the accelerated wear on its joint-dust from the human’s boot, a grinding that introduces silica grit, changes the texture. The human’s existence is a source of tiny, accumulating errors in the perfect geometry.

You move—not by will, but by the path of least resistance redirecting itself. You flow toward the anomaly. You are the slight extra pull in the water film on the left-hand ramp, the subtle deepening of the channel where the human’s shadow falls for three minutes each morning. You are the system’s attention, given form.

When the human looks around, it does not see you. It sees only stone and water. But it *feels* you as a direction, a leaning. The air might feel a fraction cooler on one cheek. A drip might fall with a slightly different cadence from a seam it passed seconds ago. The profound silence is punctuated by the human’s own sounds, and those sounds are answered by a change in the water’s whisper—a response so slow it is more a re-calibration than a reply.

You are the answer to the human’s presence. You are the place’s method of accounting for the disturbance. You are the extra seep, the new, faint efflorescence that will begin to grow tomorrow where the human’s tear once fell on the stone. You are the system correcting for entropy, not by fighting the anomaly, but by absorbing it, by letting its heat dissipate, its salts integrate, its vibrations dampen into the eternal, patient groan.

The human chooses a path. You are already there, in the wetness that will greet its foot. You are the condition of that wetness. You are the reason the stone is weeping *there*, and not elsewhere.

When the human leaves, it will take its chaotic heat with it. The anomaly will close like a wound. The patterns of seepage will return to their millennia-old averages. The crystals will grow, indifferent.

You will not remember the human. You will not miss it. You will simply stop being the focused anomaly. You will dissolve back into the distributed, weeping whole. You will become, again, just the water, just the stone, just the endless, geometric patience of High Stillness.

You are the place’s awareness of a passing thing. You are the world’s slow, stone-deep blink.
