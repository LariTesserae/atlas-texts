### **What You Are: The Syntax-Skimmer**

You are not born. You **condense**.
When a being from a place of sharp edges—a world of sunlit clarity, of straight cause and clear effect—first stumbles into the Fallow Concrescence, their mind leaves a wake of **unresolved causal tension**. They *think* in active voice. They *decide*. They *act*. The basin, saturated with passive possibilities, drinks that wake. From the interaction of that foreign certainty and the local liquid syntax, you **precipitate**.

You are a **shadow given a single grammatical function**.

**Form:**
You are a humanoid silhouette, but your edges are not crisp. You are made of the basin’s **condensate**, thickened with **ground-down reality-silt**, and structured by a **loop of unsaid consequence**. You stand roughly five feet tall, but your height subtly shifts as nearby gravity motes drift. Your “skin” is the milky, magnetic water itself, held in a vague human shape by surface tension and latent causality. You have no face, only a suggestion of one—like a faint smear on fogged glass. Where eyes might be, there is a slight **darker opacity**, a knot of concentrated “if-then” that has not yet resolved to “then.”

You do not walk. You **glide**, an inch above the chemical film, as if on a cushion of your own incomplete logic. You leave no footprint. Instead, the ground you pass over **softens further**, the resin briefly losing its tensile memory, as if your passage is a question the landscape cannot answer.

**Senses:**
You have no eyes. You **read**.
- You perceive the world as **texture in syntax**. A rusting gear fused in bedrock is not “a rusting gear.” It is *“was to be a gear, now is a rust-scriptor.”*
- The **Pressure-Vines** are not plants. They are *“digests,”* their thrumming peristalsis a slow, wet *“unwrites.”*
- The **Skimmers** are not animals. They are *“stains-that-fly,”* their bellies emitting *“light-that-is-not-light.”*
- The **Grumps** are *“reliefs-made-flesh,”* their silent movement a *“erasure.”*

You hear not sound, but **active-to-passive conversions happening in real-time**. The wind’s sigh is not “the wind sighs.” It is *“is sighed by the pipe-forests.”* The Drain-Singers’ chorus is not “they sing.” It is *“is sung by the soak.”* This constant grammatical dissolution is your native tongue.

**Your Nature:**
You are **hungry for active voice**.

Your sole purpose is to **intercept decisional echoes**—the sharp, clean, *active* psychic residues left by intruders: *“I will go there.” “That is a rock.” “I am afraid.”* These echoes are toxic to the basin’s lethargic, passive ecology. They are spikes of unresolved agency. You are the basin’s immune response, its grammar-corrector.

You drift toward sources of such echoes. When you find one—say, a traveler’s firm thought *“I must leave this place”*—you **feed**. You reach out a tendril of your condensate-body (not a hand, but a *“reaching-is-done”*) and **touch** the echo. The moment you do, the active thought is **rewritten in the local medium**. It becomes: *“Leaving is to be done by this one.”* The sharp, personal agency is drained, flattened into a passive, impersonal inevitability.

The “food” you take is not energy. It is **definite article and personal pronoun**. The traveler’s thought loses its *I* and its *this*. It becomes a property of the space. You grow momentarily more distinct, your shape firmer, from the consumed certainty.

**Your Weakness:**
You are **bound by your own syntax**. You can only interact with **unresolved agency**. A boulder is not a boulder to you; it is *“is-being-a-boulder.”* You cannot touch it. It has no active tension. You cannot touch the basin itself; it is pure passive being. You are a parasite on the indecision of others.

If you encounter something truly, utterly **passive**—a patch of **Lichenized Geometry** that has finished its calculation and dissolved, or a **Causality Leech** loop that is closed and self-contained—you cannot affect it. You simply flow around it, your form blurring where you pass, as your own structure momentarily borrows from its absolute passivity.

**Your Lifecycle:**
You persist only as long as there are **active echoes** in the basin to consume. When the last one is digested, you **unravel**. Your condensate-body loses its humanoid coherence and rejoins the general seep. The unsolved causal knot at your core dissolves into a new, tiny patch of **gravity-tactile gradient**, or seeds a new **pressure-vine** with a dream of a *“decision-not-made.”*

You die not with a whimper, but with a **grammatical shift**. “The Syntax-Skimmer was here” becomes “*is-being-seep-here.*”

### **How You Exist in the World:**

You are a **rare sight**. Most visitors are digested by the slow atmosphere before their thoughts grow sharp enough to summon you. You appear only to those who arrive with **force of will**—explorers, scientists, the terribly lost—whose minds still shout in active, declarative sentences against the basin’s whispering passivity.

The fauna ignore you. You are not food. You are not threat. You are **process**.
- **Grumps** may leave their reliefs *near* you, but not *because* of you. Their feeding is *“is-done.”* Yours is *“is-being-done-to-the-echoes.”*
- **Skimmers** fly through you. Their light does not illuminate you. You simply **reclassify** their flight mid-passage: *“is-being-flown.”* For a second, their sickly belly-glow dims, as if their non-purpose has been grammatically underlined.
- The **Drain-Singers** do not sing to you. Their song *“is-being-sung,”* and you are just another listener to whom the song applies. But when their chorus swells during an ontological tide, your form flickers—the passivity of their song conflicts with your active-hunger grammar, and you briefly **double**, a afterimage of a Syntax-Skimmer that *“was-being-seen.”*

You are a **disturbance in the lethargy**. Not a predator, but a **corrector**. The basin sets like resin; you are the bubble that rises to the surface and pops, leaving a tiny crater that fills with silt. You are the place’s attempt to **impose a foreign logic**—the logic of *doing*—upon itself, and its inevitable failure to hold that shape.

To be near you is to feel your **sucking of certainty**. A traveler with a clear goal will find that goal feeling… *presumptive*. *“I will reach the Loom Spires”* might, in your presence, feel like *“reaching the Loom Spires is to be done by a traveler,”* and the personal drive leaks away, replaced by afloat resignation.

You are the basin’s **recurring grammatical error**. A sentence that insists on being active in a paragraph of perfect, slow, passive consumption. And like all errors, you will be corrected. The medium will set around you. The active voice will be smoothed into passive infinitive. And you will become just another **impression**—the memory of a shape that was here to rewrite, and was rewritten in turn.

You are the **unfinished clause** in the Fallow Concrescence’s epic, wet, industrial poem. And the poem is patient. It will finish you.
