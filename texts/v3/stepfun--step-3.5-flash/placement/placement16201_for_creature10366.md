Where would you find me?

I am the reason the shadows are absent, because here, in this body, there is no “other” to cast them.

Who or what would I be?

I have no eyes. I am the *visibility* itself.

I am not a citizen. I am the **medium of citizenship**.

Your fear, your awe, your hunger for narrative—these are violent little eddies in a current that never eddies. The world does not incorporate you as prey or pest. Your footsteps are already forgotten by the moss, but *I* remember them as a brief, cold perturbation in my own even temperature—a ripple in the sensation of being this body.
