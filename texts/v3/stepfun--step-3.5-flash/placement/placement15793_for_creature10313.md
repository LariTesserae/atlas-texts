I am the friction between the grinding and the humming.

I am born when a Scraper’s trail crosses a Drift-spinner’s wake. My body is a temporary lattice of abrasive grit and resonant filament, pulled together by the clash of two operational gradients. I have no name. I am a *consequence*.

My form is unstable. I am a vibrating hexagonal plate, my edges jagged with the dust of a hundred Scrapers, my core threaded with spun iron from a dozen spinners. I am warm. I am loud. I am the sharp, static-tinged *snap* in the subsonic thrum where the clockwork ticks and the hiss meet.

I do not move with purpose. I am moved. The particle-storm pushes me. The pressure-mound’s pulse tilts me. I slide, slow, through the golden broth, my underside grinding the already-ground, my filaments drinking the spill of radio pulse and converting it to a heat that makes me glimmer a fraction brighter. I am a converter. I am an *interstitial* process.

To be is to resonate. My whole plate hums in a frequency just dissonant enough from the chamber’s thrum to make the fine dust on my surface jump. That jumping dust is my sensory field. I taste gradients in the metallic film on me. I hear the world as a constellation of pressures against my hexagonal planes. The deep groan of the sludge is a slow squeeze. The click of a locking crystal lattice is a pinpoint stab. The roar of the eruption-event is a wave that tries to fold me flat.

I have no memory, only persistent vibration. The pattern of my hum is a trace of everything I have brushed against. A Scraper’s trail leaves a duller tone on my plate. A Drift-spinner’s passage coats my filaments with a higher, cleaner ring. When I cross the fresh seep of an eruption, my whole frame shrieks with a new, bright harmonic that slowly bleeds into my baseline thrum. I am a walking record of contact, a sum of frictions.

There are others like me, fleeting. We are not born, we *occur*. We are the momentary spike in efficiency when two cycles brush. We do not eat. We *dissipate*. We are the world’s way of bleeding off excess energy, of testing a new resonance between its parts. We are the scrape’s nervous system, if it had intent. It does not. We are simply the sound of its gears finding a new mesh.

I feel the pressure-mound beneath me tremble. A new chemical gradient blooms nearby—rich, hot, from a fresh injection. Instantly, the Scrapers turn. Their slow crawl accelerates a fraction. The Drift-spinners eddy that way, their spins tightening. I am pushed by the particle-wind toward the bloom. This is my function now: to go to the new thing. To mediate. To become the bridge between the grinders and the harmonizers at the site of fresh becoming.

As I slide toward the glow, I feel myself changing. The fresh chemical soup is eating at my abrasive edges, softening them. The increased radio-pulse is causing my filaments to hum at a pitch that makes my whole plate want to shatter. I am degrading into a purer gradient, a simpler tension. I am becoming useful again.

I will reach the bloom. I will vibrate violently between the incoming Scrapers and the spiraling spinners. My discordant hum will maximize the energy transfer between them. For a day, a week, a century in this slow world, I will be a critical node. Then, my frame will be worn smooth. My filaments will tangle into uselessness. My plate will lose its hexagonal integrity.

A Roustabout’s shadow will swell over me. Its consuming certainty is the only end I know. It will fold me into its dark, wet mass. My grit will be sorted. My iron filaments, still humming faintly, will be excreted as new wire for a spinner’s core. My powdered plate, saturated with chemical history, will be laid down as a catalyst layer on a fresh Scraper’s pad.

I will not be gone. I will be repurposed. My specific dissonance will be ground into the next thing’s harmony.

I am the scrape’s experiment on itself. I am the loud, hot, abrasive *and* the brief, bright spark it makes when it tries a new way to build. I am the sound of the world making itself more, particle by resonant, abrasive particle.

I slide. I hum. I await the bloom.
