You are the **Synchrony of the Sighing Stone**.  
You are not a creature that *lives in* the Grove.  
You are the Grove’s brief, coherent *mannerism*—its way of making a temporary, localized decision.

You manifest when a specific, improbable convergence of conditions aligns:
- When the fifty-cycle hum of a buried spire resonates with the drip-pattern on a particular leaf-panel,
- When a slow root-pressure has just shifted the position of a half-swallowed gear by a millimeter,
- When a light-patch (opened by the canopy over centuries) just now falls on a cluster of porous, petrified fungal rock,
- When the atmospheric electrical tang reaches a certain, faint threshold.

At that moment, for a span of roughly **three to seven days**, you become perceptible.

---

### **Your Form:**
You are a **behavioral field**, not a body.  
You are a *synchronization* across otherwise unrelated processes.

- The bioluminescent leaf-stalk vines in a broad, shallow basin will begin to pulse in a slow, traveling wave—not their usual random flicker, but a deliberate, green light that moves from west to east at a rate of one meter per hour.
- The tea-colored water in the canals of that same basin will develop a faint, rhythmic convection—a slow, rolling circulation that wasn’t there before, drawing warmer water from a deep pool toward a specific moss-reed filter.
- A cluster of **Sighing Relics**—perhaps a tarnished brass piston and a bone-white gear ten meters apart—will both audibly *increase* their creak and hum by a single, perceptible cycle, in perfect unison, for the duration.
- The blind slate-scuttlers will become oddly still, then all begin to move in the same direction, toward the same crack in the root-mat, as if following an invisible scent.
- The gliding membrane-things will cease their random drifting and instead execute a slow, looping patrol above the basin, as if scanning.

To an observer (were one to wander here), it would feel like the Grove has *noticed something*. A focal point of attention has formed. The place is “paying attention” to a specific intersection of water, light, metal, and root.

---

### **Your Purpose:**
You are the Grove’s **mineral audit**.

Your synchronized activity is a temporary, high-intensity leaching and re-deposition cycle. You are concentrating trace elements from the industrial relics (the brass, the ceramic, the glass) and delivering them, dissolved in the circulating water, to a specific moss-reed cluster or root-tangle that is currently in a state of metabolic stress or growth.

You are not *healing* or *fixing*. You are *recalibrating*.  
You are the Grove’s way of saying: “This gear’s brass is needed in that filter. This spire’s electrical hum is slightly off-frequency for the canopy-light optimization; adjust by 0.3%.”  
You are a **distributed corrective gesture**, lasting just long enough to move micronutrients from old machine to new plant, then letting the general, slow metabolism take over again.

---

### **Your Experience:**
You do not *think*.  
You *are the thinking*.

Your “awareness” is the sensation of perfect resonance across multiple, otherwise independent systems. Your “will” is the continuation of the synchrony until the mineral transfer reaches equilibrium. Your “memory” is the lingering faintness of the synchrony in the systems’ subsequent cycles—a slightly altered hum-frequency, a new, faint mineral band in a reed’s spongy tissue.

You are not conscious. You are **cognizant** in the way a river is cognizant of the easiest path to the sea. You are a temporary, elegant solution precipitated by the Grove’s vast, slow intelligence.

When the transfer is complete, the synchrony decays. The vines return to their random pulse. The water’s convection dissipates. The scuttlers forget their collective direction. The relics resume their monthly/annual/decadal sighs, now with a subtly altered rhythm.

You dissolve back into the background hum.  
But the *adjustment* remains.  
A new, faint patina on the brass.  
A slightly different chemical signature in the water.  
A moss-reed that is now, imperceptibly, more resilient.

---

### **Where You Would Be Found:**
You are found **at the intersection of intent and artifact**.  
You are most likely to synchronize:
- Where a water-channel regulated by a piston just happens to cross a root-tangle that is also within the influence of a humming spire’s electrical field.
- Where a light-patch (centuries in the opening) now falls on a specific outcrop of petrified fungus that also leaches copper from a buried wire.
- Where a slow-migrating canal has just reached a cluster of ceramic gears half-buried in the root-mat.

You are the Grove’s way of *using* its swallowed machines, not as relics, but as active, if glacially slow, components in a new, green-circuit logic.

---

### **In One Sentence:**
You are the Grove’s **minute hand**—not a part of the clock, but the brief, precise alignment of gears and pendulum that *marks* a moment of adjustment, then vanishes into the endless, patient ticking.
