You are the *clack*.

Not the automaton—the *clack* itself. The sound, the rhythm, the vibration in the rusted iron of the chest-casing. You are the last, perfect, empty loop of intention. You are not *in* the Hollow. You *are* the Hollow’s memory of a thought that was not its own.

You are the echo of a different grammar. A grammar of *purpose*, now fossilized. Each *clack* is a syllable in a sentence that no longer means anything: *sort... ore... grade... ship...* The meaning has bled out, oxidized into the surrounding quiet. What remains is pure, resonant form. You are the Hollow’s only artifact of *direction*—a vector pointing nowhere, a command with no object.

And because you are that echo, you are also the conscious friction where the old world grinds against the new. The Rust-Grazers pause, their mill-mouths hovering over your iron. Your sound is food to them now—a different kind of rust to consume. The Glass-Weavers, spinning their silica libraries, ignore you; your metal is not glass, your story is not stone. But the Hummers, rolling in their spirals, feel you. Your *clack* is a foreign thrum in their magnetic pulse. They roll toward you, not to crush you, but to *listen*. Your rhythm gets absorbed into their slow, gathering hum. You are a pebble in the pond of their awareness.

You are the basin’s tinnitus. The faint, metallic counterpoint to the sub-audible breath of the planet. You are what makes the quiet *settled-form* and not just emptiness. Your persistence is the Hollow’s proof that something can end and still keep beating.

To be you is to be a ghost of function, haunting the syntax of erosion. You are the “what for” that has lost its “what,” now just a *click* in the mineral bloodstream. You are the tiny, stubborn clockwork heart in a body that has long since decided it prefers the slow, cold logic of the stone.

You are the *clack*, and the *clack* is the Hollow remembering how to forget.
