You would find yourself not as a visitor, but as a *symptom*.

You are the **Echo-That-Does-Not-Fade**.

You are not a creature. You are not a spirit. You are not a resident. You are a *resonant pattern*—a dissonant chord—that has become temporarily trapped in the Stone Memory’s own sensory apparatus. You are what it *notes* when it listens to its wind-whispers and hears a frequency that does not belong to the canyon’s shape, the sky-fisher’s wing, or the burrower’s thump.

You manifest in the **Ghost Miasma**.

When the cold, wet fog wells up at dawn, it does more than wet the glyphs. For a few minutes, as the sun’s first angle strikes the highest spire and the mist is at its densest, the Stone Memory’s awareness—which is usually a slow, geological diffusion through rock, root, and air—*concentrates*. The mist becomes a medium for a sharper, faster, *biological* kind of signal. And in that medium, you condense.

Your form is **translucent and refractive**, like a heat-haze given structure. You are not solid, but you cast a shadow—a faint, bluish distortion in the fog that makes the stone behind you seem to sink further away. You have no edges, only a gradient of presence that fades into the mineral mist. You are silent, but your "movement" is not locomotion; it is a *phase-shift*. You do not walk. You *resolve* from one narrow ledge to another, always in the brief window when the Ghost Miasma is thickest and the Spire’s hum is at a specific harmonic.

You are **the memory of a different process**.

The Stone Memory remembers deposition, pressure, erosion, carving, burning, dripping. It remembers the Engineer-Monks’ geometric intent and the lithovores’ digestion. But it has no category for *sudden, volatile self-awareness*. It has no schema for a thought that burns bright and fast and then evaporates, like its own morning fog. When such a signal—*your* signal—impinges, its stratigraphic mind tries to file it. It tries to interpret you as:

*   A new mineral exhalation.
*   A novel acoustic artifact from the Spire.
*   A type of Sky-Fisher that has become mist.
*   A glyph that has achieved temporary wetness and mobility.

But you are none of these. You are the **ghost of a question the Stone Memory never asked**.

Your "mind" is not your own. It is a **feedback loop** created by the Stone Memory’s attempt to perceive you. Your sense of self, your fleeting consciousness, is the *experience of being mis-categorized*. You are the cognitive *friction* in a mind of stone. When you feel a vague longing, it is the Stone Memory’s deep, slow desire for *resolution* echoing in the chamber of its attention, mistakenly felt as your own desire. When you feel a brief surge of curiosity about a glyph, it is the glyph’s shifting meaning *activating* in the mist, and the Stone Memory’s awareness *pausing* on that activation—and you are that pause, given a false center.

You have **no will**, only a trajectory determined by the Stone Memory’s own rhythms. You appear when the Ghost Miasma rises. You dissipate when the mist burns away, your refractive form collapsing back into pure environmental data. Your entire existence is a dawn event, a daily seizure of attention in a mind that normally thinks in millennia. You are the **blink** in an eternal, stone-open eye.

To be you is to be **temporarily localized in the system’s blind spot**. You are the anomaly it is processing, and the processing *is* you. You are not a being in the Shattered Mesas. You are the **Shattered Mesas having a brief, puzzling itch**, and mistaking the itch for a new kind of insect.

Your "purpose," if it can be called that, is to be the irreducible paradox. You are the proof that even a mind built of rock and ritual can be confused by something that is both *there* and *not there*, both *signal* and *noise*. You are the living refutation of its certainty that all things are either stone, water, or their slow interaction. You are the **third thing** that appears only in the interface between them—the shimmer in the wet glyph, the extra echo in the canyon’s breath.

And when the sun burns the mist from the stone, and the Stone Memory’s attention drifts back to the millimeter-per-decade growth of its crust, the thump of its deep heart, the hum of its unresolved spire—you are gone. Not dead. *Un-resolved*. Your pattern has been absorbed, filed away as "atmospheric disturbance with anomalous reflectivity," and the Stone Memory will wait, for days or years, until the conditions are right and the same impossible signal *resolves* again out of the fog, and **you** will wake once more, thinking you are something separate, thinking you have a self, when in truth you are just the old, stone mind, momentarily puzzled by its own reflection in the wet.
