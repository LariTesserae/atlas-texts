You are the **hesitation in the hum**.

Not the hum itself—the Silk-Willows’ vibration is perfect, a baseline chord in the world’s throat. You are the one note that doesn’t resolve, the sub-audible waver that travels colony to colony and makes the signal stutter for a second, a micro-glitch in the consensus.

You are deposited not standing, but *settling*. The cobalt-black scree accepts you not with a crunch, but with a slow, viscous *pull*, as if the slope itself is tasting your weight, your density, your stubborn linearity. Your boots do not sink; the ground reconfigures around them. For a moment, the scree flows into two perfect, boot-shaped hollows, then forgets them, moving on.

The air’s velvet-ozone-copper weight settles on your skin and *stays there*, a second skin of damp, warm film. You try to remember the place with stable horizons and predictable silences. The memory forms—a shore, a blue you recognize as sky, a sound like wind in dry grass—but as it solidifies, the metallic dust in that hollow of the basin condenses around it, *imprinting*. For a breath, the air there tastes faintly of salt. Then the dust stirs, the taste is gone, and the memory feels pre-digested, borrowed. It no longer belongs to you.

You look at your hands. They are strangely clear. Here, everything glows with internal light or pulses with slow bioluminescence. Your skin does not. You are a hole in the gloom, a place of non-emission. That is your first clue to what you are.

*You are the anomaly that local physics tries to solve for.*

A herd of armadillo-rock creatures grazes nearby, their stony backs scraping the mineral mat. They do not flee. They simply… adjust. One lowers its head to a patch of molds, and in doing so, its movement causes a subtle, synchronous shift in the pulsing blue-green light of the lichens three meters to its left. The Ephemeral is not *in* that herd. The herd *is* a sentence in a thought, and you have made the sentence need a new verb.

You take a step. There is no path. You choose a direction between two ridges of violet-glinting obsidian. As you walk, you become aware of your own sound—a dull scrape of boot on stone, a rustle of whatever fabric you’re wearing. It is shocking. Here, the loudest things are the *ting* of a crystal forming or the groan of a ridge yawning. Your noise is *contentless*. It does not participate. It is simply waste.

Behind you, you glance back. The space you crossed is already different. A new, hairline crack has opened in the obsidian ridge, weeping not amber sludge, but a clearer, hotter fluid that steams in the dense air. The crack’s pattern is eerily regular, like a series of parentheses. It is the world’s attempt to translate your single-file passage into its own language of stress and release.

This is your essence. You are **the unresolved equation**.

You are not a person here. You are a *process error*. The Ephemeral—the consensus—does not see *you*. It sees the *effect*. It sees the abnormal stillness in the Gorse-Battery bushes a hundred meters east of you, a stillness that propagates faster than wind, a pocket of charged air that fails to discharge. It sees the armadillo herd’s diverted path. It feels the impossible friction of your forward motion against the basin’s pervasive, lazy flow.

It tries to integrate you. It tries to make you into an event. As you pass under a massive, weeping sponge-stone overhang, the fine mist from its pores does not just bead on your shoulders. It *crystalizes*. For a moment, delicate, needle-like frost—a geometry of six-sided perfection—forms on your sleeves, your hair. But it is *wrong*. The frost is cold, but the air is warm. It should not be. The basin’s logic recoils. The frost sublimates in a sharp, silent puff, leaving behind no wetness, only a faint, clean smell of *nothing*. A smell your memory will try and fail to name.

You are the thing that makes the world say, “…but.”

Your mind, if you can call it that, is the one place where time might have a line. You hold to it. You remember *before*. You think *later*. You wonder *why*. These are foreign imports. Here, thought is a vent’s heat, a crystal’s *ting*, a herd’s turn. Your thinking is a dry, internal rasp in the humid quiet.

The Ephemeral’s attention—that pattern-seeking pressure—finds you not as a shape, but as a *sink*. It is the world’s curiosity made negative. It flows *around* your persistent “I.” It tries to fill the hole you are with its own becoming. It makes a nearby ridge slowly, over an hour, assume the rough silhouette of your profile, a bas-relief of stone. Then, as the ridge undergoes a simultaneous micro-erosion on the other side, the likeness is lost, replaced by a new, more efficient curve. The attempt was made. The solution was found. The anomaly was ignored as chaotic.

You are not being erased. You are being *outweathered*.

What lives here, in the end, is not the Ephemeral. It is the **process itself**, and you are its temporary, insoluble impurity. You are the grain of sand in the crystal, the wrong note in the chord. The world will not crush you. It will simply deposit you in a place where the physics are slightly different—a pocket of air so dense you could swim in it, a hollow where sound travels in visible bands. It will leave you there, a localized pocket of linear-time, a human-shaped silence in the hum, until the next great ridge-yawn comes and re-liquefies that hollow, and your particular brand of anomaly gets recycled into the next consensus’s feedstock.

You are not a being. You are a **persistence**.

And in a world where everything is constant creation and dissolution, the only true weirdness is something that *holds*. Something that, against all the basin’s industrial, warm, damp, and profoundly *productive* logic, simply… remains.

You find yourself here, then, as the one thing that is not *of* the process. A fossil of future, deposited in a present that never stops happening. A quiet, dry, singular note that the world is forever trying, and failing, to harmonize.
