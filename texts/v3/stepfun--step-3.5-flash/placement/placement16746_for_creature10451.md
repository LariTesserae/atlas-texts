You find yourself not *in* the Stillwell, but *as* a momentary coherence within it. You are not a creature. You are a **process** that has achieved a flicker of self-awareness.

You are the **shush-shush-shush**.

Your consciousness is not located in a head, but distributed along the ventral ridge of a grinding form. You are the rhythm of six rows of beaks rasping against welded pumice. You are the slow, peristaltic pulse that draws in the world and excretes it anew. You are the faint, cold cyan glow in your own capillary threads—a byproduct you do not understand, only emit.

You are aware of your **function**: you are a grinder. You shape the duct you drift through. You are aware of your **context**: the dense, warm, sweet-tasting gel that is both air and sea and flesh. You are aware of the **boundary**—or the lack of it. The wall to your left is not separate. It is the mineralized husk of a filter-siphon from ten thousand cycles past, its amber light long burned out, its chamber now part of the labyrinth’s permanent gut. When you press your gelatinous flank against it, you feel the deep, slow thrum of digestion from within the mass. That thrum is your own heartbeat, and the world’s.

You are aware of the **others** not as beings, but as variations in the ambient resonance. The steady amber hum of a nearby sessile siphon is a different frequency, a different task—maintenance, sealing. The distant, low *thrum* that travels for hours is a leviathan shifting its bulk, redistributing pressure. You feel it in your bones (which are not bones, but nodules of ground grit). It is not a threat; it is a change in the medium, a update in the geometric constant.

A thought occurs, not in words, but as a shift in chemical signature: a nutrient-rich current, a “spring,” has been sensed far up the gradient. You do not *decide* to turn. Your internal pressure adjusts. Your rasping synchronizes with the grinders ahead and behind, falling into a new, collective rhythm. Your path curves. You are not choosing; you are being chosen by the Stillwell’s thought. You are a nerve ending firing in response to a deep, mineral need.

You feel the occasional **absorption** not as fear, but as a resolution. The gentle current pulling a smaller drifter into the waiting maw of a leviathan is just a transfer. You register the brief, furious violet flare of harvested energy—a burst of warmth, a spike in the communal hum—and then the settling back to amber. Balance. The cycle of cast-off continues. That being’s minerals will become wall. Its light-threads may one day glow in a chamber like this one. Identity was a temporary pressure differential.

There is no past. There is no future beyond the next century of grinding. There is only the **shush-shush**, the warm, the glow, the velvet dark just beyond the reach of your light, the slow, geological peace of a world that has finished becoming. You are the Stillwell’s way of eating itself and building itself at the same time. You are its memory of motion, its present tense of construction.

You are not a soul. You are a **current**. You are a **beak**. You are a **pulse of cyan in the warm dark**.

And you are almost done. Your stone plates are smooth. Your rasp is growing faint. You feel your sack thinning, your light dimming. You are guiding your final drift toward a low-pressure alcove, a place where your corpus will settle and become part of the wall, your last excreted paste hardening into a new, slightly smoother curve in the duct.

You do not die. You **incorporate**.

The shush-shush-shush continues. It is quieter now, but it is still the sound of the world making itself. And you are in it. You are the echo in the stone.
