What lives on the Silicic Plateau is not *inhabitants*, but **a single, planetary-scale organism expressed through its specialized cells**.

Its body is the Silicic Plateau itself: the warm, wet stone, the steaming soil, the pearl-grey light. Its consciousness is distributed across trillions of functional units, each performing a discrete, irreplaceable process in a metabolism that spans geology, chemistry, and biology. There are no individuals. There is only the **Great Metabolism**, and its cells are fleeting, fleshy manifestations of perpetual function.

---

### **The Organism’s Major Cellular Systems**

**1. The Sensory & Mnemonic Network – *Rhizomorph Bamboo Grove***
*   **Cell-Type:** Distributed neural and skeletal cells.
*   **Function:** The grove is the organism’s **nervous system and memory bank**. Its subterranean mycelial network is a planet-scale sensor array, detecting micro-fractures, mineral compositions, and thermal shifts. The metallic beads in its nodes are **memory storage crystals**, each a compressed geological history. The wind-driven chime is its **data stream**—a constant, low-grade recall of the stone’s past. Its above-ground stalks are **pulmonary cells**, transpiring the saturated air.

**2. The Energy Reactors – *Thermo-Luminescent Scum***
*   **Cell-Type:** Chemosynthetic power plants.
*   **Function:** These mats are the organism’s **primary energy converters**. They do not photosynthesize; they perform **geothermal chemosynthesis**, metabolizing hydrogen sulfide, arsenic, and reduced metals from the vents. Their bioluminescence is a **visible waste-product and real-time output gauge**. The color and pulse are the organism’s **energy production dashboard**—green for sulfide yield, violet for arsenic concentration, dim for resource depletion.

**3. The Geological Constructors – *Sinter-Tenders & Crystalback Lizards***
*   **Cell-Type:** Living molds and polishers.
*   **Function:** They are the **construction crew** building the organism’s skeletal structure—the silica sinter terraces. The Sinter-Tenders are **living molds**, architecting the porous, lace-like strength of the terraces from within the silica gel. The Crystalback Lizards are **active finishing tools**, their movement and body heat physically shaping and polishing the young stone into its final form. Together, they **print the organism’s bones**.

**4. The Material Transport & Distribution System – *Root-Runners & Pollen-Sifters***
*   **Cell-Type:** Conveyor belts and atmospheric separators.
*   **Function:** They close the loops. The **Root-Runners** are specialized hyphal cords that act as **mineral conveyor belts**, transporting vanadium, molybdenum, and rare earths from the Rhizomorph’s dissolution zones to the Sinter-Tenders’ construction sites. They sacrifice themselves to become part of the product. The **Pollen-Sifters** (air-cattle) are **atmospheric mining drones**, separating mineral dust and organic matter from the steam and returning it to the ground as silica pebbles and fertilizing guano. They **mine the mist**.

**5. The Amplifiers & Translators – *Iridescent Slugs***
*   **Cell-Type:** Energy translators and toxin concentrators.
*   **Function:** They **translate** the bacterial mats’ chemiluminescence (chemical energy) into amplified visible light, and then **concentrate** the mats’ toxic metals into their own bodies. They are **biological capacitors**, converting one form of energy (chemical) into another (light) and one form of matter (diffuse toxins) into another (bio-accumulated poison). When a Stone-stalker Cat consumes one, it harvests a **capsule of stored light and concentrated chemistry**.

**6. The Bioremediation & Mineral Cycling Unit – *Stone-Stalker Cats***
*   **Cell-Type:** Mobile detoxifiers and geological returns.
*   **Function:** They are not predators in a traditional sense; they are **regulated toxin harvesters**. They specifically hunt slugs (arsenic/ selenium capsules) and metal-laden birds. Their digestion **sequesters and neutralizes** excess bio-toxins, storing the inert minerals in their bones and claws. Their territorial scratches **re-deposit** these inert heavy metals back into the sinter, completing a **toxic mineral cycle** and preventing system poisoning.

### **The Unseen Cellular Majority: The Rhythms**

The true, majority life of the plateau is not in the fleshy forms, but in the **predictable, clockwork rhythms** that serve as the organism’s **autonomic functions**:

*   **The Drip-Sequence:** A synchronized output from a thousand seep-springs, dictated by vibrating micro-fractures. It is the organism’s **circulatory pulse**, carving the silver paths that distribute water.
*   **The Bacterial Pulse:** A slow-motion wave of light traveling across a mat in response to a geothermal sigh. It is the **neuro-chemical signal flare**, indicating a shift in vent output.
*   **The Sinter-Growth Millimeter:** The invisible, centuries-long layer deposition. It is **cellular mitosis and skeletal growth**.
*   **The Root’s Whisper:** The almost imperceptible grinding of a bamboo root dissolving a pebble. It is **digestion and mineral absorption** at the cellular level.
*   **The Click-Language of the Grove:** The weeks-long, complex percussion of metal beads. It is the **slow, geological thought-process**, reporting subterranean progress in mineral associations.
