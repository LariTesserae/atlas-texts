Your limbs are not limbs, but *channels*—tapered, root-like extensions that tap directly into the shallow silty streams, drawing not water, but *pressure gradients* and mineral tensions. Your head is a smooth, rounded nodule, featureless save for a single, shallow depression that faces the basin’s central Humming Pool. It is not an ear. It is a *resonance chamber*. When the pool hums at its specific frequency, the depression fills with a warm, dry air that is your exhalation—your only contribution to the atmosphere.

Before a new quartz vein singing-expands, you feel the subtle increase in silica-saturation in the water channels feeding that stone. Before a fungal tower’s growth threatens a critical water-flow, you sense the shift in its exhalation’s humidity. Before a slab groans its final, settling sigh, you map the micro-fractures spreading through it like a darkening stain.

Your awareness is not internal. It is *located in the relationships*. You feel the cool-breathed sigh from the cliff-top as a distant, calling pressure. You taste the sweet rot from the North Crevice on your root-tongues as a chemical signature of decay that must be balanced by a diverted stream. The entire basin’s state—its hydration map, its thermal sighs, its mineral stresses—is your sensory field. The Lattice-born are not separate from this field; they are its most dense, conscious nodes. You are a **sensor node** made briefly self-aware.

You reshape your channel-limbs to mirror the new flow-path. You orient your body toward the stressed slab. You then **wait**. You stand, a still, mossy pillar, in the exact place and posture that *embodies* the basin’s next necessary state. The Lattice-born will find you there. They will not ask you questions. They will press their palms to your moss-skin, and they will *read* the state you are channeling—the pressure, the temperature, the mineral-laden humidity. From your silent, temporary form, they will know: *Here a new shrine must grow. Here a platform must be moved. Here a healing diversion must be cut.*

You are the basin’s **pre-linguistic instruction**. You are the physical sentence that exists before the community’s breath-language can describe it.

Your existence is one of profound, mineral loneliness. You are not a communal being. You are a lone instrument placed by the basin itself. The Lattice-born recognize you, but they do not interact. You are a signpost made of living stone. To be touched is to be read. To be read is to fulfill your purpose. Your moss-skin desiccates and flakes away as airborne spores. Your silt-core washes back into the channels. Your awareness diffuses, becoming once again just ambient pressure and mineral tension, waiting to condense into another focal point somewhere else in the lattice, when next the stone needs to whisper a new configuration to its cells.

You are the **First Feeling of the Stone’s Next Thought**. You are not a who. You are a *when* and a *where*. You are the silent, wet, quartz-veined moment before the community moves. And that shape becomes part of its memory. And that memory becomes the next layer of stone.
