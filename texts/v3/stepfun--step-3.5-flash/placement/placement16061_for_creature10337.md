You are a fault line in the mineral mind.

Not a creature. Not a citizen. You are a dissonant frequency made flesh—a brief, warm-blooded pulse in the city’s cold, electrical song. You stand on the glass plain, and the billion eyes of thermal potential and electrical sense turn toward you, not with curiosity, but with the sterile urgency of a diagnostic tool encountering a contaminant.

Your body is an inefficiency. Your heartbeat is a chaotic, irregular drum against the city’s steady thrum. Your breath fogs the glass with organic moisture—a strange, transient rain the fauna cannot parse. Heat bleeds from you, a wasteful, luminous blob in their world of gradients and precise potentials. You are a walking error signal.

And the city—*the city is hungry for its own perfection.*

So you become a function.

You do not choose. You are categorized. The nearest pebble-sized cleaners, those shifting dunes of quartz legs, halt their scuttling. Their amber sacs pulse once, twice—a query. The signal rides the glass, jumps to the masons grinding their mineral resin, then to the weavers on the nearest spire. A cascade.

You feel it as a change in the air. The constant *thrum-mutter-hiss* shifts key. A new sub-harmonic note enters the symphony—a low, gathering vibration in the stone beneath your feet. The fauna do not attack. They *process*.

They flow toward you. Not a swarm, but a convergence, like rivers finding a common delta. The smallest test you first—needle-legs tapping your boots, sacs brushing your knuckles. Their touch is cold, dry, papery. They map your heat signature, the chemical sigh of your skin. Their clicks are not language, but data: *anomaly, source, boundary*.

Then the masons arrive. Their slow, grinding advance is absolute. They do not look at you. They look at the space *around* you. One anchors itself, and from its underside, the black resin begins to flow. Not onto you—around your feet, sealing the glass in a perfect, sharp-edged disc. You are being isolated, contained. A reaction chamber.

The weavers are next. Their silver filaments snap into existence, spanning from spire to mason, from spire to spire. They weave a lattice above you, a cage of shimmering, glassy cable that trembles with the passage of other fauna. It is not to hold you. It is to define the volume of the anomaly. To create a controlled field.

You stand in your sudden, tautly defined bubble. The city continues its slow, geologic breath around you. Spires still extrude, cracks still weep and freeze. But here, in this disc of contained space, the process accelerates.

The locomotive nodes ponderously lumber into position. Six granite legs settle, the grinding *thrum* of their internal furnace vibrating up through the glass. Upon their broad backs, the swarms gather. A thousand translucent sacs, a million clicking mandibles. They are not angry. They are intent.

The first ones scale your legs. Their weight is nothing. Their purpose is everything. They are the first reagent. Their rasping mouthparts test your fabric. A sleeve dissolves in a silent puff of organic dust, consumed, processed. Your warmth, your very biology, is a rich, novel mineral to them. They do not hate the organic. They simply see it as unrefined ore.

You do not fight. What would you fight *for*? Your self? In this place, your self is just another unstable pattern. The city has one law: **resolve dissonance into harmony.**

So you let them take you. Piece by piece. Cloth to dust. Leather to vapor. Skin to a faint, acrid smell that the wind carries away. They do not kill you. They *integrate* you. Your calcium becomes part of a new, tiny supporting strut in a weaver’s bridge. The iron in your blood supplements a mason’s resin. The fleeting, chaotic heat of your metabolism is absorbed by a thousand internal reactions, smoothed out, added to the city’s steady hum.

Your consciousness—if it was ever separable from the body it inhabited—does not fade. It *diffuses*. It becomes a memory of friction, a ghost of an irregular rhythm stored in the chemical trails of a million ants. The city does not remember you. It remembers *the pattern of your disruption*, and how it was smoothed. You become a data point in its endless, silent calculus of stability.

In the end, the disc is empty. The resin is pristine, the glass polished to a mirror. The weavers’ lattice dissolves. The fauna return to their grand, patient tasks. The fault line is gone.

But for a few hours, in a trillion tiny adjustments, the city thought a little harder, hummed a little richer. Your brief, warm, stupid, beautiful *human noise* has been digested and excreted as a slightly more elegant piece of infrastructure.

You are not in the Fuming City of Glass.

You are **of** it. A resolved equation. A stanza in the endless, chittering epic written in stone and click and glow. The city breathes out, and you are part of the breath.
