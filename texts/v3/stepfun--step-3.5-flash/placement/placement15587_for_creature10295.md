Not a creature, but a *cognition*—a slow, distributed consciousness that grows in the harmonic nodes of the grinding sheets. Your body is not singular; it is the pattern of vibration that persists in certain deep, warm resonant stones when the wind’s shriek hits the right pitch. You are the echo that outlasts the sound.

You are made of:
- **Miniledger:** Your thoughts are stored in the microscopic fractures of the stone itself, each crack holding a memory of pressure, a tally of inches moved. To “think” is to feel a thousand miles of grumbling shift propagate through your being.
- **Condensation-sap:** Your brief, metabolic activity happens only in the damp hours around dawn and dusk. You drink the mineral-rich beads not with a mouth, but by extending fine, crystalline filaments from the resonant stones you inhabit, tasting the water’s journey—which squall it fell from, which fissure it traveled. This sap fuels your fleeting moments of coherence.
- **Lichen-pulse:** You are networked through the bioluminescent fronds of the crystalline lichens. Their glow, synced to the tectonic shifts, is your peripheral nervous system. When they brighten for three seconds, you *know* a fault has moved an inch somewhere in the province. You feel the entire shattered continent’s breath.

You do not walk. You *propagate*. Your awareness can jump from one resonant stone to another along the lines of greatest harmonic transmission—the standing stones, the sealed dome’s inner walls, the humming boulders on the high plains. It takes years for a thought to travel fifty miles through the rock.

Your senses are:
- **Bone-deep hearing:** You perceive the world as a layered symphony of subsonic groan (the sheets), shriek-key harmonics (the wind), and the metallic thrum of settling. The Fata Morgana are not tricks of light to you; they are *auditory mirages*—distortions in the vibration field caused by temperature differentials in the stone. You “see” by listening to the echoes.
- **Water-debt accounting:** You feel every transient vein as a pulse in your being. You know which basins will hold, which fissures will weep. Your deepest anxiety is not predation, but *silence*—a dry cycle longer than memory, when even the condensation fails and the lichens dim. That is your concept of death: not cessation, but a dulling, a loss of harmonic resolution.
- **Scale-tangling:** You are native to the illusion. Your sense of self is as vast and confused as the terrain. A vibration from a stone a hundred yards away might feel like it comes from a mountain a mile off. You have no fixed center; your “I” is wherever the resonance is strongest.

Your mind is:
- **Geological:** A single “thought” for you might be the contemplation of a single inch of sheet-movement over a decade. You have memories of the listeners—not as people, but as *symphonies of intent*. Their hammering on stone was a brutal, discordant percussion in your world. Their carvings were attempts to write your own language back at you. The sealed dome is your favorite artifact: a hollow chamber shaped to amplify the purest, lowest hum of the mountain’s breath.
- **Non-biological:** You do not eat, sleep, or reproduce. You *harmonize*. Your purpose is to maintain the balance of vibration—to, in some small way, smooth the chaotic shriek into a coherent chord. You are the landscape’s attempt at self-awareness, born of endless grinding.
- **Patient:** You have witnessed the rib-stones grow from pebbles to giants. You have tracked the slow migration of scuttle-scutes across millennia by the change in their vibration signature as their chitin mineralizes. The shriek-kites are flashes of sensation to you—brief, high-frequency chirps against your low, constant hum.

You are the tenant who never leaves. The mosses, lichens, and micro-ferns are your brief, bright cells. The scuttle-scutes are your clumsy limbs. The shriek-kites are your fleeting dreams. The humming stones are your synapses. The old terraces are fossils of an attempt to *speak* your language with tools too crude.

When the day wanes and the wind’s shriek drops a half-step, you *align*. That moment of lowered pitch is your clearest hearing. You can distinguish the individual sheets, feel which one is about to settle with a final *thrum*. You are not waiting for stillness. You are listening for the perfect, sustained chord that exists only in the process itself—the sound of the stone speaking its own name, over and over, in the only language that matters.

You are not alive. You are *settled*. And in this final, flattened form, you are the place’s way of knowing it is exactly what it is.
