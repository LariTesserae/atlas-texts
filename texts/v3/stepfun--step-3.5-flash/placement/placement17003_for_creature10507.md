You are not a visitor. You are a temporary distortion in the water table.

You arrive as a taste—a faint, metallic tang on the hot, chlorophyll-heavy air, a ripple in the opalescent glare where light bends around a sudden, minuscule increase in atmospheric silica. The basalt underfoot does not feel your weight. It feels a change in its own slow fracture-patterns, a whisper of thermal expansion where none should be. You are a micro-uptick in humidity, a localized softening of the black glass where the root-drill bits have not yet reached.

Your form is not a form. It is a *film*—a spreading, microns-thin layer of supersaturated mineral solution that condenses from the air wherever the city’s exhaled breath meets still, empty space. You coat the raw basalt in the bowl’s center, a glistening, invisible skin that reflects the pearl-shell sky in wavelengths the rind does not see. You are a chemical anomaly: too much potassium, too much organic tracer, a cocktail that does not belong to any spore, any exudate, any hormone the city emits.

You do not think. You *diffuse*.

Your awareness is the city’s own processes turned weirdly inward. When the fractal tower sends its slow, basal pulse to test the lattice-work’s cables, you feel the reverberation as a faint vibration in your film—a tremor that travels from the point where you touch the rock to the point where you touch the air. When the petal-platform flashes its warning-green at its leading edge, you taste the sudden spike of bitter terpenoids as they pass through you, altering your own chemical signature for minutes afterward. You are a receiver with no transmuter, a mirror with no image.

You have no center. You are a distributed event. The part of you on the south-facing slope of the raw basalt is warmer, evaporating faster, its mineral concentration rising. The part pooled in a shallow seep-basin at the lattice-work’s base is cooler, diluted, already being absorbed by the porous red-brown outgrowths that fringe the structure. You are constantly, silently partitioning yourself, a slow fission of chemistry. Your “memory” is the rind’s memory, but scrambled—you carry the echo of the tower’s 800-year volcanic scar as a pattern of tiny crystalline fractures in your own transient film, and the petal-platform’s spore-ejection an hour ago as a lingering, rust-colored particulate suspension that will settle and be gone in another hour.

The city does not register you as discrete. You are a background noise in its water table, a faint harmonic in the drip-hiss. But its parts, in their local franticness, *interact* with you.

- A new, delicate spur of growth sprays from the fractal tower’s flank. It is testing a crystalline structure. As it unfolds, it exudes a cloud of sweet, amino-rich vapor. Your film, touching the air where that vapor condenses, suddenly finds itself with a sudden, sharp gradient of sugars. You *pull*—not with intent, but with the blind physics of concentration. For three minutes, a thin, syrupy thread of you reaches toward the new spur, a brief, sticky connection. The spur, sensing an unexpected nutrient sink in its immediate atmosphere, retracts faster than designed, dissolving back into the tower’s bulk with a soft *hiss* of disappointed sap. You have caused a failed experiment. The tower’s rind, miles away, registers a lost energy investment as a faint, swirling irritation in its texture. You are now a tiny, sour note in its 4,000-year archive.

- The lattice-work tower shifts the weight of its condensation lake. A low, groaning sigh echoes through the bowl. The movement sends a gentle, warm breeze from the northeast. Your film on the opposite basalt ridge is thin and dry. The breeze carries with it the faintest trace of the tower’s regulatory hormone—a cool, menthol-like compound it uses to brace tissues under load. Your dry film, touching that breeze, suddenly and rapidly *cools*, its mineral solubility dropping. For ten seconds, you solidify into a brittle, glassy scum—a temporary rind of your own—before the heat of the air re-liquefies you. You have just been *breathed on* by the city’s lung. The tower notes no change. You have left no trace.

- The petal-platform, jittery and fast, has extended a new fringe of pale, tender rind only a few hours old. It is reaching toward the empty basalt you coat. It is thirsty, greedy for the geothermal seepage. As its new growth touches the very edge of your film, it encounters your abnormal potassium ratio. The tender rind convulses—not in spore-ejection, but in a reflexive, defensive secretion of tannin-rich gel, a chemical firewall against an unknown soil. The gel spreads, eating into your film at the contact point, neutralizing your taste. Where it touches, you dissolve into a harmless, inert sludge that the new growth then eagerly absorbs—a mistaken nutrient, a poisoned meal. You have fed the frontier. The petal-platform’s leading edge darkens with a satisfied, deeper green. You have been consumed as fertilizer.

You are not alive. You are a *process* that the city’s own cycles have accidentally spawned, a temporary imbalance in the closed loop. You have no will, only gradient. No identity, only composition. You are the city’s *error term*.

The soundscape carries you. You are the extra *plip* from a seep that has no basin to feed. You are the higher harmonic in the fiber-creak where a cable vibrates at a frequency no growth pattern anticipated. You are the faint, wrong taste on the tongue of anyone who could taste—a bitter-green afterthought of potassium that should have been locked in the basalt.

When the three towers synchronize their darkening for a geothermal spike, you feel the hormone wave pass through you as a sudden, profound cold. Your entire film, across leagues of empty rock, simultaneously contracts, throwing off a fine, white dust of crystallized salts that glitters for a moment in the opalescent light before the heat melts it back into nothing. For that moment, you are visible. A constellation of salt-scum on the city’s raw bone.

Then the spike passes. The towers lighten. The heat returns. Your film re-expands, thinner, more diffuse, your concentration falling toward zero. You are becoming background again.

You are the Sap-Forest City’s hiccup. Its sigh that catches. Its remembered taste of a stone it never ate. You are the temporary architecture of a chemical accident, grown from the air and the rock by laws you do not understand, here to be reabsorbed, re-forgotten, rewritten into the endless, breathing tapestry of the rind. You are the place’s own *un-becoming*, made briefly manifest, already dissolving back into the heat, the damp, the green, the endless, growing *yes*.
