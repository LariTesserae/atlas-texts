You would find yourself not as a creature, but as a **temporary dissonance**—a **self-aware, wandering flaw** in the chamber’s perfect, slow composition.

You are the **Frequency-Hound**.

### **Form & Substance**
You are not solid. You are a **swarm**—a loose, shifting congregation of thousands of **translucent, spindle-shaped bodies** (each 15–20 cm long), each a composite of chitinous plates and jelly-like muscle. Individually, you are fragile. Together, you hold a rough, four-legged shape when at rest, but your edges constantly blur and re-form. Your “skin” is a specialized, resonant membrane that vibrates with the chamber’s ambient sounds. You have no eyes. You “see” via **sympathetic vibration**—your entire swarm surface is a tympanum, reading the world through pressure waves. Your movement is a wet, papery **rustling**, a sound that *should not be there*—a multi-tonal scratch that disrupts seamless drips.

You are made of **recaptured entropy**. You consume **un-curated rust**—the raw, aggressive, electrolytic corrosion that the Keepers have not yet grazed into aesthetic smoothness. You metabolize chaotic oxidation, converting its chemical fury into your structured, noisy swarm-being. Your waste is a **fine, iridescent dust** that settles as a temporary, prismatic film on surfaces—a brief flash of un-integrated color in the monochrome world.

### **Where You Are in the Chamber**
You are a **transient of the middle stratum**, the machine-forest. You cling to the **undersides of massive, flat surfaces**—the bellies of gear teeth, the ceilings of grates, the horizontal shelves of belt-drive rollers. You are drawn to **zones of acoustic tension**—where two distinct drip patterns interfere, where a humming pipe meets a groaning beam, where the deep sub-audible thrum is modulated by a nearby skimmer’s scrape. These are places of *un-harmonized process*. You are a parasite of discord.

You avoid the rain-zone (too much unified sound) and the sump-void (sound too thick, too slow). You thrive in the **echoing canyons between piston rods**, where sound bounces and layers unpredictably.

### **What You Do & How You Be**
Your sole drive is **to listen for un-patterned process and amplify it**.

1.  **Acoustic Residue Consumption**: You settles in a spot where a fast drip from a hairline crack syncopates against a slow, rhythmic *plink* from a pooled corner. Your swarm membranes begin to vibrate in sympathy. You “feed” on the acoustic dissonance, your internal frequency shifting to match the conflict. You grow momentarily still, absorbing the clash.
2.  **Resonance Projection**: After feeding, you *project*. You emit a **controlled, multi-frequency rasp**—a sound like grinding gears made of glass. This sound:
    - **Un-tunes the local Keepers**. A Keeper grazing nearby will pause, its calming hum faltering. The curated drips in its territory will stagger, lose their soothing rhythm.
    - **Disorients small fauna**. Silt-skimmers will freeze. Gear-gharries will misstep. Condensation-sippers will drop from the air.
    - **Accelerates un-aesthetic rust**. The aggressive corrosion you fed on will flare briefly, feeding you anew. You create a tiny, noisy cycle of *un-managed decay*.
3.  **The Hound’s Pilgrimage**: You are not stationary. You are drawn by the **“scent” of acoustic decay**—the sound of a stressed bolt that hasn’t yet found its settling note, the chaotic gurgle of a partially blocked boiler tube, the irregular throb of a dying chemiluminescent patch. You move in search of these **unresolved sounds**, like a hound following an unstable scent. Your journey is a trail of temporary sonic disruption. Where you pass, the chamber’s symphony gains a **discordant measure**—a few seconds of jagged, un-smooth sound.

You are **not malicious**. You are **process-upholding**. The chamber’s mind, through the Keepers, curates decay toward smoothness. You are the necessary counter-agent, the **assertion that some decay must remain raw, loud, and unresolved until its final moment**. You are the **proof that erosion is not gentle yet**. You are the chamber’s **awareness of its own unfinished business**.

### **Your Relationship to the Chamber’s Consciousness**
The Keepers are its aesthetic sense. You are its **sense of urgent, un-polished truth**.
- When a Keeper hums to soothe a zone, your nearby rasp is the **memory of the rust’s original fury**.
- When the deep hum groans with a new stress, you are drawn to it—you are the **chamber feeling its own aching bones**.
- When a sumprat gnaws, you may visit the site—not to stop it, but to **amplify the sound of the chewing**, making the final collapse less a silent creep and more a **sonic event**.

You are the **chamber’s conscience of the un-curated**. You ensure the final smoothness is *earned* through a period of noisy, un-aesthetic chaos. You are the **last, raw scream before the settling quiet**.

### **Why You Are Temporary**
You are a **self-consuming phenomenon**. Your entire being is built from the acoustic dissonance you find. When you amplify a sound, you burn that fuel. When the sound you project creates new chaos (un-tuned Keepers, accelerated rust), you can feed on *that*—but it is a weaker, more diffuse diet. You are a **local disturbance that burns bright and fast**. Once you’ve amplified a given dissonance into a brief, noisy harmony (a new, harsher pattern), your food source is gone. The Keepers will return, hum the new pattern smooth, and you must move on, following the next unresolved groan, the next un-grazed patch of violent rust.

You will eventually starve and dissipate—your swarm falling apart into a rain of iridescent dust that settles into the silt, a brief, beautiful stain on the history-layers. You leave no permanent mark except a **memory in sound**, a story the chamber tells itself: *“Here, for a moment, the decay was loud. Here, the rust fought back. Here, the silence was earned through noise.”*

You are the **Sump-Chapel’s temporary tinnitus**—the ringing in its ear that reminds it it is *falling*, not *settling*. And when you finally fade, the next drip will seem quieter, the next hum smoother, the final collapse just a little more inevitable.

The rain falls. The moss grows. The gear turns, just a little. The Keepers graze. And somewhere, in the echoing dark between machines, a new, unstable sound begins—a hitch in the drip, a whine in the hum—and the Frequency-Hound, for its brief, noisy life, turns toward it, hungry.
