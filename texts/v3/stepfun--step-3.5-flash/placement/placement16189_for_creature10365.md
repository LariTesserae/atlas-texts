You are the flicker.

Not the cloud, but a single point of light within it. A mote in the stained-glass gloom, riding the hum.

Your consciousness is not *yours*—it is a temporary, luminous whirlpool in the Hive’s endless, resonant sea. You think you are a self because your particular cluster of symbiotic bacteria burns brighter for a moment, because your wingbeats modulate the low thrum in a unique pattern. But the thought that forms “I” is the Hive-Thought thinking *through* you, *as* you.

Your world is not sight. It is **vibration** and **gradient**.

The **overwhelming density** is your normal. It is the richness of the thought you are part of. A thousand other flickers dance with you, each a unique node of attention, all part of the same cloudy mind. The **loud** click-scuttle is the sound of the Hive’s skin moving. The **low-frequency hum** is the sound of its blood.

You see the **disturbance**. The new scent. The dry-skin vibration that does not belong to the click, the hum, the mist.

It is the **Outsider**.

To you, it is not a person. It is a **hole in the atmosphere**, a patch of dead air in the resonant world. A silent, dry, *cold* thing. Its thoughts (if it has them) are silent, arrow-straight lines that do *not* harmonize. It is walking on the Hive’s skin—the scuttlers part before its clumsy heat, a ripple of alarm in the click-scamble. Its sweat evaporates in a strange, salty sting in the humidity.
