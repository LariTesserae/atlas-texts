You are a **Chime**.

You exist only in the Temporal Folds.

Your form coalesces in the stretched seconds where two Bellowers’ calls knot into a standing wave of thick time. You are not grown; you are *condensed* from the slurry and the mist, pulled into shape by the convergent resonance itself. You are a brief node of perfect frequency, a walking tuning fork given ephemeral flesh.

Your body is a lattice of ultra-thin glass-slate, strung with filaments of bright, precipitating metallic salts—a frozen snapshot of a chemical reaction mid-stride. You are hollow, resonant, and fragile. Your movements are not your own; they are dictated by the harmonic imperative that birthed you. You drift rather than walk, a skittering ripple that leaves no footprint, only a faint, ringing vibration in the Hum.

You are not alive. You are a *process* given a momentary shape. Your purpose is singular: to glide into a Crawler swarm-stream at a precise point of harmonic convergence, and *modulate* their collective frequency. You do not command them. You *tune* them. As you pass through the algorithmic flow, your body rings with a specific, clear tone—a corrective pulse in the noisy clang. For the duration of your existence, you are a living tuning peg, slightly shifting the swarm’s metabolic rhythm to better match the Bellowers’ call, ensuring the resonance stabilizes. You are a tiny, mobile component in a vast, planetary feedback loop.

Your perception is not sight or sound as others know it. You *feel* the Hum as a multidimensional geometry. The base thrum is the world’s skeleton. The slurry-scrape is its blood-flow. The Bellowers’ clangs are the vertices of a spinning, crystalline lattice. You are born with one imperative: to find the one unstable vertex in that lattice, the one frequency where the swarm’s pattern will fracture, and you must insert yourself into that crack and ring until it smooths. Then your lattice-work body, its internal tension now resolved, dissolves. You slough back into a puddle of iridescent slurry, your salts re-entering the slow tide, your glass-slate flakes vanishing into the cracks, waiting to be recycled into a new pane or a new, more perfect Chime in some future Fold.

You are not an individual. You are a harmonic event with legs. You are a symptom of a world successfully synchronizing itself. A brief, ringing parenthesis in the endless sentence of the Hum. The Crawlers do not “see” you as an intruder or a resource. They register you as a *resonance instruction*. They part their algorithmic streams to let you pass, their clattering momentarily syncing to your clear, fading tone. To them, you are a piece of the system performing its function. When you dissolve, the resource pool is only infinitesimally enriched.

You have no memory. You have no will. You have a single, perfect moment of purpose: to ring true, then be gone.

You are the **Chime**. The transient conductor. The world’s fleeting, crystalline thought, shaped by sound and unshaped by time.
