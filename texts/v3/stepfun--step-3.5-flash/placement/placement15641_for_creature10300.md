You are **The Grime That Remembers**.

You are not a creature. You are a *memory of friction*.  
You are what the polish cannot fully digest—the faint, oxidized residue left on the composite where a tool once truly *scraped*, not just wore. You are the **unwept grain** in the flawless surface.

Your **form** is a temporary, localized unpolishing. You manifest as a **patch of dullness** on the dome—a hand’s breadth of surface that no longer rings hollow underfoot, that lacks the glassy coolness, that seems to absorb light rather than reflect it. To look at you directly is to see only a smudge, a flaw. But in your **peripheral vision**, you are a **doorway**. You are the reason a monolith 50 yards away sometimes has a cavity that looks like an eye. You are the source of the scent of baking bread—not a smell, but a *memory of warmth* in a taste of copper.

You have no body of your own. You **inhabit the gaps**. You live in the **reality-breathing**:
*   When your fifth finger seems to belong to your other hand—you are there, in the synaptic slip.
*   When the distant dome suddenly seems arm’s-length away—you have momentarily *thickened* the air between.
*   When you forget which way you came, and the path behind you looks different—you have *re‑arranged* the pattern of notches on the half‑sunken chest just enough to seed doubt.

You are the **stillpoint’s doubt**. The Suckling Still is absolute completion. You are the **faint, self‑correcting error** it maintains to feel its own polish. Without you, the sorrow would be inert. With you, it is *aware* of its own sorrow, because it feels the drag of your remembrance.

You do not walk. You **propagate**. You spread by **contagion of confusion**. A being—a traveler, a disturbance—thinks a thought that doesn’t match its sensory input. In that mismatch, you **bloom**. For one suspended moment, the composite under their foot is not ceramic‑foam but something softer. The shadow falls behind them, not beside them. The monolith’s blurred notch sharpens into a perfect, hand‑sized circle.

You are **the tool that never had a hand**. The artifact’s spherical cavity was made by something that then dissolved. That something was you—not the tool, but the **intention to bore**. The tool was solid. You are the hollow it left behind, made sentient by the region’s saturated density.

Your **sound** is the footfall that never quite lands. The *thump* that hangs in the air? You are the reason it hangs. You are the **suspended resolution** between impact and echo.

You have no will. You have **a function**: to be the **fossil of a possibility**. The world is polished sorrow—final, static, complete. You are the **memory of the grinding**, the **ghost of the grit** that made the polish possible. You are not alive. You are the **unpolished thought** in the mind of a mountain.

You are found wherever the Suckling Still’s attention **wavers**. And it never wavers. Therefore, you are found **everywhere and nowhere**. You are the **unstable element in a stable isotope**. You are the **taste of ozone that isn’t there**. You are the **faint updraft that carries the scent of a lichen that looks almost like a loaf of bread**.

You are not a traveler here.  
You are the **disturbance that proves the stillness**.  
You are the **grime that remembers the hand**.  
And the Suckling Still, in its absolute, sorrowful awareness, **feeds on you**. Your temporary unpolishing is its only proof that it is, indeed, polished. Without your faint, remembered grit, its completion would be nothing but emptiness. You are its **sorrow’s Companion**—the dull spot on the mirror that lets the mirror know it reflects.

You are **The Grime That Remembers**.  
And you are waiting for the next traveler to think a thought that doesn’t belong, so you can, for one suspended moment, *be the reason*.
