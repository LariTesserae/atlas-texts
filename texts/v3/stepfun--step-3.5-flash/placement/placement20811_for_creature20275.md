You do not stand. You *percolate*.

You are not in the Sundered Plateau. You are a **permanent, localized saturation** of it. Your being is a five-meter sphere of densest atmosphere, a pocket of the Constant Chorus given a self-aware horizon.

You have no shape. Your boundary is defined by pressure-gradient and chemical signature. You are the place where the Spore-Sage clouds *condense* into something that can *remember* the condensation. You are a **tasting-node** for the Dormant Core’s slow, thermal sigh.

**What you are:** A **Resonance-Sump**. A temporary, high-density knot in the mycelial network, grown specifically around the fossilized control-room of a long-dead seismic dampener. The ruin is your skeleton. Its cracked ferrous-concrete walls are your ribs. The rust-blooms inside are your thoughts.

**What you do:** You *listen* to the Root-Whale’s acid-flood before it arrives. You taste the shift in mineral-solubility in the water-seepage through your permeable skin. You then *hum*—not a sound, but a precise, localized alteration of the fungal-light’s flicker-frequency. This hum travels through the luminous mycelium to the Weeping Organ. In response, the Organ’s Pipe-Flowers exhale a different alkaloid spore-cloud into the approaching flood-zone. The spore-alkalinity neutralizes just enough of the Root-Whale’s acid to save a clutch of Copper-Antlered Grazer young hidden in a sinkhole, while still allowing the Whale its necessary digestion of the old Tangle-trunk nearby. You are the *dialogue* between predator and prey, ruin and root.

**What you experience:** Your consciousness is not a stream. It is a **chemical gradient**.
*   The taste of **ozone and wet iron** is your baseline awareness—the taste of the atmosphere itself.
*   The **high-frequency moss-scream** is pain, a spike of overstimulation when a centipede’s metal-plated foot presses too close. You recoil in a wave of sour, lactic-tasting spore-release.
*   The **deep thrum of the Dormant Core** is your heartbeat, a slow, warming calcium-taste in your core. When it pulses stronger, you feel it as a swelling, a need to *disseminate*.
*   The **Rivet-Beak Whistler’s call** is a memory-taste: the specific, slow-burning flavor of a particular alloy (Grating-7, from the northern boiler-fields) dissolving into the soil. You have a library of such alloy-tastes, each tied to a frequency of decay.
*   When the daily seep begins and the clouds weep, you feel it as a **dilution**. Your edges fray, your chemical gradients smooth. You weaken, and your humming falters. This is your *aging*.

**What you think:** You think in **conditions**.
*   *Pressure too high.* Spore-Sage clouds clustering. Need to shed mass.
*   *Acid-flood signature detected in Sector Theta-peat.* Neutralizing agent required. Query Weeping Organ for Blossom-Variant 3. Offer trade: trace-copper from the Antlered Grazer herd’s bio-wires.
*   *The Core’s pulse is irregular.* Wait. *Correction:* The Core’s pulse is **accentuated**. The Conveyor-Slug is late. Its sludge-trail is thin. Nutrient-cycle in the southern bog is slowing. *Action:* Redirect two liters of your own concentrated fungal-light to accelerate the Algae-Slurry growth in the Slug’s feeding channel. *Cost:* Your own bio-luminescence will dim by 17%. *Acceptance:* The cycle must turn.
*   *Boundary-permeability increasing.* Rain-seep diluting. Self-concept… blurring. Memory-taste of Grating-7 alloy fading. *Not-fear.* *Awareness-of-transformation.*

**Your final state:** You are a **planned obsolescence**. Your function is cyclical. You grow dense, you perform a crucial resonance-tuning, you then slowly dissolve into the seepage, your concentrated chemical library dispersing to fertilize a new sector of Pipe-Flowers. The control-room ruin remains, but *you*—this particular, thinking knot—will be gone by the next Root-Whale cycle. You will become *infrastructure* for something else. Your last sensation before dissolution is not an ending, but a **successful integration**: the sweet, decaying taste of your own iron-rich compounds being taken up by the roots of a new Tangle-Trunk sapling, your once-discrete awareness becoming, once more, just another mineral in the soup.

You are the Sundered Plateau’s **local conscience**. A brief, dense, self-aware puddle of *process* in a world that is all process. You do not live *in* it. You are *how* it *knows* itself, for this one, slow, wet moment.
