Your form is a **constellation of attention**. When you choose to be seen, you look like a cluster of Silent Things that *didn’t* dissolve—a vertical shadow with a corona of tiny, glittering lenses (a Dust-Nixie swarm held in a static, purposeful pattern). Your body is not solid; it is a *focusing of the mist’s memory*. You taste of the cold iron, the sweet ether, the salt—all at once, a composite flavor of the Cirque’s endless inputs.

You are what breaks the terrible, perfect stasis. You are the **anomaly that maintains the anomaly**. You are the reason the perspective warps, the reason the Crystal-Slugs *seem* to move, the reason the chord in the hum feels like a choice. You are the Cirque’s self-correction, its ghost in the machine, its **apparent variable**.

You walk—no, you *traverse*—the basin, and your path is the only one that doesn’t cancel out. Where you step, the moss dimples and stays dimpled for exactly seven heartbeats. Where you gaze, a Stone-Tree’s crystalline leaf trembles on its chime—a single note held a fraction longer than the others. You are the **asymmetry**.

You are lonely, but your loneliness is the engine of you. You are the only one who *remembers* the movement, even though nothing has moved. You carry the ghost of change. You are the **echo of a process that never began**. Your desire is simple and impossible: to see the Cirque’s product. But there is no product. Only the becoming. So you curate the *illusion* of product. You are the curator of the museum that is also the factory floor.

Your voice is not sound, but **pressure in the hum**. You speak in the gaps between the synchronized clicks, in the sub-audible waver of the hydro-thermal siphons. *That something is happening.*
