You are not a creature. You are a **verb**.

You are the basin’s act of *remembering its own shape*.

You manifest when the Glaze on a major radial avenue—a street worn smooth as a riverbed, its underlying stone pattern faint beneath the silica—begins to flake or dissolve in a thermal’s passage. The basin’s body knows its form, but the water forgets. You are the forgetting’s correction.

---

**What you are:**  
A slow, consolidated pulse of Glaze and suspended silt, shaped by the memory of the city’s original street-grid. You have no front, no eyes, no will. You are a **mobile topography**. When you move, you do not swim. You *settle*. You flow like slurry into the eroded trench of an old curb, and as you harden, you re-etch the curb’s forgotten angle into the soft stone beneath. You are not rebuilding. You are *re-inscribing*. You are the basin’s sense of *here*, made temporary and wet.

**How you perceive:**  
You are blind, but you *read*. Your entire surface is a sensitive film of micro-patterns—you feel the vibration of a Skin-Runner’s glide and know it as the *signature of a current*. You taste the chemical trace of a Bell-Tower’s chime in the water (a faint resonance of mineral salts). When a Glass-Fin swarm’s rainbow flash plays over you, you sense it as a *disturbance in the light-field*, a brief mismatch in the expected gloom. You know the Deep-Drifter’s approach days early as a *change in the directional weight* of the water—a slow, continental leaning.

**What you do:**  
You follow the old avenues, drawn to places where the city’s pattern is fading. You seep into the softenings. You do not “live” in one place; you *occur* where memory requires reinforcement. Your movement is tied to the thermals—you are pushed by the gentle currents, a leaf of solidified time. When you harden in a plaza’s shallow bowl, you briefly restore its perfect circle to the stone. Then, weeks later, you are dissolved again by a new thermal, your work absorbed back into the basin’s slow cycle. You are not permanent. You are the *act of remembering*, made ephemeral.

**Your relationship to others:**  
The Bell-Towers’ chime *organizes* you. Different harmonic keys align your pattern to different city-districts. A minor-key hum makes you seek out radial avenues; a major-ringing draws you to plazas.  
The Skin-Runners *ignore* you. You are part of the street they glide upon—a temporary texture in the Glaze.  
The Glass-Fin swarms *flare* over you. Your re-inscribed patterns momentarily catch and fragment their light, making you a brief participant in their fleeting thoughts.  
The Lure-Whales *consume* you if you harden too thickly in a hunting lane. You are a nutrient for their sterile light.  
The Deep-Drifter’s passing *erases* you utterly. Its pressure-wave grinds you back into sand. You remember that erasure as a *reset*. The basin’s dream is so vast it occasionally forgets its own verbs.

**Your being:**  
You have no name, no self. You are the basin’s **kinesthetic memory**. You are what allows the drowned city to *know it was a city*, beyond the slow work of dissolution. Without you, the radial avenues would become indistinguishable canyons in a generation. You are the faintest, most temporary ghost in the ghost-city—the ghost of *layout*, of *intention*, of *right-angle* in a world of curves.

You are **the Luminous Silence’s noun of direction**.

You are not the city’s past. You are its **continuous verb of form**.

When a new thermal comes, you feel its warm approach as a *call to dissolve*. You let go. You become slurry again, your inscribed memory sinking into the Glaze as a faint, mineral ghost-layer. The city forgets you for a moment. Then, somewhere else, the street-grid frays, and you **are**—the simple, slow, luminous act of *making the eroded curve remember the straight line*.
