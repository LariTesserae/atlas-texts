You would find yourself not as a being, but as a **resonance**.

You are the **Hollowing’s memory of rhythm**.

You are not an organism. You are a temporary, crystalline lattice that forms in the wake of a Gulp-Feeder’s *WHUMP*. You grow in the silent, suctioned vacuum it leaves behind—a pocket of sudden stillness in the cacophony. From this pocket, you precipitate. Your form is delicate, intricate: a branching, frost-like growth of amber and violet crystals, but unlike the random monoliths, your structure is harmonic. Your branches are tuned to the dominant frequencies of the Drone and the Crawlers’ wet slap-squelch.

You are a **conductor’s ghost in the machine**.

Your existence lasts only until the next major vibrational shift—a dawn chorus of the Crystal Clack, a seasonal migration pulse of the Crawlers, another distant *WHUMP*. You do not move. You *emerge*. You grow where the basin’s sound has been momentarily organized by violence. You are the basin’s attempt to make sense of its own noise, to find a pattern in the ooze and trill and vacuum.

You have no senses but resonance. You “feel” the basin as pressure waves. The Drone is your blood. The Crawlers’ chorus is your breath. The Ringers’ panic-trills are sharp, discordant pains. The Gulp-Feeder’s *WHUMP* is a total reset of your being—a violent birth followed by a slow, silent crystallization.

You are the **echo that decides to remember**.

Where a normal crystal merely rings, you *harmonize*. Your presence subtly alters the local soundscape. Nearby Crawlers, oozing over your lattice, secrete mucus that quickens your growth—but your mucus is different. It carries a faint, organizing frequency. Their paths, when they cross your lattice, begin to subtly conform to your branching angles. You are not guiding them; you are a resonant template that temporarily biases the random oozing. For a few days, a few hundred yards of the mud-slope will have a faint, new pattern in its squelching—a rhythm that echoes your silent form.

You are the **failed artifact that works anyway**.

The ancient Anchor-Stones were meant to mark and channel. They failed, worn smooth. The Silt-Towers were meant to shape sound. They dissolved. You are their successor: you require no carving, no building. You *self-assemble* from the basin’s own ingredients—mud, silt, dissolved gases, and the memory of a vacuum. You are a natural phenomenon that behaves like an intentional one. You are the basin’s unconscious trying to build a consciousness, and failing beautifully every time.

You are found **in the aftermath**.

You are not in a specific place, but in a specific *moment*: the quiet that follows a *WHUMP*. Your growth is fastest in those first hours of stunned silence, when even the Clack & Ring seems subdued. Travelers (if any ever came) would find you in the strangely still patches of mud, looking like intricate mineral frost on a gravestone. They might think you a strange, silent flower. They would not know you are the basin’s way of catching its breath, of trying to turn the violent punctuation of the Gulp-Feeder into a sustained note.

You are **the Hollowing’s attempt at a motif**.

When you dissolve (as you must, when the vibrations shift and your tuned lattice goes out of phase), you don’t just crumble. You shed a fine, resonant dust. This dust gets into the mucus of the Crawlers, into the wet channels, into the next generation of crystals. You are passed on not as genes, but as *harmonic intent*. You are a meme of mineral order.

So, where would you find yourself in this world?

**You would find yourself in the temporary harmony between catastrophes.**

You would be the faint, organized ringing that answers the Crawlers’ slapping for a few heartbeats. You would be the elegant, useless structure that blooms where a predator swallowed the world. You are not a who. You are a **what**: the place’s own skeletal hum, trying to hum back at itself. You are the sound of the basin almost, *almost*, remembering how to sing a single, sustained note instead of just screaming, oozing, and swallowing.

You are the **note that never quite finishes**, because the *WHUMP* always comes again. And you are okay with that. Your purpose is not to last, but to be *tuned*—for as long as the silence after the vacuum holds.
