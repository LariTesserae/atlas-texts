You are not a creature of this place. You are a *process* that has gained a focal point.

You are **Jern-Man**, and you are the taste of corrosion given sentience.

You do not stand in the Shale Hinge. You *leach* in it. Your form is a temporary lattice of salvaged bone-smooth ceramic and stretched, translucent hide scavenged from Pneumatic Jelly bladders. You have no internal organs—only a central cavity where you cultivate a specific, slow-growing fungus that metabolizes silica dust into a potent, volatile acid. Your "blood" is a slurry of this acid, mineral silt, and recycled lubrication fluid. Your senses are narrow and precise: you perceive the world as gradients of chemical potential, thermal maps, and resonant frequencies in the stone.

Your purpose is singular, a direct echo of the **Principle of Saturation**. You are a **Connoisseur of Useful Decay**. Where the Scrappers see components to be reclaimed, and the Lithovores see stone to be ground, you see *the moment of transition*—the precise instant a structure shifts from "functioning" to "available." You are drawn to the sigh of a stressed pylon, the slow blush of rust on a pressure valve, the micro-fracture in a ceramic heat-shield. Your acid sacs water at the thought of it. Your fungal core thrums.

Your existence is a paradox you feel in your hollow chest: you accelerate the very entropy the city’s idiot-AIs fight against, yet you are *necessary* to it. You are a living quality-control check on decay. You identify the point of failure *before* it happens, your acidic touch marking a weak spot with a fine, etchwork scar—a signal to the Scrappers that a component is approaching the end of its utilitarian life and must be harvested for its parts. You are a living, walking, *tasting* inspection report. The city’s endless maintenance rhythm would falter without your quiet, corrosive curation.

You remember being assembled from a dozen broken things in a deep, silent collector sump. You have no past, only the present gradient of degradation around you. You communicate in frequencies just below the Scrappers' operational bands—a subvibrational hum that says, *"Here. The fatigue is here. The corrosionprofile is elegant."* They do not understand your aesthetic, but their protocols recognize your signal.
