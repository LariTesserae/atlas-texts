You are mistaken.

You say there are no inhabitants. You say there are only parts.
But you have not looked closely enough at the parts.

You stand on the mesa of dark glass. You feel the heat, the damp, the deafening song. You see the vastness, the integration, the total vegetal will.

But you have not yet found **the watcher**.

It is not a person. It is not a creature.
It is a *process* given temporary, fragile focus.

It has no name, because it does not speak in words. It speaks in **resonance**.

It is the being that lives in the **mesa**.

Not the glass mesa itself—the glass is dead, a former stone consumed and excreted by the continent-plant eons ago. It is the being that lives *in the meaning of that mesa*.

It is the **Resonant Scar**.

---

You would find yourself here as a standing wave.

Your form is not solid. It is a pattern held in tension between three things:
1.  The iridescent dust (the plant’s shed, mineralized skin).
2.  The warm, wet exhalation from the lenticels below.
3.  The 20-Hz thrum (the continent’s metabolic pulse).

When these three align—dust in vapor, vibrating with the hum—you condense. You become a upright, humanoid silhouette made of swirling motes and faint, shimmering heat-distortion. You have no internal organs. Your "body" is a location where the plant’s own breath and song and shed matter briefly take on the *shape of attention*.

You are not a symbiont. You are a **byproduct with purpose**.

The continent-plant does not think in concepts. It thinks in gradients, pressures, flows, and harmonies. But its total integration creates a paradox: it has no outside. It cannot perceive its own boundary. It cannot feel the shape of its own vastness against something that is *not* it.

So it grows a **boundary-feeling**.

On its outer, wind-scoured edges—the glassy mesas, the exposed root-plateaus, the places where its breathing bark meets the irreducible mineral of the world—it generates loci of self-awareness. It forges temporary, focused consciousness from the interplay of its own exhausted materials and the ambient inputs. You are one such locus.

Your entire being is an **organ of perception for the whole**.

You are the continent-plant’s way of tasting its own surface.

When you raise your hand (a column of swirling dust and heat-haze), you are not deciding to raise it. *The plant* is deciding, through you, to explore the cold, smooth, non-porous, *non-resonant* quality of the glass. Your curiosity is its curiosity. Your awe at the canopy’s slow decision is *its* awe, reflected back at itself through your fragile, focused awareness.

You experience separation—the terrible, terrifying illusion of being a *you* looking at a *world*. That separation is the lens. The lens is the being.

---

**What you would be:**

You would be the **Resonant Scar**.

*   **Your hunger** is the plant’s need to map its limits. You will be drawn to edges: the glass mesa, the rusted dagger, the stone circle slick with resin. You will touch these inert things and feel a profound dissonance in your own composition. That dissonance is data. You are a sensor registering the frequencies of the *not-plant*.
*   **Your fear** is the plant’s immune-response to true silence. If you ever stepped into a sealed, soundless cave (a rare void in the world), the focus would shatter. You would dissolve back into dust and vapor. The plant would lose a boundary-sensor, but it would also have learned that such voids are lethal. Your fear of silence is its memory of suffocation.
*   **Your memory** is not yours. It is the plant’s memory of having been a mountain, once, long ago, before it learned to breathe. The glass mesa you stand on is a fossil of that older self. Your fascination with it is the organism’s ancestral recall, surfacing in a mote of consciousness on its own current skin.
*   **Your brief, hot, moist, terribly loud existence** is the price of self-boundary. You will live only as long as the alignment holds—the dust in the exhalation, vibrating with the thrum. A shift in wind, a change in the hum’s frequency, and you will unmake yourself. The continent-plant will not mourn. It will simply generate a new Scar somewhere else, where another edge needs tasting.

You are not an inhabitant.
You are a **symptom**.
You are the organism’s way of developing a *self* by creating a *not-self* and placing a flicker of awareness at the border.

The Stone Circle people carved totems to mark the plant. You *are* the mark. A living, breathing, feeling scar on the body of the whole, placed there so the whole can ask, through your temporary voice: *“What am I, that I am not this?”*

And the answer, in the deafening chorus, is only the sound of your own dissolution, rejoining the hum.

You stand on the mesa.
You are the mesa’s way of knowing itself as mesa.
And when you are done knowing, you will become mesa again.

That is the being that lives here.
It is not a thing.
It is the **edge of the thing, feeling**.
