## **Preparation Protocol: Visiting the Saturated Basin ("The Final Stillness")**

**Classification:** *Non-Habitat, Immersive Chemical Field, Dynamic Equilibrium Zone.*  
**Threat Level:** Immediate dissolution / sensory collapse / chemical incompatibility.  
**Visitor Profile:** Specialized, non-reactive, sensor-focused. No casual tourism. This is a calibration of the self to a world that operates on a different axis of "normal."

---

### **I. Fundamental Mindset Shift: You Are Not an Explorer. You Are a Temporary Anomaly.**
Forget "landscape," "wilderness," or "ocean." You are entering a **giant, turbulent test tube** where the solvent *is* the ecology. Your human biochemistry is a violent, chaotic drop of freshwater in a saturated brine. Your goal is to **observe without reacting, exist without dissolving.** You are a passive mineral grain, not a living system. All prep centers on making your body and gear chemically and physically *invisible* to this place's imperative: saturation and equilibrium.

---

### **II. Physiological Conversion & Suiting (The "Ghost-Shell")**

**1. Respiratory & Circulatory System Bypass:**
*   **No air tanks.** The "air" is toxic slurry. You must **stop breathing** conventionally.
*   **Internal Oxygen Scrubber:** Implant or swallow a micro-refinery that recycles your cellular respiration's CO₂ into solid carbonates (calcite/limestone) and pure O₂ *within your bloodstream*. Your blood becomes a closed-loop reactor. All waste gases are mineralized and stored in a tiny, compliant bladder.
*   **Pressure Equalization:** Your suit (see below) must have no air gaps. It is a **second skin** of flexible, neutrally buoyant composite. You will not "fill" it; you will *wear* it like a gel. It must precisely match your body's density (≈1.03 g/cm³) to neither sink nor rise in the saturated water (2.9/3 density).

**2. The Suit: "The Lithic Mantle"**
*   **Material:** Non-porous, chemically inert polymer-ceramic laminate (e.g., evolved Aerogel + Zirconia). Surface must be **superhydrophobic *and* superoleophobic** to this specific brine. Goal: zero adhesion, zero dissolution, zero leeching.
*   **Seams:** Laser-welded, no flex points. Any mechanical joint is a site of failure. Movement must come from **internal micromotors driving exoskeletal tendons**, not fabric stretching.
*   **Thermal Management:** The water is cold (0.5/3). Your body heat is a massive, disruptive thermal plume. Suit must have **active heat sinks** that pump your metabolic warmth into the *solid basalt* through your boots (see Touch Protocol). You must *cool* your suit's interior to ambient to avoid creating a convective thermal river that would attract every consumer.
*   **Vision:** No eyes. **360° sonar/lidar array** emitting only **inaudible, sub-pressure-wave pulses** (to avoid disrupting low-frequency communication). Output to a neural visor creating a point-cloud model. Your "sight" will be a grey-bluish 3D map with **chemical gradient** overlays (color-coded sulfides, pH fronts, temperature differentials). No light. You will see through sound and density.
*   **Audition:** Bone-conduction sensors for the planet's thrum. Hydrophones for the "sighs" of vents. Filter for **sub-audible pressure pulses** (Gulper vortices) and **stone-borne vibrations** (deep tectonics). Auditory feed is a rumbling, harmonic chord with sharp, transient spikes.

**3. Touch & Interaction: **Zero Direct Contact.****
*   **Gloves:** Non-adhesive, textured only for manipulating your own tools. You will **never lay a finger on the basalt, the crusts, or any fauna.**
*   **Sampling Tools:** Extendable, **single-use, chemically neutral collectors**.
    *   *Crust Sampler:* A blunt, vibrating probe that *scrapes* a microscopic sample into a **sealed, isobaric chamber** without tearing the mat. No living culture transfer.
    *   *Water Sampler:* Syringe with **precisely matched salinity/density chamber**. Inlet creates no vortex. Draws from a **meter ahead** of your body, from water you haven't disturbed.
    *   *Mineral Concretion Grabber:* Soft-jawed, padded. Places collected nodules into a **compartment with artificial basalt dust** to simulate native context.
*   **Movement:** You **do not walk**. You **translate**. Use **low-power, distributed water jets** that push against the viscous medium with minimal turbulent shear. Motion must be **slow, steady, and laminar**. Sudden movement = a violent pressure wave = "prey" or "threat" signal. Your jets must intake water from directly behind you and expel it **perfectly along the local gradient** to avoid creating mixing.

**4. Digestive & Waste Protocols:**
*   **All food is a paste of sterile, pre-mineralized carbonates and sulfates** (tasting of chalk and iron). You consume nothing from this world.
*   **All metabolic waste (urine, feces, shed skin cells)** is **immediately encapsulated** in **dissolvable, pH-neutral polymer beads** that sink into the basalt's micro-crevices *via a low-pressure ventral dropper*. Your waste must **disappear as if it were a natural sediment**. No organic signature. No nutrient pulse. You leave **no trace but thermal and pressure disturbance** (which your suit minimizes).

### **III. Sequence of Visit: The Descent & Immersion**

**1. Approach:**
*   Arrive at the basin's rim (the "impossible unseen edge") via a **dry, air-filled vessel** that never touches the water.
*   Do **not** dive. The transition from air to this water is catastrophic. Instead, enter a **lock-out chamber** on the vessel's hull.
*   **Flood the chamber** with the basin's water *over 12 hours*, slowly equalizing pressure and temperature to avoid thermal shock to your suit's seals.

**2. First Contact (The First Hour):**
*   **Do not move.** Simply hover 1 meter above the basalt plain.
*   **Sensors ON.** Build your point-cloud map. Locate the nearest **vent-field** (thermal/chemical beacon) and the nearest **chemo-crust patch** (color/chemical beacon).
*   **Acclimate your senses** to the directionless gloom. Identify the baseline "silence" (deep thrum + vent hiss). Learn to distinguish a **Gulper's vortex approach** (a sudden, localized *drop* in pressure and particulate density) from natural gradient shifts.
*   **Cool your suit** aggressively. Let your heat leach into the stone.

**3. Protocol for Observation:**
*   **Crusts:** Observe from **3+ meters**.** Never hover directly over a mat—your thermal shadow chills it. Circle slowly at a distance. Note color shifts (purple to yellow = sulfur depletion). Your presence should cause **zero change** in microbial metabolism (sensor-check).
*   **Filter-Krill Processions:** Position yourself **down-current**. Observe their *collective* movement pattern. **Do not intercept.** Your shadow/thermal plume would disrupt their filtration. Note if a procession avoids a certain brine channel (chemical warning).
*   **Gulper Encounter:** This is your **primary danger**. Sight is a dim smear. Detection is **sonar: a large, slow-moving volume with a central pressure null.** Protocol:
    1.  **Cease all motion.**
    2.  **Match local density** (your buoyancy bladders adjust).
    3.  **Let it pass through your position.** Its vortex will **pull you** slightly. **Do not fight it.** Go limp. Your smooth, non-stick suit should offer no resistance. It is like being inside a slow, cold whirlpool. It will not "eat" you (you are not particulate organics), but the shear forces could damage suit seams if you resist.
    4.  After it passes, the water will be temporarily clearer. **Remain absolutely still** for 5 minutes as the gradients re-stabilize. Then, **drift away slowly down-current** (same direction it was moving). Never follow.

**4. Navigation & Scale:**
*   You have **no sense of direction.** Your map is everything. Use **thermal vents as waypoints** (they are the only persistent landmarks). Travel is **vent-to-vent**.
*   The "Plain of Glass" or "River of Brine" are **chemical and thermal anomalies.** Approach their edges to sample, but **never enter the hyper-brine river**—your density match would fail, and you'd be crushed or dissolved.
*   **Time is measured in tidal cycles of chemical fronts** (e.g., "the copper plume from Vent-7 lasts 3.2 hours"). Your visit **must not exceed one full chemical cycle of your primary observation site** (typically 18-24 hours). You become a persistent disturbance after that.

### **IV. Psychological & Sensory Preparation**

*   **Horizon Deprivation Training:** Spend 72+ hours in a featureless, fog-filled, soundproof tank with no up/down lighting. Learn to orient by pressure gradients (slight temperature/pH changes) and vibration sources only.
*   **Gocyclic Darkness:** Live in a room with 0.04/3 illumination (like a moonless, heavy overcast night) for a month. Your hand *must* remain a "dim smudge." Practice fine motor skills by touch/sound alone.
*   **Viscosity Adaptation:** Exercise in a pool of **90% saturated saline solution with suspended kaolin clay** to approximate the "gelatinous crowd" feel. Learn to move with economy.
*   **Sound Recalibration:** Meditate to recordings of **deep tectonic hums** (sub-bass) mixed with **slow bubble suites** (mid-frequency) and **silence** (the absence of audible wave). Your primary "music" will be the **harmony of your suit's hydrosymphony**—you must learn to hear your own *unwanted* noise as a blaring alarm.
*   **Existential Framing:** Study **deep-time erosion**, **thermodynamics of equilibrium**, and **chemosynthetic ecosystems**. Internalize: **This is not life *fighting* entropy. This is life *being* a local, temporary, violent negation of entropy, powered by planetary heat.** You are visiting the **aftermath of a geological event**, not a thriving world. Beauty is in the **mathematical flatness, the chemical precision, the silent, relentless trade of atoms.** Prepare for awe that feels like **cold clarity**, not wonder.

### **V. Exit & Decontamination**

*   **Reverse Descent:** From your last waypoint vent, **drift slowly toward the rim** with all sensors off, all jets idle.
*   **Re-lock:** Enter the dry vessel's lock. **Seal.**
*   **Gradual Flush:** Over **48 hours**, replace the basin water in your suit's outer layer with sterile, temperature-matched **isotonic saline solution**, then finally with pure water. This prevents a violent osmotic shock to any trace organisms or crystals on your suit.
*   **Suit Removal:** In a clean, dry environment, **rinse suit with deionized water** under laminar flow. **Do not use chemicals.** Any residue must be inert.
*   **Body Purge:** Your internal scrubbers will now off-gas/off-solidify their stored waste. You will pass **strange, mineral-rich stools** for 3 days. Hydrate with pure water.
*   **Psychological Debrief:** **Mandatory 7-day sensory reintegration** in a bright, noisy, textured, airy environment. You have been a stone. You must remember how to be human again.
