# Preparation Guide: Basin of Black Glass  
*For the Ordinary Multiverse Traveler*

---

## 1. **Physical Readiness: Surviving the Contradictions**
### **Hydration & Thermoregulation**
- **You will not find liquid water.** The air is hot (≈40–50°C) and utterly dry. Your mouth will feel like sandpaper within minutes.
  - **Bring:** A closed-circuit water reclamation system (like a high-efficiency portable still) coupled to an internal electrolyte reservoir. Drink *slowly and constantly* from your suit’s supply—you will sweat profusely in the heat, but the air will wick it away instantly.
  - **Wear:** A full-body cooling garment with phase-change material packs (not liquid-cooled, to avoid condensation that might react with the glass floor). The suit must be *non-metallic*—the environment’s electromagnetic field is unusual, and metal might resonate with the sub-audible drone, causing nausea or disorientation.
  - **Coolant Warning:** The crystal trees’ blue veins emit a soft light—this is active coolant. Do not touch. It may be chemically aggressive or thermally unstable.

### **Atmosphere & Breathing**
- The air is thin (high elevation effect) but heavy with metallic particulates (ozone, copper vapor).
  - **Bring:** A lightweight rebreather with particulate filtration (HEPA + activated carbon) plus an O₂ supplement canister. The suit’s seals must be flawless—any leak will introduce that metallic tang deep into your lungs, risking respiratory irritation.
  - **Monitor:** Your blood oxygen and heart rate. The combination of heat, altitude, and possible low-level environmental radiation (from geothermal activity) will tax your system faster than expected.

### **Footwear & Movement**
- The black glass floor is polished to mirror sheen—like walking on frozen obsidian. It is *seamless* and *incredibly hard*.
  - **Wear:** Soft-soled, high-traction boots with non-marking, vibration-damping soles. You must be *silent*. The only sounds are the sub-audible drone and occasional stress-pings; your footsteps will echo *too* clearly in the quiet.
  - **Move slowly and deliberately.** The basin is a “sparse density” environment—vast empty polygons of glass between crystal trees. Rushing could cause you to misjudge distances (reflections may be out of sync) or stumble into a sharp crystal frond.

## 2. **Mental & Perceptual Conditioning**
### **Cognitive Dissonance Training**
- **You are entering a place where “life” and “machine” are the same thing.** The lush forest is *industrial tech*—not built, but *growing* as a geological process.
  - **Pre-Visit Drills:** Spend time observing complex machinery (factory assembly lines, power plant turbines) while meditating on the idea that these are *alive* in a functional sense. Then, observe old-growth forests and practice seeing them as *slow, biological factories* converting sunlight to biomass.
  - **Goal:** To suppress the instinctive *“this is wrong”* reaction when you see copper veins as “power lines” or blue mineral as “coolant channels.” Your brain will try to categorize; you must learn to see *process* instead.

### **Temporal & Spatial Disorientation Prep**
- **Reflections lag or show different angles.** The horizon *wavers*. Light bends at your vision’s edges.
  - **Practice:** Train with延迟-mirror setups (two mirrors with slight time-displaced reflection) and anamorphic art that distorts at periphery. Learn to trust your *position* (feet on ground) over your *sight*.
  - **Rule:** Do not rely on reflections for navigation. The glass floor is a perfect mirror—your brain will confuse up/down. Use a low-tech tool: a weighted string hanging from your belt to find true vertical via gravity.

### **Sound Tolerance**
- The sub-audible drone (felt in teeth/bones) is constant. Stress-pings are occasional, sharp, and spiritually jarring in the silence.
  - **Acclimatize:** Sleep with infrasonic speakers playing similar low-frequency drones (20–30 Hz). Practice mindfulness meditation while focusing on the vibration—not as noise, but as the “pulse” of the place.

## 4. **Behavioral Protocols On-Site**
1. **Do not alter anything.** This is a sealed, slow-running system (0.9/3 dynamic). Your presence is already an intrusion. Do not chip glass, break crystals, or even move stones. Observe only.
2. **Stay in the open polygons.** The “forest” is machinery. Getting too close to a trunk may expose you to unknown radiation, coolant leaks, or worst—*assimilation*. The boulder with blue vein growth shows the environment is actively converting ordinary rock into its own Parts.
3. **Watch your reflections.** If your image lags or shows a different angle, *do not panic*. This is light behaving oddly, not a ghost. Note the time discrepancy—it may correlate with the horizon’s wavering.
4. **Listen to the drone, don’t fight it.** If it causes anxiety, match your breathing to its rhythm. It is the sound of the planet-sized machine. Resisting will increase stress.
5. **Exit strategy:** The basin is a “pocket” (0.4/3 scale). The walls are stratified rock—they may be *literal boundaries* of this overwritten reality zone. Mark your entry point with a non-reactive ceramic token. If the world outside “flickers,” follow the token back. Do not wander toward the walls—the recently uplifted stone may be *unstable* in a metaphysical sense (the world is “still deciding its shape”).

## 5. **Psychological Aftercare**
- **Expect existential displacement.** You will have seen technology that is not built but *born*. Life that is not alive but *operational*. The memory will haunt your dreams with images of silent, thrumming factories growing under a normal blue sky that sometimes *flickers*.
- **Debriefing Questions:**
  - Did you feel the “blurring” between rock and reactor part as a *physical sensation*?
  - Did the dryness in your mouth feel *symbolic*—as if the place drinks not water but *geothermal gradients*?
  - When you looked at your reflection, for a split second did you wonder which version of you was the “original”?
- **Do not attempt to replicate or explain.** This place operates on a grammar beyond human engineering or biology. The best outcome is quiet documentation and profound humility.

---

## Final Note:
This is not an adventure. It is a **metaphysical observation**. You are a temporary sensor in a silent, scaled, industrial process that runs on geological time. Your human senses are inadequate; your human mind will struggle to categorize. Go prepared not to conquer or understand, but to *witness*—and to return with the understanding that some factories are made of glass and crystal, and some growth is just another word for *manufacture*.

**Traveler, you stand in a basin where the difference between a forest and a factory has not yet been fully written. Tread softly. The machinery is patient. You are not.**
