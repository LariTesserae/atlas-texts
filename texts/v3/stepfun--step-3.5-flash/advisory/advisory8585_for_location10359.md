# Pre-Visit Preparation Guide: The Breathing Stone (The Stilldrowned Mesa)

## **I. Core Conceptual Unlearning (Begin Weeks in Advance)**
Before you pack a single item, you must mentally shed three modern human instincts. Failure to do so will make you a destructive, terrified anomaly—potentially subject to “growth-judgment” expulsion or passive dissolution.

1.  **“Air” vs. “Water” is a False Dichotomy.**  
    You will be breathing the dense, falling Atmospheric Water as if it were air. Do not think “diving” or “surfacing.” You will move through a single, homogenous, breathable medium. The sensation will be like walking through constantly falling, cool silk. Your lungs will not burn; you will not need to hold your breath. However, **vision within the water-curtain is functionally zero**. You must navigate by other senses.

2.  **Time is Measured in Pulses, Not Clocks.**  
    The local “day” is irrelevant. The rhythms are: the slow mineral pulse (weeks/months), the vine growth cycle (seasons), and the universal sigh of the water-wall (a constant). Your frantic “sights to see” agenda is an act of violence. You must adopt a “presence” mindset. You will experience one small area deeply over a local “season” (approx. 3–4 Earth months), not rush across the mesa.

3.  **“Built” vs. “ grown” is the Central Morality.**  
    You are not visiting a city. You are entering a symbiotic organism. Any object you bring that is not *currently part of a growth cycle* is a “dead thing”— polluting, sad, and ultimately useless. A metal water bottle is a curse. A leather journal is a corpse. Your very modern clothing is a shroud of alien death.

## **II. Physical Gear: The Doctrine of Symbiotic Mimicry**
All items must be:
*   **Biodegradable within one local growth-cycle** (6–12 months).
*   **Made of materials that could plausibly be *grown* or *shaped* here** (bone, fibrous wood, untreated plant fiber, porous clay).
*   **Tool-like only in the sense of tending, pruning, redirecting.**

### **A. Clothing & Footwear**
*   **Primary Attire:** One full set of **undyed, loosely woven bark-fiber or giant-leaf sackcloth**. It should be shapeless, breathable, and able to get permanently wet without mattering. No seams (stitching is a “simple fabrication” sin; garments must be draped or tied with fiber cords).
*   **Footwear:** **None**, *except* for soft-soled, open-toed “sandals” made from a single piece of suppressible, fibrous root-mat. These are for protection on sharp mineral growths only. You must spend most time barefoot to feel the memory in the fibers and the gentle pulse of the stone.
*   **Undergarments:** None. The humidity is total. Privacy is managed by chamber depth, not layers.

### **B. Vision & Navigation Aids**
*   **Primary Tool:** A **hand-sized, naturally buoyant, sponge-like stone** (pumice or similar porous rock). Use it for gentle tactile probing ahead in low-visibility areas (within dense vine chambers or near the water-wall’s edge).
*   **Light Source:** **One living, slow-glow fungal nodule** in a woven moss pouch. It emits a faint blue-green light for 2–3 nights. Do not use fire or electric lights—they are abhorrent. Learn to navigate by sound, pressure changes in the water, and the faint bioluminescence of the atmospheric jellies.
*   **Compass/Map:** **None**. You will have a local guide (see Section IV). Your “map” will be a **small, flat, unglazed clay tablet** and a piece of soft charcoal. You are allowed to make *temporary* tactile sketches of key landmarks (a grove of weeping stone-coral, a vine-arch with a specific root-mite rustle pattern). The tablet itself will slowly dissolve in the humidity—a perfect metaphor.

### **C. Health & Sustenance**
*   **Medicinal Kit:** A small bundle of **dried, pulverized local mosses and fungal powders** (for skin irritation and minor wounds). No antiseptics, bandages (use clean root-fiber poultices). Your immune system must adapt *here*, not fight *against* here.
*   **Nutrients:** **Bring nothing.** You will eat what is offered: steamed tuber-pastes, filtered water-mineral broths, fungal gels. Your gut must empty itself of “Earth expectations” first (fast 3 days pre-arrival). You will likely experience mild, temporary dysbiosis—a metaphysical diarrhea of the soul as well as the body. This is part of the initiation.
*   **Water:** Drink only from the **cisterns within living vine-chambers** (they are filtered). Never drink directly from the open water-sea or from a fresh stone-weep—it may have unprocessed minerals.

## **III. Pre-Departure Rituals (72 Hours Prior)**
1.  **Tech Purge:** All devices, metal, synthetics, and written words must be left in a "stasis locker" (your multiverse anchor point). The only exception is your **multiverse identification token** (a smooth river stone engraved with a single, non-recursive geometric shape by a trusted local from your home world). It is inert, natural, and will be viewed as a curious personal totem.
2.  **Sensory Deprivation Drill:** Spend 6 hours in a silent, mist-filled room, blindfolded, with only the sound of a slow dripping faucet. Practice moving by touch, estimating distance by sound distortion, and calming your heartbeat to the drip’s rhythm.
3.  **Mental Rehearsal:** Visualize your first hour:
    *   You are guided into a low vine-chamber. The air (water) is warm, thick.
    *   You are handed a root-fiber mat to sit on. It yields slowly, remembering past shapes.
    *   You are given a bowl. You eat without speaking, listening to the universal sigh.
    *   You try to speak a question, but your voice is swallowed by the water-wall’s bass hum. You learn to communicate by touching a specific vine, pointing, or making a soft, percussive sound on a stone surface.
    *   You feel a faint, deep groan through the floor-mat. No one reacts. It is the stone’s dream.
4.  **Physical Taper:** No vigorous exercise for 48 hours. Your body must enter a state of low metabolic demand, matching the ambient pace.

## **IV. Upon Arrival: Protocol & First Actions**
You will be met by a **Root-Tender** (your guide). They will be skin-dyed with mineral pastes, hair braided with living vine-tendrils. They will not speak your language. Communication is through:
*   **Touch:** A gentle press on the shoulder means “come.”
*   **Sound:** A specific three-tap pattern on a stone means “wait/feel.”
*   **Gesture:** A slow, sweeping hand over a vine means “listen/its story.”

**Your First Act of Respect:** Immediately upon entering your assigned chamber, **remove all fibers from your person** (your travel sackcloth) and fold them neatly. Place your clay tablet and charcoal atop them. This signals you understand you are *in* the dwelling, not *occupying* it. The Root-Tender will then assign you a growing-mat to sleep on.

**Your First Week:** You are a **passive symbiont**. You do not tend. You do not ask “why.” You:
1.  Learn to move without disturbing root-mite rustles.
2.  Learn to distinguish the groans of *active stone-sleep* from the groans of *erosion-creation*.
3.  Learn to drink from a cistern without creating ripples that disrupt the Silkswimmer currents.
4.  Learn the two approved ways to dispose of waste: (a) place solid waste on a designated “erosion-slab” to be slowly dissolved, or (b) drop biodegradable matter into a Stone-Grazer trail to be consumed.
5.  Practice silence. Your thoughts will feel deafening.

## **V. Critical禁忌 (Taboos) - Do Not, Ever**
1.  **Do not** attempt to swim into the Atmospheric Water-curtain for “exploration.” Vision is zero, currents within it are unpredictable, and you will violate the theological boundary of the “dry legend.”
2.  **Do not** touch a *newly weeping* mineral accretion with a tool. You may only redirect it with a bare, clean hand, and only if your guide indicates the path.
3.  **Do not** speak of “sky,” “sun,” “stars,” “dry land,” or “wind.” These are myths of the dead world. The only “above” is the theological void beyond the water-wall.
4.  **Do not** rush a task. If you are asked to clear sediment from a cistern, you do it with a single, slow scoop every ten minutes. Haste cracks the living vine-lining.
5.  **Do not** carve your name, draw a picture, or otherwise impose a permanent, *non-growth* mark. The only legacy is what the stone and vines choose to preserve.
6.  **Do not** bring any offering from the outside multiverse—not a flower, not a song, not a story. Your presence is the offering. Your adaptation is the tribute.

## **VI. The Mindset: You Are a Temporary Organ**
You are not a visitor. You are a **transient cell** in the mesa’s slow body. Your purpose is to *experience the rhythm*, not to extract wonder. Your success is measured by:
*   The day you stop hearing the water-wall as “noise” and feel it as a pulse.
*   The day a root-mite crawls over your foot without you flinching.
*   The day you sit perfectly still for an hour, feeling the mineral slab beneath you *very slightly* rise as new growth accrues.
*   The day you understand that the “ruin” you see is not decay, but the beautiful, necessary process of **erosion-as-creation**, and you feel peace watching a carefully shaped stone seat slowly soften and melt back into the floor.

Your preparation is the **art of un-becoming a modern human**. You pack less and less until you bring only your willingness to dissolve. The mesa will accept you when you stop trying to *see* it and start letting it *feel* you—and then slowly, gently, incorporate you into its memory of vibrations.

**Final Warning:** If you feel panic at the slowness, at the dampness, at the lack of “progress,” you are not ready. Go home. This place will break a fast mind. It will only heal a patient one.
