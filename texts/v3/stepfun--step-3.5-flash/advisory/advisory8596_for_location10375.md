### **Phase 1: The Physics of Survival (Gear & Suit)**
You are a fragile bag of water and organic chemistry in a place designed to dissociate and reassemble matter. Your suit is your temporary skeleton, skin, and ecosystem.

*   **Primary Hull (Pressure & Contamination):** You need a hard-shelled, full-environment suit. Not a simple space suit. Think **deep-sea submersible or hazardous materials suit**. The shell must be:
    *   **Abrasion-Resistant:** Multi-layered composite. Outer layer of sintered tungsten-carbide or ceramic platelets. Middle layer of shear-thickening fluid (like modern liquid armor). Inner layer is a sealed, flexible polymer. The goal is to survive brushing against the viscous conglomerate-sludge or flying abrasive dust.
    *   **Sealed & Positive-Pressure:** Absolutely zero permeation. The internal atmosphere must be maintained at a slight positive pressure to prevent the hyper-oxidized, metallic-particle-laden external air from infiltrating. All seals are mechanical/explosive-bolt, not adhesive. Any breach is a catastrophic oxidation event for your suit and flesh.
    *   **Thermal:** Active cooling is non-negotiable. A powerful, closed-circuit liquid cooling system with a heat exchanger rated for a 60°C+ ambient. The suit's surface should be highly emissive (white or mirrored) to radiate what you can. Insulation is useless here; you must *pump* heat out.
*   **Atmosphere Supply:** Self-contained, compressed-air (or nitrogen-oxygen mix) supply for a minimum of **8 hours**. Do NOT rely on external processing. The air is not just toxic; it's actively corrosive and saturated with conductive particles that would foul any filter in minutes.
*   **Sensory Override:**

    *   **Tactile:** The suit's limbs must have force-feedback actuators. The ground has an unpredictable consistency—sometimes honey-like, sometimes crunching with crystal. You must "feel" the terrain through the suit's feet/mandibles to avoid sinking or getting trapped.
*   **Mobility:** You cannot walk. The sludge will swallow a leg. Think ** miniature, armored crawler or a stabilized, armed exoskeleton with "snowshoe" or "marshshoe" adaptations**. Speed is impossible; movement must be slow, deliberate, and planned, testing the viscosity ahead.

### **Phase 2: The Rules of Engagement (Behavioral Protocols)**
Your presence is a biological anomaly in a world of pure, industrial process. You must be as inert and uninteresting as a pebble in a river.

1.  **The Prime Directive: Do Not Contaminate.**
    *   No organic matter. No food, no waste, no skin cells, no breath moisture. All outputs (CO2, heat, sweat) must be **fully captured, contained, and brought back**. You are a closed system. A single dropped granola bar or shed eyelash is an organic intrusion that the local "process" will detect as novel chemical potential. The Roustabouts would engulf it, dissect it, and its foreign biochemistry would propagate as a "virus" in their mechanical ecology—potentially creating a catastrophic cascade.
    *   No magnetic fields. Your suit's motors and power system must be shielded. The Drift-spinners harvest electrical potential; a strong external field would be like a dinner bell.
    *   No resonant frequencies. Do not hum, whistle, or create steady vibrations. You are a soft object; your goal is to be acoustically invisible against the planet's own deafening chorus. Your movements must be as smooth and non-rhythmic as possible.

2.  **Movement is a Survey, Not an Expedition.**
    *   You do not "climb" a pressure-mound. You **ascend its flank** at a barely perceptible rate, testing every step. The mounds pulse. A "footrest" that is solid one minute may soften slightly the next.
    *   Your path is a parabola. Never go straight. Your goal is to cross between mounds via the higher-viscosity "channels," but you must **minimize contact time** with any single patch of sludge. Prolonged presence may trigger localized "recycling" protocols where the Scrapers increase activity on *your* footprint.
    *   **Eruption-Event Protocol:** When a flash occurs (they are irregular, from minutes to hours apart), **FREEZE**. Do not move. Do not switch on any lights. The event injects new, unstable material—this is a moment of high systemic flux. You are a static, inert object in a storm of becoming. Your HUD should log the event's spectrum but remain passive.

3.  **Observation is Passive Logging.**
    *   Your tools are scanners, not manipulators. No sampling. **No touch.** Use long-range spectroscopic analyzers, seismic microphones, and particle collectors with ultra-fine, one-way intake valves that seal the sample inside an inert canister immediately. You are taking "photographs" with physics, not collecting souvenirs.
    *   Watch the process-entities, but do not interfere. A Scraper's path is a design. A Drift-spinner's pulse frequency is data. A Roustabout consuming a Scraper is a recycling event. You are watching a self-writing book.

### **Phase 3: The Mental & Philosophical Prep**

To stand on a pressure-mound and see, across a sea of identical, trembling blisters, the slow, inevitable, glorious noise of a world building itself from dust and heat and pressure.
