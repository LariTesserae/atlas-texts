Excellent. A place that is not a place, but a *process*. A traveler cannot simply pack a bag and walk in; they must prepare to become a temporary, non-reactive component in an engine that has been running for epochs. Here is how an ordinary human, armed only with multiverse-hopping capability and mundane wits, should prepare for the Cistern of Chorformed.

### **Phase 1: Psychological & Philosophical Calibration (The Most Critical Phase)**

1.  **Shed the "Visitor" Mentality:** You are not a tourist, an explorer, or an intruder. You are an anomaly—a single, free-moving unit in a system of perfect, deterministic interlock. Your goal is **passive integration**, not interaction. Your primary rule: **Do not optimize, do not improve, do not repair.** Your presence is a perturbation; your task is to make that perturbation as mathematically insignificant as possible.
2.  **Embrace Slow Time:** Your human perception of seconds and minutes is an illusion here. The system operates on decadal, geological, and *conceptual* time. You will feel impatience, curiosity, the urge to *do something*. You must deliberately slow your internal clock. Practice sitting motionless for hours, observing a single patch of Scraper-laden wall. Your survival may depend on your ability to out-wait a slow reconfiguration.
3.  **Accept Geometrical Insanity:** Your brain will rebel against the 113.7-degree angles and scale-fluid components. You must not fight the cognitive dissonance. Instead, practice a form of **"peripheral acceptance."** If a corridor seems both close and far, do not stare. Let it be both. Your navigational tools must be tactile and relational (touch, counting junctions, noting pigment patterns on Foremen), not based on Euclidean distance.
4.  **Reframe Life & Intelligence:** The Foremen are not your friends. The Vent-Moths are not pests. The entire ecology is the **nervous system of the Cistern**. A Scraper is a skin cell. A Foreman is a white blood cell with a tool. Any attempt to communicate, aid, or hinder them will be processed as a foreign body presenting a antigen. Your "language" will be extreme stillness and non-interference.

### **Phase 2: Physical & Gear Preparation**

**Clothing & Armor:**
*   **Material:** Do not wear synthetics that shed or fluoresce. Opt for a single-piece, seamless suit of **undyed, tightly-woven hemp or linen**. It should be slightly loose to avoid snagging on microscopic edges. The goal is to be as uninteresting material-wise as the ambient dust.
*   **Color:** **Absolute Matte Grey** is the only camouflage. Any logo, stripe, or bright thread is a flashing beacon in the bleached light.
*   **Footwear:** Thick-soled, seamless boots with **no aggressive tread**. You are walking on cast glass and sintered slag. Your steps must not leave marks that could be interpreted as stress. Consider **felt-soled slippers** worn over boots, to be removed when stillness is required.
*   **Protection:** The environment is a **delicate abrasive**. Sharp edges are everywhere. Wear **simple leather or thick canvas gloves** and **full-face coverage** (a smooth, grey hood with a **smoked-glass visor** at a 90-degree angle to your line of sight—this breaks direct sightlines, helping with the geometry warping). No visor polish.

**Tools & Supplies:**
*   **Navigation:** A **simple, silent wind-up pocket watch** (for personal pacing, not timekeeping here). A **set of smooth, numbered river stones** (to mark junctions by tactile count). **No electronics.** Any signal, pulse, or emission is a scream in the Quiet Symphony.
*   **Water:** The only reliable source is the condensation weeping and the periodic mist pulses. You must **passively collect it**. Carry a **polished, wide-mouthed, sterile glass vessel** (non-reactive). Place it under a weeping overhang or in a designated basin during a pulse. Do not channel or dam flows. Your vessel must be inert and leave no trace.
*   **Food:** The local "flora/fauna" is not for you. You must carry all sustenance. Think **high-density, zero-crumb, neutral-flavor food bars** wrapped in **waxed paper**. Consumption must be done in a sealed, non-descript location, with all waste sealed in an **impermeable, odorless container** to be carried out. You are a closed system.
*   **Signal & Emergency:** A **single, smokeless, slow-burn chemical flare** (red light, not white) for ONLY absolute life-or-death (e.g., a Foreman-directed subduction event). Its use will mark you as a catastrophic anomaly. A **loud, sharp whistle** is a last-resort to startle a Scraper swarm if one is crawling *into* your clothing.
*   **Medical:** **Basic trauma kit only.** No exotic antiseptics (smell/chemicals). Simple bandages, a tourniquet. Assume all wounds are from your own stupidity. The system will not help you.

### **Phase 3: Behavioral Protocols**

1.  **The Stillness Drill:** At least once per "day," find a shadowed patch of glass. Sit perfectly still for **4 hours**. Do not scratch, adjust clothing, or speak. Observe. Let the Scrapers crawl over your boots. Let the Vent-Moths land on your shoulders. Your goal is to become part of the scenery, a static mineral formation.
2.  **Movement as Deposition:** Do not walk. **Glide.** Place each foot with infinite care. Test surfaces with a gloved hand before full weight. You are not traversing; you are being *deposited* from point A to point B. Your path should mimic the slow curves of conduits, not cut straight lines.
3.  **Reading the Foremen:** From a distance of 10+ meters, observe one. Catalog its pigment shifts. Do not mimic them. Do not stare directly. Your recognition of their language is itself an intrusion. Simply note the *correlation* between a shift and an action (a Foreman taps a wall, pigment flashes amber; a moment later, a nearby conduit's temperature changes by 0.5 degrees). This is how you learn the ambient rhythms.
4.  **The Mist Pulse Protocol:** When the rhythmic mist vents begin (a deep, subsonic creak you feel in your molars), **do not run for water**. This is the system's exhalation. Move to a pre-identified basin *well before* it starts. Sit still. Let the mist coat you. Your collection vessel will fill passively. When the mist stops, you may, with glacial slowness, seal it and continue.
5.  **The "Material Memory" Test:** If you accidentally strike a wall, **freeze**. Do not look at the ghostly afterimage. Look away. The system is registering stress. Your further attention may amplify it. Wait for the ghost to fade (it always does) before moving. Treat it like you've coughed in a library.
6.  **Scale Fluidity Countermeasure:** If you perceive a Foreman as both thumb-sized and house-sized, **close your eyes**. Touch the nearest object (a pipe, the glass) and ground yourself in its *actual*, singular tactile reality. The ambiguity is a perceptual error, not a physical one. Your body is one size. Anchor to that.

### **Phase 4: Mindset & Exit Strategy**

*   **You are a ghost.** Your memories, your sense of self, your narrative are the only chaotic elements here. Bracket them. Function on pure, immediate sensation: *cold glass underfoot, smell of ozone, weight of the visor, soundless vibration*.
*   **Your exit point is not a door, but a condition.** You will hop from the multiverse to the Cistern. You must return the same way. Before you leave your home universe, designate a **single, unique, small, inert object** (a specific grey stone, a particular coin). Place it in your pocket. Your return hop will be anchored to that object's *memory in your mind*. The return signal is not sent; it is *remembered*. Therefore, **do not lose the object, and do not forget its exact sensory details.** If your mind becomes overwhelmed by the Cistern's logic, you may forget how to return. This is the greatest risk.
*   **You will not be the same.** The Cistern does not change you by magic; it changes you by **perspective**. You will leave with the indelible understanding that **everything is a component, and persistence is the only value.** You may find yourself unable to tolerate waste, chaos, or empty space in your home world. This is the souvenir.

**Final Word:** The Cistern of Chorformed is not hostile. Hostility implies intent. It is **indifferent actualization**. It is a theorem made manifest. You are an unscheduled variable. Your success is measured by your ability to be ignored, to pass through its gears without causing a single, additional micron of friction, and to remember—with crystal clarity—the precise feel of the grey stone in your pocket, so you can remember your way back to a world that, for a little while, will seem terrifyingly *un-made*. Prepare for silence. Prepare for perfection. Prepare to be nothing.
