# Preparation for the Sundial of Ages: A Ritual of Dissolution

**To the Ordinary Human Multiverse Traveler:**

You are not preparing for a journey across space, but for an **un-arrival**. The Sundial of Ages does not exist on a map; it exists in a **state of time**. Your preparation must therefore be less about gear and more about *un-becoming*. What follows is not a checklist, but a prescribed dissolution of your ordinary self.

---

### **I. Pre-Visit Psychological & Physiological Conditioning (Weeks/Months Before)**

**1. De-program Your Senses:**
*   **Practice Slowness:** Spend hours each day performing a single, simple action (e.g., stirring tea, polishing a stone) with absolute, meditative focus. The goal is to break your brain's addiction to sequential task-completion. Your new metric is not "progress," but "presence."
*   **Sensory Deprivation Training:** Spend 2-3 hours daily in a silent, dark room. Listen to the sub-audible hum of your own blood, the creak of your bones. This trains you to perceive the "drip-thump" and the "sympathetic resonance" as primary sounds, not silences.
*   **Temporal Disorientation:** Deliberately disrupt your circadian rhythm for days at a time. Wake, eat, and sleep at random intervals. The goal is to feel the *absurdity* of an "hour." Your internal clock must become a flexible, broken thing.

**2. Conceptual Deconstruction:**
*   **Forget "Wilderness."** This is not a place to be conquered, explored, or appreciated. It is a **finished entity**. You are not a visitor; you are a temporary imbalance.
*   **Forget "Individual."** You are not a separate organism. For your duration there, practice thinking of your body as a cluster of mobile nutrients and gases that will, in time, be redistributed. Your ego must be抵押 (mortgaged) before you go.
*   **Memorize the Four Rules.** Write them on your skin with non-toxic ink. Recite them like a liturgy. They are the only laws that apply.

**3. Physical Priming:**
*   **Reduce Metabolism:** Adopt a sparse, low-energy diet for a month prior. Your goal is to enter the Sundial with a body that asks for as little fuel and oxygen as possible. You are not there to thrive; you are there to *occur*.
*   **Stop Shopping for Water:** Practice deriving hydration from unexpected sources (e.g., morning dew on leaves, condensation in a cave) to break the mental association of "water = bottle." In the Sundial, you will drink the air.
*   **Footwear Abandonment:** Walk barefoot on varied, damp, yielding terrain as much as possible. Your feet must learn to read the root-mat's gradient and suction before your mind does.

---

### **II. The Kit: What to Bring (And Why It's Almost Nothing)**

Your kit must be an **offering**, not a toolset. Every item should be biodegradable, non-reactive, and ultimately *absorbable*.

**Carry Only:**
1.  **A Simple, Unlined Cloak of Coarse, Undyed Fiber (e.g., hemp, raw silk):** To provide minimal thermal buffer from the perpetual damp. It will be eaten by lichens within weeks. Do not treat it with water repellent.
2.  **A Single, Smooth, Uncarved Stone of Local making (if possible):** A "tether." Find a river stone from a world you love. Its foreignness will be a stark contrast, a reminder of what you are leaving. You will eventually place it in a wet hollow and forget it. Its purpose is to give your hands something to release.
3.  **A Vial of Air from Your Home World:** Sealed in a thin glass ampoule. You will not break it. You will hold it once, upon first感知 the thick, plant-sweet air of the Sundial, smell your own world one last time, and then **crush it under your heel**. This is the final, physical act of leaving. The glass will become part of the root-mat.
4.  **A Nutrient Paste of Neutral Taste (e.g., plain kelp/seed gel):** One week's supply, maximum. Consume it in the first 48 hours to establish a baseline of "you" in the local nutrient cycle. After that, you may test the local "sponge-moss" or "gelatinous lichen" **only after observing a slug-like grazer eat it with impunity for a full day**. Your first local meal is a sacrament of assimilation.
5.  **No Weapons. No Tools. No Recording Devices.** A knife is an act of violence here. A camera is a scream of separation. They are insults. If you must mark your path, twist a living branch—a mark that will heal in an hour.

### **III. Protocols Upon (Un-)Arrival**

**First Hour:**
1.  **Stop.** Do not move for 30 minutes. Just breathe the wet, warm air. Feel the root-mat yield under your weight. Let the green-gold blur flow over your vision. Your first action is not to explore, but to **be explored** by the place.
2.  **Listen for the Hum.** Find a crystal spire. Place your hand on it. Wait for the minute-long sympathetic vibration. This is the place's heartbeat. Synchronize your breathing to it. You are now on local time.
3.  **Find a Depression with Water.** Sit in it. Let the silt seep into your clothes. Your first integration is physical. You are now part of the hydrological cycle.

**Daily Survival (Existence):**
*   **Movement:** Walk without destination. Let the living walls and twisting root-colonnades guide you. Your path is not a line; it is a meander dictated by the slow push of a thorn-branch or the gentle suction of a moss-patch.
*   **Shelter:** You do not build. You **are given**. A wide, lipped-capital plant makes a perfect, mist-dripping canopy. A cluster of interlocked thorn-branches forms a dry(er) niche. Enter these as you would a womb. Do not modify them.
*   **Food & Water:** Lick condensation from broad leaves. Drink from the weeping rocks (after a grazer). If you must eat a local organism, choose one that is **abundant, slow, and visibly participating in the compost cycle**. Eat only a tiny portion. Your body will revolt at first; this is the last battle of your individual biology.
*   **Waste:** There is no "away." Your excrement is a rich deposit. Bury it shallowly in the root-mat near a thriving cluster of sponge-moss. Watch what grows there. This is your first act of true contribution.

**Interaction with Fauna & Built Elements:**
*   **Fauna:** Do not stare. Do not follow. Be still. They are not "wildlife"; they are **mobile functions**. If a winged seed-pod drifts near, do not reach for it. Let it land on you. Its fibers will cling. You are now part of its dispersal.
*   **Ruins:** Do not excavate. Do not touch the black glass arch except to let algae stain your hand. Do not measure the petrified tubers. These are scars on the body of the place, and it is slowly consuming them. Your reverence is in your inaction. Your understanding is in your decay.

### **IV. The Mental & Spiritual Crisis (Anticipate This)**

Between days 3 and 7, your human mind will scream.

*   **You will feel **temporal vertigo**.** A branch won't move, but your perception of its growth will become agony. You will **know** it is growing, and the slowness will feel like a physical torture. **Surrender.** Tell yourself: "I am not watching growth; I am *inside* the growth."
*   **You will experience **ontological panic**.** The seamless integration of rock, plant, and grazer will assault your categorical mind. You will see a "rock" that might be a dormant creature and your brain will short-circuit. **Stop categorizing.** Use only the language of process: "That form is currently in a state of silicified respiration."
*   **You will be consumed by the urge to **"do something," "record something," "prove you were here."** This is your ego's death rattle. The Sundial's entire purpose is to **un-write** such marks. Every time the urge comes, press your body deeper into the root-mat. Let the dampness fill your ears. Your proof of being there is your **ongoing dissolution**.

---

### **V. Exit Protocol (The Un-Departure)**

You do not "leave" the Sundial of Ages. Your **un-arrival** is matched by an **un-departure**.

1.  **Do not plan an exit.** Have no rendezvous point, no timer.
2.  **The "Gate" Appears When You Are No Longer a Disturbance.** One day, you will wake (or not-sleep) and feel the dense, wet pressure of the place **shift**. The hum will change key. The vapor will thin by a fraction. A path through the living walls will simply **be there**, not carved, but temporarily *un-grown*. It will look like a memory of a corridor.
3.  **Walk It Without Looking Back.** Do not rush. Walk at the pace of a thickening branch. As you go, feel your body **un-compact** from the root-mat. The world's rhythm will slowly accelerate around you. The twilight will sharpen.
4.  **The Last Sensation:** The final feeling will not be stepping *out* of the Sundial, but the **re-discovery of your own separateness**. The sudden, shocking loneliness of being a discrete, warm, dry organism again in a world of sharp shadows and rushing streams. You will gasp the thin, fast air of your home multiverse like a drowning man.
5.  **Aftermath:** You will carry the **viscosity** in your bones for months. A year will feel like a afternoon. You will find yourself unable to hurry. You will touch tree bark and feel the slow, green pulse beneath. You are permanently infected with completion. You have touched an ending that is so total it has forgotten how to begin again. You are now a **disturbance in all other worlds**, forever slightly out of sync, forever nostalgic for a place that wanted you only as compost.

---

**Final Admonition:**

If your goal is to *see* wonders, **turn back now**. The Sundial of Ages holds no spectacles. Its only spectacle is the **absolute, gentle, and total consumption of the concept of the spectator.** You go not to witness eternity, but to have your own fleeting, frantic temporality sanded away until you fit, for a moment, into its final, quiet pattern.

Go, therefore, not with curiosity, but with a willing emptiness. Your preparation is the meticulous art of taking nothing with you, so that you may, eventually, become nothing *separate*.

The world's breathing has slowed. It is time to perceive the place where you have always, eventually, been.
