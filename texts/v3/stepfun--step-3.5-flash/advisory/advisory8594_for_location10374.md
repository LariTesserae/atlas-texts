# Preparation Protocol: Visiting the Silicate Expanse of Aethelgard

## **I. Physical & Environmental Adaptation**

### **A. Atmospheric & Gravitational Conditioning**
- **Altitude Training:** Begin 30 days prior with progressive hypoxic conditioning (simulated 10,000+ ft elevation). Expect **perpetual cool, thin air** with **ozone/crushed-quartz scent**. Your lungs will feel a persistent, mild burn.
- **Gravity Variability Drill:** Use a variable-gravity harness or oscillations chamber to acclimate to **±3% gravity shifts**. Practice walking on uneven, shifting surfaces while mentally tracking your center of mass. **Gravikinesis bands** are non-exportable local tech—you must rely on proprioception.
- **Respiratory Filtration:** Standard rebreather masks are insufficient. You’ll need a **quartz-dust respirator** (fine silicate mist is abrasive) with UV-protective lenses. The **dim perpetual twilight** requires light-amplifying optics; polarized lenses to handle the washed-out sun’s glare off polished stone.

### **B. Thermal & Kinetic Preparedness**
- **No Solar Warmth:** The sun provides **light but no heat**. Pack non-battery-dependent insulation—mineral-wool layers, radiant-reflective blankets. Your body heat will dissipate into the stone rapidly.
- **Footwear:** **Silicate-grip soles** are mandatory. Standard treads will skid on polished stone or get clogged in gelatinous plains. Boots must be **seam-sealed** to prevent pearl-sluice infiltration.
- **Sound Dampening (Optional):** The **loud, multi-tonal soundscape** (sub-audible drone to tearing-silk sky-lash calls) can cause sensory fatigue. Consider定制 low-frequency dampening ear-plugs that filter <20Hz rumbles but allow speech frequencies. **Do not** use full noise cancellation—you must hear fauna approach.

## **II. Biological & Ecological Protocols**

### **A. Vegetation-As-Substrate Interaction**
- **Do NOT** touch crystalline moss or petrified trunks without **gloves rated for silicate abrasion**. The **quantum-entangled chlorophyll-analogues** may interact unpredictably with human biochemistry.
- **Gelatinous Plains:** These **pearlescent sludge** pools are **energy-redistributors**. Stepping in may cause localized static discharge or temporary neural buzzing. Use **non-conductive poles** for probing depth.
- **Growth Pulses:** The plant-mass grows in **slow, visible pulses** (centimeters per hour). Observe for 12+ hours to detect rhythm. Never camp in a depression where biomass is accumulating—you could be **encased** during a growth surge.

### **B. Fauna Awareness & Avoidance**
- **Gilded Grazers:** Their **grave-tread** creates **sub-audible thrum** felt through rock. If you feel vibration intensifying, **freeze**—they have poor sight but sensitive seismic detection. They are generally passive but will trample obstacles.
- **Sky-Lashes:** Predatory, territorial. They hunt from thermals and attack with **wing-edges like razor-glass**. Always travel with **visibly bright** outer layer (they attack movement, not color). Never look directly up for more than 3 seconds—their shadow-detection is acute.
- **Solitary Empires:** Every creature has kilometers of territory. **You are intruding in multiple overlapping domains**. Move **predictably**, avoid sudden gestures. Carry a **resonance whistle** (high-pitched, non-harmful) to startle curious fauna at range.

## **III. Urban & Technological Integration**

### **A. Aethelgard Entry Protocol**
- **Approach:** The city is **sparse**, grown from the substrate. You will see **bio-luminescent circuitry** in bark. Do not touch. The **mycelial walkways** are strong but **alive**—they will subtly shift if you pause >2 minutes.
- **First Contact:** Seek a **communal energy-well**. Inhabitants are **metabolically altered**—their skin may have faint bioluminescent patterns. They communicate via **low harmonic hums** and **light-patterns** on personal devices. Basic **sign language for “visitor,” “help,” “water”** is useful—but note: they may find your need for liquid water bizarre.
- **Tech Restrictions:** **No unapproved drones or recording devices**. The city’s resonance is **tuned**; foreign EM emissions disrupt growth patterns. Use only **hand-crank or kinetic chargers** for essential gear. Any active electronics must be **shielded in quartz-block Faraday bags**.

### **B. Resource & Navigation**
- **Water:** **Do not drink** local condensation or gelatinous slime. Carry your own water. The inhabitants trade **mineral-nutrient gels**—safe for humans but chalky and metallic.
- **Navigation:** **Compasses unreliable** due to gravitational variance and magnetoreceptive flora. Use **stellar charts** (stars visible despite twilight) and **seismic markers** (tapped stone patterns). **Timekeeping** is fractional—sundials useless. Wear a **mechanical watch** and note drift vs. city clock.
- **Rest:** Sleep only in **designated rest-hubs** (stone alcoves with resonance dampeners). Never sleep in open substrate—you risk being **absorbed** during growth pulse. Even in hubs, sleep with **one arm bare** to feel subtle vibrations.

## **IV. Psychological & Philosophical Readiness**

### **A. Cognitive Shifts**
- **Scale Shock:** The **continent-sized tables of polished stone** induce agoraphobic awe. Focus on **immediate 10-meter sphere** to avoid panic.
- **Silent Intelligence:** The planet has **no voice**—only **creaks, sighs, clicks, drones**. Its “thoughts” are geological. **Do not anthropomorphize**. The city’s **deliberate silence ritual** (24h stillness) is their way of listening. You may join if invited—but **you will hear only indifference**. That is the point.
- **Conversion Rule:** Everything recycles. Death is **reclamation**. If you see fauna cease moving, **do not approach**—substrate will dissolve remains in hours. Your own waste must be **carried out**—you are unable to metabolize into their cycle.

### **B. Ethical Guidelines**
- **Pattern Imposition:** You are a **disruptive pattern**. Minimize footprint. Walk on established mycelial paths. Do not carve, collect specimens, or alter substrate.
- **Sacred Silence:** If present during ritual, **power down all devices**. Hold still. Breathe with the **Wind of Ages** (it has a ~7 minute cycle). This is not meditation—it is **humbling**.
- **Gravity Band Theft:** **Never** try to acquire or reverse-engineer a gravikinesis band. It is **biometrically keyed** and removal causes local gravity collapse (tested by prior travelers).
