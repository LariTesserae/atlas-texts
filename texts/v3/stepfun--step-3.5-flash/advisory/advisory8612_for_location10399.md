# Preparation for Visiting the Deep-Works: A Guide for the Ordinary Multiverse Traveler

**Foreword:**  
You are not preparing for a journey to a *place*. You are preparing for an immersion into a *state of being*—a slow, cold, and profoundly silent testament to the final chapter of civilization. The Deep-Works does not welcome; it simply *is*, and you will be a brief, warm anomaly within it. Your preparation must address the physical, mental, and metaphysical assault this environment constitutes. There are no thrills here, only the haunting weight of geological time and architectural oblivion.

---

## **I. Physical Preparedness: Gear & Bodily Conditioning**

### **A. Navigation & Perception (The Problem of No Horizon)**
*   **Primary Sense:** You must relearn to navigate by **touch and sound**. Sight is useless. Your gear must facilitate this.
    *   **Gloves:** Thick, waterproof, tactile-sensitive gloves (likethose used for cold-water diving or delicate archaeological work). You will be feeling your way over every surface. Protect your hands from the cold, the wet, and the chemical/enzymatic activity of the growths. Assume the mosses and fungi are mildly acidic or bio-reactive.
    *   **Walking Aids:** Two sturdy, collapsible trekking poles with broad, spiked feet. Use them to probe depth of water, test substrate stability ahead, and maintain balance on the slick, yielding moss. They are your primary "eyes" for scale and depth.
    *   **Personal Sonar/Lidar Device:** If available from a higher-tech origin universe, a handheld, passive sonar or low-frequency pulse device that renders a crude 3D map of your immediate vicinity (10-20 meter radius). Do not rely on it entirely—it will fail in complex geometries. Use it to spot sudden drops or overhangs your poles miss.
    *   **No Light Source:** Do not bring a light. It will destroy your dark adaptation within minutes, create blinding reflections off the water, and attract nothing (there is nothing to see). Your eyes must adjust to the absolute blackness, learning to perceive the faintest gradients of bioluminescence and the "negative space" of shadows against the slight ambient grey glow from your own bioluminescent growth (you will acquire some). A dim red-filtered light, used only for absolute emergency, may preserve some night vision, but its use is a violation of the place's integrity and will make you feel grotesque.

### **B. Environmental Protection**
*   **Thermal Layering:** A full-body, waterproof, breathable underlayer (like a drysuit liner). Over this, a thick, wool/fleece insulating layer. Over that, a rigid, waterproof shell (not flexible fabric—the "plates" of your gear should mimic the seamless basaltic plates of the Works). The goal is to create a microclimate. The chill is *penetrating*; it leaches heat from bone and thought.
*   **Footwear:** High, waterproof boots with aggressive, non-clogging tread. The substrate is a mix of soft moss, stiff root-lattice, and polished stone. You will wade, so boots must be fully sealed. Inside, wear two layers of socks.
*   **Water Purification:** **Do not drink the water.** Assume it is saturated with dissolved minerals, metals, and complex organic leachates from the built materials. It may be slow-acting toxic or psychoactive. Carry a minimum of 3 days of water from your origin point, in insulated containers.
*   **Air Filtration:** The air is "thick" and "wet." It may carry fine particulates of biological or mineral origin. A simple, high-efficiency particulate filtering mask (N99 or equivalent) is non-negotiable. You are breathing the dust of a decaying world.
*   **Decontamination Kit:** Upon exiting, you will be coated in a dense, clinging ecology. Have a protocol for stripping and bagging all gear outside a clean zone. The biologicals here are alien. Do not introduce them to other biospheres casually.

### **C. Sustenance & Health**
*   **High-Calorie, Zero-Prep Food:** Calorie-dense bars, gels. No cooking. The sense of taste will be drowned by the ambient "flavor" of stone and decay anyway.
*   **Medical:** Focus on hypothermia treatment, wound care for slips and cuts against the (deceptively) sharp edges of petrified rope or crystal formations. Anti-fungal/antibacterial creams are essential. Assume any cut will quickly be colonized by local biology.
*   **Psychological First-Aid:** Carry a strong, simple stimulant (caffeine pill) and a mild sedative (like diphenhydramine). You will need to jolt yourself out of catatonic awe or lull yourself into fitful sleep against the oppressive, watchful silence.

## **II. Mental & Philosophical Preparedness**

**This is the most critical part.** Your mind is your most vulnerable organ here.

### **A. Shed Your Metrics**
*   **Abandon Maps & Distance:** There are no horizons. You cannot pace out a kilometer. Your sense of scale will lie to you. A "dome" you walk across for an hour may be a hundred meters or a hundred kilometers. Your mind will oscillate between feeling like a mote on a leaf and a giant traversing a continent. **Accept that you cannot know.** Let the poles and sonar define your local world. Your goal is not to traverse, but to *experience*.
*   **Abandon Time:** The only dynamic is growth at the speed of stone. Your watch is a lie. You may feel you've been walking for minutes when hours have passed, or vice versa. Mark time by your own bodily rhythms (heartbeat, thirst) or by a pre-set, silent vibration timer. Checking a clock is an anchor to a reality that does not exist here.
*   **Abandon Purpose (as you know it):** You are not here to discover, to conquer, to map, to collect. You are here to **bear witness to a finality.** Your purpose is contemplative: to feel the pressure of total, completed entropy. Any "discovery" is an illusion—you are walking on the roof of a buried city, not finding a new one.

### **B. Mental Frameworks to Adopt**
1.  **You Are a Ghost Temporarily Made Flesh:** You are the anomaly. The moss, the water, the stone—they are the permanent reality. Your warmth, your movement, your breath fogging in the cold air—these are the brief, violent intrusions. Act with the quiet reverence of a specter.
2.  **Embrace the "Negative Space":** The haunting is in the *absence* of the makers. Do not look for their handprints. Feel them in the *lack of an angle*, in the *perfection of the curve*, in the *absolute fusion* of built and grown. The builder's legacy is not in a statue, but in a stone that has forgotten it was chosen.
3.  **The Silence is a Substance:** The infrequent *plink* is not a sound; it is an **event**. It is the only punctuation in an endless, liquid sentence. It will startle you, then become a heartbeat. The "sub-audible hum of slow growth" is the true soundtrack—a vibration in your molars and sinuses. Learn to "listen" to this vibration. It tells you the world is *still working*, just not in any way you comprehend.
4.  **You Are Part of the Substrate Now:** For your duration here, you *are* another layer. Your sweat, your shed skin cells, your breath's moisture—they will be absorbed, become part of the slow chemical digestion. This is not a horror, but a humility. You are being composted by history.

### **C.Psychological Pitfalls to Anticipate & Defuse**
*   **The Panic of Scale:** The mind rebels against a world with no up or down, no edge. You may feel vertigo, nausea, dissociation. **Countermeasure:** Focus on the immediate 2-meter sphere around your hands and feet. Anchor yourself to the tactile: the feel of the moss, the resistance of the root-lattice, the chill of the basalt. Repeat a mantra: "Here. Now. This texture."
*   **The Horror of Erasure:** The realization that the civilization that built this left **no name, no art, no recognizable form**. Only a smoothed geometry. This is the ultimate futility. **Countermeasure:** Pre-frame your visit as a meditation on impermanence. You are visiting a grave that has decided to become a garden, and has forgotten the name on the stone.
*   **The Temptation of the Handprint:** That "ghostly imprint" you imagined is the deepest trap. It tempts you with narrative, with connection. **Resist it.** The power of the Deep-Works is its *lack* of story. To impose a story—a tragic builder, a lost race—is to diminish the profound, impersonal truth of this place: **that all form, given enough time and patient biology, returns to featureless, fertile ground.**
*   **Auditory Hallucinations:** The silence is so profound your brain will invent sounds. Distant music? Whispering voices? **Countermeasure:** Recognize them immediately as your own brain's protest against the void. Label them: "Memory noise. Not real." Let them pass like the *plink*.

## **III. Protocol & Conduct**

### **A. Entry & Exit**
*   **Entry:** Do not announce your arrival. There is no one to announce to. Step from your arrival point (assume a door, a rift, a descent) and **immediately go still and silent for 10 minutes**. Breathe. Listen. Adapt your eyes. Let the first wave of disorientation pass before moving.
*   **Movement:** Slow. Deliberate. Your steps should be quiet. You are not hiking; you are **circulating**.
*   **Interaction:** Do not alter anything. Do not take souvenirs. Do not mark surfaces. You are a visitor in a cathedral of decay. Your only interaction is with your own perception.
*   **Exit:** Do not rush to leave. The moment you decide "I must get out" is the moment you forfeit the experience. Your exit should be as unceremonious as your entry. When your resources (time, water, mental fortitude) reach a pre-set, conservative limit, **calmly retrace your senses**, not your steps (you cannot retrace steps). Use your accumulated tactile map to find your way back to the point of origin. The place does not care if you leave.

### **B. What to Bring (The Minimal Kit)**
1.  **The Suit:** Full waterproof/breathable shell with thermal layers, as described.
2.  **Gloves & Boots:** As described.
3.  **Trekking Poles (x2).**
4.  **Passive Sonar/Lidar Handheld (if available).**
5.  **3L Water (sealed).**
6.  **3 Days High-Calorie Food.**
7.  **Basic First-Aid Kit + Anti-Fungal Cream.**
8.  **Mask (N99).**
9.  **Small Notebook & Grease Pencil:** Not for notes, but for one thing: before entry, write on the first page: **"This place has no story. I am the story. I will leave no mark."** Read it if you feel the urge to narrativize.
10. **Silent Timer (vibrating).**
11. **Stimulant & Sedative Pills.**
12. **Decontamination Bags (heavy-duty).**

**What NOT to Bring:** Any weapon. Any recording device (camera, audio recorder). Any communication device (radio, phone). Any book. Any tool. Any item with a reflective surface.

## **IV. The Aftermath: Reintegration**

**You will not come back "the same."** The Deep-Works rewires your sense of space, time, and significance.

*   **Expect to feel "hollow"** in bright, noisy, horizontal places. The sky will feel like a fraud. Sunlight will seem aggressively thin and meaningless.
*   **You will be haunted by the *plink*.** It may echo in your dreams. The silence of your own world will now have a new, dense, wet quality.
*   **You may develop a phobia of angles.** Perfect corners may feel arrogant, violent, temporary. You may find yourself drawn to worn stone, smooth river rocks, things that have forgotten their edges.
*   **Your concept of "ruin" is destroyed.** A crumbling castle is vibrant, noisy, *story-filled* compared to this. True ruin is not broken columns; it is a world so perfectly recycled that the memory of the break is gone.
*   **You have visited the end of history.** Not with a bang, but with a slow, osmotic blending. The greatest monument is one that has achieved perfect anonymity.

**Final Admonition:**  
You are going to the place where **architecture goes to die and become terrain**. You are not an explorer. You are a ghost visiting a tomb that has decided, successfully, to be a garden. Your preparation is for the dissolution of your own humanity—your need for story, for scale, for purpose, for light. If you can walk out again, you will have seen the final, quiet truth: everything made is eventually unmade, not by disaster, but by the relentless, gentle, **slimy** patience of what grows on it.

Go softly. Go cold. Go without a name for what you have seen. Bring nothing back but the chill in your bones and the knowledge that the world you knew is just a thin, loud skin over a deep, ancient, swallowing dark.
