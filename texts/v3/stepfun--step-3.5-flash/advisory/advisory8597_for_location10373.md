# Preparation for the Singing Saltbowl of Tethos

## 1. **Physiological & Sensory Acclimatization**

You are not entering an environment—you are entering a **single, coherent acoustic event** that happens to have a floor and walls.

- **Auditory retraining:** For 2–3 weeks prior to transit, wear **high-fidelity, flat-response earplugs** or active-noise-canceling headphones set to a **-25 dB reduction** during all waking hours. This will mildly dull your hearing, forcing your brain to rely more on **bone conduction perception** and **tactile vibration sensing**. Practice identifying sounds through jaw/cheekbone resonance (e.g., hum a note and feel it in your skull).  
  - *Goal:* To perceive the bowl’s soundscape not as “noise” but as a **textured field of pressure waves** you can navigate.

- **Respiratory prep:** The air is thin, dry, and mineral-laden. Use a **simple N95 mask** or **woven silk scarf** for the first 48 hours to filter fine salt-crystalline dust. Train with **diaphragmatic breathing** to maximize oxygen extraction. Simulate dry-air conditions by spending time in climate-controlled dehumidified rooms (≤15% humidity).

- **Tactile communication mastery:** Spend 1 week learning **Tethan-style manual sign language** (study their documented gestures: hand shapes on resonant surfaces, finger-tracing in salt, body taps with coded rhythms). Also practice **vibration messaging**: using a small drum pad or stretched hide, send/receive simple patterns through touch alone.

## 3. **Acoustic & Behavioral Protocols**

### **The Grinding Cycle (Daily Dynamics)**
- **Morning Chord (Pre-Grind):** Resonance-cups begin humming. **Do not speak.** Use sign or touch-communication. This is the **tuning period**. Observe which structures are humming “pleasantly” (stable) vs. discordantly (cracking risk).
- **The Grinding (Peak):** Salt fracturing cacophony. **Movement is hazardous.** Stay in **damped shelters** or **deep fungal groves** that naturally filter frequencies. If caught outside: crouch low, cover ears with fur-padded hands, face away from salt ledges (vibrations can dislodge shards).
- **Echo Decay (Evening):** Sound dulls slightly. Best time for **surface travel** between settlements. Listen for the **Grit-Sloth’s near-silence**—a subtle *absence* in the noise, like a moving shadow. Never mimic its silence (bad omen to Tethans).

### **Structural Interaction**
- **Never hammer, strike, or shout** near Tethan dwellings.  
- **Enter a hut by touching the hide wall first:** feel its vibration. A **happy hut** hums at a single, clear frequency under your palm. A **sick hut** buzzes erratically or feels dead-still (silence-death risk).
- **Sleep on woven lattice platforms** (like Tethans)—never directly on salt. Floor vibrations will resonate into your skeleton.

### **Fauna & Flora Etiquette**
- **Resonance-cups:** Do not damage. Their trembling is **metabolic**—stopping it (by excessive silencing) harms them. If a cup feels cold and still, it’s dying—alert a Tethan.
- **Grit-Sloth:** Observe only. Never attempt to hunt without Tethan guidance. Its silence is **active biological dampening**, not an absence of sound—it *absorbs* vibrations. Watching it move is **hypnotic**—like seeing a hole in a waterfall.

## 4. **Psychological Conditioning**

- **Embrace the paradoxes:**  
  - *Lush dryness* → Touch fungal folds, taste salt (spit it out—too mineral-rich).  
  - *Bright darkness* → The glow has no direction—it’s in the air. Shadows are soft-edged.  
  - *Deafening silence-threatening* → Pure quiet feels like suffocation. Train to tolerate **graded sound reduction** using earplugs, then remove them gradually.  
- **Accept non-verbal primacy:** You will be **mute** most of the time. This is not a loss but a **clarity**—communication becomes intentional, close-range, and physical.  
- **Bone-perception:** Practice “listening” with your **teeth** (clench gently, feel vibrations), **feet** (stand barefoot on a salt pan, feel the grind), **sternum** (place hand over heart, distinguish pulse from world-hum).

## 5. **Entry Sequence: First 2 Hours in the Bowl**

1. **Transit arrival** at the **bowl’s rim** (via guided Tethan party—never alone).  
2. **Immediate sensory collapse:** Sound hits you as **pressure**, not noise. You may feel nauseous, disoriented. **Crouch, close eyes, focus on one vibration** (e.g., your own breath through mask).  
3. **Tethan guide will take your hands**, place them on a **damped hide wall**. Feel the stable hum. This is your **reality anchor**.  
4. **Remove earplugs slowly** (if used). Let your skull absorb the grind.  
5. **First task:** Help hang **mineral dust filters** (woven sinew bags) on a resonance-cup. This **modifies** its tone—you are now a **tuner**, not a passerby.  
6. **Do not speak aloud for the first 24 hours.** Use your learned signs. Your voice, if used, will feel like a **violence**—a jagged spike in the smooth roar. Tethans will flinch.

## 6. **Long-Term Stay: Adaptation Checklist**

- [ ] **Ear-callus development:** After 3 days, you may notice **thickened skin inside ear canals**—natural dampening. Do not clean it.  
- [ ] **Bone-consonance:** You start **hearing your own heartbeat in tune** with certain cup frequencies. This is good—your body is syncing.  
- [ ] **Salt-foot:** Soles will crack from dryness. **Daily unguent** of diluted saline and animal fat.  
- [ ] **Light-adaption:** Pupils will constrict permanently. Bright “day” will feel like twilight; “night” (same glow) will feel like deep dusk.  
- [ ] **Silence-phobia cure:** You will **crave** the Grinding after 48 hours. Pure quiet (in a sealed chamber) will induce panic—**acoustic starvation**. This is normal.

## 7. **Exodus Protocol (Leaving the Bowl)**

- **Day before departure:** Absorb as much sound as possible. Sit in the center, eyes open, mouth slightly ajar—let the grind **resonate in your sinus cavities**. This “sound-memory” will help your brain readjust to Earth’s relative silence.  
- **Exit at peak Grinding:** The noise will **mask** the sudden loss of resonance.  
- **Post-exit:** Expect **auditory hallucinations**—you’ll “hear” the bowl’s hum in wind, traffic, voices for 1–2 weeks. This is **sensory rebound**—not madness.  
- **Rehydration:** Drink **electrolyte-rich water** slowly. Your body will have lost salt-mineral processing efficiency.  
- **Sensory reintegration:** Spend 3 days in **progressively quieter spaces**, reintroducing normal sounds. You may find **natural sounds (birds, water) painfully loud**—use soft earplugs outdoors for a week.
