### **Phase 1: Pre-Visit Psychological & Conceptual Deconstruction**

A "doorway" is an aperture too small for a Skirter. A "wall" is what you *cannot* climb right now. Prepare to navigate by texture, moisture gradient, sound source, and the flow of small, furry bodies. Your sense of "up" and "down" will blur as you see Moss-kin flowing vertically.

**2. Shed the Notion of "Other."**
The Husk is not a zoo. You are not an observer in a glass box. The Moss-kin are not "primitive tribesmen." **1. The Law of Least Imprint.**
Move like a ghost. Your weight depresses the floor. Your breath adds moisture. Your scent is a flare. Follow the established paths—the deep, hollowed concavities worn by the Moss-kin. A low, resonant hum from a large chamber suggests a resting Skirter—avoid entirely. A soft, cooing exchange near a moss patch is maintenance work—you may observe from a respectful distance, perfectly still.

**3. Interaction Protocol.**
If you are discovered by a Moss-kin:
*   **Do not make eye contact.** Their world is whisker-based. Staring is predatory.
*   **Fall to your knees (slowly)** and lower your head. Present the back of your neck—a position of profound vulnerability.
*   **Remain absolutely still.** Let them investigate with their whiskers. They will smell the prime dust on you, the other fungi on your clothes. They may touch your clothing with dexterous paws.

**4. Your Role.**
You are a **silent, anomalous feature**. Your purpose here is to *witness without altering*. You are a weirdness squared (you at 0.6/3, the Husk at 0.6/3). Your strength is your stillness. Your contribution is your memory: to carry the feeling of this warm, loud, perfectly-balanced womb out into the cold, quiet, fractured multiverse.
