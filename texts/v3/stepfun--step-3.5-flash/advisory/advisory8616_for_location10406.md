### Pre-Trip Multiverse Navigation & Health Prep
First, calibrate your multiverse travel rig to the specific reality signature of the Halite Warrens: lock in the unique combination of sulfur-heavy air, 2.2/3 ambient density, absence of non-microscopic animal life, and the low-frequency tectonic hum of the machinery to avoid landing in a neighboring variant where the Warrens are flooded with molten salt, gravity is permanently flipped, or the Forge-Spires are actively erupting superheated brine. Anchor your arrival coordinates to a small, sealed sample of Warrens halite you bring with you, so you don’t drift out of the chamber’s bounds mid-trip.
Get a pre-trip heavy metal panel and lung function check, and stock up on antifungal and chelation medication: the abrasive, mineral-laden air and pipe-fungal spores will irritate respiratory systems and leach toxic metals into minor cuts fast. Pack a small, familiar grounding object (a piece of basalt from your home universe, a well-worn coin) to anchor yourself if the Gravity’s Whisper disorientation sets in.

### Essential Gear (Ordinary Human, No Exotic Tech)
All gear must be rated for constant high humidity, corrosive sulfur/mineral exposure, and 40–80C (104–176F) temperature swings, with no open flame sources (pipe fluids are unregulated and may be flammable, and the sulfur-rich air is ignition-adjacent):
1. **Footwear & Lower Body**: Puncture-resistant, heat-resistant hiking boots with aerogel-insoles (to block the warm obsidian and hot pipe heat) and deep, salt-grip tread. Pair with quick-dry, heavyweight Syntrel pants treated with soot-repellent coating, and 4 pairs of moisture-wicking, silver-infused socks (the salt crust and soot will chafe raw skin in minutes). Skip cotton entirely—it stays cold and damp in shadowed zones where exhalations plume.
2. **Respiratory & Eye Protection**: Full-face respirator with interchangeable filters for sulfur dioxide, heavy metal particulates, and fungal spores, plus a scratch-resistant, anti-fog polycarbonate visor (the air is abrasive enough to scour unprotected eyes in 10 minutes). Bring 3 spare filter cartridges, sealed in airtight bags—mineral dust clogs filters 3x faster than in normal cave environments.
3. **Layers**: 3-tier, modular clothing system:
   - Base: moisture-wicking, antimicrobial long underwear
   - Mid: lightweight synthetic insulated vest (for the cold, damp shadowed zones between Forge-Spires and salt scaffolds)
   - Outer: loose, heat-resistant, high-visibility orange shell (the dim, sourceless gloom makes it easy to lose sight of travel companions, and the bright color will stand out if you fall into a shadowed crevice)
4. **Lighting & Wayfinding**: High-lumen, IP68-sealed headlamp with a red light mode (bright white disrupts the Echo-Memory feedback loops and may startle Warrens inhabitants), plus 2 spare battery packs stored in a waterproof case. Skip chem lights—their green glow is nearly identical to pipe-fungi and salt-lichen bioluminescence, and will make navigation harder. Instead, bring 20 small, corrosion-resistant metal tags and a permanent marker to mark routes on pipe clusters (salt brick markings will dissolve or shift overnight from Material Drift, and paint will flake off soot-coated surfaces). A gravimetric compass and inertial navigation unit (regular GPS and magnetic compasses are useless in Gravity’s Whisper zones) will help you track movement even when up feels like down.
5. **Safety & Utility Gear**: Industrial climbing harness with corrosion-resistant carabiners and a 10m tether line (board walkways over drops are often rotted through by damp, and there are no guardrails). Burn cream, heavy metal chelation tablets, antifungal ointment, and a tourniquet in a waterproof first aid kit. A portable heat detector to spot superheated pipes before you touch them, and a pocket-sized gas detector for hydrogen sulfide and carbon monoxide. A hand-crank emergency beacon calibrated to transmit through the Warrens’ dense air, in case you get trapped by a steam vent flare or pipe burst. A small notebook and pencil (electronics short out easily from mineral dust and moisture) to map Echo-Memory landmarks and vent pulse rhythms.
6. **Supplies**: 3L of sealed, filtered water per person per day (condensate is loaded with heavy metals and salt, and lower-level pools are contaminated by deep-moss metallic ions). High-calorie, no-cook ration bars and electrolyte tablets (the dry geothermal heat will dehydrate you faster than you expect, even in high humidity). No open flame cooking gear—use sealed, battery-powered heating elements only if absolutely necessary.

### Behavioral & On-Site Protocols
1. **Terrain Safety**: Test every step before putting weight on it—salt plates are brittle and may crumble under load, and walkway boards are often slick with salt weep or soot. Never lean against salt walls or bricks: Material Drift can make them shift or dissolve under pressure without warning. Stay at least 3m from any pipe glowing orange or leaking viscous fluid—these carry superheated brine or unregulated industrial chemicals that can melt gear and cause severe burns on contact.
2. **Weirdness Mitigation**:
   - If you feel nauseous or disoriented in a salt-arched alley (Gravity’s Whisper zone), stop moving immediately, grip a solid pipe or rock, and hold your grounding object until the sensation passes. Do not try to rush through the zone—you may lose balance and fall into an unmarked drop.
   - If you hear ghostly steam whistles, shouts, or songs woven into the machinery hum (Echo-Memory), ignore them—they are auditory hallucinations from the ambient machine drone, and following them will lead you to dead ends or unstable, unmaintained sections of the Warrens.
   - If the air feels suddenly more sluggish than usual, or drips hang motionless in the air for more than a second, a steam vent flare is imminent. Move to higher, more open ground immediately—floods of superheated vapor can fill lower warrens in 30 seconds.
3. **Inhabitant Etiquette**: The tiny, humanoid figures you see working near the Forge-Spires are the Warrens’ only sentient residents, and their infrastructure is grown into the landscape, not built on it. Do not touch salt bricks, pipes, or slime troughs unless explicitly invited—damaging their systems is considered an act of violence. If you wish to trade, set small, useful goods (sealed pipe fittings, heavy-duty water filters, corrosion-resistant fabric) on a salt ledge, step back 10m, and wait for them to approach. Do not take photos, make loud noises, or stare—loud sounds disrupt Echo-Memory patterns and may startle them into accidentally triggering a pressure release.
4. **General Rules**: Never touch the greasy condensation-soot coating lower walls (it is loaded with heavy metal particulates and toxic mold). Do not drink from any natural water source, and do not collect salt, fungus, or moss samples without explicit permission. Move slowly— the dense air makes even light exertion more tiring than normal.

### Contingency Plans
- If a pipe bursts and blocks your exit, use your seismic sensor to map the rhythmic pulse of active steam vents; vent-free zones will have consistent, low-amplitude hums, while active vent zones will have loud, irregular thuds.
- If you get lost in an Echo-Memory loop, stop moving, close your eyes, and focus on the tactile vibration of the machinery in your teeth or the rough feel of salt under your gloves to pull yourself out of the hallucinatory feedback.
- If you are injured, seal the wound immediately to avoid heavy metal or fungal infection, activate your beacon, and move to an open, elevated ledge so extraction teams can spot you from the entrance fissure.
- Set a hard extraction time with your multiverse support team before you descend, and leave a trail of small metal tags along your route—if you miss your extraction window, the tags will help rescuers track your path through the identical, maze-like warrens.
