## Preparation Protocol for Visiting Veyl (The High Plateau *Teer*)

**1. Psychological & Cognitive Conditioning (Highest Priority)**

This is not a destination for conventional exploration. The primary threat is not predation or starvation, but **ontological destabilization**. Your mind, evolved for a world with consistent physics, predictable time, and biological life, will be under constant assault.

*   **Reality Integrity Training:** Undergo intensive sensory deprivation and hallucination-resistance drills. The goal is not to "fight" the wrongness, but to develop a **meta-cognitive observer** within yourself—a part of your consciousness that can silently note, "My spatial memory has just been contradicted. This is a property of the location, not my madness." Practice suspending belief in your own sensory input.
*   **Temporal Decoupling:** Train to exist without a reliance on a linear, measured personal timeline. Use mindfulness techniques that focus on pure, extended perception of a single, unchanging stimulus (e.g., the fixed shadow of a spire, the pulse of a sub-audible tremor). The goal is to reduce panic when time stutters or distances deceive.
*   **Acceptance of Non-Biology:** Meditate on the concept of "pattern-based existence." Study examples of complex, self-organizing, non-living systems: crystal growth, fluid dynamics, sand dune formation. Reconcile yourself to the idea that intelligence, if present, may be as slow and distributed as a geological process. You are not looking for "creatures"; you are looking for... intentional patterns.
*   **Loneliness & Scale Immersion:** Pre-exposure to extreme, barren landscapes (high desert, dense fog, deep antarctic ice fields) to habituate to the feeling of being a negligible, temporary anomaly. Read existential and cosmic horror literature *not* as a story, but as a training manual for emotional neutrality in the face of the incomprehensible.

**2. Physical Gear & Suit Design**

Your suit is not for *working*, but for *being a passive sensor platform*. It must isolate you from the environment's subtle, reality-warping influences while allowing minimal, non-intrusive interaction.

*   **Material:** Full-body coverage with **non-conductive, non-metallic, non-silicate** fabrics. Think advanced aramid weaves or experimental polymers. The goal is to be as chemically and electrically "boring" as possible. No metal fasteners, no electronics in direct contact with the external environment. All tech must be shielded in insulated, inert casings.
*   **Insulation:** The air is cool and thin. However, the greater risk is **thermal shock from conductive surfaces**. The black glass can be extremely cold at "dawn" and potentially warmer (though still cool) after the sourceless brightening. Boots and gauntlets must have thick, non-adhesive insulating layers to prevent heat loss and avoid picking up the conductive dust.
*   **Dust Mitigation:** Seams must be absolute. All joints (wrist, neck, ankle) require positive-pressure seals with filtered air intake. External surfaces will be coated in a **non-stick, hydrophobic, non-conductive nano-coating** to prevent the tacky condensed-gas film and the fine metallic dust from accumulating. You will **not** touch the carved surfaces or the quartz nodules.
*   **Navigation & Communication:**
    *   **No GPS/Radio:** The non-Euclidean geometry and potential for temporal distortion will render satellites and radio signals useless or dangerously misleading.
    *   **Celestial Navigation Only:** A sealed, mechanical or spring-driven **gyro-stabilized astrolabe** focused solely on the position of the red-tinged star. Its position is the only reliable, fixed coordinate. A simple, pre-calibrated **rangefinder using that star as a fixed point** for triangulation between carved features.
    *   **Local Communication:** **Laser-based, line-of-sight communicators** using extremely low-power, monochromatic beams. These are less susceptible to atmospheric interference than radio, and the narrow beam reduces the chance of "echoes" or distortions from the local physics. Assume latency and potential message corruption.
    *   **Mapping:** Do not attempt to create a 2D map. Your log must be a **descriptive, non-geometric narrative**. "From the amphitheater with the three fused quartz circles, facing the star, I walked 1200 precise paces (measured by a mechanical pedometer with no electronic components) until the cliff with the slow seep was directly ahead." Record sensations, not just coordinates.

**3. Scientific & Sensory Equipment (All Passive)**

*   **Seismographs & Tiltmeters:** To record the sub-audible tectonic sighs and any minute, slow gravity or slope shifts. These will be your primary "weather" reports.
*   **High-Dynamic-Range, Long-Exposure Optics:** To capture the faint glow of the violet salts, the movement of the tar-like seep (over hours), and the potential rearrangement of quartz nodules. Cameras must be completely shielded from local electromagnetic interference.
*   **Environmental Samplers (Sealed):** For atmospheric analysis (ozone, metallic particulates, trace gases). Any attempt to *touch* the environment for a sample is a severe contamination risk and likely无效. All sampling is remote-spectroscopy only.
*   **Passive Radiation & Energy Detectors:** To monitor the "cold glow" of the salts and any other anomalous energy emissions (from the "predator" distortions or the seep).
*   **Sonic & Sub-Sonic Recorders:** To attempt to pick up vibrations in the glass itself, which may be a form of local "communication" or tectonic process.

**4. Behavioral Protocol on Arrival**

1.  **Immediate Anchoring:** Upon stepping onto the black glass, do not move for **at least one full local cycle** (dawn to dawn, or sourceless brightening to next). Simply observe the fixed star. Establish its relationship to your position. Feel the tremors. Let your initial disorientation pass in a state of deliberate stillness.
2.  **The "Observer" Stance:** You are a **feature**, not an explorer. Move with extreme slowness. Your goal is to be as boring and inert as a rock. Do not alter arrangements (do not move nodules, do not block flows). Your presence should be a minimal perturbation.
3.  **No Hypothesis Testing:** Do not try to "test" the distortions. Do not throw a stone to see if it curves. Do not try to walk the "short" path that seems to warp. **Accept the path as given when you come to it.** Your data is your experience, not the result of an experiment.
4.  **Prioritize Tracing Slow Processes:** Follow the slow seep. Watch a cluster of quartz nodules. These are the planet's "active" moments. Your frantic human speed is your biggest liability here.
5.  **Exit Strategy:** **You do not have a fixed exit route.** Your return to the "lip" must be a re-tracing of your *original sensory path*, not your logical path. Describe to your log, at every step, exactly what you saw, felt, and heard when you came *in*. Your memory of the incoming journey is your only true map. If it contradicts the outbound path, *trust the incoming memory*. The planet may have re-carved itself around you during your visit.

**5. Mental & Spiritual Preparation (The Non-Negotiable)**

You must internalize this truth: **You are not visiting a city. You are visiting a mind-state made of rock and time.** The "inhabitants" are likely not entities you can converse with. They may be the *process* of the slow seep, the *pattern* of the quartz circles, the *persistent anomaly* of the non-Euclidian horizon.

Your purpose is not to understand, but to **bear witness**. To let this place's profound, mineral patience exist in your awareness without your human need for meaning, cause, or narrative. The loneliness you will feel is not the loneliness of being alone, but the loneliness of being **temporarily conscious in a consciousness that thinks in millennia**.

Carry something small, familiar, and utterly useless to this place: a smooth river stone from your world, a dried flower, a printed poem. Place it on a ledge in Veyl as your offering—a token of a different kind of time, a different kind of life. Then leave it. Your preparation is complete when you can do this without expectation of return, without need for comprehension, and with the calm acceptance that the rock will, in its own time, incorporate your token into its next slow, dream-carving breath.
