# Preparation Guide: Visiting the Verdant Loom of Sighing Stone

## I. Philosophical & Mental recalibration
**Do not approach this place as a "hike," "exploration," or "tour."** You are entering a digestive system. A slow, green, metallic lung. Your mindset must shift from *observer* to *temporary organ*—a brief, conscious participant in a metabolism that has already consumed a civilization.

*   **Embrace Slowness:** Your body's natural rhythms (heartbeat, breathing, step) will feel disrespectfully fast. Practice moving in extreme slow-motion for hours before arrival. Learn to find profound information in stillness. The most significant changes here (a leaf unfurling, a gear's creak) take days.
*   **Silence is Primary:** You will be tempted to speak. Resist. Your voice will be a brutal, sharp intrusion in a soundscape of deep bass and crystalline drips. Communication must be pre-arranged hand signals or written notes on waterproof paper. If you must make sound, it should be a low hum, matching the subsonic thrum.
*   **Abandon Notions of "Nature" vs. "Machine":** You will see brass growing from moss, ceramic under root. Do not mentally categorize. See only *form*. A piston is a stone is a root is a spire. This is not a garden with ruins; it is one thing.
*   **Accept Assimilation:** Your gear *will* be changed. A metal buckle will gain a patina of rust-moss. A synthetic fiber will be weakened by the moist ozone. Plan for this. Nothing you bring will remain "yours" for long. See it as a fee.

## II. Bodily Preparation & Health
The atmosphere is a living broth. Your body is about to be colonized.

1.  **Respiratory Filters are Non-Negotiable:** The air is thick with:
    *   **Chlorophyll-laden mist:** Will coat lungs.
    *   **Electrically charged particles:** From the spires. Can cause micro-tingling, potential lung irritation.
    *   **Spores/Microbial Films:** Ubiquitous. While most are benign, the ecosystem is post-biological. Unknown fungal-viral hybrids are possible.
    *   **Preparation:** Use a full-face respirator with **P100/N100 filters** (for particles) **and an activated carbon layer** (for ozone/volatile organics). The mask should be comfortable for 8+ hours of continuous wear. You will not remove it. Period. The smell of wet chlorophyll is intoxicating; do not breathe it deeply. It may contain psychoactive mycotoxins.

2.  **Skin is a Portal:** The warm, gel-covered root-mat will be in constant contact with your legs/feet.
    *   **Preparation:** Treat any cut, scrape, or abrasion *weeks* in advance. Use antibacterial/antifungal ointment and waterproof bandages. Consider wearing **seamless, breathable, silver-impregnated undergarments** (for antimicrobial properties) under your main suit. All exposed skin should be coated in a **thick, non-greasy barrier cream** rated for chemical/moisture protection. Reapply every 4 hours.

3.  **Gastrointestinal Fortitude:** The only "food" you should consume is your own sealed, high-calorie, low-residue rations (see Gear). Under no circumstances eat local flora, fauna, or drink the water. The microbial soup will likely cause violent dysbiosis. Take a **broad-spectrum antiparasitic/antibiotic** as a prophylactic *before* entry, prescribed by a doctor aware of the environment. Carry **strong anti-diarrheals and rehydration salts**.

4.  **Ear Protection:** The soundscape is physically damaging.
    *   **Preparation:** Custom-molded **high-fidelity earplugs** that reduce volume by 25-30 dB but preserve frequency range. You must still hear the choral drip and sighing creak to navigate. The deep bass (felt in chest) cannot be blocked; you must learn to endure it. Use **passive isolation**; do not use active noise-canceling (its whine will clash).

5.  **Eyes:** The diffuse green-gold light is even and non-damaging. No special eyewear needed. However, the constant mist will cause minor eye irritation. Use **lubricating eye drops (preservative-free)** hourly.

## III. Gear & Equipment: The Principle of Non-Interference
**Rule: Everything you bring must either be 1) eaten/consumed by you, 2) worn on your person, or 3) removable without trace.**
No single-use plastics. No batteries that will leak. No greases, oils, or fuels.

**Worn/Integrated:**
*   **Suit:** One-piece, **breathable, waterproof, chemical-resistant shell** (like a high-end drysuit or hazmat coverall). Material must be **PTFE-coated nylon or equivalent**. Seams taped. No external pockets (they'll catch on vines). Inner layer: **merino wool** (warm even wet, antimicrobial).
*   **Footwear:** **High-top, seamless waterproof boots** with **deep, aggressive lugs** for gripping the springy, gel-slick root-mat. Boots must be **lace-free** (zipper or soft shell) to avoid snagging. Inner sock: **synthetic blend with silver thread**. Outer sock: **waterproof breathable liner**.
*   **Gloves:** **Long cuff, waterproof, grippy palm**. Must allow dexterity for note-taking, tool use. Under-glove: thin **silk or synthetic liner**.
*   **Backpack:** **Frameless, waterproof roll-top dry bag**. All contents inside **compression sacks**. No external frames, no dangling straps.

**Carried (Minimal):**
*   **Navigation:** **Waterproof, "eternal" notebook** (stone paper or polymer) and **titanium or ceramic pencil**. No electronics (moisture, electrical interference ruins them). Navigation by **pre-drawn mental map of key relic landmarks** (the "Brass Lung Piston," the "Glass Spire that hums C-sharp"). The place has magnetic anomalies from the spires; compasses useless. Use **sundial/gnomon** for the even light; timekeeping by **mechanical wind-up watch**.
*   **Light:** **No electric lights**. The ambient light is sufficient for vision. For reading notes, use a **mechanical squeeze lantern** (hand-cranked LED, but keep in dry bag until use) or a **chemically fueled glow stick** (green, to match ambient). Fire is impossible (wet everything) and would be a catastrophic violation.
*   **Tools:** **One multi-tool with ceramic/titanium bits** (no steel, it will rust instantly). **One小ceramic knife**. **One roll of **biodegradable plant-based tape** (for emergency seal). All tools in waterproof pouch.
*   **Water & Food:** **All water you need, sealed in rigid, non-plastic containers** (stainless steel or glass). **3 days' emergency ration** of dense, low-fiber, high-fat bars ( Pemmican-style). Consume slowly. **No local hydration.**
*   **Sanitation & Waste:** **Sealed, biodegradable "Wag Bags"** for human waste. **Plan: pack out all urine (in sealed bottle), pack out all feces.** A single gram of foreign nutrient will be proliferated by the microbial soup. Leave nothing.
*   **First Aid:** **Comprehensive, waterproof.** Focus on **antifungal/antibacterial creams, barrier repair ointments, burn gel (for electrical spire contact), elastic bandages, aspirin/ibuprofen.** No oral antibiotics in kit (you're on prophylaxis).
*   **Communication:** **NO RADIOS, NO SATELLITE PHONES.** The electrical hum will fry them. You will have **no contact**. Your exit point is pre-arranged and timed by the slow cycles (e.g., "return to the root-gel aperture when the light-patch reaches the Moss-Reed Arch"). You are on your own. Tell no one where you are going. If you don't return on schedule, assume you were assimilated.

## IV. Logistical & Exit Protocol
1.  **Entry/Exit Point:** Must be a **pre-identified, stable geological feature**—the base of a specific stem-tree, a particular petrified fungal outcrop. The place shifts; canals move. Your point will be gone or buried in a decade. Mark it mentally with **unmistakable, non-interfering landmarks** (e.g., "the three-stem fusion that parts like fingers").
2.  **Duration:** **Maximum 48 hours.** Your respirator filters will saturate with spores. Your body's microbial balance will collapse. Your mind will begin to think in slow cycles. You must leave while you are still *you*. Time is measured by your **internal clock**; do not rely on the place's time (a "sigh" cycle can be a month).
3.  **Decontamination Protocol (CRITICAL):**
    *   **Before Exit:** At your designated exit root-gel aperture, **do not touch any living structure.** Use your ceramic knife to clear a small path if needed.
    *   **Post-Exit:** You will have a **sealed decon tent** waiting (far from the aperture). Inside:
        a. Strip **naked** inside the tent. Place all gear in **heavy-duty, sealable polymer bags** (these will be burned later).
        b. Use **industrial-strength, biodegradable solvent wipes** (like those for hazmat) on every square inch of skin, focusing on folds, ears, nails. Do not shower yet.
        c. Place used wipes in waste bag.
        d. Don **clean, sealed** clothes from a fresh bag.
        e. The gear bags go into a **second, outer sealed container**. This container and all waste go with you for **incineration at a licensed facility**.
    f. Only after this, may you use a **regular shower**. Scrub vigorously.
    g. **Monitor health for 30 days.** Any rash, respiratory issue, neurological tingle—report to a specialist and mention "possible post-biological spore exposure."

## V. What It Will Feel Like: An Intuitive Summary
You will step onto warm, springy ground that yields like a memory foam mattress soaked in vegetable broth. The air will be a **wet blanket on your face**, tasting of **yeast, cut grass, and the faint, clean smell of a welding torch left to cool for centuries**. The sound will not be heard with your ears first, but **felt in your molars and sternum**—a slow, tectonic heartbeat beneath a waterfall of glass beads.

Your eyes will adjust to a **perpetual twilight under a green canopy**. Nothing moves quickly. A leaf may drip a single, perfect droplet for a full minute. A gear the size of a car will turn a millimeter in the time it takes you to breathe ten times. You will feel *impossibly* loud, *impossibly* fast.

The deep bass from the spires will vibrate your fillings. The choral drip will become a tinnitus of the soul. The sighing creak of the stem-trees will sound like the world's biggest violin bow, drawn over a continent.

You will see **brass with veins of root**, **ceramic with a fuzzy green coat**, **wires that are living stalks**. The smell will not change; it will be a constant, humid womb-smell. You will be a **blinking, gasping, noisy mammal** in a place that has moved beyond such things. Your terror will be at its slowness. Your awe will be at its patience. Your exit will feel like a birth *out* of a slow, green, metallic womb.

You go not to conquer or understand, but to **bear witness to a digestion**. And to get out with your soul still distinguishable from the root-mat.
