# Preparation Protocol: Visiting the Steam Flats (Pyrefall Basin)

**For the Ordinary Multiverse Traveler:**

Visiting the Steam Flats is not a "trip." It is an **environmental interrogation**. You are not entering a landscape; you are being **dissolved into a process**. Your Earth-born instincts—for sky, for dry land, for clear air, for macroscopic life—will be your primary enemies here. Preparation must be comprehensive, psychological as well as physical.

---

## **I. Pre-Departure Conditioning & Mindset**

1.  **Shed Your Celestial Bias:** There is no sun. The light is a prisoner of the haze. Your circadian rhythm will have no anchor. Mentally prepare for a world of **permanent, bleached dusk**. The amber glow from the ground is your only "moon."
2.  **Reconceptualize "Life":** You will not see animals, birds, or insects. Life here is **aerosol, film, and mineral catalysis**. The buzzing in your ears is not insects; it's the pressure of a trillion microbial motes. The rust patterns on the rock are digestive waste. Seeing "activity" requires a microscope, not a gaze. Expect profound loneliness masked by the feeling of being *constantly observed by the medium itself*.
3.  **Accept Thermal Ownership:** You do not control temperature. The earth's core does. Your body will be **cooled or heated by your suit only**. The ambient air is a warm, wet blanket. The ground is a heating pad. "Comfort" does not exist; only **tolerable thermal variance**.
4.  **Abandon Notions of "Ground":** This is not soil. It is **firm, damp mud** and **porous, weeping rock**. You cannot dig, garden, or find "clean" earth. Everything is saturated, slick, and chemically active. Assume *all* surfaces are potentially corrosive or biologically active.

---

## **II. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) - The "Coccoon-Suit"**

Your survival depends on a sealed, pressurized, thermally regulated encapsulation. This is not a jacket and pants; it's a **mobile habitat**.

*   **Shell:** Multi-layered. Outer layer is **abrasion-resistant, ceramic-impregnated fabric** to protect against sharp basalt and crystalline pings. Middle layer is **aerogel-insulated** for thermal break. Inner layer is **impermeable, sulfur-resistant polymer**.
*   **Atmosphere System:**
    *   **Closed-Cycle:** Must be **completely sealed**. Inhaled air is scrubbed of aerosols, particulates, and sulfides via a multi-stage filter (electrostatic precipitator + chemical scrubber using iron oxide media).
    *   **Supply:** Carry **minimum 48 hours of pure, dry air** (compressed tanks). The ambient "air" is a toxic, living soup. Do not trust any external intake.
    *   **Pressure:** Slight over-pressure (1.1-1.2 atm) to prevent inward leakage of environmental aerosols into seals.
    *   **Humidity Control:** Your suit's internal atmosphere must be maintained at **<30% RH**. The external humidity is ~100%. Any breach or leak will instantly fog your visor from the inside with warm, metallic mist.
*   **Thermal Regulation:**
    *   **Active Cooling Essential.** A **phase-change cooling vest** (like advanced astronaut undergarment) powered by a high-density battery. The ambient warmth (likely 40-50°C / 104-122°F) will sap your energy and cause rapid heat stroke without active removal.
    *   **Heating:** Minimal, for emergency static situations. The environment provides enough heat.
*   **Visor/HUD:**
    *   **Material:** Thick, **anti-fog, impact-resistant polycarbonate** with an **electrochromic tint** to manage the constant twilight/amber glow. You will need to manually adjust light transmission frequently.
    *   **Display:** Your **most critical tool**. Must overlay:
        *   **Atmospheric toxin levels** (if any sensors penetrate the suit).
        *   ** suit integrity status** (seal, pressure, coolant).
        *   **GPS/Inertial nav** (GPS may be unreliable; rely on inertial and pre-mapped safe corridors).
        *   **Thermal imaging** to see the warmer rock structures and potential geothermal vents.
        *   **Audio feed** from external microphones, filtered to remove the sub-audible thrum and amplify human-range sounds (like a distant city bell or pipe clang).
*   **Footwear:** **Heavy-duty, sealed boots** with **vibro-resistant soles** (to dampen the feeling of the sub-audible thrum) and **excellent grip** on slick, mineral-coated surfaces. The ground is not stable; it slowly weeps and shifts.
*   **Communications:** **Short-range, encrypted, suit-to-suit radio**. The metallic haze and ionized mist will scramble unshielded signals. Assume **no long-range or external comms**.

## **III. Navigation & Interaction Protocols**

1.  **Stick to Certified Corridors:** Do not wander. The terrain is a **fractal deathtrap** of hidden fissures, unstable mud pits, and sudden steam blasts. Travel only on **marked, maintained catwalks and pipeline routes**. These are the "roads" of Pyrefall.
2.  **The City is Not a City:** Pyrefall is an **industrial organ**. There are no parks, no plazas, no "streets" in a human sense. You move through **pipe galleries, pressurized corridors, and gantryways**. Signage is minimal—labels on valves, pressure gauges, flow meters. Your navigation is by **function** (Distillation Hall 3, Boiler Bank Gamma).
3.  **Encountering Locals:**
    *   They are **industrial technicians**, not merchants or hosts. They are focused on **processes**.
    *   They will view you as a **catastrophic contamination risk**. Your suit is a "sterile object" in their bio-thermodynamic world.
    *   **Interaction Rule:** **No physical contact. No unsolicited approach.** Communicate via suit radio through a **designated airlock interface**. They will likely speak in a jargon of pressures, flows, and mineral yields.
    *   **Do not offer Earth concepts** (beauty, nature, freedom). They will see you as dangerously naive. Frame questions in **thermodynamic or geological terms** ("What is the pressure gradient in this pipe?" "How do you manage silicate fouling in the condensers?").
4.  **"Currency" is Nonsense:** There is no economy as you know it. Access is granted by **clearance and necessity**. If you need safe passage or a guide, you must offer **unique external data** (e.g., comparative atmospheric chemistry from another world, geological survey techniques) or **perform a manual labor task** (helping to clear a mineral-clogged vent) to earn temporary trust.

## **IV. Physiological & Psychological Harms & Mitigations**

*   **Sensory Deprivation/Overload:** The constant thrum, the bleached light, the feeling of a living atmosphere will cause **disorientation and anxiety**. Practice **sensory deprivation tank exercises** beforehand to learn to center yourself when your senses scream false data.
*   **Thermal Stress:** Even with cooling, the sheer thermal mass of the environment is draining. Limit excursions to **2-3 hour maximum** in the field. **Hydration** is internal (suit water supply) only. Do not sweat; your suit's humidity trap will handle that.
*   **Psychological Alienation:** The absence of familiar life—no birds, no wind in trees, no flowing streams—is a **deep, existential void**. The "life" is everywhere and nowhere. This can trigger **depersonalization**. Carry a **tactile focus object** from Earth (a smooth stone, a piece of wood) to remind yourself of a different physics. **Meditation is not optional; it is survival training.**
*   **Auditory Harm:** The sub-audible thrum can cause **nausea and resonance in bone cavities**. The suit's padding helps, but **pre-trip vestibular training** (using bone-conduction headphones with low-frequency noise) is advised to build tolerance.
*   **Chemical Exposure Fear:** Knowing your suit is your only barrier against an atmosphere that can **dissolve rock and metabolize iron** creates primal terror. Suit drills—**emergency seal repair, rapid donning, buddy-check procedures**—must be **second nature**. Confidence in your cocoon is your only psychological anchor.

## **V. What to Bring (Minimalist)**

*   **Yourself**, in your **fully serviced Coccoon-Suit** with 48h+ air and coolant.
*   **Data Tools:** A **sealed, ruggedized tablet** with all maps, schematics of Pyrefall's known sectors, and a **localized chemical database** (to identify unknown aerosols/fluids via suit sensor readings).
*   **Exchange Items:** **Five (5) small, sealed vials** of **pure, crystalline water** from a high-purity source (like a glacier). In a world where water is a corrosive medium, *pure, inert H₂O* is a **sacred reagent** for delicate calibrations or as a high-value sample. Also, a **perfect, polished quartz sphere** (for optical calibration in their smoky light).
*   **Personal Memento:** One **non-porous, non-reactive object** from Earth (e.g., a polished titanium sphere, a glass marble). Its sole purpose is **psychological grounding**.

---

## **VI. What NOT to Bring / Do NOT Do**

*   **NO OPEN FLAMES.** The atmosphere is rich in hydrogen sulfide and other flammables. A spark is a detonation.
*   **DO NOT REMOVE YOUR GLOVES OR HELMET** outside an airlock. **EVER.**
*   **DO NOT TOUCH** any living film, microbial mat, or "lichen." Contact will either degrade your suit polymer or transmit unknown bio-agents.
*   **DO NOT DRINK** any local water, even from "pools." It is a saturated mineral soup, likely acidic or superheated.
*   **DO NOT EXPECT SILENCE OR DARKNESS.** The hum is constant. The glow is constant. Your "night" will be the same as your "day."
*   **DO NOT ASSUME GEOLOGY OR BIOLOGY FROM EARTH APPLY.** A "volcanic" rock here is biologically altered. A "pool" is a chemical reactor. **All observations must be filtered through the lens of a world where life and geology are a single, slow, hot process.**

## **VII. Final Mental Mantra**

Repeat until it is your core thought:

> **"I am a temporary, cooled anomaly in a world that is feverish, alive in ways I cannot perceive, and utterly indifferent to my existence. My suit is not armor; it is a fragile membrane separating my fragile biology from a planet that metabolizes stone. My goal is not to conquer or understand, but to *witness without being absorbed*, and to leave no trace but data."**

The Steam Flats do not welcome visitors. It **processes** them. Your preparation is not for adventure, but for **controlled non-assimilation**. Go with the humility of a germ under a microscope, and the meticulous care of a bomb disposal expert. Your life depends on it.
