## Pre-Visit Preparation Guide: The Sintered Plateau (Green Silence)

**Overview:** You are not visiting a landscape. You are entering a **slow, sensing entity** disguised as terrain. Your usual assumptions about ecology, time, and industry will fail you here. Success depends on treating the Plateau as a **living system with its own temporal and sensory rules**, not a backdrop for your adventure.

---

### **I. Psychological & Temporal Conditioning (Most Critical)**

1.  **Slow Your Internal Clock:** Your body and mind operate on human timescales (seconds, minutes, hours). The Plateau operates on **decadal and centennial pulses**. You will experience profound cognitive dissonance.
    *   **Preparation:** Practice multi-hour silent observation in a completely still environment (e.g., a sensory deprivation tank, a deep forest at dawn). Train to find richness in extreme stasis. Meditate on the concept of "a century as a single breath."
    *   **Mindset:** You are a **brief, noisy, warm anomaly**. Your presence is a violent perturbation. Adopt a posture of profound humility and non-interference. Do not "conquer" or "catalogue." *Observe and resonate.*

2.  **Sensory Recalibration:**
    *   **Silence Training:** The acoustic silence is a physical pressure. Your brain will strain to create sound (tinnitus, hallucinations). Prepare by spending time in anechoic chambers or vast, soundless deserts. Learn to **value the sub-audible**: the creak of stone, the internal hum of your own blood. Carry a notebook for "silence sketches"—describe what the *absence* of expected sound feels like.
    *   **Brightness Acclimation:** The light is paradoxically bright yet sunless. It will cause no squint, but extreme retinal fatigue. Spend time in uniformly lit, misty environments (e.g., foggy coastal mornings). Practice navigating and performing delicate tasks under these conditions.
    *   **Touch Discipline:** **DO NOT TOUCH THE MOSS CASUALLY.** Its communication is slow but pervasive. A brief touch might initiate a physiological response you cannot perceive for hours or days. Your touch is a shout in a library. Treat the ground as you would the skin of a sleeping, sensitive being. All sampling must be pre-planned, minimal, and with awareness you may trigger unknown cascades.

---

### **II. Gear & Physical Preparation**

**Primary Directive: Minimize Impact, Maximize Passive Sensing.**

**A. Clothing & Footwear:**
*   **Material:** Seamless, non-abrasive outer shell (softshell, heavy-duty silpoly). **Absolutely no sharp soles or exposed metal.** Boots must be thick-soled, flexible, and quiet. You are walking on a living carpet that can react to injury.
*   **Color:** Muted, matte tones (deep grey, moss-green, ochre). The Glimmerers are masters of camouflage; do not disrupt their visual field with brights or reflective strips.
*   **Layering:** High-wicking base layer. Insulating mid-layer (fleece). **ABSOLUTELY NO COTTON.** The damp is constant; hypothermia risk is high even if the air is "cool." The mist will penetrate anything not truly waterproof.
*   **Extremities:** Gaiters that seal fully over boots. Gloves with tactile fingertips but full moisture protection.

**B. Navigation & Communication:**
*   **Primary Nav:** **Dead reckoning and manual sketching.** The Canopy-Frames cause minor spatial warps; GPS signals may be erratic or subtly "bent." Magnetic compasses may be influenced by the Smolders' sub-audible fields.
*   **Comms:** Assume **no long-range radio or satellite uplink will function reliably**. The smog from the Smolders is not smoke, but **transduced energy**—it likely creates a persistent, low-frequency electromagnetic interference bubble. Use a **hardline buddy system** with physical tethers if in a group. Carry **pre-scripted, time-delayed message balloons** or reflective panels for aerial search (if you must be found).
*   **Marking:** Use **biodegradable, pH-neutral chalk** or removable flagging **only on bare rock** (not moss). Consider leaving no trace *at all*.

**C. Supplies:**
*   **Water:** The pools are pristine but **shockingly cold** (likely near 0°C). Drinking will shock your system and lower core temperature. Bring **all water you need** in insulated containers. Do not consume pool water without rigorous multi-stage filtering and warming—unknown mineral/biological content.
*   **Food:** High-calorie, no-prep foods. **No open flames or combustion.** Any fire-starting tool is forbidden. The Plateau's energy system is transduction-based; combustion is a profane, violent act here. Use chemical hand-warmers or insulated containers for food.
*   **Power:** Solar is useless under the pearl-grey mist. Bring **sealed, long-life batteries only**. Assume all electronics will fail eventually due to the ambient field. Have a **fully mechanical watch** and a **paper topographical map** of the known features (Smolders, Rail-Trails).
*   **Medical:** Focus on **hypothermia treatment** (shelter, dry packs) and **minor wound care** (abrasions from obsidian). Bring a strong **antifungal/antibacterial**—your immune system is an invading force to the local microbiome. Assume any cut will host unknown, slow-growing organisms.

### **III. Protocols for Specific Features**

**A. The Mineral-Moss (The Network):**
*   **Rule:** Do not step on the same patch repeatedly. Your weight is a significant distortion. Move like a **sleepwalker on eggs**.
*   **Observation:** Use binoculars or a long-focus camera to observe Glimmerers and colour-shifts. **Do not attempt to communicate via touch.** The "electric tingle" is real—it is likely bioelectrical. Note any skin sensations and log them.
*   **Sampling:** If absolutely necessary for research, use a **sterile, single-use ceramic biopsy tool** to take a **pinhead-sized core** from a moss patch that shows no active colour-shift. Seal immediately in a non-conductive container. Log location to the square meter.

**B. The Smolders (Transducers):**
*   **Rule: DO NOT ENTER. DO NOT TOUCH.** The base is dry and warm—a radical anomaly in the damp. This is a **thermal/energy boundary**. Standing near one for >30 minutes may induce nausea, dizziness, and metallic taste (interference with your nervous system).
*   **Observation:** Maintain a **minimum 500-meter distance** when possible. Use a theodolite or laser rangefinder to map plumes. The "steam-colored smoke" sinks—observe where it contacts the moss. That is a **nourishment zone**. Do not disturb it.
*   **Hazard:** The sub-audible hum can resonate in your skeletal structure, causing fatigue and disorientation. If you feel it in your molars, you are too close. Leave immediately.

**C. The Rail-Trails (Grooves):**
*   **Rule: Do not walk in them.** They are **voids in the system**. The moss does not grow here because the underlying energy field is different—likely a direct conduit. They are the only "straight lines" in this curved world. They may be hazardous conduits for energy or something else.
*   **Use:** Use them as **linear navigation reference points only** from a safe distance.

**D. The Canopy-Frames (Warpers):**
*   **Rule: Do not approach closer than 100 meters.** The light-warping effect is a symptom of a persistent, structured field. It may cause spatial disorientation, nausea, and visual hallucinations. Your chronometer may drift.
*   **Observation:** Use telephoto lenses. Document the geometry. They are inert, but they are *imposed*. They do not belong to the moss-world's organic logic. They are the most "artificial" and potentially **dangerous** relics.

**E. Fauna (Glimmerers & Stone-Skimmers):**
*   **Rule: Non-interference is law.** You are a blur of noise and motion to them. Your presence will cause them to freeze for hours. Do not attempt to feed, touch, or herd.
*   **Observation:** Find a vantage point and **observe a single individual for an entire day**. This will teach you the true pace of the Plateau. Note the exact duration of a single grazing motion.

### **IV. Exit & Return Protocol**

1.  **Time Awareness:** You will lose track of time. The silence erodes your internal clock. **Set a rigid, vibrating (not auditory) alarm on a separate device for turnaround.** The Plateau's effect on your perception may mean 4 hours feel like 4 minutes, or vice-versa.
2.  **Decontamination (Mental & Physical):** Before re-entering a "normal" multiverse hub:
    *   **Physical:** Strip all gear outside a clean airlock. Hose down with **high-pH, surfactant-rich water** to dislodge any moss spores or biofilms. Gear should be quarantined for 72 hours in UV light.
    *   **Mental:** Undergo a **sensory re-immersion**. Spend 12 hours in a noisy, brightly lit, chaotic urban environment. Re-train your brain to expect variable sound and sharp shadows. Seek a therapist familiar with **ecological shock**.
3.  **Data Integrity:** Assume all digital recordings are **corrupted by the ambient field**. Store data on multiple, shielded analog backups (microfiche, engraved metal plates). Correlate data points over days after return—the weirdness may only become apparent in comparison.
4.  **The Moss Itself:** **Check your skin daily for the next month for persistent, slow-shifting colour patterns or tingling sensations.** Do not dismiss these as imagination. They may indicate a **persistent bio-electrical link**. Report immediately to a xenobiological specialist.

### **Final Warning & Philosophy**

You are stepping into a **fossilized moment of industry turned ecology**. The Smolders are not volcanoes; they are dormant engines. The moss is not just plants; it is the **planetary integument** that absorbed the industry and made it part of its slow, green thought. The Canopy-Frames may be the bones of the builders, or the first tools of the moss itself.

**Your goal is not to understand, but to *not disturb*.** You are a ghost passing through a dream the planet is having about itself. Leave no footprint, no memory, no scent. Your best report will be: *"The silence is so dense you can touch it. The light has no source. The green is thinking, and we are almost too fast to see."*

Come prepared to have your sense of reality, time, and scale permanently altered. Return only when you can stop hearing the human world's noise.
