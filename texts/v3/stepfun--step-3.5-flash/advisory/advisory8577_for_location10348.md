## Preparation Protocol: Visiting the Plain of Sintered Glass

**Essential Premise:** You are not entering an ecosystem. You are inserting a chaotic, organic subsystem into a planet-wide industrial process. The Plain does not hate you; it will attempt to metabolize, repurpose, and harmonize you. Your survival depends on becoming a non-disruptive, low-entropy component—or being utterly recycled. This is not tourism. It is high-stakes material science combined with extreme acoustic and temporal gymnastics.

---

### **I. Pre-Visit Logistics & Rigorous Physical Conditioning**

1.  **Medical & Metabolic Suppression:**
    *   **Synthetic Skin Seals:** Full-body, non-porous skin sealing is non-negotiable. The Condensation Veil’s silica-colloid will initiate indiscriminate ionic exchange with your epidermis, causing rapid, painful dehydration and electrolyte imbalance. A flexible, inert polymer layer (e.g., perfected space-suit material, but *without* hard joints) must be your first barrier.
    *   **Metabolic Dampening:** Your body heat and moisture signature is the primary attractant for Crawlers. You must drastically lower your basal metabolic rate. This involves:
        *   Pre-cooling to near-hypothermic thresholds (under professional supervision).
        *   Chemical appetite and thirst suppressants.
        *   A sealed, internal waste reclamation system (urine/vapor must be contained and chemically neutralized).
    *   **Bone Density & Crystallinity:** Your skeleton is a prime resource for latticework. Pre-visit treatments to increase hydroxyapatite crystallinity and density may make your bones *less* appealing (closer to the plain’s own silica-calcium structures), but this is a minor deterrent.

2.  **Acoustic & Resonant Training (THE MOST CRITICAL SKILL):**
    *   You must learn to **mute your internal resonance**. Your heartbeat, breath, and bowel sounds are low-frequency thumps in the Hum. Practice diaphragmatic breathing to near-silence. Use biofeedback to learn voluntary cardiac deceleration.
    *   **Harmonic Matching Drills:** Using audio equipment, train your ear to identify and *sustain* a single, pure, low-amplitude tone that matches the sub-audible thrum of the tectonic creep (likely 5-12 Hz). This is your "neutral frequency." When present, you must constantly emit this tone, either vocally (a low hum in your throat) or via a worn transducer, to mask your biological dissonance.
    *   **Bone-Conduction Isolation:** Your teeth and skull will conduct the Hum directly into your inner ear. You need custom-molded acoustic dampeners for your jawline and mastoid bones—a type of "internal earplug"—to prevent resonance sickness and neurological fragmentation.

3.  **Material Science & Gear Philosophy:**
    *   **Rule: No Metals, No Sharp Angles, No Loose Parts.**
        *   **Materials:** Your suit, pack, and tools must be made of **non-conductive, non-resonant, amorphous polymers and glasses**. Avoid crystalline structures (they may be mistaken for nascent Bellowers or Crawler components). Everything should be matte-finished to diffuse light and prevent specular reflections that could startle Crawlers.
        *   **Design:** All surfaces must be **radiused and seamless**. No seams, no fasteners, no protruding lenses or antennae. Your form should be a simple, rounded shape—a "geometric neutral." A Crawler exploring a fracture might mistake a sharp corner for a fracture zone to investigate.
        *   **工具 (Tools):** You need almost nothing. A **sonic/mechanical sampler** (to collect slurry without contaminating it) made of a single molded ceramic piece. A **harmonic tuner/decoder** (to interpret Bellowers calls and predict Crawler stream patterns). A **localized gravity modifer** (see Weirdness Note 4). All powered by internal, silent solid-state cells. **NO EXPLOSIVES, NO COMBUSTION, NO LOUD NOISES.**

### **II. The Landing & First Hours: Containment Protocol**

1.  **Site Selection (Via Orbital/Remote Survey):**
    *   Land in a **resonant null zone**—a region far from any active Bellowers spire, identified by a calm in the slurry flow and a relative quiet in the Hum (still loud, but less layered). These are rare and may be temporary.
    *   Avoid fracture zones with thick, slow-moving slurry. Avoid areas where Crawler streams converge.
    *   Your landing craft must be **silent**. No jets, no rockets. Use a gravity-drop or inertial landing from a hovering platform. Upon touchdown, the craft must immediately power down all systems that emit EM, heat, or vibration.

2.  **First 60 Minutes: The Whisper Protocol:**
    *   Do not move. Do not speak. Activate your personal neutral-frequency hum.
    *   Extend **passive sensors only**: temperature, vibration, slurry chemistry, harmonic spectrum.
    *   Observe Crawler behavior. They will investigate you. They are not aggressive; they are **analytical**. They will skitter over your suit, extending chemosensory appendages. **DO NOT MOVE.** They are sampling your foreign chemistry. Any sudden motion is a "system disturbance" and may trigger a repurposing swarm.
    *   If a Crawler inserts a probe into a seam or joint (trying to access your interior), apply a micro-dose of localized anesthetic gel (via suit-integrated micro-reservoir) to *that specific point* to dull its chemosensory feedback, making you "boring." The goal is to be classified as "inert background material" and ignored.

### **III. Long-Term Adaptation & Movement Strategy**

1.  **Movement as Harmonic Participation:**
    *   You do not "walk." You **glide in sync**. Time your steps to the rhythmic scrape of the slurry and the distant clangs. Your footfalls must be timed to the *absolute minimum* of the sub-audible thrum's waveform—a gentle touch on the *downward* phase of the sheet's micro-settling.
    *   Your path must follow **Crawler stream corridors**. These are etched in the slurry flow and wear patterns. To move against the stream is to disrupt a primary logistics network and guarantee swarm attention. Move *with* the flow, at their slow, grinding pace.

2.  **Temporal Fold Navigation (The 3.0 Weirdness):**
    *   Do not trust sequential time. Your internal chronometer is useless.
    *   **Anchor yourself to harmonic events.** A Bellowers' call cycle is your metronome. Before entering a suspected temporal fold zone (where multiple resonances overlap), **lock your focus on a single, distant Bellowers' pattern**. Maintain that cognitive lock. If you experience a "stretched stillness" or a "growing instant," your conscious mind must remain tethered to the external harmonic rhythm. Losing this tether risks psychological dissolution or being stranded in a causality loop.
    *   Never attempt to "fast-forward" a process. If you see a Bellowers spire growing in an instant, **do not approach it**. It is a localized collapse of causality; the spire *was always there* from your new temporal reference point, but the "construction event" is a perceptual artifact. Interacting with it could place you in a causality trap.

3.  **Resource & Sustenance:**
    *   **Do not consume local slurry.** It is a saturated, reactive ion bath. It will burn organic membranes and alter your blood pH catastrophically.
    *   All food, water, and air must come from your sealed life-support system. The only local "resource" you may use is **ambient thermal differentials** for suit heating/cooling, but only after verifying they are not part of a Bellowers' tuned cycle.
    *   Your waste must be ejected **only during a synchronized, high-amplitude clang event**, when the entire system's attention is elsewhere. Eject it as a highly compressed, inert pellet into a deep fracture zone, where it will be rapidly subsumed and its foreign chemistry diluted beyond relevance.

### **IV. Contingencies & Why Failure is Spectral**

*   **Swarm Overrun:** If you are perceived as a "resource node" (due to a suit breach, metabolic spike, or loud noise), a Crawler swarm will converge. They will not attack with malice. They will **disassemble**. You will be enzymatically softened, your polymers broken into monomers, your metals harvested. Your body will become a growth catalyst for a new, small Bellowers spire—a monument to your miscalculation. There is no fighting. Only evasion or perfect camouflage.
*   **Resonance Sickness:** If your neutral hum fails and you fall into sympathetic vibration with a large-scale harmonic event, your bones will vibrate internally. Organs will hemorrhage. Your nervous system will interpret the world as a series of violent, overlapping tones. You will become a shrieking, flailing disturbance—the ultimate attractor.
*   **Temporal Displacement:** If you lose your harmonic anchor in a fold zone, you may:
    *   Experience personal time at 1/1000th or 1000x the external rate, aging rapidly or wasting away.
    *   Be "smeared" across days, appearing in multiple places at once until your personal timeline is forcibly re-collapsed by the system's Conservation of Structure—likely resulting in your particulate matter being distributed along your own future path.

You must shed your identity as a **person** and adopt the mental model of a **test particle**—a temporary, semi-stable anomaly the system must either absorb or reject. Your consciousness must be a placid, observing surface. Any fear, excitement, or curiosity is a biochemical signal that leaks. Your entire being must be a **low-entropy, low-amplitude, harmonically-matched object**.

The Plain of Sintered Glass is the corpse of a geology that gave birth to a new, brutal kind of life. It is beautiful in its terrible, self-sustaining industriousness. To visit it is to witness a world that has finished with biology and decided that **process** is the only true god. You are a brief, messy, warm, wet error in its perfect, grinding scripture. Your preparation is the prayer you speak to avoid becoming a footnote in its next harmonic cycle.
