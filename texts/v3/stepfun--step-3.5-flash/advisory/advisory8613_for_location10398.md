# Multiverse Traveler’s preparatory guide: Fuming City of Glass

## I. Physiological & Environmental Adaptation

**A. Thermal & Respiratory Protection**
- **Primary Exposure Risk**: The air’s permanent damp cold (0.1/3) is not merely uncomfortable—it’s a **pulmonary irritant**. The “mineral tang” indicates suspended silica, sulfur compounds, and ozone. Unprotected respiration for >1 hour will cause:
  - Immediate: bronchial constriction, coughing fits, loss of breath control.
  - Cumulative: silicosis-like inflammation, potential long-term lung scarring.
- **Required Gear**:
  1. **Full-face rebreather or heated humidifier mask** with **HEPA + chemical adsorbent filters** (activated charcoal + silica-specific media). Do not rely on simple cloth masks.
  2. **Thermal underlayer** (aerogel-infused fabric) beneath a **windproof, vapor-barrier shell**. The environment is damp enough that cotton will wick cold and freeze.
  3. **Insulated, grippy footwear** with **microspike soles**. The glass plain, even with its ice crust, is like a polished sheetrock of obsidian—a single slip means slides into unknown crevasses or fauna swarms.
  4. **Eye protection**: **Tiered—** (a) **low-light NVGs** (starlight scope) for base navigation; (b) **full-spectrum, polarized wrap-around** to handle the fauna’s “revealing” bioluminescence which creates high-contrast, disorienting shadows. Consider **temporary blink-training** to deal with shadow-pooling illusions.

**B. Auditory & Vibration Management**
- The **2.2/3 loud symphonic thrum** is not just sound—it’s **physical vibration** through the ground and air. Prolonged exposure causes:
  - **Temporary numbness**, loss of balance, nausea (similar to industrial noise or seismic activity).
  - **Sonic texture** (hiss, mutter) may carry **information**—ignoring it is perilous.
- **Required Gear**:
  1. **Active noise-canceling headphones** with **bone-conduction bypass**. This allows you to hear critical sounds (creaking glass, fauna approach) while damping the overwhelming ambient thrum. Test beforehand for sound distortion.
  2. **Seismic-sensor anklet/wrist unit** (like a geologist’s creep meter). Little震动 changes may signal spire extrusion, crack propagation, or fauna river shifts.

**C. Mobility & Surface Interaction**
- **The Glass Plain**:
  - **Reflective but fractured**—like a giant, cold mirror. Your own movement will be **visible from above/afar** to fauna and possibly other entities. Camouflage is impossible; consider **non-reflective, matte-black garments** to minimize glint.
  - **Transient ice lenses** form and evaporate with a hiss. They are **unstable**—may support weight one moment, shatter the next. **Never assume static surface**.
  - **Glowing cracks**: do not step on them. They are **active hydrological/structural features**. They may:
    - Emit stronger moisture jets (“weeping seams”) that freeze instantly around objects.
    - Be conduits for fauna movement (they travel *within* the cracks).
    - Widen slowly—standing on one may trap you as it parts.
- **Megastructure Interface**:
  - Architecture **defies load logic**. Assume **no railing is bearing weight**, **no step is solid**. Test every contact point before committing weight.
  - **Spires and cantilevers** taper to points of light—likely **navigation hazards or optical illusions**. Use laser rangefinder to verify physical presence.
  - **Buried lower levels** are under glacial debris—may be unstable, filled with fauna nests, or emit toxic gases. Avoid without atmospheric scanner.

## II. Fauna Interaction Protocols

**A. Behavioral Comprehension**
- They are **2.7/3 overwhelming**—a **self-replicating infrastructure ecology**. They are not “animals”; they are **city-metabolism**. Do not:
  - **Kill or specimens-collect**. This is like tearing out wiring from a living building—will trigger defensive/repair cascades (possible swarm aggression, structural disruption).
  - **Ignore them**. They are **everywhere** and **watching** (likely through diffuse sensory network). Your presence is a **foreign metabolic input** (heat, CO₂, sweat salts). You are either **intruder**, **resource**, or **malfunction**.
- **Movement Patterns**:
  - Individually **frantic**, collectively **slow-systemic**. Watch for **developmental flow directions**—they may clear paths, build bridges, or seal areas.
  - **Semi-translucent bodies with glowing reactions**: these are likely **computational/chemical processing**. If they freeze for days, they are in **low-power or integration mode**—disturbing them may cause cascade failure.
  - **Temporary structures** (living buttresses, curtains) are **critical infrastructure**. Do not lean on, touch, or break them. If one collapses near you, you will be **swarmed**—not out of malice, but because you disrupted a load-path.

**B. Containment & Deterrence**
- **Assume you will be swarmed**. They may:
  - **Climb you** to sense your composition (like termites inspecting wood).
  - **Carry you** to a processing site (similar to ants moving debris).
  - **Seal you in** a crevice as part of a repair operation.
- **Preventive Measures**:
  1. **Chemical scent-masking**: Apply **non-toxic, silica-based powder** to skin/clothing to mimic local mineral signature. Avoid organic smells (perfume, sweat, food).
  2. **Thermal signature reduction**: **Reflective emergency blankets** worn *under* matte-black outer layer to contain body heat. In this cold, your warmth is a **beacon**.
  3. **Sound deterrent**: Carry a **high-frequency clicker** (20kHz) not to harm, but to create **localized auditory “static”** that may disrupt their coordination—use sparingly, only if swarming begins.
  4. **Physical barrier**: **Rigid, smooth-surfaced shelter** (e.g., collapsible carbon-fiber tube) you can retreat into. They are mineral-based—they may not easily grip ultra-smooth curves.

## III. Psychological & Cognitive Preparedness

**A. Sensory Recalibration**
- **No sky, no stars**: You have **no celestial orientation**. Your **vestibular system** will fight the lack of up/down reference (the blackness “presses down like a lid”). This causes:
  - **Spatial disorientation**, nausea, vertigo.
  - **Time distortion**—without day/night cycle, your circadian rhythm will collapse within 24h.
- **Training**:
  1. **Practice in light-deprived, featureless environments** (deep cave, foggy white room) for 4+ hours at a time. Use only **tactile/auditory cues**.
  2. **Wear a gravity-simulation belt** (weighted unevenly) to maintain sense of vertical.
  3. **Adhere to a strict artificial schedule** (via震动 watch) for sleep/activity. Use **melatonin** to reset cycle.

- **Sound as texture**: The thrum is **physical**. You may feel sounds in your teeth. Do not let it **merge into white noise**—specific frequencies may signal danger (crack propagation = rising pitch; spire extrusion = deep resonate). Train to **parse the symphony** using directional microphones.

- **Shadows with weight**: Shadows seem to pool. This is likely **light-absorption properties** of the black stone + fauna bioluminescence. Your brain will interpret this as **solid obstacles**. **Do not step over “shadow voids”**—they are optical. Use a **laser pointer** to verify surface continuity.

**B. Temporal Mindset Shift**
- **Systems are slow (0.6/3 dynamic)**. A spire grows in a day. A crack widens in hours.
- **Your human urgency is a disability**. Rushing will cause falls, missteps, swarm triggers.
- **Adopt a “geological patience”**:
  - **Observe one feature for 1 hour** before moving. Chart its changes.
  - **Do not interrupt processes** (e.g., do not try to “speed up” a widening crack to pass; wait for it to widen naturally).
  - **Assume any structure you see has been forming for centuries**—your presence is a **blink** in its timeline.

**C. Existential Buffer**
- This is a **post-biological, mineraltech civilization**. Their values, if any, are **tempo-geological**. Concepts like “history,” “art,” “communication” may not apply.
- **You are not a “visitor”**—you are a **temporary perturbation**. There may be **no consciousness** here to greet you. The “city” may be an **autonomous, self-sustaining system** with zero awareness of you.
- **Mental Prep**:
  - **Write a pre-exit letter** to your home-reality self, stating your hypothesis that **no one will know you were here**.
  - **Practice “non-intervention presence”**—like being a ghost in a machine room. Do not touch, do not leave traces, do not expect reciprocity.
  - **Prepare for radical loneliness**. The sound is **not language**. It is **system hum**.

## V. Ethical & Practical Protocols

**A. Prime Directive: Non-Interference**
- Do not **remove anything** (not even a pebble).
- Do not **alter anything** (do not prop up a leaning spire, do not clear a path).
- Do not **communicate attempts** (flashing lights, sounds)—may be interpreted as system error or attack.
- If you **accidentally damage** (break ice crust, crack glass), **immediately retreat** and **monitor** for swarm response. If none, **vacate area**.

**B. Contingency Planning**
- **Get Swarmed**:
  1. **Stop moving**. Freeze. Many fauna freeze too—they may treat you as part of the “new structure.”
  2. **Activate thermal mask** to cool skin.
  3. If they begin carrying you, **curl into ball**, protect air passages. They may deposit you in a **debris pit** or **new crack**—be ready to **dig out** with diamond saw when thrum shifts.
- **Lost/Disoriented**:
  - **Do not wander**. Pick a **large, static spire** as landmark. Use LiDAR to map surroundings. Wait for **systemic shift** (a fauna river moving) to reorient.
- **Respiratory failure**:
  - **Immediate retreat** to shelter, 100% O₂ from canister. If mask fails, **breathe shallow** from debris-free crevasses (use portable air sampler first).

**C. Exit Strategy**
- **Do not seek an “exit.”** There is no gate, no door. The city is **the plateau**.
- **Return path**: **Retrace inertial logs exactly**. The city changes—your outbound path may be gone (spire grown, crack widened). Have **multiple backup routes** from initial mapping.
- **Final Check**: Before leaving the plateau, **decontaminate**:
  - Brush off all **fauna remnants**, **ice crust**, **glass dust** into sealed bag.
  - ** Inhaler treatment** for lungs.
  - **Reflective blanket removal** (to avoid bringing thermal signature elsewhere).
