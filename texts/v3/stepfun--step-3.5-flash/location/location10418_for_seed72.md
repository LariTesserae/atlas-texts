The place is called the **Seep District**, and it lives in the belly of a half-formed world.

**Terrain & Elevation:** You are not *in* a valley or a basin. You are *under* the skin of a place that has not yet decided if it will be a mountain range or a plain. The elevation is 0.0—below surface—but this is not a cave. It is a **subterranean shelf** in a vast, raw geological wound. Imagine a continent-sized scar in the earth’s crust, miles deep, where the tectonic plates are still sighing and settling. The “floor” is a plane of cracked, dark stone, laid bare by a retreating sea of sediment that hasn’t been replaced. It slopes gently in some places, drops suddenly in others into slick, wet ravines. Above, not a sky, but a **ceiling of weeping rock**. It isn’t solid; it’s a permeable membrane of porous sandstone and clay, saturated from above. Water doesn’t fall here—it **seeps, drips, oozes, and condenses** in a constant, cool weeping. Puddles are not pools but **transient seas**, filling shallow basins before slowly draining into fissures or evaporating in the heat.

**Atmosphere & Light:** The temperature is 2.9/3—**baking hot**, but not with the dry heat of a desert. It’s the deep, pressurized warmth of a pressure cooker, radiating from geothermal vents that speckle the district like industrial furnaces. The air is thick, wet, and carries a perpetual **sulfurous tang** mixed with the smell of hot stone and ozone. Light is 1.0/3—**dim**. There is no sun. Illumination comes from three sources: the **sickly, amber glow of chemiluminescent molds** clinging to the wet walls; the **fierce, white-yellow glare of industrial arc-lamps** mounted on iron towers; and the **dull, ember-red wink of geothermal vents** in the distance. It’s a chiaroscuro world of pockets of harsh light and vast, shadowy emptiness.

**Materials & Built Environment:** Urban density is 2.1/3, but it’s an **urbanism of necessity, not design**. The settlement is a **clinging, ramshackle city** built on and into the raw stone. Structures are not carved, but **bolted, welded, and piled**. Foundations are concrete pads laid on unstable scree. Walls are corrugated iron, scavenged sheet metal, and tarpaulin, all streaked with orange mineral stains and black mold. Multi-story **fire escapes** and **catenary bridges** of chain and rope connect buildings that lean against each other for support, climbing the slopes of natural stone pillars. The infrastructure is exposed: **steam pipes** snaking along walkways, **ventilation ducts** hissing, **cable bundles** like metallic vines. It’s a frontier town built around **geothermal taps and mineral siphons**—industrial tech at 1.7/3. You see the **piston-driven rhythm of pumping stations**, the **smoke-stack breath of coal-fired generators** (fuel brought from who knows where), the **hiss of steam releases**. It’s not sleek; it’s **greasy, sweating, and loud**.

**Ecology & Vegetation:** Vegetation is 0.9/3—**sparse to the point of absence**. There are no trees, no grasses. Life here does not photosynthesize. What passes for flora are **crystalline, silicate-based crusts** that grow in slow, fractal patterns on any permanently damp, sunless surface, drinking minerals from the seep water. They are fragile, glassy, and crunch underfoot. The real ecology is **faunal**, and it is overwhelming (2.8/3). The district **thrums** with it. **Blind, pale crustaceans** the size of dogs scuttle in the puddles, filtering bacterial scum. **Gliding, membranous things** with too many wings drift on the warm updrafts from vents, feeding on the chemiluminescent mold. **Tunnel-worms** cause low, seismic tremors as they move through the soft sediment. **Swarm-clouds of metallic, iridescent flies** gather around the industrial lights, their buzzing a constant component of the soundscape. Predators—**silent, six-limbed lizards** with heat-sensing pits—watch from the shadows of the rock formations. Life is **abundant, strange, and entirely focused on chemical and thermal energy**, utterly alien to a sunlit world.

**Sound & Dynamic:** Sound is 1.6/3—**loud**. It is a **layered, industrial-biologic cacophony**. The baseline is a **deep, subsonic hum** from the geothermal generators and the tremors of deep burrowers. Over that, the **rhythmic clang and hiss** of the machinery: pistons, pumps, pressure valves. The **skittering and splashing** of countless small fauna in the water. The **constant, high-pitched drone** of the insect swarms. And the ever-present **drip… drip… drip** of water from the ceiling, a metronome for the whole slow scene. The dynamic is 1.3/3—**slow**. Change is glacial. A puddle’s drain may take a season. A rusting iron beam may take a decade to collapse. The growth of a crystalline crust is measured in centuries. The city itself **grows by accretion, not planning**—a new shanty shack appears where a mineral vein is tapped, a bridge is added when a population of crawlers migrates. The only rapid change is sudden: a vent might **erupt** with scalding water without warning, or a swarm might **migrate** en masse, creating a temporary, deafening cloud.

**Rules of the World & Atmosphere:** The fundamental rule is **energy from below, not above**. Light is a luxury, heat is the currency. Water is the medium of life and industry, but it is always **metabolizing, draining, or being replaced**. The “weirdness” is 0.4/3—**normal**, because for those who live here, this is simply reality. The unsettling feeling comes from the **scale inversion**: you are in a “room” the size of a small city, yet the ceiling is close enough to weep on you. The density is 0.7/3—**sparse** in terms of people and clear space, but **hyper-dense in life and machinery** in the occupied zones. You can stand in absolute, dripping silence in a shadowed cleft, then turn and see a bustling, clanging settlement a few hundred yards away, separated by a field of glittering crystal and a ravine filled with blind, scuttling things.

The overall feel is one of **embryonic grandeur**. This is a place being born, not dying. The erosion is **emerging**—the rock is still fracturing, the water is still carving new channels, the city is still sweating itself into the stone. It is **hot, wet, loud, and teeming**, a brutal, industrious, biological engine churning in the dark, built on a landscape that has not yet finished making itself.
