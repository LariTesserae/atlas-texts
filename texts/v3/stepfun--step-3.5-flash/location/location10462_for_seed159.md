## The Rattle-Caverns of Silt-Heart

You do not enter this place. You are **accreted** into it.

The elevation is below surface, yes—but the scale is open, and so the world is a **subterranean continent**, a hollowed basin under a thinner crust, lit not by sun but by the pervasive, normal-light glow of warmth itself. Think of a valley the size of a small country, buried under miles of rock, its ceiling a lost sky of blackened stone ribs and hanging dunes of sinter. The temperature is a constant, damp-heat, like the breath of a sleeping engine. The air is thick with motes of mineral dust and the **sound**—oh, the sound is **loud**, a physical pressure in the ears. It is not one sound, but a **turbulent** superposition of millions: the skitter-teeth of chitin on stone, the drip andplash of warm condensation from the ceiling into pools that never fully evaporate, the low-thrum of vibratory communication passing through the rock itself, the explosive *puff* of gas vents releasing built pressure, the crash of small mineral collapses from overhangs. It is the sound of a place that is **emerging**, **dense**, and **dynamic**—a living geology.

There is **no vegetation**. None. The parameter is 0.4/3—no plant life. Instead, the base of this world is **energy-pattern and mineral**. The floor and walls are coated in a thin, iridescent **fungal-or-simpler** film, a symbiotic slime-mold and chemosynthetic bacteria mat that extracts trace metals and thermal energy from the rock. It is not Plant. It is **Process**. It glistens in wet patches, shifts colors with its metabolic state, and pulses faintly in the dark. Where it is thickest, it forms stiff, leathery ridges and shelves. This is the **primary producer**.

From this **non-recognized biology** springs the **overwhelming fauna**. Fauna: 2.6/3. They are not animals as we know them. They are **phenomena given form**. The density is 2.0/3—many presences per volume, near-touching, layered. You do not just see them on the ground. They are in the air (dense swarms of winged, membranous things that hum), on the walls ( crawling, limbless slats that move by peristalsis), in the shallow pools (jellied spheres that ripple with internal light), and in the slime-mats themselves (the mats *are* the slowest of the fauna, a colony-consciousness grazing).

One example: the **Rattlers**. Size of large dogs, segmented bodies of overlapping black plates, they move with a constant, rapid side-to-side shudder that produces the dominant skitter-teeth sound. They have no eyes; they “see” by projecting and receiving low-frequency vibrations through the ground and their own plates. They are filter-feeders, sweeping the slime-mats with brush-tongues. Where they pass, the mat is briefly depressed, then flows back into the grooves their plates leave. They are **everywhere**. In some wide, open chambers, the floor is a chittering, living carpet of them, a single organism of ten thousand individuals, parts separating and re-merging.

Another: the **Glow-Kites**. Buoyant, gas-filled sacs the size of hats, trailing tendrils that sting and paralyze smaller flying things. They drift on thermal updrafts from vents, their sacs pulsing with a soft cyan bioluminescence that provides the only directional light in the vastnesses. Thousands drift in slow, chaotic currents, their tendrils occasionally snagging on a Rattler’s plate with a sharp *tick*.

The **erosion** is **emerging**. This is not a worn, ancient world. It is **pre-formation, under construction**. The landscape is raw geology in motion. The **weirdness** at 1.3/3 is in the slow, deliberate *change* of the very stone. In some areas, Shelf-Fungi (thick, crystalline growths from the slime-mats) are actively **eating** the rock face, dissolving it with acidic excretions to release locked minerals, leaving behind intricate, lace-like hollows that then fill with new slime. In other areas, **venting spires** of sinter are growing at a visible rate, inch by inch per hour, from mineral-rich exhalations, their tips glowing from internal heat. The ground in some low-lying sections **breathes**—a slow, rhythmic rise and fall as gas pockets inflate and deflate beneath a fragile crust. The **dynamic** is 2.9/3—a **bazaar** of creation and dissolution, a **Solaris-like** planet that thinks in rock and slime.

The **built** structures are **scattered** (1.0/3), **primitive** (0.8/3). They are not cities. They are **thermal anchors**: simple arrangements of flat stone slabs and stacked rocks, placed over the warmest, most stable vents. Sometimes a ring of stones contains a deeper pool of the slime-mat, cultivated and “farmed” for its densest nutrients. Occasionally, you find a **tool**: a sharpened sliver of obsidian lashed to a bone handle with a tendon-thread from a Kite, used for scraping prized, metal-rich Fungi off rock faces. Or a hollowed stone bowl used to channel vent-gases into a contained space for warmth. The makers are rarely seen—perhaps a taller, reclusive form that moves with a silent, four-limbed gait, seen only as a disturbance in the Rattler crowds, a gap that flows behind it. Their **tech** is **early-tool-use, manual-craft**. They do not build *up*; they **intervene** in the existing turbulence, marking stable points in the flux.

The **water** parameter (1.4/3—damp) is satisfied by the constant **condensation environments**. The ceiling is a dripping, weeping membrane of rock. Warm, moist air from deep vents meets the cooler rock of the cavern roof, and a perpetual, gentle rain falls. This rain collects in **pockets of water in larger dryness**—shallow, warm pools that steam slightly, their surfaces alive with darting, shrimp-like swimmers and the floating, medusa-like forms of the pool-fauna. The sound of this dripping is a fundamental rhythm beneath the louder chitter and hum.

The **atmosphere** is warm, wet, heavy with mineral dust and the faint, sweet-rot smell of the slime-mats. The **light** is **normal** but diffuse, emanating from the Glow-Kites, the warm rock faces, and the faint pulse of the liveliest slime-thickets. Shadows are soft, indistinct.

The **rules of this world**:
1.  There is no separation between geography and biology. The “terrain” is a living, metabolic process.
2.  Stability is temporary. A floor you stand on may soften or rise in an hour. A vent you rely on may seal or explode.
3.  Density is not a count of things, but a state of being. You are *in* the fauna, not *among* them. Their communications vibrate in your bones.
4.  Value is placed on thermal and mineral constancy. The scattered built works are prayers or treaties with the turbulent geology.

You stand on a ridge of black, glassy volcanic glass (`erosion: emerging`), looking out over a **regional** (`scale: open`) vista. Below, a wide chamber stretches into blue-tinged darkness, its floor a heaving, chittering plain of Rattlers, broken by islands of glowing slime-m plantations. Above, a river of Glow-Kites drifts in a slow vortex around a growing sinter spire that glows red at its tip. To your left, a thermal rain falls in a steady curtain onto a pile of loose scree that *breathes*. The sound is a single, unified roar of life and becoming. This is not a place that *is*. It is a place that **does**. And you, with your sense of separate self and silent bones, are the deepest anomaly here.
