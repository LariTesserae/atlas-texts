You stand on a stone that is not a stone. It is the remnant of a mountain’s marrow, worn smooth by a single, endless motion. The world is water. Not a sea, but a *condition*—a globe-encircling, deep, cold, mineral-rich broth. There is no horizon of sky, only a gradual darkening from a murky, greenish grey above to an absolute, lightless black below. You are at the interface, where the last photons from a distant, veiled sun expire after fathoms of travel.
