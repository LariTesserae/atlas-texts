**Name:** The Stillstone Trough  
**Other Names:** The Whispering Lode, The Hush-Cradle, The Bright Below  

---

You find it not by digging, but by *listening*—or rather, by the sudden, profound cessation of sound. One moment you are in a windswept scrubland of pale, wiry shrubs (the sparse, cool world above), and the next you are standing at the lip of a vertical shaft, looking down into a place that contradicts its own parameters.

**The Setting: A Pocket in the World’s Womb**  
It is a single, contained cavity—a trough-shaped hollow carved not by water, but by *absence*. The scale is intimate: a chamber perhaps 200 meters long, 50 wide, and 30 deep at its deepest. You are on a narrow ledge. The opposite wall is close enough to throw a stone across. Yet it feels vast because of its *verticality*. The ceiling is a dome of black, glassy rock, seamless and fused. It is not a cave; it is a *negative form*, a space where something was pulled out of the earth and left this smooth-sided scar. The elevation is below surface, but you are not under the ground—you are *within* a geological experiment that never finished.

**Light: The Unblinking Eye**  
At the far end, mounted on a natural pillar of rose-hued crystal, is the source of the 2.6/3 brightness: a softly humming, fist-sized sphere of pure, white light. It casts no shadows. Everything is illuminated from everywhere and nowhere, as if the air itself is luminous. The light reveals every grain of the walls, but it has no direction. Your eyes cannot adjust because there is no contrast to adjust *from*. This is not sunlight or firelight. It is *display light*. The sphere is one of the “scattered built” artifacts—a single, silent, unpowered (or perpetually powered) object that is clearly *placed*, not grown. It does not obey local physics. It is an axiom here.

**Terrain & Erosion: The Unfinished Symphony**  
The floor is a mosaic of materials in a state of becoming. Patches of fine, white silt (the “damp” 1.0/3) are intermittently wet, not from rain, but from condensation that beads on surfaces and then *flows uphill* for a few centimeters before vanishing into porous, honey-colored stone. The “erosion: emerging” is visible as slow-motion catastrophe. Curtains of what looks like fused sand hang from the walls, but they are not solid. They are *viscous air*—regions of space so dense with suspended mineral dust they behave like thin, dripping liquid. One such curtain, two meters tall, is currently “flowing” sideways across the floor at a rate you could follow with your eye, dissolving the silt beneath it into glistening, opal-like film.

The walls are layered: bands of dark basalt, veins of glittering mica, strata of chalky white. But the layers are *wrong*. Some are bent backwards into the wall. Others are stacked in angles no tectonic force could produce. This is raw, pre-logical geology, a place where stone remembers being magma and sediment at the same time.

**Atmosphere & Sound: The Held Breath**  
Sound is 0.5/3—a thick, padded silence. Your footsteps are muffled as if on thick felt. Your voice comes back thin and remote. The silence is *physical*, a pressure. It is broken only by:
1.  The soft, sub-audible hum of the light-sphere (felt in the teeth, not heard).
2.  A distant, rhythmic *tick… tick… tick…* like a slow clock, coming from somewhere in the rock—a mineral stress fracture, or something else counting.
3.  The faintest rustle, not of wind (there is none), but of countless tiny movements.

**Ecology: Abundance in the Spare**  
Fauna: 2.4/3. They are *everywhere*, and yet you see almost none at first. The “sparse” density refers to *visible, macroscopic bodies*. The abundance is in the micro and the paradoxical.

*   **The Dust-Skimmers:** In the luminous air, millions of specks drift. They are not dust. They are gelatinous, seed-shaped organisms, translucent and pulsing with a slow, internal bioluminescence that matches the sphere’s glow. They feed on the mineral-laden condensation. When you hold very still, one will land on your hand. It feels like a cool, damp raindrop that subtly rearranges itself.
*   **The Wall-Crawlers:** On the strange stone curtains, you see the “dripping” motion is not just the stone. It is also due to dozens of limbless, slug-like creatures the color of wet slate. They meter through the viscous curtain, consuming the suspended particles. Their trails leave faint, phosphorescent blue smears that fade in seconds.
*   **The Silence-Deer:** High on a ledge, you spot three of them. They are deer-like, but with legs that seem too long and joints that bend the wrong way. Their coats are made not of hair, but of fine, black glass threads that drink the light. They make no sound as they move. They have no ears. Their heads are smooth, with a single, large, faceted eye like a deep-space telescope. They are not looking *at* you; they are looking *through* the space you occupy, calibrating their position in a geometry you cannot perceive. They are the apex predator here, but you do not see what they hunt.

Vegetation: 0.8/3. There are no plants. Instead:
*   **Sinter Moss:** Patches of rust-red, felt-like growth on the cooler stone. It feeds on atmospheric ions and the light-sphere’s stray emissions. It is cold to the touch.
*   **The Glass-Fronds:** Delicate, branching structures that grow from wet silt patches. They are made of transparent silica, and inside each branch is a slow, amber current of fluid—a circulatory system for a sessile, photosynthetic organism that uses the strange light.

**Weirdness: The Breakdown (2.9/3)**  
This is the core. Reality here is *negotiable*.
*   **Non-Euclidean Proximity:** The too-close opposite wall sometimes *feels* farther away. Corridors in the rock seem to open and close when not observed directly.
*   **Temporal Slip:** The “tick” from the rock is not constant. Sometimes it speeds up to a rattle, then slows to a minute-long pause. When it speeds up, the Dust-Skimmers zipping around you seem to leave after-images.
*   **Material Ambiguity:** Is that a rock formation or a petrified creature? Is the water on the floor condensation, or the exhalation of the porous stone? Is the hum a sound, or a sensation of your own bones resonating?
*   **The Scattered Built:** Besides the light-sphere, there are “artifacts.” A perfect sphere of obsidian, half-buried. A chair-shaped formation in the rock that is too symmetrical. A flat, yellow metal plaque with no seams, covered in glyphs that shift when you blink. They are not *of* this place, but they are not *in* it—they are part of its fabric, like fossils of a different reality.

**Rules of This World:**
1.  **Observation Affects Gradient:** The more you focus on a weird phenomenon (like the flowing stone curtain), the more stable and “normal” it becomes. Look away, and it reverts to full strangeness.
2.  **Silence is a Substance:** Sound does not travel far; it is absorbed and converted into a faint heat. Loud noises create temporary, invisible “heat mirages” that distort the light.
3.  **The Light-Sphere is a Anchor:** It is the one constant. Its presence defines the pocket. If you were to destroy it (impossible by mundane means), the chamber would likely dissolve into a formless, screaming geometry.
4.  **Fauna Navigate by What’s Missing:** The creatures here sense the gaps in reality, the non-Euclidean folds. They move in straight lines *for them*, which look like impossible zig-zags to you.

**The Feeling:**  
It is a place of breathtaking, eerie calm. The overwhelming abundance of silent life moving through a dying, raw landscape, under the gaze of a single, alien artifact, in a room that is both a crypt and a nursery. The cool air carries the mineral scent of wet stone and ozone. The pervasive light makes everything look both hyper-real and utterly insubstantial. You are not in nature, nor in a ruin. You are in a *draft*—a geological sketch, a biological prototype, a corner of the world that was laid out with a ruler that had no fixed points, and then abandoned, left to run its quiet, active, deeply weird processes in a pocket of bright, silent forever.
