## The Polished Scablands of Khepri

It is not a desert of sand, but of **prisms**.

The world is a low, sweeping plain of geometrically perfect, six-sided columns, each rising from the next like the teeth of some colossal, fossilized saw. They are not stone, not crystal, but a substance that mimics both: a vitreous ceramic alloy, polished by processes older than memory to a mirror sheen that drinks the light and returns it as a flat, blinding glare. The scale is planetary. The horizon in every direction is a perfect, unwavering line where the endless forest of prisms meets the infinite sky—no mountains, no valleys, only the gentle, unimaginable swell of a world worn utterly smooth.

**Terrain & Materials:** The prisms are uniform, varying only in height (from ankle-high to low-hanging clouds) and in the minute, frost-like fractals that spider across their upper faces. Their bases are fused together into a continuous, tessellated pavement, cracked only by the slow, seismic sighs of the planet itself—deep, resonant groans that travel through the ceramic bedrock. Touch one, and it is cool, vibration-dampening, inert. There is no soil, only a fine, metallic dust—the pulverized residue of the prisms themselves, ground down by epochs of wind that carries no sand, only this razor-grade powder. Walking upon it is like treading on powdered glass that never cuts, because nothing here *moves* fast enough to create friction.

**Atmosphere & Light:** The air is thin, sharp, and odorless. Light is the dominant presence—a white, uncompromising sun in a sky of perpetual, cloudless cobalt. There are no shadows, only areas of greater and lesser brilliance. The prisms catch and fracture the light into static rainbows that hang in the air like ghostly afterimages. The brightness is physical, a pressure on the eyelids. It does not warm; the temperature is a constant, oppressive warmth, like the breath of a sleeping engine.

**Sound & Silence:** The parameter for sound is 0.3/3—near silence. Here, silence is not an absence, but a **substance**. It is a thick, absorbent medium that muffles even the crunch of boots on the dust. The only regular sounds are the planet’s groans—sub-audible tremors that you feel in your molars—and the whisper of the eternal wind, which is less a noise and more a change in the pattern of light on the prism-faces, a slow, silent migration of luminance. Your own voice sounds dead, swallowed after a few feet.

**Ecology & Fauna:** Vegetation is sparse (0.7/3). Life clings not to the ground, but to the vertical. On the north-facing faces of the taller prisms, in the perpetual niches of twilight, grow **lensing lichens**. They are flat, silver-black discs that focus the bright ambient light into microscopic, internal fires, generating the scant chemical energy they need. They do not move, reproduce with geological slowness, and are the only green thing—a chlorophyll-like pigment that gleams with a captured, internal gold. Fauna is rare (0.6/3). The sole animal is the **Refraction-Strider**: a limbless, ribbon-like organism of iridescent chitin that flows *between* the prism-faces, navigating the silent maze by detecting minute variances in polarized light. It feeds on the lichens and on the metallic dust itself, digesting it with enzymes that should be impossible. It moves with a silent, undulating grace, leaving no trace.

**Built Environment & Tech:** Built is total (2.6/3), tech is beyond modern (2.6/3). This is not a *ruin*. This is a **place that has always been made**. The prisms are not a remnant of some fallen city; they are the fundamental geology. The consensus is that this is a **planetary-scale fabrication project** from a civilization so ancient its motives are now part of the world’s physics. The "erosion" (2.6/3) is not wear from weather, but the **final stage of a designed longevity test**—the slow, inexorable conversion of engineered structure into stable, inert form. The prisms are settling into their end-state: a perfectly smooth, silent, self-maintaining grid. The "density" (1.9/3) comes from the sheer, overlapping presence of the columns—a cubic kilometer contains millions of unique, identical objects, a saturation of manufactured form.

**World Rules & Dynamics:** This is a world of **conserved stillness**. The dynamic (2.1/3) is active but glacial. The "weather" is the **Crystallization Tide**. Once per planetary cycle (roughly a local year), a wave of cooler, denser air—still warm by human standards—sweeps from the western datum line. It does not bring rain. It brings **supersaturation**. The metallic dust in the air instantly plates onto the prism surfaces, growing a new, microscopic layer of the same ceramic alloy. It is a silent, shimmering blizzard of construction. Then, the heat returns, and the weakest, newest layers on the lowest prisms flake away in a slow, sandy cascade, feeding the dust-sea. This is the only true cycle of creation and erasure, the planet’s slow, mechanical breathing.

The "weirdness" (1.5/3) is subtle, cumulative. Light behaves slightly wrong here; distances are deceptive, as if the mirror-faces are collectively bending perspective. Time feels elastic. The silence has weight. You become aware of the **sound of your own thoughts** in a way that feels invasive, as if the environment is listening with its perfect, polished surfaces. The rules of material science are bent: the dust, the lichens, the Strider—they all incorporate and metabolize the prism-material, a substance that should be utterly inert. Something in the fundamental chemistry of this place is *permissive*.

**The Feeling:** It is a place of profound loneliness not because it is empty, but because it is **full of purpose you cannot comprehend**. Every prism is a statement. Every silent, blinding acre is a syllable in a sentence written across a world. The Khepri-Engine, as some shadowy records call it, was not a machine to *do* something, but a machine to *be* something: a final, stable answer to a question no one remembers. You stand in the bright, windless silence, a chaotic, wet, brief biological error on a surface polished by deep time into perfect, mute legibility. The only movement is the slow, silent plating of new ceramic onto old, and the silent, ribbon-like flow of the Strider, hunting the silver lichens in the cool, eternal twilight between the teeth of the world.
