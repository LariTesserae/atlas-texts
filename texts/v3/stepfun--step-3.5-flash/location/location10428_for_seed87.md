## The Still-Flood

You are not underwater. You are *within* water. The distinction is critical.

The place is a cavern, but the word fails. It is a **subterranean sea** that does not end. The ceiling—if it can be called that—is a distant, velvety blackness, lost in the dim, Bioluminescent twilight. The walls are not rock, but a living geology: vast, slow-growing **mineral forests** of porous, honeycombed stone and glassy, crystalline fronds that drink the brine. They are the city’s bones, its scaffolding. The "streets" are the winding channels of water between them.

**Terrain & Materials:** The floor is a soft, oozy sediment of ground-shell and metallic silt, warm from below. It yields underfoot like dark dough. The dominant material is **chalcedony and hydromagnesite**—moonmilk and fluted globs of pearlescent stone, some still forming, dripping from above in silent, viscous beads. The "built" environment is entirely organic and geological. There are no bricks, no beams. Instead, you see **colonies of filter-feeding polyps** that secrete impossibly strong, parchment-like tubes in perfect, repeating arches, forming clustered neighborhoods. **Giant, bladder-like fungi** tethered to the mineral walls pulse with internal light, providing the ambient glow. Dense **curtains of salps**—translucent, barrel-shaped creatures—drift in the currents, linking and unlinking in silent, coordinated waves.

**Atmosphere & Light:** The air is thin and cold (0.5/3), tasting of ozone and salt. You breathe easily, but your breath mists. The light is **deep-sea dim** (1.3/3), a blue-green bioluminescence that comes from everywhere and nowhere. It originates from the resident life: the pulsing fungi, the shy flare of buried anemones, the faint shimmer along the sides of passing fauna. There are no shadows, only gradients of glow. Visibility is poor, perhaps thirty feet, then the blue-green fades into absolute, lightless black.

**Sound:** It is not silent (0.0/3 is a paradox here), but the sounds are **sub-audible, felt rather than heard**. The primary sound is a **constant, ultra-low hum**—the groan of the tectonic plate above, the sigh of brine moving through impossible pores in the stone. It vibrates in your molars. Occasionally, a deep, slow *thrum* passes through the water: a breaching leviathan far below, or a mineral collapse in the newly-forming strata. The only crisp, clear sound is the **occasional, crystalline *tink*** of a mineral growth completing, a sound like a single ice cube dropping in a glass miles away.

**Ecology:** **Fauna is abundant** (2.2/3), **dense** (1.9/3), and **strange** (1.8/3). Life is squishy, gelatinous, or armored. **Pillar-like annelids**, segmented and laced with bioluminescent veins, stand in silent fields, filtering nutrients. **Jelly-predators** with trailing nets of stinging filaments drift in lazy, concentric patterns. **Fungal grazers**—slug-like things with radula teeth of iridescent chitin—scrape the luminous walls. The food web is tight, visible: a smaller swarm of luminescent krill is suddenly erased by a silent, gaping maw from the gloom, then the maw’s owner is itself enveloped by a slower, blanket-like tangle of filaments. It is a city of constant, quiet consumption.

**Vegetation** (1.2/3 — sparse) is not green. It is **chemosynthetic**: microbial mats in rust-red and sulphur-yellow on warmer stone vents, and the slow, towering **calcite trees** that grow from mineral-rich flows, their branches brittle and white as bleached bone. They are more landform than plant.

**Rules of the World:**
1.  **Growth is Structure.** "Urban" is a misnomer. This is not a settlement; it is a **single, colossal, slow-motion organism** or a symbiotic super-colony. The "buildings" are the bodies of its inhabitants, grown in place over millennia. The architecture is dictated by fluid dynamics, nutrient flows, and mineral deposition.
2.  **Pressure is Time.** The deeper you go (the elevation is 0.3/3—below surface, but this is a *shallow* subterranean sea, a bubble in the crust), the slower everything moves. The active dynamics (2.0/3) are geological: a mineral column might grow an inch in a century. The biological dynamics are equally paced—a predator’s strike takes seconds, but its digestion takes months.
3.  **Silence is the Medium.** Sound travels strangely here, distorted and dampened by the dense brine (density 1.9/3) and the labyrinth of absorptive, porous stone. Communication is primarily **chemical and bioluminescent**. A sudden flash from one polyp cluster propagates through the network like a shout.
4.  **The Flood is Eternal, but New.** The "submerged" (2.6/3) state is absolute. This is a world after the last flood, but also before the next. The **erosion is emerging** (0.3/3): you see it in the fresh, sharp mineral sprays, the unstable overhangs of growth, the raw, glassy stone where a chunk of the ceiling recently fell. This place is not wearing down; it is **crystallizing into being**, layer by layer, drop by drop. The "wall of rain" is the constant, gentle dripping from the distant ceiling, each drop a builder’s brick.

**The Emerging Image:**
You stand on a yielding shelf of compacted salp husks. Before you, a "plaza" opens—a clearer space in the mineral forest, floored with fine, black sand. Here, the **density** is overwhelming. Dozens of translucent, floating **jelly-urchins** pulse in a slow, synchronized vortex. Their gentle luminescence is reflected in the wet, curved walls of a thousand polyp-tubes that hedge the square. The air is cold. The only sense of scale is the oppressive, intimate closeness of it all. You are not in a cave. You are **inside the lung of a sleeping stone beast**, and its dreams are made of slow growth, silent hunger, and the cold, eternal drip of creation. The world is not hidden (0.0/3 scale)—it is *intimately, claustrophobically present*. There is no horizon, only the next gelatinous body, the next glowing wall, the next silent, mineral tear forming in the dark above.
