You are not underwater. You are *inside* something alive.

There is no "shore" because there is no "outside." The water you breathe is thick with suspended plant spores, oxygenating microbes, and the fine, organic mist the entity perpetually exudes. This is not a flooded world; it is a *hydrated* one. The "wall of rain" is the constant, gentle drizzle from the fungal bracts hanging in the high canopy, and the "deep sea" is the endless, dimly lit root-maze below, where the entity’s plumbing carries nutrient-rich fluid slower than a heartbeat.

The vegetation is total, but its form is sparse and cathedral-like. Great vaults of space exist between the main support stems, filled only with drifting spore-clouds and the occasional drifting, jellyfish-like filter-feeder. This is not a jungle; it is a living warehouse, a distributed body.
