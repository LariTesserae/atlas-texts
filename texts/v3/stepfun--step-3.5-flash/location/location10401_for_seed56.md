**The Verdant Under-Crypt**

You do not enter this place. You *submerge* into it.

The world begins with a sound—a deep, resonant **hum** that isn’t heard with the ears but felt in the molars and the base of the spine. It is the sound of a planet breathing, a subsonic chorus of a trillion root-hearts tapping in unison against stone. This is the **sound** parameter made manifest: **loud**, but not with cacophony. It is a saturated, omnipressure of noise, a single, sustained chord played on the instrument of the world itself.

**Light** is **dim**, a perpetual twilight filtered through a canopy not of leaves, but of **total vegetation**. The vegetation score of 2.9/3 is not "dense forest." It is *substrate*. The very air is thick with plant-matter: drifting, photosynthetic spores the size of coins; filaments of capillary-vines carrying slow, emerald sap; and the vast, overarching planes of what might be fungal leaf or bryophyte sky. It is a **plant-as-architecture** ecosystem grown to its absolute limit. The ground is not ground; it is a **living mattress** of spongy, water-absorbing moss and lichen, several meters deep in places, overlying a softer, warmer layer of decomposing vegetal mat. Every surface is covered, saturated, *alive*.

**Water: 1.8/3 — wet.** But here, water is not a separate thing. It is the *medium*. The entire **below-surface** landscape is **near the water table**, a vast, shallow aquifer made flesh. The **marsh-edge** is everywhere. You are always standing at the shore of something. Mist, cool and carrying the scent of crushed mint and wet stone, constantly condenses from the humid air and weeps from the porous ceilings of root-vaults. liquid beads on every surface, not as droplets, but as a constant, shimmering film. The "rocks" are dripping, sweating stone, worn smooth not by erosion but by perpetual, gentle saturation.

**Temperature: 0.0/3 — cold.** This isn't a biting cold, but a deep, constant chill, like the interior of a glacial cave. The sun, even if it penetrated this dense biomass, would be a rumor. The heat is trapped deep within the decaying heart of the veg-matter, a slow, geological compost-fire radiating upwards into the damp air. Your breath plumes, and the moisture from your mouth is instantly absorbed by the voracious atmosphere.

**Erosion: 0.2/3 — emerging.** This is the most unsettling parameter made real. Nothing here is *worn*. Everything is **pre-formation**. You see landscapes in their conceptual state. immense, **cathedral-scale** vaults where pillars of intertwined root and lichen are only just now crystallizing from the substrate, still soft and dripping. Great, curved walls of living matter are **under construction**, their surfaces pulsing with growth-rings that form in observable, slow pulses. This is **raw geology** procedurally generated by biology. It feels less like a formed world and more like a **world thinking itself into being**, a slow, dreamt landscape solidifying from a green, wet idea.

**Scale: 0.6/3 — small — grove-or-room scale with boundaries.** The space feels enclosed, a **single-feature area**. You are always within a defined chamber or grove, separated by sudden, steaming curtains of vapor or dense curtains of fibrous root. The **density: 1.8/3** is oppressive. The air is thick with spores and motile plant-seeds, brushing against skin. The vegetation isn't just layered; it is **near-touching, high-saturation**. You push through curtains of felt-like moss to find another chamber, each with its own micro-ecology.

**Fauna: 2.2/3 — abundant.** And here the **weirdness: 2.3/3** bleeds through. There are no animals as we know them. "Fauna" here are **mobile plant-structures**. Slow, grazing things like **walking lichens** — amorphous, multi-footed blobs that scrape mineral films from the dripping stone. Flocks of **spore-kites**—gelatinous, bell-shaped entities that drift on thermal updrafts from warm decay-piles, trailing_threads_of_ fertilizing silk. Predators are **snap-vines** that lash out from the walls with hydraulic speed, coiling around a grazing blob and injecting digestive enzymes before retracting to digest their meal in peace. All are extensions of the total biosphere, part of its **engineering current physics doesn't fully describe**. This **tech beyond modern** is not machines, but *biological process as infrastructure*. The hum you feel? That’s the data-stream of the global mycelial network, coordinating growth, nutrient cycling, and structural reinforcement across miles. This is a **nanoscale-or-cosmic** intelligence expressed in cellulose and spore.

**Built: 0.5/3 — none.** And truly, there are no artifacts. No ruins, no tools. But the **rules of the world** suggest a kind of **undeveloped, untouched** perfection where "construction" and "growth" are the same verb. The distinction between natural and artificial is meaningless. A bridge of woven root is as natural as a stalactite.

**Atmosphere:** The air is cool, wet, and **plant-thick**. It tastes of loam, ozone, and chlorophyll. The constant **hum** vibrates in your chest. Smells of sweet decay, mineral wetness, and a sharp, clean, almost mentholated note from the ubiquitous spore-clouds. Your skin feels perpetually damp, and a fine, green dust settles on every surface—the ambient pollen of a world that is all pollen, all seed, all potential.

You are not in a forest. You are inside a **single, planetary organism in its infancy**, a biosphere so complete it has rewritten the laws of geology and engineering into its own biology. It is **cold, wet, dense, and unbearably alive**, a place that is not just *unlike* Earth, but a fundamental rejection of Earth’s separation between rock, water, and life. Here, they are one, and they are still dreaming themselves into full solidity.
