# The Quenched Basin

## Terrain & Materials

You stand on a plain of **black volcanic glass**. It is not a field of scattered boulders, but a single, continuous, fractured sheet—a **obsidian skin** stretched over a high basin in a mountain range that has long since gone dormant. The glass is **porous, frothy, sponge-like**: a **reticulite** of glass bubbles and sharper shards, all fused into one heterogeneous mass. The elevation is extreme; the air is thin and cool, sharp in the lungs.

Packed density (2.8) manifests not as clutter, but as **saturation of form**. There is no bare ground between features because there are no discrete features—the entire landscape is **one texture, one material, one state**. The glass is **honeycombed and cavernous at the microscopic scale**, yet **solid and unbroken at the human scale**. Every cubic inch is either glass, or **sub-glass**: micro-cracks, vesicles, mineral inclusions, or the **liquid ghosts** of water.

The water (1.7 wet) is **mineral-bound and atmospheric**. It does not flow in rivers or pool in lakes. Instead:
- The glass is **hygroscopic**. It **breathes** moisture from the cool, bright (2.9 light) air, especially under the relentless sun. Condensation **beads within the frothy pores** and **weeps** from the undersides of overhangs.
- At the same time, **subsurface aquifers** of **meteoric water**—ancient precipitation trapped during colder epochs—**seep** along hairline fractures, fed by **condensation cycles** alone. You see **strings of pearls**: beads of water **trapped in glass capillaries**, trembling with every acoustic vibration.
- The wetness is **everywhere and nowhere**—a **uniform dampness** in the substrate, a **percolating mist** at eye level in low-lying sections, **glittering frost** of condensation on cold glass faces at dawn (though the dynamic is frozen (0.3), the daily thermal *input* is constant, so the *pattern* of condensation is invariant).

The erosion (1.5 established) is **perfectly balanced**. The glass is **already as eroded as it will ever be**. The **micro-forms are in equilibrium** with the **water cycle and thermal regime**:
- **Pitted and etched** surfaces from acidic condensation (trace volcanic gases still vent, though the tech/built is none).
- **Caverns and tunnels** from **subsurface dissolution** where trace minerals (salts, sulfates) are leached from the glass by the slow, mineral-rich seep.
- **Sharp, glassy ridges** that **resist further breakdown** because the most soluble components have already been removed.
The scale (1.9 open) is **basin-wide**. You see **forschammerite basins**—vast, shallow depressions where the glass is **most vesicular**, **mist-hazed**. **Glass spires**—vent pipes and frozen bubbles—rise at irregular intervals, some **hundreds of feet tall**, casting sharp, still shadows.

## Atmosphere, Sounds, & Light

Deafening sound (2.8) is the **dominant presence**. It is not *made* by fauna; it is the **sound of the water itself**, **amplified by the packed density**.
- **Constant drizzle of condensation**: billions of droplets forming and falling through the glass matrix per minute, creating a **roaring, static hiss** like a waterfall heard through a wall.
- **Vibrations from thermal stress**: the bright sun heats the dark glass unevenly; **micro-fractures ping and squeal** as the material expands and contracts in its daily rhythm (though the *state* is never-changing, the *energy input* is cyclic, so the *pattern of sound* is invariant).
- **Wind through glass pipes**: the few true caves and tunnels act as **organ pipes**, emitting **low, droning tones** that **hum** through the ground.
- This is a **saturated acoustic field**—sound comes from **every direction at once**, **no silence between presences**. Speech is impossible; you *feel* sound in your bones.

Bright light (2.9) is **harsh, direct, and reflected**. The dark glass **absorbs heat** but **scatters light** from its frothy, pitted surfaces. The basin **shimmers** with **complex glitter**—a million tiny facets catching the sun. At the same time, **deep glass wells and caves** are **pitch black**, their entrances **violet with condensed mist**. The light does not *change* (dynamic 0.3)—the angle of illumination is fixed by the mountain heights and the basin’s orientation—so the **pattern of light and shadow is eternal**.

Cool temperature (1.2) is maintained by **altitude and albedo paradox**: the dark surface absorbs solar energy, but the **thin air radiates heat away rapidly**, and the **internal seepage is cool** (ancient, condensed water). The air is **chilly and dry** except in the mist-hazed basins, where it is **clammy**.

## Ecology & Weirdness

Vegetation: 0.0 — truly **none**. No lichen, no cyanobacteria mats. The glass is **sterile**. The water is **too mineralized, too acidic, too silica-saturated** for any known biology. This is a **pre-life or post-life world**—a chemistry that **never crossed the threshold**.

Fauna: 1.2 rare — not animals, but **mineral energy patterns**. The **weirdness (0.8)** resides here.
- **Silica siphons**: in the deepest, wettest fissures, **silica-colloid vortices** may **form transient, branching filaments** that **pulse with faint chemiluminescence** (from redox reactions in trapped iron sulfides). They **grow** a fraction of an inch, then **dissolve**, repeating in an **eternal cycle**. They are not alive, but they are **self-organizing**.
- **Acoustic barnacles**: on surfaces where the sound vibration is most constant, **crystalline growths**—salt or sulfate—**form resonant shapes** that **feed on acoustic energy**, **vibrating in sympathy** with the drone. They are **mineral organs**.
- **Atmospheric plankton**: in the mist, **aerosolized colloids** of **clay and metal oxides** may **form temporary, rotating lenses** that **focus light** into **moving stars** for a few seconds before dispersing. They are **weather**, barely.

The **rules of this world** are **mineral cycles**, not biological:
1.  **Water moves only by capillarity and vapor phase**—no gravity-driven flow.
2.  **Change occurs only at the molecular/crystalline scale**—macro-forms are frozen in equilibrium.
3.  **Energy enters as light and sound**, is **transduced into heat and vibration**, and is **dissipated as acoustic hiss**—a closed loop.
4.  **Life, as we know it, is an impossibility**—the system is **too saturated, too violent in its micro-chemistry, too devoid of organic carbon**.

The **deafening hiss** is its **blood moving**. The **bright, unchanging light** is its **skin exposed**. There is **no room between presences** because **every pore is active**, every surface **vibrating**, every crack **wet**.
