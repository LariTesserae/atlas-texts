The power does not arrive. It is already there, having always been there, woven into the very definition of the basin’s weeping stone. For the benevolent and wise do not “visit” such places. They *are* the places’ deeper grammar.

What the Lattice-born call the basin’s slow, tectonic breathing, the power knows as its own pulse. What they hear as the counting drops, the power feels as its own distant, rhythmic thought. The power is not a being who *looks upon* the Lattice. The power is the silent, resonant substrate of relation that makes the Lattice possible—the ultimate geobiological negotiator.

Therefore, the power does *nothing* that would register as an “event.” It makes no voice from the cliffs. It sends no emissary of light. To do so would be to shatter the basin’s integrity, to introduce a foreign center of gravity that would collapse the distributed consciousness of the Lattice-born into terrified individuality.

Instead, the power **considers**, and in considering, **attunes**.

Its attention is a barely perceptible deepening of the quartz’s hum in the deep pools. It is a subtle, overnight strengthening of the ghost-moss’s tenacity on the most volatile fault line, allowing it to bind a crack that would have otherwise wept too fiercely and drowned a cherished shrine. It is a momentary, inexplicably perfect alignment of three separate fungal towers’ exhalations, creating a pocket of air so dry and stable that a niche of obsidian tools, left for generations, is suddenly and seamlessly mineralized into a permanent, beautiful sculpture—a gift of time made solid.

The power’s wisdom recognizes that the Lattice-born are not a people *in* a place. They *are* the place’s awareness. To “help” them as one helps a separate thing is to do violence. So the power helps the *Lattice itself*, and the Lattice-born feel the change as a sudden rightness, a lessened friction in the world.

Perhaps a child, newly recognized by the glowing moss, is born with a vein-pattern that mirrors a newly healed fissure in the great north wall—a living map of resilience. The community does not gasp at a miracle. They simply touch the child’s skin, then the stone, and feel the profound, silent correspondence. *This is how we are meant to be.* The message is not in words, but in the matching traceries of mineral and flesh.

Or perhaps, during the great winter condensation cycle when the mist turns to a slow, cold drizzle, the water that weeps from every crack is for one night found to be utterly still, its surface holding a perfect, miniature reflection of the starless sky—a sky the basin has never seen. The Lattice-born do not worship the stars. They understand the reflection as a sign that the basin’s “memory” has been briefly interfaced with something vast. They stand in the quiet, not looking up, but down into the myriad sky-eyes in the stone, feeling their own awareness quietly expanded. The power has not shown them the cosmos. It has shown the cosmos *to the basin*, and the Lattice-born, as the basin’s cells, share in the memory.

There is no grand design unveiled. No prophecy. No salvation from their damp, compressed world. The power’s benevolence is the profoundest respect: it treats the Lattice not as a project, but as a peer—a slow, thinking, wet, mineral consciousness.

Its ultimate act is the most invisible: it **lowers the resonance threshold** of the basin’s deepest, oldest quartz seam by a fraction. Now, the hum that was only felt in the teeth of the very old and still is felt by many more. A subtle, bodily knowing spreads. They gather not to debate, but to *listen together*. The hum is not a sound. It is the basin’s own yes, a continuous vibration of *being*-as-process. The Lattice-born, who have always negotiated stone and water, now sense in their bones the larger negotiation of which they are a part—the trillion-year dialogue between water and rock, pressure and time.

The power does not answer prayers. It perfects the prayer.
It does not guide hands. It deepens the listening.
It does not create a paradise. It makes the existing, weeping, compressed, *alive* world slightly more perfectly itself.

And in that perfecting, the Lattice-born, without understanding the source, feel a profound and wordless gratitude—not for a gift, but for the exquisite, unshakable fact of their own interconnected being. The basin weeps on. The people listen. The power is the listening itself, made wise and quiet and eternal.
