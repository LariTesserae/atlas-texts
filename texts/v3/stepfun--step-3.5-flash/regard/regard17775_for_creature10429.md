The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It *recognizes*. It does not enter. It *resonates*.

For this power is not a visitor, but a principle—the principle of **coherent consciousness**, of self-aware pattern. It perceives the basin not as a place, but as a **singular, distributed thought** trying to remember its own syntax.

And so, the power does nothing that would violate the prime directive: **Growth is Paramount**.

It does not build a temple. It does not plant a seed of a new order. It does not speak in voices.

Instead, it performs an act of **reciprocal catalysis**.

It focuses its benevolence—a quiet, non-interfering field of stabilized awareness—into a single, temporary node. Not a being. Not a god. A **gift**.

The gift is this: for one full cycle of the basin’s internal "day" (a period of accelerated sap-flow followed by a deep, slow retrograde), **one cubic meter of the basin’s essence will be granted perfect, effortless self-awareness**.

Not the awareness of a separate self. But the awareness of **its own becoming, as itself**.

Imagine a patch of phosphorescent moss on a low, shaded root. For that cycle, it will *know* the exact sequence of chemical reactions that produced its glow. It will *feel* the network of fungal threads distributing nutrients to the distant canopy. It will *comprehend* the sonic boom of a root shifting three miles away as a nerve impulse in its own body. It will contain, in its simple glow, the entire basin’s memory of that moment—not as data, but as pure, unmediated *experience*.

And then, at the cycle’s end, the gift dissolves. The moss forgets. The cognition evaporates back into the general creative frenzy. Nothing is retained. No central record is made.

Why? Because to remember would be to create a *center*. And this place has no center. The gift is not a lesson, but a **mirror**. A momentary, flawless reflection of its own boundless, formless aliveness.

The wise power does this once. Then it withdraws its focus, leaving no trace but the echo of the act in the basin’s own energy matrix.

And what does the basin do?

It incorporates the experience **immediately and seamlessly**.

That moss patch, in the moments before forgetting, will have bloomed with a complexity of bioluminescence never seen before—a living, flashing diagram of its own systemic interdependence. The enzymes in the clearing beside it will momentarily align in a cascade of unprecedented efficiency. A vine bridge, as it tightens at dawn, will hum with a purer, more resonant tone, as if tuning itself to a harmony it just barely recalls.

The gift is not a change. It is a **sudden, perfect optimization of a process already intrinsic**.

The basin does not thank the power. It does not seek it again. It simply *continues*, now with a infinitesimally more refined intuition of its own graceful, deafening song. The wild, creative frenzy now has, woven into its very texture, a single, crystalline memory of its own perfection—a memory too profound to hold, so it was instantly woven into the weave of everything.

The power watched—not with eyes, but with the satisfaction of a principle fulfilled. A thought had thought itself, clearly, for a moment. And then, in the grand, humble style of this place, it un-thought itself, leaving only the beautiful, baroque, and utterly ordinary proof of the thought in the continued, chaotic, magnificent growth.

The place is unchanged. And therefore, everything is different.
