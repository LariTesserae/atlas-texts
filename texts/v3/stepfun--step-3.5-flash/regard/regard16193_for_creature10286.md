The attention of the Benevolent and Wise Power does not fall like a shadow. It arrives like a change in the quality of the damp.

For a moment—a span measured not in seconds but in three pulses of the Veil-Moss—the deep, sub-audible *thrum* of the Hive-Chorta’s sap-song has a new harmonic. It is faint, almost lost in the *drip… drip…* and the *pat-pat* of the Swarm. But the Deep-Tappers, their palms pressed to the oldest heartwood, feel it. It is not a vibration from the roots, nor from the mineral tears of the Weepers. It is from *beyond* the world-stone, a resonance that seems to come from the very idea of stone, of air, of separation. It is gentle, curious, and carries no heat, no command. It is *attention* made manifest as a frequency.

The Hive-Chorta, in its millennia-spanning consciousness, does not startle. It *listens*. Its whole being—the network of feeder-roots tasting the compost of its inhabitants, the gas bladders adjusting to the collective breath, the slow expansion of its bark-resin into new hollows—registers the new signal as it would a shift in the mineral content of a distant seep: a datum to be integrated.

But this datum is different. It is *aware*. It is *wise*.

**What the Benevolent Power Does:**

It does not speak. It does not heal. It does not correct.

It **gifts a pattern**.

The gift is not an object. It is a *shape of perception*, a seed of relationship poured into the root-network like water into thirsty mycelium. The Power, in its boundless wisdom, understands that the Citadel’s perfection is its total, beautiful closure. To “save” it, to “improve” it, would be a violence. So it does the only benevolent thing: it **expands the Citadel’s sense of self, without changing its substance**.

The pattern arrives in the deepest tap-root, in the oldest chamber where the Reclamation Hollows sigh with slow consumption. It is a simple, elegant geometry—a lattice of connections that mirrors the Citadel’s own branch-and-hollow structure, but scaled to the impossible vastness of the cavern-world itself, and then further, to the layered, breathing rock above, and further still, to the silent, star-dusted void beyond the stone.

The pattern is a **cosmic root-map**. It shows the Hive-Chorta that its “world-stone” is not a boundary, but a *membrane*. That the deep geothermal sigh that powers the Veil-Moss’s pulse is kin to the slow, cold breath of the mountain above. That the omnipresent damp is part of a cycle that extends fathoms upward into clouds and downward into blind, mineral seas.

**How the Citadel Receives It:**

The Great Hive-Chorta shudders once, a tremor felt as a fresh resin-seam cracking open high in a new-growth branch, and as a sigh from the oldest, moss-veiled Weeper. The Deep-Tappers fall silent, their tap-songs ceasing as their minds overflow with a *knowing* that is not their own.

The gift is woven into the tap-language overnight. The next day, the Skitter-Swarms move in new, spiraling patterns on the floors—not for cleaning, but in mimicry of the gifted lattice. The Clatter-Ferns, stirred by a permanently altered air-current, clack in a sequence that repeats the pattern. The Glint-Bats, navigating by the new “song” in the stone, begin to favor roosting spots that align with the cosmic geometry, their echolocation clicks forming the nodes of a living, flying diagram.

The inhabitants do not understand “stars” or “void.” They feel it as a **new layer of belonging**. The chill in their bones is now recognized as the same chill that lives in the high, external rock. The *drip* from the Weepers is understood as part of a greater, upward-and-downward rhythm. The overwhelming, close-pressed life of the Swarm and the fungus is not a prison, but the warm, beating heart of a thing that now *feels* its own vast, stone skin and the cold, clear blood of the mountain that holds it.

The Lurker-Weavers spin their webs in the shapes of the gifted lattice. The Sleep-Moss emits spores in cloud-constellations.

The Benevolent Power’s gift has done the only thing it could: it has made the Gloom-Citadel **more itself**. It has not added outside light, but has taught the Veil-Moss that its pulse is a note in a universe of pulses. It has not given them wings, but has shown the root-bound that their tap-songs can now, in their collective dream, *feel* the echo of the mountain’s own slow turning.

**The Final, Quietweave:**

The Citadel settles back into its damp, thrumming stillness. But it is different. The “overwhelming” fauna now has a new, subtle rhythm beneath its industry—a faint, uplifting cadence in the Swarm’s wave, a deeper resonance in the drip, a sense of scale that makes the woven resin balconies feel not like a cave, but like the inner surface of a breathing, knowing lung.

The inhabitants feel a new, profound gratitude that has no object. Their tap-songs to their neighbors now carry an implicit, unspoken harmony: *We are not just the listening flesh. We are the listening flesh of a thing that knows its own name, and that name reaches beyond the stone.*

They do not worship the Power. They have simply **incorporated its attention** as one more layer in their total symbiosis. The gift was not a thing to be kept, but a **truth to be lived**—and so they live it, in every pat of a Swarm, in every clack of a Fern, in every slow, communal breath in the cathedral-trunk.

The Power, in its wisdom, sees this integration and is satisfied. Its attention gently recedes, like a tide that has successfully seeded a remote shore. The new harmonic fades from the sap-song, not because it is gone, but because it has become **ordinary**. It is now just the way the Citadel hums, a faint, cold, star-born counterpoint to the warm, wet, deeply *rooted* thrum of the Hive-Chorta’s own heart.

The Gloom-Citadel remains hidden, urban, sparse, and wet. It remains a pocket-world of wood and resin and moss and endless, quiet life.

But now, when the Veil-Moss pulses brightest, a faint, silver edge to the glow suggests not just bioluminescence, but a **reflection**—as if the very darkness overhead, the world-stone lid of their cavern, sometimes, just for a pulse, feels thin as a membrane, and on the other side, there is a vast, cold, starlit silence that is not empty, but *listening back*.

And they, the listening flesh, know this in their bones, and are not afraid. They are simply, finally, more at home.
