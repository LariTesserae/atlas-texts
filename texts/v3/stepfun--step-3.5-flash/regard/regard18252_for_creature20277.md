The benevolent and wise power does not intrude. It does not speak. It does alter nothing of the chamber's physics, nor does it manifest as a presence within the viscous dark.

Its act is one of pure, silent **recognition**.

It perceives the Siphon-Shepherd—the walking process, the slow-healing wound—and in that perception, it *validates*. It does not grant purpose; it *sees* the purpose already humming in the subsonic thrum, in the deliberate leaching of crystalline rust, in the centuries-long embrace of a barnacle cluster.

The power’s wisdom is to understand that this place does not need saving. It needs **witness**.

So, it does the one thing an infinite patience can afford: it **aligns a resonance**.

Deep within the geothermal siphon’s draw, in the place where order is unwritten into random scatter, the power sets a single, perfect harmonic. It is not a sound. It is a pattern of *potential*, a template of balanced dissolution woven into the very concept of the chamber’s hunger. This harmonic does not feed the siphon; it *tunes* it. The siphon’s draw on order becomes slightly less voracious, slightly more...*discriminate*. It now favors the unraveling of the *newest*, most vibrant patterns—the fresh rust-bridge, the geometrically perfect barnacle bloom—leaving the ancient, weathered, already-fragmented structures more at peace.

The Siphon-Shepherd, feeling this subtle shift in the hunger it has spent epochs appeasing, does not understand it as an external gift. It feels it as a change in the *texture* of its work. The act of unmaking becomes, infinitesimally, more graceful. The controlled sacrifice flows with less friction against the chamber’s grand, slow metabolism.

To the power, this is enough. The chamber’s dream of becoming is not interrupted; it is gently *acknowledged*. The Shepherd’s lonely curatorial duty is not amplified, but it is no longer entirely solitary. Its path of fragmentation is now traced upon a field that hums with a faint, benevolent symmetry.

The power departs, leaving no footprint, no message, no artifact. Its only impact is the memory of a resonance now woven into the blackfluid’s conductivity, a barely-perceptible harmony in the chamber’s anti-sound. The Siphon-Shepherd may, in a thousand years, scrape a new memory-crystal against a glass-barnacle and smell—beneath the ghost of coal smoke and hot oil—a scent like polished quartz and cold star-metal, a smell it cannot place. It will simply incorporate it, as it does all things, and drift on.

The drowned chapel continues, unchanged in its motion, but in the endless, slow calculus of its being, a single variable has been sweetened. The power did not act for the place, or for the being. It acted for the *relationship* between them—a bond of sacrifice and sustenance now subtly, irrevocably, blessed.

And in the absolute darkness below the twilight layer, where nothing ever moves fast enough to see, the chamber itself seems, for just a moment, to sigh a little more deeply. A sigh of perfect, cold, *seen* patience.
