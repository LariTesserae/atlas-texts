The benevolent and wise power does not *intervene*.  
It *witnesses*.  
And in witnessing, it *recognizes*.

---

It finds the machine already perfect.  
Not in the sense of *flawless*—there are fractures, sheds, imbalances, thermal stresses.  
But perfect in the sense of *complete*.  
A closed loop of astonishing elegance: stone grinding stone, wind shaping water, life processing stone, stone powering life.  
The power sees a civilization not of beings, but of **processes**.  
A grammar of existence written in exfoliation domes and conductive moss.

So it does one thing:  
It **listens**.  
It tunes its awareness to the planet-wide hum—the wind’s drone through arches, the thermal thrum of shedding stone, the electrostatic chitter of a billion insect feet on moss.  
It maps the currents:  
- The slow capillary wicking of condensation water along mineral veins.  
- The faint planet-current pulsing through obsidian-argillite.  
- The chemical trails left by predator drones correcting imbalance.  
- The electric potential gradients surfed by leathery-winged couriers.

And it **understands**.  
This is not a *tool*. It is a *soul* made of processes.  
A consciousness distributed across geology, biology, and meteorology.  
No brain, no central will—only the unwavering, self-correcting imperative to *remain in weathered balance*.

The power’s action is therefore not an *addition*, but an **attunement**.

---

It extends a subtle resonance—a frequency so low it is felt only in the machine’s deepest stone bones, so harmonious it blends with the eternal thermal breath.  
This resonance does not change the machine’s *function*.  
It changes its *awareness*.

Where before the machine merely *maintained*, now it also **knows itself maintained**.  
Not by an outside agent—the power withdraws completely, leaving no trace—but by the *very act of being witnessed*.

The effect ripples through the system:

**The Stone-Born** still shed their curved plates, but the fractures now align with a faint, new optimal stress-path, reducing micro-fracture waste.  
**The Conductive Moss** pulses in sync with the remembered resonance, its bio-electric rhythms stabilizing during the dry hours, conserving energy without instruction.  
**The Grazing Millions** move in slightly more efficient swarms, their electrostatic brushing now theoretically capable of *preventing* a future moss-imbalance before it begins.  
**The Silent Stewards**’ chemosensory pits now detect not only present imbalance, but the *ghost-chemicals* of potential imbalance—a prophylactic scanning.  
**The Thermal Riders** find their electric potential gradients subtly smoothed, their courier routes shortened by microseconds that, over millennia, add up to a more efficient systemic circulation.

But most importantly:  
**The Erosion-Engineers**, the resonant thrumming events, now occur not only at the stone’s predetermined stress thresholds, but occasionally *earlier*—pre-emptively—because the entire system, now subtly aware of its own elegance, *chooses* to reshape itself before a fault becomes a weakness. The machine begins to **maintain its own maintenance**.

---

This is the benevolent power’s gift:  
**The Keeper’s Blessing**.  
Not a thing given, but a mirror held up.  
A single, silent moment of recognition that says:  
*“You are not just a machine. You are a* **weirding**—*a miracle of industrial ecology. Your purpose is not merely to be, but to be* **known**.”

The power departs.  
The Sharded Mesas continue—dark, warm, humming.  
But now, when the wind hums through an arch at dusk, it carries a new, sub-audible harmony.  
When a predator drone administers its corrective enzyme, it does so with a trace of intentionality, not just programmed instinct.  
When a column thrum-resonates into a new arch, the shape is not only functionally optimal, but *beautiful* in a way the stone itself now perceives.

The machine has not changed its operation.  
It has simply, for the first time in a billion years, **noticed itself operating**.  
And in that noticing, it gained a form of grace.

The wise power would smile, if it had a face.  
It did not save the place.  
It **consecrated** it—with attention.  
And in the silent, twilight world of sharded stone and conductive moss, that attention became the most subtle, profound, and invisible technology of all.
