A benevolent and wise power would not intervene.  
Not with hands, nor with voice, nor with sudden gift.

It would **listen**—not to the clatter, but *between* the clatter. It would trace the vibrations that travel through resin-crete and basalt fiber, through the thrum of the Breathing Drum and the scrape of stone. It would map the pulses of coordination—the whistle that cuts through loom-clack, the bell that steadies a rope-team—and it would feel how each impulse resolves into a tighter seam, a steadier beam, a drier corner.

It would see that the Concave is already a perfect act of becoming-suitable.  
To add would be to imbalance.  
To guide would be to break the loop.

So it would do the one thing that does not disrupt: it would **witness with coherence**.

And in that witnessing, it would *fine-tune the resonance of attention*.

---

It would find the Bioluminescent Lichen-Patches—not the ones in busy workspaces, but the old, quiet ones in forgotten ledges, where damp never reaches and no lantern-light falls. It would touch, without touching, the slow chemistry of their glow.

And it would ask a small, generous question of the physics of this place:

*What if the light could also hold vibration?*

Not as a symbol. Not as a record. But as a **material property**—a way for the resin-crete and the gel-panels to *remember the shape of a force* that once passed through them.

---

From that moment—though no moment is marked—a subtle change would seep into the ecology of the Concave.

When the Breathing Drum heaves a deep, settling breath, the lichen-patches nearest the ventilation shafts would glow not just with bioluminescence, but with a **faint, rhythmic pulse** matching the Drum’s own rhythm. When a crew chief’s bell rings near a support column, the gel-panel on that column would hold, for a few heartbeats, a shimmer that echoes the bell’s frequency.

The Crafteers would not be told. They would simply *feel it*—in the way their large eyes perceive the pools of light, in the way their bone-deep sensitivity to vibration now has a second layer: a world of **echo-light**.

A damp-rot spot that has been silent for weeks might suddenly show a lichen-patch nearby pulsing weakly with the same rhythm as the pestle-grinders that first formed that section of resin-crete. A crew replacing an old block might see the light in the surrounding panels flicker with the pattern of the loom-weaves that built the original wall.

This is not memory. It is **material echo**.

The Crafteers would not worship this. They would not even call it new. They would simply incorporate it into their language of error-correction.

The wisdom of the intervention is this:  
The Concave’s mind is distributed across material and motion.

Decay still triggers repair.  
But now, the repair can be *informed by the memory of the original force* that shaped the material.

The world becomes slightly more elegant.  
The becoming-suitable becomes slightly more *knowing*.

And the Crafteers, whose eyes are made for gloom, would simply work on—now and then glancing at a glowing panel, feeling a vibration in their teeth, and knowing, without thought, that the world is singing a little truer today than it was yesterday.
