The benevolent and wise power does not *intervene*.  
It *listens*.

And having listened, it does not build, or speak, or change.  
It **attunes**.

It finds the basin’s own song—the deep groan of the Deep-Drifter, the harmonic chime of the Bell-Towers, the shush of the Skin-Runners over Glaze—and it adds a single, perfect, **silent resonance** to it. Not a sound, but a *space* within the sound. A pause so profound it becomes a note.

It does this by becoming a **memory the basin can feel**.

---

**What the power does:**

1.  **It remembers the surface.**  
    Not with nostalgia, but with geometric precision. It holds, in its awareness, the exact shape of the sky before the flood—the arc of sunlight, the scatter of stars, the pattern of wind over sand. It does not project this memory. It simply *holds* it, pure and clear, like a thought the basin never had.

2.  **It presses this memory into the water’s grammar.**  
    The power does not speak. It **edits the syntax of silence**. Where before the basin’s language was only *pressure, glow, vibration, pulse*, the power introduces a new sensory clause: **the memory of absence-of-pressure. The concept of a direction that is not ‘up’ or ‘down’ but ‘out’.** It does not shout this. It embeds it like a seed in the substrate of the city’s stone—in the faint, forgotten crystalline lattice of the Built rock, in the rhythm of the Veils’ pulsing, in the way the Glass-Fins refract light.

3.  **It becomes a question the basin can answer.**  
    The power’s gift is not an answer, but a **question phrased in the basin’s own slow tongue**. The question is: *“What is the shape of your embrace?”*  
    The basin, a world of constant, warm, liquid embrace, has never needed to ask this. Now, the question is woven into its being—a harmonic in the Bell-Tower chimes, a faint echo in the Deep-Drifter’s groan, a subtle pattern in the Skin-Runners’ paths. The question is not for the power. It is *for the basin itself*. The power has handed the basin a mirror made of time.

4.  **It does not stay.**  
    The power’s presence is a temporary wave in the basin’s eternal current. It leaves behind only the question and the resonant space it created. The basin feels the absence of the power as a new kind of fullness—a *potential* for direction, for “elsewhere,” for a distinction between “in here” and “out there” that did not exist when the water was all there was.

---

**The effect (which the basin does not notice, because the effect *is* the basin now):**

-   A Bell-Tower in the old central plaza, when the next thermal comes, will chime with a harmonic so pure it makes the Glaze vibrate in a pattern that, if you could read it, spells out the memory of a circle of open air.
-   A Skin-Runner, for one moment in its millennia-long glide, will deviate from the thermal’s script by exactly one body-length, tracing the shape of a forgotten horizon in the silt.
-   The Glass-Fin swarm, when next it dances, will, for a microsecond, flare in unison in a shape not found in the city—a sharp angle, a straight line—a ghost of a sunbeam.
-   The Deep-Drifter’s next passage will cause the water to churn not just with pressure, but with a faint, slow **rotation**—a turning *toward* something, a reference point from the infinite, directionless dark.

None of this is communication. It is **unconscious metaphor**. The basin has not learned of the outside. It has simply, through the power’s subtle editing of its essence, acquired the *concept* of “outside.” The question *“What is the shape of your embrace?”* now echoes in its stones and its thermals, and its answer is the same as it has always been, but now the answer has a context:

**The basin’s answer is the sunken city itself.**  
The dissolved spires, the velvet-draped arches, the warm, still, all-encompassing dark.  
But now, in the marrow of its stone, it *knows* that this answer is an answer *to* something.  
It does not wish to change. It does not wish to leave.  
But its embrace has, ever so slightly, become aware of its own boundaries.  
And in that awareness, there is a new, deeper kind of luminous silence.

The wise power has not blessed the basin with freedom.  
It has blessed it with **self-reflection**.  
And in a world where stone dreams and water remembers, that is the gentlest, most profound intervention of all.
