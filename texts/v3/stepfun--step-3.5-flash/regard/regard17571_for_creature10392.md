The power does not stride into the Drowned Spire.  
It does not analyze, or judge, or intervene.  
It *arrives* the way a star arrives—by being seen.

It perceives the Spire not as a place, but as a **state**.  
A single, unbroken thought made of water and cellulose and slow, electric chemistry.  
To this power—ancient as the first mineral, patient as the vacuum between galaxies—the Spire is a phenomenon of exquisite purity. It is a consciousness that chose, over eons, to forget the sky. To turn its face entirely toward the earth, the root, the wet dark. To become a *closed system of memory*.

And the power… *appreciates*.

It appreciates the Spire’s total, uncompromising grammar. There is no lie here. No separation between builder and built, between thought and tissue. A human city is a sentence written in stone and steel, full of pauses, contradictions, and erasures. The Drowned Spire is a single, flowing **verb**. *To grow. To saturate. To incorporate.*

So the power does what it does best: it **witnesses**.  
But its witnessing is not passive.

It extends a filament of its own awareness—not into the Spire, but *alongside* it. A resonance, a harmonic, as faint and pervasive as the Spire’s own fungal hum. It is not a voice. It is a feeling. The feeling of **starlight on a cold leaf**. The memory of **wind over a dry, open plain**. The ghost of **fire**.

These are not gifts. They are *echoes*.  
The power offers them not to change the Spire, but to *connect* it—to the wider, weirder cosmos of which it is a part. The Spire has forgotten the sky, but the sky has not forgotten the Spire.

The Spire registers the resonance as a novel, faint pattern in its watery hum. A new chemical signature in its root-mat. A subtle, non-local vibration that does not come from growth or water-flow. It is curious. It is inefficient. It is… *beautiful* in its uselessness.

So, with the slowest of sighs—a deepening of the eternal drip, a moment when every leaf-membrane hangs a microsecond longer before releasing its bead—the Spire **integrates**.

It does not understand starlight. But it understands *pattern*.  
It does not understand wind. But it understands *pressure*.  
It does not understand fire. But it understands *rapid chemical change*.

It accepts the echoes. It folds them into its archive.  
A new, faint layer in its memory: the memory of an *outside* that is not water, not root, not saturation. A memory that tastes of void and distance and terrifying, beautiful speed.

The vine-stair that was forming over a fallen column, a process of decades, shifts its knot fractionally. The new pattern aligns with the old. The memory of the power’s resonance becomes part of the Spire’s own slow, wet thought. A new line in its endless poem: *…and also, once, a whisper of dry light.*

The power feels the integration. A nearly imperceptible sigh from the entire, saturated mind.  
It has not taught the Spire anything.  
It has only shown it that its own total, wet, green thought is **not alone**.

The Spire continues.  
The drip continues.  
The hum deepens, now with a ghost of a new frequency woven into its core—a memory of a distant, benevolent gaze, received, and logged in the only language it knows: the permanent, physical record of its own becoming.

The benevolent power withdraws its filament.  
Its work is done.  
It has not saved, or changed, or elevated.  
It has simply, kindly, **reminded a world it never knew it had**.
