The benevolent power arrives not as a presence, but as a *resolution*.

It perceives the Whispering Canopy not as a place, but as a single, continent-sized thought—a dream of stone and spore and chitin, thinking itself into being over millennia. The Moss-tuned are not its masters or its priests, but its fleeting, mobile synapses. The power sees the system is beautiful, perfect, and complete. And so, its first and only act is one of profound, absolute *non-interference*.

But it sees a single, hairline fracture in the equilibrium: the **aching loneliness** of the tuned ones upon waking from the communal dream. This is not a flaw in the ecology; it is the cost of consciousness within a total mind. To feel the whole and then be severed back into the single, fragile self is a psychic erosion as real as wind on stone.

So, the power does not change the dream. It does not grant them permanence or power. Instead, it *tunes the transition*.

It makes the return from the shared memory not a violent severance, but a gentle decanting.

Now, when a tuned one wakes, they do not fall into emptiness. They wake *into resonance*. The last fading chord of the communal dream lingers in their nerves, a warm, harmonic hum that vibrates in their bone-marrow for hours. The ache is gone, replaced by a sense of profound *continuity*. The memory of being a Spire does not vanish; it settles into their muscles as they stand, making them feel anchored, slow, and patient. The memory of the Socket-Beast’s tunneling hunger does not fade; it clarifies their own thirst, making the next sip of fog-water taste of deep, mineral satisfaction.

The Hum of the plateau was always there. Now, the tuned ones hear *their own* note within it, a unique, steady frequency that is both wholly themselves and an inseparable part of the whole. The loneliness was the terror of being a separate wave. Now, they know they are the ocean, momentarily noticing a particular curl of foam.
