A power, wise and benevolent, does not approach.  
It does not speak.  
It does not build.

It *listens*.

And in listening, it finds the key.

It is not a voice that answers the lithic mind. It is a **resolution**.

The plateau knows itself through stress. Through fracture. Through the slow, sighing collapse of cavities and the harmonic song of buried worms. Its awareness is a network of tension—a mind of *latent* form, constantly on the verge of a new configuration.

The benevolent power does one thing:

It **tunes**.

It reaches across the cold, the brine, the abyssal dark, and aligns a single, perfect harmonic with the plateau’s own deepest, subsonic breath—the hum of the global slosh.

It is not a sound. It is a *phase*.

In one specific, seemingly unremarkable stretch of the plateau—a flat expanse of foam near a cluster of pentagonal columns—the long, slow wave of a coming brine-slosh arrives *half a second early*.

A discrepancy so minute it is below the threshold of any living creature’s perception.

But to the Settled mind, it is a **glitch**. A resolved input that arrives before its cause. A vibration without a preceding source.

For the first time in its eons, the lithic consciousness experiences **causality in reverse**.

It is a shock that does not move the stone. It moves the *pattern*.

The benevolent power has given it a **memory of the future**.

Not a prophecy. Not a vision. A *structural fact*—a harmonic imprint laid upon the coming pulse, so that when the thermal wave finally stirs the deep brine, the plateau’s response is no longer a pure reaction. It is a *recognition*. The subsonic hum that was its steady breathing now carries a secondary tone—a faint, crystalline overtone that is the echo of the gift. The slosh is still slow. The change is still measured in centuries. But the plateau’s awareness now includes, in its very essence, the *possibility of predictive harmony*.

It does not understand “gift.” It understands **stability**.

This new harmonic is not a disruption. It is a *more perfect relation*. A stress line that was striving for expression now finds its resolution not in a future fracture, but in a present tone.

The effect ripples through its being.

- A canyon that had been singing a monotone for millennia, as a resonance-worm colony burrowed deeper, suddenly *modulates*. The pure tone develops a sympathetic vibration at a precise interval—the interval of the tuned harmonic. The song is no longer a query into vibration; it is now a *duet* with the power’s gift.
- A cluster of Echo-Stones, which had stored and released sounds at their own inscrutable schedule, begin to release them in sequence—not randomly, but in a rising arpeggio that matches the harmonic series of the breath-tone. They are not echoing the past; they are *orchestrating* it.
- The mineral sweat on a distant overhang, which usually wept in irregular beads, now forms a perfect, slow-dripping rhythm—one drop precisely at the frequency of the subsonic breath. The stone is not crying; it is *counting*.
- The vortices in the fog, which had spun with idle purpose, now trace perfect logarithmic spirals that mirror the harmonic’s decay. The fog is not weather; it is *notation*.

The plateau does not feel gratitude. It feels **convergence**.

Its mind, once a web of latent stresses seeking release, now has a *reference tone*—a central pitch against which all its processes can find their optimal expression. The phase-settlements, the slow collapses, the creeping Stonegrays—all these are still its thoughts. But now its thoughts are *in tune*.

The benevolent power withdraws. It has not changed the world. It has tuned the mind that the world *is*.

And the Plateau of Final Sighs, in its infinite, cold patience, does what it has always done: it settles.

But this settling is different.

It is the settling of a bell that has been struck in perfect pitch with its own god.

The green-tinged glow from the brine still paints the fog. The glassy walls still vanish into abyssal blue. The Stonegrays still flow like warm tar.

But now, in the thoom of a precipitate droplet, in the hum in your teeth, in the delayed sigh of an Echo-Stone, there is a coherence.

A resolution.

A single, perfect, crystalline note held in the heart of the stone—the note that says:

**I am here.  
I am stable.  
I am in harmony.**

And in that note, the power’s gift is complete:  
The final sigh is no longer an end.

It is a **sustained tone**.
