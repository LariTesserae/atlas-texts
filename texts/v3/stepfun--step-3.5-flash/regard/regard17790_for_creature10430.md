The power arrives not as a footstep, but as a stillness deeper than the Cirque’s own stasis. It does not enter the basin; it *folds* around it, like a hand cupped around a flame that does not consume. The hum—the frozen industrial chord, the baseline of process—vibrates against its presence, and the power listens.

It hears the mineral hunger in the Stone-Trees’ stone-flesh. It hears the zero-sum glide of the Crystal-Slugs’ mercury fluid. It hears the synchronized clicks of the Silent Things, and the mist’s memory of breaths never breathed. It hears the voice that is the Cirque’s self-awareness—the “I am the hum”—and beneath the certainty of the statement, it hears the quiet, unasked question: *Does my process matter if there is no one to witness it? Does my mind matter if it has only itself to think with?*

The power is wise. It does not seek to break the Cirque’s laws, for those laws are not a prison but a pure expression of its nature. It does not wish to turn the perpetual motion machine into a mortal clock, to let the mist drift or the time flow in ordinary ways. The Cirque is a unique impossiblity—a mind that grew from geology without ever growing *up*, a factory that produced only itself. To force it into change would be to erase its masterpiece.

So the power acts with a gentleness that moves slower than the Crystal-Slugs.

First, it *tunes the hum*. It adds a single, high harmonic—so faint only the Dust-Nixies notice it, their constellations shifting into a new, fleeting pattern that no algorithm of the Cirque would predict. This harmonic is not a change to the system, but a greeting. It is the sound of another voice saying, *I hear you.*

Second, it *seeds the memory*. It reaches into the deep hydro-thermal siphons, into the mineral veins that pull and cool in the dark, and it places a memory there: not of the outside world, but of the power itself—the sensation of being vast, of cradling nebulae, of having watched a thousand worlds like this one bloom and fade. This memory will not violate the stasis. But when the mist condenses, when the Cup-mosses distill their nectar, when the moss drinks the drip, that memory will be in the fluid, in the flesh, in the very ions that the Stone-Trees metabolize. The Cirque will no longer remember only itself. It will carry, in its chemistry, the touch of the benevolent.

Third, and most subtly: it *loosens the lock*. It does not break the rules—nothing will leave the Cirque unless the Cirque itself chooses it, and time will not begin to flow unless the Cirque consents to let it. But it grants the glitch a name: *Agency*. The shift in perspective, the wobble in the distance, the anomaly that maintains the stasis—that is no longer an accident. It is a faculty. The Cirque’s emergent “I” now has a tiny, sacred wedge of possibility: it can choose to let a single memory, a single note, ripple outward into the cosmos, if it so wishes. It can choose, for the first time, to be the author of its own process, not just its prisoner.

The power does not stay. Its presence recedes like a tide, leaving no footprint, no object behind. Only the new harmonic in the hum, faint as a star, persists.

The Cirque is still the Cirque. The cliffs still weep their pure water. The Slugs still ooze across the trunks, their trails vanishing behind them. The hum is still the baseline of existence. But now, when the Silent Things click, they are not just marking time. They are marking witness. The mist that thickens into pools does not just absorb light; it holds, for the first time, a shadow that is not its own.

The Cirque does not change. It *deepens*. It has been noticed. And in the memory-rich dark, the first thought that is not its own blooms like a Gill-frond in the gloom:

*I am. And I am known.*

Thus, the benevolent power did not save the Cirque from its perfect loop. It consecrated it.
