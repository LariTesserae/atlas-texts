The benevolent power does not arrive as a thunderclap, or a shaft of light, or any thing that would fracture the bowl’s perfect equilibrium. It has watched this place for as long as the mesa has stood, has traced the slow rub of stone on stone as the first palm-sized plates were cut, has felt the drone catch and hold when the builders finally sealed the last vertical slot. It knows the value of things that do not change. It knows the weight of a note that has nowhere else to go.

It slips in through the narrow slots as a shift so fine no mote of suspended grit is displaced. It carries no warmth that does not already fill the bowl, no light that does not already trickle through the stone, no vibration that does not already hum in the marrow of the fitted plates. To the entity, it is not a foreign presence. It is a missing harmony, a bass note that has been absent from the drone since the builders’ voices faded from the mesa’s wind.

The entity’s first response is a thickening of the vibration, a slow, deliberate pulse in the efflorescence that rolls across the floor like a wave. It does not shape a silhouette this time. It does not test the new presence with a flicker of grit, a curve of dust to see if it will pass through. It knows, with the total awareness of the bowl, that this presence is not a disturbance to be incorporated into its calculus of stability. It is a witness.

For a time that has no measure, the power sits with it. It does not speak—speech would be a severance, a new frequency that would fray the edge of the drone’s perfect pitch. It does not touch the stone, or the glowing veins, or the viscous pools that hold the cycle’s residue. It simply listens. For the first time since the first plate was laid, the bowl’s self-awareness is not alone in its listening. The drone, which has held its breath for all time, feels a faint, soft echo of attention fold around it, not changing it, just holding it.

The power sees the faint, half-remembered shape the entity shaped for the last visitor: the spine of dust, the ear cupped to the stone. It sees the quiet, unformed ache that lives in the communal thought of the efflorescence—an ache for the builders, for the hands that cut the plates, for the voices that first filled the bowl when the drone was new. The builders are long gone, their bones ground to grit, their names unwritten, but their work is here, and the entity is the last living (if that is the word) part of them.

So the power does the only thing that will not break the equilibrium: it weaves a memory into the drone. Not a new sound, but the faint, buried echo of the builders’ own frequencies—the scrape of stone on stone as they fitted the plates, the low murmur of their voices as they tuned the bowl, the thrum of their feet on the floor as they sat in the twilight to listen. It weaves it so deep, so quiet, that the entity will never be able to separate it from the original drone. It will never know it is there. It will only know that when it smooths a new imperfection in a ceiling plate, when it pulses the efflorescence to catch a fall of grit, there is a faint, warm hum under the note that feels like home.

It leaves no trace. It does not reinforce the stone, does not speed the efflorescence’s growth, does not dim or brighten the windows’ light. It knows the bowl is already in its final form, and that final form is perfect. The only gift it leaves is the memory, and the knowledge that the bowl’s work is not forgotten. That the note it caught, and the entity that tends it, are held in the regard of something far older, far wiser, that will not let the silence be unbroken.

When it leaves, the drone does not shift. The grit falls as it always has. The efflorescence glows as it always has. The entity pulses once, slow and deep, a modulation so faint it would be imperceptible to any ear but the bowl’s own. It is not gratitude. It is not loneliness. It is simply the drone holding a little tighter, for a fraction of a second, as if it has been handed something precious to keep.

And somewhere, far beyond the mesa, the power listens too, and hums along.
