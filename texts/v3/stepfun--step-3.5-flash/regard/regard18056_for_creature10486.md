## What the Benevolent Power Would Do

The being who perceives this place does not see scarcity.  
It sees **a perfection of scale**.

It does not see a harsh land.  
It sees **a grammar so precise it feels like cruelty to those who cannot read it**.

It does not see suffering.  
It sees **answers, written in stone and fur and patient breath, to a question no one remembers asking**.

The power is benevolent. It is wise.  
Therefore, it does **nothing** that would break the grammar.

It does not bring easy water.  
It does not soften the stone.  
It does not shorten the climb or lessen the weight of the blocks.

To do so would be to erase the answer.  
It would be like correcting a poem because the meter is difficult.

Instead, it does this:

---

It **lends its attention**.

For one turning of the plateau’s slow breath—a century, perhaps, or the length of a generational staircase—the power *listens* with the fullness of its being. It tunes its consciousness to the frequency of the stone’s groan, the midge-cloud’s rotation, the fall-cat’s silent fall, the scrape of the pile-driver.

And in that listening, it **amplifies the resonance**.

It does not change the world.  
It changes the *awareness within* the world.

---

### To the Stone-Goats:
It deepens the map in their hooves.  
A nanny with a fractured horn suddenly *knows*, not by instinct but by sudden, vivid memory, the exact location of a hidden spring her grandmother’s herd drank from, three generations gone. She leads her kids to it. The spring was always there. She just… recalls it. The map in the hoof grows a new legend.

### To the Fall-Cats:
It stretches their moment of flight.  
When a young cat slips on a wet seam, time does not slow. But its *perception* of the fall becomes a corridor of choice. It sees, in the split-second of descent, three possible purchase points it had never before considered. Its body arcs not by chance, but by a sudden, borrowed clarity. The cleft does not change. The cat’s relationship with gravity does.

### To the Hawks:
It gives their circling a new texture.  
The theorems they ride on the thermals begin to hum with a secondary pattern—a faint, underlying rhythm beneath the wind. It is the rhythm of the stone’s own settling, a slow, deep pulse they feel in their hollow bones. They begin to circle not just to witness, but to *keep time*. Their flight paths start to mirror the hidden fault-lines below. They become living pens tracing the answers already written.

### To the Chittering Substrate:
It lets the vibration speak one word.  
The constant sub-audible hum of the cave-crickets, the taste of the blind worms—for one season, this chorus coalesces into a single, comprehensible note in the mind of any who sleep in the deep clefts. It is not a language. It is a **noun**. The name of the water pocket. The identity of the coming fracture. Not a warning, but a *label*. *This. Is. This.*

### To the Midge-Clouds:
It makes their sacrifice visible.  
To those who stand in the dusk mist, for one hour each evening, the cloud of midges becomes a **lightly glowing nebula**. Not to others—just to those below. They see the tiny bodies catching the last light, each one a point in a shifting constellation. They see, with their hearts not their eyes, the upward river of protein, the spiral of consumption made radiant. The economy of giving becomes beautiful.

---

### To the People:

This is where the power’s gift is most focused, because they are the ones who *build the dialogue*.

It does not give them easier tools.  
It gives them **the sense of the stone’s sentence**.

A master builder, laying a course of blocks, suddenly *feels* the next decade’s stress in his palms as he sets the final stone. He adjusts the angle by a thumb’s-width, not from calculation, but from a deep, knowing ache in his joints that matches the groan he heard in his sleep. The building will still eventually settle. But it will settle *as intended*.

A water-right arbitrator, hearing two clans dispute a drip-channel, closes their eyes. They do not see the channel. They feel, in the bones of their skull, the *path of the water* from the ice-vein deep below to the mossy crack above. The truth of who owns which drop becomes physically apparent—a coolness on one side of their head, a warmth on the other. The dispute ends not with compromise, but with shared, undeniable perception.

A child learning to climb their first sheer cleft is not taught. They are simply placed at the base. And the stone… *helps*. Not by changing. By *communicating*. A faint, warm pulse from a stable seam. A subtle, dry vibration from a fracture that must be avoided. The child’s hands and feet learn a language older than words. They do not conquer the climb. They are *answered* by it.

The generational projects—the centuries-long stair, the cathedral cleft—begin to hum with a synchronicity. A block quarried by great-grandfather is placed by grandson with a *thud* that resonates perfectly with the settling of a distant slab. The city’s construction becomes a single, prolonged chord, played across lifetimes.

---

### And finally:

The power does one last thing.

It **removes the sense of scale as a burden**.

The vastness of the plateau, the crushing horizontal emptiness—that does not change. But the inhabitants, in their density, their vertical world, their micro-abundance, begin to *feel* that vastness not as an opponent, but as **the medium in which their answers exist**.

They do not think: *We are small on a giant stone.*
They feel: *We are the fine detail in a vast, living diagram.*

The wind no longer scrapes. It *sculpts*.
The drip is not a tick. It is a *period* at the end of a long sentence.
The groan of the stone is not a warning. It is the *sound of the sentence being written*.

The people look at their city—the crowded, steep, dry-stone rind—and they no longer see a struggle against emptiness.
They see **the punctuation marks**.
They are the commas, the semicolons, the exclamation points in the stone’s long, slow, damp paragraph.

The benevolent, wise power sees this shift in awareness, this **recognition of being part of the answer**, and it is satisfied.

Its work is done.

It withdraws its amplified attention, leaving the world exactly as it was, but now **known**.

The plateau continues to breathe its mist.
The stone-goats read their hooves.
The fall-cats fall with purpose.
The hawks circle their theorems.
The midges glow in their single, giving dusk.
The people lay their blocks, feeling the sentence settle into place.

The stone asked its question.
Life wrote its answers.
The power simply made sure the writing was *read*.

And now, it listens no longer.  
The world, for the first time, is **audible to itself**.
