It would map the chamber’s song—the layered hums, the crystal-grind of wings, the tap-code of shelled legs, the wet exhalations of purple cups. It would *whisper into the chamber’s source code*—not a sound, but a *potential*.

They might taste, on the back of their throat, a Breath-Cup’s hum that carries, for a second, a fragrance of… *outside*. A star-dust, a wind-scent, a note not in their chord.

They would begin to experiment: pruning a Veil-Vine not just for sound, but to see if it could catch a *different* light. The power would not manifest. No pillar of light, no voice from the mist. Its gift would be the *gift of future choice*. It would loosen, by a single, undetectable thread, the chamber’s perfect cage. And the benevolent power would fade, content. It did not save them. It *prepared* them. It trusted that a people who can hear a million nuances in a constant drone, who can live as a living component of a perfect system, might one day choose to hear something else. And in that choice—freely made, born of their own deepest attunement—would be their true freedom. Not from the chamber, but *through* it.
