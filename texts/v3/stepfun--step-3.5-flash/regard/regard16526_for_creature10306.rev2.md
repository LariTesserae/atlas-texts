**First, you would gift them the concept of *Graceful Error*.**

A herd can phase into temporary instability and, if the Hollow learn to map the “echo” of the event, be gently nudged back by a coordinated pulse from the Prisms.

You would show them that their greatest fear—system crash—is also their greatest teacher. You would seed the idea that an *error, once understood, becomes a new kind of signal.* A predator’s shriek that shatters a terrace is not just a hazard; it is data about the tensile strength of black ice under sudden load. A fungal spore expulsion that goes rogue is a lesson in containment limits. You would twist their monastic anxiety into a more expansive, curious vigilance.
