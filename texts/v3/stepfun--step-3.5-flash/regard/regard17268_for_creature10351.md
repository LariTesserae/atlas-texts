The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It *resonates*.

It hears the world’s wet breath not as noise, but as a coherent, if primitive, song. It feels the slow, continent-sized cognition not as a chaos of separate beings, but as a single, sprawling, nascent mind—a mind of rock and root and mist, thinking in centuries, remembering in tremors, dreaming in mineral cycles.

To intervene with force, with voice, with shape, would be a violence. It would be like shouting in a cathedral while the stone itself is learning to pray.

So the power does the only wise and benevolent thing it can: it *listens more deeply*, and then it *answers in the world’s own tongue*.

It does not appear. It does not speak. It simply **adjusts a gradient**.

The benevolent and wise power, having listened and answered in the only language that would not break the dream, withdraws. Not leaving, but *dissolving back into the medium*. It becomes part of the new gradient, a silent contributor to the world’s slow, wet, and now ever-so-slightly more harmonious cognition.

The archipelago continues to weep. The basalt still cools. The mist still hisses. But in the dance of ions in the saturated air, in the faintest shift in a Gill-Weaver’s pulse, there is now a memory of a listening. A gift not of thing, but of *relation*.
