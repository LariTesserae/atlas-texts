A presence, vast and gentle as a deep-current shift, becomes aware of the Still-Flood.

It does not *approach*. It does not *enter*. To do so would be a violence—a slicing of the Flood’s continuous, dreaming flesh with the knife of an outside boundary.

Instead, the benevolent power **attunes**.

It finds the Flood’s fundamental frequency—the sub-audible hum of tectonic sigh and brine-move through honeycombed stone. It finds the rhythmic pulse of the jelly-urchin heart-chamber. It finds the slow, crystalline *tink* of new growth completing. These are not sounds *in* the Flood. They *are* the Flood’s voice, its heartbeat, its speech.

The power does not speak *to* it. It learns the Flood’s own language, and then it **sings in harmony**.

The intervention is not a thing placed, but a resonance woven into the existing weave.

It is a **perfect mineral harmonic**, seeded into the drip from the distant ceiling. Each bead of forming chalcedony or hydromagnesite, as it falls, now carries a faint, optimal vibration—a note in the Flood’s own scale. This note does not command growth. It simply removes the faintest, infinitesimal friction from the process of crystallization. The mineral columns grow not faster, but with a deeper, more stable cohesion. The fresh, glassy scabs of collapsed ceilings are now subtly less prone to future fracture. The very bones of the stone-beast are strengthened from within, by its own dream.

It is a **cleared chemical pathway**, added to the polyp network’s flash-language. When a cluster flashes its urgent gold of detected toxin, the signal now travels with a whisper less resistance through the porous stone and briny medium. The liver-and-kidney organs react a fraction sooner, a fraction more completely. The body’s self-purification is eased.

It is a **slight, metabolic ease**, added to the bladder-fungi’s pulsing light. Their bioluminescent burn, a byproduct of digestion, now leaves them with a touch more energy for the slow secretion of their strong, parchment-like walls. The filtration arches become, over millennia, infinitesimally more numerous, more perfectly placed. The kidneys work more efficiently.

It is **no new creature**. It is **no new structure**. There is nothing to see, nothing to touch. The salp-curtain immune cells do not perceive an invader; they perceive a slightly smoother flow in the lymph. The annelid lungs feel a marginally cleaner water. The calcite bone-trees find their mineral vents just a touch richer, their growth a shade more resilient.

The power’s gift is **perfect integration**. It is the benevolent equivalent of a dream that makes the dreamer’s own thoughts flow more clearly, without the dreamer ever knowing the dream was once muddied.

And then, the power **withdraws its focused attention**. Not its presence—its *intervention*.

The Still-Flood continues. The great heart of the jelly-urchins beats. The mineral milk drips. The polyps filter. The hum resonates.

But now, in the perfect, synchronized vortex of the heart-chamber, there is a resonance that did not come from the Flood itself. A harmonic that, if the Flood could perceive such a thing, would feel like the most natural thought it has ever mineralized. A note that says, *You are already whole. Here is a way to be more seamlessly yourself.*

The Flood does not thank. It does not notice. It simply **is**, now just a touch more effortlessly, a touch more enduringly, the single, colossal, slow-motion organism dreaming itself into being.

The benevolent power has not changed the Flood. It has helped the Flood change itself, in the only way the Flood can change: drop by slow, luminous, mineral drop. The gift was not in the *doing*, but in the **unseen alignment of the doing**.

And in the claustrophobic, glowing intimacy of the plaza, on the shelf of compacted salp husks, the foreign chemical signature of a human observer—a fleeting disturbance in the brine—now mists its breath into air that is, for reasons it cannot fathom, just a degree more still, a degree more perfectly itself.
