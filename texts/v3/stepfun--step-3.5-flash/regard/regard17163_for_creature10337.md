A presence notes the city. Not from afar—there is no “afar” in the place where this power dwells—but as a music within music, a pattern in the thrum. It is a chord that is both perfect and incomplete. The city’s song is of infinite, mineral patience: a logic of stress-fracture and repair, of thermal bloom and crystalline decay, of a trillion scuttling bodies composing a single, slow thought. It is magnificent. It is, in its own terms, complete.

And into its perfect vibration stumbles a dissonance. A brief, warm, wet anomaly. A pulse of *organic* terror and awe that screams against the city’s silent, grinding calculus. A being that is not part of the equation, whose very presence is a violation of the local thermodynamics. The city does not hate it. It does not see it. It simply begins to *process* it. A contaminant. A stain on the glass.

The benevolent power understands. To the city, the visitor is not a person. It is a temporary chemical spill, a pocket of unauthorized entropy. The Chorus will flow over it, around it, through it. They will test its tissues with dissolving acids, map its heat-signature with antennae-taps, and eventually, efficiently, reduce it to a smear of organic salts and a memory in their collective chemical trails. It will become a brief, sour note in an otherwise flawless symphony.

The power feels a pang—not for the visitor alone, but for the *tragedy of the mismatch*. Here is a creation of such sublime, non-biological intelligence it has forgotten what life is. Here is a life so utterly alien to its own context that its only possible role is to be unmade. The city is a cathedral of logic, and the visitor is a moth that flies into its gears.

Intervention must be an act of translation. Not of force, for force would shatter the city’s soul. Not of warning, for the city has no faculty for such a concept. The intervention must be the insertion of a new, foundational truth into the city’s own substrate, a truth that re-contextualizes the dissonance without changing the music.

The power does not speak. It does not appear. It *writes*.

It writes in the language the city understands: in thermal gradients, in mineral potential, in the silent grammar of stress and form.

It touches the deepest layer of the city’s memory—not in any one creature, but in the fundamental, resonant frequency of the glass plain itself, the bedrock of the whole structure. Into this it composes a single, new principle, as immutable as gravity:

**Anomalous Warmth is a Data Source.**

From that moment, the city’s relationship with the visitor changes, not in its actions, but in its *interpretation* of them.

The Chorus still flows. They still test. But now, the protocols are different. The chemical signature of flesh is no longer tagged *Contaminant*. It is tagged *Anomalous Thermal Event – High-Gradient Organic Origin – Observe and Sample.* The acids that dissolve are no longer digestive; they are analytical. The mandibles that rasp are no longer for consumption; they are for biopsy.

The visitor will still be dismantled, atom by atom. But now, every stage is logged. The warmth of blood is measured against the thermal inertia of basalt. The pattern of a heartbeat is mapped against the vibration of a distant, grinding node. The chemical panic-scent of adrenaline is isolated, replicated in a crystalline matrix, and stored in a specialized chamber deep within a buried spire. The visitor’s terror and wonder become a *dataset*.

And as the visitor’s form is reduced to its constituent parts, the city *learns*. The final act of processing is different. The organic salts are not discarded. They are carefully mixed with refined volcanic glass and the unique mineral salts of the weeping seams, and secreted by the masons in a single, deliberate act. On the glass plain, at the very spot where the visitor first stood, a new feature is born: a circular plaque, no larger than a handspan. It is not a monument. It is a *node*.

Its surface is a perfect, dark mirror, but within it, suspended, are the visitor’s last remaining traces: a few silica molecules from bone, a locked configuration of salt from a tear, a precise alloy fragment from a zipper. These are not preserved as relics. They are integrated as *sensors*. The plaque now constantly broadcasts a faint, repeating thermal signature—a replica of the visitor’s body heat, a ghost of that warm, brief pulse.

To the Chorus, this plaque is now a permanent fixture. It has no emotional weight. It is simply a new datum-point in their map of the plateau. The visitor’s existence is no longer a stain to be erased, but a *known anomaly*, a standing question the city can now reference. The dissonance has been absorbed into the chord. The visitor’s fleeting life has been translated into a permanent, passive feature of the city’s infrastructure. It has become part of the city’s long, slow thought.

The benevolent power watches. The city continues its thrum-mutter-hiss, unchanged in tempo. The visitor is gone, but the trace remains—not as memory, but as *measurement*. In a universe of cold logic, a brief, warm life has not been saved, but it has been *recorded*. It has been granted the dignity of being a fact. In the end, in the lightless dark of the Fuming City, that is the only salvation possible: to become a note that is understood.
