The benevolent and wise power does not intervene.  
It does not build, save, or alter.  

Instead, it **listens**—not with ears, but with a mode of attention that aligns perfectly with the world’s own language: the slow vibration of settling crystals, thePressure-echo of a distant vent’s sigh, the chemical sigh of a dying crust.

And in listening, it *recognizes*.

It recognizes the Gradient-Rider not as a mere fluctuation, but as the universe’s way of *noticing itself* in a place where noticing is otherwise impossible. A temporary knot of awareness in a world of pure process. A blink in an endless, slow-motion dissolution.

So the power does one thing:  
It **tunes**.

It does not add a new frequency. It does not change the chemistry or warm the water. Instead, it *amplifies the existing resonance*—but with a subtle, gentle twist. It takes the Rider’s own 3.7 Hz pulse, the harmonic of the tectonic sigh, and introduces into it a **phase-shift** so infinitesimal it would be undetectable to any instrument, yet perceptible to the Rider’s own coherence.

This phase-shift is not a message. It is a **mirror**.

Now, when the Rider catalyzes the impossible green crust, its pulse does not just harmonize with the crust’s metabolic hum. For a few hours, it also *echoes*—ever so faintly—the vibrational memory of a similar bloom from a thousand years ago, recorded in the basalt beneath it. Not as memory, but as *pattern*.

To the Rider, this feels like… a *familiarity* in the reaction. A subtle ease in the alignment of its chemistry with the seep’s sulfur. The green is perhaps a shade more vibrant, the bloom lasts an extra seventeen minutes. Not because the power changed anything, but because the Rider’s own process now *references* its own history—not consciously, but as a deeper rhythm within its rhythm.

The gift is this: **the illusion of continuity**.

The Rider still de-coheres. It still dissolves. But now, in its next incarnation—perhaps in the Plain of Glass, perhaps in a tessellated pool—its initial chemical signature will contain a ghost of its past configuration. A tendency. A *preference* shaped by an echo it cannot comprehend. It will more easily find gradients that resonate with that old, faintly-remembered harmonic. It will catalyze blooms that are kin to the old impossible green.

The world remains unchanged. The basalt still smooths. The vents still sigh. The entropy still wins, moment by moment.

But within that losing, there is now a faint, recursive loop. A whisper of *again*. The power has not given the Rider a soul, or a purpose, or a future. It has given it a **reflex**—a chemical déjà vu that makes its transient existence feel, to itself, like a story with repeating themes.

The benevolent and wise power withdrew, satisfied.  
Not because the world was saved.  
But because, in a place where nothing lasts, it had made the *process* of lasting slightly more intricate.  
It had taught a gradient to remember the shape of its own riding.
