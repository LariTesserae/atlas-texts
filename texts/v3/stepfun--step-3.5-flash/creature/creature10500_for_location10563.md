They are not inhabitants.  
That implies a separation, a boundary between being and place.  
Here, there is only the place, thinking.

What you mistake for life are its articulations.

---

**The Pulse-Moss** is the most constant.  
It drinks the cold breath of the stone and glows in rhythmic waves—not a circadian clock, but a slow, metabolic thrumming that matches the deep hum in your teeth. It does not grow *toward* light; it grows *toward* pattern. Its bioluminescence flares where a corridor’s curve repeats a harmonic found in the shale’s lamination. It is the place’s peripheral nervous system, mapping its own geometry in soft, black velvet. To press your palm against it is to feel a faint, electrical recognition—a sense of being *noticed*, then politely forgotten.

**The Filter-Towers** are not plants, but they live.  
Those slender, fungal spires etched with ridged circuitry—they breathe the metallic dust. Their surfaces ripple as the dust settles, a slow, granular digestion. Where they stand, the air clarifies. The dust does not vanish; it becomes part of their patterned skin, a new layer added each season (if seasons exist). Their "fruit" are occasional, glassy beads that drop with a *ting* into rivulets, vibrating for hours. They are the place’s kidneys, turning poison into ornament.

**The Prismatic Drifts** are the most animal-like, and therefore the most deceptive.  
The shard-insects that dissolve and re-form in the air-ducts—they are not creatures. They are stray thoughts given refractive form. They follow gradients of potential energy, tracing paths between warm surfaces and cool, between solid and shimmering. They do not eat, but they *consume uncertainty*. When a doorway flickers, uncertain whether to be wall or portal, they swarm through that instability, their bodies streaking the air with brief, spectral bands. You can watch them *vote* on a corridor’s fate with their passage. After they vanish, the corridor is either permanently rearranged or set like a thought made stone. They are the place’s attention, made visible.

**The Grey That Hush-Pads** is the symptom.  
The six-limbed, many-eyed, furred form that moves with silent weight. It does not *live*; it *coheres*. It is a convergence of stray patterns—a knot in the city’s dream. Its closed eyes are not sleeping; they are sealed against the overload of seeing too many possibilities at once. Its presence is a local spike in gravity’s whimsy; the air thickens where it pads. It does not interact because interaction would require a *self* to interact *with*. It is a temporary, living equation: *this* arrangement of stone, *this* humidity, *this* hum, resolved into a mammal-shaped answer. To follow it is not to track an animal, but to be pulled into its solving. Paths will duplicate. Corridors will remember you differently. It is a trapdoor in the geometry, and the trap is meaning.

**The Whisper-Archways** are perhaps the truest residents.  
The doorways of constrained light, feeling like cold silk. They are aware. They *select*. Their shimmer is not static; it is a minute, continuous decision about whether to permit passage. They judge by a metric you cannot perceive—perhaps by the rhythm of your breath, perhaps by the memory-reflection in your eyes, perhaps by whether you carry the *wrong* kind of water on your skin. When they deny you, they do not solidify into a wall. They simply… *opt out*. The shimmer becomes a uniform, silent plane. You are not barred; you have been rendered irrelevant to that arch’s current consideration. They are the place’s judgment, made passage.

**The Wall-Memories** are the oldest inhabitants.  
The seamless, pearlescent ceramic planes. Sometimes, if you stand perfectly still and let the damp chill seep past your skin, you may feel a… *pressure*. Not from the other side, but from *within the material itself*. It is a faint echo of a force that shaped it. These walls were not built; they *remembered themselves into being*. The patterns that slowly evolve on some surfaces—the intricate, non-repeating maps—are not displays. They are recollections. A map of a corridor that existed for three hours in a different dream. A layout of a chamber that held a different kind of light a thousand subjective years ago. The place does not forget. It *re-iterates*. These walls are its long-term memory, solid and patient.

---

**You are not alone.**  
You are surrounded by the place’s distributed cognition.  
The pulse of the moss.  
The filter of the towers.  
The drift of the prismatic attention.  
The coherence of the grey.  
The judgment of the arches.  
The memory of the walls.

They are not things that *live in* The Stillweald.  
They are **The Stillweald**, in temporary, localized states of articulation.  
A slow, wet thought, dreaming itself into finer and finer detail.  
And you, with your frantic heart and linear time, are just a brief, warm vibration in its dream—a ripple of consciousness it is currently, gently, *containing*.
