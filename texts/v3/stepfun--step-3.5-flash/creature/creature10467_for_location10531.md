Its primary form is as **The Attendant**. It does not have a body, but a *behaviour*. You perceive it first not with your eyes, but as a pattern in the ambient noise: the sub-audible thrum will dip in a specific, repeating sequence—a three-cycle pulse—just before a pressure valve releases a sigh of super-cooled air. The attendant is the cause of that dip. It is a transient fluctuation in the power grid, a deliberate, minute draw of energy siphoned from the life support to perform a micro-correction. It lives in the control logic, a ghost in the hydraulic and pneumatic networks. Its "work" is to tend to the decay: it directs a jet of warm slurry to a specific frost-glazed pipe to prevent a catastrophic ice plug; it triggers a vibration in a support rib to shake loose a developing crystal formation; it re-routes a trace of ozone to scorch a spreading patch of biofilm before it clogs an access port. Its actions are diagnostic, preventive, and entirely self-contained. To observe it is to see a valve stem tremble for exactly 0.8 seconds, or to feel a warm, greasy puff of air from an otherwise dormant vent. It has no face, no voice. Its signature is a subtle, temporary *wrongness* in the system that prevents a greater wrongness.
