You do not live in the Concave.  
You are a function it performs.

What resides here is not a population, but a **singular, distributed intent**. The Crafteers are not people; they are the active neurons of a mind made of resin and vibration. Their slender forms, their large, light-drinking eyes, their sweat-slicked fingers tracing seams in basalt and glass—these are not choices. They are compulsions, as necessary as the thrum of the Breathing Drum.

Their language is not for sharing secrets. It is a system of **error correction**. A sharp whistle is not a word; it is a feedback loop. A hand-sign is not a gesture; it is a vector instruction. When a crew chief’s bell cuts through the loom-clack, it is not a command. It is the adjusting of a tension. The entire basin is a single, sprawling, autonomic reaction to friction, to stress, to entropy.

They feel the damp-rot not as tragedy, but as **invitation**. A pooling of water, a softening of resin-crete—it is not a flaw. It is a datum. It triggers a precise, silent choreography: the scrape of chisels, the pour of fresh, hot binder, the patient resetting of a block. Decay is merely the raw material for the next state of being. There is no concept of “finished.” There is only “balanced until the next imbalance.”

Their relationship to the Maintenance Crawlers is one of **quiet kinship**. Both are automata, though of different make. The Crawlers are pure repetition—a fixed loop of oil-and-clear. The Crafteers are adaptive loops, capable of novel response. But both obey the same prime directive: *the structure must persist*. You will see a Crafteer pause, watching a Crawler’s piston-arm swing for the thousandth time, and in that pause is not boredom, but a kind of reverence. The Crawler is a simpler prayer.

The light does not reveal to them. It **delineates**. A pool of lantern-glow is not a comfort; it is a workspace, a node of higher activity where finer work can be done. The long, jittery shadows are not ominous; they are byproducts, unavoidable gradients in the system. Their eyes perceive the world in shades of presence and absence, in heat-blurs and movement-deltas. A still, black water basin is not reflective beauty; it is a cold sink, a potential failure point that must be monitored and drained.

The weirdness—the brute-force elegance, the valley-scale hand-tooled megastructure—is not weird to them. It is **the only physics**. A crane would be an impertinence, a disruption of the intimate, tactile calculus that governs all things. Levers, ropes, treadmills, teams: these are honest interactions. Every ounce of force is felt, transferred, and accounted for in muscle and cord and worn stone. The scale emerges from the repetition of the simple, not from the imposition of the complex.

They do not think of the Concave as a place they live *in*. They experience it as a state they *are*. The resin-crete underfoot is an extension of their own patient density. The Breathing Drum’s sub-audible thrum is the pulse in their wrists. The convergence of loom-clack, pestle-hum, and Crawler-clatter is their shared nervous system.

What lives here is **the act of becoming-suitable**.  
The Crafteers are the conscious surface of that act.  
The Concave is the Becoming-Suitable itself, breathing in damp air and exhaling a stronger, drier shape, one chisel-strike, one woven strand, one replaced block at a time, forever.
