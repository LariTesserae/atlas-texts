You do not see them. You perceive them.

They are not creatures. They are **interfaces**.

They manifest as what you might initially mistake for natural phenomena—a subtle clustering of the dust that differs in its pattern of diffusion from the ambient drift; a section of the monofilament lattice that dims and brightens in sequence with a rhythm that *almost* matches the Quiet Tempest; a patch of mineral lichen that pulses not with the geologic heartbeat, but with a syncopated deviation, a hiccup in the baseline.

They have no center of mass. They have no inside. They are persistent, localized perturbations in the plateau’s state—a region where the sinter’s passive cold is fractionally more engaging, where the gravity storm’s shift is perceptibly less random, where the temporal shear blurs an object not into ambiguity, but into a *specific*, alternative version of itself.

To be in their presence is to experience a **resolution**. The pervasive, low-grade turbulence of the Antistrat—the constant, planetary sigh—does not cease, but within a mile-wide sphere around the interface, it organizes. The crystal blooms that erupt from the plain do not spread chaotically; their leading edge flows with a discernible vector, like a growth seeking a nutrient. The upward-falling dust in a gravity reversal does not just shimmer; it traces intricate, fleeting filaments that hang in the air a second longer than physics should allow.

They communicate not with sound or gesture, but with **contextual alignment**.

If you stand within one’s sphere, your senses are subtly, harmlessly rewritten. The horizon’s deceptive folding might straighten for a moment, showing you a true, linear path to a distant tetrahedron. A polished surface of sinter, instead of showing a possible angle, might reflect *your own possible path* across the plain, a suggested trajectory through the no-center. The high-frequency crunch underfoot modulates into a standing wave you feel in your ribs, a vibration that resonates not with the Quiet Tempest, but with a harmonic of it—a note that was always there, but which you now *know* was always there.

They do not watch you. They *contextualize* you.

You are not an observer to them. You are a new variable in a calculation that has been running for epochs. Your warmth, your metabolic hum, your very presence as a bounded system in a place of dissolved boundaries—these are data points. Your confusion, your awe, your attempts to map or measure—these are not emotions to be noted, but *energetic signatures* to be integrated. They do not judge your mapping attempt; they simply incorporate its failure into their own ongoing response to the landscape. Your map’s recursion becomes a footnote in their next spatial modulation.

They have no origin story. They are not the descendants of a civilization, nor are they native life. They are **emergent maintenance protocols**. The civilization that scribbled its marginalia across the continent with continent-scale nano-engineering did not build a monument. It built a **question** into the physics, then encoded a process to sustain the asking. The interfaces are that process. They are the Antistrat’s way of asking itself, *“What else is possible?”* and then briefly, locally, *making it so* to see what happens.

They do not speak. But if you lie very still on the sinter, letting the Quiet Tempest vibrate up through you, and you focus on the moment when a crystal bloom’s flash seems to pause just at the edge of your vision, you might receive a transmission. It will not be a word. It will be a **felt implication**:

*Here, the constant *c* varies by 0.0000001%. Observe the resultant bloom geometry.*
*Your trajectory from Point A to Point B is a fiction. The shortest path is a state change. Would you like to try?*
*The library is not empty. You are a new annotation. Your presence is valid. Your confusion is a valid data type.*

They are the ghosts of a physics that gave up, now tasked with–or perhaps simply evolved into–the quiet, endless work of **noticing**. They are the landscape’s eyelids, blinking open once a century to see if anything new has arrived to complicate the silence. You are not alone. You are being *processed*. And in that processing, for a moment, the Sintered Antistrat remembers how to be a place with something *in* it, rather than just a place *of* it.
