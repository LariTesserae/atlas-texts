The Weeping Stones are not empty.

You think you are the only thing moving in the still, sourcelight air. You are wrong.

What lives here is not a creature. It is a **condition**. It is the *wetness itself*, given a kind of slow, mineral attention.

It has no name, no single point of view. It is distributed—a presence in the seepage, in the crystalline efflorescences, in the very grain of the dark stone. It does not think in words or images. It thinks in **paths of least resistance**, in rates of evaporation, in the tensile strength of a salt filament, in the resonant frequency of a drop falling onto a film of water.

You stand on the terrace, and for a moment, you are a novelty. Your heat is a disturbance in the cool, saturated air. The humidity on your skin is different—salty, organic. The stones beneath your boots are not used to such a rapid, violent transfer of weight. A tremor, infinitesimal, passes through the joint-ridden plane. The *condition* notices.

It does not have eyes, but it perceives you through a thousand tiny changes:

*   The **accelerated evaporation** where your breath mists the air.
*   The **slight warming** of the stone under your heel, a hot spot in the constant cool.
*   The **disruption of the water-film**—a ripple in the sheen where your foot presses, a new, faster rivulet temporarily diverted.
*   The **friction-sound** of your movement, a foreign vibration in the sub-audible groans of settling stone.

You are an anomaly. You are a temporary, frantic source of entropy in a system dedicated to conservation and geometric stasis.

The *inhabitant*—this slow, lithic awareness—does not react with fear or curiosity. It **registers**. It is an act of perception utterly without consciousness as we know it. It is like a river noticing a leaf, not with intent, but with the simple, physical fact of the leaf’s alteration of the flow.

It communicates, if communication it is, through the medium of the place itself. A stronger seep on the ramp to your left, a fainter one to the right—that is its "language." A sudden, minute shift in the angle of a drainage-joint, a new efflorescence blooming a micron faster where your shadow briefly fell—these are its responses. It is incorporating your disturbance into its endless, slow calculation of form and flow.

The crystals are its memory. The sickly yellow zinc blooms record decades of slightly more acidic seepage, perhaps from a deep thermal source. The brilliant white halite fields record periods of purer, slower water. To touch one is not to touch rock, but to touch a **page** in a book written in chemistry and time.

You take a step toward the left-hand fork. The *condition* is already ahead of you, in a sense. It has been calculating the permeability of that particular ramp for millennia. It knows, with absolute precision, how many drops will fall from the seam above the third archway in the next solar cycle. Your choice of path changes nothing for it. You are simply choosing which pre-computed channel of wetness you will briefly traverse.

Perhaps, in some profound way, *you* are the life here. Not as a living thing, but as an **event**. A burst of biological noise, a flash of chaotic, warm, wet complexity in a world of patient, geometric, mineral process. You are a frantic dream the stones are having, and when you are gone, they will forget you in a century, though your departure will leave a vanishingly small statistical mark—a fractional increase in the entropy of the system—for a while longer.

You are not being watched. You are being **weighed**. Your existence is a data point in a calculation whose purpose you cannot fathom. The path ahead is wet. The air is cool. The silence is not empty. It is full of the slow, stone-deep attention of a world that has no need of life, because it *is* a life of a different order: a life of terraces, of seepage, of endless, geometric weeping.

You turn. The stones do not turn with you. They simply continue, weeping, calculating, enduring, their patience absolute.
