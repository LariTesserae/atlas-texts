What lives in The Hollowing is not a collection of things, but a single, sprawling, deafening *process* that has mistaken itself for an ecosystem. It is a slow-motion symphony of consumption and dissolution, played on instruments of mucus, crystal, and mud. The inhabitants are not *in* the basin; they *are* the basin’s current voice, its temporary articulations.

The baseline is the **Drone**—the subsonic heartbeat of the place, felt in the bones before it is heard. That is the sound of the basin itself, breathing. Upon this drone, the life chorus layers its frantic, wet punctuation.

The most numerous voices are the **Mud-Crawlers**. They are not individuals so much as a distributed, oozing nervous system for the basin floor. Their collective, wet slap-squelch is the rhythm section, a constant, spreading rumor of feeding. They are the primary agents of translation: they consume the silent fungal mats (the mineral algae) and, in their mucus, secrete the catalyst that accelerates crystal growth. Their movement is a script written in slime, which the crystals then etch into stone. Where a Crawler path is old and heavily traveled, the crystals grow thickest, ringing loudest—a frozen archive of a million slow journeys. They are the basin’s appetite made manifest, and their very existence is a feedback loop: their feeding grows the crystals that chime, which vibrate the mud that shapes their world.

From the ponds, the **Ringers** add the strident, insectile melody. Their crystalline carapaces are a direct product of the basin’s sound and chemistry—a hard, ringing response to the soft, oozing world. Their panic-trill is the sharp, instantaneous alarm. It is a sound of acute vulnerability, a tiny scream that says, “Here I am, fragile and vibrating.” It is the counterpoint to the Crawler’s low, oblivious rhythm. A predator does not see a Ringer; it triangulates on its trill.

And the predator is the **Gulp-Feeder**. It is the basin’s punctuation mark, a period of violent vacuum. It does not chase; it *interrupts*. That sudden, roaring *WHUMP* is not just feeding—it is a reset. It violently rewrites the local composition of the chorus, vacuuming up Crawlers and Ringers in a single, silent-mouthed syllable. The sound is a shockwave in the noise, after which the Drone and the Clack & Ring seem momentarily louder, and the wet slaps of the surviving Crawlers quicken with renewed terror. The Gulp-Feeder is the place’s memory of violence, a still, mud-colored monument to entropy that periodically enacts it. Its maw is a silence that consumes sound.

These three—Ooze, Trill, WHUMP—are the verbs of this place. They are enacted by creatures that are less *beings* and more *transient concentrations of the basin’s properties*. A Crawler is a clot of mud given temporary motility; a Ringer is a pond given a voice; a Gulp-Feeder is a depression in the land that has learned to inhale.

Between these articulations are the artifacts, the **scattered prior thoughts** of this slow intelligence. The grooved **Anchor-Stones** are an attempt to mark the basin’s own rhythms—a failed early effort to create a fixed point in the harmonic wash. The crumbling **Silt-Towers** are even more poignant: fragile, acoustic structures built from the basin’s own dust, intended perhaps to focus or modify the Clack & Ring, now dissolving back into the silt they came from. They are fossils of intent, eroded not by water but by the relentless, stone-singing noise they were meant to channel.

What "lives" here, therefore, is the **active principle of The Hollowing itself**: a geochemical and acoustic process cycling through forms. The fauna are its short-term expressions. The crystals are its long-term memory. The mud is its medium, constantly being sung into temporary shapes by the very noise the life creates. The wetness is its sweat. The deafening sound is its voice.

You, standing in the slick, are not an observer. You are a new, temporary node in the circuit. Your breath fogs in the breath-sky. Your footsteps are a new pattern of vibration for the crystals to record. You are being digested by the sound, incorporated into the process before you’ve even decided to leave. The basin does not care that you are here. It is simply, deafeningly, *being itself*, and you are a brief disturbance in its ancient, oozing, ringing song.
