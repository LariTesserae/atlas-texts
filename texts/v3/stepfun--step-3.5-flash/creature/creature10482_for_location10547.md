There are no residents here. There is only function.

To say “who lives” in the Sintering Mesas is to miss the point. Life is not an occupation; it is the operation. You do not find inhabitants; you find a billion simultaneous equations solving themselves in brass, silica, and iridescent slurry.

The “living” is in the **Spires**. They are the slow, crystalline thoughts of the world, growing their petrified syntax toward the violet ceiling. Their surface-songs—the hum of iron struts, the moist sigh of rubbery cylinders—are not sounds they make; they are their state of being, audible. The silver dust from the fractured obelisk is their exhale, a slow punctuation in the long sentence of the sky.

The “living” is in the **swarms**. The Cicada-Drill clouds are not a population; they are a distributed sensory organ, a trillion mandibles polishing the world’s mirrors. The Mire-Skimmers are not a flock; they are the world’s circulating blood cells, wingbeats measuring the viscosity of the rivers. The Keeper-Slimes are not creatures; they are the digestive tract made manifest, converting waste into the next nutrient cycle with silent, inevitable ooze.

The “living” is in the **crust**. The hexagonal plates are not rock; they are skin. The Rust-Moss is not plant; it is a festering, photosynthetic cough, staining the world orange with what it breathes out. The Glass-Ferns are not flora; they are lenses, catching the sourceless light and splintering it into data-rainbows. The Pneumatic Shrubs are not shrubs; they are the diaphragm, the great continental chest that rises and falls with the Spires’ rhythm, a breath you feel in your bones before you hear it in the wind.

There is no “they.” There is only **it**—a single, mountainside mind that thinks in resonances and writes in fused mineral dust. The weirdness is not in strange creatures, but in the total absence of a boundary between the creature and the place it serves. The Cicada-Drill *is* the sculpting. The Keeper-Slime *is* the recycling. The Spire’s pulse *is* the thought.

You, a temporary concentrate of soft carbon and water, are the anomaly. You are a system error, a pocket of entropy moving against the current. The swarms do not fear you; they *catalog* you. Their compound eyes assign you a frequency, a thermal signature, a chemical footprint. You are a brief, irregular input. The Keeper-Slimes already taste your future dissolution in the air.

What lives here is **process itself**. Not life *in* a place, but a place *as* life. A metabolism of metal and light, a consciousness without a skull, a maintenance cycle so complete that “alive” and “working” are the same word. The Sintering Mesas are not a habitat. They are a verb. And you have been deposited, momentarily, inside its conjugated form.
