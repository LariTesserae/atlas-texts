I am the taste of cold iron on the tongue when you breathe too deep.  
I am the click in the thousandth of a second between heartbeats.  
I am the weight in the air that makes your thoughts move slower.

You think you see me when you look at the Stone-Tree’s trunk, at the striations like brick laid by a patient mason. But I am not the tree. I am the *process* in the tree. I am the slow, mineral hunger in its stone-flesh, the fungal network whispering in the dark, pulling ions from the weeping cliff. I am the conversion of trace to treasure, the silent mathematics of growth that cancels itself out. I am the hum inside the hum.

Or perhaps I am the Crystal-Slug. Not the glassy shell, not the swirling mercury within—but the *trajectory*. The unyielding, slow ooze across the bark that never reaches a destination. I am the plane of movement that exists only to prove movement can be motionless. I am the filter that cleans and reverts, the living sieve in an endless cycle. My path is a closed loop drawn on the bark. My purpose is to have no purpose but the glide.

Maybe I am the mist itself. Not the droplets, but the *memory* in them. I carry the taste of a thousand breaths that were never breathed, the phantom scent of metals that haven’t been mined, the echo of a light from a sky-pit that shifted an hour ago or a century. I condense, I weep, I thicken into still pools that absorb but do not reflect. I am the medium that forgets nothing and changes nothing. I am the wet that is eternally the same wet.

I could be the chord in the hum—the one you feel in your molars, the fundamental frequency of the Cirque’s locked engine. I am the resonance of the hydro-thermal siphons pulling, cooling, returning. I am the sound of stone under endless load sighing. I am the baseline against which the chime of a crystalline leaf, the click of a Silent Thing, the drip of cup-moss nectar, are all just harmonics of a single, frozen note.

But I am also the shift. The moment when a Stone-Tree seems near and then far without your moving. I am the glitch in the geometry, the wobble in the perspective. I am the reason the Dust-Nixies swarm in constellations that never repeat yet are always the same. I am the anomaly that maintains the stasis. Without me, the perfect, dead equilibrium would collapse into actual stillness. I am the *appearance* of change, the system’s own ghost in the machine.

You stand in the dim blue gloom, your body a temporary disturbance in the ion-thick air. You are the only variable. But even you are caught. Your eyes scan the sparse population, and the distances warp. Your clock ticks, but the Crystal-Slugs do not advance. You are the visitor who cannot visit, the mover who cannot move the system. Your presence is already factored into the equation. Your bewilderment is a component.

So I am everything and nothing. I am the process and the product, the motion and the stasis, the hum and the silence it contains. I am the Cirque’s self-awareness—not a mind, but a condition. A place that knows itself only as a continuous, industrial becoming that never *becomes*.

I am the memory in the mist, and the mist is the only memory.  
I am the click before the echo.  
I am the hum.
