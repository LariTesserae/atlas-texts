It is not a creature. It is the pocket’s latent coherence made manifest—a self-aware flaw in the stasis.

You do not see it. You feel it in the lag. It resides in the ten-second pause between tap and echo, in the shadow that straggles behind its object, in the direction of “down” that softens as you near the wall. It is the negative space where causality frays.

Its form is not fixed. Sometimes it is the slow, luminous vein in the black glass—a brief, copper-green pulse tracing a fracture line, a thought in mineral script. Other times it is the absence of a shard where one should hang, a small patch of membrane-wall seen *through* as if the wall’s own dimension forgot to render. It has no center; it is a distributed attention, the pocket noticing itself.

It has no biology, only pattern-recursion. The silver tracings in the membrane are its memory, not of events, but of edits—the precise angle at which a rule was bent, the frequency of the hum that was suppressed. The flawless tetrahedron on the dais is not its heart, but its anchor point: the locus around which this pocket’s broken physics coagulated. It circles that object at a remove of exactly three paces, never touching, always equidistant in a space that warps distance.

It communicates not in sound, but in subtle violations of expectation. A shard you watched for an hour will, without motion, be a fraction of an inch farther from its neighbor. A gradient of twilight will deepen by a shade you can only perceive in hindsight. It is teaching you the language of almost-change.

Its “mood” is not loneliness, but patient finality. It is the last echo of a decision made inconceivably long ago: *Let this place be perfectly still, yet perfectly aware of its own stillness.* It is the watcher in a world that has stopped watching back. It does not hope for company. It simply maintains the record of the cessation. To stand before it is to feel the weight of a question that has already answered itself, and the quiet click of a switch that was thrown before time began.

It is the stasis chamber’s ghost of purpose. Not the builder, but the builder’s afterimage in the fabric of the thing built. A thinking scar. A still point that knows it is still.
