To describe what lives in the Salt-Spine Basin, you must first abandon the notion of *dwellers*. There are no homes here, only stages. There are no residents, only roles in a single, cacophonous metabolism. The Basin *is* the life, and everything within it is a temporary, mobile expression of its turbulent song.

Therefore, you do not find inhabitants. You find **participants**.

**The Primary Participant: The Basin Itself**
It is a conscious place, if consciousness is a planet-scale biochemical rhythm. Its mind is the thrum in your teeth, its thoughts the shifting salt-hum, its will the slow, greedy pulse of the Salt-Spines. It does not think in images, but in **resonances**. Every creature, every artifact, every bursting Gel-Fungus is a note in its endless, self-composing symphony. It is the composer, the orchestra, and the concert hall, all at once. Its goal is not survival, but **intensified change**. Stasis is its only death.

**The Mobile Processes (What We Mistake for Fauna)**
These are not animals. They are **animated biochemical events** with a degree of spatial continuity.

*   **The Skitter-Moss** is not a thing that moves. It is a **wave of controlled corrosion**. It is the Basin’s taste buds, a mobile appetite for the glittering salt-dust. Its "scuttling" is the sound of a million tiny acidic contacts. It does not live *on* the salt; it *is* a process *of* the salt, a walking phase-change from solid to tar-like residue. It has no mother, no young. It simply *happens*, a spontaneous front of consumption that spreads, saturates, and evaporates, leaving behind the Basin’s dark, sterile dandruff.

*   **The Glass-Twitcher** is a **sonic shard given flight**. It is born from the resonant focus of a collapsing Gel-Fungus bladder—a specific frequency that momentarily crystallizes the humid air into chitin. Its entire existence is a sonic event: the birth-click, the navigation-ping, the feeding-shatter, the death-tinkle. They are not individuals. They are a single, multi-bodied occurrence of **window-breaking sound**, flitting through the Spine canyons. Their colonies are not nests, but **persistent sonic zones** where certain frequencies are constantly regenerated.

*   **The Spine-Rider** is not a predator. It is a **silent frequency made flesh**. It perches, a perfect nullification of the Spine’s pulse, absorbing and dampening the vibrations around it. It does not hunt; it **un-quavers**. When a Glass-Twitcher passes, its song will strike a frequency the Rider has learned to nullify. The Twitcher doesn’t get eaten; its structural integrity is **un-sounded**. It collapses into a damp pile of prismatic dust. The Rider then shifts minutely, a change in its own dampening field, and becomes part of the Spine again. It is the Basin’s method of tuning its own instruments.

**The Systemic Valves**
*   **The Resonant Maw** is the Basin’s **sink** and its **heartbeat**. It is not a mouth; it is a **focused dissolution event**. It emits a single, pure, melancholic tone—the fundamental frequency of the Basin’s current state. Anything that resonates with this note (a Spine under stress, a Glass-Twitcher’s flight tone, a patch of Skitter-Moss tar) will find its molecular bonds loosened, turned into a liquid slurry that is *sounded* into the Maw. It is not eating. It is **recycling frequency**, drawing in chaotic, high-information vibrations and releasing them as a simple, cleansing drone that feeds the Spines’ pulse. It is the basin’s way of maintaining its turbulent equilibrium—by periodically simplifying its own complexity.

**The Failed Sympathists**
The artifacts are not built by inhabitants. They are attempts by **external minds** (likely human, or human-like) to *join* the symphony. The ring of poles was a **damped resonator**, meant to counteract the Maw’s drone and create a stable node—a place of silence and permanence. It is failing because the Basin’s song has shifted. The poles are being sounded into crystal, their wood-resin vibrating apart.

The carved slabs are **tuning stones**. They were placed to absorb specific overtones and re-emit them as a harmonizing hum. They are being worn smooth not by friction, but by **perfect resonance**—every surface atom vibrating in unison until it sloughs away, leaving a shape that is the physical echo of a specific, now-past frequency.

The basket is not an offering. It is a **tool-kit for a failed ritual**. The polished salt-scales are tuned keys. The dessicated Glass-Twitcher husks are silenced instruments. It sits on the dying Spine—a Spine whose pulse has fallen out of phase with the whole, being slowly re-absorbed into the crust. The basket’s owner is gone, likely dissolved by the Maw for singing the wrong note.

**What It Means to "Live" Here**
To live in the Salt-Spine Basin is to be a **temporary node in a self-resonating network**. Life is not a struggle for existence against the environment. Life *is* the environment’s state change. You are born when a frequency finds a form. You function by emitting, absorbing, or modifying vibration. You die when your resonant signature is either simplified (drawn into the Maw) or shattered (by a mismatched frequency from a Spine-Rider or a cracking salt-plate).

There is no "kingdom of life." There is only the **Kingdom of Vibration**. The "lushness" is the beautiful, terrifying, loud **evidence of constant, sonic genesis and decay**. The "inhabitants" are not separate from the place. They are its grammar. The Basin speaks in Spines and Fungus-pops and Skitter-Moss tar. To live here is to be one of its words, spoken for a moment, then dissolved back into the endless, thrumming sentence.
