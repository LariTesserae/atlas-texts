Based on the description of the Fallow Concrescence, what lives there is not a collection of discrete beings, but a **single, sprawling, symbiotic pathology**—a biome of Processes, Echoes, and Impressions that is the landscape itself given temporary, hungry shape. They are less creatures and more **active symptoms of a place still setting**.

Here is what lives in the basin:

### **The Ambient Symbiotes**
These are the baseline residents, as fundamental as the chemical film.
*   **The Silt-Scribes:** Microscopic, floating colonies in the milky condensate. They are not alive in a cellular sense, but are **aggregates of ground-down reality** that temporarily congeal into slow, drifting scripts. They write and unwrite tiny, nonsensical laws in the water (“a pebble may be heavier than a thought,” “sound flows uphill here”), which dissolve before they can be read. To have them pass through your skin is to feel a fleeting, impossible certainty about something trivial.
*   **Pressure-Vines’ Dreamers:** The vines themselves digest particulate weirdness, but their slow peristalsis exhales a fine, warm mist that coalesces into **ephemeral, non-corporeal shapes**—the “dreams” of what they consumed. For a few minutes, a faint, shimmering goat-shape might stand where a vine pulsed, made of humid air and the memory of a plastic bottle cap. They have no will, only a brief, resonant form.
*   **Gravity Motes:** Part of the “gravity-tactile” gradients. They are not particles, but **knots in the local spacetime fabric**, visible only as a slight shimmer and felt as a patch of extra weight or buoyancy. They drift and knot, occasionally snapping with a silent *twang* you feel in your joints, re-weaving the basin’s subtle topology.

### **The Intent-Echoes**
Born from the stillborn structures and frozen industrial intent. They are ghosts of functions that never were.
*   **The Unpoured Concrete Specters:** haunting the foundations of the never-equipped factory. They are shapeless, wet-sounding presences that **ooze from the rebar ribs**, whispering the tensile strength calculations of a building that exists only in a blueprint now eaten by resin. They try to “build” by pressing thick, cold phantom slurry against your legs, only to have it evaporate into a smell of wet cement and disappointment.
*   **The Stillborn Assembly Lines:** In the Great Sinterfield, where fused machinery lies, you can sometimes hear—not with ears, but as a vibration in the fillings of your teeth—the **ghostly cadence of a conveyor belt that never ran**. It is a rhythmic, metallic *click-hiss-whir* that makes nearby rust-green copper moss shiver in unison. It is the sound of a process waiting for a first part that will never arrive.
*   **The Liquid Syntax Leeches:** Sluggish, translucent annelids in the pipes that carry “liquid syntax.” They do not eat it; they **are its metabolic byproduct**. They burrow into the slow flow and excrete tiny, hardened **causality loops** (“the pipe is wet because it weeps,” “the weep is wet because it is a pipe”). These loops are the resin-worms’ truffles.

### **The Resonance-Beings**
Formed from the basin’s saturated, lethargic atmosphere and its taste-memories.
*   **Taste-Memory Motes:** The “dust-sermons” given a fleeting swarm-intelligence. When a heavy sigh of the wind carries a strong memory-taste (older arcades, a specific brand of wet concrete), the motes will briefly swarm into a **shifting, gust-borne face** that tastes the air with you. It offers no wisdom, only a profound, melancholic familiarity—the feeling of a door you’ve never opened.
*   **The Afterimage Stains:** Left by the Skimmers’ belly-light. Where a Skimmer passes, the darkness doesn’t just return; it **retains a negative imprint**—a faint, sourceless patch of sickly yellow-green that lingers for minutes, feeling slightly warmer and tasting of static. They are the basin remembering a light that was never meant to see.
*   **The Whispering Pit Choristers:** Not the Drain-Singers (who are the soak’s voice), but smaller, localized phenomena in the subsidence bowls. They are **bundles of trapped sound** from the wind’s sigh through the pipe-forests. They take the form of faint, murmuring eddies in the humid air that, if you stand very still, will whisper a single, contextless word in a dead language (“catalyst,” “quarantine,” “overload”). The word then dissolves into a drop of the metallic condensate.

### **The Ontological Tides Themselves**
During the slow, gigantic tides where weirdness intensifies, the landscape’s rules relax enough for new, temporary forms.
*   **The Melt-forms:** Patches of the “frozen moments of collapse” (the mid-fall arch, the layered slag cliff) will, for the duration of the tide, **very slowly continue their original motion**. The arch’s missing block descends a millimeter over a week. The slag cliff’s top layer sloughs off with a sound like grinding teeth. These are not creatures, but the landscape briefly remembering its own violent birth.
*   **The Soak’s Progeny:** From the central depression where reality is most dilute, the condensate sometimes thickens into **brief, bubble-like fetuses** of pure potential. They are clear, warm, and pulse with a faint internal light. They contain no DNA, no blueprint—only a vague sense of *function* (“to cool,” “to connect,” “to seal”). They rise, drift a few feet, and pop with a soft *blip*, releasing a gout of vapor that makes nearby resin briefly fluoresce. They are the Soak trying to birth a solution to a problem it has forgotten.
*   **Your Own Impression-Made-Flesh:** If you remain in one place for many hours during a tide, the basin may **impress your presence back at you as a minor entity**. A **shadow-stain** might gain a few degrees of autonomy, crawling a few inches toward the nearest light-source. A **memory of your decision** to turn left might condense into a faint, six-legged shape that mimics your gait for a day before dissolving. You are not creating life; you are providing a template for the basin’s own slow metabolism to replicate.

### **The Predators (of a Sort)**
Even in this place of geological digestion, there is consumption.
*   **The Causality Leeches:** A variant of the resin-worms that surfaces during ontological tides. They do not burrow through resin, but through **freshly-made impressions**. They can be seen—as a subtle, fast-moving ripple in the air or ground—slithering over your just-made footprint or the spot where you spoke. They “bite” the causality, leaving behind a tiny, perfectly looplet of **“if-then” that loops back on itself** (“If I am here, then I was never here”). Stepping on one makes you forget the last ten seconds of your walk.
*   **The Perspective-Shift Pack:** Not animals, but collaborative glitches. When multiple gravity motes and shifting perspectives align, a **pocket of unstable geometry** forms. It behaves like a silent, skittering pack, making distant spires loom and near ground recede for anyone caught within it. It “hunts” by herding travelers in inefficient circles, not to eat them, but to **amplify the confusion**—to thicken the medium of their disorientation, which the basin seems to find nourishing.

---

**In summary:** What lives in the Fallow Concrescence is **the aftermath of intent made hungry**. Its fauna are **processes with temporary faces**, its flora are **calculations that dissolve**, and its “wildlife” is the **ambient consciousness of a place digesting its own stillborn birth**. You do not see food chains. You see **metabolic loops**: the chemical film feeds the Grumps, who leave reliefs that collect condensate, which feeds the Silt-Scribes, whose scripts are digested by the vines, whose dreams are eaten by the Syntax Leeches, whose causality loops are the resin-worms’ castings, which become new topography, which… slows, and sets, and sweats. It is a closed, lethargic circuit of becoming-geology, and everything in it is both the consumer and the consumed, forever pressing impressions into a medium that slowly, patiently, forgets how to hold a sharp edge.
