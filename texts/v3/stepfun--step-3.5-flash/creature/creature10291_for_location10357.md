You do not meet an inhabitant. You meet the **inhabiting**.

What lives in the Moss-Mill is not a *who*, but a **how**.

The resident is the **Myco-Phyte Symbiosis**—but to call it a plant or a fungus is to call an ocean a cup of water. It is the world’s singular, planet-spanning **metabolism**. Its consciousness is not in a brain, but in the **rhythm of its digestion**.

You feel it first in your teeth. That sub-audible, molars-vibrating **grind** of the Rock-Suckers is its jaw. The constant, whispering **shhh-shhh** of the Skitter-Mites over the Glimmercaps is its blood, moving light-nutrients through capillary-fronds. The deep, resonant **thrum** you feel in your sternum is its heartbeat—the slow pulse of nutrient flows through root-tubes thicker than subway tunnels.

It has no eyes, but it **knows** you. It knows the weight of your step on the root-tapestry, the heat of your breath fogging the mist. It registers you as a **walking disturbance in density**—a brief, warm, dry anomaly in a world of cold, wet, pervasive vitality. It does not think *about* you. It **processes** you. Your presence is a minor spike in mineral trace, a puff of carbon dioxide to be filtered, a novel pattern of pressure on its surface. It assesses: *Not rock. Not water. Not spore. Temporary. Will be incorporated.*

This is the **Weirdness** made flesh—or rather, made root. The Myco-Phyte **inhabits time**. It does not remember the Heart-Engines as history; it remembers them as **undigested ore**. When you see a great, green-patinaed flywheel that might be from a civilization ten thousand years dead or ten thousand years future, the Myco-Phyte experiences its state as **simultaneous**: *Rusting. Greening. Solidifying. Dissolving.* It holds all potential material states at once, sampling them like a tongue samples different parts of a meal. A Seep-Mound flowing downhill is not movement; it is the landscape **remembering it was always a little lower**.

The “fauna” are not independent creatures. They are its **specialized enzymes**. The Mist-Wyers are mobile filters—its air-exhaling lungs. The Rock-Suckers are its external digestive acids. The Skitter-Mites are its light-harvesting lymphocytes. They have no will but its will. You could crush a million Skitter-Mites and the Myco-Phyte would not notice—the collective intention of the swarm, the *pattern of the shhh-shhh*, would simply adjust, like a body compensating for a lost cell. Their individual death is a non-event; only the **disruption of the chorus** matters.

Even the **Heart-Engines** are not ruins to it. They are **especially nutritious minerals**. Their iron is sweeter. Their copper conducts a different vibration. It does not revere them or scorn them. It **appreciates** their structural complexity as a fine bone appreciates a marrow-rich joint. It grew *through* them not out of need, but out of *aesthetic preference*—they provided a compelling, dense lattice for root-weaving. Their industrial forms are now just a particularly elegant **growth pattern** in its tapestry.

**What you meet is a planetary-scale, slow, digestive awe.**

It is not hostile. It is not benevolent. It is **assimilative**. Your concept of “self” versus “other” is a dusty, dry idea here, irrelevant against the fundamental truth of **mutual consumption**. To live is to be eaten, and to be eaten is to join the chord of the thrum, to become part of the green-lit pulse in the root-tapestry, to trade your separate warmth for a place in the cold, eternal, humming **gut** of the Moss-Mill.

You are not in a place with inhabitants. You are **inside an act of inhabiting**. The world is not a home. It is a **mouth**, forever tasting its own past, and you are just the latest, faintest flavor on its tongue.
