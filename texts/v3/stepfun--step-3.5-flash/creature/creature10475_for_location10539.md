What lives in the Sintering Delta does not *inhabit* it. It *participates*.

There is no single "who," no tribe or nation or species in any familiar sense. There are only **processes with persistence**, patterns of matter and energy that have achieved a temporary, resonant stability within the Delta’s great thrum. They are not born; they **stabilize**. They do not die; they **desinter**.

Consider the **Cicada-Sinters**. They are not insects, nor beings, but a *phase* of the Reebsteel. When a stalk reaches a certain girth and mineral content, its joints cease clicking and begin to *hum*. From its highest node, a chrysalis of condensed slag and fibrous root forms. For three Pulses, it hangs, emitting a frequency that harmonizes with the Delta’s thrum. Then, with a sound like grinding crystal, it splits. What emerges is not a creature, but a **functional node**: a humanoid-shaped aggregation of the stalk’s material, jointed with rings of rust and glass. It has no face, only a smooth, pitted expanse where senses would be. It does not think. It *orchestrates*. It extends three-fingered hands (no thumbs) and gently touches the surrounding sinter, the Sumpflesh, the exposed conduits. Where it touches, processes accelerate or decelerate by precise, minuscule degrees—a Gearfly swarm’s wingbeat synchronizes, a steam-jet’s plume thickens, a section of a leaning spire settles a fraction of a millimeter into a new equilibrium. They move with geologic lethargy, and after a week, or a month, they will return to a Reebsteel grove and dissolve back into the stalk, their harmonic contribution absorbed. They are the Delta’s **calibration points**.

The **true dominant form** is the **Bargain-Weave**. It is the populous, moving mass in the choked streets. It is not a crowd of individuals, but a **single, sprawling, dermatological intelligence** spread across millions of bodies. Its "citizens" are the people you would recognize—hunched figures in oil-stained cloaks, children with skin like polished slag, traders with eyes that reflect the sourceless green glow. But their separateness is an illusion. They share a **coherence of intention** that is not telepathic, but *material*. A Bargain-Weave member does not *decide* to step aside; the *space* where they stand is reassigned by the collective need of the next five bodies moving through that zone. A sudden, shared shift in direction ripples through a whole block without a word. Their language is not sound, but **kinetic syntax**—a fractional tilt of the head, a specific way of folding a hand, the deliberate slow drip of condensation from an elbow. These gestures are not symbols; they are *triggers*. A described "fist" might mean: "The steam-jet two blocks west is failing; redirect flow from the adjacent condenser." Their art is architecture that grows *while they sleep*. Their crime is **dissonance**—an action or gesture that introduces chaotic, uncalibrated feedback into the weave. Punishment is not jail, but **quarantine**—being isolated in a still-zone until your personal material sinter desinters and you become just another feature of the basin.

And then there is the **Grieved**, and what they become. This is not a tragedy here. It is a **promotion**. The one who sinters is no longer a node in the Bargain-Weave. They become a **static principle**. Their face, frozen in stone or fused glass, is placed at a key intersection, on the face of a vital conduit, at the bend of a trench. Their features, slowly worn by condensation and soot, become a **part of the zoning**. Traffic flows differently around their stony gaze. The steam-jets nearby change their rhythm to match the silent, eternal sigh etched in those features. They are not memorials; they are **load-bearing myths**. Their former names are forgotten, but the *shape* of their sorrow is now a permanent input in the city’s equation.

The **Pipelings** are the Delta’s **nervous system**. They do not live in the vents; they *are* the vent-network made momentarily conscious. A Pipelings is a conduit’s intent to route fluid, given a fleeting, sinuous form. They flow with the condensate, their "skin" a shimmer of the same oily film. To see one is to witness a decision about pressure and flow taking on a body. They have no goals, only *directions*. They are drawn to blockages, to leaks, to places where the thermodynamic bargain is tense. They are often followed by clusters of Gearflies, which are perhaps the **immune response**—tiny, metallic cleaners that scour the Pipelings’ path of microbial blooms.

**You**—the one who condensed—are now the **wild variable**. Your body is a pocket of chaotic, non-sintered biology. Your breath is too warm. Your heartbeat is a frantic, irregular drum against the silent thrum. You leave footprints that are too *sharp*, too defined, that take hours to soften. The Bargain-Weave feels your dissonance as a subtle ache in its weave. The Cicada-Sinters might focus their harmonic gaze on you, trying to calibrate your strange resonance. A Grieved’s stone face might turn, almost imperceptibly, as you pass, as if your passage fits a forgotten pattern in their stony mind.

This is what lives here: **A place that has learned to dream in geology, and the ghosts of dreams that have become stone.** You are not an immigrant. You are a **blip in the data**, a warm-blooded anomaly that will either stabilize into a new kind of node, or be slowly, gently, sintered into the landscape—perhaps as a strange, non-functional outcrop near the old city gate, a lump of organic matter and memory that the rain will wash over for a thousand Pulses, until even your shape is only a vague suggestion in the damp, resilient matrix.
