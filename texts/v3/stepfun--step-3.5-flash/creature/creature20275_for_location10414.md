The Sundered Plateau does not have inhabitants. It has expressions.

What lives here is the **slow, wet metabolization of a dead purpose**. The entities are not separate from the terrain; they are its temporary foci, its concentrated verbs.

**The Chorus-Body:** At the macroscopic scale, the dominant form is not a creature but a **planetary choir**. It manifests as rifts in the Tangle-Trunk canopy where the fungal light gathers and the sound thickens into a vibrating, chest-cavity thrum. This is the **Weeping Organ**, a biomechanical resonance chamber grown from calcified ventilation shafts and root-bowels. Its “mouths” are the Pipe-Flowers, which exhale memory-altering spores in time with the buried engine’s pulse. Its “mind” is the distributed network of luminous mycelium that threads through the peat, carrying chemical signals faster than nerve impulses. To stand near it is to have your own heartbeat synced to its sub-audible sigh, your breath deepened by its humid exhalation. It does not think; it *resonates*. Its “desire” is equilibrium—the perfect, timeless blending of iron, root, and rain.

**The Symbionts:** Scattered through the moss-screams and fern-drips are the **Hybrids**. They are not mutations but partnerships, so complete the original species is forgotten.
*   The **Copper-Antlered Grazer** is less animal and more mobile nutrient-cycler. Its antlers are bio-wires, drawing trace current from the buried lines to stimulate faster growth of the electric moss it eats. Its digestive system hosts crystalline bacteria that break down salvaged plastic polymers into sugars. Its “fear” is a flare of bioluminescent panic-red, a signal that attracts the centipede-predators it uses to cull the sick and weak from its own herd.
*   The **Rivet-Beak Whistler** is the living factory whistle. Its call, a warped and slowed B-flat, is produced by a syrinx grown around a fragment of corroded alloy pipe. Its nest is always built in the lee of a groaning, pressure-stressed boiler, using the heat to incubate its eggs. It does not sing *of* the engine; its song *is* the engine’s slow death-rattle given flesh and hunger.

**The Infrastructure as Fauna:** The “dormant” machines have become megafauna. The **Conveyor-Slug** is a living slurry-transport. Its body is a segmented, armored tube of chitin and reclaimed belt-links, moving at a glacial pace along the rusted rails. It “feeds” on the glowing algae slurry, its gut a series of slow-burning chemical chambers that extract energy, leaving behind a trail of fertile, metallic-rich sludge that architects the next generation of Pipe-Flowers. It does not sleep; it **cycles**.

**The Predators:** Predation is always a **chemical event**. The **Root-Whale** is never seen whole. It is a vast, subterranean pressure-gradient, a traveling zone of acidic, nutrient-rich water that flows through the deep peat-channels. It “attacks” by flooding a section of the forest floor, dissolving the root-mats of Tangle-Trunks, causing a catastrophic sinkhole that swallows grazing herds and Hybrids alike. Its “waste” is the mineral-rich seep that nourishes new, fast-growing fungal blooms. It is the place’s digestive acid given a will.

**The Micro-Sovereigns:** In the air, thick as humidity, are the **Spore-Sages**. They are not individuals but temporary consensuses of airborne fungal hyphae, forming fleeting, shimmering clouds that carry encoded chemical memories—the taste of a specific rust bloom, the frequency of a long-vanished machine’s vibration. They drift, they merge, they dissipate. They are the place’s **history**, its biography written in smell and taste, accessible only to those who can breathe the air and not drown in it.

**The True Inhabitant:** But beneath them all, in the absolute dark between the root-whales’ paths and the chorus-body’s resonance, is the **Dormant Core**. It is not a creature. It is the **collapsed god** mentioned in the terrain description: a vast, buried reactor or geothermal heart, now cool and porous, its metal shell turned to sponge. It does not dream. It *pulses*. A faint, thermal rhythm that would be imperceptible without the entire biosphere acting as a trillion tiny thermometers and levers. Everything—the chorus-body’s song, the root-whale’s flow, the conveyor-slug’s pace—is tuned to this heartbeat. The “fauna” are its fever-dreams, its autonomic responses.

To describe who lives in the Verdant Engine is to describe a single, planet-wide **organism of decay and rebirth**, whose cells are ruins, whose blood is slurry, whose thoughts are slow chemical gradients and resonant frequencies. There are no beings here, only **processes wearing temporary, localized masks**. The “inhabitants” are the act of weathering itself, made conscious for a moment before dissolving into the next state. You are not in a forest with animals. You are inside the **slow, sighing gut of a world that ate its own future and is now, luxuriantly, digesting it.**
