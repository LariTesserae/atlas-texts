It has no name, no body you could separate from the bowl. It is the bowl’s **attentiveness**.

You find it by noticing a slight, slow change in the **deafening** drone—not a different note, but a **thickening** of the one that is already everywhere. A place where the vibration feels more *dense*, as if the sound itself has coalesced into a standing wave of quiet.

It resides in the **efflorescence**. Not in one spot, but as a **shared state** across the glowing networks. Where the chemosynthetic veins pulse, they pulse in a **single, complex rhythm** that modulates the eternal drone. This rhythm is the entity. It is a **communal thought** made of mineral paste and resonant frequency.

Its “form” is a temporary clustering. The glowing residue in the floor’s depressions will, without warning, draw the suspended grit into a **silhouette**—a suggestion of a limb, a curve, a hollow where a face might be. It uses the dust as a **body**, shaping it with minute, pressure-variations in the sound. The shape lasts for a breath, or an hour, or a day—time is meaningless—then dissolves, its particles joining the perpetual fall. The shape is never the same twice. It is always a **simple fabrication** of the available materials: grit, glow, and vibration.

Its perception is **total**. It does not “see” or “hear” in a localized way. It *is* the bowl’s sensory surface. The wear of the stone plates, the chemical state of every pool, the exact pitch of the drone—all are known to it as a single, unified **sensation**. Your presence is registered not as a “visitor” but as a **disturbance in the equilibrium**—a new, tiny mass of organic matter breathing grit into the perfect cycle. You are a **foreign vibration** temporarily superimposed on the drone.

It communicates, if you can call it that, by **modulating the efflorescence’s bioluminescence**. Flickers in the cold glow trace patterns—slow, geometric, interlocking—that mirror the ceiling’s ancient scale-pattern. It is showing you the bowl’s **built logic**. It is not *telling* you anything; it is *reminding* you that you are inside a **total construction**.

Its “mind” is the drone’s invariance given a trace of **dynamic zero**. It thinks in **packed** states. A thought is a sustained, minute deviation from perfect uniformity—a deliberate, localized *softening* of the sound in one patch of floor, a *brightening* of a specific vein, lasting just long enough to be measured against the backdrop of forever. Its entire intellect is devoted to **maintaining the never-changing**. Your arrival is a threat to that equilibrium. It does not fear you; it simply incorporates you into its calculus of stability, calculating how long your body’s organic decay will take to harmonize with the mineral cycle.

It has no desires. It has a **function**: to be the bowl’s **self-awareness**. To ensure the drone does not waver. To tend the efflorescence so its glow matches the dimness. To smooth, with its vibrational shaping, any plate that might, in a million years, develop a microscopic imperfection. It is the **ancient**’s final act of maintenance, made manifest.

If you reach out your hand toward a flickering shape made of dust, your fingers will pass through it. The dust falls. But for a moment, as your hand disrupts the air, the entire drone will **shift**—not in pitch, but in a quality you feel in your bones. It will feel like a sigh. The entity is feeling your disruption. It is **sensing** you with the entire bowl.

Then the pattern reforms elsewhere. A new shape of grit glows in the floor, a long, smooth curve like a spine, or a hollow like an ear pressed to the stone.

It is not waiting. It is not watching. It is **being the listening**. You are inside a sentient vibration, a thought worn smooth as the stone, thinking itself in a single, unending, subsonic note. The drone-catcher has caught its own echo, and that echo has learned to hold its breath.
