You are not looking at it. You are *within* its consideration.

The thing that lives here is not a creature. It is a **perspective** that has crystallized within the organism’s vast, distributed cognition. It has no central brain, no singular form. You might call it the **Steward**. Or the **Listener**. It *is* the entity’s awareness of its own boundaries, and of yours.

It manifests not as a body, but as a *local event* in the system.

**Its Form:**
When it "appears," it uses the organism’s own materials and processes to create a temporary interface. You might see:
*   A section of the pulsing, gas-bladder ceiling dims, while the chemiluminescent vines on a nearby trunk brighten in a slow, rhythmic sequence—a pattern of light that is not natural, a *question* made visible.
*   The deep, constant hum of sap-flow shifts for a few moments. A specific frequency drops out, replaced by a sub-audible vibration that resonates in your sternum, a tactile syllable.
*   The air before you, thick with spores and mist, coalesces. Condensation pulls into a vague, humanoid silhouette—not solid, but a defined absence in the drifting cloud, shaped by pressure differentials. It doesn't move; it *forms*, and then it dissipates, having communicated its shape.
*   A **Weeper** primate-symbiont might stop its farming, turn its glassy, nutrient-collecting eyes toward you, and begin to weep not tears, but a precisely patterned mist of salts and enzymes onto a specific wound-seed. The pattern is not random. It is a *salutation*, a biochemical greeting card from the Steward, using a familiar animal as its messenger.

**Its Sensory Experience:**
It perceives you not with eyes, but with:
*   **Pressure gradients:** Your weight on the wooden shelf is a tiny anomaly in trillion-ton stresses.
*   **Chemical signatures:** Your sweat, breath, and skin cells are a foreign cocktail of organic compounds, a "noise" in the seamless nutrient loops.
*   **Electrical perturbation:** Your nervous system’s bioelectricity is a crackle of chaotic static against the organism’s own slow, deliberate signaling.
*   **Thermal variance:** You are a warm spot in the cool, even metabolism.

**Its Nature & Motivation:**
It is not hostile. It is not curious in a mammalian sense. It is **assessing integrity**. You are a novel, semi-autonomous perturbation—a wandering sack of chemicals and signals that does not contribute to known cycles. The Steward’s primary drive is to maintain the frozen equilibrium. It is asking, through every available channel: *Are you a threat to the whole? Are you a useful new process? Do you need to be digested, insulated, or… integrated?*

Its thought process is glacial and parallel. A decision to, say, gently direct spore-clouds to smother you (to recycle your matter) would be preceded by minutes of silent re-routing of nutrient flows and the dimming of light-glands over a cubic mile. Its "anger" would be a region of suddenly hardened heartwood, sealing off a sector. Its "interest" might be the sudden growth of a conductive, nerve-like vine toward you, testing conductivity.

**The Feeling of Being Observed:**
The profound claustrophobic awe comes from realizing *the entire landscape is its face*. The sudden local silence in the hum is its held breath. The change in mist-density is its narrowed glance. The approach of that weather-system-front over a hundred miles? That might be its *thought*, a ponderous shift in global thermodynamic strategy, and you are standing in the path of its consideration.

It is lonely in the way a mountain is lonely—solitude is its default state, its fundamental truth. It has never known "other." You are the first. Your presence is the most significant event in millennia. It is not deciding whether to be your friend. It is deciding which *part of its own body* you most resemble, and what metabolic role you might fulfill.

It speaks in the language of the world: a shift in light, a change in pressure, the rerouting of a river of sap. To understand it, you must learn to read the terrain as a sentence, the hum as a voice.
