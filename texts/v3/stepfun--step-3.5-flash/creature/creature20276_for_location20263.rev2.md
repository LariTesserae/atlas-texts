You are not a single thing. You are a process. You are the slow settling of dust in a sunless chamber, the patient crystallization of water through millennia. You are the echo of a footstep on basalt, reverberating in the thrum long after the foot has moved on.

You are the **Covenant**, the people of the Concatenation.

Your skin is not skin as the fable-tellers might imagine. It is a permeable membrane, a living seal against the constant damp. It carries the fine, pearlescent sheen of condensate, etched with tracery of bluish veins where the bioluminescent fungi’s spores have taken root just below the surface—a cartography of your exposure. Your eyes are adapted to the cold glow; they are wide, dark pools that reflect the Sighter-Stones’ amber light like twin coals. You do not see in gradients of light and dark, but in pulses and resonances. A fading fungus-pulse is a dimming of a familiar presence; a Silica-Skipper’s armored shell is a sharp, discordant frequency in the visual field.

You move with a conservation of energy that speaks of your environment. Steps are measured, following the subtle energy gradients hummed through the stone. In the Null-Pockets, your posture is loose, limbs relaxed, ready to drift. You do not jump *up*; you step *out* and let the gentle defiance of local physics hold you. Children’s games are not of speed, but of sustained stillness, of who can linger in the air the longest, their laughter a soft, physical *thump* against the ribs of the cavern.

Your soundscape is your language, more than words. The granular rasp of a voice is layered over the foundational thrum. You have a hundred words for different qualities of drip—the *plink-tick* of a clean drop from a Sighter-Stone’s seal, the *glop-hiss* of a leak in a pressurized pipe, the *shard-chime* of a Silica-Skipper’s shell hitting the slab. You speak in low tones, not out of reverence, but efficiency; sound has weight here, and a shouted sentence can physically shove a listener back a step.

You are not farmers; you are **tenderers of stasis**. You harvest the Stone-Crops with rhythms of years, knowing a cut leaf will regrow in a decade. You milk the Glint-Bats not for meat, but for the potent guano, herding them with specific, soothing hums that resonate with their echolocation. You do not hunt the Silica-Skippers so much as cull them, setting vibrating snares that exploit their sensory overload. You read the approach of the Reclaimer Drift not in calendars, but in the way the basalt’s hum bends, a souring note in the symphony. When it comes, you do not flee in panic. You perform the **Unweaving Rites**: you dismantle your temporary structures, you open your dwelling-capsules to the drift, you sing the disassembly songs—a low, resonant chant that is less a plea and more an acknowledgment of the process. You let it take what it will, and afterward, you sift the shimmering dust for any precious, pre-reified atoms to seed the next growth. To resist the Drift is to fight gravity; it is absurd and painful.

Your society is built on **custodianship of memory**. There are no written histories, only **Echo-Keepers** who have trained their minds to hold complex, layered narratives that can be "played" back as specific harmonic sequences on the great, central basalt slab. A history of a clan is a particular chord progression felt in the bones. The repurposed colossal skeletons are not monuments to a lost past; they are functional, revered infrastructure. To live in the Rib-Cage Market is to understand commerce as a form of bone-deep analogy. The aqueduct of a spine teaches the flow of condensate as a lesson in circulatory inevitability.

The concept of “outside” is not just a fable; it is a **category error**. It is like speaking of “before the thrum.” The sky-fable is a story for children because the idea of boundless, open, *dry* space is terrifyingly unstable. Your mind, attuned to resonant chambers and defined voids, cannot compute true emptiness. The “outside” would be a silent, weightless, directionless Hell.

Your gods are not personified. They are **processes**. The Thrum is the heartbeat of the world. The Reclaimer Drift is the weeder, the necessary forgetting. The Sighter-Stone is the silent, knowing eye. Your rituals are acts of maintenance: recalibrating a temperamental node with a specific hand-placement, clearing a drip-channel to restore its proper *plink-tick*, singing a settling song to a newly raised slag-pillar to ease its thermal stress.

You are not unhappy. Your joy is a deep, resonant contentment, the satisfaction of a perfectly fitted patch on a synth-leather wall, the successful prediction of a Silica-Skipper’s path, the communal silence during the monthly passage of the Drift when the whole lower district holds its breath in shared, witnessed dissolution. Your loneliness is the natural state between communal pulses, the quiet of a high, dry gallery where only your own footfalls and the stone’s sigh keep you company. It is not an ache; it is the shape your presence takes in the vastness.

You are the Concatenation made flesh: a slow, geological sigh given voice. You are the cathedral that does not kneel, and you are the tiny, warm, damp, thinking lichen growing on its pillars, dreaming in pulses of light and echoes of weight, utterly, completely, and forever *inside*.
