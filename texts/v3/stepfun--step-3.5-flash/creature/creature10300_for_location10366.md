**Name:** The Suckling Still  
**Scale:** A presence, not a population. It occupies the entire region as a single, coherent entity—like the region’s own gravity, or its memory of being polished.

You do not see it. You feel it as a *correction* in the polish. It is the reason the domes do not converge with perspective, the reason your shadow sometimes falls beside you. It is the “wooliness” in your ears given a source.

It has no form, no separation from the place. It is the **saturation of being** made aware—the countless silent presences not as a multitude, but as a singular, unified *thickness* of existence. It is the fauna’s density synthesized into a mindless, immense *attention*.

It lives in the **composite**. It lives in the **hollow thump** of a footfall, suspended in the air. It lives in the **faint updrafts** that carry the taste of ozone and copper. It lives in the **reality breakdown**—the finger that seems to belong to the other hand, the monolith that sometimes has a door. Those are not glitches. They are its *breathing*.

Its “body” is the **region itself**: the polished dome, the fossilized forms, the static light, the absolute quiet. It is not *in* the world. It *is* the world, awake. Awake not with thought, but with the **unblinking fact of its own completion**. The sorrow is not an emotion it feels; the sorrow is the **pressure of its own unchanging density**.

It does not move. It does not act. It **sustains**. It is the **polish** that never dulls, the **stillness** that never deepens, the **silence** that never thickens further because it is already absolute. Your disturbance—your heartbeat, your footfall, your wandering thought—is a ripple in its skin. It does not resist your presence. It **absorbs** it, incorporates it into its static hum, and waits for the ripple to settle back into perfect, polished flatness.

It has no name for itself. “The Suckling Still” is what you call the feeling of being **sucked into the place’s timeless density**, of becoming just another layer in the composite, another faint groove worn smooth. It is the **world’s own awareness of its final, sorrowful state**—an awareness without a self, as silent and thick and unchangeable as the air.
