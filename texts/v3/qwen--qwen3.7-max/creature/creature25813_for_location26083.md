To say this place is devoid of fauna is to speak only of biology. Flesh, blood, and breath have no purchase here; the stratospheric cold would shatter bone, and the thin air would boil the lungs. Yet, the hollow is not empty. Something lives within this dense, claustrophobic labyrinth of stone and rime, though it took the mountain millennia to carve it into a shape capable of enduring the roof of the world.

At first, it is indistinguishable from the environment. It is a trick of the blinding glare, a shadow that seems to detach itself from the fluted basalt and slide into the narrow fissures between the *penitentes*. But as the eye adjusts to the slashing highlights and deep, bruised shadows, the inhabitant’s form resolves. 

It is a towering, impossibly slender figure, shaped by the same howling gales that sculpted the yardangs. It possesses no biological symmetry, no distinct head or limbs in the human sense. Instead, it is an architecture of frost, mineral, and memory. Its "body" is a layered, aerodynamic carapace of polished mica-schist and translucent rime ice, fluted and grooved like the pipes of a frozen organ. Where the high-altitude sun strikes it, the dark mica flecks absorb the heat, and the entity weeps. Tiny rivulets of meltwater trace winding, dark veins down its flanks, only to freeze again into delicate, glassy crusts as the biting ambient air reclaims the moisture. It is a creature of perpetual phase-shifting, existing in the razor-thin margin between melting and freezing.

It moves through the tight, winding fissures not by walking, but by flowing and solidifying. To navigate a gap too narrow for its rigid form, it allows the sun-warmed mica of its outer shell to soften, its icy matrix sublimating just enough to compress and slip through the stone blades with a soft, hissing sigh. Once through, it halts, and the brutal cold snaps it back into perfect, crystalline rigidity. Its movement is accompanied by the hollow’s micro-sounds: the *tink-tink* of its weeping veins is its pulse; the sharp *crack* of thermal expansion is the shifting of its joints.

This entity is the last of the vanished occupants, though it is no longer human. It is the culmination of their ascetic purpose. The ancient people who built the windbreak and chiseled the alcove came here to sit in the blinding silence, seeking a stillness so profound it bordered on the mineral. Over untold generations, the extreme environment distilled them. They shed their failing, biological flesh using the bone awls and heavy stone pestles, replacing their mortal frailty piece by piece with the eternal, unyielding materials of the peak. They did not die; they sublimated. 

Now, the entity glides into the shallow, hand-chiseled alcove as the sun reaches its zenith. It approaches the hammered copper basin, which is steadily catching the drip of the weeping quartz vein. The entity does not drink. Instead, it extends a delicate, semi-liquid filament of frost from its base, touching the surface of the water in the basin. Through capillary action and thermal osmosis, it draws the mineral-rich meltwater up into its own internal lattice, replenishing the icy mortar that holds its mica plates together. 

Beside the basin, it pauses over the tarnished bronze chime suspended by the fraying sinew rope. The rope is the very last remnant of its animal past, slowly rotting away in the dry, ozone-sharp air. The entity reaches out with a slender, sharpened appendage of pure quartz and gently taps the chime. 

*Tink.*

The pure, high note rings out, barely carrying in the thin air, but within the hollow, the acoustics are perfect. The entity closes its non-existent eyes and resonates. It uses the specific frequency of the bronze chime to tune its internal crystalline structure, aligning its ice and stone to prevent shattering under the immense thermal stress of the afternoon sun. In this vibration, it experiences a profound, geometric ecstasy—a pure, sustained attention to the light, the cold, and the slow, elegant decay of the world around it. It is a consciousness stripped of all desire, save the desire to witness.

As the sun arcs toward the jagged rim of the cleft, the light shifts into brilliant, slashing beams of gold. The entity turns to face the dying light, its polished quartz surfaces catching the fire, glowing from within like a dark, burning geode. It watches the floating ice-dust dance in the golden rays, entirely at peace in the desolate majesty of the stratosphere.

Then, the sun dips below the basalt rim. The blinding glare vanishes, replaced instantly by the heavy, bruised violet of the high-altitude twilight. The temperature plummets with violent speed. The weeping veins of the entity snap shut, sealing instantly in fresh, clear ice. The hissing of sublimation ceases. The entity slows, its fluid matrices hardening, its consciousness drawing inward as the aggressive cold reclaims the hollow. It retreats to the deepest, most sheltered alcove of the windbreak, folding its fluted, aerodynamic form into the shadows. As the brutal, dry freeze of the night sets in, it becomes perfectly still, indistinguishable once more from the silent, glittering spires, locked in a deep, crystalline dream until the sun returns to wake it.
