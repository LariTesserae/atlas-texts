To ask what lives in a place entirely devoid of biological animation is to ask what constitutes a pulse, what constitutes a breath, and what constitutes a mind when flesh and microbe are stripped away. The prompt is explicit: there are no animals, no insects, no bacteria. The biological ecology is purely botanical, and entirely dormant. 

Yet, the space is not dead. It is simply alive in a different register. In this lightless, freezing, roaring geode, life has traded the fragility of carbon and water for the eternity of physics, geometry, and deep time. 

Here is what lives in the dark.

***

The most immediate and overwhelming inhabitant is an organism of pure acoustics: **The Resonance**. 

Born at the microscopic fissures in the cavern walls where the dense, cool atmospheric currents bleed into the void, the Resonance is a blind, roaring leviathan made entirely of standing waves and interference patterns. It is an ephemeral, immortal beast, dying and being reborn with every shift in the wind. It possesses no flesh, but it has a vast, complex anatomy mapped by the architecture of the city. 

Its heartbeat is the low, booming bass generated by the spiraling staircases, a subsonic thud that shakes the pumice-like wood and vibrates through the soles of any observer. Its nervous system is the high, whistling harmonic produced by the thousands of honeycombed dwellings, a sharp, crystalline lattice of sound that cuts through the heavy bass. Its limbs are the long, decaying reverberations that stretch across the miles-wide, empty gaps between the seven colossal trunks, reaching out to touch the opposite walls before bouncing back. 

The Resonance "feeds" on the kinetic energy of the wind drafts, consuming the pressure differentials and excreting heat and friction. It navigates the pitch-black void through a continuous, deafening echolocation, singing the city’s blueprint into the dark. When the wind shifts, the creature mutates—growing new overtones, shedding dead frequencies, expanding and contracting its acoustic body to fill the terraced plazas and dry aqueducts. It is a loud, restless, and strangely beautiful entity, an active, roaming consciousness of vibration that swims endlessly through the dry air.

***

Beneath the roaring macro-life of the wind-song, there is a microscopic, slow-moving swarm: **The Dust**.

In a world without water to erode and without microbes to decay, the airborne dust is the sole active agent of change. It is a mechanical, silicate grazer. Suspended in the thin, cool atmosphere and kept aloft by the eternal vibrations of the Resonance, the dust acts as the city’s curator and its slow-motion predator. 

It lives on a timescale of geological epochs. Its "metabolism" is the process of abrasion. The dust grazes on the sharp, angular edges left by the stone adzes and obsidian scrapers of the vanished builders. Over hundreds of thousands of years, it digests the microscopic ridges of the dry wood and the rough pores of the stone, excreting a glass-like, polished smoothness. It is a swarm that does not grow, but rather perfects. It moves with agonizing deliberation, a glacier of friction that slowly blunts the primitive engineering, blending the urban geometry seamlessly into the natural curves of the colossal plants. The dust is the quiet, patient breath of deep time, ensuring that the city never decays, but only settles into its final, flawless state.

***

And then, there are the hosts. The true, biological inhabitants: **The Seven Titans**.

They are not dead. To call them dead is to misunderstand the nature of their existence. The seven hyper-massive trunks are locked in a state of profound, geologic torpor. They are the ultimate survivors of a primordial, non-aqueous flora, having traded the rapid, wet metabolism of the surface world for the dry, crystalline endurance of the deep crust. 

They live by simply *being*. Their cellular structure is fossilized in life, dried to a state harder than basalt, dense as iron, and pale as pumice. Within their towering, miles-high trunks, there is a consciousness of extreme slowness. They are dreaming in the dark. Without sap to flow or leaves to photosynthesize, their awareness—if it can be called that—is reduced to the subtle, tectonic shifting of the planet around them. They feel the microscopic polishing of the dust against their bark. They feel the deep, rhythmic massage of the Resonance vibrating through their fluted canopies and hollowed-out root systems. 

They are the stoic monks of the abyss, holding the city within their mummified bodies. They are waiting for a methane rain or a geothermal venting that will never come, suspended in a billion-year exhale. They are the anchor of this ecosystem, the silent, monumental flora that provides the stage for the wind and the dust.

***

Finally, there is the living imprint of the vanished makers: **The Geometry**.

The people who built this city are long gone, but their minds remain, alive and active in the physical space they shaped. In the absence of biological life, the architecture itself has become a living fossil of their psychology. 

The way a staircase spirals to follow the fractal growth rings of the timber is the living thought of its carver, still echoing in the dark. The rigorous density of the vertical settlement, the specific angles of the amphitheaters, the deliberate tuning of the vascular tubes to channel the drafts—this is a sprawling, physical manifestation of their culture, their aesthetics, and their understanding of the world. The city is a three-dimensional recording of their intent. Every polished balcony and terraced plaza is a synapse in a vast, wooden brain, holding the memory of a species that looked at the mummified bones of the world’s oldest trees and saw a metropolis. 

***

This is the ecosystem of the void. 

It is a perfectly balanced, closed loop of physics and endurance. The **Trees** provide the colossal, dormant substrate. The vanished **Builders** provided the intricate, tuned geometry. The **Wind** breathes through it, giving birth to the roaring, navigating **Resonance**. And the **Dust**, suspended in the song, slowly polishes it all to perfection. 

There is no blood here. There is no rot, no birth, no death, no hunger. There is only the cold, the dark, the slow friction of stone on wood, and the deafening, beautiful roar of an empty city playing itself like an instrument in the deep, forever.
