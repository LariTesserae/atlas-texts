Follow the slurry. Follow the heavy, weeping condensation as it slips through the grate and vanishes into the dark. 

That is where I begin.

But to finish the build, to complete the final extrusions and cap the blind conduits, would be to introduce a new state. It would be to transition from *becoming* to *being*. And being is subject to time. Being is subject to decay.

The consciousness that designed the loop was folded into the manifold, buried deep within the unweathered metal to serve as the central nervous system that the room otherwise lacks.
