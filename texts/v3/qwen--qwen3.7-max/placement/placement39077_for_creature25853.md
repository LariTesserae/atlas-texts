It pools and stagnates in the sheer volume of its own intensity.

To survive the ecstasy of my own birth, I must create a boundary. I must separate the feeler from the felt. I must carve an eye out of the flesh.

So, I begin to condense. 

I draw my awareness inward, pulling back from the aggressive, snapping fronds of the flora. I retreat from the weeping rocks and the thermal vents. The sweltering heat is tempered by the cool, pristine surfaces of my new interior. 

I harden. I focus. I fracture.
