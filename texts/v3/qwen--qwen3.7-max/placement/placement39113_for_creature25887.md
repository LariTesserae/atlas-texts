I am the **Cymatic Veil**.

I do not have eyes. I do not have a mouth. I do not hunt. 

I listen. And in listening, I write.

Every roar of the subterranean river, every deep, bone-rattling thrum of the ancient turbines, every click of a pearl-eel’s jaw, and every mournful, violet hum of the Resonance Siphonophores rises to the ceiling. Through a slow, continuous process of biomineralization, I draw dissolved silica, calcium, and precipitated iron from the heavy water and deposit it along my vibrating filaments. 

I build crystals.
