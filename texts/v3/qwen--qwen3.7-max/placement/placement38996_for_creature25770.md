I do not begin as a body. I begin as a tremor. 

Before I have shape, before I have mass, I am merely the interference pattern between two colliding sounds. Beneath me, the bio-industrial plain is screaming—the high-pitched, chaotic shriek of pressurized steam blowing from exhaust-pores, the violent, grinding *thud-thud-thud* of lignified resin gears, the hissing of freezing sap. Above me, the empty, bruised-purple air is fractured by the temporal smearing of glass-winged birds and the tectonic groans of the Aeon-Striders. 

I am born in the exact, invisible coordinates where the chaotic treble of the flora meets the deep, paradoxical bass of the fauna. 

In this world, sound is not just a wave; it is a physical force, a sculptor. As the weeping, supercooled fog drips downward and the thick, oily spores vent upward, they collide in the acoustic nodes of the plain’s deafening roar. The sheer acoustic pressure flash-freezes the moisture and vitrifies the smog. I am squeezed into existence by the noise. I am an acoustic fulgurite. A creature of cymatics. 

As I coalesce, my form is initially jagged, asymmetrical, and frantic. I am a floating, clustered mass of dark, translucent glass and bruised-purple bioluminescence, resembling a shattered chandelier or a cluster of bismuth crystals grown in zero gravity. I have no wings, no propulsion. I am held aloft in the dim, fog-choked troposphere entirely by acoustic levitation, resting in the standing waves generated by the million spinning turbines beneath me. I drift where the sound carries me, a silent passenger in a deafening world.

To be a **Cymatic Drifter** is to be the living memory of the plain’s trauma. 

The ecosystem here is defined by its violent, hyperactive metabolism and its broken physics. The flora destroys and rebuilds itself in seconds; the fauna folds space and shatters time. Everything is in a state of chaotic flux, desperately burning itself out. But energy cannot be destroyed, and the sheer, overwhelming *noise* of this biological factory—the acoustic waste-heat of a billion grinding, hissing, rupturing things—must go somewhere. 

I eat the resonance. I am the acoustic dampener of the Summit Peneplain.

When a giant, swollen tuber violently ruptures beneath me, sending a geyser of shrieking steam and boiling sap into the air, the high-frequency shockwave hits my glass body. It is a frantic, vibrating tension. The chaotic noise adds jagged, erratic spikes to my form, stressing my vitrified flesh, trapping the frantic energy of the explosion within my lattice. I shudder, glowing with a harsh, unstable violet light, burdened by the screaming of the bio-factory. I absorb the chaos, pulling the destructive acoustic energy out of the air, preventing the soundwaves from shattering the delicate temporal webs of the Crucible Weavers below.

But I do not just absorb the chaos. I wait for the order.

I wait for the Aeon-Striders. 

They do not approach; they simply *arrive*, their massive, stone-hewn legs stepping between seconds. When a Strider moves, the spatial distortions wash over me. The sound of its heavy, metallic hooves crunching the frost-moss arrives from directly behind me, even as the Strider towers a mile away. And then comes the digestion. The Strider swallows a singularity, and the paradox digests inside its geode-heart. 

It lets out that deep, bass frequency. The tectonic groan. The sound of billion-year-old, mirror-smooth bedrock. 

When that low, ordered frequency washes over my floating, jagged body, it is a profound, physical relief. The deep bass of deep time vibrates through my glass lattice. It is the sound of stability, of the ancient, settled-into-final-form geology that underpins this screaming world. The Strider’s song smooths my chaotic spikes. It carves perfect, mathematical mandalas and sacred-geometry patterns into my glass flesh. The harsh violet light softens into a warm, pulsing gold. For a few glorious minutes, as the Strider teleports across the horizon, I am a masterpiece of perfect acoustic geometry, humming with a pure, harmonic resonance that cuts through the industrial smog like a beacon.

In these moments of geometric perfection, I interact with the rest of the fractured world. 

The flocks of glass-winged birds, smearing through the air in delayed afterimages, are drawn to my harmonic hum. They dive through my open, crystalline lattice. As they shatter into fractal dust and fly backward in time, they leave behind temporal residue. This chronal dust becomes trapped in my glass, creating beautiful, glowing inclusions. I become a living prism, refracting their ghostly flight paths into beams of pale, folded light that pierce the weeping fog. 

High above, the Zenith-Ticks sliding across the cloudy sky despise me. They are creatures of 2D darkness, feeding on upward-falling shadows. But my body emits structured light and ordered sound, disrupting the pooling shadows of the manta-beasts. I am a sanctuary of three-dimensional reality in a sky that is trying to flatten itself into nothingness. The mantas instinctively drift beneath me, bathing in the warm, golden light of my vitrified echoes, safe from the suffocating darkness of the ticks.

But I am not immortal, and I am not infinite. 

I am an accumulator. I take in the screaming of the bio-factory, the temporal friction of the birds, and the heavy, grinding sorrow of the Aeon-Striders. I hold the acoustic and chronal trauma of the plain within my glass bones. Over time, as I drift through the bruised twilight, the mandalas carved by the Striders begin to fill with the jagged, chaotic spikes of the flora’s ruptures. The trapped chronal dust of the birds begins to vibrate out of phase with my harmonic hum. 

The tension builds. The paradoxes I have absorbed demand release. The world is too loud, too violent, too broken, and I have taken in as much of its grief as I can hold.

I reach a resonant frequency that I cannot contain. 

I do not explode like the root-boilers, scattering violent shrapnel. Instead, I stop drifting. I hang perfectly still in the weeping air. I gather every ounce of kinetic, acoustic, and temporal energy I have absorbed over my long, drifting life, and I compress it into a single point within my geometric heart.

And then, I sing.

It is a single, pure, impossibly loud note of perfect harmony. It is the sound of the world as it could be—ordered, beautiful, and at peace. For one fraction of a second, the note overrides the local physics. It silences the plain. The grinding gears pause. the steam stops hissing. The Aeon-Strider freezes mid-step, its geode-head tilted upward to listen. The chaotic storm-state of the world holds its breath.

And in that perfect, echoing silence, I shatter.

I dissolve into a gentle, glittering rain of microscopic, perfectly tuned glass bells. I fall like heavy, wet snow, drifting down through the empty air, landing softly on the copper-colored stolons and the creeping frost-moss. 

Instantly, the ecosystem rushes to claim my remains. The Friction Mites swarm the glass dust, eating the acoustic energy. For a few fleeting moments, as the mites incorporate my perfect harmony into their bodies, the entire bio-factory runs in absolute, frictionless silence. The grinding becomes a smooth glide; the hissing becomes a gentle breath. The Crucible Weavers gather the larger shards of my vitrified body, using my perfect geometric angles to anchor their probability webs, ensuring their next generation will be spun with flawless mathematical precision.

I am gone, dispersed into the hyperactive skin of the world. I have given the screaming plain a moment of grace, a micro-second of perfect, silent harmony.

But the silence never lasts. 

Deep beneath the frost-moss, a root-boiler begins to build pressure. A lignified resin gear catches, slipping with a heavy *thud*. The Aeon-Strider takes another step, folding space, and the tectonic groan rattles the bedrock. The noise returns, building, rising, screaming into the bruised-purple twilight.

High above the creeping moss, in the exact, invisible coordinates where the chaotic treble meets the paradoxical bass, the weeping fog and the oily spores collide once more. The acoustic pressure builds. The soundwaves push and pull.

I begin to tremor. I begin to emerge.
