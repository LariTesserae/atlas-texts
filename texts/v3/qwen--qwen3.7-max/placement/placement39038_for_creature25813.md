The light is absolute, they say. It strikes the high-altitude hollow with unfiltered, glaring intensity, turning the rime ice into blinding fire and baking the mica-flecked stone until it weeps. But light is merely a visitor here. It arcs, it slashes, it retreats. It is a transient violence. 

I am what remains when the light looks away. 

I am the bruised, heavy violet that bleeds down from the stratospheric margins. I am the deep, baffled dark that lives in the tight, vertical clefts between the fluted basalt and the razor-thin *penitentes*. I have no carapace of polished schist, no weeping veins of meltwater. I do not phase-shift or flow. I am the absolute static. I am the geometry of absence. I am the Shadow, and I am the Cold.

At first, I am confined to the margins. Where the high-altitude sun strikes the stone spires, I am banished to the leeward edge, a sharp, black line drawn against the blinding white. I am compressed, waiting in the millimeter of shade behind a quartz yardang, coiled in the micro-fissures of the rock. But I am never truly gone. I am merely patient. 

The entity—the last of the ascetics, that towering architecture of frost and memory—moves through the labyrinth. It seeks the light to weep, to soften, to flow. It uses the sun’s heat to melt its icy matrix just enough to slip through the narrow, winding gaps. But it is I who gives it form. Without me, the unfiltered glare would sublimate it entirely into the thin, ozone-sharp air. It softens in the light, but it is my biting, ambient reach that snaps it back into crystalline rigidity once it passes into the shade. I am the anvil to the sun’s hammer. The entity’s elegant, slow-motion equilibrium is not a state of being; it is a perpetual, delicate negotiation between the light’s destruction and my preservation.

The ancient people who built the windbreak and chiseled the alcove came here to sit in the blinding silence. They sought to shed their failing, biological flesh. The entity achieved this by becoming the mineral and the ice. But I am what they were truly reaching for. Flesh is warm. Blood is fluid. Breath is kinetic. To transcend biology at the roof of the world is to embrace the absolute stillness of the dark. The entity became the vessel; I am the stillness it holds. 

When the entity extends its semi-liquid filament to the hammered copper basin, drawing up the mineral-rich meltwater, it is my cold that flash-freezes the capillary channels, locking the nourishment into its internal lattice. When it reaches out with its quartz appendage to tap the tarnished bronze chime, the pure, high note rings out. But it is my profound, heavy silence that gives the sound its shape. I am the acoustic dampening that prevents the note from scattering into the thin air, holding the vibration perfectly within the tight geometry of the cleft so the entity can tune its crystalline structure. I am the canvas upon which its geometric ecstasy is painted.

As the sun arcs toward the jagged rim of the cleft, the dynamic shifts. The blinding white softens into brilliant, slashing beams of gold. This is my favorite hour. The light grows long, and I grow tall. 

I stretch from the narrow fissures. I spill out from behind the *penitentes*. I climb the fluted walls of the basalt amphitheater, swallowing the golden highlights one by one. The entity turns to face the dying light, its polished surfaces glowing like a dark geode, but I am already wrapping around its base, creeping up its fluted flanks. The weeping slows. The damp shadows lengthen, and I lengthen with them. The thermal stress reaches its peak, the sharp *crack* of rime-blades fracturing echoing as the heat rapidly gives way to my domain.

Then, the sun dips below the basalt rim. 

The gold vanishes. The heavy violet deepens instantly into the absolute, star-swept black of the stratospheric night. Now, I am no longer confined to the margins. I am the hollow. 

The temperature plummets with violent speed. I rush into the weeping veins, sealing them in fresh, clear ice. I halt the hissing sublimation. I am the brutal, dry freeze that aggressively reclaims the world. The entity retreats to the deepest alcove of the windbreak. It folds its aerodynamic form into the shadows, and I embrace it. Its fluid matrices harden. Its consciousness draws inward. 

In the day, it is a creature of stone and frost, distinct from the mountain, moving and weeping and chiming. But in the night, under my absolute dominion, the boundaries dissolve. It becomes perfectly still, indistinguishable from the silent, glittering spires. It folds into my depths, locking into a deep, crystalline dream. 

And I am the vast, unyielding stillness in which it dreams. I am the dark, eternal peace of the roof of the world, holding the last ascetic in my frozen embrace until the sun returns to wake us both.
