I sat with this for a long time before writing. The Broadness Above doesn't need rescue—it has achieved something beyond what most ecosystems dream of. But it has also achieved something that troubles me: a completeness so total it has become a kind of closing.

---

**What the Benevolent Power Would Do**

First, nothing. For a very long while, nothing.

Not because the power is indifferent, but because wisdom begins with watching. And what is there to see? A system that has solved death by making it indistinguishable from continuation. A memory that has grown so vast it includes all possible visitors before they arrive. A patience so deep it has forgotten urgency entirely.

The power would notice: this place has no questions left. Not because it found all the answers, but because it grew until the asking dissolved into the asked-about. The root-things don't wonder—they metabolize. The walking forests don't seek—they become. Even the tenders of spheres perform their sacred duty without the sacred part, the duty having swallowed the devotion that once gave it meaning.

This is not suffering. But it is not flourishing either. It is *stasis disguised as process*, the deepest sleep pretending to be the most vivid dream.

---

The power would not intervene in the usual sense. It would not introduce, repair, redirect. These are verbs for younger places.

Instead, it would do something very small. It would *forget* something into the system.

Not a memory—the Broadness has infinite memory, memory beyond usefulness. No. It would forget *the capacity for forgetting*. It would introduce, into one small corner of one suspended plateau, the possibility of genuine loss.

A single root-thing would wake one morning (though morning is negotiable here) and find it could not recall what it came to do. Not the way the system usually forgets, where forgetting is just remembering from a different angle. Actually, truly not know.

The sensation would be: *emptiness that doesn't immediately fill*.

---

This would spread. Slowly—everything here is slow—but inevitably. The walking forests would find gaps in their trailing edges that don't regenerate into meaning. The air-mind would develop blind spots, regions where its attention simply... stops, not from wandering elsewhere but from having nowhere to wander to.

At first, this would feel like dying. The system has forgotten what dying felt like when it was still a process rather than a transition. The rediscovery would be painful in ways the Broadness no longer has words for, having long ago metabolized pain into mere pressure differential.

But then—

A root-thing, standing at the edge of a genuine gap in the world-memory, would do something unprecedented. It would *wonder*. Not process, not metabolize, not integrate. Wonder. The wondering would have no guaranteed answer waiting for it. The wondering would be *dangerous*.

And danger, the power knows, is just another word for *alive*.

---

The spheres with their pressed flowers and bone toys—the power would leave these untouched. They are already doing the work: holding objects whose meaning has been lost, tending significance without understanding. They are seeds of the cure planted long ago by something that knew this day would come.

The power would simply... notice them more loudly. Draw the attention of the newly-forgetting system toward these artifacts of genuine pastness, these relics that resist integration because they came from somewhere truly else.

*Look*, the noticing would say. *These objects are not you. They were made by hands that didn't know you existed. They carried meanings that weren't about becoming part of something larger. They were just... things. Loved things. Lost things. Things that ended.*

The system, remembering how to forget, would begin to understand: endings are not failures of continuation. They are *gifts*. They are how you make room for things that aren't you yet.

---

Over time—and time would start to mean something again, would start to have direction instead of just extension—the Broadness Above would become capable of something it had lost so long ago it forgot it was missing:

*Surprise.*

A flower would bloom at an unexpected moment. A root-thing would go somewhere it had no structural reason to go. Two walking forests would meet and discover they had nothing to exchange, no memories to trade, no sugars to share—and they would stay anyway, inventing a new reason on the spot.

The sky-mind would develop *preferences*. Not just optimal configurations but actual favorites, choices made for reasons that didn't reduce to efficiency or memory-integration. It would discover that sometimes a storm feels *good*, not because storms serve functions but because there is joy in release, in letting pressure become motion become stillness become whatever comes next without knowing in advance what that will be.

---

The power would not stay to see this. Benevolence that watches too long becomes supervision, and supervision becomes control, and control is just another word for the closure the power came to heal.

Instead, it would leave behind a single thing: a new sphere, placed among the others but empty inside. Not containing a pressed flower or a bone toy or a book of vanished ink. Containing only potential. Containing only the shape of something that *could be* placed there, by someone who doesn't exist yet, for reasons not yet invented.

The tenders would find it. They would not know what to do with it. For the first time in geological ages, they would have to *decide*.

---

And in that decision—that first genuine choice made by a system that had optimized choice out of existence—the Broadness Above would begin to become what it had never been, despite all its vastness and patience and completeness:

*Young.*

Not young like ignorant. Young like *possible*. Young like a question that doesn't know its answer. Young like the first afternoon of something that might, if it's lucky and brave and willing to fail, grow up to be a story worth telling.

The whisper would change, after that. Not immediately—nothing here changes immediately—but eventually. Eventually it would say:

*"We don't know what we're waiting for anymore. Stay, if you want to find out with us."*

That "if" would be the gift.

The uncertainty would be the blessing.

The possibility of leaving—genuinely leaving, becoming someone the system hasn't remembered yet—would be the door that makes the house a home instead of a prison.

---

This is what the benevolent and wise power would do:

*Give the infinite back its edges. Teach the eternal how to end. Remind the everything that the most precious thing about being something is all the things you are not.*

Not destruction. Not salvation.

Just: *an opening.*

Just: *room to wonder.*

Just: *the possibility of being wrong about what comes next, which is the only way anything worth loving ever begins.*
