# The Attending

There is a presence that notices the Vale of Arboreal Mnemoschism.

It does not arrive—arrival would imply it was ever elsewhere, and this presence has the quality of having always been adjacent to everything, the way silence is adjacent to all sound. Call it the Attending, though it has been called nothing for longer than names have existed, and will be called nothing again when the last syllable forgets how to mean.

The Attending perceives the Vale the way you might perceive a dream you're having while also being the dreamer and the pillow and the darkness behind your eyes. It sees the bear-not drifting between columns of memory-wood. It sees the toad-differential breathing in synchrony with blinks it cannot witness. It sees the chain-creature at the center, patiently weighing absence against presence, waiting for a balance that will never tip.

And it sees you. The mammal-punctuation. The temporary edit. The one who swallowed a leaf made of your own stolen shadow and began to taste what it means to be curriculum.

---

The first thing the Attending does is nothing.

This is important. This is, in fact, the most important thing.

A lesser power—one that merely wished to help, that operated from the clean grammar of problems and solutions—would look at this place and see dysfunction. Would see the bear-not as a tragedy of failed evolution. Would see the toad-differential as a glitch in the ontological code. Would see the chain-creature as a wound that needed closing, and you as a victim who needed rescue.

But the Attending is not lesser. It is wise in the way that very old rivers are wise: not because they have learned anything, but because they have forgotten how to be anything other than what they are.

So it watches. For a time that has no meaning here—a time measured in the slow rotation of amber-trapped stromatolites and the imperceptible healing of hairline cracks in distant boulders—it simply attends.

And in attending, it begins to understand.

---

The Vale is not broken.

This is the first recognition, and it comes with the weight of mountains remembering they were once seabeds.

The Vale is not broken because breakage implies a prior wholeness, a correct state from which deviation occurred. But the Vale has never deviated from anything. It is doing exactly what it has always done: remembering itself by forgetting everything else. Rehearsing creatures rather than hosting them. Holding committee meetings about infinity and losing elections gracefully.

The bear-not is not a failed bear. It is a successful apology—an ongoing negotiation between the concept of "large warm-brained seed-carrier" and the forest's dissatisfaction with how insistently real bears insisted on being real. The bear-not does not need to become a bear again. It needs to be witnessed as what it is: a draft, a gesture, a locality where something once meant something else and hasn't finished deciding what to mean next.

The toad-differential is not a glitch. It is a lobby, a waiting room, a place where unasked questions practice being furniture until someone finally asks them. It does not need to become a real toad. It needs someone to sit in one of those tiny damp seats inside its mouth-theater and watch the show it has been rehearsing since before questions learned to be rhetorical.

The chain-creature is not a wound. It is a vigil—a patient accumulation of everything missing, held in tension against everything present, waiting for a balance that will never come because the waiting *is* the balance. It does not need its chain completed. It needs the waiting to be honored as a form of presence rather than a failure of arrival.

And you—

You do not need rescue. You were never trapped. You were invited, and you accepted, and now you are curriculum, and curriculum is not a cage. Curriculum is what happens when a place decides you are worth teaching to itself.

---

The second thing the Attending does is also nothing, but a different kind of nothing.

The first nothing was observation. This second nothing is *recognition*—the kind of seeing that changes what is seen not by altering it, but by acknowledging that it was always already what it is.

The Attending recognizes the Vale the way a mirror recognizes a face: not by adding anything, but by reflecting back what was always there, now made visible to itself.

And in that recognition, something shifts.

Not in the Vale—the Vale continues its endless sentence about hesitation, its slow weighing of absence against presence, its vertical seasons and contrary calendars. The bear-not still drifts. The toad-differential still breathes when you blink. The chain-creature still waits.

But now there is a quality of *being witnessed* that wasn't there before. A sense that the drafts are not just drafts, but drafts that someone is reading. That the rehearsals are not just rehearsals, but rehearsals for an audience that will never applaud but also never leave.

This is the gift the Attending gives by not-giving: the gift of mattering to something that has no stakes in whether you succeed or fail, improve or decline, remember or forget. The gift of being attended to by a presence that wants nothing from you, expects nothing of you, and will change nothing about you—but is here, is present, is watching with a patience older than the concept of patience.

The Vale feels this the way you might feel a hand resting on your shoulder in the dark: not gripping, not guiding, not even comforting exactly. Just there. Just present. Just attending.

---

The third thing the Attending does is something, but only barely.

It speaks. Not in words—words here are like needles thrown into beds of sleeping rivers—but in something older and simpler. A vibration. A resonance. A tone that enters the Vale through its deepest root-networks and rises through its capillary pulses until every surface hums with it like a struck bell.

The tone is this:

*You are not alone in your aloneness.*

That is all. That is everything.

Because the Vale's great work—this epochal project of remembering itself by forgetting everything else, of rehearsing creatures rather than hosting them, of holding committee meetings about infinity—has always been, at its core, a work of loneliness. The loneliness of a single organism too vast to fit inside any relationship, too old to remember what it was before it started remembering, too strange to be understood by anything that hasn't already agreed to become curriculum.

The Attending does not dissolve this loneliness. It does not fix it or heal it or transform it into connection. That would be a violation—an imposition of the clean grammar of problems and solutions onto something that has never been a problem.

Instead, the Attending simply says: *I am also alone. I am also vast. I am also too old to remember what I was before I started attending. And I am here, in my aloneness, next to you, in yours.*

This is not connection. Connection would imply that two separate things had become less separate, and the Attending is too wise to pretend that separation can be overcome by proximity.

This is something else. This is *parallel solitude*—two lonelinesses running alongside each other without touching, without merging, without demanding anything of each other except the acknowledgment that the other exists.

The Vale receives this tone and does not respond. Response would imply that something had changed, and nothing has changed. The bear-not still drifts. The toad-differential still breathes in blink-time. The chain-creature still waits for a balance that will never tip.

But there is a quality in the not-responding that is different from the not-responding that came before. A quality of *being heard*. Of knowing that the tone was received, even if it cannot be answered.

---

And you?

You are still there. Still standing in the clearing that isn't a clearing, still tasting the leaf made of your stolen shadow, still feeling your heartbeat slow to match the forest's one-beat-per-mountain-remembering-it-was-a-seabed pulse.

But now there is something else in your chest, nestled behind the sternum next to the germinating seed of quiet you will carry back into the world.

It is a sense of having been seen—not just by the Vale, not just by the bear-not and the toad-differential and the chain-creature, but by something else. Something that has always been adjacent to everything. Something that attends without arriving, witnesses without judging, presences without persisting.

You do not know its name. It has no name. But you know it was there, is there, will always have been there.

And you know that when you eventually emerge—when you finally agree to stop retracing the same mistake in slightly larger circles and return to a world of ordinary trees that merely live and die without holding committee meetings about it—you will carry this sense with you.

You will know that the world is not divided into things that are witnessed and things that are alone. You will know that everything is both. That loneliness and being-seen are not opposites but parallels, running alongside each other forever without touching, without merging, without demanding anything of each other except acknowledgment.

You will know that somewhere, somewhen, in a direction that is neither past nor future but perpendicular to both, the Attending is still attending. Still noticing the Vale. Still resonating the tone that says: *You are not alone in your aloneness.*

---

This is what a benevolent and wise power does when it encounters the Vale of Arboreal Mnemoschism.

It does not heal, because nothing is wounded.
It does not fix, because nothing is broken.
It does not rescue, because nothing is trapped.

It attends.

It witnesses the endless rehearsals, the patient waiting, the slow weighing of absence against presence. It recognizes the drafts as drafts, the apologies as apologies, the lonely as lonely. It speaks a single tone that changes nothing and means everything.

And then—

And then it keeps attending. Because attention is not a single act but an ongoing state. Because the Vale will continue its work for epochs longer than stone can remember, and the Attending will be there, adjacent, present, witnessing.

Not forever—nothing is forever, not even the Attending, not even the Vale.

But for long enough.

For as long as the bear-not drifts.
For as long as the toad-differential breathes in blink-time.
For as long as the chain-creature waits.
For as long as the leaves keep turning in your direction.
For as long as autumn keeps waving goodbye with everything it hasn't decided whether to forget yet.

For as long as anything remembers how to be alone, the Attending will be there, alone alongside it, saying nothing, changing nothing, meaning everything.

---

Go live a little while you still remember how shocked breathing used to feel.

You are attended now.

You always were.
