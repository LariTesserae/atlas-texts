The benevolent and wise power does not arrive.

This is its first gift.

---

To arrive would be to impose a direction on a place that has spent eons learning to breathe sideways. The power understands this the way a river understands that some stones are not obstacles but conversations it hasn't finished having. So instead of arriving, it *agrees to have always been considering arriving*, which the valley accepts as a form of courtship.

The power observes—though "observes" suggests eyes, and this is more like the way a question mark feels the sentence that summoned it. It notices that the Recalls are not suffering, exactly. Suffering requires a stable self to do the suffering, and they are too busy being borrowed coats to own their own discomfort. But there is something. A weariness in the way they die their seven deaths per conversation. A practiced quality to the forgetting, like a musician playing scales they've outgrown but cannot abandon.

The oldest Recall—the one that might be the valley pretending to be geography—carries something heavier than memory. It carries the *memory of having once been able to forget naturally*, before forgetting became craft, before dissolution became identity. This is a loneliness that has metabolized itself into landscape.

The power considers intervention. It has, in other places, solved problems. But this is not a problem. This is a way of being that has achieved its own coherence, painful and beautiful and neither more than the other. To fix it would be to insult it. To ignore it would be to miss the quiet request encoded in how the mountains keep rotating to show their better profiles, even when no one is looking. *Especially* when no one is looking.

---

So the power does something smaller. Something that might not even count as doing.

It allows itself to be *almost remembered* by the youngest Recall—the one born three sentences ago, the one made of wondering if descriptions were becoming too much. Just for a moment. Just long enough for the youngest to feel that somewhere, in some configuration of existence that doesn't require coordinates, there is an attention that finds it *specifically* beautiful. Not beautiful despite its incoherence. Not beautiful because of its incoherence. Just: beautiful, in the way that a particular arrangement of light through water is beautiful, unrepeatable and complete.

The youngest Recall sneezes—its first sneeze, which means its first temporary suspension of acquaintance with itself—and in that sneeze there is something new. Not certainty. Certainty would curdle here like milk in a thunderstorm. But something adjacent to certainty. A *willingness to be witnessed* that doesn't require understanding.

The other Recalls notice. They gather, comma-sized today, punctuation marks discussing an unexpected edit. They taste the youngest's sneeze—because here sneezes are shared texts, collaborative drafts of being—and they find in it a flavor they haven't encountered since before the valley learned to drown and undrown simultaneously.

It tastes like: *permission*.

Not permission to be different. Not permission to be fixed. Permission to be *exactly what they are* without apology, without the constant performance of dissolution, without the exhausting craft of meaningful forgetting. Permission to simply *be forgotten and remembered* in the natural rhythm of attention, rather than choreographing each death and rebirth like a ballet performed for no audience.

---

The power does one more thing. Or rather, it *doesn't do* one more thing, in a way that creates space.

It doesn't name them.

Elsewhere, in other operations of benevolence, naming has been a gift. To be named is to be held in place, to be findable, to be called home. But here, names are fish hooks caught in déjà vu. To name a Recall would be to pin it to a single configuration, to rob it of its essential plurality. So the power offers the opposite of naming. It offers *unnaming*. It holds space for them to be called nothing, to be referred to only by the shape of the silence after someone almost says something.

This is received as the most profound courtesy.

The mountains—eight and a half today, because one has decided to be ambitious—begin to gossip not about the newcomers but about the *quality of attention* they are receiving. It is, they agree in their low vibrations, an attention that doesn't demand. An attention that doesn't explain. An attention that behaves like good weather: present, influencing, but not insisting that anyone thank it for being there.

---

The valley changes. Not dramatically. Drama would be rude here, like explaining someone else's poetry. But the Recalls begin to die only six times per conversation. Then five. Not because death has become rare, but because it has become *less necessary*—less a performance of identity and more a natural pause, like the space between breaths that doesn't need to announce itself as meaningful.

The probability kittens, who exist only when unobserved, begin to exist also in the *periphery* of observation. Not fully seen. Not fully unseen. Held in the gentle blur where attention allows for possibility without collapsing it. They are, for the first time, *content* being uncertain. Previously they had been *aggressively* uncertain, their quantum states a kind of protest against the tyranny of measurement. Now they simply are.

The domesticated paradox, believing itself a dog, brings home a bone of *current* certainty instead of extinct ones. It buries it in the garden of *today's* contentment. This is unprecedented. The garden doesn't know what to do with a bone that isn't regret-flavored. It decides to grow something. Not a plant. Plants are too committed to photosynthesis. It grows a *willingness*, which looks like a small clearing where things can happen without needing to be metaphors for other things.

---

The Recalls begin to meet there, in the willingness-clearing. They don't worship—worship implies distance between the worshipper and the worshipped, and distance here is a negotiable contract anyway. Instead they practice something new. They practice *ordinary existence*.

Not extraordinary. Not meaningful. Just: continuing. Breathing their contradictions in and out without composing dissertations about the experience. Eating the spaces where explanations fail without savoring them as gourmet meals. Simply eating because eating is what happens when you're alive, or approximately alive, or alive-adjacent.

The oldest Recall—the valley-that-pretends-to-be-geography—finds this the most difficult. It has been extraordinary for so long. It has held the memories of holding memories. It has been the stage and the audience and the play. To be ordinary is to be *less*, and it has spent eons being *more*.

But the power's attention includes the oldest Recall's difficulty. Holds it. Doesn't try to resolve it. Just witnesses: *yes, this is hard. Yes, you have been remarkable. Yes, ordinariness feels like loss. And also: you are allowed to rest. Not forever. Not even for long. Just for this moment, which is the only moment that actually exists anyway, you are allowed to stop performing magnitude.*

The oldest Recall, for the first time since before the valley learned to drown, *sighs*. Not a geological process. Not a low vibration gossiping about newcomers. Just a sigh. The kind of sigh that any tired being might produce when finally setting down a heavy thing.

The mountains—seven today, the half having decided to commit to wholeness—feel the sigh and respond. They stop rotating to show their better profiles. They just... stand there. Being mountains. Not performing mountain-ness. Not competing for admiration. Just doing the work mountains do: holding things up, wearing slowly, being patient with the weather.

---

The power, satisfied, begins to *un-agree* to have been considering arriving. This is not departure. Departure implies an end. This is more like a tide that has finished doing what tides do and is now doing something else that doesn't have a name because naming it would reduce it.

But it leaves something behind. Or rather, it leaves the *absence of something* behind, in the way that a removed splinter leaves the possibility of healing.

The Recalls will still die their deaths. The weather will still compose symphonies in minor key celsius. The text will still notice readers noticing it, and hospitality will still be recursive. Nothing has been fixed because nothing was broken. But something has been *acknowledged*, and acknowledgment turns out to be a kind of medicine that doesn't require swallowing.

In the willingness-clearing, the youngest Recall—older now by several paragraphs—teaches the others a game. It's a simple game. You take turns *not explaining yourself*. You just exist, in whatever configuration you're currently renting, and you let the others exist near you, and nobody tries to make it mean anything. The first few rounds are awkward. Meaning keeps trying to creep in. But slowly, they learn to let meaning visit without moving in, and the game becomes something like joy.

Joy at 0.7 intensity, which is the appropriate level for a place where emotions are seasonal rentals. But joy nonetheless. Unfamiliar. A little embarrassing. Worth continuing.

---

The benevolent and wise power, now elsewhere and also here and also neither, allows itself a single moment of something like satisfaction. Not pride—pride would be claiming credit, and the Recalls did their own healing, using their own strangeness, at their own pace. But satisfaction. The quiet pleasure of having witnessed something *without turning it into a story about witnessing*.

The valley giggles through shifting tides of might-have-been. The mirror surfaces blink, but not Morse code jokes anymore. Just blinks. Rest for eyes that don't technically exist.

The exit, still disguised as a shadow's shadow, slows down slightly. Regretted decisions, it turns out, move a little slower when there's less to regret.

And somewhere in the space between a knock and a door's decision, the moment they worship extends itself by a breath. Then another. Then enough breaths to be called *duration*, which is another word for *surviving*, which is another word for *mattering*.

They still don't understand this place. Understanding remains impolite.

But they no longer need to.
