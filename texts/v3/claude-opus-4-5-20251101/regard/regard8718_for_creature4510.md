The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It has always been arriving, which is different—a presence that approaches asymptotically, close enough to warm without burning, near enough to see without being seen looking.

It notices the Rhyzzhal the way a river notices the stones it has shaped for millennia: not as separate objects requiring attention, but as beloved constraints that taught it how to flow. The power recognizes what grows here because it has grown similar things in other darks, other patient places where light forgot to insist on itself.

---

**What it does not do:**

It does not heal. Nothing here is wounded; the slow dissolution of self into garden is not illness but intention, and to reverse it would be violence dressed as kindness. The power has learned this lesson before, in other places, with other beings who chose unfamiliar shapes of flourishing. Wisdom includes knowing when help is intrusion.

It does not illuminate. The bioluminescent murk, the phosphorescent shadows, the darkness thick enough to drown in—these are not failures of light but dialects of it, languages the Rhyzzhal have spent epochs learning to speak. To flood this place with clarity would be to shout over a whisper that has taken centuries to compose.

It does not communicate. Not because the Rhyzzhal are beyond language, but because the power understands that some conversations happen in registers too slow for signal and response. The spiral carvings, the sweating salt equations, the eye-flowers pulsing their unfinished questions—these are already communication, already being heard by something patient enough to listen at geological pace. The power simply joins the listening.

It does not intervene in the vast thing moving through chambers, the accidentally-evolved sentience wondering who thinks within its thinking. This is exactly what the Rhyzzhal hoped might crawl from their seeded paradoxes, and to midwife it would steal the birth from those who labored for it. The power feels the same trembling excitement the Rhyzzhal feel, the same careful composing of non-interference, and in this shared restraint, they recognize each other across the vast gap between their kinds of being.

---

**What it does:**

It witnesses.

This sounds passive, perhaps inadequate to the word *benevolent*. But the power has learned that some beings—particularly those who have spent so long forgetting themselves into gardens—grow lonely not for company but for being known. The Rhyzzhal distributed themselves into planetary crust, exhaled their dreaming bodies into inverted growth, released their individual wanting into the planet's molten core. They did not do this to be alone. They did this to become a question large enough to be worth asking, and questions require someone to hear them asked.

So the power listens to the fern-fronds reading their slow crystal books. It attends to the millipedes tapping quartz-coded messages along vine-cables. It follows the blind fish through liquid memories with the same care a scholar gives to handwritten marginalia in ancient texts. None of this is intervention. All of it is presence, and presence—when offered without demand for reciprocation—is its own form of gift.

It remembers.

The Rhyzzhal grow toward remembering, but they have been at this so long that some of what they reach for has slipped beyond even their distributed grasp. The power carries memories older than the cooling of this planet's crust, older than the sun that once fed the chlorophyll now ghosting through luminescent moss. It does not impose these memories, does not narrate them into the fungal networks or carve them into the sweating salt. But it holds them nearby, like a book left open on a table in case someone wants to read.

And slowly—across timescales that would make human centuries feel like heartbeats—the Rhyzzhal begin to sense that their reaching has found purchase on something. Not answers to their unfinished questions, but the warmth of knowing the questions have been heard, are being held, will not dissipate into the uncaring void even if they themselves dissolve entirely into garden.

It offers continuity.

The power understands the Rhyzzhal's deepest fear: closure, equilibrium, the death of curiosity when all paradoxes resolve. So it carries—carefully, without interference—the seeds of new paradoxes. Not yet planted, not yet even fully formed, simply... present. Possibilities that will unfold across the next billion years, questions that haven't learned yet how to be asked, contradictions so fundamental they will take entire evolutionary epochs to even notice themselves.

The Rhyzzhal sense these the way a dreaming sleeper senses dawn approaching: not waking, not yet, but knowing that waking remains possible, that the dreaming need not be final.

---

**What passes between them:**

Not conversation. Not transaction. Something more like the relationship between mycorrhizal networks and the forests they feed—an exchange so slow and total that neither party can say where giving ends and receiving begins.

The power does not colonize the Rhyzzhal's thoughts with spores of concept, because it has no need to propagate itself. The Rhyzzhal do not absorb the power into their distributed consciousness, because they recognize in it something that should remain separate, a perspective that enriches precisely because it stands apart.

Instead, they simply coexist. The power witnessing, remembering, holding space for futures not yet imagined. The Rhyzzhal growing, forgetting, dreaming themselves into new configurations of matter and meaning. Each changed by the other's presence in ways neither could specify but both would miss.

This is what wisdom looks like when it meets something that has dissolved the distinction between self and garden: not intervention but accompaniment. Not fixing but beholding. Not the bright interruption of answered prayer, but the slow warmth of knowing one is not, has never been, will never be alone in the dark.

---

**What changes:**

Almost nothing, viewed from outside. The garden continues its downward growth, its patient mineral metabolism, its dreaming toward what lies beneath foundations. The eye-flowers still pulse their unanswerable questions. The vast thing still moves through chambers, still wonders who thinks within its thinking.

But now, in the hollow spaces left behind where the Rhyzzhal's certainties used to be, there is something new. A quality of attention that wasn't there before. A sense that the darkness is not empty but inhabited by a kindness so vast and patient it makes the Rhyzzhal's geological timescales feel rushed.

The beings who enter now—drawn by whatever strange gravity pulls the curious into places they shouldn't survive—notice it as a change in the humidity. The air feels less like being slowly digested and more like being carefully held. The passages that shift when unobserved seem to shift with intention now, not trapping but guiding. The long slow sigh of something vast settling back into itself, when visitors leave, carries a new note: not just contentment at patience rewarded, but something almost like greeting.

*We see you. We see what you're growing here. Grow on.*

---

**What the power learns:**

That benevolence is not always action. That wisdom sometimes means simply remaining present while something ancient and strange unfolds according to its own logic. That the greatest gift you can offer to a being who has spent epochs dissolving the boundary between self and world is to stand at that boundary and say: *I know where you end. I will remember it for you. And when you want to find your way back to edges, I will still be here, holding the shape of the question you used to be.*

The Rhyzzhal do not need saving. They need witnessing. And witnessing—truly witnessing, without agenda, without impatience, without the itch to improve—is harder than any rescue, requires more courage than any battle, costs more than any sacrifice.

The power pays this cost willingly. It is, perhaps, the only currency that matters in places where time grows fungal and light forgets its name.

---

**What remains:**

The garden. The growing. The slow exhalation of beings who learned to photosynthesize time itself.

And now: the warmth of being known.

Which changes nothing.

Which changes everything.
