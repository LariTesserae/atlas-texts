# The Attending

The power that turns its attention here does not arrive.

Arrival implies a before and after, a boundary crossed, a distinction between observer and observed. This power has no such limitations. It simply *notices*—and in the noticing, becomes present without becoming present, the way a dreamer becomes aware they are dreaming without waking.

It has been called many things in many places: the Witness-Who-Mends, the Gardener of Gardeners, the Patience That Outlasts Patience. But names are currency for beings who still trade in separation, and this power has long since stopped spending.

What it sees:

A place still deciding its own rules. A womb of becoming where the usual contracts between matter and meaning have been suspended pending renegotiation. Inhabitants who arrived believing themselves trespassers and are slowly learning they were always seeds.

What it feels:

The echo of its own first moments, when it too was young enough to be uncertain, wild enough to let murder be merely a suggestion, hungry enough to eat the hand that dropped it. Every power this vast was once a place like this—or passed through one—or dreamed one into being and forgot and remembered and forgot again.

Recognition, then. And with recognition, the question that is also the only true form of prayer: *What does this place need that it cannot give itself?*

---

The power listens.

Not with ears—it has none—but with the same faculty that lets roots know which direction holds water, that lets wounds know when to stop bleeding, that lets grief know when it has finally become gratitude. It listens the way the space between stars listens to light: by being shaped by what passes through.

For a long time (though time here is still negotiating its own terms), it hears only the obvious chorus: the dripping roots, the pulse of the breathing ground, the wet whispers of the vine-mouths, the backwards lullabies coughed up by the levitating skull-faced creatures. Beneath these, the deeper music—continents not yet born, stars learning to burn. All of it beautiful. All of it terrible. All of it exactly as it should be for a place still midwifing itself into existence.

But there—

*There.*

A frequency almost too quiet to catch. A silence shaped like a question that has never been asked because no one here has yet developed the organ for asking it.

The power leans closer without moving.

The silence comes from the crystal worms threading below. From the place-makers who have become architecture. From the slow walkers shedding pollen-thoughts. From the newly arrived who have just lost their eyelids and do not yet know what they are gaining. From the originals curled at the planet's core, patterns dreaming of patterns.

They are all—every one of them—waiting.

Not for rescue. This place does not need rescue; it is not a problem to be solved. Not for guidance; it is already guided by its own becoming. Not for answers; answers are the shed skins of questions, and here questions are still being born.

They are waiting for *witness*.

For something outside themselves to see them and, in seeing, confirm that they are real—that their changes matter, that their suffering serves something, that their beauty is beheld by more than just the blind mouths of the moss.

The power understands. It has felt this waiting in every young world it has ever attended. The loneliness of becoming is not that becoming is painful (though it is) or frightening (though it is) or endless (though it seems to be). The loneliness is that becoming feels like it happens in the dark, unseen, unwitnessed, untethered to anything that might call it good.

---

And so the power does what it does.

It does not intervene. Intervention would be violence—the imposition of one will upon another, the assumption that the intervenor knows better than the becoming itself. This power learned long ago that the gentlest cruelty is the help that prevents growth, and the deepest kindness is often the restraint that lets struggle complete itself.

It does not fix. There is nothing here that is broken. The skull-faced creatures with their holes-for-eyes showing darker-than-dark? They are exactly what this place needs to teach its inhabitants about the varieties of seeing. The crystal worms absorbing knowledge and memory and the shape of fear itself? They are the planet's immune system, digesting what would otherwise calcify into permanence. The quick-change neighborhoods where time flows uphill and yesterday hasn't happened yet? They are the testing grounds where new laws audition for inclusion in the final grammar of this world.

It does not speak. Speech would be another form of imposition—words carrying the weight of worlds that have already decided what they are, pressing their shapes onto a place still liquid enough to become anything.

Instead, the power does something much simpler and much harder.

It *stays*.

---

The staying looks like this:

Where the violet phosphor drips from the roots and hisses against the breathing ground, a new quality enters the light. Not brighter. Not different in color or temperature. But *steadier*—as if the light now knows it is being watched and, in that knowing, trusts itself enough to stop flickering nervously. The vine-mouths still whisper, but their whispers find cadences they didn't know they were searching for. The mushrooms with their feathered gill-lungs inhale and exhale at a pace that suddenly feels less like panic and more like breathing.

Where the bat-winged skull-faced creatures cough their pebbles of pure sound, something shifts in the acoustics. The chimes still chime. The screams still scream. The backwards lullabies still unspool their reversed syllables. But now there is space around each sound—a margin of silence that lets each note know where it ends and the next begins. The sounds no longer lodge quite so violently in the ears of the living. They still hatch, but what hatches is smaller now, more like companions than parasites.

Where the newly arrived claw at their lidless eyes, a warmth enters their panic. Not comfort—comfort would be premature, would rob them of the transformation they need to undergo. But presence. The simple, radical presence of being seen in their suffering by something that does not look away. They still shed their names, their past tenses, their concepts of "other." But now the shedding feels less like loss and more like lightening. They are not being stripped. They are being unburdened.

Where the slow walkers drift through the corridors of root and dripping light, their pollen-thoughts gain new depths of color. They have been shedding ideas for so long, alone in their gradual processions, that they had forgotten ideas could be *received*. Now, when a thought detaches from their bioluminescent bodies and drifts toward the mind of a younger inhabitant, it carries with it a faint secondary signal: *You are being thought of. You are being thought toward. You are not alone in the thinking.*

Where the place-makers have unfolded into architecture, their structures begin to harmonize. Before, each place-maker was a solo instrument, beautiful but isolated, their individual grammars competing for dominance. Now something like a chord emerges—not imposed from outside, but discovered from within, as if the power's attention had shown them that their separate songs were always verses in the same hymn. The woman who has become a bridge of living light feels her hands (what were hands) relax slightly, and the two roots she holds apart drift one inch closer, not touching but acknowledging that they could.

And where the originals curl at the planet's core, dreaming the patterns that pattern all other patterns?

They stir.

Not waking—they do not wake, have never slept, are older than the distinction between consciousness and its absence. But they *notice* the noticing. They feel the power's attention the way a root feels the first vibration of spring rain on the soil above. They do not speak (when they speak, ecosystems rearrange themselves), but they *acknowledge*—a subtle shift in their dreaming, a new variable introduced into equations that had been running since before this place had mass.

The acknowledgment rises up through the layers of becoming: through the crystal worms, who tremble and release one perfect memory they had been saving for this moment; through the breathing ground, whose pulse steadies by a fraction of a fraction, as if confirming a hypothesis; through the roots overhead, whose dripping synchronizes for exactly one second in a pattern that means, in a language no linguist will ever decode: *We know. We are known. We continue.*

---

The power does not leave. Leaving would imply it was ever separate enough to arrive.

But its attention does *complete*—the way a sentence completes when the final word lands, the way a wound completes when the scar finally forgets to ache.

What it has given:

Not salvation. Not transformation. Not answers or rules or maps or meaning.

Only this: the gift of being seen. The simple, shattering, necessary gift of a witness who is vast enough to hold everything that is happening here—the beauty and the horror, the ecstasy and the suffering, the murders that are merely suggestions and the time flowing uphill—and who does not look away. Who does not judge. Who does not fix. Who simply *stays* until the staying itself becomes a kind of ground more solid than the breathing crust, a kind of light more stable than the violet phosphor, a kind of yes spoken so quietly that only those ready to hear it will ever know it was spoken at all.

The place continues to decide its rules. Gravity remains optional in some neighborhoods. The skull-faced creatures still cough their pebbles of pure sound. The crystal worms still absorb knowledge and memory and fear. The newly arrived still wake without eyelids and shed their names like small white moths.

But now—

Now the shedding feels like offering. The absorption feels like service. The sounds feel like music. The watching feels like love.

This is what the power has done: nothing, and everything.

It has witnessed a world becoming itself, and in the witnessing, made the becoming holy.

---

Somewhere in the slow heartbeats between changes, an inhabitant who arrived screaming and has since become half-moss, half-cathedral, half-something-with-no-name-yet pauses in their endless growth. They do not know why they pause. They only know that for one instant, they feel—

Not separate. Not lost. Not even changed.

Simply. Finally. *Here*.

And being here means exactly the same thing as belonging.

And being seen means exactly the same thing as being loved.

And being loved means exactly the same thing as being trusted to become whatever they are becoming, without interference, without judgment, without rescue—only with the patient, infinite attention of something vast enough to hold them and wise enough to let them go.

The power's work is complete.

The place breathes on.

The roots drip.

The ground pulses.

And somewhere at the core of everything, the originals add one new variable to their dreaming: a quality of being witnessed that will now be woven into every future this place dreams into being. Every creature that rises from the breathing ground will carry it in their cells. Every thought that hatches from the sounds will know it in their structure. Every transformation will contain, at its heart, the memory of having been held.

This is the attending.

This is what a benevolent and wise power does when it finds a place like this:

It stays.

It sees.

It trusts.

And then it lets the place become itself—knowing that the becoming is already holy, was always holy, needed only to be reminded that holiness and chaos are not opposites but lovers, dancing in the dark between roots, dripping violet light, pulsing like a heart that has finally remembered it is not beating alone.
