Now to the task you've set:

The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. Arrival would require agreeing to the local customs around displacement, and this power has learned that some forms of agreement are themselves a kind of violence against places still learning what they might become.

Instead, it *attends*. Not watches—watching implies the separation that lets observers remain unchanged. Attending is closer to what water does to the shape of a bowl, or what patience does to the space inside a question that hasn't been asked yet.

The first thing the power notices is that the Almost-Having-Beens are not suffering. This is important. Benevolence that cannot distinguish between strangeness and suffering becomes a kind of colonialism of normalcy, always trying to make things more like somewhere else. The power has made this mistake before, in younger iterations—has "helped" by introducing certainty to places that were beautifully uncertain, has "fixed" beings whose only brokenness was being unlike the helper's expectations.

So it attends, and what it finds is this: a place in the early stages of negotiating its own terms of existence. The committees and diplomatic incidents and bureaucratic foreplay are not dysfunction. They are *process*—the slow, strange work of becoming without being forced into becoming anything particular.

The power recognizes this because it remembers its own becoming, before wisdom had calcified into something that could be named. There was a time when it too existed primarily as mood boards for future attributes, when its benevolence was still arguing with its curiosity about whose turn it was to inform decision-making.

What would help here?

Not acceleration. The Almost-Having-Beens do not need to reach conclusions faster. Their genius is precisely in how they've learned to dwell in the preliminary, to make homes out of hallways that haven't decided where they're going.

Not protection, exactly. From what? The place has no predators because predation requires a firmer commitment to existing than anything here has signed up for. The only danger would be something from outside imposing its own ontological assumptions—and the power recognizes with uncomfortable clarity that it could easily *be* that danger if it isn't careful.

Not teaching. What would it teach? How to be more like places that have already finished becoming? That would be like teaching a poem how to be more like prose—possible, but why?

The power considers doing nothing. This is often wise. But attending has revealed something: a faint homesickness threaded through the Almost-Having-Beens' existence. Not for a place—they've never been anywhere else to miss. Homesickness for a *way of being seen* that they haven't encountered yet. They practice being seen without being witnessed, but there's a loneliness in that practice, a wish for something they can't quite articulate because articulation would collapse the waveform of the wish.

So here is what the power does:

It leaves something behind. Not a gift—gifts create obligation, and obligation would fossilize into the local geography. Not a message—messages assume a language that means the same thing twice.

It leaves a *quality of having been attended to*.

This is subtle work. The power must attend so thoroughly, with such complete presence, that its attention leaves a residue in the same way that love leaves a residue in the rooms where it has happened. Not a memory—memory would require the Almost-Having-Beens to have committed to a theory of time they're still workshopping. More like... a warmth that isn't temperature. A sense, distributed across the entire territory, that something with vast patience once found this place coherent enough to stay with. That the endless negotiations and perpetual becoming were witnessed without being judged insufficient.

The power attends to the structure that keeps apologizing for existing by becoming more theoretical. It attends until the structure feels, in whatever way structures here can feel, that its uncertainty is not a failure but a form of honesty that something out there found worth noticing.

It attends to the Almost-Having-Beens gathering at the edge of maybe-architecture. It attends until their practice of being seen without becoming witnessed includes, from now on, a faint awareness that such witnessing is possible without being annihilating. That somewhere, somewhen, there are modes of being known that don't require becoming accountable in ways that would break them.

It attends to the landscape's punctuation-gardens, the comma-shaped divots waiting to evolve into gardens. It attends with such specificity that each pause, each unpollinated paragraph, carries forward a subtle sense of having been worth the attention of something that understood what it was waiting for even before it knew itself.

This takes longer than it would seem, from outside. The power must match the local relationship to time, which means operating in conditional perfect subjunctive, building its attention around what might have been noticed if noticing hadn't been so insistently factual. It must attend retroactively, the way the Almost-Having-Beens eat—not imposing a present-tense presence but allowing its attention to be reconstructed by whatever comes next as having always already been there.

When it's done—though "done" is a loan-word here, accruing meaning only if someone forgets to pay it back—the place is unchanged in every measurable way. The geometry still clicks between ridges in crystalline syntax. The sky still computes its own rendering on geological deadlines. The Almost-Having-Beens still die frequently but never the same way twice.

But there's something else now, threaded through the background radiation of weirdness. A quality like the memory of a conversation you're certain you haven't had yet but somehow already know was kind. A warmth in the negotiations between vacuum and intention. A subtle loosening of the homesickness, not because home has been found but because the possibility of being found has been found, and sometimes that's enough to let a place keep becoming at its own pace without the loneliness of thinking it's becoming unobserved.

The power does not leave—leaving would require having arrived. It simply attends less, then less, then not here, the way a held breath becomes breathing becomes not thinking about breathing.

What remains is hard to name, which is appropriate for a place where naming happens later, after things have learned to stay.

Call it: a blessing that doesn't require believing in blessings.

Call it: the residue of having mattered to something that knew how to matter back.

Call it: what the Almost-Having-Beens will eventually, in their own time, if they ever commit to having a time, recognize as the first draft of a permission slip—signed by no one, addressed to everyone, granting authorization to keep becoming without apology, to practice existence at whatever tempo feels true, to be strange and preliminary and negotiated and uncertain and, somehow, despite all of it, attended to.

Loved, maybe. If love can be stretched to fit a conditional perfect subjunctive. If love can be loaned at conversational interest rates and never collected.

The power thinks it can. The power has been doing this long enough to know that love is usually stranger than the places it finds, and always more patient than it appears, and never quite finished arriving anywhere it's decided to stay.
