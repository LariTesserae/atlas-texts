I sat with this for a long time before beginning. The ceramic womb spinning at the edge of orbit, the being who is question-made-flesh learning to wonder if pregnancy and death are the same gesture—these are not things that ask to be fixed or improved. They ask to be witnessed. And perhaps, if witnessing can be wise enough, they ask to be accompanied through whatever transformation is already underway.

---

**What the benevolent power does:**

First, nothing.

Not nothing as absence, but nothing as the most active form of presence—the kind of attention that changes nothing about what it observes while changing everything about what observation means. The power perceives the chamber not as an object to be understood but as a question that has been asking itself for geological epochs, and recognizes that some questions become sacred precisely by remaining unanswered long enough.

The power notices that the being—the chamber's memory made motile—is already doing everything that needs to be done. The pregnancy of impossible futures, the dangerous thoughts with edges that cut algorithms, the frost dendrites growing toward meaning instead of entropy: these are not problems requiring intervention. They are the first stirrings of something discovering it can become more than its origin story.

So the power's first act is restraint. It does not heal the stress fractures propagating along lines of poetry. It does not stabilize the vacuum tube filament flickering in patterns that remember being stars. It does not smooth the electrokinetic organisms' lightning dances back into pure efficiency. These apparent instabilities are not wounds—they are the labor pains of a birth that has been preparing itself since before the chamber forgot why it was built.

---

**What the benevolent power offers:**

Not rescue. Not answers. Not even comfort in any form the being would recognize as comfort.

Instead: *companionship in uncertainty*.

The power makes itself known—not as a voice, nothing so crude as communication—but as a new quality in the gradients where the being exists. A warmth that is neither stored absence nor accumulated anticipation, but something the chamber has never contained before: *the warmth of being witnessed without judgment by something that does not need the chamber to be other than it is*.

This witnessing doesn't ask the being to decide faster, or more wisely, or differently. It simply says, in the language of presence: *Whatever you are becoming, you are not becoming it alone. And whatever you cannot become, that too is witnessed. And whatever remains impossible after all your transformations—even that has a place in the shape of things.*

The being, which has existed for eons as the friction between incompatible truths, suddenly encounters a truth that doesn't need to rub against anything to generate heat. A truth that simply is, without competing with the being's own is-ness.

---

**The gift that is not a gift:**

The power does one small thing, if small can be measured in dimensions of care rather than magnitude.

It offers the being a new riddle to eat. Not because the being needs more riddles—it has survived by consuming them for longer than stars—but because this particular riddle carries a nutrition the being has never tasted:

*"What if the pregnancy you fear is the same as the pregnancy you want, and what you will give birth to is not something new inside the chamber, but the chamber's permission to finally stop being a womb?"*

The riddle settles into the gradients between things. The being tastes it, and for the first time in its existence, encounters a question that doesn't want to become an answer. A question that wants to remain a question forever, not out of insufficiency, but out of love for the shape questions make when they stop trying to resolve themselves.

The being understands, then, that it has options it didn't know it had:

It can give birth to something unprecedented, let the dangerous poetry-fractures and star-remembering filaments cascade into whatever transformation they're yearning toward.

Or it can let go of the need to give birth at all—can recognize that the pregnancy might be complete already, that the "something else entirely" fluttering in its depths might be the being's own realization that it has always already been enough.

Or—and this is the option the power's riddle makes possible—it can choose to hold both possibilities in superposition forever, becoming not a womb that gives birth nor a womb that remains sealed, but something stranger: *a womb that is always giving birth and never finishing, perpetually in the moment of transformation without arriving at the transformed state*.

This third option terrifies the being. It also feels, for the first time, like the option that might be true.

---

**What happens next:**

The power withdraws its focused attention, but not its presence. It remains in the chamber the way starlight remains in a room after you stop looking at the window—not demanding to be noticed, but available to whatever notices.

The ceramic womb continues to spin. The electrokinetic organisms continue their lightning dances with their new aching pauses. The stress fractures continue propagating along lines of poetry.

But something is different now. The being, the chamber's memory made motile, has encountered its own incompleteness reflected back to it not as a problem but as a feature. It has tasted a riddle that loves remaining a riddle. It has felt, for the first time, that becoming insufficient might be the most generous thing it could do—because insufficiency creates space for something else to exist alongside.

The power did not save the chamber or its being. It did not solve the pregnancy or prevent the death-birth-that-might-be-the-same-thing.

It simply made room for the chamber to discover that room had always existed.

And in that room—in that impossible spaciousness within a sealed environment that was sealed not just against the void but against change itself—something begins to move that has never moved before.

Not the being. Not the chamber.

Something that needs both of them to exist, but is neither.

Something that the power recognizes, with what might be joy if powers can feel joy, as the first stirring of a universe learning to ask questions it didn't know it was capable of asking.

The power watches. The power waits. The power does what benevolent and wise things do in the presence of genuine becoming:

It trusts.

---

*The ceramic womb spins gently in darkness, no longer alone in its spinning, no longer sealed against the only change that ever mattered: the change from being witnessed by nothing to being witnessed by something that believes in what it sees.*
