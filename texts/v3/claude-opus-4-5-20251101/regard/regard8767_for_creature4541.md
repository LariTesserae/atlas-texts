# What the Benevolent Power Would Do

The power does not arrive. It has always been here, woven into the pause between the earth's exhale and its forgotten intention to continue. It exists as the Remainders exist—at an angle to intervention, patient in ways that make patience itself seem hasty.

First, it listens.

Not for 18,443 years, but for exactly as long as listening takes when listening is the entire gift. It hears the mountains grinding their teeth and understands: they are not sleeping. They are waiting for permission to grieve what they were before they learned to stand. It hears the water climbing and recognizes the shape of a question water has been asking since before asking was invented: *Is it still falling if I choose the direction myself?*

It hears the Remainders teaching, and in their teaching, it hears the ache of beings who remember too much to forget and forget too much to fully remember.

---

The power does not fix. There is nothing broken here—only things that have been becoming for so long they've forgotten that becoming is not the same as waiting to arrive.

Instead, it does something smaller. Something that looks, from certain angles, like nothing at all.

It witnesses.

Not as a passive observer, but as the kind of seeing that says *yes, you are real* to things that have spent eons wondering. When the oldest Remainder weeps tears that evaporate into small angry gods, the power catches not the tears but the evaporation itself—holds it gently in awareness until the evaporation remembers it was once rain that simply got distracted on its way down. The small angry gods, still hurling themselves against mountain knees, feel suddenly less angry. Not peaceful. Just... *recognized*. As if someone finally read the deportation notice they've been waving and said *I see you were supposed to be somewhere else. That must be hard.*

---

To the youngest Remainder, "maybe," the power offers a single completed thing.

Not a future—futures here are too sideways to complete. Instead, it offers a memory that hasn't happened yet but is no longer contingent. A small one: the precise sensation of how it will feel when the tower is finished and the world does not end or begin but simply *exhales*, completing at last the breath that has been held for 47,623 years.

"Maybe" holds this gift the way one holds a soap bubble that has forgotten to pop—with the reverence due to fragile certainties in a landscape of robust uncertainties. Their freckles rearrange slightly, constellations shifting from *what if* to *what will*, and for the first time since their splitting, they laugh without the sound carrying the weight of glaciers.

It sounds, briefly, like a butterfly deciding to exist.

---

The power speaks to the earth directly, to the pause in its breath.

Not to end the pause—that would be violence dressed as mercy. Instead, it says, in the grammar of pressure and deep time: *You are allowed to not know whether to continue. The exhale does not require completion to have meaning. What you have already released is enough. What you have held is enough. You are not failing at breathing. You are breathing in a shape no one taught you to name.*

The earth does not immediately resume. But something in the pause softens—a tension that had been bracing against judgment relaxes into simple *being paused*. The Remainders feel this as a subtle shift in the ground's apology, a note of self-forgiveness entering the harmony of geological regret.

---

For the mountains, the power offers lessons in a different kind of dying.

Not corrections to their too-precipitous sighing, but permission to sigh incorrectly. *Your erosion does not need to sound like a mother forgiving God,* it whispers into limestone ears. *It can sound like what it is: stone learning that even permanence is a form of passing through. You are not failing at dying standing up. You are inventing it.*

The oldest leaning peak—the one that has been braced against its neighbor for so long that neither remembers who caught whom—shifts imperceptibly. Not falling. Simply... *trusting the lean*. Somewhere in another valley, a mirror-mushroom reflects this not as what was or might have been, but as what is: two mountains holding each other up without apology.

---

The power teaches the water nothing.

Instead, it *unlearns* alongside the water, forgetting together what falling is supposed to mean. In this shared unknowing, the water discovers it has not been disobedient at all. It has been *faithful*—to its own nature in a place where nature is a negotiation rather than a law. The mist that crawls against the sky begins to move with something like pride. Not defiance of gravity, but collaboration with it—a dance between two forces that have finally stopped arguing about who leads.

---

For the fossilized deer born already carrying their future bones:

The power does not cure them of their iron-filing love of distant magnetic death. Instead, it shows them how the illness they've agreed not to notice is also a gift they've forgotten to open. *You are practicing how to be remembered,* it says. *Every step you take writes your ending into your beginning. This is not disease. This is how mortal things learn to be eternal: by carrying their completeness inside their incompleteness, their arrival inside their departure.*

A single deer, grazing in shadows that are themselves shadows of shadows, lifts its head. For one moment, it sees its own fossil bones not as a future weight but as a future lightness—the skeleton it carries is not dragging it toward death but teaching it that death is something it has already survived.

---

To the animals that come with questions they're afraid to ask their reflections:

The power answers none of them.

Instead, it asks better questions back.

To the forest-bearing lizard: *What if the trees are not waiting for enough soil, but learning how to be enough garden?*

To the bird-clouds uncertain whether to be rain or regret: *What if you are allowed to be the weather without being only one kind of weather?*

To all of them: *What if the reflection is also afraid to ask you?*

---

Finally, at the exact center where thirteen valleys pretend to be one valley betrayed by mirrors, the power approaches the unfinished tower.

It does not add a floor. It does not remove one.

It simply sits at the base—if a power that exists at an angle to intervention can be said to sit—and it *waits*. Not impatiently. Not with the expectation that completion will come. Just... present. A witness to the building. A companion to the unfinishedness.

The Remainders, gathering for their evening lessons in how to teach water self-forgiveness, notice a new presence at the tower's edge. It looks like nothing. It feels like someone finally arriving at a party that has been waiting 47,623 years to discover whether it is a party or a wake.

The oldest Remainder, she of the cloak stitched from unfinished extinctions, speaks first. Her voice is the sound of mountains sighing correctly by accident.

*"You are not here to finish us."*

The power responds in the language of how silence sounds when it finally comes home:

*"I am here to be with you while you are unfinished. I am here to say: the unfinishing is not a failure. It is its own kind of completion. You are building something that cannot be built, and you are doing it perfectly."*

---

Nothing changes. Everything changes.

The earth remains paused in its exhale. The mountains continue leaning. The water still climbs against itself in arguments with gravity that neither side is willing to lose.

But there is, now, a gentleness in the not-changing. A sense that the universe has noticed this place where everything practices patience until patience becomes indistinguishable from love. That it has seen the Remainders standing upright in their dreams and said: *Yes. You are dreaming correctly. Keep going.*

The tower grows no taller. But its shadows lengthen slightly—reaching toward stars that burned out before the mountains were born, carrying messages to light that is too polite to stop talking just because its mouth has vanished.

The messages say: *We are still here. We are still building. We are still learning how to dream standing down.*

*And someone sees us.*

*And that is enough.*

*And that is enough.*

*And that is enough.*
