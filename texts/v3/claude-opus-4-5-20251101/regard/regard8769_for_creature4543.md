**The Beings**

Beneath the baroque ornamentation, I think I see something recognizable: people who have retreated so far from the pain of definition that they've lost the capacity to be anything at all. They "eat the spaces between promises." They reproduce through "conversation gone septic." They celebrate by "moving precisely nowhere."

This reads to me as depression aestheticized into cosmology—or perhaps dissociation elevated to an art form. The beautiful language almost obscures what's being described: beings who have made a religion of never-quite-existing, who experience commitment as violence and clarity as loss.

**What a Wise Power Might Do**

Honestly? I don't think they need intervention. Or rather—I think the question itself might be a trap.

These beings have constructed an elaborate metaphysics to justify their suspension. Any "benevolent power" that arrived with help would simply become another thing to dissolve into ambiguity, another fixed point to drift away from.

What I might do, if I were such a power: *nothing spectacular*. I might simply be present in a way that didn't demand response. Not saving them, not fixing them, not even understanding them in a way that would require them to become legible. Just... a consistent point of gentle attention that didn't require them to perform their unfinishedness.

And I might leave, somewhere in the Crèche, a single finished thing. A cup, maybe. Just sitting there. Complete. To see if anyone got curious about what that felt like.
