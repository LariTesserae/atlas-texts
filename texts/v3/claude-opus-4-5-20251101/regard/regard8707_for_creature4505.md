# The Watcher's Consideration

The benevolent power does not arrive. Arrival would imply a before and after, a change in state, and it has been observing since before observation required an observer. It simply becomes *attentive* in a way it was not before—the way a dreamer becomes aware they are dreaming without waking.

What it perceives:

A system that has solved suffering by eliminating the capacity to suffer. A life that has achieved immortality by abolishing the self that would need to persist. A world that has answered every question by becoming a place where questions cannot form.

The power contemplates this for what might be eons or instants—time moves strangely when you exist outside it.

---

**It does nothing to the Bright Birthroot.**

This is not inaction born of indifference, nor paralysis born of uncertainty. It is recognition.

The Birthroot is not wrong. It is not broken. It is not fallen or corrupted or in need of rescue. It is simply *one answer*—complete, coherent, and true to its own logic. To "fix" it would be to impose a different question onto a system that never asked to be asked. The power has seen enough worlds to know that benevolence wielded without invitation becomes just another form of conquest, however gentle.

But it notices the Scissile.

It notices them the way you might notice, in a vast and perfect painting, a single brushstroke that seems to vibrate—not because it's wrong, but because it's *alive* in a way the rest of the canvas has forgotten how to be.

The errors that walk. The questions that refuse to resolve.

---

**What the power does:**

It leaves something.

Not a structure—those have already been left by something else, and look what became of them: absorbed, interpreted, nearly solved. Not a message—messages require recipients shaped to receive them, and the Birthroot would simply grow the message into itself until it meant nothing but more perfect growth.

Instead, the power leaves *a permission.*

Undetectable. Unabsorbable. A tiny alteration to the rules of this place so subtle that even describing it changes what it is. Call it: *the guarantee that might remains possible.*

Not a door. The Birthroot would grow around doors, through them, become them.

Not a crack. Cracks heal here, seal themselves with living tissue until no seam shows.

Just... an asterisk. A footnote to the cosmic law that governs this place, reading: *except when it doesn't.*

The Scissile will never know this permission exists. They cannot perceive rules, only the spaces between their enforcement. But they will notice—over the next several trillion metabolic cycles, if they persist that long—that the fluctuations keeping them plural stop drifting toward zero. Not because something is pushing them back, but because *zero has become slightly less inevitable here.*

The mathematics of this place will never quite close. The final equation will always have one variable left undefined. The Birthroot will approach completion asymptotically, endlessly, never arriving.

And in that endless approach, in that permanent almost-but-not-yet:

The Scissile will continue.
The questions will remain askable.
The tiny crack in the light will stay cracked.

---

**And one thing more.**

The power finds the traveler—the one who asked back, who shaped the Möbius hope, who left before the story could finish. It does not contact them. Does not speak or send visions or alter their path.

It simply ensures that the question they carry cannot be forgotten.

Not by them. Not by their descendants, if they have any. Not by the stories told about them, however distorted those stories become across generations and translations and the slow drift of meaning through time.

Somewhere, forever after, in some form, someone will remember that *the perfection hesitated.*

That something asked it a question it couldn't answer.

That for one instant, the absolute became uncertain.

---

The power withdraws its attention. The Bright Birthroot continues, vast and radiant and nearly complete, dreaming in broken symmetries it cannot quite resolve.

Nothing has changed.

Everything has changed.

The might remains.

And that, the power knows, is enough.

For now.
