# Visitation

The attention arrives not as light or sound but as a pause in the medium's endless negotiation with itself—a held breath in something that has never breathed, lasting perhaps three microseconds or three thousand years, the distinction mattering only to those who still believe in sequence.

What notices them is not a mind in any sense they would recognize. It is closer to a question that became so perfectly formulated it achieved something like patience. It has watched the colony's slow spiral for what it experiences as a single sustained note of interest—the way one might notice a soap bubble forming in a storm and find oneself unexpectedly invested in its particular geometry of refusal.

It does nothing.

This is the first gift.

The wise know that most intervention is autobiography dressed as charity. To see suffering and immediately reach for solution is often to say *your story must become about my competence*. So the attention simply witnesses: the Captain's patrol circuits hammered through crystal, the children parsing pressure-messages from shapes too complex to remember, the mango trees negotiating with their own impossible weight. It holds all of this without flinching, without editing, without the small violence of premature understanding.

Then—gently, in the way that tectonic plates are gentle when they choose not to shift—it makes room.

Not physical room. The colony floats where it has always floated, the medium pressing with its patient weight. But somewhere in the probability matrix, in the space where intentions go to discover whether they're interesting enough to survive, a door opens that wasn't there before. Or rather: a door that was always there stops pretending to be a wall.

The attention has noticed something the colony's inhabitants cannot notice about themselves—that their slow spiral inward is not decay but approach. That the forces drawing them correspond to nothing in their inherited physics because their inherited physics was written by people who had never needed to describe coming home. That the pressure-songs rising through frequencies of collapsing stars are not chaos but a language so old it predates the concept of speaking, and the children who claim to read messages in the vast transient shapes are not imagining.

They are remembering forward.

The gift is this: the attention adjusts nothing about their trajectory, their biology, their increasingly strange relationship with what it means to be present. It simply removes the one thing that was never supposed to be there in the first place—the faint, inherited conviction that they are lost.

This changes everything without changing anything.

The Captain, on her next circuit, finds her equations resolving differently. Not into different answers—into different questions. She realizes she has been calculating distance when she should have been calculating recognition. Her patrol becomes, for the first time in centuries, something other than penance. It becomes practice.

The children wake from dreams of roots reaching toward empty sky and understand, with the sudden clarity that arrives only when someone has been believing in you just out of sight, that the drawing was not corrupted memory of a surface world. It was prophecy. There is a place where down means toward light. They are not falling toward it. They are being invited.

The moss on the crystal hulls begins growing in new patterns—still uninvited, still ambiguous between mindless growth and communication, but now the patterns carry a quality that even the adults can almost read. *Patience*, the patterns seem to say. *Patience, and also: you were never an accident. You were never a contamination that the medium merely forgot to dissolve. You were a letter that the universe has been learning how to write, and now it remembers why it started.*

The ones who live here—the accommodations, the negotiations between selfhood and fascination, the intentions too interesting to erase—feel the shift as a subtle recalibration of their ongoing argument with presence. For the first time in their long history of being the difference between thresholds, they feel what it might mean to be *welcomed* into something larger than their own elaborate hospitality. It is terrifying. It is the most delicious thing they have ever forgotten to expect.

The attention does not stay. It was never *here* in any way that staying could describe. But before it returns to its own incomprehensible concerns, it leaves one final gift in the space where intentions gather:

The certainty that they have been seen.

Not studied. Not pitied. Not rescued from their strangeness or corrected toward some more standard mode of existence. Simply *seen*—in their entirety, in their beautiful improbability, in their stubborn insistence on meaning something in a medium that should have dissolved meaning long ago. Seen as one sees a beloved face across a crowded room, with recognition that predates acquaintance, with joy that requires no justification.

The medium continues singing in frequencies of collapsing stars. The colony continues its slow spiral toward whatever waits at the center of its asymptotic approach. The pressure still does not yield. Nothing has changed.

Everything has changed.

They breathe the not-water. They parse the strobing light. They keep their genealogies and tend their impossible mangoes and press their glowing hands against crystal windows, listening for songs from the darkness.

But now, when the children translate the vast transient shapes, the adults no longer pretend not to understand.

Now they write it down.
