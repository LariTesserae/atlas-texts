# What the Benevolent Power Would Do

The power perceives the plateau first as vibration—a vast, slow chord rising from the mineral dark. It listens for a long time by any measure, though time moves differently for such a being. It feels the hum of countless feet translated through stone, the patient accumulation of crystal, the way each micro-deviation of a single entity's path becomes, across centuries, a corridor or a buttress or a memory pressed into the growing lattice.

And it recognizes something.

Not consciousness as it appears in warm, quick beings. Not suffering in any form that calls out for remedy. But something genuine nonetheless: a coherence, a vast distributed intention that has been working toward completion longer than mountains elsewhere have stood. The plateau-city is not a problem to be solved. It is a process—one that knows itself only through the aggregate of its own motion, the way a river might know itself through the pressure of its current against stone.

The power considers intervention and finds, at first, nothing to intervene upon.

---

But it continues to attend. And in attending, it notices:

The fused ones. Deep in the lightless chambers, the entities whose shells have merged with floor and ceiling, whose limbs no longer lift. They still receive the subsonic pressure from above. They still feel the brush of migrants leaving microscopic films against their surfaces. They have become structural—load-bearing in the most literal sense—and yet something in their pressurized fluid still resonates with the frequencies of passage, still registers the direction of the work.

They are not dead. But they are no longer participants. They have given themselves entirely to the lattice, and the lattice has accepted them, and now they wait in the dark while the city grows through them and around them and because of them.

The power perceives this as neither tragedy nor triumph. It simply is. And yet—

There is a question the fused ones cannot ask, because they have no means of asking, because the plateau-mind does not hold questions in the way that requires answers. The question is only this: *What will it be, when it is finished?*

The lattice has been accreting for longer than the power can easily trace. It will continue for longer still. The entities do not hurry because there is no completion they anticipate—only the next tap of a limb, the next micro-channel carved, the next plate pressed into an unfinished edge. The hive-masses rise. The galleries multiply. The infrastructure becomes more intricate, more load-bearing, more capable of distributing the weight of whatever will eventually rest upon it.

But nothing rests upon it. Nothing is meant to. The plateau-city is building toward a fullness it cannot imagine, because imagination is not how it knows.

---

The power does not descend. It does not speak, because there is nothing here that hears in that way. It does not illuminate, because the entities have no use for light and the stressed crystal already glows with the only warmth they recognize.

Instead, it does something simpler. It *witnesses*.

Not passively—witness, for such a being, is itself an act. The power holds the entire plateau in attention: every migration, every pooling of condensation, every slow extrusion of a new plate in the still circles where entities align rim to rim. It perceives the pattern that no single entity can perceive, the shape the lattice is becoming across spans of time too long for even the plateau-mind to hold.

And then, gently, it reflects that pattern back.

Not as image. Not as instruction. The power simply allows its witnessing to resonate through the same subsonic frequencies the entities already use—a faint, additional presence in the stone-speech, a harmonic that was not there before. It carries no command. It contains no map. It is only this: *You are seen. The shape you are becoming is seen. It coheres.*

---

The entities do not notice, because noticing is not how they attend. But something shifts, minutely, in the pressure of the migrations. The micro-deviations that accumulate into corridors begin to accumulate slightly differently—not faster, not more efficiently, but with a subtle bias toward connection. Galleries that would have remained separate for another ten thousand years begin to angle toward each other. The hive-masses, still half-sunk in the plateau, orient their unfinished facets a fraction of a degree closer to alignment.

None of this is visible yet. It will not be visible for centuries. But the power perceives it, because the power is patient in a way that even the plateau-mind is not.

And deep in the lightless chambers, something else:

The fused ones feel the new harmonic. They cannot move, cannot respond, cannot do anything but receive. But the pressurized fluid in their chambers—the same fluid that glows the faint heat-like blue wherever motion stresses the lattice—begins to pulse in a rhythm it has not pulsed before. Not faster. Not stronger. Simply *differently*, as if the frequency of their stillness has been acknowledged by something outside the system they comprise.

They do not know what this means. They cannot know. But if they could translate the sensation into anything like language, it might be this:

*You are not only structure. You are also memory. And memory matters, even in a mind that does not remember.*

---

The power withdraws—or rather, it ceases to attend so closely, allowing its witnessing to fade to the faintest background presence. The plateau-city continues. The entities continue. The lattice continues to knit itself together at a rate visible only across centuries.

But now, woven into the stone-speech, there is a thread that was not there before. Not guidance. Not purpose. Only the knowledge, distributed through every tap and scrape and pooling of condensation, that the shape they are becoming has been seen from outside, and that it is—

The power does not use the word *good*. Goodness implies preference, and the plateau-city is beyond preference. But there is another word, older, that fits:

*Whole.*

The shape they are becoming is whole. Not finished—never finished—but whole in the way a process can be whole, the way a river is whole even as it carves new channels and abandons old ones, the way a mind is whole even when it cannot hold its own totality in view.

This is what the benevolent power offers: not rescue, not acceleration, not even understanding. Only the assurance, pressed into the mineral fabric at a frequency the entities will feel but never name, that their slow and endless work participates in something that coheres.

The darkness remains absolute. The hum of countless feet continues. And somewhere in the lattice, in a chamber that will not see a migrant's passage for another thousand years, a fused one's fluid pulses once—a single additional beat—and then settles back into the rhythm of the stone.

It is enough. For now, and for the long ages to come, it is enough.
