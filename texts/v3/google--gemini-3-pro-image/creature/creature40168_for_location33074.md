It is the gardener of this violent womb, a creature distilled from pressure, cold, and raw geology.

It is huge, occupying a significant portion of the chapel-sized space, yet it moves with a terrible, grinding slowness that contrasts sharply with the frantic energy of the translucent grazers. It is vague in form, a hulking asymmetry of lumped flesh that mimics the embryonic rock surrounding it. Its hide is thick, hairless, and the color of wet slate and bruised amethyst, slicked permanently with the same gelid sweat that covers the walls. It has no eyes—the searing luminescence from the ceiling would render them useless—but its entire surface is sensitive, a shivering landscape of nerve endings that taste the mineral dampness of the air and feel the sub-audible groaning of the bedrock.

Its limbs are not legs or arms, but hydraulic pseudopods of muscle and gristle that extrude from its central mass. They end not in hands, but in clusters of hard, bone-like nodules and spatula-shaped keratin tools.

The creature ignores the thousands of glass-grazers that swarm over its body like lice; they are merely part of the environment's ecosystem. Its focus is entirely on the bulging, "emerging" walls. It progresses around the perimeter of the room in an endless, obsessive circuit. Where the rock groans loudest and bulges most threateningly, looking like wax about to burst, the creature presses its massive bulk against the stone. It applies counter-pressure, its body vibrating with the strain, acting as a living buttress against the crushing weight of the deep earth.

While braced against the wall, its smaller, more delicate appendages work with feverish intensity. It acts as a crude surgeon for the geology. It scrapes away patches of the albino slime mold and collects the dripping condensation into its own pores. Most disturbingly, it utilizes the ancient detritus scattered about the room. It will locate a fossilized femur or a rounded tooth from the central cairn, and with shocking strength, jam the object into the soft, putty-like fissures of the emerging rock. It packs the area with the mineral slurry from the floor, smoothing it over with its splayed tools, attempting to create the "scar tissue" you observed earlier.

It is a desperate, unending task. The creature is trying to impose structural integrity onto a space that wants to either collapse or tear itself open to the blinding energy above. It is a lonely, sweating custodian of a geological hiccup, forever patching a wound in the earth that refuses to heal.
