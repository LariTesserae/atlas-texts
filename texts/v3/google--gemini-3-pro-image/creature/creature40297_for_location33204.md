The emptiness is not absolute. In a realm defined by deafening chaos and the violent birth of matter, the things that thrive are those that can harness the turbulence, entities that are less biological lifeforms and more sentient expressions of the specific, warped physics governing this stratum.

They are the Weavers of the Lattice.

They are colossal, skeletal constructs, vast enough to be mistaken for the transient crystal-reefs themselves, yet possessed of a terrifying, glacial mobility. They have no flesh, no blood, no soft tissues to be bruised by the cavitating booms or torn by the gravimetric shears. Instead, their bodies are composed of the same hyper-sharp, fractal metallic-crystalline rock that erupts from the void.

Imagine a shifting, polyhedral cage, kilometers across, a complex geometric tumbleweed made of glittering, razor-edged girders. They are mostly negative space, a design necessity that allows the thundering, super-saturated currents of the aerated ocean to pass *through* them rather than smashing against them. They do not swim so much as they are vectored by the shifting gravity tides, riding the multi-directional storms with an unsettling, mathematically precise grace.

They are the active agents of this realm's strange creation engine. They drift through the endless gray-blue churning luminosity, waiting for the moment a geyser of raw elements flash-freezes from the nothingness. When this "embryonic matter" erupts, the Weavers converge.

They do not eat in any conventional sense. They integrate.

Using fields of manipulated gravity—smaller, localized versions of the tides that buffet the realm—they capture the newly formed, jagged lattices. Their limbs, which are fractal extremities capable of subdividing into nano-scale manipulators, seize the raw material. In a process dazzling to behold in the flat, shadowless light, they swiftly alter the crystalline growth, weaving the fresh matter into their own structure or grafting it onto the existing, drifting reefs. They are the gardeners of this razor-sharp geology, ensuring the spontaneous generation of matter adheres to a rigid, glittering geometry.

They are utterly silent in themselves. They have no voices. Yet, their presence modifies the deafening roar of the environment. As the ceaseless shockwaves pass through their immense, crystalline lattice-bodies, the structures vibrate like tuning forks the size of mountains. This creates a horrific, resonating chime—a piercing, multi-tonal harmony that overlays the background white noise, a sound both beautiful and unbearably intense, signaling their approach through the crushing haze.

They are indifferent to the sparse, glassy mosses that cling to the larger reefs, ignoring them as a mountain ignores lichen. They are cold, tireless, and inhuman, vast geometric intelligences drifting forever through the wet, thundering void, dedicated solely to the endlessly repeating task of knitting solid reality out of chaos.
