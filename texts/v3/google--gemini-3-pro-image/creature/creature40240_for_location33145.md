The beings that inhabit this splintered womb are less flesh than they are animated geology. They are the necessary answer to an environment composed entirely of edges. To survive here, one must be as hard as the world itself.

They are squat, brooding shapes, easily mistaken for outcroppings of rock when still in the shadows. Their physiology is a testament to the terrible abrasion of their home. They possess no discernible skin, hide, or scales. Instead, their exterior is a thick, shifting crust of mineral accretions—a natural, ablative armor grown from within. They look like walking geodes that have turned inside out.

The outermost layer of this living carapace is a chaotic jumble of sharp, vitrified shards, mirroring the terrain around them. It is dull and dusted with sulfurous powder, scarred by countless scrapes against the environment. Beneath this sacrificial outer crust lies a smoother, denser layer of dark, obsidian-like plating that acts as their true skeletal structure. As they move through the razor-narrow chasms, the outer shards grind and snap off against the canyon walls with sharp *cracks*, a sound that is simply part of their breathing rhythm. They grow this armor constantly; they are beings in a perpetual state of breaking and remaking themselves.

They are humanoid only in the loosest sense—they are bipedal, possessing two thick, powerful arms, but there the resemblance ends. They are hunched, their heads recessed deeply between massive, sloping shoulders to protect the neck. Their center of gravity is low, crucial for maintaining balance on the treacherous, crystalline footing. Their hands do not have fingers, but rather opposing, articulated clamps of solid rock, capable of exerting immense crushing pressure. These "hands" are used as much for gripping jagged handholds as they are for wielding their heavy volcanic glass chisels. Their feet are broad, splayed pedestals of rough stone, gripping the precarious surfaces like grinding wheels.

In the perpetual gloom, eyes would be a vulnerability. These beings are sightless. Where a face might be, there is instead a smooth, multifaceted expanse of polished mineral, usually a deep, light-absorbing violet. This "faceplate" is extremely sensitive to vibration and thermal gradients. They navigate the static dark by feeling the deep hum of the geothermal vents beneath the floor and the minute tremors caused by the distant, scraping passage of the giant millipedes. They "see" the world through the soles of their feet and the heat against their armored skin. Deep fissures along the sides of their skulls serve as olfactory organs, constantly filtering the thick, hot air for the metallic tang of specific nutrient-rich mineral veins.

They are a silent people, mirroring the stillness of their tomb-world. Communication is likely tactile or based on low-frequency vibrations sent through the floor, too deep for human ears to register. They move with agonizing deliberation. Haste in this realm means impalement. Every step is tested, every handhold verified before weight is committed.

Like the giant chitinous grazers, these intelligent inhabitants are lithophages. They do not hunt; they mine their sustenance. They seek out softer, chemically complex mineral deposits, using their chisels to crack open the veins. They then use their grinder-like hands to pulverize the rock into a fine powder which is ingested through a narrow, armored slit located beneath their chin. They consume the geology, processing the minerals in furnaces within their dense bodies, their metabolism fueled by the pervasive ambient heat.

They are solitary beings, coming together only rarely. The scarcity of their crude stone lean-tos suggests a life spent mostly on the move, a slow, eternal pilgrimage through the glittering dark, scraping an existence from the sharp edges of an unchanging world. They are patient, durable, and utterly devoid of softness, perfectly preserved in the hot, silent stasis of the hollow jewel.
