The beings that claim this electrified corpse of a world are as severe and magnificent as the landscape itself. They are not creatures of flesh, blood, or water chemistry. To survive the grit-laden scream of the wind and the killing dryness, life here had to follow the path of the Ur-Weave, mineralizing and evolving into intricate structures of conductive ceramic, spun metalloids, and polished silicate chitin.

They are tall and terrifyingly gaunt, echoing the spires of their thorn-cities. A typical individual stands perhaps nine feet in height, an exquisite, skeletal assembly of segmented plates. Their exterior "skin" resembles the petrified surroundings—mottled slate-greys, deep indigos, and streaks of verdigris—making them nearly invisible when still against the oxidized bark of the ancient world. This outer plating is incredibly dense, capable of weathering the ceaseless abrasion of the atmosphere.

Beneath this armored carapace, they are beings of pure conduction. They do not have veins in a terrestrial sense; instead, intricate lattices of gold and electric-blue circuitry are fused directly onto their skeletal structure, pulsing in rhythm with the energy currents beneath the ground. They are, effectively, sentient extensions of the city’s exposed nervous system. When they move, they do not clank like the brutish fauna of the plains; they chime with a resonant, crystalline humming, the sound of high-voltage capacitors discharging micro-bursts of power.

They have no need for eyes in the dim, violet twilight. Their heads are elongated, elegant cowls of fluted ceramic, streamlined against the wind. Where a face might be, there is instead a complex cluster of sensory filaments—delicate, antenna-like structures of spun glass and palladium that taste the ozone and perceive the world through electrolocation. They "see" the precise voltage of the air, the magnetic signature of the geode-grazers miles away, and the flowing currents of the deep Ur-Weave beneath their feet.

The deafening, discordant drone of the planet is not noise to them; it is their baseline reality, a carrier wave upon which they communicate. They speak without sound. Instead, they modulate their own internal electrical fields, projecting complex, structured bursts of static and magnetism that other members of their species perceive instantly. A conversation between them is a silent, rapid-fire exchange of arcing lightning between their sensory cowls.

They rarely touch the ground. While they possess long, multi-jointed limbs ending in gripping talons suited for climbing the sheer faces of the thorn-towers, locomotion across the open expanses is achieved through their gravimetric technology. The "harnesses" mentioned are not worn equipment, but integral parts of their physiology—grown crystalline nodes fused to their spinal plating. These nodes manipulate local gravity fields, allowing them to decouple from the planet's heavy pull.

They do not fly; they surf. They latch onto the immense, glowing veins of energy embedded in the petrified ground and ride them like currents. A traveler across the plains appears as a stoic, motionless figure, arms crossed over their chest, gliding inches above the surface at tremendous speeds, a wake of blue plasma trailing behind them as they follow the ancient grain of the world.

They are a civilization of deep time and infinite patience, tenders of the planetary machine. In the constant, screaming gale, they are islands of absolute stillness, their minds synchronized with the pulsing rhythm of the re-ignited Weave, watching their electric garden bloom in the dark.
