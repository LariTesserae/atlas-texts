The environment is so total, so overwhelming in its frozen violence and monolithic scale, that it seems incapable of supporting anything other than the microscopic infinitesimal. The prompt explicitly states fauna is "almost nonexistent," saving only those tiny specks in the dew.

Therefore, what lives here must not be "fauna" in any recognizable sense. They are not visitors to this petrified garden; they are extrusions of it.

They are the **Resonant Weepers**.

They are found only clinging to the flanks of the colossal, mountain-sized bastions that rise kilometers apart. They are exceedingly rare; perhaps only one or two exist on any given spire, separated by miles of vertical, calcified bark.

At first glance through the thick, warm mist, a Weeper is invisible. It looks simply like a distortion in the wet air, a patch of atmosphere thicker and heavier than the rest. Closer inspection reveals a gelatinous, translucent entity, roughly the size of a large draft animal, but with no fixed morphology. They have no bones, no eyes, no mouths, and no skin. They are vaguely ameboid, immense globs of semi-solidified biological hydro-gel that adhere with incredible tenacity to the slippery, vertical surfaces of the hardened plant-towers.

They are beings born of the two dominant forces of this world: the ubiquitous moisture and the deafening Thrum.

Their bodies are nearly 98% water, harvested from the saturated air and the weeping walls they cling to. Because they are so clear, you see deep into their internal structure, which consists not of organs, but of a slowly churning suspension of fibrous strands—like nerve ganglia made of wet spun glass.

Their primary characteristic, however, is their relationship to the sound. The deep, pipe-organ drone that shakes the entire landscape vibrates intensely through their gelatinous forms. A Weeper does not hear the sound; it *physically embodies* it. Its surface is never still. It constantly ripples with complex cymatic patterns, geometrical standing waves that shiver across its watery skin in perfect synchronization with the monotonous roar of the planet. They look like living, trembling tuning forks made of clear jelly.

They are utterly sedentary. A Weeper might move only a few inches over the course of a century, sliding infinitesimally slowly on a layer of self-produced mucus down the face of a bastion. They do not feed. They exist solely to resonate. It appears their function is to act as biological dampeners or perhaps amplifiers for the geological sound, focusing the vibrational energy at specific points on the mega-structures.

If one were to stand next to a Weeper on a high ledge of a bastion, the environmental Thrum would cease to be just a sound and become a harrowing physical assault. The air pressure around the creature fluctuates violently. The vibration inside one's own chest would sync with the visible shuddering of the creature’s translucent viscera, creating an unbearable sense of pressure and vertigo.

They are silent guardians of the static uproar, solitary sacks of trembling water clinging to the dead monuments, shivering eternally in the wet, roaring dark.
