The vibration is the first thing. It is not a sound I hear, for I have no ears, but a tectonic rhythm that defines the boundaries of my being. *Thrum-hiss. Thrum-hiss.* It travels up through the fractured basalt, transmits into the buried iron hulk, and resonates directly into the gelatinous, shivering substance that is *us*.

I am a node. I am a recently formed extrusion of sensory flesh, a thumb-sized polyp of translucent, wet-bone pallor, pushed out by the colossal parent-body to investigate a new fissure in the engine block.

I am pressed flat against a rusted flange, my slick outer rind conforming perfectly to the irregular, corroded metal surface. Through my thin membrane, I taste the sharp, metallic tang of oxidizing iron and the rich, mineral soup of the incessant mud-slurry.

To my left, a vast, calloused root—thick as a trunk and scarred white from constant burning—throbs with the passage of superheated fluids. I feel the heat radiating from it, a wave of scalding ecstacy that keeps the damp air around us comfortably thick.

I do not see the towering ferns or the hanging mosses. To me, the world is a gradient of temperature and pressure. Above me is the "cooler-wet," where the condensation drips endlessly onto my skin. Below and ahead is the "hot-hard," the intricate maze of metal pipes we are slowly consuming.

Wait.

The rhythm changes. The deep *thrum* hitches. Pressure builds behind the metal plate I am pressed against. I feel the iron groan, a vibration distinct from the engine's heartbeat. It is the preamble to release.

Suddenly, a micro-fracture opens microns from my boundary layer. A needle-thin jet of steam erupts with concussion force. It strikes my flank, instantly searing the translucent flesh into a tough, white blister. The pain is exquisite; it is growth. I send a chemical cascade back along the neural pathways to the vast, blind center of the organism: *Heat source located. Breach confirmed.*

Already, the parent-body is responding, diverting flow, pushing more vascular tissue toward my position to plug the new leak, to drink the heat. I pulse in anticipation, my small form vibrating in sync with the titanic gardener that anchors this choked, drowning world. I am a blister on a blister on the shoulder of a titan, and I am thriving.
