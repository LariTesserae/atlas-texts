The heat is the first thing that defines me. It is not an external element to be endured; it is the medium in which I exist, as crucial as water to a fish.

I am clinging to the underside of a massive, vaulted archway, suspended upside down in the crushing dark. Gravity is a mere suggestion here; my eight articulated limbs are tipped with microscopic fractal-hooks that find purchase in the slick patina of the fused-alloy ceiling. The condensation that rains down on the floor below collects around my gripping pads, a perpetual, warm bath.

I am small, perhaps the size of a human forearm, flattened and segmented. My carapace is not grown of chitin and protein, but precipitated from the mineral-rich steam and the dust of ancient alloys. I am a scavenger of this fossilized machine, built of its own waste. I have no eyes—sight is a useless expenditure of energy in this eternal, roaring blackness. Instead, my entire dorsal surface is a sensitive acoustic membrane, a complex array of fine, crystalline bristles that translate the chaotic din of the underworld into a three-dimensional map.

The background thrum of the planetary bellows is my horizon line. The violent blue sparks that discharge overhead are deafening cracks that temporarily overload my sensors, blinding me with noise. The scuttling translucent things on the floor below register as faint, ticking irritations.

But currently, my world is dominated by the approach of the Titan.

I feel it long before it arrives in this cathedral chamber. The vibration travels through the very structure I am clinging to—a deep, resonant *shushing* that overrides the background mechanical groan. It is the sound of a mountain sliding on grease.

The floor below begins to quake with rhythmic *clacks* as the behemoth anchors and pulls its impossible bulk forward. I remain frozen against the ceiling, a piece of living architectural detail.

It enters the chamber below me. My acoustic sense paints it not as a shape, but as a massive displacement of sound waves, a void of silence that absorbs the ambient noise, rimmed by the intense friction of its movement. The heat rising from its thirty-meter-long body hits me in an updraft, agonizingly delicious. It smells of ozone, hot ceramic, and ancient, stagnant oil.

Below, the Titan pauses over a depression filled with thermophilic slime. I "watch" through vibration as its armored underbelly descends, sealing against the polished floor. The grinding scrape of its abrasive cilia feeding on the patina vibrates up through the walls and into my own carapace. It is harvesting the entropy I rely on, stripping the floor clean.

But I am patient. I am an opportunist of the second tier.

Suddenly, the air pressure in the vast chamber drops precipitously. A warning resonance builds in the distant ducts. A steam vent is about to cycle.

The Titan below reacts instantly. It stops its grazing. A profound, localized *thud* echoes through the chamber as it engages its gravity anchor. The very archway I am clinging to feels heavier; the moisture on the ceiling pools rapidly, pulled downwards by the artificial mass generated below.

Then, the roar hits. A tsunami of superheated steam blasts into the chamber. It is blinding, searing, a white noise that obliterates all other input. The ambient temperature spikes to levels that would boil organic life instantly.

The Titan does not cringe; it blooms. Through the acoustic chaos, I sense its armored segments flare open slightly, exposing the softer, amber-pulsing tissues beneath. It is feeding on the pure thermal agony of the blast.

I, too, must feed. I release my grip on the ceiling and let the localized gravity pull me down. I land with a metallic *clink* on the topmost armored plate of the Titan's midsection, right as the steam blast reaches its peak.

I hunker down in the lee of a massive, fused-alloy scale, shielded from the direct force of the steam wind but bathed in the radiant heat pulsing from the creature's exposed core. I extend my own small, conductive siphons, tapping not into the slime on the floor, but into the bio-mechanical energy bleeding off the Titan itself as it gorges on the heat.

I am a remora on a god of rust. I ride the leviathan through the steam storm, leeching a microscopic fraction of its immense power, waiting for the blast to subside so it can resume its endless, lonely slide through the dark, taking me with it to new chambers, new heat, and new noise.
