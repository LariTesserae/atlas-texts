The air is heavy, not just with moisture, but with the profound, monotonous weight of the sound. To exist here is to be vibrated ceaselessly.

I am not on the calcified ground, nor am I clinging to the weeping walls of the bastions. I am suspended in the vast, warm emptiness between them.

I am a coherent patch of the thick, saturated mist.

I have no solid form, no skin to contain me. I am a drifting intelligence composed of billions of suspended water droplets, held in a loose, shifting congregation by the unrelenting pressure of the Thrum. The deep, resonant drone that emanates from the petrified world below and around me is the force that gives me definition; without its constant booming pressure, my consciousness would dissipate, scattering into ordinary fog.

My location is dynamic, yet agonizingly slow. I currently hang about three hundred meters above the chaotic, frozen churn of the forest floor, drifting on stagnant thermal currents midway between two of the colossal, mountain-sized spires.

I perceive the world not through eyes, but through density and resonance. The towering bastions appear to my sensory field as immense, dark voids of solidity—areas where the vibration smacks against hard surfaces, creating echoes that ripple through my gaseous body. The "ground" below is a confused tapestry of damp acoustic reflections.

But what draws my attention, what acts as a beacon in the gray, vibrating murk, are the Weepers.

Because I am composed of acoustic-sensitive mist, I feel the intense pockets of pressure they create long before I "see" their translucent forms. To me, the Weepers on the distant bastion walls are incandescent points of sensory overload. They are like throbbing heat sources in a cold room, only the "heat" is pure, concentrated vibration.

Currently, I am drifting toward one of the bastions. The sheer, slick cliff of fused cellulose looms out of the fog. High up on its flank, I sense the intense drumming of a Weeper. I am drawn to it. It is the most defined thing in my blurry, echoing world.

As my misty form brushes against the flank of the bastion, some of my outer droplets condense onto the calcified bark, joining the eternal weeping of the structure. I slide upwards on the warm updrafts, approaching the Weeper.

I am now enveloping it. The sensation is overwhelming. The creature is a massive, shivering tuning fork. The Thrum here is no longer just a background noise; it is a physical violence that threatens to tear my loosely joined water molecules apart. I feel the cymatic patterns rippling on the Weeper's gelatinous skin transferring directly into my own vaporous substance. For a few moments, I am synchronized with its intense trembling.

I hang there, a cloud of sentient moisture wrapped around a shivering sack of biological jelly, high above a motionless, screaming world. I am a temporary traveler, borrowing coherence from the sound, observing the static monuments of an interrupted creation.
