The air is thick enough to taste—a metallic tang of bloody pennies and the sharp, headache-inducing scent of ozone. You are wedged tightly in a chaotic intersection of textures. To your left is the sheer, weeping face of the geological fracture, cold slate bleeding streaks of brilliant turquoise oxidized copper. To your right, pressing hard against you, is a fused mass of industrial refuse—a rusted engine block cemented to a shattered ceramic insulator by a hardened, vibrant blue paste.

You are near the bottom of the gorge, deep in the choked density of the place. The primary sensation is a vibration that permeates everything, a low-frequency thrum that resonates up through the compacted junk floor. It is the deep, subterranean bass note of the Resonance Crabs buried far below, a sound felt in the core of your being rather than heard.

But over that deep hum, there is an immediate, frantic soundscape. *Drip. Hiss. Click.*

Condensation gathers on a jagged overhang of copper wiring above you, falling with a heavy *splat* onto a patch of iridescent sludge inches away. The sludge sizzles faintly, the bacteria within turning the moisture into a weak acid that eats at the metal beneath it. You feel the heat of this constant chemical churning; the gorge is feverishly warm.

You are entirely immobile. Patience is your defining characteristic. You are tucked beneath a protective canopy of fused circuit boards, your body pressed into a dark crevice. If anything were to look at you, they wouldn't see a living creature. They would see a smooth, bulbous lump of pale, rigid material, identical to the fractured ceramic insulators littered throughout the chasm. You are insulated, compact, and still as stone.

Below your perch, the damp, glittering ecosystem continues its churning cycle. A massive Conduit-Slug heaves its translucent bulk through the copper muck, the electrolytic cells inside its segmented body glowing with a faint, gaseous light. It leaves a trail of warm, refined metal nodules in its wake. You ignore it. It is too heavy, too grounded.

Your attention is focused entirely on the intricate structure you have spent the last two days constructing across a narrow gap in the debris. It is invisible in the dim light filtering from above. It is not made of sticky silk, but of incredibly fine, ductile copper filaments you harvested and extruded from the environment. The web is strung taut between a weeping copper vein in the wall and the exposed terminal of a decaying industrial battery.

You can *feel* the potential energy humming through your trap. You are tuned to the voltage of the gorge.

A vibration reaches you through the junk pile—sharp, jerky, mechanical. *Scrape-click-scrape.* A scavenger is coming.

A Galvanic Roach skitters into view on the ledge opposite your trap. It is a grotesque mosaic of rust flakes and tin snippets, its legs moving with the frantic, ticking rhythm of a broken clock. It pauses, its antennae—two stiff lengths of salvaged wire—twitching as it tastes the air for the sulfurous bloom of corrosion it feeds upon. It sees a patch of rich verdigris on the other side of the gap.

It tenses, its hinge-jointed legs compressing, and leaps.

It doesn't see the copper strands until it is mid-air. A jagged, conductive segment of its carapace brushes against your web.

*SNAP.*

A blinding blue arc of electricity discharges across the gap. The sound is a sharp crack that momentarily silences the dripping of the gorge. The roach spasms violently in mid-air, its mechanical rhythm instantly fused, and drops like a stone onto the sludge below, smoking faintly.

Your patience pays off. You detach from your ceramic camouflage, your multiple, articulated legs unfolding. You do not lower yourself on a thread; you climb down the electrified copper lattice of your own web, your insulated body impervious to the current that just killed your prey. You reach the paralyzed roach. Its armored plating is thick, but your mandibles are designed to shear through thin-gauge steel. You bite down through a rusted seam, injecting the potent, bubbling acids that will dissolve its metallic viscera into a nutrient slurry.

You are a **Ferro-Weaver**, and the current has provided.
