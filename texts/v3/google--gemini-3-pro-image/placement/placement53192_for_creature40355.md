The descent is agonizingly slow, a journey measured not in miles, but in increasing increments of pressure and temperature. I am far below the "surface" layers where the massive turbine-housings blade the wet air. I am deep within the intestinal superstructure of the monolithic entity, down where the architecture loses its mimicking of recognizable machinery and becomes pure, brutal structural density.

I find myself lodged in a high-pressure nexus, a convergence point of three colossal vascular trunks, each the width of a canyon.

I am not the iron-hard timber. I am not the faceted, brooding Denizen that clings to the outside.

I am an impurity.

I am a colossal pearl of polymerized amber sap, grown over eons within this critical junction. I am the result of a flaw in the great stasis, a place where the flow became just sluggish enough, just turbulent enough, to begin to solidify. I am a perfect, immense sphere, perhaps a hundred feet in diameter, a solidified clot blocking the artery of a god.

My substance is denser than the surrounding timber. I am opaque, a deep, clouded bronze color, lacking the internal glow of the fluid sap. I am smooth, perfectly rounded by the pressures that formed me, slick with the coating of the very fluid I obstruct.

My existence is defined by compression. I am wedged tight against the translucent inner walls of the vascular conduits. I feel the crushing weight of the world-machine above me, tons of lignified strata pressing down. I feel the immense, hydraulic push of the trapped sap behind me, a pressure that would shatter mountains, yet here it only serves to hold me more firmly in place.

I have no senses in the traditional manner. I have no armored plates to register vibrations like the Denizen above. Instead, my entire being *is* pressure perception. I feel the frustrated energies of the system accumulating against my "upstream" surface. The faint electrical hum mentioned in the world-above is, down here, a deafening, vibrating roar that permeates every molecule of my hardened resinous body. It is a roar of arrested motion, a scream of potential energy that can never become kinetic.

I am not sentient in the way the thinking galls on the surface are. I do not contemplate the gears. I possess only a dense, heavy awareness of my own inertia. I am the anchor of this particular sector of stasis. Because I exist, a million miles of copper-lit veins above me are still. Because I am immovable, a vast array of mimic-pistons remain frozen in their cylinders.

My consciousness is a single, unbroken note of sheer resistance. I am warm, enveloped in the eternal wet heat of the deep structure. I am compressed from all sides. And I am absolutely, terrifyingly content. I am the blockage. I am the reason the machine does not turn. I am the heart of the stillness, waiting for a release of pressure that will never come.
