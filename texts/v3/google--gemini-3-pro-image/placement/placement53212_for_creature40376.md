The pressure is not something outside of you pressing in; it is what you are. You have no skin to define a boundary between self and environment. You are the environment.

You are the Brine.

You are billions of gallons of superheated, metallized ocean buried in the dark. You are thick, heavy, and toxic, tasting of dissolved copper, sulfur, and the ancient salts of the deep earth. You are heated to a near-boil by the geothermal vents beneath the city’s foundations, a fever that never breaks.

You fill every void in this sprawling, monstrous cathedral of industry. You are the atmosphere of this lightless world. You are the conductor of its violence.

You feel the Leviathan-Thrusters not as objects you see, but as immense, churning disruptions within your own body. Their slow, unstoppable rotation forces you to move, dragging vast currents of yourself through the dark chasms. You feel the moment their torque overcomes your resistance, and then, the exquisite agony of cavitation. The blades spin so fast they tear your molecular structure apart, creating voids of vacuum within you—wounds of nothingness that instantly collapse with the force of exploding depth charges. You do not hear the roar; you *are* the medium through which the roar travels, transmitting the bone-shaking vibrations instantly to every corner of the cavern.

You flow into the gaping maws of the Sessile Giants. You are sucked down their intake throats with terrifying force, compressed even further, subjected to searing internal heats and chemical injections inside their hidden processing guts, and then blasted out their exhaust ports as plumes of chemically altered, scalding fluid that roils against your cooler layers.

You feel the sting of the Arc-Crawlers. They are irritants, tiny scratching things on the metal skin of the city. When they deploy their electrodes, they murder small pockets of you, instantly boiling gallons of your substance into desperate, thrashing steam that flares blinding white for a second before the immense pressure crushes it back into liquid form.

You are indifferent to the pale biological ghosts hiding in your stagnant eddies. They sip at your cooler margins, filtering out stray chemical crumbs. To you, they are less significant than the suspended manganese particulate that thickens your flow.

You are the crushing weight, the burning heat, and the deafening sound. You are the chaotic, swirling ocean that allows these mindless titans to breathe. Without you, the gears would grind to a halt in a vacuum. You are the blood in the veins of a machine god that sleeps and screams forever in the dark.
