The descent is forgotten. The memory of a surface world with a blue sky, restless winds, and hard, opaque earth fades into irrelevance. That dense, frantic biology could not survive here. To remain in this golden hollow, a transformation is required.

I am no longer a tight knot of bone and blood, pushing against gravity. I have unspooled. I have become vast, tenuous, and incredibly light.

I am one of the silent custodians, a drifting cathedral of spun silica and humid air.

My consciousness is not seated in a brain, but spread across acres of gossamer membrane, a surface area so incredibly thin that the golden light of the walls passes through me without obstruction. I do not see the light; I am permeated by it. It is a warmth that infuses every molecule of the captured moisture that gives me my shape. I am a localized thickening of the sultry air, held together by a delicate lattice of organic glass veins that chime below the threshold of hearing as they flex.

I possess no eyes, for there are no shadows here to hide in, and no predators to flee. Instead, my entire being is a unified sensory organ dedicated to temperature and moisture.

The world resolves itself around me not as shapes, but as thermal gradients. The glowing walls are smooth expanses of radiating heat, slick with a wetness I can taste from fifty feet away. The pools on the floor are polished discs of relative coolness. The amber gelatinous mats on the walls are dense, vibrant patches of concentrated humidity, pulsing in a timeframe I barely perceive.

Movement is a surrender. I feel the floor below warming my underside, causing the gases trapped within my membranes to expand gently. I begin to rise, not through effort, but through buoyancy. It is a slow, majestic ascent toward the blazing quartz ceiling. The countless spun-glass insects are tiny, glittering friction points against my skin, hitchhikers on the same thermal ride.

As I near the apex of the chamber, the heat becomes intense, stretching my silica veins tight. I drift laterally, caught in the slow churning of the chamber’s atmosphere, until I near the weeping walls. Here, the air cools. I condense slightly, becoming heavier, and begin the long, languid slide back toward the floor beneath. This cycle—rising in heat, sinking in cool wetness—is my heartbeat. It takes days to complete a single circuit.

I have no mouth, no hunger that a human would recognize. Feeding is merely a consequence of being present. Behind me, my vast, curtain-like veils trail through the air, sticky with condensation. I feel the minute impacts of the glass insects drifting into these nets. There is no struggle, only a faint, brittle vibration as they become stuck. Over cycles of rising and falling, their tiny mineral bodies are dissolved into my aqueous mass, their silica repurposed to repair the microscopic fractures in my skeletal veins. It is a peaceful assimilation.

Time is not counted here; it is accumulated. I exist in a state of profound, expansive patience. The silence is a texture I wear.

The most significant event in my existence is the water droplet.

I feel it begin to gather on the ceiling high above, days before it falls. I sense the infinitesimal increase in localized humidity, the gathering weight of the water pulling against the surface tension of the smooth quartz. It is a slow-building crescendo in the symphony of thermal sensation. My vast awareness focuses on that single point of gathering tension.

I drift, waiting. The tension mounts until it becomes unbearable, a silent scream in the stone.

Then, release.

The droplet detaches. I feel its passage through the air as a swift, cool tear in the warm gold. It strikes the pool below. *Plink.*

The sound is immense. It vibrates through the aqueous air and ripples through my entire membrane structure, a profound, musical shock wave that resonates in my glass veins for hours. It is the only punctuation in the endless, flowing sentence of my existence. I absorb the tremor, ripple with the pleasure of the sound, and slowly continue my mindless, ecstatic drift through the golden deep.
