The primary sensory experience, however, is wetness. The atmosphere is so super-saturated that the air itself feels like a thin liquid, condensing instantly on any surface, slicking skin and metal alike with a greasy sheen.

The rock faces are sharp, crystalline fractures of basalt and schist that look as though they were violently torn apart only yesterday. Water weeps convulsively from every fissure in the ceiling and walls, cascading down in shimmering sheets and collecting in deep, oily pools that cover most of the floor.
