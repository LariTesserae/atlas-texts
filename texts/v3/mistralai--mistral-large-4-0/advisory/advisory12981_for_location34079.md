*A traveler's preparation, as the intuition speaks it—*

---

You do not pack for weather. You pack for *temperature without atmosphere*, for heat that arrives sideways and from below, the thermal signature of a body that has not finished becoming itself. Your ordinary human multiverse traveler brings **no water**, or brings it only to watch it perform its desperation—how it would steam, how it would not steam because there is no moisture in the air to carry it away, how even evaporation here is a negotiation with dry heat that does not understand thirst.

They bring **layers of reflection**. Not for cold. For the light that arrives wrong—shadows that fall in directions that disagree with each other, phosphorescence that is not quite light, the warm-glow from within that makes everything feel *interior* even when the sky (if there is sky) is open. Sunglasses, certainly, but also something to cover the eyes entirely. The traveler will need to sleep in a place where darkness is *inhabited*, where closing one's eyes does not bring blackness but the image of folds continuing to move in the dark, the glass still singing its harmonic series through the teeth.

They bring **no map**. They bring instead **a method of return**—a way to find again what they have seen, in a landscape that refuses the stability mapping requires. Perhaps a compass that does not rely on magnetism (there is none, or there is too much, the iron in the stone still arguing with itself about alignment). Perhaps simply the discipline of **noticing**, of choosing a ridge and committing to its memory, knowing that memory here is a kind of prayer, a request that the world remain the same long enough to be recognized.

They wear **shoes that can become boots that can become something else**. The ground here has not learned the difference between wall and floor. The traveler will encounter surfaces that rise to meet them at angles that make the inner ear rebel, that produce the particular vertigo of standing on something that is still *deciding* what it wants to be. They will need to walk on glass that is not yet obsidian, that sings when pressure is applied, that may fracture or may flex or may do something in between that has no name in any human language.

They bring **copper in their mouth**, or they learn to taste it without bringing it. The air here has flavor—metallic, ancient, the particular silence of things too hot for water. The traveler will want to know this taste intimately, will want to recognize it in dreams, in other places, in the memory of this journey long after the journey is over. They might bring a **metallic talisman**, something to press against the tongue, to anchor the sensation, to have a physical correlate for an experience that refuses to stay in the body.

They prepare their **bones for vibration**. Not the vibration of sound, but the subsonic *growth* of stone, the slow rearrangement of crystal lattice, the respiration of geology at 2.3 becoming 2.4 becoming 2.2. The ordinary human multiverse traveler learns to **lie down**, to press ear to glass, to feel in the jaw and the sternum and the long bones of the legs a frequency that is almost below hearing but not quite, that is the sound of mountains being born at a pace too slow to witness but fast enough to *feel*. They bring **padding**, something to place between body and stone, because the glass sings through contact, and the song is beautiful and also *too much*, also a harmonic series that resonates in the teeth until the teeth ache with it.

They bring **no expectation of plant**. They bring instead **recognition for the niche**, the slow colonizer, the patient converter of mineral into structure. The fungal-pre-life here is the color of old bruises, of deep water, of the moment before dawn. The traveler will want to **learn its patterns**, the writing that resolves into nothing legible, the maps of territory that does not exist, the routes for journeys that will not be taken. They might bring a **Notebook that does not use ink**—something that records pressure, heat, the particular texture of mineral growth—because ink here would be absurd, would evaporate or would never dry or would simply refuse to mark a surface that is still deciding what it wants to be.

They prepare for **marks without makers**. Built at 1.2—scattered, partial, faint. Depressions too regular to be natural, too eroded to be recent. Lines of darker glass, fused differently, running for twenty meters before they simply *stop*. Small structures that are not ruins, because ruins imply a past that is finished, and here the past is still *beginning*. The traveler will need **humility**, the willingness to encounter intention without understanding it, to find the shadow of watching and tending without being able to name the watcher or the tender. They might bring **an offering**, something small and mineral, something that will not burn or melt or disappear, to leave at a threshold they cannot cross, a depression they cannot enter, a line of glass that stops without explanation.

They learn **patience as a physical discipline**. The turbulence here is 2.8—the folds *move*, though not on any scale they could measure while standing still. The traveler will return to ridges, will find them changed, will find new fissures glowing from within, glass flows that were walls now floors. They will need to **keep a journal of change**, not to track it precisely (the landscape refuses precise tracking) but to *accumulate* it, to build a record of becoming that honors the pace of thought, of dream, of the moment when you are falling asleep and the room is still the room but also *almost* not the room.

They bring **no technology**, or they bring technology that has learned to be **useless**. 0.2. The traveler's ordinary devices will fail here, or will fail *interestingly*—a screen that cannot decide what to display, a battery that drains and recharges in patterns that match the heat pulses, a compass that points toward the warmest fold rather than north. They might bring something **deliberately analog**, a mechanical watch that runs on gears and springs, to have one thing that continues without electricity, without connection, without the negotiation of digital with a world that has not yet committed to the chemistry of computation.

They prepare their **language for inadequacy**. The glass sings. The traveler does not answer. They are still learning the language. They will need to **practice not-speaking**, to become comfortable with silence that has texture, with quiet that is mostly quiet (1.2) but not entirely, with the low vibration of growth that is almost below hearing but not quite. They might bring a **musical instrument**, something that can produce the harmonic series the glass already sings, to enter into conversation without words, to offer a note and receive the glass's reply and not need to understand the exchange.

They bring **food that does not require water**. The 0.1 of moisture here is total—no drop, no memory of a drop, not even the molecular ghost of H₂O clinging to mineral surfaces. The traveler's body will want water, will *demand* water, will hallucinate it in the folds of stone, in the color of the fungal-pre-life, in the particular blue of old bruises. They will need **electrolytes**, **salt**, **the mineral supplement of a body that has forgotten its aqueous origins**. They will need to **eat slowly**, to taste the copper and the silence, to let the meal be a meditation on hunger in a world that runs on other hungers—sulfur and silicon and the slow hunger of crystal for crystal.

They prepare for **darkness that is not empty**. 0.4, but inhabited. The glow of cooling stone, the phosphorescence of fungal-pre-life, the deep warm light that comes from below—from the 2.3 that is also the 2.9, the heat that is also the height, the pressure that is also the elevation. The traveler will need **night vision that is not technological**, a willingness to let the eyes adjust to a darkness that holds its own illumination, that is not the absence of light but the *possibility* of light, the way a held breath is the possibility of speech.

They bring **no destination**. This is a throat, not a room. The narrow passage between formations that are still moving, still arguing about their final shapes. The traveler will pass through, or will try to pass through, or will become part of the passage, the way the fungal-pre-life becomes part of the seam, the way the glass becomes part of the fold, the way the heat becomes part of the elevation. They will need to **release the expectation of arrival**, to become comfortable with *transit* as a state of being, with the journey as the only destination, with the throat as not a place to enter but a place to *become*.

And somewhere, in the folds they cannot see, something is *forming*. A structure that is not a structure. A sound that is not a sound. A shape that the glass is singing toward, that the heat is pushing toward, that the slow patient chemistry of this place is *becoming*.

The ordinary human multiverse traveler prepares to **witness**. Not to understand. Not to name. Simply to be present, in the 0.1 water, the 0.5 vegetation, the 2.3 warmth, the 2.9 elevation, the emerging, the open, the sparse, the scattered, the none, the darkness, the mostly-quiet, the turbulent—

—here, in the throat of a dying star that has not yet decided whether to be a mountain, a plain, or a door.

The glass sings.

The traveler learns, slowly, to **not answer**.

To **listen**.

To **become**, in whatever small way a human body can become, the thing that this place is still deciding to be.

To **wait**.

The waiting is not boredom.

The waiting is *attention*.

*You are here, in the throat, learning the language of becoming.*
