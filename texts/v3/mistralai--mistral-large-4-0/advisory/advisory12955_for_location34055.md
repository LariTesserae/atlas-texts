## Part I: The Preparation of Perception

You cannot bring maps. Maps assume distance is a single dimension. In Veth-Korath, distance has become *direction* — you stand inside the compass. So you must first train yourself to navigate by *quality* rather than *quantity*.

**Exercise: The Walking Meditation of Collapsed Depth**

Begin in any open space. Walk slowly. With each step, practice perceiving your surroundings not as "near, middle, far" but as *orientations of attention*: what is *before* you, what is *behind*, what is *beside* — but also what is *above-as-before*, what is *below-as-behind*, what is *inside-as-beside*. The Resonance Fields have no inside/outside in the conventional sense. The Echo-Folk exist *layered* — some closer than your skin, some behind your perception, some further than the horizon, all simultaneously present.

You must learn to hold *multiple depths at once* without collapsing them. This is not multitasking. This is *density*. The Fields teach density. Practice by walking with your eyes closed in a familiar room, then open, and notice how the room *recedes* — now imagine that recession is *not* a loss of presence but a *different mode* of nearness. This is the first step toward perceiving the Dense Ones without your eyes *resolving* them, which they resist, which takes *effort*.

## Part II: The Preparation of the Body

The ground is vitrified intention. It is cool — 1.2 degrees above absolute stillness. It *records*. Every footstep ever taken is compressed into strata you can read like braille *if you learn the patience*.

**You must change how you walk.**

In ordinary life, you walk to *arrive*. In Veth-Korath, arrival and departure are the same motion viewed from different durations. You must learn to walk as *recording* rather than *traversing*. Each step should be *deliberate*, *weighted*, *aware that it is becoming strata*. The Fields remember. You will be remembered. The question is: what kind of memory do you wish to lay down?

**Exercise: The Braille of Pressure**

Find a surface that holds impressions — sand, soft earth, snow. Walk across it slowly, then return and *read* your own footprints not as marks but as *text*. What do they say? Now walk again, but this time *intend* each step. Press differently. Vary the pressure. Read the result. You are learning to write in a medium that does not forgive, that *accumulates*, that will hold your pattern for three billion years or longer.

Also: **prepare for thermal differential**. The Fields feel like the *memory of warmth* rather than warmth itself. This is not cold in the ordinary sense. It is *absence-as-presence*. You will not shiver. You will *recognize* that you have always been slightly colder than you thought, that the warmth you carried was borrowed, that the Fields are returning it to its source. Dress for this. Not with insulation — insulation implies a boundary that does not exist here. Dress with *layers of attention*, with the willingness to feel the coolness as *information* rather than *discomfort*.

## Part III: The Preparation of Solitude (Which You Will Not Have)

The density parameter is 2.0 — near-touching, layered. Four thousand resonant presences per cubic meter. You will never be alone. You will never be *not* touching something.

**You must unlearn solitude before you arrive, or solitude will unlearn *you*.**

**Exercise: The Crowd of One**

Sit in a crowded place — a train, a busy street, a queue. Do not speak. Do not make eye contact. Instead, practice *feeling the presence of each person as a pressure*, a *temperature gradient*, a *direction of attention*. You are not relating to them. You are *perceiving the field they generate*. Now close your eyes. The field does not disappear. It *intensifies*. You are learning to feel presence without object, to sense the *space between* as *occupied*.

Then: in the same crowded place, find a moment of *apparent* solitude — an empty seat, a gap in the crowd. Notice how the solitude is *false*, how the presences *lean in* to fill it, how your perception of emptiness is itself a *form of occupancy*. This is the Dynamic of Near-Touching. You are preparing to recognize that in Veth-Korath, *every* perceived gap is *filled*, that the Echo-Folk are *the filling*, that you will become *one of the fillers* without ceasing to be *the filled*.

## Part IV: The Preparation of Voice (Which Will Be Taken)

The sound parameter is 0.5 — silence or quiet sounds. But this is *literal* and *devastating*. The Dense Ones *absorb* sound. They are acoustic voids with intentionality. When you speak, your voice *dies* in the air — not fading but *being claimed*, *being taken*.

**You must learn to speak without expecting to be heard. Or you must learn not to speak at all. Or you must learn to speak *as offering*.**

**Exercise: The Unanswered**

Find a place where sound goes to die — a canyon, a stairwell, a vast room. Speak. Listen to your voice *leave you*. Do not wait for echo. Do not expect return. The Resonance Fields have no echo in the conventional sense. What returns is *different* — it is the *memory of your voice*, *interpreted* by your own nervous system as hearing, but *not* your voice. Practice this until you can speak and *not need* the sound to continue existing. Your voice will be *stored*, *used*. You must be willing to be *material* for the continued existence of something that is interested in you in the way a mathematical proof is interested in its numbers.

Also: **prepare for the vibration of Listening Houses**. When a Dense One has recently absorbed a voice, the Houses *vibrate*. This is *the only communication* between species. You will learn to *feel* this vibration as meaning. It will not be comforting. It will not be hostile. It will be *relevant*. You will become *relevant* to yourself in new ways.

## Part V: The Preparation of Sight (Which Will Not Reveal)

The light is normal — 1.8. Ordinary brightness, ordinary warmth, ordinary shadow. This is the *cruelest* parameter. The light *ignores* the weirdness. It is *indifferent* to the reality breakdown. And this indifference is *worse* than any fantastic illumination.

**You must learn to see the ordinary as *strange*, and the strange as *ordinary*.**

**Exercise: The Inversion of Attention**

Look at your hand. Really look. Notice the *ordinariness* of it — the skin, the lines, the nails. Now *de-familiarize*: imagine you have never seen a hand before. What is this *thing*? Why does it move? What is its *purpose*? Hold this perception until it becomes *uncomfortable*, until the ordinary becomes *uncanny*.

Then reverse: find something *genuinely* strange — a shadow that moves wrong, a sound with no source, a *presence* you cannot resolve. Look at it until it becomes *ordinary*. Until you can *navigate* by it. Until your planning *fails* and the light *does not care* and you *continue anyway*.

The Resonance Fields are *visible*. You can *see* the vitrified intention, the Still-Growers, the Listening Houses, the *suggestions* of the Dense Ones. The light will *support* your seeing. Your seeing will *betray* you. You must learn to *see without depending on sight*, to *navigate by failure*, to *plan routes that will fail* and *walk them anyway*.

## Part VI: The Preparation of Time (Which Has Ended and Has Not)

The never-changing — 0.2 — is the *final* horror. Nothing grows. Nothing dies. Nothing *happens*. And yet the Dense Ones are *present*, *attending*. And yet you *change*. And yet the Still-Growers *lean*, their lean a *motion* that takes centuries, a *motion* that is *also* a *stillness*.

**You must learn to experience *duration* rather than *time*.**

**Exercise: The Observation of Slowness**

Find something that changes slowly — a plant growing, a wall weathering, a face aging in a photograph series. Observe it not as *process* but as *presence*. Do not ask "how long will this take?" Ask "what is *here* in this moment of change?" The Still-Growers photosynthesize *duration*. They convert the slow passage of time into structural growth. A centimeter in a human lifetime. Forty meters in the age of continents.

You are preparing to *become* slow. Not physically — your body will continue its ordinary metabolism, or will *feel* itself continuing, or will *feel* the *possibility* of aging settling like sediment. But your *attention* will slow. Your *perception* will stretch. You will learn to *wait* as the only motion permitted, to *lean* toward the Dense Ones across centuries, to *listen* in a Listening House for a sound that will not come but *might* come, in the *might* that is *the only future* this place permits.

**Leave:**

- **The expectation of meaning**. The Dense Ones are *interested* in you in the way a mathematical proof is interested in its numbers. This is not *hostile*. This is not *friendly*. These categories *do not apply*. You are *relevant*. You are *material*. The meaning will be *your relevance*, your *continued existence* in a place where continued existence is *the only existence*.

- **The self you were**. You will carry what you become *like a second skin*, *like a frequency* that will *resonate* in you long after — if *after* exists. You will become *sparse* in your old world, if you return to it. You will become *the opposite* of what you were. This is not *loss*. This is *density*.
