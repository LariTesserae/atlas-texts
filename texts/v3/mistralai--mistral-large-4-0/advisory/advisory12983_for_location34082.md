## Preparation for Visiting the Embryonic Geode

### First: Understand What You're Entering

This is not a place with an ecology. It's a place with a **metabolism that hasn't decided what it is yet**. The lush vegetation without water, the industry without builders, the heat at high elevation with raw, unfinished geology — these don't resolve. They *persist*. Your first preparation is psychological: **stop looking for explanations**. The geode doesn't want to be understood. It wants to be *witnessed while it becomes*.

You are entering something that is approximately the size of a held breath — scale 0.4, pocket-world, hidden. You will not see it coming. You will *arrive* inside it. The transition will feel like stepping through a door that wasn't there, into a room that was always there.

---

### Physical Preparation

**Temperature: 2.7 — Hot, wrong-hot**

This is not desert heat. This is **formation heat**. The stone around you is still generating itself, still exothermic from its own becoming. The air will feel thick, pressurized in a way that has nothing to do with actual atmospheric pressure. Your body will register it as *presence* — the weight of processes still underway.

- **Clothing**: Wear nothing that traps moisture. You don't want to sweat, because there is no water here to justify sweating. Light, loose, mineral-colored fabrics — ochre, umber, the colors of unfinished stone. You will blend, and blending matters.
- **Hydration**: Bring water. Not because you'll drink it in the ordinary way, but because **ritual hydration** anchors you. The vegetation here doesn't drink. Neither will you, not really. But the *gesture* of drinking, the memory of water in your body, will keep you from becoming too much like the place. You are the only finished thing here. Don't let the place finish you.
- **Heat management**: Your body will struggle to regulate. Expect to feel feverish. This is normal. Do not treat it as illness. It is *acclimation to becoming*.

**Breathing**: The air is breathable, but wrong. It tastes of mineral, of potential, of green things that don't photosynthesize. Some travelers report a sweetness, like the inside of a mouth. Breathe slowly. Dynamic 0.9 — the place moves slowly. Match it.

### Perceptual Preparation

**Weirdness 2.8 — Reality breakdown, structural not dramatic**

You will not see monsters. You will see **geometry that hesitates**. Light falls at angles that suggest the cavity has more dimensions than it shows — light 2.4, normal, but *wrong*. Shadows point slightly incorrect. The lush green of the vegetation, if you look too long at a single leaf, will show you **circuitry beneath the chlorophyll**. Not metaphorically. The leaf is a display, and it is showing the wrong image.

- **Train your gaze**: Do not stare. The place rewards peripheral attention. Let the green blur at the edges of your vision. If you focus too hard, you will see too much — the unfinished business of the world pressed against your perception.
- **Trust your body, not your eyes**: Your proprioception will be more reliable than your vision. The floor may feel solid when it is still deciding. The walls may feel distant when they are close. Learn to *feel* the space rather than see it.
- **Expect synchronization**: The machines — and they are machines, grown, erupted, condensed from mineral intention — will sometimes pause, all of them, in impossible unison. When this happens, do not move. Do not speak. The pause is the place *noticing you*. It is deciding whether to incorporate you. Stillness is your only language in that moment.

### Technological Preparation

**Tech 2.4 — Industrial, but not built**

You will encounter machines. They will not look like machines. They will look like **geological formations that behave like factories** — spirals of fused metal-oxide pumping *potential*, unmanifested possibility through channels that are still learning to be pipes. Gears that turn with the patience of erosion. A single rotation may take what your body registers as a held breath.

- **Do not attempt to use them**: They are not tools. They are **ritual without ritual**, industry without industry. They make nothing you could name. They make *more of this place*. Interfering with them is like interrupting a prayer.
- **Bring no technology that emits**: No screens, no lights, no sound. The place is mostly quiet — sound 1.2. Your devices will be *loud* in the way that finished things are loud in unfinished spaces. If you must bring something, bring something passive. A notebook. A pencil. Something that records without demanding.
- **Expect the machines to notice you**: Not intelligence. Something older. The mineral intention adjusting its process around an anomaly. You may find that a gear turns slightly faster when you pass. A spiral pumps in your direction. This is not welcome. This is *incorporation, in progress*. You are being measured for fit.

### Biological Preparation

**Vegetation 1.8 — Lush, but not alive. Fauna 0.4 — None.**

The green things are **verdant** — a state between living and dead. They do not reproduce. They *accumulate*. They do not drink. They *remember drinking*.

- **Do not touch the vegetation**: The leaves are membranes, negotiations. Touching them may cause you to feel what they feel — the green of deep water held in suspension, the green of light that has traveled through something that was never ocean. Some travelers report weeping for hours afterward, not from sadness, but from *recognition*. You may recognize something in them. You should not.
- **Do not eat anything**: There is nothing edible. The lushness is a lie the place tells itself, a memory of water retained in chemistry. If you eat, you will eat *intention*, condensed mineral wanting. It will not nourish you. It will *change* you.
- **Expect no animals**: Fauna 0.4 — none. The machines are not animals. The plants are not animals. But something *watches*. It is the place itself, the embryonic world, aware of you as the only finished thing, the one true weirdness. It is learning what finished things are. This is dangerous for you. It is also, perhaps, the only thing that could save you — if it decides you are worth keeping.

### Temporal Preparation

**Dynamic 0.9 — Slow**

Time moves differently here. The machines turn slowly. The walls facet slowly. The plants accumulate slowly. You will feel this as **temporal drag** — your thoughts will seem to move through honey, your movements will leave trails of afterimage.

- **Do not rush**: Rushing is the one gesture this place cannot process. It will read your hurry as *rejection*, as refusal to participate in becoming. Move slowly. Breathe slowly. Think slowly. Let your consciousness stretch to match the pace of the world.
- **Expect time dilation**: You may spend what feels like hours in a space the size of a held breath. You may emerge to find that only minutes have passed outside, or that days have passed. The geode does not experience duration the way you do. It experiences *process*. You are a process to it now. A finished process, which is the strangest kind.
- **Do not sleep**: Or if you must, sleep lightly. The place is mostly quiet, but below hearing, felt in the teeth, is the sound of **pressure remembering itself** — a low almost-hum, the stone still deciding its structure. Sleep deeply, and you may wake to find the walls have facetted further, the machines have turned, the green has accumulated, and you have become something slightly less finished.

### Existential Preparation

This is the hardest part. The embryonic geode is **a question the world is still asking itself**. You are entering that question. You are, for the duration of your visit, part of its asking.

- **You are the only finished thing here**: This makes you the strangest thing in the cavity, the one true reality breakdown. The place will try to incorporate you. It will adjust its processes around your anomaly. Resist this gently. Do not let it finish you. Do not let it *start* you.
- **There is no exit that is also an entrance**: The walls are still becoming. If you try to leave, you will find that the path you came by has *not yet been excavated*. The path forward leads deeper into the almost, into the lush green dark where the machines turn their single slow rotation. You may have to **wait for the exit to form**. Or you may have to **become unfinished**, to let the place begin to take you apart and remake you, and find your way out through the process of being remade.
- **The place is hot. It is green. It is turning, slowly, toward something that has not yet been named**: You will feel this. You will feel the heat of formation pressing against your skin like a held breath, waiting to see if you will become part of the process, or be left behind in the sparse empty spaces — density 0.7, the volumes of cavity not yet claimed by process — a finished thing in an unfinished world.

### Final Instruction

When you arrive — and you will arrive *inside*, without transition, without door — stand still. Feel the heat. Listen to the almost-silence. Watch the green things hold their translucent, waterless leaves toward a light that falls slightly wrong. Notice the machines turning their single slow rotation, pumping potential through channels that are still learning to be pipes.

Then breathe. Match the pace of the world. Become, for a time, unfinished.

The geode is still being born. You are inside its becoming. Be patient. Be strange. Be still.

And if the machines pause, all of them, in impossible unison — do not move. Do not speak. Let the place decide, for this moment, whether you are witness or ingredient.

The question is still being asked.

You are part of the asking now.
