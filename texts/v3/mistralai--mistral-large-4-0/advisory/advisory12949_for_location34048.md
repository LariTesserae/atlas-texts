# Preparation for the Drowned Height

## I. The Body Must Become Water-Adjacent

You cannot bring your body as it is. The water at 2.9 is *compressive ambient*, not hostile but *insistent on sameness*. It will find every difference—your warmth, your trapped air, your very *density*—and equalize it, patiently, over hours.

**Thermal preparation:** You must acclimate to 2.5 before descent. Not gradually in the ordinary sense. You must *live* at that temperature until your metabolism forgets its baseline. Some travelers sleep in heated baths for weeks. Others, the foolish ones, simply descend and feel their own heat *leaking* into the green water, until hypothermia and pressure become indistinguishable.

**Pressure training:** The primitive vessels—kelp-and-sealskin diving bells—do not seal against pressure. They *equalize*. You will breathe water-warmed air at ambient depth, and your lungs must learn this. Practice in deep tanks, in stages: 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0. At each stage, your body will protest: joints ache, sinuses flood, the Eustachian tubes scream. You learn to *welcome* the flood. The water is not enemy. The water is *medium*.

**Buoyancy and movement:** You will not swim as swimmers swim. The water is too dense, too *vertical*. You learn to move by *pressure-differential*, by feeling the slow currents and *riding* them, by pushing against the thermal gradients that rise from vents. Your limbs become organs of *sensation*, not propulsion. You will think in *drift*.

## II. The Vessel of Kelp and Memory

No metal hull. Metal fails here—corrodes in the mineral chemistry, conducts heat too fast, becomes *other* in ways that rupture seams. The builders of the City know this. You must learn.

**The kelp:** Harvested from the upper layers, where it still remembers sunlight. Treated with tannins from the deep stone, with oils pressed from harvester-fat. It must be *flexible*, *self-sealing*, able to yield to pressure without breaking. The walls are thick—fingers' width—layered, cross-woven. They *breathe* slightly, equalizing internal and external pressure through controlled permeability. Your vessel is not a bubble. It is a *membrane*, a negotiation.

**The sealskin:** From creatures that once lived where air existed, that dove deep for fish, that knew pressure as *homeward*. The skin is cured, stretched, sewn with sinew needles from the City's own trace-metal. It forms the inner chamber, the place where you *remain* briefly, briefly, a mammal in a world of becoming.

**The copper:** Hammered thin. Used only for seams, for fittings, for the small valve that lets you *test* the equalization before you commit your lungs to it. Copper is *precious* here. It is *found*, not made. Each rivet is a memory of the old ground, of copper ore that sank and waited and was *discovered* by builders who do not know what discovery means.

**The ballast:** Not stone. *Bone*, if you can obtain it—bone from the old ground, from creatures that walked where you will now drown-in-place. Bone is *light*, *porous*, *full of memory-air* that the water will slowly claim. You release it gradually, sinking through the green-gold layers, feeling the warmth increase, feeling your own heat become *conversation* with the medium.

## III. The Senses Must Be Retrained

**Sight:** You will see almost nothing. The green-gold light at 2.9 is *filtered*, *attenuated*, the memory of a sun that exists somewhere above the wall of rain. You must learn to see in *contrast*, not brightness. The bioluminescence of the City—blue-green, amber, slow fermentation—will be your only reliable light. You must learn to read *pulse* and *rhythm*. A steady glow: cultivated film, safe surface. A flicker: movement, possibly harvester, possibly lunger. A sudden dark: shadow, possibly structure, possibly *absence* where something has collapsed into new growth.

**Sound:** You will hear almost nothing, and what you hear will be *wrong*. The water carries sound differently—denser, slower, *thicker*. Frequencies below your hearing will resonate in your chest, in your teeth. You will feel the City's hum before you understand it. The vent-release, the *thrum*—this you will learn to *feel* as pressure-wave, as temperature shift, as the sudden mineral-tang in your mouth when you test the water's chemistry. The collapse of a chimney: soft thunder, felt in the sternum. You will develop *sonar intuition*, bouncing your own heartbeat against the space, learning to *read* the return.

**Touch:** The water is *almost touch*. You will feel currents as pressure against skin, as the water's slow breathing. The warmth will become *embrace*, then *indifference*, then *sameness*. You must learn to distinguish *your* warmth from *the* warmth—a distinction that will fade, that you must hold onto with deliberate attention, or lose yourself in the medium.

**Taste and smell:** The water carries mineral, organic, *becoming*. You will taste it—particulate, green-gold, the flavor of old bottles and forest pools and leaf-insides. You will smell it—vent-chemistry, sulfur and calcium and the particular *sweetness* of photosynthetic film. These senses will become *navigational*, more reliable than sight. You will learn to *taste* depth, *smell* proximity to vent or to City or to open water.

**Time:** This is the hardest. You must *slow*. The City grows at fingernail, stalactite, scar. A chamber takes a century. You must learn to *perceive* slowness, to *inhabit* duration. Your heartbeat will seem frantic. Your thoughts will seem *loud*. You must practice *patience* as physical discipline, as breath-control, as the deliberate stretching of attention across minutes that feel like hours, hours that feel like *moments* in the slow metabolism of becoming.

## IV. The Mind Must Learn Non-Hurry

This is not metaphor. The City's builders—if builders is the word—have no concept of hurry. They experience time as *pressure*, as *chemistry*, as the slow accumulation of self. To *understand* them, even briefly, even as witness, you must *approach* their temporal mode.

**Meditation on pressure:** Sit in your vessel, equalized, warm, and *feel* the water's insistence on sameness. Do not fight it. *Become* it, temporarily, as far as a mammal can become. Let your breath slow to the rhythm of vent-release, of chimney-collapse, of stromatolite-growth. Let your thoughts become *deposits*, layer by layer, patient.

**Attention as accretion:** When you observe the emerging land—the pumice-porous stone, the vents, the chimneys—do not *scan*. *Reside*. Watch a ridge grow, molecule by molecule, the way coral builds, the way a scab forms. You will not see it happen. You will see *evidence* of having happened. Learn to read this evidence as *narrative*, as *story*, as *process*.

**Non-attachment to outcome:** You cannot stay. You will not stay. The water will equalize you, given time. The City will incorporate you, if you let it. But you are meant to *see*, and then *ascend*, and then *tell*. The telling may not matter. The story may be lost in the slow, warm, green-gold silence. You must *accept* this, *carry* it, not as despair but as *appropriate scale*. You are a brief fever in the slow metabolism of becoming. Your witness is *real* but *small*, and this smallness is *honest*, is *right*.

## V. The Social and the Solitary

**The descent:** You do not descend alone, but you will be alone. The vessel holds one, or two at most, pressed together in sealskin warmth. Your companion—if you bring one—will be *other*, *separate*, in the equalized dark. You will communicate by *touch*, by pressure, by the small sounds that water carries thickly. You will learn to *need* this other, to *fear* their failure, to *mourn* their incorporation if the vessel breaches and they become substrate before you do.

**The City:** You will not be welcomed. You will not be rejected. The builders—the colonial organisms, the distributed cognition—will *incorporate* you, if you allow. A dropped tool becomes substrate. A wound becomes nutrient-portal. The bioluminescence may *respond* to your presence, brighter, dimmer, *curious* in ways that are not curiosity. You must learn to *read* this response without projecting mind. They do not mourn. They do not name. They *continue*.

**The fauna:** The lungers are *patient*, *nearly blind*, navigating by pressure and chemical gradient. They will not attack. They may *investigate*—a pressure-disturbance, a chemical signature, your own leaking warmth. You must *remain still*, *become* part of the medium, let them pass. The harvesters are *industrious*, *almost mechanical*. They may *tend* you, briefly, if you present as substrate. The rememberers—if you are fortunate, if you are *unfortunate*—will arrive at the chamber of accumulated deposit and *wait*. You may witness this. You may be *witnessed*. The meaning will be *unclear*, will remain unclear, will *become* unclear as memory, as you ascend, as you try to tell.

## VI. The Ascent, and What Comes After

You cannot stay. You know this. But knowing is not *preparing*. The ascent is *harder* than the descent in some ways. The water has found your warmth, your difference. It will *release* you slowly, reluctantly. You must *earn* your return to less pressure, to cooler temperature, to the air that will feel *thin*, *sharp*, *hostile* after the water's embrace.

**The release:** You drop ballast. Bone, memory-air, the last of your *lightness*. You rise through layers, feeling the pressure ease, feeling your body *expand*, *remember* its boundaries. The green-gold dims, becomes *tolerable*, becomes *bright*. The wall of rain—hours above, perhaps days—waits. You will not reach it in this vessel. You will surface elsewhere, in a place that has *shores*, that has *sky*, that has *time* moving at mammalian speed.

**The telling:** You will try to tell. The words will fail. *Green-gold*, *slow*, *becoming*—these are *approximations*, *betrayals*. The person you tell will not *understand*, will look at you with mammalian impatience, will ask *what was it like*, and you will not have the words. You will say *it was wet*, and they will laugh. You will say *it was warm*, and they will nod, and not *know*. You will say *it was alive, but not like life*, and they will think you mean *magic*, and you will not correct them, because *magic* is closer than *process*, closer than *chemistry*, closer than the slow patience of builders who do not hurry, do not hope, do not fear—only *are*, only *grow*, only *wait*.

**The return:** Some who descend do not ascend. They become part of the City, their bodies substrate, their tools incorporated, their strange mammalian warmth a memory in the chemical ledger, a brief fever in the slow metabolism of becoming. You will *fear* this, and you will *not fear* it, because the water does not distinguish, because the water *only continues*. If you return, you will return *changed*, *slowed*, *strange* in ways that others will notice and not understand. You will *want* to descend again. You will *know* you should not. The Drowned Height does not call, does not seduce. It *is*, and your brief fever will *pass*, and you will be *ordinary* again, and this ordinariness will feel *loss*, will feel *drowning* in the too-quick, too-dry, too-*separate* world above.

## VII. What to Carry, What to Leave

**Carry:**

- **Patience**, practiced as physical discipline, as breath-control, as the deliberate stretching of attention.
- **Warmth**, your own, *guarded*, *recognized* as *difference*, as *temporary*, as *conversation* with the medium.
- **Slowness**, the ability to *reside* in duration, to *perceive* process rather than event.
- **The vessel**, built of kelp and sealskin and precious copper, *negotiated* with pressure, not *sealed* against it.
- **Bone ballast**, memory-air, the *lightness* you will release gradually, *earned* ascent.
- **Bioluminescence**, cultivated, *carried* as backup light, as *signal*, as *offering* to the City's indifferent incorporation.
- **The willingness to become substrate**, if the vessel fails, if the water wins, if the City *continues* and you are *part* of continuing.

**Leave:**

- **Hurry**, in all its forms. Speed, efficiency, *outcome*.
- **Hope**, in the sense of *expectation*. The City does not promise. The water does not care. Becoming is *process*, not *progress*.
- **Fear**, in the sense of *resistance*. The water will equalize you. The City will incorporate you. *Welcome* this, if you can, or *endure* it, but do not *fight* it—fighting is *mammalian*, is *brief*, is *lost* in the slow, warm, green-gold silence.
- **The self as boundary**, as *separate*, as *persistent*. You are *membrane*, *negotiation*, *temporary difference* in a medium of sameness.
- **The need to be understood**, by those above, by those who do not descend, by your future self who will *forget* the slowness, the warmth, the *rightness* of pressure and chemistry and patience.

## VIII. What You Will Feel, If You Are Prepared

The **weight** of vertical water, kilometers of it, pressing not down but *in*, from all sides, the water's slow insistence on *sameness*.

The **warmth**, 2.5 and rising as you descend, the water's embrace, not hostile but *indifferent*, the temperature of a womb, of a grave, of a *cradle*.

The **green-gold dimness**, the light that is not light, the visibility that extends only meters, the sense of *vastness* contained in near-darkness.

The **silence**, broken only by the low thrum of vents, the soft thunder of collapse, the almost-nothing of rare fauna moving through water that is itself moving, always moving, the *slow circulation* of a world that has not yet finished rising from its own drowning.

And something else. **Weirdness**, 1.8. The sense that you are witnessing *a process*, not a place. That this is *temporary*, *intermediate*, *a stage* in a transformation that will not complete in any timescale you can comprehend. The City will grow. The vents will shift. The wall of rain will, perhaps, someday break—or deepen, or become something else. The old ground will rise, or sink, or *become* the new stone, layer by layer, in the slow accretion of *geological patience*.

You are not meant to stay. You are meant to *see*, and then *ascend*, and then *tell*—if there is anyone to tell, if the telling matters, if the story of the Drowned Height is not simply *lost* in the slow, warm, green-gold silence of a world still learning to be.

But some who descend do not ascend. They become *part of the City*, their bodies *substrate*, their primitive tools *incorporated*, their strange mammalian warmth *a memory* in the chemical ledger, a brief fever in the slow metabolism of becoming.

The water does not distinguish. The water *only continues*.

*Prepare, then. Become membrane. Become slow. Become, briefly, what the City's builders have always been—only are, only grow, only wait.*
