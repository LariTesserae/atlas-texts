## Preparation for Visiting Orum

### First, the Body

You will need to become **slow**. Not just physically—though you will need to move slowly, because the heat is not like sun-heat, it is **thick**, **pressing**, **a presence in the lungs**, and it changes how your body thinks. But more than this: you will need to become **responsive**. In Orum, there is no "doing" in the surface sense. There is only **asking and receiving**, **tuning and harmonizing**, **waiting for the world to answer**. Your nervous system will need to learn **a new speed of decision-making**: not fast, not slow, but **thermal**, **following the pulse of the vents**, **the cycle of growth**, **the rhythm of the singing**.

**Breathe before you enter.** The gas is not poisonous—it is **wrong**, **not air**, **thick with mineral vapor**, **sulfur and iron and salts that coat the tongue**. Your first breaths will feel like **breathing soup**, **breathing stone**. You will cough. The people of Orum do not cough—they have **never coughed**, **their lungs grew up with this gas**, **but you will cough**, **and they will hear it**, **a foreign sound**, **a surface-sound**, **and they will be curious**, **or wary**, **or kind**. Let them be kind. Cough into your hand. Do not be ashamed. The cough is **the sound of your body learning**.

**Wear nothing, or almost nothing.** Any cloth you bring will **saturate with heat**, **become a second skin that burns**, **or a barrier that traps sweat you cannot spare**. Better to bring **layers of mineral fiber**—you can find these in Orum, **woven from crystal-silk**, **they will teach you how to wear them**—but arrive **bare**, **let the heat touch your skin directly**, **let your body learn the temperature gradient of the place**, **where it is warm**, **where it is hot**, **where it is too hot to stand**, **the map of heat that is also the map of life**.

### The Senses

**Your eyes will adjust wrong at first.** The light is **dim**—0.6, ember-light, **no sun**, no blue, no green, only **amber and rust and deep orange**, **the colors of cooling metal**, **of sodium flame**, **of the world's own metabolism**. You will want to **see sharply**, **to focus**, **to make out details**, but Orum does not work that way. The clarity you need is **not visual**, it is **thermal**, **you learn to see the heat**, **the glow of vents**, **the brighter crystal**, **the darker shadow where gas pools**, **you learn to read the world in its own light**, **the light it makes for itself**, **not for you**.

**Your ears will be overwhelmed.** The sound is **loud**—1.5, **total**, **the roar of vents**, **the chime of crystals**, **the moan of thermal wind**, **human voices singing across distance**, **drums and horns**, **the singing of the gardens themselves**, **thousands of thermal expansions**, **a forest of bells that never stops**. You will want to **block it**, **to find silence**, but there is no silence in Orum. There is only **different kinds of sound**, **and the skill is to find the voice you need in the chorus**, **the particular vent's roar**, **the particular tower's song**, **your own breathing**, **made strange by the gas**, **made present by the absence of anything to hide it**. Bring **no earplugs**. They will not work. They will only make you **lonely in a place that has no loneliness**, **because loneliness requires silence**, **and Orum has none**.

**Your skin will become a sense organ.** The heat is **not uniform**—it **pulses**, **it varies**, **it tells you where you are**, **what is growing**, **what is dying**, **what the vents are doing**, **what the weather is becoming**. You will learn to **feel the temperature gradient on your face**, **the direction of the thermal wind**, **the nearness of crystal-growth**, **the health of a garden**, **all through skin**. This is **not uncomfortable**—it is **intimate**, **the world touching you back**, **the world that grows**, **the world that sings**, **the world that is always in motion**.

### What to Bring

**Tools for listening.** A **resonator**—a **horn of carved crystal**, or a **drum of stretched mineral membrane**—so you can **sing back**, **become part of the soundscape**, **not just a hearer but a voice**. The people will **teach you the songs**, **the names of vents**, **the genealogies of garden-lines**, **the laws of harvest**, **but you need to be able to make sound first**, **to prove you are not deaf**, **not stone**, **not surface**, **but something that can answer**.

**Tools for seeing heat.** **Polished metal mirrors**, **small, hand-sized**, **to signal with light**, **to communicate across distance when sound fails**, **to read the glow of distant vents**, **to find your way in the ember-dark**. They will **give you better ones**, **Orum's own metal**, **but bring your own to start**, **to show you understand**, **that light is language here**, **that the world speaks in incandescence**.

**A container for moisture.** This sounds strange, because **water is the thing Orum lacks**, but **you will sweat**, **a little, always**, **a thin film that evaporates before it falls**, **and in Orum, moisture is precious**, **it will be collected**, **it will be used**, **it will be part of the mineral cycle**, **the closed loop of heat and growth and return**. Bring a **small skin**, **a bladder**, **something to hold what your body gives**. They will **thank you for it**, **or they will laugh at your ignorance**, **either way, you will learn**.

**Nothing else.** No weapons—there are **no animals**, **no threats**, **no need for violence**. No food—you will **eat crystal**, **young growth**, **tender tips**, **precipitate before it hardens**, **warm**, **nutrient-dense**, **tasting of vent**, **tasting of the world's own blood**, **strange at first**, **then necessary**, **then craved**. No books—you will **learn by singing**, **by doing**, **by being shown**, **the slow education of a place that does not hurry**. No maps—there are **no paths that stay**, **the world changes**, **vents shift**, **gardens grow and die**, **the city rebuilds**, **you will learn to navigate by heat and sound and light**, **by the feel of the place**, **by the voices of the people**.

### What to Leave Behind

**Your expectation of animals.** Orum has **none**. This is **the deepest strangeness**, **the one that will haunt you**, **the one that makes the lushness feel wrong**, **the gardens growing**, **abundant**, **producing**, **and nothing eating them but people**, **nothing moving through them**, **no grazer**, no browser**, **no thing on legs or wings or vapor-fins**. You will **expect to see something crawl**, **something swim in the vapor**, **something eat the gardens**, **and you will see nothing**, **and the nothing will feel like a wound**, **a loneliness you cannot name**, **because loneliness requires company**, **and Orum has only you**, **and the crystal**, **and the heat**, **and the singing**.

**Your expectation of water.** Orum is **dry**, **dry as a thought**, **dry in its chemistry**, **its solvent is heat itself**, **its rain is condensation of metal vapor**, **its rivers are channels of molten sulfur**, **flowing slow and orange**, **carrying nutrients to the growing things**. You will **thirst**, **at first**, **your body will demand water**, **the surface-world habit**, **the mammalian need**, **but you will learn to drink differently**, **to take moisture from crystal**, **from vapor**, **from the mineral slurry that is the blood of this place**, **and your thirst will change**, **become something else**, **a hunger for heat**, **a need for growth**, **a desire to be part of the cycle**.

**Your expectation of scale.** Orum is **pocket-scale**, **hidden**, **intimate-room**, **embedded-within**, **the inside of a thing that has no outside-name**, **only a shape**, **a vast cooling cavity**, **entered through a throat no wider than a room**, **opening inward to a volume that would hold a thousand cathedral-naves**. But it **feels vast**, **because of the emptiness**, **the density: 0.4**, **the distance between towers**, **hundreds of meters of open air**, **of sound-carrying emptiness**, **of the space where the wind moans and the crystals chime and nothing else moves**. You will **lose your sense of size**, **of how big things are**, **of how long it takes to cross the hollow**, **because the scale is hidden**, **pocket**, **intimate**, **and yet the distances are real**, **the walking is real**, **the singing across the void is real**, **and you will learn to hold both**, **the smallness of the entrance**, **the vastness of the interior**, **the way a place can be contained and infinite at once**.

### How to Move

**Sing when you move.** Not loudly—**you are not a voice yet**, **you do not know the songs**—but **hum**, **make a sound**, **let the world know you are there**, **let the people hear you**, **let the crystal-gardens feel your presence**, **the small disturbance in the gas**, **the small warmth added to the system**, **the small voice that answers**, **that will learn to sing back**.

The slurry is **warm**, **thick**, **carrying nutrients**, **carrying heat**, **carrying the minerals of the deep**, **you will learn to taste the difference between vents**, **between gardens**, **between seasons of growth**, **the subtle chemistry that tells you what your body needs**, **what the world is giving**, **what the cycle requires**.

Let the dreams come. They are **not wrong**, **they are the ghost of where you came from**, **the shape of a need you have learned to meet differently**, **and in the morning, you will wake to the singing**, **to the heat**, **to the crystal**, **and the dream will fade**, **become a story you tell**, **a surface-story**, **a joke among the people who have never known water**, **who have never needed it**, **who have grown in the heat**, **in the dark**, **in Orum**.
