I feel this place. Let me tell you how to prepare.

## What You Are Walking Into

You are not visiting a cave. You are being *gestated*. The Throat of a Forgetting Mountain is a place that has not finished deciding what it is, and this indecision is not metaphor — it is the physics you will move through. Your body will pass through spaces that disagree with each other about where they are. Your mind will be asked to hold contradictions without resolving them. The preparation is mostly *unlearning*.

---

## The Body: Temperature and Damp

The coolness here is *active*. It is not the passive chill of stone; it is the temperature of something still metabolizing, still generating its own internal processes. You will sweat and you will shiver in the same breath. Your clothing must be **layered for contradiction** — something that breathes when you are warm, something that insulates when you are not, and something outer that does not mind being *damp*.

The damp is not wetness you can wipe away. It is a *parameter* — the air carries water the way other places carry dust. Your skin will prune. Your joints will complain. Bring **wool or synthetic blends** that retain warmth when damp. Cotton is a death sentence here. Your boots must be **sealed but not airtight** — the pressure shifts as passages open and close, and your feet will feel it.

The fauna — those pale, many-segmented things — are not dangerous in any conventional sense, but they *test* your body. They gather where you are warm. They dissolve when you move suddenly. Some travelers report a sensation of *being measured*, of heat leaving the body in patterns that follow no logic of blood or breath. This is not parasitism. It is *curiosity*. But curiosity, repeated, can exhaust. Bring **external warmth** — chemical heat packs, a small stove if the passages permit fire (they often do not), a companion whose body generates enough heat to share.

Your **breathing** will be affected. The air is breathable, but it is *thin with meaning* — you will find yourself holding your breath without deciding to, as if the mountain is teaching you something about attention. Practice **slow exhalation** before you descend. The place rewards patience in respiration.

## The Mind: Geometry and Narrative

You will lose your sense of direction. Not gradually — *strategically*. The mountain opens and closes passages with what feels like *politeness*, as if it is apologizing for the inconvenience of your disorientation. This is a trap. The apology is genuine and the disorientation is real. You cannot out-navigate a place that is dreaming itself into being.

**Do not try to map it.** Not with tools, not with memory. The map will lie to you, and worse, *you will believe the lie*. Instead, practice **landmark-feeling** — not "the passage that turns left" but "the place where the light turns blue-white and the drip sounds like a single note held too long." Memory that is *sensory* survives here. Memory that is *spatial* does not.

You will encounter **echoes of yourself**. Not reflections — *imitations*. The mountain practices being human using your voice, your movements, your patterns of attention. When you hear your own voice speaking words you did not say, **do not answer**. To answer is to teach the mountain your shape more completely. To ignore is not rude — it is *protective*. The mountain will try again. Let it. Silence is a language here.

The **scale** will shift. You will feel small in a room that is vast. You will feel enormous in a passage that should not accommodate you. This is not hallucination — or rather, hallucination and reality have *negotiated* a treaty here, and the terms are unstable. Prepare by practicing **acceptance of strangeness without interpretation**. When something is wrong, do not ask *why*. Ask *what is it doing now*, and follow the answer as if it were weather.

Time moves differently. Not faster or slower — *unevenly*. You may spend what feels like hours in a single basin of water, watching fauna dissolve and reform. You may blink and find a passage sealed, the mineral sigh already finished. **Do not carry a watch.** Do not carry a phone. The mountain will *eat* your timekeeping devices, not destroy them — incorporate them, render them into geology. If you need to know how long you have been inside, **sing**. The mountain will remember your song and return it to you when you emerge. This is the only clock that works.

## The Spirit: What the Place Wants

The Throat of a Forgetting Mountain is *lonely*. Not in a way that seeks comfort, but in a way that seeks **witness**. It is being born. It does not know if it will be a cave, a canyon, a cathedral, or a grave. Your presence is *part of its erosion* — you are contributing to the decision by being here, by moving through it, by *being changed* by it.

The vegetation — those frond-structures that photosynthesize internal light — will orient toward you. This is not hostility. It is *negotiation*. They are trying to grow in your direction, and you are trying to move through them. **Move slowly.** The fragmentation-under-stress reproduction means that hurried movement *creates more of them*, more negotiations, more territory being argued over. Slowness is respect here. Slowness is *participation*.

The fauna eat absence. They eat the silence between sounds, the space between thoughts. This means **your thoughts are food**. Not your *emotions* — your *attention itself*, the gaps in it. When you stop moving, they will gather. When you stop *thinking*, they will press closer. This is not a threat. It is an *offering*. The place is offering to eat your stillness, your unoccupied mind, in exchange for **not eating you**. Accept the terms. Let them eat the silence. Practice **intentional emptiness** — meditation, but not for peace. For *sustenance*.

## What to Carry

**Light.** Not electric — the internal illumination is *competitive*, and electric light will be absorbed, rendered meaningless, possibly *misinterpreted* as a challenge. Carry **bioluminescent sources** — cultivated fungi, chemically treated cloth, the eyes of things that once lived here (if you can find them, if you dare). Or carry **no light at all**, and let the mountain illuminate you. Some travelers report that the rock *prefers* this. That it flares brighter around those who do not compete with it.

**Water.** The condensation is not potable. It is *pre-potable* — water that has not decided what it wants to be. Bring your own, and **share it**. Pour a small amount into any basin you find. The fauna will gather. The mountain will notice. This is *courtesy*.

**Food.** You will feel hunger you cannot name. This is not physical hunger. Do not eat to satisfy it. Eat to *demonstrate* that you are still a body, still a pattern of metabolism, still *real* in the way the place needs you to be. Bring food that **does not require preparation** — the mountain will not permit fire, or will permit it only in places that are *argued-over*, and you do not want to be present for that argument.

**A companion.** Not for safety — the danger here is not physical. A companion for **disagreement**. Two people will experience the geometry differently. When one sees a passage, the other may see a wall. This *argument* is valuable. It is the only map that works. But choose your companion carefully. The mountain will *imitate* both of you. It will practice being each of you. If you are not prepared to hear your companion's voice speaking your companion's thoughts back to you, do not bring them.

**Something to leave.** The structures here are *partial*, *argued-over*. They are maintained by processes no one witnesses. You can witness. Leave an offering — a tool, a written word, a lock of hair, a photograph of a place that *finished* being what it was (the mountain has not, and envies this). The offering will be incorporated. You will become part of the architecture. This is not a threat. It is a *gift*.

## What to Expect When You Leave

You will not leave the same way you entered. The wound in the world does not close — it *cannot* — but it *shifts*. You may emerge at a different elevation, in a different season, in a different *version* of the world that has been negotiating with itself while you were inside.

You will carry the **coolness** with you. It will persist in your bones, in your dreams, in the way you orient yourself to light. You will find yourself **hungry in ways you cannot name**. You will hear **your own voice** speaking in empty rooms, and you will not know if it is memory or imitation.

You will want to **return**. The mountain is still deciding whether to keep you, and you will spend years wondering if you were *kept* — incorporated, becoming geology, becoming architecture — or if you were *released*. The distinction will matter less than you expect.

And eventually, you will understand: **you were never visiting.** You were *being born into* a place that does not know what it is, and your presence was part of the not-knowing. The erosion continues. The damp persists. The fauna gather in warm places and dissolve in light.

You are part of the negotiation now. You always were.

---

## Final Preparation: The Descent

Before you enter the wound in the world, **practice being forgotten**. Stand in a place that does not remember you — a road, a room, a moment of waiting. Let yourself be *unnecessary* to the world around you. The Throat of a Forgetting Mountain will ask this of you, repeatedly, and the practice will serve you.

**Breathe the damp.** Let your lungs accept moisture as a *gift*, not an intrusion. The mountain weeps without knowing why. Let yourself weep without knowing why.

**Listen for the chime.** The mineral growth makes a sound like ice thinking. When you hear it, **do not move**. Let the sound finish. Let the mountain finish its thought.

And then — **descend**.

The mountain does not close behind you. It *cannot*. You are inside its becoming. You are part of its erosion, its damp, its sparse and stubborn and *abundant* life.

It is still deciding whether to keep you.

Walk as if you have already been kept.
