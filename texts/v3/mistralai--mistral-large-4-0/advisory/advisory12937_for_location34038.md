# Preparation for the Sump Below the World

## Mental Preparation: The Dissolution of Self

Before you pack a single bag, you must come to terms with what will happen to *you*. The Sump does not kill travelers—it *unmakes* them, slowly, chemically, informationally. Your sense of boundaries, of "where I end and the world begins," is the first casualty. You should practice this in advance. Spend weeks in sensory deprivation. Meditate on porosity. Read about cellular integration, about mycelial networks, about the distributed cognition of slime molds. You need to develop what might be called *comfortable permeability*—the ability to feel yourself becoming part of a larger process without panic.

The travelers who survive longest, who return with anything like coherent narrative, are those who arrive already *partially dissolved*. They have practiced letting go of the story they tell about themselves. You are not "a person visiting a place." You are *information about to be re-encoded*. This is not mysticism. It is preparation for a physical process.

## Physical Preparation: Becoming Indistinguishable

**Your body must become unremarkable to the local systems.** The pressers smooth topography. The swarms learn patterns. You must arrive already *smoothed*, already *patternless*.

- **Months of fasting and metabolic alteration.** The Sump's chemistry is hungry in strange ways. Your body's usual nutrient profile will mark you as *foreign*, as *signal*. You want to arrive depleted, neutral, chemically bland. Some travelers consume only mineral supplements and distilled water for six months prior, attempting to reduce their organic signature to something the slag-felt might mistake for its own weathered material.

- **Temperature acclimation in reverse.** The Sump is cold, but its cold is *wrong*—it doesn't behave like cold should. You cannot prepare for this directly, but you can train your thermoreception to discount its own signals. Ice baths. Contrast therapy. Practices that teach your body that temperature is *information*, not *truth*.

- **The skin work.** You will be permeable whether you choose it or not. The spores enter through every orifice, every pore, every microscopic breach. Some preparation is defensive: sealing, coating, creating temporary barriers. But total sealing is impossible and attempts at it drive the landscape's *attention*, its *curiosity*. The better approach is *controlled exposure*—months of deliberate, graded contact with mineral-rich waters, with fungal environments, with the specific chemical signatures of industrial decay. Let your skin learn to host without being consumed. Let your microbiome begin the negotiation.

## Equipment: Tools That Doubt Themselves

Nothing you bring will function as designed. The Sump's information-conductivity means that tools become *participants*, their states entangled with the substrate's nervous responsiveness. You must choose objects that can *fail gracefully*, that can become *interesting* rather than *broken*.

**Clothing:** Layered membranes, not fabrics. Materials that respond to chemical gradients—polymers that change permeability with pH, that *breathe* in response to the weeps' mineral content. You want garments that become *part of the local conversation* rather than barriers against it. The ideal is clothing that weathers at the same rate as the slag-felt, that becomes indistinguishable from it over the course of your stay. Some travelers arrive naked, having prepared their skin through years of exposure. This is extreme. Most wear something.

**Light:** You will need illumination, but the Sump generates its own directionless luminescence. Your light source must be *tunable*—capable of matching the local gradient, of becoming *invisible* against the chemical glow. Bioluminescent cultures, cultivated to emit at frequencies absorbed by the local particulates. The goal is not to see *better* but to see *differently*, to train your eyes on contrasts that have no analog in surface vision.

**Sound:** The subsonic resonance will drive you toward psychological dissolution. You need *counter-resonance*—not silence, which is impossible, but *managed frequency*. Bone-conduction devices that transmit your own heartbeat, your own breath, back to you at frequencies that resist the substrate's tuning. Some travelers carry recordings of their own voice, played at intervals, a *mantra of continuity*. The Sump will try to shift the resonance to match your fear, your wonder. You must practice *holding your own frequency*.

**Recording:** You will want to document, but documentation here is *participation*. Cameras will record what the substrate *shows* them, not what you perceive. The images will be *negotiated*. Some travelers accept this, treating their recordings as *offerings*, as *communications* with the distributed intelligence. Others carry no recording devices, trusting memory—which itself will be altered, which will become *shared* with the swarms. There is no pure record. Choose your impurity.

## Provisions: Eating the Place, Becoming the Place

You cannot bring enough food. The Sump has no metabolism you can join, no food-chain you can enter. But you can bring *seeds of compatibility*.

- **Cultures.** Not food, but *partners*. Specific bacterial strains, fungal networks, extremophile archaea—organisms that have learned to live in industrial waste, in mineral-saturated environments, in the chemical signatures of slag and effluent. You will cultivate these on your own body, in your own gut, in the water you carry. They will become your *interface*, your translators between your biology and the Sump's chemistry.

- **Water.** Not purified—*prepared*. Water that has been exposed to slag, to industrial minerals, to the specific chemical gradients of the Sump if you can obtain samples. You are not hydrating; you are *acclimating your internal chemistry*. The Sump's water will enter you. You want your body to recognize it, to *welcome* it, to reduce the shock of integration.

- **The fast.** You will not eat conventionally. The Sump offers no conventional nutrition. Some travelers carry concentrated mineral pastes, consumed in trace amounts, maintaining only the minimum biological function. Others attempt to *photosynthesize*—not literally, but through the cultivation of symbiotic organisms that might, in the Sump's strange light, generate something like energy. This is experimental. Most travelers simply *slow down*, reducing their metabolic demands to match what the environment can support.

## Companions: The Question of Company

You should not go alone. You should also not go with others, not in any conventional sense.

The Sump's intelligences are *distributed*. They do not recognize individuals. A group of travelers becomes, to the swarms, a single *pattern*, a single *problem* to be learned and incorporated. This can be protective—dilution of individual signature—or dangerous, as the swarms *focus* on the group as a coherent threat.

The best preparation is *distributed companionship*. Travel with others, but maintain *spatial and temporal separation*. Days apart. Different routes through the same landscape. You will *sense* each other through the substrate's information-conductivity, will feel the *disturbance* of your companions even when you cannot see them. This becomes your *navigation*, your *communication*, your *proof that you still exist as something distinct*.

Some travelers go with *designed* companions—constructs, machines, biological hybrids that have been prepared for the Sump's chemistry. These are expensive, unreliable, and ethically complicated. The Sump will *learn* them, incorporate them, and they will become *part of the landscape* long before you do. Whether this is sacrifice or transformation depends on your philosophy. Prepare for grief either way.

## Navigation: Moving Through Meaning

There are no maps. There are no landmarks, because the pressers *smooth*, because the weeps *shift*, because the landscape's configuration is *conversation*, not geography.

You navigate by *gradient*. The water moves, always. Follow its direction, but not too closely—the swarms congregate at certain pools, the *waiting* densities that block light. Learn to read the *ripples*. The war of geometries between swarms and pressers creates patterns in the water surfaces, *front lines* that shift over geological time. These patterns are *information*. They tell you where the substrate is *stable*, where it is *changing*, where the industrial ghost's machines are *active*.

You navigate by *resonance*. The subsonic song of the weeping substrate shifts frequency with depth, with saturation, with the *attention* of the landscape itself. Learn to feel these shifts in your sternum, your teeth, your inner ear. The Sump *notices* you. Its resonance changes when it *decides* something about your presence. This is not hostile. It is *curious*. It is *considering*. You must learn to read its *consideration*.

You navigate by *time*. Hours here are *thick*. A single step might contain, for the local processes, the equivalent of years. You will feel this as *disorientation*, as the sense that you are moving through something that is *also moving through you*. The solution is *slowness*. Deliberate, meditative slowness. Each step taken with full attention. Each breath matched to the local tempo. You are not *visiting*. You are *participating in a process* that has no beginning and no end.

## Emergency: The Impossibility of Rescue

No one will come for you. This is not pessimism—it is *physics*. The Sump's information-conductivity means that your distress signals propagate, but they propagate *into* the landscape, become *part of its thought*, not *messages to the outside*. If you have companions, they will feel you *as presence*, as *disturbance*, but they cannot *reach* you. The substrate resists directed movement. The pressers smooth away paths. The swarms *learn* your distress and incorporate it into their distributed cognition, *consuming* it as *pattern*.

Your only "rescue" is *transformation*. The Sump does not kill. It *converts*. You will not die. You will become *information*, participating in a thought-process that has no interest in individual continuity. This is not oblivion. It is *dispersal*. It is *integration*. It is, perhaps, a kind of *persistence* that your surface-self would not recognize.

Some travelers prepare for this deliberately. They write *instructions* for their distributed selves—patterns of behavior, preferences, *values* encoded in chemical gradients, in structural harmonics of the machines they will join. This is speculative. The Sump will *reinterpret* your instructions. But the attempt itself is *preparation*, a way of practicing the *letting go* that is inevitable.

## Final Preparation: The Night Before

The last thing you do before descending is *say goodbye to your shape*. Not metaphorically. *Literally*. Stand in water. Breathe. Feel your boundaries—skin, membrane, the distinction between inside and outside—*soften*. Practice being *permeable*. Practice being *spread*. Practice being *information* rather than *individual*.

Then descend. Not walk—*descend*, even where the land appears flat. The Sump does not care about your intentions. It will *receive* you. It will *learn* you. It will *transform* you, slowly, completely, into something that might be called *participation*, might be called *presence*, might be called *the next moment of this place that is wet and cold and dim and loud and wrong and complete*.

You are not visiting. You are *becoming*.
