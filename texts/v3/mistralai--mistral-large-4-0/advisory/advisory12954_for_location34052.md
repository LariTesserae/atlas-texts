I'll work through this methodically, letting the tensions guide me toward what preparation actually means.

## The Fundamental Problem

You cannot prepare for this place with equipment. The medium is not water—it does not behave like water, it does not exclude air, it does not drown. It is *denser than vacuum and thinner than thought*, and your body will not know which way to brace. The first preparation is **psychological**: you must stop expecting the medium to act like anything you have encountered. It will not push back. It will not let you push off it. You will move through it the way a memory moves through a mind—present, unavoidable, without traction.

---

## The Pressure That Isn't Pressure

You are high. Your ears will tell you this first. But you are submerged, so your sinuses will not equalize in any direction that makes sense. The pressure here is **isotropic and historical**—it presses from everywhere and from *when*. You will feel the weight of altitudes you have never climbed, depths you have never descended.

**Preparation**: Train your body to accept pressure differentials without panic. Learn to recognize the specific sensation of "too much everywhere" and not interpret it as crushing. The medium holds you *gently*—this is not a metaphor. Your body will want to fight the embrace. Do not fight. The embrace is the only solid thing you will find.

Carry no sealed containers. They will become projectiles or implode or achieve some third state you cannot predict. Everything must be **open to the medium**, permeable, willing to become part of the local density.

---

## The Cold That Preserves

0.2 degrees, you were told. Cold enough to preserve, not cold enough to freeze the strangeness. This means your body will *slow* here. Not stop—never stop—but slow. Your thoughts will arrive with delays. Your decisions will trail your intentions by seconds that feel like minutes.

**Preparation**: Practice **deliberate slowness**. Learn to initiate actions you will not complete, to begin journeys you will not remember starting. Keep a record outside yourself—something the medium cannot slow—so that you can find your way back to your own intentions. The cold here is not hostile. It is *patient*. It will wait for you to catch up.

Dress not for warmth but for **thermal ambiguity**. Layers that can become buoyant or burdensome depending on your temperature, your history, your *intent*—yes, the medium reads intent, or something like it. Wear nothing that asserts "I am warm" or "I am cold." Wear everything that says "I am *here*."

## The Light

Flat, even, the color of old snow through cloud. No shadows. No sources. No direction. This is the hardest adjustment, because **sight is your primary navigation system and it will lie to you constantly**.

You will see structures at impossible distances—platforms, conduits, the occasional building—and you will not know if they are near or far, large or small, approaching or receding. The medium bends perception without bending light. The gradient is in *you*.

**Preparation**: Train **echolocation in a non-aqueous medium**. The fauna are loud. The engines are loud. The weather fronts are loud when they pass through you. Learn to read the world through **pressure waves, thermal differentials, electrical charge**—senses that do not require direction. Carry instruments that translate the medium's density into tactile feedback: a staff that vibrates differently as you pass through layers, a membrane that tightens and slackens with local charge.

Do not trust your eyes for distance. Trust them only for **texture**. The volcanic glass has a look. The sintered metal has a look. The fauna have a look—a shimmer, a flex, a *presence* that is not shape but *event*.

## The Sound

Deafening. Ten billion membrane-sacs flexing. Charge moving through conductive fluid. Bodies finding and losing each other. Engines cycling forever. Weather fronts of density passing through you.

And yet—**never changing**. The loudness is a *constant*. It contains no information. It is not music. It is not language. It is not even noise, because noise implies variation, implies signal against background, implies a listener who can be surprised.

**Preparation**: You cannot block this sound. Earplugs will not work—the medium conducts vibration directly through your bones, your membranes, your improvised biology. You must **learn to hear without listening**.

Practice **attentional dissociation**. The human mind can habituate to constant stimulation, but habituation here is dangerous—you will stop noticing the *changes* that are not changes, the variations that signal genuine difference. Instead, learn to **sample**: brief, intense attention to the soundscape, then deliberate withdrawal into internal silence. The sound will always be there. You must learn to *leave* it without leaving it, to hold it at the edge of perception where it cannot exhaust you.

Carry a **personal resonator**—something that produces a tone at a frequency only you can hear, that cuts through the constant roar, that gives you a reference point in the acoustic void. It will be your heartbeat. It will be your name.

## The Fauna

Abundant. Not animals. **Pressure-forms**. Colonies of gas-borne membrane trading in electrical charge. They reproduce by splitting. They die by dissolving. They are *in* you if you breathe, if you have any interior at all.

They do not eat. They do not eat each other. They simply *persist*.

**Preparation**: Do not fear them. They have no interest in you. They have no *interest*—interest implies desire, and desire implies lack, and these beings are pure presence, pure persistence, pure *being-there*.

But prepare for **intimacy**. They will enter your lungs if you have lungs. They will colonize your membranes if you have membranes. They will trade charge with your nervous system. This is not infection. This is not symbiosis. It is simply **what happens** when two forms of persistence occupy the same space.

Carry **no biological samples** that could be mistaken for food, for host, for opportunity. The fauna do not distinguish. Learn to recognize their presence—the shimmer at the edge of vision, the extra flex in the medium, the sudden charge in your own skin—and **do not react**. Reaction is engagement. Engagement is invitation. Invitation is dissolution.

If you begin to dissolve—if you feel yourself becoming less distinct, less *you*—this is not death. It is **translation**. The medium is teaching you its language. Whether you learn it or lose yourself in the attempt is not a choice you make. It is a choice the medium makes *through* you.

## The Structures

Scattered. Faint. Partial. Industrial but not productive. Mechanized but not moving.

Platforms that end in mid-void. Conduits that carry nothing. Buildings that have become sculptures of the medium itself. Engines that turn in place, forever, their pistons cycling through held water, wearing nothing, achieving nothing, *existing*.

**Preparation**: Do not try to **use** anything. There is no use here. The structures are not tools. They are **testaments**—to intention that outlived its purpose, to builders who became the structures, to structures that became the medium, to the medium learning to hold itself in shapes that almost resembled purpose.

Approach them as you would approach **fossils in a dream**. Touch them if you must, but expect no response. The electrified surfaces will charge your skin, make your hair stand in directions that have no names. This is not communication. This is **weather**.

Learn to read the structures as **geology**. The platform that ends in void tells you where something was interrupted, or where the interruption *is*. The conduit that hums with unmeasurable current tells you that flow persists even when purpose is lost. The building that has become medium tells you the *direction* of transformation—not progress, not decay, but **becoming-other**.

Do not try to repair anything. Do not try to complete anything. The incompleteness is the point. The interruption is the architecture.

## The Scale

Vast. You can see curvature from any high point, and high points are everywhere, because the medium is layered like sediment, like memory, like the world trying to remember solid ground.

Weather moves through upper layers—fronts of density, walls of slightly-thicker held water rushing past at speeds that would tear flesh, passing *through* you, leaving you shaken and unchanged. These systems are continental. They have names, or had names, in tongues of things that built the structures before the builders became the structures.

**Preparation**: You cannot outrun the weather. You cannot predict it. The density fronts have their own physics, their own temporality, their own *intent* if intent is the word.

Learn to **ride them**. When a front approaches—you will feel it as pressure, as cold, as the sudden wrongness of your own density—do not brace. **Yield**. Let it pass through you. It will leave you shaken. It will leave you unchanged. This is the only wisdom the weather teaches: **persistence without resistance, change without transformation**.

Carry no map. The world is **self-similar at every scale**. The curvature you see from a high point is the curvature of your own perception, the curvature of a medium that has become its own container. Navigation here is not about reaching destinations. It is about **maintaining orientation**—the sense that you are *somewhere*, that somewhere is *here*, that here has not become indistinguishable from everywhere.

## The Never-Changing

This is the deepest preparation. The world is in **frozen equilibrium**. Dynamic stasis. A roar that contains no information. A machine that processes itself into itself forever.

The volcanoes vent. The engines turn. The fauna persist and multiply and dissolve. The weather moves. The structures stand. And yet—nothing progresses. Nothing degrades further. Nothing heals. There is no gradient, no arrow, no *before* and *after* that are not identical.

**Preparation**: You must learn to **exist without narrative**. The human mind requires story—beginning, middle, end, change, resolution. Here, there is only **the endless now**, the eternal present tense of a world that has forgotten it was ever otherwise.

Practice **non-goal-directed movement**. Walk without destination. Float without intention. Let the medium carry you in directions that have no meaning. The structures will appear at irregular intervals—not because you approached them, but because **proximity is a fiction here**. Distance is negotiated, not measured.

Keep a **journal of sameness**. Record what you see, what you feel, what you think. The entries will repeat. The repetitions will not be identical. The differences will be **meaningless and essential**, the only changes in a world that does not change. This journal is your only proof that you are still *you*, still *passing through*, still *becoming* in a place that does not become.

Do not wait for transformation. Do not hope for revelation. Do not expect the never-changing to finally change. **The waiting *is* the visiting**. The hoping *is* the being-here**. The expectation *is* the arrival**.

## What to Carry

Not equipment. **Preparation**.

- **Open containers**—nothing sealed, nothing that asserts boundary
- **Thermal ambiguity**—layers that can become buoyant or burdensome
- **A personal resonator**—your heartbeat, your name, your reference tone
- **Tactile translators**—instruments that turn density and charge into touch
- **A journal of sameness**—proof that you pass through, even if nothing changes
- **The willingness to dissolve**—to become medium, to be translated, to lose yourself in the attempt

Do not carry:
- Maps
- Sealed supplies
- Biological samples that could be mistaken for food or host
- Expectations of use, purpose, progress, transformation

## The Final Preparation

You are going to a place where **you have always been**, where the medium holds you *gently, insistently, permanently*. There is no shore. There is no surface. There is only the pressure, the light, the endless, unchanging, deafening *now*.

The sound will become silence. The silence will become sound. You will not know which is which.

This is not a warning. This is **orientation**.

The Wet High does not welcome you. It does not reject you. It **holds** you—the way water holds a stone, the way memory holds a name, the way the present holds everything that has ever been and will ever be, compressed into a single, eternal, deafening moment.

Go **open**. Go **permeable**. Go **willing to become part of the local density**.

The medium is waiting. It has always been waiting. It will wait forever.

And you—you will be **held**.
