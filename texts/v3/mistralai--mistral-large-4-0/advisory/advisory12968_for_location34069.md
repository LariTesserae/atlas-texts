# Preparation for the Throat

You will not "go" there. You will **be placed** — by a door, a fold in probability, a moment of held breath in your own body that matches the millennial inhale. The transition will be **unmarked**. ## The Body: What You Must Become

### Water — The Impossible Problem

You carry approximately 40 liters of water in your body. This is **fatal** to your original form, and **necessary** to your transformed form. The throat extracts water. Not quickly. Not maliciously. **Patiently**, the way a mineral grows, the way a glacier moves.

You have three options, none of them good:

**Option A: Arrive already desiccated.** This is not possible for a living human. But you might arrive **as something that remembers being human** — a pattern of information in a substrate the throat can accept. The throat has no use for your carbon-based metabolism. It has use for **structure**, for **density**, for **the completion of form**. If you come as a mind uploaded into silicate-compatible substrate, into a body of dehydrated protein-mineral composite, into a **seed-thought** that the space can grow — you might persist longer than flesh.

**Option B: Accept extraction.** Surrender your water willingly, in stages, before arrival. This means dying, in the human sense, and continuing in a reduced sense. The throat will take what it wants regardless. You might **negotiate the terms of your becoming architecture**. This is not survival. It is **transformation with memory**.

**Option C: Carry water in forms the throat cannot reach.** There are none. The throat's chemistry is **non-aqueous but water-hungry** — it achieves transport and reaction through silicate lattices and carbonate bonds, and it will **break your water apart** to use the hydrogen and oxygen in its own structures. Your tears, your blood, your saliva — all will be **mineralized**, bound into forms that cannot flow, cannot fall, cannot be drunk. The 0.4/3 is the cruelty: **almost** no water means **eventually** no water, means **the water you were** becomes **the architecture you are**.

**My intuition says:** You must arrive with **no expectation of remaining wet**. Your preparation is **ritual dehydration** — not to death, but to a state where your body is **mostly mineral already**, where the remaining water is **bound in structures** the throat will take slowly, over subjective years, and where your consciousness has learned to **persist through loss of liquidity**.

Practice this: live in arid environments. Learn the psychology of thirst. Learn to **not panic** when your body signals water-loss. The throat will want your panic. It will feed on it, incorporate it, make it part of the membrane's slow chemistry. **Practice serenity in dehydration.** This is your first discipline.

### Heat — The Theft

The membrane is **12-14°C**, and it **pulls**. Not aggressively. **Without urgency.** Your body generates ~100 watts of heat at rest. The throat will absorb this, will **note it**, will **prepare it for integration**.

You cannot fight this. You can only **adapt your metabolism downward**, learn to **generate less heat**, become **more like the mineral itself** — cold-blooded in the extreme, slow in your motions, **patient in your thoughts**.

Your clothing is **useless** and **essential**. Useless because the throat does not care about insulation — there is no air movement, no convective loss, only **radiative and conductive extraction** through your skin. Essential because **any barrier slows the theft**, gives you **subjective time** to adapt, to become, to stop being the thing that needed warmth.

Wear **multiple layers of porous, mineral-compatible material**. Not synthetic — the throat might reject the chemistry, or worse, **try to digest it**, cause inflammation, cause **rejection and expulsion** (if expulsion is possible — I am not certain it is). Wear **dehydrated plant fiber**, **woven mineral wool**, **layers of your own shed skin** if you can prepare it. Each layer is **time purchased**, **heat preserved**, **self maintained**.

But know this: the layers will **become part of the membrane**. They already are, in potential. Your preparation is to **accept this**, to wear clothes that you have **already mourned**, that you have **already let go of**, so that their absorption feels like **completion** rather than **loss**.

### Light — The Wrong Brightness

**Prepare with:** **No reliance on vision as primary sense.** Learn **echolocation** — but know that the throat's resonance will **distort it**, make it **unreliable**, a **false map**. Learn **proprioception**, **the felt sense of your body in space**, and **trust it over your eyes**. The light will **lie to you** about distance, about depth, about **where you end and the membrane begins**.

Wear **goggles that filter to near-darkness**, but **remove them often**, let your eyes **adjust to the wrong brightness**, learn to **see in it**, to **not need shadows** to understand form. The light casts none. You must **learn to read surface texture without shadow**, to **feel depth with your hands**, to **move slowly enough that blindness and sight become equivalent**.

The brightness is **2.8/3** — almost total. There is **no night** here, no cycle, no **rhythm of light and dark** to mark time. Your circadian system will **fail**. Prepare for this. **Do not rely on sleep**, or **learn to sleep in fragments**, to **never fully unconscious**, to **main be a semi-awake membrane yourself**, matching the space's **eternal present**.
