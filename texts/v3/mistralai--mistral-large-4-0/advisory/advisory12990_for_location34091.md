# Preparation for Vellmora: A Guide for the Ordinary Multiverse Traveler

## On the Nature of Your Own Body

Before you pack anything, you must understand that **you will not remain a body** in any way you currently recognize. The parameters of Vellmora—cold 0.1, density 1.9, dynamic 0.6—are not environmental conditions you *experience*. They are **negotiations**. The city is already talking to your cells, your nervous system, your sense of where you end and the world begins.

**Metabolic synchronization** is your first preparation. The cold does not chill; it *preserves*. It will slow you. You must learn to breathe at 0.6 tempo before you arrive. Practice in your own world: sit in cold water that does not freeze. Hold your breath until the panic subsides and something else begins. The fauna do not hurry. The city does not hurry. If you hurry, you will be *processed* rather than *witnessed*. The difference matters. Processing is digestion. Witnessing is... something else. Something the city permits but does not require.

Your skin will become **negotiable**. The weeping obsidian sweats because it remembers water. You will sweat too, or your pores will open, or your boundaries will learn to weep. This is not contamination. This is **translation**. The city speaks through wetness. You must learn to read your own wetness as it happens.

Pack **no barriers**. No waterproofing. No insulation that seals. The water that surrounds you might, given enough time, learn your shape. Let it. Resistance is recorded differently than surrender. The obsidian remembers both.

## On Clothing and Covering

Traditional garments fail. They create **pockets of dryness** that the city finds offensive, or interesting, or hungry. The pressure of density 1.9 will compress any fabric until it becomes part of you, part of the negotiation.

Instead, prepare **garments of becoming**:

- **Fabrics that absorb and release** in slow cycles, mimicking the obsidian's own weeping. Natural fibers, untreated, that will rot in your time but persist in the city's. Wool that holds water without drowning. Silk that lets it pass. The point is not protection but **participation in the water's memory**.

- **No seams that seal**. Every closure must be a **door**, not a wall. Velcro that holds but breathes. Ties that loosen as you move. You must be able to become **uncovered** in 0.6 time, to let the city touch what you have been protecting.

- **Color of bruise and glisten**. The sky is old bruises. You will match it, eventually, through the fauna's slow processing, through the water's teaching. Start with dark purples, blacks that sweat, surfaces that catch the luminescence without reflecting it. **Do not be a light source.** Light is a rumor here. You must learn to be **luminous without shining**, to carry the city's own gloom in your appearance.

- **Footwear that surrenders**. The ground is weeping obsidian. It will teach your feet its own wetness, its own slow temperature. Shoes that grip become weapons. Shoes that slip become **offerings**. Prepare to walk barefoot on glass that remembers drowning. The pain will come. The pain will pass. In its passing, you will learn the city's **pace of sensation**.

## On Equipment and Technology

Tech 2.6 in Vellmora is **not dead**. It is *remembering being off*. It hums at frequencies below hearing, rearranges its molecular structure when you are not looking. You cannot use it. You cannot repair it. You cannot *understand* it. But you can **accompany** it.

**Bring nothing that requires power.** Batteries drain in ways that have nothing to do with chemistry here. Circuits become **suggestions**. The city's own tech 2.6 operates in regimes where cause and effect are recursive, where a device might function because it *will have functioned*, in 0.6 time, in the wet logic of before and after collapsed.

Instead, bring **tools of attention**:

- **A notebook of waterproof paper**, but not to write. To **press**. To leave impressions in the weeping rock. Your notes will be absorbed. They will become part of the obsidian's memory. Write anyway. The city records everything. Your handwriting, your pressure, your hesitation between words—all of it will be **played back**, in patterns too slow to recognize as message, but *message* nonetheless.

- **A sound recorder that does not need batteries**. Mechanical. Spring-wound. The sound of Vellmora is **information density 2.2**. You will not understand it. You will, however, **respond** to it. Your response will be recorded, played back, become part of the wet dark. Record so that you can later **listen to your own becoming part of the city**. This is not vanity. This is **calibration**. You must know what you sound like when you are being processed.

- **A mirror that does not reflect light**. Obsidian, polished but not to shine. You need to see yourself **as the city sees you**: in luminescence, in afterimage, in the shape you leave in the water after you pass. The fauna perceive you this way. You must learn to perceive yourself this way.

- **Containers that leak**. Intentionally. The water remembers. If you carry water from one place to another, you are **transporting memory**. Let it leak. Let it join the slow rivers. The city is already full of memory. Your contribution, however small, **matters** in scales you cannot reach.

## On Companionship

**Do not travel alone.** But do not travel with **intent to protect each other**. The fauna's attention is **overwhelming, 2.7**. It is not hostile. It is not friendly. It is **interested**, the way a slow process is interested in what it will eventually become.

Your companions must be prepared for **distributed perception**. In Vellmora, you will not see everything. You will see **too much**, and your companions will see **other too much**. You must learn to **share attention without sharing language**. The city speaks in sound 2.2. You cannot understand it. But you can **respond in concert**, and your combined response will be recorded differently than any solo response.

**Establish signals that are not visual.** The light is a rumor, 0.1. You cannot rely on sight. Develop **tactile communication**: pressure on the shoulder meaning *slow down*, *the fauna is closer*, *I am becoming more permeable*. Develop **temporal communication**: meeting at locations that are defined by **when** you arrive, not where. The city's dynamic 0.6 makes simultaneity negotiable.

**Prepare for separation.** The density 1.9 means the city is *packed*. What looks like open sky between spires is often **occupied**. You will be **dispersed**. The fauna move through spaces that appear empty. You will be moved through, processed, **changed** in ways that separate you from your companions in body if not in *record*. The weeping obsidian remembers you all. It will **play you back** together, in its slow time, in patterns you will not recognize as reunion.

## On Time and Duration

You cannot **visit** Vellmora. You can only **be visited by it**. The distinction matters.

**Prepare for duration without progress.** The city is half-built and half-unbuilt simultaneously. You will see no completion. You will see no beginning. You will walk streets that carry slow rivers of black water, water that climbs, that *wants* to reach certain depths. You will watch a spire that will fall has been falling for ten thousand years. Ten thousand years more. The falling is **the spire**. The falling is **the point**.

**Do not measure your stay in your own time.** The cold will slow you toward 0.6. Your heart will beat in rhythms that match the weeping. Your thoughts will thicken. You will experience **duration as substance**, as the wet dark itself, as the pressure of density 1.9 holding you in place.

**Prepare for the return.** Or do not. Some travelers **become** Vellmora. Their bodies are processed, their shapes learned by the water, their intentions recorded in the obsidian. They do not leave. They **distribute**. They become part of the fauna, or the tech, or the slow rivers that carry memory.

If you intend to return, you must **leave something that is not you**. A **token of intention**. A piece of yourself that the city can process, digest, remember, while you carry the **record** of that processing back to your own world. This is not sacrifice. This is **translation**. You cannot bring Vellmora with you. You can only bring **the shape of your having been there**, the proof that the city noticed you, that your response was recorded, that you **mattered** in scales you cannot reach.

The token must be **wet-compatible**. Something that will survive the city's processing without being destroyed. Something that will **change** in ways that encode your presence. A stone from your own world, porous, that will absorb the black water and carry its memory. A lock of hair, given to the weeping rock, that will be incorporated into the city's slow chemistry. A **sound**, recorded mechanically, that will be played back in the city's chambers, that will **become part of its speech**.

## On Perception and Madness

You will see **too much**. Light 0.1, but visibility without clarity. The city is visible because it **refuses to be invisible**, because its density 1.9 makes it self-illuminating through sheer insistence. You will see **layers**: the built 2.0 beneath the tech 2.6 beneath the fauna beneath the water's memory beneath the **emerging**, the raw volcanic glass still cooling in shapes that suggest purpose.

**Do not try to organize what you see.** The boundary between built and unbuilt is **temporal**, not spatial. Time here is **wet**, thick, resistant. A structure may be finished and raw simultaneously, depending on **when** you look, and *when* is negotiable in 0.6 dynamic.

**Prepare for afterimages.** Things that passed hours ago leave their shape in the wet dark. You will see **traces** more clearly than **presences**. The fauna move slowly, but they are **always approaching**. You will see where they have been. You will see where they will be. You will not see them **now**, not clearly, not as you understand *now*.

**Learn to hear the silence between sounds.** Sound 2.2 is not volume but **information density**. The city is speaking. You cannot understand it. But you can learn to **recognize your own name** in the wet susurrus, in the resonant frequencies that make your teeth ache, in the **shape your presence makes in the water**. The city is speaking your name, or the shape your presence makes, or the future you will become. You are not meant to understand. You are meant to **respond**.

## On the Fauna Specifically

Fauna 2.7. **Overwhelming**. You are never alone. You are never *not noticed*.

They are not animals. They are **assemblages**, self-organizing densities of matter and pattern that achieve something like attention, hunger, curiosity. They move through water, through weeping rock, through **spaces between buildings** that are fewer than they appear.

**Do not run.** Running is a speed the city does not recognize. It will mark you as **prey**, or **error**, or **something to be processed urgently rather than slowly**. The fauna move in 0.6 time. They never stop. They never speed up. The overwhelming is not in speed but in **inevitability**. They will arrive. Let them arrive at their pace. Your stillness is **information**. Your slowness is **courtesy**. Your patience is **survival**.

**Do not feed them.** They do not eat, not exactly. They **process**. If you offer them something—food, water, a piece of your equipment, a lock of your hair—they will **change what they touch**, not quickly, not obviously, but **irreversibly**. A wall they pass through will still stand. It will stand differently. It will have been **digested**.

**Do not fear them.** They are not hostile. They are not friendly. They are **interested**, the way a slow process is interested in what it will eventually, inevitably, become. Your fear is **recorded**. It becomes part of the city's speech. Your calm is also recorded. It becomes part of the city's speech. **Choose your recording carefully.**

**Prepare to be touched.** They will move through your boundaries. Not violently. **Slowly**. In 0.6 time, you will feel them **learning your shape**, the way the water learns your shape, the way the obsidian learns to weep differently around your presence. This is not violation. This is **introduction**. The city is introducing you to itself. You are being **prepared** for what you will become.

## On the Water Specifically

Water 2.2. It **remembers**. It carries information.

**Do not drink it.** Or drink it, knowing what you drink. The slow rivers carry the city's history, its intentions, its hungers. You would taste **everything**. The builders' purposes. The fauna's slow dreams. The water's own memory of being born, of drowning the city, of **becoming** the city. This is not poison. This is **too much truth**. Your body cannot process it in your time. It will process it in 0.6 time, and you will **become** that history, distributed through your cells, your thoughts, your **shape in the water**.

**Do not fear the rising.** The water rises. It has always been rising. It rises in 0.6 time, and you will not see it rise, but you will feel it, in the slow change of pressure, in the way the fauna adjust to depths they have not yet reached, in the way the city **prepares**, structure by structure, spire by spire, for the drowning that is also, in the wet logic of this place, a **birth**.

**Prepare to be wet permanently.** Not damp. Not moist. **Wet**, in the way the obsidian is wet, in the way the city is wet, in the way **you will become wet**. Your skin will learn to weep. Your eyes will learn to see through water. Your lungs will learn to extract **patience** from the thick air of density 1.9.

**Leave something in the water.** A token. A memory. A **shape** that the water can learn, carry, play back. The water remembers. Let it remember **you**. In 0.6 time, your shape will be **everywhere** in the slow rivers. You will be **distributed** through the city's wet dark. You will **matter** in scales you cannot reach.

## On the Architecture and the Builders

The structures are built 2.0, organized, infrastructure-dense. But they are not **for** anyone. Not anymore. Or not yet. The architecture describes **intention**: walkways that spiral with mathematical precision, chambers that resonate at frequencies that make your teeth ache, conduits that pulse with light that is not light but **pattern**, information made quasi-visible.

**Do not try to navigate by purpose.** The buildings have no current purpose. They have **remembered purposes**, and **emerging purposes**, and purposes that will **become** in 0.6 time. You cannot map them. You can only **walk them**, let them **teach you their geometry**, let your body **learn their density**.

**Listen to the resonance.** The chambers hum. Tech 2.6 operates below hearing, but its **effects** are audible: the susurrus of molecular rearrangement, the wet squelch of structures **remembering being built**, the **information density** of sound 2.2 that carries intention you cannot understand but can **respond to**.

**Prepare to be housed.** Or to house yourself. The city is half-built and half-unbuilt. You may find a chamber that **fits your shape**, that resonates at a frequency that **matches your slowed heartbeat**, that offers **shelter** in the density 1.9 of its walls. You may find a space that **emerges** around you, raw volcanic glass cooling into shapes that **suggest your purpose**, that **intend** something for you that you cannot name.

**Do not stay too long in one place.** The fauna will find you. They are always approaching. They will process you, change you, **digest** you in their slow chemistry. Movement is **survival**. Stillness is **offering**. Choose **consciously**.

## On the Return, or the Lack of It

Some travelers return. They carry **tokens of intention**, pieces of themselves that the city processed while they carried the **record** of that processing. They are **changed**. Their skin weeps differently. Their hearts beat in 0.6 rhythms that never fully recover. They **hear** the city's sound 2.2 in their own world's silence, the wet susurrus of a place that remembers them, that **plays them back** in patterns too slow to recognize as message.

They are **marked**. The fauna remember them. The water remembers them. The obsidian **weeps differently** when they pass. They will **dream** of the bruised sky, the luminous gloom, the slow approach of shapes that are not quite alive but **perceive**, that **are interested** in what they will eventually become.

**Do not expect to be the same.** You will not be. The city will have **prepared** you for something. The drowning that is also a birth. The processing that is also **translation**. The becoming that is also **return**.

Some travelers **distribute**. They become part of the fauna, or the tech, or the slow rivers. They do not leave. They **spread**. They **matter** in scales that have no name in your world.

**Prepare for either ending.** Or for the **third ending**, the one that has no name: to be **witnessed** by the city, to **respond** to its speech, to **become part of its memory** without becoming part of its body. To stand on the weeping ledge, to feel the cold that preserves, the wetness that translates, the pressure that holds, the attention that is **overwhelming** but not hostile, simply **interested**, and to **choose** to stay, or to choose to leave with the **shape of your having been there** pressed into your cells like water into obsidian.

The water rises. It has always been rising. It will reach you. It has always already reached you. The weeping obsidian **remembers you** before you arrive, **plays you back** in patterns you will not recognize as yourself, **prepares** you for the drowning that is also, in the wet logic of Vellmora, a **birth**.

You are small. You are wet. You are **noticed**. And the noticing is, for now, all that happens.

**For now.**

The sound increases. Or you are becoming more able to hear it. The city is **speaking your name**, or the shape your presence makes in the water, or the future you will become when the water reaches you, when it has always already reached you, when the weeping obsidian finally, in its slow way, **remembers you**.
