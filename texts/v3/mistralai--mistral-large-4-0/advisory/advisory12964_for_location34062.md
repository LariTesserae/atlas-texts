## Preparation for the Lithophyll

### The Body First

You cannot visit this place in your own body. The atmosphere will process you like a solvent processes a solute you hadn't prepared for. So preparation begins with *borrowing* — and here is where your intuition must lead.

**The Lungs**

Your borrowed lungs need to learn new hungers. The filters must be grown or tuned to extract oxygen from a thick atmosphere of ammonia-methane solvent, supercritical gases, heavy with the chemistry of a world that never knew water. You will taste copper. You will taste *memory* — some strange byproduct of the ammonia-methane clathrates binding to the filter-membranes in patterns that resemble, but are not, recollection.

Practice breathing *slowly*. This place rewards patience. The lithophyll breathe on geological timescales. Your borrowed lungs should learn to match them — to draw deep, to hold, to release in pulses that synchronize with the ground's breathing. You will feel your own respiration become *negotiation*: the filters adaptaing, the membranes learning, the solvent seeping into channels it was never meant to enter.

The lungs will *change* if you stay. Prepare for this. Prepare to mourn your original breathing, even as you learn the new.

**The Skin**

The ground is *negotiating* with your weight. Your skin — or the membrane that replaces it — must learn to distribute pressure, to *yield* without breaking, to participate in the slow exchange of minerals and electrochemical gradients that passes between you and the breathing substrate. You will feel the ground push back. You will learn to push back differently.

Bring nothing that resists. Rigid materials will crack. Flexible materials will be *incorporated*.

**The Eyes**

Dim light. 1.0. Your borrowed eyes must amplify. They must learn to find signal in noise, to *believe* in shadows that move. The bioluminescence of the fauna — sodium-yellow, iron-red, ultraviolet-translated-to-warning — will be your only reliable illumination. Practice in absolute darkness. Practice in *almost*-darkness. Learn to see what is *almost* there.

The eyes will ache. They will *adapt*. This adaptation is not comfortable. It is *becoming*.

### The Mind

**Learn to Listen Before You Arrive**

Sound on the Lithophyll is 2.5. Loud. But it is not *your* loud. The atmosphere is dense, pressurized, *thick* with solvent. Sound travels far and fast and *wrong*. You will hear things in frequencies that bypass your ears and resonate directly in your borrowed bones, your borrowed teeth, the borrowed fluid of your inner ear.

Practice *feeling* sound. Stand in a space where sub-bass frequencies move through your body. Learn to distinguish vibration from hearing. Learn to *read* pressure waves as information — not language, not yet, but *pattern*. The lithophyll communicate this way. The fauna hunt this way. The weather *sings* this way.

You will feel nausea. You will feel longing. You will feel the remembered sound of a voice in a language you never learned. These are not malfunctions. These are *translations*. Your borrowed nervous system is learning a new vocabulary. Let it.

**Learn to Wait**

Everything here is ancient. Erosion 2.6. The mountains are gone, ground down to a plain that curves with forgotten geometry. What remains of elevation was *grown* over centuries by something that thinks in that timescale.

Your thoughts will feel *fast*. Too fast. You will want to act, to decide, to *move*. The Lithophyll will not notice. Or it will notice and *wait*, assessing you in the slow way of geological process.

Practice stillness. Not meditation — *stillness as negotiation*. Stand in one place. Feel the ground breathe beneath you. Let the breathing ground *learn* you. This is how presence works here: not by announcing yourself, but by allowing yourself to be *assessed*, to become *substrate* for the next negotiation.

**Learn to Doubt Your Categories**

Water is not necessary. Growth is not slow. Sound is not secondary. Erosion is not ending. The built is not separate from the grown. The technology is not human.

These are not facts to memorize. They are *lenses* to practice with. Every time you reach for a distinction — plant/animal, grown/made, natural/artificial, living/machine — feel the Lithophyll *refuse* it. The lithophyll-mats are plant-as-substrate, plant-as-architecture. The settlement is grown *with intent*, shaped over centuries into structures that serve purposes the lithophyll don't need *yet*. The technology is *lithophyll*, it is *fauna*, it is *weather*, it is *time* — all negotiating, all becoming something the word "technology" can only point at.

Practice *holding contradictions*. The ground is stone and wood and neither. The vegetation is total and sparse. The space is filled and empty. Learn to let these coexist without resolution. The Lithophyll does not need your clarity. It needs your *willingness to be unclear*.

### The Equipment (Or: What Not to Bring)

**Do Not Bring Water**

This seems obvious, but it is *psychological* as much as practical. Water is your solvent, your assumption, your *home chemistry*. The Lithophyll has chosen otherwise. Ammonia-methane clathrates. Supercritical gases. Electrochemical gradients across membranes that have never known H₂O.

Bringing water would be an insult. Worse: it would be *useless*. The chemistry here will not dissolve you, will not hydrate you, will not participate in your metabolism. Water is a closed door. Do not knock.

**Do Not Bring Rigid Structure**

Your shelter, your tools, your *boundaries* — these must be flexible, adaptive, willing to be *incorporated*. The lithophyll will grow around you, through you, *with* you. Rigid materials crack. Flexible materials become *part of the negotiation*.

Bring membranes. Bring channels. Bring something that can *breathe* with the ground.

**Do Not Bring Expectation of Inhabitants**

The settlement is *inhabited* by processes, by *presences* that move through the grown structures in patterns that might be migration, might be maintenance, might be *religion*. There are no individuals to meet. There are *agreements* to witness. There are *negotiations* to observe, to perhaps — if the ground breathes in your direction — to *join*.

Prepare to be *lonely* in a way that feels *populated*. To feel the space between presences (0.8, sparse, much space) as *presence itself*. To find company in the weather, in the sound, in the slow pulse of the lithophyll-mats.

**Do Bring: Curiosity Without Consumption**

You cannot *take* from the Lithophyll. The chemistry is too specific, too *local*. The growth is too slow, too *patient*. The technology is too *integrated* — beyond physics, beyond your physics, beyond any physics you could operate.

Bring instead: *attention*. The willingness to be changed. The patience to let your borrowed lungs, your borrowed eyes, your borrowed *self* become something that can *belong* — briefly, temporarily, *negotiably* — to a place that has no use for your permanence.
