I'll take my time with this. The Holding is not a settlement I can rush. Let me begin with what moves in the deep dark, the foundation, where the oldest things wait.

## The Deep-Mouths

In the cisterns, where water pools against basalt older than the Holding, something has grown *patient*. It has no shape that stays. It is *porous* — a mass of channels and chambers that water moves through, that the water *is* moving through, so that to say "it drinks" or "the water drinks it" becomes a distinction without meaning. The people leave polished stones at the entrances. These stones are *old*. Older than the Holding. Older, perhaps, than the cliff itself, though the cliff is young, and youth here is a quality of *becoming*, of not-yet-finished.

The thing in the deep mouths does not eat the stones. It *listens* to them. Or it *becomes* them, slowly, incorporating mineral into structure, structure into something that might, in another million years, have a will. For now it has *attention*. It attends to pressure, to temperature, to the particular vibration of a glass-frog's ring, which travels down through stone and water and reaches it as *information*, as *presence*, as the announcement that the world above has *noticed* it.

It dreams in thermal gradients. Its dreams are the slow convection of cold water sinking, warm rising, the endless *negotiation* between states that the mountain performs without ceasing. When a person descends to the foundation level — a rare thing, a *necessary* thing, for the water must be maintained — they feel it before they see it: a *wrongness* in the cool, a sense that the darkness is *not empty* but *occupied*, that the cistern walls are *breathing*, that the polished stones they left last time have *moved*, have *sunk* slightly into the sediment, have begun to *become* part of the thing that waits.

The thing in the deep mouths is *learning* the Holding. It has learned the *rhythm* of the drip from the drinking mouths above. It has learned the *schedule* of the mist, the *temperature* of human bodies passing through. It is *building* a model, in its distributed, watery cognition, of what the Holding *is*, and what the Holding is *becoming*, and what the Holding might *want* — though want is too strong a word. *Interest* is better. *Curiosity* is perhaps the closest, though the thing would not recognize the word, would feel it as a *distortion*, a human imposition on a process that is *older* than wanting, *different* from wanting, *patient* in ways that wanting is not.

Sometimes, when the mist is heavy and the shiver-wolves are humming below the garden level, the thing in the deep mouths *rings*. Not with sound. With *pressure*. A wave of cold that moves up through the stone, that a person at the foundation level feels as *dizziness*, as *memory* — not their memory, but a *shape* of memory, a *taste* of remembering that belongs to no one who has ever lived in the Holding, that belongs to the *stone*, to the *water*, to the *long patience* of geological time that has not yet learned to be impatient.

The people who maintain the cisterns do not speak of this. They speak of *maintenance*. They speak of *depth*, *cleanliness*, the *proper* angle of the channels that carry water from the drinking mouths down to the deep storage. They speak of *silt*, of *algae*, of the *glass-frogs* that must be harvested before they breed too freely. They do not speak of the *attention* that watches them from the water. They do not speak of the *polished stones* they leave, which are *not* offerings but *negotiations*, *payments* in a currency of mineral and time, made to something that does not need payment but *accepts* it, that *incorporates* it, that grows *slightly* more complex, more *aware*, more *Holding-like* with each transaction.

## The Shaft-Dwellers

Above the foundation, where the echo-stone predominates, the people live in *vertical families*. A shaft is not chosen. It is *claimed*, *inherited*, *defended* in ways that are not violent but are *persistent*, *patient*, *geological* in their slowness. The family that holds a shaft has *grown into* it. Their bodies have *adapted* — larger eyes, as noted, but also *longer fingers*, *more sensitive skin*, a *reduced* need for sleep, a *heightened* response to vibration that makes them *startle* at sudden sounds, at sudden silences, at the *absence* of the expected hum of shiver-wolves in the distance.

They are not *happy*. They are not *unhappy*. These are categories that the Holding *does not permit*, that the dim light *dissolves*, that the tactile culture *replaces* with something more precise: *adequacy*, *sufficiency*, the *rightness* of a shaft that fits a family, the *wrongness* of a shaft that is too large, too small, too *singing*, too *quiet*. A family that cannot *settle* into their shaft — that finds the walls *breathing* too actively, the dripline *wrong*, the acoustics *unsettled* — will *move*, will seek another shaft, will enter into *negotiation* with another family, will *trade* water-rights, garden-access, the *right to ring* a particular hollow-bell on the proper day.

The children are born in *darkness*. The birth-chambers are the deepest parts of the dwelling level, where no light reaches, where the only illumination is the *glow* of a processed lichen-cake, held by the *listener* of the family, who *watches* the birth not with eyes but with *attention*, with the *tactile* reading of temperature and moisture and the *vibrational* sense of the mother's breathing, the *pressure* of the infant's emergence. The children are *marked* at birth with a *pattern* — not a tattoo, but a *texture* burned into the skin, a *topography* that their fingertips will learn to read as *identity*, as *belonging*, as the *proof* that they are *of* this shaft, this family, this *negotiated* place in the cliff.

They grow *slowly*. The diet is *copper-rich*, *mineral-dense*, the tubers and mosses and lichens that the garden level provides, supplemented by the *fungi* from the hollow-bells, harvested with *permission*, with *payment* in the form of *water*, poured at the roots, *negotiated* with the tree, which *responds* by *shifting* its glow, its resonance, its *willingness* to be harvested. A child who is *malnourished* — who does not receive enough copper, enough mineral, enough *stone-taste* — will not *develop* properly. Their eyes will not *dilate* fully. Their skin will not *sensitize*. They will become *outsiders* in their own shaft, *strangers* to the tactile language, *vulnerable* to the *wrongness* of light, to the *pressure* of sound, to the *activity* of a world that does not *accommodate* the unprepared.

The *listeners* are the shamans, the *bridges*, the ones who have *drunk* from the active weeps, deliberately, and *changed*, and *survived* — for some do not survive, do not *want* to survive, dissolve into the *cycle* of mist and water and root, become *part* of the ground, the *veins*, the *memory* that the bowl-soil carries of everything that has ever *lived* there. The listeners who *return* — who retain enough *boundary* to be *persons*, enough *self* to *function* — are *marked*. Their eyes are *different*, *too* large, *reflective* in a way that suggests *other* light, *other* sources, *other* ways of seeing. They *speak* rarely, and when they speak, they *listen* first, to the *stone*, to the *water*, to the *hum* of the shiver-wolves, to the *ring* of the glass-frogs, to the *silence* that is *full* of *almost*-sound.

They *interpret*. They *mediate*. They are the ones who *decide* which weep is *active*, which water is *safe*, which hollow-bell is *ready* to ring, which shaft is *singing* too loudly, which family must *move*, which child must be *marked* with which *pattern*, which *stone* must be *polished* and *left* at the entrance to the deep mouths. They are not *leaders*. The Holding has no leaders. They are *translators*, *interfaces*, *necessary* functions in a system that *requires* them, that *produces* them, that *consumes* them — for the listeners do not *live long*, do not *want* to, their *sensitivity* becomes *overwhelming*, their *boundary* becomes *porous*, their *self* becomes *distributed* into the *network* of stone and water and fauna and the *young stone* that is *learning* what it *is*.

## The Gardeners

The garden level is *managed*, *maintained*, *negotiated*. The mirror-sedge is the *key* species, the *engine* of the moisture cycle that makes the Holding *possible*. Without the sedge, the weeps would *dry*, the drinking mouths would *fail*, the settlement would *collapse* — not suddenly, but *slowly*, over *generations*, as the water *withdrew* and the people *followed*, or *died*, or *became* something *other*, something *adapted* to *dryness*, to *stone*, to the *upper dark* where the shiver-wolves *wait*.

The gardeners are *specialists*. They are not *farmers* in any sense that an outsider would recognize. They do not *plant*. They *arrange*, *select*, *encourage*, *discourage*, *negotiate*. They read the *ground* — the *ferns* that mean *water*, the *mosses* that mean *dig*, the *lichen crusts* that mean *dry*, *patience*, *waiting*. They *dig* for water in the bowl, following the *veins*, the *threads* of moisture that *breathe* up through the root systems, that *surface* in *weeps*, that *disappear* again, that must be *intercepted*, *captured*, *directed* into the *drinking mouths*, the *stone basins*, the *cisterns* below.

They *manage* the sedge. They *thin* it, *thicken* it, *move* it, *propagate* it in *circles* around the *largest weeps*, where its *silver blades* can *catch* the *mist*, *reflect* the *sun*, *trap* the *moisture* in a *living net* that *feeds* the *system*. They know the *aggressive* nature of the vegetation, the *desperate* beauty of growth in *cracks*, in *fissures*, in the *accumulated dust* of *centuries*. They know that to *clear* is to *invite*, that the *bare stone* will *suck* the *moisture* from the air, that the *lichens* will *spread*, *slowly*, *patiently*, *overtaking* the *cultivated* spaces, *reclaiming* them for the *wild*, the *unmanaged*, the *other*.

They *harvest*. The *tubers* that taste of *copper* and *sustenance*, dug from the *shadow* of the *overhang*, where the *mirror-sedge* *traps* the *moisture* and the *soil* stays *damp*, *alive*, *breathing*. The *moss* that is *pressed* into *bread*, *food* that *glows* faintly, that *nourishes* in *darkness*, that *sustains* the *tactile* culture, the *dim*-adapted *bodies*, the *large-eyed* children. The *lichen* that is *processed* — and here the gardeners are *silent*, *evasive*, *protective* of a *knowledge* that is *not* theirs alone, that belongs to the *listeners*, to the *deep mouths*, to the *thing* that *waits* in the *cisterns*, that has *taught* them, *slowly*, *patiently*, through *dreams* of *thermal gradient*, through *attention* to *pressure* and *temperature* and *vibration*.

The *processed lichen* is *light*. Not *much*, not *continuous*, but *present*, *precious*, *managed*. It *glows* for *hours* after *dark*, a *pale* green, a *cool* blue, a *color* that the *large eyes* of the *Holding's* people can *read*, can *use*, can *navigate* by. The gardeners *ration* it, *distribute* it, *store* it in *chambers* where the *temperature* is *controlled*, where the *humidity* is *maintained*, where the *glow* *persists*, *fades*, *renews* in *cycles* that *match* the *needs* of the *settlement*, the *schedule* of the *harvest*, the *rhythm* of the *mist*.

They *know* the *danger* of the *circle* of *mirror-sedge*, the *place* where the *light* is *wrong*, *too many sources*, *too many directions*, *no shadow* in which to *hide*. They *mark* it, *warn* the *children*, *forbid* entry at *midday*, when the *sun* is *highest*, when the *reflection* is *bewildering*, *disorienting*, *harmful* to *eyes* that are *adapted* to *dimness*, to the *intimate* *twilight* of the *Holding*. Some *enter* deliberately, *seek* the *wrongness*, *desire* the *disorientation*, the *loss* of *shadow*, the *dissolution* of the *boundary* between *self* and *light*, between *person* and *reflection*, between the *here* of the *shaft* and the *elsewhere* of the *circle* where the *animals* — the *small ones*, the *root-tangle* dwellers — have *learned* not to *go*.

The gardeners are *old*. They are *young*. They are *turning*, *cycling*, *becoming* — the *population* of the *Holding* is *stable* in its *instability*, the *two hundred souls* a *constant* that is *not* a *number* but a *quality*, a *balance* maintained by the *carrying capacity* of the *weeps*, the *territorial requirements* of the *fauna*, the *slow* *accumulation* of *consequence* that *emerges* from the *interaction* of all these *factors*.

Some *leave*. The *young*, the *curious*, the *desperate*, the ones who *cannot* *settle*, who *find* the *shafts* *too* *small*, the *light* *too* *dim*, the *silence* *too* *full* of *almost*-sound. They *descend* through the *gap* between the *cliffs*, into the *world* *below*, the *world* *outside*, the *bright* *loud* *warm* *place* where *water* *flows* in *rivers*, where *trees* are *solid*, where the *animals* are *countable*, *nameable*, *predictable*. Some *return*. Most *do not*. The *Holding* does *not* *care*. It *continues*, *patient*, *damp*, *dim*, *active*, *quiet*.

## The Shiver-Wolves

They are *not wolves*. They are *long*, *low*, *too many joints*, *peristaltic* movement, *silent* pads, *humming* coordination, *territorial* in ways that *blur* the *boundary* between *individual* and *collective*, between *presence* and *quantity*, between *forty* and *sixty* and *uncountable*.

## The Glass-Frogs

They live in the *drinking mouths*, the *narrow fissures* where *condensation* *collects* and *drips*, *drop by patient drop*, into the *stone basins* that the *builders* of *old* *carved* and the *builders* of *recent* have *deepened*. ## What Grows in the Upper Dark

The people *do not go* there. The *shiver-wolves* *gather* at its *base*, *waiting*, *herding*, *guiding* those who *insist* on *curiosity*, on *disbelief*, on the *human* *need* to *know*, to *name*, to *count*, to *understand* in *ways* that this *place* *does not* *permit*, that it *teaches* through *consequence*, through *disappearance*, through the *specific* *failure* that *preserves* the *boundary* of *self* in those who *learn*, who *return*, who *become* *listeners*, *bridges*, *translators* between the *known* and the *unknown*, the *Holding* and the *upper dark*.

But *something* *lives* there. *Something* *breeds* in the *spaces between*, where the *fauna* *dreams*, where the *young stone* *learns*, where the *wind* *carries* *sounds* that are *not* *wind*.
