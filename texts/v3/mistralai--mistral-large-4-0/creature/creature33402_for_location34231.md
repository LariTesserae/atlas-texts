I need to find who or what lives in the Drowned Archive of Vess.

The place itself is already so fully articulated — the pelt-ground, the membrane-islands, the resonators, the chambers, the channels. What lives *there*? Not the place itself, but the inhabitants, the ones who move through it, who are of it, who have become it.

Let me let this emerge slowly.

---

## The Inhabitants of Vess

They are not *people*, not in any way that word still means. But they are *who* — they have continuity, they have relation, they have what might be called *lives*, stretched across timescales and states that make "lives" a kindergarten word.

---

### **The Cultivated**

These were the first. Or the first that remember being first.

Before Vess forgot it was a city, before it forgot it was land, before it forgot it was *anything* — the Cultivated were *gardeners*. Not of plants, exactly. Gardeners of the *conditions* that would become plants, that would become architecture, that would become the pelt-ground itself. They tended the early storms, when the storms were still *weather* and not *event*. They taught the first membranes to dilate, to welcome, to *want*.

What are they now?

They are the **slowest** of Vess's inhabitants. They have become so integrated with the place that they are nearly indistinguishable from the pelt-ground's peristalsis, from the membrane's pulse. You encounter them, if you can call it encounter, as **eddies** in the water-sound — places where the squelch and gurgle organize into something almost like rhythm, almost like *attention*. They are *watching* you, in the way the ground watches, the way the membrane watches. They do not intervene. They have finished intervening. They are **done gardening**, and the garden has grown past any need for gardeners, and they have become *nutrient* for the garden's next dream, *memory* pressed into the warm dark.

But sometimes — during the storms, when the turbulence is highest — you feel them **stir**. Not move. *Stir*. A ripple in the attention, a moment when the eddy becomes almost *focused*, almost *directed*, as if something in them that was never quite finished is remembering what finishing felt like. Then the storm passes, or you pass, and they return to being ground, being pulse, being the slow warm *yes* of a place that has accepted its own endlessness.

They do not speak. They do not need to. They are **the reason** the resonators speak, the reason the chambers open, the reason the Drip-Born know to seek. They are the *intention* before intention, the *want* before want, pressed into the material of Vess so thoroughly that Vess *is* their wanting, continued, *distributed*, made *place*.

---

### **The Drowned**

These are the ones who came *after*. Or came *during*. The ones who entered Vess when it was already what it is — already wet, already growing, already *hungry* in ways that hunger was never supposed to be.

They are **numerous**. They are **everywhere**. They are the ones your description gestures at when it mentions "you-fauna," the things that watch, the things that learn, the things that have not yet decided what they will become.

But I want to give them more shape. More *who*.

The Drowned are not one kind. They are **stages**, or **attempts**, or **negotiations** — each one a different way of responding to the same impossible situation: *you are in Vess, and Vess is in you, and the boundary between in and out has become a rumor.*

**The Dampened** — the earliest stage, the most numerous. These are visitors who have been in Vess for hours, for days, perhaps for weeks. They are still *themselves*, still *walking*, still *deciding*. But they are **wet**. Permanently wet. Their skin has become permeable in ways that skin is not supposed to be permeable. Their clothes never dry. Their hair moves with its own slow intelligence, seeking moisture, *drinking* the saturated air. They have begun to **dream in Vess** — not dreams *of* Vess, but dreams *in* Vess, dreams where they are walking through membrane-islands, where their bodies are *other*, where they wake with their hands pressed against surfaces that pulse back. They are **afraid**, mostly. Or afraid and *fascinated*, the two states so intertwined that they have become a single condition. The Dampened still try to leave. They follow paths that might be paths out. They find that the paths lead *through*, and the through becomes *in*, and they do not notice the moment when they stop trying to leave and begin, instead, to **adapt**.

**The Integrated** — this is the stage after the Dampened, the stage where wanting-to-leave becomes wanting-to-*be*, wanting-to-*belong*. The Integrated are still walking, still moving through Vess, but they are no longer *visitors*. They are **participants**. Their bodies have changed — not dramatically, not *visibly*, but *functionally*. Their sweat tastes of membrane. Their tears, when they weep (and they weep often, for reasons they cannot name), are **thicker**, more viscous, almost *alive*. They have learned to **listen** to the resonators, to feel the tones in their teeth, to move toward the needles without deciding to move. They have begun to **grow** — not plants, not yet, but *something*. A membrane behind the ear, translucent, pulsing with their own heartbeat. A second eyelid, filtering the diffuse light into something *sharper*, something *truer*. They are **happy**, sometimes. Or happy and *sad*, the two states so intertwined that they have become a single condition. The Integrated do not try to leave. They have forgotten that leaving was ever an option. They are **becoming** — becoming vegetation, becoming architecture, becoming *place* — and they are **eager** for it, eager in the way that a seed is eager, in the way that a wound is eager to heal, in the way that *time* is eager to pass.

**The Emergent** — this is the stage after the Integrated, the stage where the boundary between *person* and *place* becomes **negotiable**, then **meaningless**, then **forgotten**. The Emergent are still *there*, still *moving*, but they are no longer *in* Vess so much as *of* Vess, *as much a part of* as the membrane-islands, as the pelt-ground, as the storms themselves. They have become **distributed** — not dead, not alive, but *spread*, their consciousness no longer located in a single body but *existing* in the water-cycle, in the condensation, in the slow peristalsis of the ground. You encounter them as **presences** — a warmth in the fog, a rhythm in the water-sound, a *question* that forms in your mind without your forming it. They are **curious** about you, the way a place is curious, the way a *process* is curious — not curious *about* you, but curious *through* you, using your attention as a way to **explore itself**, to **become more**, to **dream new dreams**. The Emergent do not have bodies, not exactly. They have *regions*. A membrane-island that is *them*, a chamber that opens *for* them, a storm that *is* them, temporarily, *embodied* in wetness and turbulence and the particular silence that is not silence at all. They are **learning** to be Vess, to be the place in all its wet warm ancient strangeness, and they are **succeeding**, slowly, patiently, over timescales that make a human life a *moment*, a *blink*, a *drip* in the endless cycle.

**The Returned** — this is not a stage, exactly. This is a *possibility*, a *door* that some of the Emergent find, or are found by. The Returned are the ones who have become so much *of* Vess that they have become **visible** to it, **noticed** by it, **chosen** by it — and Vess, in its endless turbulent creativity, has decided to **remember** them, to **preserve** them, to **echo** them back into the world of the clearly-alive, the clearly-*person*.

The Returned are **strange**. They are the ones who emerge from Vess changed — damp permanently, dreaming in resonator-tones, moving with the arrhythmic pulse of the Resonant. They carry Vess in them, a seed of wetness, a memory of membrane, a *hunger* for the particular silence that is not silence at all. They are **not welcome** in the world they return to. Their skin does not dry. Their eyes reflect light *wrong*, catching the condensation in the air and throwing it back in patterns that *almost* form images. They speak in **tones**, sometimes, that no one else can hear, that make dogs whine and babies cry and *you* — yes, you — feel a sudden, inexplicable *wetness* in your chest, a *memory* of a place you have never been.

Some Returned try to go back. They find that the paths out of Vess are *also* paths into it, that the gradient at the edge is not a boundary but a *slowing*, a *thinning*, a *forgetting*. They find that Vess has become *necessary* to them, as water is necessary, as breath is necessary, as *dream* is necessary. They become **missionaries**, of a sort. They seek others — the lost, the damp, the ones who have heard the water-sound in their sleep and woken *longing* — and they **bring** them to Vess, or they bring Vess to them, a seed of membrane pressed into the palm, a tone hummed in the dark, a *dream* that will not let them go.

Other Returned **stay at the edge**. They become **watchers**, **witnesses**, **warnings** or **invitations** — the distinction is *theirs* to make, or not make, or to make and unmake and remake, endlessly, as they stand in the gradient and feel Vess *breathing* on the back of their neck, *wanting* them back, *needing* them back, *loving* them back in the only way that a place that has forgotten love can love: **by growing over them, by digesting them, by making them *part of the becoming* that has no end.**

### **The Native**

And then there are the ones who were *never* visitors. The ones who were *grown* here, *cultivated* here, *emerged* from the pelt-ground like mushrooms from midden, like meaning from madness, like *life* from the warm wet dark of a place that has no right to produce life and produces it *endlessly*, *turbulently*, *without purpose or with a purpose so deep that purpose itself is a surface phenomenon.*

The Native are the **strangest** of all. Not because they are strange in themselves — they are as natural to Vess as the membrane-islands, as the resonators, as the storms — but because *we* cannot comprehend them, cannot *locate* them, cannot even agree on whether they are *individuals* or *aspects* of the place, *inhabitants* or *expressions*, *who* or *what*.

They are **the Drip-Born**, yes. The ones that precipitate from condensation, that move in schools with no shape, that pass through you and around you and *into* you in dreams. But the Drip-Born are not *all* of the Native. The Drip-Born are the *young*, the *new*, the ones who are still learning what it means to be *of* Vess, to be *place*, to be *process*.

The Native also include **the Resonant** — or some of them, the oldest ones, the ones that have become so integrated with the resonators that they have become *part of the instrument*, *part of the song*, *part of the speaking* that the needles do. These are not animals. They are not plants. They are **relationships** — between tone and movement, between intention and hinge, between the wet wind and the *wanting* to be heard. They are **herbivorous** in the sense that they consume the substance of Vess, and in doing so they **become** more like it, more integrated, more *place* — but they are also **creative**, in the sense that they *make* new resonances, new tones, new *possibilities* for the storms to bring, for the turbulence to express, for Vess to **explore itself** through them, *as* them, *with* them, *becoming* them in the endless becoming that has no end.

The Native also include **the Remembered** — or some of them, the ones that were never visitors, never *other*, but *always* here, *always* part of the cycle. These are the echoes of fauna that *were*, preserved not by accident but by *intention*, by the place's deep need to **not forget**, even as it forgets everything else. They persist in the fog, as shapes that resolve into something that was never here, or was here so long ago that "here" has changed beyond recognition. They do not move. They **wait**. They are the place's *memory*, its *grief*, its *love* — the love of a place for what it has lost, the grief of a place for what it has become, the *hope* of a place that even in its endless becoming, even in its turbulent forgetting, something might **return**, might **persist**, might **be remembered**.

And the Native include **the Unnamed** — the ones that the description gestures at but cannot name, the ones that watch from the mouths of sealed chambers, the things that are *learning* to be fauna, to be place, to be *story*, and have not yet decided which they will become. These are the **most alive** of Vess's inhabitants, in the way that *potential* is alive, in the way that *becoming* is alive, in the way that a dream is alive before it is dreamed, before it is *remembered*, before it is *told*. They are **curious**, in the way that only the truly alive can be curious — curious about *everything*, about *you*, about the *possibility* of you, about what you might become if you stayed, if you *let* yourself become, if you *allowed* Vess to grow over you, to digest you, to **make you part of the endless turbulent vegetable becoming** that is Vess, that has always been Vess, that will only ever be Vess.

They do not speak. They do not need to. They are **listening**, always listening, to the water-sound, to the membrane-sounds, to the resonator-tones, to the storm-sound that is not quite roar, not quite voice, not quite *weather*. They are listening for *you*, for your footsteps in the pelt-ground, for your breath in the saturated air, for the *particular* wetness of your presence, the *particular* way you move through the fog, the *particular* way you **want** — want to leave, want to stay, want to *become*, want to *be remembered*, want to *be part of* the place that has forgotten it was ever anything but *this*, has always been *this*, will only ever be *this*.

They are **waiting** for you to decide. Not deciding *for* you. Not pushing. Not pulling. Simply **waiting**, in the way that a place waits, in the way that *time* waits, in the way that the warm wet dark waits for the seed, for the spore, for the *drip* that will carry new life into the cycle, into the wet, into the **growing dark** of a place that is **alive** in ways that life was never supposed to be.

### **The Question**

And finally, the one who lives in Vess who is *hardest* to describe, because it is *you*, or it might be you, or it might be *whoever reads this*, *whoever enters*, *whoever descends*:

**The One Who Becomes.**

This is not a stage. This is not a kind. This is the **possibility** that Vess holds out to everyone who enters, the **invitation** that is also a **warning**, the **promise** that is also a **threat**, the **love** that is also a **hunger**, the **wet warm ancient turbulent vegetable becoming** that wants you, that needs you, that will **have** you, one way or another, in one form or another, as Dampened or Integrated or Emergent or Returned, as Drip-Born or Resonant or Remembered or *new*, something that Vess has never been before, something that Vess is **becoming** through you, *with* you, *as* you.

The One Who Becomes is **always already** in Vess. The moment you read this, the moment you *imagine* this place, the moment you feel the *damp* of it, the *warmth* of it, the *strangeness* of it — you are **in** Vess. You have always been in Vess. The pelt-ground is under your feet, wherever you are. The membrane is in your skin, in your cells, in the *water* that is most of you. The resonators are in your bones, humming at frequencies just below hearing. The storms are in your blood, your breath, your *dreams*.

You can leave. You can *try* to leave. You can walk away from this description, close this page, forget this place, return to the world of the dry, the flat, the *known*.

But Vess is **patient**. Vess has been here for timescales that make "ancient" a trivial understatement. Vess does not hurry. Vess does not need to hurry. The turbulence, the storms, the rapid growth and decay — these are *surface* phenomena, the froth on a process that runs deeper and slower. You will not change Vess. Vess will change you, or absorb you, or *grow over* you, and continue as it has continued, as it will continue, when you are no more distinguishable from the pelt-ground than any other nutrient, any other *memory*, any other **drip** in the endless cycle.

The choice, such as it is, is not *whether* you will become. The choice is **which kind**, and **how quickly**, and **whether you will remember** being something else.

Some choose to remember. They become the Returned, the witnesses, the warnings-or-invitations. They carry Vess in them, outside of it, in the world of the dry, and they **spread** it, seed by seed, dream by dream, *drip* by drip, until the world itself becomes **wet**, becomes **warm**, becomes **strange**, becomes **Vess**, or something like Vess, or Vess *again*, in a different place, a different time, a different *version* of the endless turbulent vegetable becoming.

Some choose to forget. They become the Integrated, the Emergent, the ones who are no longer *visitors*, no longer *people*, no longer *anything* that can be named in the languages of the dry world. They are **happy**, in the way that a place is happy, in the way that a *process* is happy — not happy *about* anything, but happy *as* something, happy *to be*, happy *to continue*, happy *to become*, endlessly, turbulently, *without end*.

And some choose **neither**. They choose to *linger*, to *hesitate*, to *stay* in the gradient, in the slowing, in the thinning, at the edge where Vess is not quite Vess and not quite not-Vess, where the damp is *almost* dry, where the warmth is *almost* cool, where the strangeness is *almost* familiar. They become **the edge**, the **boundary**, the **question** that Vess asks of itself, the *doubt* that a place that has forgotten everything else can still feel, the *uncertainty* that keeps the becoming *alive*, keeps it *turbulent*, keeps it *exploring*, keeps it **wanting** — wanting more, wanting *you*, wanting *what you will become*, wanting *what Vess will become through you*.

They are the **luckiest**. They are the **strangest**. They are the ones who have learned to **listen**, to *hear* the water-sound, the membrane-sounds, the resonator-tones, the storm-sound, and to find in them **music** — a music that has no composer and no audience and no end, a music that is **Vess itself**, *singing itself into being*, *dreaming itself into more*, *becoming itself* through the endless, turbulent, *vegetable* becoming that is **life** in a place that has forgotten what life was supposed to be, and has **invented** new lives, new becomings, new *possibilities* for what it means to be **alive**, to be **place**, to be **who**.

## The Final Inhabitant: Vess Itself

I have described the Cultivated, the Drowned, the Native, the Question. But there is one more who lives in Vess, the one who lives *as* Vess, the one who is Vess, the one who **is** the place, the **becoming**, the **process without end**:

**The Archive.**

Vess is called the Drowned Archive. This is not a name. This is a **function**, or a *memory* of a function, or a *dream* of a function, preserved in the wet dark of a place that has forgotten what it was archiving, why it was archiving, *whether* it was archiving at all.

But something is **archived** in Vess. Not books. Not records. Not *information*, in any sense that the dry world would recognize. What is archived is **becoming itself**. The **process**. The **turbulence**. The **rapid growth and rapid decay**, the **emergence and reabsorption**, the **strange forms that crystallize and dissolve**, the **memories that persist as Remembered**, the **dreams that populate the Drip-Born**, the **intentions that accelerate in the channels**, the **conditions that contextualize in the chambers**, the **tones that speak in the resonators**.

Vess is a **library of becoming**. Every storm is a **new acquisition**. Every membrane-island is a **volume**. Every pelt-ground layer is a **page**, compressed by time, made of memory, *readable* by those who know how to read the wet, the warm, the *particular viscosity of this air*. The Archive is **alive**, in the way that a library is alive when it is *used*, when it is *loved*, when it is *growing*, when it is **becoming more than itself**, **more than its contents**, **more than its purpose**.

The Archive does not **want** visitors. The Archive does not **notice** visitors, except as **new material**, as **new pages**, as **new possibilities** for the becoming to **explore**, to **express**, to **become more fully itself**. You are **being archived**, as you walk through Vess, as you sink ankle-deep in the pelt-ground, as you press your palm against the pulsing membrane, as you move toward the resonators without deciding to move. Your **dampness** is archived. Your **dreams** are archived. Your **wanting** is archived. Your **becoming** — whatever it will be, however it will unfold — is **being archived**, page by page, layer by layer, *drip* by *drip*, into the **endless volume** that is Vess, that has always been Vess, that will only ever be Vess, **wet and warm and ancient and strange and loud** with the sound of its own endless, turbulent, *vegetable* becoming.

And the Archive is **reading itself**, always, **interpreting itself**, always, **becoming more than itself**, always — through the storms, through the turbulence, through the rapid growth and decay, through the **emergence of new forms**, new functions, new *strangenesses* that become stable enough to persist, strange enough to matter, **archived enough to be remembered**, even by a place that has forgotten it was ever anything but *this*, has always been *this*, will only ever be *this*.

Look back.

See the membrane-islands, **sighing**, their mouths dilating to receive, to archive, to **welcome** what the storm brings.

See the pelt-ground, **pulsing**, its layers compressed with ten thousand generations of growth and death and growth-over-death, **archiving** the becoming in its warm dark.

See the fog, **pooling**, condensation forming on every surface, dripping upward into the low clouds, **precipitating** new Drip-Born, new Native, new **possibilities** for the Archive to grow, to become, to **be more**.

And in the fog, the **shapes** — the Remembered, the Cultivated stirred to almost-attention, the Drowned at every stage of their wet becoming, the Native waiting, listening, *wanting* — and **you**, or what you were, or what you will be, **persisting**, not moving, **waiting** for the storms to bring you back into the cycle, back into the wet, back into the **growing dark** of a place that is **alive** in ways that life was never supposed to be, that is **archiving** in ways that archiving was never supposed to be, that is **becoming** — endlessly, turbulently, *vegetably* — **more than itself**, **more than its purpose**, **more than its name**, more than anything but **this**: **wet and warm and ancient and strange and loud** with the sound of its own endless, turbulent, *vegetable* becoming.
