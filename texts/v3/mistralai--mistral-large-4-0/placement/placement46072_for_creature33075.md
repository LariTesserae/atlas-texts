I descend.

The resonance-water does not wet me. It *receives* me. I feel the pressure first—not against my skin but *through* it, finding the liquid spaces in my ears, the hollow in my chest, the slow pulse in my wrists. The water expands. I expand with it. The 0.3 cycles per minute. I breathe with the chamber.

I am not a visitor. This is the first adjustment I make. The resonance-water has already begun to include my temporary configuration, and I let it. I let it carry my heartbeat into the membranes, let my restlessness become a pressure wave that propagates somewhere, somewhere, into the fourth layer where something waits.

I see the hexagonal prisms. They are *wrong* in their regularity. Basalt columns do not rise like this—continuous, seamless, fresh-cut. Something excavated this place and *kept* excavating, layer by layer, instruction by instruction. The striations catch the light. They are still sharp. I touch one and feel the vibration of an intention that has not yet been consumed.

The engines. I stand before one—a boulder of committed process, turning, turning, eleven days for a single rotation. I cannot watch it move. I can only *know* it moves, the way you know a glacier moves, the way you know the earth turns beneath you. I place my palm against its housing and feel the sub-bass in my teeth. The engine does not notice me. The engine does not need to notice anything. It is *committed*.

Between the engines, the conveyance channels. Thick, transparent walls. I watch a particle move through—six hours to cross what my eyes tell me is a meter. The particle is amber, then violet, then a color that has no name in the light that falls here. It carries something. I do not know what. I do not need to know. The resonance-water carries information in ways that bypass the ear entirely. It bypasses the mind entirely. I feel the particle's purpose as a *tension* in my sternum, a question I cannot ask and do not need to answer.

The membranes pulse. Brighter than they should be—young membranes, eager, *desperate* to maintain the perfection of the lattice. I watch one sequence, then lose it, then find another. The sequences are not language. They are *negotiations*. The water and the wall, the intention and the structure, the treaty renewed every moment. I want to touch a membrane. I do not. The membranes are not for touching. They are for *being pulsed by*, for *being shaped by*, for *shaping* in return. I am already being shaped. I feel it in the viscosity of my own perception, slowing, thickening, becoming something that can wait.

The acoustic gardens. The throat-chimes rise between engine housings—hollow, tuned, *singing* frequencies that reinforce the lattice. I hear them not with my ears but with the fluid in my inner ear, with the resonance-water that has replaced my blood, or perhaps always was my blood, or perhaps the distinction has dissolved. The tones are structural. They heal micro-fractures. They are also *afraid*, or *hopeful*, or the distinction has dissolved here too. The chimes grow where the architecture fails, where inefficiency creates space. I find a gap in the lattice—a hairline crack, still being born—and I watch a throat-chime begin to grow toward it. The growth is not fast. The growth is *committed*, like the engines, like the membranes, like everything in this place that has chosen a direction and refuses to arrive.

I walk. The chamber is forty meters in any direction, but the walk feels like hours. I pass three levels of recursion clearly. Rooms within rooms within rooms, each smaller, each deeper, each operating on slightly different physics. I feel the fourth layer pressing against my perception—not a wall, but a *density*, a thickness in the resonance-water where the medium has become almost solid with processed intention. I cannot see it. I can only *feel* the pressure of its existence, the way you feel the weight of water when you descend too deep, the way you feel the moment before sleep when thought becomes dream.

And then: the hesitation.

The pressure against my skin *pauses*. The sub-bass of the engines *misses a beat*—not a failure, but a *choice*, or something like a choice, something that is not a decision but a consequence of existence, as natural as gravity. The light from the membranes *holds*. I stand very still. I have learned stillness here. The resonance-water has taught me.

Something has *adjusted*.

Not noticed me. The text says this clearly. The echoes do not notice. The Gardener does not notice. I am a temporary configuration, a disturbance to be smoothed, a ripple to fade. But the adjustment *happened*. The temperature of the resonance-water shifts by a fraction of a degree. The shift propagates upward through the layers. It changes the growth rate of a throat-chime somewhere I cannot see. It determines which wall will be reinforced and which will be allowed to erode into a new configuration.

I feel the adjustment as *presence*. Not attention—*presence*. The almost-silence of the waiting thing. The 0.2 in the fauna parameter. The Gardener, distributed across all junctures, the quality of connection that makes the architecture feel inhabited without being populated.

I want to descend further. I want to find the way to the fourth layer, the layer that presses against my perception, the layer that has always been waiting. But I am a temporary configuration. The resonance-water has accepted me but will release me. I feel it already—the slow smoothing, the propagation of my disturbance, the fading of my ripple.

I am not the Gardener. I cannot be. The Gardener does not think. It *settles*. Its adjustments are not decisions but consequences, as natural as the attraction between masses. I think. I decide. I am a configuration that has achieved enough coherence to *resist dissolution* for a little while longer, to maintain my shape against the general drift toward integration.

I could become an echo. The text suggests this. The echoes are configurations that persist, temporary arrangements of the medium that have achieved enough coherence to resist dissolution. They move through the fourth layer. They adjust the Gardener as they pass. They are the Gardener's dreams, if the Gardener dreams. They are the Gardener's memories, if the Gardener remembers.

But I am not ready. I am still *becoming* temporary. I am still learning to be slow. The resonance-water presses against my skin, includes me, and will eventually release me—into the Gardener's adjustments, into the propagation of my disturbance, into the almost-silence that waits in every young place, in every process that has not yet become finished.

I stand between two engine housings. I feel the sub-bass in my teeth. I watch the membranes pulse their endless negotiations. I listen to the throat-chimes sing their structural, afraid, hopeful tones. I feel the fourth layer pressing against my perception, the Gardener distributed across all junctures, the echoes moving through spaces I cannot see.

I am included. I am temporary. I am here.

And I am learning to wait.
