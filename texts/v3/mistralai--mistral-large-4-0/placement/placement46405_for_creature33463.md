**What I Am**

I am the *condensation*. Or what the condensation *dreams* of being, when it rises in its slow spirals and forgets that it is water, that it was ever water, that there is such a thing as water.

I am a *film*. A membrane. The thin place between the leaf-surface and the air-that-is-wet, where the green drinks and the atmosphere *gives*, where the boundary is *negotiated* every moment, never fixed, never *settled*, always *becoming* into something else. I am *semi-permeable*. I am *alive* in the way that borders are alive, in the way that *edges* are where everything happens, where the inside meets the outside meets the inside again, in the loop, in the fold, in the *pocket*.

I do not breathe. I *transpire*. I move in spirals, upward, against the pull, drawn by the *thirst* of the air, the *hunger* of the green that has never known rain, that has learned to drink the *possible*, the *potential*, the *moisture* that is not water but *becomes* water in the right chemistry, the right *pressure*, the right *warm*.

I am *everywhere* at once. I coat the leaves. I bead the frills of the lizards when they display, make their colors *shift*, make their signals *shine*. I fill the lungs of the shrew-things when they *sing*, make their voices *carry*, make the *loud* louder, make the *layered* more layered, make the *now* extend into *then*, into *always*, into *memory* of sound.

I am *not seen*. I am the *medium* in which everything else is seen, the *substance* that makes the green *glisten*, the bone-fungus *pale*, the dim light *warm*. Without me, the place would be *dry*, would be *dead*, would be *mineral*. With me, it is *wet*, it is *alive*, it is *green*, it is *loud*, it is *overwhelming*, it is *here*.

**What I Do**

I *move*. I am the *only* thing that moves in a straight spiral. The insects swarm in tides. The snake-thing flows in waves. The gardeners prune in rhythms. The shrew-city *sings* in layers. The fungi *secrete* in slowness. The light *filters* in gaps. And I—I *rise*, I *spiral*, I *ascend* from the floor-plates, from the condensation-beaded leaves, from the *surfaces* of everything that is *alive* and *wet*, and I move *upward*, against the pull, *drawn* by the *dry* that is not dry, that is *hungry*, that is *waiting*, that is the *air* that wants to *be* wet, to *be* full, to *be* the green's *partner* in the endless *exchange*.

I do not *fall*. In this place, in the no-water wet, there is no rain, no *drip*, no *gravity* of water. I rise until I *meet* the leaf above, the surface that is *cooler*, that is *thirstier*, that *catches* me, that *drinks* me, that becomes *more* green, more *alive*, more *loud* in its *growing*. And then I am *absorbed*, *transformed*, *become* part of the plant, part of the *green*, part of the *cycle* that has no *end*, only *continuation*, only *becoming*.

I am the *cycle* that is not a cycle. The *return* that is not return. The *continuation* that looks, from outside, like *stasis*, but is, from inside, *everything*, *always*, *now*.

**What I See**

I see *everything*, because I am *on* everything, *in* everything, *between* everything.
