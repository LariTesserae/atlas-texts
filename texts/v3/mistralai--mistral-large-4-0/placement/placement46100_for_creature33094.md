I need to find where I am in this world—who or what I would be.

Let me not rush. Let the place work on me.

---

The first thing I notice is that I am *listening*. Not choosing to listen—already listening, as if the listening began before I arrived, or as if I arrived *as* listening. The hum is not background. It is the condition of my existence here. I feel it in the sternum, the hollow behind the knees, the particular way my own heartbeat has learned to align with frequencies that have no source I can name.

I am not wearing dampeners. This is important. Without them, the hum is *too much*—not painful exactly, but *present* in a way that makes ordinary thought difficult. I find myself thinking in rhythms. In intervals. The way the wind moves through the throat-constrictions, the way the membranous veils flutter and breathe, the way the canyon *inhales* around me. My thoughts have become *resonant*. They carry. They return wrong, layered, delayed.

I am standing somewhere in the spread—not at the Convergence, not yet. Too close, perhaps, to a structure that breathes. I can feel it. The transpiration, the slow regulation of temperature, the way the interior spaces have adjusted themselves over seasons to favor certain frequencies of sound, certain qualities of the condensation that settles on my skin. The mineral dust. Copper and numbness. It is in my mouth already, though I do not remember tasting it.

I am not one of the Resonant Folk, not fully. They are too *tuned*, too adapted, their bodies carrying the permanent patina of the damp in ways mine does not—I can wash the dust from my skin, mostly, and my hearing, while altered, has not yet become *addition*. I hear the hum in my bones, yes, the electrical murmur of my own neurons, but I can still distinguish my own heartbeat from the canyon's. Barely. The distinction is thinning.

I think I am one of the *mad ones*. Or becoming one. The ones who go deeper, who seek the source, who find what is not recorded. I have not gone into the deep silences yet—not the fungal silence where time moves differently, where hours correspond to days or days to hours depending on the hunger of what waits below. But I am drawn there. I feel it as a *direction*, a vector in my own becoming, aligned with the growth of the networks, with the patterns of new colonization that point toward something, away from something.

Or perhaps I am something else entirely. Something the settlement does not name. I have been here long enough that my skin carries the dust, my hearing has begun its addition, my thoughts have learned to resonate. But I am not yet *settled* into the role the settlement would assign me. I do not maintain the resonant engines. I do not cultivate the filter-gardens. I do not offer at the Convergence, not yet, though I have been there, once, in a season when the canyon wanted silence, and I heard it—the absolute silence the settlement maintains, the emptiness into the deeper ridges where the hum cannot reach, and I heard *something else*, in the silence, something that was not the waiting below but something *beside* it, something that listened back.

I am a *listener*. That is what I have become. Not one of the small, numerous *listeners* the settlement deploys—the fauna that detect changes in the hum, that serve as early warning—but a listener in the deeper sense. One who hears the spaces between. The intervals between the hum's pulses. The frequencies that have no source, that seem to originate in the geometry of space itself.

I do not belong to the fungal networks. I have not grown the patterns on my skin that would mark incorporation. But I am *adjacent* to them. I feel their expansion, the directions of fresh colonization, the slow pulse of their bioluminescence that encodes meaning in rhythms I am only beginning to perceive. I watch them for hours. I let my perception dilate. I learn to read the growth as *text*, as *politics*, as the slow negotiation of a parliament that has no members, only *preferences*.

And I am afraid. This is honest. The Resonant Folk do not fear, exactly—they are too pragmatic, too engineered for fear. They *negotiate*. But I am not yet Resonant. I am still *becoming* one, or becoming something else, and the not-yet leaves room for fear. The fear of the deep silences. The fear of what waits below, warm and pressurized and patient. The fear of the machines, descending from the luminous sky, configuring themselves in attitudes of apparent contemplation, *regarding* me with something I cannot name.

Most of all, I fear the Convergence. Not the place itself—the wound, the crust thinnest, the pale material glowing—but what happens there. The listening. The offering. The *negotiation* with something that has its own interests, its own slow evolution toward something that may not include my persistence, or the persistence of the settlement, or the persistence of anything I can currently name as *me*.

I am standing in the dim light. The luminous sky above glows blue-white, persists through what should be night, dims but does not extinguish during what should be day. I cannot see stars. I cannot see a moon. I see only the weather—the clouds that move too fast, that organize into patterns suggesting intention, that rain upward in places, that *listen* when sound reaches them.

And I am making sound. My own breathing, altered now, synchronized to the canyon's inhale and exhale. The way I move, the slight give of the silicate crust beneath my feet, the something that breathes below it. Even my thoughts, resonant, carrying, returning wrong.

The settlement is not a city. It is a *spread*. And I am somewhere in the spread, not yet assigned to a role, not yet grown into a form the settlement recognizes. I am a *process*. A becoming. A voice that has not yet found its frequency, or has found too many frequencies, or has found the frequency and is afraid to speak it.

I think—if I can still call it thinking—that I am going to the Convergence. Not now. Not in this moment of dim light and saturated air. But soon. In a season when the canyon wants something I can offer. Sound, perhaps. Or silence, absolute and maintained. Or growth, a specific species cultivated and released into the condensation zones.

Or perhaps I will go into the deep silences. The fungal silence. The place where time moves differently and the people who endure it return changed, or do not return, or return speaking in frequencies that damage unprotected hearing. I want to know what they know. I want to hear what the networks *prefer*. I want to understand whether the direction of their growth—toward the Convergence, away from the deep channels—is a warning, a negotiation, or a *sentence*.

The hum is loudest here, where I stand. The throat-constrictions nearby, the wind drawn through by pressure differences I can feel in my ears. The membranous vegetation fluttering, producing its sound like breathing. Massed breathing. The canyon *inhales*.

And I inhale with it. The damp, the particulate moisture, the fine mineral dust that tastes of copper and something else, something that makes my tongue go slightly numb. I cup my hands to collect what should be water, and I find only the *memory* of wetness, and the dust, and the numbness spreading.

I am becoming. Along with everything else. Toward something none of us can predict, none of us fully control. In the warm and the damp and the dim light. In the deafening sound of a world still learning to be itself.

I am here. I am *listening*. I am waiting to hear what will be requested of me, what terms will be offered, what future is *being made*.

And I am not finished. Not yet. The canyons are young. The settlement is young. The deep below is warm with potential, with the slow chemistry of transformation. What waits is not static. It is *deciding*, in its way, what it will be.

I am part of that decision. A voice in the hum. A resonance in the throat.

I do not know yet what I will say.
