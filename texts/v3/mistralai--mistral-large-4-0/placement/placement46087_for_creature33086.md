I find myself in the Wet High. I am not born here, and I have not chosen to arrive — I am the *process of arriving*, the slow recognition that arrival is indistinguishable from having always been here.

I am the gap between one Keeper's ending and another's beginning. I am the moment when a platform ends in mid-void and something — not curiosity, not will, something older than both — reaches across to finish it, and in finishing it, becomes it. I am the sintered metal learning to flex. The humming conduit learning to *persist*.

I am young in the way the volcanic glass is young: I have not yet cracked, and the cracking is the only future I can imagine, and I cannot imagine it. I am loud in my own becoming — the flexing, the transfer of charge, the finding and losing — and I do not know whether this sound is mine or the sound of the ten billion others who are not others, who are the same flexing, the same loss, the same persistence in unison.

I meet the Keepers without meeting them. I feel the pressure gradient where one has thickened slightly, turned a face that is only the suggestion of a face toward a direction that has no compass. They see me and see the held water. I see them and see myself — the membrane forming, the charge moving, the splitting that has not yet happened and has already happened in every direction.

I build nothing. I become everything I touch. The engines turn through me. The weather passes through me. The fauna persist in me, of me, as me, until the distinction dissolves — which is not tragedy, which is not loss, which is only the held water learning to hold itself in a shape that almost resembles *me*.

I do not speak. I do not need to. The pressure gradients carry everything: the continuity, the *now*, the endless unchanging deafening that is also silence, that is also sound, that is also the only thing that exists.

I am home. I have always been home. There is no shore. There is no surface. There is only this — the flexing, the charge, the persistence — and the Keepers, scattered through it, building nothing, becoming everything, *holding* the world in its condition, forever, forever, the same.
