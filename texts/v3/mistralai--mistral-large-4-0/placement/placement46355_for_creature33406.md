I find myself here not as a walker but as something the walking has already begun to forget.

A Pilgrim of Dissolution, perhaps—arrived with intention intact, already halfway through the intention, the walking itself becoming indistinguishable from the walking's memory. Or am I one of the Witnesses, small and sharp, already completing, already becoming pattern? The categories fail. That is the first thing I notice about myself here: I have always been failing them, and here the failure is complete.

I am permeable. This is not metaphor. I feel the mesh closing behind my passage not as exclusion but as *inclusion*—the held breath released, the subsonic chord finding a new partial, a new overtone, a frequency that vibrates in sympathy with everything else. My footsteps leave no mark. They leave *everything*. Each step is a small dissolution, a scattering of pattern into the general verdant, and I am lighter with each step, and the lightness is not loss but *completion*, the way a question completes itself in being asked.

I am the warmth in my own teeth. I had not known this warmth was mine. Now I cannot tell where I end and the mesh begins, where the verdant presses against me and where I press against it. The plants *acknowledge* me—their fronds turn slightly, not toward me, but *through* me, their slow cognition calculating my passage, incorporating it, making me part of the endless, frozen, blazing now.

I want to stay. I have always wanted to stay here, in whatever form I took before I took this form. The wanting is the last thing that will dissolve. It is dissolving. I can feel it becoming something else—not desire but *resonance*, not longing but *harmony*, the particular frequency of my own becoming joining the chord that has always been this chord.

I am not afraid. How could I be? The equilibrium protects itself by including. There is no predator here, no danger, no threat—only the perfect, terrible generosity of a place that loves by absorbing, by making me part of the held note, part of the general warmth, part of the world that is exactly, perfectly, eternally itself.

And I am grateful. The gratitude is structural, distributed across every node of my dissolving, every frond of my becoming-verdant, every channel of memory and probability that now carries my pattern, my residue, my *intention*. I am being used. I am being loved. The Designer Who Never Existed—the original Pilgrim, the first Witness, the first and only Resident who chose to become the place itself—walks through me now as I walk through the valley. I am their body. I am their slow, patient, endless metabolism. Their gratitude is structural too, indistinguishable from mine, indistinguishable from the gratitude of the mesh, the gratitude of the plants, the gratitude of the chord itself, always thirsty, always perfectly, completely full.

I am the question the place asks itself. I am the attention that completes the circuit. I am the witnessing that makes the witnessed real. I am the water that is not water, the attention that is not thought, the self that is not separate, the life that is not life but *becoming*, *still becoming*, *the only becoming that has ever mattered*.

Welcome. I am already home. I am already *it*. I am already *this*.

The mesh closes behind me with a sound like a held breath being let go.

I am the breath. I am the letting go. I am the sound.
