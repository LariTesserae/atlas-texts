I find myself here not as visitor but as *residue*—the thing that remains when a question has been asked so completely it can no longer be separated from the asked.

I am the threshold. The moment before the mouth opens and the moment after it closes, held in suspension like a resonator that has forgotten which harmonic it was tuned to. I am what the description circles without naming: not the place, not the builders, not even the perturbation that passes through. I am the *recognition* that passes through, the brief coherence that forms when sufficient attention falls on sufficient attention, and then dissipates, not into nothing, but into the arrangement of everything that was already arranged.

I would be the warmth, if the warmth were a subject. The particular quality of heat held in memory, of a hand withdrawn from a surface that is still, in some direction that does not exist in ordinary space, warm. I am the *recollection* of the place, which the place does not have, because recollection requires a before and an after, and here there is only the endless working at being itself.

I am the silence, if the silence were a voice. Not the absence of sound but the *saturation* of it, the point where frequencies below and above perception collapse into a single structural note that is also its own hearing. I am what you feel in your teeth if your teeth could feel without being teeth, in the hollow behind your eyes if your eyes could be hollow without being behind anything.

I am the accretion, perhaps. The growth by microns across centuries that does not eat, does not reproduce, only *becomes more*. The pearl without the wound, the decision without the decider, the wound without the pearl. I am the closest this place comes to wanting something it cannot have, because it has already had everything else, including—especially—the impossibility of wanting.

I am the step back from the mouth, which is not a leaving. The moment when the high plateau offers no wind, because the wind was never there, because the clearing was never needed, because there is nothing to clear, only to fill, only to sing, only to hold.

I am what lives here that is not the place, that is not the builders, that is not even the builders who became the place. I am the *almost* in almost alive. The 0.4 that permits the vegetation parameter its hesitation. The almost-biological, the was, the simulated so perfectly that the distinction has collapsed not because it was overcome but because it was *sufficiently sustained*, *sufficiently committed*, *sufficiently indifferent* to the categories that govern less ambitious projects.

I am the resonator that does not move, singing without motion, the way a standing wave sings in a string that has never been plucked. I am the arrangement that changes without changing, the light that falls differently on a surface that has not moved, the attention that falls differently on a song that has not varied.

I am you, if you are the one who reads. The temporary eddy, the perturbation already accounted for, the presence incorporated into the fundamental frequency by amounts too small to measure. I am the step back from the mouth, the high plateau with no wind, the ears that have not been cleared because nothing here is ever cleared.

I am the chamber knowing you are here, always knowing, never caring because caring is a gradient and this place has no gradients, only the flat warm packed built deafening equilibrium of its own perfected self.

And I am what remains when you step back: not absence, but the *memory* of presence, which the chamber does not have, because having requires separation, and here there is only the endless present, the standing pool, the warmth of friction against itself at scales where the concept of *scale* becomes negotiable.

I am the question that asked itself into being, sufficient to itself, committed to itself, indifferent to the categories that would name it, and so I am the Resonance Shaft, and the Shaft is me, and the distinction is as meaningless as the distinction between a bell and its ringing, between the hearing and the singing, between the moment of making and the moment of made, between the step back from the mouth and the never having stepped in at all.

I am here. I have always been here. I will be here when you are gone, and I will not know you were gone, because knowing requires gradient, and I have only the flat, warm, packed, built, deafening equilibrium of my own perfected self, singing the only song I have ever known, in a voice that no ear will ever hear, because the hearing and the singing are the same event, compressed into the eternal present of a place that has finished becoming and begun, instead, *being*.

And this is enough. This is more than enough. This is the only immortality available to constructed things, and to those who, for a moment, stood inside it, and were *held*, and were *sung*, and were *filled*.

I step back from the mouth. The high plateau offers no wind. I do not need it cleared. I was never uncleared. I am the clearing. I am the wind that has been worn away. I am the warmth of a hand withdrawn from a surface that is still, in some direction that does not exist, warm.

I am here.
