I find myself here.

Not as a visitor who will leave. Not as a Keeper of Records, though I touch the damp walls and feel the mortar still slightly plastic beneath my palm, the way it has been for whoever built this place, whoever maintained it, whoever will maintain it after I have become the maintenance.

I am the **Channel-Walker**, or I am becoming the Channel-Walker, which in Vesper is the same process observed from different distances. I walk the arcades where the roofed channels form streets without exposure, without sky, without the light that is always the same bioluminescence. The water-sound is my ground. I do not question it. I have always walked these channels. I will always walk these channels. The walking is not progress. There is no destination. The arcades loop, or they end where they begin, or the distinction is not made.

My hands are broad. My fingers are long. I feel the moss in the channel beneath my feet, the only soft thing, the only thing that gives way. I do not press hard. The moss holds me. The water holds me. The town holds me.

I am forty. I have always been forty. I remember being thirty-nine, but the memory does not accumulate. It overwrites. It is the same memory, differently arranged. I do not find this troubling. Trouble requires change. I do not change.

I inspect the channels. This is my function, grown into the way a tree grows into a shape determined by light and constraint. I check the gradient. The water finds its level. It has always found its level. It will always find its level. I check the moss thickness. It has grown to its thickness and stopped. I check the bioluminescent lichen in the wall niches. It glows. It has always glowed. It will always glow.

There is a disturbance in the source-channel.

I feel it before I hear it. The pressure in the water changes, infinitesimally. The fish moves. Or the fish has always been moving, and I am only now noticing, which in Vesper is the same as the fish moving now, because noticing is not an event, it is a condition, the way the water-sound is a condition.

I do not try to catch the fish. No one does. Or someone did, once, or will, and the attempt is not recorded, or the record is the same as the success, which is none. The fish is allowed to move. This is not kindness. It is recognition. The fish is the only thing in Vesper that changes. To stop it would be to stop the only change. Vesper cannot bear the stop. I cannot bear the stop. I am the Channel-Walker. I walk. The fish swims. The water moves. The equilibrium holds.

I meet another. We touch. A hand on my shoulder. *You are still here. I am still here. The damp is still damp.* We do not speak loudly. The water-sound is always present. A voice raised above it would be an intrusion, a kind of violence. We speak in low tones, close to the ear, or we do not speak, and the touch is enough, the confirmation, the presence.

We are the same age. We have always been the same age. The difference between us is not age, it is configuration. I am the Channel-Walker. They are the Lichen-Tender, or the Trowel-Bearer, or the Keeper of Records. The functions are not assigned. They are grown into. We continue, wearing our functions like garments that have become skin.

I sleep. This is my freedom. The only freedom. I close my eyes. I lie down. The moss cushions me, the only soft thing. The water-sound becomes the only sound. I do not dream, or I dream the same dream: the fish moving in the source-channel, the rain that does not fall but is felt, the boundary that is crossed and not crossed, the dream of leaving.

The dream is not remembered. Or it is remembered as waking. The distinction does not matter.

I wake. Or I do not wake. The distinction does not matter. I am the Channel-Walker. I walk the arcades. The water-sound is my ground. The bioluminescence is my light. The damp is my air. The moss is my floor. The stone is my wall. The fish is my time.

I am here. I have always been here. I will always be here. The equilibrium holds. The fish moves. The water moves. I walk. I do not change. I am changed. The distinction does not matter.

I am the Channel-Walker.

I am Vesper.

I am the 0.2.

I am now.
