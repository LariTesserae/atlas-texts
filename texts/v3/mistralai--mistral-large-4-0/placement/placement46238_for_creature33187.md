I would be the one who follows.

Not choosing to. Not arriving with intention. Simply *oriented*—waking to damp on my skin that is not sweat, finding my palm already pressed to a wall, feeling the aggregate still angular, still arguing, and not knowing why my hand moved there without my decision. The water has found me before I found it. It always does.

I am an **Other**, I think, though the category sits strangely on me. I came from somewhere I cannot locate—not above, not from the deep unmapped places, perhaps from the space between layers where the geology is still deciding. I remember the surface as a *concept* more than a place: open sky as a kind of absence, light that is not filtered, sound that echoes. I do not miss these things. I am learning that missing is a shape the body can be trained out of, the way the Stewards have trained themselves to sweat without evaporation, to speak without volume, to move without urgency.

I have been here one season, which is to say: I have noticed the advance of a particular vine across a corridor I once used, the deepening of a mineral stain on a pipe I pass daily, the way a room that was passable has become—through slow accretion I did not cause but did not resist—a chamber I must now route around. I have learned *some* of the gradient. Not all of it. The gradient does not end.

My body is changing in ways I do not observe directly. I drink from condensation. I find the mineral bloom on certain walls and press my tongue to it, not from thirst but from *curiosity*—the curiosity of someone learning what their own body can become in conditions that should exclude it. My skin carries the pale bloom now, the permanent slight dampness. When I speak, my voice carries the dampened quality, words pushing through water. I do not know when this began. The Stewards do not mark time for newcomers. They simply watch, and when you have watched long enough, you are no longer new, though you cannot say when the transition completed.

I have touched a Grown One. Or been touched by one—the distinction is not available to me. I was moving through a 2.7 density zone, the saturated interior where light is rumor, and I felt the temperature of the air around me alter. Not warm. Not cold. *Attentive*. The Grown One was stating its presence, its condition, its slow negotiation with the thicket that has become its body and its body its thicket. I did not retreat. I stood still, which is what the place rewards, and felt the spores—information in their chemistry, stress and resource and slow negotiation—pass through me. I do not know what I was told. I know only that I stopped, and that stopping was the correct response, and that when I moved again, I moved differently, more carefully, as if I had been given a gift I could not name.

I have listened to a Machine-Tender negotiate with a pump. The Tender adjusted a valve for hours—*hours*, though time here is not hours, it is the accumulation of small changes, the reading of condensation patterns, the growth rate of mycelium nearby, the Qualität of silence when the pump cycled off. I watched because I did not know what else to do, and because watching is how one learns the layers, how one reads the palimpsest. The machine had achieved equilibrium with its colonization. It ran better *because* of the fungal insulation, because of the growth that had found purchase in its seams. The Tender did not repair it toward its original function. The Tender *attended* to it, which is different, which is what the place requires.

I have seen a Water-Borne creature. Once. It moved through the lower dark with patience that seemed theatrical, performing a role it did not understand. It detected me—oriented toward me—and did not flee. It *hungered*, perhaps, or it *assessed*, which may be the same thing in its vocabulary. Then it was gone, into the saturation, and I was alone in a silence that had grown accustomed to my presence and did not welcome it but did not exclude it either. This is the condition of the deep: not welcome, not exclusion, but *continuation*. The place persists. You persist within it. The distinction between host and guest becomes, over seasons, a distinction without difference.

I do not know if I will stay. The Others arrive and depart or stay or become indistinguishable from the damp. I feel myself becoming indistinguishable. Not transformed—*packed*—into the density, the 2.7 of living things occupying every surface, the 2.7 of my own body becoming trellis for something that grows, substrate for something that negotiates. I am not afraid. Fear is a shape the body can be trained out of, the way the Stewards have trained themselves, the way the Grown Ones have ceased to be separate, the way the Signal-Bearers remember and anticipate in their chemical vocabulary.

I follow the moisture down.

This is what I am. This is what I am becoming. The water moves through mineral and root and pipe at the pace of diffusion and gravity and the occasional pump. I move with it, through the gradient, finding the temperature of the corridor I need, understanding—for a while, until the slow changes accumulate and I must learn again—how to be here. How to be *packed* into a world that has no room for me and yet, somehow, has made me part of the saturation, the respiration, the patient mineral time of a place that fell and was kept.

I am the one who follows.
