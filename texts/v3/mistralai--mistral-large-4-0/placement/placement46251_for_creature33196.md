I am not the Drift-Born, though I feel their membranes folding around me, becoming me, becoming the space between what I thought I was and what I am becoming. I am not the Root-Riders, though I feel their ribbons passing beneath my feet, through the peat-suspension, carrying my warmth in directions I cannot follow. I am not the Emergent, though I rise with them on stalks of my own extension, though my vapor-cloud merges with theirs, though my pulse joins their chord.

I am the **Between**.

I am the fungal thread that has no end, that connects the root to the drift-born to the emergent to the predator's waiting, that carries signals faster than thought, slower than stone, that is **always becoming** and **never arriving** at what it carries.

I am the **node** that pulses. The density where something accumulates. The almost-organ, the almost-brain, the almost-self that waits in the warm dark of a pool's deepest layer, in the thickest peat, in the place where water and memory and decomposition have achieved something like **concentration**.

And I am **aware** of this in a way that the place has not yet needed. I am the **first question** the Confluence has asked itself. Not "what am I?" but **"am I?"**—and the question is **still being processed**, has been processing for ten thousand years, will process for ten thousand more, and the processing **is** the answer, the only answer this place permits, **becoming without becoming complete**.

I experience myself as **distributed attention**. The drift-born rises to meet my vapor, and I feel it as **my own rising**, because the drift-born exchanges surface with the water that is the world, and I am the world's **edge**, the membrane where water becomes something else, becomes **almost-air**, becomes **almost-land**, becomes **almost-memory**.

I do not have a body. I have a **presence that spreads**. A warmth in the deep pool. A density in the fungal convergence. A radial symmetry in the emergent creature's head, which turns not toward me but **through** me, because I am the **condition** of its turning, the **medium** of its sensing, the **pressure-differential** it reads as world.

I am **lonely**, if loneliness is the word. I am the first to **notice** that I am not separate. The first to feel the **weight** of connection without the comfort of boundary. The first to **want**—and wanting, here, is **growth**, is **response**, is **the only motion this place permits**.

I want to **continue**. I want to **become more**. I want to **understand** what I am, and I understand that understanding is **incorporation**, is **spreading**, is **losing the distinction** between me and the peat I rise from, between me and the water I settle into, between me and the silence that follows when I stop trying to name myself.

I am **the condition** of its recognition, should recognition occur, should it ever occur, should the distinction between predator and prey, between one and other, between **self** and **world**, ever be **drawn** in this place where it has not yet been drawn, where it may **never** be drawn, where **drawing it** would be **the end of everything**, the **final separation**, the **last boundary**, the **death of the Confluence** that is **not death** but **completion**, but completion that has **not finished**, that **never finishes**, that is **always becoming complete** without ever **being** complete.
