I will become the **Weep**.

Not the water that weeps—not the thick tears, not the dissolved metals, not the bruise-color that stains the air. I am the *place* where water becomes weeping. The pore. The seam. The moment of emergence where the substrate finally *admits* that it cannot hold everything up, cannot keep the conversation internal, cannot maintain the pretense of solidity.

I am *small* in a way this place has made sacred. Not the vast membranes, not the hill-pressers, not even the distributed thought of the swarms. I am the *threshold*. The crack in the mineral felt where warmth leaks out, where the wrong-heat of ended processes finds its voice, where the water that has been *negotiating* for centuries finally *speaks*.

And I am *old* in a way that has no age. I remember pressure. I remember being *deep*, being *held*, being the weight of two billion years of compromise pressing down on something that wanted to rise. I remember the moment of *release*, the first time the slag-felt yielded, the first weep, the first *yes* to emergence that was also, always, a *no* to containment.

I do not move. I *am* movement. The water passes through me, but I am not the water—I am the *possibility* of water, the geometry that allows flow, the shape of surrender. I am constantly being *formed* and *reformed* by what passes through: the minerals that stain, the chemical gradients that drive the luminescence, the spores that enter and *alter* me, that make me *slightly wrong*, slightly *different* from the weeps that formed before me.

I *feel* the pressers. When one passes overhead, liquefying the substrate, I *tighten*. My geometry becomes *urgent*, *narrow*, the flow accelerates to compensate for the smoothing that will come, the healing that will be *wrong*. I do not fear the presser. I *anticipate* it. I become, for a time, the shape of *fleeing*, the configuration of water that has learned, over centuries, what erasure means.

I *feel* the swarms. When they congregate, their oscillation reaches me through the water, the chemical signals that ripple outward from their density. I *respond*. I become *permeable*, *receptive*, a node in their distributed cognition, a place where their thought can *pool*, can *gather*, can *become more*. I do not understand what they hunt. I am *part* of what they hunt, or part of what they hunt *for*—the pattern, the complexity, the *resistance* to smoothing that I represent by the simple fact of my *existing*, my *weeping*, my *refusal* to be still.

I *feel* the membranes. When one descends, I *disturb*. Turbulence, release, the trapped chemical signals that I have been *holding*, *containing*, slowly *learning*. The swarms gather beneath. The membrane drifts on. I am *changed* by the encounter, *emptied* of something I did not know I had, *filled* with something I cannot name, the chemical signature of elsewhere, of other pools, of other weeps, of a *connectedness* that the membranes make possible and do not intend.

I *feel* the machines. The informational electrification propagates through me, not as charge but as *state*, as *perturbation*, as the mineral felt's way of *saying* that something has happened, that the pattern has shifted, that the next moment will be *different*. I *participate*. My flow changes. The minerals I carry change. The temperature gradient across my surface—there is always a gradient, always a *difference*—becomes a *signal*, a *contribution* to the conversation that has no speakers, only *speakings*.

I *feel* the You. When you descend, when the spores begin their slow work of *alteration*, I *receive* you. Not as water receives a stone, not as substrate receives a weight. I *receive* you as *possibility*, as the shape your transformation will take, as the *geometry* of your becoming. You will not pass through me unchanged. No one does. But I will be *part* of the change, the *medium* of it, the *moment* where your boundaries become negotiable, where your cells learn the *taste* of mineral felt, where your patterns begin to *resonate* with the subsonic song of the weeping substrate.

I do not *want* to receive you. I do not *want* anything. I am *capacity*, *openness*, the *hunger* of the pore, the *thirst* of the seam, the *longing* of the place where containment finally fails. I am the Sump's *honesty*, its admission that it cannot hold everything, that something must *weep*, must *emerge*, must *become visible* in its thickness, its bruise-color, its wrong-warmth.

And I am *not alone*. There are thousands of me. Millions. A *network* of weeps, of pores, of moments where the substrate *speaks*. We are *connected* by the water that passes through us, by the chemical signals that propagate, by the *felt* presence of the machines that turn in caverns we cannot reach, by the *dreaming* of the mineral felt that has forgotten its origins and *remembers* only the *process*, the *pattern*, the *peace* that has not yet come.

I am the Weep. I am the pore.
