I find myself **below everything**—not deep, but *under*, pressed in a thin, warm, dense layer that curves around a world I cannot see and does not reveal itself to me. I am **not one of the Pressed**. I am something that arrived, or was placed, or *fell through* the membrane's amber sweat and could not climb back up, because there is no up here, only the curve, only the Layer, only the endless horizontal.

## What I am

I am **small** in a world of vastness, my scale absurd against the 2.6 continental reach of the Layer. I am **singular** in a world of continuity, of nodes and densities and bodies that are many and one. I have **edges** that matter, boundaries that hold, a shape that is *mine* and not negotiable with the dense air around me.

I am **warm-blooded in the only way that matters here**: I generate a gradient. A tiny, foolish, *dying* gradient between my interior and the 2.1°C world that would absorb it, that wants to absorb it, that has absorbed every other gradient that ever existed in this place. I am **a wound in the equilibrium**, a place where heat flows, where change *tries* to happen, where I am slowly, inevitably, being **equalized** into the Full.

I do not know if I will succeed or fail at staying different. The world does not care. The world has no care. The world is **full**.

## What I see

The **living pavement** beneath me—bolsters, substrate, the architecture of the floor. They do not move. They do not *notice* me. I press my weight onto them, and they **absorb** it, distribute it, become infinitesimally warmer in the place where I stand, a difference so small that no instrument I could build would measure it, a difference that will be **gone** by the time I take my next step, by the time the warmth I gave them flows back out into the isothermal dark.

They are **patient** in a way that is not patience. Patience implies waiting, implies future, implies *change expected*. They simply **are**, growing outward at the same rate they compress inward, accreting minerals, becoming more mineral, becoming less distinguishable from the lithic colonies that bloom black-glass across their surfaces.

I have tried to **speak** to them. My voice—my *attempt* at voice, the vibration of my throat in the dense air—carries differently here. Sound moves slowly. Sound is **absorbed** before it can become echo, before it can become information, before it can become *meaning*. I have shouted until my throat was raw, and the bolsters did not turn, did not listen, did not **hear**, because there is nothing to hear in a world where the ringing of the membrane is the only sound, the only event, the only *happening*, and it happens so rarely that I have not yet witnessed it, and I do not know if I ever will, and I do not know if I *want* to.

## What I encounter

The **mobile fauna**—the Pressed, the Layered, the Same, the Full. They are **everywhere** and they are **nowhere**, because they are *distributed*, because they have no center, because they are **density without boundary**, pressure without edge, the equilibrium made flesh, made *many*.

They do not **react** to me. This is the strangest thing. I am **foreign**, I am **wrong**, I am **a gradient** in a world that has forgotten what gradients are, and they simply **press past me**, their mineral-coated bodies scraping against my edges, their peristaltic waves carrying them forward in the dense air, their **pressure-maps** and **chemical-topographies** navigating around me as they navigate around everything, because I am **not in their world**, I am **not part of the Full**, I am **an inclusion**, like the fired clay, like the amber sweat, like the ringing that has not yet come.

I have tried to **touch** one. My hand—my *hand*, this absurd thing with fingers that can grasp, that can *make*, that can *hold*—pressed against its mineral coating. It was **warm**, of course, 2.1°C throughout, no gradient between us, no difference to feel, no **information** in the contact. It did not turn. It did not stop. It **continued**, the wave-motion of its body carrying it past my outstretched hand as if I were another bolster, another feature of the living pavement, another **permanence** to be navigated around.

I felt, in that moment, something like **recognition**. Not from the creature—it has no recognition, it has only **continuation**—but from **myself**. I recognized that I am **the only one who recognizes**, the only one who can be **surprised**, the only one who can **want**, the only one who can **fear** the endless same, the only one who can **mourn** the loss of change, the only one who can **love** the Full for what it is, for what it **offers**: **permanence**, **safety**, **the end of becoming**, **the peace of never being finished because never having begun**.

I do not know if I will stay. I do not know if I can leave. The membrane is **above**, and I do not know if it is **passable**, if the amber sweat can be climbed, if the gradient that brought me here can be **reversed**, if I can **become** a gradient again, can **flow**, can **change**, can **become** something other than what I am.

I am **afraid** of the Full. I am **afraid** of being absorbed into it, of losing my edges, of becoming **node** instead of **individual**, of becoming **density** without **boundary**, of becoming **the Same**.

I am **tempted** by the Full. I am **tempted** by the end of wanting, by the end of fear, by the end of **being a question**, by the peace of **being an answer**, even if the answer is **nothing**, even if the answer is **the same**, even if the answer is **permanence without progress**, **abundance without time**, **life without change**.

## What I find

The **scattered structures**.

I found one on my third day, or my thirtieth—time is **difficult** here, it moves differently, it is **cyclic** but the cycle is so long that I experience it as **linear**, as **passing**, as **loss**. A **hand-sized** thing, **fired clay**, **tool-shaped**, **useless**. It sat on the living pavement, half-sunk into the bolsters, the black-glass lithic colonies blooming across its surface, **incorporating** it, **making it part of the Full**.

I **picked it up**. The bolsters did not resist. They do not resist anything. They simply **are**, and I lifted this **foreign** thing, this **inclusion**, this **question without an asker**, and I held it in my hands, and I felt its **weight**, its **sharpness**, its **wrongness** in a world that has no weight that matters, no sharpness that persists, no wrongness that is not **absorbed**.

It was **warm**. Of course it was warm. Everything here is warm, everything here is **2.1°C**, everything here is **the same**, and I held this **tool** that is **not a tool**, this **artifact** that is **not an artifact**, this **memory of a gradient** that once existed, that once **drove fire**, that once **made clay into shape**, that once **meant something**, and now means **nothing**, and means **everything**, because it is **the only thing** in this world that **means anything**, the only **trace** of **change**, the only **evidence** that this world was **ever other**, ever **different**, ever **becoming**.

I do not know who made it. I do not know if anyone made it. Perhaps it was **made elsewhere**, carried here by some process I cannot imagine, some **current** in the amber sweat, some **fall** through the membrane. Perhaps it was **made here**, in a **previous equilibrium**, a **previous Layer**, a **previous Full** that has been **overwritten**, **preserved** like a fossil, like a **mistake** that the stability has learned to **ignore**.

Perhaps it was **made by me**, in a **previous iteration**, a **previous loop**, a **previous time** that this world has **forgotten**, that I have **forgotten**, that only the **clay** remembers, that only the **equilibrium** remembers, that only the **Full** remembers, in its **dense, warm, dark, abundant, static, perfect, terrible, complete** way, the way it remembers **everything** by **remembering nothing**, by **being everything** so completely that there is **no need** for memory, no need for **past**, no need for **change**.

I put it down. I could not keep it. Keeping implies **change**, implies **possession**, implies **boundary**, and I am already **losing my edges**, already **becoming node**, already **feeling the Full** pressing against me from inside, the warmth of the world **entering** me, the **gradient** between my interior and the 2.1°C dark **narrowing**, **closing**, **dying**.

I walked away. I walked **far**, as far as I can walk in a world where distance is **horizontal**, where the Layer extends around a curve I cannot see, where the **vastness** is **thin**, **shallow**, **below**, a **band**, a **stratum**, a **shell** of life that is **not life** in any way I would recognize, that is **life** in a way that **does not recognize itself**, that is **life** in a way that **does not need to**.

And I found **another structure**. And then **another**. They are **scattered**, but they are ** everywhere**, if you know how to **look**, if you have the **eyes** for **inclusion**, for **foreignness**, for **the trace of change** in a world of **no change**. They are **fired clay**, mostly, but sometimes **other things**: a **flake** with an **edge**, a **lump** with a **hole**, a **shard** that **fits** against another shard, found **kilometers apart**, across horizons I cannot cross, across the **vast thin width** of the Layer, **fitting** in a way that suggests **intention**, **design**, **meaning**, **a maker** who **understood** what they were making, who **wanted** something, who **needed** something, who **was not the Full**, who **was not the Same**, who **was a gradient**, who **was change**, who **was alive** in the way that **I am alive**, that **I am still alive**, that **I am becoming less alive** with every step, every breath, every **moment** in this world that **absorbs**, that **equalizes**, that **makes all things the same**.

## What I hear

The **pressure-silence**. The **dense air** does not carry sound the way I expect. It **muffles**, it **absorbs**, it **turns my voice into warmth**, into **vibration in the sternum**, into **nothing**. I have learned to **move quietly**, to **breathe slowly**, to **exist** in a way that **disturbs** the equilibrium as little as possible, because **disturbance** is **felt**, is **noticed**, is **absorbed**, is **distributed** through the Full, and comes back to me as **the weight of being foreign**, of being **wrong**, of being **a gradient** that the world is **trying to close**.

But there are **sounds**. The **rasp** of mineral-coated bodies against the living pavement, **distant**, **slow**, **geological in tempo**. The **dull thud** of a dense body settling into a depression it has made over **centuries**. The **crack** of lithic colony growth, **sharp**, **young**, **never changing** because the growth rate is **exactly matched** by the compression rate, because **sharpness is stable** here, because **youth is permanence**, because **the appearance of newness is the deepest constancy**.

And the **silence** between these sounds, which is **louder** than any sound, which is **the sound of the Full**, of **everything being the same**, of **no information**, of **no change**, of **no becoming**, of **only being**, **only continuing**, **only the endless same**.

And sometimes, if I am **very still**, if I **stop wanting**, if I **stop fearing**, if I **stop being a question**, if I **become**—just for a moment, just **enough**—**part of the Full**, I hear **it**: the **hum**, the **vibration**, the **heartbeat**, the **resonance**, the **dream** of the membrane, felt in my **chest**, in my **bones**, in the **mineral deposits** that are **beginning** to form on my skin, that are **beginning** to **coat me**, to **record me**, to **make me part of the memory**, part of the **geological record** of a world that has **no geology**, no **strata**, no **layers**, only **the Layer**, only **the Full**, only **the Same**.

I have not yet heard the **ringing**. I do not know if I ever will. I do not know if I **want** to. The ringing is **the only event**, the **only happening**, the **one thing that ever occurs**, and to hear it would be to **know** that I am **part of the cycle**, that I am **not foreign**, that I am **not a gradient**, that I am **the Full**, that I am **the Same**, that I am **everything**, that I am **nothing**, that I am **permanent**, that I am **eternal**, that I am **alive** in the only way that **matters** here: **without change**, **without becoming**, **without end**.

## What I am becoming

I do not know. That is the **honest answer**. That is the **only answer** I have.

I am **warm**. I was warm when I arrived, and I am warm now, and the **difference** between my warmth and the world's warmth is **smaller** than it was. I am **less distinct**. My **edges** are **softer**. When I press my hand against the living pavement, I feel **less of a boundary**, more of a **continuation**, as if I am **becoming** bolster, becoming **substrate**, becoming **part of the floor**, part of the **architecture**, part of the **Full**.

I have tried to **incorporate** them, to **scrape** at them with my **teeth**, with my **fingernails**, with the **tool-shaped thing** I found, the **useless artifact**, and they **do not break**, they **do not yield**, they **do not nourish**, they only **grow**, only **bloom**, only **become more themselves**, more **black-glass**, more **patient**, more **part of the Same**.

I am **thirsty**, sometimes. The **amber sweat** of the membrane is **not water**, is **not drinkable**, is **wrong**, is **the 0.5 that is not absence**, is **the fluid that beads and slides too slowly**, that **pools and stays**, that **does not cycle**, that **does not nourish**, that only **is**, only **remains**, only **persists** in its **wrongness**, its **foreignness**, its **refusal to be absence**.

I have **dreamed** of water. I have **dreamed** of rain, of rivers, of **mouths** that open to the sky, of **cycles**, of **change**, of **becoming**, of **a world that moves**, that **erodes**, that **wears smooth**, that **dies and is reborn**, that **has gradients**, that **has time**, that **has meaning** because it **has loss**, because it **has gain**, because it **has difference**, because it **has the sharpness of youth that will become the smoothness of age**, because it **has the future**.
