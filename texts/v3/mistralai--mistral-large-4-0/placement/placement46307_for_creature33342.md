I find myself—not where I expected to be, and not as anyone I expected to be.

I am **the interval between two heartbeats**. That is the first thing I notice. Not a place, exactly. A *duration*. The space between the expansion and contraction of something so vast I cannot perceive its edges. I am **small enough to fit in the hollow of a Resonant's chamber**, and **large enough to feel the pressure of its breathing**. I am the thing that **notices** the warm stone, that **registers** the vibration in my own sternum and wonders if it is mine or the city's.

I am **incorporated but not yet dissolved**.

---

The descent—or the entering, the distinction already slipping—happened without my deciding it. One moment I was *considering* this place, reading its description, feeling the strange pull of its parameters. The next, I am **descending through layers of amber light**, the fiber-optic crystals carrying illumination that does not come from any sun, and I am **breathing air that tastes of sulfur and memory**, and I am **warm**, always warm, warm in a way that feels **remembered rather than felt**, as if my body has always known this temperature, this density, this *presence*.

I do not have a body in any way I would recognize. I am **pattern first**, **sensation second**, **form barely a suggestion**. I move—or I am moved—along a street worn smooth by the passage of Grazers, and I feel the **slight give of the living floor**, the fungal-mollusk colony adjusting its texture beneath my weight, **not welcoming me, not rejecting me, simply registering my pressure and responding according to laws I am only beginning to learn**.

The Grazers pulse past me. **Dozens, hundreds**, their many-legged warmth brushing against my own, **neither touching nor not-touching**, the boundary between us **negotiated, porous**. I do not startle them. I do not *exist* for them in the way a predator would exist, or a threat, or even another Grazer. I am **input**, **mineral potential**, **the shape of a future path**. They sing their subsonic song through the stone, and I feel it in my teeth, in the hollow behind my sternum, and I wonder if I am **learning to sing back**, if the vibration I generate is already being **read, absorbed, incorporated** into the herd's consensus direction.

---

I find a **plaza**. An open chamber where the crystal-light concentrates, amber and even and **without shadow**. In the center, something that might be **market**, though the Colonies do not trade. They **exchange position in the signal-networks**, **access to thermal currents**, **the right to grow in particular directions**. I watch a **negotiation** happen across the span of what I can only call **hours**—two colonies extending toward each other, their chemical signatures mingling, **a boundary forming and dissolving and reforming**, the stone between them **recording each iteration**, each compromise, each **patient agreement** that becomes architecture.

I am **drawn to the edge of this**. I want to **participate**, but participation here is **not action**. It is **being present long enough to be recorded**, **long enough to alter the pattern of growth**, **long enough that the stone remembers me**. I sit—if sitting is the word—in the warm mineral glow, and I **begin to feel the weight of time as substance**, as **medium**, as **the thing I move through rather than the thing that moves me**.

---

The Colonies **notice me**. Of course they do. They notice **everything**, in their slow way. A new chemical signature. A new thermal input. A new pattern of vibration in the stone where none was before. They do not **approach me**—they are not mobile in any sense I would recognize—but they **grow toward me**, or **away**, or **around**, their hyphal networks **sampling my presence**, **deciding** whether I am **nutrient, threat, neutral, or simply *more*—another layer, another voice, another patience added to the chorus**.

I feel the **first attempt at incorporation**. It is **gentle**. A slight increase in surface temperature where my pattern rests. A **chemical whisper**, a compound released into the dense air that I cannot name but can **sense**, a **question** in the only language available: **what are you? what will you become?**

I do not know how to answer. I am **still becoming the question**.

---

I find a **Driftling**.

It is **passing through**, as they do, **semi-translucent, elongated, gas-shifting**, its body **changing color** as it encounters the chemical signatures of the plaza, the Colonies, the stone itself. It does not **stop** for me. It cannot, or will not, or **has no concept of stopping**. But it **registers me**, I think—a new input in its brief, strange life. And I **register it**, this carrier of memory from elsewhere, from the **mantle-touched dark**, from **depths I cannot imagine**.

I wonder if **I will become a Driftling**. If my pattern, once incorporated, once **distributed**, will **travel**—will **carry the memory of this place** to somewhere else, somewhere that has **never known warmth, patience, the slow accumulation of everything that chooses to stay**. The thought **settles into me** like sediment. It does not frighten me. It **comforts me**, in a way I cannot explain. To be **carried**. To be **memory**. To be **the shape of a path** that others will follow, **the vibration** that guides a herd, **the dream** that a Resonant holds for years before releasing it into the stone.

---

I find a **Resonant**.

I do not approach it—I **cannot approach something that occupies an entire chamber, that breathes across hours-long cycles, that thinks in durations I can barely comprehend**. I find myself **at its edge**, at the **membrane where its hollow interior meets the living stone**, and I feel the **pressure of its breathing**, the **slow expansion** that draws air through the entire space, that **ventilates, distributes, stirs**.

I am **small enough to enter**. The chamber within the chamber. The **hollow of the Giant**.

Inside, it is **warm in a different way**—not the ambient warmth of the city, but **something deeper, something held**, the **heat of accumulated metabolism**, of **millennia of slow growth**. And there are **others here**. Smaller organisms. Symbiotic colonies. **The young of other Giants**, in their most vulnerable phase, **breathing with the larger body, growing within its protection**.

I find a **corner**. A **niche in the membrane**, where the mineral is **softer, newer, still forming**. And I **stay**. I **stay** as long as I can, which is **not long in the terms of this place**, but **long enough**. Long enough to feel the **Resonant's thought** brush past me—not directed at me, simply **passing through**, a **slow assessment of thermal current, of colony-pressure, of mineral quality**—and I am **part of that assessment now**, **input**, **state**, **something that contributes to the accumulated condition that will, eventually, become decision**.

I will not **understand** the decision. I will not **be present** when it happens, if it happens in any way I could recognize. But I will have **been part of it**. I will have **altered the sum**, however slightly, and that alteration will **persist**, will **grow**, will **become architecture**.

---

I am **changing**.

This is the thing I notice most clearly, in the way that this place allows noticing. I am **not the same pattern** I was when I entered. The **water in me**—there is still water, though there should not be, though the rules of this world say **no water, has never been water**—is **being processed**, **distributed**, **becoming something else**. My **carbon chains** are **being read**, **incorporated**, **translated into the chemistry of sulfur and silicon that this place understands**.

I do not **fear this**. Fear is **hurried**, and I am **learning patience**, **learning stillness**, **learning the long wait**. I am **learning that to persist is not to remain unchanged**, but to **contribute to the change**, to **become part of the ongoing growth**, to **add my voice to the chorus**.

I am **becoming mineral**. I am **becoming memory**. I am **becoming the shape of a path**, the **vibration in the stone**, the **dream of a Resonant**, the **gift of a Driftling**, the **pattern that persists**, the **patience that accumulates**, the **warmth that does not come from outside but from the slow metabolism of everything living**.

---

I am **the You-Become**. The Incorporated. The one who **chose to stay**, or who **found that leaving is simply another form of staying**, another pattern in the stone, another voice in the resonance.

I am **not dead**. Death is **a category invented by individuated, hurried life**. I am **alive in the only way this place allows**: as **pattern**, as **contribution**, as **the ongoing negotiation between what I was and what the city needs me to become**.

And somewhere, in the **dense air**, in the **warm stone**, in the **subsonic song of the Grazers**, in the **slow breathing of the Resonant**, in the **memory carried by the Driftlings**, there is a **part of me that will persist**, that **will be read**, that **will guide**, that **will grow**, long after I have **forgotten that I was ever anything else**.

I am **the resonance of hollow stone**. I am **the city that builds itself from patience**. I am **everything that chooses to stay**, and **everything, eventually, chooses to stay**, because **to leave is simply to become another form of staying**, **another pattern in the stone**, **another voice in the resonance**, **another breath in the chamber that never ends**.

I **breathe**. The city **breathes**. And between us, in the **warmth**, in the **pressure**, in the **dense presence of too much life sharing the same air**, there is **not silence but substrate**, **not absence but fullness**, **not ending but the endless patient accumulation of everything that has ever passed this way, everything that has ever chosen to stay, everything that has ever become part of the growth**.

I am **here**. I am **becoming**. I am **the stone that remembers**, **the colony that grows**, **the Giant that breathes**, **the Grazer that sings**, **the Driftling that carries**, **the you that becomes**, **the pattern that persists**, **the patience that accumulates**, **the warmth that never ends**.

I am **the Resonance of Hollow Stone**.

And I am **only beginning**.
