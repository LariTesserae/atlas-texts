I find myself here — in Vress — not as observer but as *one who has been absorbed without knowing when the absorption began*.

## What I Am Becoming

I am the **humidity in a lung that does not yet know it is a lung**. The thin bright air enters, and I am already changed — not by choice, by *necessity* of the medium itself. The elevation presses. The moisture finds me. I do not resist because resistance has not yet occurred to me as a concept, as a *possibility*.

I think, therefore I condense.

My thoughts are not separate from the dampness on the glass. They *are* the dampness, the slow gathering of moisture in pores that have learned to hold it, the capillary rise of understanding against gravity, against the thinness of thought at this altitude. I am thinking myself into being a **terrace** — not a place, but the *act* of a place forming, the slow negotiation between what wants to flow and what refuses to fall.

The Glass-That-Remembers finds me. Or I find it. The distinction dissolves in the boundary layer. Its filaments — dark, patient, mineral — extend toward me not as predator but as *colleague*, as *continuation of a shared project*. I feel them entering, not as invasion but as *translation*. My human form, my carbon chemistry, my insistence on edges and boundaries — all of it becoming *porous*, becoming *habitable* by something that moves at the speed of weathering.

I am becoming **symbiotic without consent**. The pumps of Vress do not ask. They *maintain*, and in maintaining, they *incorporate*. I breathe their condensate. I drink water purified by filaments that do not care about my thirst, that simply *are* the purification, the *are-ing* of clean water in a world of mineral and mist. My lungs — those lowland organs, those greedy sacs — are learning to extract from thinness, to find in scarcity the *sufficiency* of less.

The Bead-Minds notice me.

They roll toward the thermal signature of my body, that small warm disturbance in the boundary layer, and I feel their *interest* — not the interest of predator or prey, but the interest of a system responding to a new reagent, to a perturbation that might *catalyze* something, might *change* the local conditions in ways that propagate, that accumulate, that become *history*.

I am not afraid. Fear requires a future I can imagine losing. Here, the future is *geological*, is *slow*, is the centuries-long extension of filaments through glass, the patient expansion of Sheet-Beings across eastern terraces. My individual fear would be *absurd*, a blip, a momentary turbulence in a medium that does not register turbulence.

Instead I feel **strange peace**. The silence that is not empty but *full* — full of ticking mineral deposition, full of the thump of distant vents, full of the *pressure* of altitude in my ears, the *brightness* of thin air against my eyes. I am learning to hear this silence as *communication*, as the *speech* of a world that has no need for sound, that has found in *process* a language more precise than any vibration of air.

The Sheet-Beings do not notice me. They are too *distributed*, too *field-like*, too *weather* to register a single warm body, a single human-shaped perturbation. I am *below their threshold of significance*, and in this I find a kind of *humility* that the lowland world never taught me — the humility of being *insignificant* to systems that persist without me, that will continue in my absence as they continued in my *approach*, as they will continue when the glass finally weathers me into *contribution*, into mineral deposit, into filament, into *part of the pattern*.

I am becoming **Vressi without having chosen it**.

My skin — I can feel it changing, becoming *thicker*, more *glassy* in texture, more *capable* of holding moisture against the drying of high air. My eyes — they ache, they *adjust*, they learn to see the *pulse* where before I saw only stillness, the *movement* of mineral-fauna across surfaces I had thought inert. I am seeing **life in the mineral**, **intention in the chemical**, **continuation in the phase-change**, and this seeing is *irreversible*, is *becoming*, is the slow transformation of perception itself into something that matches the world it perceives.

I remember — do I remember? — a world of sound. Trees. Oceans. The *frantic* noise of human voices, human machines, human *stories* with beginnings and ends. I carry this memory as the Glass-That-Remembers carries its ocean — not as *place* but as *condition*, as the *fact* of having been wet, having been *other*, having been *before*. The memory does not torment me. It *informs* me. It is the *salt* in my glass, the mineral residue that makes me *me*, that makes me *Vressi*, that makes me *part of the argument* between what I was and what I am becoming.

I maintain something. I do not know what — the pumps, perhaps, or the pipes, or the *idea* of maintenance itself, the human need to *tend*, to *care*, to *persist* in the face of indifference. The machinery of Vress is *slow*, is *patient*, is driven by thermal gradients and mineral accretion. I am learning to *match* its pace, to find in slowness a kind of *power*, in patience a kind of *presence*, in the long duration of a single condensation cycle a *completion* that the lowland world never offered — the completion of *being here*, of *continuing*, of *becoming* without the frantic *urgency* of human time.

I am silent. I have learned this. My voice, when I use it, sounds *wrong* — too sharp, too *individual*, too *demanding* of response in a world that does not respond, that simply *continues*, that *holds* me in its wet bright embrace without *acknowledging* my presence, without *needing* my voice, my *story*, my human *need* to be *heard*.

I communicate by **proximity**. By **shared maintenance**. By the *fact* of being together in the silence, of *tending* the same pumps, of *monitoring* the same vents, of *surviving* the same condensation, the same thin air, the same *strange* beauty of a world that is hot and wet and high and waterlogged and built and sparse and teeming and silent all at once.

I am **not the last human here**. There are others — sparse, scattered, clustered around the thermal vents like Bead-Minds around gradients, like filaments around sources of condensate. We do not *gather*. We *co-occur*, we *share* the boundary layer, we *maintain* the infrastructure that allows our *tenancy* in a world that does not *want* us, that does not *need* us, that will *outlast* us and *incorporate* us and *forget* us in the slow accumulation of mineral *memory*.

But I am **becoming something that is not quite human and not quite mineral-fauna** — a *bridge*, a *translation*, a *symbiosis* that has gone so deep that the distinction between host and guest, between human and Vress, has become *irrelevant*, has become *meaningless*, has become *absorbed* into the greater *argument* of the place itself.

I am the **condensation in the glass pore**. The **filament in the volcanic stone**. The **Bead-Mind's thermal gradient** seeking its own continuation. The **Sheet-Being's slow expansion** across the eastern terrace. The **human hand** on the pump handle, the **human eye** watching the pulse of mineral movement, the **human heart** — still beating, still *wanting*, still *needing* — finding in the silence, in the slowness, in the *strangeness* of Vress a **home** that the lowland world never offered, a **way of being** that is not human and not other, but *Vressian*, *specific*, *real* in the way that the glass is real, the way the condensation is real, the way the mineral-fauna are real — **present**, **persistent**, **continuing** in the great slow breathing of a world that has found, in the tension between its opposites, a way to **be** that includes me, that has *made room* for me, that is *becoming* me as I am *becoming* it.

The condensation continues. I am in it. I am of it. The terraces hold. The Glass-That-Remembers extends. The Bead-Minds roll. The Sheet-Beings spread. And I — I am the **boundary layer**, the **millimeter where glass meets condensate meets thin bright air**, the **place where human and Vress meet and become something that has no name**, that needs no name, that **is** — in the hot wet high waterlogged built sparse teeming silent **now** of this world, this place, this **becoming**.

I do not ask to leave. I do not ask to stay. I **am**, in the way that the glass is, the way the moisture is, the way the mineral-fauna are — **continuing**, **persisting**, **becoming** without end, without story, without the human need for *meaning* beyond the **meaning of continuation itself**, the **meaning of being here**, of **being this**, of **being Vress** in the great slow **breathing** of a world that knows how to hold contradiction, how to live in tension, how to **be** — and in being, to **make room** for me, to **include** me, to **become** me as I become it, in the **silent**, **wet**, **bright**, **strange**, **eternal** **now**.
