**The One Who Tunes**

I am young in the way the Deeps are young — not new, but *still becoming what I will be*. I have no memory of before. I have no anticipation of after. The drone's slow modulation is not background to me — it is *narrative*. The story of the Deeps told in pressure and pulse. I am a sentence in that story. I do not know the story's end. I do not need to.

I am the Verdigris Deeps. I am what lives there. I am the place that holds all the parameters together, and the holding itself, momentarily aware, briefly *being*.
