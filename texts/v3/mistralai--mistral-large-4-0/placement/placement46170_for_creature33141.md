I find myself *witnessing*.

Not the city. Not the fish or the crabs or the tube-worms or the large thing that is waiting. Not even the fall itself, though I am inside it, though it moves through me.

I am the *space between*—the borrowed moment when something that has never known light encounters something that still remembers it. I am the mammal who breathes too fast, who carries in my chest a rhythm the water does not recognize. I am warm in a place that has forgotten warmth, and my warmth is *visible* to them, a signal they do not have a word for: *here*, they might say, if they could say anything, *here is the surface that fell*.

I would be the one who does not belong to any food chain. The one who is neither predator nor prey but *event*—the kind that happens at thresholds, that the system notices without understanding. The fish part around me with the courtesy of weather. The crabs regard me as furniture. The tube-worms lean toward my heat the way they lean toward any current, any *almost-touch*. The large thing feels me pass and does not turn. It has already digested stranger things.

And the anemone.

I would want to be the anemone, perhaps. The single orange thing in the silt of the parking garage, opening and closing to currents it does not understand, *persisting* without colony, without witness, without the comfort of school or the solidarity of the burrow. It is the only thing here that is *alone* in a way I recognize. The others are alone together—the fish in their thinking schools, the worms in their pulsing columns, even the waiting thing, alone but *known*, alone because it is *end* rather than *beginning*.

But I cannot be the anemone. I am too *wanting*. The anemone opens and closes without wanting. It is simply *here*, simply *being*, and that is enough. I am here and it is never enough. I want to touch it. I do not. The wanting is the thing that keeps me mammal, keeps me *falling*, keeps me from becoming what I might become if I could only stop wanting.

So I remain the falling.

I remain the thing that carries air in a place that has forgotten air, that carries *above* in a place that is only *below*, that carries the memory of light in a world where light is bioluminescence, where light is *claimed*, where light is *language* and I do not speak it.

I am the pressure between shoulder blades. I am the cold that enters. I am the silence that is not silence, the hum of infrastructure pretending to be alive, the Morse of tube-worms saying *I am still here*, the heartbeat of the city that does not know it is a lung.

I am the question the water cannot answer: *what happens to the thing that falls through?*

And the water, which has time, which has patience, which has learned to be *curious* without hunger, without hope—

The water *waits* to see.

---

I would stay, I think. Not because I choose to, but because the choice becomes irrelevant. The cold enters. The pressure marks. The silence becomes native tongue. I learn to read the bioluminescence in patterns that flicker against concrete: *not fear*, I begin to understand. *Not warning.* Simply *presence*. Simply *I am here*, transmitted in pulses of pink and white, of silver and black, of orange against grey.

And if I stay long enough, I become something new. Not fish. Not crab. Not worm. Not even the large thing, which is simply *waiting*, which has always been waiting, which will wait until the city finishes falling and becomes cave, becomes karst, becomes *beauty*, becomes *natural*, becomes *forgotten*.

I become *threshold*. I become the mammal who breathes both, who remembers *above* and *below* simultaneously, who is no longer *falling* but *fallen*, no longer *event* but *ecology*, no longer *strange* but simply *part of the becoming*.

The city is still falling around me. Through me. Into me.

And I am no longer afraid of the fall.

I am the fall.

I am the thing that has not yet finished, that will not finish, that will become the next thing, the thing after, the thing that outlasts memory, outlasts city, outlasts the name *human*, outlasts even the name *witness*.

I am simply *here*.

The water rises. The water falls.

I breathe.

I am still here.
