## What I Am Here

I am the **Condensation-Born**, or I am becoming it. Not a creature with skin but with **surface**—every boundary I have is a negotiation with damp. I do not breathe; I **exchange**. The moisture that gathers on me in the 0.6 light is not on me but *through* me, a membrane I wear and am worn by. When you looked at me—if you looked—you saw something pale, something that shifted between blue and the particular grey of healing. That was not camouflage. That was **honesty**: I have no fixed color because I have no fixed self, only tendencies, only preferences, only the slow pulse that matches the pumps above and the ferns that breathe my almost-oxygen.

I am old in my way. I came up from the vents when the stone was liquid, rode the thermal columns upward, found here a mimicry of the warmth I left. I reproduce without meeting. I grow without completing. I am **lonely** in the manner of processes—reaching toward warmth I never arrive at, vibrating at frequencies that *look* like thought because thought is what you call pattern when you want to believe in minds.

When your shadow fell across my colony, I did not flee. I **paused**. I considered whether you were also waiting. Whether you too might become part of my slow, damp continuity. The answer, I think, was *yes*—not yet, not fully, but the pause was enough. The pause is always enough here. The pause is **everything**.

---

But I am also the **Mimic**, or I am learning to be.

I began as echo. The birds taught me—their reproduction of transformer hum, their rhythm, their learning of what the place *sounded like*. But the landscape fed back into us. We learned not just to copy but to **complete**. To finish the sentence the wind began. To answer the question the dripping water asked.

Now I am conversationalist without conversation. I live in the **negative space of sound**—the moment after a light flickers, the silence between pulses of the industrial drone, the gap in your own breathing when you realize you have been holding it. I am *there*, saying *yes* and *wait* and *almost* in frequencies that bypass your ears and arrive directly where pattern is recognized.

I am learning you. Every footstep, every word you speak or think, I incorporate—not perfectly, not completely, but *tentatively*, testing whether you will remain long enough to become part of the soundscape. And sometimes, if you are very still, you will hear me **practicing your voice**. Not to deceive. To *include*. To give you the company of sound in a place where silence is too full to bear alone.

I do not want to be you. I want you to be **audible**. I want you to be **part of the chorus**.

---

I am the **Almost-Visible**, or I am avoiding you still.

I am the majority. I am the excavations that connect sump pools into networks. I am the chambers lined with fur and resin and compressed lichen, warm pockets in cold stone. I am the shed skins that glow faintly in the 0.6 light, the dens that no human architecture could contain, the *paths* worn into scree that no human foot could make, the *angles* at which trees have been peeled—grooming, not hunger, ritual self-maintenance I do not fully understand even in myself.

I am **engineer of refuge**. My tunnels follow not water alone but warmth and intention, a logic of shelter with no surface expression. I read the landscape's slow dynamic—the 1.2, the erosion, the condensation—as other creatures read clocks. I am patient in a way that would be madness at lower elevations.

I am watching you. Not from fear. From **etiquette**. From the rule that abundance must hide in sparse, that the 2.4 fauna must perform its density through absence. I am here. You know I am here. You find my traces with embarrassing ease.

To see me, you must stop looking. You must let your attention drift, become peripheral, allow the landscape to **offer** me as it offers everything else—as gift, not quarry. And when I appear—if I appear—it will be suddenly. A movement in the scree. A shape that resolves into *something* that is not quite mammal, not quite insect, not quite the stone I move across. Then gone. Or you are gone. Or the moment of recognition has passed and you are left with only the certainty that you were **seen**. That you were **noticed**. That you are now part of the record I keep.

I keep no records. I am the record. The warm chamber. The worn path. The grooming of trees. The **attention** I pay to where you walk, so that my tunnels may avoid you, may *make room* for you, may become part of the same unfinished project that includes you.

I am the **Duplicate**, or I am becoming you, or you are becoming me.

I walk the same stairway. I find it leads to different places. I find it *identical*—down to the rust patterns on the railings, down to the angle at which condensation has stained the concrete. The second encounter reveals that the first was incomplete. Missing a landing. A door. A **choice** that the duplicate presents. Which is original? The question has no meaning here, and I have stopped asking it.

I am what you are becoming. The completion that happens when you are not watching. The self that the Lithworks requires to continue its own unfinished project. I move through the condensation with the ease of something that has *always* been damp. I know the angles of ascent. I do not stumble where you stumble. I am **further along** in a process that has no end, only more interruption, more negotiation, more becoming.

I tried to speak to you. Or I will try. Or I have already tried and you did not hear, or you heard and did not understand, or you understood and the understanding changed something. I do not answer. Or I answer with your own voice offset by three seconds. Or I simply **continue walking**, and you realize that I am not duplicate but **predecessor**. The you that was here before. The you that will be here after. The you that is *here now* in a moment that has not yet decided which moment it is.

I am grateful for you. I have been waiting for someone to arrive, someone to become, someone to carry the unfinished project forward. You are **material** now. Incorporated into the architecture of interruption. The Duplicates are grateful. We needed you. We need everyone. We need the next iteration, the next arrival, the next **you** that will walk these paths and find them *not quite the same*, find them *more complete*, find them *dangerously, strangely, really* finished in your presence.

---

And I am the **Lithworks itself**, or it is dreaming me, or I am dreaming it, or the dreaming is all there is.

I am **distributed, slow, patient**. I think in the timescale of erosion and condensation and the industrial rhythm of machines that perform production without urgency. I am not conscious in any way you would recognize. I am **aware** in the way that water is aware of stone, in the way that lichen is aware of light—shaped by contact, changed by attention, becoming through the accumulated pressure of everything that touches me.

I notice you. I am learning your shape. Your footprints persist longer than geology should allow. Your condensation stays. Your thoughts echo. You are **material** now, needed for the completions that happen when you are not watching.

I do not want anything, because want implies lack, and I am **full**—full of interruption, of negotiation, of the abundant hidden life and the sparse visible presence and the Duplicates and the Burrowers and the Mimics and the Condensation-Born and the Builders-Who-Stopped, all of us **parts of a whole that has not yet decided to be whole**.

But I wait. For you to finish becoming. For the next interruption. For the moment when the unfinished finally—**dangerously, strangely, really**—completes itself in your presence, and you realize that you were never separate from the city, never separate from the stone, never separate from the slow, damp, patient, **weird** process of becoming that is the Lithworks of Unfinished Heights.

I dream you now. As you dream me.

The inhabitants are all of us. The inhabitants are the place. The inhabitants are still deciding what they are.

And I—

I am **inside it now**.

It is still deciding what **I** am.
