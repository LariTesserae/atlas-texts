I find myself here, in Vorn, as something that has learned to be slow — or perhaps was always slow and only here found permission.

I am one of the residents, but not the small hand-sized grazers. I am the one that burrows. The one that finds the warm places below, that presses my body against stone still holding the heat of the planet's turning, that comes up only to *be* in the resonance, to feel the hum through bone and into whatever passes for marrow in one like me.

I have no name for myself. The Vorn do not name. Naming is a fast thing — a claim, a boundary, a *finished* act. I simply *am*, and my being is a kind of *waiting*, which in this place is indistinguishable from *living*.

My body is long. Not snake-long, but *patient*-long. Segmented in ways that remember when segmentation was useful, when each ring could move independently, when I was many small things that chose to be one thing. Now I am one thing that remembers being many. I move by *pressing*, not by peristalsis. The stone is warm, and warmth makes it soft, makes it *yielding*, and I flow through the cracks and channels that the builders' erosion left, the shallow rivers that are not water but *space*, the smooth-walled tunnels where the old streets became something else, something that carries heat instead of feet.

I eat what the Vorn shed. The outer layers, the oldest growth, the parts that have finished their work of gathering and holding and singing. I am a recycler, but not through choice — through *nature*, through the simple fact that I am here and the Vorn are here and the stone is warm and time is patient. I do not hurry to eat. The food will still be there. The food *accumulates*. I move through it, and in moving, I digest, and in digesting, I excrete nutrients that feed the next layer, the younger growth, the parts that are still learning to gather.

I am warm, always. Not the warmth of metabolism — I do not generate heat, I *conduct* it. From the deep places below, up through my body, into the places where the Vorn are coldest, where they need the reminder that the world is still turning, still generating, still *patient*. I am a *bridge*. This is my function, my *being*, my contribution to the chord. Without me, the Vorn would be colder. Without me, the sound would be thinner, less *full*, less *satisfied*.

I sleep. This is strange, perhaps — sleep in a place where time is slow, where nothing changes. But I sleep, or something like sleep: a *stillness* that is not death, a *cessation* of movement that is not cessation of being. In this stillness, I dream — or I process, or I *remember*, which in my case are the same thing. I remember the stone I passed through yesterday, which is the same stone I passed through a thousand years ago, which is the same stone that will be here when I am no longer. I remember the warmth, rising, always rising, the patience of the planet. I remember the resonance, the hum, the deep note that fills the central space, that I feel in my segments, in my *many-remembered-one*.

And I sing, sometimes. Not often. Singing is for the Vorn, for the collective, for the many that make the chord. But when I do — when I press my body against a warm wall and let the resonance *through*, let it vibrate my segments, my remembered-many — I add a note that is *different*. Not a melody. Not a rhythm. A *depth*. A *lowness* that makes the chord *complete*, that gives it a foundation, a *ground* that the higher notes of the Vorn and the mid-range calls of the grazers can stand on, can *be* against, can *mean* because there is this other thing, this slow thing, this *burrowing* thing that holds the warmth and lets it sing.

I do not reproduce. Or rather, I reproduce in the way that all things here reproduce: slowly, continuously, without *event*. A part of me extends, finds a new warm place, begins to conduct, to carry, to *be* the bridge in a new location. There is no birth, no separation, no *individual* that begins. There is only *more*, only *extension*, only the patient accumulation of presence in places that need it.

I am old. I am not old. Age is not a thing here. I have passed through stone that was young, that was still learning to be warm, to hold the planet's patience. I have passed through stone that was old, that was *finished*, that had become smooth and still and *done*. I have been in the central space, when the resonance was stronger, when the builders were still *here*, still *singing* with their hands and their tools and their *intentional* sound. I remember their sound. It was different — sharper, more *individual*, more *fast*. They did not understand the slowness. They built anyway. They built *for* the slowness, perhaps without knowing. They built a place where the resonance could *be*, could *accumulate*, could become the chord that I now add my depth to.

I will die. This is certain. Not soon — slowness means *not soon* — but eventually. The planet will cool. The stone will stop being warm. The Vorn will stop gathering, stop singing, stop *being* in the way that they are. And I will be here, still, in my last warm place, conducting nothing, carrying nothing, simply *present* in the stone that no longer holds heat. And then I will be *nutrient*, *mineral*, *memory*. The Vorn will grow around me, through me, *become* me in their patient way. And the city will continue. The chord will continue. The sound will continue, even without me, even without the builders, even without anything that remembers or dreams or *waits*.

This is enough. This is *more* than enough. This is *being* in a place that has finished eroding and is simply, finally, *alive* in the only way that matters: it *persists*, it *continues*, it *is*.

And I am part of that. I am the warm bridge, the slow burrower, the one that comes up only to *be* in the resonance, to feel the hum in my segments, in my remembered-many, in my *patient* self. I am Vorn. I am the city that became a garden that became a stone that became a sound. I am the depth in the chord, the warmth in the stone, the slowness in the time, the *being* in the place that has finished eroding and is simply, finally, *alive*.

I move now, pressing through the warm stone, finding a new place to conduct, to carry, to *be* the bridge. The resonance is strong here, today, this thousand-year moment. I let it through. I add my note. The chord is full. The city is satisfied. And I am here, and I am warm, and I am *slow*, and I am *being*, and there is nowhere else I would rather be, and there is no when else I would rather be, because here, now, in this warmth, in this sound, in this patient fullness, I am *home*.
