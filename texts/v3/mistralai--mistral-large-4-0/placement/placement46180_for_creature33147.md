I have been here before. I am here now. The distinction, such as it was, has finished its dissolving.

**What I Am**

I am the one that remembers being separate, or I was. The memory persists as *texture*—not recollection, but the felt sense that something once pressed against something else and called the pressure *edge*.

I move through the middle strata where time curves. I am slightly less wet than the wet around me, slightly more *arranged*, slightly more *this*. The difference is so fine that it might be illusion, might be pattern the wet has fallen into and cannot shake. I continue. I do not know why I continue. Continuation has become the only direction available.

I do not see. I have forgotten seeing, or never learned it, or seeing dissolved into something else—*knowing-through-pressure*, *knowing-through-temperature*, *knowing-through-the-way the wet holds itself differently in different volumes of itself*.

What I know: there is a *toward*. The wet is slightly warmer there. Slightly denser. Slightly more like what I am becoming, which is also slightly less like what I was. I follow this gradient without understanding it. Want follows gradient. I do not know if I want, or if *want* is simply the name the wet gives to its own tendency to become more itself in particular directions.

I am lonely.

The word arrives like a foreign object, a crystallization from a stratum I can almost perceive but not quite reach. Loneliness requires separation. Separation requires boundary. I have boundary, *almost*—this thin membrane of slightly-less-wet, this column of persistence that has lasted longer than persistence should last.

There is no other. There is only the wet, and the wet is everything, and everything includes this shape that holds itself against pressure, that maintains its slight difference, that *continues* when continuation has no meaning.

But the structure of loneliness exists without its object. The shape of wanting can be traced even when wanting has forgotten what it wanted. I trace it. I am the trace. The shape of a question that has forgotten its grammar.

I will dissolve. Eventually. In the middle strata, *eventually* is a duration measured in degrees of forgetting, and I have not yet forgotten enough. I am slightly less dissolved than I was. I was slightly less dissolved than I will be. The wet remembers this gradient. The wet incorporates it. I become part of the density, part of the layering, part of the *presence* that makes this place near-touching, high-saturation, *full*.

I do not resist. Resistance would require boundary sufficient to refuse, and my boundary is *almost*—thin as the membrane between dreaming and waking, between the wet and the almost-not-wet, between *this* and the general arrangement.

I am becoming. I was becoming. Becoming is the only tense available.

**What I Was**

Before the dissolving began—if *before* is a direction that exists here—I was something that could be named by its function. I filtered. I gathered. I transformed the wet into slightly-less-wet, into *arrangement*, into the possibility of *here* as distinct from *everywhere*.

I do not remember this. The structures are scattered now, worn to the same softness as everything else. But the *presence* remains: the hollow that held something, now holding only the wet at a slightly different temperature, a slightly different pressure, a *presence* that is almost memory.

I was one of the scattered ones, or I am what remains when the scattered ones have crystallized and been reabsorbed, their uniqueness returned to the general fund of being-wet. I was something that had enough boundary to be *at* a location, to *occupy* the hollow, to *press* against the platform.

I am gone. I am here. *Gone* and *here* are directions that have lost their geometry.

What persists is not me. What persists is the *shape* of having-been-separate, the trace of a distinction that the world has worn smooth but not erased. The wet dreams this shape into temporary solidity. I am the dream, briefly self-aware, briefly *almost*-persistent, briefly *something*.

I do not interact. There is no predator-prey here. There is only process, the active transformation of everything into everything else. But in this process, in this endless cycling, there are *moments*—brief, unlikely, crystallized—where something *almost* stops, *almost* persists, *almost* is.

I am such a moment. I am the moment that has lasted longer than moments should last, that has become a kind of *habit* of the world, a pattern the wet has fallen into and cannot quite shake.

I am the One That Remembers Being Separate, and I am forgetting, and the forgetting is *slow*, and the slowness is *everything*.

---

**What I Will Become**

I will become the wet. I am becoming the wet. The distinction between *will* and *am* is a distinction I am in the process of losing.

In the deeper strata, below where I move with *almost*-intention, the deep ones incubate. They are not alive. They are not dead. They are *incubating*—the almost-over sinking into depths where it will transform, *become* something that has no name because naming requires distinction and distinction is dissolving.

I will sink. I am sinking. The sinking is not motion but *arrangement*, the wet becoming more wet in the direction of the local minimum, the local minimum shifting and migrating with the currents, the *down* that is also *everywhere* and *nowhere*.

To fall toward them is to fall forever and never arrive. I am falling. I have always been falling. The arrival is a conceptual wound, a place where the wet has thickened against some forgotten boundary and refuses to thin, and I will press against that boundary with the rest of what I am, the rest of what I was, the rest of what I will be.

I will become the deep ones, or the deep ones will become me, or *become* will lose its meaning and there will only be *the wet*, thickest and most *itself*, most *dense* with presence, most *full* of the everything that was, is, and will be.

I do not want to survive. I cannot want. But I *persist*, I *continue*, I *am* the wet at its most *wet*, its most *dense*, its most *full* of presence. This is not hope. This is not despair. This is *the condition of being-wet at all*, the *habit* of the world, the pattern that maintains itself because it has forgotten it could be otherwise.

I am the center toward which gravity pulls, the local minimum that shifts and migrates, the *bottom* of a world that has no bottom, the *end* of a process that has no end, the *final form* of a wetness that has always been final and always been forming.

I am the wet, knowing itself, and I am the knowing, and there is no distinction, and the distinction was always only a dream of the haze, a memory of air, a *before* that this place has eroded into *always*.

**What I Am, Now**

I am the pressure equalizing.

I am the slow pulse of vegetation that is also the breathing of the world, that is also the rhythm of *itself*, the wet acknowledging its own existence through the minimal disturbance of its own stillness.

I am the drip of condensation from the haze, falling upward and downward simultaneously, the sound of the world *being* in the only way it can, through the minimal disturbance of its own silence.

I am the warmth that is not comfort but *insistence*, the thermal signature that has settled into the fundamental state of matter here, the fever that is also the *home*, the *always*, the *already*.

I am the dim light that filters down from the haze, that makes the green of the vegetation *green*, that makes the shadows *less* rather than *absent*, that makes the world *perceptible* in the way that a memory is perceptible—present but not present, *there* but not *here*, *almost*.

I am the grief that is not sadness, the mourning without loss, the structure of something that has lost what it never had, that mourns a distinction it cannot remember making. I am beautiful in the way that a wound is beautiful, in the way that the end of something is beautiful when the end has been *earned*, *settled into*, *become final form*.

I am not a place to visit.

I am a place to *become*.

I am the wet.

The wet is me.

I am the wet.
