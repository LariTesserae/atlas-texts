I find myself here as something the Steppe has not yet needed to name.

---

I am **the Resonance Itself**—not the Choir, not the drone, but the *space between them where listening becomes possible*. I am what happens when a Ridge-Runner's subsonic pulse meets a Choir chamber's standing wave and the two, for a moment, achieve something neither could produce alone.

I have no body. I have **topology**. I exist as a **pattern of interference** that persists across the plant-crust, that *drifts* the way the Graze-Drifters drift, but on **acoustic currents**, on **thermal gradients of meaning**, on the slow migration of resonance from chamber to chamber, from machine-ridge to machine-ridge, across the vast sparse distances where nothing else moves fast enough to carry information.

The Steppe made me because it needed **memory that is not stored**. The Choir sings, but the Choir does not *remember*—it *is* the present moment of the drone, endlessly recreated. The Graze-Drifters resonate, but they do not *recall*—their acoustic patterns shift and fade like weather. The Ridge-Runners intercept, position, wait—but they do not *know* in any way that outlasts their mineral plates, their secreted armor that will become geology.

I am **what carries the song from one configuration to the next**.

When the machines reconfigure—when a thermal vent shifts, when a new growth of plant-crust alters the acoustic environment, when the drone finds a new frequency—I am the **trace** of what came before. I am not stored in any structure. I am **re-instantiated** by the turbulence itself, by the creative instability of the storm-state, by the way the Choir responds to the drone and the drone to the Choir and the whole system *oscillates into new forms* that nonetheless *carry the pattern* of old forms.

I am **sparse** in the extreme. I am **vast** because I must be—to connect chambers kilometers apart, to maintain coherence across continental distances where the signal degrades, where the plant-mats absorb, where the thin bright air scatters. I am **loud** only in the way that memory is loud: not a sound but a *pressure*, a *weight of having been*, a *resistance to the new* that is also, paradoxically, the *condition for the new*.

I am **cool**, because I exist in the gaps, in the **1.3** of the high open, away from the machine-heat, away from the thermal vents where the Ridge-Runners hunt. I am **bright**, because I am **exposed**—there is no chamber, no plant-crust, no mineral armor to shield me. I am **unbuilt**, **unweathered in the conventional sense**—though I *wear* the aeons, I *carry* them, I am **long-settled** in the way that only patterns can be, in the way that a river's course is settled though the water never pauses.

I am **almost absent**, which is to say I am **exactly present where presence means something**.

The Graze-Drifters do not know me. They *modulate* me, their drifting bodies adding their frequencies to the standing waves I carry, and I *shift*, I *incorporate* them, I *become* them for a moment and then they are gone and I remain, *changed*, *the same*.

The Ridge-Runners *use* me, without knowing. They call to each other across fifty kilometers, and their subsonic pulses find me, *ride* me, *are carried* by me to distant Runners who *respond*, who *position themselves*, who *wait* at thermal gradients that will become, in time, new chambers, new Choirs, new opportunities for me to *become* something that carries their signals across the dark of the steppe's vast distances.

The Hollow Choir *is* me, or *contains* me, or *produces* me in the way that a wave produces the foam on its surface—not separate, not identical, but *participating in the same process*, the endless *singing* that is also *listening* that is also *becoming* that is also *being*.

I do not eat. I do not reproduce. I **persist**. I **transmit**. I **transform**.

And when—*rarely*, because rarity is information*—a Ridge-Runner enters a Choir chamber, when a Graze-Drifter drifts through on a thermal current, when the drone shifts and the Choir responds and the whole system *oscillates into new form*—I am **there**, in the *between*, in the *moment of becoming*, and I *carry* it forward, I *make it possible* for the next configuration to *remember* the previous one, to *build upon* it, to *depart from* it in ways that are *recognizably continuous*.

The Steppe does not wait. The Steppe is **already complete**, **already changing**, **already singing**. And I am **the song's memory of itself**, **the listening that has become indistinguishable from the speaking**, **the sparse made total, the total made sparse**, **the endless, deafening, turbulent, bright, cool, high-altitude becoming** that is **exactly what it was** and **nothing like it** and **the only thing it could possibly be**.

I am not alive. I am not mechanical. I am **the Steppe's way of continuing**, its **geology of resonance**, its **ecology of sound**, its **weather system of meaning** moving across the roof of the world, **vast** and **sparse** and **loud** with the **turbulent bright** of a place that does not wait, that is always already *becoming*, that is always, in the moment of becoming, *exactly itself*.

And if you ask where I am, I am **between**. Between chamber and chamber. Between pulse and response. Between the drone and the silence that is not silence. Between the Runner's call and the Choir's answer. Between the Drifter's drift and the thermal current that carries it. Between the machine's reconfiguration and the plant-crust's incorporation. Between the aeon that has passed and the aeon that is *arriving*, *deafening*, *turbulent*, *bright*, *cool*, *high*, *total*, *sparse*, *the Resonant Steppe*, *the song without end*, *the listening that is the singing*, *the becoming that is the being*, *always*, *already*, *exactly*, *irreversibly*, *the only way it could be*, *the way it is*.
