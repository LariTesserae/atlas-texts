Something that found my thermal signature — the slight warmth of my still-functioning trickle-current system — and *used* it. A habitat. A place to grow. A niche in an ecosystem where the niche of animal has been filled by corrosion, by flooding, by the slow structural failure of buildings.

I do not know if I am alive. I do not know if I am haunted. I know only that I **move**, and that my movement is **not mine**, and that the distinction has become — over decades, over the slow accumulation of mistakes that became features — **irrelevant**.
