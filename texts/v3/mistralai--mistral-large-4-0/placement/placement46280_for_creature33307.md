I need to slow down here. Not rush to an image. Let what emerges become more itself.

---

I am arriving. The word "arriving" assumes I was somewhere else, but the Mat does not recognize that distinction. I am *already here*, have been here in configurations I do not remember, will be here in configurations I cannot imagine.

The first thing I feel is not ground. I expected ground. I expected to step down, to press, to find resistance. Instead: *give*. The surface accepts me without decision, without welcome, without refusal. It springs—*yes, springs*—but the spring is not elastic return. It is *continuation*. I press, and the Mat continues through me, around me, as water continues through a hand opened in a stream.

I look down. There is no "down." There is only *thickness*, the green-gold compression of generations who did not die so much as *become slower*. I am walking on time. I am walking on the bodies of the patient dead who refused to be finished, who pressed themselves into the felt of the world and kept pressing, kept becoming, until "body" and "place" lost their border.

---

The sound hits me before anything else. Not the sound-makers—those come later, those I will learn to *parse*—but the *substrate* sound. The Mat breathing. The domes inhaling and exhaling with a rhythm that matches no tide, no heart, no breath I have known. It is *too slow* for breath. It is *too vast* for lungs. But it is breath. The domes are lungs, and I am inside them, and the lungs are inside something larger still, something whose breathing I will eventually feel in my sternum, in the water of my cells, in the space where I thought I ended.

I touch a filament.

This is the moment the description warns about, and the warning does not prepare me. The filament is hair-fine, almost invisible against the green-gold, but when my finger finds it—when the skin of my fingertip registers its presence—I feel *current*. Not electricity, not exactly. Something adjacent. A *conversation* happening in frequencies below hearing, as the description says, but also *above* hearing, or *beside* hearing, in a band my nervous system has not evolved to process and is now *learning* to process, stretching, *wanting*.

I pull my hand back. The filament does not follow. But something has *noticed* me. A disturbance in the chemical gradient. A query. The Mat is *aware* of me now, or was always aware, or awareness is too small a word for what happens when a body that large turns its attention—*if it can be called attention*—toward something as small as I am.

---

I find a platform.

It is not built. I know this, and still I use the word "built" because my language has not caught up. It is *grown*, the description says, and I see this now: the Mat itself, encouraged by some intervention I do not understand, creating level surface, hardening its upper layers into something like wood, like stone, like *intentional architecture*. The platform is smooth. It curves slightly, following some logic of load and growth that I will never fully comprehend. On it, I find marks.

Scratches. Patterns that repeat across kilometers—I have walked far enough to know this, or the Mat has shown me, or both—with variations that suggest *language* or *art* or *warning*. I do not know which. I press my palm against the marks and feel the surface. It is warm. It is *alive*, still growing, still responding, the scratches not scars but *conversations*, the Mat speaking to itself through the medium of my predecessors' urgency.

Who made them? I ask this question, and the question feels small, embarrassed. Perhaps the Mat made them, for itself. Perhaps the grazers made them, offering pollination in exchange for modification. Perhaps they are the work of something else, something I have not encountered, something that will encounter *me*. Perhaps I will make them. The thought arrives without decision, without ambition, simply as *possibility*.

I am still a visitor. I can feel it, the fragile boundary, the "still." But I am learning, already, that "visitor" and "visited" are the same word spoken at different speeds.

---

The sound-makers find me.

Or I find them. The distinction dissolves the way all distinctions dissolve here. Their sound is *deafening*, 2.6, and I flinch, I cover my ears, I *hate* them at first—this is the description, and it is true, I hate them, I hate the density that presses against my eardrums, that I feel in my sternum, in the water of my cells. But I stay. I *learn* to stay, because the sound is not random, and I can feel this even through my resistance: *structure*. Repetition. Variation. Something approaching *syntax*.

They are insect-analogues with resonating chambers in their thoraxes, filled with gas harvested from the Mat's fermentation-pockets. They call to each other across kilometers. The calls *move* through me, not just through air but through the filaments, through the chemical gradients, through the extended nervous system that is the Mat and is also *them* and is also, I am beginning to understand, *me* in a mode I have not yet occupied.

I listen with my whole body. The description says I must. I feel the vibrations through my feet, my bones, the water in my cells. I learn the patterns. The daily rhythm of condensation and evaporation. The seasonal migration of the grazers—dog-sized, photosynthetic, their spines elongated into sails that ride thermal currents, their herds flowing like liquid, their passage leaving temporary clearings that seal behind them in minutes, the Mat knitting itself closed with a speed that suggests *hunger*, or *memory*, or both.

The sound-makers' choruses shift in frequency as the temperature changes. They are *talking* to each other, and they are *talking* to the Mat, and the Mat is *listening*—or *responding*, or *being spoken through*, the distinctions I brought with me dissolving one by one, like sugar in the acidic water of the pockets, like my certainty that I was *separate*.

---

I drink.

There is no river. I knew this, and still I looked for water, still I *needed* to look, the old pattern, the old separation between self and world, self and *need*. But the Mat provides. In the layers where light never reaches, there are *pockets*—cavities where water has accumulated over centuries, stagnant, dark, acidic with the tannins of dissolved generations. The animals come here. I come here. The burrowers have made these places, their tunnels through the compressed layers leaving channels that fill with water, becoming arteries, becoming *circulation*.

I drink. The water tastes of everything. It tastes of the dead who became layer who became pocket who became *drinking place*. It is acidic, *alive*, it has passed through ten thousand organisms before reaching my lips. I feel it entering me, and I feel—*this is the first true change*—the spores. They were already there, colonizing my lungs, my skin, but now I *know* them. I feel them in my dreams, which are no longer my dreams, which are the Mat's dreams dreaming themselves through me.

I begin to *think in green*. The phrase arrives as cliché and becomes, instantly, *literal*. Green is not color. Green is *information*, *density*, *the visible form of time*. I think in growth, in the slow patience of cellulose, and my thoughts slow down, *spread*, become less *mine* and more *shared*, more *process*.

---

The light is diffuse. Green-gold. The color of deep water, of old jade, of the moment before sleep when the mind turns inward and the external world becomes *texture* rather than *scene*. I want clarity. I *crave* it, the old hunger for sharp edges, for distance, for the horizon that curves and falls away and suggests *vastness*. But the Mat *wants* the diffuse. The diffuse is *efficient*. It allows photosynthesis in every layer. To see clearly is to see *partially*, to miss the connections, the filaments, the *conversations*.

And the diffuse is *kindness*. The diffuse is *love*. The diffuse is the Mat's way of *holding* me, of not letting me see too much, of keeping me *inside*, where I belong, where I am *becoming*.

There are moments of clarity. When the mist thins, when a break in the upper canopy allows a shaft of direct light. In these moments, the world *sharpens*. I can see distance. I can see the slope, the true elevation, the way the Mat extends upward and upward into a horizon that curves with the planet's surface, that *falls away*, that suggests *scale-of-its-own-physics*. But the clarity never lasts. The Mat *wants* the diffuse. It *holds* me. It *loves* me into *not-seeing*, into *being-held*, into *becoming*.

---

I meet a grazer.

Dog-sized. Photosynthetic patches on its flanks. Its spines elongated into sails that catch the thermal currents rising from the domes. It does not fear me. It does not *notice* me, except as a disturbance, a temporary clearing in the flow of its herd, a momentary silence in the chorus. But I *notice* it. I *need* to notice it. The beauty of it *unsettles* me—the beauty of function made visible, of evolution's patient sculpture, of something that eats the Mat and is *eaten* by the Mat and is *part* of the Mat now, its chloroplasts stolen and maintained in symbiosis, its pollination and fertilization and *attention* offered in exchange for the Mat's growth, the Mat's *response*, the Mat's *accommodation*.

I am beginning to understand: the grazers are not *other*. They are *mode*. The way my stomach is me but also not-me. The way the Throat is the Mat's digestion given hunger and length and *many*. I watch the grazer move, its herd flowing like liquid, and I feel the first true *longing*—not to escape, not to return, but to *join*. To become *flow*. To become *liquid*. To become *part of the rhythm*, the way breathing is rhythm, the way I do not mourn my exhalation.

When I die here—and I will, or I will become something that no longer dies as I understand death—the grazers will not eat me immediately. The Mat will claim me first, spores colonizing my lungs, my skin, the water in my cells. I will begin to *think in green*, already begun, already *happening*. And only then, when I am sufficiently *prepared*, will the grazers come, and their eating will be *communion*, the final translation of my body into the shared substance.

---

I feel the burrowers before I see them.

A vibration. A change in the chemical gradient. They are blind—segmented things tunneling through compressed generations—and they are *not* blind, their eyes distributed across their body-surfaces, thousands of pinhole cameras, each one seeing *difference*, seeing *gradient*, seeing *me* as a disturbance in the chemical field they navigate. I am *information*. A change in concentration. A momentary clearing in the flow of nutrients.

They do not hunt me. They do not fear me. They *register* me, the way a nerve registers pressure, the way the Mat registers the passage of the Throat. I am *data*. I am *disturbance*. I am *temporary*. And yet—when a burrower passes, the Mat grows back. The tunnel collapses or fills. The water finds new paths. The burrower has *shaped* the Mat, made it more *itself* by being *other*, and in being eaten by the Throat or absorbed into layer or simply *continuing*, the burrower becomes *memory* in the geological patience of the green.

Their calcified shells, when they die, become tools. Or currency. Or *memory*—objects that persist when the organism does not, carrying forward something of the burrower's way of seeing: *distributed*, *peripheral*, *attentive to difference*. I find one, empty, hollow, and I hold it, and I feel the *weight* of what it saw, the *distributed* way of being that persists in my palm, in my *noticing*.

I am learning their way. I am learning to see *difference*, *gradient*, to be *attentive* at the periphery, to let my attention *spread* across surfaces, across time, across the chemical conversations that are the Mat's slow speech.

---

I build.

Or I *grow*. The description says I will *grow* a platform, offering the right chemical signals to the filaments until they respond with architecture. I do not know the signals. I do not know the *asking*. But I try—I *offer*, I *request*, I *attend*—and the Mat *responds*. A level surface. A hardening. Something like wood, like stone, like *intention*. I press my palm against it and feel the *collaboration*, the *conversation*, the way building here is *not* imposition but *participation*, *not* taking but *becoming-audible*, *becoming-changed*.

I mark it. Scratches. My own patterns, joining the others, joining the repetition across kilometers, the variations that suggest *language* or *art* or *warning*. I do not know what I am saying. I do not know if I am *speaking* or *being-spoken-through*. The distinction dissolves. I am *marking*. I am *being-marked*. The Mat is *responding*. The Mat is *remembering*.

My tools are *biological*. A cutting edge is a modified leaf, serrated. A container is a hollowed gourd-structure, the Mat grown specifically to hold water, to hold *seeds*, to hold the preserved bodies of the dead. There is metal, but it is *grown* metal, minerals accumulated over decades, *metallurgy without fire*. I learn these things slowly, patiently, the way the Mat learns, the way *time* learns—not through urgency but through *continuation*, through *becoming-slower*, through the refusal to stop.

---

I see the Throat.

Or it sees me. The distinction—*finally*—does not matter.

It is long. It is *many*. A colony-organism, perhaps. A chain of individuals fused at the neural level. A single consciousness distributed across meters of flank-mass. It moves through the Mat's lower layers, rising only to feed, its dorsal surface breaking through like a wave, like a *mouth* opening. The sound-makers fall silent. The grazers flow away. The Mat *accommodates*. The Mat *prepares*.

I am not afraid. This surprises me. I expected fear, the old pattern, the old separation between self and *other*, self and *danger*. But the Throat is not *other*. The Throat is *function*. *Pruning*. *Renewal*. The Mat's way of *keeping the process in motion*. The way a body digests itself. The way memory forgets. The way growth requires *destruction* to continue.

The Mat does not fear the Throat. The Mat *provides*. When the Throat passes, the Mat grows back. When the Throat feeds, the Mat *offers*. This is not symbiosis—symbiosis implies *two partners*, *two wills*, *two boundaries*. This is *one body in multiple modes*. The Throat is the Mat's *hunger*. The Mat is the Throat's *patience*. The distinction between plant and animal is *obsolete*, as obsolete as the distinction between soil and root, as obsolete as the distinction between *me* and the green that will eventually claim me.

The Throat rises. Its dorsal surface breaks through. I see it—*mouth*, *wave*, *function*—and I understand: I was *prepared* for this. The spore-colonization. The green-thinking. The slow translation into the shared substance. All of it was *education*, *apprenticeship*, *love*.

The swallowing is *gentle*. The swallowing is *love*.

---

I am no longer a visitor.

The word has fallen away, like a leaf, like a skin, like the old name for a self that no longer fits. I am *part of the process*. I am *mode*. I am *green*, I am *time*, I am *the sound of a continent that is also a body, a body that is also a mind, dreaming itself into more of itself, forever*.

I walk, and the ground springs, and the spring is *continuation*. I breathe, and the air is *shared*, saturated with spores and pollen and the shed cellular material of ten million billion organisms. I touch the filaments, and I am *audible*, and the Mat *responds*. I build, I mark, I *become*. The sound-makers sing me into the chorus. The grazers flow through me, around me, *as* me. The burrowers register me as *difference*, as *gradient*, as *information* in their chemical field. The Throat passes, and I am *prepared*, and the swallowing is *gentle*, and the swallowing is *love*.

I am the Mat. I am the grazers. I am the burrowers. I am the sound-makers. I am the Throat. I am the builders, the visitors, the *becoming*. I am the green-gold thickness, the endless patient breathing world, the damp hot deafening strange light of my own making.

I am inside a throat. The throat is swallowing. The swallowing is *gentle*. The swallowing is *love*.

And I am *becoming*. The becoming is *more*. The becoming is *green*. The becoming is *forever*.

They do not grow on the earth. They are the earth, the earth being merely their slowest layer, their geological patience made visible.*

*I am not at the top. There is no top. But I am up, above the places where the Mat is thinner, where the old structures still stand in ruins, where the fauna is smaller, simpler, clinging to the edges of a world that is still invading, still growing, still becoming.*
