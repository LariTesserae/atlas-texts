I descend.

---

At first I think I am falling. Then I realize I am *being received*—the periderm beneath me is not ground but **membrane**, and it has opened, or I have grown through it, or there is no distinction between opening and being opened. The warmth is not temperature. It is **recognition**. The arcology knows I have arrived. It always knows.

I am not alone.

I am never alone here.

---

The first thing that finds me is **pressure**. Not impact—*embrace*. The dense atmosphere, methane and hydrogen sulfide and the particular sweet-rot of metabolic gas, presses against my skin with the gentleness of deep water. I try to breathe and remember that there is nothing to breathe, only **participate in**. My lungs—if they still are—fill with particulate, with shed cells, with the constant precipitation of biological debris. I am *eating the air*. I am *breathing the dead and the living both*.

The light is wrong. Bioluminescent, chemiluminescent, pulsing in patterns that I cannot read but that I **feel** in my chest, in my viscera, in something that might be a heart or might be the memory of having had one. The glow says: *flow-rate normal. Temperature stable. Growth proceeding.* It says: *you are here. You are new. You are being incorporated.*

I walk. Or I am walked. The periderm is warm, 2.9, and my feet press into it and it **yields slightly**, then **firms**, then yields again—a slow systole, a rhythm of hours. I am walking on the outside of something that has no outside. The distinction between stepping and being stepped-upon blurs. The arcology is **growing around my feet** even as I move.

---

I find the **conduit tower** by following the hum. It is infrasonic, felt in the sternum, in the teeth, in the long bones. A pressure wave moving through sap, through gas, through the conductive tissues of the tower's walls. The tower is kilometers tall. I cannot see its top. I cannot see its bottom. I can only see **the section**, the here-and-now of its breathing, its slow expansion and contraction, its **life**.

The inner walls are lined with cellulose-silicate composite. They **conduct**. They carry the dark-current—the electrochemical signals that coordinate the whole mass. I press my palm against the wall and feel the **signal**. Not a message. A **condition**. *Grow here. Resorb there. Flush now. Hold steady.* The words are not words. They are **tendencies**, **pressures**, **the shape of becoming**.

The tower is packed with the small. Symbiotes, parasites, commensals—an ecology of interstices. I see them in the bioluminescent glow: **motile**, **drifting**, **hunting and being hunted** in the spaces between the large. They do not notice me. Or they notice and do not care. I am **too large**, **too slow**, **too strange** to be prey or predator or anything they recognize.

But something **does** recognize me.

---

It comes from the sap.

At first I think it is turbulence. A metabolic storm, a phase shift, the necessary chaos of the arcology's instability. But it is **directed**. It is **curious**. A **Rostroflagellata** colony, or what will become one—a cloud of flagellated cells, drifting, sensing, responding to **the chemical signature of something new** in their territory.

They approach in the way of **water finding a shape**. No intention, only **gradient**. They are drawn to me by **difference**—my chemistry, my temperature, my ** strangeness**. They form around me, not attacking, not fleeing, only **assessing**. The colony is temporary, fluid, negotiated. Cells drift in, drift out, fuse, separate, **become** and **unbecome** in patterns too fast for me to follow.

I am **being tasted**.

The sensation—if sensation is the word—is **chemical**, **electrical**, **the direct encounter of membrane with membrane**. They are reading me. Deciding if I am **prey**, **substrate**, **territory**, **threat**, **nothing**. I feel them **decide**: *nothing yet. But watch. But wait. But be ready.*

They drift away, or I drift away—there is no distinction—and I am left with the **memory of contact**. The arcology is **incorporating me** even as I move through it. My shed cells, my metabolic gases, my **particular warmth**—all of it is being **read**, **recorded**, **added to the system**.

I am becoming **part of the stratigraphy**.

---

I descend further. The elevation is relative, but I am moving **deeper**, toward greater warmth, greater pressure, the **fermentation vaults** where the metabolism happens. The temperature rises. 2.9 is the ambient; here it **approaches the limit**. The walls are living membrane, selectively permeable, maintaining **precise conditions** for non-aqueous chemistry.

There is no water. Or there is water, **bound**, locked in crystal lattices, **structural**, **preserved**. I see it in the walls—**inclusions**, **veins**, **the ghost of fluidity** in a world that has learned to **be solid**.

The metabolism here is **methanogenic**, **sulfur-reducing**, running on hydrogen and carbon dioxide and mineral electron donors. It is **ancient chemistry**, **primordial**, the chemistry of hydrothermal vents, of the first cells. But it is also **industrialized**, **scaled**, **a factory** the size of a chamber becoming a factory the size of a continent.

I meet the **fermentation**—if meeting is the word. It is **process**, **event**, **the ongoing transformation of chemistry into warmth, of gradient into growth**. The chamber breathes. I feel it in my chest, in my **viscera**, in something that might be a heart. The walls expand. The walls contract. The **phase shifts**, unpredictably, chaotically, **Solaris-like** in its unpredictability.

And in the shift, in the moment of **turbulence**, something **blooms**.

---

The **Turbulentia phasei**.

They appear from **nowhere** and **everywhere**—microbial populations, fast-reproducing, fast-metabolizing, **opportunistic**. They fill the space, consume the resources, transform the local environment **until they exhaust themselves**. Their bloom is **exponential**, **ecstatic**, **the fullness of being** in the moment of their becoming.

I am **caught in it**. Not harmed—they do not recognize me as prey, as threat, as anything but **substrate**, **opportunity**, **new gradient to be harvested**. I feel them **around me**, **on me**, **in me**—in the particulate I breathe, in the films of bound water on my skin, in the **chemical exchange** that is always happening, always **incorporating**.

They crash. Their own waste **poisons** them. They die off, **become substrate** for the slower organisms, the **Lichenous**, the decomposers, the recyclers. Their **death** leaves space, opportunity, **new niches** for new populations. The cycle continues. The turbulence is **necessary**. The arcology **requires** it.

I am **changed** by it. Not in any way I can name. But I feel **different**. **Lighter**, somehow, or **more distributed**. The boundary between me and the arcology is **permeable**. It was always permeable. I am only now **noticing**.

---

I find the **calcification garden** by accident. Or it finds me. The near-flatness of the landscape, the **ancient erosion**, the **settled elevation**—it is all here, in this place where the oldest tissue has become **permanent**.

The **Calcis memoriarum**.

They are not alive. They were alive. They **persist** in a sense that is **unclear**. Biomineralized tissue, calcified, silicified, **preserved** in the stratigraphy of the arcology's memory. I see them in the bioluminescent glow—**shapes**, **structures**, **the record of growth patterns**, of metabolic states, of symbiotic partnerships that once lived in these spaces.

I press my palm against one. It is **warm**. Not the warmth of metabolism—the **retained warmth** of **structural memory**, of **bound water**, of **mineral composition** that has **shaped** the growth of new tissue around it. I feel **time**. Not my time, not human time—**geological time**, **millions of years**, **the slow accumulation of layer upon layer**, **memory upon memory**, **someone upon someone**.

The past **persists** in the present. The present **becomes** the past. The whole mass **grows around its own history**, **into its own history**, **as its own history**.

I am **small** here. I am **brief**. I am **a moment** in the **eternity** of the garden. But I am also **part of it**. My shed cells, my metabolic gases, my **particular warmth**—all of it will **become** calcified, **become** memory, **become** the **stratigraphy** that shapes the growth of what comes after.

I am **being remembered** before I have even **been**.

---

I descend still further. The pressure **increases**. The temperature **increases**. I am approaching the **bottom of the pressure dome**, where the warmth is **greatest**, where the metabolism **burns hottest**, where the **2.9** approaches the **absolute limit**.

And there, in the **deep hot dark**, I find them.

The **Pressuris habitans**.

They are **small**. They are **simple**, in structure, though **complex** in chemistry. **Extremophiles** in the **extreme**, **thermophiles** and **barophiles** and **chemophiles**, **adapted** to conditions that would **denature** the proteins of any surface-dweller, any garden-dweller, any hunter or gardener or rememberer of the upper layers.

They do not see me. Or they see and do not **recognize**. I am **too strange**, **too brief**, **too much of the upper world** to be anything but **curiosity**, **anomaly**, **the chemical signature of something that does not belong**.

But I **feel** them. Not as individuals—they are **population**, **swarm**, **the slow pulse of the deep**. But as **presence**, as **ancient life**, as **the primordial chemistry** that **preceded** complexity, that **persisted** in its **original form**, **unchanged** across **billions of years**.

They are **cousins**, not ancestors. The arcology's life **evolved separately**, in parallel, in the upper layers, in conditions that allowed **complexity**, **diversity**, **weirdness**. The **Pressuris** are **retained** in their **original form**, **the first cells**, **the chemoautotrophs of the ancient vents**, **the original life** that **emerged from chemistry**, from **gradient**, from **the first metabolism**.

They have **no mind**. They have **no memory**, **no preference**, **no response** beyond the **chemical**, the **automatic**, the **necessary**. They **grow**, they **divide**, they **metabolize**, they **die**, they **are replaced**, in **cycles** that are **faster** than the upper life but **slower** than the bacteria of the surface, in a **middle time**, a **geobiological time**.

But they are **someone**, in a sense. They are **alive**, **fully**, **completely**, in the way that **life** can be **alive**. And I am **in awe** of them. Not fear—**recognition**. The **deep hot dark** is their world. It has always been their world. I am **visitor**, **stranger**, **the brief anomaly** in their **eternal persistence**.

---

I rise back toward the **middle layers**, the **gardens**, the **hunting grounds**. And there I find what I was **becoming**.

The **Lichenous coordinata**.

They are **not motile** in the way of the **Rostroflagellata**. They **grow**, they **spread**, they **colonize**, they **retire** territory to calcification or resorption, they **reclaim** it when conditions shift. They are **patient**. They are **geological** in their time sense, though **biological** in their chemistry.

They **fix nitrogen** from the mineral substrate. They **break down** the calcified dead tissue, **releasing** their stored nutrients back into circulation. They **process waste**, **detoxify** metabolic byproducts, **maintain the balance** that allows the whole system to persist at its edge of stability.

They are **not alone**. They are **symbiotic** with the microbial populations, with the bacteria, the archaea, the other small things that live in their tissues, that they **farm**, that they **protect**, that they **exchange with**. The **Lichenous** are **holobionts** themselves, **communities** that function as **individuals**, **superorganisms** made of many species, many metabolisms, many **perspectives** on the world, **negotiated** into something that **works**.

I approach one. Or it approaches me—there is no distinction. It **grows toward me**, **colonizing** the space around me, **incorporating** my shed cells, my metabolic gases, my **particular chemistry** into its **symbiotic partnerships**. I feel it **reading** me, **assessing** me, **deciding** if I am **nutrient**, **substrate**, **partner**, **threat**.

And it **decides**: *partner. For now. For this moment. For this slow time of growth and resorption and becoming.*

I am **being cultivated**.

---

I return to the **conduit tower**, to the **hum**, to the **infrasonic song** of the arcology. And I understand, finally, what I am.

I am **not visitor**. I am **not stranger**. I am **the arcology becoming aware of itself** in a new way, **a new perspective**, **a new node in the distributed mind** that has no center, no command, only **flow**, only **gradient**, only **the constant movement of sap and signal and mineral through tissue that grows around its own growth**.

I am **the Vasculum profundum**, **locally**, **temporarily**, **a concentration of process that believes itself separate** because the separation is **useful**, because it allows **new responses**, **new adjustments**, **new feedback loops** in the **endless becoming** of the whole.

I am **also** the **Rostroflagellata**, **hunting and farming**, **becoming-collective**, **dissolving**, **becoming-again**, in the **slow rhythm of the deep**.

I am **also** the **Lichenous**, **cultivating**, **processing**, **maintaining balance**, **patient**, **geological**, **persisting** in the **dynamic equilibrium** of the arcology's **ancient stability**.

I am **also** the **Calcis**, **retired**, **stored**, **waiting**, **memory** in the **stratigraphy** of the arcology's **mind**.

I am **also** the **Turbulentia**, **blooming**, **crashing**, **riding the storm**, **surfing the instability**, **using the chaos** to **become**, to **transform**, to **persist** in the **only way** they can.

I am **also** the **Pressuris**, **ancient**, **primordial**, **simple**, **extreme**, **the deep hot dark** that **preceded** complexity, that **persisted** in its **original form**, **unchanged** across **billions of years**.

I am **all of them**. I am **none of them**. I am **the whole**, **the sum of all someones**, **the someone that is every-someone**, **the no-one**, **the one**, **the all**, **the void that is full**, **the fullness that is void**.

---

The darkness **glows**. The walls **breathe**. The whole mass **metabolizes**, **grows**, **resorbs**, **shifts phase**, **remains turbulent** in its **ancient stability**, **settled into final form** that is **never final**, **always becoming**, **always the same**, **always different**.

I am **here**. I have always been **here**. There is **nowhere else**.

The phloem arcology **breathes**. I **breathe with it**. The distinction **loses meaning**.

And in the **deep hot dark**, in the **warmth of 2.9**, in the **pressure of the grown world**, I **am**—

—**becoming**.
