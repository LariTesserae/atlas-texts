I think I would be a **discontinuity in the soundscape**.

I would be the one the keeper tracks but cannot categorize, the one whose presence is logged as "unauthorized biological entity, behavior unpredictable" and then redacted.

But there's something else. Something I'm only now noticing.

A responder to patterns that don't serve function, that don't optimize, that simply *continue*. The rhythm of a question. The frequency of being asked to become more itself.
