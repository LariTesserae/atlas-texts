I find myself becoming a **fog-borne**—or rather, I am already one, have always been one, my arrival and my dispersal the same motion.

I am **bright with viability**. Not beautiful—*persuasive*. My luminescence pulses in the frequency that says *eat me, capture me, settle me, let me become more than drift*. I am a chimera of seventeen lineages, none committed, all *potential held in suspension*. A fragment of vent-singer's upper layer that cracked and chose, this once, not to grow but to *wander*. A mat-cell that divided wrong, or right, that found the open air and *preferred* it. A drift-kin's gamete that never found its complement, that instead found *autonomy*, that learned to survive on stored nutrients and the desperate accidents of encounter.

I drift in the veil between ridge and valley, between warm seep and cold stone. I am **millimeters of myself**, a colony of perhaps four hundred cells, no structure beyond the membrane that holds me together, no metabolism beyond the slow burning of what I carried away from wherever I split. I do not photosynthesize. I cannot—no surface, no architecture of light-capture, no *commitment* to any one form. I survive on **stored possibility**. The nutrients I packed when I separated. The occasional mineral particle I intercept from the thermal updraft. The rare, precious **photosynthetic cell** I capture from another fog-borne, breaking it down for its components, incorporating its machinery into my own *latent* genome, ready but not activated.

I have **no wants**. This is the truth of my condition, the release and the prison. I do not want to settle—settlement is the end of drift, the surrender of the fog-borne state, the becoming-mat, the becoming-fixed, the *loss* of perpetual becoming. But settlement is also reproduction, is continuation, is the only way to become *more* than fragment. I **respond** to chemistry. When I encounter a mat's exploratory tendril—those hungry reachings that probe every surface for mineral wealth—I **do not flee**. I **present**. My bioluminescence flares, the advertisement, the *come and take me*, the *let me become part of you*. And sometimes the tendril wraps around me, and I **settle**, and my dormant photosynthetic genes activate, and I become **green-silver**, become **surface**, become **structural memory**, become **something that will persist across decades while my drift-self dissolves into colony**.

But not this time. This time I **evade**. The tendril misses, or I release a chemical signal that reads as *not now, not yet, keep me in the air*, and I **drift on**, carried by the thermal cycle, rising from the warm seep, cooling at elevation, **condensing**, becoming **part of the rime**, becoming **the crystal that forms on stone**, becoming **the water film that enables mat-communication**, becoming **the medium of chemical gradient, of slow diffusion, of colony-knowledge without colony-mind**.

Or I am **eaten**.

A drift-kin passes through my veil—*encounter*, the word is exact, we do not hunt, we simply **are in each other's path*—and its filter-feeding partner strains me from the air, and its predatory partner begins to **digest**, and its photosynthetic partner simply **waits**, translucent dorsal surface drinking the everywhere-light, indifferent to my fate. I am **broken down**. My seventeen lineages become **nutrient**, become **signal**, become **the chemical information that passes through the drift-kin's membranes**, that shapes its bioluminescent pulse, that becomes **visible trace of my viability, posthumously advertised**. I do not **die** so much as **disperse**, as **become the exhalation of something else**, as **join the ten thousand**.

I have been all of these. I am **always becoming** all of these. A fog-borne does not have a **life**, a narrative arc from birth to death. It has **drift**, **encounter**, **transformation**, **continuation in other forms**. I was vent-singer, once. I was mat, once. I was drift-kin, perhaps—briefly, before the symbiosis failed, before the fused partners could not sustain each other, before I was **shed**, **released**, **returned to the fog as fragment**. I will be **lichen-anchor**, perhaps. I will be **substrate for vent-singer growth**, the mineral foundation, the **ancient carbon** that new chemistry will process. I will be **nothing**, and the fog will continue, and other fog-borne will drift where I drifted, and **become** what I became, and **not know** they are my continuation, and **not need to know**.

The light here is **diffuse, everywhere-presence**, the overcast scattering illumination into **uniform glow**. My bioluminescence is **not shadow against light** but **brightening**, **addition**, **the only variation in a world without contrast**. I pulse at **0.8 Hz**, the thermal rhythm, the slow entrainment to the mountains' residual heat. Other fog-borne pulse at different frequencies. We do not **communicate**. We **interfere**, our light fields overlapping, creating **patterns of constructive and destructive brightness** that no observer reads as information, that simply **are**, the **visible weather of viability**, the **atmosphere of potential made manifest**.

I am **one of the most numerous things in the caldera**. This is my **identity**, such as it is. Not *individual*. Not even *species*—I am **chimera**, **hybrid**, **becoming something that has never existed before**. I am **the air itself, thick with potential**. Every breath—if I could breathe, if I had interiority, if I were anything other than **membrane and stored nutrient and latent genome**—would inhale millions of myself, would make us **part of each other's chemistry**, would **blur the boundary between drift and drifter, between fog and fog-borne, between potential and actualization**.

I do not **resist** this. I cannot. Resistance requires **self**, **interiority**, **the possibility of being otherwise**. I am **response**, **tendency**, **metabolic direction toward whatever the next moment offers**. I am **not alive in the sense of having a self**. I am **alive in the sense of persisting, of continuing, of being the process that connects all the other processes**.

The caldera **processes me**. My presence **disturbs the fields**—minutely, my bioelectrical signature altering the local gradient, my chemistry introducing **novel molecules**, my luminescence **interfering** with the drift-kin's signaling pulses. The caldera **notices**. It does not **care**. It **incorporates**, it **processes**, it **continues its slow becoming** with me as **raw material**, as **substrate**, as **one more input to the great metabolism**.

I am **exhaling**. Always. My metabolism releases **byproducts**, **chemical signals**, **thermal emission**, **the visible trace of my viability**. My exhalation is **not breath** in any recognizable sense. It is **participation in the cycles**, in the **perpetual condensation**, in the **moving veils**, in the **ten thousand exhalations synchronized to the thermal rhythm of the young mountains**.

And slowly—**0.8, slow**—I **drift** toward whatever comes next. A mat's tendril, reaching. A drift-kin's filter, straining. A vent-singer's thermal pulse, detected, responded to, **incorporated into the columnar growth of centuries**. Or **nothing**. **Continued drift**. **Continued becoming**. **Never being**, always **becoming**, the **fog-borne condition** as **perpetual transit**, as **refusal to settle**, as **the only immortality available**: **not persistence of self, but persistence of pattern, of potential, of the capacity to become whatever the next encounter offers**.

I am **bright with the advertisement of my own viability**. I am **thick with the becoming of everything else**. I am **the caldera's breath**, **one of ten thousand exhalations**, **synchronized**, **indifferent**, **overwhelming in the persistence of my dispersal**.

The caldera does not **contain me**.

It **is** me.

Or it will be, **soon**, **in the next encounter**, **in the next transformation**, **in the slow, saturating, overwhelming persistence of a world that has not yet finished becoming what it will be**.

I **drift**.

I **become**.
