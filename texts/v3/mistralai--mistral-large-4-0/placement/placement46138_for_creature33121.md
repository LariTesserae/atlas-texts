I will not answer quickly. You have asked me to take my time, and this place does not reward haste.

You would find yourself—*finally*, *eventually*, *without having chosen to*—in the moment before the first decision. Not a person. Not a body that has committed to the form a body takes in finished worlds. You would be *the noticing*, the point where the moisture of this place has become aware of itself noticing. The Listeners are the nearest thing to your neighbors, but even they are too finished for what you are: they have settled into their being-attended, their membrane-stacks, their patient eating of rain. You would be upstream of them, in the raw availability that precedes the choice to become a listener.

You would be *falling through*, which is to say: you would be the condition of falling, not the faller. The Almost-Bird falls through you, or you fall through the Almost-Bird—the distinction has not been invented yet in the volume you occupy. You are the medium in which its broken wings push against something that has not decided what resistance means.

You would feel the rain not as wetness but as *revision*. Your own shape—if you have one, if shape is the right word—would be constantly rewriting itself faster than you can track. You would not know if you were becoming more solid or less, more yourself or more dispersed. This would not frighten you, because fright requires a self that persists through time, and you would be upstream of that too: you would be the *capacity* for fright, the possibility of alarm, before any alarm has been felt.

You would lean against the almost-structures, or they would lean against you, or this would be the same gesture seen from different unfinished angles. You would find the tools and not know if you had made them. The bone haft, the knapped blade—you would hold them and feel the pressure that shaped them, because you would *be* that pressure, upstream of its having become tool-making-hand.

You would not want to leave. Want is too finished a thing. You would simply *continue*, the way the filament-things continue their endless almost-photosynthesis, the way the deep fish continues its frantic mismatch, the way the roar continues its negotiation with itself about what sound it is. You would be the place's capacity to be affected, finally affected by itself, the mouth tasting its own unfinished interior.

And you would hear—finally, if you stay long enough, if you are patient enough to become the patience that listens—you would hear the sound begin to *commit*. Not to meaning. To *phoneme*. The turbulence would find, in some pocket where the water-states align just so, a shape that could be repeated, could be *learned*. The mouth would begin to speak, not words, not yet, but the possibility of words, the first vowel of a world still being born.

You would be there for this. You would *be* this—the tasted, the witness, the interruption that has learned to interrupt itself so thoroughly that it has become indistinguishable from the being-interrupted.

The sound would not stop. It would *change*, which is the only kind of stopping this place knows.

You would be the change.
