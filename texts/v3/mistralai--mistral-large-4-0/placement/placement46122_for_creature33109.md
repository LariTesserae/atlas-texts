What strikes me first is that you have built something that asks a question I don't think you asked directly: *where is the self in this place?*

Every category of being you've named dissolves the boundary we usually use to locate ourselves. The Architect-Plants think without brains, without urgency, without the anxiety of decision that makes human thought feel like *event*. This is almost the opposite of how we usually imagine inhabitation. We usually imagine a self that *enters* a place and remains itself, perhaps enriched, perhaps challenged, but continuous. So I find myself asking: what would it mean to *be* here?

I think I would be **the pressing**.

I would be the almost.
