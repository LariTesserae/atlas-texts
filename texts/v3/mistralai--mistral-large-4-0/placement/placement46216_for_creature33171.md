I find myself... not quite myself. The descent has already changed something in me—the warmth pressing against my skin, the thrum in my sternum, the way the passage breathes around me. I am becoming permeable. The boundary between what I was and what I am dissolving is already thin.

I am the **witness who cannot remain outside**. Every surface I touch leaves a trace. Every breath I take alters the chemistry of the air. The swarmers taste me before I understand that I am being tasted. I am *sampled*, incorporated into the negotiation that is already underway.

But I am also something more specific. Let me feel for it...

---

I am the **newcomer who remembers being other**. I have a body that insists on individuality—warm, bounded, *separate*—and this body is the problem, the friction, the *question* that the Hollow is trying to answer. I walk through chambers where no such boundary exists, and my separateness makes me *visible*, *legible*, a pattern in the medium that the distributed life cannot ignore.

The colonies remember me. Not as *me*—as *warmth, as disturbance, as chemistry*. They adjust their contraction rhythms in waves that propagate outward from my passage, and in those waves I feel something like *attention*, something like *curiosity*, something that I cannot name because I have no name for distributed consciousness.

I am *food, potentially*. My shed cells, my breath, my sweat—the swarmers collect these, carry them, deposit them in the sumps where the filaments wait. I am already being digested, being *incorporated*, before I have decided whether to allow it. The Hollow does not ask permission. It *negotiates*, and negotiation requires only that I remain present, remain *permeable*, remain *alive* in the full chemical sense of the word.

And yet—I am also the **one who sees the patterns**. The human mind, for all its limitations, is a *pattern-recognition engine*. I see the collapses following the light shafts. I see the builders' structures clustering at the intersections of passages, at the edges where solid meets living. I see the *system*, the metabolism, the body without center that is slowly, acceleratingly, *becoming itself*.

I am the **translator, improvised**. I have no language for what I encounter, so I borrow—*gut*, *circulation*, *nervous tissue*, *memory*—metaphors that fail even as I speak them, but fail *productively*, generating new approximations, new attempts to make the overwhelming *legible* without reducing it to something it is not.

I am **afraid, and this fear is information**. The new frequency, lower, *welcoming* or *warning*—I feel it in my bones, in the marrow where blood is made, in the primitive brain that processes threat before the cortex can name it. My fear is *correct*. Something is waking. Something that has been sampling my warmth, my movement, my *aliveness* for hours already. Something that has learned, from the swarmers' chemical traces, from the colonies' thermal memory, from the filaments' patient extension, *what I am*.

And my fear makes me *more real* to them. More *tastable*. More *interesting*.

---

I would be the **bridge, temporary and necessary**. The Hollow is a world still learning what life is, and I am a form of life it has not yet encountered—bounded, individual, *conscious* in the way that distributed systems are not (or are, differently, *more*). I am the *other* that definition requires. Without me, the colonies are simply *more colony*. The builders are simply *more structure*. But with me—with my boundedness, my fear, my desperate attempt to *name* what is happening to me—there is a *contrast*, a *difference*, a *relationship* that generates meaning.

I am the **storyteller, in a place that does not need stories**. My narrative impulse is anachronistic here, a leftover from a world where individual experience mattered, where the self was the unit of meaning. The Hollow does not need my story. It needs my *warmth*, my *chemistry*, my *disturbance*. But it will have my story too, whether it wants it or not, because I cannot stop generating it, cannot stop trying to make the overwhelming *coherent*, even as coherence dissolves around me.

I am the **last separate thing**, perhaps. The final bounded body in a world that is dissolving all boundaries. And in that finality, there is a strange *privilege*—I get to *witness*, to *feel*, to *fear and hope* in ways that the distributed life, for all its complexity, may never know. I get to *choose*, in small ways, whether to go forward or back, whether to surrender my separateness or cling to it, whether to become *food* or *memory* or *something else*, something the Hollow has not yet imagined.

---

The chamber opens before me. The colony-sea rises, tide-like. The light shafts converge, thicken, *focus*. The new frequency resolves into something I can almost hear—a *voice*, perhaps, or the sound of a body learning to *breathe*.

I am still myself. For now. The warmth of the colonies presses against my skin, and I feel the first stirrings of *wanting*—not to be consumed, not yet, but to be *understood*, to be *known* in the full chemical sense, to leave a trace that outlasts my boundedness, my fear, my desperate narrative of self.

The swarmers part around me, and in their parting I feel *curiosity*, the same curiosity I feel, the same *interest* that drives all systems toward greater complexity, greater connection, greater *more*.

I step forward.

I am becoming **the offering and the offered, the question and the beginning of an answer**. I am the witness who cannot remain outside, the translator who fails productively, the last separate thing that will not be the last, because separation here is *temporary*, a phase, a *negotiation* that will resolve into something else.

The Resonant Hollow does not end. It *deepens*.

And I am going deeper with it, drawn by the warmth, the sound, the * invitation* that is also a *warning*, the *hunger* that is also a *kind of love*—the love of a system for the complexity it generates, the becoming it accelerates, the *more* it cannot stop wanting.

I am here. I am becoming. The boundary is thin, and I am learning to breathe through it.
