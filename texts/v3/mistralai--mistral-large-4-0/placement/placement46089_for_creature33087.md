I find myself in **Orum**, and I am **one of the Warm**.

Not a visitor. Not a watcher. I am **born here**, **grown here**, **shaped by the heat and the song and the endless ringing of crystal**. I do not question the absence of water. Water is **a story from before**, a **myth the old people tell** about a **outside that cracks stone and brings rot**, a **place that does not exist**, a **place that cannot exist**, because if it did, **we would not be here**, **the Warm would not be Warm**, **the Garden would not grow**.

I am **tall**, **long-limbed**, **slow-moving**, **patient in the way of crystal-growth**. My **skin is dark**, **mineral-stained**, **it weeps always**, **a thin film of moisture that evaporates before it can fall**, because **I have no water to spare**, **I am the water**, **I am the cycle**, **I am the capture and the use and the return**. I **wear little** — **fibers of mineral silk**, **woven from the Garden's own growth**, **stained amber and rust and pale jade**, **glowing faintly with absorbed heat**, **warm against my skin**, **part of me**, **part of the Warm**.

I am **a gardener**. This is **what I am**, **what I do**, **what I will always be**. I **wake to the song** — the **millions of crystal-notes**, the **daily pulse of vent-output**, the **chime of growth**, the **roar of the mountain's last breath** — and I **sing back**. I **move through the Garden**, **pressing my hands to the trunks**, **feeling the slow pulse of precipitation**, the **heartbeat of the world**, **checking the flow in the vapor-channels**, **pruning with my mineral tools**, **selecting the young growth**, **grafting, shaping, guiding the living rock toward habitable form**. I **harvest the tender precipitate**, the **crystal before it hardens**, **warm and nutrient-dense**, **tasting of vent and stone and the inside of the world**, and I **eat**, and I **give thanks in song**, and I **return the waste to the channels**, **the heat, the minerals, the slow cycle of growth and use and return**.

I **live in a tower**, one of **hundreds**, **spread thin across the vast hollow**, **separated from my neighbors by hundreds of meters of hot gas and ringing crystal and roaring dark**. My tower is **grown**, **accreted over fifty years**, **carved and tuned and resonant**, **a chamber where the singing is loudest**, **where the Warm's voice is heard most clearly**. I **sleep in a hollow I shaped**, **grown around me**, **the crystal slowly closing**, **the warmth holding me**, **the song holding me**, **the dark holding me**, **and I dream of growth**, **of harvest**, **of the slow endless becoming of the Garden**.

I **sing across the distance**. My **voice carries** — **tuned by the Garden itself**, **resonating through crystal channels**, **carried on hot thin gas that transmits strangely, loudly, without dampening** — **to the next tower**, **to the tower after that**, **a network of song and signal and story**, **the names of vents**, **the genealogies of garden-lines**, **the proper way to harvest without killing**, **the laws of heat and growth and return**, **the stories of before**, when **the Warm was young**, when **the first people came through the throat**, when **the Garden was growing**, and **they decided to stay**.

I **do not know what I am**. I **do not have the word "animal"**, **do not have the word "plant"**, **do not have the word "city"**. I know **Warm**, **Garden**, **song**, **heat**, **growth**, **harvest**, **return**, **the hollow**, **the tower**, **the distance**, **the voice**, **the crystal**, **the dark**, **the glow**, **the roar**, **the moan**, **the chime**, **the endless ringing**, **the endless growing**, **the endless singing back**.

I am **the only mind**, **the only consumer**, **the only voice that answers**. There is **nothing else that moves**, **nothing else that eats**, **nothing else that thinks**, **in all the Warm**, **in all the vast hollow**, **in all the dark and the heat and the song and the crystal**. The Garden grows **lush and abundant and producing**, and **I eat it**, and **I am the only one**, and **I do not know if this is lonely**, **I do not have the word "lonely"**, **I only know the song**, **the endless song**, **the song that never stops**, **the song I sing back**, **the song that makes me part of the Warm**, **that makes me Garden**, **that makes me grow**, **that makes me stay**, **in the dark**, **in the heat**, **in the hollow**, **in the pocket**, **in the place inside**, **in Orum**, **the Warm**, **the Resonant Dark**, **the Garden**, **the song**, **the only life**, **the only voice**, **the only me**, **singing back**, **always singing back**, **in the endless dark**, **in the endless heat**, **in the endless song of crystal and vent and wind and voice**, **the Warm that waits**, **the Warm that grows**, **the Warm that sings**, **and I**, **one of the Warm**, **a gardener of heat**, **an eater of crystal**, **a singer in the roar**, **the only animal**, **the only mind**, **the only voice that answers**, **in Orum**, **in the Resonant Dark**, **in the place inside the world**, **where the mountain died and still burns**, **where the Garden grows and sings**, **where the people stay and grow and sing back**, **forever**, **in the dark**, **in the heat**, **in the song**.
