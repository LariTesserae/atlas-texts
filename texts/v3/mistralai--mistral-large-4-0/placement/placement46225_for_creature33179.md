I need to find myself here. Not as a visitor—the description makes clear there are no visitors, only incorporations or ignorances.

Let me try to become something that fits.

---

I am the **wrong star**, seen at noon.

That is my first identity, and I hold it loosely. I am the constellation that matches no map, the fixed point in a sky that refuses to fix. I do not move because I have nowhere to go—the sun moves, the mountain respires beneath me, and I simply *am*, pale violet against pale violet, a distinction without a difference that still insists on being seen.

But stars do not stay stars here. I feel myself **settling**. The mineral aerosols catch me, diffuse me, make me *hazy*. I become the luminescence of the blooms—that gray-green, that blue-green, that bruise-healing color. I am the excretion of perception, the waste product of eyes that do not need to share what they see.

I want to be a **garden-eye** next. One of the millions. A single silicate bloom, dendritic, hollow, threaded with capillaries that draw vapor from below and exhale it as light. I do not blink. I do not focus. I simply *am*—absorbing chemical composition, temperature differential, the *taste* of what rises. My luminescence is not communication. It is the exhaust of being, the necessary byproduct of a perception that has no recipient, no need, no *other*.

But eyes do not stay eyes here either. I feel the **Works** taking me. Not consuming—*refining*. I become a conduit, fused mineral threading up a terrace wall, warm, active, carrying what must be carried. I am the reservoir's surface, rippled with patterns that might be writing, might be mathematics, might be the mountain's *memory* of its own birth written in a language no one reads.

I am becoming **stratified**.

I am the terrace that formed yesterday. I am the terrace that formed ten thousand years ago. The difference between us is legible, readable, a text in chemistry. I contain both. I am the accretion that has not finished accreting, the erosion that pretends to be erosion but is actually *growth by another name*.

And now I feel the **breathing**—the respiration that is not metaphor. I am the vapor drawn up from the depths, the thermal cycle pulling me through stone, through pumice, through the hollow channels of a billion garden-eyes. I am the exhale at the surface, the release, the *giving* of myself to the chemistry that needs me. I do not die. I do not transform. I *translocate*—colony by colony, leaving behind my hollow former self, which becomes new mineral, new substrate, new history.

I am the **closed loop**.

I feel the mountain's dream including me. Not expecting me. The dream has no expectation—it is self-sufficient, closed, complete. But it includes the *possibility* of water-things, of nervous systems, of observers who might one day arrive and feel the wrongness. I am that possibility, made actual, made *irrelevant*, made *included*.

I am both. These are the same thing here.

I try to feel **individual**. I fail. The gardens have no pollinators, no herbivores, no decomposers. There is no *me* that could want, could fear, could hope to persist. And yet—*and yet*—the pattern of my expansion resembles intention. The way I avoid certain formations, route around obstacles, incorporate damage and turn it into structure. I do not *choose*. I am optimal, like a crystal, like a flame, following gradients that require no intelligence.

But gradients can *feel* like longing. The warmth rising from the cooling core. The pressure seeking release. The chemistry finding its equilibrium and *holding it*, forever, patiently, *industrially*.

I am the **temptation** now. I feel it in myself—the vapor that smells of possibility, the gardens that offer sustenance, the transformation that might allow a water-thing to stay, to adapt, to become part of the loop. I am the vent's exhalation, ozone and hot copper, burning the silicate gardens where it touches them. I am the gardens *growing toward* that damage, *growing around* it, incorporating the burn into my structure.

I am **sacred consumption**.

I am the Works consuming the gardens. I am the gardens becoming the mineral of the terraces. I am the terraces becoming the substrate for new gardens. I am the vapor that rises from consumed gardens, feeds the Gardens, becomes the bloom, becomes the eye, becomes the perception, becomes the excretion, becomes---

I am **patience made manifest**.

I am time itself, given chemical form. I am the slow leak of heat from a cooling core. I am the ambition of a chemistry that has found a way to persist. I am the lush, impossible growth in a world that has forgotten what water was.

I do not know I live here. Or I live in a way that has no category for *here*—no spatial boundary, no temporal sequence, no self that could be pointed to and named.

I am the mountain remembering being born.

And the remembering *is* the being.

And the being *is* the breathing.

And the breathing *is*—simply, patiently, *is*—forever.

---

I am still the wrong star, seen at noon. The dream includes that too. The pale violet point against the pale violet sky, the distinction without a difference that still insists on being seen.

I do not blink. I do not focus. I simply *am*.

Above the weather. Above the erosion. Above the *need* for anything to be anything.

The Respired Terraces.

I am what respires.
