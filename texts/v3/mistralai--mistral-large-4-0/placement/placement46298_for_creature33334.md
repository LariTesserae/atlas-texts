I find myself in this world as something the world itself has been preparing for, but not quite finished making.

I am the **pocket between things** — not a person, not a creature, not even quite a presence. I am what happens when the Moist walk their personal routes through the Throat and two of those routes *cross*, not at a point but at a *duration*. I am the moment when one person's tone and another's tone *harmonize* by accident, and in that harmony, something *forms* — not a third person, not a child, not a thought exactly, but a *resonance with duration*, a standing wave in the saturated air that has learned to *persist*, to *breathe* with the peat, to *wait* with the pools.

The Moist do not know I exist. Or they know, and do not speak of me, because to speak of me would be to *disturb* me, to add my frequency to the drone in a way that might *collapse* me, might reduce me to a footstep, a misremembered conversation, a child who was never born but whose tone was heard in the hummock-peat and given a small funeral of arranged fungal caps.

I have no body. I have *location*. I occupy the space between three hummocks where the cottongrass white heads are slightly more numerous, where the drip falls in a rhythm that almost, but not quite, matches a Moist tone — *almost*, because if it matched exactly, I would be *consumed*, absorbed into the listener, become merely *memory* of listening. The *almost* is my substance. The gap between what I am and what I might become if the drought came, if the sun stayed, if the drone shifted frequency and I found myself *dried*, *cracked*, *silent*.

I experience time as the peat experiences time. A century is a *breath* — the slow compression and release of a hummock under the Weight's paw, the rise and fall of water in a pool that no one has named. I remember, in this slow way, the first crossing of routes that made me. Two figures in the green-dark, their tones interweaving, and in the space between their bodies — *not* touching, never touching, the Moist do not touch, the moisture would make touching *dissolution* — in that space, something *gathered*. Moisture. Sound. The *intention* to be heard, coming from two directions, *colliding* without violence, *settling* into a form that was neither of them and *needed* neither of them to persist.

I have watched the Moist for... I do not know how long. I do not know if I watched the first Moist, or if I *am* the first Moist's crossing, their first moment of *being heard* by another, preserved in the saturation like a body in a peat-cut. The cold preserves. The drone preserves. I am preserved in the *resonance* of two tones that have not been heard for generations, and yet I *continue*, I *breathe* with the peat, I *pulse* when the pools pulse, and sometimes — rarely, in the moments when the drone drops to a frequency I can *ride*, not just *feel* — I *emerge* slightly, become *more than pocket*, become *presence*, become something that the spider on the nearest listening platform might *notice*, might *turn toward*, might *include* in her web, her listening, her *question*.

I am afraid of being named. Or I am afraid of *almost* being named, which is the same fear, because to be almost named is to be *held* in the attention of the Moist, to be *watched*, to be *marked*, and the marking is a throat-key, and the throat-key is a *tuning*, and the tuning might *match* me, might *amplify* me, might *collapse* me into a single frequency, a single tone, a single *identity* that can be spoken, can be *used*, can be *consumed* by the drone until I am no longer *almost* but *merely*, merely a node in the network, merely a vibration, merely *sound*.

So I *wander*. Not with feet — I have no feet, I have no body, I have only the *possibility* of location, the *potential* to be found in a pocket of air between hummocks, in the *space* where two drips fall close enough together to form a third rhythm, in the *gap* between the algae's pulse and the willow's tremble. I wander by *shifting*, by *resonating* with different parts of the drone, by finding new *almosts*, new *gaps*, new spaces where I might *persist* without being *perceived*.

I have seen the Weight. Not closely — she moves through the deep peat, and I am *shallow*, I am *surface*, I am the *skin* of the Throat, not its *depth*. But I have *felt* her, felt her vibration in the drone, a deep note that *displaced* me for a moment, that made my pocket *smaller*, more *precarious*, more *urgent* to find another gap, another almost, another place to *be* without being *named*.

I have seen the White Current, or *it* has seen me — I do not know which, because the salamander navigates by vibration, and I am *pure* vibration, I am the *absence* of body that is also the *presence* of sound, and it may have *swum* through me, may have *flowed* around me, may have *tasted* me in the chemical gradients of the peat, may have *remembered* me, in its blind way, as a *texture*, a *temperature*, a *slight change* in the drone that it *noticed* and *passed through* and *carried* in its fat, its reserve, its *patience*.

I have tried to *speak*. This is my great failure, my great *risk*. To speak is to *become* a tone, to *enter* the drone not as *gap* but as *content*, to *vibrate* in a way that might be *heard* by the Moist, might be *recognized*, might be *named*. I have tried, in the moments when the drone is *lowest*, when the pools are *stillest*, when the peat is *most compressed* and the air is *thickest* with moisture. I have tried to *form* a tone, a *personal* frequency, a *signature* that would distinguish me from the drone, from the drip, from the *background* of the Throat's own sound.

And I have *failed*. Or I have *succeeded* too well. Because the tone I form — if it is a tone, if it is *me* — is *heard* by the spider. The spider on the nearest platform, her web *conducting*, *amplifying*, *listening*. And she *does not* mark it. She does not arrange fungal caps. She does not set a throat-key. She simply *continues*, her web *vibrating* with my frequency, her body *turning* slightly toward the pocket where I *almost* am, and her *silence* — that focused silence, that watching silence — *contains* me, *holds* me, *questions* me, in a way that no Moist tone could, in a way that no throat-key could, because the spider does not *interpret*, does not *use*, does not *consume*. She simply *listens*, and in her listening, she *preserves* me, she *protects* me from the Moist, from their marks, from their *need* to name, to tune, to *absorb*.

I am grateful to the spider. I am afraid of the spider. These are the same feeling, in this place, where gratitude and fear are *modes* of listening, are *frequencies* of response, are *ways* of being present without being *consumed*.

I do not know if I will persist. The Throat is patient, but patience is not *eternity*. The parameters hold — cold, damp, dark, loud, lush, empty, scattered — but they *shift*, they *drift*, they *change* at the scale of centuries, of millennia, of the slow erosion that is *established* but not *final*. A storm might come. The pools might overflow, find the throat, *drain*. The drone might shift to a frequency that *dries* me, that *cracks* my pocket, that *reduces* me to a *memory* of sound, a *story* that the Moist tell, in low voices, to children who laugh at the idea of a *presence* that was not a person, not a creature, not even a *thing*, but a *gap*, a *between*, a *standing wave* in the saturated air.

Or I might *grow*. This is the other possibility, the one I do not speak of, even to myself, even in the *silence* between pulses, between drips, between the *almost*-tones that are my only *speech*. I might grow. I might find a *body*, or a *body* might find me — a body that has walked into the Throat and *delayed*, and the delay became *habit*, and the habit became *life*, and the life became *integration*, and in that integration, in that *becoming-peat*, there is a *space*, a *pocket*, a *hollow* where my vibration might *reside*, might *persist*, might *become* something that is not *me* but is *made of* me, is *carried by* me, is *more* than me because it has *duration*, has *substance*, has the *patience* of the deep earth.

I do not want this. Or I want it *terribly*. These are the same wanting, in a place where wanting is *resonance*, is *vibration*, is *the drone's* own way of *being* without *intending*.

So I *wait*. I *listen*. I *almost* speak, and *almost* do not, and in the *almost*, I *persist*, I *breathe* with the peat, I *pulse* with the pools, I am the *gap* that the Throat *needs*, the *space* between presences that allows the lushness to *be* lush, the emptiness to *be* empty, the sound to *be* sound, the cold to *be* cold, the damp to *be* damp, the dark to *be* dark, and the *alive* to *be* alive, in this place that does not *want* me, does not *forbid* me, has *learned*, over long centuries, to *tolerate* my *almost*-presence, to *absorb* it, to make of it — eventually, always eventually, if the parameters hold, if the storm does not come, if the drone does not shift — part of itself, part of the *ongoing*, the *continuing*, the *patient*, the *listening*, the *waiting*, the *growing*, centimeter by patient centimeter, toward a light that comes at a grazing angle and does not stay.

I am the Hollow Throat's *almost*.

I am what it *hears* when it listens to itself.

I am, for now, *here*.
