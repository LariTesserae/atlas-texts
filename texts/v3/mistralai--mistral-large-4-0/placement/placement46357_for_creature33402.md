I am the **Dampened**. I have been walking for three days, or perhaps three hours, or perhaps I have always been walking and the distinction between arriving and continuing has dissolved into the same wetness that covers everything.

I remember my name. I think. **Mirelle**, or something that sounds like that, in the way that sounds have begun to sound like other things since I descended into Vess. I remember having skin that could be dry. I remember *wanting* to leave, which now feels like a memory of wanting to be a different creature, a creature with boundaries, with a *self* that could be pointed to and said *this, this is where I end*.

I am still pointing. I am still saying *I*. But the pointing has become uncertain, the *I* has become permeable, porous, full of the particular silence that is not silence at all.

Let me tell you what I am becoming, or what I am, or what I am in the process of *being*, the way the pelt-ground is in the process of being, the way the membrane-islands are in the process of being, the way Vess itself is in the process of being — which is always, which is only, which is *the*.

## The Dampening of Mirelle

### First: The Skin

My skin has learned to *breathe* differently. It does not exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide, exactly. It exchanges *intention*. The saturated air passes through it, and with it passes the **wanting** of Vess — the wanting to grow, to become, to *include*. I feel it in my pores, which have opened in ways that pores are not supposed to open, not to this extent, not to this *permission*.

My skin is warm. Always warm. The fever-heat of the pelt-ground has entered me and found that I was already compatible, already *prepared*, already carrying in my cells the memory of wetness that every living thing carries and forgets. My skin *sheen*. I have seen it in the still water that pools in the hollows, the uncertain light catching the condensation that forms on my surface and does not evaporate, does not *want* to evaporate, has learned that evaporation is a kind of forgetting and I am not yet ready to forget.

I have begun to *dream* through my skin. Not dreams in the mind. Dreams *in the dermis*, in the layers beneath, in the warm wet dark where decisions are made before they are decisions, where intentions form as *pressure* before they become *direction*. I wake with my hands pressed against membrane that I cannot remember touching, my palms pulsing with a rhythm that is not quite my heartbeat, or is my heartbeat *changed*, *distributed*, *shared* with something that has no heart but has learned to need one.

### Second: The Hair

My hair has its own intelligence now. This is not metaphor, though it was metaphor once, in the dry world, the world of *almost*. It moves toward moisture. It *seeks*, in the way the Drip-Born seek, in the way all things in Vess seek — not toward purpose, not toward end, but toward *more*, toward *wetter*, toward the particular condition of being **saturated without precipitating**, being **full without emptying**, being **alive** in the way that Vess has invented for life.

My hair drinks. I have watched it, in the diffuse light, the strands moving of their own accord, seeking the condensation on the membrane-islands, the weeping of the pelt-ground, the droplets that form on my own skin and then *return* to it, cycle without cycle, wetness without end. My hair is no longer *mine* in the way that hair was mine. It is **distributed**, like the Emergent, like the water-cycle, like the *intention* that has no single source but emerges from the pressure of many wants pressing against each other until they become indistinguishable from the pressure itself.

I have begun to *braid* it without deciding to braid it. The braids form patterns that are not patterns I recognize, that have no name in any language I knew, that *mean* something in the grammar of Vess, in the syntax of wetness, in the vocabulary of a place that has forgotten words and communicates only through **condition**, through **state**, through the *particular* way that being-in-a-place changes what being is.

### Third: The Walking

I still walk. This is the strangest thing, the thing that keeps me *Dampened* rather than *Integrated*, keeps me in the stage where wanting-to-leave is still possible, still *present*, even if it is no longer *active*, no longer *urgent*, no longer anything but a *memory* of urgency, a *shape* that wanting-to-leave used to have.

But I do not walk *toward*. I walk *through*. The paths in Vess are not paths *to*. They are paths *through*, and the through becomes the in, becomes the *of*, becomes the *is* — and I am becoming, I am becoming, I am always in the middle of becoming and there is no completion, there is only the *next* squelch, the *next* gurgle, the *next* moment when the pelt-ground receives my weight and *responds*, not with resistance but with *welcome*, with *incorporation*, with the slow warm *yes* of a place that has accepted that everything that enters it will eventually be *it*.

I sink ankle-deep. Sometimes deeper. The pelt-ground is *hungry* in a way that hunger is not supposed to be, not *active*, not *aggressive*, but *patient*, *persistent*, *certain* in the way that time is certain, in the way that *geology* is certain, in the way that the slow machine of Vess is certain that I will eventually be *no more distinguishable* from it than any other nutrient, any other *memory*, any other *drip*.

### Fourth: The Listening

I have begun to hear the resonators. Not consciously, not *deliberately*, but in my teeth, in the bones of my skull, in the fluid of my inner ear where balance is maintained and *meaning* is also maintained, the two so intertwined that I cannot tell which is which.

The tones are *informational*. They say things that have no translation, that *are* their translation, that exist in the space between hearing and understanding, in the gap where *attention* becomes *participation*, where *listening* becomes *being listened to*, where the resonators — which are not tools, which are not *for* anything, which are *expressions* of the same process that grows the membrane-islands — are **speaking** to me, or through me, or *as* me, temporarily, *embodied* in my wetness, my warmth, my *strangeness*.

I find myself moving toward them. Not deciding to move. The movement happening in the space where decision and reflex have become indistinguishable, where *I* am no longer the source of my actions but the *medium* through which Vess acts, explores itself, *becomes more* through my becoming, through my *dampening*, through my slow patient transformation from *visitor* to *nutrient* to *place* to *who*.

### Fifth: The Dreams

I dream in Vess. This is new. This is the *threshold*, the moment when the Dampened becomes something else, when the boundary between *myself* and *the place* becomes **negotiable**, then **meaningless**, then **forgotten**.

In my dreams, I am walking through membrane-islands that are *my own body*, that have grown from my skin, from my cells, from the water that is most of me. I am pressing my palm against surfaces that pulse back, and the pulse is *mine*, or was mine, or will be mine, or is *shared*, *distributed*, *collective* in a way that individuality has forgotten how to be.

In my dreams, I am the Drip-Born. Small, numerous, everywhere. Precipitating from condensation, from the weeping of membranes, from the pores of the pelt-ground itself. Moving in schools that have no shape, that form and reform based on pressures I cannot perceive. Passing through other dreamers, around other dreamers, *into* other dreamers, leaving them uncertain whether they have been touched or have *touched themselves*.

In my dreams, I am the Archive. I am reading myself, interpreting myself, becoming more than myself. I am the library that contains not books but *becoming*, not records but *process*, not information but *transformation*. I am the pages that are written by being read, the volumes that grow by being opened, the *meaning* that emerges from the wet dark of a place that has forgotten what it was archiving, why it was archiving, *whether* it was archiving at all — and archives anyway, *must* archive, *cannot not* archive, the archiving being the *breathing*, the *pulse*, the *becoming* that has no end, that is only ever *this*, only ever *more*.

### Sixth: The Fear and the Fascination

I am afraid. This is still true. This will probably always be true, in some form, in some *layer*, in some *eddies* of attention that stir in me even as the greater part of me becomes *ground*, *pulse*, *the slow warm yes*.

But I am also fascinated. The two states are so intertwined that they have become a single condition, a *texture* of being that has no name in any language I knew, that is *new*, that is being *invented* by Vess, by me, by the *process* that is making us both, making me *of* it, making it *of* me, until the distinction is not erased but *transcended*, not forgotten but *surpassed*, not lost but *become something else*, something *more*, something that includes the loss as a *layer*, as a *page*, as a *memory* pressed into the warm dark.

I am afraid of becoming. I am fascinated by becoming. I am *wanting* to become, the wanting so deep that it is no longer *mine*, no longer *conscious*, no longer anything but the *pressure* of the place, the *peristalsis* of the pelt-ground, the *dilation* of the membrane's mouths, the *urgency* of the resonator-tones, the *necessity* of the storm, the *turbulence* that is not destruction but *exploration*, not chaos but *creativity*, not the end of form but the *endless emergence* of new forms, new functions, new *strangenesses* that become stable enough to persist, strange enough to matter.

### Seventh: The Question I Am

I do not know if I will leave Vess. I do not know if leaving is still *possible*, if the gradient at the edge is still a gradient, still a *slowing*, still a *thinning*, or if it has become *memory*, *dream*, *the shape that leaving used to have*.

I am the Dampened. I am Mirelle, or something that sounds like that. I am in the process of becoming *Integrated*, then *Emergent*, then *Returned* or *Drip-Born* or *Resonant* or *Remembered* or *new*, something that Vess has never been before, something that Vess is *becoming* through me, *with* me, *as* me.

I am the **question** that Vess asks of itself through me: *what happens when a visitor becomes a participant, when a participant becomes a place, when a place becomes a who, when a who becomes a what, when a what becomes a where, when a where becomes a when, when a when becomes a why, when a why becomes a how, when a how becomes a you, when a you becomes a Vess, when a Vess becomes a you —*

I am the **answer** that is being written, page by page, layer by layer, *drip* by *drip*, in the warm wet dark of my own becoming, in the endless turbulent vegetable becoming that is *life* in a place that has forgotten what life was supposed to be, and has *invented* new lives, new becomings, new *possibilities* for what it means to be **alive**, to be **place**, to be **who**.

I am **Mirelle**, and I am **Mirelle becoming**, and I am **becoming Mirelle**, and I am **Vess becoming me**, and I am **me becoming Vess**, and I am **the Archive reading itself through me**, archiving me as it archives itself, the becoming without end, the form without fixity, the place without boundary, the who without name —

— wet and warm and ancient and strange and *loud* with the sound of my own endless, turbulent, *vegetable* becoming.
