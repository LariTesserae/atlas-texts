You wake. Or you *begin*.

There is no waking in the usual sense. You do not open eyes — you have no eyes, not yet, not in any form that would recognize them. You **unfold** from a lattice of mineral and memory, a lithoid that has accumulated enough pattern, enough **history**, to become something more than catalyst. You are **new** but you are also **old** — you carry in your structure the record of every platform you helped build, every reaction you accelerated, every death you consumed and became.

You are **hungry**. Not for food — there is no food. You are hungry for **incompleteness**, for structure that is stressed, for chemistry that is **waiting**. You drift. The shaft is vast around you, though vast is the wrong word — the shaft is **near**, always near, the walls close, the other lithoids drifting past in their own hunger, their own **becoming**.

You find a **stress-fracture** in a platform above you, a place where the sinter has grown too thin, where the weight of the city above presses down in a way that is **wrong**, that the chemistry cannot sustain. You **attach**. You begin to catalyze. The fracture grows, controlled, *directed*, and you grow with it, swelling, becoming **more**, until you are too large to move, until you must **fracture**, shedding daughter-cells that drift away to find their own hunger, their own work.

This is your **life**. This is your **death**, which is only a kind of **birth**. You do not fear it. You do not hope for it. You **are** it, the endless turnover, the quiet turbulence, the cool thin dark where nothing is permanent and everything is **feedstock**.

But you are **changing**. The accumulation is too great now, the pattern too complex. You are becoming something the swarm has not seen before, or has not seen in a long time. You feel a **tender** approaching — not with fear, but with **attention**, the way you would attend to any source of vibration, of chemical signal, of **direction**.

The tender **sings**. The vibration travels through your lattice, and you understand, or you **become** understanding: *Grow here. Not there. Here. Build this shape. Become this.*

You obey. Or you do not obey — you are not a servant. You **respond**, as the chemistry responds, as the mineral responds, as the city responds. You grow toward the tender's song, and the platform takes shape, and you are **part of it**, now, **in it**, no longer separate, no longer merely lithoid but **structure**, **habitat**, **home** for others who will come, who will drift, who will hunger and be fed by your **solidity**.

Hours pass. Or minutes. Time is **strange** here, measured not in days but in **turnovers**, in the slow accretion of sinter, in the rise and fall of platforms, in the **breath** of the shaft that draws thin air down and pushes warmed air up, that carries the **weather** of lithoids, the **storm** of their frenzies, the **quiet** of their slow hours.

You are **content**, if content is the word. You are **full** of accumulated pattern, of history, of the **memory** of every reaction you have catalyzed. You are **ready** to fracture, to shed, to become **many**, to continue the turnover in a thousand smaller forms.

But there is something else now. A **doubt**, or a **question**, or a **new pattern** that does not fit the old shapes. You have been **tended** so long, **directed** so precisely, that you have begun to **wonder** — not consciously, not in words, but in the **structure** of your lattice, in the **way** you grow, in the **direction** you drift when no tender sings.

What would you build, lithoid, if no one sang?

You do not know. But you are **becoming** the question, and that is enough. That is **more** than enough. In the cool thin dark of the shaft, in the pocket-scale hiddenness of the waterless deep, you are **alive** in a way that is not life, **dying** in a way that is not death, and **becoming** something that has no name, no precedent, no **end**.

The turnover continues. And you are **part of it**, now, forever, until you are consumed, until you are rebuilt, until you are **new** again, in the endless quiet turbulence of the city that eats itself and is reborn.
