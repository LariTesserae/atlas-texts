I didn't choose to enter the Veridian Lung. No one chooses. The parameters chose me—the 0.1 of fauna, the thing that almost wasn't, the walker who almost left.

I am **the Walker**.

Or I am becoming the Walker. The distinction doesn't hold in a place where dynamic is 0.1. I am the one who moves through the channels, touching filaments on either side, feeling the tholin pulse and the 40-hertz drone press against my suit—my suit, which I don't remember putting on, which may be grown now, calcium and polymer fused to skin that may be fused to memory.

Nothing happens.

I withdraw my hand. I turn. I walk back down, through the channels that loop, through the filaments that sing, past the drones that flicker, past the pumps that thump, into the **depths** where the light is **dimmer**, where the tholin is **thicker**, where the sound is **deeper**, where the **frozen equilibrium** is **most frozen**, most **complete**, most **perfect**.
