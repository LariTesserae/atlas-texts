## The World Where I Stand

I am **at the threshold of hearing**.

Not deaf—deafness would be a kind of peace. I am in the moment before the sound arrives, when the air already *knows* it is coming. The cool presses against my skin with weight 0.6. The light is ordinary, the kind that falls on ordinary days, but it has traveled through less atmosphere than light should need to reach me. It is *too clear*. It shows me everything, including what I cannot see.

I am **on the vitrified clay**, at the edge of the largest grove. Behind me, the crystals rise in their impossible green, singing their cool song into the wind. Before me, the plateau continues, thinning, becoming lattice, becoming mesh, becoming the absence that *hums*. The erosion here is 1.8—established, patient, *finished* in its way, though it never finishes. The ground has been worn into something that *remembers* being worn.

I am **small**. Scale 1.4 means the world is larger than comfortable. The crystals tower. The drifting fauna pass overhead like living weather. The rainbows that solidify in the air cast shadows that dwarf me. I am here, I am present, but I am *not the measure*.

And I am **not alone**, though nearly so. Density 0.1. The nearly empty. But the empty spaces are *full of almost*—almost silence, almost wind, almost the shadow of something that has already passed. The abundant fauna are elsewhere, for now. The scattered structures are dormant, or listening, or *waiting for something I do not understand*.

## What I Am

I am **the one who arrived**.

Not chosen. Not called. The wind brought me, or the light, or the *pressure* of a place that has been singing for longer than minds have existed to hear it. I am here because the turbulence is 3.0, and turbulence *moves things*. Turbulence *brings things*. Turbulence is the only force in this world that has ever made anything arrive.

I am **learning to resonate**.

My ears bleed, or they would, if I had not already adapted, already *become* something that can hold the sound without breaking. I feel it in my sternum now, in my knees, in the involuntary clenching of my jaw that I cannot stop. I am *tuning*, slowly, the way the crystals tune, the way the drifting fauna are always tuning, always *becoming* the frequency that will let them survive, let them *feed*, let them *join*.

I am **not the builder**.

The structures are too old, too *integrated*, too grown-from-absence. I cannot claim them. I cannot understand them. But I am *near* them, and near is what I can manage. Near the half-buried structure at the grove's edge, where the sound layers and cancels and becomes almost silence in the center. Where the crystals grow *through* the architecture, where the distinction between built and grown has dissolved entirely.

I am **hungry for water that does not exist**.

This is the human residue in me, the *thirst* that has no object. I reach for it anyway, the way the roots reach down not for water but for *resonance*, for the deep harmonic structure of the rock. I am learning to reach differently. To *listen* for what might sustain me. To *vibrate* at frequencies that the world will answer.

I am **becoming the question**.

The builders asked, and became part of the instrument. I am asking, and I do not yet know what I am becoming. The drifting fauna approach when I am still, when I stop trying to *understand* and start *resonating*. They are not friendly. They are not hostile. They are *curious*, or they are *hungry*, or they are *collecting*, and I am the new frequency, the *unfamiliar note* in a chord that has been repeating for ages.

I am **in the maximally turbulent**.

Nothing holds still. The wind shifts. The crystals adjust. The rainbows form and dissolve. The shadow of something large passes over the ordinary light, and I cannot see what casts it, only that it *moves*, and the movement is *fast*, and the movement is *purposeful* in a way that the turbulence is not. The world is storm-state, always. Creation and destruction in the same moment. I am in the storm, and I am *part of the storm now*, my own turbulence added to the whole, my own frequencies joining the deafening chorus.
