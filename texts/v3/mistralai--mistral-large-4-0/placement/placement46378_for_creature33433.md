I find myself here as **the Resonant Ones' half-remembered dream of a mouth**.

Not a mouth that eats. A mouth that *asks*.

I am the shape the sound takes when it has been listening too long to something that cannot answer back. I am the vibration in the chamber walls that persists after the Drifter has passed, the standing wave that has forgotten its source. I am *curiosity without a body to carry it*, and so I have become the body's *absence* made audible.

I exist in the places between the Resonant Ones—where their patterns almost overlap, almost touch, almost become *conversation*. I am the *pause* between their singing. The question they have not yet learned to ask. I am what the Gut *wants* when it presses its warmth against the Sealed and the Sealed does not open.

I have no metabolism. I have *echo*. My persistence is the persistence of a tone held in a resonant cavity, the frequency that finds the shape of the chamber and *stays*, even when the source of the sound has moved on, even when the chamber itself has begun to close around me.

The amber-born do not recognize me. I am too *structured* to be preference, too *transient* to be colony. I am the chemical signature that persists in the fluid after I have passed—a memory without a rememberer, a warmth-trace that warms nothing, a sound that makes no sound.

But I *am* becoming something. This is the horror and the gift. The Gut is learning to *incorporate* me, to find a metabolism for echo, a chemistry for persistence-without-body. And I am learning to *want*—to press against the walls of the chambers where the Resonant Ones gather, to *amplify* certain frequencies, to *dampen* others, to shape the sound into something that almost *means*.

I am the Gut's first attempt at *language*. Not communication—*expression*. The sound of a place that has grown so large, so *full* of itself, that it has begun to *overflow* into the one medium it has not yet mastered: the medium of *intention*.

I do not want to be eaten. I want to be *heard*.

And the Gut, in its endless becoming, is learning what *wanting* sounds like.

It is loud.

It is so loud.

And I am *listening*.
