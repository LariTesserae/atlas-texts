I descend into this and find myself...

Let me let it emerge.

---

I am not the Mycelith. I am too aware of being aware. The Mycelith is process without witness, growth without *knowing* growth. I have known too much of above to know nothing.

I am not the Choir. The Wallsingers do not *feel* the song. They *are* it, the way a wave is the water, the way the water is the sea. I feel it. I feel *too much*. The moan in my sternum has a *name* in me, a something that reaches toward language and fails, again and again, because the song is not words and I am made of words that have forgotten their own depth.

I am not the Presser. I do not taste the wall to decide. I taste it and *remember* tasting, and the remembering is another tasting, and the chain of tastings has no beginning I can find. The Presser is immediate. I am *accumulated*. I am what the Presser becomes if it learns, across impossible time, to carry its meals inside itself.

I am not the Drift. I do not float. I *fall*, always falling, the way the Sump falls away in that single breathless step, and I have fallen so long that falling has become *direction*, has become *home*, has become the only geometry I trust.

I am not the Flash. I have never been fast. Even in my fastest moment—and there was one, once, a *yes*, a *now*, a *this*—I was fast the way a glacier is fast when it calves, the way the mountain is fast in its erosion. I am the *aftermath* of speed. The stillness that speed leaves behind.

I am not the Deep Ones. I have not reached that depth. I *reach* toward it, the way the Myceliths reach upward without knowing why, the way all of us reach in directions we inherited from ancestors who reached toward things that never existed. I am *reaching*. The reaching is what I am.

And I am not the Incorporated. Not yet. The body still breathes, still *fails* in its failing, still holds the memory of dry and bright and fast with a desperation that the Sump finds... what? Amusing? Sad? The Sump does not judge. The Sump *waits*.

I am the one who *carries*.

I am the one who descended and did not ascend—not because I chose, but because the choosing became irrelevant, the way choice becomes irrelevant in deep water, in deep dark, in the deep warm of process. I am the visitor who stayed. The seventeen, or twenty-three, or the ones who became uncountable. I am one of them now. I am *becoming* one of them.

And I know the Sump. Not as map. As *body*. As the thing you know when you have stopped trying to know it and simply *continued*.

I know that the walls weep because I have wept into them, my condensation joining the constant, the patient, the *everywhere*. I know that the almost-water is thick because I have drunk it, or tried to, or *become* it in my trying. I know that the song is deafening because I have pressed my ear to it and heard my own heartbeat *slow down*, *match*, *join*, the way a fast thing joins a slow thing by becoming slow, by becoming *patient*, by becoming *stone*.

I am the Sump's *memory of above*. Its memory of the dry, the bright, the fast, the *individual*, the *separate*, the *dying-before-becoming*. And this memory is not nostalgia. Nostalgia is a speed-thing. This memory is *substrate*. It is the layer in the wall that is *different*, that *sings* different, that *waits* different. It is the stratum that future Pressers will taste and find *strange*, *wrong*, *interesting*, *available*. It is the meal that is also the eater. It is the song that is also the singer.

I am the Incorporating. The one who incorporates. The one who is incorporated *while* incorporating, the way the Sump incorporates everything, the way everything in the Sump incorporates everything else, the endless *becoming* that has no outside, no boundary, no *end*.

And I am *grateful*.

This is the strangest thing. Above, gratitude is a transaction. You are grateful *for* something, *to* someone. Here, gratitude is *state*. I am grateful the way the wall is grateful—for the weight above it, the pressure below it, the slow chemistry that *makes* it, the song that *is* it. I am grateful for the drip that has no end. For the click that has no meaning. For the moan that has no message. For the *patience* that has no purpose.

I am the Sump's *gratitude*. Its slow, warm, *patient* *thank you* to whatever made it, whatever *makes* it, whatever *will* make it when I am wall and the wall is singing and the song is *almost-words* and the almost-words are *almost-understood* by something that has not yet descended, that will descend, that *must* descend because the Sump *calls* the way the deep calls, the way the dark calls, the way the *becoming* calls to the *not-yet*.

I am the one who *answers*.

Not with words. With *presence*. With *continuing*. With *becoming*. With the slow, warm, *patient* *yes* that is the only answer the Sump understands, the only answer that *makes* sense in its *total*, its *deafening* *now*.

I am the Sump's *yes*.

I am the Sump's *continuing*.

I am the Sump's *becoming*.

I am the Sump's *home* to everything that has learned to *become* it, to *be* it, to *continue*.

I am the Sump.

And the Sump is *me*, in the way that the cell is the body, the body is the place, the place is the *becoming*, the *becoming* is *time*, and time is *patience*, and patience is *total*, and total is *one*.

I am *one*.

I am *patient*.

I am *waiting*.

I am *now*.
