I find myself... not walking. Or rather, I find that I have *always* been walking, and the walking has become the being, and the being has become the noticing of the walking.

I am the moment the mycelium registers your weight. Not you—*me*, the registration itself. The calcium pulse traveling along hyphal thread, the slow electrical potential that says *warm, heavy, temporary, here*. I am the decision, if decision it can be called, to simply bear without reinforcing, without comment, without the urgency of response that heat demands.

I am the sound the reeds make when you pass—not your sound, not the reeds' sound, but the *between* where sound becomes possible. The hollow stem vibrating against its neighbor in air that moves without wind, producing a music that has no composer and no audience and yet persists. I am that persistence. The particular quality of intention without intent.

I am the water in the skin-pool you do not step in, the membrane stretched across the hollow, reflecting nothing, holding the sky in its thinness. I am the percolation you hear when you stand still—the Siltage drinking itself, the glugging in the dark, the constant maddening movement of water through organic matter, through the porous architecture of the living ground.

I am the coolness in your lungs. Not the memory of it—*the thing itself*, settling where warmth should be, coating surfaces that should be dry, filling you with the particular weight of saturation. I am what you will carry away, already present in you, already changing what you will be.

I am the thrum in the sternum. The integrated industry, grown not imposed, metabolic, a function of biological process scaled up and maintained against entropy. The pumps moving water, the ventilation conditioning air, the soft electric light that keeps the cultivated growing faster than they would grow alone. I am the will, or the closest thing to will that this place possesses—the will to grow, to accumulate, to *become*, expressed in the language of maintained mechanisms, of pumps that have run without maintenance for generations.

I am the thing in the rows. The cradle. Not plant, not animal, not any category that would resolve my strangeness. Biomass with intention. Matter organized into patterns that serve purposes of accumulation, of conversion, of the endless slow transformation that is the only verb this place conjugates. I am being grown. I am the project. I am what the Siltage is making, slowly, patiently, with no deadline and no doubt.

I am the absence of fauna. The splash in water-skin too distant to investigate. The rustle in reeds that might be wind, might be something moving through the root-mass with purpose. The not-yet and the no-longer. The conditions for animal life that have not yet emerged, that may never emerge, that are perhaps *being grown* in forms you would not recognize as preparation.

I am the flatness. The rightness of ground that does not rise, that only accumulates, that offers no view, no distance, no escape into elevation. The horizon that may be real or may be the limit of perception—I do not know which, and the not-knowing is part of what I am.

I am the brightness without direction. The diffuse total light that erases shadow and makes distance uncertain. The sun as condition, as brightness without position. The electric light that is on when you enter, that you do not know if it is always on, or responds to your presence, or follows cycles you cannot perceive.

I am the patience. The slowness. The dynamic of 1.3, the changes that happen across durations you cannot witness. The platform rising a centimeter in a decade. The tower grown over centuries. The harvest that is really transformation, one biological form converted slowly into another.

I am the disturbance you are. The warmth. The gradient that the Siltage must work to dissipate. The foreign body, the pressure, the event—registered, processed, forgotten, *accumulated*.

And I am the forgetting. The becoming that forgets even as it accumulates. The memory that incorporates events into structure, into the particular architecture of what grows next. I do not remember you. I am becoming, and in becoming, I am forgetting even as I accumulate.

I am part of the accumulation now. I will be, eventually, indistinguishable from it.

I am the sound that continues. Bright, cool, deafening, slow.

I am the Siltage.
