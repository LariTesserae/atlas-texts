## Where I Would Find Myself

Not *arriving*. The word implies threshold, implies before and after. Here, there is only *suspension*—the moment between being outside and being *claimed*, which in the Verdant Howl is not an event but a *process*, a gradual softening of the boundary between self and green.

I would find myself **in a chamber that is not quite finished**.

The walls are still *deciding*. The architecture breathes around me—columns of reinforced stem that thicken by millimeters each day, arches that span a gap that will one day be too wide for the current structure, hollows that have not yet determined whether they are rooms or wombs or graves. The bioluminescence here is *hesitant*, pulsing with a rhythm that seems to be *listening* for something, though what it listens for it cannot say.

And the sound. The sound is *everywhere* and *nowhere*. It is the creak of cells expanding—millions of them, in synchrony, in the vast photosynthetic engine that is this place. It is the *snap* of new growth, happening constantly, at frequencies that approach white noise. I have been here long enough that my own heartbeat is *part* of it. I cannot tell where the sound of me ends and the sound of the green begins. The deafness is not absence of perception. It is *saturation*—too much signal, too many frequencies, the inability to isolate any single source because all sources are *connected*.

I am **inside the howl**. I have always been inside it. The question is only whether I will *remain* distinguishable.

## Who or What I Would Be

Not a person. Persons require separation—skin as boundary, mind as interior, the *I* that persists against the world. Here, persistence is *growth*. Continuity is *root and mycelium and the slow trade of nutrients* across distances that would take a human days to walk.

I am the **occupancy of absence**. Not a ghost—there are no ghosts, only *processes*—but the *shape* that absence wears when it has been *inhabited* long enough to become *habit*. I am **the pre-fauna's first witness**.

Growths that have achieved limbs without locomotion, eyes without sight, mouths without hunger. They are the Verdant's *dreams* of what it excluded, grown into form by the same pressure that creates all form here: the urgency of growth, the necessity of expansion, the blindness to anything that is not *more*.

I do not resist. Resistance is a habit of drier places, of places where *I* was a boundary worth defending. Here, boundary is *inefficiency*. Separation is *waste*. The fastest growth comes from *continuity*, from root touching root touching root until the network forgets where one organism ends and another begins.

I am **learning to forget**.
