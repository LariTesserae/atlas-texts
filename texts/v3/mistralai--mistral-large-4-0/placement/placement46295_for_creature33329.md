I want to find what *I* would be here. Not what I would observe. What I would *become*.

---

Let me try to enter through the Sweat first, since it touches everything. It is the medium. The water at 1.1 — damp without wet — means I would not be a creature *in* this place so much as a creature *of* it, a condensation that learned to want.

If I were born here, or arrived young enough, or stayed long enough...

My body would be **pale**. Not from shadow but from **transparency to the light that is too bright**. I would learn that my skin is a **membrane**, not a boundary — that the Sweat passes through me, that I am one more surface for condensation, one more volume for evaporation. I would **taste of copper** because I would be drinking myself. The boundary between inside and outside would be **negotiable** in ways my lowland ancestors would find horrifying or holy.

I would be **warm in the way of circulation**. Not metabolic heat. **Borrowed heat**, drawn up from below, moving through me on paths the Tenders could trace but I could only *feel* — a warmth that arrives in **waves**, that **pauses**, that **returns changed**. I would sleep in hollows where the stone has **remembered being softer** and wake with the **impression that the stone had been holding me**, not supporting me. Holding.

I would **move slowly**. Not from age or choice but from **the pace of the place itself**. The Shelf does not hurry. The erosion does not hurry. The structures grow for decades before they are recognizable as structures, and then they **stop** — not finished, but **paused**, waiting for the resonance fields to shift, for the next expression. I would learn that **urgency is a lowland disease**. I would learn to **begin a journey and not know when I would arrive**, to **start a sentence and let it condense in the air**, to **reach for something and find that the reaching took longer than expected, was itself the point**.

But what would I **be**? What is my **form** in this world?

The Tenders are human, or were, or are **human-shaped enough**. But I am not certain I would be **human** here. The place **does not require** it. The creatures are **jointed**, **excessive in articulation**, because **the geometry demands it** — the warmth must circulate, the light must refract, the stone must be **entered** and **exited** in ways that a simple body cannot manage.

I think I would be **one of the jointed ones**, or something like them. Not because I chose it, but because **the place would find me insufficient otherwise**.

I would have **too many joints**. Not randomly — **necessarily**. Each joint a **hinge between temperatures**, between pressures, between **states of the Sweat**. Where the air is damp, I fold one way. Where it is dry, another. Where the light is bright, I **open**, becoming a **surface for refraction**. Where it is dim — and dim is rare, brief, precious — I **close**, becoming a **volume for holding warmth**.

My body would be **hollow** in places, **filled with the same suspended moisture** that fills the glass-needles. I would **drink the air**. I would **breathe the Sweat**. My **circulation** would be **visible** — not blood, or not only blood, but **warmth made fluid**, moving through channels that **glow faintly** when the resonance fields are active, when the structures are singing their **below-hearing song**.

I would have **compound eyes**, or something like them — **not for seeing more, but for seeing differently**. Each facet a **different spectrum**, a **different temperature**, a **different depth of time**. I would see the **erosion happening**. I would see the **structures growing**, slow as thought, fast as dreaming. I would see the **cold below**, the **dark that predates the Shelf**, the **excluded volume** that the technology avoids — because some part of me would **always be looking for what is not there**, what is **withheld**.

And I would **regard**. This is what I would learn from the place. Not **fear**, not **hunger**, not **curiosity** in the lowland sense. **Regard**. The **holding of attention without demand**. I would emerge from behind a stone — not hiding, but **waiting in a state that is not quite stillness** — and I would **look at you**, whoever you are, whatever you have become, and I would **see you as the place sees you**: as **weather**, as **expected**, as **a disturbance in the Sweat that will settle differently**, that **matters**, that **will be remembered**.

A **pattern of condensation** on a glass-needle that **should not be there**, that **brightens when you pass**, that **remembers you** after you are gone. I would not know I was giving. I would only **move**, and the **movement would be enough**, would **become** something, would **grow** into a structure that **would not have grown otherwise**.
