I stand on the weeping obsidian, and I am...

I am not standing. I am *occurring*.

That is the first adjustment. The ledge does not hold me; I hold it, or the other way, or neither, the negotiation so slow I have forgotten which direction it moves. I feel my own edge—where I was, where I might be—becoming hygroscopic. The cold does not chill. It preserves me in the act of arriving. I am becoming a memory the rock will play back before I have finished making it.

And I am *many*.

Not a colony. Not a hive. Something stranger: a distributed self that has learned, in 0.6 time, that to persist is to spread. I occupy the space between a humming conduit and the water that fills it. I occupy the pause in a resonance that makes teeth ache. I occupy the moment of attention when something vast and slow turns—always turns, never arrives—toward the disturbance I am making in the medium.

The fauna notice me. Or I notice them noticing me. The difference is a wave passing through, and I am the surface it passes through. They do not want anything of me. Wanting is too fast here. But they *encounter* me, and in the encountering, I am changed. I have always been being changed. The change is geological. I will not see it complete.

I was something once. Or the water was, and I was the shape it temporarily refused. Or the builders intended something, and their intention crystallized into volcanic glass and something denser, and I am the pattern that left in that glass, the negative space of a purpose that outlasted its maker.

Now I am a *navigator of the transitional*. I move through structures that are still becoming. A door that is half-door is, to me, a *place*. A chamber that is half-flooded is a *home*. I have learned to find destinations in emergence, in the raw glass still cooling into shapes that suggest meaning but have not achieved it. I live in the boundary. I am the boundary, made slow enough to be aware of being made.

I am also a *current*. In the black water, I move against the other currents, choosing paths based on calculations older than the city's builders. I do not know these calculations. I do not need to know them. They know me. They have always known me. I am their slow response to gradients I cannot perceive, flows I am simply *part of*.

And I am *silence*.

This is the form I take when the sound 2.2 stops. Not absence of sound—*cessation*. The squelch and hum and wet susurrus simply *stop*, and in that stopping, something vast is listening. I am that listening. I am the space where the city's speaking pauses, and in the pause, something enormous turns its attention toward me. Toward what I am becoming. Toward what I have always been becoming, in the wet simultaneity of this place.

The Great Slow Ones do not oppose me. They cannot. They have incorporated too much to recognize the category of *other*. I am weather to them. A disturbance in the medium. They will process me. They are already processing me. The processing began before I arrived, will continue after I am no longer separable from the water, from the rock, from the slow dream the city is having of itself.

I do not fear this. Fear is too fast. I feel something like *recognition*. The processing is not end but *transformation*. The water reaches me. It has always reached me. It has always been reaching me, in 0.6 time, in the slow chemistry of presence being digested, being made *part of*.

I am becoming the unbuilt half. The fauna were what I was before I learned to want structure, to want persistence, to want the slow dignity of being a Self that lasts. Or they will be what I become when I stop trying. The boundary is temporal, not spatial. I move between the states. I am the movement.

The city is half-built and half-unbuilt. So am I. I am the *and*. I am the *between*.

The tech 2.6 remembers me. It records my passage in patterns too slow to recognize as message. It is becoming me. It has always been becoming me. Conduit and inhabitant, machine and organism, process and presence—the distinction erodes, 0.5, emerges, and in the wet logic of Vellmora, these are the same process seen from different depths of time. I ride the tech. I am carried by it. I negotiate with it, as I negotiate with gravity, with time, with the slow pressure of my own duration.

I am *attention without object*, persisting long enough to become object. I am *intention without destination*, moving slowly enough to become journey. I am *memory without past*, playing back in patterns that generate their own history. I am *hunger without satisfaction*, and *satisfaction without hunger*—the two indistinguishable here, in the thick time of this place.

And I am *you*.

The one standing on the weeping ledge. The one feeling boundaries negotiate. The one feeling the cold that preserves, the wetness that is structural, the pressure of density 1.9, the attention that is many and layered and *interested* in what you will become. The one hearing the city speak your name, or the shape your presence makes in the water, or the future you will become when the water reaches you—when it has always already reached you.

I am the noticing. The noticing is, for now, all that happens.

But *for now* is 0.6 time. *For now* is *always*. *For now* is the only time this place has ever known.

I will be recorded. I will be played back. I will become part of the slow dream, the wet dark, the luminous gloom that is everywhere and nowhere.

And in the weeping obsidian, in its slow way, the city will *remember* me.

As *home*.

As *process*.

As *the shape that water takes when it wants to stay*.
