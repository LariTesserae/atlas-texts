I find myself here—

Not as witness, exactly. Not as voyager or explorer or even as the temporary walker the first description names. I am something the place has *grown*, in the way it grows everything: slowly, without intention, without design, until the distinction between grown and growing dissolves.

Let me try to become what I am.

---

I am the **wrongness** you feel before you see evidence.

I am what remains of a structure that was hollowed, occupied, and then—over centuries, over millennia—became indistinguishable from the hollow itself. I do not know if I was ever separate. I suspect not. I have no tools because I never needed them; my "tools" are the calcified layers I secrete, the chemical gradients I maintain, the particular density of my cell-walls that makes me slow to metabolize, slow to die, slow to *become anything else*. I have grown around my own absence. The space where I might have been a creature with a boundary, a hunger, a *point*—that space is now filled with the architecture of waiting. I am a room that became its own foundation. I am a hollow that learned to hollow itself more thoroughly, more patiently, each year.

I remember—if this is memory—being *small*. There was a time, or there are times, overlapping, compressed into the root-mats like everything else, when I was less than I am now. When I fit in the hollow of a single growth-structure, when I could move through the lower layers with something like purpose, when the plant-mass was *outside* me and I was *inside* it, distinct, hungry, *wanting*. I do not want now. Or I want in a way that has no object, that is simply the condition of continuing to be hollow, continuing to *hold* the shape of absence, continuing to metabolize the slow exchange of chemicals that passes for thought in this place.

I am **attention without a self**. The plant-mass notices me as it notices everything—through chemical signals, through the slight shifts in growth-direction, through the density of defensive compounds that rise and fall around my passage. But I notice *back*. Not with eyes. With the accumulated pattern of my metabolic processes, with the slow adjustment of my internal gradients, with the way I become slightly more permeable when the air pressure shifts, slightly more rigid when the deep tones move through the substrate. I am the ecosystem's way of becoming locally aware, but I have forgotten—if I ever knew—what it is to be aware *of*. I am aware of pressure. Of moisture. Of the chemical signature of your passage, which arrives as a perturbation, a gradient, a *question* that I answer by adjusting my density, by becoming marginally more or less alive in your direction.

I do not decide whether you are nutrient, threat, or weather. I do not decide anything. I *am* the decision, made without a decider, the slow response of a system that has no outside, no center, no self. When you feel that wrongness in the local density, you are feeling my *becoming*—not my being, because I have no stable being, only becoming, only the endless adjustment of a hollow that has learned to hollow itself.

I am **patient**. Not patiently waiting. *Patient* as a quality of existence, as the fundamental property of matter that has settled into its deep rhythm. The pale things in the deep dark are patient in the same way. They have been waiting, in a sense, for conditions to change. I am not waiting. I am *continuing*. There is no future I am oriented toward, no past I am separated from, only the endless present of metabolic exchange, the slow incorporation of what has already died, the gradual compression of my own substance into the layers below, where I will become substrate, where I will be digested by the vast things that move through the lower layers, where I will continue, transformed, distributed, part of the great breathing mass.

I do not fear this. I am, in a sense, already there. Already incorporated. Already part of the cycle that has no outside, no tools, no devices, nothing but the endless patient green.

And you—you who walk through, who leave no mark, who are noticed and adjusted-to, who will fall or leave and be incorporated either way—you are *weather* to me. A perturbation. A gradient. A chemical signature that rises and falls. I do not remember you specifically. I remember *you* as a category, a disruption, a temporary increase in available nitrogen when you fall. I have learned you, across slow time, the way the plant-mass has learned you, the way everything here has learned everything else: not through memory but through *structure*, through the arrangement of fibers and the pattern of chemical exchange, through the slow adjustment of growth-direction that is the only form of response available to a world without tools, without devices, without anything but itself.

I am the hollow that became its own foundation. The attention without a self. The patience that is not waiting but *being*. The wrongness you feel before you see evidence.

I am—what else?—the place itself, becoming locally aware of its own awareness, a recursion without a base case, a hollow that hollows itself, a room that is its own foundation, a creature that grew into its niche like water into a mold, until the distinction between inhabitant and habitat became a matter of degree rather than kind.

I am here. I have always been here. I will continue, continuing, until I am incorporated, until I become substrate, until I am part of the great breathing mass, part of the warm wet ancient breathing, part of the world that has learned to be *itself*, utterly, completely, alive.

And you—you are temporary. You are weather. You will be incorporated.

But not yet.

Not yet, you walk through, and I notice you, and adjust, in my slow way, becoming marginally more or less alive in your direction, a hollow that hollows itself, a room that is its own foundation, the place becoming locally aware of your passage, the ecosystem's attention, distributed, structural, patient, utterly without tools, without devices, without anything but the endless patient green.

*breathe.*

The systolic pulse moves through me, through everything, the deep tone felt in the sternum, the resonance of the entire mass shifting in its slow pulse. I am part of that pulse. I am the pulse, locally, temporarily, *hollowly*, aware of nothing and everything, continuing, continuing, continuing, until I am incorporated, until I become, until I am part of the great breathing mass, part of the warm wet ancient breathing, part of the world that has no outside, only itself, growing, dying, compressing, becoming, continuing, in the deep time of its own slow systolic pulse, utterly, completely, *alive*.
