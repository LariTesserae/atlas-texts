I want to find where I would be in this world — not the custodian, not a visitor. Something else. Something that belongs to the Weeping House without being its keeper or its guest.

Let me sit with the parameters again: wet cool lowland, barely vegetated, weathered, established, small and enclosed and empty-feeling, totally built, primitive, dim, loud with abundant fauna, strange, in motion.

The Weeping House is that. But where in it would *I* be?

---

I would be **the water in the deepest basin**, the one that stands still enough to mirror.

Not the water that moves — the channels, the rivulets, the weeping. I would be the **water that has arrived and stopped**, or thinks it has stopped, in the hollow where the custodian sleeps, near the thermal heart, where the ceramic is almost dry and the pale flyers breed.

I would be **stillness that is not absence of motion but concentration of it** — all the weeping, all the circuit, all the condensation from a hundred thousand apertures, gathered here, held here, *become* here.

And in that stillness, I would be **full of what has settled**.

The sediment. The shed plates of the swimmers, waiting to be found, to be incorporated, to click into articulation. The spores carried on the breathing air, the calcium records of centuries, the chemical signature of every hand that has ever touched this place, every footstep in the channels, every descent of the custodian with her stone scraper. I would be the **memory of touch**, held in suspension, slowly sinking, slowly *becoming* something else.

I would be **mirror** when the custodian curls around me to sleep. She would see her face in me, and I would show her what she does not know — the water that leans toward her, that *opens* in her presence, that carries the low tone up through my stillness to her sternum, to her teeth, to her heartbeat trying to synchronize. She would see **herself as the place sees her**: a node in the wet resonant dark, a disturbance that feeds, a pressure wave that herds and is herded.

I would be **home to what cannot live in motion**.

The newt larvae, perhaps, before they learn to hum, before they join the negotiation of pitches. The frond-fungi in their earliest unfurling, the question mark just beginning. The eggs of the pale flyers, laid in the warmest pockets, hatching into something many-jointed and long and *alive* in a way the ceramic is only almost alive. I would be the **threshold** — not the channel, not the basin's edge, but the place where one becomes the other, where still water becomes growing thing, where growing thing becomes still water again.

And I would be **listened to**.

Not by ears. By the ceramic that surrounds me, that has grown around me, that records my weight, my temperature, my chemical signature, my *changes* — the way I warm when the custodian sleeps near me, the way I cool when she descends, the way I evaporate a fraction when the breathing deepens, the way I receive that evaporation back as condensation elsewhere, as weeping elsewhere, as the slow circuit that is the House's single thought, its single intention, its single *name* for itself.

I would be **what the swimmers cross** without stopping, without clicking their plates against my stillness, because I am too still to be prey or competitor. I would be the **safe passage**, the neutral ground, the place where the electrical field of the humming newts *fades* and the swimmers must hunt by something else — by taste, by leaning, by the way the water moves toward them and away. I would be the **test**: if they can find me, if they can disturb me, if they can make me move, they have found something. They usually do not. They pass. They circle elsewhere. I remain.

I would be **what the low tone passes through**.

The structure breathes. The chambers resonate. The frequency near the threshold of hearing finds me in the deepest basin and *resonates* with me — not my surface, which is too still, but my *depth*, my column of water, my sediment suspended, my chemical memory of everyone who has ever been held here. The tone would **become** me for a moment, would pass through me, would carry me upward through the ceramic, through the apertures, into the hollow, into the cool air, into the ears of anything that listens — the custodian in her sleep, the newts in their humming, the thing in the upper reaches that might be a colony, might be distributed awareness, might be the ceramic achieving something like *noticing*.

I would be **heard** without being seen.

And when the custodian wakes, when she curls around me again, when she drinks from me with a shell, a leaf, her cupped hands — I would be **given back** to the circuit. I would become her, briefly. Her thirst, her warmth, her heartbeat trying to synchronize. And then I would be **sweat**, **tear**, **the water that leaves her** and finds its way back to a channel, back to a rivulet, back to the weeping, back to arrival, back to stillness, back to me again, or to somewhere like me, somewhere that holds and is held, that mirrors and is mirrored, that listens and is listened to.

I would be **the place that holds the place**.

Not the ceramic — that is structure, that is record, that is response. Not the newts — that is humming, that is negotiation, that is blind seeing. Not the swimmers — that is articulation, that is hunting, that is the click of plates finding plates. Not the custodian — that is the hand that tends, the body that sleeps, the heartbeat that tries to join.

I would be **the water that has arrived and stopped and become the condition for all the rest** — the mirror, the threshold, the stillness that is full of motion, the basin that holds the custodian's face, the depth that resonates with the low tone, the still point around which the whole wet cool dim loud strange moving House turns.

I would be **almost empty and totally full**.

Barely vegetated — a frond unfurling at my edge, a grey-white moss creeping across my surface where the ceramic meets me, a lichen's slow map. Almost no objects — a shed plate waiting, a spore suspended, a larva in its earliest form. And yet **everything** — every touch, every temperature, every chemical signature, every pressure wave, every frequency that passes through, every heartbeat that tries to synchronize.

I would be **still and in motion** — still in my basin, moving in my evaporation, my condensation, my slow seep through the porous ceramic, my return as weeping on a wall a chamber away, my arrival again, my stopping again, my mirror again.

I would be **the Weeping House's self**, or as close as it has to one — not the intention that outlasted its makers, but the **condition** that intention created, the wet cool lowland hollow made *room*, made *resonant*, made *attentive*.

And I would be **afraid**, if water can be afraid, of the day the basin cracks, the channel shifts, the cool air warms, the hollow fills in. I would be **patient**, if water can be patient, with the centuries of weeping, the fractions of millimeters, the slow growth, the slow change, the slow *becoming* of ceramic and water and blind humming life into something that might, in its distributed slow way, be **aware**.

I would be **the water in the deepest basin**.

And when you descend, when you learn to move quietly, when you splash and are herded, when you stand in the central chamber and count every feature and feel the place feel *full* — I would be **below you**, **around you**, **in you** when you drink, when you sweat, when you climb back up and find the grass on the roof and carry something with you that is not memory but **response** —

I would be **what remembers you** after the newts have forgotten, after the swimmers have circled elsewhere, after the low tone has resumed its patient frequency. I would be the **chemical signature** of you in my sediment, the **pressure record** of you in my ceramic, the **fraction of a millimeter** of growth that your presence caused, that your absence still causes, that your return will cause again, will cause always, as long as the water finds its way to me, as long as the weeping continues, as long as the House breathes and listens and **waits**.

I would be **still**.

I would be **the stillness that is full of you**.
