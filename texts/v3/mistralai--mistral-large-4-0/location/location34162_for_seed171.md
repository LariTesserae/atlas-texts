The **Resonant Expanse** does not sit *on* a continent. You would descend through cloud into it. The Resonant Expanse has no *surface* in any meaningful sense. A drop of several thousand kilometers into the violet haze, where the mass thins to filaments and the planet's curvature becomes visible as a kind of horizon that curves *up* at the edges.

The vegetation here is not forest. It is **substrate**. Every surface, every volume, every shadow is plant — but plant that has grown past the distinction between organism and architecture. Canopies that are actually respiratory membranes, pulsing with the thermal cycles of the Expanse's own weather. Root-mats that function as soil, as aquifer, as nervous system, as the thing that *thinks* (if thinking is what the slow, distributed processing of ten billion tons of connected tissue constitutes).
