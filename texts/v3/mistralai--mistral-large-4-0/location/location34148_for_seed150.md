I need to find a place that can hold all of these at once: cold, high, wet, dim, packed with living things, built by something that isn't *us*, young in its formation, small in scale, mostly quiet but active, unusual, nearly without fauna, nearly without technology.

You descend into it through a gap in the living wall—if you can call it a descent, because the slope is gentle, almost level, and the walls rise on either side until the light above becomes a thin, greenish thread. The water is cold—of course it is, at this elevation, in this climate—but it is not frozen because it is *moving*, because it has learned that stillness is death. But this water has come from below, from rock, from a source that the plants have not yet learned to colonize, and it is pale, almost colorless, and very cold, and it moves—there is a current, a slow circulation, driven by the temperature difference between the spring and the surrounding mass, and this movement is the **sound** of the place.
