**The Sump**

You descend. That is the first thing — you are always descending, though the elevation parameter says low, the *feeling* is of having fallen somewhere deep and being kept there. The walls are not walls. They are **packed compacted chitin and carbonate and the mineralized husks of things that died before they had names**, compressed over epochs into a substance like wet concrete that has forgotten it was ever wet. It is cool here. The temperature sits at the edge of comfort, the kind of cool that makes you aware of your own heat, your own *wrongness* in this place.

There is no water. The parameter is 0.2 — trace, residual, the memory of moisture held in crystal lattices and the occasional sealed vacuole in the wall-stone. What the **fauna** — overwhelming, 3.0, *three point zero* — runs on is something else. They do not drink. They *exchange*. A chemistry of lipids and salts and slow-burning reactions, non-aqueous, the way deep Earth microbes dream of hydrocarbons. The place pulses with them.

They are *processed* by it, and emerge — changed? — or perhaps they simply pass through, and the machine is *waiting*, industrial patience, for a function no longer needed.
