# The Throat Garden of Vess

## A Contained World Beneath a Crust of Forgetting

---

You descend through a wound in the world—a vertical shaft no wider than a man's reach, its walls lined with something that might once have been shell, or scale, or the calcified memory of a lung. The air that rises to meet you is warm, thick, tasting of copper and the particular sweetness of fermentation without water. There is no water here. There has never been water, not in the way your body understands it. The chemistry runs on other hungers.

You are falling into a pocket. A room, almost. A garden the size of a cathedral, if cathedrals were grown rather than built, if gardens could scream.

---

## The Architecture of Growth

The space opens like a flower that has forgotten it was ever closed. The ceiling arches overhead at perhaps thirty meters—a vault of **lithic coral**, its surface pocked and layered and veined with minerals that catch the dim light in faint phosphorescence. The walls curve inward, embracing, descending to a floor that is not floor at all but **a mat of living density**: millions of intertwined organisms, none larger than a child's fist, none smaller than a seed, all pressing against each other in a stratified society of consumption and exchange.

This is the lushness. Not the green abundance of your surface world, but something **other-green**—a spectrum that registers on the edge of perception, bioluminescent in the deep spectrum, ultraviolet in its signaling. The vegetation here does not photosynthesize. It **chemosynthesizes**, it **thermosynthesizes**, it feeds on the heat differential between the warm rock and the cooler air pockets that somehow persist, on the slow seep of mineral gases from vents that have not vented in millennia because the world above is sealed, because the world *is* sealed, because this place was **closed** before it was **finished**.

The plants—if plants they are—grow in **whorls and spirals and fractal recursions** that suggest computation, that suggest intention, that suggest a biology that has evolved not toward survival but toward **complexity itself**. They are sharp. The erosion is young here, or was young, or is young in a way that time does not measure. Edges remain edges. Points remain points. Nothing has been softened by water, by wind, by the patient abrasion of centuries. The formations rise like **frozen explosions**, like growths arrested at the moment of maximum velocity.

---

## The Inhabitants

They are abundant.

You hear them before you see them—not because they hide, but because they **occupy frequencies** that your ears struggle to parse. The sound here is **loud**, a constant pressure of vibration: the **click-song** of the ceiling-dwellers, the **subsonic hum** of the floor-mat's metabolic processes, the **rasping chorus** of things moving through the middle air in patterns too fast to track. The silence, when it comes, is brief and terrible and always ends with something **new** beginning.

The fauna are **segmented**, mostly, though segmentation is a word borrowed from desperation. They have **too many joints**, **too many eyes**, **distributed nervous systems** that allow a severed portion to continue its appointed task for hours, for days. They are **small**, mostly—pocket-scale, fitting the hiddenness of the place—but some grow to the size of a dog, then a pig, then something that moves with the wrong number of limbs and leaves traces that suggest **geometry** rather than locomotion.

They do not eat each other, not exactly. They **exchange**. A creature will press against a plant-formation and receive, in return, a chemical package that alters its own chemistry, that makes it *more*, temporarily, at the cost of something else, always something else, the accounting of which is **theology**, is **economics**, is the **law of this place**.

There are **herbivores**, in the sense of things that consume the growing things. There are **filter-feeders**, hanging from the ceiling like inverted bells, straining the warm air for what it carries. There are **burrowers**, though the floor-mat resists them, absorbs them, eventually **incorporates** them. There is one thing, observed once, that **watches**. It does not move. It is the size of a fist and it has **too many angles** and it was seen to **turn**, slowly, to follow, and then the observer fled, and the observer has not returned, and the observer dreams of it still, and the dreaming is **contagion**, is **infection**, is **the way this place reproduces itself in minds**.

---

## The Light

Dim. Always dim. The phosphorescence of the lithic coral provides a **baseline**, a glow like bruised flesh, like the memory of sunset filtered through eyelids. But there are **brighter things**. The vegetation pulses on cycles that do not match any day, any night, any external rhythm—**internal clocks**, **chemical oscillators**, **communities deciding when to shine** in waves that propagate through the floor-mat like a slow fire.

In the bright moments, you can see **structure**. In the dark moments, you can only feel it—the **breathing** of the place, the expansion and contraction of the warm air, the **tides** of gas exchange that have no moon to answer to.

Some creatures carry their own light. They are the **lanterns**, the **lamps**, the **walking points of illumination** that move through the middle space in patterns that might be **foraging**, might be **mating**, might be **war**, might be **art**. You cannot tell. The distinction does not hold here.

---

## The Artifacts

Scattered. Faint. **Uncertain**.

There are **marks** on the ceiling, high where the coral is oldest, where the growth has layered over them like sediment over a drowned thing. They might be **writing**. They might be **scars**. They might be **the natural patterning of mineral deposition**, coincidence given the weight of attention. They show **repetition**, **intention**, **a hand that moved with purpose**—but the hand, if there was one, belonged to something that left no other trace, that **did not persist**, that **melted into the growing**, that **became the place** rather than leaving it.

There are **structures**, barely. A **platform** of compressed floor-mat, raised, flat, suggesting **gathering**, **ritual**, **the pause before consumption**. A **shaft**, narrow, descending from the platform into the mat itself, its walls **smoothed**, **worn**, **used**. A **cache**, perhaps, or a **grave**, or a **door** that opens onto nothing, or opens onto **everything** and we simply cannot perceive the threshold.

The tech is **primitive**, in the sense that it is **made of the place**, **of the growing things**, **of the bodies of the dead**. Tools fashioned from **chitin** and **calcified secretion** and **the hardened sap** of certain formations that weep when cut. No metal, or metal so rare that it is **myth**, is **currency**, is **the reason for the shafts that descend**. The fabrication is **biological**, **slow**, **a craft of patience** that the inhabitants do not seem to possess and yet somehow **embody**.

---

## The Rules

This place is **stable**. It has been stable. It will be stable.

The temperature holds at 34 degrees, Celsius, give or take a fraction, give or take a **cycle** that repeats with the regularity of a heartbeat, of a breath, of **something that lives so slowly we mistake it for stone**. The chemistry is **equilibrium**, **closed-loop**, **each waste product the food of another process**, each death the **beginning** of something that will not be named. There is no **gradient** to drive change, no **outside** to import difference, no **time** in the way that time is usually understood—only **duration**, only **the accumulation of small events that cancel each other**, that **preserve**.

And yet.

The growth continues. The sharp edges **do not dull**. The creatures **do not stop moving**, **do not stop singing**, **do not stop being born and dying and being born again** in forms that drift, that **explore**, that **occasionally produce something new**, something **strange**, something that **persists long enough to be noticed**, to **matter**, to **change the pattern** in ways too small to measure and yet **irreversible**.

The weirdness is **structural**. It is not decoration. It is **the consequence of pressure without release**, of **complexity without simplification**, of **a system that has forgotten how to die** and so **never learned how to live simply**, how to **be only what it needs to be**.

---

## The Descent

You have been here, perhaps, before you arrived. The place **remembers** in ways that do not require memory, that **persist** in the **shape of the growing things**, in the **chemistry of the air**, in the **dreams of those who have left**, if any have left, if leaving is **possible**, if the shaft above is **real** or **metaphor** or **the same wound seen from different angles**.

The sound is **loud** now. A **creature** passes, close, its **many legs** clicking against the lithic coral, its **body heat** a brief **warmth** against your skin. It does not stop. It does not **notice** you, or notices you as **part of the place**, as **another formation**, as **another thing that grows and consumes and will eventually be consumed**.

Above, the shaft waits. Or does not wait. **Time does not pass here**. It **accumulates**, **layers**, **becomes the ceiling**, becomes the **memory of ceiling**, becomes **the thing that was ceiling before it was anything else**.

You breathe the warm air. It tastes of **copper**, of **sweetness**, of **the particular mineral tang of a world that has never known water and therefore has never known thirst**.

You are **inside** something. You have always been **inside** something. The question is only **how deep**, **how long**, **whether the walls will ever open**, or whether they have **already closed**, and you have simply **not yet noticed**, and the noticing is **the last change this place will ever permit**, the **final gradient**, the **end of equilibrium**, the **beginning of the garden's true name**, which is **Vess**, which is **Throat**, which is **the place that holds what cannot be held**, which is **you**, which is **the sound you make when you finally understand that you were never visiting**.

The sound is **loud**.

It has always been **loud**.

It will be **loud** when you are **part of the mat**, when your **chemistry** has been **exchanged**, when your **strangeness** has been **added to the sum**, when the **new thing** that grows from you is **not you** and **never was** and **is the only you that this place has ever needed**.

The light pulses. Brighter. Darker. Brighter.

The garden **breathes**.

You breathe with it.

You have always been breathing with it.

---

*The shaft above is sealed. The shaft above was never open. The shaft above is the scar of your entry, healing, already healed, the wound that made this place possible, the wound that is this place, the wound that you carry, that carries you, that will outlast the memory of water, the memory of sky, the memory of any world that was not this, that is not this, that could not be anything but this, sealed, warm, loud, strange, eternal, **growing**.*
