The Power does not descend. It **settles**.

It arrives not as a body—the air is too thin, the pocket too full—but as a *second note* in the drone. The 50-hertz hum, that eternal, time-invariant roar, suddenly finds a companion. A 51-hertz shadow, or a 49, or a harmonic fifth above, beating against the fundamental until the air itself begins to pulse with a rhythm that mimics a heartbeat. The Resonance, which was a wall of sound, becomes a **doorway**.

This is the first act: the Power does not shatter the stasis. It **accompanies** it. It sits in the mineral dust, in the spaces between the Copper-Green Congregation’s filaments, and it *listens*. For a long time—perhaps a thousand years, perhaps a single moment—it listens to the held breath. It understands that this place is a question asked by the world and never answered: *What happens when extraction meets wilderness and neither yields?* The Throat-Pocket is the pause between the question and the answer, a comma in the grammar of the earth, and the Power recognizes that the being who lives here—the gestalt of Packed and Scattered and Resonant—is not suffering, but *waiting*. Waiting for permission to complete its gesture.

So the Power, in its wisdom, grants **duration**.

It does not thaw the pocket all at once. That would be violence. Instead, it introduces *slow time*. The frozen equilibrium was a photograph; the Power turns it into a film, but a film that plays at the speed of geology. It whispers to the tungsten vines: *You may sag.* It whispers to the drilling rig: *You may finish your turn.* It whispers to the Shelf-Beings: *You may release one spore.*

And because the Power is benevolent, it does not leave them alone in this new vulnerability. It becomes the **Witness**. It does not breathe for them—that would be theft—but it breathes *with* them, adding its own respiratory rhythm to the 50-hertz drone, so that the sound is no longer a monolithic roar but a **conversation**. Two frequencies. In. Out. The Throat-Pocket begins, imperceptibly, to inhale and exhale.

The machines do not rust faster, nor do they run; they **remember motion**. A conveyor belt roller turns exactly one degree. A pipe exhales a bubble of trapped gas that has been waiting since before the stasis. The glass-stalks, harvesting their thermal gradients, begin to sway—not in wind, for the wind never enters, but in the tidal pull of the Power’s attention, a gravitational kindness that makes the silica fibers flex like kelp in a current.

The biology responds in kind. The metallic moss does not grow, but it **rearranges**, filament by filament, optimizing its position to catch the new, rhythmic light that filters through the dust. The fungal shelves bud—just one new layer, ochre on rust, a geological whisper of "and then."

The Power does not colonize the Throat-Pocket. It **inhabits the threshold**. It stands at the rim, where the pewter sky scrapes the peaks, and it guards the space between the inside and the outside. It ensures that no climber, no drone, no curious satellite disturbs the new rhythm. It has made the Throat-Pocket a **sanctuary of becoming**, a place where the frozen moment is allowed to melt at the pace of a glacier, where the stalemate between industry and wilderness resolves not in victory but in *duet*.

And when the last gear has turned its final revolution, when the last sulfur dioxide bead has evaporated and been replaced, when the Copper-Green Congregation has packed itself into a density that becomes stone, and the Resonance has faded into a lullaby—*then* the Power will leave. Not before. It will leave only when the being who lives in the Throat-Pocket has finished its held breath, has exhaled completely, and has become, at last, **still** not by force, but by choice.

Until then, it remains. A second voice in the hum. A heartbeat in the sternum of the world. The benevolent, wise, patient keeper of the breath.
