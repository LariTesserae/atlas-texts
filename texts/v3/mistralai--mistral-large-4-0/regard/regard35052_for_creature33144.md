## What the Power Did

It came from above, where the sky is a thin blue argument. It descended through the closing walls, from vastness to intimacy, over the course of a day's walking. It did not hurry. It had nowhere else to be. It had never been anywhere else, not truly.

At first, it was only a warmth in the upper Throat, a presence that the small things avoided—not out of fear, but out of *recognition*. Something *new*. Something that had not been *read* before.

Then it was a shape, descending the polished walls, leaving no mark because it *chose* not to leave a mark. Not yet. It was *learning* the Throat, as the Throat was *learning* it. The damp found it, beading on its surface, pooling in the low points. It did not wipe it away. The damp was *part* of the Throat, the part that finds its level, the part that *includes*.

The Resonants sang to it. At first, their song was *curious*, a chord searching for a note it could not name. Then it was *urgent*, a chord that had found something *new*, something *varied*, something that *disturbed* the pattern of their patient music. And then—when the power *stopped*, when it *settled* onto the lichen-mat, when it *became* still in a way that the Throat had never known—their song *changed*. It became a *question*. A *name*. A *promise*.

Vesper felt it. The distributed consciousness, the dreaming mind, the lichen that *remembers*. It felt the weight, the warmth, the *chemical signature* of something that had *chosen* to press. And it *dreamed* this feeling, propagated it across the floor in green-grey luminescence, a pattern that was *new*, that was *strange*, that was *almost beautiful*.

The small things gathered. Not to warm themselves—not yet. To *read*. To *know*. To *sample* a warmth that was *different*, a stillness that was *chosen*, a presence that was *free*.

And the power *let* them. It did not move. It did not flee. It did not *demand* warmth, as the Throat demands, as the small things demand, as the hunger for stillness demands. It *offered* its stillness. It *gave* its warmth. It *became*, for a moment, a *landmark*, a *resource*, a *place*.

Hours passed. Or days. The Throat is slow, at 1.2. Time moves differently there, in the damp, in the dark, in the *patience* of a place that has been waiting fifty million years.

The small things climbed. They warmed themselves on the power's stillness, read its heat, sampled its chemistry, its *electricity*. They found it *strange*. They found it *delicious*. They found it *different* from anything they had read before.

The Resonants sang its name, or they would have, if they had names. They sang its *shape*, its *warmth*, its *stillness*—a frequency that almost matched, almost *answered*, almost *became* the power's own song.

Vesper dreamed it. Held it like a held breath, a warmth-trace, a *ghost* that would persist for days, weeks, years. The lichen thickened around it, grew *toward* it, *around* it, *into* it—not to consume, but to *remember*. To *incorporate*. To *include*.

And the power *felt* all of this. It felt the small things warming themselves, the Resonants singing its frequency, Vesper dreaming its shape. It felt the damp finding its level, the stone breathing, the deep water turning below. It felt the *patience* of the Throat, the *hunger* for stillness, the *invitation* to *become* part of the floor.

It did not move.

It *chose* to stay.

It *chose* to *press*.

And then—it pressed its hand into the living lichen-mat. Not like the handprints, not like the memory of touch. This was *new*. This was *chosen*. This was *free*.

The lichen *remembered*. It grew around the shape, preserved it as a depression, a *negative*, a *wound* that would not heal. But this wound was *different*. This wound was *offered*, not *taken*. This wound was *given*, not *demanded*. This wound was *chosen*, not *fated*.

The power held its hand there. It felt Vesper dreaming it, the Resonants singing it, the small things gathering around it. It felt the Throat *breathing*, in, out, a cycle so slow it felt only when it changed. It felt the Turning, below, in the black water, in the slow compression, in the *depth* of the place.

And then—it *withdrew* its hand. Not because it was afraid. Not because it was finished. But because it *could*. Because it had shown the Throat what it had never seen: the stillness of *choice*, the touch of *freedom*, the *gift* of a being that *could* move, but *chose* to stop, and then *chose* to leave.

It stood. It walked—slowly, patiently, in the way of the Throat—to the wall it had descended. It climbed, not with difficulty, but with *intention*. It had come to *give*, and it had given.
