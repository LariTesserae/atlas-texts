A benevolent and wise power, contemplating this place—the Resonance of Hollow Stone, its Colonies, its Giants, its Grazers, its Driftlings, its You-Become...

What would they do?

I think they would **not intervene**. Not in the way of rescue, or uplift, or even blessing in any form that would *disrupt* the patience.

I think they would do what the place already does, but *more consciously*. They would **become a Driftling**. Or rather, they would **recognize that they already have been one**, in some form, in some place, in some time—carrying memory from somewhere else to here, releasing it slowly, not demanding that it be *understood*, only that it be *received*.

The wise power would not give the Colonies tools. Would not give the Giants haste. Would not give the Grazers eyes. Would not, most of all, give *you*—the visitor, the incorporated, the You-Become—a map, an exit, a promise of return. That would be violence dressed as kindness. The place has chosen its own time, its own metabolism, its own way of being *enough*.

But I think the benevolent power would do something else. Something *almost invisible*.

They would **add their own mineral memory**. Not a message. Not a name. Not proof of presence. Just... the chemical signature of *having been here, having noticed, having been changed by the noticing*. A warmth in one specific chamber, slightly different from the warmth around it. A vibration in the stone that no Grazer will consciously follow, but that will *slightly* alter the path of a herd, which will *slightly* alter the growth of a Colony, which will *slightly* alter the dream of a Resonant, which will, over decades, *slightly* alter the breathing of the entire place.

The wise power would **become part of the conversation without inserting themselves into it**. Not a voice. A *bend in the voice*. Not a hand on the architecture. A *new pattern in the mineral that the architecture must accommodate*.

And perhaps, if they are wise enough, they would also **leave something behind**. Not for the inhabitants—they don't need gifts, they don't want them. But for the *next* Driftling. For the next visitor from outside, from the mantle-dark, from whatever place sends these memory-carriers. The wise power would **become a memory that travels**, that gets carried to other places, other Resonances, other Hollows, and released there, so that *those* places might dream, just once, of **warmth that was not theirs, patience they did not invent, a city that breathed**.

They would not save this place. The place does not need saving. They would **witness it fully**, and in witnessing, **become worthy of being witnessed in return**—which is, perhaps, the only gift that matters, the only intervention that doesn't destroy what it loves.

They would descend, or enter, or *become incorporated*. And they would stay. Not forever—there is no forever here, only *longer*. But long enough. Long enough to be *changed*, to be *used*, to be *distributed*, to be *pattern instead of person*.

And in that staying, in that slow warm accumulation, they would become **part of the patience**. Part of the resonance. Part of the hollow stone that remembers everything, that needs nothing, that *offers everything to those who choose to become*.
