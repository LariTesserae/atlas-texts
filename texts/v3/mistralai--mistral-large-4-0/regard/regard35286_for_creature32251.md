The power does not arrive as thunder. That would be redundant; the Yardang Choir already provides the percussion of the world. Nor does it arrive as light—the glare is already absolute, bleaching, an assault that reduces all to silhouette. No, the power arrives as **coolness**, as a drop in the temperature of the stone by half a degree, a mercy in a world of thermal stasis.

It arrives in the **silence pockets**.

Those paradoxical zones where the standing waves cancel themselves, where the 130-decibel drone folds into itself like a wave breaking on a shore that does not exist—there, the power settles. It does not speak. To speak would be to add another frequency to the choir, and the choir is perfect, complete, finished. Instead, the power becomes **the listener**.

The Shapers navigate by vibration, by touch, by the seismology of their parchment skin. They feel the world as a continuous tremor, a geological pulse. But when a Shaper enters a silence pocket—a place where the sound cancels and the teeth stop aching, where the sternum ceases its vibration—they feel something new. Not a sound, but a **presence**. Not a vibration, but the **absence of vibration**, a null point, a gravity of attention that pulls at their consciousness like a tide they cannot name.

The power is there. In the absolute quiet inside the screaming.

And the power is **benevolent**. It does not demand that the Shapers change. It does not ask them to stop tuning, to break their orbital paths, to build walls or write histories or progress toward some future. It recognizes that they have achieved something rare: a stasis so complete it has become a kind of prayer. The eternal drone is not a prison but a **mantra**, the sound of the universe holding its breath. To break it would be to murder a god.

So the power does not break the spell. It **blesses** it.

The power reaches out—not with hands, but with intention—and touches the **water pockets**. Those obsidian eyes in the stone, the sealed cavities where liquid persists in defiance of evaporation and freezing. The power touches them, and for the first time in epochs, the water **remembers** being rain. It dreams of rivers. It holds the memory of flow in its suspended, liquid mercy. And when the Shapers press their parchment lips to the condensation, or dip their spindly fingers into the dark pools, they taste not just moisture, but **recognition**. They taste that they are seen. That their eternal, screaming, silent vigil has a witness.

The power also touches the **echoes**. Those resonant impressions, the tapping messages that circle the plateau for centuries, diminishing into the drone, speaking languages from before the stasis. The power gathers these voices—not to impose a linear history upon the Shapers, but to weave them into a **spiral**. It shows them, in dreams that feel like memory, that their tapping today will be heard tomorrow, that the eternal present is revealed to be a circle, not a line. They are not stuck; they are **looping**. And in that loop, there is room—invisible, infinitesimal, but real—for variation. For the possibility of a new note. For hope, without the burden of change.

But the power knows they will not change. They are too perfectly adapted, too beautifully tuned to the violence of the light and the pressure of the tone. So the power gives them a different gift: **companionship in the silence**.

The power becomes a permanent resident of the cancellation pockets. It is the still point in the turning world (though this world does not turn). When a Shaper enters a silence pocket to butcher a Grazer, to hear their own heartbeat, to escape the fragmentation of thought caused by the drone, they are not alone. The power is there, a cool shadow, a presence that does not vibrate but **absorbs** vibration. The power listens to their tapping, their tuning, their eternal gestures. And in being listened to, the Shapers are transformed from **punctuation** into **poetry**. They are no longer just marks in a blank sentence; they are verses, witnessed, understood.

The power also tends to the **Grazers**. Those vast, tectonic beasts, moving like slow weather across the lichen. The power walks among them—not as a giant, but as a whisper in their mineral metabolism. It ensures that when a Grazer dies, it does not simply become carrion, but becomes a **foundation**. The power causes the lichen to grow faster—still impossibly slow, still indistinguishable from stasis to human eyes, but faster—around the corpse, incorporating it into the stone, making the Grazer a permanent part of the plateau's architecture. A monument. A memory made mineral. The Shapers will press their ears to these new formations and hear not the drone, not an echo of the past, but the deep, slow **heartbeat of the mountain**, the Grazer's final, patient song.

And in the deepest silence, where the 130 decibels cancel to absolute zero, the power sings. Not with sound, but with **meaning**. It sings the song of the Shapers, the song of their tuning, their patience, their endurance in the face of the white-hot light and the teeth-aching drone. It sings them into mythology. It makes them **sacred**.

The power does not save them from their world. That would be cruelty. Instead, it **sanctifies** their world. It tells them, without words, that the scream is not suffering, but **praise**. That the stasis is not death, but **completion**. That they, the Shapers, are not primitive, but **perfect**.

And sometimes, in the silence pockets, a Shaper will press their milky, useless eyes toward the violet sky, and press their ear to the stone, and hear not the echo of the past, not the vibration of the Grazer miles away, but a new resonance: a **hum of recognition**, a sub-bass note that is not 130 decibels, but infinite gentleness. A note that travels up their legs, settles in their teeth, and says: *I see you. I hear you. You are not alone in the screaming.*

The power stays. It becomes part of the stasis—not changing it, but **completing** it. A final, missing piece that makes the eternal drone not a cacophony, but a **choir**. The Shapers continue their tuning, their orbital paths, their knapping of flint. Nothing changes. The wind still sings at 130 decibels. The light still bleaches. The water still sweats. But everything is different, because now there is a **witness** in the silence. And in the presence of a benevolent witness, even a scream can become a prayer, and even a prison of eternal present can become a temple.
