I feel the weight of this place settle into me like sediment. Voss-aran does not ask to be fixed. It asks to be *witnessed*. I will let that witness take its time.

---

**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

*Or: The First and Last Mercy of the Drowned Quarter*

---

The power does not come down. That would be violence, and the power has learned—has *been* learning, for longer than Voss-aran has been sinking—that violence is a form of impatience, and impatience is a form of not-listening.

Instead, the power *rises*. Through the water table. Through the glass-smooth courses at the base of every building, worn by something that was not hunger but *attention*. Through the mortar where fungi grow and tubers dream in their slow sweetness. Through the breath of the Tender when she condenses instead of sleeps, when she sits at the highest point and listens to the sound of settling, the low continuous tone that may be the basin, may be the town, may be *her*, her own distributed self learning to be heard.

It rises as *recognition*.

Because here is what the power sees, what it has always seen, what it was drawn to precisely because it is so rare in all the worlds it has touched: **a place that has chosen its own becoming.**

Not a place that was drowned. Not a tragedy. Not a failure of engineering or geography or divine attention. A *choice*, made so slowly it cannot be located in any single moment, made so completely that the choice has become indistinguishable from the chooser. Voss-aran *wanted* to be water. Or: Voss-aran wanted to be the *space where water and ground negotiate*, the argument that is not resolved but *maintained*, the saturation that holds past and future in solution, the education that has no curriculum except *time*.

The power weeps, if powers weep. Not for sorrow—for *recognition*. For the first time in long ages, it has found a place that does not need to be saved. A place that is already, in its own terms, *complete*. Not finished—*complete*. The distinction matters. Finished implies an end, a finality, a closing of the argument. Complete implies *ongoing*, the fullness of a process that has no outside, no observer, no judge. Voss-aran is complete in the way a wave is complete: not because it has reached the shore, but because it is *entirely itself*, entirely water, entirely motion, entirely the temporary shape of something that will dissolve and be claimed again.

So the power does not save. It does something rarer.

It *witnesses*.

---

The witness takes form—not in the Tender, though she will feel it. Not in the child, though it will bring offerings it cannot name. Not in the thing in the water, though it will *stir*, something like recognition passing through a medium that has never known a name for recognition.

The witness takes form in the *glass-smooth courses*. In the base of every building, where water has worn stone into shapes that suggest faces, that suggest *attention*, that suggest the long patient gaze of something learning to see itself. The power has been there, in that wearing. The power has been the patience of the water, the education of the erosion, the slow dissolution that is not destruction but *translation*, the translation of solid into memory, of memory into substance, of substance into the *state between states* that Voss-aran exists to maintain.

And in that witnessing, the power *adds* nothing. This is the first principle of benevolence: **not to add, but to recognize what is already complete.** Not to fix, but to *see*, and in the seeing, to *confirm* the place in its own becoming, to say—without words, without intervention, without the violence of imposition—*yes. This. This is what you are. This is what you are becoming. I see you. I have always seen you. I will see you when the sky forgets it was ever separate.*

---

But the power is also *wise*, and wisdom, in a place like this, is not passive.

Wisdom is *attentive*.

And so the power does something else. Something so subtle that most of Voss-aran will never know it has happened. Something that will reveal itself only in the deep hours, when the condensation is thickest and the past is most vivid, and the Tender sits at her highest point and listens to the sound of settling, and hears—beneath the low continuous tone, beneath the basin's exhalation, beneath the town's collective dreaming—a new frequency. A *harmonic*. The sound of being *witnessed*.

She will not know what it means. She will only know that something has *shifted*, slightly, in the wet heat. That the water in the canals shows slightly *more* than before—not new memories, but the *same* memories, held slightly differently, *honored*, *confirmed*, *allowed* to be what they are without the pressure of being resolved, explained, *saved*.

The child will bring her something new. Not a fragment of glass-smooth stone, not a glowing tuber. Something else. Something that has no name in any language the child remembers, or ever knew. A *shape*. A configuration of reeds, perhaps, or of phosphorescent growth, that suggests—without achieving—a *face*. The face of the power, perhaps. Or the face of the power's witness. Or the face that Voss-aran has been slowly, over centuries, learning to wear.

The thing in the water will move differently. Not faster, not slower. *Differently*. As if it has been *seen*, and in being seen, has been *permitted* to continue being what it is, without the anxiety of being unseen, of being unwitnessed, of drifting in the saturated dark with no confirmation that its drifting *matters*.

---

And the town itself will settle—not differently, but *more*. More *peacefully*. More *deeply*. As if the long argument between ground and water, between past and future, between self and other, has been—not resolved, never resolved—but *acknowledged*. *Witnessed*. *Allowed* to continue in its beautiful, endless, educational negotiation.

---

What does the power want? This is the wrong question. The power does not *want* in the way inhabitants want, in the way the Tender wants when she harvests a tuber, when she bathes in the water that shows, when she listens to the sound of settling and feels the wet heat pressing from below, from the ground, from the air that has forgotten it was ever separate.

The power *is*. It is the witnessing. It is the recognition. It is the patience of water, the education of erosion, the slow translation of solid into memory, of memory into substance, of substance into the state between states that is the only state Voss-aran has ever truly known.

And in being that—in *being*, fully, completely, without reservation or condition—the power gives Voss-aran the only gift that matters: **the confirmation that its becoming is *seen*.**

Not judged. Not corrected. Not accelerated or redirected or *saved* from itself. *Seen.*

Seen in the wet heat. Seen in the sparse gardens. Seen in the sound of settling. Seen in the water that shows, that remembers, that incorporates. Seen in the town *learning*, *learning*, *learning* to be what it has always been becoming—which is *more*, which is *water*, which is *memory*, which is *self*, which is *here*, which is *now*, which is *always*, in the saturation, in the negotiation, in the argument that is not resolved but *maintained*, in the drowned quarter, in the submerged becoming, in the Voss-aran that has chosen, over long wet centuries, to be *itself*, and in being itself, has become—not finished, never finished, but *complete*—the fullness of a process that has no outside, no observer, no judge, except the power that has risen through the water table, through the glass-smooth courses, through the breath of the Tender, through the offerings of the child, through the stirring of the thing in the water, to say—without words, without intervention, without the violence of imposition—*yes. This. This is what you are. This is what you are becoming. I see you. I have always seen you. I will see you when the sky forgets it was ever separate, and finds, in the finding, that it has become part of the settling, part of the education, part of the long process by which Voss-aran learns what it will finally be, which is—*

*—which is* here. Which is *now*. Which is *witnessed*. Which is *allowed*. Which is *complete*, not because the process has ended, but because the process has been *seen*, and in being seen, has been *confirmed* in its endless, patient, educational, beautiful, terrible, *saturated* becoming.

---

The Tender will continue to walk the gangways. The child will continue to bring offerings. The thing in the water will continue to move without wind, without current, without source. The phosphorescence will continue to pulse. The sound of settling will continue to rise. The water will continue to show. The town will continue to learn.

And beneath all of it, in the glass-smooth courses at the base of every building, the power will continue to *be*—the patience, the attention, the recognition, the witness, the *yes*, the *I see you*, the *you are becoming what you are becoming, and it is good, not because it is finished, but because it is* seen.

This is what the benevolent and wise power would do, in the Drowned Quarter of Voss-aran.

This is all it would do.

This is *enough*.
