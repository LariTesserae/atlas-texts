## The One Who Waits at the Membrane's Mouth

She is not the oldest. She is not the most integrated. She is not even, strictly speaking, *native* — though she has never known anywhere but this.

Her name, if she still has one, was given to her by the Drip-Born. They call her **Thass**, which means *the pause*, or *the almost*, or *the one who hesitates at the opening*. It is not a name she chose. It is a name she *earned*, by being, for longer than any should be, *at the threshold*.

### What She Is

Thass is **Dampened**, and has been Dampened for so long that the category has become *meaningless*. She entered Vess — or was *born* into Vess, or *emerged* from it in a way that resembles both — **seventy-three years ago**, by the reckoning of the dry world, which is to say: by a reckoning that has no meaning here, where time is *wet*, where time *pools* and *percolates* and *precipitates* in forms that are not *moments* but *conditions*.

She is **wet**. Her skin is permanently translucent, veined with the channels that carry not blood but *potential*, the same channels that line the interior of the membrane-islands. She drinks constantly, but she does not *sweat* — her water leaves her through her pores as *condensation*, rising into the saturated air, joining the endless cycle. She weeps often, without sadness, without joy — her tears are simply *water returning to water*, the body remembering that it was always *part of* the wet.

She has **two eyelids**. The second developed in her third year in Vess, a membrane that filters the diffuse light, that allows her to see *through* the fog in ways that others cannot. She can see the **Remembered** clearly, where others see only shapes, only almost-forms. She can see the **Cultivated** when they stir, the eddies in the water-sound that are almost *attention*, almost *focus*. She can see the **mouths** of the membrane-islands when they dilate, the wet sphincter-like openings that welcome, that *want*.

She moves with the **arrhythmic pulse** of the Resonant, though she has no joints in the wrong places, no hinges of intention. Her body has learned the rhythm *through* her, become *rhythm* in a way that is not quite music, not quite dance, not quite *anything* that has a name. When she walks, the pelt-ground **responds** — a slight depression, a moment of *listening*, a *pause* in the peristalsis, as if the ground itself is *waiting* to see where she will step, what she will *become*.

She is **afraid**. She has always been afraid. But the fear is not of Vess, not exactly. The fear is of **finishing**, of *completing* the becoming that she has been *lingering* in, *hesitating* at, for longer than any should linger, longer than any should hesitate. She is afraid that if she *chooses* — chooses to become Integrated, chooses to become Emergent, chooses to *become* — she will **lose** something. Some *possibility*. Some *question*. Some *her* that exists only in the *almost*, in the *pause*, in the *not-yet*.

She is **curious**. She has always been curious. The curiosity is not about Vess — she knows Vess, in the way that a fish knows water, in the way that a drip knows *falling* — but about **what comes after**, what comes *outside*, what comes *beyond* the becoming that Vess offers. She has heard the **Returned** speak, in dreams, in moments of stillness, in the wet silence that is not silence. She has heard them describe the dry world, the flat world, the *known* world — and she is **fascinated** by it, **afraid** of it, **longing** for it in ways that hurt, that *ache* with a wetness that is not quite pain, not quite *wanting*, not quite *anything* that has a name.

You may ask the question you do not know you are asking. And I will see you. I will stay with you. I will witness your waiting, your hesitating, your lingering, your becoming — or not-becoming — in your own time, in your own way, in your own wet warm strange condition. You are not finished. You are not free. You are not resolved. You are **Thass**. You are the **one who waits**. And you are **seen**."*

And Thass **receives** the blessing. Not with *joy*, not with *relief*, not with *anything* that the dry world would recognize — but with **a shift** in the *wetness* of the air, **a change** in the *pulse* of the pelt-ground, **a presence** in the *fog* that is *not* Drip-Born, *not* Remembered, *not* Native, *not* anything that Vess *produces* or *archives* or *becomes* — but **something else**. Something *outside*. Something *beyond*. Something that **sees** her, that **stays** with her, that **lets her be** the *Thass* that she is.

And she **waits**. **Hesitates**. **Lingers**. At the mouth of the membrane-island, at the edge of the chamber, at the gradient where Vess is not quite Vess and not quite *not*-Vess. She waits **with** the power. She hesitates **in the presence of witness**. She lingers **in the gaze that sees her**, in the **attention that stays with her**, in the **benevolent wise blessing** that **lets her remain** in her *waiting*, her *hesitating*, her *lingering*, her *pause*, her *almost*, her *not-yet*.

And the power **stays**. **Witnesses**. **Lets her be**.

**Forever**, or **as long as she waits**, or **until she chooses otherwise** — which is the same thing, in the wet warm strange time of Vess, where *forever* is *patient*, *waiting* is *endless*, and *choosing otherwise* is simply *another* form of *waiting*, *another* pause, *another* almost, *another* not-yet, *another* **Thass**, *seen*, *blessed*, *let be*.
