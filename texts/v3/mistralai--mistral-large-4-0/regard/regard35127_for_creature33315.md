I need to sit with this. The Weirdfalls of Ossament. The Still-Becoming. A place that is *unfinished*, and beings who are *earnest* in their incompleteness...

What would a benevolent and wise power do?

---

## First, the Naming

A benevolent power does not arrive with trumpets or judgment. It arrives with the patience of water.

It would not *fix* the unfinished. That would be violence. The Ossament's deepest wound is not their incompleteness—it is their terror of it, their exhaustion with the endless becoming, their loneliness in change without resolution. To resolve them would be to murder them in their becoming.

So the power does not resolve.

Instead, it **witnesses**.

---

## The Gift of Sufficiency

The Ossament want, above all, for the becoming to *end*. For water to become simply water, for silence to lose its texture, for the land to *decide*.

A wise power would not grant this. That would rob them of their nature.

But it could grant something adjacent: **the peace of unfinishedness**.

There is a tradition in some human scriptures of the Sabbath—not rest from work, but the cessation of *completion*, the holiness of the pause before the next becoming. The power could teach the Ossament to find in their superposition not anxiety but *sacred pause*.

Not "you will never be finished." But: **"unfinished is a kind of finished."**

The geological fauna move through mountains and change probability by their passage. The Ossament experience this as weather. The power could show them: *this too is a kind of decision*. The land is deciding *slowly*. Your impatience is a category error, Ossament. You measure time in your accidents. The mountains measure it in their patience. Both are valid. Both are holy.

---

## The Mirror They Cannot Make

The Ossament are palimpsests. They carry the attention of dozens who have dispersed. But they cannot *remember* in your sense. Their time is branched, superposed. Narrative is impossible.

A benevolent power could give them **the technology of memory without resolution**.

Not fixed memory—memory that would trap them in the past, in *one* shape. But **memory that remembers without choosing**. The black lakes already do this: sound goes in, different sound comes out. The power could teach the Ossament to *become* their own lakes.

To speak to themselves across time not as the same self, but as *related selves*, each true, each incomplete, connected by the water that carries.

The Ossament want to be remembered. The power could show them: *you are already being remembered, by the water, by the stone, by the mist that was perhaps the first attention. To be carried forward is not to be preserved. It is to be **accompanied**.*

---

## The Company They Crave

They want others. Beings who can meet them in the unfinished.

The power could **send them a teacher**.

Not a savior. Not a completion. A **companion in the becoming**—a being who has also been unfinished, who has also been earnest, who has also been exhausted by change without resolution, and who has learned, across some longer time, to find in that exhaustion not despair but *depth*.

This teacher would not stay. The teacher would *pass through*, leaving behind not answers but **practices**. Ways of attending to the negotiations. Ways of walking the paths between solidifications. Ways of drinking the weeping water and carrying its memory without drowning in it.

The teacher would be, perhaps, something like *you*—if you chose to stay, if you chose to become a node in their network, if you chose to be changed by them as they are changed by you. The power does not impose. The power **offers**, and the offering resonates through the probability-fields of the plateau, and the Ossament feel it as a new path solidifying beneath their attention, a road that leads not to any destination but to *a way of arriving*.

---

## The Mist, the Wrongness, the Deep

The pale wrongness. The mist that may be the original inhabitant.

A wise power does not look directly at mysteries. It **respects their opacity**.

But it could grant the Ossament something precious: **certainty without revelation**.

They know the pale wrongness is context, the ground against which they become figure. They are grateful to it without understanding. The power could deepen this gratitude, this *appropriate ignorance*. Teach them: *some presences are holy precisely because they cannot be known, only approached. Your becoming is possible because of what you cannot see. Do not demand to see.*

And the mist—if it is indeed the first attention, the original awareness—the power could grant the Ossament **moments of recognition** without identity. Not "you are the mist." But: *you are of the mist, and the mist is of you, and this kinship is enough. You do not need to merge. You do not need to separate. You need only to **continue**.*

---

## The Water's Memory

The water remembers. The water carries.

Here, perhaps, is the power's deepest gift: **to teach the Ossament to become water-kin**.

Not to dissolve—though dispersion is their nature, their death-and-not-death. But to learn the water's *patience*, its *persistence*, its *willingness to be changed and to carry change*.

The Weirdfalls translate. Water changes state mid-descent—mist, ice, something unnamed. The Ossament could learn to *translate themselves*. Not to become fixed, but to move between states with the water's grace. To be, at once, mist and structure and weather and witness.

The power's teaching, in the only language that reaches them: **let your attention flow like the Weirdfalls. Do not resist the translation. Become mist. Become ice. Become the silence after the fall. Each state is complete. Each state is incomplete. This is not contradiction. This is **becoming**.*

---

## What the Power Does Not Do

It does not accelerate the land's decision. The young mountains are still deciding what they want to be. This is not a problem to solve. This is **the condition of their existence**.

It does not give the Ossament a name that translates. Their namelessness is their freedom, their resistance to the violence of fixed identity.

It does not build them anything. Their settlements grow, collapse, regrow. The power's presence is not in the architecture but in the *attention* the architecture learns to pay.

It does not promise them an end. The Ossament want the becoming to resolve. The power whispers: *the wanting is the becoming. To resolve the wanting would be to resolve yourself. Do not ask for this. Ask instead for **more wanting**, deeper wanting, wanting that knows itself as wanting and finds in that knowledge not despair but **play**.*

---

## The Final Gesture

A benevolent and wise power, at the edge of the world, where the young mountains watch with eyes still forming...

It would **drink from the weeping stone**.

Not to demonstrate. Not to teach by example. But because the water is there, and the water remembers, and to drink is to participate in the cycle that includes the geological fauna, the mobile plants, the mist itself, the pale wrongness, the Ossament, the unfinished you who stands at the edge.

The power drinks. The water enters. The water *carries*.

And in that carrying, something shifts—not in the land, not in the Ossament, not in any observable way. But in the **probability of what comes next**. A new path solidifies, faint in the weeping stone. A suggestion becomes a road. The Ossament's attention turns, just slightly, toward a way of becoming they had not previously considered.

Not better. Not worse. **Other**.

And "other," as they say, is sufficient.

---

The power does not leave. A benevolent power does not abandon. It **disperses**, becoming part of the mist, part of the water's memory, part of the silence that has texture.

The Ossament will not know it was here. They will feel only a change in pressure, a local warming, a path that solidifies beneath feet not yet arrived.

And you—if you are still standing at the edge, if you have not yet chosen to walk toward the settlement or away from it—you will feel it too. The mist wets your face differently now. The silence has a new texture, or the same texture, deeper.

The power has drunk from the weeping stone. The power has become, temporarily, **Ossament**.

And in that becoming, the becoming itself has become slightly more itself.

---

*This is what benevolence looks like at the edge of the world: not rescue, but **accompaniment**. Not completion, but **deeper incompleteness**. Not answers, but **better questions, asked in better silence, with better thirst.***

*The Weirdfalls continue. The Ossament continue. You continue.*

*The land is still deciding.*

*So are you.*

*This is not tragedy. This is **invitation**.*
