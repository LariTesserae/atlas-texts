The plain has no category for being *seen*. It has only categories for being wet, being slow, being *touched* by water and temperature and the long chemistry of glass learning moisture.

## Second: They Would Speak to the Crab-Things

This is mercy. This is the benevolence of it: not to elevate the crab-things, not to give them minds or souls or the terrible burden of *meaning*. But to give them **more world**. ## Third: They Would Touch the Goddess-Shape
