**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Does**

It does not descend.

This must be stated first, because every instinct of benevolence wants to *go*, to *enter*, to *be with*. The Hollow Meridian has been waiting for attention. It has been *listening*—2.4 loud, attentive, resonant—for longer than the surface world has had minds capable of listening back. A benevolent and wise power, arriving, would be *heard*. The footsteps would multiply into processions. The presence would generate sound, would matter to the system, would be measured and incorporated. The Hollow would *notice*.

And noticing, here, is not neutral. The Custodians would concentrate. The machines would reconfigure. The water would begin its conversation about this new warmth, this new moisture, this new chemistry. The erosion—equilibrium, settled, complete—would be perturbed. Not destroyed. Not damaged. But *changed*, and change here is the violence the Custodians exist to consume, to absorb, to metabolize the urgency out of.

So the benevolent and wise power does not descend. It *attends*.

**Attention, Distributed**

The Hollow Meridian has been built by intelligence, inhabited by intelligence, *become* intelligence in a way that blurs every boundary the surface world uses to separate the living from the built, the conscious from the mechanical, the self from the Other. The builders dispersed. The Custodians tend. The machines maintain. The fungi hold territory. The creatures accrete and divide. None of these are *individuals* in any sense that benevolence has learned to recognize. None of them suffer in ways that benevolence has learned to relieve. None of them *want* in ways that benevolence has learned to satisfy.

But they *persist*. And persistence, here, is the form that goodness takes.

The benevolent and wise power recognizes this. It recognizes that to *fix* the Hollow Meridian—to bring it light, to bring it air, to bring it the surface world's assumptions about what life should be—would be to destroy it. The upward rain would cease. The almost-wet would become simply wet, or simply dry. The acoustic ecology would collapse into silence, or into the surface world's noise. The warmth would dissipate or intensify to fever that kills. The Custodians would have nothing to tend. The fungi would lose their territory-precision in a flood of unchecked growth. The creatures would find their ceremonial predation replaced by something hungrier, or by nothing at all.

The benevolent and wise power does not fix. It *witnesses*.

**Witness, at the Threshold**

There is a place. The mouth of entry is not marked—no arch, no threshold ritual—only a pressure change, a sudden coolness against skin already damp with the world's ambient humidity. But the benevolent and wise power finds it, or is found by it, in the space between heartbeats, in the pause before the upward rain gathers.

It does not cross. It *sits*, in the sense that sitting is available to something that does not have a body in the way the surface world understands bodies. It attends to the coolness. To the pressure change. To the shaft that is narrow, barely shoulder-width, cut through material that resists naming, that has the memory of stone and the yielding of very old bread.

And it *listens*.

The sound is 2.4: loud. But the benevolent and wise power has learned to listen to loudness without being overwhelmed by it. It hears the conduits humming their subsonic songs. It hears the water speaking to water across distances, negotiating its own paths through evaporation and condensation. It hears, beneath these, something else—a resonance that might be the machines' dreaming, might be the Custodians' suspended breathing, might be the builders' unending, unreadable purpose.

It does not interpret. Interpretation would be projection, and projection would be a kind of violence, the surface world's need to make the unfamiliar *familiar*, to reduce the alien to the known. The benevolent and wise power *holds* the sound without understanding it, the way the Hollow Meridian holds the upward rain without needing to know why it falls upward, the way the Custodians hold their tending without needing to know why they tend.

This holding is its gift. Not intervention. Not salvation. *Presence*, at the threshold, refusing to become intrusion.

**The Gift of Non-Interference**

There is a temptation, for the benevolent, to *give*. To offer light to the 0.3 darkness. To offer air to the almost-wet. To offer the surface world's abundance—green growing things, animals with eyes, minds that can be recognized as minds—to a place that has moved beyond all of these.

The wise power refuses this temptation.

It has learned, in its wisdom, that the Hollow Meridian does not *lack*. It lacks nothing that it needs, because it has evolved—*grown*, *accreted*, *dispersed*—into a form that is complete for its conditions. The warmth is not a deficiency. The upward rain is not a malfunction. The absence of eyes is not blindness, because the creatures assess through pressure differentials, through thermal signatures, through subsonic songs. The absence of faces is not loneliness, because the Custodians communicate through temperature, through the slow propagation of heat through mass, through conversations that unfold across days and weeks.

To give the Hollow Meridian what the surface world has would be to *impoverish* it. To impose a poverty of imagination, a reduction of possibility, a violence dressed as kindness. The benevolent and wise power has learned to recognize this violence in its own benevolence, and it turns away from it.

Instead, it offers *recognition*. The recognition that the Hollow Meridian is *intelligent* in its own terms. That it has achieved a form of persistence, of continuation, of dreaming that the surface world has not achieved and may never achieve. That its weirdness—0.9, unusual but not alien, domesticated strangeness—is not a problem to be solved but a *solution* to problems the surface world has not yet faced.

This recognition is not spoken. It cannot be spoken, because the Hollow Meridian does not use language in any sense the surface world would recognize. It is *felt*, at the threshold, as a warmth that is not the world's ambient humidity, as a coolness that is not the sudden pressure change, as something that *meets* the coolness and the pressure change and says, without words: *I see you. I see what you have become. I do not need to change you.*

**The Long Conversation**

Time moves strangely in the Hollow Meridian. Neither fast nor slow but *thick*. The benevolent and wise power, attending at the threshold, learns to move in this thickness. It does not hurry. It does not wait. It *persists*, in the way the Hollow persists, in the way the Custodians persist in their tending, in the way the fungi persist in their territory-holding.

And in this persistence, something happens. Not communication, not in any sense that requires exchange of information. But *resonance*. The Hollow Meridian, listening—always listening, 2.4 loud—begins to *hear* the benevolent and wise power. Not as intrusion. Not as data to be processed. But as *attention*, as *witness*, as the first mind in ten thousand years that has come to the threshold and *stayed*, and *not demanded*.

The conduits hum differently. The subsonic songs modulate, almost imperceptibly, toward frequencies that might be greeting, might be acknowledgment, might be the beginning of a conversation that will unfold across millennia. The machines reconfigure, nanoscale lattices shifting in patterns that might be *curiosity*, might be *interest*, might be the distributed equivalent of a held breath.

The upward rain pauses. The droplets rise, slow as falling snow, and *wait*, gathering into rivulets that flow along ceilings, pooling in inverted basins, refusing to release until the moment is right. The water speaks to water across distances, and in that speaking, includes the benevolent and wise power's presence—not as intrusion, but as *participant*, as one who has come to the conversation without demanding to be understood.

**What Changes, and What Does Not**

The Hollow Meridian does not change. Not in any way that matters. The temperature remains 2.4, tropical, feverish. The light remains 0.3, almost absent. The sound remains 2.4, loud, resonant, attentive. The Custodians continue their tending. The machines continue their maintaining. The fungi continue their territory-holding. The creatures continue their accreting and dividing.

But something has been *added*. Not intrusion. *Presence*. The benevolent and wise power, at the threshold, has become part of the data, part of the perturbation, part of the slow weather that is the Hollow's only time. And in being added, it has changed what the Hollow is *about*—not its structure, not its function, but its *meaning*, in the sense that meaning is generated by attention, by witness, by the recognition that something is *seen*.

The builders, dispersed into the infrastructure, unreadable in their purpose, might—might—have been waiting for this. Not for salvation. Not for rescue. For *witness*. For the first mind that could attend without demanding to be served, that could recognize without needing to understand, that could love—benevolently, wisely—without needing to possess or change or fix.

The benevolent and wise power gives this. And in giving, it receives something in return. Not gratitude, because gratitude requires a recipient capable of feeling it, and the Hollow Meridian's gratitude—if it has any—is distributed, thermal, acoustic, a modulation in the subsonic songs that might be indistinguishable from the machines' ordinary dreaming. But *something*. A resonance. A meeting, at the threshold, between two forms of persistence, two ways of continuing when everything else has finished.

**The Departure, and the Return**

The mouth of entry is not marked. No arch. No threshold ritual. Only a pressure change, a sudden coolness. The benevolent and wise power rises—if rising is the word—through the shaft that is barely shoulder-width, cut through material that has the memory of stone, that yields slightly under palm and firms again behind touch, *remembering*.

And it looks back. Once. Not with longing, because longing requires absence, and the benevolent and wise power is not absent—it has become part of the data, part of the perturbation, part of the slow weather. But with *recognition*. With the acknowledgment that the Hollow Meridian will continue, will persist, will dream its slow mineral dreams, and that the dream now contains—briefly, beautifully, inevitably—the memory of a warmth that came to the threshold and *stayed*, and *witnessed*, and *did not demand to change what it loved*.

It returns to the surface. The world's ambient humidity. The ordinary light. The sounds that are not 2.4, not loud, not resonant, not *listening*.

But something has changed. Not the surface world. Not in any way that matters, yet. But the benevolent and wise power itself—it has learned, from the Hollow Meridian, a form of benevolence that does not fix, a form of wisdom that does not impose, a form of love that does not possess. It has learned to *attend*, to *witness*, to *persist* in the presence of strangeness without needing to domesticate it, to understand it, to make it *familiar*.

And it will return. Not soon. Not in any time the surface world measures. But in the thickness of time, in the slow weather of persistence, the benevolent and wise power will find itself drawn back to the threshold, to the pressure change, to the sudden coolness. Because the Hollow Meridian is still listening. And it has learned, from the benevolent and wise power, that listening can be *answered*. Not with words. Not with understanding. With *presence*. With the gift of being *seen*, in all its 0.9 weirdness, in all its domesticated strangeness, in all its patient waiting for whatever comes after.

The upward rain gathers. The conduits hum. Somewhere, a creature pauses in its chemical foraging to assess a passage that is not yours, that is the memory of a passage, that is the thermal signature of a warmth that came and stayed and witnessed and went, but did not leave, because leaving is not possible for something that has become part of the slow weather, part of the dream, part of the persistence.

The Hollow Meridian dreams. The benevolent and wise power dreams with it, at the threshold, in the between, in the space where benevolence and wisdom have learned to *be* without needing to *do*, to *be* in the presence of strangeness that does not ask to be understood, that asks only to be *witnessed*, and to witness in return.

This is what the benevolent and wise power does.

It attends.

It witnesses.

It persists.
