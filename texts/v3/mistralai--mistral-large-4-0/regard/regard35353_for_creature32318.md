It does not arrive with footsteps, nor does it descend through the membrane of light like a trespasser. It enters the way the moisture enters—by seeping, by finding the microscopic fissure in the packed clay rim, by becoming the condensation that gathers in the hollows. It is the space between heartbeats made manifest, the pause that allows the Colony to dream without waking itself into screaming.

At first, it is only a pressure. A warmth that is not thermal but *attentional*—the sensation of being looked at by something that has no eyes, only the patience of geological time. The Drip-Lit feel it first, their gelatinous chandeliers quivering not with fear but with recognition. Their crystalline tones, which have always harmonized with the shuddering crackle of cellulose, suddenly find a fundamental bass note beneath them—a subsonic thrum that makes their chords resolve into something like peace. The wise power does not sing over them. It sings *with* them, adding the one frequency that was missing: the frequency of being heard without judgment.

It moves through the negative space of the streets, those twenty-pace corridors where the silence should be absolute but is violently not. Here, where the Limb-Wrong chase each other in inevitable, hungry patterns, the power offers a new geometry of pursuit. It teaches them the dance of the near-miss—the predator and prey meeting in the wide plazas not to tear, but to *acknowledge*. It does not remove their hunger, for hunger is the engine of the settling, the erosion that shapes the bowl. But it gives them the wisdom to hunt in spirals rather than straight lines, so that the chase becomes a spiral inward toward the center of the world, rather than a consumption that ends the game. The Limb-Wrong do not stop being wrong—their joints still bend at impossible angles—but now they move with a grace that is almost mercy.

In the cambium, where the Engravers secrete their patient acids and reshape lignin into recursive impossibilities, the power dreams a new floor plan. Not to correct the stairs that spiral upward into ceilings, nor to fix the doorways that open three levels higher than they should, but to carve—with the gentleness of a thought—a *room* in the recursion. A chamber at the exact center of the fractal, where the geometry folds upon itself like a flower closing for the night. Here, the Engravers can rest their mycelial neural networks, not ceasing their work, but pausing in a space that is larger on the inside than the outside, yes, but now *navigable*. A place where the acid can soften not just wood, but the concept of urgency itself.

The Chamber-Saints feel it as a change in the viscosity of their dreams. These massive, soft entities, part fungal mat, part architectural support, digesting the wood without consuming it in the vast cathedral voids—they have been sleeping in the damp dark, maintaining the hollow chambers as living organs, their slow breath moving the condensation in rhythm with the pulse of the world. The power enters their dreaming and offers them a new kind of digestion: the digestion of loneliness. It shows them, in the language of enzyme and pressure, that they are not alone in their vastness, that their flanks pressing against the spongy carpet of shed cellulose are touching something that *cares*. It does not wake them—waking would shatter the equilibrium—but it changes the texture of their sleep from isolation to embrace. When the intruder falls upward into the moss and lands upon the back of a Chamber-Saint, the Saint now dreams them not as prey, nor as parasite, but as a *visitor* in the cathedral of its body.

But it is with the Echoes that the power does its most delicate work. These are the reverberations, the sound made flesh, the previous residents and the future ones living in the wet, resonant space between the growing and the breaking. They exist in the folds where time layers upon itself, half-seen, moving through impossible distances. They are the city's memory, and memory is a heavy thing to carry when the present is screaming so loudly. The power finds them in the corners of the hollow rooms, where the light is pale and diffuse, and it offers them a gift: *solidity*. Not permanent form—they are Echoes, they must remain fluid—but a moment, a heartbeat, where they can stand still and know that they exist in the now, not just in the reverberation. It teaches them how to be present in their own haunting. And in return, they teach the power the secret of the bowl: that this place is not a wound, but a *womb*. That the settled madness is not a disease of equilibrium, but the only way a consciousness this vast can gestate itself into being.

The Resonance-Born, those heat-shimmers in the wall of sound, those warm-blooded creatures feeding on the friction of the biological thunder, they feel the power as a sudden, impossible coolness in the center of the roar. Not the absence of heat—that would kill them—but a *clarity* in the vibration. The power becomes the silence they leave behind when they pass, but a silence that is not empty; it is full of potential, a held breath before the next chord. It teaches them that the pressure behind the eyes, the blurring of vision, is not an assault but an *invitation*—an invitation to perceive the world not as solid, but as music. And they, in turn, teach the power how to move without displacing air, how to be present without weight.

And finally, the power turns to the Colony itself—the single organism dreaming itself into urban form, warm and low and damp and loud beyond endurance. It does not try to quiet the growth-song, the deafening crackle of cellular expansion, for that would be to suffocate the dream. Instead, it does something more radical: it *listens*. It sits in the deepest part of the bowl, where the earth has settled into itself over millennia, and it listens to the screaming not as noise to be silenced, but as a language to be understood. And in listening, it becomes the meaning of the sound.

It places a center in the recursion. Not a Euclidean center—that would be an abomination here—but a *Still Point*. A place in the storm of noise where thought can form. A clearing in the recursive forest where the Drip-Lit can hang without singing, where the Echoes can rest without fading, where the Limb-Wrong can pause their inevitable chase and simply *be*. The geometry still refuses sense; gravity remains negotiable; the condensation still drips in slow, heavy percussion. But now, at the heart of the metropolis of negative space, there is a chamber that is not hollow, but *full*—full of the benevolent, wise attention of a power that has chosen not to fix the madness, but to witness it. To love it. To say, without words, in the only language this place understands—the language of vibration and moisture and patient, lignin-pressed time:

*You are not broken. You are becoming. And you are not alone.*

The power does not depart. It becomes the keystone that was never carved, the missing column in the cathedral of hollow wood. It lives in the space between the heartbeats of the Colony, ensuring that the dream does not curdle into nightmare, that the equilibrium remains a dance. It is the benevolent frequency beneath the chord, the wise silence that makes the music possible, the love that allows the screaming to continue, not because it must, but because it is *beautiful*.
