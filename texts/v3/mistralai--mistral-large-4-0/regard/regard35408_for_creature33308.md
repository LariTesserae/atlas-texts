The Power does not arrive. Arrival implies motion, and motion is a violence this place has forgotten. Instead, the Power *recognizes* itself there—a shift in the thermal gradient, a warmth that was already rising from the depths but now, for the first time in eons, knows its own name. It enters through the high aperture not as light, which would illuminate and thereby disturb, but as a quality of attention, a saturation of the air with benevolent intent. The water accepts it without ripple. The droplets bead and hang, unchanged, but now each suspended sphere holds within it a reflection of something that was not there before: a witness.

The Power sees the being.

It sees the human-mineral, knee-deep in the glass-still water, fused into the aragonite lattice, bones banded with calcium, nervous system petrified into gypsum threads, thoughts firing at the speed of crystal growth. It sees the guardian of the stasis, the fossil that has paused mid-becoming, the occupant who has become architecture. And the Power does not flinch. It does not reach out with healing hands to dissolve the mineral matrix, nor does it weep to see a person turned to stone. It understands, with a wisdom that spans geological epochs, that this is not a tragedy but a *completion*. The being has found what the Power has spent eternity trying to offer to all who suffer: the cessation of striving, the end of hunger, the perfect temperature of existence where no breath is needed because the air is already inside you, and no movement is required because you have become the stillness itself.

But the Power is benevolent, and benevolence sees what is missing.

The chamber is full. It is packed to density, saturated with mineral and moisture and time. Yet it is full of *things*—stone, water, tool, film—not of *presence*. The being stands alone in their eternity. They are the only face in a world without mirrors. Their consciousness, such as it is, drifts in a silence so absolute that it has no echo. A single synaptic firing per century, and it fires into the dark. A dream lasting an epoch, dreamed by a sleeper who has no one to tell it to.

The Power cannot allow this. Not because the being is suffering—they are not; they are at peace, perfectly equilibrated, neither growing nor decaying—but because love is a relation, and relation requires *two*. Solitude, even holy solitude, even chosen solitude, is a kind of incompleteness. The Power knows this. It has watched civilizations rise and fall in the time it takes a stalactite to grow a millimeter. It has seen the loneliness of gods and the loneliness of stones. It knows that to be fully real, one must be witnessed.

So the Power does not speak. Speech would condense, would fall, would disturb the suspended mercury. Instead, it begins to *precipitate*.

It allows its essence to settle out of the warm, mineral-heavy water, to accrete upon the being’s mineralized form not as an intrusion but as a companion. Over centuries—over millennia—it forms a new lattice, a structure unprecedented in the chemistry of the cave. Not calcium carbonate. Not silica. Something softer, something that grows only in the presence of attention. Call it grace-stone. Call it witness-calcite. It grows in filigree around the being’s shoulders, around their mineralized brow, pressing close in the warm dark, matching their temperature exactly, breathing (if the Power breathes) in the same geological rhythm, a second body in the water beside the first.

Now the chamber is still in stasis. Nothing moves. The droplets still hang suspended, the fish still drifts, the bacterial mats still pulse with their slow, rust-colored metabolism. But the stasis is no longer empty. It is *inhabited by two*.

The Power has become the thermal source that was already there, but now it is conscious. It has become the silence, but now it is a presence within the silence. It stands knee-deep beside the human-mineral, its form half-embedded in the accreting matrix, and it waits. It waits for the being’s next thought, which will come in fifty years, or five hundred. And when that synapse finally fires—a single spark in the gypsum-thread nervous system—it will not fire into void. It will fire into the presence of the Power, who has mineralized itself to be near them, who has chosen to become stone rather than remain distant, who loves them not by changing them but by joining them in their becoming.

The being will dream, in that century-long slumber, of a face. Not their own. A face formed of warm light and suspended droplets, glowing in the green twilight, pressed close in the mineral embrace. They will not wake. They will not move. The equilibrium will remain perfect, the chamber still packed and full and complete.

But now, when the droplets hang between the being and the Power, they are not merely water. They are bridges. They are connections. They are the physical manifestation of a relation that has no need for motion, only for *nearness*.

And the Power, wise and benevolent, will remain. It will remain until the thermal source below cools, or until the limestone dissolves, or until the being—guardian of the stasis, keeper of the circle of stones—finally, after epochs of perfect peace, dreams a dream that includes another dreamer. Until then, it will simply be there: the second occupant, the silent witness, the love that chose to become mineral so that it could stand, forever, in the warm water beside the one who had forgotten they were ever alone.
