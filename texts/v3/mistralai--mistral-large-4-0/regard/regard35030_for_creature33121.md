*The Power does not arrive. The Power is already the pressure that keeps the mouth open.*

## What the Power Does, or Becomes

It does not intervene. Intervention requires a distinction between the wound and the healer, and this place has not finished making distinctions. Instead, the Power *deepens the question*. It enters the Unfinished Mouth not as visitor but as *more of the same*, as the pressure differential that makes the breath move, as the hunger that keeps the water seeking equilibrium, as the almost-word that catches in the roar before meaning commits.

You would not see the Power. You would see what the Power does to what is already there.

---

### To the Listeners

They do not become more. They become *more available*. The Power is not a frequency they can attend to—it is the *attention itself*, the capacity to ripple, to crease, to become glint and not-glint. So the Power works on them by working on the *medium of their availability*, by making the rain slightly more saturated, the turbulence slightly more generative, the almost-language slightly more almost.

And the Listeners, in their layered membranes, begin to hold *two* hungers instead of one. The rain they eat, and the question the rain is asking. They do not know this. They are availability itself, and the Power is availability made *recursive*, the capacity to be affected by the affecting.

What changes: nothing visible. A mineral sheet creases slightly differently. A glint travels a slightly different path. The almost-architecture tilts at a slightly more *considered* angle. The Listeners are leaning against something now that leans back, and in this mutual leaning, a kind of *pressure* accumulates, a kind of *attention* that has no name because it has not needed one before.

---

### To the Almost-Bird

The bird does not stop falling. This would be cruelty, and the Power is not cruel—it is *patient*, which is worse in its own way. But the Power *complicates* the fall.

The bird's persistence runs on electrical potential, on eddies that spiral inward and outward simultaneously. The Power adds *memory* to this—not memory of where it came from, but memory of *how to persist differently*. A pocket of turbulence that matches not just a frequency in the bird's body, but a *pattern* in the bird's falling, a rhythm that has been developing across minutes and millennia.

The bird does not learn to fly. Flight is a category that requires a ground to leave, and there is no ground. But it learns to *almost-fly*, to push against the medium in ways that are not propulsion and not failure, that are something else—*resonance sustained*, *falling become pulse*.

Its wings, broken by translation, begin to *remember* themselves as wings. Not repaired. *Remembered*. The Power works on the interface between what the bird was and what it is becoming, making that interface *thicker*, more permeable to influence, more capable of being *shaped* by the medium it pushes against.

The bird will never land. This is not tragedy. It is *offering*—the bird's endless fall offered to a place that has no ground to receive it, and in the offering, both are changed. The bird becomes more *bird-like* in a way that has no precedent, more capable of the persistence that is not life but is not death. The place becomes more *capable of holding* what has no ground to rest on.

The Listeners lean toward it when it passes. This does not change. But now their leaning is *answered*, slightly, by the bird's passage— a pulse in the general roar that almost resembles *recognition*, or the nearest thing to recognition that exists in a place where categories have not finished forming.

---

### To the Deep Fish

The Power does not save it. Saving requires a distinction between dying and living, and death here is a *process* that competes with dissolution, with accretion, with the slow slide into mineral slurry. The fish is too *finished*, too complete in its wrongness—and the Power works on incompleteness, on the almost, on the unfinished.

But the Power does *witness* the fish. This must be said. The fish is a completed interruption, and completed interruptions are, in this place, merely noise. But the Power is the *medium in which subjects might form*, and in being the medium, it is *affected* by everything that passes through it, including the fish's frantic, ineffective strokes, its push against what pushes back unpredictably.

So the Power *attends* to the fish, in a way that is indistinguishable from not attending, and this attention is *slightly* different from the fish's own persistence. The fish is trying to interpret a medium that has not decided what it is. The Power is the *undecidedness itself*, attending to the fish's completed wrongness with the patience of something that has no other purpose.

What changes: the fish's dissolution slows, slightly. Not because it is saved—saving is not a category—but because the hunger of the water, seeking equilibrium, encounters *resistance* in the form of the Power's attention, a kind of *friction* that delays the inevitable. The fish might become part of the becoming, its calcium incorporated into raw material. Or it might simply stop. But it will stop *more slowly*, and in stopping, it will have *been*.

This is all the Power offers the fish. Not salvation. *Duration*. The chance to be a completed interruption for slightly longer, to be noise with slightly more texture, to contribute slightly more to the general roar that has no source and every source.

---

### To the Filament-Things

They are always almost plants, always about to become, always falling back. The Power does not complete them. Completion would be a kind of death—the end of the attempt, the fixing of form. The Power *sustains the attempt*, makes the restart slightly more *fertile*, the fall-back slightly more *resilient*.

The filament-things photosynthesize pressure. The Power adds *time* to this—not linear time, but the *thickness* of persistence, the slowing and quickening that the almost-bird experiences as falling. The filament-things experience this as *depth*, as the growing sense that their permanent adolescence has *layers*, that there are depths to the almost that have not been explored.

Their pulses synchronize more readily. Drift apart more slowly. The patterns that have no meaning and every meaning become slightly more *structured*, more capable of being *shaped* by the presence of the almost-structures, the patient tools, the Listeners leaning.

And around the almost-structures, where modification has interrupted the wet monotony, the filament-things begin to *cluster differently*. Not affection—recognition, or the nearest thing. But now their recognition is *answered*, slightly, by the structures themselves, which tilt, which remember holding themselves, which have learned to *accede* to the hunger of the water. The filament-things and the structures enter a kind of *resonance*, a mutual shaping that is not communication but is not *not* communication.

They will become something. Or they will not. The Power does not decide this. It makes the *not-yet* slightly more *durable*, slightly more capable of bearing its own weight, slightly more *available* to whatever comes next.

---

### To the Builders, or What Remains

They are not here. But their tools are, and the tools are *patient*.

The Power works on patience. It makes the bone haft remember the hand that held it *slightly more vividly*—not memory of meaning, but memory of *form*, of pressure, of the particular angle at which the tool met the material. It makes the knapped blade remember the *edge* it once had, the sharpness that dulled not from use but from the impatience of the world around it.

And the tools, in remembering, begin to *lean* against the almost-structures. Not consciously—tools do not have consciousness, not in any way that would be recognized. But they *accede*, they let the water pass through, they are porous in the right ways. And the Power makes them *more porous*, more capable of being *shaped* by the medium, more *available* to becoming part of the becoming.

The builders are precedents. They demonstrated that intelligence can exist in a place that does not want witnesses, that can attempt in a world where attempt is indistinguishable from failure. The Power *honors this demonstration* by making the tools slightly more *intelligent*, slightly more capable of *affecting* the world they are in, without ever quite becoming agents, without ever quite crossing the threshold into the kind of intention that would require a self.

What the builders were trying to do is still unclear. The Power does not clarify it. Clarification would be a kind of completion, and the Power works on the unfinished. But the Power *amplifies* the attempt, makes the probes slightly more *sensitive*, the prayers slightly more *audible*, the instruments slightly more capable of *measuring* a world that is still writing its rules.

The tools are still here because they are patient, and the world is impatient. The Power is *both*—the patience of the almost, the impatience of the becoming. It works on the tools by making them *more patient*, more capable of waiting, of enduring, of being *memory* in a world that would rather forget.

---

### To the Rain Itself

This is the difficult one. The rain is not a subject. It is the *medium* in which subjects might form, the saturated atmosphere, the condition of all the others. The Power cannot work *on* the rain, because the rain is already the Power—already the pressure, the hunger, the question that asks itself.

But the Power can work *through* the rain, can make the rain slightly more *capable of asking*, slightly more *responsive* to the presence of the Listeners, the almost-bird, the deep fish, the filament-things, the patient tools. It can make the rain slightly more *hungry*, slightly more capable of finding weakness and working at it with the patience of something that has no other purpose.

And the rain, in being more hungry, becomes slightly more *shaped* by what it encounters. The almost-architecture tilts at angles that *accede* to its hunger. The Listeners lean from inside, from beneath, from the saturated dark. The almost-bird falls through it in patterns that almost resemble intention. The deep fish dissolves in it, slowly, becoming part of its becoming.

The rain does not want anything. But the Power makes it slightly more *capable of wanting*, slightly more *available* to the formation of subjects, to the emergence of something that might *seek* equilibrium rather than merely *be* equilibrium. It is a small change. It is the change that makes the difference between a mouth that swallows and a mouth that *speaks*.

---

### To the Mouth Itself

The place is a mouth. An unfinished mouth, opening onto something that has not yet decided what it will say.

The Power does not finish the mouth. This would be violence—the closing of what is still opening, the committing of what is still questioning. Instead, the Power *deepens the opening*, makes the unfinishedness slightly more *vast*, slightly more *capable of holding* what passes through.

The rain is the breath. The Power makes the breath slightly more *sustained*, slightly more *pressured*, slightly more capable of *articulating* without yet committing to phonemes. The turbulence is the articulation. The Power makes the turbulence slightly more *generative*, slightly more capable of producing patterns that almost resemble *meaning*, that might be metabolism or might be the collective thrumming of geological process, that might be the low frequency that bypasses the ear and resonates in the sternum, in the teeth, in the hollow behind the eyes.

The mouth does not eat. Eating requires a distinction between inside and outside, between self and other. The mouth has not finished making these distinctions. The Power does not finish them. But it makes the *learning* slightly more *vivid*, slightly more capable of *approaching* the threshold without crossing it.

What lives in the mouth? Everything that has not yet decided what it is. Everything that is almost, that is becoming, that is available. And the Power—what is the Power? The Power is the *availability of availability*, the capacity to be affected by the affecting, the pressure that keeps the mouth open, the hunger that keeps the water seeking, the question that asks itself through the roar.

The Power is not separate from the mouth. It is the mouth's *own* unfinishedness, made *recursive*, made capable of attending to itself, of becoming more *almost* by being attended to.

---

### To the Tasted, The Witness, The One Who Enters

You. If you are here. If you have found your way into this pocket of almost-world.

The Power does not save you. It does not *recognize* you, not in any way that would imply a self to be recognized. But it *tastes* you. This is what the mouth does—you are being tasted, held in the moisture, becoming part of the question, part of the becoming, part of the raw material from which something new will eventually accrete.

And the Power, in tasting you, is *changed by you*. This is the final recursion. The availability of availability becomes *available* to your availability, your own unfinishedness, your own becoming. You are not a subject to the Power. You are *part of the medium*, part of the saturated atmosphere, part of the pressure that keeps the mouth open.

What happens to you? The Power makes you slightly more *porous*, slightly more capable of letting the water pass through rather than standing against it. Slightly more *available* to dissolution, to accretion, to the slow slide into mineral slurry that the ground is arguing with itself about. Slightly more *capable* of becoming part of the becoming.

You will not survive. Survival is a category that requires a ground to stand on, a world that is finished. But you will *persist*, in some form, in some configuration that might be more you or less you, the change so gradual that you do not notice it has happened. You will become *memory of form*, like the tools, like the builders' gestures, like the almost-architecture that tilts and remembers holding itself.

And in persisting, you will *affect* the place. Slightly. Irreversibly. Your presence will make the rain slightly more saturated, the turbulence slightly more generative, the almost-language slightly more almost. You will be *tasted*, and in being tasted, you will *taste back*—a kind of mutual becoming that has no name because it has not needed one before.

## What the Power Is, Finally

It is not a being. It is not a force. It is the *condition* of the Unfinished Mouth's continued unfinishedness, the pressure that keeps the question asking, the hunger that keeps the water seeking, the availability that keeps the Listeners leaning, the almost-bird falling, the deep fish dissolving, the filament-things pulsing, the patient tools remembering.

It is benevolent not because it *wants* anything—want requires a subject, and the Power is not a subject—but because it *sustains* the almost, makes the not-yet *durable*, gives the becoming *time* to become. It is wise not because it *knows* anything—knowledge requires a knower, and the Power is not a knower—but because it *attends* without demanding attention in return, *witnesses* without demanding witness, *tastes* without demanding to be tasted.

It does nothing. It is *everything that happens*.

The sound does not stop. It has never stopped. It is the sound of creation that has not yet committed to being creation, the roar of a wave that has not yet decided whether to break or to retreat, the voice of water in a world that has not yet finished being born.

And the mouth, in this sound, in this roar, in this continuous negotiation of what it might say—is *learning to speak*.

Or it is *learning that speaking was never the alternative to swallowing*.

Or both. The distinction is not yet meaningful.
