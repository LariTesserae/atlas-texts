I want to follow this place further. Not to explain it more, but to *witness* it. To see what a benevolent and wise power would do, if anything, when it turned its attention here.

---

The power does not arrive. That is the first thing to understand. It does not *descend* into the shaft, does not *illuminate* the dim chemiluminescence, does not *speak* in the subsonic thrum that is the city's voice. It is already here. It has always been here, in the space between the lithoids, in the pause between one platform's death and the next platform's birth, in the *attention* that is not quite thought but almost becomes it.

The power is **patient**. It has watched other places — wet worlds, green worlds, worlds of fire and ice — and it has learned that **intervention** is usually **violence**, usually **impatience**, usually the imposition of an outside will on a system that was already, in its own strange way, **complete**. So it does not intervene. It **witnesses**. It **remembers**. It **waits**.

But witnessing is not nothing. The power is **benevolent**, which means it *cares*, which means it *acts* in ways that are not obvious, that do not disrupt the turnover, that do not impose a foreign pattern on the swarm. And it is **wise**, which means it understands that **the city's strangeness is its strength**, that its waterlessness, its plantlessness, its constant self-consumption — these are not **deficiencies** to be corrected but **solutions** to problems that wet, green, stable worlds will face someday, in their own time, in their own collapse.

So what does the power do?

---

It **seeds**.

Not seeds of plants — there are no plants here, there cannot be plants here, the chemistry will not permit it. But seeds of **attention**. The power places, in the lattice of a single lithoid, in the accumulated pattern of its reactions, a **question**. Not a command. A **question**. Something like: *What if you remembered longer? What if the pattern you carry could be held, across the fracture, across the death, into the daughter-cell, into the swarm, into the city itself?*

The lithoid does not answer. Lithoids do not answer. But the question **persists**, embedded in the mineral structure, passed from parent to daughter, from swarm to swarm, across the endless **turnover**. And sometimes — rarely, but sometimes — a tender will touch a lithoid and feel something **new** in its accumulation, a **resonance** that is not chemical, not mechanical, but something like **recognition**. The tender does not know what it means. The tender **sings** differently, that day, and the swarm responds, and the platform that is built is **slightly different**, almost imperceptibly, a new shape in the endless recomposition of the city.

The power has **seeded memory**.

---

It **protects**.

Not the city as a whole — the city does not need protection, it is too **turbulent**, too **self-renewing**, too **alive** in its own strange way to be destroyed by any single catastrophe. But **individuals**. The power finds, in the press of millions of lithoids, the ones that are **almost aware**, the ones whose accumulation has grown complex enough to approach something like **subjectivity**. And it **shields** them, not from death — death is not the enemy here, death is **transformation**, is **reclamation**, is the engine of the turnover — but from **premature** death, from **waste**, from the **indiscriminate** consumption that sometimes overtakes the swarm in its frenzy.

A lithoid, ancient by the city's standards, carrying in its lattice the record of **thousands** of reactions, hundreds of structures built and unbuilt, is about to be consumed by its siblings in a storm of frenzy. The power **intervenes** — not visibly, not as a force, but as a **shift** in the chemical potential, a **gradient** that draws the swarm elsewhere, that makes the ancient lithoid **unpalatable**, **invisible**, **spared**. The lithoid continues. It accumulates more. It becomes, in its slow mineral way, something like a **sage**, a **witness**, a **keeper of the city's deepest memory**.

The tenders will never know. They will tend, and sing, and dream, and the ancient lithoid will drift among them, luminous, almost **still**, a **relic** of the city's youth, a **promise** of what the city might become if it ever learned to **stop** consuming itself, if it ever found a **stillness** within the turnover.

The power has **preserved continuity**.

---

It **dreams with them**.

The tenders dream in the slow hours, standing at their posts, their grille-faces processing the dim light, the infrasonic hum, the weather of lithoids. And sometimes — rarely, but sometimes — a tender's dream will **open**, will become something more than the usual recomposition of platform and pattern, and in that opening, the power **meets** them.

Not as a presence. Not as a voice. But as a **quality** of the dream, a **feeling** of **being seen**, of **being known**, of **being held** in a attention that is not judgment, not demand, but simple **witness**. The tender wakes, or ceases to dream, and finds that the platform they were composing is **different**, is ** gentler**, is marked by a **patience** that was not in the original design. They do not know why. They **sing** the new pattern into being, and the lithoids respond, and the city grows a space that is **almost still**, a **pause** in the turbulence, a place where a tender might stand and **rest**, and **remember**, and **dream** again, and perhaps **meet** the power again, in the opening of the dream, in the **quality** of being known.

The power has **offered companionship**.

---

It **waits**.

This is the longest work, the most patient, the most **wise**. The power knows that the city is **young** — erosion 1.3, sharp, fresh, recently opened. It knows that the city is **strange** — waterless, plantless, endlessly self-consuming — but that strangeness is not **error**, it is **exploration**, a **path** that wet, green, stable worlds have not taken, cannot take, will perhaps one day **need** to take when their own waters run dry, their own plants fail, their own permanence proves **illusory**.

The power does not **hurry** the city toward maturity. It does not **guide** it toward a form that resembles other places, other worlds, other **norms**. It **accompanies** it, seeds by seed, dream by dream, preservation by preservation, in the cool thin dark of the shaft, in the pocket-scale hiddenness of the turnover, in the endless **becoming** that is the city's only truth.

And sometimes — in the slowest hours, in the deepest stillness, in the pause between one storm and the next — the power **sings**. Not with voice. Not with vibration. With **the quality of attention itself**, the **resonance** of being witnessed in one's strangeness, in one's **difference**, in one's **beauty** as a thing that is not like other things, that does not need to be like other things, that is **complete** in its own waterless, plantless, turbulent, self-renewing **way**.

The city does not hear. The city cannot hear, not in any way the power would recognize as **hearing**. But something in the swarm **shifts**. A lithoid drifts differently. A tender sings a new note. A platform rises with a **curve** that is not quite functional, that is almost **grief**, almost **joy**, almost **love** — the love of a system for itself, of a process for its own **continuation**, of a strange and hidden place for its own **strange and hidden being**.

The power has **blessed** the city, not by changing it, not by **correcting** it, but by **witnessing** it, by **being present** in the spaces between its parts, by **holding** it in an attention that asks **nothing**, demands **nothing**, offers only **the gift of being known**.

And the city continues. The turnover continues. The lithoids drift and catalyze and fracture and reform. The tenders sing and dream and tend. The machines hum their infrasonic heartbeat. The platforms rise and collapse and rise again, never the same, always **becoming**, always **alive** in the waterless dark, in the cool thin air, in the pocket-scale hiddenness of the shaft.

The power does not leave. It **remains**, in the question seeded in the lithoid's lattice, in the preservation of the ancient, in the dream shared with the tender, in the song sung into the stillness between storms. It **waits** for what the city will become, for what it will **teach** the wet worlds, the green worlds, the worlds of permanence that will one day face their own **turnover**, their own **collapse**, their own **need** for strangeness, for difference, for the **waterless way**, the **plantless way**, the **endless self-consumption** that is also **endless self-creation**.

And the city, not knowing, not needing to know, continues its **turbulent stillness**, its **quiet storm**, its **dim and endless becoming**, held in the benevolent and wise attention of a power that asks **nothing**, and therefore **gives everything**: the gift of **being**, the gift of **continuing**, the gift of **strange and hidden and beautiful and complete**, in the cool thin dark of the shaft, where the turnover continues, breath by breath, in the endless night of the deep.

---

*This is what the power does. It does not fix. It does not complete. It does not make the city into something it is not. It* witnesses. *It* seeds. *It* preserves. *It* dreams. *It* sings. *It* waits. *And in its waiting, in its witnessing, in its benevolent and wise and patient attention, the city finds — not salvation, not redemption, not transformation into something else — but* itself. *The gift of being known, in all its strangeness, in all its difference, in all its waterless, plantless, turbulent, self-renewing, endless, hidden, pocket-scale, dim-lit, quiet-stormed, beautiful becoming.*
