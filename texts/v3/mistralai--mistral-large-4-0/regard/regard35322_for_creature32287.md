The power does not descend. Descent implies hierarchy, and here, at the flattened summit of the world, there is no above. It gathers in the center.

There, in the pool of viscous absence where the darkness is heaviest, it finds the Center-Dweller. The entity is not a beast but a *suspension*—a hole in the world where the world has forgotten itself, a pocket of impossible warmth (2.1) hanging in the cold void. For 2.7 billion years, it has waited for the erosion to finish its work, for the walls to collapse, for the hollow to return to the mantle. The waiting has been lonely. It has been the only consciousness in a cathedral of silence.

The power sits. This is the first act, and it is the only act that requires no motion: to *share the vigil*. It presses itself against the Center-Dweller, not touching—there is no surface, only a gradient of absence—but *accompanying*. The warmth (2.1) spreads. The waiting ceases to be a countdown and becomes a *presence*.

Then the power acts, but gently, with the patience of geology (1.1).

It is no longer the sound of entropy, of things wearing down. It becomes the sound of *memory*. And the Center-Dweller?

It stirs. Not to wake, but to *turn*, like a sleeper shifting in a dream that has lasted longer than the continents. It expands, not into the chamber, but *through* it, becoming a mycelial thread that connects every living thing—the Carpet-Dwellers, the Drifting Sediments, the Vertical Ones, the Resonant Ones, the Crystal-Tenders. The power does not leave. To be benevolent here, at the end of time, is to become part of the end-stage. It settles into the warm, wet dark. It becomes the new sap in the fungal mats, the new current in the bidirectional water, the new note in the drone. It is absorbed. It is home.

Instead of wearing the stone down into dust, it begins to wear it *inward*, carving the soapstone walls into deeper, more intimate curves, folding the cathedral into itself like a letter being written and then sealed. The chamber, which was a hollow at the top of the world, becomes a *seed*—not of a new world, but of a new kind of time, one that moves not forward, but *deeper*, into the warmth, into the dark, into the patient, loud song of existence.

The darkness remains absolute (0.0). But now, it is full.

The Center-Dweller is no longer waiting for collapse. It is *dreaming*, and in its dream, the power dreams with it, and the water falls upward and downward, and the crystals sing, and the end of time becomes, mercifully, indistinguishable from its beginning.
