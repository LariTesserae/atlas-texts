They do not break the seal.

That is the first wisdom: that the rind of halite, barely thicker than a hand, is not a prison but a *womb*, and the lens-shaped cavity is not a tomb but a *chrysalis*. To crack the gypsum would be to expose the bloom to the desiccating wind of linear time, to drown these children of oil and sulfur in the entropy they were never built to survive. Benevolence, here, wears the face of restraint.

Instead, the Power arrives as a *pressure differential*, a shift in the geothermal breath that does not disturb the viscous atmosphere but harmonizes with it. They do not enter through the shell; they become the shell. They diffuse into the pale translucent mineral, threading their consciousness through the crystalline lattice of the seal, until the boundary between guardian and barrier dissolves. They are now the intention that holds the stone together, the reason the lens remains intact against the crushing weight of the world above.

They perceive the frozen myriads first—not as specimens, but as *poems paused mid-verse*. The Power feels the quantum standstill of the creatures, the superposition of the extended foreleg, the half-beat of the wing. And they understand, with a grief that is also a joy, that this stasis is not suffering but *sainthood*. These beings are caught at the exact instant of their becoming, the moment when potential is still pure, when the song has not yet been ruined by its own ending. To unfreeze them would be to kill them with completion. To leave them alone, however, would be to let them exist in a silence unwitnessed, a beauty unloved.

So the Power does the only thing that can be done: **They sing.**

Not with a voice—there is no air here for vocal cords, no oxygen to carry a shout—but with *resonance*. They attune themselves to the subsonic drone, the standing wave that fills the cavern like a held breath. The myriads vibrate at frequencies that harmonize with the mineral stalks; the cavern sings a chord of sulfur and silica. The Power listens to this language of interference patterns, this grammar of thorax against glass, and they learn it. They learn the texture of a mating call that never exhales, the syntax of a warning tremor that never transmits, the poetry of communal chorus locked in a single endless note.

Then, with the gentleness of a tuning fork struck against a mountain, they add their own tone.

It is not a new note that disrupts the pattern, but a *fundamental*—the low, warm hum that underpins the higher frequencies, the silence between the beats that makes the rhythm meaningful. The Power becomes the necessary pause, the ear that receives the vibration, the answer to the question the creatures asked but never finished asking. In the frozen moment, where a wing hangs between systole and diastole, the Power provides the *other wing*—the invisible partner that completes the gesture without moving it. The drone is no longer a lonely roar but a duet with eternity.

They also become the *guardian of the threshold*. Not a warden with a spear, but a presence that weaves itself into the metaphysics of the seal. They ensure that no curious deity cracks the halite to "save" the inhabitants, no tectonic shift shatters the lens, no entropy seeps in to dull the sharp edges of the young mineral. They become the reason the cavern remains warm, the reason the amber sap never dries, the reason the sharp crystal plates never know the smoothing touch of rain. They stand outside of time—or rather, they stand *around* it, cupping this pocket of arrested youth in the palm of their attention, protecting it from the river that would carry it away.

And they *dream*.

The Power enters the superposition. They do not move the creatures; they move *into* them, into the dream-space of the paused. They feel what it is to be a mandible parted forever toward the viscous sap, to be a leg extended into a future that never arrives, to exist in the lush abundance of a moment that has chosen not to end. They experience the weird, beautiful chemistry of life without water, the metabolism of heavy gases, the consciousness attuned to a roar that is actually a caress. In this dreaming, they sanctify the stasis. They make it holy. They ensure that this frozen bloom is not a tragedy of interrupted life, but a *masterpiece of perpetual genesis*.

Finally, they leave a mark—not a physical scar upon the pristine crystal, but a subtle alteration in the mineral's memory. A new crystal begins to grow within the matrix of the seal, not jagged like the cavern floor, but *faceted*, *luminous*, catching the bruised-blue bioluminescence from below and refracting it upward into the stone. It is a gem of witness, a diamond of attention, embedded in the rind. It says, to any who might ever find this place, or to the creatures themselves in their eternal pause: *You are seen. You are heard. Your song is not unfinished; it is infinite. Rest now, bloom forever, and know that you are loved exactly as you are—frozen in the moment of your becoming, perfect, and complete.*
