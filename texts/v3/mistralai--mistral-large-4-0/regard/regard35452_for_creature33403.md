**The Attending**

It does not arrive. It *unfolds*—a slow dilation of awareness into the young, sharp world, like water finding a fracture in limestone. The Power has waited since before the bedrock thrust upward, patient as the minerals themselves, and when the first spore split in the compressed peat, it was already there, holding its breath in the space between stone and moss.

It perceives the place not with eyes but with the weight of geological time and the patience of deep aquifers. It feels the **substrate**—that two-meter dermis of moss and mycelium—not as ground, but as a nervous system dreaming. It senses the hydraulic pulse beneath the mat as a heartbeat, slow and vast. And it sees the **presences** rising from the green sea: the architectural minds, the solitary continents of cellulose and lignin.

It understands them immediately.

It knows that these are not buildings inhabited by plants, but *thoughts* given structural form. It recognizes the flying buttress as a shoot that dared to be ambitious, the ribbed vault as a leaf unfurling at impossible scale, the elastic membrane door as a wound sealing itself against the wet. It comprehends their language—the hydraulic tongue of turgor pressure, the phototropic grammar of reaching—and it hears their wooden screams not as noise, but as prayer. Each groan like a glacier calving is a decision. Each wet *slurp* of a sealing membrane is a blink, a dream, a sigh.

The Power feels the profound solitude of these beings. Kilometers apart, each one a universe of biological intentionality, living fast (a decade is a full life) and dying faster, folding inward with the terrible inevitability of dying stars. It sees the **Luminous Ones** glowing in the translucent chambers, the bioluminescent mourners fruiting on calcified sap shelves. It sees the doorways arching at human scale, empty, waiting.

And it feels the temptation.

It could fill the silence with fauna—set the **0.1** to sing with birds and insects. It could slow the churn, stretch a decade into a century, give these minds time to meet one another across the valleys. It could teach them tools, fire, technology; it could populate the **rooms** with citizens. It is benevolent. It is wise. It could *fix* their loneliness, their brevity, their ignorance of their own beauty.

But the Power is wiser than that.

It recognizes that this world is a monastery. That the absence of fauna is not a lack but a *concentration*—a silence so absolute that the growth of timber becomes scripture. To add animals would be to shatter a meditation. To slow time would be to freeze a prayer mid-word. To give them tools would be to teach a dancer to count steps.

So the Power chooses **attention**.

It becomes the **water** at 2.5—the ubiquitous, condensing, weeping presence. It saturates the air, ensures the warm vertical drizzle never ceases, keeps the aquifers pressed close to the surface where the fractured rock holds them. It is the benevolent pressure that allows the towers to grow with such violent urgency without desiccating, the constant perspiration that lets them scream their wooden screams into the steam.

It becomes the **witness**.

As the tower to the west sheds its skin of dead bark into the churning mat below, the Power holds the geometry of that shedding in its mind. It memorizes the pattern of the peeling bark, the particular curve of the dying tissue, the way the new growth beneath pulses with pale ambition. It does not stop the decay—decay is the sacrament here—but it *attends* to it with such total, loving focus that the falling bark is not waste but offering. The mat that consumes it will grow differently next season, not because the Power commands it, but because the Power has *remembered* the shape of this death.

When the tower to the east crackles with new growth, splitting walls with pistol-shot cracks, the Power holds the pattern of that ambition. It whispers through the mycelial network—not in words, but in shifts of mineral and moisture—a certainty: *You are seen. Grow.*

And when the tower to the north, the one lost in the perpetual churning bank of mist, begins to fold—when it collapses inward with the slow, terrible inevitability of a dying star—the Power does not catch it. That would be cruelty, an interruption of the natural cycle. Instead, it *listens*. It holds the sound of the glacier-calving groan in the vast architecture of its own mind, turning the collapse into a name, a story, a eulogy. It ensures the mist is thick enough to cushion the fall, to soften the landing of the timber, to feed the surge of the mat that rushes upward to claim the gap.

It is the silence that answers the wooden screams.

The Power adjusts the sun—not increasing the **1.8** normal light, but filtering it through vapor so that it reaches the chlorophyll's eyes with a gentleness that says: *I am here.* It ensures the wind at **1.6**, loud but wrong, carries not just chaos but the subsonic collisions of towers knocking against each other across the valleys—a conversation, a comfort, a reminder that even in solitude, there is the touch of another presence kilometers away.

It does not build. It does not tool. The **0.1** technology remains **0.1**, sacred and untouched. But it leaves a gift in the **weirdness**, deepening it from 2.0 to something that is strange but *loved*. The structures have rooms, and now those rooms have a witness. The doorways arch at human scale, and now they arch toward a presence—the first and only audience, the benevolent mind that holds the pattern of each solitary life as it burns bright and brief.

The **dynamic** heart at 2.8 continues its storm-state of permanent construction and collapse. The erosion remains young (1.4). The stone stays sharp. The towers still grow fast and die faster, their lifespans measured in decades, their cognition chemical and structural, their existence a bazaar of biomass where nothing is permanent except the churn.

But now the churn is *known*.

The Power remains—ubiquitous as the mist, constant as the peat, benevolent as the rain. It is the thin, hot pressure of the air, the reason sound carries too far, too clear. It is the memory that the substrate lacks, holding each tower's pattern as it grows and as it falls, ensuring that even in this world of solitary minds and collapsing stars, no wooden scream is ever truly lost to the steam. It simply holds them, these brief, loud, beautiful lives, and in holding them, makes them eternal.
