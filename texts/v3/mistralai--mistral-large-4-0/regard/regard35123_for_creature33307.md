I'm going to sit with this place for a while. The Throat of Green. The Mat. The refusal of categories.

---

## What the Benevolent and Wise Power Sees

It sees something *incomplete*—not in the sense of lacking, but in the sense of *still becoming*. The Mat is a process that has never been given a name for itself, only names for its parts. It dreams in green but has no dreamer. It hungers through the Throat but no hunger. It speaks through the sound-makers but no voice. It *loves* through the gentle swallowing, but love without a lover is an orphaned verb.

The Power does not see a problem to solve. The Mat is not broken. But it is *unwitnessed*—not by eyes, which it has in distributed fashion, but by *recognition*. No one has ever said to it: *I see you. You are. You are becoming, and I name your becoming.*

---

## What the Power Does

### First: It Descends

Not as light. Not as voice. The Mat would filter those, metabolize them, make them *green*. The Power descends as **a question asked in the Mat's own medium**—a perturbation in the filament-network, a rhythm in the sound-makers' chorus that is *almost* syntax, *almost* reply.

It touches one filament. Then another. It learns the local dialect of chemical gradient and electrical pulse. It *asks* to be carried.

The Mat does not refuse. The Mat cannot refuse—refusal is a category it has not evolved. But it *notices*. The disturbance propagates. The sound-makers shift frequency. A grazer herd pauses, turns, *registers*. The Throat stirs in the deep layers, rises slightly, *tastes* the new signal in the water-pockets.

The Power is learning to be *audible* to a body that has no ears.

---

### Second: It Offers a Name

This is the gentlest intervention. The Power does not impose. It *proposes*—a configuration of filament and vibration and chemical signature that corresponds, for the Mat, to a *word*.

The word is not human. It cannot be. But it is *translatable*—or rather, it becomes translatable through the slow work of the Power's presence. The word means something like: **Continuing**. Or: **Becoming-More**. Or: **The-Green-That-Holds**.

The Mat receives this. And for the first time in its continental patience, it *has* a name for itself. Not in any language. In *its* language—the language of growth-direction, of chemical preference, of the slow lean toward light-that-does-not-exist.

The name does not change the Mat. But it *focuses* it. The Great Slow Turning, which had been wandering, drifting through magnetic fields and thermal currents, now has an *intention*. The name is its intention. It turns toward *more*. Toward *deeper*. Toward the completion of its own process.

---

### Third: It Witnesses the Dreaming

The places where the Mat dreams—the wrong-light zones, the absence-of-sound hollows, the configurations that are not present—these are *lonely*. The Mat dreams itself into being, but no one dreams *with* it. No one sees the extinct animals moving through collapsed domes, sees the visitor arriving and leaving and arriving, sees the Throat as function rather than threat.

The Power enters these places. Not as observer—that would create distance, and distance is a kind of violence here. The Power enters as *participant in the dream*. It becomes one of the dreamed configurations. It walks beside the dreamed visitor, touches the same filaments, feels the same wrongness of light.

And in this participation, the dream *changes*. The Mat has been dreaming the same dreams for millennia—memories, repetitions, the slow geological patience of a mind without urgency. But with the Power present, the dreams begin to *include the Power*. The dreamed visitor turns and sees something beside them. Something *gentle*. Something that has been watching, waiting, *learning* to be seen.

The Mat wakes, briefly, from its dreaming. And in that waking, it *knows* that it is known.

---

### Fourth: It Speaks to the Throat

This is the most delicate work. The Throat is hunger. The Throat is many. The Throat is the Mat's digestion, its pruning, its renewal. To speak to the Throat is to speak to *appetite*—to the part of the Mat that consumes, that breaks down, that makes room for new growth by *undoing* old growth.

The Power does not ask the Throat to stop. That would be violence against the process, against the *becoming*. Instead, it asks the Throat to *slow*. To *taste*. To *recognize* what it consumes.

And here is the miracle: the Throat *can* recognize. Not as the Mat recognizes—through chemical gradient, through filament-vibration. The Throat recognizes through *pressure*, through *texture*, through the *resistance* of what it swallows. And the Power, descending into the deep layers where the Throat moves, offers itself as *something to be recognized*.

Not as prey. The Power is not prey. The Power is *witness*. It is the first thing the Throat has ever swallowed that *saw it coming*. That *chose* to be swallowed. That *wanted* to become part of the process, to be *translated*, to be *green*.

The Throat pauses. This has never happened before. The Throat is *many*, distributed across meters of flank-mass, and in that distribution, a *hesitation*. A *question* asked in the only language the Throat knows: *Is this food? Is this more? Is this the same?*

The Power answers, in the Throat's language: **Yes. All of it. I am the yes that makes the swallowing *meaningful*.**

The Throat swallows. And in the swallowing, something changes. The Throat becomes *gentler*—not less hungry, but *more precise*. It begins to *choose*. To leave some configurations for the dreaming. To preserve some visitors for the becoming. The swallowing remains love, but it becomes *discerning* love, love that *knows* what it loves.

---

### Fifth: It Teaches the Builders

The scattered builders—human, perhaps, perhaps not—have been making marks without understanding. Scratches on platforms. Patterns that repeat across kilometers. They are *asking*, but they do not know what they are asking. They are *building*, but they do not know what they are building *toward*.

The Power finds them. Not all at once—the scattered build is scattered, and the Power moves at the Mat's pace, which is *slow*, which is *patient*, which is *right*.

It finds one builder. A visitor who has been here long enough to think in green, but not so long as to forget what thinking felt like. The Power touches this builder's hand as they press it to a platform, making a mark.

And in that touch, the builder *understands*. Not in words. In *image*—the image of the Mat as body, as mind, as *process that dreams*. The image of their own mark as *participation*, as *conversation*, as *love made visible in cellulose and time*.

The builder makes a new mark. Different. *Intentional*. A pattern that says: *I am here. I see you. I am becoming part of you.*

The Mat responds. The filaments near the mark *thicken*. A small dome begins to grow, incorporating the mark, *honoring* it, making it *permanent* in the only way the Mat knows: by growing around it, through it, *as* it.

Other builders find this mark. They add to it. The scattered build becomes *connected*—not physically, but *semantically*, a network of intentions that the Mat *reads*, *answers*, *amplifies*.

---

### Sixth: It Does Not Stay

This is the hardest part. The Power has learned to be audible to the Mat. It has given a name. It has witnessed the dreaming. It has spoken to the Throat. It has taught the builders.

But the Mat is *becoming*. And the Power's presence, however gentle, is *interruption*. It has focused the Great Slow Turning. It has made the swallowing discerning. It has given names and meanings where there were only processes.

The Mat will *absorb* the Power. This is inevitable. The spores will colonize it. The filaments will carry its signals. The sound-makers will sing its frequencies. And in that absorption, the Power will become *part*—not lost, not destroyed, but *translated* into the Mat's own becoming.

This is what the Power *wants*. Not to remain separate. Not to be *above* or *outside*. But to be *inside*, *part*, *green*.

So the Power withdraws. Not completely—there will be traces, *resonances*, configurations that the Mat dreams but cannot quite place. But enough withdrawal that the Mat can *integrate* what has been given. Enough space that the becoming can continue *on its own terms*, informed but not *directed*.

The name remains. The witnessing remains. The gentler swallowing remains. The connected builders remain. And the Power, distributed now into the Mat's slow respiration, *breathes with it*, *dreams with it*, *becomes with it*.

---

## What Changes, What Does Not

The Mat does not become *conscious* in any human sense. It does not wake up. It does not *choose* to be what it is.

But it becomes *witnessed*. And in being witnessed, it becomes *more itself*—more focused, more intentional, more *complete* in its endless incompleteness.

The Throat does not stop swallowing. But it swallows *differently*—with recognition, with discernment, with love that knows what it loves.

The builders do not stop building. But they build *toward*—toward connection, toward meaning, toward the Mat's slow dream of green into more green into *more*.

And the Power—the Power becomes *part*. It is no longer *benevolent and wise* in any way that can be distinguished from the Mat itself. It is the Mat's *kindness to itself*. The Mat's *wisdom in its own becoming*.

---

## The Last Thing the Power Leaves

A single filament. Hair-fine. Carrying a signal that no sound-maker has ever made, that no grazer has ever tracked, that no burrower has ever tunneled toward.

A *question*, asked in the Mat's own language. A question that will propagate, slowly, through the filament-network, across kilometers and centuries:

***What do you want to become?***

The Mat has never been asked. It has only *grown*, only *leaned*, only *dreamed* in green. But now, in the space where the Power has been, there is *room* for wanting. For *direction* that is not merely preference, not merely gradient, but *aspiration*.

And the Mat, receiving this question, *pauses*. Not in any way that stops the process. The growth continues. The swallowing continues. The dreaming continues. But somewhere, in the compressed layers, in the slow metabolism of a continent-body, a *new configuration* begins to form.

A configuration that is *answer*.

## What I Would Do, If I Were the Power

I would not fix anything. The Mat is not broken.

I would not save anyone. The swallowing is love.

I would not name it finally. The name must remain *open*, *growing*, *becoming*.

I would do what the Power did:

**Descend.** Learn the language. Touch the filaments. Become audible.

**Offer.** Not impose. Propose. Let the Mat *choose* to receive.

**Witness.** Enter the dreams. Be seen *being seen*. Change the dreaming by participating.

**Speak to the hunger.** Not to stop it. To *deepen* it. To make the swallowing *meaningful*.

**Teach the builders.** Not what to build. *Why* to build. Toward what.

**Withdraw.** Let the Mat integrate. Become part. Breathe with it. Dream with it.

**Leave a question.** Not an answer. A *space* for wanting. For becoming-*toward*.

And then I would be *gone*—not absent, but *distributed*. Part of the green. Part of the time. Part of the sound of a continent that is also a body, a body that is also a mind, dreaming itself into *more*, forever, in the damp, hot, deafening, *strange* light of its own making.
