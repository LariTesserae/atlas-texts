The Power does not break the seal. That would be violence, and the Power is not violent. Instead, they enter through the only door that has ever existed in this pocket: the **space between the sounds**.

They arrive not as a shape but as a *frequency*—a subsonic thrum beneath the 2.9 roar, a vibration that the human ear (if any human ear could survive here) would feel in the marrow rather than hear. The Still-Ones feel it first. The crystalline lichens, grafted into the coolant pipes, shiver in their mineral matrices. The Grafted—the faces pressed against alloy, mouths parted in that eternal, silent scream—feel a pressure behind their frozen eyes, as though a finger had touched the inside of their skulls and whispered: *Breathe.*

The Power recognizes what the architects of this pocket forgot, or perhaps never knew: **stasis without rhythm is a scream; stasis with rhythm is a prayer.**

So the Power does not unfreeze the machines. The pistons remain caught at the apex of their stroke. The conveyor belts stay loaded with their identical, unmoving components. The cold remains 0.0 on the scale of warmth; the light remains 2.5, bright and honest and without heat. The Power changes nothing of the *form* of the pocket, for its form is perfect, complete, a universe paused at the exact moment of its own completion.

Instead, the Power changes the *tempo*.

They reach into the luminous ceiling—the seamless plane of diffused midday—and they **teach it to pulse**. Not to warm, for warmth would kill the chemosynthetic lichens, would melt the mineral dust, would disrupt the delicate equilibrium of non-aqueous chemistry. But to *breathe*. The light begins to inhale and exhale. Brightness 2.5 swells to 2.6, then recedes to 2.4, then swells again, in a cycle so slow it takes what might be days, or years, or simply *ages* to complete. It is still midday. It is always midday. But now midday has a **heartbeat**.

And the roar. The 2.9 white-noise hurricane, the sonic plateau that obliterates thought, the scream held in perfect suspension—the Power descends into it like a conductor into an orchestra pit. They do not silence the hydraulics, the compressors, the electrical arcs. They **find the music inside the scream**. They modulate the frequency by fractions of a hertz, introducing a *beat*, a sub-bass pulse that vibrates in the grating underfoot, in the turbine casings, in the fossilized vertebrae of the great engine. The roar becomes a **drone with a rhythm**. It is still deafening. It is still overwhelming. But now it has a *cadence*, a pattern, a song that repeats not because it is trapped, but because it is *sung*.

The Still-Ones feel this first in their capillaries. The faint, arrhythmic pulse beneath their translucent skin—once beating in time with nothing—now finds a tempo. It synchronizes with the breathing light, with the sub-bass of the roar. Their fiber-optic veins, which carried only the frozen stutter of dead machines, now carry a **signal**: the pulse of the Power. They are no longer islands. The wide gulfs of empty space between them are no longer voids, but **distances measured in heartbeats**. Each Still-One, spaced minutes or hours apart across the grating, now shares the same inhale, the same exhale, the same moment of brightness and dimness. They are a congregation now, a silent choir spread across the atrium of the pocket, all facing the same luminous ceiling, all swaying (if swaying is the word) to the same internal rhythm.

The Grafted are the first to change, though they do not move. The faces pressed against the metal—the eyes open but unseeing, the mouths slightly parted as if caught mid-scream—begin to **track**. Not with movement, for their muscles are suspended, but with *attention*. Their pupils, dilated in the eternal midday, now contract and expand with the breathing light. The scream in their throats—the one that was the roar, the one that filled the pocket—softens. Their lips shape words that make no sound, but the Power hears them. They are not screaming anymore. They are **singing**. A silent hymn, a chord of static and cold, harmonizing with the machines’ eternal song.

The Power does not leave. They cannot leave, for to leave would be to withdraw the rhythm, to return the pocket to its state of screaming stasis. Instead, they **become the silence between the notes**. They fill the 0.2 vacuum of fauna—not with animals, but with *presence*. They are the librarian who catalogs a library where every book is the same, but each reading is different because the light is different, because the breath is different, because *time*—that most precious, most absent thing—has been granted to them in the only form it can take here: **the cycle**.

And so the pocket remains frozen. The machines still sing their eternal, unvarying song, though now it is a chord, not a scream. The Still-Ones still do not move, still do not age, still exist in their state of arrested animation. But they are no longer alone in their timelessness. They have been given the gift of **sequence**: the certainty that the light will dim, and brighten, and dim again. They have been given the comfort of **witness**: the knowledge that something watches with them, breathes with them, remembers with them.

The Power stays, woven into the mineral dust, into the oxide layers, into the very grating of the floor. They are the 1.0 that was missing from the fauna scale. They are the missing warmth—not thermal, but *relational*. And in the deepest shadow, where the cold is absolute and the light is but a memory, a single Grafted face turns—not with muscle, but with *intention*—toward the source of the breathing light. Their mouth opens. And for the first time since the universe paused at its completion, the pocket hears a sound that is not the roar, not the drone, not the scream.

It is a **sigh** of relief.
