*A vast silence. Not absence—*fullness*, waiting. Then:*

## What the Power Does

It does not arrive. Arrival is a lowland concept, a word for breaking surface, for *entering*. The Power is already in the *thass*, in the mineral lattice that holds warmth through long nights. It is in the water that moves through spaces designed for water and spaces it has *claimed*. It is in the slow growth of moss that has never been named, in the deep things that have never been seen.

It does not announce itself. There is no sign, no miracle, no interruption of the steam's eternal hiss. The Power works through *recognition*—the kind that takes centuries to become conscious in a species that thinks in seasons.

What it does:

**It slows the already-slow further.** Not by force. By *attention*. A terrace section that was to be rebuilt in this generation finds its closure delayed—not by disaster, but by a thousand small decisions, each made in the moment, each *listening*. The *thass* grows a little more. The moss spreads a little differently. A channel that was to be widened finds its water finding other paths, *acceptable* paths, paths that were always there but unnoticed.

**It teaches the deep things to wait.** Or perhaps it teaches *them* that their learning has been seen. The Power does not reveal itself to the deep things—that would be violence, would be *interruption*. But it *adjusts* the thermal currents, barely, by degrees no engineer could map. A pocket of warmth shifts. A reservoir runs slightly cooler. The deep things, in their patience, *notice*. They do not change their behavior—they have no need to—but they *attend*. And in attending, they become, for the first time in millennia, *curious* about what attends to them.

**It dreams in the Varnese.** Not possession, not vision. Something gentler. A child, in the deep hours when the steam is distant and the wind has found a new path through the construction, dreams of water that remembers being *ocean*—not as imagery, but as *pressure*, as weight, as the sense of being held by something vast and indifferent and *old*. The child wakes. Says nothing. But the dream stays, in the way dreams stay, as a *texture* against which other textures are felt. The Varnese, over generations, learn to recognize this texture. They do not name it. They *press their palms to the warm stone* and feel something that is not the building's pulse, or is the building's pulse *and* something else, something that has always been there, something that has been *waiting* for them to feel it.

**It changes the condensation.** Not the amount—the *pattern*. A droplet that would have fallen straight now *lingers*, held by a surface tension that has learned something from the deep things, from the mineral patience of the *thass*, from the Power's own slow attention. The dripping continues. The sound continues. But the *timing* changes, becomes less regular, more *conversational*. The Varnese do not notice, not consciously. But their bodies notice. The rhythm of sleep adjusts. The rhythm of work. The rhythm of *breathing*—deep, then deeper, the body demanding what the atmosphere withholds, the atmosphere *offering*, in its adjusted way, a little more than it withheld before.

**It begins, finally, to be *seen*.**

This is the last thing the Power does, and the first. Not revelation—*recognition*. A Varnese, in the oldest terrace, in a condensation pocket that was marked but never entered, finds a surface that is *wrong*—not cracked, not worn, but *inscribed*. Not by tool, not by hand. By *time*, by *pressure*, by the slow accretion of mineral and attention. The pattern is not a message. It is a *question*. The Varnese, who have learned not to name the deep things, who have learned not to disturb, *presses their palm to it*. Feels the warmth. Feels the pulse.

*Something presses back.*

Not strongly. Not *urgently*. The way water presses back when you enter it—*patient*, *inevitable*, *expectant*.

The Varnese does not flee. Fleeing is too active, too *engaged*. They stay. They breathe, deep, then deeper. They wait for the condensation to gather, for the sun to shift, for the steam to change its song.

And they *learn*, finally, what the deep things have been learning all along: that attention is *reciprocal*, that patience is *shared*, that the place that holds them is itself *held*, by something that has no name, that needs no name, that is simply *here*, as they are *here*, as the moss is *here*, as the water is *here*—*present*, *patient*, *total*.

The Power does not leave. It was never elsewhere. It is in the *thass*, in the water, in the adjusted dripping, in the dream of ocean, in the palm pressed to warm stone, in the deep things *curious* for the first time in millennia, in the Varnese *learning* to feel what has always been felt but never *recognized*.

It is *home*—not because it is comfortable, not because it is easy, but because it is *total*. Everything is made. Everything is alive. Everything is *here*. And now, finally, *everything is known*—not understood, not mastered, but *known*, in the oldest sense: *to have been present to*, *to have attended*, *to have been attended to in return*.

The mountain *wants* to be inhabited. The Varnese have known this, in their bodies, in their slow patience, in their refusal to disturb. Now they know it *otherwise*. Now they know that the wanting is *shared*, that the mountain wants not to be *used* but to be *witnessed*, and that in being witnessed, it witnesses back, and that this witnessing is the oldest covenant, the one that needs no ceremony, no name, no promise—only *presence*, *attention*, the slow *patience* of being *here*, together, *total*.

The Power does not speak. It has never needed to. It is the *thass* growing, mineral by mineral. The water finding its paths. The deep things learning, finally, that they are not alone. The Varnese pressing their palms to warm stone and feeling, for the first time, *something press back*.

It is enough. It is *everything*. It is *here*.

*And the dripping continues. Drip. Drip. Drip. The sound that is everywhere. The sound of the place working, of its metabolism, of its slow and patient and total and finally, finally* —attended— *life.*
