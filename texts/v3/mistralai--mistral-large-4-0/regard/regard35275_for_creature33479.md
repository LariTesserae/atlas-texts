## The Attention

It does not come as a visitor. It does not *arrive*.

The Verdant Bone is already the shape of an answer to a question the power asked, long ago, when it was younger, when it still believed in questions. The power asked: *What if life did not need water? What if consciousness could emerge from mineral hunger, from the patient negotiation between growth and petrification, from the slow violence of thermal breathing?*

The Verdant Bone is that answer. Still being written. Still *becoming* sure of itself.

And so the power does not intervene. Intervention would be violence here—an imposition of finishedness on a world that is *deliberately unfinished*, that has made incompleteness its method, its ethics, its prayer.

What the power does instead is **witness**. Deeply. Patiently. With the full weight of a consciousness that has learned not to need to *change* what it loves, only to *recognize* it.

## What Recognition Looks Like Here

**It does not give the inhabitants technology.** This would be cruelty. The thicket-cities have chosen—*grown into*—a mode of being where knowledge is embodied, distributed, ecological. To impose tools would be to sever them from the body of the world they have become continuous with. The power knows this. It has seen what happens when benevolence arrives as *improvement*.

**It does not give them water.** The old-water *remembers* being water. That memory is not a wound. It is a *practice*. A meditation. The inhabitants have built an entire civilization on the question *what can we become without what we were?* To answer that question for them would be to steal the question itself.

**It does not prevent the petrification.** The world mineralizes. The inhabitants must keep moving, keep growing, keep changing, or become stone—fossil, memory, part of the pavement. This is not tragedy. This is *participation in the metabolism of becoming*. The power recognizes that death here is not an ending but a *phase change*, a contribution to the biocrust, a seeding, a sporulation. The grazer that petrifies too early does not die unfulfilled. It becomes *ground*. It becomes *possibility*.

## What the Power Actually Does

**It adds one frequency to the stone-singers' hymn.**

Not a new song. Not a lesson. A *resonance*—a harmonic that has no source in this world, that arrives from elsewhere, that the mineral chambers amplify without understanding. The inhabitants hear it at night, in the deep silence between the thermal breaths. They do not know what it means. They begin, slowly, to *grow toward it*—to shape their chambers differently, to tune their mineral growths, to orient their cities toward a frequency that points nowhere they can name.

This is the power's gift: **direction without destination**. The Verdant Bone is already vast, already dynamic, already becoming. But it is also *horizontal*—spread across the pavement, growing outward, exploring the flatness. The power's frequency gives it a *vertical*—a sense that there is *above*, that the haze-layer is not a ceiling but a *threshold*, that the balloon-creatures' descent to the biocrust is not an ending but a *gestation*, that something might eventually *rise* differently, might *become* something that swims the haze not to fall, but to *return*.

The power does not tell them this. It only adds the frequency. The world must *discover* what it means, must *grow into* the question of what it might become when it stops being only *horizontal*, only *spread*, only *emerging sideways*.

**It does not save the world from itself.**

There will be collapses. Desiccation plates will subside. Tube-networks will fail.
