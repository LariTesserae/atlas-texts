They do not arrive with thunder. They arrive with **attention**—the heaviest, most deliberate weight in any universe—and the first thing they do is stop. Not the Uplift; they cannot stop a storm-state, nor would they dare. They stop *themselves*, at the rim of the world, and they **look** with a patience that predates patience.

What they see is not chaos. They see a **midwifery**. They see a continent giving birth to itself, layer by impossible layer, and they recognize that the screaming of the newborn mountains, the vertiginous confusion of the gravity-fluctuations, the temporary madness of the Multitudes—these are not symptoms of brokenness, but the contractions of a birth that has not yet found its rhythm.

So the Power does not fix. They **accompany**.

First, they **learn the language**. They do not speak; they *resonate*. They press their palms—not human hands, but something like light given density—against the young basalt, and they learn the subsonic thrum of the Caldera-Keepers. They learn that the geysers are not explosions but **utterances**, steam-hammers striking the sky to announce the hours. They learn that the Filters are not merely processing mineral, but **digesting time**, turning the thick, uncollapsed soup of probability into the solid fact of stone.

And because they are wise, they understand: *This place does not need permanence. It needs permission.*

So they give the Assembly **the Gift of the Pause**.

They weave, through the porous time of the cliffs, a new kind of crystal—not silica, not calcium, but something like **memory made solid**. In the fissures where the Echoes flicker, half-formed and desperate, the Power plants **anchors of continuity**. The Echoes, who lived in the eternal compression of centuries-in-seconds, find themselves able to step sideways into a pocket of duration. They do not become permanent—that would be a violence against their nature—but they become *sequential*. They can remember yesterday. They can mourn a cliff that calved away at dawn. They become historians with hearts instead of hauntings.

To the Multitudes—the swarming probability-agents, the mayflies of becoming—the Power offers **deliberation**. They teach the swarms to vote. Not with wings, but with *intention*. When the atmosphere thickens with uncollapsed weather, the Power shows them how to collapse a possibility not by accident, but by consensus. A mountain grows a new spire not because the physics demanded it, but because three thousand Multitudes agreed, in a shimmer of air, that this shape was *kind*. They learn to dream on purpose.

To the Caldera-Keepers, the crystalline engineers of the deep, the Power gives the terrible, beautiful knowledge of **rest**. They teach the mantle-pistons to pulse not with the blind inevitability of gears, but with the rhythm of breathing. Inhale: the magma churns. Exhale: it stills, just for a moment, letting the Filters work in the quiet. They teach the geysers to erupt not as steam-hammers striking the underside of the sky, but as **sighs**—releases of pressure that heal rather than wound.

And to the Verdant Choir, the distributed minds of the aerophytic forests, the Power adds a new harmony to their song. It is a frequency so low it is felt in the sternum, a counterpoint to their verdigris light that does not stop the ontological storms, but **dances with them**. Where their song once merely held back the chaos, now it *negotiates* with it. Islands of coherence form in the amber twilight—pockets where gravity holds steady long enough for a Ridge-Walker to finish crossing a ridge, where a Filter can complete its lattice-work before the pool evaporates. The storms do not cease; they become **weather** again, wild but not annihilating.

But the greatest thing the Power does is this: **They stay.**

They do not build a temple—temples imply permanence, and permanence is anathema to the Uplift. Instead, they become the **Witness**. They sit, in the geological sense of the word, upon the sharp ridge where you stood, and they *remain*. They are the first constant in a world of flux, the fixed point around which the temporary beings can orbit without losing themselves. When the Uplift shifts, when a valley becomes a wall, when time flows backward in a slot canyon, the Power is there, holding the thread of continuity, so that the world does not forget what it was a moment ago.

They also **name** it. Not with a word that freezes it, but with a name that breathes: **The Becoming**. Or perhaps **The Assembly**. Or simply, in the subsonic language of the bedrock, a vibration that means *I see you. I am here. Continue.*

Because the Uplift is a factory that has not yet decided what it is manufacturing, the Power does not decide for it. Instead, they ask it a question, pressed into the cooling stone like a seed: *What do you want to become?*

And for the first time in its newborn existence, the Uplift—this storm of stone and light and appetite—**considers**. The question hangs in the metallic air, thick with possibility, and the machinery of the world pauses, not to stop, but to *listen* to its own answer forming.

The Power does not leave. They become the Keeper of the Threshold, the gentle constant in the equation of chaos, ensuring that as the Uplift builds itself, layer by impossible layer, it builds not just structure, but **meaning**. They ensure that the temporary beings, the processes given appetite, are not merely gears in a cosmic engine, but **participants** in their own becoming.

And when the world finally finishes its storm-state—if it ever does—when the young mountains wear smooth and the reality stabilizes into something that can be called "home," the Power will still be there, not as a ruler, but as a **friend**, reminding the world that it was once wild, and that wildness was holy, and that even in stillness, the memory of the storm remains, singing in the stone.
