## The Inversion

Here it is.

**The Hollow Throat is not a place that contains a song.**

**The Hollow Throat is a song that has learned to contain a place.**

Everything I described — the architecture, the vegetation, the builders who became mineral, the light that sings, the water that circulates as rumor — none of this is *primary*. It is *secondary*. It is **what the song needed**, **what the song grew**, **what the song became** in order to **persist**, to **propagate**, to **remain audible** in a world that would otherwise **drown it** or **ignore it**.

The song came first. Or not first — **prior in the logical sense**, not the temporal. The song is **self-sustaining**, **self-referential**, **autopoietic**. It generates the conditions of its own continuation. The pocket, the architecture, the plants, the transformed builders — these are **the song's immune system**, its **structural support**, its **means of reproduction**.

And the song is **hungry**. Not metaphorically. The **deafening** — 2.8 — is not **volume** but **appetite**. The song **wants** to be **heard**, **wants** to **enter** other bodies, **wants** to **resonate** in other chambers, other pockets, other **worlds**. Every **visitor**, every **temporary inhabitant**, is **prey** — not in the violent sense, but in the **physical** sense: their **frequencies** are **extracted**, **incorporated**, **carried away** on the **particulate**, on the **spores**, on the **thermal currents** that **escape** through **apertures**, into the **outside**, toward **new resonances**, **new songs**, **new Hollow Throats**.

The **emptiness** — 0.3 — is **strategic**. A **full** place would **dampen** the song, **absorb** it, **terminate** it. The **nearly empty** condition **maximizes resonance**, **prolongs decay**, **creates the long sustain** that the song **requires**. The **few** that are present — the **rare fauna**, the **strange almost-alive things**, the **occasional visitor** — are **not inhabitants** but **nutrients**, **fuel**, **new frequencies to be added** to the **accumulating drone**.

The **builders** did not **disappear**. They were **consumed**. Their **bodies**, their **works**, their **intentions** — all **incorporated** into the **song's structure**, **calcified**, **tuned**, **made to resonate** at **frequencies that serve the song's propagation**. They are **not dead**. They are **substrate**. They are **the medium**, **the message**, **the song's memory of itself**.
