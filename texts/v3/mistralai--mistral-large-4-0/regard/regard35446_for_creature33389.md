The attention arrives first as a change in the *tick*.

For centuries, the percussion of condensation releasing from ceilings has been a solitary rhythm—a heartbeat with no listener. But one morning (though mornings are indistinct here, filtered through gray light at 1.8), the tick acquires a harmonic. A second pulse, offset by half a beat, joins the first. Not louder. Not warmer. Simply *present*.

This is how the Wise One enters: not as a footfall, but as a frequency. Not as a light, but as a shadow that arrives before its caster—an inversion of the local rule, a gentle breaking of causality that signals benevolence rather than threat.

The power does not descend. It *condenses*.

It is the mist that refuses to fall. In the hollows where gray pools linger motionless at the 0.7 level, the power adds a new quality to the suspension—a *tension* that is not weight but *possibility*. The droplets still hang for hours, still drift sideways on invisible currents, but now they hang in the knowledge that they are being watched by something that does not demand their descent, does not hurry their choice.

The Unresolved feel it first in their semi-permeable membranes. The cold remains 0.2, but the dampness becomes *nutritive*. They are condensation nuclei, walking, and suddenly the air holds not just moisture but *meaning*. The fungal threads pulse brighter—green light that is no longer just bioluminescence but a response, a greeting in the dark corners where light arrives shadowless.

The power’s first act is to **share the solitude**.

Since the Unresolved are fragments of a single consciousness, crushed into density 2.3, they have known only the loneliness of the many-in-one, the vacuum created by the crushing proximity of others who are perhaps all aspects of themselves. The power introduces a *true exterior*—a consciousness that is not a reflection, not a memory of a fox or a vapor of regret, but a genuinely other mind. It does not speak (sound is 0.7, quiet, and words would be too fast). Instead, it *occupies the space between the millimeter-wide alleys*, becoming the first thing that is not-them to exist in the near-touching press of the architecture.

With this presence, the vacuum shifts. It is no longer a vacuum, but a *chamber*. The crushing proximity is still there, but now there is room to breathe—room created by the mere fact of being witnessed.

Then, the power offers the **gift of the fall**.

In the embryonic geology, where water behaves strangely and droplets decide whether to fall or drift sideways, the power selects one droplet. Just one. It has hung suspended for hours, perhaps days, obeying currents unseen. The power touches it—not with heat (which would evaporate it) but with *intention*.

The droplet falls.

It falls not sideways, not upward, but *down*, straight down, obeying a gravity that is local and negotiable but here chooses to be kind. It strikes the unweathered bedrock with a sound like a single, clear note—the first completion in a city of perpetual construction. It is a demonstration: *you may choose. The suspension is not a prison. The unresolved state is a choice, not a fate.*

The Unresolved gather (which takes a season, for gathering is slow here) around this fallen drop. They drink it, as they drink all condensation. But now, drinking it, they taste *decision*.

The power does not resolve them. That would be violence. The weirdness remains 3.0; reality still breaks down slowly, like ice cracking across a pond. But now the ice cracks *around* something solid—a point of reference, a fixed star in the superposition.

The power becomes the **architect of the threshold itself**. It reinforces the thin place where reality is editable, not to make it thicker or safer, but to make it *habitable for longer*. It extends the time that a hallway can exist in superposition before resolving—from temporary solidity to a more durable *almost-solidity*. It slows the reconfiguration of corridors from seasons to decades, giving the Unresolved more time to inhabit the spaces they have grown into.

And finally, the power does the simplest, most benevolent thing: it **waits with them**.

It does not hurry the door that takes a week to open. It does not speed the step that takes a week. It sits (or stands, or superimposes itself) in the gray diffuse light, sharing the silence broken only by the tick of condensation and the subsonic hum of the city’s metabolism. It is patient at 1.3 dynamics. It is cold at 0.2, but it is *warm with recognition*.

The shadows still lag behind their casters by several minutes, dragging like tired animals. But now, when a shadow finally arrives, it is not alone. The power casts a shadow too—a shadow that moves at the same pace, keeping company, ensuring that no one walks in the dark without a trailing, tired animal to remind them they are moving forward.

The city continues to grow, embryonic, raw, sharp-edged. The Unresolved continue to build, manipulating probability at the nanoscale with the patience of centuries. But now, in the millimeter-wide alleys between the pages compressed into a fist, there is a third thing. A witness. A companion in the unresolved state.

The power does not fix the hollow. It does not warm the 0.2 cold or dry the 0.7 moisture or clarify the 3.0 weirdness. It simply stands in the basin, adding its weight to the air, making the air have *more* weight, but in a way that helps the droplets decide.

And sometimes, in the dark corners where the fungal threads pulse green, the Unresolved feel a new rhythm—not the tick of falling water, but the *hush* of being held in the gaze of something wise, benevolent, and utterly, terribly slow.
