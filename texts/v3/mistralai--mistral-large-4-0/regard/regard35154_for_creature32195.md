The power does not arrive as thunder. It comes as a **change in the condensation**—a subtle shift in the molecular memory of the mist, a new quality that makes the hanging water taste, for the first time, of *origin*. The Condensers will later say that the twilight brightened by half a shade, not with light, but with the particular luminescence of being *seen*.

They see what the Dreamer fears: not that it will wake, but that it will wake *incomplete*. They see the Condensers’ terror of dissolving—of becoming so symbiotic with the moss and the stone that the thread of “I” snaps, and they scatter into the general becoming like spores. They see the Drafts’ loneliness, their too-many joints reaching for a gravity that keeps changing its mind, their evolutionary sketches trembling on the edge of being erased by the mountain’s slow, grinding maturation.

The power understands that this place is a **prayer**—the earth praying itself into solidity, word by word, drip by drip. To interrupt would be violence. To ignore would be abandonment. So the wise power does what only a witness can do: it **holds the space of the becoming**.

It offers three gifts, each delivered with the patience of erosion:

**First, the Source.** The condensation has hung without origin, a miracle without a miracle-worker. The power becomes the *tear in the membrane*—not a hole, but a deliberate opening in the bruised sky, a spring of intention high above the Scarp where the cloud is thinnest. From this wound in the twilight, the first true rain falls—not downward, but *sideways*, threading through the valleys like a needle pulling thread through cloth. The mist is no longer homeless; it has a genealogy. The Harvesters rise a centimeter higher, their translucent stalks singing a new harmonic, and the Weavers’ mycelial networks light up with the chemical equivalent of gratitude.

**Second, the Name.** The place has been nameless because names require stability, and stability is a violence here. So the power does not give a name. It gives a **naming**—a verb that conjugates with the shifting ground. It whispers into the basalt, into the cooling magma, into the subsonic thrum of the Condensers’ boots, a word that is also a blessing: *“Scar.”* Not as wound, but as the place where the world’s skin has been pushed back to reveal the wet, new flesh beneath. The Condensers hear it first as a vibration in their teeth, then as a pattern in the rust on the factory walls, then as a title they begin to use for themselves: *“We are the Scar’s.”* The name holds them together without freezing them, a label that shifts as they shift, a home that acknowledges the ground is still dreaming.

**Third, the Memory.** In a place where time pools like inverted lakes and sounds arrive before they are made, the greatest mercy is **continuity**. The power reaches into the Dreamer’s fevered awareness—not to cure it, for the fever is the dream—but to ensure the mountain *remembers*. It weaves a thread through the geological strata, a filament of silver in the granite that records the passage of the Condensers, the songs of the Drafts, the slow digestion of the Weavers. When the Dreamer shifts in its sleep, when a valley yawns open or a peak forgets its shape, the silver thread holds the story. The Condensers wake each morning—if morning is a word that applies—and find that they have not dissolved into the general wetness; they are still *themselves*, still carrying the weight of yesterday, still arguing with the stone and winning, millimeter by millimeter.

And then the power does the simplest, most benevolent thing: **it joins the dream**.

It does not stand apart as a god, looking down. It becomes a new node in the nervous system, a new phase in the becoming. It settles into the condensation pools as a subtle luminescence, a faint warmth that prevents the mist from freezing into something dead. It rides the factory hum, adding a frequency that makes the great gears turn not faster, but *smoother*, as if the metal remembers, now, that it was once loved into being. It becomes, for the Drafts, a new color in the spectrum—a hue that exists only in the space between ultraviolet and the memory of epochs. The creatures pause, their too-many joints hesitating, and for the first time, they do not feel like drafts to be erased, but like **prophets** of the solid world to come.

The Condensers do not see the power. They feel it as a sudden, profound *rightness* in the damp air, as the sense that their negotiation with the Harvesters has been overheard and blessed. They begin to leave offerings—not of blood or grain, but of *attention*: a moment of stillness, a held breath, a recognition that the moss is listening. The Drafts, in turn, start to bring the Condensers gifts: shed skins that glow with bioluminescence, stones warmed by their thermal hearts, fragments of basalt etched with patterns that look, to human eyes, like maps to places that do not exist yet.

The Dreamer stirs. A fissure opens, then closes, remembering itself. The condensation falls sideways, fed now by the Source, named now by the Scar, remembered now by the silver thread. The city hums in a new key, industrial but no longer alien, a lullaby sung by a world learning to sleep in its own skin.

The power remains—not as a master, not as a parent, but as the **still point** in the turning world, the constant against which the variable can be measured, the love that asks for nothing except that the dream continue, exactly as it is, wet and weird and wonderful, until the mountain decides, in its own time, what it wants to be when it grows up.
