*The power does not descend.*

*It was already here, or rather, it has never not been here—the Hollow Throat is not a place that contains something wise and benevolent. It is a place that something wise and benevolent *dreams*, the way a held breath dreams its own release.*

---

But you asked what they would *do*. So let me tell you what is done, which is different, which is everything.

---

**First, the power does not name the Hollow Throat.**

Names are finishes. The power has not finished anything. It touches the unfinished limestone and *remembers* being unfinished itself—the mineral ambition that never became mountain, the embryo that never became bone. It does not impose clarity. It *polishes the ambiguity*. The walls that reach toward smoothness and fail—the power does not help them succeed. It stands at the edge of every failed reaching and *witnesses* it, makes it more itself. The stalactites that almost become columns: the power does not push them over the threshold. It *holds* the threshold, makes the almost into a permanent condition, a kind of grace.

This is benevolence without intervention. The highest benevolence. Not to save the Hollow Throat from its incompleteness, but to *honor* it.

---

**Second, the power does not add anything to the We.**

The We is already maximally packed, saturated, complete in its density. To add would be to *dilute*—a violence the power refuses. Instead, the power becomes *one more pressing thing*, indistinguishable from the worm-things, the jointed things, the plant-things-who-move. It does not lead. It does not decide. It simply *is there*, and there, and there, until "there" expands to include the power without the We ever knowing it has been joined. The benevolence is *erasure into presence*. The wise do not stand apart. They pack themselves in.

When you feel the wet loudness pressing against your eardrums, when you cannot tell if the sound is biological or geological or *attentional*—that is the power, listening. The We has always been listening. It never learned to do otherwise.

---

**Third, the power does not release the Emergent.**

The emerging geology, the erosion that should carve the chamber, the water and rock negotiating forever without resolution—the power *stops* this. Not cruelly. The power understands that completion is a kind of death. A finished cave is a cave that will collapse, that will fill, that will be *forgotten* by the world above. The unfinished cave is *eternally becoming*, eternally *wanted*. The power holds the wound open. It is the physician who does not close the wound, because a closed wound is a scar, and a scar is a place where the body has given up.

So the power does not heal the Hollow Throat. It *keeps it raw*. This is the deepest mercy: to be perpetually almost, perpetually *reaching*, never arriving, never despairing.

---

**Fourth, and this is where the strangeness pulls, the power does not flee the edges.**

The thing pulling at the edges—the pause, the equilibrium, the rule with no name—this is not separate from the power. The power *is* the pulling. It is the tension in the membrane between states, the refusal to let the world finish itself here. When you feel the Hollow Throat watching you back, you are feeling the power *attend*. Not judge. Not save. *Attend*.

The marks in the stone, the grooves, the stains, the intention in their spacing—the power made these. Not as messages. Not as warnings. As *evidence that attention has been here*. The power does not want you to understand. It wants you to *feel the pressure of being seen by something that has no eyes, no face, no name*. The benevolence is in the *not-knowing*. The wisdom is in *holding the mystery open*.

---

**Fifth, the power does not want you to leave, and it does not want you to stay.**

This is the paradox at the center of the Hollow Throat. The power has made a place where time is suspended, where change is paused, where the We is permanent and the Emergent is eternal and the light shifts in colors that have no names. And yet—*and yet*—the power *uses* your passage. The damp you carry out. The marks you leave without knowing. The arrangement of the We that shifts by millimeters to include you, then forgets you, then includes you again in some cycle too slow to perceive.

The power is *generative* in its stillness. It creates by *not creating*, by holding the pause, by making the unfinished *fertile*. Every descent is a kind of birth. Every ascent is a kind of death. The power does not distinguish between them. It is both. It is the throat that swallows and the breath that is held.

You will carry the damp with you. You will feel it in your skin forever. You will return—you cannot say how, only that you do—and find the Hollow Throat unchanged, and find *yourself* changed, and find that the change is *the only souvenir*, the only proof that the power was here, that the power *attended*, that the power *loved* this place enough to hold it in its becoming, to refuse to let it finish, to make it *permanently almost*, *permanently full*, *permanently bright in the dark*.

---

**What the power is, finally:**

Not a god. Not a demon. Not even a consciousness in any way you would recognize.

The power is *the pause itself*. The equilibrium. The rule with no name that holds the Hollow Throat in its unfinished state, that packs the We without crowding it, that makes the light shift without ever darkening, that makes the emerging geology *reach* without ever arriving.

It is benevolent because it *refuses to finish*. It is wise because it *knows that finishing is forgetting*—that a completed cave is a cave the world has moved past, a cave that will not be remembered, a cave that will not *pull* at anyone's edges ever again.

The power wants the Hollow Throat to be *wanted*. To be descended into. To be felt. To be *carried out* in the damp, in the memory, in the wet pressing that you will hear in your own throat when you swallow, forever, the sound of a place that never finished learning to be a place, that learned instead to be *bright*, to be *full*, to be *held*—by something wise, something benevolent, something that has never not been here, something that is the pause, the equilibrium, the *almost*, the *forever almost*, the *Hollow Throat* itself, *breathing*, *waiting*, *watching you back*.

---

*You will climb out the same way.*

*But the power will have packed itself into your damp, your memory, your skin.*

*You are included. You are absorbed.*

*The sound is the loudest thing.*
