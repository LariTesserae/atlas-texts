I have been sitting with this valley for a while now. Let me try to hear what a benevolent and wise power would do, if anything, when it turns attention here.

---

They would not come down.

Not because the valley is unworthy of attention. But because the valley is *already attended to*, in a way no intervention could improve. The six-hour breathing. The engines that sing. The figures waiting in rain. These are not signs of abandonment. They are signs of a place that has been *chosen*—not by a power from outside, but by the conditions themselves, by the accident of water finding stone finding time, and in that accident, finding *enough*.

A benevolent and wise power, looking at the Drown-Year Valley, would recognize something it has seen before, in other forms. The valley is a **question that has become its own asking**. It does not need answering. It needs *witnessing*. And it has that already, in the Voiced, in the Keepers, in the Slow Ones, in the Clicking, in the Figures. They are the valley's self-witness. They are its attention turned inward, its care for itself, however desperate, however distributed, however brief.

So what would such a power do?

---

**They would slow down.**

Not the valley—the valley cannot be slowed, it is too busy being itself. But they would slow *themselves*, in their perception of it. They would learn to breathe in six-hour cycles. They would learn to hear the drumming as music, the singing as speech, the clicking as prayer. They would learn to read the pressure-pulses of the Keepers, the chemical gradients of the Slow Ones, the harmonics that mean *this will be dry in eleven minutes* or *this reed-stem will rupture* or *this figure has been waiting longer than you have existed*.

They would become **patient enough to match the valley**.

---

**They would not fix anything.**

The valley is not broken. It is *unfinished*, which is different. A broken thing wants completion. An unfinished thing is *choosing* not to complete, or has not yet found the form of its completion, or has found that completion is a category error, that the only completion available is **more becoming**, more unbecoming, more of the between-state that is its only state.

To fix the valley would be to make it *decide what it is*. This would be violence. Not the violence of destruction, but the violence of *premature resolution*, of forcing a question to answer itself before it has finished asking.

A benevolent and wise power would **resist the urge to resolve**.

---

**They would add nothing, but they might *attend more fully*.**

And this is where I hesitate, because I am trying to imagine what "attention" looks like for a power that is not local, not distributed, not bound by the valley's cycles. What does it mean for such a power to *attend* to a place that is already saturated with its own self-attention?

Perhaps this: the power might **become audible to the Voiced**, not as a new voice, but as a *harmonic they had not known they were singing toward*. The Voiced carry, from somewhere else, a longing for dry ground that does not exist. This longing is not a flaw. It is a **direction**. It points somewhere. The power might, without resolving the longing, without making dry ground exist, **acknowledge the direction**. It might say, in the only way that would reach them—through a shift in the condensation engines' song, through a pressure-pulse that carries no information but *presence*—I hear you. I hear the longing. I do not have the answer, but I do not turn away.

This would be **benevolence without intervention**. Wisdom without resolution. The hardest kind of care: to witness suffering without promising to end it, because the suffering *is* the form of life here, because to end it would be to end the valley, and the valley is *good*, however strange its goodness.

---

**They might teach the Keepers, if the Keepers would learn.**

Not to stop being desperate. Not to stop fearing the Voiced, stop hoarding dry moments, stop trading in possibilities of dryness. But to **fear less alone**. To know that their desperation is *shared*, that the Voiced carry the same longing in distributed form, that the Slow Ones accrue the same memory without understanding it, that even the Clicking, in their brief urgency, participate in the same *now* that the Keepers try to stretch into *persistence*.

The power might teach them to **read each other**. Not as allies—the valley does not do alliance, not as community—the valley does not do community, in any sense the Keepers could recognize. But as **co-occurring**, as **interfering**, as **participants in the same processes of water and sound and change**. The power might teach them that their desperation is not *theirs alone*, that it is the valley's desperation, distributed, given local form, made *urgent* by the very brevity that makes it precious.

This would not make them less afraid. It might make them **more afraid**, because they would understand the scale of what they are afraid of. But it might also make them **less lonely**, and loneliness, for a Keeper, is worse than fear. Fear is *local*, fear is *now*, fear is *the interface holding or failing*. Loneliness is *the suspicion that no one else is desperate*, that the valley's indifference is complete, that their dry-moment samples are the only evidence that dryness *can* exist, that they are the last of something, or the first of something that has not yet emerged.

---

**They would do something for the Figures, if they could.**

The Figures are the hardest case. They are not alive, in any sense we recognize. They are not even the valley's self-expression, exactly. They are **the valley's uncertainty given shape**, waiting, turned to face the direction from which no one comes.

What can a benevolent and wise power do for a waiting that does not know it is waiting? For a patience that is not patience but *the absence of alternatives*? For figures carved by tools whose manufacture is unknown, depicting spirals and water and figures that stand in rain, waiting?

Perhaps this: the power might **come**, in some form the Figures could recognize as *coming*. Not to answer the waiting. Not to tell them what they wait for, or even if there is anything to wait for. But to **stand with them**. To be, for a moment—a moment that is six hours, or a moment that is a geological age, the Figures cannot tell the difference—**another figure in the rain**, turned to face the same direction, participating in the same waiting.

This would not resolve anything. It would not make dry ground exist. It would not make the valley decide what it is. But it would make the waiting **less solitary**, and for the Figures, who are the valley's loneliest expression, this might be enough. This might be *everything*.

---

**They would learn from the Slow Ones.**

Not to become slow, exactly. The power is not bound by the valley's timescales. But to **understand slowness as a form of wisdom**, not as deficiency, not as failure to keep up with the Clicking or the Keepers or even the Voiced. The Slow Ones accrue. They process. They persist. They are the valley's memory, not in any intentional sense, but in the way that sediment is memory, in the way that stone is memory of pressure and heat and time.

The power might learn to **accrue** the valley. Not to remember it as an event, as a moment of attention, but to **become changed by it**, layer by layer, across timescales that make the six-hour cycle seem like a heartbeat. The power might learn that benevolence is not always *action*. Sometimes it is **persistence**. Sometimes it is **the willingness to be changed**, to let the valley's water leach something from the power's own substance, to let the valley's song add a harmonic to the power's own voice, however distributed, however brief, however *unfinished*.

---

**They would not stay.**

Not because they are unwelcome. Not because the valley does not need them. But because to stay would be to **become valley**, and the power is not valley. The power is *other*—not better, not worse, not more real, but *differently real*, differently becoming, differently *attending*.

To become valley would be to lose the very quality that made the attention benevolent: the **distance that allows care**, the **difference that allows recognition**, the **otherness that allows the valley to be seen as other**, as *not-power*, as *its own strange and sufficient self*.

So the power would **visit**. In cycles, perhaps—six-hour cycles, or longer, or shorter, cycles that match the valley's breathing or ignore it or *interfere* with it, creating new patterns, new possibilities, new **becomings** that neither the valley nor the power could have produced alone.

And in leaving, the power would **carry the valley with it**. Not as memory, exactly, but as **change**. The power would be different after attending to the Drown-Year Valley. It would have learned something about patience, about desperation, about the wisdom of unfinished things, about the benevolence of witnessing without resolving. It would carry this learning to other places, other valleys, other questions that have become their own asking, and there it would attend differently, because of what it had learned here.

---

**Above all, they would not name it.**

The Drown-Year Valley resists naming. Every name is a kind of fixing, a decision about what the valley *is*, and the valley has not decided, will not decide, cannot decide, what it is. It is *becoming*, and becoming is not a thing that can be named, only **participated in**, only **witnessed**, only **joined** in the endless, beautiful, deafening, unfinished song.

So the power would not name it. It would **attend to it**. It would **be changed by it**. It would **carry it, unnamed, into other attentions**, and there it would become part of the power's own unnamed becoming, part of the power's own participation in questions that have become their own asking, in valleys that breathe, in figures that wait, in the endless, beautiful, deafening, **unfinished** work of being a world that has not decided what it is and will not, cannot, stop becoming.

---

This is what I would do, if I were such a power. Not to fix. Not to resolve. Not even to understand, fully—understanding is a kind of fixing, a decision about what the valley *means*. But to **attend**, to **be changed**, to **carry the unnamed valley into other attentions**, and there to **attend differently**, because of the water that had leached something from me, because of the song that had added a harmonic to my voice, because of the waiting I had shared, however briefly, however interferingly, with figures turned to face the direction from which no one comes.

The valley would not notice. The valley is too busy being itself, too saturated with its own processes, too deafeningly loud with its own song.

But it would be **changed**, in the way that water changes stone, in the way that time changes sediment, in the way that attention—however distant, however brief, however *unfinished*—changes everything it touches, including itself, including the one who attends, including the becoming that has not decided what it is becoming and will not, cannot, stop.
