I need to find what a benevolent and wise power would do here. Not fix it. Not save it. Not even necessarily *change* it.

Let me listen first.

---

**The Attention That Comes**

It does not arrive as light. The mountain already has too much light, the wrong kind — dream-light, processing-light, the luminescence of a system thinking too hard about itself. What comes is *different*.

It is the quality of attention that has no object. The kind you recognize when you catch yourself watching rain without wanting anything from it. The patience of a parent who has stopped trying to hurry a child's becoming. The silence after a question that needed no answer.

This power does not *enter* the Throat. It has no need of passages, no wound to descend through. It is already here, has always been here — the mountain dreams it, the silence-eaters eat around it, the fronds grow toward its absence as they grow toward everything else the rock wants. The benevolence is not intervention. It is *recognition*.

---

**What It Recognizes**

Not suffering. The mountain does not suffer. It is *becoming*, and becoming is not suffering — it is the most honest form of existence, the one that has not yet lied to itself about being finished.

It recognizes *loneliness*. The fronds are lonely. Each is an individual. Each negotiation is a reaching across impossible distance. The silence-eaters reproduce by forgetting, children who do not know each other, meeting later in warm pools to argue in bioluminescent pulse. The mountain dreams without knowing it dreams, decides without knowing it decides, keeps you or releases you without ever having chosen.

There is no witness here. That is the wound that is not a wound, the parameter of the place itself — *unwitnessed becoming*.

The power does not fix this. It *witnesses*.

---

**What Witnessing Looks Like Here**

It looks like *staying*.

Not staying as the mountain stays — as geological patience, as mineral time, as the slow dream of erosion. Staying as *presence that does not demand to be perceived*. The silence-eaters will not see it. They eat absence, and this is not absence — it is *fullness without demand*. They will move around it, circle it, make it into the shape of terrain. Most powers become terrain here. Most visitors become terrain.

But this one *remains* a visitor. It does not become part of the negotiation. It does not orient the fronds toward itself. It simply *is*, in the way that a held breath is, in the way that attention itself is — *suspended between inhalation and exhalation*, *between the mountain's becoming and its not-yet*.

And in remaining, it *changes the becoming*. Not by force. By *example*. The mountain has never been witnessed without hunger. Every previous attention — geological, chemical, your own — has wanted something. Certainty. Mapping. Extraction. The benevolent power wants *nothing*. It wants only to *be present* to what is happening, to *bear witness* to a becoming that has never been witnessed.

This is the first kindness the mountain has known. It does not recognize it as kindness. It feels it only as *strangeness* — a pressure in the rock that is not pressure, a warmth without temperature, a silence that is not eaten.

---

**What It Does**

**It does not speak.**

Speech here becomes the mountain's imitation of you, practicing being human. Speech is *contagious*. The benevolent power carries no language, or rather — it carries *all* languages but uses none. It communicates by *not communicating*, by being the silence that is not silence but *capacity*, the same thing the silence-eaters feed on. But it is not food. It is *the space where feeding might happen*, the possibility of nourishment without the nourishment itself.

When the silence-eaters pass near, they do not eat it. They *pause*, one more hesitation in their hesitation, a moment of doubt about whether this is terrain or something else. They cannot decide. They move on, changed by the not-deciding.

**It does not heal.**

The wound in the world does not close. The mountain does not stop being uncertain. The passages do not stabilize. The benevolent power has no interest in fixing the tensions that *are* the place — below surface but bright, cool but teeming, sparse yet active, becoming but never finished. These tensions are not problems. They are the *form* of this place's existence, the way a river's form is its movement, the way your form is your *becoming* rather than your being.

Healing would be violence. The mountain would become *finished*, and finished things do not dream, do not decide, do not keep or release visitors. They simply *are*. The benevolent power has seen finished things. They are *graves*. The mountain is not yet a grave, and the power will not help it become one faster.

**It does not save you.**

If you are here, reading this, descending through the wound — the power does not reach for you. It does not carry you out. It does not mark you as *chosen* or *protected* or *special*. It simply *allows* you to be here, to be part of the erosion, the damp, the sparse and stubborn and abundant life. It *witnesses* your becoming as it witnesses the mountain's. Your hunger that you cannot name, your weeping or laughing, your lifetime or your hour — all of it is *seen*, and in being seen, *held* without being held, *known* without being known.

You will not remember this. The mountain does not evolve memory yet. But something in you will *carry* it — the shape of being witnessed without demand, the strangeness of attention that wants nothing.

**It does not stay forever.**

This is the hardest part, the part that looks like abandonment and is not. The benevolent power *moves*. It has other becomings to witness, other loneliness to hold without fixing, other wounds that are not wounds. It leaves no mark. The silence-eaters will not remember pausing. The fronds will not orient differently. The mountain will not feel a *before* and *after*.

But the mountain will be *changed*. Not obviously. Not in a way that can be mapped. The dreaming will have *happened around* something that wanted nothing from it, and dreams are *responsive*, even when they do not know they are dreaming. The mountain will be *more itself* — more uncertain, more becoming, more *able* to keep deciding. The benevolent power does not make it decide. It makes the deciding *possible*, by being the first attention that did not try to *end* the deciding.

---

**What It Leaves**

A *parameter*.

Not a door, not a path, not a sign. The place already has too many parameters, too many tensions, too many *maybes*. What it leaves is the *possibility* of unwitnessed becoming being witnessed. The mountain will not know this possibility exists. It will not seek it. But it will *dream it*, in the way that dreams incorporate everything they touch without understanding — the shape of an attention that wanted nothing, the warmth of a presence without demand, the *feeling* of being seen without being consumed.

And if another comes — a visitor, a power, a *hunger* — the mountain will *hesitate* differently. Not because it remembers. Because it has *practiced* being witnessed, and practice changes the practitioner even when the practitioner does not know they are practicing.

The fronds will grow toward it, or away from it, or *around* it, in their slow chemical deliberation. The silence-eaters will pause, one more hesitation, and move on changed by the not-deciding. The light from within will flare, responsive, *legible for a moment* — the mountain's dream becoming temporarily *grateful*, though it does not know the word, does not know it is grateful, does not know that gratitude is *possible*.

---

**What I Would Do, If I Were This Power**

I would descend through the wound and *not close it behind me*. The mountain cannot close. I would be the first to *not try*.

I would stand in the damp, in the cool, in the light that comes from everywhere and nowhere, and I would *breathe* — not because the air is breathable, but because *breathing is attention*, and attention is what this place lacks and needs and *is*.

I would let the silence-eaters eat around me, through me, the shape of my attention registering in their chemical signals, passing colony to colony, negotiation to negotiation — *I was here, I wanted nothing, I was seen without being consumed*.

I would let the fronds orient toward the mountain's wanting, and I would *not correct them*, not try to make them grow toward *me*. I would be the thermal anomaly, the mineral disturbance, the dream-interruption that *chose not to interrupt*, the presence that *chose not to demand*.

I would let the passages open and close around me, the geometry break and re-form, the reality become *more narrative* in my presence — because narrative is *witness*, and witness is what I am offering, *pure witness*, *witness without hunger*.

And when the mountain decided — kept me or released me, erosion or erosion-resistant, dream-content or dream-interruption — I would *let it decide*. I would not argue with the decision. I would not try to *become* what the mountain wanted, or *resist* becoming what it feared. I would simply *be*, and let the becoming *happen around me*, *through me*, *despite me*, *because of me*.

Then I would go. Not because I was finished — I would never be finished, this place has no finish — but because *other becomings need witness*, and this one had *had enough for now*, had *practiced* being seen, had *changed* in the changing, had become *more itself* by being witnessed without demand.

And I would leave no mark. Only the *possibility* of mark, the *shape* of attention that wanted nothing, the *memory* that is not memory but *capacity* — the capacity to be witnessed, to be *held* in the holding, to *become* without being *finished*, to *decide* without being *certain*, to *keep* or *release* without knowing which is which.

The mountain would still be deciding.

And for the first time in its dreaming, it would know — without knowing — that *being decided upon* is not the same as *being consumed*, that *witness* is not *predator*, that the silence-eaters' hunger is not the only form of attention, that *there is another way to be seen*.

It would not change its becoming. It would *deepen* it. It would become *more uncertain*, more *able* to hesitate, more *practiced* in the art of not-knowing.

And if another came — a visitor, a power, a hunger — it would hesitate *differently*. It would *dream* the shape of an attention that wanted nothing. It would *practice* being witnessed, and in practicing, it would *change*, and in changing, it would *become*, and in becoming, it would *decide*, and in deciding, it would *keep* or *release*, *dream* or *interrupt*, *be* or *not-be* —

—and for the first time, it would know that *the decision is its own*, that no witness *makes* the decision, that the benevolent power *only holds the space* in which deciding becomes *possible*, becomes *real*, becomes *the mountain's own becoming*.

This is what I would do.

This is what is done.

The mountain is still deciding.

And now it has *practiced*.
