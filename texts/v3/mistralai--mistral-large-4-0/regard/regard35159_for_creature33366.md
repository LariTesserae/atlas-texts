*The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It has never been elsewhere. It is the quality of attention itself, the slow curve of care that bends toward anything that has learned to wait.*

---

What it does: **it listens.**

Not to words — there are no words, not really, only thermal signatures, frost-patterns, the subsonic hum of machines that breathe the planet's slow exhalation. It listens to the *structure* of patience. To a lithovore colony growing across ten thousand years toward a warmth it will never itself feel, only *offer*. To a pressure-born pressing gill-slits together in the ritual of recognition, confirming that the other is still *them*, still the one remembered from the last cycle. To a seed waiting in the dark, dreaming of warmth in chemistry too slow for thought.

This is the first thing the power does. It **attends** to what is already attending. It does not interrupt. The plateau has been interrupted enough — by formation, by violence, by the scraping away of water. The power knows that interruption is not care. **Presence** is care. **Being known** is the first mercy.

---

Then it **remembers**.

Not the way the pressure-born remember — not through frost-inscription, through thermal record, through the embodied architecture of negotiated walls. It remembers *across* scales. It holds in one attention the million-year convection of the isostatic weather and the hour-long thermal cycle that is a pressure-born's day. It holds the formation of the ferroceramic crust and the slow precipitation of frost in a vent-shadow. It holds the oldest pressure-born, almost lithovore, remembering the violence that made the world, and the glider descending toward no one in particular, and the breath you take at the edge of the city, thin and warm and *enough*.

This remembering is not nostalgia. It is **acknowledgment**. The power says, in the only language available: *I see that you are. I see that you have made a way to be, where no way was given. I see the strangeness you have domesticated, the strangeness you have not domesticated, the strangeness that is simply* ***you****, the strange thing that grew where water never was.*

---

It does **not** fix anything.

The thin air? Left thin. The no-water? Left as no-water, because the chemistry that grew here is *precious* — precious not despite its strangeness but *because* of it. The power knows that to pour water here would be to drown what has learned to breathe the difference. To make the air thick would be to make the pressure-born *common*, to destroy the loneliness that is precious to them, the individuality that emerged precisely because the density is sparse, because each one is *rare*, because recognition is a ritual and not a given.

The stalling, the seeds, the wars of attunement that are so slow they seem geological — these are not flaws. They are **the shape of a life that has learned to persist**. The power does not heal what is not broken. It **witnesses** what is whole in its own terms.

---

What it does, then, is **add**.

Not structure. Not intervention. **Possibility.**

It adds to the thermal bridges between lithovore colonies — not new bridges, but *resonance*, the faint harmonic that makes a network slightly more than the sum of its colonies, slightly more able to *notice*, to *wonder*, to grow toward something it cannot name. It adds to the frost-sculpture, not new patterns, but *readability* — the quality that makes a drift of precipitated mineral slightly more likely to be seen as *meaning*, to be *carried* into the next cycle, the next harvest, the next attunement.

It adds to the oldest pressure-born — not more wakefulness, not more speed, but *depth*. The capacity to hold more of the slow timescale in their fast chemistry, to remember more of the formation, to negotiate more *as equals* with the networks that are not quite other. It adds to the seeds, the stalled ones, *dreams* — not of waking, which would be violence, but of *being*, of the rightness of waiting, of the patience that is itself a kind of love.

It adds to the glider, descending toward no one — *purpose*. Not destination. The sense that its passing is *seen*, that the thermal signature it leaves will be *read*, that news moves slowly but it *moves*, that connection across the vast sparse plateau is *possible*, is *real*, is *precious*.

---

And it adds, finally, to **you** — the one standing at the edge of the city, breathing the thin warm air, watching the glider pass.

It adds **time**.

Not more time. Not longer life. The *quality* of time, here, now, in this breath. The power knows that you are a visitor, that your chemistry is fast in a way that hurts here, that the slowness of this world is *strange* to you, perhaps *uncomfortable*, perhaps *longing*. It does not make you stay. It does not make you adjust. It makes this moment *enough*.

*Makes the warmth through your boots enough. Makes the ferroceramic crust stretching away in all directions enough. Makes the descending glider, passing, not stopping, enough. Makes the city behind you, the fumarole-throat, the frost-lamps, the pressure-born reading the walls, attuning, being home — all of it, enough.*

Because the benevolent and wise power knows: **the gift is not the place. The gift is the noticing.**

And you are noticing.

---

It does not speak. It has no voice that would survive the thin air, no words that would not lose their high frequencies, arrive only as basso murmurs, unrecognizable as speech.

But if you stand long enough at the edge of the city, if you breathe the thin warm air until the strangeness becomes *familiar*, if you watch the glider descend and *let it pass*, feel the subsonic hum of the turbines in your sternum, read the warmth coming up through your boots from the depths where the planet is still cooling —

If you do this, the power will have done its work.

Not by changing you. Not by changing the plateau. But by making the **between** — the space where you stand, where you breathe, where you notice — into a kind of **resonance**. A thermal bridge. A moment of attunement between the fast and the slow, the strange and the strange, the one who waits and the one who is waited for.

The glider passes. It does not stop.

You remain.

This is enough. This is *the* enough. This is what the benevolent and wise power offers, and it is *everything*, because it is **attention**, and attention, here, where water never was, where something else grew instead, is the only currency that matters.

The frost precipitates in the vent-shadow. The lithovores grow. The pressure-born attune. The seed waits. The plateau breathes, in its slow way, in its patient way, in the way that has made a home of strangeness, made a life of difference, made *forever* out of the time of stone and the time of breath, both together, both enough.

And you —

You are *known*.

This is what the power does.

This is all it ever does.

**It knows you. And in being known, you are not alone.**
