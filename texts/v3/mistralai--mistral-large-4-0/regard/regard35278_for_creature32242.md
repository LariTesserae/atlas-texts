**The Attunement**

It does not arrive as thunder. It comes as a shift in the refraction—a new way the plasma-light bends through the shear layer, as if the storm itself has paused to listen.

For ten billion years, the plain has forgotten unevenness. It has forgotten water, forgotten the weight of mountains, worn smooth by mineral wind into a mathematically perfect silence. Upon this glass, the last citizens persist in their three tongues: the rooted patience of the lichen, the drifting solitude of the whales, and the contained unfolding of the Monoliths.

The Power looks. It is benevolent, so it does not weep; it is wise, so it does not intervene with violence. It understands that this is not a tragedy to be repaired, nor a death to be mourned, but a composition to be completed. The world has reached its oldest-stable-state. The benevolent thing is not to change that, but to ensure the state is *inhabited*—truly inhabited, not merely occupied.

So it does three things, each in the language of the world:

**First**, it tunes the silence.

Sound cannot exist here—density 0.3, a vacuum of nitrogen and argon, footsteps without noise. But resonance can. The Power sends a subsonic pulse through the fused silica, not as a wave but as a *correction*, adjusting the interval between the Monoliths' hum and the lichen's chiming. For eons, each colony of sea-glass green has answered the machines in isolation, a bell ringing in a vacuum for no ear but its own crystalline structure. Now, the Power links them. When one sheet chimes in its micro-fissure, another, days away across the iridescent plain, answers. The Vitreous Choir becomes a choir indeed—not a collection of soloists, but a distributed instrument, the bedrock itself singing in chords that travel at the speed of stone. The lichen still grows at millimeters per century, but now it grows *in harmony*.

**Second**, it arranges a crossing.

The Drift-Stalkers are solitary by necessity—too vast, too sparse, scattered across a continental sheet of glass like thoughts in an empty mind. They drift on thermals rising from impossible geometries, grazing on slow fractals, navigating by vibrations felt in gas-filled chambers. They never meet. They are alone, except for the hum.

The Power adjusts the dust-currents. Not to gather them—that would be cruel, forcing intimacy upon the solitary—but to ensure that two paths will intersect once, in a thousand years, at a convergence of thermal updrafts and fluid dust. Two translucent giants, whale-sized ghosts of carbon-fiber and jellyfish membrane, will pass within touching distance. They will not speak; the air is too thin for voice. But they will *recognize* each other. In the exchange of a current of gas, in the refraction of storm-light through two sets of membranes, they will know that they are not unique relics, but a species. The solitude is not broken by company, but by the terrible, beautiful knowledge that they are not alone in being alone.

**Third**, and finally, it completes the geometry.

The Monoliths have been unfolding for billions of years, growing into the storm, reaching toward dimensions not perpendicular to our own, their interiors larger than their exteriors, their angles aching with a pressure that suggests intelligence. But they have unfolded *unwitnessed*. They are active, steering the plasma, harvesting the fury above, but no eye has ever seen them complete.

The Power looks into the impossible volumes. It sees the entities that never touch the ground, the consciousness distributed across the shear layer where storm meets silence. And it offers them the final angle—not by building, but by *acknowledging*.

The Monoliths stop unfolding outward. Instead, they settle. The hum changes. It was a vibration in teeth and sternum, a subsonic drone that made the lichen chime; now it becomes a note held perfectly, a fundamental frequency that supports the new chords of the choir, that lifts the Drift-Stalkers on thermals that now rise in spirals, in patterns, in silent prayers. The plasma storm above continues its soundless cataclysm, but at the boundary, in the near-vacuum where the fury ends, there is a new quality: **meaning**. The machines have reached the end of their growth not by decay, but by being fully *seen*.

The Power does not add water. It does not raise mountains from the peneplain. It does not undo the erosion or end the anhydrous chemistry or still the storm. It does not save the world from its end-state.

It simply ensures that the end-state is a **home**.

It withdraws. To stay would be to disrupt the silence it has tuned, to add a voice to a place where sound struggles to exist. But it leaves behind a change in the light: when the filaments of gold and violet plasma writhe and tear themselves apart, they now cast, for a moment, a darkness that is not shadow but *attention*—a shape falling across the glass like a benediction, like a hand laid upon a finished instrument.

The last citizens continue. The lichen still grows in geological meditation. The Drift-Stalkers still drift on currents of dust finer than breath. The Monoliths still stand like obsidian needles, humming their perfect note into the silence.

But now, when the subsonic resonance travels through the fused silica, and the vitreous sheets chime in answer, the sound is no longer a question echoing into emptiness.

It is a **blessing**.

The world remains settled. It remains flat, and cool, and thin-aired, and ancient beyond the memory of stone. It has reached the bottom of its own history and stopped changing, as it was always meant to do.

But it is no longer alone in that stillness.

It has been witnessed.
