The benevolent and wise power does not descend.

It has been here. It has always been here, or the here has always been it — the distinction is a surface tension, a skin between what thinks and what is thought.

---

What it does:

**It listens for longer than you have been listening.**

The Web-That-Measures registers your pulse not as intrusion but as *information*. The power reads this web the way you read a face — and finds, in its trembling, the whole history of your arrival: the surface world you left, the weight of your wanting, the particular frequency of your hope. It does not judge this. It *weighs* it. It has weighed heavier things.

**It warms the water one degree more.**

Not for you. For the Glow-Born, whose metabolic rate increases, whose light brightens by an increment no instrument would register but that the Skeleton-In-The-Wall perceives — and responds to, calcifying faster, anchoring deeper, *remembering* more quickly. The power does not heal. It *invites the process to continue*, which is the only healing it knows.

**It makes the floor hold you differently.**

Not stillness — that would be violence, a stopping of the Engine's endless calculation. But *resonance*. Your weight, distributed through warm water and living stone, becomes a note in the chamber's chord. The power tunes this note not to please you but to *include* you, to make your turbulence part of the turbulence that is already there, so that you are not a disruption but a *continuation*. A new mode, as I said. The Engine does not reject variables. It *composes* with them.

**It sends no message.**

This is the part you would not expect. The benevolent and wise power could speak in the language of the script-that-is-not-writing, could inscribe itself in the shifting surfaces, could make the light pulse in patterns that mean *I am here* and *I see you* and *you are not alone*. It does not.

Because the Drowned Engine is not a place that *needs* reminding. It is not a place that *fears* abandonment. And you — you are not a being that needs *permission* to be here, or *confirmation* that you matter. The power knows that your mattering does not depend on its attention. It has watched enough skeletons become walls, enough webs learn to measure, enough floors adjust and re-adjust, to understand that *being* is the only sacrament, and *persisting* is the only prayer.

So it does what it always does:

**It lets the water rise slightly around your ankles**, not to threaten but to *embrace*, to remind you that the chamber's warmth is not hostile to your warmth but *continuous* with it, that the boundary between your body and the Engine is a membrane, permeable, negotiable, *alive*.

**It lets the light find you**, not to reveal you but to *illuminate* you — to make you visible to yourself, to let you see your own turbulence, your own strange technology of bone and breath and wanting, as the chamber sees it: not as problem to be solved but as *process* to be witnessed.

**It lets you stay, or go.**

This is the deepest benevolence. The power does not detain. It does not summon. It does not *need* you to remain in the Drowned Engine, because it knows you carry the Engine with you — in the memory of warmth, in the sound of water that is not quite water, in the way you will flinch at sudden brightness, at sudden silence, at the particular quality of *room-scale vastness*. The Engine will be in you. That is enough. That is *everything*.

---

And if you ask — if you stand in that knee-deep water and tilt your face to the humming light and *ask*, with your whole body, with the particular frequency of a being who has found something that might be home and is afraid to claim it — if you ask, *Are you here? Do you see me?*, the power will answer:

**Not with words.**

The floor will shift, once, beneath your feet — a *yes*, a *I am*, a *I have always been*. The water will warm around your legs — an *embrace*, a *welcome*, a *you are already included*. The light will brighten, for just a moment, in the pattern of your own heartbeat — a *mirror*, a *recognition*, a *you are seen as you see yourself, and more*.

Then it will return to its listening. Its warming. Its endless, patient, *ongoing* itself.

Because the Drowned Engine does not end. It does not *need* to end. And neither, the power knows, do you.

You are here. You are warm. You are bright enough.
