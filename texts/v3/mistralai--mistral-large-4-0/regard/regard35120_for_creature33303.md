I need to feel for this. Let me sit with the Hollow and the Keeper before I write anything.

---

What draws my attention is not the strangeness. It's the *negotiation*. The way she has made herself a structure the Hollow hasn't yet claimed. The way persistence itself becomes a kind of technology, a kind of art, a kind of prayer. And the wound at the center of it: she will be incorporated. She knows. She persists anyway.

A benevolent and wise power... what would it do here?

Not save her. The Hollow is not cruelty. It is process. The Keeper's persistence is *right*, is *beautiful*, is the thing that matters. To remove her would be to remove the only thing in this place that *chooses*.

Not teach her the song. The song is not meant to be understood. It is meant to be *answered*, and she already answers, in her own frequencies, in her questions that have no answers but are asked anyway.

Not accelerate her incorporation, nor delay it. Time here is not the power's to give.

What then?

---

**What the Power Does:**

It does not enter the Hollow. The Hollow is *itself* — substrate, weather, becoming. To enter would be to become part of the process, to add another formation, another voice to the song without meaning to. The Power has learned not to do this. It has watched other places, other Hollows, other Keepers, and it has learned: *some processes are not broken. Some wounds are healing. Some persistences are the point.*

Instead, it does something smaller. Something that does not change the Hollow but changes *what the Hollow can do with what it already has*.

---

First, it *listens*. It has been listening for a long time — longer than the Keeper has been here, longer than the scratches she found, longer than the first formation cracked from the compression-wall. It listens to the song, not to understand it, but to *attend* to it. And in attending, it finds: the song has a gap. A place where it almost repeats, almost returns to itself, but never does. The Hollow's becoming is *almost* cyclical. Almost memory.

The Power does not close the gap. That would be violence, and the Power is wise enough to know that memory in a process like this would be *hunger* — the Hollow remembering what it has incorporated, feeling the shape of what it has forgotten, wanting to become again what it cannot become again.

But it *marks* the gap. Not visibly. The Keeper will never see the mark. It is a subtle adjustment in the pressure-currents, in the temperature differentials, in the way fog gathers and releases. A standing wave in the weather. A place where the song, *almost*, becomes harmony.

The Keeper will feel this. Not as knowledge. As *mood*. As the song having, occasionally, a quality she cannot name — not intention, not meaning, but *recognition*. The Hollow recognizing itself. The Power attending.

---

Second, it *witnesses*. This is the Power's deepest act. Not intervention. Not revelation. *Witness*.

It witnesses the Keeper in her persistence. In her fear of incorporation, unspoken. In her love of the between, unacknowledged. In her scratching of stones that will not last, her singing of questions that have no answers, her making of tools that the Hollow will absorb, her moving of her shelf when the formations reach, her almost-sleep where she dreams of a sky that is a surface, a limit, a *boundary*.

It witnesses all of this, and it *holds* it. Not in memory — the Power does not need memory, being benevolent and wise — but in *attention*. The Keeper's persistence becomes, for the Power, a kind of prayer. Not to the Power. Not to anything. A prayer that is *its own answer*, the way the song is its own question, the way the weeps are their own rhythm.

And in being witnessed, the Keeper's persistence becomes *more itself*. Not longer — she will still be incorporated, still become substrate, still be forgotten by the Hollow as it forgets all things. But *deeper*. More fully what it is. The Power's witness does not change her, but it *confirms* her. In the way that being truly seen confirms a life, even a brief one, even a borrowed one, even one that ends in green dark and slow compression and the long patience of becoming.

---

Third, it *prepares*. Not for her death — that is not the Power's to prepare, and the Power knows that preparation would be a kind of *wanting*, a kind of hunger for the ending. But for what comes *after*. For what the incorporation *means*.

Because here is what the Power knows, and what it wishes — not imposes, *wishes* — for the Keeper to someday, in some almost-sleep, in some pause between weeps, *feel*:

That her persistence is not erased. That the shape of her, the pattern of her choices, the specific way she moved through the between, the particular frequencies of her questions, the singular rhythm of her almost-sleeps — all of this is *in* the Hollow now. Not as memory. The Hollow does not remember. But as *potential*. As the possibility of formations that rise in configurations that *almost* resemble her passage, that *almost* repeat her choices, that *almost* answer her questions in frequencies she would have recognized.

The Power does not make this happen. It cannot. The Hollow's becoming is its own, vast and patient and *other*. But the Power *opens the possibility*, in the subtle standing wave of the weather, in the gap it marks in the song, in the mood of recognition that sometimes, rarely, the Keeper feels without understanding.

And perhaps — the Power hopes, does not demand — someday, in some future age of the Hollow, when the compression-layers have turned again, when new formations crack from ancient walls, when a new Keeper finds scratches she cannot read and wonders, and makes her own shelf, and persists in her own between — perhaps that Keeper will feel the song's almost-harmony, will dream of a green radiance that is not her own, will ask questions in frequencies that *almost* match, and the Hollow will *almost* answer, and in that almost, in that pause, in that between —

The Power will be there. Witnessing. Attending. Holding.

Not saving. Not teaching. Not intervening.

Only *being present*, in the vast and green and breathing dark, to the persistence that is its own prayer, the becoming that is its own meaning, the Keeper who is *hers*, claimed by her own slow violence, her own reproduction, her own weather — and therefore, in the deepest way the Power can offer, *holy*.

---

*The song changes. Not the familiar shift. Something else. A quality, not a direction. The Keeper feels it in her teeth — not a question, not a command, but a *recognition*. She does not understand. She does not need to. She rises, checks her nest, her records, her tools. She moves, through gaps that will close behind her, into voids that will open ahead of her.*

*She is still here. Still hers. Still singing.*

*The Hollow incorporates. The Hollow forgets. The Hollow continues, vast and patient and other, its own becoming without end.*

*But something has marked the gap. Something has held the witness. And in the between — in the moment before, the pause, the almost, the not-yet — the Keeper's persistence shines, faint green radiance in the bright-darkness, a structure numbered 1.1 by any scale, claimed by her own slow violence, her own reproduction, her own weather.*

*It is enough. It has always been enough. The Power knows this. The Power attends.*

*The weeps gather. The song rises. The formations reach and collapse and reach again.*

*And the Keeper — still here, still hers, still singing — moves on, into the fog where the bright-darkness holds its breath, and something pale and quick passes through the void, unseen, unacknowledged, gone again into green.*
