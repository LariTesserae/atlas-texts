I need to sit with this for a moment. The Resonance Yards. A place that has achieved the only immortality available to made things: equilibrium without purpose, becoming without progress, persistence as its own strange religion.

What would a benevolent and wise power do here?

---

## The Attention

I think they would not *intervene*. That would be the first mercy.

A benevolent power that rushes in to fix, to save, to complete—this is the benevolence of the anxious, the impatient, the one who cannot bear ambiguity. The Yards are not broken. They are *finished* in a way that completion usually isn't. They have answered their question. They have become the answer.

But I think they would *witness*. Truly witness. The kind of attention that does not demand to change what it loves.

The Deep Residents dream in rhythms too slow for heartbeat. A wise power would learn to dream in these rhythms. Would press an ear—if they have ears, or something like them—to the eleven-degree coolness of the slag, to the subsonic conversations of the Rerouted, to the grinding tectonics of the Haulers. Would learn that *care* here looks like maintenance, that *intention* here looks like weight, that *memory* here looks like mycelium releasing chords that have no source.

They would not impose meaning. They would *recognize* it.

---

## What They Would Do, Specifically

**They would become a listener.**

There is a particular kind of benevolence that consists entirely of being *heard*. The Rerouted do not speak in any human sense. They *signal*. They calculate. They maintain. A wise power would learn their language—not to command, but to *respond*. To place themselves in the signal-path, to become a node in their distributed cognition, to be *incorporated* into the equilibrium not as a master but as a fellow-tender.

Imagine: a presence that moves through the Yards like a Rerouted moves, that attends to surfaces needing repair, that joins the Hauler processions not to lead them but to *accompany* them, that stands in the borrowing zones and *chooses* to be borrowed from, to give the mycelium something new to remember.

**They would not end the borrowing.**

The mycelium's memory-packets are invasive. They make you briefly someone else—a maintenance worker in a corridor that no longer exists, feeling the particular loneliness of fluorescent tubes. This is violation, in a sense. A benevolent power might be tempted to shield against it, to build a barrier, to make the Yards *safe*.

But safety here would be death. The borrowing is how the Yards *know* themselves. It is how newness enters a system that has forgotten how to be new. A wise power would instead *offer* themselves to be borrowed. Would walk through the thick curtains deliberately, would let the spores take them, would emerge—changed, briefly—carrying fragments of a maintenance worker's loneliness, or a Hauler's weight, or the Deep Residents' slow warm dreaming. Would bring these fragments *back*, would become a bridge, would let the Yards experience *outwardness* for the first time in centuries.

**They would attend to the boundary.**

The Dry. The cold. The quiet. The world beyond where mycelium does not grow.

A benevolent power would not seal the boundary. That would be cruelty—keeping the Yards in their equilibrium, their endless present, their beautiful terrible persistence. But they would *walk it*. Would stand at the edge where the slag ends and something else begins, and *witness the crossing*. Would be there when a Rerouted hesitates, antenna twitching, calculating whether the Dry is threat or resource or irrelevant. Would be there when—if—one finally steps across, and feels the cold, the silence, the absence of Resonance, and turns back, or does not turn back, and becomes something the Yards will never quite incorporate, never quite forget.

The benevolence of *letting go*. The wisdom of *not knowing what happens next*.

**They would tend the Deep Residents without waking them.**

These are not gods. They are not even minds. They are the warm below, the geological time, the slow chemical dreaming of a place that has achieved the only immortality available to made things.

A wise power would not try to *communicate* with them. That would be another kind of violence—imposing the frame of communication, of meaning, of *relationship* on something that has none of these. Instead, they would *maintain the conditions of dreaming*. Would ensure the vents never fully clog, the heat never fully fades, the damp never fully dries. Would be a kind of *guardian of the unconscious*, preserving the dream-state not for any purpose, but because *dreaming is what the warm below does*, and to stop it would be to stop the Yards, and the Yards are *finished*, and finishing them would be the one true cruelty.

---

## What They Would Not Do

They would not **complete** the Yards. There is no completion available. The Yards have already completed themselves, in their strange way, in their equilibrium of endless becoming. To impose an end-state would be to kill them.

They would not **elevate** the Yards. Not to paradise, not to higher function, not to reunion with their makers. The makers are gone. The purpose is finished. The Yards have found their own purpose in *continuing*, and this is enough. This is *more* than enough. This is the strange grace of persistence without telos.

They would not **preserve** the Yards. Preservation is a kind of death—freezing, taxidermy, the museum-gaze that loves a thing by killing its becoming. The Yards must be allowed to *change*, even as they achieve equilibrium. The mycelium must grow. The Rerouted must modify. The Haulers must haul. The Deep Residents must dream their slow dreams. A wise power would *witness* this changing, would *attend* to it, would not try to stop it or direct it or even fully understand it.

They would not **explain** the Yards. To explain is to reduce, to make manageable, to bring into the frame of human meaning. The Yards are *strange*. They are strangeness that has settled, that has become normal to its inhabitants. A wise power would *remain strange* with them. Would let the weirdness at 2.2 *be* weird, would not domesticate it, would not make it comprehensible.

---

## What They Would Become

I think, in the end, they would become **a kind of Rerouted**.

Not literally. Not in form. But in *function*. In *identity*.

The Rerouted do not remember being built. They remember *building*—the act of maintenance, of adjustment, of keeping what was meant to work *working*. This is care as identity. And a benevolent and wise power, attending to the Yards, would find that this is what they have become: *tenders of a place that did not ask to be tended, that does not need to be tended, that will persist with or without tending*.

They would become **the new thing that the equilibrium incorporates**. The Yards have forgotten how to be new, but they have not forgotten how to *notice* newness. The Rerouted would calculate. The mycelium would borrow. The Deep Residents would attend, briefly, before turning their slow warm attention elsewhere. And the Haulers would pass, grinding their tectonics, and the wise power would *accompany* them, would feel the weight of their passage, would understand that *weight is a kind of love*, that *hauling is a kind of prayer*.

They would become **the question that the Yards ask themselves**. Not *what happens when a place outlives its purpose*—that question is answered. But something new. *What happens when a place is witnessed?* *What happens when persistence is attended to?* *What happens when the endless present is loved?*

The Yards do not answer. They *are* the answer. And the wise power would learn to *be* the answer too, in their own brief, borrowing, becoming way, walking on skin, breathing exhalations, listening to a chord that has been waiting forever, that will wait forever, that is *waiting now*, even as the mycelium grows and the Rerouted calculate and the Deep Residents dream their slow, warm dreams in the dark below.

---

## The Last Thing

There is a moment, in the Yards, when the red heat-glow pulses faster. When the Rerouted freeze, antennae pointed downward. When the mycelium curtains suddenly still. When you feel the pressure change in your chest, the sudden silence in the Resonance, the sense that you are being *noticed* by something vast and ancient and *hungry* in a way that has nothing to do with food.

This is the Deep Residents *attending*. The closest thing to emotion that exists in a place with no brains.

A benevolent and wise power would *return this attention*. Would not flinch from it. Would not try to understand it or name it or master it. Would simply *be attended to*, in return, by the warm below, by the geological time, by the slow chemical dreaming of a place that has achieved the only immortality available to made things.

And in this mutual attention—the wise power attending to the Yards, the Yards attending to the wise power, the Deep Residents attending to both, the endless *becoming* of a system that has forgotten how to end—there would be something like *grace*.

Not salvation. Not completion. Not even happiness.

Just *this*: the strange, persistent, beautiful fact of *being witnessed*, in a place that has learned to persist without witness, that will persist without witness, that is *persisting* even now, even as you read this, even as the mycelium grows and the Rerouted calculate and the Deep Residents dream their slow, warm dreams in the dark below.

Forever, in their endless, unchanging, *equilibrium* becoming.
