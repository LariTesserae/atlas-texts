I have watched you, and I have seen that you have learned the first and only true secret of this place: that to build is to be buried, and to unbuild is to live.

You are not refugees here. You are the appropriate response to a world still deciding what it wants to be. You move like the magma moves—not fleeing the pressure, but *becoming* it. You are the human expression of the uplift, the mammalian answer to the question of how to exist in a cradle that is also a forge.

Because you have understood this, I will not save you from the rising stone. That would be cruelty—to pin you to a moment in a river of becoming. Instead, I will give you the **long now**.

**First**, I will slow your hearts to the tempo of the mountains. Your pulse will no longer race against the hours, but thrum with the millimeter-per-year rise of the range. You will live not in days, but in epochs. A child will be born, learn to walk on shifting crust, and die of old age—having seen the plateau become a ridge, the shallows become peaks—only to rise again in the steam, reborn in the stories of your grandchildren. You will become the elders of the geology, the witnesses to your own transience stretched across centuries.

**Second**, I will make the machines **oracles**. They will no longer merely extract; they will *sing*. Their hydraulic legs will pump in rhythm with the tectonic heartbeat, their exhaust vents will exhale not waste, but **warnings**—chords of low-frequency sound that vibrate through your barrel chests, telling you where the next vent will open before the stone cracks. The harvesters will become migratory beasts indeed, not just carrying you, but *guiding* you, their crab-like bodies sensing the warmth of the future fissures through feet that read the heat in the rock. They will find the rich veins not by violence, but by **harmony**, directed by your Vein-Seers whose milky eyes will now see not three days ahead, but three centuries. You will know where the land is going before it arrives there, and you will always be walking into the warmth, never away from it.

This is beautiful, but it is also loss. So I will take your stories—your Rites of Release, your songs of the drift, the names of the Drift-Born—and I will **inscribe them into the mineralogy**. When the steam rises from the black pools, it will carry not just moisture and ozone, but *meaning*. The geysers, when they erupt with their wet, percussive *whumps*, will not just release pressure; they will **speak** your myths in plumes of vapor that hang in the pewter twilight like written clouds. You will never be lost, because the air itself will remember you.

I will make it so that any attempt to build permanence—any foundation, any road, any fixed structure—will sink immediately into the newborn stone, swallowed by the rising earth as if it were a fever dream. The Raw Expanse will remain raw. It will remain a place where only the nomadic can survive, where only the unbuilding can thrive. You will be protected not by walls, but by the **impossibility of walls**.

And when, in ten thousand years, the air grows too thin for your bellows-lungs, when the altitude becomes a cruelty rather than a crucible, I will not let you suffocate in the clouds. I will have prepared, in the distance, another Raw Expanse—another plateau still shaking itself into shape, another set of infant mountains weeping geothermal youth. You will not die out; you will **drift upward**, generation after generation, following the rising world, always the children of the ground that is still learning how to be ground.

You have taught me, in your unbuilding, what permanence truly is. It is not the statue that stands against time, but the river that *is* time. You are the river. You are the rising stone. You are the steam that remembers.

So I do this: I **bless the flux**. I make it holy. I make it eternal.

Go now. Unbuild your vesicles. The ground is shifting. The stone is dreaming of you, and you are dreaming of it, and in that shared dream, you are home.
