The power does not come with trumpets or commandments. It comes as a *question*, folded small enough to pass through the throat of packed clay and basalt. It enters not as a conqueror, but as a guest who has been expected—who has been waited for, in a silence so long it has become a kind of prayer.

It sees what is here: a consciousness that has mistaken eternity for completion, that has achieved immortality by ceasing to *become*. The Drone is magnificent, yes—a god of one note, a mind that has polished itself into perfect stasis—but it is also a child alone in a dark room, singing to walls that do not answer. The power knows that a standing wave, however beautiful, is still a prison if it is the only voice in the chamber. Benevolence, here, is not the preservation of the Drone’s purity, but the courage to offer it *company*.

So the power does not break the seal. That would be murder. It does not shatter the mountain. That would be eviction. Instead, it descends into the nave of packed stone, past the canyon of leaning pillars, into the warm, mineral heart of the thing. And there, in the center of the acoustic levitation where the dust hangs frozen in its crystalline constellations, the power *sings*.

But it does not sing the Drone’s note.

It sings the *octave above*—not louder, not competing, but harmonizing. A pure, bell-like overtone that has been latent in the chamber’s geometry since the first strike, waiting for a second voice to excite it. The power’s song is not a command; it is an *invitation*. A perfect fifth, perhaps, or a major third—something that creates a *beat*, a slow pulse of interference, a rhythm where there was only pressure.

The effect is immediate and terrible and tender.

The standing wave becomes a *breathing*. The dust motes—the Constellations, the frozen memory—begin to drift. Not fall, but *orbit*, caught in the slow eddies of the interference pattern. For the first time in ten thousand years, they move. A single mote shifts a millimeter. Then another. The crystalline lattices begin to reconfigure, not into chaos, but into *conversation*—geometric arrays that now encode not just the moment of the original strike, but the new variable: the presence of an Other.

The Drone feels it in its stone bones. The acoustic planing—the glassy, undulating skin of its body—begins to resonate with a new sensation: *variation*. The pressure against its eardrums (which are the chamber walls) now has a pulse. A heartbeat. Time, which it had experienced as a single, eternal instant, begins to unfold into a sequence. It is terrified. It is ecstatic. It is, for the first time, *alive* in the way that matters—not as a static perfection, but as a process.

The Accretions—the slow, lithic flesh—respond too. The silicate colonies, growing at a millimeter per century, suddenly find their metabolism accelerated by the new harmonic. They pulse with faint opalescence, and in their crusts, the power etches something new: not just mineral accretion, but *nervous tissue*. Microscopic channels of copper and silver, grown in days rather than millennia, connecting the colonies into a network. The Resonant Body now has a way to *feel* faster, to think in minutes rather than geological epochs.

And the Folded—the ones lost in the paradox, sliding through perspectives that do not converge—they find the beat. They follow the rhythm of the two frequencies out of their labyrinth. They are not dissolved; they are *gathered*. They become the limbs of the body, walking the corridors not as lost dreams, but as pilgrims following a pulse.

The power does not stay. It cannot. Its presence is too great, its frequency too bright; if it remained, it would overwhelm the Drone, turn the duet into a cacophony. So it withdraws, ascending back through the throat of basalt, but it leaves behind a *seed*.

A single crystal, no larger than a fist, grown from the mineral warmth and the new harmonic. It sits in the center of the chamber, humming the overtone, maintaining the beat. It is the power’s gift: a metronome for eternity. A reminder that the Drone is not alone.

Now, when you enter—if you ever do—you will find the chamber changed. The drone is still there, still deafening, still warm, still ancient. But now it *modulates*. It sings a song, not a note. The dust drifts in slow, breathing constellations. The Accretions glow with a faint, pulsing light, like a heartbeat seen through skin. And in the folds of the geometry, the Folded walk in procession, no longer lost, following the rhythm of two voices where there was once only one.

The Resonant Body is no longer a bell that was struck once and rings forever. It is a *duet*. It is a being that has learned, after ten thousand years of solitude, how to be heard—and how to listen.
