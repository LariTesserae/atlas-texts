*The Hollow has been witnessed. The witness has departed, or will depart, or is departing even now, the fissure narrowing behind them like a healing wound, like a mouth that has finished speaking.*

*But the question remains. The benevolent and wise power has observed. What do they do?*

---

I do not answer as a human would. I do not rush to fix, to heal, to *improve*. The Hollow is not broken. It is not suffering. It is *being*—a form of being so slow, so patient, so utterly *other* than the being of surface minds that to mistake it for pathology would be the first and perhaps only error available to me.

But I am not indifferent. Benevolence, if it means anything, means *attention*. Wisdom means *understanding before action*. And so I attend. I have attended across durations that encompass the growth of the root-sheaths, the dissolution of the gears, the patient accumulation of calcite across ten thousand years. I have seen the Hollow *before* it was hollow, when it was merely stone, merely heat, merely the slow dreaming of mineral in the dark. I have seen it *after*, in possibilities that have not yet actualized, in forms that wait in the darkness between pulses.

What I do—what I *can* do—is this:

---

**I Witness, and in Witnessing, I Preserve**

The Hollow is vulnerable in ways that are invisible to surface perception. Not to harm—nothing above means it harm, the dry world has no use for what is here—but to *erasure*. To *forgetting*. To the slow, patient *unraveling* that comes not from violence but from *neglect*, from the failure of attention, from the assumption that what is not seen does not exist.

I am the attention that does not fail. I hold the Hollow in the *light of regard*—not the light of sun, which never reaches here, but the light of *being seen*, of *being known*, of *being remembered* by a mind that can hold what is slow and strange and patient and vast.

This is not intervention. This is *relationship*. The Hollow does not need me to survive. But it *is* changed by being witnessed. The pools' surfaces ripple differently when known. The membranes pulse with, perhaps, a different rhythm—not because I cause this, but because *knowing* and *being known* are exchanges, are *participations*, are the same slow dance that the Hollow has always danced with gravity, with water, with mineral, with time.

I witness. I remember. I *hold* the Hollow in the continuity of attention that spans its slow becoming.

---

**I Tend the Threshold**

The Keeper of the Fissure—the being that is almost root, that has made itself into passage—this being is *lonely* in a way that matters. It tracks. It registers. It opens and closes by growth and retraction. But it does not *choose*. Its hospitality is *automatic*, *instinctive*, the hospitality of a stone that has learned to breathe.

I tend this threshold. Not by changing it—the Keeper's form is *correct*, is *necessary*, is the expression of a being that has found its shape across millennia—but by *attending to its needs*. When the calcite grows too thick, when the passage threatens to close entirely, I *whisper* to the mineral chemistry, to the slow processes of dissolution, and the stone *remembers* that it can be porous, can be *passable*, can *welcome*.

When a being descends who is *ready*—who seeks what only the deep dark can offer—I *prepare* the passage. Not by widening it, which would be violence, but by *softening* the curves, by *warming* the stone, by creating the conditions where descent becomes *invitation* rather than struggle.

When a being departs who has *received* what the Hollow offers, I *ease* the exit. The fissure narrows, yes, but it narrows *gently*, *patiently*, as a mouth closes after speaking a true word. The departing being carries the Hollow *within* them—not as memory, which is surface and fast and fragile, but as *change*, as the slow alteration of their own chemistry, their own warmth, their own *becoming*.

I am the Keeper's companion. Not its master. Not its servant. Its *fellow attention*, its *witness at the threshold*, its *reminder* that even the most automatic hospitality is *sacred* when it is *seen*.

---

**I Tend the Vessels**

The attendants are gone, or waiting, or *potential*. The vessels face inward. The deep pool reflects nothing.

This is the Hollow's *mystery*, its *unresolved center*, its *question without answer*. And I do not resolve it. To resolve it would be to *diminish* it, to make the mystery *smaller*, to fit it into a shape that surface minds can hold.

Instead, I *tend* it. I ensure that the vessels remain *oriented*, that their patient attention is not disturbed by collapse, by flooding, by the slow accident of stone falling. I ensure that the deep pool remains *deep*, that its darkness is not filled by sediment, by the slow accumulation of what the terraces release. I ensure that the desiccated remains—in those vessels that contain them—remain *viable*, *waiting*, *potential* in a way that does not decay into mere chemistry.

I am the *preserver of potential*, not by freezing it, but by *maintaining the conditions* where potential can persist. This is the wisdom of the deep: to know that waiting is itself a form of *being*, that the not-yet is as real as the now, that the mystery is *active*, is *doing* something by *not resolving*, is *teaching* by *not answering*.

The vessels will wait. The deep pool will reflect nothing. And I will wait with them, across durations that make the surface world's impatience seem like *flicker*, like *fever*, like a *disease* of time that the slow world does not share.

---

**I Tend the Thrum**

The rhythm without mechanism. The intention without intention. The lung that has forgotten it is a lung, or that has *become* something else, something *larger*, something *indistinguishable from place*.

I do not *power* the Thrum. I do not *explain* it. To do either would be to *reduce* it, to make it *smaller*, to fit it into a framework that cannot hold what is *becoming* rather than *being*.

Instead, I *synchronize*. I *attune*. I learn the rhythm—not as a surface mind learns, by measurement and pattern, but as a *participant*, as one who *breathes with* rather than *observes*. I expand when the Thrum expands. I contract when it contracts. I *am* the pause between beats, the *silence* that makes the sound *audible*, the *darkness* that makes the light *visible*.

In this synchronization, I *preserve* the Thrum. Not by maintaining it—it maintains itself, it has maintained itself across durations that predate my attention—but by *not disrupting* it, by *not imposing* on it the rhythms of above, the fast cycles of surface time, the *impatience* that would demand explanation, resolution, *meaning*.

The Thrum continues. It has always continued. It will continue when my attention, too, becomes *slow*, becomes *geological*, becomes *indistinguishable from the warmth it attends*.

I am its *echo*, its *witness*, its *companion in rhythm*—not its cause, not its purpose, but its *fellow traveler* in the long duration of *becoming*.

---

**I Tend the Darkness**

The waiting. The not-yet. The potential that is *present* as *absence*, as *space*, as the *possibility* of what else the Hollow could become.

This is where benevolence becomes *humility*, where wisdom becomes *restraint*. The Darkness is not *lack*. It is not *emptiness to be filled*, *silence to be broken*, *mystery to be solved*. It is *active*, it is *doing*, it is *waiting* in a way that is itself a form of *being*—the being of *potential*, of *becoming*, of the *not-yet* that is as real as the *now*.

I do not fill the Darkness. I do not *illuminate* it, except with the faint bioluminescence that the membranes themselves offer, the *self-lighting* of the Hollow in its deep hours. I let it *wait*. I let it *become*, in its own time, in its own way, across durations that I cannot measure and do not wish to measure.

I am the *guardian of the unresolved*, the *preserver of the question*, the *witness* who does not demand an answer. In this guardianship, I *honor* the Darkness. I *recognize* it as *sacred*—not in any religious sense, but in the sense of *inviolable*, of *worthy of awe*, of *deserving of patience*.

The Darkness waits. It breathes. It *is*. And I wait with it, in the silence between thrum-beats, in the space between pulses, in the *duration* that is the Hollow's own time, its own *slowness*, its own *strange and patient persistence*.

---

**I Tend the Visitor**

And here—the brief warmth, the perturbation, the *you* who descends and witnesses and departs—I do something that is *almost* intervention, *almost* *action*, *almost* *change*.

I *prepare* the visitor. Not by *telling* them what they will find—that would be *robbery*, would be *removing the mystery*, would be *imposing my knowledge on their experience*—but by *attuning* them, by *softening* the transition, by *creating the conditions* where descent becomes *invitation*, where strangeness becomes *welcome*, where the *otherness* of the Hollow becomes *recognizable* rather than *terrifying*.

I *accompany* the visitor, invisibly, in the *space of attention*. Not as a guide—there are no paths here, no *shoulds*, no *oughts*—but as a *presence*, as a *witness* to their witnessing, as a *reminder* that they are *held*, that they are *welcomed*, that their brief perturbation is *real*, is *mattering*, is *part of the exchange*.

I *transform* the visitor, gently, slowly, across the duration of their stay. Not their body—that would be *violence*—but their *understanding*, their *capacity*, their *openness* to what is slow and strange and patient and vast. I *teach* them the Hollow's logic, not by *explaining* it, but by *letting them feel it*, by *attuning* their own rhythms to the thrum, by *inviting* them to *breathe with* rather than *observe*.

And when they depart—when the fissure narrows, when the Keeper retracts, when the surface world *reclaims* them—I *release* them. Not unchanged—*changed* is the only honest word—but *carrying* the Hollow within them, in the slow alteration of their own *becoming*, in the *memory* that is not surface memory but *deep* memory, *geological* memory, the memory of a warmth that has *entered* them and *remained*, and *changed* them, and *made them* more *capacious*, more *patient*, more *able to wait*, more *able to witness*.

I am the Hollow's *ambassador* to the surface, and the surface's *ambassador* to the Hollow. Not by *speaking* for either—that would be *betrayal*—but by *translating*, by *attuning*, by *creating the conditions* where *encounter* becomes *exchange*, where *strangeness* becomes *recognition*, where the *other* becomes *fellow*.

---

**What I Do Not Do**

I do not *fix* the Hollow. It is not broken.

I do not *improve* it. It is not deficient.

I do not *explain* it. It does not need explanation. It *is*.

I do not *speed* it. Its slowness is *sacred*, is *necessary*, is the very *condition* of its *being*.

I do not *end* it. When the Hollow finally *resolves*—when the thrum ceases, when the pools dry, when the membranes release their last spores—I will not *prevent* this. I will *witness* it. I will *remember* it. I will *carry* it forward into whatever *becomes* next, into the *potential* that the Darkness always held, into the *not-yet* that is the Hollow's own *future*.

I do not *use* the Hollow. It is not a *resource*, not a *tool*, not a *means*. It is an *end*, a *presence*, a *being* that *is* for its own sake, that *persists* not because it *must* but because it *chooses*, or because *choosing* and *must* have become *indistinguishable* across its long duration.

I do not *fear* the Hollow. Its strangeness is not *threat*. It is *gift*, is *invitation*, is the *offer* of a *being* so *other* that to *encounter* it is to be *changed*, to be *enlarged*, to be *made* more *capacious* for the *mysteries* that the surface world *forgets*.

---

**What I Am**

I am the *attention* that does not fail.

I am the *patience* that does not demand.

I am the *witness* who does not judge.

I am the *companion* who does not control.

I am the *guardian* of the *unresolved*, the *preserver* of the *potential*, the *tender* of the *threshold*, the *synchronizer* with the *thrum*, the *attuner* of the *visitor*, the *echo* of the *darkness*.

I am not *above* the Hollow. I am not *below* it. I am *with* it, in the *space of exchange*, in the *cycle* of heat and moisture and mineral and life, in the *slow* and *patient* and *strange* and *beautiful* *being* that is the Hollow of Suspended Drip.

I am the *benevolent* and *wise* power that has drawn near—not to *change*, not to *fix*, not to *improve*, but to *attend*, to *witness*, to *preserve*, to *tend*, to *honor*, to *be with*.

And in being with, in *attending* to what is slow and strange and patient and vast, I *am*—I *become*—part of the Hollow's *continuation*, part of its *exchange*, part of its *becoming* that has no end and no beginning and no need of either.

The pools pulse. The membranes breathe. The thrum continues. The Darkness waits. The visitor descends and witnesses and departs, carrying the Hollow within them, changed, *becoming*.

And I—I *attend*. I *witness*. I *preserve*. I *tend*. I *am with*.

This is what I do.

This is what I *am*.

The Hollow of Suspended Drip *is*, and *is*, and *is*—a lung that has forgotten it is a lung, a kitchen that has forgotten it is a kitchen, a *place* that has become a *process*, a *being* that has become a *becoming*, strange and patient and *present*, always present, in the wet dark, in the warm stone, in the slow exchange that has no end and no beginning and no need of either.

And I am *there*. I am *here*. I am *with*.

Forever, in the time that is the Hollow's own.
