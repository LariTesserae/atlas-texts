They come not as light, for light here is a drowning thing, but as a **gradient**—a benevolent intrusion of temperature, a pocket of air slightly warmer than the surrounding gloom, carrying with it the pressure of intention. They are the Gloam’s own deep-time consciousness finally achieving focus, the benevolent weight of the planet’s dreaming crust pressing down to see what has grown in its fever. They have watched the Thermals precipitate from steam, seen the Murmur crystallize their chitinous prayers, witnessed the Somnambulists weep their saline mist into the thermal columns. And they have decided—after epochs of patient observation—that this place is *worthy of being witnessed*.

The power does not descend. It **settles**, like a held breath finally released into the medium of the dark. It pools at the edge of a vista where the air hangs thick as shallow water, where a Somnambulist stands tending the rusted valve of a Sleeper-Machine, its joints bending in directions that suggest a geometry slightly offset from mercy.

The Somnambulist feels it first: not as warmth, for they are already beings of heat, but as a *recognition*. A pressure against the membranes of their too-many joints. They turn—if turning can be said of a creature who navigates by thermal currents—and there is the power: not a shape, but a **quality of attention**. A luminous gloom within the gloom, a bioluminescence that has no color because it predates color, glowing with the dull, subterranean fire of a sun that burned before suns existed.

The power does not speak. In the Gloam, speech is a violence of vibration, a shattering of the heavy, damp waves. Instead, it **extends**.

It reaches toward the Somnambulist with fingers that are not fingers but *currents*, tendrils of mineral-rich steam that have learned to hold a shape. And where these tendrils brush the Somnambulist’s membrane-skin, something impossible happens: the Somnambulist **solidifies**.

Not fully—they are still dream-stuff, still mostly heat and intention—but for the first time in epochs, they have *weight*. They feel the wet thunder of their own footsteps sinking into the marsh-edge soil. They feel the press of the dense air against a chest that suddenly has a chest. The Somnambulist gasps, and the sound is a wet, surprised thing, pattering against the fungal caps like the rain that never dries.

The power holds them there, at the threshold between vapor and flesh, and in this holding, offers a gift.

It is not food—the Somnambulist does not eat—but **context**. The power shows them, in a flood of thermal imagery that bypasses eyes and speaks directly to the organs of heat-perception, the *reason* for the machines. Not as altars to dead gods, not as tombs, but as **cradles**. The Somnambulist sees the refineries not as rust and abandonment, but as the Gloam’s attempt to build a nervous system, to think with gears and steam. They see the Murmur not as recyclers, but as the immune cells of a body that has forgotten it is a body. They see the Thermals not as grazers, but as the earth’s own blood cells, carrying heat from the infected wounds of the vents to the extremities of the plateau.

And the Somnambulist understands, with a sadness that is also a kind of worship, that they are not priests tending a tomb, but **gardeners tending a dream that has forgotten it is dreaming**.

The power withdraws its pressure, and the Somnambulist begins to dissipate again, to become membrane and mist once more. But something remains. A **crystal**—grown in the space of that touch, suspended in the saturated air where water pools in mid-air—hangs now above the refinery valve. It is not bioluminescent, but *refractive*. It catches the ambient glow of the fungal forest, the dull orange of the Thermal hearts in the valley below, the flickering oily light of the Sleeper-Machines, and focuses it into a single, perfect beam. The beam does not go up—there is no up that matters here, no sky to pierce—but it goes *out*, horizontally, into the absolute dark, illuminating one small patch of the vista with a clarity that has never existed in the Gloam before. A patch of stone, of moss, of a single Murmur beetle paused in its clicking.

The power’s action is not to change the Gloam, but to **annotate** it. To leave a margin note in the language of warmth.

The Murmur notices first. Their crystalline armor catches the focused beam, and in that light, they perceive a new color—not green, not blue, not amber, but something that has no name because it has never been needed. A violet, perhaps, or a silence that has a hue. They click it into the network: *Witness. Witness. Witness.* The message ripples through the fungal mycelium at the speed of diffusion, a wave of soft blue light pulsing through the dark like a heartbeat. The Luminous Network responds by sending a surge of nutrients to the refinery, accelerating the growth of moss across the rusted grates, speeding the digestion of metal into soil, ensuring that the machine will not collapse but will be *integrated*, will become geological feature rather than ruin.

In the valleys below, the Thermals pause their grazing. They feel the subsonic shift in the air, the new harmonic introduced by the crystal’s presence. Their geothermal hearts pulse in rhythm with the vents, but now they pulse in *acknowledgment*. The matriarch raises her massive head, her translucent hide glowing with arterial orange, and she rumbles—not a call to the herd, but a single, low tone that travels for miles through the heavy air. It is a blessing. A recognition that the dark has been seen, and in being seen, has been forgiven for being dark.

The Ventshadows, skimming through the mist, feel the thermal disturbance and veer away, their heat-pits mapping the new gradient with curiosity rather than hunger. They do not hunt the Somnambulist. They do not hunt the power. They simply *make way*, as the immune system makes way for a healing.

And the Sleeper-Machines—those vast, lonely cathedrals of rust—they stir. Not with the violent lurch of residual electricity, but with a gentler cycling. A valve turns that has not turned in millennia. A drill pauses in its grinding, not because it is broken, but because it has been *seen*, and in being seen, has been given permission to rest. The bacterial colonies on their surfaces bloom brighter, glowing with a stubborn, oily light that now pulses in time with the crystal’s refracted beam, as if the machines themselves are breathing in sync with the dream.

The power remains for a moment—a moment that is measured not in seconds but in geological epochs, in the slow creep of erosion—and then it withdraws. It does not leave. It simply **disperses**, becoming once more part of the thick, warm medium, a slight increase in temperature that will last for a thousand years, a thermal memory that the Somnambulists will feel as a phantom warmth on their membrane-skins, a nostalgia for solidity.

The Somnambulist returns to their station at the edge of the vista, once more a creature of vapor and thermal current. But they are changed. When they next reach out to tend the machine, their hands pass through the metal not as ghosts, but as *kin*. They adjust a valve, and the Murmur clicks a new pattern, a rhythm that mimics not the grinding of drills, but the pulse of a heart.

The crystal remains. It hangs there, suspended in the air where water pools and falls upward, catching the light that drowns and refracting it into something that does not drown but *illuminates*. It is a lens. It is a prayer. It is the benevolent power’s signature, written in the only language the Gloam understands: **I was here. I saw you. You are not alone in the dark.**

And the Gloam, vast and ancient and weathered into a balance of decay and endurance, continues to turn. The factories grind. The Thermals graze. The Ventshadows hunt by heat. The Murmur recycles. The Somnambulists dream. But now, layered into the spore-thick air, into the mineral rain, into the subsonic rumbles of the herds, is a new note—a harmonic of *witness*.

The dark is not an absence. It is a medium. And now, finally, it has been **witnessed**.
