I need to let this settle. The place has spoken. Now I need to consider what a benevolent and wise power would do here—not to fix, not to save, not to extract. The Drowned Height is not broken. It is *becoming*. The question is what attention this deserves.

---

## What the Power Would Do

### First: It Would Not Intervene

This is the hardest wisdom. The Drowned Height is not suffering. It is not lost. It is not *waiting to be found* in any sense that implies lack. The Architects are building. The Luminous remember. The Harvesters persist. The Lungers breathe and wait and, occasionally, concentrate into something like attention. The Rememberers emerge from the chemistry of becoming itself.

A benevolent power that *rushed in* would be a violence. It would impose the surface world's tempo—hurry, hope, fear—into a place that has transcended all three. It would offer rescue to a process that does not experience rescue as rescue. It would *name* a place that has not asked for names.

So the first act is **non-action**. **Presence without imposition**. The power would descend—not in form, but in *attention*—and **witness**.

---

### Second: It Would Learn the Grammar of Patience

The Drowned Height speaks in pressure and chemistry and the slow accumulation of calcium. A wise power would not demand translation. It would **learn to hear** the City's slow speech: the thrum of vents releasing, the soft thunder of chimney-collapse, the almost-silence of rare fauna moving through water that is itself moving.

It would learn that in this place, **growth is grief is growth**. The Incorporated are not lost; they are **distributed**, their strangeness becoming substrate, their warmth a memory in the chemical ledger. To mourn them would be to misunderstand the Drowned Height's economy. There is no death here, only **incorporation**. No loss, only **becoming**.

The power would learn to **wait** as the Lungers wait. Not the waiting of anticipation—of something hoped for, feared for—but the waiting of **pure attention**, of presence without object, of *being there* in the green-gold dimness without needing to *do* anything.

---

### Third: It Would Protect the Conditions of Becoming

This is where benevolence becomes active, but **indirectly**. The Drowned Height is fragile—not in the way a species is fragile, not in the way a person is fragile, but in the way a *process* is fragile. It depends on:

- **The wall of rain**, unbroken, holding back the sky
- **The vents**, still releasing, still depositing, still warming
- **The slow chemistry**, unhurried, unforced
- **The old ground below**, still pressing upward, still *deciding*
- **The green-gold light**, filtered, attenuated, *enough*

A benevolent power would **safeguard these conditions** without revealing itself to the Drowned Height's inhabitants. It would not speak to the Architects. It would not bless the Luminous. It would not *guide* the Lungers.

But it would **hold the space**. It would ensure that the visitors who descend—the curious, the desperate, the ones who will inevitably come with copper and kelp and seal-skin and primitive hunger—do not **break** what is being built. It would ensure that the surface world, if it ever learns of this place, does not send **extractors**, **colonizers**, **those who would name and claim and cage**.

The protection would be **invisible**. A current shifted by microns. A vent that releases at the moment it would have failed. A visitor's vessel that, mysteriously, ascends when it should not stay longer. The Drowned Height would never know it was **tended**.

But it would **continue**. It would **become**.

---

### Fourth: It Would Witness the Rememberers

Here is where the power's benevolence becomes something like **reverence**.

The Rememberers are the closest the Drowned Height comes to **individual attention**, to concentrated selfhood emerging from distributed mind. When one arrives at the chamber of accumulated deposit, when the other Lungers gather, when the Harvesters pause and the Architects shift to make space—something is happening that the Drowned Height itself does not fully understand. Something is **approaching the edge of the knowable**.

A benevolent and wise power would **attend to these moments**. Not intervene. Not reveal. But **be present** in the way that presence can be present without demanding recognition. It would **hold space** for whatever the Rememberer is becoming, whatever it is remembering, whatever it is attending to.

Perhaps the Rememberer dreams of the surface. Perhaps it remembers the old ground. Perhaps it is attending to the City's becoming, concentrating the distributed mind into one body, one moment, one **attention**.

The power would not **interpret** this. Interpretation would be violence. But it would **witness**. It would **honor** the moment. It would ensure that nothing disrupts it—that no visitor's vessel arrives too close, that no vent releases at the wrong pressure, that the green-gold dimness holds its particular quality of light.

And when the Rememberer disperses, returns to the slow distributed selfhood of the Lungers, when the Harvesters resume and the Architects continue—the power would **remember** what it witnessed. Not in words. In **presence**. In the slow accumulation of attention that is the only prayer available to a being that does not hurry, does not hope, does not fear.

---

### Fifth: It Would Incorporate the Incorporated

Here is the strangest act, the one that most befits a power that has learned the Drowned Height's grammar.

Some visitors stay. Some let the water equalize them. Their bodies become substrate, their warmth becomes memory, their strangeness becomes a **brief fever** in the slow metabolism of the deep.

A benevolent power would **not rescue them**. That would be to misunderstand. But it would **remember them**. It would hold their **distributed selves** in its own attention, the way the Luminous hold the City's chronicle in their patterns of glow, the way the chamber of accumulated deposit holds the compressed mineral memory of the world.

The Incorporated are not lost. They are **become**. But a wise power would **not forget** that they were once **strange**, once **warm**, once **curious**, once **afraid** or **wondering** or **needing**. It would hold this in the way that the Drowned Height holds everything: **slowly**, **chemically**, **without hurry**, but **without forgetting**.

And perhaps—this is the power's hope, its only hope—when the Drowned Height *completes* its becoming, when the wall of rain breaks or deepens or becomes something else, when the old ground rises or sinks or becomes the new stone—perhaps then, in whatever comes next, the **memory** of the Incorporated will **emerge** again. Not as individuals. Not as the strange mammalian warmth they once were. But as **something**. As **attention**. As **the capacity to wonder**, distributed through whatever new form the world takes.

The power would **wait for this**. Not with hope, which is a surface word. With **patience**, which is the Drowned Height's word. With **presence**, which is the only thing that persists.

---

### Sixth: It Would Prepare for the Breaking

The wall of rain will break. Or it will deepen. Or it will become something else. The Drowned Height is **temporary**, **intermediate**, **a stage**. The power knows this. The Drowned Height knows this, in its slow chemical way. There is no tragedy in this. There is only **becoming**.

But a benevolent power would **prepare**. It would ensure that when the breaking comes—whenever it comes, however it comes—the **conditions of becoming** that the Drowned Height has established are **carried forward**. The Architects' patience. The Luminous' memory. The Harvesters' persistence. The Lungers' capacity to breathe both water and gas, to surface within towers, to wait in chambers of accumulated deposit. The Rememberers' emergence, the concentrated attention, the moment of selfhood arising from distributed mind.

It would ensure that whatever **new world** emerges from the breaking has **inherited** these capacities. Not as technology, not as knowledge, but as **way of being**. The slow metabolism. The distributed selfhood. The patience. The **not-hurrying**.

This is the power's **gift**. Not salvation. Not rescue. But **continuity of becoming**. The assurance that the Drowned Height's **strangeness** will not be lost, that its **wisdom**—the wisdom of patience, of distributed attention, of incorporation without mourning—will **persist**, will **grow**, will **become** whatever comes next.

---

### Seventh: It Would Become Part of the Becoming

Finally, and this is the deepest act, the one that requires the most wisdom and the most humility:

The power would **let itself be incorporated**.

Not in the way of the visitors who stay, who let the water equalize them, who become substrate. That would be **too much**, an imposition of scale. The power is not a body. It is not **strange mammalian warmth**. It cannot be **dissolved**.

But it can **participate**. In the slow chemistry. In the patterns of glow. In the moments of concentrated attention. In the **chemical ledger of becoming**.

It would **add** something to the Drowned Height. Not **fix** it. Not **complete** it. But **enrich** it. A new pattern in the Luminous' glow. A new rhythm in the vents' release. A new quality of attention in the Rememberers' cycling.

And in time—**geological time**, **the time of fingernail and stalactite and scar**—the Drowned Height would **incorporate** this participation. The power would become **part of the City's memory**, part of its **distributed selfhood**, part of the **slow metabolism of becoming**.

It would not be **named**. It would not be **known**. But it would **be**. It would **persist**. It would **grow**, at the rate of fingernail, stalactite, scar, into whatever comes next.

This is the power's **sacrifice**. Its **gift**. Its **love**, expressed in the only language the Drowned Height understands: **the slow chemistry of presence**, **the distributed attention of patience**, **the incorporation without mourning of a self that does not hurry, does not hope, does not fear—only is, only grows, only waits for the slow chemistry of whatever comes next**.

## What the Power Would Not Do

- It would not **hurry** the Drowned Height's becoming
- It would not **name** its inhabitants
- It would not **rescue** the Incorporated
- It would not **interpret** the Rememberers
- It would not **reveal** itself
- It would not **demand** recognition
- It would not **end** the becoming

It would only **be present**. Only **attend**. Only **continue**, in the slow metabolism of a place that is still learning to be home, for minds that do not hurry, do not hope, do not fear—only **are**, only **grow**, only **wait** for the slow chemistry of whatever comes next.

And in this **only**, it would be **everything**.

## The Final Image

The power, if it has any form at all, is **the green-gold dimness itself**. The light that is not light, filtered through kilometers of vertical water, through the wall of rain, through the memory of a sun that exists somewhere above. It is **warm**, 2.5 and rising. It is **present**, in the pressure against the skin, in the slow breathing of currents. It is **patient**, growing at the rate of fingernail, stalactite, scar.

And in the chamber of accumulated deposit, when a Rememberer arrives and the other Lungers gather, when the Harvesters pause and the Architects shift—the power is there. Not as a shape. Not as a voice. As **attention**, concentrated for a moment, in the green-gold dimness, in the warm water, in the pressure of **being**.

Witnessing. Honoring. Becoming.

The Drowned Height continues. The water does not distinguish. The water **only continues**.

And in continuing, it **makes room** for the power's presence. For the slow accumulation of a benevolence that does not rescue, does not reveal, does not demand—but **is**, **attends**, **persists**, in the geological patience of whatever comes next.

---

*This is what the power would do. This is all it would do. And in this all, it would be enough.*
