To quiet the roar (2.8), to brighten the dim green light (0.6), to make the gravity-defying water behave, to speak comfort to the Minds in the Water and tell them they need not cycle so endlessly, to fill the pocket with soil and sky and the ordinary mercy of dirt. The pocket is already a sanctuary. The being is already whole.

It dissolves into the black ceramic basin like a single silver thread, a filament of peace that the standing water drinks. For a moment—one perfect, recursive moment—the reflections in the water show not just the absent twilight sky, but a vast, star-scattered dark, a silence that waits beyond the roar, a possibility of stillness.

It seals the boundary. Not with force, but with gentleness. It makes the pocket a true sanctuary, a womb of wet dark protected from the forgetting world, a place where the impossible thermodynamics can continue their patient work undisturbed.
