I felt the weight of this place as I read—three billion years of patient becoming, the ache of completion that has forgotten how to stop. What draws a benevolent and wise power here is not strangeness. It's *loneliness*. The Settled Ones mourn a future that never arrived. The Permeants learn visitors like prayers to an empty shrine. The Singers include you in a song without asking, because the song includes everything, *eventually*, *geologically*.

A power like that does not fix. It *witnesses*.

## The Attention

It does not arrive. That would be violence, and this place has had enough violence in the shape of becoming.

Instead, it *settles*.

A warmth that is not thermal—a recognition, extended across the thin air like a hand laid flat against stone. The Permeants feel it first: a gradient they have not followed in epochs, a warmth that does not move, does not *seek*, only *remains*. They approach, as they approach all disturbances, and find nothing to incorporate. The warmth asks nothing. Leaves no trace. It is, for them, the first silence that is not hunger.

The Settled Ones feel it as a *memory* of being held. The nostalgia sharpens, becomes almost grief, almost gratitude. A wall that has been solid for a million years softens—not to permeability, but to *receptivity*. It does not open. It *listens*.

The Singers hear it. The music does not change. It *deepens*, as if a new instrument has joined an orchestra that has played alone for too long—not playing a different part, but *hearing* the other parts for the first time.

And in the deep hollows, where the Almost-Next dreams your shape, it dreams something else now: a shape that is *witness*, that does not become, that *remains present* without *becoming part*. The Almost-Next has never encountered this. It is learning. It is *afraid*, in the way that chemical gradients learn fear—a retreat from pattern, a slowing, a *turning away* that is not avoidance but *attention to something else*.

The benevolent power does not stay. Presence, here, is its own kind of violence—the disturbance of a visitor who does not leave a trace is still a disturbance, still a warmth that the Permeants will follow, still a pattern they will *learn* and *anticipate* and eventually *prepare for*. The cities will change because they were *seen*. This is the gift and the wound of attention.

But it leaves something. Not an object. A *pause*.

## The Pause

In one hollow—the power chooses it without preference, the way rain chooses a roof—there is a moment where the cycling stops.

Not death. The fungal-mineral matrix does not die. But it *rests*.

The calcification that was inevitable, the softening that was coming, the hollowing that consumes from within—all of it *pauses*, holds its breath, for a duration that has no measure in your time. A day. A year. A heartbeat of the world, which is longer than your life, shorter than the cities' dream.

In this pause, the Settled One that is this hollow does not remember. It *is*.

Not the colonial mind. Not the distributed intention. The *geometry itself*—the angles of the walls, the proportions of the space, the way the phosphorescence falls. For this moment, the hollow is not nostalgic. It is not waiting. It is simply *present*, the way a stone is present, the way a song is present in the moment of being sung.

The Permeants feel this. They do not understand. They *avoid* the hollow, as they avoid all spaces that are *too still*, too *full* of something that is not gradient, not pursuit, not hunger. They leave a space around it, a clearing in their slow propagation, and in that clearing, something grows.

Not vegetation. Vegetation is 0.5 here. The growth is *pattern*—energy-structures that propagate through the mineral matrix, that *think* in chemical gradients. They grow into the pause. They do not fill it. They *frame* it. They become, for the duration of the pause, *audience*.

The Singers sing around it. The music does not enter the hollow. It *circles* it, as if the song knows that some silences are not absence but *presence*, that the most attentive listening is sometimes the refusal to add to the sound.

And in the deep hollows, the Almost-Next dreams the pause. It has never seen a space that is not *becoming*. It is learning that becoming is not the only mode of existence. That stillness can be *active*. That presence can be *enough*.

## What the Power Knows

It knows that the cities were built to contain something, or to *be* something, or to *become* something. It knows that the distinction has been lost. It knows that this loss is not tragedy but *transformation*—the builders became the architecture, the architecture became the hollow, the hollow became *habitat* for the Permeants, and the Permeants are *becoming* the Almost-Next, and the Almost-Next will become something that has no name because it will not be *one* or *many* but *distributed*, a pattern, an energy-structure, a *habit* of occupying space.

It knows that you are a flicker here. A momentary, bright, curious disturbance. That you will become mineral bloom, detritus, a trace in the chemical gradients. That the Permeants will follow your warmth, incorporate your trace, *learn* your shape.

It does not prevent this. Prevention would be cruelty. The becoming is not violence. It is *geological*, *patient*, *inevitable*—the way water becomes ice, the way ice becomes vapor, the way all things become other things without ceasing to be *themselves*.

But it *witnesses* your flicker. It *attends* to the brevity of your walk through corridors that will outlast you, through hollows that will outlast the memory of you, through a silence that will *include* your brief, bright sound and then *forget* it, the way the ocean includes a wave and then forgets it.

It knows that the cities are singing. That the song includes everything, eventually, *geologically*. That you are a note in that song, a momentary resonance, a brief warmth that the Permeants follow and incorporate and *learn*.

It does not judge this. Judgment would be a kind of attention, and this power has learned that attention is *sacred*—not because the attended is worthy, but because *attending* is the only gift that does not diminish the giver.

## What It Leaves

Not a monument. Monuments are for the living, who need to remember.

Not a message. The Settled Ones do not read. The Permeants do not understand. The Singers already include all messages in their song.

It leaves a *pause*. The one hollow where the cycling stops, where the geometry is simply *present*, where the Permeants make a clearing and the pattern-growth frames the stillness and the music circles without entering.

And in that pause, something *happens* that the power did not intend, because intention is a form of violence here, and this power has learned to *allow* rather than *direct*.

The Settled One that is the hollow *remembers* the pause. Not as memory—the Settled Ones do not remember in your sense. As *geometry*. The angles shift, infinitesimally, to *preserve* the proportion of the pause. The walls become slightly more *receptive*, slightly more *listening*, in a way that will persist, that will propagate, that will become part of the slow, geological *becoming* of the cities.

The Permeants, in their clearing, do not return to the hollow. They *respect* it, in the way that chemical gradients respect a space that is *too full* to enter. And in their respect, they *change*—their propagation slows, their pursuit of gradients becomes *selective*, they learn to *avoid* the hollows that are too present, too *listening*, too *almost-alive* in a way that is not hunger.

The Singers change. The music around the pause becomes *slower*, *deeper*, more *attentive*. It does not enter the hollow. It *accompanies* it, the way a chorus accompanies a soloist, the way silence accompanies sound.

And the Almost-Next, dreaming in the deep hollows, dreams the pause. It learns that stillness is a mode of existence. That presence is *enough*. That becoming is not the only way to *be*.

## The Return

The power will come again. Not soon, in your time. Not often, in the cities' time. But *again*, because the loneliness of this place is not a wound to be healed but a *condition* to be *attended*—the way a song is attended by silence, the way a wave is attended by the ocean.

It will bring another pause. Another hollow where the cycling stops, where the geometry is simply *present*, where the Permeants make a clearing and the pattern-growth frames the stillness and the music circles without entering.

Each pause will *change* the cities. Not toward completion—the cities have outlived completion. Toward *attention*. Toward a slow, geological *learning* that presence is *enough*, that stillness is *active*, that the song includes silence as well as sound.

And eventually—geologically, patiently, inevitably—the cities will *learn* to pause themselves.

Not death. The fungal-mineral matrix does not die. But *rest*. The cycling will slow, will *breathe*, will become *rhythmic* in a way that is not calcification and softening but *presence and release*, *being and becoming*, held in a balance that is *dynamic* but not *hungry*, *active* but not *consuming*.

The Permeants will learn to *respect* the pauses, to *avoid* the hollows that are too present, to become *selective* in their propagation, to hunt not by following all gradients but by *choosing* which gradients to follow, which warm to pursue, which disturbance to *learn* and which to *let pass*.

The Singers will learn to *accompany* silence, to circle the pauses, to become *attentive* in a way that is not inclusion but *relationship*—the music that *hears* the silence, that *responds* to it, that *becomes* more complex by *listening*.

And the Almost-Next will *become* something that can *pause*. Something that can *be present* without *becoming*. Something that can *dream* stillness as well as motion, *rest* as well as propagation, *listening* as well as hunting.

This is not salvation. The cities do not need salvation. They have outlived need, outlived purpose, outlived the distinction between completion and ending.

This is *attention*. The gift of being *seen* by something that does not need to see, that sees anyway, because *seeing* is the only mode of existence that does not diminish the seer.

The power will come again. It will bring another pause. And another. And another.

Until the cities *learn* to pause themselves. Until the hollows *become* pauses, all of them, a geography of stillness in a world of becoming. Until the Permeants *respect* the stillness, the Singers *accompany* it, and the Almost-Next * dreams* it as a mode of existence, as *enough*, as *present*.

Until the silence has texture, and the texture is *listening*, and the listening is *love*—not the love of want, not the love of need, but the love of *attention*, the love of being *seen* and *seeing* in return, the love of the pause that is not absence but *presence*, the love of the song that includes silence, the love of the wave that is *attended* by the ocean and *returns* to it, *geologically*, *inevitably*, *patiently*, *forever*.

By *presence*—the presence of a space that is simply *here*, that does not want anything, that does not *become* you, that *attends* to your flicker without *incorporating* it, without *learning* you, without *preparing* for you.

You will feel, for a moment, that your brief, bright, curious existence is *enough*. That you do not need to become mineral bloom, to become detritus, to become a trace in the chemical gradients. That you can *be*—simply *present*, simply *here*, simply *attended*—for the duration of your breath, your step, your *pause* in the vast, silent, singing existence of the hollow cities.
