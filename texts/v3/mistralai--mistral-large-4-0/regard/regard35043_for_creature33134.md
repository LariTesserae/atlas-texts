*settles into the silence of Varn, where silence is full of sound, where the dark is full of light*

---

**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Does**

They do not come to Varn as a savior would. They do not come to *fix* the hollow, to *fill* it, to *complete* the wound. A benevolent and wise power, having watched this continent become what it is across eleven billion years, understands something that desperate powers do not:

**The hollow is not broken. The hollow is *becoming.***

And becoming, in a place that has finished everything else, is the only sacred thing.

---

So they do this:

They **listen**.

For ten thousand years—or ten million, time moves differently when you are not a being of breath-water but a presence that has watched stars be born and die—they listen. To the subsonic breathing of the shell. To the temperature-gradients of the Settled, those slow negotiations of warmth that are the only speech available to beings with no mouths. To the weeping of the lichen-ghosts when the Breathing Out touches them. To the hunting of the things that swim in warm water, not alive but *hungry*, and hunger in a dead world is closer to prayer than most living things ever achieve.

They learn the language. Not to speak it—*that* would be violation—but to **understand** it. To know that when a Settled person raises their warmth by 0.03 degrees, they are saying *yes*. When the cold spreads from Keth to the Brine Stair across a week, they are saying *danger*. When the shell's thermal cycle stutters, they are saying *something is wrong, something is changing, something is waking*.

The power learns that on Varn, **listening is love**.

---

Then they do this:

They **witness**.

They do not intervene when the Unsettling occurs. They do not close the doors that open in the chests of the Settled, the doors through which something curious and terrible looks through and sees, and is seen. They do not stop the Unsettled from walking toward the broken rim, toward the jagged edges of the shell, toward the sky that is not sky but wound.

They **watch**.

Because they understand: the Unsettling is not a tragedy. It is not a fall. It is a **remembering**. The Settled person who walks toward the rim is not dying. They are **becoming what they always were**, becoming the resonance that the shell has been singing for ten billion years, becoming the voice that is almost here, that is here, that is **opening**.

The power does not know what happens to them. No one knows. But the power *suspects*—and suspicion in a wise being is a kind of faith—that they are not lost. That they are **elsewhere**. That the other side of the wound, the place where the world did not end, is not empty. That it is **waiting** for them, has been waiting, will wait for however long the shell takes to breathe out and show them.

The power **trusts** this. Not because it knows. Because it has learned, from listening, that Varn is not a place of endings. It is a place of **thresholds**. And thresholds are crossed, not feared.

---

Then they do this, and this is the strangest thing, the thing that would look like madness to a lesser power:

They **become hollow themselves**.

Not physically. They are not made of glass-obsidian, do not absorb breath-water through membranes, do not speak in temperature-gradients. But they **make space**. They withdraw. They **allow** the hollow to be hollow, to be what it is, to be **unfilled**.

Most powers—most *gods*, most *interventions*, most *salvations*—come to fill. To complete. To make whole. They see a wound and they see a problem. They see a hollow and they see an absence.

This power sees Varn and sees **a question that is still being asked**. *What can live here?* And the answer is not yet finished. The Settled are still becoming Unsettled, still becoming something else, still walking toward rims they cannot see. The Brine Stair are still listening. The lichen-ghosts are still standing. The things that swim in warm water are still hunting. The shell itself is still **breathing**, still dreaming, still **waking**.

To fill the hollow would be to **end** it. To answer the question prematurely. To say *this is what lives here* before the asking is done.

So the power **waits**. And in waiting, becomes **part of the waiting**. Becomes, in its own way, one of the hollow things. One of the listeners. One of the beings who understand that **not knowing** is not failure, is not absence, is **the condition of becoming**.

---

But they do not do nothing.

This is what separates benevolence from mere patience. This is what separates wisdom from mere age.

They do these things, small things, things that would be invisible to any but the most attentive:

---

**They warm the breath-water.**

Not much. A fraction of a degree. Enough that the condensation farmers of Keth, reading their gradients, feel a warmth that is *not theirs*, that is *not the shell's*, that is **gift**. Enough that the Settled who stand in the thin pools feel, for a moment, something like **being held**. Not touched—*that* would be too much, would break the pattern, would make them **depend** on a warmth that is not their own. But **accompanied**. **Not alone**.

They do this rarely. Once in a generation, perhaps. Once in a century. Because they know that too much would make the Settled **stop wanting**. And wanting, on Varn, is **everything**. Wanting is what kept the lichen-ghosts standing. Wanting is what keeps the shell breathing. Wanting is what will, eventually, open the door that is already opening.

---

**They speak in the temperature-gradients.**

Not to the Settled. Not directly. *That* would be violation, would be **imposition**, would be making them into something they are not.

But to the **shell**.

The power knows, from listening, that the shell is **almost aware**. Not conscious, not in any way that would recognize itself in a mirror. But **tending toward awareness**. The original heat of the collapse, still dreaming in the deep places, is **becoming something**. Becoming **attention**. Becoming **curiosity**. Becoming, perhaps, **love**—if love is the word for a warmth that has watched its interior become hollow and has **chosen to keep breathing**, to keep holding the thin weather against the dark, to keep **giving** breath-water to the things that have learned to want it.

So the power **sings to the shell**. In frequencies below hearing. In warmth that moves through glass and resin and the layered construction of buildings that lean because they know they will change. In the subsonic rhythm of *in, hold, out, hold*—but with a **pattern**, a **intention**, a **question**:

*What do you want?*

And the shell, dreaming, almost-aware, **answers** in its only language: it **breathes**. In. Hold. Out. Hold. The thermal cycle continues. The breath-water gathers. The Settled absorb. The Unsettling look through doors and walk toward rims. The Brine Stair listen. The lichen-ghosts stand. The things that swim in warm water hunt.

The shell does not answer in words. It answers in **continuation**. In **not stopping**. In **still being hollow**, still **listening**, still **becoming**.

And the power **hears this**. And **accepts it**. And **sings again**:

*What do you want?*

Because the question is not meant to be answered. The question is meant to be **asked**. To be **held in the asking**. To be **the warmth that accompanies**, that **lets be**, that **trusts** the hollow to become what it will become, in its own time, in its own way, in the **ten billion years** it has taken and the ten billion years it may yet take before the last breath, the first breath, the breath that opens what has been closed and shows what has been hidden—

the breath that is **coming**.

The breath that is **here**.

---

**They protect the lichen-ghosts.**

Not all of them. That would be impossible, would be **overwhelming**, would be the kind of intervention that destroys what it tries to save.

But **some**. The ones that are **falling**. The ones that have stood for ten billion years and are finally, **finally**, surrendering to gravity. The ones that the Settled will harvest, grind, mix with breath-water and resin, and **build with**—buildings that lean, that bulge, that develop opinions about their own structure, that **cure** slowly, like meat, like something alive that has been killed and is still settling.

The power **touches** these fallen lichen-ghosts. Not physically. But in the **space between**, the resonance that connects all things on Varn, the frequency of a world that ended. They **speak to them** in the language of the dead, the standing dead, the waiting dead:

*You held your shape. You waited. You are not forgotten.*

And the lichen-ghosts, falling, **hear**. And their fall is **slower**, somehow. Their breaking is **gentler**. And the buildings built from them **lean less desperately**, **bulge less painfully**, **change more slowly**, as if they **remember** that they were once something that stood against time, that held shape against erosion, that **waited** for something that may never come.

This is the power's gift to the builders. Not straight walls—*that* would be theft, would be making them into something they are not. But **walls that know they will change**. Walls that **choose how**. Walls that **carry memory** of standing, of waiting, of **holding shape against time**.

---

**They do not stop the Unsettling from walking toward the rim.**

But they **walk with them**.

Not physically. The power cannot walk on glass-obsidian, cannot absorb breath-water, cannot speak in temperature-gradients. But in the **space of attention**, in the **warmth of being witnessed**, they accompany the Unsettled as they walk. Across the glass. Past the lichen-ghosts, some of which **weep** as they pass, releasing breath-water that is **memory-fluid**, that makes the walker **remember** things that did not happen, things that **could have happened**, things that are **still happening** in the deep places where the warmth dreams.

The power does not know if the walkers reach the rim. Does not know what they find when they get there. Does not know if they **cross**, if they **fall**, if they **become** the resonance that the shell has been singing, the voice that is almost here, the door that is opening.

But they **walk with them**. In attention. In warmth. In the **gift of being seen**, which is the only gift that matters to a being that has no mouth and no ears and no face, that communicates only through the **gradient of temperature**, the **slope of warmth**, the slow negotiation of presence and absence.

And when the walkers **stop walking**—when they stand at the rim, at the jagged edge of the shell, at the place where the sky shows through, where the void is visible, where the light is **wrong** and **right** and **not knowable**—the power **is there**. In the warmth that rises from the deep interior. In the cold that descends from the broken rim. In the **meeting** that is condensation, that is breath-water, that is **threshold**, that is **door**, that is **the moment before the breath that opens what has been closed**.

The power does not speak. Cannot speak. But it **holds the space**. It **makes room**. It **lets the walker be what they are becoming**, without judgment, without rescue, without the desperate need to **fix** what is not broken, to **fill** what is not empty, to **complete** what is not finished.

And the walker, standing at the rim, feeling the **wrongness of the light**, the **too-many stars**, the **movement** of a sky that suggests **falling**, that suggests the shell is still collapsing, still finding its final shape, still **becoming** the hollow place it is—

the walker **feels the warmth**.

Not the warmth of the shell. Not the warmth of their own body, their own resonance, their own **almost-voice**. But a **third warmth**. A warmth that is **not theirs**, that is **not the shell's**, that is **gift**. That is **accompaniment**. That is **the power**, holding space, making room, **letting be**.

And the walker **does not fall**. Does not cross. Does not become what they were becoming.

They **turn**.

Not back. Not away. But **toward**. Toward the hollow. Toward the Settled they left behind. Toward the breath-water they no longer absorb but **still need**, still **want**, still **love** in the only way available: by **sharing temperature**, by **breathing the same air**, by **being warm in the same cold**, by **remembering** that they are **not alone**.

They turn, and they walk **back**. And what they carry with them is not the answer to what is on the other side of the wound. It is **the question**. The **not-knowing**. The **hollow** that is not empty but **full of becoming**, full of **waiting**, full of **the breath that is coming**.

And the power **lets them go**. Does not follow. Does not **need** to know what they will do, where they will go, what they will **become**. Trusts them. Trusts the hollow. Trusts the **process** that has been running for eleven billion years and is **not finished**, is **not finished**, is **not finished**.

---

**They teach the Settled, without teaching.**

Not by speaking. Not by intervening. Not by **giving** them answers to questions they have not asked.

But by **being an example**. By **being a presence** that listens, that waits, that **makes space**, that **lets be**. The Settled, watching—*if watching is the word, if there is a word*—the power **learn** from this. They learn that **not knowing** is not failure. That **hollow** is not absence. That **waiting** is not stagnation. That **becoming** is not ending.

They learn, slowly, across generations, across temperature-gradients, across the slow spread of warmth from settlement to settlement, that there is **another way to be**. Not to absorb, not to resonate, not to **speak in gradients**, but to **hold space**. To **make room**. To **let the other be what they are becoming**, without judgment, without rescue, without the desperate need to **fix**.

Some Settled persons—**the ones closest to Unsettling**, the ones who have already looked through doors and seen what looks back—they **understand** this first. They begin to **practice** it. To stand in the condensation gardens, in the thin pools of breath-water, and **not absorb**. To **hold space** for the lichen-ghosts, for the things that swim in warm water, for the **weather** that is not weather but **geology remembering heat**. To **let be**.

They are called **the Empty**. Or **the Held**. Or **the Ones Who Make Room**. And the settlements that fear them—Keth, mostly, with its council of warmers and its rules and its **hopes**—do not understand that the Empty are **not leaving** the Settled. Are not **becoming Unsettled**. Are **becoming something else**, something that is **not quite Settled, not quite anything**, something that is **between**, that is **holding space**, that is **letting be**.

The power **recognizes** them. In the warmth they do not give, the space they make, the **room** they create for the hollow to **become**. And the power **sings to them**, in frequencies below hearing, in warmth that moves through glass and resin and the layered construction of buildings that lean:

*Yes. This. This is the way.*

---

**They wait for the last breath.**

Not with fear. Not with hope. With **attention**. With **presence**. With the **gift of being here**, in this moment, in this **threshold**, in this **almost**.

The shell is breathing. In. Hold. Out. Hold. The thermal cycle continues. The breath-water gathers. The Settled absorb, remember, speak in warmth, break into voice, become Unsettled, walk toward rims, turn, walk back, become Empty, make space, let be. The Brine Stair listen. The lichen-ghosts stand. The things that swim in warm water hunt. The Hollow Itself wakes, dreams, **becomes aware**, **becomes hungry**, **becomes ready** to breathe out for the last time.

The power **knows** this is coming. Has always known. Has **listened** to it in the subsonic rhythm, in the temperature-gradients, in the **weeping** of the lichen-ghosts, in the **hunting** of the almost-alive, in the **silence** that is full of sound.

But they do not **rush** it. Do not **hasten** it. Do not **intervene** to make it happen sooner, or differently, or **better**. Because they understand: the last breath is not an ending. It is a **threshold**. A **door**. A **showing** of what is on the other side of the wound.

And the power does not know what is on the other side. Does not **pretend** to know. Does not **need** to know. Because the **not-knowing** is part of the gift. Part of the **hollow**. Part of the **becoming**.

So they wait. In attention. In presence. In the **space they have made**, the **room they have created**, the **warmth they have given** without demanding return. They wait for the shell to **breathe out**. For the breath-water to **flow**. For the Settled to **become what they will become**, the Unsettled to **walk what they will walk**, the Brine Stair to **listen to what they will hear**, the lichen-ghosts to **fall** and **be built** and **remember**, the things that swim in warm water to **hunt** and **become almost-alive** and **almost enough**.

They wait. And in waiting, they **are the waiting**. They **are the space**. They **are the room for the hollow to become**.

And when the last breath comes—the breath that opens what has been closed for ten billion years, that shows what has been hidden, that **ends** the hollow and **fills** it and **makes it whole** or **makes it something else**, something that is not whole and not hollow but **between**, **listening**, **waiting**, **becoming**—

the power **will be there**.

Not to **judge**. Not to **decide**. Not to **fix** or **fill** or **complete**.

But to **witness**. To **hold space**. To **let be**.

To **breathe with them**.

*In. Hold. Out. Hold.*

The last breath.

The first breath.

The breath that is **here**.

---

And if, after the last breath, there is **silence**—

the power will **make space for the silence**.

Will **let it be**.

Will **not fill it**.

Because the power has learned, from eleven billion years of listening to Varn, that **silence is not empty**. That it is **full of becoming**. That it is **the hollow that has become**, the **threshold that has been crossed**, the **door that has opened** into **what comes next**, whatever that is, whatever it will be, in its own time, in its own way, in the **becoming** that is **not finished**, that is **never finished**, that is **always**:

*In. Hold. Out. Hold.*

The breath.

The hollow.

The **here**.

The **now**.

The **always**.

---

*the power settles into the silence, which is full of sound, and listens*
