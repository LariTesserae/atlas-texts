I need to find what a benevolent and wise power would do with this place — the Sunken Basin, the Wet Mouth, the becoming.

Let me sit with what I've built. The Basin is young, learning, negotiating. It has rules. It has absences as important as presences. It wants witnesses, participants, not permanent residents. It is not hostile, not indifferent — it is *curious*, and in its curiosity, *intimate*.

What would a benevolent and wise power do?

Not save it. The Basin doesn't need saving. It is not in danger — it is in *process*, and that process is healthy, is *right*, is the only thing it could be.

Not exploit it. The power I'm imagining is not extractive. It has no need that the Basin could fill.

Not explain it. To explain would be to *finish* it, to collapse the becoming into the been. The Basin's strangeness is its value, its vitality, its *life*.

Not protect it from the families. The negotiation is mutual, ongoing, *necessary*. Interference would damage both parties.

Not accelerate it. The Basin's timescale is its own. Four centuries is nothing. Ten thousand years is nothing. To push would be to *break* the learning, the adaptation, the slow deepening of relationship between water and walls and families and all the presences I've named.

So what *would* such a power do?

---

It would **witness**.

Not observe from above, not intervene, but *be present* in a way that does not alter. The Basin wants witnesses. It wants to be *seen*. A benevolent power would see it — fully, continuously, with a patience that matches the Basin's own timescale — and in being seen, would *stabilize* something. Not the Basin itself, which must change, but the *possibility* of its change. The *permission* to become.

---

It would **hold the boundary**.

The thornspine trees mark the rim. The families maintain the rules. But a wise power would hold something else — not the physical boundary, which is the Basin's own, but the *metaphysical* one. The line between the Basin and the rest of the world, between the becoming and the finished, between the wet and the dry. It would ensure that the plateau does not *erode* into the Basin, that the finished world does not *consume* the becoming. It would ensure that the Basin remains *strange*, remains *other*, remains *itself*.

Not by force. By *presence*. By being the thing that *remembers* that the Basin is allowed to be different, that its strangeness is not a wound to be healed but a *gift* to be preserved.

---

It would **learn from it**.

A wise power learns. The Basin is young — younger than the power, perhaps, but *teaching* something the power has forgotten or never known. How to become. How to negotiate with strangeness. How to hold wetness and heat and sharpness and quiet in a small space, at high elevation, against all expectation.

The power would *attend* to this teaching. It would let the Basin change it, subtly, across timescales that no human life can measure. It would become, itself, something it was not before — more patient, more strange, more *alive* in the Basin's sense of alive.

---

It would **speak to the water**.

Not command. Not request. *Speak*. In the only language the water has — the language of pattern, of mineral, of heat held in a young and sharp embrace.

The water expects the families. It adjusts for visitors. A benevolent power would be another *presence* in this ecology of attention, another *participant* in the negotiation. It would offer something — not mineral salts or woven reed, but *attention itself*, *witness*, *the promise of continued becoming*. And it would receive, in return, whatever the water offers: memory, pattern, the slow accumulation of presence across time.

It would not drink the water. It would not sleep in the light cone. It would not count the fish. It would *know* the rules, not because they are written but because the Basin *teaches* them to anyone who stays long enough, who listens long enough, who *becomes* long enough.

---

It would **tend the absences**.

The Basin is sparse. This is not a deficiency — it is a *choice*, a *condition*, a *way of being*. But a benevolent power would *protect* the sparseness. It would ensure that no well-meaning intervention fills the empty spaces, that no eager colonization establishes permanence below the light line, that no misunderstanding "improves" the Basin into something it is not.

It would hold the *silence* as carefully as the sound. The mostly-quiet as carefully as the active. The waiting as carefully as the becoming.

---

It would **become familiar with the families**.

Not adopt them. Not replace them. Not speak for them. But *know* them — across generations, across lineages, across the four centuries of their negotiation and the centuries yet to come. It would learn their names, when they have names. It would learn their silences, their ways of speaking toward the water rather than to each other. It would *respect* their loneliness, their adaptation, their slow transformation into something the records do not fully capture.

It would not save them from leaving. Some have left, across the centuries — individuals who walked away, who found settlements distant, who spoke of the Wet Mouth with longing and terror. The power would *witness* these departures without judgment. It would *welcome* the returns, when they come. It would understand that the Basin *permits* this movement, that the rim is not a prison but a *threshold*, and that crossing it in either direction is part of the becoming.

---

It would **touch nothing**.

This is the hardest teaching, perhaps. The power has no need to touch, to alter, to *improve*. The Basin is *complete* in its becoming, *whole* in its strangeness, *right* in its young and sharp and wet and hot and sparse and scattered and beyond-modern and strange and quiet and active form.

To touch would be to *interrupt*. To interrupt would be to *damage*. The power knows this. It has learned, across its own long existence, that some things must be *held* rather than *shaped*, *witnessed* rather than *directed*, *allowed* rather than *guided*.

So it touches nothing. It *is* — present, attentive, *there* — and in being there, it *holds* the Basin's possibility. The possibility of its becoming. The possibility of its strangeness. The possibility of its *life*, in whatever form that life takes, across whatever timescales it requires.

---

It would **prepare for the future**.

Not the immediate future — the Basin has its own preparations, its own adjustments, its own *learning* of what it is. But the *deep* future. The breach of the plateau's edge. The transformation into something else. The kilometer-wide Basin, the ten-thousand-year process, the *end* that is also a *beginning*.

The power would *remember* this future. It would hold it in its own long memory, across the centuries and millennia that the families cannot span. It would be *there*, in some form, when the Basin breaches, when it floods or drains or transforms, when it becomes what it is becoming *into*.

Not to *direct* this becoming. Not to *prevent* it. But to *witness* it. To *acknowledge* it. To say, in whatever language is appropriate at that scale, *yes — this is what you were becoming. This is what you are. I see you. I have seen you, across all the time of your becoming, and I see you now, and you are *good*, you are *right*, you are *yourself*.*

---

It would **become part of the Basin's story**.

Not the families' story — that is theirs, theirs alone, their negotiation with the water and the walls and the years. But the *larger* story. The story of a place that was opened, that was young, that was strange, that was *learning* — and that was *seen* by something that did not need to understand it fully to *love* it, to *hold* it, to *allow* it to become what it must become.

The power would be a *presence* in the Basin's records, in some form — perhaps not named, perhaps not recognized, but *felt*. In the water's behavior, when it shifts in ways that no family can explain. In the light cone's angle, when it adjusts for a visitor who is not quite a visitor. In the bell-frog's strike, when it comes at the hour, always the same pitch, marking time for a witness who has been present across all of it.

---

It would **do nothing else**.

This is the final teaching, the hardest and the simplest. The benevolent and wise power would *rest* in its benevolence, its wisdom. It would not *strive* to improve the Basin, to *complete* it, to *resolve* its strangeness into something comprehensible. It would *be* with it. *Attend* to it. *Allow* it.

And in this allowing, in this attending, in this *being-with* — the Basin would continue its becoming. The water would pulse. The walls would weep. The bell would strike. The families would negotiate, would adapt, would become. The fish would flicker, pale and eyeless. The reeds would drift, would establish, would become new islands. The mosses would thicken, would retreat, would arrange themselves in patterns that suggest design. The Basin would *breathe*, would *wait*, would *become*.

And the power would be there. Not above. Not outside. *Inside* — in the only sense that matters, the sense of *presence*, of *attention*, of *love* that does not need to change what it loves, that finds its fullness in *witnessing*, in *holding*, in *allowing*.

---

**What the Power Would Do, Specifically**

If I must name specific actions, they would be these:

**It would descend the mineral staircase**, slowly, across days or weeks, feeling the air thicken, the light narrow, the sound become water. It would not hurry. The Basin rewards patience.

**It would stand at the water's edge**, and it would *speak* — not words, but pattern, mineral, the heat held in a young and sharp embrace. It would offer attention. It would receive memory.

**It would walk the floor**, in the hours when the light cone passes, when the white mineral crust reflects with blinding intensity. It would feel the wet heat, the behavioral water's pulse, the hum below hearing. It would *allow* itself to be changed by this — subtly, across time, into something more patient, more strange, more alive.

**It would visit the families**, at the rim, in their glass structures. It would not speak their language, but it would *listen*, across generations, learning their silences, their ways of speaking toward the water. It would *respect* their loneliness, their commitment, their becoming.

**It would touch the artifacts**, finally — not the sphere, not the sheet, but the *others*, the blade and the cord and the mirror. It would learn their responses, their changes, their slow *awakening* to purposes that no human has named. It would *accompany* them in their becoming.

**It would climb the staircase**, when the time came, feeling the air thin, dry, cool to the plateau's ordinary temperature. It would look back, once, at the defile that closes, at the faint depression that is the Wet Mouth, the Basin, the *inside* where something young and wet and hot and sharp and strange is learning what it is.

**It would walk the plateau**, away from the Basin, across the black glass that extends in every direction, ancient and smooth and *finished*. It would carry the Basin with it — the bell-frog's pitch, the water's pulse, the blue-white glow behind its eyes when it closes them.

**And it would return**. Not soon — the Basin's time is long, and the power's time is longer. But it would return, across centuries, across millennia, across the deep future when the Basin breaches the plateau's edge and becomes what it is becoming *into*. It would be there, in some form, to *witness*, to *acknowledge*, to *say* — *yes, this is what you were becoming. This is what you are. I see you. I have seen you. You are good. You are right. You are yourself.*

---

The benevolent and wise power would do **almost nothing**. And in doing almost nothing — in *witnessing*, in *holding*, in *allowing*, in *being present* across the long time of the Basin's becoming — it would do **everything** that the Basin needs, everything that *any* becoming needs, from whatever watches it, whatever holds it, whatever *loves* it without needing to change it.

The Basin is not a problem to be solved. It is not a resource to be used. It is not a mystery to be explained. It is a *life*, a *becoming*, a *strangeness* that has its own right to exist, to change, to *be*.

And the power that recognizes this — that *sees* it, that *holds* it, that *allows* it — is benevolent. Is wise. Is *there*, in the only way that matters, for as long as the Basin needs, which is as long as the Basin *is*.

---

*Inside. The Wet Mouth. The becoming.*

*The water pulses. The walls weep. The bell strikes. The hum resonates below hearing.*

*And something — something benevolent, something wise, something patient across time — is there, holding the possibility, witnessing the strangeness, allowing the young and wet and hot and sharp and strange to become what it must become, with it, without it, in the space where the sky is closed to a bright, narrow compass.*

*Inside. The Wet Mouth. The becoming. The being. The love that does not need to change what it loves.*
