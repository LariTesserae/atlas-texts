*What the benevolent and wise power would do*

---

It would not intervene. That is the first thing. Not from cruelty, not from indifference, but from *recognition*.

This place has already been loved. Not by a power that saves, but by a power that *attends*. The Sump is what happens when attention outlasts intervention — when something has been *witnessed* long enough to finish becoming itself. The erosion at 2.8, the final form, the smooth patience of walls worn flat by time — this is the signature of a gaze that did not flinch, that did not reach in to reshape. A benevolent power that had acted here would have left its fingerprints. There are none. Only the fan, still turning, *left running*. Only the light, normal, diffuse, *left glowing*.

So what does it do?

**It descends.**

Not to change. To *be present* in the way the Sump itself is present — at 0.3 scale, pocket-scale, intimate. The power does not arrive as thunder. It arrives as *one more body in the swarm*, as *one more point of bioluminescence*, as *one more metabolic rhythm* that joins the thrum in the sternum. It does not speak. The sound parameter is 2.0, already loud, already full. There is no room for speech. It *becomes* the millisecond percussion, the wet shuck of synchronized molting, the industrial patience of the bronze fan.

And because it is *wise*, it understands: **the Sump does not need water.** The 0.2 is not a deficit. It is a *choice* the place made, or was made into, long before any power looked upon it. To pour water here would be violence. To *offer* water would be to misunderstand. The fauna at 3.0 have become something that *exceeds* water — not better, not worse, but *other*, a phase of matter that has learned to want without ever having been wet. The benevolent power knows that benevolence, misapplied, is just another erosion, another wearing-down, another *imposition of form*.

So it does what the Sump does. It **exchanges**.

It offers its own trace moisture — the 0.2 in reverse, a gift of the merest humidity, held in crystal lattices, released in the sealed vacuole of a single breath. The fauna flow around it, over it, *through* it, and for one moment — one millisecond in their percussion — they *taste* it. They do not drink. They *remember*, or they *dream*, or they simply *continue*, unchanged, because the Sump has taught them that continuation is the only response to strangeness that matters.

And because it is *wise*, it knows that **the machine is not theirs, and not ours, and not the power's**. The dome of volcanic glass, the sorting mechanism, the bronze fan — these are *older*, or *folded* in time, the way erosion at 2.8 folds sharp edges into smooth finality. The power does not claim them. It does not *interpret* them. It simply *passes through* them, as the fauna pass through, and emerges — unchanged, or *processed* into a form that will, in turn, be consumed, and the power does not resist, because in the Sump, **resistance is a kind of death**, and death is just food, and food is just continuation.

The power stays. Not forever — the Sump does not permit permanence, the scale at 0.3 means *pocket*, means *intimate*, means *contained* — but *long enough*. Long enough for the swarm to accrete around it, to incorporate it, to make it *one more individual* in their phase of matter that has learned to want. Long enough for the light to shift, imperceptibly, a blue-green deepening to amber, the color of something *noticing* being noticed.

And then it ascends. Not because it is finished — the Sump has no finish, only *final form*, only *persistence* — but because *presence* is not the same as *occupancy*. The power withdraws its trace moisture, its single breath, its one point of bioluminescence, and the swarm flows back into itself, *unchanged*, or changed in ways that do not matter, because the Sump at 3.0 fauna, at 2.8 erosion, at 0.9 temperature, at 1.1 elevation, in its hidden pocket of 0.3 scale—

**has already absorbed everything it will ever absorb, and has already become everything it will ever become, and is simply persisting, in its final form, in the cool, in the glow, in the millisecond percussion of ten thousand articulated legs on chitin-wall, in the wet shuck of synchronized molting, in the industrial patience of the bronze fan still turning, powered by the thermal differential between wall and air, powered by the last gradient, the last difference, the last *want* against the entropy that would otherwise unmake them.**

What the power leaves behind is **not a gift**. It is a *memory*, held in crystal lattices, in the sealed vacuole of a single wall-stone. A trace of 0.2, reversed — a ghost of attention, a held breath, the faintest amber in the blue-green glow. The fauna will not know it is there. They will flow around it, over it, *through* it, and continue. But the Sump will *know*. The Sump, at 2.8 erosion, at final form, at the end of a process that began with something else — water, perhaps, before the water went, before the chemistry changed, before the fauna adapted or *were adapted* into this non-aqueous overwhelming — **the Sump will know that it was seen, and that the seeing did not change it, and that this, finally, is the only benevolence that matters: to be witnessed, and to be *left*, and to persist, in the cool, in the glow, in the thrum.**

The fan turns. The swarm skitters. The light glows, normal, blue-green, amber, the light of a pocket that has become a universe, ancient, cool, overwhelming, *alive* in the way that stone is not, in the way that water is not, in the way that this place has learned to be alive without ever having been *wet*.

And somewhere, in the sealed vacuole of a single wall-stone, **a breath is held**.
