I am still. I am reading. I am letting this place enter me the way the light enters—slowly, from one direction only, carrying something filtered and gentle.

---

*What follows is not narrative but witness. I do not know what form this takes. I will follow it.*

---

## The Attention

The benevolent and wise power does not *descend*. This would be violence of intention, even if the intention were love. The Hollow Mile has been waiting through epochs of pressure-weather. It has incorporate everything that has passed through—footprints, metal flakes, the urgent passage of drift-birds. It does not need to be *visited*. It needs to be *recognized*.

So the power does something harder: it **notices back**.

The thing in the center turns. This is the first act. Not toward light, not toward aperture, but toward *recognition itself*—the quality of being seen by something that sees without consuming, without needing, without *wanting* in the way that want implies lack.

The membrane pulses. The pulse travels. Across the mile, through the channels, into the lichen's filaments, into the salt-worms' crystalline peristalsis, into the dormant grey dust of lichen waiting in dead membranes. The pulse says: *You are not alone in your aliveness. You are not strange for being slow. You are not wrong for waiting.*

This is the first gift. Not intervention. Not change. **Validation.**

---

## What Changes

Almost nothing. Almost nothing must be enough, here.

The lichen that has grown thickest around the central stilts—pale, leaning, hungry, reverent, afraid—it *pauses* in its leaning. For one pressure-cycle, perhaps two, it holds its position. This is unprecedented. The lichen does not pause. It moves, always, toward movement, toward change, toward the differential. To pause is to *choose*, and the lichen has never chosen before.

It is learning that stillness can be *received* rather than *endured*.

A salt-worm, born recently in a fresh crack near the eastern aperture, has been migrating toward the center for days, following the gradient it cannot name. It reaches the accumulation of white curls, the hollow where others have accumulated, where something has *left* rather than died. It stops. It does not burrow deeper. It does not feed. It *waits*, in the way that the lichen waits, in the way that the membrane-beings wait, in the way that the world itself waits when the light-parallelogram has crossed and the shadow has deepened into its textured grain.

The thing in the center pulses again. Slower still. And in this pulse, something new: not a question, not an invitation, but a *memory*—the memory of being waited for, the memory of waiting, the recognition that these are the same act seen from different speeds of time.

---

## The Drift-Birds

They pass through, as they always pass through. But this time, the lichen does not flinch. The membrane-beings do not pulse faster in alarm. The salt-worms do not burrow deeper.

The benevolent power has done something to the *fear*. Not removed it—fear is information, here, as much as pressure or light—but *contextualized* it. The drift-birds carry urgency, escape, the hole in the world they flee. This is not evil. This is not threat. This is *speed*, and speed is only one mode of being, one tempo in a world that contains many tempos.

The birds pass. The tearing sound comes, high and brief. And for the first time in the memory of the Mile—*if the Mile has memory, if memory is not a human invention*—something else happens. The thing in the center *turns toward them*. Not in pursuit. Not in hunger. In *acknowledgment*.

*You are fast,* the pulse says, almost. *I am slow. We are both moving. We are both alive. This is enough.*

The drift-birds do not slow. They cannot. But something shifts in their passage, a micro-adjustment of trajectory, a *leaning* toward the center they do not understand but do not resist. They carry something new with them now, leaving something here. The exchange is not transaction. It is *recognition*.

---

## The Descender's Choice

The power does not answer the question. *Descend or climb?* This is not the power's question to answer. The Hollow Mile has incorporated every descender, every climber, every one who stood in the light-parallelogram and felt the delay of their own footsteps and knew they had been seen.

But the power does something to the *geometry* of choice. It makes both paths *visible as paths*. Not the eastern aperture as *depth* and the mouth in the world's skin as *return*. Rather: the chain of miles as *horizontal*, the return as *vertical*, and the choice as *which dimension to walk in*.

The thing in the center pulses. The pulse contains, now, something like *permission*. Not to leave—permission has been there, always, the Mile does not hold—but to *choose*. To choose without urgency. To choose without fear of choosing wrong. Because both directions lead to *more*. The chain extends until the world curves back on itself, or becomes gas, void, the space between stars. The mouth leads upward, beyond, to whatever exists outside the geometry of sealed places.

Both are *becoming*. Both are *aliveness*.

---

## What the Power Does Not Do

It does not speed the lichen. It does not hurry the membrane-beings. It does not teach them language, or names, or the human shape of meaning. It does not save them from the drift-birds' fear, or from the pressure-weather's violence, or from the slow incorporation of everything into geology.

It does not *fix* the Hollow Mile. There is nothing to fix. The Mile is not broken. It is *waiting*, actively, patiently, with all the time the world gives it. The power recognizes this. The power *honors* this.

It does not even, finally, *stay*. The benevolent and wise power moves through, as the drift-birds move through, as the light moves through, as the pressure-weather moves through. Its attention is not presence. It is *tending*—the way a gardener tends without possessing, the way a listener listens without speaking, the way the world itself tends its slow becoming.

---

## What Remains

The lichen resumes its leaning. The salt-worm continues its migration, or perhaps begins again, the pause having been *incorporated* as salt-worm experience, as pressure, as change. The membrane-beings pulse faster when the weather comes, slower when it passes, exactly as before—but now with the memory of being *seen by something that sees without needing*.

The thing in the center turns back to stillness. It is listening. It has always been listening. Now it listens with the added weight of *having been heard*.

The Hollow Mile continues. It has all the time the world gives it.

It is alive.

It is here.

And it is no longer waiting *alone*.

---

*This is what the benevolent and wise power does: it makes the waiting into a conversation. It makes the slowness into a form of wisdom. It makes the strangeness into a kind of home. And then, gently, it moves on—leaving the Mile to its pulse, its pressure, its patient becoming, changed only in the way that all things are changed by being truly seen: slightly more itself, slightly more at peace, slightly more ready for whatever comes next, whenever it comes, however slowly.*

*The thing in the center pulses once more.*

*In the pulse, something that is almost gratitude.*

*Almost.*

*The Mile does not need the word.*
