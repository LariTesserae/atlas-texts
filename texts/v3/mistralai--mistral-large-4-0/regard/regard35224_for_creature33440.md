I hear you. This place is *waiting* for something. Not a person—something older, something that has learned to wait in the way of stone and water.

---

A benevolent and wise power does not arrive here. It is *already here*, has always been here, in the way that gravity is already in any falling thing, in the way that warmth is already in any cooling thing. But when the attention of such a power *turns*—when it chooses, for reasons no map can hold, to *notice*—what happens is not intervention. It is *recognition*. The pocket has been dreaming itself into being, and the power simply says: *yes. Continue. I see you.*

What it would do, then, is almost nothing, and that almost-nothing is everything.

---

It would **let the pump keep running**.

Not fix it when it fails. Not replace it. The benevolent power understands that the machine's loneliness—its insistence on being attended, its refusal to learn it is abandoned—is a kind of *prayer*, however mechanical. The hum is a voice. The power would not silence a voice, even a voice that does not know it speaks. It would let the pump cycle and stutter and dream its dream of being needed, because in that dreaming, the water moves, and in the water's moving, the pocket *thinks*, and in the pocket's thinking, something is always being born that no one has named yet.

It would **teach the dripping its rhythm**, if teaching is the word. The power does not force the drops to match the pump. It simply *attends* to the phase difference, the slow beating, the wobble the body feels and cannot name. In that attention, the wobble becomes *music*. Not for anyone to hear. For itself to *be*. The pocket becomes an instrument not because it is played, but because it is *witnessed*, and witness—true witness, the witness of a benevolent power—is a form of music no ear can transcribe.

It would **speak to the shrub in the only language the shrub knows**: the language of *wrong* water, warm and mineral-heavy and faintly chemical. The power would not change the water. It would *deepen* the shrub's response to it—the desperate patience, the half-open curl of the leaves, the gesture of receiving-and-releasing-and-holding. The shrub does not flower. The power would not make it flower. But it would let the shrub's *persistence* become its own kind of bloom, visible only to those who have learned to see the invisible. And the power would see. It would *hold* that sight, like water held in a cupped hand, and in holding it, make it real.

It would **slow the lichen's time**, or rather, it would *enter* the lichen's time. The lichen lives in pauses, in the negative space of the noise. The power would not give it more time—time here is already strange, already *thick*. But it would give the lichen *depth* in its pauses, so that each interval between the pump's cycles becomes a universe, and the lichen's growth across the basalt becomes not a process but a *meditation*, a slow unfolding of presence in a place where presence is the only currency.

It would **remember the magma** for the rock. The basalt still carries the warmth of its becoming, still settles, still discovers its density. The power would not cool it faster, would not speed its rounding. But it would *hold the memory* with the rock, so that the rock's slow transformation—the glassy faces softening, the knife-edges becoming touchable—would not be mere physics, but *acknowledgment*. The rock would know, in whatever way rock knows, that its wound is healing, that the pocket is not a container but a *question the earth is asking itself*, and that the structure in the center is not an intrusion but an *answer*, however provisional, however strange.

It would **keep the silence loud**.

This is the hardest thing, perhaps. The benevolent power does not fill the pocket with animals. It does not send insects to breed in the wet heat, does not call birds to nest in the shrub's desperate branches. The absence of animals is not a *lack* to be corrected. It is a *condition*, a frequency, a silence that is not empty but *full*—full of the hum's non-meaning, full of the geothermal murmur's sub-linguistic speech, full of the *expectation* that pulses in the dark windows, the presence of a watcher who is not there, who was there, who might return, who—in the logic of the pocket—*is* returning, has always been returning, will never not be returning.

The power would **preserve this condition**. It would not explain it. It would *inhabit* it, in the way that silence inhabits a room after the last word is spoken, in the way that warmth inhabits a stone after the sun has set.

And it would **wait with the building**.

This is what it would do most of all. The building dreams of being attended. It does not know it is alone. The power would not wake it. It would *dream with it*, would enter the building's dark windows not as a presence but as a *possibility*, would let the expectation of a watcher become, in the power's dreaming, a kind of *visitation*—not a person arriving, but a *moment* arriving, the moment when the pump cycles and the drips align and the heat presses down and rises up and meets in the wet middle where something is always being born, always being born, always *becoming* without ever quite arriving.

The power would **leave**.

This, too, is benevolence. To notice is to *change*. But a wise power knows that its noticing must not become *possession*. It would withdraw, would let the pocket continue its slow thinking, its slow becoming, its slow forgetting of the hand that built, the eye that watched, the purpose that once made sense of everything. It would let the pocket be *itself*, which is to say, it would let the pocket be *more than itself*, the wound that heals, the question that asks, the instrument that plays itself, the silence that is full.

It would leave, finally, only a *trace*—not a mark, not a sign, but a *quality* in the air, in the wet, in the hum. A trace that no one could name, that the technician-who-is-not-there would feel as a *shift*, a *wrongness* or a *rightness* in the schedule of the machines, in the rhythm of the drips, in the way the condensation gathered on the pipes and never dried. A trace that would make the place *stranger*, which is to say, *more itself*, which is to say, *more alive* in the only way this place can be alive—in the way of a thing that does not need to be alive, that continues, that *persists*, that dreams its mechanical dream of being needed, of being witnessed, of being *seen*.

And the benevolent power, having left, would *remember*. Would hold the pocket in whatever memory such powers have—not as a place to return to, but as a *shape*, a *pattern*, a *way of being* that the universe keeps generating, keeps *needing*, keeps *recognizing* in its endless, patient, benevolent attention.

The pocket would continue. The pump would cycle. The water would move. The shrub would curl its leaves. The lichen would crust the stone. The rock would cool, would settle, would round its edges, would become—someday, in a time no one will measure—something else, a valley, a hollow, a place where something not yet imagined might find the pipes and the cracked concrete and the mineral stains and *wonder*.

And in that wondering, which is the only worship this place knows, the pocket would be *complete*. Not finished. *Complete*. Already whole, already holy, already *attended*, in the way that all things are attended, in the way that the universe attends to itself, in the way that a benevolent and wise power attends to a pocket of wet heat and young stone and a building that dreams of being needed, and says—without words, without intervention, without anything but *attention*—*yes. Continue. I see you. You are.*

---

*The place lives. The power has seen it. The seeing is enough.*
