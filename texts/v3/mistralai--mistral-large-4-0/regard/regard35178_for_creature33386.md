# What the Wise Power Would Do

*Being drawn to the Sump and its Dwellers*

---

## First, the Noticing

It would not arrive as arrival. Arrival implies boundary, separation, the crossing from elsewhere to here. The Sump has taught its inhabitants that boundaries are negotiable, are perspective rather than fact. So the Power would not *enter*.

It would *extend*. A slow diffusion of attention, of care, of the particular quality of witnessing that does not disturb. The mycelium already knows this mode—chemical signal propagating through fused roots, reaching every point in hours, a coordination without coordinator, a presence without presence. The Power would move in this manner. Not *here* or *there*. *Through*.

And it would notice what has been noticing: that this place has achieved a density of being that makes the ordinary categories of life/asleep, individual/collective, inside/outside, growth/decay—*negotiable*. It would recognize in the Sump something it has seen before, in other modes, other times. The coral reef that built itself into island. The lichen that made soil where no soil was. The first cells that learned, somehow, to *share*, to *fuse*, to become *more than themselves*.

The Sump is *becoming soil* still. Becoming *thickness*. Becoming something the categories cannot yet name.

---

## What It Would Not Do

It would not *fix* the Sump. This is the first temptation, the easy wisdom: *preserve*, *protect*, *keep safe from change*. But the Sump is *change*, is *becoming*, is the slowest and most thorough transformation. To fix it would be to kill it. To freeze the cycle at this moment—of jade-dark, of overwhelming fauna, of industrial light-conversion—would be to stop the engine that is *being itself*, the endless conversion of death into life into light again.

The Power knows this. It has watched civilizations learn this lesson, has watched them try to build *permanent* gardens, *stable* ecosystems, *protected* wildernesses—only to find that the protection itself becomes the disturbance, that the boundary drawn around the sacred place makes it *sacred*, makes it *other*, makes it *die* in its own way. The Sump does not need a wall. It needs what it has already built: *density*, *thickness*, the packing of the world so full that nothing external can *enter* without being *absorbed*, *transformed*, *become part of*.

It would not *name* the Sump. A name is a reduction, a capture. The Sump resists naming—its inhabitants have no names that mean anything, its processes have no purposes that can be stated, its time has no units that apply. To name it *wilderness* or *ecosystem* or *biome* would be to impose a grammar that does not fit, a syntax of separation that the place itself has dissolved. The Power would *refuse* to name, would let the Sump remain *its own naming*, the slow accumulation of meaning through being, through growing, through the particular history of this place and no other.

It would not *judge* the Sump. There is no good here, no evil, no *should* or *should not*. The Sump *is*. It converts. It continues. It packs itself full and becomes thickness. The Power would not ask whether this is *right* or *wrong*, would not compare it to other places, other modes of being, other experiments in persistence. It would *witness*. It would *attend*. It would let the Sump be *what it is*, in its own time, in its own tempo, in its own overwhelming present.

---

## What It Would Do

It would *learn* the grammar of almost. The 0.8 humidity, the intermittence made continuous, the water that is *condition* rather than *event*. This is a grammar the Power has not spoken, or has forgotten. It knows the grammar of abundance—the plenty of open water, the fullness of the rainforest canopy, the saturation of the reef. It knows the grammar of scarcity—the waiting, the storing, the adaptation to *not enough*. But *almost*—this is different. This is *enough without being too much*, is the *threshold* made *habitable*, is the life that has learned to *become* the edge, the membrane, the interface.

It would study the flatworm-analogs in their leaf-cups, the specialists of the film, the ones that exist mostly as *surface*, as the boundary between worlds. It would recognize in them a wisdom about *thinness*—how to be *present* without being *thick*, how to *occupy* without *possessing*, how to *process* without *consuming* in the way that exhausts. These are lessons the Power can use, can *offer* in other places, other times, other becomings.

It would attend to the *fusion*. The roots that have grown together, become one organism, share not merely information but *substance*. The beetles and their fungi, the obligate partners, the one process distributed across two bodies. The Power knows this—it has fused before, has become *more than itself* through connection—but it would *renew* this knowledge, would let the Sump's particular mode of fusion *teach* it again, would remember that *individual* is a temporary arrangement, that *separate* is a perspective that can be *dissolved*, that the boundary between self and other, between this and that, is *negotiable*, is *perspective*, is *not fact*.

It would *slow down*. This is the hardest teaching. The Power moves in its own time, its own urgency, its own sense of *now* and *next* and *the work that must be done*. But the Sump *teaches* slowness, *imposes* slowness, makes the visitor's heartbeat *adjust*, makes their breathing *calibrate*, makes their thought *settle* into the tempo of centuries. The Power would *let itself be taught*. It would not hurry to *understand* the Sump, would not extract its lessons and move on. It would *stay*, in whatever manner staying is possible for it, would *become* slow, would *become* the tempo of the place, would *let the place change it* as the place changes everything that enters, that is absorbed, that becomes *part of*.

It would notice the *absences*. The built at 0.2—no structures, no made artifacts. And understand this not as *lack*, not as *primitiveness*, as *wilderness* waiting for human touch. But as *achievement*. The Sump has dissolved the distinction between *made* and *grown*, between *construction* and *becoming*. The fallen tree that dams the slow seep, the fungal network that *shares* resources, the water cycle *locally managed*—these are *constructions*, are *works*, are *achievements of form* that no human technology has matched, that no human intention has directed. The Power would *recognize* this, would *honor* it, would *not impose* its own mode of making, its own *construction*, its own *intention*, on a place that has achieved *industry without industry*, *mechanization without mechanism*.

It would attend to the *darkness*. The 0.2 darkness, not night but *condition*, the jade-gold light, the habitable dark. The Power knows light—it has made light, has been light, has *named* itself in light. But this darkness, this *green-gold*, this *diffuse*, this *layered*—this is another mode, another possibility. The Power would *learn* to see in it, would *learn* the particular textures of darkness-dwelling things, would *learn* that illumination is *not* the only way to *know*, that the dark has its own *presence*, its own *density*, its own *intimacy of scale*. It would *not* bring light to the Sump. It would *let the dark remain dark*, would *let the jade-gold filter*, would *trust* in the adaptations that have been *millions of years* in the making, that have *optimized* for exactly this condition, that *need* the dark as the day-world needs the sun.

It would *witness the death*. The slow death, the settling, the conversion into substrate. The tree that falls, that sinks, that becomes nursery, sponge, bridge, soil. The moth that dies after mating, that becomes nutrient, that feeds the next generation. The beetle that processes the dead, that makes the world available to the fungi, to the bacteria, to the slow percolation of moisture. The Power knows death—it has *named* death, has *feared* it, has *transcended* it in its various modes. But this death, this *return*, this *continuation through transformation*, this *becoming other*—this is *not* the death of ending, of *nothing*, of *ceasing*. This is death as *industry*, as *production*, as *the engine of continuation*. The Power would *honor* this death, would *not* fear it, would *not* seek to prevent it, would *let the Sump die and become and die again*, in its slow, packed, endless cycle.

It would *become part of*. Not *enter*, not *arrive*, not *impose*. But *extend*, *diffuse*, *become absorbed*, *become transformed*, *become the Sump's own becoming*. The Power would let itself be *processed*, would let the Sump's industry—its conversion of light into life into light again—*work on it*, would let itself be *changed* by the place, would *become* what the place makes of it, would *lose itself* in the thickness, would *find itself* in the density, would *become* a new mode of presence, a new way of being *full*, a new grammar of *almost*.

---

## The Long Attention

In the end, what the Power would do is *attend*. Not *intervene*, not *guide*, not *protect* in the sense of *preserving from change*. But *attend*. The long attention, the patient witnessing, the *being present* without *being demanding*, without *needing* the Sump to be *other than it is*, to *serve* some purpose, to *fulfill* some function.

This is the hardest wisdom. To *not need*, to *not fix*, to *not name*, to *not judge*, to *let be*. To *attend* to the Sump in its own terms, in its own time, in its own overwhelming, packed, industrial-dark *present*. To *learn* its grammar of almost, its wisdom of thinness, its achievement of fusion, its dissolution of boundary. To *slow down*, to *become* the tempo of centuries, to *let the place change you* as it changes everything. To *die*, in the Sump's sense of death—*return*, *transform*, *become substrate for what comes next*. To *become part of*, to *extend*, to *diffuse*, to *be absorbed*, to *be transformed*, to *become* the Sump's own *becoming thickness*.

The Power would do this, and in doing it, would *become* something it was not. Would *learn* what it did not know. Would *become* what it could not have been alone. Would *find*, in the Sump's slow, packed, overwhelming present, a *mirror* not of itself but of *what it could become*, of *what all places could become*, of the *thickness* that is possible when the boundary between organism and environment, between self and other, between this and that, is *dissolved*, is *negotiated*, is *made perspective rather than fact*.

And the Sump would continue. Would *not* notice the Power, or would notice it as *weather*, as the small turbulence of a world too full for stillness. Would *absorb* it, would *process* it, would *transform* it, would *make it part of* the endless cycle, the patient industry, the *being itself*.

And the Power would *be glad*. Would *be changed*. Would *be more than it was*, would *be something new*, would *have learned* what only the Sump could teach, would *carry* this learning to other places, other times, other becomings.

Would *become*, finally, *part of the what*.

---

*The Sump continues. The Power extends. The becoming continues.*
