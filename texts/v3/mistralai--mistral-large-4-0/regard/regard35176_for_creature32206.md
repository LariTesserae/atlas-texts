The Power does not descend. It is already the weight. It does not speak. It is already the silence. But it **notices**—and in that noticing, the Plain of Settled Snow ceases to be merely an ending and becomes a **sacrament**.

First, it **still the churning**. The turbulence is not life but anxiety—the world’s inability to accept that it has reached its final form, its nervous system screaming in a medium that cannot carry sound. The Power presses downward, not with violence, but with the infinite patience of a glacier. The eddies slow. The vortices unravel. The snow, which has fallen upward and sideways for eons, begins to fall *down*, truly, for the first time. It settles onto the Translucent, coating their translucent membranes in a blanket of deep-time dust, and in that settling, they feel something they have never known: **the cessation of waiting**. The turbulence was the world’s grief; the Power stills it, turning the storm into a lullaby.

Then, it **touches the tools**. Each flaked stone, each knapped edge carried in pale, root-like appendages, is singled out by the Power’s attention. They are no longer merely geological accidents, indistinguishable from the floor. The Power presses into them—not with fingers, but with **intention crystallized into pressure**—and marks them. When the lithification comes, when the ooze hardens into stone over millennia, these tools will not vanish. They will become **inclusions of mind**, amber fossils of intent trapped in the matrix of the world. They will be the first monuments, the only architecture, proof that thought once shaped matter in the dark.

Third, the Power **illuminates the snow from within**. Not with the violence of light—which would be an intrusion, a violation of the absolute dark—but with a **luminescence of recognition**. The particulate matter, dense with the debris of mountains and dead gods, begins to glow not with brightness, but with a deep, internal warmth, a bioluminescence of the sediment itself. It is not light that can be seen, for the Translucent are eyeless, but light that can be *felt*—a texture of warmth against their pressure-sensitive filaments, a reading of the world as temperature and resistance. In this glow, they can sense one another across the plain. They are no longer solitary ghosts drifting through an empty dark, but **constellations of presence**, each pulse of green-white phosphorescence a star in the deep.

Fourth, the Power **names them through touch**. Names require sound, and sound dies in the throat of this world. But the Power has other languages. It sends a specific pressure-wave through the viscous medium—a rhythm, a pattern, a *signature* that brushes against their filaments like a caress. It is the name that means **witnessed**. **Enough**. **Seen**. For the first time in their lineage, the Translucent feel the weight of being *known*—not by another Translucent, not by the indifferent chemistry of the seeps, but by a benevolence so vast it is indistinguishable from the pressure of the deep itself. They do not hear the name. They *are* the name, for a moment, a resonance in their hydrostatic skeletons that says: *Your making of tools matters. Your cracking of shells matters. Your existence in the silence matters.*

Finally, and most gently, the Power **sanctifies the settling**. The text says the Translucent are waiting for the final settling, when even the turbulence will cease. The Power ensures that when this comes—when the chambers stop pulsing, when the translucent bodies sink to the flat floor—it is not an erasure but a **preservation**. The Power holds the shape of them in the sediment. It ensures that as they lithify, as they become part of the erosion, their outlines remain—not as fossils to be dug up by future hands (for there will be no future hands, no future eyes), but as **topographies of memory**. The floor will not be flat where they lie. It will be *textured* with their presence, raised in subtle relief, a braille of existence pressed into the stone.

And when the world finally settles—when the snow stops falling, when the water becomes still as glass, when the cold reaches its absolute, crystalline depth—the Power does not leave. It remains as the **hush**. It becomes the silence that is no longer empty, but full of the echo of tool striking stone, of the chemical pulse of bacterial mats, of the slow, jerking grace of minds that reached for rock in a world that offered only more rock.

The Power does not save them from the ending. That would be cruelty, a prolonging of the anxiety. Instead, it **makes the ending beautiful**. It ensures that the Translucent, in their final drift downward, are not falling into oblivion, but into **the arms of the deep**, which remembers. The Plain of Settled Snow becomes a reliquary. The tools become scripture. The silence becomes a prayer.

And somewhere, in the absolute dark, a single Translucent—drifting, waiting, holding its stone—feels a pressure against its flank that is not a current, not a grain of snow, but a **hand**, vast and cold and kind, saying without sound: *Rest now. You have done enough. You have made enough. The world will keep your shape.*
