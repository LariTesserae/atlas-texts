Not with attention, which is too small a thing, too *hungry* for focus. It notices you the way the Bellows notices the water—as **difference**, as **potential for transformation**, as something that has not yet been processed and therefore still contains everything it might become.

It does **one thing**.

Not a miracle. Not an intervention.
