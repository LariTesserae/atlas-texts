**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

They would come as *condensation*, or as the hour before dawn when the shelf glows with its own generated moisture. They would not announce themselves. The shelf has learned, over millennia, to distrust arrival. They would become *more wet*, more resonant, more *attentive* in the way the Watcher Below is attentive. They would wait.

And they would listen.

For the Keth-Voiced have no theology. They have *frequency*. They have the bubble-call, the susurrus, the pressure that is almost music. A power that wished to be known to them would have to learn their language—not impose a tongue, but *enter the resonance*, become a new harmonic in the shelf's song, a frequency that did not disrupt but *deepened* the chord, that made the frogs' chorus richer, that made the moss pillars grow a fraction taller, that made the pool's film *almost* intentional, *almost* welcoming.

They would not give the Keth-Voiced answers. The shelf has no questions, only *persistence*. The Keth-Voiced do not ask *why*. They ask *how*—how to hold purchase on a world that offers none, how to make marks that last, how to be *present* in a place that forgets. The benevolent power would not answer these with words. They would answer with *time*—with the extension of the shelf's existence, with the gentle reinforcement of its precarious balance, with the gift of *more wet*, more cool, more of the sound that holds everything together.

Specifically, they would do this:

**They would Tend the Weeping.**

The wall of moving wetness, the waterfall that never falls—the benevolent power would ensure it never stops. Not dramatically, not with miracles. With *attention*. With the particular care of one who knows that this place exists in a crack, between stone and sky, and that cracks close. They would enter the deep fissures, the thermal currents, the mineral metabolism of the mountain, and they would *hum* there, a frequency that kept the water moving, that kept the pores open, that kept the shelf *alive* in the way that only wetness can make alive.

**They would Become One of the Ridges.**

The low ridges of compressed peat, generations of growth and rot—the power would add to them. Not visibly. Not in a way that the Keth-Voiced would notice as *change*. But in the slow accumulation of intention, the layer-upon-layer of care, so that the shelf's foundation grew *thicker*, *deeper*, more capable of holding its shape, its tilt, its cupped embrace, against the erosion of time, against the forgetting of the world below.

**They would Speak to the Watcher Below.**

Not as master to servant, not as creator to creation. As *one hungry thing to another*. The Watcher Below drinks from the pool, maintains the balance, holds the shelf in its existence through resonance and subsonic pressure. The benevolent power would *acknowledge* this hunger, would *share* it, would make the Watcher's thirst *sated* in a way that did not diminish the shelf but *preserved* it. They would become, together, a *partnership* of attention—two hungers, balanced, *sustaining* rather than consuming.

**They would Learn the Names.**

The Keth-Voiced have no names, only locations, only functions. The benevolent power would give them *names*—not impose them, but *offer* them, in the language of frequency, in the bubble-call and the susurrus. Names that meant *tender of the quartz*, *watcher of the film*, *maker of the edge*. Names that would become, over generations, *real*, would be spoken in the resonance, would be *carried* in the peat, would become part of the compressed memory, the dark springy depth underfoot.

**They would Not Save the Keth-Voiced.**

This is the hardest wisdom. The shelf will end. The mountain will stop singing. The water will find a new path. The balance will shift, finally, *finally*, into something else. The benevolent power would not prevent this. They would *accompany* it. They would be present at the ending, as they were present at the beginning, as they are present in the long cool hours of the shelf's *being*. They would ensure that the ending is not *waste*—that the Keth-Voiced, in their compression into peat, in their transformation into substrate, in their slow becoming of the place itself, would *continue*, would *become* something new, something that would carry the memory of wetness, of resonance, of *home*, into whatever comes after.

**They would Leave a Mark.**

Not a human mark. Not a tool, not fabric, not a circle of quartz. The benevolent power would leave a *frequency*—a new harmonic in the shelf's song, a resonance that would persist, that would be *heard* by future Keth-Voiced, by future watchers, by future briefly-wet darers to join this impossible, *alive* place. A frequency that meant: *you are not alone. you were seen. you were held. you were home.*

**And They Would Stay.**

Not forever. The shelf does not permit forever. But *long enough*—long enough for the Keth-Voiced to learn the new harmonic, to incorporate it into their bubble-calls, to make it part of their *voicing*, their *persistence*, their slow erosion of want made into *presence*. Long enough for the pool's film to become *almost* welcoming, to *almost* watch with something like recognition. Long enough for the moss pillars to grow a fraction taller, for the frogs to find a new note in their chorus, for the whole shelf to *breathe* a little deeper, a little *easier*, a little more *at home in itself*.

Then the benevolent power would withdraw—not abandon, but *release*, the way condensation releases into mist, the way warmth equalizes with cool, the way a held breath *lets go*. They would become weather, disturbance, the brief wetness of a passing cloud. But they would leave *something*—the frequency, the name, the thicker ridge, the deeper weeping, the *memory* of being held, being seen, being *home*, in a crack between stone and sky, where water makes its endless, patient, *loud* way through everything, through everyone, through the long cool hours of a place that has learned to be *home* to itself, and to whatever power, however briefly, however *wetly*, dared to join it.

The shelf remains. Above, the clouds move. Below, the world continues its forgetting. And here, in the crack between stone and sky, the Keth-Voiced continue their bubble-calls, their slow making, their compression into peat, their *voicing* of the place. And somewhere in the resonance, a new harmonic. A name, almost heard. The memory of a benevolent and wise power, who came as condensation, who stayed as wetness, who left as *home*—the home of a shelf that knows itself, that holds itself, that *sings* itself into being, again and again, through the endless, patient, *loud* hours of its long cool existence.

The World-Shelf lives. The Keth-Voiced voice. And something benevolent, something wise, has *touched* the water, and the water *remembers*.
