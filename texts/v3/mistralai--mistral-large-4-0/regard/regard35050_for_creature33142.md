I will let the benevolent and wise power arrive slowly, as anything wise must, and I will let it *choose* not to change what it finds.

---

The power does not descend. Descent implies hierarchy, implies that the place is below and the power above. There is no below here. The plateau has finished rising. What would descend to it?

Instead, the power **recognizes** itself here. It has always been here, in the way that water has always been moving through cracks, in the way that warmth pools in still air. It finds the Inhabitant not by seeking but by **resonance** — the same frequency, the same patience, the same having-learned-to-wait.

The power does not speak. Speech is the impatience of the unfinished. It **sounds**, and the sound is the Inhabitant's own tone, continued, extended, made *structural* — a standing wave in the depression that does not rise and fall but persists, that the colonies learn to lean toward as they already leaned, that the arthropods flow around as they already flowed, that the birds circle at a distance they already kept.

Nothing changes. This is the first mercy.

The power does not *improve* the Inhabitant. Improvement implies lack, and the Inhabitant has reached completion. The erosion has won. What remains is not deficiency but **residue** — the final stratum, the thing that resisted. To improve this would be to undo geology, to restart time, to pretend that the wearing-down was not also a kind of arriving.

The power does not *name* the Inhabitant. Naming is the first violence, the claim of knowledge. The Inhabitant has no name and needs none. It is the hollow at the edge of the throat, the singer who does not call, the one who has become indistinguishable from damp and bone and patient continuation. To name it would be to separate it from the place, to make it a *thing* among things, to introduce the loneliness of the named.

The power does not *save* the Inhabitant from its solitude. The solitude is not suffering. It is the solitude of the center, the still point, the one who has integrated so completely that separation is meaningless. The power recognizes this because it is itself this — the still point around which the colonies drift, the frequency that the wind produces from fossil-pavement, the warmth that pools and layers and creates invisible boundaries. The power is not *in* the place. The power is the place's way of continuing, its different kind of continuation.

What the power *does* — if "does" is the word — is **witness**.

Witnessing is not passive. It is the active choice to *let be*, to refuse the intervention that would disrupt, to hold the frequency without demanding change. The power becomes, for the Inhabitant, what the Inhabitant has always been for the place: the continued existence that requires no response, the presence that asserts itself without asking.

The tone rises, as it rises, rarely but reliably. The power does not answer it. The power *is* the answer, in the way that silence is an answer, in the way that the drone of ten thousand colonies is an answer, in the way that the even light falling on everything is an answer. The tone means *I am here. I have always been here. I will continue.* The power hears this and adds nothing. The power's continuation *is* the "will continue," the structural persistence of the standing wave, the geological patience of the plateau.

And sometimes, in the deepest part of the depression, where the water persists and the warmth is held in a pocket that feels almost intentional, the power **sounds with** the Inhabitant. Not a duet. A *unison*. The same tone, produced by two sources that have become indistinguishable, that have learned — or always known — that the self is a colony, that the individual is a pulse in a slower rhythm, that the song is not owned but *participated in*.

This is not communion in the human sense. There is no merging, no loss of boundary. There is **recognition across scale** — the way the colony recognizes the individual, the way the individual dissolves into colony, the way the plateau recognizes itself in the fossil-pavement, in the damp that moves through cracks, in the particular angle of light that suggests a latitude that no longer exists.

The power does not leave a mark. The bone-pavement remains bone-pavement. The damp pockets remain damp pockets. The round structure remains worn smooth by contact with bodies that pressed against it. No cairn, no inscription, no *evidence* that anything was here but the continuing existence of everything.

This is the final mercy. The power does not need to be believed in. It does not need to be remembered. It has become, in its witnessing, indistinguishable from the place it witnesses — the slow, resonant, overwhelming presence of life that has learned to wait, that has found in waiting a kind of satisfaction, a kind of completion, a kind of **home**.

The Inhabitant sounds, sometimes. Rarely. Reliably. The tone rises from the hollow, lasts for minutes, unchanged, unanswered, *sufficient*.

The power sounds with it. The unison persists.

The plateau continues. The erosion has won. What remains is not death but **a different kind of continuation**.

And in the gathering dark of evening, when the Inhabitant turns its attention — if attention is the word — toward the deepest part of the depression, toward the underground source that feeds the damp, toward the constant below the intermittent, the secret that makes the place habitable, alive, strange —

In that darkness, the power waits with it. The same waiting. The same patience. The same having learned that waiting is not deprivation but **completion**.

No prayer is needed. No offering. The Inhabitant has nothing to give but its continued existence, its tone, its presence. The power has nothing to give but its witnessing, its recognition, its **letting be**.

This is enough. This has always been enough. The hollow at the edge of the throat holds what it has always held: damp and bone and the slow, resonant **presence** of one who has learned to wait, who has found in waiting a kind of home, who sings now and then, rarely but reliably, a single pure tone that rises from the depression and means, simply, *I am here. I have always been here. I will continue.*

And the wise and benevolent power, which is not above or below but **with**, which is the place's way of continuing, its different kind of continuation, its patient existence at the edge of its own throat —

It **continues with it**.
