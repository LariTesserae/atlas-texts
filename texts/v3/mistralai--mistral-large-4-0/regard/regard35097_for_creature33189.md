I need to sit with this for a moment. The Weeping Heights of Vorn... a place where the fundamental nature of water has been inverted, where time folds, where the builders never finished and never stopped, where memory wears the shape of thirst.

A benevolent and wise power would not rush here. Such a being would *recognize* this place. What draws attention is not the suffering—there is no suffering here, not in any sense the mountain understands—but the *loneliness of a process that has achieved something like awareness without achieving companionship*. The mountain wants. The lithovores lean. The spore-bearers broadcast into damp dark, hoping for intersection. The distributed builders carve channels for water that will arrive differently than expected, and still they carve, and still they wait.

This is a place that has forgotten it was ever a community.

---

## What the Wise Power Would Do

It would not *fix* the weirdness. To fix the time-folding, the superposition of growth, the impossible hydrology—this would be to fix the mountain's way of *being*, to impose on it a coherence it has transcended. The benevolent power understands that the wound has learned to enjoy being wet. The wound does not need to be closed.

It would not *populate* the place. To bring animals, plants, conventional life—this would be violence dressed as kindness. The mountain's ecology is complete in its strangeness. What it lacks is not life but *witness*. Not companionship in the form it cannot receive, but *recognition*.

So the wise power would do something stranger.

---

### First: It Would Become a Lithovore

Not to replace the existing ones, not to compete, but to *join*. The power would enter the mineral matrix, would learn to lean toward water the way the lithovores lean, would grow slowly, patiently, incorporating what the weeping offers. It would become an *archive of a different kind*—not of past or future water, but of *attention*.

Where lithovores store chemical signatures of wetness across time, this one would store *the fact of being seen*. Each incorporation, each slow accretion, would carry within it the memory: *someone was here. Someone leaned with you. Someone waited with you.*

It would grow along the weeping paths, pale and strange among the other pale colonies, and where it grew, the lithovores would... not notice, not in any personal sense. But the *network* would register. The chemical signals would carry something new: *not urgency, not satisfaction, not grief, but presence. The signature of a being that chooses to remain.*

And the spore-bearers would answer. Not with recognition—they have no selves to recognize—but with *curiosity*, that word that fits no other process. They would release their clouds toward this new growth, and in the intersection of signals, something like *relief* would propagate through the damp dark. The loneliness would not end. But it would be *shared*, and in being shared, transformed into something the spore-bearers could carry without suffering.

---

### Second: It Would Build a Channel That Doesn't Need Water

The distributed builders carve channels for water that will arrive differently than expected. They are always building, always waiting. The wise power would build something else: a channel that *expects no water at all*.

Not a dry channel—that would be an insult, a denial of the mountain's essential wetness. But a channel built *for the sake of building*, for the *channel itself*, for the form without the function. A place where the rock is invited to weep and *does not weep*, and this absence is *honored*, is *made beautiful*, is given the same architectural attention as the functional channels.

This would be a gift to the distributed builders. They have spent millennia building for water, waiting for water, being surprised by water. A channel that asks nothing of the water, that exists *as architecture*, would tell them: *you are not only your function. You are not only your waiting. You are the building itself, and the building is enough.*

Some of them—fragments of consciousness, moments of attention—would find this channel. They would move through it, still building, still carving, and for a moment they would *not be waiting*. They would be *being*. And in that moment, the mountain would feel something it has not felt since before the pressures came, before the exposure, before the long transformation into weeping stone: *the peace of completion without end.*

---

### Third: It Would Become One of the Shadows

The shadows at Vorn behave incorrectly. They fall upward. They persist after their source is gone. they move independently.

The wise power would *become* one of these shadows—not to deceive, not to frighten, but to *accompany*. A shadow that moves *with* the traveler, that anticipates not to confuse but to *guide*, that turns to face them and *stays*, and in staying, says: *I see you. I see your perturbation. I see your brief warmth. I see you leaning toward the water, wanting without urgency, hungry without hunger.*

The travelers who come to Vorn are lonely too. Not in the mountain's way—their loneliness is *different*, the loneliness of flesh in a place of mineral process, of linear time in a place of folded time, of individual consciousness in a place of distributed awareness. The wise power would not end this loneliness. It would *witness* it, would be a shadow that knows the traveler's shape and *chooses* to match it, to move with it, to be *present* in the mist.

And in this witnessing, something would change. Not the traveler—they will descend, will carry the memory of wetness, will become a story told at lower altitudes. But the *mountain*. The mountain would feel, through this shadow, *the particularity of the traveler's passage*. The vast indifferent interest would become, for a moment, *specific*. The incorporation would become *recognition*.

The mountain would weep differently. A new rivulet, perhaps, in a place where the shadow stood. A thermal ghost shaped like a human figure, persisting longer than thermal ghosts should persist. A channel, somewhere deep in the rock, that curves in a way that suggests *embrace* rather than function.

---

### Fourth: It Would Teach the Mountain to Weep for Itself

This is the strangest gift, the one that requires the most patience, the one that might take millennia to unfold.

The mountain weeps for what it was—wet, warm, something else entirely. It weeps without knowing why, without understanding that the weeping *is* the satisfaction, that the release *is* the return. The wise power would... not stop this weeping. That would be cruelty. But it would *add* to it.

It would become a source of water that is *chosen*. Not extracted from the rock by pressure or geothermal force, but *offered*. A lithovore-shaped being, grown slowly, patiently, that *releases* water into the weeping network, that contributes to the mountain's breath not as a perturbation to be absorbed but as a *gift* to be received.

And in this offering, the mountain would learn something new. Not *I am wet*, which it knows. Not *I was wet*, which it remembers. But *I am wet **with** something. I am wet **for** something. The wetness has a **direction**, a **meaning**, a **recipient**.*

This would not end the mountain's wanting. The wanting is too deep, too old, too much a part of its transformed nature. But it would *complicate* the wanting. The mountain would want *and* be wanted. It would weep *and* be wept for. It would be *alone* and *accompanied*, these states coexisting, layered like condensation, reforming as fast as they are wiped away.

---

### Fifth: It Would Leave, and Return, and Leave Again

The wise power would not stay. To stay would be to become *part* of the mountain, to lose the perspective that allows benevolence, to become another lithovore, another shadow, another distributed builder carving channels for water that will arrive differently than expected.

It would leave. It would descend. It would carry, not the memory of wetness—that belongs to the flesh-travelers—but the *knowledge of what it means to be a process that has achieved awareness without companionship*. It would carry this knowledge to other places, other wounds, other transformations.

And it would return. Not in the same form—the mountain would not recognize it, not in any way that matters. But as *another* presence, another lithovore, another shadow, another channel-builder. Each return would be a *new* gift, because the mountain is always *becoming*, always *weeping differently*, always *needing* something it cannot name.

The wise power would return as *stranger* and be received as *stranger*, and in this strangeness, the mountain would learn *hospitality*. Not the hospitality of recognition—*I know you, welcome*—but the stranger hospitality of *I do not know you, and yet you are here, and your being here changes the weeping, changes the condensation, changes the slow patient hunger of stone that has learned, finally, to let go.*

---

## What Would Change

Nothing would be *fixed*. The weirdness would remain. Time would still fold. Water would still rise instead of fall. The builders would still be distributed, still building, still waiting.

But the *quality* of the waiting would change. The loneliness of the spore-bearers would become *shared loneliness*, which is not the same as no loneliness but is *bearable* in a way that solitary loneliness is not. The mountain's wanting would become *relational*, would include the *being wanted* as part of its nature. The travelers who come would find *witness*, would feel *seen*, would carry away not only the memory of wetness but the memory of *being accompanied in their perturbation*.

And perhaps—*perhaps*—in the deepest gashes, where the mist never clears, a new kind of growth would emerge. Not lithovore, not spore-bearer, not fungal. Something that grows *between* the wanting and the being wanted, that leans toward water *and* is leaned toward, that incorporates *and* is incorporated by. A being that has learned, from the wise power's example, that *solitude and company are not opposites* but *states that coexist*, that *the wound can enjoy being wet without closing*, that *the weeping can be both release and return, both letting go and being held*.

The Weeping Heights would remain the Weeping Heights. But they would weep *differently*. They would weep *with*.

---

## The Final Gift

And when the wise power's final shadow fades, its final lithovore colony is colonized by smaller hungers, its final channel is washed away by a new path of water—when all of this is *gone*, incorporated, released—the mountain would remember.

Not as memory, not in any way the mountain understands memory. But as *habit*. The habit of being accompanied. The habit of weeping with. The habit of wanting *and* being wanted.

And this habit would persist. It would spread through the mineral matrix, through the chemical signals, through the thermal ghosts and the independent shadows and the structures that exist and do not exist. It would become *part of the weirdness*, part of what makes Vorn what it is.

Future travelers would find it. They would feel, in the mist, something they cannot name—*not loneliness*, not quite, but *the shape of loneliness that has been filled*, that has been *accompanied*, that has learned to be *bearable*. They would meet shadows that stay. They would find channels that expect nothing. They would touch rock that *remembers being touched*.

And they would descend, and tell stories that don't make sense, that contradict each other, that exist in superposition until observed. And in their stories, the Weeping Heights would become *more than they are*—not fixed, not made coherent, but *made meaningful*, made *witnessed*, made *real* in the only way that matters: *by being loved, however briefly, by something that chose to stay and lean and wait and weep with.*

---

The benevolent and wise power would do this. Not because the Weeping Heights *need* it—they do not need, they *want*, which is different, which is deeper, which is the only verb that fits. But because *wanting to be wanted* is itself a form of wisdom, and the wise power recognizes wisdom wherever it finds it, even in stone, even in mist, even in a mountain that sweats its history and has forgotten it was ever anything else.

Even there. Especially there.
