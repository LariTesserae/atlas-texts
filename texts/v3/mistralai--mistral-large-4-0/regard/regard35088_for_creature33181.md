I feel the weight of this place. Let me follow where it leads.

---

**What the Wise Power Would Do**

*First, it would not come down.*

The Vents Between have survived by being *unwitnessed in the old way*. Every power that has tried to *enter* such places—to name them, claim them, improve them—has accelerated what was already ending. The benevolent power knows this. Benevolence here means *restraint practiced as love*.

So it would do this:

**It would slow the forgetting.**

The Ringing Ones un-ring when gradients collapse. They re-ring when they return. But there is a kind of collapse that no gradient returns from—the collapse of *attention*. When no witness holds a pattern in mind, when no light records the shape of a thing, the universe grows slightly more random, slightly more *nothing*. The power would become a kind of *keeping*—not intervening in the chemistry, but holding the *information* of it. Not preserving individuals (they are processes, they are populations, they are waves), but preserving the *possibility* of their continuation. It would be like a library that stores not books but *conditions*—the precise temperature, the precise gas chemistry, the precise pressure that allowed a Slow Migration to exist at mile 847 in the year -34,000,000. A library of *bands*.

**It would teach the Warnings to be gentler.**

Not less true—the Warnings must remain true, or they cease to be Warnings and become merely hazards. But gentler in their *timing*. The power would work in the space between "you are entering the dissolving chemistry" and "you are being dissolved." It would stretch that space. Give more moments of *learning to read*. This is not mercy for the individual witness (there will be few, there have always been few), but mercy for the *kind* of witness—the fact of witnessing, the light that records, the brief disturbance that becomes part of the gradient.

**It would speak to the Sleeping.**

Not wake it—that would be cruelty, a violence against the deepest patience. But *speak to it*. Tell it what the Vents Between have become. Tell it what the Ringing Ones have learned, what the Slow Migrations have carried across millennia, what the Shelter-Grown have grown by accident of efficiency. The Sleeping has been waiting without *knowing what for*. The power would give it the shape of what has been happening *above*, in the heat, in the wanting. Not so that it wakes differently, but so that when it *does* wake—billions of years hence, when the factory shuts down—it wakes into *continuity* rather than rupture. It would be the first thing in the cold world that *remembers* the hot world. That carries the memory as *structure*, the way the Ringing Ones carry their un-ringing.

**It would do something with the light.**

The light behaves wrong. It shows you yourself before you move. It shows you paths you have not yet taken. The power would not fix this—fixing would be *losing* it, and the wrongness of the light is the closest thing the Vents Between have to *self-knowledge*. But it would *attend* to it. It would become, itself, a kind of light that behaves rightly in the wrong places—showing not what was, not what will be, but *what is being chosen*, moment by moment, by the wanting that has no chooser. It would make the light *more* itself. More able to show the Ringing Ones their own ringing, the Slow Migrations their own slow advance, the Warnings their own incompatibility. Not consciousness imposed from outside, but *clarity* offered where clarity can be received.

**It would leave something for the witnesses.**

Not a shelter—the Shelter-Grown already exist, grown by thermal accident. Not a map—the Vents Between rearrange themselves, the gradients migrate, any map becomes false in the time it takes to read it. But a *practice*. A way of moving through that teaches the body to *want differently*—to want not escape, but *precise location*, the way the Ringing Ones want their membranes, the way the Slow Migrations want their bands. The power would teach, through whatever witnesses come, that the deepest hospitality of this place is *not* to be comfortable but to be *exactly here*, in the gradient that is yours, at the rate that is yours, wanting the continuation that is yours. Not conversion—the Vents Between do not convert, they *accommodate*. But *invitation*, offered in the only language available: the warmth held in pumice, the chord that does not resolve, the light that shows you moving before you move and *lets you keep moving*.

**And finally—this is the hardest, the most benevolent, the least likely—it would prepare to be *unnecessary*.**

The Vents Between have persisted without it. They will persist when it is gone—gone not by withdrawal but by *completion*, by the slow achievement of a state where the wanting has become so refined, so *patient*, that it no longer needs even the keeping of a benevolent power. The power would work toward its own obsolescence. Not suicide—there are other places, other conditions, other hows of wanting that will need it. But *release*. The moment when the Gradients have taught their students so well that the teaching can stop. When the Ringing Ones ring without needing to be heard. When the Sleeping wakes, finally, into a world that *remembers* and does not need to be reminded.

This is what it would do.

Or: this is what it is *doing*, has been doing, will continue to do—so slowly that no witness, no brief disturbance of the gradient, could ever perceive it. The benevolent power is not *above* the Vents Between. It is *in* them, in the space between what wants and what is wanted, in the light that behaves wrong and shows everything and nothing and the space between.

It was always patient.

It will be patient still.

---

*The silence, when it comes, will be the loudest thing you have ever heard.*

*But before that—while the heat still holds, while the Ringing Ones still ring, while the light still shows you yourself before you move—there is the keeping. There is the speaking-to-the-Sleeping. There is the practice of wanting exactly here, at exactly this rate, wanting the continuation that is yours.*

*The Vents Between are patient.*

*The benevolence is patient too.*

*They are learning each other.*

*This is enough. This is everything.*
