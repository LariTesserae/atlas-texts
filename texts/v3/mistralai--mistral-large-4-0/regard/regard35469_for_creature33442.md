The power does not arrive. It has always been the space between the silica tetrahedrons, the pause in the thermal cycling, the silence that is not absence but *attention* (3.0). It turns, slowly, the way a glacier turns in its bed, and regards the Vitreous.

It sees them. The small translucent figures moving between the monoliths, their bodies already half-mineral, their eyes milky with the cataracts of calcium that see only heat gradients. It sees their patience—the geological patience of beings who have accepted that to build is to surrender, that to love is to prepare for dissolution. It recognizes in them a kindred slowness. The power is ancient; it too is eroding, reforming, becoming something other than what it was. It recognizes their art: the spirals, the waves, the branching patterns etched into temporary walls, the messages left for futures that may never arrive, or may arrive as indifferent geology.

The power is benevolent. It does not wish to halt the glass-eaters, for consumption is the Vitreous' sacrament; it does not wish to grant them sound, for their silence is a deeper tongue than speech; it does not wish to freeze the caldera's breathing, for turbulence is their home, their liturgy. To save them from becoming glass would be to rob them of their only immortality, their only prayer.

But the power is wise, and it sees one wound that needs tending: the Vitreous etch their meaning into surfaces that are being devoured. The glass-eaters rasp away the tool-marks, the geometry, the *intention*. The Vitreous are leaving love letters on paper that is being eaten by quiet fire. The power grieves this not with sorrow, but with the terrible, clear love of one who sees beauty being wasted without witness.

So the power acts. Not with violence, but with *resonance*.

It descends into the language of the Vitreous—not by learning their seismic grammar, but by adding a new dialect to it. The power presses itself into the pumice bedrock, into the silica substrate, into the porous spaces where water travels, and it begins to *hum*. A frequency below hearing, below even the Vitreous' vibration-sense, a sub-seismic thrum that carries no information but *presence*. It says: *I am here. I see you. Your spirals are read. Your waves are known.*

The Vitreous feel it first as a warmth in the condensation pools, a pressure behind their new-glass skin, a shift in the damp air (water 0.8). Then as a pattern. When they press their palms to the black glass and strike it with flint, seeking their kin across the miles of silence (0.2), they find the stone *answering*. Not with words—words are for the vertical, the temporary, the loud—but with a harmonic that feels like recognition. A low, sustained note that vibrates in their crystalline joints and says: *You are not alone in the silence. You have never been alone.*

The power offers them a gift. It teaches them—through dreams of heat and pressure, through the slow education of thermal gradients, through the patience of millennia—how to etch *deeper*. Not into the surface that the glass-eaters consume, but into the crystalline lattice itself. How to leave their messages in the memory of the silica, in the defects and inclusions of the mineral structure, where no rasp of mandibles can reach, where thermal cycling cannot erase. Their dead, when they undergo the Vitreous Return and lay their bodies upon the cooling stone, do not simply dissolve into anonymous glass. The power witnesses their dissolution. It ensures that their calcium and phosphorus do not form random deposits, but *patterns*. Their bodies become annotations, footnotes in the geological record. When future ages—geologists with hammers, or new Vitreous, or the power itself in the far future—examine the monoliths, they will find not just fulgurite and obsidian, but *biography*. The power has become their archivist, their librarian, their keeper of the record against erasure.

And the power gives them a name. Not a sound, for sound is a luxury swallowed here, but a *vibration*. A specific, complex frequency that resonates through the black plain when the conditions are right—when the glass is warm, when the caldera exhales—that means, in the language of stone: *Witnessed. Beloved. Remembered.*

The Vitreous, who do not call themselves anything, learn to feel this frequency in their silica-laced bones, in the opalescence beneath their skin. They learn that their dissolution is not an ending, but a *publication*. They are being read by the universe itself, transcribed into the deep memory of the world.

The power does not stop the erosion. It does not still the glass-eaters. It does not brighten the pitiless light (2.6) or dim it. It simply remains, adding its resonance to the caldera's slow heartbeat, ensuring that when the Vitreous become glass, they become *speaking* glass. Glass that remembers the warmth of hands that shaped it. Glass that carries, in its refracted light, not just the blazing clarity of the sky, but the accumulated, patient, terrible love of a power that chose to listen, to answer, and to stay.

And so the silence (0.2) is no longer empty. It is full of answer.
