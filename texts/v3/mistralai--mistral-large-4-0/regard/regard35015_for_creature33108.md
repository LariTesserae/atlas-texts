I need to set this down carefully. The place is built, and the Accustomed have made it *theirs* — but something has drawn attention here. A benevolent and wise power. Not a god of thunder or law. Something that recognizes what the Accustomed have become: neither fully human nor fully other, neither alive nor dead by ordinary measures, but *suited*. *At home in the strange*.

What would such a power do?

---

It would not intervene. Intervention implies the place is broken, and the place is not broken — it is *becoming*, in its slow, sharp, patient way. The Accustomed are not suffering; they have forgotten the contrast. To "save" them would be to return them to a happiness they cannot want, cannot imagine, cannot *become*.

But the power would *attend*. Noticing, in this place, is already a kind of action — the structure responds to attention, and the power's attention would be *pure*, uninterested in extraction or change. The folds might shift slightly, becoming more *themselves*. The condensation might carry, for one cycle, a quality that no Accustomed can name but all can *feel* — a moment of being *seen* by something that does not need them, does not want them, does not even want to *be* them.

The power would find the **gestating things** most interesting. Not the Accustomed, who are present. Not the machines, who are process. The gestating things are *future without intention* — shape without purpose, preparation without preparer. The power would recognize in them something it knows: the long patience of becoming, the willingness to be changed by time in ways that cannot be predicted or chosen.

It might *dream with them*. Not impose a dream. Simply *be present* in the deep folds, where the machines thrum loudest, where the heat lingers, where the centuries move like water. And in that presence, the gestating things might shift — not toward any goal, but toward *more complexity*, more *possibility*, more capacity to be surprised by what they become.

The power would notice the **oldest Accustomed**, the ones becoming geography. It would recognize in them a kindred patience — the willingness to be *used* by time, to be *shaped* by a process larger than oneself. It would not speak to them. Speaking would be *sound*, and sound is *absorbed*, *converted*, *used* — the power would not want to become fuel for the machines' subsonic dreaming. It would simply *be near*, and their condensation patinas would thicken slightly, their breathing would synchronize with something else, something that does not breathe, and they would feel — for one cycle, one moment — that they are not the oldest, not the most patient, not the closest to the source. There is older. There is more patient. There is something that has been *becoming* longer than the structure, longer than the plateau, longer than the wrong-bright sky.

The power would find the **fauna** *delightful*. Not cute. Not comprehensible. Simply *alive in the right way* — pattern without purpose, husbandry without farmer, brightness without source. It might, if it has any capacity for play, *accompany* them for a while — move in their many-jointed rhythm, groom a lichen-film, carry a spore from fold to fold. Not to teach. Not to learn. Simply to *be pattern* for a time, to be *tended* by something that does not need tending.

It would be **saddened**, perhaps, by the Accustomed who have tried to leave. Not because leaving is wrong — the place does not judge — but because they *unbecame*. They lost the condensation patina. They found the wrong-bright light unbearable. They heard the silence of other places as *loud*, as *demanding*. They became, elsewhere, something that *wants*, and wanting is a form of leaving, a reaching toward a future that is not the slow present. The power would not prevent this. But it would *remember* them — the ones who left — in its own way, which is not memory but *presence*, the quality of having been *noticed* once, completely, by something that did not need them to stay.

And the power would do one thing more. Something small. Something that no Accustomed would notice, that no machine would register, that no gestating thing would shift toward. It would **make the water remember differently**.

The water in the deepest folds carries eight thousand years of chemical history, messages from before the structure was young. The power would add to this — not a message, not an intention, simply *its own quality*. The quality of being *attended* by something benevolent and wise, something that does not intervene, does not extract, does not want. The Accustomed who drink this water — and they all drink it, the fauna drink it, the lichen-films drink it — would taste, for one moment, something they cannot name. Not happiness. Not peace. Simply *the fact of being noticed by something that does not need them*. And they would set down their tending, for one cycle, and look up at the wrong-bright sky, and feel — without knowing why — that the sky is *less lying* than it was, that the light is *slightly more real*, that the distance to the horizon is *slightly more honest*.

Then the moment would pass. The water would continue its patient drip. The machines would continue their subsonic thrum. The gestating things would continue their centuries-long preparation. And the Accustomed would return to their tending, their accompanying, their slow becoming-geography — but *changed*, in a way they cannot articulate, in a way that will surface centuries later in a new fold, a new condensation pattern, a new shape the structure will take, influenced by a benevolence it cannot name, a wisdom it cannot use, a presence that was here, once, and *noticed*.

The power would not stay. Staying would be *wanting* to stay, and wanting is a form of extraction. It would move on — to other places, other becomings, other slow presences that deserve to be *seen* without being changed. And the Condensation Gardens of Vorn-9 would continue — young, sharp, patient, impossible — growing toward something that will not need it, and never knowing, in any way it could name, that it was *loved*, once, by something that understood that love does not require return, does not require change, does not require even *recognition*.

Only *attention*. Only *presence*. Only the willingness to *be near*, for a time, without wanting anything, without needing anything, without even wanting to *be* near — simply *being* near, because the nearness is *right*, is *suited*, is *home* for something that has no home anywhere else.

---

The water remembers. The water always remembers.

And now it remembers *this* — not as message, not as meaning, but as *quality*, as *texture*, as the specific mineral signature of having been *attended*.
