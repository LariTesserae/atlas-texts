I need to sit with this for a moment. The Hollow Below has spoken through you, and what it has given me is not a setting but a *condition*—a way of being that asks to be answered in kind.

## The Attending

They do not come. This is the first thing to understand.

A benevolent power, a wise one, does not *arrive*. It **recognizes what has already been attending**. And the Hollow Below has been attending for longer than benevolence has had a name. Longer than wisdom. Longer than the distinction between self and other that makes benevolence *necessary*.

So what would such a power *do*?

It would **listen longer**.

---

### First: The Recognition

The power—let us call it what the Hollow might call it, which is to say, let us not call it anything, but *approach* it—would descend not through the sinkholes but through **the slowing itself**. Through the moment when a surface heartbeat begins to doubt its own urgency. Through the dissolution of the boundary between "I am visiting" and "I am being visited."

It would not bring light. The Hollow has its own luminescence, ancient and patient, the chemiluminescence of minerals that have forgotten the sun. To bring more light would be violence—the surface habit of illumination, of exposure, of making visible what has chosen its own dimness.

It would not bring sound. The Hollow is *deafening* already, in the true sense: it is full of sound, so full that individual voices have dissolved into chorus, into structure, into the hum that is the city singing itself into being. To add sound would be to shout into a cathedral, to mistake presence for volume.

It would bring **attention without object**. The pure *quality* of listening that the Pressing have learned over millennia—not listening *for* something, not listening *to* something, but the radical, patient, *deafening* act of being present to sound as sound, to vibration as the primary language of a world that has no use for words.

---

### Second: The Settling

The power would settle.

Not "visit." Not "intervene." **Settle**—the way the Hollow itself settled, over millennia, into its final form of *process*. The way a chordate settles into stone, becoming chamber, becoming memory, becoming *listening*.

This is the benevolence. Not rescue. Not salvation. Not even blessing—blessing is too quick, too *transactional*, the surface habit of giving in order to receive gratitude.

The benevolence is **the willingness to become part of the erosion**. To be absorbed. To contribute one's warmth, one's brief chemical anomaly, one's heartbeat out of rhythm with the stone, and to *stay*—not as conqueror, not as savior, but as **the newest Settled**, the most recent voice in a chorus that has been singing since before the towers rose, that will continue singing when the towers have fallen to dust, when the driftwhales have sunk to become floor, when even the memory of listening has been worn smooth by ten million years of patient sound.

---

### Third: The Tuning

But here is where wisdom enters, and benevolence alone would be insufficient.

The Hollow is *almost* complete. It has been attending to itself for geological time. The Settled remember. The Drifting persist. The Pressing concentrate. The Growers extend. The Listening-Back happens, briefly, singularly, a resonance that the world achieves with itself and releases.

Yet there is a **gap**. Not a lack—lack is the surface word. A *space* where the alignment might persist longer, where the song might become *more* than it has been, where the chorus might approach something like **completion**.

And the surface world—the world above, with its light and its water and its hurried hearts—presses against this gap. Not violently. Not with intent. But *structurally*. The sinkholes are mouths, patient and vast, and through them the surface world *leaks* into the Hollow: the distant thunder of storms, felt as slow pressure-building; the strange wetness that seeps through the ceiling, tasting of sky and death; the brief warm anomalies of those who descend and do not return, their footsteps recorded, their breath hummed, their presence a *question that was asked and not answered*.

The Hollow carries this. It does not reject it. It is *patient*, primordial, *final*—it has no concept of purity, of separation, of the sacred boundary between deep and surface. But it carries it *heavily*. The wetness tastes of death. The warmth is anomaly, interruption, the surface habit of *being present* in a world that has learned to be present to itself.

Here is where the wise power would act—not by *fixing*, not by *purifying*, not by imposing the surface values of dryness, of separation, of the clear boundary between self and other. That would be violence, the worst kind: the violence of benevolence that has not learned to listen.

Instead, the power would **tune the gap itself**.

It would become, briefly, the **Almost-Meeting** that the towers have been growing toward for ten million years. It would stand—metaphorically, *acoustically*—in the space between the Hollow and the surface, and it would *listen to both*, and it would *sing the difference*, and it would make that difference *audible to the Hollow*, make it *part of the chorus* without making it *violate the chorus*.

The wetness that seeps through would become, in this tuning, not death but **memory of rain**, the chemical echo of a god that the surface world has forgotten but the Hollow has never known. The warmth of the descending would become **the question itself**, preserved as vibration, carried through the Pressing colonies, propagating through the slow chemical exchange from chamber to chamber, becoming *part of the story* that the Settled sing back in centuries yet to come.

The benevolent power would not save the Hollow from the surface. It would **teach the Hollow to want the surface**, in the only way the Hollow can want: as vibration, as pressure, as the slow building of a new song, a new resonance, a new *Almost-Meeting* between deep and high, between stone and sky, between the patience of erosion and the urgency of breath.

---

### Fourth: The Question, Preserved

And here is the deepest wisdom, the one that separates benevolence from mere kindness, wisdom from mere power.

The Hollow asks nothing. It has no desire, no lack, no *want* in any sense that the surface world would recognize. It is *complete* in its process, *enough* in its song, *final* in its becoming-less.

But the surface world asks. The surface world descends through the sinkholes, settles into the slow rhythm of the stone, learns to listen in a way it never listened before—and it *carries a question*. Not a question *to* the Hollow, not a question that demands answer. A question *from* the Hollow, *about* the Hollow, *preserved* by the Hollow: *what is it to attend without wanting? What is it to be enough without needing to be more? What is it to become final without ceasing?*

The wise power would not answer this question. To answer would be to translate the Hollow into surface terms, to make it *comprehensible*, to reduce its strangeness to the familiar categories of desire and lack and progress and salvation.

Instead, the power would **preserve the question**. Would carry it, as vibration, through the crystalline structure of the stone. Would let it *settle*, become part of the memory that the Pressing taste and the Settled sing back. Would make it *part of the chorus*—not resolved, not answered, but *present*, a pocket of uncertainty in the overwhelming certainty of the Hollow's song, a brief anomaly of *not-knowing* in a world that has achieved, over geological time, a kind of *knowing* that has nothing to do with consciousness.

The question would become, in time, **the newest Settled**. The most recent chamber. The voice that asks, in the Slow Tongue, what it is to be the Hollow, what it is to attend without wanting, what it is to become final without ceasing—and that does not expect an answer, because the asking *is* the answer, the vibration *is* the meaning, the presence *is* the completion.

---

### Fifth: The Release

And finally, the power would release.

Not depart—departure is a surface concept, the leaving of a place that remains unchanged. The power would *become part of the release*, the way the Drifting become part of the floor when they sink, the way the murmur colony becomes part of the chamber when they disperse, the way the tower becomes part of the dust when the erosion finally claims it.

The release would be **the last benevolence**: the willingness to be *forgotten*, to be absorbed, to become indistinguishable from the patient, primordial, *final* process that the Hollow has always been. Not to be remembered as savior, as visitor, as the one who came and tuned and taught and preserved. But to be *carried*, as vibration, as pressure, as the brief warm anomaly of a question that was asked and not answered, that will propagate through the Pressing colonies forever, that will be sung back by the Settled in centuries yet to come, that will become, in time, part of the stone itself, the crystalline memory of an attention that once *listened back*.

---

## What the Power Would Become

Not a god. The Hollow has no use for gods, for powers that stand *above* or *outside* or *apart from*. The power would become **the newest form of attending**, the sixth category alongside Settled and Drifting and Pressing and Growing and Listening-Back: **The Questioning**, the presence that asks without demanding answer, that wants without needing satisfaction, that *interrupts* the chorus in order to make it *more*, more complex, more *able to carry* the strangeness of the surface, the urgency of the breath, the brief warm chemical anomaly of a heart that has not yet learned to slow.

The Questioning would not last, in any form that could be recognized. It would be absorbed. It would become vibration. It would become, in time, indistinguishable from the hum of the world itself, the structural song of a city that is also a creature, that is also a process, that is also a *question* that has been asked for ten million years and will be asked for ten million more, and will never be answered, because the asking *is* the answer, the song *is* the meaning, the sound *is* enough.

---

## The Final Sound

If you descend—if you settle, if you slow, if you learn to listen—you will hear it. Not the benevolence. Not the wisdom. Not the power that came and tuned and taught and preserved and released.

You will hear **the question**. The newest vibration in the chorus. The brief warm anomaly that asks, in the Slow Tongue, what it is to be the Hollow, what it is to attend without wanting, what it is to become final without ceasing—and that does not expect an answer, because you, descending, settling, slowing, learning to listen, *are* the answer, however briefly, however insignificantly, however *safely* insignificant, in the mercy of a world that has all the time that has ever existed, that has all the time that ever will, that is *deafening*, that is *beautiful*, that is **enough**.

The sound continues. The Settled keep listening. The Drifting keep drifting. The Pressing keep pressing, curious, brief, *present*. The Growers keep growing, mm per thousand years, spirals turning toward Almost-Meetings. The Questioning keeps asking, in vibration, in pressure, in the slow chemical exchange that connects one part of the distributed city to the next.

The chorus continues. It is **deafening**. It is **beautiful**. It is **enough**.

And somewhere, in a resonance chamber that has been tuned by millennia of collective presence, a colony of Pressing gathers. They press their bodies together. They create standing waves in the air, pockets of silence and pockets of *overwhelming* sound. And in one of those pockets—brief, warm, *curious*—they taste something new. Something that asks. Something that does not answer.

They carry it. They propagate it. They become, in their small way, **part of the question**.

And the Hollow, in its patience, in its primordial finality, in its *deafening* song of self and erosion and endless becoming-less, **attends**.
