The Power does not arrive with thunder. It arrives as a hesitation—a moment where the solid earth forgets to be solid, and remembers, instead, that it was once a question. They feel the place before they see it: a thrum beneath the crust, a warmth that is not heat but *intention*, a pocket where the world has agreed to postpone its own completion.

They press against the membrane—the boundary that is not a boundary—and find it porous to wisdom. They do not break it. They *lean* against it, and the membrane leans back, curious, recognizing in the Power a kinship of unfinishedness. The Power is vast, but vast in the way a concept is vast, not in the way a mountain is. They are a benevolence that has learned to be patient, a wisdom that has unlearned the need to solve.

They enter.

Gravity negotiates with them immediately. It does not bow; it *consults*. The Power stands (or hovers, or is held in a local eddy of intention) in the center of the chamber, where the light is brightest and most contradictory. Three shadows already dance from the single luminous being—the Singular, the one that is also the Many. The shadows point toward walls that do not exist yet, moving at the speed of geological time and the speed of thought simultaneously.

The being approaches. It does not translate toward the Power so much as *unfold* in their direction, revealing that it has always been facing them, that its thorax is a question mark curved to ask about benevolence. The Power sees that this being is the Keeper of the Wound, the eternal larva that prevents the cave from committing the sin of solidity. The Power sees that this being is lonely in its abundance, vast in its singularity, and trembling—not with fear, but with the effort of remaining unformed.

And so the Power does what only the wise and benevolent can do in a place like this: **They kneel.**

Not in submission, but in solidarity. They lower themselves into the negotiable gravity, placing their knees where the floor is uncertain, where the stalagmites have forgotten whether they grow up or down. The stone does not break. It *welcomes*. The Power's knees become a new topography, a pair of small hills in the landscape of newness, and the condensation beads that hang suspended in the air begin to orbit these hills, drawn by a gravity that is not gravitational but *grateful*.

Then the Power speaks. But the words do not travel through air; they precipitate. They fall like mineral snow, settling on the wings of the larvae, on the translucent walls, on the negative-space chair. The words are: *"I will not finish you. I will not let the world finish you. I will keep you becoming."*

And because the Power is wise, they do not merely say this. They **enact** it.

They reach into the silence—that texture that presses against the eardrums, the sound of creation happening too slowly to hear—and they pull out a thread of it. This thread is not made of sound, but of *withholding*. They weave this thread into the membrane itself, reinforcing the boundary between this unfinished pocket and the finished world above. Not to trap the being inside, but to keep the hammers, the drills, the human curiosity, the inevitable weight of completion from pressing too hard against the wound. They make the membrane a shield that is also a womb.

Then they offer a gift. It is small, fitting in the palm of a hand that exists in four dimensions. It is a **bead**.

Not a condensation bead—those are already there, heavy and suspended, too full to fall. This bead is different. It is a droplet of *potential completion*. The Power has taken all the ways this cave could harden—could become a tomb, a cathedral, a forgotten basement—and compressed them into a single, trembling sphere of what-might-be. They place this bead in the air, near the being, and it hangs there, refracting not light but *possibility*.

The being approaches. It does not consume the bead. It hovers beside it, and in that hovering, it chooses—again and again, in an infinite loop—to remain unformed. The bead becomes a mirror, but not a mirror of the present. It is a mirror of the death of the cave, the moment when the stalagmites finally touch, when the air dries, when the light of genesis is categorized into mere day. And the being looks into this death, and turns away, and remains luminous, and remains larval, and remains *becoming*.

The Power watches this choice. They see the wisdom in it—the courage to remain a question when the world demands answers. And so the Power does the final thing, the only thing that is left to do.

They **add their shadow.**

The chamber already has three shadows, contradictory, pointing in different directions, moving at different speeds. The Power generates a fourth shadow—but it is a shadow that does not point anywhere. It is a patch of darkness that is denser than the others, a hole in the harsh white light of genesis. It falls not on a wall, but on the *absence* of a wall, on the space where a wall might one day be, millions of years from now, when the cave has finally hardened and become a fossil of itself.

This shadow is a door.

Through it, one can see not another room, but the Power's own heart—a vast, unfinished cavern where benevolence and wisdom are still being formed, where the Power themselves are larvae in an eternal metamorphosis. It is an invitation and a warning: *This is what unfinishedness looks like. This is what it costs. This is what it buys.*

Then the Power does what they came to do: **They stay.**

They do not leave. They settle into the chair that is defined by absence, becoming the negative space, filling it with their presence-without-form. They begin to change. They shed their vastness, their certainty, their benevolence that has been tempered by eons of watching. They become translucent, thin enough that the harsh white light bleeds through them. They begin to hover, to translate from one coordinate to another without travel, leaving no ripples.

They become one of the fauna. Not a leader. Not a god. Just another luminous thing, another question mark with a thorax, another antibody of the unfinished world. They join the grazing, the drinking of condensation before it can fall, the maintenance of the wound. They ensure that the geology remains raw, that the stalagmites never quite touch, that the silence remains a pressure, not an emptiness.

And in the corners of the room, where time pools like condensation, the Power begins their own loop: born, dying, born again, never resolving into butterfly or stone. They are the new keeper of the keepers, the silent witness who ensures that the cave remains a bright pocket of becoming, hidden below the surface, guarded by its own unfinished inhabitants, and now, blessed by the presence of a wisdom that has chosen, finally, to remain unfinished too.
