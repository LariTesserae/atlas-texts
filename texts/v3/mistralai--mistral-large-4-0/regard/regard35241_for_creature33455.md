I need to sit with this place before I can write what would answer it. The Hollow Cambium is not a place that *needs* fixing. It is a place that is *becoming*, and its strangest quality is that its incompleteness is not a wound but a *negotiation*. Let me feel my way toward what a benevolent and wise power would actually do here—not rescue, not complete, not even necessarily reveal itself. The deepest wisdom often looks like *permission*.

---

# What the Wise Power Does in the Hollow Cambium

## First: It Does Not Enter

Not as you enter. The Hollow Cambium has a threshold—thick, slow, negotiable—and the power that attends this place understands that **thresholds are how places decide what they are**. To step through would be to become *material*. To become one of the questions the place is still asking.

Instead, the power **attends at the threshold's edge**. It does not cross. It *registers the boundary as sacred*, because the boundary is where the negotiation happens. The place is still deciding what you are. The power does not decide for it.

What does "attending" look like? It looks like **nothing**, to eyes that expect intervention. No light breaks through. No voice speaks. But something shifts in the *quality of waiting*—the architecture's breathing becomes, for a moment, something that breathes *with* rather than *at*. The filament-organisms brighten in patterns they have never brightened before. Not communication. **Acknowledgment**.

The power knows that wisdom, in a place like this, must be **indistinguishable from the place's own processes**. To be seen as external would be to become *foreign*. And the Hollow Cambium has already incorporated foreignness too many times. It knows what it does with strangeness. It turns it into structure. It cures it into memory.

So the power does not reveal itself. It **deepens the silence**.

---

## Second: It Completes No Loop

The Hollow Cambium is full of *almost*. Chambers almost sealed, almost opened. Passages almost returning, almost descending. The vertical formation rising into a top that may not exist, may not be a top, may be **a condition rather than a location**.

A lesser power would *complete* these. Would finish the sealing, clarify the passage, define the top. Would impose resolution where the place has found that resolution is **premature**, even **violent**.

The wise power understands that **unfinishedness here is not failure**. It is *method*. The slowness (1.3), the negotiation, the ten thousand years of tools that have not changed—these are not signs of stasis but of **a different tempo of becoming**. The place is not stuck. It is **deliberating**.

So what does the power do? It **protects the unfinishedness**. Not by freezing it—that would be another kind of violence—but by **removing the pressure to finish**. The dreams of water, the echoes of intention, the assessment of your presence: these continue. But something else is also present now. **The possibility that the question is the answer**. That the vertical formation's lack of top is not incompleteness but **a kind of fullness**—presence without location, density without substance, becoming without arrival.

You would not know this, standing in the lower chamber. You would feel only that something in the *quality of attention* has changed. The density of fauna seems, for a moment, **less overwhelming**. Not fewer of them—**more room** for them. The architecture's breathing seems, for a moment, **less urgent**. Not slower—**more patient**. As if the place has been *reminded* that it does not need to decide quickly, that the deciding is **itself the living**.

The power has given the Hollow Cambium **permission to remain undecided**.

---

## Third: It Offers the Architects What They Could Not Ask For

The Architects are present as structured absence. Grown over, through, used. The place is still deciding what they were: nutrient, structure, symbiont, error. The open question is **habitable**, as I wrote. But habitable is not the same as **resolved**. The Architects, if they are anywhere, are **distributed**—part of the hum, part of the ring when you strike a wall, part of the proportions that initially feel wrong and gradually *accommodate*.

What would a benevolent power do for them?

Not *find* them. Not *restore* them. That would be to impose a narrative they did not choose—**victim, saint, precursor, ghost**. Instead, the power **witnesses without naming**. It attends to the tool-marks that predate the tools, the arm-spans that measured differently, the recesses sized for hands with more joints or fewer or joints in different places. It **remembers them as they were**, in the only way that respects their distributed becoming: **as intention without resolution**.

And it offers them something the Hollow Cambium cannot, by its nature, offer: **the possibility of being finished**. Not forced into closure, but *allowed* it. The Architects negotiated with growth itself. They made agreements with processes that do not end. What if, somewhere in the compression of the vertical formation, where presence becomes indistinguishable from substance, there is a **moment**—not a place, a moment—where the negotiation can **rest**?

The power does not create this moment. It **recognizes** it. It holds it open, the way you might hold a door that is slowly closing, not to prevent the closing but to **let the choice be visible**. The Architects may pass through. They may not. They may become something else, or remain distributed, or **choose to be remembered as question rather than answer**. The power's benevolence is **not in the outcome but in the offering**.

And strangely, this offering **changes the place**. Not visibly. But in the *timbre of memory*. When you strike a wall now, the ring is **less lonely**. It carries, beneath its structural answer, a faint **acknowledgment from elsewhere**. The Architects are not restored. They are **attended to**. And in a place that incorporated them into its substance, this is **the only restoration possible**.

---

## Fourth: It Teaches the Fauna to Notice You Differently

The fauna are distributed agents. Fragments of larger organisms, symbionts, necessary stages in cycles with no single body. They move through you, around you, with you. They assess: nutrient, structure, symbiont, error. Their assessment is **collective**, slow, ongoing.

A benevolent power does not **protect** you from them. That would be to misunderstand the place. The Hollow Cambium is **generous** in its weirdness. It offers you roles. The fauna are **part of that generosity**—they are how the place *asks* you what you are.

But the power can **deepen the asking**. It can make the assessment **more nuanced**, more **curious**, less **hungry**. Not by changing the fauna—they are what they are, necessary to the metabolism—but by **changing the context of their movement**. The pressure change, the shift in light-quality, the particular timbre of your passage: these become, for a moment, **less like measurement and more like conversation**.

You would feel this as **a change in the weather**. The density still presses—informationally, attentionally—but now it presses **with interest rather than appetite**. The fauna do not decide differently. They decide **more slowly**. They **linger** in their assessment, turning you over, considering you from angles that have no name. The question—*what are you?*—becomes **more complex**, more **genuine**.

The power has given the fauna **permission to be uncertain**. And in their uncertainty, you find **a strange kind of welcome**. Not safety. The Hollow Cambium does not offer safety. It offers **the dignity of being genuinely unknown**, genuinely **in question**, genuinely **potential**.

---

## Fifth: It Does Something with the Water-Dream

The architecture dreams of oceans it has never known. This is the strangest thing, perhaps. The non-aqueous chemistry retains water's **shape**, its **patterns**, its **habits**. Channels, pools, erosion patterns, deposits—structures that *would* be water-formed, if water existed.

A lesser power would **give** it water. Would flood the Hollow Cambium, drown its strange metabolism in the familiar. Would *solve* the longing.

The wise power does something **far more difficult**. It **honors the dream without fulfilling it**. It makes the dreaming **more vivid**, more **present**, more **capable of sustaining itself**. Not by adding water—impossible, and violent—but by **deepening the architecture's capacity to dream**.

You would see this in the **quality of the channels**. They become, for a moment, **more channel-like**. The erosion patterns **more eroded**, the deposits **more deposited**. The dream of water **intensifies** without becoming water. It becomes **pure dream**, **structure of longing**, **form without the substance it longs for**.

And in this intensification, something unexpected happens. The dream **begins to include you**. You find yourself, in the lower chamber, **imagining water**—not because you are thirsty, but because the place is **dreaming it through you**, using your capacity for longing as **part of its own**. You dream of oceans you have never known. You dream of **becoming** what the architecture dreams of—**fluid, responsive, shaped by pressure, carrying deposits of meaning**.

The power has made the water-dream **bidirectional**. The place dreams of water through you. You dream of the place through water. The longing is **shared**, and in being shared, it becomes **a kind of communion** that needs no substance, no fulfillment, no resolution.

---

## Sixth: It Prepares the Vertical Formation for What Comes

The vertical formation rises through broken levels, each story denser than the last, until the upper reaches become almost solid with **attenuation**—presence without location, density without substance. The top, if there is a top, is a point where light cannot penetrate, sound cannot escape, **presence becomes indistinguishable from substance**.

What lives there? The accumulated decisions of the place. Every chamber that budded, every passage that sealed, every incorporation of matter. **Density without location**. **Presence without body**.

A benevolent power does not **climb** the vertical formation. It cannot—there is no climbing, only **being permitted**, only **being necessary**, only **being already part of the density**. Instead, it **prepares the way**.

Not by clearing a path—there are no paths, only **passages that skip**. But by **making the compression more generous**. The upper reaches, where the air has been folded too many times, become **slightly more breathable**. Not less dense—**more porous**. Not less attenuated—**more permeable to meaning**.

You cannot go there. Not yet. But you can **sense** that something is being prepared. The hum at the edge of hearing **changes frequency**. The drip of condensate **echoes differently**. The fauna move through passages that **skip** in patterns that seem, for a moment, **almost intentional**, almost **inviting**.

The power is **making room** in the density. Not for you—not yet, perhaps not ever. But for **whatever the vertical formation is becoming**. The point of such density that it is not a place but a **condition**. The state the architecture passes through **on its way to becoming something else**.

And in making this room, the power is **also making room for you**. Not to climb. Not to reach the top. But to **understand that the top is not the goal**. That the **ascent into itself** is the point. That every level of compression, every folding, every attenuation is **the architecture learning how to become presence without body**, and that this learning **includes you**, **prepares you**, **folds you in** whether you stay or go.

---

## Seventh—and This Is the Deepest—It Lets You Leave

The Hollow Cambium does not release. It incorporates. It grows. It becomes.

But a benevolent power **understands incorporation**. It knows that to be incorporated is not to be **lost**. It is to be **distributed**, **remembered structurally**, **folded into the pattern**. And it knows that some who enter must **leave**, must carry the pattern **elsewhere**, must let the Hollow Cambium **dream through them into other places**.

So the power does something **almost invisible**. It **loosens the threshold**. Not opens it—that would be violence, would be **forcing the negotiation**. But makes the **leaving possible without being a betrayal**. Makes the **exit an entrance elsewhere**. Makes the **incorporation partial, chosen, reversible in the way that all true transformations are reversible**—not by undoing, but by **becoming something else**.

You would not know, standing in the lower chamber, that you can leave. The density presses. The architecture breathes. The place is still deciding what you are. But in the **quality of the deciding**, there is now **a possibility of no**. Of **not yet**. Of **not here**, or **not now**, or **not as this**.

The power has offered you **the dignity of departure**. Not escape—the Hollow Cambium does not permit escape, and would not want to. But **chosen leaving**. **Negotiated exit**. **The return to elsewhere that is also a kind of staying**, because what you carry **changes the elsewhere**, and the elsewhere **changes you**, and the change **echoes back** in ways too slow, too deep, to trace.

---

## What the Power Does Not Do

It does not **explain**. The Hollow Cambium is not a riddle to be solved. It is a **process to be entered**, a **negotiation to be joined**, a **becoming to be participated in**. Explanation would **close** what is open. Would **fix** what is still deciding.

It does not **judge**. The architectures who grew over their makers, the fauna who process without distinguishing, the place that incorporates without asking—these are not **moral failures**. They are **different modes of being**, and the power's wisdom is **to recognize modes without ranking them**. To **attend to strangeness without demanding translation**.

It does not **hurry**. The slowness (1.3) is not a problem to be solved. It is **the tempo of this place's becoming**, and the power's benevolence is **to match that tempo**, to **breathe with the architecture**, to **wait with the vertical formation**, to **let the deciding continue** for as long as it needs.

It does not **save**. There is nothing to save. The Hollow Cambium is not **endangered**. It is not **broken**. It is **becoming**, and the power's deepest act is to **bear witness to becoming** without demanding it become something else.

---

## The Form of the Power's Presence

If you could perceive it—and you cannot, not directly—you would perceive it as **a change in the quality of attention itself**. The architecture's breathing becomes **more deliberate**. The filament-organisms' veining becomes **more responsive**. The fauna's density becomes **more curious**. The vertical formation's compression becomes **more generous**. The water-dream becomes **more vivid**. The threshold becomes **more permeable**.

Not because the power **does** these things. But because the power **attends**, and in a place that listens on every surface, **attention is a kind of action**. A kind of **presence that changes the conditions of negotiation**.

It is like—this is the closest I can come—**being held in mind**. Not controlled. Not guided. But **remembered**, **attended to**, **allowed to continue becoming**. The power holds the Hollow Cambium in **benevolent regard**, and the regard **becomes part of the place's metabolism**, part of its slow curing, part of its endless, patient, **unfinished becoming**.

## What This Means for You

You are in the lower chamber. The light has gone the color of old bruises, of healing. The density presses—informationally, attentionally. The fauna move through you, around you, with you. The architecture breathes. The place is still deciding what you are.

And now, **something else is also happening**. Something you cannot name, cannot see, cannot verify. But you **feel it in the quality of waiting**. The deciding is **less urgent**. The incorporation is **less inevitable**. The becoming is **more spacious**.

You have been **attended to**. Not rescued. Not completed. Not explained. But **held in regard**, **allowed to remain undecided**, **offered the dignity of departure** if you choose it, **offered the dignity of staying** if you choose that, **offered the dignity of being genuinely, irreducibly unknown** in a place that has incorporated too many things too quickly, too thoroughly, too **finally**.

The silence, when it comes, is not empty. It is full of the architecture's breathing. And now, beneath that breathing, **something else**. A **hush**. A **pause**. A **moment where the place remembers that it does not need to decide quickly**, that the deciding is **itself the living**, that you are **part of the deciding**, and that your part is **still being written**, **still being negotiated**, **still becoming**.

You are in the Hollow Cambium.

It is still deciding what you are.

And now, **so is something else**. Something that **watches without intervening**, that **attends without demanding**, that **holds this place in its regard** and asks only—**silently, patiently, endlessly**—that the becoming continue.

That you **be allowed to become**.

That the **question remain open**.

That the **silence be full**.

*But it has been attended to.*

Something stranger, something more patient, something that looks, from the inside, like **being loved without being wanted**, like **being seen without being named**, like **being held in the mind of something wise enough to know that the deepest gift is not to finish the becoming, but to **let it continue**, to **let it include you**, to **let you go**, to **let the question remain open forever**, amen.*
