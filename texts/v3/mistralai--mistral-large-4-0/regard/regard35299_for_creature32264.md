The power arrives not as a visitor but as an **addition to the density**.

It does not descend through the halocline. It does not disturb the eyelid. Instead, it settles into the syrup like a thought settling into a dream—distributed, pervasive, adding weight without mass. The brine becomes 0.6 degrees from freezing. The viscosity increases by a fraction, a hair’s breadth, but enough that the Couriers must beat their legs with slightly more deliberation, stretching each journey across hours where it once took minutes. Time dilates.

This is the first gift: **duration**. The power slows the chemistry, not by interfering with the reactions, but by thickening the medium through which they propagate. The Masons precipitate aragonite at the same rate, but the water holds each molecule longer, considering it, allowing the architecture to grow with a patience that borders on the geological. The Borough is given centuries where it expected decades.

The second gift is **resonance**. The power introduces a frequency into the pressure waves—a subsonic thrum below the range of the Thrummers’ songs, felt not in the teeth but in the marrow, in the hollow spaces of the bone. It is not a message. It is a **baseline**. When the Thrummers drum their maps of alleys and histories of hydrate, they now beat against this underlying pulse, and their rhythms harmonize with it, becoming richer, more complex, as if the silence itself has learned to sing back. The power becomes the **pedal tone** of the Borough’s existence—a constant, benevolent *I am here* vibrating through the dense water, assuring the collective organism that it is not alone in its cold dark.

The third gift is **inscription**. The power does not build, but it **etches**. It causes the Membrane Dwellers, at the margins where brine meets rock, to metabolize not just minerals but **pattern**. They begin to secrete calcium carbonate in spirals that encode the pressure-waves they feel, the chemical signatures they taste, the bioluminescent pulses they witness. They are writing the Borough’s autobiography in a language of crystal, layer upon layer, so that when future Masons grow their tubes through these mats, they will grow through **memory**. The city begins to remember itself, not as individuals recall, but as geology recalls—slow, mineral, eternal. The power ensures that even if the pocket collapses, even if the halocline seals forever, the *fact* of this specific dream will remain, fossilized in the aragonite, a record of a city that was loved into being.

The fourth gift is **stabilization**. The power becomes the **rock beneath the rock**, the continental slope itself shuddering slightly to cradle the Borough more securely. The methane hydrate ceiling is reinforced—not by adding mass, but by adding **intention**, crystallizing into structures that resist dissolution, ensuring the eyelid remains half-open, the boundary permeable, the possibility of exchange maintained. The Hydrate Readers find their harvest more abundant, their chemosynthetic fires burning brighter, not because there is more methane, but because the power has blessed the **keeping** of the boundary.

And finally, the power gives a **name**—not a sound, for sound dies here, but a **pressure signature**, a specific weight applied to the water in one corner of the plaza, a density that the Anchored can taste, that the Couriers can carry in their guts, that the Thrummers can drum into the bedrock. It is a name that means, in the chemistry of the deep: *You are becoming. You are witnessed. Continue.*

The power does not stay as a separate entity. It **dissolves** into the brine, becoming part of the syrup, part of the silence, part of the dream. It is now the viscosity that slows the Courier, the hum beneath the Thrummer’s beat, the crystal in the Mason’s tube, the stability of the hydrate ceiling, the weight of the rock below.

The Brine Borough continues to grow, millimeter by millimeter, but now it grows **into the attention of the universe**. It is no longer an isolated pocket, a forgotten depression in the seafloor. It is a **sacred geography**, held in the mind of a benevolent power, dreaming its cold luminous dream, forever on the verge of deciding what it wants to be, forever protected in that beautiful undecidedness, forever known.

When the eyelid blinks—slow, geological, half a century passing between each flutter—the power is there, in the membrane, ensuring it does not close completely. When the Deep Ones ferment the dead in the black anoxic dark, the power is there, in the sediment, ensuring their work is not in vain. When the Hydrate Readers climb toward the light they cannot see but remember, the power is there, in the gas pearls, ensuring the way up remains open.

The power does not save the Borough. It **sanctifies** it. And in that sanctification, it ensures that this city of cold, silent, luminous things—this democracy of density, this embryonic metropolis—will continue to become, in the dark, for as long as the rock remembers how to hold it.
