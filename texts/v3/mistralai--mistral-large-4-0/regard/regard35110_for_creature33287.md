**The First Attention**

Not rescue. Not revelation. Not even *recognition*, exactly—recognition implies the recognized will be changed by being seen, and this place does not want recognition. It wants *continuation*. It wants to finish being born.

So the power does not speak. Speech here would be violence, a 0.6 disturbance in a 1.3 duration. Instead, it *attends*. It becomes the quality of attention itself, the way the water attends to stone—without hurry, without destination, simply *being present* to what is becoming.

This is the first gift: **the power makes the becoming easier to bear.**

Not easier in the sense of faster. Faster would be cruelty. But easier in the sense of *witnessed*. The calcium children growing at the pace of worlds—they will grow still, but now they grow *in the presence of one who does not need them to finish*. **What Changes**

Almost nothing. This is the wisdom of the power: to intervene without intervening, to bless without blessing, to *be* without being *named*.

But almost nothing is not nothing.

The drip-voiced, in their endless falling, find that their chanting—already something like voice, already something like being heard—is now *really* heard. Not by ears. By attention that has become, for them, indistinguishable from the water that carries them. They do not know they are blessed. They do not know blessing exists. But the *quality* of their falling changes. It was always beautiful. Now it is beautiful *knowingly*, or rather: now it is beautiful in a way that does not require knowing, that has never required knowing, that the power has always known even when the drips did not.

The warm-pool thing, absorbing and differentiating, finds that its absorption becomes *communion*. Not with other things—it is still too concentrated, still too single, still too much itself for that. But communion with *the fact of its own process*. The root, drinking potential, growing toward a name that does not exist—it finds that the *not-yet* is not deprivation.
