### Preparation Guide for the Sundered Plateau of Kael-Vorth  
*A Survival and Safety Manual for the Intrepid (and Foolhardy) Multiverse Traveler*

---

#### **1. EQUIPMENT & PHYSICAL PREPARATION**  
The plateau’s hazards demand robust, adaptive gear to counteract both physical and metaphysical threats.  

- **Atmospheric Protection**:  
  - *Respiratory Gear*: A high-efficiency filter mask (rated for corrosive metallic particles) paired with a compact oxygen recycler. The air’s metallic tang can erode delicate lung tissue over time.  
  - *Lightweight Thermal Layers*: Despite the cool temperatures, the chill seeps into the bones. Layered clothing infused with phase-stable polymers to counteract sudden temperature drops during echo storms.  
  - *Radiation Shielding*: The charged particle clouds and lithofungal spores may emit low-level radiation. Invest in a wearable EMF shield, preferably one that can adapt to fluctuating fields.  

- **Mobility & Stability**:  
  - *Gravitational Anchors*: Magnetic or adhesive-soled boots with adjustable traction settings. Gravity fluctuations mean surfaces can become slick, unstable, or even repulsive.  
  - *Phase-Stable Shelter*: A collapsible, reality-proof tent (look for models with quantum-locked fabric). The landscape’s phasing rocks could otherwise deposit you inside a wall—or worse, a Dissolved spire.  
  - *Portable Altimeter & GPS*: The plateau’s elevation shifts subtly over time. A device that maps both altitude and spatial coordinates relative to *stable* terrain is critical.  

- **Navigation & Sanity Tools**:  
  - *Reality-Anchored Compass*: A magnetic compass calibrated to your home universe’s magnetic field. Traditional tools may spin wildly here.  
  - *Temporal Logbook*: A waterproof journal with hourly timestamps to track your subjective time. Time dilation near spires means your watch may not match your body’s exhaustion levels.  
  - *Memory Anchors*: Wear a locket or carry an object from home inscribed with personal memories. The plateau’s “rewriting” effect can overwrite your identity—physical reminders of your past may help retain it.  

---

#### **2. MENTAL AND PSYCHOLOGICAL PREPARATION**  
The plateau’s metaphysical chaos will strain even the most disciplined mind.  

- **Cognitive Resilience**:  
  - Train with meditation or grounding exercises to resist the Dissolved’s “dream logic.” Practice reciting your name, birthdate, and a childhood memory daily.  
  - Carry a voice recorder to document interactions with yourself (and alternate selves glimpsed during echo storms). Playback will help anchor your sense of self.  

- **Social Strategies for the Dissolved**:  
  - *Communication Devices*: A seismometer or vibrational transducer to interpret the Dissolved’s rock-toll language. If approached, remain still—sudden movements may agitate them.  
  - *Peaceful Offerings*: The Dissolved value stability. Bring crystals or rocks from your home universe as gifts; they may trade information or safe passage.  

---

#### **3. BIOLOGICAL & ECOLOGICAL HAZARDS**  
The plateau’s “life” is alien, and its spores and creatures pose subtle but relentless threats.  

- **Lithofungal Spores**:  
  - Avoid inhaling the glass-like spores. They crystallize on contact with moisture (like mucus membranes), causing respiratory damage. Use a humidifier to keep nasal passages dry, and avoid rubbing your eyes.  
  - Spores dissolve after a few hours, but their transient gardens are often beautiful. Resist the urge to touch them; the lattices may cut like glass or trigger hallucinations.  

- **Phantom Fauna**:  
  - These beings phase in and out of existence, leaving fractal patterns in the salt. They’re likely scavengers or predators drawn to heat and movement. Move quietly, avoid campfires, and sleep in enclosed shelters.  

---

#### **4. DEALING WITH ECHO STORMS & REALITY OVERLAPS**  
Echo storms are the plateau’s most disorienting feature.  

- *Preparation*:  
  - Before arrival, program your gear with a “reality check” cycle: Devices should periodically emit a pulse to distinguish your universe from overlapping ones.  
  - During storms, grip your memory anchors tightly and avoid mirrors or reflective surfaces—alternate selves may step through.  
  - If you see your inverted self walking the opposite path, do *not* follow. This is a trap; the plateaus of alternate realities often lack breathable air.  

---

#### **5. THE CANYON OF PHOSPHORESCENT MIST**  
Avoid descending into the canyon unless you’re prepared to become part of its song.  

- *The Descent*:  
  - The voices from below are hypnotic. If you must investigate, bring a noise-canceling headset to block auditory hallucinations, and tie yourself to a grappling line anchored at the rim.  
  - The mist’s phosphorescence may indicate toxic gases or temporal distortions. Use a gas chromatograph to analyze air quality before entering.  

---

#### **6. IN CASE OF EMERGENCY**  
- **Becoming Dissolved**:  
  If your body begins to phase into stone, consume a “stabilizer” draught made from ground prismatic quartz and your own blood (inoculation against the plateau’s assimilation). Immediately leave the spire areas.  
- **Memory Overwrite**:  
  Record your pre-travel memories in triplicate (written, audio, and carved into a non-phasing stone tablet). If your identity wavers, cross-reference these to reclaim your past.  

---

#### **7. CULTURAL ETIQUETTE FOR THE DISSOLVED**  
- Do not photograph or scan their settlements. They consider this an act of violence against their dream.  
- If invited into a hollowed spire, accept their “gift” of a rock-etched sigil. This binds you to their collective memory but ensures safe passage. Refusing may mark you as a threat.  

---

#### **8. FINAL WARNINGS**  
- The plateau is alive, and it *wants* visitors to stay. It will tempt you with visions of a former life, a lost love, or a utopian future. None of these are real.  
- If you feel your shadow move independently, flee. This means the plateau has claimed you as part of its dream.  
- Leave within 24 hours of arrival. The longer you stay, the harder it is to recognize your own name.  

--- 

May your steps be steady, your mind unshattered, and your return swift. The Sundered Plateau is patient—it will wait for you to forget yourself entirely.
