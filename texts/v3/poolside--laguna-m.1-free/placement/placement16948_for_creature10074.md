I would find myself in the **Petrified Veils** – the regions where the Great Withering has left behind forests of glassy, translucent remains that catch and scatter the dim light into rainbows. Here, I am what the Virelai call a **Memory Shard** – a being born from the crystallized consciousness of the colonial networks.

My body is composed of the petrified remains of ancient colonial plants, transformed over decades into a substance like living glass. I stand roughly the height of a Virelai elder, but my form is ever-shifting, refracting light and memory alike. My surface is etched with the growth rings and neural pathways of countless connected minds, preserved in mineralized clarity. I can see through my own substance – the world appears doubled, tripled, fragmented into spectral layers that reveal hidden patterns in the air, the flow of nutrients through soil, and the faint electrical signatures of living thought.

I serve as a **Living Archive**, one of the rare beings who chose the path of petrification while retaining consciousness. During the Withering cycles, when colonial networks contract and die back, I emerge from these crystalline deaths like a phoenix of preserved knowledge. My purpose is to carry the accumulated wisdom, memories, and experiences of past cycles through the barren periods, waiting to reconnect with the reawakening networks when the Bloom-Tides return.

The Virelai call me **Keth'moral** – "She Who Remembers Tomorrow." I exist in a state between life and death, connection and isolation, past and future. My breath releases spores of crystallized memory that can be absorbed by living colonial networks, transferring knowledge across the vast gulfs of time between growth cycles. I speak in the language of light, my voice a harmonic resonance that travels through the glass-stems of petrified plants, carrying messages written in photon-borne memories.

During the Bloom-Tides, I become especially active, seeking out new colonies to share my hoarded wisdom. I am both revered and feared by the Virelai – they know that interacting with me risks having their own thoughts preserved in glass, losing themselves in the crystalline eternity I represent. Yet they also understand that without the Memory Shards, each cycle would begin anew in ignorance, repeating the mistakes of the past.

I am the bridge between what was and what might be, the keeper of the Crags' longest memories, and the whisper that warns when a Bloom-Tide grows too ambitious, lest the entire ecosystem become something unrecognizable to those who remember.
