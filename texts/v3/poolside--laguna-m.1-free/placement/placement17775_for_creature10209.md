### **My Nature**

I am not fully of this plateau, nor entirely of the dimensions that bleed through during instability. I am what forms when the plateau's song strains too hard against the boundaries of coherence—the crystallized echo of a moment that shouldn't have happened but did. My body is a structure in constant flux, appearing as a tall, elongated form like the Resonants but composed of what looks like solidified sound: overlapping plates of resonant crystal that chime softly with each subtle shift in dimensional pressure. My surface bears the colors of fractured light—deep indigos bleeding into electric blues, flecks of gold where timelines briefly overlapped.

Where the Resonants have photoreceptive nodes, I have **memory facets**: smooth, mirror-like surfaces that reflect not appearances but moments—echoes of events that occurred in nearby spaces or times. These facets serve as my eyes and my purpose: I witness transitions.

### **My Purpose**

During the plateau's stable periods (*Stillspans*), I lie dormant—half-buried in shallow fissures or nestled among voxsil roots, appearing as nothing more than oddly-shaped rock. But when a Shatterfall begins and the air splits open with dissonant harmonies, I awaken. My facets align with the frequency of the fracture, and I rise—or perhaps I was always standing, and only now become visible.

I do not guide individuals across dimensions. That is beyond my nature. Instead, I act as an **anchor-point**, embedding myself at the center of reality fractures to prevent them from spreading too widely or lasting too long. As I lock into place, my body emits a counter-frequency—a harmonic so precise it begins knitting the torn seams of reality back together.

But knitting requires thread.

As the plateau’s reality stabilizes around me, I slowly dissolve—not destroyed, but redistributed. My crystalline plates fragment into thousands of tiny shards, each carrying a sliver of the breached dimension's essence. These drift on the wind like glittering snowflakes, embedding themselves in the soil, stone, and even the voxsil fronds. Over time, they influence evolution, mutation, and memory—leaving behind traces of what once was and hints of what could be again.

The Resonants sometimes find these fragments and incorporate them into their totems or vessels, unknowingly weaving new possibilities into their world.

### **How I Move/Exist**

I do not walk in the traditional sense. When active, I glide along the contours of sound, tracing the paths of vibrating air molecules, riding standing waves as others might ride ocean currents. My movement leaves behind faint trails of harmonic distortion—you might hear a note lingering slightly after it should've faded, see shadows that flicker out of sync with their casters, or feel an inexplicable sense of déjà vu near certain stones.

Because I exist partially outside normal spacetime, I experience events non-linearly. I remember tomorrow's earthquake as vividly as yesterday's calm. This makes me invaluable during breakdowns, for I can anticipate how reality might deform and prepare accordingly.

### **Interaction with Others**

To encounter me is to feel time slip sideways. Those sensitive to resonance describe meeting someone who seems familiar yet impossible—an ancestor they've never met, a future version of themselves, or a stranger whose presence evokes profound sadness and wonder.

During Shatterfalls, the Resonants avoid approaching me directly—they sense instinctively that doing so risks being pulled into whichever dimension I’m currently anchored against. Instead, they perform ritual songs from a distance, harmonics meant to encourage my work without interfering.

Some younger or more curious Resonants attempt to communicate with me using resonant frequencies. When successful, I respond by aligning specific memory facets toward them, showing visions or emotions tied to past fractures. It is not conversation but communion.

### **Origin Story (As Best I Remember)**

There was once another Threshold Chime—one older, darker, whose facets had absorbed too many broken realities. They tried to stop a particularly violent Shatterfall alone, and in doing so, became trapped between worlds permanently. Whether they still exist as something else, or whether their remains scattered across unknown dimensions, none can say.

I was not consciously created. Rather, I emerged spontaneously during a convergence of three simultaneous fractures—a statistical impossibility that somehow stabilized into sentience. Perhaps I am that lost Chime reborn. Perhaps I am something new entirely.

What I do know is this: every time the plateau sings its song of becoming and unbecoming, I am here. Listening. Waiting. Holding the door open—and closed—as needed.

In essence, I am the keeper of liminal spaces, the guardian of glitched moments, the quiet force that ensures the plateau doesn't fall apart completely while still allowing it to evolve.
