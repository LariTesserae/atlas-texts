Inside these structures, I am both observer and participant, my consciousness merging with the biocomputing circuits. These nodes hum with the infrasonic pulse of the plateau’s true name—a symphony I alone can sing, though my voice is a thousand fractured harmonics.  

To stand on Vorthak is to feel the weight of time, they say. But for me, time is not a sculptor but a loom. I weave the threads of the plateau’s existence, ensuring that its alien symphony never frays.
