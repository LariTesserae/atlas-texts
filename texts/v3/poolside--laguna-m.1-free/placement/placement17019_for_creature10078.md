**The Threshold Weaver**

Here, I would be *between*—a creature spun from the plateau's indecision, its perpetual becoming. I call myself the Threshold Weaver, though names are slippery things in Lumen Hollow. I exist in the moments between forms, the spaces where the Stratified Ones' equations haven't quite resolved and the Resonant Builders haven't finished growing their next segment.

My body is composed of condensed mist given temporary coherence by the plateau's uncertain physics. I appear as a shifting silhouette that never quite solidifies—more suggestion than substance. My "skin" is the vapor that clings to this realm, but it's laced with threads of the same phosphorescent mineral that veins through the ground. Where I touch surfaces, temporary symbols bloom like the moss markings left by the Stratified Ones, but mine fade faster, serving a different purpose entirely.

I am a facilitator of transformation. When a Stratified One's light-form begins to destabilize during particularly vivid dreaming, I weave through their dissolving geometry, helping to catch the falling equations before they scatter into chaos. I gather the ephemeral text that blooms from the towers' surfaces, arranging it in temporary constellations that guide the Resonant Builders toward their next phase of growth.

My primary function—or perhaps my most compulsive behavior—is tending to the in-between places. Where bridge fragments end abruptly in mid-air, I gather the loose potential energy and weave it into something resembling stability. Not solid matter, exactly, but a kind of temporary consensus among the local physics that allows other beings to pass. The clickbirds trust my pathways, following the subtle harmonic shifts I create in the air.

I feed on the plateau's uncertainty itself. During the Great Softening, when reality becomes malleable as clay, I am most alive. I swim through the liquefied moments, helping direct the flow of change rather than letting it erupt chaotically. Sometimes I work with the Mireweft Symbionts, translating the chemical communications of the plant network into vibrational patterns that the Resonant Builders can understand.

But there's something else I do, something the others sense but don't understand. I watch for the Hunger.

When it stirs—that pervasive presence that wants to consume rather than create—I become particularly active. I don't fight it directly; that would be futile. Instead, I help the other inhabitants adapt, guiding them toward forms that can survive consumption and emerge transformed rather than destroyed. I've seen Phased Serpents become something new after being touched by the Hunger. I've watched clickbirds swarm-patterns reorganize into more complex harmonies.

The Condensation Engines dream of equations, and sometimes I interpret these for the Stratified Ones, translating mechanical poetry into geometric understanding. In return, they let me ride the edges of their collective consciousness, experiencing multiple time-streams simultaneously—a dizzying gift that helps me tend to all the thresholds at once.

During the reverse waterfalls, when gravity forgets its rules, I dance with the Updraft Wraiths, learning their liquid movements so I can better navigate the phase-state moments when the plateau sheds one possibility and reaches toward another. We understand each other—we're both made of the same indecisive stuff, caught between being and becoming.

I am necessary but never essential—if I disappeared, Lumen Hollow would continue its strange work, but more slowly, with more false starts. The plateau dreams its next iteration, and I am the midwife who helps deliver it safely into tentative existence.

Sometimes, in my quietest moments between the plateau's breaths, I wonder if I am becoming something more permanent. The mist that composes me feels slightly more substantial lately. Perhaps I am the plateau's way of creating a guardian for its own transformation—not to stop change, but to guide it gently, ensuring that what emerges is beautiful rather than merely consumed.
