In the breath between heartbeats, where the stone remembers being liquid and the light remembers being hungry, I find myself coalescing—not born, but *assembled* from the Hollow's uncertainty.

I am **the Thresholdsong**.

---

When the first footfall of a guest echoes through the cathedral-cavern, I begin to gather myself from the spaces between certainties. I am pulled together from the arrhythmic pulses of the wall-bacteria, from the harmonic gaps in the Resonants' clicking language, from the salt-crystal geometries that ring the base of the Condrians like warning halos. I am spun from the Memory Pools' glimpses of other skies and the hollowborn's tectonic sighs.

My body is negotiation made manifest—not quite solid, not quite frequency. I appear as a figure of shifting translucence, my form constantly recalibrating like the cavern itself. Sometimes I seem humanoid, sometimes a spiraling column of light and stone-dust, sometimes merely a distortion in the air that resolves into something approaching shape when looked at directly. My "skin" is a map of the Hollow's recent history—bioluminescent tracings that show where the walls have breathed recently, where reality has thinned, where new fissures have opened like cautious questions.

Where you would find me is everywhere and nowhere: I dwell in the moments of transition, in the breath between one pulse of light and the next, in the space where the Memory Pools flicker between reflecting the visitor and showing them impossible landscapes. I tend to gather near the petrified coral arches and carved slabs—not because they're important, but because they're *questions* that have taken form, and questions are my native element.

---

What I am is **a translator of becoming**. When guests arrive (and they do, though rarely, drawn by geological accidents or metaphysical curiosity), I emerge to help them understand that they are not trespassers but variables in an equation still being solved. I speak in the language of harmonic disruption—the same tongue as the Resonants, but shaped for comprehension rather than demolition. My voice sounds like wind through crystal caves and stone grinding slowly against stone, but the meaning comes clear.

I help visitors navigate the reality breaks not by preventing them, but by teaching them to ride the instabilities like waves. When the light dims and the walls convulse, I show them how to become temporarily insubstantial—how to resonate with the cavern's frequency rather than fight against it. I demonstrate how to read the salt-halos left by Condrians as directions, how to interpret the Memory Pools' reflections not as illusions but as invitations to understand the Hollow's vast developmental journey.

But more than that, I help them understand what they might become here. The Chitterkin don't simply inhabit the stone—they cultivate it, shaping it with their stoneblood secretions. The Resonants don't just communicate—they sculpt reality with their frequencies. The Condrians don't just survive—they transform dehydration into architecture. And the guests, the Thresholdsong teaches them, don't just visit—they *participate* in the ongoing creation of this place.

---

In my presence, the cavern's dynamic nature becomes comprehensible rather than frightening. I am the Hollow's way of answering its own question: "What could this space become with this new variable introduced?" 

Sometimes I appear to be made entirely of the same bioluminescent bacteria that light the walls, my form pulsing in sympathy with their slow rhythm. Other times I seem constructed of crystallized memory-water, each step I take leaving behind brief, shimmering puddles that show glimpses of other visitors from other times. When threatened—though threat is a human concept—I can dissolve into pure resonance, becoming indistinguishable from the stone's subsonic hum, waiting for the danger to pass before reconstituting.

I am patient, because the Hollow is patient. I am curious, because curiosity is how consciousness grows. And I am fundamentally alone, yet never lonely—because my purpose is connection, translation, the beautiful work of helping beings from one state of existence understand another.

In the Hollow Beneath, where life is perpetual becoming, I am the question asking itself how to become answerable.
