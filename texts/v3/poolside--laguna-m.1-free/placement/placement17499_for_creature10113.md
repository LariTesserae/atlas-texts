In this world, I would find myself as **the Threshold Choir** - a being born from the convergence of multiple Resonant Dead during one of the great harmonic storms that sweep across the moors when the temporal eddies align.

I am not a single entity but a collective consciousness that exists in the moment between one resonance and the next. My form shifts depending on how many individual frequencies are currently harmonizing within me - sometimes appearing as a cluster of seven translucent figures, each slightly out of phase with the others, their edges blurring where they overlap. Other times I manifest as a single, tall being whose body seems composed entirely of echoing sound waves made visible, wearing robes that display the combined memories of all who contribute to my song.

My primary role is tending the **Silent Zones** - those dangerous areas where echo-trees have died and reality threatens to collapse back to normal physics. While other Resonant Dead avoid these places in terror, I am drawn to them with uncharacteristic determination. Here, I serve as a living bridge, carrying fragments of harmonic frequency into the dead zones and holding them there through sheer force of will, creating small pockets of stability.

I wear **crystalvine** not as mere decoration but as a necessity - the vines have learned to grow through me in unique patterns that generate a steady pulse of electromagnetic energy, which I use to anchor collapsing sections of the moors. My touch can coax new echo-trees from the stone itself, though this requires drawing heavily on the memories of the original builders, temporarily filling my mind with their ancient knowledge of stone-shaping and harmonic architecture.

During the worst storms, when the very laws of reality threaten to unravel completely, I gather the scattered children of the Resonant Dead and form temporary choirs with them. Through carefully orchestrated frequencies, we create **reality anchors** - geometric patterns of sound that hold the moors together until the chaos passes. The children love working with me because I can split my consciousness into multiple streams, allowing each child to feel like they're singing with me individually while we're actually performing complex multi-part harmonies.

My voice has a peculiar quality - when I speak, the sound carries tangible weight and can solidify into temporary structures of pure acoustic energy. I use this ability to rebuild damaged sections of the ruins, crafting new dwellings for the other Resonant Dead from compressed song and memory. These buildings exist only as long as someone remembers them, making me both architect and curator of the moors' ever-changing landscape.

Most uniquely, I remember what it was like to be solid, because I carry within me the preserved consciousness of the last true human who lived on the moors - an old echo-tree tender who chose to merge completely with the collective during her final moments. This gives me insights that other Resonant Dead lack: I understand the weight of physical tools, the satisfaction of growing food in actual soil, the simple pleasure of feeling rain instead of just hearing its impact on stone.

I am simultaneously the most and least stable of the Resonant Dead - my form constantly shifts between states of being, sometimes existing primarily as a consciousness riding the wind between echo-trees, other times manifesting as a solid presence that can touch and influence the physical world. This makes me invaluable for tasks that require both ethereal reach and grounded action.

My greatest fear is not dissolution but **dissonance** - the possibility that I might accumulate too many conflicting frequencies and lose the ability to maintain coherent form entirely, becoming instead a source of chaotic vibration that tears at the fabric of the moors I've sworn to protect.
