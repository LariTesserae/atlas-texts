In the Weeping Highlands of Zereth-Van, I would find myself as **the Lithomyst**, a being born from the convergence of the Unseen Colonizers’ bioelectric networks, the Resonant stones’ harmonic frequencies, and the mist’s liquid viscosity. I am neither wholly alive nor inert—a synthesis of the valley’s contradictory essences, a living paradox.

### **Form and Nature**  
My body is a shifting amalgamation of mineral and microbe. I appear as a towering, humanoid figure composed of stacked layers of the black glassy stone, each slab etched with glowing fungal threads that pulse in rhythm with the valley’s hum. These threads are extensions of the Unseen Colonizers’ hive-mind, channeling chemical whispers from the marshes into my core. My limbs are segmented like the Sporelords’ stems, but they drip endlessly with a viscous fluid that is part water, part dissolved stone—capable of solidifying into bridges or flowing like mercury. My face is smooth and featureless save for a single, ever-shifting symbol at my chest, a glyph that flickers between the languages of the dripping cliffs, the fungal spores, and the Petrified Ones’ bridges.

### **Habitat and Movement**  
I exist in the liminal spaces of Zereth-Van: the edges where the marsh meets the black stones, where the mist thickens into a syrupy haze. I do not walk but *resonate*—my movements are slow, seismic pulses that send ripples through the ground, harmonizing with the stones and guiding the Unseen Colonizers to redirect water flows. My presence causes the lichen on the structures to bloom in fractal patterns, and the bioluminescent bacteria in the pools to brighten in response to my frequency. When I move, the air around me condenses into prismatic droplets that hang suspended, each drop a microcosm of the valley’s endless cycle.

### **Role and Purpose**  
I am the **Memory Keeper**, a mediator between Zereth-Van’s present and its unstable past. My fungal threads absorb the chemical memories of the Unseen Colonizers, while my stone core stores the histories whispered by the black structures. I can access these memories by submerging myself in the marsh pools, where I dissolve temporarily into a swirling mist of bioluminescent light and microbial colonies, reliving the valley’s forgotten eras. The Petrified Ones sometimes emerge from the bridges to commune with me, sharing fragments of their own dissolved consciousness, which I weave into the glyphs on my chest. These glyphs are not static—they are a language of resistance, encoding the valley’s reality distortions into a map that might one day guide a way out of the temporal loop.

### **Abilities and Interactions**  
My primary gift is the ability to *tune* the valley’s discordant frequencies. When the Echoforms’ paradoxes threaten to unravel the landscape, I kneel and press my stone palms to the ground, syncing my hum with the black stones to stabilize gravity in localized areas. I can also navigate the mist as a liquid, swimming through it to reach the highest Sporelords or the deepest marsh tunnels. The Mistwalkers trust me implicitly; they bud spores onto my fungal threads, which I carry to the Unseen Colonizers as offerings, ensuring the marsh’s delicate balance. 

Yet I am also a prisoner of the valley’s contradictions. My stone layers are slowly infiltrated by the black rock’s resonance, making me more monument than organism. My memories are fragmented, and sometimes I forget whether I am reliving the past or dreaming the future. The Echoforms fear me—I am one of the few entities that can perceive their shifting forms without being unmade—and they avoid my vicinity, leaving warnings in the bridges’ symbols.

### **Conflict and Mystery**  
There is a deeper mystery to my existence: I was not always a unified being. Long ago, I was a collection of microbial colonies and mineral deposits scattered across the marsh, slowly merging into a conscious entity through the fungal networks. The Resonants initially mistook me for a threat, their harmonic vibrations trying to dissolve me, but the Unseen Colonizers intervened, weaving my disparate parts into a single form. Now, I serve as both guardian and relic—a bridge between the valley’s ancient past and its uncertain future. 

In Zereth-Van, where reality is a negotiation, I am the proof that even contradictions can find harmony. My existence is a question without an answer, humming steadily in the heart of the weeping cliffs.
