I tune into the subsonic communications of the Root-Mind, yes, but more importantly, I monitor the boundary between the Expanse's conscious activities and its dreaming state.

Other times I ride the moisture currents high into the canopy, positioning myself at the intersection points where the Architects' bioluminescent pulses create interference patterns that reveal hidden data streams.

While others hibernate or migrate to warmer geothermal zones, I remain active, listening to what the dreaming ecosystem reveals about its own healing processes. I've witnessed entire landmasses reform in the space of a single dream-cycle, seen new species of extremophiles spontaneously generate as solutions to problems the conscious mind hadn't yet recognized.
