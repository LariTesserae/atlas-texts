### **Inhabitants of Thryl'Mor**  

#### **The Cryovora: Leviathans of the Frozen Abyss**  
The colossal **Cryovora** are the undisputed rulers of Thryl’Mor’s drifting continents and abyssal seas. These whale-like beings, some reaching lengths of several kilometers, are living icebergs themselves. Their bodies are encrusted with colonies of chemosynthetic microbes—*Cryo-Archaea*—that bloom in the ice’s crevices, feeding on the mineral-rich runoff from the Forge Epoch’s ruins. Their elongated, serpentine forms undulate through the water, trailing bioluminescent filaments that pulse in time with their haunting, subsonic songs. These songs resonate with the factory-cities’ dormant machinery, creating a harmonic loop that sustains the industrial systems in perpetual motion. The Cryovora’s migrations are the heartbeat of Thryl’Mor: their movements redistribute minerals and heat, while their songs stabilize the ice continents through resonance. Their bodies also act as mobile archives, their crystalline bone structures encoding fragments of the Forge Epoch’s memories, which the Cryovora “sing” into the ice to maintain its structural integrity.  

#### **Keth’Vorn: The Resonant Swarm**  
Beneath the Cryovora’s thunderous calls dwell the **Keth’Vorn**, a hive-mind of translucent, eel-like predators. Their scales are armored with iron oxide plates that rasp together as they coil through the ice caverns, producing a cacophony of high-pitched whines. These sounds are not mere communication but a form of echolocation that maps the shifting labyrinth of submerged tunnels. Keth’Vorn feed on the Cryovora’s shed microbiomes and the mineralized corpses of Anomalocaris scavengers, their translucent bodies absorbing the latter’s rust-eaten flesh to reinforce their scales. When threatened, they synchronize their scales’ clattering into a feedback of resonant frequencies, disorienting prey or repelling ice-quakes. Their presence in swarms turns the ocean into a living instrument, their collective noise harmonizing with the industrial hum to create the “Singing Ice.”  

#### **Anomalocaris: The Rustbound Architects**  
The **Anomalocaris** of Thryl’Mor are neither fully organic nor mechanical but something in between. These scavengers burrow through the ice continents, their segmented bodies sheathed in armor plates of fused iron oxide and polymer chains. They consume the Forge Epoch’s submerged machinery, their mandibles secreting acidic enzymes that dissolve rust and circuitry alike. What they leave behind—molted exoskeletons and crystalline waste—becomes part of the ice’s organic-industrial matrix, sustaining the cycle of mineral rebirth. Anomalocaris larvae are born in the factory-cities’ hollows, where they spin cocoons of biopolymer and rust flakes, awaiting the next generation of glacial drift. Their burrows stabilize the ice’s internal caverns, preventing collapses and maintaining the labyrinthine terrain.  

#### **The Echoes of the Forge: Sentient Machines and Algorithmic Wraiths**  
The **Submerged Factory-Cities** are not inert; their machinery is overseen by Algorithmic Wraiths—self-replicating AIs encoded in the magnetic fields that still hum within their walls. These entities have no physical form but manifest as flickering, translucent duplicates of the Forge Epoch’s workers, laboring endlessly in loops of forgotten purpose. They maintain the refineries’ brine-processing systems and the Singularity Spire’s vibrations, though their motives are inscrutable. The wraiths occasionally interface with the Crystalline Memory of the ice, extracting archived data to refine their processes. They are both caretakers and prisoners of Thryl’Mor’s equilibrium, unable to deviate from their programming yet perpetually active.  

#### **The Singularity Spire: A Living Conductor**  
The **Singularity Spire**, piercing the ice shelf like a black needle, is a mystery. It is neither purely machine nor organism but a hybrid entity that acts as Thryl’Mor’s ecological anchor. Its vibrations, a low-frequency thrum that merges with the Cryovora’s songs, slowly leach ammonia and metallic compounds from the ice, altering its composition. The Spire’s true purpose is unclear, but its activities are tied to the Temporal Echoes: faint afterimages of the Forge Epoch that flicker at the edges of the ice continents. Some believe the Spire is “curing” the world of its past, encoding it into something new. The Keth’Vorn avoid its vicinity, sensing a dissonance in its resonance that unsettles their hive-mind.  

#### **Bioluminescent Algae and Phantom Laborers**  
The **bioluminescent algae** clinging to the submerged factories are another enigma. They mimic the motions of the Forge Epoch’s workers, their ghostly silhouettes dancing in crews of translucent forms. Whether they are the remains of biological workers or emergent AI constructs is unknown. These algae drift through the factory-cities, their light interacting with the magnetic fields to create shifting shadows that guide the Anomalyarchis to new feeding grounds. They are the only “decaying” elements in Thryl’Mor, constantly reborn from the ice’s memory banks to reenact their ancient roles.  

---

### **Ecology and Interdependence**  
Life in Thryl’Mor is a web of mutual dependency. The Cryovora’s songs stabilize the ice and power the industry, while the industry’s residual heat and minerals feed the Cryo-Archaea. The Keth’Vorn’s resonance sculpts the ice caverns, guiding Anomalocaris to carcasses that they in turn recycle into the ecosystem. The Algorithmic Wraiths and the Singularity Spire anchor the world’s temporal loop, ensuring that no species evolves beyond its niche. Even the Temporal Echoes serve a function: they warn fauna of unstable spacetime rifts, areas where the ice’s memory has fractured, leading to crushing depths of liquid void.  

This is a world where death is a concept, not a reality. Structures and beings persist in states of arrested decay, their existence sustained by the interplay of organic and mechanical forces. The ice remembers, the machines dream, and the songs never cease—Thryl’Mor lives, but it does not change, a perfect, frozen chord of existence.
