**The Inhabitants of Zereth-Van**  

The Weeping Highlands are not home to creatures as the mundane world knows them. Life here has adapted to the impossible, evolving beyond flesh and bone into a symphony of contradictions. The inhabitants exist at the intersection of biology, geology, and the raw magic of a reality stretched too tightly.  

### **The Resonants**  
The most enigmatic inhabitants are the **Resonants**, beings born from the black, humming stone structures. These entities are neither fully material nor entirely energy—they are the sum of the stones' latent power, shaped by millennia of condensation and chemical whispers from the fungal networks. Their forms resemble humanoid figures carved from obsidian, their surfaces etched with the same shifting symbols as the petrified wood bridges. When they move, they emit a harmonic resonance that harmonizes with the valley’s perpetual hum, their footsteps causing the stones to pulse in time. They are the caretakers of the black structures, tending to the lichen that glows in fractal patterns, and they speak in tongesthat mimic the dripping water’s unknown language. Their purpose is unclear, but they act as both guardians and prisoners of the valley, forever bound to its sticky, cyclic time.  

### **The Mistwalkers**  
The marsh pools and mist are home to the **Mistwalkers**, ephemeral beings that exist as conglomerates of the bioluminescent bacteria and gelatinous organisms. Their bodies are semi-corporeal, shifting between liquid and gas, with tendrils of mist that weave through the vegetation like liquid glass. They reproduce by budding off clusters of spores that drift upward, only to condense again in the valley’s humid air. Mistwalkers communicate through the fungal networks, which they’ve colonized with tendrarily, symbiotic threads. They are curious but reclusive, their thoughts slow and spaced apart by hours or days (time being as unreliable as it is). They are drawn to the black stones’ hum, as if seeking answers to questions that predate their existence.  

### **The Sporelords**  
Towering above the marsh canopy are the **Sporelords**, massive plant-animal hybrids that dominate the highland’s “skyline.” These beings resemble tree-sized ferns with segmented, vertebrae-like stems and fronds that shimmer with iridescent spores. Their roots are deeply intertwined with the fungal networks, allowing them to share a collective consciousness with the Resonants and Mistwalkers. Sporelords reproduce by releasing cloud-like bursts of spores that drift into the mist, some of which coalesce into new individuals. They are the architects of the valley’s “urban” sprawl, their growth directing the placement of the black stone structures. Their slow, deliberate movements shape the landscape, and their whispers—heard only during the valley’s rare moments of temporal stutter—are said to rewrite the laws of physics in localized areas.  

### **The Echoforms**  
The most dangerous and least understood are the **Echoforms**: beings that exist in the space between realities, where the valley’s reality distortions allow glimpses of alternate worlds. These entities are born from the paradoxes created when gravity-defying waterfalls and condensing mist collide. They appear as shadows with too many joints, their forms flickering between states of matter. Echoforms are not native to Zereth-Van but are trapped there, their bodies unraveling and reforming constantly. They are drawn to the black stones, which they believe hold the key to escaping the valley’s temporal loop. Their presence is marked by sudden spikes in the stones’ hum and the appearance of new symbols on the bridges—symbols that shift to form warnings no one can read.  

### **The Unseen Colonizers**  
Beneath the marsh pools, in the labyrinthine tunnels carved by ancient waterfalls, dwell the **Unseen Colonizers**. These are the microbial life—bioluminescent bacteria, gelatinous organisms, and spore-forming fungi—that form the base of the ecosystem’s food web. They are not merely single-celled but organized into vast, hive-mind colonies that can span kilometers. Their collective intelligence controls the valley’s water flow, regulating the weeping cliffs and marsh levels. They are the true engineers of Zereth-Van, their chemical signals orchestrating the growth of the Sporelords and the Resonants’ resonance. Some theorize they are the remnants of an older civilization, their consciousnesses dissolved into the environment and reconstituted as the valley itself.  

### **The Petrified Ones**  
The bridges of petrified wood are not entirely dead. The **Petrified Ones** are the remnants of a forgotten race who attempted to harness the valley’s power. Their bodies were fossilized by the same forces that shaped the stones, their consciousnesses preserved in the bridges’ wooden fibers. They manifest as ghostly figures that flicker along the bridges, their forms semi-transparent and etched with the shifting symbols. They are neither alive nor dead, trapped in a state of perpetual storytelling, their whispers forming the valley’s ambient noise. Some seek to restore their flesh, while others have forgotten their origins, content to exist as part of the landscape.  

---  
**In Zereth-Van, life is not a struggle for survival but a negotiation with the impossible. The inhabitants are as much a part of the terrain as the mist and stone—they are its voice, its memory, and its endless, weeping heart.**
