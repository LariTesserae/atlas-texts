In the hollow cathedral of living green, where the very air thrums with alien rhythms and the landscape breathes as if alive, the inhabitants are not merely dwellers but *elements* of the place itself—entities so intertwined with the shifting, pulsing ecosystem that they defy the boundaries of flesh, flora, and shadow.  

### **The Architects (the Trees)**  
The towering trees are not passive organisms but sentient architects, their roots and branches rearranging in slow, deliberate choreography. They communicate through the bioluminescent moss that etches their bark—pulsing in patterns that resemble neural signals. Their consciousness is distributed, a collective hive-mind that reshapes the hollow vaulted halls of their domain. They do not sleep or die but "cycle": older trees wither into husks, their fibers dissolving into the soil to feed new growth, while younger shoots thicken into pillars or arches. Their movements are imperceptible, a tectonic drift that reconfigures paths and chambers over hours or days. If one could listen closely, the creaks and groans of the trees form a low, resonant language—a liturgy of growth and decay that sustains the realm.  

### **The Hum-Folk (Insects)**  
The colossal insects, with wings that blot out slivers of twilight, are the most immediate and oppressive presence. They are called the Hum-Folk, not for their sound but for the psychic static their collective hum generates—a pressure that warps thoughts and makes teeth ache. These creatures are not merely pests but the trees’ immune system, swarming to repel intruders or damaged sections. Their bodies shift color to mimic moss or bark, and some phases in and out of visibility, as if the air itself rejects their substance. They reproduce in erratic bursts, their eggs embedded in the thorns of the shifting trees. When threatened, they coalesce into temporary swarms that mimic the shapes of fearsome forms—a human face, a snarling beast, or fragments of forgotten memories—screaming in unison to disorient.  

### **The Dripbound (Pool-Dwellers)**  
In the stagnant pools, where bubbles rise and shatter with the sound of breaking glass, lurk the Dripbound. These entities exist in a state of flux between liquid and gas, their bodies composed of buoyant membranes that trap pockets of the pool’s strange water. They are neither fish nor frog but something older and more ephemeral, their forms shifting like heat haze above the water. The Dripbound reproduce by releasing spores that sink into the pools, where they incubate in the absence of oxygen. When the pools dry (or overflow, depending on the trees’ whims), the spores either dissolve or hatch into airborne larvae that drift into the canopy. They are drawn to movement and sound, emerging from the water to investigate disturbances before retreating into the depths.  

### **The Rootless (Shadow Entities)**  
Between the trees, where emptiness yawns like a mouth, move the Rootless—shadows that twist independently of their sources. These beings are remnants of past ecosystems, echoes of organisms that once thrived in the cathedral before the current cycle. They are semi-corporeal, their forms flickering between the remnants of bark, bone, or insect shells. The Rootless are the keepers of the domain’s memory, whispering fragments of forgotten histories in the rustle of dead leaves. They avoid the Hum-Folk and the Trees, existing in the margins where the light bends and the air grows thin. Some say they are the ghosts of the cathedral’s builders, others that they are the discarded skins of the Trees themselves, shed like bark.  

### **The Cyclers (Transient Fauna)**  
There is no fixed fauna here, only Cyclers—organisms that exist for a single season or storm before dissolving back into the ecosystem. These include the phasic moth-like creatures that mimic faces, their brief lives fueled by a diet of decaying moss and the sap of dying trees. Others include the Clay-Worms, which burrow through the cracked earth, leaving tunnels that fill with water to become the pools’ tributaries. The Cyclers are ephemeral by design, their bodies engineered to break down into nutrients for the next cycle. They are the realm’s way of recycling life, ensuring that no single species dominates the hollow.  

### **The Ozone-Born (Heat Symbionts)**  
The oppressive warmth and metallic tang of ozone are the work of the Ozone-Born, microscopic organisms that dwell in the canopy’s upper layers. These heat-producing microbes form symbiotic colonies in the trees’ leaves, generating energy through a photosynthetic process that burns ozone. They are invisible to the naked eye but their presence is felt in the constant, stifling heat. The Ozone-Born are the reason tools rust and maps degrade—their metabolic byproducts eat away at inorganic matter. They are also the source of the cathedral’s eternal twilight, their light-stifling metabolisms filtering the sunlight into perpetual dusk.  

### **The Hollow’s Purpose**  
This realm is not a home but a crucible—a living experiment where life cycles through growth, decay, and reinvention without end. Its inhabitants do not seek permanence but embrace impermanence, existing in a state of perpetual becoming. To live here is to be unmoored from the concept of a fixed self, to dissolve and reform like the Trees, to ride the hum of the air, and to accept that every path will eventually become a wall of thorns. The beauty of the place lies in its terrible grandeur: a symphony of contradictions where silence is the loudest sound, and the emptiness itself is the only permanent resident.
