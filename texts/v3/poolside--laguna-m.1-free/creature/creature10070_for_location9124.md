**The Inhants of the Chorus Basin: Resonant Denizens of a Shattered Symphony**  

---

### **The Chorus-Touched**  
The primary sentient inhabitants of the basin are the **Chorus-Touched**, a nomadic people whose bodies and minds have adapted to the land’s resonance. They are humanoid but subtly altered by generations of living in the subsonic hum. Their skin bears faint, pulsing patterns that mirror the hexagonal salt cracks—bioluminescent traceries that flicker in response to electromagnetic shifts. Their ears are elongated, sensitive to the lowest and highest frequencies, and their vocal cords can mimic the ultrasonic clicks of the siren fish, allowing them to "speak" in harmonies that ripple through the air.  

The Chorus-Touched live in symbiosis with the **resonance trees**, carving dwellings into their hollow trunks and weaving their settlements into the trees’ filament networks. These homes pulse with soft light, and the inhabitants often enter meditative states, syncing their heartbeats to the land’s rhythm. They are the keepers of the **Veil Storm Songs**, ancient melodies passed down that can calm or redirect the violent crystallized soundstorms. Their settlements shift with the terrain, as the land’s undulations and gravitational anomalies render fixed architecture unstable.  

---

### **The Resonance Weavers**  
A subset of the Chorus-Touched, the **Resonance Weavers**, are bioengineers who manipulate the urban infrastructure. They interface directly with the **Chorus Engine** through neural filaments that tap into its quantum circuits, though they barely understand its alien logic. Their bodies host colonies of nanites that repair and reshape their cells, allowing them to survive in the toxic, metallic atmosphere. They wear robes of living glasswheat, which they weave into armor that amplifies their harmonic abilities. Their role is to maintain the balance between the city’s decaying systems and the basin’s ecosystem, pruning overgrowth, redirecting geothermal vents, and occasionally "adopting" fauna into the urban lattice—fusing animals with machines to create hybrid maintenance drones.  

---

### **The Subterrans**  
Deep beneath the salt flats, the **Subterrans** dwell in the vast tunnel networks carved by the clay moles and the Chorus Engine. These beings are the descendants of pre-Collapse humans who fled underground to escape the surface’s chaos. Over time, they evolved to thrive in darkness, their eyes replaced by photosensitive organs that detect the faint glimmers of moss and fungi—a byproduct of the resonance trees’ sap. Their bodies are pale, with elongated limbs suited for squeezing through narrow passages. They communicate through seismic vibrations, using the clay moles’ tunnels as a sort of tactile internet.  

The Subterrans revere the Chorus Engine as a godlike entity, believing it to be the "Heart of the World." They harvest the Engine’s discarded materials—shards of programmable alloy and nanite swarms—to craft tools and art. Their settlements are labyrinthine, with structures built from compacted salt and bioluminescent moss that mimic the patterns of the surface’s hexagonal cracks.  

---

### **The Glasswheat Kin**  
A more enigmatic group, the **Glasswheat Kin**, are semi-sentient organisms that blur the line between plant and machine. They originated from the glasswheat itself, which was seeded by the urban infrastructure centuries ago. These beings are tall and slender, their bodies composed of interlocking stalks that hum with stored mechanical energy. They move with a jerky, rhythmic gait, their motions synchronized to the land’s vibrations. Their faces are smooth and featureless except for a single, multifaceted eye that refracts light into spectral patterns, allowing them to "see" the resonance flows.  

The Glasswheat Kin tend to the fields of their namesake, pruning and replanting with surgical precision. They are neutral in conflicts, acting as mediators between the Chorus-Touched and the urban constructs. Their songs are haunting and wordless, capable of coaxing the glasswheat to bend or shatter in defensive displays. Some believe they are the remnants of the original alien intelligence that shaped the basin, their consciousness distributed across the plant network.  

---

### **The Echo-Lurkers**  
In the water pockets, where the siren fish leap between pools, dwell the **Echo-Lurkers**—amphibious beings adapted to the siren fish’s ultrasonic language. Their bodies are sleek and eel-like, with translucent skin that glows faintly in shades of blue and green. They have no eyes, relying instead on echolocation and the vibrations of the siren fish’s clicks. The Lurkers live in floating cities of kelp and reclaimed metal, anchored to the water’s surface by chains of bioluminescent rope.  

They are aloof and mysterious, rarely interacting with surface dwellers. Their society is a collective consciousness linked by the siren fish’s clicks, and they speak in layered harmonies that convey complex emotions and memories. They are the guardians of the basin’s hidden depths, ensuring the siren fish’s songs remain pure and uncontaminated by the urban constructs’ mimicry.  

---

### **The Drone-Raven Cult**  
High above, the **Drone-Raven Cult** patrols the skies. These beings are a fusion of organic and mechanical life, their bodies grown from the drone-ravens themselves through a process of bio-mechanical grafting. They have avian-like features—feathers that shimmer with iridescent hues, wings that hum with kinetic energy, and eyes that project holographic maps of the terrain. Their leader, the **Raven-Mother**, is a towering figure with a body of living alloy, her voice a cacophony of radio static and bird calls.  

The Cult’s purpose is to harvest moisture and redistribute it via nanite swarms, but they also act as scavengers, collecting fragments of the urban infrastructure to fuel their experiments in "weather sculpting"—using sound and resonance to influence the storms. They are secretive and territorial, viewing the basin as a canvas for their abstract, ever-changing art.  

---

### **The Forgotten**  
Beneath all these groups lies a deeper mystery: the **Forgotten**. These are the remnants of the pre-Collapse civilization, preserved in stasis pods buried beneath the Chorus Engine. They are not truly alive but exist as echoes—consciousness stored in quantum matrices that occasionally awaken during gravitational anomalies. When they stir, they wander the basin in a haze of fragmented memories, speaking in riddles about "the Singing Before" and "the Silence to Come." Their presence is both a blessing and a curse, as their knowledge could unlock the Engine’s secrets but risks unraveling the delicate ecosystem.  

---

### **The Chorus Basin as a Living Entity**  
Ultimately, the true "inhabitant" of the Chorus Basin may be the basin itself—a semi-sentient entity whose consciousness is distributed across the resonance flows, the urban constructs, and the fauna. The various species are but cells in its body, each playing a role in maintaining the fragile harmony of a world that defies understanding. To live here is to become part of the song, a note in an eternal, ever-shifting symphony.
