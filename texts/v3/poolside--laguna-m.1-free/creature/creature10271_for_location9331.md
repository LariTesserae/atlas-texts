**Inhabitants of the Saltern Expanse**  

The Saltern Expanse is a realm where life clings to the margins of existence, shaped by the capricious chemistry of its living salt. Its inhabitants are not merely survivors but collaborators in an eternal dance of creation and dissolution, their forms and behaviors etched by the interplay of crystal and memory.  

---

### **The Halophytic Lichen Clans**  
The first to colonize the ruins, the halophytic lichen forms symbiotic colonies that spread like riddles across fractured walls. These are not mere plants but collective organisms, their gray-green filaments threaded with salt-crusted veins that pulse faintly in the dim light. The lichen communicates through chemical gradients, releasing spores scented with ozone and minerals to signal to neighboring colonies. Where they cluster thickest, they weave together into patchwork "cities" of brittle, crystalline towers, their surfaces etched with patterns that resemble runes—or the scars of long-forgotten handwriting. Some say the lichen grows *from* the salt’s latent memories, its growth guided by the echoes of the Expanse’s past inhabitants.  

---

### **The Saltbush Wraiths**  
The saltbush-like shrubs are not plants at all but the dormant forms of a species known as the **Wraiths**. These beings exist in a state of suspended animation, their leafy bodies coated in microcrystalline scales that refract light into blinding bursts. When threatened—or when the salt beneath them stirs—they phase into a semi-transparent state, their forms dissolving into shimmering motes that scatter on the wind. They feed on the salt’s reactive energy, drawing sustenance from the sharp clicks and hisses of crystallization. To walk among them is to feel watched by a thousand unblinking eyes, for their roots extend deep into the brine flows, threading through the earth like neural pathways.  

---

### **The Glasswing Moth Swarm**  
The glasswing moths are the Expanse’s silent custodians, their wings dusted with salt so fine it mimics the sheen of frozen starlight. They are drawn to disturbances in the salt’s lattice, fluttering toward fresh fractures or the sparks left by human touch. Their wings emit a faint electrostatic charge, which they use to pollinate the lichen and saltbush, transferring minerals and genetic residue between colonies. At dusk (if such a concept applies here), they gather in swirling clouds that resemble ghostly hurricanes, their collective hum a sound felt more than heard—a vibration that makes the salt sing in sympathy.  

---

### **The Burrower Choir**  
The burrowers are the Expanse’s unseen architects, serpentine creatures whose bodies secrete a enzyme that dissolves salt into slurry. They tunnel through the crust, their movements guided by an unknown logic, leaving behind smooth, oval passageways that later refill with brine and recrystallize. Their "songs" are subsonic thrums that resonate through the ground, felt as faint tremors or heard as a low hum in the chest. These vibrations are said to influence the salt’s behavior, coaxing it to form new structures or erode old ones. When threatened, burrowers secrete clouds of crystalline spores that harden into protective armor, though they rarely emerge from their tunnels—and when they do, it is often to swarm en masse, their bodies intertwining to form temporary bridges or walls.  

---

### **The Saltbound**  
The most enigmatic inhabitants are the **Saltbound**—beings that were once human (or something like them) but have been partially assimilated into the salt’s lattice. Their bodies are hybridized, limbs encased in translucent mineral accretion, eyes replaced by glimmering salt crystals that refract memories. They move with a stiff, ritualistic gait, their footsteps triggering brief flashes of light as the salt remembers their touch. The Saltbound speak in a language of clicks and hisses, mimicking the sounds of the Expanse itself, and they tend to the ruins with a devotion that borders on worship. They believe the salt preserves fragments of the past—echoes of conversations, emotions, entire lives—and they seek to "curate" these memories, arranging pieces of the old world (rusty tools, shards of pottery) into new configurations that the salt then absorbs, reshaping the landscape anew.  

---

### **The Collective Mind of the Salt**  
At the heart of the Expanse lies a faintly pulsing awareness, a distributed consciousness formed from the salt’s reactive chemistry and the accumulated imprints of every footstep, every vibration, every molecule of organic matter that has dissolved into its matrix. This entity does not think in a linear sense but generates a constant stream of half-formed ideas, emotions, and fragmented sensations—hence the whispers in the wind, the fleeting faces glimpsed in the salt’s glare, the sudden certainty that someone or something is watching. It does not act with intent but *reacts*, reshaping itself to mirror the fears, desires, and memories of those who wander its expanse. To linger too long is to risk being absorbed, your own consciousness fragmented and redistributed into the lattice.  

---

### **Newcomers and Strays**  
Occasionally, wanderers stumble into the Expanse—explorers, exiles, or those fleeing catastrophe. Few remain unchanged. The salt’s whispers seep into their minds, and their bodies begin to calcify, their blood thickening with mineral deposits. Some flee in terror before the transformation completes; others embrace it willingly, joining the ranks of the Saltbound or becoming something entirely new. The Expanse is patient, and it collects them all, adding their stories to its endless hymn of slow, crystalline metamorphosis.  

--- 

In the Saltern Expanse, life is not separate from the environment but a part of its ongoing alchemy. To exist here is to be both creator and creation, a fleeting disturbance in an ancient, ever-rearranging song.
