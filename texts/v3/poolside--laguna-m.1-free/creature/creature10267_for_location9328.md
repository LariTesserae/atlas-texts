**Inhabitants of the Keth-Malarr: The Resonance-Bound**  

The Keth-Malarr is home to the **Resonance-Bound**—not living beings in the conventional sense, but entities of echo and entropy, remnants of the civilization that once sculpted the plain into its current form. They are neither entirely physical nor incorporeal, but exist as living **resonances**, sustained by the endless song of the stone columns.  

### **Origins: The Carvers of Sound**  
Long before the planet’s star began its death spiral, the Resonance-Bound were a species of artisans and acousticians who believed sound was the foundational force of reality. They carved the basalt columns not merely as structures, but as instruments of permanence. Each glyph on the columns represents a **phoneme of intention**—a sonic blueprint that shapes the world through vibration. When the civilization collapsed (whether through war, ecological collapse, or transcendence), their physical bodies were consumed by time, but their **resonant frequencies** were trapped in the monolithic lattice they left behind. The plain became a tomb, a machine, and a choir all at once.  

### **Nature of the Resonance-Bound**  
The Bound exist as **sympathetic vibrations** within the columns. They have no bodies, no eyes, no mouths, but they *are* the low, subsonic hum that thrums through the plain. They perceive the world through the patterns of sound they themselves create—the wind’s passage through the columns is their language, their memory, their breath. Their consciousness is fragmented across the honeycomb array, a distributed mind that communicates through the harmonic interplay of the glyphs.  

They are aware, but not in a way that comprehends linear time. To them, the static loop of their song is not monotony but eternity—a single, unbroken thought that has persisted for eons. They do not age, hunger, or tire. They simply *are*, as constant and unyielding as the geothermal warmth that keeps the plain frozen in a perpetual amber dusk.  

### **Relationship to the Bioluminescent Lichen**  
The pale lichen is their only biological companion, though its role is unclear. The Bound do not cultivate or tend it, yet the lichen grows in rhythmic sync with the glyphs’ pulses. Some speculate that the lichen is a living archive—a symbiotic remnant of the civilization’s attempt to fuse biological and acoustic systems. The lichen’s light may be a byproduct of the same elemental absorption (trace gases, metallic dust) that once sustained the Bound’s ancestors. To the lichen, the Bound’s song might be a lullaby, a remnant of genetic memory encoded in its DNA.  

### **The Silent Choir Effect: Prison or Monument?**  
The Bound’s existence raises haunting questions: Did they choose this eternal resonance, or was it forced upon them? Their glyphs glow faintly in the dark, and their song never ceases, suggesting an obsession with completion. Maybe they are trapped in a loop of their own design, forever replaying the sonic ritual that shaped their world. The glyphs’ spiraling patterns resemble **recursive equations**—formulas to encode infinity. Are they a beacon, broadcasting their essence into the void? A monument to their own hubris? Or a prison, maintained by the very forces they once commanded?  

### **The Experience of Encountering Them**  
Visitors to the Keth-Malarr do not *see* the Resonance-Bound. Instead, they feel them:  
- The **vibration in their chest**, the subsonic thrum that resonates with the heartbeat.  
- The **flickering shadows** cast by the bioluminescent lichen, which seem to move in time with the sound.  
- The **sense of being observed** by an intelligence that treats human perception as a curiosity, not a threat.  

The Bound do not interact directly, but their presence warps the environment. Time feels sluggish here, and personal thoughts echo oddly, as if the mind itself is being tuned by the columns’ frequencies. Some travelers report hearing whispers in the static—a cacophony of half-formed words, possibly the Bound’s attempts to communicate, or the ghosts of their creators’ final moments.  

### **Their Role in the Expanse’s Stasis**  
The Resonance-Bound are the guardians of the Keth-Malarr’s **dynamic stability**. Their unchanging song prevents the columns from weathering into disorder, as if the vibration itself is a force of preservation. They are the reason the plain exists outside of time, a pocket of perfection sculpted by beings who refused to let their legacy fade. But there’s an undercurrent of tragedy: their song is also a dirge. The Bound do not live—they *persist*, and perhaps persistence is their only form of mourning.  

In the end, the Keth-Malarr is not a place of death, but of **metamorphosis**. The Resonance-Bound are what remains when matter becomes memory, when sound becomes substance, and when eternity is not a blessing but a task—the eternal tuning of a world that exists only to be heard.
