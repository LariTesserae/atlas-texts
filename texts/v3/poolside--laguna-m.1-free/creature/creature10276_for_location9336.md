The **Inhabitants of the Cathedral Depths** are a tapestry of survival fused with the land itself, creatures and beings who have become inseparable from the primordial stillness and churning chaos. They are neither entirely alive nor dead, but something in between—a reflection of the Depths’ eternal unraveling and rebirth.

---

### **The Glasskin**  
The dominant sentient life here are the **Glasskin**, the last remnants of the civilization that once carved the terraced ruins. Their bodies are a fusion of flesh and mineral, their skin streaked with veins of obsidian-like glass that hum with the earth’s residual heat. They are tall and gaunt, their limbs elongated and jointed like the petrified wood that surrounds them, allowing them to navigate the uneven terrain. Their faces are smooth, featureless except for a single vertical slit where a nose might be, and their eyes are gone—replaced by clusters of bioluminescent fungi grafted into their skulls, allowing them to perceive the fungal network’s pulsing light.  

The Glasskin communicate through the fungi, their voices a resonant hum that mimics the geothermal tremors. They dwell in the amphitheaters and tunnels, wearing robes woven from fungal filaments and clay, dyed in the same hues as the glowing carpet below. They revere the **dynamic turbulence**, viewing the fissures and rebirths as a form of divine renewal. Their tools are hybrids of ancient and new—flint blades embedded with quartz shards, ropes spun from fungal mycelium, and vessels that blend compressed clay with molten glass from the walls.  

They are a nomadic people, constantly reshuffling their settlements as the earth cracks and reforms. Their greatest ritual is the **Chiming**, conducted when a fissure opens: they emerge from tunnels to "sing" into the new void, believing the sound will guide the earth’s next transformation. Their settlements are never built to last, only to honor the momentary stability of stone and fungus.

---

### **The Echo Crawlers**  
Beneath the Glasskin, in the deeper tunnels, dwell the **Echo Crawlers**—massive, eyeless beings that navigate through an extrasensory web of vibrations. Their bodies are segmented and armored in overlapping plates of chitinous stone, which they continuously shed and regrow as the geothermal vents reshape the caverns. They are the Depths’ most enigmatic fauna, neither predator nor prey, but architects of sorts.  

The Crawlers leave trails in the dust and mist, which the Glasskin interpret as maps of the earth’s moods. They are drawn to the quartz veins, which they consume and excrete as smooth, polished stones used by the Glasskin in their rituals. Their movement is slow and seismic, and their presence is heralded by sudden, violent tremors. Some say they are the land’s immune system, devouring weak structures to make way for new growth—both fungal and architectural.

---

### **The Prism Beetles**  
Scattered along the walls are **Prism Beetles**, crystalline arthropods whose bodies refract light into prismatic bursts. They swarm the ruins in erratic clouds, their carapaces clicking like wind chimes in the still air. They are drawn to the bioluminescence of the fungi, and some Glasskin believe they are the souls of the ancients, trapped and reincarnated as living prisms.  

The beetles are both pests and pollinators, their mandibles dripping a sticky sap that fertilizes fungal spores. They nest in the amphitheaters’ crevices, and their molted shells are collected by the Glasskin as raw materials for tools. Their movements are erratic, as if they are chasing something invisible—or fleeing from something far worse.

---

### **The Myconid Choir**  
The bioluminescent fungi themselves are semi-sentient, forming a **myconid choir** that pulses in unison with the earth’s heartbeat. The Glasskin believe these fungi are the collective consciousness of their ancestors, preserved in a state of eternal growth. The fungi’s light shifts color depending on the emotional state of nearby beings—emitting a calm blue when the Glasskin are in harmony with the Depths, but flickering to a frantic green when the geothermal vents rage.  

The choir is both deity and drug; the Glasskin consume certain fungi to commune with the ancestors, experiencing visions of the world aboveground (a place they’ve never seen, but know exists only through myth). Some Glasskin have fused fungal grafts directly into their nervous systems, becoming **myco-sages** who can predict the next fissure by reading the choir’s patterns.

---

### **The Hollow Folk**  
In the shallow pools and mist-laden basins, something else lurks—the **Hollow Folk**, beings so insubstantial they’re often mistaken for shadows or illusions. They are humanoid but translucent, their forms rippling like water disturbed by unseen hands. These are the echoes of the extinct civilization, preserved by the fungal network as imperfect, wandering memories.  

The Hollow Folk cannot speak, but they mimic the last actions of their lives endlessly—building phantom structures from mist, wielding tools made of condensed vapor. They are drawn to the Glasskin, and during the Chiming, they dance in the periphery of rituals, their presence a reminder of what was lost. Some Glasskin believe that touching a Hollow Folk grants glimpses of the past, but the experience is addictive and dangerous: to stare too long into their fluid forms is to risk forgetting which memories are your own.

---

### **The Depths’ Guardians**  
At the farthest reaches of the catacombs, near the planet’s molten core, dwell the **Pyroclasts**—elemental entities born from the geothermal vents. They are living eruptions, their bodies composed of superheated steam and molten rock, constantly shifting between amorphous and humanoid shapes. They rarely interact with the Glasskin, but their eruptions dictate the rhythm of the Depths. When a Pyroclast rises, entire districts of the city are buried or reformed, and the Glasskin flee to higher terraces, singing to appease the entity’s wrath.  

The Pyroclasts are both destroyer and creator, and their language is the language of fire and pressure. The Glasskin’s most skilled myco-sages can interpret the tremors they send through the stone, believing these to be prophecies.

---

### **The Unseen**  
There are others, perhaps, who exist in the spaces between breaths: creatures that slip through fissures as they open and close, beings that feed on the mist and silence, or entities that may have evolved from the petrified wood itself. The Cathedral Depths are a place where the rules of life dissolve into mineral and motion, where existence is defined by adaptation to chaos. Until the next transformation, the Glasskin and their kin endure in the liminal space between ruin and rebirth, their fates tied to the earth’s eternal sigh.
