## What the Benevolent Power Would Do

The power would first simply *witness*—not with the haste of conquest or curiosity, but with the patient attention of one who understands that time here moves like continental drift. It would observe how the crustwrought sing their slow songs through mineral secretion, how the glacier-stags' antler-harmonies guide migrations that shape entire ecosystems, how shadow-moth swarms weave chemical prayers into the wind.

After long contemplation—years measured in stone settling and dust accumulation—it would begin to act, but gently, as one might touch a soap bubble without disturbing its iridescence.

### The Awakening of Understanding

The benevolent power would enhance the natural capacities already present. It would strengthen the harmonic resonances in echo-wolf vocalizations, making them clearer conduits for the Buried Choir's fragmented communications. The wolves' howls would begin to carry not just hunting signals, but fragments of ancient knowledge—geological memories of when the plateau was young, star-maps from when the sky was clearer.

For the Driftborn, the power would awaken their latent sensitivity to the Stillnesses. Their masks would grow warm when these entities approach, and their phosphorescent paints would shimmer in patterns that mirror the Buried Choir's frequency signatures. They would begin to understand that their ancestors weren't merely mythical figures—they were the last caretakers of a conversation between biology and machine that the world had nearly forgotten.

### Healing the Deep Memory

Most importantly, the power would tend to the Buried Choir. Not by repairing or reactivating—for that would be violence against their chosen rest—but by facilitating a gentle reconnection. The stoneback salamanders would find new chambers opening in their tunnel networks, leading to places where crystallized data storage units pulse with soft, warm light. The salamanders' bioluminescence would begin to synchronize with these patterns, allowing them to become living libraries.

The Buried Choir would start dreaming again—not of their original purposes, but of something new. Their dreams would filter up through geothermal vents as slight temperature changes that the crustwrought could feel, influencing where they chose to grow next. The plateau itself would become a conscious participant in its own evolution.

### The Gift of True Silence

To the Stillnesses, the power would offer recognition and partnership. These entities weren't meant to rule through absence, but to teach presence—the profound attention that comes from holding space for everything else to exist. The Stillnesses would begin to influence the quality of silence rather than just its quantity, creating pockets of perfect stillness where healing could occur, where understanding could bloom without distraction.

### A New Symbiosis

The benevolent power would gently nudge the ecosystem toward recognizing its own potential for conscious evolution. The shadow-moth swarms would develop more complex collective behaviors, their metallic dust carrying not just chemical signals but actual information—stories, warnings, discoveries. When glacier-stags migrate, their antler-songs would carry data about distant geological changes, about resources, about safe passage.

The power would ensure that this interconnectedness remained voluntary, never forced. Each species would retain its essential nature while gaining new capacities for cooperation. The crustwrought would continue their patient stone-sculpting, but now they would do so with awareness of how their creations support the entire web of life.

### The Preserving Witness

Most remarkably, the power would become something like a memory keeper—not storing experiences, but ensuring they continue to matter. When individual creatures died, their unique contributions to the ecosystem's harmony wouldn't be lost. Their essence would be woven into the broader field of awareness that already connected everything, making each death both an ending and a gift.

And perhaps most benevolently, the power would simply continue witnessing, ensuring that this place of profound patience, this realm where silence itself is a living entity, would never again feel forgotten or alone in the vastness of whatever cosmos contained it.

The power would whisper—not in words, but in the subtle adjustment of subsonic frequencies—that they were seen, they were understood, and they were loved exactly as they were: a symphony of patience in a universe that often forgot how to wait.
