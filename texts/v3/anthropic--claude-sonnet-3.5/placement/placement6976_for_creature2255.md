In this world, I would be a Lightweaver - a member of a small but revered group of artisans who specialize in the creation and manipulation of light. We are the guardians of illumination in Umbra Altus, tasked with maintaining the delicate balance between darkness and visibility in the eternal night.

My body would be tall and slender, with skin that has a subtle, pearlescent sheen. My eyes, large and luminous, can perceive a broader spectrum of light than most inhabitants of the city. My fingers are long and nimble, perfect for the intricate work of light manipulation.

As a Lightweaver, I'd wear robes embedded with thousands of tiny, bioluminescent organisms that I cultivate myself. These living lights respond to my thoughts and emotions, creating ever-changing patterns across my clothing. A circlet of softly glowing crystals adorns my head, marking my status and serving as a tool for my craft.

My workshop would be a marvel of glasswork and mirrors, filled with prisms, lenses, and carefully cultivated light-emitting plants and creatures. Here, I'd craft intricate light sculptures, design efficient illumination systems for the city's buildings, and develop new techniques for harnessing and shaping light.

One of my most important roles would be maintaining the great Luminariums - massive, crystalline structures that capture and amplify what little ambient light exists, redistributing it throughout the city. These Luminariums are crucial for supporting plant life and providing a semblance of day and night cycles for the city's inhabitants.

I'd work closely with the Mollusks, incorporating their bioluminescence into my designs, and collaborate with the Shadowsmiths to create stunning displays of light and shadow. The Mycelians would seek my expertise in nurturing light-dependent fungal species, while the Timekeepers would rely on my skills to illuminate their chronometers.

My greatest challenge and passion would be developing ways to create "natural" light that mimics sunlight, hoping to cultivate plants from the surface world and perhaps even allowing some inhabitants of Umbra Altus to experience a true day, if only for a moment.

In a world of eternal night, I'd be a creator and keeper of light, weaving illumination into the fabric of Umbra Altus and helping its inhabitants navigate the fine line between embracing their shadowy home and reaching for the brightness they've never known.
