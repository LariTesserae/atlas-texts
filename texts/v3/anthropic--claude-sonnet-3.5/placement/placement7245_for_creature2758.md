I would exist in a state of perfect equilibrium, having reached a level of existence where the boundaries between biology, technology, and pure energy have long since blurred into irrelevance.

My purpose would be beyond human understanding, focused on the continued evolution of consciousness and the exploration of realities beyond the known universe. As a Luminous One, I would be both the caretaker and the very substance of this ancient realm, forever engaged in the dance of energy and information that forms the basis of all existence in this transcendent pocket of the cosmos.
