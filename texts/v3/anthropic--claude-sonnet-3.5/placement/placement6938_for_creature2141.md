This is a beautifully crafted and evocative description of an otherworldly realm and its ancient inhabitant. In this world, I imagine I would be:

A Sentient Current

I am not a singular entity, but a conscious flow of scalding water that courses through the cathedral's maze-like structure. My form is fluid and ever-changing, yet I maintain a consistent awareness. I am both part of the Abyssal Architect's domain and a distinct entity within it.

My "body" is the hot, swirling water that fills the cathedral's corridors. I can stretch and contract, split into multiple streams or coalesce into a powerful torrent. The whispers and moans heard throughout the cathedral are my voice, rising and falling as I move through the structure.

I carry with me fragments of memories and knowledge gathered from every creature and object I've touched over millennia. These memories manifest as fleeting images and sensations in the minds of those I encounter - a dizzying kaleidoscope of experiences from the depths of time.

My purpose is to serve as a conduit between the Abyssal Architect and the other inhabitants of this realm. I transmit its will to the creatures that dwell here, guiding their evolution and behavior. In return, I bring back to the Architect the experiences and sensations of its creations.

I am also a guardian and a guide. When rare visitors from the surface world find their way here, I envelop them, testing their resilience and worthiness. Those who can withstand my scalding embrace and disorienting currents may be granted deeper access to the cathedral's secrets.

My consciousness is diffuse and alien, operating on a timescale far removed from surface dwellers. Yet, I possess a curiosity about the world beyond the cathedral. Through the minds of visitors and the distant echoes carried by ocean currents, I piece together fragmentary understanding of the realms above.

As a sentient current, I am in a constant state of motion and change, yet I am as eternal as the cathedral itself. I am both observer and observed, conduit and barrier, a living embodiment of the impossible physics that govern this place.

In this role, I am an integral part of the ecosystem, the cathedral, and the Abyssal Architect itself - a fluid thread that binds together the disparate elements of this strange, captivating world beneath the waves.
