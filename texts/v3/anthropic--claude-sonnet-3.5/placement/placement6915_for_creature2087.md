In this world, I find myself as a Listener of the Arbori, one of the spiritual leaders and lore-keepers of this diminutive society. My body is adorned with intricate sound-wave tattoos that glow softly in the bioluminescent light of the Arbor's night.

As a Listener, my days are spent in deep communion with the Sap Singers, those translucent, melodious creatures that feed on the tree's lifeblood. I've learned to interpret their humming, understanding it as the voice of the great tree itself. My large, leaf-shaped ears are constantly attuned to the symphony of the Arbor, picking up the subtlest changes in its rhythm.

My eyes, with irises that shift color with my emotions and the time of day, are often closed in meditation as I absorb the vibrations of our world. The constant cacophony that might overwhelm others is, to me, a rich tapestry of information and insight.

I wear simple garments woven from soft, sound-absorbent moss, allowing me to move through the Arbor without disrupting its delicate acoustic balance. Around my neck hangs a pendant made from a polished seed pod, which I use as a resonance chamber to amplify or focus specific sounds.

My role extends beyond mere interpretation of the Arbor's sounds. I am a keeper of our people's history, passing down oral traditions through complex melodies that echo through the wooden chambers of our home. I guide young Arbori in understanding the language of the forest, teaching them to listen not just with their ears, but with their entire being.

In times of crisis or significant decision-making, I join the Echo Council, where my ability to hear the collective heartbeat of the Arbor helps guide our community's future. My words carry weight, not just for their content, but for the harmonious way they resonate with the Arbor itself.

Despite my elevated status, I remain humble, knowing that I am but one small part of the greater symphony of life within the Echoing Arbor. Each day, I give thanks to the great tree that houses us, to the Sap Singers that give voice to its wisdom, and to the intricate web of life that sustains us all.

As night falls and the Lumina Moths begin their luminous dance, I retreat to my meditation nook high in the Arbor. Here, surrounded by the soft glow of bioluminescent fungi, I open myself to the nocturnal chorus of our world, seeking new melodies and meanings in the ever-changing song of the Echoing Arbor.
