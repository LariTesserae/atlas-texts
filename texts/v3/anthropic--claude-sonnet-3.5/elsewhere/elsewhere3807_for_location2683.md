The relationship between the Verdant Heights and the Mycorrhizal Nexus is one of balance and mutual dependence. This symbiosis extends to the maintenance of the world's delicate equilibrium. The Mycelial Core works in concert with the collective consciousness of the Chlorophyllians to regulate global processes, from weather patterns to the growth cycles of the plant-cities.
