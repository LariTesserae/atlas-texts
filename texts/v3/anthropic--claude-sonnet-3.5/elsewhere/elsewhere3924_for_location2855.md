They flow between islands, creating rivers and streams in the sky that connect the entire ecosystem.

Huge, balloon-like beings drift lazily through the air, their translucent bodies revealing complex internal ecosystems. Swarms of tiny, crystalline insects form ever-changing patterns in the sky, acting as a collective intelligence that helps maintain the balance of the entire realm.

The connection between the Aether Gardens and the deep sea realm is subtle but profound. The Nexus and the Prismatic Leviathan are in constant communication, exchanging information and energy. The rifts in reality that appear in the deep sea often have counterparts in the Aether Gardens, creating a network of spatial-temporal passages between the two realms.

This cycle maintains the balance between the two realms, each one feeding and being fed by the other. It's a harmony of opposites - the chaotic depths and the ordered sky, the fluid sea and the crystalline islands, the singular consciousness of the Leviathan and the collective mind of the Nexus.
