Communication between the Core is slow and deliberate, occurring through gradual shifts in the energy patterns of their geode homes. These exchanges can take what might be perceived as years in surface time, but in the timeless expanse of the underground, such concepts hold little meaning.

Occasionally, a Core entity will achieve a state of heightened consciousness, triggering a process akin to mitosis. The being splits, with one half remaining in the geode while the other ascends through the quicksilver sea to the surface, transforming into one of the more dynamic Flux beings.

This ascension process is a crucial link between the two realms. It provides the surface world with a constant influx of new energy and consciousness, fueling the endless cycle of creation and destruction observed in the crystal spires and quicksilver sea.

Conversely, when a Flux being on the surface reaches a state of perfect harmony or achieves some inscrutable goal, it may choose to descend into the depths, merging with a Core entity and bringing with it the experiences and knowledge gained in the world above.

The boundary between these two realms is not fixed. In some places, the quicksilver sea becomes thin, allowing glimpses of the geode world below. These areas are characterized by intense energy fluctuations, where the already malleable nature of space and time becomes even more pronounced. Flux beings are drawn to these places, engaging in complex energy exchanges with their Core counterparts.

This dual nature of the world - the frenetic, ever-changing surface and the slow, deliberate depths - creates a balance that sustains the entire ecosystem. The surface world of crystal and quicksilver serves as a crucible for evolution and experimentation, while the underground realm of geodes acts as a reservoir of energy and a keeper of accumulated experiences.
