The crystals themselves are alive in a sense, slowly growing and changing over millennia.

These creatures range from microscopic organisms to massive, whale-like entities that navigate the depths using echolocation.

The crystals of the Abyss glow with an inner light, their patterns synchronizing with the pulsations of the Hive's moisture-harvesting structures.
