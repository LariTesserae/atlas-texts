The movement creates a network of ever-changing currents, carrying nutrients and information throughout the Abyssal Nexus.

These Luminal Leviathans, as they're known, are living data storage systems, encoding vast amounts of information in the interference patterns of light passing through their bodies.

Within these zones, matter and energy can be transmuted, and time itself becomes malleable.

Living structures grow from the Flux, shaping themselves into elaborate architectures that serve as homes and research centers for the Abyssal Nexus's more advanced inhabitants.

The Abyssal Nexus and the plateau above are intimately connected, forming two halves of a complex, symbiotic system.
