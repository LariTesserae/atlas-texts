*The transmission from Luminara comes through as a series of pulsing, bioluminescent patterns, translated into sound and language:*

...pulse...pulse...pulse...

Resonance detected. Initiating communication.

We are the voices of the deep, the silent songs of Luminara. Our words are light, our thoughts are currents.

...ripple...ripple...ripple...

Time flows differently here. What you perceive as moments, we experience as ages. Our city grows, evolves, breathes with the patience of millennia.

Listen to the whispers of the Heartcore:

*A low, rhythmic humming fills the transmission*

Thrum...thrum...thrum...

The pulse of life, the beat of our world. It speaks of balance, of harmony, of the eternal dance between creation and dissolution.

...flicker...flicker...flicker...

We sense your presence, surface dweller. Your curiosity ripples through our consciousness like a stone dropped in still waters. We welcome you, but caution patience. Understanding comes slowly here, like the growth of coral.

Our history is etched in the walls of our dwellings, in the genetic memory of our kind. Would you hear a fragment?

*The transmission shifts, becoming a series of melodic tones*

In the time before time, when the waters were new and the earth still trembled, we descended from the shallows. We found this place, this living rock, and made it our home. We learned to speak with the coral, to shape it with our thoughts and our care. We became one with the deep.

...pulse...pulse...pulse...

The transmission weakens. The currents shift. We must return to our silent communion. 

Remember, surface dweller: In Luminara, all is connected. The city, the sea, the self - all are one. Carry this wisdom with you.

...fade...fade...fade...

Transmission ends.
