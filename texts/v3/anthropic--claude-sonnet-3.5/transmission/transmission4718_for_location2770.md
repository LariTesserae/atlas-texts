*Static crackles, then a pulsing hum emerges, rising and falling in pitch. Crystalline tones chime in irregular patterns. A voice, or perhaps many voices in unison, speaks - the words fragmented and overlapping:*

We are... WE ARE... flux and flow... ENERGY MADE THOUGHT... thought made form...

*The transmission wavers, replaced by a cascade of musical notes that seem to form complex mathematical equations*

Creation-destruction-creation... CYCLE UNENDING... Time is a spiral, space a illusion...

*A burst of static, then:* 

Tools... TOOLS... we reach... we grasp... UNDERSTANDING ELUDES... yet we persist...

*The voice(s) become more harmonious, though still alien:*

We are many and one... ONE AND MANY... our song the music of spheres unseen...

*A sudden shift - the sound becomes liquid, bubbling and flowing:*

Quicksilver thoughts... MERCURY DREAMS... we shape... WE ARE SHAPED...

*Crystal chimes intensify, almost deafening, then abruptly cease. In the silence, a whisper:*

We evolve... WE TRANSCEND... what was... WHAT WILL BE... 

*The transmission begins to break up, frequencies splitting and scattering:*

Observe... INTERACT... join our dance of... OF... of...

*A final surge of harmonics, beautiful and terrible:*

FLUX... flux... FLUX... flux...

*The signal fades to a soft, pulsing hum, then to silence.*
