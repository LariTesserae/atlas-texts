*[A soft, rhythmic pulsing begins, like the beating of a great heart. As it continues, other sounds layer in - the rustle of leaves, the drip of water, the hum of insects. Gradually, a voice emerges, speaking in harmonics that blend with the ambient sounds.]*

Roots-that-sing to branches-that-sway,
We are one, we are many, we are always.
Time flows like sap, slow and sweet,
In our veins, in our leaves, in our deep retreat.

*[The voice pauses, replaced by a series of clicking sounds and soft whistles]*

Visitor-from-above, we sense your presence,
Your breath, your warmth, your essence.
We welcome you to our living cathedral,
Where past and future are eternal.

*[A chorus of voices joins in, creating a layered harmony]*

We are the shapers, the tenders, the guardians old,
Our memories stored in rings of gold.
Each frond a sensor, each tendril an arm,
To nurture, to build, to keep from harm.

*[The harmony fades, leaving a single voice that seems to emanate from all directions]*

Listen closely to the cavern's song,
Feel the pulse of life, ancient and strong.
We are Chloromorph, plant and beast,
Keepers of balance, from greatest to least.

*[The voice begins to fade, blending back into the ambient sounds]*

Stay if you will, learn if you may,
But tread lightly in our realm, we pray.
For we are the Hollow, and the Hollow is us,
Eternal, alive, in harmony thus.

*[The transmission ends with a long, resonant hum that gradually fades into silence]*
