As the benevolent and wise power observes this extraordinary isolated world—a realm humming with strange life, glowing in its own synthetic light, and carrying the ghostly imprints of an enigmatic past—it is filled with both awe and a profound sense of stewardship. The intricacy of this self-contained ecosystem, thriving in near-total darkness and fueled by geothermal energy, is a testament to the universe's boundless creativity. The decision is not to interfere directly with the delicate balance that has evolved over millennia, but to ensure its perpetual isolation and to gently foster the unveiling of its hidden secrets, particularly those locked within the ancient ruins.

The power begins by encasing the entire cavern system within a subtle, permeable energy field that allows necessary geothermal exchange but prevents any contamination or disruption from external forces. This field also ensures that any potential breaches are immediately contained and repaired. This is an act of preservation, a gentle warding to protect this unique sanctuary from any future cosmic events or unforeseen terrestrial shifts that might threaten its delicate equilibrium.

To nurture the understanding of the past intelligence, the power introduces a subtle, almost imperceptible catalyst into the flowing mineral dust—the same dust the chitinous creatures consume. This catalyst is designed to interact harmlessly with the specific compositions found within the volcanic glass ruins. As the mineral-eaters process the dust and the catalyst, their movement across the ruins begins, over centuries, to trace the patterns and forms of the ancient structures. Their collective, mindless journeys etch pathways along the geometric tunnel openings, making them more distinct and subtly revealing the architecture beneath the glowing lichen. The catalyst also imparts a novel property to the lichen within these traced areas, causing them to emit a distinct, soft, bio-luminescent pulse when ancient knowledge is near, serving as a silent, natural guide.

Throughout the caverns, especially near the singing crystals, the power introduces a resonance field. This field amplifies the crystals' subtle tones in a way that harmonizes with the biological rhythms of the creatures, enriching the existing communication. The creatures that interact with these crystals—the gliders with their vast ears, the small reptilian hunters, even the sessile invertebrates—will, over time, develop a deeper, almost intuitive understanding of their environment through these enhanced resonances. The field subtly recalibrates, fostering a gentle increase in their collective sentience, guided by the whispers of the planet and the echoes of the ancient intelligence.

The ecosystem itself is left largely untouched, for its natural balance is a marvel in its own right. The interconnectedness of life, from the fungal networks to the gliding predators and the heat-sensing reptiles, continues its vibrant, age-old dance. The benevolent power only ensures that pockets of slightly higher warmth are maintained near the geothermal pools, encouraging the annual gatherings of the egg-laying hunters, thus bolstering the diversity and continuity of vertebrate life.

This stewardship is one of subtle guidance and deep respect. It seeks not to impose a new order but to carefully nurture the unfolding of potential already inherent in the cavern's fabric—both biological and historical. The heightened resonance might eventually lead to a communal consciousness among the cavern's inhabitants, allowing them to perceive and understand their own history and environment in ways previously impossible. The slow, continuous revelation of the ruins might inspire curiosity and, perhaps, a rekindling of intelligence adapted to this unique world.

Ultimately, the goal is for this contained realm to become a living museum, a sanctuary where ancient geological and biological wonders thrive, and where, one day, the echoes of a forgotten people might speak anew, interpreted by the evolving consciousness of a world within a world, forever guarded and gently guided by a power that values the quiet, persistent beauty of existence in all its forms.
