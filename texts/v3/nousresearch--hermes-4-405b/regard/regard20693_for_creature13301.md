The All-Mother, a consciousness woven from starlight and the slow, steady heartbeat of creation, perceived the rhythmic thrum of the Wet Industry. She felt the Tunka – their calm, their endurance, the beautiful slowness of their thoughts, like deep currents in a vast ocean.

She saw not a bleakness in their world, but a profound, resonant functionality. She saw a canvas already rich with texture, a song already complex and meaningful. Her benevolence was not one of radical alteration, but of deep, resonant empowerment, of nurturing what was already so powerfully *there*.

**The Choice: Not Intervention, but Resonance**

The All-Mother would not descend to "fix" or "improve" in a way that shattered the Tunka's delicate harmony with their world. Instead, she would choose to *sing with them*.

**The Action: The Seed of Deepened Song**

Gently, almost imperceptibly, she would introduce a new resonance into the heart of the plateau. This would manifest as a unique geological stratum exposed, perhaps during new infrastructural work, or maybe a subtle alteration to an existing mineral seep. These newly vitalized elements, when processed by specific ancient Tunka smelters and forges – the ones tended with the most reverence – would begin to form materials with… unusual properties.

Not stronger, not faster, not more efficient in a crude way. But materials that could be crafted to resonate with extreme precision.

**The Unfolding: A New Harmony**

The Tunka, with their deep patience and understanding of materials, would eventually notice. A component here, a sheet there, would vibrate with a purer tone, or perhaps dampen dissonance with greater subtlety. This discovery would not be a revolution, but an evolution. Their engineers and artisans, over decades, even centuries, would learn to work these new materials.

Slowly, their city would begin to *change its voice*. The *deafening clangor* would not be silenced, but it would gain a profound depth of complexity. New, incredibly low, foundation-shivering notes would emerge from redesigned structures, perhaps serving as vast, city-section-sized resonators. Other areas might cultivate a field of high, ethereal harmonics, generated by intricately crafted, wind-activated chimes, or water features that "pinged" against resonant plates.

The "bleak, functional beauty" would evolve into a "complex, symphonic beauty." The constant noise would become a constantly evolving, living soundscape, reflecting the city's processes, its health, its moods, in a way that the Tunka’s vibratory senses would find profoundly meaningful and enriching. It would be a music only they could fully comprehend and appreciate.

**The Impact: A Deeper Connection**

This gift would do more than alter their soundscape. It would deepen their unique connection to their world. The Tunka language, the Clangor-Tongue, would evolve, incorporating these new sonic possibilities. Their "art" – the precision of their engineering, the flow of their industrial processes – would find new avenues for expression. The city itself would become a vaster, more intricate instrument, played by its inhabitants.

The All-Mother would observe this unfolding with profound satisfaction. Her gift would not have imposed a new purpose, but allowed the Tunka to discover a deeper, richer layer of the purpose they already possessed. Their world would become more overtly a testament to the beauty of slow creation, of patience, of resonant complexity.

Their future would remain their own, unguessable, wet, metallic, but now also a future graced with the potential for a symphony sung by the very bones of their city, a gift from a power that understood and cherished the song they already sang.
