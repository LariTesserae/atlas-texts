The City of Frozen Moss would remain undisturbed. The benevolent and wise power, observing this place and its inhabitants, would recognize the delicate balance, the self-contained perfection, and the profound harmony that already exists within this hidden realm.

There would be no grand intervention, no sweeping changes imposed from above. Instead, the power would choose to act as a silent guardian, ensuring that the city’s seclusion remains intact. It would subtly reinforce the natural barriers that shield this pocket of life—the surrounding erosion that keeps the entrance narrow and hidden, the impassable walls of rock that define the city's limits. In this way, the power would protect the sanctity of the frozen moss and the quiet lives within, allowing them to continue their slow, rhythmic existence without interference.

The City of Frozen Moss, in its unchanging and self-sufficient state, needs no external benevolence—it already embodies a form of wisdom in its harmony with the environment. The wise power would understand that the greatest gift it could offer is the preservation of what already is, ensuring that this luminous, moss-carpeted world remains exactly as it is—an eternal, secret enclave.
