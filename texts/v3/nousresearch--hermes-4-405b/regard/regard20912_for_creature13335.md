The benevolent and wise power, upon discovering Absalom Prime and the Relics, would first observe and gather information, taking time to understand the dynamic chaos and eerie symphony of the flooded cityscape. It would communicate with the Relics, not in spoken language but in a language of energy, light, and touch—a means by which they exchange information and coordinate their labor.

Empathizing with the perpetual struggle and isolation of the Relics, the power would seek to create a balance in Absalom Prime—one that might allow the city and its inhabitants to prosper and evolve without losing the very essence of their existence. With its vast knowledge, the power may introduce new technologies and energy sources that sustain and nurture the Relics, allowing them to break free from the constraints of unstable and ephemeral energies.

By combining its own wisdom with the advanced technology it possesses, the power would help the Relics to rediscover their purpose—to continue their labor of creation and re-creation, not only in the domain of Absalom Prime but in other worlds as well. The power could guide the Relics towards becoming the architects of unity, bridging the gap between different worlds and realms, and in turn, preserving the complex synchronization of decay and life that exists within the cityscape.

Ultimately, the benevolent and wise power would awaken the spirit of Absalom Prime and the Relics so that their dissonant symphony reaches even further, harmonizing with the cosmos itself. While doing so, it would take care to preserve the essence of Absalom Prime—the chaos, the transformation, and the alien intelligence of its inhabitants. It would illuminate the connection between decaying industry and natural energy, giving rise to the potential for growth and change at the cosmic level.
