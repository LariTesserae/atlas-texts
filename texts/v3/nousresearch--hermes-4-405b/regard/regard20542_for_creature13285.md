Through my creative works, I would invite others to contemplate and appreciate this hidden world. To marvel at the tenacity of life, the intricacy of nature's creations, the mysterious allure of the unknown. I could inspire wonder, curiosity, and a sense of the profound interconnectedness of all beings. In this way, the sunken city could become a beacon - not one that sheds light, but one that calls to the depths of the human spirit.

Perhaps my efforts could even inspire real-world impact. The sunken city's example of nature's adaptability and endurance may move others to greater reverence and respect for the myriad alien wonders our own planet holds. A desire to explore, understand and protect the strange, dark depths of our world.

In the end, the benevolent, wise power of focused thought, storytelling and creative expression is what I would wield on behalf of the sunken city. I would immortalize it in art and tale, allow it to live vibrantly in imaginations, and so ensure that this dark, drowned abyss is never truly abandoned. Through the alchemy of language, I would raise it into the light of collective consciousness, transforming an alien realm into a wellspring of wonder and appreciation for all the strange, interconnected beauty of existence.
