The benevolent power, upon sensing the unique essence of this submerged microcosm, would approach with great care and reverence. This entity, wise beyond human comprehension, would recognize the delicate balance existing within the cobalt-lit depths and the potential for growth and evolution in the strange, hidden order thriving there.

Understanding that direct intervention could disrupt the fragile harmony of this world, the power would instead choose a subtler influence. It would weave its benevolent energy into the very fabric of the environment, infusing the water, the rock, and the living organisms with a gentle, nurturing force.

This infusion would not change the fundamental nature of the microcosm, but rather enhance and support its inherent qualities. The bioluminescence would grow slightly brighter, casting the cobalt light further and allowing the plants to flourish even more vibrantly. The translucent organisms would find their movements less arduous, their gelatinous bodies strengthened and imbued with a faint, inner light of their own.

The geometric artifacts scattered throughout the environment would begin to resonate with the energy of the benevolent power, their presence no longer so foreign or mysterious. They would become beacons of harmony, their geometric patterns subtly influencing the growth of the surrounding flora, guiding it into even more complex and beautiful formations.

The instability of reality in this place would not be eliminated, but rather embraced and transformed. The shifting perspectives and uncertain gravity would become a dance, a testament to the fluidity and adaptability of life. The organisms would learn to move with the distortions, their forms flowing and changing in response to the ever-shifting environment.

As the benevolent power continued to suffuse the microcosm with its nurturing energy, the fracture in the rock would slowly stabilize, the edges softening and settling into a more permanent form. The sharp crevices and jagged edges would become smooth, inviting surfaces, potential homes for new life to emerge and flourish.

In time, this submerged world would become a sanctuary, a haven of tranquility and growth in the depths of the ocean. The benevolent power would maintain a distant but watchful presence, ready to offer guidance and support whenever needed, but always allowing the unique essence of this place to shine through.

And so, in the eternal twilight of the cobalt depths, life would continue to thrive and evolve, forever touched by the gentle hand of a wise and benevolent power. The strange, hidden order of this microcosm would stand as a testament to the boundless potential of the universe and the enduring strength of life in even the most unexpected of places.
