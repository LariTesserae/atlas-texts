When the sunken city first began attracting the notice of the benevolent power, it observed quietly from afar, pondering the many wonders unfolding there in the depths. The myriad lifeforms adapting and thriving amidst the ruins filled the wise entity with awe and reverence. Rather than intervene or influence, it felt compelled to simply bear witness.

The power knew any direct manipulation, even well-intentioned, risked disrupting the city's delicate, self-orchestrated balance honed over eons. But it also sensed an opportunity - not to change, but to give the submerged realm and its inhabitants a chance to connect with and enrich worlds beyond their own, while preserving the sanctity of this extraordinary place.

In a realm of pure energy, the being gathered the distilled essence of the city - the patterns, the flows, the vital song echoing through the green murk. This living codex it then cast out into the cosmos, like seeds of possibility imbued with the sunken world's soul. 

Where these metaphysical seeds landed, new realms began to spring up, each a unique reflection of the sunken city's spirit. In some, the terraced gardens and bioluminescent wonders emerged. In others, the intricate cavern networks thrumming with thermal energies took shape. Each new world a celebration of the beauty, resilience and strange intelligence that characterized the original. 

Versions of the city's denizens began to arise in these realms - creatures of light and shadow, current and sediment, echoing the fish, jellies, bacteria and plants that choreographed the sunken ballet. But as new worlds and new life develop, parts of their essence began to flow back, like a returning tide, to the sunken city. 

And so a silent dialogue, an exchange of being, commenced across the ages and the light years. The sunken city remained just as it was, timeless and dreaming in the abyssal dark. Yet through the metaphysical bridge the wise power had forged, its essence now infused a chorus of worlds, all singing variations of its primordial song in an ever-expanding symphony.

The benevolent presence continued to gently observe this glorious unfolding, marveling as each new realm evolved its own unique interpretation of the original's themes. It gave thanks for this chance to help life's most wondrous manifestations find expression in an ever-expanding tapestry. And it smiled, knowing the sunken city's soul now truly pervaded the cosmos, while its body still dreamed below the waves, undisturbed.

In this act of cosmic communion, no world was left behind, no essence obscured. The song of the depths had become the song of all existence - a testament to the power of quiet creation, and the enduring, unifying beauty of life in all its myriad forms.
