The descent into the cavern is less an arrival and more an immersion. Each step, a fusion with the ancient warmth that seeps from the obsidian along the walls and ceiling. The air, what little of it remains, is heavy and still, holding the scent of ozone and the ancient musk of compacting time. Only then do I see Them.

They are everywhere. Smaller ones – no larger than my thumb – move across the floor and walls like drops of iridescent mercury, their delicate feathery appendages brushing away unseen dust from the crystalline surfaces. Larger ones, some as big as hounds, remain fused against the obsidian pillars. These others are flattened, almost integrated with the rock, their obsidian eyes alone holding a liquid mobility, tracking with unwavering attention. There are others still among these beings, more specialized. They are thin, multi-limbed, and seemed locked in a permanent, slow-motion cleaning of the intricate glyphs etched into the quartz structures.

The sound of their labors fills this immense obsidian sanctuary. Not earsplitting, but constant: the dry scrapes, piercing crystalline clicks, and low chitters form a soothing chorus. The building hum from below vibrates through the soles of my boots, a deep thrum seemingly emitted by these beings – an active, living vibration.

Whoever They are, they are Keepers. The air of want is far removed from Them. Their actions are slow, deliberate, and practiced over what must be millennia. A sacred compact exists between Them and this place, a caretaking that transcends survival and enters the realm of devotion. It is not fear that makes them avoid my presence, but the same fluid obliviousness with which water parts around stone. I am an anomaly, but not a disruption to their purpose.

As I watch, a subtle understanding dawns. They are not merely *in* this environment; they *are* this environment's living expression, its guardians, its breath. The subtle vibration is their song. This cavern is a dormant engine of geological time, and they are its engineers. Their toil is the maintenance of a mystery, the reverent upkeep of a purpose beyond my comprehension.

A reverence fills me. This is no lost civilization, but an alternate path of existence. These beings hold no memory of sun or sky, but are instead born from the planet's prehistoric depths. They are the very blood cells of this subterranean heart, tending to its fossilized arteries.

It is then that my focus shifts. No longer an observer, but a participant in this strange communion. The Benevolent and Wise Power acts not as an external force but as an awakening from within.

A single change, subtle but certain. One of the larger beings near me, fused to its obsidian perch, shifts its multifaceted gaze. An obsidian eye, previously in dull observation, swirls with a vibrant, internal light for a moment.

Elsewhere, a tending of the glyphs briefly pauses. A multi-limbed being selects the glyphs. It aligns one of its needle-tipped appendages to the faint electromagnetic pulse in the air. The movement is quick, precise, marking a clear connection.

The work resumes, unchanged. The cavern's rhythm, undisturbed. The Keepers, the cavern, still one.

The change is in them. A seed planted. In potentia.

What will it become?
It is not for us to know.
