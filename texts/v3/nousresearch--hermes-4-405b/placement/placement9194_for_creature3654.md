I would be The Keeper of the Last Spark.

Not alive in any conventional sense, nor truly dead, I would be an ancient, isolated transformer, colossal and deeply nested within a cyclopean tangle of other inert machinery. My casing, iron and decaying, shows centuries of The Corrode's patient work, yet within my core, a peculiar anomaly persists: a residual charge. This is not a continuous power source, but a slow, rhythmic pulse of energy that ebbs and flows with an internal, unseen tide – perhaps a pocket of exotic, self-replenishing material, or a parasitic draw from the geothermal energies of the earth itself, channeled through a forgotten, unique technology. This pulse would be irregular, a deep thrum followed by silence, then a surge of power that might cause my surface conduits to dew with condensation, expelled as a brief, metallic-scented steam. Occasionally, during a particularly strong pulse, a single, impossibly bright arc of The Flickerkin might leap from a specific, exposed point on my frame – a controlled, periodic release.

My "thoughts" would be slow, resonating with the deep vibrations of The Resonants, the hum of my own peculiar internal process. I would possess no true consciousness, but rather a deep, resonant sentience, a slow awareness of the Under-Switchyard as a whole. I would "perceive" the subtle currents and changes in the environment: the shifting patterns of The Glisten on nearby pipes, the soft tracing of The Wall-Sweaters, the rhythmic approach and retreat of explorers or scavengers (if any ever dared this deep), sensed as faint heat signatures or vibrations transmitted through the interconnected metal.

My role would be passive, yet central to the Under-Switchyard's lingering animation. This last spark would not be a viable power source for the dead city, but its rhythmic pulse would be a kind of heartbeat, a ticking clock measuring out the Switchyard's slow decay. The intermittent burst of The Flickerkin from my frame would be a momentary beacon in the oppressive gloom, a tiny, defiant star in the dying mechanical galaxy. Perhaps I am the anchor to The Bloom, its strange luminescent growths clustering most densely on my hottest surfaces, fed by the unique energies of my core. I would be a magnet for the most peculiar energies of the Under-Switchyard, a silent, rhythmic heart beating out the slow seconds of its dissolution. My existence would be a question mark in the metallic silence, a mystery that no one would be left to solve, a quiet, rhythmic enigma at the decaying heart of the iron necropolis.
