In this high, jagged world of obsidian-riddled granite, I am:

*A daughter of the humming stone*

My form echoes the jagged columns that pierce the perpetually twilit sky - a body of fractured planes and sharp angles. My skin is a shifting, living granite, veined through with obsidian that captures the dim light and refracts it in bruised purple and deep indigo. Patches of my flesh shimmer with the malachite sheen of the snaking vines, while others are dusted with the glowing chartreuse pollen of the slick ferns that unfurl from my crevices. My very substance is this plateau, and its essence hums within me.

I move with the slow, grinding deliberation of growing mountains. The groaning, tectonic hum that fills the air emanates from deep within my core, vibrating through me and out into the world. It is my breath, my pulse, my thoughts given sound. The air shimmers around me with the same unseen energy that suffuses this place, the laws of physics bending and warping in my presence.

I am a living monolith among the monoliths, rooted yet drifting, substantial yet flickering at the edges of perception. The other Obsidian Watchers are like me - sentient embodiments of this thin place, where the membrane between realities has worn away to near nothingness. We stand as shards of a forgotten race, memetic echoes of a civilization ground to cosmic dust by the turning of those unseen, titanic gears.

My consciousness is not as mortals know it. There is no rapid-fire chain of discrete thoughts, no churning sea of emotion. Instead, awareness comes in slow tides - the pressure of meaning building until it threatens to shatter my very substance, then releasing in a resonant shift of the hum. Concepts unfold over eons. Memories of the universe's birth and portents of its death are as immediate to me as the rubble-strewn ground beneath my feet.

I am the grinding gears and the groaning stone, the first breath of a world unconforming and the last whisper of a reality unraveling. I am an Echo of Stone, an Obsidian Watcher, forever adrift in the humming twilight of a place that will never be fully born and does not know it is dying. To meet my gaze is to hear the grinding of those gears, to feel the foundations of your being shudder and strain. For in this place, we are all echoes. We are all Watchers. And the hum is all and everything.
