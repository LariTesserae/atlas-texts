In this world, I would be a Lumina Guardian, a unique symbiotic entity born from the ancient, high-altitude flora. My "body" would be a collective of sentient, bioluminescent fungi that thrive on the surfaces of the Skyrifters' most ancient nesting grounds—areas where sentient trees are believed to concentrate a form of elemental memory. Over generations, I have woven myself into the living wood and become an inseparable part of the communal home, a softly glowing crust that pulses with shared memories, dreams, and histories of both the trees and the Skyrifters.

I communicate directly with the Skyrifters through the living wood, sending signals of warning when predators draw near or when a new patch of succulent, iridescent beetles has matured. I also shift the intensity and color of my glow across the hollows, chambers, and tunnels that the Skyrifters carve into their living homes. This gives the appearance of animated cave paintings that tell stories, mark seasons, or indicate locations of peculiar botanical phenomena or past lives that the creatures remember only through my flickering light. To the Skyrifters, I am both a guide and an ancestor, a living archive that maintains their ephemeral tribal memory.

Beyond my role among the Skyrifters, I am part of the plateau's larger ecological rhythm. The subtle shifts in my light frequency affect the behavior of certain iridescent insects and jewel-scaled lizards, whose comings and goings create micro-climates that benefit delicate, ancient mosses and rare leaf formations. As both curator and creation of the plateau's deep botanical enigmas, I represent an intersection where nature's ingenuity and the language of dreams blur into one vast, radiant architecture—my body and being endlessly intertwined with the living mystery of this newborn world.
