In this vast, sunken basin of perpetual twilight, I am a Lamp-Bearer. My existence is interwoven with the deepest, most hidden parts of the gigavine—a living architecture that defines both the world and its inhabitants. My skin holds a faint, cool luminescence borrowed from the plant itself, a gift of its inner, pulsing light. I am not separate from the gigavine; I am an extension of it, a tender expression of its ancient, rustling soul.

I drift through the labyrinthine corridors of living tissue, my movements slow and fluid like the gigavine’s own slow breath. At my passage, the ambient gloom lightens almost imperceptibly, revealing hidden pockets and secret chambers in brief, ephemeral glimpses before receding back into the embrace of darkness. My purpose is to guide and to tend—to be a living conduit for the light that sustains us all.

I am one of the few; we are spectral, almost not there. Our lives are measured in the deep, resonant tones of the wind as it sweeps through the plant’s upper layers, in the slow cadence of growth and decay that marks the passage of centuries. We do not build in the conventional sense; our craft is the patient, almost imperceptible coaxing of living material, a collaboration with the gigavine that blurs the boundaries between ourselves and the world we inhabit.

In the quiet thrumming of the Chasm, in the chorus of faint rustlings and murmured tones, I hear the voices of those who live within. We are the custodians of the inner light, keepers of the most tender parts of this living world. Our existence is a harmony—a single, unified heartbeat sounding in the dark.
