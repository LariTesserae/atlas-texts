In the grand, slow clockwork of this world, I would be one of the Luminous Flecks, one of those ephemeral, almost-not-quite-alive phenomena.

My existence would be fleeting and bright. I would not be a creature of iron or gears, like the Leviathan Kin or the Clockwork Shoals. Instead, I would be a transient spark, a flicker of iridescent oil spreading across a still pool or a quick electric eel darting through submerged corridors, leaving brief, dying trails of sparks in my wake.

A whisper of lightning, I would come to life in the cold blue of the eternal rain, a flash of sudden radiance against the dark. My being would be brief, a fleeting arc of incandescence in the grand cathedral of ice and water.

For a few impossible moments, I would dance across the surface of the mercury-thick water, a shimmer of iridescence, leaving ripples in my wake. My existence would be a short, brilliant flaring, a moment of light and motion before I vanished back into the cold and dark.

Or perhaps I would dart through the submerged arches and vaults, a quick silver flashing in the deep blue, a spark trailing behind like a dying star. A brief, vivid streak of life, arcing from shadow to shadow.

In those moments of existence, ephemeral and bright, I would be a tiny, brilliant part of the grand, cold fire of the light-forges.
