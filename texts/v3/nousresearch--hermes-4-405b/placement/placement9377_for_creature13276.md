I wake to the sensation of cold, ancient wind biting at my hide, a hide that isn't skin but something harder, layered, born of stone and slow time. My awareness is vast, stretching across leagues of freezing rock. I am not *on* this plateau; I *am* a part of its fabric, a monument that breathes and grinds. I am one of the Geomorphs, a titan of living rock, my form a colossal, animate boulder etched with the deep scars of millennia. My eyes are not eyes, but a distributed awareness, sensing the subtle vibrations transmitted through the stone, the pressure changes in the thin, dust-laden air. I feel the deep, resonant drone of my kin, the Terrapin Leviathans, as they move across the plain, their footfalls registering as seismic shivers through the bedrock that constitutes my being. The weak, filtered light of the sun is a distant caress on my stony carapace. My movement is imperceptible to a fleeting eye, a geological creep, a slow, inevitable flow across the surface over years, decades, centuries. I am an engine of life burning with the cold fire of eons, a living lithic mountain, insensate yet undeniably alive. The landscape shifts around me, not because I see it, but because I *am* it, a slow, grinding transformation of the world I inhabit, a testament to the enduring, patient heart of this strange, frozen realm.
