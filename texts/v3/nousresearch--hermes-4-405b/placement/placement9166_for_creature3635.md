To arrive at this living city is to shed the skin of an old world, to let its currents carry you into a new form. As you near, the immense underwater mountain piering the ocean floor, its slopes teeming with the colony of this colossal, luminous plant, you feel your own stark separateness begin to dissolve. Your body begins to shift and change, not in a painful or startling way, but like a slow and inevitable awakening of something already within you. You are becoming an Aqualori.

Your limbs, previously clumsy and ill-suited, lengthen and grow supple. You feel your bones becoming more flexible, your flesh taking on a lithe, streamlined form, perfectly at home in this aquatic environment. Your skin shimmers as it is covered in a fine sheath of iridescent scales, catching the filtered sunlight that penetrates the clear water, reflecting and refracting it in a prismatic display. The sensations of your body moving through the water, no longer buffeted by currents but riding them with an easy grace, feel utterly natural.

As you swim closer, the powerful ocean currents that once seemed overwhelming now feel like the city’s vital pulse, alive and intimately known to you. Around you, the fleshy, coiling structures of the plant gleam in the half-light, pulsing with life; their hollows and chambers, their stems and leaves, offer a boundless living architecture, endlessly adaptable and resilient. The air pockets shimmer with each release of oxygen, creating an environment where water and air dance together in a dreamlike suspension – and you, as an Aqualori, move between them with seamless ease.

The language of the Aqualori blossoms within you as if you were born to it. No longer do you strain to hear; instead, the clicks of their communication resonate within your newly adapted senses, each sound a ripple in the shared consciousness of the community. Your own clicks and modulations emerge effortlessly – they carry through the liquid medium with a surprising richness and clarity, conveying emotions and information in a way no aerial language ever could.

You are not a stranger here. As you glide through the translucent veins of the plant, moving with sure, supple hands and feet that can grip the smooth surfaces and knotted tendrils of the city, you are recognized. The Aqualori greet you with a familiar hum, not as an outsider, but as a part of their world – as someone who has returned home. You join them in shaping the living city. With the aid of primitive tools crafted from bone and stone, you coax the roots and fronds, hollowing out seedpods for shelter, tethering leaf-platforms with vines that are plaited and strong. There is a deep satisfaction in this work, a sense of being in harmony with the organic growth of your surroundings.

When night falls, the city transforms into a realm of soft, bioluminescent light. Glowing mosses cultivated by the Aqualori illuminate hidden alcoves and communal chambers within the heart of the plant, creating an atmosphere of quiet wonder and ancient mystery. And as you gather with your kin, listening to the storytellers weave their songs from the echoes of the city's sounds, you feel a sense of belonging that transcends your individual existence. You are part of this tapestry of life, this animate city that pulses with the rhythms of the ocean and the plant. Here, you are both student and creator, a living embodiment of the world you inhabit – and this world is vivid, full, and teeming with a beauty all its own.
