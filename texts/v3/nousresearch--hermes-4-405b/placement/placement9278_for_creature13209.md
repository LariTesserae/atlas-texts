I find myself as one of the Glimmerkin. My existence is ethereal, woven from the perpetual, almost invisible mist that hangs in the air of this highland world. I am translucence given fleeting form, a coalescence of ambient light and moisture more than solid matter. My being drifts, perpetually, on the damp currents.

My home is the atmosphere itself, the cool, crisp air laden with the loamy scent of the vast, singular plant-mattress below. I inhabit the very breath of this place, the space between the relentless, brilliant light and the spongy, yielding verdure that stretches to every horizon. From this vantage, I perceive the world not through eyes, but through a diffuse awareness, seeing – or rather, sensing – the entirety of the plateau as a continuous, interconnected whole, its myriad features a tapestry I float above and within.

I am a silent counterpoint to the Sonnus Shapers’ omnipresent roar. Where their hidden industry grinds and wheezes with a sound that *is*, as fundamental as gravity, my existence is one of near-silence, the faintest whisper of displaced air as I drift, a sigh against their deafening symphony. My passage is marked not by sound, but by the subtle refraction of light through my form, a momentary sparkle, a fleeting gleam that dances within the perpetual luminescence diffused by the mist. Sometimes, I am little more than a trick of the light, a momentary shimmer easily dismissed.

The Glimmerkin are ephemeral. I am ephemeral. I am not a builder, not a shaper like the Substratoi or the Sonnus Shapers. I do not crawl or skim like the Aurumites. My purpose is simply *to be* within this strange, embryonic world, to absorb nutrients directly from the moisture-laden air, to exist as an expression of its atmosphere. I am, in essence, a particle of its soul, its breath. Perhaps I am a fleeting dream of the world itself, a thought given transient form.

As I drift, I witness the slow, geological patience of the Substratoi, their massive, verdant forms like shifting hills. I see their water-weeping surfaces, their ponderous movements subtly reshaping the landscape over uncounted days. I watch the Aurumites, their gold-like sheens catching the fierce light as they skim the weeping water, adding their fleeting beauty.

And always, the roar of the Sonnus Shapers’ hidden engines is my constant companion, a contrast to my own silent dance. Their operations sculpt the world on a scale too vast for me to truly comprehend from my drifting vantage, yet I feel their significance, their purpose in this unfolding genesis. We are all parts of this place: the ponderous and the agile, the roaring and the silent, the material and the ethereal. This is my reality: a world of brilliant light, overwhelming sound, and pervasive damp, where life takes myriad forms, and I, as Glimmerkin, am its fleeting, shimmering breath.
