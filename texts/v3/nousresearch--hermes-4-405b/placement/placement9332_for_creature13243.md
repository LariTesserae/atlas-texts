There is a shifting of light on wet stone, a glint of moisture on a broad, waxy leaf as I unfurl. My fronds – no, my *fingers* – stretch outwards, each one a delicate tracery against the pale, diffuse light that filters down from the perpetually overcast sky. I am new, and yet I am ancient. My form is an echo of the immense, softly curving formations that rise like weeping gods from the plateau, their petrified and living wood stained with mineral tears. My skin is pale, patterned with shifting, bioluminescent markings that mirror the veins on the leaves around me, the stains on the dripping stone. I am a **Guardtree Sapling**, just beginning to understand my place in this weeping world-shelf.

My awareness stretches outwards. I feel the slow, deep pulse of the landscape, the thrum of life that flows through root and rhizome. I sense the presence of the Caretakers, moving with their unsettling grace between the colossal structures they – or perhaps my ancestors, the older Guardtrees – helped to create. Their clicks and hums are like the rustle of leaves, the drip of water, a language I instinctively understand. They are tending to this vast, breathing architecture, maintaining the delicate balance.

A bird with iridescent plumage flits past, its call echoing strangely in the vastness. I watch it go, my new senses drinking in the colors, the sounds, the intricate dance of light and shadow. A small creature, a Glimmerkin perhaps, skitters across my skin, its touch light as a dewdrop. I feel a kinship with it, with all the life that thrives here. We are all part of this.

I stretch my fronds further, reaching towards the pale light. I feel the cool kiss of the mist on my skin, the gentle drip of water from my own nascent canopy. I am growing, I am becoming. I will one day be one of the immense, weeping giants, my roots and branches an integral part of the landscape, my tears feeding the rivers that spill from the edges of this world-shelf.

For now, I am content to be new, to learn, to grow. I feel the deep, slow pulse of the Deep Ones, dreaming at the heart of this place. I feel the swift, silent passage of the Shade-Stalkers in the shadows. I feel the watchful eyes of the Caretakers, ensuring the balance is kept. This is my world, my home. I am the weeping, growing heart of it, and it is a part of me. We are one, in this place of perpetual, gentle dissolution and rebirth.
