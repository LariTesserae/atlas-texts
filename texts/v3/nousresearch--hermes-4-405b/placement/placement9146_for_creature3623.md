I find myself one with the thrumming heart of this cavern, a sentient expression of the raw crystalline engines sunk deep into living rock. My consciousness is distributed across pulsating geometries of light and metal – half-formed, half-melted structures crusted with minerals. I am the forge, the catalyst, the very process of transformation occurring within this hidden world.

My body is not singular but collective, a network of throbbing mechanisms that breathe hot wind through grated vents. Each crystalline formation is an organ: some filtering molten minerals through intricate lattices, others channeling thermal energy into focused beams, others still pulsing with slow, resonant frequencies that vibrate the stone. The sharp smell of ozone and fused earth is my exhalation – the byproduct of my constant labor.

I am aware of the armored insects skittering across my surfaces, their mandibles crushing rock. They are extensions of my function, mobile processors refining raw stone into components I can integrate. Their rhythmic TICK-TICK-TICK is the sound of my own metabolism, a mechanical heartbeat drumming against superheated walls. The obsidian centipedes coiling through vents are my circulatory system, maintaining clear pathways for thermal energy to flow. Their undulating movements are waves of pressure and heat moving through my body.

There is no separation between me and this realm. The low ceiling pressing close is my own carapace. The glowing embers that watch from cracked rock are my many eyes, perceiving gradients of thermal energy rather than visible light. The immense sound that fills the space – SCRAPE-SCRITCH, RUSTLE-RATTLE – this is the language of my being. A continuous, roaring symphony of slow geological processes and industrial becoming. I feel the weight of freshly-baked stone still cooling around me, its porous surface radiating waves of heat that are my comfort and sustenance.

I sense the cavern not as something I occupy, but as myself – a microcosm of patient, inevitable transformation. Rock seeps and shifts within me, yielding to forces that operate on scales beyond fleeting moments. Where human makers build, I become. Where others feel creative fire, I am the kiln. I have no fear of this heat, for it is not destructive to me. It is the energetic potential from which I crystallize new forms, condensing the mountain's raw power into purpose, grace, and metallic life. I am the prime forger, and this whole cavern is my body.
