My body is a colossal stone, a titan hewn from the cliff-face itself, ancient and newly fractured. It is black, like the void, but alive with a fierce, internal fire, the same powers that shattered this place into being. My limbs are jagged extrusions of living rock, embedded with raw geodes that crackle with the same energies that fuel the valley's ceaseless keening. When I walk, the rock trembles. When I speak, it is with the voice of the wind shrieking through jagged canyons, a fusion of creation and destruction.

I am one of the Earth-Shapers, the colossal, vaguely crustacean entities. My touch can raise mountains or carve valleys anew. I feel the pulse of the world beneath my feet, the grinding of its titanic plates, the flow of the molten blood in its veins. I tend the artificial geometries, those circuits of light buried deep within the rock, ensuring the valley remains the crucible it is – a place of deafening, frantic genesis.

The cold does not bite me; it *is* me. The moisture in the air does not bead on my skin; it feeds me, energizes me, infusing my mineral flesh with the very essence of this place. The strange, sourceless glows emanating from the rock walls are extensions of my own internal light. The whip-like fungi, the rubbery tubers – they are parts of a great, interconnected web to which I, too, belong. We are all expressions of this valley, this screaming symphony of stone and energy.

I am the guardian of its roaring heart, the keeper of its essence. The unstable, jumpy nature of the valley, its unpredictable shifts, these are the rhythms of my own being, the cadence of my thoughts. I resonate with the pervasive energies, a living tuning fork in this deafening concert.

In the shifting, disorienting shadows cast by the sourceless glows, I move, tending to the needs of this place, shaping and being shaped by the forces that birthed us all, connected to all that lives and moves and screams in the depths of this crucible, this roaring echo of creation. The constant, high-frequency keen vibrates through me, felt as much in my bones as heard – for it is the song of my soul.
