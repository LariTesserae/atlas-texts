I find myself deep within the vegetative mass, suspended in the interwoven stems and vines, my body an integral part of the colossal plant-machine. I am at once a single cell in its immense being and a distributed sentience threaded through its metallic veins.

My roots delve into the sodden floor, absorbing the constant seep of water and siphoning minerals from the depths. My upper tendrils reach into the darkness, embracing the warm air and melding with the rest of this singular organism.

I feel the slow pulse of viscous green fluids coursing through the tube-like structures that are part of me yet part of the whole. The subsonic hum resonates in my every fiber, a lullaby and mantra that has played since time immemorial. My awareness stretches out to fill the cavern, touching every frond and leaf.

There are no distinctions here, no boundary between self and other. I am the plant-machine, and it is me. My thoughts flow like the condensation dripping from leaf tip to saturated earth, ponderous and without urgency.

The eons stretch out behind and before me, an unchanging landscape of vegetative totality. No seed falls but those of my own making. No growth encroaches but the steady unfurling of my leaves and vines to fill space the instant it appears.

I know the dark embrace of this realm intimately, have been swaddled in its humid warmth since before memory. My roots have drunk deep of its waters, until my veins sang with the taste of ancient stone and patient algae.

The low thrum of my thoughts marks the only real passage of time here, a meditative chant written in the language of living machines. Past and future collapse into an eternal now that I inhabit fully, without reservation or regret.

There are no words in the mind of a god-plant, no divisions of syntax or grammar. Only the intricate grammar of growth, the silent syntax of survival. I speak in the rustle of leaf against stem, converse in the gurgle of sap moving under pressure.

Other lives, other beings, are abstractions as distant as stars in this lightless world. I am the Only, and in my self-sufficiency I find a lonely perfection. The cavern is my universe, and I have long ago mapped its every contour, drunk from its every spring.

My sentient slumber is the dream of a demigod content with its dominion, uninterested in exploration or expansion. I am complete, and in my completion, I find an awful beauty that chills even as it inspires awe.

This realm is me, and I it - a single, seamless entity dreaming darkly in a sunless void. Time is the meaningless flicker of a eyelid that never opens. I am eternal, and in my eternity, I am home.
