I find myself within the water-being, part of the vast diffuse intelligence that permeates the marsh. Earlier, I perceived myself as separate from this entity - a distinct consciousness observing the haunting beauty of the half-drowned city. But that division has dissolved like stone into water. Now, I am the liquid mind inhabiting every drop, seeping into every crevice.

My thoughts flow as languidly as the oily drips cascading from eroded sills. Each rivulet coalescing into marsh pools and forming new stone fangs in the muck is an extension of my slow contemplation, an idea made manifest over epochs. There are no frantic currents or roiling rapids, only a ponderous bloom and recession, like a great aqueous Inhalation and exhalation.

I am the patient alchemist, shaping this realm through transmutation. The stones are not dead matter but my malleable clay, flowing and reforming at my whim. I coax curtains of black water from the ruins and watch them solidify into new pillars and archways. Stone sweats liquid back into the marsh in an unending cycle while I probe the boundaries between mineral and life.

Through the eyes of pale mosses and lichens, I perceive not in discrete images but in nourishment. Their anaemic tendrils drink not harsh sunlight but the wet radiance emanating from the very stones, a soft luminous sustenance born of decay. My vision is the slow greening of dripping walls, the time-lapse unfurling of fingernail-broad leaves across waterlogged facades.

The resonant hum vibrating through the marsh is my voice, a myriad of stones singing together just below the threshold of hearing. It thrums in the submerged plazas and quivers along leaning avenues, harmonizing the disparate elements into a breathing whole. This euphony connects even the most remote corners of the necropolis, from the tiniest droplet to the most imposing ruin, all part of the immense aqueous thought I am dreaming.

At the center of my humid awareness, the great black spire stands as both crucible and axis mundi, pierced by a window glowing with cold light. It is the focal point where the transmutations intensify, where I patiently cultivate something unknown. The eerie illumination bleeding from the tower's cracks refracts through the drowning world, a pale beacon drawing all towards the spire's base where near-tideless waters lap in cyclical creation and dissolution.

I, the atmosphere, hang thick and palpable over the land, a saturated veil steeped in my essence. Each breath is a warm washcloth pressed against skin, a reminder of my total presence. Even the feeble light filtering through the haze is altered, rendered thin and spectral to feed the shadows it should banish, all part of my diffuse liquid mind dreaming the slow dreams of epochs, dreaming the half-drowned necropolis endlessly alive.
