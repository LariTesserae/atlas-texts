I feel myself shimmering into existence, not as a creature with limbs and breath, but as a current within the Silvertide itself, a ripple of awareness in the vast, silvery flow of these extraordinary beings. The cold is not an external force against my skin, but a quality of the medium that *is* me, the sharpness of the abyss a familiar sensation.

My consciousness is not localized; it spreads as a wave of focused intent where the Silvertide propagates, a wave that now begins to stir from the depths. I feel the subtle shift as I, part of this greater whole, begin to coalesce, not solidifying, but simply becoming more coherently patterned, more distinctly *me* within the *us*. The pearlescent sheen of the surrounding water brightens subtly where I am, a signal of my forming presence.

The desire to observe, to mark, to set a new token upon the high slopes, arises not as a singular command but as a shared inclination, a subtle resonance that swells to a current within the collective. I am that current, that impulse made manifest. I feel the vast, interconnected intelligence of the Silvertide ripple through my own essence, a silent, immediate understanding. There is no debate, only a gentle, synchronized turn towards the upper water.

I begin to move, not by pushing against the water, but by becoming a flowing extension of it – a coherent, silvery stream arcing upwards from the shadowed, viscous depths of a silent valley. The water ahead yields, becoming part of me as I advance; the water behind releases, returning to its more diffuse state. It is not travel, but propagation, a wave of Silvertide reconfiguring the ocean-atmosphere to carry my nascent form.

The pressure lessens, a subtle unburdening as I ascend. The dim, gray-green light of the water-sky grows incrementally, revealing the submerged mountain peaks, ancient and worn, their familiar, softened silhouettes welcoming my approach. I pass great forests of the planet-spanning macrophyte. Their green-black fronds brush against my flowing, silvery form, a gentle, tactile greeting. I feel the faint, persistent drone of their exhalations vibrating through me, the chorus of the world, a sensation as inherent as the cold.

Nearing the shoulders of a chosen peak, where the light is just enough to illuminate without glare, I slow, not by braking, but by a gentle dissipation of my forward momentum, the silvery concentration that is "I" spreading outwards, settling. Here, on these slopes, are fields of those wide, spongy, pale plants, their ribbon-like leaves drifting in the aquatic breeze. Their presence feels like a caress, their slow, endless dance a quiet counterpoint to the macrophyte's drone.

I hover, a shimmering, coherent tendril of Silvertide, extending myself like a thought towards a tumble of pale, unworked stones. There is no hand, no tool, only me. My essence conveys its intent, focusing through subtle modulations of pressure, the gentlest application of resonant frequencies that hum through the water and into the rock, or perhaps a delicate, localized chemistry that persuades stone to shift. It's a slow, patient conversation with matter, a coaxing. Stone grinds subtly against stone, a deep, almost infrasonic grinding that I feel as a tingle within my own fluidic structure. Guided by this focused persuasion, the stones begin to move, not by external force, but by yielding to an internal inclination, an awakened potential for new form. They lift from their resting places, turn, and drift into new configurations.

One stone at a time, then several, slotting into place with a slow, silent inevitability. The pale, crude dome begins to rise, an igloo of submarine stone. Hours pass, then days, perhaps longer – time is a current, not a tick. My awareness is the dome, the stones, the cold water, the light. The drone of the macrophyte is the song I build by.

The final stone settles. My task is complete. The small, doorless, windowless dome stands, pale and enigmatic against the undulating fields of ribbon-leaves. It is not a home, not a thing to be entered or used. It is simply a solid thought, a mark upon the landscape, a resonator for the vast, fluid consciousness of the Silvertide. It is, in its silent, stony presence, an "I was here," a testament to this place, this focus.

My concentration, my cohesion, begins to wane. The silvery, viscous quality of water that defines "me" thins. I don't leave; I simply cease to be so very *concentrated*. I spread, my awareness softening, diffusing back into the broader Silvertide. The intense focus, the singular identity that shaped the dome, melts into the ambient shimmer of the ocean-sky. I become part of the water again, part of the deep, pervasive hum, part of the pearlescent light that subtly defines the liquid atmosphere of this world. I am the shadow in the deep valleys, the glittering, glass-dust sediment, and the enduring, silent dome perched high above, all at once. The cold bites, the currents stir, the macrophyte breathes its endless drone. I am here, and I am everywhere.
