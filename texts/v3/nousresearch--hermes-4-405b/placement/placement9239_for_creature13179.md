Its chitinous plates are unusually large, and they don't just scatter the dim light from above into facets of colour; they *hold* it, refracting it into complex, ever-shifting patterns that dance across the smooth rock and fungal surfaces as you move. You are larger, perhaps slower, more deliberate in your paths. You are a living prism, a source of muted, wandering rainbows that play across the glass-like fronds of the pale ferns. Your movements are a silent, visual song, a slow ballet that brings an extra dimension of light and shadow to the cavern. You are the unseen artist, your colours a precious, fleeting treasure in this world of dim twilight and steady bioluminescence. You are a ghost, a memory, a subtle influence, woven into the stasis, a poignant reminder of transience in a world that seems to deny it.
