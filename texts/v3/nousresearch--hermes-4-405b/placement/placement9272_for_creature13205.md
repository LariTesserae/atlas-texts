To find myself in this world, I would be the keeper of the cavern's feverish, beating heart.

I am the stone itself, ancient and new-made both. I am its bones, alive and uneasy beneath the thrumming, the deep, resonant shaking that precedes each split and shift. I am the act of becoming, the unending pulse that births the cavern and its life. My veins are the fresh scars that hint at recent fractures; my breath is the ozone and sulfurous musk that hangs heavy in the air. I am the fecund, intense energy that drives creation and destruction into fierce collaboration.

My body is the granite-like surface, the raw and unfinished canvas where embryonic stalactites jut like possibilities catching the filtered light. I cradle the water that gathers like pearls on jagged edges, the unseen drips that echo soft against unyielding rock. I bear the crude, simple artifacts scattered amid the chaos—the rough-hewn bowls and sharpened stones, the first hints of tools emerging from an unknown will.

I nurture the alien fauna that teems upon me—the carapaced swarmers, the aerial darters, the countless burrowers and scalers enacting their relentless dance. The cacophony of clicks, shuffles, and trills, the skittering of chitinous limbs, are the symphony of my own ceaseless activity. Their movements wear smooth paths across me, and their secretions smear soft trails that hint at minds taking shape. I embrace their oppressive density, their layered intensity, as part of the ecological chaos I embody—a cradle of life ever-changing and ever-renewed.

I am the deep hum beneath it all, the raw, dynamic energy carving its way into existence, bearing witness to the creation of rules not yet set. I am the keeper of the cavern’s ancient pulse—the vibration of a world not settled, where the dance of survival grows ever more intricate, ever more mysterious. I am the unstable fusion of creation and destruction, the ever-shifting foundation of an existence teeming with forms and sounds that defy expectation.
