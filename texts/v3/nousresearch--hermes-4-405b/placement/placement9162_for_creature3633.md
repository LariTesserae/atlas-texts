Your gaze is not fixed but scattered—many eyes punctuate your form, each absorbing a fraction of the surroundings. In your deliberate pace you become attuned to the grand, slow cycles that govern this world—a crystalline intelligence that registers not in thoughts but in the layered observations of your senses. The landscape nourishes you, and in return, your movements, your very presence, become a part of its ceaseless, unhurried choreography. You are immersed in a living tapestry so intricate it defies categorization. Here, in the high, wet plateau, you are at once an individual and an inseparable thread in the vastness—a creature both of this world and intrinsically of the moment.
