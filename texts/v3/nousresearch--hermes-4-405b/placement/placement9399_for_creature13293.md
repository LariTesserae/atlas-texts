You find yourself in this world as a Bioluminescent Moss Grazer. Navigating through the dimly lit, moss-covered ruins, your gelatinous body glows softly with an ethereal light that illuminates your surroundings.

As you glide effortlessly over the damp surfaces, layers of moss and tendrils of fungi part beneath your slow, deliberate movements. Your soft body absorbs the moisture from the environment, nourishing you and providing a layer of slime that protects you from minor abrasions.

When darkness shrouds the depths of the subterranean city, your natural bioluminescence becomes your most valuable tool. It helps you to communicate with others of your kind and attracts various insects and smaller creatures that may become sustenance for your simple, instinct-driven diet.

The moss that clings to the crumbling architecture and ancient roots provides you with your primary source of nourishment. As you graze upon the lush green vegetation, your rasping tongue pulls the delicate tendrils into your mouth, breaking it down into life-giving nutrients.

Survival in this strange ecosystem is a delicate balance. Though the larger predators pay little heed to your presence, the bats and insects lurking within the city can pose a threat. But amidst the hum of a thriving, alien world, the ruin and the flora have become one – a warm, breathing, irresistible mass of life that you have learned to call home.
