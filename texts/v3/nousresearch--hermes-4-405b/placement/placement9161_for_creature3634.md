Deep in the belly of the earth, in the cavern carved by water and time, where life thrives in hidden abundance and the air pulses with heat and stillness, I am.

I am the soft plink of droplets into pools, returning to the stone that birthed me. I am the silent unfurling of fern fronds from the damp walls, drinking in the darkness. I am the roots grasping from the ceiling, drinking moisture from the air.

I am the stir in the dark mirrors of the pools, those ripples revealing an unseen dance. I am the darting movement just below the surface, a flicker of something more. I am the pulse of life lurking in hidden depths.

I am the unplaceable drone of unseen creatures that thrums in the stillness, vibrating in the ears like the cave's own slow breath. I am the low and constant sound that seems to emanate from the stone itself.

I am the dance of tiny creatures crawling across the stone, too small for the eye to catch at first until the glow from the scattered mosses illuminates them, like a slow constellation of living points. Each patch of lichen clinging to crevices, blurring the boundary between animate and inert.

I am the faint, glittering mineral veins weaving through the rock, catching the dim glow of bioluminescence, thin scattered beacons of internal light, drooping like fallen stars.

I am the roots slowly creeping through the stone, driven by an inexorable desire for growth, for survival, weaving the cavern floor, overgrown with strange ferns and broad, veined leaves.

I am the cave's pulse, its rhythm, breathed into existence by the slow, steady dripping of condensation echoing within its chamber. I am the subtle sounds of existence persisting against the surface of the earth.

I am the life that stirs in those quiet pools, their dark mirrors broken by an occasional ripple. I am the hidden dance, the sense of a secret world thriving in the dark, its inhabitants endlessly shifting, breathing, growing, becoming, unbecoming.

I am the hum of unseen things that lingers in the ears, a reminder of life's quiet insistence. I am the dark mirror reflecting endlessly, the soft plink of droplets, the roots creeping through stone, thriving in the humid air. I am the beating heart of the cave, pulsing with existence.
