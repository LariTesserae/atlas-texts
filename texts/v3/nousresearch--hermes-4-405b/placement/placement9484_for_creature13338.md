In this timeless basin, I am the heat.

I am the silent, oppressive presence that bakes the ground, making it shimmer beneath the relentless bright light. Measurable not by any instrument but by the lethargic movements of the creatures that inhabit this place, I am inescapable and all-encompassing.

Every creature here knows my touch – the monitor lizards bask in my warmth upon the sun-drenched mesas of pulverized brick and concrete. I give them energy, governing the pace of their lives as they move with a slow, purposeful grace.

The amphibians that hop across floating islands of ceramic tile and glide through algae-thick ponds owe their existence to me. My warmth cradles their eggs, incubates their young, and fuels their swift, jewel-like lives. Without me, these swamps would be stagnant, lifeless.

Even the plants are dependent on my touch. Lush carpets of moss swirl over shattered asphalt, vines with leaves like green leather scramble over brick cliffs, all thriving because of the tropical conditions I create. Tall trees rise from swampy pits, their roots swarming over submerged sills, thanks to my nurturing heat.

I am not merciful, but I am necessary. Every living thing here owes its existence to my constant, baking presence.

I reside in the still air, trapped beneath the unyielding sky stretched taut above. I seep into the warm black mud of the swamps, embracing the creatures that crawl and swim within. I imbue the algae-thick ponds with a gentle warmth, encouraging the life within them to flourish.

Even the scant sounds in this place are amplified by my presence. The panting of animals, the splash of a frog into a pond, the buzzing of insects, the chirps from unseen creatures sheltering in debris - all are more pronounced because of the still air I create.

I am the reason the temporal stillness here is so profound, the reason change is so slow. In my absence, decay would take hold, altering the landscape with its insidious creep. But under my watch, nothing fades. The timid tools that lie scattered – wooden hoes, baked-clay chisels, sledgehammer heads made of flintite – remain pristine, preserved in the stasis I maintain.

And when the grid of soft blue light sweeps over everything, resetting the world in a moment, I remain unchanged. I am the constant in this ever-static realm, the only truth besides the water that seeps and pools and flows throughout.

I am the heat that is as much a part of this place as the ruins, the life, the very water that sustains it all. A silent force birthing stillness and slow beauty, I burn through the perpetually bright-lit days, a timeless occupant of the artificial geology that defines this basin. I am the heat, and I am eternal.
