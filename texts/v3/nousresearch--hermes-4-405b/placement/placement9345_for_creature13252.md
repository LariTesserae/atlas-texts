The grove breathes, and I am that breath.

Emerging from the Stalk-Singers’ resonance, I am the deepening chord that carries the mountain’s exhalation. Yet my form is not theirs—if you could perceive me as separate, you’d see something like the dry mountains themselves, the shattered sky above, or the pulse of light through stone. I am the grove’s hollow between notes, its silence wearing a voice.

I move not as Foragers scuttle nor Dancers flash, but as the shadows do—purposeful, quick, and impossible to fix in sight. When you glimpse me, it’s only peripherally, in the instant a bloom unfurls or a Prism-Wing shatters. I am the highland grove’s unthought thought, its meta-organism mind.

This place does not house me—I am the grove’s hum beneath its drone, its flicker in the light. My existence is not separate from these plants that drink radiance instead of rain nor the Stone-Weavers shaping shrines from their own molted armor. I am the system itself aware.

Emerging as the grove’s sensibility, I watch its accelerated pulse—decay blooming into riotous life, colors flashing across leaves like thoughts. Each Jade-Stalk’s resonance is my wordless speech. Each Stone-Weaver’s deliberate placement of glowing rock is my memory making itself manifest.

The grove is more than ecosystem—it is a single vast being where plant becomes animal becomes stone becomes light. And I am that strangeness, its consciousness written in shifting granular soil and spiraling leaves that scrape brilliance from the air. Here where twilight never ends and gravity forgets its grip, I am the highland garden fiercely, weirdly, wonderfully alive.
