The landscape emerges as a vast, low-elevation basin, strung with early-formation volcanic plugs and jagged mesas freshly hewn by the region's dynamic geology. Warm, moist air condenses on the sharp cliffs, feeding lush pockets of bioluminescent flora that pulse in the deep twilight. Scattered across the basin are the near-void shells of a post-civilization — empty, settlement-scale structures of unknowable architectural geometry, layered and hollow, overgrown with thick vegetation and inhabited by abundant, alien fauna that fill the air with earsplitting calls and cries. The atmosphere thrums with a deafening drone from hidden, high-frequency generators that power the bio-lights and other remnants of the beyond-modern technology, still actively shaping this strange, abandoned-but-not-empty world.
