The landscape begins as a cavernous realm, a vast subterranean valley extending past the reach of sight. The air is noticeably cool, thick with the dampness of intermittent moisture seeping through fissures above, and droplets collect on the edges of freshly formed rock formations. Early-formation stone — young, recently emerged — shows sharp, uneroded features, its surfaces only spotted with sparse lichen or fungi in places where the occasional rivulets carve their paths.

There are no signs of anything built here — no artifacts or constructions mar the virgin rock. The light is dim, yet not oppressive; rather, it is luminous and diffuse, coming from a source not immediately apparent, casting a dreamlike haze over every surface. Though the cavern is mostly stone and stillness, a subtle warmth of life pulses beneath its quiet exterior.

As the eye adjusts to the dimness, a shape emerges — a titan whose immense body arches overhead in a gentle curve, its massive bulk pressing against the cavern walls, but also leaving an enormous open space beneath it. The creature’s skin is a mosaic of plate-like scales, dense yet pliable, and almost pearlescent in the dim light. Its slumbering form is immense — so large that at first glance its outline merges with the cavern contours, as if the rock itself had grown a living veneer. Wispy, translucent membranes, like delicate sails, trail from its spine and limbs, hardly stirring in the cavern's still air. Finer, thread-like appendages festoon its flanks, their delicate ends glowing with a faint inner light. As it rests in perpetual, frozen equilibrium, this colossal being is the cavern’s defining feature, its existence as natural as the stone that frames it.

From every side, distant patterns of movement reveal species assembling: luminescent insect-like creatures have taken flight, their paths curving like shimmering rivers in the dim air as they gather on the immense flanks of the titan. Their soft, winged drone mingles with the dripping of moisture from above, and with the slow, rhythmic sound of the titan’s own great breaths. Beneath its massive, clear feet—each toe like a translucent shell—smaller creatures crawl, their tiny forms rustling like leaves as they restore minute imperfections in the creature’s underbelly or tend to the glowing filaments that drape almost petal-like over the cavern floor.

Strange, root-like tendrils extend from the titan’s underbelly, plunging into the cavern floor and creating a wide, circular pattern. At their extremities, shallow pools of water glint — formed by seeping moisture that has been drawn to the creature’s quiet magnetic presence. Between the pools and along the cavern’s edges lies a light, pale moss — its color indistinguishable from that of the titan’s skin — that exudes warmth and soft, amber luminescence where it meets the cool rock of the cavern floor.

The air shimmers slightly with the motion of the myriad lives tending the titan. A constant light rustle fills the cavern as they move, their collective activity a symphony of soft, natural industry. Occasionally, a low, resonant hum issues from the creature’s massive chest, reverberating through the walls as if they too are alive. Far in the distance, beyond the pools, other creatures move—some with the slow, graceful gaits of beings at home in a landscape whose every contour is in equilibrium, others scurrying in the shadows, their faint, primitive tools in hand as they tend to minute tasks nearly invisible in the diffuse glow.

Despite the teeming life and the ceaseless, delicate work of its many attendants, the titan remains unmoving, its immense eyes closed in a slumber that seems unending. It is strange that no erosion marks its skin; nor do the portions of the cavern wall not in direct contact with its bulk show any sign of weathering. The landscape itself is in a state of frozen equilibrium: while the fauna live and work and tend, nothing changes. The great creature does not decay or grow; its attendants restore themselves from the materials they tend; the cavern walls remain freshly formed, and the moisture that seeps through its fissures is returned with each breath of the titan's vast, placid lungs. In this profound, overwhelming presence, time appears suspended—a dream of stone, water, and living light, set in motion yet never changing.
