The light here is not harsh, exactly — it’s pervasive, a diffuse and ever-present glow that seems to emanate from every surface without casting shadows. Overhead, the sky is lost behind a vast, gently curving dome of seamless white that stretches to the horizon and hints at continuing far beyond. It’s warm, the air dry and still, so quiet you can hear the faint, low hum of the world operating beneath your feet. You stand on a plain of pale, perfected material, something like ceramic or polished stone but impossibly smooth, veined with faint silver traceries like circuitry or fossilized roots. The ground is flat, or so it seems at first glance — in time, you begin to discern the incredibly shallow grades, the almost imperceptible slopes that descend into depressions or rise over low crests, worn nearly flat by time beyond reckoning. Nothing seems untouched here; every surface, every curve is deliberate, the product of engineering that operates on a scale we can't fathom, where physics is both tool and material.

Vegetation is sparse but striking where it appears: low, widely spaced growths like living crystals or geometric blooms of iridescent chitin, their symmetrical, fractal patterns glowing faintly from within. They catch the light in ways that suggest an internal power source, a slow and deliberate pulse that you feel more than see. Further off, vast structures rise like the bones of the world, impossibly tall and slender towers or arches that seem to defy gravity, their surfaces covered in intricate, shifting patterns that might be circuitry or art. These structures are everywhere, in every direction as far as the light allows you to see. They are not merely built upon the land but seem to be an extension of it, growths or integral parts of some colossal, dormant machine. There is no water, no rivers or lakes — only the dry air, the warm light, the smooth stone beneath your feet, and the low thrumming in the ground that might be the heartbeat of this place. The scale is immense, planetary; the silence is deep. You are a tiny presence in the midst of vast, ancient workings.
