It is a realm known only as the Flooded Precinct: a small, fully submerged relic of impossible engineering, suspended high on the abyssal flank of an oceanic trench. Water rules here completely — cold, absolute, a black and silent world as solid as a cathedral carved from midnight. There are no fish or plants stirring the waters; only the rare passage of deep-sea giants, their slow shadows like mountains drifting above the structures.

The architecture itself is a cluster of sharp-edged towers, domes, and angular courts; their surfaces, pitted only by the recent slow deposit of mineral. Shaped from a single seamless substance, a polished obsidian-like stone that time itself seems to flow around. There are no growths of coral or rust or deep-sea flora; nothing lives here but the quiet perfection of the built environment.

The city or settlement does not breathe. Its scale is small enough to keep entirely within the close, almost intimate embrace of this darkness; it feels contained, a compact realm of engineered silence rather than an open ocean above which sunlight plays. Yet its lines are stark, precise, impossibly young and perfectly placed as if they emerged from the seabed only moments ago, a frozen moment of creation that the ocean now encloses.

The only quality that approaches variability is a faint light — subaqueous, phosphorescent, a cold blue steady luminance — swimming up through the structures. It is generated by the very material of the walls and towers, glowing from below or within as though lit from the core of the planet itself. In such absolute dark, the deep blue luminescence feels less like illumination than its opposite: the form of darkness when it dreams of light. And as the outer blackness presses in, pressing itself into the sharp geometries, the city seems less a built thing than a set of sculpted night pressing back into itself.

A perfume of unknown minerals breathes through the water, the air here still, cold, and saturated with a sense of being within, always, this one perfect moment: a stillness that holds forever all that ever is: the Flooded Precinct exists as its own perfect equilibrium — a frozen, eternal stillness, deep beneath the ocean, beneath light, as if physics itself has simply, finally, achieved its one perfect form.
