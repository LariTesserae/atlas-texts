The landscape is a vast, domed metropolis of corroded iron and weathered copper, its surface seamed with rivulets of constant runoff from the high elevation and perpetually condensing atmosphere, amounting to a persistent, cool dampness in the air. This urban sprawl is a fully constructed, dense warren of factories, engine-houses, and maze-like pipeline labyrinths, packed to the point of claustrophobia, with little space between its towering structures. All of this rests upon and is surrounded by lush, verdant, vegetation — thick carpets of moss, hanging gardens of ivy, and even patches of robust, dark fern-like plants sprouting in every available crevice and crack in the metal, thriving under the dim, perpetually diffused light.

The environment is a strange juxtaposition of bustling industry and serene nature. The atmosphere is dank, with cool, damp air carrying the loamy, wet-petrichor scent of the abundant greenery, underlaid by the oily tang of machine processes. While the urban center itself hosts a myriad of mechanized activities in a state of apparent chaos—whirring gears, hissing steam vents, arcing electricity, pounding presses—it is somehow contained within this self-sustaining system, the hum of activity both intense and strangely quiet.

Despite this constant, dynamic industrial turbulence there’s an eerie stillness to the wider air, where sounds are damped and softened, the dense foliage muffling the clangs, rumbles, and engine hums of the factories. This creates a sense of hushed, almost silent industry, with the drip and trickle of water as the most prominent sound in some areas. The entire landscape appears ancient and well-worn, its metallic surfaces weathered and eroded from prolonged exposure to the moist environment, in a way that Cullen murmur in the air like a strange, emergent rhythm. It exudes a feeling of cool stability, of it being concentrated life and complex, indifferent machinery at once, an environment of contradiction, an feeling of calm and steady transformation, that seems to be in a state of balance.
