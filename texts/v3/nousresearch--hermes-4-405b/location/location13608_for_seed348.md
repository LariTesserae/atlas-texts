The city hunkers in perpetual cool dampness — a mineral world where no plant life takes root. Stone crumbles underfoot, the only testament to partial botany found in meandering lichens that whisper across rock faces before vanishing. Around the city, slow rivers wind past, their gradual erosion a companion to the ever-present drizzle that slicks the pavement.

Towering structures loom in tight-packed rows, their architectural bones long settled into grooves of weathered permanence. Some buildings sprawl low to the ground, their walls thick and fused as if poured from a mold. Others stretch upwards in narrow towers that brush the sky, a delight of clustered arches and windows of milky glass. A few slump randomly, their ruin softened by water-stone smoothed edges. Yet amidst the damp decay, the city hums.

Within this constructed maze, a realm of engines and circuitry unfolds. Pipes coil like serpents up the sides of factories, exhaling thick plumes of steam that mingle with the fog. Flickering electric lights shine from within, their glow wavering with the piston-churn of hidden machines and the deep thrum of turbines. The air vibrates with noise — a constant grinding of gears meshed with the hiss of vented gas — all of it loud and unrelenting, a mechanical clamor that fills the stone canyon streets.

This urban expanse teems with life — yet the life here is not what one expects. Strange machines scuttle on countless legs, their segmented bodies humming with a weird energy as they move through the damp streets. Gargantuan clockwork beasts stride through the alleys, their internal gears a symphony of turning cogs and hissing steam. Everywhere, cursory edits of flesh — half-machine and half-unknown — cluster and jostle in the corners of the city.

Every sight, every sound, every whisper of steam and echo of engine pulses with the same unvarying rhythm. In its own state, unchanging, frozen in equilibrium; the world has settled into this precise and relentless song.
