I struggle for footing on a shallow slope that plunges deep into black water. Dark shapes shift below: packed masses that press close in the cold gloom, leaving no open space between them. Turbulent currents churn the depths constantly, a chaotic roil that sends chill liquid swirling around my legs. A near-silence hangs in the frigid air above, broken only by the lapping of water and the drip from rare, clinging fronds of primitive vegetation. It is a primordial place, deeply eroded and ancient, submerged beneath a world untouched by human works.
