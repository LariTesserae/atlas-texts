The city sprawls through a lowland basin, a place of perpetual dampness where the warm, saturated air often condenses into fine mists that blur the edges of things. The light here is dim, filtered through the haze and through the uppermost layers of the native flora: vast, dark green tendrils that rise like living skyscrapers, their surfaces slick with moisture. Lower down, the city streets wind between these towering growths – a place where lush vegetation thrives in every available pocket – riotous ferns in every shade of green, luminous fungi clinging to building facades, thick mosses carpeting the ground. The city itself is built into this living landscape, sleek bio-integrated structures and crystalline walkways rising organically amidst the native plants. It possesses an undeniable strangeness, a beauty both alien and ancient-seeming – in part because energy fields shimmer at the edges of perception, hinting at technology beyond modern understanding, making the very air feel charged.

These structures of the city are constructed of dark, living stone native to the area, smoothly sculpted by processes unseen. Where the rock has broken away, the fractures are sharp and recent-looking, suggesting ongoing geological activity beneath the surface. Yet the city is spread out, with generous open spaces between the larger buildings – open plazas paved with intricate geometric patterns, cultivated parks bright with exotic flowers, quiet residential districts half-hidden behind groves of trees. Such a place feels somehow balanced, saturated but not overcrowded; its presences are punctuated by stretches of emptiness that invite quiet contemplation.

Wildlife is rare here, creatures are seldom seen. But the ecosystem is in constant, active motion: vines visibly climb the walls, flowers open and close with the passage of unseen suns, and the ever-present moisture cycles in veils of mist. Occasionally sounds drift through the quiet – a chime from a hidden source, the drip of water from high above, the distant murmur of flowing water, the soft hum of strange energies in the earth below. The rules of this world are subtle, a living city nestled within a landscape that is itself alive and breathing, shaped by forces that operate on scales beyond easy comprehension.
