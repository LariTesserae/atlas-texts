The air hangs thick and wet, a saturated veil through which feeble light strains. You stand at the edge of a sprawling, layered ruin—a city of cyclopean stones and light-eating shales, sunken into a permanent marsh. Water sheens every surface, beads and drips from sills too deep for shadows, yet the darkness persists, as if the water feeds on light. Only the thinnest glaze of green clings to the stones, pale mosses and lichens that seem half-starved. The intense heat steams from the soaked ground, the air itself feeling like a warm washcloth pressed against the skin. A low elevation ensures no wind reaches here; the atmosphere is stagnant, palpably heavy with suspended water.

This is a half-drowned necropolis, its grandeur eroded into soft angles, the stone drinking the waters that rise from perpetual springs beneath. The scale is vast: coliseums tilt at angles, avenues and plazas stretch under the murky shallows. In this perpetual dimness, there is no animal life, no insect hum, no birdcall. Yet, the world is not dead: it is transmuting. The stones sweat and weep and ripple, one surface feeding into the next. Arches weep streaks of glinting wetness that merge into the marsh, while stone fangs protrude from the muck. Here, the distinction between inorganic and ‘living’ breaks down. Countless small events transpire constantly, stone sweating into marsh, the wetness rippling into new formations, an active, inscrutable alchemy.

At the heart of this humid dark rises the central spire: a great black tower pierced by a single window, glowing with an eerie, cold light. Ivory and obsidian grains glitter in the stone. It is a dark germ in the city's core, its presence felt in the water that runs unnaturally, its faint, cold light bleeding out through the cracks of the drowning world. That cold light illuminates the near-tideless water lapping at the tower's base, at once a generative and terminating force. The entire landscape breathes a low, resonant hum, as of many stones vibrating together just below the threshold of hearing. This is a place where reality is porous, where stone flows like water, and water solidifies, where something of unknown purpose is being patiently cultivated in the dark and wet.
