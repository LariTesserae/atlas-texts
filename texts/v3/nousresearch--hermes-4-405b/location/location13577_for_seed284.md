The world is a pocket; an entire cityscape squeezed into a vast, unnatural fissure within the highest peaks of an inhospitable, waterless mountain range. Here, in the thin, cool air, the perpetual twilight is weakly lit by a dim and distant sun, barely filtering down through the jagged, narrow canyon overhead. There is no liquid water, only mineral veins in the rock. The only life is fungal, clinging to the dark, shaded crevasses in between the dense human construction below.

This city is built, dense and layered upon itself many times over. Its rising, jagged architecture mirrors the sharp, barely-eroded faces of the cliff walls that enclose it on all sides. Here, there is only industry: engines churn endlessly, factories belch thick smoke, and electric sparks flash ceaselessly in the gloom. The city is turbulent, thick with chaotic energy. Things are always being built and un-built in a frenzy of constant change.

The inhabitants are as abundant as the city is dense. Swarms of small, dark, multi-limbed creatures rush through twisting streets and crowded plazas. They're insect-like, scurrying in a frenzy of constant motion. Their clattering, scraping, squeaking sounds are nearly drowned out by the perpetual roar of industrial toil; it swirls together in a mostly quiet chaos, only occasionally punctuated by explosive crashes from within the towering factories.

This place is alien: an outpost built by some unknown things into the top of the world, where no water flows, and the light is always dim. The city is monstrous, alive and changing all the time, like an industrious hive of nightmare insects. Built with human hands or not, this city is strange, alien, unsettling, hidden away in the high places of a dead world.
