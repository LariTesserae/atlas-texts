The landscape resolves as a narrow, perpetually shadowed chasm wedged deep within peaks of unimaginable height. Water manifests not as rivers or pools, but as a constant, heavy condensation coating every surface, a clinging chill that beads upon stone and drips plink-plonk onto a carpet of prolific, almost unnervingly green moss. This vegetation thrives in the damp dimness, furred over boulders and draping from the chasm's sheer walls in thick layers of lace and velvet, creating a dense, almost claustrophobic tapestry of life.

Yet despite the lushness, the chasm feels cold. The rock itself, new and sharply angular from recent, violent upheavals, doesn't retain any warmth. High above and unseen, industrial reverberations pound through the mountain stone. Their origins are beyond compass, but the effect here is a constant, oppressive thrumming - a deep, metallic heartbeat vibrating through rock, amplified by the tight space. It shakes droplets from leaves, sets the air itself shivering as if from a chill wind.

The enclosed space feels both vast in its overwhelming nature and crushingly small, a singular feature-room within the silent, greater mountain fastness. Light is a rare and precious commodity here, a memory of forgotten sun. Sounds are few under the industrial dirge: the soft percussion of water, the whisper of displaced air. For no animals move here, nothing stirs the green except the drip and the shudder. The world is harsh, newly broken stone and relentless growth. Deep in the cold, wet heart of stone, life persists, but it is a slow, vegetative existence under the shadow of a giant's industry, vibrating with booming sound and the slow, grinding energy of immense, unseen machinery. Still, all is as it should be; the laws of this world hold firm; nothing here is truly strange, just the natural processes of rock and water and the persistent thrum of work from high above.
