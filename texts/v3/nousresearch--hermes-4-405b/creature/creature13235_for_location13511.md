Here, amidst the planet-sized profusion of life, live the Skyrifters, small mammals no bigger than a terrestrial cat. The air, heavy with moisture and the intoxicating scent of chlorophyll, adheres to their smooth, dark fur. Their large, ovoid eyes, adapted to filter the thin light beneath the canopy of the colossal flora, shift with a liquidity, absorbing their lush and overwhelming surroundings with an almost mechanical precision. They are nimble, light-footed, capable of clinging to the sheer, silvery trunks of the ancient, skyscraper-tall trees and navigating the precarious networks of taut, thick vines that form natural bridges across impossible chasms. Their diet subsists of succulent leaves, vibrant mosses, and succulent, iridescent beetles. For the Skyrifters, the towering forest floor of twisted, gargantuan roots is not only terrain, but also a vast, shared nesting ground, their young sheltered in warm hollows gouged deep into the living, ancient wood. They are gentle beings, communicating not through sound, but rather through intricate dances of shadow and bioluminescence created by the swarms of tiny, glowing gnats that accompany them in hypnotic clouds, a language reflecting the profound and alien beauty around them.
