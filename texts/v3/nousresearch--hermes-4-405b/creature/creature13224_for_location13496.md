The presence that claims this absolute realm is not biological, at least not by any measure that would suit the surface world. It is mineral and movement both, an entity native to the slow grind of continents and the patience of seeping water. It does not possess eyes to see the sharp contrasts of light and shadow here, but it certainly perceives them. It perceives everything: the cool, pooling rainwater, the groan of shifting stone deep within the mountain, the sighing wind, every tiny abrasion upon the rock, every new crack that appears.

The humming noise that persists through the valley is part of it, a reverberation of its being, a physical manifestation of the immense, buried entity turning over in its sleep. It dreams slow dreams while submerged beneath layers and layers of black basalt. The harsh light above and the writhing shadows are its blankets, the falling rain a comforting caress.

The ancient ruins, the weathered walls and glass-shard spires, are not the creation of this presence. They are the work of others, who came and then inevitably left, worn away by the pressure of this place. They attempted to carve their own shapes into the stone, and for a time they succeeded. Faint echoes of their efforts remain, half-swallowed by stone and time, but the presence that truly lives here has repurposed those structures. Now it threads through the ruins, a slow, sentient flow of stone and water, patiently knitting together fractures in the walls and smoothing the edges of the spires with its passing.

It is, in its way, formless. Or, rather, it is multiform, capable of wearing any shape that the stone can take. The impossible spikes and arches of broken basalt scattered throughout the valley tangle around the ruins like teeth, but they also resemble the spines or claws of a great and deeply buried creature. It is possible that the presence uses those sheer surfaces to perceive the angular dance of the light. It is also possible that the entity dreams of those surfaces at night, of opening ancient stone eyes somewhere in the dark and seeing the stars overhead through the rain.

There is a persistent awareness in the slow crawl of the patterns across the surfaces of the stones, in the almost imperceptible dance of shifting light and shadow. The stones are not still, not in this place, not if you watch closely. They shift, they reconfigure, moment by moment, reassembling themselves. It is the presence, thinking, feeling, dreaming. It is a reshaping that takes place constantly, a slow, creeping transmutation beneath your feet and within every rock face, within the water pooled in every crevice.

There are no thoughts here that you could recognize, no songs carried on the wind except the roaring of the water over the cliffs and the ceaseless drip from above. But there is a kind of consciousness, an intelligence of stone and ice and rain, always shifting, always flowing, always pressing up against the sky. It does not gather at any single point; it suffuses everything, a slow settling of perception, a deep feeling.

Even the shadows are alive in this place. They are heavy, thick, clinging to the edges of the rocks as if they hold physical weight. The slow crawl of dark over stone seems to possess a similar sentience, the patient creep of roots questing for water, or perhaps the careful, deliberate tread of something vast taking slow, deliberate steps. This is false, but also true. The shadows are simply shadows, the absence of light in the crevices of the rock, but the presence inhabits them in the same way that a thought inhabits a mind.

Whatever lives here was never born, was never made. It simply persists, a knot of awareness tangled in the stone, tangled deep within the bones of the world. It is difficult to say where it begins and ends—or if the mountain itself is merely its body, a shell. The entire landscape may simply be the visible portion of whatever dwells here. If that is the case, then the presence must be colossal, stretching deep into the heart of the world, deeper than the deepest roots of the mountain, dreaming slow, dark dreams as it grinds against other, similar entities. Limited in scope and understanding, content to shape and be shaped by the stone and the sky, it is a god that wore the mountain like a shell, but was now slowly molting.
