In this perpetually damp and mineral city, strange machines and clockwork beasts make their home. Scuttling on countless legs, the segmented bodies of these machines hum with an energy that seems to emanate from the city itself, pulsing through the stone canyon streets. The gargantuan clockwork beasts stride through the alleys, their movements a symphony of turning cogs and hissing steam.

Everywhere, there are creatures that are half-machine and half-unknown, their forms a patchwork of metal and flesh. These beings cluster and jostle in the corners of the city, their presence an inextricable part of the urban landscape. The life here is a constant grinding of gears, a mechanical clamor that fills the air, mingling with the hiss of vented gas and the deep thrum of turbines.
