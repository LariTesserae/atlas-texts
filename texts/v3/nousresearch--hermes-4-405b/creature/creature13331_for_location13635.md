Deepwater Falls is not entirely devoid of life – an 'after a fashion.' The residents are two distinct entities, born of this sunken, pressurized, dark ecosystem – neither entirely understood by human explorers.

Firstly, there are the **Scuttlers**, fleeting, elusive, and nocturnal. These pale chitinous creatures are primarily chitinous exoskeleton designed to help them navigate this dark cold environment. Small, spindly-limbed shadows, each about the size of a large house cat. They boast a form of bioluminescence that allows them to move unexpectedly – and unnervingly – through the darkness, skittering on walls and rubble with unnatural, fluid grace. They move like liquid in this liquid world. They communicate through a series of chirps and clicks that might be interpreted as curiosity or territorial warnings. Their intelligence is impossible to gauge, and they prefer to observe you from the periphery. They are undeniably *there*, blending seamlessly with the mineral formations and the detritus of this rewilded city.

Secondly, and far more imposing, are the **Deepwater Leviathans**. These vast, roughly ovoid structures are easily the size of small blimps. They are composed of countless layers of those deep purple and turquoise mineral deposits accreting around a hidden core. They are primarily composed of a porous, honeycombed material, almost sparse and delicate in structure, but within these internal spaces lies a mystery: a dense, almost-solid matrix of fine, silvery filaments that shimmer faintly with a cold, internal light. They drift with the slow, ponderous currents of the deep, exuding an aura of ancient, almost geological patience.

They occasionally discharge a cloud of fine, dark particles from several orifices along their mineralized hide, an act that remains a mystery. Spores? Eggs? Waste? These acts add to their enigmatic nature, making them seem less like creatures and more like strange geological processes made mobile. Their purpose, their life cycle, their very nature remains unknown. They are silent, slow-moving titans, the ghost-inhabitants of Deepwater Falls, their existence a testament to the city’s transformation and the strange new life that has taken root in its bones.
