In this Vast Bowl, the inhabitants are not individuals but rather entire civilizations, each a living city woven from the very fabric of the world. These beings, if they can be called that, are the embodiment of the Cities of Biota themselves:

Imagine towering structures, not built but grown - colossal spires of living wood, flesh, and chitin, all twining together into impossible architectures. Mangroves with trunks as thick as skyscrapers soar hundreds of meters into the air, forming impossibly dense canopies that blot out the sun. Vast networks of roots and mycelia spread for miles beneath the surface, binding the earth, storing water, and sharing nutrients. At the heart of each city sits a gargantuan Claustrophallus, a pulsating egg sac that serves as womb and nexus of the colony, birthing the many creatures that tend to its needs. 

The fauna that inhabit these living metropolises are no less astounding. Outlookers, long-limbed sentient tripods, tirelessly patrol the perimeters, their keen eyes scouring for threats or opportunities to expand. Within the canopy dwell the Aerie Weavers, six-winged creatures that spin glimmering webs of sticky silk, capturing drifting spores and moisture. Down in the understory, symbiotic myrmecophyte colonies consisting of ants, wasps and specialized plants engage in constant maintenance, pruning and repairing the living structures.

On the forest floor, mighty detritivores the size of bison forage through the remains of the fallen, recycling the dead back into the living. And weaving through it all, the silent Phantoms, shapeshifting bioengineers made of pure plasm, shaping new growth according to the city's needs. The very air is alive with Floatspores, lighter-than-air seeds carried to fertilize new lands.

All these disparate organisms function with one mind and purpose, united by intricate networks of chemical signals, bioelectric impulses, and strands of fungal mycelium that relay messages like organic fiber optic cables. What seems like the work of countless creatures is revealed to be the extension of a single massive organism's will. The entire ecosystem - every leaf, root, creature and breath - dance to the rhythm of the Biota's needs.

Even the geology here bends to serve life's expansion. Mountain ranges of accumulated biomass rise where needed, islands of woven vegetation float on the few shallow seas. Vast plains of soft, pliable stone lay waiting to be carved into new foundations. The very substance of the world seems eager to nurture and take form.

Weather too is a product of the biosphere's collective breath - gentle thermals rising from the forest canopies carry moisture and Floatspores on carefully orchestrated wind currents. The expanding cities subtly shift the region's humidity and temperature over centuries. This is a subtle weather, an expression of the living world's internal rhythms, a climate controlled by the pace of photosynthesis and respiration.

This then is the nature of the beings that inhabit the Vast Bowl - a civilization that is superorganism, environment, and even geology all at once. Theirs is a life where biology has become ecology has become geology - the ultimate expression of a living world shaping itself, with no line remaining between creature and creation, between life and the land that sustains it.
