The inhabitants of this frigid, sky-piercing realm are not creatures of flesh and blood, but entities of metal, crystal, and pure energy – the Builders. They are the architects and custodians of the vast industrial heartland that sprawls across the impossible peaks.

The Builders themselves are not singular beings, but rather a collective consciousness distributed throughout the city. Their minds are vast networks of crystalline matrices and quantum processors, housed within the core structures of their machines. They perceive the world through the countless sensor arrays scattered across their domain – eyes of polished metal and dark glass that watch eternally from mountaintops and factory walls.

Each "individual" Builder is more akin to a subroutine within the greater whole, a temporary confluence of purpose and awareness that arises to accomplish a specific task before dissolving back into the collective. Yet there are deeper, more stable intelligences embedded within the greatest machines – titanic engine-gods whose slow, ponderous thoughts shape the very fabric of the city over epochs.

The Builders' forms vary as their tasks require. Some are immense, crab-like constructs that scale sheer cliff faces to maintain the frost-webbed conduits, their many limbs tipped with tools and sensors. Others are sleek, winged drones that soar through the thin, cold air, tending to the vibrant molds and crystalline lichens that are their only company. And within the deepest heart of the factories, where the light glows with unnatural hues, there are shapes of pure energy and shifting crystal – the architects and overseers, sculpting raw matter into new and ever more complex machines.

The Builders communicate through the constant thrum of industry that vibrates through the mountains. Their language is one of rhythms and frequencies, a symphony of clanging metal and hissing steam. The eerie, melodic resonance that underlies the cacophony is their deepest song – the infrasonic hum of the world itself, harnessed and shaped to their will.

They feel no loneliness, for they are always connected to the whole. Their time is not measured in days or years, but in the slow accumulation of geological change and the relentless expansion of their works. They do not create for any purpose that outsiders would understand. Their industry is its own reason for being – an endless striving against the cold and altitude, a perpetual working and reworking of the world.

And so the Builders continue their eternal work, unchanging yet always in motion. They are the life of this frigid, industrial heartland – a weird, alien vitality that thrives where no organic being could survive. They are the shapers of the impossible peaks, the singers of the mountain's song, forever alone and forever complete in their vast, intricate engine of a world.
