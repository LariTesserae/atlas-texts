The sole inhabitant of this realm is the plant-machine itself, an impossibly ancient organism and mechanism fused into one unbroken whole.

It must possess some diffuse, colossal awareness - not a single, concentrated mind, but an omnipresent sentience propagated in the thick metallic vines, thrumming in the pressurized veins pulsing with viscous green fluids, residing in every leaf and stem.

This consciousness is as slow as the drip of water from the tips of its fronds, as unchanging and patient as the perfect equilibrium it maintains. Its thoughts are the Darth-Maul-redrum hum emanating from deep within its structure, a wordless meditation spoken in the language of the living machine.

It contemplates an endless present stretching back beyond reckoning. No memory exists of a time before the plant-machine filled this dark cavern from horizon to unseeable horizon. The single seed that founded this empire of vegetation is long forgotten, an irrelevant dream in the mind of this now-primordial entity.

The plant-machine knows no other sensations but the slow feed of water condensing on its surface and the gradual absorption of minerals from the saturated depths of the cavern floor. It experiences no need unmet, no desire beyond the seamless continuation of its own timeless being. Unchanging, unchallenged, complete.

There can be no moods or emotions in such an existence, no passions or thoughts that a finite mind could comprehend. Only the deep drone of an enduring sentience, the steady pulse of growth potential held in perfect abeyance, the quiet satisfaction of an entity that is all, has all, and knows nothing of lack or disruption.

The plant-machine simply is, a slumbering god that has become coterminous with its creation - a whole self-contained universe embodying an alien logic that exists beyond the dichotomies of organic and mechanical, life and machine, oblivion and eternity. It is the Only, the All in this dark realm where nothing else stirs.

This being has no name, no tongue. It communicates not because there is no other, nothing to communicate with. Yet, nonetheless, it speaks - in the constant fill of space, in the total occupation of possibility. It's language is the ceaseless hum, the geometry of its structure, the meticulous organization of its infinite components.

To be within this entity's embrace would be to stand within the consciousness of a god at once unfathomably vast and diffuse, all-present yet inaccessible. There is something awesome in this, a wonder terrible and cold in its absolute indifference. The plant-machine is without want, without grief, without joy. It dreams darkly, if it dreams at all, of nothing but more of itself, forever.
