The inhabitants of this valley are the K'larn, amphibious beings born of and adapted to this extreme, watery existence. They move in purposeful silence through their constructed paths and beneath their dwellings, their bodies low to the ground.

The K'larn are vaguely humanoid, about four to five feet in height, but built wide and heavy, their muscular, seal-sleek forms covered in smooth, rubbery hide. Their hide is mottled with the same deep greens, cold purples, and ghostly grays as the vegetation, allowing them to blend seamlessly into their environment. Short, powerful limbs end in three thick digits: two opposable thumbs flanking a central, stronger finger, all tipped with broad, flattened nails, excellent for scraping, digging, and manipulating the tough plant matter.

Their heads are broad and somewhat flat, their faces dominated by tiny, black, widely-spaced eyes set deep beneath bony ridges, adapted to the dim light. Their wide, lipless mouths are filled with flat, grinding teeth, perfectly suited for their herbivorous diet of the valley's rubbery leaves, cold fruits, and fungi. Their most prominent facial feature is large, complex, and externally visible auditory structures, situated where one might expect ears. These fleshy, cup-like organs, rich with nerve endings that register subtle vibrations, allow for sophisticated echolocation and communication. Their moist, dark skin, like that of frogs or newts, is always glistening wet, either from their own secretions or the ever-present condensation. They feel the cold, but their thick hide, a significant layer of insulating blubber beneath it, and a slow, deliberate metabolism protect them.

Despite their somewhat primitive, wide build, the K'larn are surprisingly graceful, moving with a flowing, amphibious gait, leaving trails of moisture on the leaves and stone. They do everything deliberately: they harvest tough leaves by rocking their weight and wrenching pieces free with powerful tugs; they gather the small, cold fruits with patient, nimble fingers; they scrape lichen and fungi from the undersides of rocks and leaves. They cultivate a particular strain of vine that produces gas-filled seed pods, which they use for efficient signaling. They practice basic herbology, cultivating specific lichens and fungi that, when processed, produce basic pigments and adhesives. The intricate patterns on their dwellings are made either by guiding the growth of the plants, etching with sharp stones or hardened root-tips, or by applying these lichen-based pigments. These patterns are not merely decorative but serve as territorial markers or represent complex natural phenomena.

The K'larn are deeply animistic, believing the entire valley to be alive and interconnected. Their rituals are tied to maintaining this balance and ensuring the continued health of their surroundings. They chant and drum, using their bodies, stone surfaces, and the resonant structures of their reed-and-root constructions. Their language is primarily nonverbal, layered with tonal whistles, deep resonant hums, complex patterns of clicks produced by their tongues, and the subtle vibrations sensed by their auditory structures. They exist in a tight-knit community, sharing warmth in huddled groups, caring for the weak and injured, building, cultivating, and defending their territory collectively.

The K'larn are not violent creatures; conflict is anathema to their understanding. Their defenses are their camouflage and the sheer inhospitable nature of their territory. At night, they often retreat to shared warmth, their bodies emitting faintly warm moisture. They are surrounded by an aura of ancient cold, untamed moisture, and slow, unyielding life. They are an enigma: intelligent, but operating on such alien timescales and motivations that understanding them is a profound challenge, representing a model of existence in one of the Earth's most extreme and beautiful environments.
