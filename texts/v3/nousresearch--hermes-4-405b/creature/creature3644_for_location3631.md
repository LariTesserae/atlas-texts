Life thrives in the impossible vastness of the Sky Reefs, though it is life forged in the crucible of deep space, sculpted by solar winds and cosmic radiation, utterly alien to terrestrial minds. It is not creatures of flesh and blood that dwell here, but beings composed of stardust and crystalline light, adapted to the near-vacuum, the strange superfluids, and the relentless divine wind.

**The Reefbuilders:**
The first true denizens are the Reefbuilders themselves – not sentient life in any conventional sense, but colossal, colonial entities existing on a scale that dwarfs comprehension. These are not the whale-like drifters, but the very architects of the glass-like structures. They are vast, amorphous, and mineral-based, perhaps akin to sentient geology or living crystal. They grow slowly, over millennia, their bodies the planet-sized shards. Their "thoughts" might be the deep, subsonic hum resonating through the reefs – vibrations that could be a form of communication or even a shared consciousness, thrumming with the heartbeat of the cooled star whose remnants they crystallized from. They generate the bizarre superfluids from their subsurface chambers, creating microhabitats. They are the silent, slumbering giants upon whose backs all other reef life exists. They may occasionally shift their positions, altering the archipelago over eons, their immense mass bending light and creating the faint auroras that dance between the shards as they slowly rotate.

**The Void Whales (Kosmothanatoi):**
Drifting like silent, ethereal titans between the shards are the Void Whales, the filter feeders mentioned in the prompt. They are colossal, their forms near-invisible, perhaps composed of the very void they inhabit – a slight distortion in the starlight, a ripple in the fabric of space, or beings of impossibly rarefied matter. They glide effortlessly through the vacuum, their gelatinous, miles-long bodies trailing veils of energy, or perhaps composed of dark matter. They feed not only on the dust and ionized gas but on the faint radiation of the distant sun and the subtle currents of spacetime itself. Their internal biology might starlight itself, processed in complex, light-harvesting organs. They navigate the solar winds, their migrations covering light-years, the Sky Reefs a waystation in their endless cosmic journey. They might possess unfathomable intelligence, their communications through modulations in gravity waves or subtle shifts in the reefs' ambient radiation. They live for millions of years, each one a universe in miniature.

**The Sky Mantas (Astrionychia):**
Smaller, though still vast by human standards, are the Sky Mantas. These are the "living kites" glimpsed riding the currents. Sleek, flat, bio-luminescent organisms, perhaps metallic or composed of condensed energy, they glide effortlessly on the solar wind. Their bodies are diamond-edged, capable of slicing through the void with precision, or perhaps their passage distorts space. They feed on the strange particulate matter swept by the solar winds, or on the exotic energies released during the X-ray flares. Some might hunt smaller, more elusive creatures that hide in the crevasses. Their "wings" shimmer with captured starlight, their patterns shifting to camouflage or communicate. During the particulate storms, they become incandescent, glowing with vibrant, alien colours, weaving intricate patterns of light as they chase each other through the reefs in silent, balletic dances that might be courtship, territorial disputes, or celestial games known only to them.

**Crystalline Flora (Lithophyta):**
The "hardy, crystalline moss" – the Lithophyta – is not true vegetation but slow-growing colonies of intricate, self-assembling crystal formations, like living geodes. They are not plants; they are geobiological constructs, sentient to some degree on a substrate level. They absorb and re-emit light, possibly storing information within the lattices of their structures. They live for millennia, accumulating archives of cosmic events, etched into their crystalline forms. They might possess a collective consciousness on a very slow timescale, reacting to stellar events by altering their light patterns, like slow stars themselves. They thrive on the X-ray flares, pulsing rhythmically with internal energy flows, casting a myriad-coloured, ethereal bioluminescence across the plains. When storms pass they return to a dormant state, their energy spent.

**The Lichen (Xenolithix):**
The "cracked lichen" – Xenolithix – is even stranger. It is a colonies of extremophile micro-organisms, adapted to subsist on the meagre nutrients and energy found in the void – solar radiation, cosmic rays, and exotic particles from the superfluids. Perhaps they forge a primitive photosynthesis using radiation. They metabolize the bare rock, slowly etching patterns that spread like patient fractals across the glassy plains. Their subtle discolorations are vast tapestries recounting the history of the cosmos. Individually ephemeral on a cosmic scale, their colonies might persist for vast stretches of deep time, ebbing and flowing with the cosmic tides.

**Relic Dwellers (Psychozoa):**
Living near or within the enigmatic, timeless artifacts are the Relic Dwellers, the Psychozoa. These are not corporeal beings in a conventional sense but perhaps colonies of sentient energy patterns. They are beings made of pure thought or light. They might be caretakers, symbiotes, or something else entirely, their nature intertwined with the relics that defy corrosion and time. Perhaps they form a hive-like entity that worships or draws energy from the ancient structures, or perhaps they are the remnant of the relics' long-vanished creators, existing now as a collective consciousness. Their forms may not exist in the three-dimensional space, but rather flicker in and out of perception, only glimpsed out of the corner of one's eye. They might be the ones maintaining the relics' enigmatic functions, able to perceive the true nature of the doors in space-time, though unable to utilize them. They are keepers of the eternal gates, existing beyond the comprehension of the other lifeforms.

The entire ecology of the Sky Reefs is a slow, majestic ballet of deep time, life intertwined with the very structure of the cosmos. The hum, the wind, the light, the energies – here, life is not an exception in space, but an integral part of a vast, silent monument adrift in the sea of stars.
