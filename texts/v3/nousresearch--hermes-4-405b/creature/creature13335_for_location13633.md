Inhabiting the dissonant symphony of Absalom Prime are the Relics, enigmatic creatures that have evolved from the very materials and machines that once defined the city’s purpose. At first glance, they appear as rough amalgamations—a haphazard fusion of rusted metal, fractured polymers, and eroded concrete, held together by a synergy of bioluminescent fungi and energetic residue. Their forms mimic the structures around them; some tower like crooked pillars, others stretch into segmented, wormlike configurations that navigate the currents like serpents of living ruin.

The Relics are not mere static remnants. They are animate with an almost mechanical life—parts move in stuttered, awkward patterns, rusted joints creaking softly as they reconfigure themselves. Their internal networks buzz with low currents reminiscent of the city’s turbines, a ceaseless energy that pulses from within. Occasionally, a strange luminescence flickers across their surfaces, mimicking the patterns of the city’s dim bioluminescent strips and adding to the eerie interplay of light and shadow in Absalom Prime.

Each Relic is intricately connected to the pulse of ancient turbines and the hum of residual machinery. Their movements synchronize with the tremors of industrial vibrations; when the turbines accelerate, so does the restless shifting of the Relics. In quiet moments, one can discern deliberate, careful movements—a subtle turning of a metal limb to dislodge stray debris or a gentle, almost sentient retraction from passing currents. These actions suggest an intelligence, however alien or rudimentary, that belies their appearance as mere assemblages of decay.

Their primary activity is a perpetual reconstruction, a reflection of the city’s endless transformation. The Relics break apart pieces of debris with methodical care, and reassemble the fragments into new, unidentifiable forms. These creations are neither fully organic nor mechanical; rather, they are a hybrid of the two, echoing the non-recognized biology hinted at by the city’s elusive energy patterns. It is as if the Relics are engaged in a slow, deliberate act of creation and re-creation, mirroring the dynamic chaos that defines Absalom Prime.

There is also an element of interaction among the Relics. They drift close to each other, occasionally touching with screeching, metallic sounds that evoke a language of sorts—a dialogue of scrap and energy, punctuated by bursts of light. It is speculated that they communicate through these contacts, exchanging information or perhaps even coordinating their continuous labor of reassembly.

Nourishment for the Relics appears to derive from the energy patterns that randomly burst and vanish around the cityscape. They collect the residues, their metallic forms absorbing the ephemeral surges and incorporating them into their ever-changing internal networks. This reliance on unstable energy sources ensures that no Relic is fixed or complete; they are always evolving, forever intertwined with the fluid and unpredictable nature of Absalom Prime.

In essence, the Relics of Absalom Prime are the living embodiment of a dead city’s memory—a testament to its industrial past and a symbol of its turbulent, uncertain future. They are the overlooked caretakers of a world both hidden and ceaselessly evolving, guardians of a dissonant symphony of dilapidated industry and natural energy, where life itself is rendered in the language of mechanical decay.
