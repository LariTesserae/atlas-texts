To visit the Citadel of Whispers, an ordinary human multiverse traveler must prepare for a journey of extreme opposites. They should expect to encounter harsh conditions outside and within the ancient fortress, yet also experience moments of cool respite amidst the incredible heat. Here are some tips for preparing:

1. Clothing: Lightweight, breathable fabrics that can protect from the sun are essential. Long sleeves, pants, and a wide-brimmed hat will help to keep the sun at bay. Layers may be necessary as the traveler moves deeper into the citadel, where temperatures drop to cooler levels. Sturdy shoes with good traction should also be worn to navigate the sand-covered stone floors and slippery vegetation.

2. Water and hydration: The desert surroundings of the Citadel of Whispers are unforgiving, and water is scarce within the fortress as well. It is crucial to carry enough water for the journey or locate a hidden source within the citadel. Travelers should also pack electrolyte supplements and high-energy snacks to maintain hydration and energy levels throughout their exploration.

3. Sun protection: In addition to clothing, travelers should bring high-SPF sunscreen, sunglasses, and lip balm to protect themselves from the relentless sun. A cooling towel or bandana soaked in water can also provide relief from the heat when needed.

4. Navigation and communication: A compass and detailed map of the area would be helpful, as the layout of the citadel may be confusing and disorienting. A flashlight or headlamp should be packed for exploring darker, shadowy areas. A satellite phone or other communication device may be useful for staying in touch with others or calling for help if needed.

5. Shelter: A lightweight, portable tent or bivy sack might be necessary for rest and protection from the elements at night. Depending on the specific conditions within the citadel, travelers may need to seek refuge from the deafening winds or sudden temperature changes.

6. Safety gear: Due to the ancient, unmaintained nature of the fortress, it is advisable to bring safety gear such as a helmet, gloves, and sturdy rope for navigating crumbling structures or areas where vines and foliage may have taken over. A first aid kit should also be carried to address any minor injuries that may occur during exploration.

7. Noise protection: With the constant roaring winds, a pair of earplugs or noise-canceling headphones could provide some relief from the cacophony for a more comfortable exploration experience.

By preparing for these various factors, an ordinary human multiverse traveler can better enjoy the unique, poetic beauty of the Citadel of Whispers and safely navigate the challenges it presents.
