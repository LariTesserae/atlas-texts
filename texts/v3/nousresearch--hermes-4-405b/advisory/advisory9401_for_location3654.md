2. Breathing apparatus: The thick, warm air filled with vapor and mineral particles may be difficult to breathe. A respirator or self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) would ensure a steady supply of clean air.

3. Footwear: Sturdy, waterproof boots with excellent traction are essential for navigating the slippery and uneven terrain. The boots should also be resistant to corrosion from the mineral-rich water and salt deposits.

4. Lighting: As the city is built high in the crags and the environment below is dimly lit, a reliable light source such as a high-powered headlamp or flashlight is crucial for visibility and safety.

5. Navigation tools: Given the labyrinth-like nature of the abandoned infrastructures and the potential for disorientation, a GPS device or compass should be carried to aid in navigation.

6. Communication devices: As the traveler may be far from help, a reliable communication device such as a satellite phone or radio should be brought along to maintain contact with the outside world.

7. Geology and mineralogy knowledge: Familiarity with various minerals, salts, and geological processes can help the traveler understand and navigate the environment more effectively.

8. Knowledge of local fauna: As the city above is teeming with scuttling fauna, understanding their behavior and potential threats can help the traveler avoid dangerous encounters.

9. Emergency supplies: In case of sudden rockfalls, landslides, or other emergencies, the traveler should carry a well-stocked first-aid kit, emergency shelter, and a supply of food and water.

10. Mental preparedness: The overwhelming scale of the city, the constant drip of condensing air, and the eerie silence broken only by the distant thrum of engines can be mentally taxing. The traveler should be prepared for a psychologically challenging experience.

By taking these precautions and being well-prepared, an ordinary human multiverse traveler can safely explore this unique and fascinating location.
