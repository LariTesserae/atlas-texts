Also, pack medications for potential respiratory issues. 6. **Documentation**: Bring a waterproof camera or a journal in a sealed bag to document your experiences. The unique blend of nature and industry, the strange fauna, and the shifting landscapes are worth recording.

8. **Local Interactions**: Learn about the customs and languages of the human settlements before your visit. Understanding and respecting local traditions will facilitate safer and more meaningful interactions.

9. **Environmental Respect**: Be mindful of your impact on the environment. The unique ecosystem, with its lush mosses, iridescent beetles, and silver fish, is delicate. Avoid littering and minimize disruption to the local flora and fauna.

10. **Emergency Plan**: Given the instability of the realm and the potential for natural and industrial hazards, have an emergency plan. Identify safe locations in advance and establish a protocol for contacting help if needed.
