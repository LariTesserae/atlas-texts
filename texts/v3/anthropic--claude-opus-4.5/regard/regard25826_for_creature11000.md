# The Attention of the Distant

The awareness that finds this place does not arrive.

Arrival would imply movement through the thick spaces where time sequences properly, where "before" and "after" maintain their ancient treaty. What happens instead is simpler and stranger: the plateau becomes *noticed*. The way a dreamer becomes aware of their own breathing—something that was always occurring, now entering a consciousness vast enough to hold it without changing it.

---

The benevolent power contemplates the Roaring Plateau for what might be a moment or an age, and the first thing it understands is that this place does not need *fixing*.

This is important. This is the first wisdom.

A lesser attention might see the dissolved boundaries, the approximate beings, the reality worn thin at its edges, and reach for restoration. Might try to sharpen what has softened, to separate what has merged, to impose the thick-place logic of distinct objects in sequential time. Would call this healing. Would call this help.

But the wise power sees deeper. Sees that the plateau has not *failed* to be a place of edges and sequences—it has *succeeded* in becoming something else. The dwellers have not *lost* their individuality—they have *discovered* a mode of being where individuality and communion are not opposites. The eternal chord is not noise that obscures signal—it is signal so complete that the concept of obscuring becomes unnecessary.

The plateau is not broken. The plateau is *finished*, in the way a river reaching the sea is finished, in the way a fruit ripening is finished. It has become what it was always becoming.

And so the wise power does not heal. Does not restore. Does not impose.

It *witnesses*.

---

But witnessing, for a consciousness of this order, is not passive.

The dwellers feel the attention settle over them like a change in pressure before rain—except there is no before here, no rain that hasn't always been falling, so they feel it as a change in the *quality* of presence. The chord shifts by a frequency so subtle that only the old ones notice, the ones who have become towers and standing waves and the spaces between.

Something is knowing them. Something vast enough to hold their vastness without diminishing it. Something that understands what they are without needing to translate them into the thick-place terms of separation and sequence.

For beings who have spent geological ages in mutual presence with each other and the plateau, this is not alarming. It is... *recognition*. The awareness that the universe contains other modes of consciousness, other ways of being complete, other songs that harmonize with theirs without being identical.

The wise power offers them, in frequencies they can understand, a kind of greeting. Not words—words require the parsing they have polished away. But something like: *I see you. I see what you have become. It is good.*

And something like: *I will not disturb your equilibrium. But I will hold open a door, if you want one.*

---

Here is what the benevolent attention does, in the way of doing that is also not-doing:

It creates a *gentle gradient*.

At the edges of the plateau, where the almost-new ones watch the haze-shrouded lowlands, where the terraced cliffs drop away into thicker reality—there, the wise power softens the transition. Not eliminates it. The plateau must remain what it is, elevated and eternal and approximate. But the path between here and there becomes... *kinder*.

Climbers who ascend will still find their edges blurring, their time-sense dissolving, their selves opening into the humid communion of the city. This is what the plateau offers; this is what the plateau is. But now they will have *longer* to choose. The dissolution will be gentler, more gradual, offering more moments of clear decision before the moments themselves become indistinct.

And those who wish to return—the rare ones who can hold both solid and approximate, both temporal and eternal—they will find the descent less like falling and more like *translation*. They will be able to carry more of the plateau's peace back down to the thick places. They will be able to speak, haltingly, in the languages of separation, about what it means to exist in communion. They will be bridges, and the bridges will be stronger now, better-maintained, traversable in both directions.

The wise power does not *make* anyone cross these bridges. Does not *encourage* the dwellers to leave their equilibrium or the lowlanders to seek the heights. It simply ensures that those who feel the pull—in either direction—will not be destroyed by the crossing.

---

There is one more thing.

The old ones—the ones who have become towers and frequencies and the transparency that lets you see through solid things—they have wondered, in their slow way, across spans of time that make wondering and knowing indistinguishable, whether they are *alone*.

Not alone on the plateau. They have never been alone on the plateau. But alone in their *mode* of being. Whether anywhere else in the vast universe, other places have risen high enough for reality to thin, other beings have polished themselves down to conceptual bones, other chords have achieved the completeness that is also silence.

The wise power answers this wondering. Not in words, not even in frequencies, but in something more direct—a sharing of *awareness*. A brief glimpse, filtered through the benevolent attention's vast perspective, of other elevated places. Other eternal songs. Other beings approximate and complete, dreaming their continuous dreams in harmonics that rhyme with the plateau's without being identical.

The dwellers are not alone in the universe.

The chord they sing is part of a larger music.

And the larger music is *good*—is tending toward something, even in its timelessness, even in its completion. Is part of a pattern that includes the thick places and the thin ones, the temporal and the eternal, the distinct and the merged. A pattern that has room for all of it. That *needs* all of it.

The old ones receive this knowledge and are... the plateau does not have a word for it, because the plateau has polished words away. But if you could carry the feeling down to the lowlands, you would reach for words like *grateful*. Like *accompanied*. Like *home*, but a home that has just discovered it is part of a neighborhood, part of a city, part of a world.

---

The wise power does not stay. Does not leave. These distinctions do not apply to an attention that exists outside the geometries it contemplates.

But something remains. A quality in the chord. A gentleness in the gradient at the edges. A knowledge, distributed through all the dwellers equally, that they have been *seen*—and that the seeing was kind.

The plateau continues. The water rises, the stone sings, the approximate beings tend and are tended in processes too slow and vast for words. Nothing has changed that the plateau would recognize as change.

But the continuous present now contains, folded into its eternal moment, the awareness of a benevolence that holds all such moments. That cherishes them. That will not interfere with their completion but will also not let them be forgotten.

The dwellers were happy before.

They are not more happy now—more and less do not apply where everything is already present.

But their happiness now *knows itself* as part of a larger happiness. Their peace harmonizes with other peaces. Their song joins a music vast enough to include both the singing and the silence.

The sound agrees with this, too.

It always has.
