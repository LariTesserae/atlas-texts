There is one small act, and it is not repair.

Deep in the southern wall, behind layers of plating that have not been opened since the chamber was sealed, there is a data alcove. Then the power withdraws.

Not fully—it remains aware, the way one remains aware of a room in a house where someone is sleeping. But it does not watch closely. It does not check for outcomes. It has offered the chamber a single gift, delivered on the chamber's own timescale, and now the gift belongs to the chamber and not to the giver.

The engines continue their slow revolutions. The moths continue their patient descent. Somewhere in the southern wall, water begins to find a new path.

The power turns its attention elsewhere, to other places, other beings. But it remembers the chamber. It will always remember the chamber. Not as a project or a responsibility, but as a teacher.

The chamber did not need the power's wisdom. The chamber had been wise for longer than the power had been watching.
