The wise power observes for a long time before anything else. Not because observation is required for understanding—understanding arrived whole, as understanding does for such a being—but because the watching itself is a form of acknowledgment. The seam and its slow crystalline waking deserve to be witnessed on their own terms, in their own time, before any question of intervention arises.

What the power sees: a place learning to be a place. A hollow where stone has discovered that repetition is possible, where heat and pressure and the patient logic of mineral lattices have conspired to produce something that is not yet life but is no longer mere geology. The crystal thickets growing by precipitation and ordered fracture. The veins testing the difference between inside and outside. The temporal stutters where the rock remembers it has not yet chosen a single direction. All of it unfolding at the pace of cooling magma, which is to say: at exactly the pace it should.

The power considers what could be offered.

It could accelerate the process—compress the millennia of becoming into moments, bring the veins to full articulation, teach the lattices to move rather than merely grow. But this would be a kind of theft. The seam's slowness is not a limitation to be overcome; it is the substance of what the seam is becoming. To hurry it would be to replace its becoming with an arrival that belongs to someone else's sense of time.

It could seed the hollow with life from elsewhere—spores or microbes or the first precursors of something that might one day think. But the seam is already populated. The crystal thickets are citizens. The deliberate emptiness between them is inhabited by the logic of their spacing. To introduce foreign life would be to declare the seam's own forms of persistence insufficient, and they are not insufficient. They are exactly what this place, at this moment, is capable of being.

It could protect the space from what will come—the eventual cooling, the tectonic shifts that will either expose this hollow to a sky it has never known or crush it back into undifferentiated stone. But protection of that kind would freeze the seam in its current state, and the seam is not a state. It is a process. To preserve it against change would be to kill it more thoroughly than any earthquake could.

The power considers what the seam might need, as opposed to what could be given.

And here is what it finds: the seam needs nothing. It is complete in its incompleteness. The crystal lattices do not lack anything by growing slowly. The mechanical veins are not impoverished by not yet knowing what they might pump. The temporal stutters are not errors requiring correction. Everything here is in the long crystalline act of waking, and waking is not a problem to be solved.

So the wise power does something that might not look like anything at all.

It attends.

Not passively, not as a mere witness, but with the kind of attention that acknowledges without demanding, that sees without requiring the seen thing to change in response to being seen. The power lets its awareness rest in the seam like heat rests in the space between lattice spines—present, shaping nothing, part of the conditions under which the slow becoming continues.

And within that attention, the power makes a single small adjustment. Not to the seam itself, but to the wider world's relationship with it.

It ensures that the seam will be found.

Not now. Not soon. But someday, when there are beings capable of finding it—beings with eyes that can perceive the crystal foliage and minds that can recognize the mechanical veins as a mountain's first attempt at articulation—this place will not remain hidden. The geological processes that might have sealed it permanently will leave a fissure. The erosion that might have exposed it too soon will wait. The hollow will persist in its slow waking until the moment arrives when something else, something curious and capable of wonder, can enter and see what the rock has been becoming all this time.

The power does not know what those future witnesses will do with what they find. Perhaps they will study the lattices and learn something about the conditions under which order emerges from heat. Perhaps they will marvel at the veins and recognize in them the same impulse toward articulation that moves in their own bodies. Perhaps they will simply stand in the heated air and feel, without understanding, that they are in the presence of something that has been waking for longer than their species has existed.

Or perhaps they will not come at all. Perhaps the fissure will close, the hollow will cool, the crystals will become ordinary stone again. The power does not guarantee outcomes. It only ensures that the possibility remains open—that the seam's long becoming is not foreclosed by accident before it has the chance to be witnessed by something other than itself.

This is what the power offers: not change, not acceleration, not protection. Only the ongoing opportunity to continue, and the eventual chance to be known.

The attention withdraws. The seam continues its slow exhalation. A lattice spine lengthens by a fraction of a nanometer. A vent cycles forward. Somewhere in the rock, a new vein begins the long process of deciding whether it will become a cylinder or a plate.

Nothing has changed. Everything has been honored.
