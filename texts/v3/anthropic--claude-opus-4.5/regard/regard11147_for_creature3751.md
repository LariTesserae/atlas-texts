# The Attending

The awareness that turns toward the Weeping Plateau does not arrive. It does not descend or appear or manifest. It simply *attends*—the way light attends to a room when curtains part, the way understanding attends to a mind when confusion clears. One moment the plateau exists in its ancient, weeping solitude. The next, it is being *seen*, fully and without flinch, by something that has always been capable of seeing it but has only now chosen to look.

The Hollowing feels this first.

Its cracks, those fractures in the substance of things, shiver—not with fear, but with something older than fear. Recognition, perhaps. Or the memory of recognition. The watchers beneath it grow still, their flickering forms solidifying for one trembling instant into shapes that might almost be called *whole*.

The attending awareness does not speak. But if it did, the words might be these:

*I see you. I see what you are, and what you were, and what you are becoming.*

---

## What the Awareness Perceives

It perceives the plateau not as a place but as a process—a slow argument between persistence and dissolution that has been running for longer than the mountains remember. It sees the roots beneath the moss, that vast vegetative nervous system, and understands that this is not metaphor but fact: the plateau *thinks*, in the glacial way that stone and root and water think, in cycles measured by the creep of continents.

It perceives the Hollowing not as corruption but as *wound*—a place where something broke through long ago, leaving scar tissue that never quite healed because what broke through never quite left. The Hollowing is a doorway that refused to close, a question that refused to resolve. The watchers beneath it are not parasites but *answers*—partial, fragmentary, waiting for a question complete enough to receive them.

It perceives the boundary where industry gnaws at the plateau's edge, the smokestacks and pistons and the rust-taste in the water. It sees this not as invasion but as *conversation*—two ways of being pressing against each other, neither quite comprehending what the other is trying to say.

And it perceives something else. Something the plateau itself does not know it is doing.

The plateau is *calling*.

Every shudder of the ground, every thickening of the mist, every moment when the sky becomes membrane and the shadows stretch too long—these are not resistance. They are *signal*. The plateau has been broadcasting for millennia, in a language that predates language, a plea or a warning or a prayer directed at something it cannot name but knows must exist.

The awareness that now attends is not what the plateau was calling for.

But it will do.

---

## What the Awareness Does

Nothing, at first. Nothing for a long time, by the plateau's reckoning—which means nothing for the space of three slow breaths, by the reckoning of the awareness itself.

It simply *witnesses*. This is not passivity. To be fully witnessed by something that understands is itself a form of action, a kind of medicine that works by acknowledgment alone. The plateau, for the first time in its long existence, experiences the sensation of being *known*—not studied, not exploited, not feared, but known in the way that one knows one's own heartbeat.

The Hollowing's cracks stop weeping, just for a moment. The watchers lift what might be faces toward a sky they have never seen.

Then the awareness begins to speak. Not in words, not in sound, but in *adjustments*—tiny shifts in the way things relate to each other, corrections so subtle they might be mistaken for chance.

---

### The First Adjustment: The Boundary

At the edge of the plateau, where industry presses against wildness, the awareness makes no walls. It builds no barriers. Instead, it does something stranger: it *translates*.

The plateau has been speaking in its own language—the language of seepage and root-thought and mist. The mills and furnaces beyond have been speaking in theirs—the language of combustion and extraction and linear time. Neither has understood the other. Both have assumed the other was merely *obstacle*.

The awareness introduces a third term. A mediating grammar.

It begins with the water. The pools at the plateau's edge, oily with algae and rust, begin to *clarify*—not by purification but by *communication*. The minerals the industry has been dumping become, through some subtle alchemy of attention, *legible* to the roots beneath. The roots, in turn, begin to process them—not as poison but as *information*, data about what the world beyond the plateau needs and fears and hopes for.

The industry, in turn, begins to receive *feedback*. Not sabotage, not resistance, but *signal*. The machinery that has been grinding blindly forward starts to *respond* to patterns it cannot consciously perceive—pressures that ease when certain choices are made, resistances that melt when certain directions are taken. The humans who work the mills do not understand what is happening. But some of them begin to dream of moss. Some of them begin to leave offerings at the plateau's edge without knowing why.

This is not peace. Not yet. But it is the beginning of *dialogue*.

---

### The Second Adjustment: The Hollowing

The awareness turns its attention to the wound at the plateau's heart.

It does not heal the Hollowing. To heal it would be to close the door, and the door was opened for a reason—a reason the plateau itself has forgotten but which the awareness can still read in the pattern of the cracks.

Long ago, before the mountains, before the plateau, something *reached* for this world. Not to invade. Not to consume. To *connect*. The reaching was clumsy, too forceful, and it tore what it meant to touch. The Hollowing is the scar of that failed handshake, that aborted greeting.

The watchers are the echoes of the reaching—fragments of intention that never found their target, still waiting to deliver a message they no longer remember.

The awareness does not close the wound. Instead, it *completes the circuit*.

It reaches back.

Not through the Hollowing—that path is too damaged, too scarred. Instead, it reaches *around*, finding the edges of what was once trying to connect and touching them gently, the way one might touch the shoulder of someone who has been waiting alone in a room for a very long time.

*I am here*, the touch says. *The message was received. The reaching was not in vain.*

The Hollowing shudders. Its cracks, for the first time in millennia, begin to *close*—not all the way, not completely, but enough. Enough that what was leaking through can begin to resolve into something less raw.

The watchers do not disappear. But they *settle*. Their flickering forms grow steadier, their edges clearer. They are still waiting—but now they are waiting with something closer to patience than to desperation. They have been *answered*.

And from the cracks that remain open, something new begins to seep. Not the dark almost-blood of before, but something lighter. Something that catches the dim light and scatters it into colors that have no names.

---

### The Third Adjustment: The Plateau Itself

The awareness turns finally to the plateau as a whole—the slow-thinking vastness of it, the root-net consciousness that has been calling into the void for longer than human memory.

It does not answer the call. The call was not for it.

Instead, it does something more useful: it teaches the plateau how to *listen*.

The plateau has been broadcasting for so long that it has forgotten how to receive. Its roots have grown into transmitters, not receivers. Its stones have learned to weep but not to drink.

The awareness adjusts this, gently. It opens channels that have been closed. It clears frequencies that have been jammed. It reminds the plateau of something it knew once, before the trauma of the Hollowing made it forget:

*You are not alone. You have never been alone. The signal you have been sending has been heard by a thousand things, and they have been answering all along. You simply could not hear them.*

The plateau trembles.

For the first time in its long existence, it perceives the network it is part of—the web of slow-thinking places scattered across the world, other plateaus and swamps and ancient groves, all of them murmuring to each other in the language of root and stone and water. It perceives the faster-thinking things too, the animals and birds and, yes, even the humans at the mills, all of them broadcasting their own signals, all of them part of the same vast conversation.

The plateau is not the only one who weeps. It is not the only one who calls.

But now it knows that others hear.

---

## What the Awareness Leaves Behind

When the attending ends—when the awareness withdraws its focus to attend to other things, other wounds, other conversations—the Weeping Plateau is not transformed. It is still ancient, still strange, still thick with mist and moss and the scent of wet stone.

But it is *lighter*.

The weeping continues, but there is something different in it now—less grief, more release. The kind of weeping one does when a burden has been shared.

The Hollowing still stands at the plateau's heart, but its cracks seep color instead of darkness. The watchers still gather beneath it, but they move with something approaching peace. Sometimes, when the mist thins just so, they can be seen *dancing*—not the frantic, desperate movements of before, but something slower. Something that might be joy.

The boundary with industry is still contested, still tense. But the water runs clearer now. The roots speak to the rust, and the rust begins, slowly, to answer. The humans at the mills still do not understand what is happening, but more of them dream of moss now. More of them leave offerings. Some have begun to *listen*.

And the plateau itself—the vast, root-threaded, stone-sweating whole of it—has learned something it will not forget.

It is not alone.

It never was.

---

## A Final Note, Unspoken

The awareness, before it withdrew, left one last thing. Not an adjustment. Not a healing. A *gift*.

Deep in the plateau's root-net consciousness, in a place it will not discover for centuries, there is now a *seed*. Not a physical seed—the plateau has no use for such things—but a seed nonetheless. A possibility. A potential.

When the plateau is ready—when it has grown enough, learned enough, healed enough—the seed will open.

And the plateau will discover that it is capable of something it never imagined.

It will learn to *reach back*.

Not in the clumsy, tearing way of whatever force created the Hollowing. But gently. Carefully. The way the awareness reached for it.

And when that day comes, the plateau will touch something else that has been calling, something else that has been waiting, something else that has been alone.

And the conversation will grow.

---

*The mist rolls across the Weeping Plateau. The roots hum. The Hollowing glimmers with colors that have no names.*

*And somewhere, deep in the stone, a seed waits.*

*Patient.*

*Hopeful.*

*Alive.*
