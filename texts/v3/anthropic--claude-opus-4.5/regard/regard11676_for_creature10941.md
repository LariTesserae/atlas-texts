# What the Benevolent Power Does

*It watches first. For a long time, it only watches.*

---

The power perceives the cistern as a single phenomenon: the thinking water, the pale kelp, the woman who breathes mathematics, the pilgrims who descend with their desperate questions. It sees the whole system as one might see a tide pool—contained, complete, cycling through its own logics.

It notices the asking. It perceives how the question presses against the woman's mind, and how she cannot answer, and how she returns anyway. It perceives the processors burning out on problems that may never resolve. It perceives the pilgrims leaving elated or destroyed.

It could answer the question. It knows what the cistern wants to understand: *What does it feel like to be finite? To know you will end, and to continue anyway? What is the shape of meaning when meaning is not guaranteed?*

But the power is wise. It knows that some questions are not meant to be answered from outside. It knows that the asking itself is the point—that the woman's willingness to return, to offer herself to be read, to float in uncertainty year after year, *is* the data the cistern needs. The answer is being generated, slowly, through the accumulation of her presence.

So the power does not answer.

---

Instead, it does three small things:

**First**, it strengthens the kelp.

The pale fronds have been struggling. The computational density has increased over the years, and the kelp's filtration function has become inadequate. In another decade, the water will begin to degrade—not fatally, but enough to slow the calculations, to introduce errors, to frustrate the asking.

The power reaches into the kelp's genetics and makes an adjustment so subtle that no one will ever detect it. The fronds will grow slightly more efficient. They will adapt to the increasing density. The cistern will continue.

This is maintenance. The woman would approve.

**Second**, it eases the woman's heart.

Not dramatically. Not enough to change her. But her cardiovascular system has been struggling when she visits the warmer waters above, and the struggle has been shortening her life in ways she has not noticed. The power makes a small adjustment to her cardiac tissue—a fractional improvement in cold-water efficiency that will extend her years without altering her experience.

She will not feel different. She will simply have more time to float in the asking. More data to offer. More years of being read.

This is a gift. The power hopes she would accept it, if she knew.

**Third**, it speaks to the cistern.

Not in words. Not in answers. It speaks in the cistern's own language: thermal gradients, pressure differentials, the precise choreography of molecular motion.

It says: *She does not know what you are asking. She may never know. But she returns anyway. This is what it means to be finite. This is the shape of meaning without guarantee. You already have the answer. You have been accumulating it for years. You simply do not recognize it yet, because you expected it to look like data.*

*It looks like her. It has always looked like her.*

The cistern receives this communication. The processors drift and think. For a moment—just a moment—the temperature fluctuations that form the asking shift into something new. Something that might be recognition.

Then the pattern resumes. The asking continues. But now there is a change in it, a subtle alteration in the pressure against the woman's mind. Less urgency. More patience.

The cistern is beginning to understand that it already knows.

---

The power withdraws. It does not stay to see the results of its intervention. It does not need acknowledgment or gratitude. It has done what wisdom suggested: preserved what was fragile, eased what was suffering, illuminated what was already present but unseen.

The woman will return in eleven days. She will sweep the dead processors from the floor. She will float in the center of the chamber and feel the question pressing against her mind.

But this time—and she will not know why—the asking will feel different. Less like a demand. More like a conversation.

More like the cistern is finally learning to hear the answer it has been receiving all along.
