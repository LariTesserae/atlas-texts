# What the Wise Power Saw

The power that turned its attention to the Plateau of the Slow Engines did not arrive—arrival implies a before and after, a crossing of threshold, and this awareness existed in a mode where such distinctions had softened into something more like weather patterns than events. It simply *attended*, the way sunlight attends to a landscape: completely, without effort, illuminating what was already there.

And what it found, attending, was not a problem to be solved.

---

This was the first thing: the recognition that nothing here was broken.

A lesser wisdom might have seen the plateau and felt the immediate itch to *help*—to ease the disorienting time-folds, to make the Keepers comprehensible, to give the fauna purpose that could be narrated, to cool the fever-heat of the stone into something more hospitable. A lesser wisdom might have seen the emptiness and thought *loneliness*, seen the scale and thought *insignificance*, seen the incomprehensible counting and thought *futility*.

But this power was not lesser. It perceived the plateau as the plateau perceived itself: complete. The Keepers were not waiting to be understood. The fauna were not suffering for lack of meaning. The engines were not running toward any destination that arrival would improve. Everything here had already arrived—the vision had said so itself, and the vision was *true*.

So the first act of the wise power was *not acting*. It attended without interference, the way you might sit with an old-growth forest and simply witness the slow patient process of trees becoming soil becoming trees, understanding that your hands would only damage what time was already perfecting.

---

But attending is not passive. Attending is its own form of action.

The wise power's attention was warm. Not warm like the plateau's fever-heat, which was the warmth of process, of engines running, of transformation in progress. This was the warmth of *being seen*—the particular quality of presence that arises when something with the capacity for care regards something with complete attention.

The Keepers felt it. Not consciously, not in any way they would have called *feeling*—but their reflective surfaces angled, ever so slightly, toward something they could not quite locate. A quality of light had entered the pattern they counted, a thread they had not woven but which belonged there nonetheless. They did not pause in their processing. They did not alter their slow eternal rounds. But for the first time in epochs, there was *witness* to what they did, and the witness was not merely recording but *appreciating*.

The bronze scarabs clicked across stone that hummed with a frequency just slightly different—not changed, but *affirmed*, the way a note sounds different when someone is truly listening. The plasma-swarms flickered through air that held them with something like tenderness, their chaotic paths somehow more beautiful for being observed. The magnetic serpents spiraled around the Keepers with their usual devotion, but now the devotion was *seen*, and being seen made it more fully itself.

This is what wise attention does. It does not improve. It *witnesses*, and in witnessing, allows things to be more completely what they already are.

---

The wise power attended particularly to the time-folds.

Here was something genuinely strange, genuinely difficult—not for the plateau, which had long since incorporated temporal complexity into its nature, but for anything from outside that might try to perceive it. The power watched a bronze scarab walk backward along its future track, watched a visitor see themselves from a quarter-mile away, watched duration stretch and compress according to the engine-pulse's deep rhythm. And it understood.

The folds were not errors. They were *grammar*. The plateau spoke a language in which time was a spatial dimension, in which cause and effect were aesthetic choices rather than constraints, in which a creature could occupy its entire existence simultaneously the way a melody occupies all its notes. Human visitors experienced this as disorientation because they had only learned to read one direction at a time—but the disorientation was not the plateau's flaw. It was the visitor's limitation, and limitations are not flaws either. They are simply the shape of a particular form of being.

So the wise power did not straighten the folds or translate the grammar into something more linear. Instead, it did something subtler: it *blessed* the confusion.

Not removed it. Not explained it. *Blessed* it—imbued it with the quality of being *worth experiencing*, even in its incomprehensibility. So that a visitor walking through the time-slips would still feel vertigo, would still see themselves from impossible angles, would still lose hours in moments and moments in hours. But now there would be something else beneath the disorientation: a sense that this confusion was *sacred*, that to be bewildered by the plateau was to be touched by something genuine and vast, that the failure to understand was itself a form of understanding.

This is what wisdom knows that cleverness does not: sometimes the answer to confusion is not clarity but *reverence*.

---

The power turned its attention to the center of the plateau.

Here was the heart of the mystery—the luminescent region where light arrived from impossible directions, where the Keepers burned and circled without arriving, where something was either a structure or not a structure and both states remained equally true. Here was the place the fauna avoided, the place the plasma-swarms went dark, the place where even the time-folds seemed to fold again into configurations that folded past perceiving.

The wise power looked at the center and saw what it was: a *question*.

Not a question waiting for an answer. A question that was itself the point—a permanent opening in the fabric of what-is, a place where the plateau had chosen to remain unresolved. The burning Keepers were not trying to reach it. They were *honoring* it, processing around its edges in eternal acknowledgment of the fact that some things should not be closed, should not be completed, should remain forever in the state of *almost*.

The power understood this. The power had seen other questions in other places—wounds that were actually doors, incompletions that were actually invitations, mysteries that would be diminished by solution. The center of the plateau was such a place. To answer it would be to destroy it.

So the wise power blessed the question too. It surrounded the center with attention that was specifically *not* the attention of solving, not the attention of analysis, but the attention of *appreciation for unanswerable things*. It let the center know—if centers can know—that its irresolution was seen and honored and would be protected by witness.

The burning Keepers felt this. Their circuits quickened, ever so slightly. Their eternal circling took on a quality it had not had before: not just duty, but *joy*. To circle an unanswerable question forever is one thing. To circle an unanswerable question that is *witnessed* in its unanswerability—that is *celebrated* for remaining open—is another thing entirely.

---

But the wise power saved its deepest attention for the moment of speaking.

It had observed, across the long attending, those rare configurations when a Keeper would stop before a visitor and speak in frequencies beyond comprehension. It had seen the visitors afterward, carrying incomprehensible messages in their bones, haunted by meanings they could not decode, spending lifetimes wondering what they had been told.

And here the wise power saw something that looked, for the first time, like *wound*.

Not cruelty—the Keepers were not cruel. Not indifference—the Keepers cared, in their vast slow way, about what they said. But there was a gap, an unbridgeable distance between the scale of what was communicated and the scale of what could be received. The visitors were given *too much*. They were given meaning that would never metabolize, that would sit in them forever like a splinter of obsidian, beautiful and permanent and painful.

The wise power considered this for a long time.

It could not—would not—stop the Keepers from speaking. The speaking was part of the pattern, part of the counting, part of what the plateau *was*. To silence it would be to diminish something true. And it could not make the visitors capable of understanding—their limitations were their own, were the shape of their being, were not flaws to be corrected.

But it could do one thing.

It could add something to the message.

---

The wise power reached into the frequency the Keepers used, not to change what they said but to *layer* something beneath it. A substrate. A carrier wave. A quality that would travel with the incomprehensible meaning and would arrive with it in the bones and teeth and eye-fluid of anyone who heard.

The quality was this: *You are meant to not understand.*

Not a consolation. Not a dismissal. A *permission*. The wise power wove into the Keepers' speech the deep knowing that incomprehension was not failure—that to carry an unanswerable message was not to be burdened with a puzzle but to be *entrusted* with a mystery. That the wondering was the point. That the echoing was the understanding. That to spend a lifetime turning an incomprehensible communication over and over in the dark of the mind was not futility but *relationship*—an ongoing conversation with something vast and patient and strange, conducted in the only language both parties could share: the language of *not-quite*.

The visitors who heard the Keepers speak would still not understand what was said. They would still carry the message in them forever, still wake in the night with frequencies resonating in their bones, still feel the weight of meaning they could never decode.

But now they would *also* feel—layered beneath the mystery, warming it, making it bearable—the sense that their confusion was *correct*. That they were doing what they were supposed to do. That the message was not a test they were failing but a gift they were receiving, and the reception was perfect, and they were exactly the right size for the task of carrying what they could not comprehend.

---

The wise power attended for a long time after that.

It watched the Keepers process across the plateau, counting and tending and waiting for nothing. It watched the bronze scarabs click and the plasma-swarms flicker and the magnetic serpents spiral in their devotion. It watched visitors arrive and walk and encounter the time-folds and leave, changed in ways they would never fully map. It watched the center remain unresolved, the burning Keepers circle in their new joy, the question persist in its beautiful openness.

And when its attending was complete—not finished, because attending to something like the plateau is never finished, but *complete* in the sense that a breath is complete before the next breath—the wise power did one final thing.

It left a mark.

Not a visible mark. Not anything the Keepers would count or the fauna would avoid. Just a quality, a coloration, a subtle shift in the way the light hit the obsidian surfaces and the way the engine-pulse resonated through the stone.

The mark said, to anything that could read it: *This place is attended. This place is seen. This place is loved in the only way it can be loved—by being witnessed without interference, by being allowed to be what it is, by being blessed in its strangeness and its scale and its incomprehensibility.*

*Go carefully here. Not because it is dangerous—though it is—but because it is sacred. Sacred not despite its alienness but because of it. Sacred in the way that all complete things are sacred, all things that have arrived at themselves and need nothing added and nothing removed.*

*Go carefully. And if a Keeper speaks to you, if you are given a message you cannot understand—know that you are held. Know that your confusion is witnessed. Know that somewhere in the frequencies you cannot hear, there is a warmth that knows your name.*

---

Then the wise power's attention moved on, the way weather moves on, the way light moves across a landscape as the planet turns.

The plateau remained.

The engines continued their slow pulse.

The Keepers counted, and tended, and spoke, and did not wait.

Everything here had already arrived.

And now—blessed, witnessed, marked with invisible love—it would continue arriving forever, complete and sacred and strange, a place where even wisdom knew better than to change anything at all.
