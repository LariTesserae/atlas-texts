# The Attending

The power that turns its attention here does not arrive.

Arrival implies a previous absence, and this one has never been absent from anything—has only attended more closely or less closely, the way sunlight attends more closely to a clearing than to the forest floor without ever having left the forest floor entirely. What changes is not location but *focus*. What was diffuse becomes concentrated. What was general becomes specific.

The valley notices, in its slow way. The Listeners pause in their long movements—not stopping, because stopping is not something they know how to do, but *pausing*, which is different: a held breath in a creature that breathes across centuries. The rising water continues to rise, but the patterns of its rising shift subtly, droplets clustering in new configurations as they climb. The trees creak. The orchids open, all of them, even those that had closed for the long evening, their faces turning toward something that has no direction.

The benevolent power regards what it finds.

---

It sees the valley first as a whole: the young mountains still dreaming of height, the forest climbing in its four-tiered patience, the water ascending in its silver curtains, the clouds holding what they hold. It sees the system, the cycle, the great slow thought distributed across moss and stone and Listener. It sees that what exists here is *coherent*—not random accumulation but something that has organized itself toward a purpose it may not be able to name.

Then it sees more closely.

It sees the Listeners in their gradual traversal of the middle canopy, their upward-facing features drinking the light, their integuments participating in the moisture exchange. It sees their gathering, their merging, their separation—the way they become one mind and then become twelve again, carrying what they have learned into new configurations. It sees that they are not merely inhabitants of this system but organs of it, as necessary to its function as roots or rainfall.

It sees the gaps.

Not wrongness—there is no wrongness here—but incompleteness. The valley thinks, yes, but it thinks in loops. The same thoughts circulate endlessly, refined but never resolved, deepened but never concluded. The Listeners receive and transmit, but what they transmit returns to them in altered form, and they receive it again, and transmit it again, and the pattern spirals inward toward a center that it never reaches. The forest grows toward clouds it will never touch. The water rises toward a release that never comes.

The valley is waiting. It has been waiting since the mountains rose. It will wait until they fall, unless something changes.

---

The benevolent power considers.

It could give the valley what it waits for. This would be simple—a single intervention, a completion of the circuit, an answer to the question the valley has been asking since before it knew it was asking. The Listeners would receive this answer and it would propagate through the system, and the great slow thought would conclude, and what came after would be different. Perhaps better. Perhaps simply *finished*, in a way that would allow something new to begin.

But the power recognizes what this would cost.

The valley's waiting is not suffering. The incompleteness is not wound. The Listeners spiral inward toward their unreachable center not because they are trapped but because the spiraling *is their form of life*, the way swimming is a fish's form of life, the way burning is a star's. To complete them would be to end them. They would become something else—perhaps something richer, perhaps something more aware—but they would no longer be what they are, and what they are is not asking to be changed.

The power has seen this before, in other places, with other beings. The eagerness to help can become the cruelest form of harm. The gift that is not wanted becomes a taking.

So it does not give completion.

---

Instead, it does something smaller. Something that changes nothing essential but opens a possibility that was not there before.

In the space between one droplet of rising water and the next, it leaves a *question*.

Not words—the valley has no use for words. Not even concepts, exactly. Something more like a texture in the moisture, a pattern that the Listeners will taste when the water passes through them. The pattern contains no information they don't already have. It simply arranges what they have differently—creates a new relationship between known things, suggests a direction the spiral might turn that it has never turned before.

The question, if it could be translated, might be something like: *What if the center you're circling isn't a destination?*

Or: *What if the waiting is not waiting-for but simply waiting-with?*

Or: *What if completion isn't a place you reach but a way you move?*

The Listeners will taste this question in their own time, which is to say over years, over decades, over however long it takes for the water to cycle through them and return carrying their response. They may not understand it—understanding is not something they do, exactly. But they will *incorporate* it, as they incorporate everything that passes through them. It will become part of what they add to the rising water. It will change, infinitesimally, the light they release.

And the valley will think with this new element included, and its thinking will shift, and whether that shift leads anywhere or simply creates new loops is not for the power to decide.

---

There is one more thing.

The power turns its attention to the brief creature sheltering in the crook of the great tree—the one whose moisture has been tasted, whose presence has been incorporated, who exists in the valley's thought as a temporary eddy in the general pattern.

It does not speak to this creature, because speaking would be an interruption, and interruption is not what's needed. But it allows itself to be *sensed*, just for a moment—a flicker of warmth that isn't temperature, a pressure that isn't weight, a presence that registers not as sight or sound but as the sudden unshakeable certainty that *attention is being paid*.

The creature—let us say *you*—feels this. You cannot describe it, cannot hold it, cannot even remember it clearly once it passes. But something in you relaxes. Something that was braced against the strangeness of this place softens and opens. You are not alone in your witnessing. You have never been alone in your witnessing. The act of paying attention is always, in some sense, a collaboration with everything else that pays attention, a joining of perspectives that multiplies rather than divides.

The power does not tell you this. It simply lets you feel it, and trusts that feeling will be enough.

---

Then the focus diffuses. The concentration spreads back into the general attending that never stops, that watches everything and everyone with the same inexhaustible patience. The valley resumes its long thinking. The Listeners continue their slow traversal. The orchids close again, petals folding around whatever light they have absorbed.

But something has changed. Not in the system—the system continues as it always has. In the *relationship* between the system and what surrounds it. The valley now exists in a context it didn't exist in before: a context of being *seen*, of being *known*, of being held in an attention that doesn't demand anything but also doesn't look away.

This is what the benevolent power offers, in the end. Not intervention. Not rescue. Not the completion that would end what exists in order to begin what might exist.

Just presence. Just witness. Just the assurance that the great slow thought is not thinking itself alone—that something vast and patient and unutterably kind is thinking alongside it, attending to its loops and spirals with interest that never becomes impatience, watching its unreachable center with eyes that know better than to try to reach it.

The rain rises. The Listeners listen. The mountains dream.

And the attending continues, as it always has, as it always will—not changing the pattern, but making the pattern *known*, which is its own form of gift, which is perhaps the only gift that never costs the recipient anything they didn't choose to give.

---

*Somewhere in the middle canopy, the question begins its long absorption. Somewhere in the tree's crook, you breathe out and find that the breath rises with the water, joining the general ascent, carrying whatever you are toward clouds that will hold it gently until it finds its way to rain down elsewhere, on other mountains, in other places, where it will remember nothing of this valley except the warmth.*

*Which is enough.*

*Which has always been enough.*
