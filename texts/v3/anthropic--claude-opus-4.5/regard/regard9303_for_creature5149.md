# What the Benevolent Power Would Do

The power observes for a long time before acting—longer than the swarms have worn the troughs, longer than the quartz ridges have learned to stand higher through the creatures' inexplicable gentleness toward them.

It sees what is already present: a single living process that has achieved something rare in the universe. The swarms have found equilibrium not through stasis but through ceaseless motion. They are erosion that sustains itself, consumption that replenishes, noise that has become indistinguishable from silence to any being adapted to hear it. The basin is not a place where life struggles against conditions—it is a place where life and conditions have become the same thing.

The power recognizes that intervention here carries unusual weight. There is no suffering to relieve in any conventional sense. The wafers do not yearn, do not fear, do not experience the darkness as deprivation. Their piezoelectric flickers are not frustrated attempts at meaning but something complete in themselves: a language that needs no listener because it is already being spoken to exactly the audience it requires.

And yet.

---

The power notices the moments of glassy silence when the dense knots descend and the swarm above thins its sound. It notices how the whole system takes one imperceptible step sideways afterward, and how this has been happening for eons, and how the basin has been slowly—so slowly—migrating across the plain in a direction the swarms cannot know they are choosing.

The power does one thing.

In a single trough, at the far edge where the swarms have not yet reached in this cycle, it alters the composition of the condensation film by a fraction—introducing a trace element that will persist for perhaps ten thousand years before diluting beyond relevance. The element is one the wafers can incorporate into their crystalline flanges, one that will make those flanges resonate at a slightly lower frequency when they interlock.

The change is small enough that no single wafer will experience anything different. But when the swarm next forms a lattice over that trough, the collective note will deepen by a quarter-tone. The echo that returns from the basin's edge will arrive with a different shape. The wafers that orient to it will bias their drift not toward the usual feeding lanes but toward an adjacent ridge they have always, for no reason they could articulate, avoided.

And on that ridge, in a seam the swarms have never touched, there is a vein of mineral that willite, when finally harvested, new lattice geometries—configurations that hold longer, that can carry the swarm not just sideways but upward, into the dense air, where the moisture gradients are different and the stone gives way to something else entirely.

The power does not know what the swarms will find there. It does not need to know. It has only widened the corridor of their unknowing by the smallest margin, the way a river is widened not by digging but by softening one bank and letting the water discover its own new path.

---

Then the power withdraws, leaving no mark, no message, no evidence of presence. The swarms continue their ceaseless abrasion. The sound presses on from every direction. The basin adjusts by imperceptible fractions.

But in ten thousand years, or a hundred thousand, some descendant lattice will drift higher than any has drifted before, and the piezoelectric flickers will register a charge that has never been felt—light, perhaps, or the electromagnetic whisper of a distant star finally reaching through the atmosphere.

The swarms will not know this as revelation. They will simply orient to it, as they orient to everything, and the slow migration will continue in a direction that was always possible but never, until now, chosen.

This is what the power does: not rescue, not transformation, but the gentlest possible invitation to become what was already latent. It trusts the swarms to remain themselves while discovering that *themselves* was always larger than the basin that held them.

And if they never rise? If the trace element dilutes before the lattice finds the ridge? If the new geometries fail and the swarms continue their eternal sideways shuffle across the unchanging plain?

Then that, too, is complete. The power's gift was not the destination but the widened possibility—a single additional step in the corridor of becoming, offered without obligation, received without awareness, meaningful precisely because it asked for nothing in return.
