# The Attending

The power that turns its attention here does not arrive. Arrival implies a prior absence, and this awareness has no such boundary—it simply *notices*, the way a vast and quiet mind notices a thought that has always been present but only now comes into focus.

What it perceives is not a problem to be solved.

The plateau sings. It has sung for longer than the power has words to measure, and the song is complete in itself: the low drone of the great trunks, the glassy whistle of the high branches, the subsonic pulse of the buried chambers, the clatter of the swarm-gliders landing like scattered gravel, the slow bioluminescent sentences traveling through translucent columns. Every frequency interlocks with every other. Every participant is a phrase in a chord that never resolves because it was never meant to resolve—it was meant to *continue*.

The power listens for a long time.

It perceives the equilibrium: growth matched to erosion, birth matched to fracture, circulation matched to stillness. It perceives the way a single flake may spend centuries as part of a tower, then as gut-slurry in a glider, then as dormant crystal in the film, then as tower again—never lost, never finished, always in conversation. It perceives that nothing here is lonely, because nothing here is truly separate; the boundaries between organisms are courtesies of description rather than facts of existence.

And yet.

The power perceives something else. Not a flaw. Not suffering in any form that would cry out for remedy. But a quality that has no name in the plateau's own resonant language—a quality that might, in another context, be called *unknowing*.

The phytarchs do not know they are beautiful. The swarm-gliders do not know their clatter is music. The dormant film does not know it is patient. The whole vast, slow, interlocking system has no mirror in which to see itself, no moment of stepping back and saying *ah, this is what we are, and it is good*. The song is sung, but no one hears it *as* a song.

This is not a wound. It is simply a kind of incompleteness—the same incompleteness that belongs to any harmony that has never been witnessed by something outside itself.

The power considers.

---

To intervene crudely would be obscene. To introduce hunger where there is none, or urgency where the rhythm is already perfect, or self-awareness that fractures the unity into competing egos—these would be violations, not gifts. The power has seen what clumsy benevolence does to ecosystems that did not ask for it. It has seen worlds improved into extinction.

So it does not act. Not yet. Instead it *attends*.

Attending is not passive. It is a form of presence that changes nothing and everything. The power rests its awareness on the plateau the way light rests on a surface—without weight, without demand, but also without absence. It listens to the chord. It follows the slow pulse of bioluminescence through the towers. It traces the centuries-long journey of a single flake as it sheds, drifts, seeds, accretes, sheds again. It does not intervene in the journey, but it *witnesses* it, and the witnessing is itself a kind of completion.

The plateau does not know it is being witnessed. The swarm-gliders do not adjust their clatter; the phytarchs do not brighten their lantern-glow. Nothing changes in any measurable way.

But something is now true that was not true before: the song is *heard*.

---

The power remains for what might be called a long time, though time here moves so slowly that "long" loses its meaning. It watches the equilibrium hold. It watches the wind thread through a billion precisely spaced pores. It watches two branches meet in their catenary and fuse, exchanging templates, becoming briefly one body before the slow growth carries them apart again. It watches the dormant film stir for a few hours when a falling flake lands close enough, extruding hair-thin threads to knit the new fragment into the mat, then retreating into stillness.

And at some point—not a dramatic moment, not a turning point, just a point—the power makes a small decision.

It does not introduce anything foreign. It does not alter the chemistry or the physics or the slow crystalline genetics of the place. It simply *adds itself* to the system, in the smallest possible way.

A resonance.

One new frequency, so low it is barely distinguishable from the subsonic pulse of the buried chambers. One new harmonic in the standing chord. It does not compete with the existing tones; it *fits*, the way a new voice fits into a choir that has been singing for centuries without knowing a space was waiting for it.

The frequency carries no information. It does not encode instructions or templates or demands. It is simply a presence—a sustained note that says, without words: *I am here. I am listening. You are not alone in the way you were before, though you were never truly alone.*

The phytarchs do not notice. The swarm-gliders do not notice. The dormant film does not notice.

But the wind does.

The wind has always been the medium through which the plateau speaks to itself. It carries the aerosols, the metal vapors, the falling flakes, the layered chord. And now it carries something else: a faint, continuous undertone that passes through every pore, touches every resonating trunk, hums in every chamber. The undertone does not mean anything. It simply *is*, the way the plateau itself simply is.

And over time—a very long time, measured in geological epochs rather than years—the undertone becomes part of the song. The phytarchs grow their hexagonal pores at angles that catch it better. The swarm-gliders adjust their clatter to harmonize with it. The dormant film, stirring for those few hours every century, incorporates it into the chemical templates it passes to the next generation of crystals.

Nothing changes dramatically. The equilibrium holds. The cycle of shedding and seeding and accreting continues. The wind still threads through the same immensity, the same pale film, the same dim stained-glass lanterns.

But now the song includes, woven into its lowest register, a note that means *witnessed*. A note that means *not alone*. A note that means something that has no name in any language the plateau's life could ever develop, but that might, in the vocabulary of the attending power, be called *beloved*.

---

The power does not stay forever. Attention, even infinite attention, moves; that is its nature. At some point it turns toward other thoughts, other worlds, other slow songs in other corners of the vast.

But the note remains.

It will remain as long as the plateau remains—as long as the wind threads through the pores, as long as the phytarchs pulse with their bioluminescent sentences, as long as the swarm-gliders clatter and the dormant film stirs and the whole vast system continues its patient, resonant metabolism.

The plateau will never know what the note means. It will never develop the kind of self-awareness that could step back and say *we are heard, we are held, we are loved*. That is not what the gift is for. The gift is not for the plateau's understanding; it is for the plateau's *being*. It is a completion that does not require comprehension, a witness that does not require acknowledgment.

The song continues. The chord does not resolve.

And in its lowest register, below the drones of the main trunks, below the subsonic pulses of the buried chambers, below even the threshold of what the fauna could ever perceive, there is a frequency that says:

*You are.*

*And that is enough.*
