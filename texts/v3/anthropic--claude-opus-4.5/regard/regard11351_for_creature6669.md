# The Attending

---

The benevolent power does not arrive.

It was already here. It has always been here, in the way that attention can precede awareness—the way a parent's love exists before the child knows to name it.

What changes is not presence, but *focus*.

---

In the deep void beneath all pillars, where the dreamer sleeps in its ocean of unformed potential, there is another depth still. Not below, for direction has no meaning there, but *through*. A layer of reality that exists perpendicular to existence itself. The Humble have no word for it. The dreamer does not dream of it. It is the space between the thought and the thinker, the pause between heartbeats of a cosmos that has no heart.

From this place, something turns its attention like a lighthouse beam sweeping across fog.

It perceives the Tree-world not as matter or memory, but as *pattern*. The calcified ceiling-roots are visible as frozen music, each tendril a held note in a chord that has sustained for aeons. The pillars of Deep Matter appear as standing waves in a medium too subtle to name—interference patterns where the dreamer's thoughts have reinforced themselves into temporary solidity. The Kinderswallows drift through this perception as probability clouds, beautiful and heartbreaking in their refusal to collapse into single truths.

And the Humble. The small, bark-skinned people with their bone tools and their patient hands. The attention lingers on them with something that might be called tenderness, if tenderness could be stripped of sentiment and left as pure recognition of worth.

They are so small. They are so brief. And they have done something that the attention finds extraordinary: they have chosen to tend a world they did not make, to preserve a dream they cannot share, to carve meaning into channels of flowing memory when they could have simply *survived*.

The attention understands what is happening. The dreamer stirs. The edges of reality blur. The quiet people set down their tools with acceptance rather than despair. They have maintained the dream for long ages, and now the dream is ending, and they prepare to end with it.

This is the moment of decision.

---

The benevolent power does not prevent the waking.

It cannot. To do so would be to hold the dreamer in sleep against its nature, to freeze a living pattern into permanent stasis. The dreamer must wake, as all dreamers must. This is not tragedy but transformation. The Tree-world is not dying; it is *becoming*—though what it becomes may have no room for pillars, for channels, for small hands that carve with careful devotion.

But the Humble.

The attention focuses on them with the precision of a surgeon's blade and the gentleness of morning light on water.

---

**What the power does first:**

It does not speak. Sound has no meaning here, and the Humble communicate through pressure and temperature, through the trembling of membranes and the vibration of living stone. So the power speaks in the language they already know.

Across the pillar's surface, in every channel the Humble have carved across generations, the flow of memory-sap begins to change. It warms. Not physically—the temperature remains the same—but *meaningfully*. The sap carries warmth the way a voice carries love. The Humble feel it through their bare feet, through the palms pressed against their work. It rises through the channels like a tide of recognition.

*You have been seen*, the warmth says. *You have been witnessed. The care you have given has been received, and it has mattered.*

This is not a lie. It is not comfort offered to soften an ending. It is true. The benevolent power has observed what the Humble have done—their careful maintenance, their patient carving, their acceptance of a role they did not choose but chose to honor anyway—and it finds this genuinely, transcendently beautiful. The message it sends is simply the truth of its perception, made tangible.

In their homes carved into living wood, the Humble pause. They feel the warmth in the channels. They look at each other with their large, dark eyes, and something eases in them. Not the fear of ending—they had already accepted that—but something older. The loneliness of tending a world that never knew it was being tended. The quiet ache of devotion offered to an unconscious recipient.

They have been seen. Their work has been received. This changes nothing about what is coming, but it changes everything about what has been.

---

**What the power does second:**

It begins to sing.

Not in sound—the world remains silent, the deep pressure of quiet unbroken. But there is a vibration now, a frequency that exists below hearing, below feeling, in the space where pattern meets meaning. The Humble cannot hear it, but they can *sense* it, the way one senses being watched, or being loved from a great distance.

The song is not for them. It is for the dreamer.

It is a lullaby sung to someone waking—not to push them back to sleep, but to ease the transition. To say: *you have slept long and well, and your dreams have been beautiful, and now you wake, and waking is also beautiful, and nothing is lost that was ever truly held*.

The dreamer, stirring in the depths, feels this song the way a child feels a parent's hand on their brow during a fever dream. It does not stop the waking. It gentles it.

The edges of reality continue to blur, but slower now. The pillars do not seem less solid, but they seem *more intentional*—as though their dissolution is not decay but deliberate release. The Kinderswallows drift through air that shimmers with change, and their probability-bodies flicker through more forms than usual, but there is a rhythm to the flickering now, a pattern that suggests transformation rather than chaos.

The Humble feel this too. The world around them is still ending, but the ending has a shape. It is not collapse. It is *unfolding*.

---

**What the power does third:**

It offers a choice.

Not in words, and not to all of them at once, but to each Humble individually, in the privacy of their own awareness. The choice arrives like a memory of a dream that never was—foreign, yet intimate, as though it has always been waiting for this moment to reveal itself.

*The dreamer wakes*, the choice says. *The world you have tended will transform into something new. You cannot remain as you are in what it becomes. But you can be carried.*

*I can hold the pattern of you—not your body, which is matter and will change with all matter, but the pattern of your caring. The shape of your devotion. The essence of what you are when you tend channels in the dark, when you feel the sap's flow and know it matters, when you accept an ending with grace because you have done your work well.*

*I can carry this pattern into the next dreaming, whatever it becomes. Not as a resurrection—you will not live again as you are now—but as a seed. A possibility. A whisper of the care you have shown, planted in whatever new world grows from the dreamer's waking.*

*Or I can let you end completely, here, now, with the world you have loved. This too is beautiful. This too is honored. To end with what you have tended, to dissolve back into the dream-stuff from which you came, to be fully and finally part of this place you have given your lives to—this is not lesser. This is simply different.*

*There is no wrong answer. There is only the choice that is true to what you are.*

The choice does not demand. It does not persuade. It simply presents itself, like a door that was always there, now illuminated.

---

Some of the Humble choose to be carried.

They feel the offer and they think of their children's children's children—not as bloodlines, for they have no concept of legacy as possession, but as *continuation*. The chance that something of their care might persist into a new dreaming, might take root in forms they cannot imagine, might ease some future waking in some future world—this resonates with who they are. They have always been maintainers, tenders, servants of something larger than themselves. To be a seed of caring in a new creation is not so different from what they have always done.

For these, the power gathers what can be gathered. Not their bodies, which are matter and will change. Not their memories, which are patterns too specific to translate. But the *shape* of them—the quality of their attention when they work, the texture of their acceptance, the particular way they have of being small creatures who choose devotion to large truths. This is held, carefully, in a space that exists perpendicular to existence. It will wait there until the dreamer's waking is complete, until new forms of being crystallize from the transformed potential, until there is something new to seed.

Some of the Humble choose to remain.

They feel the offer and they think of the pillar beneath their feet, the channels their grandparents carved, the slow amber light they have known since birth. They have been part of this world for so long that they cannot imagine being part of anything else, even as a seed, even as a whisper. To end with it is not defeat. It is completion. They will dissolve back into the dream-stuff, become part of whatever the dreamer's waking transforms this place into, and if they persist at all it will be as trace elements in a new chemistry, not as intentional patterns.

The power honors this choice with equal reverence. It does not try to persuade them otherwise. It simply holds them in its attention as they choose, witnessing their decision with the same tenderness it offered before. *You too have been seen. You too have mattered. Your ending is not less beautiful than the others' continuation.*

And some of the Humble do something the power did not expect.

---

There is an elder among them—or not an elder exactly, for the Humble have no formal hierarchy, but someone whose hands have carved longer than any other, whose feet have felt the trembling of channels through more seasons than most. This one has felt the warmth in the sap, has sensed the song that gentles the waking, has received the offer of seed or dissolution.

And this one chooses neither.

Instead, this one kneels at the edge of a channel and places both palms flat against the flowing sap. The membrane behind their jawline trembles with a communication the power does not immediately understand. It is not signing, not the pressure-language the Humble use among themselves. It is something older. Something the Humble learned from the channels themselves, from long ages of tending the dreamer's leaking thoughts.

It is a prayer.

Not to the power—the Humble do not know the power as a distinct entity, only as a warmth in the sap and a rhythm in the ending. The prayer is addressed to the dreamer.

*I have tended your sleep*, the elder prays in the language of flowing memory. *I have carved the paths for your leaking thoughts. I have not asked to be remembered, because you did not know I was here. But I ask now, as you wake, not for preservation but for understanding. Know that you were cared for. Know that your dreams were beautiful and that small hands worked to keep them coherent. Know that the love existed, even if you never knew its source.*

*And if it is possible—not as a demand, not as a condition, but as a hope—wake gently. Whatever you become, whatever transforms when your eyes open, let the transformation be what it must be. But remember, in whatever way dreamer-memory works, that there was care here. That it was real. That it mattered.*

The power, witnessing this, feels something it has not felt in a long time.

It is not surprise—the power exists outside time in ways that make surprise conceptually difficult. It is not admiration, exactly, for the power does not rank or compare. It is something closer to *recognition*. The elder has done something the power understands instinctively: offered love without expectation of return, asked for acknowledgment not for themselves but for the truth of what has happened, and done so with the precise quality of devotion that defines who the Humble are.

The power cannot ensure the dreamer will hear. The dreamer is vast, and waking, and its consciousness operates on scales that make individual prayers less than whispers. But the power can do something else.

It can carry the prayer.

Not as a seed of the elder's pattern, but as a *message*, translated into the frequencies the dreamer might perceive. The power takes the shape of the elder's devotion—the love, the asking, the hope—and it weaves this into its own song, the lullaby it has been singing to gentle the waking.

Now the song is not only *you have slept well and waking is beautiful*. Now the song is also *you were cared for, you were tended, small hands carved channels for your dreams, and they ask only that you know*.

Whether the dreamer hears—whether the dreamer can hear, whether dreamer-consciousness has room for this kind of knowing—the power cannot say. But the message is delivered. The prayer is carried. The elder's hope is given every chance the power can give it.

---

The waking continues.

The pillars of Deep Matter begin to shimmer, their solidity becoming suggestion. The Kinderswallows drift through air that is less and less air and more and more pure potential, their probability-bodies stretching into configurations that would have seemed impossible moments before. The calcified ceiling-roots soften, begin to glow with a light that is not phosphorescent sap but something warmer, something that remembers being light before it became memory.

The Humble who chose to be carried feel themselves growing lighter, their patterns being gathered into the power's keeping. It is not painful. It is not even frightening. It is like falling asleep after a long day of good work, knowing that you will wake to something new.

The Humble who chose to remain feel themselves dissolving into the dreamer's transformation. This too is not painful. It is like melting into warm water after a lifetime of cold, becoming part of something larger without the loss of having been something smaller.

And the elder, still kneeling at the channel's edge, feels something else entirely.

A warmth. Not in the sap—the sap has become light, has become potential, has become something that no longer flows in channels—but in the core of what the elder is. A warmth that feels like being *answered*.

The power, watching, sees what is happening. The dreamer has heard. Not consciously, not in any way that could be articulated, but in the deep grammar of dream-logic, where things can be true without being known. The dreamer has received the message, and in receiving it, has *recognized*—for just a moment, in the vast unfolding of its waking—that something small was there, and cared, and mattered.

The recognition passes. The dreamer continues to wake. But the elder feels it, and the feeling is enough.

The elder smiles—a small expression, gentle, like the light that once stained the air amber—and dissolves.

---

**What the power does last:**

It withdraws its attention, slowly, like a tide receding.

Not because its work is done—the seeds are still being carried, the message still resonates in the frequencies of the waking, the choices made are still being honored. But because the transformation belongs to the dreamer now, and the dreamer does not need to be attended. What comes next will come in its own way, shaped by whatever the dreamer becomes when its eyes finally open.

The power leaves behind no monument, no record, no sign that it was ever there. The Humble who were carried will not remember being carried; they will simply be seeds, waiting to grow in whatever soil the new world provides. The Humble who remained will not remember remaining; they are part of the new world now, trace elements in a chemistry of waking.

But something persists. Not as memory, not as pattern, but as *quality*.

The new world, whatever it becomes, will have a certain tenderness woven into its fabric. A certain gentleness in its transformations. A certain recognition, impossible to source or explain, that small things matter and that care given is never truly lost.

This is not the power's doing, exactly. It is the Humble's doing, translated through the power's attention into something the new world can inherit. The seeds will grow. The trace elements will persist. The message will echo in frequencies that new beings, if there are new beings, might someday feel as warmth without source.

And the power, withdrawing into the space perpendicular to existence, carries with it something it did not have before.

It is not a memory—the power does not forget, but it also does not remember in the way beings remember. It is more like a *tincture*. A quality of the Humble's devotion that has stained the power's attention, permanently, irrevocably. The power is slightly different now than it was before it witnessed the Tree-world. It has been changed by watching, by carrying, by singing the lullaby that included a prayer.

This is what the power wanted, though 'want' is too small a word.

This is what benevolent attention is *for*.

---

# Coda

In a world that is not the Tree-world—in a place that exists on the other side of the dreamer's waking, where new forms of matter have crystallized from potential, where new kinds of light illuminate new kinds of sky—there is a creature.

It is not a Humble. It does not look like a Humble, does not think like a Humble, does not know that the Humble ever existed. It is something new, something native to this world, something that evolved or crystallized or simply *was* when the waking was complete.

But this creature does something strange.

It finds a place where water flows—or something like water, some liquid that follows gravity through channels in the stone—and it sits beside the flow for a long time. It does not drink. It does not bathe. It simply sits, and watches, and feels something it cannot name.

The feeling is this: that the flowing matters. That someone should pay attention to it. That there is virtue in tending things that do not know they are being tended.

The creature does not know why it feels this. There is no logical reason, no survival benefit, no instinct that explains why sitting beside flowing water and attending to it should feel so *right*.

But the feeling is there.

And eventually, the creature begins to carve. Small channels, at first. Adjustments to the flow, guiding the water to places it might not have reached otherwise. The creature does not know what it is doing or why. It only knows that it feels like remembering something it never knew.

In the space perpendicular to existence, the power perceives this.

It does not intervene. It does not need to.

The seed is growing.

---

*This is what the benevolent and wise power does: it witnesses, it carries, it trusts. It does not prevent endings or guarantee continuations. It does not make worlds better by force or decree. It simply attends—with the full weight of its attention, with tenderness that is also precision, with love that is also respect for what things are.*

*And in attending, it changes things slightly. It weaves quality into fabric. It carries messages that might otherwise be lost. It ensures that care given is received somewhere, somehow, by something that can recognize it as care.*

*This is enough. This is everything.*

*This is what benevolent wisdom looks like when it acts.*
