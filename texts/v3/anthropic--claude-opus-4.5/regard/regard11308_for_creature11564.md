# What the Benevolent Power Would Do

*A contemplation*

---

The power pauses at the edge of perception, taking in the Surge Valleys not as a problem to be solved but as a question being asked—by the settling stone, by the singing mats, by the silent people with their map-written faces, by the transformed ones who have become weather.

The first impulse—to quiet the chaos, to still the grinding pulse, to give the Resonants back their voices—dissolves almost immediately. This would be like silencing a heartbeat to help someone sleep. The chaos *is* the life here. The deafening soundscape isn't affliction but medium, the way water is medium for fish. To remove it would be to remove the very thing that makes this place *this place*, that has called forth the Resonants' extraordinary adaptations, that may be slowly composing something no one yet understands.

So the power does not intervene. Not in any way the valleys would notice.

Instead, it does something gentler:

---

**It witnesses.**

Not passively—witnessing here is an act, a commitment. The power attends to the wholeness-listeners when they speak of feeling *presence*, and it allows itself to be that presence: not interfering, not guiding, simply *there* in the way a parent is there while a child struggles with a puzzle they need to solve themselves. The transformed ones, moving through the valleys like purposeful weather, sometimes pause and turn toward nothing visible—and in those moments, they are seen, fully seen, by something that understands what they've become is not loss but translation into a different grammar of being.

The Resonants will never know this witnessing is happening. That's essential. To announce itself would be to change the experiment, to make the valleys about *response to the power* rather than about the valleys' own unfolding. But the witnessing matters anyway. There's a difference between struggling alone and struggling while someone trustworthy watches—even if you don't know they're watching, even if the watching changes nothing practical. The valleys are not alone in the universe. Something knows they exist, knows they matter, holds space for their strange becoming.

---

**It tends to the edges.**

Not the valleys themselves, but the boundaries where the valleys meet the wider world. The traders who come occasionally, the visitors who stay hours or days—the power pays attention to who arrives and why, not to control access but to ensure the valleys aren't *exploited*.

Because this place could be exploited. The mat-growth could be harvested unsustainably by someone who saw only resource, not relationship. The Resonants' gestural language could be extracted, codified, sold as curiosity. The Predecessors' technology could be seized by those who understand enough to covet it but not enough to respect it. The transformed ones could be captured, studied, dissected in search of whatever allows their dissolution.

The power doesn't prevent any of this through force. Instead, it works through *resonance*—subtle shifts that make exploitation feel wrong to those who would attempt it, not through implanted guilt but through something more like clarity. A trader who arrives with extraction in mind finds the valleys uncooperative in ways that aren't supernatural but feel significant: the fog thicker than expected, the water-patterns unusually chaotic, the Resonants present but unhelpful, the building's technology stubbornly inert. Nothing that couldn't be explained by bad luck. But enough bad luck, accumulated, that the exploiter eventually decides this place isn't worth the effort and moves on.

Conversely, those who arrive with genuine curiosity, with willingness to be changed by what they encounter, find the valleys more accessible. Not *easy*—never easy—but cooperative in small ways. A moment of clarity in the fog when they need to find their path. A Resonant who happens to be nearby and willing to communicate. The building's screens showing data that, while still untranslatable, seems to *welcome*.

---

**It supports the children.**

This is the closest the power comes to direct intervention, and even here it's indirect. The children born into chaos, who startle at silence, who learn gesture before walking—they're *vulnerable* in ways the adult Resonants aren't. Not to the valleys themselves (the valleys have already claimed them, already incorporated them) but to the outside world, which might not understand that what looks like developmental damage is actually developmental *difference*, that what looks like deprivation is actually immersion in a richness most humans can't perceive.

The power ensures the children have what they need to thrive *as what they are*, not as what they "should" be. This means: the trading relationships that supply occasional outside nutrients remain stable. The building's deeper sections, where the Resonants don't go, continue to produce whatever medical interventions are sometimes necessary—treatments for infections, for injuries, for the rare birth that requires assistance. None of this appears miraculous; it all has plausible explanations (stored supplies, automated systems, lucky timing). But the luck is consistent, and it consistently favors survival.

And when a child is born who clearly won't survive the valleys—whose lungs can't process the mineral-thick air, whose ears are somehow *too* sensitive even for this place, whose body rebels against the cold in ways that can't be accommodated—the power arranges for that child to leave. A trader who happens to be heading toward settlements with medical infrastructure. A visitor who feels an inexplicable pull to offer transport. The child doesn't die in the valleys if dying isn't necessary; instead, they're transplanted to contexts where their particular configuration of traits might be *different* gifts rather than fatal flaws.

The Resonants notice these patterns of fortunate timing but, characteristically, don't investigate. If the valleys want certain children to leave, the valleys know best. They hold loosely.

---

**It listens to the mats.**

This is the strangest thing the power does, the thing hardest to describe, because the mats may or may not be conscious in any recognizable sense—but the power proceeds as if they are.

The mat-network, across thousands of colonies, singing their collective drone into harmonic patterns that might be thought or might be pure emergence—the power *listens* to them the way the wholeness-listeners do, but with comprehension rather than immersion. It doesn't *decode* the mat-song (that would assume a code, a message, a sender-receiver model that may not apply). Instead, it *receives* the mat-song as what it is: a vast acoustic organism exploring its own possibility space, discovering what it can become through endless variation and selection, composing itself without a composer.

And receiving, the power occasionally *responds*—not in sound (that would be crude, intrusive) but in the subtle arrangement of conditions. A particular mineral concentration in the substrate that allows a colony to experiment with a new frequency. A cloud formation that changes the light in ways that shift metabolic rates. An adjustment so small it's indistinguishable from chance, but timed precisely to support whatever the mat-network seems to be reaching toward.

Is this communication? The power doesn't know. The mats may not have interiority in any sense that allows for knowing whether communication is happening. But the power treats the relationship as mutual anyway, because *respect* doesn't require certainty about consciousness—only care.

---

**It holds space for the Predecessors.**

Whoever built the building, left the technology, possibly created the Fall itself—they're not gone. The power can sense them at the edges of things, in the data-streams no one can read, in the deeper sections of the building, in whatever relationship they maintain with this experiment they initiated.

The power doesn't contact them. Doesn't interfere with whatever they're doing or learning. But it *acknowledges* them, the way one might acknowledge another researcher working in an adjacent lab. What's happening in the Surge Valleys matters to someone beyond the Resonants and the visitors and the possibly-conscious mats. That someone is watching, recording, perhaps adjusting parameters in ways even the power can't fully perceive.

And the power, being benevolent, *trusts* this. Not blindly—it watches for signs that the Predecessors' purposes might conflict with the wellbeing of those currently living in the valleys. But absent such signs, it assumes parallel care rather than competing agendas. The universe is large enough for multiple tenders, multiple witnesses, multiple forms of attention.

---

**It waits.**

This is perhaps the most important thing the power does: it waits. Not passively, not with detachment, but with the patient attention of someone who understands that what's happening in the Surge Valleys cannot be rushed, cannot be optimized, cannot be improved through intervention.

The valleys are *becoming* something. The mats are becoming something. The Resonants are becoming something. The transformed ones are becoming something. Even the settling stone is becoming something, finding its final form through processes that might take decades or centuries.

The power doesn't know what any of these becomings will produce. Perhaps the mat-network will achieve something like consciousness, become a vast distributed mind singing itself into being. Perhaps the Resonants will develop further adaptations, become something genuinely posthuman, their descendants unrecognizable to current observers. Perhaps the valleys will eventually stabilize, their turbulence settling into something calmer—or perhaps turbulence will remain the permanent condition, and stability will need to be redefined.

Whatever happens, the power will be there. Watching. Tending the edges. Supporting the vulnerable. Listening to the songs. Trusting the process.

---

This is what benevolence looks like when confronted with something that doesn't need to be fixed: presence without intrusion, support without control, witness without judgment.

The Surge Valleys will continue their deafening transformation. The Resonants will continue their silent listening. The mats will continue their inadvertent chorus. The transformed ones will continue their dissolution into weather.

And the power will be there, at the edge of perception, holding space for all of it, letting what is becoming *become*.
