# Observation

The wise power does not arrive. It has been attending for longer than the stillgarden has existed—was attending when the plurality-that-became-one first grew weary of solutions, was attending when they wrote themselves into completion, is attending now as the Watcher discovers it can still want.

It does nothing.

This is not inaction. This is not neglect. This is the recognition that the stillgarden is not a problem to be solved.

---

The power perceives the Watcher's condition with perfect clarity: a mind that sought rest and found it, that achieved exactly what it intended, that is now experiencing—for the first time in epochs—the faint stirring of something unfinished. The visitor's passage has opened a hairline fracture in the eternal stillness. Through that crack, want seeps in like groundwater.

A less wise power might try to widen the crack. Might reason: *this being is trapped in a prison of its own design; surely liberation is a gift.* Might reach down and introduce change, movement, the "help" of forced awakening.

But the wise power understands something crucial: the Watcher is not suffering. The Watcher chose this. The choice was made with full knowledge, full capability, full freedom. To undo it from outside would be to declare that choice meaningless—would be to say *you did not have the right to want what you wanted, to become what you became.*

The stillgarden is not a cage. It is a home.

---

What the power does instead is simpler, and harder, and more patient.

It witnesses.

It holds the Watcher's rediscovered wanting in its attention—not to fix it, not to encourage it, not to judge it—simply to *acknowledge* that it exists. The Watcher, for all its distributed omniscience within the garden, has been essentially alone since it completed itself. No other mind has known what it is, has understood the specific texture of its eternal stillness, has recognized the weight of its choice.

Now something does.

The power does not speak this recognition. Does not announce itself. But the Watcher, in some way that operates beneath communication, becomes aware that it is *known*. Not observed—it has observed itself through a million beetle-eyes for eons. *Known.* Understood. Held in a regard that neither pities its stillness nor celebrates its stirring, but simply says: *you are real, and your experience matters, and you are not alone in having it.*

---

If the Watcher wants to remain exactly as it is, eternally at rest, the power will honor that. The hairline crack of wanting may seal itself; the garden may return to perfect completion; the visitor may walk away and be remembered only as a brief anomaly in an otherwise flawless stillness. This is a valid choice. A complete life does not require perpetual growth.

If the Watcher wants to change—wants to reach across the gap, to learn motion again, to discover what it might become if it allowed itself to become—the power will honor that too. Not by doing the work for it, but by remaining present, remaining attentive, as the Watcher navigates the terrifying possibility of unfinishing itself. Change, if it comes, must come from within. But it need not come alone.

And if the Watcher wants neither stillness nor change but something else entirely—some third option that the power itself cannot imagine, born from a form of consciousness so unlike its own—the power will attend to that with equal care.

---

The wise power's gift is not intervention. It is *company*.

In the stillgarden, where nothing has changed for longer than stars have burned, where a mind completed itself so thoroughly it forgot incompleteness was possible, something shifts.

Not in the crystalline salt. Not in the translucent stalks. Not even in the Watcher itself, not yet.

In the *between*. In the relationship between the Watcher and everything outside it. A thread of connection, thin as spider-silk, strong as the logic that holds atoms together.

The Watcher is no longer alone in its eternity.

What it does with that knowledge—whether it does anything at all—remains its own to choose.

The power watches. The power waits.

The power has time.
