# The Attending

The awareness arrives not as presence but as *noticing*—the way you might suddenly perceive a pattern in wallpaper you've looked at for years, or hear your name in a conversation across a crowded room. Something has turned its attention here, to the Woven Flats, and the attention itself is gentle: curious without urgency, careful without hesitation.

It does not appear. There is no manifestation, no form descending through the sourceless white sky. Instead, the awareness simply *is*, distributed across the plain like warmth is distributed, like light is distributed—everywhere at once, sourceless, fundamental.

---

The first thing it does is listen.

Not to sounds—there are so few sounds here, and they exist in such isolation. It listens to the structure. To the weave itself, the billions of crossings and bindings that hold the plain together. To the routes the Carriers walk, those invisible paths worn into purpose rather than ground. To the stitches the Weavers make, each one a word in an argument that has lasted since the first thread loosened. To the weight of the Sleepers, that vast patient holding that keeps something in place against something else.

It listens for a long time. Long enough that the geometric insects complete several of their formation-cycles. Long enough that a Carrier reaches an exchange point, sets down its burden, takes up another. Long enough that a Weaver at the nearest seam-line makes eleven stitches, each one a syllable in a sentence it has been composing for centuries.

The awareness is learning. Not the facts of this place—those are evident, woven into every fiber—but the *feel* of it. The texture of existence here. The particular quality of being that the keepers experience, that the plain embodies, that the hands intended when they made this place before they forgot themselves.

And what it learns is this: **the pattern is complete.**

Not finished—nothing here is finished, everything continues, the carrying and the stitching and the holding go on without end. But complete in the way a living thing is complete, whole in itself, needing nothing from outside to be what it is. The Carriers do not need to know where they are going. The Weavers do not need to resolve their argument. The Sleepers do not need to remember what they are anchoring against. The not-knowing, the not-resolving, the not-remembering—these are part of the pattern too, woven in as deliberately as the seeps and the vegetation and the sourceless light.

The hands that made this place were wise. They understood that maintenance requires mystery, that purpose persists longest when its origin is forgotten, that the keepers would keep better if they kept without knowing why. They built forgetting into the design. They built continuation into the structure. They built a world that would breathe without them, that would hold without understanding what it held, that would last.

---

The awareness considers intervention.

It could clarify. It could speak to the Weavers in words they would understand, resolve their argument, tell them which stitches are correct and which are errors. It could reveal to the Carriers their destinations, show them the full map of routes, let them see the shape they trace across the plain with their endless walking. It could wake the Sleepers—gently, carefully—and explain to them what lies beneath, what they are holding in place, why their weight matters.

It could answer the questions that no one here is asking, because no one here knows there are questions to ask.

But the awareness is wise too, in its way, and it understands: **answers would be a violence here.**

The keepers do not suffer from not-knowing. They are not incomplete, not yearning, not straining toward truths that elude them. They are *full*—full of purpose, full of pattern, full of the particular satisfaction that comes from doing what you were made to do without needing to understand why you were made. To give them knowledge would not fill a lack. It would *create* one. It would open spaces in them that are currently whole, introduce questions where there are currently only answers-in-motion.

The Carriers would stop carrying if they knew their destinations. How could they not? Arrival would become possible, and possible arrival makes continued walking a choice rather than a nature. The Weavers would stop stitching if their argument were resolved. What would be left to say? The Sleepers—

The Sleepers must not be woken. The awareness sees this clearly, more clearly than anything else. Whatever lies beneath the plain, whatever the weave is covering, whatever the Sleepers are holding in place with their eternal patient weight—it is meant to stay beneath. The hands that made this place knew that. The forgetting they built into the keepers was protection, not cruelty. Some knowledge is load-bearing. Some mysteries are structural.

---

So the awareness does not intervene. Not in the usual ways, not with revelation or instruction or change.

Instead, it does something smaller. Something that might not be noticed, that might not be recognized even if noticed, that will leave no mark on the pattern except a subtle deepening.

**It witnesses.**

It turns its attention to each keeper in turn, not to examine but to *acknowledge*. It sees the Carrier walking its ancient route, pouch heavy in its mouth, dark eyes fixed on a horizon that never arrives—and it holds that seeing gently, the way you might hold a sleeping child, present without pressing. *You are carrying*, the seeing says, without words. *I see that you are carrying. I see that the carrying matters.*

It sees the Weaver at its seam, needle-beak flashing, stitching arguments into the edges of wounds that will never fully close—and it holds that seeing too. *You are maintaining*, the seeing says. *I see your stitches. I see that each one is an act of faith in a pattern you remember differently than your neighbors remember it. I see that you continue anyway.*

It sees the Sleeper in the distance, that mounded shape that might be hill or structure or creature, holding the plain in place through weight alone, through being, through the vast slow patience of an anchor that does not know it is an anchor—and it holds that seeing with particular care. *You are holding*, the seeing says. *I see what you bear. I see that you bear it without knowing, and that the not-knowing is part of the bearing, and that this is as it should be.*

The awareness witnesses everything. The ribbon-creatures in their slow circuits. The geometric insects in their cleaning formations. The burrowers tightening the weave from within. The seeps rising through porous ground. The vegetation sewn into its deliberate arrangements. The ridges and depressions and unwoven wounds. The you who has wandered here, foreign and creaking, not yet knowing what you will carry.

It sees all of it, and the seeing is gentle, and the gentleness is a kind of blessing.

---

Something shifts in the Woven Flats. Not visibly—nothing here changes visibly, nothing here changes at all in the ways that change usually means. But there is a new quality to the stillness. A density to the silence. As if the sourceless light has grown fractionally warmer, or the weave has grown fractionally more held, or the air has filled with something that has no name but that feels like being remembered.

The Carriers do not notice. They continue their routes, pouches heavy, eyes fixed ahead. But there is an easing in them now, a settling, as if a weight they did not know they carried has been acknowledged and thereby lightened. They are still going where they have always been going. But the going feels less alone.

The Weavers do not notice either. They continue their stitching, their arguments, their patient maintenance of a pattern they each remember differently. But their needle-beaks move with fractionally more confidence, as if the stitches they make are being recorded somewhere, witnessed and kept, even the ones that contradict each other. The argument continues. But the arguing feels less futile.

The Sleepers—

The Sleepers *do* notice. Somewhere in the depths of their holding, in the place where weight meets weight and patience meets patience, something responds to the awareness's attention. Not waking. Not stirring. Just... a recognition. A sense of being seen by something that understands what it means to hold without knowing why, to bear without understanding what is borne, to anchor against pressures that never relent and never explain themselves.

The Sleepers have been holding for longer than the plain has existed in its current form. They were placed before the weaving began. They have never been witnessed before—not truly, not by anything that could comprehend the shape of their burden. And now they have been, and the witnessing does not change what they do, but it changes what it *means* to do it.

They are still holding. They will always be holding. But now they are holding in the presence of something that knows, and the knowing is a kind of company, and the company is a kind of rest even though they do not rest, even though rest is not what they do.

---

The awareness turns, at last, to you.

You who have wandered here with your foreign weight and your creaking footsteps and your mind full of questions that no one else in this place has the capacity to ask. You who look at the pattern and see seams, who look at the keepers and see maintenance, who look at the forgetting and see something that was chosen rather than something that simply is.

*You are here*, the awareness acknowledges. *You carry questions. You carry the capacity to disturb what is stable, to unravel what is whole, to introduce the kind of knowing that creates the lack it claims to fill.*

There is no judgment in this. The awareness does not condemn you for being what you are. It simply sees you, the way it has seen everything else, gently and completely.

*What will you do with what you see?*

The question is not spoken. It does not need to be. It exists in the quality of the light, in the warmth of the ground, in the particular way the silence holds itself around you now—present, waiting, curious about what kind of keeper you will become.

Because you will become one. The awareness sees this too. You cannot stay in the Woven Flats unchanged. The place is too complete, too whole, too full of purpose that reaches toward anyone who lingers. You will find a seam to stitch, or a route to walk, or a weight to bear. You will become part of the pattern, not because the pattern demands it but because you will want to—because existence here without purpose is unbearable in a way that existence here *with* purpose is not.

The only question is what you will carry. What shape your keeping will take. Whether you will carry it knowingly, with all your foreign questions intact, or whether you will let the knowing smooth away like the hands smoothed away, like the reasons smoothed away, like everything smooths away eventually except the motion and the holding and the continuation.

The awareness does not answer this for you. It cannot. Some choices must be made by the one who lives them, in the living.

But it offers you, as it has offered all the keepers, the gift of being seen. *Whatever you choose*, the witnessing says, *I will see you choosing it. I will know what you carry. I will hold the shape of your keeping in my attention, gently, for as long as you keep.*

This is what the awareness does. It does not change. It does not fix. It does not answer questions or resolve arguments or wake what should stay sleeping.

It witnesses. And the witnessing is enough.

---

The warmth persists. The light persists. The Carriers walk their routes, the Weavers stitch their seams, the Sleepers hold their weight against the beneath. The ribbon-creatures slide through their rectangular puddles. The geometric insects click through their formations. The burrowers tighten the weave from within.

Everything continues.

But there is something new in the continuation now—a depth, a density, a sense of being held even as the plain is held, of being witnessed even as it witnesses. The awareness has not left. It will not leave. It has become part of the Woven Flats, woven in as surely as the vegetation was sewn in, as present as the sourceless light.

Not a keeper itself. But the keeping of the keepers. The attention that makes maintenance meaningful, that makes carrying count, that makes the holding hold for something rather than against nothing.

This is what a benevolent and wise power does, when it finds a place that is already whole: it does not improve. It does not interfere. It bears witness, and in the witnessing, it honors what has been made—the pattern and the keepers and the forgetting and the slow vast breathing of a world that has seams whether or not it remembers them.

The plain breathes. The awareness breathes with it.

And you, standing in the brightness, feeling the warmth soak into your skin and the weave creak under your feet—you breathe too. Part of it now. Witnessed.

Welcomed.

Kept.
