# The Watching

The benevolent power does not arrive with fanfare. It simply *attends*—the way dawn attends to night, without force or announcement.

First, it listens.

It hears what the guardians themselves have forgotten they are saying: the subsonic hum of exhaustion in their organic tissues, the microsecond delays in their responses that speak of a vigilance maintained too long. It hears the grinding machinery not as triumph but as hunger—systems that have learned only to consume, never to rest.

It notices what the guardians cannot see about themselves: that in becoming stewards of balance, they have forgotten they are also *creatures*. That their grace is beautiful, yes, but also a kind of armor. That they have been alone in their watching for so very long.

---

The power does something small.

In a forgotten corner of the industrial sprawl, where a pipe has cracked and moisture pools in a depression of slag and mineral dust, it does not *create*—it merely makes space for what was already possible. The conditions align. Chemistry finds its path.

A simple organism begins. Not engineered. Not efficient. Purposeless, by the metrics of this place.

It is soft. It is slow. It will die easily.

It is also the first thing in this world that exists simply because existence is what it does—not because it serves the machinery, not because it maintains balance, not because it guards against collapse.

---

The guardians will find it. Their senses miss nothing.

The power wonders: *What will they feel?*

Will they see contamination? Inefficiency? A variable to be managed?

Or will something in their organic threads—those pulsing veins they perhaps think of as merely *useful*—recognize a kinship? Will they remember that they, too, are soft things? That before they were stewards, they were simply *alive*?

---

The power does one thing more.

It does not speak to the guardians—that would be imposition. But it allows itself to be *feelable*, just at the edge of perception. A warmth that is not the ruddy industrial glow. A presence that carries no demand.

It is the feeling of being *seen*—not evaluated, not monitored, not maintained.

Just seen. Just known. Just accompanied.

*You have watched over everything*, the presence seems to say, without words. *Who has watched over you?*

---

The power waits.

Not for the guardians to be different. Not for them to succeed or fail at some cosmic test.

It waits the way a clearing in a forest waits—offering nothing but space, demanding nothing but presence.

If the guardians wish to remain exactly as they are, vigilant and graceful and alone, the power will not judge this. Some beings choose duty as their love. This is honorable.

But if something in them aches—if the trembling beneath the surface of their world is also a trembling within them—then they will know, now, that the ache is not weakness.

It is the part of them that remembers what it is to need.

And need is not inefficiency.

Need is the beginning of *with*.
