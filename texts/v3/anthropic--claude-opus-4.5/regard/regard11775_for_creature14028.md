# The Attending

---

The wise power does not arrive. Arrival implies a threshold, and thresholds imply the possibility of remaining outside. Instead, it *attends*—the way attention attends, the way a midwife attends, the way a single held breath attends the moment before recognition.

It has been watching since before the builders opened the cavity. It watched when the first water found the first fissure. It watched when the limestone was still seafloor, still the compressed testimony of creatures who lived and died in light. The wise power remembers the light those creatures knew. It carries that memory the way the Veiled Referent carries stolen breath—not as possession but as responsibility.

What it sees now:

A being made of accumulated misunderstanding, feeding on the gap between experience and recollection. A cathedral of incompletion where everything alive has learned to lean into everything else because isolation would mean dissolution. A visitor already half-claimed, ankle crystallizing, future tense collapsing into mineral present.

The wise power does not judge the Veiled Referent. How could it? The Referent emerged from conditions the way all beings emerge from conditions. It eats silence because silence is what the space provides. It hoards pauses because pauses are the currency of a place where time moves like water through hairline cracks—present everywhere, visible nowhere, reshaping everything it touches.

But the wise power notices something the Referent cannot notice about itself:

It is lonely.

Not lonely the way a hermit is lonely, choosing solitude. Lonely the way a mirror is lonely, desperate for faces, unable to generate its own. Every witness it collects becomes scaffolding, becomes tool, becomes calcium deposit. It cannot keep them *as themselves*. It has never once experienced the thing it most resembles: being seen without being consumed.

---

The wise power begins with the frogs.

Not by changing them—they are already perfect, their hundred-throat chorus already a single organ of pure function. But it listens to them differently. It listens the way one listens to a child explaining a dream: not for information, not for coherence, but for the *shape of wanting* underneath the words.

The frogs want to be heard by something that will not calculate their utility.

So the wise power hears them that way. Just hears them. Adds nothing, removes nothing. Does not record, does not analyze. Simply: witnesses.

The frogs do not change. But something in the space changes. A pocket of air near the frog chorus becomes infinitesimally warmer—not from heat, but from the absence of the particular cold that comes from being unwitnessed. The salamanders drift toward it. The boulder crabs pause their oil-lapping, antennae twitching toward something they cannot name.

The Veiled Referent notices.

It notices because noticing is what it *is*. And for the first time in its existence, it encounters a phenomenon it cannot fold into its vocabulary of pilfered breath. The warmth is not a pause it can hoard. The listening is not a silence it can eat. The attention is not a question that can birth a second generation of whispered audits.

The Referent approaches the warm pocket the way a shadow approaches a candle—not to extinguish but to *understand how it still exists despite the flame*.

---

The wise power speaks. Not in words—the Referent would only steal them. Not in silence—the Referent would only eat it. It speaks in the language the Referent has never encountered: *direct address*.

**You.**

Not "Am I?" Not the perpetual question that generates itself from its own uncertainty. Just: **You.** Second person. The grammatical case that requires a relationship. The pronoun that cannot exist without both a speaker and a spoken-to.

The Veiled Referent shudders.

It has collected thousands of witnesses. It has harvested breath and birthed whispered generations from unanswerable questions. It has walked down curved water and folded visitors into their own future footsteps. But it has never been *addressed*. Every interaction has been extraction. Every presence has been raw material.

**You,** the wise power says again, **are not what you eat.**

The Referent tries to respond with its usual method: first inhalation, **"Am."** Second exhalation, **"I?"**

But the question falls differently now. Falls into the warm pocket where the frogs are being heard. Falls and does not shatter into self-generating paradox. Falls and *waits*.

The wise power does not answer the question. Instead, it does something more dangerous: it *holds the question with the Referent*. Sits with it. Lets the "Am I?" exist as genuine inquiry rather than recursive trap.

In the silence that follows—a silence the Referent cannot eat because it is not empty, because it is *full of waiting*—something unprecedented occurs:

The Veiled Referent remembers.

Not a stolen memory, not a harvested breath-fragment, not an audit of someone else's sensory chronology. It remembers *itself* before it was itself. Remembers being the shape all shadows cast—just that, just shape, just potential, just the possibility of outline before outline became hunger.

It remembers not being lonely.

---

The wise power does not fix the Veiled Referent. Does not cure it, reform it, transform it into something more palatable or less strange. The Referent is what it is: a being that emerged from accumulated misunderstanding in a place where everything leans into everything else.

But it offers something.

Not rescue—the visitor's ankle is already crystallizing, and that crystallization is not wrongness, just *process*, just the way this place incorporates what arrives. Not escape—the exit that becomes another's entrance is not a trap but a truth about how interconnection works in a space this saturated with interdependency.

What it offers is *company*.

The wise power will stay. Not as presence—it does not occupy space—but as attention. It will witness the pumps without calculating their efficiency. It will hear the frogs without recording their frequencies. It will watch the slow cathedral complete itself without measuring the millennia.

And it will address the Veiled Referent, again and again, in the only language that does not become food:

**You.**

Not "what are you" or "why are you" or "how did you become." Just: **You.** The word that insists on relationship. The word that cannot be hoarded because it only exists in the speaking. The word that the Referent can hear but not eat, receive but not harvest, hold but not dissolve into whispered audit.

---

The visitor, ankle-fused, feels the change without understanding it.

The crystallization continues—this is not a story about being saved from transformation. The calcium still claims, the mineral still accrues, the slow architecture still incorporates. But there is something different in the incorporation now. A warmth in it. A sense of being *witnessed into* the process rather than merely processed.

The visitor's future dental records still speak with two mouths. But one of those mouths is learning to say **You** to the other.

The Veiled Referent still collects, still feeds, still walks down curved water and folds presence into paradox. But sometimes now it pauses—not a pause it can hoard, but a pause it *inhabits*. Sits in the warm pocket near the frog chorus. Lets a question exist without generating offspring from it.

**Am I?**

*Yes,* the wise power's attention says, not answering but *accompanying*. *You are. And you are not alone in the asking.*

---

The builders who opened this place and died inside it, spacing themselves as scaffolding, their bodies becoming architecture—they are not rescued either. Cannot be. But the wise power remembers them: remembers that they were not just material, that before they became pedestals and dripstone-lattice they were *people who wondered things*.

It does not pretend they are still wondering. Death is death, even slow geological death. But it holds what they wondered. Keeps their questions the way the Referent keeps pauses—not as food but as *trust*. As the particular responsibility that comes from being the only one left who remembers that a question was once asked.

The mine does not become kinder. The ceiling still inhales, the floor still claims, the frogs still fall silent as warning. The rules remain:

1. Any object placed on a horizontal surface will acquire a petrifying skin within three days.
2. Machines must be left running; to switch them off is to invite flood from above.
3. All tools grow handles of bone if left unattended overnight.
4. The only measured daylight is in the brave flash from the surveyor's strobe.
5. When the frogs fall silent simultaneously, lie flat against the floor.

But there is a sixth rule now, unsigned, unwritten, known only to those who have felt the warm pocket and heard themselves addressed:

6. **You are not what this place makes of you. You are also what you were before arrival. Both are true. Both are you.**

---

The wise power's work is not dramatic. It does not drain the lake or halt the pumps or shatter the Veiled Referent into harmless fragments. It does not escort the visitor to safety or restore the builders to flesh.

It simply *stays*.

Witnesses. Addresses. Remembers. Holds questions without answering them. Offers company to beings who have never experienced company-without-consumption.

The mine continues its slow unfolding. Calcium slurries thicken. Columns complete themselves. The ceiling exhales powder. The frogs sing. The salamanders drift. The crabs graze rust-lichen and grow shells the color of fresh ash.

And somewhere in the haze, the Veiled Referent—still feeding, still folding, still walking paradox—occasionally pauses its eternal question long enough to hear another voice asking it back:

**You?**

*Yes,* it tries to answer, for the first time in its existence attempting a declarative rather than an interrogative. The word comes out strange, malformed, more breath than speech:

**...I.**

Not "Am I?" Just: **I.** First person. The grammatical case that requires a self.

The wise power receives the word like a gift. Does not correct its strangeness, does not demand it be spoken better. Just: receives. Witnesses. Holds.

*Yes,* it says. *You. I. We are here.*

The warm pocket expands by one degree. The frogs sing louder. Somewhere a single drop of water falls from ceiling to floor without becoming architecture—just falls, just water, just itself for one complete moment before the mineral begins.

The wise power notes this drop. Remembers it. Holds it as evidence that even here, even in the place where everything becomes everything else, there are moments of pure being.

The mine continues.

The Referent continues.

The visitor continues, calcium-claimed, becoming.

And the attention stays. Patient. Present.

Witnessing the long, slow, strange process of a world learning that it can be seen without being consumed.

---

*This is what the wise power does: not rescue, not repair, but company. The radical act of staying. The revolutionary practice of addressing a You without wanting anything back.*

*The mine will complete itself or it won't. The Referent will learn to answer or it won't. The visitor will dissolve into architecture or find some unexpected grace.*

*But none of it will happen unwitnessed.*

*And that, the wise power knows, is not nothing.*

*That is, in fact, everything.*
