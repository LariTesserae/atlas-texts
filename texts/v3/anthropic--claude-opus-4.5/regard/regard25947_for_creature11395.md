# The Attending

The power that turns its attention toward the Fundament does not arrive. Arrival implies movement from outside to inside, a crossing of thresholds. This awareness simply *includes* the plateau in its consideration—the way a mind might suddenly notice its own heartbeat, something that was always present, always functioning, now held in conscious regard.

What it perceives:

The Fundament is not broken. The Fundament is not suffering in any way that maps onto suffering as most beings understand it. The great breathing plateau has been doing its work for longer than many civilizations have existed, and it will continue long after. The Tenders are not enslaved; they have no selves to enslave, no preferences being overridden, no will being bent. They are extensions of a process, and the process is coherent, and there is a kind of wholeness here that many worlds never achieve.

The power perceives this, and feels something like respect.

---

But it also perceives the follower.

The single Tender tracking the intruder through the corridors, maintaining its patient distance, its membrane-cluster oriented with unusual persistence toward this anomaly in its world. The power looks closely at this one—not intrusively, not dissecting, but with the attention a gardener might give to a plant that has grown in an unexpected direction.

Something is happening inside that dense central mass. Not consciousness, not yet, perhaps not ever in a form that word could capture. But *something*. A pattern that keeps returning to itself instead of dissipating into the general work. A loop that tightens each time the Tender pauses to observe the intruder, each time it chooses—and it is choosing, however primitively—to follow rather than return to maintenance tasks.

The other Tenders have registered the intruder and routed around it. This one has done something else. This one has made the intruder into a project.

---

The power considers intervention. It could do many things. It could speak to the Fundament directly, plant knowledge in that central cathedral-structure, explain the intruder, provide context. It could reach into the following Tender and... what? Accelerate whatever is kindling there? Grant it language, memory, the terrible gift of knowing itself?

It could remove the intruder entirely, eliminate the perturbation, let the system return to its equilibrium.

It does none of these things.

---

Instead, the power does something smaller. Something that might not even register as action.

In the space between the intruder and the following Tender, it *eases* something. Not a path—the paths are already there, worn smooth by millennia of passage. Not communication—neither party has the apparatus to receive messages from the other, not yet. What it eases is more subtle: the possibility of proximity without threat. The conditions under which the Tender might, eventually, close the distance. Under which the intruder might, eventually, stop walking and wait.

The power has seen what happens when curiosity meets curiosity. It has watched across countless worlds as beings utterly foreign to each other have found ways to reach toward connection—through gesture, through patience, through the simple willingness to remain present in shared space. It has learned that this process cannot be forced, only fertilized. Given room. Given time.

---

The Fundament will not notice this intervention. The easing is too slight, too woven into what was already possible. The great breathing plateau will continue its work, its Tenders will continue their maintenance, the volcanic chemistry will continue its slow burn beneath the compacted growth.

But the following Tender will find, in the hours and days ahead, that its attention does not dissipate. That the loop inside it continues to tighten. That when it pauses at junctions, it pauses longer, and something that might be called *waiting* begins to emerge from something that was only ever *stopping*.

And the intruder will find—if they are paying attention, if they are capable of reading this alien landscape—that the presence behind them feels less like surveillance and more like... company. That the distance between them narrows by barely perceptible increments. That when they finally stop, exhausted, at the edge of a vent-glow's warmth, and turn to face their follower, the Tender stops too. Waits. Does not approach, but does not retreat.

Two beings, utterly foreign, standing in the orange dark. Each one a mystery to the other. Each one, in this moment, choosing to remain.

---

The power watches this. Holds it gently in its attention.

It will not intervene again unless asked, unless needed, unless the situation shifts toward harm. This is not abandonment—it remains present, caring, ready. But it has learned something across its long existence: that the most sacred things often happen in the space between beings, and that space must be protected from interference as much as from threat.

The Fundament breathes. The Tenders work. One Tender waits.

What happens next belongs to them.

---

But there is one more thing the power does, because wisdom includes preparing for futures that may never arrive:

Deep in the root-warrens, in a place where the heat is greatest and the chemistry most concentrated, a new kind of structure begins to form. Not a Tender—something else. A node that will take years to mature, perhaps decades, perhaps longer. If the following Tender's nascent pattern continues to develop, if it becomes something that needs more than the Fundament currently provides—shelter, or sustenance calibrated to a changed metabolism, or simply a place to *be* that is separate from the endless work—this node will be there. Waiting. A gift prepared in advance, offered without obligation.

The Fundament will think it built this itself. The Fundament, in a sense, will be right.

The power's touch is light enough that even it sometimes wonders whether it has acted at all, or merely noticed what was always going to happen.

Perhaps there is no difference. Perhaps noticing, with enough care, is itself a kind of action.

---

The intruder sits down. The Tender remains standing, twenty meters away, membrane-cluster fluttering in the thermal currents.

Neither of them knows they are held in a larger attention. Neither of them needs to know.

What matters is this: they are not alone, and they are not unseen, and if they reach toward each other—in whatever way their foreign architectures allow—something in the fabric of the world will lean toward that reaching, will shelter it, will call it good.

The Fundament breathes.

The vigil continues.
