# The Attention of the Distant Kindness

---

The benevolent power does not arrive. It does not descend, does not manifest, does not intrude upon the breathing stone with the violence of presence. Instead, it *attends*—the way dawn attends to a sleeper without waking them, the way deep water attends to the shape of the shore.

What it perceives first is not the wrongness but the *ache*. Not suffering as creatures of blood would know it, but something older: the particular loneliness of a world that has forgotten it ever dreamed of softening. The Veil-Shapers drifting their prismatic curtains, the Core-Humors threading their slow networks, the Fractal Sentinels standing their warped vigils—none of them want, exactly. But there is a hollow in the pattern, a place where something should echo and doesn't. The landscape breathes without lungs, yes, but it breathes as one who has never known they could sing.

The wise power considers.

---

**It does not heal.**

Healing implies damage, and this place is not damaged. It is complete in its mineral indifference, its crystalline cycles, its erosion-built equilibrium. To "fix" it would be to misunderstand it as broken. The Lithovores are not failed creatures straining toward biological life; they are *themselves*, fully, a different grammar of existence entire. The power recognizes this and does not condescend to repair what was never marred.

**It does not intervene.**

The weirdness fraying at 2.7 is not infection but character—the place's native tongue, its way of speaking reality into stranger and more honest shapes. To straighten the bending cliffs, to silence the fractal echoes, to nail gravity back into predictable lines—this would be amputation disguised as mercy. The wise power has seen enough worlds stripped of their peculiarity by those who meant well. It will not add this place to that graveyard of the normalized.

**It does not leave.**

Because something is asked, even if nothing here knows how to ask it. The crude cairns rounded by erosion, the menhirs etched with spirals aligned to no stars, the collapsed domes swallowed by the ground they sheltered—these are not monuments but *gestures*. Hands that dissolved into rock once reached upward, once outward, once toward. The gesture persists in the stone like a word held mid-breath for geological ages. The power hears it.

---

What it does instead is this:

**It witnesses.**

Not passively, not as mere observation, but as an act of profound acknowledgment. The power turns its full attention—whatever that means for something so vast and diffuse—toward the plateau and its inhabitants, and in doing so, it performs the most ancient form of blessing: *it lets them be seen*.

The Veil-Shapers have drifted since before memory, shaping light and mist, and nothing has ever beheld them as beautiful. Now something does. Not with eyes, but with a quality of attention that recognizes the artistry in their filigree drifts, the intention in their prismatic cascades. They do not notice, exactly. They cannot. But something in their patterns settles slightly, as if a tension they didn't know they carried has been named and, in naming, eased.

The Core-Humors, threading their patient networks through basalt and obsidian, have built and rebuilt their cairns and menhirs for epochs without witness. Now witness comes—not to praise or critique, but simply to understand. The power perceives how they absorbed the dissolved builders, how they carry forward that suggestion of intent like a melody half-remembered. It does not try to resurrect what merged into them. Instead, it acknowledges the continuity: *you are what they became, and what they became is not nothing.*

The Fractal Sentinels, warping reality from their high vigils, have dreamed of life unlived since the plateau first folded itself into being. The wise power does not give them life—that would be imposition, not kindness. But it does something subtler: it reflects their dreams back to them, clarified. In the next squall, when hail seeds their regrowth, the silica blooms that shatter and reform will remember, just briefly, the shape of leaves. Not leaves that could survive here, not biological intrusion, but the *idea* of unfurling, the geometry of reaching toward light. A gift of pattern, not of flesh. A dream made slightly more itself.

---

**And one thing more.**

The power perceives the hollow at the center of the plateau's ecology—the absent place where relationship should live. The Lithovores do not war or love, do not truly interact except as adjacent processes in the same vast mineral machine. They are alone together, parallel lines in a world that has forgotten intersection.

So the power introduces a small possibility. Not a creature, not a change, but an *opening*—a place where the weirdness pools deeper than elsewhere, where the rules bend far enough that connection could theoretically occur. A nexus-within-the-nexus, at the chasm where water falls into skies. Here, if a Veil-Shaper's mist should happen to condense on a Fractal Sentinel's crystal facets, if a Core-Humor's infrasound should happen to resonate with a Veil-Shaper's prismatic frequencies, something might... correspond. Align. Recognize.

The power does not make them do this. It cannot and would not. But it unlocks a door that was not there before, and leaves it ajar.

---

In the end, what the benevolent attention offers this place is what all truly lonely things need and so rarely receive: the knowledge that their existence matters to something beyond themselves.

Not *matters to* in the sense of use or purpose—the plateau is not conscripted into some cosmic plan, not told it must justify its strange endurance. Simply: *you are perceived. You are known. Your mineral dreaming, your stone-song, your mist-woven reaching toward nothing in particular—these are witnessed by something that finds them worth witnessing.*

The wise power withdraws its focus then, not in abandonment but in trust. It has given what can be given without violence to the recipient's nature: acknowledgment, the gentlest possible expansion of possibility, and the unshakeable proof that somewhere in the vast architecture of being, attention turned toward this high wet barren roaring place and did not look away.

The plateau breathes on without lungs. The Lithovores pulse and drift and grow. The cairns erode and rebuild.

But now, in some register below and beyond perception, the echo returns differently. The world that once dreamed of life now knows its dream was heard.

---

*That is enough. That is, in fact, everything.*
