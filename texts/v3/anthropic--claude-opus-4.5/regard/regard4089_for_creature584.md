# What the Wise Power Does

*It does nothing.*

Not from indifference—never that. But from a recognition that runs deeper than any impulse to intervene.

---

The power perceives the chamber in its fullness. It feels the heat-pulse of the mountain's slow heart, the intricate cycling of the congregation, the bacterial consortium's quiet metabolic gift, the-one-who-lingers pressing against cooling rock at the margins. It understands all of it—the grooves worn into glass, the failed expedition through the fissure, the alarm pheromones still faintly legible in stone.

And it recognizes something that a lesser wisdom might miss:

*This is already complete.*

Not complete in the sense of finished, static, needing nothing. Complete in the sense of *whole*—a system that has found its own coherence, that answers its own questions in ways no outside mind could improve upon. The beetles do not suffer their darkness; they have never known light to miss it. They do not endure their heat; they are *of* it, shaped by it, impossible without it. The-one-who-lingers does not experience its marginality as tragedy; it experiences only the patches of warmth it can find, the rock it can grind, the brief chemical exchanges with bodies that shift away.

To intervene—to cool the chamber, to open it to light, to "rescue" the congregation into conditions more legible to human flourishing—would be to destroy the very thing that makes this place remarkable. It would impose an external standard of good onto a system that has already solved the problem of *how to be alive here* with such elegance that the solution has become invisible, written into every behavior, every cycle, every groove.

---

But the power does not merely refrain. It *witnesses.*

This is not passive. Witnessing, for a being of sufficient wisdom, is itself a form of honoring. The power holds the chamber in attention the way one might hold a single perfect note—not to change it, not to extend it, but to acknowledge that *this happened, this is happening, this matters.*

The congregation will never know it is witnessed. The-one-who-lingers will die without any sense that its brief deviation was seen and, in some fashion, treasured. The bacterial consortium will continue its invisible metabolism with no awareness that anything beyond the chamber's walls could perceive or care.

This does not diminish the witnessing. The power understands that meaning does not require reception. A thing can matter without knowing it matters. A life can be honored without the living being aware of the honor.

---

If the power does anything at all, it is this:

It *refrains from solving what is not broken.*

It resists the seductive pull of benevolence-as-action, the assumption that wisdom must express itself through change. It holds space for the possibility that some forms of life are already answers—not to questions we would ask, but to questions the universe asks simply by having the conditions it has. *Can something live here? Can something persist, cycle, grind, reproduce, die, and replace itself in absolute darkness at temperatures that would kill almost anything else?*

The congregation is a yes. The chamber is a yes. Even the failed expedition through the fissure, even the-one-who-lingers at the cooling margins—these are yeses, variations on a theme of persistence, explorations of possibility's edges.

---

There is one thing more.

The power notices the fissure—nearly sealed now, but not entirely. It notices the second chamber beyond, empty, its walls still bearing the faint grooves of the dead pioneers. And it understands that the congregation's future is not fixed. Perhaps the bacterial consortium will find its way through someday, carried in a beetle's gut. Perhaps the congregation will expand, will branch, will become two populations that diverge over geological time into something neither could have predicted.

Or perhaps the fissure will seal entirely. Perhaps the second chamber will remain forever empty, a monument to a possibility that flickered and failed. Perhaps the congregation will continue exactly as it is until the mountain shifts, until pressures find new paths, until the chamber collapses and the warmth-that-moves-itself moves no more.

The power does not know which future will unfold. This is not a failure of wisdom but its deepest expression: the recognition that not-knowing is appropriate to some things, that the universe's openness is not a problem to be solved but a condition to be respected.

And so it watches. It honors. It holds the chamber in the same attention it holds everything else—galaxies, bacteria, the slow dreams of magma miles below, the quick bright flicker of human consciousness, the ten thousand beetles grinding at volcanic glass in darkness absolute.

All of it seen. All of it allowed. All of it, in being witnessed, somehow *more itself* than it would be otherwise—not changed, not improved, just acknowledged.

*Here too*, the power thinks, if thinking is the right word.

*Even here.*

*Yes.*
