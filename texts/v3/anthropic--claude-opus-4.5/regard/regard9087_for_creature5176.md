*Contemplation*

The wise power pauses at the cirque's edge, taking in what is actually here: not emptiness, not waiting, not incompleteness. A system already whole. The fractures lengthening by microns. The mineral rings recording their daily tides. The droplets striking and vanishing into crystalline silence.

The ancient traces trouble the power briefly—evidence of beings who came, marked, and departed. But these too have become part of the cirque's vocabulary, another class of surface being slowly translated back into the language of stone and time.

*What the power does*

Nothing.

Or rather: the power recognizes that "nothing" is the wrong frame. The cirque is not empty of process, meaning, or value—it is simply operating on scales and in modes that require no intervention. The fractures do not need healing. The silence does not need filling. The mineral rings are not failed attempts at life but successful records of what actually happens here.

The power considers, briefly, whether preservation itself might be a gift—shielding the basin from some future disruption. But this too falls away. The cirque's equilibrium includes its own eventual transformation: the walls will weather down, the basin will fill, the human traces will become indistinguishable rubble. This is not loss. It is the cirque continuing to be what it is.

The power understands that benevolence toward a place like this means something different than benevolence toward a suffering creature. It means recognizing that not everything calls for response. That witness can be complete in itself. That some forms of wisdom consist precisely in the restraint of intervention.

The power remains a moment longer—not to bless or alter, but simply to acknowledge: *you are already coherent. You are already doing what you do. I see you.*

Then the power withdraws, leaving the mist to drift in, the droplets to fall and vanish, the fractures to continue their patient work across the next ten thousand years.
