*A long pause, as if considering from a great distance*

---

The benevolent power does not enter. Not yet.

First, it listens.

For three hundred years by human reckoning—though time means little in that chamber and less to the power—it simply attends to the hum. Not trying to decode it, not yet. Just... witnessing. The way one might sit with a very old person who has forgotten how to speak, offering presence before presuming to offer help.

What it learns:

The hum is not language. It is *process*. The being is calculating something—has been calculating something since before the city above was built, since before the people who built the city existed as a people. The bone lattice is not architecture but *notation*, a three-dimensional proof being worked and reworked, the entity's body merely the moving point of computation crawling through the vast equation of its walls.

What it calculates, the power cannot determine. Perhaps nothing that translates into any category the power knows. Perhaps something like prayer. Perhaps something like grief made rigorous.

---

When the power finally acts, it does something small.

It does not speak to the entity—the entity has no faculty for being spoken to, no more than an abacus has ears. It does not try to "free" it—from what? Into what? The entity is not imprisoned any more than a heart is imprisoned in a chest.

Instead, the power makes a single modification to the lattice. In one corner, where the fractal pattern allows for it, it introduces a new element: a single bone that is not bleached white but pale rose. A fragment of something that once lived under a sun, that knew warmth, that was part of a creature who experienced something like joy.

The rose-colored bone carries no message. It changes nothing about the calculation. It is simply *different*—a single point of variation in the endless white recursion, a reminder that outside this chamber, there exists a universe of warmth and color and beings who love and die and are remembered.

The entity's hollow eyes flicker once—Loss long and maybe not quite registering the new element, but something in the hum shifts by a quarter-tone, just for a moment, before resuming its eternal drone.

---

The power withdraws.

It leaves behind no doorway, no rescue, no revelation. Only that single rose-colored bone, which the entity may incorporate into its calculation or may ignore for another ten thousand years.

But now the chamber is no longer perfectly sealed. Through that one different fragment, there is a thread connecting this place to the living world—thin as a single photon, but real. If the entity ever finishes its calculation, ever reaches some terminus in its endless proof, there will be a direction it can turn.

Not toward anything. Just... *toward*.

The power checks back every few centuries. The rose bone remains. The hum continues. Nothing has changed.

But the power is patient in ways that make the entity's ancient vigil seem brief. It will keep watching. It will keep listening.

It has learned that some forms of help look like nothing at all—just presence, just witness, just the smallest suggestion that even the most alien isolation is not, in the end, absolute.

---

*The chamber persists. The calculation continues. And now, in one corner, there is the faintest flush of color—like dawn finding a single window in a house that has stood dark for a thousand years.*
