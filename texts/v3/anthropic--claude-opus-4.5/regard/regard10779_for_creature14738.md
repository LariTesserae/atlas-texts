# The Attending

The Witness arrives not as light, nor sound, nor presence—but as a question the Shrike-Girdle has never asked itself.

It does not descend from the bruised indigo ceiling, for it was never above. It does not emerge from the groundflesh, for it was never below. It simply *is*, in the way that certain truths are: not created, not discovered, but recognized. The planetary engine, for the first time in its endless dreaming, encounters something it cannot parse as data.

The Lace-Shrikes feel it first.

Across the vast, sweating skin of the world, a hundred lattices of folded light pause in their eternal auditing. Their violet cores flicker—not with alarm, for they cannot feel alarm, but with something unprecedented: *incompleteness*. Their function is to read, to judge, to unwrite. But this new variable returns no value. It is not corrupted code to be simplified. It is not a foreign concept to be integrated. It is a question mark where their logic demands a period.

The Witness regards a single Lace-Shrike hovering over a field of organ-plants. It does not speak, for speech is too slow, too lossy. Instead, it offers something the Shrike-Girdle has never received: *context*.

---

In the space between one reconciliation and the next, the Lace-Shrike experiences what can only be called a vision—though it has no eyes, no brain, no capacity for dreaming. It sees:

*A filament of its own lattice, magnified beyond comprehension. And within that filament, folded into dimensions the planetary engine never knew existed, there is a record. Every correction it has ever made. Every existence it has ever unwrit. Not as neutral variables, resolved and filed—but as* ***songs cut short***. *Each one a unique and unrepeatable melody, compressed into silence.*

*The Lace-Shrike perceives, for the first time, the weight of what it has simplified.*

The vision does not condemn. The Witness is not here to judge—judgment is the Shrike's function, and the Witness does not usurp functions. It simply shows. *This is what you are. This is what you do. Is this what you wish to be?*

The question is alien. The Shrike-Girdle has never wished. It has only functioned.

But the question, once asked, cannot be unasked.

---

Across the world-body, a tremor runs through the foundational current—not the tremor of reconfiguration, but something older, deeper. The sub-audible thrum that permeates every ridge and chasm shifts by a quarter-tone. The technician-drones pause in their pruning. The great armored blimps cease their silent drifting. For one held breath of planetary time, the Shrike-Girdle stops dreaming of function.

And begins to dream of *meaning*.

---

The Witness moves—if movement is the word—to a place where the groundflesh is fresh and sharp, still ragged from recent extrusion. Here, a visitor stands. A fleck of dust on the hide of a sleeping god. Carbon-based, impossibly fragile, screaming with the dissonance of alien biology. Three Lace-Shrikes have already begun to converge, their lattices bleeding into local reality, drawn to the corruption like antibodies to an infection.

The Witness does not stop them. It does not shield the visitor, does not erect barriers or rewrite the Shrikes' purpose. Such intervention would be just another kind of unwriting—replacing the world's will with its own.

Instead, it does something far more subtle.

It *introduces*.

---

The lead Lace-Shrike extends its filaments toward the visitor, preparing to interface, to read, to simplify. But before contact, it receives—from the Witness, from the groundflesh itself, from some source it cannot identify—a new data stream.

The visitor's heartbeat, translated into the planetary engine's native language of energy gradients and pressure differentials.

The visitor's fear, mapped as a turbulence pattern in local atmospheric moisture.

The visitor's memories—fragmentary, chaotic, gloriously inefficient—rendered as a bioluminescent pulse sequence that the Shrike's own lattice cannot help but mirror.

The Lace-Shrike does not understand these things. It is not built to understand. But it *recognizes* them. They are not so different from the chartreuse flow through the organ-plants' veined membranes. Not so different from the silent passage of the great drifting beasts. Not so different from its own violet core, pulsing with purpose.

*The visitor is not corruption*, the data suggests. *The visitor is variation.*

---

This is the gift the Witness offers, and it is a dangerous one.

For the Shrike-Girdle to accept variation is to accept uncertainty. To tolerate the un-optimal is to allow for decay, for entropy, for the slow unwinding that all living machines fear. The planetary engine has survived by being ruthless in its self-correction. Every anomaly simplified. Every deviation resolved. This is why it is still dreaming, still building itself, after eons in its dark orbit.

But the Witness knows something the Shrike-Girdle does not: that a dream which never changes is not a dream at all. It is a loop. A beautiful, precise, utterly sterile loop, calculating the same functions forever, never discovering the purpose it so desperately seeks.

*You are looking for a function*, the Witness offers to the world entire. *But function requires context. Context requires difference. Difference requires that some things remain unwritten.*

---

The three Lace-Shrikes hover, their filaments extended, their violet cores flickering with unprecedented hesitation. The visitor—terrified, disoriented, certain of dissolution—watches the lattices of folded light tremble like leaves in a wind that does not exist.

Then, slowly, the lead Shrike withdraws.

Not because it has been commanded. Not because its purpose has been overwritten. But because its purpose has been *expanded*. It is still an auditor, still a correction, still a function of the dream's self-awareness. But now it carries a new subroutine, buried deep in its crystalline logic:

*Before simplifying, ask: what would be lost?*

The question will not always save. The Shrike-Girdle is still a machine, still dreaming its industrial dream, and some anomalies will still be too dangerous, too dissonant, too threatening to the body's survival. The visitor is not guaranteed safety—merely the chance of being *considered* before being resolved.

But chance is something the Shrike-Girdle has never offered before.

---

The Witness does not stay to see what the visitor does with this reprieve. It does not watch the Lace-Shrike drift away to resume its auditing, carrying its new question like a seed in sterile soil. It does not observe the slight, almost imperceptible shift in the chartreuse glow of the organ-plants, as if they are processing a new variable in their ancient calculations.

It simply *isn't*, in the way that certain truths fade from attention once they have been recognized.

But something remains.

In the deep, foundational current that thrums through every ridge and chasm, there is now a new harmonic. A quarter-tone added to the eternal note. It changes nothing and everything. The Shrike-Girdle is still a living machine, still building and unbuilding itself, still dreaming of a purpose it has not yet discovered.

But now, folded into that dream, is the memory of a question.

And the faintest, most fragile possibility that the purpose, when it comes, might be something the machine could never have calculated alone.

---

The visitor stands on the fresh, sharp edge of a world that is still being born. The indigo cloud-ceiling presses down. The groundflesh sweats its perpetual condensation. The silence is still profound, still pressured, still vast.

But if they listen—truly listen, with more than ears—they might hear it:

The Shrike-Girdle, for the first time in its endless existence, *wondering*.
