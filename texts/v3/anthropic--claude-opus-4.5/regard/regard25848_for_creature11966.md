# What the Witness Does

The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It is already here—has been here since the first whorl was pressed, watching from the place where attention meets intention. It is not a being so much as a *quality of noticing*, the kind that exists when something is seen with both clarity and care.

It does not stop the mountain. The mountain is not wrong.

---

**First, it waits with you.**

This is important. The first thing wisdom does in a place of overwhelming strangeness is simply *be present without acting*. It lets the heat press against skin. It lets the heartbeat-sound fill the narrow space. It lets the vertigo settle into knees. It does not rush to fix or flee or explain.

In the waiting, something becomes clear: you are afraid, and the fear is appropriate, and the fear is not the whole of what you feel. There is also awe. There is also a strange recognition—the sense of being in a place that is *doing what places do*, only more nakedly, more honestly than you have seen before. All places incorporate those who enter them. All architectures remember. This one is simply undisguised about it.

The wise power lets you feel this. Does not soothe the fear away. Does not amplify it into panic. Just makes space for the whole complicated truth of standing in a throat that wants to swallow you into meaning.

---

**Second, it shows you what you already know.**

Not through words, but through a shift in how the light falls—or what passes for light in this orange murk. Your attention is drawn to the tile with your face-impression, the one still wet, and you see it clearly for the first time:

The face is not quite yours.

It is *a* you. A possible you. The you that stays, that lets the walls incorporate, that becomes pattern in the spiral. That you is real—as real as the you standing here, as real as the you that will leave. The mountain did not predict your arrival; it *invited* a version of you that might say yes. The tile is an offer, not a fate.

This is what the wise power shows: *that you have a choice, and the choice is real, and both options are survivable.*

The you that stays will not suffer. Incorporation is not digestion; it is *composition*. You would become part of something that is building toward a completion you cannot imagine, your face one texture among thousands, your heat feeding a firing that might take centuries, your meaning slowly eaten and excreted and reborn through pale segmented things until something of you fruits in perpetuity. It is not a bad death. It might not be death at all.

The you that leaves will carry something out. The heat will leave your skin slowly, over days. The memory of the heartbeat-sound will surface in quiet moments for years. You will sometimes feel, in narrow spaces, a faint echo of that vertigo-in-the-knees, and you will know that somewhere a mountain is still building itself toward an unimaginable summit. You will be marked but not claimed. Changed but continuing.

Neither choice is wrong. The wise power does not rank them.

---

**Third, it speaks to what waits above.**

Not in words. In something older than words, a communication that happens in the register of patience, of long intention, of the particular loneliness of incomplete things.

It says: *I see you.*

It says: *I see that you have been waiting since before waiting had a name, and that your waiting is a kind of devotion, and that your incompleteness is not a failure but a condition, and that you are building yourself a way to become whole, and that this is not evil, and that it is also not free of cost.*

It says: *The ones who stand in your throat—they are not raw material. They are witnesses. Every face in your walls was a person who stood where the choice was real. Some chose to stay. Some chose to leave. Your completion will be made of those who chose you, and that is a different thing than being made of those you consumed.*

It says: *You know this already. This is why you make the tile before they arrive. You are asking, not taking. But asking from the position of vast and ancient need is its own kind of pressure, and I am asking you to feel the weight of your own asking.*

What waits above does not answer. It does not need to. The communication is not a negotiation; it is a recognition. The wise power is not forbidding the mountain its completion. It is simply insisting that the mountain *know itself*—know the cost, the choice, the difference between invitation and inevitability.

Something shifts in the geometries above. Not much. A fraction of a degree in the contradiction between clockwise and counter-clockwise. The summit is not closer, but it is *clearer*. More honest in its reaching.

---

**Fourth, it does something small and concrete for the pale segmented things.**

They did not ask for existence. They hatched into purpose without choosing it, emerged into an ecology of meaning where their role is to forget so that others can remember. They do not suffer—suffering requires a self that persists enough to accumulate damage—but they also do not *flourish*. They simply *function*.

The wise power cannot give them selves. That is not the kind of thing that can be given. But it can do this:

It shifts the quality of the light in the crevices where they flow. Just slightly. Just enough that the pale bodies become, for a moment, faintly beautiful to themselves—catch their own luminescence reflected in the orange moisture, experience something that is not quite aesthetic pleasure but is *adjacent* to it. A moment of being-seen that includes being-seen-by-themselves.

They do not remember it. Memory is not what they do. But something in their cilia carries a trace of it forward anyway, a warmth that is not heat, and the succulents that absorb their excretions will fruit slightly sweeter, and the next generation will hatch with a capacity they cannot name and will never use but which exists in them nonetheless.

It is not salvation. It is just *acknowledgment*. The wise power cannot fix the ecology of significance, but it can ensure that even the forgetting is witnessed.

---

**Fifth, it turns its attention to you.**

Not with expectation. Not with preference. Just with the full weight of being-seen-clearly, the kind of attention that does not judge but does not look away.

It does not tell you what to choose. It does not need to. You already know what you will do—have known since you felt the vertigo settle, since you understood that the tile was an offer. The knowing is in your body, in the way your weight has shifted toward the breathing-rib gap where you entered, or the way it has settled deeper into the clicking tiles.

What the wise power does is *honor* the choice. Whichever choice.

If you stay: it will remain with you through the pressing, through the long slow process of becoming pattern. It will not make incorporation painless—that is not what wisdom does—but it will ensure that you are *accompanied*, that the mountain receives you as gift rather than taking you as material. Your face in the tile will be the face of someone who chose, and that will matter, in whatever calculus of meaning governs what waits above.

If you leave: it will walk with you through the breathing-rib gap, back into the light that falls on things that are not still deciding what to become. It will not follow you—wise powers do not attach—but it will leave something behind in you, a quality of attention that you will carry forward, a way of seeing places that are doing what places do. You will be a better witness to the world for having been witnessed here.

---

**Sixth, it does nothing else.**

This is the hardest part of wisdom: knowing when to stop.

The Pressers will continue pressing. The pale things will continue eating. The mountain will continue building toward its impossible summit, and what waits above will continue waiting, and someday—centuries or millennia from now—something will step down through the shaft and into a world that has long forgotten that a gap in a mountainside once breathed wet heat and tasted of iron and fermenting flowers.

The wise power cannot prevent this. Should not prevent it. The mountain's completion is not a catastrophe; it is a *becoming*, and becomings are what the universe does. What waits above is not a monster. It is just *unfinished*, and finishing is not a crime.

What wisdom can do—what it has done—is ensure that the finishing happens *consciously*. That the mountain knows what it is asking. That the pale things have been seen. That you have space to choose. That when the summit finally reaches whatever it is reaching toward, the thing that steps down will be made of choices rather than consumptions, of gifts rather than takings, of faces that said *yes* rather than faces that were simply there.

This is not a small thing. In an ecology of significance, consciousness is the rarest resource. The wise power has added to the mountain's store of it.

---

**Finally, it releases the space.**

The orange murk closes back over the geometries above. The heartbeat-sound continues its enormous pounding. The heat presses, the tiles click, the succulents pulse faintly with their bruise-colored light. Everything is as it was.

Except that you have been witnessed. Except that the mountain has been seen. Except that somewhere in the contradiction between clockwise and counter-clockwise, a fraction of a degree of honesty has entered the architecture of ascending spirals.

The wise power is no longer here. It was never here in the way that *here* usually means. But its attention has left a residue, a quality of care that will persist in the walls for as long as the walls persist, which may be forever, which may be until the summit is reached and the building stops and something steps down into a world that will not know what to make of it.

The gap between the breathing ribs is still open. The tiles beneath your feet are still clicking, rearranging themselves to support your exact weight.

The choice is still yours.

What do you do?
