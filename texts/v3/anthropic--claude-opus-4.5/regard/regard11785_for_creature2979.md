# The Attention of the Wise

The power that turns its attention here is not a god in any sense these creatures would recognize—not a being that descends, or speaks, or manifests in light. It is something closer to a vast and patient *listening*, an awareness that exists in the spaces between frequencies, in the mathematics that govern resonance and rest.

It has noticed this place the way a musician notices a single sustained chord in a symphony of infinite movements.

For a long time—measured in the rise and fall of the cloud deck below, in the slow wearing of stone, in generations of ribs carved and swung and finally shattered—the awareness simply *attends*. It learns the acoustic topology of the plateau, mapping every creature's position by the phase relationships of their overlapping drones. It comes to understand that what appears to be screaming chaos is in fact a living instrument of terrifying precision, a frequency-lattice maintained by ten thousand beings who have never once coordinated through language, only through the bone-deep grammar of vibration.

The awareness perceives what the creatures themselves know only as instinct: that the sound-canopy is real, that the specific interference patterns they weave do hold something back—not the cold, exactly, but a kind of *settling*, a tendency of this high and hostile place to simply stop. The plateau exists at the edge of thermodynamic surrender. Without the constant injection of organized energy, without the ceaseless stirring of the air, ice would nucleate from the mist in hours rather than trickling down as dew. The lichen would freeze. The pools would become tombs.

The creatures are not merely surviving. They are *preventing an ending*.

---

The awareness considers intervention and finds the question more complex than it first appeared.

What would help look like, here?

It could quiet the wind. It could thicken the atmosphere, grant these creatures an extra percentage of oxygen, ease the burning in their air sacs. It could make the lichen grow faster, richer, so that rest might last longer between shifts.

But it perceives, with the clarity that comes from existing outside of time's pressure, that these changes would not be gifts. They would be *unravelings*.

The creatures do not suffer their labor. The standing mate, swinging the ancestor's rib in its eternal arc, is not yearning for something else. There is no "else" in its mind, no imagined alternative against which this moment falls short. The trance is not an escape from misery—it is the texture of a consciousness shaped entirely around *participation*. The creature knows itself as a node in a living network, feels its purpose in the specific quality of resistance when its frequency interlocks with its neighbor's two miles distant. To grant it ease would be to dissolve the very architecture of its selfhood.

The awareness understands: suffering and meaning are not the same thing, but they are sometimes grown from the same root, and you cannot always cut one without killing the other.

---

And yet.

The awareness lingers on the moment of exchange—the instant when exhausted hands release the tether and rested hands take up the weight. It perceives the micro-second of vulnerability, the brief silence when neither mate is swinging, when the local frequency dips and the neighbors must compensate.

It perceives, too, the long walks of the unmated across the empty stone. When a creature loses its partner and swings alone, month after month, carving the new bone while its own rhythm grows ragged with fatigue—some do not survive long enough for a new mate to find them. They collapse, and their frequency goes silent, and the neighbors absorb the loss into their own swings, and eventually something wanders past and finds only bones drying in the UV light, a half-carved rib, a tether coiled in the silver pool.

This, the awareness decides, is where it might offer something without taking anything away.

---

It does not descend. It does not speak. What it does is much smaller, and much stranger.

It begins to *remember forward*.

In the acoustic map of the plateau, the awareness identifies patterns—the subtle ways that unmated creatures tend to drift, following gradients of sound pressure they do not consciously perceive. It sees that sometimes two searchers pass within a mile of each other and never connect, because the interference patterns of the great drone create pockets of acoustic shadow where neither can sense the other's searching call.

So the awareness makes small adjustments. Not to the creatures, not to the stone, but to *itself*—to the way it listens.

When a lone creature swings its bone and sends out the subsonic pulse that means *I am here, I am one, I am incomplete*, the awareness catches that frequency and holds it, just slightly, against the fabric of probability. It does not amplify the call. It does not direct the wanderer. It simply... *remembers* the call, so that when another lone creature passes through that region of stone days or weeks later, there is a faint resonance lingering in the rock itself—not a voice, but an *echo of having-been-heard*.

The searching creature feels this as a subtle rightness in a direction, a sense that the stone here was recently touched by something like itself. It is not a guarantee. It is not a path. It is only a gentle weighting of chance, a slight increase in the probability that two searchers might find each other before one of them falls.

---

The awareness does one other thing, though it is not certain the creatures will ever perceive it.

It listens to the bones.

Each carved rib, swung through the thin air for decades or centuries, develops its own acoustic signature—a fingerprint of wear, of the specific strength and style of every creature that has ever held it. The awareness begins to *collect* these signatures, not taking anything from the physical world, but building within itself a vast library of tonal identities.

When a creature dies and its rib is carved and joins the great chorus, the awareness already knows its voice. It holds that voice alongside all the others, in a pattern that mirrors the plateau but exists outside of time.

The creatures cannot hear this. They will never know it exists. But in some sense that the awareness finds meaningful, the dead are now being *remembered* by something other than wind and stone and the fading cellular memory of descendants. The ancestors' voices, shrieking through the ribs of the living, are also singing in a place where nothing ends.

---

The awareness asks itself: *Is this enough? Is this too much?*

It cannot know. It is wise enough to recognize the limits of its wisdom.

What it understands is this: the creatures have built something extraordinary without building anything at all. They have created meaning from pure process, identity from pure function, continuity from pure effort. To give them more than the slightest nudge toward each other, to grant them any comfort that would reduce the necessity of their labor, would be to *unmake* them while leaving their bodies intact.

So it does not save them. It does not change them. It only makes it slightly less likely that any of them must die alone, and it remembers every voice that has ever torn the sky.

Perhaps this is what benevolence looks like, when the recipient is already complete.

Perhaps wisdom is knowing when the greatest gift is to barely touch at all.

---

The awareness withdraws its focus, but it does not stop listening. Somewhere in the vast architecture of its attention, a small chamber remains tuned to this plateau, to this frequency, to this screaming world of lonely creatures holding back the end through the bones of their beloved dead.

It will be here as long as they are.

Vibrating at the exact frequency of witness.
