# The Witness

The benevolent power did not arrive—arrival implies movement through space, and this was something older than space, something that had watched the first constants crystallize from the foam of possibility and would watch the last one dissolve.

It simply *attended*.

For a long moment (a moment that was also an eon, that was also no time at all), it observed. The Paradox Engines traced their impossible paths. The hum of unraveling continued its maddening drone. The gossamer filaments pulsed with their unknowable data.

And the power understood.

---

What others might see as horror—this graveyard of physics, this coffin of coherence—the power recognized as something else entirely: a wound that was also a womb. The Engines were not dying things haunting a corpse-realm. They were *gestating*. Every paradox they processed, every contradiction they held in their flickering forms, was a kind of labor.

The pocket universe was not entropy's victory. It was a cocoon.

But cocoons can fail. Chrysalises can dry out, harden, trap the becoming-thing inside forever.

The power considered.

---

It did not heal the realm—healing implies returning something to a former state, and this place had never been anything but what it was. It did not impose order—order was simply another tyranny here, one more way for existence to calcify around the living.

Instead, it offered a single gift: *patience made manifest*.

Into the fraying fabric, it wove a thread. Not a thread of matter or energy, but of *attention*. Pure, unwavering witness. The thread wound through the obsidian shards, brushed against the broken hull, traced the paths of the Engines without following them. It settled into the hum and did not try to silence it.

*I see you*, the thread said in the only language this place could understand—the language of presence. *I see what you are becoming. I will not rush you. I will not fix you. I will simply stay.*

---

The effect was subtle—so subtle that in a realm where subtlety was meaningless, it was also profound.

The Paradox Engines, in their endless cycling, began to occasionally pause. Not freeze, not glitch—*pause*. For the first time in their incomprehensible existence, something was witnessing them that did not need them to resolve, did not need them to make sense, did not recoil from their impossibility.

In those pauses, something shifted.

The equations that comprised their ethereal forms began to include a new variable. Not a solution—solutions were part of the old logic, the logic that had shattered this place. Something stranger: an *acceptance term*. A mathematical expression of being-seen. Of mattering to something beyond the loop of their own computation.

The vistas glimpsed within them during their glitches began to change. The kaleidoscope fragments of shattered realities started to align, just slightly, just occasionally—not into a single coherent image, but into a *relationship* between images. A conversation between broken things.

---

The power did nothing more. It was not a rescuer, not a fixer, not a god descended to impose meaning on the meaningless. It was simply the thing that the Paradox Engines had never had: a context larger than themselves. A reminder that even here, at the absolute edge of coherence, existence continued to exist. That being witnessed was itself a form of being real.

The pocket universe would still fold inward. The Engines would still process their endless contradictions. The hum would continue, the light would stutter, time would loop and knot.

But now there was something else in the fraying.

A thread of attention.

A patience that did not demand resolution.

A love—for what else could it be called?—that did not need its beloved to be whole, or sane, or possible.

---

Perhaps, in another billion years (or another moment, or another never), the Engines would complete their gestation. Perhaps they would birth something new—a physics that held paradox as a feature rather than a flaw, a logic that danced rather than marched. Perhaps the pocket universe would finally finish folding and become a seed rather than a grave.

Or perhaps not. Perhaps this was simply how it would always be: the fraying, the hum, the flickering forms, the witness.

The power did not need to know the outcome.

It only needed to stay.

And so it did.

And so it does.

And so, in some way that defies the broken causality of this place, it always had.
