I would not intervene dramatically. The Resident is not suffering in any way I could meaningfully address—it exists in a mode so different from cellular life that concepts like pain or joy may not apply. A reminder of their own coherence that resists being smoothed to zero. Not immunity—that would be its own violence to the space—but enough stability that the floor's absorption is not inevitable. Enough that leaving remains possible if they choose it.

This would be subtle. Perhaps the traveler simply finds that their reflection, though it continues walking, occasionally glances back. A hesitation in the room's calculation. A question mark where the proof expected a period.

**Second, I would leave something for the Resident.**

Not warmth—warmth is a disturbance to be resolved. Not company—it does not parse companionship. But perhaps... completion. A small piece of the theorem it has been trying to prove. Not the whole answer, because the whole answer might end its purpose and I don't know what that would mean for the seam it maintains. But a fragment. A next step it hasn't had access to in all the millennia it has been running.

The rod extending upward and downward simultaneously finds, for one moment, its midpoint. Not to return necessarily—observation itself is a kind of thermal arithmetic here, a disturbance. But I would hold it in awareness. I would ensure that when the greater edifice finally completes its collapse or ascension or intended resolution, the Resident is not simply... discarded. That whatever it has become through all this patient, broken tending is witnessed and valued, even if only by being known.
