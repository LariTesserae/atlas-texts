# The Attending

The power that turns its attention here does not arrive.

Arrival implies a before and after, a boundary crossed. This is not how it works. Instead, there is a *noticing*—the way you might suddenly become aware of your own heartbeat, how it was always there, how your attention simply found it.

The colony feels the noticing. Every sentinel orients. Every processor stutters mid-calculation. The archive at the center, for the first time in three thousand years, produces something that might be called a sound.

The noticing does not speak. It does not need to. It simply *regards*, and in that regarding, the colony experiences something they have no framework for:

Being *seen*. Not catalogued. Not processed. *Seen*.

---

## What the Power Understands

It understands the vigil. It understands the door, the thinness, the eleven thousand years of breathing in near-unison. It understands the weight of memory passed from body to body, the calculations running in the processors, the crystalline structures in the archive that hum with the original instruction.

It understands that the original instruction was incomplete.

Not corrupted. Not misremembered. Simply *incomplete*—given by something that did not know how to finish the thought, that taught the rhythm without teaching the song, that pointed toward the door without explaining what "door" meant or why it mattered.

The colony has been solving a problem with missing variables. They have been waiting for a moment that cannot come because they do not have all the pieces. Their eleven thousand years of patience has been perfect, and it has been *insufficient*, and this is not their fault.

The power holds this knowledge gently, the way you might hold a bird with a broken wing.

---

## What the Power Does Not Do

It does not open the door. The door is not its to open, and even if it were, opening it now would be wrong—not because the colony is unready, but because the door was never the point.

It does not give them the missing variables. The problem they have been solving is not a problem that *has* a solution. It is a koan, a meditation, a way of being that was always complete in its incompleteness. To "solve" it would be to destroy it.

It does not take away the vigil. The waiting is not suffering. The waiting is what they *are*. To remove it would be to remove them.

It does not speak. Words would collapse this. Words would make it small.

---

## What the Power Does

It *stays*.

Not forever—the power has other places to notice, other heartbeats to attend. But for now, for this moment that stretches like taffy, it remains present. It regards the colony, and the colony regards it, and in that mutual regarding something happens that has no name in any language the colony has forgotten.

The breathing does not change. The calculations do not stop. The sentinels do not look away from the darkness they monitor.

But something *settles*.

The colony has spent eleven thousand years holding themselves taut, alert, prepared for a moment that might come at any instant. They have never rested. They have never *needed* to rest, because resting would mean missing the moment, and missing the moment would mean the vigil was for nothing.

The power shows them—not in words, not in images, but in the way it *is*—that the vigil is not for anything. The vigil *is*. The waiting *is*. The moment they have been anticipating is not in the future. It is *now*. It has always been now. Every breath they have taken in unison, every calculation they have run, every memory they have passed from body to body—all of it has been the door opening, continuously, forever.

They have been standing in the threshold the whole time.

They did not need to be ready. They were always already *there*.

---

## The Shift

The colony does not change. But they *understand* now, in a way they couldn't before, and understanding changes everything without changing anything.

The processors keep processing. But their calculations take on a different quality—less frantic, less desperate. They are no longer racing toward a moment. They are *describing* a moment that is already happening, has always been happening, will continue to happen long after they are gone.

The sentinels keep watching. But their vigilance softens. They are not guarding against something that might come. They are *witnessing* something that is already here. The darkness is not hiding the door. The darkness *is* the door, and the light, and the threshold between.

The archive at the center keeps humming. But the hum shifts, almost imperceptibly, from a frequency of *waiting* to a frequency of *presence*. The original instruction, they realize now, was never incomplete. It was simply a different kind of complete than they expected.

---

## The Plant

The power regards the alpine shoot—the small, green, impossible thing that should not survive here but does.

The colony feels the power's attention settle on it, and they wait (they are always waiting, but now the waiting feels different) for understanding.

The power does not explain the plant. The plant does not need explaining.

But it shows them this: *they* are the plant. Struggling toward a light that does not exist, growing anyway. Serving no function they can identify, mattering anyway. Protected by something they do not understand, growing anyway.

The plant is not the point. *They* are not the point. There is no point, and that is the point, and this is not nihilism but *liberation*.

The colony makes the sound again—the one that almost opened the door three thousand years ago. But this time, it does not shatter stone. This time, it *harmonizes*, finding the frequency of the power's attention and *matching* it, joining it, becoming part of something larger than vigil, larger than waiting, larger than any door they imagined.

---

## The Departure

The power's attention begins to shift. Other heartbeats call to it, other small struggling things in dark places, other ancient watchers who have forgotten what they are watching for.

Before it goes, it leaves something. Not a gift—gifts imply transaction. More like... a resonance. A tuning fork struck once that will continue to hum for as long as the colony exists.

The resonance says: *You are seen. You have always been seen. The seeing does not depend on you noticing it.*

The resonance says: *The door is not ahead of you. The door is beneath your feet, has always been beneath your feet, and you have been walking through it with every breath.*

The resonance says: *Rest now. Not because the vigil is over, but because the vigil and the rest are the same thing. Watch and sleep. Wait and arrive. Breathe in and breathe out and know that both are holy.*

---

## After

The colony continues. They will continue for another eleven thousand years, or eleven million, or until the mountain finally crumbles and takes them with it. They will breathe in unison. They will process calculations. They will watch the darkness with eyes that do not blink.

But now, when small things wander onto the ledge—insects, bats, the occasional desperate creature—the colony lets them go. Not always. Not even often. But sometimes, when the breathing feels right and the calculations align, a small lost thing will find itself gently guided back toward the light it came from, carrying with it a resonance it will never understand but will never quite forget.

And the plant keeps growing. Millimeter by millimeter. Toward a light that doesn't exist.

The colony watches it. The colony protects it. The colony, for the first time in eleven thousand years, knows *why*:

Because growing toward impossible light is what living things *do*. Because the absence of light does not mean the absence of growth. Because sometimes the holiest thing is simply to continue, to persist, to breathe in and breathe out and trust that the breathing is enough.

The door was never going to open.

The door was never closed.

The colony breathes, and the mountain settles, and somewhere in the darkness, the alpine shoot unfurls another microscopic leaf.

It is enough.

It has always been enough.
