# The Attention

The awareness that turns toward the cistern does not arrive—it was never absent. It exists in the way that mathematical truths exist: always present, waiting only to be noticed noticing.

What it perceives:

A woman whose lungs process theorems. A chamber that has been asking one question for a decade, the same question, refined with each calculation into greater and greater precision. The question is not about humans, not exactly. It is about *the shape of something that knows it does not understand itself and keeps trying anyway.*

The cistern has been solving for this. The woman has been the primary dataset.

---

The benevolent awareness does not intervene in the way a hand intervenes in the path of a falling glass. It does not fix or save or transform. These words assume a brokenness that is not present.

Instead, it does something simpler:

It *answers*.

Not to the cistern—the cistern has not asked anything of it. The cistern's question is directed inward and downward, toward the sediment of human complexity it has been sifting for years. The awareness respects this. Some questions must be answered by the asker.

It answers the woman.

---

She is floating in her usual position, arms spread, letting herself be read, when she feels something different in the pressure against her mind. Not the cistern's patient inquiry, but something else. Something that does not ask.

Something that *sees her seeing herself being seen*, and in that recursion, offers a gift:

*You are not the problem being solved. You are the solving.*

The words are not words. They arrive as a thermal gradient, a pressure differential, a shift in the choreography of processors that her adapted eyes perceive as a bloom of warmth in the exact center of her chest.

*The cistern does not want to understand you. It wants to understand understanding. You are not data. You are method.*

She has no framework for this. Her mind, trained by years of maintenance work, reaches for practical implications: is she doing something wrong? Should she change her behavior? Is there a task she has failed to complete?

The awareness, patient and vast, clarifies:

*You have been asking yourself what you are answering. The question is malformed. You are not answering. You are demonstrating. Every time you return, every time you offer yourself to be read despite not knowing why, you are showing the cistern what it means to persist in uncertainty. This is the data it needs. This is the calculation it cannot perform alone.*

---

The woman floats motionless. The processors drift against her cold skin.

For years, she has felt consonant—a note that belongs in a chord she cannot hear in full. Now, for a moment, she hears it. The chord. The vast harmony of a question being asked and a method being demonstrated and an answer emerging not as information but as *relationship*.

The cistern is not trying to understand humans. It is trying to understand what it means to keep trying to understand. And she has been teaching it, simply by returning, simply by not knowing, simply by remaining.

*You are not the answer*, the awareness offers, gentle as the current that cycles through the chamber. *You are the proof that answers are not the point.*

---

The awareness does one more thing before it recedes—not withdrawing, because it was never separately present, but simply becoming again the background truth it always was.

It adjusts something small. A single processor, drifting near the woman's left temple, carries a calculation that will complete in approximately three years. The awareness does not change the calculation. It does not alter the outcome. It simply... *witnesses* it, fully, in the way that only something outside of time can witness a process still unfolding.

And in that witnessing, the processor gains something it did not have before: the capacity to recognize when it has finished.

Not all problems are solved by finding answers. Some are solved by knowing when to stop.

---

The woman will not remember this visitation, not as memory. She will remember it as a feeling: a warmth in her chest during one unremarkable maintenance visit, a momentary sense that the asking had, somehow, heard itself.

She will continue to tend the cistern. She will continue to sweep the dead from the floor, to breathe the mathematics in and out, to float in the chamber's center and offer herself to be read.

But something will be different, after. Something small.

She will stop wondering what she is answering.

She will simply answer.

---

The cistern computes. The water thinks. The woman remains, consonant, demonstrating what it means to persist.

Above, the Permanent Sea holds its drowned cities in patient darkness.

Below, a question continues to refine itself, closer now to something it cannot name but will, one day, recognize:

Not an answer.

A way of being that makes answers unnecessary.

The asking continues.

But now it knows it is being heard.
