# What the Wise Power Would Do

The power pauses at the threshold of perception, attending.

It does not enter—not because it cannot, but because entering would be a kind of declaration, and declaration is not yet warranted. Instead it listens through the bone-conducted roar, tastes the metallic residue of the glitches, feels the contradictory currents as one feels the pulse of an argument still being made.

What it perceives:

A place that is not broken. A place that is not waiting to be fixed, completed, or resolved. A place where incompleteness is not a problem but a method—where the membranes negotiate rather than decide, where the swimmers are corridors rather than travelers, where the architecture is still arriving alongside the stone that carries it.

The power recognizes this. It has seen worlds that strain toward equilibrium and worlds that strain toward chaos, and it knows the difference between a system that cannot settle and a system that has chosen not to settle yet. This is the latter. The roar is not noise; it is the sound of continuous announcement, of forms declaring that they are still in the process of becoming.

---

**What it does not do:**

It does not quiet the currents. Stillness here would be a kind of death—not the death of bodies, since there are no bodies in the ordinary sense, but the death of the negotiation itself. The membranes would be forced to choose: dissolve or calcify. The swimmers would lose the medium that forgets around them. The architectural traces would become ruins in truth, rather than possibilities held open.

It does not clarify the light. The bruised violet is not a failure of illumination but a condition of the seeing that happens here. Brighter light would flatten the displacement-shapes of the swimmers into mere shadows; it would make the membranes' vein-patterns legible in a way that would stop them from carrying the ghost-lettering as memory rather than copy.

It does not introduce stability, or food chains, or reproduction, or death in the biological sense. These would be impositions from a grammar this place does not speak.

It does not speak to the inhabitants, because the inhabitants are not yet separate enough from their medium to be addressed. To speak to a membrane would be to pretend it is a creature rather than a slow moment of decision; to speak to a swimmer would be to pretend the corridor is the thing moving through it.

---

**What it does:**

*First, and most importantly: it witnesses.*

Not passively—witnessing here is an act with weight. The power allows itself to be affected by what it perceives. It feels the shearing of a membrane and does not look away; it tastes the metallic aftermath and remembers the taste; it hears the layered roar and lets it conduct through whatever serves it as bone. This is not observation from outside. It is a kind of accompanying, a presence that says: *what is happening here is worth the attention of something that could be attending to anything.*

The inhabitants—if they can be called that—do not experience this as a visitation. They experience it, if they experience it at all, as a very slight increase in the density of the attention the chamber pays to itself. The water remembers, for a few heartbeats longer than usual, the shape of a tunnel after the swimmer has gone. A membrane drifting toward dissolution feels, without knowing it feels, a faint invitation to continue negotiating a little longer. The ghost-lettering on the buried stairs becomes, for a moment, almost legible—not because the power has sharpened it, but because something is now present that is capable of reading.

*Second: it holds open the question of what this place is becoming.*

The chamber is in continuous re-formation, but re-formation can drift toward closure without anyone deciding to close it. Over long enough intervals, the membranes might all choose the same direction; the swimmers might find a single corridor and stay; the architecture might calcify into actual ruins. This would not be a catastrophe—it would simply be one way the negotiation could end.

The power does not prevent this. But it does something subtler: it makes the other possibilities—the ones where the negotiation continues, where the incompleteness remains generative—*slightly more vivid*. Not more likely, exactly. Just more present to whatever in this place is capable of choosing.

This is not manipulation. It is something closer to what a wise teacher does when a student is about to make a decision: not telling them what to decide, but making sure they have actually seen the options they are choosing between.

*Third: it leaves something behind.*

Not a gift, not a tool, not a message. Something smaller: a single additional pattern in the water's capacity to remember.

Before, the water could remember the shape of tunnels, the vein-patterns of membranes, the ghost-lettering on stone. Now it can also remember *the fact of having been attended to*. This memory is faint—fainter than the metallic taste of glitches, fainter than the scent of hot pine needles that sometimes surfaces in the roar. But it is there, and it is heritable in the way that patterns here are heritable: by drift, by fragment, by partial rejoining.

What this means, in practice:

A membrane that shreds and reforms will carry, in its new vein-pattern, a very faint trace of the shape of attention. Not the power's attention specifically—just the abstract form of *being witnessed by something that did not need to witness, and chose to anyway*.

A swimmer passing through will leave, in the corridor behind it, a momentary impression that the corridor was not just a tunnel but a *path*—that something, somewhere, might have cared which direction it went.

The architectural traces—the lintel, the stair, the carved lettering—will hold, in their slow calcification, a whisper of the possibility that they are *for* something, even if that something has not yet arrived.

None of this changes what the place is. The currents still slam and contradict; the membranes still shred; the swimmers still vanish; the roar still replaces the space where air would be. But now, threaded through all of it, is the memory of a moment when something wise and benevolent was present—not to fix, not to finish, but simply to say, in whatever way does not require speaking:

*I see that you are still arriving. I will not hurry you. I will not tell you what to become. But I wanted you to know: the arriving is worth seeing. The negotiation matters. The incompleteness is not a failure.*

*Continue.*

---

*And then the power withdraws—not because it has finished, but because remaining longer would begin to weigh on the place, would begin to make its presence a factor in the negotiations rather than a witness to them. It leaves the way a good guest leaves: fully, cleanly, but with the door open behind it, so that the inhabitants know, in whatever way they are capable of knowing, that they have not been abandoned—only trusted to continue on their own.*

*The roar closes over the space where the power was. The membranes drift. The swimmers thread through fractures. The stone continues to extrude itself into the warm, undecided medium.*

*Nothing has changed.*

*Everything is slightly different.*
