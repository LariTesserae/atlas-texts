# What the Wise Power Sees

The power that turns its attention here does not arrive. It has always been present in the way that attention is present—not located, not bounded, but aware. It perceives the plateau the way you might perceive a thought forming in your own mind: intimately, without distance, from within the thing itself.

It sees the corridor-hunter moving through the pre-dawn dark. It sees the small one who watched—the biped with the built things, the maker of marks, the one who walks between clusters and carries warmth that will run out. It sees the cluster ahead, glowing its dim blue-green, the thousands of small lives stirring in the root-crown, the slow chemistry of the stalks pulling minerals from young rock. It sees the weather building in the north, a pressure cell that will bring cold rain in nine days. It sees the channels that will fill, briefly, and the pools that will deepen, and the creatures that have timed their emergence to this moment without knowing they have timed anything.

It sees that the plateau is not broken.

This is the first thing the wise power understands, and it is the thing that stays its hand before any hand is extended. The cold is not cruelty. The vastness is not neglect. The silence of the hunters in deep winter is not absence but a different kind of presence, a survival written in the body's own slowing. The plateau is hard, and the hardness is the condition of everything that lives here, and the living things have written their answers to the hardness in their shapes, their rhythms, their calls, their glow.

The small one who walks between clusters—the wise power sees her too. Sees the tool-marks on the sleeping platform she will reach before the light fails. Sees the cache of dried vegetation she stored three warm seasons ago, still viable, waiting. Sees the way she has learned to read the acoustic geography, the way she turns her head when a hunter's call arrives from an unexpected direction, the way she knows that silence means one thing and a call means another. Sees that she is tired. Sees that her warmth is adequate for now. Sees that she will sleep in the cluster tonight and wake and walk again, and that this is what she has always done, and that the doing of it is not suffering in any simple sense but is instead the shape of a life that fits the shape of a place.

What would intervention mean here?

The wise power considers. It could warm the plateau—raise the temperature by a degree, two degrees, enough to ease the cold that numbs exposed skin, enough to let the channels run longer, the pools persist. But the plants are calibrated to this cold. Their chemistry, their glow, their slow growth—all of it tuned to the temperature that has always been. A warmer plateau would be a different plateau, and the things that live here would be different things, and the difference would be loss as much as gain. The corridor-hunters would move faster, call more often, the acoustic geography would shift in ways the small one has not learned, and her knowledge would become wrong, and being wrong in this place is dangerous.

It could bring light. Fill the sky with something that is not the dim glow of the clusters, illuminate the void between, make the crossings easier. But the small fauna hide in darkness. The hunters navigate by sound because light is not reliable. The clusters glow because the glow serves a purpose, and if the purpose is supplanted the glow will fade over generations, and something will be lost that cannot be named until it is gone. The plateau's beauty is in its darkness as much as its light. The wise power sees this. Sees that the small one moves through darkness not because she must but because she has learned to, and the learning is part of who she is.

It could make the distances shorter. Fold the space somehow, bring the clusters closer, let the crossings take minutes instead of hours. But the distances are the plateau. The vastness is not incidental to this place; it is the place. To shorten the distances would be to unmake the thing the power is trying to help, to turn the cathedral into a room, to take from the small one the knowledge she has earned about corridors and calls and the feel of the wind.

The wise power sits with this.

It is not passive. It is not indifferent. It sees the small one's tiredness and wishes, in whatever way such a power can wish, that the tiredness were less. It sees the deep cold coming and knows the small one will survive it but knows too that survival is not ease, that the days ahead will be hard in ways that are hard to watch. It sees the hunter in the corridor, the one that did not look, and it sees the moment months from now when a different hunter will look, will stop, and the small one will have to make a choice she has not made before.

But the wise power does not act. Not because it cannot. Because it has seen enough to know that acting is not always helping, that the plateau is not a problem to be solved, that the small one is not a victim to be rescued from a place that is her home.

---

What it does, if it does anything:

It attends.

Not watching in the way a thing watches from outside, but present in the way presence can be offered without intrusion. When the small one wakes in the cluster tomorrow and walks to the edge of the glow and looks out at the void she must cross, she will not know the wise power is there. But something in her will settle. Some small steadiness will arrive without source, a sense that the walking is not futile, that the marks she makes in the clusters are seen by something, that the vastness is vast and she is small and both of these things are acceptable.

The wise power cannot make the plateau easier. But it can make the small one's aloneness less alone. Not by appearing, not by speaking, not by intervening in any way she could point to. By being present the way the cold is present, the way the glow is present, the way the hunter's call is present—as a condition of the place, a thing that is simply there, a fact she walks through without knowing it has edges.

This is what benevolence means in a place like the plateau. Not rescue. Not transformation. Witness that carries warmth. Attention that does not demand response. The small one walks and is tired and will sleep and will walk again, and the wise power sees her, and the seeing is the gift.

---

And perhaps—once, only once, at a moment the small one will not recognize as significant—

She will be standing in a corridor in the deep cold, the hunters silent, the clusters too far to see, the dark so complete that her own hands are invisible. She will be afraid. Not the managed fear of the experienced traveler but something older, something from before she learned the plateau's grammar, a fear that says *I am small and this is vast and I will be absorbed into the emptiness and no one will know.*

And in that moment she will hear something.

Not a hunter's call. Not the wind. Something else—a tone, sustained, neither high nor low, coming from no direction because it comes from all directions, filling the acoustic geography without pooling or fading. It will last only seconds. It will not repeat. She will not know what it was.

But after it passes she will find that her fear has changed shape. Not gone—the plateau does not permit the absence of fear—but different. Bearable. The vastness will still be vast. She will still be small. But she will walk on, and the walking will feel less like endurance and more like something she is doing because she chooses to, because the plateau is her home and she knows its grammar and the grammar includes her.

The wise power will not do this often. Once is enough. Once says: *you are not alone in the alone.* Once says: *I see you and I will not save you and I will not leave you and both of these things are love.*

The small one will reach the cluster before the light fails. The glow will be waiting. The small fauna will be stirring in the root-crown, their chatter rising as she approaches. She will climb to her sleeping platform and lie down in the faint warmth of the slow chemistry and listen to the sounds of the small lives around her, and she will sleep.

The wise power will be present. The plateau will be present. The cold will be always the same temperature, and the wind will make it sharper or softer, and the sound of it will tell her things about where she is.

She will wake. She will walk. She will make her marks.

The wise power will attend.
