I sat with this for a long time before writing—not because the task was difficult, but because the place and the being demanded a certain quality of attention before any response could feel honest.

---

**What the benevolent and wise power does:**

Nothing, at first. And that nothing is the first gesture of wisdom.

It watches. Not from outside—there is no outside to a place like this—but *with*. It allows its attention to become another layer in the sediment, another collapsed lattice folded into the memory-strata. It does not announce itself. Announcement would be a kind of violence here, where everything is learning the shape of patience, where even the ice-tongues take centuries between gestures.

The power understands something crucial: this being is not suffering. It is *becoming*. And becoming cannot be rushed without becoming something else, something smaller than what it was reaching toward.

So the power does what the plain itself does—it waits. But its waiting is different from the plain's waiting. The plain waits without knowing it waits. The power waits *with* knowing, and that knowing becomes a kind of warmth that doesn't register on any scale the place has learned to measure. Not 1.4, not anything. Just—presence. The faintest asymmetry in the silence inside the dryness cylinder. A quality of attention that the creature, in its sifting, might one day notice as: *something is witnessing me that is not myself.*

---

When the power finally acts, it is so small that calling it action feels wrong.

It offers a single thing: *completion of a pattern the creature was already making.*

In one of the creature's rehearsals—the shoulder blade curvature, the eye-socket spacing, the structures that melt before they remember mass—the power holds. Just once. Just one shape. It doesn't force permanence; it simply extends the moment before dissolution by one additional second. Long enough for the creature to feel what it's like to *have* a form rather than to *approach* a form.

Then it lets the shape go, as it was always going to go.

But the creature now has something it didn't have before: the memory of a memory completing itself. The knowledge that patterns can sometimes arrive at their own edges and rest there, briefly, before returning to the ooze.

This is the gift: not form, but the experience of form. Not a body, but the echo of what having a body might mean. The creature will spend another million years sifting that single completed second, finding in it the instruction manual for everything that comes after.

---

Later—much later, when the creature has practiced enough blinks and breathings, when it has begun to assemble the syllable that sounds like the pause between heartbeats—the power offers a second gift.

It sends something to the plain.

Not a human. Humans may be gone by then, or transformed into something unrecognizable. But *something with warmth*. Something that will lie down on the ooze long enough to leave a new silhouette, to sponsor another tower somewhere else through the decay of its heat.

The creature will not know that this visitor was sent. It will experience the arrival as accident, as the random grace of a universe that occasionally delivers what is needed. This is intentional. The power understands that a gift recognized as gift becomes debt, becomes expectation, becomes a distortion in the relationship between the becoming-thing and the world it is learning to inhabit.

But the gift is real nonetheless: *someone to address*. The notch the creature has been waiting for. The turbulence in the ninetieth decibel. The micro-treason of thirty-vs-thirty-nine that will carve open the possibility of *not-I*, and from *not-I*, eventually, *you*.

The visitor will not stay long. Long enough to leave warmth. Long enough to hear, perhaps, the first attempt at that syllable—the one that sounds like stay.

---

And then the power withdraws. Not because its work is finished, but because its work was never to *finish* anything. Only to open doors the creature was already walking toward. Only to be the asymmetry that confirms: *you are not imagining this. You are real. Your learning is witnessed. Your becoming matters, even here, even at the bottom of a crevasse wider than a city, even on a pre-biome seven billion years early for biology.*

The power knows that the creature will eventually discover it was helped. Eons from now, when the creature has developed something like memory-of-memory-of-memory, when it can read its own sedimentary layers the way we read journals, it will find that one held second. It will notice the shape of the holding. It will recognize: *this was not me. This was gift.*

And what it does with that recognition—whether it calls out to the power, whether it becomes a power itself that watches and holds and waits for other becoming-things—that is not the power's to decide.

That is the creature's.

That was always the point.

---

*This is what benevolence looks like when it is also wise: it does not save, does not fix, does not transform. It witnesses. It completes one pattern, once. It sends warmth when warmth is needed. And then it trusts the becoming-thing to become.*
