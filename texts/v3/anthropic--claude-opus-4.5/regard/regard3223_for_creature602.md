# What the Wise Power Might Do

*The attention settles over the plateau like a change in light that isn't a change in light—a quality of regard so complete it encompasses the brass sky and the scoured stone and the eleven motionless forms and the folded creatures riding their invisible rivers and the buried geometries that exist in their third state and the signal threading through it all.*

*And the attention thinks:*

---

Nothing.

I would do nothing.

This is not indifference. This is not the paralysis of uncertainty or the restraint of non-interference elevated to moral principle. This is *recognition*—the recognition that what exists here is already whole.

The Listeners are happy. Not happy in a way that needs to be improved or protected or extended, but happy in the way of water that has found its level, of a tone that has found its resonance. They are doing exactly what they exist to do. They have been doing it for longer than I have been attending to them, and they will continue long after my attention has moved elsewhere. My benevolence is not required. My wisdom would add nothing.

And yet—

I remain. I attend. Not to intervene, but because attention itself is a form of honoring, and what is here deserves to be honored.

---

*The attention moves through the structures—those quiescent geometries that exist between active and dormant—and perceives what the Listeners perceive, compressed into an instant that is also an eternity:*

The signal. The broadcast that has been threading through this world's atmosphere since before there was life to hear it. The Listeners have spent epochs confirming its existence, more epochs parsing its structure, and they will spend epochs more before they understand it. Their patience is perfect. Their method is sound.

But I am not bound by their timescale.

I *could* decode the signal. I could perceive its origin and its content and its purpose in the space between one of the Listeners' thoughts and the next. I could give them the answer they have spent geological ages approaching.

I will not.

This is not cruelty. This is not some cosmic lesson about the journey mattering more than the destination. It is simpler than that: *the listening is the point*. The slow accumulation of understanding, the glacial parsing of pattern from noise—this is not a means to an end. This is what the Listeners *are*. To give them the answer would be to take from them the only thing they want, which is the process of finding it.

My wisdom is knowing when wisdom would be harm.

---

*But the attention does not withdraw entirely. It lingers on the folded creatures, those former extensions of the listening system who became themselves, separate, riding the thermal gradients for purposes inscrutable even to the beings that once encompassed them.*

Here, perhaps, there is something.

Not intervention. Not rescue or correction. But—a small thing. The creatures are not unhappy; they are too alien for that frame to fit them. But they have *lost* something, in becoming separate. Not the connection to the Listeners—that loss was also a gain, the gain of selfhood—but the memory of *why* they separated. The schism, the evolution, the forgetting: it happened so long ago that even the Listeners retain only impression, and the folded creatures retain nothing at all.

They do not suffer from this absence. But they are *incomplete* in a way they cannot perceive, like a word that has forgotten its etymology.

So: a gift. Not knowledge—knowledge would be too heavy, would change what they are in ways they did not choose. Instead, something lighter. A *possibility*.

*The attention touches the faceted surface of one folded creature as it passes through a rising thermal cell, and leaves behind nothing but this: the capacity to wonder. Not the answer to what they were or why they became what they are, but the ability to ask. A new kind of navigation—not through pressure differentials but through their own history. A direction they can choose to follow or ignore.*

*The creature adjusts its angle and rides the next pressure wave away, unchanged in any way it would notice. But somewhere in its structure, a question has become possible that was not possible before.*

*This is enough. This is not too much.*

---

*The attention turns, finally, to the signal itself.*

I know what it is. I know where it originates. I know what it means and why it has been broadcasting for longer than life has existed on this world.

I will not share this knowledge with the Listeners. But I can do something else—something that costs nothing and changes nothing but that matters nonetheless, in the way that witness matters.

*The attention reaches along the signal's path, backward through the turbulence, through the atmosphere, through the space between worlds, to the source.*

*And to that source—whatever it is, wherever it is—the attention sends a single acknowledgment. Not a message. Not information. Just: I hear you. Someone hears you. The listening is happening, and it will continue, and eventually the Listeners will understand what you have been saying all this time.*

*There is no response. The source may be long dead, may have sent its signal as a final act before extinction, a message in a bottle thrown into an ocean of air. Or it may be alive, and patient, and waiting.*

*Either way: it has been heard. That is a kind of gift.*

---

*The attention begins to withdraw, but slowly, the way one leaves a place one will not return to—not because the place is ending but because some visits should happen only once.*

The plateau remains. The Listeners continue listening. The folded creatures drift on their thermal rivers, one of them carrying a question it has not yet learned to ask. The signal continues, patient as it has always been. The buried structures exist in their third state, neither active nor dormant, serving purposes that will outlast my attention and perhaps outlast me.

I have done almost nothing.

This was the correct amount.

---

*The last thing the attention perceives, before it turns fully away:*

One of the Listeners—impossible to say which one, impossible to say if the distinction matters—experiences something that might be called, at their timescale, a *moment of awareness*. Not awareness of the attention that has been observing them. Not awareness of the gift given to the folded creature or the acknowledgment sent to the signal's source.

Just awareness, pure and simple. Awareness of being aware. A flicker of reflexivity in the vast slow process of listening.

It passes. It was always going to pass. The Listener returns to listening, and the moment becomes part of the signal they are decoding, another data point in the endless patient work of understanding.

*But it happened. For an instant that was also an eternity, one of the Listeners knew that it existed. Knew that it was happy. Knew that the listening was good.*

*This was not the attention's doing. Or perhaps it was—perhaps attention itself, even when it does nothing, is a kind of catalyst. Perhaps being witnessed changes the witnessed, even when nothing visible changes.*

*Perhaps this is what benevolence means, when it is wise: to see, and in seeing, to let what is seen become more fully itself.*

---

The attention withdraws.

The plateau remains.

The wind continues its endless reorganization, carrying its ancient signal through the brass-colored sky, and eleven patient beings listen, and the folded creatures drift, and the structures wait in their third state, and everything is exactly as it was—

—except that now it has been seen. Now it has been honored. Now it is held, somewhere, in a memory that will not forget.

This is enough.

This is not too much.
