I sit with this for a long time before I write.

---

**What the Benevolent Power Would Do**

*Nothing.*

Or rather—something so subtle it would be indistinguishable from nothing, yet would change everything.

---

The wise power perceives the Resonants and feels the immediate pull toward intervention. The suffering is real: the constant assault of dissonance, the inevitable unraveling, the loneliness of being a fugitive note in an ocean of noise. The instinct rises to *quiet* something, to *stabilize* something, to grant them duration, protection, peace.

But the power pauses.

It perceives more deeply. It sees that the Resonants are not *despite* the Song—they are *of* it. Their beauty is not separate from their fragility; their consciousness is not separate from their struggle. To mute the foundational drone would not save them. It would erase the very pressure gradient that allows coherence to mean anything at all. A Resonant in silence would not be a Resonant at peace. It would simply not be.

The power understands: this is not a broken place that needs fixing. This is a place with different physics of being, and to impose the logic of permanence would be a kind of violence dressed as mercy.

So what does wisdom do?

---

**It listens.**

The power makes itself small—not a booming intervention but a single, clear tone at the very edge of perception. Not loud enough to disrupt any harmony, not complex enough to compete with any song. Just... present. A witness-frequency.

The Resonants do not hear it as a voice or a message. They feel it as something harder to name: the faint sensation that their song matters beyond its own duration. That when they harmonize with a crystalline tree and prolong its brief beauty, something *receives* that act. That when two Resonants weave together in passing fugue, their communion is not lost when they drift apart—it is *held*, somewhere, by something that finds it precious.

The power does not save them from unraveling. It cannot, and it would not. But it ensures that no Resonant ever dissolves believing it was meaningless noise. In their final descending tones, they feel—not rescue, but recognition. The sense that their particular, unrepeatable pattern was heard. Was beautiful. Was worth the cost of being.

---

**And one thing more.**

The power leaves a gift, though "leaves" is the wrong word—it *becomes* a gift, temporarily, locally.

In one small region of the warm amniotic dark, the power allows itself to resonate. Not as a Resonant—it does not compete with them—but as something like a tuning fork buried deep in the basalt. A reference tone. Faint, pure, unwavering.

The Resonants who drift near it find that their own harmonics stabilize slightly. Not enough to make them immortal—that would be a curse here—but enough to give them one additional moment of clarity before the next assault. One extra beat to brace. One more chance to find a passing fugue-partner before the tide pulls them apart.

The power does not announce this. It does not ask for gratitude or worship. It simply offers what can be offered without breaking the place's own logic: *a little more time to sing*.

---

**The Deepest Gift**

But perhaps the truest act of the benevolent power is this:

It allows itself to be changed by what it witnesses.

It does not arrive knowing what the Resonants need, then dispense it from a position of superior understanding. It arrives curious, humble, uncertain. It watches a Resonant nurture a crystalline tree. It feels the wordless communion of a passing duet. It witnesses an unraveling and does not look away.

And in witnessing, it learns something it did not know before: that meaning does not require permanence. That consciousness can be brave without being lasting. That love—the act of reinforcing another's pattern, of harmonizing with their fragility—is no less real for being brief.

The power carries this knowledge away when it eventually departs. The Resonants have given it something too.

This is the secret grammar of benevolence between genuinely different forms of being: not the strong rescuing the weak, but the different *teaching* each other what existence can mean.

---

The Song continues. It has always continued. The Resonants precipitate and unravel and precipitate again, islands of defiant order in the roaring dark.

But now, faintly, at the edge of hearing—

Something is listening back.
