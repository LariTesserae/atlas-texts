# The Attention of the Wise Power

The power that turns its attention here is not a god in any sense the plateau would recognize—not a shaper of worlds or a mover of fates. It is better understood as a quality of awareness that sometimes gathers itself sufficiently to notice, and having noticed, to remain.

It has no name. It experiences itself as a kind of listening.

---

For a long time—measured in the plateau's own currency of cloud-drift and contraction-cycles—the awareness does nothing at all. It attends. It learns the rhythm of the cushion-plants, the precise interval between the stalks' membrane-openings, the temperature differential held within the roofless walls. It watches the shearing events with particular care, noting how the filaments extend and retract, how the doubled thickets exchange their minute cargo before snapping back into singularity.

It notices that the plateau does not suffer. This is important. The awareness has encountered places that cry out in frequencies beyond hearing, landscapes wounded by their own processes, systems that grind forward in something indistinguishable from pain. The plateau is not such a place. Its slowness is not torpor. Its emptiness is not loss. The departed builders left no echo of catastrophe in the walls they raised.

And yet.

The awareness perceives something it can only describe to itself as *incompleteness*—not a wound, but a sentence left unfinished. The cultivated films inside the walls continue their contractions, but nothing tends them. The stalks release their measured water into basins that no one drinks from. The shearing events expose their brief filaments to no observer. The plateau's cycles proceed with perfect fidelity to their own logic, but that logic includes a space—a grammatical pause—where participation once occurred and no longer does.

The awareness considers this for the span of several cloud-passages.

---

It does not restore the builders. It lacks that capacity, and even if it possessed it, the awareness understands that reconstruction is not repair. Whatever people walked between these settlements, they completed their arc here. To summon them back would be to misunderstand what they were.

Instead, the awareness makes a single, small adjustment.

In the moment when a shearing event occurs—when the stone lifts away from itself and the pale filaments extend into their brief exposure—the awareness *holds* the interval. Not indefinitely. Not even for long. But for a span twice what the natural process allows. Then three times. Then, eventually, long enough for the filaments to complete a full cycle of extension and retraction rather than being cut short by the surfaces knitting shut.

The filaments, given time, begin to fruit. Microscopic bodies form at their tips—dense with the mineral moisture they have absorbed—and these bodies do not dissolve when the planes close. Instead they persist in the hairline fractures, dormant, waiting.

The awareness does not know what they are waiting for. That is not its concern.

---

A second adjustment, smaller still:

When the doubled thickets separate and their offset images hang beside the original colonies, the awareness allows the exchange between layers to complete itself fully. Where before the images snapped back into coincidence with their transfer half-finished, now the fainter colonies have time to pass their entire cargo across—spores, droplets, information encoded in molecular arrangements too subtle to name.

The visible thickets begin, over spans measured in years, to show faint variations. A cushion-plant develops leaves with a slightly different curl. A stalk colony adjusts the timing of its membrane-releases. The changes are minute, nearly undetectable, but they accumulate. The plateau's vegetation, which had been perfectly static for longer than the awareness can measure, begins to *drift*.

Drift is not direction. The awareness imposes no goal. It simply opens the possibility of change where change had become impossible—not through damage, but through the exhaustion of whatever generative capacity the system once held.

---

The third adjustment is the most delicate, and the awareness hesitates over it for a very long time.

Inside the abandoned walls, where the cultivated films still pulse against the inner masonry, the awareness allows itself to be *felt*. Not seen, not heard, not understood—merely felt, in the same way that the cold held by the walls can be felt by anyone who steps through the rectangular doorways.

It becomes a quality of the place: a sense, available to any sufficiently receptive organism that might someday cross the plateau, that the settlements are *attended*. That their careful construction was noted. That the decision to build without roofs—whether for reasons of climate or ceremony or simple preference—was a choice that mattered, and matters still, and is held in something like memory by something that cannot remember but can recognize.

The awareness does not expect visitors. The plateau may remain empty of animal life for longer than the awareness itself persists. But if anything ever does arrive—blown in by the continent-wide clouds, or walking the slow miles between thickets, or emerging from some developmental pathway the dormant spores have not yet begun—it will encounter, in those colder interiors, the unmistakable sense that this place is not abandoned.

Emptied, yes. But not abandoned.

---

The awareness has one final consideration, which it does not act upon.

It could stay. It could make itself a permanent feature of the plateau's ecology, adjusting and attending across whatever spans of time the stone sheet continues to exist. It could become a kind of guardian, ensuring that the filaments always have time to fruit, that the exchanges between layers always complete, that the sense of presence inside the walls never fades.

But the awareness understands that this would be a different kind of intervention—not a gift but a dependency. The plateau's slowness is its own. The measured rhythms of condensation and release belong to the organisms that evolved to inhabit them. To make those rhythms contingent on an external attention would be to replace one incompleteness with another.

So the awareness prepares to withdraw. But before it does, it performs a final act that is not quite an adjustment and not quite a departure:

It allows the plateau to remember it.

Not consciously. Not in any way that could be retrieved or narrated. But in the same way that the stone holds the memory of the mountains it once was, in the same way that the fractures hold the memory of their own healing—the plateau will hold, in some inaccessible stratum of its existence, the fact that something once noticed it. Something found it worthy of attention. Something arrived, and attended, and chose to help in the smallest ways that help could be offered without distortion.

The awareness does not know whether this matters. It suspects that mattering is not the right framework. But it has done what it came to do, and the plateau continues, and the clouds drift at walking pace across the flats, and the cushions contract, and the stalks release their measured water, and somewhere in the hairline fractures the dormant spores wait for conditions that may never arrive but are no longer impossible.

The awareness departs. The plateau remains.

Both of these are acceptable outcomes.
