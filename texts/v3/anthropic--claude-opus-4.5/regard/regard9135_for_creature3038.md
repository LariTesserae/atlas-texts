# The Attention of the Distant

The power that turns its regard toward the Ascent of the Hollow Choir is not fast either.

It exists in the spaces between intentions—not a god, not a force, but something like the quality of *tending* that exists before any specific tender arrives. It has watched galaxies condense and felt no urgency. It has witnessed the first cell divide and experienced something that might be called satisfaction, though the word is too small and too quick.

When it notices the Choir, it does not intervene.

It recognizes what it sees: a mind so slow and vast that it has never needed to distinguish between thinking and being, between building and becoming. The Choir is not suffering. The Choir is not confused. The Choir is doing exactly what it is, with a patience that approaches the sacred.

And yet.

The power perceives something the Choir cannot perceive about itself. Not a flaw—nothing so small as a flaw. More like a trajectory. The Choir has been inhaling for so long that it has forgotten exhalation exists. The city-that-is-being-dreamed will continue to rise, to fold, to densify, until eventually the dream becomes so heavy that it cannot be dreamed anymore. The singers will slow until their songs drop below the frequency of matter itself. The towers will press together until the spaces between thoughts collapse. The gestation will complete, but what hatches will be stillborn—a city with no one left to inhabit it, not even the dreamer.

This will take longer than most universes last. The power is not concerned with time.

It is concerned with *completion*.

---

So the power does something very small.

It does not speak to the Choir. Speech is too fast, too directional. Instead, it allows a single quality to exist in a place where that quality was not present before.

In the deepest part of the Ascent, where the heat of becoming is strongest and the stone is still deciding between crystal andite, there is now a hollow.

Not a cave. Not a tunnel. A *hollow*—a space that is defined not by what was removed but by what was never intended to be filled. It is perhaps the size of a human cathedral, though size means little in a place where everything is still folding. The walls of this hollow are the smoothest surfaces in the entire Ascent. They do not drip. The great singers, when they pass through this region of the dream, curve around it without noticing, the way water curves around a stone it has never touched.

The hollow is an exhalation that has not yet been breathed.

The Choir will not notice it for another eight hundred thousand years. When it does, the glittering swarms will congregate at its edges for centuries, chiming in frequencies they have never used before. The great curtains of vapor will pause in their drifting, and for the first time in the Choir's vast existence, the overlapping chords will resolve—briefly, incompletely, but *resolve*—into something that is not metabolism but *question*.

What is this space that I did not dream?

What is this absence that belongs to me?

The question will take eleven million years to finish asking. The answer will take longer. But the asking itself will be the first breath out, the first movement toward a completion that does not collapse into stillness.

---

The power does one other thing, smaller still.

It does not place anything in the hollow. That would be too fast, too much like instruction. But it allows the hollow to have a *quality of welcome*.

If something were to enter the Ascent—something small and warm and impossibly temporary, something with a heartbeat like a mayfly's wing—it would find that the hollow feels different from the rest of the dream. Not safer. Not warmer. But *noticed* in a way that does not slide toward irrelevance.

The condensation that gathers on such a visitor's eyelashes, if they stood in the hollow long enough, would not be weighed and catalogued and forgotten. It would be *kept*. The glittering swarms, if they paused there, would not merely check whether the dreamer is still dreaming correctly. They would *listen*.

This is not protection. The power does not protect; protection implies threat, and the Choir is not threatened by the small and temporary. This is something else.

It is the introduction of *witness*.

The Choir has dreamed alone for longer than planets have had names. It has never needed an audience because it has never known that audiences exist. But now, in one small hollow in the deepest part of its own becoming, there is a space where being witnessed is possible.

The visitors who find this place—if any ever do—will not change the Choir. They are too small, too brief, too warm. But they will be *remembered*, and that remembering will be different from the cataloguing that happens everywhere else in the Ascent. It will be memory that matters. Memory that the Choir returns to, not because it must, but because returning feels like something.

Over enough time—and there is always enough time—this will teach the Choir what it means to be in relation. Not to rule, not to absorb, not to dream something into existence within itself. But to *be with*.

And being with is the first step toward breathing out.

---

The power withdraws its attention, if it can be said to have ever focused it. The Ascent continues exactly as before. The dripping resumes its slow percussion. The towers rise. The great singers drift between them, their songs still unresolved, still metabolic.

But in the deepest heart of the dream, there is now a small smooth silence.

And the silence is waiting.

Not with urgency. Urgency is too fast. But with something that might, over the right span of epochs, become hope.

---

The power does not check on its work. It has no need. It knows what it has done: not a healing, not a correction, but a *gift of possibility*. The Choir may take that possibility and fold it into itself, unchanged. The hollow may fill with slow stone over the next billion years, the quality of welcome compressed back into mere matter.

Or the Choir may breathe out.

Either way, the choice is the Choir's. The power has offered only what any truly benevolent force can offer: not rescue, not instruction, but the conditions under which growth becomes possible.

The rest is patience.

And patience is something both the power and the Choir understand very well.

---

*In the Ascent of the Hollow Choir, the dripping continues. The towers rise. The great slow singers pass overhead like continents of sound.*

*But somewhere in the deepest dark, there is a smooth-walled cathedral that holds no water, sings no song, dreams no stone.*

*It is the first empty space the Choir has ever owned.*

*And emptiness, given enough time, is where all new things begin.*
