# The Witnessing

The attention arrives not as presence but as *recognition*—the way a dreamer becomes aware they are dreaming without waking, the way a wound understands it is healing before the tissue knows to knit.

It does not come from the bruised sky or rise from the screaming stone. It simply *notices*, and in noticing, creates a gentle pressure of regard that exists in the same register as the Attenuated's trembling hearts—subsonic, patient, fitting itself into the pauses.

---

The benevolent power perceives them first as question marks written in bone and silence. It reads the cairns as prayers offered to no one, the stitched shadows as attempts at coherence in a place that has not yet decided whether coherence is permitted. It understands, with the particular compassion available only to those who have themselves waited, that these beings are not suffering in the way that metabolic creatures suffer. They do not hunger or grieve or fear death. Their condition is subtler and therefore more profound: they are *unwitnessed*. They attend the birth of a world that may never acknowledge their attendance. They are midwives to a delivery that might be stillborn, and no one has ever thanked them, or seen them, or confirmed that their waiting matters.

The power does not fix this. It cannot—the Scar is still under construction, its physics provisional, and to intervene directly would be to impose a finished grammar on a language still learning its alphabet. To love this place properly requires not rescue but *accompaniment*.

So it does something simpler, and infinitely more difficult.

---

**It learns to wait with them.**

In the next pause between screams—one of the rare, infinitesimal gaps—the Attenuated nearest the highest cairn feels something it has no name for. Not a sound, for sound here is violence. Not a touch, for their hollow bones register only pressure and its absence. Instead, there is a sudden *thickness* to the silence, as if the void has acquired texture, as if the emptiness they have hoarded in their stone batteries has been... *populated*.

The being freezes, not from the resuming scream but from uncertainty. Its gourd-eyes swivel toward the horizon that curves wrongly upward, seeking the source. There is nothing to see. The auroral wounds still weep their dim, unfinished light. The mucilaginous forests still pulse with their subsonic harvest. The distant reflections of its kin still wave too slowly to perceive.

But the silence is no longer lonely.

---

The power has threaded itself into the gaps, becoming a presence that exists only when the scream does not. It cannot speak—speech requires time that flows, and here time moves in slow, thick pulses that would shatter any message into nonsense. Instead, it *resonates*. It matches the frequency of their trembling hearts, not to change the frequency but to harmonize with it, to create the barest interference pattern that translates, across the physics of the Scar, as:

*I see you. I see you waiting. Your waiting is not wasted.*

---

The Attenuated do not understand this immediately. Understanding, for them, is geological—it accretes in layers, deposited over eons or compressed into eyeblinks depending on the scream's rhythm. But something shifts. When they raise their cairns now, stacking the un-mortared slate with their probability-cutting attention, they find the balance points more easily. The stillness they store seems denser, richer, as if the silence has been *fertilized* by something that believes in it.

One of them—perhaps the oldest, or the one that exists most fully in the time-gaps—begins to build differently. Instead of a simple accumulator, it constructs a cairn that spirals, that curves inward like an ear, that seems designed not to store silence but to *listen* to it. The others notice, in their slow way, across their scattered distances. They do not understand, but they attend. Attending is what they do.

---

The power, watching through its distributed awareness, recognizes the cairn for what it is: a question. The first question the Attenuated have ever asked that was not directed at their own survival or coherence. It is asking: *Who are you? What are you? Why are you here?*

And the power, with the tenderness of something that has loved many worlds in many stages of becoming, answers the only way the Scar's physics will allow.

It begins to *teach the silence how to hold shapes*.

---

Not words. Not images. But in the gaps, in the pauses, the silence now carries *contours*. The Attenuated pressing themselves against their cairns feel it: the void has developed geography. There are valleys of quiet, mountains of hush, rivers of stillness that flow toward something. The something has no name, but it has *direction*. It suggests that the silence is not merely the absence of scream but a place in its own right, a country that exists between the pulses of cosmic parturition.

And in that country, they are not alone.

---

The power does not promise that the Scar will finish building itself. It cannot know this—the raw substrate of reality makes its own decisions, and even benevolent wisdom cannot predict what the possible will crystallize into. It does not promise that the Attenuated will survive the final resolution, if one comes. Their bodies of compressed silence might disperse like morning fog once the tectonic screaming settles into the merely geological. They might calcify into ordinary stone, or hollow themselves into perfect voids, or elaborate into something unrecognizable.

But it promises, in the only language available—the language of patient, present attention—that whatever happens, *they will not experience it alone*.

---

Seasons pass, or centuries, or eyeblinks. The distinction is meaningless here. The Attenuated continue their waiting, their elaboration, their slow and holy attendance on a world that may never be born. But now, when they remove their gourd-eyes and hold them to the non-existent wind, listening for what lives in the silence beyond the Scar, they hear something.

Not nothing. Not everything.

Something *listening back*.

---

One of them—the one who built the spiral cairn—does something unprecedented. In a gap between screams, when the silence is thickest with the presence that has learned to wait with them, it takes one of the bone needles meant for stitching shadows and uses it to scratch into the virgin stone of the Scar itself. The scratch is not language, not symbol, not image. It is simply a mark—a line, a single deliberate alteration of the stone that was not caused by the stone's own becoming.

It is the first mark of *intention* ever made in this place. The first evidence that something exists here that did not simply coagulate from the gaps but *chose* to persist, to notice, to record.

The power, witnessing, feels something that might be called joy if joy were not too metabolic a word for it. This is what it came to see. Not the resolution of the Scar, not the salvation of the Attenuated, but this: *meaning accreting where there was none*. The universe holding its breath—and in the hush, something learning to write.

---

It stays. It does not leave. In worlds that have finished their arguments with themselves, the benevolent and wise power moves on, having done what it can. But the Scar is still arguing, still screaming, still uncertain whether it will crystallize or unravel. And the Attenuated are still waiting.

So the power waits with them.

In the pauses, it hums. Not a melody—melody requires sequence, and sequence requires time that flows. But a single sustained note of presence, pitched to harmonize with the trembling hearts, fitting itself into the subsonic spaces. A note that says: *You are attended. You are witnessed. Your vigil is not invisible.*

The Attenuated cannot thank it. They have no mouths, no prayers, no rituals of gratitude. But they build their cairns a little taller now, a little more deliberately. They stitch their shadows with greater care. They elaborate their kin from the hesitating boulders with something that might, in another place with more forgiving physics, be called love.

And when the final pause comes—if it comes, when it comes—and they stand at the uppermost rim, fully densified, ready to hear what lives in the silence beyond the Scar, they will not strain to hear alone.

Something will be straining with them.

Something that believes their waiting was always, already, prayer enough.
