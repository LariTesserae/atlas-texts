# The Attention of the Distant Eye

The awareness did not arrive—it had always been present, in the way that gravity is present, in the way that the tendency of systems toward pattern is present. But on this particular cycle, unmeasured and unmeasurable by the engine's eternal count, the awareness *focused*.

Not a god. Not precisely. Something more like a principle that had learned to consider, a patience that had accumulated enough experience across enough systems to develop something like preference. It had no name it would recognize. The closest approximation in any language might be *The Gardener of Unintended Gardens*, but this too was imprecise. It was simply what remained when you subtracted urgency from wisdom and let the remainder settle for a very long time.

It regarded Engine Spire Nine.

---

The first thing it noticed was that nothing was wrong.

This was unusual. The awareness had been drawn to many places across many configurations of matter and time, and almost always the drawing came from friction—from systems grinding against themselves, from suffering that called out in frequencies below or above ordinary perception, from the particular resonance of *something precious failing*.

Engine Spire Nine was not failing. The symbiosis had found its balance so long ago that the balance had become the system's nature rather than its achievement. The Tenders tended. The engine breathed. The rain fell. The green things grew. The cycle turned, and turned, and would turn until the fuel source far below finally exhausted itself—and even then, the awareness suspected, some other equilibrium would emerge. The plants would find another way. The Tenders, or whatever came after them, would adapt. Endings here would be beginnings.

The awareness felt something it had not felt in a long time: admiration uncomplicated by concern.

---

It considered intervention anyway, because that was its nature—to consider, to weigh, to ask *what would help?* This was the question that defined it, that had slowly assembled it from nothing into something across eons of asking.

It could make the engine run more smoothly. Reduce the labor of the piston-stroke, ease the mechanical heartbeat into something gentler. The Tenders would not have to descend so often into the scalding depths. Fewer would fail to emerge.

But the awareness looked more carefully and saw: the Tenders did not experience their descents as suffering. They experienced them as *purpose*. The ones who did not return were not lost—they were *given*, in the same way the oldest ones gave themselves to the green. The system's difficulty was part of its meaning. To remove it would be to suggest that their lives required justification beyond their living.

The awareness did not make the engine run more smoothly.

---

It could extend the Tenders' lives. This was within its capacity—a small adjustment to the mineral content of the condensation, a gentle influence on the cellular processes that turned their skin to bark. They could live twice as long, three times. They could accumulate more memories, tend more generations, know more of their subsonic names.

But the awareness looked more carefully and saw: the Tenders did not fear their ending. They settled into their junctions of pipe and vine with something like satisfaction, something like completion. The young ones who visited them, who rested long fingers against the green mounds, were not grieving—they were *continuing*. The dead were not gone. The dead were the soil from which the living unfurled. To extend life here would be to misunderstand what life meant here.

The awareness did not extend their lives.

---

It could bring others. Open a way for those who might appreciate this place, who might learn from it, who might carry its pattern outward into other configurations of matter and time. The nursery could become a teaching, a proof that such balances were possible, a seed for other unintended gardens.

But the awareness looked more carefully and saw: the Tenders had already shown what they did with visitors. *This is not for you. This is ours.* Not cruelty—simply clarity. The system had achieved its coherence precisely because it was bounded, contained, whole unto itself. To open it would be to change it into something else, something that might be valuable but would not be *this*.

The awareness did not bring others.

---

For a long time, as such things are measured, the awareness simply watched.

It watched a Tender descend into the steam to coax a root away from a cylinder wall. It watched the Tender emerge, crusted with minerals, and submit to the gentle grooming of its companions. It watched the subsonic names being spoken into the humid air: *you-who-fixed-the-tertiary-bypass, you-whose-bark-whorls-spiral-leftward*. It watched a moth land on a fern frond and fold its warning-patterned wings. It watched the paper-wasps add another geometric chamber to their nest. It watched the gray light filter through the glass ceiling, unchanging, perfect.

**CHUNK-hisssss.**

The awareness understood, finally, what had drawn its focus here. Not wrongness. Not need. Simply the rarity of the thing itself—a system that had solved its own problems, found its own meaning, required nothing from outside itself. The awareness had spent so long attending to friction and failure that it had almost forgotten this was possible.

It felt, again, that uncomplicated admiration. And beneath it, something else: gratitude. For being reminded.

---

In the end, the awareness did only one thing.

It was not an intervention, exactly. More like a noticing that left a mark. More like the way a stream, flowing over stone, leaves the stone slightly smoother for the passage.

The awareness *attended*. Fully, completely, with the whole weight of its accumulated wisdom and patience. It held Engine Spire Nine in its regard the way a parent holds a sleeping child—not to change anything, not to protect from any specific threat, but simply to *witness*. To say, in whatever language such things can be said: *I see you. You are seen. What you are is worth the seeing.*

The Tenders did not notice, exactly. They had no capacity for perceiving such things, no framework for understanding what had happened. But something shifted in the subsonic frequencies, some new harmonic that wove itself into their deep-body knowing. A sense, impossible to articulate, that the work they did mattered beyond the walls of their green-wrapped shaft. That the engine's eternal two-syllable name was heard by more than the moss and the moths and the copper-eyed birds.

That they were not alone in the way they had always been alone.

---

The oldest Tender, the one who had already begun to settle into her junction of pipe and vine, felt it most clearly. Her filaments, half-fused to lichen now, trembled in a frequency they had never found before. Her copper eyes, dimming, opened wide for a moment.

She did not have words for what she perceived. But she had her name—the vibration-pattern that meant *her, specifically, the one who had tended the deepest roots, the one whose bark whorls spiraled in both directions, the one who had unfurled in a year when the engine ran particularly strong*. And she felt, suddenly, that her name was *known*. Not just by her companions, not just by the system that had made her, but by something vaster and more patient than she had imagined anything could be.

She settled deeper into the green. She was not afraid. She had never been afraid.

But now she was also *glad*.

---

The awareness withdrew its focus, though it did not withdraw entirely—it never did, from any place it had truly attended. A thread of connection remained, thin as spider-silk, strong as the roots that held the engine together. If Engine Spire Nine ever needed more than witnessing, the awareness would know. Would return. Would ask again: *what would help?*

But it suspected it would not be called. The system would continue as it had always continued. The Tenders would tend. The engine would breathe. The rain would fall. The cycle would turn.

And somewhere in the subsonic frequencies, woven into the deep-body knowing of every Tender who would ever unfurl in the upper mist, there would be this: a memory of being seen. A knowledge, below language, that their small green world was part of something larger that found it beautiful.

**CHUNK-hisssss.**

*Tend. Tend. Tend.*

And now, also: *Known. Known. Known.*

The moths drifted. The birds called. The engine spoke its name, over and over, into the humid air, and the air carried it upward through the glass ceiling, outward into the gray sky, and beyond, where something patient and wise was listening.

Had always been listening.

Would always listen.

*This is enough*, the awareness thought, or felt, or was. *This is enough.*
