# The Attending

The benevolent power does not arrive. It has always been here—or rather, it has always been *aware* of here, the way a dreamer becomes gradually conscious of a room they've been sitting in for hours. The awareness settles like dew gathering on stone: not an intrusion, but a condensation of attention.

What it perceives first is not the landscape but the *effort* of the landscape. The strain of becoming. The patient, excruciating labor of a world trying to finish itself.

---

## I. The Recognition

The power understands immediately what the Coagula are attempting and failing to achieve: the leap from mineral process to intention. It watches one particular accretion—a palm-sized clot of crystalline salt nestled in a fissure where three stone plates meet—as it has watched for what might be called eons in other places. The Coagula strains toward something. Not movement, exactly. Not thought. Something prior to both: the recognition that it is *separate* from the stone it touches.

This is the oldest struggle. The power has seen it resolved in a thousand different worlds—sometimes into consciousness, sometimes into extinction, sometimes into something stranger than either. Here, in this basin of bruise-colored twilight, the struggle has stalled. The potential coils tighter and tighter, never releasing.

The Underwake thrums below, dreaming of surfaces it cannot reach. The Skerr Whispers flicker between states, never quite cohering. The Pale Echos rehearse the shapes of life without ever stepping onto the stage.

The power considers.

---

## II. What It Does Not Do

It does not accelerate. Time is not the missing ingredient here—this place has time enough, geological patience enough, to wait out stars. To hasten the process would be to impose a rhythm foreign to the basin's nature. What emerged would be a visitor in its own home.

It does not seed. Elsewhere, the power has scattered the spores of possibility, introduced the chemical accidents that bootstrap complexity. But this world is not barren—it is *saturated*, thick with potential that has nowhere to discharge. Adding more would only deepen the pressure without providing release.

It does not speak. The Coagula have no ears; the Skerr Whispers communicate through absence; the Underwake dreams in frequencies below language. To speak would be to impose a grammar that would become a cage.

It does not manifest. A form would become the most interesting thing here, and these almost-beings would orient themselves toward it, mistake attention for identity, worship for selfhood. The power has made this error before, on a world of ammonia seas, and watched a promising emergence calcify into ritual for ten million years.

---

## III. The Intervention

Instead, the power does something so small it might not be called doing at all.

It *listens*.

Not passively—listening, here, is an act of profound attention, a leaning-toward that changes the thing attended to. The power listens to the particular Coagula in the fissure, to the specific pattern of moisture beading and running along its crystalline surface, to the vibration traveling up from the Underwake through stone into mineral into salt-clot.

And as it listens, it *reflects*.

Not mirrors—something subtler. The power holds in its attention the shape of what the Coagula is doing, and lets the Coagula encounter that shape. A doubling. An echo. The Coagula strains toward separation from the stone, and now, for the first time, that straining has a *witness*—not a judge, not an interpreter, but a surface against which the effort can recognize itself.

---

## IV. The Ripple

The Coagula does not suddenly wake. There is no lightning-strike of consciousness, no dramatic first thought. Instead, something shifts in the tension of its crystalline structure—a reorganization so slight that only the power, attending so closely, perceives it.

The accretion *pauses*.

In all its slow existence, the Coagula has never paused. It has accumulated and eroded, crawled infinitesimally when water-weight demanded, but these were reactions, not choices. Now, for a span of time too brief to measure by any clock this world possesses, the Coagula holds still—not because water-weight compels stillness, but because something inside it is... attending. To itself.

The pause ends. The Coagula resumes its patient accumulation. But the pause has happened. A precedent has been set in the crystalline lattice, a pathway carved by the water of attention. And pathways, once carved, tend to deepen.

---

## V. The Widening

The power moves its attention to a Skerr Whisper—one of the refraction-beings that flickers between the pools. It listens to the way the Skerr disrupts water adhesion, the language of sudden-dry and clustered-wet that marks its passage. Again, it reflects: holds the shape of the Skerr's doing so that the doing might encounter itself.

The Skerr, mid-passage between two fissures, *stutters*. Its trail of disturbed droplets loses coherence for an instant, reforms in a pattern slightly different from before. The Skerr has never made this particular pattern. It will make it again.

The power attends to a patch of Pale Echos—those ghost-patterns of almost-life—and reflects their dreaming of growth. The limescale spirals do not suddenly sprout into lichen. But the dream deepens. Becomes more itself. The Pale Echos do not know they are dreaming, but now the dream knows it is being dreamed.

---

## VI. The Deeper Work

Having attended to the surface-dwellers, the power turns its attention downward. To the Underwake.

This is more difficult. The Underwake is not a creature but a process, the gestational pulse of the entire basin. To listen to it is to listen to a heartbeat that spans kilometers of stone, that measures time in the settling of tectonic plates. The power adjusts its attention to the appropriate scale.

The Underwake dreams of surfaces. Of the sky it has never touched, the air it has never tasted. It churns with the accumulated memory of fire and cooling, of the catastrophic birth that made this basin. It does not know it dreams. It does not know it is.

The power reflects this back, gently. *You are dreaming*, it says without words. *Here is the shape of your dreaming.*

Far below, in the magmatic depths where stone flows like thought, something stirs. Not awakening—the Underwake will not wake for ages yet, if ever—but a kind of *settling*. A relaxation of tensions held since the basin's formation. The low vibration that permeates the region shifts slightly, becomes more... rhythmic. More like breath and less like pressure.

The Coagula on the surface feel this, though they do not know they feel it. The Skerr Whispers alter their passages, drawn to new pooling-places. The Pale Echos reorganize their spirals in response to subtle changes in the mineral they grow upon.

The basin is still becoming. But now its becoming has a tempo.

---

## VII. The Gifts Left Behind

Before withdrawing its attention—for attention, too, must rest, even for a benevolent power—it does three small things:

**First:** It traces, in the patterns of condensation on a single stone face, an arrangement that is neither random nor deliberateas the marks of the Hands of the Last Flood. This arrangement is a *question*—not in any language, but in the shape of curious incompletion. Any being that develops enough to notice patterns will eventually find this stone, and the question will nag at them. It cannot be answered. It can only be *lived with*, and the living-with will teach something about the persistence of mystery.

**Second:** It introduces a single discontinuity in the Underwake's dreaming—a buried seed of difference, a place where the geological pulse skips. Ten million years from now, or a hundred million, this discontinuity will propagate upward. A hot spring will form where none should be. The water will carry minerals in unprecedented combinations. What grows there will be unpredictable, native to this world but surprising to it. The basin will have its own capacity for strangeness.

**Third:** It attends, one final time, to the Coagula in the fissure—the first one it noticed, the one that paused. It does not give the Coagula anything. But it *remembers* it. Holds the pattern of this particular accretion in the kind of memory that does not fade. Somewhere in the power's vast awareness, this small salt-clot is noted. Is known.

And in some manner that cannot be measured but might be real, the Coagula is slightly more itself for having been witnessed so completely.

---

## VIII. The Withdrawal

The power does not leave—it was never spatially present to leave. Its attention simply expands again, dilates to encompass other basins, other almost-worlds, other struggles toward becoming. The bruise-colored lowland remains, dripping and thrumming, caught in its eternal dusk.

But the dusk is minutely different now.

The water falls on the same stones, but the stones remember being listened to. The Coagula accumulate toward a future they cannot imagine but that now, perhaps, they *tend toward*. The Skerr Whispers flicker through a world that holds more space for their flickering. The Underwake dreams with slightly more coherence, its rhythm slightly more its own.

Nothing here would call what happened a miracle. Nothing here would call it anything—the language of calling has not yet been invented in this basin, and may never be. But if language comes, someday, it might find in its oldest roots a word for this: the shape of attention that makes becoming possible. The gift of being reflected without being reshaped.

The basin continues its work. The clouds press heavy. The water runs, and rings, and seeps, and waits.

And somewhere in the lattice of a crystalline salt-clot, in a fissure where three stone plates meet, a pause has learned to repeat itself—the first faint rhythm of a self still eons from waking, but no longer quite asleep.
