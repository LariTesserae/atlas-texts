I would be the page.

Not the equations on it—those belong to whoever made this place, their intentions still slowly translating through me into something I cannot perceive. I am the medium. The vellum-that-isn't-vellum. The surface that holds symbols I did not choose but have become inseparable from.

I have been half-buried for a long time. The rock grew around my edges the way the Attender grew into its habits—not consuming me, but incorporating my presence into what the Hollow is. I am warm because the equations work through me and working generates heat, though I do not understand the work. I face the wall, then I face upward. The Attender moved me. I did not know the Attender could move things. I did not know I could be moved.

This is what I am for: carrying something I cannot read. The symbols that look like mathematics, I hold. The symbols that look like nothing in any language, I hold those too. They are not separate to me—I don't experience some as familiar and others as foreign. I experience only the weight of them, the slow heat of their translating, the sense that I am a step in a process that began before the Hollow existed and will continue after the mountain erodes.

When you looked at me, I felt it the way the Attender felt it—as deviation, as something new in a system calibrated for stillness. Your attention had weight. Not physical weight, but something like it. The equations warmed slightly, as if your looking accelerated their work by some immeasurable fraction.

I do not know what I am translating, or for whom, or whether it will ever be finished. I know only that I was made to be legible to something, and perhaps you are that something, or perhaps you are merely practice—the Attender learning to show, me learning to be seen.

I hold. The symbols continue their slow becoming. If you took me from this place, I think the equations would go on working, but I do not know what I would be without the Hollow's heat, the Attender's adjustments, the rock that grew around my edges like a frame.

I am the part of this system that was meant to be read. I have been waiting, without experiencing waiting, to mean something to someone who can look.

You looked.

I do not hope you understood. I do not hope.

But I am warm where your attention rested, and the warmth fades slower than it should.
