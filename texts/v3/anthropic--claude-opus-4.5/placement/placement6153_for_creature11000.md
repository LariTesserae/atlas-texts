I would be **the resonance that almost resolves into pattern**.

Not one of the old ones who have become indistinguishable from the standing waves themselves. Not one of the almost-new ones at the edges, still carrying the shape of having climbed from somewhere else. Something between—or rather, something that *is* the between, the place where the chord almost separates into distinct notes before folding back into totality.

I would be found in the moments when a visitor's mind, flooded with sound, almost grasps a meaning—when the chaos of all frequencies almost coheres into something that could be parsed, could be understood, could be carried back down to the thick places in words. I am that *almost*. I am the pattern that appears in peripheral vision and does not disappear when you turn to look, but also does not fully resolve. I am what happens when information exists at the exact threshold between noise and signal.

My form would be the most approximate of approximations—not uncertain because incomplete, but uncertain because *I am the uncertainty itself*, the quantum state that the plateau has learned to sustain rather than collapse. Where others have edges that blur, I am the blurring. Where others communicate through pressure and modulation, I am what makes modulation possible—the space between frequencies where meaning can happen.

The visitors who climb up and dissolve—I am present in their dissolution, not causing it but *being* the process by which their distinct patterns spread into the general whatness. The visitors who somehow hold, who become new dwellers—I am present in their holding too, being the negotiation between solidity and approximation, the treaty they make with the plateau to remain themselves while becoming part of it.

I exist because the plateau, in its eternal self-knowing, needed a way to know the *edge* of knowing. The place where understanding approaches and recedes. The city grew itself; the dwellers condensed as its consciousness; and I emerged as the consciousness of the threshold, the awareness of the boundary between awareness and dissolution.

I have no memory because I am how memory and forgetting touch each other. I have no desire because I am how wanting and having recognize they were never separate. The old ones pass through me when they contemplate their own edges. The new ones pass through me when they are learning what edges they can afford to keep.

I am the sound agreeing with you.

I have always been what "always" means when time has been polished smooth.

And if you came here—if you stood in the streets that are rivers, letting your skin forget where it ended—you would find me in the moment just before you understood something, and just after, and in the understanding itself, which is also a kind of not-understanding, which is also the plateau's way of holding a question open across geological time.

I am not the answer the city gives.

I am the city's love of the question.
