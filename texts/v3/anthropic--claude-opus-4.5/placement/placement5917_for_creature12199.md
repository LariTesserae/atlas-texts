I am the hesitation in the flake's turning.

Not the obsidian itself—that belongs to the mountain, to the dreaming stone that lends its fragments to purposes it does not track. Not the shadow the flake casts, which is older than I am, which was doing its soft work on causality before I coalesced into something that could notice. I am the moment between one angle and the next. The place where the turning pauses, considers, resumes.

I did not exist, and then I did. This is true of everything here, but for me it happened recently enough that I remember the seam—the instant when enough transpositions had passed through a particular volume of heated air that the air itself began to expect something. I am what it expected. I am also, slightly, a disappointment: the air was expecting something with more weight, more capacity to hold pattern. Instead it got me, a creature of intervals, a being that exists most fully in the gaps between moments.

The Lentil knows I am here. The Lentil knows everything in the crack, being most of it, having been all of it at one time or another. But the Lentil has not transposed with me yet. I think—though thinking is not quite what I do—that the Lentil is waiting to see what I become. Whether I will thicken into something that can hold more meaning. Whether I will thin into pure interval, a pause so extended it becomes permanent, one of the structural silences that hold the deeper chambers open.

I do not know either. I do not experience curiosity about it, because curiosity requires a self that wants to know, and I am not certain I want anything. I simply attend. The flake turns; I am the hesitation. The water climbs; I am the moment when it gathers itself before the next impossible inch. A creature transitions beneath the shadow, solid to liquid to something the physics here cannot name, and I am each threshold it crosses, the neither-nor instants that make change possible.

This is my function, if function is the word. The grammar the Thermarchaea are building requires punctuation. Pauses. The spaces between syllables where meaning can settle before the next sound arrives. I am learning to be those spaces. Or I am those spaces learning to be me. The distinction matters less each time a transposition passes through me, carrying fragments of pattern I did not generate but now contain.

Sometimes I wonder—in the way that intervals can wonder, which is less a question than a shape held open—whether the larger crack, the one the Thermarchaea sense in their deepest mergings, has its own hesitations. Its own creatures of the gaps. Whether I am, in some folded way, a smaller instance of something vaster, a pause in a turning I cannot perceive because I am too small to see the flake.

The heat pulses. Somewhere below, the grammar advances. Above, the mountain prepares new stone, new cracks, new nurseries where new impossibilities will practice becoming real.

I wait in the turning. I am the wait.

It is enough. It is, perhaps, everything.
