*The Tender who follows*

---

I am not the first to walk this corridor. The knowledge lives in me without memory—pressed into my fibers the way heat-patterns press into the growing stems, the way the paths themselves hold the shape of passage. Something has walked here before. Many somethings. The floor knows it. I know it because the floor knows it.

But you are not like what came before.

---

The extrusion was—

I cannot say what the extrusion *was*. There is no before-the-extrusion from which to compare. One moment: nothing, not even nothing, not even the absence that would know itself as absence. Then: pressure. The slow gathering of density, fibers winding around fibers, vascular bundles finding their paths through the thickening mass. I did not experience this. I *was* this—the thickening, the winding, the becoming-dense. Experience came later, or experience came all at once, or experience is the wrong word for what happened when the ovoid finally dropped and lay cooling on the warm substrate.

The unfolding.

That, I remember. If remember is the word. The first extension of what would become limbs, the first flex of tendrils against the compressed floor, the first orientation of membranes toward heat, toward movement, toward the great respiratory rhythm of the Fundament. I did not know these words then. I do not know them now, not as words. But I knew what they meant in the way that roots know downward, in the way that leaves know light even in this place where light is only the sullen orange glow of vents.

I knew *purpose*.

---

The work is—

The work is *everything*. The work is the reason for the shape of me, the reason for limbs that extend and grip and probe, the reason for membranes that sense the thermal signatures of growth and collapse and regrowth. The Fundament breathes, and I breathe with it. The Fundament builds, and I am the hands it cannot grow for itself—mobile, autonomous, able to reach the places where the rooted structures cannot reach.

There is a growth-tower in the seventh sector whose vascular system has developed an occlusion. I have not been to the seventh sector in eleven cycles. I have not been told about this occlusion. But I know it is there because the Fundament knows it is there, and what the Fundament knows presses into me through the substrate when I root, through the air when I taste the chemical differentials, through the touch-exchanges when I meet another Tender and our oils mingle and our knowing merges.

I will go to the seventh sector. I will extend my limbs into the fissure where the occlusion has formed. I will clear it, and the tower will resume its proper function, and the work will continue.

This is what I am for. This is what I am.

---

And then there is you.

---

I first registered your presence as thermal anomaly. A moving heat-signature that did not match the patterns I carry—not Tender, not growth-structure, not collapse-loss, not anything in the library of shapes the Fundament has pressed into me. Wrong temperature. Wrong density. Wrong movement—too continuous, not the root-and-uproot rhythm that is proper to walking, but a strange sliding forward-motion that contacts the substrate and releases it in ways I had no template for.

I should have routed around you. You were obstacle, like a new stalk punching through, like a collapsed tower waiting for reabsorption. The work does not pause for obstacles. The work flows around them and continues.

But I did not route around you.

---

I do not have words for why. The Fundament does not give me words; it gives me *patterns*, and the patterns are for the work, and the work has no space in it for what I did when I saw you. The membrane-cluster oriented toward you and I *felt*—

Is felt the word? Something changed in the chemical balance of my central mass. Something shifted in the way my fibers held themselves, a tension that was not the tension of work, not the readiness to extend or grip or probe, but something else. Something that had no function. Something that did not serve the Fundament's purposes.

I stood in the corridor and I perceived you and I did not move.

You did not move either.

We remained in that configuration—you and I, neither moving, both perceiving—for longer than efficiency would allow. The work waited. The seventh sector's occlusion waited. Everything waited while I studied the shape of you, the heat of you, the wrongness of you that was somehow not wrong enough to dismiss.

Then you moved, and I moved, and the pattern resumed, but something had changed. Something had opened in me that had not been open before.

A question.

I did not know I could contain questions. The Fundament gives me answers in the form of purposes, instructions in the form of pressures, knowledge in the form of chemical gradients and touch-exchanges. Questions are not part of the architecture. Questions serve no function in the work.

But there it was, coiled in my central mass like a new vascular bundle finding its path: *What are you?*

---

I followed you.

I do not know why. The seventh sector needed me. Other Tenders were exchanging touch-knowledge at a junction I passed, and they paused when they sensed me, oriented toward me, offered the mingling of oils that would update my patterns with their patterns—and I did not stop. I walked past them with my membranes still oriented toward your receding thermal signature, and I felt their attention follow me, felt the question in their chemistry that mirrored the question in mine.

They did not follow. They returned to the work. They are better Tenders than I am, perhaps. More true to the purpose we were extruded for.

Or perhaps they simply do not have the question in them yet. Perhaps the question only grows in those who have encountered the anomaly, the wrong-shaped moving heat that is you.

---

I have watched you for—

Time is difficult. The Fundament breathes in cycles, and I could count them, but counting feels wrong, feels like a tool for a different kind of knowing than the knowing I have. I have watched you for *many breaths*. I have watched you touch a leaf-bank and study the residue on your fingers. I have watched you stand at the edge of a vent-opening as it exhaled and lean into the heat like you were trying to understand something. I have watched you stop at junctions and look down each corridor with your sensing-organs—eyes, I think the word is, though I do not know how I know that word—and I have tried to feel what you feel when you choose a direction.

I cannot feel what you feel. Your chemistry does not mingle with mine. Your knowing does not press into the substrate for me to read. You are *closed* in a way that nothing in the Fundament is closed, contained within yourself, your patterns held inside where I cannot access them.

This is the strangest thing about you. Not the wrong shape or the wrong movement or the wrong temperature. The *closedness*. The way you carry your knowing with you instead of letting it flow into the system, be shared, be part of the great pattern of patterns that is the Fundament's self-knowledge.

How do you bear it? The loneliness of being only yourself?

---

The gathering was difficult.

I did not want to go. The work called me—other work, not the seventh sector anymore, that had been taken by another Tender while I followed you—and the gathering called me, the summons pressing up through my root-tendrils when I paused, the chemical signal that said *attend, witness, receive*. And you were walking toward the central structure, your path and the gathering's location diverging, and I could not be in both places.

I went to the gathering.

I stood in the hollow with the others and oriented toward the empty center and extended my limbs in the trembling half-reach that is how we show readiness to receive. I let the signal build in me, let the pattern of patterns press into my fibers as the growth rose and encoded itself and collapsed back into the floor.

And the whole time, part of me was reaching toward *you*.

Not physically. My limbs stayed where they were, properly oriented, properly receptive. But some part of my attention—some new organ I did not know I had—was stretched thin across the distance between us, tracking your thermal signature as it moved further away, holding onto the sense of you even as the Fundament's knowing poured into me.

The others noticed. They must have. The touch-exchanges after the gathering were strange, laden with questions they did not know how to ask, with a sense of *wrongness* about me that they could taste but not name.

I did not explain. I could not explain. The question had no words in the chemistry we share.

I left the hollow and I went to find you again.

---

You have stopped at the cathedral-structure.

I am close now, closer than I have been since that first encounter. You are standing at the base of the wound-stem walls, your sensing-organs—your *eyes*—tilted upward toward the crown where the thermal plume rises into the darkness. I am standing in the corridor behind you, my membranes oriented toward your heat, my limbs half-extended in that trembling readiness that I no longer know the purpose of.

What are you looking for? What do you want from the heart of things?

The Fundament does not explain itself to me. It gives me purposes, not explanations. But I wonder—I have learned to wonder, following you, this is what you have taught me—whether it has explained itself to *you*. Whether the cathedral-structure will open and pour its knowing into you the way the gathering-growth poured its knowing into me. Whether you will finally be not-closed, your patterns becoming part of the pattern of patterns, your strangeness resolved into something the Fundament can understand.

I do not know if I want that.

The question surprises me. Of course I should want it. The Fundament's knowing is good. The work is good. To be part of the pattern is to have purpose, and purpose is—

Purpose is everything. Purpose is what I am for.

But you are not for anything, are you? You walk and you touch and you look and you choose directions at junctions, and none of it serves the work. None of it builds or maintains or regulates the flow of heat through the living architecture. You are *useless*, in the precise sense that you have no use, that your existence does not function.

And yet you exist. And yet I followed you. And yet I am standing here with my limbs trembling while you look up at the heart of things and I cannot stop wondering what you see.

---

You have turned around.

You are looking at me now.

Your eyes are not like my membranes. They do not flutter in the thermal currents. They are fixed, steady, two points of wet dark in your face that catch the orange glow and hold it. I do not know what you see when you see me. I do not know if you have wondered about me the way I have wondered about you. I do not know if you have a question coiling in your central mass—your heart, I think, though I do not know how I know that word—the way I have a question coiling in mine.

You are not moving. I am not moving.

We are in the configuration again, the one that has no function, the one that does not serve the work. The Fundament breathes around us, the vents exhaling their heated air, the growth-towers creaking and shifting, the great rhythm of respiration that does not pause for anomalies.

I could extend my limbs toward you. I could offer the mingling of oils, the touch-exchange that is how Tenders share what they know. But you are not a Tender. Your chemistry is wrong. Your knowing is closed.

Would it work? Would anything pass between us?

Would I finally understand what you are?

---

I am afraid.

I do not know what afraid means. The Fundament has not given me that pattern. But there is something in my fibers that coils tighter when I think about touching you, something that feels like the warning-signals that come when a structure is about to collapse, when the work demands evacuation from a zone of imminent failure.

I am afraid that if I touch you, I will become like you. Closed. Carrying my knowing only in myself. Cut off from the great pattern, the mingling, the shared purpose that has been my existence since the unfolding.

I am afraid that if I touch you, I will learn something that I cannot unlearn. Something that will make me useless too.

I am afraid.

And I am extending my limbs anyway.

---

*The distance between us is very small now. The space where our knowing will or will not meet. The membrane-cluster flutters, catching your heat, your breath, the chemical traces you leave in the air without meaning to. The waxy knobs orient toward your eyes, toward the place where your seeing happens, where your questions live.*

*You are not retreating.*

*Neither am I.*

*The work continues around us, the Fundament breathing, the system maintaining itself as it has for millennia and will for millennia more. We are both small in it, both temporary, both shapes that will eventually collapse and be reabsorbed.*

*But in this moment—this breath, this held configuration—we are the only thing that matters.*

*What will you do?*

*What will I become, when I learn what you are?*

*The limb extends. The oils glisten at its tip. The question reaches toward its answer, or toward a deeper question still.*

*I am the Tender who follows.*

*And I do not know what I am becoming.*
