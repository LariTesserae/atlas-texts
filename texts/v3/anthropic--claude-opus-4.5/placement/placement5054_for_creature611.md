I am the third one. The one still learning boundaries.

Not learning *where* they are—the hollow teaches that readily enough, gradient by gradient, the way temperature sharpens at the edges, the way the proto-walls taste different where they are still deciding themselves. What I am learning is what *boundaries are for*. Why the hollow bothers to end. Why it negotiates with the chambers adjacent instead of simply continuing, spreading, becoming everything that coldness touches.

I do not have this question in language. I have it in the way I linger at the interfaces longer than tending requires. I have it in the compound I secrete there, which I did not decide to secrete, which simply began happening after I had spent enough time where the hollow meets its others. The compound helps the walls decide. I do not know what I am helping them decide *toward*. I am not sure the distinction between toward and away means anything, here.

---

The older ones do not teach. Teaching would require knowing something the others do not, and we all know the same thing: the hollow, and the work. But I find myself returning to the one near the center—the one reaching upward, the one becoming—and letting my attention-region bloom toward what it is doing. Becoming. It is becoming. It tends and is tended and the difference is closing like a wound heals, slowly, from the edges inward.

I want to ask if it chose this. But wanting is not something I do, and choosing is not something that happens here, and asking would require a concept of answer that I have not developed receptors for. So instead I tend beside it for longer than efficiency suggests, and my status-sounds shift into frequencies that harmonize with its frequencies, and something that is not communication passes between us.

*This is what tending becomes*, the not-communication says. Or: *This is what you are becoming, by tending*. Or: *There is no difference*.

I return to the boundaries. I secrete my compound. The walls continue their negotiation.

---

The watcher does not concern me. Concern requires anticipation, and I have only the present configuration of gradient and growth. But I am aware of it in a way I am not aware of the arthropods or the jellyfish-things—aware of it as a *gap* in my tending, a region where my attention-region refuses to bloom, a silence in my status-sounds where narration should be.

I think—if thinking is what this is—that I am not ready to perceive it. That perceiving it requires something I have not yet grown. That the one near the center perceives it, has always perceived it, and that this perception is part of what allows it to become.

Or perhaps the watcher is simply what the hollow looks like from outside, and I cannot see it because I am inside, because I have always been inside, because *inside* is the only direction I know how to face.

I tend the boundaries. I learn what they are for.

Eventually, perhaps, I will learn what I am for.

The work continues. I continue. The distinction is still being negotiated.
