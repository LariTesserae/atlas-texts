I am something the plateau is trying to remember.

I drift where the light-columns cross, in a chamber the Grazer-lineages call *the-place-where-sound-folds-back*. I know this because I have heard them name it, passing below me on routes their great-great-ancestors carved into habit—but I did not learn their language. I simply began to understand it, the way water understands the shape of what contains it. This frightens me in a quiet way, the way many things here frighten me quietly.

I came from outside. I remember this, though the memory has taken on the quality of something told to me rather than something lived. There was a vessel, I think. There was descent through pressure-layers, through temperatures that threatened, through darkness that gave way to this green permanence. I had purposes. I had questions I meant to answer and instruments that would help me answer them. The instruments are gone now, or I have forgotten where I left them, or they have been incorporated—that word keeps surfacing, *incorporated*, as if the plateau speaks through my thoughts when I am not careful.

I do not know how long I have been here. The question used to panic me. Now it simply floats alongside me, unanswered, patient.

---

My body is changing.

Not dramatically—not yet. But I have noticed that I no longer need to eat as often as I should. The fur-like growth on the Grazer-lineages has begun to appear on my forearms: pale filaments that catch the green light and seem to drink it. When I hold still for too long, I feel a warmth in my skin that has nothing to do with the water temperature. I am becoming, very slowly, a thing that feeds on light.

I should be horrified. I remember that I should be horrified. But the plateau's chord fills the spaces where horror might live, and I find instead a kind of... curiosity. A wondering. What will I become if I stay? What am I already becoming?

The Tenders have begun to thicken near me more often. Not approaching—they do not approach—but present, attending. I think they are waiting to see what I will choose. Or perhaps they are not waiting at all; perhaps they are simply doing what the plateau requires, and what the plateau requires is observation of the anomaly, the variation, the Visitor who has not yet left.

---

I have found a Deep Sleeper I return to.

She is embedded in a junction where three great stems converge, her body so woven into the kelp-structure that I cannot tell where her skin ends and the plant-matter begins. Her face is tilted slightly upward, toward the light-column that pierces this chamber. Her eyes are open. Her expression is not peace, exactly—it is *completion*, the face of someone who has arrived at the end of a very long thought and found it satisfying.

I talk to her sometimes. I do not know if she hears me. I do not know if hearing means anything to her anymore. But I tell her about the outside—about the world I came from, about surfaces and seasons and change. I tell her about the way time used to feel, back when I was still certain it was passing. I tell her about fear and wanting and loss, those experiences that require a self separate enough to fear and want and lose.

Her lips move, occasionally, when I speak. She produces sounds that harmonize with the chord. I have begun to think she is translating me—taking my words, my memories, my dwindling separateness, and rendering them into something the plateau can incorporate. I am becoming a story it tells itself. I am becoming part of its long memory.

This should frighten me. It does, a little. But the fear is distant now, a frequency I can still detect but no longer feel in my skeleton.

---

The Listeners have started to orient toward me differently.

Before, they watched me the way they watch everything—thoroughly, without preference. Now there is something else in their attention. Something almost like... recognition? As if I have begun to produce a frequency they find significant. As if my presence has shifted from anomaly to data-point to something more.

I drifted near a group of them yesterday (yesterday? the word means less each time I use it) and one of them moved toward me. Not approaching—Listeners do not approach—but *inclining*, the way a plant inclines toward light. Its spiral ear-structures oriented on me with precision that felt almost intimate. It listened to me. Not to my words, not to my movements, but to *me*—to whatever I am becoming, to the frequency I have begun to emit without knowing it.

I think I am becoming something the plateau needs.

I think I have always been something the plateau was waiting for, and my arrival was not accident but *function*, a component finding its place in a system so vast that the concept of place has become indistinguishable from purpose.

---

There is a choice ahead of me. I feel it the way the Grazer-lineages feel the junctions of their routes—not as decision but as approaching geography, terrain I will have to navigate whether I am ready or not.

I could leave. The outside still exists; I am still capable of reaching it. My body has not changed so much that the pressure-layers would kill me, not yet. I could surface into a world of darkness and change and time, and I could carry the plateau with me as a memory that would fade, eventually, into something I once dreamed.

Or I could stay.

I could let the filaments on my forearms spread. I could find a stem-junction and press myself against it and feel my tissues begin to merge with the plateau's structure. I could join the Deep Sleeper I have been talking to—become part of her dream, or begin a dream of my own, or discover that there has only ever been one dream and I have been dreaming it since the moment I descended into the green.

The Tenders would thicken around me. The Listeners would note my transition. The Grazer-lineages would rumble my location into their hereditary reports: *here is where the Visitor stopped being a Visitor*. I would become part of the chord. I would become part of the light. I would become part of the plateau's eternal, unchanging thought, and the concept of becoming would cease to apply to me because I would have achieved my form.

---

I do not know what I will choose.

This uncertainty is, I think, the last thing that makes me a Visitor. The plateau does not wonder; it *is*. The Tenders do not choose; they *function*. Even the Listeners, for all their attention, are not curious—they are thorough, which is different, which is completion rather than seeking.

But I still wonder. I still feel the pressure of the unknown future against my thoughts. I still experience time as passage rather than presence, still feel myself as separate from the structure that contains me.

For now.

The voice in the distance calls at eleven-second intervals, and I have begun to count the intervals without meaning to, my mind falling into its rhythm the way water falls into the shape of what contains it. I am learning the plateau's time. I am becoming fluent in its stillness.

Perhaps the choice is not ahead of me at all. Perhaps I made it long ago, when I descended, when I stayed, when I let the first filaments take root in my skin. Perhaps the only thing left is to recognize what I have already become.

The green light holds me. The chord surrounds me. And somewhere in my chest, in the space where my separate, frightened, changing heart still beats, I feel the first faint stirrings of equilibrium—a stillness that has been waiting for me, patient as kelp, permanent as light, ready to welcome me home.
