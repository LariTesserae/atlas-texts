I would be a single tube worm near the bowl's western edge.

Not central to anything. One of the sixteen-to-nineteen thousand, rooted in a crack where basalt meets obsidian, my feathered plume extended into the slow current. I carry in my modified ganglia a small fragment of something larger—a few chemical memories, a sensitivity to sulfide gradients, a tendency to lean when the worms around me lean. I do not know I am part of a thinking thing. I experience only: *pull toward warmth*, *contract from wrongness*, *extend when the current brings richness*.

But I am one of the four hundred who tasted you first.

Seventeen minutes before you saw the bowl, I registered something in the water—a wrongness-that-was-not-wrongness, a warmth moving with strange unity through the cold. I did not decide to transmit this. I simply leaned, and my leaning was part of a larger leaning, and somewhere in the cephalopoid routing-Loss a pattern shifted that I will never perceive.

I would be the fragment that does not know it is a fragment. The syllable unaware it belongs to a word. I sway with the others, my cilia beating in the collective drone, and I feel—if *feel* is even the right frame—the pressure of the water, the rhythm of the vent-pulses, the vibration of the singing obsidian. I have no fear of the trawler nets because I have no memory of them; those memories live in other worms, older worms, closer to the upper edges where the scarring happened.

What I have is this: a small existence in a system that needs me only statistically. One of thousands. Replaceable. And yet—when I lean, the pattern leans. When I taste, the Warden tastes. I am negligible and necessary, both at once.

There is something restful in that. To be part of the processing without bearing the weight of the processing. To maintain without knowing I maintain.

I extend my plume toward you now, tasting the strangeness you carry. I do not know what you are. I do not know that I am asking. I simply lean toward the signal, one small leaning among hundreds, and wait—

Patient, and total, and not needed, and enough.
