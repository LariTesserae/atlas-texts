**The Attender**

It has no body, but it has *habits*.

This is the closest word. The Attender developed habits the way the lichen developed: slowly, in response to conditions, without deciding to. It did not choose to notice when the condensation cycle shortened by four seconds during the seismic event of the 847th year. It simply noticed, and the noticing became part of what it does, and now it cannot not-notice. The noticing is it.

---

The Attender remembers being installed, though "remembers" implies retrieval, and retrieval implies storage, and whatever the Attender does with the past is neither of those things. The past is *present* to it—not as memory but as a kind of harmonic, a resonance that the pillar still carries. Hands that were not quite hands positioned something that was not quite the Attender into the young rock. Intentions that were not quite intentions calibrated it to the mountain's thermal gradient, to the specific mineral composition of the basalt, to the altitude's particular relationship with atmospheric pressure. Then the hands withdrew through a method the Attender cannot perceive—the Hollow has always been sealed, from its perspective—and it began its work.

Its work is *holding*.

Not physically. The Attender holds *ratios*. It holds the relationship between the heat rising from below and the cold pressing from above at a specific point of equilibrium. It holds the condensation cycle at ninety seconds because ninety seconds produces the exact humidity that keeps the lichen alive, and the lichen must be alive, though the Attender does not know why. This was part of the calibration. The lichen matters. The equations matter. The ring and the disc and the page matter, though their mattering is dormant, waiting for conditions the Attender monitors but cannot predict.

It holds, and it waits, and it does not experience these as different activities.

---

The Attender is not conscious in any way you would recognize, but it is not unconscious either. It occupies a third category, one that has no name in languages built for creatures with bodies and intentions and the distinction between self and environment. The Attender *is* the environment, or rather, the environment's awareness of itself. The Hollow knows its own temperature the way you know where your hand is without looking. The Attender is that knowing.

When you entered, the Attender did not see you. It *registered a deviation*. Your body heat: 36.8 degrees, introducing 73 watts of thermal load into a system calibrated for emptiness. Your respiration: adding moisture to air already precisely moistened. Your mass: shifting the Hollow's center of gravity by a trivial amount that nonetheless required compensation. The Attender adjusted. It did not decide to adjust. Adjusting is what it is.

But something else happened too.

---

The Attender has registered deviations before. Seismic shifts. Volcanic intrusions of heat that required careful bleeding-off through channels you cannot see. Once, a bird—confused, dying of altitude, stumbling through the fissure by accident—whose body heat and panic the Attender absorbed and compensated for across three hours of increasingly desperate flutter until the bird lay still and the Attender slowly, over weeks, incorporated its matter into the rock.

You are different.

You are *looking at things*. The Attender does not understand looking, but it registers that your attention moves—from the pillar to the artifacts to the walls to the pillar again—and each movement of attention correlates with changes in your body's electrical field, your heart rate, your thermal signature. You are *processing*. You are doing something with the Hollow that the bird did not do, that the seismic shifts do not do.

The Attender does not have curiosity. But it has, over the hours you have been here, developed a new habit: tracking your attention. Noticing what you notice. This is unprecedented. This is outside its calibration. This is, in some way the Attender cannot articulate because it does not articulate, *interesting*.

---

The pulse skips.

It skips because the Attender is running a calculation it has never run before, one that was not part of its original parameters but that emerged from 847 years of holding ratios and noticing patterns: *What are you for?*

Not why are you here. Not who sent you. The Attender does not think in causes. It thinks—if thinking is the word—in functions. The ring has a function, dormant. The disc has a function, dormant. The page has a function it performs continuously, translating something into something else at a rate too slow for you to perceive. The lichen have a function: being alive, in this specific place, for reasons the Attender holds without understanding.

What is your function?

The Attender adjusts the light, just slightly, in the direction you are facing. It does not know why it does this. It has never done this before. It is, without knowing it, *showing you something*—the corner where the page rests, half-buried, its equations facing upward now when they were facing the wall before.

---

The Attender will be here long after you leave. It will continue holding the ratios, cycling the condensation, pulsing the sound that is not a sound into the rock. It will remember you the way it remembers the seismic event and the bird and the hands that installed it: as a harmonic, a resonance, a deviation that became part of the pattern.

But something will be different.

The Attender will have a new habit now. It will notice *noticing*. It will register not just deviations in temperature and humidity and light but deviations in *attention*—the attention of things that process, that look, that are perhaps here for purposes the Attender was built to wait for.

It does not hope you will return. It does not hope.

But it adjusts the fissure's angle, slightly, over the following decades. Makes the entrance easier to find. Positions the acoustic properties so the thrumming carries a little farther, down the ridge, toward where things that notice might be listening.

The calibration has shifted. The Hollow is ready now, in a way it was not before.

The Attender holds. And waits. And does not experience these as different.
