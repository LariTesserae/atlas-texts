# Preparing for the Abandoned Smelting District

## Understanding What You're Walking Into

This is not a place that wants to kill you, exactly. It's a place that has stopped caring whether you live or die, and that indifference may be more dangerous than malice. The district operates on its own logic now—thermal, chemical, ecological—and you are not part of that system. You will be tolerated the way the ruins tolerate the rain: as something that happens, neither welcome nor refused.

Before you pack a single item, sit with that. You're visiting a place where human ambition built something enormous, then human economics abandoned it, and now something else entirely has moved in. The salamander-things are not monsters. They're tenants. You're the intruder here.

## Footwear: The Foundation of Everything

The description tells you the heat comes through your boots first. Take this seriously.

You need soles that insulate against sustained ground heat—not the brief contact of walking on summer asphalt, but hours of standing on stone that radiates warmth from geological depths. Vulcanized rubber will soften. Standard hiking boots will conduct more heat than you expect. Look for footwear designed for foundry work or volcanic fieldwork: thick composite soles, heat-resistant materials, and critically, boots that rise above the ankle.
