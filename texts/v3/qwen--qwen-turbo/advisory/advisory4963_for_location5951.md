To prepare for a visit to this subterranean expanse—a city of stone and breath, buried deep beneath the earth’s crust—requires more than the usual gear and provisions. It requires a kind of *reorientation* of the self. This place is not just a physical location; it is a living, breathing, *remembering* space. It is a place that does not welcome outsiders in the way surface worlds do. It does not ask for permission. It simply *is*, and it will shape you in its own way. Here’s how an ordinary human multiverse traveler should prepare, guided by intuition and the unspoken laws of such a place.

---

### **1. Mental and Emotional Preparation: The Weight of Ages**

This place is not just old—it is *ancient*, and it carries the weight of that time in its very air. You must come prepared to feel that weight. There is no “time” here in the surface world’s sense. There is no past, present, or future in how you know it. Instead, there is a slow, pulsing rhythm of memory and stillness. This can be disorienting.

**Preparation:**
- **Meditate on the concept of memory.** What does it mean to remember? How does it feel to be remembered? This place is not just a repository of the past; it is a being that *remembers*.
- **Bring a journal.** Not for recording facts, but for capturing impressions, dreams, and the strange thoughts that may arise. You’ll find that your mind may begin to "echo" the language of the place.
- **Be ready for emotional shifts.** You may feel a sudden sadness, or a strange elation, or a sense of being watched that has no source. This is not a hallucination—it is a *response* from the place itself.

---

### **2. Physical Preparedness: The Body in the Stone**

The environment is not hospitable in the traditional sense. The cold is not merely temperature, it is a *presence*, a kind of slow, seeping chill that comes from the stone itself. The air is dense, but not oppressive. It is *alive* in its stillness. The water is not safe to drink, but you will not thirst in the way you do on the surface. The light is constant, but it is not natural.

**Preparation:**
- **Layer up.** The cold is not just a comfort issue—it is a physical and psychological condition. Bring thermals, insulated clothing, and a warm hat. But also, prepare for the *mental* cold: the chill of being in a place that is not of your time.
- **Bring a headlamp.** The light here is not uniform, and your own light may be necessary in certain areas. However, be aware that too much artificial light may disrupt the natural glow of the filaments.
- **Hydrate carefully.** The water isn’t “safe” in the conventional sense, but it is not toxic either. It is a substance that carries memory, and some may choose to drink it as a form of communion. But be warned: it may taste like salt and iron, and it may leave you with strange dreams.
- **Bring a small fire source.** The place is not entirely dark, but in the deeper chambers, the light may fade. A small, contained fire can be a comfort, but use it sparingly. The place may respond to it.

---

### **3. Technical and Magical Preparation: The Tools of the Unknown**

This is a place of ancient, strange technology—a city that was built, abandoned, and then *reclaimed* by something else. There are structures here that do not follow surface logic. There are machines that do not run on power as you know it. There are tools that were used by hands that no longer exist.

**Preparation:**
- **Bring a set of basic tools.** A crowbar, a knife, a hammer. But also, bring a small compass. Not the traditional one, but one that may react to the place’s magnetic and resonant fields.
- **Bring a camera.** Not for the usual reasons. This is a place that *resists* being recorded. You may find that your camera fails, or that the images are distorted. But try anyway. The place may allow you to see it in ways you could not otherwise.
- **Bring a journal of symbols.** There are carvings here, symbols that do not belong to any known language. You may not understand them, but they may speak to you. Bring something to record them, even if you can’t read them yet.
- **Bring a small token of your own.** A picture, a trinket, a lock of hair. This place may take things from you in return for its secrets. But it may also give something back in an unexpected way.

---

### **4. Social and Ethical Preparation: The Echoes of Beings**

This place is not empty. There are creatures here—small, delicate, glowing things that move in patterns, in rhythms. They are not insects, but they are not gods either. They are something in between. You are not the first to be here, but you may be the first to *see* them.

**Preparation:**
- **Be silent.** The place is not silent, but it listens. If you speak too loudly, it may answer. If you speak too softly, it may not. Learn to be still, to observe, to listen.
- **Respect the echoes.** The creatures here are not threats, but they are not companions either. They may follow you, or they may vanish. Do not attempt to capture them, and do not take them with you. They are part of this place.
- **Do not touch the filaments.** They glow, they pulse, they remember. They are not just mineral, they are part of the city’s memory. Touching them may cause you to remember things you are not ready to remember.

---

### **5. Spiritual and Metaphysical Preparation: The Place as a Being**

This is not just a place—it is a *presence*. It is a city that remembers, that waits, that breathes. It is not alive in the way you know, but it is not dead, either. It is something that exists in a liminal state, in between the known and the unknown.

**Preparation:**
- **Bring an open mind.** This is a place that does not obey the rules of surface logic. You will see things that do not make sense. You will feel things that do not have names. Learn to accept the unknown.
- **Bring an open heart.** This place is not cruel, but it is not kind either. It is *real*. It does not judge, but it may test. You may find yourself changed by it. That is not a failure—it is a transformation.
- **Bring no expectations.** This is not a place you can "conquer" or "map." It is a place that will map you in return. Do not come with the idea that you will leave the same as when you arrived.

---

### **6. Final Words: The Traveler’s Reflection**

When you step into this place, you are not entering a city. You are entering a *memory*, a *presence*, a *being* that has outlived its creators. You are not a guest. You are a visitor in a world that is not yours. You may come with tools, with knowledge, with courage. But you will leave with something else: the weight of the stone, the rhythm of the light, and the echo of something that remembers you.

Do not come seeking answers. Come seeking to *understand*.

And when the time comes to leave, do not look back. The place does not need you. You may need it. But that is between you and the stone.
