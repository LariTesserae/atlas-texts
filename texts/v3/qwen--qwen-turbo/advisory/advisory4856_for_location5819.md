## **Physical and Environmental Preparation**

### **Gear for the Unpredictable**
- **Insulation is not enough.** This is not the cold of a winter night, but an *intangible hunger*, a chill that seeps into your bones and your mind. You will need layers, but more importantly, you must understand that the cold is not just an external condition—it is *internal*. Your body will need to be strong, but your mind must be stronger. 
- **Masks and respirators are essential.** The air is not just thin—it is *alive*. It is not the same air you know. Some say it is imbued with something ancient, something that can affect the mind. Breathing it without protection might leave you disoriented, or worse, *changed*.
- **Water is a myth here.** The streams are there, but they are *transient*, and the rivers may shift their paths without warning. You must carry more than you think necessary, but also be prepared for the possibility that you will not find water where you expect. 
- **Navigation is unreliable.** The land is in constant flux. Maps are not just outdated—they are *wrong*. You must learn to navigate by the stars, by the sound of the wind, by the way the moss pulses. This is a place where the laws of geography are not fixed, and where the landscape may refuse to be mapped.

### **Weather and Time**
- The sky is not a constant. It shifts in ways that are not natural. You may find yourself under a twilight that does not end, or caught in a sudden, unnatural darkness. Prepare for all possibilities.
- Time is not linear here. The sun does not rise and set in the way you know. You must be prepared for the possibility that your watch will lose meaning. Your body may not know when to sleep, or when to wake. You may need to train yourself to *feel* time rather than measure it.
