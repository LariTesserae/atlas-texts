You may need to develop an awareness that extends beyond the senses you’ve been given. It may be that your body can draw energy from the mycelial undergrowth, or that your consciousness can be sustained through a connection to the network. This requires a deep trust in the environment, not as a host, but as a *co-creator*.

- **Let Go of the Idea of "Escape":** This is not a place you can leave in the way you would leave a city. It is a living system, and it does not recognize the idea of boundaries. It is not a labyrinth in the traditional sense, but a *network*—one that shifts and reconfigures in response to your presence. You must accept that you are not merely a visitor; you are *part of the system* now.

You must prepare to be disoriented, to have your perceptions challenged, to experience time in a way that is not linear. The city is not a place of *time* as you know it, but of *cycles*, of growth and decay, of renewal and transformation.

You must learn to listen—not to hear, but to *become* the listener. This may require a form of meditation or a surrender to the rhythm of the place.

#### **3. Metaphysical Preparation: The Self as a Node**

- **Be Aware of the Will of the City:** The city is neither good nor bad. It is not a place of intent in the human sense. It is an *intelligence* that has evolved beyond the need for external goals. It is not interested in your survival, but it may allow it. You must be aware that the city has a will, and that will is not yours. You must learn to *coexist* with that will, not resist it.

- **Accept the Possibility of Transformation:** The city is not static. It grows, it changes, it reconfigures itself. It is possible that you, too, may be changed by it. This is not dangerous, but it is not safe. You must be prepared to *shift*—to become something other than you were. This is the true test of a traveler here: not whether you can survive, but whether you can *change*.

- **Understand the Concept of "Place":** The city is not a place. It is a *system*. It is not a location, but a *state of being*. You are not entering it; you are being *integrated* into it. This is not a journey, but a *metamorphosis*.

To prepare for this place, you must first *unlearn*. You must unlearn the idea of space, of time, of life, of death, of boundaries. You must unlearn the concept of "travel" as you know it.
