### Physical Preparation

**Clothing:**  
You will need clothes that are breathable, but also able to handle the constant dampness. Light, moisture-wicking fabrics are ideal, but don't rely on them entirely. The air is thick with humidity, and the ground is spongy. You will be walking on a surface that feels like it is breathing beneath your feet. So, choose shoes that are sturdy, well-cushioned, and water-resistant. Avoid anything that will trap sweat or allow moisture to seep in. A pair of hiking boots with good grip is essential—this is not a place for slipping, even if the ground is soft.

**Gear:**  
A lightweight, waterproof backpack is a must. You will need to carry water, but not too much—because the water here is everywhere, but not always accessible. You may find yourself needing to filter it or boil it, depending on what you find. A small, compact water filter or purification tablets are necessary. You also need a reliable light source—because while the sky is always soft, the light is diffused, and shadows can be deceptive. The land is open, but it can be disorienting. A headlamp or lantern will be invaluable, not just at night, but during the day if you find yourself in a place where the mist obscures the sun.

**Navigation:**  
This is not a place for maps. Or at least, not the kind of maps you're used to. There are no landmarks, no structures, no clear paths. The terrain is flat, but subtle changes in elevation and moisture can make it difficult to orient yourself. A compass is useful, but even that may be affected by the unusual magnetic properties of the land. A GPS device is helpful, but not foolproof. The best way to navigate is to *listen*—to the sound of the water, the rhythm of the wind, the pulse of the earth. This place *moves* in ways that are not immediately perceptible. You must learn to *feel* it.

**Supplies:**  
You will need more than just water and food. This is a place where you will need to *wait*. So, bring snacks that are high in energy and easy to eat. A journal is also a good idea, not just for recording what you see, but for reflecting on what you feel. This place is not about action—it is about *presence*. You may not see much, but you will feel *a lot*.
