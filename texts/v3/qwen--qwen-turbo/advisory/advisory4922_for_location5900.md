To prepare for visiting this place, you must first understand that you are not stepping into a location, but into a *state of being*. This is not a realm you can map, not a place you can truly *know*—only feel, endure, and perhaps become part of. It resists the logic of the known world, and to approach it with the tools of ordinary understanding is to risk becoming lost, not just physically, but in your sense of self. Here’s what you must do:

---

### **1. Abandon the Concept of Time**
This place does not move in the way you expect. Time is not linear here. It is not even *present* in a way that you can measure. You may arrive at dawn and leave at dusk, but when you return, it may be the same moment—or a different one. The land does not *age* in the way you do. It remains in a state of *becoming*, of *remembering*, of *unfolding*. You must be prepared to lose your grasp on clocks, schedules, and the rhythm of the sun. If you carry a watch, you might find it broken, or perhaps it tells a time that doesn't match your own. You must not fight this. You must *adapt* to the rhythm of the place, even if it is not a rhythm at all.

---

### **2. Relearn the Concept of "Ground"**
The terrain is a living question. You cannot trust your feet. The earth is not solid, not in any human sense. It shifts, it sighs, it *welcomes* you with deceptive layers. You might think you're standing on firm earth, only to feel it sink beneath you. You may be walking on a shallow pool that seems to hold your weight, only to realize it has begun to *suck* you in. The water is not just liquid—it is a presence. It is sentient in the way that a storm is, or a tide. You must learn to feel the ground, not just with your feet, but with your body. You must move slowly, deliberately, with a reverence for the unseen. You must be prepared to sink. You must be prepared to be *found*.

---

### **3. Learn to Breathe in the Unbreathable**
The air is thick with something that is not air. It is not toxic, but it is not *safe*. It is *alive* in a way that defies your understanding. It clings to your skin, it fills your lungs, it makes your breath visible even when it isn’t cold. You may feel the weight of it in your chest, as if you are breathing through a veil. You must not resist it. You must *let it in*. You must be prepared for your body to react in ways it is not used to. You may dream in the air, or feel its weight in your dreams. You may speak in a language you do not know, or not speak at all. This is not a place for haste. It is a place for stillness, for listening.

---

### **4. Prepare for the Unseen**
This is not a place of sight. It is a place of *feeling*, of *hearing*, of *touching* the intangible. You may not see the creatures, but you will feel them. You will hear them in the marrow of your bones. You may not see the structures, but you will *know* they are there. You may not see the light, but you will feel its glow, even in the dark. You must be ready to *see* with your mind, not your eyes. You must be ready to accept that what you perceive may not be what is. This is not a place of illusions, but of *realities* that are not your own. You must be prepared to be *changed* by what you encounter, even if you cannot explain it.

---

### **5. Leave Behind the Tools of the World**
Do not bring maps. Do not bring GPS. Do not bring a compass. This place does not obey the laws of navigation. It does not have north or south, east or west. It is a place that turns itself around. You will not find your way by looking up. You will find it by *following* the land, by listening to the hum of the water, by feeling the pulse of the earth. You may not need to carry a torch, because the place will light itself. But if you do, bring one that does not burn. This is not a place for fire. It is a place where the very air is damp, where the light is cold and old. You may find that your tools are useless, but that is not a failure. It is a sign that you are where you are meant to be.

---

### **6. Carry No Words**
This is a place that does not want to be named. It does not want to be described. It resists language, as it resists time, as it resists understanding. You may find that your voice falters, that your words seem to slide off the air like water. You may speak, and the place may answer you in a language you do not know, or not at all. You must be prepared to be silent. You must be prepared to *listen* in a way that you never have before. This is not a place for stories. It is a place for *experiences*, for *truths* that cannot be told, only felt.

---

### **7. Be Ready to Be Lost**
This is the most important thing. You will not find your way back the same way you came. You may not even remember the way you came. This is not a place that allows for easy return. It is a place that *remembers* you, that *watches* you, that may choose to let you go or not. You may leave, and find yourself in a different world, or in the same one, but changed. You may return to your own time, or you may not. You must be prepared for this. You must not fear it. Because in this place, you are not just a traveler—you are a part of something larger. You are part of the *land*, even if you do not yet know it.

---

### **8. Understand That You Are Not Alone**
This place is not empty. It is not quiet. It is filled with something that is not *present*, but is *there*. You are not the first to come, and you will not be the last. You may feel the presence of others, or you may feel the absence of them. You may feel the weight of their stories, their lives, their deaths. This is not a place of death, but of *remembrance*. It keeps what it absorbs. It does not forget. It does not release. It holds. And if you stay long enough, it may hold you too.

---

### **9. Walk with Humility**
This is not a place for arrogance. It is not a place for conquest. It is a place that does not need you. It does not care for your goals, your purposes, your ambitions. It exists outside of your understanding. You must approach it with reverence, with patience, with the humility of a child. You must not try to control it. You must not try to *master* it. You must *be* with it, as it is with you.

---

### **10. Accept That You May Not Return**
This is not a warning. It is a truth. This place does not let go easily. It does not release what it has taken. It is not a place of passage. It is a place of *residence*. You may leave, but you may not return. Or you may return, and find that the world you left behind has changed, or that you have. You must be prepared for that. You must be prepared to be *part of* this place, even if you are not *from* it.

---

This is not a journey. It is a transformation. You will not return as you were. You will return as something more, or something less. You will return as something *other*. But you will return.

And you will remember. You will remember the place where the world forgot itself. You will remember the water that sings, the rocks that weep, the silence that is not empty. And you will know that you are not the same.

You are part of it now.
