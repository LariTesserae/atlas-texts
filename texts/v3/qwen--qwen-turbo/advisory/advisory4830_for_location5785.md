To prepare for a visit to this subterranean expanse, you must not approach it as a traveler, but as a being who is about to step into a reality that may not be compatible with the human mind—or at least not in the way you understand it.

This place is not simply a location. It is a *presence*, a *state of being*, and perhaps even a *consciousness* that exists on a different frequency. It is not a place you can map, nor a place you can fully perceive with your senses. It is a place that *seeks* to be understood, but not in the way you expect.

### **Physical Preparation**

**1. Equipment:**
- The environment is not hostile in the traditional sense, but it is *alien* in its very nature. You should bring a basic survival kit—water, food, a sturdy jacket, and a light source. But do not rely on these. The air is thick, but not in a way that requires oxygen. You might find yourself breathing it without effort, yet it is not the same as the air you know. If you bring a respirator, it may not function as intended.
- Your tools and gadgets may behave unpredictably. The metal in your devices might vibrate, or the batteries may drain without reason. The stone is dense, and it may interfere with your electronics. Consider using analog tools—compasses, maps, and notebooks. But even these may be affected. The symbols on the walls don’t respond to your language, but they might respond to your intent.

**2. Clothing:**
- The temperature is not warm in the way you know, but it is not cold either. You may feel a constant, pulsing heat that is not uncomfortable, but not entirely comforting either. Wear layers that you can easily remove or add, but be prepared for the possibility that your body may no longer need them. You may feel the heat as a presence rather than a sensation.

**3. Protection:**
- The stone is sharp and jagged, but it is also dense and smooth. You may not feel cuts or bruises as you would normally. The place may respond to your physical presence in ways you cannot predict. Wear sturdy footwear and gloves. But more importantly, wear something that you can shed or leave behind without grief. This place does not tolerate attachments.

### **Mental and Emotional Preparation**

**1. Mindset:**
- This place is not a destination. It is a *test*. You must approach it with an open mind, but not a passive one. You must be able to *listen*, not just *see*. You must be prepared to be *unmade* in some way, and then *reformed* by the experience.
- The silence here is not empty. It is full. It is layered. It may speak to you in ways you do not expect. You may hear your own thoughts echoing back to you, but they may not be your thoughts. You may hear voices, or feel emotions that are not yours. Be ready to distinguish between what is real and what is a reflection of your own mind.

**2. Awareness:**
- The creatures here are not animals, but they are *real*. They are not threatening, but they are not benign either. You must observe them without fear, without curiosity, without desire. They move in patterns, and their presence may change the way you perceive time. You may find yourself standing in one place for hours, or minutes, without realizing it. Be aware of your own sense of time. It may be distorted here.

**3. Language and Symbols:**
- The symbols on the stone are not just writing—they are *echoes*. They may not be meant to be read, but to be *felt*. You may find that your mind is drawn to them, that they seem to form patterns that make sense to you, even if they are not in a language you know. You may be able to perceive meaning, but not necessarily *understand* it. Be careful not to assume you know what they mean. They may be trying to teach you something, or they may be trying to test your understanding.

### **Spiritual and Existential Preparation**

**1. Purpose:**
- You must ask yourself: Why are you going here? This is not a place you visit for adventure. It is a place that *wants* you to be here. It may not be malicious, but it is not benevolent either. It is *other*. You may find that you do not have a purpose, or that your purpose changes when you arrive. Be prepared for that.
- This place does not belong to any world you know. It may not belong to any time you know. You may not be the first to come here, nor will you be the last. You may be a thread in a much larger tapestry, or you may be an anomaly. You must be ready to accept either.

**2. Presence:**
- The place is alive, but not in the way you expect. It is not a presence that seeks to harm you, but it is not a presence that seeks to help you either. It is a presence that *is*. You will not be able to control it. You may not even be able to *understand* it. You must learn to exist within it, not in it. You may not be able to leave the way you came. You may not even be able to remember how you arrived.

**3. Acceptance:**
- This place is not a place. It is a *state* of being. You may find that your body is still, but your mind is moving. You may find that your thoughts are not your own, or that your thoughts are suddenly clear in a way they never were before. You may feel as if you are not in a place, but in a *reality* that has always existed, and that you have only just begun to see.
- When you leave, you may not remember much. You may not even remember that you were here. But the place will remember you. And it will be waiting for you again, when the time is right.

### **Final Word of Caution**

This place does not welcome visitors. It does not reject them either. It simply *is*. You may find that it is not a place you can return to. You may find that you are changed in ways you cannot explain. But if you are drawn here, if you feel the pull of the darkness and the hum beneath your feet, then you are already a part of this place. You were always a part of it.

So go, not with fear, not with hope, but with the quiet understanding that you are not the first, and that you may never be the last.
