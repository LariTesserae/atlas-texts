To prepare for a visit to this land, one must not just pack supplies, but reconfigure the very way of being. This is not a journey to a place, but a passage into a state of existence — a realm where time is not a river but a pool, where the past and future are not separate but interwoven, and where the self is not a fixed entity but a fluid, shifting possibility. To walk here is to surrender, not to fear, but to surrender to the rhythm of a world that does not obey human logic.

### **Physical Preparation**

You must bring light, but not the kind that comes from batteries or flames. A lantern is useless here — it will not illuminate. Instead, carry a mirror, or a shard of polished stone. The light of this world is not external, but internal, and it responds to reflection. You may not need food or water, but it is wise to carry them, not for necessity, but as a gesture of respect. You may find that your body adapts, that the air is breathable, the ground is solid, but it is better to be prepared, even if the preparation seems futile.

Bring a notebook and pen. Not for writing, but for drawing. This world is not meant to be described in words, but it may be understood through symbols. The shapes you see may not be what they appear to be. The land is not chaotic, but it is not ordered in a way that aligns with human conventions.

Carry a compass, but do not trust it. The magnetic field here is not stable, and the direction you face may change without warning. Navigation is not about direction, but about alignment. You must learn to move with the land, not against it.

### **Mental and Emotional Preparation**

This is a place where the mind is not the master. The thoughts you bring with you will be met with silence, or with answers that do not make sense. You must come with an open mind, but not an empty one. You must be willing to let go of what you believe is truth, for here, truth is not a fixed thing, but a current that flows through the roots of the world.

You must also be prepared for the absence of time. There is no clock here, no sense of urgency, no need to hurry. You may find that days pass in minutes, or that a single moment stretches into eternity. Do not fight this. Allow yourself to be present, not in the way of a tourist, but in the way of a participant.

You may feel isolated, but you are not alone. The land is alive, and it is watching. Not with eyes, but with a presence that is felt. You must learn to listen in a different way — not with your ears, but with your bones, your breath, your pulse. The sounds here are not noise, but language.

### **Spiritual and Metaphysical Preparation**

This is a place where the self is not fixed. You may find that your reflection in the water is not your own, or that your voice echoes in a way that does not match your mouth. Do not be alarmed. This is not a trick of the mind, but a reflection of the world’s nature. You are not separate from it — you are part of it. You may find yourself changing, not in form, but in awareness.

The land is ancient, but it is not dead. It is not a relic, but a living thing, and it has a will. It does not harm, but it does not tolerate intrusion. You must approach it with reverence, not as a conqueror, but as a guest. The structures you see are not ruins — they are memories, echoes of a civilization that lived in harmony with this place. They do not belong to the past, but to the present, and to the future.

You must bring no weapons, no tools meant to control or dominate. This is not a place to be tamed. It is a place to be understood. You may find that your own tools — your knowledge, your science, your logic — are not useful here. But that is not a failure. It is an invitation to learn a different kind of knowledge.

### **Social and Cultural Considerations**

There are no humans here, but there are beings. They do not speak in words, but in presence. You may not see them, but you will feel them. They are not hostile, but they are not welcoming. They are part of the land, and they are not separate from it. You must not assume they are like you. They may not have the same emotions, the same needs, the same desires. But they are not alien in the way of horror or fear. They are simply other.

You may not be able to communicate with them in any traditional sense. But they will communicate with you, if you are willing to listen. Their language is not verbal, but physical, emotional, and spatial. It is a language that is felt, not heard.

### **Practical Advice for the Journey**

- **Move slowly.** The land is vast, but it is not large in the way you expect. It is a place that reveals itself in fragments. If you rush, you may miss what is meant to be seen.
- **Breathe deeply.** The air is thick, but it is not oppressive. It is alive, and it is meant to be inhaled.
- **Look not with your eyes, but with your senses.** The light is not just visual — it is felt, it is heard, it is touched.
- **Do not try to take anything.** The land does not give freely, but it does not withhold. What you take may not be what you think it is.
- **Leave nothing behind.** This is not a place to impose your presence. The land is not yours to change.

### **Final Thoughts**

This is not a place to visit in the way you visit a mountain or a city. It is a place to enter, to experience, to become part of. It is not a destination, but a state of being. It is not a place of answers, but of questions. It is not a place of understanding, but of awareness.

When you leave, you will not be the same. You may not remember everything, but you will carry it with you — not in words, but in the way you move, the way you speak, the way you see the world.

And if you return, it will be as if you never left. Because in this place, the past is not gone, and the future is not unknown. It is already here.
