This is a place that remembers, and its memory is dense, almost oppressive.

The stone beneath your feet is not inert. It is folded and creased as if the earth itself were sleeping, and you are walking on its skin. Other areas are fractured, pitted, or broken—evidence of something older, something that once stood, only to be consumed by time. The walls rise in jagged arcs, some leaning, some shattered, suggesting that this place was once more than a hollow—it was a *container*, perhaps, of something that no longer exists.

### **Preparing the Body**

Your physical preparation should not be about strength or endurance in the usual sense. This is not a place for hikers or climbers. Fire may not be welcome here, or may react in ways you cannot predict. Bring a small light, but not a strong one—because the darkness here is not void, but a *presence*, and light may not be the answer.

If you come with the need to control, you will find that control is an illusion here.

### **Preparing the Spirit**
