### **Physical Preparation**

It's a cold that clings, that moves with the land, that seeps into your bones. You will need **insulated clothing**—not the kind you’d wear hiking, but something designed for **hypothermic environments**, with thermal layers, moisture-wicking fabric, and airtight seals to prevent the **mineral-rich liquid** from seeping into your gear. This is not water. It is a thick, oily, **bioluminescent fluid** that can be corrosive, sticky, and possibly even alive. You will need **waterproof and chemical-resistant suits**—preferably with a self-contained oxygen system, because the air, while breathable, is damp and rich with **unidentified particulates**. You might feel a sense of **pressure or dizziness** from the unfamiliar atmosphere.

You should also be prepared for **uneven terrain**. The ground is not solid, but shifting. It breathes, it moves, and it can collapse. Sturdy, flexible boots with **grippy soles** are essential. You may find your footing changing mid-step. A **retractable climbing device or exoskeleton** could be useful. The city is a **vertical labyrinth**, with structures that rise and fall, roots that coil into labyrinths, and bridges that stretch in impossible directions. You must be **adaptable**—not just in movement, but in mindset.

These are not hallucinations, but manifestations of a world that is **not just alien**, but **intentional**. You must be ready for the possibility that you are being watched—by something that is neither purely organic nor purely mechanical. You must be ready to **not know**.
