### Physical Preparation

You will need **clothing that breathes**, because the air is thick with presence, not just moisture. Your gear must be lightweight, flexible, and capable of withstanding a heat that is not oppressive but **inevitable**. The temperature is warm, but it doesn't burn — it *invites* you to soften, to melt into its rhythm. You'll want breathable fabrics, but not too much. The place doesn’t need you to stay cool. It needs you to be **present**.

Your footwear must be sturdy, but not rigid. The ground is not wet, but it is **saturated with memory** — and that memory may shift beneath your feet. The rocks are sharp, but they do not erode. They remember. They hold. So your shoes should not be fragile. You'll want something that allows you to move with purpose, but also with care. You may not be walking on ground — you may be walking **through** it, or **within** it.

You’ll need a **water source**, but not one you can drink from. This is not water in the way you know it — it is a **murmuring presence**, a living thing that clings and weeps. It may not be safe to drink, but it will be **present**. You may want to bring a container, not to hold the water, but to hold the memory of the place.

You will not need a compass, but you may need a **sense of direction that is not spatial**. This is not a place you can map. It’s a place that maps you. Your internal compass — your sense of self, your place in the world — will be tested. You must come with the ability to **feel your way** through it, not just see it.
