And now, they hum, not with power, but with memory.

Not to you, not to the moment, but to something that has been here longer than time. The whispers in the dark are not voices, but *echoes*—of machinery, of biology, of something that no longer has a name.

It is the breath of something that has been alive for too long, that has seen too much, that has learned too much.

It is a greenish haze that moves like a living thing, that shifts with the slow rhythm of the place. It does not illuminate; it *suggests*. It does not reveal; it *hides*. This is not a place of beings, but of *presence*. It is not a place of motion, but of *potential*. It is not a place of sound, but of *resonance*. And it is not a place of being, but of *being seen*.
