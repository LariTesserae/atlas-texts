It lives in the hum of the pipes, in the way the steam rises and falls like breath. It lives in the shadows that shift when no one is there, in the way the light bends and twists, as if the space itself is watching. It lives in the quiet, in the spaces between sounds, in the places where the silence is not empty but full of something just beyond perception.

It is the ghost of a city that was never meant to be lived in, but only to be used. And yet, it has not been used for a long time. It has been left to itself, to the slow erosion of time, to the weight of its own existence.

It is the memory of tools, the residue of labor, the lingering idea of a system that once had a function. It is the sound of a place that was never meant to be quiet, but has become so. It is the weight of something that was built with intention, but never with care.

It lives in the cracks of the concrete, in the growth of moss that tries to reclaim the space, in the way the vines creep through the metal. It lives in the damp air, in the heat that clings like a second skin. It is not a being, but it is not nothing. It is the kind of presence that makes you feel watched, even when you are alone. It is the kind of place that makes you wonder if you are the intruder, or if you are simply the first to arrive.
