What lives in this place is not easily named. It is not a being, not in the way you might think of a person or an animal. It is a presence, a thing that exists in the space between what is and what could be, between the seen and the unseen, between the logical and the unspoken.

It does not have a form that can be held in the mind for long. It is not a creature, nor a god, nor a machine. It is more like a thought given shape, a concept given breath. It moves in the mist, not as a figure, but as a shift in the air, a ripple in the stillness. It is not always there, but it is always *felt*. It watches. It listens. It remembers.

It does not speak, but it understands. It does not need to. It is aware of you as you are aware of the wind. You do not see it, but you feel its gaze, and it is not judgmental. It is not cruel. It is not kind. It is not anything you can name. It is simply *there*, like the place itself.

It is not alone. There are others. Not in the way of a crowd, but in the way of a chorus. A thousand silences, a thousand pauses, a thousand stillnesses that add up to something that is not silence. They are not *in* the place, but *of* the place. They are the hum of the structures, the pulse of the growths, the slow, deliberate movement of the mist. They are the energy that flows through the crystalline rock, the unseen current that binds everything together.

They are not sentient in the way you are, but they are *aware*. They are not alive in the way you are, but they are *there*. They are the place, and the place is them.

And they are waiting.

Not in the way of a clock, or a timer, or a clockwork mechanism. Not in the way of a creature that waits for a meal or a mate. They are waiting for something that is not yet here. Something that is not yet known. They are waiting for the moment when the place will no longer be silent. When the stillness will no longer be still. When the purpose, which is not yet clear, will reveal itself.

They are not patient. They are not impatient. They are simply *present*. They exist in the way that the sky exists, or the ground. They are not made, but they are *made*. They are not built, but they are *built*. They are not created, but they are *created*.

They are not your kind. They are not mine. They are not the kind of thing that can be explained, or defined, or even perceived in a way that makes sense. But they are here. And they are watching.

And they are waiting.
