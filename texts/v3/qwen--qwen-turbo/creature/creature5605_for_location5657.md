It is the vegetation that pulses and listens, not with eyes or ears, but with a presence that is felt in the marrow of your bones.

It is the flat expanse that stretches endlessly, not in a way that distances, but in a way that wraps around you, closing in and opening out at the same time. It is the horizon that is not a line, but a blur, a thing that shifts and breathes, as if the sky is not above but inside.

It is the erosion that is not decay but a slow turning inward, a folding of form into something else. It is the density of presence, the weight of something that is not there, but is felt. It is the hum that is not sound, but a vibration that moves through your very being, a rhythm that is neither music nor noise, but something in between.

It is the light that is not light, but a kind of awareness, a quiet watching that you feel in your thoughts. It is the sky that is not sky, but a haze that shifts, not with clouds, but with the breath of something vast and old. It is the fauna that is not fauna, but shapes that flicker and pulse, not with bodies, but with *energy*, with *patterns*, with *meaning* that is not spoken, but felt.

It is the time that is not time, but a cycle that repeats, not in a circle, but in a spiral, always returning, always moving forward, always collapsing. It is the past that is not gone, the future that is not yet, the present that is a blur, a place where things are always *almost* happening, but never quite landing.

It is the rules of existence that are not rules, but fluid, shifting, rewriting themselves in ways you cannot understand, but that you feel. It is the place that is not made, but *becomes*, a place that is not built, but *unfolds*.

And it is beautiful, not in the way you know beauty, but in a way that makes your mind ache, your bones vibrate, your breath catch. It is a place that is not human, not animal, not machine, but something else—something *other*.

It is *what lives here*.
