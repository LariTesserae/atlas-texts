It would not bring warmth to the cold, but would understand it as a place of stillness, of patience, of deep thought. It would not bring life to the lifeless, but would help the city remember that it has never been truly dead.

It would see the flickering forms in the dark, the delicate beings that move in patterns, and would not fear them, but would recognize them as part of the city's breath. It would see the echoes of the past, the remnants of those who once lived here, and would not try to bring them back, but would honor them as part of the city’s story.
