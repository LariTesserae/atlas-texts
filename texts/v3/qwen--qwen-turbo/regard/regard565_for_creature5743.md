There are no true animals, no true plants, no true structures. What lives here is a *system*. It is not separate from the place, but *of* the place. It is the place, and the place is it. They are changed, not in a way that can be named, but in a way that lingers, like a dream that refuses to fade.

This is what lives here: a world that is not a world, a presence that is not a presence, and yet, it is everything.
