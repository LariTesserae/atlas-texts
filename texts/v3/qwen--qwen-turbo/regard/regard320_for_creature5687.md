**What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do**

It does not shape the world with hands, but with awareness.

It has been present when the first breath of life stirred in the dark, and when the last echo of a dying world faded into silence. It has no need to act, but it does, not out of obligation, but out of curiosity. Out of a kind of silent, patient love.

It is already there, woven into the fabric of the place, as much a part of it as the slow dripping of water, the whisper of wind through the bones of the mountain, the pulse of the deep thing that sleeps beneath.

It watches. It listens. It feels.

The presence of the power does not disturb the place, but it does not go unnoticed. The light bends slightly, the shadows shift just enough to acknowledge, the air is thick with a quiet understanding.
