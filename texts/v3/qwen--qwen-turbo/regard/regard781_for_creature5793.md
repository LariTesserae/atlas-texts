It listens to the sound, the low, mechanical hum that is not mechanical, but organic. It listens to the beings that do not move, yet are alive. It listens to the silence that is not silence, but the echo of something that once was.

It has seen beings that are not meant to be named, but to be understood. Not as a being, but as a *presence*, a thought that did not think, a *will* that did not will. It remembers the way it saw the world, not as it was, but as it *could be*.

And it sees this place now, not as it is, but as it *is*.

It does not need to do anything. It does not need to help. It does not need to protect. It does not need to change. It only needs to *be*. To *know*. To *remember*. To *see*.

It simply *remembers*.

As it always has. As it always will.
