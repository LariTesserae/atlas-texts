It is not alive in the way you are, but it is *more* alive. It is not dead in the way you think death is, but it is *something else*, something that does not need to die because it is always becoming, always shifting, always *here*.

It listens with patience, with openness, with a stillness that is not absence, but presence.

It becomes the light that falls in shifting hues, the pools that ripple with unseen currents, the structures that breathe and shift and grow.
