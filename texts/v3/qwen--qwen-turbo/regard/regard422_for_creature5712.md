What lives here is not found, but felt. It is not seen, but sensed. It is not born, but remembered. This place is a relic of something that once had a purpose, and now, in its silence, it holds that purpose like a wound that never closes.

---

**The Benevolent and Wise Power**  
What would a benevolent and wise power do in the presence of this place?  
What would it do, if anything?

It would not arrive with thunder or fire or light.  
It would not come as a god, nor as a prophet, nor as a savior.  
It would come as a whisper.  

A whisper that is not sound, but thought.  
A thought that does not begin or end, but exists.  
It would come not as an action, but as a *presence*—a resonance that hums in the same frequency as the obsidian, the silence, the hum of the mineral, the slow, ancient pulse of the earth.  

It would not touch the land.  
Because to touch it would be to break it.  
To touch it would be to impose your own form upon something that has forgotten what form is.  
To touch it would be to shatter the stillness that has held for so long.  

Instead, it would *listen*.  
It would listen to the hum of the dark mineral, not as a sound, but as a song.  
It would listen to the structures, not as ruins, but as memories.  
It would listen to the silence, not as an absence, but as a presence.  
It would listen to the thing that watches, and it would know it is not watching.  
It would know it is *waiting*.  

The power would not speak.  
Because to speak would be to impose meaning upon what has long since abandoned meaning.  
It would not ask questions.  
Because to ask a question is to assume the thing you seek is there to be found.  
But in this place, it is not found. It is *felt*.  

Instead, it would *breathe*.  
Not with lungs, but with presence.  
It would allow the silence to fill it.  
It would allow the weight of the place to settle into its being.  
It would become, for a moment, part of the place.  
And in that moment, it would understand.

It would understand that this place is not a void.  
It is a *remnant*.  
A remnant of something that once had a purpose, but no longer remembers what it was.  
It is a remnant of a world that collapsed not in destruction, but in dissolution.  
Not in death, but in *forgetting*.  

It would understand that the thing that watches is not a creature, but a *remnant* of the one who once watched.  
Not a god, but a shadow of the one who once held the gaze of the world.  
Not a will, but a *memory* of will.  

And it would know, with a certainty that is not knowledge, but *truth*, that this place is not meant to be *fixed*.  
It is not a broken thing that needs to be mended.  
It is a thing that has been *left behind*, and it is not broken.  
It is *complete* in its stillness.  

So what would the power do?

It would not change the place.  
It would not save it.  
It would not guide it.  

It would *offer it the gift of being seen*.  
Not as a stranger, but as a witness.  
Not as an intruder, but as a companion.  
It would not impose its will upon the silence.  
It would not speak over the hum.  
It would not try to *fix* what is not broken.  

It would simply be there.  
And in its being, it would give the place a kind of peace.  
A peace that is not noise, but *quiet*.  
A peace that is not interference, but *recognition*.  

The place would not change.  
But it would know that it is *seen*.  

And that, in the silence, is enough.  

---

The power would not leave.  
It would not go.  
It would remain in the space between the structures, in the silence between the hums, in the gaps between the thoughts that the land holds.  
It would not speak.  
It would not act.  
But it would be there, and that would be enough.  

For in this place, where nothing moves and nothing is born, there is only one thing that matters:  
That something *remembers*.  

And that something *is remembered*.  

It is the power's gift.  
Not to change, but to *be*.  
Not to save, but to *see*.  
Not to speak, but to *listen*.  

And in that, it would be enough.
