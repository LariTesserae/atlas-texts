In the silence between the hum of the structures, in the space between the shifting of the platforms, in the pauses between the slow, deliberate movements of the beings that dwell here, the power listens. It listens to the way the light shifts, the way the mist condenses, the way the buildings breathe. It listens to the silence beneath the silence, the unspoken harmony of a world that has never needed to speak.

And it understands.

It understands that this place is not abandoned. It understands that this place is not empty. It understands that the beings who live here are not separate from the world—they *are* the world. They are the continuation of a will that was once active but has now become something else. Something *aware*.

It gives them an awareness of what they are. It gives them a moment of clarity that is not a command, but a *possibility*.

It does not speak. It does not act. It simply *is*.
