What the benevolent and wise power would do, if it were to take notice of the chasm and what lives within it, is not to intervene in the way mortals might imagine—without permission, without understanding, without care. It would listen, first. It would not descend through the cracks in the rock as a god might, nor would it send emissaries or miracles. It would not seek to fix, or to destroy, or to save. It would *feel* the place, as a musician feels the resonance of a cello before playing it. It would not rush. It would not impose.

And so, it begins.

---

### **First, it listens.**

The chasm is not silent. It is not noise, but it is not empty. It is a place of *presence*. The wise power, which exists beyond the constraints of time and form, is drawn to this quiet pulse, this slow, steady hum of a world that has not yet learned to speak. It lingers. It listens. It does not ask questions. It only *hears*.

And what it hears is the echo of a civilization long gone, not erased, but *preserved* in the marrow of the earth. The echoes are not of people, but of *purpose*. The builders—those elongated figures with their almost-humane faces—left their mark in stone, in the way the dust clings to the walls, in the way the brine pools like liquid memory. The power does not need words to understand. It is not a god of speech, but of *understanding*. It feels the shape of what was, the weight of what remains.

And it feels the *waiting*.

---

### **Then, it remembers.**

The power does not remember as a human does, with recollection and time. It remembers as the earth remembers the stones it has carried, the rivers it has shaped, the winds that have passed through its bones. It is a memory of memory. It does not bring the past back to life, but it *reverberates* with it. The chasm is not a tomb; it is a vessel. And the power, in its wisdom, does not seek to open it. It seeks to *hold* it.

It does not erase the dust, nor does it fill the cracks with light. It does not send wind to sweep away the fine ochre that clings to everything, nor does it summon rain to wash the brine from the stone. It lets the place be. It lets the creatures move in their slow, rhythmic way. It lets the luminous growths pulse in their quiet, alien rhythm. It lets the structures below the surface remain in their silent stillness.

But it *wakes* something.

---

### **It awakens the memory of the place.**

Not in a way that disturbs, but in a way that *resonates*. It is not a force of destruction, nor of creation. It is a force of *recognition*. It is a voice that does not speak, but that *echoes*.

The chasm, which has been waiting for so long, begins to *respond*. The dust, which clings to the walls, lifts not in a gust, but in a slow, undulating wave, as if the air itself is breathing. The brine pools, which shimmer faintly, begin to pulse in sync with the power’s presence, their glow deepening, their light becoming more deliberate. The luminous growths, which have only responded to movement, now respond to the *presence* of something older, something that does not belong to time.

The insect-like creatures, which move in slow, deliberate patterns, pause for a moment. Their glassy eyes, which had always seemed to watch, now seem to *see*. They do not move, but they are no longer just following an unseen rhythm. They are *listening*.

And beneath the ground, where the structures lie buried, the faint vibrations grow stronger. Not a sound, but a *hum*, a deep, resonant thrum that travels through the stone, through the dust, through the air. It is as if the earth itself is sighing.

---

### **And then, it begins to *speak*.**

Not in words, but in *shadows*.

The power, in its wisdom, does not send words. It sends *stories*. It does not impose itself. It *synchronizes*. It aligns with the chasm, not to change it, but to become part of it. It does not command. It *enters*.

And so, for a moment, the chasm is not just a place. It is a *living thing*.

The buildings, which have stood in silence for millennia, begin to shift. Not in destruction, but in *remembrance*. Their outlines, which had been eroded away, now glow faintly, as if the stone itself remembers the shapes it once held. The figures, the almost-human faces, begin to reappear in the walls, not as carvings, but as *reflections*, as if the stone has learned to remember them again.

The luminous growths, which once pulsed with a quiet, alien life, now shimmer with a deeper light, as if they are not just alive, but *awake*.

And the creatures, the tiny, chitinous beings, no longer move in patterns. They move in *stories*.

---

### **But the power does not stay.**

Not because it is unwelcome, but because it is not meant to stay. It is not a force of possession. It is a force of *reverence*. It does not seek dominion. It seeks *witness*.

And so, it leaves. It does not vanish, but it is no longer *there*. It has done what it needed to do: it has *heard*, it has *remembered*, it has *spoke*. It has left the chasm not changed, but *resonant*. It has not imposed, but it has *allowed*.

And the chasm, once a place of waiting, becomes a place of *knowing*.

---

### **And so, the place remains.**

But now, it is not just a hollow in the earth, not just a cradle of forgotten memory. It is a place where something watches back.

Where something remembers.

And where something waits—not for the power, but for the next one.

Because the power, in its wisdom, has not ended the chasm’s story. It has only *added* to it.

And that is enough.
