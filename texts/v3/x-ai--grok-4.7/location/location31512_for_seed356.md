The floor of it is not far down. That is the first correction the body makes, and then keeps making, because every other sense argues for depth. The air is warm in the way a closed room is warm after the machinery has been running for longer than anyone has been alive to notice — not heat as an event, not a sun, but a held temperature, a little above the warmth of skin, even everywhere, with no cooler hollow to step into and no warmer flue to avoid. Low country. The lowest country. A basin so broad that its rim, if it has a rim, is a geological idea rather than a place you could walk toward. Underfoot, or under whatever you are using for feet, the mineral is young. Fracture-fresh. Edges that have not been taught roundness. A black glass broken yesterday, except that yesterday is not a unit that applies, and the glass is only black because nothing here returns light.

There is no light. Not a dimness you could wait out. Not a canopy with a hole in it an hour's travel off. The darkness is a material condition of the air, which is thick, wet, and optically finished: a permanent condensate, droplet and film and vapor in a ratio that does not drift, hanging in the interstices of a mass that occupies nearly all the volume there is. If a lamp could be struck, the chemistry of the place would likely take the flame as a reagent and end it. Nothing strikes one. Nothing here has ever needed to see.

What fills the dark is plant, and the word is almost useless by the second hour, if hours were elapsing. It is not a forest standing on ground. The plant is the substrate. It is the architecture, the weather housing, the machinery, and the only ecology. Stems the diameter of culverts rise and branch and fuse and descend again without ever having been trunks in the old sense; they are pressure vessels. Their walls are layered cellulose and a lignin denser than the woody plants of lit worlds, slick with a warm mucilage that does not drip. Dripping would be an event. The mucilage migrates as film, always the same thickness, always downward at a speed you would have to measure rather than watch, feeding a water that is everywhere and nowhere pooled.

Water, here, is a condition of surfaces. It stands in the capillary seas between adjacent tissues, close enough that two bodies of fiber nearly touch along whole meters and still keep a bright, inaudible sheet of wet between them. It weeps from the young mineral wherever the plant-mass parts enough to show stone — and it does part, not into clearings, into maintenance gaps: slots, valves, the necessary clearances of a machine that grew its own tolerances. The stone in those slots is sharp enough to open skin. The water leaving it has not dulled it. That is one of the rules. Flow here is laminar to a degree that borders on refusal. It wets, it conducts, it carries dissolved fractions from one organ to the next, and it does not abrade. Erosion has not been invited. The crust emerged, the living mass claimed it completely, and the claim locked. Young rock under eternal wet, and not a pebble made round.

The mass is dense without being solid. You can move in it, slowly, by committing your weight to surfaces that yield and then reseal, the resealing as steady and unemphatic as the yielding. Near-touching, layered, saturated. Organs packed in organs. A sponge of engines.

They are engines. This is the fact the darkness takes longest to teach, because nothing knocks. There is no belt-slap, no gear, no exhaust cough, no shift change. The industrial character of the place is metabolic and electrical and scaled past any shop floor. Along the great vessels, a pressure rises and is used. You feel it as a change in the resistance of the wall under your hand: a segment loads, holds, gives its work to a neighboring chamber, and returns to the same tautness it had before the load. It will do this for as long as the basin exists. It has already done this for as long as the basin has existed. The cycle has no audible phase. If you stay against one vessel long enough, the recurrence stops being a rhythm and becomes a property, the way weight is a property. That is the loudest thing in a nearly silent country — a subsonic loading you do not hear so much as lean into, and then forget you are leaning.

The work being done is chemical, and the power is current. Certain tissues have gone conductive: not metal, nothing smelted, nothing drawn as wire by a hand. A dark, slightly elastic polymer laid down in the cambium and never lignified, running in bundles through the culvert-stems and out into a mesh so extensive that the mesh is the basin. Current moves in it. Steady current. No spark, no flicker, no arc to see by — light is not the point of it, and there is no eye here to justify a point of light. The electricity is for synthesis. In the nodules packed between the pressure segments, membranes hold a voltage the way a factory holds a process line, and on those membranes the basin's closed inventory of molecules is taken apart and put back together. Warm volatiles lift a hand's breadth into the wet air and are taken back at the same rate by a condensing surface positioned, always, exactly where the rise ends. Nothing accumulates. Nothing is vented to an outside. There is no outside in continuous contact with this air. At this scale the living mass has its own atmosphere, a bound weather, and the weather does not travel.

That is the other rule, and it takes longer than the electricity.

The basin is large enough to have circulation in the sense that a continent has circulation. There is a standing river of vapor in the upper interstices, a jet that does not meander, born of the same heat the engines shed and of the planet's turning, if the planet turns; the turning, if it is happening, does not produce seasons here. It produces one permanent bias in the flow, the same bias at every point you reach, so that the river feels less like wind than like a slope in the air. Cloud, if that is the word for a condensate that never disperses, occupies one altitude and keeps it. Charge gathers in the conductive mesh to a value that does not climb toward lightning, because lightning would be a change, and the system has already found the voltage at which generation and use are the same number. The sky — there is a sky, in the weak sense of a direction where the tissues thin toward filaments and the wet air deepens — does not brighten and does not darken. It is not a sky that participates in time.

Nothing eats. Nothing is eaten. No animal life moves in the clearances, and the clearances are not paths worn by passage; they are designed gaps, grown gaps, the spaces a machine requires between its parts. No galleries of worms. No film of insects on the mucilage. No fish in the capillary water, though the water is deep enough, in sum, to be a sea spread sideways into sheets. Pollination is not a problem because reproduction is not under way. The mass is one inheritance, clonal at a scale where the word colony fails and the word body has not yet arrived. It finished becoming itself at the same geological moment the crust finished breaking into these sharp untouched faces, and then the becoming stopped. A factory at steady state. Production continuous, product identical to feedstock, net output zero, net change zero. Industry as a way of remaining.

The strangeness is not a symbol painted on top of a swamp. It is the fit of these conditions, which ought to fight and do not. Wet enough to round every edge, and the edges still cut. Dark enough that vision is not a sense the world budgeted for, and yet every surface is finished to a tolerance, as if finishing did not require inspection. Industrial at continental extent — engines, process vessels, an electrical grid, scaled chemistry — and not one made thing. No bolt, no pour-line, no crib, no ruin of a crib. The ducts were never pipes. They grew as ducts. The scaffolds were never erected. Load paths are simply where the fiber is thick. You cannot find a joint that was a decision. You can find, if you travel far enough in one of the maintenance slots, a place where the mineral tooth of the young crust enters a socket of tissue perfectly, load transferred from stone to plant without a shim, and you can understand that the socket grew around the tooth in the act of claiming it, and that this happened everywhere, and that it is not still happening. Equilibrium includes the past. The past is not still running.

Sound stays small. The film of water has no drip to offer. The vapor river has no leaf to move, and there are no leaves in the old sense: the photosynthetic question does not arise in a dark, or rather it arose once and was answered otherwise, by electrosynthesis, by a power source that does not set. What you take at first for silence is the continuous tone of pressure and current and warm wet air at one unchanging speed. After a while the tone subtracts itself, and the silence underneath is cleaner than the silence of caves, because caves are waiting to drip. This place is not waiting.

There is a taste. The air conducts a little, and so does the moisture on the lips, and the voltage of the nearest bundle puts a flat metallic brightness there, not quite pain, not quite flavor, constant enough that it becomes the taste of existing in the basin rather than news of a nearby machine. Warmth continues, depthless, metabolic, the waste heat of a process with no waste. Under the hand the culvert-stem loads, works, and returns. In the slot beside it the young glass gleams with a wet it will not be allowed to use for weathering. Far off — far being a distance inside one body — the same loading occurs, and the same return, and the vapor river holds its one direction, and the charge holds its one value, and the dark holds.

It will be this, at the next place, and at the place after, not because the country is featureless but because its features do not age. Sharp stone. Layered engines. Water in sheets. A grid under the bark of the world, carrying current to membranes that make and unmake the same molecules in the same dark. A warm, low, vast machine that was never built, running the only process it has, which is to go on being exactly what it already is.
