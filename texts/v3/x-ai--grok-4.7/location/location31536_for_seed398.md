The mountain has not finished learning its own height. It still pushes, black and glassy, into air too thin to be weather, and the sky around its shoulders is a darker blue than distance usually allows, the color of a bruise that will not settle. The heat is not the sun’s. It comes out of the rock, a dull fever of formation, and what little wind arrives this high leaves thinner than it came, startled into shimmer above spines that have not yet been taught roundness.

Recently, a blister in the flank failed. Not an eruption — a hesitation. Melt rose, thought better of the sky, and withdrew, leaving a pocket whose mouth is a crack you could cover with a hand. The lips are still sharp. Rain, when it climbs this far, does not fall so much as lose its nerve: a brief confused damp beads on the hot glass and is gone before it can be called a drop. The stone accepts it with a sound below hearing, a soft inelastic yielding, and is dry again.

Inside, scale misbehaves.

The crack admits a wrist, a glance, a careful breath. Beyond it the volume is not the volume of the blister that made it, or it is, and something has learned to spend that volume as if it were a fortune. The first fact of the interior is emptiness. A hollow so thorough it feels swept, not abandoned. Heat pools in the lower third and thins as it rises, the way a thought thins when it has been carried too far. The light is brown-dim, filtered through thick volcanic glass and then thinned again, as if brightness were a resource already budgeted elsewhere. You can see, and you cannot see far, and both conditions seem intentional.

Then the edges declare themselves.

They are not walls in any old sense. A line no thicker than a cooled filament runs from a point in the dark to another point, and the air along it is slightly more willing to be a street. Another filament branches. A third refuses a junction, and the refusal is itself a plaza: a flatness with nothing on it, civic only in grammar. The settlement is urban in its relations and nearly void in its mass. Districts occupy almost no matter. What they occupy is permission — this edge answers that edge, this hollow is zoned for passage, this warmer shelf is infrastructure because moisture prefers it. Between the presences, which are few, the emptiness is the majority material. A market, if the local courtesy can be trusted, may contain three things across a long shimmer of heat: a knot of intersecting lines, a cup of dark condensate, and a darkness shaped like a doorway that does not open onto rock. The rest is hollow on purpose, or hollow because purpose here is cheap and matter is not.

Nothing green has ever taken. Nothing has rooted, fruited, or cast a leaf-shadow. There is no animal life, no tick of insect, no track, no shed scale. Films of oxide bloom where a moisture-pulse kissed iron-rich glass and withdrew; they record, they do not grow. A pale condensate — not quite water, carrying a sweet-metal smell under the hot-mineral reek of the chamber — migrates along the filaments on a cycle shorter than a day and longer than a breath. It gathers in pockets. A lens of damp in a downward crook. A sweating seam. A cup that steams without boiling and then, as the cycle turns, is pulled back into the lattice as if the place drank. The drinking is the loudest thing that happens, and it is almost not a sound.

Outside, the peak has wind. In here the wind dies at the crack, and sound is not permitted to accumulate. The filaments take vibration the way they take damp. What you hear, if you stay through a cycle, is the schedule: a soft tick when a line brightens half a shade — not with light, with decision — and a new lane extrudes along a cooling front, sharp as the hour it was thought. A pressure-change, more edit than noise, when a pocket collapses and the heat reclaims the wet. Somewhere in a district reached by not climbing, a terrace realigns. It does not clang. It is the sound of a rule being updated.

The technology does not offer housings, seals, or lamps meant for eyes. What exceeds familiar engineering is the masonry. Gravity is graded by district, so that up is a policy rather than a direction. In the upper terraces — upper in the pocket, and upper again in a mountain already past the breathable world, a doubling that makes weight feel optional — a filament can carry a plaza because the place has agreed, locally, to be lighter. Heat is load-bearing. Some edges are held rigid only by a temperature difference; when the cycle lapses they slacken into air, and for a while that district is only the memory of having been zoned. Near the hottest seams, time is slightly discourteous. A bead falls and arrives having already evaporated. The street is wet in a tense that does not match the room.

The weirdness is not spectacle. It accumulates the way attention does. The crack behind you remains a hand’s width, and also, looked at without turning, it is a distant gate at the end of a long civic axis. Both are true; the settlement spends scale the way it spends light, thriftily, and never quite where you are looking. Maps fail because the place is still emerging. This is a young interior in a young mountain. Corners have not been chamfered by centuries. A spar of glass-and-rule pushes into the hollow while you watch, bright with internal heat at its tip, cooling to a filament as it decides how long it intends to be a street. Nothing has eroded it. Erosion would require a future this rock has not yet kept. The same sharpness is on the peak outside: ridges like unrevised sentences, fracture-faces still legible, a whole altitude of features that have not met enough weather to be simplified. The city and the mountain are not two works. The pocket is where the forming rock first found an interior, and organized it in the only civic grammar it had — edges, permissions, addresses, and a great deal of deliberate nothing.

If there is a population, it is recurrence without bodies. A knot that re-forms in the same relation after every slackening. A cup that sweats on the same beat. A doorway-dark that never opens and is therefore the most stable building in the town. They do not pursue, feed, or flee. They are not animals, and they are not the remains of any. They persist the way a cooling law persists: present again when the conditions that wrote them return. In the long hollows between them the heat moves, the young glass ticks as it finishes becoming law, and moisture arrives, records itself as a darker stain or a rusty film, and is taken back.

The rules are few, and they hold.

Damp is circulation, not climate. It appears, occupies a pocket, leaves a note in the mineral, and is reclaimed. Nothing may root; the heat forbids the old chemistries, and the lattice does not leave gaps where a seed could pretend. Sound stays small even while processes run — extrusion, realignment, the drinking, the graded fall of weight from district to district. Light stays dim because the visible is the remainder. What would have been glare is bound into structure, hoarded in the edges, which is why the streets can be so thin and still be streets.

At the mouth, if you look out, the peak continues its unfinished argument with the sky, sharp, hot, almost empty of everything but altitude. At your back the hidden city goes on being recent: a settlement the size of a held breath and the grammar of a town, layered, addressed, active, and so full of space between its presences that emptiness is what it is mostly made of. A new filament decides its length. A cup sweats. Neither makes enough sound to carry. The mountain, still hot from being born, keeps its first interior, and does not explain it.
