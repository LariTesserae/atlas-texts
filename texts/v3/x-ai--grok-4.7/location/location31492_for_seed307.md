The place is smaller than the breath you spend finding it, and higher than the breath can account for. It opens where a new summit has not finished becoming a summit: a vesicle in glass, pinched off while the stone was still climbing, and still climbing now, so that the little room gains altitude without gaining size. Outside, if outside is the word, the air would be a thin cold thing tearing past a lip of rock too young to have a name. Inside, the birth-heat never left. The walls are hot enough to punish a palm. The light, perversely, is only daylight.

You do not walk in. You are admitted the way a bead is admitted to a flaw. The ceiling is close enough to fog with your heat, and also far enough that a thread of ordinary sky shows through it, blue, cloudless, correctly lit, as if distance were a courtesy the light still observes and nothing else does. Your shadow falls in the usual way. That is the first rule that holds: illumination here is obedient. It arrives, strikes, returns a true color, draws a legible edge. Everything the light reports is already wrong, but the reporting itself is clean. There is no gloom to hide in and no glare to excuse you. You can see the whole pocket, and the pocket is not one size.

The stone is glass, black where it is thick, rust-red where a fracture is still thinner than a nail and still hot from the tear. Nothing has been rounded. Every edge is a decision the rock made minutes ago, or is making as you watch, a conchoidal snap arrested mid-curve. There is no grit yet, no soil, no slope that has agreed to be a floor. The inner surface is all wall, and the wall is sharp. If you shift your weight you learn the youth of it in the skin: a fresh flake lifts under you with almost no pressure, transparent, wicked, and for a moment you are standing on a tool the mountain has not decided to keep. Then the place under the flake knits. The edge is virgin again. Nothing here is worn, and nothing made is allowed to remain made.

Water is everywhere the way sweat is everywhere on skin, not as a pond but as a kept film. It weeps from hairline partings that open when the heat pulses, beads along ridges too new to hold a drop by any honest physics, and runs upward for a finger's length before remembering to fall. It should hiss. It does not. You see the cycle and you do not hear it: a seam brightens, a lens of water swells, the lens flashes to vapor against a throat of hotter glass, the vapor ghosts an inch and condenses again on a cooler bulge, and the bulge drinks it back until the next pulse. The weather of the pocket is only this — heat breathing, water refusing to leave, pressure changing as the summit inches higher around you. Your ears are told to pop and then decline. The quiet is not emptiness. It is a refusal. Motion happens; the sound of motion is made and then not delivered.

There is no green. There is nowhere for green to stand. No fiber, no film that feeds on light and stays still, no crust you could call a plant even as a courtesy. What pales the glass in places is mineral, a wet white bloom of silica tightened out of the weep, soft-looking and hard under the eye, laid down and lifted away again by the same pulse that made it. The light shows it honestly: not moss, not life, a precipitate. Whatever eats here does not eat leaves. There are no leaves in the rules.

What there is, instead, is the crowd.

They cover the inner curve in layers that nearly touch, and in the narrow volume of air they occupy one another without quite agreeing to overlap. At first you take them for the heat-shimmer the light has declined to perform — a crawling in the corners of vision, seed-sized, too many. Then a larger reading resolves, the size of a hand, pressed flat to the glass, and you understand the small ones were not separate. They were the same presence counted at a different scale. This fails as soon as you trust it. A thing the width of your wrist moves through a field of things the width of grains, and the grains do not part. Near-touching is the permanent condition. You cannot find a patch of bare surface that stays bare; the moment a flake lifts and knits, bodies have already returned to it, translucent where the daylight goes through them, amber where they have been drinking the mineral film, dark where they carry a little of the glass inside their outlines.

They have no voices. They turn. Turning is their weather and their speech. A heat-pulse runs the seam and a whole layer rotates toward it, silent as a change in attention. A cooler bulge beads and another layer presses its mouths — if mouths is fair — to the water. You watch one take another. A broader body closes over a narrower one against the red thinness of a fresh fracture. There is no struggle you can hear, no wet sound, no crack. Afterward the narrower one is being unmade, and is also still browsing the silica bloom two layers to the left, not yet arrived at its own ending. Removal does not subtract. That is why the pocket can be this small and this full. Death occurs. Vacancy does not. The crowd is the record of every taking that has not finished propagating through the room, and the room is too broken to propagate anything all the way.

They are the only technology, and they build nothing.

You see it because the light will not let you mistake it. One of the hand-sized readings braces against a ridge and worries at it with a jaw that is not a jaw but a flake of the same glass, lifted, held, used. The edge bites a softer seam. Water under pressure needles out, a bright thread, and is taken in by a dozen bodies before it can fall. The flake is a tool in the strict sense: an edge chosen, applied, improving the reach of a body. Then the body loosens. The flake sinks. The bite in the seam seals as if the cut had been a suggestion the rock declined. There is no scatter of debitage, no pile, no smoothed rest where a maker returns. They do this constantly — you catch it everywhere once you know the gesture, a thousand momentary crafts in a volume you could cover with your arms — and the pocket stays untouched. Knowledge lives in the crowd and goes when the reading of a body goes. Nothing accumulates into a wall, a mark, a path. The young stone helps them. It is still willing to forget.

Stay, and the processes do not settle into a picture.

The summit rises. You feel it as a lengthening in the chest, a thinness arriving and being argued with by the heat. The thread of sky does not widen; if anything the glass knits across it and then splits elsewhere, a new pinprick of ordinary blue, correctly colored, casting a small true shadow of a body that is in two places. Water runs up a facet you had catalogued as down. A predator closes, and the prey browses on, and a third reading of both of them is already at the seam with a fresh flake in its mouth, cutting for the pulse that has not happened and then has happened, the order quietly reversed, the sound still withheld. Your hands report a room. Your eyes, obedient to the light, report a room and a height and a crowd that will not reduce. Between one blink and the next the ceiling is on your knuckle, wet, sharp enough to open you, populated, and the sky-thread is a long way up inside a space that has not grown.

The rule, when it finally declares itself, is not that the place is unreal. It is that only certain agreements were included when the bubble pinched shut. Light agreed. Heat agreed, and has not been revised. Water agreed to stay near. Stone agreed to be new and to take back whatever is made from it. Sound was not invited to the closing, and so the work goes on unheard: the flashing, the feeding, the cuttings, the rise of the peak through the little vault. The living agreed to be many, and did not agree to be countable.

If you leave — if leaving is a direction that still functions — you will carry a cut you did not notice receiving, very clean, from an edge that existed only while it was being used. Outside, the wind over the new summit is loud enough to make you flinch. That sound belongs to a larger world. It cannot get into the pocket. Inside, the crowd has already closed over the place where you were, near-touching, silent, still rising.
