What covers the plain is not a population that arrived and settled. It is a seating. Every animal you can separate with your eye is a place where the seating became thick enough to have an edge.

Lift one, if your nail will take the arris. The shell is a low dome, wet slate gone pale at the crown where the rain polishes and the mill below replaces polish with thickness at the same rate, so the crown never thins and never grows proud of its neighbors. There is no door. The opening is a seam on the side that faces the drift, and the drift is so slight you only believe in it because the seam is always there, never on the other face, as if current had been drawn on the plate before the animal was. Inside, you do not find a life arranged as a story—no gut leading to a future meal, no organ set aside for a season that has not come. You find the same fold copied until the volume is used. Lamellae, packed to a tolerance, each one doing what the last is doing. What the water brings does not pass through and leave. Mineral, warmth, the faint sweetness of metal, enter and stay as structure. Feeding and remaining are one act, and both are already complete. The animal is not getting fed. It is holding the condition of having been fed at the density the drawing required.

Set it back. It seats. It does not resume, because it had not paused. The bright metal under it, younger than the shell, is not a wound. It is the part of the specification the body is not asked to cover, a witness face the mill likes to keep clean, the way a gauge likes an unpainted patch to read against. In an hour the film returns, and the shell is no older.

They touch on every side. That is the first correction to the idea of them as many. A crowd touches and then doesn't; these have been in contact since the shell was thinner than the water film, and the contact has no news left in it. Pressure at the margin is constant. Chemistry at the margin is constant. If anything passes between them it passes the way heat passes through a wall that has already reached the temperature of both rooms—no message, only continuity. You can walk across them and feel, underfoot, not individuals flinching in series but a single give, distributed, as of upholstery cut from one hide and fastened down on a grid. The grid is the machining. The hide grew into it and stopped at the fastener line.

On the uprights the hide changes posture and not kind. The finger-long mouths are the same fold turned vertical, drawn out until each one is almost all surface. They do not comb the air for something missing. There is nothing missing; the rain is the concentration they were shaped at, and their shape is the record of that concentration, held. What you take for feathers are gaskets. They seal the metal against a dryness that is not coming. Densities vary by a hair from sheet to sheet, and if you measure the hair you find it matches the hair of variation in the plate behind—weld bead, extrusion mark, the memory of a pass. They are not decorating the build. They are the build's way of being wet on a wall.

In the channels the seating learns a motion, and the motion is the nearest thing here to behavior, which means it is not behavior. Broad plates lie in the warm run, overlapping until you cannot say whose edge is whose, and once in a long while a plate flexes. The flex takes longer than your patience wants it to. It is not a stroke toward food and not a startle; food is the water, and the water is already inside the flex as much as outside it. Watch a second plate and the flexion is the inverse of the first, a little, as if the two of them were keeping a sum. They are. The channel is specified to resist the flow by a fixed amount, and the bodies are that resistance, and when one thickens by a film the neighboring flex shortens by a film, and the pumps—those low headlands, turning without complaint—meet the same load they were matched to on the day the edge was cut. Appetite, averaged until it is a material, looks like this: not a wanting, a conductance. The animals in the water are how the water agrees to be pumped.

Nothing hunts because hunting would be a deviation with a direction, and direction has been spent. Nothing flees because fleeing would require a self that can be elsewhere, and elsewhere, on this plain, is the same seat with the same rain. You will want, after long enough, to catch one in a private act—an animal doing something not identical to its neighbors, so you can grant it an interior. The plain offers you candidates and then withdraws them. A shell smaller than the rest: you follow the ratio across a gantry, across a block of factory, across a run of pump-curves repeating to the haze, and the smallness keeps its proportion, like a fastener that comes in two lengths. It is not young. Youth would be a change in the ratio over time, and time here is a maintenance interval, not a life history. A plate with a notch in its margin: the notch matches a hob mark in the channel wall, and the next notched plate matches the next hob mark. Injury would scar randomly. These are the print of the tool, worn as anatomy.

Now and then a shell comes free. You hear it, the click that is not for you, shell leaving shell or shell leaving the seat that grew it. It does not lie. The film-conveyors, those closed errands, take what falls; under the deck something mills; the sweetness in the water is that milling returned. If you need the word death, use it for a valve event. The count on the surface does not drop. A seat does not stay empty long enough to be a fact you can bring back to the next gantry. Replacement arrives not as offspring but as continuity of number, a budding that has no parent because the specification is the parent, and the specification does not die and does not conceive. It holds.

Eyes, or what would be eyes if they were for seeing you, occur in the pavement at intervals a machinist would recognize: pigment set in cups, dark, wet, aimed at the fixed sky. They do not follow. They measure the milky light for departure from itself. Your body is a departure. While you stand, a local warmth and a local shade register as error inside tolerance—too brief, too narrow, to trip whatever the cups are for, if they are for anything a response could answer. When you move, the error walks off the cup and the cup returns to the cloud, which has not traveled. You were not observed. You were a fluctuation that corrected. The manners of the plain, the way nothing startles and nothing makes way, are not welcome and not indifference of the feeling kind. They are the result of a sensorium tuned to the set-point and to nothing else. A visitor is not below notice. A visitor is orthogonal to notice.

Under the decks, if you find a way down, you do not find the other population you have been half-expecting—the keepers, the millers, the small hurried lives that in any ordinary ruin would have inherited the machinery. The under-deck is viscera. The same lamella, in the dark, doing the work the upper bodies defer: taking the milled shell, the scraped film, the slow silt of metal, and returning them to the condition in which the surface can stay finished. It is not a separate people serving the first. It is the first, folded under, the way a gut is not a servant of the mouth. Pumps turn on that folding. Factories, if you open a panel, exhale the same wet warmth the decks exhale, and their ducts are not delivering a product to a market that is nowhere. They are closing the loop the cloud opens. The stack light's single color is not a status. It is a pulse you could take at the wrist, if the wrist were the size of a headland.

So the creature, when you finally allow the word, is not any shell. A shell is a pore. The creature is the occupancy at continental scale: pavement, curtain, channel-plate, and the unlit folding beneath, plus the pumps and the cloud insofar as those are organs it did not grow so much as coincide with, having been drawn in the same sheet. It has a metabolism you can point to—the rain, which is its heat leaving and coming home; the sweetness in the water, which is its own spent mineral sent around again; the ankle-depth on the walking deck, which is a level it keeps the way a body keeps a pressure, not by deciding. It does not have a center you will reach by walking. The repetition is the center, everywhere. It does not look out of the pigment cups toward a world beyond the joint, because beyond the joint the floor continues, and continuation is not an outside.

What it has in place of an interior is the refusal to incorporate you. That is the most personal thing on the plain, and it is still not personal. Finished things do not take a new object into their count. Your warmth is equalized. Your shade is closed over. The shells you stood among are already back at the posture of never having been shaded, which is not forgiveness and not forgetting but the absence of a record that was never part of the spec. If anything like a mind is here, it is the mind of a held value: vast, patient, without remainder, aware of deviation only as a quantity to be returned to zero. It does not know you crossed it. It knows the light is correct again.

There is no young of it coming, and no wildness coiled in it waiting for the mills to fail. Failure is not one of its postures. It lives as a completed sentence lives, by not going on to the next, and by being, at every point you test, still exactly the sentence it was. The animals are how that sentence feels to a hand—shelled, feathered, flexed, densely and indifferently alive—and the animal, the one, is the sentence itself, seated in a world that was machined to be its body and then left, sharp-edged, under a cloud that has nowhere else to rain.
