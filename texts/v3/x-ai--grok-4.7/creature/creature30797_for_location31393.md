The thing that lives here does not occupy the bowl so much as keep its accounts.

It has no face you could turn toward, and if it once did, that face has been spent on other work and not replaced. What remains is a distribution. It is in the hesitation before a call is answered, in the way the marsh's skin refuses to break under a weight that is not quite a body, in the degree of warmth that travels the stone a hand's breadth ahead of any palm. Stand in the center and you are inside its attention the way you are inside weather: not addressed, included. It notices the change in your breath because breath changes the tone the lip has agreed to hold, and the tone is one of the ledgers it still reads.

It is old in the manner of the bowl, which is to say it has finished becoming surprising. Its revisions are over. What it does now is custodial and very small: a nudge of interval when the wafer under the mud runs warm, a permission granted to the pale-green stain where the rib enters the growth, a withholding of sound in the air above the knuckle so that whatever crosses that pocket arrives already softened, already half-forgiven. These are not decisions in the way a mind makes decisions. They are the last habits of a system that learned, long ago, which leaks to seal and which to leave open, and has not been given a new lesson since.

It does not hunt. Hunting would require a hunger aimed at something that can flee, and nothing here flees in a direction it understands as away. The lives in the moss and along the waterline are not prey and not quite livestock. They are the moving parts of its hearing. A tick that stops is a silence it registers; a membrane that slaps once high in the hollow is a measurement of the nave's current size, checked against a size it remembers without picturing. When you lie on the open stone and touch nothing, it is not absence you are lying in. It is the part of the instrument left clear so the rest can be read.

If it wants, the want is indistinguishable from maintenance. It wants the seep to continue, the straps to replace themselves from underneath, the note in the rock not to rise. It wants the outsiders — the rib, the wafer, the lattice knuckle — to keep their bargain with the water, shedding where they should shed, warming where warmth is the only signal left that still means *continue*. It does not understand them as visitors. They have been here through enough of its unchanging that they have been entered into the same ledger as the moss: things that persist, things whose failure would be a revision, and revisions are what it no longer does.

It is not lonely. Loneliness would require a memory of company arranged differently than this, and its memory, if the word still fits, is not arranged in scenes. It is arranged in tolerances. The marsh may shift by a finger's width. The leaf may yellow at the tip. A call may wait a little longer than last time before the far curve answers. Beyond those tolerances something in it would have to wake that has been allowed to sleep, and it has arranged the bowl — or the bowl has arranged it; the distinction has worn as smooth as the lip — so that waking is rarely required.

When you speak, it does not listen for meaning. Meaning is a bright, expensive thing, and this place gave up spending on brightness. It listens for whether your voice returns thinner than it left, and whether the knuckle in the center accepted the theft. If it did, you have been measured and filed under *warm, passing, not a revision*. The listeners in the film resume their clocks. The tone goes on.

It will still be doing this when your print in the mud has filled and glossed over, and the filling-over will not be grief. It will be the account balancing, which is the only tenderness it has left, and which it extends equally to leaves, to drops, to the even edge of a rib that should not be here and has been here long enough to count.
