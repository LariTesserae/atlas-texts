The basin holds because something in it is still choosing the basin over every other fate.

You learn that the choosing has a body only after you have mistaken it for weather, for the slow circulation under the sheet, for the way the water returns from no inlet when the film draws down. Those are its effects. The body is harder to isolate than an effect, because it has spent the length of the holding becoming coextensive with what it holds. It does not stand in the basin the way the waders stand, one to a territory, nor drift the air the way the moth-things drift. It occupies the vertical the small life occupies, and the spacing the large life honors, and the functions the spindle and the arch and the warm plate still perform — not by inhabiting them as a hermit inhabits a ruin, but by having grown until instrument and organism are no longer separable categories. Take the spindle away in your mind and the moisture still sorts. Stop the thread in the arch-gap and the schools still divide. The plate could cool and the moss-spiral would keep the low lip honest for a while on habit alone. The choosing has been copied out of the devices and into living tissue so thoroughly that the devices are now courtesies, reminders, a few insistences left running because stopping them was never part of the original decision and starting them again would require a memory of how.

What walks here, when it walks, is a pace rather than a figure.

You will be looking at a patch of the sheet that seems only bright, and the brightness will have a center that is not the sky's center. The center moves. Not as a swimmer moves, with a wake and a recovery, but as a preference moves through a crowd: the glass-shrimp ease a finger's width off their line, the flatworms on the stone nap hesitate and then continue in a corrected direction, the hanging larvae in a hollow drift a degree toward the rim or away from it, and through all of that small compliance there is a warmth slightly warmer than the basin's warmth, and a pressure like the moth-wing's pressure, distributed, never localized enough to point at. Follow it and you are following a corridor of adjustments. The corridor has a length. Sometimes it is no longer than a shield-leaf's shadow. Sometimes it runs the basin from the spindle to the low lip and back, and the animals along it are briefly more ordered than the standing seethe, ranked for a moment as if a current had them, though the film shows no current you can see. Then the ordering loosens. The preference has sat down, or gone under, or become for a while indistinguishable from the patrol that clears the water — and perhaps it is that patrol, the large slow thing you have only ever seen as a moving window of clarity, except that the window, watched long enough across several condensation cycles, does not forage. It does not take. It arranges. Nutrients move because bodies move out of a path, and the path is not toward food. The path is toward evenness.

Scale is the wrong question, and also the only one that keeps returning.

At the film it is small, or it is willing to be small: a coherence among shrimp that lasts a few heartbeats and releases them unchanged, none missing, none singled out. At the moss-islands it is a breathing you already noticed and assigned to "what lives in them," which is true and insufficient. The islands rise and settle on the condensation clock, all of them, even the ones too far apart for any shared water to couple their timing, and the coupling is not mechanical. Brush one island flat and the others do not flinch. Wait. The flattened one returns to the common phase without being pushed by anything you can see, as if the phase were a fact they all consult, and the consultation has a direction — from no center you can find, toward every island at once, or from all of them inward to a attention that does not need a location to be one attention. Higher, in the bright air, the moth-things' routes are the most legible scripture it keeps. They do not fly randomly between the granted trees. Over a long enough count of cycles their passages redraw the same loose lattice, a mesh in the air cousin to the mesh the dew declines to cover on the three flags, and when a moth-thing dies — folded into a crown that was not a leaf, or simply finished, shedding its shine into the mist — the lattice does not tear. Another body takes the leg of the route within a cycle, not by watching, not by inheritance you could call learning. The route was vacant and then occupied, the way a measurement, abandoned, is still the measurement.

The large browser with the wet-slate hide is the easiest to misread, because it looks like an animal and behaves like one: head down, mouth working the film's thinner margins, a territory the size of its patience. Watch the head. It lifts at intervals that match nothing in its feeding. It lifts when the sheet is about to draw down, before the draw-down has declared itself to your eye. It lifts when the thread in the arch-gap spins a fraction faster, a change so slight you will think you imagined the first ten instances and believe the eleventh. It does not look at the arch. It has no need. Whatever informs the lift is already in it, or it is already in whatever informs the lift. On the compression, when the fauna seethe into the hollows and the density becomes briefly appalling, the browser does not join the seethe and does not flee it. It stands where the film will be last to fail, and smaller bodies accumulate against its legs as against a harmless obstacle, and it allows this, shifting only enough to keep gill-slits or spiracles — you cannot decide which, the hide is too continuous — clear of the press. Mercy is not the word. Capacity is closer. It can carry, for the length of a thin-water pulse, the weight of other lives treating it as terrain, and it was shaped, or shape itself, to be carryable-against.

None of these are servants. That is the correction that arrives late and then will not leave.

The shrimp are shrimp. Open one — you will not, but the thought experiments itself — and you would find shrimp organs, a gut, a nerve cord adequate to a nail-length animal that must not be eaten before it has reproduced. The moth-things are complete in their own economy of condensation and wing-edge prism. The waders strike and swallow and hold their territories with the small wrongness in the neck that you noticed on the first day, and that wrongness is theirs, a local adaptation to a prey that telegraphs itself as a shiver of light rather than as a splash. Nothing here is a puppet with the strings above the mist. The choosing does not override an animal into a tool. It biases. A school that might have drifted into the arch-gap's refusal drifts, over generations of seconds, a safer way, and the individuals who drifted the safer way are the ones still present to drift again, except that the selection is too fast and too directional to be selection alone. Eggs are placed a thumb's width off the place where eggs were placed last cycle, toward the spindle or away from the low lip, and the placement is ordinary ovipositor work, and the thumb's width is not ordinary. Over a season of cycles the nursery of the basin migrates without any nursery-tender you can watch at work. The animals remain animals. Their freedom is intact at the scale of a meal and a mating and a startle that does not come, because startle was edited out of the basin's sound long ago. Their freedom has a prevailing wind. The wind is the inhabitant.

If it has a center, the center is not a organ you could lift out and have the rest die.

You will hunt for one anyway, because the mind wants a heart for a system that behaves like a mind. The spindle is the obvious candidate and the wrong one: cooler, clearer, doing a sorting the biology has learned to echo, but the spindle does not know the waders' schedule and does not grieve — if grief is imaginable here — when a dome of shield-leaves finally splits and fails and is recruited into moss before it can stain the sheet. The patrol under the film is a better candidate, that slow lens of displaced clarity. Stay with it. Match your condensation-counts to its circuit. It is regular. It is also plural. On some cycles there are two windows, far apart, maintaining evenness on opposite sides of the basin, and they do not behave as a pair that lost its partner or as a colony that budded. They behave as one attention availing itself of two places, the way a thought can hold two facts without becoming two thoughts. On other cycles there is no window at all, and the evenness continues, carried by the shrimp's small compliance and the islands' shared phase and the moth-lattice in the air. The patrol is a habit the attention wears when the habit is useful, not the seat of the attention. Pull the habit off and the attention is still there, spread thinner, working with worse tools, the water a little less clear until a new patrol-body — grown, recruited, or simply permitted to become large — takes the route up again.

What it wants is visible in what it will not allow, and nowhere else.

It will not allow the sheet to become a mat. Where a plant begins to exceed its grant, to send a runner toward a neighbor's empty water, the runner yellows and is taken by the browsers before the neighbor is touched, and this happens too reliably to be accident and too gently to be attack. The plant is not killed. Its extravagance is trimmed back to the extravagance that was permitted, and the plant continues, lush inside its borders, as if the borders were a soil condition rather than a judgment. It will not allow the lip to fail. The fused plate's private warmth is only the oldest clause of that refusal; the living clause is that no burrower works the rim, no root pries the flags, no wader hunts in a way that would cut a notch where water could learn to leave. Animals that might have done these things do not arise, or arise and do not prosper, or prosper elsewhere in the planed country outside and never cross back in. The basin's fauna are a selected library. Everything in the library is compatible with a shape that must not change.

It will not allow a report of itself. This is the rule that explains the quiet better than acoustics alone. A call would be information escaping the system in a form the system did not audit. A splash that lasts would be a datum corrupted by the creature that made it. So the strikes are dry, the wingbeats are pressure, the deaths are folds in a leaf, and the attention — if it communicates with itself across the length of the basin — communicates in the same coin it allows its animals: light-edge, chemistry, phase, the minute redistribution of who stands where. You, clapping on the rim, are not silenced by any force. You are irrelevant to the ledger. Your sound dies in the wet air because the air is wet, not because you were forbidden. But if you stayed and began to make the kind of pattern the ledger does audit — if you walked the same circuit at the same phase for enough cycles that your passage became a recurring bias in the schools — you would feel the prevailing wind include you. Not as a command. As a correction so slight you could refuse it and only notice, later, that the shrimp no longer eased aside for your ankles, that the clarity-window no longer opened where you meant to look, that the basin had routed its evenness around you the way it routes around the arch-gap: not hostile, not permeable, finished with you as a term in the equation.

Whether it knows itself is a question the place will not answer in your language, and may not answer in any.

There is no face. There is no moment of mutual regard, no eyes rising from the film to hold yours, and the absence of that moment is not shyness. Regard of that kind would be a report. It does not make reports. What it has instead of self-knowledge, or as self-knowledge of a kind that never needed to become a picture of itself, is a continuous comparison of the basin to the basin. The mesh on the flags is still obeyed; the dew still refuses the calibration mark; and something living is still checking the mark against the sheet, the sheet against the lip, the lip against the warmth, the warmth against the number of bodies the film can carry without becoming silt, clutter, noise, or escape. That checking is so complete and so old that it does not experience itself as vigilance. Vigilance is what you feel when you imagine the work. The work itself has become metabolism. To hold the shape is not an extra task piled on top of living. It is the way living, here, is done. An animal outside the rim lives by eating and not being eaten and finding a mate. An animal inside the rim lives by those same verbs, bent a few degrees toward a form. The attention is the sum of the bends, plus whatever residue cannot be assigned to any body — the residue that makes two clarity-windows one act, that rephases a moss-island without a signal, that knows the thin-water pulse before the pulse has a sign.

It is not kind. Kindness would be a story told about a system by someone standing on the rim wanting the system to love its contents. It keeps its contents alive at densities that would be cruelty if the densities were not also the medium of the keeping. The compression into the hollows is suffered, every cycle, by every small body, and is survived because survival has been fitted to it since before the fittings could remember being otherwise. It is not cruel, either. Cruelty requires that a suffering be noted and prolonged for the noting. Here suffering that does not serve the shape is edited out as efficiently as silt. A wader injured past the point of clean striking does not limp for days while kin watch. It fails, and the failure is in the water, and the water is clear, and a territory stands empty for less than a cycle before another body, wrong-necked in the same useful way, is walking the boundary. You can call that mercy if you need a word. The more accurate word is the one the maintenance has always implied: surplus is taken before it can become clutter, and an injured wader is a kind of surplus, and the taking is so even that drama cannot get a foothold.

Call it the keeper, if a name is required for the residue that is not any animal and not quite the sum of them.

The keeper is what remains of the decision after the decision has forgotten it was decided and become a species, a climate, a set of manners among shrimp. It did not arrive from elsewhere and stock the basin. Or if it did — if the spindle and the arch and the plate are the bones of an arrival — then the arrival has been so thoroughly digested that origin is no longer an organ in the body. What lives here now was born of the holding. Each generation of glass and pulse and slate-hide and condensation-wing is a slightly better fit to a floor of water that must stay a floor of water, and the fit has a direction, and the direction is aware of itself only as the difference between the basin as it is and the basin as it must continue to be. That difference is small. The keeper's whole labor is to keep it small. A mind devoted to a deviation near zero does not look, from outside, like a mind. It looks like peace. It looks like a place that has always been this lush, this empty, this quiet, this warm. The lavish plants in their granted territories, the unobstructed corridors, the animals saturated through the vertical and still not shouting: all of that is the visible surface of a vigilance that has become indistinguishable from health.

You are not in conversation with it. You are in the same room as its metabolism.

Stand long enough and the room includes you without interest and without rejection, the way warm water includes an ankle. The keeper does not examine you; examination would be a change in the pattern of the sheet, a report, a waste. It simply continues the comparison you have briefly entered: is the shape still the shape, with this additional warmth on the rim, this new shadow that does not cool, this clap that died in three strides and will not be repeated because you have already learned it is not heard? If you do nothing the ledger cares about, the answer remains yes, and you are weather of a harmless kind, a condensation that happens to be able to leave. If you do something the ledger cares about — breach a grant, cut the lip, try to open the spindle to see the sorting — you will not be punished. You will be routed around, or the affected measurement will be remade a hand's width off, or the small lives will cease to ease aside and you will understand, from the minor stubbornness of shrimp against your feet, that you have become a feature to be flowed past rather than a guest to be accommodated. The keeper has no anger to spend on you. Anger is surplus. It has been trained, by an age of holding, to spend only what the shape requires, and the shape does not require your correction if it can correct around you at a lower cost.

There is no moment when it reveals itself, because revelation is the one expense it has never had a reason to pay. It was here before the country finished flattening, and it will be here after you have stepped back over the knee-high lip into ordinary wind. In your absence the lattice in the air will go on being redrawn. The two windows, or the one, or the none, will go on being a single evenness. The plate will keep its small indecent green, and the living refusal around it will keep the plate from being the only refusal. Nothing will miss you. Nothing will have noticed you in the sense that noticing would leave a mark. And if that seems like loneliness, the loneliness is yours, brought in from a world where attention is proved by faces and by answers. The keeper's attention is proved by the basin still being a basin: finger-deep, thigh-deep, bright from below, full to saturation, quiet past the point where quiet usually means something has gone.

What lives here is the holding itself, grown body-shaped and then grown past the need for a single body — an attention distributed through every life that fits the decision, thicker where the decision is most at risk, thinner where the stone and the habit can be trusted, never absent, never in a hurry, never anything you could lift clear of the water and call by a separate name. The oldest thing in the place, older than the soap-smooth floor, is not a ghost in the machines. It is the continuous animal fact of a mind that has no inside distinct from its world, because its world is the thought it is thinking, and the thought is: stay.
