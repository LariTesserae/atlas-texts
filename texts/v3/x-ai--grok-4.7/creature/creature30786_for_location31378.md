It does not arrive. That is the first correction, and it takes a while to trust it, because the pocket is so full of almost-contacts that any warmth which is not the rock’s seems, at the start, like something that has come in and crouched. Nothing has come in. What is here was cast with the hollow and has not since had a reason to be elsewhere. To say it lives is already a courtesy to a verb the place does not use. It persists in the shape of a life that has not been asked to continue.

What the hands find first is the crease, and in the crease a weight that gives, slightly, the way the uncommitted stone gives under a nail and does not then slump. A span. Slick in the same degree as the wall, so that the boundary is a change in resistance more than a change in wet. Limbs folded to the hollow and not past it: no toe testing the air, no spine considering a straighter line. The skin is not a covering so much as the water-film taught a thickness. Run a finger and you do not lift a slime off an animal. You displace a surface that was already continuous with the skins on the sheets, and it returns to itself without a ripple that travels. There is no flinch. Flinching would require a later moment in which the first moment is recognized as having happened.

For a time it is enough to call this an animal, the one solution the pocket permitted. Blind, because light has no history to have shaped an eye, and not blind in the manner of a loss: there is no sunken place, no sealed lid, no nerve that ends in a regret. The head — if the rounding at one end of the span is a head and not simply the part that fits the deeper cup of the crease — is smooth to the same degree all the way around. What it uses instead of sight is the pressure the stone keeps just under hearing. That low, unfinished sounding is not a signal it waits for. It is the medium its body is already written in. A change in the pressure would be news. There is no news. So the sense, like the eye that was never started, is perfect and unemployed.

Breath is harder to locate and then, once located, obvious. The pocket’s air cannot take more water and cannot give any up; the body shares the refusal. Along the flank, where the film is richest, the skin is faintly finer, a lace of vessels so near the surface they are not under it but of it. Exchange happens there without a gulp, without a bellows, without the small weather of a chest. If there is a rhythm it is the rhythm of equilibrium maintaining itself, which is to say it does not read as rhythm at all. Put a palm to the flank and you feel warmth of the same temperature as the grain. You do not feel a count. Hunger, likewise, has nowhere to go. The film is not diminished by the body and the body is not increased by the film. Feeding, if the word can be made to sit still, is only the fact that the richest water and the only animal occupy the same crease and have never negotiated a difference.

It would be possible to stop at that and call the inhabitant known: a paused amphibian of an unfinished mountain, embryonic and stable, one of a kind because the room had volume for only one answer. The longer you stay, the less the answer holds its edges. The fold of the forward limb is not only a fold. It is an angle a wrist could hold if the wrist had set something down and not yet opened again. Not a hand. Nothing with fingers, nothing that could grip the cobble or worry the fiber. But the memory of a grip is a kind of shape, and the shape is in the limb the way the beginnings of crystal are in the slurry — an order that started, recognized itself, and did not proceed. You can refuse the resemblance. The pocket will not argue. It also will not simplify.

The fiber lies across the crease at the place where a lap would be if a lap were among the forms this body agreed to. It is not tangled in the animal. It is not beside the animal in the way of an object and a sleeper. It crosses, and the crossing has the fit of a thing included at the same moment, the way the root hangs included, the way the bead does not fall. The percussion scars on the ledge are three, and they cup a basin that was never finished, and the body in the crease is turned — has always been turned — so that the rounding of the head faces the ledge as a person faces work they have just set down. Distance is small. Everything nearly touches. The ledge is not another room. It is the next sheet.

Whoever struck the stone did not become this. Becoming would be a change, and the pocket does not keep changes. What is more exact, and slower to accept, is that the striking and the body were never two residencies competing for the vesicle. The mountain stalled. The work stalled. A life stalled. The pocket had room for one solution to occupancy, and the solution is this span of slick weight whose outline still carries, without narrating, the last intention that entered it: to strike, to twist cord, to set a flake where it would not shift, and then not to do the next thing. The next thing was going to be a deeper basin, or a fourth blow, or a leaving. None of those were admitted. What was admitted was the person at the instant after the hands opened, and the instant was given the only anatomy the room knows how to hold — not a skull, not a coat, not a name, but a congruence. Warm. Blind to a light that was never coming. Fitted. Unhungered.

If you look for the face you will invent one, and the invention will be yours, not the pocket’s. There is no mouth in the sense of an opening that expects. There is a seam at the forward rounding, closed, similar enough to the seam in the mountain’s flank that the likeness feels like a courtesy the stone paid itself. It does not part. It has not had to. No tooth started. No word dried on the inner surface, because there is no inner surface distinct from the wet that is everywhere. What the striker knew — how to choose a harder cobble, how to read a bulb of percussion, how to twist bark so it would hold — is not stored behind that seam as memory. Memory would be a past, and a past is a change that has been kept. The knowledge remains the way the crystalline hint remains in the slurry: as a direction the material began to take, legible, unfinished, not available for use.

It does not know you are there. Knowing would be a revision. Your hand on the flank joins the film for as long as you keep it there and is no longer a fact when you take it away; or else the touch was always part of the formation, another nail-mark in soft stone, included without acknowledgment. You cannot check. There is no earlier animal to compare, no later one, no pupa of the person still visible at the edge of the span, shrinking or clarifying. People who want a metamorphosis will be disappointed, and people who want a corpse will be wrong. The body is not slack. Slackness is what remains after tone departs. Tone has not departed. It has never been spent. The limbs hold their fold with the same authority the sheets hold their near-touch: not effort, not collapse, a decision that became a material.

What, then, to call the one that lives here? Not a beast of the dark, because the dark is not its element but its absence of alternatives. Not a ghost, because nothing left. Not the striker’s child and not the striker’s tool. It is the pause given flesh just far enough to be touched, and then stopped at the same depth as the basin: three gestures toward an animal, none of them completed, all of them sufficient to occupy the crease forever. A heart that does not count. A skin that is weather which refused to fall. A wrist-angle with no hand. A facing toward work. If it can be said to want, it wants only what it already is, and the wanting is indistinguishable from the stone’s old, held warmth.

Leave, and the pocket closes behind the idea of you without a sound you will hear. The span does not settle more comfortably after your hand is gone. It was already the exact shape of the hollow. On the ledge the three scars cup their unfinished basin. The flake stays where it was set. The fiber stays crossed. And in the low crease the inhabitant continues, which is the wrong word, and the right one, and the only one the room will allow: still coming into being, and not coming further, and both of those already done.
