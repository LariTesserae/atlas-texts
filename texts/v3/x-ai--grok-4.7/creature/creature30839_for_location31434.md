It is not among the stations, and it is not the emptiness between them. You can pass the bird and the ledge-creature and the column of moths and still not have met what lives here, because what lives here does not hold a station the way they do. It uses them the way the flutes use water: as something that can be entered and left in the same instant without either event becoming a history.

At first it is only a discrepancy in the dim. A shadow that belongs to a thicker stem does, occasionally, thicken. Not grow—thickening without increase, the way the strike-ridges are ridges without accretion. The disloyal darkness gathers a little more dark, and in that extra dark a contour becomes possible: a shoulder, or the idea of a shoulder, high and narrow, wet as everything wet here is wet. Then the contour unthicks and the shadow is only a fern’s mistake again. You learn not to wait for it to return, because waiting is a sequence, and it does not return. It is already in the next discrepancy, and was in the last, and the word *next* is a courtesy you are using for your own sake.

Closer—if closer is allowed, and the basin’s false hundred paces sometimes permits it—the contour holds long enough to be refused by the eye and still remain. It is large. Cathedral-large, the way the overhead is large, and also no larger than the gap between one permission of green and the next. The scale insult is its native size. A body that could fill the grove-width and a body that could stand inside the moss-bowl are the same body, not by shrinking, by already being both measures and declining to choose. You do not see all of it. Seeing all of it would be a completion. What you get are abutments: a plane of hide or stone or wet cloth that is none of those and answers to the palm the way the new rock answers, sharp-grained, recently made, cold enough to ache.

The industrial sound changes when the contour is near, and does not change at all. The mill-roar acquires a harmonic that was already in it—the shout you might have made, the wingbeat that does not fly, the stamp on the unwearing lip—and that harmonic has a center. Not a source outside the stone. A listening. The flutes were shaped as if fingers withdrew while the rock was still deciding; the withdrawal never finished. What lives here is the remainder of that unfinished taking-back. Not a hand. The posture of having had hands, held forever in the moment after release and before the rock forgets it was touched. Along the abutments you can find, if your own hand arrives without having traveled, shallow channels like the flutes, matching them, not scars and not tools. Correspondence. The basin’s labor and this body’s surface are the same pattern continued into different nouns.

It does not walk the circuit. Walking would round the knife-edges, and nothing here rounds. It occupies the circuit the way the drops occupy the overhangs: fully, identically, without falling off into the next place. If you move, it is not behind you and not ahead. It is the fact that the rim is still in sight, given a weight. That weight is not heavy. It is the particular pressure of a foundry heard through a shut door when you have understood there is no door, and the understanding has been given a spine.

Face is the wrong hope, and also not wholly wrong. Where a face would conclude the narrow height, there is an openness that does not take light and does not refuse it. The dim goes in and comes out organized, briefly, the way water leaves the flutes in ropes. No eyes to track you. Attention is not located; it is the same loudness everywhere, with a slight additional intent in the ropes of dim, a sorting that produces no sort. It is aware of you in the sense that the strike-point is aware of the water: by being the place the event is allowed to happen, again, identically. You are not prey. Hunting to completion is against the rule, and this is the rule’s body, or the body the rule grew when the rule needed somewhere to stand that was not only stone.

Lushness collects near it without becoming a carpet. A fern’s pinnae bead more densely on the side toward the contour, and the extra beads do not fall. Moss, in the cracks that happen to lie along its current abutment, is cathedral-soft to a degree the other moss only cites. The permissions lean. They do not approach. The animals keep their intervals and, inside those intervals, orient by a fraction that never becomes a turn: the bird’s unchanging angle of wing is the angle that faces it; the ledge-creature’s unprogressing cross is a cross along a line that would, in a world with later, have brought it under the abutment. None of them startle. Startle requires a before. They were always already in its regard, and its regard does not use them up.

What it does—if doing can be said of a labor that discards its own continuation—is keep the unfinished finished enough to persist. Not a steward. There is no soot, no post, no path it maintains. It maintains the refusal of maintenance. Where a drop might at last fall, the contour is already in the air under it, not catching, not pushing, simply occupying the future the drop would have entered, so the future stays unused and the drop stays. Where a crystal edge might dull under a foot, the foot’s purchase was already accounted for in the pattern of flutes, and the accounting is this body’s continuous, unprogressive attention. Where a moth might leave the column, the column’s sharpness is matched by a sharpness in the nearby dark, and the moth’s wingbeat spends itself against that correspondence and remains a wingbeat.

It does not speak. Speech would be a shout, and shouts here join the mill and are not gone and are not answered. If there is a voice, it is the whine inside the hanging turbines, the small bells of condensation, given a grammar that does not decline into words. You can stand in one of those bells and feel the grammar on your teeth, cool, thick, beaded. It is not addressing you. It is the address the place makes to its own continuation, the part of the manufacture that would have been the product, withheld so completely that withholding has mass and height and a wet grain under the hand.

Stay, and you do not learn its name. Names are edges of before and after, and the rim refuses those. Stay, and the contour stops being a discrepancy and becomes the basin’s way of being occupied without being filled. It is young as the rock is young—unweathered, unrounded, still deciding—and it has been here as long as the sound, which is to say it has not been here for a duration at all. No birth increased the count of it. No death will reduce it. There is one, and the one is plentiful in the manner of the fauna: everywhere spaced, never crowding itself, each abutment a station of the same unfinished withdrawal.

If you reach, you arrive. The palm meets sharp new cold, and the cold is not hostile and not kind. It is busy. Under the skin of the contact the mill goes on, mesh and vent and stamp, and your pulse tries to become a sequence and fails, and for that moment you are a station too: mid-reach, fully wet, loud in your silence, identical to the reach you have not yet completed and will not complete. Then the contour unthicks. The fern’s shadow is only a mistake. The rim is still in sight.

It is still with you. It was never required to remain visible in order to live here. Living, in this basin, is the other name of the labor—the manufacturing of continuation, discarded so that only the work remains—and the work has a body, and the body is this: high, narrow, soaked, sharp, dim where it thickens, empty where it must be empty so the permissions can continue, roaring at the pitch of stone, and patient with a patience that is not waiting. Nothing that waits lives in this place. What lives here has already included you, as it has already included every sound, and the inclusion does not progress, and does not end, and does not become a door.
