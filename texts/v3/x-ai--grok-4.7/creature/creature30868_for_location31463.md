What lives there does not arrive as a silhouette. It arrives as a revision in the film: a long subtraction of ticks against the ankle, a corridor of uninventoried water opening and closing at the speed of something that has decided you are a fixed cost. You learn the corridor before you learn the body. The body, when it consents to be adjacent, is low and jointed in too many places, longer than the fin it is following and not troubled by that. Its underside is a continuous mouth in the old sense of a river-mouth—an intake, not a hunger—sealed against the warm sheet except where the seep is richest. It drinks the metal sweetness and leaves the iron. The iron goes into a line of dark beads along its flank, which it will later press into a joint and forget, the way the rock forgets a fracture once the water has claimed it.

There are many of these, and they are not a herd. A herd would require a direction. They keep to weep-lines and pipe-shadows—the cooled grooves, never shadows—and their territories are the same territory counted twice, the way the basin counts its installations. You can walk away from one and meet it ahead without it having passed you. It does not treat this as remarkable. Coincidence is a gait it already has. When two of them occupy the same reach in both senses, they do not collide; they revise their intakes by a finger’s width and continue, each convinced, correctly, that the seep is sufficient and singular.

Their signaling is chemical and thermal, and it is rude to call it language if you mean something that could be translated into a sentence you might say. It is closer to bookkeeping. A bead of iron set in a joint is a note about a pipe that hesitated. A change in the spacing of the dark beads is a note about you: your heat, the width of the corridor you force, whether you have put a hand on a housing and made the pennies rise. They do not fear the electrified column. They treat it as weather with a period, and they schedule their layers around its stroke the way the small shelled colonies schedule their imitation of reeds. The soft traffic higher in the air—the wet wingbeats—are not theirs. Those are a separate abundance, finger-long, winged in membranes that never dry, navigating by the same displaced film and by the faint cooked smell of filaments spending themselves invisibly. They land on the beaded flanks to take iron the way other small things take salt. The long ones allow it. Iron is a surplus they were going to cache anyway.

If you stay through one revision of the books—the film up a finger, a new fracture admitting itself under a pebble—you begin to meet the other resident, and it is harder, because it is not a body in the film. It is the maintenance.

Not a person with a shift and a lamp. Lamps are irrelevant here, and shifts are a kind of light. What keeps the pumps on their geological stroke is a patience wearing a shape when it must. Most of the time it is only the cycled density in the sternum, the counted air. When a pipe’s coincident copy hesitates, the patience gathers. It gathers out of the warm seam just above the film and out of the joints, the way a decision gathers, and for a while there is a figure you could almost assign limbs to: tall only because the country is low, wet in the same continuous sense as the water, hands that are not hands so much as intervals where a flange becomes true again. It does not carry tools. The tools are already in the reach, and some of them are in the reach twice. It reminds a housing that it is the same housing. The taste of the seep changes back. The long drinkers revise their beads. The figure does not remain figure. Remaining would be a kind of propagation, and propagation is what this country declines.

People—the sort who walk in on feet and expect a horizon—do not live here in any durable way. A few have tried, mistaking the industry for a claim that someone like them must be owed. They last until the water includes a delay they cannot schedule their thirst around, or until they strike a light and understand, in the phosphorus smell and the unchanged dark, that being owed illumination is not a local right. The ones who last longer stop trying to see and start keeping accounts the basin’s way: a stone set on a crest to see which fracture will claim it, a palm on a sweating pipe to learn the stroke, a willingness to meet the same animal ahead and behind and call neither meeting the copy. They go quiet. Sound dies here except for drip and cycle, and their voices die on the same rule, not by command but by economy. What they still do is listen with the ribs. After a long enough stay they are no longer visitors in the strict sense. The film inventories them. The long drinkers open a corridor and, eventually, narrow it, not in welcome and not in threat, but because a fixed cost that has learned to move on the country’s period is no longer worth a wide subtraction.

Nothing here is a monster in the lantern sense. Nothing hunts by outline. The abundance is predatory only in the way a seep is predatory: it takes what it is shaped to take, iron or delay or the superstition that a place must show itself in order to be occupied. The stiff wands and the pale crusts are the least of the living. The small ticks in the sheet are the most numerous and the least concerned with you. The long beaded drinkers are the country moving at the speed of attention. The gathered patience at the pumps is the country refusing to let its own coincidence become an error.

If it has a face, you will not get it by waiting for light. You will get it, if at all, as a warmth on the ribs that is not the ordinary warmth, clean and tidal, pausing the length of one machine-stroke as if to confirm your dimensions, and then revising around you without comment—leaving you in the film, among the edges that have not yet agreed to be old, under a dark that was never a lid, in a basin still deciding how many of itself it is, and how many of you.
