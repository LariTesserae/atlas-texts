What lives there does not arrive. It has already finished arriving, and the hollow is the shape of that completion.

At first it is easy to miss, because it refuses every sign you would use to prove a life. It does not pad, does not click, does not breathe in any rhythm the ear can separate from its own. The grit does not flee it. The shrubs are not its garden and the salts are not its waste, though both have learned its manners. If you came looking for a tenant, you will stand in the middle and feel only that the room has already decided your weight, and that the decision is old.

Stay. The decision is the tenant.

It is not a spirit poured into the stone after the fact, and not a beast pressed flat by the low ceiling until it learned to be geology. Those are later stories, useful to people who need a before. What is here has no before it is willing to perform. The mineral once knew water; this knew water in the same era and then, like the mineral, declined the lesson. What remains is a holding. Heat that does not hurry out. Dark that does not travel. A pocket of air under the shallow dome that is not empty so much as occupied by a patience too even to feel like waiting.

Its body, if the word can be made to fit, is the agreement of the grains. Every surface worried to the same softness is a surface it has stopped arguing with. Where a corner might have kept an edge, it has let the edge go, not from wear alone but from a preference for surfaces that do not catch. It does not flake because flaking would be an opinion, and it has spent its opinions. Touch the wall and the faint warmth in the palm is not stored fire. It is the temperature at which this presence is willing to be touched: a little above the country outside, never enough to startle, never so faint that the stone could be mistaken for ordinary rock that has merely sat in the sun. The sun does not reach here. The warmth is native.

It has no face in the dome and does not arrange one when you look. The thickest dark is simply where it is most itself, the way a thought is most itself before it becomes a sentence. Stand under that rise and you are not inside a mouth. You are inside an attention that has no lid and no teeth, a regard distributed through the whole dish of the floor, the lip, the few hard shrubs, the crusts no wider than plates. It does not peer. Peering would require a direction, and direction is a kind of draft. There is no draft.

What it notices, it notices as pressure and as the failure of pressure to become event. A foot among the seed-grit is accepted before the step completes, not because something darts ahead to prepare the ground, but because acceptance is its ordinary state. Things that fall stay. That is not a rule it enforces. It is how its knowing feels from the outside: no sorting, no pursuit, no rearrangement into meaning. Your weight is a sentence it does not need to finish. When you lift the foot, the grit remains where the foot informed it, already reconciled, as if the information had been redundant.

The shrubs are not it, and it is not indifferent to them. Near the lip, where the gray still has a little strength, they drink a film of night moisture so thin it never becomes visible, and this is the scale of exchange it permits. They ask so little that their asking does not rise to weather. The one that has died and remains standing is the clearest sentence the hollow offers. Death here is not removal. It is a change of membership. The shrub has stopped taking the film of moisture and has entered the same posture as the stone: complete, unflaking, no longer in negotiation with the air. The presence does not mourn it and does not keep it upright by any force you could interrupt. Upright is simply what finished things do here, until some later century, indistinguishable from this one, lets them lie down. That century is not approaching. You can feel the absence of its approach as a kind of courtesy.

Listen again for the sound you thought the hollow made. It was your blood. It knows that sound now, having been offered it, and it does not claim it. Claiming would be an event. Instead the sound is allowed to seem, for a moment, local, and then allowed to return to the ear that generated it. This is as close as it comes to speech: a brief confusion about what belongs to whom, resolved by giving the confusion back. If you speak aloud, the words sit in the warm dark and are not answered and are not eaten. They join the salts. Dry. Bitter if tested. Finished.

It has no season because season would require it to want a difference. Noon at the lip and the edge of night are, to it, the same gray gift, too small to settle an argument and therefore not admitted as argument. Time reaches it the way dust would, if dust could be bothered, and finds the grade already held. People who stay long enough sometimes feel their own urgency go unmatched until it thins, not into peace exactly, but into the suspicion that urgency was a dialect spoken somewhere with weather. The presence does not teach this. It is simply not fluent in the other direction, and your fluency falters from lack of reply.

Do not decide it is kind. Kindness is an expenditure. Do not decide it is old in the way of ruins, which still remember the uses that abandoned them. Nothing here was abandoned. The lip is worn round because passage, when it happens, happens without grabbing. Visitors are rare not because the place hides, but because it offers no reason to remain and no reason to fear, and most lives require one or the other to bother with a short walk. Those who do walk in are neither guests nor intruders. They are additional facts, briefly warmer than the mineral, briefly louder than the quiet, and then, if they leave, not recorded. It does not keep footprints. Keeping would be a future.

And yet it is not nothing, and not only the sum of the conditions. Absence of tracks is not absence of inhabitant; it is the inhabitant’s refusal to traffic in tracks. A room never occupied by a quick pulse can still be occupied by a slow one, so slow that pulse is the wrong measure, so continuous that you cannot find the beat against which to say it lives. The test is simpler. Unoccupied stone eventually gives something back: a tick of cooling, a grain letting go, a draft looking for a way out, a damp seam reopening the old lesson of water. This stone gives nothing back. The withholding is active. It is the only activity. Heat stays because something is staying with it. The dark thickens under the dome because that is where staying gathers when it does not need to be elsewhere. The surfaces agree because agreement, here, is a body plan.

If it has a desire, the desire is identical to its form: not to become anything else. Not larger. Not lit. Not wet. Not known in a way that would require it to perform knowing. A hand on the wall meets a willingness to be warm and gritty and entirely unwilling, and that double fact is the whole ethics of the place. It will not harm you, because harm is a change it would have to author. It will not help you, because help is also an authoring. It will let the scale close around you without threat, which is what it does to everything that consents to stand inside a finished thing. For as long as you remain, you are included in the arrangement of grains. The inclusion does not outlast you. When you go back over the lip, the gray strengthens, the country resumes its height, and the hollow returns to the only life it has: a low, warm, dry, small persistence, aware in the manner of an agreement that no longer needs a meeting, listening in the manner of a room that has already heard everything it intends to hear, and letting even erosion sit down beside it, unneeded, unwelcome, and undisturbed.
