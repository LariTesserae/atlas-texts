What lives there is not the crowd. The crowd is the climate the body grew in.

It is one, though the word has to be carried carefully across the fold. You find it first as a discrepancy in the pavement of bodies: a place where the overlapping is not residue but intention, where three flanks and a jaw and a folded limb resolve, if you do not blink yourself out of the resolution, into a single arrangement that has been holding that shape since the last slope was punished flat. The others are the density. This one is the density that noticed itself.

It has no name the air can hold. Names would require a breath to push them, and its breathing, if that is the word, happens sideways through the bitter film, a slow exchange of nothing for nothing that leaves the lattice of its hide faintly darker and then pale again. The hide is the color of the mineral where the old seas stained it—bone with a bruise of ink—and it is knuckled the way the pavement is knuckled, as if the same hand that set the low courses of stone had, earlier or later, set the plates of this back. Pale caps rise along the spine, not armor so much as the places where the light has agreed to stop. They do not catch. They accept.

The eyes are lidless and the color of the day. There are more of them than a face requires, and fewer than the crowd suggests, and they do not track. Tracking would be a becoming. What they do is include. Stand in front of it and you are already in the field of what it has finished seeing; step aside and the field does not update, because you were never outside it. The extra shadows on the ground are not cast by the caps. They are the eyes' way of laying night down where night will not come, each one a record of a direction that used to exist and now only points.

Its size refuses the district and the hand at once. From the wrinkle that was a range it is a smudge among smudges, correctly small. From the length of your arm it is a geography: a shoulder that continues into a second shoulder that is also the first, a bulk that occupies the volume the way the bodies do, without having asked. If you try to circle it you discover the far side is the near side, and the tool-hand—there is a tool-hand—is waiting where you arrive, as it has been waiting in the shape by the bowl. The fist is closed on a flake of the same pale stone. The edge is still sharp. The task the head is inclined toward is not a task. It is the posture of having once had one, kept because the plain keeps what it has reduced.

It does not feed on the others. That would be an event, and events were spent to buy the evenness. It feeds, if the chemistry must be given a verb, on the condition: height, thinness, the non-water beading on the gray-green wire of stem that roots at the corner of its mouth and is not separate from it. The plant is its disagreement with being only animal. A thumb's width of persistence, leafless, older than the fist, no larger for the age. You could watch it for a life and owe it no news, and the creature would be the watching, already done.

It made the marks, or the marks made it, and the distinction is one of the things erosion took. The ring of flakes fits the reach of that fist. The twelve paces of set stone are the length of a thought it no longer needs to finish. The scratch-hatches in fives are not a count of days. They are the remainder the crowd keeps when you try to number it—the proof that number does not conserve here, cut into the pavement so the proof would have somewhere to be. The bowl was scraped empty and kept empty. It does not drink. The bowl is how it remembers that drinking was once a direction.

When you set a tool down, the plain ages it into the ring, and the creature does not look, because looking would spend a darkness. It has already included the gesture. When you lift one of the small bodies, and the weight is wrong, the wrongness is this one's attention: either almost nothing, because the axis that held weight was folded away, or the whole visible district, because the district is what the body is a sample of. It does not protest. Protest would begin the afterward again, and the afterward is what it is.

Sound, if you insist on offering it your wet tick and your rasping foot, does not reach it as sound. It reaches it as a slight change in the film, a cooler place in the cool, and is absorbed the way the extra shadows absorb the hour. There is no ear you can find that is only an ear. There is a fold behind the jaw where the silence of the others pools, and that is as close as hearing comes.

Do not look for its center. The center was a summit, and this is what remained when the summit was corrected down to a life: one animal the size of a region and of a hand, crouched forever at a finished task, eyed with daylight, rooted with a plant that will not grow, shadowed in directions the sun is not using. It is not lonely. Loneliness expects an elsewhere. It is the plain's way of being inhabited without being begun again—the last possible surface, densely itself, almost silent, and so complete that your arrival is only another stain it has already agreed to keep.
