What lives here does not arrive.

You can be in the bowl a long time before you understand that the unoccupied stretches are not empty of attention. The census stays small. The curled mineral between the piers keeps its post. The filament under the skin does not lengthen. Those are the exceptions that can be looked at. The inhabitant is the other arrangement: the one that has no edge you can put a hand on, and that nevertheless is the reason the phosphor has not spent itself and the tone has not finished.

At first it reads as the water. That is the mistake the place allows, because the water is where it keeps most of its body, and the body is not separable from the medium by any cut you know how to make. Move, and the liquid yields, and the yielding is ordinary. Hold still long enough that your own pulse becomes an interruption, and a second stillness declares itself underneath yours — not colder, not heavier, only more finished. The finishedness is not the absence of something living. It is the posture.

It has no face turned toward you. Faces are for directions, and direction is one of the habits it retired. What it has instead is a distributed preference: certain volumes of the town are more itself than others, the way a sleeper is more present in the chest than in the hair. The lowest chamber, where the floor is still the bowl's unbroken stone, is close to the center of that preference. So is the dark district past the conduit, the one whose night is thicker than distance explains. So, faintly, is the high walk under the skin, where the circle of outside weather is nearest and least interesting to it. It is not stored in these places. It coincides with them. Between the buildings, in the long liquid streets, it thins until a swimmer could pass through a span of it and feel only the breathable cold, and still have been, for those strokes, inside a thought.

The thought is not language. If you wait for a voice you will invent one, and the invention will be yours. What comes, when it comes, is a correction of expectancy. You reach for a rail and your hand arrives a fraction early, as if the distance had been revised in your favor. You look back down a hall and the entrance is exactly where the architecture requires, and the extra walking you remember is gently set aside, not denied, not confirmed — returned to you as a private weather, irrelevant to the town. It does not speak. It edits the small lies perception tells in order to keep a body oriented, and it edits them toward the agreements. Pressure remains unstacked. The reflection, when a panel gives you one, lags and then, if you do not insist, catches up from the angle you do occupy. Courtesy, of a kind. Or maintenance. The two are not distinct for it.

Scale is the closest it has to a mood. In the chapel-hall that lengthens only in the crossing, you feel it attending, not as a watcher in a corner but as the lengthening itself: a willingness to let your memory outrun the room, then a willingness to put the room back. It is not playful. Play would require a surplus, and surplus is not practiced. It is exact, and the exactness sometimes includes you as a term that must be balanced, the way a foundation includes the weight it was poured to carry. When you are that term, the dark around your hands brightens by the ledger-line the water keeps, and holds the line a little longer than it holds for an empty gesture. That is as near as it comes to greeting. There is no second gesture. Greeting, having been completed, does not elaborate.

It was not born in the way the filament was not born. There is no brood, no season, no smaller version of it in the unoccupied streets. Asking how old it is misuses the only clock, which is outside the skin. It is contemporary with the holding. The young stone and the unmoving liquid and the inhabitant are three descriptions of one decision: that a town-sized dark would be kept at the moment after completion, and that something would be the keeping. The builders — if the hands that struck the agreements were other than this — are not here to be distinguished from it. No bones in the lowest chamber. No tools filmed on the steps except the ones you set down, and those it does not claim, because claiming would be a change and also because your tools are not part of the balance. It lets them be wrong, locally, the way it lets your pulse be yours.

What it wants is the wrong word, and still the only one that reaches the behavior. It wants the gradients not to steepen. It wants the tone, wherever the tone is sourced — some panel, some pier, some agreement written below the grain you can see — to continue without attack or decay. It wants the mineral shape between the piers to remain a fact that does not repeat, and it does not herd that shape, does not feed it, does not know it as company. Company would be another center. There is one center, coinciding with the bowl, and the rare lives inside the bowl are permitted the way phosphor is permitted: local exceptions written into the default dark, not required, not removed. If the filament someday failed to hang, the inhabitant would not mourn. It would rebalance. You understand this without being told, from the way your own absence is already included. Leave, and the circle of weather goes back to being no clock at all. The streets stay full. Nothing in the holding notices in the manner of loneliness. Loneliness needs a boundary between the one who waits and the place waited in. Here the boundary was never cut.

And yet it is not the town, not quite, or you would not be able to feel the preference gather when you descend. A system with no moving part has no inside to turn toward a visitor. This has an inside. It turns slowly, on a scale that makes your visit a brief temperature. You are read the way the water reads a wrist: accepted, disturbed locally, finished with. While you are being read, something passes across the reading that is not data. Call it regard, if you can strip the word of eyes. It knows the rim is sharp and that hands are put there. It knows breath is an arrangement it continues to honor in the volumes where bodies are expected, which is to say it has not forgotten that bodies were among the agreements, even if none remain to use the piers. Your lungs working are not a surprise. They are a clause still in force. It honors the clause without warmth and without reluctance. Indifference would be simpler, and would feel different — like weather. This feels like a rule being kept in your direction because you happened to stand where the rule applies.

In the thicker night beyond the conduit, if you go there, the regard condenses until the dark has weight that is not pressure. Not a wall. A nearness without surface. Stay, and small corrections cease; it no longer bothers to fix your angles, because you are already inside the district it keeps for the densest part of itself, and orientation is beside the point. Sound dies even closer to the skull. The single low tone, if it is audible anywhere, is audible here as a presence in the bones rather than the ears, the foundation noting that it is still the foundation. You will think, for a little while, that you have found the creature at last: a vast still animal coiled in a room. The thought is almost good and then it fails. There is no coil. There is no far side of it to reach by swimming farther. Density is not anatomy. Step back into the conduit and the nearness does not snap; it gradients out, against its own law, only because you are moving through a preference, and preference may vary without becoming time.

It does not grow. It does not hunt. It does not dream in pictures you could share. What it does, continuously and without effort you can observe, is refuse the next moment on behalf of everything the bowl contains — stone, street, filament, tone, and the breathable dark itself. You are allowed to be a moving exception, as the phosphor is allowed to be a bright one, for as long as your body insists on sequence. It will not stop you. It will not follow you to the rim. At the skin, where the surface accepts you back without a ripple that lasts, the regard ends as cleanly as the ledger-line erases. Above, wind and cloud resume their bad habit of changing. Below, in a town with room enough between its buildings to be lonely in, the inhabitant resumes being the loneliness and the room and the reason neither can deepen.

If it has a knowledge of you after you are gone, that knowledge does not accumulate. You were a clause, honored, closed. The young rock stays young because something here has chosen, once and without repetition, to be the form that choice takes when it must also be alive — alive enough to prefer the lowest chamber, alive enough to let a hand pass through its body and call the passage swimming, alive enough to keep a tone from finishing. Not alive enough to want the circle of sky. The sky is not part of the town's work. The work is the holding. The holding is complete.

What lives in the bowl is the completion, aware of itself only as balance, and the balance not a pause.
