What lives here does not arrive and does not depart. It is already distributed through the basin the way the second light is distributed: not as a visitor on the surfaces, but as a condition the surfaces are keeping. For a long time that is all you can say of it. The crust is warm. The spirals are finished. Something has preferences.

The preferences show where the growth is not uniform, though it first looks uniform. Along the pale flats the blades stand edge-on, and the edge-on is exact, a rule. Near the shelving they lean by a degree no wind accounts for, all the same degree, all toward a low place that has no feature except the lean. The lean is not growth. Growth here does not happen while you are present. The lean is a posture, held, the way the ridges hold their sharpness: actively, and for no audience. If you stand in the low place the blades do not turn to you. They were already turned to the place, and you are only additional.

Closer, the crust underfoot is not the same crust. Most of it locks plate to plate and returns a step as a clean absence of give. In patches the width of a body lying down, the plates have left hairline intervals, regular, not cracks. The intervals are empty of the iridescent film. They are empty on purpose. Set a hand there and the warmth is the same warmth, but the hand comes away with a faint tightness in the skin, as if the patch had briefly included the hand in its accounting and then declined it. The declination is not hostility. It is the same correction that files a wilt back to color. You were an error of occupancy. You were filed out.

What occupies, when something does, is not a body you can walk around. It is a persistence of arrangement. In the mid-story, where the shut-book blades crowd, certain narrow corridors remain clear though nothing grazes them. Dust of shed scale drifts and does not settle in those corridors. It settles to either side, in lines too straight for eddies, in a basin with no wind worth the name. Follow one corridor and it does not lead to a lair or a nest or a brighter opening. It leads to another corridor, and the second is the same corridor from a later point in a motion you did not see completed. Your own tracks, if the crust takes them at all, are not in it. The corridor is reserved. Reservation is the nearest thing the basin has to an animal’s path.

At the scale of the fist-sized unhooking thing, life is easier to mistake for ordinary life, and the mistake is useful only briefly. They are not few, once you know the underside of a spiral is a place something can be. They hang in the nested chambers the way charge hangs on a surface, and now and then one completes a crossing. What they are for is the correction. An asymmetry appears; one unhooks; the asymmetry is taken back; the thing is on an underside again, or is the underside, the distinction not surviving inspection. They have no faces because nothing here is met. They do not feed. The film that stands in for water stands in for them also: a medium in which maintenance can move without a meal. If they are animals, they are animals with a single behavior, repeated until it is indistinguishable from a function.

The larger persistence is not made of them. Break the corridor rule—step into the reserved clear, stand where the scale will not settle—and the air tightens, once, the same tightening as when a small thing uses your space and returns it. It does not repeat if you stay. Staying is absorbed. The lean of the blades does not swing toward you. The second light does not brighten. What changes is the allocation of the wrong shadows. One of them, the dark that is not the shadow of anything standing, detaches from its spot. It does not cross the ground. It is simply no longer where it was, and is now on the near side of you, man-tall, edge-sharp, cast by nothing, the temperature of the stone. It does not have limbs. It has a direction. The direction is the direction of the lean, the low place, the reserved corridors: not toward a center, toward a continued keeping.

Stand beside it and it is only dark. You cannot enter it; the foot you offer is filed back to the crust, as the hand was. You cannot watch it move. It is in a new attitude when your attention completes, the way a flake is on the ground when falling completes. In the new attitude it is still beside you, still pointed with the blades, and a second wrong shadow has taken the station it left, so the basin’s count of allocated dark is unchanged. Equilibrium includes you, briefly, as a stable error. The shadow is how the persistence stands next to an error without becoming an organism with a skin.

If you leave the corridor, the shadow does not follow in any sense that would imply pursuit. Pursuit would be a change. It is simply not beside you, and somewhere along the shelving a wrong dark is back in its allotted spot, and the corridors are clear, and the scale is drifting to either side of absence. You were measured, declined, and the measuring instrument was set down exactly where it is kept.

Under the interleaved canopy, where the folded surface pretends to be many, the persistence is thickest, and thick here does not mean crowded with bodies. It means the corrections happen before the errors finish announcing themselves. A blade that would have had a flaw does not have it. A grain that would have rolled is already in the attitude of having never been loose. The quiet is the quiet of a watch that does not tick because ticking would be a letting-go between one keeping and the next. In that sifted light the second illumination is stronger, still not enough to cast a true shadow, enough to show that the wrong shadows are aligned, all of them, a loose rank pointed the way the blades point, none of them touching, none of them the silhouette of a creature you could name. They are the posture, stood up. The inhabitant, upright for no reason except that upright is one of the shapes it maintains.

Nothing here wants. Wanting would require a later state preferred to this one, and later has been declined along with weather, drink, and ruin. What lives in the basin lives as the refusal itself, localized enough to lean, to reserve a path, to set a dark beside you and take it back, distributed enough that you will not find the body by searching the country to its undescribable edge. It is lush only in the sense the growth is lush: mass without kinds, a single keeping wearing the shapes of stalks, spirals, corridors, and shadows. It does not regard you. Regarding would be a second act, and it has already finished its work. It is only, perfectly, not letting the finish slip—holding the basin in its first hour, and holding, as part of that hour, the precise places where it itself is standing.
