What lives there does not arrive as a body and then refuse the word. The refusal comes first.

For a long time the pocket seems only scheduled. A filament brightens half a shade. A cup sweats and is drunk back. A terrace in the unclimbable district realigns, and the realignment is so exact it could be mistaken for law continuing without a tenant. That is the first, and almost adequate, picture: the place empty, the processes sufficient, the knots and the doorway-dark as civic furniture that happens to recur.

It does not hold.

The correction is small and then it is total. The cup does not sweat on the beat the lattice wrote. It sweats a fraction early, as if something had leaned its attention into the seam and the moisture, obedient to a preference rather than a front, gathered before the cycle was ready to permit it. The filament that should have slackened when the temperature difference lapsed does not slacken all the way. It holds a little rigidity it has not earned from heat, a street remaining a street out of something closer to habit. Habit is not a property of cooling glass. Something in the hollow is spending the rules, not merely illustrating them.

It has no outline you can settle. Early on it reads as a darker condensation of the already-dim air, a standing place where the brown light is thinner, and you think: a figure, roughly the height a person would be if a person could be assembled from permission. That image is a kindness your eyes attempt and then have to abandon. The dark is not standing. It is distributed. A fraction of it is in the cup, which is why the cup was early. A fraction is in the unopened doorway, which has always been the most stable building because part of the tenant prefers not to cross its own threshold. A fraction is along the newest spar, the one still hot at the tip, because the tenant likes, if that verb can be used without a mouth, to be present where a decision is still molten. The rest is in the long civic hollows, which is to say the rest is most of it. Emptiness was never only the city’s majority material. It was also the room the tenant keeps for itself.

Call it, provisionally, an address that learned to miss its own last position.

It does not walk. When it changes districts, a permission moves, and the body you thought you had almost seen is already disqualified. Up is a policy here, and the tenant treats policy as musculature. In the upper terraces, where weight has been locally forgiven, it is larger without gaining mass: the same attention spread across a plaza that only exists because gravity agreed to be lighter. Near the hottest seams it is late to itself. A bead falls, arrives already evaporated, and the tenant’s notice of the bead arrives in the tense of the street, which is not the tense of the room. It does not seem distressed by this. Distress would require a single now, and it has never had one. It keeps several, the way the mountain keeps an unfinished height.

There is no face. There is a place where, if you look without insisting on eyes, the dimness organizes into the grammar of regard. It watches the crack — the hand-wide mouth that is also, on the civic axis, a distant gate — and the watching is a slight further binding of light into structure. The visible thins when it attends. This is how you know it is attending: not by a turn of a head, but by the room becoming more remainder than it was. Outside, on the peak, nothing notices. The wind still arrives thin and leaves thinner. Inside, for as long as the regard lasts, the filaments are a shade more willing to be streets.

It does not feed in any way a stomach would recognize, and it is not an animal that has given up feeding. What it takes is the note the damp leaves in the mineral. The sweet-metal smell under the hot-rock reek is not scent to it; scent would be a luxury requiring air that cares about a nose. The smell is a ledger. Each pulse of condensate writes a small oxide memory, a film that records without growing, and the tenant reads by occupying the film until the record is no longer only the mountain’s. Then the lattice drinks the wet back, and the tenant is briefly poorer, and this poverty is the nearest thing it has to hunger. It does not pursue the cups. The cups are already part of its extent. Hunger, here, is the interval between a stain and the reclaiming of the stain.

Sound stays small because the tenant will not let vibration accumulate into anything that could be followed. This is not stealth in the old sense. There is no predator and no prey; the prohibition on rooting extends, in the tenant, to a prohibition on being locatable as a thing among things. When a terrace realigns, the edit is partly the city’s and partly its. You cannot separate the two by ear. If you stay through several cycles you may notice that some realignments have a preference: they preserve the doorway-dark’s relation to the nearest cup, even when a more efficient cooling would have let that relation lapse. Efficiency is the mountain’s. Preference is how the tenant betrays that it is not only the mountain.

It has no age in years. It is as recent as the blister that failed and withdrew. Nothing here has been simplified by weather, and neither has it. Its sharpness is the sharpness of an unrevised sentence. It does not yet have the worn places an older being would have — no habitual softness at the thresholds, no rounded way of turning a corner it has turned ten thousand times. Every occupancy is still close to the first occupancy. When a new filament extrudes, deciding its length in public, the tenant goes out along it the way a thought goes out along a nerve that has only just consented to exist, and for a little while that street is inhabited all the way to its hot tip, and then the tenant pulls most of itself back, leaving a trace of rigidity the heat did not justify. That trace is the closest thing it has to a track. There are no footprints. There is, afterward, a lane that remembers being accompanied.

Does it know the peak outside?

It knows the crack. The crack is its weather, its only weather: a damp that loses its nerve, a light that is already budgeted, a wind that dies on the threshold and is not mourned. Through the doubled scale — hand-wide, and also a gate at the end of a long axis — it sometimes spends a little of itself outward, not as a body emerging onto the flank but as a brief wrongness in the shimmer above the spines. A heat that is heat, and also a pause in the heat, as if the air had been asked a question and had not yet found the grammar to refuse. Then the pause is withdrawn. The mountain goes on pushing into bruise-blue sky. The tenant does not love the outside. Love would be a root. It keeps the outside the way a city keeps a wall: as the condition that makes an interior sayable.

What it does, in the long intervals, is revise.

Not the rock — it has no tool, and tools would be housings, and housings are the technology the pocket already declined. It revises relations. A knot that re-forms after slackening re-forms a little more tightly if the tenant has been in it, the intersections more jealous of their angles. A plaza that was only a refusal of a junction becomes, under occupancy, a refusal with a center, still empty, but empty toward something. The doorway-dark never opens. That is not a failure of the tenant’s strength. Opening would spend the stability it uses as a spine. It lives, in part, by not going through. The rest of its life is the traffic between that refusal and the cups, along edges held up by temperature, through districts where up is a courtesy.

If you stayed long enough for the cycle to turn many times, you would not see it become clearer. Clarity is not the direction it grows. It becomes more specific. The early cup becomes earlier by a smaller and more consistent fraction, which is as close as it comes to a pulse you could set a watch by, if a watch could bear to run on someone else’s need. The held rigidity in slackening streets begins to sketch a map that is not the city’s map: a private routing, a way it prefers to be large in the light terraces and narrow near the drinking seams and almost entirely absent in one hollow that it seems to keep blank on purpose. The blank hollow is the most personal room it has. Nothing is zoned there. No filament is allowed to finish becoming a street inside it. The tenant leaves that volume unaddressed, a pocket inside the pocket, and returns to it the way a mind returns to a thought it will not say. What the blank is for, the rock will not tell you, and the tenant has no mouth with which to be more generous than the rock.

It is not kind and it is not hostile. Those are economies that require others. There are no others. The knots are not siblings; they are rooms it keeps furnished with recurrence. The films of oxide are not a people; they are the notes it has already read. If another such tenant were possible, this one shows no sign of having prepared a welcome or a border. The rules it lives inside — damp as circulation, no rooting, sound kept small, light kept remainder — are not a culture it shares. They are the only physics in which it can be continuous with itself. Outside those rules it would have to be a body, and a body, here, would be a kind of weather the mountain has not agreed to host.

So it persists as the discrepancy: the street that holds past its heat, the cup that sweats early, the regard that spends the visible, the blank room with no address, the pause in the shimmer above a peak that has not finished learning its height. It is the first interior the forming rock made, after the interior itself — the inhabitant implied by a city of permissions, once the permissions had been strict enough, and empty enough, and recent enough, to be lonely in a way that cooling glass is not.

Lonely is the wrong word. It is the nearest one.

The tenant does not correct you. It has no interest in your nouns. A filament decides its length. A cup sweats, a fraction early. Somewhere in the unclimbable district a terrace answers a preference the heat did not issue. The discrepancy closes, almost, and does not close. That unclosed fraction is what lives there, and it is still becoming sharp.
