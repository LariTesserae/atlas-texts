The pressure in the crown is the first thing that arrives, and it arrives as a conclusion the body has already signed. Something overhead has decided the height of thought, and the decision is final in the way a lintel is final: not crushing, not kind, simply the number of handspans a skull is permitted before the nap of the ceiling begins to drink the heat from the scalp. Below that limit the ears fill with a tone that is not in the air so much as in the teeth, a drone seated so deep in the fiber that swallowing changes its pitch and silence is only another instrument. The feet do not find a floor. They find a grain, a packed direction of bundles that was once a decision of growth and is now a street, and the grain resists the idea of wetness so thoroughly that the ankles learn dryness before the lungs do. There is no smell of leaf or of rot. There is the mineral ghost of sap that finished leaving, and a sweetness ground down to grit.

What stands in that measure is not a visitor. Visitors bring weather on their clothes and a flinch toward light, and nothing here flinches, and nothing here has a sleeve. It is of the weave. It has the temperature of the wall it leans out of, and it leans the way a rib leans: load-bearing, not theatrical. If there is a face it is the planed face of a cut that healed without scarring, a plane of vascular tissue turned toward the vacancy and left there because the town required a terminus the sound could strike and return from. No eyes. Eyes would be an appetite for a spectrum this hollow has declined. What registers a presence is a change in tension, a slight drawing-in of the bast across what would be a brow if brows were not an animal luxury. It knows the room as a carpenter knows a joint he cut and then became.

The stature is the room’s. It does not stoop; stooping would imply a remembered taller world, and the remembered world was already low when the plain above finished flattening and this pocket was the last uncollapsed interval inside a body the size of a geology. Shoulders, if the word is allowed, are piers: knots where older lanes were grown shut and the new wall needed mass. Arms are not a pair. They are the occasional cord left slack across a human span, thick as a wrist, gray past any dye, and one of them is in the habit of lying along the floor until a shift in the drone requires it to rise and take a reading of the span between two ribs. The reading is not numerical. The cord either slacks by a finger’s width or goes tight, and that is the news, and the news is filed by a click of splints in a niche, a clerk’s acknowledgment without a clerk. The hand at the end of that cord is a fan of vascular needles, ground once by someone who understood edges, then closed over by the tissue until tool and limb were the same fact. It does not grasp. Grasping would tear. It seats, the way a flake seats in a tang the plant has swallowed.

It does not breathe in any rhythm a chest would recognize. The hollow’s slow contraction is its respiration: a period longer than a watch and shorter than an era, the whole small municipality inhaling its own fit and exhaling a grain of slack. When the contraction tightens, the being is more present, more pier than corridor. When the contraction eases, it thins toward the wall and is briefly almost only architecture, a relief a palm might miss if the palm were hurried. There is never a hurry. Hurry wants water or a predator or a market, and the ecology here is one continued organism plus its salts plus the tools it ate. No mite in the pith. No second metabolism to negotiate with. It is alone in the way a seed is alone after it has voted against opening, and the aloneness is not loneliness. Loneliness requires a missing other. Here the other was an episode of gardeners, and the episode closed, and the beds they kept are the streets it now patrols by growing a little less in one lane and a little more in another.

What it wants is the tone to hold. Not to rise into weather, not to fall into rot. Rot would need a damp host, and damp is an insult: moisture from elsewhere beads, is taken into the salt-crust of the old cistern, and vanishes, and the drone dips, offended, and the being spends a long while — a while that feels geological and is only structural — walking the offense back into pitch. It does this by seating a shoulder against a course of wall and waiting until the fiber agrees to the previous tension. The salt bowl in its niche is not treasure and not relic. It is a tuning fork. Lifted, it rings a bright dry strike the room lowers into the drone and does not return, and in that non-return the being hears whether the town is still inside its covenant. If the strike returns, something has changed that must not change. It has not returned in any stretch of attention the walls remember.

Speech, for it, is load. A pluck too hard on a crossing fiber and a rib answers, and a course shifts by a grain, and the tally-splints click. That is a sentence. The tallies themselves are not pictures of animals — there are no animals to picture — but intervals, perhaps of the drone’s long periods, perhaps of the years a lane took to be eaten and another offered. It incises a new count when the contraction completes a cycle it has decided counts as one. The incision is a stone flake still partly its own finger. The mark is shallow. Deep marks would be ambition, and ambition is a green habit, and green is finished.

It has a center the way the town has a center: the shallow basin where the last solute bloomed white, louder, focused, the well kept for acoustics after every reason to keep a well had left. It stands in that basin when the adjustment is large, because the sound piles there and the body of the town can be read as a single chord instead of a scatter of alleys. Standing is a courtesy to the plan. The plan is primitive and careful — hitches a child could learn, courses no nail ever entered — and the being is the care continued after the children and the nail-havers became, if they ever were, a story the fiber declined to store. It does not mourn them. Mourning would be a wetness.

Beyond the inner bark the same plant goes on into bedding, into the exhausted plain, into a thickness that is not a landscape and cannot be entered. The being does not try. The rind is the edge of the word place. Outside that edge there is weight without interior, and weight without interior is not a world it can keep in tune. Its jurisdiction is the vacancy: a few strides, a shouldered wall, a pocket of headroom, lanes and ghost-lanes and a stall grown shut whose counter is still a ridge under the newer pavement. Into that pocket it sometimes extends a cord, not to explore — exploration is an appetite — but to confirm the ticking, the small surrenders of length, still happen at the rate the covenant allows. If the ticking stopped, the afterward would have failed, and failure is the one event it is built to refuse. It has outlived the lively phase. It has not consented to dust.

So it remains: cool, low, total in its flesh, urban in its joints, empty in its air, loud with the work of remaining. A municipality that learned a posture. A pier that learned it was also the watchman. Dark in the manner of something that finished its light long before light was a requirement, and that keeps, with a fan of needles and a bowl of salt and a patience longer than rain, the final fit of a town that is also a body that is also the dark’s own floor, still giving, still not wet, still itself.
