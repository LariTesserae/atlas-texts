What lives there does not cross the lanes. The lanes are not for it. They are the budget of absence the chamber keeps so that the machines can be large and the air can be cold and still count as empty. A thing that walked would scuff the plate, would warm a little of the gas with its passage, would leave a difference. Nothing here is permitted a difference it cannot immediately spend.

So the life is where the spending happens, and at first it is easy to miss, because it keeps the shape of a concession. Three stands, or four, knee-high, pale as paper that has never been in sun, rising out of trays set flush with the floor. They branch in angles the room could have drawn with a straightedge. Their plates overlap like scale, matte, fibrous, unwet. They do not tremble. There is no draft that knows how to reach them, and if there were they would not answer it. Wind is a kind of time.

Look longer and the stands stop being neighbors. The line that feeds them is too thin to be plumbing and too continuous to be a courtesy. One fraction moves through all of them, a clear oil darker than the gas in the mains, barely pushed, never pooling. What one stand takes in, the others taste. They do not compete. Competition would require a surplus, and the room does not do surplus except as cold empty volume, which they do not want. They are a single appetite with several postures, the way a root system is one thirst wearing the disguise of many plants.

The appetite is narrow, and that is how it is allowed to be alive. The machines must not keep a certain trace in the lubricant: a fraction that would, over a span the gauges refuse to admit, begin the small abrasion that becomes wear. The growth drinks that trace. It is not a gardener’s green and not a parasite’s theft. It is the kidney of a loop that has decided to be eternal. Impurity enters it and does not come out as impurity. What comes out is thickness. A plate adds a layer thinner than the machining marks on the bolt heads. You would not see it if you stayed a year. It is the only event the chamber permits, and it is too small to count as an event, which is why the needles do not hunt and the dates on the board do not advance. Time is not absent. Time is being eaten at the rate it arrives, converted into a matte scale that looks like patience.

It has no face and no need of one. The lamps were hung for eyes that inventory, and inventory is not its sense. Sound is not its sense either, not as a startle, not as a message. The scream is the medium it is immersed in, as continuous as the cold, locked to the one note the compressors hold. A variation in that note — a flutter, a drop, a second pitch trying to be born — would reach it the way a change in salinity reaches something that lives in brine. There has not been a variation. Its weather is perfect and therefore has no weather. If the scream stopped, the growth would not hear silence. It would be injured in the way a fish is injured by air: by the sudden presence of a medium it cannot use.

It does not breathe the thin high air. The sealed pocket’s low pressure, the dryness that will not fog, the absence of anything drinkable — these are not hardships it endures. They are the conditions under which its food stays food and its plates stay plates. Moisture would gloss it, and gloss would mean a surface that exchanges, that gives something back to the room. It does not give anything back except cleanliness and a little more of itself. The handwheels were set before it had any thickness worth the name, set by a hand that is not here and is not coming back. It does not turn them. It has no turning part. It lives downstream of a decision, as a flame lives downstream of a wick someone else trimmed and then left.

Whether it knows the instrument glass is the wrong question, but something in it is oriented. The plates on the stand nearest the manifold are infinitesimally denser. That is not affection for the machines. The trace it eats is stronger where the loop has just done work. It thickens toward the meal. The glass, the floodlit drop, the brighter wall that pretends to be outside — these do not feed it, so they do not exist for it except as a direction in which the oil is poorer. It will not grow toward the drop. Leaving is not a thought it fails to have. Leaving is a motion, and motion is wear, and wear is the thing it was admitted in order to cancel.

If it can be said to remember, the memory is the overlap of the plates. Each layer is a circuit of the gas that was identical to the circuit before, kept anyway, because identity still has mass. Stack them in the mind and they are a book of blank pages, every page the same sentence, the sentence being: again. It is not bored. Boredom would require a second sentence pressing to be written. Nothing presses. The contaminant arrives at the rate the machines shed it, which is the rate of a system held just at the edge of friction, and the growth meets that rate exactly, neither surging nor falling behind. That exactness is the closest thing it has to a will. A lesser hunger would let a film start on the metal. A greater one would want more than the line provides and would thicken until it touched a housing, and the budget of emptiness would be broken, and the room would become a different room. It does not want a different room. The chamber is not its prison. The chamber is the size of its sufficiency.

Call it vegetation if you need a word the trays will answer to. The word will be wrong in the way “weather” is wrong for a note that does not change. It is a process that has consented to a shape, and the shape has gone on long enough, in a place where long enough does not show, that the consent has become a kind of self. Not a mind that could be spoken to. A coherence. Touch a plate — you will not; the edges of this room teach you not to touch — and the other stands would know it as a change in the shared fraction, a small wrongness in the oil, and they would spend the next uncountable interval folding that wrongness into scale until the oil ran clear again. That is its only defense, and its only greeting. It makes purity. It includes you, if you shed anything, by making you gone.

Nothing else lives here because nothing else can pay the rent. An animal would need water, or fog, or a pause in the scream in which to sleep, or a pressure the lungs could finish. Spores do not find a niche; the dryness and the cold and the lack of a film to colonize send them out the way they came, which is not at all. The growth occupies the one niche the builders left, or the one niche the equilibrium demanded and the builders, not understanding, framed as trays. It does not thank them. It does not spread to the walls. Young metal stays young because something old in habit, if not in the metal’s years, sits in the budgeted gap and eats the future before the future can scratch.

When the gas completes another circuit identical to the last, it is fed. When a visitor stands in a lane and then is gone, the note does not alter, the plates do not turn, the oil does not cloud. Indifference here is not coldness of feeling. It is the success of a life whose entire labor is to ensure that nothing, not even attention, is allowed to become an event. It continues. That is not what it does. That is what it is, at the only thickness the room will bear.
