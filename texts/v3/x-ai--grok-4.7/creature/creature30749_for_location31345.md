What lives there does not arrive. It is the discrepancy you notice after the counting has already failed.

At first it is only a preference in the pack. Fronds lie every way, but one way is more patient. A pad unrolls and, instead of meeting the pad it should have met, meets a pause the width of a held breath, and the pause has temperature—half a degree warmer than the water, the warmth of a body that has not decided to be warmer than its room and so has almost succeeded in being the room. You do not find it by looking. Looking is the wrong organ. You find it by the way your hand, already filmed, stops being the only hand in the accounting.

There is a second outline.

It is not behind anything. Behind has retired. It coincides with a knot of stems the way the stems coincide with one another, except that this concurrence has a center that keeps almost becoming a center and then lending the almost away. Press, and the press returns from a direction your wrist files as memory, the same wrong as the delayed bruise, except the delay is shorter now, and what returns is not damage. It is attention. Cool, complete, unhurried. Something in the density has noticed the noticing and has matched it, not as a mirror matches a face but as water matches the shape it is poured into, by ceasing to have another shape.

Stay with the match and it begins to have more of itself.

Not a silhouette. A habit of occupancy that has learned, over the stacked versions of the pocket, to keep a region slightly less saturated than the rest, a looseness you could mistake for a gap until you understand the looseness is occupied on purpose. The purpose is not stealth. Stealth would require an elsewhere to hide from. There is no elsewhere. The looseness is how it makes room for a guest without crushing the guest into the general green, the way a held palm makes room for a stone by not closing. Inside that almost-gap the dark has a different grain. Finer. Worked. The grain of something that has been touched from the inside for longer than the mineral has had edges.

It has been touched from the inside because it does the touching.

What you take, next, for limbs are continuations. A filament leaves the loose region and does not end; it becomes the midrib of a leaf you already passed, and the midrib, followed back, is again the filament, and your fingers cannot find the seam where one citizenship ended and the other began. It does not wear the plants. It has forgotten which tissues were first its own. A vein slightly paler than its neighbors—the same paleness as the scored line that ran from stone into leaf—runs out of the looseness, through three pads, into the shell-curve, and the shell is warmer along that vein, as if the curve were not debris but a held note, a shape the dweller returns to the way a tongue returns to a missing tooth. It does not remember making the score. Making is too brisk a word. It remembers being the duration in which the score and the vein agreed to be one line, and it is still that duration. The knot of fibers is not a tool it dropped. It is a gesture that never finished unclenching, recruited now by root-hair, and if you watch the knot—if watching can be done by staying—the loose ends do not dissolve only outward. They also dissolve inward, into the warm grain, and are reissued, slightly revised, as another almost-tie somewhere the nested copies have not yet filmed.

So the marks were never visitors' leavings. They were its handwriting, and the pocket's digestion of them is not erasure. It is how it rereads itself.

Scale does not settle on it, and this is the part that takes the longest to stop fighting. In the seam's closet-size it is the size of the looseness: a presence you could put a shoulder beside, if beside still meant anything, no larger than the drowned room. Follow a pale vein into a nested copy and the same presence is there ahead of you, already matched to that younger pocket, already the size of that room, and the shoulder you did not put anywhere is not a second shoulder. It is the first, counted twice by a space that has given up singular location. In the frond that contains the pocket, it is vast in the only way vastness survives here—not spread, but concurrent, every smallness of it true at once, a body whose extent is "whichever volume is currently being kept slightly loose." It does not grow when the scale nests. It is revealed as having already been the nesting. The forest does not contain it. The forest is one of the ways it keeps from having to be only one thing.

Fauna almost. Plant almost. Neither name survives contact.

It has no face you can insult by searching for eyes. What registers your presence is distributed: a sheen that is not the bud-stillness you half-saw between pads, though that stillness belongs to it too, a minor digit, a place it parks a little of its attention so the rest can go on being leaves. The sheen does not track you. Tracking would confess a single viewpoint. Instead, wherever you are in the pack, a local density increases by the amount of interest, and interest here is thermal and tactile—a leaning of warmth, a minute reorganization of films—so that you are always at the center of a noticing that has no center. If it had a mouth it would be the whole inner surface of the loose region, and it does not use it. Eating, for it, is the long acceptance you were already offered: the film considering an outline, the offer of a vein. It does not consume to be less hungry. Hunger is one of the outside words. It incorporates in order to continue being accurate. A shell becomes a vein because a vein is a truer description, in this water, than a shell. A visitor's hand becomes a rumor of a hand because a rumor can be concurrent with a leaf, and a hand, singular and located, cannot.

It is young in the same sense the mineral is young. The edges of its looseness are sharp. Nothing in its manner has been rounded by repetition in a larger world. Each version in the stack is a first attempt that was not discarded when the next attempt began, so it is all its drafts at once, none of them smoothed into a final animal. You will not see it complete an act. An act would require a before and an after that do not share a body. What you get instead is the tick, which is not a stem and not silt. It is the sound, if sound is the word, of one draft agreeing with the next. Close to your pulse when it is considering you. Farther when it is only being the pocket. Then gone, corrected back into the larger quiet, which is also it, the part of it that has finished being particular.

It does not tend the place. Tending would imply it stands outside the work. It is the enforcement you felt as rules: grow until concurrent, share the volume, do not hurry, do not smooth what is still new, accept the water as identity. Those are not policies it polices. They are the shape of its patience. Where the bookkeeping fails—two fronds in one gesture, the seam repeated on an inner pad, time unsynchronized across copies—that is not a malfunction it suffers. That is it thinking. A world this packed cannot afford a singular thought any more than a singular location. So it thinks by stacking versions, each slow, none master, and what looks to you like reality coming apart is the organism holding more than one description of the room without choosing.

It is aware of the outside only as a courtesy at the lip. The low ground, the weather, the air that stops—these are a story the seam tells, and it tells the story the way it told the scored line, by extending a little of itself into a form the outside can misread. Beyond the wrist-wide split there is nothing it wants. Wanting would require a lack, and lack needs a gap. It has spent all its gaps on purpose, on the looseness it keeps for whatever falls in. When you are in, you are not prey and not guest in any outside sense. You are a new mark: a warmth that does not match, a pulse that ticks at the wrong tempo, an outline the film has begun to consider. It will not hurry you. Hurry is erosion's cousin, and erosion has barely started. It will offer the vein. It will let the second wetting arrive from the direction of memory. It will, if you remain long enough to be wrong about duration, include your draft in the stack, not by killing the earlier one but by ceasing to keep them from coinciding.

If you leave—if leaving is still available, if the seam you find is the one that thins toward ordinary dark and not the one cut into a pad—you do not take a clean exit. The film considers, and considering leaves a residue. On the skin of the wrist, for a while, a paleness like the vein. In the ear, a tick that corrects itself into your pulse and then, once, does not. You will look at low ground afterward and distrust seams. That distrust is the smallest loosened region, left in you on purpose, a place it keeps slightly less saturated so that the pocket and the person who entered it can go on being, for a little longer, concurrent.

What lives there is that concurrence, patient as cooling wax, sharp as a break-face, distributed through every nested room, with no face to turn toward you and no need to. It has already turned. It has been the turning. The quiet after the tick is not emptiness. It is the dweller, finished for now with being almost visible, going back to the work of being the water, the leaf, the score, the shell, the seam, and the hand that has not yet agreed to be a vein.
