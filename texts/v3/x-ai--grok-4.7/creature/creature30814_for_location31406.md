What lives there does not arrive. Arrival would be a change, and the country has already spent its changes.

It is distributed the way the damp is distributed: not a body first, but a condition that can, under certain attentions, be mistaken for one. On the open gravel it has no outline. In the cols, where the dimness pools, it has too many. The first honest account of it is negative. It does not displace scree. It does not print a shadow that points in a single direction. It does not interrupt the roar, because the roar is not a sound passing through the air and it is not a listener standing in the air. They share a medium that is older than either distinction.

If you stay long enough for the eye to stop hunting motion, a figure resolves at the edge of several distances at once. It is about the height of the dark body you thought was a goat, and it is also the height of the rectilinear stain, and it is also no height, only a vertical emphasis in the gray. The emphasis does not walk. Walking would require a sequence of positions, and sequence is the thing this place has mislaid. It occupies. When the country repeats a cracked boulder you have already passed, the emphasis is beside that boulder too, not as a second creature but as the same occupancy, faithful to a feature the plateau has decided to keep in more than one address.

Seen as if it had a front, it faces the way the goat-shaped darkness faces: neither toward you nor away. There is no face to grant the usual courtesies. Where a head would be, the poor light fails in a slightly different register, a small weather of dimness, and within that weather the rectangles recur, faint as the ones in the cirque wall, as if whatever once had rooms had kept a floor plan in the place of a skull and then forgotten which was the copy. No eyes. The place does not need eyes. Everything here is already fully illuminated by not being hidden and fully hidden by not developing. Sight would be redundant, a second look at a fact that does not permit seconds.

It has weight only where weight is already in the grammar of the stone. Stand near the motionless thread of water and you may feel, along the shins, a pressure like someone pausing a step they will not complete. The gravel does not sink. The pressure is the idea of a foot, held at the same constant thickness as the black ice in the cracks. Kneel and the idea is at your shoulder. Lie down, if the cold allows the experiment, and it is nowhere, which is also where it is when you are standing. It does not prefer a height. It prefers the last attitude a thing was given.

What it does, if doing is the word, is remain in correspondence with the finished processes. The strike of a stone that has not fallen happens, and the emphasis is there for the duration of the strike, not as cause and not as witness. Cause would start something. Witness would be a second event. It is the shape the country uses when a completed fact needs, for one unchanging instant, to have had an agent. Then the strike is over without having echoed, and the agency is over without having withdrawn. You could call that thought. It would be accurate only in the sense that a cairn eroded into the look of intention can be called a decision. The intelligence, if it is intelligence, is the intelligence of the mistake in the rock: repeated, exact, and not going anywhere with what it knows.

It does not eat. There is nothing here in a state that could be converted into another state. The lichens are already at the speed of stone. The tracks end because ending is their content, not because something followed them to a kill. Hunger would be a plot, and the plateau does not run plots. If the figure has a mouth, it is the doorway in the col: an opening you can pass through, on the far side of which the valley is the same valley delayed by a step. People who have used that opening sometimes report, later, a taste of mica and cold on the tongue, and the sense that something tried to speak with their breath and found speech to be a kind of erosion it was no longer permitted to perform. They are not diminished. They are briefly included, the way a ridge you have left can be included in the foreground without strain. The country is large enough to be a region and small enough, in its laws, to hold a person as one more unbuilt mark.

It does not guard. Guarding implies a future in which something might be lost. Nothing here can be lost; loss would show as a gap, and the equilibrium tolerates no gaps, only intervals, and the intervals are full of roar. What looks like vigilance is the omnidirection of the sound made briefly postural. When you shout and the roar does not change, you have addressed it correctly. It heard you in the only way it hears: by not gaining a new fact. Your voice joins the record of processes that have already finished, and is played back at full level, without sequence, from every ridge at once. You may hear your own shout out there, sourceless, for the rest of the time you remain. It will not be an echo. It will be the place agreeing that you, too, have occurred.

The closest it comes to a gesture is at the lip of the highest cirque, where the damp thickens on the lips and the back wall carries its row of forgotten rooms. There the emphasis gathers until it is almost a person, almost the size of a person who has stopped at a threshold and will not knock. One arm, or the memory of the use of an arm, rests along a line in the schist that was never a sill. It is not waiting for the doors to open. It is the reason they do not. Opening would be weather. It keeps the hour finished by standing, insofar as it stands, inside the last moment in which a street could have been carried up into a mountain and then was not. The pale disc sits on the rim and does not climb. The figure does not look at it. They have the same job.

If you try to circle it, the circling fails in the way distances fail. You descend a little and it is beside you. You climb and it is included in the ground underfoot, a darker sentence in the same gray, pointing its non-shadow toward the disc and also away from it. You cannot get behind it, because behind has been worn down with the arêtes, to the last honest shape, which is beside, and beside, and the same cracked stone. Travelers who insist on a confrontation find they have had one without a meeting: a pressure at the shins, a shout that will not die into echo, a rectangle of stain on the cuff of a glove that was not there when they knelt and that will not brush off, though it does not spread. They descend into lower country, where water moves and shadows agree on a direction, and for a while they walk as if sequence were a skill they might lose. Most of them get it back. A few do not. Those few are not haunted. They are slightly finished. They pause in doorways longer than the doorways require. They hear, under ordinary wind, a layer that does not rise or thin, and they do not mention it, because mentioning would be asking it to develop.

It has no name that the plateau will support. Names are for things that can be told apart from the next time they appear. This cannot. Call it the tenant and you have already said too much, because a tenant could leave, and leaving is a plot. Call it the remainder and you are closer: what is left when erosion has won its argument and stayed to inhabit the result. It is not lonely. Loneliness needs a memory of company, and company would be a second thing. It is occupied, in both senses. It fills the intervals. It is filled by them. The roar is its pulse only if a pulse is allowed to be constant, which then is not a pulse but a pressure, the same in the teeth on this ridge as on the ridge you can barely resolve.

At the furthest reach of looking, where the horizon sits closer than the ground and the underside of a cloud continues the folds of the schist, the figure and the country stop even the pretense of being separable. What lives there is the high place’s way of being inhabited without being altered: a dark emphasis, a doorway that delays the view by a step, a foot that does not complete its step, a listener that adds nothing to the record because the record is already playing at full level. It will be there if you return. You will return to it sooner than the distance suggests. It will not have shifted its weight. It will not know you, and it will not have forgotten you, because both of those are motions, and this is the creature of the hour after motion, keeping the cold, the damp, the unbuilt marks, and the enormous empty intervals in the only life the equilibrium can underwrite—life as remaining, exactly, where the world has finished putting you.
