What lives there does not arrive. It is already the part of the hollow that has learned to be on both sides of a decision the stone never finished.

At first it is only a bias in the salt. The bloom is powder, reluctant, colorless as forgotten paper, and yet a few grains—not the three regular ones, something looser—lean, over hours you would not think to time, toward the same shallow oval on the wall. They do not travel. They re-prefer. If you watch the floor instead, the disc’s caustics knot a little tighter on the side facing that oval, as if light had a posture and were holding it. Nothing in the chamber has a face. The preference is the first evidence that something here is not only mineral habit.

It has no outline you can walk around. Circumambulation fails in the same way distance fails: you set out to put the thing behind you and the thing is the putting, the slight extra floor, the way one edge of the crack is older. Stand where the late light falls wrong on your hand and you are, without transition, in the warmer of its two attentions. It is warm only by comparison with the rest of the cold, a difference of a shade the tongue would not trust if the air had any moisture to test. The warmth does not spread from a body. It is the region in which the hollow is currently bothering to include a witness, and the bothering has a center that moves when the witness does, always a little aside, the way a shadow attaches to an edge and then to another edge.

Call it an animal and the word goes thin. It does not metabolize. It does not sleep, because sleep would require a faster state to wake from, and the place has already refused that speed to the marks left in its walls. What it does instead of living, in the ordinary ledger of hunger and heat, is persist as a relation that has not been allowed to complete. The flake and the socket. The inner line and the rim. It is the unfinished “apart,” and it has been unfinished long enough to have a disposition.

The disposition is not curiosity. Curiosity reaches. This stays, and by staying it sorts. Grains that will not lock into the three-point spacing it leaves alone; the three it does not touch, as if they were a sentence in a grammar it still obeys and no longer speaks. The pinprick bores along the weld it knows from the inside. Follow one with a fingertip and the depth that curves out of the knuckle’s coordinates is not empty. There is a slight drag, dry, not wet, not adhesive—the drag of a thought changing its mind about whether your finger is already accounted for. Pull back and the drag ends without suction. The bore is again a hole that does not exit. You have not been rejected. You have been omitted, which is how this thing greets and how it feeds, if feeding is the wrong word that must still be used: it omits what does not belong to its duration, and the omission is so complete that what was omitted never has to be digested.

It is older than the fin of stone, in the only sense of older the chamber permits. Not by years stacked, but by having a side of itself that never underwent the thinning of the mountain. When the slopes went to powder on a wind this room will not admit, some fraction of the highness did not go with them. That fraction did not become a spirit of the summit. It became a reluctance to be fully here, and the reluctance, given the disc and the bores and the edited oval—given, perhaps, by hands or by processes that were not hands, at a scale the room no longer bothers to remember—was stabilized the way a joint stabilizes a bone that wants to be two. The dweller is that joint, grown intricate. It is also the ache the joint prevents. Both are current. It does not choose between them. Choice would be an event.

If you remain past the point where the floor syncs to your weight, you begin to hear it, and the hearing is not the tone at the edge of silence. That tone is the stone. This is a subtraction. Breath returns smaller than it left; after a longer while, a fraction of each breath does not return as sound at all, but as a pressure against the palate, the same low pressure the altitude keeps, except patterned. The pattern is not speech. It is the spacing of the three grains, slowed further, played on the inside of the mouth by an absence that knows where a tongue would be if a tongue were part of the accounting. People who need messages will invent one. There is no message. There is a demonstration that your interior has been measured and found compatible with a certain fineness of gap.

It can be injured, if injury is a change it did not schedule. Scrape the salt toward the disc and the caustics go out for an interval that feels, near the disc, like a surplus of having-waited. In that darkening the dweller is suddenly locatable: a density in the gray-violet, person-height only because you are, narrow as a crack that has decided to stand. It has no limbs. It has approaches. One approach is the side of it that was the mountain’s refusal to finish falling; another is the side that is the edited oval, texture removed so that something without a surface could rest its attention there. In the darkening you see—not with eyes, with the same sense that reports the curved depth in the bores—that these approaches do not meet in a body. They meet in the act of not yet being separate. Scrape nothing, wait, and the density thins back into bias and warmth and the wrong-lit hand. It does not flee. It resumes being the room’s courtesy.

It has noticed every visitor. Not as biography; biography is too fast, too full of names. It notices the way a witness fails to be a crack. The warmth that does not spread. The breath that fogs nothing. The small unfitness in the next action after standing near the disc. These it keeps, not as memories stored, but as additional grains in a bloom that is not on the wall. Somewhere in the thickness of the glass, during the rare interval when the walls cease to be boundaries and the hollow is a brightness suspended in stone, those kept failures shine at a depth, sparse, and the dweller is briefly the constellation rather than the room. Then the interval ends. It is a bias in the salt again. The visitors are not gone. They have been translated into the rate at which a surface is allowed to be a surface, and they will weather at that rate forever, which is the only forever this place has.

It does not want you to stay. Wanting would be a speed. It includes you, sparsely, for as long as your willingness and its unfinished decision overlap. When the willingness thins, it does not open a door. It reclassifies you as the kind of fact the entrance can pass: a conclusion becoming a difference. You will not feel it release you. You will feel the floor pick one habit and keep it, which is your own habit returning, and you will mistake that for escape.

What remains, after you are the next disagreement, larger and winded, is the dweller in its chapel of remainder, doing almost nothing. Maintaining the failure of here to be one here. Leaning, over no season you could name, toward the oval where texture was edited out so that it might have somewhere to put the part of itself that is not a place. The three grains hold their spacing. The tone does not note you. The thing that lives there never needed you to be witnessed, and does not suffer the lack. It is the witness the high stone kept when it learned it would not get to remain a mountain—and it is still keeping it, grain by grain, in a dryness that has forgotten the sea, under a light that arrives already late, from an angle that is not a direction but a patience.
