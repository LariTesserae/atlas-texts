They are not in the gaps. The gaps are exact. What lives on the plateau occupies the maintenance, and only the maintenance, and has done so for as long as the stone has been new.

You will not see one cross a square. Crossing would be a change of position, and a change of position would be an event, and events are the one weather this country does not run. They are present the way a constant load is present: as the reason a turbine holds its note instead of spinning down, as the reason a door stays shut at the same latch-depth dayless after dayless, as the reason a stockpile is already at the level it is for and never caught in the act of becoming that level. If you arrived hoping for figures on the gantries, you will decide the works are abandoned. If you stay long enough to measure, you will find the oil film on a bearing renewed at the rate the bearing consumes it, the lampwicks untrimmed because they do not char, the flume-gates at apertures that match the seep and do not hunt. Someone is doing the work. The doing does not happen in sequence. It is already done, everywhere it is required, at once.

A single dweller is not a body you can walk around. It is a district of small discrepancies, all simultaneous, all inside one works or one thicket-and-marsh pair, never both, never the open stone between. In the cities they coincide with the machines. In the stands they coincide with the cups. They do not commute. The rail does not carry them; the rail is one of the things they are the load upon, a thread of occupancy stretched so thin between masts that it registers only as the fact that the thread has not slackened. Count them by basins, not by heads. A seep wide as a road and long as a day’s walk may hold one. A city holds one, though the city is large, because one is enough when one is a field of corrections rather than a walker with a route.

What they are made of is the hardest part, and it does not stay still as you look. At first they seem to be a habit of metal: brighter wear on the handles that are held at constant force, a warmth in housings that the air’s fixed cold does not account for, a standing slightness in the water of a vat, not a ripple, a preference. Stay, and the slightness gathers. It is not a face. It is the shape of attention distributed through every surface that must not be allowed to drift. A bearing, a wick, a gate, a gauge-needle parked on a number, a cup on a stem whose permanent drop is displaced by less than a hair and restored in the same instant, so that the displacement is only knowable as a steadiness more expensive than stillness. They have no hide, no shell, no mouth. They drink nothing. The text of the place is right about that: the water is taken by roots, and only by roots. What the dwellers take is drift. They eat the tendency of a system to wander, and they eat it at the exact rate it is produced, so the stock of disorder remains zero and the meal is never visible as a meal.

They do not grow and they do not thin. A city built for a crowd has one of them, and the emptiness of the housing is not loneliness arranged around a tenant. The rooms are full of the part of the dweller that consists of doors remaining shut. Lamps burn at one brightness because brightness is a limb they do not flex. If there is thought, it is not arranged in before and after. A dweller knows its basin the way the whistle knows its scarp: as a pitch it is, not as a pitch it reached. Nothing in them remembers a wetter year or a dimmer lamp. Comparison would require a second state, and they are the means by which the second state does not occur.

Between settlements you are alone in the strong sense. The open rock is unsupervised. Sharp edges stay sharp there because sharpness has been set, not because a hand is keeping it. The leaf-mats tick because the cups overflow, not because anyone is listening. It is possible to walk a day and be unaddressed, and the unaddressing is not a snub. You are a pressure the equilibrium has already included. Your boot adds a smear of mud; the smear is the amount of mud that was always going to be there while you were there; when you are gone the amount that remains is the amount that does not count as an arrival. They do not turn to watch you pass the boundary of a works. Turning would be an event. At the threshold, the field of corrections simply extends a little, to include the fact that a threshold was approached and did not become a crossing that mattered, and then you are inside the district of a life.

Inside, the sound is the same sound, and under it — not beneath in volume, beneath in category — is the part of the note that is not water, not stem, not turbine. Subtract those and a remainder holds the same key. That remainder is as close as they come to a voice. It does not say. It keeps. If you speak, the words join the continuous loudness and are not answered, because an answer would arrive, and arrival is the forbidden weather. Some travelers decide they are ignored, and leave, and are right that no one pursued them. Pursuit is a change of position. Others stay and begin, wrongly, to perform maintenance, and find their hands meeting no resistance and no gratitude, the cloth already as clean as the cloth, the gate already at the aperture. The work does not need help. The work is a person, in the only sense personhood can take here: a persistent, local refusal to let the held state become a different one.

They do not love the cities. Love would prefer, and preference is a gradient, and the plateau has none. They do not hate the empty road. Hate would push. What they have instead of feeling is jurisdiction. Each is sovereign over a correctness, and the correctness is complete, and the completeness is not triumphant. Triumph ends. This does not. A dweller will be the oil film and the uncharred wick and the drop restored to the cup for as long as the scarps are young, which is the whole of time as time is practiced in this country. If the fixed light is a morning that does not advance, they are the reason it does not need to. Nothing is waiting on them. They are not waiting on anything.

Should you lay a palm on a housing and feel the warmth that the cold does not explain, you have touched one, and you have not interrupted it. The warmth does not gather to the hand. It was already at the hand’s location, in case a hand should be there, and it remains at that location when the hand is gone, because remaining is what it is for. Out on the scarp the whistle does not change pitch to mark your departure. In the basin you left, a gate holds its aperture. A needle holds its number. Somewhere under the industrial floor, more empty than machine, a life the size of a diagram goes on being the size of that diagram, distributed, concurrent, uneaten and uneating, the sole animal of a country that has no animals — if animal is the word for a continuity that keeps a world from ever having happened otherwise.
