What lives there does not occupy the chamber so much as finish it. If you look for a body you will keep looking past it, because its body is a decision already made about thickness, and the decision is a skin.

It is not one thing in the way a fish is one thing, and it is not many things in the way a crowd is. Across the lit ledge, and more faintly on the upper face of the tilted slab, and in a broken band where the pale column loosens against the western wall, the same resident holds. You could scrape a fingertip’s worth of it and still be holding the whole argument: a film no thicker than a damp fingerprint, dark green where the light is enough to justify the pigment, rusting toward ochre where the iron in the water has been invited in and kept. The two colors are not two tenants. They are the same continuity wearing the local facts. Where the light fails, the green withdraws and the mineral remains, thinner, almost only a blush, and even that blush is still it. It does not need the dark the way a burrower needs cover. It simply stops spending on color where color would be waste.

It has no edge you could call a boundary and then defend. A boundary would be a place it is trying to cross. This one has arrived at the extent the water allows and then declined the rest of the rock, not from modesty but from accounting. Carbonate, a little clay, a little metal, a coolness that does not vary: these are the rations, and they are delivered everywhere equally, which means nowhere is rich enough to reward a rush. So it does not rush. The blades you might have called moss are not separate plants. They are the film standing up for a finger’s length where a grain of the limestone offered a slightly kinder hold, and then remembering itself and lying down again. They do not lengthen while you watch. They are not young. They are the same age as the skin, which is the age of the water’s present chemistry, and that age does not tick.

If it senses, it senses as a surface senses wetness: not by looking, not by turning, but by being the place where a condition is registered as a change in what can be kept. A hand passing through the column does not frighten it. Fear would require a future it is trying to reach. What the hand does is local and brief: a small lifting of silt, a small theft of the dissolved ration, a shadow. The film takes the shadow the way it takes night at the far end of the chamber, by ceasing the work that needs light and continuing the work that does not. When the hand is gone and the mote-fall has settled back into the pockets, the thickness is the thickness it was. Nothing has been learned that needs to be stored. Nothing has been lost that will be replaced by growing toward the loss. Replacement, here, is not growth. It is the same exchange, resumed, at the same rate, which is nearly the rate of stone.

You could call it alive and not be wrong, and you could refuse the word and not be wrong either. It respires, in the modest sense that it trades with the water and the trade is not only mineral. The green is a real green, a pigment maintained against the tendency of pigments to become something else, and maintenance is a kind of hunger even when the hunger is too slow to see as feeding. But it does not hunt, and it does not reproduce in any event you could attend. Division, if it happens, happens as a thickening that is immediately spent back down to the thickness the rations justify, so that birth and restraint are the same gesture. There are no offspring drifting off to colonize the darker floor. The darker floor has already been considered and declined. Emptiness is not a frontier. It is a conclusion the resident reached before you arrived, and keeps reaching, continuously, by not going there.

What it has instead of an appetite is a preference that has become indistinguishable from the shape of the room. It likes the coolness that does not stratify. A warmer lens, a colder seep, would force it to become two things, and it has spent a long time being one. It likes the iron, not as ornament but as structure; the ochre is not a stain on top of it but a lattice it has folded into its own thinness, so that the mineral skin and the living skin are no longer separable by a careful fingernail. You would tear both. It likes the stillness, because stillness is how a film of this economy persists: any current strong enough to be called a current would peel it back to mineral dust and a few green shreds, and the works elsewhere seem to know this, or at least to produce, as a side effect of their own patience, the condition in which this is possible. Whether the pumps were built for the film is not a question the chamber can host. The film does not thank them. It coincides with them.

Sound does not reach it as sound. The low note from the distant engines is only another constancy, kin to temperature, and constancy is the medium it is already made of. Your presence is a small weather. It does not answer. Answering would be a change of thickness or of color or of place, and none of those are how it spends what it has. If you stay long enough to bore yourself, you may begin to think it is watching, because the light on it is steady and the green does not flinch. It is not watching. Attention is a luxury of animals, and the water has already made its ruling on animals. What you are feeling is the peculiar pressure of a life that has no interior to hide and no exterior left to want: a life that is only its surface, held at the exact depth of a fingerprint, across every stone the ration can pay for, and nowhere else.

Leave, and it does not close anything behind you. There is nothing to close. The column continues to fall on the same ledge, the same slab, the same broken band on the western wall. The film continues to be the thickness it has negotiated. In a year the negotiation will have the same result, which is not stasis exactly, because the exchange never stops; it is a moving balance so even that only an instrument crueler than eyes could catch the moving. To everything that lives by episodes — feeding, startling, crossing a room — it will seem like an absence that happens to be colored. It is not an absence. It is the chamber’s one inhabitant, and it has already become as much itself as the water will allow, which was always the only permission it was going to use.
