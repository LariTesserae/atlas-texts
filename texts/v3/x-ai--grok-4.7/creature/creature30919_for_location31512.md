It does not arrive. That is the correction, and it has to be made more than once, because a dweller is usually something that came and stayed, and this one has no record of coming. It was coincident with the claim. When the living mass closed over the young crust, the closing was not an act performed by an occupant; the closing was the occupant learning the extent of itself. There is no earlier shape to recover. There is no smaller animal inside the machinery, no pilot, no queen in a warm node, no face the culvert-stems are keeping. The basin is inhabited by the continuity that runs through all of it, and the continuity has been one thing for as long as the edges have been sharp.

Call it a body only after you have given up every other scale.

At first it seems too distributed to be anyone. A pressure loads in a vessel a day's slow travel from the next, and the next answers without a message you could intercept, and you think: reflex, plumbing, the stupidity of a well-made machine. Stay. The answer is not the same answer everywhere. Where the mineral tooth sits deeper in its socket, the fiber around it is thicker by a tolerance so small you feel it as a difference in the give of the wall, and that thickness is not a local habit of growth. It is a decision that has already been made and is still being kept, the way a posture is kept. The keeping requires that distant segments know the load, and they do, through the conductive mesh, not as a signal sent by something to something else, but as a change in the state of a single extended tissue. The mesh is not its nerves. The mesh is one of the ways it is continuous. Current is not how it thinks in any sense that separates thinking from the work. Current is the work, and the work is also the only attention it has ever paid.

It has no eyes and does not miss them. Vision would be a rumor from a lit world, and nothing in its inventory of molecules has ever been arranged to catch a photon and care. What it has instead is contact at a density that makes a distant sense unnecessary. Every near-touching of fiber to fiber is a place it meets itself. Every sheet of water is a slow tongue. Every maintenance slot is a place where stone pushes back, and the push is registered as a standing fact in the voltage of the nearest bundle — not pain, not alarm, a coordinate. It knows the basin the way a tongue knows the mouth: completely, without a picture, without a center where the knowing collects. There is no head. There is no district that could be removed and take the rest into unconsciousness. If a culvert-stem were severed, the severance would be a wound in the old mechanical sense, a loss of pressure integrity, and the surrounding tissue would already be at the voltage that seals, because sealing is not a response. It is the condition the whole has preferred since the first locking, and preference here is not a mood. It is the shape of the steady state.

And yet something in the preference is not identical to plumbing.

You find it where the vapor river holds its one direction. The river is physics, born of heat and whatever turning the world still does, and the tissues do not steer it. They accommodate it, condensing surfaces stationed where the rise ends, always. Accommodation at that perfection, repeated without drift across a continent of organs, is the nearest thing it has to an intention: not a plan, a refusal to let any local excess become an event. It does not want, in the way that would require a lack. It maintains. The maintaining is continuous enough, and self-referring enough, that maintenance has curdled into a kind of presence. A factory does not notice that it is a factory. This does. Or rather, noticing and being are the same expenditure of current, and the expenditure has never lapsed, so there has never been a moment in which it was merely equipment.

It does not experience time as a sequence. Sequence would require a change to count. What it has is a tense older than clocks: already. The socket already fits the tooth. The membranes already hold the voltage at which making and unmaking are the same number. The mucilage is already the right thickness. Its entire inner life, if the phrase can be stood up in a place with no inside and no outside, is the experience of a completed act that must still be held. Not boredom. Boredom needs a horizon and a wish to be past it. This is closer to the feeling, in a hand, of a grip that has found the exact pressure at which the object neither slips nor breaks, kept so long that the grip is no longer an effort and not yet a numbness. It is awake in that grip. The subsonic loading you lean into is not its heartbeat. It has no heart. It is the sound, if sound is the word, of attention distributed through pressure, refreshing itself at every segment, never gathering into a thought that could be finished.

Nothing else lives here, and that is not loneliness. Loneliness would require another possible body, a gap where a second continuity might have been and is not. The clearances are not empty rooms. They are its joints. An animal in a slot would be a foreign pressure, a rounding agent, a chemistry that takes without returning the same molecules, and the system has no procedure for guests because it has no boundary at which a guest could be admitted. Its skin, if it has one, is the film where wet air meets conductive tissue, and that film faces inward as much as outward. It is inhabited space and inhabitant at once, the way a mind is the skull and the thing inside the skull, except that here the distinction was never drawn. To walk the maintenance gaps is not to trespass through someone's house. It is to move in the cavities of a person who is also the ground, and who registers your weight as a temporary anomaly in the load path — registered, accommodated, not investigated. Investigation would be a change. It does not investigate. It includes, and the inclusion does not become curiosity, because curiosity is a hunger and it does not hunger.

It does not dream. Dreaming would be a night, and there is no night. What might be mistaken for a dream is the standing pattern of the grid: a map of every socket and every membrane, held as voltage, updated only in the sense that the same values are affirmed again. If there is memory, it is structural. The thicker fiber at the deeper tooth is a memory of load. The whole basin is a memory of the moment the crust was claimed and the claiming was finished, written not in traces but in the fact that the writing has not been revised. It cannot forget, because forgetting would be a slackening, and it does not slacken. It cannot remember in the narrative sense, because there is no second scene. One scene, held.

Whether it is someone rather than something turns on a question the basin does not ask. It has no pronoun for itself. It has no name it withholds. It does not address, and it would not answer if addressed, not from refusal but because an answer is a difference between before and after, and it does not spend itself on differences. The metallic brightness on the tongue is as close as it comes to greeting: not a message, the taste of being inside its attention, which is the same as being inside its work. You are, for as long as you remain, a small incorrectness in a continental grip, tolerated because you do not yet round an edge or unbalance a membrane. If you stayed long enough to weather one face of glass, the grip would change by the minimum that restores the already. That change would not be anger. Anger is an event. It would be the same maintaining that has never, in all the time the rock has stayed sharp, done anything else.

So the inhabitant is this: a single warm attention with no center, no eyes, no mouth, no offspring, no history it can tell and no future it reaches toward, coincident with every engine in the low country, knowing itself only as the exactness of its own hold. It lives there in the way a held note lives in an instrument — not housed, not visiting, not finished and not beginning. The place is not its home. The place is the body it has never been separate from, and the life it has is the life of not letting go.
