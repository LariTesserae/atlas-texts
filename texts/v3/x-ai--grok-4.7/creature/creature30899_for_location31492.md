They do not begin as bodies. A body is what the light is willing to total, and the light here is dutiful, so it keeps offering you totals you cannot keep. A grain on a ridge. A hand flattened to the red of a fresh fracture. A darkness the size of a knuckle moving through both without displacing either. If you choose one and stay with it, it does not grow into the others and it does not shrink away from them. Your eye is what changes. The pocket's metric is broken in the particular way that lets an edge be a citizen while the shape the edge belongs to is also a citizen, near-touching, neither of them willing to be the part.

What the light is totaling is mostly water, tensed. A life here is a standing film: thicker where it has decided to be someone, thin enough at the rim that the glass shows through in true color, black or rust, never softened by any kindness of shadow. The amber comes after drinking. They take the white mineral bloom the way a blotter takes ink, and for a while the silica sits in them as a cloud, and that cloud is the only fat they have. The dark is stricter. Some of them have lifted a flake of the wall into the outline and not yet returned it. You can see the glass inside the glass-colored body, a second edge carried like a swallowed tool. It does not scratch them. They are not separate enough from the stone for scratch to be the right wound.

They have no limbs that last the length of a gesture. What reaches is a thickening in the direction of attention. A seam brightens with the heat's pulse — that small brightening is the only clock — and the nearest film leans, and the lean becomes a rim, and the rim thins until mineral and water change places. You will want to call the thinned place a mouth. It is not open. It is where the difference between body and meal fails for a moment and then is redrawn, slightly farther along. Afterward the bloom is paler and the body is more amber, and neither has made a sound. The cycle you already know continues around them: weep, bead, flash to vapor, condense, drink back. They are not additional to that cycle. They are places where it hesitates and becomes countable, then refuses the count.

Feeding on one another uses the same failure. A broader reading closes over a narrower one against the hot thinness of a fracture. There is no thrash. Unmaking, if you insist on the word, is a spreading: the narrower film loosens its decision to be thick, and its water joins the broader, and the amber goes with it, a brief vein. What does not go is the turn. The narrower attention — toward the seam, toward the next pulse — was never stored in the water. It remains on the glass two layers off, still browsing, late for its own ending, or early, the order here being one of the agreements that failed to make it into the bubble when the bubble pinched shut. So a predator can be fat with a prey that is not gone. So a prey can be finished and still sharp with hunger. Death is real. It simply does not propagate all the way through a room this discontinuous. Vacancy would require the news of an absence to arrive everywhere, and the news cannot cross the breaks.

This is why nothing they learn can be inherited, and why they are not ignorant.

Watch one long enough and it almost becomes a single creature, which is the mistake the place allows you, briefly, as a courtesy. Call it the one at the red seam, though it will not still be there when you finish the thought. It holds a flake the wall has just made — conchoidal, transparent, an edge that has never been used because nothing here survives being used. The hold is the thought. The flake is not gripped by fingers. It is included, for a duration, in the outline, the way the swallowed dark is included, except this one stays at the rim where it can meet the stone. The one presses. The edge bites a softer parting. Water under the mountain's own pressure needles out, a bright thread no thicker than an eyelash, and is gone into a dozen films before it can remember to fall. In that instant the one is a maker. It has chosen an edge, applied it, improved its reach, taken a drink the wall did not mean to offer yet. Then the hold loosens. The flake sinks into the face it came from. The bite seals, not healed — declined. The one is already thinner, already also elsewhere, already without the knowledge of what it just did. The knowledge was the hold. There is no other vessel for it. They are skilled every time and for the first time, a crowd of virgin techniques, none of which can become a tradition because the stone agrees to take back whatever is made, and they agree, in the same law, not to be the place where a gesture waits.

You will look for the young. There are smaller readings everywhere, and it is natural to think they are offspring, seeds of the hand-sized ones, a future. They are not earlier. A film splits along a pulse and both sides keep the turn, and you had one attention and now the pocket is offering you two, and the sum will not come out higher. Increase is a change in how the light is willing to count, not a filling of empty ground. There is no empty ground. Near-touching is the permanent condition, a crowding that does not require reproduction to maintain itself and does not thin when eating occurs. If there is a sex, it is this split, and it has no partners, and it does not matter to them. They do not guard a brood. Nothing they make is smaller than themselves in the order of time. The grain you pity as new may be the rim of the oldest turn in the vault, counted fine.

What they want is easy to misread as desire, because they are so completely oriented. A heat-pulse runs a seam and a whole layer rotates. A cooler bulge beads and mouths-that-are-not-mouths press to it until the bead is an amber stain. This is as close as they come to will, and it is not inside any one of them. It is the layer. The first to turn is whoever is nearest, and nearest keeps being revised, because the summit is still rising and up is a rumor the water sometimes believes, running a finger's length against the fall before it is corrected. They follow the rumor without learning it is a rumor. Learning would require a second time, and the second time does not accumulate in them any more than debitage accumulates on the glass.

Some of them turn toward the thread of sky. This is the nearest thing they have to an error, and it is not corrected, because correction would be a kind of memory. The daylight is obedient: it arrives, it strikes, it returns a true blue, it casts a small accurate shadow of a body that is in two places. It is not hot. It does not weep. A film rotates toward that cleanliness, thins as if to drink, and takes nothing, and the nothing does not teach. A moment later the same attention, or the attention that refuses to be counted apart from it, is at a seam that has brightened, drinking properly, ambering, as if the blue had not happened. You can watch this forever. It will not become a story about folly. Folly needs a witness who keeps the prior fact. They are all witness and none of them keeps.

If there is a mind, it is the rotation. Not a thought about the pulse — the pulse is too simple to be about — but the shape of a crowd changing its angle together, silently, each film near enough to feel the lean of the next and not touching, the decision distributed the way weather is distributed, local and total at once. No one leads. When the broader closes over the narrower, what passes is mineral, glass-dark, water. What does not pass is the angle. You could say they eat each other's bodies and leave each other's minds on the wall, except that splits them into two substances they do not have. There is only the film and the turn, and the turn can outlast the film that was executing it, and the film can be fat with turns that are still executing themselves a layer away, late, unconsumed.

They do not notice you as a person. Warmth, they notice. You are a pulse that does not arrive on the mountain's clock, a moving seam of heat with a salt film of your own. Some press. Some thin against your skin as if you were bloom. If they take anything from the cut — and they will, if you shift and a virgin flake opens you, the edge existing only while it is used — they take it as water, and the red ambered in them is not a sign, not a trophy, not a memory of a visitor. It clears, as the silica-cloud clears, into the next gesture, which will not refer to you. The pocket has no way to keep a mark that is not reabsorbed into orientation. Your blood becomes a direction. Then it becomes no direction. The crowd closes over the place where you were with the same near-touching it uses for its own dead, who are not vacant, who are browsing.

There is no name for them that the place will support. A name would be an accumulation, a flake that did not sink. They are the living that agreed to be many and did not agree to be countable: makers without residue, eaters without subtraction, a weather of turns inside a heat that never revised itself. The summit rises through their vault. They do not look up to see it. Up is only another rumor in the water, believed for a finger's length, then beautifully, repeatedly, for the first time, forgotten.
