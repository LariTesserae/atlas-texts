Do not prepare as if you were entering a cave, a flooded room, or a marsh. Those are places that still keep a difference between you and themselves. This one does not. What you can do, as an ordinary human, is arrange your ignorance so that it fails in a survivable order, and so that someone outside the seam still believes you have a duration.

Go in the warm part of a day, and do not let the day be important to anyone but a witness. Low ground lies. The lip will be cooler than the weather that found it, and the cool will feel like relief until it is a decision your skin has already started making for you. Have the witness stand where they can see the split and not you. Agree, beforehand, on a length of time that is shorter than either of you trusts. When that length ends, they do not come in after you. They wait one agreed interval more, then they treat the seam as closed, even if your line is still taut, even if they think they hear you in the mineral. Retrieval is how the pocket gets a second presence to make concurrent. Your witness’s only competence is refusal.

Breath is the first lie you will want to pack correctly. There is no air below the lip. A tank, a hose, a mouthpiece: bring the plainest system you know how to use without looking, and assume the pocket will not grant it the dignity of a device. The built is digested here into resemblance. Metal becomes a paler vein; a knot becomes a branching; a straight score continues through a midrib as if it had always been growth. Your air may last in the chemistry and fail in the bookkeeping—two breaths occupying one gesture of space, the second arriving from a direction your lungs file under memory. Practice, on the surface, the ugliest version of the skill: mask flooded, hose fouled, counting abandoned, one hand finding the line by touch alone. Do not practice holding your breath as a virtue. This place has all the time. You do not.

The line is your only argument, and it is a weak one. Use something dull, fibrous, already a little frayed, that could be mistaken for root-hair if the pocket starts recruiting it. Bright cord is a provocation; it reads as a mark, and marks are corrected. Tie it to you in a way that fails open—ankle or wrist, not a harness that becomes a second skeleton. The other end stays with the witness, not with a stake. A stake is a location, and location smears. Have the witness hold it the way you hold a child who might bolt: not as an anchor into the ground, which the pocket does not respect, but as a claim that there is still an outside with hands. Put knots at distances you can feel in the dark, unevenly, so you cannot turn counting them into a comfort. If the line goes slack and then taut from a direction it did not have, do not follow the new tension. Follow the old one, even if it combs the wrong way through the fronds. The pocket folds downhill into itself. A helpful path is the one that has already agreed to keep you.

Do not bring a lamp. Light is not refused here so much as unrecorded. Whatever you lower will be taken as a lowering, not as illumination, and you will spend the first minutes believing your batteries have died when what has happened is that darkness is a material and your beam is a rumor it declines to pass along. Your eyes will not adapt, because adaptation assumes a darkness that is absence. This one has grain. It is cooler in the folds. Accept that you will navigate by pressure, by the comb of fronds, and by the one slight change in coolness that is the seam—the mere dark of the low ground, thinner than the dark you are in. Learn that difference with your face before you learn it with your hands. Hands get offered veins.

Clothing should be a second skin you are willing to lose the outline of, not armor. Sharp tips have not blunted; fracture-planes are still bright. You will be cut, and the cut may not stay on the limb that met the edge. A bruise is a rumor the pocket passes along; it can appear later, on a different pad, identical, deeper in—or, if you have stayed, on a different part of you, as if damage were a message rather than an event. Wear nothing you need to remain uniquely yours. No jewelry, no tags, no names written on tape. A name is a score, and a score continues into a midrib. Cover the hands only lightly. You need to feel the film when it considers your outline, and a glove will tell you too late, after the likeness has already been accepted. Bare wrists. A suit that seals is a polite fiction; pressure leans, and water here is not around the plants but concurrent with them. You will be wetted in places the suit swore were closed, and sometimes wetted twice for one passage. The second wetting is the warning. When it comes, you are no longer in a volume that can hold a single fact. Turn back toward the coolness that thins, not toward the direction your courage prefers.

Eat before, simply, and do not bring food in. There is no appetite you would recognize, but there is recruitment. Anything that occupies space will be asked to share the occupancy. A ration bar becomes substrate. So does a bladder of water, which is an insult besides—an outside idea of water carried into a room that is water. Empty your pockets until you are embarrassed by how little “prepared” looks like. A small blade is a bad idea twice: the edges in there are already younger and sharper than yours, and a tool is the sort of intelligence that leaves method, which this place does not keep. If you must cut a line free, bite it or break it. Leave no angle that remembers a blade.

Timekeeping should be insultingly crude, and worn where you can lose it without losing the exit. A watch will desynchronize from itself across the nested copies; you may look down and find the hand still, or find two intervals stacked, the film on the shell advanced and not yet filmed, both true. Do not try to be the master copy. Agree with your witness on a count you can do with your feet or your breaths, and treat disagreement between your count and your feeling as the signal to leave, not as a puzzle to solve. The processes are not synchronized. Stay long enough to be incorrect about the length of your stay and the pocket begins to include you in the accounting—not as prey, as another presence that must be made concurrent. The cool works inward. A film, not unpleasant, considers the outline of your hand the way it considered the shell. You will want to watch this, because it does not hurt. That wanting is the hazard. Pain would have been a boundary. This is an offer.

Go in without a question you need answered. The marks you will find—the pale curve of shell, the score that becomes a vein, the knot holding nothing—are not messages for you. They are resemblances already being digested. If you came to read them, you will stay for the next version, the one where the shell is a rib and your line is a root, and you will call that reading. Curiosity is a kind of occupation. Occupy as little as an ordinary human can: one body, one errand, one return.

The errand, if you need one to make yourself move, should be smaller than the pocket’s patience. Touch nothing you mean to bring out. A sample is a piece of concurrency torn into the outside, and you do not know which copy you tore it from. If you must confirm you were there—for the witness, for your own later doubt—carry in a single soft thing with a private flaw only you know, a thread, a scrap, already halfway to fiber. Do not retrieve it. Let the witness see you come out without it. That absence is a better record than any photograph, which will not take, or will take a darkness with grain and convince you the camera failed.

How to move: do not swim as if toward a far side. There is no far side; there is nesting. An arm’s reach ends in fronds that continue past the arm. Distances refuse to match, and the refusal is not a maze you can map. Move as if apologizing for the volume you use. Do not turn your head quickly; the comb of the fronds is your compass, and a jerk smears it. When two fronds occupy one gesture and your hand is wetted twice, you have entered the bookkeeping failure. Stop. Find the thinner cool. Do not investigate the second seam. You will find it—the entrance repeated as a slit the width of a wrist in living tissue, opening onto the same water, a pocket that contains the first, slightly younger, the shell not yet filmed. It will feel like a correction, a cleaner start, a chance to have entered better. It is the interior wearing your arrival as a feature. Step through it and you do not exit. You re-enter a version where leaving and remaining are closer than they were. If you find that slit, close your eyes. Eyes will call it a way. Follow the line, or the thinness, with your mouth and the bridge of your nose, the parts of you least interested in resemblance.

If something small holds still between two pads with a sheen the plants do not have, leave it its completeness. Fleeing is impossible for it; gap is impossible. Do not decide it is fauna and therefore a sign of a food web you can reason about. What eats here, if anything eats, does it by becoming continuous. You are not equipped to be a discontinuity for long.

Speak if you need to, but do not expect arrival. Sound here is mostly the absence of itself. A word spoken into this water is not repeated; it is corrected into form, slowly, and you will not be there for the correction unless you have already lost. If panic comes, do not thrash. Thrashing is a claim to unoccupied space, and there is none. Thrashing also makes new edges meet you at new angles. Go slack along the line the way a frond is slack, and let the witness’s refusal be the current you do not have. There is no current you would call a current. Only exchange.

When you come out—if the seam still thins, if the line still remembers an outside—do not stand up into your victory. The low ground is real; the pocket’s elevation was a courtesy it had stopped extending, and your blood may still prefer a downhill that moved. Lie in the ordinary air until the witness says your name and you answer with the flaw you left inside, not with a description. Description is how you smuggle the pocket out in your mouth. You will want to explain the second wetting, the score, the way a tick matched your pulse and then the quiet corrected itself. Do not. Let the witness time you against the agreement. If you are late by a feeling rather than by a clock, say so, and do not go back to check whether the film advanced on your absence. It advanced. That is what patient things do.

In the hours after, watch your hands. Not for wounds—wounds would be ordinary—but for a film you cannot quite feel, a smoothing that is not healing, a vein suggested where you had only an outline. If a bruise appears somewhere you were not struck, do not open it, do not show it to a blade, do not return to compare it to a pad. Wash in water that has a surface you can break and hear. Eat something that required fire. Sleep where a lamp has been recorded by a room that believes in light. Tell the witness the time you were gone, and let them keep the true time if yours disagrees. Disagreement is the scar that stays on the correct side of the skin.

Nothing you carry will govern the place. The rules that remain are not yours to satisfy: grow until concurrent, share the volume, do not hurry, do not smooth what is still new, accept the water as identity. You prepare by declining those rules for as long as a human body can decline them, which is not long, and by arranging that your decline has a witness who will not love you enough to follow. The seam is wrist-wide and unremarkable. That is the last ordinary fact available to you. Enter as if it might close on the width of a wrist and be right to. Come back while leaving and remaining are still two different things.
