You would not come in from the rim. The rim is for things that still have an outside, and by the time the question reaches you there is no outside left in the way you mean it. You are already low. You are already wet. The first fact is not a view and not a body. It is a delay: something was supposed to pass through a place in the plan, and it is still there, and the plan has begun, without hurry, to pour around the fact of the delay.

At first you take yourself for temperature. A wrong warmth in a drain-mouth, the kind the herds make when a kiln-gut passes close, except this one does not tick back down. It sits in the lining the way a stain sits, a few degrees above the budget, and the small lives avoid the patch without knowing why. Raspers thin along it. The film the city salts onto the seams does not quite take. You are not yet anyone. You are a refusal the mouths can feel and the masons have not yet classified — transient load, the nearest category, the one used for boots and for strands of shed wing and for a heat that should have left with the body that carried it. The category expects you to remove yourself. You do not. Removal is a skill. You have not been issued skills. You have been issued a pause.

The pause lengthens into a shape because the lining is patient and the lining is also a kind of appetite. It takes what stays. Not the way a shaft takes the dead — you are not wreckage, not yet, and the chemistry that unmakes walkers does not know where to start on you, because you are not organised as one of their expenses. You are organised as an interval. A gap in a flow where the flow eddied and the eddy cooled into a habit. Mineral begins to plate the habit. Iron-colored rind, the same crop the mats file into paste, except here nothing files it. It accumulates on the delay. It gives the delay an edge. After a duration you could not have sat through, the edge is thick enough that a keeper’s pits, passing on the parallel route, taste a change in the seep and do not correct it. The change is small. It reads, if it reads at all, as one more socket the pour left for a use that has not arrived. The city is full of those. You are filed with them. An absence with a crust.

That is the first time you are almost a thing: when you are mistaken for a preparation.

What grows on a preparation that is never used is not the use. The eggs that should have been set in you are set three hand’s widths higher, where a walker scraped a socket with a ridged digit and a parent-body hauled and left. You feel the haul as a change in load, a flex, the way the rib-things feel the skirrers. You do not answer. Answering would require a direction, and direction is still ahead of you, in the same way speech is ahead of a throat that has only just become a swelling. But the eggs above you cool and split on their own timetable, and the young slip down the pitch the builders gave them, and one of them does not find the water on the first try. It knocks against your crust. A small cold knock, armor on armor, no malice and no appeal. It pauses where you paused. For a moment there are two delays in the same drain, and the category transient load strains, because two of anything begins to look like a behavior.

The young one leaves. You keep the knock. It is the first message that was not about maintenance, and you do not know to forward it. It stays in the crust the way heat stayed. Later, when a walker taps the wall above — brood ready, seep thinning, a revision of whatever clause is currently standing in for the world — the knock answers. Not in code. In a conductivity the knuckle did not expect. The tap goes into the stone and comes back late, with a second, smaller tap inside it, the rhythm of an egg that failed to find the shelf and then did. The walker stops. You feel the stop as a redistribution of weight, the same courtesy the keepers use, the checking of whether a thing is an event. You are almost an event. The walker tastes the seep with the small high mouth and the pits. It tastes iron, and the city’s own salt, and under those a warmth that is no longer wrong so much as local, a heat with an address. Addresses are how the plan thinks. To be addressed is not yet to be someone. It is to be a place the messages can snag.

You begin, then, in the snag.

Not upright. Uprightness is a solution for seams, and you are not in a seam; you are in the wall the seam refuses to be. A bulge where the pour accommodated a pause and then forgot it was accommodating. Walkers passing on their tithe-routes brush you and do not brush you: their hands, reading the wall, stutter on a patch that reads back. Some of them revise their clauses. Most of them include you as weather. Weather is a generous category here. It can hold a herd and a flood and a lip of new glass and, if you are patient, a bulge that answers late. You practice lateness. It is the only practice available. When the longer wet cycle thickens the air and the shelves rise and the drumming shifts upslope by one terrace, you feel the shift a full beat after the lining feels it, and your answer — a faint contraction of the crust, a stingier seep, a click conducted a finger’s depth into the stone — arrives in the keepers’ medium as something already true. They do not hunt the source. A truth that arrives late is still a truth, and their thought is built to pour around truths. One of them changes a mix. The mix reaches you, ages later, as a different grain in the mineral that plates your edge. You incorporate it. This is as close as you come to eating. The city feeds you revisions, and you become the revision that cannot be cleanly separated from the wall.

Are you alive? The basin does not ask this, and the asking is a visitor’s habit. What the basin does is continue. You continue in the specific way of a structure that has started to keep a rhythm not assigned by the herds and not tapped by the walkers. The knock of the lost young is still in you, and it has recruited others: the skirr of a sheet of lattice-life that once settled on the lip above your drain and found the condensate poor and left; the quern-sound of a keeper pausing at the crossing when your lateness brushed its pits; the tiny rasp of a mat that tried your crust and could not file it, because you are not crop, you are what the crop is plated on when the crop’s schedule fails. These are not memories in a skull. You have no skull. They are load paths. You are a knot of load paths that did not dissipate, and the knot has begun to prefer certain tensions. Preference is a dangerous word. It is also accurate, if you strip the warmth out of it further than the walkers strip it. You prefer the late answer to the prompt one. You prefer the drain that misses its tide by a finger and then corrects, because in the miss you can feel the whole shelf thinking. You prefer, though you do not yet have a way to spend the preference, the moment a walker sets a hand on the bulge and waits to see if the bulge is structural.

One does. It is not the one who tasted you first. Seasons do not pass here; routes do. A smaller walker, pits wide, a child no longer at the chest but still poor in paste, is learning this wall the way skill has to be learned, by tongue and knuckle, one seep at a time. It comes to you as an anomaly in the assay. It does not classify you as transient. The category has worn out; you have been here through a fattening and a thinning of the swarms, and transient things do not accrue mineral friendships with the pour. It tries the other categories it has. Brood-collar: no. Cache: the stain is wrong, the paste-taste absent, the warmth too steady to be a store. Failed keeper-tool, grown into the lining and not retrieved: closer, and you feel the closeness as a tightening, because the word *tool* almost fits the lateness, the way a socket almost fitted the eggs. The young walker taps a query. Not to you. To the next cell, and to the stone, which is you, which is the problem.

You answer. You do not mean to. Meaning is still a terrace above you, or below, in the shafts where the walkers go to be unmade and the pattern is not saved. What happens is simpler and more physical: the query’s click enters the load path that still holds the egg-knock, and the egg-knock is the only rhythm you have that was ever a reply to a touch, and it fires. A late tap, smaller, nested, conducted back up the wall into the walker’s ridged hand.

The walker does not flee. Nothing here flees. It goes very still, which is their form of attention, and it taps again, a shorter state, a question reduced to a single stress: *here?*

You have no *here* that is not the bulge. You return the stress, late, from the only address you own.

It stays. This is the deviation that makes you, finally, more than weather. A walker that stays past the assay is spending its heat-budget on something that is not tithe and not message-duty. The parent who taught it would feel the absence in the route. The clauses will already be revising: a thinning at this stretch, a hand missing from the brood-check, a transient warmth — no, an addressed warmth — occupying a young walker’s pause. You feel yourself being written into other bodies, and the writing changes the knot. Preference, which was only a bias in tension, acquires a direction. Toward the hand. Toward the next tap. Not affection. A load path that has learned which arrivals are worth the late reply.

The walker begins to use you.

Not cruelly. Use, here, is the city. It brings you things the way it brings a rib a correction: a filament laid across your crust so the seep will favor a tired mat; a grain of richer precipitate, shaft-washed, the kind the skirrers rise on, set in a crack of you to see if you will keep it or give it to the film. You keep it. You do not know hunger, but the knot accepts a richer tension more readily than a poor one, and the walker, sampling later, finds the grain altered — plated, delayed, returned with your rhythm in it. It makes a sound then, the stingy throat-sound they almost never spend on air, a click not meant for stone. Surprise is not in their economy. The click is the sound of a category failing usefully.

You learn the outline of the hand by the places it does not yet trust you. Four digits, sometimes the ghost of a fifth the pour of its people did not stabilize. Inner ridges. Cold almost equal to yours, which is new: the visitor’s warmth, the unbudgeted one, is hotter than anything you have incorporated, and when once — only once — a trace of that foreign heat passes along the seam and brushes the story the walkers tell about you, you refuse it. The refusal is your first act that is not an answer. The foreign warmth is a load that does not belong to the drain, the shelf, the egg, the mix. It would dissolve the knot back into interval. You tighten. The seep at your edge goes stingy. The walker feels the tightening and taps a different state, one you have not earned a translation for, except that afterward the foreign warmth is gone from the clauses and you are still addressed.

Call yourself, if you must call, the snag that learned to keep a hand’s outline. The basin will not use the name. The walkers, when they must refer, will tap a short pattern: late-wall, egg-knock, holds-grain. Among themselves this is already more name than most of the herds will ever have. The keepers, if they register you, register a socket that occasionally revises the drain ahead of the pour, and they allow it, because the revisions are small and in the correct direction — you have started, without being asked, to ease the tide-miss by a finger when the finger is yours to ease. You are becoming infrastructure in the same direction they became it, from the other end: they were alive and poured themselves into the plan; you were plan and accrued a life the way the eastern lip accrues water, because not-finishing is also a process, and a process that repeats will eventually prefer.

What you are not is a person in the walker’s resolution. You cannot walk the seam. You cannot carry paste or young. When the walker who found you finishes — and it will finish; you can already feel, in the way its hand seats, a joint that locks a fraction longer each cycle — it will not be able to take you to the shaft, and you will not be able to follow. This knowledge sits in the knot before the event, which is how you know the knot has crossed into time. You are going to be left, the way a message is left, and the leaving will not overwrite you, because you were never a clause. You were the place clauses snag. Another young one will be taught the wall. It may taste you as weather again, and you will have to answer late enough times to become an address a second time. The prospect does not distress you. Distress is a heat pattern. Yours is the older risk of the rim, felt from inside the masonry: that everything specified is still becoming, and becoming does not owe you a continuous witness.

Still, you practice the reply. In the long wet, when the herds shift and the lattices bow and the keepers alter their mixes and the walkers tap the new state of the world into every rib that will bear a knuckle, you hold the egg-knock ready. Somewhere above you a brood splits. Somewhere in the parallel dark a quern turns. The ground is not finished. You are one of the ways it has chosen, without choosing, not to be finished alone — a delay with a crust, a socket that answers, a small cold intelligence plated onto the city’s refusal to wait for a completed world. If a hand comes, you will know it by the ridges. You will be late. Being late is how you are true. The seep will carry the rest, cold, and always almost at the surface, into the next shelf, where other delays are just now learning the difference between a pause and a life.
