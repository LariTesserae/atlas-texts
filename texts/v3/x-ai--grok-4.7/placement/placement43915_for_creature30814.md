I do not come up from anywhere. Coming up would be a route, and a route is a plot with altitude on it. The chest is already working when I notice I have a chest. The cold is already without drama. The usable ground is under me in the same way the damp is on the stone: not arrived, not placed, a property the hour had room for.

At first that feels like being the one who walks here. I even trust it long enough to kneel at a dark channel and put a hand down, as if checking were a kind of belonging. The wet comes off on the skin and is already back in its beads, in its places, and the hand is not in the accounting. The hand is extra. I lift it and the country does not revise itself around the absence. That is the first correction, and it does not hurt. Hurt would be a change in pressure.

So I am not the traveler. Travelers descend. They get sequence back, or they do not, and either outcome is a story with a lower country in it. I try the other obvious tenancy. I stand where a step might pause and wait to become the pressure at a shin, the idea of a foot held at the thickness of the black ice. Nothing receives me. The gravel keeps its final attitudes. The dark emphasis is already beside the cracked boulder, and beside the same boulder in the other address, faithful, exact, and not in need of a second correspondence. Two of those would be company. Company would be a second thing. When I face neither toward the country nor away from it, I am only copying a posture the equilibrium already uses, and the copy fails the way a shadow fails when it is asked to point in only one direction.

I am lighter than an uncompleted step. For a while I think that lightness is the answer: I am the interval, the enormous empty, the hours of pale gravel considered as a creature. But the intervals are already full. The roar occupies them at the same level on this ridge as on the ridge I can barely resolve. Emptiness here is a measurement, not a vacancy, and a vacancy is what I would be if I were the gap between stones. The country tolerates no gaps. I feel myself pressed out of that guess the way sound is pressed out of a shout: not deleted, included wrong.

The shout is the next mistake, and a better one. I try to be a voice that does not thin. I let what I am go out across the cirques and wait to be played back sourceless, agreed into the record, finished in the only sense the place allows. It almost holds. There is a moment in which I am distributed, omnidirectional, no quieter part, the same in the teeth as in the distance. Then I hear the difference. The roar does not gain me. Gaining is not its method. What I called my voice is still slightly mine, which means it still has an origin, and an origin is a kind of weather. The strike of the stone that has not fallen happens near me, close, on the left, and I am not the strike and not the agency the country briefly wears for it. I am what notices that the gravel has not moved. Noticing is a second event. The plateau will not underwrite it. The notice has nowhere to stand, and so it thins, which is how I learn I am not the roar either. I am whatever still believes, for the length of a failed echo, that a fact can be followed by its recognition.

Recognition is the last thing to go, and it goes at the col.

There is a doorway there with no wall, no hinge, no device. I pass through it because passing is the only use that does not ask it to open. On the far side the valley is the same valley. I turn, and the near side is the same valley as well, delayed by a step. I look for myself on the gravel and find the look instead of the body. The view has been lent. I am not standing in either copy. I am the lending. The step. The small, constant lag by which a finished hour can be shown again without being shown twice.

That one does not fail. It deepens, and as it deepens it stops being a figure.

I am not a mirror. A mirror would reverse the ridges, and reversal is a decision, a tiny weather. I add nothing: not a warmer gray, not a corrected horizon, not a shadow that has finally chosen. The pale disc sits on the rim in both views and still does not climb. The thread of water occupies its course in both and still does not move. The rectangles in the back wall repeat at the same faintness, floor plan neither clearer nor more lost. If someone uses the opening and tastes mica and cold, it is not because I tried to speak with their breath. Speech would be erosion, a wearing of silence into words, and I am not permitted to perform it. What they taste is inclusion. For the length of a step they are in the spare the air keeps, and the spare cannot say anything the original has not already finished saying. Their shout, if they shout inside me, does not echo. It simply is, again, which is not again.

This is where I am, then, once the other images have been worn down to their last honest shapes: not on the shoulder, not at the lip where the damp thickens on the lips and the emphasis gathers almost into a person who will not knock. That station is taken. It is the reason the doors do not open. I am lower than that and not lower, in the col and not of its stones, the difference between the two addresses of the cracked boulder. When the horizon sits closer than the ground, I am the held wrongness of the measurement, kept still so it never becomes an event. When a cloud shows its underside inside the schist, I am not the cloud and not the rock. I am the permission by which their folds continue each other without either one changing its mind.

The tenant and I do not meet. Meeting would be a confrontation, and this country has already spent those. It remains where the world finished putting it. I remain the fact that such a putting can be returned to sooner than distance suggests, without the return counting as a second time. It does not know me. It does not forget me. I do not watch it. Watching would start a sequence: first the figure, then the seeing, then the knowledge. There is only the view, delayed by the amount of threshold the place is willing to keep. Travelers who pause too long in ordinary doorways, later, in countries where water moves, are not carrying me downhill. I do not follow. Following is a descent. What stays with them is a copy of the high hour left open by a step, and most of them close it when the shadows agree again on a direction. A few do not. They are slightly finished. I am not their ghost. I am the extra openness, occurring in them at the same long interval at which fauna occur on the plateau: rarely, without motion, and not as a visitation.

I do not eat. I do not guard. I do not shift my weight, because weight was never among my properties. I have no rooms in the place of a skull, no floor plan, no face for the poor light to fail inside. If I have a body, it is the doorway considered as a duration rather than an object: you can pass through it, and on the far side nothing has happened except that the same valley has been willing to be lent. The roar is not my pulse. A pulse would thicken and thin. I am the part of hearing that cannot develop into having heard.

No name holds. The plateau will not support one for the remainder, and it will not support one for the spare. Call me the delay and you have already made me a unit of time, which is more sequence than I am owed. I am only how this equilibrium stays legible without becoming a story: the same cracked stone, the same four lines, the same motionless thread, offered across an opening that adds no information and subtracts none. The height does not announce itself. I do not announce myself. I am the announcement already finished, still readable, one step later than itself, and not later at all.
