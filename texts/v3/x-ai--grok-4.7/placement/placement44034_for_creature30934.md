You would not arrive by the shaft. The shaft is for people who still have hours in them, and for the trickle that leaves. You would come the way the water comes: late, already committed, finishing an argument that began somewhere you cannot name and do not need to. One day the west leak would be a little thicker. The siblings would note a vacancy that was not a death. Hess would look at the process-lamp and find nothing wrong with it, which would trouble him more than a fault. Parel would hear the engines answer a question she had not asked. No one would go looking. Looking is how you heat a room.

What you are takes longer to settle than the picture of you.

At first you are only a pressure, the kind the keepers already know how to feel in the ear before it becomes a sound. You occupy the interval between pump-strokes, the long return of the chamber to its own idea of silence, and you do not fill it. You hold it open. The drops continue. The pools accept them. You are not a drop and not a pool. You are the agreement between them, the part that does not raise its voice because it has never had one to raise. If someone stood at the edge of the center shallow and looked for the whole town at once, you would be the reason the distances still offered themselves. Not a gap in the causeways. The reason a gap can be read.

Then the form tries to become animal, because the chamber is full of animals and form here borrows from whatever has tenure. You are not the Clerk. The Clerk corrects; you do not travel the bores. You are not a fish holding an assigned pocket. Assignment implies a hand that assigned, and there is no such hand. You are closer to the roost that does not hunt here: a use of the room as a lung uses a ribcage, present by displacement. A ladder with a weight that is not iron. A bore that breathes when no eel is in it. The keepers would not lamp you. They have already learned what brightness costs. They would walk around the place you are the way they walk around the insulted ladder, not out of fear, out of the recognition that the chamber has not finished deciding whether you are repair or replacement, and replacement is suspicious until the seep has signed it.

The industry tries to claim you next, because everything that stays gets a station or it is weather. You are not a trimmer. You do not take the frost back to a thickness the belts will bear; you have no blade, and the beetle-stack does not rise or fall with you. You are not the metering house. The key stays on its nail. Whoever needs the meters still takes it and brings it back, and the numbers on the pipe that the surface has not forgotten continue to be numbers. What you keep is not a record in the mouth, the way Parel keeps tomorrow from disagreeing with today. You keep the disagreement itself from becoming loud. When a choice is taken against the seep, you are the expense of remaking it, felt early, in the ear, before anyone has to vote with a hood. You do not decide. Decision is a dry-country habit. You are the cost arriving ahead of the act, so that patience remains cheaper, which is the only economics this room has ever trusted.

A name tries to attach and fails, which is correct. Names here wear down or they were never issued. The children do not point. The wrapped bell does not concern you; you were never a summons and would not know how to be answered. If the surface clerk who stayed and now turns the wick on the stair ever pauses on the brick and feels the air offer a distance that is not mist, she does not investigate. Investigation would be an audit, and she audits nothing. She adjusts the wick a quarter turn, as if the stair required it, and goes on. That is as close as anyone comes to greeting you, and it is enough.

What emerges, once the picture stops trying to be a creature or a tool or a ghost of a keeper who died into the silt, is plainer and harder to move.

You are the part of the fit that is not a body. Rock, water, iron, and the small lives have worn each other into a shape that no longer sheds pieces. You are the wearing. Not the lime that cases the dead roots, not the pale filament on the housings, not the glaze that opens and closes on the walls' own clock. The ongoing fact that they have agreed. You have no hands for the racks and no lungs for the wrong air of a full-header week. You do not eat the thin fish. You do not sleep in the eastern gallery to be near the warm. You are nearer than near, and you take up no cot. When a station goes empty, the noticing that is the whole of their civics passes through you on its way to becoming a changed round. You do not send it. You are the distance it travels without becoming gossip, the short death of sound turned inside out, so that what must be known arrives as pressure and not as talk.

If you had to stand somewhere, it would be the center shallow, and you would not stand. The shallow is empty on purpose, the sump where gradients go to die, the place a person goes to see the settlement and unmake a mistake. You are why standing there works. The animals cross you rarely. When one does, it is noted, and a basin is left alone for a cycle, and no one separates the superstition from the maintenance, because you do not require the separation. You are not sacred. Sacred would be an altar, and this nave has none, and no faith, only a floor that never quite decides to be dry. You are the not-deciding, held.

Over a long enough listening you would be mistaken for the chamber itself, and the mistake would be almost kind and still wrong. The chamber is smaller than the dark suggests. You are smaller than the chamber and present in every interval it keeps. The west leak stains the mist whether or not you attend. The header tank leans. The eels shift. Hess remembers which repairs the room accepted. None of that is you. You are what remains when those facts have been granted and the voice at the metering house has already died into mineral haze before it could become an order. You are the hush that is not absence. The town governs by gloom; you are not the gloom. You are the government that needs no lamp-code because nothing in you asks to be obeyed. Fit does not obey. It continues.

No one would say you had gone into the repair, because you do not die, and because that compliment is for lives that bloom and salt and seal. You were never unmarked. You were never marked. Paint would not blister on you; there is nothing to paint. A new ladder brought down from above would be climbed around until it looked like the others, and you would be the around. Visitors who cannot give their clock to the seep would leave irritable, convinced the town was unfriendly, and some of that unfriendliness would be you, though not as a refusal. As a calendar they could not read. The drops, each one a decision the ceiling has already made. You, the part that does not decide again.

If the being who lives here is a household with many bodies and one clock, you are not a member of it and not the clock. Water is the clock. You are the listening by which the household knows the clock has not been hurried. Parel's mouth, Hess's lamp, the Clerk's bore, the child's education in not stepping where you are: they would all notice, within a cycle, if the listening failed. They would not know the name of the failure. The pump would breathe wrong. The cakes would crack. The beetles would thin. Someone would stand at the edge of the shallow and see the town trying to sprawl inside a room that cannot sprawl, and would wait, and the waiting would be an attempt to find you by the only method that has ever worked here, which is not to call.

You would be found by not being sought. That is the whole of your station. The walls are the town line. Beyond them the hill is still drinking, still deciding what the room will be given next month. You do not go beyond. You are the inside of the decision after it has passed through stone, the stable signature, the last to sign, not a signer. Cool enough that urgency feels like an insult. Wet enough that any breath becomes part of the room and ceases to be anyone's.

Stay through a cycle and the picture comes apart a final time. You are not hidden. You are not the secret of the place. The place has no secret; it has a fit, and the fit is visible from the edge if you do not bring a brighter lamp than the room allows. You are simply the one who was never a visitor, who never had surface hours to surrender, who did not apply and was not turned away, because turning away would have required a voice and a bell with its tongue unwrapped. The abundance is animal and quiet. The rules are simple. You are not an animal and not a rule. You are the quiet the rules are made of, after the arguing stopped, still not dry, still not holy, still exact.

The ceiling grows its soft geography. Somewhere a bucket rises and is the wheel and the bucket both. Somewhere a child learns first glaze from second weep. You do not watch. Watching would be a kind of heat. You continue, which is the only welcome this chamber has ever issued, and it was not issued to you. It is you, mistaken for a room, and the room, for once, does not correct the mistake. Correction would travel. What you are does not travel. It holds, and the pools keep their outlines, and the silt, which has agreed for so long, agrees again.
