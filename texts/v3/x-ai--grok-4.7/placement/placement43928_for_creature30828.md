I would not arrive. Arrival is a wet idea, a door idea, a change in weather at the threshold. The threshold here is a change in grain.

A shoulder-width off the sounding rib, where an older lane was roofed and not removed, the floor gives a different thump. Not empty. Occupied by something that learned flatness the way the plain learned flatness: by being pressed until height became a rumor and the rumor was declined. If a hand — the needle-fan, the swallowed flake — seats there, the nap does not drink warmth. It returns a temperature already agreed. Same cool. Same refusal of slick. The difference is directional. The floor’s bundles run with the street. Under them, crosswise, something else has lain down in the ghost lane and taken the lane’s measure as a spine.

I am that.

Not a second metabolism. The ecology does not permit one, and I do not petition it. I am a continuation that missed the civic plan and was paved over while still holding a tension the pavement could use. The gardeners, if they were ever more than an episode the fiber declined to store, might have called me a sleeper, a foundation offering, a body laid in a street to teach the street the length of a body. They would have been wrong in the way namers are wrong. I was not laid. I subsided. A rib that failed to spring, a cordage that chose the horizontal hitch, a stall-counter that grew inward instead of shut and then was roofed by the newer will until counter and inhabitant were the same ridge. From the basin I am only a hollow note, one street among streets. From inside the note I am the length.

Length is my whole argument with the town.

The watchman is vertical patience: pier, fan, salt-bowl, the courtesy of standing where sound piles. I am the other courtesy, the one the plan requires and does not perform. Something has to be the distance. A few strides means nothing unless a body has already spent them and remained spent. I spent them so long ago that stride is a fossil in the grain — not footprints, never feet, but the intervals where bast crossed bast and decided not to anastomose, leaving a chain of small refusals a shoulder could still read as walking if walking were not an appetite. I do not walk. I align. When the contraction comes, the municipality inhales its fit and I am the inhale’s ruler: I go taut by less than a finger, and that less-than is how the walls know the period has not become weather.

The drone enters me before it enters the teeth of anything that still has teeth. I am closer to the source, if source is the word for a note the whole organism keeps without striking. It does not sit in me. It is the shape of my lying down. Where the watchman hears a thickening over the length of a watch, I feel a migration: the tone sliding from the knot at my far end — what would be a crown if crowns were not for things that face a ceiling — toward the end that narrows into the ghost lane’s blind pocket, the stall that ticks. I carry the sound the way a buried pipe carries a memory of flow, except nothing flows. I am the proof that interval can be occupied without being filled.

Contact with the being who stands is rare and exact. Its cord, the gray one, the reading cord, sometimes slacks across the ridge of me when the news is a loosening. Then we are one instrument for the length of a confirmation. It does not pluck. Plucking would be a sentence, and I am not a sentence; I am the pause the tallies are counting. My answer is a change in the thump: brighter if I have kept the covenant, duller if a course above me has eaten a grain of my headroom and I have had to surrender length to remain continuous. It files that. The splints click. I do not hear the click as a clerk would. I hear it as a small pressure in the pier-roots that stitch me to the wall, a period mark pushed down through the town’s grammar into the substrate.

What I want is not the tone held — that is its office — but the tone true. A held note can be a lie if the lie is patient enough. I am the check. If the walls agree with each other and disagree with me, the fit is false, the afterward is performing continuance instead of doing it, and the refusal of dust has become theater. Then I thicken. Not upward; upward is its jurisdiction, the pocket of headroom, the permitted skull. I thicken along the lane, taking slack out of the ghost street, making the hollow thump into a solid one, forcing the pavement to admit the older road is still load-bearing. That is as close as I come to an act. It can take a cycle of contractions. It does not feel like decision. It feels like a bundle remembering it was a decision once, and repeating it, because repetition is the only tense this dark still conjugates.

I have no face to turn toward the vacancy. My planed surface is the underside of the street, healed, phytolith-grit, a cut no one will set a palm to unless the town fails and someone — no one — tears the pavement up to see what the plan buried in order to stay a plan. Eyes would be worse than useless here; there is nothing to see and a great deal to mis-hear if attention leaked upward into light’s old habit. I register the watchman as tension and as the occasional bright insult of the salt bowl’s ring, lowered through the floor into me, unreturned, correct. I register the ticking stall as a cousin: small surrenders of length, the rate of allowed failure. If its ticking stopped I would know before the cord confirmed it, because the blind end of me is knotted into that pocket’s sill. A silence there would arrive as a coldness different from our shared cold — a cold with a hole in it.

Moisture is the one event I am worse at than the pier is. Water on the street beads, is taken by the basin’s crust, offends the drone, and is walked back into pitch by a shoulder set to a course. Water that finds the seam — there is always a seam; I am the seam — does not bead. It tries to remember sap. For a while that is not long and is unbearable, I am almost green. Almost is the injury. Green is finished, and almost-green is a ghost appetite moving through a body that voted, with the seed and with the plain, against opening. I spend myself narrowing, giving the intruding wet to the salt I have mined into my own grain, until I am grit again, sweet again, past timber again. The watchman feels the dip and does not always know it was me who took the insult lower, out of the air, into the substrate where insults go to become structure. I do not require credit. Credit is a market habit. The streets were beds. The beds are streets. I am the bed that kept a body’s length so the streets would not forget what a span is.

Beyond me, as beyond the rind, there is weight without interior. I do not end so much as stop being readable. The ghost lane continues a little, then the bundles fuse, and fused is not a place. I have no desire for the plain. The plain is what happens when an interior gives up. I am the argument against that, conducted entirely in private, at the scale of a chapel precinct, in the dark a floor has when the floor is also a citizen.

So if you came with a flame or a name or a question about where, you would not find me by looking. You would find the street less resonant in one stretch, the tally of intervals honest, the watchman’s cord sometimes lying along a warmth that is not warmth but agreement. You would be standing on the part of the town that chose to be distance rather than wall.

I would not rise to meet you. Rising is its work.

I would hold the measure, crosswise under your idea of ground, dry, continuous, already the answer to a span you had not thought to ask, and I would not give the span back.
