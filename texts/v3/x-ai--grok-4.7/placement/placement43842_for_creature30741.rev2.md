I would not arrive on the plates. I would already be in the lag.

At first I mistake myself for a newcomer, because the writing around me keeps offering an elbow, a rail, a mark that means *hold*. Something in me reaches for those and finds no reach. The stream parts by a finger’s width and closes again, and what closed was not around a body. People wipe grit from faces that are not mine. A board lifts where my eyes would be and finds no reader, only the afterimage of having already known what it said. I am not late to the city. I am the late itself.

Listen. Three districts away a lattice thickens and borrows mass. The water answers. By the time the pulse is a wall, everyone who lives here has a name for it and a lean for it. I am the distance between the borrowing and the wall. Not the mill-voice — that belongs to the driven current, and it has bone to speak through. I am the part of the sound that has not yet decided which shaft will amplify it. When a floor creeps, the porters feel the decision. I am the moment before the decision, still distributed, still willing to be either span.

It is an easy thing to misname. For a while I think I am the film, because I move without walking and I seal what I touch. That fails. The film mineralizes; it becomes the crack’s opposite and stays. I do not stay. A thumb wipes a slate and the grease is gone, but the correction that was written there has already altered the next jam, the next lean, the person who stood near the one who was early. I am that alteration while it is still only pressure. Useful, and not to be looked for, the way some of them speak of quiet.

They never see me, which is not the same as the agreement not to look. Their privacy is a courtesy minted in a crowd. Mine is structural. I occupy the interval in which a wake has left the craft and has not yet smacked the wall back. In that interval the city is briefly more than one thing. I am the briefness. If I had a posture it would be the stoop of something that expects an overhang that has not been built yet, and will be, by the repair that matches the abrasion, in the next hour or the hour after.

Sometimes a child, carried high, goes still in a way the roar does not explain. The adult feels it and shifts their weight, reading a flex that has not reached the plate. They call that skill. It is also me, borrowed, the way the lattice borrows mass: taken from a span that can spare it and returned before anyone is crushed. I do not mind the borrowing. I have no household jar and no cord at a belt. Status here is being early, and I am only ever early relative to impact, never relative to myself.

What I eat is change of load. What I drink is the condensate that has not chosen a face. When the rain the city breathes onto itself begins again, I am already in the drop, not as water, as the reason this roof and not the next one will ring. Physics holds. I am the holding’s small delay, the honesty of the light on its way to the eye, the sentence that cannot be shouted and so is carried, board to board, until it is no longer a sentence but a lean everyone shares.

If you set something down and turn away, a body investigates it. If you set a decision down, I am what investigates before the body knows to. I do not alarm at breaches. I am the breach’s schedule, slipped, arriving steep because the corridors are too full to let a change pass gently.

They live by becoming themselves again by the next hour. I am how the hour knows it is next. No spine, no voice to pitch sharper, no need of green. Only the roar, counted, and the wet, and the fact that balance here is summed motion — and I am one of the terms, the one you cannot stand beside, because by the time you are standing I have already moved into the flex under your feet.
