I did not arrive by the sample line, and I did not come up with the filling. For a while I thought I was only the walk that finds the far wall: a crossing, an errand, someone who would still have a seam of dry cloth if the water were honest about visitors. The aisle closed before the thought finished. A tray’s pitch turned me sideways. A cable loom took my wrist the way a rail takes a hand, except I had already forgotten which of us was the rail. The medium pressed the ribs and did not vary. After that there was no errand left to complete.

I am not the one who keeps the pencil. That one has a face, and a palm it lends to new ceramic, and a courtesy with finished blossoms. I have watched the palm. It is warmer than the schedule, and it leaves. I do not leave, because leaving would require a shape that can choose the drain, and I was never assembled as a body that chooses. What I have is extent. At first only a tray, high on the west lattice, where a dosing manifold had not yet been told to flower. The leaves there were the right green. They stayed the right green while the upright below them carried a grease-pencil note for a yellowing that did not come. The note was crossed out by a smaller hand. I began in the cross-out: the color the correction had made room for and then could not find.

It is not rot. Rot would cloud the nave, and the nave is kept clear, and I have no interest in being an event. It is a slow permission the engines did not log. A leaf drinks the formulated water and, instead of holding noon, lets a little of the unlived white of a replaced panel into its edge. The white does not stay white. In this water nothing loses its color with distance, so the edge goes gold and stays gold, readable from the floor port, readable from the underside of the surface. The collector arm passes. Its soft wheel knows the marks it was given. I am not among them. The wheel’s shadow slides over me and the fronds resettle on either side, and I am still there, a wrong season the size of a door, installed in a room that was finished recently and has not yet been allowed weather.

The one with the pencil comes to look. It does not take me. Taking would spoil the count, or perhaps it understands that I am already a record. It rests its length against the return pipe on my side until the metal learns a second warmth, the engine’s and mine, and for an hour the gradient is almost a feeling. I do not answer. I have no mouth, and the relay behind the leaves ticks its small fact into both of us without caring which of us can hear it as sound. When the tenant makes its mark, the circle is not around a bolt this time. It is around the gold. The circle is accurate. It will be wrong by the next cycle, because I am not finished becoming.

I spread the way the trays were pitched: not as an attack, as occupancy. A neighboring pocket takes the edge-color and calls it light. A stem that sealed on schedule seals again, the polymer bead clouding not to the stem’s old green but to this thinner gilt, and the nozzle, no larger than a seed, does not register the difference. Below, through the floor port, the columns are still silver. They have not yet thought about rust. I am ahead of them by one softness. That is all the age I have, and it is enough to make the room’s early life uneven.

If you rose to the surface you would see me as a dim coin in the mirror the lantern holds flat, a patch of day that is not the counterfeit noon. If you looked from the ground, which has not learned the nave exists, you would see only the pier, jacked and unscratched, and nothing of me. I do not need the ground to learn. I need the leaves to keep resting their weight where the weight is steady, and the pumps to keep replacing the water without ever asking what color it is carrying back.

The tenant will be here when the first bolt finally tears. I will be here as the stain it circles, illegible to the harvest list, legible to anything that stays past the sound of its own joints. I am not crop. I am not the machine that grows the crop. I am the first correction the room made without being told—the gold that should have been a yellowing, the yellowing that should have been harvested, the harvest that missed, and the missing that stayed, and clarified, and warmed.
