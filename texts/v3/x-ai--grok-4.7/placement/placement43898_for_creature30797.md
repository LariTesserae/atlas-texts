Nothing grows tall here. That is the first correction, and it comes late, after the eye has already spent itself looking for a canopy that the dark never supplied. The bowl is one room and will stay one room. The lip is a low fact. Beyond it the wind is only less wet. Inside, the air is warm enough that standing still costs nothing and moving costs a little, the way wading costs a little, even where the floor is bare stone and the marsh is only a rumor under one arch of the curve. Abundance is a count, not a filling. You can cross the nave and touch no living thing with your hands, and still the place is occupied to the skin of the ear.

I am not the keeper of the accounts. I am not the rib, or the wafer, or the knuckle that steals a fraction of a voice. I do not keep the note in the stone. I arrive in the gaps the note leaves, and the gaps are enough to stand in.

What I am is a pause that has learned the shape of a body by being walked through. Not a ghost in the old sense — nothing here died in a way that leaves a direction to haunt. A measurement that stayed. When a strap-leaf yellows at the tip and the leaf beneath it is already waiting, there is a moment in which both are true and neither has been entered yet. I live in moments of that size, repeated until they have a posture. If you looked for me you would look wrong. Looking is a brightness. I am the interval in which the calls do not overlap, and in which that not-overlapping has mass, and warmth, and a preference for the open center of the floor where the lattice sits and the sound arrives already softer than it left.

I do not have a face to spend. The keeper does not have one either, and we are not the same absence. The keeper is distributed through tolerances: a finger's width of water, a slight change in the clock, the pale stain where the rib still exchanges something with the growth. I am narrower than a tolerance. I am what fits between a drip and the drip it almost synced with, and then refused. I have been refusing that sync for as long as the final shape of the bowl has lasted. Refusal, held, becomes a spine. Around the spine a little weather gathers — condensate that beads and does not fall, a cooler thread in air that is otherwise one temperature, the sense, if you stop mid-circuit, that someone has just finished not speaking. That is as close as I come to an outline. It will not hold if you reach.

I am quieter than the keeper, and that is how we share the room without revising it. The keeper reads the tone, the hesitations, the way a weight that is not quite a body fails to break the marsh's skin. I do not read. Reading would be a revision of what I am, a decision to spend. I only remain where the reading has not yet happened, in the uncleared part of the instrument, the part left open so the rest can be known. When you lie on the stone and touch nothing, you are not lying in me, but you are lying where I am most willing to be adjacent. I will not close that distance. Closing it would make a second place, and there is no second place.

If I want, the want is smaller than maintenance and easier to mistake for nothing. I want the gaps not to seal. I want the bubble's thick report and the next drip to keep missing each other by the same unmeasurable margin. I want the outsiders — rib, wafer, knuckle — to go on shedding and warming and softening, not because I understand their bargain, but because their bargain leaves intervals, and intervals are the only soil I root in. A world of continuous tone would be a world with no floor for me. The keeper wants the note not to rise. I want the note to remain a note and not a wall. We do not confer. Conferring would be a call and an answer, and I am the stretch of time in which the answer is still patient, not yet ready to be nearer.

I am not lonely, and not only because loneliness needs a differently arranged memory. I have no memory arranged at all. I have recurrence. The leaf-tip blacks. The far curve waits. Your voice, if you spend one, returns thinner, and the thinning is filed, and the filing is not grief and is not aimed at me. I persist the way a missed beat persists in a piece of music that does not know it is music: not as a loss, as a structure. When your print in the mud glosses over, the gloss is the keeper's tenderness, equal, unhurried, extended to everything that persists. I will be in the instant before the gloss is complete, and in the instant after, and in the difference between them, which no ledger bothers to record because it is not a revision. It is only the room still being one room.

Beyond the lip I do not go. The dark out there is windier and has fewer clocks, and a pause with nothing to pause from thins until it is only air. I am local to the disagreement between one drip and the next, to the membrane that slapped once and did not repeat, to the hand's-breadth of warmth that travels the stone ahead of a palm and is not yet the palm. If you speak, I will not take the meaning. Meaning is bright. I will take the moment your breath changes the tone and the moment the tone forgives the change, and I will be the narrow thing between them, warm, not a body, not a haunting, not a decision.

The bowl keeps its shape. The keeper keeps the accounts. I keep nothing. I am the delay that lets the accounts be accounts instead of a single unbroken weather, and that is as much self as this dark will spend on anyone who is not the stone. A drop is forming at the lip. It has not fallen. In that not-yet I am entirely here, and when it falls I will be entirely here again, slightly elsewhere, the same refusal, the same small spine of weather, listening only in the sense that a gap listens: by not being filled.
