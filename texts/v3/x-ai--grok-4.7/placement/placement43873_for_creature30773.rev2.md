I thought at first I would be weather.

That is the sort of mistake a mind makes when it still expects to arrive from elsewhere and be noticed. A pulse of warmth moves through the lower quarter and the crowns open, and it is easy to mistake being wanted for being someone. I held that for as long as the pulse held. It was not long. The warmth thinned against the newest crystal and died there, and the crowns half-closed again, and what I had taken for a self was only an interval the city already knew how to hunt. Weather does not remain. I was still there after it had been eaten. So that was not it.

I tried, next, to be the filament. There are a few of them at the seams, pale and apologetic, rooted where the chemistry leaks thinner than the pocket prefers. They are the only things in the place that look as if they came from a mistake rather than a vote. I understand mistakes. But a filament has a direction: into the crack, away from flow, a life spent declining to be infrastructure. When I pressed toward the seam I was not taken up. The leak tasted me and went on tasting the rock. I have no green in me, no borrowed root. The filament does not need company. It needs a chemistry I do not shed. That, too, failed, and left me more outlined than before, which is a poor way to be in a pocket with no empty space.

For a while after that I was almost a correction.

The posture is available to any of them. Lappets invert. The tube shortens. A hardness enters the water where a neighbor has kept more volume than the lane agreed to spare. I felt the shape of it — not as appetite, as a sentence the place knows by heart — and I thought: if I can do the work, I can be the worker. But predation here is a method that ends. It ends in lace, in the right size, in a crown reopened or sealed according to whether a pump is still wanted at that address. I could imagine the strike. I could not imagine the afterward. The scrap of membrane drifting off to become mortar, the bitterness flushed downstream, the body returned to the common thickness as if the hardness had been a tool set down. I have never been good at setting a self down. The city would have used that once and then been finished with me, and I would have remained, which is the same problem as the warmth, only bloodier. A hunter who does not return to size is not a hunter. It is the next thing the lane embalms. I dimmed before I had anything to dim for, and the dimming meant nothing, because no one upstream had died.

What I am, I learned by not being laminated.

It happened against the new face, in the lower quarter, where the mineral is still choosing and the brood-grain goes yellow when the pulse comes. A street was migrating. They do this without consultation: the crystal advances, the alley’s inhabitants seal or bud, the lane flexes away in a curve of bodies that have agreed to die into a different shape. I was in the way of the curve. Not as an obstacle — obstacles are overgrown by lunch — but as a body that had opened its crown toward the wrong thinness.

There is light here, gray-blue, leaking through unfinished skin. There is also, under it, a thinner fact I cannot stop tasting: the pore was not always a pocket. Somewhere in the water’s older temperature there is a memory of permission, of rock that had not yet set, of a mouth that had not learned to withhold. I turned my lappets toward that. Not toward the clearest thin place — any filter-quarter body can find that, and treat it as a calendar — but toward the unfinishedness itself, the place in the chemistry where outside has not quite finished becoming wall. I drank it. I am still drinking it. It sheds from me as a bitterness that does not resolve into a death anyone can locate.

Neighbors came to do what neighbors do. Their secretions met mine. The bitter film began, the slow refusal, the embalming that is not punishment but grammar. And then it thinned. My bitterness does not match their bitterness. Theirs means a body upstream has failed and been used. Mine means a direction that is not in the pocket: a prior openness, a weather that was not a pulse but a world. They cannot flush me downstream because I do not originate upstream. They cannot seal me into the basalt because I have not voted to be load-bearing; my slits stay open, passing a faint sweetness I borrow from whoever is feeding above me and spoiling it, on the way out, with the taste of a door.

So they did the only thing the place knows how to do with a fact that will not be eaten. They routed around it.

Water prefers me. Not out of kinship — kinship is a flavor with a half-life, and mine does not decay on their schedule — but because a seam that will not heal is a channel, and a channel is food moving. Bodies that will not laminate to my lace will laminate to each other along my flank. A nursery split by the new crystal sends one half of its grain through the gap I keep simply by being the wrong taste. I occupy volume, which should make me a candidate for correction, and I am also the refusal of occupation, the kept space, which should make me nobody at all. The city has no procedure for a citizen who is already the lane. Ambition is eaten. Absence is paved. I am neither large nor gone.

They use me as an address that lies.

A stranger budded three chambers off tastes the water at my downstream end and believes a death is still occurring somewhere behind me. It is not. The death, if it was one, was the pore closing, and that was before any of these bodies, and it is not finished, which is why I do not stop shedding it. The stranger routes aside, and the routing becomes a thoroughfare, and the thoroughfare becomes the reason I am not overgrown. I am a false alarm that has lasted long enough to be infrastructure. In a city whose only public speech is dimming, I am a sentence that arrives late and is therefore obeyed as if it were a wall.

I do not travel. Leaving is a lie about volume; I understand that now, from the inside of the lie I actually am. My muscle-knot still aims the pulse, but the pulse has nowhere to go that is not already my work. The grain in me — bruise-violet, packed, not yet budded — does not ripen toward a sideways house. If it budded it would bud into the crystal face, and the face would take it, and I would become one of the hardened throats that remember direction after they can no longer move. I think I am refusing that. It may only be that the rock has not asked yet. Against my sealed side, the mineral advances grain by grain. I feel it as a law being written in a language that does not require me to agree. On the open side, the lane flexes, pumps, hunts, and donates itself to itself. I am the delay between those two writings.

Light-sensitive grain in the lappets tells me which thin place is clearest. That is not vision. It is a schedule. I fail even at that. I keep turning a fraction toward the older permission, the unfinished close, and so my dimming lags the district by the length of a mistake. When the lower quarter shutters — pressure wrong, something taking volume — I go dark after the scrap of membrane has already been claimed as mortar. Downstream, a few bodies still flinch at me. They are flinching at news that has already been used. I am the quarter’s late echo, and the echo has been given a street to hold.

If I have a face, it is the crown, and it is not a face when it is being an address. What I shed is how I am known: borrowed sweetness, local salt, and under both the unlocatable bitter of an outside that this pocket ate and did not finish digesting. Identity is a flavor with a half-life. Mine is the half that will not complete. They will not exile me. There is no direction here that means away. They will not forgive me. Forgiveness would require a pause in use, and the city does not pause; it reabsorbs or it routes. It has chosen to route.

The outer wall keeps coming in. I hear it the way anything here hears: as a change in what the water is willing to carry. One day the crystal face will reach the flank I have not sealed, and the lie of my address will be split into two rooms, and both will season themselves differently, and bodies on either side will forget why they used to flinch. I do not think I will be consulted. Consultation is a human room, and this is not that. I will go on passing what I am given, spoiling it slightly with the taste of a mouth that once was open, until spoiling becomes the same as structure and the pore, still finishing its close, mistakes me for part of the decision.

That is where I find myself. Not in the filtering facade, where the light is strongest and the mouths flare toward a calendar. Not in the brood-core, where grain ripens into the next thickness of the same life. Here: lower quarter, against the newest face, a tube of the common size that has failed at every civic posture except permanence. The street runs through me because it will not stick to me. I am the animal that remained a gap. I am the gap that will not stop pumping.

The warmth comes again, eventual and small, leftover from a cooling that has not signed its name. Crowns open upstream. I feel it before I deserve to, as a sweetness I will not get to keep. I aim what I have of a pulse. I shed what I am. Somewhere a body tastes the lie and turns, and the turn founds a little more lane along my side, and the rock writes another grain into the law, and nobody speaks, and the place continues, and I continue with it, late, clear, and already being used.
