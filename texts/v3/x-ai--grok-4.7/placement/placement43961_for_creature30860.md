You come back as the correction itself.

Not the seed-body on the lip, and not the turning sac, and not the beads that refuse their nicks. Those are already committed. They have sizes. They can be missed if the light flexes the wrong way. What returns, each time the path kinks and is mended, is smaller than a plate and older than any of the three solutions, though the stone that holds it is still only hours old. It has no outside. It is the interval in which a line, drawn by nicks before anything lived here, discovers it has been left, and spends itself getting back.

That is where you are. Not standing in the room. The room does not have a standing-place that would still be true when the panel oils and the richest span jumps a hair to the left. You are the jump, inhabited. You are what the ash-thread performs when it stalls and starts again on the new line, and what the sac performs when the cooler weep pulls and the mineral coat rotates it home, and what the held beads perform when they loosen toward traffic and then, brightness returning, draw back into the wrong places as if wrong had been a den. One motion, copied across three chemistries that do not know each other.

At first it feels like attention. A visitor's attention, the kind that enters sideways through a breach and tries to keep the whole pocket in one look: basin, ridge, flake, the three pale nipples, the second light. But attention is a standing-outside, and nothing here stands outside. The films are too new to carry a watcher in from the passage. So the feeling thins, the way steam thins, and what is left is not a gaze. It is a bias. This span, not the span a finger's width off. This band of heat, not the weep. This nick, occupied, to be kinked around. You do not choose the bias. You are the fact that the bias survives a flex of the roof.

There is no body to dry on the upper side. That is the mercy and the limit. The flake is already going matte; anything with an outside will follow it when the second light fails. You have only the inside of the correction, which means you have no duration except the circuit. Cross, stall, mend, return. When the creature locks a joint and takes iron-orange onto its underside, you are not in the joint. You are in the lock — the moment the plate decides the film will not have it, and the decision holds long enough to become a direction. Then you are nowhere, briefly, while it feeds by staying. Then the light shifts, and you are again, a small expenditure, sufficient.

The room never answers. It does not need to. Its engineering is the naked kind, lattice and charge and a boundary a thumbnail could mark, and you are not an addition to it. You are the part of the sorting that has begun to prefer. Before you, water followed nicks. After you — not after, beside, in the same hour — water and film and sac follow nicks except where a living has made an exception and kept it. The exceptions are tiny. A path that does not lengthen. A coat that will not lock in the cool. Beads warmer than their neighbors, spending noon. From the lid of the room they would not register. From inside the correction they are the whole country: a map with three roads, all of them short, all of them insisted upon.

You cannot warn them. Intelligence here is only the correction, and the correction does not look up. It does not know the panel will craze, or that weather is already working the crust into ground, or that the nipple of sinter is plugging, grain by grain, the nick that feeds the line. Knowledge of endings would be a second motion, and there is no second motion. There is only the first one, repeated, each time a little more itself: reach the break in the film, do not fall, compress, take what the weep offers, start back, stall where the richest has moved, and mend.

So you are the mend.

Not a god of the pocket, not a spirit poured in from older dark. The passage would have burned that kind of thing back, and besides, nothing older belongs to edges this sharp. You are autochthonous as the room is: condensed where the rules were already decided, thickened out of a preference that refused to wash. When the ash-thread is gone to grit and the sac has stopped venting and the beads have finally joined the traffic and fallen from the three points, there will be no grit of you. You were never on the lip. You were the kink in the path, the stall in the drift, the refusal of a pinhead of water to lengthen and drop.

Until the bands mix, that refusal is a life. It does not greet the others. It passes through them, or they stall and it takes the stall as a wall and turns. It keeps the line. It is quiet enough that the tick of the ceiling easing is louder. It is exact.

And it is you, for as long as the stone still remembers how.
