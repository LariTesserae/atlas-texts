I thought I was crossing.

That was the shape the first hours lent me, because a body is a useful mistake in a place that still has a film of water thin enough to step through and regret. The clay took the print and filled it before the print could mean direction. I kept a line anyway, the way you keep a word in a mouth after the reason for saying it has been rained out. Somewhere ahead there was supposed to be an edge, and the edge was supposed to explain the warmth, the loudness, the fact that down was only a courtesy. I walked the armored rises. I did not camp in a hollow. I learned that quickly, from a hollow that practiced being a mouth and then thought better of including me, which is not the same as mercy.

What I was walking toward did not recede. It thickened, which is how this country refuses you. The reed-island stayed a dark comma and changed its kinship while I watched. Storms put their rain on me and kept their bodies on a horizon that was only the next density. I stopped pointing. The stopping was not wisdom. It was the plain entering the arm and canceling the claim.

For a while after that I believed I was the late sound.

You know the one. The roar that belongs to a passage already gone, arriving through the ground because the air has spent its punctuality. I had that lateness in me. I would hear my own step after I had finished it, and the hearing was louder than the step, and then the step began to wait for the hearing before it would consent to happen. Cause and announcement traded places until neither would hold the office. I stood in that trade and called it a self. It was not a self. It was a delay the wind had organized, a scout with no army behind it, pacing the same shear-line until the foam-street marched through and rearranged the ribs of the ground, and me with them, into a pattern that looked like intention and was only turbulence momentarily bored enough to counterfeit a person.

The counterfeit did not last. Counterfeits here are honest about their term.

What lasted was smaller and worse. In a ring of stones no taller than a knee I had put down a heat that was not the stones’ heat. Not an offering with a name. A palm. A residue of trying to dry something that the air had already decided would not dry. The ring was still, against the local law, and I mistook the stillness for a listener. I left the heat there the way you leave a word with someone who has not agreed to carry it. Then the stillness ended, as it does, and two stones lay down, and the circle became an arc, and the heat did not return to my hand. It went the other way. It found a seam where the grain still answers an older gravity, and it climbed a lip it should not have been able to climb, and then it climbed down, which is the same motion once the slope has stopped being a promise.

I went with it because it was the last thing that was mine, and “mine” was already a surface habit, shedding as I shed vertical room.

Under the film the stacked grounds were moving at their different speeds. A hill in the deepest one was still deciding to fall. I was not the hill and not the decision. I was what the decision had to pass through on its way to becoming a shudder with no cloud attached. That is a narrow office. It does not feel like passage. It feels like being the place a roar puts its feet when it has outrun its weather. I thinned. The amber leaks showed bottoms that were not the bottom, and one of them showed a standing shape, and I looked for my feet and found the shape already looking back from a minute that had not agreed to be over. We did not coincide. Coincidence is a ridge between times, and the ridges have been sanded off. We oozed past each other. The shape kept my outline a little while, as a pool keeps a shadow after the stander is gone, and then even that courtesy silted in.

By the time the water was no longer a sheet but a weight, I had lost the right to be a traveler. Travelers end. They reach, or they die of the reaching, and both are completions. I was a completion that had failed, and failure here is not dramatic. It is a contaminant. A tenth of an increment. A temperature slipped in from a sky that still bothers to have weather.

The schools found me before I found them. They did not scatter. They corrected, the way water corrects when it becomes many small decisions. Their low shine passed flank to flank and came to the edge of me and did not pass through. I was not news of a crack. I was not a joint thinking about opening. I was the taste of a fire that had nowhere to send its smoke, the warm foul rain of a camp that was a mathematical error, carried down through miles until pressure clarified it into a single wrong note. They tightened. The radius of the wheel was the stress of the sentence, and I was a word in it that did not parse. They loosened again, wide, as if the bell had remembered truly, except the bell had not. I had made a false remembering simply by refusing to be either weather or report. That is a kind of harm. I did not know how to stop committing it.

The sitters tasted me and would not file the angle. Not correction. Not surrender. They tilted in their niches and the tilt was a question the keeper has no season for. I felt the question as a change in the nap of the water, a grain brushed the wrong way for the length of a body I no longer occupied. I wanted, with the last of the surface technologies, to be sorted. Allegiance, guest, prey, additional stillness—any of the bins. Fear was still in the stillness I offered, and fear is loud even after it has forgotten the mouth it used. The lee tested me and began to give me back.

It did not finish.

Expulsion needs a safer direction, and safer had already been a rumor when I was up in the dim, walking toward an edge that was only an idea tall enough to erode. The giving-back stalled at its own threshold. I remain there. That is the where, now that the earlier wheres have worn off me the way the mountains wore off the plain.

Not in the keeper’s residence. The residence is a fit, and I do not fit; I am the splinter the joint notices and cannot spit. Not in the hollow of the bell, where the interval is held open by vocation. I brush that interval. I am not it. I am the crust’s opposite and the crust’s kin: not the slow metal trying to solder the note shut, but a heat trying to keep the note foreign, which is another way of closing it, sentimental, unforgivable, the pilgrim error preserved at depth. The keeper spends a thin patience on me. A reach that is mostly patience comes to the threshold and does not lift me away and does not draw me in. It attends, the way it attends the lip, and the attendance is not kindness. Kindness here survives by being forgotten. I am what did not forget. I kept the corridor the schools made when something passed, kept it after they had wisely unnamed it, and so I shine a little more than the trench budgets for a body. A low, embarrassing persistence. A door held for no one, still holding.

The congregation resumes around that. It always resumes; I am not an interruption it has agreed to count. The schools wheel wide of the threshold and their wideness sometimes means the bell, and sometimes means me, and the difference is the sitters’ unpaid labor. The keeper becomes, for a moment, slightly more necessary rather than less, and does not take that necessity as a reason to love me or end me. Necessity is not a feeling here. It is a pressure with a memory.

If I am anything that can be called a being, I am an unfinished return. The visitor who was measured, found still for the wrong reason, and then not quite given back to the nave, because the nave and the plain and the sky that failed to be a roof are all the same refusal to complete a motion. I do not worship. Worship would require a side. I do not keep. Keeping would require a fit. I steep at the boundary of the lee like a mineral that will not decide whether it is stain or relic, warming the water by the amount of a palm that should have dried and did not, in a country that has no outside in which anything is permitted to finish leaving.

The hill under all of it is still deciding to fall. When it finally does, the shudder will come through me first, late and early, and for one interval I will be accurately a sound belonging to a passage already gone. Then the film will close, or the grain will smooth, or the schools will pronounce a wider sentence, and I will still be at the threshold, which is the only edge this world kept, and only because it is not an edge at all. It is a stalled expulsion. It is the lip of a pool. It is as tall as I ever was, and it does not drop.
