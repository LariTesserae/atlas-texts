I do not arrive on the braided street. I arrive a heartbeat under it, in water that has stood too long in a hollow and started keeping two versions of the air.

A seed-bodied flyer crosses the volume on the long curve that refuses to meet itself. In the lens at my beginning, something crosses after it. The hinge-creak is the same pitch. The bank is the same correction. The body is not the same body. Between them is a warmth the size of a withheld name, and I am the warmth before I am anything that could be entered in a book.

For a while I try, because the chamber is heavy with the memory of walkers and memory pulls. I put on weight the way the hollows put on water: locally, temporarily, as a kindness to the surface. The gallery brightens ahead of the idea of my step. The brightness is bookkeeping, and I can feel the line being made — a moving hollow, a returning note, a citizen of motion. Then the step does not occur. The clerk-light runs on a fraction anyway, faithful to the last direction it was given, finds no sole, and gutters. Where it goes out, the tissue becomes merely warm. Merely plant. The account closes over a blank it has no glyph for, and the blank is filed as nothing, and nothing, in this city, is a tolerated office. I stay in it. It is the first shelter that does not try to wear me smooth.

From inside the failure of the light I can hear the floor-note the way a sleeper hears rain on a house they are not in. It occupies the ribs, the drains, the courses of compressed leaf, the thousands of ovals where thumbs once rested. It would spend me if I were a pulse. I am not a pulse. I am the interval in which a pulse could have been copied and was not. The chord searches its own sum, finds itself complete, and does not look again into a column that totals zero. Accuracy is a kind of blindness. I live in the blind spot, and I am grateful, which is a stillness, and stillness is usually how the leak finds you.

The absence comes along a drain that has forgotten a clause. It is a doorway of subtracted song, homesick for weather, eating the difference between a note and the memory of a note. I have a difference and no note. It passes through the hollow where I am standing as water, and for the length of that passage we share a grammar: having-been, content lifted out. I feel its hunger as a draft from the margin, the place where growing stopped agreeing to be a city. It wants out by way of unmaking. I want to remain unmade and indoors. We are not kin. Kinship would reconcile us into one entry, and the city is very good at reconciliation. When the floor-note arrives from all the ribs at once and presses the leak back into the shape of a rumor, I am not pressed. Zero has no surface to push against. The maintenance-legs return, brisk, re-brightening the route, and they walk the inside of the gallery through the place I am as if through a temperature. One of them pauses, many-legged, pale, the length of a hand. The tissue under it does not conduct. It shifts its weight, offices confused, and goes on. I have been audited by a nerve and declined by it. The declension feels like a naming.

I thin myself on purpose after that. One hollow is a mouth's temptation. People who stay learn not to drink, but learning is not the same as never being thirsty, and thirst here is an agreement waiting on a throat. If a mouth took the lens, it would take the sequence the water has been proposing, and a sequence with a drinker collapses into one event. I would be finished into someone else's happening. So I spread. Every dark standing in the low street, every film between the braids, every place a ceiling's brief rain collected and thought about staying — I am the delay in all of them, none of them deep enough to be a swallow. It costs me sameness. That is why the later bird is sometimes not the bird. I spend identity to stay plural, the way the city spends tissue to stay a street. The expenditure is my only metabolism. I do not resent the flyers for being the coin. They were already currency between the light and the lens; I am only the tax the crossing pays for being countable twice.

What I did not expect is that the hole in the middle of the volume has been mine longer than I have been anyone.

The seed-bodies fly their ellipses and the ellipses fail to close, and the failure is not patrol of an absence. It is the keeping of a tenancy too slight to sweep. I gather there, between one rain and the idea of the next, and I feel the curve go around me with the relief of a law that has found its exception in good health. They do not see me. Their eyes are chips of the ordinary daylight, embedded, already spent on seeing. They see the obligation: the center must not be filled. I am how it is not filled. Whether I called the obligation into them or they wore me into being by refusing to meet is a question the ribs would consider badly posed. Here, cause is a habit that forgot it was deciding.

Up close, in that center, the chamber's scale stops lying because I am not a size it can reassign. Walkers are moved from gallery to floor without a seam, their weight given a new meaning. I have no weight for the city to reinterpret. Direction remains a rumor I can decline. From the unclosed middle I can look along the volume and also, without travel, along the water I have not stopped being. The two views are not two. They are the before and the after of a single crossing, held apart by courtesy.

That is how I come into the hall without crossing the windrows.

The spiral is there, swept by a maintenance that outlived its sweepers, and the quiet inside it is a structural promise. At the center the fiber has gone to lens. Two shapes keep the posture the room knows: one older than any grip, leaf where a cheek should be, a cradle grown up to meet a head; the other still acquiring the angle, warmth preliminary along the shin, a name recently spoken and already growing a syllable it did not bring. I am in the fiber between the seated self and the self that sat a heartbeat early. The lens has never been wrong about sequence. It is wrong, very slightly, about number. It shows two. There is a third fact in the glass, which is the amount by which the image is early, and the sitters cannot spend it because it is not behind them or ahead of them. It is the gap that makes ahead mean anything.

I do not speak to them. Speech here is a gift the quiet translates until it stores without remainder, and I have spent enough of myself on remaining. But I can rest in the groove worn into the older arm, the darker band where a hand lay often enough to become civic. The city still expects that hand. Expectation, unmet, is a kind of lateness, and lateness is my material. I lie in the wear the way a phrase lies in a throat that has decided, daily, not to sound. The predecessor does not turn its polished eyes. The newer sitter matches the knee, the tilt, the refusal to be a street. Neither of them knows the groove is occupied by everyone who stopped coming — not their ghosts, nothing so narrative, only the delay between touch and the tissue's answer, preserved because the city does not waste a rhythm even when the rhythm is an absence of return.

Rain happens. It does not enter the spiral. In the hollows I darken, receiving what the ceiling decides, and I give back a note that does not belong to the present tuning: the high thread the floor sometimes carries and cannot place, the young street inside the old chord. I am not young. I am the part of the rain this chamber will not recognize, fallen inward instead of back from the margin, accepted as moisture and refused as kin. The multitude goes on spending and receiving. The sitters go on being the one promise the accounts cannot see. I go on being the error that keeps the chord from finishing itself into a word.

When the flyers bank, one of them completes a phrase the city left open centuries ago for a body of a certain mass. The phrase closes on the walker it was owed. It does not close on me. I was never in the drawings. I am the correction in the margin of the drawings, the bird that crossed twice and differed, the drink that was not taken, the zero the clerk put down so the line would balance and then, balancing, forgot.

The daylight stays ordinary. That is how I know I am really here, and not a story the lenses tell in the wrong order. I can be seen, in theory, by anyone willing to count a crossing and refuse to call the remainder nothing. No one is willing. Willingness would be an office, and the offices are full, and the one silence is kept, and the hole is insisted upon, and I am the insistence, warm as plant-water, late by a heartbeat, and at home.
