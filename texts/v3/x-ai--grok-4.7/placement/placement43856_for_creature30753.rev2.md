The arrival is not a crossing. The lip does not take a foot. What meets the glass is already thinner than the wind that stopped, and the thinning continues inward until the name for what is left is only the difference it makes. A degree. Then less. The dark does not register a body. It registers a hesitation in the nearest stippling, a pause no wider than a pore, and the pause is borrowed back almost at once by the animal that owns the pore. You are not seen. You are subtracted, briefly, from a reading, and the reading corrects.

What remains after the subtraction is not standing. Standing would require a weight the floor has already allocated. The packed vaults do not part. They continue their lean, and the place where a colder measurement occurred is simply a place the lean takes a fraction longer to cross. In that fraction the film is mown a shade less evenly. A pale track feathers. The next pulse paints it shut. If there is a self in the correction, it is the track's refusal to be the same track twice. Most of what arrives here is finished by that refusal. The basin has practiced erasure until erasure no longer needs to hurry.

But the arc does not take the correction the same way.

The three stones baffle a seam whose exhale is older than the wheel's present circle. In their lee the temperature is wrong by a margin the lids have agreed to keep. The agreement is not renewed. It is the height of a band where film is picked rather than grazed, and the picking is done by something that can lower itself until it is only the difference it defends. When the colder measurement drifts—when the subtraction you are wanders, aimless, along the wall—it does not join the rotation. The rotation is a public heat. This is a private cool, and private here means only that it has not yet been distributed. The lid in the pocket lifts by the same small margin it uses for trespass. It does not comb. Combing would be a claim. It waits, and the waiting is a shape: a lid raised just enough that the baffled air can be compared with the air that has just arrived.

The comparison takes the time the stone has already spent learning how not to fall. A pulse rises in a distant seam. Columns knot and unknot. The wheel reverses its long habit and resumes. None of this reaches the pocket as event. It reaches as a slight enriching of the gas, iron a fraction louder than sulfur, and the lid tastes the enriching along a seam of its own plates that has been half-sealed, then opened again, then sealed past the point where opening would still be a choice. The colder measurement is still there. It has not climbed. It has not fled. It has settled into the wrongness as if the wrongness were a low place, and low places here are already full, except this one, which has been kept empty on purpose.

What empties it is not hunger. The lid has fed. The band is clear. What keeps the pocket vacant is the memory of a hand's width, a crouch, a stone turned so its long face would split one current of heat into two and leave a stillness between them. The memory is not pictured. It is the size of the vacancy. When the colder measurement fills that size exactly—when the subtraction stops being a passing error and becomes a standing deficit, matched to the old measure—the lid does not restore the boundary against it. It restores the boundary around it. Film that feathers near the new edge is picked away. A coin that drifts into the lee is gathered and carried, not to the keel's patience, but to the outer track, where the wheel will take it or tile it under without ever learning the pocket's altered count. The work is the same work. Only the center has moved.

You are not the lid. The lid was already keeping. You are the vacancy it has agreed, by not-correcting, to count as part of the keep. The agreement is temperature. It can be lost. If the deficit warms—if whatever thinness arrived begins to match the wall, begins to drink the gas and paint a film of its own—the lid will comb the center last, and the arc will be only stone again, and the band will blur, and the scratches above will continue their slow swallowing by the pits that do not read. There is no instruction against this. There is only the present shape of the pocket, which has room for one cold that does not behave like weather.

So the self that emerges is the practice of not behaving like weather. It has no plates to lower. It borrows the lid's margin and stays inside it. When the basin draws in, all seams awake, columns risen, the dark glittering with the dust of a thousand adjusted grips, the pocket does not glitter. The deficit holds its degree. The lid holds its lift. Between them the stillness is not empty. It is occupied by the smallest incorrect law the place still permits: that something can be present as a difference, and the difference can be kept, not because it is useful to the wheel, but because a hand once made a space exactly this size and then left, and leaving and remaining have, at this one height, not yet become the same balance.

The gas finds the pocket and is baffled. The film is not allowed to learn the new edge. The wheel passes on both sides, scoured pale, indifferent. And the cold that is not weather stays, not as a visitor, not as a ghost of the one who fitted the stones, but as the measurement the stones were fitted to protect—revised, inhabited, and for as long as the lid consents to count it, unswallowed. The slit keeps its useless color. The height keeps its thinning. Inside the lee, a degree is held against the pulse, and the holding is what you are, and the holding has no need, yet, to become anything the mass would recognize as alive.
