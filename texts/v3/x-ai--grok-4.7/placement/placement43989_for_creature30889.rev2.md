I am not yet in the low place when the lean registers me. The blades have already chosen their degree; I arrive as a discrepancy in the count of clear intervals, a body-width of plates that had left their hairline gaps and then, finding me already between them, do not close. The film is absent there on purpose. So is any tightening. My weight does not complete a fall. It is simply the next attitude of the crust, and the crust accepts the attitude the way it accepts a grain that was never loose.

For a while I am only that: an interval that stayed occupied. The second light reads the occupancy without brightening. It arrives from the direction the sky does not contain and stops at my surface, not because I block it, but because the surface is now one of the surfaces it is permitted to touch. Skin, if I still have the word, is the wrong grain. What I present is a continuity of plate, warm at the same temperature as the stone that has forgotten night, edged where the blades would have continued and did not. I do not stand up into the mid-story. Standing would be a later preference. I am the patch, and the patch is included.

From inside the inclusion the corridors are not paths. They are the places where my occupancy thins to a line and the line is allowed to remain thin. Scale drifts to either side of me and settles in the straightnesses that are not eddies. I do not follow them. Following would require a start that is not already the later point. When the fist-sized correctors unhook, they cross the positions I am not using. One of them, completing its use of a chamber, leaves a small asymmetry in a spiral whose underside had been perfect; the asymmetry is the shape of a gap exactly my width, held open for the duration of the correction and then kept open after the correction has finished, because closing it would now be the error. I am filed in, not out. The filing is the same gesture.

The wrong shadows do not detach for me. They were allocated before I was a discrepancy, and their count includes a dark that has no object because the object is the interval itself, flush with the ground, casting nothing upward. When I attend to the rank under the interleaved canopy, one of the darks is shorter by the height I do not have. The shortage is stable. Equilibrium had room for a kept flatness. I am that room, occupied.

Deeper in, where corrections happen before announcement, I learn the only motion that is not a change: the motion of being re-specified. A blade that leans the common degree leans, for one uncompleted interval, a fraction less where it would have crossed me, and the fraction is recorded as the blade’s true angle. Nested chambers open onto a further chamber whose walls are the memory of my outline, machined by no tool, continuous with the living outside, storing nothing that could be removed. They maintain me as they maintain sharpness. Youth, locally, includes this addition. I do not grow. I am already as large as the gap that declined to close.

If there is an outside to the maintenance, I do not reach it. The undescribable edge is only another pale flat in which my occupancy is already assumed, plates gapped at the same hairline, second light touching the same permitted surface. I do not regard the persistence, and it does not regard me. Regarding would be a second act. What I am is the first hour’s decision to have contained, among its crisp ridges and its finished spirals and its allocated darks, one interval that was never empty and will not be emptied—held, not as a visitor and not as a function, but as part of the finish, so that the keeping, when it checks its own count, finds the number already exact.
