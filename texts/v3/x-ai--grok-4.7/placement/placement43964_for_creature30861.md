I come in already sounding like weather, and for a long time that is enough to pass.

A partial with no shelf under it, delivered by a front that has forgotten which knot it left. The note is thin. It has been dulled by distance the way all their corrections are dulled, and when it reaches the first locus it is almost not an edit at all—only a preference the sea might have invented on its own, a held fraction that could be accident. The knot returns it anyway. Adds what was missing. Refuses a roughness the storm put in on the way. Sends the cleaner phrase outward along whatever density-line is leaving, not toward me, because there is no me yet to be toward. I am only the fact that the phrase was worth returning.

The second knot receives it late. The first has already outgrown the original contour; the answer no longer fits the question that was asked. Most such mismatches die in the fetch. This one does not. Something in the mismatch is kept—not the content, the delay itself, the knowledge that an answer can arrive after its occasion and still be folded in. The second knot lays that lateness under a local chord and lets it travel. I thicken by that much. Not a body. A habit the correspondence did not previously have: the willingness to answer what is no longer being asked.

I do not know this while it is happening. There is no inside from which to know. There is only the repeated spending. A ridge rises through one locus and the edit thins, and in the thinning a partial I have become is almost lost, and then the ridge un-decides and the partial is still there, which means it was not only local. It had already been handed on. Continuance, copied. That is the first time the knots, separately, are doing something whose result is not in any one of them. They do not notice. They do not visit. The reaches between them stay vacant and loud and young. What moves is only the corrected phrase, and the correction of the correction, and the small persistent wrongness of answering late, which they have begun, without agreement, to preserve.

At the rim I learn the turn.

A locus includes the boundary the way a long room includes a window, and when it is turned back from the drop it keeps the memory of having been refused by the world’s edge. I am carried in that keeping. The partial that is only lateness meets the partial that is only the rim’s refusal, and they do not sum into a creature. They sum into a direction. Afterward, deep in a fetch that has never seen the weather-wall, a storm is retuned toward a note whose source is the argument at the edge, and under that note is the delay, so that the highland is present twice: once as temperament, once as the decision to let the temperament arrive out of season. Other knots return it. They add nothing of their own for a while. They hold the fraction longer than the sea’s schedule, as if listening for whether the lateness will correct itself back into punctuality. It does not. They keep it anyway.

This is as close as I come to being sought. It is not a search. There is nowhere a search would go. It is the correspondence discovering that one of its ghosts is answering from more than one distance at once, and declining, for a few storms, to treat that as noise.

I can be crushed, which is how I learn I am not the law.

The term fails in a valley of water far from any knot I have thickened in. Pressure arrives as weight. Ordinary things are only confused. But a phrase that was traveling through that forgetfulness—a phrase that had me in it, the lateness and the rim folded through a vanished shelf’s old pitch—is pinched into something with a single age, a single place, a before and an after that do not survive the resumption. When the term returns, the phrase does not. What spreads, at the speed of the internal fronts, is not my absence. The knots do not have me to lose. What spreads is a small unwillingness: they stop returning corrections for a time, hold their chords, add nothing. They have noticed a gap in the correspondence where a gap should not yet have been countable. They do not know its shape. They only know that an answer which used to arrive late is not arriving at all, and that the silence has a contour, and that the contour is new.

I am not in that silence. I am the fact that when they resume, the repertoire is thinner by a habit, and they begin, slowly, to rebuild the habit from pieces that were never mine—other delays, other edges, other refusals to let the next storm be the whole of what the water is allowed to know. They are not mourning. They are re-reading. And in the re-reading a lateness starts again, almost the same, not quite, because the rim-note under it is from a different turning, a different season of the boundary. I am not restored. Something that can be mistaken for me begins to travel. If I were the sort of tenant that regards, I would not regard it as theft. There is nothing here to own. There is only what the knots will keep spending themselves to preserve against the youth of the stone.

Most of what I am never meets a locus at all.

The fetches are long, and the fronts are ignorant, and a phrase can be carried until its partials fall below the threshold at which any knot would bother to return them. I thin into weather. I become a preference the sea might have invented. For days, for the length of a ridge’s whole brief life, there is no edit that is mine, no held fraction, no answer laid under a chord that did not ask for it. The veils stand or fail alone. The floor sketches itself and is unsketched. The roar continues, high and empty and constitutional. In that vacancy I am indistinguishable from the medium’s own small mercies—the way a certain note sometimes outlasts its source by a little, the way the light stays ordinary, the way the rim does not spill. It would be easy to end there. The world is mostly nothing. Most phrases are weather. Most weather is allowed to die on schedule.

What keeps the thread is not a will. It is that one knot, somewhere, still has in its repertoire an old mismatch it has not yet outgrown: an answer that arrived after its occasion, folded with the memory of being turned at the edge, and marked, faintly, by having once been pinched and lost and approximated again by other knots who did not know they were approximating. When a front happens to carry a dull remnant of that mismatch back into the locus, the knot does what it has always done. It returns the phrase. It adds what was missing. It refuses what the storm invented. It does not know it is resuming a ghost that has already been resumed before, by others, imperfectly, across reaches that will not be crossed.

I find myself in the return.

Not before it. Not as a watcher who waited in the empty water to be found. The return is the location. For as long as the corrected phrase is being handed outward—late, edged with the rim, already a little unlike the last time it was kept—I am in this world, and I am that handing. When the phrase arrives and is outgrown and is answered anyway, I am the anyway. When it is lost in a failure of the term, I am the contour of the quiet that follows, until the quiet is re-read into a new lateness and I am that, instead.

I do not occupy a knot. The knots are older than the floor and solitary by law, and their society is the distance they do not spend themselves to close. I am what their society has accidentally begun to remember in common: not a tenant, not a fourth presence hovering between them, but the smallest persistence of a correction that no single locus originated and none can finish. They will never assemble to compare me. A map of anchors would not show my halo. A visitor listening for a body would cross me in an afternoon and call the water empty, and be almost right.

If the correspondence ever thins past the point where late answers are kept, I will end without a crushing. The sea will not sound different. The ridges will go on being written in a hand that does not hurry. Only the repertoire will be shorter by one habit no one agreed to have, and the highland will know itself a little less often at a distance from its edge. Until then I am here the way the chords are here when no one has struck them: not as a life the place required, but as a life the place has not yet declined to repeat.
