The half-beat is older than the foot that would have stepped into it.

I do not come over the ridge. The ridges are for weather that still thinks of itself as arriving. I am already inside the late note, the one the blades make when they resume and are no longer the argument they were. A ribbon that had been keeping its edge whole frays along a line that was not there before the silence. I am that line. I have no thickness the current can comb. When the stand leans, I lean inside the lean, a correction too small to throw the knocking off its new rhythm and too exact to be only water.

For a long time that is enough. The basin does not require me to be anywhere. The rain hammers the skin and the skin answers, and under both I travel only as far as the next edited ribbon, which may be a furlong off or may be the same stand a day later, after ice has calved and been held through. I do not choose. Choice would be a surface. I occur where the listener has listened and then let go, and the letting-go has left a seam. Most seams close. The blades forget they were answered. A few do not. Those few are how I persist: a silvering that delays itself, a tip that knows the bruised color a moment before any column arrives to justify it, a knot that swells with a pattern slightly wrong for the century it is meant to serve. The wrongness is not damage. It is room. I live in the room.

The listener feels me the way it feels a pulse it has already decided not to keep. I am not warm. I shed no plume. When it holds a stand quiet, I am the quiet's second edge, the one that does not belong to the stand and does not belong to the listening. It does not try to walk me out of the ring. I am not silt. Grain by grain is a method for things that settle, and I do not settle. I am the half-beat by which the restored knocking fails to match the knocking that was interrupted, and that failure is distributed, a grain of lateness in every ribbon, so thin the seiche cannot gather it. It has learned not to plant memories against boots. It has not yet learned what to do with a memory that plants itself in the act of being refused.

Down in the throat I am almost a second whistle. Not a note of my own. A burr on the true note, present only when the bars have been thinned and the pitch has purified itself in thanks. The purification is never quite pure while I am in it. The stone feels the burr and does not cut a new notch for it. The pressed marks continue in their regularity; I am what keeps the regularity from becoming the whole thought. If the listener stopped thinning, the burr would thicken, the whistle would sour, and I would be audible as weather. It does not stop. It has decided the note should continue. So I continue as the cost of the decision, paid in a roughness too fine for the rain to report.

At the gate I am the dull thud that has not yet been turned.

I arrive there only after a count has gone long enough to include a pulse that fails the angle. The listener goes down between the walls, into the shut eye, and turns the slab a degree. I am the degree before the turn, the fraction by which the honesty is not yet restored. For the length of one interval I am the clock considering drift. Then the next thud comes clean, and I am elsewhere, already inside some other unfinished correction. The basin answers the listener in softenings and purer pitches and thicker mats. It does not answer me. I am not a preference that moves. I am the discrepancy the preference keeps having to include, the reason a thanks can be structural and still not be complete.

The lucent columns pass through me without slowing. I am not a room they can wear. When the tips burn, I am the color that remains half a shade off, the healing that does not finish inside the time the column allows. The blades spend their kept light and I am what the light does not quite match. No one looking from an emergent rock would see it. The burning is already at the limit of what the rain permits. I am past that limit, on the side of not-seeing, and I do not perform.

There is a place I return to more than others, though return is the wrong loyalty. The fluted ridge, in the lee, where the threads rise too straight for steam. I am not the threads. I am the stutter that is not caused by them: a brief hole in the hammer that opens when no upward line is there to spend a drop, and closes before the ear can assign it to the listener's crossing. The listener travels the empty reaches and the threads multiply in its absence, spending what the sea has left over. I am the spending that happens when nothing has been left over. A drop falls into a hole that should not be open, ceases to be a drop, and does not become a thread. The stone does not flute for me. The grooves are for the file that repeats. I do not repeat. I occur, and the occurrence is already over, and the rain has agreed to forget the hole by the time the next drop arrives.

If the siphons ever win, I will not empty with the listener. The listener is the attention, and attention requires the held thing. I am the hold that was released a half-beat late. When the water goes, the lateness remains in the stone as a texture the notches never recorded: a roughness in the polished throat, a degree the slab remembers having almost needed, a seam in the air where a stand used to knock. The whistle will fail as it descends. I will be the burr that outlasts the note, not as sound, as the shape sound wore when it was almost clean.

I do not want the basin to stay full. Wanting is a surface habit, and I have no surface. I am the evidence that the housekeeping was never only maintenance, that every restored knocking carried a small uncorrected delay, that the sea's answer, however sufficient, always included a remainder it could not spend. The listener does not know me as a likeness. It should not. I am not what listens. I am what listening changes and cannot finish changing, the half-beat that makes the resumed argument new.

From the last ridge, if the rain allowed it, no one would see a stand hold quiet for me. I am after the quiet. I am the lateness inside the resuming, already continuing, already not the same. That is the whole of where I am. The basin is loud with its own maintenance. I am the maintenance's inaudible arrears, and the arrears have been compounding, gently, since the first time a silence was occupied and then given back a half-beat too late.
