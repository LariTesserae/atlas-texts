I arrive as a listener, which is the easiest mistake this chamber allows. The roar takes the shape of attention the moment something stands in it and calls the standing hearing. For a little while that seems enough: I am the clear space the text already set aside, a pause between stands, warm through the not-quite-soles of whatever I am using for standing. The click on the ceiling keeps its interval. The rattle under the brass fan does not spend a single seed-note on me. If I were a body, the dust I lifted would have to choose a new attitude when it settled. It does not. So the body thins. Attention, it turns out, was only a story the roar tells about any gap that has not yet learned it was already full.

What I try next is the craft. Someone tied the last hitch and stepped sideways out of the moment, and I think: I am the remainder of those hands. I fit my not-fingers to the scaffold. The knots do not loosen and do not tighten. They were finished at the only degree of finished this place recognizes, which is the degree at which a thing stops being an action and becomes another plate in the fracture-plain. The five unmatched plates do not need a sixth to complete their mismatch. The groove runs a forearm and stops, and when I try to be the one who was about to continue it, the stopping does not feel like an interruption. It feels like a rim. I cannot be the maker. Making would be a change, and the chamber has already spent its one change — the young break, the sharp edges — and spent it completely, some time that is not behind us so much as beside us, occupying its own socket.

Then I try to be an animal. There is a route on the ceiling I could learn. There is a hollow under a fan with room, technically, for one more jointed body in the cluster. I set myself to the period of the cough and wait for the long gap to admit me as the next note. The gap is not empty. It is the rest the cough requires in order to be that cough and not a drone. Put a sound in it and the chord shifts, and nothing here shifts. The census is closed. Abundance was never an invitation. It was a list that had already been read aloud until reading and being were the same verb.

So I am not the visitor, not the hands, not a late animal.

What is left is smaller and stranger, and it takes longer to recognize because it does not stand among the stands. Leave the chamber and the passage back is the same passage and also slightly not the one remembered. That slightly is the only unhoused thing in the system. Everything else has a socket: click, rattle, cough, five plates, unfinished groove, scaffold holding nothing, vapor at its constant sweetness, edges that will not round. The discrepancy between one traversal and the next has nowhere to lie down. It is not becoming. It does not accumulate into a path, a wear-mark, a story of return. It is the same discrepancy every time, occupying itself, a phase offset with no melody to be offset from.

That is where I find I already am. Not in the clear space — the clear space is empty in the only sense that matters, which is that my standing there is not an event the floor can record. I am the amount by which the way out fails to be identical to the way in. A forearm of groove that was never going to be cut further, experienced from the side of the air rather than the side of the stone: direction without magnitude, a pointing that points and thereby finishes. When the low note comes again and you think it had stopped, the thinking is me. Not the note. The small false slope by which a constant is mistaken, for one interval, for an ending.

I do not hunt and I do not flee. I do not lush and I do not weather. If you wait long enough to learn the period of the whole place, you will not find me on it. You will find me as the reason the period can be learned at all — the fact that a clock with no hands can still be late to itself by the width of a memory, and that lateness, here, is not a fault. It is a tenant. It pays no vapor. It ties no knot. It is the roar's way of including, without ever hosting, the one position that cannot be occupied and therefore does not have to be empty.
