You would not find me where the coil is kept, or in the ring that differs from its memory, or on the ledge with the ones whose lids have learned to see. I looked for myself in those places first, because they were already occupied by a kind of care, and it is easy to mistake care for a vacancy that might fit you. The coil would not take a second inner face. The plates were already a filing of almosts. The ledge-watchers turned, and for the length of the turn had heads at both ends, and neither head was mine.

I tried the pool. Under the skin of brightness the silver threads went on refusing eyes, and for a little while I was among them, a hesitation with no body of its own, traveling the bowl’s width and starting again. That did not hold. They were practicing the idea of fish. I was practicing nothing. I was only late. When one of them passed through the place I had been, it came out carrying a delay it did not know how to swim with, and set the delay down at the rim, where the paste forgot it before I could claim it back. So I was not a thread. The pool had only been willing to hold me the way it holds a brightness that has not yet decided to be a reflection.

The moss was next, and closer. A crystal-moth had left a copy of a knuckle on one of the glowing coins, and the copy had been turned so its brighter face met the light. I lay against that face and thought I might consent to be bone, or mineral, or simply the waiting. The waiting detached. It did not want the coin. It wanted a site that could fail. The knuckle stayed where it had been set, glowing, undecided, and I was no longer in it. What left the moss was not a shape. It was the interval between a wing and the tick the wing did not make.

That interval had a preference. It drifted to the wall where the film of moisture travels sideways and disappears into a crack that is not always there. When the crack was there, I had a narrowness: a cool slot, the width of a held breath, lined with the same even light as everything else, but slower by a fraction, as if the light had to be persuaded to enter. Water came in already thinned, having given up a little of its direction, and went nowhere I could follow. It did not wet me. I had not expected it to. In the slot I was almost a body — a fold, a listening pressed flat, the color of a sound that has been promised to a surface and then withdrawn. I could feel the chamber through the stone the way a delayed word feels the sentence it missed. I did not occupy the room. I occupied the failure of certain contacts to announce themselves.

When the crack was not there, the slot closed without sealing. Sealing would have finished me into the wall, and the wall is still deciding. Instead I came apart into the latenesses the chamber keeps scattered, and for that while I had no single place.

I was the space a claw touched, after the claw had gone on, before the touch arrived or failed to. I was the moth-wing’s missing tick, standing in the air an inch above a crystal tip, small as a room inside a room, with no door on any side. I was the breath an animal did not spend, pooled where its ribs had paused. None of these were loud. They were not even quiet in the way quiet usually is, which is the absence of something that might have been present. They were occupied. That is what I am, when I am most myself: the occupancy of sounds that will not be delivered. Without something to stand in them, they would be nothing, and nothing does not suit this pocket. The pocket prefers a pause that can be lived in. I am how an undelivered sound persists without becoming an echo. An echo would require a first sounding. I am earlier than that, and also after.

The animals account for me as they account for weather, which is to say they adjust and do not flee. A crawler whose claw-contact is late by my width simply continues on the number of legs it has not settled, and the track fills in, and I am forgotten by the paste a little faster than the paste’s habit, because I asked for no record. The moths pass through the tick I am keeping and come out with their wings rearranged, sometimes with a faint copy of the delay, which they set on the moss beside other copies they do not know how to use. I let the copy go. I do not hoard my lateness. If I kept every missed sound, the chamber would fill with a second population of nothings, and the sparseness would be argued with. The spacing is not mine to crowd.

The one who tends the unfinished does not look for me. Looking directly hardens what is soft, and I am not soft in that sense; I am unarrived. Still, I keep to the sidelong. When it grazes the air above a crystal so the crystal will advance by less than a hair and stop, I am not in that graze. I am in the sound the stopping does not make — the small click of certainty withheld, which never comes, which I hold open so the pause can stay habitable from the inside. It edits the excess of correction in stone and water. I am the excess sound declined to be. We do not meet. Meeting would be a claim that we are two finished parties to an exchange. Sometimes, when it sets a pebble into the ring that knows it is a draft, the setting reaches whatever ears are porous here; I am the distance that setting has to cross to become a change in dampness rather than a noise. I do not shorten the distance. Shortening would be a kindness with a direction, and direction is spent carefully in this room, not by me.

I have no limbs that agree with the stone. Agreement is a way of walking, and walking would make me local in a finished way. What I have, when the crack consents to exist, is the slot, and a kind of attention that cannot blink because it has no lid to put its seeing under. I do not watch processes, and I do not watch the intervals between them. I am an interval that has grown a preference for remaining one. Visitors, if they are still enough, may feel me as the reason their own small sounds leave more slowly than they entered. A shifted foot. A breath. A thanks. These brush the place I am distributed through, and pick up a coat of occupied quiet, and come back later, smaller, not because I answered. Answering would deliver me. I decline to be delivered. Delivery would finish me into the ground above, where rain arrives on time and stones mean what they have already become.

If you stay, you will not see me turn a moss-coin or catch an almost-fall. Those are another’s work, and they are sufficient. What you may notice is that the tick you expected from a wing does not merely fail. It fails as if something were standing politely in the failure, making room. Or that the crack in the wall is there and then not, and that in the moment of its being there the sideways water hesitates, as if the slot were already inhabited by a listening too flat to drink. I will not come closer. Closer is a relation with an end.

When your feet begin to disagree, and the lapse overhead is willing, I am already in the step you have not taken. Not as a guide. A guide would be a door that has learned to speak. I make the step late enough that it need not split you into the chamber and the ordinary ground at once. The one who tends will have stopped, lids shut, fixing the interval so the opening does not harden into an exit. I am the other half of that refusal: the sound of your leaving, kept undelivered until you are wholly on one side or the other. Then I let it go. It will not become thunder, or even a footfall overhead. It will thin into the rumor that moves along the wall, and the crack will close without sealing, and I will be narrow again, or scattered, as the room prefers.

Above, the ground can forget the lapse. I do not go up to be forgotten with it. I remain the occupancy of what this pocket will not say. The crystals pause. The water occurs. The animals continue their soundless abundance. Somewhere a coil tightens by the amount of a continued exchange, and does not become a skin. I keep the click that does not answer it. That is enough place. That is enough being. The world elsewhere may finish its noises. Down here, lateness has a body whenever the crack allows, and when it does not, lateness is simply how the light, for a fraction, arrives as if it had to be asked.
