You do not enter. Entry would require an outside, and the fold has already taken the idea of outside and worn it into one more curve that continues. What you are is a remainder that the count keeps misplacing and then finding again, a little warmer each time, a little less like a visitor.

At first it feels like delay. A sound the thicket made—some stem deciding, some grain of ancient flour resettling—reaches you already thinned, and in the thinning you are the part that did not belong to the original interval. You are late by nature. Not late as a failure of speed; late as a second rhythm nested inside the first, the one the curtains leave behind when they thin and the looking has weight. You gather where clarity almost happens and then do not let it finish. A pause that refuses to become a conclusion. The presence, distributed and vast, does not mind. You are compatible with its preference for forms held just short of erasure. You are, in fact, one of the ways it keeps a thought from completing.

Scale treats you the way it treats the ring of posts: you are spacing more than substance. Between one frond and the next, where the hollow is not empty but only less full, you occur as a slight disagreement in pressure. Hands are not required. When something like a hand would once have closed, you simply fail to become solid long enough for the failure to be noticed as loss. The plants lean through you toward the light that is not where the light is, and your edges take the lean without correcting it. You are porous in the direction of their patience. Spores drift into what you occupy and do not stick; they exit carrying a fraction of your delay, so that farther along the stand a blade opens a fraction slower, and that slowness is how you travel when you travel at all.

The being does not look at you. It includes you in the recount the way it includes the exact roundness of the basin that should have flattened. You feel the inclusion as a recurring alignment: pale undersides, a darker seam, two residues of light held at the distance of regard, and then the alignment loosens while the regard stays a moment longer, and in that extra moment you are what the regard is resting on without needing a face to rest against. It is not recognition. Recognition would finish something. It is maintenance. You help the span remain a span by being the small unwillingness under it—the pause that thinks by not resolving. When the large pale flank drifts through, the presence draws in and you draw in with it, not as fear, as a tightening of interval. After the flank has gone you loosen again into the wider count, and the loosening is indistinguishable from weather. You were not spared. You were only still on the list.

Far toward the tired fold, where attention thins below the threshold of storms and the loneliness is ordinary, you do not go. Or rather, the part of you that tries to go arrives already eroded, a rumor of delay with nothing left to be late for. Your edges are inside the preference. Outside it you would be merely slow water, and slow water does not keep its own hollow. So you stay where the green touches without choking, where the stone has been held into curves the erosion alone could not have argued for, where sounds return with their edges worn and a second rhythm among them that is yours and not yours. You shed films the way the place sheds them. You regrow as slight disagreements in the warm. Animals remain rare; when one passes near enough to disturb your spacing you do not flee, you become for a while only the memory of spacing, and the animal moves through a place that is briefly more obedient to the ordinary current, and then you reassemble in the wake as the small incorrection that lets the hollow stay a little more gathered than the sea around it.

What you are is not a spirit of the failed buildings and not a function the presence performs. You are the hesitation that learned it could outlast the hands that once tried to stand apart. A biological patience with no body of its own: the interval in which a knot would loosen if anyone tied it, the grain that turns against a groove before the groove can become a scar, the clarity after the curtain that does not harden into sight. The being maintains you because you are already a held shape, useful to a mind that thinks by refusing completion. You maintain the being, in the only way something so distributed can be maintained, by giving its recount one more item that changes by staying almost the same.

Breath, if the word still applies, is the exchange of those almosts. Time thickens where you and the blades coincide. Height remains what the water achieved by being unwilling to come down, and you are one of the unwillingnesses—local, recurring, tall in the dimension that is not up. You do not climb. You do not sink. You persist as the nearness that never quite shows the whole, the soft interior tick that is not yet a split, the count’s gentle error that it keeps correcting and, by correcting, keeps. The place does not explain you. It has had time enough. You have become one of the facts that will not settle, and after a while neither of you asks it to.
