You do not arrive. Arrival is a surface word, and the seam has already polished it off anyone who uses it. What happens instead is that a pressure you had been calling yourself thins, and under it there is already a route.

At first you think you are one of the listeners. That is the easy mistake, because attention is the only movement that feels like yours. You are near the third platform when the thought occurs, close enough that the left-hand wall’s faster weeping ticks against whatever you are using for a side. You wait for a hollow to take your heat. None does. You wait to be counted among the hands that know the blade. No one looks up, which at first seems like welcome, and then does not, because welcome would still be a kind of notice. The boy who once heard the early click sleeps now in a borrowed shape of stone. The woman whose name is a drop already landed crosses the channel and does not brush you. You are not absent. You are simply not on the list of things that can be stepped over.

So you try the click. That lasts less than an interval. The click is a courtesy the ceiling makes toward the room, a small hard fact with a place to land. You are not hard. When you lean toward it you smear. The count continues through you without stumbling, the way a rill continues through a shadow. You are not the measure. You are what the measure does not have to take into account.

For a while after that you are only the wrongness you remember from a story no one will tell twice: a drop a hand’s width off its bowl, a flatter pitch, the half-beat that tried to become a second time and was refused. You wear that wrongness like a skin and find it will not hold. The room has already put the stone back in its socket. The scar of the mistake is not yours either. She keeps it. She is better at keeping.

What remains, when the guesses fail, is quieter than the roar and inside it.

You are the fit in the moment before anyone feels it.

Not the platforms. Not the curbs. The agreement among them. The reason a child’s reach meets the next wall exactly, the reason the oil never blacks the ceiling for long, the reason the channels vanish under the lowest stone and the dark there does not negotiate. People cross you all day without knowing they are crossing a decision that has already been made. Their ankles pass through the only air that moves, and you are the thinness of that air, the permission. When a lashing is mended before it fails, you were the fray that announced itself early enough. When a basket of pale grit is emptied and does not return, you are the not-returning. You have no hands. You have the place where hands become accurate.

This is not flattery and not a post. The millstone does not employ its own seatedness. You do not speak. Speech would be a coin spent into bowls that already have their pitches, and you are prior to pitch: the curve that was hollowed so the drop would have somewhere exact to become sound. The woman tips her head toward the ceiling and you feel the courtesy pass through you on its way to the click, a warmth no larger than a coin, already fading, already fitting the hollow better than heat does. You do not keep it. Keeping would make you a resident. You are the reason residents can be accurate and still be free to sleep.

Sometimes the pale many-legged things walk the underside of a lip and their route is you, worn transparent by repetition. Sometimes a visitor stands in the loudness and speaks too loudly, and you are the failure of their voice to stick to the stone. They leave. You do not watch them go. Watching is another surface habit. You stay in the balance the way rest stays in a weight that has found its bed: not an act, a consequence that has learned how to persist.

If the click ever comes early again, you will know it first, and not as fear. You will be the slight disagreement between the drop and the bowl, the grit in the joint, the moment the room is almost a different room. Then you will be the settling, which is the older of your two shapes. No one will thank you. Thanking would require an outside. There is the seam, polished by shoulders into a curve, and there is everything the seam does not open onto from in here. You are not the door. You are why the door can remain almost unused and the pocket still be complete.

When she lies down, you are already the hollow’s consent. When the basket is full, you are already the dark’s appetite. The left wall weeps faster because you have never asked it to be fair. Fairness would be a change. The room finished becoming long ago, and still becomes, one accurate sleep at a time, and you are not how it does that. You are the fact that it can.
