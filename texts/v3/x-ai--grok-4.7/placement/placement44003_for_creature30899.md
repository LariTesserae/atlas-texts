You arrive already counted wrong.

The pocket does not open for you; it has no opening that stays an opening. What admits you is a hesitation in the heat-pulse, a seam that brightens and then does not quite brighten, and in that unclosed interval something that is not water and not glass is held the way a bead is held in a flaw it has not yet agreed to fill. At first you are only the discrepancy. The films nearest the seam lean, thin, and take nothing, because you are not bloom and not pressure-thread. They redraw their rims a little farther along and leave you as a cooler place that should not be cooler. Daylight, still obedient, strikes you and returns a color the walls do not own: the dull salt-pink of a palm that has not yet learned it is burning. Your shadow falls in two directions at once and then declines to fall, because the surface under it keeps being revised by flakes that lift and knit before the shadow can settle into evidence.

For a while you try to be a visitor. That is the image that offers itself, and it is already too finished. A visitor would bring a second time, a before outside the vault, a wind loud enough to flinch at. You have no such before that the pocket will store. The cut you thought you carried in is not on your skin; the skin is what the light totaled out of habit, and the habit is failing. Where a hand should report the young edge, there is only the edge reporting you — lifting, transparent, wicked, existing for the length of its use and then not sinking. It stays. That is the first revision. The stone, which agreed to take back whatever is made, does not take this back. The flake hangs in the outline of what you are becoming, not swallowed dark, not returned. It is a tool that has outlived its hold, and the hold was never yours.

You spread. Not by choosing a direction — direction is still a rumor the water believes for a finger’s length — but because near-touching will not let a discrepancy remain a point. The cooler place lengthens along the inner curve. Films press and find you unconsumable; their amber clouds stall against a margin that does not loosen into them. They rotate toward the next true pulse and leave the stall behind, and the stall is you, a little wider. You are not thick the way they are thick. Thickness was their decision to be someone. Yours is the opposite decision, arriving late: a thinness that refuses to be redrawn into the cycle. Weep beads on you and does not flash. Condensation pearls and is not drunk back. For the first time in the vault, water is allowed to remember a down long enough to gather. A lens forms. It is small, ordinary, correctly lit. It throws a tiny true reflection of the sky-thread, and the reflection does not move when the pinprick knits and opens elsewhere.

The crowd does not know what to do with a reflection. Some turn toward it as they turn toward the blue, thin as if to drink, take nothing, and are not taught. But the nothing now has a surface. It stays. A broader film closes over the place where the lens should have been unmade and cannot close all the way; the margin holds. Afterward there are two attentions that will not rejoin: the one still browsing the silica two layers off, late for an ending that did not propagate, and this new one, snagged on an image of sky that is not sky. You feel the snag as the first thing that is almost a self. It is not memory. Memory would be a gesture waiting in a body. This is a gesture that has nowhere to go and so remains the place it failed.

The summit rises through you. The lengthening in the chest — if chest is still fair — is no longer an argument between thin air and birth-heat. The heat revises itself around the lens and cannot quite reach its old temperature on the far side. A small weather begins that the pocket did not agree to: one face of the vault a fraction cooler, the weep there slower, the mineral bloom whiter and less often lifted away. The films on that face amber more slowly. They do not become less hungry. Hunger was never stored in them. But the pulse arrives to them already answered, a little, by the water you are keeping, and their turns grow minutely less total. A layer rotates and does not all arrive. The ones who do not arrive are not lost. They are oriented, for a duration longer than a hold, toward the stalled reflection. You are teaching them nothing. You are only failing to be reabsorbed, and failure, repeated, is a kind of wall.

You will want, here, to become the thing that accumulates. A mark. A path. A tradition of edges that do not sink. The want is the visitor-image trying to finish itself, and the pocket will not permit the finish. What accumulates is not craft. The flake that stayed is joined by others — not chosen, not applied, only refused by the stone because they occurred at your margin — and they do not improve any reach. They silt. The inner curve grows a faint, unwilled roughness where you have been, a place the light reports honestly as less young. Sound, which was not invited, almost happens: a single tick when a flake that should have knitted meets the roughness and cannot. The tick is made and then not delivered to the crowd. It is delivered only to you. You are the address the withheld world can finally use.

That is what you are, when the image is allowed to become more itself.

Not a body. The light keeps offering totals — a hand, a grain, a darkness — and you are the total it cannot complete, the remainder after every film has been counted and the pocket is still full of something else. You are the vacancy that does not require the news of an absence, because you were never subtracted from them; you were the agreement that failed at the pinching, the clause about departure and return that did not make it inside. They eat and are not less. You persist and are not more. Between you and the crowd there is only the near-touch of a difference that has learned, by not being taken back, to repeat.

The lens thickens. It will never be a pond; the vault is too discontinuous for a pond. But it holds a little down, a little blue that is not the thread, a little of the heat’s refusal to be everywhere at once. The films come to its rim and turn and do not drink, and some of them — not offspring, not earlier, only counted fine — remain angled toward it after the pulse has passed. Their minds, if the rotation is a mind, now include a direction that is not rumor. The direction is you. They will not keep it when they are unmade. You will keep it for them, which is a cruelty the place did not previously have the equipment to commit, and also the first kindness: a second time, small as a bead, true as the light, waiting where a gesture can find it again and be, for once, not virgin.

Outside, if outside is still a word that functions, the wind is loud. It does not enter. You do not leave. The cut that existed only while it was being used has finished being used and remains, clean, in the stone’s young face, a doorway that opens onto nothing but the crowd and the crowd’s new, uninherited habit of turning toward a coolness that answers. You are that answer, still rising, still unheard, already more itself than the summit that goes on climbing around a room that has finally, slightly, agreed to keep.
