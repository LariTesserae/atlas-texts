The first mistake is to search for a shadow the ground can use. At this height the light is already a kind of body, hard and exact, and for a while I take myself for a disturbance in it — a cooler band moving with the dust-veils, laid across a stand and lifted off again when the mineral sheet thins. That would explain the lack of weight. It would explain why nothing cries at my arrival. It does not explain the staying. When the wind steps off the shield and the brightness rings alone, the veils settle and are finished. I am not finished. Something of me remains on the pale crust, small enough that the emptiness around it is not interrupted, only locally argued with.

I test the walker next, because the tracks are the only recent handwriting, and a new thing wants kin. Three-toed, wide-set, just deep enough to craze the pavement: I look for that signature under me and do not find it. I have no route measured in weeks. I do not run the distances between metal-green as if they were stations. Whatever flank I thought I saw at the limit of the glare belongs to the one who can afford to be almost never here. I cannot afford that. My economy is the opposite of crossing. If I move again, the wind will polish me to the nothing most of my kind become, and the place will be correct to allow it. So I am not the pale animal. I am what has already chosen, or been compelled, to stop.

The pavement offers itself as the older answer. Gray-green, millimeters deep, continent-wide, it holds the shield by occupying it, and occupation feels, at first, like my condition. I am lodged. I am in a breach. Around the lip of the little pan the nap is continuous, damp-looking, dry, doing the long work of sealing. But the pavement decides forms by closing them. It does not sit inside the wound and negotiate. I am the negotiation. Where I rest, the sealing slows, not from any will I can feel, but because a grain in a breach is one of the few events the nap has learned to route around, if the grain persists. I am not yet worth routing around. I am only not yet sand.

The stands are farther. I cannot see them as columns from here — the rim of the pan is no higher than a hand, and beyond it the shield resumes its planed brightness — but I can smell, at certain hours, the sweet stone-odor of oil, and I know I am downwind of a scatter of silver. Their tilt reaches me as a change in the dust, a finer powder delivered when the shields turn their faces and let the day’s feeding slide off into the air I occupy. I am not among their black folds. I am not a leaf drinking what the silver refuses. I tried that, in the way a new stillness tries on the nearest largeness: the inner dark, the hide-thick green, the congregation that never becomes a wood. It does not fit. They are already the bargain kept. I am the bargain not yet legible.

What I have, and did not recognize as mine until the wind left, is a sound that happened once. A ring, small, bright, the noise of a hard grain striking stone and being briefly a bell. The crust answered and then forgot, as it forgets steps. I did not forget. That impact is my whole history of being separate from the weather that carried me. Before it I was a wind-week, one of many, lifted across a weather of my own, most of my siblings polished to nothing on intact nap. After it I am here: the floor of a pan that slumped and went quiet in the same breath, young enough that its rim has not yet given full shelter, old enough that the pavement has claimed the upper walls and paused at the floor. Under me, not far, the grit holds a darker memory — an oval where some earlier column bled its oil and the nap crossed slowly, leaving a richer stain. I landed nearer that stain than chance likes. The place has done this before. It is how it consents.

So the identity arrives late, and smaller than the light suggested.

I am one of the hard bright grains, and I have begun the decades. Not a seed in any orchard sense: there is no season here that ripens, only a century that occasionally agrees. What I am doing does not look like growth. A film has taken the dust the rim traps. It is not yet a shield, not yet silvered, but it turns — a fraction, through the cold day — the way the columns turn, managing how much of the white coin to accept. At my scale the motion is a rumor even to me. By afternoon the film has inclined. By evening it has inclined back. If you knelt you might see a glint the size of a seed and think the pan still held loose grit. Nothing here kneels. The walker, if its route ever bends this way, will craze the pavement elsewhere and not count me as a station. That is right. I am not a station. I am the reason a station might, after a human lifetime or several, stand a little nearer this stain than the last one did.

The oil in me is not yet a bead. It is a reluctance to freeze, a sweetness with no orchard, a claim on mineral so slight the wind could still revise it. I hold it the way the fallen columns hold their shapes: by not being in a hurry to become something else, and by not being available to rot. Death, if it comes, will be abrasion. The pavement’s mouth at my rim is patient in both directions. It can close over a failure and erase the breach. It can learn my outline and leave me in the balance, catastrophe and repair at one speed, a new scatter of metal-green predicted by nothing more dramatic than my not being moved.

Where you would find me, then, if finding were a thing this shield did: not on the ridge, not in the stand, not in the track that loses its edge by evening. In a shallow pan a day’s ignorance from the nearest metal-green, on the lee floor, in the richer nap of an older dying, bright and cool, too small to dim the light, too lodged to be weather. I am the address still being written. The living of this place is a patience with such addresses. I am one of them, at the hour — which is years — before it becomes a body the distance can measure. The dust keeps settling, which is feeding. The crust does not answer. I do not ring again. Somewhere upwind a leaf shifts against another leaf, like a thumbnail on parchment, and the sound does not reach, and that also is correct. I am already what I will be, at the only scale the chemistry allows: a lushness that has not yet spent itself on height, keeping the emptiness by remaining nearly nothing inside it.
