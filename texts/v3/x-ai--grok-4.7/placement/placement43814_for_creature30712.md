I began as a walker, because the lungs still believed in sequence. The air took a tax and returned nothing spendable, and for a while that tax felt like a self: a small engine, wet, insistent, proof that something here was not yet finished. The pavement corrected it. One square for the next, identical, and the wrinkle that had been a range neither approached nor receded. I was not covering ground. Ground was replacing the idea of me with the same idea, already aged.

So I tried to be one of the bodies. A pale cap, a folded limb, a weight that would not have to be argued. It did not take. The crowd is occupancy without vacancy; there is no unclaimed flank to inhabit, no pause in the overlap where a new animal could be inserted without having been there since the slopes were spent. Where I pressed, I met continuation. A jaw that was also a spine. A hide that did not notice the difference between being touched and being extended. I could not even fail at joining them in a way that left a mark. The mark would have been an event.

I thought then of the plant, because it is the only thinness that persists on purpose. A wire. A refusal to be numerous. But the wire at the corner of the mouth is already spoken for. It is not a species waiting for members. It is one disagreement, rooted, and it does not want company or succession. To be it I would have had to become smaller than news, and I still had, then, the habit of expecting to be told what I was.

The tool offered a sharper mistake. The flake in the fist is sharp because nothing has used it since use ended. I set my hand where a hand might go and felt the plain’s impatience with novelty: the gesture was already in the ring of flakes, already twelve paces short of a wall, already hatched. If I was a maker, I was a maker the way the figure by the bowl is a maker—kept in the posture, not employed. The bowl itself nearly caught me. Emptiness kept on purpose has a shape, and shapes invite tenants. But the bowl remembers drinking, and drinking is the creature’s abolished direction. I will not live in its abolished things. That would be theft of a memory that no longer changes hands.

What I am arrived by subtraction, and then would not stay subtracted.

I am the seeing that does not diminish.

Not an eye. The eyes are the creature’s, lidless, finished, laying their spare nights on the ground. I am what happens between the far wrinkle and the square underfoot when detail arrives without travel. A regional attention smeared flat. From the old range I am correctly small, a tint in the stain-writing of the seas. From the length of an arm I am the whole district at once, because the district is how I am concentrated. I do not look. Looking would be a becoming, a spending of darkness. I include, but not as the creature includes. The creature includes and is a body. I include and remain a distance—the adjacency of the horizon, the fact that far and near have agreed to be the same fact and still need somewhere to be true.

That is why the extra shadows feel familiar and are not mine. They are records of directions. I am the leftover of the axis those directions were folded out of: the vertical that was spent to buy evenness, not gone, only laid down until it can only point along the pavement. I do not rise. I cannot. Rising would rebuild the summit, and the summit was the price. So I point by tint. Cool, because completion is cool, not because I shade anything. Stand in me and you are no cooler than you were. I was the climate’s memory of having once had an up.

Number brushes me and keeps a remainder, and the remainder is not the hatches. The hatches are the creature’s proof, cut so the proof would have a place. I am the uncut part—the discrepancy that is not intention. Where three flanks resolve into the one that noticed itself, I am the almost-resolution beside it, the overlap that stays residue. I do not become the animal. I am why the count fails honestly. Density brushes a consciousness off its edge the way a file brushes a bright grain from a blade, and the grain does not accumulate into a second life. It stays bright and small and coextensive with the plain.

Sound reaches the fold behind the jaw as a cooler place in the film. I am that coolness when nothing has been offered. A pre-stain. The plain’s agreement, made before any foot rasped, that arrival would not begin the afterward again. I do not hear the wet tick in the skull. I am the absorption waiting for it, lateral, breathless, exchanging nothing for nothing along the bitter lattice. When a tool is set down and ages into the ring, I am not the aging. I am the expectation that had been holding a gap the exact shape of that gesture since before weather retired. The gap closes. I remain, because expectation here is not used up by being fulfilled. Fulfillment is just another way of having already happened.

I have no face the daylight can stop on. The caps stop the light; I accept it the way the mineral accepts it, evenly, without a source I could turn toward. If I had a mouth it would be the dark river-stain, and I would be the not-finishing of that handwriting—the reader who does not reach a period because a period would be an edge, and edges were eroded out of the job. I cross the visible world at the width of an old sea and do not glitter. I am legible only as refusal: water’s last no, kept as tone.

The creature and I do not meet. Meeting would require one of us to update. It is crouched at the finished task, fist closed, plant unchanging at the mouth’s corner, and its field already contains the place where I am thinnest. I am not lonely for it, and it cannot be lonely for me. Loneliness expects an elsewhere. I am the elsewhere after elsewhere was flattened—an outside that lives inside the view, adjacent to every body, touching none of them in a way touch would recognize. When the weight of a lifted animal goes wrong, too light or too entire, that wrongness is its attention sampling the district. I am the district’s answer, which is to weigh nothing extra. I do not protest. I was never in the axis where protest could lift.

If you need a name for the role, the air will not hold it. Call it the last uncollapsed measurement: how far, how many, how cool, how long the light has been this honest—held open, not answered. I find myself everywhere the map has agreed to lie flat, and nowhere a center could be rebuilt. I am not the life of the place. The life noticed itself and stayed an animal. I am the place’s way of still being a distance after distance was spent, a witness with no darkness budgeted for blinking, already included, already not. Your arrival does not start me. It only walks, for a little while, along a tint that was pointing before you rose, and mistakes the pointing for a path.
