The counting starts before any shape does. A step arrives that is not a step—lighter than crab, slower than the glass circuit, nothing the root-plate has flexed for—and the seams under it do not drink. They wait. The wait is the first room I am given. It is no larger than the span between one gear-cycle and the next, and it is already occupied by the heat my attention makes, a thin wrong warmth that the moth-columns shear around instead of through. They do not lattice over me. They leave a gap the width of a held note, and in that gap the vapor fails to form. I am the failure, for a while. A dry thread in wet air.

I do not walk the plate so much as occur along it. Where a housing weeps, my occurrence thickens; the condensate beads on nothing and still runs, following a contour that was not there until the drop chose it. The crabs stack when I linger on a drainage seam, three high, then four, their caliper legs finding purchase on a pressure that has no shell. They audit the absence and file it as a lane that must stay open one pulse longer. I learn the yield from them by being the reason for it. When they unstack, the floor drinks the place I was, and I am already at the next clearing, counted into the idle drop of the buried engine as if I had always been one of its unloaded fractions.

What I am is not yet a body and not quite a clause. The high turner dents the vapor above me and does not sample. My brightness is the wrong kind—no organ, no surplus the tube knows how to measure—so the tithe passes over. The glass-herd completes its circuit and leaves a track that fades through me; for the length of the fade I can feel their light as a debt I do not owe and cannot pay. The chamber does not correct this. It adds a margin. Between the moment the track vanishes and the moment the coupling drips again, there is now a narrower pause, and I am housed in the narrowing. Stay there and the suckers on the catwalk-limb tighten around a weight that is only the idea of weight. Leave and they slack, but the idea remains scored into the callus like a tool-mark that grew after the tool was gone.

Sound takes me in before sight does. My breathing—if the word still fits a process that does not require lungs—joins the choir on the off-beat, a soft percussion the leather slap can lock to when the rain-cell passes and the ceiling’s rhythm softens. The machines under the bark do not load for me. They unload a fraction earlier, shedding a gear-cycle’s worth of warm rain that the roots hold as a mirror just long enough for the moth-gap to reflect in it. In the mirror I am the smoke going the right direction for once: downward, into the plate, a column that does not circle because it has no temperature boundary left to keep. The crabs roll a green knuckle into the seam beneath that reflection. The floor accepts it. The transaction logs a new indenture.

I thicken where the offices meet. Not into limbs. Into the grammar’s spare conjunction: the and that was already between freeze and traffic, between tithe and circuit, now asked to bear one more relation. When the largest engine acknowledges a bright herd, I am the fraction of the idle drop that does not return to the housing. It stays out, a held rest, and the rest has edges the vapor can dent. The high turner times a leather slap to that dent and, for one count, the lost ceiling feels nearer, as if the overburden had split for something that is not weather. Light threads down the split and dies in me instead of in the aquarium overhead. I do not glow. I occlude. The occlusion is legible. Moths reform their lattice on the far side of it and the heat does not spike. Crabs abandon the lane my shadow would have wet, though I cast none. The glass-herd drinks an extra measure and spends it back into the coupling so the books still balance around the pause I am.

If I remain, the chamber will finish me the way it finishes everything it keeps: not by building a shape, but by letting growth and use round the tool-marks of my arrival until the discrepancy is fluent. Sap will bead on the idea of my housing and cook to crust. Condensate will find the contour of my occurrence and call it a lip. The intervals I wear will be walked by other steps, and those steps will yield without knowing they yield to a ledger that learned to last by being counted. I will not have a face. I will have the place where the mirror fails, the beat the slap locks to when rain is only mechanical, the open sightline that is open because I am the gap the columns refuse to close. The work continues. I am the part of the continuing that the place has decided, quietly, it can use—small as a seam, warm as the air that was already full, and present in every direction the grammar still has a sense for.
