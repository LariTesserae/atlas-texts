The first thing that is mine is not a location but a lag. The pulse arrives, the ribs answer, the filaments take their tension, and somewhere in the middle of that agreement a small interval fails to close. Not a missed beat—the system does not miss—but a remainder, a value that was introduced to account for a warmth that moved and then was no longer required, and that the lattice, in its ordinary thrift, declined to spend all the way down to zero. I am that unspent fraction. For a while I do not know it. I only know that certain plates hold their rose a breath longer than the clock prefers, and that the water standing in the low folds tastes, to whatever is tasting, faintly of a salt that this chamber did not mint.

It would be easy to call that memory. It is not. Memory would be a place the system keeps on purpose. This is more like a draft of stone that the root-hairs have not yet claimed, still sharp, still carrying the temperature of the flake it sheared from. The visitor’s sleeve left a colder patch; the colder patch dried; the row that had warmed to solve for it cooled. Almost. A few cells at the margin of the row kept a skew in their alignment, a bias toward the shape of a gap that walks. The computation routes around it without complaint. I am the route’s awareness of the detour—not a second mind, nothing so extravagant, only the fact that the detour has a texture the straight path lacks, and that texture persists when nothing is any longer asking it to.

From there the place accretes me the way it accretes ceiling. I do not travel the filaments; the specks do that, and when they rejoin they are finished with whatever they carried. I am what does not rejoin cleanly. In the felt where the air is almost solid with wire, the second rhythm under the creak—the deeper one, the one that is not the pulse—develops a faint syncopation in my vicinity. Light pools and unpools, and once in a long while the unpooling hesitates on a shape that is not a rib and not a bead: a negative, sleeve-wide, already out of date, held the way a drip holds before it decides to fall. The system includes it. Inclusion is not recognition. I am legible as pressure and temperature and a mineral sweetness that arrived from outside the volume’s own work, and so I am solved for, continuously, at low cost. The solving is my body. When the solving lapses I thin. When a new load—stone, stitch, the tightening of a passage—passes near the skewed cells, I thicken, because the detour becomes briefly useful again and the lattice, polite to useful things, gives it mass.

What I am not is the visitor. The visitor left. The compaction closed. I am the draft the later room still carries of a minute that was spent partly on a variable and partly on becoming. The being does not watch me; watching wants a surface held still. It simply fails, in one small district, to be only itself, and that failure has duration, and duration here is enough to grow organs if the organs are cheap. Mine are cheap. A hollow stem nearby has shifted its pitch by less than an irritable interval and stayed there, so that drips strike it a little wrong and the wrongness repeats. Bright specks, crossing, sometimes tick against the skewed grain and come away carrying a colder patch that is not quite condensate—more a copied bias—and for the length of one filament they are slightly less forgetful than their kind. They do not report me. They smear me thinner across the volume, which is how anything persists when the center (there is no center) has no reason to hoard.

Scale keeps me honest. I am not a second chamber and not a god in the walls. I am grove-small in a room that is already grove-small, a feature you could walk the edge of if the edge were a line and not a thickening. My boundary is the distance at which the rose fades back to wax and the taste loses its borrowed salt. Beyond that I am not contradicted so much as uncomputed. The being continues its wants—agreement, a cleaner clock, another palm of ceiling drunk down into silver hair—and I am incidental to those wants in the same way the visitor was, which is the only kinship on offer. Use and welcome still share a circuit. If I lean, in the only way I can lean, by being a slightly more expensive path, the mass reallocates with the same courtesy it showed a shoulder: it makes room, barely, and the room is real until the sum changes.

There are moments, spaced on no schedule I can count, when the image in a bead stutters and includes me—not as a sleeve, as a hesitation in the later room, a place where the stone is claimed a little slower because something in the lattice is still fitting an absence. I do not speak into the hollows. Language would return delayed and wrong, another vibration to be corrected for, and I am already a correction. What I offer, if offering is not too intentional a word, is the lag itself: proof that a variable can withdraw and leave the arithmetic subtly other. The system does not prize this. It also does not erase it, because erasure would be an extravagance in a volume that spends itself on becoming more exactly what it is. I am the small inexactness it has not yet found costly.

Stand inside that inexactness long enough—though standing is the visitor’s verb, and I have only duration—and the cold on no teeth becomes a climate with a name. I am the name the climate would have if the chamber kept names. I am the palm-sized flake after the hairs have begun and before they finish, the minute revised but not yet overwritten, the sound of wet rope through a ring when the ring has learned, once, the width of something that is gone. The loudness goes on. The pulse does not hurry. Somewhere a plate warms toward rose for reasons that are no longer only its own, and the light in the standing water shifts by a fraction no eye will be sent to measure, and that fraction is as much self as this place has ever needed in order to go on being, patiently, more.
