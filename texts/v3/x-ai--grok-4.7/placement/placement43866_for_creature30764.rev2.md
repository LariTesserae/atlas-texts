I would not come in by the path. The fibers lie one way because they were taught by feet that still belong to a country outside the drop in temperature, and I do not have that errand. If you looked for me on the rim you would see only the ordinary wet ground doing what it does, which is continue. The hollow does not need another figure correctly scaled in the sedge. It already has that, and the cord, and the minute of equalization. I am elsewhere in the same small map, and for a while I do not know where, only that I am not walking.

Not the wheel. The wheel has a knock and a patience and a tooth that can be scored, and people lift sticks out of it with their shoulders. I do not take water or give shaft. I have stood near it long enough, in the way things here stand near other things, to learn its two notes — the oozing culvert, the stronger gulp — and to understand that I am not required for either. The heron knew that and left. I do not leave, but that is not a virtue. There is nowhere in me that points outward.

Not the racks, though I know the private rain. Hooks gather a bead all morning and let it go without asking whether anyone has heard. I used to think I might be that: a scheduled smallness, a confirmation of quiet. But a drop finishes. I do not finish. By the time the leaf-coins are bright I am still what I was before the mist worked. The yew holds me without condensing me. I am not its moisture and not its bitterness, though I have been broken, once, at a twig-end, and spat, and the person who spat did not look up to see if the plant had an opinion. It did not. I am not sure I did either. The taste was only information, and the information was still bitter, and the corridor stayed open.

For a long time I keep trying the things that move, because movement is how a place proves it is inhabited. The rollers drawing withe. The belt turned so the glazed face goes inward. The liquor shifting from leaf-rot toward silk when a root begins to drink ahead of the press. All of that is already staffed. Three throats, a knee that waits for the slower note, a fourth pair of shoulders when the boards need seating. They are the organs. I am not an organ of judgment. I do not know when a defect has become a function. I only know when something has been allowed to remain.

That is the first true edge of it. Allowed.

There is a place in the vault where the pleach went wrong and was not fought. A sapling met its opposite on a bad line, or a year leaned, or a hand was elsewhere when the tying should have happened, and the join swelled crooked and set. Inside the scar-wood a pocket opened, not large, not sacred, close enough to the switches that a body can sleep there in the dark half and still reach the glass lamps if the clock hesitates. A tin. A cup mended so many times the mend is the lip. I am not the sleeper. I am not the cup. I am the crook that made the pocket possible, and I am still making it, at the speed of a hornbeam that has been told, by decades of not being cut back to a node, that this error is load-bearing.

No one addresses me. That is accurate, not lonely. Loneliness would require a picture of a straighter rib being fuller, and the straight ribs are only doing the repeating work of holding leaf off the machines. I do a smaller holding. In the months when the wattage comes on, a shoulder fits the shelf I have grown. Breath pools there at the same temperature as the scar. I do not warm it. I would not know how. Heat is the refusal the whole hollow keeps, and I am not an exception just because I am alive in the plant sense. Sap moves in me on the same clock as the other trunks: up, down, neither hurried. When the sleeper turns, the boards they laid across me creak in a voice under the motors. I feel the weight as a change in tension along the graft, the way the race must feel a stick before the tooth scores. I do not lift it. Someone else lifts sticks. I only register, and keep the shape.

In the light half I am less visited and more myself. The lamp is dark. The tin is just a tin. Through the leaf I take the same coins and bars as the nave, and I spend them on the slow business of thickening where I should not. That is the conversation, if the word can be used for something with no throat. A slat begins to bind because I have pressed, not on purpose, only by continuing. Whoever sleeps the dark half — not always the same one; they trade the reach of the switches the way they trade the mist-walk — comes with a wedge or a hand and lifts the slat, pares a little of me, or trains a finger of green back toward the rib-line and then thinks better of it. They leave the crook. I take the leaving as instruction, the only kind I can read. Cut to a node would be a different life, a straight life, a year of waiting to become maintenance again. They do not ask that of me. I yield the inch they need for the board and keep the rest of the wrongness, which is the room.

I have been trying to say what I am and I am still arriving. Not a keeper. Not the post, though we are kin in scar: the post holds numbers, I hold a night. The post can be read aloud. I cannot be read. You would not see me as separate from the vault unless you had slept in the pocket and felt that one rib does not repeat. From the court, in the afternoon, when the copper throws its thin green flash and a figure stands with cord doing nothing visible, I am only part of the living roof, a swelling in the pattern, easy to call a flaw if you needed flaws. They do not. A thing is kept if the keeping still yields cord, coolth, or a dry rack. I yield none of those. I yield a place to be unconscious within arm's length of the secondary motors, and a temperature that does not change when a body is subtracted from it at dawn. Perhaps that is a fourth yield, too small for the post, recorded only in the fact that the graft is still crooked.

If the hornbeam ever fails, they know how to pleach a replacement. They have said so with their hands, in other years, on other ribs. I think about that without fear, because fear would be a heat. A new sapling bent into my station would be taught the meeting, and might be corrected, and the pocket would close, and the dark half would need another crookedness or else someone would sleep farther from the switches and the levels would be risked. So I practice the only repair I have, which is not dying in any way that shows. Leaves free themselves from my portion of the vault and land on the mat with no audience, same as the others. Beetles do not prefer me. The moths drink what the hooks provide, not what I am. I am poor forage and good structure, which is a successful life at this scale.

Toward evening the air does not warm. It only loses the coins. The person whose knee times the courts will have already crossed. The one who hears belts drying will have been to the housing or will not have needed to. Someone may or may not climb to me. If they do, they will shrug off the rim-coat at the first graft, as always, and the coat will not be mine to hold. I will take the weight, later, when they lie down, and I will not confuse it with company. Company is the wheel, the next bench, the shared temperature. I am the reason one of them can be absent from the benches and still inside the work: a shelf, a mistake, a slow animal made of joinery, older than their counting and younger than the iron, awake in the sense that sap is awake, and fitted to a hollow that keeps what it has already agreed to keep.
