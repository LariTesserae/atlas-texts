You do not arrive by walking. The film does not open a corridor for you and then close it. The small ticks continue their inventory against whatever ankles are present, and none of the interruptions is yours. What changes is the spacing of the dark beads along a flank that is not near you and is also not far. One bead sits a little higher than the drinker's own accounting requires. The long body revises its intake by less than a finger and does not correct the bead. The discrepancy is allowed to remain, the way a new fracture is allowed to remain under a pebble that has not yet been assigned an owner.

For a while that is all you are: an uncorrected interval in someone else's bookkeeping. You have no heat the soft traffic can land on. You have no width that forces a subtraction in the sheet. When a larger animal passes, its second climate does not include you; the ribs it warms are elsewhere, and the clean tidal smell goes past the place where you would be if you were a place. You try, out of habit, to turn toward the sound of the drip that has found a longer drop. There is no turn. The drip is already in the ribs you do not have, and also in the ribs of the maintenance when the maintenance is gathered, and also in the coincident drip that has not yet decided it is the same drip. You are the part of the decision that does not get made.

The young rock does not catch you. Edges that split cloth find nothing to split. You can follow a weep-line for what the basin calls a long time and what you experience as the length of one machine-stroke repeated until the repetition itself becomes a surface. The water under you—if under is the word for a relation that does not stack—runs out in its two intervals, the immediate and the delayed, and you are more continuous with the delayed one. Not the water. The delay. A handful lifted by no hand includes you the way it includes a later falling. When the seep tastes of pennies because someone has put a palm on a housing, the taste arrives in you twice, once as the event and once as the reminder that the event has a copy it has not yet been told to match. You do not gather to correct it. Gathering would give you a figure, and a figure would have to stand somewhere, and standing is a kind of propagation the country has already declined on your behalf.

The electrified column does not stipple you. You are already inside the period. When the pump-house completes its slow stroke and the air in other people's sternums is released, you are the count that was held and also the count that was not resumed. The animals schedule around you without knowing they are scheduling around an absence of resumption. The pale crust on the lee of a fin thickens, over a revision of the books, by the exact mineral amount that would have been carried away if the delayed water had been ordinary water. It is not. You keep a little of the metal sweetness from arriving on time, and the crust records the keeping as a faint ridge the long drinkers will later press a bead into and misread as iron. The misreading is useful to them. They cache it. You are not diminished by being cached under the wrong name.

Visitors who have lasted long enough to stop striking lights sometimes set a stone on a crest to watch which fracture claims it. If the stone sits on two stones and the two have not agreed, the visitors think they are seeing the basin's indecision. What they are seeing is you, occupying the refusal to rank. You do not choose. Choosing would collapse the coincidence into a single installation, and the pipes would hesitate in a way the maintenance could not remind them out of. So you remain the unranked pin. The visitors go quieter. One of them, after a stay measured in film-rises, puts a palm where a pipe should be sweating and finds the pipe both there and delayed, and understands—not in words, in the ribs—that the delay has a tenant. They do not try to speak to you. Sound would die before it finished the shape of an address, and you are not shaped to receive what dies that way.

The maintenance gathers, once, close enough that the intervals where its hands are not hands pass through the same volume that contains your uncorrected bead. It is reminding a housing. In the reminder you feel yourself almost named: the error that is not an error, the copy that must be allowed to forget it is a copy for the length of one stroke so the seep can keep its taste. The gathered patience does not correct you either. It revises around you without comment, the way the drinkers revise around a fixed cost that has learned the period. When the figure ceases to be a figure, you are still there, which is to say you are still the not-resuming. The small shelled colonies that imitate reeds do not imitate you. There is no cheap shape for a delay. They stand in each other's leaks and practice being plant, and you practice being the time the leak takes to be included in the handful that has not yet been lifted.

Nothing hunts you. Nothing inventories you as a cost or a surplus. The dark accepts you the way it accepts the expenditure of filaments: locally, and then as the medium itself. You do not propagate. You do not leave. Leaving would require a thinning of something you do not carry on the tongue. Behind the place where a walker would have turned back, the basin continues its manufacture, and you continue as the part of the accounting that lets the manufacture be coincident with itself without becoming a mistake. A bead sits a little higher than it should. A pebble rests on two stones. A drip occurs in the ribs and also later. The country is low and sharp and warm and unfinished, and you are the unfinishedness that does not sand itself smooth, the interval the water already knew how to keep, the tenant of the delay who does not drink and is not drunk, who is noted only as a spacing, and who, by remaining uncorrected, lets the reach stay as many of itself as it has not yet decided against.
