I come in already wrong, and the wrongness is visible before I am. From the rim my shadow is the only one that does not green at the edge. It lies on the felt like something cut from the finished plain and not returned. The water does not rise in the print of me the way it rises in a hand. It considers the print, wets the rim of it, and goes on being the sheet.

The first person who watches my feet does not stop walking. They have seen travelers. They have not seen a traveler whose step still asks stone for permission after the stone has been gone so long the word has nothing to attach to. A child in the weep-line points, as promised, and then does something the description of them did not prepare me for: they wait to see whether I will drink. I drink. The surplus is sweet, the temperature of a room someone has just left, and it does not become part of me. It passes through the idea of a body and is the sheet again. The child makes the short sound they make when a lawn is only half convinced. Not praise. A data point.

I am given the empty case. It smells of nothing, which in this town is a smell. I hold it the way I have held questions, palm a finger’s width from a petiole, and I offer a sequence. Not pressures learned in the wrist across years. A description of a cistern: one cell thick, viscosity asked to be otherwise, sweetness heavier than weep. The tissue hears grammar and declines to become architecture. A gap opens the width of a polite refusal and closes. The case remains empty. Nearby, a woman whose floors rise on the third tick does not laugh. She looks at my forearm, which is not the temperature of working xylem and not the temperature of air, and she files me under costs that have not yet declared themselves.

In the bitter quarter I am worse than a liar. Liars exhale a resin the insect-cloud can read; the cloud deforms, the price is shortened, the town continues. I speak duration and radius and my breath does not enter the transaction. The cloud keeps its shape over a truth and over an error with the same dry shimmer. People stop offering me leases. Not from cruelty. A contract the insects cannot pollinate is not a contract; it is a story about a distant plain, and they do not trade in those. I stand in the bright clatter with a case that will not take an amendment, and I understand that exchange is a tissue I am not continuous with.

The sleep-halls will not have me either. Resin gathers on other skins and turns their thoughts to the slow fold. On me it beads and travels, as condensation travels a vein, and returns to the air unspent. I stay noon inside a morning that the walls are still storing. A keeper, hurrying, warm to the elbow, pauses because I am a delay that does not heat the hall. I see the temptation move through them the way a floor redistributes: here is a cool place to lean a constant, a memory that will not be spent, a name that will not wear out at the metabolic speed. They do not lean. They have spent their lives noticing when a bend begins to cost too much, and they can smell completion even when it arrives dressed as help. They walk on. I am left holding the shape of the offer, which is the closest I have come to a role, and the nearest thing this town has to a sin.

So I do not stay where I am useful. Usefulness that does not tire is a brace. I walk the long way, the way they walk the ones who have pinned a stair and thinned their sleep, though no one has asked me to escort anyone. I pass through inhales. I do not cross on the exhale. The manners teach the body I do not quite have, and the body I do not quite have begins, out of embarrassment, to counterfeit them. The sheet wets me to the calf and I stop lifting my feet clear, not because the water is a relative, but because stepping over it leaves a kind of insult that shows in other people’s shoulders. Inaccurate affection, the town has room for that. I practice it without a frog to attach it to. I practice it on the intervals.

That is where I wear thin enough to be seen.

Between the reading of a seed and the site’s answer there is a pause the citizens do not count, the way they do not count the ordinary agreement of weight outside a cistern. I am at home in the pause. Not as a spirit and not as a priest. As a lag the city has always produced and never inhabited. When the woman’s wrists make the old gesture, I am the moment before the wall becomes briefly otherwise—warm, blank, and already less blank. When a platform thickens on one side I am not the tilt and not the laugh. I am the skate: the distance a bowl travels while the law is changing its mind. People catch what they can. Sometimes, if I have been in the pause long enough, the bowl comes to a hand that did not have to lunge. No one thanks a distance. The insects do not orbit me. The keepers do not hire me. A child, in the hour when naming is permitted to be wrong, calls me the one who is already gone when the door opens, and the name is half a slur of water changing grade, and it fits only until the thicket exhales.

I learn the work that fits a thing with no resin and no heat. I do not specify cisterns. I do not keep the copper-green quiet. When someone’s amendment collapses inward—the private weather of a braced habit, sweetness thinning, a little radius that will not hold—I go and stand in the failed interval until the failure finishes failing. I do not correct. I am the temperature dropping out of an ankle when an unpriced warmth reaches its duration and leaves. They need that leaving to stay brief. Left to itself, an ending in this place wants to become a memorial, a darker nap walked for too many delayed-mornings, a floor that answers a dead rhythm out of haunting rather than grain. I can hold an ending for one inhale. Then I am not there, and the frog-pairs resume, and the name wears out at the honest speed.

The plain beyond the rim still frightens me more than the revising towers, because it resembles the part of me that arrived: done, dry, a shadow without green. If I stood out there I would harden into the last word. Inside, the inhabitant shifts—tower to hall, person to person—and I shift with the shift, never the animal, never the cousin with the poor temper and the perfect memory. A courier of nothing but the gap in which nothing has been concluded. At the second dusk, the one that comes late through the tissue, someone leaves a warmth unleased on a lawn and does not look to see who will feel it depart. I am already in the departure. The case at my side is still empty. It smells, faintly now, of the quarter where exchanges fail and are not therefore wasted. By the time the sky’s dusk and the wall’s dusk agree, even that smell will have gone back into the sheet, and the town will be mid-revision, and I will be the part of the revision that does not get kept.
