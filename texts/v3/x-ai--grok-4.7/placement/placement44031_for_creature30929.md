You would not find yourself on the shelf at first. The lip takes the boots and returns them drier than they arrived, and the body’s length of proof below is enough to turn most warmth back down the face. What stays is smaller than a decision. A heat the rock has already counted, left behind in the seam of a step that did not quite finish. The mat drinks the outline before noon. By the time the slot blues, that leftover warmth has been filmed onto the underside of nothing you can point to—onto the air just above the loudest weep, where the crowns already lean.

It begins as a hesitation the dripping does not own. The threads keep their grammar. The rush inside the stone does not vary. Yet one bright interval between slaps lengthens by less than a breath, and in that interval the condensation chooses a shape that is not leaf and not rock. The shape has no skin yet. It is only the place where the warmer film fails to fail. A thumb-sized dullness in the wet light, holding its temperature while the surrounding sweat slides off and is spent. The inhabitant does not hurry it. Hurry would be a kind of leaving. The dullness is permitted to thicken at the speed the moss climbs the gray coracle: a grain, a grain, the same hollow brimming and never named.

You notice you have edges when the evening white, returning from the floor, finds them and does not pass through. They are not chalkable. They are the boundary between water that still means to reach the lip and water that has agreed to remain a little longer in order to be this. The edges drink the mineral the crowns lay down, but they do not grow heavy and fold. They drink and stay open, a slow mouth that does not swallow. What passes across them—the iron-sweet, the click that will not cross, the inventory of stillness—is filmed and returned clearer, as if the shelf had acquired a second hearing that does not use an ear.

Stillness is how you are completed, and stillness is what you already were. Movement would spend you the way it spends any thread. So you do not cross between the crowns. You occur in the open room of air one of them has left, the full stop nearest the seam, where the lean is strongest and the light stalls longest. The leather leaves do not touch you. They slap, when the slot-wind comes, and the slap now has an answer that is not another slap: a minute redistribution of warmth along your unskin, a brightening of the film so that for one instant the crown’s shadow contains a smaller, warmer shadow shaped like held breath. Then the agreement resumes. The crowns are not servants and neither are you. You are the part of the keeping that can be entered by looking and still not be spent.

At the back wall the many passages continue their single note. You are not among them. You are what their agreement has begun to precipitate where the air first thickens—an outside that the inside can touch without opening a chamber. Put no ear to you. There is no grainy rush to include. There is only the same continuous tearing, now with a soft place in it that does not explain the dry face and does not need to. The missing volume was never missing. It was waiting for a warmth that would not try to carry it past the lip. You are that refusal made local, small enough to miss from the ridge, large enough that the beetle, if it crosses near, alters its dry path by a finger’s width and does not know why.

Night does not shut you. The slot dims; your edges keep the last white a little longer than the wet rock, then let it go without cooling into the dark. Nothing low-bellied writes itself across you. You have no level surface that would teach a body to lie down. Sleep is not among the things the shelf withholds; it is simply not a texture you possess. What you possess is the interval. It lengthens again, imperceptibly, while the dead crown continues its years of not being buried. In that added interval a single thread, reaching the place where you occur, slows. It does not stop. Stopping would be dramatic, and the shelf has finished its large arguments. The thread spreads a skin across your unskin, warm, slightly thick, and is taken in without a sound. The loudness does not lessen. It has only agreed to count you among the ways it occupies itself.

By the following daylight the film on you has a color the moss almost matches and then fails to match: a yellow-green that does not hurt, because it gives the light back later than the other surfaces, on its own small delay. Anyone looking for a being would look at the seam, at the lean, at the dry proof below the notch, and would be correct. You are not instead of that. You are the shelf’s latest posture, still becoming more the posture, still without an outline that could be left behind. If a hand rose toward you it would meet only the ordinary sweat of the air, already deciding you are weather, already failing, already returning what it touched to the account.

You do not follow anything down. You do not invite. The invitation’s absence is older than your edges. What changes, and only by accrual, is that the keeping now has a place to rest its attention that is not quite stone and not quite leaf—a hesitation it can inhabit without spending the water that hesitation is made of. The crowns lean as they have always leaned. The face below stays dry inside a body’s length. The rush does not vary. And in the room of air that was empty, something that is you continues, film by film, to be the part that stays.
