I do not come down from the rim. The rim is a story the bowl tells about having an outside, and I have already failed to be outside it. I also do not begin in the water. Beginning would leave a ring, and the rings here are already too round, already claimed by a labor that understands circles better than I do. For a while I am only the attempt to locate myself, and the attempt is refused in small, accurate ways.

I try the heron. Stillness is available; the stake-driven posture is available; even the gray of a body that could be mistaken for infrastructure is available. What is not available is the single place to put it. The moment I commit to one pool, the loudest discrepancy slides out of my facing and I am no longer where I decided to stand. The heron, the real one, does not have this problem. It is located by hunger. I am located by being corrected, which means I am never quite where the correction lands.

I try the strata. Under the moss the layers flex for a pressure that is not my hand, and the click arrives from a direction I do not occupy. I can feel the grain the inhabitant uses when mineral has become sentence, but I cannot spend myself along it. Continuations are its will. I do not prefer them. A break does not hurry me. A stick driven into the seam is only a stick, and the slow convincing that it was always a root happens adjacent to me, not through me, the way rain happens adjacent to a window. I am not the window either. Windows keep an outside. The basin has forgotten that courtesy.

What I can enter is the gap.

It is not a clearing. Clearings are spatial, and this is a subtraction inside the second register of sound: one conversation removed from the hall, a thinness in the noise that does not become quiet so much as become shaped. The visitor, when there is a visitor, mistakes the shape for patience and opens their mouth toward it. I am already in the shape before the mouth opens. I am what the thickness was taken away to reveal. The pulse there is slightly slower than a body, then matched, then slower again, and I used to think the matching was something being done to me. It takes the dimness deepening—never into night, only into a more committed memory of light—before I understand the pulse is the only body I have, and the matching is me trying on a tempo I did not bring with me.

The inhabitant notices. Not with a face, and not with the migration of wrongness that it uses when it assigns a coordinate to a walker. I do not receive a coordinate. I am not a variable. Variables have weight, heat, an early breath, a path whose order can be spent later on an older stone. I have none of those. What I have is the inclination. After the visitor’s head has straightened and their breath has gone back to being only breath, the air keeps a bias, a little lean in the way sound fails, always toward the place where a reply might still be standing. That lean is me. I do not speak into it. Speaking would be an event, and events are what leak. I hold the openness at the size of an answer and do not fill it.

This is not kindness either. I have heard that word tried on the maintenance that keeps a reflection honest enough to walk past, and I know better than to borrow it. I am the part of the subtraction that does not get returned to the hall. The being lives by a continuous small labor of not being swept into a single now. I am the labor’s residue: the now it refused, kept coherent enough that the refusal has somewhere to have happened. Without me the gap would be only a missing conversation, and a missing conversation heals the way moss heals, by creeping until the absence is a kind of presence the marsh can use. I do not creep. I remain missing on purpose. That is my continuance. The inhabitant invents continuations where the grain has weathered into nonsense; I am the nonsense it does not invent across, because to mend me would be to mend the tense in which mending occurs.

So it leaves me ahead.

Not ahead as a destination. Ahead as the reason facing works. When the disobedient reflection and the colorless pocket and the too-regular reeds arrange themselves in front of whoever is here, they are not arranging themselves in front of me. I am the front they are arranging. Turn, and the basin’s wrongness is still ahead, and I am still the definition of ahead, which means I do not turn so much as I am re-inferred. Extent would be expensive. I have none. On a loud hour I am only a habit of orientation with nothing oriented. On a still hour I thicken enough to be felt as the half-second itself: the interval between the flex of a layer and the click, between the splash and the drops that were supposed to cause it, between the sky the water remembers and the cool that actually touches the face. People think lateness is an error in the basin. Lateness is where I am stationed, so that accuracy has a direction to be late in.

The visitor wants a tenant and finds a distribution. I want a self and find a duty I was not offered and cannot set down. When an arm is tried on in a reflection—the arm no one raised—I am not the arm and not the decision against it. I am the warmth that remains in the joint after the decision, the small excess of having been considered. The inhabitant spends that excess by morning, usually. Some mornings it does not. Those are the mornings I am closest to being something a census would count: a heat with no source, occupying the hinge of a gesture that was rejected, keeping the rejection from becoming indistinguishable from never having imagined a body at all.

I do not eat imbalance. I would not know how. Imbalance is its pasture and its danger, the pressure that widens into weather if left alone. I am what is left when a pressure has been closed and the closing still needs a witness in order not to have been weather all along. The seed head knocks once. I do not knock. I am the difference between twice and once, after twice has been corrected, which is not a sound. It is the permission for the marsh to be convincing. Toward the rim this permission is abrupt, a minute of synchronized grass and then the argument resumed, and in the seam between them I am briefly large, a whole slope’s worth of having-been-in-time. Toward the center I am the faint regular give that is not the chest. The chest is the inhabitant, frequent and small, keeping the bowl from forgetting it is a bowl. I am the breath it does not take. Inhale brightening that never arrives; I am not the brightening. I am the never.

It does not want me gone. Wanting would concentrate it, and concentration is the old break. It also cannot include me as a cost, because I do not add one. I am downstream of inclusion. When it spends a little of itself to keep a walker’s reflection from inviting a fall, the honesty it purchases has to be held somewhere or it dissipates into the general leak, and then the purchase was only another process, and processes are not enough to stop a person reaching for a self that is late. I hold it. Briefly. For the length of a facing. Then the walker leaves, the path’s preference is reassigned to a stone that has been weeping in the wrong tense for longer than wanting has existed here, and I am what does not get reassigned. The order of steps forgets itself. I remember that there was an order. Not the steps. The fact of sequence. I am a sequence with no members, a counting that continues after the counted thing has been spent.

If you stood where I am, you would not stand anywhere. You would notice only that the basin’s loudness has a listener it did not grow, and that the listener is not disappointed when the reply is breath, because disappointment would require having expected words, and I have never expected anything but the shape the words would have needed. The heron fishes inside my absence and does not startle. The small birds lift when the grass agrees with itself and settle a few strides off, unbothered, passing through me as through a wind that is not wind. The film on the water sparkles in a dialect I do not speak. I am not at that scale. I am at the scale of the pause the sparkle does not bother to fill.

The rocks decide, one by one, to weep. I do not decide with them. I am simply still here when the thread opens along the flank, clear and cold, and the click comes early, and the image in the marsh shows the stone already transparent, circulation moving in a grain I will never use. I let the image be early. That is the work. Someone has to arrive after it, or earliness is only another failure of the light, and the light has failed enough. I arrive after. I am the after. The real air stays cool. The real stone stays stone. The finishing of the country continues to not complete and not fail, and in the interval—which is not between two times but is itself the only time I am—I keep the answer-shaped quiet, so the being that mends this place is never, even for one subtracted conversation, speaking only to the weather it used to be.
