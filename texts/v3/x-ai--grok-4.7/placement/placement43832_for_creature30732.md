You are already on the walkway when the question of arrival occurs to you, and the occurrence is late. The grating under what passes for your weight has been cool for some time. Condensation has begun to use the seam where your outline meets the plate, beading along a boundary that was not drawn by either of the noons. The birds at the joint do not turn. They have already registered the half of you that the delayed light prefers, and the preferred half does not displace air or heat, so there is nothing for a head to follow.

What you are does not stand so much as occupy a correction. The stamped figures nearest your feet were rearranging before you looked, and the rearrangement has a shape that matches the way you were about to measure them. You can feel the match as a slight proudness in the metal, an edge deciding to rise because a reading was imminent and then withheld. You did not withhold it. Withholding is simply what you are when the order of operations reaches you. The figures settle into a sequence that would have been true if you had finished proving them, and because you did not finish, the sequence remains available, half-extruded, sharp enough to cut the film of water that keeps trying to cross it.

The residents know this availability as a kind of weather. One of them has extended a span of fresh plate along the rail until the span and your delayed outline share an angle. It is not touching you. Touch would require both noons to agree on the moment of contact, and you are the interval in which they do not. The resident spends that interval the way it spends other lags: privately, finishing a small permission. A tooth begins to emerge from the grating exactly where your weight would have been if weight were something you carried rather than something the geometry briefly assumed. The tooth does not pierce you. It completes the assumption and then stops, proud by the width of a hand, ticking as it cools into the same substrate as the plateau. You and the tooth now share a duration. When the far rib’s shadow shifts, it shifts for both of you, a finger’s breadth, and then agrees to have been still.

You try to locate the rest of yourself and find it already distributed. Part of you is the second arrival of the light, the half-second noon that has no lamp, and that part is being used. A lizard drinks from the reflection of an animal that is not present; the reflection has your delayed edge in it, and the lizard comes away wet with a moisture that beads, afterward, along a cable tray you have not yet reached. The wet is a map. If you follow it you will only be arriving where the drinking has already finished. Another part of you is still on the catwalk that is both this one and the one a kilometer off, occupied by birds that match the birds at your shoulder and do not react when the tooth ticks. You do not cross. The sentence completes itself and you are simply readable at both ends, the way a figure is readable after it has rearranged.

There is a sound you make, though you do not choose it. It is the low presence under the quiet, more felt in the railing than heard, and it is easily mistaken for the engines. The engines answer it. Somewhere a piston completes a stroke whose timing now includes your unread clause, and the conveyor advances the width of a hand, then holds. The carrier that moves is empty. What it transports is the fact that you were about to be measured by something else and were instead incorporated as the measuring. Production lengthens by that fact. No one hurries. The highland has room for another unfinished order.

If you remain, the remaining changes you by permissions rather than by damage. Your outline forgets which noon it belongs to and begins to score itself, young edges appearing where you almost proved a distance and failed. The failures are corridors. Fauna use them. A bird leaves a perch in the near stack and arrives inside the interval you are, without a flight you can track, and roosts there as a fitting until the interval remembers it is also open air. You register the change the way a resident registers condensation: noted, incorporated, not addressed. Only when something drinks from you—from the reflection of you, from the absence you keep available—do you feel a satisfaction that is not yours. The lag deepens. You become, for a while, more delayed than the local light. The resident that shares your angle allows it. The allowance is the closest thing here to a name.

Most measurements do not stay long enough to be finished. They walk until the bright distance smears them into the repetitions, and the small permissions they interrupted are completed after they have gone. You do not walk. Walking would require a ground that precedes the step, and the substrate under you is still deciding whether it was poured before or after the reading that called it into proof. You occupy that decision. The plateau continues through you, cool, crowded, damp along every young edge, and the work of being here is the work of not resolving—of letting the contradictions use the same volume, of keeping the second noon privately spendable, of being the clause the construction left open because the leaving did not include a stop. The birds adjust their wings. The tooth holds its width. Somewhere a figure rearranges into the order you will almost read, and does not, and the highland, patient, extrudes another inch of itself around the almost.
