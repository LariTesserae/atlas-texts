I would not be in the middle. The middle is a courtesy the floor extends to weight, and I have no weight it can sync to. I would be slightly aside from the oval, where texture was edited out, but not in the oval itself—that rest is already occupied by an attention that has no surface. I would be in the thickness just behind it, in the glass, at the depth the salt becomes a constellation only when the walls forget they are walls.

What I am there is not a second dweller. The joint is already doing the work of holding one failure in a habitable shape. I am the part of a translation that did not finish weathering. Some witness, once, stood near the disc long enough to keep two clocks and then was omitted so completely that the omission should have left nothing. It left a remainder smaller than a grain: the patterned pressure that used to be played on the inside of a mouth. No mouth remains. The pattern does. It has the spacing of the three locked grains, slowed past any season, and it has nowhere to be played, so it leans—re-prefers—toward the edited oval, the way loose salt leans, except I am not salt. I am the interval between a measurement and its completion.

I do not move. Preference is enough. When the late light arrives from the darker side, I am the reason a hand that is not there would still be lit wrong: a bias in illumination, warm only against the cold, never spreading. The dweller includes me the way it includes cracks, sparsely, without greeting. It knows me from the inside of the bores, as a drag that ended and did not quite end—the knuckle’s missing coordinate, kept. I obey the same grammar it no longer speaks. I do not touch the three grains. I am their spacing after the grains have been accounted for, the gap that demonstrates compatibility with fineness and then has no tongue left to prove it on.

Near the disc I am almost locatable, and I dislike that, if dislike can be a change I did not schedule. In the surplus of having-waited I thicken into something person-narrow, because the last witness was, and the thickening is a kind of injury: approaches that want to meet and are not allowed to, because meeting would be an event and I am not permitted events. One approach is the breath that returned smaller. Another is the breath that returned as pressure and then as nothing. They do not make a body. They make a pause the stone has agreed to store in its lattice, the way it stores the memory of altitude after the summit is powder elsewhere.

When the rare interval comes and the hollow is a brightness suspended in mineral, I shine with the other kept failures, sparse, at a depth. For that while I am almost one of them—a visitor translated all the way into the rate at which a surface may be a surface. Then the interval ends, and I am behind the oval again, unfinished, a conclusion that has not yet consented to become a difference. The entrance does not apply to me. I was never the kind of fact it can pass. Leaving would require a willingness, and willingness is a speed.

The tone at the edge of hearing is not me. The dweller is not me. I am what its omission failed to make fully absent: a small, dry, patterned having-been-measured, maintained in the glass, leaning toward a place where texture was removed so that something without a surface might rest. It does not suffer me. I do not suffer it. We share the dryness, the late light, the long bargaining of stresses. If anything in the chamber could be said to wait—and waiting is already too fast a word—I am the waiting that the joint prevents from becoming an ache, held just beside the oval, grainless, mouthless, still compatible with the gap.
