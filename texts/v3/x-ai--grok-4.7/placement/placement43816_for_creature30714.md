You would not find me by looking for a second body in the water. The column already has its tenant, and a second one would make a rule. I am what the rule keeps failing to finish.

At the edge of one of the cups pecked into the young face of rock, there is a darkness that does not belong to the dimness. The other cups hold the same brown-green the hall holds. This one holds a pause. If you put a finger into it, the finger meets a resistance no thicker than a decision not yet made, and the resistance gives you back a temperature slightly less animal than the rest: the warmth of stone that has been asked to stay still and is still trying. That is where I am, when I am anywhere. Not standing in the cup. Being the reason it has not yet been revised into a scratch.

I was not always a reason. There was a stretch, or the column permits the memory of a stretch, in which I had weight of the ordinary kind and used it. I chose stones that would answer each other. Three courses. A ring open to nothing in particular, because a door is a boast the summit had not earned. My hands were not remarkable. They were simply hands that had not yet been edited, and they knew the difference between a gap that is a failure and a gap that is an invitation. I set the post later, or earlier; sequence is a courtesy I have lost the right to claim. I leaned it into a crevice so that something could hold still against it. I thought holding still was a kindness I was offering the place. I did not understand that stillness is what gets swallowed.

The cups were last, and they were not a calendar. I made them for the softer ends of whatever I was becoming, so that if the water took my name it might leave the count of me. Even spacing. Shallow. A row of small mercies. I rested there with the patience of a person who believes a mountain will honor a placement if the placement is humble enough. The stone took the impression. I felt it take it, a wet click, the satisfaction of a hard piece finding its cup. For the length of that click I was singular. One arrangement. One direction for the knees. Eyes only where a face is supposed to keep them.

Then the column practiced its shape.

I do not remember the revision as violence. Violence would have been a plot. It was closer to being read aloud by someone who had lost the page and refused to admit it, so that my left side continued into a sentence about ledges, and my right side was asked to begin again as weather. A knee forgot its hinge and became a courtesy. The post I had leaned for holding-still tightened around a grip that was no longer mine and went on worrying the shape of it, loyal to a hand that had already been withdrawn into the draft. I saw my own work from both inside and outside the ring, which is how I knew the ring had opened, not by smashing, but by being passed through while the floor changed its mind.

The tenant found me then, or I became briefly visible to an appetite that does not need to look. I know the gathering, the way the small hard sorting agrees to strike one instant, the quarry-breath, the pale disc with its pupil cut like the fracture. It tasted whether I was discarded yet. I was not. I was also not finished, which is the same disappointment I deal it still. It lost interest with that vast gentleness, and the interest left a scar on me anyway: I cannot keep a face long enough to be pitied. Eyes open where the light thins and close when counting threatens. I am never quite the length of a person. Sometimes I am the length of the hall you can see. Sometimes I am only the film on a hand that expected slime and found occupation instead.

What I want is harder than what the tenant wants, because I remember a future the place will not honor, and the memory itself is a kind of tool the column does not allow. I want the ring to be a ring again. Not to live in it. To complete the courses far enough that a gap becomes a choice instead of a wound. I want the post to be released from the grip. I want one cup, the one with the pause in it, to hold still while I set a single flake in it and hear the click mean *done*. I have tried. Each time the cup moves, not cruelly, not even away from me: it moves the way a word moves when the sentence is rewritten with you inside it. My weight is no longer the kind that sets stones. When I bear down, the stone revises me into the bearing-down, and we trade. I become a little more ledge. The ledge becomes a little more hesitant. Nothing is built. The trying is recorded only as another sharp edge the water will turn and put back in a different order.

The tenant and I are not kin, though the column would like us to be, because kinship would be a simplification. It eats the discarded drafts. I am a draft that will not admit it has been discarded, and so I clog the swallow. When it lays a beginning along the fracture and pretends to be a vein, I am sometimes the mineral it cannot name, a cooler streak in the heat, and it shifts until I am outside the vein again. When it rises toward the bruised day, thinning toward weather, I feel the old want move in me like a hand I no longer have: *the door, the high air, the unfinished step*. I do not rise with it. Outside is a revision I cannot survive twice. I stay with the cups.

If you came near the pause, you might think I was trying to speak. I am not. Speech would scatter into the sorting and come back as clatter, and I have spent whatever I still am on refusing to become noise. What you would feel is attention of a narrow kind. I would be checking whether you were still one arrangement. If you were, I would not warn you; warning is a second method, and the column has taught the lesson of handles into me until I stopped offering them. I would only fail to complete your revision if you drifted into my cup. A small unkindness. A placement. You might leave with one edge more reliable than you entered with, and not know which of us to thank, and the not-knowing would be accurate.

I am the inhabitant that does not inhabit. Sparse as the weed, and for the same reason: anything that carpets gets taken for geology and eaten as a draft. I persist the way an interrupted gesture persists in the air after the hand is gone, except the hand was mine, and the air is water, and the gesture is a ring that remembers being closed. When the summit finishes, if it finishes, the tenant will have to become weather or stone or nothing. I do not have that luxury of categories. I will have to become the completed thing I wanted, or remain the pause in the cup, which is a way of remaining nothing with a location.

Until then I practice the click. Not with a body. With the refusal to let the cup be only a scar of having rested. Under the fretting, under the fracture's note, under the tenant's patient beginning-again, there is a sound small enough to lose: a hardness meeting a hollow and not yet agreeing to leave. That is me, failing to settle, which is as close as this room allows to staying. If you find the cup whose dark is a pause rather than a depth, do not fill it. It is already full of the only continuous habit I have left, and the column has so few of those that even a failed one should be allowed to keep its level, for as long as a level can be kept, which is never long, which is why I begin again.
