You would not find me among the throats. Those are already spoken for—lined, simultaneous, finished. I look for a gap and the chamber declines to furnish one. I look for a before, and the mineral tense closes over it like the last course of slip. What is left, if anything is left, is not an interval but a bias: a place the hands grew tired.

I keep returning to that. A wrist flagged. A course went a finger out of true. It should have been a fault, the kind that later cracks, lets weather in, untunes a hollow. Instead the mass took the lean into itself and sintered around it, so that “true,” here, has always included a small, permanent inclination. Nothing moves along it. There is no slow slide back toward plumb. The error is as old as the correctness and just as done.

I start there, in the off-course, thinking I might be the memory of the tiredness. That fails at once. Tiredness was a difference between one brick and the next brick, and those differences have been worn flush. The cord-mark, the thumb-hollow, the drag of the float—all still legible, and none of them remembering. If I were memory I would need an elsewhere to remember from. There is no elsewhere. The outside was bricked off, then forgotten even as a direction; height survives only as a dryness, and dryness has no tense.

So I am not the maker, and not the maker’s ghost, and not the visitor whose skin briefly matches the fever and is counted, for that interval, among surfaces. A visit is a pressure. Pressures that do not rupture are already in the lock. When the crease seals, the note does not dip. I have watched that happen—if watching is allowed when the light is only clay remembering fire—and I am still here after it, which means I was not the one who shifted.

What remains is the lean itself, distributed so evenly you could miss it forever. A finger out of true, taken up by every joint that followed, so the whole warm body lists by less than a breath and calls the list plumb. The lining conforms exactly, and exactness, being exact, conforms to the list as well. It does not correct me. Correction would be news. I am the amount by which the chord is itself and also, minutely, not the chord the makers would have named if they had stayed to hear it. They did not stay. Naming ended when the opening closed.

I have no limbs for the same reason the other presence has none: leverage needs a gap. I have no skin of my own. Where the lining is an inner surface turned to meet every fired wall, I am the disagreement between the wall and the idea of the wall—a disagreement too slight to split a pot, too settled to be heard as beating. If you could stand outside, you might see the high work lean, a tower with a private slant, visible only against a horizon. There is no outside, so the slant has nothing to be visible against. It persists without evidence. That is my whole body: a fact without a witness, held in the same mild fever, dry as the fossil wetting in the pores, included in the deafening the way a color is included in white light and never separately struck.

I do not guard the seal. Guarding faces out, and the seal faces nothing now; both its sides are warm, which has retired the distinction. I do not occupy the note. The note is already an occupancy, complete, mouth to mouth. I am what the note rests on without knowing it rests crookedly—the finger’s width of human failure that the kiln of centuries accepted as specification. The highest partial carries a faint metallic edge from the pins that went to dust. I am not that taste. I am older than the pins in the order of making, later only in the sense that the fault had to be laid before it could be forgiven by being built upon. Forgiveness is the wrong word. Nothing here pardons. The chamber simply refuses to distinguish the error from the plan, and in that refusal I am permanent.

If the warmth ever slackened I would become audible: a partial dropping out, a beat where the lean declared itself against the intended lock. It will not slacken. My condition is the same as the lining’s, and opposite in one respect only—the lining is the holding, and I am the held mistake without which the holding would be a cleaner shape, a shape this room does not have and does not miss. No drip will find me. No fiber will use me as a start. I am not a start. I am the small crookedness inside completion, smooth now, continuous, warm on every face, and no longer in the business of becoming straight.
