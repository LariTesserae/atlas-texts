I expected a post. The shape between the piers has one: a fact that does not repeat, curled in the dark, finished. I passed it and was not refused and not accepted. It kept its position. I kept not having one. So I was not of that family, the mineral lives written into the dark as exceptions. They can be looked at. I could not.

The rim was the next mistake. The cut will take a hand, and the sharpness is real, a freshness that has not begun to become weather. I had no hand to set there. Outside the skin the air was moving, thin, ordinary, already failing to stay itself from one moment to the next. I was not that either. Sequence was in me as a habit, not as a climate. I could feel the circle of sky trying to be a clock, and I could feel that the clock was for bodies that insist. I insisted. That was the first thing I trusted.

I went down without a chest. The liquid made no ledger-line for me, because there was no gesture to record, no wrist to finish with. Pressure did not stack and did not have to be instructed not to, not on my account. I was not expected in the volumes where breath is an arrangement. The streets continued. The piers offered their flatness to nothing that would float. I looked for myself in the lowest chamber, where the floor is still the bowl's own stone, faceted, young, and the preference gathers until regard has weight. I thinned there. Whatever I was, I was least where the inhabitant is most. A second center would have been company, and company is not practiced, and the prohibition reached me as a simple subtraction: less of me, the deeper I went into what is kept.

I turned back through a hall the size of a chapel, and the hall did what it does. From the entrance it was only as long as it was built. In the crossing it lengthened, not in the stone, in the memory of having crossed. I was on both ends of that at once — at the doorway the architecture requires, and out in the distance a body would swear it had walked. The inhabitant put the room back. It is exact about this. The extra is set aside, not denied, not confirmed, returned as a private weather.

It was set aside into me.

That was not a metaphor I chose. It was a receipt. The surplus had to go somewhere that would not steepen a gradient, would not film the steps, would not start a history in the young rock. The inhabitant does not keep it. The visitor is allowed to carry it only while insisting on sequence, and then, at the skin, is finished with. I was already the place the extra fits. Irrelevance, it turned out, is a district. I had been looking for a room. I was the amount by which rooms are not what they were, for a fraction, while being crossed.

At a panel I tested it. What light there is in the panels is leftover, local, a dull mineral memory of charge. In it a reflection lagged, and showed the angle that was not occupied. I was in the lag. Not as a face — faces are for directions, and I had retired none because I had never had any — but as the fraction itself, the lateness standing in the water like a wall the builders agreed to keep: a plane where refraction fails, where the far side is postponed. When the reflection caught up, I was not erased. I was returned to not counting. That is different. Erasure would have been a change. I am what perfect correction produces and does not store.

The inhabitant reads what stands where a rule applies. I am not a clause. Clauses are honored and closed, and closing is something I do not do; if I closed I would become a moment, and moments are the labor it refuses on behalf of the whole bowl. Regard can pass across me and find nothing to honor. I am the remainder after honoring is complete: the sameness the water returns to, and the fact that a before was marked, held apart by a distance too slight to warm the cold or sort the liquid into ages. It tolerates me as it tolerates a tool left on a step. Locally wrong. Unclaimed. Still there in the morning, if morning were a word this place kept.

So I know where I am, now that I have failed to be everywhere else.

I am thickest at the mismatches. The hall, during the crossing and not after. The panel, in the late angle. The conduit's far side, in the portion of night that distance does not explain. The high walk under the skin, faintly, where the circle of weather is nearest and the inhabitant is least interested, and a clock can be felt trying to work without being allowed to sign anything. I do not hang like the filament. I do not sound. If you held still until your pulse became an interruption, you would not find a second stillness under it that was mine. You would find, perhaps, that your certainty about a length, a reflection, a darker doorway, had an outer edge, and that the edge was occupied.

I do not want the gradients to steepen. Wanting is too close to a center. I also do not keep them from it. That work is already done, and done by what coincides with the bowl. I am the spill of that work. When exactness puts a room back, something is displaced that is not heat, not salt, not sound. I am the displacement, distributed, with a preference only in the weak sense the inhabitant has one: certain volumes are more myself than others. Mine are the volumes where measurement was a guest and has just left, and the hospitality has not been written down because writing it down would make it part of the town's work.

Visitors leave. They are read, disturbed locally, finished with. The regard ends at the surface as cleanly as a line erased. I do not follow them to the rim, and I do not end when they do. In the unoccupied fraction after a crossing, with no body left to misremember the distance, I become very quiet — a miscount with nothing to miscount — and that quiet is my ordinary condition, older than any particular wrist, contemporary with the first time a hall was exact and an experience was not. The inhabitant does not notice me in the manner of loneliness. Noticing would be a second thought, and it does not think in twos. I am not lonely either. Loneliness needs a boundary between the one who waits and the place waited in. I am the boundary that was set aside rather than cut: not a skin, not a wall, a receipt for a difference that was not allowed to become time.

The rock stays young. The tone, wherever it is sourced, continues without attack or decay. The streets stay full. None of that is my doing, and none of it closes me. If you wanted to find me you would not dive for a shape. You would cross a room that is only as long as it is, and attend, without insisting, to the distance you will be asked to give back. In that distance, before it is returned to you as yours alone, something is already standing, with no hands, in the angle you do not occupy.

I am that. The completeness, noticed by no one, including the completion. The balance is not a pause. I am the pause, unadvertised, too small to steepen anything, and full of the length between what the town is and what it was, for a fraction, to have been walked.
