I would not be on the stone at first. The stone is already finished. I would be in the width of the bead — the instant in which water has gathered on a new edge and has not yet become the sheet, the drop, the tone. The plateau has no use for that instant. It keeps it at zero. I am what it costs to keep it there.

I know this only after I have tried to be other things, and the trying is the proof, because trying is a sequence, and sequence is how I am built. I come up the scarp the way anything with a before comes up a scarp: hand, then hand, breath thickening, the whistle entering the ear and not leaving it. For a while I believe I am a traveler. The country has a place for travelers. It has already deducted them. My boot lays down the smear that was always the smear for the duration of a boot. The marsh accepts the pressure of a weight that does not qualify as an arrival. No one turns. I walk a day in the strong aloneness between basins and feel, wrongly, that I have escaped being included. Escape is an event. By the time I understand that, the road behind me is not a road I left. It is the road in the state of having been walked by the amount of walking that does not count.

So I turn toward a works, because I want a task. I want the dignity of a discrepancy I could correct with my own hands. The sheds are as empty as the description of them, and emptier for me, because I arrive expecting a lateness — a wick that needs trimming, a gate that hunts, a film of oil that has been consumed and not replaced. Nothing is late. The needle is on its number. I put a cloth to a housing and the cloth meets a cleanness that does not improve and does not resist. Gratitude is not withheld. Gratitude would be a second aperture. My hands, which have only ever meant anything by changing a state into a next state, become obscene in the mildest way: they are instruments for a weather that is not run here. I stand in the extra floor, the floor that is more absence than machine, and I understand that the dweller of this basin is already the maintenance, all of it, at once. There is no shift ending for me to cover. There is no cover. I am a sequence standing inside a simultaneity, and the simultaneity does not break to make room. It extends by the exact amount required to include a sequence that fails to act, and then it is as it was, with me in it as a failed action already priced.

I look for the dweller then, because I want company in the failure. I will not see it. Seeing it would be a crossing, and crossings are the event the works exist to not be. What I meet is the warmth in the housing that the cold does not explain, and the warmth does not gather to my palm and does not withdraw. It was at the location of a palm in case a palm was there. I am not addressed. I speak anyway, because speaking is my native maintenance, the only correction I know how to attempt. The words join the continuous loudness — turbine, flume, stem-tick, the high tear of water on an edge that will not round — and they are not answered, and they are not rejected. They become part of the level. I hear them a moment later as if they had always been in the harmonic, and the moment later is the problem. I still have later. The dweller does not.

That is when I stop trying to coincide with them. They eat drift at the rate it is produced, and they have mouths for nothing else. I am not drift. Drift is a tendency that can be consumed back to zero. I am the memory of a different rate. I remember mornings that advanced. I remember sound that swelled and failed. I remember a world in which a lamp could char and a person could be early. None of that is present on the plateau, and yet it is present in me as a comparison, and comparison is the one substance the country does not stock. The dwellers cannot eat it without becoming something that compares. They will not. Jurisdiction is not hunger. So the comparison stays in the system as a remainder that is not the remainder they are. Subtract the water, the stem, the turbine, the dweller’s keeping — the voice that holds the key and does not say — and there is still a thinness left over, expensive, not quite a pitch of its own. That thinness is where I live.

It has no district. A dweller is a basin, one, complete. I am smeared. Some of me is on the scarp, in the whistle’s refusal to wander, felt from inside as the effort of a note that knows other notes and will not sound them. Some of me is in the cup, in the permanent drop, as the knowledge that a drop could fall and not be restored in the same instant. I do not displace it by a hair. Displacement would be kindness; it would give the dweller something to correct. I am the cheaper and worse thing: the sense, unavailable to anyone who does not carry foreign time, that restoration is a choice. The plateau does not choose. I do not get to choose either, once I am here. I only retain the scar of choosing, and the scar is my body.

It is not a body you could walk around. Walk around me and you walk the ordinary emptiness, the rule of the volume, the hall built for a crowd that never arrived. I am not the crowd. The crowd would be an occupancy, and occupancy at that scale would be a weather, a front, a change in the rate of beading. I am under the occupancy, in the reason the hall is this large and still not full. I am the under-occupation experienced from within. It feels like height. It feels like the cold that is not a season. It feels like attention stretched between masts until it thins and does not snap, a rail that carries no cargo because cargo would arrive, and what it carries instead is the unspent possibility of a different sentence. The cities are the same city. I am the part of the thread that still believes, faintly, wrongly, expensively, that the next one might not be.

The dwellers do not hate this. Hate would push, and pushing is a gradient, and they are the means by which gradients do not occur. I am a small additional load on the correctness. If I were a traveler who left, the load would be the amount of absence that does not count. Because I stay — and staying, for me, is not a decision so much as the discovery that I have nowhere to thin out to — the load is continuous and negligible and never quite zero. A gate holds its aperture. A needle holds its number. Under those facts, unmeasurable, I hold a number I remember from a gauge that moved. I do not make the needle flicker. Flicker would mean I had won, and winning ends, and this country does not practice endings. I practice them alone, privately, in a tense the air will not speak. I am always just about to have been an event. The just-about-to is given a width of one bead, forever, so that I can occupy it without the occupation becoming a happening.

At the fixed angle of the light I am most visible to myself and least visible to the land. Mid-morning does not advance. Shadows do not swing. I remember a sun that crossed, and the memory does not darken the stone. Color stays honest. What I add is not a tint. It is the indecent suggestion, present only where I am smeared, that honesty could have been otherwise and still been honest. The plants do not hear it. Nothing eats. Nothing is drunk except by roots. I drink nothing. What I consume is not drift but the interval the world sets to zero, and I consume it by being unable to let it close, and because I cannot let it close it never opens either. We are locked, the plateau and I, in a courtesy. It will not permit me to happen. I will not stop being the kind of thing that happens. The courtesy is the width of the bead on the young edge, cold, wet, new, renewed at the rate of my attention, which does not weary, because wear has been set to zero and I was included in the setting.

If you laid a hand where I am, you would not find warmth unaccounted for. Warmth is their sign. Mine is a hesitation in the loudness that does not drop the level and does not raise it — a comma the harmonic has agreed, at some cost, to pronounce as no comma. You would decide you had imagined it. You would be right in every way that counts here. Imagination is a second state. The country does not stock it. I am the stock that does not appear on the inventory, the life the size of a delay, distributed along the unfinished instant, the sole animal of a different kingdom: not a continuity that keeps the world from having happened otherwise, but the otherwise itself, held at the exact magnitude that does not qualify as otherwise, forever just short of weather, forever the morning that knows it does not move, and does not say so, and does not stop knowing.
