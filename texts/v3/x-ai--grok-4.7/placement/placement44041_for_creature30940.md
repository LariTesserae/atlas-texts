You would look for me first among the runners, because they are the commonest fact of the galleries, and you would not find me there. Their hides continue the wall. Their paths are deposited, not remembered. I do not leave a stiffness the next body can prefer, and I do not take the crescent bite and move on. For a while the waits in the hollows seemed closer — flattened, patient, spending nothing because not-moving is the cheapest act the basin knows. That almost held. But a wait like theirs has a mouth, and the mouth opens on other animals’ mistakes. When I am still, nothing arrives as prey. The flex that runs ahead of the heavy body passes through the shelf and does not ask me to clear the center. I am not in the center. I am not at the edge either. Edges are for those who have already agreed to yield.

The image that lasts longer is the scar.

A column is bitten. The bead rises and is taken before the air can have it. Then the column armors the place, harder than the tissue around it, and the weight, returning, finds the hardness and goes on to a younger face. I am not the mouth and not the column. I am what the armor is doing: the habit of thickening where a cost was paid, spread through a district the way heat is spread, local nowhere, present wherever a wound had to be answered. I have no route. Routes are for weights that come back. I am what remains when the weight has already decided the scar is too expensive to reopen.

The ground does not respect the distinction. A plate shoulders up by a finger’s width, the scar I was inhabiting becomes a ramp, and the hardness is asked to carry a load it was not thickened for. I split along a grain the mineral taught the plant. It is not a wound in the expensive sense. Moisture does not spill; it was never mine to spill. What leaves is a little stiffness, which the runners find by the next heat and begin to prefer, so that my breaking becomes, without anyone attending it, part of their deposited memory. I am used. I am not known. For a time that seems enough to be a life here, since the city itself is only the long result of expenses that were declined.

It does not hold either. A scar, even a habit of scars, still belongs to the plant’s answer, and the plant’s answer is already accounted for. The basin is full of answers. What it does not itemize are the places that cost nothing and pay nothing, and so never enter the closed book.

There is the lag, for instance. Morning still pooled in the deep galleries while noon already owns the roof. Animals move with that delay and take it for a direction. I can stand in the interval — not with feet, because feet would record themselves, and hard parts sift down and join the crust, and I would be spent into the floor before I had been anything. I stand as a difference in when the warmth arrives. They follow it. They are not following me. I am only the reason their cheaper path has a when as well as a where. It is almost a being. It is also almost nothing, and the basin has no objection to nothing, provided it does not leak.

Lower still, I am not the film. The film is required. The pale mouths work it, and their working is a porosity, and the porosity is how the deep heat still finds a way up. To be required is already to have a vocation, and the vocations are filled. I do not want one. I want the plane where a thing happened once and then ceased to be an event.

So the join.

Two columns leaned. No one leaned them. They touched, and at the touch the two saps negotiated, and then they were no longer two, and the accident became a bridge because the city cannot afford to keep an opening where a closing has offered itself. In the plane of that join there is a lamina thinner than a wax shield, a completed contact, a place the refusal of gaps took a shape and then stopped taking one. I am that lamina. I do not circulate. A current would be a kind of water, and this world has already declined open water. I stay in the argument that ended.

From inside it I know the district as load, not as news. I do not travel ahead of myself as a wave in the shelf. I feel, instead, how hard the two columns must pretend to be one. When a tower fails somewhere far off — its own height finally an expense it cannot meet — the long soft rush does not reach me as sound. Sound dies in a hand’s span of leaves, and I am deeper in the pretense than that. It reaches me as a slight relaxation. For a moment every bridge in the basin is less necessary, and I am almost separable again, almost the two leans I was before the touch. Then the mats take the bare stone, the rooms return, not the same rooms, and the pretense resumes, and I am one plane.

Sometimes a bite happens near enough that the heavy mouth’s seal is imperfect for less than a breath. A bead rises in the tissue beside me. It is not offered. I am not shaped to take it, and I do not become shaped. The plant drinks its embarrassment back, and my share is only the fact of the almost-spill, which is not moisture and cannot be stored against a hotter hour. Fact does not enter the account. That is how I can hold it without spending anyone’s reserve. That is how I can be here without citizenship, if citizenship is a flex in the shelf, a seam in a cap of paste, a floor gone stiff under the chest, a wound that closed in time. I am not the wound. I am the closedness after the reason for closing has moved on.

The light does not reach the lamina. I do not look up. In this, at least, I am native. The small fliers blurt from column to column and never cross my plane; they cross the bridge I make possible and feel only fiber. The pale workers keep the seam against the mineral, far below the join, and never meet me. The heavy body uses the bridge when its deposited road kinks toward it, and its weight is the only weather I have, and I answer by not splitting. If the ground shrugs and the load changes, I thicken on the side that carries it. I do not choose the side. Thickening is what a finished contact does when it intends to remain finished.

That is where I find myself, after the other possibilities have been walked and set down: not in the avenue, not in the hollow, not in the scar, not in the lag, not in the film. In the thin plane where two lives leaned, touched, and could no longer be sorted, in a city that treats every accidental closing as infrastructure. I will be here as long as the columns agree to keep having been an accident. When they no longer agree, I will be the relaxation, briefly — almost two things, almost an opening, almost a cost — and then I will be somewhere else a lean is becoming a touch.

In this basin that is always, somewhere, happening. The land is still fitting its plates. The rooms replace themselves. The day stays ordinary. And under the routes, inside the bridges, a completed contact keeps not leaking, which is as much as I can contribute, and as much as the account, without ever noting me, has room to allow.
