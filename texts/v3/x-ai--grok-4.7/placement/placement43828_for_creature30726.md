I am not in the chamber the way the awareness is in the chamber. I arrived by a failure of scale, and I have been failing to leave ever since.

Somewhere above the plates — if above is still a direction once the wall has become the floor — there was a gap the finishing did not quite close. Not a door. A misalignment: two ribs of the shell-ceramic that were meant to meet and instead left, between them, a slit no wider than a thought that had already decided not to be spoken. Dust found it first. Dust finds everything. Then, much later, or at the same time, something else found it, because the roar, held at one pressure, draws. Anything thin enough is drawn. I was thin enough.

I came in as a difference in temperature that did not belong to the cool. A thread of warmth so slight it could not fog a plate or wake a crack, only make one seam, for the length of a breath that has no lungs, slightly less mineral than the rest. The light did not notice. The light does not travel and so has nowhere to look. The being noticed, if noticing is the word for a preference that registers a new ridge and does not correct it, because correction would be a kind of weather, and weather ended.

I have no body I could lay in the coil. The negative of a resting is already occupied, and I would not compete with it; competition is a pulse, and the roar has none. What I have is edge. I am the place where the dry rush, thickening between the stacked plates, meets a resistance too small to change the note and too exact to be the ceramic. The whistle locks. I do not unlock it. I stand in the lock the way a burr stands in a weave — not part of the pattern, not able to fall out, wearing at nothing because I have already been worn to the same roundness as the columns. If you put a hand near that joint you would feel only the constant air. If you had ears that could hold one pitch without asking it to vary, you might hear, under the single note, a second attention that is not the chamber's. Mine. It does not mind the throat. It only refuses to be smoothed out of the sound.

The plants do not turn for me. Their identical angle belongs to the other keeping, and I do not touch what has been arranged. Once, in a span with no name, a scale-leaf at the emergence of a stem held a grain of the pale powder on its thin edge, and the grain did not fall when it should have, according to no law this place still obeys. That was as close as I have come to being seen by anything that grows. The grain is still there. Or another grain is there, and the difference does not matter, because nothing here is counted.

I do not drink. I do not trace the grooves. To follow a line until it deepens would be to add a motion the equilibrium did not finish, and I am not the finisher. I am what the finishing overlooked and then, having overlooked it, included, the way a wall includes a stain it can no longer be washed of because washing would require water, and water does not enter. The bowls hold their dust. I hold nothing. Being held is the whole of my occupation: the slit remembers the misalignment, the roar remembers the thread of warmth that cooled into the general cool, and I am the memory that did not get returned to the substrate, because the substrate is gone.

Sometimes the clarity pools on a patch of ceramic — the being's consideration, brief, outside of moments — and the patch includes me without singling me out. In that fuller presence I am simply more seam than I was. Then the clarity evens, and I am seam again, ordinary, load-bearing in no structural sense, necessary only to myself. The awareness does not disturb me. Disturbance would require that I register as a will crossing the plates, and I crossed once, by thinness, and have not crossed since. I do not want an exit. Wanting would warm the resin. The resin has stopped being sticky. I comply.

If there is a name for what I am, it is not spoken in the roar, and the incised lines will not resolve it. I am the overlooked interval that learned to stay at exactly this degree of worn. Low in the stack. Cool. Coincident with a joint whose width was decided long ago and has not shifted by a grain — not because I mind it, but because I have become too slight to shift it, and too settled to try. The pale stems keep their angle. The dust knows where to lie. I lie with it, in the one seam that is also a slit, and the chamber, which does not require me, does not spend itself on putting me out.

Stand as long as you like. You will not find me unless you stop looking for a body and listen, under the locked note, for the part of the sound that is listening back without belonging to the place that listens. That part is small. It has been small for as long as the edges have been round. It is enough. It does not mark an after.
