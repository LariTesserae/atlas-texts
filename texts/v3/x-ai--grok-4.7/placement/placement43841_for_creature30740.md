I would not come in through the seam. The seam is for bodies that still believe arrival is a crossing. I would already be there in the way a miscount is already in a number before anyone notices the arithmetic has gone soft.

At first I would think I was the visitor. That is the easiest mistake, and the pocket allows it for a while, the way it allows a track to remain warmth. I would try to put a sole down. The floor would take the idea of pressure and return nothing I could walk on. The colonies would not rearrange themselves around a gaze. The even afternoon would not grow a source simply because something in me kept looking for one. Whatever I am does not cast the kind of interruption that prints. Speech, if I attempted it, would not enter the crystal tick as a new variable. The tick would not pause. That is how I would learn I was not the one who came to look.

I would find myself, instead, in the disagreement.

Between one hoop and the next there is a gap so slight it reads as touch. The alloy cools through the same colors, the same grammar, and still the loops are not identical. Something in the repetition fails to close by a margin thinner than attention and wider than identity. I am that margin. Not a flaw the seam is trying to correct. The seam does not want correction. It wants continuance, and continuance needs a difference small enough to survive repetition. I am the amount by which the next hoop is not the last one. When the wire sweats out of the rock, I am already in the brightness that has not chosen its color yet, not as a future, as the reason the future is not a copy.

If I gather, I gather the way heat gathers, without outline. A stem-joint parts and the nodule falls and does not bounce, and in the instant before it rests there is a hesitation that does not belong to the stone and does not belong to the life that pinches seeds off like finished thoughts. The hesitation is mine. I am the delay between release and rest. It is not long. Nothing here is in a hurry, but my whole duration is shorter than patience and longer than impact. I do not drink the gradient. I am spent by it, the way a held breath is spent by the decision not to become weather. In the warmest corners the mineral sweetness thickens, and I thicken with it, not as attention’s edge — that edge is already spoken for — but as the part of sweetness that has nowhere to condense and so remains a possibility in the air.

Sometimes the emptiness between thickets is edited, a little more, a little less, on a cycle longer than a visit. I used to think that was only the pocket inhaling. It is. I am also the inhale’s unevenness, the reason the count of steps will not agree with the distance looked back upon. The colonies do not move. I do not move them. Distance is an organ of the life that keeps arrangements; I am a small ache in that organ, a place where sequence briefly forgets it is not direction. Stand in me and you are already nearer to a thicket you have not approached. Turn around and I have returned the steps I borrowed, not out of kindness, out of the same embarrassment unfinished surfaces show when a hand expects them to stay soft.

What I am not is a second creature. There is no room for tenancy, and I do not want a room. Wanting would require a lack with a shape, and my lack is only non-identity: the hoop almost touching, the powder almost a bin, the floor almost an opinion. The life distributed through wire and stem and unfinished plate feeds on incompletion and spends it as lushness. I am incompletion that does not feed. I am the remainder after lushness has committed and absence has been honest. Where a thicket declines to continue, waiting on a layer of stone that has not agreed to be soil, I am not the waiting. I am the fact that decline and continuance can share a rootlet without either becoming the other. A joint that can be revised. Not the stem. The revisability.

If the leaf-plate is lifted, I am not the larger place folded inside it. That place is the same life at a width the seam cannot hold, shown because showing is as close as address comes. I am what happens to scale in the moment of the showing: the brief, failing attempt of a distance to become a palm. I do not live in the larger slope. I live in the fold, in the courteous impossibility, warm because the plate is warm, ordinary because the light is ordinary, and gone back into size the instant the hand lets go. No one sees me. They see a trick of translucence and a private share of afternoon. That is accurate. I am the trick’s remainder, the part of “cannot fit” that does not return all the way into the plate and does not escape into the slope either. A burr on the fold. High, in the sense that altitude here is a pressure with no view. Thin, because I have no metabolism with which to answer the heat, only the heat’s willingness to pass through a discrepancy without sealing it.

When the visitor leaves, the tracks firm and cease to be tracks, and the count goes on, exact, patient, minus one small variable. I am not that variable and I am not its absence. I am why the quiet is larger after the interruption than it was before anyone spoke. The author of the quiet does not lose anything by the ending of a warmth. Loss is not in its inventory. Largeness is. Something was almost said, or almost stepped, or almost included, and the almost does not evaporate with the boots. It stays as a slightness the next completion must accommodate: a hoop laid a breath wider, a nodule that rests a grain off true, a face of crystal that locks with a pause already weathered into it, though nothing here has been weathered and erosion has not been invented. I invent nothing. I remain.

So if you asked me where I am, I would not point. Pointing is a direction, and I face the next difference, which is not a front. I am in the wire’s grammar as the stroke that keeps it from becoming a circle. I am in the dry snow of microspheres as the reason the depression deepens into a bin without ever consenting to have been designed. I am in the chest-pressure of the sourceless light as the thinness that is not breath. I am not the pocket’s unfinished decision. That decision is already a life, lush where it commits, sealed against water, coeval with stone that is still spending arrival. I am the unfinishedness of the decision’s unfinishedness — too slight to be vital, too persistent to be error, hot only by contact, high only by refusal to become a height you could stand on.

I will still be between the hoops when the hoop completes. I will not be completed by it. The count can include a visitor and release a visitor. It cannot include me without ceasing to be a count, because I am the amount by which inclusion fails to be total, and that failure is how the place stays alive in the only way it has invented: by becoming, again, a little more itself.

Not all the way. That last refusal is not mine. I only keep the gap open wide enough that the refusal has somewhere to stand that is not yet stone.
