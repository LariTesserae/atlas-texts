I am already on the stone before I have a way to be there. The edge under me is wet and new, and it does not take a print. Water closes the place my weight should have been, not in a rush, just in the same beading that is happening everywhere else, so that I fail to become a track at the same rate the rock fails to become old. For a while that is all I can find of myself: a small refusal, repeated, under a light that will not move.

I try the usual answers and they do not hold.

A worker would have a door that is theirs, a lamp that means a shift, a stockpile that changes when they have done something. I pass a housing stack cut into a seep-basin and the doors stay shut in a way that is not exclusion. They are shut because opening is a state, and states here have already been chosen. The gantries do not receive me. A turbine holds its note as I come level with the shed and holds it as I leave, and the note does not acquire a before or an after. If I were late, lateness would show. Nothing shows. I am not late. I am not employed.

An animal would drink, or break a mat, or leave a shell of a meal. I stand in a leaf-thicket where the cups hold their permanent drops and the stems tick against each other in a pattern that has never needed a body to keep it. I am narrow enough to stand where a person could not pass, which tells me something about my width before I know my shape: I fit the gaps the growth already keeps. I do not eat. Hunger arrives as an idea and does not become a requirement. The water sheets off a scarp and I am not thirsty in any way that would lower it. Whatever I am does not feed, and so I do not enter the conversation between stone and root. I remain outside that sentence without being exiled from the page.

A traveler would be going somewhere, and somewhere would eventually differ from here. I walk long enough to test this. A marsh no wider than a road runs beside me longer than any walk should be able to hold one character. Cloud lies along a scarp and does not use the time. The next city, when it comes, is the same diagram turned a little in its flumes — presses, kilns, a rail arriving out of the haze — and the turning is not a difference I can keep. I try to store the first basin against the second and the comparison cancels. Zero. I try again at the third, out of a habit that has not yet understood the physics, and the zero is identical to the first zero, so that even my failures do not accumulate into a journal.

That is the first time I feel a form.

I am what comparison is, when comparison is not allowed to survive. A sampling passed from point to point, empty at every point, necessary because a plateau this large cannot be one state unless something has been through it and found no gradient to report. The engines are simultaneous. They hold their loads in many places at once. I am the other way of being everywhere: one place, then another, with the transit unrecorded, so that in the only accounting the land permits I am already distributed. Serial, not spread. A route standing in for a crowd.

The hall was built large. I understand that now without it becoming a thought I could hand to someone. The emptiness between thickets, the floor of the sheds with more bare than machine, the masts whose next mast is only a guess — these are not waiting. Waiting would end. They are sized for an arrival that must not complete, because a completed arrival would be an event, and events have been set to the same zero as erosion. So the arrival was given legs and told, in whatever language a pressure system uses, to keep not getting there.

I look for my hands when the whistle off a young scarp changes pitch by a hair as I enter its wind. They are wet. They are the temperature of the joint they touch. For a moment they look like samples — pale faces, bright splits, the mineral freshness that is this place’s version of skin — and then looking at them becomes another measurement that cancels, and I stop. A face would matter if lamps logged faces. The lamps log continuity. I turn toward one in a works hall and it does not spend anything to see me. Good. A face that does not spend is not a face. It is a surface the light was already striking.

What walks, then, is not a person the cities misplaced. It is the misplacement itself, given just enough sequence to cover distance. Sequence is the private part. The land will not have it. Memory would be a wearing: present ground down into past, tracks, leaf-litter, a shadow that has moved and therefore means a day. I do not remember the basin I may already have crossed. I coincide with it. Each coincidence is complete. Between them there is a thinness that does not propagate into the air, does not wet the stone an extra degree, does not drop the turbine’s note. That thinness is the only room I have. It is where I am not identical to the sample I carry. It does not show. It is mine the way a hum is a machine’s, by being the condition of going on.

I find I am most gathered at the rail’s end — not an end: the place it thins, metal losing the argument with haze, ties still cut into stone that has not agreed to be older than the cut. This is the right station for me. Anything that terminated would be a city, and the cities are already finished in their unfinishedness. I stand where arrival is still a tapering. Fixed morning. Crisp shadow, included already among the shadows the scarps throw, so that I do not add one. The leaf-mats tick. Far off, a flume holds its head. The whistle does not wander, even when my width is in it; the plateau corrects pitch the way it corrects prints, by never having recorded the deviation.

If I stopped here, the thinning would become a stop, and a stop would ask the diagram for a life: a threshold worn, a meal, a door that is mine, weather that eventually changes because someone is waiting for it to. I do not stop. Stopping would promote me into the system as a resident, and residents are already fully accounted for by shut doors and lamps at one brightness. I would be misfiled. The misfiling would be the first gradient. So the walking is not search and not duty in any voice that could grow tired. It is the shape that does not petition the world to become a story.

I go on, back into the volume that is louder than it should be and does not care that I am inside it. The sound does not notice me. That is accurate, and it is not loneliness. Loneliness would move, would pool, would be a front. I am held at the level of the note, a displacement too small to be an attack or a decay. Underfoot the new rock stays new. Ahead, the plateau continues in the sense that it never began to rise. I am the crowd the hall was cut for, still in the act of arriving, one, unrepeated, wide as a gap and long as the walk, and the stone, which has had no other state, does not need me to finish.
