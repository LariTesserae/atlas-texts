You notice the sound before you notice that you are the one who has been making it.

It is not a voice. Voices do not travel here; the fiber takes them and returns only pressure, a change in the way the air sits against a rib. What you have been doing, for longer than the last oculus opened and closed, is a small dry click, repeated, the sound a beetle makes when it tests a surface and finds it already known. You have been testing. The path under you is a cord of root worn glossy by other feet, and each time your weight settles the cord answers with a faint give, and you answer the give with the click, as if confirming that the give is still the same give it was yesterday. You do not remember deciding to do this. The decision is older than the part of you that could have refused it.

The cup is the next thing. It is a leaf, doubled and pinned with a thorn, and it holds water that has already been rain twice since dawn, if dawn is the word for the hour when the coin of sky is widest. The water smells of iron and of the clean work the glittering things do in the film. You are carrying it toward a joint that has begun to prefer the wrong direction. You know this the way you know the count of your own steps: not as a thought you could set down in charcoal, but as a tightening along one side of the body when you pass a certain weeping. The riser there has slowed. Three sleeps more and the gallery beyond it will drink from the wrong end, and the lawn that keeps a herd will go bitter, and the herd will sidle farther than is safe, and the thing in the column will have to commute through a room that has not agreed to be crossed. You do not want that. The want is not dramatic. It sits in the hands that keep the cup level, and in the feet that take the longer cord even though the short one is still, for now, a path.

When you kneel, the floor begins its usual forgetting. The print of the knee deepens, drinks, starts to lose the particular shape of you. You pour carefully, not all at once, because a riser that has grown sullen will refuse a flood and take only what it can lift without choking. The water crawls upward along the bundle. You watch it the way the flocks watch a change in roost, without credit. If the riser accepts the gift, the joint will weep true again by the time the fog lifts and falls once. If it does not, you will have to bring chewed cane as well, and stay long enough that the span remembers the weight of someone who is not bleeding and not abandoning a body. You have done this before. The before is in the skin of your knee, where the fiber has risen and subsided so many times that the skin itself has learned the texture of being slightly, temporarily, part of the ledger.

You are not large. A heavy print lasts too long, and lasting is how the hungers read a room. Your hands are the hands that have always carried the cup: pinch strong, nails short, the pad of the thumb ridged where thorns and fire-hardened points have asked the same question of it for longer than faces stay in memory. You do not meet others often. When you do, it is at a cache, in the close dark, and what passes between you is the press of a knuckle and the shared knowledge of which oculus closes from the west. You do not travel together. Together is a vibration. The herds pay their price in silence; you pay yours by remaining a local event.

Still, as the water climbs and the joint considers you, something in the span answers with a preference that is not quite tropism. The footing under your other knee firms. A fiber that had been lifting to close over the pour-site subsides. It is not thanks. It is the continuance noticing that you are already, slightly, inside it, and that your continuance and its continuance have not yet diverged. You click once, soft, the beetle-sound, confirming the give. The cup is empty. You fold it smaller so it will not catch on the lashings, and you stand before the floor has finished forgetting the second knee.

Ahead the gallery bends in a way the cord-map will have to be corrected for, if anyone still carries charcoal to this fold. The air is warmer where the water has agreed to rise. Somewhere above, a flock resettles a perch that has not gone short. You do not look up. Looking up is for the moment the coin slides across the film and the glittering resolves into the lives that keep the iron-smell clean. Your work is lower, and nearer, and it does not need an audience. The path takes your weight. You answer it. The room, which is the body, allows the small dry sound of you to continue a little farther into the vastness, one honest joint at a time.
