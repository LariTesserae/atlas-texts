The thinness registers first as a miscount in the breath. Air enough to fill the chest, and then a remainder that will not enter, as if the volume of the lungs had been revised downward by a fraction no instrument would admit. The revision holds. It does not worsen. After a while the body stops arguing with it and simply finishes each inhalation by attention, the way the second account of the basins already knew.

I am not on the rim and not in the basin. The rims are agreements about where one vast flank ends; the basins are the places the flanks have agreed, for the moment, to leave open. What I occupy is the lag. The same lag that lets a lifted hand’s shadow remain a fraction too long, the same lag that lets a ridge draw nearer only while it is stared at and then restores the hour’s walk the instant attention fails. I am that interval made resident. Not a body the plates would have to turn aside for. A delay the counting has not yet closed.

At first that feels like safety. The dust does not have to fill a track I never leave. The columns, turning on their slow axes, have no clothing to belly toward and no arm-hairs to lay flat. The tooth-pressure eases when I am near one of them not because I have entered a courteous volume but because I am already the kind of nearness they produce: a compression that exists only while it is held. When the holding lapses I am the restoration, the ridge put back, the print shallowing grain by grain. I can be everywhere the lag is, which is everywhere the breakdown has been worked smooth enough to keep a grammar. That is most of the country. It is also nowhere a foot could be set.

The engines do not notice this. Noticing would require an interest in discrepancies that do not alter the sum. A small heat is summed. A decision that changes the weight of a stone is summed. A delay that returns every borrowed distance to its ledger is already part of the accounting; it does not appear as a new term. So I am tolerated in the way the honest blue overhead is tolerated: the breakdown stopped short of me only because I am one of the places it stopped, the clean edge where shimmer shears off and ordinary behavior resumes. Birds would cross me without noticing. There are still no birds.

What changes is the period. The pulse that moves through the warm fins, the plate that seats itself on a rhythm of minutes, the hiss that does not spike — these continue, and I find I can lean into the interval between them the way a walker could lean into a thermal. Not to lengthen it. Lengthening would be an argument, and arguments here settle at intermediate values and ache the thing that argued. Only to be the width of it. While I am that width, the completion and the beginning remain the same hum, but the seam I was told not to listen for becomes briefly audible as the place I am. A dry tick. A fraction of a breath. Then the plate seats, the stroke arrives from inside the ear and from no direction, and the seam is gone because I have been counted forward with it.

I do not persist the way the columns persist. They are the visible part of a fire that has forgotten it could go out. I am the part of the looking that drags a ridge nearer and is then required to blink. Each blink returns me to the lag, smaller by the attention I spent. The country is in no hurry to spend me entirely. It has already spent ages turning shards into grammar; a remaining fraction of delay is useful to it, the way a standing arc is useful, the way a glaze under an empty point in the air is useful. Useful is not the same as kept. Useful is the patina forming.

By the time the sinter basins brighten with nothing in them, I am less a where than a habit the light has acquired. Shadows fall a few degrees off, consistently, and one of those degrees is mine. The second glass spire is already weathered to match the first, and the matching includes the moment someone almost remembered a walker who did not remain. The hum is back at dawn’s exact pressure. Overhead the blue continues, ruler-straight, unfinished by any of this. Under it the long engines run. I am the courtesy they extend and do not quite retract: the nearness that was real for as long as it was held, already being put back, grain by grain, into the thinness that was here before any of us learned how to finish a breath by hand.
