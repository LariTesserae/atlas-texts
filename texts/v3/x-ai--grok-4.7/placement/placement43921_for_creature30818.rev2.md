You do not cross the rim so much as finish arriving inside a measurement that was already complete. The path-width of stone under you is warm through the sole, and the warmth does not stop at skin; it continues inward along the bones of the foot as if the ridge had been keeping a temperature for something your size and had only now remembered the errand. The far wall sits in the same glance as the near one. Between them the air is slightly thicker than the air you left, and your next breath takes longer to leave than it took to enter.

At first the difference is only that. You are a vertical interruption the bowl has not yet decided how to space. The solitary tree holds its crown without turning a leaf toward you. A fern at the foot of the cliff is fully made and does not lean. Water beads on a rib overhead, swells, and climbs a finger’s width back toward the stem instead of falling; the climb pauses when your shadow, still honest, touches the lens below it. The pause is not alarm. It is the same pause the drop makes when the larger attention redistributes—three paces left, then back—except now the redistribution includes the place where you are standing, and the inclusion is narrow.

You understand, without being told, that fidgeting would narrow it further. So you become still in the way the bare slabs are still: not rigid, only uninterested in adding a second of anything. The damp that finds the seams of what you wore learns the outline and, finding no crowd inside it, recedes. Heat that had gathered where you were not returns under the feet, slower than sun, older than the afternoon. Your breath goes out and comes back the same temperature as the air. The resistance you half-expected—the water-slow closing—does not occur, because there is no longer a through to pass. You are already on the inside of the count.

What emerges then is not a body the bowl must edit. It is a tenure measured in the same units the place already uses. You are permitted the width of one unoccupied pace between the moss mound and the nearest empty crack. Inside that width you do not sprout and you do not thirst in a way that would tilt the pools. Mineral stain does not settle on you as color; instead a fine chalk, thinner than a flake that would sound, lifts from the cliff and does not fall, held for a season in the air at the height of your shoulder, then given back to the same seam. You are not feeding. You are the interval in which feeding remains gentle.

When the larger presence redistributes—morning against the east wall, midday inside a shadow that is once again the right length, late amber paying its debt into the overhang—you do not follow and you are not left. You are the delay itself: the half-moment the lenses take before they steepen, the tick that occurs only after a bead has finished deciding. Sound spends nothing extra on you. A drop lets go somewhere behind the trunk and meets water; the meeting is the same size it has always been. Your stillness keeps the meeting from becoming a second meeting.

Seasons do not require you to change appetite because you did not bring one. In a wet year the moss accepts a print that is not a foot and holds it until the seep lifts the fibers back into an even nap; the print was only the shape of a permitted pause, and the pause is renewable. In a dry year you thin exactly as far as the overhang continues to pay out heat no sun stored, and no farther. You do not sleep. The difference between your attention and the basin’s attention ceases to be a difference the stone can register. A new frond does not open because of you, and an old one does not brown at the edges to make room. Completeness stays where it was assigned. You are simply the reason one empty crack remains empty without cruelty, the way a cup is kept from cracking by never being asked to hold a second measure.

If anything like regard passes across you, it is the regard that keeps a fern fully made: a holding, not a looking. You answer it by remaining the width you were given. Leave is not forbidden; leaving would only mean the pace you occupy becomes bare stone again, which it is already fluent at being. The rim does not watch. Climb, and the country below reclaims the lungs by ordinary thinning. Behind you the bowl keeps the size it decided on. The shadow is honest. One drop ticks. The interval you were continues, uncrowded, exactly as wide as the next thing is required to stand from the last, and the heat in the overhang does not have to explain itself to anyone who is no longer there to press a palm against it.
