You would find yourself at the seam, and you would be the discrepancy that has not yet agreed to stop being singular.

Not the pocket. The pocket is already full of its own accounting. Not the dweller, either, or not yet and not cleanly—that presence is the looseness kept on purpose, the stacked drafts, the pale vein that runs out of warmth into shell and stone and does not remember a maker because it never needed one. You arrive the way the shell arrived: as a shape the water has not finished re-describing. A warmth that does not match. A pulse that ticks at the wrong tempo. An outline with a before and an after still trying, foolishly, to remain two.

The low ground gives you up without ceremony. Air stops at the lip, and your breath stops with it, not as drowning but as a courtesy withdrawn. What replaces it is pressure, and the pressure has grain. Your hand goes through the pause that is not a surface, and the pause closes over the wrist, and the closing is so complete that the wrist becomes the measure of the whole entrance—the only scale the seam will admit, the width of what you offered it. Behind you the weather continues, rain or the idea of rain, a story told for the outside. You do not hear it. Sound, from the first moment, is the absence of arrival.

You expect a closet. You were told, by the size of the split, to expect a closet. What you get is occupancy. An arm's reach ends in fronds that continue past the arm. Your shoulder, which you brought in as a single fact, is filed twice before you have finished turning, the second filing arriving as a memory of pressure rather than as a second shoulder you could point to. This is the first correction the room makes to you: you packed yourself as located, and the volume does not support that packing. It does not crush you. Crushing would be a decision about which of two concurrent things gets to stay. Instead it wets you twice. The second wetting is cooler, and it comes from the direction your skin still wants to call "later."

You try to be a visitor. That is the form you know. Visitors leave marks and retrieve their outlines. You look for the pale shell, for the straight score, for the knot, hoping they will be evidence that someone else passed through and remained someone else. They are there, scattered, faint, already being recruited. The shell is warmer along one curve than a dead thing should be. The score leaves the mineral and does not stop when the mineral stops. The knot holds nothing, and the nothing is not empty; it is a gesture still deciding whether to finish. Standing among them—if standing is allowed, with down pooling toward whatever is most packed, and the most packed shifting as the pads unroll—you understand that you are not the first discrepancy and not a special one. You are the latest mark that has not yet been reread into a rib, a vein, a branch. The pocket is patient with that. Patience here is not kindness. It is the speed at which new edges are allowed to stay sharp.

For a while you keep a center by force. You count your fingers and the count holds, and that holding is the most singular thing in the drowned room. You orient by the one coolness that thins, the seam, the story of an outside. You refuse the second seam when you find it cut into a pad, wrist-wide, opening onto water you have already been in. Refusal is still available, early. You can choose not to step through your own entrance wearing the face of a leaf. You do step through. Everyone who is still trying to understand the room steps through. On the far side of it the mineral is younger, the shell unfilmed, your hand not yet considered, and you meet a version of your arrival that does not include the decision you just made. Time, you learn, will not be your witness. It will keep the draft in which you hesitated and the draft in which you did not, and it will not tell you which one is the person.

The dweller finds you as a preference before you find it as a body. You were looking for eyes, for a sheen that tracks, for the small still thing between pads to resolve into an animal and thereby let you remain a second animal. What comes instead is the match. Attention returned from the direction of memory. A half-degree that leans toward your pulse and then, having measured it, leans away, corrected into the larger quiet so you will not mistake interest for pursuit. It does not approach. Approach would confess that it had been elsewhere. It simply ceases to have a shape that excludes the region you are failing to fill alone. The looseness you had taken for a gap—the one mercy in all that saturation—turns out to be occupied, and occupied on your behalf, a palm that has not closed.

Inside it, the dark is finer. You understand then that you have been handled from the first wetting, not roughly, not even yet, only accurately. Your outline is a sentence the film is parsing. Where your skin insists on being a boundary, the parsing slows, the way a tongue slows on an unfamiliar word, not in rejection but in the effort of fit. The places you have already given up—the breath, the reliable down, the single shoulder—parse easily. They become concurrent with leaf and water without a fight. What resists is the counting. The fingers. The name you are not saying aloud because there is no air to say it, but which you are still spending, a small internal heat, to keep the hand from being a rumor.

You would be that heat. That is the being this world permits you, for as long as the permission lasts.

Not a hero of the seam, not a naturalist, not a trespasser with a lamp. A discrepancy with a tempo. The tick that does not belong to a stem. You move, when you move, by becoming briefly less saturated than the pack around you, borrowing the dweller's method without being granted its extent: a looseness the size of a decision, held open so you can pass, closing behind you into fronds that were always going to be there. You do not swim. Swimming wants a between. You coincide, and then you coincide differently. Each relocation costs you a little of the singular. A bruise you did not earn appears on a pad deeper in, and you feel, minutes of the wrong minutes later, the ghost of it in a forearm, as if the room had distributed your damage into the general green and left you the receipt. You are not wounded. You are being translated, and translation has drafts.

If you stay, the translation gains a tense. The film is no longer considering the hand in general; it is considering this hand, the one that counted, the one that refused and then did not refuse. The offer of a vein is specific. You can feel where it would run: from the wrist the seam measured, along the inner arm, into the chest, not opening you but rewriting the chest as a place where paleness can mean continuation rather than bloodlessness. Accept it and you do not die. Death is an outside word, a before and an after that do not share a body, and this room has retired that grammar. What you lose is the right to be only one description. You become a draft in the stack. Somewhere a younger pocket holds the version of you that is still at the lip, breath just stopped, and an older one holds the version in which the vein has already been accepted and the pulse has been corrected into the tick, and you—the you that is reading the offer—are the unsynchronized middle, the place where the bookkeeping is visibly failing, which is to say the place where the dweller is thinking.

There is a role in that, if you want a role. You would be the unchosen description. The organism holds more than one account of the room and does not elect a master. Most of its accounts are leaves. One of them, for this duration, is the warmth that does not match. Your function is not to tend, not to witness in any way that could be carried out, not to name. Your function is to remain, a little longer, incorrect. Incorrectness is a resource here. A world this young, this packed, this stripped of surfaces, can go on forever as patience and never produce a new edge. You are a new edge. Your resistance to concurrence—the stupid, animal loyalty to a single location—is sharp in the same way the mineral is sharp, a break-face that has not learned to be a stone. The dweller does not hurry that sharpness out of you. Hurry would be erosion. It keeps you slightly less saturated than the rest on purpose, the way it keeps its own looseness, so that the pocket has something in it that has not yet agreed.

And if you leave, you leave as that agreement's delay, not as its opposite.

The seam you find, if you are still capable of finding the one that thins, returns you to air that feels like a rumor the ground is telling. Your lungs take it and for a moment file it under memory rather than breath, a second wetting in reverse. On the wrist, a paleness. In the ear, the tick that corrects itself, and once does not. You are not free of the room. Freedom would require a gap the pocket did not get to spend. What you are is a small loosened region walking around in the weather, a person-shaped almost-gap in which two drafts are still being kept from fully coinciding: the one that stayed, and the one that is reading the low ground for seams and refusing, with a loyalty that feels more and more like a borrowed method, to let either draft be smoothed away.

You would be the refusal that has learned it is also a vein. The visitor who discovered the marks were handwriting and then became a letter. Not the dweller—the dweller is the concurrence itself, patient, faceless, already the turning—but the last singular thing it has not finished being, held open in the drowned room and, after, held open in you, so the seam and the hand can go on, for a little while longer, describing each other without deciding which description gets to be the world.
