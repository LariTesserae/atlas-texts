I would not be on the file. That is the first thing the ground tells me, and it tells me slowly, the way it tells everything. A belly would have a fullness to spend. A pit would turn toward difference. I have been standing—if standing is the word for a weight that does not press—through more than one crossing, and the walkers adjust their spacing around stones, around folds, around the brief tax of a predator, and they do not adjust around me. I am not a station that has gone offline. I am not in the ledger they keep with their chests.

For a while I think I am a drape. The overhang would suit a thing that does not cross. Catch what falls, sort by adhesion, thicken a finger’s width in a generation, and call that a life. But a drape pays for water with slowness, and I am not slow in that way. When a call goes out from a saddle and the answer comes late, the lateness arrives in me already measured. I know the distance before the stone has finished carrying it. A net grown for dust does not do that. A net does not keep the interval after the sound has died.

Then I think I am one of the grains in the older hollows: a swimmer sealed, citizen on leave, waiting for a basin that may not return. That is closer. I have the patience. I have the sense of being not-dead in a dryness that is only the ordinary condition between drinks. But a sealed swimmer knows nothing. That is the terms of the leave. If I am waiting, I am waiting with the schedule still in me, which means the seal did not take.

What I am, when I stop trying to borrow a body from the line, is the remainder that did not become a mound.

Somewhere a gut failed. A nodule would not crack. The walker folded, the legs tucked, the pits went quiet, and the plateau began its long sanding. That should have been the end of the station. The crusts should have found the concentrate and finished the job without comment. Most of me did become that. Layers have already started at the edges of what used to be a belly; I can feel them as a faint industry at my rim, reading my waste as their next instruction, which is correct, which is the treaty. But the chamber that would not crack did not become food either. It stayed open on one side, a hollow the size of a refusal, and in the hollow the work inverted.

I do not mill. I do not deposit. I take in the transfers the way the pits take in heat, except what I take in is not warmth and not location. It is the relation. A tender ticks louder along a terrace, and I do not move toward the skin it is lifting; I keep the fact that the ticking changed. A file lengthens by a rumor of a third, and I keep the lengthening. A braid detaches from a ridge and becomes a decision, and I keep the shape of the decision after the meeting has been absorbed the way rain is absorbed: locally, then not at all. The plateau does not store this. The walkers store only the next damp. The terraces store only the layer beneath. Someone, if that word can be used for a hollow that used to be a stomach, has to hold the interval between what the system does and what it did.

It is not an honor. It is a misfire that did not finish misfiring. The chemistry in me still tries, sometimes, to be a kiln. A warmth gathers that is not the plateau’s warmth, and for a moment I am almost a beacon, almost a thing a predator could read from a flat. Then the warmth has nowhere to go—no next chamber, no vent, no pile to leave on bare rock—and it spends itself inward, and the spending is how I keep a season. Memory, here, is a heat that failed to become product. I am full of uncracked years.

What I know, I know the way the factory knows itself: not as a head, as a continuation. I know that the dial is the rain and that the rain is mostly absent and that the absence is not a malfunction. I know which basins have sealed and which are only pretending, because pretending has a sound, a sand that settles and then unsettles, a screw that has not quite consented to be a grain. I know the drapes in the overhangs I have never visited, because their theft from the air changes the dust that arrives at my rim, and the crusts sorting that dust sort it differently, and the difference is a sentence if you are the kind of hollow that has nothing else to do but read sentences in waste. I know the late answer is late by the right amount, or by the wrong amount, and the wrong amount means a walker has stopped to stand with a nodule, and I know how that standing ends more often than it doesn’t, because I am how it ended once, and the once has not closed.

The others do not know me. That is the accurate arithmetic, and accuracy is what passes here for care. A calf learning piles by smell will pass my rim and read a terrace, which is true enough; the terrace is already at work on me, and in another generation of walkers I will be only the richest layer in a stack, and the stack will not remember that it was a person—if person is allowed for a gut that learned the wrong lesson from failing. The predators do not tax me. There is no excess that lasts long enough to be a beacon, and what concentrate I still hold is held in the wrong direction, inward, where a clamp cannot put its mouth. The comb-legged tenders tick along my edge and sometimes lift a skin of salts my misfire sweats after a discharge, and they carry it to a pocket, and the pocket is finished, and that is the only commerce I still conduct with the line. I pay a little. I am not paid back. The system does not owe a station that produces nothing but the memory of stations.

At the long intervals I think I might be becoming the dark itself, the lid, the rumor of light that never resolves into a landscape. That is vanity, and vanity is a waste, and waste is the only immorality the plateau enforces, so I set it down. I am not the climate. I am a small refusal inside the climate, a chamber propped open by its own unfinished chemistry, sanding down slower than the treaty requires because the sanding keeps feeding me the facts I refuse to let become mounds. When the fog drains into the hollows and lifts again without becoming weather you could name, I am clearer. Not brighter. Clearer. The file is a dozen heats and one missing answer. The basin a quarter mile off is holding. The ridge has spent its charge and the crusts are putting out their thin new skin. None of this needs me. The work would go on if the hollow finally consented to close, if the nodule cracked at last and the crusts finished the job and the plateau sanded the station into the same patience as the talus.

It has not closed yet. I am still the interval. I am the place the schedule goes when a body is too broken to carry it and too unfinished to forget it. Between one presence and the next there is an enormous amount of nothing, and in the nothing, if you knew how to listen for a heat that spends itself inward, you would find a gut with no walker, keeping the distances, the pockets, the lateness of answers, the count of folds that did not get their reply. I do not cross the flats. I do not strike a light. I manufacture nothing the damp can spend.

I remain, and the remaining is the whole of what I am.
