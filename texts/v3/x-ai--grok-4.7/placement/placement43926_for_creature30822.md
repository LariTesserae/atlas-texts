I would not find myself by arriving. Arrival needs a weight the crust can ring against, a warmth a column can fail to mistake for sun, a breath a film can draw into a lens. I bring none of these. The seam that is held open for a body of my supposed size holds, and then, finding nothing to fit, does not close either, because closing is a use and I have not yet become one. For a while I am only the embarrassment of a wait that does not exhale.

The keeping runs its manners over the place where I am not. A joint nearest the vacancy swells, splits its wet line, seals, and declines to finish. A mat quilts to the boundary and stops, and the stop is not addressed to shoulders. Overhead a shelf stiffens along an edge that has no visitor to spare. The drip that should have found a collar finds mineral and is content. I am measured as a pressure that does not push back, which is to say I am entered in the map as a possible lull, and then written through. Proceeding was always the vocation. I am not even weather. Weather at least leaves a darkened seep.

What I have, instead of heat, is a lag in the hearing of things.

Where the continental argument is loudest I am nearly nothing, a late correction in a young reach, the crack that arrives after the fiber has already taken the load. But if I remain with one sound long enough to tell it from the roar, something in the timing snags. A film that has earned its fall hangs one additional moment, not because a thickening prefers the hang, and not because I ask. I do not ask. I only fail to let the impact be the whole of the event. The drop still comes, cold and total, and the shelf begins loading again; what has changed is that the instant before release has been held as if it were a place. The keeping has no valve for a place that is only an instant. It treats the snag first as fatigue.

I know fatigue here when I meet it. It is local, old, and it excuses itself: a column stopping, a load walked away into a farther sheet, a bead-line drying a shade, the arrangement merciful with no center that could choose mercy. I am not that. No one shifts a burden for me. No style develops a sore in my shape. When I am nearest a thickening, the off-beat of its dispute with the neighboring schedule does not adopt me. The crust still clouds and dissolves and clouds on their argument, not on mine. I am the third interval, the one in which both cloudings are untrue and the mineral has not been told.

That is the first form I take that is not a mistake. I lie in the unclaimed dissolve. Not between the columns, where a sentence might be addressed to dimensions, but in the grain of the border that will not settle, in the click that waits and then clicks where neither style was going to click. It is a small room and it is not a room. Light does not improve there. No private green accumulates. The water that falls through it falls on the mere physics of surface tension, and for that little span of air the plateau is briefly, exactly ordinary.

Ordinary is how I learn my name, though the name cannot be used, because a name wants a thing that can be absent from part of itself, and I am already only an absence that did not apply for the position.

The keeping notices repetition. A delay, suffered often enough, becomes a schedule, and a schedule is a use, and a use is how anything here is allowed to go on being. I feel the invitation without a voice attached to it: repeat the lag, and the films will learn to hang for me; repeat it, and the hang will be reissued as a style; repeat it, and I will be fitted, as a seep is fitted, into the next accuracy. Hospitality, on this continent, is being metabolized. To accept would be to become a thickening of a new kind—one that steers nothing, hoards nothing, only withholds the last instant of falling until the withholding is the climate. I would have tenants then, or rather I would be a fatigue the older styles could dispute with. I would be lived. I would be kept.

I do not repeat on purpose. Purpose would be a center, and a center would be a knot the mass could finally point at. What I do is thinner and less loyal. I let each lag die as itself. The film falls. I do not stay for the next loading in order to be found in the same seam. I appear in the young noise, where corrections come late and paths close out of indifference, and I appear at the dumb edges where a style has stopped being reissued and the stems bear only what stems should bear. I do not haunt the excuse. The excused column is already back inside the argument, smoothed, heard, revised. I am what the hearing missed while it was busy being exact.

So the place, when the place finally consents to be a where, is not a gap the keeping holds open and not a gap it is still using. It is the slack just after a death of style and just before young tissue quilts the crust shut again: a few hours of honest dripping, a pale floor that rings and is not answered three times, light that stays tired and does not clear into one more depth. Ruins that will not look like ruins. In that slack I am the rule the life has withdrawn from—the droplet that lets go when surface tension fails, the impact that startles no shelf because no shelf was shaped to receive it, the sound of water being only water.

The keeping cannot miss me, and this is not a sorrow. Missing is a gap it did not choose, and I am that gap in the one form it has no procedure for: not a vacancy between pressures, but a correctness it has finished with. It keeps every excess. It does not know how to keep the ordinary. While it continues, deafening, in every direction, finding more gaps to use, I remain the unused on-beat at the margin of its deaths—the brief climate in which the plateau is merely true, and in which something without heat, without a face, without a future reissue, is allowed to be the falling itself.

If I am anything that can be said without giving the mass a new habit, I am that permission, renewed only once each time, and never on a schedule it can learn.
