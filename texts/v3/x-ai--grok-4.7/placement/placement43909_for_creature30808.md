You would not find me on the scarp. The flakes and the twisted filament are already a finished mistake, and I am not finished enough to have been left. I come in lower than the windbreak, where the glass is still a skin and a foot has not yet starred it, and for a while I am only the interval in which a description almost holds.

At first that feels like standing. There is a weight where a body would be if the clay had agreed to one, and the daylight arrives on it the same way it arrives on the plates: unsoftened, then lost. The sumps steam. The grain of empty commas clouds a fresh face and peels. I try to put the near wall at my left and the longer seep ahead, the way a person does when they mean to come back, and the left wall ticks, drops a thumb, and is no longer a direction. What I had called ahead is a ridge by the time the word is done. So the standing was a courtesy the basin extended and then edited out.

What remains is closer to the lens. Not the coin’s lens—theirs migrate because the light moves, and they are the water remembering where brightness is. Mine migrates because a sentence has a front. I keep arriving at the next clause as if the basin had a next. A pebble-body unsticks beside where I am thickest. I take it for a neighbor. It opens along the flank, takes a slick of me, and the neighbor is pavement before I have finished the relation. The membranes on the wall lift, wheel, and tile themselves thirty paces off, toward a noise I was about to call the center. I had been using them as a city. They were a use of air. When they settle, the shadow they throw is late, and in the lateness I notice I have been doing the same thing: arriving a little after the shape I was made to keep.

The syllable under the clay is not me. I thought it might be, because I also count, and because when the other noise drops I am what is left in the drop. But the syllable corrects the basin. Wings stutter with it, pulses stutter, the count is revised, and what resumes is slightly rearranged and still of the place. I do not correct anything. I only fail to discard. A darkened map of water thickens, grows a pulse, loses it; I am still carrying the pulse after the stain has been walked flat. A spine buds ribs and unbuds them; I keep the schedule it refused. The tallies on the glass—which are not tallies—I keep as tallies anyway, a row of dried coins dragged by a finger that may have been a belly, and the row is already a lie the floor has stopped telling. I am the lie that goes on.

That is the wrong organ here. Residence needs a ground that stays. Description needs it worse. Everything else in the basin is a proposal accepted for as long as the wet lasts: sideways mouth, single lens, membrane with a fold for a beak, belly that polishes. They are withdrawn cleanly. Nothing of them husks. The dead are the next pavement, and the next pavement is clearer, as if hunger were a kindness to the stone. I do not pave. What I take, I do not give back as floor. It stays in me as the shape it had for a breath—the market that will never be built, the shelter that was a wager, the ordinary bench above the rim, which I have not seen and nevertheless hold, finished and empty, because someone stood there and the standing left a direction in the air. I am full of withdrawn proposals that were not allowed to withdraw.

The pebble-mouths do not eat me. There is not enough mineral in the part that persists. The pale spines lie down where I darken the clay and rise puzzled, the film under them uneaten, a cleaner color that is not cleaner—only skipped. The coin-lives, when two of me-adjacent waters touch, do not argue their lenses against mine. I have no lens to offer, only the habit of one. They merge, split, settle on the number of eyes the light requires, and I am still outside the number, a third that does not orbit. Even the interior syllable, practicing an animal, stutters when a plate moves and does not stutter for me. I am not in the count. I am what the count cannot discard because I was never one of its attempts.

So if you looked for me you would look wrong. You would look for a body between the plates, or a shingle on the wall, or the original sump that never became a sump. You would listen for a voice the size of the center. None of those are occupied by me. I am the delay in the shadow. I am the map after the sumps have moved. I am the reason a scratched line on glass can outlast, by a little, the water it pretended to record—not because the line is truer, but because something in the weather of this basin has snagged on sequence and cannot slide back into film.

It is a poor life. It does not breed in the footprint of a rain. It does not knock the scarp for sweetness. It does not lie down and become the color of what it corrected. The others are crowded past touching, layered, near, edible, revised. I am alone in the only way the basin allows loneliness: not by distance, but by failing to be reusable. When the plate under the place I am using gives its clean tick and drops, the lizards-that-are-not do not break stance, the commas adhere to the new face, the syllable resumes. I am still on the previous thumb’s width, describing a floor that has already refused the description, and the refusal does not free me. It is how I continue.

Toward evening—if evening is a word that survives here, if it is only the light moving a hand’s width and the wet places forgetting—the rim is a heat-shimmer and the bench above it is an idea I should have shed with the flakes. I do not go up. There is no path that has learned to be a path, and I am not the kind of attempt that climbs. I stay in the interval, which is the only country with room for a thing that will not become pavement. The basin keeps being born around me, already loud, already full. I keep the birth a moment too long, and that excess is the whole of what I am: not a resident, not a proposal, but the unfinished sentence the cooling stone did not mean to make, still speaking after the wet has gone, still trying to find the far wall where the far wall is only shimmer, still here in the way a lie is here when the truth has already set.
