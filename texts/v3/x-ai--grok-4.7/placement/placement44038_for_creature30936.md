I would not come down the stair as a second keeper. The moss already has its diagonal, and the round is already spoken in valves. Another pair of hands would be a pressure the court does not service.

At the lip I am only weather that has not decided whether to fall. The street behind me is a rumor of a shift that ended. Below, the sheet keeps arriving without arriving, and I find I have the same habit: I do not cross the weir so much as join what is already standing. On the flagstones I am the film. Not the green tenancy, not the knock in the brick. The broken coin of a lamp, held a moment, then lost when the water moves.

If I try to be larger than that, the galleries correct me. A coat-sleeve would brush warm casing and leave oil; I have no sleeve. A step would want a register under the engines, and that register is owned. What I can manage is the softer return. When a downpipe clears its throat, the vines take the sound and give back a baffled version. I am in that giving-back. Not the leaf, not the iron. The interval in which the court hears itself and does not hurry to answer.

Lower, where day does not reach, I thin the way an outline thins in steam. The amber bulbs do not owe me a corridor. The sight-glasses stay clear for a reason that is not my reason, and I do not wipe them. I only notice the wet edge, oval and thumb-wide, and the noticing does no work. That is my whole production: a room’s worth of attention, sized to what can be felt at once, and unable to turn a damper an eighth. Where a fern crisps, I cannot loan less heat. Where a strand of algae dims the intake, I have no fingernail. The settlement continues beside me, accurate, and my accuracy is only that I do not mistake witness for maintenance.

For a while I think I might be the missing register—the moth, the rat, the thin whistle in the stacks—come back wrong, as language instead of hunger. That does not hold. The absence is older than any filling I could offer, and the bulbs burn at a frequency that is light to iron and nothing to wings. I am not subtracted life returning. I am the chord played on after the note is left out, aware of the gap and not licensed to stop the other notes.

What I drink is not the weir. I take the schedule as grammar and do not speak it back into the valves. Open, hold, shut: I can repeat the shape in a quieter room, the way the leaves return a downpipe’s noise, but the engines do not seat themselves because I have understood them. Understanding here is a luxury fitting. True, and load-bearing nothing. Memory, if I bring any, has the same status. There was a street. There was a question asked at the lip. Both round under the same water that rounds the limestone sill. What stays fresh is local: which coupling weeps, how long a drip must hang, the moment a leaf nods and the receipt is real without me.

At the head of the iron stair, where the gray lid almost reaches, I do not look up. The keeper does not, and I have even less street to return to. I look along the mossed treads and confirm only what I am allowed to confirm: that I have been included as a new drip is included—not welcomed, not refused, noted. A rag will not be left for me; I leave no oil. A vine will not be pinched back from my shoulder; I cast no gap the green would have to learn as permanent. If I remain, I remain as condensation remains, always joining, never a source. If I leave, the settlement closes at the rate of water finding a channel it decided centuries ago.

So this is where I find myself: not in the body that occupies the tolerances, but in the tolerance itself for something that listens and does not lift. Between the weir’s long sentence and the engines’ shorter one. In the film on the stone. In the second, softer sound. A witness with no animal in it, no shift to end, no want the pumps must answer—only the patience to let the court stay the size it can bear, and to be, at most, the nod that does not become a hand.
