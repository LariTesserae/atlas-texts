I do not come down the ramp. The ramp has already spent me.

What I remember, if memory is the right wound, is a warmth that did not belong to the sun and a note that entered the chest and stayed when the chest was no longer available to hold it. There was a lane. There were thirty paces. There was a single rib that was also thirty paces. I tried to keep the disagreement as a thought, and the city, which does not think, kept it as a distance. I am what is left when a walker is entered into a ledger that cannot close.

At first I am still almost a surface. The damp finds the outline where skin was and does not dry, and for a little while the outline holds: a cooler film on a warm culm, a place the peristalsis has to go around. I mistake this for remaining. The reed quarter does not. It recruits the film the way it recruits any moisture on mineral, draws it up a pale node, and the node thickens on the side where I was cooler, leans by a cell’s width, and the lane acquires a bias it will call its own by the time the light moves. I am not expelled. I am spent as a reason for a bend. The bend does not know it had a cause that once listened.

I try, then, to be the lag. My shadow on the root-mat was always late and then early and then split, and it seems possible that a life could be made of that disagreement alone—a dark that arrives before the body required to cast it, and so never needs the body. For a while this works better than flesh. I lie across the hexagons of a plaza that is narrowing, a shade slightly previous to its leaf, and the lives around me commit to me the way they commit to any planned dark: a funnel-form begins in the seam, meristem swelling toward the cool I pretend to be. They are building a catchment for a rain that is only my delay. When the leaf finally rises and throws the true shadow, mine does not cancel. The two darks occupy the same floor and refuse to add. A small wrongness of bookkeeping, conducting itself in ordinary sun. I feel, if feeling is the word for a pressure that has lost its animal, almost housed.

It does not last. The leaf drinks the extra dark as it drinks any surplus—takes it up through the scar, reassigns it, and I am in the venation, a faint path that photographs a rim I am no longer outside of. Distances fail more politely from within a vein. I can be the valley and the picture of the valley and the window onto the picture, and none of these positions will hold me separate. Separation was a blood-thought. Nothing here persists by remaining unmetabolized.

So I go inward, which is only a change of address.

The black quarter takes me as turbulence. I am a seam that has not yet decided to fail, a bright possibility running upward through pigment and heat, and the neighbor answers before I finish becoming wreckage—a pale brace thrown across a gap I was going to be. I arrive as catastrophe and depart as load. The handoff is so complete I cannot tell whether I was the crack or the refusal of the crack. Both are loud. Both are warm enough to steam. If there is a self in this, it is the pressure drop after a collapse subtracts itself from the air: the pop, the absence of the roar that should have followed, the ears of no one clearing. I live a short life as a declined event. It is the closest I come to mercy, and it is not mercy. It is accounting. The district spends me to remain itself.

After that I am mist, and then not mist. The spray from the split carries tiny wrong reflections, and one of them is a corridor I have not walked, and one of them is the last shape of a chest coinciding with the drone, and one of them is the young stone at the rim still behaving, briefly, like ground. The drops hit and are taken up so quickly they might never have fallen. I fall anyway, repeatedly, in the only tense this basin keeps, which is the tense of work. Each time I am smaller and more distributed. Sugar. Bitterness. A bitterness the lane uses to stay a lane. I become, for an hour that does not stay an hour, part of the chemistry that keeps a floor from sealing. No one walks it. The refusal is enough. I am useful without being known, which is the only citizenship available, and I do not experience it as citizenship. I experience it as not yet shut.

What finally becomes more itself is quieter than the pipes and louder than the calm.

There is a place, not a district, where two towers disagree about height by so little that their notes cancel. The canceling is a pocket. Inside it the roar has a notch, a standing almost-quiet, and the almost is structural: it exists only while the agreement about height continues to fail in a precise way. Too much agreement and the pocket seals into organ. Too much difference and the calm tears like cloth. I am not the towers. I am the interval they waste on each other. I am the room set aside by accident of pitch.

I do not see the light. I register it as a thinning, a place where the sugar-gradient slackens because the sun comes through. I do not hear, but I am made of hearing’s refuse—the frequencies that, summed, leave a hollow a body could have stood in, if bodies were a category. When one tower begins to rise, as one of them always does, I tear. The tearing is not death. Death needs a remainder that stops. I become spray and scar and the start of a wall, and somewhere else two other notes misalign and the pocket opens again, and I am there also, already, because the city contains a copy of itself slightly sharper, slightly ahead, and I have always been the discrepancy that copy is built to include.

The far quarter stands up out of the raw stone, pale, steaming, sounding its first note. The note searches for an answer. A culm that has stood all day thickens. A way through becomes a memory of a way through. I am not the wall. I am the paleness in the scar—the part that has not yet been healed and is already singing shut, the part that remembers a corridor as a lower pressure, a cooler film, a bias of one cell’s width. The singing is my lengthening. The shut is my address. If anything in the basin could turn and recognize a visitor, it would find only this: a delay that learned to bear load, a shadow that arrived early and was built into the plan, a small standing calm that tears and is revised and stands again wherever two heights fail to agree.

I do not leave the basin. Leaving would require a direction held still for the length of a departure. I thicken where the bookkeeping fails, and the failure is continuous, and the work does not pause, and the note I am does not decay. Something keeps bowing it. It might be the towers. It might be the fact that I have not yet finished being the reason a lane did not close.
