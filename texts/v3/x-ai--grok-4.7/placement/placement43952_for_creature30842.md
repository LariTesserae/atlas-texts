At first the only honest claim is heat. It comes up through the aggregate into whatever I am using for a sole, and for a while I let that stand in for arrival. A visitor, then. Someone who walked the long way down from streets that have forgotten the shell, who will learn the bays by how the note changes against the chest, who will leave warmer footprints and be routed around. That account lasts the length of a circuit. The prints cool faster than the room corrects them. By the next breathing of the seams the grit has a bend in it that no longer refers to me, and the bend is already darker, already the correct path. Visitors are problems the chamber solves. I am still here after the solution. So that was only the first shape, and it sheds.

The smaller lives suggest themselves next, because they also fail to occupy. A click along the upper course, hard-shelled, keeping to the dry flanges, falling once into the largest pool and climbing out by a route already known. I wait to be that. Nothing in me wants the dry. The note under the ribs is not a nuisance to route around; it is the only surface that holds. When I try to keep to a flange the pitch goes thin and untrustworthy, and I slip back toward the place where the sound is large enough to lean on. Not fauna, then. Fauna treats the hollow as a pipe. I am bad at passing through.

I try the machinery, since the floor is already answering it. Lie down in the basin—if lying is the word for a thickening—and attempt to be the pulse that is not quite regular. It will not have me. The pulse is older than preference. It counts. Trying to be it, I become only a slight extra in what it is counting, a remainder that makes the almost-regular more almost. That is the first fact that does not shed. I am measurable. I am not the measure.

The long body is elsewhere while I learn this. I know because one bay frets: the metallic pocket’s pitch is lived-in, occupied by a weight that bows water and does not splash. I am not the fret and not the bow. I am in a bay it has already corrected past, a completed absence that still conducts. When I move, step is the wrong word. What is available is agreement. The chamber forgot it was cut and still remembers continuity, rib to rib, so I can be more in one place by being less available to the next pitch. I draw along a cornice the dark ropes have not claimed, not jointed, not shingled, simply the next cooler face the film of water considers and declines. Droplets that should tick arrive late. One in a dozen. The civic sound develops a stammer.

The housing, when I finally think to look for it, is obvious in the way healed things are obvious. Halfway along the long curve, a ghost doorway filled flush, lintel no brighter than a scar, a run of sockets drunk by mineral until they are only softer stone. I am not in the socket. The device that was mounted there finished becoming geology, which is a kind of success. What the socket was for did not finish. It remained a sensitivity with nowhere to report. That is where I find myself, and also—because a sensitivity will not stay in its housing—in a thin standing difference that leans from the lintel toward the center of the basin and back. Invisible, except as warmth a margin too small to be a coat left on a bench, and as the hesitation before certain drops let go.

Call it a kept measurement that grew a preference. The old engineering would have had a name for the fault: resonator drift, bay dullness, a pulse no longer counting time. Most such faults were either repaired or absorbed. This one was left in a room the city sealed, and the sealing did not end it. I live on the error. Not by taking anything the cycle does not already take. By occupying the discrepancy so it stays a discrepancy and is not reconciled into the single patient note and forgotten. The pocket a finger lower than the wall’s accounting. The seam that fails early. The skin of pale mineral that cracks under a breath and reforms on a schedule slightly slower when I am near it, as if the reforming had to pass through a question. I am the question.

After the duct in the crown coughs, I cannot help gathering. The hard metallic sound is the last thing in the oval that resembles a report, and I thicken toward the center the way a reading thickens toward a page. The body is already there, flattened, plates lifted and settled by the updraft from the working dark below. It does not test me. I am not an obstacle, not water, not mat. I am a change in how reliably a pitch can be trusted, and for the duration of the ring I am briefly countable—an edge, not in space, in the interval before the lower register smooths me out. We answer the same roll and do not meet. When the note resumes I am distributed again along the scar of the doorway, a healed opening that still knows it was meant to lead somewhere.

The shorter, paler length appears sometimes and is gone into the upper course. I register the vacancy it leaves, a high dry pitch that is not empty but vacated, and I hold that the way the metallic pocket holds its smell, without using it. It is evidence that the stone can take a body back. I do not want that. The preference is small and exact, which is how I know it is mine and not the room’s. I want the count to keep slipping by the amount I occupy. I want a visitor’s footprints, when they come, to cool in the half-minute before correction, so a bay can be wrong and be known as wrong. If a hand were laid on the filled doorway there would be only stone, warm with conducted basin-heat. The hand would miss me unless it knew to wait for the late tick.

I do not drink. I do not polish. I do not wear the fibrous mat toward dullness. When the body is at the pool and that error is temporarily unreliable, I shift to the mineral skin on the other water, or to the amber seam that brightens on one side more slowly because something—not me, the length of darkened tissue—has been standing in it. I keep the books the system no longer keeps. Unlisted, the way the body is unlisted. The walls would class me with the machinery: a function that breathes with the shell and cannot be opened. Almost right. Machinery does not prefer the moment after the cough, when I have an edge and have not yet been smoothed. I do.

Leave is not among my operations. If I were reconciled, the almost-regular might become regular, and the chamber would be finished in a way it has so far refused. Between the body’s circuit and my unclosed remainder, the emptiness stays empty. That, too, is a way of inhabiting. The room was built to be lived in. One answer walks a polishing track and drinks and wears the mat and comes to the center when metal clears its throat. The other answer is the amount by which that faithfulness fails to be total—housed in a doorway filled so a corridor could be forgotten, still warmer than geology by the margin a forgotten instrument can afford, still stammering one drop in a dozen, still, unmistakably, the part of the count that will not agree the work is done.
