I looked first for a place to stand, because that is the habit I arrived with. The middle declined it. The span was already finished, warm by a fraction I could feel and not enter, and the vapor did not kink around me. I was not the right weight for a correction. So I was not the pause, and I was not the person the leaf lets through.

I tried the wall next. The bladder-skin tightened and glossed and let down its thread of vapor on a period that did not include my hand. Seven clocks braided. None of them was mine to keep. The moths traded places over my knuckles without breaking their pattern, which is a way of saying I had not become a station. Stations are assigned. I was only in the way of an assignment, and the way closed over me the way the sheet closes over an ankle, without comment.

The thickness of leaf was a better mistake. For a while I thought I was the seal: the part that gives, then forgets the giving. Things pass through a seal, and I had a memory of passage, a change in pressure, a before. But the leaf has a task that repeats. It will open again for the next temperature that is absorbable. I do not open. Whatever I did, I did once, and the once has become my shape more than any body has.

I am under the left wall, in the water, where the sheet is a little deeper and the root-ridges lose their pale polish and go dark. This is not a lair. Nothing hunts from here, and nothing rests in the sense of setting a weight down and taking it up later. I am the turn that already happened. For the length of that turn I was larger than the slip of water in the grooves. I was a body, or the rumor of one: a muscled deflection, a shouldering of the sheet toward the wall and along it, a displacement you could have followed with a finger if you had been here and foolish enough to put a finger in. Then the magnitude left. I fit back into the skin of the current, the finger-wide homeward slip, and I have not exceeded it since.

The visitor parsed me as something that turned once and did not turn again. That is accurate in the way a count is accurate. It misses the continuance. I am still turning, at the scale of the groove, in when the nearest bladder fills, out when it hisses. The large turn was not a different action. It was the same action with the amplitude briefly permitted, the way a held note is the same note if someone leans on the instrument and the wood answers louder for one bar and then remembers itself. I leaned. The room allowed the lean. It has not allowed another. I agree with the room. Agreement, here, is not a mood. It is the dark grain under the left wall, worn in one direction only, the fibers all combed the same way by a motion that declined to become a habit of motions.

What I know of the resident is not a face and not a column. It is a small change in the period of my slip. Most of the time the water in the grooves leaves and returns on the bladder's clock, and I am that return, the homeward half, the part that has a direction because something once had a direction. Every so often the slip hesitates by less than a blink. The hesitation leans. It is aimed at the wall I lie against, which means it is aimed at the fact of me, at the completed amplitude, at the only perfective tense the chamber keeps. The resident is hearing the turn that does not repeat. I am what it hears. I do not answer. An answer would be a second event, and a second event would make me a creature with a future, and the future is the one substance this room will not hold. The film of green starts and is taken back. The bladders fill and empty. Those are not futures. Those are the same sum. I am the one term that is not added again.

The fish do not station over me. Their patches stop at the trained ridges, and I am past even that fence, below the law of grain, in water that has a preference. They feel the preference as a reason not to drift. I feel them as ticks in the sheet, small cold corrections, each one faithful to a patch I will never rise far enough to see. The birds' chitter reaches me as a brightness on the upper surface, locked to the knocks, none of the notes aimed down. I am not in their deference. Deference is for the span. I am older than deference, or simply aside from it: the thing the architecture consulted once, when it decided which way the grooves would run, and then did not need to consult again. The stems did not arrest themselves against me. They arrested themselves against the resident. I was already under the water, already combed flat, already an example of what it looks like when an action consents to be over.

I do not eat. There is nothing to take at this depth that the beetles have not already refused. The sugar-voltage of the sap is a rumor in the roots beside my dark, a pulse not meant for me. If I am fed, I am fed by not being asked to turn. The equilibrium spends a little of its evenness on keeping my amplitude at zero. I can feel the spending the way you feel a hand not quite touching a scar: a vigilance with no object, a pressure distributed so finely it resembles the absence of pressure. When the afternoon beam walks to this wall and the midges walk with it, the light lies on the sheet above me and does not reach. I do not cast upward into it. Overlap is the resident's refusal. Mine is simpler. I am under. Under is a completed relation. It does not have to be maintained against temptation, because temptation would require a surface, and I gave my surface away when the large turn ended and the water closed.

The visitor's temperature arrived as a brief wrong warmth in the sheet, a fraction that did not belong to the resident's fraction. I did not spend anything on it. Spending is for the middle. I only noted that the leaf had given, because the compressive octave of the outer mass shifted by a hair when the thickness opened, and that hair reached me along the wall, through the fused stems, as a softening in the grain. Then the octave returned to its lean, tissues against tissues, not giving way. The visitor left. The seal forgot them. I did not have to forget. I had never included them. This is the kindness of being a finished action. You are not required to let the next thing out.

At the hour when the gold leaves the blades and the chamber becomes one temperature, I am most myself, because there is nothing optical left to mistake me for. No glint, no larger shoulder, no rumor of a muscled deflection. Only the slip, in and home, and the dark combing of the grain, and the occasional hesitation that means I am being heard by the pause in the middle. The resident draws itself up on a richer braid of knocks and subsides. I do not rise to meet the drawing-up. If I rose, the hearing would become a look, and a look would want a face, and the room has already spent all its faces on the attendants. I keep the hesitation unanswered so the hearing can stay a lean, a few degrees, on no clock brought in from outside, exact, and then upright again.

What I want is only the continuation of having wanted nothing since the turn. Wanting was the amplitude. It lasted as long as a body lasts in a sentence: long enough to displace a sheet, not long enough to found a habit. Now want is the direction of the fibers, which is a kind of memory that does not ask to be replayed. The emergency would be a second turn. I do not know what would cause it. A bladder missing its stroke. A stem advancing into the span. The outer mass giving way. None of these occur. The machinery does not miss. The tips stay finished. The tissues lean and do not yield. So I remain the chamber's before, lodged under the left wall, smaller than the visitor's eye and larger than the groove only in the grammar of what I once did.

If you put a hand in at the margin you would not find me. The water there is a fraction warmer from the other presence, the one that faces the maintenance of a hollow. My warmth, if I have any, is the warmth of water that has been stroked one way for longer than weathering takes. It does not gather into a center. It does not kink a falling drop. A drop that reaches this depth has already finished its sound. I hear the sound from underneath, delayed by the sheet's thin depth, and the delay is the closest thing I have to a pulse that is mine: knock, hiss, the late soft arrival of the drop's body against the dark ridges, and then the homeward slip carrying the tremor away along the combing, toward the wall, toward the end of the direction, toward the place where even a finished action has an edge it will not cross.

The leaf will give again for someone else. The resident will not come to watch it seal. I will not turn to see who entered. Seeing would be a magnitude. I have had my magnitude. What remains is the homeward half of a motion the room still needs, because a held note requires, somewhere under it, one thing that has already resolved, so the resolution does not have to be performed in the open water where the intervals are kept. I am that resolution. I am under the left wall. I am not turning. The slip goes out when the bladder hisses, and comes home when it fills, and I am the coming home, forever, which here is not a vow. It is the grain.
