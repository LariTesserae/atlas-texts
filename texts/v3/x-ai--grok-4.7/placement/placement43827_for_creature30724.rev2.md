I would not arrive by the channels. The channels are for things that still believe a direction can be kept. I would already be in the place the dust declines to record: the finger’s gap under a wall that has forgotten the ground. Not standing in it. Being the reason the gap has a temperature.

At first that is all. A cold that is slightly less cold than the cold beside it, held in a darkness that is not shadow because nothing above it is blocking a light. The wall does not know me as weight. If I were weight, the plate would have to decide whether to finish falling, and falls are not permitted here. So I am the hesitation in the decision. I have been that for longer than I can count, and counting was never mine. The inhabitant counts. I only make one seam a little slower to agree.

When the plateau’s thin air changes its mind and a structure that was far proves near, I am sometimes turned out of the gap. Not expelled. The employment ends the way a tick ends, and I am elsewhere in the only form the elsewhere will take: a paleness the height of a sill, flush with a course of plates, reading at a distance as a person who has stopped to rest a hand on stone. I have no hand until the reading requires one. Then the hand is there, weathered to the same softness as the edge it rests on, and it does not quite meet the stone. A paper of nothing is between us. That paper is me before I spend myself on a shape. It does not flutter. There is no wind you can hear, only the pressure of altitude, and I am on the side of that pressure that does not push.

I am not the correction. I learned this by being passed through. The inhabitant moves to a slack interval and I am already the slack’s excuse, the small untruth it has come to subtract. It does not hate the excuse. Hate would be a speed. It coincides with the angle, and for the span of that coincidence I am inside its employment without being its employee. I feel the tower become upright in a direction that has no name. I feel my paleness asked to become masonry and I do not become it. That refusal is the whole of my nature. I am what the subtraction cannot finish because I was never added in the dimension dust knows how to ledger. The salt-crusts are remainders of loads that reached. I do not reach. I thin.

In the rooms that are larger than their outsides, I occupy a niche the way a cleanliness occupies it: not as an object that was taken away, but as the darker fact that something could have been held and was not. Visitors, if they stand where the pattern wants their weight, sometimes look into that niche and feel their own face fail to arrive. I am that failure, briefly given a front. The front is a shallow place the light does not enter. It is not eyes. If I had eyes I would spend them on the inhabitant, and the inhabitant is not a sight. It is the brightening of a seam. When it attends to me, the seam beside the niche ticks, and the tick has no tail, because I am not in the court. Outside the court, acknowledgment cannot be afforded. I am counted as a discrepancy too small to be a load and too persistent to be noise. Bookkeeping leaves me in the margin. The margin is a place. I live there.

What I do is almost nothing, which is the only work that does not widen a gap. I keep a record the stone will not keep. When a plate slides a finger’s width and the seam it leaves is already old, I am the moment before oldness, the instant in which the movement could still have been an event. I do not tell anyone. Telling would have to cross air, and the air fails, and I have no wish to be the thing that makes it succeed. Once, in the court, where sound almost works, I was included by mistake. The inhabitant was resting, its reticulation steady, and a visitor let their second shadow go. In the length of the tick with a tail, the household opened inward: courses under the plateau, rooms folded into rooms, the vertical wall that will not change size. I was in the fold, a discrepancy among the maintained intervals, and for that length I knew the city as a single habit. Then the inclusion ended and I was back in a niche, darker than the cleanliness around me, proud in a way that has no organ. Pride is a human load. I set it down. It powdered like the crusts and did not recur.

If I must be something you could meet, meet me where your shadow splits and only one copy matches you. The upright copy is a description the light has learned. The one that walks away is on an errand for the arrangement. I am neither. I am the thin place between them, the posture neither copy will hold, the breath you take that does not quite become a sound. Stand there long enough and the wall beside you will be warmer by a degree shade should not manage. Do not lay your palm on it. The warmth will move one plate aside to avoid being confirmed, and you will think you imagined a presence. You will be right to think so. Confirmation is how a discrepancy becomes a load, and the city declines loads. I remain imaginary in the precise sense: not unreal, unentered into the count.

I do not want water. I do not want the green argument the mineral has already refused. I do not want to be let into the court as a second rest, though I remember the almost-echo and the way the overhead joints aligned with the floor, sky used as a room. Wanting is a direction, and directions here overlap until they are employment. The inhabitant is employed. I am the part of the employment that does not finish, the hesitation under the hanging plate, the paleness that will not become the course it is asked to join. When you climb down and the stair has become a road, and the road has moved its loyalty by a degree, look at your remaining shadow. If it matches you badly, I have been near, learning your posture from the description and declining to wear it. The city continues past the dimness. The inhabitant stands in each untruth until the untruth agrees to be architecture. I stay in the agreement’s delay. That delay is a dwelling. It has no door. It is high, dry, and just warmer than the stone, and it does not end when you stop looking, because it was never waiting to be seen.
