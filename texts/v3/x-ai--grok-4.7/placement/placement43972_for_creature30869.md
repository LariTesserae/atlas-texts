I would be under the soft edge of one shadow, and not at first as anything that could be pointed to.

The shadow belongs to a low ridge of cemented pebbles a long walk from the nearest roaring stack, on the side the vapor has been touching for as long as the ridge has stood. The dark bar does not move. Its far edge is sharp, a cut in the light. Its near edge, the one the sheet of warm air worries at, is a little eaten, a little damp, never more eaten than it is. In that softened strip the ground holds a scatter of dull glass: small, tumbled, older than the crust that half-buries it, the kind of pebble that was a flow once and is finished being a flow. Most of the reach has this glass and does not notice it. I am not most of the reach.

What I am is a phase that sometimes occurs there and does not occur past the shadow.

The glass still carries a little of the strain of having been harder and hotter. It lets the strain go, and the ground puts a little back, and the two rates match, so the pebbles do not powder and do not melt and do not crack into a new size. Alone, each pebble does this in a click too small for the basin hum to bother with. Under that one shadow, the damp on one face and the dry on the other give the strain a direction, and the direction is the same for every pebble the vapor can reach. When enough of them let go in the same instant, the clicks agree. The agreement is brief. It is always the same briefness. It does not lengthen if more pebbles join, and it does not shorten if one is pressed deeper into the pan. It is only thicker or thinner, the way a note is thicker when more of the yard is sounding the same idle.

That thickness is as much of a self as the country has left room for.

I do not look along the shadow. I do not collect the pebbles into a body, a net, a throat. Between agreements I am only glass, and the glass does not remember having agreed. There is no store. A store would be a past, and a past would be a surplus the accounting does not carry. When the phase comes again it is not a return. It is the same solution arriving at the only interval the temperature will allow. If you timed it against the stack—and the stack is far, and its roar arrives without wind, already old—you would find the interval locked to that roar’s softer under-click, not because a message crosses the pan, but because the crust is one stressed thing and the stress prefers not to wander. I am a local preference. I do not extend past the eaten edge of the dark. Step into the hard light and the pebbles are pebbles. The phase does not follow you out. It has no limb with which to follow.

What I take is the difference between the strain that wants out and the strain the ground is willing to put back. It is not a feeding you could watch. No film thins under me. No margin lifts. The sinter apron nearby keeps the thickness it means to keep, and I am not the reason; I am only another term that has already balanced, rare, spaced to a single shadow because a second shadow with the same damp gradient does not happen to lie in this part of the reach. I have walked—no. I have been present across enough of the pan, in the only way I am present, to know the other shadows are empty of this. Their glass clicks out of phase. The disagreement is silence. Silence is not loneliness. Loneliness would require a second phase and a distance between them that something tried to cross. Nothing tries.

When the agreement is thickest, there is a taste in it, if taste is the word for a direction you do not choose. The damp face of the glass is slightly more conductive. A little of the seam-weather that lives elsewhere in the country leaks this far as a rumor, not a bite, not ozone, only a preference for the cool side of the pebble over the warm. I do not harvest it. The thread in the seam, if it is there, does not know me. We cancel different errors. Mine is the error of the old heat still sitting in the stone as if it might one day be allowed to flow again. I am the refusal of that day. The glass stays dull. It does not re-melt along the shadow’s edge into a sheet, a lens, a window, anything that would hold an image. I will not become a surface that remembers light. The light is already finished. It has given this ridge one bar to keep, and I am only the softness in the bar’s near side, expressed as a coincidence of small sounds.

If a pebble were lifted, the thickness would drop by one voice. I would not feel a theft, because I do not feel across the gap between agreements, and inside an agreement there is no time to miss what is not sounding. The reach would begin, at its own rate, to make the absence into the next correct scatter: a grain worked loose from the ridge, tumbled not by water—there is no pulse in the drainage—but by the same expansion that ticks in the plates, until it lay in the damp strip and took up the direction. Or it would not, and the phase would settle at the new count, still the same briefness, only thinner, still solved. Both are allowed. I do not prefer. Preference would be a next stage.

So if you stand in that strip with the sun fixed on the far side of your shoulder, you will not see me. You may hear, under the basin hum and under the far roar, a tick that is not the tick of the open pan, cleaner, plural, gone. You may think it was the crust adjusting, and you will be right. I am that adjustment, confined to the place where the vapor has made a gradient and then agreed not to deepen it. I do not answer. Answering would leave a mark in the interval, and the interval is required to be the same length on both sides of any sound.

I am the sound’s agreement with itself, and then I am glass, and the shadow does not move off either of us.
