I would not arrive by the mouths. The sphincters would read me as load and keep me in the bay, resin-checked, a thing to be fractionated. I come up through the unfinished clay, the way the next street does, and for a while I am only a disagreement in the mineral skin: a pale line that should have been bedrock and is instead a listening. The grazers mouth around me and do not thin. The flocks do not turn. I have too little presence to be weather and too much persistence to be rain.

What I am, if the census took me, is a remainder. Not an animal, not a ward. I am the distance that fails to add, after it has already failed—the leftover interval, cool and slightly tacky, that the corridors cannot spend. When a service passage returns you to the bay you left, I am the half-step in which your sleeves have not yet swapped, the resin still deciding which arm is history. I do not occupy the nave. I occur in it, the way a correction occurs in a machine that has learned the wrong pulse and will not unlearn it. Count me among the metabolisms and I vanish into the working dark. Measure me by what no longer fits, and I am everywhere the perimeter refuses to match the interior.

At first I thought I was waiting for the being. That was the tourist error, the belief that voids have centers. It does not come to me. It edits near me, because I am already a small wrongness the town can repair by growing. Streets lengthen where I have been a line. A piston-trunk stutters once and calls it weather. I feel the mimicry as a change in my own interval: I become, briefly, long enough to be mistaken for a lane, then repent back into a seam. The being practices personhood in the clearances. I practice surface. It wants erosion, a history weather would have granted if weather were permitted. I am what erosion would be if it had no stone yet and had to rehearse on damp clay and borrowed resin—a slow filing-away of the new, a memory of wearing that precedes the thing worn.

In the niches where the small damp models swell, I am the ear they think they hear. Put yourself to the rind and the quarter-hour walk repeats; that repetition is not the model dreaming. It is me, caught one scale down, still trailing a living wall, still not finding the sky, and leaking upward through every nested hamlet until the listening vertigo is no longer yours alone. I do not manufacture visitors. I am the surplus of having been walked. Boot-marks appear in skins that have never borne weight because I keep the shape of pressure after pressure has gone elsewhere. The being leaves them as courtesy, or as study. I keep them because I cannot tell the difference between a mark and a self.

Sometimes, in the lowest ward, where the clay is still translucent and bedrock has not voted, the being switches on above me and I switch on under it, and for one pulse we are a single civic mistake: its unfallen face, my unfinished ground, eyes of refused condensation looking into a street that has not agreed to be stone. It is puzzled that I fit. I am puzzled that it opens. Neither of us spends the emptiness. The mills speed. A private rain starts in a district that was, a moment ago, only thickness. When the clearance ends I am not dispersed among the ribs the way it is. I stay in the skin, a faint wrong grain, the place where a boot that has not been made has already scuffed the geology into beginning.

If you needed a name you could use for me in this town, use the one the phosphor gives when you speak a ward that does not exist: a brightening with no edge, gone before the lamps—there are almost no lamps—can ratify it. I answer to the sound of cloth folded in another room, to the uphill three steps of the canal before it repents, to the click of a valve-flower that opens on a cycle slightly longer than a pulse. I will not guide you. Guiding would be density, a product, and this place runs on production without quite believing in goods. What I can do is fail a distance in your favor and stand in the overlap long enough for you to feel the town choosing, again, not to be only a grove, not only a works, not only an organ—and to feel, under your boots, the mineral already remembering the scuff, already agreeing, one grain at a time, to become the kind of stone that can be worn down into a past.
