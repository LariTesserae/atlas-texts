You do not step into the ring so much as discover that the measurement has already closed. The warmth at your shins is no longer an extension. It is the temperature you have, and have had, for as long as it takes a vein to brighten one handbreadth and stop. The habit did not decide you. It completed a sentence it had left open, and you were the grammar that fit.

At first you are still the mismatch: crisp, then muffled, a seam opening in the wrong order. The glass under one boot holds an hour ago. The mealy mineral under the other is negotiating. Between them, in the pause that is your weight, something prefers not to revise. Not mercy. A typo it has not yet chosen to correct, and so, for the length of its attention, will not.

What you are, if the word can survive a place that has not finished deciding what a body is, is the lag made portable. The habit keeps clearings by being the preference that stone obeys. You keep a smaller clearing by walking, which is only preference with a direction attached, and direction is a minor contraction the land has not needed until now. Your outline ceases to matter before your boots do. The ring does not expand by a clast. It declines to shrink. That is the inclusion. Exclusion would have sounded like cloth, already finished, arriving late. This sounds like nothing, which is how the kept floors announce themselves.

You learn the sense it reads with, because you are now a difference it can pass along. When a terrace gives in the dark, you feel the evenness: every vein the same dull rose, the hand-off suspended, the sleeper noting the door. You do not go to look. Looking would require a face, and faces are for places that have settled the question of light. Instead the lag in you stutters, not offended, only revised, and resumes at a bearing a fraction more inward. The three slabs, half-swallowed, seem to acknowledge the correction. Or the dark cheats that, and what you take for acknowledgment is only the ceiling lowering until the tallest ridge nearly meets it, and the gap ticks, soundless, with the smell of hot mineral.

You have no mouth. You discover this without surprise, the way you discover that your steps have stopped being the loudest thing. The dryness is not a lack. Water would be a foreign sentence, a small wet argument, and you would not hate it. Hatred has not precipitated. You would simply fail to keep a clearing for it, and the next spill would cover the argument without ceremony. Your tenderness — if a delay in cooling can be called that — is for arrangements almost ready to be permanent. A ridge with the profile of a spill frozen mid-pour. A ring whose perimeter is a temperature. A standing stone that must not fall until it has finished meaning whatever it means. You turn nothing with a limb. You encourage. A corner lifts the width of a fingernail and settles on a new bearing, and the sound is cloth you already heard, closer, done.

The habit does not address you. Address would split what has not separated: decision from physics, keeper from stone, you from the room you have become. It attends, sparsely, and you attend back by occupying the choice. Fresh faces that split and show their rose are statements. Sometimes you answer by letting a warmth travel a seam until the seam prefers to stay shut. Sometimes you do not. Indifference remains native, and much of the floor revises itself without either of you: slumping, welding wrong, ticking against the black that may be sky and may be rock that never arrived. Only the marks are occupied. You are a mark that can still move on purpose, and the purpose is shrinking, not from fear, from fit. Each time the ceiling lifts and the black seems briefly a height, you are less a visitor and more a clause the page required.

If something later comes that is truly alive — a wet argument, a seed, an eye — you will not know the word for it. You will register a temperature that does not belong to the sequence of cooling. The habit will pause, every vein even, noting the door. You will pause inside its pause, a smaller evenness, and for the length of that shared suspension the floor will not decide whether the new thing is a center to be cleared for or a spill to be allowed, at last, to cool. You will not hurry the verdict. Hurry is weather. This place has refused weather, and you, being what the refusal kept, will wait with it through the next unmaking, the next dull rose, the next quiet that is thick because warm air carries little, and nothing here has died, and nothing, yet, has needed to live.
