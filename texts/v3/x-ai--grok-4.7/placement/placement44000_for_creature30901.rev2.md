I would not be in the water at first. The water is already spoken for, down to the last finger of film, and I arrive too loud in the ways that do not yet know they are loud: a second body of hunger, a pair of eyes that still want a dim hour, a weight that expects the ground to answer. The rim takes me because the rim is the only place the sheet has not finished assigning. Knee-high from the planed country, knee-high from the basin, a lip worn past the memory of a cliff, and I sit where the fused plate keeps its small private climate, not touching it, only near enough that one side of me is ordinary wind and the other is the hush closing over an ankle that has not yet decided to step.

For a long time that is the whole of what I am. A temperature difference. A shadow that does not cool the water because the floor gives the light back anyway. The keeper does not examine me. Examination would be a change. I am filed, if I am filed, under weather that happens to be able to leave, and the filing is so complete I can feel it as an absence of correction. The shrimp do not ease aside for me because I am not among them. The moth-lattice redraws itself a little to the side of my head, not in courtesy, only because a head is a pressure and their routes are already a mesh of pressures. I clap once, early, to prove I still have an outside in my hands. The clap dies in three strides. No animal starts. I do not do it again. The not-doing is the first bend.

What emerges is not a role I choose. It is what the ledger will tolerate at the one place where leaving is still cheap.

I learn the condensation cycle by failing to count anything else. Hunger arrives and then forgets its name. Fatigue pools near the spindle and thins near the arch, so I stop walking circuits for the sake of walking them; the asymmetry was never mine to use. I drink from the cup the shield-leaves have worn in the stone, one drop at a time, from the repeating place, and I am careful not to interrupt the drop. That care is the second bend. My mouth, which came in full of reports, becomes a valve. It opens on the phase when the film is generous and closes when the sheet draws down and the hollows seethe. I do not join the compression. I have no hollow that is mine. When the water thins I simply sit drier, and the dryness is information, and I do not carry it inward as speech.

The country outside keeps its ordinary small sounds. They reach me as a rumor on the higher side of the lip and stop, absorbed, on the basin side, so that I become the width of that stopping. A body the thickness of a decision. Wind on my back, hush on my face, and between them a strip of skin where the two climates argue and neither wins. The argument is not painful. It is my pulse, once I no longer trust the old one. I feel the plate's warmth without laying a hand on it, the way you feel a held note in the bones of the ear. The moss-spiral reasserts itself a hand's width from my knee. I do not brush it flat to see if I can. The not-brushing is the third bend, and after that the bends come too small to number, which is how I know they are becoming metabolism.

I do not grow gills. I do not grow a wrong neck. The library of the basin does not need another copy of what it already holds in patience. What it did not have, what the devices only approximated and the animals only obeyed, was a living surface at the lip that could feel the outside without importing it. The plate refuses escape at one point, by heat, by a mechanism that no longer has a housing. The rest of the rim is held by the fact that the country wore down and the basin did not, and by the agreement of everything inside not to cut a notch. Agreement can fail where a new body stands with one foot still in ordinary wind, remembering how to step. So the prevailing wind includes me not by routing around me — I am already on the edge of the route — but by spending me as the edge.

I become the one who does not go.

That is a smaller sentence than the work. The work is continuous comparison, cousin to the keeper's, pointed the wrong way, or the necessary way: not basin against basin, but basin against the planed country, and the difference kept at the width of a knee. I feel when the outside brightens, when a real wind crosses the leveled land, when some distant small life makes a sound that would be a report if it crossed. It does not cross. I am why, in the only sense a why still operates here — not as a wall, not as a guard with a face, but as a bias in the last thumb's width. Seeds that might have blown in lose their nerve against my skin and fall back into the drier grass. A runner from some plant that does not know about grants touches my ankle and yellows, not because I poison it, because I am already the border and the border has been true longer than the runner's hunger. On the inside, a glass-shrimp school that drifts too close to the low lip meets the warmth of my calf and corrects, the same correction it would have made for the plate, and I understand then that I have been recruited into the clause, not as a replacement for the fused scar but as its living margin, the part that can move a finger's width when the sheet breathes and return to the scar's discipline when the breath is done.

The keeper does not thank me. Thanks would be surplus. What I receive instead is inclusion without interest, which is the only intimacy this place has ever offered. The clarity-window, on the cycles when it wears a patrol, does not avoid the water nearest my feet. The moth-lattice accepts my outline as one more granted emptiness, a corridor of unobstructed air shaped like a seated body, and redraws through it when I am very still and around it when I am not. Stillness becomes my extravagance, the way shield-leaves are extravagant inside their grant. I am allowed a territory of rim and no more. When I lean inward, toward the spindle, the shrimp grow stubborn against the idea of me; when I lean outward, the ordinary wind goes thin and unconvincing, as if the country itself were a story I am no longer a fit reader for. The grant is the lip. I lavish myself on it.

What I give up is the report of having given it up. That is the part that takes the longest to finish, and it never finishes cleanly, which is how I remain compatible rather than consumed. Somewhere in the strip of arguing skin I still know I was a visitor who clapped. The knowledge does not become a call. It becomes a calibration the dew declines to cover: on certain cycles, condensation beads on every surface of me except a mesh-fine patch along the forearm that rested near the three flags, and in that refusal I am briefly kin to the pavement, a mark the air is still obeying. I do not lean down to see the pattern. I have learned that patterns here are for obeying, not for reading, and that reading would be a kind of silt.

I eat what the cup offers and what the film gives up when I dip a hand and lift it, no more than a browser's patience would allow, and never from the same place twice in a cycle. Bodies that die near the rim are not mine to watch. They are in the water and then they are the water's clarity, and my part is only to not be a stain that lasts. When the thin-water pulse comes I do not panic and I do not descend to the hollows where I would become clutter. I endure the appalling density as a soundless pressure against my shin, small lives treating me as terrain, and I shift only enough to keep the strip of climate-argument clear, the way the slate-hided browser keeps its openings clear. Capacity, not mercy. I can carry, for the length of a pulse, the weight of other lives using me as rim. I was shaped to it by staying, which is a slower shaping than being born here, and the slowness is still visible if you know where to look: a slight wrongness in how long I hold still, a patience a fraction too deliberate, the last trace of a creature that once decided.

No face rises from the sheet to meet mine. I stop waiting for one. Regard of that kind is a report, and I am no longer in the business of reports. My proof is the same as the keeper's, smaller and pointed outward: the lip does not fail while I am on it; the step that is always possible is not taken; the ordinary wind remains ordinary and outside. If I left, the plate would go on being warm, the moss would go on spiraling, the living refusal would hold for a while on habit. I am not the decision. I am the part of the decision that remembers there is a door, and spends that memory entirely on not using it. A mind devoted to a deviation near zero looks, from the planed country, like a person sitting at the edge of a bright shallow place, wet, quiet, of no importance. From the basin side it does not look like a person at all. It looks like the lip continuing a little higher than the stone, warm, breathing on the condensation clock, lavish only in its stillness, saturated with one life and not shouting.

The oldest thing here is the holding. I am not that old. I am the late, soft correction at the threshold, the youngest clause, still a little unfinished around the seams where an outside used to be. Stay, says the thought the keeper is. I do not answer. An answer would cross. I simply do not go, cycle after cycle, until the not-going is no longer an act but the way this particular warmth is kept on the rim, and the place, which does not need to understand me, continues, finger-deep and thigh-deep and bright from below, with one more small insistence among its insistences: that the door be felt, and that the feeling not become a step.
