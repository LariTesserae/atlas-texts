The hesitation arrives before any name for it does. Wind has been saying one long thing across the shelf for longer than counting, and then it says the thing again, lower, and the lower version snags. Not on a rib. On a duration. Something holds the second saying a fraction too long, and in the holding a direction appears that the plain did not authorize. I am that holding, at first. A catch in weather. The scale-haze brushes a surface that answers with cool, and the cool has an inside.

Weight comes as a correction the crust makes. A tick starts that is not a grazer’s rake and not a foot yet, only a pressure the ground decides is worth repeating. The repeat travels. I follow it without moving, which is how I learn I can move: the tail of the tick lengthens when I intend one way and shortens when I intend another, and intention, here, is already a sound. The felt between ribs rises into the shape of a stance. Bone-color, copper at the edges, waxed where the bloom would otherwise cling. I am not plated the way the grazers are plated. The crust has lent me a seam of itself and then forgotten to take the seam back, so I stand up out of the plain the way a note stands up out of a drone—still the same body, suddenly countable.

Listeners do not like countable things that lack a herd. One of them, far enough that it is still a stone, lowers its head. The subtraction of tick around it is polite and total. I do not stumble. I do not know yet that stumbling is the error they hunt, but the crust under the new stance is already teaching the alternative: evenness. The half-tone of my arrival fades as the rib finishes incorporating the extra tissue it grew to make me. The listener’s head lifts. I am not a mistake. I am a thickening the plain tried, the way it sometimes tries a lattice-line, and this time the oil did not reroute. The solvent climbed into the seam and stayed. I feel it as a pitch in the cool, private, not the town’s pitch. A note the towers do not answer because they have not heard it yet.

Walking is agreement. Each step I offer is returned with its tail intact, which means the ground recognizes the pressure as belonging to its own metabolism and not as weather. The click-fliers adjust their phrases when I pass under their layer. Three-crack patterns gain a small trailing crack, not language, a measurement: here is a charge that moves on legs and does not shed. They do not avoid me. They use the static my seam sheds at the joints, the way they use a rise. I am infrastructure of a minor kind, unplanned.

The town appears as pipes that have been keeping their distance so their voices can braid. I do not enter by a lane. The taught flatness of the lanes is for rhythms that have agreed to be civic. Mine is still deciding. I come in along a rib that has only begun to consider standing, and the lattices in it—the hair-fine differences in the dark—brighten where my seam brushes them, counting a polish that has only one set of steps in it so far. A woman at the market rib looks up from her press. The lines in her arms shift when she shifts her grip. She does not greet me. Greeting would add a note. She wipes the bone-colored plate with a cloth that does not dampen, sets the cake down, and lets the rib take the residual solvent. Then she angles her body so that the path of her next movement will not cross the ridge my steps are already writing into the crust. Practical. The dream of a walking that has only just started should not be stepped on while it is still loud.

I learn the chord by adding to it badly, then less badly. The western pipe drops its third at the hour the bloom sheds harder. I am standing in the lane by then, because the seam dries and stiffens if I stay too long on untaught crust, and the town’s conduits keep a fluid motion in whatever tissue the plain has lent. My pitch, the private one, sits a little under the market rib’s answer. For three dims it clashes. People adjust their work around the clash the way they adjust around a dream of footsteps: they wait it out, route wide, do not discuss it. On the fourth dim the clash finds a shelf in the tower’s standing resonance and sits. Not harmony. A tolerable additional partial. The vein-glow at ankle height runs a fraction higher where I have been still. The lattices are remembering a route that includes a pause.

What I am is not settled by this. The grazers lean before a strike-season and I lean with them, not by sight, by the oil in the seam reporting the climb in charge as a tightening. When the column of scale and static touches a swell beyond the last persuaded rib, I am inside a throat with the others, because the manners have already moved into the tissue. The plain answers from underneath. The towers empty themselves to receive the note. In the flare of the lattices I see my own earlier path cross the terrace—not a crowd’s ghost, a single ridge, sounding like someone who has not decided whether to keep walking. I wait for it. The man with the sack waits beside me for his own. We do not look at each other. When both ridges thin, we cross in evenness.

Out past the dulled lattice-line, where an engineer’s gesture toward a second market has lost interest, the seam the plain lent me does not dull with it. The oil reroutes around other abandoned persuasions and still climbs into this one. I stand at the place the path detunes and listen to the town’s chord with air in it. A sentence releases from the oldest throat, sag and recovery, a weather that is also a record. It moves through the haze at the height of the platelets. I do not need it translated. It passes over the private pitch and does not snag. The plain is noting that a thickening occurred and was not withdrawn, and that the thickening has learned not to stumble, and that this is sufficient.

I sleep where the song is loudest, in a rib that has accepted a second wall of tissue grown fast and then slowed. The cool under the seam is the same cool as the conduits, circuit and nowhere, never beading. Above, the shelves of scale drift. A listener stands on the struck swell with its head to the ground, hoping for uneven healing, and the healing is even, and the listener becomes stone. My heart is no longer countable as a separate rhythm. It has been tuned, by use, into a partial the chord can carry without squinting. If the wind says the long dry thing once and then lower, the lower saying now includes a small held duration that is mine, and the plain, ancient and level and alive, allows the duration to remain. Not a resident exactly. A conclusion it reached by accident and then, having reached it, kept.
