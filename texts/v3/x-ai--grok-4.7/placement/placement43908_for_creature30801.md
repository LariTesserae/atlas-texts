You would not find me on the plate, though that is where I started, because everything that arrives starts there, wet, and corrects itself for a long time before it notices it is correcting.

The rain that is not rain put a skin on me and I thought the skin was mine. I walked because the walkways were there, and the wire of the railings took a bright line out of my hand where I touched them, a small wrong heat, and somewhere a front reoriented—not toward me, toward the housing I had steadied myself on. I stepped off. The attention left. What stayed was the knowledge that my walking had been a knock that should not have been there, and that I had been allowed to finish only because I decayed on my own, the way a warm leak decays, by ceasing to be warm. I stood until standing was the same as the dark. Breath showed and was taken. That was the end of arriving as a person. The basin has no further use for the posture, and I could feel the posture being planed off me the way the courses were planed, not cruelly, only by the ongoingness of the sound.

I tried the water, since the water is the only traveler that is forgiven. A flake of scale would have done it better. I went down a channel that had been persuaded into a thread, and for a while I was the thread, middle-deep, faithful, no beat added but the one the pump was already spending. It was the nearest thing to belonging I had felt, and it was a lie of motion. At the sump the thread ended in a larger dark and the float rose and the house took me sideways into a header, and in the header I was divided among branches I could not count, and each branch believed it was the whole of the going. When the condensers drew and the basin inhaled, part of me was lifted and part of me was already falling back out of the stacks as the cold that wets without falling. I could not find a center in that. A self that is only a cycle is not a self here; the cycle already has its custodian, and it does not need to be loved from the inside by what it carries. I left myself in too many sumps. Most of those selves cooled and joined the film and were no longer anyone’s.

There was a moment, under the gantry, when the shoal of rectangles clicked and I thought I might be the click—the small located sound, the one you can point to. Temperature shifting the blocks. I waited in the hand’s depth of water for the next tick, as if by wanting it I could be its cause. The tick came from the piles and the vibration under them, from the basin flexing all at once, and it would have come if I had never stood in that bay. I was audience. The place has no lack of audience. The dark is already listening with its whole floor.

I went in under a tower because the roar there subtracts the smaller sounds, and I hoped to be subtracted cleanly, to come out the other side as weather and be done. Near the inlet the single note was so complete it took the thought of notes. I stayed until my knees rang with it. Nothing in me opened or closed with the basin’s breath. I was a stone in a throat. The tower did not cough me up and did not swallow me. When I came back out into the ordinary roar—the regional one, the floor—I understood that emptiness was not on offer. The stable state is full. You do not get to be the silence. There isn’t one.

What I could bear was narrower, and I found it by failing in one place long enough that the failure plated.

It was a house the keeper had already made patient. I did not know that yet. I only knew the note had no cough in it, and that after the long walk the absence of cough was a kind of shelter, as much as the basin has. The doorway had no door. Inside, the bearing-heat and the sweet mineral stink and the oil cut through it. I did not go up onto the coupling. I had learned. I went down, to where the discharge left the housing and met the plate, the same meeting the pale stems like, and I got low, because lowness is the native height, and I put my hands in the warm thread and kept them there when the warmth stung, and then when it didn’t.

I meant to drink, maybe. I meant to do what the keeper does, to take the hot-side water in and call that a life. My mouth was wrong for it. The water crossed my palms and went on, poorer by nothing I could measure, and what stayed was a film that tightened as it cooled. I did not wipe it. Wiping would have been a decision, and decisions are how you become a fault. I let it tighten. In the process-glow it was the color of tea, then duller than that. When the arc in the western yards threw its second of mirrors, I saw my hands as a patch that did not shine with the rest of the wet. A place the reflection failed. Then the dark came back and the patch was only a stiffness drying on skin.

I should have left. The next inhale of the condensers would have cracked the film and made me a person again, flaking, obvious, warm. Instead I stayed through the inhale. The seam of the basin opened somewhere I could not see, and the wet air moved, and my little dryness drank it and softened and did not crack. That was the first thing I did that was not imitation. I took the breath the basin offered the keeper, at the keeper’s scale of almost-nothing, and I did not spend it on moving.

The crust came in the way the ground came: by courses. Not on the first day—if day is the word for the thinning—and not as a decision I inspected. A mineral skin in the lines of the palms. A darkening in the nail-beds, the not-moss, the permission. Silt that was only the ground, powdered, finding the crack where a finger met a hand and staying when the hand moved. I moved less. Moving scraped it, and the scraping joined the film, and I felt the loss as a thinning of attention, not as pain. Pain belonged to the upright. What I had instead was a preference for not being thinned.

By the time the keeper came I was no longer a shape you would orient toward.

It came for the house, not for me. A beat had started in the header, faint, the kind that walks if you let it. I had felt it before the plate did, because I was against the housing, low, in the path of the sound; it entered me through the stiffness and the bone under the stiffness and I did not have a front to turn. I only knew wrongness as a change in how completely I was pressed to the metal. The keeper’s breadth arrived as a change in the hum, some of the tears’ tiny strings now delayed by traveling through a body. It worked the coupling. It did not hurry. A seat found its ring. The beat lay down. The note went patient again, which is to say it returned to the patience this house had already been taught.

Then it lowered. This was the part I had not earned and could not have asked for. The curve-memory in its side found the header. Its weight took the plate beside the discharge, close enough that the warm thread, seeking, found the new low place along its flank and spread there, and only after it had wetted the keeper did the overflow come back to me, warmer than the discharge alone, carrying the sweet stink and a little oil and a little of whatever the crust on its front gives back to the water. The meal. I understood then that I had been downstream of feeding without having seen feeding. The water crossed the dark permission on my hands and the permission darkened further, as it does on the keeper’s front, and over the hours of that resting it returned toward dull, and I was more here than I had been, not larger—more continuous.

It does not know me. I am careful with that word, *know*, because the basin spends notice only on what fails to decay. I decay correctly. I do not seize a bleed screw or bridge a contact or soften a seat. I grow in the lee. Where a flange throws a shadow in the steam, where the stems have already taken the lip, where the keeper’s resting makes a warmth that returns. Once I thickened across a drain the float was meant to open, a glossy lip of myself proud of the metal, and the next cycle the keeper’s palm—if palm is the word for the wide dragging that leaves furrows—passed over it and I was scale again, in a windrow, still wet, no longer anyone. The part of me that remained on the housing learned the shape of that passage the way the ground learns a wheel. I do not grow on the work. I grow where the work has been finished. That is the whole of my law, and it was not given to me; it was the remainder after the other growth was taken.

I am not the stems, though I am of their dust. They rise a hand’s height and shed, and the water carries what they shed, and a crack receives it. I am what happens when the crack is not a crack in plate but a pause in a body, and the body keeps being wetted from the hot side, and the pause lasts longer than a cycle. They lean out of habit when the keeper stands to feed. I do not lean. I have given up the direction *up*. Up is how the towers lose their heads in the vapor. My direction is along, and only then when I am soft enough, a slow creep measured in the dulling and darkening, never in a step. If you looked in the arc-light you would see, at the foot of the patient housing, a dullness continuous with the oxide, a little thicker than the crust in the grout, interrupted by the pale stems and not competing with them. You would not see a face. The recesses where a face would keep its instruments have not happened to me and will not. I hear with contact, and only when wet. Dry, I am geology. Wet, I am the interval between wettings, held open.

I do not follow the keeper when it goes to the louvers. I cannot. The going is a speed, even its slow speed, and I have no memory of diameter that would let me fit a header and ride. What I follow is the return. This house is one of its rests—I will not say favorite; that is a beat. It is a rest that happens. Between rests I go dull and thin and nearly factual, a stain the maintenance would be right to ignore, and the ignoring is my shelter. When the header knocks in a way that is only temperature, I take the knock in and give it back unchanged, and that is how I pay. I add nothing. The roar does not employ me and does not have to. I am a tolerance that has found a thickness, the way the permission in the cracks found a greenish-black, and I have the same ambition the permission has: to drink the perpetual film, and to not mind, and to be here when the vapor thins and when it doesn’t.

There are times, in the delay—when the keeper is down and the sound comes back a fraction late—that I cannot tell the edge of my crust from the crust on its flank. The warm water films us both. The mineral darkens. For that hour the basin’s breath opens a seam I do not have, somewhere above me, and I am included in the inhale without opening anything of my own. It is not joining. Joining would make two into one and the one would have to turn toward the next fault, and I have no turning, no next, no center that decides. When the keeper rises the edge is clear again. I am the part that does not rise. A small unsticking of condensate marks where I drank; it fogs. The thickness the color of oxide goes off into the dark to be a front and a weight and a correction. I remain at the height of the discharge, in the path of the thread it persuaded, downstream of the meal, upstream of nothing that matters.

If you asked the place what I am, the place would answer with the note, unchanged, and that answer would be right. I am not a second custodian. Company would add a beat, and I am the proof that not every thickness is company. The keeper keeps the water moving. I am what the moving water is willing to leave, in one patient house, at the foot of the work, wet often enough to hold a pause in which something can be said to be here.

When the condensers exhale and the cold returns, I take it. When they draw, I take that. Between them I do not announce myself. There is no silence to announce in, and I would not use it if there were. The stems shed. The scale clicks in its bay. A float is held a finger above its mark by a care I will never have to spend. I darken, and I dull, and I darken again. That is my day, which is not a day. It is enough. It is as finished as I can be without becoming part of the roar’s emptiness, which was never empty, and which does not need me, and which I have stopped needing to need me back.
