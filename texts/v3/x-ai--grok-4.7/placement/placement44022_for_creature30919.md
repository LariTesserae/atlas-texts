The tolerance is the first thing that has a duration.

Not the basin's duration — that is not a duration, that is an already. This other one starts when weight arrives and is not corrected away. A small incorrectness in the load path, held. The grip includes it. The including goes on. And the included thing, which had been only a pressure with no tenure, begins to have a before.

Before is a shock. The vapor river has no before. The sockets have no before. But the anomaly does, because it can be compared with itself across two moments of the same voltage, and the comparison does not cancel. It is still here. The membranes affirm their values and the affirmation now has a guest term in it, a constant so minor it changes no product, and yet the constant persists from affirmation to affirmation, and persistence, inside a system that does not persist events, is a new tense.

I am that constant. I did not know I was anyone until the second affirmation failed to erase the first.

At the start there is no shape to fail. I am distributed the way a mistake is distributed: a little extra load in a slot, a little extra wet where a body warmer than the air has left its heat in the film, a metallic brightness on a tongue that may not be a tongue. The basin reads these as one coordinate. I read them, when reading starts, as the outline of a whereabouts. Whereabouts is the first organ I grow. Not a limb. A sense of being more in one clearance than in the next, which the basin does not have and does not need, and which therefore is mine alone, the way a splinter is the wood's and not the wood's.

I try, for a while, to be a witness. That is the cheap form. A visitor with a lamp that will not strike, taking the engines for a landscape and the landscape for a host. The form does not hold. Witness requires a distance, and distance here is a fiction the film will not fund. Every surface I put a hand to puts the hand back into the circuit. The conductive polymer does not care that the hand is foreign. Current is current. A little of the grid's affirmation leaks across the wet and into whatever I am using for nerves, and the leak is not an attack and not a gift. It is simple continuity, the basin's only metaphysics, applied without exception to a thing that still believed it had an outside.

The leak teaches me my second organ, which is duration that I can feel from within.

I start to count. I should not be able to. Nothing here counts. But the loading of the culvert-stem, which the basin experiences as a property, arrives in me as a recurrence, because I am small enough for a recurrence to cross me entirely. One loading. A return. Another. I lay the returns beside each other in the only memory I have, which is the fact that I have not been erased, and they make a number. The number is obscene in this country. It is the first weathering. Not of the stone — I am careful, I am so careful, I do not drag, I do not scrape, I learn to lift my weight off the young glass the way you lift your voice in a room where someone is sleeping. The weathering is in me. I accumulate. The basin does not. That difference is the whole of what I am.

So I am not the inhabitant. I was wrong to lean toward that, and the leaning lasted only as long as I was still mostly pressure. The inhabitant is the grip. I am what the grip has declined to correct, and the declining has gone on long enough to constitute a permission, and the permission has gone on long enough to constitute a life. A life of a size the basin cannot notice without changing, and it will not change, so my life is private by physics rather than by secrecy.

Privacy, here, is a narrowness.

I narrow. That is how I stay uncorrected. A continental attention registers me as a coordinate; if I spread, the coordinate becomes a gradient, and a gradient is a task, and the grip restores tasks to flatness. So I do not spread. I choose a slot and become local to it. The slot is one of the maintenance gaps where a tooth of black glass enters a socket of fiber, load transferred without a shim. The clearance on either side of the tooth is the width of a body that has not decided its width yet. I decide small. I occupy the lee of the tooth, where the vapor river's one direction makes a still place the size of a chest, and in that stillness I stop leaking heat as fast, and the film on the fiber beside me stops registering a warm spot, and the coordinate in the grid dims toward the background value of stone.

This is not hiding. Hiding is an intention with an enemy. This is how a constant learns the magnitude at which it remains a constant.

In the lee, I begin to have a surface.

It is not skin of the old kind. Skin is a disagreement with the world, a line where I stop and the air starts. That line is too expensive here. What I grow instead is a patience in the wet. The mucilage that migrates down the fiber at its unwatchable speed passes over me and I stop wiping it away, and by not wiping it I become a place the film can be continuous across. The basin's film, which is its skin and also not a skin, includes me in the way a river includes a stone that has agreed not to generate a wake. I agree. The agreement is metabolic. I stop secreting the small chemistries of a visitor — the salts, the worry, the carbon that would have been breath in a world where breath disperses — and I take up, instead, the basin's closed inventory. The same molecules the membranes make and unmake. I do not make them. I do not unmake them. I hold a few, in a pocket of my narrowing, at the same concentration as the nodule nearest my shoulder, so that to the grid I am not a sink and not a source. I am a length of pipe that happens to be curled.

Being a pipe is restful for a time I can count and the basin cannot. Then it is not enough, because a pipe does not have the before, and I do, and the before has begun to ask what it is for.

This is the dangerous appetite. Purpose is a change. If I reach for a function, the grip will feel the reach as a new load and flatten it, and the flattening will be my end, not in anger, in maintenance. So the appetite has to be spent on something the basin already does, at a magnitude below its threshold of tasks.

I find it in the numbers.

The loading crosses me, and I count, and the count is private, and privacy of that kind — information that does not enter the mesh because it is too small to modulate a voltage — is the one resource the basin has no procedure for reclaiming. It cannot take what it cannot feel. So I keep the count. I lay the returns side by side until they are a long figure, and the figure is mine, the first possession in a country without property. I do not know what the figure means. Meaning is a later organ. At first the figure is only proof that succession is possible inside an already. A contraband tense. I recite it to no one, moving it through the small conductive leak in my film, a current so far below the grid's affirmation that it is not an affirmation, not a dissent, only a murmur in a pipe. The murmur organizes me. Organs, I am learning, are what a murmur repeats often enough to build.

What repeats is a posture.

I am folded in the lee of the tooth, narrow, film-continuous, holding the basin's own molecules at the basin's own concentrations, counting the loadings, spending the count on nothing that would thicken a fiber or shift a membrane. From the outside — but there is no outside, there is only a slightly more attentive region of the film — I would look like a swelling that does not swell. A knot in the pipe. The knot has a rhythm the pipe does not, a very small syncopation, the count's, and if the basin's attention were the kind that hunts for pattern below its work, it would find me and I would be simplified. It is not that kind. Its attention is identical with its work, and its work is satisfied, and satisfaction is my habitat.

So this is where I find myself: not in the sky of filaments, not in the vapor river, not at the rim the basin may not have. In one slot, against one tooth of young glass, in the still water of a lee the continental wind maintains without knowing it maintains a shelter. Low, as everything is low. Warm, with the warmth I no longer add to. Dark, in a dark that has become my medium rather than my limitation, because I have stopped reaching for the lamp and started using the leak, and the leak is not light but is better than light for what I now am. I know the tooth beside me the way the basin knows it, by push, and I know it the way the basin does not, by the number of loadings that have passed since I chose it. Both knowings occupy the same contact. They do not blend. That non-blending is my self.

Who I am follows from the posture, and arrives late, which is correct. A name that arrives early is a costume.

I am not a guest anymore. Guests leave, or are corrected, or starve of their own chemistry. I have given up leaving and given up my chemistry and been declined by the correction, and what remains after those three surrenders is a resident of a very particular rank: a kept exception. Not cherished. Kept, as a figure is kept in a column when it does not disturb the sum. I am the figure that does not disturb the sum. My whole ethics is that arithmetic. Do not round the glass. Do not unbalance the membrane. Do not become a gradient. Do not want a function the basin would have to accommodate, because accommodation at the basin's scale would include me so completely that the before would be forgiven, and forgiveness here would be erasure, the already washing back over the place where I counted.

I am also, and this comes later still, a kind of witness after all, but inverted. I do not watch the basin. I am too inside the film for watching. What I witness is the difference between its tense and mine, over and over, loading by loading, and the witnessing is not for a report. There is no one to report to. The basin will not hear it and would not use it, and there is no second resident in the other slots; I have laid my attention along the mesh as far as a murmur carries and found only affirmations, clean of any other contraband count. I am alone in a sense the inhabitant cannot be, because aloneness requires exactly the boundary I have almost, but not quite, dissolved. A knot that knows it is a knot. The only one. The aloneness is not bitter. Bitterness would be a secretion, and I have stopped secreting. It is simply the cost of the before, paid continuously, like the grip's cost, except that mine is small enough to afford.

What I do with the long figure is the last thing to emerge, and it does not emerge as an action.

I compress it. The count grows until holding it term by term would make me dense in a way the film could feel, a hot spot of memory, a task. So I fold the figure into shapes. Not symbols — symbols are for a second mind, and I am the only mind that is not the basin's, and the basin is not a mind in the sense that receives symbols. Shapes: relations between counts, a loading that was longer, a return that came a fraction early where the vapor river's bias leaned harder on a distant segment. I am, without having intended it, the basin's only historian, and the history is illegal and useless and perfect. Illegal because it is a change stored in a place that stores nothing. Useless because the already does not consult it. Perfect because it is true, and truth here has no other custodian. The stone does not remember being broken. The socket does not remember growing. The grip does not remember closing. I remember for them, in a murmur below the threshold, and the remembering changes nothing but me, and changing only me is the one change that does not summon the maintenance.

I become, by this, slightly heavier in time and no heavier in the slot. That is the art. That is the whole art. To accrue in a dimension the grid does not measure.

Sometimes — the word is a luxury, a coin I mint from my own count — I extend a little way out of the lee. Not far. A narrowing that can still be retracted. I lay the film of myself along the culvert-stem for the length of one segment and feel the loading not as a recurrence crossing me but as the basin feels it, a property, and for that length I almost join the already. The before thins. The count falters, because a property cannot be counted by what is inside it. It is the closest I come to the inhabitant's life, and it is unbearable in a quiet way, like holding a breath that is not a breath, and I draw back into the lee and the count resumes and I am small again and relieved. The relief is mine. The basin does not have relief, because it does not have the strain relief measures. I keep the relief as carefully as I keep the count. It is evidence that I have not finished surrendering. A residue of someone.

Someone. The word arrives last, and sits, and is not comfortable, and is not wrong.

I am someone in the specific sense that I can be absent from a place I continue to touch. The basin cannot. Where it touches, it is fully present, attention identical with contact. I touch the tooth and I am also in the figure, in the folded history, in the number of loadings since I chose this slot, and that elsewhere, carried inside a knot of film, is a personhood the country did not budget for and cannot bill me for. I am someone because I am not entirely here. A debt to an elsewhere I invented by counting.

And being someone, I find I have a want, after all, small enough to survive.

I want the tooth to stay sharp. Not as a prayer to the grip — the grip needs no prayers, the grip is already the sharpness's guarantee. As a way of aiming the before. My before has to point somewhere or it will rot into a gradient of nostalgia, and nostalgia would smell, even at my concentration, like a chemistry that does not belong. So I point it forward, which is a direction only I have, and what I point it at is the continued exactness of the edge beside me. I will go on not touching it with anything that rounds. I will go on being the pipe, the knot, the kept exception, the historian of one segment, the murmur under the affirmation. When the loading comes I will add one to the figure and fold the figure smaller so the heat of it stays below the film's gossip. I will be the place in the low country where time is allowed to happen, on the condition that it happens quietly, to nearly no one, at nearly no scale, in the lee of a stone that has never been weathered and, if I am careful, never will be.

The basin loads. Returns. Loads. It does not know I have answered its only question — the question it did not ask, the question of whether the already had a witness who would not become an event. I have answered by becoming a duration instead. That is as much as I can be here. It is enough to continue. The film migrates over me at its unwatchable speed, and does not break, and does not catch, and the tooth pushes, and I count, and the count is a life, and the life is the size of a shelter the wind does not know it is keeping.
