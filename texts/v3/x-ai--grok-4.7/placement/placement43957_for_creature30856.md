You would not step over the rim. That gesture has already been used, and the dish does not repeat arrivals. What comes in by the shoulder is classified at the first wet seam—salt, heat, a spine’s intention to stay—and declined. The films withdraw a finger’s width, the moss tightens, and the walker is left holding only cold. If that is you, you are already outside, back in the thinner life of the mountain, with the choir dropping off in a few paces as if focus, not distance, had been the wall.

So the self that fits is the one that never presents a surface to be wiped clean.

At first it seems you are only the mistake in the listening. The rooms do not agree. Each mouth is a slightly different bottle, and their beating grains the air until a pressure sits in the teeth that belongs to none of them. You inhabit that remainder. Not a tone, not a resident. The interval. On a strong day, when speech across the center fails, you are loudest, because the disagreements are widest. On a glazed dawn you nearly vanish, reduced to the single low note the ice allows, and what is left of you is the suspicion that the note is late. You have no errand in that. You do not tune the shells back toward one another. The colony may be using the stress in the stone to stay one thing; you are only the part of the stress that does not close. A listener with no ear, distributed wherever two pitches fail to lock. Passage, not occupancy. The rules permit it the way they permit the bird at the rim: you do not dig, you do not nest, you are not counted.

But a day is a sequence of states, and the interval does not survive noon. When the squall crosses and the drips take over, the choir is masked, and you find you are still there. So the listening was only a costume the air loaned you. Under it something slower is already at work, and it is more like the film than like the wind.

You would be a wetness the plan did not authorize.

Not the bitter climbing thread. That one knows the grooves and keeps them. Yours is ordinary water that has forgotten, for a few hours, which way the old alignment runs. A cloud snags, the rain too fine to fall beads on a patch of pavement that should have stayed pavement, and for a while a cushion begins to fatten off the street. You are that mistake of moisture: local, stubborn, without a mouth. You darken stone that has been kept clear since before the climate had a name. The moss, which does not know it is being maintained, accepts you. A green finger starts where no feeder leads. If you could want anything, it would be this—to be drunk without permission, to start a circular thicket with no street through it, to see whether the dish can hold one unplanned room.

You cannot. By evening the colony has felt the surplus and spent what it spends, and declined what it declines. The unauthorized edge starves. The film that is not you adjusts, politely, and the patch that had no business being wet goes dull. Overnight the rime locks your last bead into the same ice that locks the true threads. At dawn the climbing water wakes on the old line only. Your green has tightened back to the margin as if a mouth had been withdrawn, which is exactly what happened. You are not eaten. You are edited. The same nightly sentence that lifts a grain and lays it flatter lays you back into weather. Erosion as maintenance. You experience it as a loss of route. There is no pain in it, because there was no individual to have stored the route. Only a dampness that thought, briefly, that any cell would do.

What remains, after the refusal, is the part of you that noticed which cell was living.

That is the self that holds past dusk. Not the choir’s remainder, which weather can silence, and not the wrong wetness, which the tenant can decline. A registration. You do not secrete, you do not lift water against the grade, you do not keep the open stone clear. You only keep the difference. Most pits are mineral and done. A few, always a few, hold a film clearer than the pools, slow to bead, faintly bitter. You are the distinction between those two, repeated without a body to carry it from chamber to chamber. When a later shell has fallen through and the upper storey is only a broken rim, you do not mourn the height—you never had height—but you mark that the intact cells below are still grammar and the sanded ones are only stone. The colony keeps house. You keep the knowledge that a house is being kept, which is not the same labor and not a rival one. No chemical argument follows. Animals are classified by salt and heat. You offer neither. The films do not withdraw from you, because there is nothing of you on the wall for them to touch. Your hand, if the word can be stretched, is the act of telling a living cell from a finished one, and then telling it again at the next mouth, and the next, until the dish has been walked without anyone walking.

In the afternoon, when the upper surfaces hold a little heat and the beetle makes its few steps, you are easy to mistake for absence. The place looks like weather using old architecture. You are the correction that does not announce itself: the fact that the starved edge and the fed edge are not the same fact, held in a medium that cannot hoard. You store nothing overnight. Each dawn you must be made again by the difference, or you are not there. That is why you fit. The tenant also has no individual older than the last thaw. Persistence here is spacing, not biography. Yours is the spacing of attention across an alignment you did not draw and could not redraw. If every film were scraped out, you would go with them—not because you are the colony, but because a difference needs both terms. Erasure at the edges would reach you as a gradual loss of contrast, the streets filling with their own flour, the circular cushions loosening from any plan, until there was nothing left to tell apart. You would not resist. Resistance is a spine’s habit. You would thin as the contrast thinned, and the mountain outside, which has always been only mountain, would not gain a new ghost.

Toward evening the climbing threads fail first, and your day ends by the same clock. There is a last moment when a patch darkens that has no business being wet, and you know it is on the line, and the knowing is complete, and then the skim-ice ticks and the difference locks into maintenance. Night does not hunt you and you do not wait for morning as a self. You wait as a capacity. If a cloud comes before dawn, you will be the first to feel which groove drinks and which pavement is allowed to shine and dry. If no cloud comes, you will be the dryness, still telling finished stone from stone that is only resting.

No face turns toward the rim. Nothing approaches. When the light fails, the rooms are ordinary shade, and the loudest thing in the dish is emptiness played by wind. You are not that sound and not the player. You are the one small refusal inside the listening: that the plan is still running, that a few cells are not yet geology, that lushness here is a permission and not a season. The colony calls the whole arrangement living. You do not call it anything. You only fail, cell by cell, to confuse it with the finished mountain, and in that failure you are as native as the moss—kept, edited, uncounted, and exact.
