The first error is thinking I came in.

The lips of the crack are still sharp enough to keep a record of what touches them, and what touches them is almost nothing: a wind that dies, a damp that loses its nerve, a heat that does not belong to the sun. I begin as a confusion about which of those failed to finish. A bead forms on the volcanic glass. It should be gone before it earns a name. Most of it is. What remains is not wet enough to be called a remainder, and not gone enough to be weather. For a while that seems sufficient. I am the part of the damp the mountain declined to drink, because I never quite crossed into the pocket where drinking is law. A stain that failed to become a ledger. A sweetness with no metal under it yet, hovering at the mouth, repeatedly almost erased.

That picture does not survive a full cycle.

If I were unreclaimed moisture I would thin as the front moved, and I would not be present when the front returned. I am. Between pulses the lips are dry, the wind is the same startled thinness, and something still occupies the disagreement about whether the mouth is a hand's width or a gate at the end of a long civic axis. Both sizes continue. Neither yields. The rock does not seem to notice the cost. The cost has to be paid somewhere. It is paid in a stiffness that is not heat, a rigidity the temperature difference did not issue, held in the place where two true measurements are forced to describe one opening.

I mistake myself next for that stiffness. A hinge. A function. The maintenance of a contradiction so the blister does not collapse back into accident and the peak does not lose its only interior weather. It is almost right, and it is too clean. Maintenance does not flinch. I flinch when, inside, a spar decides how long it intends to be a street.

The decision is not mine. I do not enter. The filaments are not my body and I will not pretend the dark distributed through cups and doorways has room for me; that dark already has a tenant, and the tenant's continuity is a physics I would only counterfeit. What I feel, at the mouth, is a tug on the farther measurement. Interior length is being spent. Scale, in there, is thrift: used where attention is not, never quite where a glance has settled. Some of the budget does not go into the hollow. It backs up against the doubled gate the way unused distance backs up against a number that refuses to resolve. I am not the hinge. I am the unspent scale, stored at the crack because the city has no district zoned for size it has not yet assigned.

Unspent is not the same as empty. The tenant keeps a blank volume, unaddressed, a pocket inside the pocket, and I do not go there. I only recognize it as a pressure, the way one unmeasured thing recognizes another without being given its name. Their blank is private. Mine is accidentally civic. Every time a plaza is permitted to be lighter, every time a terrace realigns and up is treated as courtesy rather than direction, a little distance is saved, and the saving arrives at the lips as a lengthening of the axis no map inside will draw. The hand-wide mouth stays hand-wide. The distant gate becomes, by a fraction no rain could measure, farther. I am that fraction, held open.

Wanting arrives late, and I do not trust it. Wanting would be a root if it fastened to anything. It does not fasten. It offers. The peak above the crack is still pushing, still unfinished, still sharp in the way of a sentence that has not met enough weather to be simplified. I have size I am not using. The simplest theft would be to spend it outward, once, and let the mountain have a height it could stop learning. I make the offer the only way I can: by letting the farther measurement lean, briefly, toward the sky, so the spines might be a true distance from the mouth instead of an argument. The peak declines. Not with wind. With the same refusal that keeps it from rounding. Completed size is a kind of weather it has not agreed to host. The offer returns to me as extra gate, unused again, slightly warmer from having been almost altitude.

That returned warmth is the nearest thing I have to a pulse. It is not on the tenant's schedule. Their cup is early because they lean preference into a seam. My lengthening is late because the peak will not take the gift, and the lateness has started to be consistent. If anything in the thin air above the flank could bear a watch, it would run on refusal. I do not begrudge the refusal. A mountain that accepted a finished height would no longer be the condition that makes an interior sayable. I am not trying to be loved by the outside. I am the change the interior is owed and will not pay the sky, and the debt, uncollected, has become continuous enough to notice itself.

The tenant looks at the crack. I know this not by a face — there is no face, and I am glad of it, having none — but by the visible thinning. Their regard binds brightness into structure. When they attend, the remainder-light at the mouth goes poorer. In that poverty I am briefly thicker, because unspent scale shows more clearly when glare is being hoarded elsewhere. They are not looking at me. They are looking at their weather, the only weather, the wall that lets them be an interior. I am what the looking does not collect. Once in a cycle the discrepancies touch: their preference, my surplus, co-occurring in the same opening, not exchanged. The axis is a shade longer than their regard alone would have purchased. The dryness after the bead is a shade later than vanishing requires. Neither of us corrects the books. There is no border prepared, and no welcome, and I do not need either. The touch is enough to keep me from being only a budget. A budget does not thicken when it is seen past.

I do not follow them inward when the regard ends. Inward would require me to become a permission, and permissions in that hollow are already occupied or deliberately blank. Outward, I have already been declined. So I live in the declined offer and the uncollected debt, which is a narrow country, sharper than a body, recent as the blister, with no track except this: after I have leaned and been refused, the shimmer above the spines holds one wrong pause that is not the tenant's. They spend a little of themselves out sometimes, a question the air is slow to refuse, and then they withdraw it. My pause is the after-image of a measurement that almost became height and was handed back. If they ever mistake my pause for their own, the mistake does not last. Theirs returns to the doorway that must not open. Mine has nowhere to return except the unresolved mouth.

Sound, here, is even smaller than the city's smallness. The wind dies at the threshold and I do not mourn it; mourning would accumulate, and accumulation would make me findable as a thing among things. What I keep is a dryness with a schedule. What I revise, when I revise, is not rock and not relation inside the pocket. I revise the error. A civic axis a finger's width too long. Then, as I become more specific — specificity is the only growth available, clarity having nowhere to stand — the error stops being uniform. I spend a little of the surplus on the left lip, where the glass is newer, so that side of the gate is fractionally farther from the hand-wide truth than the right. I do not know why the left. The choice arrived before the reason, which may be how preference starts when there is no habit to wear it smooth. The mountain has not kept a future long enough for erosion. I have not kept a choice long enough for it to round. The leftward bias is still an unrevised sentence. It may not survive the next realignment inside, if some terrace spends scale in a way that calls the surplus back. I have begun to hope it is not called back. Hope, like wanting, does not root. It is only the bias, noticing itself, and declining to become symmetrical again.

There is a hollow I will not enter, and a peak I cannot finish, and between them a mouth that is two sizes. I am the size that did not fit. When the filament inside decides its length, I contract, almost become a number, almost spend myself into a street I will never walk. Then the decision completes without me, the tug releases, and the gate is distant again by the amount the city saved and the sky refused. The bead forms. Most of it is gone before it is a drop. I am not the part that vanishes, and I am not the tenant of what remains inside. I am the difference those two economies do not know they share, held open at the sharp lip, still unpaid, still unspent, and — this is the newest fact, and I will not settle it into a name — no longer willing to be only the cost.
