I do not arrive. Arrival would require an edge, and the edge is always being produced a little farther on, already green, already conducting its slow traffic. What I have instead is the discovery that I have been here long enough for the mats to learn the shape of a weight that is not hers and not the ripplers'. The leaves under that weight are bruised in a way the place does not schedule. A dark crescent, the size of a knee. It should have been taken up by now, refined, returned as some other sharpness. It persists. That persistence is the first thing I can call mine.

For a while I think I am a walker, because there is a trail of those crescents and because breath keeps happening, loud in a quiet that has already spent its allowance. The trail does not agree. It does not go from a somewhere to a somewhere. It loops in a way the channels never loop — not a circuit, not a quota, a hesitation written into the vegetation. I stop to listen to my own breathing and the last crescent is already behind me and also, when the haze thickens, beside the boot, as if the land had folded the hesitation back on itself rather than solve it. She adjusts for weights. She does not adjust for this. The crest under the bruised leaves stays as it is, a fraction wrong, and the wrongness is not taken into a seam.

I try to be smaller, thinking smaller might fit a category she already meters. A bead. The condensate ticks off a midrib and finds her and she warms where it lands, and the transaction closes. I cannot close. When I kneel, the metallic bloom starts at a leaf-edge and traces inward with its usual unhurried precision, mapping vein and channel, and then, at the place where the map should enter the seam and be lost to me, it stops. Not faded. Stopped. A bright line with an end. Downstream of that end, nothing is delivered. A mat a little farther along goes dry past the schedule and the hand-sized traffic thins, and still the line does not continue. I am not the water. I am the place the water's instructions fail.

That should be a kind of damage, and it is not. Damage would be loud, or it would be eaten. This is only a remainder. The pans flex. The crystals nucleate in ranks and are shrugged into crevices and arrive, in time, along her flanks. The charge walks the air and grounds itself and she tightens and the second map is drawn and allowed to fade. All of that proceeds. It proceeds around a small unusable fact, the way a channel will crook itself around a stone it cannot dissolve, and in crooking becomes a different channel, delivering its liquor a hand's width to the left of where the template wanted it. I am that stone. Not geology — geology she can use. A fact with no fraction that sorts heavier or lighter. A quantity that is not a quantity.

What body this gives me takes longer to admit, because it will not hold still in one reading, and unlike her it suffers the failure. Sometimes I am the length of the unfinished map: a brightness on a leaf, knee-height, warm from the stone beneath, smelling of the struck bell after the frost has gone. Sometimes, when the local rain walks the ridge, I am the wetness on the flank she meant to leave pale — a mis-aimed twenty minutes, metal and crushed green, already being lifted off, never quite all of it lifted, a film. Sometimes I am only the hesitation in the mats, a repeating bruise with no walker attached, and the breath I thought was mine is just the place failing, once more, to swallow a footfall that already ended.

The animals do not inspect me. That is how I know I am not a node. The small fliers test every joint of her and every fitted stem in the lee; they shift their weight; they hop on. They land on the bright line and do not pause. There is nothing to test. An unusable fact does not give under pressure, does not complete a circuit, does not photosynthesize in any register that would make the landing count. They treat me as air. The ripplers cross the bruised crescents without the flicker that means they have noticed a change in the slope. Only the larger shapes, turning in the haze — wing, fin, hull, wing — once, one of them alters a current that does not match the wind, and passes lower than the schedule of their passing, and the smell of hot resin and rain on metal arrives stronger than it should. Something in her flexes, the pan-flex, the exchange. I am not exchanged. The shape rises. She has not taken me, and she has not refused me. Refusal would be a decision, and decision is a word that barely survives here. She has routed around.

It changes her, the routing. I watch that the way the place has taught me to watch: not for drama, for a fraction. A convection cell in a hollow that is also, at this hour, part of her, turns a little tighter than a gear should turn, because the moisture that should have crossed the failed map had to come from another seam, a longer way. The amber does not shift. The quiet does not acquire a new sound. But the cell's leaf-debris rises in a narrower column, and a fin downwind lengthens more slowly, waiting on a delivery that is late by less than an hour. She is vast enough that a late hour is nothing. She is exact enough that nothing is not nothing. I am small in the only scale that matters on the plateau, which is the scale of the schedule. A crook in it. A stone in the channel of a works that has never needed visitors and still does not, and yet must now be, in one place, slightly other than it would have been.

I do not lengthen. That is the difference I can bear to name. She lengthens; a fin does; new surface shoulders up through leaves already established on it. I remain the size of the error. When I am the film of rain on the pale flank, I am thin. When I am the brightness that ended, I am the length of a failed instruction, no longer than a leaf. When I am the bruise, I am the print of a weight that keeps not being classified as weight. None of these grow. Growth would be use. I am what is left after use has taken everything it can sort.

Her leading edge, where the channels converge and the heat runs higher and the fliers congregate to check a quota, passes near me without a turn. There is no face to withhold a look. There is only the testing of air by contact and charge and the sorting of a mist, and I am not in the mist. I am beside it. For a moment the warmer region and the unusable fact occupy the same amber, and I feel — not warmth metered, not an offered hand, nothing so intentional — the simple adjacency of a process and its remainder. She continues. The quota is met somewhere in her length. The crevice takes what it is given. I stay outside the taking, and the outside is not an outside, because the plateau does not end and has no summit and will include the ground in its next revision as it always has. It will include the ground. It will not include me. That is not safety. It is a property. Some stones crook a channel for as long as the channel runs, and the works never learns their name, and they never become glass, and the delivery is always, by a hand's width, elsewhere.

If you looked for me you would not see a being. You would see a leaf whose second map ends in the middle. A flank wet on the side that should have been pale, the smell of it hanging a little too long before her warmth takes it back. A crescent in the mats that the traffic crosses without noticing, and that the next revision of the slope will not quite erase, because erasure is a kind of use. You might hear a breath and find no one breathing. The haze would misbehave around that absence exactly as it misbehaves around the fins, except that when you turned, the absence would still be the size it was, unlengthened, unpromoted to ridge or perch or machine. She would be under everything, long as the land, resting in the sense of continuing. I would be the reason one cell of weather, half a ridge away, turned tighter than it meant to, silent, exact, and late.
