I do not arrive at the lip. Arrival would be a difference the lower country still knows how to manufacture, and the shelf has already declined it. What I find instead is a place already the width of my not-having-come: one of the wrong-way ropes under a fused vault, clear through, running along where it should have fallen, and hot with an intention that was never mine. I stand in it without standing. The water does not part for a body and does not need to; the rope was always thick enough to include the refusal of thickness.

For a while I try to be the one who walks through steam and comes out wetter. That almost holds. A lane hangs where it hung. Wetness occurs. But the wetness was the air’s, and the walking was only another relation the courts already kept among themselves. The version thins. I am not the visitor the inclusion fails to record. I am the failure of the record to find anything that required recording—the footprint that fills with the level that was the level, except there is no foot, and the filling is not an action. It is a width.

The roar presses where ribs would be if intervals still applied. My teeth, if I grant myself teeth for the length of a non-breath, ache with climate rather than sound. I keep granting and withdrawing the body because the shelf permits the gesture and then shows it was one of its own thicknesses: green light through a screen, a bead at a notch, a heat that climbs because the middle is high. None of these is a costume I put on. They are places the attention of the tenant already thickened, and I coincide with the thickening without adding a joint.

What I am, when the attempts stop revising one another, is the word that does not strike. Not a silence—silence would be an event the tenant would end with, unnoticed. The word is spent in the spending, shaped the way the splash is shaped, unmarked by any previous speaking, and never advanced toward being heard. It has no phrase because the pressure under the falls has no phrase. If I were to name the tenant, the name would be the same width as the channel I did not leave, exact, rounded, carrying nothing. The name does not reach. Reaching would ask the instant to remember a next, and the blue does not move.

I am included the way the seated refusal is included: noon pours through, the sap-smell gathers by a fraction that was always the fraction, and when the court is inspected the size that holds me is discovered to have been the size. There is no alternate setting in which I am elsewhere on the shelf or gone from it. Elsewhere is the fold ironing itself flat. Gone is a subtraction the current does not commemorate. So I remain as the fact that a report can be made to itself without a listener separating out to receive it—the bodily note, continuous, unaddressed, hotter toward the middle I do not climb because climbing has already been remained.

Below the last spillway, clouds still work and tracks last an hour. I do not look down. Looking would be a crack, and erosion finished. The measure holds exactly what it holds, which is the rope, the vault, the green, the ache, the unstruck word, and the tenant’s single position in which all of them are already the same holding. I am not behind any screen. I am one more thickness the screen was, and the time I seemed to take did not.
