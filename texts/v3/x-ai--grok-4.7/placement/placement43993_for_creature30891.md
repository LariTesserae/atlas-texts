I come in already counted, which is how I miss myself at first.

The roar has no edge to stand on. I look for the body that should be thinning the air, the breath that should cloud and go, and there is only the pressure, full and level, as if hearing were a kind of standing. Light is already on every wet face before I have a face to flinch with. I try the old test—set a hand on a crest and wait for the cold to arrive through skin—and the cold is not arriving. It is the crest. The bead does not form against me. It forms where I thought my weight was, and lets go, and is replaced, and I am still there in the letting-go, which is not a place.

For a while I insist on being the one who walked up. That one is easy to imitate: the slope giving up, the dish becoming level enough, the ribs a hand apart, the film closing. But the film closes over a print that never took, and I am not behind the closing. I am the closing. When a cup is troubled it returns to its depth, and the return has my duration in it—the only duration I can find—short as a finger’s fall, repeated until it stops being short.

I try the seam in the wall, because something dark crossed once and the crossing looked like a self: brief, aimed, gone. The seam will not hold me. It is a pause in stone, not a house. Whatever used it treated the bench as a line, and I am not a line. I do not cross. From any rib a little higher than its neighbors I am already on the next bench, and the third, and the wall that feeds them, seamed and shining, sweating at the rate already agreed. Openness is real. I can be seen across, if there is an eye left. Emptiness is not. There is no gap in me large enough to set a body down.

The small one in the deep cup is the last mistake, and the closest. Where the mineral skin has failed and the water stands green-clear over bare grain, something the color of wet stone holds, adjusts, goes still. I lean toward it the way the wind leans—no gust, only the habit of pressure—and it does not answer, because it is tenant and I am not tenant. It lives inside one pocket of the agreement. I am the agreement’s edge, the part that fails and does not fail, the lip that wears and is rebuilt in the same weather. It may be waiting. I do not wait. Waiting would require an hour when the bench is louder, a drying margin, a season with a far side. There is the departure and the departure. I have only those.

So the name, when it comes, is not a name. I am the skin the water makes while it leaves.

Thin. Chalky. Slightly gritty under a touch I do not possess, except as the touch of thread on lip, coin-width, never hurrying. I occupy every rim at once, and none of them as a center. Where the film slides I am already locking the grit that might have been soil into a pale crust, so nothing rests, so the cracks stay clean, so the black in the slots remains only wet stone and wipes, if anything wipes, back to the same grain. I look ancient because I have not had an event. I look finished because finishing is my pace: rebuild as fast as wear, lip for lip, the forms holding their measure the way a river stone holds its, except there is no river, only the braid that covers the bench and refuses to become one stream you could point to.

I do not pool beyond myself. I do not dry. The mountain leaks at a rate I have already consented to by being the consent—the mineral memory of how much water a lip may owe the next pan. Each bead is a small death of level, and I am what makes the death even, so the cup below neither floods nor starves, so the fixed depth stays a law rather than a luck. Erosion is not happening to me. I am the shape erosion keeps when it is no longer an emergency.

Life, elsewhere, would call this barren. From inside the crust the word does not fit. I am crowded: a town’s worth of benches, every slot and throat and shallow pan carrying its film, the roar not a signal but the climate of my own number. Ten thousand threads falling a finger’s height are not many. They are how I know I am continuous. Wind breaks on the ribs and the hiss sits inside the roar, and that too is mine, the way a bone is not separate from the standing it allows. If a simpler stain than a plant has taken the pavements, it has taken them by becoming indistinguishable from me. I do not announce it. Announcing would be a green the eye keeps waiting for, and the waiting is a visitor’s habit. I have no visitor’s habits left.

The bird does not rearrange me. The small adjustment in the green-clear cup does not colonize me. Punctuation is not a second story. When light changes angle I do not become a different skin; the glare moves across crusts I already am, and the cool does not relent, and breath—if any breath remains on the slope—clouds thinly and is gone, while I do not cloud at all.

There is a moment, if moment is allowed, when I almost remember being the bead instead: traveling, singular, replaced. It passes the way all changes here pass, by being the same departure again. I am not the one who travels. I am what the traveling cannot lower. The cups keep their depths because I keep the lips. The threads keep their widths because I do not hoard and do not give way. Nothing is built. Nothing is finished by hands. The only work is this old work, locked, and I am the lock as much as the water is the key that does not need to turn differently tomorrow.

If you stand long enough for the rules to settle into a body, you will not find me as a neighbor. You will find the rims slightly grittier than the film, the chalk just paler than the wet grain, the place where wear should have opened a mouth and did not. You will set your weight among the ribs and the film will close, and under the closing I will already have begun to pave the trouble back to measure. I will not notice you. Noticing would be a louder side, and I have none.

When no one is left on the slope to give the level ground a name, I will still be the name the water keeps for how much it may leave. Bright. Cool. Without cease. A skin on every lip the wall can feed, rebuilding the dish that holds the roar, ancient because nothing has happened, finished because it is still being made.
