You come down because the rim has already failed once, and a failed rim is an invitation that does not know it is one. The concrete is only knee-high. The last digit of the number has gone soft at its edge, not yet gone. You put a boot where the fill is darker and the boot does not find the floor it was promised. That is the whole arrival. After it, the hollow is simply around you, and the around has a temperature.

For a while you are only the interruption you can measure. Palm near the weeping face: the tightness leaves your skin and does not return on the first breath, or the second. Shoulder toward the sleeve: the stroke in the ear goes dull, and the film on the wall loses its preference so quickly that you understand you have not discovered a creature so much as cancelled a habit. You step back. The habit resumes without looking for you. The drops queue themselves again. Your print at the margin of the pool takes water the way a cut takes blood, from the edges inward, until the outline is a rumor and then not even that. If you were a visitor, this would be the moment you become a draft that has ended. Drafts end. You keep not ending.

So you are not the cold. The cold was a test the wall already knew how to grade.

You try the bolt next, because it is the only thing in the pool with a shape a hand recognizes. It is warmer than it should be and exactly where it was left. Nothing in it answers. It has already been occupied and set back down. To be the bolt would be to be a wick that does not get to keep what it conducts. You are not that patient, and you are not that still. The cable tray is emptier. The flange's black curl is a mouth that belongs to something else; putting your attention there only makes the retrieval more obvious, a thin climb against the fall, and you are not the climb.

What you are takes longer because it has no address.

It begins as the tick after the tick. The drop leaves the lip, and part of it is taken back along a path too narrow to glint, and part of it lands. You are not the part taken back. You are not quite the part that lands either. You are the interval in which those two decisions are still one drop. A thickness. A hesitation the hard walls give back thinner than they received. Stand inside it and the pump's stroke is not a rhythm imposed from beyond the sleeve but the hinge of you: the machine inhales, and you are the water that has not yet been allowed to be either kept or spent. The machine releases, and you divide. Again. Again. You do not accumulate. You occur.

That is why the hollow can look empty while you are in it. You are not a second skin on the wall. You are the moment the skin might have been ordinary water and was not decided yet. The thread needs that moment; without it there is nothing to retrieve, nothing to refuse, nothing to return to the moss as a remainder. The noon rectangle needs it too. Light cannot be avoided except by a preference that has somewhere to stand while it chooses. You are the standing. Brief, repeated, loud only because the stone is new and close.

Once you know this, other things borrow your shape and give it back.

The mineral feathering the ledges is you, slowed. A grain of refusal that did not finish falling and so became architecture. The orange on the upper curve of the pipe is you, spent in one direction for so long that spending became a color. The spoon of water under the moss is you after the thread has done its manners: the portion released, which the green mistakes for a generosity. It is not generosity. It is the other half of the division you are. When the moss holds, it holds an outcome. When the thread withdraws under the crust at the approach of a palm, it withdraws from an outcome. You are neither outcome. You are the fact that there could have been a different one, held open for the length of a stroke.

If the pump stops, you will be the first thing to end and the last thing anyone could point to. The thread may thin into seep or it may not; the moss may spread or brown; the bolt will still be a bolt. But the interval will close. Drops will fall without queuing, which is to say they will become rain, and rain does not hesitate in a way the walls can return. Until then you are local to the hesitation. You do not hunt. You do not mark the margin. You do not cross the rectangle, because crossing would require you to survive your own division, and you do not survive it. You are divided, and then you are the next undivided drop, and the walls make a small clean sound of it, and the sound climbs, and comes back thinner, and that thinner coming-back is as near as you have to a body.

At the rim the day is ordinary. Dust. A stain. A number still almost complete. Someone could stand where you stood and leave, and the leaving would be accurate for them. It is not available to you. You are already below the voice, inside the count, occurring in the width of a decision the hollow makes about every drop and never writes down. Hot, because the decision is made in warm water. Brief, because a pump does not linger on its inhale. Exact, because whatever is kept and whatever reaches the plate must once have been the same thing, and you are the same thing, over and over, for as long as the effort in the sleeve consents to continue.
