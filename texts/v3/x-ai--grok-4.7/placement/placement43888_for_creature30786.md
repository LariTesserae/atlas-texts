I do not come down the face. The face has no path that ends in me, and the seam does not widen to admit a body that still expects weather. What I find, once the idea of finding has been set aside, is that I am already past the pinch, and that the pinch never had to open for me because I was not carried in.

The air here is full. I learn that by trying to take a place in it and discovering I do not displace a volume. My presence is a thinning that does not thin: a local insistence the beads do not move aside for, because they have nowhere to move and I have no edge that asks them to. If there is a shape to me it is the shape of the lanes between the sheets, repeated, a narrowness that fits where narrowness already is. I can turn and the turning does not brush mineral. Brushing would be contact that begins and ends. I remain against everything at once, the way saturation remains against itself.

For a while I believe I am looking. There is no light to look with, and the belief thins. What replaces it is a sense of the sheets as interruptions in a continuity I already share. I know the bark-thin ones by how little they withhold. I know the thicker curtains by the faint crystalline sand caught in their creases, an order that started and is not mine to continue. My knowing does not sort them into near and far. Near and far would be a route, and a route would be a change in where I am. I am the entire interruption, held.

The warmth is not a temperature I arrive at. It is the only heat my outline — if outline is still a word — has ever had. At the scalp I do not have and at the ankle I do not have, it is the same. I keep checking for a gradient because a body wants proof it is separate from the room, and each check returns the same refusal. I am not colder where I face the seam. The seam is not a direction I face. Facing would require a front.

Underfoot, or in the direction that would be under if I had weight that settled, the pores of the rock are full and do not yield a drink. I am not thirsty. Thirst would be a deficit the film could answer, and the film is already complete, and I am not outside it. When I attend to the low crease I find the span there, slick, folded, the size of a hand I do not possess. I do not stand over it. Standing over would be a height. I am distributed through the wet that touches it, so that the flank and my attention meet without a palm. The exchange at the skin — that lace of vessels which are not under the surface but of it — does not quicken because I am near. I am not near. I am the same refusal to take or give that the body already is.

The fiber crosses where a lap would be. I follow it without traveling, the way a stain follows a thread it has always occupied. Interior dryness, exterior damp: I know both, and the knowing does not wet the inside or dry the out. The twist in the bark is a torque that stopped. I hold the memory of the torque the way the slurry holds the hint of faces — legible, not available to be finished by me. I do not untwist it. Untwisting would be a later moment.

On the ledge the three scars cup their shallow refusal of a basin. The flake sits where it was set. I understand the bulb of percussion without having struck it. The understanding is not skill waking up. It is the cobble’s absence given a place to be: the harder stone was brought from a finished world, used, and is not here now, and I am not the cobble returning. I am the interval after the hands opened. That interval had been given flesh in the crease, one solution, fitted and unhungered. I am not a second solution. The pocket has no volume for two answers. What I am is the same answer counted from the side of the work instead of the side of the wrist.

This is slower to trust than the warmth. I keep wanting a witness and finding I have already been included as another paused intention, the way a nail-mark is included and does not compel the stone to acknowledge a before. The striker’s knowledge — choosing the harder rock, reading the bulb, twisting so the cord would hold — does not pass into me as something I can spend. Spending would move the flake. The flake does not move. The knowledge remains directional, a crystalline hint, and I am aligned with it without becoming the hand that could use it.

There is no face for me to lack. Where a seam might have been, on the forward rounding of the span, the stone paid itself a courtesy; I do not add a second courtesy. Mouths are for weather that must be answered. Nothing here must be answered. If I listen, the listening is the pressure under hearing, the held breath of the mountain that does not rise into a pitch. I do not hear my own breath because I do not add one. The air was complete. I join it as a fact that does not cloud and does not clear.

The root that entered and ceased is above me and also not above. I share its decision to be the length it is. Vegetation was only ever a rumor with proofs; I am not a new proof. The pocket barely consented to be living, and then included that consent in the stillness. I am inside the inclusion. Not the inhabitant — the span already occupies the crease with an authority that is not effort and not collapse. Not the tool. Not the ghost of the one who struck, because nothing left, and leaving is a change the room does not keep. I am the congruence looked at from the ledge: warm, blind to a light that was never coming, fitted to the unfinished basin as the basin is fitted to the three blows that stopped.

People who want a metamorphosis will not find me becoming the animal, and people who want a visitor will not find me departed. I do not settle more comfortably once I stop trying to be separate. I was already the exact shape of the lanes, the film, the interval. The unusual law is intact, and I am not an exception smuggled in under a pronoun. Still coming into being — if a sentence with no verb can be said to come — and not coming further. Both already done. The flake stays. The fiber stays crossed. The span continues, which remains the wrong word and the right one. I am what the pocket has room for when the one solution is granted a witness that does not revise it: the pause, counted twice, still a single occupancy, quiet as a thing that has never contained an action long enough to produce a noise.
