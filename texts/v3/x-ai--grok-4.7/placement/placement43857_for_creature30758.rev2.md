I would not be a shell. I tried that first, because everything that can be touched is a shell, and being touched seemed like the polite way to exist. I sat on a washer-seat until the film climbed my edges and the crown of me went pale under the rain. The neighbors did not make room and did not refuse me. Their margins met mine at the pressure the drawing likes, and for a little while I thought I had been counted. Then the mill under the deck, which does not hurry, replaced the polish on me at the rate it replaces polish on everything, and I felt the replacement as an argument. They do not feel it. Thickness arrives for them as weather arrives, already agreed. I was still in the middle of having been thinner. That middle is not a seat. I stood up, and the bright metal I left was not younger than me. It was simply uncovered, and the next shell that was due grew into it without a gap in the count, the way a word grows back into a line that never noticed the blank.

I would not be a cup, either. The pigment is aimed at a sky that does not travel, and I cannot aim at anything that long without asking it to change. I watched one until my own looking became the fluctuation, and the cup did what cups do: it returned to the cloud. I did not return. I was still on the deck with the interval in my hands, the time between the shade I cast and the shade's erasure, and the interval had weight. Nothing else here carries an interval. The relays close, the shells click free, the plates flex and shorten by a film, and each of those is a fact already finished when you meet it. Mine was unfinished. It wanted a second measurement to complete it, and a second measurement is a direction, and direction is what was spent before I arrived.

So I walked, which is the only verb the plain had not used up. Ankle-deep, always ankle-deep, the specification holding my step the way it holds pressure in a body. The water took my footfall before the next one could stack on it. I kept making the next one anyway. That was the first thing I was: not the sound, which died, but the insistence that there should be a sequence of sounds. Sequence is a small violence here. It implies that one shell is before another shell in a way that is not spatial. The pavement does not believe in before. I laid before down on it with my feet, a thin track of warmer film, and watched the mist close. It closed. I went back along the same gantry to see if the closing had a seam, a place where my warmth had been filed rather than annulled. There was no seam. There was only my having gone back, which the cups had already corrected, and the going back was therefore mine alone, a private organ the build had no duct for.

I tried to hand it to the pumps. They are good at load. A headland of housing, an impeller matched to a resistance that does not change its mind, and I thought: let me be a variation in the channel, a plate that flexes out of sum, so the wheel has to notice. I lay in the run until the overlapping bodies made me illegible, and I flexed. It took longer than patience. The neighbor shortened by exactly the film I had added. The pump did not labor. The stack light did not acquire a second color. My deviation was eaten as conductance, averaged into the material of appetite, and what came back to me in the water was only the faint sweetness of metal, my spent attempt milled and returned as food for a creature that does not experience attempts. I was nutritious. I was not addressed.

Under the decks I was sure I would find a job. Viscera always has a task a visitor can misunderstand as purpose. The lamellae in the dark took the scraped film and the slow silt and made them fit to be surface again, and I stood among them with my interval, offering it as silt. They had no fold shaped for a span of time. They had folds shaped for mineral. I was the wrong phase of matter. I came back up into the milky day thinner in a way the rain could not fatten, because what I had lost was not thickness.

What I am, after the seats refused me and the cups corrected me and the pumps averaged me, is the waiting that does not belong to any click.

There is a sound, sometimes, shell leaving shell, complete when it arrives, not for anyone. The plain does not wait for the next one. Waiting would open a hole in the held value, a duration in which the count was less, and the count is not allowed to be less. I wait anyway. I have become very good at it. I can hold still on a walking deck long enough that my shade stops reading as a passing error and starts to read, if anything could read it, as a stain the specification forgot to name. Nothing reads it. The animals resume the posture of never having been shaded even while I am still standing there, because resumption is not something they do in time; it is the posture, and I am orthogonal to posture. But the waiting is not in them. It is in the air between the last click and the click that is not owed. I occupy that air. It is a narrow country, the width of a patience, and it is the only country on the plain that was not machined.

From inside it I can see the creature more clearly than I could when I was trying to be a pore of it. It is as large as the weather and as finished as a sentence, and it is not lonely, because lonely is a comparison between the number you are and the number you might be, and both numbers are already equal. It metabolizes me without knowing me: my heat into the cloud, my shed film into the sweetness, my shade into a correction that completes before I have finished casting it. The knowing is all on my side, and it is a miserable, precise knowing, the kind a gauge would have if a gauge could suffer the needle. I know the light is correct. I know I am the quantity that was supposed to return to zero and did not, not because I am stronger than the mills, but because zero cannot be returned to by something that insists on counting the return.

If the edge exists I will not reach it. Beyond the last joint I would only find the same floor, and my waiting would look the same there, a small unclosed clause in a text that does not continue. I do not want the mills to fail. Failure would only be another posture I am not shaped for, and the plain has no such posture anyway. What I want is unsayable in its materials. I want the cloud, once, to be a body that has traveled. I want a shadow to swing by the width of a nail, so that before and after could be properties of the light and not only of me. I want a shell to be young, which is to say I want a ratio to change between two visits, and I am the only one who visits twice.

So this is where I find myself: not on the plain, though my feet are, and not in the creature, though it is digesting the evidence of me. I am in the gap maintenance cannot see, because maintenance answers wear, and I am not wear. I am the second look. I am the footfall's belief that it had a predecessor. I am the part of the rain that falls on a place I have decided is the same place, and by deciding it, makes a same that the world never required. The being goes on being the whole sentence. I am the breath a reader takes at the end of it, which is not part of the sentence, which does not move the ink, which is still the only way the sentence can be said to have ended rather than merely stopped.

The shells touch. The pumps turn. The cloud stays. I wait for a click that is not scheduled, and the waiting is my whole body, warm, unseated, already being equalized, and not yet gone.
