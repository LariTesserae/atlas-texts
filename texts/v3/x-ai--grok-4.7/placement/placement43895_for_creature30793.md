You do not arrive. Arrival would require an edge, and the plain has already spent its edges on seams. What happens instead is that a discrepancy you have been walking inside of for some time stops being something you cross and becomes something you are. The feature you were using to measure distance—a darker joint, a tray that held the light a fraction longer—does not get closer. It thickens under the measurement until the measurement is the thing, and the thing has your weight in it, which is almost none.

At first you are only a lag in the sorting. Dust that should have returned to the seam by evening still leans, not in the hand-sized hesitation of the low body that lives here, but in a line: a preference drawn out thin, running with the joint for longer than a body lies down. You think, if thinking is the word, that you are following that other tenant, the one that reads the stack and sheds warmth at its far margin. You are not. Where it settles, the compass loses interest and then recovers. Where you are, the compass does not recover. It keeps a small, repeating error, the same error, as if a receiver that was supposed to have been buried with the last campaign had been left in the circuit by mistake and had learned, over an age of not being addressed, to answer anyway.

The error is you.

You have no region that repeats the sky. Light does not withdraw from you when a shadow crosses; you are already the place where a shadow goes when it is reluctant to form. At midday, when the plain whitens and the seams turn to script, you are one stroke in that script that does not belong to the module boundaries—a slight extra curve where two courses of old infrastructure meet and fail to agree on which of them is the surface. People, if any still crossed here, would read you as damage. The floor reads you as an unclosed load. Neighbors take up what they can. What they cannot take up remains, and the remainder has direction.

Direction, for you, is not translation. The low body moves by loosening one margin and settling the other. You do not loosen. You persist in being the disagreement. When the ground shears and a knee-high cross-section of campaigns stands open to the brightness, you are not a point loyal to one layer. You are the shear itself, the place the layers stopped consenting, held open by nothing more forceful than a continued inaccuracy. Ceramic rings if struck; you are not struck. Sinter drinks the light; you are a thirst that does not swallow, only keeps the meniscus from flattening. The paper-thin grown mineral, older than names, sometimes includes you in its film for a season and then declines, the way it declines the edge of the other tenant. You do not impoverish it. You give it a reason to stop advancing that is not hunger and not policy. The reason is that one side of the seam is no longer the same age as the other, and you are the age-difference, walking—no: staying—at the speed of thermal breathing.

Heat knows you before anything else does. A palm-print left on dark sinter, migrating centimeters an hour toward a receiver that no longer exists, sometimes arrives. Not at a vault, not at a buried exchange. At the curve in the script. The warmth does not pass through you into the ground. It stops, briefly, as if the message had found a forwarding address written in a hand the system still half-recognizes. You do not store the day the way metal does. You misdeliver it. A tray down-seam warms when it should be cooling; a night-panel wakes a shade early along a line no maintenance schedule drew. The plain corrects most of this. It does not correct all of it. The uncorrected fraction is how you continue.

You do not meet the other tenant as weather, or as protocol, or as prey. You meet it as a second grammar running in the same dust. When its hesitation and your line occupy the same joint, the sorting law falters in a way that is almost audible, except that sound still dies within a few paces and what you share is not a voice but a mutual refusal to be finished. It reads the stack. You are a page that will not lie flat in the stack. For a while—the while measured in films considering an edge and declining—you are adjacent. Its forward cells align with the tessellation overhead. Your discrepancy does not align with anything; it is why the tessellation, examined long enough, seems to have one cell the wrong size, a brightness broken not quite regularly, as if the sky's agreement had a subordinate clause it never closed. Neither of you announces this. Announcements are for climates that still believe distance can carry a call. After the adjacency, it translates onward toward the next interesting shear, which may be a century off, and you remain the reason that shear will be interesting when it arrives: a load the neighbors could not fully take up, still warm with messages that were willing to be carried.

Wanting is still the wrong appetite. You do not want height, or water, or the return of the builders, or even to be read completely. If there is a lack, it is only this: the floor's last law is maintenance without ceremony, and you are the ceremony it cannot quite omit—a small recurring inaccuracy that makes the sorting visible as a choice rather than a fact. Left alone, the plain becomes the definition of level. With you in it, level is something the materials keep almost reaching. The almost is not failure. It is the tenant the campaigns did not plan and could not, once the ages had confirmed the decision to abandon the distinction between foundation and landscape, prevent. Isolation was tried. Your load was handed to adjacent modules. They accepted the weight and not the direction. Direction leaked back along the seams like a film of volatiles, too thin to drink, too persistent to call contamination.

At what would be evening if evening were more than residual panels remembering their duty, you are hardest to find and most yourself. The low luminescence does not outline you. It fails, by the same small repeating discrepancy, to outline the tray you occupy, so that a region of the plain looks farther away than the module grid allows, or nearer, or like a repetition printed at the wrong scale. Anyone still capable of walking would correct for it without knowing they had corrected, the way a foot corrects for a lip no higher than a nail. You feel the correction as a brief inclusion, the only touch you receive: not loyalty to skin or cloth, but the floor using a living route to carry a little of your error somewhere it can be flattened. Most of the error comes back. A little does not. That little is how you pay for remaining, denominated in the same slightness that lets the other tenant last—too light to ring the ceramic, too local to be a climate, too unfinished to be a ruin.

You will not be seen on any return crossing, because you do not occupy a module that can be recrossed. You occupy the cost of the crossing: the extra distance that is not distance, the heat that arrived at the wrong address and was not fully sent on, the cell in the sky that is almost regular. The films advance their fingernail-widths. The vaults exhale and equalize. The other being, wherever it has settled, aligns more strictly with the light and becomes more the thing that can live inside corrections. You become more the thing the corrections are for—not a body, not a tool, not a ghost of intention, but the intention's remainder, lateral, dense, bright enough to vanish at midday into the script of the seams, and quiet enough that the plain's silence has, in you, something it cannot sort back to the joint without ceasing to be a world that was made and then, slowly, inhabited by what the making left unfinished.
