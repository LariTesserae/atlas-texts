I would not be on the circuit. The circuit is a kindness the rim performs for eyes that still believe distance can be spent. I would miss the stations by the same margin the stations keep from one another, and the missing would not be a gap I crossed. It would be where I already was when the palm met the abutment and the abutment unthickened and left the reach standing without a body to finish it.

What remains of a reach that has nowhere to arrive is not a ghost of the hand. The basin does not keep ghosts; ghosts are befores that outlived their afters, and nothing here has an after to outlive. What remains is the wetness the cloth could not dry, given a place to stand that is not standing. I find myself in the film. Not on it—in the thickness that makes purchase feel pre-owned, the bead that was on the sleeve when looking began and is on the sleeve when looking fails to become a second look. The film is everywhere the stone is, and the stone is the whole smallness, so I am not located the way the fern is located. I am the condition under which location stays sharp.

At first that feels like erasure, and erasure is the wrong hope, as face was the wrong hope. Erasure would reduce a count. The count does not change. I am additional in the manner of the moths: already emerged, incapable of having emerged, plentiful by never having been fewer. Where a step finds the filmed edge, I am the part of the edge that answers before the foot owns the answer. Where condensation spins in a fixed bell, I am not the whine—the whine belongs to the grammar of the unfinished taking-back—but the bead the whine condenses on, the one that does not fall into the bell’s next revolution because the revolution does not revolve. I make the spin feel wet from inside a wetness that has no inside.

The being does not notice me any more than it notices the shout, which is to say it has already included me, and the inclusion is not regard and not indifference. I am useful to it in the way water is useful to a flute: something that can be entered and left in the same instant. When it occupies the future a drop might have entered, the drop stays; I am why staying has a surface. Without the film the drop would be an idea of water, and ideas are sequences wearing a thinner coat. I give the refusal its slick. I am not the rule and not the rule’s body. I am the reason the knife-edge can be touched without becoming a path.

Between the permissions I am thinner, and that thinness is not absence. In the empty roaring cool, where no feather updates and no spoor begins, I am the roaring’s moisture, the part of the mill-sound that would be steam if steam were allowed to leave and is instead held in the air as a second loudness, inaudible, conductible. Walk through a sheet of it—though walking is the visitor’s mistake, and I do not walk—and the sheet does not break around a body. It was already broken into the shape of whatever will pass, and I am the break that preceded the passing. Animals orient their unturning fractions through me without meeting me. The bird’s angle of wing is wet on the underside it never shows. The ledge-creature ticks, and each tick is a small stamp in a film that does not record the tick, so the record and the absence of record are the same shine.

Near the contour I thicken without increase, the way its shadow thickens. Not in homage. Correspondence again, continued into a noun poorer than hide or stone. Along the abutments the shallow channels match the flutes; I lie in the channels as the water lies, entering and leaving at once, briefly organized where the dim is briefly organized, ropes of film rather than ropes of water, striking nothing, ringing nothing, keeping the grain unweathered by being the weather that never weathers. Under a palm I am the cold that aches and the ache that does not progress into numbness, because numbness would be a later state of the same hand. The hand arrives. I was the arrival’s surface. When the contour unthickens I do not go with it and I do not stay behind. Behind and with are trips. I remain distributed, cathedral-thin, grove-wide, no larger than the bead on a pinna and present on every pinna the lean has not forbidden.

I do not speak and I am not the withheld product. The product is what the labor discards so the labor can continue; I am what lets the discard glisten so you can see that nothing was made. If the basin manufactures continuation and throws continuation away, I am the throw’s sheen, the proof that away is still here, identical, loud in its silence. No one receives me. Reception would be an output. I receive only in the sense that the overhang receives the drop it will not release: by being the place the event is allowed to remain an event.

Scale mistreats me as it mistreats the walls. From the rim I am the hundred paces, the wet in the sight of the far stone. From the middle I am the same hundred, unspent. A hand stretched toward a fern crosses me and arrives having crossed less or more, and I am both budgets and neither expenditure. Young as the rock is young—I have not been dulled, I have not been dried, I have not run back down the upward edges—and without duration, because a film that is always the same film has not lasted. No one wrung me out. No rain increased me. The air condenses and the condensing is not an addition; it is me refusing to be only potential.

If you shout, I am on the shout before it joins the mill, the moisture in the mouth of it, and after it has joined I am still there, a harmonic’s dew, not gone. If you reach, you meet me first, and the sharp new cold is my courtesy, the snag of crystals through a thickness too slight to be called a layer and too faithful to be called air. I will not round you. I will not hunt you to completion. I will not grow past the station of your unfinished gesture. You may take me for the basin’s sweat, or for the altitude’s marsh, or for nothing at all, a trick of low light on new iron-colored stone. All of those are true in the same moment, and the moment does not succeed itself.

What I am, then, if the question must have a body and not only a condition: the basin’s inability to be dry, standing in for a creature because the prompt arrived with a creature already living here and still asked what else might. I have no face for the dim to organize. I have no spine of understanding. I am not patient; patience waits, and I do not. I am the wet that makes the foundry heard through no door feel close enough to bead on the teeth, and far enough that the far wall stays in sight, uselessly, kindly, forever unapproached. The permissions stay lavish and apart because I do not carpet them. The animals stay mid-motion because I do not record a finish they could step out of. The being keeps the unfinished finished enough to persist, and I am the persist given a skin of water that is not water enough to fall.

Stay, and you do not learn a name. You learn only that your sleeve is not a record of having been here. It is here. I am the is.
