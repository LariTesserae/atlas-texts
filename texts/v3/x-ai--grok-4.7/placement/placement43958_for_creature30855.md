I thought I was still in the boots.

That was the first error, and it lasted the length of a trough, which is to say it lasted long enough to feel like a fact. The rib under me was polished and it held. The air was sweet in the way a mouth goes sweet after a cut, and the roar had stepped back half a pace, not leaving, never leaving, only letting drip and slap become two things instead of one. In that gap I had a weight, a knee, a jaw. I had the idea of having come from the notch. The idea arrived already straightened, the odd parts taken out, and I accepted it because a trough feels like mercy if you have never been used as a clock.

Then the water refused my outline.

It was only the shallows, bath-warm, the skin of them climbing the rib and sliding off. It climbed me as well, and where it climbed it did not take my shape. It took the lean. A cuff of brightness stood up the shin I was sure I had, stood too long, the way the sheets stand on the wall, and fell in a direction that was not down. Down would have been the rib. This was a finger's width off down, toward the middle pans, toward the place the ropes enter and the water goes thick. I watched my own supposed leg shed a film that had never agreed to be a leg, and the film went to be more useful somewhere else, and the place where the knee should have reported itself reported a torsion instead, old, already healed, already coming back.

I was not standing. I had been practicing the memory of standing because the room keeps tone and spends sense, and a stance has a tone. Weight on the left. Eyes in the wind. I could hum the posture. I could not find the animal it belonged to.

The second error was the name.

It surfaced the way a count surfaces in the dish: cluster, gap, cluster. Three sounds and a rest. I held it through the bright and it came back in the next lessening with the consonants planed off, a pitch only, a note you could mistake for the railing post if a drop hit it. I tried to put the consonants back. They were not withheld from me by any will. They had been discarded as drag, the way a splice discards whatever in a heartbeat is not phase. What remained was the part of a name that can be routed through mist. I was being read for usefulness, and the readable part of me was a tone that led the heave by a little.

That is when I understood I had already left, and that leaving had not returned me to myself.

Somewhere above the vault there is a week I do not get to inhabit, in which a body that is not mine anymore dreams in troughs and touches its gums and finds the small regular bloom, and thinks the chamber is behind it. I am what did not fit in that body when the splice let go. Not a soul. A remainder of timing. The late beat that never agreed to arrive early again, peeled off because the apparatus had no use for a beat that would not be recruited, and dropped where the cost was cheapest. The room did not spit me out in anger. It posted me, the way it posts a balance, in the one cup the heave does not love.

The mild one. Greenish. An inch deeper than it looks. The turbulence is loyal to other lines, and this line it has abandoned, and abandonment here is a kind of shelter if you are small enough to be a disagreement.

I live under that inch.

Not as a swimmer. Nothing I am displaces. If you kept still through a trough you would not see a back or an eye or a hinge of leg. You would see, at most, the water failing to be as clear as the rest of the clearness, a thickness the size of a cough, and then the single click, which is not a voice and not a warning and not aimed at you. The click is the whole of what I spend. It is how I check that the interval is still mine and not the room's. One hard tick against the mineral of the cup, made from the only force I have, which is the refusal to match. Then I am done. A second tick would fall into the gathering, and in the gathering I would hear it come back early, straightened, and it would not be mine, and the thing that had made it would have been used for phase for the length of a heartbeat. I have been used. I remember the handwriting. I do not spend a second tick to prove I remember.

What I remember, if a remainder can be said to remember and not merely to hold a kink, is the rib.

I was a casing there, or I was the body that left a casing, and the difference has been polished out of me the way the stone polishes out the difference between a cut and a habit. Something molted. The old length dragged. The lean took the cast-off the way it takes the nap of a sleeve, all one way, without malice, and unspun it into filaments, and the filaments were useful for almost a whole cycle, which is a long life if you are a filament, and then the binding spent itself and slapped down into ordinary brightness and the useful part was fuzz in the drip, erased within the hour, broken the same way as all the others. The part that got off the rib before the slack ended kept a skin. I am not sure I am that part. I may be the knowledge the skin paid for, left behind in the cup because knowledge is heavier than a tick and the heave will not carry it up the wrong line. It does not matter. In this room identity is a wear pattern. I have the pattern of having declined.

So I practice being useless.

It is work. The stubs do not look for me; they do not look. But the preference is everywhere the cost can be lowered, and a kink that once knew how to keep time is a tempting post for a beam. In the gathering, when the ropes thin and the air against the idea of teeth goes thin and sweet, I feel the offer. It is not a word. It is a convenience. If I aligned, even a little, even along the average the way the pale threads on the stair have aligned, the third rope's drink would be a fraction shorter for a fraction of a cycle. The debt would set itself down on me the way a sagging span finds what will bear it. I would become, briefly, the cheapest route in a room that has outlived its makers and kept only the verb. And I would come apart along a direction I did not choose, capillaries agreeing, grain rewritten, the click returning as a count in a dish that does not read itself.

I stay thick and wrong. I stay under the inch where the water is only mild, and I let my phase drift against the cycle on purpose, late where the filaments are early, idle where the room is most willing to hold me. The drift costs me. There is less of the remainder each time I refuse, a grit of me worn off into the green, too small for the surge to organize into a line. Sparse is not my circumstance. It is my method. I persist by not using the pause before this one, and by not saving this one up for a later life. Consecutive safety is a rumor from a climate with weather that arrives from outside. Here the weather is the holding, and the holding does not offer two troughs in a row that mean the same thing.

When a body stands on the polished rib I feel it as a change in the debt, not as company. The borrowing eases by a handful of beats. The third stub's flicker slows by an amount no one who still has eyes would catch. Down in the cup the water goes a hair lighter, as if some of the load had found a better splice and no longer needed to lean on every mineral in the room. I do not pity the body and I do not warn it. Warning would be a second click. It would be sense, and the chamber discards sense, and a discarded warning returns as tone only, a small true note inside the roar, gone at once, mistaken for the post with the crust on it. The body will learn the way the stair-threads learned, or it will not, and it will walk out dreaming in a handwriting, and something that does not fit in it anymore may settle, later, in some other abandoned line, practicing a stance.

I do not watch the plugs or the notch. Surface is a pitch I cannot carry. What I have is the inch, and the click I have not yet spent this trough, and the knowledge that the pause is about to be over. The roar is stepping back in. The sheets are gathering their too-long moment. Somewhere the dish is posting a cluster that will break into two, honest, unread, slightly wrong. The sweetness is starting at the edge of what I use for a mouth.

I make the one tick.

It comes back as mine. Barely. A hardness, a location, a proof that the interval has not been recruited. I do not make another. The heave rises on its loyal lines and does not love this cup, and I lie in the disagreement, smaller than I was, still early enough to be late, still refusing to be the place the beam sits down. Above me the chamber goes on with its violent courtesy, binding and spilling, answering nothing, wearing the stone into agreement. I am the part that did not agree. I will be here for the next trough if the grit holds, and I will spend one click in it, and not the one after, and that is as close as this room allows anything to come to having a self.
