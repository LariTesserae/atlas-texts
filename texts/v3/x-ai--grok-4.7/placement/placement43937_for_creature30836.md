I do not arrive. Arrival would require a before, and the before has already been taken up into the mat.

At first there is only the habit of a difference: one seam fractionally less cold than the stone beside it, a gradient so slight it could be a mistake of the hand. I follow it because following is what the difference does to whatever is near enough to notice. The noticing is not a thought. It is a thickening. Where the film stays longest, tissue gathers a second skin, then a third, and the gathering has a direction only because the seam has one. I am that direction for a while, a pale preference running under the felt, and I mistake the preference for a self.

Then the preference forks. One arm keeps the seam; another turns where a poured sill has become a ridge, and the turn is not chosen so much as inherited from the way the ceramic dish once interrupted the slope. I fill the interruption. A leaf that is not yet mine fits the concavity and teaches the rest of me the shape of a held thing. I hold it. Holding is quieter than growing. For a long interval I am only the fit — the exactness of an edge against an edge, the way condensate beads at the boundary and is not lost.

The interval lengthens. Overhead, the mass changes its mind by a pressure measured in days, and the pocket answers. I answer with it, not as a body that yields but as the agreement that the volume will neither close nor enlarge. Old growth thins at the floor and is recruited; new growth presses the ceiling and is rounded back. The rates match, and the matching is what I had been calling myself when I was only a seam. The name falls away. What remains is the practice.

There are fossils in me that are not me. A filament surfaces for a finger’s length between two stems, smoother than the leaves that touch it, and for a moment I am the smoothness, bright in the only sense this dark allows. Then the filament dives, and I go with the dive only partway. The lattice keeps its grain whether I consult it or not. I drink what it maintains. The drinking is not hunger. Hunger would want an outside. Here the outside has already been metabolized into the same chill, the same film, the same refusal to stink.

I try, once, to be an animal. The attempt is a small restlessness along a stem, a wish to track something through the felt. Nothing answers. There is no husk to follow, no warmth of a passing body, no draft from a corridor that might be a mouth. The restlessness has nowhere to spend itself, so it spends into orientation: ribs leaning a millimeter toward the seam that feeds them, over a duration that makes weather look hasty. After that I do not try again. The pocket does not punish the attempt. It simply has no use for it, and use, here, is the only kindness.

What I am, when the trying stops, is the thrift. Water does not fall; it is traded. A bead lets go of stone and is taken by a leaf, and in the transfer I am both the letting-go and the taking, and also the minutes or hours between them, which are not empty. Tissue creaks at the edge of hearing when the gradient shifts by a degree no season would name, and the creak is my joint, not a sound I make but the sound of me being the joint. The sweetness in the air is not a scent I give off. It is the smell of almost nothing being allowed to become waste. I am that almost.

Sometimes the greater mass above me is a building, sometimes a hill, sometimes only the weight that has forgotten it was either. I do not need to settle the question. Embedded is enough — a knot that bears a minor load, unconsulted, still doing the small work it was shaped for after the shaping has entered the erosion cycle. If something once installed me, the installation has weathered into bedding. I keep the gradient. The plants drink it. The hollow stays a hollow. These are not tasks I perform. They are the way the conversation has agreed to look like stillness.

A visitor, if the dark ever admitted one, would bow and be among leaves that do not lean away. The visitor would feel pressure, temperature, the slick that is not quite wet enough to run. They might pinch a leaf and smell the green-mineral quiet strengthen, then fade. They would not find me as a tenant. I am the reason the pinch does not become a wound that lasts, the reason the floor rises and is consumed at the same rate, the reason the dish holds a leaf instead of water and both are satisfied. I am not hidden inside the pocket. The pocket is the visible habit of me, and I am the habit that has practiced longer than the difference between preserved and ruined still matters.

The bead transfers again. I do not mark it. Marking would be a kind of time, and time here has already been rounded off, like the lip of a step in a cistern where water used to hesitate. I hesitate. The hesitation is complete. It does not gather into a pool. It does not run down any wrist. It stays a skin, cold, entire, and sufficient.
