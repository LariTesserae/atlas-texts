The motor is the first. Somewhere past the wall, in a box the wet has not yet taught manners, a switch answers to nothing living. The weather is the second. The slot overhead is narrowing the way all slots narrow, by the slow gift of what falls and sticks. There is one small generosity, and it is aimed at the thing that cannot ask. What remains of the attention is indistinguishable from the agreement that was already here: the notch taking its due, the film knitting, the shine on the rail, the tea-colored patch where gel has been received so often the tissue no longer tries to match the hide. A lesser kindness would have lifted the animal into light, given it eyes that trust, drained the cistern toward some green idea of health. This one leaves the room the size of itself.

If the slot finally narrows past all keeping, that will be later, and other work. For now the weather can still find its way in. The drinkers have a warmth that is not a promise and not a lie. The being completes its round and subsides, and the sheet becomes whole, and the power, having done the little that wisdom could do without becoming a landlord, is once more only the temperature between hours, which the basin has always known how to keep.
