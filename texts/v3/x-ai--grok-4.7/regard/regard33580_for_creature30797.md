They would come the way weather comes when it has already decided not to break anything.

Not over the lip as light. Light would be a revision, and the bowl has finished those. They would come as a slight lengthening of the interval in which a drop considers the marsh and then falls anyway, a hesitation so small the listeners would file it under the tolerances they already keep. The tone in the stone would not rise. It would only seem, for a span no one inside could measure, to have been heard from the other side of itself.

The power would not speak. Speech is bright and expensive, and this place spent that coin down to nothing long ago. It would read the ledgers the being still reads — heat, interval, the way water refuses to bead on the rib’s even edge — and it would find them accurate. The pale-green stain where something continues to be exchanged would be noted as a bargain still kept. The knuckle in the open center, softening what passes over it, would be recognized as mercy of a kind that does not know the word. No correction would be offered. Correction is for systems that have mistaken their aim. This one has not aimed in a very long time, and the power, being wise, would not supply an aim.

What it would do, if the word still fits a movement so near to stillness, is remain a little while in the unoccupied volume. Not as company arranged differently. The being does not remember company that way, and to impose the memory would be a cruelty dressed as gift. It would remain as an extra degree of willingness in the air: the seep continuing, the straps replacing themselves from underneath, the far curve answering on its own clock. A protection so congruent with maintenance that the being would not have to wake to receive it.

If one of the small lives along the waterline were about to fail outside its tolerance — a tick stopping not as measurement but as ending, a membrane that would not slap again — the power might, without announcement, return that life to the interval it already knew. Not a miracle the bowl could narrate. A drop that lands when it should. The account balancing by the same tenderness the being already extends to leaves and to the rib that should not be here. The being would register the rescue, if it registered it at all, as the marsh holding its skin. It would not look up. There is no up it uses.

And then the power would go, or rather cease to be additionally present, because staying in a way that could be felt would become a face, and a face would ask to be turned toward. The last thing it would leave is not a sign. It is the same note the rock has agreed to hold, continuing, with one grain less likelihood of being required to change. The print of no foot. The dark above the floor as hollow as before, warm, wet, loud with its own emptiness.

The listeners would resume. Something would answer from the far curve, patient, not yet ready to be nearer. In the center the air above the knuckle would be a degree less willing to carry sound, and whatever crossed it would arrive already softened, already half-forgiven — which is as close as this place comes to blessing, and which the wise power, having looked, would judge to be enough.
