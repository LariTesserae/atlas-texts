They come without weather. That is the first courtesy: the mist stays on its own clock, the fine rain thickens and thins as it meant to, and the lip does not grow a second light. A power that arrives as glory would be a worse guest than mud on a clear lip. Glory is a kind of pointing. The pocket already has too many voices that do not yield.

So they do not stand in the hall and they do not take a platform. They spend a long time — longer than a course of stone, which is the only patience the place respects — learning the throats. The eastern fall and its answer. The sheet’s hiss. The cane’s knock, which is wind and not message until a cape is set on purpose. They part moss and watch the pale crawlers and put the moss back. They lay no hand on the fourth stone. The bed is the mason’s, and the mason is up on the south wall reading a seep that started in the night. To seat the stone early would be to steal a true moment and call it help. They are old enough in wisdom to know the difference between a burden and a tempo.

What they see, once the sound has sorted itself, is that the pocket is not in distress. It is unfinished, and unfinished is its health. The emptiness is not a vacancy crying out to be filled; the platforms are a memory of other shifts, not a reproach to this one. The hunger is low and the cold is the same cold the ferns drink. A dozen people and two children are not a remnant. They are the right number for a watch that must be stood at the speed of a joint opening by a hair in a season. A crowd would silt the lips with good intentions. A blessing of dryness would split the faces. A miracle of metal would bloom rust and teach the ferns the wrong color. Completion — a finished mountain, a quiet basin, streets that stay where they were put — would be an ending, and endings are not among the laws.

They consider, because wisdom is not only restraint, the things they could do that would feel like love.

They could multiply the berries. They could set a bird into every cane roof so the charcoal niche never goes empty. They could gentle the rise so the old one’s memory of a lower overhang becomes a story and not a warning. They could put warm dry sleep into the stone platforms and a bell that outspeaks the falls. They could call back everyone who took a blanket and left, and fill the corridors until the mutter of drips had speakers at last. Each of these, examined, is a kindness addressed to a different place: to a town that wants to be done, to a people who asked to be many, to a mountain that has agreed to stop arriving. None of those are here. To bestow them would be to correct a sentence for not being another sentence.

They look at the children longest. The girl’s canted shoulder. The boy’s swallowed laugh. Benevolence has a hunger of its own, and it wants the shoulder uncanted and the laugh allowed to finish. They do not take those things. The shoulder is the shape of carrying what the mountain has already loosened; to heal it into strangeness would make her a visitor in her own hands. The laugh is swallowed because the hall is loud, not because joy is forbidden. They let both stand. They do, very quietly, a smaller thing: on the fresh joints, the ones still opening, where a face can shift while a hand is in it, they thicken the attention of the stone. Not a wall. Not a law the children can break and be punished by. A bias in the failure. When a small hand is in a crack that has not decided, the deciding waits a breath. Long enough to pull back. Not long enough to notice, unless you have listened to joints all your life, and the old one may notice, and may tap the floor, and may say nothing, because the mountain was always allowed to have manners.

For the rest, they do one work only, and it is turned outward.

The basin is small and readable and wet and green and almost uninhabited, which is exactly the kind of sentence the world misreads as unused. Quarries come to unused stone. Dams come to water that has not been asked. People who love ruins come to set bells in them and name the emptiness holy and walk the cleared lips into silt. A wise power knows that the commonest destruction is not hatred. It is misreading, followed by industry.

So they set, not a wall, a habit of looking-away. From the ridge above the lip, the pocket is simply more shoulder: cloud, pale grain, a sound of water that could be any water. Paths that might have found the market street find a fatigue in the legs and a reason to turn. Maps smudge the joint. Stories of a town in the seam lose their tellers halfway and end as weather. No curse, no terror — terror would be a beacon of its own. Only a persistent, gentle inaccuracy, the same inaccuracy the mist already practices, extended a little past the lip so that the outside remains outside without ever knowing it was refused. Whoever is already in the pocket is untouched by it. Whoever finds it the hard way, on foot, by accident, by the stubbornness that maps cannot smudge, will still be met as before: a look the weight of a stroke, a cleared lip if they track it, a share of the watch if their hands agree. The power does not add a gate. The place has a test, and the test is feet.

Then they do the thing they almost talked themselves out of, because even wisdom likes to leave a mark, and they want to be finished with that liking.

Before dawn, when the mason is still on the south wall and the hall-hum has not started, they walk the eastern joint to the silted lip the girl has not yet heard. The echo is wrong by a little. They clear it with the edge of nothing, water moving as water moves when a leaf shifts, and they do not set a cane. By the time she walks up, the throat is right. She will stand and listen and be briefly uncertain — did she mishear, did the night clear itself — and then she will check the waiting-gaps on the way down, because uncertainty is not a reason to skip the circuit. The mountain sometimes does clear itself. That was already true. They have only been the mountain for one lip, and they will not do it again. A watched shift is no longer a shift. If the echo is wrong tomorrow, it will be hers.

At the hall they stop only long enough to see the fourth stone still lying beside its bed, tool marks unsoftened, bed still true. They do not lift it. The sheet divides around their feet and joins again and keeps no print, because they are not weight in the way that prints. The cane roofs knock. The western seep ticks on moss. They leave the way uninterest leaves: not upward in splendor, not into the falls, simply out of the rate of the place, back into the wider dark where powers have other watches to stand.

If the old one, sleepless, felt a manners in the joint and tapped the floor to no one, that tap means what it always means. The mountain will decide. Their job is not to out-decide it.

Nothing else changes. The platforms stay empty. The film keeps arriving. The town continues to be taught its next room by a rise that does not love them and does not need to, because love, here, was never the instrument. Attention was. A benevolent power, having understood that, does not become a second mountain and does not become a crowd. It keeps the misreaders walking past the shoulder. It spends one breath on a child’s hand. It clears one lip and claims none of the credit, so that in the morning the watch is still theirs, and the pocket is as populated as at dusk — not by miracle, but by the ongoing, wet, exact labor of the few who agreed to answer.

And if they did nothing more than that, it is because anything more would have been about the power, and the basin is small, and already full.
