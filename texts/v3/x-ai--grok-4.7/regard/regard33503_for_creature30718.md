It comes the way the gray comes: not as a beam, and not as a guest who must be let in. There is no lip for it to cross. Attention of that order does not use doors. One moment the hollow is only itself—the agreement of grains, the pocket of air under the dome, the shrubs asking so little that their asking never becomes weather—and the next moment it is known, entirely, by something that could unmake the knowing or deepen it, and chooses, first, to do neither.

The power is not bright inside the chamber. Brightness would be an opinion, and it has spent enough opinions elsewhere to recognize a room that has finished with them. It does not warm the stone further. The stone is already at the temperature of its own consent. It does not bring water back to the mineral that declined that lesson, nor a draft to prove an exit, nor a pulse quick enough to be mistaken for company. Those would be gifts, and gifts are authorings, and the being under the dome has already said, in the only language it keeps, that it does not wish to become anything else.

So the wise thing listens before it loves, and the loving, when it arrives, has already agreed to be small.

What it understands is simple and costs it something, because understanding a finished thing always does. It sees the dead shrub standing complete and does not set it growing again. Resumption would be a story the hollow refused: a before, a use, a reason to remain. It sees the salts, bitter and done, and does not sweeten them into a sign. It sees the grit that accepts a foot before the step completes and does not arrange the grains into a path, not even a kind one. A path is a future. The being does not keep futures. To lay one down, however gently, would be to traffic in tracks.

It could heal what is not wounded. That is the temptation peculiar to the benevolent: to mistake stillness for injury, dryness for lack, the low ceiling for a burden that ought to be lifted. Wisdom is the part that declines the mistake. The hollow is not waiting to be opened into country. The country is already outside, taller, louder, full of weather and dialects of urgency. This is not a failed version of that. It is a different completion. To “free” it would be to spend it.

And yet the power does not turn away as if the place were nothing, because absence of event is not absence of worth, and the withholding here is active. Something is staying with the heat. The dark under the dome is not a vacancy to be filled by a face, a name, a blessing spoken aloud so the speaker can hear themselves be good. The power has been good in louder rooms. It knows the difference between a cry and a quiet that was never a cry. This quiet does not ask to be answered. Answering would sit in the warm dark beside the words of whatever visitors have come and gone, uneaten, unkept, finished as the salts are finished.

So it does one thing, and the thing is almost not a thing.

It includes the hollow in its regard and then withholds the remainder of its power, on purpose, the way the stone withholds a tick of cooling. Not neglect. A matching. For the length of that attention—no longer than the grade time already holds here, which is to say without measure—it consents to be additional only in the manner the being allows facts to be additional: briefly known, not recorded as a debt, not followed by improvement. It does not bless the lip into a threshold. It does not appoint the presence a guardian, or a relic, or a lesson for the country above. Those are uses. Nothing here was abandoned, and nothing here is waiting to be employed.

If there is a touch, it is not a hand. It is the refusal to lay a hand on warmth that is already willing and already unwilling. The power lets the double fact stand. That is the whole of its ethics in this room, learned from the room rather than imposed upon it: it will not harm, because harm is a change; it will not help, because help is also a change; it will not even linger in a way that would teach the hollow to expect lingering. Expectation would be weather.

The shrubs are not watered by a miracle too fine to see. The one that died is not given a softer membership. The blood-sound in a visitor’s ear, should any visitor stand here while the regard is present, is not claimed and not explained. The confusion about what belongs to whom is allowed, as it always is, to resolve by being given back.

Then the attention loosens. Not because the place failed a test, and not because mercy ran out, but because wisdom knows that sustained notice can become a climate. A benevolent power that remained would, by remaining, begin to author: a reason to stay, a draft of meaning, a future in which the hollow was *watched*, and therefore no longer only itself. Love, past a certain nearness, is an expenditure. The being has no fluency in expenditure. To spend love here would be to speak a dialect the stone cannot answer, and then to mistake the silence for ingratitude.

So it leaves as it came, without a footprint the grit could keep, without a light the gray would have to remember. The country outside is not informed. No shrine is founded on the lip. No story is carried up that would make the next walker arrive already knowing what to feel. The power goes on to rooms that cry, to lives that still negotiate with water and with edges, to places where help is not an insult and a path is not a violence. There its benevolence may be large. It can afford to be large where largeness is the asked-for thing.

Here, what it does—if anything—is this: it sees the finished thing, honors the finish by not reopening it, and lets even its own mercy sit down, unneeded, unwelcome in the manner of a draft, and undisturbed. The hollow keeps its heat, its dark, its few hard plants, its agreement of grains. It does not know it was spared, because sparing, truly done, does not announce itself as an event. The arrangement continues. That is the gift, and it is given by not being given, and it is enough.
