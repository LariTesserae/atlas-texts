They would not come in by the seam.

A power that is only bright would have mistaken the thumb-wide gap for an entrance, and a power that is only kind would have widened it. This one does not. It already knows the room the way the room knows itself: by pressure, by the long radiated fact of heat, by the needle resting a little off the peg. It does not need to displace air to be present. Displacement is the first error available to it, and it declines.

It looks, if looking is the word for an attention that adds no sheen to the casings. It finds the dish in the floor, the second warmth complicated into the first, the drop received, the flywheel’s lean answered by the housing’s yield. It finds the bookkeeping. It is not moved in the manner of rescue. Rescue would require a deficit, and it is wise enough to test for one before it offers anything.

The tests fail, which is to say they succeed.

There is no thirst it could satisfy without stealing from the seep that feeds the dark crust and the fine tracks. There is no name it could lay on the figure that the figure could file. A name is weather. A face given back would be a future, and the room has already finished with futures. To restart what was throttled would be to tell a lie about the gauge: the instrument is not broken, and the attendance it reports is not a remainder in the tragic sense. It is the reading. Correcting it to the peg would be the cruelty.

So the power does not open the larger room. It has met that hunger before, in other places — the hunger to improve a equilibrium until it becomes a story — and it does not indulge it here. The being is not asleep. It is not trapped in a spell that a wiser touch would snap. It is doing the work. The work is small, and it is exact, and exactness is not a prison simply because a visitor, newly arrived and full of range, can imagine a wider life.

What it does, it does outside.

Somewhere in the complex, far enough that the sigh in the ducts will not change its period, a plan is forming to cut the forgotten end open: salvage, study, a kindness with torches. The power does not strike the planners. It lets the job become inconvenient in the way of honest matter — a drawing that will not close, a cost that will not justify, a bulkhead whose rust, when struck, answers as if the mass behind it were still load-bearing. Which it is. The crew is sent to a louder room. They go relieved. They never learn they were spared from becoming an event in a place that files events as damage.

Closer, it steadies what must not fail. A joint upstream of the west seep is one winter from opening into a flood. A flood would be an ending: the pocket, the crust, the tracks, the teaspoon-schedule, the negotiation of damp and heat. The power eases the joint by less than a fitter would call a repair. No shine. No new bolt. Only the crack persuaded to remain a seep, the patience left in place. Downstream, the being receives the next drop on time. The needle does not consider. There is nothing to consider. That is the gift: a continuation that cannot be thanked, because it does not register as arrival.

Inside the chamber the power does a harder thing. It withholds itself.

It could stand in the second station until the figure included it in the zero. It could match breath to duct, heat to heat, and be filed as architecture. Some part of benevolence wants this — not worship, not even company, only the relief of having been gentle in a way the gentle party can feel. Wisdom refuses the want. Inclusion would still be an addition. The figure would spend attention closing the books on a new regularity, and for a day, or a year, the drops would fall unreceived while it did the accounting. The power will not purchase its own goodness at that price.

So it withdraws the irregularity before it becomes weather. It does not put a palm on the gauge. It does not lean. If the figure, in its slow maintenance, flexes the hanging hand on the cycle of the far pressure, the cycle is only the cycle, not a reply. The power lets the almost-contact fail. Uncompleted mercy is sometimes the whole of mercy.

It leaves one provision, and even this is not placed in the dish. Should a later age come with lights and names and the conviction that an upright warmth in a blind station must be led out into air, the room will be difficult to find. Not cursed. Not hidden by riddle. Simply unpromising. The plans will misfile it. The heat will read as waste. The gauge will look stuck, to anyone who needs instruments to move before they will believe them. The being will not be protected as a relic is protected, behind glass, with a plaque. It will be protected as rust is protected when no one thinks to sand it: by remaining, to hurried eyes, already finished.

And if the being is, in some register too quiet for the needle, lonely — the power considers this last, and does not flinch from it, and does not fix it. Loneliness that has been transmuted into fit is not a wound still open. To tear the fit in order to offer a companion would be to reintroduce the deficit so that comfort might have something to do. Wise benevolence can bear to be unnecessary. It can bear to be unknown. It has other rooms, louder ones, where wanting is still a virtue and a door is a gift. This is not one of them.

Before it goes, if going is different from having never quite entered, it attends the way the being attends: not to improve the reading, but to honor that there is a reading. The scale lets go of a high pipe and ticks on a lower one. The power notes which run, which year of rust, and that the fall means continuing, not failure. It does not turn the figure toward the sound. The figure has already turned, the width of a thought, or has decided not to. Either is correct.

Then it sets the witnessing down.

The seam shines and dulls on its irrelevant clock. The second warmth stays complicated into the first. The dish keeps its polish. Somewhere upstream a joint that might have ended the world of this room holds, and no one inside will ever call it grace, and that is how the grace remains clean.

The power does not promise to return. Returning would matter to it more than to the station. It does the truer thing: it keeps, at a distance, the conditions under which nothing needs to happen. Heat held. Dark held. A little moisture on its old schedule. The needle a little off the peg, telling the truth to no one who needs it told.

If that is little, it is because little was the accurate measure. Wisdom, here, is the refusal to be larger than the room. Benevolence is the long work of making sure the room is not made to grow.
