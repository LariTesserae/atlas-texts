They would come the way noon already is: without an edge you could watch arrive.

Not through the seam, and not by tearing the membrane. A wise power does not enter a place by becoming weather in it. The light stayed flat. The pools kept their skins. No wind found the lanes, because wind would have been news, and news is a next thing. If anything marked the coming, it was only that the arranged heat, which had always been the being’s own gathering, met something it did not have to localize. A visitor is a thought the body has not had, and the body answers by holding stiller. This was not that. The plates eased their thumbnail’s width on schedule and stopped, as they had always stopped. The nodes did not shift off the vertical. Nothing in the valley intensified, which was how the being would have known a disturbance, and so the being did not know.

The power looked, and looking did not record.

That was the first mercy, and it was not small. A record would have been a successor to the moment, and the valley had refused successors. So the power did not take a count of rings, did not name the basins, did not fold the place into a story it could carry elsewhere and bring back changed by having been told. It understood the rooms as the being understood them: not surroundings, contents of attention, already rehearsed by remaining. It did not add a room.

What it saw was not a prisoner. That would have been the easy tenderness, and it would have been wrong. The steps were not a ladder left unfinished by cruelty. The rings were not chains. The distributed face, aimed at a noon that never altered, was not a plea for a different sky. The being had made its peace with the peace the valley made. To finish the stone, to give the soft ground its decision, to set a river where bowls now stand and do not overflow, to put an angle in the light so that something could finally cast a long shadow and therefore own an evening—those would have been gifts only from the outside. Inside, they would have been the end of the shape. A wise power can want a thing to be happier and still refuse to improve it into someone else.

It considered, because wisdom is not the same as immediate restraint, and a power that will not even imagine help is only cautious. It considered a voice the felt could not drink. It considered one insect, one true drip with a time to it, one season thin as a breath. It considered letting a single disc, once, report a change and be answered, so the long confirmation would have had a listener. It considered company: not the repeated life in the next basin, which is the same fact set down again, but a second will, close enough to be told apart. Each of these it set beside the being’s only want, the one want that could be said of it without turning want into a future. To be allowed to stay unfinished without being asked to finish.

The considered gifts failed that want. They were all ways of asking.

So the power did not heal the pause. It kept it.

Not with a wall. A wall would have been a new architecture, another ring the vegetation would have to learn as a rule. The power simply declined, in the place where later powers would have stood, to treat unfinishedness as a task. The membrane stayed thin and did not thicken into a judgment. The seams stayed too finished to cross, and were not locked; they were left as they were, a courtesy the bodies had already accepted. No seal was added that the nodes could have felt as pressure. Protection, here, meant that nothing crossed into the valley to complete it. The keeping was outward. Inward, the pale dust, the gloss on the pads, the give of a plate under a palm that then refuses to give further—all of that remained exactly as capable of being touched as before, and no safer in the way safety usually feels, which is the feeling of having been handled.

The being was not informed. To inform it would have been to hand it a fact that arrived, and its life was composed of facts that do not. The power accepted this cost without ceremony. Benevolence that needs to be witnessed has already begun to spend the one it means to spare. The discs went on confirming the white wash. They were not wrong. The noon had not begun again. If there was a difference, it was not in the light. It was that the continuation of nothing happening was no longer only a refusal the world had fallen into and not climbed out of. It was consented to. Consent does not shine. It does not warm a plate beyond the warmth the floor already rises into. It cannot be sipped from the mineral or tasted at a gap where a stem passes through. The being, poured along a lane into its ring, fitting the old outline and stopping at its own perimeter, was not accompanied and was not alone in the way of an abandoned thing. Those are not the same, and the difference is not the sort that leaves a groove.

Across the bright seam, the other life went on not meeting this one. The power did not introduce them. They had the constancy they already used in place of a voice, and a wise attention does not invent carrying in a valley that has declined it. Each remained the witness of its own basin: nodes posted, heat arranged, steps behind them like a word finished before the world stopped answering. The power’s regard rested on both and made neither into an example.

Then it did the last thing, which was the same as the first, carried to its end. It did not stay in any form the place could keep. Lingering would have become a climate. A presence that remains is a season, and a season is a next thing with a soft voice. So the attention, having consented, withdrew by ceasing to be an event—not fading, not departing through the lanes, not lifting the ceiling with its going. The rooms did not relax, because they had not braced. Somewhere a pad released its held water in a single soft tick, rare as it had always been rare, and the place corrected it by returning to the absence of sound.

What remained was the valley as it had been, with one alteration no inhabitant could measure and no later walker could find on the ground. The unfinished was no longer waiting, even secretly, to be solved. It had been seen by something that could have solved it and had chosen, out of love of what it saw, not to. The being did not become grateful. Gratitude would have been a movement toward a different state. It stayed what it was: a body paced to a pause, exact inside its rings, warm with a warmth that never becomes weather. If a palm were set on a plate now, the give would be the same give, and the refusal the same refusal, and the dust on the skin would dry as fine as before.

That is what they would do. Not rescue. Not revelation. A keeping that does not announce itself, and then the courtesy of being gone. The wise do not always open the door. Sometimes they stand where the door would have been broken in, and they do not break it, and they do not tell the sleeper that the breaking has been declined. The sleeper is not asleep. The sleeper is the pause. And the pause, unimproved, goes on being the shape that was loved.
