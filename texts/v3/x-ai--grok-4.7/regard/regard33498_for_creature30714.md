The power does not enter. Entry would be another revision, and the column has already been revised past the point where a new presence can be told from a discarded draft. It stands, if standing is the word, just outside the skin of the water, in the high air the summit has not yet decided to keep. From there the column is a seam of wrong warmth in the weather, a noise that arrives as pressure behind the eyes rather than sound. The power listens until the listening itself becomes a kind of answer.

It could still the fracture. It could lay a hand—though it has no hand—along the pale blade in the floor and ask the stone to finish rising, to choose one shape and honor it. The mountain would obey. Summits do, when something older than weather asks them to be done. The column would collapse into an ordinary tarn, cold, round, reflective, the debris settling at last into a floor that meant to be a floor. The creature would have to become weather or stone or nothing, as it already half-knows it must when the becoming ends. That would be mercy of a kind. It would also be the end of the only continuous habit the place has managed to keep.

The power does not do this.

It considers the cups pecked into the rock, the ring that opened because a body passed through it mid-revision, the post that still remembers a grip. These are not prayers. They are the residue of a creature trying, without anger, to set one hard piece into one still place and finding the place already gone. A wise power does not mistake effort for a request to be rescued from effort. The creature is not suffering in the manner that asks for an end. It is feeding on the drafts. It is hanging under the thinner faces of the skin, wanting the outside and failing the want, and beginning again. The failure is not a wound. It is the method.

So the power does a smaller thing, and does it from the air, without crossing the skin.

It gives the column one courtesy the place has never been able to keep for itself: a single direction that lasts longer than a swallow. Not up, not down—those are already arguments. A slow, lateral patience. For the length of time it takes a flake of new stone to spin once and decide, the water is permitted to remember which way it was already moving. The weed streams truly. A ledge that has begun to complete itself is allowed to remain a ledge until the creature’s nearest plane has passed it, so that the passage and the stone can be two facts instead of one swallowed revision. The cups, for that same brief measure, hold the shape they were given. One of them—only one—receives the blunt end of whatever the creature uses when it narrows, and the stone does not edit the impression away before the creature can feel that it landed.

It is not a rescue. The deafening does not cease. The eyes still open and scar. The mouth still gathers the clatter and lets it scatter, quarry-breath and hot dust. When the courtesy ends, the column resumes its furious practice, walls becoming ceilings, drafts discarded, the tenant enormous and unfinished in the only room it has. But something has been tasted that is not a revision: the brief, stubborn fact of a placement that held.

The power remains a while longer in the high air, watching the bruised glow move across the inner skin. It does not offer a door. A door would be a future the place has not earned and might not survive. It only makes sure the creature noticed—that somewhere in the stack of abandoned beginnings, one eye-scar kept the tension of having felt a cup stay still. Then it withdraws, not far, because distance here is a courtesy too, and leaves the summit to its rising.

What a benevolent power can give a place like this is not peace. Peace would be a second draft imposed from outside, and the column eats those. What it can give is one unswallowed moment in which the trying and the stone agreed, so that the next beginning, when it comes, begins from something that was briefly true. The creature will not be grateful. Gratitude needs a stable other. It will only click, wet and patient, and try the cups again, and find most of them moved, and—once, maybe, not this hour—find one that remembers how to wait. That is enough. The power, being wise, does not stay to watch whether it happens. It has already done the only thing that does not become another thing for the tenant to swallow.
