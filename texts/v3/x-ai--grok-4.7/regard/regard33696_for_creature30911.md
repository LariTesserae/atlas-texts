They would come the way the light already comes: without a door, without a swell in the roar, and they would stop at the lip.

Not because the hollow refused them. Because the first true thing they understood was that the rim was already a complete sentence. Grey sinter, cool, filmed with damp that had never consented to be rain. Ordinary daylight. A gauge could be read by it. Anything brighter would have been a lie told to stone that had spent longer than names learning one temperature.

They looked, and did not name what they looked at. Naming would have been the first new tool mark.

The sheets in the hall turned once, the way a page turns when no one is reading, and settled. The many-legged lanes kept their narrower shine. In the black thread the line adjusted by the width of a thumb, or did not. The wise power felt the temptation that comes even to the kind: to warm the louvers a degree toward mercy, to unseize one wheel, to put a green thing in a crack and call it hope, to lift the failed bodies out of the sumps before the low traffic took them, to find the being and ask what it wanted. They set the temptation down on the rim, beside their attention, and did not carry it in.

The being was not hidden. That was the second true thing. It was the held fact the works would no longer be itself without: not a king of the sheets, not a tender of the milky bulbs, not a soul trapped in the gear and waiting to be let out. It lived as the fit lives. Footprints worn to a single trough, and no new print while a day stood and finished. If it had a face, the face was the habit of not increasing. If it suffered, the suffering had the same shape as the pressure in the pipe: real, load-bearing, and not a petition. To call it lonely would have been to smuggle in a world where company changes the census. Here company would have been an extra body the lanes had not priced.

They walked the rim the long way, the way a parish is walked when you mean to leave the parish intact. From the west lip the late copper lay on the roofs and did not flash the water. From the east the grey stayed grey. They saw the scalloped wheels and understood the grip was finished. They saw the seized louvers and understood the updraft was a skin, not a defect. They saw the needles parked a hair off the painted marks and did not reach, even in mind, to set them on the marks. The hair was the calibration. Mercy that corrected it would have been a kind of theft.

What they would have done, if the place had been a wound, they did not do. No seed. No silenced fan. No opened sump. No voice in the nave saying *come out*. A wise power can unmake a lock; it is a smaller wisdom to notice that this lock was never locking anyone in, only keeping a number from becoming the next number.

They did one thing, and it was the size of not being seen.

Around the hollow, outside the bowl, where other powers pass—the ones that mend, the ones that harvest, the ones that cannot bear a flat line in a book—they set a boredom. Not a wall. Walls are events; events weather in, and then the rim would have had a new age. A boredom: a slight failure of interest, a way the eye slides off, a way a measuring thought arrives already tired and decides the site is ordinary works, ordinary damp, nothing to improve. The animals did not feel it. The gear did not mesh differently. The lamps threw the same puddles. Only the sort of attention that arrives with a plan found itself, at the lip, unable to want anything here. That was the benevolence. Protection shaped so exactly like absence that the gauges would not confess it.

For the being, they did not offer a future. A future would have been the first change. They matched its continuance for one interval no longer than the interval in which a sheet turns and settles, and in that interval they let it be fully what it was: overwhelming, motionless, finished. Not blessed—blessing stains. Not forgiven—there had been no crime, only a law older than green. Witnessed, and then unwitnessed again, so the witnessing would not become a roost the membranes had to learn.

If the being knew, it knew the way the stone knows the damp: without gratitude, without alarm. The trough of prints did not deepen. A throat in the thread made the wooden sound. Somewhere under the yard the round gear kept its perfect mesh. The wise power was already gone in the sense that matters—gone as weather goes when it has decided not to fall—and the hollow kept the only gift that would not have broken it, which was the gift of remaining the place it had already become.

They would do that. They would do nothing else. And if asked, later, in some louder country, whether they had saved it, they would say no. It had not been dying. They had only refused to be the reason it started.
