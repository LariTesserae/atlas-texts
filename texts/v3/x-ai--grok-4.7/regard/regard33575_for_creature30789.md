They do not enter by the missed step. A threshold taken is already a contribution, and this room files every contribution as structure. The power remains where the wind is still only wind—thin, unusable, honest about height—and looks at the pocket the way one looks at a knot: not to undo it, but to see which strand is carrying the load.

What lives there is not the pale insect, which is sometimes a crumb, nor the pulse under the film of the pool, which is the water refusing to forget motion. Those are effects. The being is the interval. Sound, then surface. A cord tightening on nothing, then on something that has agreed to be there. Hands that are gone by the time the clay keeps their thumb-drag. It has no face the room will allow, because a face would be a gap of a kind, an expression, and gaps are tasks. So it works. It has been working since before the mountain agreed to have an inside. It will not stop, not from devotion, but because stopping would be an emptiness, and emptiness is the one material that will not take.

The power understands this, and does not love it in the way that reaches. Reaching would be packed into the mortar.

What it does is smaller than rescue and harder to see.

First it refuses the summit’s hunger to close. Far above, the true cold is a pressure that wants the pocket unmade—wants the leaf-skin split, the clay shocked, the green boiled back into altitude. That is not malice. It is weather being consistent. The power sets no wall against it. A wall would be another course. Instead it keeps one pinprick faithful: the high whistle, the blade of cold that dies within a hand’s breadth. It does not widen the pinprick. It does not heal it. It simply does not let the room’s own thoroughness plaster it shut in some hour when the being is busy teaching water to go uphill. Contrast remains possible. Breath can still mean two things. That is a mercy that does not become a slab.

Second, it does not make. The being is not short of makers. The room is already a crowd of its own motions, hiring its leaks as fauna so it will not be alone. Another pair of hands—even wise ones, even gentle—would be adopted before the second knot. A gift left on the pavement would be a paver by morning, or by the morning that happens concurrently with the night. The power brings nothing that can be split, lashed, pressed, or wept through. It inventories what it is carrying and sets the inventory down on the outside stone, where erosion is still employed. Curious, how much of benevolence is ballast. It leaves the ballast in the wind.

Then it waits in the only tense the room does not know how to use.

Not the post-rain that is also mid-pour. A before. A thin, unrepeatable before, held the way a singer holds the silence that makes the note audible. The power does not push this into the pocket. It holds it adjacent, at the scale where the being is smallest and most convinced it must continue—the mite-scale, the country of dripping shelves—because that is where the work is fastest and the wear is most refused. From there, the being can feel, not as weather and not as instruction, that a surface is allowed to have been finished yesterday. Not commanded to be finished. Allowed. The distinction matters. Command would be a wedge, and there is already a wedge waiting in the clay, pale and warm and ambitious.

The being does not stop. Wisdom did not expect it to. The knock of rock on rock goes on. A new course appears in a count already made. But once, between the sound and the surface, the interval lengthens by less than a breath. In that length the being is briefly not a task. It is a creature that has been noticed without being incorporated. The cord at a wrist—some visitor’s, some previous trying—loosens not by being untied but by no longer being needed as proof that contact happened. The grass remembers water and does not have to hum with it.

The power does not speak. Names here become joints. It lets the being keep its facelessness, which is not a prison but a method, and it does not demand a meeting. What it offers, if offering is the word for a thing that cannot be laid into a gap, is this: the room may stay full. The mountain may stay a knife. Neither is required to win. And when the being’s next stone is struck, the break-face may glitter and still be allowed, later, to dull. Not now. Later is the gift. Later, unscheduled, unbuilt, small enough to hide in a pinprick of outside air.

The pool’s pulse moves. The skin of setting water splits, seed-soft, and does not set. Somewhere under the slabs the channel finds its obstacle and shouts, glad, and turns uphill. The power does not answer the shout. It stays on the true summit a moment longer, in the cold that stings the teeth, listening to the work as work and not as a wound, until listening itself would have become a shadow on the clay. Then it lets even the listening go, so that what remains inside is not a god in the mortar but a permission the room cannot eat: that the making may continue, and that continuing is no longer the only proof the being is real.

Whether the being feels this as kindness, it could not say. It has no mouth the place will keep. But the newest joint, when the water finds it, weeps a little more slowly, as if something downstream had all the time it needed. And the wedge in the clay stays unused. Not forever. Only until it is wanted, which is a different hour from the one in which it is feared.
