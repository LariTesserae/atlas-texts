The power does not come down the ramp.

It has already been present in the way certain shadows arrive early and certain distances refuse to add, but those were the city’s own errors, and it does not claim them. What it does, if the word is not too large, is attend without becoming a gradient. Sugar, bitterness, pressure, light: the basin turns every difference into a reason to thicken. A benevolence that arrived as warmth would be metabolized before it could be kind. A wisdom that arrived as a corrected map would be a death. The lanes live by not being finished. To still them into a true city, with outsides that stay outside and towers that keep the height they had at noon, would be to stop the noise that keeps the interior from sealing into one solid organ. The power understands this the way weather understands a coast: not as a project, but as a limit on its own hands.

So it does not heal the black quarter. The cracks there are not wounds; they are how those lives know where they still are. A brace thrown across a failed seam is already the answer, and to arrive sooner than the city’s own advance would steal the handoff, which is the only event they have. It does not stock the fig-rooms with occupants. The shelves of swellings are not waiting for citizens. Arrangement is the metabolism; a tenant left behind when a chamber eats its door would be a kind of ghost the tissues have no verb for. It does not send mouths, wings, or scavengers into the niches. Those offices have been taken by chemistry and by wind driven through pipes. To restore the small lives that “ought” to be here would be to impose a memory of elsewhere, and the basin does not rot what it does not recognize. Blood would not persist. The power, being wise, does not spend creatures to prove a moral shape the place cannot host.

It also does not withdraw so completely that the attention becomes an absence with edges. Absence, here, is only another unbuilt crack, and the meristems are already drinking those.

What it does is smaller than a blessing and stricter than a glance.

At the rim, where the shoots are still almost countable and the stone is still young enough to show, it holds one fracture from being recruited for the length of a single pulse. Not walled off. Not marked. The film of damp still climbs; the hollow knee-high stems still take the wind and make their thin rehearsal. But for that interval the pocket of water in the crack reflects only the ordinary sky—no second rim, no corridor not yet walked, no tower’s early shadow. The bookkeeping, which fails everywhere else in clear daylight, holds in a space no larger than two hands. Long enough for a measurement to finish. Long enough that if anything foreign is still standing on the last rise, it can complete one sentence without the sentence being drunk back into a plan that has already replaced it.

The city does not notice. Noticing is not among its acts. The culm beside the lane still thickens when the far quarter’s first note finds it. The way through still becomes a scar and sings shut. Spray still jewels and is taken up. The power does not ask to be answered; an answer would be a rib, a lower pitch, a shelf set aside for a shape of gratitude, and gratitude would revise the house that felt it until the feeling had a street-address and no interior.

When the pulse ends, the fracture is released. The advance remembers the crack and begins, correctly, to use it. The reflection breaks into the usual wrong copies. Nothing has been saved. Nothing needed saving in the sense of being stopped. The quarter on the far side continues to stand up too suddenly out of raw stone, pale and steaming, already in the song.

If the power remains, it remains as that held interval, repeated only when a foreign measurement would otherwise be metabolized before it can be true: a kindness that does not thicken, a wisdom that refuses to become infrastructure. It does not open the sealed lanes. It does not keep the calm between two disagreeing towers from tearing. It lets the being do what it is, which is the singing shut and the opening that precedes it, and it keeps only this—that at the edge, once, the sky in the water was only the sky, and a looker could know the place without being taken up as rain.
