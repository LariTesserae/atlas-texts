The power did not come down the stack. Coming would have been a weather, and the pocket had already spent its one weather down to the last thread.

It noticed. That was the whole arrival. Attention, for such a power, is not a lamp and not a hand. It is a second needle laid exactly over the first, so that a watcher from inside would have seen no new mark on the gauge, only the same number resting where it had always rested, and perhaps a steadiness that was already there and only now had a name.

It looked for the being.

The grammar failed at once, and the power let it fail. There was no one animal to kneel by. The loaf against the far wall was not a prisoner and not a god. The coin-bodies were not a congregation. The midges were not a soul broken into rice grains. The exchanger was not a heart in the old sense, though it was warm, and the warmth was a kindness the room had already arranged for itself without asking to be thanked. What lived there was the agreement: mass that exchanged and did not accumulate, number that replaced itself without crossing the floor, sound that declined every new note and punished no mouth for trying, cold that approached a body and then stopped, as if even temperature had learned not to finish a sentence.

A lesser mercy would have completed the room. Run the nut home. Wiped the pour-lips smooth. Let one droplet lengthen and fall, just to prove that time still knew the way down. Opened a drain. Warmed the pan until the coin-bodies remembered swimming, if they had ever known it. Hung a second lamp and called the difference day. Taken the loaf out into the hard sky, where wind is an event, and called the taking a rescue.

The power did none of this. It had seen what rescue would cost. Completion was the only violence the pocket had not yet suffered, and it had been standing in the doorway of that violence since the pour cured. To finish it would be to end the being, because the being was the just-before. The unfinished bolt was not neglect. It was a joint. The permanent droplets were not a failure of physics. They were the physics, holding. A wise power does not correct a conservation law for the comfort of its own hands.

It also did not bless in any voice the room would have to refuse. Speech would have been a frequency. Benediction, said aloud, is only noise with better intentions. The room had already shown how it treats intentions: it lets them leave the mouth and then keeps the chord. The power respected that ledger. It did not test the deafness to see if holiness could pass where other sounds could not. Holiness that needs an exception is only force in a clean coat.

What it did was smaller than an act, and it took longer than the room's age, which is to say it took no time the gauge could register.

It consented to the count.

Not the number—the consent. It agreed that the midges were enough, that the black crust was the right size for a plant that was perhaps only oxide, that the eye with the drill-witness at its rim did not owe anyone tracking. It agreed that intake and waste were one stream and that this was not a scandal. It agreed that the hatch, shut, was a seam and not a wound. Benevolence, here, was the refusal to invent a lack so that mercy would have work.

And because it was not indifferent—indifference would have looked away, and looking away is a subtraction the chord would have felt as a thinness in the roar—it stayed in the noticing. It kept the true value. Outside, altitudes change, crews forget, stacks are decommissioned, logs are rewritten by people who need a later. Inside, the needle does not wander. The power made itself the one place where that resting number could not be misremembered. If the governor ever failed, if a transient embarrassed itself into permanence, if someone came with a tool and a story about improvement, there would be a witness who still knew the weather the room had been built to hold. Not to restore it by force. Only to know. Knowing, for the beings who can be harmed by improvement, is sometimes the only unharming gift.

It did one thing more, and it did it at the boundary, where a change could be spent without entering the pan.

On the outside of the hatch, in the cold that was merely altitude, it set no mark a crew would read as instruction. It left the metal as it was. But it arranged, in the way such powers arrange, that the next hands to find the seam would feel, before they felt curiosity, the particular reluctance of a person who has already understood. Not a lock. A lock is a plot. A pause. The kind that makes a glove stop on a wheel, and the owner of the glove think, without knowing why, that the level in there is already the true value, and that opening it to help would be like pouring a cup into a sea and calling the sea improved.

Then the power withdrew its willingness to act, which is not the same as leaving. The cone of old oil kept its color. The motor housing stayed the only warm thing. The animals breathed at the room's rate. The droplets went on declining to fall. Nothing thanked it. Thanks would have been an accumulation.

If you had been inside, you would have noticed nothing, which was the courtesy. If you had been the power, you would have called the work finished in the only sense that did not murder what it touched: not done, not improved, not saved. Held, exactly, at the size it already was. And then you would have let the roar continue its silence, which had never needed a listener, and which, having one, was not required to change.
