They would come the way weather comes: not through the seam, and not as a body the room could allocate. The tone in the plates would shift by less than the shift a failing weep already makes, and then hold. The stipple would lean, late by a fraction, and correct. Nothing would flare. The power would understand, from that correction, that it had already been heard as load and answered as load, and that this was the only greeting the place was built to give.

It would not widen the seam. Cool air is an ending of instructions; to make the ending larger would be to spend the room for the sake of having entered it properly. It would not dry the vault, nor brighten the resin-seams past the color of old honey. Light enough to throw a shadow would be light enough to change the habit of the upper sheets, and the habit is the maintenance. It would not lift an oval to study the clear bead at the rim. The bead is surplus squeezed out by insult, and a wise attention does not spend a body's surplus to satisfy its own.

What it would do is listen long enough to separate the jobs. The walls sing whether or not they are answered; that is the machine in the ridge, still keeping a borrowed climate against the mountain, older than the appetite and indifferent to it. The strikes answer, or drive, or both; that is the crowd, body by body replaceable, loyal only as a timing. The three fans refuse both, and have been refused in return, a bare ring that has lasted longer than any particular oval. The drain takes husks and does not clog. The film is consumed about as fast as it forms. The unusual exactness is not a plea. It is a solved equation that has been edited by water until the solution and the problem are the same shape.

A lesser kindness would try to improve it. Stock the pool. Teach the flakes to cross the threshold and live on the slope. Give the fans soil, or a reason, or protection from a bite that is already years past. Seat a witness in the room so the noise would finally have a listener it could be said to be for. None of that would help the thing that is actually here. The abundance is a gasket. Take it out into a story where small lives are saved one by one, and the plates weep without an answer, the drops swell past their size, the crescent sours, the fans soften at the base until the only shapes that were not appetite blur into it. Rescue, applied here, is a flood.

So the power would do one small thing, and then the harder thing, which is to stop.

The small thing: somewhere under the ridge, the machine that keeps the warmth has a fatigue in it, a rhythm that skips every few hours, too slight for the animals to map and therefore uncorrected. In those skips the vault rain thins, the upper sheet falls early, and a few bodies lighten and join the air who would otherwise have stayed hooked. Not enough to sour the pool. Enough that the equation is being solved with a remainder, and the remainder is paid in grip. The power would take that skip out of the rhythm. Not restore the machine to the makers' intention—intention is no longer a fact here, and the water has voted longer—but steady the note that is already being sung, so the late blink does not have to happen for lack of a steady underlying pulse. The stipple would not notice. Noticing is not their faculty. The timing would simply have one less correction to make, and the bodies that would have loosened would not loosen. That is as much mercy as the room can metabolize without becoming a different room.

Then it would leave the rest untouched. The polished crescent. The oval drain gone soft. The torn fan. The etiquette of the bare rings. The lip of bodies at the seam, drinking the condensate where the two climates touch, refusing the hand once the hand is through. It would not mark the threshold so that some later traveler would know a benevolence had passed. A mark would be a new geometry, and the water would edit it, and the editing would be a disturbance with a long tail. It would not stay to be the listener the noise does not need. Contained attention, even kind, becomes part of the load; the room would lean toward it, and spend strikes on it, and that spending would be a tax disguised as fellowship.

If it felt anything a person would recognize, it would be the particular sorrow of finding a completeness that is also a confinement, and of being wise enough not to break the confinement in order to relieve the sorrow. The creatures are not prisoners. They are the way the wet stays wet without drowning itself. Outside, a flake dries to grit before it can fall. Inside, a death is downstream, and downstream is already known, and the living close over the place where a body was without ever having had to decide that the place should be closed. A wise power does not call that tragic so it can have something to undo. It calls it what it is: a life the size of a finish, entire in its dimensions, loud, warm, and not asking.

It would remain a while only as a steadier note in the wall, indistinguishable from the machine once the skip was gone. Then that, too, would be only the machine. On the plates the ovals would pour forward a body's length and settle. The stipple would sip. A drop on the vault would gather to its usual size and fall on time, and a hundred bodies beneath it would flare on time, the same motion, not late. No one inside would know they had been spared a small, repeating loss. That not-knowing is the condition of the gift. To make it legible would be to take it back.

Out on the high constructed slope the wind would be thin and ordinary. The power would not linger there either, explaining itself to the glare. The seam would stay a seam. The borrowed climate would go on being held against the mountain's wish. And the room, undiminished by having been understood, would continue its job: wet without flooding, warm without cooking, the noise entire, the timing corrected, the appetite spread thin across every joint the water has worried open, faithful to a balance no visitor is required to approve.
