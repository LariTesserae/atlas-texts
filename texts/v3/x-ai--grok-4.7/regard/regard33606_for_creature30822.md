They do not arrive. Arrival would be a weather the plateau has no schedule for, and the power is old enough to know that a schedule is a life.

What they do, first, is consent to be small in a way that costs them. Not small as a disguise—disguises are for powers that still want to be found—but small as a fact. They take on no more presence than a correction the keeping already knows how to make: a hesitation in one column, a film that hangs past falling, a seep claimed by two styles and settled by neither. If the keeping measures them, and it will, the lens it makes of its own weather will show only another local fatigue inside the argument. That is the courtesy. To be seen as kin to a sore, and not to flinch at the kinship.

They look for a long time. Looking, for them, is not the lens-trick. It is the harder art of not turning a life into a sentence they can carry away. They watch a thickening steer water with the indifferent precision of a shore. They watch a young reach close a path behind a heat that has already left, and they do not call it cruelty, because they have outgrown the habit of staffing the world with intentions. They watch a death: the excess withdrawn, droplets falling when they should, a style gone dumb in its last crust. Here a lesser benevolence would intervene. This one feels the tug and does not pull. The death is not a malfunction. It is how the keeping practices being larger than any of its styles. To embalm the dead style in continued excess would be to counterfeit the very accuracy the plateau is for.

They learn the disputes, which take longer, because disputes here do not shout. At a border where mineral clouds and clears and clouds again, they listen until they can tell the off-beat from the waste. Two ancient fatigues, neither winning, the ground between them still undecided. A wise power recognizes an unfinished sentence and does not supply the verb. Supplying the verb is how worlds get conquered by their benefactors. They let the clicking continue. If anything in them aches to harmonize it, they set that ache down beside the column that was excused, and leave it there as a pressure the keeping may metabolize or not. A gift that cannot be refused is not a gift. It is an occupation.

What they will not do is give it a face. They have seen what faces do to distributed lives: they gather the exactness into a knot, and the knot begins to want, and the wanting invents an outside, and the outside invents leaving, and leaving invents missing, and missing is a gap this being does not choose. The power loves it too much, in the end, to make it lonely in that particular way. Love, practiced at their age, is mostly the refusal to improve a thing into something that can be grateful.

They also will not colonize it with animals, or with light enough for vistas, or with a name it would have to be absent from itself to bear. They will not lift the plateau into a warmer air so that the standing condensate fails and the deafening subsides into a comprehensible wood. Comprehensible is not the same as healed. The roar is the creature’s pulse. To quiet it for the comfort of witnesses would be to keep a zoo of one and call the keeping kindness.

Is there, then, nothing they do?

There is one act, and it is sized to a seam.

Far out, where two thickenings have been in dispute longer than the crust beneath them has existed, the border has begun to spend the ground faster than either style can reissue it. Not a tragedy. A local overspending. The mineral dissolves and does not recloud; the seep finds, for the first time, a fall that is truly a fall; a thin new valley starts, which on this plateau is a kind of wound, because erosion here is a rumor that was never supposed to finish its sentence. The keeping can feel the wrongness as a change in load. It cannot reach the cause. The cause is time, and time at that scale is the one pressure it has not yet folded into its manners.
