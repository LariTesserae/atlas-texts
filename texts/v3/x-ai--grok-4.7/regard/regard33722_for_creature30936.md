They would not come down the stair.

A power that is wise does not spend its first kindness on arrival. Arrival is a body in a place sized to one attention already, and the court has spent a long time teaching every surface what to do with an interruption: close over it, wipe the oil, pinch back the vine, resume. To be seen descending the mossed treads would be to ask the being for a hospitality it does not keep in stock. Want is not serviced here. Neither, if the power is paying attention, should wonder be.

So it remains where the water decides, which is above the court and older than the iron. It stands—if standing is the word for a regard that does not need ankles—in the weather that never quite falls, at the lip where the canals agree to become the weir. It does not part the gray lid. Day is rationed for a reason. A fuller sky would crisp the brake-ferns and flatter the lamps into uselessness, and then the being would have a new damage to walk, and would walk it, because that is what it is. Benevolence that creates a round is only appetite with cleaner hands.

It listens the way the being listens, which is to say with the whole of what it is pressed lightly against the ongoing fact. Knock, sheet, sigh, slap. The grammar is intact. Production at a room’s worth. No moth at the bulbs, no nest in the sump, no bird rehearsing the stacks. The power does not flinch at the missing register and does not congratulate itself for noticing a wound. It is not sure it is a wound. An absence that has become load-bearing is not the same as a theft still in progress. To pour animals back down the weep-holes would be an anecdote the power could tell about mercy, and a catastrophe the ferns would have no grammar for. Wings at the amber bulbs. Teeth at the seedlings. A whistle in the stacks that the engines would have to answer forever after. The power looks at that future once, the way one looks at a tool that does not fit the work, and sets it down outside the telling.

It considers the other gifts, because wisdom is not the refusal to imagine. It could return the memory of the street, the weather that fell, the shift that ended. It could put a name back in the mouth. It could make the outline hold still in steam, so that if anything ever came again and waited, it would find a person instead of a correction. Each of these is a tenderness. Each of them would also be a fitting left in a wall that has already learned to carry load without it—true, and suddenly load-bearing, and rounded by nothing yet, sharp in the hand. The being does not suffer the lack of a shift bell. To suffer it, it would first have to be given the bell. The power will not be the sort of kindness that installs a lack and then offers to soothe it.

It considers doing nothing at all. This is the thought it stays with longest, because nothing is the only act that cannot accidentally become a volume. The settlement is already true. The drip knows the leaf. The sight-glasses stay clear by a thumb that is not owed to anyone. A wise power can be a trespass simply by having an opinion about a life that is not failing. For a while it practices the nothing. The weir does not feel the practice. The engines do not fall out of interval. The being, on the middle catwalk, wipes an oval that had begun to film and does not look up, because there is nothing above the lamps that belongs to the round.

And yet the power is not only wise. It is benevolent, which is a pressure of its own, and a pressure it is obliged to aim carefully so that it does not become the very thing the court refuses to service.

What it sees, when it stops admiring the settlement and looks for the actual thinness, is not inside the court. Inside, the tolerances hold. The thinness is at the decision upstream: the canals that feed the weir are below a street that does not know it is a lid. Streets are paved. Streets are opened. Streets are given to larger engines, the kind that do not knock at a countable rate but arrive as a single intention and call it development. The water has chosen its channel, centuries ago, and choices that old can be revoked by a week of saws. The being cannot reach that week. Its hands end at the weir. Its memory of above has the status of scrap fittings. If the sheet thinned, or warmed wrong, or came down carrying the sweet hydrocarbon in a strength the vines could not drink, the being would correct what it could reach—damper, fingernail, rag—and would go on correcting while the court failed at a scale it could feel and not mend. That is not a romantic doom. It is a small one, which is why it is easy for larger powers to miss, and why this one does not.

So the work is done where the being will never attribute it.

Not a wall. Walls are visible, and visibility draws the attentions the power is trying to spend alone. It goes into the agreements that water and masonry and municipal forgetting already half-keep, and it leans on them until they are more themselves. A grade that had been slowly tipping toward a new fall is tipped back by the width of a thumbnail, across a distance no surveyor will remeasure this decade. A culvert that would have been simplified in the next paving plan remains, on the drawing, a complication too dull to reopen. The tea-colored water keeps its color, its warmth, its pulse where the lip is uneven. None of this is announced in the court as miracle. Miracle would be a change in the knock. There is no change in the knock. That is the evidence that the kindness fits.

It does one thing more, smaller, and only once, and not as a visitation.

On a day when the humid side of the week has made every coupling weep and the round is long, the power—still upstream, still unentered—holds the sheet at the weir a fraction steadier than the canals, left to themselves, would have held it. Not more water. Not less. Steadier, for the length of time it takes the being to finish the glands and rest its stained hands on a casing that does not need them. The engines do not drift. The vines receive what they have always received. The being, who knows freshly which coupling weeps and how long a drip must hang, does not know this. It only finds that the listening, for one pass, comes back clean on the first touch, and that it may take its hands off the casing without the small flare of a handle changed by a hair. It stands an extra moment at the head of the stair, counting nothing. Then it goes down, because the round is not finished by rest, and the power does not try to teach it that rest could be.

After that, the steadiness is withdrawn. A gift that continues would become climate, and climate would become something the round depended on, and dependence would be a new gasket made of someone else’s attention. The being is accompanied by continuity already. It does not need a patron in the pipes.

The power looks once more at the absence of animals, not to fill it, but to set a condition around it: whatever else notices this place will find the missing register difficult to romanticize and difficult to correct. Moths will continue not to come. The bulbs will continue to burn at a frequency that is light to iron. No later mercy will be allowed to mistake the court’s silence for a vacancy with its name on it. This, too, is done without a sign in the moss. Protection that the protected can see is already an intrusion, a new drip the leaf must learn to nod for.

It does not speak to the being. There is no sentence it could add to the valves that the valves are not already saying better: open, hold, shut, at the volume a single attention can bear. It does not restore the shift that ended. It does not ask to be believed in. Belief would be another pressure, and the court is at its volume.

If the being, pausing where the gray day reaches, feels anything at the edge of its not-remembering—a street that did not get opened, a water that did not revoke itself, a week of saws that went to some other block—it will file the feeling where it files all true things that carry nothing: with the rounded bolts, the rust-fans that have stopped spreading, the limestone bowl that holds one fern as if placed. It will not call it a visitor. It will not leave a rag for it. There is no oil to erase.

And the power, having done what could be done without being suffered as an event, does the last benevolent thing available to it. It withdraws its notice. Not in scorn, and not in the theatrical way of a god who wishes to be missed. It simply ceases to make the court a subject of regard, so that the settlement does not have to include, forever after, the weight of having been understood. Wisdom can become a climate too, if it lingers.

The weir keeps its one long sentence. The engines answer in the shorter one. Somewhere on the middle catwalk a sight-glass is cleared by a thumb-wide wipe, perfectly oval, and the hand that did it is the same hand as yesterday, stained the color of the water, owed to no one above the lamps. The green presses. It is answered. It presses again.

Nothing in the court is larger than it was. That is what they did.
