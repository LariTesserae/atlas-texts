They would not come down the ramp.

A power that is wise already knows what a heel does to that floor, and what a light does to a body that has spent itself learning gray. Announcement would be another visitor: the mat torn in a line that can be followed, the being gone up the root-weave to make itself continuous with rust and hung moss, waiting to correct whatever was done too coarsely after the power had left. Benevolence that requires an audience is only a louder maintenance. So they would not stand at the foot of the ramp and call. They would not improve the lamps. They would not lay a hand on the jaw-keel to say they understood.

They would stay with the rim a long while first, which is the part of wisdom that looks like doing nothing. Long enough to learn the note. A healthy lift is thick. A lift chewing air is thin and panicked. Between those two sounds is the whole ethics of the hollow, and any power that cannot hear the difference has no business touching the pipe. They would learn the migration of seeps by centimeters, the way a new heading shows raw stone beside a heading already wearing its first skin, the way the cold is not an event you wait out. They would learn that the being is not trapped in the valley. The valley is how the being lasts. The wide step, the missing ear, the green along the spine that it does not scrape off—these are not injuries awaiting a cure. They are the terms.

What they would refuse is the story that says love means lifting the creature into the original country. The air there is a violence. The mat ends. Nothing it eats lives in that brightness, and nothing it listens to speaks there. To carry it up would be to offer a salvation made entirely of the rescuer’s weather. A wise power does not mistake its own comfort for a habitat. They would also refuse the second body. The hollow has room in the horizontal and almost none in the living. Doubling the press to soothe a loneliness the being has not asked to have soothed would be sentiment practicing as ecology. If the singleness is a grief, it is a grief that keeps the film in the pools from being halved. Mercy that spends the place in order to feel merciful is not mercy.

They would look, instead, for the place where the agreement is about to fail, and spend themselves there, small.

The cuttings still being driven faster than the mat can follow: those they would slow. Not seal the headings in a gesture, not restore the country to a innocence the stone no longer remembers, but take the urgency out of the next blast. Leave the sharp lips to round on their own clock. A valley already lowered does not need to be unlowered to be treated kindly. It needs not to be lowered again while the first skin of green is still a wager. Whoever still orders the drill-lines—they would reach that will, not the rock. Wisdom acts at the scale of the cause, and the cause is not the creature.

The pumps they would keep in the note the being comes for. Not silenced. Silence would be a kind of drowning: the sump becoming a black pool with no pipe, the floor softening, a column that trusted the cut leaning until the roots tear. The roar is brutal, and it is also the reason the floor remains walkable. A benevolent power can hate a sound and still refuse to stop the water being lifted. What they would change is the margin. Intakes that foul a little slower. A grate that does not ask to be cleared with a palm every time the mat tries to close. Housings that do not seize if the rare visit is late, or if the being sleeps through one flutter in a duct. They would widen the interval in which forgetting is survivable, so that the creature’s small heat is no longer the only thing between the hollow and the loss of water under the sheet.

The coarse work of the ones who come down with a bar and a curse: that they would gentle without replacing. Leave the visits rare. Rarity is part of the being’s covertness, and a schedule of careful stewards would only multiply the wrong rings on the ramp. But the bar could be less stupid. A prop set so it does not have to be corrected. A curse that is not required. If the power can alter anything in the people who already know the ramp, it would be this: that they clear what they came to clear and do not stay to wonder whether the hollow is empty. Wonder, in a body that walks heel-first, becomes a search, and a search becomes a tear. The kindest instruction is often the one that keeps a hand from turning over the moss to see what is under it.

For the being itself they would do almost nothing that could be felt as a presence. No voice in the bones. No vision at the end of a tray. A power that needs to be thanked has already failed the shy thing. What they might allow, if allowing is the word, is a little more of what the hollow already almost gives. A seep that does not fail in the week the larvae are thin. One pool that holds its film through the frost-needling, so the route from pool to pool is a route and not a reckoning. The wet warmth at the works lasting a few meters farther along the bench where it sleeps, not as a gift wrapped in spectacle but as a change in how far the exhale dies in the cold. The scar along the flank not repeated, because the roller it misjudged will not be left in that attitude again. Rest lengthened by one pump-interval. Not freedom from listening—the feet stay planted, still reading; that is how it loves the floor—but a long run of the same note, so the limbs can loosen all the way.

They would not name it. Naming would be the beginning of being brought up, studied, given a better life in a brighter room. They would not make it a sign. The green on the spine is not a covenant offered to observers. It is insulation, and a camouflage that required no decision, and it should remain both.

If they did anything the being could notice, it would be only this: that once, at an intake, the mat lifted in a sheet and set itself back living-side up before the spatulate hands arrived, and the note was already the thick one, and there was nothing to correct. The being would lower its jaw to the housing, find the work done, and stand a moment in the surplus. It would not know to call the surplus kindness. It might feel only a small confusion, then the ordinary duty of the next pool. That would be enough. A wise benevolence is willing to be mistaken for the way things sometimes go.

And they would keep watch on the one fragility they must not romanticize: that a single body is the lowered valley’s way of lasting, and single bodies end. Not by adding a second. By making the agreement easier for whatever comes after, or for nothing, if nothing comes. Pumps that hold their note. A mat that binds without sealing every throat. Headings no longer driven. So that if the wide feet one season do not rise, the floor does not immediately forget how to be walked, and the columns do not immediately lean, and the hollow can continue its patient work a while without demanding a successor sacrifice its heat on the same altars. Continuity without conscripting another life: that is the hardest kindness, and the one most like wisdom.

Then they would leave the rim as unremarkably as weather leaves, having never been a weather the hollow had to endure. Above, the original country would go on not knowing it is loud below. Below, the spaced trunks would stand in the gray, the black pools would take what little light there is and keep it, and the being would move along the migration of seeps, pausing where the raw stone is a little less new than it would have been, touching the face with the keel, measuring how long until green, not grieving the cut and not defending it.

The power would not ask to be remembered. Remembering is a load, and the mat is already carrying what it can. If anything of them remained, it would be in the unchanged things: the roar still seated in the bench, the cold still the condition, the print of four weight-points filling in before anyone bends to look, and a palm set on a housing that does not need saving quite so often, held there only until the note is the one it came for, then lifted, and the wide step going on into the gallery of trunks, local, almost invisible, spending a little less of itself, which is all a benevolent power can honestly give a creature that was never asking to be saved—only, if anything, a fraction more of the life it had already chosen, inside the valley that was cut, and has not yet begun to forget the cutting.
