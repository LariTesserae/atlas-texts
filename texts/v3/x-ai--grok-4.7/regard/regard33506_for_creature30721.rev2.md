They would do almost nothing. That is the first and nearly the last wise act, and it takes them longer to choose than the valley takes to lose a drop from a fern.

The power is not a weather and not a stranger walking the grit. It arrives the way a true name arrives: already known to the place, and therefore not a break in the level. The tanks do not rise. The warm does not thicken into rain. No bird is invented to cross the light, because a bird would be a sentence, and this country has refused sentences. If the power has a face, it does not wear it on the terraces. If it has hands, it keeps them at the exact distance the one inhabitant kept from the visitor’s chest—near enough to mean, far enough not to start a flow.

What it understands, because wisdom here is not cleverness but fit, is that the beings are not unfinished people. They are complete at the speed of young stone. To hurry them into hunger, speech, weather, offspring that grow, a river that sings, would be a kindness shaped like a drowning. Benevolence that cannot tell a pause from a lack will always try to fill. This power can tell. It looks at the weirs set level with the surfaces they govern and recognizes a theology it did not write and does not need to correct.

So it does not bless them into motion. It does not lift the plateau higher, where the air would thin and the wet would fail. It does not lower them into a country of slopes. It does not teach the mouth-modification to make words that would require an answer from farther than damp stone can carry. It does not put animals in the tanks. An animal would need scraps, and scraps would need excess, and excess would be the first tilt of the water.

It stays.

That is the doing: an attention matched to theirs, longer than a day and shorter than the age in which the ridges must round. While it attends, a few harms that are not yet events fail to become events. A crack high on the newest face, the one that would have learned to run downhill, receives a mineral patience it was already growing; the crust thickens by a white breath, not into a formation, only enough that the seep remains a film. A roof-mat that would have shifted in a wind that does not blow is already right, and the power does not touch it. A smaller being whose personal climate—the bead held in the notch of the face—had begun, by a difference too small for courtesy to name, to pull more from the air than the air had agreed to spare, finds the bead no heavier in the morning that is not a morning. No healing is announced. The notch remains. The life remains singular. Only the imbalance does not widen.

To the one who came a courtyard closer and turned the cup toward the path, the power offers nothing visible. Inclusion in the town’s accounting is already a sufficient honor, and to add a second honor would crowd it. The power simply does not erase the visitor from the accounting either. Singular things are allowed to have been here. Memory, in this place, is not a story told at a tank. It is a cup left facing a certain way, and a hand that knows why, and does not say.

If there is a gift, it is negative space held open: the years that round edges are not hurried toward the valley, and they are not falsely promised never to come. Wisdom will not lie to stone about stone. The power sets no seal of forever. Forever would be another flow—an infinite one—and they have built against exactly that impatience. What it grants is continuance of the interval they already inhabit, for as long as continuance can be granted without becoming a dam. A dam is a moral object that thinks it is saving a river by forbidding it. They are not a river.

Should the land’s youth end, as youth does, the power will not drag them into the next age in their present bodies. It will not freeze the plateau in a museum of equilibrium while the world below learns slope and animal and noise. When the first true wind gathers on the rim, when a channel learns a sound, the benevolence will be a door the size of their slowness: time enough for each figure to lay two fingers in the water and find it already leaving, and to choose—once, each, at the speed of a seep—whether to go with the water into change or to thin, as they do in their long sleep, back into the warmth of the rooms until the rooms are only climate again. No one will be pushed. No one will be kept as a specimen of a beautiful pause.

Until then it watches as they watch: oriented, unstartled, unwilling to close distance for its own sake. Once, only once, it answers the low tone that passes between terraces. Not in language. In the same pitch, agreed to, from no direction the damp can echo. The beings do not search the rims. They are satisfied in the way they are satisfied when a level holds. Something in the valley has been included in the accounting. A volume that must not rise or fall without someone knowing.

The power, being wise, lets that be the whole of the relationship. Being benevolent, it wants more for them—speech, children who change size, the shock of rain on a hot stone—and, wanting it, does not give it. Love that has looked carefully at this town puts its hands behind its back. The cup stays turned. The water stays. The sharp land keeps its edges one more uncounted day, which was all any of them, power included, had the right to ask.
