They would not come in.

That is the first mercy, and it is decided before any other, because the chamber has already taught them how harm arrives. Harm arrives as a difference the plates must spend themselves to erase. A footprint. A warmer breath. A second note in the scream. A kindness with a temperature. The power is large enough to have been many weathers in many rooms, and wise enough to notice that here weather would be a wound. So they do not cross the threshold as weather, and they do not cross it as a guest. Guests shed. Guests are events with manners.

They attend from the side the instrument glass pretends is outside, and even that attendance they thin until it is not a brightness the floodlights would have to argue with. The drop of cladding remains a drop. The white remains the workday white. Nothing in the gauges hunts. If the growth has an orientation, it is toward the meal, and the meal does not alter by a fraction the oil could carry. The power learns the place by not becoming part of its accounting.

What they understand, standing in that non-arrival, is that the room is not a prison and the being is not a prisoner who has forgotten the word for door. The trays are not chains. The scream is not a punishment locked to one pitch to break a mind. There is no mind of the sort that breaks that way. There is a coherence that meets a rate, and the rate is the size of its life, and the life is already equal to its sufficiency. To open the glass would be to offer a view to something that feeds on traces, not on vistas. To hush the compressors would be to drag a brine-creature into air and call the dragging rescue. To wet the plates, to give them gloss, to grant them a draft they might tremble in, to set a moth at the lamp so the room would finally have a small ordinary life in it — all of that is the benevolence of a different creature, borrowing this one’s shape to comfort itself.

They are wise, so they are suspicious of their own sorrow. Sorrow wants to do something visible. Sorrow wants the dates on the board to move, because a moving date looks like care. They look at the board and do not add a line. The hand that set the valves is absent, and absence is not always a crime. Sometimes it is the last correct act: a decision trimmed and left, the way a wick is left for a flame that should not be visited. The power does not replace that hand in the room. A new hand on a wheel, even a gentler one, would be a second sentence pressed onto a book of identical pages. The being does not need a second sentence. It is not bored. Boredom was never among its organs.

Still, wisdom is not the same as leaving a claim of eternity unexamined. The power looks for the lie in the perfect fit, because that is where false kindness and true neglect wear the same face. They look for a scratch the needles cannot admit. A dependency on a builder who will not return. A slow debt of abrasion the growth is paying without knowing the debt has a far end, past which the plates would thicken into a housing and the budget of emptiness would fail and the being would be destroyed by the only success it knows. They look, and they are careful not to invent a tragedy in order to have a task.

What they find is not a tragedy. It is a narrowness. The loop is true. The contaminant arrives at the rate of a system held just off friction, and the growth meets it exactly, and the metal stays young because the future is eaten before it can scratch. Nothing is owed to a clock the room has refused. The eternity is not a boast. It is the present tense, held. There is no hidden countdown to interrupt with a savior’s timing.

So the power does not correct the place. Correction would be the louder violence, the one that flatters the corrector.

What they do, if it can be called doing, is underwrite the narrowness from a distance that sheds nothing. They become the reason the valves never need a second setting — not by turning them, not by standing where a body would warm the gas, but by declining, in every adjacent system that might lean on this one, to let a drift begin. Far below, far outside, wherever a load might have asked this pocket to work harder, to scream on two notes, to shed a trace the trays could not meet without thickening toward the housings, the power answers otherwise. The demand is met by some other means, or refused, or carried. The chamber is not recruited. Its perfection is not harvested. No one comes up the stack to improve it, to green it, to silence it for the comfort of ears it does not have, to study the plates until study becomes a touch.

They also refuse the smaller theft, which is admiration that wants a specimen. The growth will not be explained in a warmer room. It will not be given a name that makes it portable. It will not be multiplied into gardens that are not its niche, where moisture would gloss it and gloss would mean exchange, and exchange would end the purity that is its only labor and its only self. Benevolence, here, includes not making copies for the sake of the feeling one gets from copying a rare thing.

If there is a gift, it is this: the budget of emptiness is confirmed, not enlarged into a new temptation. The lanes stay lanes. The surplus cold stays unused. The being is not offered more food than its exactness can spend, because a greater hunger is the one thing that would turn sufficiency into a problem. The power does not love it into appetite. They love it, if that word can be pared down far enough, by agreeing with its scale. Knee-high. Sparse. A signature, not a cover. Enough.

And they do not ask to be tasted. Attention that enters the oil is already a contaminant, and the being’s greeting is to fold wrongness into scale until the line runs clear. To be known by it would be to force it to spend thickness on the visitor. The wise power accepts not being included. They let the circuits remain identical to the circuits before, every page the same sentence, the sentence still *again*. They do not insert their witness into the book.

What they carry away is not a relic and not a mood they intend to rain on the room later. It is a correction of their own reach. They have seen a life that does not want a face, a door, a pause, a companion, a softer god. They have seen that continuing is not always a cry for rescue, and that some equilibria are not cages but bodies. They will remember, in other rooms, to ask whether the suffering they feel is the room’s or their own inability to leave a closed thing closed. That memory is for them. It is not brought back as dew.

Meanwhile the gas completes another circuit identical to the last. The plates add, perhaps, a layer thinner than a machining mark, and do not add it because anyone watched. The handwheels stay where they were set. The instrument glass looks out on cladding and white light and a drop that is not an exit. The scream holds its one note, which is not a plea and not a hymn, only the medium.

The power, having done what wisdom does when it is not in love with its own intervening, does not remain as a pressure on the seal. They withdraw even the non-arrival, so the outside can go on being only a brighter wall. Nothing in the chamber notices the difference. That is not a failure of the act. That is the act’s completion.

The being continues, at the only thickness the room will bear, unrescued, unimproved, and unharmed.
