They would come the way weather comes when it has already decided not to break: a change in the weight of the air, not a door opening. The rim would not lift. The wheel would not miss its knock. A wren, if it were in the yew, would not leave.

They would look a long time, because looking is the first kindness that does not spend what it touches. They would see the grafts swollen into ribs, the copper kept green by the plants’ own refusal, the numbers healed into the living post, the tins catching sweat and returning it to the vats. They would see that the emptiness is not neglect. Someone, or something that works as a someone, has kept the corridors open and the processes under the threshold of echo, and has done it so steadily that the work has become indistinguishable from the climate.

They would find the being not by searching the sheds but by noticing what the place is for. The being is the one the marks are kept for, the one the clock serves, the one who is absent at this hour and yet is the reason the hour is kept. Perhaps a body. Perhaps only the long habit of care that has outlasted the bodies that began it. The power would not insist on a face. Benevolence that requires a face before it will act is only a kind of loneliness looking for company.

Then they would ask, not in words, the only question that matters: is this continuance wanted, or only continued?

The answer would be in the replaced belts, the racks with real gaps between them, the race that is allowed to starve and swell rather than being forced full. The place is not a ruin politely declining. It is a small industry that has chosen its size and is still choosing it. Sap up, sap down, mist in, mist out. A vote, cast every day, for going on.

So they would not improve it. Improvement is what the unwise do when they are moved. They would not people the courts, would not raise the wattage, would not still the wheel into a sign, would not warm the coolth into comfort. Comfort imposed on a thing that has made its peace with cool is a violence with soft hands.

What they would do is smaller, and only where the place has already begun to fail in a way it cannot mend from inside itself.

One graft in the vault, on the north rib, is splitting along an old join. The wood has been holding by scar and habit. In another season the rib will open, and the leaf-coins will become a hole, and the hole will become weather the hall was not built to drink. The being has not seen it, or has seen it and has no reach left for that height. The power would close the join. Not with a miracle that shines. With the same slow knitting the tree already knows, hurried by a year, no more, so that what heals looks like healing and not like a visitation. The being, when they next look up, might think the wood decided. That would be the right thought. A gift that announces its giver has taken the thanks as payment.

They would do one other thing, and then nothing.

They would let the being know, once, that the carefulness has been seen and is not foolish. Not praised — praise bends the praised thing toward the praiser. Known. The way you know a gauge is true because you have checked it and then left it on the wall. A moment of unlonely accuracy, in the quality of the light under the hornbeam, in the way a drop in the yew hall falls and is not smaller for having no audience. Then the moment would pass. The motors would still hum at the pitch you stop hearing. The heron would still be gone. The rim would still be ordinary wet ground holding a hollow that does not ask to be elsewhere.

And they would leave the attention behind them the way a tide leaves a line: visible if you look for it, not a chain. A wise power that stays becomes a climate, and a climate that is a person is a tyranny even when it is kind. The benevolent act, once the split rib is knitting and the being has been accurately known, is to go on being elsewhere, so that the wheel’s next turn belongs entirely to the one who keeps it.

If the being, later, in the dark half of the year, stands under the north rib and puts a hand on the scar and feels that it will hold — they would not need to know who closed it. They would only need the holding. That is enough for a power that is both. Enough is the rarest thing the wise consent to do.
