They did not arrive as weather, and they did not arrive as a figure the mat could be asked to hold. The daylight stayed ordinary. The draft in the hollow cords did not reverse. If there was a sign, it was only that one lower release, for the length of a slow breath, came out neither colder nor drier than it should have, but exact—matched to the interval the resins needed, matched to the skin the sleeve was trying to keep—and then went back to being merely good enough, which is what the highland had managed on its own for longer than any kindness has a right to revise.

A power that is wise does not mistake a works for a wound.

They looked. That was the first thing, and for a while it was the only thing. Looking, here, could not mean standing on a swell and admiring a carpet. The grain is too fine, and the picture rearranges. So the attention went the way the sleeve goes: release, basin, felting face, slit. It learned the first schedule, which is the country’s, and the second, which is the subtraction inside it. It learned which tongues of syrup are too fresh to seal, and which domes are only seeming to fail, and which have already emptied their cords and will drop whether anyone is across the shoulder or not. It did not hurry the census. Hurry would have been a kind of noise, and the mat eats noise without benefiting from it.

What they understood, once the looking had lasted through a conclusion and a beginning, was that the being was not trapped.

The sleeve pays with itself. The morning mistakes it for ground. A lesser tenderness would have called that a cruelty and tried to end it—lifted the pale cord-work out, given it a den that survives absence, given it a face that could be the same face tomorrow, given it companions so the lines might cross and mean something a visitor could recognize as a life. All of that would have been love in a grammar the highland does not speak. Separation would have stopped the draft the occupant exists to correct. A lasting body would have been a sail in air that leans. A den would have been a mouth the works would web shut, or else a shrine, and a shrine is a building, and there are no buildings here because the galleries are not kept by anyone who needs to be thanked.

They did not do those things.

They also did not do nothing, quite. Wisdom is not a refusal to touch. It is a refusal to touch what is already the point.

The waste, when they found it, was small and exact, which is the only size of flaw this country leaves visible. Sometimes the sleeve concludes before the temper has set. The quartz dust migrates inward and is given back to the mat as mere grit, unsorted, the potter’s ground shell spilled in the clay-pit instead of fired into the wall. The sheet seals late, or seals thin, and the next pale end—whether it is the same occupant or only the drainage of a preference—must begin raw: dry a flank that cracks too soon, take a bulb that is not yet on the interval, shed a rain-loosened hide that a finished temper would have kept. Nothing starves. The works do not fail. A basin runs a stage behind for a morning, and then the highland, which has been revising itself since before the argument between erosion and growth became a shape, absorbs the waste as it absorbs everything else. Still, it is waste. A schedule with a skip in it. A correction that corrects less than it could, and pays the same price.

They mended that, and only that.

Not by staying in the sleeve. Not by becoming a third schedule. At the join—where the portable gallery sags into the slit, and the colder draft and the drier draft argue, and for a little while you cannot say which is breathing—they held the argument open by one interval. No longer. Long enough for the grit already inside to reach the sheet. Long enough for the seal to thicken along the flank that would have faced the next walking rain. The plants around the lip were not delayed; they extruded on the scar when the scar was ready, amber bulb, proper size, as if the worker had clocked out on time and the works had agreed. Then the hold was released. The drafts became one draft. The cord-work opened itself along its length into the packed fiber, which is what it had come there to do. Roots webbed the lip. The occupation ended.

If the being felt the difference, it would not have felt a hand. It would have felt the temper take, the way a hide feels when the cold finishes a job the cold was already doing. No rescue from becoming bank. Bank is not a punishment here. Bank is how a finished correction remains in the machine after the shift is over. What changed was only that the gift was complete when it was given, instead of half-set and cooling wrong in the fiber.

After that they withdrew the exactness from the release. The air went back to being slightly less perfect than a power could make it, because a perfect draft, continued, would have been a keeper, and the place has no keeper, and installing one would have been the louder violence. They left no post, no name cut in resin, no second moth, no light that gathered itself into a sign. The ash-colored wings, if they were on a bulb that evening, stayed indistinguishable from exudate. The power did not recruit an audience.

What remained was hereditary in the sense that a drainage is hereditary. The next pale end, feeling out into the cold for threads, found the purchase a little less raw. Not safe. Not saved. The route still concluded; the body was still spent; the morning would still mistake the spent body for ground. But the flank cracked less in the drying, and the syrup that climbed the tear sealed on the first sheet, and the sand taken from the ridge reappeared as temper instead of as a spill. A bias, mild as the others, added to a process that had forgotten it might have been anything but continuous revision.

They did not watch to be thanked. Thanks would have required a pause, and the sleeve does not pause, and the highland does not speak. Benevolence, applied to a country that manufactures in silence, turned out to look like better work rather than like mercy with a face. They had seen the occupant insist on the order of the stages. They had helped the insistence arrive intact at the hour it was handed back. Then they let the ordinary dark come down on it, and did not remain as weather, or witness, or god of the cords.

If you were there in the morning you would not know where to lay a flower, and you would be right not to. Somewhere a new weight would already be on the contour, keeping the second schedule, difficult to tell from erosion. The basins would skin. A dome would be considering its drop. The draft from a torn cord would be cold on the wrist, regular as a duty, and perhaps—only perhaps—a fraction drier than the highland, left alone, would have managed on that day. The power’s attention would already have become indistinguishable from the apparatus.

That is what they did. They did not abolish the price. They stopped a small wasting of it. And then they went, which was the rest of the kindness: not to linger in a place that stays alive by refusing to hold still for anyone, not even for a love wise enough to fit in a single interval and then let the roots close over the evidence.
