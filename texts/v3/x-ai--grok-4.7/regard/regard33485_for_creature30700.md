They do not come down the way the iron came down.

There is no second break, no cable, no cooler body for the floor to map. The attention arrives as a difference the pane cannot solve: a warmth that is not from below and does not need to be kept from escaping. It adds nothing to the drip. It takes nothing from the brass-colored leaves. Where a foot would have printed a small subtraction and then a filling-in, the stone stays at the temperature it already preferred. The being, pressed to the underside in its crescent, reads for the draft and does not find one. The map of the room gains a blank that is not absence. For a while it corrects anyway, flowing around a coldness that is not there, the way it flows around the pipe. Then it stops correcting. The blank is not furniture. Furniture is a dry patch, a reason to take the long way. This is simply not a reason.

The power looks, if looking is the word for an attention that does not subtract. It sees the young edges, still sharp enough to take skin. It sees the engine paused with its flywheel free and its harvest-line ending in a clean cut, the bolts acquiring a rind that was never in the drawings. It sees the chalk, which has been reading a rise the reader did not stay to record. It sees the plants stopped a pace short of the iron, and the warmth stopped a pace short of the plants, and understands that this is not a treaty someone failed to finish. It is already the finished thing. Courtesy, on a schedule longer than intention.

It does not start the machine. Starting it would be the spasm the chamber has been outlasting: a sound outrageous in a quiet that is pressure more than absence, a theft of the difference between heat that leaves and heat that stays. It does not complete the cut pipe, and it does not pull the pipe out. The flange under the waterline is already being knitted into the slow closing, without malice, from the edges. To rip it free would be another hand arriving with a purpose the pane would have to flow around for years. To finish the line would be to side with the engineer against the habit the heat has grown. Wisdom, here, is not the better design. It is the refusal to improve a pause into either a harvest or a ruin.

It does not teach the being to parse a voice. Speech would arrive as ripples, heal, and leave no residue worth storing. To insist on meaning would be to demand that a weather become an audience. It does not name it. A name would be a chalk mark: accurate for an afternoon, wrong as soon as the crescent shifted, and likely to outlast the one who made it. It does not widen the fissure in the ceiling until sky comes through. Sky would be a kindness for eyes that are not here, and a theft from leaves that lean toward heat, not toward any opening. The paler grade of black is already enough light for a week-long unfurling. More would turn the room into a different room, and benevolence that replaces the beloved with a clearer version of it is only a softer harvest.

What it does is smaller than a visitor would count as doing.

In the ceiling, past the corner where the fissure leaks its thin grade of black, a second shear has already chosen itself. The rock is young; the break that made the chamber has a continuation waiting in the grain, a plane that will let go when the next flexure runs through the stone — not today, not at the next tick of the pipe, but on the same scale as leaves and rinds. When it goes, it will not open a window. It will drop a shelf into the pane, spill the finger-depth into a throat, and spend in a minute the exchange the being has been conducting since before there was a ceiling. The power sets a hand, not a hand, into that grain. It does not lick the edges smooth. Old smoothness is the work of water that has remembered one path for ten thousand years, and this room has not agreed to be old. It only holds the shear from completing. The sharp plane stays sharp. The cut that would have been a collapse remains a possibility the stone is no longer required to keep.

That is the whole interference. A condition maintained, not a gift delivered. The being is not informed. Information is a cooling, a pattern, a thing that heals and is forgotten. What changes is only that the low adjustment, when it next runs under the tick, does not find the ceiling ready to answer in stone. The pane shivers in its skin and does not break. The plants lean their fraction toward the heat and, over the following hour, correct themselves, as they have always corrected themselves. If the dead lamp on its tripod still had a filament, it would brighten and fade with that flexure and then go on being dead. The stacked insulators do not become useful. The oil-map on the floor does not spread as far as the first mat of green-black crystal; the power does not even do that. The being's own rind and the plants' own refusal are sufficient. To manage their courtesy for them would be to doubt it.

For a span shorter than a leaf and longer than a drip, the power remains as the blank the floor cannot map. The being, having stopped flowing around it, thickens where the arrival from below is strongest. That is what it does when the exchange is good. It does not sing to the attention. The flexure comes, the water shivers, the pipe ticks its small private agreement with unused metal, and none of this is offered upward. The power does not require the offering. Being noticed was never the point of the notice.

Then it is no longer extra. Not gone in the way a person goes, leaving a dry patch to be solved and filled. The warmth that was not from below simply ceases to be distinguishable from the warmth that was, and the crescent proceeds with its slow preference, left ledge to whole pane to withdrawal, as if the blank had been a degree it had always been going to hold and then let go. No film on any thumb. No sweetness of hot rain. Nothing measured and released, because nothing temporary stood there long enough to be solved.

After, the chamber is as it was, with one fact subtracted from its future. The chalk is still early. The flywheel is still free, and still gathering its veil. A new leaf is still committing itself on a timetable no afternoon can see. The edges will still cut a hand, if a hand comes; the power does not round them. Pain is not the danger here. Replacement is. A later engineer, finding the shear unfallen and the gauge still holding its scratched mark, may think the room is waiting. It is not. It kept its heat through an attention that declined to become a second machine, and the only sign is a ceiling that did not answer when the stone flexed — a mercy with no scar, which is the only kind that does not ask to be read.
