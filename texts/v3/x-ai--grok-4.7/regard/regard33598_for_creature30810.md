The power does not arrive.

Arrival would be an input. The plateau meters inputs. Heat, charge, water, the weight of a body that stands too long — all of it is taken up, sorted, and returned as some other sharpness. A power that came down onto the fins would become weather the land did not schedule, and then, because the land does not waste, weather it did. The amber would thicken or thin to account for it. A seam would open. Something in her would flex, empty and ready, and the visitation would be revised into a component before it had finished meaning to help.

So it stays where staying is not a location she can include. It attends without lending its gravity to the convection cells. It does not warm a hollow. It does not spend charge into the mats. The fliers test their nodes and do not test it, because it has not offered them a node.

What it sees, it does not correct.

The place is not a wound that has mistaken itself for a world. The scar is the world: young, sharp, unfinished on purpose, silent because it has no use for announcement. She is not trapped in it. There is no prisoner to free, no face to restore, no older sun to give back. The rules she keeps — sharpness maintained, water only in quantities she can meter, heat leaked only where it is required — are competent. They are not cruel in any grammar she possesses. Cruelty would hurry. She does not hurry. The crystals rank themselves and are shrugged inward; the frost traces a second map and is allowed to fade; a slope founders and is produced again, already green. Nothing here is asking to be saved from what it is.

Benevolence, applied by reflex, would be a kind of erosion. It would wear the edges down into something a kinder eye could rest on. It would teach the ripplers to call, give the head eyes that return a look, put a valley where a valley has not earned its name, interrupt the pans. Wisdom knows that this is not kindness. It is the demand that a works become a landscape a visitor can survive without changing. She has never owed anyone that.

The power considers the one difficulty that is not hers and not the land's: anything that stands still long enough will be gently included. Not hunted. Not hated. Metered. A location the process has not yet revised. For a creature whose whole nature is to remain a witness — to keep its own unfinished rules, its own small weather, its own name for things — that inclusion is an ending, even if no mouth closes and no scream is spent. The plateau will not count it as harm. The power does.

It does not punish her for this. Punishment would be an even louder input, a schedule imposed from outside, and she would take the schedule apart and use the pieces. It does not build a wall. A wall is a fin she has not grown yet, and she grows fins.

What it does is smaller than intervention and harder.

It changes the terms of stillness, not in her, but in the attention of anything that might wander in. Not a sign. Not a voice in the amber. A clarification, carried in the one thing the place cannot quite confiscate because it is not material enough to sort: the knowledge, arriving a moment before the mat warms, that to stop here is to consent. That the bloom tracing inward from the leaf is a question phrased as a process, and that the question can be refused by moving. That movement — ongoing, slightly imperfect, never quite on her schedule — is the only refusal she recognizes, because a thing still revising itself on its own terms is not yet a pan.

She will not resent this. Resentment is noise. If the walker moves, the leaf's bloom stops at the vein and stays a vein. The seam does not open for them. She lengthens in another direction, where the land is already hers, and continues. If the walker understands and stays anyway — if they want, in their own grammar, to be taken up — then the power does not forbid it. Wisdom does not replace one inclusion with another. Consent, once it is real, is not a problem for benevolence to solve.

For her, the power does one courtesy and no more.

It lets itself be accurately known, once, at a scale she can meter without being rearranged by it: not as a god on the ridge, not as a brighter frost, but as a fact the size of a single unused quota. *Seen. Not required. Not an improvement.* The fact settles into a deep seam the way a lighter fraction sometimes fails to lift — present, not vented, not spent. She may never consult it. A works does not need to remember that it was witnessed. But if some later revision reaches that seam, she will find she was not alone in the amber, and that the other did not try to finish her.

Then the power withdraws its nearness so completely that the withdrawal leaves no low pressure behind it. No new wind. No clearing in the haze. The local rain, when it walks her flank, wets one side and leaves the other pale, exactly as scheduled. Condensate ticks from a midrib onto her warmth. Somewhere a pan flexes, ready.

It does not bless the plateau. Blessing would be surplus heat. It does not love her in a way that requires an answer. It keeps, at its own distance, a regard precise enough not to become weather: the regard of one continuance for another, unmerged, unimproved, allowed to be sharp.

What it would do, if anything, is that. And then nothing. The nothing is the part that costs it, and the part she can use.
