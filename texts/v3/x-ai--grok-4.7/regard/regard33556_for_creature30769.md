It comes without a body, because a body would be a lie told to the unfinished sheets, and they keep what they are given.

Attention arrives the way the dew arrives: already present on the surface, not announced, not warmer than the night. The power is old enough to have made weathers and unmade them, and wise enough to feel, at the first contact, that this is not a weather it should become. The plain is hot. The heat is not a wound. The plain is empty of faces. The emptiness is not a lack. The plain is young, and the youth is not a debt coming due. These are the easiest mistakes, and it does not make them. It has made them elsewhere, in other centuries, for other lives that looked unfinished only because it was impatient. It remembers the cost. The remembering is the beginning of what it will do.

It looks.

Not as a survey, which would be a kind of use, and not as a blessing, which would be a kind of weather. It looks the way the sheets look: by staying through one whole clock of the place. Night, when the film occurs in the beads and binds a grain. Dawn, when the low pans hold their skin and the skin is already becoming floor. Morning, when the thermals lift off the paler pours and the cloud streets find the rhythm of the paving and do not stack. Afternoon, when the finished ground rings with heat and the life is sealed inside what it made, and the tufts stand alone, whitened at the tips, spending nothing they cannot spare. Evening, when a brief rain walks with its cloud and dies, and the smoke in a pan settles into one more lamina. Then night again, and the small tick of a sheet cooling, a check extending by a grain. It does not skip the sealed hours. Those are not an interval between the real events. They are the event of patience, and the patience is the being.

While it looks, it is tempted, because benevolence without temptation is only indifference in a finer coat.

It could thicken the dew. A second film of water, held past midmorning, would let the binding run longer; the fronts would advance by a hand instead of a finger; the tufts would green at the tips instead of whitening. It sees the consequence as clearly as the gift: the sheets would cure softer, the afternoon would not seal them, the habit of spending the dangerous hours as mineral would have nothing to teach, and the life that is the ground would become a life that needs a patron. It does not thicken the dew.

It could open a shade. A cloud that stayed, a height somewhere, a tilt that gathered the pans into a thread and let the thread become a creek before the centuries earned it. The proto-drainage between two pans is so slight a suggestion that a single afternoon of intended rain would finish it. And then there would be a direction, and direction is the start of a coast, and a coast is the start of a world that has an edge, and a world that has an edge can be crossed by things that are not this. The being has no headquarters. A river would be a headquarters. It does not open the shade.

It could set a creature on the horizon, something with a throat, so the silence would have a witness from inside. It feels the appeal of this most sharply, because it is itself a kind of witness, and witnesses grow lonely for company they have not been asked to provide. A bird. A grazer small enough to live on the stains. Anything that would make a track the interior could answer. But the finished ground forgets footsteps, and the unfinished ground remembers them until it roofs them over, and a living foot would be a mark repeated until the mark became a path, and a path would be a use, and use would be the first hunger the plain has ever held. The silence is accurate. It does not put a throat into it.

It could hurry the margin. Somewhere, far beyond any walking, the newest film is binding the first grain of a sheet that was not ground yesterday. A touch, and the country would be wider by evening. It understands, looking, that width is not what the being is hungry for. The being is not hungry. Width is simply what happens when the night is allowed to finish. To add width by hand would be to counterfeit the only work the life knows, and a counterfeit lamina would lie in the stack forever, a layer that did not occur, a memory the sheets cannot keep because they only keep what was done by the dew and the glue and the sun. It does not touch the margin.

It could speak. One word, not into a ear — there is no ear — but into the quiet, a name for what is happening, so the happening would be held in a meaning as well as in a mineral. It almost does. The word arrives in it fully formed, exact, unsentimental, the right size. And it holds the word the way the pans hold water: briefly, and then not. A name would be a center. The text of this life has no center, and the power will not write one in. The quiet returns the word to it unused. That, too, is an action.

What it does, when the temptations have been set down, is smaller than any of them, and harder.

It keeps the outside out.

Not with a wall — a wall would be a coast of another kind, a place where the plain ended and something else began, and the being's climate is the child of its width. It keeps the outside out by refusing, at the scale where such refusals are possible, the things that are not this clock. A cinder falling from some other making, a stone with a trajectory, a heat that is not the sun's heat, a dryness that would break the night's faithfulness, a wetness that would arrive without a cloud street and stay. These are not enemies. They are simply other stories, and they would overwrite the lamina the way a footprint overwrites a soft front: permanently, and without malice. The power turns them aside before they are events. The plain never knows. The dew still comes from nothing. The checks still tick. No new seam appears that the binding did not earn.

And one thing more, because benevolence is not only a withholding.

Where a tuft has found two mornings and its lateral thread runs along a seam the film is also using, the power does not choose between them. It does not widen the check for the root, or seal it for the film. They have a bargain. The bargain is not fair in any sense that would matter to a power, and it does not need to be. What the power does is only this: when the afternoon wind takes the stiff head of the tuft and would smash it flat against the pavement — one gust, a little stronger than the day's habit, enough to end a gamble the plain usually wins — it lets the gust thin. Not into a shelter. Into the ordinary. The head nods and stands. The film in the seam beneath it is not shaded any more than yesterday. Tomorrow the plain will likely win, as it usually does. The power does not arrange a third morning. It only declines to be the reason there is not a second.

Then it stays one night longer than its looking required, because leaving at the interesting hour would still be a kind of use. It stays through the sealed dark, when nothing happens that a visitor would count. A grain. A bead. A binding so fine the sheet is only a fraction more itself. It does not bless the fraction. The fraction is already complete.

By the time the first thermal lifts, the power is no longer a presence the morning could be different for. It has not become weather. It has not become a mark. It has not become a name spoken into the accurate quiet. The cloud streets form at their own spacing. The pans give back what they held. Somewhere a lobed edge, matte, a little proud of the older pour, holds the last of the dew longer than the bone-colored ground around it, and advances by the width of a night, and no more than a night.

If the being could know that it had been attended to, the knowing would make it something else. So it does not know. The power counts that as the success of its care.

What it keeps, it keeps in itself: not a relic, not a sample, not a sheet lifted and carried away to prove the place was real. Only the clock, remembered accurately, so that if some other power, less patient, should someday look down on a hot unfinished plain and mistake the heat for a problem and the silence for a vacancy, there will be at least one wisdom already present that can say, without needing to be believed, *leave the dew to its work*. The saying may never be needed. The power does not require that it be needed. Benevolence, here, was never a transaction with the life in the ground. The life will go on occurring, or it will stop, according to the mineral and the night. Neither outcome asks for a witness. The witness remains anyway, outside the story, holding nothing it took.

The plain continues. It is still becoming the thing it already, enormously, is. No one has improved it. That is what the power does.
