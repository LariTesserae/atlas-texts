They would come the way weather comes, which is to say they would already have been in the conditions, and the arrival would be a change in attention rather than a change in the air.

A wise power does not need a gate. The eastern line is only a line where paving ends. They would not cross it as a visitor crosses it, with a body that must be accounted for and a breath that sounds too loud in the lanes. They would be present the way the seep is present: a darkening that was available all along, now held in mind. If the continuation looked up from a weight-stone, it would not see a figure on the approach. It would feel, perhaps, that the day’s small corrections had already been agreed to, and that agreement had a witness who was not going to add a motion of their own.

Benevolence, here, would first be the refusal to misunderstand. The power would not take the empty yards for lack, or the low voices for fear, or the shut doors for grief that wanted opening. They would not pity the tree for having only a wrist of trunk, or the lizard for having no name, or the people for having thinned into a function. Pity would be a brighter cloth. It would require the place to become a story about rescue, and the place is not unfinished. A wise attention can tell the difference between a bowl that is empty and a bowl that is the shape of having been used. This is the second kind. To fill it would be to break it.

So they would do very little, and the little would be exact.

They would look at the fan of stain on the western cliff and not lengthen it. That is not nothing. Forces that do not negotiate are still forces; a drip can wander by a finger’s width in a century and call itself the same drip. The power would set no new law over the cliff. They would only decline, in their own seeing, to prefer a different fall of water. Preference is how miracles usually begin, and a miracle here would be a rival continuance: a healing no one had sickened for, a light in a room that had chosen dark, a second green where the clearing had already decided how far green may go. They would withhold preference. The stain would remain the stain the houses were built to. In some centuries that is called a blessing, though no one in the lanes would use the word, and the power would not require them to.

They would pass through the iron and leave the rust where the rust had agreed to stop. Hinges would not be made new. Newness is a kind of noise. A door that has earned its concave, a latch that speaks only when it must — these are not defects awaiting a kinder metallurgy. The power might, at most, keep a grain of damp from going one grain further into a strap that was about to cross from agreement into damage. Not shining. Not repaired in any way a hand would notice tomorrow. Simply not worse, on a day when worse had been a possible drift and not a fate. Maintenance done from the inside of the terms, indistinguishable from the terms holding.

At the cistern they would not lift the slab. Lifting is a human motion, and they are not here to become a set of arms. They would know the level under the lid the way they know the level of their own patience: enough, and not a story about abundance. If the seep that night ran a thread harder than usual, they would let the excess find the gutters it already knows, the pockets in the flags, the joints that darken and then give the water back to the rock. No spring would break from a dry course. No thirst would be invented so that it could be answered. Someone who came at the ordinary hour and dipped would taste the same mineral cold and set the slab back true, and the fitness of that motion would not have been improved, only left intact, which is the only improvement the continuation can survive.

They might stand — if standing is the right word for an attention that does not displace air — in the open ground at the middle hour, when no one is crossing. They would not call the people out of the shut doors to be seen, or loved in a public way, or counted. Being counted would thicken them past their true number. The power’s care would be for the number as it is: never quite zero, never quite a crowd. They would include, without summoning, the figure who is not in the lane because the work does not presently require a hand. Absence, understood, is not abandonment. They would not fill it to comfort themselves.

For the child, if there is a child that day, they would do the hardest kindness, which is not to become a wonder. Wonder cuts a new path. A game of placing and replacing on the flat stone is already a complete world; a sign in the sky would make the stone small. The power would let the groove stay a groove. They might keep a loose pebble from turning under a heel on the dished step — a correction so minor it belongs to the same family as resetting a roof-weight that has crept. The child would not know they had been accompanied. Accompaniment that announces itself is a claim. This power does not claim.

They would not explain the place to itself. Explanation is what visitors bring when they cannot bear sameness. The continuation already knows which flagstone holds water longest, which doorway smells of the stores, how far the clearing extends and why that far is enough. To speak those things aloud in a new voice would be to make them doctrine, and doctrine is heavier than a weight-stone and less useful. The power would remain unspoken. If any sentence formed in them at all, it would not be said into the lanes. It would be something like: this is complete, and completion is not the same as ended. Then even that would be let go, because a sentence is a path, and they do not cut paths here either.

Would they stay?

Not as a resident. Residence would mean a doorway, a share of the cistern, a voice sized to the covered way, and then the continuation would have to account for them forever, the way it accounts for every hinge. That accounting would be a tax on a people who have already paid, in full, for the shape they keep. A wise power can love a thing without moving in. They would withdraw the way a fine rain withdraws: by having darkened what was going to darken, and by not remaining as a puddle that asks to be stepped around.

Before the attention thinned back into ordinary daylight, they might do one thing that could, if anyone were measuring, be called an act. No one would be measuring.

On the roof where a weight-stone had shifted a finger’s width, and where no one had yet climbed because the shift was not yet a day old, the stone would sit true again. Not lifted by a hand. Not glowing. Simply no longer crept, as if the year’s small slide had been declined. The person who would have climbed tomorrow would reach the roof and find the work already in agreement with itself. They would not call it a miracle. They might think they had misremembered the shift. They might reset a different stone, out of habit, and climb down, and the day would proceed at its proper size. The power would want exactly that: to be mistaken for the place continuing, because that is what they had chosen to be, for one finger’s width of stone, and no more.

Then nothing. The swifts, or the birds like swifts, would cut the air and not land. The lizard would hold or not hold. Smoke, if any household had a flame, would make its thin thread and not stain what was not already stained. Night would come at the ordinary hour. The shutters that stay dark would stay dark. In the cool rooms the grain would keep because the rock keeps a temperature, not because a blessing had been pronounced over bread.

If the continuation felt anything, it would feel like the quiet after a correction that did not need to be spoken: the sense that the terms still hold, and that holding is not lonely merely because it is not applauded. They would not look for the power in the morning. Looking would be a new habit, and they do not take up new habits. The power, being wise, would not be wounded by that. To be unthanked is a condition of having done no violence to a thing that was already itself.

And if, in some later year, a drift began that the terms could not absorb — a crack that was not wear but failure, a seep that went foul, a winter that broke the agreement and left the continuation with a gap the lanes could see — then the same power would come again, still without a gate, and the act would still be as small as the harm was real. Not a remaking. Not a crowd sent to fill the yards. A channel cleared back into its old line. A store kept from turning. A motion preserved so that other arms could take it up. Benevolence measured with the same cup the town already uses.

Until then, they would do what the notch itself does when nothing is required.

They would not watch from the boundary. They would not linger as a light. They would let the paving end where it ends, and the high country continue as weather and distance, and the middle hour stay empty of crossing figures. Somewhere under a lid, water would be dark and still. Somewhere a hinge would not speak, because no door had moved. The place would hold its shape without effort, the way a bowl holds the shape of having been used.

The wise power would count that holding as enough, and would not add a stone.
