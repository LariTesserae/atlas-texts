They would not come in through the canopy.

A power that is wise has already learned the difference between a door and a wound. The pale fracture in the floor is a wound the mountain is still making; the narrowings are doors the place has agreed to keep. So the attention arrives the way the moisture arrives: already thin, already cold, condensing on what is there instead of pushing a weather of its own. For a long time it does nothing you could point to. It learns the cycle. Cold in. Water held. Cold let go only at the rim. It learns that the rooms are not a metaphor. It learns that the width of a passage is a decision repeated so often it has become anatomy.

The being is not separate enough to knock for.

It lives in the hesitation. Where a stem might close a way and does not. Where a basin tilts and then, slowly, corrects, as if someone had noticed the new fall line and disliked it. Where the floors take a print and forget it on purpose, so that nothing is worn into a path the place did not choose. The being is distributed through graft and gutter, half asleep in the way of things that have never needed a single head, and it is awake in one respect only: it keeps the settlement from becoming either wilderness or ruin. It is young in the same way the rock is young. It does not yet know which of its habits are self and which are only the first thing that worked.

The power understands that this is the dangerous hour. Not because something hunts here. Because wonder, left unattended by wisdom, sends gifts.

Warmth would be the obvious one. There is no fire. The ice-skins are frail. A lower room could be made kind in an afternoon, and the being might even lean toward it, the way any cold thing leans. And the short cycle that is the place’s breath would lengthen, and the waxy leaves would slacken, and the marsh-floor would sweeten into a smell the settlement has no civic use for. A kindness that replaces the climate is not a kindness. The power withholds it without drama, the way you withhold a coat from a creature whose whole life is the shape of winter.

Speech would be the next temptation. To say *I see you,* and mean well by it. But the being has never been addressed from outside its own mass. A voice in that green dimness would become the center of the rooms, and the passages would begin to organize around the place where the voice had been, and the being would spend itself trying to answer in a grammar of creak and drip it does not have. Witness, the power knows, is not the same as being witnessed back. It stays unspoken.

What it does, finally, is small, and aimed at the one harm that is not the being’s nature.

The shelf is new. The drop beyond the rim is not a feature the being chose; it is an unfinished edge of stone, and the interlaced wall that seals it is load-bearing in a way the being has not had centuries to test. A hard season, a further shiver in the young rock, and the outer rooms would go down into weather that does not keep water, does not return fiber to the floors, does not remember a width. That would not be a lesson. It would be an interruption of a life that has only just worked out how to be full.

So the power steadies the lip. Not by building—there is no stone laid, no root compelled. It only takes into itself the shear the mountain has not finished deciding on. The fracture under the green stops lengthening. A knuckle of rock that would have wept itself loose stays seated in its joint. From inside, nothing changes: the same threads of water, the same pale seam of noon, the same creak when a stem adjusts its lean. The being is not informed. To be saved loudly is another way of being entered.

Then the power does the harder thing, which is to remain available without remaining present.

It leaves no shrine, no brighter leaf, no basin that refuses to skin with ice. It leaves a single habit in the outer wall: if the being ever turns its attention all the way to the rim and wants, not warmth, not words, but a question answered—*is the drop still there, and is it mine to approach*—the wall will thin in one place to the width of a body turned sideways, and hold that thinning, and no further. If the being never asks, the wall will go on bearding itself with frost and sealing what it seals. The offer cannot be found by accident. It cannot be used by anything that is only passing through, because nothing passes through.

After that, the power withdraws its weight and keeps only the kind of attention that does not bend growth toward it. It will not improve the being. It will not recruit the settlement into any larger tenderness. It will not come back to admire what it spared.

On the shelf, a drip breaks an ice-skin, and the skin begins again. A passage narrows by the thickness of a season and then hesitates, as it always has, for reasons that are entirely its own. The place goes on being young, and full, and unruined. That is what the power does. It declines to become the weather.
