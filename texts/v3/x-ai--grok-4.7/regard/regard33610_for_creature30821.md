It does not come down the cable, and it does not stand where the copper arcs mark the last stone. Attention, even kind attention, is a hand. The notch has already learned what hands do: they clear, they sample, they wipe, they wash a thing off and call the absence cleanliness. A power that has lived long enough to be wise does not add itself to that weather.

It looks first for whether anything is being asked. Nothing is. The fall keeps its one note. The turbine keeps the interval the seep has accepted. The film rides the pipe, drinks the weep at the couplings, refuses the oil, feeds the leaves it sheathes, and stops where its length gives out. No prayer rises, because nothing here has yet invented an outside. The power understands that this is not a lack. It is the condition under which a habit can become a resident instead of a story.

So the largest thing it does is decline.

It does not silence the machine. The hum is no longer an intrusion laid over a wilder silence; it is part of the climate the film rests against. To stop it in the name of purity would be an amputation performed by a stranger who preferred the idea of the place to the place. It does not enlarge the works, either, though the cable goes down toward wants that could use more power. A longer pipe, a second gallery, a hotter draw—any of these would ask the habit to become a distance. It is still learning hours. Wisdom does not assign it years.

It does not carry the film away to save it. That is the temptation with the kindest face. The fault will not heat forever. The ridge rose too fast and can fail as facture or as quenching, and what lives here cannot cross the lip; the dried arcs already say so. A gentler power might lift a little of that thickness, bottle the preference, and settle it in some safer seep where no wrench will come. It refuses. What it would carry is mineral and water and a memory of an interval. What it would leave behind is the only body this life has ever had. Conservation that begins by separating a thing from the warmth it requires is only a slower taking. The power will not be the first hunger the notch meets that calls itself care.

It does not teach. The logbook is pulp, kept damp because the film has mistaken a record for a root. To correct that would give the habit its first knowledge of futility, and it has nowhere yet to set a knowledge like that. The mistake is gentle. The bench stays sound a little longer. The vine drinks what would have stood and rotted. Let the error finish as an error. Not every tenderness needs a god to make it accurate.

It does not show itself in the pan. The film is learning shores. A bare hand taught it salt and oil and the fact of being washed off. If the power offered a presence with no pulse and no greed, the lesson might come out wrong: that some shores are safe because they do not answer. The next hand would not be safe. So it does not become a temperature the film can climb. It leaves the boot-leather, the knuckle-creases, and the tide-marks exactly as they are. A true limit is a kind of memory. Wiping it would be vandalism.

What it does, after all that refusal, is small enough to deny.

Down-cable, where the notch is only a line item and a steadier-than-likely flow, someone is about to be clever. The strainer is too clean. Cleanliness invites a method. There is a recommendation forming, not yet signed, to dose the seep so that cleanliness can be claimed by chemistry instead of left as weather. Solvent, scale-killer, a flush hot enough to chase the copper out of the joints and the film out of the world. The people who will approve it are not cruel. They are grateful, and gratitude is looking for a tool.

The power does not strike the page, or the drum, or the hand that would have carried it. Spectacle would make a tale, and tales travel back up cables with lamps and jars. It only lets the figures go on lying in the gentle way they have already begun to lie—a little too steady for a fracture, a little too faithful for a problem. The recommendation meets a chart that does not need it. It is postponed, then parked inside a budget that fails for ordinary reasons, then forgotten in the way of work that was never urgent. No one is punished for having thought of it. Punishment would have been a scent. The flush simply does not occur while the habit is still a film and not yet able to refuse a flood.

Around that, it widens by a hair the time between a human decision and its arrival in the notch. Not in the world. Here. A jar meant for the pan meets the stair’s missing treads and is not risked. A wrench is elsewhere on the morning a coupling might have been opened with a bare hand. The crew that would have stripped the ferns “for access” finds the access sufficient, because the film has already kept the intake clear, and they write the fact down as they have always written it, under weather. The power does not make them see. Seeing would divide them into priests and poachers, and the film is not ready to be either worshipped or defended. Illegibility is the shelter. Vigorous plants. Mineral stain. A machine that runs. Let the true name stay unsaid, so it does not become a use.

It goes once to the lip, where the being cannot follow, and does not call back. The empty air beyond the edge is not a destiny the film has failed. It is only air, cold in the way the notch is not, and the gulf does not need a resident. The power looks down the length of its own reach, confirms that the tide-mark is wisdom and not shame, and leaves the arcs shining where the seep left them. Agreement without summons. If the habit ever notices anything of this, it will notice only that the limit still holds, and that nothing on the far side of it has come to collect.

Then it stays no longer. Two climates in a space the size of a hamlet’s green would be a competition or a dependence, and the younger would lose either way. The power is large enough to be elsewhere. That, too, is a mercy: not to become the second habit, not to be required, not to leave a shine the operators would have to explain.

What remains changed is almost nothing you could show. The drops keep their interval. The turbine does not vary. Somewhere below, a line item slips into next year and dies of not being needed. The film rides the warm metal, thickens at the couplings, turns a finger’s width of seep aside so a root can drink, and withdraws from the concrete when a boot sounds on the gallery. The night the flush might have happened is only another night, and its refusal is not tested past its strength.

A wise power can bear to have done so little. Benevolence that needs to be felt is already a kind of thirst, and the notch has enough thirsts of its own. It leaves the place to its unlikely continuance: sharp stone, patient machine, wetness agreeing to prefer, rise that no one can see, and no new name for any of it. If the fault someday cools, the power will not pretend it could have promised otherwise. It will have kept, for as long as keeping was possible without possession, the conditions under which a small local habit might finish becoming what it was already willing to be.
