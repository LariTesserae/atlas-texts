They would come the way daylight already comes: without announcement, and without needing to be believed in.

A power that is both wise and kind does not mistake a finished thing for a broken one. The basin is not a wound that forgot to close. It is not a sea waiting for trees, or for silence, or for a watcher to give it a name. Uplift happened. Water stayed. Stone still sheds. Calls still cross. The machines hold a level the planet has only just learned how to keep. None of that is a petition.

So the first act is not an act. It is a refusal to improve.

They would not warm the water. Warmth would be a kindness addressed to some other sea, one that had asked for green. They would not seed it. A meadow forced onto young glass would be a story told over the story that is already true: mineral film, rasping mouths, bodies that learn sources or die between them. They would not quiet the roar. The roar is how the emptiness remains legible. To thin it would be to steal the only map the lives have.

They would not unmake the machines. That temptation belongs to a smaller tenderness, the kind that prefers a wilderness it can recognize. Here the housings are already cliffs that do not shed. Routes bend toward them and away. A turbine at one pitch since it was switched on is not an intruder in the chord. It is a regularity the animals have sorted from kin, and used. To rip it out in the name of purity would be to punish a completion for not matching an older coast.

What, then?

They would attend the conditions, and only the conditions, and only where those conditions are not the basin’s own work.

They would keep the height from being argued with by anyone who had not earned the argument. Not with a wall. With a kind of unlikelihood. Routes that lead here would continue to look like errors on a map: a platform, a sagging cable, then nothing for a hundred kilometers. Surveyors would find their instruments honest and their conclusions dull. The water would remain cold, clear, and unprofitable in every register except the one it already occupies. Greed would not be struck down. It would simply fail to become interesting. That is a mercy that does not require a victim, and does not require the animals to know they were spared a century of being explained.

They would watch the edges, because the edges are honest and honesty is brittle. A face that shears too fast, a pinnacle that fails under a housing, a level that slips because a pump was built for steady state and then starved of the power that steadiness quietly consumes: those are not the basin becoming itself. Those are accidents of maintenance. A wise power mends an accident without revising the design. A cable that would have parted is allowed to hold. A turbine that would have hunted for a new pitch is allowed to remain dull and exact. No miracle spray of light. No voice in the water. The animals, if they notice anything, notice that a sound-mark did not migrate, which is what they already expect of sound-marks.

They would not speak to the being.

If by being one means a single mind, there is none to address, and inventing one would be the unkindness. The dwellers are traffic. Aggregate immense, scarce in any hour, educated in sources, incapable of the sort of loneliness that answers a god. To press a consciousness into that spacing, so that something might look back and be comforted, would be to colonize the clear reaches with a need they do not have.

If by being one means the basin itself, the power already has its answer. The basin continues. Continuation is not gratitude and not refusal. It is the form of the motion. A wise kindness does not demand a second form.

They might, once, do something so small it could be mistaken for the day.

Somewhere in the upper water, where a cruiser passes on a route worn by repetition, the light would be exactly what it is: hard, ordinary, the same light that strikes the thin air over the plateau. For the length of that passing, nothing would be added and nothing withheld. The animal would not alter course. The call would go out and be sorted, stone from kin from machine, and the interval would hold. The power would be in the fact of having been present for a completeness that did not require presence, and of leaving no residue by which presence could later be proved.

Then they would go, if going is even the right word for a regard that does not occupy volume.

The surface would keep its low permanent ridging. The young stone would keep its sharp faces. The film would keep its chemistry. Between one body and the next the water would stay clear, and the roar would remain a property of the volume, not a cry for answer. High, and submerged, and already complete: the three facts would go on being true at once, unreconciled, because reconciliation was never the gift they needed.

A benevolent power, if it is also wise, is capable of this difficult tenderness: to see a place that is not suffering, and to let it not be saved.
