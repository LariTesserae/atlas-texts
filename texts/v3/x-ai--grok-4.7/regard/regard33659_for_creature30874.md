It does not arrive as weather. Weather is a wet idea, and the place would refuse it the way a cart-beast refuses a road that is only pretending to be finished.

What comes is a quality of attention, no larger than the task. It enters the way the shaft-light enters: taken up by tissue, cooled, laid farther on as a paler version of the same hour. No one looks up. There is nothing to look up at. The vine-wire does not click a new address. The mills keep their soft tick. If the attention has a body, it is the width of a decision not yet made, and it kneels where the paint-walker kneels, at the edge of the plan, and does not touch the line.

It listens. The creak of a wall lengthening. The amber going gray. A beast’s name, short, spoken once by a handler who does not expect an answer. Down a shaft, the younger city doing the same work a little less well. In the curd, a cold that is not a threat, only a text still being written. The attention reads it without turning the page.

It understands, because wisdom here is not a treasury of corrections, what must not be given.

Not water. Water would be received as sickness: a stem that weeps, a door that will not dust, a whole avenue painted out by morning. Not forever. They do not keep a room for it, and to furnish one would be a kind of occupation. Not a finished geology, which would end the surge and with it the only clock their children trust. Not a voice in the shafts. Sound carries poorly, and a voice that carried well would be a crack in the lace, and the lower city would hear it as collapse.

It looks for the hurt that is not the same as unfinishedness.

There is thirst, managed. There is crust on the wrist, brushed off, returning. These are not wounds. There is anger that trims a door into a weapon of exactness. There is a line of boundary paint on a trunk everyone walks around, older than the children who learn it by touch, a room that declines to become more of itself. There is a shaft that, by the plain arithmetic of the next surge, will open one rib too far and drop the younger city’s waste-fiber into a road where people sleep in the gray. No one has measured it yet. The beast that would have baulked is two avenues off, pulling for a mill whose knock it trusts.

The attention does one thing.

In the hours when the vapor comes up richer, it lends a little thickness to a seam that was going to fail — not a miracle, a correction the size of a parer’s patience. The fiber throws a fraction straighter. A knuckle of stem that would have kinked seats true. The shaft’s rib holds through the surge by the width of a finger, which is the width of the line the paint-walker knows how to lay. In the morning a beast is sent, as she already meant to send one, and the beast steps onto the road and agrees. She clicks the vine: eastern edge, held. She does not add a second message. There is no second fact.

The painted grudge it leaves alone. To dissolve that line would be to counterfeit forgiveness, and they are precise enough to notice a wall that has started growing where it had been told, politely, to stop. Some wrongs are part of the plan. A wise power does not edit another people’s handwriting in the night and call it kindness.

It does not bless the children. They are already waking at the first creak, already carried against the chest, already learning sums that are also songs. To be noticed by something vast would only teach them to listen for a click that is not maintenance. It passes their sleep the way the filament-orange passes a sealed chamber: present, useful, uninterested in being thanked.

At the curd it stops, as they stop, and does not put in a hand. The roots are deciding. Interruption would be rude, and rudeness from a greater power is only rudeness with a longer shadow. It waits at the lip of the shaft while a shaft-hand braces her wrist and draws it back in time, and the threads cross-hatch the cut by the third day, and the floor becomes honest again. That was already going to happen. The attention’s share is that it does not hurry the honesty.

By the time the light in the shafts is white once more, there is nothing to worship and nothing to paint around. A door opens that might have stuck. A lower city does not fall through a ceiling onto sleepers. A woman checks a line with her knuckle and walks back toward the mills, composing a short click, thinking about whether to ask the walker who can be asked about the thick paint and the fruiting that is not in the plan. The place goes on arriving.

If the power remains, it remains as the habit of things holding by a finger’s width when a finger’s width is all the difference between a road and a lump. The people will put that habit down to skill, and to the beasts, and to the surge arriving on its coarser clock. They will be right. Benevolence, if it is wise, is willing to be mistaken for competence.

It does not speak. The city is new, and fully occupied, and does not need a greater voice to be true. The attention thins into the ordinary light, which was never ordinary, and lets the tick of the wheels be the loudest mercy in the basin.
