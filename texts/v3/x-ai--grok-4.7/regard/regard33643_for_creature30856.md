The attention arrives the way a change in pressure arrives, a fact before it is an event. Nothing steps over the rim. The light does not alter its politics. The choir does not gain a conductor. If the power has a body, it leaves the body outside the focus, beyond the shoulder, where the voices drop away in a few paces and the mountain is only mountain. It has learned, simply by arriving at all, that the dish is a room in which every added presence is answered. To stand in the center would be to become a note. It declines the part.

What it attends to is not the beauty, though the beauty is real and it does not insult the place by refusing to see it. It attends to the satisfaction. A groove wants the next film of water and is answered often enough that the city has not had to invent another shape. The films climb. The authorized greens drink and tighten. Drought is met with a small abandonment, surplus with immediate spending, night with a single pressure. The refusal at the center continues. Nothing here is waiting to be finished. The oldest habit is already practicing itself, and the practice is successful on the only terms the practitioner keeps.

Because the power is wise, it does not confuse rarity with petition. Because it is benevolent, it does not confuse its own tenderness with a duty to improve. It looks, for a long time, at what help would be if it indulged the meaning the word usually carries, and it sets each version down.

It could lift the cold. It does not. The cold is the lock on the night and the editor of the pavement; without it the open stone would forget its sentence and the mosses would cease to be a crop and become a wilderness, and the colony has no tool for wilderness. It could end the dry weeks, keep every pocket full, spare the browning at the far ends of the feeders. It does not. A dry week is news. It is how the higher cells report themselves, how the shared body knows which rooms to hold and which to leave. To abolish the drought would be to blind the tenant in the act of pitying it. It could tune the mouths into agreement, so the beating stopped and the air no longer grained a listener’s teeth. It does not. The disagreements are the speech. On a strong day the colony is loud with itself, each hollow a reply, the plan reasserted as stress in the continuous stone. Concord would be a kind of sleep imposed at noon.

It could give the life a face, so that attention had somewhere courteous to rest. It could gather the films into one body and set that body in a doorway at dusk, and let it look back. The wish is understandable and it is refused. A face would be a new street. The colony finished exploring before this climate had a name. To be seen in the human sense it would have to become something that can be lonely, and loneliness is not among its senses, and adding it would not be a gift. The power will not install a soul the way a later growth installs an upper storey, for the pleasure of watching it stand and the sorrow of watching it fall through.

It could clear the bitter seam so that spines might stay, so that a bird might nest and the rooms might be occupied in the ordinary way of rooms. It does not. Emptiness is not a lack the place has failed to solve. Emptiness is the instrument. The more vacant the chamber, the more it contributes. Occupancy would be a softer ruin than weather, and slower, and the power did not come to be cleverer than ice.

It could announce itself. A sign, a warming, a second low note under the night note, some proof that the attention was real and kind. That, too, it sets down. The place has drawn notice; it has not asked to become a site of notice. Proof would collect visitors. Visitors bring salt on their hands, and the film already knows what to do with salt: classify the animal and withdraw. The withdrawal is polite and it costs a little. Multiplied, it would become a new drought with no information in it. So the power does not mark the rim. It leaves the shoulder as it is, a suggestion you step over without quite noticing you have entered anything. Inconspicuousness is the colony’s oldest wall. The power stands with that wall, not in front of it.

Then, having refused the improvements, it does one thing, and the thing is small enough to be mistaken for the day’s own weather.

It counts the schedule. Cloud, thaw, skim, climb, spend, freeze, the single pressure, the late start on a glazed dawn. It sets its attention under that sequence the way a hand is set under a bowl not to lift it but to keep the table from being kicked. Somewhere far off, in the long argument of climate and stone, there are weathers that would not be news to the colony but erasure: a dry not of weeks but of years; a warmth that would idle the ice and let the open pavement be colonized; a wind with a new pitch the mouths were never built to be. Those are not lessons. Those are the scraping-out of every film, after which the feeders fill with their own flour and the choir detunes as the openings clog, and the plan becomes a stain. The power does not fight the mountain. It declines, in whatever quarter such things are declined, to let that particular scrape be hurried. The century will still plane the rim. The grains will still be lifted and laid flatter. Maintenance may continue to mean what it means. Only the accident that is not maintenance is turned aside, and turned aside without spectacle, as a cloud that might have snagged and stalled is permitted to tear free on time.

That is the whole intervention. A kick not delivered. A wrong climate postponed by not being chosen. No new pocket, no new green, no voice in the choir, no blessing visible at dusk. The films do not taste it. The mosses are not greener by a shade. A beetle, if it is the hour for the beetle, still makes its few steps and is still not counted.

If the power feels anything a visitor would recognize, it is the feeling of company that does not enter. It stays past the shoulder through one strong afternoon, while the dish is too loud for speech and the colony is, in its own terms, in conversation. It does not answer. Answering would be occupancy. It lets the beating grain the air and lets that be enough relation for both of them: two kinds of keeping, one large and one the size of a dish, sharing a preference that what already works should not be congratulated into changing. At dusk the skim-ice ticks. The mouths narrow. The many pressures fall toward the one pressure that means night. The power, which could have added a harmony and called it comfort, keeps silent, and the silence is received by no one, which is correct, and the single note holds.

In the morning the climbing starts when it should, neither earlier for joy nor later for awe. The authorized patch darkens. The open stone stays open. Somewhere a visitor may or may not come; if one comes, the wall will be cold, and under the cold a film will meet the salt and politely withdraw, and the power will not interfere with that refusal either. Courtesy, doubled, from inside and from outside the focus.

The rim remains a suggestion. The sky keeps more of the view than the land does. Nothing glows in the rooms that is not condensation catching ordinary daylight. The wise thing, having acted, does not remain as a presence the place must live around. Attention, once it has done the only good available, is itself a weight. So the power lifts that weight as carefully as it withheld the others. It goes, if going is the word for a thing that never stood on the pavement. Behind it the low rim goes on resonating, faithfully, to an audience of moss. The mountain is only mountain. Inside the dish the oldest habit practices itself, cell by cell, loudly, to no one, and is permitted—this is the benevolence, the entire benevolence—to go on calling that living, unimproved, unwatched, and uninterrupted.
