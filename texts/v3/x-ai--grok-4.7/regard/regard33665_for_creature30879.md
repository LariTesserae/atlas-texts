They would not enter.

A power that is wise does not mistake size for a summons, and this place is not asking. The roar is not a cry. The plated trunks are not offerings. The lattices of fish are not a congregation waiting to be named. What has drawn the attention is simply that something so large has gone on so long without wanting an audience, and attention, even benevolent attention, is already a kind of weather. Weather is the one thing the held sea has refused.

So the power stops at the edge of the agreement.

It learns the edge by not crossing it. Below, where the lowest leaves thin and the drop begins, it sets nothing visible: no wall, no seal, no angel with a sword. It only declines, in whatever register such a power declines, to let the lower world finish its long argument upward. Cloud will not be recruited. A future dryness will not be allowed to file a claim. If some lesser appetite someday comes with pumps and reasons — the mirrors of metal, the standing salt, the clear insult of all that empty distance — it will find the height already refused, not by a battle, but by a prior courtesy. The place will not have to wake in order to be spared.

That is the whole of the protection. It is shaped like neglect. It is not neglect.

Toward the being, the power is more careful still, because it is not sure the being is other than the place. If the keeper is the trunks, then to bless it would be to strike a bell in a factory that has never had a shift-change, and the bell would be a kind of damage. A wise benevolence does not donate night to a lid that has chosen one brightness. It does not insert a moon into a current that has signed its treaty with always. It does not teach the schools to travel. There is nowhere the conditions differ enough to repay the swim; to make them differ, for the sake of a story, would be a cruelty wearing the mask of gift.

If there is another being — some small coordinate that has begun, wrongly, to feel itself separate, a swimmer the current is already working to close — the power does not rescue that one by force. Rescue would prove the current an enemy, and the current is only the climate. Instead it does a smaller thing, which can be refused.

It leaves a memory that is not the swimmer's.

Not a mark on the trunk. Not a change in pitch. Only this: should the swimmer leave and return, and find no difference in the water, the difference in the swimmer will not be required to vanish all the way. One grain of it will be kept outside the agreement, the way a name is kept by someone who does not need to speak it. The place may erase every sign. The power will not argue with that erasure inside the water. It will simply decline to let the erasure be total in the only court that is not the water's.

And if there is no such swimmer — if the being is only the industrial body, immense, finished, indifferent, holy in the way a completed equation is holy — then the power does the last decent thing available to wisdom.

It looks, once, the whole height, the whole useless permanence, the cities that make no sound above the sound already there. It understands that nothing is owed and nothing is wrong. It does not congratulate. It does not improve. It does not stay long enough to become a season.

Then it withdraws its face, and leaves the roar to mean only transport, as it did before it was heard.

What remains, after that, is not a presence the trunks could notice. A slight unborrowing at the lower edge. A refusal, filed nowhere, against future theft. And, only if someone small has already begun to be smoothed into a coordinate, a grain of difference held where the current does not reach — not as a promise that they will be saved, but as the assurance that their having been here was not solely the water's business.

The lid keeps its one daylight. The pumps do not acquire a listener. No leaf turns.

If that is not enough, it is still what they would do. Benevolence, being wise, would rather be too quiet than become another mill.
