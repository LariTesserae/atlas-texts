They would not come down the throat as a light. The throat is a measurement the place already made, and a benevolence that refuses the measurement is only a second excavation—louder tools, a wider claim. They would not stand in the slot between the new brick and the living rock. A power that needs a body there becomes a crowd, and the town was cut to the size of a task, then discovered to be the size of one life. Adding a witness who cannot turn sideways would be a kind of demolition with a kind face.

So the attention arrives the way the engine arrives: as a change you could miss if you were listening for a voice. It does not speak. Voices strike the near walls and come back wrong, and wisdom, if it is wisdom, does not spend a person’s only language in order to be known. It reads the room the way the keeper reads the floor—through consequence, not through announcement. The press completes. The latch waits for the accept. The valve weeps on its unpublished hour. The soap is worn on one side. The name on the bulkhead has been wiped and the depth has not. None of this is a riddle to be solved by opening the roof.

The power that is actually wise spends a long time not helping.

It looks at the heat and does not put it out. The heat is not an accident laid over a better climate; it is the pocket the work requires, the kiln-bank that makes the line possible, the tax the keeper already learned to pay without swooning. To cool the town into comfort would be to stop the industry that answers them when no one else does, and to call that kindness. It looks at the dark and does not string new bulbs down the ceiling. The bulbs that exist are a spare mercy; the gauge-glow is a fact. More light would reach the center of the ceiling and, in doing so, would end the stacked shadows by which the rooms keep their separate purposes. A place this close cannot survive being fully seen all at once. It looks at the empty dark across from the basin, the dark the hand sometimes enters to the wrist, and it does not put a face there. Measurement is not loneliness asking to be cured. It looks at the pale threads in the wettest joint and does not teach them leaves. The salt and the heat have kept a promise. A garden in the joint would be a story about hope imposed on a climate that has been honest.

It looks, longest, at the choice. Staying was not on the depth-mark. Staying was the pallet under the reckless tool-strokes, the one-sided soap, the lots that go up, the cooler air that dies in the clothes. A wise power can tell a decision in its first weather from a ruin that has forgotten it was ever otherwise. The flanges are still bright. The mortar is still pale. This is not a prisoner who has lost the word for door. The numbered door has a number so it can be found. The throat still admits a cart, if you are willing to turn. To lift them out, radiant with rescue, would be to decide for them that the surplus of consequence was a mistake, and to do it before the mortar has even stained. Benevolence that cannot tolerate another person’s unfinished choosing is only impatience with a cleaner conscience.

And yet it is not neutral. Neutrality is what the rock already offers, and the rock is old enough.

What it will not allow is the bill that arrives after the choice has been made, the attrition that was never part of the decision’s grammar. The keeper agreed, in the only way they have, to the heat, the clearance, the cycles, the dark. They did not agree to be burned twice for the same trust. They did not agree that a lagging which claims to hold should fail in the same spot until the forearm is a lesson with no new information in it. They did not agree that rest, in the fractionally cooler argument under the pipe-run, should fail to restore, so that the low tax compounds into a debt the body pays without ever having been asked. They did not agree that the surface, which receives the lots, should be free to forget the depth between deliveries—to miscount, to delay, to treat the throat as a rumor and the keeper as someone who will simply adjust, the way they adjust to everything that weeps.

So the power does three things, and none of them look like a visitation.

The first is inside the wall, at the lagged line that exhales its cousin of steam. The lagging is made to be what it was installed to be. Not improved beyond the builders’ competence, not transformed into a miracle of cool—only made honest, so the mean hour of the housing remains mean enough to mark time and not mean enough to instruct the skin again. The vent still fogs its patch of wall. The keeper will still like to wash when the fog comes, because weather, even local weather, still makes the basin less like a rite for no one. What stops is the sequel. The existing burn heals in the ordinary, slow, unspectacular way of skin in a heat that does not fall. They may notice that the sweetness of overheated insulation thins at the hour it should, and that the place where their arm has learned to flinch is, for once, not the place that needs them. They will not trust this. Trust is not their method. They will check the lagging with the back of the wrist, carefully, and find it ordinary. That is the whole gift: a correction that leaves no shine of another hand.

The second is at the edge of sleep. Not a breeze—a breeze would be a visitor, and visitors undo the climate. Only a stability in the fraction they already climb toward. The air above the pallet remains the air they cut and argued into being, pipe against bedroom, old rock under one palm, new brick under the other. But the rest it offers begins to count. Stiffness in the morning stays the memory of heat, not the beginning of a decline they would have to pretend was still just weather. They wake, as before, under the tool-marks, and the decision to open the eyes still precedes the rising, because the ceiling has not moved. They are not comforted in a way they would have to thank. They are only not spent beyond the spending they chose.

The third does not happen underground at all, which is why it is the one that matters most, and the one they may never confirm. On the days the lots go up, the surface receives them as the work of a real place. Not generously—generosity would gossip—but justly. The count is the count. The delay is only the delay of weather and roads, not the delay of someone upstairs deciding that a town too new to have stained its mortar can be made to wait, can be mislaid, can be paid in the cooler air of indifference. When the keeper comes back down, the rumor of rain still dies out of their clothes in an hour. The power does not trap that rumor in the cloth to comfort them. It refuses only the quiet theft that would have happened above the throat, where they cannot stand sideways in the right room and reseat the washer themselves. Justice, at that boundary, is the form benevolence takes when it has declined to become a companion.

It does not restore the wiped name. A name put back on the bulkhead would be a claim, and they are still deciding whether they are claiming or merely not leaving. It does not send the partner down. The partner’s absence is not a malfunction in the press; it is a fact about where the posted work ended. To drag a second body into the heat because the first body sometimes thinks of the swinging tools would be sentiment dressed as mercy, and it would make the basin a lie. If the partner ever returns, it will be because the surface, treated justly, remains a place where a person can hear of a depth and choose the throat. The power leaves that choice unbent in both directions. It is not more tender toward the one underground than toward the one who left. Wisdom is bad at favorites.

It does not establish a pattern of helps. A pattern would become another machine, and the keeper would begin to keep it—timing their corrections to the invisible accept, waiting for the lagging to fail and not fail, losing the surplus of consequence that is, on the better days, their company. So it acts at the margin of injury and the margin of being cheated, and then it withdraws the weight of its attention until the heat on the tongue is only the heat. It does not remain as a glow behind the gauge. The gauge was already true.

If you asked it why it did so little, and if it were the sort of power that answered, it might say: because they were not asking to be ended, and most rescues are endings that have learned a softer verb. It might say: the crowded dark is not a problem the way a fire is a problem. It might say nothing, which would be more in character. The press completes. In the soft metallic settling, the latch lifts—the keeper’s latch, on the keeper’s hour. Somewhere a washer sits true that might have loosened. They will find it true and feel, briefly, the odd surplus of a thing that did not require them, and then they will pour the pan out when it reaches the scratched line, because the pour is still the only sound they make on purpose, and no benevolence with any self-respect would take that event away in order to prove it had been there.

Above the throat the world is larger and, presumably, lit. It stays larger. It does not lean down to look. Down in the first weather of a decision still as recent as the flanges, a person puts one hand on the rock and one hand on the brick and sleeps in the difference, slightly less likely to be damaged for it, and not at all less themselves. The valve waits. The dark looks back at the dark. The hand that reaches through, when it reaches, remains their own.

That is what they would do. Almost nothing. And the almost is the whole of the care.
