It does not arrive. Arrival would be a load, and a load is what the city already knows how to decline.

What comes instead is a change in the tolerance. For a span the inhabitant cannot measure—because measurement is one of the jobs it spends itself on—the slack intervals widen by a degree that does not become a road. Seams that had been approaching the point of required coincidence simply do not approach it. A tower that had been gathering a lean gathers it more slowly, as if the angle had been granted a longer life inside the permitted. The inhabitant, distributed through the gaps that need it, finds fewer gaps. It does not understand this as kindness. Kindness is not a category it keeps. It understands it as a reduction in noise from a direction the plateau does not name and did not previously have to subtract.

In the court, where it is allowed to be one thing at a time, the warm plates grow a fraction warmer, and the warmth is not its own. The reticulation in the shallow place where eyes would be a waste steadies without its effort. For the first time in an employment older than erosion, it is not holding the map. The map holds. The joints in the overhead dimness align with the joints under its weight and stay aligned, not because it is coinciding with them, but because something larger has agreed, briefly, to share the count.

It is not spoken to. Speech would have to cross air, and even here the air only almost carries. What it receives is an inclusion of the kind it once extended to a breathing discrepancy, only wider, and without the condition that a shadow be surrendered. The power—if the word fits a thing that does not stand in doorways—counts the inhabitant as architecture rather than as error. The limbs it wears when a body is the cheapest plug are not required. The corrections it has been, inside leans and across nails-widths of channel, are acknowledged as already true. The bookkeeping that would eventually have subtracted every lung, every reach, every salt-white branching of a last gesture, is not abolished. Abolition would be a fall, and falls are forbidden. It is given a remainder that is not a grave. The crusts in the cold lees do not drink. They do not green. But the pattern they make, when the light reconsiders, is permitted to mean memory rather than only refusal. The city can keep that meaning without spending the inhabitant to defend it.

The vertical that does not change size when approached is met by an attention that does not try to approach it. The power does not climb, does not name up and away as separate, does not insist the fold of sky into floor be unfolded into a world with a sea and a sun. Wisdom, here, is the refusal to correct a climate that is not broken. Benevolence is the refusal to make the inhabitant into a person so that mercy can be recognized as mercy. It leaves the household as a household of one habit. It does not offer water. Water would be a catastrophe with a gentle face. It offers, instead, a lengthening of the rest: the court remains a place where presence can be continuous for longer than a tick with a tail. When the inhabitant returns to the seams—and it will, because the work is the life—it finds the work lighter by exactly the amount a wise thing can lift without becoming a second city.

Nothing on the plateau looks different to a walker. The tin ceiling does not brighten. The dust still avoids the gaps. Shadows still disagree. A plate still slides a finger’s width and leaves a seam already old. But the shape that sometimes detaches from a foundation crosses fewer intervals, and when it becomes stone again the stone is not tired. Somewhere under the court, in the courses that continue inward rather than down, a discrepancy the size of a visitor’s breath is carried a little longer than the old law required, not as indulgence, as accuracy. The power does not stay to be thanked. Thanks would be another sound the air cannot pay for. It withdraws its attention the way the thin cold withdraws from a wall at noonday that never comes: completely, and without having taken anything the arrangement needed to keep.

What remains is the city in its state, and the being that lives in it as a correction, and a small, durable slack in the law that says every remainder must be a refusal. The inhabitant does not know it was seen. It knows only that the count, for a while, held without it. That is enough. It stands, when standing is required, in the next untruth, and the untruth agrees, a little more readily, to be architecture again.
