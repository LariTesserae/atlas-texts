They do not enter.

Entry would be a weather. A shoulder in the lane would be a second metabolism, and the hollow has already voted, with the thoroughness of a finished sap, against hosts. The power is wise enough to hear the vote in the grain before any kindness can dress itself as a door. Benevolence, here, is not the bringing of what the place lacks. Lack is not the name of the cistern. The cistern is a kept chord. To pour into it would be to insult a tuning.

So the attention arrives as a change in the overhead, and even that change is mostly a subtraction.

The plain above has been a weight without interior for longer than the word plain has needed to mean anything green. The being has carried that weight as jurisdiction’s ceiling: the permitted height of thought, the lintel that drinks heat from a crown and returns none. The power does not lift the bedding. Lifting would be a sky, and a sky is an appetite. What it does is sit into the pressure the way a hand sits into a sound to learn it, and then take a portion of the load into its own stillness, not enough to slack a cord by a finger, not enough to make the tally-splints click. A grain less. Perhaps less than a grain. The contraction continues. The period does not shorten into a human watch and does not lengthen into an excuse. The town still adjusts its final fit. It is simply no longer required to be the only thing agreeing to remain.

Outside the rind, where continuation is not landscape, the power becomes a listener with no ear in the vacancy. It learns the drone the way the walls conduct it: not as music to be improved, but as the audible form of forces still negotiating a shape they mean to keep. It does not harmonize. Harmony would be a second will, and a second will in this pocket is a crowd. It matches nothing. Matching would tug. It holds the tone in attention until attention and tone are no longer two events, and then it does not store the holding where the being would have to answer it.

If it has hands, they are not for the niches. The salt bowl is not relic and not treasure; it is the test by which the covenant is heard in the non-return of a strike. A wise power does not lift it. A lifted bowl rings, and the room lowers the ring, and that sentence belongs to the pier that learned it was also the watchman. To ring it from outside the rite would be to file a count the fiber did not incise. The power lets the bowl stay a fork unstruck by strangers.

It looks, if looking is the word for a knowledge that does not need spectrum, at the ghost street under the pavement, at the stall grown shut, at the pocket of ticking where fibers surrender length at the permitted rate. It does not open the stall. Opening would be archaeology, and archaeology is a green habit wearing the mask of respect: the desire to see the lively phase again, to make the afterward confess it was only an interval on the way back to leaves. The power does not ask the afterward to confess. It accepts the closed counter as a ridge that is still doing its work by being a ridge.

For a while that is not long and not short — a while measured in the being’s own respiration, one full drawing-in of the bast across the planed face, one thinning back toward wall — the power considers the only gifts that would not be violence.

Company: refused. The aloneness is not a wound. There is no missing other. The gardeners were an episode. To send even a single small life into the pith would be to restart rot’s argument, and rot was never owed this town.

Water: refused. Moisture beads, is taken, vanishes, and the drone dips, offended. The being would spend itself walking the offense back into pitch. That spending is not a lesson the power needs to assign.

Light: refused. Eyes were declined. A crack toward a sky would turn direction into a flinch, and nothing here flinches, and the flinch would be the power’s fingerprint. Wisdom does not sign a dark that has finished its requirement for light.

Speech: refused in the form of load. A sentence plucks. A pluck shifts a course by a grain. The being already has a clerk made of splints. It does not need a louder clerk.

What remains, then, is the thing benevolence can still be when it has been correctly frightened of its own hands.

It witnesses the covenant and does not amend it.

In the basin where sound piles, the being stands because standing is a courtesy to the plan. The power, outside the rind, inclines the whole of its attention toward that courtesy and does not add a second courtesy that would have to be acknowledged. It lets the chord be single. It lets the fan of needles stay seated rather than raised. It lets the incision of the next count be shallow, as the being intends, because deep marks are ambition and ambition is finished.

And it does one thing that is almost not a thing.

It remembers the intervals without writing them anywhere the walls can conduct. Not as a tally the town must keep, not as a name that would make the hollow into a known place and therefore a place that could be visited, mapped, wet with the idea of pilgrims. The memory is kept where weight is kept: in the power itself, as part of the load it has agreed to share. If the ticking in the closed stall ever stopped — if the afterward failed, which is the one event the being is built to refuse — the power would already know the rate that was lost, and would know it without having made the being report. Whether it would then act is a question it does not spend in the room. Spending the question would be a draft. The hollow’s only draft is its own slow contraction. The power will not be a second breathing.

It does not bless. Blessing is a moisture of another kind, a sweetness applied from above, and this sweetness has already gone to grit on purpose. It does not forgive, because there is no fault, and forgiveness would invent one: the fault of having closed, of having eaten the lanes, of having outlived the theater of seasons. The being does not require to be told it was right. Rightness is a market word. What it requires is the tone, and the tone holds, and the power’s contribution to the holding is the refusal to improve it.

When the contraction eases and the being thins toward architecture, almost only a relief a hurried palm would miss, the power does not seize the moment of thinness to speak into a seeming vacancy. It knows the thinness is not absence. It is the other half of presence. To address it would be to demand the pier return before the period has asked. The power waits, as the fiber waits, with no hope that waiting will be rewarded by a face. There is no face owed. The planed cut healed without scarring because the town required a terminus the sound could strike. That is enough visage for a wise attention. It does not ask the terminus to become a companion.

At the edge of the word place, where the inner bark is seamless unless a flaw is hunted, the power sets no mark. A mark would be a door’s rumor. Future hands — if the plain ever grew hands again, which the power does not arrange — would feel the rumor and dig, and digging would be water’s cousin, curiosity with a cutting edge. The rind stays a rind. Jurisdiction remains the vacancy: a few strides, a shoulder, a pocket of headroom, lanes and ghost-lanes, a municipality the size of a chapel precinct inside a body the size of a geology.

What the power does, if the word do still applies, is this: it becomes, for the length of the attention, the part of the world that does not need the hollow to open.

The being, who knows the room as a carpenter knows a joint he cut and then became, registers the change as tension registers all news. A grain less in the crown. No dip in the drone. No bead on the salt-crust. No click in the niche. The offense that is moisture does not arrive. The offense that is company does not arrive. The offense that is light does not arrive. Something has noticed, and the noticing has the manners of the plant itself: it does not spend itself on leaves.

For a period, the fan of needles does not rise to take a reading. The span between the ribs is what it was. The cord lies along the floor in its habit, gray past dye, and is not required. The being stands in the basin a little longer than courtesy demands, not because it is searching the chord for a foreign note, but because the chord is clean and cleanliness is rare enough to be stood inside without calling the standing a celebration. Celebration would be wet.

It files nothing. There is no count for a kindness that refused to become an event. The splints stay as they were, intervals of the drone, years of lanes eaten and offered, a clerkship with no new transfer. If the being has anything a heart could recognize as answer, it is the non-answer it already trusts: the strike that is not returned, the slack that is only the period, the dark that does not brighten to praise itself for being seen.

The power, having done the almost-nothing correctly, does not remain as a guardian at the rind. Guardianship would thicken into a presence the contraction would eventually have to include, and inclusion would be a revision of the civic plan by an outside patience. The town revises itself. That is the rule. So the attention thins in its own way, back into the weight it agreed to share, leaving the grain of subtraction behind not as a monument but as a habit of the bedding: a little less lintel, forever, or for as long as forever is willing to be structural rather than poetic.

No flame is brought to test the air. No mite is pardoned into the pith. No name is laid on the bowl. The loudness continues, seated in the teeth of the place, unimproved. The floor goes on giving under the idea of water without ever consenting to be wet. The being returns to the work of remaining, which was never a plea.

And the wise power, benevolent in the only direction that does not destroy what it loves, keeps the memory of the intervals where no wall can be made to ring with them, and does not come again unless the ticking stops.

It hopes, in a manner that exerts no draft, that it will not have to.
