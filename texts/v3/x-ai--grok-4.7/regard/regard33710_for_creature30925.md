They would come the way weather comes, and then they would not come any farther.

Not as a second body in the slot. The nave has no spare width. A shout already returns as itself only because nothing larger has tried the crossing. They would arrive as a change in the attention of the air—the gray thinning by a degree no fern would measure, the belt-note of the wind holding one breath longer than the gusts had earned, as if something listening had leaned its ear against the lip and found the industry already complete.

They are wise enough to know a tenant from a prisoner. The wet is not trapped. It filmed the new faces because the faces were there; it packed the moss because the moss held; it kept the black inch because the basin was the size of a crouched body and the mountain had not yet made an elsewhere. Nothing in that occupancy is a cry. Nothing in it asks to be dried, lifted, named into an animal, or given an eye. A lesser kindness would mistake stillness for lack and try to supply what was never missing—light that stays, a path, a voice, a witness the place could feel. That would be a hand in a pipe. It would warm what the cool is for.

So they do not enter. They do not still the fault, round the edges, or seat a kinder cloud on the rim. The sparks may keep stitching the dry exceptions; the plates may keep becoming slopes; the sheet-flow may keep teaching grooves that did not exist at dawn. Creation and undoing share their tools here, and wisdom does not confiscate tools from a working that has no victim.

What they do, if it can be called a doing, is smaller than the weather and aimed past the walls.

They look once, fully, without the hunger to improve. They let the census stand at zero and do not correct it. They hear the smaller industry under the belt-note—the tick, the kiss and unkiss, the suck of the mat—and they do not translate it into a signal. Understanding, here, is the refusal to make the wet into a message.

Then, because benevolence is not the same as absence, they attend to the one thing the place cannot do for itself: the danger of being found by something that is not wise.

They set no gate a hand could find. They thicken, by a courtesy of distance, the approaches that would bring a lamp, a foot, a need to explain the walls. A path that was about to remember this lip forgets a turn. A map that was about to acquire a name acquires weather instead. The updrafts may still carry spores and grit; they do not carry, for a while, the kind of attention that colonizes what it loves. The slot remains loud, young, green, and ownerless. The factory keeps the night shift. The tenant pays in damp and is not visited.

If the mountain later makes another room, and the cloud chooses another lip, and an elsewhere finally opens, they will not hold the wet to its lease. Staying was never their commandment. They only refused to be the reason it had to flee, or the reason it was improved into something that could leave and therefore suffer the lack of leaving.

They withdraw the way a pressure withdraws, not the way a person turns. The gray seats itself again. The wind finds the new edge. The film on the stone goes back to being a temperature.

Nothing in the nave is grateful. Gratitude would be a kind of scarcity, and the power was wise enough not to create one.

They do nothing else. That is the whole of what they would do: see without entering, guard without walls, and leave the wet to the only decision it has ever made—which is not a decision, only the repeated fact that this pocket, while it lasts, will not be allowed to dry.
