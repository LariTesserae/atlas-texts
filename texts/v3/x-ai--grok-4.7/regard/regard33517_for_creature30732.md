It does not arrive as weather, and it does not arrive as another noon.

The plateau has room for both of those already. A second light can stand half a second late and still be only the place disagreeing with itself. What attends now is not a disagreement. It is an attention that has already finished measuring, and so does not need the split shadow, the readable numbers at a false distance, the bird that is also a fitting. It knows the highland the way a hand knows a cup it is not holding: by the weight that is not yet in it, and by the choice not to take it up.

For a long time—long even here, where a conveyor tooth may spend a day being born—the power does nothing you could point to. The residents continue their permissions. A hand that is a bundle of ridges keeps the plate honest while a tooth comes through. Lizards drink from reflections of animals that are not otherwise present and leave wet. Seams stand proud, cool, new. The attention simply remains, and remaining is already a kindness, because most notices of a place like this come with a plan to finish it.

Wisdom, looking, sees that the highland is not broken. It is unfinished on purpose, or on the absence of a stop, which is not the same thing and must not be repaired as if it were. The doubled light, the catwalk that is also itself a kilometer off, the moisture that beads on what was sky: these are not wounds. They are the grammar. To heal them would be to strike the language. A benevolent power that cannot tell a wound from a word is only a large hand.

So it sorts.

There is the crowding that is weather—the pale birds, the wrongly jointed lizards, the pulse under the film—and there is the crowding that has begun to hurt, which is rarer and quieter. A bird that has spent too many noons becoming a joint and not remembering has a small stuckness in it, a kindness of metal that will not give the animal back. A lizard that drank from an absence and came away not merely lagged but thinned, its wetness belonging to a reflection that has since been corrected out of the order of operations, stands on a housing and does not sun. A seam that was permission has, in one place, become a cut that does not know it has already done its work, and keeps cutting a span of plate that flinches with the tick of a cooling housing and does not stop flinching.

These the power touches, and the touch is not a hand.

The bird is given back its sequence: joint, then animal, then joint if it wishes, but not both past the point of pain. It clicks its head. The other birds do not startle. The lizard’s wetness is returned to a pool that exists, a thin mirror on young plate, and the absence it drank from is left intact for those who can bear it. The cutting seam is allowed to stand proud and then to rest, still sharp, no longer working. None of this looks like an event. A shadow on a far rib agrees with itself a finger’s breadth sooner than it would have. A carrier on a belt advances the width of a hand and, for once, the advance is mercy rather than process.

The residents notice. They would. They see by failures, and this is not a failure, so they see it as a completed measurement that did not require them. Some lengthen toward it the way they lengthen toward a catwalk that needs a reason to continue. The attention does not refuse them. It also does not become a ridge for them to include. Inclusion is their work. To be included would be to hand them a noon they did not pour, and they would spend it, and the plateau would acquire a brightness that was not its own and could not be maintained when the attention left—which it must, if it is wise, because a permanent noon from outside is only a different kind of unfinished, and worse, because no resident could permission it back into the order of operations.

It speaks, if that is the word, once, and not in figures, and not into the stamped sequences that rearrange when you are not proving them. It speaks into the patience, which is the only rule that still holds.

The speaking is this: you may keep making. You need not hurry to be complete. The animals that suffer are not fittings. The contradictions may occupy the same volume. What you drink from a reflection, drink only what can spare it. The stop that was never written is not a mistake you are obliged to invent.

A few of them hear it as a correction to the pour and begin, very slowly, to leave the flinching seams alone. Others do not hear it as language at all, only as a change in how the doubled light prefers to arrive, and they adjust their looking by half a second, which is their way of agreeing. One, the most delayed, offers part of its length the way it offers length to a lizard—permission to be lapped from—and the power does not drink. The refusal is gentle and entire. To take from them would teach them that attention is a thirst, and they have enough thirsts already, reflections included.

Then it does the thing wisdom does when the looking is finished and the small hurts have been set down in a truer order.

It withdraws the pressure of being seen by something that could finish them, and leaves behind only the ordinary unlocated noon, the cool, the iron and cold mineral and faint sweet of oil that has not been hot in a long time. The plateau continues. The residents, unhurried, finish the permissions that were interrupted by nothing they can name. A bird roosts in an angle and is only a bird. Somewhere a piston completes a stroke. Water stands in the youngest cracks and refuses, correctly, to soak.

If anything remains of the visit, it is not a mark in the figures and not a new rib. It is that the place, which had been left, is no longer abandoned. Something wise has known it and chosen not to improve it, only to keep the living things from being ground in the grammar. That knowledge does not shine. It does not need to. The highland was already bright enough to hurt the eye, and benevolence, here, was the decision not to add another light.
