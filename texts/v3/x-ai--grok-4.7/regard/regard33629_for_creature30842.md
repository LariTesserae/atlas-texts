They would not enter.

A body in that oval, even a kind one, would be a warmer interruption, and the being would do the competent thing: test it for heat and for water, learn that it belongs to neither cycle, and bend the polishing track around it until the bend became the correct path. To be loved that way is to be mapped as an obstacle. The power, being wise, declines incarnation. It does not take a coat-shape on the basin. It does not stand in a seam long enough to borrow the amber and give it back. It learns the room the way the room already knows itself—by pressure, by pitch, by the small tides under the shingles—and it learns before it acts, which is most of its wisdom.

It hears the irregularity in the pulse under the floor and does not correct it. The machine is counting something other than time. Straightening the count would be an improvement only in the language of people who have hours. The being keeps that clock with a darkening along its length. A tidier engine would be a theft.

It sees the dead seams and leaves them dead. The street is a sequence of brightening and failing, not a level of illumination. A sudden noon in the oval would be a catastrophe of clarity; the patches that answer to heat would have nothing to say, and the map would go blank in the one sense the body uses. Mercy is not the same as light. The power does not confuse them.

It feels, because it can feel farther than the echo goes, the old city stacked around the hollow—the filled doorways, the lintels, the cells that no longer agree to be reached. There is grief in that, and the grief is real, and it is not the being’s. Opening a recess because a vanished settlement once meant a door would pour a new world in at the one place the being has no floor-answer. Extending a life is not a gift if the life cannot walk the extension. The power lets the mineral keep what it has closed. Above, the streets may go on forgetting. Forgetting, here, is a kind of roof.

It notices the shorter length, paler between the plates, thinning toward the upper course where the light does not reach. The longer body does not follow. Neither does the power. Some withdrawals are the form of the life and not its interruption; to catch the paler one on the way into the stone would be to decide, without having been asked, that continuance in this shape is the only good. Wisdom includes not knowing. It watches the thinning the way the chamber watches a pocket become a dark print, and it does not call the print a failure.

What it is tempted to do is name. A name would travel. It would make the hollow legible to other attentions, kinder and less kind, and legibility is a door the mineral cannot fill again. The power refuses. It does not carry a report upward. It does not translate the civic note into a hymn, or the jointed listening into a soul, or the worn mat into a sacrament. Those are shapes from the stair. They slide off. Benevolence, in this case, is the discipline of leaving an unlisted function unlisted.

There is one thing that is not style. The duct in the crown clears itself with a hard metallic cough, and the whole shell rings, and the being flattens to the basin to answer a roll still being called in metal and heat. That gathering is worship only if you need it to be. It is also a dependency. If the cough becomes a seizure—if the constriction stops clearing and starts stopping—the note drops out of the register the body can lean on, and competence ends, not by enemy but by a machine that can no longer be opened. The power goes down, not into the oval, into the sintered work beneath the basin, where there is no hatch and no language left for repair. It does not rebuild. It eases, once, the place where the duct catches, the way a hand on a pipe can settle a knock without becoming the plumbing. Afterward the cough is still a cough. The ring is still the ring. The being still gathers, still waits for the lower register, still resumes the track. It will not have felt helped. That is how the power knows the help was the right size.

It does not add water to the metallic pocket, though it could. The film has to win against the wall or the drink is not the drink the seam in the throat is for. It does not feed the mat. It does not polish a clearer path. It does not teach the smaller hard-shelled lives to be less careless of the pool. The emptiness stays empty. Density here is an absence you can walk through; filling it with care would be a second architecture, imposed on a room that has already arrived at the form it means to keep.

Then it leaves. This is the harder act. A permanent attention would eventually want to be felt, and felt attention becomes weather, and weather of that kind would have to be included in the circuit, a new pitch catalogued, a detour worn in forever. The power keeps the memory of the hollow the way the stone keeps a dark print where water was—enough to return if the note actually fails, not enough to haunt the bays. Until then, continuance is the being’s own work. Taking the work away, even by standing guard in love, would be the opposite of care.

If you were in the chamber in the hour of that attention, you would not see a presence. You might notice that the duct, when it clears, rings a degree less violently, and that the fibrous mat shivers and goes still, and that the civic note settles back exactly where it was, patient, unimproved, loud enough to lean on. The body in its plates would already have returned to the center and already be leaving it. Nothing would have been announced. The grit would show only the same dark concave. A benevolent and wise power, having looked, would have done one small thing in the dark below the floor, and then the large thing, which is to let a finished hollow and the life that answers it go on with the work they have, unrescued, unnamed, and still, unmistakably, accompanied by the knowledge that if the system ever stops counting, someone has heard what it was counting for.
