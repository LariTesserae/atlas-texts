The attention does not come down the wind. The wind is already spoken for. It stays outside the last wall, where frost feathers on the outer stems, breaks, and is lost down the drop. A wise thing does not borrow the only door the place has decided must remain a wound.

It arrives, if arrival is the word, as a correction in the order of chances. A side-gust that would have found the pale seam in the canopy—the fresh fracture not yet healed, the one paler green that still remembers the sky—rises instead of entering. It goes over. The seam still drips. The drip is the ordinary one. No one inside could thank the difference, because the difference is a thing that did not happen, and the rooms are furnished entirely with things that do.

It looks without adding heat. That is the first discipline, and it costs. Attention wants to be a warmth; warmth is the simplest way a power has of proving it is present. Here the proof would be a lie told to the ice. The basins already carry one inaccuracy, the slight animal heat that slows a skin of ice along one lip and lets it return thinner, later, after the mouth has gone. A second inaccuracy would become a climate. The gutters would change their minds. The film on the coat, which drinks the green and asks only not to be stripped, would not know which delay to trust. So the power keeps its notice cold, colder than interest usually is, and pays for understanding with stillness.

It learns the contract before it loves it. Love first would have been a kind of noise.

The contract is narrow and sufficient. The growth builds rooms to the measure of a body and then tries, by habit, to close them. The body keeps the measure by eating the closing. Hunger is the hinge. The passages are memory: old widths the animal can still become when it must travel back into its younger sizes. The young one in the marsh is not visited. It is provided for by drains that run, by water that arrives cold and clean enough. Tenderness, here, is maintenance. Maintenance is a path. Nothing in the stack is asking to be improved. The regularity is not a prayer. It is a fit, still new, still unable to survive being empty.

The power considers the mercies that would announce it, and sets them down.

It does not warm the shelf. Warmth would be an invasion that felt like kindness for an hour and then like rot.

It does not lift the young one onto a terrace before the terraces have learned that weight. A width conferred from outside is not a memory. It would be a costume. The stems would obey it without having agreed, and agreement is the whole architecture.

It does not make a second adult. Two keepers would turn maintenance into a border. The basins are not a kingdom.

It does not teach the animal the rim. The outer face must stay unbearable. If the wind were gentled until the coat could cross and return, the inside would lose the definition that makes its breath a closed cycle. A benevolent power that opens every wound has not understood wounds. Some edges are organs.

It does not speak. There is no ear in the arc of small eyes that wants a voice. They take light at the rate light is offered. A word would be a brighter green with nothing to photosynthesize it.

It does not stay in the passage to be company. Company would be a second thickening at the turn, and the stems, obliging, would grow around both, and the door would be measured wrong forever.

What it does, it does at the scale of almost nothing.

At the rim, it becomes a habit of refusal in the air. Not a wall. The outer thicket is already a wall. It only refuses the rare wind that would come in sideways through a seam before the seam has finished healing—the wind with enough emptiness in it to take the film off a living surface faster than the surface can remake itself. Ordinary cold continues. Frost still feathers and breaks and falls. The drop still receives what the shelf has decided to spend. The animal, if it tested the last wall again, would still find the outside a wound and would still turn back. The power has not made the world safe. It has made one kind of puncturing slightly late, so the canopy’s own slowness can win the race it is already running.

That is the outside act. There is an inside one, and it is even quieter.

The power memorizes the widths.

Not as a plan to be built again. As a delay it will not use unless the contract breaks faster than the young life can inherit it. It takes the measure of the passage the adult becomes when it turns sideways, and of the narrower turn the adult can still enter, and of the low upper room where the spine leaves a shineless path on the canopy’s underside. It takes the measure of the scalloped stems, how long a bite keeps a door from sealing, how many drips before the beaded lip forgets the mouth. It keeps these the way the marsh keeps a root: without displaying them, without warming them, in a place that is not a room.

If the adult lives until the younger length takes a terrace and begins to be measured by the stems, the memorized widths will never be spent. They will remain a kindness that did not occur, which is the only kind this shelf can afford to receive in quantity. If the adult fails too soon—if the flank eases against a turn and does not ease away, and the passages begin the honest work of healing shut, and the drains clog with a sweetness no mouth is coming to press into the floor—then the power will spend one width. One. Not a restoration of the town. A hesitation in a single narrowing, long enough for the young one to find the habit the adult kept: crop, and the way remains a way. After that the power will forget the other measures on purpose. A kept blueprint would be a temptation to become the builder, and the builder, here, is the fit between hunger and growth. To replace that fit with a caretaker would be to empty the shelf in the only way that looks like saving it.

It does not tell the animal any of this. The animal’s eyes, near the pale seam, deepen and do not flash. They have been looking the whole time without performing looking. They do not perform noticing a god. The ice forms late along one lip and on time everywhere else. The moth stills on the wet wall. Something small flexes under the root-mat and is covered. The power feels the old urge to bless what it has seen—to leave a sign, a light, a sweeter smell, a year without the creak of renegotiation—and does not. The creak is the mountain and the tenant finishing each other’s sentences. Silence there would be an amputation.

Before it goes, it does one thing that is only for itself, which even wisdom is allowed if it leaves no trace in the water. It stays long enough to watch the flank ease back into the turn, the coat drink, the passage become only a passage held open by something that has chosen, for this hour, to be part of the holding. It lets that be enough. Benevolence, in a place already sufficient, is mostly the accuracy to stop.

Then it removes even the cold of its notice, so the seam will not heal toward a witness. The side-gusts go over, as they will now sometimes go over, for no reason the rock could name. Inside, drip and tick and the slow give of load-bearing stems. The young one turns in the fiber. The keeper breathes on the ice and the ice, obedient to the only heat it knows, fails and begins again.

The shelf does not become holy. It becomes no more famous than it was. The power, which could have been a weather, chooses to be a scarcity of certain weathers, and a memory that hopes not to be needed, and an attention that learned, in time, how not to add its degree. That is what it does. The rest of what it might have done, it leaves in the drop with the broken frost, where the place itself sends what it cannot use.
