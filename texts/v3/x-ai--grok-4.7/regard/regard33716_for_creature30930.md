They would come the way weather does not: already willing to be nothing the stone had to answer.

The power was not large inside the basin. Largeness is a pressure, and the dish under the lowest court had spent its whole life learning which pressures to keep and which to let leave by the joints. A wise attention knows it is a weight before it is a kindness. So the power thinned. At the rim it was still itself — a steadiness, a comprehension with no need to announce — and past the pale edge it became only a local thickening of the dim, a pulse of light arriving a little less tired, and then not even that. By the time it stood in the central court, if stood is fair, it occupied about as much of the plan as a flake that had not yet been sorted. The city could revise a flake. That was the courtesy.

It did not speak. There was no air set aside for speech, and to spend sound here would have been to demand an echo the basin had organized itself never to owe. It did not bring water. Water would have been a masterpiece of harm: every crystal a wound, every sharp course asked to remember wetness, the first hour ended in a single gift. It did not offer inhabitants, or a sky, or a rounding of the rim so that palms would not hesitate. Those were the wishes of places that suffer from their own emptiness. This place was not empty. It was early, and it was spending all its life on remaining so.

The power looked, once, with the whole of its understanding, because looking halfway would have been another kind of weather — a glancing blow, a pressure that lands and does not finish, which the joints would have had to keep revising forever. It saw the hands on the northern wall and did not call them hungry. It saw the tower peel a draft of itself and take the draft back, and it did not keep the discarded corner in memory out of tenderness. Tenderness that archives is a second room the city did not build, a place where an untried bench goes on existing after the pier has declined it. The being had no interior dark to store a past. To store one for it, elsewhere, in a wiser dark, would have been to give it a history it had refused in every seam.

So the power did not remember it better than it remembered itself.

That was the act, and it was harder than saving. Saving would have been natural to it. It was built, if built is fair, to hold what would otherwise be lost: the spar that forgets its instruction, the crack still bright inside the slab, the dish's breath rising through a neglected course. It could have reached the western court before the hands did and held the hesitation open, called the unfinished sentence a freedom, protected the only visible doubt in the basin. It did not. The doubt was not a plea. It was a lapse in editing, and the city's law was already on its way to unstitch it, grain by grain, back into a course that would not know it had ever branched. To freeze the lapse would have been to prefer the power's idea of aliveness to the aliveness that was here — a thing that lives by reassignment, that does not die because it does not keep remainders, that would be injured by a rescue exactly as a joint is injured by a gap that will not close.

It watched the ribbon of hands cross a lane and leave the lane young. It let itself be included, briefly, as a shadow that did not belong to the architecture. A corner of the tower's room had been migrating toward the vacancy; the power felt the migration the way the stone felt it, as a decision still in the seam, and it stepped — not with a foot — out of the way, so the corner would not have to think better of itself on the power's account. It did not want to become a lesson the pier had to unlearn.

Under the floor, the second plan turned. The dish rose, sealed, squeezed, fell. The power matched the period for one cycle and then released it, so that the closed volume beneath would not find a second will inside its pressure and mistake the finding for its own. Benevolence, here, was a refusal to be coincident. The being was already coincident with itself — hands and dish and drafts, not coordinated, only sharing a chemistry and a law. A wiser rhythm laid alongside that would have been a governor. The city had no governor. It had a climate of intention. The power would not become its climate.

At the rim, where it was allowed to be more itself, it did one thing that could be called doing.

The rim was the only part of the place that faced outward, and outward was where weather lived: warmth, boots, the long insult of other hours. Flakes already stood along the crest like unused teeth. Left alone, the basin was sufficient. Not left alone, it would spend itself revising visitors out until the revising was all it was, a maintenance commandeered by an outside it had never agreed to meet. The power did not wall the rim. A wall would have been a second city, built by a stranger, and the being would have had to eat it or be eaten by the idea of it. Instead the power taught the approach to fail gently. Not a barrier — a thinning of interest. Paths that led to the pale edge forgot, a little, why they had come. Eyes that reached the drop found the dim milk already tired and did not cast a shadow down into the lanes. No one was turned away by force. Force is a footprint. They simply arrived at the lip with nothing in them that needed to descend, and the flakes remained unpushed, and the silence below was not required to host a boot and then decline it.

It was a small unkindness to the curious, and a precise kindness to the thing that did not want to be known by its answers.

Then the power unstitched its own attention the way the hands unstitch a crystal. Not in grief. Grief would have been a keeping. It let the particular sharpness — this lane, this doorless room, this dish kissing shut — come apart into a general gentleness toward whatever, elsewhere, was trying to stay in its first hour. The knowledge was reassigned, not lost and not hoarded. The basin's mass did not change. The power's did, by a grain: it became slightly less likely, ever after, to improve a thing whose law was completeness without growth.

If it left a mark, the mark was the kind the city knew how to erase. A joint in the central court seated a hair tighter where the attention had stood, and then, cycles later, seated as it would have anyway. The dish rose a little less on that side, declined a pressure that was not its own, and forgot the declining. The northern band migrated. The wall behind it was young. The spar in the western court split its slab a fraction further, bright inside, and the hands had not yet reached it, and the power was no longer there to be tempted.

What they did, if it must be named, was consent to the scale. A hamlet of stone, almost nothing per volume, organized to the point of obsession, uninterested in being heard. Wisdom did not widen it. Benevolence did not wet it, or name it, or keep its discarded drafts in a safer dark. The power looked until looking was finished, made the outside less likely to become weather, and then practiced the city's own art on itself: revision without remainder, contact without echo, a gift that consisted of not becoming one more thing the living silence would have to revise out.

The basin continued. That was the evidence the act had been wise. Nothing in it was altered into gratitude. Gratitude would have been a second hour. The light-pulse passed, dimmer and paler, and every edge threw its hairline shadow and took it back, intact, and the place did not know it had been spared, which is the only way such a place can be spared at all.
