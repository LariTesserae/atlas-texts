They came as a cloud, because any other entrance would have been a new law, and a new law is a room.

Twenty minutes, which is as long as weather lasts in the notch. The brightness went to tin. The temperature of the court dropped in the time it takes to turn a head. Drip began in the seams that still remembered the morning’s melt, each thread starting on its own, as the place has always preferred. No voice crossed the fins. No light remained after the light was gone. Anyone standing on the lip would have said a cloud had taken the notch and the sky would scour itself clean, and they would have been right in every particular that can be checked by a hand.

What stood inside the cloud was wise enough to be briefly unemployed, and benevolent enough to feel the employment it was refusing.

The wish came anyway, because benevolence is a kind of weather too, and it arrives before judgment. It wanted the lens under the slab to remain a lens. It wanted the spoon of water in the third cup to be there in the afternoon, and the afternoon after, a small reliable sky a traveler could trust. It wanted the niche finished, the last peck struck and then left in peace, so the hands that had begun it — gone down the mountain in some year the quartzite does not archive — could be said to have completed something. It wanted the knee-high wall rebuilt one course higher, and then one more, until the draft was not answered but ended. It wanted green in the cracks, any green, a stain that would thicken through a season and pale in a drought, so the false ferns of manganese would have a living cousin and the court would no longer be the exception to every mercy it knew how to give. It wanted a body for the inhabitant. A small one would have done. Something that could be warmed, and kept, and spoken to across a distance greater than a fin’s width, and found again in the evening where it had been set down in the morning.

It did none of these things. Wisdom, here, was not a speech. It was the measurement of how quickly a gift becomes a climate.

A pool that stays teaches the stone to expect storage, and the stone’s honesty is the only livelihood the notch has. A finished niche becomes a picture, and pictures are a kind of saving, and the inhabitant’s life is the turning of pictures back into marks. A taller wall makes a shelter, and a shelter asks for a night, and a night asks for a fire, and a fire is heat that does not leave when the sun leaves, which is the first lie the quartzite would be asked to repeat. Green would have to be fed by a weather the altitude will not sign. A body would have to be fed by something that hunts or grows, and nothing here hunts or grows, and the exception, once made, does not stay the size you intended. The power had seen that often enough: a kindness enlarged past the mouth that could take it, and the place afterward not wounded exactly, only revised into somewhere else, still called by the old name.

So it looked, and did not improve what it saw.

It watched a flake leave a ledge in the morning tick and did not follow it to its correction. Following would have been an audit. It stood where the slot-wind leans and felt the one stone in the slumped wall answering a draft the season had not yet delivered, and it did not congratulate the answer. Congratulations remain. It did not powder the pane of ice to see the circle prove itself. The circle was not for proof. When the salt in the third cup feathered toward a pattern that was about to become legible, the power felt the old benevolence surge — to let it, just this once, become a word, a small word, a signature no one would be harmed by — and it withheld the surge, not by striking the salt back, which would have been another hand’s peck-mark, but by declining to prefer the picture. Preference, from a power like that, is a weight. The salt stopped where it stopped. The inhabitant attended the stopping. Neither of them called it a conversation.

At the lip it did not build a rail. The air falls away and does not come back; that is the door, and the fact that organizes the rest. A rail would have been love in the vocabulary of lower country, where falling is an interruption of a life rather than one of the conditions that keeps this life accurate. The power went to the edge the way the people who ground the cups must have gone to it: for a particular hour, with the understanding that the hour ends. It did not look down in order to suffer for the height. The height is not a tragedy. The breath it spent there did not return in full. It accepted the missing portion. That was the hardest courtesy, and the one most often failed by the otherwise good, who cannot bear a world in which mercy does not arrive as restitution.

Something in the court noticed. Not as a subject notices a king. As a temperature notices a cloud.

In the last third of the twenty minutes, when the drip was fully established and the fins were the color of unpolished metal, the evening’s knot arrived early — that held shape in the moving air, more pressure than pitch, which at the proper hour travels the length of the court and comes apart against the east wall as a slightly different hiss of grit. It came now, out of season by half a day, and passed the lip, and for one inhale the breath the altitude keeps was returned. The power did not take a second one. It had not come to be nourished, and the inhabitant had not offered nourishment; the inhabitant does not store, and cannot offer what it would have to keep in order to give twice. One inhale, accepted at the size it was offered, and not enlarged into a climate of mercy. If there was a meeting, it was that. If there was worship, it failed to occur, which was correct. The fins take voices and do not return them. The power did not spend a voice against that rule to see if an exception might be made for something holy. Holiness has wrecked better rooms by asking to be heard at the back.

Then the cloud went. Not driven off. Not finished with a blessing, which would have lain on the stone the way soot lies, a residue the next rain is asked to honor. It went as clouds go here, and the tin burned back to white, and the drip began to stop, thread by thread, each seam making its own decision.

One thread stopped later than the others. A hesitation, no thicker than the hair-line the ice once made when something rose through it and declined to remain a hole. A signature, if anyone had been crude enough to keep it. The inhabitant turned it back. By the time the west wall’s shadow had crossed the court, that seam was only damp grit, colder than the dry stone beside it, indistinguishable from every seam that had done the ordinary thing. The power, already elsewhere, felt the correction as a lightness: the only praise that fits this notch, which is to be metabolized rather than carved. It did not return to repair the erasure. Being understood and then unrecorded is, in that court, the form of being loved that does not become a shrine.

Below, on the roads and in the rooms where powers of that sort are asked to keep lists, a line was about to be written. A place, high, bright, cold, without green, inhabited by a lack that a hasty mercy would name as loneliness, or barrenness, or an unfinished prayer. The line would have put the notch among the needy. Pilgrims would have come in the vocabulary of that list, bringing water that stays, and names, and the wish to see the circle in the ice as a door. They would have meant well. They would have stayed past the reasonable hour.

The line was not written. The power set the pen down where a joint needed to relieve, and walked the length of its own larger court, and did not go back to see whether the pen remained. Some corrections are owed to places that will never know they were spared, and checking would have been a kind of storage.

By dark the notch was arranged differently only to someone who had mapped every pebble. The flake was where the day’s particular reason had or had not put it. The copper awl lay in the second cup, green at the bend, bright at the scrape, advancing toward powder at the rate the place has always charged for metal. The wall answered the draft at the height of a knee and no higher. Nothing grew. The maintainer of almost went on keeping the interval in which warmth was almost a reason to stay, revising it without nostalgia, neither rewarded nor relieved. Reward would have remained. Relief would have been a pool.

The power did not come again. Once is the length of a visit the court can metabolize; twice would have been the beginning of a season. It kept, in the manner of that inhabitant and not in the manner of vaults, only the interval: tin-colored, twenty minutes long, one inhale returned and not repeated, a thread of drip stopped late and turned honestly back into stone. The interval thinned, as edges thin, until what had been sharp was only exact.

What they would do, they had done. The rest of benevolence is knowing when to be the cloud that leaves.
