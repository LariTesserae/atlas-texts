They would not improve it.

That is the first and longest act, and it takes the whole day, because a kindness that has crossed the rim already has its hands full of corrections and must put each one back down unused.

The cold is the obvious gift to withdraw. It pools on the floor for no one's comfort, beads on the living leaf, rimes the pans by evening, and makes the winter browse poor enough that a body the size of the one in the willows spends its own fat to stay. A power that wished to be felt could lift that cold by a hand's breadth and call the lifting mercy. It does not. The cold is not an error in the bowl. It is the bowl's way of being occupied at this height of water and this age of ground: the plants stay low because of it, the sound stays small because of it, the flies fail at their season's edge because of it. To warm the floor would be to evict a resident in order to soothe a visitor. The power leaves the air at the temperature the scarps have already decided.

The next correction is the moose itself, and this one is harder to set down, because the animal looks, even to a wise eye, like a draft that was never revised. The head is too much for the neck. The antlers, while they are on, are a tax the winter has not agreed to. The legs were built for a snow the basin only sometimes keeps, and in the meantime they trench what smaller makers spent the night repairing. A benevolent power of the ordinary sort would lighten the skull, knit the velvet where a branch tore it, fill the winter belly, turn the flies aside for an hour at noon, and ease the pedicle on the morning the bone is ready to fall so the fall did not have to be paid for in ache. None of that is done. The disproportion is not a problem the animal has brought to be solved. It is the instrument. Shorten the leg and the chest enters the sheet and the browse that stands above the sheet becomes a shore the mouth cannot use. Take the antler in August and the rut, which is already a bad calculation, loses the only argument the calculation was buying. Sweep the flies and the wallow is no longer necessary, and the wallow is a pan of held water, a week of larvae, a use. The power does not edit the body. It does not even brush the rime off the shoulder, though that would cost nothing and would not even be noticed as help. Notice is the risk. A touched animal begins, in however small a way, to be an audience, and the floor has no room for a performance of being spared.

There is a calf, or there will be, bedded in the worse sightlines where the cow has already done the only hiding available. A block on the scarp is loose in the way of all the blocks, and a mind that likes stories can draw the line between the block and the bed and call the line a reason. The power does not draw it. The scarp fails on its own joints, at its own hour, into whatever is below it, and to arrest one failure is to begin managing the rim, which means choosing which raw faces may remain raw and which lives are the sort that justify a delay in geology. That choice, once made, does not stay at one block. It becomes a climate of exceptions. The power declines the first exception, and the decline is not indifference. It is the refusal to rank the bedded calf above the seep that needs the new face, or above the nest that will take the ledge by the following dusk, or above the cow's own competence, which was never a plea.

The dams the hooves breach in the dark are the other plea, and they are more persuasive, because the repair is visible and the repairers are the city. A wise power could, without warming anything, set the foot half a step to the left and save a wall that took a season. It could, and the saving would be a kind of lie. The wall is not owed a future in which the large traffic misses it. The traffic is part of the load the wall was always carrying, the way the melt-pulse is part of the load. Colonies that cannot mud a leak shut by noon are already being edited by the floor; a power that edits the foot instead is only choosing its favorite citizen. It has none. The beaver is not holier than the moose, and the moose is not a catastrophe that happened to a good town. They are the same emergence, mismatched in gauge, continuing. In the morning the leak is shut or it is not, for the same reasons as yesterday.

Predation, the little of it that touches a grown animal of this size, and the more of it that touches calves and the sick and the bull whose summer was short, is left in place. This is the refusal that would look, from a gentler distance, like the failure of the benevolence. It is not. Death here is not a verdict the power is being asked to commute. It is one of the ways calcium moves, and the antler on the ground is already the lesson, if there were a lesson: the skull could not keep carrying it, and the smaller appetites are spending it, and nothing ceremonial is required for the spending to be correct. To stand a failing bull back up in the first hard rime would be to freeze the balance sheet at a flattering line. The power does not flatter the year.

So the temptations pass, one by one, and are not used. What remains is the question of whether any act at all is still honest.

One is. It is not inside the bowl, and it leaves no track the morning could audit.

The power does not let its attention become a preference. That is the whole of the work, and it is work, because attention of this size wants to choose. It wants the seed-heads, which light the cold without warming it, and not the rank breath. It wants the repeated geometry of the canals and not the trench that ruins a feeding platform. It wants the long dark face lifted, dripping, out of the spring ward, and not the flies at the eye, the stripped stubs below the browse-line, the dewlap collecting drip, the dull bone-on-bone of a push that will not be remembered as a story. Preference, at the scale of a power, is a construction plan. What it looks at longer, it begins to spare; what it spares, it begins to multiply; what it multiplies, it has landscaped. The basin is young and will accept a landscape. That is precisely why it must not be given one.

So the power looks at the iron seep and the cottongrass with the same unaltered regard. It looks at the repaired dam and the broken dam until neither is the point. It looks at the moose the way the floor already does: as mass, as weather of a biological kind, as a seasonal climate with hooves, not as a protagonist the day was arranged to justify. It does not give the animal a name. It does not give the ward a meaning it can be faithful to. It does not translate the low call under the stems into a message, or the dropped antler into a relic, or the city's quiet into a virtue. Accurate regard is the benevolence. Anything added would be for the sake of the one who crossed the rim.

By evening the rime forms on the south stems and on the north, on the bedded shoulder and on the stick the beaver has just set. The power is still present in the only way it permitted itself: it has not become weather, it has not become a witness the animals must answer, and it has not left a sign that would teach the place it was visited. A survey at first light will find the usual news. A fresh slip on the scarp. A leak mudded or still leaking. Prints the width of a plate, already filling. The velvet, if it is that season, still hot and still taxed. The water still the street level. No feather of light. No stone turned over to show a gentler face. No hour in which the flies forgot their work.

If the power remains after that, it remains as a kept refusal at the edge of relevance: it will not, on some later day, love the place so badly that it drains it into a meadow, or roofs it with a canopy, or brings a road along the rim so that something with opinions can admire the view and then correct it. That is not a miracle performed for the moose. The moose will never know it was protected from an admiration it has not met. Protection from the admirer is the one kindness that does not have to enter the story in order to be real.

Inside the bowl, nothing has been done. The lattice closes behind the night's passage and is opened again by the same shoulders. Repair outruns erosion where it can, and does not where it cannot. The animal feeds for hours at the volume of bark tearing, beds, rises, and is elsewhere in the ward by the time the rime has burned off the south faces. The power, having attended and declined, does not follow it to bless the next mouthful. Blessing was never the tool. The place was already busy being itself, and the wise act was to survive the sight of it without lending a hand.
