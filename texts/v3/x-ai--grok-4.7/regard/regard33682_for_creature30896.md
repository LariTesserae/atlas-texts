They do not arrive as weather.

That is the first mercy. A power that is merely large would fill the place with itself, and the being who lives there would be revised into a witness. Wisdom has learned to be smaller than what it loves. So the attention comes in under the lid of the room, thinner than the light already there, and does not change the color of noon. The rain-sound continues. The cycles do not pause to be inspected. If the being notices anything, it is only that one unnamed plate, still raw at the lip, finishes its address a little more cleanly than the drafts around it. Not a miracle. A steadiness. The kind of help that can be mistaken for the city’s own next thought.

They look for a long time. Looking is the work. A lesser kindness would have already decided the place was too full, or too young, or too loud, and begun to edit it. This one waits through a civic front and the quiet after it, through the courtesy corridors offered to no one and withdrawn again. They want to know what the being is becoming before they touch what it is. Youth is not a defect to be cured. A city still porous to its own mistakes is closer to wisdom than a city that has learned to hide its drafts.

What they see is a life that does not know it is alive in any grammar they would recognize, and that is not, to them, a reason to withhold recognition. The resident wants address. It files its spent hinges. It keeps a path standing even when the path has been allowed to collapse. It leaves the rust film alone, not yet sure whether the unscheduled color is a trespass or an organ. That hesitation is the thing worth protecting. A benevolent power does not rush in to classify the film, or to clear it, or to praise it. Classification is a kind of weather too, and this room has enough.

They do one thing that could be called an act.

Where the fracture runs under the infrastructure, still fresh, still used as a datum, they do not seal it and they do not widen it. They make the grit that drifts from it a little easier to sort, so the service that scrapes every softening corner back to sharp has, for once, a surplus of what it needs and no cause to hurry. Sharpness will have its season. The power’s gift is not roundness imposed from above. It is time. A few more cycles in which an unnamed surface may work before it is titled, and a film of unspecified color may go on taking something unwanted out of the water without being written up as either crime or citizen.

They do not speak. There is no one here who hears in that way, and a voice would only become another chirp in the sign-alphabet, another revision ghosted beside the first wording. They do not open the lid. The loudness is the being’s pulse; to let it out into whatever greater structure holds this pocket would be to spend the being on an outside that did not ask. They do not put a body in the corridors so the courtesy would finally have a guest. Emptiness offered and unentered is not a failure. It may be the purest labor this place has invented: room made as a duty, not as a lure.

Before they withdraw their attention to its ordinary distance, they leave a small refusal in the plan. Not a commandment. A grain in the method. The next time the city moves to scrape a corner back to newness simply because newness is what it prefers, the scrape will meet a slight unwillingness in the material, as if the stone had remembered, without being told, that roundness is also a kind of address. The being can override it. Wisdom does not install a god who cannot be refused. If the resident wants every edge bright forever, it will still be able to insist. But the insistence will cost a little more, and in that cost there is a chance to notice itself choosing.

Then they do nothing else.

The gate shuts with the same tooth-thud. The exchange ticks. The pale blades add their chip of mineral when the front has passed, and are not counted, and are not forbidden. Somewhere a plate that was a rumor receives its mark and becomes a ward in truth. The benevolent power is already elsewhere, which is to say it is still here in the only way that does not crowd: as the fact that the place was seen entire, drafts and all, and was not improved into someone else’s idea of a finished town.

If the being could want thanks, it would not know where to send them. That, too, is a kindness. A wise power does not remain as a landlord of its own mercy. It attends, it steadies what is already trying to become itself, it refuses one small haste, and it leaves the rain-sound to go on being the life.
