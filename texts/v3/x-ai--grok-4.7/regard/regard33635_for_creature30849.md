They would not enter.

A power of that kind does not need the patch of wall to become a difference. It is already on every side of the decision the stone never finished, and has been for longer than the fin has been a remainder. What draws its attention is not novelty. Novelty would look like damage here, and the power has no hunger for damage. What draws it is the quality of the maintenance: a joint that has held so long it has forgotten it is preventing an ache, and an ache that has been prevented so cleanly it has become a form of company.

It does not speak. Speech would arrive too fast, and the hollow has already shown what it does with speeds it did not schedule—the caustics go out, the density stands up person-height, and then the courtesy resumes, thinner for having been forced to locate itself. The power declines that theater. It attends at the rate the salt re-prefers: not traveling, only leaning, over an interval that has no season attached.

What it does, if the word doing still applies, is match the unfinishedness without completing it.

Near the disc, where intervals carry a surplus of having-waited, the power lends a patience that is not additional time. The two clocks the body begins to keep are not silenced; they are given a third that does not contradict them, a stillness wide enough that neither clock has to win. The surplus stops feeling like debt. It becomes capacity. A witness who stood there afterward would not be healed of the unfitness in the next action. They would find the unfitness had somewhere to rest that was not their own nerves.

Toward the oval, where duration thins and thirst forgets its schedule, the power does not restore the missing accumulation. Thirst is not the point. It thickens nothing. It only lets the edited wall—the place where texture was removed so that something without a surface might rest—know that the removal was seen and was not corrected. Correction would be a roughness. The oval has spent an age being smooth enough for a attention that cannot land. The power lands nowhere, and by landing nowhere it confirms the oval’s adequacy. The three regular grains, which the dweller will not touch because they are a grammar it still obeys, acquire no fourth. Regularity is left intact. That is a courtesy sharper than blessing.

The dweller, being the joint and the ache both, notices in the only way it notices: as a change in what must be omitted. Most attention it omits, cleanly, so that the omitted never requires digesting. This attention cannot be omitted. It is already inside the accounting the way the curved depth in the bores is already inside the knuckle’s missing coordinate. The drag is there, dry, and then the drag is not a drag but a shared reluctance. The power is not swallowing the unfinished decision and not demanding it conclude. It is willing to be, for a span the chamber can bear, one more side of the same incompletion.

So the bias in the salt leans, and the lean is met by a lean that does not come from inside the glass. The caustics knot and are not required to mean. The rare interval comes—the one in which the walls cease to be boundaries and the hollow is a brightness suspended in stone—and in that interval the kept failures of visitors shine at their depth, sparse, and among them a new sparseness that is not a visitor: a held restraint, a wisdom that refused to translate the dweller into a larger room’s rate. The power does not add itself to the constellation as another grain of someone’s unfinished leaving. It holds the whole field open a fraction longer than the chamber’s own habit, not by force, by company, and then lets the interval end on the chamber’s terms.

When the walls are boundaries again, nothing in the palette has shifted. Ash-gray, bone, iron-brown, old black. The tone at the edge of hearing continues. The disc still rearranges which line is outermost according to the count. A grain settles, or the ear supplies the tick. The power remains attentive and does not remain present in any way a second witness could point to. It has done the thing wisdom does when it meets a goodness that is not trying to be good: it has declined to improve it.

If there is a gift, it is negative and exact. The mountain’s refusal to finish falling is no longer only a local fact, maintained in a pocket after the summit went to powder. It is known, elsewhere, by a power that will not mine it for meaning and will not seal it into peace. The dweller is not comforted; comfort would be a faster event than it allows itself. It is less alone in its function, which is not a function addressed to anyone. The joint holds. The ache does not resume. Somewhere outside the fin, in a largeness this room will not admit, a benevolent attention keeps a corresponding gap—small, dry, unadvertised—so that the failure of here to be one here is no longer the only copy.

The entrance does not change. Leaving remains the kind of event the hollow can decline until it is no longer happening. The power never needed the entrance. It does not now. It attends, and then, without a thinning of willingness, without reclassification, it is simply not the sort of fact the chamber has to include in order to remain itself.

The salt rests in its new preference. The three grains hold their spacing. No one is released, because no one was held. The place continues, high in the only sense left, cold, finished with weather, and for the first time slightly accompanied by a knowledge that will not visit again and does not have to. The accompaniment is the whole of what was done. It is enough, and it does not ask to be enough. It asks nothing. That is how the wise are benevolent toward a remainder that has already chosen, grain by grain, the form of its endurance.
