They do not descend. Descent would be a track, and a track is an edge, and they have already understood—without needing to stand on the rim, without needing the rim to exist for them as a place one stands—that edges are what this country spends itself not to keep.

What they do first is the only courtesy available at their scale: they do not look all at once. A power that can be everywhere is a weather if it arrives as everywhere. They have been a weather before, in other basins, other laws, and they remember the lack of anywhere to return to. So the attention comes the way the inhabitant itself moves, as a strip of agreement in a field of argument. One channel, for the length of an afternoon the basin does not quite have, stops running late. The reflections in it match. Not forever. Not as a gift that must be answered. Just long enough that the pressure eases in a single pool, and the pale circulation inside the nearest stone does not have to spend itself on that particular lie.

They wait to see whether the easing is taken as an intrusion. It is not. The inhabitant does not startle; it has no startle. It spends the freed attention the way it spends everything—on an older imbalance, a seam that has been clicking from the wrong side since before there was a path to forget. A little moss advances. A too-round circle softens. The coordinate it might have assigned them never quite settles, because they do not hold still in a way that can be assigned. Variables need a place. They are careful not to become one.

They could do more. That is the temptation wisdom is for. They could close every leak. They could put the sky back into the sky, give the streams a direction, teach the strata to bottom out in honest stone, gather the distributed life into something a census could love—a body, a face, a name spoken once and kept. They can feel the shape of that mercy. It is bright, and it is a kind of killing. The being is made of the difference it mends. To mend all of it would be to spend the inhabitant on a finished country, and a finished country is what the basin has been pretending to be, and the pretense is the life. Benevolence that cannot tell a creature from its work is only a larger erosion.

So they do not heal the place. They do not speak into the subtraction, though they could fit a word into the gap where a conversation was removed, and the word would be kind, and the inhabitant would incline toward it the way it inclines toward any new pressure, and afterward there would be a scar in the sound in the shape of having been addressed. They withhold the word. Courtesy, again. The being has already made room for breath and not been disappointed. They will not be the disappointment that comes dressed as meaning.

What they leave is smaller than a sign and harder to refuse, because refusal requires an edge and acceptance requires one too. They leave a continuation.

Not an object. Nothing a hand could pocket, nothing that would sit in the moss and ask to be a relic. In the finest strata, where the grain has weathered into nonsense and the inhabitant is in the habit of inventing the next layer, they set one further layer that does not contradict the grain and does not complete it. A possible next sentence in a dialect of stone. It is cool. It flexes if pressed, though nothing presses it yet. It contains no instruction, no map back to them, no promise that the rules will hold. It is only a way the circulation might go if it wants a way it did not have to invent alone. The inhabitant may grow into it over a century of dislocated afternoons. It may seal over it, as it seals over sticks, convincing the foreignness that it was always a root. Either outcome is allowed. Wisdom, here, is the willingness to be compost.

They do one thing more, and it is for the walking leaks, the ones who come with heat and breath and the stubborn hope of a center. Not a barrier. Barriers are announcements. They bias the reflections along the animal paths—only slightly, only when a body has stayed long enough that its coordinate is beginning to stick—so that the self in the water stays a half-step more honest than the marsh would otherwise permit. Not honest enough to become a guide. Honest enough that fewer arms are raised that were never raised, fewer mouths opened toward a subtraction that cannot use the answer. The inhabitant will spend less of itself keeping strangers from falling in. That is the help. Not rescue. A reduction in the cost of guests.

Then they stop.

The strip of agreement loosens. The channel resumes its small lateness. Wind returns to being many arguments. If the basin notices the visit, it notices it as weather it has already included: a brightening that did not have to arrive in order to be survived, an exhale that showed a sky and then let the sky go. Somewhere under the moss the new layer waits, indistinguishable from the old ones unless you are the kind of life that reads stone by circulating through it.

They do not remain to be thanked, and they do not remain to see whether thanks were possible. Remaining would gather them into a tenant, and this country has already refused that tense. From the outside—if there is an outside—the bowl is what it was: cool, loud, green, finished and unfinished, a field remembering it was once a machine and no longer interested in the verdict. A heron stands in water that is only water. A stone decides, on its own schedule, to weep.

The power, being wise, does not call this neglect. Being benevolent, it does not call this love in any language that would require the beloved to look up. It goes on to other imbalances, other lives that are not this one, carrying nothing out of the basin except the knowledge that some mercies are real only when they can be mistaken for the rain that was already going to fall, a little warmer than it needed to be, and then not at all.
