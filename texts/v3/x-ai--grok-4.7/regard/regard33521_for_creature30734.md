They would come the way weather comes when it has decided not to be an event.

Not down the notch, where a stranger is seen before he is wet to the waist, and not over the northern lip, where the swifts already account for every change in the sheet of melt. A power that is wise does not enter a bowl of thirty people by the only two mouths the bowl has. It would be among them before the question of knocking arose, and it would not knock.

What it would notice first is not the beauty. Beauty is the rim's opinion, and the power would not stand on the rim. It would notice the refusal. The ash that has to be scraped because someone asked a fire to last. The third causeway that was not braided. The newts left uneaten. The roads that stay pale only because they are walked, and the walking that is not called holy. It would understand, quicker than any visitor, that this is not a people waiting to be completed. The plan has settled. The argument with improvement has already been had, and the basin usually finishes it. A gift that arrived as *more* — warmer water, a longer fire, a lining that never split, a school of trout that did not have to be forgotten into holding — would be a second argument, and this one the basin could not finish, because the gift would not gutter.

So it would not give them more of anything they already have in the right amount.

It would look for the crack that is not their doing. There is always one, even in a place that has made its peace. Not the cold: they have worn the cold down into a condition. Not their fewness: the tarn does not feed a crowd, and they have never asked it to. The crack, if it is there, is at the edge of what maintenance can reach. A warm rain two valleys over that will arrive in a year as a thicker thread at the southern lip, teasing a causeway they will walk, and walk again, and still lose by a finger's width of braid they cannot see until it sags. A sickness in the hair-weed three seasons out, small enough that the mat knits over it and the roads still read pale, until the newts thin for a reason no one in the columns can tell you, and the lesson the feet teach stops being the whole lesson. The slow bite of iron in the tarn changing its mind by a degree no tongue will name, so the leaf-linings transpire a little less, and the sweetness in the air-pockets goes off by so little that the sleepers wake only almost clear, and blame their own age.

Those are the things it would touch, and only those, and only enough.

It would do the work in the register they already trust, which is the register of causes too slow to be events. The rain would fall where it was already going to fall, but the rock above the notch would take a little more of it and give it back later, through channels no one walks, so the thread thickens inside the range they already know how to walk back into a road. The hair-weed would thin in a clump off the pale tracks, where a basket-scoop never goes, and thicken where the newts census, and no one would count the difference because the difference would not announce itself as a year. The iron would stay. The leaf-linings would go on needing to be cobbled. A split would still be a split. The old would still have something to refuse.

It would not heal a hand. It would not warm a child who has learned, correctly, that the world moves under him and does not drown him. It would not lengthen the life of the one whose flush no longer comes, the one whose work now is to wait out the younger talk of stiffer linings. That death belongs to the columns. To take it would be to install a chief, and they have no chief, and the lack is load-bearing.

It would not be seen. An appearance would become the most important thing that had ever happened in a place whose importance is that nothing peaks. The lintel-knock would grow a second meaning. Someone would begin to keep a mark. The copper would be called back from wherever the work is and set apart, and once set apart it would stop being a weight and a needle and start being a story, and a story is an improvement they have no ash-scrape for. The power knows this. Wisdom, here, is the willingness to be indistinguishable from the tarn continuing.

If it left a signature at all, it would be one only the mat could read, and the mat does not tell them. A root that had been going to fail in the under-braid of the west causeway would not fail. The pole would find what it expected to find. The person poling would talk about the trout and not about the luck, because it would not feel like luck. It would feel like the condition under which mending is possible, which is what they already call the sweet air, the roads that do not close, the crowns in the glass. They would go on not describing themselves as keepers. The power would go on not describing itself as one of them.

And then it would leave in the same way, which is to say it would not leave so much as stop being extra. The fall would hold its volume. The overflow would not pause to mark a departure. Under the surface the fish would hold in a thicker shade and call it night. Someone would still pole out before dawn if the thread had thickened, not to stop the water — they have never imagined stopping it — but to see which causeway the extra pull was teasing.

It would have made that pull one they could answer.

That is the whole of the kindness. Not a blessing they could thank, and not an indifference dressed as respect. A hand on the far side of the causes, keeping the scale of the trouble matched to the scale of the people, so that their methods, and no others, remain enough. They would never know they had drawn anything's attention. In the columns, that evening, someone would cobble a lining before discussing it, and the lining would hold the way a good cobble holds, which is to say it would need cobbling again, and that would be allowed to be the good news.
