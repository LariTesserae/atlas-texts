They would not come down.

The slit is not a door, and the power that has looked through it knows better than to spend a body on a room that metabolizes every difference into either film or wheel. It does not need a body. What arrives is a change in how the dark is held: for the length of one long pulse, the basin is seen all the way through, keel and coin and lid and scratched glass, without being cooled by the looking. The mass does not eddy. No one turns. Attention, here, is only a virtue if it refuses to become weather.

What it sees, it does not improve. Improvement is the mistake the hands may once have made, and the basin finished that argument without them. Water would be a cruelty. Light in any honest quantity would split the plates and glaze the combs shut. A gentler air, a wider sky, a stream cut for mercy's sake—these would be a second extinction wearing a kinder name. The numbers hold. The film is eaten at the rate it is painted. The longer animals correct the vaults; the vaults correct the film; the stone corrects everyone by being too hot to leave and too closed to flee. A wise benevolence does not break a peace simply because the peace is dry.

It looks longest at the arc.

Not because the three stones are holy. They are not. They are a baffle that outlasted the seam they were set to baffle, and a habit that outlasted the hands. The scratches are not redeemed into a sentence. Whatever they were—tally, map, plea—they are geology now, and reading them back into meaning would only heat the glass with a voice the place has spent ages learning to eat. The power does not translate. It does not kneel. It does not set a fourth stone to complete a shape the basin never asked to finish.

What it does is smaller than a pulse and shaped like the scripture the lid still practices.

At the boundary the small animal keeps, it allows the wrongness to be exact. Not colder. Not newly sacred. Only unmistakably itself: the same degree of baffled heat, held without flicker for the time it takes the lid to lift, comb the one tongue of film that has crossed, and settle again. In that interval the pocket does not have to be defended against doubt. Nothing in the creature's pores is given a revelation; revelation would be another trespass, and this animal has put its whole life into refusing those. It is given, instead, an ease so slight it will never be stored as memory. The work is the same. The work is, for once, not also an argument with erasure.

Then the power lets the degree go back to being merely difficult.

The wheel leans. A scale ticks from the rim and vanishes under plates. A longer animal opens a line in the mass, feeds, and is architecture again. The coins press into cracks and are, most of them, the ration they have always been. No second law is written over the first. No witness remains inside the nave to be leaned toward, because a permanent observer would become a seam, and the columns would rise for it, and the balance would spend itself learning a god.

Before the attention thins, it does one thing that is not an act inside the basin at all.

It remembers, outside, and on the basin's behalf, what the basin cannot carry without distorting: that the arc was fitted, that the scratches were worried by something that had hands, that a boundary can be kept after the reason has powdered, and that this keeping is neither obedience nor grief. The memory is not sent back down as a sign. Signs are heats. It is simply not allowed to be lost in the only place where minds still mistake durability for emptiness. The power will not come again unless something less wise finds the slit and calls the wheel a wasteland to be cured. Against that, and only against that, it will be weather of a stricter kind—closure, misdirection, a height that stays too thin to cross. Not salvation. Custody of a choice already made.

The lid is down. The band is clean to the old height. The gas paints what the combs will take. In the slit the color of altitude does not change. If the place felt the pause, it has already spent it, as it spends every other pulse, and is not poorer for having been understood.
