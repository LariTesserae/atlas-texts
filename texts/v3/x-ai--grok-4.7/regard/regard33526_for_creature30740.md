They do not enter.

A wise power, meeting a life whose vitality is the refusal of total lock, does not cross the seam. Crossing would be a step, and a step is a warmth the floor has never been asked to keep. Benevolence, here, begins as restraint: the understanding that arrival is already an edit, and that this place is still deciding what may be edited.

So the power stands where the unfinished stone only almost has a depth, and looks the way weather looks at a mountain it will not climb — fully, without landing. Attention, at that scale, is not a gaze that focuses the light from inside. It is a wider quiet laid against the pocket’s own quiet, fitted so exactly that the tick of completing crystal faces does not pause. The count does not gain a variable. That is the first kindness, and it is difficult. Most powers, even kind ones, cannot bear to be unregistered. This one can.

What it sees, it does not translate into lack. No thirst to be answered with water. No loneliness to be answered with company. No incompletion to be hurried toward a finished product — wire into cable, powder into a bin with walls, stems into a forest that knows its season. The power has ended worlds by loving them into their conclusions. It does not do that now. It recognizes continuance, and lets continuance be the name of health.

If it acts, the action is at the perimeter, and small.

Somewhere outside the seam — in the ordinary stone that has already agreed to be stone — there are pressures that would crack a young pocket without meaning to: a fault preparing a slip, a colder drainage seeking any thickness that is not yet thickness, a human survey that measures anomalies by drilling them. The power does not seal the place. Sealing would be a wall, and a wall is a decision imposed. It only eases those pressures a finger’s width aside, the way a hand, not touching a glass, can still turn a draft. The fault locks a little elsewhere. The cold finds an older joint. The surveyor’s instrument reads ordinary basalt and is put away. None of this is announced inside. The microsphere snow keeps its schedule. The wire writes the same loose grammar. The being, distributed through seam and stem, is not told it has been spared. Mercy that requires an audience is a smaller mercy.

Then, because benevolence is not only protection, the power offers one thing that can be refused without residue.

It does not speak. Speech would pause the tick. It leaves, in the ordinary rock just short of the seam, a single completed crystal of no use to industry and no kinship with the pocket’s alloy — clear, cold, already finished, the size of a nodule. It is not a seed and not a message. It is a sample of total lock, set where the place may someday reach if it ever chooses to reach. The being can ignore it for longer than any visit. If a stem-joint ever parts toward that edge, and a rootlet tests the foreign face, it will find a surface that does not give, does not firm in embarrassment, does not join the count. A limit, available, not enforced. Wisdom is willing to be declined by a life that feeds on not-yet.

The power does not wait to see which happens. Waiting would be a pressure of its own. It withdraws its attention the way the even light is already withdrawn from any source: not gone, simply no longer gathered. The mineral sweetness does not thicken. No larger place unfolds inside a leaf for lack of a palm to hold it. Tracks are not printed because no boots came. The floor keeps its lack of opinion.

Inside, a hoop of wire cools from white to straw. A soft tip caps itself in dry glass. Somewhere a nodule drops and does not bounce.

The quiet becomes larger, and stays the quiet’s own.

That is what they do, if anything: keep the world from finishing the place by accident, leave a finished thing where it can be refused, and do not ask to be remembered. A benevolent power, wise enough, loves an unfinished life by remaining, itself, slightly outside — dense where it commits, absent where commitment would be a lie.
