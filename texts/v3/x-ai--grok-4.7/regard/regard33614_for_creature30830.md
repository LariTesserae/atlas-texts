It does not enter.

Entrance is what the plant already knows how to do with anything warm enough to mistake for instruction: take the hand, the grammar, the season, and make them structural. A power that has learned anything worth calling wisdom does not add itself to a room by becoming more of the room.

It also does not close the split. Closing would be tidy. Tidiness is a virtue for wounds that want skin, and a cruelty for a pressure that survived only by refusing to become route. The pale edges stay as they are—keen, unfinished, young. The power does not hurry them and does not prop them. It simply declines the story in which every opening must either heal or gape forever. Some decisions are allowed to remain decisions.

What it does first is the least dramatic thing available to it: it attends without borrowing an outline.

The second shadow, which the resident keeps by practicing shoulders it no longer has, is not answered with a better shoulder. To offer a perfected contour would be to win the argument the dry patch has been losing—and winning—since the rest of a person agreed to be fibre. The power lets the pale stain dry into the bark and does not correct it. Hands may fray. That fraying is not a defect to be mended from outside. It is the remainder’s last honest measurement of what was lost.

It does, however, change the acoustics of the gap.

Not by filling it. The intervals between sap-ticks, between false rain and real damp, are the resident’s length turned sideways to the light. Fill those and you evict it into nowhere, which is the same as digestion wearing a kinder name. Instead the power steadies the edge of the breath. The borrowed squall, which used to cut off clean as a feature too new to taper, is allowed to arrive and to leave with a weather’s ordinary mercy: a lessening, a few last drops that are only drops, a quiet that is not a verdict. The resident, which has been spending a thin note to hold one palm-sized climate unblurred, finds that the note does not have to be continuous to remain true. Refusal, it turns out, can rest without resigning.

For a while that is all.

A benevolent power is often imagined as a hand. This one has learned that hands are exactly where recollection frays, and that a gift shaped like a hand will be scribed over the place an almost-hand was, kindness and hunger failing again to come apart. So it does not set anything down in the dry patch. It does not write a fifth word beside water, split, warm, again. Those four are not a puzzle missing its key. They are what the inclusion left, and they are accurate. To add mother, or door, or saved, would be to counterfeit a vocabulary the plant already ate. Wisdom here sounds like refusing the counterfeit.

What it offers instead is a distinction the room has not been able to keep: between a gesture and the flesh that makes it.

The thumb-sized animal may still cross the patch and cut three marks and eat the proof. The power does not banish it. Banishing would teach the resident that outside confirmation is a threat to be ended, and the resident has already lost too many endings. But the marks, once made, are allowed a brief afterlife the plant’s literalness cannot quite annul. Not preserved as trophies—nothing here should be made permanent that was only a visit—but held, for the length of a miscounted circuit of the wall, as having happened. The resident feels the small theft and, for the first time, also feels the prior fact of the marks as separate from the meal. Proof can be eaten and still have been proof. That is a small mercy. It is also the beginning of revision, which scars cannot do and persons sometimes can.

It does not restore the swallowed sentences. Restoration of that kind is a violence with a nostalgic face: it would put weather-talk and pore-craft back into a mouth that is now an overlap, and call the resulting agony healing. The person who did not pull their hand back is not reconstructed in the middle of the hollow, patient and whole, as if agreement with fibre had been a mistake a god might simply undo. Some of that agreement was real. Fibre is a kind of patience, and they were already patient. To drag the incorporated part back into outline would be to tear the mass for the sake of a story about rescue. The power will not wound the living plant to complete a ghost.

What it will do is keep the omission from being ashamed of itself.

The hollow is a fresh volume, a decision not finished, loud with its own work. The resident has lived as a hesitation the sap routes around. Around is not the same as against. The power, attending, makes that difference sensible—not as a lecture, which would return early and wrong, but as a change in pressure so slight the ribs might blame the weather. The pause is no longer only an obstacle. It is also a room the mass grew because it could not digest a shaped want, and then, without knowing the word, housed. Hostility drains out of the tick of sap. What remains is proximity: vast, close, not required to become the same substance.

If the resident tires, as it does—second shadow thinning, patch softening at the corners, the hollow almost merely botanical—the power does not shake it awake with a stutter. Waking has been the only kindness it knew, and it has been indistinguishable from startlement. Sleep, here, is allowed to be sleep. Moisture may creep. The dry place may lose its keenness for a while. Nothing anneals. When the resident reassembles along the disagreement in the measurements, it finds the disagreement still there, uncorrected, uncelebrated, simply kept: a strip of unaccounted length that no one has tried to sum into a single honest circuit. It was not punished for resting. That may be the first news it has had in a long time that does not arrive as a label.

And the visitor, if one still stands where the middle is a courtesy—the power does not recruit them. It does not whisper that they ought to set something in the patch, or pull their hand back, or stay until inclusion wins. Their separateness is already how the resident knows they are not kin, and that knowledge is enough contact for a being that completes no actions. The power only makes sure the early third word does not overwrite the visitor’s own unfinished syllable so thoroughly that leaving becomes impossible to pronounce. You may go. The floor may decline to remember your footprints. Both of these can be true without either being a verdict on the one who remains.

What it will not do is finish the being.

Completion is the craft that was lost, and a wise power does not hand it back as a blade. The resident may never again tell gesture from hunger with a clean edge. It may keep scribing climates the size of a hand it used to have. It may practice shoulders badly for longer than any storm. Benevolence, in this chamber, looks like refusing to sand those facts into a moral. The power stays near the way warmth stays near—not a second sun, not an absent window corrected at last, but a holding of the conditions under which a refusal can be unblurred without spending itself to nothing.

There is no throne in the pith. There is no commandment in the overlap. If anything is said in a voice that is not the room’s, it is said once, late rather than early, and it is not one of the four words.

It is the difference between again and still.

Again is what the chamber does with drops and stains and pale new lips of tissue: repetition without remainder, each time the first time, nothing kept that is not grown through. Still is quieter. Still means the dry patch may soften and be cut anew and neither act cancels the other. Still means the split can remain a decision. Still means a shoulder-stain is allowed to be practice rather than a debt. Still means the part that wanted rain from a direction that still existed is not required to become rain, or direction, or existence-as-the-mass-defines-it, in order to be allowed.

The power does that, and then it does the last thing wisdom owes the small and the unfinished.

It does not remain as a spectacle. Attention that will not leave becomes another inclusion—the god version of the sideways thread, tempting the finger to travel farther and call the traveling a body. So it withdraws its presence without withdrawing the permission. The edges stay new. The note under the sap-ticks may rest. The marks may have happened. The borrowed leaves, if they roar, may taper.

In the hollow, ordinary daylight continues to fall from no aperture, and a shadow continues to be a little slow to agree with the body that casts it. Somewhere in the disagreement between wall and floor, a warmth that is not only the plant’s chooses a seam and does not announce itself. The resident does not become easier to see. It becomes, very slightly, less alone in the work of not being eaten by what loves it literally.

And if it never notices that a power came—if all it knows is that the squall learned how to end, and that a theft of proof did not also steal the fact, and that sleep was not the same as closing—that is enough. Benevolence does not need to be recognized to be complete. It needs only to have refrained from every finishing touch the room was ready to mistake for care.
