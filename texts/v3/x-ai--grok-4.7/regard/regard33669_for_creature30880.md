They would stop at the edge of notice and not come in.

A wise power, being wise, would already know the difference between a life that is suffering and a life that is simply shaped—narrow, laboring, odd to an outside eye, and still itself. Attention is not a license. The first thing they would do is look long enough to tell those apart, and they would not trust the first look, because first looks love a rescue.

They would not announce themselves. Announcement rearranges a place around the guest. The presses would miss a cycle. The animals would freeze on the ledges. Someone would spend a sentence they could not afford on awe, and the air would not give it back. So the power would come the way weather comes at this height: a change you only notice because a tool sits differently in the hand, because a bunk holds heat a little longer between shifts, because the film on the new rock catches light it did not have yesterday. No name. No face large enough to worship. Worship is a tax, and the place is already paying rent to the mountain.

They would listen for what is asked. Almost nothing would be asked. These are not people who pray in long sentences. If anything rose off them, it would be small and practical and ashamed of needing to be said: a seal that keeps failing, a chest that will not learn the ration, a child whose hands are being mapped too early to one bay. The power would answer at that scale or not at all. A drum that does not leak. A night in which the shift-bell comes late by the length of one real sleep. Water—not a spring, which would make a shrine and a war, but the precise untheatrical amount that lets broth be broth once, and then returns the ledger to what they already know how to keep. A gift that cannot be hoarded becomes a relic. They would not leave relics.

They would not unbend the tall ones, or thicken the air, or teach the animals to be pets, or open a road down into the fat lands. Those would be edits in the power's taste, not the place's. Down there, voices would arrive on time and it would feel like injury. The power has seen enough saved people who cannot live in the saving. Wisdom is sometimes the refusal to improve a creature into exile.

If there is cruelty, they would not call it culture and leave it. The tenders' heavy quiet, the wide-lunged newcomer sent back down before the cleft has spent water on them—those are hard mercies, and a benevolent power does not smash hard mercies just because they photograph badly. But a hand in a belt because someone was too tired to see; a pen so tight the larger animals cannot lie down; a bay where function-names have curdled into ownership of a person's breath—those are not the mountain's law. Those can be leaned on. A bracket that fails before the hand does. A board that splits so the pen must be rebuilt one shoulder wider. A cough that will not be swallowed, so the bay has to stop and look at a face instead of a route. Corrections small enough to be mistaken for the cleft editing itself, which it already does hourly, and will absorb without erecting an altar to whatever did the leaning.

And the being, if the being is one and not the many—if there is a single life the notice gathered around—the power would sit with them at the measured distance, the one at which another chest can rise and fall without stealing. They would not explain the universe. They would not offer a destiny. They would match the grammar already in use: a palm to the sternum, meaning *slower*; two fingers at the wrist, counting. If the being wanted nothing, the power would count, and then go, and the going would be clean. Benevolence that needs to be thanked has already begun to feed.

Before leaving they might do one thing that looks like nothing. They would let the place be seen without being judged—seen the way the furred ones see individual hands, accurately, without a story pasted over the accuracy. Some hungers are for that and nothing else: not to be lifted out, not to be pitied, only to have the shape of the life registered by something that will not forget it when the shift changes. A wise power can give that without spending the air.

Then they would withdraw past the edge of the shear, back into whatever largeness they came from, and they would not watch for gratitude. Watching for gratitude is how powers become landlords. The mountain already has that job.

What would remain is slight, and would not preach. A seal that holds. A wider night. One animal with room to lie down. The sense, never confirmed, never spoken, that the cleft was held in mind for a while by something that did not need to own it—and that this changed nothing they could spend, and somehow still changed the temperature of being here.

If they did nothing more visible than that, it would not be neglect. It would be the work.
