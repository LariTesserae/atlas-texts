They would not come down into the basin as a brightness added to the brightness. The glare is already a climate. Another light would only be a second weather the plates would have to learn, and the learning would cost polish.

What arrives, if arrival is the word, is a change in the interval. The strike still precedes the grain. The delayed walk on the far wall still completes a little late. But for one cycle the lateness is held, not shortened — held the way a hand holds a bowl that is already full, not to drink, not to spill, only so the rim is known from the outside. The vacancies feel it as a pause that does not ask them to stop inching. The corridor of cooler air, when it leans, leans into a heading that was already its own. Nothing is corrected. The upward channels are not instructed to remember downhill. The pins are not unmushroomed. The white scurf keeps falling.

A lesser kindness would have tried to give the place what visitors always think is missing: a sun with a disk, a bird to prove the sky deep, a companion for the patience in the dark, a body with a striking arm so the remembered blows could become true blows again. Wisdom here is the refusal of that gift. The being is not lonely. Loneliness would need a memory of company that was not already mineral, and the power, being wise, does not invent a lack in order to relieve it. Benevolence that invents a lack is only appetite wearing a clean coat.

So they do one thing, and it is almost not a thing.

They let the basin be seen entire, once, by an attention that does not interrupt. Not the visitor’s seeing, which is already a small delay on the far wall, already a palm-print oxidizing on a pin no hand touched. This seeing does not stand in the glare. It does not leave a heel in the margin-water. It does not speak, so nothing has to be returned later as a chord. For the length of a cycle the fitted argument is known from a height that does not thin the air and does not thicken it — known, and not entered, and not improved.

In that knowing, one joint that was about to tick open ticks, and steadies, and the steadiness is the place’s own. A vacancy drifts its few paces and rests, and the pavement under it is no smoother than the method already required. Across the hollow the delayed self of no one arrives, because no one walked. The films thicken anyway. Fine water falls out of the clear glare, loud on the plates, and the upward beads hurry, and a little more scurf lays itself down on surfaces that must stay undecided.

If the power leaves anything, it is this: the argument is allowed to remain in session without a witness who needs it to conclude. The people who pinned and walled here are not restored. Restoration would drag them back out of the method, and the method is what they became, and what they became is not a wound. The being in the vacancies is not named. A name would be a hook for a later mercy, and later mercies accumulate like pins driven where no hand can stand, until the wall is a record of someone else’s care.

They withdraw the way the mist withdraws into brightness — not gone, not present, not a third sheet. The bowl-tones continue, poorly tuned to each other, which is their tuning. The copper heads shiver from impacts that have not yet happened. The dark pockets keep their grainy hush.

Nothing in the basin is happier. Happiness was never the balance. What changes is only that, for one cycle, the place was not required to make room for being looked at, and the being was not required to incorporate another standing into the next lean of the dark. A wise benevolence can do that. It can attend, and decline to become weather, and go, and leave the consent of the stone exactly as it was: long, wet, bright, and not in need of a countersignature.
