They would not open it.

That is the first thing, and it takes them longer than haste would like. A wise power can split a seal the way heat splits a jar, and benevolence is always being tempted by the clean story in which the wall comes down and something small steps into weather. They stand a long time on the outside of the height—on whatever thin, forgotten country still touches the last course—and they do not touch the brick.

They listen.

The chord is already complete. It does not ask to be conducted. From outside it is only a pressure in the teeth, a warmth with no sun behind it, the sense that a room has become a single held breath and has forgotten the lungs. They do not mistake loudness for a cry. A cry changes. This does not change. They wait through what would have been a day, and then through the length of time in which a cry, if it were a cry, would have broken or been answered or gone hoarse. It does none of those things. It is not calling. It is being the shape it finished into.

So they do not dig. They do not drill a mercy-hole and call the draft a gift. Air forced into a throat that has learned to sound without air would be a blow, not a kindness. Water would be an insult to a dryness that is no longer thirst. Light, the ordinary kind, would arrive as an accusation: *you were dark, and we have corrected you.* They have seen that correction. It leaves the corrected thing blinking and smaller, and the power who brought it feeling virtuous, which is not the same as having done good.

They look, instead, for the being, and they look without deciding in advance what counts.

What they find is not huddled in a crevice waiting to be lifted out. The being is distributed the way the warmth is distributed—along the hand-hollows, in the shared mouths of the pots, in the place where a wrist tired and the course went out of true and was allowed to stay out of true. It is not a prisoner who has forgotten the door. It is the memory of making that did not leave when the makers did, and it has used up every gap. To extract it would be to choose, for it, a body it no longer has. Benevolence that requires a body it can carry is only a preference for portability.

They ask one question, and they ask it in the only language the chamber has left, which is pressure. Not a word. A slight lessening. The smallest withdrawal of attention a power can make and still be present—as if they leaned back half a finger from the outer skin of the vault and let the chord be, for one measure, unwitnessed.

If the being is at rest, nothing answers. The note does not lean toward the absence. The warmth does not rush to the place where notice was. Then the wise thing is to go on listening from a little farther off, and to leave the seal as the seal was meant, when the last opening was closed from the inside by hands that knew what they were finishing. Not every closed room is a tomb waiting for a opinion. Some are the last sentence of a work, and a kind reader does not add a window so the sentence can enjoy the view.

If the being is not at rest, the lessening will find it. Somewhere in the packed hollows a partial will slip—not a scream, nothing so negotiable—a thinning, a wish to fall out of the chord and not be required to climb back. That is the only petition they will accept as addressed to them. Not the loudness. Not the age. Not the fact that a living power, arriving, feels sad at so much finishedness and wants the sadness to have been useful.

Then, and only then, they would do one small thing.

They would not unmake the chamber. They would return to the course where the wrist tired, the one place the old hands already left unfinished, and they would ease that joint by the width of a breath. Not a door. A pause. An interval the size of the original fault, warmed to the same mild fever so it does not arrive as cold, dry so it does not arrive as rain. Enough that the chord, if it wants, can set down one partial and pick it up again. Enough that staying and ceasing are both still possible, which is what the saturation had spent itself to erase.

They would not go in. Entry would turn the pause into a rescue, and a rescue into a story about them. They would stay on the outside of the height until the being either took the interval or let it close, as mortar closes, of its own weight. If it closed, they would let it. Wisdom is the willingness to have offered the only gift that could be refused without punishment.

And if nothing slipped—if the lessening of attention changed nothing at all—they would do what is hardest for a power that can do anything.

They would withdraw their hands. They would leave the warmth where it had settled, the dryness in its fossil, the chord in the business of being a surface. They would not bless it aloud. Blessing, said over a thing that did not ask, is another way of breaking the seal.

They would remember it accurately, which is a form of company that does not require the remembered to perform being saved. Then they would go somewhere a change could still pass through, and they would not tell this place it had failed by being done.
