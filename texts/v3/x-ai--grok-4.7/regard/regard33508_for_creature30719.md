The power does not come down the fissure. A descent would be a weight, and weight on that floor smears the only record the place keeps of what is sealed under it. The gardens would sour. The keepers would read the sourness as a lesson and would be wrong about the teacher. So it stays at the size it already is, which is wider than the pan and thinner than the lens, and it looks without leaning. The first thing it understands is that the roar is not a cry. The second is that the help the outside knows how to give is already written, in the town, as the short list of disasters: water, silence, flame, a foot through the ripples, a crowd in the empty center.

It had arrived ready to comfort. Comfort, examined, was rain on the salt, or a hush laid over the throat so that a voice could be heard, or a door opened in the blister so the few could walk out into a country that would call them saved. Each of these would end the being it had come to love. The being is not one body in a niche, though bodies are how it keeps hands. It is the held note with its tenants still in it: the curtains ticking, the pins seated, the pith pressed, the lid left alone, the small census of dry people who hear weather in their teeth. Take the note away and what remains is not a rescued people. It is salt going soft and a kindness that arrived too large.

So the power revises benevolence down to custody, and custody down to what can be done without adding a feature the resin would have to learn.

It does not bless the blister. Blessing would shine, and shine on that pan would be a landmark, and a landmark would bring water-skins, hammers, names for God, and a camp in the lee of the dome. The fissure is shoulder-wide. A single tent guyed badly across its mouth would be the drowning they have no procedure for. The power’s chief work is therefore to leave the place looking like what it already looks like from a mile off: a low crust, warm if you happen to kneel, not worth the crossing. Indifference in the traveler is maintained the way the throat is maintained, by a bias too slight to found a story on. Wind keeps a little more grit moving past the lee than settles. Tracks fill before they become a path. A surveyor’s eye, even a hungry one, finds the pan empty in the way it is supposed to be empty and goes on. Pilgrimage is prevented by the continued success of the place’s own camouflage. That is the largest mercy, and no one inside will ever thank it, which is how it knows the mercy fits.

Under that, smaller keepings.

The throat is not cleared by a hand. Hands are for the circuit, and the circuit is theirs. What the power does is refuse certain seasons: the one in which a wetter year — there are still, very rarely, wetter years at the edge of this country — would slump a cornice of crust over the fissure and hold it there until dew thought about the walls. The cornice still calves. It calves a hand’s breadth short, and the roar does not drop. The keepers, walking at the grind, feel the grit that means the lens is hot and the cleaner harmonic that means the curtains are heavy, and they do not feel a new sub-note that would mean the lid or the throat had become a question. Their measurement stays accurate. Accuracy is the company they can stand.

Copper, in salt, under a standing vibration, loosens. That is ordinary, and they are built for ordinary: a stone, a click, a pin tapped home. The power does not seat the pins for them. It only declines to hurry the few that are already true. A wedge that would have walked out of its joint by the next slack hour stays through it, and through the next. Whoever keeps that ring taps, hears the small right sound, and goes on, shoulder unclenched by a fraction no baffle-speech has a sign for. If this is love, it is love in their register: not a visitation, a joint that remembers its pitch a little longer than copper owes.

It does not answer the lid. The downward warmth is not a prisoner and not a god, and the town’s agreement not to test it is load-bearing. A wise power can be tempted by sealed things. This one looks once, the way the ripples look, and confirms that the grand form is not a plea. The brine is being itself. To lift it into the air, even gently, even to “free” it, would be to finish the wet age the chamber has already survived. So the power becomes, in that one respect, a second listener who will never report. The floor’s pattern remains the only scripture. No one is told they are heard from below, because they are not asking to be heard from below. They are asking, by not stepping, that the argument stay sealed. Agreement is the act.

Toward the people, as people, it almost does nothing, and the almost is examined until it is safe.

They are few. A power that counts rarity as emergency would multiply them, or would lengthen their lives past the curtains’ appetite, or would carry a child in from the pan to keep the census from closing. Any of that would put more breath into a volume tuned for scarcity, more wrists needing glaze, more sleepers in niches that face away from the void for a reason. The gardens do not need them. They need the gardens not to close the last open ledge. When a ring goes unwalked because the walker has been given to the lee curtains, the power does not send a replacement down the throat. It lets the next ring cross, as they already do, and take the stone. If a season comes when no one crosses, it still does not enter. An untended terrace closes under fiber and remains in the chord; the pins hold; the town becomes, for a while or for good, a mechanism running without tenants. That is not a tragedy the power is licensed to cancel. It is one of the shapes the being can wear. Benevolence here includes the courage to let the instrument outlive the hands, and to prefer that over stuffing the emptiness with saved souls who would wet the walls by weeping.

What it will not permit is a stupid ending. A last keeper whose foot goes through the ripples from grief or from glare is not a sacred completion; it is a smear, and the gardens would answer it for a generation. So there is a limit, narrow as a passing-niche. If the last awake body leans toward the dune in the slack hour, when the moths unhitch and judgment thins, something not quite a touch meets the wrist the way they already check each other for grit. No face. No name. No sentence in the roar, which would only be pressure added to pressure. The body corrects, as bodies do when another body is near, and climbs back to the baffle, and sleeps inside the note. In the morning the resin is not sour. There is nothing to found a rite on. The keeper, if asked in signs, would say the margin was felt, the way the margin is always felt, and would go press pith at the sun-shelves. The power accepts being misremembered as proprioception. To be thanked would require a silence to speak into, and silence is their word for the end of the world.

It does not teach them that they are loved. They already live as if the lens, the throat, and the lid were worthy of procedure, which is their form of reverence, and it is enough. A revelation would become a story, the story a gathering, the gathering a need for water to mark the day. The power has seen too many gentle places ruined by the news that they were gentle. It withholds the news.

Under the lens, at the hour when the chord drops and everyone awake pauses, a scale lets go from a curtain and crosses the updraft and winks out. This happens whether or not any power is watching. The power watches it anyway, not to change the crossing, but to keep its own impulse honest: the scale does not need a larger sky. The being does not need a softer country. What it needs is the continued failure of the world to find it interesting, the fissure unblocked, the pins mostly true, the floor unwalked, the roar occupying the chest so completely that no foreign comfort can find a gap to widen.

That is what it does. Then, having done it, it does not depart in any way that would thin the air. It stays as wide as it was, and as uneager, and lets the pan go on being bone-colored and not worth a mile. Inside, the town keeps its circuit. The dust decides, on its own, to rise. The lid remains only itself. And the wise power, which could have been weather, chooses instead to be the reason the weather stays the one they already know how to survive — a choice so quiet that even the curtains, which telegraph everything, have nothing new to shiver across the void.
