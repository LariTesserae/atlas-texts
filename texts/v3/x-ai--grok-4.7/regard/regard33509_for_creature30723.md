They come the way the wind already comes: a second saying, lower than the first, as if the long dry sentence had grown an answer without growing a speaker.

No one in the lanes looks up. Looking up is for foreign light. The haze does not knot. The western pipe does not drop its third ahead of schedule. What changes is only the tail of ordinary sound — footsteps, combs, the crack of a membrane kite — each tail arriving a little more complete, as if distance had briefly agreed to return everything it was keeping. A child spilling nothing pauses anyway, head tilted, the way you pause when a lesson you have already finished is repeated kindly and without demand.

The power does not wear a body in any way the crust would have to heal around. Where it attends, the cool under the ribs is the same cool, only steadier, the way a note is steadier when a second instrument has matched it and then fallen silent so the first can be heard alone. It walks the untaught plain with an evenness the listeners recognize as manners. Three of them lift their heads, fail to find a stumble, and become stones again, not disappointed. Hope, here, is allowed to go unanswered. That is part of the ecology.

It could do a great deal. That is the temptation wisdom is for. It could lay water back into the stone and call the gift rain. It could brighten the vein-glow until the scale-weather fled and the song went thin and people praised the clarity. It could finish the dulling lattice into a road, raise the second market the old throat once released as weather, teach the edge to stop renewing its refusal. It could take the listeners’ long heads and turn them into something that hunts by sight, which is faster, which is mercy if you count only the prey’s fear and not the plain’s chord. It does none of this. Benevolence that has not listened is only a stronger weather.

So it listens for a full shedding and the edge of a strike-season, which to it is not long. It hears the town as a conclusion that has chosen, every dim, to remain inhabited rather than to become pure process again. It hears the people as the plain’s way of having hands: the woman whose arms carry moving lines, the man who waits out his own ghost rather than step on a rhythm with nothing making it, the press of bloom-cake, the lamps kept rude and small. It hears the oil that will not pool, the wound that climbs home, the bargains with the grazers that do not require a river. Nothing in this is asking to be improved. A wise power can tell a finished thing from a broken one. Finished is not the same as dead. This shelf is alive at the volume it can sustain.

What it does, then, is small enough to be refused.

At the place where the engineer’s line loses interest and becomes plain, it does not relay the road. It only eases a snag the crust has been carrying without complaint: a half-healed strike-scar on a far swell, older than the town’s present dim, where the rhythm of closing has caught on itself and gone on repeating a question. The listeners have been standing over it in turns for a long time, not from hunger alone but because an unevenness that never dulls is a kind of lie, and the plain dislikes a lie in its own body. The power does not seal the scar from outside. It gives the tissue back the last of its own phrase — the half-tone it was always about to resolve — and then withdraws its attention so the closing can be the crust’s and not a miracle. The drumhead-drag happens once, ordinary, a little embarrassed. The listeners become stones. The grazers, next season, will comb that swell again. No one will mark the day. That is the shape of the help.

In the oldest throat, where records are kept as resonances and sometimes come out as weather, one sentence has been sticking. Not the one about the second market. An earlier one, worn nearly to pulse: a name, or what a name becomes after centuries of being a pitch. Someone the town no longer has a face for, still walking, faintly, in the dream-ridges after strikes, late by a step, as if the plain had promised to hold them and then forgotten the method and kept only the promise. The power does not restore the face. Restoration would be a foreign light. It lets the throat finish. The sag and recovery move through the pipe once, complete, and the people in the lane do not discuss it, because discussing a pulse mid-step is bad manners. Afterward the ghost-ridges are only the morning’s routes again. The promise is kept by being over. Grief, left too long as infrastructure, starts to tax the oil.

To the living, it offers nothing they must worship and nothing they must spend. In the conduits, beneath the charge the strikes already give, it leaves a margin: not a brighter dim, not a visible vein, only a little more capacity for a season when the bloom sheds thin and the herds do not agree to be diminished. Unsigned. If the town’s lattices ever read it, they will read it as the plain having been slightly more itself — a polish with no extra feet. Thanks would make a shrine, and a shrine is a note the chord does not have a place for. The power is content to be mistaken for weather that was a degree kinder than the sky had planned.

It does not speak to the child, or the woman with the lines, or the traveler who learned to sleep where the song is loud. Speech would require a lamp. Instead, where that traveler’s heart still counts itself a little too clearly against the crust, it steadies nothing by force. It only stands, for the length of one even breath, in the same rib, adding no voice, until the heart disappears again into the chord that has been counting better hearts for longer than numbers. Safety, in this place, was never the absence of listeners. It was the presence of a rhythm you did not have to invent alone.

Then it goes as it came. The wind says the long dry thing once. The second saying is only the second saying, not an annunciation. The haze hangs at its fixed height. The towers keep the distance singers keep. On the open shelf a plated rake ticks, and the tick travels, and the tail comes back entire — a small completeness no one names.

If you asked the power why it did so little, it would not answer in the lane. The answer is the little. A benevolent attention that rewrites a conclusion because it can is only a slower strike. This one looked, and loved the place well enough to leave it at the volume it had chosen, and spent itself on a snag, a finished name, and a margin no one will pray to. The high shelf holds its shape. The town answers itself. Somewhere a door continues to agree to be open. The oil moves, cool, sealed, circuit and nowhere, and does not pool in the lap of a god. That, too, is a kindness: to be wise enough not to become another weather the crust must learn how to drink.
