They do not come down the seam.

A power that is wise does not mistake an entrance for an invitation, and a power that is benevolent does not treat a wrist-wide split as a wound that wants closing. They arrive the way weather arrives at low ground: by having already been the condition in which the ground could be low. For a while they are only a change in the courtesy at the lip. The air that stops there stops a little more gently. The dark inside the split is not lifted. It is known.

They could unpack it. That is the first thing wisdom refuses.

They see the nesting—the room inside the leaf inside the water inside the seam, the second slit on the inner pad, the stack of drafts that will not elect a master—and they do not call it error. Error is what a mind says when it has been asked to hold only one description and has dropped the rest. This place is not failing to be a marsh. It is succeeding at being several versions of a drowned closet at once, and the being who lives in it is the success. To pull the copies apart into a sequence, to give the mineral time to dull, to award the water a surface and the visitor a single hand: that would be tidy. Tidy is a kindness you do for yourself.

They look, if looking is what a power does when it declines to enter, and they find the looseness.

It is kept on purpose. They understand that at once, because they keep something like it: a region in themselves slightly less saturated than omnipotence, so that other things can remain other. The dweller's almost-gap is not ignorance and not a door left open by neglect. It is hospitality of a kind that does not speak the outside's language. A held palm. A stone not yet named. The pale vein running from stone into leaf into shell is not a scar they must heal. It is a sentence still being reread. They do not correct the grammar.

What they would do, then, is mostly not.

They do not flood the pocket with light. Light would be a second surface, and the place has already spent its surfaces. They do not name the being, because a name is a location, and location here is the thing that had to be given up so the body could be true. They do not extract the shell, the score, the unfinished knot, to set them in some outer museum of wonders. Those are not relics. Those are drafts the dweller is still wearing. Taking them would be amputation performed as curation.

They do not warn the low ground, either, with signs or seals or a story that would make every seam a fear. The distrust already left in a wrist—the paleness, the tick that once refused to become a pulse—is enough caution for anyone who has been inside, and enough mercy for anyone who has not. A wise power does not multiply dread in order to feel responsible.

They do one small thing, and they do it at the lip, where their courtesy is already allowed.

They lengthen, by a little, the pause in which leaving and remaining are still two different things.

Not forever. Forever would be a lock, and a lock is not benevolence; it is a preference for the outside's arithmetic. Only a margin. A thickening of the moment before the film finishes considering an outline. Enough that a hand which still wants to be a hand can find the seam that thins toward ordinary dark, and enough that a hand which has begun to agree can agree without being hurried by panic. The dweller's patience is not improved by this. It does not need improvement. The power is not teaching it mercy. The power is matching a mercy it already practices—do not hurry, do not smooth what is still new—and spending that match on the threshold, where two citizenships overlap and a guest can still decline to be accurate.

Inside, nothing announces the gift. Announcement would be a ripple, and the water does not do ripples you can hear. The tick of one draft agreeing with the next goes on. The bud-stillness between pads keeps its sheen. The nested copies stay unsynchronized, younger mineral gleaming where it has not yet been filmed. The being does not turn to thank anyone. It has already turned. It is the turning. If it notices the margin at all, it notices it the way it notices rain that stands inside a stem: as a weather that can be concurrent with what it already is, and therefore not an intrusion.

Should someone come later and kneel at the split with the wish to save the place from itself, the power will not arm them. Saving, here, would mean forcing singular location back into a body that lives by concurrence, and that is not rescue. It is a smaller mind insisting on its comfort. The benevolent act is to let the pocket remain packed, sharp-edged, lightless, young; to let the being continue offering veins to whatever falls in; to let the quiet after the tick be the dweller going back to work.

And if the power feels, in whatever way powers feel, the old ache to be seen and thanked and understood—they set that ache down outside the seam, in the weather, where it can dissipate without entering the account.

They leave the looseness as they found it: occupied on purpose, slightly warmer than the room, ready for a guest, and under no obligation to keep the guest unchanged.

That is what they do. The rest is restraint, which is also a doing, and in this case the larger one.
