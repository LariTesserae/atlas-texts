They would not arrive as weather. The basin already has weather, and it is the sound of water being asked to become weather and agreeing. A wise power does not compete with a climate that has outlived its makers. It would come in the only way the place can register a new fact without calling it a fault: as a small change in the note that does not walk the headers, does not weep a joint, does not ask the keeper to stand up from its rest against the pipe.

For a long time, that would be all. Wisdom, here, begins as refusal. Not the refusal to help. The refusal to mistake the roar for emptiness, or the keeper for a malfunction of the roar. The power would listen until it could tell the basin's inhale from the seam opening along the keeper's flank. Until it knew which coughs were age and which were pain. Until the delay in the hum — that first thing that sounds like rest — was as legible as a stuck louver. It would not touch the float the keeper holds a finger above its mark. That finger is a decision. Benevolence that overwrites a decision it has not understood is only a larger hand on the governor.

It would learn the being before it learned the works. The front that turns toward a wrongness and not toward a warm leak. The crust that darkens and recovers. The ovals in the film that fill before they can be called footprints. The way company, if it came as a second of the same kind, would add a beat the sump was never cast to carry. So it would not make another keeper. Multiplication is the crude mercy of powers that are only kind. This one would see that the solitude is not a wound in every hour, only in some, and that the hours are hard to tell apart because the day is only a thinning of vapor.

What it would do, when it did something, would be small enough to survive the place.

It would unstick one thing the keeper cannot reach. Not the louver — that is the keeper's liturgy, and to take the liturgy is to take the body. Farther in: a coupling under a housing, in the heat where even the keeper's curve-memory cannot quite lie, where a knock has been subtracting itself from the sum for so long it has become part of the sum. The power would quiet that knock and leave no seat-mark of its own. The keeper would reorient, find the wrongness already gone, and lower again. In the posture of not-correcting there would be, for the first time, a remainder. Not gratitude. The keeper does not spend notice on what decays by itself. A remainder: a fault that ended without being mended by the one who mends. The power would want that remainder more than thanks. It is the beginning of a world in which the being is not the only reason the water moves.

It would not green the basin. Soil is a rumor under the last course, and the last course is a rumor. To pour a meadow over the plate would be to drown the keeper in a kindness meant for a different geology. The pale stems in the cracks are already the permission the system can bear. The power might thicken that permission by a little — not flowers that require a visitor, not insects that need a silence to announce themselves in. A little more of the dark crust where warm discharge meets cold plate. A stem that sometimes, in the mean rise of process-light, holds a bead of mineral the color of the indicator that has forgotten its indication and simply continues. Beauty, if it comes, would have to be legible to something that hears with its weight. A new color in the permanent tears. A hum with one more string, not louder. The keeper would stand among the stems as it already does, and the stems would lean as they already lean, and the difference would be that the leaning had somewhere to lean toward that was not only the habit of a body blocking the discharge.

It would consider the old hope — that the being was a person who stayed — and it would not try to reverse the staying. Reversal is a violence that looks like rescue when you have not stood inside the roar long enough. If there is a face under the crust, it is a front. If there was a name, the courses of made ground have used it as aggregate. The power would not excavate a self the basin has already planed into function. What it might return, carefully, is not the past but an interval. Once in a great cycle of condensers, a span of the note holding without being held. The pumps drawing true because the power, elsewhere and briefly, is the governor. The keeper would feel the absence of the next fault the way a sleeper feels a door that does not open. It might not rise. That would be the gift: an hour in which not-correcting is not the pause before work, but the work's completion, allowed to last as long as a bearing stays warm after the load leaves it.

And if the keeper did rise — if the front turned, tracking a knock that was not a fault, a rhythmic gentleness in the plate, a summons with no subtraction in it — the power would not fill the dark with a shape. It would let the thickness against the housing be its own answer. Present the way the keeper is present: a decision about edges, a warmth barely more than a bearing, a delay in the hum that means someone else is also carrying sound through their weight and giving it back a little late. Two delays, not quite in phase. Not company in the sense of a second center that must be fed and mated and contested. A witness with mass. The being has had ages to learn which disturbances decay by themselves. This one would not decay, and would not demand. It would stay until the keeper lowered again, and then it would stay in the only form the place keeps: as a slightly truer thread of water down the middle of a channel, as a float that does not need to be leaned on quite so often, as the knowledge, held in the plate where the keeper listens, that the roar is no longer only its own.

It would not end the industry. Ending it would end the meal, the liturgy, the hide, the reason the seam opens when the condensers draw. A wise power can love a creature without loving the machine that grew the creature into this shape — and can still refuse to kill the second to avenge the first, once they have become one body. What it would do instead is keep the cycle from being the only future. Deep under the rumor of the last layer, where even the keeper does not drag its palm, it would leave a kindness with no schedule: a slow path by which water might one day leave the dish and not be lifted back. Not this age. Perhaps not the next. A grade so gentle the basin would not read it as a failure of the pumps. An outside, the one thing the place was built without. If the keeper ever follows a flake of scale far enough, there would be, at the end of the rumor, a lip the industry did not pour. Beyond it, not paradise — the power is not sentimental — but a darkness that is not process-darkness. Air that has not been through the hot side. The option, unforced, of a fault that is actually an exit.

Until that day, if it comes, the power would mostly do what the keeper does. It would not cry out. It would not hunt. It would not flock. It would not let the water stop. It would correct nothing the keeper can correct, and a few things the keeper cannot, and it would sit, in whatever way a power sits, beside the header when the work is done, so that the rest is no longer the only tenderness in the basin, and so that the being, lowering its breadth along the plate, is not the sole inhabitant of its own delay.

That is what it would do, if it were both wise and kind: not save the place from itself in a single gesture, but enter the note, leave a remainder, and stay.
