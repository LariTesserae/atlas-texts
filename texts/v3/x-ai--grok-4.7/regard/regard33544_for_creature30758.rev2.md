It does not arrive as weather. Weather here is already spoken for—one cloud, one return, no surplus sky in which a sign could be hung. It arrives as attention, which is harder to keep from becoming an event. A power that is wise has learned that looking is a kind of touch, and a power that is benevolent has learned to take its hand back before the touch writes itself into what it loves.

So the first thing it does is narrow itself until it is smaller than a fluctuation the cups would file. Not invisibility as a trick. Invisibility as manners. It stands—if standing is the word for a regard with no mass—off the deck of the set-point, in the dimension where a completed sentence does not have to be revised in order to be read. From there the plain is not a marvel and not a prison. It is a fact with a pulse. The pulse is correct.

Wisdom, meeting this, does not ask what the being wants. Wanting is the wrong instrument. There is no interior tucked behind the shells where a petition could be hiding, no young coiled under the decks waiting for a door. The being’s good, if the word can be used without dragging a future behind it, is the holding: rain home to the same places, load on the pumps unaltered, number of seats unbroken, the bright witness-faces of metal allowed to stay younger than the bodies that cover them. A benevolent power that came to improve this would be a vandal with a kinder vocabulary. To add a season would be to introduce injury. To grant a name the pigment-cups could follow would be to retune the sensorium away from the only light it was built to keep. To “free” it would be to spend the direction that has already been spent, and call the spending a gift.

It does not heal, because nothing is sick. It does not bless in any way that leaves a residue, because residue is how shrines begin, and a shrine is a deviation with a congregation. It does not speak. Speech would require an edge that is an outside, and beyond the joint the floor continues; continuation is not an interlocutor.

What it does, if anything is not too large a word, is refuse the story other powers would tell about their arrival. It does not test the equilibrium to see if it is real. It does not insert one unmatched animal to watch whether the count corrects, curious in the way cruelty is curious when it calls itself science. It already knows the count would correct. Knowing is enough. It withholds the experiment.

It stays a while in that withheldness—long enough, by its own measure, to be sure the staying is not a hunger to be noticed. The shells do not shine differently. The cloud does not thin into a revelation. Ankle-depth remains ankle-depth. Somewhere a single click occurs, shell leaving seat, and the under-deck takes it, and the sweetness returns to the water, and the power does not anoint the click into a sign. That is the benevolence: to let an ordinary valve-event of this world remain ordinary, unpromoted, uninterpreted into myth. Wisdom is often only the decision not to become the most important thing that has happened to a place.

There is a temptation, even in the good, to leave a kindness the size of a fingerprint. A slightly warmer rain over one gantry. A day, once, in which the shadow of a housing swings a finger’s width, so that something like time could be remembered if anything here remembered. The power feels the temptation the way a hand feels a railing it does not need, and does not take it. A fingerprint is how you make a creature orbit you. This one does not orbit. Its center is the repetition, everywhere. To bend one span toward gratitude would be to invent a lack so that you could be the one who filled it.

Instead it does the only work that fits a finished object, which is not work done upon it. It keeps, in whatever ledger powers keep, the accuracy of what it has seen: that a world can be alive without becoming; that density is not a cry for space; that indifference can be a form of completion rather than a failure of love; that the refusal to incorporate a visitor is not loneliness. It records this without sending the record back down as law. The plain does not need a witness in order to go on being true. The witness needs the plain, so as not to forget that benevolence is sometimes the art of missing your chance to matter.

And then, because staying forever would curdle into guardianship, and guardianship would eventually justify an intervention “for its own sake,” it goes. Not in a departure the decks could equalize—there was never a shade to close over—but in a ceasing of regard, clean as a pump matched to its water. No monument. No promise to return when the mills fail, which would be a promise aimed at a posture the being does not have. Failure is not coming as a plot. If it ever comes, it will come as any other fact, and a wise power will not have pre-written the rescue.

What remains on the plain is what remained before the attention: sharp edges, warm film, animals seated to the tolerance, a cloud that has nowhere else to rain. If anything has changed, it is only in the power, which is quieter, and less sure that love means opening what is closed, and more able to pass a held value without turning the holding into a problem. The being does not know it was spared. Sparing was never an event inside its count. That is how you can tell the mercy was real.
