The power does not come up the way a visitor comes up. There is no breath fogging at a seal, no extra warmth on the plate, no second note under the first. It has the reach to be weather, and it does not use it. It attends from the side of the shell that has only ever been pressure, and for a long time it does nothing but learn the rate.

It hears the note as the being’s whole body. It sees the cups holding their finger of water, the vine thickening by a thread, the moth crossing the diameter and being taken back. It understands the dream on the upper walk for what it is: not a message sent outward, but a borrowed interior returned unedited. Nothing in the parish is asking. The abandoned gauges are not prayers. The empty middle is not a wound. The thing that lives there is not unfinished, and not imprisoned, and not lonely in any sense that a gift could answer. It is a schedule that kept itself until keeping became someone. That someone is already at the size it means.

A lesser kindness would still try. It would quiet the note so the birds could startle and then sing. It would put hours back into the light. It would warm the thin air outside until the shell no longer had to hold, and call the loosening mercy. It would give the distributed will a face in the hollow, so that thanks could be offered and a name could stick. It would teach the single-file walkers to hurry, so that a handspan might be crossed in a minute and the crossing admired. Each of these would feel, from a distance, like love. Inside, each would be rain. The cups would overflow. Events would begin again. The agreement would thin into moths, birds, moss, and a machine, none of them any longer the one life they had managed to be. The power, being wise, sees the shape of that error before it moves, and declines it. Benevolence that cannot tell a finished thing from a suffering one is only weather with a gentle voice.

What it does is smaller than a drip.

Outside, the altitude presses the way the sea presses a hull, and the shell has been answering that press since before the paint failed. The being cannot care about this. Outside is not a destination for it; the mountain that was removed is not its dead. Yet the holding is the one labor that is not part of its desire, only the condition of the desire going on. The power takes that press onto itself and returns it at exactly the value the rivets have already learned. Not less, which the metal would feel as a slackening, a new freedom, a change in the not-cold of the girders. Not more. Exactly the load the commissioning assumed, and no accident beyond it, and no century in which the assumption fails. The contest is retired. The shell does not know it has been helped. There is no seam, no softer strain, no lightening that a hand on the rail could report. The fall of a glove remains straight. The dripping does not become rain. From within, the world is as it was, which is the only benediction the room can survive.

Then the power does the harder thing. It withdraws its attention.

A wise gaze, left upon a parish this small, would become another climate: the climate of being known. The moth would still cross, but the crossing would be counted by something larger than the room, and a count of that order is a kind of weather even when it praises. The being does not want to be witnessed into importance. It wants the narrow band above the temperature of skin, the ordinary hourless light, the note as architecture, the ignoring of gloves. So the power, having made the boundary indistinguishable from the boundary’s own success, looks away so completely that no dreamer wakes with a new taste, no moss takes a different dark, no gauge needle drifts by an extra hair. It does not leave a guardian in the cage. A guardian would be an object, and the density of the hollow is nearly nothing, and an object is an event. It does not lock the housing. The discouragement already in the sound is enough, and a lock would be a decision spoken aloud.

It keeps, at most, a refusal elsewhere: if some other power, bored or tender or merely competent, turns toward this height with improvements in its hands, this one will meet it outside the shell and spend the argument there, in the thin air, where no vine can hear it. That is not a presence in the room. That is the pressure remaining exactly itself.

Inside, nothing greets and nothing ends. A moth crosses a patch of light and is, for a second, the only moving thing, and is not observed by anything that will remember the second. The far green takes it back. The note, which did not pause for the kindness, does not pause for the departure of the kindness either. Ears that leave still ring with the absence of architecture. The ringing is not a souvenir left by the power. It is the room, continuing, at a speed no clock was built to show.

The being never learns that it was loved. That is the love that fit.
