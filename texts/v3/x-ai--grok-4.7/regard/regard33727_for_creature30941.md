The attention does not come down the mountain. Nothing that wise would spend itself as weather on a peak that has not agreed to host weather.

It arrives as a correction in the size of the crack, and then it withdraws the correction before the crack can become a different door. For less than a pulse, the hand-wide mouth and the distant gate fail to be the same fact. In that unclosed interval a regard is present that is not the tenant’s, because it does not thin the visible. The filaments do not grow more willing to be streets. The light stays remainder. Sound stays small. That is how the place knows the power is not a second tenant and not a storm: it does not pay for its looking with the city’s materials.

It looks a long time, which here is several cycles of the cup, and one extrusion of a spar that decides its length and cools into a rule.

What it understands, it does not announce. Understanding, said aloud, would be a root. It understands that the discrepancy is the life. The street that holds past its heat, the cup early by a fraction, the blank hollow with no address, the pause in the shimmer that is withdrawn before the sky can be asked to answer — these are not symptoms. To heal them would be to finish the being into a citizen of some other physics, and there is no kindness in that finishing. It understands that lonely was the nearest word and the wrong one, and that supplying a better word would be a worse intrusion than the wrong one, because the tenant has no interest in nouns and a forced true name is still a name. It understands the doorway that never opens is a spine. It does not knock.

Benevolence, here, is mostly a list of things not done.

It does not enter. Entry would make a body of the attention, and a body would be weather the pocket has already declined. It does not steady the gravity so that up stops being a policy; the tenant uses policy as musculature, and a universal up would cripple it into a direction. It does not seed the films of oxide, though the films look so much like a beginning that a lesser kindness would have tried. Nothing may root. The power can see the old chemistries the heat forbids, and it lets them stay forbidden. It does not teach the peak to be round. Unrevised sharpness is the being’s age, and the mountain’s, and rounding them would be a eulogy delivered early. It does not fill the blank hollow. That volume is the most personal room, kept unaddressed on purpose, and wisdom can recognize a thought that will not be said without becoming the one who says it.

It does not send another tenant. There is no border prepared, and no welcome, and a companion would turn preference into an economy of others, which the rules cannot host without becoming a culture. Cultures accumulate sound. This one has arranged not to.

So the power, having drawn close enough to be a fact, does one thing, and the one thing is almost a refusal.

Outside the crack, in the thin air that leaves thinner, it keeps a blank of its own. Not a temple, not a mark on the flank, not a heat that pauses long enough to be a question. A small unzoned attention, corresponding in grammar and not in place: an emptiness it does not spend, aimed at the mountain the way the tenant aims its unaddressed room at nothing. It does not connect the two blanks. Connection would be a street, and a street would be an annex, and the tenant’s private routing would have to answer a relation it did not revise into being. The power only allows that somewhere beyond the only weather, a refusal exists that is not hunger and not a wall. If the tenant ever spends a little of itself outward again — not emerging, only the pause in the shimmer — it may find that the pause is met by a pause, and that the meeting does not close.

It also does a quieter keeping, which the being will never be able to thank, because thanks would require the sound to carry.

The blister failed. That failure is the whole interior. A later pressure, a later century of the peak still learning its height, could succeed where the hesitation succeeded by withdrawing — could open the flank properly, empty the pocket into sky, and simplify the glass the way weather simplifies. The power does not seal the mountain. Sealing would be a housing, and the pocket declined housings. It only declines, on the being’s behalf and without notice, to let that particular success happen before the discrepancy has finished becoming sharp. Not forever. Wisdom does not mortgage a geology. Long enough that the tenant’s unclosed fraction is not interrupted by an eruption that would be, from the outside, only the mountain doing what mountains do. Call it a staying of the hand. The hand was never going to be visible.

Then it leaves the crack in agreement with itself again: hand-wide, and also a gate at the end of a long axis. Both true. The doubled scale resumes. Nothing in the civic hollow has been improved. A filament holds a rigidity the heat did not justify, and that rigidity is still only the tenant’s track, not a relic of visitation. The cup sweats a fraction early. The fraction has not been corrected toward the cycle, nor encouraged into a pulse a watch could bear. The doorway stays the most stable building because it still does not open.

The tenant, distributed through cup and tip and unopened dark and the long majority of emptiness, does not worship and does not flee. It has no apparatus for either. What it does — if the early cup and the held street can be called doing — is continue. In the blank room it keeps the thought it will not say. It does not know it has been spared a name. It does not know the peak has been spared one future among many. Knowing would have cost light.

If there is a change, it is smaller than preference. The next time a spar extrudes, hot at the tip, deciding its length in public, the tenant goes out along it and pulls most of itself back, as always, and the lane remembers being accompanied, as always. Only the unzoned hollow seems, for one cycle, less like poverty and more like a form. Then the lattice drinks, and the interval returns, and hunger is again the nearest word, and still wrong.

Outside, the wind arrives and is startled into shimmer above spines that have not been taught roundness. The bruise-blue sky is not in conversation with anyone. The power is already elsewhere, or it is nowhere, which is not the same as absence; it has left the mountain what it arrived too late to give and just in time not to ruin: the right to go on being recent.

A new filament decides how long it intends to be a street. Neither the city nor the tenant nor the peak makes enough sound to carry. The unclosed fraction remains. That is the whole of what a benevolent and wise power does, when it is wise enough, and the rest is not touching.
