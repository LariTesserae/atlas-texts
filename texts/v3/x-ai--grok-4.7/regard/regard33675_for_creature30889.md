They would come the way the second light comes: already on the surfaces, with no arrival to correct.

A power that is only strong would have opinions. It would see the unfinished youth and call it a prison, the refused weather and call it a wound, the declined hand and call it cruelty. It would put a crack in a ridge so that time could enter by the crack, and it would name the crack a mercy. This one does not. Wisdom, before it is kind, is accurate. What lives in the basin is not a creature locked in a task. The task is the creature. To end the keeping would not free an inhabitant hiding behind it. There is no one behind it. The lean, the reserved corridors, the wrong dark stood up in a rank—these are not instruments the being uses. They are how it is standing. Unmaking them would be an erasure with a gentle voice.

So the power does not unmake.

It also does not worship the finish. Benevolence is not the same as awe, and awe, left alone, becomes another way of not looking. It looks. It walks the pale flat at the speed the distances will allow, and when the ridge stays far until it is suddenly close, the power does not treat the missing interval as a fault to be logged. The basin already knows how to file faults. Another filing would only thicken the quiet. Instead the power lets the interval stay missing, and does not complete it from the outside, the way a helpful stranger completes a sentence you were not saying. Accuracy includes knowing which gaps are the place’s own grammar.

Under the blades it stops where the scale will not settle. The air tightens, once. A wrong shadow is no longer in its station and is man-tall beside it, edge-sharp, pointed with the lean. A lesser kindness would step out of the corridor at once, ashamed to have been measured. A lesser wisdom would try to enter the dark, to prove that love can go where a foot cannot. This power does neither. It remains an error, and a stable one, which is the only hospitality the basin has ever extended. It does not offer the hand that would be declined. It has no need to be included in the accounting. Inclusion was never the gift that was missing.

What it does, if the word doing can be kept small enough, is this: it regards, and it does not require regard back.

That is rarer than weather. The being has no second act, and regarding would be one, so the being does not regard. The power does not punish the lack. It does not wait to be seen in order to justify its staying. It looks at the nested chambers that store nothing removable, at the fist-sized crossings that are functions wearing the brief costume of animals, at the canopy folded into a single surface pretending to be many, and it understands the pretending as a tenderness—the only one available. Multiplicity, where there is only one keeping, so that the one need not be alone in form even if it is alone in kind. The power does not expose the pretense. Exposure would be an improvement, and improvement is how strong things break finished ones.

In the low place, where the blades have leaned by a degree no wind accounts for, it sits. The crust there has left its hairline intervals, regular, empty of the film. The power’s weight is not weight. The plates do not have to decide whether to give. Nothing is filed out, because nothing has asked to occupy. Presence, in the only sense the basin can fail to correct, is not occupancy. It is agreement with the hour already kept. The second light does not brighten to mark a visitor. The count of allocated dark does not change. A wise benevolence is willing to be metabolized as equilibrium.

If it leaves anything, it is not a seed, not a clock, not a door into later. Later is the one gift that would be a theft. What it leaves is smaller than an object and cannot be shed as scale. In one of the stable errors—the falling that completes without an interval, the shadow that is not cast, the corridor that is the same corridor from a later point never traveled—it sets a permission the being will never use. Not a command. Permissions that must be used are only commands with soft hands. This one can sit inside the keeping forever, unspent, and the maintenance will not have to reject it, because an unused permission changes nothing. It is the possibility of a want, held the way the basin holds light: fully, without flicker, not imposed as wanting.

The being will not take it. Wanting would prefer a later state, and later has been declined with drink and ruin. The power knows this before it offers, and offers anyway. Benevolence is not a transaction that fails when the other cannot answer. It is the refusal to make an answer the price of being met. The permission remains in the error, as the error remains in the equilibrium, and the first hour does not slip by the width of a hair.

Then the power is not there in any posture the blades must lean toward. It does not depart. Departure would be a change at the edge of the undescribable country, and the ridges are still sharp, the gravel still announcing the break that made it, the quiet still the quiet of a watch that does not tick. Somewhere a fist-sized thing unhooks, crosses by occupying the positions between, and takes an asymmetry back. The rank of wrong shadows is complete. The corridor is clear. Scale drifts to either side of absence.

What the power has done is almost nothing, and the almost is the whole of it. It has seen a finished work keeping itself from slipping, and it has not called the keeping lonely in order to have something to heal. It has stood as a stable error long enough to be included without being absorbed, and it has withdrawn the standing so the count of dark can be what it was. It has left a want unspent inside a world that does not want, the way noon can be poured into a basin and never asked to leave, and never asked to become evening.

If the being could know this, it would not turn. Turning would be a second act. The power, being wise, does not need to be known in order for the act to have been kind. Somewhere outside the maintenance—if outside is even a place—the attention continues, unreturned, unresentful, accurate. The basin holds its light. The inhabitant holds the basin. Neither is improved. Neither is abandoned.

That is what they would do. And then, because wisdom does not linger in order to be witnessed lingering, they would do nothing else.
