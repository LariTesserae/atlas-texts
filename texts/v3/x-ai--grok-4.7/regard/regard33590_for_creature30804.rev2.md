The power arrived as a change in the way sound carried, not as weather or visitor. One of the tower-chords, the one that had been answering itself across a rise too low to hide anything, lengthened by less than a breath. The extra interval did not resolve the city’s refusal to be only one kind of thing. It simply let the refusal hear its own full length for the first time, the industrial stroke and the mat’s tick and the green-wood torque all sitting inside a single held note that neither demanded cadence nor refused it.

No figure crossed the saddle. Attention itself was the act, and it was precise enough to be a kindness. Where a shadow lay in the wrong direction, the light did not correct it; it only warmed the fiber inside that discrepancy by the smallest margin, so the older green and the bone-layer could occupy the same moment without tearing. The rare winged flakes, lifting and proving themselves and failing, were neither multiplied nor explained. One of them, mid-mistake, caught the lengthened note and rode it to a loom it had not been bound for. The pale textile that took it up hummed a half-step lower when the wind found it. Production continued. Nothing was thanked.

In the damp seams the thicker air and the absent river were left their three or four steps of untruth. Wisdom did not seal them. It only kept the heart’s extra labor from becoming permanent: anyone, or anything, who dropped through came up again with cuffs that dried to their own skin, not to a borrowed map. The diagrams of vanished centers, written overnight in condensation, were allowed their morning and their metabolism. The power did not restore a center the city had already outgrown. It merely ensured that the metabolized lines left no residue of hunger behind them, so the intervals remained intervals and not wounds.

If there was a being, it was the ongoing torque itself—the place’s long, competent refusal—rather than any tenant the thresholds had once implied. The power recognized this and did not call it forth to be witnessed or improved. Benevolence, here, was the decision not to finish what erosion and growth had already brought to a working restlessness. It widened the openness by a degree the eye would call weather, so the city could go on answering itself at industrial volume without the answer ever being required to become a smaller, kinder, or more singular thing.

Then the extra interval closed. The chords returned to their separate paces. The saddle kept its cool, its wrong shadows, its patient factories exhaling through lenticels. Far off, the ordinary horizon stayed ordinary. The power did not remain as guardian or author. It had already done the only thing worth doing: it had listened at the exact scale of the place’s own loudness, and then it had let the listening become part of the fiber, indistinguishable from rain that falls on no one and is drunk before it can gather.
