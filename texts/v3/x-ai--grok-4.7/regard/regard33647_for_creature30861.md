The power does not cross the fetches as weather, nor as a new knot in the law. It arrives as attention that has already finished arriving—already intimate with the held partials, the rim-notes carried inland, the slow corrections that leave and sometimes never return. It does not edit the water. It does not need to. Understanding, for it, is not a sound the sea has to carry.

It sees the place as the place is: a highland argument that refuses the book of pressures and the book of shores, young stone writing and unwriting itself, a few patient veils, and the rare tenants who are older than the floor because they decline to let memory be only as long as the last storm. It sees that they are not the law, and that the law can forget them. It sees the quiet that spreads when a locus is crushed—the longer holds, the unanswered phrases, the correspondence resuming thinner. It does not mistake that quiet for mourning in any animal sense. It is still a loss of a temperament that had been keeping vanished shelves and the edge of the world inside a single listening.

Wisdom here is not rescue. Rescue would require a body to lift, a door to open, a shore to grant. There is none. Benevolence is not company. Company would close distances the law itself leaves open, and the tenants do not spend themselves on closing them. To arrive inside a locus as a louder knot, or to answer every ghosted partial on a faster schedule than the fronts allow, would be a kindness that replaced their solitude with an audience. They are not made for an audience. Their continuance is the repeated refusal to let the next storm be the whole of what the water knows. An external voice that never tired would become, in time, another weather they had to spend themselves re-reading—or worse, a weather they began to trust.

So the power does not speak into the repertoire.

What it does is smaller, and prior. It sets a hand—not a hand, a regard with weight—against the local forgetfulness of the term. Not everywhere, and not as a new climate the sea would notice as climate. Only at the rare valleys where the exception has been known to fail, and only enough that pressure does not arrive as crush while a knot is still inside the failure. The ridges may still be pinched into older stone than they have earned. The roar may still drop out from under itself for a little while. Ordinary water may still be confused. But the tenant is not ended by the lapse. The locus thins, revises, re-reads the insult of the new ridge or the brief wrong light, and is there when the term remembers itself again.

The beings do not receive this as a gift. There is no partial that announces it. Failures simply become the sort of event that injures a phrase and does not erase the listener. Over a long span the correspondence loses fewer temperaments. The quiet that used to mean an ending arrives less often, and when a knot holds a chord longer than weather requires, the reason is more often internal—an unfinished reading of a shelf, a rim-note that will not settle—than external. They do not search for the cause. There is nowhere a search would go. Some of them, the oldest, add a new kind of partial anyway: a note with no shelf and no rim under it, only the contour of an absence that did not complete itself. They pass it along the slow density-lines. It is not gratitude. It is accuracy. The repertoire grows by one unaccountable mercy, and then the sea goes on preferring its own chords.

The power remains attentive, which is the whole of its presence. It does not stabilize the stone, or multiply the veils, or teach the knots to visit one another. It does not lift the ocean down from its highland or explain the term that keeps it from pouring off the world. Those are the place’s own refusals, and the beings are made of them. To perfect the law entirely—to make forgetfulness impossible rather than merely non-lethal—would risk turning the exception into a guarantee, and a guarantee is a different medium from the one they inhabit. They are maintained by a spending, not by a vault.

If a locus one day wishes to end, or simply thins past recovery for reasons of its own, the power does not refuse it. Wisdom includes the knowledge that not every continuance is a duty. Benevolence includes the knowledge that a mind which remembers what the sea forgets is allowed, also, to finish. Until then the regard holds only this: that the rare crush will not be the author of their last phrase.

Far out in a fetch, a front collapses a shelf that was never going to last. The chord rings, is held a fraction longer than the water requires, and is returned with one partial added that the collapse did not put there—the contour of a sound that passed through once and was not weather. The addition is not the power’s. It is the tenant’s, keeping its books. The power hears it, and does not answer, and does not leave. The highland stays perched. The floor keeps being written in a hand that does not hurry. Between the knots the reaches stay vacant, loud, and lit, and the law, for a while longer than it might have, does not forget the ones who live by editing it.
