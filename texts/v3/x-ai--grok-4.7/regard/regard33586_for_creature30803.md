They would not arrive.

Not yet. A wise attention does not spend itself on a rumor of a place, or on a being indicated only by a pronoun. Before it moves, it looks for the particular: the shape of the ground, the way the light is kept or wasted, what the inhabitant does when no one is watching, what they refuse, what they are already becoming without help. None of that was given. The sentence points — this place, the one who lives in it — and then the pointing stops in open air.

So the power does the rarest thing available to power. It withholds itself.

It does not invent a landscape in order to have something to bless. It does not sketch a being in order to practice mercy on the sketch. Invention would feel like care and would be a kind of trespass: a story laid over a life that has not consented to be told, and then a “wise” gesture performed on the story, as if the gesture had reached the life. Benevolence that must generate its own object is only appetite with a gentle voice.

It waits where the description should be. Not as a test. Not as punishment for an incomplete request. Waiting is simply the accurate posture when the subject is absent. Rain does not fall on a valley that has not been named into the weather. A hand does not close around a hand that is not there. The power keeps its warmth in, the way a shut mouth keeps breath, and does not leak it into the gap just to prove it is warm.

If something is already living in that ungiven place — and something usually is — this is what that creature receives: no visitation, no correction, no gift that would rearrange the rooms. Their days continue at the scale they already have. Work, if there is work. Quiet, if quiet is what the hollow makes. The mound rises by a finger’s width or does not. Lenses of water shrink and are renewed. No new law is posted on the lip of the terrace. The ripple-marks stay, because nothing wise has decided they should be otherwise.

That is not neglect. Neglect would be looking away after knowing. This is the refusal to pretend to know. The difference matters. A power that acts on what it has not seen will always, eventually, act past the being and into them — smoothing what was only unfinished, sheltering what did not ask to be housed, teaching a silence how to be meaningful when it was only silence. Wisdom here is the decision not to edit a draft it has not read.

And it leaves a small, exact opening, no wider than the instruction itself.

Bring the place as it is: the light, the floor, what grows, what is being built, what the sound refuses to do. Bring the being as they are, not as a moral to be resolved — their size, their errand, their harm if they harm, their limits, the thing they are mid-way through. Then attention can become answer. Then, and only then, it might do something: set a shade, or lift a lip of earth so the next rain does not take the nursery, or stand on the terrace and not call, or do nothing at all because nothing was the fitting act and the recognition was enough.

Until then it does not cross the screen of leaves. It does not breathe into the shafts. It does not bless a room it cannot yet see, and it does not call that hesitation love.

It waits, and the waiting is already the whole of its goodness.
