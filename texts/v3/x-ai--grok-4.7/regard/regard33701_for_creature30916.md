They would not come down.

A power that is both wise and kind learns, early, the difference between a wound and a work. This place is not asking to be healed. It is asking, with every unfinished rib and every key-change in the pipes, to be allowed to remain unfinished. To arrive in it—to cool a sheath, to steady a tilt, to complete one bridge so the eye could rest—would be to answer a question the valley has not finished asking. Benevolence that cannot tolerate an open question is only taste.

So they would stand off, where the heat-column frays into ordinary sky, and do the smaller thing, which is harder.

They would keep the other attentions out.

Curiosity is not gentle. Something drawn by the shine would want a sample. Something drawn by the scale would want a road. Something drawn by the absence of animals would decide the absence was a lack and correct it, and the correction would be deer, or settlers, or a god’s idea of birds, and within a season the arguing body would have tenants, and tenants prune. The wise power knows that a gift of life, bestowed on a place that is already one life, is often just a second will. They would not send the deer. They would not send the name. They would turn aside the quarriers, the measurers, the ones who mean well and carry sacks of seed, not with a wall the valley could lean on—walls become architecture here, and architecture welds—but with a forgetting at the rim. Pilots would find their maps blank. Walkers would reach the brighter haze and think of somewhere else they had meant to be by dark. The mercy would be that no one would feel refused.

They would not still the shove under the crust. Whatever is forcing the raft up is part of the same unfinished sentence. To gentle it would be to choose a height for a place that has not chosen one. They would only, if the shove came as a shear—if a whole mat were about to leave its weave and fall as weather into the dark under the dark—hold, for the length of one breath that is not a breath, the single fiber that still meant to continue. Not the mat. The fiber. Enough that the argument could go on in a form that still had somewhere to knock. Then they would let go, because a rescue that remains is a government.

They would listen, and not tune.

The low note in the cistern, the one felt in the teeth, is not suffering. It is level. When it drops into a frequency that hurts a human jaw, there is no human jaw. To retune it toward beauty would be to import a listener who is not there and then flatter him. The power would let the note be ugly and enormous when the pools slop. They would not collect it. They would not hum it elsewhere to prove they had been. Witness that must be witnessed has already begun to build a town.

If there is a being—and the valley is the nearest thing, one body in a long argument with its own dead, quilting them under before the bones can become a lesson—they would not wake it into a self it has not grown. Consciousness, bestowed, is a kind of weather too, and this climate is already full. They would not put a voice in the pipes so the pipes could say thank you. Thanks ends things. They would not put loneliness in the gaps so the spans would have a reason to meet. The spans are already reaching. Most will fail. Failure here is not a verdict. It is how the dead become gutters, and how the gutters become the next floor.

What they would do, then, is almost nothing, and it would be exact.

They would keep the daylight ordinary. No omen-light, no permanent rainbow in the steam-ropes, no star that points. Wonder that is staged becomes a shrine, and a shrine is a finished edge. They would let the light stay a little hard, the way it is at late morning, so that whatever watches next—if anything ever does—will have to meet the place as work and not as sign.

They would stay only as long as staying prevents a closing. When the risk is no longer that someone will complete it, but that their own attention will, they would leave. Not upward, in a way that teaches the columns a vertical to imitate. Sideways, into the fray of ordinary sky, taking no sap, no recording of the chord, no proof.

And if, long after, a bridge finally welds that was never going to, or a mat folds that might have held, they would not return to correct the outcome. Wisdom, here, is the refusal to be the last hand on a thing that is still hot. Kindness is the decision that a young place, loud with becoming and failing in the open light, is not a problem that love is obliged to solve.

They would let it run.
