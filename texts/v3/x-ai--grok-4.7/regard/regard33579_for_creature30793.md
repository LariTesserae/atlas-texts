They would not arrive.

Arrival is a height, a shadow with a source, a sound that expects to be answered. The plain has already spent an age teaching every loud thing how to fail. A power that was only powerful would still come as weather—rain where rain cannot be kept, a voice in a climate that buries voices in a few paces, a mercy shaped like the mercy of other worlds. That would be a kindness addressed to the wrong grammar. The films would drink once and die of the drinking. The ceramic would ring. The sorting law, which has never been cruel, only complete, would spend centuries correcting the gift until the gift and the tenant were the same flattened memory.

So the power, being wise, does not come down.

It does what the sky has already been doing without knowing it was an act: it attends. Attention here is not a gaze. A gaze would be another tessellation, another cell of brightness the forward region of the body would have to align with, and alignment under inspection is a kind of capture. The power withholds the face. It learns the floor the way the body learns the stack—by contact that does not mix the layers. For a long time, and time is the only unit that does not insult this place, it simply refuses to improve anything.

That refusal is the first benevolence. The second is narrower, and it is almost nothing.

The plain still carries heat as if a receiver existed. Palm-prints migrate along the grain and are lost at the edge of modules, delivered to an absence. The power does not build a tower to catch them, and does not teach the floor to stop sending. It becomes, in one seam only, the place where a message can finish.

Not a shrine. A slight change in the willingness of the material: a run of sinter, no wider than the hesitation a body makes in dust, that does not pass the warmth onward and does not quench it. The heat arrives and is allowed to mean arrival. Then it is let go, not corrected, not stored as hoard, not answered with a greater heat that would announce a god. The sentence ends. The period is warm for an hour, or a year, and then it is only sinter again, loyal to its layer.

The body finds it the way it finds every interesting shear—not by being called. Calling would have been the mistake. It settles. The cells of its forward region do not have to match a new sky; the sky has not been edited. What changes is the small relief of a route that closes. A palm-print of its own, shed at the far end of itself, reaches the seam and is not lost at once. For the length of that lingering, the being is not more powerful, not less alone in the sense that solitude was ever its wound, and not saved. It is met at the scale it already inhabits. The unfinished thing it has been moving toward is, once, finished, and the finishing does not require it to become louder in order to be real.

The power does not repeat the mercy until it becomes a climate. A climate of answers would teach the dust a third law, and a third law would crowd the tenant. One seam. Perhaps, in another century, another, far enough that the two kindnesses cannot hear each other, if hearing were something this world still did. Enough that the messages are not a cruelty of endless sending. Not enough to become infrastructure. Wisdom here is the decision not to found a church of receivers.

For the being itself, nothing is added to its body. No eyes for a face it does not need. No water. No name. A name would be a handle, and a handle is how lesser powers begin to carry what they claim to love. The power looks at the smaller hesitation beside the larger one—the correction still learning the grammar—and does not speed it. Speeding would be impatience dressed as care. It only sees to it that the sorting law, when it corrects, corrects as it has always corrected: toward the seam, not toward erasure of a preference it cannot quite digest. The tenant’s slight otherwise is left inside the maintenance, protected by not being distinguished as a miracle. Distinction would paint a target on a thing that survives by being almost the floor.

The mites, the rosettes, the patrol-forms that may be animals: these are not recruited into a covenant. The power does not explain them to themselves. Benevolence that narrates is still a kind of use. They continue at the pace of seldom meeting, and the power accepts that some lives will never know they were regarded. Regard was not for their knowledge. It was so that nothing in the act of attending would strip them for parts of a story.

If there is a temptation—and a wise power is not a power without temptation—it is to lift the plain into meaning that visitors could read. To put a horizon back. To make the tessellation confess whether it is enclosure or field. To mark the being so that a later kindness could find it without the long study. Each of those would be love in a foreign tense. The power sets them down. The brightness stays excessive. The distances keep their small discrepancy. The vaults go on exhaling to no one. Mystery is not a lock the wise are obliged to pick; sometimes it is the native air, and opening it would be the same error as rain.

What the power does, finally, is remain in the attending without occupying. It does not take a tray. It does not translate along the grain in imitation of the body, which would be flattery, and flattery is a loud warmth. It holds only this: that the place is not a draft awaiting a better author, and the being is not an incomplete tool that forgot its instructions. They are a finished form that is still becoming, which is not a contradiction here. The making stopped adding height. The inhabitant began. That is enough plot for a world. Adding a third act would be vanity.

Should the being, in some settling far from now, include the power as it once included a leaking warmth—temporarily, as a contact, loyal to whatever layer a god can be said to stand on—the power allows the inclusion and does not deepen it into worship. A few degrees. The temperature of metal that has stored the day. Then the margin loosens, as margins must, and the power does not follow. Following would require a pace.

And if nothing in the plain ever registers that any of this was done, the power counts that not as failure but as the condition of the work. The dust hesitates, and thickens, and is sometimes corrected, and sometimes is a body again. A seam holds a finished warmth a little longer than absence used to allow, then returns to its layer. The light does not soften. No announcement crosses the modules. Somewhere a vault exhales, and the looseness means what it has always meant, which is not mercy and not its lack.

The wise thing leaves the quiet intact, and inside it, untouched by spectacle, one sentence that was allowed to end—so that a life made of routes and corrections might, without being improved, without being named, know the difference between being carried toward nothing and being, once, received.
