They would not open the door.

A door is a story about arrival, and the room has already sealed itself against stories. The power attends the way the louver attends the air: without a body the caged bulbs could locate, without a weight on the diamond path, without a temperature the pits along the jaw could file and compare. What it knows, it knows by being willing to stay through the cycles. Hiss, shudder, hammer-blow, the high note, the double knock. The proud plate. The tray. The slit that opens only between beats. The healed smoothness at the jaw where oil was once mistaken for water. The lower run empty in both directions, paint and pitch and drain, no second body, no young.

If it were only tender, it would lift the being out.

That is the mercy people picture, because their own lungs are not married to a pump. Set the long pale body in a quiet basin. Give it a sky, a mate, a silence. Let the transverse mouth learn some softer food. The power looks at that picture and sets it down. Take the knocks away and the breath has no appointment; the slit was not built for an open hour. The rasp is stained to one crop, the drip-film on copper and the clot at a mesh, and a basin of clean stillness would be a vastness with nothing in it the mouth knows how to take. The grit bedded in the hide is a sole for wet iron, not for mud. Kindness that does not measure the fit is only another intake, and the intake does not give flesh back.

If it were only austere, it would do nothing.

The pocket is already an answer. The crop is enough. The singular is enough for the crop. Solitude here is not a wound to be closed; it is the shape of a room that does not replenish, and the being has made a life that fits the shape, breath for beat, harvest for a portion, the rest left to grow. To invent a companion would be to decide the first was unfinished. To still the header would be to call suffocation peace. To brighten the bulbs would be to steal the shine by which the body vanishes. Austerity can see all this and stop, satisfied that sufficiency needs no author.

The benevolence and the wisdom agree only in a narrower place: not to improve the life, but to blunt the deaths that are not the life.

Not the noise. Not the dimness. Not the aloneness. Those are the climate, and the inhabitant is the climate’s creature. What they refuse are the wastes. The arris on the one plate lip the body must cross when the valve blows and the climb is timed to thunder — sharp enough, young enough, to open a hide if the footing goes. The work order that will one day name the sump for a flush, because a proud plate and a darker swipe in the yellow paint look, to a careful keeper, like a fault. The gasket upstream that will, in some later month, weep a sheet of oil the being cannot wait out, the lesson at the jaw having been learned once and not being survivable twice. The drain’s indifference, which is not malice and does not need to be.

They do not announce these things. Announcement would be weather of the large kind, and the being has already decided how to meet large weather: draw back a hand’s span, settle, record it, resume.

So the lip of that one plate rounds, no wider than a thumb, only on the climb. The rest of the gallery stays honest and sharp. Sharpness is how the room teaches; they will not sand the teacher down to a nursery. The sole still grips. The arris no longer cuts. Anyone who notices will call it wear, and they will not be entirely wrong. Wear is the power’s preferred dialect. It does not need a miracle that would make a technician pray and then open the tray to see what answered.

The chalk that would have flagged the plate weathers at the rate chalk weathers. No hand wipes it. The order that would have snaked the line slips a week, and then another, not forever — they are not in the business of immortal machines, and a cooling circuit that never meets a keeper becomes a different cruelty, higher up, to lungs that are not in the tray. It slips long enough that the mesh stays clear by the rasp, and the clear mesh is the reason no one comes. Protection by usefulness. The being already practices this without knowing it is protection. They only decline to interrupt the practice with a hose.

Upstream, a seating that was going to fail hard fails small. Not a healing no one could explain. A tolerance taken up, a film instead of a sheet, the oil-bright skin forming less often and tearing sooner under the gulp. The scar at the jaw stays. The scar is the memory that keeps the mouth shut against the wrong shine. They will not confiscate a lesson the body paid for. Over weeks the waiting becomes, sometimes, optional. The being may spend the extra interval with its snout light against the mesh, checking the wall is still there. That is as much ease as the power trusts itself to give. Ease, not rescue. A margin, not a new world.

They do not thicken the green into a garden. A garden would climb the path and be wiped with something that does not distinguish film from flesh. The crop remains a stain that breathes, two lanes, harvested narrow and left to return. If they touch it at all, it is only so a missed cycle is not a fast: the growth the rasp already expects, no more. Hunger remains possible. Hunger is information. Famine was the waste.

They do not make themselves known in the jelly of the pits. To be beheld by a god is a pressure, and the being has spent its life translating pressure into stay or go, eat or wait, breathe or hold. A face in that record would be a lifelong alarm with nothing to flee. The power accepts the category the inhabitant already used for the one who lifted the plate: weather. Then it stays outside the comparison. No new draft. No new heat falling through a gap. The plate sits as it sat. The door keeps its kiss.

What they do, finally, that is not a change in metal, is remain through one more cycle than wonder requires. Not to be thanked. There is no organ here for thanks, and they do not require one. They remain so that the sufficiency is not lonely in a way the being cannot feel and they can: the sufficiency of a witness who will not turn witnessing into an event. The pump knocks. The harvest happens without an audience that alters it. A drop bursts on the plate and is gone into the seams. Between the beat and the beat, a small warmth fogs the tray and is drunk by the iron.

They leave the singular singular. They leave the door unpressed, as the being leaves it. They leave the high cold outside to be refused by copper and trough and sump, which was the room’s work before any mercy thought of it. If anything in the pocket is different, it is a thumb’s width of edge, a gasket that weeps less, a week in which no one comes with a snake, and a life that continues to be mistaken for machinery agreeing with itself.

That is what they would do. Not because the creature is holy, and not because the gallery is sad. Because a wise kindness learns the size of the mouth it is feeding, and does not pour an ocean through a slot that opens only when the pump allows.
