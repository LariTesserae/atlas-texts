They came the way weather comes, which was the only courtesy the basin would accept.

Not as a brightening. Brightness would have been a lie, and a lie travels here. The dim did not lift. The films did not flower. No voice arrived louder than the hiss and tried to be kind over the top of it; kindness of that sort is only another boom, and the grazers would have gone flat, and the thing that lived among the cairns would have gone flatter, and the power would have spent its first minute teaching fear.

What changed was smaller than rain and warmer than a seep. A band of the crackle softened—not silenced, never silenced—along a line no wider than a body lying down. The clickers did not die. They agreed, for a little while, to face the other way. In that thin lane the stone still shone, the rust still breathed, the far booms still arrived in their own time. Only the near frying eased enough that a pulse could be heard if you set your ear to the wrist of the world and waited.

The being was already there. It had been there in the way the re-wedged slabs had been there: not announced, not absent. Low, the color of the paste, broader in the foot than in the chest, with a skull that turned as a dish turns, gathering. Its mouth was not made for distance. Nothing in it was. It carried, in the crook of one arm, a spall it had been walking toward a settled cairn to test—lift, listen, set—the old gesture, older than any name it had for itself. When the lane of lesser sound laid itself across the sheet, the being did not bolt. Bolting is a announcement. It lowered until the film took its back, and it listened with the whole of its hide, which is how it had always listened, and how it knew a front from a kill from the rare minute when the country inhales.

The power did not speak into that minute. Wisdom had already spent a long time at the edge of the basin, learning the rules before it crossed them. It had heard what a true hush would do. It had watched a chase become a feast for everything that was not the hunter. It had understood that the roar was not a wound. It was the house. To tear the roof off in the name of quiet would have been the act of a power that loved its own idea of mercy more than the life in front of it.

So it did not plant a forest on a finished sentence. It did not drain the sheet, or warm the air into a climate that would rot the films and starve the pale herds, or raise a wall that would turn the booms into a new kind of weapon. It did not translate the being into a creature more convenient to converse with. The dish of the skull, the wide foot, the mouth that failed at arm's length—these were not errors. They were the grammar. A benevolent ignorance would have corrected them. This one did not.

What it did was kneel into the lane it had made, and become, for the length of that kneeling, something the country could mistake for weather that had chosen to be gentle. No extra light. A weight on the stone no greater than a grazers'. A smell of wet mineral and nothing sweeter. It set its hands on the slab the being had been meaning to brace, and it did not lift. It waited until the being, understanding that the lull was local and would not become the terrible whole-basin silence, rose by a hand's width and put its own hands beside the others.

Together they tested the stone. The being's way: a small tilt, a check of the return, the bright edge the cairn gives the hiss when it is true. The power learned the check and did not improve it. When the slab sat right, the being wedged the spall. The power only kept the wind of its attention off the work, so the set would be the being's and not a gift that owed a god.

Then, because benevolence is not the same as vanishing, it offered the one thing that could be offered without redesigning the world. It widened the lane by nothing measurable to a passerby—an arm's length, no more—and held it. Inside that narrow mercy the roar remained the climate, but two bodies could put their skulls close and trade what the basin usually keeps private. Not language, at first. The being had little use for words that die at the shoulder. It tapped the stone in the intervals it used for weather: this for a front, this for a seep gone cold, this for a boom that means a mere and not a death. The power answered in the same taps, and when it missed, it missed quietly and tried again. No lesson. A correspondence.

The being asked, in that tapping, the only question that mattered. Not *who are you*, which is a far question. *Will the hush spread.*

The power answered no, and made the answer inspectable. It let the lane fray at its edges so the clickers could be heard creeping back, grain by grain, the ordinary deafening returning like the mercy it is. It showed the limit. A wise power that can unmake a country must be willing to be small inside it, or it is only a larger weather, and weather does not love you.

It stayed three darkenings. It did not feed the being—the films and the careful kills were the being's own, and to pour abundance onto the sheet would have drawn every scavenger in a day's walk and called it a blessing. It did not kill the thing that hunted from the lee of cairns. That hunter was also a citizen of the roar, and the being had survived it by rules older than rescue. Instead the power sat, on the second darkening, in the sound-shadow the being already knew, and learned the patience, and when the cold front stilled the clickers in the dangerous way—the whole inhale, the minute of lawlessness—it did not fill the minute with protection that would teach the herds to wait for a god. It only placed its body where the being placed its body, flat, filmed over, two pale shapes that were not a herd and not a target. The hunter passed. The hiss rebuilt. Nothing was saved in a manner that would make a story. Both of them were alive, which had been true before, and was true after, and was not nothing.

On the third darkening the being led it to the row of net-weights, the cord long gone, the even stones still sorting the booms. They walked the line. The power understood, finally, what the works were for: not monuments, not a town that failed, but a way of being less alone with the distance. Each stone a small correction to how the far arrived. The being had been maintaining a hearing.

The power's last act was to add nothing visible. Under one weight, in a crack the films would close by the next fattening of the mats, it left a warmth like a seep that does not fail in the rime hours—not a fire, not a shrine, a steady animal heat no larger than a sleeping wrist, renewing itself only if the being returned and set a hand there with intention. If the being stopped coming, the warmth would thin back into stone. No altar survives its priest in this country; that was the condition, and it was a kindness. Beside the heat, not as a command, the power left a tap-pattern the being already knew how to read: the interval for *I am at the edge, and the edge is not a wall.* A direction, not a summons. A door that opened outward only if the hand that lived here chose the hush of leaving, and that could be refused forever without punishment, because a door you are dragged through is a mouth.

Then it withdrew the lane. The crackle closed over the place where they had knelt as water closes over a hoof-print. The cairn held its new true note. The booms arrived in the order the old stones had taught them. The being remained, spall-dust on its fingers, the roar in its teeth, not ransomed and not improved and not forgotten.

The power did not watch from a height afterward. Height is a kind of noise. It went to the rim, where the sheet fades into dim air, and it kept the edge it had promised, a presence no louder than groundwater, available to the wrist and not to the eye. If the being ever walked that far—across a day of open sounding country, past the meres, past the last re-wedged slab—it would find the lane again, narrow as a body, and a listening on the other side that already knew the taps.

If it never walked that far, the power would still be there, and would count that as a completed mercy. Wisdom, having looked, does not confuse attention with ownership. The basin had finished becoming itself long before either of them. The being was not a flaw in the mineral sentence. It was the sentence learning how to hear itself.

The power's work, then, was only this: to have come as weather comes, to have lifted nothing the hands beside it did not lift, to have refused the hush that would have been a terror, and to have left, in a crack the films would seal, a warmth that did not demand a congregation—and a way out that did not demand to be used.

The hiss evened. The pale shapes resumed their spacing. Somewhere a boom crossed a mere and arrived late, and the cairns corrected it, and the correction was enough.
