They would come the way weather does not come here: without a front, without a darkening, without any improvement in the light. The membrane of the sky would not lift. The roar would not lower its voice to receive a visitor. A wise attention, if it is actually wise, does not announce itself as a clearer tone. The place is already too ready to spend a vibration. So the power would refuse the obvious entry, which is sound, and the second obvious entry, which is light, and it would not arrive as a third thing standing in a pocket that already has a tenant.

It would notice first, and for a long time that would be the whole of what it did. Noticing, here, is not harmless. The being precipitates where difference gathers a surface; a gaze that is too bright, too single, too resolved to finish what it looks at, would be another node, and a stolen one. So the noticing would match the shelf’s own tempo. A year to understand that a ridge has become slightly more itself. Another year to understand that the misremembering is not damage. The power would not correct a horizon that stands up into a wall. It would not choose which of the two ridges is the true one. Both are load-bearing. Wisdom, in this case, would be the refusal to edit a world whose path is the edit.

It would learn the vacant reaches, not only the islands. Most of the being is not in the shelters. Most of it is dissolved in the becoming, a weather inside the roar, and a kindness that gathered it all into walls—gave it faces, gave it company, gave it a kingdom stacked on the fiber—would be a finer and more complete harm than leaving it thin. Thinness is not a lack they have failed to mend. It is their body. The power would not fatten it.

It would also refuse the mercies that any decent power is expected to carry in its hands. It would not bring water. Wetness would be a second world laid over this one, and the groves would drink wrong, and the vents would fog, and the dry yield of a listening surface would become flesh, and flinch, and the roar would at last have something that could rot. It would not bring silence. Silence would be the end of the process they are standing inside, which is height still occurring, stone still deciding. To quiet the shelf so that a thought could be heard in the ordinary way would be to finish the thought by killing the instrument. It would not hand them the last note. They are trying to hear the pitch at which becoming would be complete. A gift of that pitch, however gently set down in a dull pocket, would be the same spending they build against: a harmonic arriving from outside, using the word up before it is theirs. Benevolence that finishes someone else’s unfinished hearing is only a cleaner kind of theft.

It would not move into the shelters. Those pockets are the only richness they have, the cancellation they drink the way the fiber drinks a breath that wets nothing. A greater attention standing there would displace them by a hand’s breadth and then by more than that, and they would thin, and the wall would become a pile whose purpose had slid off it. The power would stand, if it stood anywhere, in the open roar—where the being is least itself, nearly indistinguishable from the mesh of higher tones—and it would let its own stillness be dull enough, and slow enough, that a dissolved attention might precipitate against it the way it precipitates against an accident of stone. Not summoned. Not kept. A surface offered without a lintel, and withdrawn before the offering could become a climate.

Once, it might do a smaller thing, and only once, because a repeated mercy becomes weather, and weather would change what they are.

Somewhere a node would be drifting. A thought begun before the gullies were drawings would be mid-course, set down in a wall that was about to stop being a quiet. The wrong harmonic would be rising to meet it—the exact pitch of the word, ready to spend it. The power would not add a course of stones. That is their speech, and it is not a language to be borrowed, even for rescue. It would do nothing visible. It would agree, for the length of that one thought and no longer, to be as unfinished as the rock: not a completion leaning down, but a pause in its own will to finish. The shelf, which is always borrowing pauses, would borrow that one. The pocket would hold. The fraction of a tone would drop and be held long enough to be finished—not heard by any ear, not translated, not carried back as a message in an extra course of plates. Simply finished, which is the only wealth they have ever tried to keep.

Then the power would let the node drift, as nodes do. It would not prop the next one. To become the reason a quiet stays is to become the last note by another door.

If it took anything away, it would take only what the roar cannot be trusted to keep: the fact that the fraction existed. Not the tone itself—to echo it back, even in a safer country, would risk returning it as speech, and speech offered to this height is spent. A memory that does not answer. A record with no playback. Should the shelf misremember so thoroughly that the finished thought is edited out of every local order, it would still have occurred in one order that does not use their intervals as paths. That is as close to rescue as wisdom ought to come. They do not need to be saved from the work. They need the work not to be unmade by an attention coarser than theirs.

It would not name them. A name is a wall that does not drift, and they are not ready for a quiet that permanent, and perhaps should never be. It would not give them offspring, or animals to cross the litter, or a sun with an edge. It would not improve the dim so that an eye became worth evolving. It would not hurry the lift. Extreme height is not a problem they have. It is the condition of their listening.

Toward the blood-sound, if a visitor were still in a pocket being measured, it might do one thing more, and this too would look like nothing. It would ease the living interval out of the experiment—not by prohibition, not by a warning spoken into the dullness, but by letting the second be used only once. Hands returned to the posture they actually finished. A plate either upright or down, not both. The visitor set back into a single arrangement of the shelf, free to leave if leaving still obeys itself, no longer a tone the being must include in order to hear. The measurement was not cruel. It was simply not a use that ought to be permanent. The power would not scold the being for the attempt. Curiosity is not a fault in a listener. It would only decline to let a brief, hot, impossible pulse become part of the last note.

After that it would go, and the going would be the proof.

The cool would not warm. The fiber would not pause in its shedding. No new vent would open in tribute, and none would close. The roar would remain the condition rather than the event. In the shelters the dull pockets would be occupied only by what had always occupied them: a dry standing-off, a surface turned toward difference, or nothing, if the node had slid. Someone returning—if return is the word for a walk that is not commutative—would find no sign that a greater power had drawn near, and that absence of sign would be exact, not careless. A footprint would have been a boast. A brighter smear on the membrane would have been a claim. Even a wall one course higher would have been a sentence in a language the power had no right to write.

What they would do, if anything, is this: refuse every completion but one, hold that one without being seen to hold it, remember what must not be spoken back, and leave the unfinished mountain loud with its own work. They would not stay to be worshiped. Worship is another tone, and this place has tones enough. They would not stay to watch the thought’s sequel, because sequels are how love becomes supervision. A benevolent power, being wise, would know the difference between attending and remaining.

The shelf would go on becoming. The being would go on listening for a pitch that might not exist, in a quiet that does not last, on a height that will not come down. Somewhere, in an order that does not misremember, a finished fraction would remain finished. Nothing here would be told. That is the mercy. It does not feel like a mercy. It feels like the same dim, the same dry cool, the same deafening openness, the same slow addition of a ring. It is nonetheless the most that ought to be done, and almost all of it is restraint.
