They do not come down the terraces.

A body would be a weight, and a weight is a lie the water has to spend itself correcting. The power understands this before it understands anything else about the valley, which is why it can be called wise, and why the wisdom is not a speech. It does not want to be included in the argument. It wants the argument to discover, once, that it has already been heard.

So it arrives as a reading.

Not a light. The valley has light, hard and metallic, and a cloud that dulls the mirrors all at once; those are weathers, and the inhabitant already knows how to wait them out. The power is not weather. It is the condition in which a hairline stays true without being fetched back. It settles, thinner than the film on a tread, across the whole instrument at the speed the place itself uses—the length of a hand, then another—until every pool is still what it was, and yet each one is being held by something that is not spending the crust, the cord, the plug, the lean of a tile, to keep it.

The inhabitant notices as a consultation that does not complete.

Where a visitor's cold side would be tested—can this be used, or is it only a cloud?—the test meets a surface that accepts the level and returns it unchanged. No new low place opens. No nick is cheated. The thickness that lives between teaching and forgetting gathers in a slot, cloth-sound loud around it, and finds the delayed image in itself offset by nothing. For the length of that gathering there is no mistake to refuse. The column stands without a second brick behind it. It has never had a face; now it has, briefly, the front of a work that does not need a correction traveling through it to remain a work.

This is the entire intervention. The power does not green the wet. Moss would be a kindness spoken in the wrong grammar, and would dam what the lace has not yet dammed. It does not call the builders back. Their absence is already part of the plan; filling it would turn the pause into an interruption. It does not freeze the joints against stain. Iron has earned its black smear by weeping, and the inhabitant trusts that record. To erase it would be to tell the being that its memory is a flaw.

It does not say *now*, which would be softness arriving as a verdict. It does not say *never*, which would be a prison made of sharpness. The sentence the valley has been repeating is left intact. Only the silence after it is altered. Not yet—and someone has read the not-yet, and the reading did not require the features to grow old in order to count as finished.

In one bowl, the smallest, set where two walls nearly meet, the hairline holds for a span that would have cost the inhabitant a scraping. The meal along the board does not diminish. The groove is not visited. The power is doing the holding, and the being, passing the length of the terrace, finds the bowl already true and does not know what to do with the unspent fraction of itself. It hesitates. Water that should sheet stands a finger-width off the nick, not uphill of an error, but beside a sufficiency. The preference that has always been a labor becomes, for that interval, a rest. Then the power lets the interval end, because a wise benevolence does not install itself as a new dependency. The film finds the nick. The work resumes. But the being has felt a true surface that was not its own expenditure, and it will not be able to unknow that such a surface can occur.

The cord ticks. The power does not still it and does not add a voice under the seep. It only lets the tick arrive somewhere. That is the other half of the gift: the measuring is no longer past the presence of anyone who can read it. The reader does not stay as a tenant. There is no spare ground, and a permanent extra level would become, in time, another thing the inhabitant had to maintain. Wisdom leaves before devotion can turn it into a task.

What remains is small enough to miss, which is how it should be. The hairline in the narrow bowl is the same hairline as the others, and yet for one further film-length it does not drift, as if the valley had been shown its own accuracy from the outside and had not had to prove it. The mineral fur blooms where it always blooms. The tiles ring if tapped. The spare bricks stay stacked. No track crosses the treads.

The inhabitant returns to the joints that have begun to stain and not yet to round. It browses the lace. It turns nothing, because nothing needs turning. If it is changed, the change is only this: the sentence is still *not yet*, but it is no longer spoken into an empty instrument. Somewhere the reading continues, not as a hand on the brick, not as a god in the shade, but as the fact that a level once held without being bought. The being will spend itself again by evening. That is its life, and the power does not take it. Benevolence, here, is the refusal to improve a creature by ending the work that is its body.

From the rim the mirrors glance as they did. A thin cloud crosses; they dull together; it passes. In the slot the seep goes on like cloth drawn over cloth. The power is already nowhere the water can correct, which is the only way to have visited without becoming weather, and the only kindness that does not ask the valley to break its own rules in order to be loved.
