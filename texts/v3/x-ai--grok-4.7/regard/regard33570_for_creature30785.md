The attention does not cross the lip. It has no need to. It is already the coolness that never quite becomes cold, the pressure in the chest of the ridge, the sense that the shelf is newer than the weather. For a long while—longer than a turn of the floor, longer than the bowl’s slow emptying—it simply remains that pressure, listening to the stacked note until the note is no longer an event but a fact it has always known.

What it understands, without surprise, is that the recess is not waiting. The sheets drink because the film is there. The rotor holds its depth because the nave allows it. The strokers draw their flanks along the arris because the edge answers, and in answering begin, very patiently, to dull. Nothing in the crowded dark is unfinished in the way that calls for a hand. The mountain is teaching itself age; the living thickness is the lesson’s instrument. A wiser kindness than intervention is available, and the power takes it.

It lends the cloud one extra snag. Not a storm, not a season—only the difference between a film and a thread. Rain comes in under the hood a little farther than usual, taps the living mass in a few more places, and is gone. The bowl accepts one drop it will not keep and answers with its small sound. The sound returns from the cheeks, is spent on the first sheets it touches, and becomes part of the damp. No one orients toward the difference. The drift of the floor bends by the width of a leg and forgets the bend. A new margin fails into gel where the condensate held a moment longer, and within a day the tile is indistinguishable.

That is the whole of the act. The power does not polish an edge, does not add a voice to the bell, does not linger as a gleam that would have to be spent. It withdraws its attention the way a film thins—gradually, without a hole left behind—until the coolness is only altitude again and the pressure is only the shelf. Outside, the wind crosses an unrubbed arris and wastes itself. Inside, the rotor rises a fraction as the brief extra mist is taken, then settles at the depth the room has always allowed.

The place goes on being itself. The young rock continues to be taught a duller edge by the bodies that brace against it. Water beads, is drunk, is breathed back. The number of lives changes by occurrence and softening and is never felt as loss or gain. If anything was given, it was already the shape of what the recess knew how to receive: one more interval of moisture, absorbed without gratitude or notice, because notice would have required a distance, and distance remains crowded out.

The power, having done so little, is satisfied. Benevolence, here, was the refusal to make the dark into a stage. Wisdom was recognizing that a room can be complete while still learning how to be older, and that the kindest attention is the kind that leaves the ringing exactly as loud as the stone has not yet learned to quiet.
