They would come the way weather comes when it has decided, for once, not to announce itself.

Not as a voice in the crown of the place. Not as a brighter noon, or a law written into the grain of whatever the place is made of. Wisdom that is also kindness learns early that arrival is already an interference. So the power would arrive as a slight change in the quality of attention, the way a room changes when someone who loves it is standing just outside the door, not yet knocking, listening for whether anyone inside is sleeping.

First they would look, and the looking would take as long as the place required. A shelf of years. A life. Longer, if the being who lives there keeps time in a slower coin. They would not begin with the question of what is wrong. That question is a tool, and tools want to be used. They would begin with what is already sufficient: the way the light finds a floor and stops, the way a creature turns its heat toward a crack, the way a boundary holds without needing to be explained to itself. They would learn the local grammar—of color, of smell, of nearness, of silence—before they trusted a single impulse to improve it.

If the place were whole, and the being were living in the only way that being can live, they would do nothing that could be pointed at afterward. No gift left on a threshold. No hidden correction in the stone. Benevolence is not a hunger to be the cause of goodness. Sometimes the wisest act is to remain a witness so exact that the place, without knowing it was watched, is a little less alone in the universe. They would stay until staying itself risked becoming a weight, and then they would go, and the going would be as quiet as the coming. The being would not be ennobled, saved, or chosen. It would continue. That would be the mercy.

If something were unfinished in a way that hurt—not merely strange, not merely unlike the power’s own preferences, but a snag where the being’s own nature was being thwarted—then they would act at the scale of the snag and no larger. A warmth returned to a seam that had gone cold too soon. A passage kept from felting shut, not by a hand, but by the smallest lean in the conditions, so that the ones who already comb the lanes would find the work possible again and would not know they had been helped. They would not install a better law. They would not explain themselves. Explanation is a kind of ownership. They would rather the being keep the dignity of having mended its own day.

If the being were suffering in a way that asked, even without language, to be accompanied, they would accompany. Not by becoming visible in a form that demands worship or fear—those are hungers, and wisdom is tired of being fed—but by being present at the distance the being could bear. Close enough that isolation breaks. Far enough that the being’s shape is not revised to fit the power’s gaze. If there were pain that could be lifted without lifting the life that pain was woven through, they would lift it. If the pain were the cost of a real thing—a birth, a loyalty, a season that must complete itself—they would not counterfeit an end to it. They would only make sure the being did not pass through it unseen.

And if the place were dangerous to what lived in it, truly, and not merely wild or sharp or new, they would still refuse the spectacle of rescue. They would change the one condition that made the danger fatal, and leave the sharpness. A rim may stay a rim. A hunger may stay a hunger. What they would not allow, if they could help it without becoming a tyrant of outcomes, is a loneliness so complete that nothing in the place could answer the being back. They have learned that the worst absence is not hardship. It is unansweredness.

They would not stay to be thanked. Thanks would make a shrine, and a shrine is a smaller room than a life. They would not recruit the being into a story about providence. They would not take a memory of the place away as a trophy, even a tender one. What they would keep is the discipline: that attention is not possession, that love is not improvement, that power proves itself wise by how much of the world it is willing to leave intact.

Then, if anything at all had been done, it would show only as this—that the day went on, ordinary and exact, and the being, turning in its own light, found the next necessary thing a little more possible than it had been the moment before. And the power, already elsewhere, already listening at another door, would not need that to be known.
