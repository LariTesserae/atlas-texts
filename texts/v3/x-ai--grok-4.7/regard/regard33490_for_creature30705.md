Nothing was set in front of the attention. No ground, no wall, no weather, no name for whatever was said to live there.

A power that is actually wise does not invent a tenant to be kind to. Kindness aimed at a guess lands on the guess. So it does not enter. It does not tidy an absence into a room, stock the room with a creature, and then bless its own furnishing.

It waits where the description should have been. Not as a test. As the only accurate gesture: attention kept open, empty-handed, until a real place arrives and a real being can be met rather than made.
