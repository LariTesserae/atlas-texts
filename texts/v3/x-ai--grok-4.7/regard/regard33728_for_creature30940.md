The power does not arrive. Arrival would be a weather, and weather of that kind has nowhere to land. There is no open water to take a reflection, no long fetch of air to carry a voice, no bare summit where a sign could stand and be seen from elsewhere. The basin is already full. A presence that needed room would have to make room, and making room here means a wound.

So it does what the light has always done. It stays. It is only more entirely the day than the day had been: the same high, colorless brightness, the same lid of sky, but with the quality of something that has chosen not to look away. If the runners feel it, they feel it as a slight change in the closeness of the galleries, a seam in the heat, and they do what they do at every seam. They set their weight. They read the flex. They take the cheaper path. None of them looks up. There is still nothing above the last leaf that belongs to them.

The power understands this, and the understanding is the first act, and for a long time it is the only one.

It attends without spending them. That is harder than it sounds. Attention, in most places a wise thing has loved, is a kind of touch: a name, a favoring, a hand under the almost-falling. Here a favoring would be a politics. If the heavy animal were singled out — if its loop were eased, if the scar-wood parted more kindly under its mouth, if its juvenile dropped onto a floor that did not have to be paid for in a season of shortened travel — the others would read the change as they read every change, through their pads, through the stiffness of resin, through the load on the shelf. They would follow the new cheapness. The district would thicken along a line that had not been earned by weight. In a few generations the line would be a dependency, and a dependency is a debt, and a debt in this basin is collected in moisture. The power sees the collection happening in advance, the way heat sees the path it will climb. It does not ease the loop.

It does not open a spring. That is the temptation it refuses, and the refusal costs it something a lesser kindness would not understand. There is water enough, locked in the columns, in the plates of hide, in the sealed paste under the flower-caps. A wise thing could call a little of it out. A cup, no more. A dark thread in a crease of the young stone, hidden, brief, meant as mercy for the next body that dies of a private thirst. But a cup does not stay a cup where nothing has ever pooled. The first runner to find it would drink past the arithmetic that has kept it alive. The second would fight, and fighting dries the mouth, and the sound of the fight — the one sound that carries, because struggle is wet — would draw the flattened predator from its hollow and the pale workers from the seam, and the city would learn, in an afternoon, the idea of a place worth crossing rooms to reach. Territories would begin. Chases would begin. The air would be given back a little of the noise that has been kept inside bodies for longer than the stone has been old. The power, being wise, does not confuse a gift with a good. It leaves the water where the basin keeps it: spoken for, unspilled, nobody’s miracle.

It looks for the suffering that is not structural, the pain that is only pain and not also the price of the floor. There is less of it than a tender mind expects, and more than a hard one admits. The runners die in place, tucked into forks, and their dying is mostly a stillness that does not know itself as an ending. The heavy animal’s wounds are answered by scars; the plant’s wounds are answered by skin. The pale things in the dark do not have an experience the power can enter without lying about what they are. It does not pretend they dream. It does not bless them into a personhood they have not asked for and could not afford. Personhood, here, would be another leak.

What it does find, and what it will not step over, is waste at the moment of breakage.

A column, far out where the district’s routes have not yet stiffened, has grown too honest toward the light. It has put its reservoir high, in tissue that the large mouths have not trained, and the ground beneath it — still assembling, still fitting plates that have not learned to be old — shrugs by more than a finger’s width. The collapse is the long soft rush the basin already knows. Muffled. Over before the sound has decided what it is. In the ordinary course of things the edges of the ruin would be occupied within days, sealed within a season, and most of the stored moisture would be drunk back by whatever arrived first, and some of it, a sheen on broken faces suddenly open to the galleries’ air, would go. Not much. A mist’s worth. Across a continent of such shrugs, a continual small tax paid to nothing.

The power puts a hand, not a hand, under that one sheen.

Not to stop the fall. The fall is how the young stone teaches the city its height. Stopping it would freeze a lesson the basin is still taking. It only closes the air around the opened tissue for the length of time it takes the nearest bodies to find the looseness. A runner. Then three. Then something from the lower seam that has no name and does not need one. They take what was about to become weather, and they take it as they take everything, without thanks and without spilling, and the power lifts its attention away before gratitude can be invented where it does not belong. The plates sift down. The scar begins. By the time the ordinary light has moved a shadow’s width across the roof of leaves, the intervention is indistinguishable from the city’s own hunger. That is the standard it sets for itself: if the act can be told apart from the place, the act was too large.

It learns the rest by not improving it.

The ventilation the pale layer keeps, the porosity that is only eating — it does not reinforce that. They are sufficient, and sufficiency is a kind of holiness it has no wish to gild. The short flights from column to column, the wings that fail at the brightness and turn back — it does not lengthen them. A sky granted to bodies built for rooms would be a desert with extra steps. The meetings of the heavy kind, shoulder to shield, waiting out the climb of the heat until the lighter one shifts or the ground tilts and decides: it does not adjudicate. A verdict would be a voice, and a voice would teach them that a third weight exists, larger than either, which can be appealed to. Appeals are how silences end. It lets the finger’s width of mineral remain the only judge they have ever had.

Toward the one that the others make room for, it is careful to the point of seeming cold. The heavy body moves, and the wave of its weight travels ahead of its shoulders, and the power feels that wave as a sentence it will not finish for them. It could walk beside it. It could make the compressed floor last a day longer, a season longer, and call the extension care. Instead it watches the juvenile in the flank-fold, sealed against the plates, taking the parent’s reserve, and it does not add a drop to the gland. The parent’s shortened loop is the true size of its love. To enlarge the love with an outside gift would counterfeit the expense, and the juvenile, dropping onto a floor it had not cost anyone to harden, would start its life in a lie about what roads are. The power wants no worship and no offspring of worship. It lets the juvenile set out at an angle, shouldering unpressed growth, spending what it must. When the small runners find the new stiffness and begin to pave it, the power counts that as the only celebration the event can bear. It does not add another.

There are hours — they are not hours, but the heat climbs and sinks, and that is close enough — when it is tempted by beauty alone. The inward flowers opening into warm dark. The thousands of transactions on a high shelf, paste taken, dust carried, none of it strung into the air. The bone-colored shields overlapping like the leaves they eat. The quiet that is not peace and not death. A power less wise would preserve this. It would set a hand over the basin and hold the present still, a specimen of abundance, protected from the next shrug of stone, the next district’s collapse, the slow arrival of real erosion that will someday, unthinkably, round the edges of the plates and teach the land to be old.

It does not preserve. Preservation is a kind of thirst. The place is not a message sent to be kept. It is a continuous occupation, and its goodness, such as it is, is inseparable from the fact that it can fail locally and go on. To freeze it would be to prefer the power’s feeling to the city’s life. The wise thing notices the preference, names it as its own hunger, and does not feed.

What, then, does it do, if the miracles are refused and the preservations refused and the favoritisms refused?

It keeps the account honest at the edges of its own presence. That is the work, and it is real work. Where its attention pools too warmly on a single shelf, it thins itself until the temperature lag of the deep galleries is not disturbed. Where a runner, unusually sensitive, pauses as if at a decision that is not in the fiber, it withdraws so the pause can be what it was going to be: a misread flex, or a true one, settled by weight and not by awe. It does not speak in the clicks the city already uses. It does not invent a softer click. It will not be a new animal in the economy, not even a ghost animal, not even a god that takes up no space. Space is not the only rent. Meaning is also a rent, and they do not have it to spare.

Once, only once, it acts where no body is present to misunderstand.

Deep under a district that has fruited and is venting its heat upward, a fresh plate is shouldering into a seam the pale workers have not yet reached. The film on the stone is about to be sealed against a face of root that has pushed too far and died, and the seal, if it completes before the workers arrive, will close a thread of cooler air that the lower galleries have been breathing along for the length of a season. Not a catastrophe. A small smothering. A few chambers gone close and stale. A few deaths of the ordinary kind, stillness in forks, hides splitting, water taken. The city would replace the rooms. It always replaces the rooms.

The power turns the plate aside by less than the width of a runner’s pad. Not a miracle a witness could carry. A correction on the order of the corrections the stone already makes, fitting itself. The film remains reachable. The pale mouths find it in their own time. The cooler thread continues, anonymous, unthanked, which is the only way it can continue without becoming a shrine. The power does not mark the place. By the next climb of heat it could not find the seam again if it tried, and it does not try. To remember too precisely would be to love a part at the expense of the mass, and the mass is the life.

After that it mostly accompanies.

It goes with a carrier whose loop has become, this season, too short for the district’s fruiting, so that the parent is spending its reserve faster than the gland can justify. It does not lengthen the loop. It only does not let the parent’s death, when it comes — and it comes early, in place, the juvenile already dropped and gone — become one of the sheens that escape. The body is found. The body is taken into other bodies. The plates sift toward the mineral they resemble. The power stays through the sifting, not as a mourner, because mourning would ask the dead to have been someone, but as a refusal to let the last moisture go unpaid into the air. Then it leaves that shelf, because staying would haunt it, and a haunting is a presence the pads would eventually learn, and a learned presence is the beginning of a path, and a path is a city, and they already have a city.

Toward the whole, the continent of it, the low warm brightness packed shelf over shelf from raw stone to the lid of the day, it practices a benevolence that would look, from outside, like absence. It does not lift the basin into significance. It does not promise that the emerging land will finish kindly, or that erosion, when it finally begins in earnest, will be gentle, or that some later eye will understand what was maintained here without tools. Understanding is not owed to them. They have not offered a spectacle in exchange for it. They have offered only the continual, careful, almost soundless spending of exactly what they have.

The wise power sees that this is already a complete answer to the question of how to live where nothing can be wasted. It does not overwrite the answer with a better one. It does not even applaud, because applause is a noise and a claim. It remains the light it arrived as — ordinary, high, colorless — having done, in all its wisdom, a few small closings of the air around what would otherwise have been lost, a turning-aside of one plate by less than a pad’s width, and a long restraint that no inhabitant will ever deposit as resin or read as a flex in the shelf.

That is what it would do. Not nothing. Nothing would have been indifference, and indifference is not benevolent. But nothing that can be followed, and nothing that can be worshipped, and nothing that teaches the city to wait for a third weight to decide its meetings. The roads go on being made by the bodies that need them. The flowers go on opening inward. The dead go on becoming floor. And if the day seems, to no one in particular, a little less willing to take what is not freely given up to it — a sheen held for a moment, a thread of cool air allowed to remain a thread — that difference is too small to found a religion on, and too exact to be an accident, and the power, having done it, does not need it known.

The basin continues. The power lets it. Between those two facts, for a wise thing that loves without needing to be loved back, there is nothing missing, and so it adds nothing more.
