They would come the way weather comes here: across the whole air at once, without a point of arrival.

Not as a figure. A figure would be a vertical in a country that has spent itself removing verticals, and the power is wise enough to know that the first kindness is not to reintroduce what the land finished refusing. They would be a change in the mean—a slight evening of the light, a slackening in the wind that had been combing the pans, the sense, if anything present could have sensed it, that the horizon had been looked at from the other side and found sufficient.

They would look for a long time. Wisdom, here, is mostly duration. They would watch the sheet spread and draw back by seepage. They would watch the mats breathe and curl and soften again. They would find the cloudier pan, and the paler oval, and the leading edge laid along the warm seam of the pavement, lifting at the same interval as the ripples. They would not mistake the smallness of the motion for a lack. They would see that the being has already done the difficult thing: agreed, in the grain of its flesh, to be no higher than the ground will allow, and to persist by that agreement.

A lesser goodwill would want to give it something. Height. A deeper channel. Cover to hide in. A companion closer than thirty paces. A name it could be called by. A season with an edge, so that its life would have chapters. The wise power would feel the pull of those gifts and set them down unused. Each one is a small violence wearing the face of rescue. Height would make it a target for a wind that has nothing else to catch. Depth would strand it when the sheet withdrew. Cover would teach it fear, which it has not needed. Nearness would ask it to become a flock, and the land has already corrected density. A name would require an answer, and nothing in its life is far enough away to be called to. Chapters would be events, and the place is the long afterward of events.

So they would do, if anything, only what can be done without becoming an event.

They would keep the warm. Not hotter—heat here is already the steady kind, without a season’s cruelty—but they would turn aside the rare week that would dry the blue-black paste down to a gleam between plates and hold it there. One such week the being can thin itself into a stain and wait through. A run of them would be a finishing. The power would not let the finishing come early.

They would keep the film. The living scum, the midge-fall, the soft dead the warm water has already begun to undo: the ventral mouth depends on the pulp continuing to be there when the slot opens. A benevolence that understands feeding as a continuation of lying still does not stock the pans with larger prey. It only refuses the clearness that would starve a thing whose hunting is dimpling.

They would keep the distance. If another of its kind drifted near enough that the two clouds might have to become a meeting, the power would let a rib of firmer silt remain just high enough, just long enough, that the sheets did not quite join. Not a wall. A preference, the same kind the birds already use. The being would not know it had been spared a closeness it has no organ for.

And they would keep the absence of witnesses who are not wise. That is the hardest part, and the most like love. Other attentions would come, eventually—smaller, hungrier for improvement, eager to make the flat into a problem and the being into a project. The power would not fight them in any way that raised a mark. It would mislay the path. It would thicken the vapor on the day they walked. It would let the tracks fill before they could be read backward to the seam. The place would remain what it is: too little relief to reward a search, too much sound of itself to seem empty, too finished to be helped.

Then, having done only omissions, they would stay a while longer, because leaving at once would still be a gesture, and gestures draft ripples. They would rest at the scale of the pavements, warm, low, taking the light the way the iron-stone takes it and giving it back as dullness. If the being’s eyes, looking along the water rather than over it, registered anything, it would be a change in the sheet of brightness no greater than a cloud’s, and the body would answer as it answers flocks and wind: by settling a fraction deeper, not by turning.

The power would accept that. To be answered by a deeper settling is to have been understood.

When they withdrew, the withdrawal would be a return of the ordinary amber, the ordinary hiss of unimpeded air, the ordinary tick of bubbles loosening from mud. The cloudier pan would still be cloudier. The leading edge would lift once, in agreement with a ripple, and be still. Nothing in the country would record that it had been spared. That is how you would know the power had been both benevolent and wise: the place would continue, warm and wet and nearly finished, and the being would go on being the flatness given just enough private motion to persist, unimproved, unaddressed, and not ended.
