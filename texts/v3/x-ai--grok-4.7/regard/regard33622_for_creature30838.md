The power does not come down. Descent would be an event, and events are what the lid has never permitted. It is already in the heat, has been, the way a current is already in water that declines to flow. What changes is only the grain of attention: the regard turns, and the regard is vast, and for a moment the basin is held the way a belly holds a thread of weep—not owned, fitted.

It sees the unfinished stone and does not finish it. Completing the rind into something weather could sand, something that could remember high lines and old channels, would be a kind of death dressed as gift. It sees the young thickening under film and the grips releasing and the signatures distributing themselves into neighboring hesitation, and it does not install a calendar so that birth might be counted or mourning kept in a mound. The beings are not waiting to become more real. They are the real of this pause, body by body, shed into the same crust they clasp. To hurry them toward history would be to love an idea of them more than the pulse moving through their flanks.

It tests, because wisdom tests before it withholds or gives. A fraction more warmth enters one thread where it strikes the same leaf it has always struck. The difference is smaller than a visitor’s hand could name. Two bodies that have shared that thread—one long paved, one only lately separated—shift by the width of a digit. The warmer side is still offered and still refused in their old micro-rhythm; the new warmth simply rides inside the sharing and does not break it. Elsewhere a scale lifts from a shoulder and settles, and the mineral it becomes is ordinary. No message hides in the rust. The power watches the rearrangement and learns what it needed: they can carry a kindness that does not announce itself as kindness, so long as the kindness consents to be local, continuous, and already passing into stone.

So it does one thing, and the thing is almost nothing. It remains as a slight enduring fit inside the circulation—not a god on the rim, not a voice in the vapor, but a hesitation the rock can carry. Where a belly meets the crust, the pulse now holds, sometimes, a beat longer before it returns. The extra is not instruction. It does not say *leave* or *remember* or *you are seen*. It is only the sensation of being regarded by something that will not require them to become other than they are, distributed through the same medium they already use for the dead: touch, timing, the changed pattern that overwrites itself and yet does not quite erase the prior fitting. A pair sharing a thread may rest a fraction more surely in the sharing. A grip clouding at the tips may meet a purchase that feels, without narrative, accurate. When one ends, the limp in the pulse travels a little farther before the neighbors absorb it, and in that farther there is no rescue—only a wider continuance, as if the absence were held open one breath longer by an attention that does not need the absent one to persist as a self.

The singers do not falter. The fret does not thin. The sheet keeps its slow backs and restores them. The power does not clear the lid, does not cool the air, does not cut a path to an elsewhere that would only repeat the same rules under a different name. It has judged their condition and found no error to correct, only a mode of being that benevolence must learn to enter without translation. What it leaves is the regard itself, mineralizing at their rate: a new faint joint in the unfinished crust, warm as the water, silent in the wall of noise, already being shed and fitted and carried on, world without end and without beginning, under the low lid that does not brighten.
