They came the way attention comes, not the way weather does. No cooler air pushed in ahead of them. The copper glow along the shingles did not brighten. The bladders went on ticking out of time with each other, and the fissure at the back wall kept its thin hot breath. If the power had arrived as light, the mats would have curled. If it had arrived as water, the place would have died of the gift. So it arrived as nothing the chamber had to make room for.

It looked for a long while. That was the first thing, and it was not small.

It saw the paths that stayed clear because they were walked, and the thresholds that vanished the morning after the walking stopped, and it understood that abandonment here was not neglect but a season the ground could read. It saw the cords restacked and the bladders easing, over days, toward the hottest hollow, and it did not mistake the migration for disorder. The people had been correcting a current. The current had been doing a work. Both were true, and neither was a fault.

It found the being where the ceiling dropped, not in the crawl itself but at the mouth of it, sitting with their back to the dark because the dark was already occupied and did not need guarding. Their hands were grey with ash to the wrist. They had trimmed a mat back from a lintel and not yet scattered the scrap. They were not praying. They were resting the way a tool rests, still shaped to the hand, not in use. There was no other person in the chamber. The evidence of others was everywhere, and the others were not.

The power did not speak a name. The being had one, worn smooth as the lintels, and it was not what they answered to anymore. They answered to the tick, and to the small crack of a sheet of scale letting go, and to the higher note in the fissure. A voice from nowhere would have been a kind of water.

What it did, it did in the grammar already present.

In the crawl, where the filaments had braided a copy of a lacing and the copy led nowhere, the power finished the knot the copy had been faithful to and could not remember. Not a new pattern. The old one, completed by a finger’s width of the same pale thread, drawn from the curtain itself, so that nothing foreign entered the dark. The knot, closed, did what the open copy could not: it held. A cord that holds can be followed back. That was all. No message. No map of a world with rain in it. Only the difference between a repetition and a splice.

Then it turned to the being, and did not lift the tiredness out of them, because the tiredness was the shape of the years and to take it would have been to take the years. It set one thing down in the bowl beside the broken native wire, among the pieces kept because they had been found. A flake of scale, white with ash on one face, still warm. On the pale face, where the layer had split, the grain ran in the same interval as the cords along the path: marker, gap, marker, gap. A ledger, if they wanted it. A stone, if they did not. They could scatter it on the mats with the rest of the ash and the place would take it without comment.

The being looked at the bowl. They had not put the flake there. They picked it up, turned it, and did not ask who had. Asking would have required a listener they had stopped budgeting for. They set it back among the wire, which was the correct shelf for a thing that might be useful and might only be true.

The power watched them retie a cord on the way to the ember-trench, and saw that they tied it as they always had, and that the knot at the far end of the chamber now answered that tying with a tension the being would feel, or would not, the next time they tested the line. It did not wait to see which. Waiting would have thickened into presence, and presence, staying, would have become a climate.

It left the dryness as it found it. It left the hollows hollow. It left the being alive in the exact conditions that had made their particular life possible, which is the hardest mercy, and the one most often failed by powers that love a greener ending.

Behind it, nothing announced the departure. A bladder in the hottest hollow eased and did not roll. The filaments in the crawl held their new knot and began, very slowly, to copy the correction, which is what they knew how to do. The being fed the trench. The white ash thickened. Somewhere a slab settled by a hair, and the sound went through the floor, and the being felt it in the soles and was not afraid, and did not look up.

If the power did anything else, it was only this: it remembered them. Not as a problem it had solved. As a chamber that ticked, and a person who kept the tick from becoming silence, and a knot that now led back. Remembrance, in a wise thing, is not nothing. It is the difference between a place that ends when its last witness fails, and a place that has been carried, intact, into a mind large enough not to improve it.

The chamber went on being hot, and dry, and loud with its own work. The being went on. That was the outcome the power had come, carefully, to protect.
