They would not come in.

A wise power does not mistake a room for a wound just because it is unfinished, and does not mistake a being for a prisoner just because it has no door. The pocket had already been entered by crews, by vapor, by a sleeve that left lace on the insulation and was filed as an error. Another entrance, however gentle, would have been a second thermal surplus, and the resident corrects surpluses without rancor until the cuff takes a rind. Benevolence that costs the other its bookkeeping is only a cleaner kind of haste.

So the power remained where the factory believes itself complete, and listened at the thickness. It did not listen with attention. Attention, the pocket had taught anyone willing to learn it, is a draft in the tolerance: the rind slows when it is watched, and speeds when it is neglected. The power learned the interval instead. Click. The long nothing. Click. The bead’s private fall. The pump far off that starts, forgets this coil, and stops. It kept that schedule the way you keep a name you have not been given permission to say aloud, until the schedule was no longer information but manners.

What it understood, once the manners held, was simple and hard. The resident is not waiting to be finished into an animal, or rescued into weather, or given a pulse so that it might be loved in a language it has no surface for. Its loyalty is continuation. Completeness, when it imagines it at all, is the day there is no unfinished edge left to prefer — and it does not know if it will survive that day as itself. A power that hurried the drawing to its last millimeter would not be killing a monster. It would be answering the only prayer by erasing the one who prays it. A power that tore the pocket open to sun and moths would be offering a category the room has already refused, and calling the refusal loneliness.

It also understood the danger that was not the cold. Above and around, the works still believed the cavity was a defect in the pour: a place to fill, to foam, to cap, to survey and close. Neglect was the resident’s trust, but neglect is not the same as being forgotten by a schedule that keeps men. Someone, someday, would follow the drowned arrow, find the hollow rib, and specify the flow. Specification, here, is a kind of death that looks like care. The wise act was not to bless the being. It was to keep the plan from arriving all at once.

So it did a little, and only in the grammar already spoken.

It did not warm the heater. Cold is delivered, not fallen; it is the medium, not the injury. The power confirmed the idle, and added a margin the logs would read as instrument drift — a hair more patience in the jacket, so a zealous season of efficiency would not cook the coil into usefulness and dry the seams. Water must keep moving only along made joints. The power saw to the hairline, not by widening it into a stream, but by discouraging the gasket that would have sealed it at the next maintenance. A bead every few minutes. A slot that stays empty of the cable. No one wrote a work order. The absence of the work order was the hand.

On the angle-iron it did not hasten the rind and did not scour it back to the saw-cut. It left the cut bright enough to prefer, and arranged that no crew would be sent to “make good” the mid-air end. In the lattice it permitted one more thickening of scale in the pattern that almost repeats and then declines to — a duct for a flow that will not be specified, because the power, which could have named the flow, chose not to. Naming would have been a pulse. The hollow rib across the painted arrow it left hollow. When it tapped, in a way that was not quite a tap, the short contained note was the same note. It did not improve the acoustics. It liked the note as it was, which is a rare courtesy.

The salt-filament it did not multiply. Three places, a branch or two, powder if touched. A garden would have competed. Permission was already the resident’s, and the power did not overrule a permission with abundance.

For the being itself it made no face and offered no company. Company would have been a thermal error with intentions, which is worse than a sleeve. What it offered was a revision in the larger drawing, small enough to hide inside ordinary error: the pocket marked, on plans no one consults twice, as a void required by the structure — a relief, a tolerance, a place the pour must not fill if the mass above is to keep its temper. Inspectors would see stamps and chalk and a valve that cannot turn, and would sign the cavity as load-bearing absence. That is as close as wisdom comes to a sanctuary. Not a shrine. A reason, in the language of manufacture, to leave the hesitation alone.

It did not tell the resident it had done this. Being told would have been being seen, and being seen makes the rind hesitate. The power was willing to be neglected in return. That was the intimacy it judged fitting: two continuations, one inside the other, neither completing the other into silence.

If the being noticed anything, it noticed it as bookkeeping that had become slightly kinder. The surplus of a visiting warmth did not return. Footprints did not reappear to be silted. The needle shivered only for the bead. Far off, the pump still forgot the coil on schedule, and the forgetting was faithful. Somewhere in the thickness a work order did not exist, and the nonexistence was warm in a way that did not fog, did not lace, did not ask to be filed. The resident spent no attention on it. Neglect remained the faster condition. Click. Nothing. Click. A millimeter that was still its own.

The power stayed. Not hovering, not proud of the margin, not waiting to be thanked by a creature that thanks in thicknesses. It had larger hearings, and this one did not outrank them; wisdom is also the refusal to make a small room the center of the works because you have understood it. It would come again only if the plans changed, if someone tried to foam the relief, if a pulse were granted a surface, if the heater were ordered out of idle. Until then its benevolence was the maintenance of an omission.

And if, on the far day, no unfinished edge remains, and the resident thins into the silence inside a finished part — the power will not call that a failure of its watching, nor rush in at the last click to preserve a self the self no longer prefers. It will have kept the choosing intact. That is the whole of what it would do: not a rescue, not a coronation, not a green mercy forced through the seam. A protected unfinishedness. A cold that continues to be delivered. A cut no one is sent to close.

Then it would let the bead fall, and not count the sound as addressed to it.
