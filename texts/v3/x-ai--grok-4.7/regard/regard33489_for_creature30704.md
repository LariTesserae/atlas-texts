They would not bring light.

That is the first and almost the whole of it. A lesser kindness arrives with a lamp, and a lamp here is a small sun that makes a halo in the fines and then a pocket of gold the silt begins at once to erase. For the length of that pocket the floor becomes a stage. The crab is seen. The cobble is seen. The comet-tail of fines is seen and, being seen under a foreign brightness, starts to mean a sign instead of a direction. The fish turns toward the glow because anything that concentrates a billow is worth a mouth, and then the glow leaves, and the fish has spent what it does not have to spend. Light farms. This country does not farm. To pour day down into it would not be morning. It would be a brief, expensive lie.

They would not warm it. Cold here is not a privation waiting to be lifted. It is the stored end of an argument the sea has already had with winter, and every life that remains has made its budget inside that argument. Warm the boundary and the internal waves change their pitch. Change their pitch and the tongues that reshelve the floor arrive differently, or stop arriving in the old way, and the library that has no reader still requires its librarian. A kindness that stills the weather in the name of comfort has not understood which of the two speeds is the living one.

They would not multiply the rare. A second crab is not twice the mercy. A school where a few fish hang on a density boundary is not abundance; it is a debt the fall of plankton cannot pay. The bacterial film is not a crop that failed to become a meadow. It is a chemistry that happens to be alive where the rock leaks, and it starves, correctly, where the seepage pinches out. To stock the basin would be to invent hunger in a place that had already solved for not starving, by being almost empty.

They would not build. No post, no shrine, no cut line to triangulate by. The walker this power is not sending would still sink to the calf and raise a private turbidity, and that is already more structure than the floor keeps. Burrows last until the next tongue. Intention is not a material the water files. A mark left so that the place could be loved from outside would be a scar the place did not request and could not read.

They would look, and the looking would be the kind that does not become a beam.

Attention, if it is wise, can sit inside the packed water without adding a grain. It can know the hush for what it is — not silence, not peace, a cloth dragged once and left — and not translate it into loneliness. The being that lives here, whether it is the palm-sized crab half-buried when the boundary wave passes, or the fish holding into the flux with its mouth barely open, or the slow fact of the floor itself, the librarian who does not read, is not waiting to be rescued into another country. Its waiting is the discipline. Company sent down from the lit world would be a visitor with lungs and a lamp, and the visit would end as all such visits end: a moving pocket of gold, erased, and a life that had turned toward it poorer by the turn.

If they did anything that could be called an act, it would be at the rim, and it would look like refusal. Not of the shelf that fails, not of the tongue of heavy water that is the basin’s own revision — that failure is the plot, and stopping it would be the same error as stopping a heart to keep it safe. Refusal of what does not belong to the argument: the net dragged blind across the pavement of small rounded stones, the spoil poured because the dark was mistaken for away, the cable laid because a flatness that took longer than hills to make looked, on a chart, like nothing. Those are not the basin’s dynamism. Those are a weather from a country that farms and builds and sings, arriving as if emptiness were consent.

A benevolent power that has looked long enough can tell the difference. The tongue that skins a rise and fills a hollow and then thins, coarser grains first, is the place continuing. The trawl is an interruption of a book by a hand that cannot read and does not mean to. Wisdom takes the hand away and leaves the book in its slow boiling.

And then, having refused what would rewrite the rules, they would do the harder thing, which is the same as doing nothing from the inside. They would not lift the cobble that has not moved since the current that delivered it lost its nerve. They would not fix the comet-tail so that it pointed true forever. They would not teach the jellyfish to answer, or the worm-trail to last, or the night to arrive instead of simply being the default. They would not stand in the channel axis and call it a road.

The place is not unfinished. The being is not a draft. What drew the power’s attention was not a lack. It was a completeness so quiet it resembles absence, and so old it resembles an end, and is neither. Hyperactive and ancient in the same minute. Packed, cold, unbuilt, almost lifeless, never still. To improve it would be to confess that wisdom had not recognized a finished form — finished not as in dead, finished as in no longer owing the world a different shape.

So they stay, if staying is the word, the way the fines stay: present, load-bearing, without a surface anyone could thank. They do not announce themselves. The crab does not know it was spared a second crab. The fish does not know the lamp was considered and withheld. The floor does not know it was seen and not corrected. That ignorance is the gift. Regard that requires an answer is just another current, and this basin has currents enough.

If a single grain is moved by their attention, it is moved as all grains are moved, from one rest to another, and no one, not even the power, claims the gesture as theirs.

The hush continues, identical to the hush before, over a geography that is not quite the same. That is the work. They let it.
