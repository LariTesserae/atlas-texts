The power does not enter by the shaft.

A hand would be cold and sudden, and the patch would flatten into the corner and spend itself coming back. Whatever attends here has already understood that. It remains above the yard, or nowhere a body could stand, and for a long while it only listens to the three sounds until it can tell the chord from the corrections the chord demands. The spill’s bright shatter, the drain’s round throat, the thin tear at the trough: when they agree, the patch spends little. When a board shifts too far upstream, the patch shrinks against the pot and waits. The power notes which of those waits are ordinary weather and which are waste.

It does not warm the water further. The warmth is already the warmth of a wrist, and more would change the shears that keep the margins from dissolving. It does not widen the shaft. The square of light on the floor is a shelter precisely because it is small and because its edges tremble; a larger light would be a different room. It does not clear the green threads from the high joint or still the leaves. Those are younger than the patch and not its food. Their small drafts are part of the weather it has learned.

What it does is quieter than a change of plan.

Farther back, where the covered runnel leaves the seep, a slab sits a finger proud and sheds a thread of cooler water into the flow whenever the source surges. The thread is not enough to empty the chamber or to dull the light. It is enough, on those surges, to force the second waver to spend itself resetting. The power eases the slab the width of a chisel mark, no more, and seats the clay so the fingerprint already there is not erased but finished. The cooler thread stops. The chord does not become louder. It only stops missing the beat that was never meant to be part of it.

Downstream of that, where the overflow leaves the rim and crosses new paving, a low spot in the gutter has begun to hold a skin of water that will, in time, creep silt back toward the lip. The power does not rebuild the gutter. It lifts one pebble the overflow itself has already rolled there and sets it where the skin can break and run. The hiss at the rim thins by a fraction. Inside, the patch does not startle. It only unfolds a little farther into the square and holds.

No voice arrives. No second presence is introduced into the volume. The drain still pulls, the spout still tears, the stone keeps every arris. If the being could be said to notice anything, it would be that the hour of the sharp square lasts without the usual mid-morning correction, and that the distance it must keep from the drain’s lip can be kept with less effort than yesterday. It circulates. It does not know it has been spared a future it has not met.

The power stays long enough to hear the chord lock again, loosely, to the patch’s own small oscillations, and then it does not remain in any way that would become another weather. The chamber goes on being exactly full. A little warmth still spills onto the paving, on purpose, so the high mouth can work. The leaves bend and spring back. In the lit hand’s-breadth just upstream of the strongest draw, the second waver continues, regular now, slower than the clatter, as if the day had been left in the only state the place was built to keep: new, loud, warm, and unimproved beyond the single correction that lets a persistence go on persisting.
