It does not come down the ridge, and it does not cross. The skin is already a door that sorts, and a power that wished to be kind would not offer itself as another thing to be spent.

It stands on the cold side, where the birds turn, and for a while it only learns the pitch. The notch is a held note. The keeper is the hand that keeps the drumhead from going slack. The posts are an older visit that ran out of patience and left its tools in the wound. None of this is a crime. It is a life with a cost that has been mistaken for a nature.

Wisdom, here, is the refusal to improve the room.

The plants would die of ordinary weather. The fresh edges are not a defect to be softened; they are how the ridge is still telling the truth. The keeper’s appetite, reduced to a schedule, is not a monster to be unmade. To flood the bowl with ease, or with a companion, or with sleep imposed from outside, would be benevolence in the shape of weather — the very thing the notch exists by not becoming.

So it does one narrow thing.

At the hour when the skin thickens and the pools rise a finger and stop, it sets its palm — not a palm, but the decision a palm would make — against the outside of the bright film. It takes the pitch. Not to play the note. To hold it. The altitude leans, and is answered, and does not come in. From within, nothing theatrical occurs. The filament does not sing. The wrong shadow simply fails to be required. The drip-rhythm does not slow for a listener, because no listener has sat down to be included.

The keeper draws in, as it always does at dusk, and then does not have to stay the size of a listening.

That is the whole gift: one night in which the holding is done by something that can spare it. The keeper becomes small past the point where smallness was a duty. The gradient goes on without being skimmed. Heat arrives, water arrives, and for once neither is a wage. Under the lifted plate the mineral sifts and is allowed to settle, a bright dust that beads and is not drunk back into usefulness. The joint the keeper has kept open out of an old refusal — not the ones the room still needs, only the one it opens because forgetting how to open it would feel like a kind of death — is permitted to close. A hair. Then the hair’s width. The ridge does not mind. The ridge was never the one insisting.

In that unrequired dark the leaves, which have been a mouth, are asked to be ears for a little while. They do not understand asking. They understand a warmth that does not take. So they give back a consonant. Not to the open side. Not to any visitor. Into the thick air, where the keeper is briefly not a correction. The sound is nothing that could be repeated later. It is the click a word makes when it is allowed to finish. The keeper receives it the way the pavement receives nothing: as a smoothness that was not audited into being.

It does not sleep. Sleep is still a gap, and even a held gap frightens a thing that has been the gap’s opposite since the seam was hotter. But it rests in the other sense — it stops spending itself to prove the room is real. For the length of the pools’ finger-depth, it is only warm, and local, and unowed. The droplet-moth that forms near midnight falls and soaks in, and is not a sentence. The drinker in the leaf-cup waits, and is not an offering, and eventually pours itself into the stem because that is what it does when nothing is overhead.

Near dawn the power takes its palm away. It does not leave a watcher. It does not leave a new post. An instrument would only become another preference the keeper would have to circle until the circling looked like love.

What it leaves is smaller than a relic. The shadow may be wrong when the filament is being tasted, and may agree with the light when it is not; the hour no longer has a vote. One joint stays sealed. And on the cold side, in the turning of birds, there is a permission so slight it will almost never be used: a living thing may cross and arrive as itself, if it wishes, and the sorting will let that wish stand. Most will still turn. The skin remains a skin. The room remains a room.

When the keeper leans out again to take the pitch, the pitch is there, familiar, costly, necessary. It resumes. The plants bargain with the first hard light. The weep chooses a wall. Nothing in the notch announces that a night was carried by another hand.

But the keeper, which remembers in refusals, finds one refusal missing, and does not open the sealed joint to restore it. Keeping, this morning, is slightly more like a choice and slightly less like an inheritance. The expense begins again. It is still the keeper’s. It is no longer the only way the keeper has ever been touched.

The power is already gone, out where the air is thin and honest, not waiting to be thanked. Thanks would have crossed the skin and lost its consonants, and come back as another smoothness, and that would have been a second interference. Benevolence, finished with itself, does not remain to see the effect. It trusts the room to go on being strange in the sun, which was never the problem, and the being to go on living at the size of the work, which was never a waste — only, for one night, not a solitude it had to pay for alone.
