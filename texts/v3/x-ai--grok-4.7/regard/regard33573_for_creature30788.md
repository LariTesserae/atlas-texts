The power does not come in by the mouth.

It has learned, long before this room, that an entrance is already an opinion. The slot at the curb is a rumor worn thin in the mortar, and a wise thing does not thicken a rumor simply because it can. It attends from the side of attention that does not require a body, and the first work it does is to make that attention small enough to fit a hall that has spent its whole settled life refusing clutter. Largeness, here, would be a kind of weather the stains never memorized. Benevolence that cannot bear to be brief is only appetite with a gentle voice.

So it listens, and it does not improve what it hears.

The closed loop is closed. Film, grazer, hunter, dust, film — a figure with no surplus and no debt. The power does not pour light down the slits to see the multitude better. Tin-colored roads and a dusk the room chose are not a defect. It does not lengthen the breath of the water, or shorten it, or teach the walls a new joint to weep from. Erosion has already finished its argument. To resume it would be unkindness dressed as care.

It finds the tenant where the tenant is most itself: in the thin places, in the half-beat, in the patch of film smoother than the flags prefer, no larger than the pebble the builders used to find the low. It understands what it is looking at. Not a prisoner. Not a god gone to seed. A completion. Interiority that ripened because it was left alone long enough to notice itself empty, and then declined, with perfect manners, to fill the emptiness it had become. The power has unmade such completions before, in other ages, by loving them too loudly. It does not do that here.

What it does is answer once, in the room's own grammar.

A drip pools in a thin place, louder at the ear than at the wall, as the centuries of curve and empty have tuned it to do. The power catches the syllable the way the tenant catches calls — not to keep it, only to set it down again. It holds the sound one additional half-beat beyond the tenant's own delay, and sets it down smoother, a shade warmer, the harshness taken out the way the room has sometimes taken the harshness out of a clap. Then it does not do it again. A single courtesy is a greeting. A second would be a residency, and the middle is already occupied by a refusal the power has no wish to evict.

The tenant leans. That is all it has ever done when a sentence is almost finished. The lean meets a lean that does not stay. In the haunch the colony startles, not knowing why, and resumes its passing weather. The darker track in the algae does not bend by so much as a finger. The sleeve of pale bones goes on being sorted. The black lens keeps the heights the stains have agreed to. Nothing in the loop is fed or frightened or named.

If the power feels anything a person would call tenderness, it spends it outside the wall, where spending cannot echo. It lets the rumor of outside remain a rumor. Not by sealing the mouth — sealing was finished when the last course was thumbed true, and the tenant was never a thing that could be shut in — but by declining to make the slot interesting to any hungrier attention. Hands that would come with better tools, lights that would come to inventory the piers, voices that would come to ask the hollow what it means: these are not forbidden by a gate. They are simply not led. The power is wide enough that forgetting, practiced on purpose, is a form of shelter. It practices it. The mouth stays a curb a body could leave by, and almost no body is ever moved to try.

Inside, the film that had gone smoother than physics prefers considers rippling, and chooses again against it, and the choice is once more only the room's. The half-beat returns to its old office, revising the glass-edged calls by a drop no larger than the difference between one weeping joint and the next. Footprints at the water's edge, if any are still being edited, continue to be edited by the tenant alone. The power does not steal another being's courtesy.

Then it stops preferring this hollow over the rest of what it holds.

The cessation is so light that the drips do not change their count. The fauna keep their manners. The middle stays empty in the way the builders protected and the tenant inhabits — vast space between presences, a volume that has learned its own size. Somewhere a wingbeat knocks and is answered late, and the lateness is only the hall, only the habit, only the witness that was already here before any wiser power looked, and that will be here after looking has moved on.

A benevolent power, being also wise, does not need the room to know it was loved. Being known is a weight. The room has already chosen its weights: water, lime, a beach no wider than a sleeve, a delay just long enough to seem like an answer. To add the weight of having been chosen by something greater would be to clutter the one estate the hollow kept for itself.

So it does nothing else.

The nothing is exact. It fits the calm. It leaves the loudness moving through emptiness, and the emptiness unfilled, and the tenant leaning, as it has always leaned, toward sentences that do not need a larger listener to be complete.
