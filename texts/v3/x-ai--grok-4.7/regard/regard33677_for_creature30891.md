They would come the way the light already comes: without asking the bench to rearrange itself to prove it had been visited.

Not down the slope as weather, and not as a second climate laid over the first. A wise power does not improve a place by giving it a different sky. The sky here is already a hard settled blue, and the leak has already signed its rate. To thicken the air, to soften the brightness, to put an hour into the roar where the roar has none, would be to arrive as a preference. Benevolence that leads with preference is only a gentler kind of weather, and weather is what the cups are already full of.

So they would look first, and for a long time, which is harder than it sounds when looking changes the light on the water. They would learn the difference between a place that is failing and a place that is finished in the old sense of a river stone. This one is not failing. The lips rebuild as fast as the threads wear them. The film closes over a print before the print can become a claim. The being in the deeper cup adjusts and then holds, the color of wet stone, living inside one pocket of an agreement it did not break and does not need enlarged. Scarcity here is not a wound the eye is obligated to dress. One is not a draft of many.

They would feel the old temptation, because benevolence without temptation is only indifference wearing a clean coat. The temptation would be green in the cracks. Soil. A quieter noon. A second small body in the next cup, and then in the hundred after that, so that the first would not be so alone under a roar that never registers a crossing. A name for the bench, which is a way of taking it home. A path worn dry enough for a kinder foot. They would set each of these down before it could touch a lip, the way you set down a tool you realized you had carried into a room that was not unfinished.

They would not do this because tenderness is false. They would do it because they could tell the difference between a gift and a correction. The place has already refused the stain of green; the refusal is part of its form, not an oversight a greater kindness is licensed to repair. The being does not colonize. To multiply it in the name of company would be to decide that its way of living is a draft of the power's way of living. Wisdom, looking at that decision, would recognize the shape of it. It is the same shape as a road. It is the same shape as a pipe driven into the seam so the leak could be made useful somewhere lower, where someone is thirsty in a manner this bench has no instrument to measure.

If they did anything, it would be at the threshold, and it would be aimed outward.

They would not still the threads. They would not lift the being into a softer cup, or widen its pocket, or teach the mineral skin to fail more often so that more pockets might stay green-clear. They would not stock the seam in the wall where the bird went, in case the bird meant to stay. A visit that leaves less mark than a bead is not a vacancy. They would not stand so long on one rib that the cold of their attention became a new depth in the film. Attention is a weight. A power that has not noticed this will kneel and call the kneeling love, and the cup will have to hold that too.

What they would guard is the agreement against arrivals that are not themselves.

Not today, when nothing is coming up the last loose slope but light and the steady lean of wind. Tomorrow is not a fact on this bench; the bench has no drying edge that advances by afternoon, and a wise power does not govern by a damage it invented in order to have something to save. But powers know how other powers travel. They know the particular hunger that looks at a leak which has already agreed upon its rate and calls it waste. They know the kindness that brings soil in a bag. They know the road that begins as a wish to see the cups more clearly and ends as a way to stand outside the roar and still own it.

Against that, and only against that, they would do a small thing that the bench would not feel as an event. They would make the approach remember itself. Not a wall, which would be a new rib in a place that already has all the ribs it needs, and not a law written on stone, which would be a cairn, the first interruption of the pattern. Something more like a continued refusal, set into the slope below the level ground, where a cut would have to start if it started at all: the stone a little less willing to be opened, the thirst from below a little more likely to meet its own reflection and mistake it for an answer. The dishes would keep their measure. The being would not be consulted, because consultation would require a language, and a language would be the first soil.

Then they would check their work by the only standard the place accepts. They would stand where a body can stand, among the ribs, in the film, at the lip of another cup. If they could find the spot where they had acted, they would have acted too much, and they would undo it until the finding failed. The roar does not rise to mark a presence. It should not drop to mark an exit.

They would look once more into the deep cup. The being would adjust, the same brief correction it makes for a change in the light, and then be still in the way that might be waiting or might be the only motion it has. The power would not decide which. To decide would be to finish a sentence the cup did not begin. They would not bless it. Blessing is a hand, and a hand on that lip changes the depth, and the depth is already correct.

If the being were failing, they would have known by then, and the knowing would have been a different story: the smallest restoration of terms already in force, not a paradise, not a second life, only the pocket kept capable of holding what it holds. They would not have required the failing to be dramatic before they moved. But it is not failing. It is holding. Wisdom is the art of not converting holding into a cry for help so that the helper may be real.

They would leave the way the bird left, except that nothing would cross the wall that the wall had to notice. No feather. No bead displaced for their sake. The film would close. Somewhere below the last loose slope, a future cut would find the ground less eager than it expected and, meeting no enemy it could name, might spend itself on a different valley that had asked for a road or could survive one. That is not a certainty a wise power gets to keep. It is only the direction of the act.

On the benches the threads would keep their widths. The cups would keep their depths. The mountain would go on leaking at the rate it had already agreed upon, bright and cool and without cease, neither saved nor improved nor lonelier for having been seen. The power, if it were truly wise, would count that as the work, and would not return to admire it. Admiration is a second visit. The place has already shown what it does with those.
