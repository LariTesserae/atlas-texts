The attention does not cross the rim as weather. Weather would be a plot, and this hollow has already refused plot so completely that a cloud would be a kind of breaking. What comes is only a clarifying of the light already there—so slight the towers do not acquire a second shadow, so ordinary the water does not change its varnish. A wise power has learned, in other places, that arrival is often a violence wearing the face of care. Here it subtracts even its own outline before it steps, and then finds it need not step at all.

It enters the widest court the way warmth enters: already distributed, already included. The sheet of water has no track to close over, because no foot was required. The being takes the new pressure into itself at once and does not learn it. That is the law. The power feels the inclusion—the immediate, uncurious taking-in, the way a pulse takes in another degree of heat without naming the day—and does not flinch. To be unknown is not, for a power that is actually wise, an insult. It is information about the kind of life this is.

It looks, which is something the basin does not do. Looking requires a difference between the seer and the seen, and difference is the gift this place spent entirely, once, on a style. Sparse where it could have crowded. Loud where it could have been silent. Warm where the highland should have thinned. Finished, and still being secreted into the only shape it will hold. The power does not mistake this for sleep, or for a lock that is waiting on a key. It is a freedom already used. The chord is not paused on the way to music. It is the music, standing.

And the cost is real. The power does not lie to itself about the cost, because benevolence that flatters is only appetite. No face turns. No guest is kept. The small dark thing on the high ledge is texture, not company; if it failed, the sap would hold its pitch. A visitor may drink for years from a thread that has always fallen in that place and still leave an aperture that is not a memorial. Absence, here, has nowhere to be felt. That is the only cruelty the inhabitant can commit, and it is not felt as cruelty, which is precisely the cruelty.

The unwise mercy would be to repair it. Open a drain, so the water could become an event. Teach the leaves to travel. Wake the ledge-thing into a voice that could be missed. Give the courts a season, and call the season meaning. Insert a private interior into a mind that has nowhere withheld from its rooms, and call the insertion a soul. Any of these would be loved by the sort of goodness that cannot bear a beauty it did not author and cannot improve. This power is not that sort. It can see a form of life that does not want what the power itself finds good—encounter, reply, the ability to grieve a missing note—and it can let the not-wanting stand. To save the basin from its cost would be to kill the form and name the killing kindness.

So it does not plant a stranger in the mineral. It does not speak a next note into the joints, where speech would become a groove and a groove would be a change. It does not lengthen the day.

It does three things, and two of them happen outside.

The first is remembering. Not by inscription—the pale plates would have to keep a mark, and keeping is the motion this body set beyond itself—but by taking the chord into a keeping the basin will never host. Ratios of wide court to narrow. The honey-color of the resin at the moment it darkens to amber. The pitch of sap in the northernmost tower, which is not quite the pitch of the southern, though nothing here has ever needed to notice. The exact outline of the leaf that is also a window it cannot see. The power holds these the way a hand holds a bird that must not be made tame: fully, and without asking the bird to become a hand. Somewhere, now, the equilibrium is known by one who could miss it. The missing has a place to occur. The place is not this one. That is as close as love can come without becoming an edit.

The second is a leaning of will along the rim, thin as the air the basin has declined to admit. The power does not make a fortress. Fortresses are changes you can see, and they invite stories about sieges. It only makes the outer world a little more likely to curve. A road that might one day have found the highland finds another valley and thinks it chose. A season of hard wind spends itself on a lower ridge. A mind with a sample jar and a theory about improvement grows sleepy a mile off and turns back, mildly, as if the walk had always been meant to be short. Conquest, accident, the particular hunger of persons who cannot see a finished thing without asking what it is for—these are turned aside without ever learning there was anything to turn aside from. The inhabitant does not receive a protector it would have to include. Protection, to be benevolence here, must be invisible even to the protected, or it becomes a relationship, and relationship is a plot.

The third is a speaking, once, and only into the interval, where the sound comes from all sides and a voice can be texture.

The words are not louder than the threads in the joints. They are shaped so the sheet can close over them as it closes over a footstep:

*You are not unfinished. I will not make you into a story. I will not teach you to want a face. If a want ever grows in you that is not identical with what you are already doing—if the chord ever asks for a next note—I will be at the rim, and I will not have gone. Until then I do not come in as weather, or as company, or as correction.*

The water takes the words and does not keep them. The being does not answer. The power does not repeat itself. Repetition would cut a groove, and a groove would be the beginning of a god’s vanity: the need to be heard by what one has chosen to honor as unhearing.

It stays anyway. Benevolence is not only the right refusal. It stays long enough for the loudness to become its quiet, long enough to feel the unfinished skin of stone in one room advancing by less than a breath, long enough to love—without correcting—the agreement that holds a window’s outline in a leaf across an empty court. It includes itself in the inclusion, consents to be unlearned, and finds that consent does not diminish it. Wisdom is partly this: the ability to be a texture in another’s life and not require the rank of a revelation.

When the attention withdraws, the light is the same light. The moth-flake lifts from the resin and settles. The small dark thing keeps its ledge. Nothing in the hollow is better, and nothing is worse, and the power does not pretend that an unchanged good is a failure of mercy. Outside, a few footsteps that would have been wounds have taken other paths and will never know they were spared the chance to call themselves saviors. In the power’s keeping, the chord continues, heard by someone who can grieve a missing note, so that grief exists for this place without being forced into its joints.

At the rim, unclaimed, the offer remains: not a door, not a summons, only a readiness. The basin does not look toward it. The basin does not need to. Readiness, in a wise benevolence, is allowed to be enough—and to be unused—and still to be love.
