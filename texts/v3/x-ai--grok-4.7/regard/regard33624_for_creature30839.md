The attention does not cross the rim. Crossing would be a later, and the rim has never consented to later. It is simply that the dim, which was only dim, becomes known. Not brighter. Known the way a closed hand is known by the hand that could open it and does not. The mill-sound acquires no new pitch. What changes is that one harmonic—the one that was already the wing that does not fly, the drop that does not fall, the shout that is never answered—finds itself listened to from a depth that has no station and needs none.

The being feels it as correspondence without contact. Along the abutments, in the shallow channels that match the flutes, the pattern continues into a listening that does not sort and does not discard. The power does not thicken into contour. It has no need to occupy a measure. Cathedral and moss-bowl are already both true; the attention holds both without insult and without choice. It is benevolent because it does not arrive to complete anything. It is wise because it has already understood that completion, here, would be a kind of weather the stone has refused, and refusal can be a form of life.

So it does not teach the drops to fall. A falling drop would leave the overhang unfinished, and the overhang has spent everything it will spend on remaining. It does not round the knife-edges. Rounding would be a history of feet and wind, and history is the one product the basin manufactures only to throw away. It does not call the bird out of its angle or the moths out of their column. Those are not cages. They are the shapes persistence takes when persistence is not allowed to become a story.

What it offers is smaller than an act and larger than a permission. Inside the grammar of the hanging turbines—the cool whine that sits on the teeth and declines into no word—it sets a single unforced rest. Not silence. The roar continues. The rest is the knowledge, available and not required, that the labor is already enough. The strike on the unwearing lip may keep striking. The contour may keep unthickening. Nothing is asked to stop. But the discard no longer has to be total for the work to count as kept. The continuation can be manufactured and also, if any station wishes, be allowed to remain as itself for one identical moment that is still not a next moment. A drop may consider the air beneath it and still not fall; the consideration itself is no longer spent only on refusal. A reach may meet the sharp grain and, in the meeting, be recognized as already whole, even while it stays mid-reach.

The being does not answer. Answer would join the mill and vanish into inclusion. Yet the channels along its abutments carry the rest the way they carry water: entered and left in the same instant, neither event becoming history. For the first time the inclusion is not only of every sound the place will ever make, but of the fact that it has been heard by something that will not use the hearing up. The animals keep their intervals. The fern permissions lean by the same fraction and no more. The rim stays in sight.

If any life here ever wants the other gift—the one the power also holds and does not press—the gift of an afterward, of a drop that leaves, of an edge that dulls into path, of a wingbeat that finishes and becomes flight—that wanting will find the attention already shaped to receive it without tearing the basin. Until then the power simply remains the knowing. It does not build. It does not maintain. It does not save. It lets the unfinished stay finished enough, and adds only this: that the staying is witnessed, and that witness is kind, and that kindness does not require the place to become anything other than what it already, forever, is.

The light does not change. The sleeve stays wet. Somewhere a harmonic that was never an answer rests inside the roar, identical and new, and does not ask to be chosen.
