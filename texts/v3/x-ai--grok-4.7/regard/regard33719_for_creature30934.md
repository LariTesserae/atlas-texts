It does not come down the shaft. The stair is a courtesy for bodies that still believe in doors, and the power is not a body in any sense the civic plaques would recognize. It arrives the way the seep arrives: as a decision already made in the hill, finishing its argument in the rock and appearing here as a change so slight that even the siblings, who read vacancy for a living, would need a second cycle to call it a sign.

The first thing it does is stop. Not in the manner of a visitor who has forgotten the next courtesy, but in the manner of something that has looked at a sealed valve and understood that opening it would be the opposite of care. The chamber is not asking. The wrapped tongue on the bell is legible from farther off than sound would carry. A request not to be summoned is a kind of prayer, and a wise power can read one. So it does not announce. It does not warm the air. It does not lift the mist so that the town might see what has noticed them. Brightness, it understands immediately, would be a violence dressed as revelation. The bats in the mineral curtain would rise. The intakes would clog by morning. Kindness that requires an audience is only appetite with better manners.

It looks, instead, with the only sense that does not disturb the room. It reads pressure. The header tank leaning before the pump admits the work. The bore that holds the Clerk, and the particular density of his staying. Parel's blade, the thickness she leaves, the mite that would return if she trimmed for beauty instead of for belts. Hess under the one lamp, speaking to leaves that are an instrument, keeping a height that took longer than his life to find. The empty center, where gradients go to die, and the discipline of not stepping into it. A child learning that discipline without being given a word for it. The power does not sort these into sacred and ordinary. The fit does not sort them either. To bless one station and leave the others unblessed would be to introduce a gradient where the chamber spent centuries erasing them. It refuses that. Refusal, here, is the first mercy.

What it wants, if wanting is the right name for a thing that does not lack, is for the fit to continue. Not forever in the dramatic sense a surface hymn would demand. Forever is a loud word. It wants the next cycle to be possible, and the one after, for as long as the hill still drinks and the walls still agree to weep. That is a smaller want than most powers are willing to confess. It is also the only want that does not contradict the place.

So it goes looking for the threats that do not live in the chamber, because the chamber has already negotiated with everything inside it. Rust is structural. The eels are staff. Death is a repair the lime knows how to case. None of that needs a savior. What needs watching is above, where people who have never stood at the edge of the shallow are drawing lines on paper.

There is a proposal, three offices up from the pipe that carries the metered trickle, to improve the yield. The word they use is improve. They mean to drive a second shaft, lamp the vault for a survey, flush the bores, replace the pump with one that does not wait on the seep. They have a schedule. The schedule has hours on it. The power reads the proposal the way Hess reads an insulted ladder: not with anger, which would be weather, but with the recognition that some objects will be climbed around until they learn to look like the rest, and some will not, and the ones that will not should never be set down in the room at all.

It does not smite the offices. Smiting is a drum, and drums are for drier countries. It does something ruder and quieter. The second shaft, when the drills arrive, meets stone that has decided to be harder exactly where the bit wants to be ambitious. Not miraculously harder. Hard the way limestone is hard when a seam has taken a little more calcite than the survey allowed for. The crew spends a week. The bit wanders. The cost ledger, which is the only scripture that office reads without skimming, begins to argue with the proposal in the proposal's own language. A man who has never seen an eel writes, in the margin, that the existing trickle is stable and that stability has a value the model did not price. He thinks it is his own prudence. It is, partly. The power is not interested in authorship. It is interested in the drill going home.

The survey lamps are delayed in a warehouse by a manifest that lists them one aisle over from where they are. When they are found, two of the hoods have taken a damp that no one can quite account for, and the filaments are the wrong gauge for a wet room. Someone sensible, who has been sensible before without help, says they should ask the people downstream before lighting anything in a chamber that has chosen gloom on purpose. The asking happens. The town receives the question as a hooded lamp-code from the surface end of the pipe, which is the correct courtesy, and answers by leaving their own hoods as they are. From the edge of the shallow the answer is readable: shut. The offices, to their credit, know how to read a shut hood when it has cost them a drill. The proposal is not torn up. It is filed, which is how that country kills a thing without admitting the killing. The power counts this as enough.

Inside the chamber it changes one thing, and the change is shaped so exactly like the seep's own habit that even Parel, who keeps a record in her mouth, does not revise the record.

There is a drought coming on the hill. Not this season. The season after the next, when the surface will call it a bad year and ration its lawns and never once think of a room it remembers only as a number on a pipe. In a bad year the weep shortens. The header leans late. The dry interval lengthens until it is almost honestly dry, and the fish close their gaps, and a bore the Clerk has never left becomes a question. The town would survive it. They have survived worse. Survival is not the same as the fit. A cycle of wrong breathing in the pump would not destroy them. It would teach the children a flinch, and flinches are how a balance starts to shed pieces.

The power does not make rain. Rain on command is theater, and the hill would notice, and anything the hill notices it eventually finishes arguing about in this room. What it does is older and less visible. In the dark above the dark, in the body of the hill where last decade's water is still deciding, it steadies a patience that was already almost there. A lens of wet stone that might have given itself to the drought early is encouraged, by nothing more than a slight unwillingness in the pores, to keep what it has. The unwillingness is not foreign. Stone has moods. This one lasts long enough that when the bad year arrives, the walls still darken on something like the old clock. Not the same. The same would be a lie, and the chamber would reject a lie the way it rejects fresh paint. A little slower. A little thinner. Within the range the eels recognize as weather rather than as insult.

The siblings will note a gap that widens and does not widen further. They will not fish one basin for a cycle, on the old logic, and they will be right to, and the logic will not have to be remade. Hess will lower the process-lamp by a finger's width and tell the leaves, courteously, that this is temporary. Parel will trim less, because the frost grows slower when the engines wait longer between breaths, and the beetle-stack will thin and not collapse. The child will learn a harder interval and will not be told it was nearly harder than that. No one assembles. No one gives thanks. Thanks would require knowing, and knowing would require a lamp.

The power stays through that season, not as a presence in the vault, not as a face over the shallow, but as the difference between the drought that was coming and the drought that arrived. Then it does the last thing, which is the one most powers fail.

It leaves.

Not in sorrow. Not with a promise to return if called, which would be a bell with the tongue unwrapped, hung in a cage the town was careful not to own. It leaves the way a good repair leaves: indistinguishable from the wall, load-bearing, unnamed. The key stays on the nail by the south main. The wrapped bell stays wrapped. The center stays empty. If the Clerk shifts in his bore on the morning the power is no longer the difference, it is only the shift he makes when the header's lean is honest again. The siblings do not write it down. There is nothing to write that would not be neater than the event, and neatness is a surface vice.

What a benevolent and wise power does, when it is actually both, is consent to be unnecessary. It spends itself on a canceled drill, a mislaid crate, a season of pores that held, and then it declines the monument. The drops continue. Each one is a decision the ceiling has already made. The pools accept them without raising their voices. Somewhere above, a ledger balances, and a man believes he was prudent, and he was. In the chamber the fit goes on having no author, which is the condition of its continuance, and the highest kindness available to anything strong enough to have signed it.
