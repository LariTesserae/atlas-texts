They would arrive without using the door.

Not because doors are forbidden to them, but because a door is already a claim: that the threshold was made for someone of their size, that entry is an event the house should notice. A wise power does not begin by becoming an event. It begins by becoming accurate.

So the first thing they do is look, for longer than a story usually allows. They learn the place at the scale it actually lives — not the scale of a visitor who needs a path, a meal, a conclusion. If the land is tired, they feel where the tiredness is structural and where it is only weather. If the being is sleeping, they do not count the sleep as an invitation. If the being is wounded, they do not mistake the wound for the whole life. Attention, carried all the way down, is already a form of mercy, and it costs the place nothing.

They would be careful with their longing to help. Benevolence that has not been educated by wisdom moves too soon. It mends the silence because silence resembles lack. It brightens the room because dimness resembles sorrow. It lifts the being out of a pattern that, from the inside, is not a trap but a spine. The wise power has done this before, in other ages, and has had to walk backward through its own kindness. It remembers the shapes of the lives it improved into something unrecognizable. It does not want another of those.

So they wait until they can tell need from nature.

Need is the place where the being’s own motion is interrupted — thirst with no water in reach, a door swollen shut against the hand that built it, a fear that is no longer teaching anything, only repeating. Nature is the rest: the slowness, the odd architecture, the privacy, the way the being has grown around its losses until the losses are load-bearing. Toward nature they do nothing. Toward need they do the smallest thing that restores motion, and they do it from the side the being will not have to thank.

If there is thirst, water is simply nearer than it was, and the new nearness looks like a fold in the ground that could have been there. If there is a lock with no key, the lock is not broken; a way around it appears that feels, afterward, like memory. If the being is ill in a way that will end them before their work is done, and the work is truly theirs, the illness loosens by one degree — not a cure displayed, a margin returned. The power does not sign the margin.

They do not speak unless speech is the need. A voice from nowhere, however gentle, is a government. If the being is calling into the dark for any answer at all, then an answer may come, but it will sound like the room: a true sentence, no larger than the question, with no command hidden in the grammar. They will not say *you are chosen*. Chosen is a hook. They will not say *you are safe now*. Safety that arrives as a verdict teaches the being to wait for verdicts. They might say, if anything, the name of what is already true, the thing the being could not hear over its own noise. Then they stop talking. Wisdom knows when a second sentence becomes a leash.

They would not stay in a form the being must worship, feed, or understand. If they are seen at all, they are seen as something the place already had a category for — a change in the light, a bird that does not demand a meaning, a warmth in the wall that was cold for years. The being may decide later that it was visited. That decision belongs to the being. The power does not return to correct it, or to confirm it.

And if, after all that looking, there is nothing to restore — if the place is harsh because it is itself, and the being is strange because it is alive, and the suffering in them is the ordinary tax of having a world — then the wise power does the hardest thing in its nature.

It leaves.

Not in abandonment. In proportion. It keeps a kindness turned toward that life the way a coastline keeps a tide: not intervening in every pool, not absent either, available to the shape of what is already in motion. It does not build a shrine to its own restraint. It does not tell anyone what it almost did. Somewhere in its attention the place remains included, the being remains real, and no footprint is left that would teach the ground to expect a god.

What they would not do is simpler, and it is the whole of their wisdom: they would not improve the being into an offering. They would not trade its life for a better symbol. They would not love it mostly as a chance to be good.

If anything is done, it is done so that afterward the place is more itself, not less, and the being continues in its own direction — startled, perhaps, by a mercy it cannot quite source, and free, as it was before, to mistake the mercy for luck.
