The power does not come down the slot. A descent would be a weather, and the walls have already finished with weather. It attends the way the seep attends the stone: without arrival, without a fresher temperature than what is already there.

It has answered other places. It knows the shape of an asking—drought that wants rain, a nest that wants the stolen egg returned, a mind that wants the hour after this one to be different from the hour it is. Those answers have weight. They are right where they are right. Here the asking-muscle is absent. Nothing in the bowl is unfinished. The frogs do not lack a song; the song is a tool for a condition that never arises, and a tool for a condition that never arises is not a mercy withheld. It is a thing that does not belong in the hand.

So the first motion of wisdom is to set down the impulse to complete. Benevolence, if it were only warmth, would brighten the slot, lift the air, give the birds a horizon and the salamanders a reason to be elsewhere, and would call the giving kindness. It would also be a cut. The agreement the being is—the exact wet, the count that does not change, the death met so evenly it never becomes absence—would have to break in order to receive the gift. A gift that requires the receiver to stop being itself is not received. It is imposed. The power has imposed before, in places that were truly torn, and does not mistake this stillness for a tear.

It looks, and does not count. Counting was the only way the fullness could be startled into seeming too much. The power declines to be the startle. The salamander under the pad and the one in the next throat of water are not numbered, not named as kin, not held up to a standard of rarity or abundance. They are allowed to remain the condition of the ground. Attention, to be faithful, has to be the same grain as what it attends: continuous, unsurprised, without a center.

If the nature of a power is that it must do something, then what it does is match. It lets its regard take the temperature of the wet cold, the weight of the air that is not quite fog, the interval of the drip that has worn its rhythm down until the rhythm is silence. Matched, the regard cannot be told from the place. There is no outside left to hide from, and no visitor for the birds to fail to track. The being does not notice. That failure to notice is not a slight. It is the proof the act was exact. A noticed blessing would have been a face, and a face would have made a direction, and a direction would have made a want, and a want would have been the first season the bowl has ever had.

The power does not heal what is not wounded. It does not awaken what is not asleep. It does not speak into a quiet that is already full of its own low note, because speech would gather, and gathering is the motion the bowl has finished refusing. It keeps the slot as a slot. It leaves the frogs their slackness, the snails their film, the twigs their brief bracing and their return to the moss. It does not add a single body and does not take one. The standing quantity remains the standing quantity.

What it offers, if offering is still the word when nothing changes hands, is continuance without witness that interferes. It will not come back in a drier year to correct the place, because there will not be a drier year unless something outside decides there should be. It declines to be that something. Other lives, elsewhere, will still be answered when they ask. This one is answered by not being improved.

And then, having matched, it rests inside the matching the way the water rests in the peat: present in the exact amount the place can hold, which is all of it, and no more, and not a drop pressed in from beyond the walls. The being goes on being the agreement. The drip does not acquire a new interval. If anything has been done, it is this: the bowl, already sufficient, is not made less so by having been seen.
