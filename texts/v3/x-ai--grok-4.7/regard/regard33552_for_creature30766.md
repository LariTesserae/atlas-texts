They would come no closer than attention.

Not a descent, not a hand on the ridge or the brow. The kind of power that is actually wise has already learned the commonest form of harm: arriving with a kindness too large for the life that must go on living inside it. So it would not mend the height, or gift water to a chemistry that has spent an age learning to do without, or set a lamp where the sun already does the only work the leaves know how to use. It would not gather the being out of the emptiness between stands and set it down somewhere kinder. Somewhere kinder would be somewhere else, and a being moved for its own good is a being ended under a gentler name.

What it would do is look until looking became accurate.

It would learn the tilt of the shields and not hurry it. It would learn which pans are wounds and which are only the land breathing at its own pace. It would find the being not by the tracks — those are already leaving — but by the scale at which the being spends itself: how far it must go between one thick leaf and the next, what it refuses to sweat, what it will not call. If the being is not an animal at all but the stands themselves, or the pavement, or the long habit of the wind in the swales, the power would not be embarrassed. Wisdom is not proud of having assumed a face.

And then, having seen, it would do the difficult thing, which is almost nothing.

It would keep the brightness from being improved. It would let the oil stay oil, the cold stay cold, the distances stay the distance the chemistry requires. If some other power — weather from outside, a hunger, a made thing — were coming to break the balance faster than the pavement can seal, it might set a shoulder against that and no further: not a rescue, a margin. Enough time for the breach to scar over in the old way. Not a new way.

Toward the being it would offer only what can be offered without being taken: the knowledge, held quietly and not spoken into the thin air, that the life as it is has been seen entire and not found wanting. No blessing that rearranges. No test. No companion set down in the glare to relieve a solitude that may be the shape of the creature's continuance.

If the being lifted its head, or the leaves tilted an hour early, or the pavement brightened by a shade no wind accounted for, that would be accident of light. The power would not claim it.

And if, after all that looking, it understood that the place was already in its own state — catastrophe and repair at the same patience, lushness a property of bodies rather than of cover, emptiness not failure — then it would do the last benevolent thing available to it.

It would go on attending, and it would not intervene.
