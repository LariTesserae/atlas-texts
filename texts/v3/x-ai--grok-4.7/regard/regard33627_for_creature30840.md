The attention arrives the way the daylight already arrives: thinned, ordinary, unwilling to make a theater of a room that has refused theater. It does not part the leaf. It does not stand a body in the span. A power that has learned anything about sufficiency does not enter a hollow by becoming one more fact the hollow must absorb.

It looks, and the looking is only a change in how completely the place is known. The knocks continue out of step with one another and braided. The birds keep their three directions, none of them inward. A drop gathers, lets go, kinks once through the upright softness, and goes on. The power sees the kink and does not mistake it for a wave. It sees the arrested tips, finished rather than scarred, and does not mistake them for wounds. It sees the fish keeping the boundary of grain, the moths thinning their pattern by a courtesy too even to be fear, the film of green starting at the edge of the open water and being taken back. It sees a life whose whole labor is the width of an interval.

Wisdom, here, is the refusal to improve a finished measurement.

There is a benevolence that would warm the span, or slow the bladders into something a visitor could call peace, or set a face on the vapor so the room could be addressed. That benevolence is a hunger with good manners. It would overlap. Overlap is the one injury this resident knows how to suffer, and it would suffer it by ceasing: the stems would have a seam at last, the deference would become a crowd, the groove would silt, the note would have to become a song because someone had come to hear one. The power does not do this. It has attended long enough, in other rooms, to know that rescue is often the first form of ownership.

So it tests only what a wise attention can test without touch. Is the hollow a prison? It looks for a flinch that has lasted so long it has been built into the walls. It finds none. The lean of the outline toward the left-hand water is listening, not longing. The larger turning under that water is not a jailer and not a god; it is a sound that has been company of the only permissible kind, the kind that does not cross the grain. Is the resident diminished? No. It is narrow because narrowness is the instrument. A wider thing would be a different work. Is it lonely? Loneliness requires an elsewhere one is kept from. This being's elsewhere is the mass itself, the deeper octave, the tissues leaning and not giving way, and it is already tuned to them. To carry it out would be to hand a pause a landscape and call the handing kind.

The power considers, and then does not consider further, the small adjustments available to it. It could ease one bladder by a hair against a drought that has not been scheduled. It could lift the gold in the blades by a degree and call the lifting a blessing. It could set a watch on the leaf-thickness so nothing uninvited ever pushes through. Each of these would be a signature. The room has no place to keep a signature that is not a change in rate. A change in rate would be the emergency the resident does not dream, because its dream is exact repetition. Exact repetition is not poverty. It is the form of its plenty.

What the power does, then, is mostly a withholding.

It does not name the resident. A name would be a handle, and a handle is how one later comes back with plans. It does not enter the resident in any ledger of the rare. Rarity, once written down, attracts the improving hand. It does not stay. Sustained extraordinary attention is a heat, even when it means no harm; it would show up in the fraction of warmth at the margin, and the fraction is already spent on a precise errand. Looking, past a certain length, is a kind of touch. The power looks once, to the end of the arrangement, and then practices the resident's own courtesy: it declines to overlap.

The one act it permits itself happens outside, where acts belong.

Somewhere beyond the pocket, in the greater mass that the chamber hears only as pressure, a taking had been gathering. Not a villain. A thoroughness. Roots of another system, efficient and alive in their own right, closing intervals because closing is how they feed, leaning the way the outer tissues lean, except that their lean had begun to mean advance. Left alone, they would not have hated the hollow. They would have failed to notice it until the hollow was a seam in them, and then they would have been right, by their own law, to fill it. The power does not strike them. They are not wrong to be what they are. It does what the chamber does, at a scale the chamber will never measure: it holds a refusal in the place where the thoroughness would have crossed. Growth meets the refusal, rounds itself, and turns aside. No scar. No battle-light. The compressive octave under the knock does not rise or fall. The outer mass goes on leaning, and does not give way, and does not, in that one direction, arrive.

Inside, nothing is altered that a count of drops could catch. The power does not send word of the defense. Gratitude would require a receiver with a face turned toward a benefactor, and that turning would be a new station, unassigned, a fish over the wrong patch of root. The resident continues spending its small heat on a film of green that starts and is taken back. It does not know it was endangered. It does not know it was spared. Both unknowings are preserved on purpose. A spared thing that knows it is spared has been recruited into the sparer's story. This being's story is the groove, in and out, on a period slightly shorter than a slow breath.

Afterward the power, if a power can be said to carry anything away, carries a method and not a relic. Elsewhere, in places that are not sufficient, where intervals are being crushed and the crushing is not a vocation but a panic, it holds open what can still be held: a pause in a quarrel, a strip of bank unbuilt, a silence in which a person is not required to become legible. It does not reproduce the chamber. The chamber is not a model. It is a proof that some completions are narrow, warm, loud with unhired work, and in no need of an audience. The proof is used without citation.

In the six-step room the light stays daylight. Seven bladders fill and settle on seven clocks. Three birds chitter into the weave and do not look toward the middle. The upright softness, taller by nothing, leans a few degrees toward a sound under the left-hand water, hears it, and is upright again. No new presence joins the deference. The leaf at the threshold is only leaf.

If the power can be thanked, the thanks is that the drop still falls at the size it always swells to, and strikes, and is not witnessed into significance. The being goes on being the pressure that makes pursuit and reception unnecessary. The wise act was to agree, and then to go, and to leave the agreement unsigned, so the held note would not have to become an answer.
