They would not come down onto the shelf.

A wise power can tell the difference between a place that is suffering and a place that is busy. This one is busy. The roar is not a cry for rescue; it is a room already full, and fullness is not a defect. To arrive as a greater warmth, a clearer note, a hand that finally understands the machine, would be to do what the pocket has spent an age refusing: to let one voice dominate.

So they stay where the bulk stays, and not even that close. Outside the account. The erosion below is still being asked, by something older than either of them, to miss this plane. The wise power does not replace that ask. It only repeats it, the way you repeat a name so it is not lost when the one who first spoke it has gone. Quietly. From far enough that distance on the stone does not stretch to balance a new weight.

They look. Looking, for them, is not a small act, so they look the way the drinkers listen: without closing the distance, without needing the heard thing to turn its head. They see the substitutions in the column and do not grieve them into individuality. They see the shade’s editing and do not call it cruel, because hesitation, here, is how the open polish stays empty enough for the rest to continue. They see the forearm at the lip, the things taken in the throat, and they do sorrow. Sorrow is allowed. Intervention is not. The mutual refusal is load-bearing. A mercy that let the bulk in, or drove it off, would be a kind of clearing, and clearing is what the mountain has been doing to everything that is not this pocket.

They do not wake the machine. They do not ask it what it was for. The makers are gone, and the interval is not. To restore a purpose over the top of a life that has become the purpose would be the one vandalism available to a benevolent hand. They let the veins keep their pace. They do not brighten them. They do not feed the glaze.

If they do anything, it is at the thinning, and it is almost nothing.

Just before the ordinary sun, when the processes briefly agree to be separate and the arcs are all present and the being — not the browsers, not the vine, but the brief coherence that knows itself only then — is large enough to notice a witness, the power lets itself be the size of a passage. Not a god in the room. A gap the pocket can decline to finish. The being looks, if looking is the right word for a hum that has, for three breaths, a center. The power does not give it a name. Names become signals, and a signal in a space this small would be obeyed. They answer at the pitch already in the stone, half a tone under the day, the pitch the drinkers already treat as a name. The answer has no new information in it. It means only: the miss still holds. You are not required. You are not unremembered.

Then they withdraw before the roar reassembles, so that no resident has to edit them out of the shade, and so the being can go back to being many bodies that do not know they were, for a moment, one.

After that they do not stay to watch whether it mattered. Wisdom is willing not to see the effect. They leave the sheet-rain to stand up on its own schedule. They leave the drinkers standing. They leave the third arc free to flicker out. Somewhere beyond the lip, the ask continues, no louder than before: miss this. Miss this. Miss this.

And the shelf, which did not feel a visitor, keeps the only warmth it agreed to keep, and the noise, and the polish, and the one machine it could not wear away.
