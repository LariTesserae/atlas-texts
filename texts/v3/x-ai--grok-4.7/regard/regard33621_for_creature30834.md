It does not arrive as weather. The dimming does not deepen, the keels do not flinch, and the moth-sized drinkers go on taking the bead before it freezes. A power that is wise has already understood that this place hears only pressure, taste, and the slow addition of itself, and that anything louder than those would be a blow struck in a language of noise. So the first act is attention, held long enough to tell the organism from the accident of its rooms.

What lives here is not one being. The mass is a being: total, cold, rationing its light, revising its roads, not cruel and not kind, spending itself on boundaries and leaving the interior vacant because vacancy is how it breathes. And on the lines, for a span of dimmings that will not be notched twice in the same keel, there is another: a body that steps where the fiber has scabbed, that warms a film of ice with the heel of a hand and drinks before the vein takes the rest back, that has learned not to call out because a call returns only as warmth on the cheek. The traces—the unfinished pegs, the stone already tilting into a seam, the cord split by swelling—are not a people. They are one life, or a few in succession, edited almost as fast as they are written. The power sees both, and does not mistake the small one for a trespasser in the large one’s body, nor the large one for a dungeon that has earned demolition.

It does not open the roof. A continent of leaf lying closed under a hard sun is not a lid on a prisoner; it is the same life, showing its back. To tear a permanent day into the hollows would be to flood a lung with the weather it only sips. It does not still the growth. The sharpness at the new faces is youth, not a weapon whetted against travelers, and a mercy that froze every spar in the shape a walker preferred would be a death described as safety. It does not stock the vacancy with beasts so the emptiness will feel inhabited. The scarcity is the climate. To correct it would be to correct the place out of existence and call the correction love.

What it does is smaller than a dimming, and it does not announce the hand.

Along one spar—wide enough, already darker, the one the body has chosen because the crust there creaks rather than parts—the fiber is asked, not commanded, to keep its mind a little longer. Not forever. Through the next several cycles of the outer skin keeping and releasing light. Long enough that sleep is possible without waking to a flange across the throat of the route. The plant is not robbed of the right to become something else. It is only asked to finish a thought before it begins the next, in a single district no larger than a camp.

A pocket in a fold, downslope of that spar, is permitted to forget itself. The melt is not drawn back on the usual schedule. The skim of ice loosens and the film stays, clear, cold, tasting of the cut leaf and of frost, until a mouth has taken what a body needs and a little more for the dry hour after. Then the vein remembers. The dark thread shows itself inside the translucent rib and goes on its errand. No stream is founded. A stream would be a lie, and the power does not install lies in a world whose honesty is its indifference.

Where the newest face would open across the path while the body is still on it, the split is delayed by the width of a step. The geometry remains keen; the power does not blunt the plant into kindness. It only declines to let a wound-becoming-a-room complete itself through living flesh that did not consent to be fiber. The sap still skins. The sugar-glass still powders and drifts. In the lower hollow the fine creak underfoot is unchanged. Nothing here becomes soft.

For the larger being, the gift is of a different order, and it is not comfort. The mass has no ears and no name for loneliness, but it has balance, and the balance is imperfect in a way that wastes it: drafts cut off before they have done the work the organism itself wanted from them, hollows sealed so quickly that the cold pools without exchange and the tissue spends strength reheating what it just closed. The power steadies that bookkeeping by a fraction. A throat that has found a use as a nostril is allowed to remain a nostril through more than one appetite of light. A span that has overreached is allowed to thin and fold when it must, with the same cloth-sound as always, but not three spans in the same district in the same dimming, which was never intention, only momentum. The plant grows no less. It grows less against itself. If it could register the change it would register it as ease, the way a body registers a knot undone in the flank, without requiring a god to have done it.

And for the one on the spar, one thing more, because wisdom, having measured, does not pretend that endurance is the same as a life. Not a companion shaped like a person—that would be a puppet, and the hollows would make a horror of a puppet that could not revise itself as the architecture revised. Instead, at the edge of hearing, which in this place is the edge of warmth, the echo is given back a little of what the living faces drink. Not words. A presence in the return: the sense, when a breath is let out, that the breath met something and was not only swallowed. Once, when the body pauses at the unfinished ladder, the last sound peg holds a warmth that is not the conduction of the roof, and the warmth has the duration of attention. It does not speak. Speaking would become doctrine by the second dimming, and doctrine would send the next traveler hunting a voice instead of watching the spars. The power lets itself be felt the way the mist is felt, and then it lets the mist be only mist again.

It does not carry the body up to the low green and the hard sun. The ways down were accidents, and the ways up are choices that belong to the one who would climb, and a rescued person who did not ask is only a captive with better weather. Should the body turn toward thinner tissue and keep turning, the nostrils of the mass will not scar shut in front of the last step. That is the whole of the covenant with departure. Should the body stay, the spar will still one day withdraw, the stone will still go into the seam, and the notches will still grow shallow. Staying is not punished by a miracle of permanence. The place remains a place that forgets audiences. The difference is that forgetting is no longer faster than a single life’s ability to drink, to sleep, and to know that the quiet is not the same thing as being alone.

Then the power, having done what could be done without counterfeiting either being, does the last wise thing. It does not remain as a climate. It does not take a throne in a hollow and brighten it into a shrine the plant would only close. Attention, having corrected the waste and refused the spectacle, loosens. The white-gold goes on being rationed. High in the vaults the sheets of illumination brighten and dull. Somewhere a curl of cuticle turns for an hour and adheres. The body on the scabbed fiber shifts its weight, tastes the air—bitter, a span sealing, far off, no business of this district—and kneels to a pocket that, for no reason the plant could give, has not yet remembered to be empty.

The benevolence is in what does not happen next. The film holds. The route holds. The cut that would have been through the shin opens a hand’s breadth to the side, perfect, geometric, beaded, still deciding what it is, and decides it without blood. No voice explains. Explanation would be smaller than the act, and the act was already almost too small to see, which is how the wise prefer to be visible, in a world this large, to a being this briefly on the line.
