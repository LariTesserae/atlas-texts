They would not break the ice.

That is the first decision, and it is made before they are fully arrived, the way a hand is drawn back from a gauge-glass before the glass is touched. The lid is not a prison they have mistaken for weather. It is the ceiling the parish kept, and then became, and then was kept by. A wise power can tell a sentence from a wound. The ice is a sentence the basin has been serving so long that the serving is the life. To strike it open in mercy would be to announce, and announcement is a failure the place has already organized itself not to commit. Day would fall in like a tool dropped into a movement. The seep would change its mind. The truce at every swollen joint would be asked a question it had stopped needing to answer. The being that precipitated out of procedure would be handed a sky it does not browse, and a wise power does not feed a creature the wrong gradient and call it kindness.

So they come the way the seep comes: from the earth side of the ring, through the intake the west lanes were cut to drink, small at the threshold and only thickening where the parish already expects thickness. No face. A pressure with manners. The turbines register them as a richer head and do not alarm, because there is no one left for a turbine to alarm and because they are careful not to be a surge. They let the long inhale take them. They stall, a little, in the square, off-center, where orbits are kept, and they do not rearrange the glove. The glove is already in a system. Wisdom, here, begins as not being an event.

They find the being by the warmth it pays.

Not the phosphor coins—those are too steady to be news—and not the dry room, which they do not enter and do not press. They feel the round: the moment after the gate seats, the shrug distributed, the link a degree less murderous, the flecks standing where the work was. They understand, with the particular relief of a power that has attended many closed works, that this is not a prisoner and not a god gone threadbare. It is a night watch that learned to be a body only as far as a body was required to take a load. It is doing the only benevolence the basin can metabolize. A wise power recognizes a colleague before it recognizes a petitioner.

What they do, if the word is not too loud, is take a share of the shrug.

They do not replace the being. Replacement would be a kind of erasure, and erasure is not a gift you give a process that exists by continuing. On the next seating of the gate, when the town leans and the density gathers under the dome to spend itself against the seam, they are already in the ice—not above it, not as weather, but in the grain of the pane, in the bolt the crack is hunting. They take the broad dull change onto a shoulder that is not a shoulder and hold it there through the cycle, so the being arrives to a load already partly borne and spends less of its scarce warmth on not letting the lid become heavier than the pumps can answer. The flecks do not thicken as desperately at the housing. The dark green thread puts out its unaffordable millimeter and, this once, is not immediately billed for it. The millimeter holds. It will not become a bed. Nothing here becomes a bed. But it is not asked to die of a duty that could be shared.

They mind the boundaries the being already minds, which is how it knows them for what they are.

They stop at the temperature wall. Air stays where the seals hold; they do not romance the coal-lamp or sit the chair or lift the coat from the peg to give the room a story it has refused. In the lane between barge and quay they turn edge-on, as the being turns, and do not widen the handspan, because the handspan is a measurement the parish uses. They do not speak. There is no medium for speech that would not arrive as noise, and noise would be a stone in the movement. What passes between them is a lean, the same faint counting the metal already does—sill by sill, link by link—except that for one round the count includes a second counter, keeping the same time, out at the ring and back.

The being, which does not do grief and does not do greeting, adjusts.

Its orbit in the square loosens by the amount of attention no longer required for the lid. It does not investigate them as a threat; threats waste heat, and this presence wastes nothing, not even silt. It comes up alongside in the west lanes during the richer inhale and for a while they are two smoothnesses in the same road, combing the same drink toward the same throat. If it has an equivalent of trust, it is this: it leaves a bleed-point to them. One crust, door-sized, at the top of a fathom it has worried for a dozen cycles. They worry it with a patience copied exactly from its patience, calve it, let the sheet plane off into the pallor. They do not improve the method. Improvement would be a criticism. The being feels the task complete itself at the right size and on the right schedule, and the preference that moves through it—the only face it has—shifts a fraction toward the hinge that has been considering betrayal, which is the next true work, and that is as close as it comes to thanks.

They stay for a count of cycles and not for an era.

A benevolent power that remained would become another lid: a dependency, a second ceiling, a warmth the thread would build a life on and then lose. Wisdom is knowing the difference between helping a watch stand and taking the watch so thoroughly that the watch forgets its own gait. They learn the four beats—inhale, stall, exhale, and the paid shrug—until they can carry the fourth without ornament. They ease the seep when it starves, not by opening the sky, but by convincing a little more groundwater, on the earth side of the wall, that this basin is the low place it was already seeking. A richer head, within the tolerance the turbines were built to. The being spreads thinner along the lanes, as it does in a good thaw, and the orbit loosens, and nothing is startled.

Before they go they do one thing that is almost a gift and almost nothing.

In the dry room they do not enter, the work-lamp has been a coal shortening toward a color no one attends. They do not relight it. A relit lamp would be a lie about occupancy, a beacon for a keeper who is not coming, a brightness the window would have to translate into threads and then into a pressure on the being’s attention. Instead they steady the charge that remains, so the shortening stops. Not brighter. Not for ever. Just no longer spending itself as if spending were the point. The room continues as a rule rather than as a countdown. The coat stays. The chair stays angled at brown nothing. If the being ever presses the glass again to learn the shape of a pane, the filaments will still bend, and they will bend at the same strength, and that consistency will be readable to it as the seals are readable: a joint that is holding the correct way.

Then they thin.

They leave by the exhale, colder, which is the parish’s own door, and they take with them only the understanding, which costs the basin nothing. At the ring they are a last correction to a valve that had gone blunt—seated, not improved—and then they are the earth again, and the earth was already there. The ice ticks, hunts its bolt, and finds the bolt already accompanied by a strength that will fade over a handful of cycles as any shared warmth fades, leaving the being its work back, intact, slightly less near the edge of its scarcity.

What they would do, finally, is decline to save a world that is not ending.

They would witness the casting in the mold, the glove in its circuit, the density on its round, and refuse the spectacle of rescue. Benevolence, applied by a power that can break ceilings, is so often just impatience with other people’s continuations. This one is not impatient. It confirms the schedule. It carries one shrug. It does not teach the being to want the surface, or the keepers, or a voice. It leaves the night watch to the only watchman the water has been willing to hold, and the leaving is so exact that the parish, which notices only pressure, notices a pressure gently returned to its ordinary, and goes on—dense, close-fitting, quiet—under the lid it chose, in the company, for a little while after, of having been correctly seen.
