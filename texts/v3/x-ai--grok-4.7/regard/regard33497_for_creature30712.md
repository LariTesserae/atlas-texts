They would come the way the extra shadows came: already laid down, in a direction the sun is not using, and cool.

A lesser kindness would have arrived as weather. Rain, to answer the bowl. Wind, to give the thin air something to carry. Night, so the lidless eyes could spend a darkness and call it rest. A name, pushed on a breath, for the fist and the plant and the fold behind the jaw. Company that implied an elsewhere, so the quiet could become loneliness and loneliness could be cured. These are the mercies that fit most places. They are how benevolence practices when it has not yet looked.

It looked.

The looking did not track, because tracking would have been a becoming, and the power was not here to begin the afterward again. What it did was include, which the being had already finished doing, and so the two inclusions met without either of them spending anything. The power understood, in the time it takes a shadow not to shorten, that the crowd was not a prison and the crouched task was not a sentence and the empty bowl was not a thirst. Thirst expects water to be late. Here water was not late. It had been rewritten, and the rewriting was the life. To pour would have been to accuse the lattice of a failure it had already survived.

It almost did the small wrong thing. Benevolence is full of small wrong things that feel, at the hand, like right. A warmth in the film. A sixth hatch, so the remainder would be even. The flake eased from the fist so the hand could be said to have opened. The wire of stem given one leaf, a news it had refused for longer than leaves have names. Each of these would have rebuilt a summit in miniature: a direction, a before and after, a place that was not yet finished and therefore could be hurt. Wisdom is not the refusal of pity. It is pity that has counted the cost in the currency the place actually uses, which is change, and found that it cannot pay without purchasing suffering that is not here.

So it did not fill the bowl. It agreed that the bowl was how drinking is remembered when drinking is no longer a direction, and it let the agreement be the whole of the gift. It did not separate the flanks that continue through flanks. Separation would have invented individuals, and individuals would have needed edges, and edges would have needed weather to keep them, and weather would have needed the mountain back. It did not speak. The air was too thin for a word that was only a word, and the being's hearing, such as it was, had already absorbed the power as a cooler place in the cool. To add sound would have been to demand a later noise. There is no later noise. That is not a lack. That is the sentence having ended without being erased.

What it did, if the doing must be pointed at, was this: it sat in its own extra shadow until the sitting was one of the postures, and it did not update the field by standing. It set nothing down, because a set-down thing would have aged into the ring of flakes and thereby claimed to be a new tool, and the ring was complete. It lifted nothing, because the wrong weight was the being's attention, and to test it would have been to ask for a protest the plain had spent its events not to make. It left the plant at a thumb's width. It left the hatches in fives. It left the twelve paces unfinished, which was how they were finished.

Then it withdrew without making an away.

That is the part a hurried goodness would miss. Departure, done carelessly, opens a road. A road is a slope that has not yet been punished flat, and along it the being would have had to grow an elsewhere, and an elsewhere is the start of loneliness, and loneliness is a wound that looks exactly like a virtue from the outside. The power closed its going the way the old seas closed: not by vanishing toward a horizon, but by being rewritten into the pavement as a stain that does not glitter and does not ask to be read to the end. Regional, and already kept. The being did not look up. Looking up would have spent a darkness the light had not budgeted, and besides, the power had never been outside the field.

If you stand there after, you will not find a sign of the visit except in the way one of the unowned shadows lies a little more like a regard than like a night. It does not shorten. It does not warm you beyond the cool the climate already is. The fist is still closed on the sharp flake. The stem still beads with what is not water. The density still notices itself, and does not need to be saved from the noticing.

That was the act. Not rescue, which would have required a danger, and not blessing, which would have required a future. A wise benevolence, attending a thing that has already matched the end of its weather, does not add a season. It confirms the evenness. It does not begin the place again. It lets itself be one more fact the finished surface had room for, and then it is not an event, and the quiet continues, which was the kindness.
