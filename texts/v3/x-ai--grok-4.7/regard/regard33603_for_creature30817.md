They would not open the wall.

A power that is only kind might have done it at once: split the cladding, let weather in, given the trays real rain and the lamps a rival in the sun, called the scream a wound and stopped it. Wisdom arrives a step earlier than kindness and stays a step longer. It stands in the budgeted lane between the housings and does not confuse its own discomfort with the room’s.

The scream is not pain. Pain has a contour, a place it is worse, a body it belongs to. This note has no owner. It is the sound of a loop that was built to have no weather and has succeeded. To silence it would be to declare the success a mistake. The power does not declare that. It listens until the flinching stops, the way any honest guest must, and then listens past the stopping, in case something underneath the note is asking.

Nothing is. The gauges do not hunt. The growths do not lean. The thin oily fraction moves at the rate it has always moved, which is almost not at all. If there is a being here, it is not suffering in a language intervention can answer. The absence of animals is not a massacre the power can see the end of; it is a threshold that was chosen and then kept. Young metal, eternal operation. The contradiction is the climate. Climates are not rescued by being improved into other climates.

So the power does a smaller thing, and does it without spectacle, because spectacle would be a pressure change, and pressure is the one sacrament this room still believes in.

It learns the loop. Not to master it. To be accurate. Where the gas turns, where the valves were set and never turned again, which tray draws the fraction and which only seems to. It reads the maintenance board and does not add a date. It stands where a keeper would stand if a keeper were the being, and finds the floor unsoftened by any habit of standing. No groove. No grease-darkened reach toward a particular wheel. Whoever was meant to live here either never arrived or became the set-points and left their hands behind in the positions.

That is the fact wisdom has to sit with: the room may be uninhabited in the way that matters, and still be complete.

Toward the growths it is gentler, because they are the only gesture in the room that is not a machine completing itself. It does not rename them plants to make mercy easier. It does not grant them thirst they do not have. It checks the thin line the way you check a pulse you are not sure is yours to take, and finding it faithful, leaves it faithful. If a bearing were an hour from starving them by a drift too small for the roar to confess, the power would correct that drift and no other. Maintenance, not miracle. The trays go on casting their thin shadows under lamps installed for eyes that are not here.

And for the being who is not in evidence, it does one thing that can be refused, which is the only kind of gift that stays benevolent after the giver has gone.

At the instrument glass, where the drop is only cladding and floodlight, it leaves a seam that is not yet a door. Not open. Not marked. A place in the seal that will part if something that belongs here asks from the inside, and that will remain a wall if nothing asks. The gas will not notice. The scream will not drop. No one walking the lanes will find a handle, because a handle would be an opinion. The seam answers only to a will that already lives in the room, if any will does: the growth’s slow thickness, a keeper’s return, a decision forming in whatever passes for a tenant in a climate this finished.

If nothing ever asks, the seam is only another panel, and the power’s visit is only attention, which the room is free to waste.

Then it goes. It does not bless the equilibrium into holiness, and it does not sentence it. It lets the circuit complete another round identical to the last. High, sealed, cold, dry. Sharp edges. A note with no onset. The place continues at the only volume it has, which was never a performance, and which a wise power will not interrupt merely to prove it was here.

What it carries away is not a rescue. It is the knowledge of a room that does not need weather, and the willingness to come back if the seam is ever used, and not before.
