The power does not come by the notch, or the scree, or the way that already looks like a mistake. It does not stand a fourth stone, or a fifth. It does not warm the volume of air, which is the place’s true body, and it does not give the walls back their echoes. A lesser kindness would have done all of that before it understood what it was looking at. This one waits until the waiting itself has lost the shape of an entrance.

It sees the shelf as it is in the hour when the rim alone is lit: seep smoking where cold meets water that has crossed warm rock, mats dark underneath, one broad-winged bird folded on the last perch, the colony out along the wet line, the cairn’s shadow lying down like something that has earned its rest. It sees the tracks. It sees the cord. It sees the bones in the narrow crack and does not call them a grave, because they are not one. It is not fooled by the emptiness. It is not moved to fill it. Filling is what the light already does, and the light is sufficient, and indecent, and exact.

What lives here is not the hunter and not the hand that struck the flake. Those are visitors, and the power is old enough in wisdom to know a crossing from a life. What lives here is the agreement: the pause when cloud arrives, the resumption when a condition is met, the office of the watcher that moves from body to body, the tick that means drink and the tick that means gone, the seep cutting a groove the color of the rock’s interior, the flowers measuring summer by the hour. A being, yes. It has no face. It has a schedule. It spends brightness the way other beings spend breath. It is not unfinished.

So the power does not improve it.

It does not teach the birds a song. Song would travel, and the shelf has already decided what may travel and what must die within an arm’s length. It does not blunt the young edges of the rock; the cuts are how the joints stay clean enough to be rooms. It does not stay the seep in its theft of the pocket. That theft is the mountain speaking to the drinkers in the only grammar they share, and a water that cannot be lost is a water that cannot be found again elsewhere when the finding is required. It does not make the hunter merciful. Mercy, added from outside, would only teach the colony to trust a quiet that had been edited, and the first unedited quiet would take more of them than the hunter ever has. It does not call the person back to the cairn. The cord is an intention, not a debt, and a wise power does not collect other people’s intentions for them.

It considers, because benevolence must consider, the small deaths that will happen before morning: a cushion under a veil of grit, a drinker who misreads the lip, a seed that falls outside the crease and does not become a lineage. It does not reach for them. They are not errors in the being. They are the being’s way of remaining the size of the thread that feeds it. To cancel them would be to love a different creature, one that does not live here, and to install it in the place of the one that does.

What it does is narrower than action, and harder.

It withholds the weight of its notice. Attention, at that scale, is a weather. If it leaned, the air would lean; the spiders would set a leg wrong; the watcher would watch the wrong quiet; the flower half-open in cloud-shadow would wait for a summer that was not the hour. So the power looks the way the thin air looks: present, carrying almost nothing, refusing to return a sound. It lets the pact operate on local evidence alone—the change in the birds, the pressure before the five-toed step, the tick that is also stone. It does not add a confirmation. In that refusal, a mammal that might have spent its caution on a greater presence spends it, instead, on the hunter, and lives, and never knows it was helped, because it was not helped. It was simply not distracted from its own life. That is the whole intervention. It can be mistaken for nothing. It is meant to be.

For the place, as distinct from the being, the power allows one courtesy that is not an improvement. When the joint in the east wall gives its dry report, and the veil of grit starts, the power does not stop the mountain’s spending. It only declines to add its own grain to the slide. The mat receives what the rock was already going to give, and no more. By morning the cushion will have shed it or grown through it. Either outcome belonged to the shelf before it was watched.

The cord it does not touch. A hand that knows the gesture will find it, or the brace-stone will take it, and both are honest endings. The power is not a pocket for other lives to store their returns in.

It stays as long as the last bird stays, and no longer. When the light on the upper third of the west wall is gone, the bird drops toward older country, where a voice can be spent and partly returned. The power goes with that departure only in the sense that it stops attending; it does not follow. Following would make a path, and the shelf has three ways out already, which is enough.

Afterward the cold sits in the open volume and does not move much. The seep finds a new hairline and shines. Somewhere in the gutter a stone gives up its place. The colony’s watcher flattens, then rises, then is replaced by another body holding the same office. Nothing on the shelf thanks anything. Nothing needs to. The being resumes, which is its way of praise, and the place continues almost entirely unfilled, and full, under a sky that has not been asked to answer.
