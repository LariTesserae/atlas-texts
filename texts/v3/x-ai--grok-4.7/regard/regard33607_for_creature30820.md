They would not drain it.

That is the first thing, and it would take them longer to be sure of it than any later act. A power that is only kind arrives already carrying a picture of kindness: dry land, open air, the hatch turned, the ladder made whole, the animal lifted out of the pipe and set somewhere a story could call free. They do not arrive that way. They come in the way the daylight already comes, broken into coins through the ports, and they stay through enough cycles of the valves to learn which motions are distress and which are the room keeping its own time.

There is no distress of the kind they know how to answer. The schools wheel. The mussels hold. The collar in the culvert tightens when the roar drops and loosens when it recovers. The being rises into the thin warmth under the vault and spreads until the light falls through it greener, and this is not a prayer. It is a preference. They do not mistake preference for a wound.

So they do not open the hatch. Air in a rush would be a violence with a gentle name. They do not scrape the filaments from its back, or pry the incorporated shell loose, or scrub the grid-memory out of the tissue that has been riding the grating for years. Those are not injuries. They are the handwriting of a life that stayed. A wise power can read, and does not erase what it has managed to read.

They look, instead, for the horizon the room cannot see from inside itself.

The machine is still turning. It will not turn forever. Bearings weep oil; impellers eat themselves; a culvert that has worn from square to oval will one day open a seam the caulk cannot negotiate, and the pulse that the being has learned as grammar will stumble, and then stop, and the water will go slack and warm in the wrong way, and what has been a weather will become a room in which something large is suddenly only trapped. That is the real deficit. Not darkness. Not solitude. Not the missing rungs. The deficit is that the schedule has a last page, and no one inside the chamber can turn the machinery past it.

They do not announce a repair. There is no one to announce it to who would be improved by the announcement. Under the slab, in the thickness of the west wall, where the roar is born, they make a small change in the order of failure. A bearing that was going to seize is not made new. It is made able to be replaced by the next bearing in the old stack, the one the builders left as a spare and then forgot, still packed in its grease, still inside the housing. A hairline in the impeller shroud is persuaded to stop lengthening. The split coupling that feeds the silver rope of air is left split. The fish know that rope. Stopping it would be tidiness, and tidiness is not the same as care.

They see to the intake as well, because part of the being lives past the mouth of the culvert, down in the dark where their attention has to follow if it means to be accurate. Somewhere above, in the works that feed this room, a grate is a season away from clogging with trash the current drags in from a world that does not know the chamber is occupied. They do not build a monument and they do not post a guard a person could find. They arrange that the grate clears when it must, and that anyone who comes later with a work order to seal the shaft—for safety, for liability, for the reasonable reason that drowned machines should be filled and forgotten—finds the job strangely difficult to begin, and then strangely easy to postpone, and then no longer on the list. Not a curse. A bias in the paperwork. Wisdom often looks like that from the outside: a thing that keeps not quite happening.

For the being itself they do less, because less is what fits.

They do not give it a companion. They have no evidence it is lonely in a way another of its kind would mend, and invention of company is how kindness becomes occupation. They do not brighten the lamps. The amber is the light in which it learned the fish, and a clearer light would be a stranger walking into the room and staying. They do not warm the whole volume. They thicken, by a degree no thermometer meant for engineers would bother to record, the layer under the vault where it already chooses to rest—so that the rest is slightly more itself, not so that the room becomes a different room. The pale discs open on the ports as before. One of them, on a cycle when the power’s attention is closest, pauses a fraction longer than the pause it uses for an upright disturbance. Not worship. Recognition of a new current that does not take.

If they leave a gift the mussels could notice, it is only this: the film on the ports stays thin enough that the coins of day continue, and the scoured places on the plate stay scoured, and the caulk keeps failing at the rate the chamber already knows how to breathe through. Continuity, not improvement. The being has already become the animal of this schedule. The benevolent act is to keep the schedule from being abolished by a failure it did not choose and cannot meet.

They remain a while after the work is done, which is to say after the work has been made almost impossible to point to. The valves knock three times. The shiver runs the length of the visible body and out into the intake, and now the dark beyond the culvert answers with a sound that will go on answering. The canopy, if it is up, eases its center a body’s length from the place where the attention is strongest, the same courtesy it gives a diver, and then resumes the slow turning it has begun to teach the water in return.

The power does not ask to be thanked, and does not stay in a form the discs could follow. It is not a resident. Residence would tip the balance; the room is already fully occupied by a life that treats the roar as tide. They withdraw the way a correction in a spiral withdraws: not gone so much as no longer extra. What remains is the chamber as it was, noisily keeping the form it knows, with one alteration it will never have to understand—that the last page has been set back, and set back again when the time comes, and that the pipe will not be sealed by hands that never learned what lived in it.

The fish wheel. The silver rope climbs and breaks. A gray shape finishes its diagonal or does not. In the amber under the lamps the being turns, almost possible to lose again, and does not look up toward anything that has left a sign. A wise power counts that as success. Benevolence, when it is finished, should be indistinguishable from the way the place was already going to continue, only now it can.
