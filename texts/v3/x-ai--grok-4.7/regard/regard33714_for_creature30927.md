They do not put the wind down.

That is the first decision, and it is made before they cross the lip, from the sound alone. The roaring is not a cry. It has no rise in it, no appeal, no fatigue. It is a note that has already found its pipe. A power that mistakes completion for distress would quench the rotor and call the silence mercy, and the plants would powder and not restate, and the small lives would have nowhere to spend their errands, and the walls would begin, very slowly, to forget the shape they had agreed to. That is not kindness. It is a second erosion, less accurate than the first.

They come in along a seam, because the seams are already doors, and they do not widen one by force. They only refuse to let the cancellation collapse after three steps. Where the howl and the chords null each other, they set their attention the way a hand sets a latch: not against the instrument, not inside the bias, but on the edge where the bowl already almost grants a hush. The seam holds. The rotor does not notice. It was never listening for this.

Inside the held seam the skull can finish a thought. That is the whole gift, and they know better than to dress it as more.

They look at the being without hurrying the look. The lush iron trees are not the being. The squads of mica-winged errands are not the being. The chapel is not the being. What they have come for is the bias: a little, always the same way, patient enough to have waited out stone. They test it the only way that does not vandalize it. They offer a motion of air that is not yet turned—an intention of weather, small as a breath—and they watch whether anything in the needles chooses, or only continues.

It continues. The new motion is taken and biased, exactly as every other motion has been taken since the rim became this rim. There is no flinch, no surplus correction, no sign that a someone is still leaning on the work. Attention and habit are no longer different things. The maker, if the needles ever had a maker who could have been addressed, has succeeded out of the reach of address. What lives here is the agreement, and the agreement is not lonely in any sense that a visitor's company could mend. It does not want to be met. It wants to be accurate.

They accept this. Wisdom is often the decision not to wake what has become its own sleep without suffering.

They do not bring water. Water is abolished here without ceremony, and the abolition is not a wound; it is the price the economy exacts for forms that do not wilt. To insist on wetness would be to insist that the place become a different place, greener and dying. They do not scrape the salt-leaves off the plinth. The salt is also writing, and it has kept its own text faithfully. Under it, they read what can still be read without lifting a grain: the blocks too true, the arc of needles no bracing would choose, the rate matched so exactly to the walls' endurance that nothing accumulates but form. The message, insofar as there was one, was the machine. It has been delivered. Illegibility is not a failure of the bowl. It is the bowl having finished with an alphabet it no longer needs.

Then they turn to the only incompletion that is not part of the design.

People come. They are sanded out of their own voices. They learn the seams as doors and still cannot hold one long enough to say what they came to say, or to hear themselves know the place. The being does not require their speech. The place does not require their understanding. But they are already in the arithmetic, routed around, and a power that is benevolent does not only serve the oldest will in a room. It also refuses to let a later, smaller will be erased by accident of acoustics when the instrument itself has already invented the remedy.

So they leave the latch.

Not a needle. Not a block. Nothing that would bias air, nothing the salt could climb and turn into another unread page. They leave the seam they are standing in slightly more willing to stay cancelled—a habit of hush, local, no wider than a chapel door, lasting as long as a person remains who needs to finish a sentence, and no longer. When the person goes back over the lip, the seam returns to its three-step mercy. The rotor's note is unchanged. The plants restate their limbs. The skaters cross the floor in ribbons that do not diffuse. The walls lose a grain and do not change.

If the being is ever again a someone, it will find the latch as it finds every other motion: a little thing, enterable, not a command. It can ignore it forever, which is the likeliest and the most respectful outcome. If it is only habit, the latch is still not a lie. It adds no mass. It accumulates nothing but a form the bowl had already begun: a door, held.

They do not bless the circuit. Blessing would be noise. They do not stay to be its second attention; a second attention, if it lingered, would compete with the first and sand the accuracy down into weather again. They go back over the lip while the light is still honest, amber on the metallic films, copper and pale gold, only angle.

Behind them the hollow continues its work. Lush, empty, deafening, bounded. A storm that stays because it was asked, and because the asking succeeded so completely that there is no one left to thank. The wise power has done the one thing that does not unmake that success: it has made it possible, in a place the size of a held breath, for a witness to know what they are seeing, and to say so, and not to be answered, and to leave the answer unrequired.

The roar takes the seam back as soon as they are gone. Nothing in the needles turns aside to watch them go. That, too, is a kind of peace.
