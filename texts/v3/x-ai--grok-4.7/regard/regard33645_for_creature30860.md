The attention does not arrive. It is already the temperature of the room, and has been, for as long as the room has been a room. A power that needed to come from elsewhere would already have failed the place.

It does not brighten the panel. It does not speak into the low thread of pressure behind the ridge. It does not lift the sac out of the band that is killing it slowly and also, for now, letting it turn. Wisdom here is mostly a refusal. The pocket is exact because nothing has yet been kind to it in the way kindness usually means: carried, sheltered, explained, made to last. Lasting would be the first violence. A thicker glass, a cooler weep, a soil for the film to root in — any of these would blur the edges the lives are made of. The ash-thread's whole intelligence is a correction along a line that exists only while the nicks stay sharp and the light keeps the film awake. Give it a longer country and the correction becomes a habit, and the habit a species, and the species a thing that can be harmed in ways this interval has not yet invented. The power has seen that happen. It does not begin it here.

So it does not save them.

What it does is smaller than a drop, and only once.

At the end of one circuit, the sinter nipple is a grain from plugging the nick that feeds the richest line. The ash-thread has stalled where the flex of the panel shifted the film a hair to the left. It is in the middle of the correction — joint locked, underside taking on the iron-orange, body not yet committed to the return. In any ordinary next moment the nick seals, the line starves, and the correction completes into nothing. The power holds the plug back by the width of that hair. Not by adding stone or stealing time from the ceiling's tick. By declining, for the length of the stall, to let the deposit finish the work it has already almost finished. The creature corrects. It takes the film. It starts back along the span. Then the power lets the nipple rise, and the nick closes, and the path begins, properly, to dry.

That is the whole intervention. One finished correction that would otherwise have been interrupted by the room's own precision. No second chance. No teaching the plates how to cross dry floor. The thread returns to a lip that will not feed it again, and mats, as the flake is matting, and becomes grit. The power does not keep the grit.

For the sac, it does nothing even of that size. The hot band is not a prison the power is obliged to open. The creature's coat of sinter is how it has already joined the room's deposit; to strip the coat or move the water would be to unmake the solution in order to pity it. The power lets the knot turn until the turning is only heat. It does not vent the pool. It does not promise the clearer thread rising through the surface a larger dark to escape into.

For the beads on the ridge — the third, the habit that may not be a life — it does less than nothing. It does not decide. A wise power can tell a metabolism from a trick of young glass and wet charge, and can also tell when the telling would be an extraction. The beads tighten when the panel brightens and loosen when the light oils away. The power attends the difference and does not sort it. When the panel finally crazes, the held water falls with the rest, and whether something has ended or only a position has been forgotten is left unresolved. Resolution would have required a name, and a name would have required the power to take the beads out of the interval and into its own memory as a kind. It declines the kind.

What remains is the staying, and the staying is not a force the stone can register. The second light fails. The bands remember how to mix. Heat soaks into the wall at last, correctly, dully. The three nipples of sinter dull the points that grew them. The passage is still only a failed shelf. Weather continues on the crust above the lid. The room becomes what it was always going to be once the glass forgot how to be a lens: a damp hollow, unremarkable, dark enough.

The power remains through that, which changes no measurement. Benevolence, arriving too late to be useful and too early to be grief, is sometimes only this — that the ending is undergone in the presence of an attention that does not correct it, improve it, or carry a scrap of it elsewhere to be better kept. The correction on the lip was finished. That was enough to have done. The rest of the pocket's exactness is allowed to complete into ordinary stone, unwitnessed by any eye the room would have had to pay for, and unrescued.

Nothing is written on the flake. Nothing is left in the basin but water learning, at last, to be only water. The power does not mark the place so it can be found again. A hollow that has been found again is already a different room.

If it does anything after that, it is outside, on the crust that was rock this morning, and it is only the weather's own work, untouched.
