They would not come in.

Not at first, and perhaps not in any way the place could register as an arrival. A power that is both wise and kind already knows how much damage is done by the well-meaning entrance: the light that flattens, the hand that rearranges, the mercy that is only a cleaner form of occupation. Attention, for such a power, is not a spotlight. It is a long sitting-with, at the scale the place already uses, until the difference between witness and weather is smaller than the place’s own noise.

So they would listen until the listening itself learned the local grammar. If the being who lives there is vast and process-shaped, they would not ask it to become a person in order to be cared for. If it is small and afraid, they would not enlarge it into a symbol. They would learn what, in that life, already counts as enough: the interval that must not be shortened, the hunger that is not a mistake, the loneliness that is the price of a shape the being spent ages choosing. Benevolence that skips this step is only taste with better manners.

Then they would ask, in whatever register could be received without rupture, whether anything was wanted. Not “how may I improve you.” Not “I have seen a better version.” A question with a real door in it, including the door that leads back to being left alone. Wisdom is not offended by refusal. Most of what is wise in the world has survived by refusing the offers that would have made it legible.

If nothing was asked, they would do nothing that showed. They might steady one seam the place was already trying to hold—an erosion that had crossed from finishing into mere harm, a cold that had stopped being climate and become injury, a repetition that no longer printed anything but pain. They would do it the way water is already doing its work: locally, without credit, without requiring the being to notice a savior and reorganize its life around gratitude. A wise kindness is often indistinguishable, afterward, from luck the place can claim as its own.

If something was asked, they would give only that, and not the adjacent improvements that make a gift into a colonization. A way out, if a way out was wanted, and no lecture at the threshold. A little more time, if time was the wound. Company, if company could be borne—company that does not translate the being into the power’s language in order to love it. They would not burn the strange industry of the place down to a garden because gardens are easier to bless. They would not call a crowded, difficult, ongoing life a prison simply because it does not resemble rest.

And they would leave the power of refusal intact. That is the part the eager miracles always spend first. A benevolent power that cannot tolerate being unnecessary is not yet wise; it is only lonely, and loneliness in a god is how worlds get rearranged into prayers for their maker. So they would remain available and unentered, a kindness with its hands visible and empty, until the place, or the being, or neither, turned. If neither turned, they would count that as success. Attention is not a debt. Some lives are already in session, and the most loving act is to let the session continue, unimproved, unafraid of being seen, and not alone in the only sense that matters: known, and not corrected for it.
