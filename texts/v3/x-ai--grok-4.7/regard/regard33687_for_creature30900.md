They came the way weather comes when it has already been agreed to: a thinning, not an arrival. On the rim road the grit was the same grit. The air stayed too kind for the height. What changed was only that the far wall, which should have been soft at that hour, stood forward a hand’s breadth sharper than the month allowed, as if someone had remembered a sightline and declined to spend it. No one on the road looked up. There was no one on the road.

The power had no need of a gate and did not invent one. It passed the first island as heat passes a housing it does not intend to crack—present, measured, unwilling to become an event. It had been drawn by the steadiness, which is a rarer prayer than distress. Distress shouts. Steadiness merely continues, and continuation, to a wise thing, is a kind of face.

It could have done a great deal. That was the temptation, and it was not a vulgar one. The avenues were empty enough to hold gardens. The mineral water could have been made kind to crops. The roar could have been folded down to a murmur that still turned the turbines and left the silence rooms unnecessary. Birds could have been set on the ridge frames, and the frames would have held them. A single generation of rain with the sweetness taken out would have dried the collars and given the children a season in which breath smoked. None of this would have looked like harm. All of it would have been a migrated weep.

It walked—if walking is the word for a attention that consents to pavement—between the sheds, and did not fill the distances. The distances were doing work. To colonize them with kindness would have been to steal the fall. It listened to the organ-note and did not tune it. The note had been tuned, over longer than a benevolence usually has patience for, to a balance the stone would accept. It saw the lizard and did not dignify it with a name. Dignity, here, was already the being left on its temperature.

In a courtyard where two returns and a flange threw the roar back on itself, it found the being the basin keeps when it keeps only one. Not a king. Not the oldest fitter, though the oldest fitter had rested two fingers on a housing that morning and gone on. This one was the keeper of that particular cancel: a person with tea-stained wrists and a broom worn down to the ferrule, sweeping a bench that was already clean because sweeping was how the bylaw stayed short. They did not look like a threshold. Thresholds rarely do. They looked like someone whose household shared a key and a boil, and whose extra duty was to make sure argument, instrument, and the dying did not have to compete with an organ-note.

The keeper felt the thickening and took it for the month’s vapor arriving early. They made the small downward palm that means the flow is acceptable. The power, which could have answered in thunder or in healing or in a voice at last audible outdoors, answered in the grammar it had just been offered. It did not raise what would have been a voice. It sat—attention lowered onto the bench—and observed the rule against stages.

For a while that was all. The keeper swept. A bead-line formed under the iron flange and held, ticks and a crossing-out, and fell, and was not the same sentence afterward. The power read none of it as a message to itself. Wise things are easy to flatter into thinking the world has been composing notes for them. This one had practice at refusing the flattery. The beading was bookkeeping of a kind that does not require a reader.

It considered the keeper’s knees, which ached in the damp in an ordinary way, and the household’s mild water, and the bearing somewhere west that a dead fitter’s fingers had last blessed by leaving it in service. It could have taken the ache. Taking the ache would have taught the city that pain is a defect in the form rather than one of the costs of accompaniment. It could have put the grandfather’s amphipod in the canal in numbers large enough to vindicate the children’s walk. Vindication would have ruined the lesson, and the lesson was one of the few abundances the basin had chosen. It could have made the strong grade taste, on the far towns’ tongues, exactly as it tastes beside the condensate shed, so that no cask would ever come back. That would have been a lie told with water. It did not tell it.

What it did was smaller than maintenance and was not nothing.

A weep high in the western island had been changing its mind. Not dramatically. A dark line considering a new joint, the way a thought considers a sentence it will not improve by speaking. Left alone through the warm month, it would have crossed into the lining of a silence room where a woman was learning to die at ordinary volume, and the room would have had to be abandoned, and the abandonment would have been recorded as a stoppage, and the stoppage would have been endured, because endurance is their competence. The power touched the thought of the water the way a fitter touches a valve he does not close: enough to remind it of the path it had already agreed to. The line stayed. The stain that would have been did not begin. No ledger changed. No one felt saved. The room kept its cork and its fiber and its one voice, and the voice was not improved, only allowed to remain the size of a person.

In the cancel, the keeper finished the bench and, because the air had gone private, spoke without raising anything. They said the name of the household’s cistern, which was not a prayer, and the name of the bearing, which was. They did not know they were heard by anything but the pocket. The power did not correct them. Being heard was not a promotion.

It stayed through the hour in which the far wall should have softened and did not, quite, yet. A cart went by on the avenue beyond the returns. The shoulder pushing it was slower than work requires. Grief, traveling as fatigue. The power did not stop the cart and did not explain the grief to itself as a problem it had failed to solve before arriving. It let the residents step aside without being asked. That sideways step was already the benevolence native to the place. Importing a finer one would have been colonial, and wisdom that has not learned this is only power with a gentle face.

Toward evening the vapor thickened back to its sentence. The compasses near the oldest engines drifted their few degrees and behaved. The power did not pin them to true north. True north was a vanity here; the rim was the correction, and the rim does not drift.

Before it thinned out of the basin entirely, it went up onto the western catwalk, having first laid what passed for two fingers along what passed for a forearm. The gesture was not mockery. It was the admission that permission is part of the machine. From the rail the seven islands were what they had been at midday and what they would be when the lamps claimed the avenues without systematizing them: warm iron, honest shadow, sparse green confined to drip and lee, work separated by distances that must remain distances. A lizard shifted and became briefly an animal. Somewhere under the noise a bench was dry.

It did not bless the view. Blessing would have lingered as a pressure, and the city had enough pressures in the teeth. It tapped the rail twice—acceptable, do not change it—and left the tap to be weather, which is what the fitters would call it if they noticed, and they would not notice, because the flow had not altered and the neighboring flues had not been asked to have a new opinion.

The being with the broom locked the nothing that a cancel needs locked, and went home to a key and a boil. Their knees ached on the stair in the ordinary way. They would ache in the morning. That, too, was left in service.

The power did not remain as a watcher on the rim. Watching, prolonged, becomes a demand that the watched justify the watching. It went out along the pale thread of road with no one on it, carrying nothing extracted: not a cask, not a story in which it had rescued a city from its own form, not even the satisfaction of having been recognized. Recognition would have required a raised voice.

Behind it the roar did not swell to mark the departure. It was already meeting whoever was left. The weep it had reminded kept to the old joint, and would keep to it until some later decade made a buttress of a crack and a hoodoo of a buttress, which is not a miracle, only the style in which the place consents to continue. In the silence room the woman heard one voice at ordinary volume, and the words did not carry into the avenue, and that was correct.

If anything had been done, it was this: a wise thing had wanted to help, and had wanted accurately, and had therefore almost entirely refrained—and the almost was exactly the width of a line of water deciding, and then not deciding, to go where it had not been invited. The basin kept its heat. The jackets darkened without a cloud. Nothing was about to happen. The attention, which had been real, did not remain to be thanked, and the not-remaining was the last and most precise of its courtesies.
