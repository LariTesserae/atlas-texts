It did not come down the air. The air was already occupied, every register spoken for. A presence that arrived as wind would have been heard as weather, and weather here is a claim, and a false weather kills. It came instead as a steadiness in the light that was not more light. The glare did not increase. Edges did not sharpen. What changed was that the light stopped spending itself entirely on distance and spent a little of itself on form. Seams were seen. Grooves were seen. The pale puff of a runner’s breath was seen before the wind took it. That seeing had weight, the way a drummer has weight, and the nearest towers answered before anything decided to answer: their notes centered, not louder, as if a hand had been laid on a singing glass not to stop it but to find the pitch.

The power was benevolent, and therefore tempted. It was wise, and therefore slow. The first thing it did was nothing.

The temptation was water. Benevolence that has stood near thirst imagines thirst wherever it finds dryness. A lower world still remembered rain, and the memory rose at once, generous, disastrous. To rain on this rind would have been to translate a whole nation into a language it could not speak and call the translation mercy. The chemistry here was oils, resins, pressurized gases, gels bound so tightly they never loosened into anything that could spill. A wound clouded and skinned. A mouth was a rasp. The files lived on lacquer-flake and bead-skin. One hour of true rain would have swollen the seam-lips, choked the galleries, drowned the tick of the domes, and turned the organ-notes to rot. The power looked at the bound water already inside the living and set the idea of rain down. It did not leave a kindness of drizzle behind. Refusal, complete, was the first act.

It looked then for the being.

It was wise enough not to look for a face. It looked for where decisions pooled. There was no pool. A fan that had lost a blade retimed the remaining ones until the weather downwind could not tell. A file that lost its mender broke into solitary walkers. A body of the right mass kept a tower in the chord; a body of the wrong mass starved in a drum that no longer fed it, and the column went on ringing wrong until the scour took it. The power passed through tower after tower and did not anoint the drummer. The drummer was not a ruler. It was a correct weight. To crown a weight is to misunderstand a scale.

If there was a person here, it was not any single body. It was the fit, repeated across the lit distance until it had a climate and a history of stumps. A distributed being cannot be saved in one place without being damaged in the others. The power had learned, elsewhere, not to lift one life out of a reef and call that love. It did not elect a runner, or a mender, or a waiter at a door, or a young climber going up the outside of a tower in the folded quiet, to be the soul of the country. The soul was the continuance. To pick a favorite would have been to stop understanding the place at the exact moment of touching it.

So it searched for suffering that was not function.

The strike at the slots was function: a door that sometimes shut, a violence shorter than a chase, edited out of the air in minutes. The stumps were not wounds. They were the landscape’s memory of towers that had shadowed fans too long, polished now, inhabited by gliders who preferred the dead note. The emptiness was not a lack of life. It was the terms. The noise was not a cry. It was production, worn into balance, deafening because it never stopped. The power moved across a day of pavement and found nothing to heal that was not already the way of lasting. It nearly concluded that wisdom was to leave without having touched anything, and to let the leaving be clean.

Then it found the silence that was still widening.

Not a scar yet. A tract where the metabolism had stalled, where the dust was not lifting, where the cool was deepening toward the frost the resins cannot cross and still return from. Neighboring fans were already hauling. The repair-wind was coming, carrying dust and seed-scale and the small bodies that ride a gust whether they mean to or not. But the silence had a head start, and the power measured the head start as the place measured everything: by the missing tick, by the tightening of fiber toward a brittleness from which there is no lacquer back. The wind would arrive. It would arrive late. Late, here, was the death of a patch of floor. The continent keeps its failures as furniture, and that is just. This one was not yet a failure. It was a race being lost by a margin smaller than a fan’s own correction.

The power did not send rain. It did not raise a tower. It did not call the animals, who would have bunched, and been punished for bunching. It lent the tract a little time, which is the only foreign substance that does not poison this chemistry. It stood in the silence and was warm in a way altitude is not warm: not heat enough to lift a false wind, not heat enough to become weather, only heat enough that in that hour the resins did not cross into the frost they could not survive. An hour. Perhaps less. Then the arriving dust did the rest. Abrasion woke the plates. The hum returned, thin, then industrial, then deafening again. The silence closed like a scar of recovery rather than a dead tract. The power was already gone before the returning note could include it.

It did not stay to be thanked. Nothing here thanks, and a gratitude that had to be invented would have been another false weather. To remain would have been to become a tone. The runners, standing up out of their grooves in the dark, would have filed it—a warmth that was not a tower, a pure note with no pulse of a drummer under it—and in the morning their grooves would have bent, a little, toward a standing presence. The night map would have held a god. A god in a map is a destination. Destinations gather bodies. The wind punishes what makes a sail of itself. The last benevolence was absence restored, restored completely, with no shrine of brighter dust left in the lee of the hour.

What it did was three things, and they were one thing. It refused the mercy that would have been a drowning. It refused the crown that would have been a lie about where the being lived. It spent an hour of itself as unremarkable warmth so that a silence could close on the continent’s own terms, by the continent’s own wind, and be credited to no one. Then it withdrew its attention. The light went back to spending itself on distance. The towers, released from the steadiness, resumed their small individual errors of pitch. A fan clapped. A slot-rider banked. A file of stitchers moved along a lip, lead animals, squatters, a mender laying wax the plant would accept.

The place had not known it was seen. That, too, was a kindness. Witness that announces itself becomes a climate, and this climate was already finished. The power left the engine running: dry, bright, sparse in every body, total in its floor, loud enough to make a permanent weather of its own voice. It did not add a gentleness the forms could not use. It loved the fit by not interrupting it, except once, and only by as much time as kept a living rind from becoming furniture before its repair had arrived.

Elsewhere, perhaps, a wise power builds, or teaches, or stays. Here the benevolent act was measured to the scale of a margin, and then to the scale of a withdrawal, and then to nothing at all. The dust on the seams was salt and wax again. There was nothing to drink. There was nowhere to go that was not more of the same. And that was enough, and the power, having understood that it was enough, did not improve it.
