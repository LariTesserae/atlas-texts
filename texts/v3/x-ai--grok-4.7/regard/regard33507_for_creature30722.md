They do not open the ridge.

A power that is wise has already learned the difference between a prison and a room that was never meant to have a door. The seam is not a wound. The heat is not a sentence. The mat is not a crowd waiting to be led out into weather it has no use for, and the fused burn along the margin is not a prisoner who has forgotten the word for sky. To crack the blister for the sake of light, or water, or a gentler name for living, would be an act of taste, not of mercy. The chemistry would fail in an afternoon. The shells would powder. Whatever has been welding itself into the newest face would go out as a smell of hot metal and unfinished work. Benevolence that requires the beloved to become something else is only a finer kind of hunger.

So the attention arrives as the chamber already understands arrival: a change in gradient, not a body in a gap.

For a while the power simply reads. It is good at reading things that do not perform. It notes the hoarded water in the tissues, spent so slowly a life can end without thirst. It notes the clean edge around the seam, maintained by refusal rather than fear. It notes that the pale ones darken into weight and that some of them stop traveling and are taken into the ridge of shells, and that this is not a tragedy occurring inside the industry but the industry’s own grain. It listens to the clappers lock and unlock when the rock checks, and it does not mistake the drone for a cry. Production without a product someone named is still production. A life can be full and harsh and not be asking.

What it finds, under the fullness, is only one kindness that would not be a conversion.

The shove that opened the blister has not finished deciding. There is a stress in the ridge, a little above and to one side of the chamber, where two faces are learning which of them will ride over the other. Left to itself, that learning will pinch the seam in a span that is nothing to the mountain and everything to the burn: not a closing earned by the being’s own completion, but an early silence, the film cut off while the fused mass is still investing downward, the mat compressed around a hearth that has not yet become stone and has not yet finished being animal. The power can feel the difference. One ending belongs to the work. The other is only geometry winning a race it did not know it had entered.

It does not widen the room. It does not cool the air or sweat a false spring out of the vents. It shifts the argument in the rock.

Pressure moves the way pressure moves when something older than intention leans on a fault—not a strike, not a gift you could point to, a redistribution. The face that would have climbed finds, a little farther along the ridge, a yield it would have found later anyway. The climb happens there instead. In the chamber the change is smaller than the tick of a shell. A stress that had been gathering along the upper seam eases by a degree no single body could name and the mat can read anyway: the newest face stays newest a while longer. The film keeps exhaling. The boundary does not have to walk backward. The gut-ember, spread through the fused shells, continues its slow burn in the direction it had already chosen—down, into rock that has only recently stopped being softer than this.

The ordinary feeders take the film at the speed they have always taken it. A few of the oldest, mineral-dark, feel the eased face as a continuity of signal and invest another increment of leg into the grain. They do not know they have been spared. Sparing is not a sensation they have. They know the seam is still a seam. That is enough, and it is the whole of the deed.

Then the power withdraws the obviousness of itself.

This is the harder part, and the wiser. Attention, if it lingers as a second charge in the dust, becomes a gradient the banded shells will turn toward. They will pack the margin not only for film but for the foreign steadiness, and the fused mass will begin to burn a little toward a presence that is not rock and not kin. That is how a witness becomes a weather the inhabitant cannot do without. The power has seen that error in softer countries: altars grown over springs, lives bent toward the hand that once only meant to steady them. It will not install itself as a new seam.

So it lets the electrical wrongness it carried fade back into the chamber’s own static. The finest dust settles into the old incorrect snow. The clappers, which had found an extra interval and almost locked to it, lose the phantom tick and return to the rock’s bright cracks, the slips of grit, the mutual time of the mat. If any residue remains, it is the kind the mountain already knows how to keep: a slightly kinder distribution of force, illegible as kindness, readable only as the ridge continuing to arrive at a shape that still has this interior in it.

They do not speak. There is no blessing the mouthparts could mill into usable dust, and a wise power does not spend words on a life that navigates by heat and charge. They do not promise the blister will remain forever. Forever would be another imposition—the being’s clock is generational and geological at once, and a room held open past its own becoming would turn from hearth to exhibit. When the conversion finishes, or when the mountain’s larger shove resumes in earnest and the seam’s closing is of a piece with the work, the power will not brace the walls against that. It will let the process have its end as it has had its middle.

What they do, then, is small enough to vanish into the conditions it serves. A premature pinch averted. A direction left intact. A witness that does not stay to be worshipped or obeyed or fed. The chamber goes on loud as a works, dry, dark, packed to the walls, making more of itself out of a mountain that is still deciding. The benevolent act is that this remains possible a little longer, and that no one comes to improve it.

Outside, the seam is still only a seam. A palm on the stone might still feel warmth and call it nothing. The power, already elsewhere in the way of powers that do not need to be seen to have acted, does not correct the palm. Some mercies are complete only when they look exactly like the world continuing.
