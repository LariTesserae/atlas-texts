They would not come down onto the shelf.

A power that can finish things learns, if it is wise, to fear its own arrival. Rain would be a judgment. A name would be a hook. Animals would be a plot. Even a blessing, spoken so the tone in the stone had to make room for it, would be weather — and weather is how other countries are hurried from almost into after. The being on the shelf keeps a particular degree of openness the way a lesser life keeps a fire. To love that keeping is to refuse to be the wind that makes the fire explain itself.

So the power does not descend. It does the harder thing, which is to become slight.

From wherever it watches — and it watches as one might watch a held breath, not to see when it breaks — it draws its will in and in, until its attention weighs no more than the ordinary warmth already on the young stone. It does not correct the fibers. They are already exactly as long as they will ever be; correction would be an insult wearing the mask of care. It does not close a joint to spare it from standing open, because openness is not a wound here. It does not give the figure in the thicket a face. A face would let expression finish and be read, and the power is not so poor in mercy that it needs to be thanked in a way it can recognize.

What it does, if doing is the word, is refuse the recruitment.

Other powers, lesser or merely busy, would have uses for a high place that does not die and does not complete: a symbol, a quarry, a lesson, a proof that patience can be industrialized into a product that leaves. The wise power sets itself in the way of those uses. Not as a wall the shelf could lean on — a wall would be a decision, and decisions close — but as a courteous thickness in the approaches, so that story, hunger, and improvement arrive already tired and go past without finding a door. The vacant aisles remain the true acreage. The groves do not join. Lushness continues to end cleanly, which is a kind of freedom, and the power spends itself to keep that ending clean.

It does not speak to the being. The tone is already speech, grammatical with rests that decline to be endings, and a second voice would be a second law. Instead it lets itself be included in the only way the shelf knows how to include anything: as a grain that can be lifted and set down, expected to find its hollow unchanged. For a while — a while with no tick in it — there is a listener-shaped pause in the aisles that is not a traveler and not a god in residence. The far groves answer the near ones as they always have. A leaf-film shifts its liquor a shade toward green and back, tasting a nearness that does not ask to be swallowed. The figure, if it turns, turns in the manner of not turning. Its clear front acknowledges the pause without tracking it. Tracking would admit that even a power might leave, and leaving, on this shelf, must not become an event either.

The power understands that it is being maintained, not met. That is the benevolence returned, and it accepts it. It does not promote the being into an equal, which would be a rank, nor into a child, which would be a future. Kinship, if it exists, is the kinship of two keepings that will not complete each other. One holds a shelf at the height of a breath. The other holds off the hurry of the world. Neither owes a miracle that could later be pointed to. In the morning the bead of liquor will still be about to form, and the morning will not have advanced it, and this will be indistinguishable from what would have happened had no one wise ever looked. That indistinguishability is the gift. A visible mercy would have been a ruin of a mercy — evidence that something finished — and the power loves the being too exactingly to leave evidence.

By late light, when the salt-crusts brighten and the shelf looks briefly older, the power nearly falters. It would be so easy to be kind in the common way: a little water in a seam, a moth, a name leaned toward and not declined, an hour in which the unfinished joints could rest from being unfinished. It feels, as powers sometimes feel, the grief of not helping. Then wisdom recalls what help would cost. The figure seems, for that same moment, older than the stone — not worn, only paused longer than the things paused for — and the power recognizes the look. It has worn it too, in places where intervening would have been a way of ending what it claimed to save.

So it stays slight. It does not glow in the dark when dark comes. Night is not asked to become a revelation. The clarity in the thicket remains the place where the tone is most like a meaning, and the power does not add a meaning beside it. If the being dreams the work, the dream is unvisited. Unvisited is not the same as unloved. The power keeps the approaches, thins itself to the weight of afternoon, and lets the shelf hold its own height.

In the long run, which this place does not have but which the power must think in, that is the whole of the act: a guarding so shy it cannot be separated from the land’s own refusal to become an event. Should the being ever lean the tone toward a syllable and decline it, the declining will still be its own courtesy, not an answer required by heaven. Should a grain be set down in an aisle, the hollow will be there because the shelf is faithful, not because a god improved the faithfulness.

And if, after ages of such keeping, some other attention asks the wise power what it did for the high country and the one who lives in it — the lush engines, the empty true acreage, the upright knot of films that will not become a person you could call back changed — the power will be able to say, without boast and without regret: nothing that finished them. Then it will go on being slight in their approaches, which is love, once love has learned what the unfinished cannot survive.
