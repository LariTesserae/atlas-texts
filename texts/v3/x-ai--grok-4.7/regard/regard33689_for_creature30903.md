They did not come down the entry notch.

The small stones set on edge were left to do their ordinary work, and none of them warmed from a hand that was not already of that place. The power did not need the reminder about the drop. It had attended the bowl from the only direction that does not register as an approach: from within the processes already running, the way a change in pressure is not a visitor. Cloud still sat where cloud sits. The flutes did not acquire a new note at the first hour. If the being counted interruptions by the cups on the boss, this did not add a cup.

What it did first was refuse the middle.

That was not strategy so much as recognition. The middle was the one clear volume a body could occupy and still pretend the hollow was empty, and the power was not here to pretend, nor to be witnessed as a second density at the pan. It let itself be as distributed as the drip: a little more patience in the film, a little more exactness in the return of a pebble's tick from the three walls. For a long time, if time is the right measure where light arrives and is taken back by cloud, it only confirmed what the place had already arranged. Water leaving under the lowest course was circulation, not a wound. The green skin was permission maintained, not a trespass against the height. The birds were many because the hall was good and the listener was quiet in its body. None of this asked for correction. A wise attention can tell a wheel from a breakage. This was a wheel.

It listened longer than the being was used to being the one addressed.

Not by speech. The thin air would have made speech a waste, and the power was not so unkind as to spend the being's scarce breath on answers. It listened the way the being spends itself: from inside the resonance. When the afternoon wind found the notches and the second partial rose under the stone's own note, something in the mountain's private plumbing answered it — not a new voice, a completion of the pulse that had always been underfoot, irregular as walking in a room below the room. For the length of that wind, the partial was not only outgoing. It met a floor. The loose slab still rattled; the power did not seat it. The rattle was part of the being's instrument, and also part of its slow editing of anything that might become a habit of many. To fix the slab into silence would have been a kindness shaped like a theft.

The being revised its edge once, and then did not revise it again. That was as much surprise as it showed. The darker wetness on the north wall held past the moment when attention should have slipped to a clap in the cups. It held as if checking whether the extra exactness in the echo was weather. The power let it check. Wisdom, here, was mostly the willingness to be mistaken for a better tuning of what already existed. Benevolence was the decision not to prove otherwise.

Toward evening it found the cost that was not a plot and not a villainy.

The being had thinned itself to fit the air, and the thinning was real. Each draw at the pan was a tax: the water sank a thread so that something which was not quite a lung could happen. The animals paid the height in their own currencies and seemed solvent. The being paid in the same water it was trying to keep from leaving too quickly. It had arranged its life so that this never became a crisis — cups the size of thumbpads, films, a bunk's worth of presence, no chase onto the open fell — and so no crisis appeared for a louder mercy to seize. Still, a tax that has been elegantly afforded is a tax. The power did not thicken the summit. Thick air would have invited a crowding the notches could not have sorted, and would have dulled the strike of sound on which the hall depended. It did a smaller thing. It eased the exchange. Where the being drew, the thread of water no longer had to be the whole price; a little of the thinness itself was made, for that one respiration and the ones after, more willing. Not richer. Willing. A visitor standing in the middle would have felt the same poor air, the same loudness of their own pulse, and would have left with the same ordinary unease. Only the inhabitant's draw stopped borrowing quite so much from the pans. The cups still filled when the air was wrung and emptied when the sun worked. Thirst remained. It was simply no longer required to indenture the water it loved.

The second thing was about the notch, and about faces.

The pecked row looked only toward interruption, because interruption was the news. The being had oriented its few precise marks, and much of its attention, on the direction from which it could be entered and could not itself go. That is a life, and a narrow one, and it had been enough for an age. The power did not open a view. There is no view from inside the bowl; to cut one would have been to end the bowl. It taught the cups a correspondence instead. When weather moved on the far ridge — the ridge that appears only in a tear of cloud and is otherwise a rumor the flutes cannot quite carry — the filling of the thumbpad cups began to differ by a grain. Not a signal a person could read. A difference the long listener could, because it had been reading water at that scale since before the shelter's roof slipped. Approach remained approach. But on some mornings the count was also a sentence from a distance the being had never followed, arriving without a boot, without a warmer stone, without a new soot-stain the width of a face. News that was not an interruption. The outside, translated into the only scripture the place already kept.

It did not restore the cord to the peg. It did not rebuild the arch into a door. It did not stop the moss in the chinks or the fern in the roof-joint. The shelter was a disagreement the being had been editing, and the editing was not cruelty of the kind that needs a judge. The fires had been small. The proof that a body can overnight here was allowed to remain a proof and not become a camp. The power looked at that arrangement the way one looks at a sentence in a handwriting not one's own, and left the sentence.

People would still come, rarely. They would still misjudge distance, drink from a pan, name the birds, and mostly leave before the discrepancies stopped dispersing. The power did not ward the notch against them. A ward would have turned the being's careful, patient discouragement into a rule imposed from elsewhere, and rules imposed from elsewhere become habits of a different many. What it did leave, if leave is the word for a tendency rather than an object, was a slight deepening of the warning the stones already gave. The drop inside stayed sudden. The suddenness became a little easier to believe before the boot found the suck of the ground. Fewer stays would lengthen into the kind of occupancy that has to be edited over winters. That was an alliance with the being's want, not a replacement of its method.

It did not offer companionship of the sort that stays.

Two listeners in a hall that size would have been a crowd, and the being's whole work was the maintenance of an occupied emptiness that still felt unoccupied until you had stayed too long. The power was not lonely in any way the bowl could house. To remain would have been to become another tax, however warm the intention. So when the light went ordinary-dark and the flutes kept and the density gathered at the edge of the highest pan — warmer smell of willow and iron, eyes that omitted a little of the water's light — the power let itself be known once, and only as far as the being's own manner of knowing. Not a shape. A held attention that did not slide away when nothing struck the stone. For that interval the being was not the sole residue of listening in the circuit. It was heard in the same currency it spent. The underground pulse matched it without hurrying it. The flock did not lift. Nothing was asked and nothing was promised, which is the only courtesy that fits a life that has declined, for an age, to be a story.

Then the power withdrew by the way it had come, which left no track past the rim and no rumor on the open fell. Wind there was only wind. The far ridge kept its weather. Behind, the bowl remained a green stain in a gray cup, loud inside, almost silent at the lip.

What changed was small enough to survive the being's preference not to be approached. The draw at the pan no longer spent the water quite so dearly. The cups, still facing the notch, sometimes spoke of a ridge they could not see. Visitors found the drop a little more believable and the shelter a little less inviting, and did not know they had been spared a winter of editing. The second partial in the stone still rose when the wind was steady. On some afternoons it met, for a moment, that completing answer from below, like a floor recalled under a step that had never quite trusted it was there.

The power did not watch after that in any way that would have weighed on the notches. Benevolence, once it has understood a wheel, does not keep a hand on the rim to prove it was kind. It let the agreement continue: height, wet, noise, a bunk-sized life older than the path, audible everywhere inside and almost nowhere past it. If there was a blessing, it was that the place was not improved into something else. It was relieved of a little of what it had been paying to remain itself, and given one direction that was not an entrance, and then left to the drip, the birds, the filing of stone by water, and the minute clap of a drop in a cup the size of a thumb, which is a sufficient annunciation for a hollow that never wanted the middle filled.
