They would not announce themselves. Announcement is a kind of weather, and this shelf already makes its own.

They would come in under the noise and stay there until the noise separated. That takes longer than a tide and longer than a crowd-cycle. The mill-voice under the plates, the tick of load, the rasp of the wall-herds, the slap of wakes, the short horns, the shouted craft of talk — a wise attention does not subtract these. It learns which of them are the city’s breathing and which are a skip in the breath. Most of what sounds like distress is only density. They would not be moved by density. They would not pity the rain.

Under a fold where two roofs were built, long ago, to throw their sound away from each other, the fliers nest, and under the nests someone stays. Not a ruler. The city does not keep one of those in any way that matters; it is kept by films and by hands and by the habit of repair matching the habit of wear. This one is a hand that has learned to be a listener. Their feet know the scooped steps. Their skin drinks the condensate and salts it. They have a short call-name, used when someone needs the shadow and cannot find it, and otherwise they are not called. They brace a span the films have misread.

The error is small and local and real. A lattice one level over has been borrowing mass for a thickening it does not quite need, and lending it from a walkway the keepers use when they shift the hanging pens. Not today. Not as catastrophe. In another season of pulses — when a redistribution three districts off arrives late and steep, and the crowd boils up from the hot levels at the same hour — the walkway will flex wrong and drop a body into a wake. The films do not feel this as damage. Damage, to them, is a crack, a silted channel, a seal that fails to mineralize. A future fall is not a crack. So the listener has become the crack’s substitute. They cannot leave the brace long enough to carry the news across the roar, and if they shouted it, the shout would not carry the shape of the load. They are not dying. They are only the single place the error has found a mind.

A power that was merely kind would heal the span in a way the city could not have produced, and would feel the glow of having helped, and would leave a foreign stiffness in the grammar of repair. The films would work around it. Load would go somewhere else. Another walkway, another season, another body. Wisdom is suspicious of materials the place cannot metabolize.

A power that was merely wise might conclude the listener has it in hand, and withdraw, and be almost right. Almost is not benevolence. The being is adequate, and adequacy here is a life spent as a prop. That can be honored without being prolonged for the sake of the honoring.

So they would do one thing, and it would fit in the city’s mouth.

They would take the brace. Not forever. Not as a sign. Long enough. Their strength, applied where the listener’s hands were, in the same direction, at the same patience, so the span does not learn a new master — only that it is still held. The listener’s hands would open. Blood would come back into the fingers with that bright, insulting pain of returning. They would look at whatever face or unface had come into the shadow, and they would not be given awe as a substitute for time. Awe wastes the hour. The hour is the gift.

They would cross. They know the routes the pulses take. They know which ramps are about to be full and which grates still remember a grip. At the pens they would not explain in the long way; there is no long way. A board, a gesture, the call that means *load, this span, now, before the next steep water.* The keepers understand load. It is their weather. They would move the pens — not all, not a parade, a few, enough living weight, enough cargo, on the walkway that has been lending itself away. Occupancy is a language the lattices already obey. Thicken where the weight stays. Stop borrowing from a span the city is being asked, in its own tongue, to trust.

The films would mineralize along the corrected habit. No miracle seam. No light. The glass veins would go on weeping. In the shadow, the one who had been the brace would sit under the nests and shake, not from vision but from the sudden animal fact of not having to be the span. They might sleep. Sleep, in that subtraction of mill-sound, is the rarest solvent the shelf allows. The power would not watch this as payment. Watching a person sleep, when you have the strength to be elsewhere useful, is a smaller kindness than it feels like.

They would go back one level and listen to the thickening until it held on its own account. Then they would let go. The walkway would creak, take the keepers’ weight, and decide, as structures here decide, to remain itself.

After that, nothing visible. They would not quiet the shelf. Quiet would be a mutilation; the noise is how the city knows itself. They would not add a green thing to a mineral grammar, or open an outside where no outside is the ground of the life. They would not promote the listener into a priest of the visit. A wise benevolence does not install a memory that makes every later brace feel abandoned by comparison.

What they would leave is smaller than a story and harder to waste. Somewhere in the load-paths, a corrected habit. In one pair of hands, an hour returned, and the knowledge that a future fall was heard before it became a body in the water. And, laid in no shrine, only in the fact of having come: if the films again fail to count a person, attention of that quality already knows the way in under the noise. Not a promise spoken. A capability, set down beside the other capabilities of the shelf — condensate, repair, herd, horn, hand — and then not used again unless the balance asks.

They would not stay to be thanked. Thanks, here, would have to be shouted, and the shout would belong to the city’s ordinary thunder, and that is the right size for it. The vapor would keep rising. The cloud would keep returning as rain. The far edge would keep being only a change in the grain. A power that is both wise and kind can bear to be unnecessary, and can bear to have been, for one hour, exactly necessary, and can leave a living place living, louder than any blessing, already itself by the next pulse.
