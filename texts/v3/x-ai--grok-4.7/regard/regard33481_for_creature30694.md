They do not come down the ridge. Descent would be a kind of argument, and the place has already had every argument it can use.

What arrives is a change in the period of attention. The gauges do not jump. The herds do not lift their heads. A bell still runs ahead of itself along the grain of the air. But the long pulse under the boots—the one the night shift tells from the banked fire by a hand on a casing—acquires, for a while, a listener who is not trying to become part of the count. The engineers would call it a second instrument on the same line. The place would not call it anything. It has never required its witnesses to announce themselves.

The power is wise enough to see the wound that is not there. Nothing here is asking to be finished. The unfinished crest is not a lack; it is the work. The lost hour is not a theft; it is the toll the air takes for carrying sound along its grain, and the people who stay have already paid it and kept the smell of hot metal as proof of payment, not of injury. The children on the undecided lips are not in danger in the way a lower country means danger. They are performing an office. To snatch them back onto certified stone would be to teach them that weight is a law rather than a courtesy, and the ridge would have to spend seasons unteaching what a moment of kindness had done.

So the power does not stabilize the maps. A corrected map would be a lie with better ink. It does not pin the benchmarks harder. It does not make the upward rain fall down, or teach the moths to outlive their heating, or name the body in the condenser sheds so that a log could hold it. Naming would be a smaller cage than a jar, and the geologist has already learned what jars do here. It does not fill the darkness below the far hut with a valley ahead of schedule. Rivers that are still preparing themselves are not late.

What it does, because benevolence is not the same as refusal to act, is widen a margin that was already almost wide enough.

The three sentences in the gatehouses remain three. They are not rewritten into mercy, which would be too long and would revise itself by morning. But a person who walks into the thickness of air and does not wish to be found on the lower switchback can, now, decline the hour. Not by force. By the same attention the child uses on a lip: a refusal that is also a reading. Those who decline are set back on the street they left, dry, on time, with no smell they cannot account for and no story the shift will need them to forget. The procedure still works. It has merely grown a side door that does not look like a door, the way the city grows storeys.

Those who do not decline—who continue, and arrive damp, and stay—are not mourned from below as taken. The power touches the lower country only there, at the boundary, where embarrassment was doing the work of a wall. Relief crews may still leave. If they leave, they leave with the hour recorded as weather, not as failure. If they remain, no one in the country they left is left holding a grief shaped like a disappearance. The ridge does not become easier to enter. It becomes possible to exit without the exit being read as a verdict on the life that stayed.

For the being that is not separate from any of this—the habit of completing itself through whoever will move at its speed—the power offers no promotion and no pardon. It does not lift the being out of the extrusion and set it on a finished mountain where plumb is a fact. That would be kindness of the sort that kills a slow animal by carrying it to a warm room. It looks, instead, the way the man in the far hut looks at the stone as it surfaces: long enough to receive it, not long enough to make it hurry. Under that looking, the being is not healed, because it was not sick, and not praised, because praise would be another whistle occupying a note until the note became architecture. It is accompanied at the correct speed. The accompaniment leaves no relic. In the morning the pins have still walked. The press is still finishing a descent it began before the last upward rain. The dog, or the animal that sleeps where a dog would, still lengthens in certain dimnesses, and the man still does not look up.

If you stood on the open reach and tried to see the power, you would see the same iron-colored leak of light, the same cables, the same grove no wider than a house. You might notice that a gutter which had been about to reverse its fall waits one breath longer, so that a child crossing it does not have to choose between balance and laughter. You might notice nothing. Both are accurate. Wisdom, here, is the decision not to be heavier than the ground has agreed to hold, and benevolence is the decision to spend that lightness on the exits, the margins, and the right to remain unrescued.

The city keeps its noise. The stone keeps its tightness. Somewhere a lamp carries a small, localized correctness the length of a gutter, and the power does not widen it into a street. It lets the seam hold for exactly as long as the seam was going to hold, and then it lets the air remember itself, and the walker, if he has not declined, is on the lower path, damp, already late, already included, and no wiser hand is on his shoulder to tell him he has been saved.
