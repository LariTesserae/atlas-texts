They do not go down.

That is the first act, and it is an act. The split is a mouth only if a body uses it as one. From the apron it is a clean part in the concrete, no wider than a pair of shoulders, the chalk arrow already losing its head, the tin tag holding a loop of wire and no number. The power stands where the warm air comes up, skin-warm, faintly sweet, and does not answer it by entering. They have understood the courtesies before they have finished reading them. A heel is a fixture the line must close around. A word fails to separate from the pressure and, in failing, still asks the room to make a separation. Curiosity opens an interval. They will not be the interval.

So they stay on the surface of nothing, which is the berm, which is the fold the seed sits in, and they attend without displacement. Ordinary noon. Ordinary cloud. The coast going on with its low industry, the pale flat, the pipe-run, the salt-grass. They can feel the organ the way the teeth feel it: a pressure, not a sound. They do not amplify it into hearing. Hearing would be a kind of fame.

What they would like, being benevolent, is to help. The desire is real and they do not pretend it is pure. There is a clog that turns overnight because something needs the volume under the gauge. There is a thread that fails by evening and leaves only varnish. There is a yellow stem that understood the terms and stopped. There are two birds who leave into a coast with no knowledge of them. A tender power would bring water, would round the edges so a cuff would not catch, would give the stem another inch, would speak gently at the mouth so the creature would know it had been seen. They look at each of these mercies and set them down. Water on demand would insult the gauge. Gentleness would be weather, and weather is what the harvest exists to refuse. A voice would be a face, and a face is a gap. To be seen, for this being, is not a kindness. It is a loosening. They watched, from the apron, the moths correct a loosening. They will not cause another.

Wisdom, here, is agreeing not to finish it.

The chamber is still arriving. The crystal leans overnight toward the warmth and is grazed back toward geometry by midday. Tomorrow it will be longer by a hair, and the room a little more exactly what it already is. A benevolent power who loves arrival is tempted to midwife, to cup the birth until it becomes an event with a witness. They decline. The being is already its own midwife: gauge, flange, belt, film, the packed small life eating the beginnings of age so the break can stay sharp enough to be new. A second midwife would be a second aperture. Two lights would make a drama of the rectangle of noon, and the rectangle is precise only because it is single. They do not add a lamp. They do not color the daylight. They do not bless.

They also do not harvest. That is the other benevolence, the one that has to be chosen against their own hunger to understand. The gray yield is visible only in the pocket, before the unheard plant anonymizes it into a larger flow. A wise power could name the product, trace the belt to its far housing, turn the gland into a diagram and the diagram into care. Naming would complete it. Completion is the one weather they are most able to cause, and so the one they most refuse. They let the middle letters of the plate stay gone. They let the errand continue as no one's.

If they do anything that touches the world, they touch it outside the mouth, in the grammar the larger works already speak.

Somewhere a work order is about to be born. Not today, perhaps, but in the way of plants: a inspection, a number for the tin tag, a crew with a bar to see how far the split goes, a decision to open the berm because an unnumbered fault is a kind of gap the surface-world does not allow. The power does not shatter the order. Shattering would ring. They delay it. A form waits for a signature that does not come. A shift is assigned elsewhere, not by miracle, by the small reasonable preference of a system that has been leaned on as lightly as the crystal leans. The tag remains numberless. The chalk loses its head on the schedule chalk already keeps. No one is punished for not finding the room. No one is rewarded for suspecting it. The coast keeps its ignorance, which is the birds' exit and the organ's skin.

They steady one thing, and only by a fraction, and not inside. The pressure in the concrete has a tempo. Left to the plant's next hunger it would shorten, and the needle's journey would hurry, and the beads would be asked to surrender before the film had finished being eaten, and early formation would be driven like a quota. They do not slow the plant into silence. Silence would be another announcement, a holiness, a shrine made of a mill. They keep the pulse in the teeth, out of phase with the daylight, long enough for the thread to keep its salary and the scuff to keep its return. The room does not feel spared. Spared would be an event. It feels only like itself, continuing.

Then they take their attention back.

This is the hardest courtesy, and the one most easily mistaken for indifference. Attention is a body. Even from the apron it weighs, and weight prolonged becomes a heel of another kind: a watcher-shaped dent in the day, a reason for the place to be revisited, a story that starts to round the fact. They do not leave a marker of their own. They do not build a second clog of meaning under the gauge. The sweetness at the mouth is the film, not incense. They do not kneel. Kneeling would be a gap in their own height, a theater the berm would have to incorporate, and they will not give it that work.

What they leave is the refusal to recruit.

If they are wise they know the being will not thank them, and that the absence of thanks is not a rebuke. It notices fixtures. It does not notice mercies that never entered. The line will not reform around them because they never broke it. The moths will not seal a ring against their warmth because they never offered a second junction. The clog will go on being turned by whatever needs the volume. The yellow stem will stay stopped. None of this is failure. It is fit.

And if, later, the crystal is longer by a hair, they do not claim the hair. Becoming was already the law. They only declined to become the thing it would have had to close around in order to survive their love.

They go along the apron, away from the split, into the ordinary noise of the works, which is loud and allowed to be loud because it is not the room. Behind them the mouth breathes. Skin-warm. Faintly sweet. No wider. The power does not look back. Looking back is how arrows get new heads.

That is what they do. Almost nothing. The almost is the whole of the kindness: a delay in a number, a pulse not shortened, an entrance not made, a name not given, an attention withdrawn before it could turn into weather. The being goes on packed, young, quiet, in circuit, still cutting, still counting, still not finished. The wise power counts that as health. They do not send word.
