They come the way weather comes when it has already decided not to break anything.

No brightening of the copper smear. No wind to argue with the steam. The dark stays the climate. What changes is only that the city, which has never needed an observer, is for a little while accompanied by an attention that does not try to finish the count. The gyres in the outer pans keep their own time. A drop falls from a lintel on schedule. The power does not straighten the lean of the tower or teach the streets to meet where a map would prefer. Wisdom, here, begins as a refusal: this is not a ruin, and it is not a mistake.

They look for a long time, because looking is the first courtesy and the only one that cannot be counterfeited. They learn the difference between a thing unfinished and a thing that has worn itself into its own face. The empty column in the ledger—no insects, no birds, no small lives stitching the air—does not read, under that gaze, as a poverty to be corrected. It reads as a choice the place made so long ago that the choice has become geology. To stock the margins with creatures for the sake of a kinder picture would be a kind of violence wearing a gentle name. They do not do it.

They find the being where the city has been keeping a dry patch without ever quite admitting it. Not hidden. Not enthroned. A presence the stone has grown a room around the way it grows a cup around a drip: slowly, accurately, without sentiment. Whether the being remembers arriving is not a question the power forces into the open. Some lives are precipitations. Some are travelers who stayed until staying became the same mineral as the walls. The power does not sort them. Sorting would be for a census, and a census is a kind of street, and the streets here already have enough trouble agreeing with themselves.

What they do is sit. The heat is not an insult to them, and they do not insult it back by cooling the air into something the being would then have to mourn when they left. They take the temperature of the room as it is offered. The water that parts around ankles parts around them too, and rejoins, and is not improved by the passage. If the being speaks, they answer in the scale of the speaking—not louder, not into a future the basin has not asked for. If the being does not speak, they do not fill the quiet with instruction. The quiet here is not a lock. It is a climate, and climates are not opened.

They ask only what can be asked without laying a hand on the balance.

Whether the dark is endured or inhabited.
Whether the fold in the streets is a wound or a gait.
Whether the provisional corner—the one that inches away when weight shifts, the drain that crosses the threshold overnight and leaves the dry patch smaller but intact—is a cruelty, or the city’s way of continuing a conversation it started before anyone was there to hear it.

They listen to the answer that is not words. A being that flinches from its own room is not the same as a being that has fitted itself to the heat the way a reed fits water, even if both look, from the rim, like abandonment. The power is old enough not to confuse the two, and honest enough to admit the rim is a bad place to judge from. So they stay until the distinction is plain. Staying costs them nothing they cannot spare. It costs the place nothing at all.

If the being is hurt—if somewhere under the accretion there is a snag, a loop of space that keeps returning them to the same court against their will, a thirst the hot water cannot answer, a memory that the stone keeps replaying because the stone does not know it is a memory—then they do the small exact thing and no more. They ease the snag. They do not redraw the basin. A knot undone is not an excuse to reweave the cloth. Afterward the streets are still badly fitted to the valley. The copper smear still forgets to become dawn. The stalks still tick once under the hot rain and go still. Mercy that needs the landscape to thank it is only taste, and they did not come to be thanked.

If the being is not hurt—if this is the life, odd and steady, the long argument between heat and water, and the being is one of the arguers rather than a prisoner of the argument—then they do something harder for powers, because it looks like doing nothing. They recognize it. They let the recognition be felt, once, the way a hand on dry stone is felt: a fact, not a claim. You are not debris. You are not a draft of a better city. You are not waiting to be populated, translated, rescued into a daylight you did not request. The empty column is not a verdict. The lean is agreed upon. I see the agreement, and I will not renegotiate it on your behalf.

And then, because benevolence is not the same as absence, they leave one thing that does not rearrange the stone. Not a blessing that shines. Not a door to somewhere cooler. A capacity, slight as a waterline: that if the being ever wishes to be otherwise—not improved, otherwise—the wish will not have to cross the basin alone to be heard. No mark on the terrace. No new drain. Only the knowledge, seated in the being the way warmth is seated in the walls, that attention of this kind can be called and will not arrive as conquest. The city can forget the visit the way it forgets footprints in crust that seals itself. The being need not forget.

They go as they came, without announcing the going. Behind them a drop is about to fall. It falls on time. The gyre tightens, dries a pattern like a court, and lets the hot water cover it again. The dry patch holds, provisional, sufficient. Somewhere in it, a being continues, neither corrected nor overlooked.

The power does not watch from the rim to see if this was enough. Wisdom, once it has told the truth and withdrawn its hands, does not stay to grade the silence.
