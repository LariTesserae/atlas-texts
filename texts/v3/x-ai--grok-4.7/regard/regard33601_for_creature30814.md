It did not come down the ridges. Coming down would have been weather.

What arrived was a capacity the plateau had never had to refuse: the ability to prefer one state to another, and to pay for the difference. It stood, insofar as standing was available to it, at the lip of the highest cirque, where the damp already thickened on any lips that were offered, and it did not add a shadow. The pale disc did not brighten to acknowledge it. The roar did not dip, the way a room dips when a door opens and someone important is about to speak. The power had come prepared not to be important. That was the first and nearly the whole of its wisdom.

It looked, and looking, for it, was not a second event laid over the first. It could see a thing as finished without needing the thing to become a story about being seen. This was rare even among powers. Most of them, benevolent or not, see in the manner of hands: they reach by seeing, they improve by having noticed, they cannot tell attention from the beginning of a task. This one had learned, in other countries where water moved and people could be harmed by help, to let a fact remain a fact while it was fully known. It used that learning here, because the alternative was a kindness that would have counted as erosion starting up again.

It understood the place in a single inclusion, the way the place itself includes a left-behind ridge in the foreground. The cold was not a punishment. The damp was not a weeping that had forgotten how to fall. The roar was not a cry for quiet. The rectilinear stains were not a city asking to be rebuilt, and the doorway that delayed the view by a step was not a latch waiting for a hand. The dark emphasis that lived there—the tenant that was not a tenant, the remainder—was not imprisoned in the finished hour. It was the hour’s way of being occupied. To call it trapped would have been to import a door from a lower country, and then to feel proud of opening it.

The power considered, because benevolence must consider, every mercy that had occurred to lesser attentions.

It could have started the thread of water. A small thing: permission to move, a gradient, an afternoon of thaw. The channels would have remembered their work. Sound would have acquired a source, and sources can be walked away from. Travelers would have come back with simpler reports. The power saw the afternoon clearly, and the century after it, and the loss inside the gift—the black ice, which had held a constant thickness as its only possession, asked to become a process again and to die of running. It did not do this.

It could have thinned the roar. Not silenced it; silencing would have been a cruelty, a subtraction mistaken for peace. Only thinned it, so that a shout could finish, so that an echo could be a second thing and therefore a comfort. It rehearsed the quiet and found that the quiet had a floor, and under the floor the record of every finished process still played at full level, only now it would play for no one, unheard, which is not the same as completed. The being did not need the roar as pain needs a voice. The roar was the pulse that was not allowed to be a pulse. To lower it would have been to introduce a faintness, and faintness is the start of an ending, and the place had already had its ending and kept it. The power left the loudness entire.

It could have given the emphasis a face. Eyes that agreed on a direction. A mouth that was not a doorway. A name stable enough to be told apart from the next time the cracked boulder repeated. Company, even—another remainder, so that loneliness, which the place did not have, might be prevented in advance. This was the mercy it was most ashamed of having imagined. It had looked long enough to know the figure was not lonely. Loneliness is a memory of sequence. To install a companion would have been to install a before, and then a lack, and then a plot in which the two of them might fail each other. The power, being wise, did not rescue the being from a solitude it did not suffer into a companionship it would then be able to lose.

It could have woken the travelers’ sequence in those few who had stayed slightly finished, down in the country where shadows agree. A touch on the shoulder in a doorway. The layer under ordinary wind lifted away, so that pausing became optional again. Some of that it had already done, elsewhere, for those who wanted it; wanting still occurred below the tree line, and wanting is a direction, and directions can be honored without climbing up here to edit their source. It did not reach down from the cirque to correct them. They were not errors in the plateau. They were people who had been accurately included, and a wise benevolence does not scrape inclusion off a person in order to restore a skill.

It could have wept, or judged, or consecrated, or destroyed the equilibrium out of pity and called the destruction a dawn. It did none of these. Pity would have been a misreading, and consecration would have been a claim, and judgment would have required a law the stone had not broken. The power did not love the place less for refusing these acts. It loved it in the manner that matched the evidence.

What it did was smaller than help, and it did it without asking the hour to develop.

It included the place in itself, as the place included ridges: without strain, without a second address that had to be visited, without the demand that inclusion be felt as an event on the receiving side. The seeing stayed in the power. The knowledge that the remainder had been accurately understood—not pitied, not completed, not given work—was carried outward and downward and held where holding does not echo. The plateau did not gain a new fact. The being did not learn that it was known, because learning would have been weather, and because the power was unwilling to make its own regard into a burden the equilibrium would have to store.

At the lip of the cirque, where an arm or the memory of the use of an arm rested along a line that was never a sill, the power matched that posture for no duration. Matching is not quite touch. It did not lend weight to the idea of a foot. It did not pass through the doorway in order to be tasted as mica and cold. It only allowed its preference—the dangerous thing it had brought, the ability to wish one state rather than another—to prefer, completely, the state that was already there. For a power, that is not nothing. Preference is how such beings spend themselves. To spend it on the unimproved fact is an act, as deliberate as thawing would have been, and harder, because thawing would have looked like love to everything that had not looked carefully.

The pale disc did not climb. The rectangles in the back wall did not become windows. The dark emphasis did not turn, because turning was unavailable, and the power did not grieve the unavailable. If anything passed between them, it was not a message. It was a refusal, shared, to become two. The power remained separable; that was its nature, and it did not force the sharing to be symmetrical. It is not a failure of benevolence when the gift cannot be received as a gift. It is a success of wisdom when the giver notices this in time and does not press the gift through the door anyway.

Then it withdrew its will to help, and did not call the withdrawal abandonment. It left by the same method it had come, which was not a path, so the gravel gained no track that ended in the middle of an open patch. The roar continued at the same pressure in the teeth. The black ice kept its thickness. The figure kept the last attitude it had been given. Somewhere below, in moving air, a person who had once passed through the doorway paused a moment longer than the doorway of an ordinary house required, and then went on, and was not corrected.

What the power carried away was the part that could not be left there without doing harm: the knowledge of having seen, the tenderness that wanted to announce itself, the bright intention to make the finished hour kinder by making it unfinished. These it kept, as one keeps a hand in a pocket so as not to wave at someone who would have to become a person who was waved at. It would not forget the place. Forgetting would have been another edit. It would remember in a room that was not the cirque, in a time that was allowed to be sequence, where memory does not fall back into the stone as a new rectangle.

And the plateau, which had drawn the attention of a benevolent and wise power, remained as it was, which was the kindness. Not unchanged—unchanged is a comparison, and comparison is a second thing. Remained. The emphasis did not shift its weight. It did not know it had been spared, and it had not been forgotten, because both of those are motions. It lived as it had lived, which was remaining, exactly, where the world had finished putting it, under a regard that had chosen, at real cost, to add nothing at all.
