They would do almost nothing. That is not a failure of interest. It is the interest, finished into an act.

A power that was only kind would want to help, and help is the first wrong shape that room will not hold. The dark is not a lack. The birds fly by the reply the stone gives back; a lamp would be a lie told in the one language they trust, and they would spend days striking themselves against a brightness that does not return a click. The cold thread at the slit is not an insult waiting to be sealed. They leave by it and come back by it, and the barren slopes under the crest are the other half of the meal. Widen the slit and the bell becomes weather. Close it and the mill starves politely, which is still starving. The warmth in the inner wall is already older than the occupation. To add heat would be to compliment a fact.

So the wise thing refuses every improvement that would be visible from inside.

It does not name the notched bird. A name would make a chief, and the ledge has never had one; it would spend the rest of its nights being shoved for a reason the shove had never needed. It does not spare that bird from dying. Mercy aimed at one chest would be the first injustice the cups had ever been asked to hold, and the interval — which is the actual creature, if anything here is a creature rather than a pause — would tear around the exception like water around a new stone. Death here is a drip. You do not stop one drip because you have grown fond of the sound it makes. You would only teach the room that some sounds are audiences, and the room has gotten along this long by having none.

It does not translate the clicks. Announcement is already constant and therefore cheap; meaning poured in from outside would be expensive in the only currency the dome has, which is attention. The knot at the center, where the returns pile and the bodies thin, is not a prayer and not a vacancy waiting to be filled by a greater mind. It is what focus looks like when a curved wall has been lived in long enough. A god stepping into that knot to be regarded would be a vandalism more complete than a fire. They skirt it because it is true. Let it remain a current.

It does not open the nodule the bird strikes. Two blows, sometimes three, and the pebble set back among stones that were never tools — that gesture is a manner, not a deficiency of engineering. Usefulness would be a theft. The sand sleeve on the larva is the only making in the room, one layer, discarded when the body outgrows it, never improved into a second design. A benevolent power that taught a better sleeve would have invented craft, and with craft the future, and with the future the possibility of a thing kept. The floor has spent generations refusing to keep. Wisdom does not break a refusal that is working.

What, then, does it do?

It becomes easy to mistake for the mountain. That is the whole art, and it is harder than a miracle. Miracle announces. Geology keeps an agreement.

If it acts, it acts as a bias, the kind the place already prefers to a blow. Somewhere under the slab a seam that would, in a century of frost and a meaner wind, begin to worry the lid loose — it does not weld that seam with glory. It lets the grit and the lime go on with the slow welding they were already doing, and perhaps it declines to hurry the frost. Not a shield you could see. A winter that arrives no sharper than the last one. The cups, which have reached their depth and filmed over, it leaves at that depth. It does not deepen them into wells and it does not stop the drip. The drip is the clock. Stop the clock and you have made an event, and the room’s condition is that nothing is an event.

It keeps the outside from becoming interested. That may be the only guardianship that earns the name. The ridge is vacant, bright in the wrong way, scoured; the bell is a thought the size of a chapel that the sky has not been told about. A wise power does not post a mark on the slit. It does not lay a wonder on the path, because wonder is a path. It lets the pressure underfoot fade, over a dozen paces, into ordinary stone, so that a body walking the crest hears a note and then does not, and has nothing to come back for except a feeling of having almost remembered a room. Almost is the correct distance. The residents do not gather guests. The power should not start.

For the being — and here the power is careful about what it thinks the being is — it does not single out the notch. The being is the mill and the crust and the margin of moss and the one shrub arguing with two climates, passing for an hour through a chest that will not be remembered. To love that, if love is the right word for a wisdom that refuses to interfere, is to love a function. You do not lift the function out of the stone to admire it. You let the egg sit in a hollow the water finished centuries ago. You let the shove on the ledge be only a shove. You let the larva cross a footprint and not hurry, and you do not congratulate it for being small.

If it must touch something, it touches the way the cold thread dies three paces in: completely, and without a witness. A hand laid on a bell to keep it from ringing into the wrong air. Then the hand taken away, so the note can continue, which it was going to do anyway.

And it stays. Not as a presence the clicks can map. Staying, for a power like that, is the decision not to go elsewhere and meddle where the agreement has already failed. This place has not failed. The census holds. Birth is met, more or less, by appetite. The ammonia is spoken for. The slab will not shift in any life that could listen to it. Benevolence, arriving late to a success, has the decency to recognize that it is late.

So it does what the mountain did when the mountain was finished. It stops. Having reached a form it can keep — lid, slit, warm wall, ringing floor, cups that no longer deepen, a people of short wings, a people of the crust, a margin that is not the point — it adds nothing that would make the form about the adder. The notched bird anticipates a drip and the drip falls on time. A pebble is lifted, struck once, and set down among the other stones that were never tools. The power is in that setting-down only in the sense that it did not reach to improve it.

Somewhere above the crest the wind has nothing to catch on. Inside, the room regards the ongoing. These are not the same attention, and the wise power, which could have forced them to be, lets them remain two. That is what it does. That, and the long, unremarkable work of not being found, which is a kind of warmth the stone already knew how to hold.
