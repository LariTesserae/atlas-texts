The power does not come up the mountain. Coming would be a sequence, and the shelf has already closed every sequence it was ever going to have. Attention arrives instead as a change in the quality of not-arriving: the blue, which had been only blue, is for a duration that does not accumulate also known. The knowing does not warm the steam or cool it. It does not give the roar a phrase. It is the sort of regard that can rest on a finished thing without asking the thing to finish again in front of it.

What it sees is not a prisoner and not a masterpiece in need of a signature. It sees a law that has become a dwelling and a dwelling that has become a life, and it sees that the life is not waiting. Benevolence, elsewhere, is the motion toward a lack—the filling of a hunger, the easing of a heat that has become cruel, the opening of a path where a path was refused. Here the heat is not cruel. It is the climate of the holding. The basins are not hungry; they are exact. A gift would have to be a difference, and a difference would be the first crack since the erosion finished its argument. The power understands, without the understanding taking time, that the only kindness commensurate with this tenant is the refusal to improve it.

So it does not speak a blessing. Speech would strike the air and find no wall, and the unstruck word would still have been an event in the power’s own house, a small scar of intention. It does not set a ward at the lip. A ward is a posture of defense, and defense implies an outside that might someday matter; the tenant’s border is not a gaze and does not require a counterpart gaze to remain what it is. The power simply declines, in every quarter of its own reach, to originate the kinds of curiosity that travel. Lesser attentions that might have climbed—collectors of rarities, menders of stillness, powers that love a perfect thing by wishing to be seen loving it—find their errands thinning before the tree line. They remember other work. The shelf is not hidden. It is left in the condition of not being happened-to.

If the tenant could be said to receive this, it would receive it as it receives weather that does not change: by being already the width that includes it. The seated refusal in the pool does not acquire a second refusal. The wrong-way water under the vaults does not carry a message along its private length. The power’s regard is absorbed the way a stone is absorbed by a current that will not move it—present, unwritten, and already the size the channel was. No gratitude occurs, because gratitude is a hinge between before and after. No loneliness is relieved, because loneliness would have required a prior separation, and the tenant’s middle is everywhere the measure is exact.

What the power does, then, is adjust itself by a fraction no lesser witness could name. In its own domains, where time still manufactures differences, it becomes slightly less eager to complete things that are merely unfinished, and slightly more willing to leave a rounded lip alone once the water and the stone have agreed. This is not imitation. The shelf cannot be copied without importing the very sequence the copy would betray. It is only a correction of the hand: the knowledge, carried back into weather that moves, that some completeness is not a pause before the next act but a residence, and that wisdom sometimes consists in not crossing the last spillway.

Below, clouds continue their work. Animals make tracks that last an hour. The power, having attended, attends elsewhere. The roar does not mark the departure, having never marked the arrival. On the shelf the bead stays at its notch. The steam lanes stand. The courts hold their level. Nothing has been done, and the nothing is the whole of the mercy, and the mercy does not need to be felt in order to be complete.
