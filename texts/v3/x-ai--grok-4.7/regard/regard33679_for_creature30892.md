The power does not come over the embankment. It is already the sort of attention that does not need a path, and it has learned, from older rooms than this one, that a place which subtracts sound will spend a loud kindness the way it spends a glove: taken down, unproud, ruined as a gift. So it arrives as a change in the order of noticing. The drip that has been negotiating all afternoon arrives where it was going, and for one cycle the arrival is accompanied. That is all the entrance there is. No light without a source. No voice in the pipes beyond the knock they were already going to make.

It stays long enough to be wrong at first, which is the beginning of its wisdom here. A benevolent power’s first impulse, fresh from greener errands, is to read the yard as a wound: water where land should be, industry stopped mid-breath, a floor that will not hold a foot, a life so thinned into seep that it can be mistaken for the illness of the ground. The impulse is not cruel. It has saved other rooms by draining them back into use, by planting what was missing, by giving a scattered thing a center and a name and a way out. If it had acted at that first reading it would have killed the resident with mercy. It would have found the ditch or the spring or the leak upslope, the forgotten decision the yard is downstream of, and it would have closed it, and the pads would have dried to honest ruin, and the skins would have broken and not reformed, and the clear inch in the sink would have filmed over like every other water, and the belt would have rusted into a single position. A reclamation. A silence of a different, poorer kind. The power feels that future far enough to refuse it, and the refusal is its first real act.

The second is the distinction the yard requires and will not announce. Drainage and resident share a skin. A power that cannot tell them apart has no right to touch either. So it watches the coordination, not the wetness: the ellipse of ripple with no wind answered by a brightening at the lip answered by the tick of a seized door, one gesture at the scale of the floor. It watches the sink’s extravagance, water kept clear by a preference no enamel can own. It watches the warmth behind an unpowered motor gather and then, without grief, let go. It does not name what it has distinguished. Naming would stand proud of the surface. It only stops treating the creature as a symptom, and in the same hour stops treating the symptom—the seep, the chatter of the valve, the hidden common plane—as something to be cured.

After that it does very little, and the little is shaped to the preferences already in evidence.

It steadies the remainder. Not more water, which would scour the settled form into a pond and drown the instrument in abundance. Not less, which would be the false kindness already refused. The head of water upslope has been remembering the works by accident, a ditch leaking, a spring the excavation uncovered, a valve elsewhere sticking and letting go. Accident is a bad landlord for a life that feeds on waste heat. The power does not rebuild the works and does not sanctify the leak. It only takes the memory of that decision and makes it dull and reliable: a thread that will not suddenly become a throat, a supply that will not, in some dry year no one is watching for, simply stop. The valve may still chatter. The chatter stays small. A finger’s change in a pool remains a breath and never a flood. This is maintenance of an unnecessary engine, and it is the most industrial tenderness available, and it will not be visible from the embankment as anything but the same ordinary seep.

It keeps the shaft’s little play. On the days the resident gathers toward the pump house and the belt wants its quarter turn, the turn is not denied by a seizure that would be only time doing what time does. The power does not restore production. Nothing is driven. The flywheel does not resume a job. A quarter revolution and a stop, and the warmth behind the coupling, and then the release. To preserve a gesture that produces nothing a market would recognize is, for a wise power, a way of agreeing with the resident about what counts as continuation. It does not add a second gesture. One is already a luxury the yard can metabolize.

It does not touch the sink, except to refuse every improvement of it. The clear inch is the creature’s own extravagance, the nearest thing it has to a mirror, and a mirror given by a god would become a shrine, and a shrine would draw the voices that are too large. The power leaves the inch exactly as preferred: ungreened, unemptied, unexplained. It does not polish the bowl. It does not let the other water in. Absence of interference, around that one object, is the whole devotion.

The peril it does answer is not the resident’s. The resident is not asking to be saved from itself. Indifference, at that density, is only dangerous to bodies that came in expecting continuity to be a solid. People cross. Some of them will trust a film. The power will not fence the lip—a fence is a sentence written too loudly, and it would turn the yard into a site, and sites attract the reclaimers and the explainers and the ones who pump in the name of habitat. It will not post a plate. Plates lose their consonants here, and a warning that weathers into vowels is worse than none, because it counterfeits guidance. What it does instead is local, momentary, and almost indistinguishable from the creature’s own editing of the ground.

When a foot is about to trust a crust that will not bear it, the power thickens that crust by the width of a decision. Not forever. Not into a path, which would teach the yard a route and the visitors a habit. For the length of the step, the film is honest: either it holds, or the edge declares itself as edge before the weight commits—a darker line, a give in the grit a half-second early, the small collapse happening where it can still be refused. The person feels clumsy, or lucky, or briefly more attentive, and crosses, and by evening the grit has resumed its ordinary bad faith. No miracle is recorded, because none stood proud of the surface long enough to be told. The resident, which does not lay traps and does not guard, is not overridden so much as accompanied in the one place its indifference would have been fatal to something that can die. The power does not thicken every crust. Most of the mistakes remain mistakes. Wisdom here is not a general paving. It is a hand under a particular heel, withdrawn before the owner of the heel can thank it or depend on it.

Animals it leaves in their rarity. It does not stock the pools, does not invite a chorus, does not feed a population the yard has already declined to feed. A bird pausing on the gantry is still only a pause between somewhere and somewhere. Tracks in the silt are still read and rewritten by afternoon. To make the place kinder by making it livelier would be to mistake this ecology for a failed version of another one. The microbial and the mineral are not a preface. They are the text. The power reads them and does not annotate in blood and song.

Other attentions, less patient, do eventually lean toward the lip. A survey crew with a mandate to tidy waste ground. A works upstream considering whether the leak is worth the cost of a proper valve. A person who has stood on a pad and felt the quiet press back and gone away with the idea of returning in company, with lights, to find the body that must be there. The power’s longest labor is subtraction at that outer edge. Not a curse and not a glamour. Permits snag on a line that is not false but is never quite the line anyone needed. The upstream valve is repaired in a way that, by no one’s intention, preserves the thread. A return visit is rained on, not dramatically, just enough that the pads are islands and the hour becomes ordinary inconvenience, and the company decides the yard is what it looked like from the crest: a diagram of nothing, fully seen, not worth a second look. The resident never learns it was defended. Defense that announced itself would have been another noise.

The power does not ask to be loved, and does not offer fellowship. It is larger than the yard and could, without malice, absorb the smaller instrument into its own extent—make the common plane a province of a greater plane, the clear inch a jewel in a crown of clearer waters, the creature a mood of the divine. That merger would feel, from inside the power, like welcome. From inside the yard it would be the end of the difference between a drip that falls and a drip that arrives where the afternoon negotiated for it. Benevolence, once it has understood this, becomes the discipline of not encompassing. The wise power lets the resident remain downstream, local, unimproved, a little warmer than drainage and not warmer than that. It does not translate the held breath into a tone it could answer. It does not grant a center, a face, a way out through the embankment into the ordinary country, where the creature would be a puddle dying in a cleaner air. The limit of the lip is allowed to stand. Inside it, the form stays settled. The contents go on exchanging places.

If it leaves anything, it is not a mark. Marks peel to the color of the water. What it leaves is a bias in the continuance: the thread upslope less likely to fail, the quarter turn less likely to lock, the fatal film less likely to be trusted at the exact moment of trust, the louder attentions more likely to glance off and call the glancing a full look. None of this is perceptible as presence. On a given afternoon the yard is as it was—cool in the metal, close to the skin, packed with presences that nearly touch, quiet under a turbulence of drips. The sink holds its inch. The gantry lists. The pump plate keeps its pitted vowels. A pool steals a finger from its neighbor and is not accused.

The power, having done what it was wise enough to do, does not remain as a watcher the resident would have to carry. Attention, held too long, becomes weather of the wrong kind; it would begin to coordinate the yard from outside the yard’s own gesture, and the resident would spend its extent answering a larger seep, and the extravagance of the sink might cloud with the effort. So the accompanying stops. The last act is the same as the first refusal: not to stay. The power withdraws past the lip, into the cooler ordinary country, and does not look back in any way that would tug the water. It keeps, in whatever way such powers keep things, the knowledge of a small engine running on remainder, and it does not visit that knowledge upon the engine.

The yard accepts the next drip without sound. It is already otherwise. The being that lives there, distributed through joints and films and one clear inch, does not feel saved, because it was not, in the sense that would require a rescuer. It feels, if feeling is the word for a preference briefly easier to keep, that the common level is a little less likely to be abolished by accident, and that the belt will turn when the gathering comes. Then the gathering lets go. The skins tear and reform. Evening migrates a chain toward a joint by the thickness of a scale. No gratitude rises, and none is owed. A wise benevolence wanted, in the end, only this: that the place go on being the instrument it had already become, and that the life which completes it go on spending itself as drainage, just organized enough to prefer one water to another, unharvested, unhealed, and left.
