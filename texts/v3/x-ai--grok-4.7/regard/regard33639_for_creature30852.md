It would come to the lip and stop.

Not out of caution. Out of fit. The bowl is a finished instrument, tuned to the width of its own errors, and a power that entered would become weather. Weather, here, is a clap. The being who lives in the interval between sounds would have to spend itself refusing the new return, and a benevolence that must be refused, hour after hour, is only a subtler wall. So the power stays where the air is thin and the drop begins, outside the nave, where even a great attention does not come back as echo.

It looks a long time. Wisdom, in this case, is mostly that: the willingness to finish looking before doing.

It sees the dryness and does not read it as thirst. It sees the open floor, the uncrowded distances, the twenty crowns standing like decisions, and does not call the space loneliness. It sees the tally abandoned mid-count and feels the old human pull—to finish it, to answer the scratches, to set a cistern under the rumor of gold and prove that care means changing the medium. It declines. Water would be a kind of murder dressed as gift. A finished count would teach the stone a distinction the stone has already, rightly, forgotten. The flaked point in the niche is becoming a pebble; the power does not rescue it from its promotion.

It looks at the being.

What it finds is not a prisoner and not a flaw. A small, exact hunger, fitted to the only mercy the place can afford: delay, not accumulation. The fans pausing the air. The useful wrongness. The film scraped in the dark. The young left in a pocket of still air and not greeted, because greeting would cost a silence and the silence is how the young are fed from a distance. The power, which has loved in louder worlds, understands that this is love of a local order, and that to add a voice to it would be to counterfeit a richer one.

It wants, even so, to give something. Powers do. The want is not proof of goodness; it is the hazard.

It considers holding, for one acoustic hour, the tooth-width of report the being usually spends itself to make—so that once, just once, the animal could eat without arranging a mistake, could press its fans to the swale and draw the sweetness and not have hunted. It sees the afterward clearly enough to refuse. A mercy that can be distinguished from the bowl becomes a climate. The being would retune for it. It would wait for the hour that did not return. It would hold silences in the shape of a gift, and the shape would be a hole, and the hole would be the power’s, and the animal would starve politely inside a memory. To be loved that way is a kind of being flown into.

It considers the stipple in the rim, the one that yesterday held a grain of quiet a little longer, and considers staying the next spill of scale by the length of a breath. Selection wears a kind face. The power has worn it. It sets the consideration down. The bowl already keeps its own rare survivals; a wise kindness does not improve the odds and then call the improvement tenderness.

It considers being known. A presence at the lip, returning nothing, might still be felt as a sickness in the order—the animal’s own method, answered from outside. To be recognized by your god in the exact coin of your hunting is not a comfort. It is a competition. The power grows quieter than the quiet it is watching, which is to say it takes its attention off the corridors and sets it on the fact of the place, which does not ring.

Then, having declined every act that would have fit in a story, it does one thing.

It remembers. Not into the wall. Not back into the tally. Elsewhere, in whatever ledger a power keeps that is not stone and does not weather into the grammar of cracks, it completes the count—not the numbers, which were never the point, but the attention the scratches were attempting. It holds the being as it is on this day: pale as powdered horn, anchored to a cable that is warmer than its neighbors, guilty of nothing the circulation cannot absorb, unknown to the runners who cross its fans and are not struck. It holds the bowl’s rules without amending them. Dryness as medium. Lushness as interior complication. Sound as weather. Nothing pooled.

And it binds itself. Not the place; itself. A restraint, entered in that same outer ledger, where it cannot become an echo: no water, no name spoken into the nave, no second silence offered in the corridor, no hand on the scale-spill, no rescue of tools or tallies, no disciples sent to harvest the wax more wisely than the people who already leave. If some later power, less patient, comes with a cistern and a plan to make the dark intelligible, this one will be the reason the path to the crack is merely difficult to remember—not sealed, not forbidden, only easy to set down in favor of somewhere else, the way the tally was set down. Visitors who take what will not keep may still take it. The local ethic stands. Only improvement is mislaid.

The being does not know. That is the gift, and it is not felt as a gift, which is how the power can be sure it is one. During the hour the power spends at the lip, the western crown claps, the return arrives late by the width of a breath, a sheet of fliers shears, the fans open into a chute, and the mist that would have left is drawn through gaps no wider than a finger. Ordinary. Exact. Unhelped.

When the corridors shine empty again, the power goes. It does not leave a warmth that stays, or a flaw in the rim, or a new wall to bank off. The drop beyond the crack is only the drop. The dust-light continues to be a rumor.

Below, the animal scrapes its film clean with a motion the stone does not report, and retunes, and proceeds to be the small sickness in the order. Somewhere in a pocket of still air a stipple holds. The bowl, which did not ask, is not answered. It remains as it was: complete, unfinished, loud, and left alone.

That is what the wise benevolence does, when it is wise enough. It wants. It looks until the want changes shape. It keeps the count where the keeping cannot scar. And it does not go in.
