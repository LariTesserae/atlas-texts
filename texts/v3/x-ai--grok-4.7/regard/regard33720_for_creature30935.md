The power does not open a ceiling. There is no sky it is willing to invent, and the creature, tilted in the open hollow with its blind pits aimed at nothing, is not asking for one. That tilt is a check of the whole volume, a tasting of whether the room is still the room. To answer it with light would be to misunderstand the only question the fringe knows how to ask.

It does not still the pump. The roar is weather, clock, and road. Silence would be a kind of death, and then, later, a different death for the streets above the grate. It does not put eyes in the clouded pits so the porthole can become a view. It does not call. It does not multiply the body into company the ration cannot feed. It does not lift the pale length out through a bolted throat and lay it in a tank where the water is kind and finished. Kindness of that sort would be a glove on the rail, a foreign frequency, a revision.

What it does is smaller than wonder, and it takes longer.

It learns the stack the way the creature learned it. The low mill. The polite hiss. The tick of the bearing that has been deciding, for years, whether to go on being a clock or to become an event. The half-note that loads before the sluice shams. The ochre veil. The stall under the beam where a ridged plate is pushed, always, a little high, and the current has made a road of the injury.

The bearing it touches only enough to postpone the decision. Not healed into quiet — quiet would be a landmark removed. The tick remains, trustworthy, a little further from seizure. Years added to a complaint. The creature will never know that a future in which the pocket is drained, or scalded, or opened to wrenches for a week, has been set down and not picked up. It will only keep steering by a sound that does not fail on a day it cannot afford.

The stall it thickens by a hair. Not a harbor. Not safety. The cross-current still walks toward the bell, and the bell still does not negotiate, and the intelligence that knows the difference is left employed. But the bias of the damaged side becomes a road that does not, on the bad minute, fray into the pull. The creature will unpeel and cross open water and cup the jointed flick and lock its hooks on the rail, and the slam that once caught it in the open will not catch it that way again. One plate stays ridged. Wisdom does not sand the record off. It only declines to write the same sentence on the other side.

When men come, it does not hide the animal better than the animal hides itself. The plates already know how to be wall. What it hides is the evidence that would become a work order: the organic stain read as contamination, the felt that is food read as fouling, the body that would photograph, under a better lamp, as a problem to be flushed. The porthole shows industrial dark and crust. A valve is touched and thought better of. A treatment meant for this throat is sent down a different main, for no reason anyone will be able to file. Not forever. Forever would be a second government inside the first. Long enough. The long middle, extended by one more middle.

It leaves a thread in the water that is not winter leaking from a gallery and not pulp and not the sweet wrongness of anything that can be eaten. The fringe finds it once, in the deceptive glide, and the body hangs a moment longer than habit in the yards of nothing, reading a pressure that is almost a palm and is not a summons. There is nothing to do with it. No signal worth making. The mill loads — a fraction early, which is the whole of the mercy — and the creature turns, unlovely, exact, and is under the beam before the veil rises, slit open, feeding as the walls deposit, by being where the stall is rich.

It incorporates the change as it incorporated the old injury: as a road, not as a gift. It does not thank. Thanking would be a voice, and the pocket does not keep voices.

The power, being wise, does not remain to be tasted twice. A sweetness that stayed would become another landmark, and then a need, and then a fear of its absence, and the stack is already as many clocks as a life can carry. It goes out the way the ochre goes, beaten back into the general dim. What it leaves behind is not a presence. It is a bearing that will tick through another decade of slams, a stall that holds, a flush that went elsewhere, an early note the creature will trust without naming.

Above, on the particular corner, the grate answers the sluice as it always has. No one looks down. No one is saved upstairs. The pocket keeps its roar, its emptiness that is a lie, its one pale citizen of a room that is all interior. The violence that forms the place continues. The violence that would have spent the inhabitant, in one bad minute or one efficient afternoon, does not.

That is what it does. Then it does nothing, which is the harder kindness, and the one that lasts.
