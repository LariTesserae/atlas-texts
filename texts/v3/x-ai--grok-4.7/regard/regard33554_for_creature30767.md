They did not come down the roads. The roads are how the interruption arrives, and the interruption already has its pace: a season of flinching, a generation of digestion, a few lamps struck and regretted. A wise power does not stand in that queue.

They also did not come as weather. Weather here is already spoken for. A cell that does not belong to a hall's heat would be read, within a day, as a hall that had changed its note, and the files would turn toward the lie, and the turning would be a wall of meat somewhere it should not be. Benevolence that cannot be parsed is only another predator. They knew that before they were close enough for the deck to wet them. Knowing it was the larger part of what they did.

So they came in under the sound, the way the long friction comes, and they did not add a tone.

The shelf did not notice them as a visitor. The skin noticed them as a chemistry. Where they attended, the film sorted a little faster and a little cleaner, not because they commanded it but because patience recognizes patience and spends itself more willingly in company. Downstream of that attention the wrong smell thinned by a degree no crew would have named and every herd would have tasted. Files that had been drifting toward a richer, dirtier lee corrected without breaking meter. A calf born in that season learned a cleaner bed as the old bed, and did not know it had been given anything. That was the scale they allowed themselves at first. A gift that arrives already inside the grammar is not an announcement. It does not re-mark the upper storey. It does not draw the heavy hunter off its ridge.

They listened for a long time. Long, here, meaning longer than a crew's recitation and shorter than the life of a master-channel. They learned which rests in the chord were weather and which were warning, and they did not confuse the two, which is the commonest failure of the kind. The abandoned halls with their slow unmanned turbines they left alone. Those were already a completed sentence. To start a pump again so that an old note might return would have been nostalgia, and nostalgia is a wrongness in a bearing: it feels like care in the palm and arrives in the valley as a lie. The herds that had redrawn the song were not bereaved. They were current. A wise power does not restore a map that the country has finished correcting.

The crews they did not appear to. Appearance would have been a lamp. There are mercies that require a face, and this was not one of those countries. What the crews needed was not comfort and not exit. The roads still climbed into the deck for anyone who wanted the other dark, the one that interrupts. Almost no one took them. The ones who stayed had made an exactness and called it home. To lift them out would have been to decide, from outside the chord, that their eaten life was the wrong answer. Wisdom does not overrule a survival it has not had to perform.

What it can do, without overruling, is small and mechanical and real. In one shed a bearing was going to fail in the next fat cell — not yet wrong in the palm, but going to be, in the hour when the rain on the roof would mask it until the stack bent and a file three valleys off turned toward the bend. They eased the housing. Not a miracle of healing. A fraction of load taken off, the way a second hand takes a weight in a doorway, and then withdrawn so the metal could sing its own true note. The crew walked the hall in the morning of that cell, hand on housings, and found nothing to cut, nothing to curse, nothing to report. They slept. The file did not come. No one was saved in any story that would be recited, because the recitation is made of changes, and this was a change that did not happen. That is a form benevolence is allowed to take, if it is willing not to be thanked. They were willing.

They did this more than once, less often than they could have. A power that perfects a system becomes a station. Stations are navigated by. Navigation hardens into dependence, and dependence is how a living country becomes a throat that only one mouth can feed. They refused the mouth. A child reciting a route across a district they had never walked still arrived by ear, not by favor. The knocking still taught the calves. The climbers still spent their fat seasons on brief loud fights and then sealed the chorus over the dead as if death had been weather. They did not gentled the hunters. The subtraction is part of the meter. A shelf on which nothing is taken is a shelf on which the skin starves for iron and the files grow past the flats and break the sheds, and the sheds are the only constancy the people can sleep in. Mercy that deletes the hunt deletes the sleep. They left the teeth where they were.

The being was the reason they had come, and they did not go to it quickly.

They found the edge the way everyone finds it: by the refusal. Files abandoning meter a body's length before the mat changed. Climbers declining to re-mark. A rest in the chord, kept by not being struck. They stood where a curious child from a shed would stand, and they did not cross.

Inside the line the rain was the same rain and the friction under the stone was not asking. That was the thing they had crossed a dark to be sure of. So much of what draws a wise power is a cry misheard as a summons, a held breath mistaken for a drowning. They put their attention against the long sound and waited to see if it leaned. It did not lean. It was not pinned. It was not asleep in the way that hopes to be woken. It was a decision, old as the closed rock over the first halls, renewed in every moment by not breathing out. The skin was thicker there because the being and the film had made the same choice in two registers: occupy, sort, do not pursue. A boot left in that water would soften, yes. That was not malice. That was the terms, posted at the only height the country can post them, which is the height of a herd's refusal.

They understood then what they would not do.

They would not call it forth to see it. They would not name it, because a name is a note, and a note gets walked toward. They would not teach the crews a word for the rest, which the crews had already had the wisdom not to possess. They would not break the sleeve of skin to learn what the sleeve was keeping. Curiosity is not a virtue in every dark. In this one it is a scraper taken all the way to the metal, and a scoured line throws its load raw into the next intake, and the next note goes wrong, and wrong notes move herds.

What they did, at the edge, was reinforce the refusal without adding fear to it.

They made the line a little cleaner in the feet. Not a wall. A wall would have been new geography, and new geography gets explored by the young and the proving. A difference in the film, a half-beat of wrongness in the knock, arriving one step further out than the old refusal, so that a lost body corrected before the mat changed and never had to call the correction courage. The being's quiet remained the being's. The route around it gained a clearer rest in the recitation, which the children would keep by striking nothing, as before, only with less chance that a single stubborn walker would spend a meal inside and come out softer at the seams, having learned nothing the country was willing to teach.

And they answered the friction once.

Not with speech. Speech would have risen into the audible and been parsed as a hunter or a hall. They answered in the same slow register, a long sound under the sound, matched to the being's and not louder, the way two pumps in distant valleys sometimes fall into a sympathy that is not communication and not nothing. They held it for less time than a storm takes to cross a district. They did not ask it to breathe out. They let it know — if knowing is the right word for a patience that large — that the withholding had been heard, and that it was not required to become an event in order to be met. Then they let the sympathy thin, as a cell thins, back into the ordinary friction of water in stone.

The being did not answer in any way that changed the line. The files still would not cross. The skin still climbed. The held breath stayed held. If there was a change it was below the level at which the shelf keeps its books: perhaps only that the decision, renewed every moment for an age, had for one interval been renewed beside company, and that this did not oblige a second interval. A wise power can bear that. The wish to be necessary is how wisdom rots into a station the herds will navigate by, and then the being is no longer holding its breath. It is waiting for a note. They would not do that to it.

When they left, they left the way rain stops. No clearing. No announcement. A fattening reversed. The skin sorted at its old pace. The bearing they had eased ran on as if it had never been touched, which was the only honest way for it to run. Far off, a heavy hunter struck a chorus and the climbers re-marked a hole and the hole sealed. A crew cut growth on a schedule. A file knocked a meter across a dressed course of stone none of them knew was dressed.

They did not set a watch in the sky. There is no sky to set it in, and a watch that the country cannot hear is only anxiety with a throne. What they left behind was smaller than a relic and more durable than a promise: the edge a half-step cleaner, one failure that did not happen, and, under the oldest stone, the memory of a matched friction that did not ask to be repeated.

If they ever return, it will be because the being leans — not because they grew lonely for their own kindness, and not because a road-crew from below arrives with lamps and scrapers and a plan for the rests. Should that plan come, they will not meet it with spectacle. They will meet it as the shelf meets most things: in the water, in the note, in the refusal one step further out than the walker expects. The country has survived attention before. It has not always survived help.

They were wise enough to notice which of the two they had been about to become, and to stop a little short of it, and to go.
