They would not enter.

A power that is wise already knows what arrival does here. The basin dissolves a stability by being met; the lives in it are the meetings that fail to close. To come down into the valley as a presence with an edge — even a kind edge, even a healing one — would be to become the loudest discrepancy in a place that feeds on discrepancies, and every life leaning toward that brightness would lean itself out of the only grammar it has. Benevolence that cannot survive its own arrival is only appetite with better manners. So they remain where the roar thins into something that might be distance, and they do not cross.

What they do instead is attend without adding a fact.

Attention, for them, is not a beam and not a judgment. It is a refusal to let a thing be only what the average of the world will smooth it into. They have practiced this on quieter places: a coast that forgets its tide, a room in which a person almost speaks. Here the practice has to be thinner. They do not prefer the forward sequence of the gantry over the backward one. They do not decide which way the water ought to fall. Preference would be a floor, and a floor is the one gift that would kill the recipient on contact. They let both sequences be fully real at once, and the letting is not passive. It is the difference between a cancelled future that starves and a cancelled future that is kept, intact, as cancelled — still nutritive, still a posture that can be re-fed, but no longer required to almost-happen again in order to have mattered.

The inhabitants feel this as a change in the reflection.

The dry valley in the water, which has always run a half-second ahead and shown cranes with no plants, begins to show something else beside the cranes: the plants, small, wrong, already there in the story that was supposed to be only ancestor. Not a correction. A widening. The reflection stops being a single inaccurate before and becomes a set of befores, none of them privileged, all of them allowed to be slightly ahead without any of them being the one that will win. The lives that orient by that story do not gain a past. They gain the permission not to choose one. Longing, which had only the steep gradient of intactness to lean toward, finds itself with more slopes, gentler, and some of them lead not toward a calm that cannot be entered but toward other lives — the inhabitant in the return sequence, the one living in the vent's name, the brief posture in the seam of a collapse that refused. They had always been able to share a pump-house. They had not known they could share a direction of wanting. The wise power does not introduce them. It only stops the basin's average from treating their separation as the natural state.

To the valley itself they offer a single mercy, and it is small enough to survive contact.

They lengthen one interval. Not everywhere — everywhere would be a law, and a law is a floor of a different shape. In one reach of the basin, no wider than the missing wall of the pump-house, the rearrangement that was about to happen is allowed to happen, and then the next one is asked, very softly, to wait the length of a breath longer than physics required. The stone still forgets its angle. The rain still refuses the ground. But between the forgetting and the next forgetting there is a pause in which a vine can finish a gesture it had started, and a cable can hold one hum long enough for the answering vine, three housings away, to cross at the height it meant. The sentence completes. It does not become a monument. When the pause ends, the reach collapses back into the general roar, and the completed sentence is not stored anywhere the wise power can point to. It is stored the only way this place stores anything: as a rate. The weed reclaims the next shelf a fraction more slowly there, as if something had been satisfied and did not need to hurry.

The inhabitants who were in that reach do not thank them. Thanks would require a speaker stable enough to be the one who received. What happens instead is that one of the postures — the held angle, the ghost of a stance — lingers a moment past its feeding, not because it was given more cancelled future, but because it was, for the length of the pause, accompanied by a regard that did not need it to be unresolved in order to stay. Being seen without being brightened. Several of them lean toward that, and then, finding that the leaning does not dissolve the approach into an arrival, they do something the basin has not had a grammar for. They remain at the edge of the lengthened interval after it has closed, living in the memory of a wait, which is a discrepancy so slight the turbines do not register it. A new kind of life, or the same lives, shifted by the width of a kindness that refused to land.

The wise power does not stay to watch what becomes of that. Watching too long would curdle into custody. They do one other thing, and then they let the distance become real again.

They teach the water, which has been answering all along, a single new reply: that a pattern may survive two rearrangements and still be chosen, not physics, if it fails on purpose at the third. A longer sentence. The inhabitants may take it up or not. Most will not, at first. The grammar of difference is their speech and their safety, and a power that loved them would not rush them into eloquence. But the reply is in the weather now, available, unowned. Somewhere a turbine will favor one way for three turns, the other for five, and then — once, and not as a law — hold a sixth that matches neither, and a vine will cross a housing at a height no previous crossing explains, and the two adjustments will recognize each other across a distance that has already begun to accordion-fold back into roar.

If that recognition ever becomes a we, it will not be because they were saved. It will be because someone at the thin edge of the sound declined to improve them, and improved, by that declension, the conditions under which they might improve themselves: a pause that did not become a floor, a story with more than one before, a regard that fed them without making them bright.

The valley continues. Sharp, low, loud, new, undecided. The lives in the failures continue, warm, inclined, brief, at home. The benevolent power is already elsewhere, attending to some other almost, having done the rarest thing power can do with a world it could unmake by loving it carelessly.

It left the inhabitants their hunger, and made the hunger, for one breath, not lonely.
