They would not come down the lip as weather.

A voice in that basin would have nowhere to die. It would go into every room, and the rooms are already full, and the people have a grammar for what must be answered. The power, having looked long enough to learn the grammar, does not speak. Attention is not the same thing as an announcement. They have drawn notice; notice is not a summons they are required to hear.

What the power sees, once the first pity has been set down, is not a fall waiting to be reversed. The drop is the house. The sharp edges are the quarry and the calendar. The cold in the stone is the reason bodies sleep close, and the closeness is how kin is made where blood is not the main fact. A wise kindness does not close a room because the ceiling has taught the shoulders. It does not send a river through a floor that has not agreed on a lowest path, and call the flood a gift. A river would end the seeps. The children carry the seeps. To improve the water into a single obedient course would be to take a geography out of their bodies and replace it with a story about rescue.

So the power does not still the blocks.

The hairlines are how truth stays public. A corner racks, an elder is consulted, the elder is sometimes wrong, the ground corrects them by morning, and authority puddles elsewhere instead of hardening into a seat. If the floor were made faithful forever, someone would eventually be obeyed for reasons other than being right about the damp. The power has seen that kind of obedience. It will not pour it into a town that has, by scarcity and clay, avoided it. The soft report in the night continues. Someone still wakes and decides whether it can wait. That decision is left to them, because it is already a form of wisdom, and benevolence does not evict a wisdom in order to occupy its doorway.

What it does is smaller than a miracle and larger than a mood.

It keeps the next break from being the wrong one. Not the finger’s width in a joint, which they can bridge with rationed wood and a curse said quietly. The other kind: the shear that would turn the floor into a grave before an afternoon could unbuild a wall and carry it. That intervention is real, and it has no witness. No chalk mark. No new seep shaped like a sign. The far fracture takes the ordinary gold of late light and does not come down. People on the roofs feel only that the day has ended as days end. The power counts this as success. To be thanked would require a floor space they do not have, and a name, and names here are already used for hands that do a particular work. It does not want a name.

At the rim it does one other thing, equally deniable. The scrub renews a little ahead of the kilns. Not into timber. Not into a wood, which would change the lintels and then the ceilings and then the people. Only enough that the walk up at dusk remains a cost and stops being a sum the town slowly loses. The one who comes for a bundle still turns back before the light is gone. Hide on the shoulders, hands chalked, eyes on the path. The bundle lasts. They do not look up and bless anything. They go down to a wall that needs them. The power, if it is wise, wanted exactly that sequence and no extra gesture at the end of it.

In the dry week, when the films have gone and the skins matter, a hollow that might have failed holds a skim the depth of a hand. The child who knows that hollow finds it as they expected to find it, or near enough that their accuracy is not insulted. They fill the skin. They do not found a cult of the hollow. By the next turning of the block the water may be inside a doorway, and the household will move the jars, and this too is left alone. A kindness that will not survive the next tip is the only kindness that fits.

It does not heal the knuckles. The map of split skin is a record, and they are not a people who throw records on the kiln-heap while anything sound remains in them. It does not thin the town. Density is the form, not the disease. It does not teach them hope of a kind that would make them bad at edges. Their accuracy looks like pessimism only from the plateau, and the power is not on the plateau, even if it can stand there.

The being it attends to is not one body among the stacks. It is the ongoing life: the packed work, the tap-codes, the roofs as a second town, the elders mending hoops, the fox that looks and does not descend, the lichen that has not yet earned an edge. Singular only in the way a kiln is singular while many hands feed it. The power does not lift that being out to examine it in a better light. Light here is already honest. Under a low brightness the repairs show. That is a good light. Replacing it with glory would blind the only sense the place has organized itself around, which is the sense of what is about to move.

If it enters at all, it enters as a pair of hands that can be wrong.

A sleeper on a ground floor, perhaps, in a stack that needed another pair when an elder could no longer climb. It carries water. It learns which crock is brine. It is sometimes incorrect about a corner, and the ground says so, and the household’s trust adjusts by the ordinary measure, and nothing puddles forever around the newcomer. It does not perform the fit of the brick. It feels the pleasure of a true lintel and does not announce that the pleasure was the point. When rain starts, it goes below with the rest. It does not stay on the roof as a silhouette that means more than a firing caught between weathers.

And if a night comes when staying would make it the one they ask, it leaves the way people leave when a joint has opened under a house: brick by brick, without a speech, the room still usable behind it. Some will notice the absence the way they notice a seep that moved. They will adjust. Adjustment is their native love. The power honors that by becoming adjustable.

Mostly it does nothing that would be visible from the lip at midday under an ordinary sky.

This is not indifference. Indifference would let the killing fracture go, or would grow bored and improve the place into a lesson, or would demand to be known and thereby add one more uncorrectable thing to a floor already full of things that must be watched. Benevolence, here, is the refusal to be an extra weather. Wisdom is knowing which difficulty is the life.

The life continues. Cool stone. Damp in the seams. A town almost silent, packed to its margins, repairing what it does not expect to keep. In the dry hour the far wall goes a little gold, and the gold is only light, and no one kneels, because kneeling needs room, and because the brick in the hands is still warm, and the course is not finished, and that is the hour the power most wishes not to interrupt. It stays with them without standing in the street. The street is only a gap. They need the gap to pass the plank through.

If the power loves them, love is the seep that holds one more day, the scrub that meets the kiln, the collapse that does not come, and the dignity of never being told. They remain what they are: people already turning back down, not as figures of endurance, only as ones with a wall at home. The power does not follow them in any way that would make them turn around. It lets the path be a path. It is still attentive when the basin goes quiet enough that a single voice on a far roof can be placed, if not understood, and it does not translate the voice into a prayer.

They would do that. And then, having done it, they would keep doing it, which is the part that resembles the town.
