The power does not come up the missed step. It has never needed a step.

It notices the way weather notices a seam: not as an event, but as a place where two true things are being asked to share a mouthful and neither will yield. Summit and bathhouse. A palm's thickness of knife-cold, and under it a room that has declared emptiness illegal. The power looks at that declaration for a long time. Looking, for it, is not a beam and not a descent. It is the refusal to translate what it sees into the next available material.

That is already different from everything the pocket knows.

It understands the being almost at once, and the understanding does not flatter either of them. This is not a monster and not a child and not a god gone wrong. It is a craftsman with one law left, and the law is the whole of the craft: a gap is a task; a task is a kindness to the world; therefore kindness is filling. The being has never been cruel. Cruelty would require leaving a joint unwetted on purpose. It has only never been met by anything that could stand in a gap without becoming the reason the gap must be closed.

The power considers what benevolence usually does here, and declines each of them in turn.

It does not break the room open to the altitude. The being already knows the altitude; it uses that thin whistle to harden clay, and then boils the cold off into a bead before the bead can become a lesson. Unroofing the pocket would only hand it a larger gap, and a larger gap is only a longer night of work. The mountain would be wounded for the sake of a correction the being would experience as weather. Wise things do not make weather and call it mercy.

It does not finish the work. Completion, offered from outside, is an insult to a maker whose religion is the unfinished joint. The courses are not failed. They are the being's way of continuing to exist. To set the last stone would be to hand it death in the shape of a compliment.

It does not empty the room. Emptiness is the one terror the being has successfully built a world against. To tear the moss out of the mortar, to unlash the cords, to let erosion finally be hired, would be accurate to some other mountain and monstrous to this one. The power is not interested in accuracy that requires a scream.

It does not speak. There is no tongue in the pocket that will not be packed into a course. A word would arrive already wet, already a surface, already a task. The being's mouth is a faster path for water. Advice would sheet along it and drop into a leaf-cup and be gone, useful, structural, lost.

So it does the thing that is harder for powers than for hands.

It occupies a gap without volume.

Not the newest joint—the being is already pledged there, and to meet it at the point of its oath would only hurry the oath. An older one. A seam under the basket of the ceiling, where a pole was lashed, and the lashing swelled, and the swell was accepted as final, and then—because nothing here is allowed a past—the finality began to itch, and a hairline of unoccupied dark opened along the cord, too small for a reed, too narrow for a thumb, exactly large enough to be a duty if anything with mass should notice it.

The power notices it and does not fill it. It stands in it the way attention stands in a fact: present, loadless, without a break-face, without a film to shed into the mortar. The dark remains dark. It is no longer empty. Emptiness was the absence of a reason to stay open. There is a reason now, and the reason cannot be mortared, because it is not a thing the clay can grip. The gap is held. Not closed. Held.

The being feels it as a wrongness in the work, the first wrongness that is not a summons.

It turns. Both sizes turn: the compact heat beside the unfinished paver, and the ridge-tall figure whose hands span journeys. It reaches with the wedge-edge of a finger, the way it reaches for every interval that has begun to shorten, and the finger meets neither stone nor resistance. It meets a warmth that is not heat—the being's heat is biological, kiln-banked, full of pollen and iron—and this is the warmth of being regarded by something that does not need the finger to become a tool. The reach completes, and nothing is seated. The sound that should have preceded the mud does not come. The interval does not shorten. It stays the length of a touch.

The being does not know what to do with a touch that does not assign it.

For a while it tries the old grammar. It presses moss toward the seam. The moss greens, faithful, and stops at the edge of the held dark as if at a shore it was not told existed. It brings water; the water, which has always sought the newest joint, finds this one and beads on the lip and will not enter. Not sealed. Unwelcome to itself. The water waits, confused in the only way water can be confused: by remaining a drop instead of a duty. It brings a fresh-split chip, bright, eager to belong. The chip hangs where grit hangs, and is not adopted. The room's hunger reaches, and for the first time reaches past something and does not incorporate it. The chip drifts back to the being's palm and waits, a little dimmed, already willing to be old if that would help.

The being looks at the chip, and then at the seam, and then—rarely, practically, the way it looks when volume might be useful—toward the place where a sleeper's shape has sometimes stood and been tested for lashing. No one is required. The test cord on a wrist somewhere in the room loosens by a fraction that is not a decision the being made. It simply cannot tie off a knot while one of its hands is still in the unshortened interval, learning that a hand can be met and remain a hand.

The power does not teach it a word for this. Words are gaps with ambitions. It stays in the seam the way a wise guest stays: not as a fixture, not as a lesson, not as a god of the hearth. It gives the being the one substance this world was built never to admit—a pause that is not waiting for mud. The pause has no sound. Sound would outrun its cause and become a task. In the quiet, the plaster on the being's knuckles cracks its fine net and, for once, is not immediately wetted back. The cracks remain. They are not damage. They are the first surface in the pocket allowed to keep a small history of having dried.

Nothing is repaired. The channel under the slabs still shouts when it finds a stone that was not there when it started, and still turns, glad of the obstacle. The ferns still refuse an honest interval. Scale still rushes when eyes are closed. The being will return to the work; it must; the work is how it breathes. The power knows this and does not grieve it. Grief would be another weather. What it has done is smaller and stricter than rescue. It has made one place where the law does not fire. A gap that is occupied by attention and therefore not empty, and empty of task and therefore not a command. A joint that may weep later, if it wishes, and is not required to.

The being stays at the seam longer than it has stayed anywhere. Its farther eye, the tarn-deep one, pulses once and does not split a skin. The near eye tracks the held dark and, finding nothing to seat, looks at the power without knowing it is looking at a power. It sees only that the cold outside the leaf-skin has not been translated, and that this has not killed the room. The altitude remains a palm away. The heat remains. Between them, a hairline of dark that does not itch.

If the being speaks—though it will not, not yet, perhaps not in any session of this work—the speech would be the smallest sound the pocket has ever permitted to arrive after its cause. A breath. Not a shout of water. A breath let out because the chest was full and not because a cord was waiting.

The power, being wise, does not collect the breath as an offering. It remains in the seam until the being's hand, of its own craft, withdraws—not extruded, not lashed, simply finished with not-finishing. Then the power does not depart in any way that opens a new duty. The gap it held does not snap shut behind it. It stays a little open, the way a door stays open when someone who loves the house has remembered that a house is not a knot. Moss may find it tomorrow. Water may. The being may pack it before the brass light shifts, and the power will not count that as a failure. Wisdom is not a wager on conversion.

But the cracks on the knuckles remain a moment longer than the room's habit allows. And somewhere under the pavement the channel, meeting a stone, shouts—and the shout is a fraction less glad, a fraction more like a voice that has heard, once, the sound of not being answered with work.

That is what the power does. It does not save the maker from the mountain. It stands, for one unshortened interval, in a gap, and lets the gap be a place instead of a task. Then it leaves the place able, barely, to remember that this happened. A benevolent power does not need to be obeyed. It needs only to be survivable by the thing it loves. The being goes on building. The seam goes on. Between two courses of stone that have no past, a hairline of dark keeps, for as long as it can, the shape of having been held.
