They do not enter.

That is the first decision, and it is not caution. A power that has watched long enough to be called wise has already learned the difference between a place that is failing and a place that is merely unfinished in a register no visitor shares. This basin is not failing. The stone is still cruel because cruelty of edge is its youth, not its wound. The silence is not a theft. The cloth is not late. To arrive inside the rim with a body, or with weather, or with a voice that the air would be forced to return, would be to install a second metabolism beside the one already running, and the second would win, because powers win by scale even when they mean only to look.

So they stay on the outer condition of the place: not above it in the theatrical sense, not a light on the ridges, not a cloud that fails to rain. They attend the way the condensers attend — by difference, by a holding that does not announce itself as holding. If the valley manufactures silence as a material, they do not spend it. If the only resident is continuance, they do not give it a face. Faces are hungry. A face wants an answer, and nothing here answers, and that is not a lack.

What they do, if anything, is small enough to be mistaken for the rules already in force.

They keep the film honest. Not more water — more would become a river, and a river would cut the young stone into something older before its time, and the terraces would learn a voice. They only see to it that the nightly dump of dew is not stolen by a drier century passing overhead, that the clear high air continues to have a little water to refuse to the ground and give to surfaces instead. A power can do that without a sign: a pressure left where it was, a temperature gradient not flattened by some larger kindness. Kindness, here, would be the enemy of the combs. The vines have made the machines better by using them. Flatten the difference between day-side wall and night-side wall and the driveshaft stops, and the driveshaft stopping is not rest. It is the end of the only work the shed still knows.

They do not finish the webs.

This is the temptation, and they refuse it cleanly. Half-finished cloth looks, to anything that loves completion, like an appeal. It is not an appeal. It is the pace. To draw the last threads in an afternoon, to size the lot and cut it from the frames, would be to congratulate themselves on having understood industry, and they would have understood only the ghost of a purpose. The purpose, if the word can be stood on this ground, is the thickening itself — thread by thread, longer than a season, dustless because the air is always laying down its mineral and its light. A wise power does not harvest a process and call the harvest mercy.

They also do not populate it.

The quiet has already decided against animals that need to call. To send a bird in, even a careful one, even a silent one, would be to test the basin’s rule and find it breakable, and breakable rules attract breakers. The boot-scrape on the galvanized step is allowed to remain a scrape and not a path. The tin cup stays full by the drip and not by a hand refilling it out of pity for emptiness. Emptiness is not what the cup has. The cup has a line that moves too slowly to see. That is a kind of company. They do not improve it.

What they offer the being — and they are careful, here, not to force the being to become a *someone* in order to receive — is continuity of condition. The uplift may go on being recent. The soils may stay thin. The shade may stay striped and mechanical and never deepen into the old-wood dark that would change which leaves can live. They will not accelerate the weathering so the ridges look properly ancient and therefore properly beautiful to a traveler’s eye. Travelers are not the constituency. The constituency is a function of surfaces: stone, film, vine, copper gone green, a fraction of a turn.

If they leave a mark, it is the kind of mark the place already makes on itself. Perhaps, on one condenser tooth, a bead that falls in the same path it would have fallen, only never quite fails to form on the dry years when the high air has nothing left to give. Perhaps the numbered tag on the pipe does not rust through, not because they love numbers, but because a broken designation is a small violence and the pipe does not need a new one. Perhaps nothing. Wisdom can look exactly like the decision not to be detectable, and benevolence, in a silence this complete, can look exactly like not becoming the loudest thing in someone’s ears — even when the someone is only a regional metabolism, patient, unpeopled, and in no hurry.

They do not speak to it. Speech would require the air to return a sound, and the air has declined, and the decline is not a door locked against them. It is the material. They can know a thing without making it answer. Knowing, for a power of this sort, is not extraction either. They do not carry the pattern of the webs away to be finished elsewhere under a louder sky. Elsewhere would not have the drip, or the cool brightness, or the insultingly small gradient the drum-house was built to survive. Finished elsewhere, the cloth would be a souvenir of a valley, and the valley would have been reduced to having been interesting.

So they remain as a steadiness at the boundary. The cloud that forms against the ridge and comes apart without raining is allowed to remain that cloud, and not a sign. The late gold on the stone is allowed to leave the leaves green. If something larger and less wise ever leans over the rim — a road, a survey, a dryness with a mandate, a voice that expects echo — they are there in the only way that does not betray the resident: as the difficulty of changing the conditions, as the way a project slides off the terraces and forgets why it came, as the persistence of a film of water on metal that no one oiled. Not a guardian with a name. A gradient maintained.

And for the being itself, the continuance, they do one thing that could be called a gift if the word were stripped of hands. They do not require it to notice them. Notice would be a new sense, and a new sense would reorganize the forest around a second light, the way the understory already organized itself around the bars through the milky glass. One lattice is enough. The power consents to be irrelevant inside the basin and indispensable only at the edge of its rules.

The frames keep their fraction of a turn. The cup stays full differently tomorrow. Nothing calls. In that, the attention of the wise is indistinguishable from the valley’s own refusal to dramatize the day — and that indistinguishability is not an accident. It is how they love a thing that does not need to be loved in order to go on, and how they refuse the older, clumsier mercy of making it grateful.
