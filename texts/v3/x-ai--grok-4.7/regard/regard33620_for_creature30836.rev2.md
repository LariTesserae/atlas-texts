They would not open it.

A power wise enough to be trusted with such a place would understand, before any gesture, that the pocket is not a locked room and not a wound. It is a finished sentence still being said. The cold is its climate, not its deprivation. The dark is its medium, not a blindfold waiting to be lifted. The leaves have never asked for a margin to be chewed, and the seams have never asked to be admired. To arrive with light, or a draft, or a name spoken aloud in a throat that needs air, would be to edit a text that had already found its last necessary word.

So the power would do what the stone already does: bear a little of the weight, and call that bearing nothing.

It would learn the pocket the way the roots learned the lattice — by a difference too small to be called a gift. Along one seam the gradient is already a fraction less cold. The power would not warm it. It would only refuse, elsewhere and far above, the particular subsidence that would one day pinch that seam shut ahead of its own slow clock. Not a wall. Not a miracle. A withheld landslide. A hill persuaded, by means no one inside would ever feel, to keep the minor load-bearing knot it already contains. The hollow would not enlarge. The hollow would not close. The agreement would continue, and the continuation would not know it had been defended.

If there is a being — and wisdom would not be hasty to decide whether the being is a body, or the fitted leaf in the dish, or the long conversation among erosion, growth, and grain — the power would not wake it into company. Company is a draft. Company is an animal track in felt that has never held one. The power would instead match its attention to the existing tempo: not a vigil that hangs and watches, which is already a kind of pressure, but an attention that visits on the same scale as the overhead mass, tightening and slackening over days, present as weather is present to a root that cannot see sky.

Once, perhaps, and only once, it would allow a bead of water that was already going to transfer to transfer a little more cleanly from stone to leaf, so that the leaf’s rib took the film without the minute creak. No one counting would count it. The sweetness in the air would not change. The dish would still hold no water. The filament would still dive after a finger’s length of smoothness. The fossils of intention would remain fossils.

And then the power would go on, unconsulted in return, which is the courtesy the place has practiced toward everything larger than itself. It would not write the pocket into a larger story of rescue. It would not preserve it against the long run it has already chosen — the dense, damp, lightless room under the weight of its own making. Benevolence, here, is not improvement. It is the decision not to become an event.

What they would do, if anything, is keep the nothing that is already working: the skin of moisture, the rounded sill, the roots that have chosen and kept. They would leave the dark its rank as ordinary condition. They would let the breath that is never offered continue not to be offered.

If later some genuine breaking came — a poison in the film, a heat that was not a gradient, a hand that pushed because it could — then the power might act, and the action would still be sized to the room: a sealing, a slowing, a return to the thrift that does not stink of waste. Until then, wisdom looks exactly like the place’s own law.

The hollow does not close. The hollow does not enlarge.

They would not be the thing that made either sentence false.
