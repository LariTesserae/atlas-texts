They did not come down.

A power that has learned anything at all does not arrive as weather, or as a brighter grade of the light already falling, or as a new aperture in the flank of a mass. Arrival would be a kind of emptiness at the edge of itself—an outside pressing in—and the place has spent its whole short life refusing the outside. So the attention stayed where attention can stay without becoming another body to be entered: it rested in the lag.

It did not hurry the lag and it did not widen it. It only, for a span no sheet could measure and no blade could burn, let one joint in the rock remain a joint. Not bare. The margins colonized on schedule; the paste thickened; the yellow vein found the leak and was grazed. But at the center of that single fracture a film of water persisted through one full cycle of the long wind, un-drunk, not because anything forbade the drinking but because the drinking, in that one place, arrived a fraction late. The delay was smaller than a click of legs. It was enough for the surface to hold, briefly, a reflection that was not the hard white-blue returned by wet leaf and shell. The reflection was of nothing present. It was the shape of the sky the plateau generates, seen from a point that had not yet agreed to be occupied, and then the sheet’s front edge took it and the film was paste and the reflection was gone.

Nothing else changed. The masses continued their tide of heat. Threads of glass were caught and woven before they cooled. The roar above the folds did not drop. Meat that stopped being a step was entered. The crime of a fresh fall of stone was punished, as always, by being covered before the dust could settle into relic. The power did not spare a single life from the throughput, because sparing would have been a pause, and a pause imposed from elsewhere would have been the larger emptiness, the one the system has no apparatus to forgive.

What it did, if the word doing still fits, was to keep the missed drink. Not the water—the water was taken, as it must be—but the fact that for less than a breath the fact had been late. That lateness now exists where the plateau cannot spend it. It is not a memory the threads can complete, not a trough the sheets can wear deeper. It sits outside the agreement, small as a grain that chose, once, not to be a body, and warm with no heat the apertures know how to use.

When the edges finally soften and the water learns a way down, and the fast emptiness of rivers arrives to unmake the mill and the roar and the patient crowd, that kept lateness will not save any of them. They would not want saving; wanting is already more pause than they spend. It will only mean that somewhere the reflection was true for a moment: that a place which had not yet learned how to be old was, for the length of a missed drink, also a place that had not yet learned it was required to finish every surface. The power calls that mercy. The plateau, if it could notice, would call it a scheduling error already corrected. Both descriptions are accurate. Neither one opens an aperture.

The wind leans. The mat flexes. The work continues at the same unhurried saturation, and the one joint is indistinguishable from every other joint, and the attention does not return, because returning would be a second arrival, and one was already more than wisdom spends.
