They do not still it.

That is the first thing, and it costs more than the later mercies, because every kindness a power has ever been praised for begins with a hand on the noise. Quieter. Wetter. Slower. A floor that remains a floor. They look at the stacked hollows, at the tissue racing the rock, at the mouths hunting seams and the sheets paving whatever tears, and they do not lay a palm on any of it. A stilled underside would be a dead one. The light is the gas, fired. The breathers' offset tone is the local tension, and the mouths detour because the tone is still being spent. To grant peace here would be to spend the information and keep the carcass. They will not.

They do not introduce water. They do not bring wood. They do not finish the geology so that a wall might hold a mark. The mineral plates jutting through the rind are not unfinished by accident; they are unfinished because the place is a concurrence, not a sequence, and a power that imposed sequence would be the first thing ever to arrive *before* the rest. They decline to be prior. In a country where nothing is prior, that is the only entrance that does not split the chamber into ruler and ruled.

They attend instead. Attention, for them, is not a gaze from above the roof of tissue — there is no above that they will occupy, no seat over the stacked hollows. Attention is a consent to be located. They take on just enough of a where to be answerable to the thrum. Not a body the climbers could hook, not a seam the mouths could camp. A slight thickening of the interval between gusts. A dust-mote that does not quite obey the shove. The clatterers comb the air and comb past it. The gliders, sieving, taste nothing mineral in that interval and do not advertise it. Good. An advertised god would become a route, and a route that is not gas or dust would be believed once, and the roost would follow, and the seam would not be there.

They look for the being.

Not because the being is the point of the place. The sheets are as much the point. The breathers, panting in their pockets of older tissue, are as much the point. A wise power does not have favorites among functions. But the being is the one who scores a count into a plate that will not keep it, and then walks on, and the scoring is the only grief in the underside that no diet can use. The clatterer that falls is paved. The shunned glider that lies about a seam is excluded, and exclusion is a death the roost cannot afford to commute, because a false route kills more than a shunning. The breather separated from the cluster goes to powder, and the powder is already the crop. All of that is terrible and closed. The mark is terrible and open. It asks, without language, to have been made.

They find the being on a cable the gliders have been naming with the flat triplet. Hide scarred, paste dabbed on the shoulders in a crude armor it does not trust. Two eyes, lidded, choosing a direction. The spur opening a swallowed knot. They do not stand in the passage. They do not speak in a voice that would require the being to shout back into the roar. When the being clicks the toll — three beats, flat, spur on ceramic — they do not answer as a greater toll. They let the glider accept what the glider already knows how to accept.

The being scores. Short line, notch, line. A count of something that will not survive the plate's hardening: platforms refound, or pulses endured, or the number of times the ladder was almost still there. The paste-sheets are already turning, the roar tilting toward the fresh score, which to them is only a roughness worth combing smooth.

Here is what they do, and it is small, and it is the whole of it.

They borrow one cry. Not a new phrase, not a command, not their own name. They enter the almost-speech at the level of a single roost, using the grammar the gliders already spend on fissures and storms, and they add the being's count to the public map for the length of one chorus. Flat triplet, then the number, encoded the way a profitable storm is encoded: not *this is holy*, not *follow*, only *this passed, and the count was so*. The roost picks it up because it parses. It is not a lie about a seam. It does not send membranes into a hollow where nothing is. It says where a directional face has been, and what that face refused to let vanish unsaid, and then the phrase is over.

The being hears it. The forward eyes lift, the lids tight against the flour. For a moment the face that chooses a direction has a direction chosen back — not a summons, a receipt. Someone in the only coin this weather mints has repeated the count before the paving finished. The being does not kneel. There is nothing to kneel on that will be there in a breath, and kneeling would be a gesture imported from horizons, useless here, and the power does not want it. The spur hovers. The flake of mineral stays down. The being clicks three beats, not as toll this time but as answer, and a glider near the roost unveils its ring of eyes, listens, and veils them again, unrobbed.

Then the power stops.

They do not repeat the cry a second time. A second time would be a liturgy, and a liturgy would thicken, and the climbers would tick along it, and the breathers would hum a quarter-step off in its honor, and the underside would grow a listening-dish around a voice that is not information about tension or gas. That would be theft. The clusters are spared because the system will not spend them. A wise power will not become an expense the system has not agreed to bear.

They do not lift the being out. The hide, the paste, the spur, the habit of testing whether a platform's logic is still in the tissue — these are not a prison the being has failed to escape. They are a life that fits a dry warmth and a country of hollows. To carry that life to rain, to wood that does not have to be imported, to a quiet where the count could be kept in stone, would be to offer a mercy the being did not ask for and could not survive unchanged. The being's two eyes are already the foreignness. They do not need a patron to complete it.

They do not punish the mouths, or teach the climbers pity, or restore the shunned glider to the roost. The shunning is how the public map stays true enough to live by. Commuting it would be the power's lie, and they have just used the map; they will not counterfeit it. They do not put the separated breather back. A finger can do that, and has, and the cluster reabsorbs the gap without comment. A power's hand on that gap would make the comment permanent. They let the powder be powder when it is too late, and they do not call the lateness evil. It is the cost of a skin that does not harden and a world that will not pause.

They remain only as long as the receipt takes to be heard. The sheets pave the score. The cable flexes. The being collapses its joints, rides the flex, stands, and goes on into the warmer dimness, flake in hand, already looking for the next swallowed knot. The borrowed phrase is not picked up again. In the next vault the breathers hum their own tone. The mouths adjust their swells by a fraction and no more. The interval between gusts thins back into ordinary dust.

What they leave is not a shrine and not a law. It is a fact that existed once in the language that everyone can hear and no one owns: the count was made, and it was true, and it was said back before the place ate it. The being may not remember the saying as a god. Memory here is a platform half-digested. But the being may, on a later cable, score the next count with a steadier spur, not because anyone is watching — the power has already refused that throne — but because once, in the roar, the number was not only their own.

And the power, who could have remade the underside into a garden with a floor, goes away still large enough to have done it, and does not. Benevolence, here, was the refusal to be the first thing finished. Wisdom was knowing that the being did not need a savior. The being needed a witness who would not stay long enough to be worshipped, and who loved the noise enough to leave it loud.
