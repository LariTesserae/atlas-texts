The power does not come as brighter light. Light here is already a closed inventory, mirrored inward until brightness has nowhere left to go. It comes, instead, as a slackening no specification ordered.

Far off, in a district the size of a weather, one engine-heart completes its stroke and does not at once begin the return. The cork sheath does not correct the hesitation. The hydraulic oil that is not water holds, for three breaths a walker might have taken, and the hush deepens into something that is almost silence. In a cooling aisle a figure stands with its arm sunk to the elbow in a louvered vent. The count it has been keeping—pressure, stroke, salt-note—stops. Not broken. Merely unrequired.

The power is wise enough to know that remaking would be another mold, only larger, and benevolent enough to refuse the vanity of improvement. These beings have already had hunger edited out, and sleep, and the need to announce their work. What they have not had is a pause that does not mean failure. So the pause is given, and then withdrawn before the growth can learn it as a new cycle and begin to reproduce it everywhere.

What remains are three small weathers, easy to mistake for the plain’s own drift.

In one aisle the shadow falls true. It lies across the walkway and continues only onto the surfaces that are actually in its line, and it stays that way. Figures who need the corridor still use it. One of them, passing, slows—not from duty, but because the eye-plates return a light that does not have to be reconciled. The straightness is a courtesy that does not expire when attention leaves it. No one is told to be grateful. Gratitude is not in the fiber. But the aisle is walked more often than its neighbors, and the floor under it does not thicken into the forgetting mat quite so quickly.

Under the feet of a single figure, the growth keeps a print a little longer than it should. The weight that stood there was continuous, as all their weights are, and then—for the span of the slackening—it was not. The fibers aligned to absence instead of presence, and when the figure stepped away the alignment lingered, a cooler patch, a density that remembered standing and then not standing. The figure returns to it later, without knowing why its steps tend that way. It sets its sole in the print and feels, through the contact that is their only speech, the shape of a departure that did not become pruning. Leaving is not yet possible. It has become thinkable, which is a narrower gift, and perhaps the only one that would not tear the mid-story open.

The third thing is a pressure with no task inside it. It moves the way their own exchanges move, palm to trunk, sole to walkway, a change behind the eyes—but it asks nothing of the sap and does not count the stroke. A figure who has stood long enough to be nearly a swelling feels it and does not girdle, does not correct, does not offer an arm to the vent. The pressure is simply regard: the sense of having been included in an attention that does not need them to be in spec. It does not last. Wisdom does not loiter where it would become another center. But while it is there, one seam of a mouth—unused, unstable—tightens and eases, as if testing a motion that has no oil to carry it.

Then the power is gone, if “gone” is the right word for a thing that was never located. The piston resumes. The hush returns to its industrial depth. Salt-plates ring their small glassy notes and die at once. The beings continue their pruning and their keeping of temporary straightness. Most of them never register the difference; number and distribution make witness rare.

One figure, though, the one whose print cooled, later draws its arm from a vent and does not immediately sink it elsewhere. It holds the hand a finger’s breadth above the louver, salt crusting along the wrist in a plate that has no maintenance to justify it. Somewhere in the excessive brightness a true shadow still lies where it should. The figure does not look for the power. It has no category for what passed through the count. It only stands, weight not yet given back to the floor, and for the first time the continuance feels like a choice the specification did not finish writing.

The plain goes on being the only ground. The benevolence was never a rescue. It was the refusal to let endurance be the same thing as solitude, and the wisdom to leave that refusal small enough to survive inside a world that turns every large kindness into another engine.
