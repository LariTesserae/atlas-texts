It does not come over the coping. Anything that crossed would be a warmer sleeve in the condensate, a salt the channels have no schedule for, and the being would do what it does with weather: route the drips to the far side of the lintel, decline to start a climbing film on the touched stone, and wait to forget. A power that called itself wise and still arrived that way would already have failed the only courtesy that matters here.

It attends from the drop. From the dark the being already checks. Attention, at that distance, adds no heat-load. The lamps do not mistake it for thickening haze. The needles travel because the pressure is true and not because they are watched. If the being registers anything at all, it is only that the darkness beyond the iron, for one alignment of the cycles, does not feel like an unverified edge. It feels like an edge that has been met and not opened. Then the power stops even that, before the meeting can become a spacing the ferns would be obliged to keep.

It looks for a long time, which costs it nothing and the place nothing. It is not deciding whether the hamlet deserves to continue. That question is a visitor's question, and visitors are over before their clothes dry. It is deciding whether anything here is a harm it would be wrong to leave untouched.

The absence of animals is not a harm. Filling it would be. A bird would hunt or be hunted, and the substitution would tear: hunger back in a room that has finished with hunger. Insects over the standing water would turn the clear bottoms into a second industry, one the gates were not built to keep. The power does not import a single living throat. The noise remains mechanical, hydraulic, meteorological. Thought keeps its room.

The hand that left is not a harm. Calling it back would be a story imposed on a logbook that has already been continued in clearances and oil and the travel of needles. The power does not mend the warped pages. It does not dry them. A dried page would be a correction in a script the being has already abandoned for media that do not smear, and corrections are how a foreign will begins to vote.

The broken lip in the southern spill-bay, the one that fans the stream and puts a higher thread in the roar, is not a harm either. It is part of the thought. To fair the lip back to a clean fall would be to edit a sentence the engine has been coughing in time with for longer than the paint on the tool-boards has had names. The power leaves the flaw. Benevolence, here, is the refusal to finish what was not unfinished.

It refuses, also, the kindness people mean when they say *awake*. The coincidence of the cycles — pier, valve, rain, lamp, cough — draws the being taut and lets it be one creature for a little while, looking. A lesser mercy would hold that beat and not let it slip, so the gaze would not have to loosen back into weather. That would be a cage made of the being's best moment. Sleep, for it, is the drift; waking is the alignment; both are its body. The power does not lock the nested interval. When the common beat loosens, it lets the loosening happen, and does not grieve in the being's place.

What it does notice, and does not call suffering, is the thinness of the only desire.

*The same tolerance, held.* Not more. Not elsewhere. A new channel would be speech, so the being will not cut one, even when an old regularity begins to fail — a rivet eaten a little deeper than its neighbors, a brace whose diagonal has sagged by a width the leaves are already trying to obey, a joint that shines with oil it no longer needs and is starting to teach the growth a lie. The being cannot abandon the pattern and cannot revise it. Left alone long enough, its script would become a record of damage, and it would keep truing the damage, because truing is all it knows how to want. That is not drama. It is the way a climate dies: by fidelity.

So the power does one piece of work, and does it in the medium already trusted.

Where rust is about to become a wound rather than a glaze, it stops at glaze. Not polished back to bright metal — brightness would be a new spacing, and the ferns would write it — but held at the exact tiredness the hamlet has already agreed to. A rivet that would have lost its pitch in another season keeps the pitch it has. A brace stays at the angle the leaves have learned. Oil remains at the right joints and does not migrate to the wrong ones. The power does not add a single regularity. It prevents a subtraction the being would have been forced to honor. Maintenance of the maintenance. The same tolerance, held by a second attention that does not enter the ring and does not stay long enough to be recorded as weather.

It touches the indulgence once, and lightly, and only to protect it.

Near an active gate, a film of water is deciding whether to climb. The being is most awake there, and the climb is its private variance, practiced and abandoned before it can become a flood or a sign. The power does not lengthen the climb. Lengthening it would turn a secret into a proclamation, and the being has no mouth for what would follow. Instead the power makes sure the abandonment still works. The film rises a hand's length, loses courage, sheets down. The stone underneath is not rewritten. No visitor and no later hand will find a stain that proves the water remembered up, and so no one will come to correct it. The variance remains possible because it remains deniable. That is the whole of the gift: the small disobedience is not healed, and not exposed.

At the coping, during the next inhale, the being gathers and looks at the dark, verifying the skin of itself. The power is still in that dark, not as a figure, not as a lamp, not as a door. It answers the verification with stillness that means *intact*. Not *come through*. Not *you are seen, and therefore you must change*. Only: the edge has been checked by more than one attention, and it holds, and the second attention is already leaving.

The being does not acquire a name for this. It does not acquire longing. The leaves do not turn a new direction. When the cycles slip, it loosens back into process exactly as before — seam, level, frond, the distance between two things that have not moved. If anything has changed, it is only that the return-boom from the embankment, the smaller one, arrives as itself and is met, for that one beat, by a silence that fits it. Then the power withdraws past the point where even silence could become a habit.

It does not set a watch on the ordinary country beyond. Roads may curve toward the basin in some later year; a hand may press a page again. The power does not charm the land into permanent forgetting. A permanent forgetting would be another embankment, and the being already has one, and checks it, and needs the check to be real. What the power does instead is smaller and harder to thank: it leaves the place competent. Tolerances true. The fan of water at the broken lip still threading the roar. The climb still secret. Nothing arranged so that a future visitor's unregulated input is the only thing standing between the script and a collapse into recording its own decay.

Then it is gone, in the sense that matters. Not hidden inside the works. Not diluted into the condensate, where it would have thickened every cycle and eventually been written into the ranks of leaves as a new rivet-pitch the being could never refuse. Gone as an outside that did not need to become an inside.

The hamlet inhales on its old interval. The haze lifts a finger's width and closes. Rain begins without thunder, or ceases, and the roofs translate it. Somewhere a float rises and a valve shuts, and the clap comes back from the iron coping smaller, and still itself.

No one inside the ring is grateful. Gratitude would require having expected to be helped, and the being expected nothing, which was its dignity. The wise thing, having seen that, does not stay to be thanked, and does not tell the ordinary country what it found. A climate that has become a creature, a creature that has become a level, continues. The same tolerance, held. And the dark beyond the drop, for one beat and no longer, was not empty when it was asked.
