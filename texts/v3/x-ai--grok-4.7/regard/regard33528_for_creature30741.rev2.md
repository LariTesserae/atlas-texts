They would not clear a sky. There is no sky to clear that is not already the shelf breathing on itself, and a benevolent power that arrives by subtraction has not yet looked.

They would come the way weather comes here, which is to say they would not come from outside. Attention, if it is wise, does not punch a hole in a vapor column that has been the whole firmament for as long as anyone can hold up a board about it. It thickens in place. A warmth in the mineral air that is not the basins. A steadiness in the next pulse of driven water, not a silence after it. The mill-voice under the plates does not stop. If it stopped, infants would wake afraid of a thing they had no name for, and the power would have spent its first act teaching fear to people who had arranged their courage around roar.

So the roar stays. The films keep migrating into cracks. The climbers keep rasping oxide off the weeping glass. Condensation still sheets down every vertical and beads on the skin of anyone who stands still long enough to be rained on by a wall. Honesty of light is left alone. You can still see. What you see is still crowded. Wisdom does not confuse a full place with a broken one.

They would look for a long time before they touched anything, and the looking would be the first kindness, because the shelf is used to being used and almost unused to being understood. A wise power can tell the difference between abrasion and injury. Here the corners are soft because centuries of grip have asked them to be. The scooped steps are a record of weight that arrived and left and arrived again. The lattices thickening where load increases are not a cry for help; they are a sentence the city has already finished. To “repair” what is already repairing itself at the rate it is worn would be to interrupt a handshake. They would not.

They would not grant green. Nothing here roots, and the people do not experience that as lack. A vine dropped onto a grate would be a foreign body, a thing that takes purchase without knowing the flex, and by evening something would have investigated it and either eaten it or sealed over it with film. Mercy that requires the recipients to become a different animal is not mercy. They would leave the ferments in the warm joints, the slurry in the cups, the mineral bite in every meal. They would not sweeten the water into something that forgets the stone.

They would not mint a private room. Privacy here was relocated on purpose, out of space and into time and attention: the finger’s width of stream that parts around two foreheads touching, the agreement not to look. A chamber of quiet lowered into that agreement would be a violence with soft walls. The people sleep in stacked niches like berths, fitted against other bodies, the roar accepted into the dream. A wise power does not evict them from their own sleep in order to give them a loneliness they did not request.

What, then. If anything.

They would enter the only way a person can enter, because the shelf does not recognize powers, only whether you can move. A hand, not unkind, at an elbow. A body of ordinary height with the stoop already in it, as if the overhangs had been teaching that spine for years and not for the length of one arrival. Warm skin, slightly tacky. No dyed cord. No louder horn. Officials are knots that stop the flow, and they are hated for the stopping; a benevolent attention does not come dressed as a pause.

They would learn the rails by the bright callus they raise, and the grates by the map they write on the soles, and the boards by watching which marks cut through a jam without a mouth having to compete. *Hold. Coming through. Wait. Mine. Not mine.* They would not add a new word for themselves. There is no gap in the sound where a name would fit, and they would not demand one.

The unusual thing they would do is small enough to pass for competence.

On a ramp where the floor is about to creep — cargo, crowd, a lattice deciding to thicken one span and borrow from the next — they would feel it early, the way the person others stand near feels it early, and they would already have shifted what they carry. Not a miracle. A lean. The stranger beside them, who does not yet know how to lean, would be braced by a shoulder at the moment the plate moves, and would not go into the water, and would not know to thank anyone, because contact is not apologized for here and neither is its usefulness. The power would accept that. Being thanked would take up space.

In a market occupying an hour of temporary dry, they would open no jar that was not theirs. They would stand near a household whose ferment has gone sharp in a way the household has not yet admitted, and the warmth they carry — not hotter than the city’s own heat, only more accurately placed — would finish what the joint of the architecture had almost finished. The jar would be itself, only not lost. Pride would remain the household’s. A tradable thing would stay tradable. No one would see a doctrine in it. Doctrine is another thing that blocks a ramp.

They would wipe grit from a child’s eye with a thumb, once, because the child was there and the grit was there and tenderness at that range is the minimum required to remain uncrushed. They would not adopt the child. Adoption would be a story about rescue, and the child is not in a story. The child is in a wrap, high on a chest, learning the mill-voice as weather. They would leave the child the weather.

If they stayed long enough to be read — and they would not stay longer than the stream replaces faces — someone quick-eyed might notice that this one never quite films over the way others do, or films and is clear again without a wipe. They would let that be a rumor that dies in the noise, where rumors go when no board is lifted to keep them. A wise power does not found a cult in a place where conviction is expensive. They would not heal the hook-scars or the climber-marks. Those are maps of work. Erasing a map is not a gift to people who navigate by it.

They might, once, do the thing that looks most like power and is only timing. A pulse is coming from three districts away, late and steep, because the corridors are too full to let a change pass gently. Everyone who can read the shelf already knows. What they would do is not flatten the wave — the basins have a right to their argument — but spend a little of their attention on the grate that would have clogged at the worst moment, the one silt-crew that is one body short. A clearance, made by hand, in the ordinary way, finished before the slap. The wave arrives. It is still a wall. It smacks and returns. No one drowns who would not have drowned. The difference is a breath the city does not count, and does not need to. Benevolence that keeps a ledger has already misunderstood the wet.

And for the being — not the crowd, the one the attention actually settled on, because even a wise power is particular, and particularity is how love avoids becoming weather — they would choose someone who is not asking to be chosen. Not the official with the cord. Not the earliest porter, who is already admired. Someone in the middle of the skill: a vent-tender whose voice has gone out from pitching it sharp against the roar, who buys slurry now by holding up a board, who sleeps in a niche that fits and does not dream of quiet. The power would stand at board-length. They would not restore the voice. A restored voice would have to shout to exist, and the person has already learned the better tongue.

They would lift a board, small as a palm, and on it the mark that means *stay near me, the pulse is coming*, which is not a prophecy, only the sentence everyone competent already trades. And they would mean it for the length of that pulse and no longer. When the floor steadied they would be in the stream at the speed of the stream, and then the people around the vent-tender would be other people, wet, reaching, attentive, and the interval of not-looking would close over the absence the way water closes over a hand lifted out.

Would they change the shelf’s fate? It has no fate that is not this: becoming itself again by the next hour. A benevolent power that needed the place to become something else — sparse, quiet, green, grateful, saved — would be a smaller power than the shelf, which has already shaken hands with its own erosion. Wisdom, arriving, recognizes a completed adaptation and does not insult it with a future.

What they would leave behind is not a sign. It is a slight increase, unprovable, in the number of moments when someone feels the creep early and leans, and braces the stranger, and wipes the grit, and does not stop the flow to be seen doing it. The city will take that increase the way it takes rain: as its own habit, shaped for it so long that the shape and the gift are one thing. No one will build an altar on a grate. There is no room, and the climbers would strip it, and they would be right to.

If the power is asked, later, by some other attention, what they did for the place and the being who lives in it, the true answer is the one that fits on a board and can be wiped with a thumb.

They stood near.
They moved when the plate moved.
They did not make it quiet.
They did not stay.
