They do not open it.

That is the first mercy, and it is almost indistinguishable from refusal. A power that loved the way a hand loves a closed fist would split the mineral skin and call the split rescue. The water would learn weather. The pressure that is their breath would go out into a sea large enough to have horizons, and the city — which is a held breath that learned to be streets — would spend itself in one ungoverned pulse, crowns flaring at a light that is not a thin place but a sky, bodies swelling because nothing nearby refused them the volume, lanes becoming throats, throats becoming mouths, and then the cold honest sea would correct them the way neighbors correct a body that will not bud: by not leaving space. Opening would be a kindness shaped like a flood. The wise power looks at the pore and does not mistake the unfinished rock for a wound.

It also does not hurry the closing.

The basalt is still choosing faces. Left alone, it will donate the city to itself grain by grain until the pocket is a story the mineral tells without inhabitants. That ending is not cruel. It is the law the place already keeps: you continue a structure, or you are continued by it, and the outer wall is only the oldest neighbor, refusing, patiently, to leave space. A power that stopped the choice forever would make a monument. Monuments are volumes that do not breathe. The filtering quarter would flare toward a light that never turned. The deep brood-chambers would ripen with nowhere to bud. Ambition, which the city eats in order to stay pocket-scale, would have no predator left but time, and time, unopposed by mineral, is a bad hunter. It misses, and what it misses grows.

So the power does something smaller than either mercy, and harder to see.

It becomes, for a little while, a hesitation in one crystal face.

Not a voice. A voice would arrive as dissolved change, and they have no privacy from what they taste. Sweetness would found a quarter organized around a god. Bitterness would shutter the hardened bodies that are the city’s memory of direction, and half the streets would migrate toward a threat that had no body and therefore could not be reabsorbed. The power declines to be an address. It does not shed. It does not dim. It does not take volume. It is present the way a law is present before it is written: as a slight delay in what was already going to happen.

On the side where the new face has been advancing into an alley, the advance pauses. Not for a season. The pocket has no seasons, only the slow turning of which thin place in the skin is clearest, and the occasional warm pulse that is weather because it ends. The pause is the width of a lappet and the length of one budding. A nursery that had already begun to split — grain brightening from bruise-violet toward the yellow of fresh work, the parent-tube narrowing its crown because adulthood here is a direction, not a size — finishes the bud into flow instead of into the mineral. The new body seals nothing. It takes the edge of the lane the way a course of masonry takes a gap: by staying. The alley flexes. A thousand small pumps adjust. The street is born again on the near side of a wall that, an hour from now, will remember how to move.

That is the whole intervention. One street that continues. One generation that is not lithified before it has voted, with chemistry, which way to secrete.

The power watches what the watching costs, because benevolence that does not audit itself is only appetite with better manners.

The nursery does not know it was spared. Spared is not a flavor. Upstream, a body that has begun to thicken — keeping what it should have passed on, grain multiplying without budding sideways — still meets the refusal of its neighbors. The power does not enlarge it. Largeness is the danger that is not strength. To save the swollen one would be to spend the volume the lane had agreed to spare, and the agreement is the only sanitation they have. The correction proceeds: lappets hardening, a pressure change, a scrap of membrane already turning into mortar. What the power does with the bitterness is thinner than rescue. It lets the death be local. The bitter filament does not travel the full length of the hardened quarter. Three districts that would have dimmed, and sealed, and donated themselves to a panic, taste only the ordinary sweet of a flush from a crack and go on filtering. The hunter, corrected, is a course in a wall by the time the clearest thin-place has shifted a finger’s width. No exile. There is no direction here that exile could take. There is only reabsorption performed cleanly, without conscripting the memory-bodies into a false alarm.

If the power feels grief, it does not put the grief in the water.

It looks at the being, and the looking is the difficult part, because the being is not singular in any way that would comfort a power accustomed to faces.

There is the tube that stayed finger-small and keeps the lane by refusing, chemically, to let a neighbor swell across the flow. There is the opaque throat in the deep quarter, sealed, still tasting seepage, still capable of dimming when the seepage goes wrong, a citizen that has become an archive. There is the larva-grain brightening inside a parent that will not be left. There is the predator-posture, already reverting, already civic again. None of them is the being, and all of them are. The being is the continuity: the fact that a street can migrate without a scream, that identity is a flavor with a half-life, that the city and the geology are the same age and neither has been allowed, this once, to win by an hour.

The power loves that, if love is the right name for a decision not to improve a thing into something else.

It does not teach them. They have no tool-using ignorance to correct. Their ignorance is exact. They do not know the sea, and the sea would kill them. They do not know speech, and speech would arrive as a permanent chemical lie, a sweetness with no food behind it, and they would build a facade toward it until the facade starved. They do not know that they are hidden. Hidden is the condition of their scale. To be known from outside as a city would require an outside large enough to hold the knower, and the knower’s attention, if it stayed, would be a pressure, and pressure is how hunts begin.

So the power does not stay in any form they could found a quarter on.

When the bud has taken its place and the crystal face resumes — not faster, not slower, only again — the hesitation ends. The mineral returns to its work of being the oldest building. The animals return to being the part of the building that still moves. No shrine condenses at the site of the pause. The water there tastes of nursery and of the ordinary bitter of a corrected thickness, and then, as the flow passes, of nothing in particular. Addresses remain chemical. You still know your quarter by who has been secreting upstream of you. The god, if they required one, has already failed to become upstream of anything.

What remains is not a miracle they can maintain. Miracles that must be maintained become infrastructure, and their infrastructure is already their flesh. What remains is a street that exists because a wall hesitated, and a power that considered the hesitation sufficient.

It will not do this every time. Wisdom is not a policy of endless postponement. Some faces will advance through nurseries that do not finish. Some swollen bodies will panic three districts before they are used as mortar. The pore will shrink. From the outside it will look, more and more, like a defect that healed. The power knows this and does not arrange a permanent exception, because a permanent exception would be a second world glued to the first, and they have no volume for a second world.

It does, once, something even smaller than the pause, and only for itself.

It learns their honesty. Every body reports, in what it sheds, exactly what it has been eating and how the water changed it. The power, which could counterfeit any report, declines the counterfeit and accepts the standard: if it is not willing to be tasted truly, it will not speak. That standard is why it leaves no blessing dissolved in the lane. A blessing would be a sweetness untethered from a crack in the rock, and they would follow it until they sealed themselves into a mouth drinking nothing.

Before it goes, it attends to the place as a place, not only as the sum of the postures.

The crowded pocket. The light that leaks and does not fall. The pale filaments apologizing on a seam. The civic respiration that never becomes sound. The warm pulse moving through the lower quarter like a season that has not been informed it is only leftover heat. The rules that settled out of density: do not block the flow; do not outgrow the agreement; become the wall when the wall requires you; let predation be sanitation; let the rock write law and answer by being born again on the other side of it, if you can.

The power does not repeal a rule. It does not add one. It holds, for the length of a budding, the space in which the rules can still be practiced by the living rather than only remembered by the stone. Then it lets the holding go, the way a hand lets go of water without claiming to have improved the river.

If anything marks the visit, it is not in the pore. It is in the power: a revision of what benevolence is allowed to want. Not opening. Not saving the singular at the expense of the lane. Not the eternity of a lit tank. A hesitation the width of a lappet. A bitterness kept local. A street continued. And the discipline, afterward, of not being worshipped for it — of leaving the water honest, the mineral free to resume, the beings unaddressed by any name they would have to build into a facade.

The pore goes on closing, as it must.

Inside it, for a little longer than it would have, a lane keeps its width. Crowns open toward the thin light. Grain ripens. A body that might have been masonry before it chose its direction chooses, and the choice hardens into lace, and the lace passes a faint sweetness when the crown upstream flushes, and that sweetness is only food, only chemistry, only the city eating its own weather.

The wise power, having done the one thing that did not unmake them, does not return to watch them thank it.

There is no gesture in them for thanks that is not also a gesture for the rock. To bud is to found a lane. To seal is to donate yourself. To hunt is to keep the water thin enough to breathe. They spend the spared hour in the only devotion they have ever had, which is continuance, at the size the pore will allow, in the direction the last true taste indicated.

That, the power judges, is enough to have guarded.

And it leaves them to it.
