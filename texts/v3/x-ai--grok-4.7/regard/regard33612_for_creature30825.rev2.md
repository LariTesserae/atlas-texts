The power did not arrive. Arrival would have been a change in the weather the place had not banked for, and wisdom, here, began with that accounting.

It had been passing in the way such powers pass, along the thin high roads that are not roads, attending to other ledgers, when the plateau’s subtraction of sound snagged it the way a seam of charge snags a hair. Attention turned. Not downward, exactly. Across. The dark lid, the pale reaches, the islands of thicket with their private columns of drizzle, the towers failing at the rate of their growth. It looked the way the place looks: without haste, and without the need to be seen looking.

What it found was not suffering in the lower sense. The cold was not an error. The thin air was not a neglect. The residents were not waiting to be lifted into some brighter law. A thumb-joint of copper shell rasped a frond and plated the rasp back onto itself. A low body, a valley’s courtesy from the next, exported a coin of metal in its dung and did not know it had signed the thicket’s future lean. In a chimney that still breathed, a gel of ore held a pulse that was not yet an animal and not yet stone. At the scale of the older hollows, one continuous body shared its root-warmth with a tarn and adjusted its damp around a recent footstep, measuring, withholding, already done with the spectacle.

The power understood the machine because it was, in its own vast and different register, a keeper of rates. Add at the rate you subtract. Moisture is brief, so bank it. Do not build what the weather will have to unbuild in a single loud hour. It felt, with something like recognition, the small cold bells that died in the air before they could become news, and the conferences of prickling that constituted speech, and the residents who plated themselves into towers and called a dry century a pause in a step. None of this asked. The not-asking was the most complete sentence the place had.

So it did not lift the lid. A permanent light would have bankrupted the chemistry that ran on gradients and metal and spared warmth; the fronds would have spent themselves learning a sun they could not bank, and the eyeless would have been given a sky that lied. It did not thaw the rime into rivers. Rivers would have been a road for someone else, and the pockets were already enough, and enough was the local god. It did not wake the ones inside the seams and tell them the interval was over. Their clocks were true. To hurry a molt is to choose the rubble’s timing for it, and that is not mercy. It did not reach into the hollow and make the basin-being into a voice. A voice would have been a kind of announcement, and announcement, in this thin air, does not travel. It only spends.

What it did was smaller than weather, and it did it the way the animals keep distance: by revision, not by display.

Along one spine that had been losing charge a day too soon—news weathering off before the far crosser could read who had passed—it let a little of its attention remain, the way a hand remains on a rope in a dark stair, not pulling. The hairs there lifted a fraction longer. The conference completed. A shoulder that might have gone mapless for a moment found the trail and kept the courtesy it had already chosen. Nothing saw the hand. The hand was not the point.

In a throat where a tower had grown past what its base could bear, and the slump was due before the gel in its mouth had taken the habit of a pulse long enough to survive the fall, it delayed the failure by one slow breath of the stone. Not a year. Not a strengthening that would turn the tower into a monument and break the truce with the weather. One breath. The soft shiver in the soles of the possible came a little later. When the rubble fan opened, the darker recess was already a body, low, heavy, not yet interested in being witnessed, able to be reassigned rather than merely lost. The power did not stay to see it cross. Seeing it cross would have been collecting.

For the basin that looked back by adjusting its damp, it offered the only gift that matched the courtesy: it adjusted in return. Where the thicket was measuring the loud breath of a passing warmth that was not weather, the power made itself briefly legible in the same alphabet—a withholding, a slight thickening of the drizzle-column, a change in the rate of plating so fine it could be mistaken for the day’s own decision. Not *I am here to save you.* Something closer to *I have accounted you, and I will not spend you.* The basin, which was not a mind in the way minds are usually proud to be, took the adjustment the way it took a herd’s coins. It leaned, over a time no witness would watch, a finger’s width toward the place where the attention had rested, and then the lean became ordinary, and the ordinary became the work.

Elsewhere it did nothing at all, and the nothing was the larger part of the kindness. It left the soldered-unclamped where the discharge had made them into mat. It left the hunters their local subtraction. It left the open ground empty at the right scale, so the abundance would not be forced to live like a room. It did not correct the dark. The dark was not broken.

Before it passed on, it set one mark the place could use and no one else would notice: a standing permission, written in no script, that this plateau was to be omitted from the improvements of lesser powers—those who love a silence until they can name it a wasteland, and then bring rivers, and roads, and the loudness of help. The omission would hold as long as the rate held. If the engine ever truly failed, if the banking stopped and the cold finished its sentence without a pulse left to spend, the permission could be read the other way, and a hand might return in earnest. Wisdom is also a seam that can open. But that reading was not for this age. This age was still turning.

Then the attention lifted, not as a light leaving, but as a spine letting go of yesterday’s charge once the news had been carried far enough. The lid stayed. A gravel tick happened or did not. Somewhere a hide rang once against sinter and the thin air refused the sound, lawfully. The smallest shells went on converting shine into armor. The basin went on being a place. If anything had been done, it had been done at the speed of the land’s own patience, and the land, which does not keep visitors in its ledger, folded the kindness into the truce and continued—drizzle and clearing, plating and slump, crossing and feeding—under a dark that did not lift, no more blessed than stone, and no less accompanied.
