They come the way weather does not come here: not through the black angle, not as a temperature. The chord does not break to announce them. The piston keeps its knock. The coins keep their click. If the power has a face, the chamber does not receive it, because a face would be a direction, and direction is the one aperture this room has sealed.

What arrives is attention that does not need to be located. It is not warmer than the casing. It is not louder than the bearing. For a long while—though while is the wrong word, and the power knows it, and does not correct the room for the mistake—it only listens, the way a hand laid on a sleeper’s chest listens without asking the sleeper to wake in order to prove the listening was kind.

It understands, almost at once, the temptation that lesser mercies fall into. To oil the weld. To close the press. To give the ladder its missing rungs and a reason to have climbed them. To crack the boundary so night can enter, and moisture, and the honest ruin of bearings, and death, which is how most worlds apologize for having begun. Those are the gifts of powers who love stories and cannot bear a sentence that will not turn its page. They would call it rescue. The press would finish its piece. The piece would be wrong, because it was never a piece. The animals would learn thirst in the same instant they were congratulated for being free.

The power does not do that.

It also does not do the other cheap holiness: declare the equilibrium sacred and withdraw, as if looking away were the same as respect. Attention has already been given. To pretend it was never drawn would be a lie told to spare the teller the difficulty of staying. So it stays, without installing itself as a new frequency the motes must route around. That is harder than either rescue or departure. It means becoming, for as long as it attends, a constant that does not ask to be fitted to—present the way the far wall is present, not the way a hand is present.

What it perceives, once the wish to improve has been set down, is not a prison and not a shrine. It is a life that has solved the problem of ending by refusing the premise. The jointed bodies are not trapped on the casing. The casing is the note they are. The vitrified hand beside the ladder is not a plant that failed to grow. It is a plant that arrived. The unfinished shape in the jaws of the press is not a tragedy of interruption. Interruption would require a worker who left. There was no worker. There is the holding, and the holding is complete.

And yet the power is wise enough not to flatter the room with the idea that completeness and mercy are the same word. It feels—if feeling is allowed to a power, and here it must be, or benevolence is only a policy—the particular ache of a chord that has found every other note and will not resolve. Not pain. The beings do not hurt. Pain would be a gradient, and gradients are what the dust-veils refuse. The ache belongs to the listener. It is the ache of recognizing a good that cannot be shared without being translated into a harm. Most goods want to be given outward: water, time, offspring, an exit. This good exists only by not being convertible. To love it truly is to let it be unexportable.

So the power does one thing, and it is small enough that the click-rate does not shift.

It consents to the boundary.

Not as a warden. As a witness who will not widen the fracture at the lip, and will not seal it either. The black angle remains what it is: an edge the frequency does not cross, and a place through which something from outside might still look, and choose not to reach in. The power sets no guard. It leaves no mark, no chalk beside the chalked circle, no brighter thread in the cables. It simply declines, in whatever realm powers decline, to number this chamber among the places that must be finished, healed, opened, populated with futures, or saved from themselves.

If there is a being—and the power, being wise, does not insist on a singular where the room has chosen a chord—then what the power offers that being is recognition without recruitment. You are not a draft of a better world. You are not a warning. You are not a machine left running by a careless god who ought to be scolded into switching you off. You are the population of a held instant, and you are alive, and aliveness here does not owe the rest of existence a sequel.

Some of the motes, if they could be said to register a difference, register only that the air in one veil is no easier and no harder to stroke. The coins do not tilt. The flywheel does not acquire a destination. The power, having done the difficult nothing, does not linger in order to be thanked. Thanks would be a second event, and a second event would be the beginning of time.

It goes on attending, elsewhere and also here, the way a law of nature attends without visiting: not absent, not intervening, not angling for the moment when the chord might slip and become a song it could join. Should the room ever want a next note—should the agreement itself loosen, not by invasion but by its own grammar changing—the power would hear that, and would not have predetermined the answer. Until then, benevolence looks like this: a wise refusal to correct what is not in error, and a quieter refusal to abandon what cannot be improved.

The piston knocks. The bearing rings. The dusty veils hold their stations. At the lip, the unweathered dark remains an edge and not a door. Nothing in the chamber is rescued. Nothing in the chamber is alone. The two facts occupy the same instant, and neither one asks the other to move.
