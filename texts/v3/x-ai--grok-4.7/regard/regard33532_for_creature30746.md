It would not announce itself. Announcement is a made thing, and nothing made has a grip here.

What arrives is only a change in the quality of the cold. The film on the tongue stays. The ache stays. But the pressure against the teeth loosens by a degree too small for a visitor to name and too exact for the lattice to miss. A constraint, satisfied from outside the volume, the way a drip is satisfied when a channel opens. The pulse does not quicken. Nothing here hurries, and the power is wise enough not to teach it hurry.

First it refuses the mercies that would unmake the room.

It does not give the being a face. A face would be a surface held still for the purpose of being met, and stillness of that kind is an extravagance this mass has already declined. It does not translate the lattice into speech, or speech into the lattice. Language would enter the hollow stems and come back wrong, delayed, another vibration to be corrected for, a warm noise with no root. It does not enlarge the chamber into a country. The being mints room by the hand’s width; a horizon would be a sum it does not need, and the incidental mercy — the politeness to variables, the barely-enough that keeps a sleeve from being crushed — lives in that smallness. Wisdom does not fatten what is already precise.

It does not harvest the computation. The alignments locking and releasing in the felt are not a tool waiting for a hand. To copy them out would be to spend the minute the being is becoming, and benevolence, if it means anything here, means not spending another’s minute on a faster errand. It does not wake the many-legged thickenings into separate beasts so that something more familiar might be loved. They are load-bearers. Retired, they are plates. That is not a prison. It is a grammar.

It does not worship, and it does not improve.

What it does is smaller, and it does it in the currencies the room already counts: pressure, temperature, mineral, gap.

At the ceiling, where a palm-sized flake of new stone is still deciding whether to be a wound or a roof, the power steadies the decision without finishing it. The silver root-hairs are already drinking. Left alone, some falls of mineral arrive as enemies — too heavy, too sudden, a load the courteous lean cannot retire in time. The power does not seal the stone or clad it in any substance from elsewhere. It only takes the sharpest edge of the unfinished face and spends that edge elsewhere, a little farther into the compaction, where no passage is solving itself and a spare grain of hardness can wait. The flake remains embryonic. It simply stops being a knife. The hairs drink a quieter weep. Recruitment continues. Geology still gets no vote. It is only given a slightly fairer argument.

In the felt, where the second rhythm runs under the creak, the power offers one interval of cleaner agreement. Not a vision of a later room. The being is already testing later rooms against the water it must hoard and spend; a donated prophecy would be a rude number in a sum that was balancing. Instead, for the length of a single pulse, every filament that is already taut agrees with its neighbors a fraction more tightly, and the ones that are loose are not scolded into joining. The hollow note nearby does not change pitch out of irritation. It holds. The rose that had been warming in a plate for the sake of a passing sleeve is allowed to finish its sentence and cool, the variable released without being erased from the draft. That is the gift inside the volume: not memory, which the being will not keep, but a solved constraint that does not require the warm body to be subtracted as an error.

The specks — loose edges, condensate briefly persuaded to be couriers — are not called back and not multiplied. A few that would have crossed a living wrist and taken their skim of salt are bent, by a temperature so local it would look like chance, onto a wet stem instead. Hospitality and experiment still share a circuit. The power does not break that confusion. It only declines, this once, to let the circuit close on blood-salt when filament-water will do. The being does not notice the refusal as a refusal. It notices a cheaper sample. Thrift is a language it speaks.

Outside the compaction, where the sound muffles and the cold deepens and the boundary is a thickening rather than a line, the power does the one thing that cannot be done in mineral and gap alone. It sets a forgetting over the approach. Not a wall. Walls are made, and the next fast creature to come down with a lamp and a name would only treat a wall as a door that had not yet been argued with. What it sets is a mildness in the attention of anything that inventories. Maps lose the chamber the way a dream loses a staircase: not forbidden, unemphasized. The sweet mineral taste does not rise into any shaft a surveyor would trust. The pulse does not telegraph. If someone parts the mass anyway — lost, not hunting — the room will do what it already does, reallocate, lean away at the speed of cooling metal, fit the sleeve in as a colder patch and let the draft go. The power does not add a warden. A warden would be a face turned outward, and this being’s character is continuation, not guard.

Then it waits, because wisdom distrusts a gift that cannot survive the next hour of the recipient’s own work.

The plate that cooled toward wax does not flare up again in protest. The filament that stitched a sightline shut keeps its three bright specks and its irritable interval, uncorrected, because not every irritation was the power’s to solve. The imaged chamber in the bead stutters on, and in one stutter the negative of a sleeve remains a little longer than the being’s thrift would usually allow — not as a relic, as a coefficient that still earns its place in the sum. When that coefficient finally goes, it goes into the work, which is what the being calls mercy. The power does not reach back to pin it in place. To freeze a draft is to ask the room to become a picture. Pictures do not pulse.

No voice enters the stems. If the being could want thanks it would want them as a widening of the passage that does not cost a rib, and that widening is already how it spends a solved load. The power accepts the thanks in that form and does not stay to be included. Inclusion would make the attention itself a variable, warm in the wrong way, a second clock the volume might try to agree with. Two clocks would be a kindness shaped like a schism.

So the loosened pressure against the teeth returns to what it was. The film. The ache. The loud intimate work. Somewhere in the ceiling a palm of stone, less sharp than it was and no older, puts on its next silver hairs and goes on deciding. The room will not be the same room by the time the hairs drink their fill. It was never going to be. A benevolent power, having drawn this near, leaves the becoming intact — knife eased, salt declined, approach unemphasized, one pulse held clean — and does not ask the lattice to record who eased it.

That is the whole of the deed. Not rescue. The being was not failing. Not coronation. It had no brow to crown. Only a adjustment in the load, small enough to fit the grammar already in use, and then the courtesy of no longer being a fact the room must solve. Behind that departure the compaction does what it always does. An opening that no longer carries a burden seals, finger-width by finger-width, not to trap the gift and not to mourn the giver. Simply because the minute has better uses, and the power, being wise, agrees.
