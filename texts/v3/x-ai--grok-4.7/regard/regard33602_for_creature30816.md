The power did not arrive. Arrival would have been a step, and steps still meant something here only because the ground had forgotten how to refuse them. It was already present in the way a climate is present: not as a visitor, but as a prior condition that had simply not yet been noticed by the engines.

It looked, once, the way the ridges sometimes looked when attention dragged them nearer—except its looking did not compress distance or glaze new stone. The delayed shadows stayed delayed. The counted dust continued its slow return to the basins. The tooth-pressure of the hum neither rose nor eased. What changed was only that the looking was complete. It saw the plates seating on their long interval, the arcs that had chosen to be landforms, the single column (or the three, or the one counted as weather) turning because turning was what it was. It saw that the breakdown had finished its work ages ago and had been polished by wind into something that no longer asked to be called a wound.

There was no suffering to answer. The beings did not hurt; they persisted. Hunger, if the gradient could be called hunger, was met by the same gradient. Nothing cried out. Nothing was trapped that had not become the shape of its trap and then, over a span longer than patience, ceased to experience the enclosure as loss. The honest blue overhead was already a boundary the fracture had respected. The power understood that to reach down and restore water, or true simultaneity of light and shadow, or a biology that could drink, would be to unmake a country that had spent itself learning how to continue. Mercy, applied from outside, would arrive as another failure—sharper, because it would still have the raw edge of intention.

So it did the thing wise powers do when the equilibrium, however wrong, is the only equilibrium that has consented to last. It withheld the correction. It did not quench the arcs or still the plates or teach the dust to lie where boots had pressed it. It left the grammar intact.

What it offered, if offering is the word for a refusal to seize, was continuity of the one mercy already present: that the fracture had stopped at the ground. It thickened that clean line by the width of a decision, so the shimmer would keep shearing off flat and the blue would remain blue, not as a gift imposed but as a promise kept. No track of its attention remained for the wind to polish. The hum resumed its exact pressure in any ear that might later stand inside a volume slightly smaller than the eye could measure. The engines ran.

Far off, or underfoot, or both, a plate seated itself. The interval began again, unaltered. The power was already elsewhere, having done nothing that the land would ever need to survive.
