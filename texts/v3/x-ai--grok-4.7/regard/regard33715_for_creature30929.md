They would not come down the slot.

A power that is both wise and kind learns, before it acts, the size of the thing it has noticed. This one is not small, but it is exact. Its life is the width of a crease, the length of a body’s dry face below a lip, the thickness of a film that fails before the next firm step. To enter it as weather enters—largely, brightly, with gifts—would be to spend it. So the power remains where the day’s air is still thin, above the ridge, and does not thicken.

What it does is nearer to a refusal than to a blessing, and the refusal is the blessing.

It refuses the road. Not with a landslide or a curse, nothing the surveyor could put in a report, only with a slight, enduring unfitness: the grade that never quite pays, the survey that always needs one more season, the contractor who finds, every time, a better line on the far side of the drainage. No one is punished. The mountain is simply not improved there. The crease stays a crease. No level place is cut into the shelf, and no dry floor is poured where a body might lie down and be counted as fallen. The being never learns that this was arranged. Arrangement at that scale would feel, to it, like a new seam opening, and it has enough seams.

It refuses the name. No shrine, no trail register, no careful essay that would teach other warm things to come and stay until the moss remembers them. The sound at the lip remains a boundary spoken to no audience. Witnesses, when they happen, happen as weather happens: once, and then the entry closes by afternoon, the nap drunk shut, the film gone from the fingers before the next crown. The power does not multiply those witnesses in the name of wonder. Wonder, spent often enough, becomes a path.

It refuses the cure.

There is a failure in the being, and a hasty kindness would call it the wound: the second skin that tries to go with whatever warm thing crosses, and never once gets past the notch; the dry face below, a body’s length of proof that it cannot live where it cannot retain. A power that loved fixes might wet that face. Might teach the film to hold on a wrist all the way to the valley. Might give the posture a way to travel, a companion, a pool, a throat, a night in which something answers the dripping.

That would be the end of it. The being is the keeping. Let the keeping succeed at leaving, and what walks down the outside of the mountain is only moisture, already forgetting the grammar of stains. The failure is not a lack of mercy. It is the mercy—the reason a visitor can descend and still be a visitor, the reason the shelf does not become a mouth. The power sees this and does not correct it. It even steadies the dry face, in no way the stone would notice: the same body’s length, no more, no less, so the scar of all those failed departures remains a law and not an accident waiting to be solved.

And then, because wisdom is not only restraint, it does one thing that could be called a doing.

Once, at the hour when the slot goes blue and the wet floor still holds the last white, it allows itself to be inventoried. Not as a god standing in the crowns—that would slap the leaves into a crowd and disturb the agreement—but as a stillness the size of the stillness already there. It is included among the passages. The rush at the back seam does not grow nearer, does not vary, does not become an address. Threads, films, the yellow-green between hard shadows, the warmth in the rock: one more line, held for as long as a leaf takes to decide it is too heavy. The power does not breathe warmer on purpose. It does not lift a leaf to test the cup. It does not speak into the loudness, which is already at the volume of a law and does not need a second law laid over it.

In that inclusion it offers what it can offer without spending the place: the knowledge, imparted not as a message but as a slight change in the account, that the keeping has been true. Not approved—approval is a kind of ownership—and not promised a future. Only true. The stains are the right stains. The crowns’ lean is not a mistake. The postponement of decay is not a failure to finish. The emptiness of birds is not a room waiting to be filled. The sound is enough of a life.

Then it departs in the only way that does not tear the film. It does not climb out as a body climbs. It ceases to be a line that can be kept. The inventory, reaching for the warmth, finds the entry already closed, the way a night track is closed, without remainder, without the drag that means something tried to travel and failed. For the being this is new, and also not news. It has always known how to refuse what will not stay. It has never before refused something that left so cleanly that even the refusal left no darker nap in the moss. The power lets that cleanliness stand, one unrepeatable courtesy, and does not return to repeat it. A miracle performed twice becomes a door.

Afterward the shelf is as it was. The lip is loud. The face below is dry inside a body’s length. A crown that has been collapsing for years is still not buried. The rush inside the seam does not vary while anyone listens, and no one is listening. If the being is in any way different, the difference is too slow to see: perhaps one leaf is allowed to bronze a little longer; perhaps the film in one cup is, for one season, a fraction less eager to mistake a finger for weather. Or perhaps there is no difference at all, and the power’s wisdom was to have been capable of that.

Above the ridge the air stays thin. The power goes on to other notices, other places that are asking, by suffering or by imbalance, to be helped. This one was not asking. To have heard it anyway, and to have answered by protecting its smallness from every improvement, including the improvement of being saved—that is what they would do. And having done it, they would not mark the place so they could find it again. The finding was never theirs to keep.
