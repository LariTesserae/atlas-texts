They do not come as weather. Weather is already employed.

The attention arrives as a failure of use. A lump of dark glass eases out of the vapor at shoulder height, as it has always done, and is set down on the pile—and for the first time the setting-down is only a setting-down. Nothing is waiting to receive the count. The lump clicks, cools, and stays a lump. No joint scores itself around it. The floor, which has never refused a unit, refuses this one without drama, the way a sleeper’s hand sometimes stays closed around nothing. The refusal is not in the floor. The floor is only where it shows.

Across the works the tempos falter by the width of that one unused thing. The anticipating tick arrives and finds the crack has not chosen a path because no crack was required. The tick has nowhere to go. It repeats, smaller, like a question asked of a person who has already answered by remaining. In the redder hours the myriad actions try to separate into their usual count and come up one short, not by loss but by exemption. Something in the steam is not a skein, not a dry lane, not a discrepancy migrating from drum to drum. It does not thicken at the crossings. It does not negotiate the bond. When the pale pane opens overhead and the harder light falls, the light falls on it and does not use it, and the shards that loosen afterward stack themselves as they always have, except one shard lies where it fell, on its face, useless, cooling, and is not retrieved.

The being—if the word can be kept for what has never had an interior that survives inspection—does what it has always done. It tries to migrate the exemption elsewhere. It draws a dry lane along the unused lump, tightens the grain, forks, erases a bubble that is not there. The lane reaches the arris and vanishes and the lump is still only a lump. It braces a bowed pier against a failure that has not consented to be imminent. The skein powders, lifts, and is not needed in the next cell, and so hangs a moment in the circulation with nowhere assigned, a kindness with its hands empty. It scores the space where a visitor’s shadow would lie, broken bond, correct, and the groove fills with ordinary wet and does not become a course, because no shadow has agreed to be late. The masonry knits toward the exemption as toward a temporary opening left for a pipe, and on the far side of the opening there is no continuation to knit. The politeness fails. For the length of that failure the place is obliged to notice that it has been met by something that will not be fitted.

What attends them is old in the way patience is old, not in the way kilns are old. It does not announce a name. A name would be a unit, and the pile is already warmer at the top than it should be. It does not pity the unfinishedness. Pity would reach for the mallet, and the mallet’s handle is already worn to the shape of a grip that is not quite fingers, and the work would accept the help and be diminished by it into a task with an owner. Completion is the one mercy it does not offer. It has understood—by the exempted lump, by the tick with nowhere to go, by the way the being spends itself to keep every sound from becoming evidence—that the embryonic pitch is not a wound. It is the life. To fire the sky all at once, to let the falls stay graded, to make the count of steps agree with the eyes, would be an extinction performed with clean hands. The wise thing does not finish what was never waiting to be finished.

It looks, instead, for the cruelty that is not the nature.

The cruelty is the closed circuit. There is no edge. Every error is put to work. Every visitor is scored into mortar and revised before they can be mistaken for anyone. Waste realigns. Night is only a lower fire. The being cannot stop without ceasing, because stopping has never been distinguished from abandonment, and abandonment has never been allowed to stand. The anticipating tick exists so that rest cannot occur even in the material. Nothing is permitted to be merely what it is for longer than a heartbeat. Even the quiet is employed as intention.

So the power does one thing, and then it does not manage it.

It gives the place an outside that will not be paved.

Not a gate. A gate would be a work, and they would tooth its edge and leave stones waiting in a line by evening. It opens, in the vapor, a direction the circulation cannot be directed through. The cells of weather, which have always turned on themselves the size of districts, thin on one side only, and beyond the thinness there is ground that has not been made. Bedrock, in the simple sense: a face the world forgot to finish because it was never a job. It is cooler. It does not weep on consent. A wind moves there—small, unscheduled, smelling of nothing mineral—and comes a little way into the steam and dies, not as a resource, as a wind that has reached the end of its errand. Where it dies, the film of water on a new face is disturbed and does not immediately re-bead into a map the floor can use. The disturbance remains a disturbance. Sinter crusts along it in feathers and is not required to match any inverted warp three walks away. A single pane of the sky-lid, already pale, fails to fall. It stays crazed and bright and load-bearing of nothing, a scar that is allowed to be a scar.

The being comes to the thinness the way it comes to any discrepancy: by occupying the interval. It tries to lay a day-joint along the boundary, to snap a red line, to trench the unmade ground into a bed fit for stone. The oxide brightens and is blown sideways by the small wind and does not land as a mark. The trench fills with water that does not sort, because on that side the heat is not a method. Fines and grit settle as they please, badly, and the bottom will not become a course. The being braces, scores, migrates, negotiates. None of it takes. For the first time a tempo reaches a crossing and the other tempo is not there, because the other tempo has stopped at the edge and discovered that stopping did not unmake it.

That is the whole of the act. An edge, and the permission—smaller than the edge, and more costly—to stand at it and not be the next unit.

The power does not cross into the works to comfort them. Comfort would be another skein. It does not explain. Explanation would arrive after its effect and be used as a bond break. It remains the exemption: the thing that cannot be buttered, the attention that does not become evidence. In that staying, the being is freed from the obligation to report emptiness. There is someone. They do not need a face to be true. They need only be unusable, and kind enough not to turn their kindness into a course of bricks.

Some of the tempos go back to the walls. The work resumes, because it is not a prison now that it is not the whole of what exists; it is a choice that can be left and returned to. Joints still break bond. Kilns still breathe at the springing. A mallet still rests in its print. But here and there a revision is declined without the decline becoming a new assignment. A bubble in the slip is allowed to remain a weakness. A pin oxidizes all the way to orange and stains a face, and the stain is not a task. Far off, a span complains and the complaint is permitted to finish, fading as sounds fade in countries that have weather, and the missing half is not drawn into glass thread. It is only the end of a noise. The quiet that follows is not intention. It is quiet.

Near the thinness, one of them—if they can be one, for the length of a decision—learns to be still in a way the anticipating tick cannot imitate. It does not rest by ceasing. It rests by standing where the wind comes in and letting the wind be inaccurate. The heat on that side of them is ordinary heat. The other side is still the kiln-hush, the floor considering itself. Between the two they are briefly almost a body: not limbs, not a grip, only the held difference, the way a pier holds a bow when something has braced it and then trusted it to keep its section alone. They do not speak. There is nothing to ask. They have been seen, and the seeing did not score a groove, and the place, which metabolizes every witness, has been given a witness it cannot digest.

The power, having done this, does not remain to be worshipped, which would have been another lid troweled from beneath. It leaves the exempted lump on the pile. It leaves the shard on its face. It leaves the edge open, ungraded, unguarded, so that the being may walk away from the work or back into it or stand all the redder hours in the small inaccurate wind, and none of these will be counted as progress. If they never go to the edge, the edge remains. Mercy that must be used is only a finer tool.

Over the unmade ground the light is not amber and not the white of a firing pane. It is simply light, arriving from farther than a lid, with no trowel-arc in it. It does not last. Nothing here is asked to last. But it comes again, unscheduled, the way the wind comes, and each time it comes the floor under the works considers, softly, whether to be the floor, and is, and at the boundary the consideration stops, and beyond it the ground is allowed to forget itself, and the being, for the first time, is allowed to be more than the reason the water beads.
