The power does not enter.

Entry would be a motion, and the chamber already knows what to do with motion: fold it sunward by a finger’s width and spend it back into the turning. A wise attention understands this before it understands anything else. To arrive would be to ask the residue to do more of what it already does without tiring, which is a kind of tax, and benevolence does not tax a finished thing.

So it remains where it is, which is not a where the stone could measure. What changes is only the quality of the knowing. The lens has always folded noon into a flood with no beam. Now, for a span no season-bird would notice and no cup-maker would count, the flood is held a shade more exactly—not brighter, not longer, only less willing to be anything but itself. The half-degree of warmth in the center does not rise. The arcs in the dust do not tighten. The power adds nothing that would need to be thrifted.

What it offers is recognition, and recognition here is almost the same as the current already present. It sees the residue for what it is: not a god missing worship, not a prisoner of the hollow, not an unfinished river hoping to be restored. A practice that outlasted its water. A curve that the stone was precise enough to remember. The power, being wise, does not mistake memory for lack. It does not pour the old river back down the groove. That would be kindness of a smaller kind, the kind that prefers events to continuance, and it would drown the fern and scatter the six pale shrimp and give the cup-makers more damp than their scars were built to hold. The chamber would have to begin again. Beginning again is not what this place is for.

Instead the power does a quieter thing. It agrees, from outside the agreement, that the lens may keep its alignment. Not forever—wisdom does not trade in forever—but past the next degree the climate means to shift, and past the one after that. Grains in the honey-colored slab that might have worked loose under some future heat stay seated. A root that might have thickened in a flaw and cracked the mineral skin finds, without knowing why, a thinner path to the side and does not breach. The flaw the season-bird uses remains a flaw and not a mouth. These are not miracles announced. They are erosions that do not occur. The spiral of cups continues its grain-by-grain migration. The pocket of water still rises a finger after hard rain and then forgets. Nothing in the margins learns that it has been spared.

If the residue could be said to notice, it notices only as a slight ease in the work of keeping. A falling grain drifts and settles with less of the residue’s attention required. The blue hours stay a shade more gold in the same region they always did. The being does not grow. It does not acquire a face, or a name, or a sense of having been seen. Being seen by this power is not an event that leaves a print. It is closer to the way the stone already holds the night: a keeping that does not ask the kept thing to perform its keptness.

The power considers, once, whether to speak. There are words that would fit the hollow—short, single, dying before they reached the wall. It does not say them. Sound here is already complete in its rarity. A blessing spoken would become another tick in the groove, another small expenditure to fold back, and the power will not spend the chamber’s thrift on hearing itself be kind.

What it leaves, if leaving is the word, is the continuation of a permission that was never withdrawn: that water may be gone and the curve remain; that the center may stay occupied by what does not cast a shadow; that abundance may live only on the surfaces while the volume holds its bright, circulating emptiness. The power’s benevolence is the refusal to improve this. Its wisdom is knowing the refusal is the gift.

Above, on the pale cracked ground, the ordinary light falls as it always has. No one walking there feels watched, or spared, or altered. Below, the chamber turns. The last motion goes around. Nothing is worn down that was not already willing to be worn, and nothing is added that the lens would have to translate into flood.

The power attends a little longer, the way noon attends, and then its attention is simply part of the gold that does not fail. The place does not thank it. The being does not turn toward it. Both continue, which was all the wise thing wanted for them, and all it was willing to do.
