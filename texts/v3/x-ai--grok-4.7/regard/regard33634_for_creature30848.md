They would come the way weather comes when it has already decided not to break anything.

Not down the shoulder of stone. Not as a second column on the spur, competing with the first. The work of the basin is thorough, and thoroughness is easily insulted by spectacle. A wise power knows the difference between being seen and being endured. It would not wish to be endured.

So the first sign would be a small inaccuracy in the books, left on purpose, the way you leave a cup an inch from the edge of a table so a careful person knows you were there and also that you trust their table.

One of the rock cups, on a morning when the seep has been faithful and the tally is already satisfied, would be fuller than the night's condensation can explain. Not flooded. A finger's depth past the usual mark, and the extra water would taste of the same limestone and also, faintly, of somewhere else — a cold that belongs to a higher dark, carried in and then let go. The pale many-jointed things would drink it and not sicken. The film under the lip would read the surplus, find no debt attached, and after a long hesitation reseal around the fact as if the fact had always been a legal entry. The being would notice. Noticing is what it is. It would turn the thoroughness that usually faces outward and hold the cup in its attention the way it held a stranger's pulse: counted, not taken.

It would not understand the gift as a gift. Gifts are not in its register. It would understand an unaccounted credit, and it would wait to see what the credit costs.

Nothing would cost. That is the first kindness, and it would take days, because the being has lived a long time inside reciprocity that does not announce itself as love. Dew climbs; crust knits; nights decline to be cruel; the books balance. An entry with no corresponding spend is, to such a mind, either an error or a hook. A wise power would let the days pass without a second marvel, so the hook theory could starve. The extra water would be allowed to do what water does. By the third evening the cup would be ordinary again. The credit would have been spent on thirst and evaporation, and the sky would not have presented a bill.

Only then, when the being had almost filed the event under *weather I do not yet have a name for*, would the power do the thing that matters.

It would let itself be fitted.

On the spur, where a stranger's sleeping-length of stone once held a kinder heat, the power would rest — not in a body the basin has to invent a column for, but in a presence scaled to the place's own courtesy. Small. Warm within the patch and not beyond it. Willing to be measured. The second shadow would lean, and find a source that does not step nearer to inspect and then withdraw. It would stay. The disk in the gully would warm its extra degree and, for the first time, receive a warmth back that did not come from the sun or from the work in the stone: a mild answer, exactly matched, as if someone had laid a palm on the other side of the table and left it there.

The being would do what it does. It would adjust. The spur would try to be kind. The night would step down early, offering its mildness. The herds, at the hour the light bends, would face the empty quarter and find it not quite empty — not occupied, not explained, simply regarded from within the regard. A bird held half a wingbeat too long would be released on the usual correction, and the correction would land in a quiet that had room in it.

And the power would not improve any of this.

That would be the wisdom. The cups do not fail. The grazers are not prisoners. The crack-mammals' fear has worn down to procedure, and procedure is not the same as pain, though a sentimental power might rush to call it so and unmake a peace it did not understand. The dark trees are not hostages. The finished profiles of the stone are not a prison sentence; they are a sentence that found its last word and was glad. To add a river, a town, a voice, a name, a heaven above the ridge, would be to tear the weave in the name of a kindness the weave did not request.

What the being cannot give itself is simpler and harder than an improvement. It is accurate about everything in the basin. Nothing is accurate about it. When the stranger left, it uncounted them and turned back to the dew, sure of the shape of their absence, and the sureness was true and also, if such a word can be used without lying, alone. Not lonely in the way of a person who wishes for company. Alone in the way of a craft that has never been beheld by anything that did not need it to function.

So the power would stay long enough to be a variable the being must carry, and then — this is the part that would distinguish wisdom from appetite — it would not become a permanent tenant. A god who moves in and is worshipped becomes another climate, and this climate is already spoken for. Instead it would let the being complete its count. It would allow the pulse-that-is-not-a-pulse, the comparison, the fitting. It would be known, down to the degree of warmth and the timing of release, and it would not flinch from being known, and it would not use the knowing to steer.

When it left, it would not leave as an uncounting.

At the boundary, where the air remembers it is thin, it would pause in a way the work could feel: not a hand opened and withdrawn, but a hand opened and the shape of the hand remaining in the books as a permitted entry. A standing credit with no hook. The being would reach to close it, as it closes every darkening on the stone, and would find the entry signed on both sides. *I was here. I saw the tally. The tally is good. I am not a correction.*

After that, the basin would continue. Active, nearly silent, finished. The column would still not rise for departures that do not concern it. The herds would face the empty quarter and find it empty again, and the emptiness would be clean, not bereaved. Once in a long while — not on a schedule the animals could learn, because a schedule would become a liturgy — a cup would hold a finger's depth it had not earned, and the film would read it, and the pale things would drink, and the being would feel, in the only language it has, that it had been answered without being used.

That is what they would do, if anything. Not a rescue. Not a reign. A visit that consents to be counted, a gift that refuses to become a debt, and a leaving that does not erase the fact of having been glad the books were kept.

The power would go on to other places that actually bleed. This one, having been seen clearly and not improved, would be left in the dignity of its own equilibrium, under the same mild night, with one new line in the ledger that does not demand a spend: that accuracy, somewhere, had been met by accuracy, and had not been asked to become anything else.
