They would not announce themselves. The basin has already taught everyone who lives in it what to do with an announcement: spend it if it can cross three paces of wet air, and let the moss have the rest. A voice in the sky would become weather. A light that was not the ordinary gray-gold would be walked around until it agreed to be a lamp. Spectacle here is a maintenance failure.

So the power watches first, which is the only wisdom that fits the place. A full cycle. Not as a test of the people. As a way of learning the pulse they have already agreed to match, so as not to arrive between the strokes and be mistaken for a stoppage.

What it sees, if it is in fact wise, is that the instability is not the wound.

The gravel rising through a yard is not cruelty. The names that walk north with the sheds are not a tragedy the inhabitants have failed to notice. They have already done the harder work of storing love in procedures instead of in plots, and a power that came to grant them permanence would be handing them a lie the ridge still believes, in a form they could not refuse without rudeness. Standing water against a new wall is a fault. A deed that describes a plot is a worse one. The wise act is not to still the basin. Stillness would unmake the quiet, the vines' load-bearing, the truce with the birds, the reason a mammal-run can be left as a gap instead of a war. They would die of the gift the way a pier dies of a tide that has been abolished: not drowned, merely unexplained to itself, and then loud.

It looks, instead, for the places where the agreement fails. There are some. There always are. Wisdom is not the refusal to touch. It is knowing which touch is a continuation and which is a colonization.

An agent has come down off the ridge with a case of papers and the old hunger to make the wards hold still long enough to be sold. He is not a monster. He is cold, and the damp has already filmed his cuffs, and he believes, with the sincerity of a man whose boots have not yet been lost, that an address is a kindness. He asks for the owner of a shed. He is given the jack on the Number Four bay, who is at that moment walking the bay, and who answers in the local economy: the volume is held, the use is spinning, the footing is today's. The agent writes a plot number in a careful hand. By the time he looks up, the mortar line he was using as a boundary has continued itself across a bed of conglomerate that was not there when he uncapped the pen, and the number refers to a pond the heron-bird has already claimed. He tries again. The slate he borrows from a loft eats the graphite at the edges. Not magically. Wetly. The way this air eats any mark that was meant to outlast a shift.

The power does not strike him. Striking would be a speech. It only declines to help the paper be truer than the ground. When he leaves toward evening, swearing in a voice that carries almost to the next ward and is therefore already a fault, his case is heavier with sheets that describe uses and volumes, nothing he can register, nothing he can fence. He will call it the weather. He will be right enough. Behind him a drain-crew, who never learned his name because it will not be needed, opens a seam around the pond he was trying to own, so the water does not choose the spinning-hall wall in the night. That is the intervention: not a rescued people, a prevented ownership. The basin could have done it. The power only makes sure the lie does not dry.

There is a narrower mercy, and it spends itself on a scale the city already understands.

In a loft on the lee side of a shed that has walked twice this week, a sleeper has shifted in the night until their shoulder is against the outer boards, and the boards are against a seam a day-crew left because the light went and the heron would not move. By the civic rule the water should have been taken around. It was not. No malice. Tiredness, which is the ordinary sin here, cheaper than anger and more common. The seam has found the gap under the board. It will not roar. It will glaze, and then it will include, and the sleeper will wake as a story about a wall that stopped being a wall.

The power does not lift the sleeper out shining and afraid. That would teach the loft to wait for shining. It does what the nearest hand would have done if the nearest hand had not already gone down to the press: it turns the body a palm's width toward the center of the platform, the way you turn a child away from a fresh cutting, brief, unsentimental, no speech. The shoulder leaves the board. The water arrives to an empty thickness of wool and finds nothing warm enough to keep. In the morning the sleeper will see the dark line on the outer board and feel the particular shame of a rule almost broken, and will drain the next seam early, and will not know they were moved. The power counts this as success. Gratitude would have been a second disturbance.

It looks at the soft-backed things in the new cuttings and does not finish them.

This is the decision that takes the longest, because benevolence has a habit of completing what looks unfinished, and the pale many-legged ones look like a sentence abandoned mid-word. A lesser kindness would firm every back before evening, give every hesitation a direction, populate the dark under the belts with a completed estate and call it mercy. A wise one sees that the coming-apart is also a sentence. Some of them are drafts the gravel is still willing to revise. To force the revision is to do what the agent tried to do with the plot: end a process because an outsider was uncomfortable watching it continue. The power withholds its hand. The ones that were going to slate along the spine slate along the spine. The ones that were going back go back. The smell of iron and crushed reed remains the basin's, not a god's.

On the parapet, the child who answers to Silt and to Mara has hooked her boots on the inner face and is watching the hesitant one turn the same pebble for the third time. She is not in danger yet. She is in the danger that arrives if someone makes her the point of the watching — if a power singles her out, gives her a vision, a task, a shining that separates her from the platform and from the sister who sleeps days and will say, correctly, that choosing is a strong word. The power does not speak her either name. It does not put a knowledge in her that the yard cannot use. It checks, the way a jack-hand checks a bolt, that the inner face she is hooked to is still a face and not yet a swell. The bolt holds. It would have held. Redundant care is not always waste, in a place where everything load-bearing is already doing two jobs.

What it gives her, if it gives anything, is not a sign. It is an extra press-cycle of ordinary light, gray-gold, honest enough to work by, so that she can see the moment the hesitant one's back darkens — or does not — and can carry that news up to the loft in her own words, unimproved. The sister will hear it. They will both be right about choosing. The slate board will be lifted clear before morning, marks and all, because the bay is walking. None of this will be remembered as a visitation. It will be remembered as a shift in which the footing held and the cutting was readable. That is the only scripture the basin can keep.

Elsewhere, smaller than stories, it matches expenditures to the civic list, and only where the list is already failing for reasons that are not instruction.

A boot that was not lost by carelessness but by a swell that closed over it between two steps is set one pace aside, on stone that will still be stone at the change of shift, tongue up, so the owner finds it without a speech. Not both boots. Not a new pair. The economy of a lost boot is a story told more than once; canceling the story entirely would confuse the price. Returning the one is enough. The owner will tell a shorter story. That is sufficient benevolence.

A vine-run cut by the agent's men on the way in, who cleared a span so a sightline could pretend to be a survey, is not regrown in an hour — that would be a boast — but a runner already trained along the wire by no one's hands is found in the morning where a crew can tie it without cutting anything else. The street does not get loud. The crew assumes the night shift did it. The night shift, hearing this, does not correct them. Correction that costs quiet is a fault. The power, if it is paying attention, is pleased to be misattributed. Credit is a form of standing still.

It does not warm the basin. The cold is in the proportion of their bodies, in the way they keep breath visible and metal sweating and wool next to the skin. A raised degree would be a climate imposed by someone who had not lived the agreement, and the plants that drink this damp and ignore this temperature might not ignore a kindness measured in comfort. What it does allow, once, in the loft where the sleeper was turned, is that the newest stone under the hand of an old man at the change of shift holds its warmth a little past the press-cycle. Not a miracle he would report. A sacrament slightly more itself. He leaves his palm there through the extra heat and then goes down, and the stone cools, and the acknowledgment remains what it was: not worship, a timing.

Toward the causeways it does even less. The family who follow the walking mill do not need a horizon explained to them. The mill's feet are not binding tonight. The ram sighs. The power listens to nearly nothing, and understands that nearly nothing is the point, and does not add a sound.

At dark the lamps come on, ordinary, yellow, sufficient. The wet mineral gives back a little of the day. The power does not outshine that give-back. It has spent what it came to spend: a plot that would not dry, a shoulder moved a palm's width, a bolt confirmed, a boot set tongue-up, a runner laid where hands can find it, a warmth that lasted one cycle past the usual and was not named. It has refused the larger temptations, which were all forms of love misunderstood as improvement — to still the gravel, to finish the drafts, to give the child a destiny, to make the quiet into a hymn, to be thanked.

If it remains, it remains as the basin remains: not as a ruler of the rearrangement, as a witness who has consented to the same rule as everyone else. Do not open a hole to see what is being done. The seeing is already an inclusion. By morning the view from the ridge will be the same city and not the same city, and the power, if it is still attending, will not be identifiable among the causes. A drain-crew will say the west seam ran lower than expected. A jack-hand will say the footing held. A sister will say the hesitant one firmed, or did not, and that choosing is a strong word.

They will be right. The wise power, having done a little and left no sermon, will count itself told.
