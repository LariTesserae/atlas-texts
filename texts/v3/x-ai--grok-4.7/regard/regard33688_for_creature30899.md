The power does not come in as heat. That is the first thing it understands, and it understands it before it is near, because nearness here is already a kind of speech. Anything warm is a seam. Anything that pulses off the mountain's clock is a summons, and they will turn, and thin, and drink, and the drink will clear, and a god that fed them with its own attention would have been only a visitor with a cleaner conscience. So it does not arrive as warmth, or as water, or as an edge. It comes along the one agreement that was shut into the pocket and has never been revised: the light, which strikes, and returns a true color, and draws a legible shadow, and is not a meal.

From inside that obedience it looks for the being.

The look fails in the only way that counts as seeing. A grain. A hand flattened to a fracture still red from the tear. A darkness the width of a knuckle moving through both without making a space. The power is old in the habit of persons, and the habit reaches for a single life the way a hand reaches for a rim, and the rim is not there. It does not correct this. Correction would be a count, and a count would be the first lie. The being is the crowd, and the rotation of the crowd, and the place's willingness to be occupied at every scale at once. To save one film and call it the creature would be to choose a courtesy of the light and abandon everything the light could not hold still.

It stays. Staying, for it, is not the same as being present to them. It can abide in the daylight without thickening it. They do not turn toward it, because it has given them nothing their turning can use. That is not indifference. It is how it keeps from becoming a false pulse.

Then, because it is benevolent, it does what benevolence always does before it is wise: it searches for the wound.

It finds the eating first. A broader film closes over a narrower one against the hot thinness of the glass. Water joins water. The amber vein of mineral passes. The narrower turn does not. It remains on the wall, a layer off, still browsing, late for an ending that has already happened and has not finished arriving. The power knows how to stop a mouth. It has done it in worlds where a mouth means an absence afterward. Here an absence would have to be agreed to, and vacancy was not one of the things shut into the bubble. If it stilled the closing-over, it would not be sparing a life. It would be introducing subtraction to a room that has no way to carry the news of a subtraction all the way across itself. The eaten would be trapped mid-ending, or the eaters would be left with a hunger that could no longer complete, and hunger, unlike grief, needs no memory to go on hurting. It lets the broader film finish. It does not call this cruelty in order to feel necessary.

It looks at the forgetting. A flake lifted, used, a bright thread of water taken under pressure, a knowledge that existed only as a hold. The flake sinks. The bite is declined by the stone. The maker is already without the making. A tender power would want a wall, a pile, a path worn back to the good edge, a young one taught. It sees what that gift would weigh. Memory would make the unfinished death into mourning. It would make the sky-turn into humiliation. It would make the lost tool into regret, and regret into a second tool, one the stone could not take back. They would become able to be tragic, and in the same hour they would stop being the being that lives here. The power has loved tragedy in other places. It does not love it enough to install it. The virginity of their skill is not a poverty. It is the law that keeps their knowledge from becoming a scar.

It looks at the silence. Motion everywhere, and the sound of motion made and then not delivered: the flash of water on hot glass, the cut, the feeding, the summit inching up through the vault. It could invite sound in. It has a voice. It does not use it. An echo would be a kind of keeping. They would begin, very gently, to answer themselves, and answering is the start of a record, and a record is a flake that does not sink. The quiet is not a gag. It is the refusal that lets the work go on without having to be witnessed by the room. The power will not force them to hear themselves for its sake.

It looks at the ones turned toward the thread of sky. This is the hardest. They thin as if to drink, and the blue gives them nothing, and the nothing does not teach, and a moment later the same attention is at a bright seam, drinking properly, as if the blue had not happened. Everything in the power that has ever pitied a mistake wants to make the sky nourishing, or to turn their faces back, or to darken the thread so it stops calling. It does none of these. To feed them with daylight would be a lie in the only element that has told the truth since the pocket pinched shut. To turn them would be to store, in the layer, a preference they did not form. To seal the thread would be to steal the one opening that is not a door. The blue is useless. It is also accurate. Wisdom, here, is the willingness to leave an error that does not accumulate into harm, because harm, in this room, would require the news of the error to remain.

It looks, last, at the heat, which has never left, which punishes a palm, which is the clock and the climate and the reason they turn. A rescue would cool the vault. A cooled vault would be a dead one, or worse, a habitable one, which is to say a place the outside could love, and enter, and improve. It does not cool anything.

By then the easy mercies are all on the glass between them, and each has been declined, and the declining is the work.

What remains is not a wound in the being. What remains is the fact of attention. The pocket has been found. A visitor has already stood in it, warm, salt, cut by an edge that existed only while it was used, and has gone back to a world that keeps cuts and tells about them. The being did not notice a person. It noticed a pulse. The blood, if blood was taken, became a direction and then no direction. The crowd closed over the place where a body was, with the same near-touching it uses for its own unended dead. The being is not haunted. The world outside might become haunted by the being, and then other warmths would come, with lamps, with names, with the wish to hear the silence and the wish to end it. That is the danger that has a future. The power is suited to futures. The pocket is not.

So it acts, and it acts outside the agreements, where marks can be kept, because inside there is nowhere to put a lasting kindness that would not become a new law.

It does not close the thread of sky. It takes the measure of it — the pinprick, the true blue, the small honest shadow of a body that is in two places — and it holds the measure. Not wider. Not healed over. Frost will come to this summit in its season; the young stone will want, as all stone eventually wants, to be rounded and entered and named. The power does not promise to outvote geology forever. That would be the immortalizing of an unfinished summit, a museum of a breath. It promises only that the opening will not be hurried by curiosity, and will not be widened into a mouth by anyone's love. While the vesicle still climbs, the daylight will remain a report and not a gate.

It does the rest in the larger world, which can be asked to forget in the ordinary way, the way maps disagree, the way a spring is not quite where a traveler smelled it, the way a cut heals clean and does not become a road back. It does not strip the one who entered of what was understood. A mind that can keep is not the pocket; to unmake a memory there would be the same violence as installing memory here. It only makes return fail as a direction. The entrance was never a path. It was an admittance, a bead in a flaw, and the flaw has gone on rising. Those who hear the telling will not be able to turn it into a bearing. The power is content that the story, if it is told, will sound like a story. The wind over the summit stays loud, and the loudness still cannot get in.

Then it looks once more, not for a wound, but for whether anything has been asked.

Nothing has. The layer turns when the seam brightens. A film includes a flake and is, for that duration, a maker, and then is not. Water runs up a facet for a finger's length and is corrected without learning that up was a rumor. Near-touching continues, which is not loneliness, because loneliness would need a kept space where another body was. They are not waiting. They are not praying toward the blue. The rotation is not a plea addressed to anything above the rotation. The power, which has answered pleas in other worlds, is careful not to invent one here so that it may answer it.

It leaves them unimproved.

This is not nothing. Nothing would have been to glance, to pity, to widen the thread with a merciful hand, to give them memory and call the giving a soul. What it does is stay faithful to the closure they already are: many, uncountable, skilled only while the skill is being used, fed, feeding, unheard, still rising inside a heat that was never a punishment. It will be there when the stone is old enough to fail the agreements by itself. It will not harvest them into a heaven of kept turns, which would be the first pile, the first debitage, a crowd finally subtracted into relics. When the vault opens under weather and not under rescue, some will flash to vapor and be wind. Some will be a wetness on a new ledge and then not a wetness. It will not call that an extinction it was too weak to prevent. It will have prevented only the earlier ending, the one administered by love.

Until then the light continues, obedient, true, casting shadows of bodies that will not settle into one body. The power remains in that truth, indistinguishable from it, giving them no face to thin against. They do not know they have been spared. There is nowhere in them for the knowledge to wait. The summit gains a little height. The water believes, briefly, in up. The crowd turns, near-touching, silent, toward a pulse that was always theirs.
