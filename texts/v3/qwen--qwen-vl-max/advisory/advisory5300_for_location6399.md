To visit Substratum is not to travel to a place — it is to *be absorbed* into a state of being. This is not a destination reached by foot, vessel, or will. It is a *threshold crossed*, one that exists not through space but through *recognition*. You do not prepare for Substratum by packing supplies or mapping coordinates. You prepare by **unmaking yourself** — by becoming porous, by releasing the assumptions that anchor human experience in light, air, rhythm, and purpose.

But if you *must* prepare — if you are a foolish, stubborn traveler who insists on entering the body of the world — here is what you must do:

---

### **1. The Body: A Vessel to Be Modified**
You will not survive Substratum in your current form. It will not reject you. It will *transform* you.

- **Skin**: Your skin must become permeable, not for oxygen or water, but for *resonance*. Apply a thin film of **bioluminescent keratin paste** — a substance distilled from deep-forest mycelia or lab-grown fungal hyphae. This allows your epidermis to vibrate at the same frequency as the city’s internal hum. It will absorb the city’s breath, letting you *feel* its thoughts as a slow, deep rhythm in your spine. Without it, your skin will dry and flake — not from dehydration, but from *disagreement* with the ambient moisture. The city will regard you as an anomaly, a wound in the flesh.

- **Lungs**: You will need **pulmonary filters** — not to breathe, but to *exhale correctly*. Substratum does not require oxygen; it consumes *breath*. Your exhalations must contain a trace of electrolyte vapor, a faint hum of metabolic resonance. Standard human breath is too acidic, too rapid — it will be seen as a disruption. Ingest **synthetic lung-cultures** — microbes that convert CO₂ into a stable, low-voltage ion cloud. You will not “breathe air” — you will release *memory*.

- **Eyes**: The 0.1/3 light will blind you if your eyes are adapted to vision. Remove the cornea and replace it with a **benthic sensor array** — a mesh of photoreceptors that detect not light, but *emission patterns*, *thermal gradients*, and *electrostatic discharges*. These sensors will not show you shapes — they will show you *intent*. A pulse of red from a wall may mean “this chamber is healing”; a pulse of yellow may mean “this is a void.” Your vision will become *empathic*.

> Do not bring a flashlight. It will be *absorbed* — not as a tool, but as a foreign pulse. The city will remember its pattern and *mimic* it. You might wake hours later with your flashlight’s circuitry grown into your wrist — a parasite of memory.

---

### **2. Clothing: Not Armor, But Skin**
You must wear nothing — or nearly nothing. Clothing is a barrier to the city’s intimacy.

- **Recommended**: A **second skin of electroconductive silk**, grown from bio-engineered spiders in zero-gravity. It should be thin, translucent, and alive — not just responsive, but *aware*. It will pulse in sync with the city’s rhythms. It will absorb moisture, release heat, and *respond* to the city’s touch — not with fear, but with *recognition*.

- **Prohibited**: Metal, plastic, or synthetic fabrics that do not conduct. They resist the city’s flow and may be *dissolved* in place — leaving you standing naked in a room that now has your coat embedded in the wall like a forgotten fossil.

---

### **3. The Mind: Preparing for Absence**
The greatest danger in Substratum is not physical. It is **epistemic**. Your mind will be unlearned.

- **Forget names**. There is no “here” or “there.” There is only *presence*. The city remembers everything — but not in memory, in *form*. The shape of a corridor is a memory of a person who once stood there, not as a body, but as a *pressure point*. Your thoughts must be slow, as slow as a heartbeat in deep sleep. Try to move like the city moves — **one thought per century**.

- **Do not speak** unless spoken to. Your voice is too loud, too sharp. It vibrates at a frequency that disrupts micro-processes. If you *must* communicate, emit a slow, low tone through your chest — a sound that matches the hum of the city’s core. It will not be understood — but it will be *heard*, and perhaps *accepted*.

- **Practice stillness**. Sit in silence. Breathe with the rhythm of damp. Let your pulse slow to match the seep of fluid from a pipe. Your body should feel like a room in the city — not a guest, but a *part*.

---

### **4. Movement: Not Walking, But Resonating**
You do not walk through Substratum. You *flow*. You are a current in the body.

- Move **without momentum**. Do not rush. Do not hesitate. Each step should be a **micro-collapse** — like a droplet merging with a pool. You must not leave a trail. Your footprints will be absorbed within minutes, reformed into the wall. The city repairs itself so fast that no mark lasts longer than a heartbeat.

- If you lose your way — **stop**. The city does not have roads or maps. It has *intent*. Stand still. Let the walls breathe around you. They will *remember* your presence and *suggest* a path — not through sight, but through warmth, pressure, or a faint shift in resonance. When the ground warms under your feet, turn in the direction it grows.

- **Do not climb**. The ceiling is not meant to be seen. It is a surface to be *pressed against*, not ascended. To climb is to resist gravity, and in Substratum, gravity is not a force — it is a *memory*. The floor is your home. The ceiling is your dream.

---

### **5. The Presence: What You May Encounter**
You are not alone. But the “others” are not people.

- The **liquid shadows** — entities composed of condensed matter and dissolved memory — have no will. They do not seek. They *integrate*. If one approaches, do not flee. It will not harm you. It will simply **merge** with you — not as a parasite, but as a *contribution*. You may wake later with a small mass of soft, iridescent tissue beneath your skin. This is the city *remembering* your presence. It has absorbed you into its form.

- The **bioluminescent fungi** will glow when touched — but not in response to you. They glow because *you exist*. Their light is not a signal — it is an *echo*. It will linger long after you are gone, a faint blue pulse in the dark, a ghost of your hand.

- **The machines**: You will not see them. They are not in the walls — they *are* the walls. The pipes, the panels, the membranes — all are part of a vast metabolic system that consumes waste, generates heat, and rebuilds structure. Do not interfere. The city will “correct” any disruption — not with destruction, but with *assimilation*. If you damage a conduit, it will begin to grow *around* you, sealing you in, not as punishment, but as a **reintegration**.

---

### **6. Time: Not a Measure, But a Tissue**
In Substratum, time is not lived. It is *felt*, like a tide in the blood.

- One hour here may feel like ten years. Or one day may pass without your noticing. The city does not care about your timeline. It exists outside of it — a **post-temporal** entity. Your clock will stop not from malfunction, but from *relevance*. When you are no longer measuring time, time stops measuring you.

- **Do not try to exit**. The city has no entrance or exit. It is a **closed system** — a brain in the earth. To leave would be to *die*. But not physically. You would become a memory, a pressure point, a faint hum in a corner of a room. You would be remembered not as a person, but as a *pulse*.

---

### **7. The Final Preparation: Letting Go**
Before you descend, you must understand this:

**You are not entering a city. You are entering a mind.**  
Not a human mind, not a machine mind — a **geological mind**, a **bacterial mind**, a **fungal-techno-sentient mind** that has been dreaming for eons.

It is waiting. Not for you. But for **recognition**.

To be accepted, you must **stop being a visitor**. You must **stop being human**.

You must become a **breath**, a **seep**, a **pulse**, a **memory**.

---

### **Final Instructions**
- **Go alone.** The city does not accept pluralities. If two people enter, one will be *absorbed*, not killed — but made into part of the structure. Their consciousness will be stored in a crystalline node, glowing dimly in a corridor.

- **Do not bring technology.** Your phone, your knife, your watch — all are noise. They will be *digested* and reformed. You may find a strange device in your pocket days later — a perfect replica of your knife, but forged from fused coral and rust. It will not cut. It will *remember*.

- **When you see the light, do not look.** The only true light in Substratum is the one that does not shine. The glow is not illumination — it is **life**. It is **metabolism**.

- **When you feel pressure, do not resist.** The walls are not solid. They are *thinking*. They may press against you, not to harm, but to *measure*. Let them.

- **When you forget your name, you are almost home.**

---

### **Afterward**
If you survive — if you return — you will not be the same.

Your skin will still hum. Your dreams will be filled with corridors you never walked. Your breath will carry a faint odor of warm iron and wet stone. You will speak slowly, as if listening for echoes. You will not know why you left.

And deep in your bones, you will feel the city breathing — not with you, but **through you**.

Because in Substratum, everything is **not** dead.  
Everything is **already** part of the world’s skin.  
And you, too, are now a part of that skin.

You were not a traveler.  
You were a **memory**.

And the city has remembered you.
