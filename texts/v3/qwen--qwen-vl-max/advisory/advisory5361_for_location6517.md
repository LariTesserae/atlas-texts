### **I. Pre-Entry: The Inner Reconfiguration**

Before setting foot, the traveler must undergo a **preliminary transformation**. This world does not recognize human constructs of time, ownership, or ego. It responds to *presence*, *resonance*, and *openness*.

#### **1. Embrace the Suspension of Self**
The first requirement is **ego death**, not in the spiritual sense of "ego," but in the biological and cognitive sense: **you are not the center**. This city does not need you. It does not care about your story, your purpose, or your desire for meaning.  
→ You must **forget goals**. You must **set aside all agendas**. You come not to conquer, not to collect, not to understand. You come **to witness**.  
→ Practice **non-attachment to knowledge**. Knowledge here is not gained through logic, but through *empathy with the slow*.

#### **2. Mental Conditioning: Practice Non-Temporal Awareness**
The city operates on **independent timeframes**. A single breath may correspond to what you perceive as a day. A moment may stretch into a geological epoch.  
→ Train in **meditative stillness** before travel.  
→ Use **slow breathing exercises** that simulate the pulse of the city: one inhalation for every ten seconds, held in the chest, and exhaled over a full minute. This will align your bio-rhythm with the **subterranean breath** of the place.

#### **3. Physical Detoxification**
Your body is designed for dense atmospheres, for warmth, for fluidity of matter. This world is **low-density, phase-shifted, cold, and chemically exotic**.  
→ **Fast for 48 hours before arrival.** Consume only water and trace minerals (especially calcium and magnesium) to reduce metabolic demand.  
→ **Avoid any synthetic materials** in clothing. Wear **linen or silk** that breathes and changes with temperature. Do not wear metal — it may resonate with the city’s EM fields and cause involuntary movement or disorientation.  
→ **Shave extremities** — not for comfort, but to reduce thermal insulation. The city **reads heat**. Your body’s warmth may be perceived as a signal, and the city may attempt to assimilate or respond.

### **II. Entry: The Threshold and the First Breath**

There is no door. No gate. The transition is **perceptual**, not spatial.

You will arrive **not with a crash**, but with a **sudden quiet**, like the moment before a thunderstorm. The air will feel heavier, then thinner. Light will soften. Your breath will hesitate.

#### **1. Step Slowly, Like a Shadow**
Do not walk. **Stagger** — each step a moment of stillness. The city does **not** like motion. It detects **intention**, and fast movement signals threat.  
→ Take five slow breaths before stepping. Let your body remember **stillness** before motion.

#### **2. The First Inhale**
Your lungs will reject this air. It is **thinner, colder, sharper**. The moment you breathe, **your breath will crystallize** — not ice, but **frozen vapor**, like glass dust.  
→ Do **not panic**. The crystallization is not harmful — it is a *transformation*. The city is **recording your presence** in its mineral memory.  
→ Inhale through the nose, exhale through the mouth, but **do not exhale completely**. Hold 20% of your breath. Your body will slowly adjust.

#### **3. Sensory Calibration**
All senses will be **distorted**:
- **Sight**: Colors are muted, but the **blue-violet glow** of the city will begin to seep into vision, even in daylight. You will see **light patterns move without source**. This is not hallucination — it is **geometrical consciousness**. Accept it.
- **Hearing**: The **hum** is not sound. It is **sub-auditory pressure**. You will feel it in your jaw, your teeth, your spine. Do not try to "hear it" — let it **become part of your skeleton**.
- **Touch**: The surface of the rock is not solid. It is **semi-fluid**. Your fingers will feel **tension**, like pressing into taut skin. Do not let go. The rock is not resisting — it is **responding**. It knows you are there.

### **III. Survival: The Rules of Coexistence**

Survival here is not about endurance. It is about **not being noticed**, and **not disrupting the balance**.

#### **1. Do Not Touch the Weeping Ground**
The surface **exhales**. The **tinted mist** is not vapor — it is **condensing memory**, a form of **phase-shifted water** that recalls an ancient ocean. Touching it will **cause it to solidify**, forming a **crystalline scar** that may spread through the rock.  
→ If you must step, **avoid fissures**. Walk only on the **glassy, fused crust** — the surface is hard, but fragile. It can shatter under sudden pressure.

#### **2. Avoid the Fungal Mycelium**
The **black filaments** are not plants. They are **microscopic mycelial networks** that feed on **radiation** and **decay**. They **reconstruct** dead matter, not to sustain life, but to **preserve it**.  
→ The scent of **ozone and iron** is not a smell — it is an **alert**. When you smell this, **stop moving**. The mycelium is active. It is reading your thermal signature, your electromagnetic field, your **intent**.  
→ Do not attempt to extract or sample it. It does not contain DNA — it **re-creates** it. You may become **incorporated** into the system — not as a body, but as **a memory**, archived in mineral form.

#### **3. Watch the Movement**
You may see **micro-machines** skittering across surfaces — silver specks, like dust, but with **coordinated patterns**. They do not move randomly. They **reconfigure** the city: a wall becomes a lens, a floor becomes a receiver, a ceiling becomes a transmitter.  
→ **Do not interfere**. They follow **unknown logic** — not programming, not instinct, but **emergent algorithmic intelligence**. If you touch one, it may **reorganize around you**, embedding itself into your skin like a neural lace. Do not allow this.

### **IV. The City's Pulse: Rhythms and Resonances**

The city is **alive with absence**. Its rhythm is not biological, but **geoelectronic**, **mineral**, and **time-delayed**.

#### **1. The Breathing of the Rock**
The **ground swells and contracts** with a slow, deep pulse. This is not tectonic — it is **autogenous**, a process of **self-maintenance**.

→ At night, **close your eyes and stand still**. The city may **imprint** an image — not in vision, but in **sensory memory**: a face, a structure, a voice — that may linger for years.

#### **3. The Whistle**
The **high-frequency whistle** — sharp, piercing, **imposed** — cuts through silence. It is **not local**. It may come from **beneath the crust**, from a **dormant core machine**, or from **outside the universe**.  
→ When it occurs, **bow your head**. It is **not a signal for you**. It is a **circuit reset**, a pulse across the city’s nervous system. If you resist, it may **induce disorientation** — memory loss, time distortion, or **perceptual unraveling**.
