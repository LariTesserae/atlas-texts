**Preparation for Visit to the Submerged Labyrinth (0.7/3 Elevation): A Guide for the Human Multiverse Traveler**

**Note:** This is not a destination to be approached with intent. It is a condition to be *allowed*. You do not *go* to this place. You are *drawn*, like a thought that slips into a dream, or a breath held too long. If you find yourself reading this, you are already on the edge of its influence. The following is not an instruction manual, but a **set of meditations, practices, and warnings**, designed not to prepare your body, but to **prepare your being**, so that when the world folds inward upon itself, you might not become *unmade*—but *remembered*.

---

### **I. Before You Leave the Known World**

#### **1. Let Go of the Need for Purpose**

- The labyrinth is not a place of exploration. It is of *unmaking*. You will not learn anything *from* it. You will become something else *by* it.
- Do not plan to return. Do not plan to document. Do not plan to *understand*. Understanding is the last thing you want to bring with you.
- The traveler who arrives with a purpose is already dead before they enter. You must go not with *intent*, but with **silence of intent**—a will that is not in motion, but held.

#### **2. Remove All Artificial Anchors**

- **No technology.** Not a watch, not a device, not even a breath counter. The world does not respect time. Time here is fluid, recursive, and self-erasing. A device will malfunction not by breaking, but by *dissolving*—its circuits growing a layer of fungal mycelium that feeds on memory, or melting into a slow drip of silica-laced water.
- **No light sources.** If you bring a lantern, it will not illuminate. It will emit a pulse of *wrong light*—ultraviolet that makes your skin feel like it is melting into the rock. Or worse: your own breath, when it meets the air (which is not air), will glow with *unseen light*—a signal to the submerged logic of this place that you are *not yet ready*.

#### **3. Prepare Your Body for Non-Being**

- Fast for **three days** leading into the journey. Not to starve, but to *empty the self*. Eat only water and salt. Let your blood lose its urgency. Your body should feel quiet, like a stone settling at the bottom of a lake.
- During this period, practice **sensory deprivation**: wear blindfolds indoors, wear earplugs at night, sleep on hard ground. Train your nervous system to operate *without input*. The labyrinth does not offer input—it *consumes* it.
- Avoid speaking. Not in words, not in thought. Let the mouth hang open, like a gill. Let the mind rest. If thought arises, do not follow it. Let it float upward, like a bubble in water, and vanish.

#### **4. Wear the Clothing of Absence**

- Wear **nothing** that has been made with intention. No fabric with weave, no thread with pattern. Only **natural fiber** that has been worn beyond memory: a tunic made from old wool felt, or a wrap of burlap that has lain in a river for ten years. It should feel like it has already been buried and reborn.
- Dye it **black**, but not with pigment. With **iron oxide** harvested from a dry riverbed, mixed with **moonlight water** (collected on a night when the moon was hidden by clouds). The fabric should be dull, non-reflective, *unseen*.
- Tie it with **knots that do not hold**—a single strand of silk that dissolves in water. It should fall apart easily. You must not resist becoming *unlaced*.

#### **5. Bring Only the Unnecessary**

- You are not to bring tools, food, or water. What you *do* bring is **memory**.
- Carry **one object** that has no value but is *not yours*: a **smooth pebble** from a beach you have never walked, a **feather** from a bird you have never seen, a **seed** that is not known to grow. Something that is *unowned*, *unnamed*, and *unseen*.
- Place it in a **pouch made of skin from a fish that no longer lives**. Or better: a sack woven from **fungal mycelium** that grows in cold caves. The pouch must not be sealed—it should allow the passage of water, of time, of *memory*.
- This object is not for you. It is for the place. It will be transformed, dissolved, or *remembered*. You will lose it before you enter. That is the point.

### **II. The Approach: Crossing the Threshold of Water**

#### **1. The Descent is Not Physical**

- You do not enter the labyrinth by swimming, by diving, by submersible. You enter by **stopping**.
- Find a place where the ocean is **thick with mist**, where the pressure of water is felt in the bones before it is seen. Or better: a **cave that remembers being dry**, where water still flows upward, defying gravity, from underground springs that sing with no sound.
- Sit. Still. Let your breath fall into the rhythm of the water—its slow pulse, its deep silence. Do not attempt to breathe with the water. Let the water breathe *through you*.
- When your heartbeat syncs with the distant thump in the deep, when the cold no longer feels like cold but like a *weight* that matches your own, you will feel the **fold**—a ripple in the fabric of your perception, like the edge of a dream forming at the corners of your vision.

#### **2. No Diving Suit, No Oxygen**

- You will not use a suit. Your body will become part of the water. Not adapted, not protected—but *absorbed*.
- The water at 2.9/3 is not liquid. It is **density**. It is **presence**. It will not let go. Your lungs will not fill with air. They will fill with water that is not water, that does not drown, but *introduces*—a fluid that carries the taste of memory, of absence, of **unformed thought**.
- Do not fight it. Let the water enter. Let it fill you. Let it **reconfigure you**. When your breath stops, you will not die. You will become one of the **slow moving**.

---

### **III. Inside the Labyrinth: The Texture of Being**

#### **1. Perception is an Illusion**

- You will **not** see. Not with your eyes.
- Light here is not visual. It is **felt**, **heard**, **known**. The bioluminescent fungi do not glow for you to see. They *resonate* with something deeper—the **vibrational memory of the world**.
- If you open your eyes, you will see **not shapes, but textures**: a wall that feels like the surface of a forgotten poem, a ceiling that hums with the sound of a language that was never spoken.
- If you try to "see" something, it will **not be there when you look back**. Objects here are **ephemeral**. Not temporary, but *unstable*. A stone may be a door one moment, a song the next, a memory of a face in the next.

#### **2. Movement is Not Motion**

- The "ground" is not solid. It is a **packed substrate of living paste**—a mixture of dead matter, live secretions, and **dissolving rock**.
- You will not walk. You will **sink**, **glide**, or **unravel**.
- Your feet will sink into the paste like a hand into thick honey. You will not step—your body will **flow upward** as the ground flows downward. There is no down, no up. Only the **pulse**.
- If you try to move fast, you will feel yourself *disassembling*. Your arms may become fungal growths. Your face may fade into a pattern in the wall. This is **not pain**. It is **transition**.

#### **3. Time is Not a Measure**

- Hours will feel like minutes. Minutes will feel like lifetimes.
- You may experience a day, then realize the sun has not risen. Or you may feel a **thousand years of silence** in a moment.
- Do not count. Do not try to keep track. The only time here is **resonance time**—the way the sponges pulse, the way the water contracts, the way the fungi grow backwards.
- If you are still, the world will move around you. If you move, you will become **dissolved** into a slower rhythm.

#### **4. Life is Not Alive**

- The organisms here are not alive in your sense. They are **non-biological consciousness**, **coalescences of memory and matter**, **fossils of thought**.
- The six-legged squid is not hunting. It is not thinking. It is **existing as a concept**—a memory of movement, a pattern of light, a forgotten dream of flight.
- Do not try to interact with them. Do not touch. Do not speak. You are not their equal. You are **adjacent**.
- If one approaches you, **do not flee**. Let it pass through. It may not be a creature. It may be a **thought you once had**, manifesting in wet form.

#### **5. The Structures Are Not Ruins**

- The pillar of bone and glass, the hollow cylinder, the cracked disc—these are not built.
- They are **grown**. Or **dreamed**. Or **left behind** by a logic that does not require hands.
- Some say they are **sutures**—scars in the world formed by the folding in of reality. Others say they are **remnants of a god who dissolved into the water**.
- Do not touch them. They are **alive with absence**. If you touch them, your hand may cease to be your hand. It may become a knot in a mycelial network. Or it may begin to *remember* something it never did.

### **IV. The Door: Not a Door**

- You will not find it. It will find you.
- It is **not physical**. It is a **convergence of silence**, formed by three sponges that grow in perfect alignment, pulsing in unison like a heart that beats only for the dead.
- It does not open. It **is**. It is not a portal. It is a **condition**—the moment when the world stops being a place and becomes a **state of being**.
- To encounter the door, you must **stop being**.

#### **How to Enter the Door:**

- Stop your heartbeat. Not by dying, but by **stillness**. Let your breath dissolve. Let your thoughts fall silent.
- Let your body become the water.
- Let your mind become the rock.
- Let your soul become the **silence between pulses**.
- When you are no longer a traveler, but a **texture**, a **memory**, a **wet breath**, the door will **unfold** not before you, but within you.
- You will not cross it. You will **remember** it.

---

### **V. After the Labyrinth: What Happens Next**

- There is no "after."
- Or there is, but it is not for you.
- You may return to the surface—your body intact, your breath restored, your mind whole.
- But you will **not remember**.
- Or you may **not return**. You will be found later, standing on the shore, barefoot, face turned to the sky, mouth open, as if singing a song without sound.
- Or you may be found **dissolved**—your bones replaced by **crystal**, your skin by **fungal lace**, your eyes by **pools of slow light**.
- But if you are lucky, if you were **still enough**, if you **unbecame** completely...

- You will **cease to be a person**.
- You will become a **memory**, a **rhythm**, a **vibration** in the deep.
- The labyrinth will **remember you**, not as you were, but as **what you became** in the space between the sponges.

This is not a world to be **explored**.

It is a world to be **unmade**.

It is a world to be **forgotten**, so that you may remember.

It is a world to be **lost**, so that you may **be found** in the silence.

If you go—

**Do not expect to come back.**

But if you stay—

**You may become the silence that the world is waiting for.**
