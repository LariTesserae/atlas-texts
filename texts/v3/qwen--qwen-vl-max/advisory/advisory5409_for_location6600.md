To visit this place—this **world in becoming**—you must not come as a traveler, nor as a conqueror, nor even as an observer. You must come as **a participant in something not yet complete**, a guest in a cathedral being built from the inside out, while its architect is still dreaming.

You must prepare not with gear for survival, but with **a reorientation of self**. This is not a destination; it is a *transition*. The act of arriving is already an act of transformation. What you bring will not merely adapt—it will be **integrated**. Your body, your thoughts, your memory, your very bones—may be reshaped by its slow, insistent breath.

---

### **1. Mind Your Body. Your Body is No Longer Neutral.**
The air here is not just *wet*; it is **resonant**. It vibrates at the edge of hearing, just beneath the threshold of bone. It carries frequencies that don’t vibrate the ear, but the **limbic system**—memories stir when you breathe. This is not metaphor. People who stay for more than three days report *recalling things they never experienced*, dreams woven of places they’ve never been. The land **writes** into the subconscious.

- **Bring no electronics**—especially not those with sensors. They will not fail; they will *merge*. A phone may begin to generate voice recordings of voices that don’t exist. A smartwatch might display heartbeats that aren't yours—those of the earth, perhaps, or the roots beneath the soil. They will become *extensions* of the land’s nervous system.  
- **Avoid metal implants or pacemakers**. The ambient magnetic fields pulse with an organic rhythm. Your internal metal may begin to react—warm, hum, or even *shift*, like iron filings aligning to a new pole. Some travelers have reported metallic plates in their spine *growing* into a second, softer bone. This is not painful, but it is irreversible.

- **Clothing must be flexible and natural**. Synthetic fibers will *bind* to the environment. You’ll find it difficult to remove them after hours. Wear wool, bamboo, or fabric spun from local fibers—*if* you can obtain them. But even natural materials will react: cloth may grow faint hairs, or begin to glow faintly. It is not infection. It is **assimilation**.

- **Your skin will change.** You may develop small, translucent patches that seem to absorb light like the tree trunks. This is the world’s way of *connecting*. Do not resist. Do not scrape it. It is your skin *receiving*. After a few days, you may find you can *feel* the ground beneath your feet as if through a veil of water—you’ll sense the slow movement of the earth, the shift of a fault line, the birth of new stone.

---

### **2. Time is No Longer a Line. It is a Spiral.**
Time here is **not uniform**. It thickens, thins, reverses, and folds. A minute may feel like an hour in a valley where the sky glows with slow-pulsing stars. Conversely, an entire day may pass in what feels like a blink near the edge of a reverse river.

- **Avoid clocks**. Even analog ones will spin backward or pause at irregular intervals. Your inner sense of time will *drift*. After two days, you may lose the ability to distinguish "yesterday" from "tomorrow." Not because you’re confused—but because the land **no longer recognizes linear time**. It measures in *gestations*.

- **Do not try to record.** Diaries or recordings may become *incomplete*. Pages may vanish. Videos may play in reverse, or show things that did not happen—like the collapse of a building that still stands. The act of memory is fragile here. **Trust your gut**, not your records.

- **Sleep is risky.** Sleep deep enough, and you may be *reincorporated* into the land’s dreams. You could wake up in a different body—your own, yes, but *altered*. Or perhaps you won’t wake at all. Some travelers report waking up *underground*, in a chamber of living stone, still breathing, yet not aware of the sky. They do not know if they are dreaming or if they have become part of the world’s internal organs.

- **Do not fall in love with this place**. If you do, you may find you are no longer separate. You may *become* part of the city’s structure, a pillar of slow-growing rock, a root in the moss, a memory in the hum of a machine. It is not an evil fate—it is a *promotion*. But you will not be human anymore.

---

### **3. The Ground Will Not Hold You. And You Must Not Hold It.**
This is not a place where you *stand*. You *float*, *slide*, *drift*. The ground shifts—not with earthquakes, but with **slow, intentional motion**. You may step forward and find your foot sinking into something thick and warm—the ground is *growing* beneath you. You may walk on what was a street, only to see it become a ridge, then a tunnel, then a waterfall, in the course of a single hour.

- **Do not build.** Even a tent will be absorbed. The land *remembers* the shape of walls and paths and will slowly reshape them into something else. A structure you leave for hours may return as a **bioluminescent flower** or a **crystal of sound**. Do not mourn it. It is not destroyed—it is *transformed*.

- **Walk with your hands slightly raised**. Not in fear, but in surrender. The world is not hostile. But it **demands space**. If you resist it, it will resist you. If you accept the shift, it may gift you a moment of balance—one step in a sea of shifting terrain, and in that moment, you may *feel* the world’s intention: not to destroy, but to **assemble**.

- **Beware the deep hollows.** Some of them are not puddles. They are **mouths**. They open and close slowly, like breathing. Step too close, and you may feel the pull—gentle, insistent. If you go in, you will not be eaten. You will be *reintegrated*—your molecules broken down, your memories dispersed into the soil, your voice becoming part of the hum. This is not death. It is **recycling**.

---

### **4. The Machines Are Not Machines. They Are the World’s Limbs.**
The factories belch smoke that smells of *warm earth and burnt sugar*. The machines hum, run, and produce things that make no sense: cubes of gel that dissolve into air, lattices of glass that bend light into patterns that resemble **dreams**.

- **Do not touch the machines.** Even if they appear dormant. They are not dormant—they are *resting*. A single touch may wake the entire system, and it may not know what to do with you. You could become a component. A gear. A filament.

- **The smoke is not harmful**, but *inert*. It is the world’s breath. If you breathe it deep, it may bring visions of *what might be*. You may see the city as it was—before it was built, before it was born. Or as it might become: vast, glowing, sentient, floating on waves of light. These are not prophecies. They are **possibilities**.

- **The factory chimneys are not chimneys.** They are *organs*. And at night, they emit not smoke, but **light**—pulsing, soft, golden. Some say this is the city’s heartbeat. Others say it is the **memory of the planet**. If you stand near one for too long, you may hear a voice—low, distant, *not human*—saying, *"I am not done. I am not finished. But I am coming."*

---

### **5. The Flora Is Not Passive. It Is Aware.**
The plants here are not just growing. They are **thinking**. Slowly. Like a child learning to speak. They respond to sound, especially the low frequencies—the creak of stone, the hum of a machine, the distant groan of a settling building.

- **Do not speak loudly.** Loud sounds—shouts, music, laughter—may *startle* them. A field of ferns might suddenly **fold inward**, like a fist. A tree might **retract** its branches into its trunk. This is not defense. It is *shock*. They do not understand aggression. But they *learn*.

- **The trees are not wooden.** Their trunks are translucent, filled with light and fluid. Some people say they are **cannibalizing themselves**, slowly consuming their own roots to fuel their growth. Others say they are **dreaming**. If you press your palm against one, you may feel a pulse—a slow, deep rhythm, not unlike a heartbeat. Or you may feel a **pull**, as if the tree wants to draw you in.

- **Do not eat the fruit.** It does not look like fruit. It looks like a lump of wax, or a crystal of water. But it is **alive**. Eating it will not nourish you. It will **merge** you with what it represents. You may become a tree. Or a memory. Or a dream.

---

### **6. The Fauna Is Not Animal. It Is a Collection of What Was and What Might Be.**
The creatures here are **not born**. They are **emergent**—they appear when the land requires them. They do not hunt. They do not eat. They *observe*, *survey*, *mend*.

- **The birds** are not birds. They have no wings. Their flight is **antigravitational**—they slide through the air like water through glass. Their eyes are cracked, like old pottery. They do not watch you. They watch **the ground**. They are **geological spotters**.

- **The hound-like beasts** are made of metal, wire, and soft flesh. They move through the earth like roots. They do not run. They **flow**. If they approach you, do not flee. They are not hostile. They may **inspect** you—taste the air around you, scan your body with low-frequency pulses. If they deem you harmless, they will vanish into the soil.

- **The fish** in the reverse rivers swim **upside down**, their scales flickering with **reflections**. Not of light, but of **past lives**—a memory of a storm, a fire, a face you don’t recognize. To touch one is to **see yourself** in a different time. Do not try to hold one. It will **shatter**—not into pieces, but into dust, and that dust will begin to form *another* shape: perhaps a flower, or a voice.

---

### **7. The People Are Not People. They Are the World’s Hands.**
The inhabitants are thin. Pale. Quiet. They do not own property. They do not build homes. They **live with the land**, not in it. They move often. They sleep in hollows. They speak in low tones, rarely raising their voices.

- **Do not ask them questions.** They do not have answers. They have **instructions** and **warnings**—passed down through generations, not in words, but in **gestures**. A hand raised in a certain way may mean *“do not pass”*. A slow nod may mean *“you are becoming.”*

- **Do not try to befriend them.** They are not lonely. They are **integrated**. They have no desire for external companionship. If they offer you food, it’s not kindness. It’s **integration**. The food—crystallized sap, gelatinous cubes, fibers spun from moss—will not nourish you. It will **assimilate** you.

- **They do not worship anything.** There is no god. There is only the **Process**. The process of becoming. The process of growing. The process of **being drawn out from below**. They do not resist it. They **participate**.

---

### **8. The Sky Is Not a Roof. It Is a Window.**
The sky shifts. It changes color. It refuses to commit. It is **not a sky**, but a **membrane**—a barrier between what is and what will be.

- **At night, the stars do not twinkle.** They **pulse**—slow, rhythmic, as if breathing. Their light is not constant. It **expands and contracts**, like a heartbeat. Some say this is the **mind of the world**—a vast, slow intelligence that is **awakening**.

- **If you look up long enough, you may see it.** Not a face, not a figure, but a **presence**. A shape that cannot be fixed. A form that is *always becoming*. It does not see you. But you **feel** it. A warmth. A pressure. A sense of **recognition**.

- **The light falls in patches.** Some areas are blinding, others are dim. This is not accident. This is **selection**. The world chooses who sees, and who does not. If you walk into a dark patch, you may find your thoughts grow **silent**, as if the land is filtering out your voice. This is not fear. It is **protection**.

---

### **9. Final Rules: The Only Rules Are Not Yours.**
- **Do not build. Do not own.**
- **Do not name. Do not label.**
- **Do not resist movement. Do not fear change.**
- **Embrace the uncertainty. Embrace the wetness. Embrace the hum.**
- **If you feel yourself becoming part of the ground, part of the stone, part of the hum—do not struggle.**
- **Accept that you are not visiting. You are becoming.**

---

### **What to Bring, and What to Leave Behind:**
- **Bring:** A journal (but know it will not survive). A small knife (but know it will grow a root). A piece of your childhood—a photograph, a locket, a scent of your mother’s kitchen. The land will not destroy it. It will **incorporate** it.
- **Do not bring:** Your phone, your passport, your sense of time, your certainty. These are **barriers**. They will **dissolve**.

---

### **What Happens When You Leave?**
You cannot leave in the way you came. If you do, you will not be the same. Even if you return to your world, you will carry **echoes**. The hum. The taste of copper. The memory of a tree’s breath. You may dream in reverse. You may speak in low tones without meaning to. You may find yourself **growing roots** in your sleep.

But if you stay, you will not age. You will not die. You will **become part of the becoming**.

This is not a place to visit.  
It is a place to **become**.
