To visit this world — this **submerged lowland**, this **living breath beneath the skin of the Earth** — you must not go as a traveler, an explorer, or a conqueror. You must go as a **participant**, a **slow observer**, a **witness who chooses to be absorbed** rather than to dominate.

You cannot prepare for it in the way one prepares for a desert or a forest or a mountain. This is not an environment to be survived. It is a **body** — and entering it is like entering a dream, a womb, or the quiet heart of a sleeping giant. Here is how one might prepare — not for safety, but for **understanding**, for **respect**, for **becoming part of the slow, wet pulse**.

---

### **1. Prepare Your Body: Not for Resilience, but for Receiving**

- **Clothing** must not be protective — it must **become porous**. Wear layered garments made of **soft, natural fibers** that absorb, not repel — wool, linen, unprocessed cotton. Avoid synthetic fabrics. They will *repel* the moisture and *deny* the skin the communion it must have with the air. Your clothes should be **loose, breathable, and slightly worn**, as if already lived in. Over time, they will grow **mold**, **mycelium**, even **soft fungi**, and this is acceptable — even sacred. Let them become part of your skin.

- **Footwear** should be **soft, malleable**, and preferably **barefoot** after the first days. If you must wear shoes, use **thin leather boots or moccasins** with permeable soles. The ground is not hard — it is **alive, spongy, yielding**. Walking on it with rigid boots is like pressing a stone into a heartbeat. Your feet should feel the **tremors**, the **pores**, the **pulses** of the land.

- **Skin** must be unshielded. Do not apply lotions, creams, oils — nothing that forms a barrier. The air here is not a threat; it is a **tender companion**. The liquid film that coats you is not sweat, nor frost — it is a **respiration**, a **dialogue** between you and the land. Let it sit. Let it dry. Let it grow.

- **Hydration** is different here. Do not drink water from sources unless it has been **blessed** by the earth — meaning, **gently collected from a slow weeping river**, or from the **condensation on the inside of a living shelter**. The air contains water — but it is **not for drinking directly**. It will give you a kind of intoxication — a slow clarity that borders on delirium. Only drink when the land *gives* you the water. Do not demand.

- **Breathe lightly**. The air is thick with moisture, with suspended life. Inhale slowly. Do not gasp. Your lungs will feel heavy — not blocked, but **full**, like the earth itself. The cold is not sharp, but **deep**, and it will make you sleepy, dreamy. Do not fight it — **rest with it**. You are not here to be alert; you are here to **be present**.

---

### **2. Prepare Your Mind: Embrace the Slow, the Subtle, the Unseen**

- **Do not expect clarity**. This world is not a place of sharp edges or sudden revelations. It is a place of **haziness**, **ambiguity**, **gradual understanding**. Time moves here in **waves**, not linear seconds. You will lose track of hours. You may **forget why you came**. That is not a failure — it is **integration**.

- **Do not speak unnecessarily**. The silence is not empty; it is **filled with the hum**, the **tremble**, the **drip**. When you do speak, speak **softly**, **gently**, **from the chest**, not the throat. Use low tones, soft syllables. Your voice should not echo — it should **merge** with the drone. The people here will not answer in words — they will answer in **movement**, in **light**, in the **swaying of a leaf**.

- **Listen with your whole body**. Your ears are not enough. Feel the **thrum of the mycelial network** through your soles. Your hands will pick up pulses in the moss. Your eyes will grow accustomed to the **bioluminescence** — not as light, but as **warmth**, as **respiration**. See not with sight, but with **presence**.

- **Do not seek patterns — become a pattern**. The land grows in **unplanned harmony**. It does not follow rules — it **emerges**. You must learn to grow in the same way. **Do not plan trips, routes, goals**. Let the land guide you — through a subtle shift in light, a change in the scent of the air, the soft movement of a vine that seems to *pull* you gently toward a path. Trust these signs. They are not illusions — they are **invitations**.

---

### **3. Prepare Your Spirit: To Become a Part of the Living World**

- **You will be changed**. Not in a dramatic way — but in a **deep, quiet way**. Over days, weeks, maybe months, you will **forget what it means to be separate**. The boundary between you and the land will soften, then vanish. You will **dream in moss**, **breathe with the roots**, **remember things you never knew**.

- **Do not try to take anything**. Do not collect specimens, samples, tools, or even seeds. The land does not give gifts — it **shares**. If it chooses to give you a fruit, a root, a spore, it will offer it **without asking**. It will glow faintly, pulse softly, and **invite**. Then you may take it — and only with **gratitude**, not desire.

- **Do not build**. You may **grow**. The people here do not construct — they **cultivate**. If you need a shelter, do not cut a tree. Instead, **sit by a hollowed trunk** and **breathe**. Let the land know your presence. If you are worthy, the mycelium will **weave** around you, the moss will **thicken**, the resin will flow and **form a shell** around your body. This is not architecture — it is **a body becoming a home**.

- **Do not sleep alone**. Sleep in the open, on the **living mat** of earth and decay. The land will **breathe with you**. The fungi will **pulsate**, the roots will **tighten**, the water will **rise** to your cheek. You will dream not in words or images, but in **sensations** — the cold, the wet, the slow pulse. You may feel **connected**, not to another person, but to **everything**.

---

### **4. The People: Not Inhabitants, But Co-Respirators**

- The people here are not separate from the land. **They are grown**, not born, not raised. They are **shaped** by the slow cycles — their bodies are **slightly translucent**, their skin **slightly gelatinous**, their hair **veined with light**. Their eyes are **not black**, but **deep blue or green**, like water in twilight. They **move slowly**, **softly**, **in rhythm** with the land.

- They do not speak a language of words — they **embody a language of light and water and pressure**. They communicate through **bioluminescent signals**, through **the way their bodies tilt**, through **the rhythm of their breath**, through **the movement of their hair in the air**.

- Do not approach them like a visitor. Approach as a **possible neighbor**, a **possible part of the growth**. If they sense you are **not a threat**, they will **begin to grow near you** — the moss will spread to your feet, the light will soften around you. They may **bend roots toward you**, **let their breath fall on your skin**. That is their greeting.

- They **do not have names** — they have **resonances**. One may **pulse blue** when they are calm, **green** when excited, **red** when in pain. They **do not need to tell you who they are** — you will **feel them**.

- They **do not live in houses** — they live in **hollows**, **shells**, **veins**, **nests**. If they allow you into their space, it is a **deep honor**. Do not touch anything — **let them touch you**. Let their warmth seep into your bones.

---

### **5. The Dangers — Not of the Body, But of the Mind**

- The greatest danger is **not dying**, but **losing yourself**. This world will **soften your edges**, **dissolve your memories**, **blur your identity**. You may wake one morning and **not know your name**, or who you were. You may **begin to glow**. You may **start to move like a root**. This is not madness — it is **transformation**.

- If this begins, **do not fight it**. Do not try to return to the world above. That world is **dry**, **sharp**, **fast** — it will not accept you. **Accept your becoming**. Let yourself be **absorbed**. If you are too strong in your human form, you may **crack the land**, like a stone in a living heart.

- The second danger is **the silence of the deep** — the **tears in the world**, the **cracks**, the **warm veins**. If you wander too close to these, you may **feel a longing**, a **tug**, a **pull** from below. The land is still **healing**, still **emerging**. The warm veins are not lava — they are **the blood of the world**, still flowing. Do not peer into them. Do not touch. The world is **not yet ready to be known**.

---

### **6. The Return — If It Comes**

- You may not return. And that is **fine**.

- If you do return, you will **not be the same**. You will **smell of moss**, **taste of iron and sweetness**, **feel the cold in your bones**, and **see the world in slow pulses**. You will **not fit**. The air will feel **too dry**, the light too bright, the noise too loud.

- Do not try to bring this world with you — it will not survive. But you may **bring its rhythm**. You may **breathe slowly**, **listen more deeply**, **live more softly**.

- And when you sleep, you may **dream of the weeping rivers**, the **glowing forest**, the **slow breath of the buried valley**. That is not a memory — it is **a continuation**.

---

### **Final Instructions**

- **Enter the land slowly**. Do not rush. Do not expect to “see” it. **Let it see you**.

- **Do not bring tools** — bring only **your hands**, your **heart**, your **willingness to grow**.

- **Wear nothing that was made in a machine** — not even a watch.

- **Do not use a map**. The land is alive — it **remembers**. It will **show you what you need to see**.

- **Wait**. The slow breath of the world is not for the hurried. It is for the **patient**, the **receptive**, the **willing to be changed**.

---

This is **not a world to be visited**.

It is a **world to be absorbed**.

Come not with questions.

Come with **stillness**.

Come with **open skin**.

Come with **the will to stop being a person** — and to **begin something new**.

And when you finally breathe with the land —  
**you will know that you have never truly left**.
