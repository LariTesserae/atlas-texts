### **2. The Body: A Tunable Instrument**
The physics of this landscape are not aligned to your biology. The **gravity is pliable** — it pulls not from below, but from wherever the **resonance is strongest**. Your body will **shift in orientation**, not violently, but subtly: your head may feel light, your feet heavy, your left leg pulled toward the east while your spine curves upward — not toward a sky, but toward a **center of sound**.

**Do not resist.**  
Let your body **realign**.

But your body must be prepared to be **redefined**.

- Wear **no metal**. Conductive surfaces will resonate too violently, drawing the ambient hum into your bones like a feedback loop. You may vibrate uncontrollably, then vanish into a fold of time.  
- Wear **flexible, organic materials**: leather, wool, fungal fiber, silk (if it is not synthetic). These **absorb**, do not reflect.  
- Do not wear shoes with hard soles. The stone is **porous, alive, breathing**. Bare feet or soft-soled foot-wraps allow you to feel its **suck-spring rhythm** — a kind of **damp pulse** that synchronizes you to the land.

Your skin should be **unobstructed** — not for exposure, but for **vibration-conductance**. This world communicates through **tactile sound**. A touch, even from a plant, may send a **sonic memory** directly into your nervous system — not a thought, but a **feeling of a past moment**, or a future one.

> *Your body becomes a **tuning fork** — not to amplify, but to **harmonize**.*

**Do not panic if you “hear” your own breath** — you will. It will not be yours. It will be **your breath as it sounded in a different time** — slower, deeper, or more frantic. This is normal.

You may find yourself **inhaling a memory** — a sensation of childhood, of grief, of ecstasy, not from your life, but **from the land’s**. This is a **resonance transfer**. Accept it. Do not analyze. Let it **pass through**.

> *The breath is not air. It is **chronometric current**.*

### **5. The Landscape: Do Not Expect a Map**

**Do not rely on landmarks.**

**Walk slowly.**

**Let the resonance guide**.

> *If direction exists here, it is **not toward**, but **through**.*

- Some **bulbs** at the tips of the leaves may **glow**, then **fade**, and then **reappear** — but not in the same place. They are **not fixed**.  
- Do not **pick**, do not **break**, do not **attempt to use**. The plants are not resources. They are **manifestations of the land’s memory** — living **sonic archives**.

If a plant **blossoms** in your presence, it is not because of you — but because **the sound has found its match**, and you have **resonated** with it.

> *You do not own the plant. The plant owns your attention.*
