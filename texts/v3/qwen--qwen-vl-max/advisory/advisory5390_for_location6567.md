### 🔹 **Preparation: Before You Step Through**

- **Do not wear boots**. The ground is not earth — it is **crystalline memory**, thin and prone to fracture under sudden stress. Wear soft soles — felt, leather, **no metal**. Let your feet *feel* the vibrations.

#### 2. **Bring a Hollow Vessel**
Not for water. For **sound**.

- A **glass bottle**, empty, sealed with wax. Or a **hollow brass tube**, open at both ends. Something that can *hold* resonance without distorting it.
- You may not need it — but if you find a **note that is lost**, you might be able to **catch it**, carry it, return it to where it belongs.

#### 3. **Leave Your Devices Behind**
Your phone, your watch, your chronometer — all are **mechanical** and **linear**. They assume time is a line. Here, time is a **tone**, a pulse, a wave.

- Your heart monitor will register **no pulse** — but you will be alive. Your brain will not register **no sound**, yet you will know it is deafening. Do not trust your instruments. Trust your **bones**.

#### 4. **Bring a Quiet Mind**
You are not visiting. You are being **observed**. The world hears you — not with ears, but with **fissures, with fronds, with the air itself**.

#### 5. **Clothing: Warm, Soft, and Absorbent**
- Wear **wool**, **cotton**, or **linen**. **No synthetics** — they vibrate at frequencies that do not belong. They will **interfere**.
- Layer it. The cold comes from the *bone*, not the air. But warmth is not the goal — **empathy with the environment** is.
- Choose **neutral colors** — white, gray, indigo. The world is not *colorblind*, but color is **a vibration**, and sharp hues may **clash**, may **shatter**.

#### 6. **Bring No Food**
You will not eat. Not because it’s impossible — because **there is no need**.

- The air is thick with **sonic nutrient**, a slow digestion of tone into sustenance. If your body is attuned, it will absorb the resonance like a plant drinks light.
