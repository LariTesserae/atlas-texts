### **1. Physiological Preparation: The Body in a Non-Aqueous World**

**Do not bring water**—not even for drinking. The air holds no moisture, yet the world is damp with vapor that does not condense. Your body, designed for aqueous balance, will *not* survive long without intervention. But even artificial hydration will fail here, because the **biochemistry is not your own**.

#### A. **Reconfigure Hydration**
- **Use a subcutaneous solvent delivery system**—not water, but a **sulfur-based hydrating fluid**, or **ammonia-charged gel**. The bioslime is saturated with **amphiphilic molecules** that can bind to your cells without triggering osmotic shock.
- **Wear a bio-skin suit**—a flexible, semi-permeable membrane that mimics the transdermal membranes of the native flora. It should allow **vapor intake** through micro-pores, but block **static discharge and thermal drift**. The suit must absorb ambient heat and release it slowly, like a thermal capacitor.
- **Ingest no food.** Instead, carry **solid-state nutrient patches** infused with **complex carbohydrates and mineralized bio-matrix**. These can be absorbed through **epidermal channels** (not digestion) via a **micro-implant system** that interfaces directly with your metabolic network.

> *Note: The body will not grow used to this world. Instead, it will begin to mimic it. After 48 hours, your skin will secrete a thin, iridescent film—one that glows faintly under low light. Do not panic: it is not infection, but *integration*. You are becoming part of the membrane.*

#### B. **Thermal Regulation**
- The ambient temperature is 28–32°C, but the **static charge in the air** will disrupt your nervous system. Install **low-resistance grounding bands** in your boots and gloves—these discharge the ambient static gently, preventing hair-standing, twitching, or cardiac irregularity.
- The land's **slow thermal convection** means the air warms from below in long, rolling waves. You’ll feel a *shift in pressure*—not temperature—a deep, warm thrum in your bones. This is **planetary breath**. Do not resist it. Embrace it.

> *If you remain still for more than an hour, you may begin to feel "unstuck." Your sense of self will blur. This is not a hallucination—it is the world’s **slow pulse** entering your nervous system. This is the beginning of recognition.*

### **2. Sensory Preparation: Rebooting the Senses**

Your senses will fail. Or rather, they will **reboot**.

#### A. **Vision: The Luminosity of Non-Green**

> *When you finally "see" the world correctly, you will realize: the **luminosity is not light, but memory**. Each glimmer is a fragment of a former chemical process, now released as energy. The moss pulses not in sync with a day-night cycle, but with a **resonance frequency from deep within the planet’s core**. The light is **the slow heartbeat of a world that remembers what it once was.***

#### B. **Hearing: Silence as a Vibration**
- The world is almost silent. No wind, no birds, no rustling. But the **air hums**—not audibly, but as a **resonance** in the bones, the teeth, the spine.
- This is **infrasound**, below 20 Hz, but **felt**, not heard. It is the **pulse of the burrow-spiders**, the **flow of heat from the core**, the **slow reorganization of the bioslime**.
- **Solution**: Wear **bone-conductive hearing implants** tuned to **0.1–15 Hz**. Do not listen with your ears—**listen with your marrow.** The vibrations will teach you rhythms.

> *After 72 hours, you will begin to hear the world in **three ways**:

> This is not noise. It is **language**.*

#### C. **Touch: The Cold That Is Warm**
- The bioslime feels **cold to the touch**, yet the air is warm. This contradiction is not physical—it is **metaphysical**. The slime is *dehydrated*, and the heat is retained in the *air*, not the surface.
- **Solution**: Wear gloves made of **thermoelectric polymers** that convert the *difference* in heat into a neutral sensation. Touch the ground, but do not press deeply—this is not soil, but **living cement**. Prolonged contact may cause your skin to *melt into it*, slowly, like wax into glass.

- If you slow down, you may **become a part of the pattern**.
