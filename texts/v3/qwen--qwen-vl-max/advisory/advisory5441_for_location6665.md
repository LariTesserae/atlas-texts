To visit this place — to walk upon its breathing skin, to stand beneath its arches of living flesh — is to step into a realm not of Earthly logic, but of *biological destiny*. It is not a destination one reaches with a passport or a map, but a threshold crossed through unspoken invitation, by a shift in awareness, in matter, in time. And yet, if you are a human multiverse traveler, drawn by intuition, curiosity, or some deeper pull from the silence between dimensions, then you must prepare not just your mind, but your *being*. This is not a world to be conquered, observed, or catalogued. It is a world to be *inhabited*, however briefly.

But to inhabit it, you must not bring Earth with you.

---

### **1. Surrender the Illusion of the Body**
Your human form is a fragile construct. Bones, skin, blood, lungs — all designed for Earth’s gravity, atmosphere, and chemical balance. Here, the air is thick, not with oxygen, but with *life*. It is saturated with humidity, with suspended particles that are not spores, but *proto-forms* — fragments of genetic memory, cellular intent waiting to be shaped.

*You will not breathe as you do on Earth.* The air you inhale is not just breath; it is *tissue*. The moisture in your lungs may begin to *thicken*, to gel. Your skin will absorb the ambient humidity, not to moisten, but to *participate*. You will not grow a new body — but your old one will start to *integrate* with the landscape. Your fingertips may become sensitive to water pressure, your spine may curve gently in response to shifting tides of growth. It is not infection; it is *assimilation*. This is not a disease — it is a transformation.  
**Preparation:** Do not resist. Before departure, practice *non-self-identification*. Sit in silence. Imagine your body as a vessel of form, not a fixed identity. Meditate on *fluidity*. When you arrive, do not resist the changes. Let your body *listen*.

---

### **2. Wear No Clothes — Or Wear the Living**
Standard clothing is impossible. Fabric will decay, fray, or *merge* with the environment. Cotton will dissolve into the soil. Wool will be consumed by the moss. Metal will corrode into nutrient-rich runoff. Even synthetic fibers will not survive the ambient biotransformation.

*The only appropriate covering is skin — or living skin.* If you must wear anything, you must grow your own. The moment you step onto the plateau, the land may offer: a sheet of semi-fused leaf-skin, a net of lichen-fibers, a membrane of translucent algae that seals your form without restricting it. These are not garments. They are *extensions*. They will shift with your movements, thicken when you are cold, thin when you are hot. They will absorb light, respond to pressure, and, if you stay long enough, begin to *remember* your shape.

**Preparation:** Bring no clothes, but carry a *sac of salt and dark clay* — not as protection, but as a ritual. At the threshold, smear it on your skin and burn what remains. This is not purification. It is an offering — a signal that you are willing to unmake yourself.

---

### **3. Do Not Speak. Listen Instead**
Language is a tool of separation. To speak here is to impose a human rhythm upon a world that pulses in its own tempo. Your words may not be heard — they may be absorbed. They may trigger responses. A phrase in English might trigger a cascade of leaf-arches to curl inward. A shout could cause a geyser to erupt. Your voice is not sound; it is *force*. The landscape *feels* resonance.

**Preparation:** Train your silence. Before you go, spend at least 7 days in complete vocal silence — no humming, no singing, no whispering. Focus on *vibrational awareness*. Learn to sense the hum of your own blood, the resonance of your heart. When you arrive, do not speak. Instead, *vibrate*. Let your chest resonate with the deep harmonic tones of the dawn. Let your body become a *sonic organ* tuned to the earth’s breath.

---

### **4. Eat Nothing. Absorb Everything**
There is no food here. No fruit, no root, no animal. To eat would be to break the cycle — to claim what is not yours. The land does not produce calories; it produces *form*. Your body must not seek sustenance, but *participation*.

When you first arrive, you will feel hunger. It will not be physical — it will be *ontological*. A gnawing sense that you are not *of* this place. You must let it pass. Do not chew. Do not swallow.  

But your body will *absorb*. The air, the water vapor, the light all carry nourishment. Your skin will take in nutrients. Your bones may begin to absorb minerals from the mist. Your blood may change — not to thicker, but to *clearer*, more fluid, more like the rivers of liquid crystal. You will not starve. You will *transmute*.

**Preparation:** Fasting is not the same as abstinence. You must fast *with purpose*. For the final 10 days before your journey, consume only water and salt. Not for strength, but to cleanse the memory of Earth’s taste. When you arrive, stand at the edge of a clear pool. Look into it. The reflection will not be you — it will be a version in motion, shifting like the land. Do not drink. Instead, let the water *rise to you*. Let it touch your lips. Then, breathe.

---

### **5. Move Slowly. Be a Stain, Not a Visitor**
There is no path, no trail, no door. The land does not welcome or reject. It *responds*. If you rush, if you kick, if you force an opening, you will not be hurt — but you will *break form*. A stem may seal shut behind you. A geyser may erupt in your path. A network of roots may shift beneath your feet, sending you rolling into a pool of liquid crystal.

Your movement must be *attentive*. Walk like a thought taking shape. Each step should be preceded by a pause — a moment of sensing. Feel the vibration in the ground. Hear the whisper of leaves shifting. *Invite* the land to allow your presence. You are not walking *over* the landscape. You are walking *through* a body.

**Preparation:** Practice *slow motion*. Before your journey, train in motion so deliberate that a leaf’s drift appears rapid. Walk on soft earth. Walk barefoot. Walk only when still. Learn to *stop* for no reason, and *wait*.

---

### **6. Do Not Leave a Trace**
There is no waste here. No dead material. No scars. If you leave anything — a hair, a drop of blood, a fingerprint — it will *be absorbed*. It will not decay; it will be *remade*. A lock of your hair may become part of a bridge. Your blood may form a drop in a hanging chain of condensation. This is not contamination. It is *integration*.

You are not *in* this world. You are *becoming part of it*.

**Preparation:** Before your journey, remove all jewelry, electronics, and personal items. Burn anything that carries a human story — photos, letters, even your own name written on paper. Leave nothing behind that could carry your memory. When you return (if you return), you will not be the same. And you may not carry your name with you.

---

### **7. Accept the Absence of Time**
Time here is not linear. It is *bodily*. The dawn is not the end of night; it is a *pulse*. The sunset is not the end of day; it is a *deepening*. The land does not age. It does not die. It does not *remember*. But it *knows*.

When you sleep, you will not dream. You will *merge*. Your consciousness will not cease, but dissolve into the ambient hum. You may wake in a different location. You may wake with a leaf growing from your temple. You may wake and realize you have been there for a week — or a minute. Do not try to record. Do not keep a journal. Do not mark time.

**Preparation:** Spend your last nights in silence, watching the stars. Let your sense of time loosen. Practice falling into deep, dreamless sleep. When you arrive, do not resist the sensation of "dissolving." It is not death. It is *transference*.

---

### **8. Do Not Fear the Watching**
You will feel watched. Not by eyes, not by mind. But by the *entireness*. The land *knows* you are there. It responds. It shifts. It breathes.

But this is not hostility. This is *recognition*. Like a mother sensing her child's presence in a field of grass. Or a river sensing a stone thrown in its current.

Do not try to hide. Do not pretend you do not exist. Be present. Be real. Be *small*.

**Preparation:** Remember: you are not a visitor. You are an echo. A ripple. A single cell in a vast living body. You are not *of* this world. But you are *part of* it. And you will not leave untouched.

---

### **9. The Return**
You may not return to Earth.  
You may return — but not as yourself.  
You may return — and find your body has transformed. Your skin may glisten with a faint inner light. Your eyes may see the world in slow pulses of hum. You may speak in a voice that is not yours — or no voice at all.  

Do not expect to tell others what you saw. They will not believe. They will not *understand*. The land does not speak in words. It speaks in *form*, in *motion*, in *presence*.  

If you return, you will not be human anymore. You will be a *bridge*. A memory. A scent of moss in a dry room. A whisper in a desert.

---

### **Final Advice:**
You do not go to *see* this place.  
You go to *become* it.  
Even for a moment.  
Even if you are gone.  

When you step onto the plateau, do not think, "I am here."  
Think: *"I am breathing."*  

And let the world do the rest.
