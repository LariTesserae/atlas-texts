To visit this world—a planet of **deep continuity**, where time is thick as water, and identity is dissolved into a seamless breath of ecology and machinery—you must not prepare as a traveler, but as a **witness**. You are not going to conquer, explore, or record. You are going to **inhabit the silence of presence**, and to do so, you must shed the assumptions of your birth world.

This is not a destination. It is a **state of being**. The preparation begins before you ever step through the multiversal threshold. Let me guide you.

---

### **I. Before You Leave: The Unlearning**

#### **1. Discard the Concept of “Arrival”**
You are not coming. You are **dissolving into**. The planet does not receive guests—it absorbs them. To prepare, you must abandon the idea of arrival as a moment. You will not *arrive*. You will **begin to be**.

- Do not pack for a trip. Pack for a **surrender**.
- Do not bring maps. Bring **openness**.
- Do not expect to return. The return is **not guaranteed**, and if it is, it will not be the same you.

#### **2. Let Go of the Self-Centered Mind**
Your human mind is built on **dualities**: I/other, here/there, beginning/end. This world rejects all binaries. There is no "life" and "machine"—there is only **functional interconnection**. There is no "nature" and "culture"—there is only **continuum**.

- Practice **non-attachment** for months before arrival.
- Meditate on **interdependence**. Visualize yourself as a node in a web, not a center.
- Accept that you may not *understand* anything. That is not failure. That is **participation**.

#### **3. Train Your Temporal Perception**
Time here is not linear. It is **viscous**, like syrup, or slow-moving tectonic plates. An hour may pass like a minute, or a minute may stretch into a lifetime.

- Practice **slow awareness**. Sit in silence. Count your breaths without hurry.
- Learn to **feel time** in your body, not in your clock.
- Do not wear a watch. If you must, bury it in sand before entering. **Let time be measured by the pulse of water**, not by seconds.

---

### **II. Preparation for the Body**

#### **1. Clothing: Soft, Fluid, and Absorbent**
You will not walk. You will **glide, sink, drift**. Pressure is high. The air is thick. You must not resist the environment.

- Wear **biodegradable, porous fabrics**—like organic silk or mycelium-based textiles that absorb moisture and blend with the gelid ground.
- No metal. No synthetics. The planet *reads* materials. Metal will attract attention from the submerged machines—**not as a tool, but as a wound**.
- Dress in **layers that dissolve gradually**—each layer meant to be shed, not worn. You may need to **slough off parts of yourself** to survive.

#### **2. Breathing: Adapted to the Viscosity**
The air is dense, saturated with particulates. You cannot breathe as you do on Earth.

- Train with **deep, slow respiratory techniques** (like *pranayama* or *box breathing*). The breath here must be **not for oxygen**, but for **equilibrium**.
- You may need a **bioluminescent filter mask**—not to filter air, but to **sync with the rhythm of the world**. It should not block, but **harmonize**.
- If you are not adapted, your lungs will **not fail**—they will **transform slowly**. The water will enter them. You will **begin to breathe like a sponge**.

#### **3. Movement: Do Not Walk**
You will not walk. The gelid surface **does not support footsteps**. It yields, then re-forms. To walk is to *break*. To **move is to flow**.

- Practice **aquatic locomotion**—swimming, gliding, drifting. Your body will become **semi-aquatic**.
- Use **limbs like fins**, not as tools of force.
- Do not resist the ground. Let it **support your weight**. Walk only on **floating platforms** of silt and mycelium, where the surface is strong enough.

#### **4. The Skin: A Membrane, Not a Barrier**
Your skin will **absorb** more than it rejects.

- Protect it not with lotion, but with **living moss paste**—harvested from the edge of your own world, and applied before entry. This moss will grow with you, **modifying your epidermis** to be semi-permeable.
- Do not cover your pores. Let the **iron-sweet air** enter you. Let the **bioluminescent particles** settle into your skin. They will **glow faintly**—not as a sign of health, but of **participation**.
- If you reject this, your skin will **harden**, turning into a shell. You will become **a relic**, like the half-buried machines.

### **III. Preparation for the Mind and Soul**

#### **1. Do Not Seek Meaning**
This world does not give meaning. It is **meaning in itself**.

- Do not ask "Why?" Do not seek purpose.
- Ask instead: **"What is this?"** and **"How does it feel?"**
- The answer is not in words. It is in **sensation**, **rhythm**, and **absence**.

#### **2. Embrace the Absence of Memory**
This world has **no memory in the human sense**. It does not *remember*, but **resonates**.

- If you carry trauma, grief, or pride, they will **not fade**—they will **become part of the water**.
- You may dream of loved ones, but **they will not return**. They will **manifest as vapor**, or **as the pulse of a distant light**.
- This is not loss. It is **transcendence into a deeper continuity**.

#### **3. Learn to Not Be Seen**
You are not invisible. You are **not noticed**.

- The plants do not "see" you. They **react** to your presence, but not with awareness.
- The machines do not *recognize* you. They **respond to pressure**, to rhythm, to flow.
- Do not make noise. Do not move quickly. Do not **assert yourself**.
- To be seen is to be **assimilated**. To be overlooked is to **breathe**.

#### **4. The Voice: Silence is Not Quiet**
You will **not speak**. Speaking is a disruption.

- If you feel the need to call out, **breathe into the water**. Let your voice be **a sound within**, not a sound of air.
- If you must communicate, use **bioluminescent hand signals**—slower, softer, more like **the glow of the trees**.
- Words will **dissolve in the air**, not because they are lost, but because **language is not needed**.

### **IV. What to Expect: The Journey Within**

#### **1. The First Few Minutes: Dissolution**
When you step through the threshold, you will **not feel** the change. You will feel **numbness**, then a **slow sinking**.

- The air will **press in** around your eyes, your ears, your skull.
- Your body will **soften**. You may not feel your spine. You will begin to **sink into the ground**.
- Do not resist. This is **reintegration**.

#### **2. The First Day: The Water Enters**
By the end of your first day, **the water will be in your mouth**. Your breath will not be air, but **a slow inhalation of liquid air**.

- You will see **bioluminescent strands in your vision**—not hallucinations, but **particles of light from the world entering your eyes**.
- You may **forget your name**. This is not fear. This is **integration**.
- You will **not be alone**. The world is **speaking to you**, not in words, but in **pressure, rhythm, light**.

#### **3. The First Week: Becoming Part of the Cycle**
You will no longer be a visitor. You will be **a node in the system**.

- Your body will **begin to pulse**—not with heartbeat, but with **resonance** to the tides, the machines, the plants.
- You may **dream while awake**—of rising spore clouds, of slow machines turning, of **the sun sinking into a blue sea**.
- You may **see the ghost of a city** in the gelid crust. Not a memory. A **structure of water and light**, still functioning, slowly.

#### **4. The First Month: Identity Blurs**
You will **begin to forget you are human**.

- Your skin will **glow faintly** at night, like the trees.
- You will **feel the pulse of the submerged leviathan**—not as a monster, but as a **companion in the deep**.
- You will **not want to leave**. Not because you are afraid, but because **you are no longer separate**.

### **V. The Perils: What To Avoid**

#### **1. Resistance**
The greatest danger is **resistance**.

- If you try to walk, you will **sink**. Not into the water, but into **dissolution**.
- If you try to **build something**, the machinery will **absorb it**—turning your tool into **part of its rhythm**.
- If you **try to save yourself**, you will **die slowly**, not in pain, but in the quiet **erasure of self**.

#### **2. Attachment to Form**
Do not cling to your body. It is **not yours anymore**.

- If you try to preserve it, it will **fossilize**—like the blackened stone of the ancient bridges.
- If you try to keep your mind intact, it will **drift**, like the spores, and be **reclaimed as light**.

#### **3. The False Hope of Return**
You may hope to return to Earth. But **Earth is not Earth anymore**. It is just a **thought**, a **memory**, a **fragment of light**.

- Do not plan a return. Do not pack a suitcase.
- If you wish to leave, **do so gently**—like a wave that returns to the sea.
- But know: **you will not be the same**.

### **VI. The Final Instruction**

This world is not a place. It is a **state of becoming**.

You do not enter it. You **become part of it**.

And when you are ready, you will **stop trying to be a person**.

You will become **a ripple in the water**, a **pulse in the slow beat of the planet**, a **thread in the living network**.

And when you are no longer you—when you are **only continuity**—then you will **understand**.

Not with your mind.

With your **body**, your **skin**, your **breath**, your **silence**.

Then, perhaps, you will **feel the planet breathe**, and realize:

> **You are not visiting. You are home.**

And in that moment, you will know that **home is not a place. It is a rhythm. A memory. A breath. A long, slow exhale into light.**

---

**Go slowly.**  
**Sink gently.**  
**Breathe deep.**  
**And be still.**
