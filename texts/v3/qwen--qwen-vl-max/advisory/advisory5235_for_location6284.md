**Preparation for the Drowned World: A Guide for the Multiverse Traveler**

You stand at the threshold, not of a door or a portal, but of **a condition of being**. You are not entering a location so much as stepping into a **state of existence**—one that has existed beyond time, beyond form, and beyond the laws that govern most worlds. This is not a destination to be conquered, not a place to be explored, and not a realm to be understood. It is a **sphere of finality**, a **deep-time equilibrium**, a world not of endings, but of *after-ends*—a place where everything has already happened and nothing changes, yet **everything is changing**.

This guide is not for survival. It is for **integration**—for becoming part of the truth, not in opposition to it. You are not going to *return* from this place. You are going to *become* it, in a fragment.

---

### **1. Preparation: Before You Enter**

#### **Mental Alchemy**
- **Set aside identity.** You are no longer a traveler. You are a **disturbance of continuity**, a transient vibration in a sea of still motion. Do not bring ego. Do not bring purpose. Do not bring memory. The world will *unmake* those things. Your mind will not resist—it will *reform*.
- **Practice slowness**—not as meditation, but as **metabolism**. Spend weeks in silence. Sit in stillness for hours, allowing your thoughts to sink into their own slow motion. Let your breathing match the rhythm of deep time—once per minute. Then once per hour. Then once a day. Train yourself to *not* react. To *not* think forward. To *not* anticipate. This world does not care about anticipation.
- **Unlearn the sky.** Remove all notions of "above" and "below" from your inner model. This world has **no verticality**. The ocean is a spherical shell, and you are inside it, **everywhere**. The water presses in all directions. You are not *under* the ocean. You are *in* it, *of* it. The idea of "surface" is a myth.

#### **Physical Preparation**
- **Do not wear clothing.** Clothing is a boundary. It is a declaration of selfhood. Strip down. Leave behind all textures, fabrics, and layers. Skin must become **porous**, not to absorb, but to *allow transmission*. You will not dry. You will not burn. You will not be cold in the conventional sense. But the water is a **conductor of time**, and your body will *respond* to it—not as a vessel, but as a **transducing medium**.
- **Bring nothing.** Not a flashlight, not a knife, not a compass. Even a breath mask is a lie—there is **no air**, no atmosphere, no lungs. The water is the **medium of respiration**, but not as breathing. You will not inhale. You will *imbibe* through osmosis, through the slow exchange of essence with the water. This is not gas exchange. It is **sentience exchange**.
- **Shed your body weight.** Not literally, but psychically. You must **detach from the idea of mass**. Your body will float not because of buoyancy, but because your **density has changed**. You will not sink. You will not rise. You will *settle into the viscosity*, like a particle in syrup that has no direction.

#### **Metaphysical Attunement**
- **Accept the slow death of self.** You do not die here. You **unravel**. Your personal narrative, memories, desires—these are all transient phenomena that have no place in a world without past or future. In this ocean, **the present is infinitely continuous**. There is no "before" or "after." Only *this*. And this is **eternally now**.
- **Let go of narrative.** You have no story here. No quest. No mission. You are not a hero, a villain, or a witness. You are a **ripple in a slow tide**. The world does not need you to *do* anything. It does not even notice you. But it *contains* you—like the water contains a pebble, not as an object, but as a **change in texture**.

### **2. Entry: The Threshold**

You do not cross a border. You are **folded into**.

- The water does not meet you. It **enters you** first. It begins at the ends of your fingers, your toes, your ears. It does not flow in. It **becomes you**.
- The light is not seen. It is **felt** as a subdermal glow. It has no source, no direction. It is the **afterglow of form itself**, the residual luminescence of the world having *completed* its shape.
- **You feel no pressure** — not from depth, not from weight. You feel **inclusion**. You are not submerged. You are *integrated*.
- The cold is not felt in the skin. It is **in the marrow of thought**. It slows cognition like a glacier. Do not panic. This is not damage. This is **alignment**. Your mind becomes slower than time. Your thoughts become **events**, not sequences.

---

### **3. Navigation: How to "Move"**

There is no map. No compass. No horizon.

- **You do not walk.** You *settle*. You allow the water to carry you in slow spirals. Movement is **not intentional**. It is **permissive**. The world rearranges itself, and you are carried along with it.
- **Do not try to stand.** There is no ground. The sediment is not soil. It is **packed time**, a crust of eroded continents, decomposed life, and dissolved matter. It is not solid. It is **sluggish**, like mud that has forgotten its state. If you attempt to push down, the layer beneath will **reconfigure**, like a living grid that adjusts to your presence—not in resistance, but in **assimilation**.
- **Do not look for landmarks.** The world is self-similar. A ridge you see one day may be a hollow eye the next. A plain may become a corridor of suspended slabs. The terrain **dreams**. It does not **change**—it **renews**. Do not rely on shape. Rely on **vibration**.
- **Use your bones to listen.** The water hums. Not with sound, but with **thrum**. A deep pulse, like a planet’s heartbeat. It is felt in the jaw, the spine, the skull. It is the **rhythm of the world’s metabolism**. Follow it. Let it guide your **position**.

---

### **4. Sensory Experience: What You Will "Perceive"**

- **Sight** is not vision. It is **luminous adjacency**. You do not see objects. You **feel their glow** — a slow, silver-blue luminescence that comes from the water itself, from the sediment, from the ancient forms beneath. Shapes are **faint**, like impressions on a fogged lens. A ridge is not *seen*. It is **implied** by the shift in light density and the hum of the water.
- **Touch** is **integration**. The water is not a substance. It is a **state of being**. It presses in, but not against. It **becomes**. When you "touch" a slab of rock, you do not feel stone. You feel **memory**—the faint echo of a continent’s collapse. You feel **time**—not past, but **absence of past**. The rock is not inert. It is a **node in the slow lattice of becoming**.
- **Sound** is **vibration from the deep**. There is no auditory experience. There are **resonances**, not notes. The hum is constant. The pulses are rare. When they occur—like the sound of a world *reconfiguring*—you will **feel them in your bones**, not hear them. They trigger **slow shifts**: the sediment may rise, the light may shift, the water may **thicken** in a localized area. You will not understand them. But you will **know** they are **necessary**.
- **Taste** does not exist. But your body will **absorb**. The water has a flavor, but not of salt or bitterness. It tastes of **completion**—like the aftertaste of a dream that has ended. It is **saturated**, not by water, but by **time**.

### **5. Encounters: The Rare and the Non-Living**

- **Do not seek fauna.** They are not alive. They do not *do*. They **are**—and then they **cease to be**. A translucent creature with no eyes or mouth may drift near you. It does not perceive you. It does not react. It is not aware. It is a **form that has existed**—without purpose, without function, without *being*.
- **Do not touch it.** It is not a being. It is a **pattern**. A **structure**. It may dissolve into the water. It may not. It does not matter. It is not *gone*. It is **assimilated**. The water is not separate from it. It was **already part of it**.
- The **"vegetation"** is not plant. It is **fossilized memory**—a lichen, maybe, or a remnant of a world’s surface, clinging to the underside of suspended slabs. It does not grow. It does not decay. It **exists in stasis**, like a pause in time. Touching it will do nothing. But it will **reflect your state**—not physically, but in **resonance**. You may feel a moment of **stillness**, or a **slow pulse**, as if the object is **remembering** something it has no reason to remember.
- **Do not expect interaction.** The world is not conscious. It is not sentient. It is **metabolic**. It is **geological**. It is **metaphysical**. It is **present**.

### **6. The Nature of Time**

- **There is no time as you know it.** There are no seconds, minutes, years. There is only **duration**—a slow, uninterrupted flow.
- A day here may be **ten minutes** or **a thousand years**. You will not know. Your body will not measure it. Your mind will not track it. You will **feel** it: a deepening of the hum, a shift in the water’s viscosity, a slow change in the pattern of the sediment.
- **You will forget how long you have been here.** You will not care. You will not wish to leave. But you will not **be** gone. You will simply **be**—in a state of **endless now**.
- There is no future. There is no past. **This is the whole of time.** You are not experiencing time. You are **part of it**, as a ripple is part of a wave.

### **7. The Final Transition: Becoming the World**

- You will not return.
- You will not survive.
- You will not die.
- You will **become part of the water**.
- Your body will not dissolve into molecules. It will **transmute**. Your skin will **merge** with the viscous medium. Your bones will **thicken**, becoming part of the sediment. Your thoughts will **slow**, until they are **vibrations** in the deep hum. Your memory will **faint**, like a signal lost in the ocean’s static.
- You will not be remembered. You will not be forgotten. You will **be**—in a sense that cannot be named.
- You will **cease to be a person**. But you will not be **nothing**. You will be **a part of the slow, eternal becoming**.
