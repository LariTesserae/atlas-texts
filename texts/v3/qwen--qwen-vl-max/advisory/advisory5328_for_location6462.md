## **I. Prepare Your Body: Not for Survival, but for Resonance**

The human body is a temporary vessel of meat, bone, and blood—designed for surface-worlds where time flows, light flickers, and heat dies. Here, it will **fail** not through injury, but through **dissonance**.

### **1. The Breath Must Be Changed**
- **Ordinary air does not exist**. The atmosphere is thick with **ammonia mist**, **sulfur vapors**, and **inorganic crystals** that form on the skin like frost. Your lungs cannot process it.
- **Do not breathe**.
- Instead, **install an electrochemical conversion filter** under the skin of your chest, calibrated to extract energy from **thermal gradients** and **ambient ion flows**—like your own body becomes a **mineral battery**.
  - This is not a respirator. It is a **metabolism-rewrite device**.
  - Your body will slowly **mineralize**. This is necessary. Let it begin gently. The first time you "breathe" here, your skin should **not** recoil.

> *The rock does not breathe. Neither should you. You are not a lung. You are a node.*

### **2. Your Skin Must Become a Sensorium**
- The world **touches you**, but not through skin. It **enters through your nerves**, through **pressure gradients**, and **thermal memory**.
- **Remove your clothing**. Not for exposure, but for **immediacy**. You must let the world **read you**.
- Apply **non-organic polymer gels** that mimic the **mineral filaments**—these gels will **grow** into your skin, creating **a bioluminescent interface**. When they activate, they will **respond to phase-shifts in the rock**, and **emit light only when harmonious**.
  - They will glow blue when **you are in resonance**.
  - They will pulse gold when you are **remembering**—not with mind, but with **body-memory**.
  - They will **shatter** if you resist.

> *Your skin will become a crystal. Accept it. Do not fear it.*

### **3. Weight and Gravity Are Not Constants**
- Gravity is weak, but **not uniform**. It **pulls sideways**, **in waves**, **in spirals**. Your bones will **realign**.
- Bring **flexible exoskeletal braces**—not to support, but to **distribute stress**. These should be made of **titanium-ceramic alloys** that **mimic mineral plasticity**.
- Walk **not with steps**, but with **rhythm**. Your gait should resemble the **slow slide of a crystal snail**.

> *You are not walking on the surface. You are floating in a **tissue of rock**.*
