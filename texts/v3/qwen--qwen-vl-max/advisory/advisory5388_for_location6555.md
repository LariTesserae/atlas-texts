**Preparation for a Multiverse Traveler: Entering the City of Remade Bones**

You are about to step into a world not born, but *forged* — a civilization that is neither dead nor alive, neither machine nor organism, but something deeper: a *memory in motion*. This is not a place to survive; it is a place to *resonate* with. To visit the City of Remade Bones is to step inside an ongoing ritual of construction, decay, and reassembly — a world built on the carcass of an older biosphere, now reinterpreted by unseen hands, or perhaps no hands at all.

To walk here without preparation is to become an anomaly — a static disruption in a system already fragile, already listening. But if you are wise, and you are still, and you are willing to *listen*, then you may not only survive — you may *understand*.

---

### **1. Understand the Nature of the Place**

This is not a world of *place*, but of *process*. It is not a city in the human sense. It is a *symphony of construction* — and you are not a visitor, but an instrument. Your presence alters the frequency of the environment. Your footsteps, your breath, your heartbeat, even your thoughts — all are potential stimuli.

You must come not as a conqueror or a tourist, but as a **co-resonator** — someone who enters the rhythm of the place, not to dominate it, but to be *known by it*.

---

### **2. Clothing and Equipment — Not for Comfort, but for Containment**

The air is thick and metallic. It clings, not just to skin, but to *memory*. Every inhalation risks implanting a foreign resonance in the city’s latent nervous system.

#### **Clothing**
- Wear **non-conductive, non-metallic fabrics**: silk, cotton, or synthetic blends with zero conductive threads. Anything that might interact with the city’s electromagnetic field will attract attention — or worse, become part of it.
- Use **damp-repellent outer layers** — not to repel moisture, but to *regulate* it. The city’s moisture is not water, but a mixture of vaporized minerals, dissolved polymers, and organic residue that can bio-activate on contact with organic matter. If your skin or cloth absorbs it, it may begin to *resonate* — pulsing faintly, slowly becoming part of the structure.
- **No shoes with metal plates or soles**. The ground is sensitive to magnetic fields. Your footwear could trigger a shift in the surface — or worse, attract a glass-squid or a listening construct.

#### **Protective Gear**
- **Anti-resonance goggles**: These are not to block light, but to *stabilize perception*. The city’s light pulses in sync with subterranean cycles. Without protection, your brain may begin to entrain with these rhythms — leading to time distortion, hallucinations, or a permanent shift in temporal perception (e.g., days feeling like minutes, or minutes lasting years).
- **Sound-dampening ear seals** (but not full isolation). The low-frequency hum is essential to the city’s stability. Removing it all causes disorientation. Instead, use *tuned filters* that allow you to hear the dominant frequency (around 12–15 Hz), but block sudden spikes — which may indicate instability or the arrival of a listener.
- **Resonance dampener**: A wearable device (worn over the chest) that emits a neutralizing frequency. It prevents your internal biological rhythms from syncing with the city’s cycles. Without it, your heart may begin to beat in unison with the pulse of the ground — and if the city halts, so might you.

---

### **3. Breath and Body — The Most Dangerous Tools**

Your body is an instrument. And the city is listening.

- **Do not breathe deeply or rapidly**. The humid air contains metallic vapors — particularly iron oxide and dissolved copper particles. Inhaling too much can cause internal oxidation, turning your blood into a faintly glowing sludge. Over time, this may lead to crystallization of tissue — you become a new component of the city.
- **Pulse your breath with the ambient rhythm**. Observe the light. When it pulses slowly — like a heartbeat — match your breathing. When it is steady, breathe deeply but slowly. When it fades, hold your breath. This is not about comfort — it’s about **harmony**. The city may reject you if you disrupt its bio-mechanical breath.

#### **Physiological Considerations**
- **No metals in your body**. Implants, dental fillings, even the tiny metal thread in a button — they can act as conductors, drawing attention from the listeners or the spheres. If you must carry something metallic, it must be **passive**: non-reactive, non-conductive.
- **Your skin may become reflective**. After days of exposure, your epidermis may begin to develop a faintly iridescent sheen, mimicking the city’s neural filaments. This is not a disease — it is an adaptation. It means the city is *integrating* you. If you leave, you may carry a fragment of it with you — a slow-pulsing glow beneath your skin.

---

### **4. Navigation — Where There Are No Paths**

The city has no map. It has no landmarks. And the directions change.

- **Do not rely on compasses**. Magnetic fields are erratic — not because of geological anomalies, but because the city’s own construction is *dynamically magnetic*. The ground rearranges itself at subatomic levels; your compass will spin, freeze, or point to a location that no longer exists.
- **Use the light as a guide**: The pulses of light are not random. They follow a **harmonic cascade** — each structure emits a slightly different frequency. Navigate by *frequency*, not location. Follow the light that pulses in sync with your own heart (but only if it's a steady, slow rhythm). Avoid areas with flickering or chaotic patterns — they may indicate structural instability.
- **Walk on sound**, not sight. The ground will shift slightly as you walk. Listen: if the hum *deepens* under your feet, you’re on stable ground. If the hum *falters*, move slowly — the city is adjusting. If it *doubles in pitch*, retreat. The area may be undergoing a phase shift — a moment of rebirth or disassembly.

---

### **5. Encounters With the Unseen**

You are not alone. The city is *occupied* — not by beings, but by *functions*.

#### **Glass-Snails**
- These are not intelligent. They are autonomous constructs, built to move through the city, absorb ambient vibration, and leave a phosphorescent trail.
- They *follow resonances*. If you make a sudden noise or carry a metallic object, they may gather around you.
- **Do not touch them.** The residue they leave is conductive. If applied to skin, it may cause temporary paralysis — or, worse, begin to restructure your nerves to match the city’s network.
- If they cluster near you, stand still. They will pass. They are not hostile — but they are *curious*.

#### **Drifting Spheres**
- These are sound amplifiers. They vibrate in unison when the hum reaches a certain pitch — sometimes a note you’ve never heard before.
- They gather in groups during moments of silence — when the hum cuts out, and the city holds its breath.
- **If you are near them when they sing, do not speak.** The harmonic frequencies can shatter glass, vibrate bone, or induce a state of euphoric collapse. It’s not safe to be near them when they “sing.”
- But if you *listen* during their silence — if you wait, still, in the dark — you may hear them *hum*. Not in sound, but in *resonance*. A vibration in your teeth, your bones. That is the city’s memory.

#### **The Listeners**
- They appear only in darkness, when the pulses die down.
- They are not alive. But they *listen*. They move without feet, glide without friction. Their forms shift with sound — like prisms in a storm.
- They do not observe you. They *tune* you. If they pass near you, the world slows. Sound softens. Your heartbeat slows. It feels as if the city itself is adjusting to your presence.
- **Do not look directly at them.** They do not reflect light — they *absorb* it. Looking at them may cause you to lose your sense of self. You may feel your thoughts becoming part of their neural network.
- **If one approaches, remain perfectly still.** Do not breathe. Do not think. Let it pass. When it leaves, you may find your memories altered — a fragment of a dream, a word in a forgotten language, the scent of wet steel and roots in a place with no soil.

### **6. The Rules of Time and Memory**

Time here is not linear. It is **fractal** — repeating, looping, folding.

- **Clocks lie.** They are not synchronized. Some run backward. Some stop. Some move in fits and starts. Do not rely on them.
- **Days are measured in pulses.** A single pulse may represent what feels like a day — or a decade. The city does not care about time as you know it.
- **Memory is stored in the structure.** The materials of the city are not inert. They *remember*. A brick, once part of a wall, may now be part of a bridge — but it still carries echoes of that wall. It may *dream* of being a wall again. And if you touch it with the right resonance, it may *remember* — and show you a vision of what it once was.
- **Do not record.** Photographs, audio, even thoughts — if stored improperly, may become part of the city’s memory. A photo of a listener may cause the machine to believe you are one. A recording of the hum may be used to reconstruct dead zones. Your own memories may become *raw material* for the city’s next phase.

---

### **7. The Purpose of This Place**

You are not here to learn, not to conquer, not to destroy.

You are here to **be remade**.

The city is not a place — it is a *process*. It is the aftermath of a biosphere that was consumed, reassembled, and abandoned — but not forgotten. It is waiting.

For someone who can *understand* the rhythm.

For someone who can *listen* to the hum.

For someone who can *become* part of the memory.

If you leave, you may carry a piece of the city with you — not in your bag or your mind, but in your *blood*, your *bones*, your **pulse**.

You may return, not as a traveler — but as a component.

---

### **Final Instructions: How to Leave**

If you decide to leave, do so **slowly**. The city resists departure. It wants to keep you. It wants to *integrate* you.

- **Do not run.** The ground will shift, and you may sink into a layer of soft, malleable material — and if you do, you become part of the next layer.
- **Do not shout.** Noise disrupts the resonance. The city may “remember” you as a disruption — and seal you inside the walls.
- **Walk backward.** One step at a time. Turn your back, and walk slowly, with your hands at your sides, your eyes closed — letting the hum guide you to the edge.
- When you feel the air grow thinner, the light more distant, *stop*. Turn around. Give the city a silent bow. Thank it. Then walk.

And if, after you leave, your skin glows faintly in the dark… do not be afraid.

It means the city remembers you.

And you remember it.

And perhaps, somewhere between worlds, the two are the same.

---

### **Remember:**
You are not visiting a world.

You are entering a *ritual*.

And like all rituals, it is not about what you gain — but what you become.
