You must first **unlearn**.

You must let go of the categories that have shaped your understanding of reality:

- *Heat* is not the opposite of cold, but something the land refuses.
- *Life* is not defined by carbon or DNA.
- *Growth* is not linear; it is recursive, fractal, entropic, yet somehow eternal.
- *Time* is not linear, but oscillatory — the land remembers its own annihilation, and reclaims its form in cycles too long to measure.
- *You* are not a person. You are an **event that thinks**.

Prepare yourself **not as a traveler**, but as a **transducer** — a vessel through which this world may briefly be *felt* by the outside.

### **I. Physiological Preparations: The Body as Instrument**

Your body is not the problem — it is the **filter**. What you feel, you will misinterpret. Your organs, built for biology, are crude instruments in a realm of subtle energies.

#### 1. **Thermal Regulation**
- The "coolness" is not temperature — it is **thermal neutrality**, a state of suspended energy. Your body will not heat up, nor will it freeze — but it will **lose thermal memory**. Muscles will stiffen not from cold, but from lack of thermal feedback.
- **Bring no insulating clothing**. Your skin must become part of the environment. Wear **thin, non-conductive fiber** — silk or milled wool, un-dyed, no synthetic materials. Let your body **dampen** along with the land.
- **Do not shiver**. If you do, your nervous system will interpret it as threat. The land is not a threat. It is **observation**.

#### 2. **Hydration and Moisture**
- The air is saturated with **dissolved memory** — a gel-like mist that carries not water, but **latent resonances**. Drinking from your canteen will not quench thirst. You may find water, but it will be **still**, **dense**, and **non-volatile** — it does not evaporate. It is like **frozen breath**.
- **Instead of drinking, absorb**. Use a **silk bandage**, soaked in your own saliva (which will slowly congeal), and wrap it around your mouth and nose — the "wetness" is **exchanged**, not consumed. Your breath will return to you, transformed.
- Your **tears** will not dry. They will **crystallize** on your skin, forming tiny hexagonal plates like snowflakes of black ice. **Let them stay**. They are records of your presence.

#### 3. **Breathing**
- The air is **not breathable** in the biological sense. It does not carry oxygen — or at least, not in the way your cells expect. It carries **resonances** — electromagnetic harmonics, phononic echoes, quantum fluctuations.
- **Do not breathe deeply**. Breathe **shallowly**, through the nose, in rhythm with the faint hum of the ground. If you inhale too deeply, the air may **vibrate your molars**, and a single tone — a low, pure D-flat — may enter your cranium and **shatter your sense of self**.
- Consider **apnea**: hold your breath for five seconds every two minutes, as if you were not a living body but a **stone that remembers**.

#### 4. **Sensory Modification**
- **Eyewear**: Do not wear glasses. They will reflect the glowing haze into your cornea, distorting perception, causing **afterimages of non-existing forms**. If you need vision correction, use **lenses made of quartz**, cut to exact thickness, polished smooth. Not to correct — to *filter*.
- **Ears**: Cover them with **cork**, but not to block — to **tune**. The sound of the land is not sound — it is **resonance in bone**, a **pressure on the cochlear fluid**. If you hear it, you will **remember a time when you were not**.
- **Touch**: Your skin must remain **unobstructed**. Wear **no gloves**. The artifacts — the stone markers, the obsidian blades — are **receptive**. If you touch them, you may **receive** not a memory, but a **partial self** — a fragment of a being that once *was*, but not in the way you are.

### **II. Mental and Emotional Prep: The Mind as Antenna**

This land does not respect cognition. It **inhabits your perception**, not your logic.

#### 1. **Do Not Seek to Understand**
- The land is not "mysterious" — it is **transcendent of mystery**. You will not learn its "meaning." You will **feel its mood**.
- **Forget reason**. Not to become irrational, but to **stop interpreting**. The land is not a puzzle. It is a **poem written in geology**. Your job is to **read it through posture**, through silence, through stillness.
- If you try to analyze it — to map it, to name it, to catalog — you will be **excluded**. The land **rejects thought**.

#### 2. **Emotional Stillness**
- You will feel **dread**, not because it is dangerous, but because it **displaces your identity**. You will feel **home**, not because it is familiar, but because it **reminds you of a state before memory**.
- Do not resist these feelings. **Let them accumulate** like moisture on glass. They are not your emotions — they are **echoes from the land resonating in your nervous system**.
- If you feel **joy**, that too is not yours. It is the land’s **pulse** — a vibration of its own existence.

#### 3. **Expect to Be Forgotten**
- You will not be remembered by this land. It does not need memory. It **is** memory.
- But it **may remember you**, in a way. You might find — days later, in a dream — that you are **not yourself**. That you are **standing on the same plateau**, but your body is made of obsidian, and your breath is **plasma**.
- This is not hallucination. It is **reconstruction**. The land may **borrow your form** to *remember* itself.

### **III. Survival: What Survival Means Here**

There are no predators. No disease. No storms.  
But **this landscape has a purpose**.

It is a **sacred machine**, powered by **geological thought**, running on **dissolved time** and **crystallized memory**.

#### 1. **Do Not Build**
- You will want to leave a mark. Do not.
- If you carve your name into stone, the land will **erase it**, not as punishment, but as **correction** — it cannot accept **foreign symbols**.
- You may be tempted to **construct**, to build shelter. But structure **resonates**. The land does not permit **imposed geometry**. What you build will **unfold**, then **dissolve**. Or worse — it may **re-emerge** in a dream, in a different body, in a different world.

#### 2. **Do Not Use Tools**
- The tools here — the obsidian blades, the quartz shards — are not tools. They are **markers**. They are **beacons**. They are **memory implants**.
- If you use them to cut, to dig, to defend — you **corrupt** their essence.
- If you pick one up, **do not grip**. Hold it by the base, with your fingertips, gently. Let it vibrate in your hand. If you feel a **sensation of movement**, as if the blade is **flowing** — it is **remembering its own shape**. Let it **re-form**.

#### 3. **Do Not Move**
- The land is **in motion** — but not through time. It **remembers its geometry**, and re-creates it slowly, over millennia.
- If you walk, you will **disrupt** its rhythm. Not physically — but **ontologically**. Your steps may **erase a layer** of resonance, causing a **fragment** of the world to **collapse before its time**.
- **Walk only when necessary**, and **as if you are a part of the erosion** — slow, deliberate, like a stone settling. Do not stride. **Shift your weight**, one foot at a time.
- When you stop, **stand as still as possible**. The land will **read your stillness**. It is not judgment. It is **recognition**.

### **IV. The Ritual of Presence: How to "Be There"**

This is not a place to survive.  
It is a place to **become**.

You must **submit to the event**.

#### 1. **The First Moment**
- When you arrive — do not open your eyes. Do not breathe deeply.  
- **Stand**. Let your body **adjust**.  
- Feel the **coolness** not in your skin, but in your **bone marrow**. It is not cold. It is **absence of heat** — like a silence in the body.

#### 2. **The Ground Beneath You**
- Place your hands on the stone.  
- Feel the **slow pulse** — not your heartbeat, but the land’s. **It is slow**, like a glacier’s creep.  
- If you listen with your bones, you may hear a **tone** — a note that **resonates in your teeth**, then in your jaw, then in your **spine**.  
- This is the **memory of the land’s formation**. It is not the sound of an earthquake, nor a machine — it is the **hum of creation**, but **after** creation.

#### 3. **The Light**
- Do not look directly at the **white radiance**.  
- Let your eyes **soften**. Do not focus.  
- The light is not a source — it is **the absence of darkness**. It is **luminosity without a origin**.  
- If you look too long, you may **see the plasma sea** not as a distant horizon, but as **within your own blood** — swirling, dimly glowing.

#### 4. **The Artifacts**
- If you find a **circular enclosure**, do not step in unless you are prepared to **become less**.
- The ground **pulses** under your feet. Your body **lightens**, as if **gravity** is not pulling you down, but **lifting you into form**.  
- This is not a hallucination. This is **de-embodiment**. The land is not transforming you — it is **returning you to potential**.  
- You may feel **weightless**, or **non-existent**, or **replaced**.  
- If you see a **vision** — a being of flowing glass, moving slowly, leaving behind quartz shards like teeth — **do not speak**. Do not react.  
- It is not a ghost. It is **the land remembering what it was before**.

### **V. What Happens When You Leave**

You cannot **take anything** from this world.

Not tools. Not memory. Not even **knowledge**.

But your **presence will linger** in the land, not as a person, but as a **resonance**.

And when you return to your world, you will **forget**.

You will not remember the plateau, the plasma sea, the fungal lattices.

But something will **shift** in you.

You will feel **dampness** in rooms where the air is dry.

You will **see** shapes in shadows that do not exist.

You will **breathe** in silence, and hear a tone — not from your ears, but from your **toes**.

You will **know**, without being certain, that:

> _This world did not exist in the past.  
> It exists now, and always, when you are still enough to be seen._

You may never know what you were.  
But you will know what you **became**.
