### **Preparation: What You Must Do Before You Step In**

> *The mind must be as fluid as the biomass, but the body must be as sturdy as a root.*

#### 1. **Do Not Come as a Conqueror, but as a Guest**
You are not here to build, to mine, to colonize, or even to study. The Suspended Green does not accept **imposition**. It accepts **presence**.

- If you come with tools, weapons, or machines, you will be *unseen*. Not in a metaphysical way, but in a physical: the biomass will not respond to your technology. It will reconfigure around it, absorb it, dissolve it. Metal will rust *into* the plant lattice, plastics will be broken down by microbial lattices in minutes, and any electrical system will be shorted by the conductive, ion-rich gel.
- **Your goal**: Be an **ant**, not a tree. Not a predator. Not a destroyer. A **reciprocal** entity.

#### 2. **Respiratory System: You Can Breathe, But Not Like Earth**
The air is breathable, yes—but it is **not Earth air**.

- Oxygen content: ~24% (slightly higher than Earth) — but it is **dissolved in organic vapor**, rich with chlorophyll byproducts, carbon dioxide, methane, and trace gases of photosynthetic fermentation (like ethylene, hydrogen sulfide, and methanol).
- **Result**: After 10–15 minutes in the atmosphere, your blood will begin to **thicken** due to ion absorption. Your hemoglobin will start to bind with plant-based hemoproteins — a natural adaptation the biomass encourages. This does **not** kill you — it *integrates* you.
- **Risk**: If you stay for more than a week, your lungs will begin to develop **alveolar chlorophyllocytes** — your body will start *photosynthesizing* in low-light conditions. This is not a disease. It is **assimilation**.

➡️ **Preparation**:
- Bring **breathing filters** made of porous charcoal and activated biopolymer mesh — but *do not* wear them permanently. Use them only the first 60 minutes.
- After that, **breathe deep and slow**. Let the world enter you.
- If you start to dream of green light, you're doing it right.

#### 3. **Physical Support: You Will Not Walk. You Will Float, Glide, or Drift**
There is **no firm ground**.

The "surface" behaves like a **living gelatinous ocean** — semi-solid, semi-fluid. It supports weight, but only by **dynamic distribution**. If you stand still, you will **sink slowly**, like a stone in quicksand, but over hours. If you move, you can **push through**, but always with resistance.

- **Your weight is felt** as a disturbance. The biomass will **reform** around you — like a wound closing — but only slowly. The deeper you go, the more it *remembers* your shape. You leave **ghost impressions**.
- The best way to move is **in harmony** — not walking, but **drifting**, **floating**, or **pushing against the current of slow expansion and contraction**.

➡️ **Preparation**:
- Wear lightweight **neoprene or bio-elastic armor** — not rigid. It must allow the biomass to *flow* around you.
- **No boots** — use **floating slippers** made from recycled mycelium foam — they absorb moisture and release it slowly, maintaining buoyancy.
- Learn to **step** not like a person, but like a **leaf** — soft, slow, and in rhythm with the breath of the world.

#### 4. **Thermoregulation: It’s Always Cold, But You Don’t Feel It**
Temp: ~0°C (32°F), but **never freezing**.

- The biomass has a **living insulation layer** — not heat, but **entropic stability**. It absorbs and redistributes thermal energy through its network of vasculature.
- **Result**: You will not *feel* cold. Your body will **cool slowly**, but your blood will begin to **slow** — the plant metabolism will encourage **hypometabolism**, a state of reduced cellular activity.
- This is not hibernation. It is **synchronization**.

➡️ **Preparation**:
- Wear **layered microfibers** — not wool or cotton (they absorb moisture and stick).
- **No exposure of skin** — the biomass can **infect** non-living tissue with fungal hyphae if left open.
- Use a **bio-rhythmic suit** — one that pulses with a frequency mimicking the ambient 0.2 Hz resonance of the world (the "deep hum"). This will help you **stay in sync** and avoid **biological desynchronization**, which causes panic, confusion, and unconsciousness.

#### 5. **Visual Perception: Light is Not Real; It is Emergent**
The sky is a **living canvas** of slow-moving, diffuse light.

- Sunlight is **refracted** through layers of spore clouds and bioluminescent matter. You don't see the sun. You feel it — as a **warmth in your bones**, not on your skin.
- The **shadow** is not absence of light — it is **density**. Where the biomass thickens, darkness pools, but it glows faintly with internal bioluminescence.
- Colors are **not wavelengths** — they are **emotional states** of the biomass. A crimson hue at dusk is not red light — it’s a **pulse of metabolic stress**, a warning to fauna.

➡️ **Preparation**:
- Wear **adaptive ocular filters** — lenses that shift to detect **phosphorescence, ion density, and pulse frequency**, not wavelength.
- **Do not try to look at the sky**. It is not a sky. It is a **ceiling of suspended memory**.
- If you stare too long, you may begin to see **faces in the mist** — ancient patterns of neural signal flow. They are real, but not **sentient**. They are **echoes of the system**, not minds.

#### 6. **Auditory Experience: You Do Not Hear. You Feel Sound**
Sound is **not a wave**. It is **vibration**.

- The "roar" of the ocean is **not sound**, but **subsonic pressure waves** traveling through plant tissue.
- Your ears will not hear it. But you will **feel** it in your **bones, your teeth, your spine** — like a constant, deep, slow **thrum**.
- The **plink** of droplets is not water. It is **cellular rupture** — a sound of **growth**.
- The **rustle** of leaves is not wind — it is **electrochemical signaling** across vascular networks.

➡️ **Preparation**:
- Do not use **external audio devices**. They will be overwhelmed by the ambient resonance.
- Wear **bone-conductive headphones** that translate vibrations into **internal sound** — but only if you're used to them.

#### 7. **Olfactory Intuition: The Air Has a Mind**
Scent is **not smell**. It is **memory**.

- The air smells of **wet earth, rot, ozone, flower, and something sweet and metallic** — like iron in a forest after rain.
- But these are not molecules. They are **biochemical signals** — the world is **broadcasting**.
- If you smell **burnt copper**, a warning of a metabolic shift is coming. If you smell **vanilla and ash**, the biosphere is in a state of **reconfiguration**.

➡️ **Preparation**:
- **Do not block your nose**. That is a defense mechanism against a world that does not need defense.
- Train yourself to **smell with clarity** — not emotion. You will become **smell-literate** over time.
- Do not **cry** — tears mix poorly with the biomass. They will be absorbed, and their salt will **initiate localized decomposition**.

#### 2. **Do Not Eat**
There is no food.

- If you eat a piece of biomass, it will **absorb you** — not digest you, but **incorporate** you. You become a **node** in the neural network.

➡️ **Do not chew**. Do not taste. **Do not open your mouth** unless you must breathe.

#### 3. **Do Not Fight**
The fauna will not attack you. **But if you attack, they will destroy you** — not violently, but **slowly**, through **systemic disintegration**.

➡️ **Do not resist**. Observe. **Be quiet**. **Be still**.

#### 4. **Time is Not Yours**

➡️ **Let go of identity**. You are not yourself. You are **a part of a greater pattern**.

#### 5. **You Will Forget Why You Came**

➡️ **This is not madness**. This is **homecoming**.
