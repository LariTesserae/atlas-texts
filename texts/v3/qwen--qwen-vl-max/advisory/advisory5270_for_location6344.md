### 1. **The Body First: An Architecture of Porosity**

The body is the first failure.  
Human biology is rigid, sealed, linear. It breathes air. It metabolizes discrete fuel. It expects **static ground** beneath the foot.  
This world demands **fluid coexistence**.

#### *Preparation:*
- **Detoxify the skin.**  
  Do not wear sealed boots, nor metal, nor synthetic fabrics. The paste *resists* impermeability. It will *seek* gaps, *find* cracks in your suit, and work its way in — not to infect, but to *integrate*.  
  Instead, wear **bio-keratin garments** grown from symbiotic fungi — flexible, self-repairing, and *semi-permeable* like a coral polyp. These will allow slow exchange: your moisture out, its warmth in. They will *resonate* with the paste’s vibrations, not block them.  

- **Train the feet.**  
  The paste is warm, viscous, alive. It will **shift beneath you** like water and earth and memory simultaneously. Your gait must change.  
  Do not walk. **Glide**. Let your movements be as slow, liquid, and rhythmic as possible. Practice **hydrokinetic walking** — a step that begins in the heel, rolls through the arch, and releases forward with a wave of weight, not force.  
  The **soles of the feet must be calibrated** — not sensitive to pain, but to **texture, pressure gradients, and phase transitions**. They should be embedded with pressure sensors that translate micro-oscillations into nerve feedback. But even this may misfire. The paste will *simulate* pain — not to harm, but to redirect. So train to **distinguish real sensation from feedback loops of the world’s intent**.

- **Respiratory adaptation.**  
  The air is thick with condensation — droplets the size of marbles slowly falling from the sky, *not* as rain, but as **exhalations**. Breathing this will not kill you, but it will **alter your physiology**.  
  Install **nasal filters with electrostatic mesh** that can neutralize particles with a crystalline lattice structure — for the paste is not merely liquid, but contains **mineral fragments that can trigger cellular replication in non-native organisms**.  
  But do not block everything. **Allow a small fraction** to enter. The paste contains **organic information vectors** — not DNA, not RNA, but **topological engrams** imprinted in the flow. Inhale too much, and your cells may begin to *reorganize*. Inhale too little, and you may miss the world’s **scent of becoming** — a smell like wet stone, ozone, and **the ghost of a forgotten city**.
