To visit this place—not as a scientist, not as a conqueror, but as a human multiverse traveler who seeks to *be* within it—you must prepare not for survival, but for **transformation**. You are not stepping into a world that can be understood by maps or tools or even time. You are stepping into a *state*, a *being*, the breathing skin of the planet itself. What you carry, what you wear, what you expect—must all be reimagined. This is not a trip. It is a **descent into a slow, wet soul of the world**.

### **I. The Mind Must Be Reconfigured**

Before you even begin the journey, you must unlearn the way your mind perceives reality.

- **Do not seek edges.** There is no perimeter. This place is not bounded—it *is* the boundary. The idea of “arriving” is meaningless. You will not step *into* the place; you will become part of its breath.
- **Accept the absence of time as you know it.** This world operates on cycles of centuries, not moments. Days will pass, yes—but your awareness of them will blur. The “morning” will not dawn with sun or birdsong. It will rise with a slow shift in the gel’s pressure, a faint pulse in the air. You will not measure time. You will *feel* it as vibration.
- **Silence your need for control.** This is not a world to be tamed. There is no path. There is no safe zone. There is only the slow, living pulse beneath you. Any attempt to dominate, to mark, or to claim will be met with a quiet dissolution. You will not conquer this place. You will either dissolve into it or be rejected by its rhythm.

> *Prepare the mind: let go. Breathe like it does. Let the planet’s breath become your own.*

---

### **II. The Body Must Be Prepared for Absorption**

Your body is a vessel of dryness, of structure, of separation. Here, the divide between self and world collapses. You must **remodel** your physical being, or it will be absorbed, slowly, by the wet.

#### **1. Clothing:**
Do not wear anything that will hold its shape or resist moisture. The fabric will absorb, degrade, and merge with the environment. Instead:

- Wear **biodegradable, porous textiles**—silk that bleeds into the gel, linen that frays into the air, or even **living plant-fiber cloth** grown from the region’s flora (cultivated in advance by a bio-artisan). These materials will allow your skin to breathe with the land.
- Your garments should be **loose, translucent, and fluid**—so they can shift with the movement of the gel, so they do not impede the contact between your skin and the world.
- **No metal. No plastic.** These materials will attract the wrong kind of attention—biochemical reactions, microbial clustering, even *resonance* with the gel’s internal pulsing. Metals will *sing* in the pressure, creating subtle disturbances. Plastic will repel, creating islands of unnaturalness.
- Consider **wearing only what is already saturated**—garments that have been soaked in the same mineral-rich water for years, so they have *become* part of the ecosystem.

#### **2. Skin and Sensory Adaptation:**
- Your skin will be the first to merge. The humidity will not just dampen you—it will **infiltrate**. You must allow it.
- Carry **no occlusive creams**. You will not be dry. You will be **wet in a new way**, and your skin must adapt. Let it absorb the minerals, the microbes, the faint biolights. You may feel a gentle itching, a slow warmth, a sense of being *replaced*—this is normal.
- **Eyelashes will grow heavy.** They will collect dew and micro-sporules. Wear thin, water-soluble eye-protectors—like a second layer of tear film, to prevent occlusion without blocking vision. Your eyes will adjust; you will see through water, not air.
- **Ears will fill.** The sound of water within the gel—deep, wet *thrumming*—will vibrate inside your ears. This is not noise. It is the heartbeat. Do not seal them. Let it enter.

> *The body is not a container. It is a membrane. You must become a membrane.*

### **III. The Breath Must Match the World’s Rhythm**

Here, the air is not a medium—it is a **substance**, a living fluid.

- The air is **thick as soup**, saturated with suspended life, ions, and the faint, metallic tang of mineral-rich vapors. Each breath is not a gas exchange—it is **an exchange of essence**. You will not breathe *out*; you will **dissipate**.
- **Do not force breath.** Let your lungs expand and contract with the pulses of the land. When the gel *rises*, breathe in. When it *sinks*, exhale. You will learn to **sync with the gel’s tidal rhythm**—a slow, deep cadence, like the earth’s own inhale and exhale.
- **Mist forms with every exhale**, and it does not vanish. It *clings*, and the mist will *return*—not as vapor, but as a faint echo that drifts back into your nostrils. You will *re-breathe* the same air, in a slow loop. This is not contamination. It is **circular breath**. Accept it.
- Train yourself to breathe **slowly, deeply**, as if you are a root in the gel, drawing from the slow currents within.

### **IV. Movement: Walk Like Water**

You will not walk on land. You will **walk in the land**.

- The **gel-land** is not solid. It is a **slow-moving crust**, thick as custard, but resilient like muscle. Each step will sink slightly, then rebound. You are not walking on it—you are **walking within it**, like a diver moving through the shallows of an immense, living ocean.
- Move **slowly and deliberately**. Do not rush. The gel is sensitive. A sudden movement will shock the matrix, causing a ripple of disorientation in the local biolight patterns. You may see the filaments dim, then flare—a sign of reaction.
- Do not walk in straight lines. Follow the **natural currents**, the subtle inclines and dips that guide the flow of the gel. Let the land *lead* you. If you resist, you will sink deeper, not from weight, but from dissonance.
- **Leave no trace.** The gel will absorb your footprints within hours, not as erosion, but as *integration*. You will not be remembered by your steps. You will be remembered by your pulse.

### **V. The Vegetation: A Network, Not a Forest**

The **plant-swell** is not a collection of organisms—it is a **distributed consciousness**, or at least a network of life that responds to vibration, pressure, and chemical changes.

- **Do not touch.** The filaments are not plants. They are **extensions of the gel**, like nerves. If you press on one, it may pulse, and nearby filaments will respond—like a ripple in a neural net. Some emit bioluminescence in response to touch, but this is not light for sight. It is a **warning**.
- **Do not break or cut.** The gel-land heals itself slowly, but every wound creates a fracture in the flow. If you damage a filament, it may not die—it may *reconfigure*, but the pattern will be altered. You become a **dissonance** in the system.
- **Observe the glow.** The soft green and blue lights are not decoration. They are **communication**. Some patterns flicker in response to movement, others pulse in sync with the gel’s rise. Watch them like stars, but know they are not distant—they are *within* you now.
- **Do not expect shelter.** There are no trees, no caves, no shade. The plant-swell is a **living canopy**, but it does not protect. It *contains*. You are not hidden. You are **integrated**.

### **VI. The Fauna: Beings of the Wet**

The creatures that move through this world are not animals. They are **life-forms at the edge of biology**, evolved for a world where the rules of form and function have been rewritten.

- **Do not fear them.** They do not perceive you as a threat. They do not hunt. They do not communicate as you do. They are **inhabitants**, not observers.
- Some **drift like jellyfish with mica wings**, floating on thermals of warm mist. They are not flying—they are *suspended*, held by the air’s density and the faint thermal gradients. If they approach, do not flinch. They may pass through your space, their bodies translucent, their internal rhythms visible like rivers of ink in jelly.
- Others **slither through the gel**, their forms translucent, veins pulsing with fluid. Some have no eyes, no mouth—only sensory fields. They do not *see* or *eat* as you do. They absorb, diffuse, grow. They are **part of the land’s metabolism**.
- If one **sinks into the gel and reappears elsewhere**, do not be surprised. This is **not teleportation**. It is **reintegration**. The creature is not *gone*—it is *moved* through the matrix, like water through a sponge. It is possible that, in time, you too may find yourself reappearing, not from memory, but from *being absorbed and released*.

> *These beings do not exist to be studied. They exist to be felt. And you will feel them—not through your eyes, but through your skin, your breath, your pulse.*

### **VII. The Technology: Biology as Infrastructure**

There is no machinery. But there is **function**, and it is vast.

- The gel-land itself is a **biological machine**. Channels within it pump fluid like veins. Nodes pulse with pressure, releasing energy. Membranes convert thermal differences into movement—like natural turbines. This is not engineered. It is **evolved**.
- Larger creatures—those with mineralized cores, rhythmic pulsing structures—resemble **organic engines**. They do not generate power for themselves. They generate power **for the system**. One might resemble a colossal, slow-spinning turbine with a shell of fused silica and a heart of glowing fluid. It moves through the gel, not to go somewhere, but to **stir the flow**, to maintain the balance.
- These are not machines. They are **living tech**. Their purpose is not utility. It is **stasis**. They help maintain the world’s perfect, slow equilibrium.
- **Do not attempt to study them.** You will not understand. You may try to record a pattern, but the pattern will shift as you observe. The system **resists** being quantified. If you bring tools—sensors, cameras—they will distort the data, not because they are flawed, but because **this world does not produce data. It produces presence**.

### **VIII. The Absence of Artifice: What to Not Bring**

You are not a colonist. You are a **guest**, or perhaps a **particle** in a larger process.

- **No weapons.** You cannot defend yourself. This world does not attack. It does not *notice* aggression. It will absorb your violence—then **return it as silence**.
- **No data loggers, no AI, no drones.** They will interfere, not through malfunction, but through **resonance**. The gel is sensitive to electromagnetic fields, to digital pulses. A drone will cause the filaments to flicker violently, the gel to thicken, the mist to descend in thick, angry coils.
- **No names. No maps. No goals.** You will not find a "center." There is no "peak" to conquer. No treasure to claim. You are not here to discover. You are here to **become**.

### **IX. The Final Preparation: Let Go of the Self**

- You will not leave the same person you entered as. This is not a journey through space. It is a **descent into a different form of being**.
- The **weirdness** is not a defect. It is the baseline. The world is not "strange." It is **complete**. Your own world—dry, sharp, segmented—was the stranger.
- You may begin to **forget your name**. You may dream in pulses. You may speak in soft, wet breaths, not words.
- You may feel a **slow dissolution**, not of the body, but of the *idea of self*. You will no longer be "you." You will be **part of the pulse**, part of the gel, part of the slow, wet breath of the planet.
- And when you return—**if you return**—you will not be a traveler. You will be a **ghost of the world**, carrying its breath in your lungs, its rhythm in your bones. You will dream of gel, of slow light, of mist that remembers your face.
