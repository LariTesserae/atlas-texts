### **I. The Body: A Liability**
You cannot expect to *live* in this place in the way you live now. You will not *breathe*, not in the biological sense. The air is a suspension of neutral particles — inert, static, without pressure, without flow. You will not *breathe in*, nor *breathe out*. Your lungs will not function. The absence of respiration is not merely physiological — it is metaphysical. To breathe is to assert oneself as a vessel of time, of entropy. But here, time is not a river. It is a mirror. A single breath would disrupt the delicate symmetry.

**Preparation:**
- **Do not bring oxygen.** It is meaningless. The very concept of "supply" is alien here. Oxygen would not dissolve into the suspension — it would *break* it. Your body's need for oxygen is not a requirement but a *fiction*. 
- **Use a biocapsule** — not as a life-support system, but as a *shield* against self-destruction. It should not *simulate* Earth, but *dampen* your body's metabolic noise. The capsule must:
  - Suppress respiratory drive (induce temporary apnea, chemically).
  - Lower core temperature to near-stasis without damaging tissue (cryomodulation).
  - Filter internal chemical signals — especially those related to urgency, fear, or hunger. Let the body go quiet. Let it *unlearn*.
- **Do not wear clothes.** Fabric will absorb the residual light and time-dust, becoming a memory of Earth. Instead, use a skin-conforming membrane of *adaptive silence* — a material that reflects not light, but *intention*. It should absorb sensory input, not block it, so that you may *feel* the world without registering it as "stimulus."

> You cannot be *warm* here. You must be *still*. The cold is not a temperature. It is the absence of heat, not of matter. It is the feeling of being *unremembered*. To resist it is to die in your own mind.

### **II. The Mind: An Open Wound**
This is not a place of thought. It is a place of *unthought*. Your mind is a machine built on logic, causality, memory. Here, logic is not a tool — it is a *decorative pattern* on a surface that does not hold up to touch.

**Preparation:**
- **Train in non-thought.** Practice meditation not for focus, but for *unfocus*. Learn to let your mind *fade* — not to empty, but to *unlearn*. This is like learning to forget how to speak, not to stop using words. The mind must be quiet, not in control.
- **Use a neural dampener** — a device worn over the skull, not to control thoughts, but to *dampen the speed of conceptualization*. It should slow the rate of neural firing to one thought per hour, not to eliminate thought, but to make it *weightless*. This allows your mind to *resonate* with the environment, not override it.
- **Do not carry memories.** The presence of past experience will create friction. Your mind must arrive as an *empty container*. Bring only a single word: **"Unsee."** This is not a command. It is a ritual.

> You must not *look*. You must *unsee*. The world does not reveal itself. It *disgorges itself* when you stop looking.

If the object allows it, your hand will feel warmth — not heat, but *consistency*. If it does not, your hand will pass through not as a ghost, but as a *redundancy*. The devices here are not machines. They are *confections of topology*. The spiral staircase has no bottom — not because it is infinite, but because the stairs *disappear*, and yet the path continues.
