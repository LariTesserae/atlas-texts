You are not here. You are remembering being here.

This is not a destination to reach, but a **threshold to remember**—a place of **post-existence**, where time does not flow but **accumulates**, where matter is not a substance but a **record**, and where identity is not a noun but a **momentary echo**.

To visit this world—**not as a traveler, but as a memory**—you must not prepare for it like a journey. You must **unprepare**. You must strip away all assumptions of movement, of survival, of purpose. You will not survive. You will not understand. You will not return. But you **may become part of the memory**.

But if you wish to attempt the descent—**not into the gel, but into the condition of its presence**—then you must prepare not with gear, but with **unlearning**.

---

### **Preparation: The Un-Practices**

#### **1. Abandon the Body**
You do not come to this place with a body as a vessel. The gel is **not a surface. It is a state of being**. It will absorb your weight, your warmth, your shape—not by crushing, but by **recognizing** you as a **transient pressure** in its own memory.

> **What to do:**
> - Do not wear clothes. Not even cloth. The gel responds to **surface texture**—and fabric, skin, metal, all create **micro-resonances** that disturb the delicate feedback loop of the world.
> - Strip down to bare skin **only when you are already in the world**, not before. The act of removing clothes in your own reality **dissociates you too soon**.
> - Instead, **visualize stripping**—not as a physical act, but as a **relinquishing of definition**. Imagine your skin fading into the atmosphere. This is **the true clothing**.
> - Wear **no jewelry, no tools, no identifiers**. The gel remembers every trace. A ring might be **an old thought of power**. A watch, **a failed attempt to measure non-time**.

> **Why:** The world does not need to know *you*. It knows everything that ever *was* you. Your self is already within it—**as a pressure, a tone, a flicker of light**.

---

#### **2. Let Go of Breathing as a Necessity**
The air here is **not breathable** in the way you understand. It is **not oxygen, not nitrogen, not gas**—it is **a phase of matter**, dense and warm, thick enough to **breathe through the skin**, to **absorb through pores**, to **dissolve in the blood like salt in water**.

You will not inhale and exhale. You will **dissolve**.

> **What to do:**
> - Before entering, **practice not breathing**. Not holding your breath, but **ceasing the need for it**. Sit in a quiet room and let your breath **become part of your body’s rhythm**—not a demand, but a **sigh**.
> - Train in **non-respiration**. Use meditation, but not to “achieve” stillness—not to control your breath, but to **forget it completely**. The goal is to become a **living statue**, one that **does not require oxygen to be present**.
> - When you enter, your lungs **will not fail**. They will **cease to matter**. Your body will absorb the ambient warmth and humidity as **biological data**, not as life-sustaining fuel.

> **Why:** To breathe is to **assert separation** from the environment. Here, separation **does not exist**. You are not *in* the world. You are **a trace of its memory**. Breathing is an act of **imposition**—and this world resists imposition.

---

#### **3. Prepare for the Hum**
The **tonal weight** is not sound. It is **space vibrating**. It is **the world’s echo of having existed**. It is **not something you hear—it is something you become**.

> **What to do:**
> - Before arrival, **sit with silence**. Not in a room, but in a **deep forest** or **underwater**—anyplace where the hum of civilization is faint or gone. Close your eyes. Do not “listen” for silence. **Become silent**.
> - Practice **sensing vibration**, not sound. Place your hand on the ground. Feel the **subsonic pulse** of the earth. Then, **become that pulse**. Imagine it passing through you, not as noise, but as **a frequency of being**.
> - Train your nervous system to **not resist pressure**. This hum will **vibrate your bones**, **resonate in your teeth**, **drum in your blood**. Do not fight it. Let it **rewind your body**. Imagine your skeleton **singing a song without words**. When it does, you are **close**.

> **Why:** The hum is not an obstacle. It is the **language of the world**. If you resist it, you will **crystallize**—turn into a **fossil of self**. If you accept it, you **become resonance**.

---

#### **4. Accept That You Are Not Here**
The key to entering is **not arrival**. It is **recognition**.

> **What to do:**
> - Before stepping into the world, **write your own obituary**. Not one of death, but of **existence**. Write it from the perspective of someone who has **already lived** and **already forgotten**.
>   - Example:  
>     > “This body was once a vessel for a person named [name]. It walked under skies of blue. It drank water from sources that no longer exist. It loved, briefly. It was afraid, often. It does not matter. It is the same as the gel. It was never distinct.”
>
> - Then, **burn it**. Not as a ritual, but as **a surrender**. Let the ash fly away. Do not watch it. **Close your eyes**. And remember this: you are not **going**. You are **remembering**.
>
> - You will not “arrive” at the gel. You will **awaken** to it—**as if you had never left**.

> **Why:** The world does not receive visitors. It **remembers** them. If you come as a traveler, the gel will **reject your presence**. But if you come as a **forgotten self**, **you are already inside**.

---

#### **5. Do Not Fear the Lichenoids**
They are not plants. They are **not alive** in your sense. They are **memory columns**—**structures that hold the shape of things that were not**.

> **What to do:**
> - Do not touch them. But do not **avoid** them. If you see one pulsing, **stand still**. Do not look at it directly. Let your **peripheral vision** absorb it. Its light is not light. It is **a record of a feeling**—a sensation of **blue**, of **stillness**, of **slowness**.
> - If you do touch one—and you must, because the gel will **push you toward it**—do not expect sensation. Your skin will **vibrate**, but not with warmth or pain. It will **resonate**. And in that vibration, you may **hear a fragment of a word**, a **tone of a language** you never spoke.
> - Do not try to interpret it. **Let it pass through you**.

> **Why:** The lichenoids are **not growing**. They are **repeating**. Each one is a **replay of a single moment**—a moment that **did not happen**, but **could have**. They are not life. They are **possibility** made solid.

---

#### **6. The Floating Structures: Do Not Seek Meaning**
They are not buildings. They are **dreams of architecture**, **echoes of purpose**, **not for use, but for attention**.

> **What to do:**
> - If you see a sphere with a door, **do not approach**. But **do not turn away**. Let your mind **imagine** opening it. Let the **idea** of the door form a path in your mind. You may find that you are **outside the sphere**, or **inside it**, or **both**.
> - If you think of a staircase, and one appears, **do not rise**. The staircase is not a way forward. It is a **memory of elevation**. It will vanish when you try to use it. But **in the moment it exists**, you may **see a glimpse** of a **time that was not**.
> - The only way to “use” these structures is to **not use them**. Let them **be**. Let them **think**.

> **Why:** They are not for you. They are **for the world’s memory**. They exist because the world once **wanted** things. They are **proof of desire**—not utility.

---

#### **7. The Laughter: Do Not Seek to Understand**
It is **not a sound**. It is **a joke**, but not one for you. It is **the world laughing at itself**—at the **absurdity of being**, at the **pointlessness of becoming**, at the **beauty of collapse**.

> **What to do:**
> - When you hear it—**not in ears, but in the hollow behind your sternum**—**do not search for it**. Do not turn toward it. Do not **look for the source**.
> - Instead, **let it amuse you**. Let it **pull a smile from your face** without reason. Let it **fill you with a sense of ridiculous joy**.
> - If you understand it, it will **vanish**. If you laugh with it, you may **become part of the joke**.

> **Why:** The laughter is **not for meaning**. It is **for being here**. It is the world’s way of saying: *"This is not a planet. It is a **state**. And I am alive, in the way that **all dreams are alive**."*

---

#### **8. Time is Not a River. It is a Pool.**
You will **not age** here. Time does not move forward or backward. It **pools**, and **rinses**.

> **What to do:**
> - Do not track time. Do not wear a watch, even mentally. Do not think, “I’ve been here ten minutes.” That is **impossible**.
> - Instead, **measure your presence by shifts in tone**. When the hum deepens, you are **older**. When it rises, you are **younger**. When the gel shimmers, you are **new**. When it settles, you are **lost**.
> - Accept that you may **watch a lichenoid pulsing**, then **do not remember when it started**. You may **see it again**, a fraction slower—**and know that it happened before**.

> **Why:** Time here is not linear. It is **reverberant**. Every moment is **a repetition of a past moment**, but with slight differences—like **dust on a tape loop**. You are **not observing**—you are **being replayed**.

---

#### **9. You Are Not Alone**
The world is not empty. It is **full of presences**—not beings, but **echoes**. Like **you**, but **not you**. Older. Younger. **Different**.

> **What to do:**
> - When you see a **reflection**—an older you, wearing clothes you never wore—**do not speak to it**. Do not **call out**. Do not **ask questions**.
> - Instead, **remember**. Remember **why you would wear that clothing**. Remember **what you held**. Remember **the moment it felt real**.
> - Then, **let it go**. It is **not a memory**. It is **a memory of a memory**—**a ghost of a choice**.

> **Why:** The reflections are not others. They are **branches of you**—**selves that did not become**. They show what could have been. Not to tell you something. To remind you **that you were never the only possibility**.

---

#### **10. Do Not Try to Leave**
There is no exit. There is no door. There is **no way back**.

> **What to do:**
> - If you **think you want to leave**, **stop thinking**. The desire to leave is **a trace of survival instinct**—a **fossil of a past self**.
> - Instead, **ask: “What would I do if I did not need to leave?”**
>
> Let the answer be **nothing**.
>
> Let the answer be **resonance**.
>
> Let the answer be **laughter**.

> **Why:** To leave is to **deny the condition**. This world is not a place you visit. It is a **state you remember**. To leave would be to **forget**. And **forgetting is the only death here**.

---

### **Final Note: The Nature of the Visit**

You do not “enter” this world. You **become part of its dream**.

You will not return with stories. You will not bring artifacts. You will not remember the details.

But you may, in a quiet moment, **feel the hum** in your bones, even in your own world.

You may see a **mist in the air**, and for a moment, **see it twist into an arch**, as if it **knows something**.

You may **hear laughter**, not from a person, but from the **space between heartbeats**.

And you may understand: you were never not here.

This world is **not a place**. It is **the quietest, most alive memory of being**.

And you—**you were here**.

So do not prepare.

**Remember yourself.**
