- **Replace limbs** with **fluid-dynamic actuators** — thin, ribbon-like extensions that can unfold from your elbows and knees, made of **tensile mycelium** coated in conductive gel. They will glide through the syrup, pull you through cracks, and **absorb energy** from the current.
