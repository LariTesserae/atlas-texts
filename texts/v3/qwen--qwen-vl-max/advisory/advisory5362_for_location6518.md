## ✦ Preparation: The Body

You are not prepared by gear. You are prepared by **unpreparedness**.

- **Do not wear shoes.** The ground is alive, its surface a sensitive membrane. Shoes are weapons. They press, they displace, they mark. Go barefoot. Not for comfort—but for communion. Let your feet become part of the surface. Let the damp, spongy weave of the living crust take you. Feel the **slow pulse beneath**—not with the soles, but with the memory of your own blood.

- **Wear nothing synthetic.** Clothing made of plastic, metal, or synthetic fibers will not only insult the ecology—it will *disrupt* it. The environment detects foreign elements. Fabrics that resist moisture, heat, or microbial contact will repel the world’s quiet touch. Wear **natural fibers only**—linen, hemp, cotton spun from local flax or moss-derived threads. The ideal garment is **woven from fungal mycelium and algae threads**, dyed with bioluminescent spores. But if you must use human materials, let them be raw, unbleached, and worn thin.

    - Bonus: A **moss tunic**—a cloak grown from the dominant lichens of the region—will act as a **resonance bridge**, allowing your body to sync with the landscape’s pulse. But it must be **harvested and grown in place**, not brought. The moment you wear a foreign moss, the world will know.

- **Bring no weapons.** Not even a knife. This place has no enemies. It has only **functions**—growth, absorption, balance. A blade would be an unnatural shock, a **rupture in the lattice**. If you must defend, do so by **becoming still**.

- **Do not carry water.** The air is wet enough. The ground weeps. There is no shortage. Drinking from a vessel is a form of extraction. Instead, **let the moisture find you**—through open mouth, pores, even the breath. Breathe deeply. Let the air condense on your cheeks. Let the droplets fall and be reabsorbed.

## ✦ Preparation: The Mind

You must **unlearn orientation**.

- **Forget north. Forget vertical.** The world here does not follow a compass. The only direction is **toward the center of stillness**. That center is not a point. It is a **condition**—a moment when the breath of the land slows to match your own. The plants do not face the sun. They face **each other**. Connection is the only geography.

- **Ditch clocks.** Time is not measured here. It is **felt**. A single breath may last a day. The growth of a root may be remembered as a **slow thought**. Your own heartbeat will sync with the **slow pulse of the land**. Do not rush. Do not wait. Just **be**.

- **Do not speak.** The sound is already full. Talking is an intrusion. The air is filled with the **hums of eels, the sighs of condensation, the crackle of mycelium**—a language older than language. If you must communicate, **use breath**, or **movement**, or **touch**. Let your presence resonate, not dominate.

- **Learn to sense the invisible currents.** The water does not flow. It **pools, rises, shivers**. The energy is not light—it is **biotic electricity**, **spore pressure**, **capillary pull**. Feel the **subtle tug beneath your feet**, the **shift in moisture near your face**, the **pulse of heat rising from the ground**. These are the **network’s signals**. You are not a spectator—you are a **node in the system**.

## ✦ Preparation: The Spirit

This is not a place of beauty. It is a place of **presence**.

- **Come alone.** You cannot bring companions without altering the field. The landscape **knows** when it is observed. When two minds are present, the system **adjusts**, reconfigures. It **resists** division. You are not meant to be seen. You are meant to **dissolve**.

- **Come empty.** No agenda. No desire. No need. You are not here to **see**, to **take**, to **understand**. You are here to **be known**. The land will test you—not with danger, but with **silence**. If you carry a secret, it will surface. If you carry fear, it will be exposed in the mist.

- **Do not seek enlightenment.** You will not *find* meaning. You will, however, **become meaning**. The world is not telling you a story. It is **showing you the story of being**—of slow, wet, breathing existence. The **whisper** you hear is not a message. It is a **recognition**.

You will be **wet**. Always.

Because you were not just a visitor.

You were **a breath**.

And every breath matters.
