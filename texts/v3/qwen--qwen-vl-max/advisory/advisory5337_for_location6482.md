### **1. Unlearn the Physics You Know**

Your bones will not know how to respond. They will scream. You will fall upward. You will step forward and find yourself floating, spinning, as if the world itself is gently rejecting your mass.
  
- **Space is not empty**. The "emptiness" is not void. It is **potential**. It is **memory**. Looking down will make you feel as though you are falling into a dream that has no floor. The abysses below are **interdimensional**, not spatial. They are **echo chambers** of the world’s forgotten processes.

- **Distance is not measured in miles or light-years**. It is measured in **time of perception**. A mile may take a week to traverse if the land resists your passage. You might walk five steps and suddenly be standing on a plateau three hundred miles away, with no memory of how you got there. This is not teleportation. This is the land **reorganizing itself around your presence**, or perhaps **correcting your timeline**.

> **Preparation**: Before arrival, spend weeks in **zero-G simulation**, but not for weightlessness. For **disorientation**. Train in **non-Newtonian movement**—floating like a seed on a current, crawling on walls as if on ground. Practice **motion without momentum**. Learn to *feel* the pull of space, not *resist* it.

> **Preparation**: Before departure, **deplete your body of normal biological markers**.
