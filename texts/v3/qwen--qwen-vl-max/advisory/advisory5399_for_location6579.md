### **Before Departure: The Mind and the Body**

You must understand that this place is not *hostile*, but *indifferent*. You are not a priority. You are not an outlier. You are, simply, a *transient disturbance* — and the land will process you as it processes all things: by absorption, by erosion, by resonance.

- **Oxygen is thin**, but *not* absent. It's there, but in a state of flux. The air carries a kind of *static* — not electromagnetic, not ionic, but *vibrational*. It hums. It *resonates*. It is not gas, not quite, but a suspended medium of energy, moisture, and memory.

  > **Preparation**: Train your body to function in low oxygen environments. Not with masks or pressurized gear — those are useless here, as the "air" resists mechanical filtration. Instead, focus on *breath awareness*, meditation, slow metabolic conditioning. Your lungs must become *antennae*, not engines.

- **Your senses will shift.** Sight will not be primary. The visual spectrum here is distorted — the light is not reflected, but *emitted* by the land itself. Colors are not "seen" but *felt*, like warmth on skin. The eyes will adjust, yes, but they will become secondary. The true perception comes through the **jaw, through the bones, through the moisture on your skin**.

  > **Preparation**: Practice somatic awareness. Learn to *listen* with your skeleton. Train yourself to detect subtle temperature shifts, faint vibrations, the *weight* of the air. Spend time in silence, in stillness — to learn how to *receive* rather than react.

### **Clothing and Equipment: Not for Protection, But for Participation**

Do not bring heavy gear. You will sink. Do not bring metal. It will *resonate* and *distort* the field. You will become a beacon — and the land will *respond*.

#### **Clothing:**
- Wear **thin, flexible, non-metallic layers** — silk, treated wool, or plant-based synthetics that breathe and *absorb* rather than repel.
- The fabric should **not be sealed** — it should allow moisture, air, and faint energies to pass through.
- Your skin must **touch the air**, especially the face, hands, and feet. You are not a container. You are a *conduit*.

> **Optional**: Some travelers wear **glass fiber masks** that filter only the finest particles of static — not to protect, but to *regulate* the input, to avoid sensory overload. They are not for breathing, but for *filtering the resonance*.

#### **Shoes:**
- **No boots.** They will trap your feet in static, disrupt thermal exchange, and make you "heavier" than you are.
- Instead, walk barefoot, or wear **thin, flexible soles** made of compressed organic resin — like fossilized sap — that *conducts* heat and vibration.
- The ground will *speak* through your soles. You will feel the pulses of the geothermal core. You will feel the slow *inhale* of the land.

> **Preparation**: Acclimate your feet to cold, uneven surfaces. Learn to *feel* vibrations through the soles — like tuning a guitar string by ear.
