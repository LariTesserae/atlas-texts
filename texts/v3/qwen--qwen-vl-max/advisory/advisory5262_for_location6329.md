### **1. Preparation: The Body as a Guest**

You are not entering a cave.  
You are walking across the **floor of an organ** — perhaps the **interior of a dormant titan**, or the **interior of an entire planetary body**, not alive in the human sense, but **alive in the way dreams are alive: vast, slow, rhythmic, and unbound by death**.

#### **Biological Readiness**

- **Footwear**: Choose soft, **moldable soles** — leather or bio-polymer that conforms to the spongy, root-laced ground. This is not for traction, but for **sensory attunement**. You must feel the ground. The **vibrations** matter. The **warmth** matters. The **texture** matters. Hard boots will isolate you — and isolation is dangerous here. These are not your feet; they are **antennae**.
  
- **Clothing**: Wear **loose, breathable, non-metallic fabrics** — natural fibers like wool, cotton, or mycelium-silk (if available). Avoid synthetic materials — they trap static, which may **interfere with local electromagnetic fields**. The air is thick with low-frequency pulses; conductive materials could **act as antennae**, amplifying discomfort, or worse, **triggering unintended responses** from the environment.

- **Skin Exposure**: Allow some **skin to be uncovered** — not for pleasure, but for **reception**. This place **reads your body**. Not through sight, but through **thermal signatures, moisture, electrical fields**. The fungus may sense your breath, your heartbeat, the way your blood flows. Let it *know*. Be **present**, not hidden.

- **Water & Hydration**: Carry **only still, filtered water** — no carbonation, no metal bottles. The air is 1.2/3 moisture — **humid, yet not wet**. You will **not sweat**, but your **respiratory system will absorb** traces of organic particles, of fungal spores, of subtle ions. Do not drink the water from the pools — they are **not for consumption**, but for **exchange**. The liquid here is **alive**, and may **integrate you**.

### **2. Sensory Preparation: Re-Wiring Perception**

This world operates on **non-linear time**, **bioluminescent rhythms**, and **subsonic resonance**.

#### **Vision**
- The light is **0.8/3**, barely enough to see. But your eyes will **adapt over hours**, not minutes. You will not see clearly — you will **perceive in suggestion**. Shapes will form and dissolve. You may see **ghosts** — **forms that breathe, not of flesh, but of light and shadow**. These are not hallucinations. They are **the interface between biology and memory**, between the chamber and its own past.

- **Bring a bioluminescent eye mask** — no lights in the conventional sense. If you *must* illuminate, use **phosphorescent paint** or a **crystalline lens that glows faintly**, mimicking the fungal pulse. **Avoid artificial light**, even dim. It will **disorient** the environment — and the environment may **disorient you in return**.

#### **Hearing**
- Sound is **0.2/3** — near-silence. You will hear:
  - The **shush** of air through root-tunnels.
  - The **drip** of condensation that never falls (it *hovers*).
  - The **pulsing** of fungi — a slow, sub-audible throb that may **vibrate in your molars**.
  - The **click** of a spore pod when touched.
  - The **sigh** from deep below — a sound like a mountain breathing.

- But **you will not hear your own voice** clearly. Echoes do not form; they **dissolve into the dampness**. Speaking can be **misinterpreted** — the creatures may **receive your tone as a signal**, a **vibration to decode**. Speak **slowly**, **softly**, **only when necessary**.

- Bring **a resonant crystal** — one that can **absorb and gently emit sound**. It will **act as a translator**, not of words, but of **intent**. Tapping it once may be a greeting. Tapping it twice, a warning.

#### **Smell & Taste**
- The air tastes **metallic, sweet, like old blood and sour fruit** — a **scent of deep time**. This is not a smell; it is **a memory**. The fungus may be **releasing biochemical traces of its own history**. Inhale softly, but **do not hold your breath**. The air is not toxic, but **overstimulating**. The taste may linger — it may **change your metabolism**.

- You may **taste light** — a faint, blue-sweet flavor when you look at glowing patches. That is not illusion. The **bioluminescence emits subtle compounds** that affect the taste buds. Do not resist it. **Let the air enter you** — as a guest, not a conqueror.

- **Time**: Do not measure time. **Hourglasses are irrelevant**. The chamber breathes: **warm → cool**, **glow → dim**, **pulse → pause**. **One cycle may be 12 hours, or 47 days**. Your biological clock will **drift**. Do not resist.
