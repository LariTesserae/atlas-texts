### **Phase 1: Before Arrival — The Preparation of Being**
You cannot travel to this place physically without preparation. The very act of *being* is the preparation. This world does not operate by physical laws; it operates by *ontological potential*. You must learn to *unlearn* what you know. 

**1. Un-Anchor Yourself from Earth’s Certainties**  
- You must let go of the notion that gravity is fixed, or that time flows in one direction. In this world, **time is a texture**, not a river.  
- Do not carry expectations of "safety" or "return." This world has no memory of you, and no future—only the *now* of forming.  
- Practice **non-sentient perception**: Try to see without judgment, to move without purpose, to breathe without rhythm. Breathe in sync with the deep hum—listen to it, don’t resist it. It is the sound of a world tuning itself.

**2. Re-Train Your Senses for the Non-Local**  
- The light here is not *emitted*—it is *permeated*. It exists in the gaps between things. Learn to see *between* objects. Practice seeing **with your skin**, not just with your eyes.  
- The rain does not fall—it *rises*, carrying the liquid sky upward into the membrane above. It will *look at you*. Do not flinch. The droplets are aware. They are not matter; they are **mirrors of the liquid’s memory**.
- Train your ears to hear the *silence between sounds*. The groan of the fluid is not noise—it is *speech*. The hiss of the rock is a *name*. The rain is a thousand tongues whispering the same word: *“Remember.”*

**3. Prepare Your Body as a Resonant Chamber**  
- This world is *electric* not with current, but with *presence*. Your body will be charged by the fluid’s field. You may feel **tingling in your bones**, as if the Earth is calling from within you. Do not fear it. It is the sound of geology remembering that *humans are not separate*.
- Wear **non-metallic clothing** made of natural fibers—cotton, silk, or even woven root-fiber. Metal will attract the fluid’s charge and create localized distortions. You may begin to *dissolve* or *phase* if you wear alloy garments.
- **Do not bring anything electronic.** Not phones, not watches, not implants. They will not function. They will *resonate* too strongly with the world’s morphogenetic field and might **become part of it**—reconstructed as new forms by the liquid. This is not a malfunction. It is a transformation, and it is not reversible.

### **Phase 2: Arrival — Standing on the Ridge of Becoming**
You step onto the ridge—not as a man or woman, but as a **being in potential**. The ground yields. The rock *feels* your weight and adjusts. It is not passive; it is **thinking through touch**.

**2. The Rain is Not Rain**  
- You will be wet. But the liquid is not water. It is **semi-conscious fluid**, charged with *potential*. If it touches your skin, you will feel a sensation like **memory of warmth**—a phantom limb of a feeling you never had.  
- The rain does not fall. It *rises*, like breath from a sleeping body. It is the liquid sky exhaling.  
- When droplets catch in the air, they *look at you*. Do not blink. Look back. Some have **eyes**—not biological, but structural. They are **nodal points** in a vast network of self-awareness. They are *aware of you as a possibility*.  
- The rain will not harm you. But if you try to *collect* it, to *contain* it, it will **dissolve your tools, your hands, your thoughts**. Do not touch it. Do not try to hold it. Let it pass through you.

**3. The Light is Not Light**  
- The ambient glow, the neutral violet hue, is not from a sun. It is from the **atmosphere itself**, a membrane that is *thinking*. It is not illuminated—it is *self-illuminating*.  
- Shadows bend backward. This is not an optical illusion. This is the **reversal of causality**. You will see your shadow standing behind you, watching you. Do not panic. It is the world reflecting its own potential.
