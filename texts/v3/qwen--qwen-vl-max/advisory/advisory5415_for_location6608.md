### **I. Physical Preparation: Reconfiguring Your Body for the Living Architecture**

#### **1. Attire: Wear No Synthetics**
- **What to wear**: Plant-based fibers — hemp, ramie, or fungal mycelium weaves (if available from a biophilic colony). The environment is **not host-agnostic**. Synthetic fibers will be detected as "foreign," triggering localized chemical responses in the surrounding organisms.
- **Why**: This city recognizes substances on a molecular level. Polymers may be ingested by fungal networks, causing subtle but persistent inflammation in the walls. Your clothes will be "read" as part of the ecosystem — and if they are unwelcome, the city may *reclaim* them — slowly, inexorably.
- **Recommendation**: Wear layered, breathable garments with micro-perforations to allow **mycelial exchange**. A thin outer layer should be made from lichened leather — not dead hide, but **living skin** grown on the surface of a symbiotic fungus.

> *"Here, even your clothes must breathe — and be breathed."*

#### **2. Respiratory Gear: Don’t Breathe the Air — *Join* It**
- The air is thick with **aerosolized spores**, fungal hyphae, and bio-luminescent particles. Breathing it is not only possible — it is **necessary**.
- **Standard gas masks are useless**. They are a declaration of separation, and the city is built on **connection**.
- **Recommended**: Use a **pulmonary interface** — a soft, sponge-like mask grown from **respiratory symbionts**. It has the texture of wet velvet, breathes with your rhythm, and allows **microbial exchange**.
  - It does not filter. It **cohabits**.
  - It will be slowly colonized by the local fauna and may begin to glow faintly after a few days.
  - It is not meant to *protect* you — it is meant to **introduce you**.

> *"You do not breathe this air — you participate in its breath."*

#### **3. Footwear: Walk Barely. Wear the Land**
- Conventional shoes will **disturb the surface**. The ground is not earth. It is a **living membrane** — a fusion of mycelium, petrified bark, root networks, and slowly calcifying exoskeletons of extinct architects.
- **Do not wear boots**. They compress, they resist, they insulate.
- **Use**: Soles made of **giant, slow-growing lichen** or **keratin-embedded bio-filaments**. These soles will adapt, grow slightly softer, and gradually **merge with the floor**.
- **Warning**: If you walk too much, too fast, the ground will **push back**. Not with pain — with **sensory overload**: you'll feel the hum of the canopy in your feet, the expansion of hyphae beneath your arches, the gentle pulse of a deep-bodied creature shifting weight below a nearby column.
- **Goal**: Move like a **part of the structure**, not a visitor stepping on it.

> *"You do not step on this city. You step *within* it."*

### **II. Sensory Re-Calibration: How to Perceive What Is Not Designed for You**

#### **1. Vision: The Light Is Not Made for Eyes**
- There is **no "normal" light** here. The glow is not from the sun. It is **bioluminescent**, originating from the **internal organs** of the great jellyfish-architects.
- Their rhythm pulses between **2.7 and 4.1 seconds** — a beat too slow to register as rhythm, too constant to ignore.
- The **yellow-hued softness** is not illumination — it is **sustenance**. Light here is **a nutrient**, a language, a **metabolic signal**.
- **Do not stare**. Your eyes will **adapt**, but they will also be **influenced**. After extended exposure, you may begin to **see the architecture as a nervous system**, with veins of light pulsing through stone and fungus, and your own veins will **resonate**.
- **Recommendation**: Carry **tinted lenses** — not to block, but to **sync**.
  - The lenses should be tuned to **4.1-second pulsing**, to harmonize with the city’s breath.
  - They will feel warm, faintly alive, and may **vibrate** when the city is “awake.”

> *"You do not look at this world. You are illuminated by its dream."*

#### **2. Hearing: The Deafening Is the Music**
- The sound is **not noise**. It is **resonance**.
- The hum is the **collective breathing** of the biosphere. The crackle is **mycelial expansion**. The chittering is **information transfer** between winged fauna — a language of touch, pressure, and infrared.
- **Do not seek silence**. There is none.
- **Recommended**: Wear **bio-acoustic implants** (if permitted) — small, organic discs behind the ear that **decode** the hum into something your brain can interpret as music, or as **mood**, or as **warning**.
- They will grow with you, and eventually become **permanently fused** to your auditory nerve.
- **Warning**: The sounds **change over time**. A tone you thought was a lullaby may become a **warning call** when the city senses a disruption — such as a visitor who fails to blend.

> *"You don’t hear this city — you become part of its song."*

#### **3. Touch: The Walls Are Not Solid**
- Touch the wall. It is **warm**. It is **pulsing**. It is **alive**.
- The masonry is not inert. It is **responsive**. It feels your presence — not through consciousness, but through **electrochemical gradients**, **pressure waves**, **temperature shifts**.
- Touch is not permission. It is **invitation**.
- **Recommended**: Touch **gently**. Place your palm and **hold**. You may begin to feel **slow pulses**, like a heartbeat 100 meters away. You may feel **tendrils** of mycelium brushing your skin. This is **not attack** — it is **recognition**.
- **Do not resist**. Resistance will cause localized **contraction** — the wall may **close** around your hand, not to harm, but to **test integration**.
- If you **remain still**, the wall may **accept you** — and your hand might **become part of it**.

> *"To touch is to become."*
