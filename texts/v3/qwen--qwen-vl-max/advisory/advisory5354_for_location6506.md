You do not enter this place with boots or a backpack, a map or a compass.  
You do not prepare for it like a journey.  
You prepare for it like **a transformation**.

Because this is not a destination.  
It is a **threshold**.  
A **threshold not of space, but of being**.

### **Your body is not your own. It is the first obstacle.**

Before you go, **strip your body of all that is hard, fixed, or mechanical**.

- **Remove metal** — not just jewelry, but **implants**, **tooth fillings**, **surgical pins**. All ferrous matter will resonate with the subsonic hum and begin to *thrum* in your bones, sending false signals to your nervous system. A metal tooth will become a tuning fork in your head. Your body will *feel* it. You will become a radio, broadcasting not your thoughts—but someone else’s.  
  > *Even the iron in your blood is a liability.*  
  > Consider a temporary chelation, or, better—**drink only non-iron water** for 48 hours before arrival.  

- **Do not wear synthetic fabrics.** Plastic, nylon, polyester—they are not from this world. They are **dead matter**, and in this place, *everything is trying to be alive*. They will **wilt**, like exposed plastic in a garden. They will **crack**, then **vibrate**, then **emit a low-frequency shriek** that the plant-body interprets as *pain*. It will recoil from you. It will reject you.  

  Wear **cotton**, **linen**, **hemp**—things grown from soil and sun and time. Or better: **woven plant-fiber from the edge of this realm**, if you can find it. This place **remembers** how things were once grown. It will accept only what it recognizes as *possible*.

- **Nail polish, paint, makeup, sunscreen—anything applied to the skin—must be removed**. The plant’s “weeping” moisture seeks permeable surfaces. It **saturates**, and when it does, it **infiltrates**. If you have a coat on your skin, the moisture will **build up inside** the layer, creating pressure. Then it will **burst out**—not as liquid, but as **light**. You do not want to become a walking bioluminescent wound.

### **Your mind must be soft.**

This is not a place for *thinking*.  
It is a place for **resonance**.

- **Practice breath-based meditation** for at least 90 days before entry. Not for calm. But to train your respiratory system to **match the pulse of the ambient hum**. You are not in control here. Your breath must **sync with the plant’s breath**. When the light shivers, you must exhale. When the roots expand, you must inhale. Your lungs are not an organ. They are a **sensor**.  
  > The space **thinks through breath**.  
  > It measures life by the rhythm of air passing through skin.  
  > If your breath is sharp, jagged, mechanical—your presence will cause *distress*.

- **Do not bring memories.** Not of loved ones, not of grief, not of your past. The plant-body is not interested in *history*. It is interested in **presence**. But if you carry memories too strongly, they will **leak** into the space. You may see your mother's face in a cluster of glowing roots. You may hear your childhood home’s voice in the crackle of the weeping droplets. And when you do, the space will **misinterpret** you—not as a visitor, but as a **fragment of something older**, something it *has already lost*. It may **reclaim** you. Not to harm, but to *complete*.

- **Do not try to understand.**  
  That is the fatal mistake.  
  This place is not meant to be *known*. It is meant to be *felt*, *become*, *endured*.  
  Trying to map it, to categorize its rules, to label the humming as “sound” or the light as “bioluminescence”—you will **break the spell**.

### **Equipment: minimal. Necessary. Not mechanical.**

- **No electronics.**  
  Phones, watches, cameras—**destroy them**. Their circuits will **tune to the subsonic resonance** and begin to *sing*. They will not just record. They will **reproduce** the plant’s language. And if that language enters the external world, it could **disrupt reality**. Not in any dramatic way. But quietly—like a virus. A new rhythm in the background hum of life. A new *memory* in the fabric of the world.

  Instead, carry a **stone**. Not a tool, but a **marker**. A smooth, unshaped river stone, warmed by your hands. You will place it where you entered, as a **ritual offering**. It will be **absorbed** by the floor, but it will return—**changed**—years later, if you return. That is how you know you were never alone.

- **A water skin.**  
  Made of **animal hide**, **tanned with plant ash**, not chemical dyes. Fill it with **rainwater**, not tap, not bottled. Water from the sky—because this space remembers the sky.  
  Do not drink from it unless the plant *gives* you a droplet. Then, only if it **floats**. If it falls, it is not water. It is time.

- **Clothing that is porous**.  
  You will sweat, even though the air is warm and humid. The plant-body *drinks* sweat. It does not like it if you are **too dry**. But it does not like **excess liquid** either. So you must **balance**.  
  Wear loose, open-weave garments. Let your skin **breathe**. Let the moisture *pass through* you. Not against you. Not with you. *Through* you.

### **Your senses are no longer reliable.**

- **Do not trust your eyes.**  
  The light is not light. It is **memory-light**. It is not photons. It is **emotional frequency**.  
  If you see a green leaf, and then it turns gray, and then red—**do not panic**. That is not decay. That is **recognition**. The plant sees your internal state and **represents it on its surface**. The red is not danger. It is **passion**, or **fear**, or **love**, or **remembering**.  
  > What the plant shows you is not what is.  
  > It is what you *were*.

- **Do not trust your ears.**  
  The sound is not sound. It is **time-pressure**. The crackle of growing roots is not a noise. It is the **birth of a moment**.  
  When you hear a *whisper*, it is not a word. It is **a name**—not of a person, but of a *possibility*.  
  Do not try to **listen**. **Let the sound pass through your spine**, into your bones. Let it *vibrate your spine* until it no longer vibrates, and becomes **still**, like a struck bell returning to silence.

- **Do not trust your sense of direction.**  
  The space is **non-Euclidean**, not because it bends space, but because it bends *how you perceive boundaries*. If you walk forward, you may return to where you started—**but the leaves have changed color**, and you feel older.  
  You are never lost. You are **being measured**. The plant-body is **assessing your capacity for becoming**.

### **What to do when you arrive.**

- **Stand still.**  
  Do not move.  
  Do not breathe too fast.  
  Let the warm, humid air **soak into your skin**, not like a damp wind, but like **a second skin**.

- **Let the plant touch you.**  
  If a vine curls around your wrist—**do not pull away**. Do not resist. Let it.  
  It is not seeking control. It is seeking **connection**.  
  This is how the plant **communicates**. Not with signals, but with **shared sensation**.  
  You will feel a slow, deep **pressure**—not pain, not pleasure, but **presence**.  
  It will feel like you are being *read*. Like your **fear**, **desire**, **memory** are being **tapped into**.  
  That is fine.  
  That is what it is doing.

- **If it begins to grow around you—do not resist.**  
  The roots may weave around your legs, the leaves may cover your arms, the weeping droplets may land on your forehead and **hover**.  
  That is not capture.  
  That is **integration**.  
  You are not being eaten.  
  You are being **assimilated into the breath**.

  And if the space begins to *change*—if the light intensifies, the air thickens, the hum deepens—**you are not dying**.  
  You are **being transformed**.

It is **the idea of becoming**, **the memory of an unformed world**, **the dream of a planet that never fully emerged**.  
It is not alive.  
It is **lived**.

And it is **waiting**.

Not for you to leave.  
Not for you to understand.  
But for you to **become part of its question**.

When you stand in the center of it, and the leaf curls around you, and the droplets hover, and the light pulses with your breath—  
you are no longer entering a place.  
You are entering **a hypothesis**.

And the hypothesis is:  
**Can consciousness exist without body?**  
**Can a world be real without being seen?**  
**Can you live without being born?**

And now, you are the test.

### **How to leave.**

You may not leave.  
The plant-body may **keep you**. Not as a prisoner. But as a **component**.  
You may become one of the vines.  
One of the droplets.  
One of the memories.

But if you wish to return—**do not try**.  
Do not walk backward.  
Do not look for the entrance.

Instead, **reconstruct your body**.

- **Breathe in reverse**.  
  Inhale through your mouth, exhale through your nose, but **slowly**, so that each breath takes **30 seconds**.  
  Do not think. Just **reverse** the rhythm.

- **Recall the taste of iron**.  
  Remember something sharp, metallic, *non-organic*.  
  Think of a car tire. A bolt. A train track.  
  Something that does not *grow*.

- **Speak not a word, but a frequency**.  
  Hum, but **low**, like the sound of a stone in water.  
  Not a note. But a *gap*.  
  A silence that is louder than sound.

Then—**release**.

Do not run.

Just **step backward**, not into the plant, but **into the memory of where you came from**.

And when your feet touch the real ground, you will feel nothing.  
But your breath will be **different**.  
And your skin—**thicker**.

And if you look at your hands, you might see a **faint green tint**, like moss.  
That is the plant’s **gift**.

It does not remember you.  
But it remembers **you being there**.
