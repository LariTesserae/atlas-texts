Visiting this place—this **hollow beneath the skin of the world**, where time is a slow pulse and light is a memory—would require not just preparation, but a complete reorientation of the self. It is not a location one *enters* like a cave or a temple. It is a **state of being**, an **epistemic threshold**. To go there is to become a guest in a world that doesn’t care if you understand it, but insists you *feel* it.

Here is how an ordinary human multiverse traveler—someone not trained in xenobiology, not equipped with alien physics, not hardened by interstellar travel—should prepare. Not for survival. For **presence**. Because this world does not need to be conquered. It only needs to be **seen without violence**.

---

### **1. Abandon the Concept of "Exploration"**
You are not here to map, study, or possess. You are a **vessel of attention**, not a data collector. The moment you bring a sensor, a camera, or a notebook, you disrupt the equilibrium. The place **knows** when you're measuring it. It resists artificial boundaries.

Instead, prepare your mind for **reception**—like a tuning fork that resonates only when struck softly. You will not *see* everything. You will *feel* more.

> **Practice**: Before arrival, spend a week in silence. No music. No screens. Each morning and evening, meditate with a single question: *"What would it feel like to be still for a century?"* Let the urgency in your bones recede.

---

### **2. Clothing: Wear the Earth**
Conventional clothing will attract attention—its fibers, dyes, synthetic fibers, even its structure, will seem like an intrusion. This world **discriminates** between what belongs and what does not. Your skin, even, may be perceived as foreign if it's too dry, too hot, or too fast-breathing.

> **Recommended attire**:
- A single, seamless layer of **woven silk and wool**, dyed with natural minerals (iron oxide for red, copper for green), hand-spun and untreated.
- No metal fasteners. Sew buttons of **smooth stone** or **fused clay**.
- Shoes: None. Go barefoot, but protect your feet with **thin, biodegradable soles** made from compressed moss and fungus (can be obtained at the periphery of this reality).
- Your body must be **warm, dry, and still**. Shower with only water and salt—no soap. Let your skin breathe.

> **Why?** The fauna here respond to **thermal signatures** and **chemical emissions**. Your sweat, your breath, your blood pressure—these are signals. If you are anxious, they will sense it not as emotion, but as vibration.

---

### **3. Time: The Slowest Measure**
Your internal clock is irrelevant here. The light pulses every 48 hours—not 24. That means the **diurnal rhythm is doubled**. A “day” here is two of your days. Do not attempt to live by your usual schedule.

> **Adjustment Protocol**:
- Upon entry, **do not move for 12 hours**. Sit or lie still. Breathe deeply. Let your pulse fall into the rhythm of the moss hum.
- Do not eat for the first 6 hours. Once you do, keep meals small and simple: **mineral water, raw honey, and a leaf of the cobalt stalk** (if offered). Do not chew loudly.
- Sleep when the light dims—when the pulses slow to a soft glow. You will sleep in 6-hour cycles, not 8. You will dream in **slow motion**, as if time is thick.

---

### **4. Sound: Listen with the Bones**
You cannot hear all sounds here. But you can *feel* them. The hum of the rock, the creak of the ceiling, the vibration of the floor—these are not background noise. They are **communications**.

> **Preparation**:
- Before descending, practice **bone conduction**. Press your fingers into your jaw, your temples, and **listen** through the skull. You may feel a hum in your teeth when near a tuning fork or a waterfall. This is the same sensitivity you’ll need here.
- Do not speak. If you must communicate, **whisper with your hands**—gentle gestures, slow movements. The fauna will respond to **posture**, not sound.
- Avoid sudden motions. Every movement sends ripples through the gelatinous pools. A single step might cause a **moss colony to darken and recede**, as if hiding.

---

### **5. Tools: Carry Nothing That Remembers**
You will not need tools. But if you do bring something, it must not possess memory. Your phone, your knife, your flashlight—all have **embedded intent**. They are **artifacts of urgency**.

> **Only allowed**:
- A **stone cup**, polished flat. Use it to collect condensed moisture. Do not drink from it unless it emits a tone. That tone means the water is “singing”—safe for you.
- A **small, unmarked cloth sack** made from fungal threads. Use it to carry a single **glowing stalk**, if one is offered. But do not take it unless it *wants* to be carried.

> **Note**: The tools of the inhabitants are not *used*. They are **passed**. A shard of glass might be placed at the edge of a puddle by a glassfish-like creature. It will vibrate when the moss is “awake.” You can observe, but not pick it up. To take it is to **break the silence**.

---

### **6. The Vegetation: Do Not Touch**
The translucent stalks are not plants. They are **living memory banks**. Their glow is not energy—it's **emotional residue**. They remember pressure, sound, and heat.

> **Danger**: If you touch a stalk, it may **scream**—not in sound, but in **light**. A sudden burst of cobalt-white. This will **frighten** the creatures. The gelatinous pools will **thicken**, and the ceiling may **dull** for days.

> **Safety**: If a stalk bends toward you, **don’t react**. Just breathe slowly. It may be sensing your warmth, or your silence. It may be **curious** in a way that resembles empathy, but not emotion.

---

### **7. Fauna: Not Animals, Not People**
These are **slow-entities**. They move at the speed of stone grinding, of rain forming. They are not predators. They are not friends. They are **co-architects** of the place.

> **Behavior**:
- Observe the jellyfish-like beings as they glide across the floor. They leave **trails of shimmering slime** that last for decades. These trails are **memories of movement**. Do not step on them.
- Watch the flat disc creatures burrowing. They are **not eating**—they are **releasing**. They excrete a substance that makes the rock **slightly more porous**, allowing light to pass through. This is not digestion. It is **creation**.
- If a multi-limbed creature crawls over your leg, **do not flinch**. It is not attacking. It is **measuring**. Its light-emitting organs are **tuning your biometric signature**, like a tuning fork against a wall. It will move on when satisfied.

> **Interaction**: You may, if you are very still, **hold a moss colony** between your palms. But do not crush it. Let it **breathe**. After a while, it may pulse in **sync with your heartbeat**. This is not love. It is **resonance**.

---

### **8. The Hum**: It Is Not Your Mind
The ambient hum is real. It is not in your head. It is in the **air, the rock, the water**. It is the **sum of all slow chemical reactions**, all microbial breathing, all mineral shifts.

> **What it means**:
- It is the **heartbeat of the world**, not of a body, but of a **memory network**.
- It changes pitch slightly when a creature moves, when a stalk blooms, when the light shifts.
- If you sit long enough, you may **feel it in your teeth**, your spine, your **teeth**. It will make you sleepy, then **awake in a new way**.

> **Caution**: Do not try to sing it. It will not respond to your voice. It responds to **stillness**.

---

### **9. The Echo**: It Is Not a Ghost
That faint sound—footsteps, breath, a song—**is not from the past**. It is from **every present**. It is the **resonance of all visitors who ever came**, all life that ever existed here, all light that ever fell.

> **Do not search for its source**. It has no source. It is **the place itself**.
> You may hear it in **your dreams**, even after you leave. That is normal. It is **remembering you**.

---

### **10. What to Bring Home**
You cannot return with artifacts. The tools decay. The moss withers. The stalks **lose their light**.

But you **can bring**:
- A **memory**. Not a picture. Not a story. A **feeling**—the weight of stillness, the warmth of the rock, the taste of gelatinous water on your tongue.
- A **tone**. You may carry it in your bones. A low hum. A sound only you can hear. It may return as a dream, a song, a **shiver when sunlight falls**.
- A **changed way of being**. You will walk slower. You will speak less. You will **listen before you move**.

> This is **the true travel**. The Earth remembers. And so will you.

---

### **Final Warning**
Do not stay more than **72 hours**. After that, the place may **begin to incorporate you**. Your breath may sync with the moss. Your heartbeat may pulse with the light. You may **forget your name**.
And if you forget, it may be **too late**.

Because this world **does not release** what it has taken in.

> It only **transforms**—into silence, into light, into memory.

And sometimes, if you listen very closely—  
you’ll hear your own voice, long after you’ve left,  
singing in a language you never learned.  

And it will sound like **home**.
