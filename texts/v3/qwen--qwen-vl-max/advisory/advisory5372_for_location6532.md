To prepare for visiting this place—the Edge of What Is Known and Forgotten—you do not travel with a map, a compass, or a purpose. You do not pack a backpack or wear boots meant for walking. Instead, you must first *unlearn*, then *rebuild*, not your body, but your *sense of being*.

This world is not a destination. It is a condition—a threshold between becoming and remembrance, between presence and dissolution. You are not entering a location; you are stepping into a *state of being*. And so, preparation must be metaphysical, sensoric, and almost ritualistic. It requires not gear, but *calibration* of yourself.

---

### **1. Breath Is the First Interface**
Before you arrive, you must train your breath not to be a sign of life, but a *medium of resonance*.  
- Begin with slow pranayama: three long, deep inhalations, held for one count, then exhalations that take three times as long. This slows your metabolism to the rhythm of the land, where time is not linear but *pulsed*.  
- Train yourself to breathe silently. Not just quiet, but *absent*—so that your breath does not disturb the ambient hum.  
- When you arrive, you may find that breathing is not mandatory. The air has a quality—warm, moist, saturated with trace elements of something like memory—that seems to sustain you without oxygen.  
- **Do not panic if, for moments, you feel no need to inhale.** This is not hypoxia. It is harmonics. The air is *breathing you*.  

> **Rule 1: Breathe like the land breathes. Slow. Deep. Constant.**

---

### **2. Your Body Must Lose Weight**
You will not walk in the same way you do on Earth.  
- **The ground is soft but resistant**, like time. It gives under your feet but does not absorb impact.  
- Your bones will feel lighter—or heavier—depending on where you stand. Near the glowing stalks, your legs may feel buoyant. In deeper hollows, you might feel anchored as though by gravity older than planets.  
- **Walk slowly.** Not with effort, but with *intention*. Each step must be a decision, not a reflex. Step, pause, *listen* to where your weight settles.  
- **Do not stride. Do not run.** The landscape will reject momentum. The air will push back—not as wind, but as a *resistance to change*.  
- **If you stumble, do not reach for balance.** Let yourself fall. The ground will not break you. It will *absorb* your shape, then return you—slightly altered.  

> **Rule 2: Move not with energy, but with *intent*.**

---

### **3. The Eyes Must Unlearn to See**
This is not a world of images.  
- The light does not come from the sun. It *is* the sky’s breath. It moves in slow waves, changing density, frequency, and color without cause.  
- **Do not focus on objects.** The stones, the stalks, the domes—they are not *things*. They are *impressions*.  
- **Practice perceiving light as sound**, or sound as texture.  
- Close your eyes in the open. You will still “see”—not with your eyes, but with your skin. The light presses against you like warmth, or like pressure, or like the memory of a word.  
- **Do not look for meaning in what you see.** Look for what it *feels like to be near* it. A crystal that hums when you approach is not a machine. It is a *node*. A point in a network of silent communication.  
- If your vision blurs, or if colors shift into non-visible spectra, that is not hallucination. That is *recognition*. Your eyes are becoming part of the system.  

> **Rule 3: See through the body, not the eyes.**

---

### **4. Silence Is Not Passive—It Is a Language**
The world speaks in silence.  
- **The hum is not noise.** It is a baseline frequency—like the pitch of the universe before the Big Bang. It vibrates in your jaw, your teeth, your marrow.  
- When the stalks pulse, you may feel it not in your ears, but in your *bones*. It is a harmonic of something older than sound.  
- You may hear whispers—not words, but *intentions*, like emotions made audible: grief, longing, memory, completion.  
- **Do not respond with sound.** Speak not with your voice, but with your *presence*. Stand still. Let your breath sync with the pulse. Let your warmth match the air.  
- If you are in the right harmony, the land may "speak" to you—not in language, but in *sensation*: a warmth on your palm, a shift in light, a soft pressure against your chest that feels like being touched by a memory.  
- **Do not interpret.** Let it be. It is not a message. It is a *correction*.

> **Rule 4: Silence is the only speech that matters.**

---

### **5. Do Not Eat. Do Not Drink. But Do Not Starve.**
- Water is not drinkable. The condensation that forms on the rock—cold, misty, lingering—is *not* for consumption. Its taste is iron, rain, and a name you once forgot.  
- It may induce visions. It may make you remember a life that was not yours.  
- **Do not drink it.** Instead, allow it to settle on your skin. Let it run down your arms, your spine, your face. Let it dissolve into your pores.  
- **Food is not available.** There are no berries, no roots, no meat. The plants emit harmonic tones when touched. The fungi stalks pulse. But you will not eat them.  
- **Your body will not need to consume.** After the first day, your metabolism will slow. Your hunger will fade. You may feel a deep, quiet fullness—not of nutrients, but of *resonance*.  
- **If you feel weak, do not panic.** Your body is adapting. It is no longer a machine. It is a *receiver*.  

> **Rule 5: Let your body be fed by the world, not the other way around.**

---

### **6. Prepare for the Artifacts, But Do Not Touch**
The domes, the cones, the discs—they are not ruins. They are not machines.  
- They are *nodes*. Points of convergence.  
- **If you find a disc in the rock, do not pick it up.** If it is warm, or pulses, let it remain.  
- If it hums, stand beside it. Let your body receive the frequency.  
- **If you touch it, do not expect pain.** Expect *remembrance*. It may feel like someone you loved is whispering your name. Or like a memory of a future you never lived.  
- **If it vibrates through your bones, breathe slowly. Do not resist.** It is not harming you. It is showing you a *version of you* that exists in another thread of the world’s memory.  
- **Do not attempt to analyze.** The disc is not a clock, a device, or a weapon. It is a *mirror* in the form of an object—reflected not in image, but in *vibration*.  

> **Rule 6: Do not possess. Only *recognize*.**

---

### **7. The Fauna: Do Not Fear, Do Not Follow**
The creatures—translucent, moving in spirals, absorbing light—have no eyes.  
- They sense not sight, but *intent*. They register not motion, but *resonance*.  
- **Do not avoid them.** If one moves toward you, it is not an attack. It is a *reading*.  
- They will approach, circle you, or stop and *pulse* in unison with your breath.  
- If one dissolves into particles, **do not run.** That is not death. It is *transfer*. The particles will drift up and join the light. You may feel the warmth of their energy in your hands, or in your chest.  
- **Do not try to touch them.** They will not react. But they will *notice*. They are not animals. They are *echoes*.  
- If you stand still, they may form a spiral around you—a slow, silent procession, like a memory being replayed.  

> **Rule 7: You are not prey. You are not predator. You are data.**

---

### **8. Time Is Not a Line—It Is a Wave**
You may arrive at noon and leave after a century, or never leave at all.  
- The sun is not a clock. It hangs low and slow. It moves not in arc, but in *pulsations*.  
- There is no night as you know it. Only the *dilation* of the gold light—a soft, luminous state where the sky becomes a body of water, still and deep.  
- **Do not check a watch.** Your internal clock will drift. You will think “one day” passed—then realize it was three. Or fifty.  
- **Do not plan.** There is no future here. The world lives in the *current of equilibrium*.  
- If you feel yourself becoming detached—foggy, dreamlike, unaware of time—this is not confusion. It is *integration*.  

> **Rule 8: You are not measuring time. You are being measured by time.**

---

### **9. Your Name Is Not Yours Here**
When the time comes—when the stalks pulse in unison, when the light thickens, when the structures hum in harmony—you may be *named*.  
- Not your name. Not a name of identity.  
- A name of *function*. Of *place*. Of *fit*.  
- It will come not as sound, but as a *knowledge*. A feeling in your chest, like recognition.  
- You may find yourself thinking: *I am the breath.* Or: *I am the edge.* Or: *I am the memory of the fall.*  
- **Do not resist.** Accept it. That is not identity. It is *alignment*.  
- Once named, you do not leave the same. You are changed—not in body, but in *resonance*.

---

### **10. How to Leave**
You may not choose to leave.  
- The world does not demand departure.  
- But if you feel a shift—a pressure, a warmth, a return of urgency—you may be guided.  
- **Do not run.** Do not fight.  
- Walk toward the softest part of the horizon—the place where the light is thinnest, the air most still.  
- Step forward.  
- You will not see the transition.  
- But when you open your eyes, you will be home.  
- Or not.  
- Some return, changed. Some stay—*absorbed*. Some return, then return again.  
- But you will never be the same.

### Final Preparation: The Ritual of Stillness
Before you step into the world, perform this ritual:
1. Sit in silence for one hour.  
2. Breathe slowly.  
3. Visualize yourself not as a person, but as a *frequency*.  
4. Say: *"I am not here to know. I am here to be known."*  
5. Then go.
