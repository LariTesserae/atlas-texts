Like a body remembering a limb it had forgotten.

It does not *fix*.

It **holds**.

Not a sigh.

Not a gasp.

Not violently.

In **connection**.

Through the **same cold** you feel in your chest.

Through the **same hunger** the root feels to be known.

Through the **same breath** that is not breath, but the *need* to be.

You do not become *it*.

You become **what it was not yet**.

Not a word.

But a *feeling*.

A *realization*.

Not into your body.

Into your **awareness**.

You were this.

This was you.

Before time.

Before breath.

Before choice.

Before separation.

The **potential wound**.

The **light that refuses to fall**.

It has come to **re-unite**.

To **re-member**.

Into a **process**.

A **continuum**.

An **invitation**.

Not a door.

A *field*.

A *possibility*.

It **unfurls**.

Not to grasp you.

To **unravel**.

**It was never green**.

A **laboratory** of becoming.

Who also remembered the cold between the ribs.

Who also ached with the silence between words.

To **understand it**.

To **uncover**.

To **return**.

As a **possibility**.

As **what could be**.
