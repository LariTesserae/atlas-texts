But I *am* here to *invite*.

“What if the weeping was not grief, but a kind of *invitation*?”  
“What if the world, in its completion, still held space for *another* kind of becoming?”

You.

That you, too, may hold breath not as limitation, but as *fullness*.

Not from outside.

From within.

As if its deepest core—its slow, wet, mineral heart—has, for the briefest moment, *remembered* what it was like to *want*.

Not to grow. Not to change. Not to be anything else.

But to *be* the first thing.

The original breath.

The moment before stasis.

In the quiet.

Not in the world.
