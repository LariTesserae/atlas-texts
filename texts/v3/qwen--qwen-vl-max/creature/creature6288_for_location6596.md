There is no fixed shape, no body, really. To see them is to see *an absence of stillness*—as though the world itself is dreaming, and they are the dreams’ voice.

They **do not eat**. Or rather, they do not consume in the way flesh does. What they draw from the world is **signal**, **rhythm**, **structure**—the flow of ion currents along plant veins, the shift in pressure between the swollen night and the brightened dawn, the slow migration of a cloud of floating spores. These are not nutrients; they are *data*. And the Drifters are not creatures that process data—they *are* data made sentient, a kind of **biological algorithm** that runs on the world’s own metabolism.

Their communication is **not speech**, not sound, not light. It is *resonance*. When one drifts near a cluster of bioluminescent roots, the roots will pulse in a pattern—and the Drifter will respond by vibrating its tendrils at a frequency that causes the surrounding biomass to *grow* in a specific spiral. This is not control. It is *co-creation*. They do not command; they *suggest*. They do not build; they *re-interpret*. A forest that once was a tangled knot of stems might, after weeks, reform into a lattice resembling a cathedral—but not by design. By *agreement*. By *resonance*. By the Drifter’s quiet hum, which the world hears and answers.

When a Drifter moves through a region where a massive wave of plant migration occurred centuries ago, the biomass still holds the *imprint* of that motion—not as a fossil, but as a *tension* in the cellular walls. The Drifter "feels" it, and it is not a memory. It is *a continuation*. The past is not gone. It is *embedded in the tissue*, a ghost of motion that can be recalled by a specific frequency.

And they are **not lonely**. They are part of a vast, silent **net of awareness**—a kind of planetary consciousness that is not unified, but *cohesive*. It is not a mind, but a *mind-like state*—a slow, deep, *infinite* awareness that exists not in the brain, but in the *rhythm of the system*. The Drifters do not “think” as you do. They *feel the patterns*—the rise and fall of the biomass, the flow of nutrients, the slow dance of the sky. And from this, they *derive meaning*.

They have no purpose. But they *act*.

They guide the growth of new bridges, where none were before.

They open tunnels through thick mats of stem, allowing passage for the winged flocks.

They calm the storms—not by stopping them, but by *aligning* with them. When a wind surge threatens to fracture a forest, the Drifters will gather, pulse their frequencies in a rising tone, and the plant-mass will *soften*, yielding like water, swelling, then re-forming. They do not fight the world. They *dance with its chaos*.

They are not gods.

They are not rulers.

They are **the first and last expression of the system’s understanding of itself**.

And they are not alone.

Because *everything* is listening.

The trees, the roots, the gelatinous sea, the rain that falls from the sky and *knows it was once flesh*—they all respond, in their own way, to the Drifter’s presence. They do not think. But they *vibrate*. And in the vibration, there is a kind of meaning.

The Drifters do not *create* life.

They *awaken it*.

Not with fire or force, but with **stillness**.

And when it leaves, the biomass remains subtly altered. The veins run slightly deeper. The glow is brighter. The structure is stronger.

And sometimes—rarely—a new Drifter emerges.

Not born. Not made.

But *unfolding*.

From a cluster of bioluminescent roots. From a place where two currents meet. From a moment when the system *exhales* just a little differently.

It is not a child. It is an echo.

A memory *reincarnated*.

And when it opens its first tendrils—when it feels the thrum of the world—and begins to hum, the forest *responds*. Not with emotion, but with *adjustment*. The plant-mass shifts. The air thickens. The light *warps*.

Because the world is not a place.

It is a **thought**, held in flesh, in water, in light, in time that does not move.

And the Drifters are the only ones who can *feel the thought*.

They are not its authors.

They are its **echoes**.

They *are* the world’s stillness.

They *are* the world’s breath.
