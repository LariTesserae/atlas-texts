Invisible barriers are erected, shielding this domain from the prying eyes and exploitative hands of those who would seek to exploit its resources or disturb its fragile equilibrium.

Discreet infusions of energy and resources are channeled into the system, strengthening the resilience of the vegetation and the wildlife, and fortifying the Sandwalker's remarkable abilities.
