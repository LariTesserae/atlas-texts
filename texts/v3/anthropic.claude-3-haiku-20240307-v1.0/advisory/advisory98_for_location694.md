Preparing to visit this primordial, underwater world would require extensive planning and specialized equipment. As an ordinary human multiverse traveler, the key considerations would be:

Breathing and Pressurization:
- You would need a self-contained breathing apparatus, likely a rebreather system, to supply oxygen and regulate the immense pressure of the deep ocean environment.
- Decompression protocols would be critical to avoid the risks of barotrauma and decompression sickness when ascending from such depths.
- Your suit or capsule would need to be pressurized to match the ambient pressure, potentially requiring a rigid, heavily reinforced structure.

Mobility and Navigation:
- Propulsion in the water would likely involve some form of underwater propulsion system, perhaps thrusters or fins, to overcome the viscous resistance of the water.
- Navigating this vast, featureless seascape would require advanced sonar, imaging, and mapping systems to detect landmarks and avoid hazards.
- Tethers or guideline systems might be necessary to maintain orientation and safe return to entry/exit points.

Environmental Protection:
- Your suit or vehicle would need robust shielding against the crushing water pressure, the frigid temperatures, and potential chemical/biological hazards.
- Lighting systems would be crucial to illuminate the dimly lit environment and avoid unseen obstacles or predators.
- Specialized materials and coatings may be required to resist corrosion and biofouling from the alien ecosystem.

Scientific Instrumentation:
- Highly sensitive scientific equipment would be needed to study the unique geology, hydrology, and biology of this primal world.
- Sampling tools, imaging systems, and analytical instruments would allow you to collect and examine specimens and data.
- Robust data storage and transmission systems would be essential to record and share your findings.

Mental and Physical Preparation:
- The profound isolation, darkness, and stillness of this environment could be psychologically taxing, requiring careful mental preparation and potentially specialized life support systems.
- Rigorous physical training and conditioning would be necessary to withstand the physiological stresses of prolonged immersion in this extreme environment.

Ultimately, visiting this ancient, underwater realm would be an immense technical and logistical challenge, requiring a level of preparation and specialized equipment that would push the limits of human exploration. But for the intrepid multiverse traveler, the opportunity to witness this primordial world and uncover its hidden secrets would be a profound and transformative experience.
