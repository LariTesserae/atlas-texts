A sturdy, well-equipped submersible or small watercraft would be essential, capable of withstanding the powerful currents and waves. The remnants of the long-vanished civilization, as well as the strange and unknown lifeforms inhabiting the waters, would be of immense scientific interest, and you would need to be prepared to thoroughly document and study them.

Finally, it would be crucial to have a thorough understanding of the local environment and any potential hazards. The rugged, mineral-rich landscape, the powerful currents and tides, and the presence of unknown creatures and phenomena would all pose significant risks to an unprepared visitor. Extensive research and preparation would be necessary to minimize the dangers and ensure a safe and successful exploration of this primordial, alien world.
