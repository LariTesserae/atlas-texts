Preparing to visit the Frostbound Expanse would require significant forethought and careful planning. As an ordinary human multiverse traveler, you would need to equip yourself with the proper gear and supplies to withstand the harsh, unforgiving environment.

First and foremost, proper insulation and cold-weather gear would be essential. Heavy, well-insulated outerwear, including a hooded parka, thick gloves, and sturdy boots, would be necessary to protect against the biting winds and near-freezing temperatures. Thermal undergarments and multiple layers of clothing would also be crucial to maintain body heat.

Reliable sources of warmth, such as portable heat packs or a compact, fuel-efficient stove, would be necessary to prevent hypothermia during extended periods of exposure. Additionally, a resilient, well-insulated sleeping bag and perhaps even a small, lightweight tent could provide essential shelter from the elements.

Provisions would also need to be carefully considered. Dehydrated, high-calorie foods and plenty of water would be required, as the damp, mist-shrouded environment would make purifying water a constant challenge. In addition, a means of starting fires, such as waterproof matches or a flint and steel, could be invaluable for both warmth and cooking.

Given the unsettling, almost otherworldly nature of the Frostbound Expanse, a compass and detailed maps would be essential for navigation, as the landscape's distortions and the ever-present mist could easily disorient even the most seasoned explorer. A reliable source of light, such as a high-powered flashlight or headlamp, would also be crucial for navigating the treacherous ravines and crevasses.

Finally, it would be wise to consider some form of defensive or protective equipment, such as a sturdy walking staff or a non-lethal deterrent, as the unpredictable nature of the environment and the possibility of encountering unknown, potentially hostile entities cannot be overlooked.

Ultimately, visiting the Frostbound Expanse would require a level of preparedness and adaptability that few ordinary humans possess. It is a realm that demands respect and caution, for those who venture into its frozen, alien embrace do so at their own peril.
