- Ensure you are in good physical condition, as the challenging terrain and potential for extended periods of exertion can be demanding

- Familiarize yourself with basic survival skills, such as fire-making, shelter construction, and navigation, in case you become separated from your group or supplies
- Consider your personal comfort level with exploration in dark, confined spaces and the potential presence of unseen, possibly dangerous creatures

- Travel in a small, experienced group, as solo exploration of this labyrinth would be highly inadvisable
- Maintain constant communication with your group and establish clear protocols for emergencies or unexpected events
- Familiarize yourself with any known hazards or risks associated with the specific subterranean location you plan to visit
- Ensure you have a reliable means of contacting the surface world for assistance, should the need arise
- Respect the delicate balance of the underground ecosystem and minimize your impact on the environment
