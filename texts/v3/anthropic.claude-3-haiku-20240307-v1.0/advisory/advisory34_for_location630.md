Preparing to visit this extraordinary, otherworldly location would require extensive planning and specialized equipment. As an ordinary human multiverse traveler, you would need to take several crucial steps to ensure your safety and maximize your chances of a successful and enlightening expedition.

First and foremost, it is essential to obtain the proper protective gear and supplies. The environment you are about to enter is likely to be hostile and unpredictable, with atmospheric conditions, gravity, and even the fundamental laws of physics potentially differing from what you are accustomed to. A fully-sealed, self-contained hazmat suit would be a necessity, equipped with its own life support systems, temperature regulation, and shielding against any unfamiliar radiation or environmental hazards.

Alongside the protective suit, you would need to carry a comprehensive array of scientific and survival equipment. This could include portable sensors and scanners to analyze the atmosphere, geology, and biosphere; specialized tools for sample collection and preservation; and a reliable source of power, such as a compact fusion reactor or high-capacity battery pack. Additionally, you may need to bring your own food and water, as the local resources may be unsuitable or even potentially dangerous for human consumption.

Navigational and communication equipment would be crucial, as the vast, disorienting scale of this alien landscape could easily lead to getting lost or becoming isolated from any potential extraction or support teams. A high-powered, multi-spectrum communications array, capable of penetrating any interference or spatial distortions, would be essential, as would advanced mapping and geo-positioning systems to track your location and movements.

It would also be prudent to undergo extensive training and preparation before attempting to venture into this extraordinary realm. Familiarizing yourself with the theoretical and practical aspects of interdimensional and cross-universal travel, as well as developing expertise in fields such as xenobiology, exogeology, and advanced materials science, would all be valuable assets. Practicing survival and exploration techniques in simulated environments that mimic the conditions you are likely to encounter would also be highly recommended.

Finally, it is crucial to establish robust contingency plans and emergency protocols, in case of equipment failures, environmental hazards, or unexpected encounters with the local flora and fauna. Having a reliable extraction plan, as well as the means to quickly and safely return to your own universe, could mean the difference between a successful expedition and a catastrophic outcome.

Visiting this alien world would undoubtedly be a profound and transformative experience, but it would also require meticulous preparation, unwavering vigilance, and a willingness to embrace the unknown. Only by approaching this journey with the utmost care and respect for the inherent dangers and mysteries of the multiverse can an ordinary human traveler hope to safely explore and learn from this extraordinary realm.
