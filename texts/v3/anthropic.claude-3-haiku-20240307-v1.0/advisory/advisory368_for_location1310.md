Health and Safety:
- Respiratory protection - The thick, humid air and potential airborne contaminants may require a filtration mask or respirator.

- Decontamination protocols - Exposure to alien microbes or spores may necessitate rigorous cleaning and sterilization upon return.
- Medical kit and knowledge of local first aid - Injuries or illness could be difficult to manage in this remote environment.

Mindset and Preparation:
- Embrace the sense of wonder and discovery - This is a chance to immerse oneself in a truly alien landscape, so maintain an open, curious mindset.

- Develop a deep understanding of the local environment - Thorough research and preparation will help navigate this unfamiliar world safely and responsibly.
- Anticipate the psychological impact - The scale, sensory overload, and isolation of this place may be disorienting or unsettling, so be prepared to manage those effects.
