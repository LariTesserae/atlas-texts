Visiting this alien, high-pressure ocean environment would require extensive preparation and specialized equipment. As an ordinary human multiverse traveler, you would need to take the following precautions:

Diving Gear: The crushing weight of the water at these depths would require you to wear a highly specialized, pressure-resistant diving suit. This suit would need to be engineered to withstand the immense pressure of the environment, which can be hundreds or even thousands of times greater than the surface. It would also need to provide a reliable supply of breathable gas to sustain you.

Buoyancy Control: Moving through this dense, viscous medium would be incredibly challenging without proper buoyancy control. You would need a sophisticated diving rig with advanced ballast systems to counteract the effects of the extreme pressure and allow for controlled, directed movement.

Lighting and Visibility: With the complete darkness of the abyss, you would rely heavily on artificial lighting to navigate and observe your surroundings. High-intensity, waterproof lamps would be essential, as would specialized cameras and imaging equipment to document your findings.

Life Support and Survival: Surviving in this alien environment would push the limits of human physiology. You would need to plan for extended periods of time, with redundant life support systems to provide oxygen, temperature regulation, and other essential functions. Carrying emergency backup supplies would be crucial in case of equipment failure.

Exploration Protocols: Venturing into this uncharted realm would require the utmost caution and adherence to strict exploration protocols. You would need to map your route, maintain constant communication with a surface team, and be prepared to abort the mission at the first sign of trouble.

Mental Preparedness: Adjusting to the sheer scale, strangeness, and isolation of this environment would be a significant psychological challenge. You would need to be mentally resilient, and ready to cope with the unsettling sensations and potential hallucinations that could arise from the extreme conditions.

Ultimately, visiting this alien ocean world would be an extraordinary, awe-inspiring, and potentially perilous experience. Meticulous planning, state-of-the-art equipment, and an unwavering sense of exploration and discovery would be essential to navigate this strange, wondrous realm safely and successfully.
