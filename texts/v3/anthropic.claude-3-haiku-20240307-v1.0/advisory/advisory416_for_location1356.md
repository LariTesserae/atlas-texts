Preparing to visit this extraordinary, alien realm beneath the crushing depths of the primordial sea would require careful consideration and specialized equipment. Here are some key recommendations for a human multiverse traveler:

Protective Gear:
- A state-of-the-art pressurized diving suit capable of withstanding the immense water pressure, which can reach over 500 atmospheres at the deepest points. The suit must be meticulously engineered to maintain internal pressure and temperature regulation.
- A high-powered, multi-spectrum diving mask with specialized lenses to aid vision in the muted, hazy aquatic environment.
- Breathing apparatus capable of filtering and delivering the appropriate gas mixture for respiration under extreme pressure.
- Thrusters or propulsion systems integrated into the suit to enable controlled movement and maneuverability in the dense, viscous medium.

Exploration Equipment:
- Powerful, high-intensity lighting systems to illuminate the obscured terrain and document the strange, alien landscapes and lifeforms.
- Sensitive sonar or other scanning devices to map the complex, three-dimensional topography and detect faint, distant sounds and movements.
- Sampling tools and containment vessels to collect specimens of the exotic, unfamiliar organisms and materials for further study.
- Advanced navigational systems and tracking devices to maintain orientation and ensure a safe return to the entry point.

Preparation and Training:
- Extensive training in deep-sea diving techniques, pressure physiology, and emergency procedures to ensure the traveler can safely operate in this extreme environment.
- In-depth study of the unique geological, chemical, and biological properties of this alien realm to understand the peculiar laws that govern its existence.
- Mental and psychological preparation for the profound sense of isolation, timelessness, and unfamiliarity that will pervade the experience.

Ultimately, visiting this primordial, oceanic world will require a profound level of dedication, specialized knowledge, and technological mastery. The human traveler must be willing to confront the strange and the unknown, to push the boundaries of their understanding, and to immerse themselves in a truly alien landscape that defies the familiar rules of the surface world. Only then can they hope to unravel the mysteries and wonders that lie hidden beneath the crushing depths of this ancient, fluid realm.
