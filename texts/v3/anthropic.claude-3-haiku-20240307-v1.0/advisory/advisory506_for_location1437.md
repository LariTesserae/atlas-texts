Preparing to explore the alien realm beneath the crushing depths of the ocean would require careful planning and specialized equipment. As an ordinary human multiverse traveler, you would need to equip yourself with the following:

1. Pressurized Suit: The immense pressure of the deep ocean environment would crush an unprotected human body. You would need a specialized pressurized suit, designed to withstand the intense forces and maintain a livable internal environment.

2. Thermal Insulation: The temperatures in this alien realm can be extreme, with areas of intense heat and cold. Your suit would need to provide robust thermal insulation to protect you from these extreme conditions.

3. Breathing Apparatus: The atmosphere in this environment is not breathable for humans. You would need a self-contained breathing system, capable of providing a steady supply of oxygen or a suitable alternative gas mixture.

4. Illumination System: The dim, ethereal lighting of this alien world would not be sufficient for safe navigation. You would need a powerful illumination system, perhaps incorporating bioluminescent or other specialized light sources, to provide clear visibility.

5. Mobility Assistance: The unfamiliar terrain and viscous fluid environment would make movement challenging. Your suit should incorporate advanced propulsion and maneuvering systems to allow you to navigate the landscape effectively.

6. Sensory Enhancement: The alien soundscape and unfamiliar sensations of this realm would require specialized sensory enhancement systems. These could include hydrophones, vibration sensors, and other specialized instruments to help you better perceive and interpret the environment.

7. Communication and Telemetry: Maintaining contact with the surface and transmitting data back would be crucial. Your suit should be equipped with advanced communication systems and data telemetry to allow for real-time monitoring and support from your expedition team.

8. Scientific Instrumentation: To fully explore and understand this alien realm, you would need a suite of scientific instruments, including samplers, scanners, and analytical tools, to collect and study the various materials and phenomena encountered.

Preparing for this expedition would require extensive training, both in the use of the specialized equipment and in the unique challenges of navigating and surviving in this extreme environment. It would be a journey into the unknown, where the very limits of human experience and understanding would be tested. Only the most intrepid and well-prepared multiverse travelers would be able to venture into this alien abyss and return with their insights and discoveries.
