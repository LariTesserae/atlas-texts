A high-quality compass and topographical map of the area, as well as a reliable GPS device, will be essential for orienting yourself and avoiding getting lost in the dense, layered forest. A first-aid kit, emergency fire-starting supplies, and a sturdy, lightweight shelter like a tarp or bivy sack could also prove invaluable in case of unexpected circumstances.

Be prepared to be both awed and unsettled, to immerse yourself in the primordial beauty of the landscape while also being mindful of the tension between the wild and the tamed. Embrace the opportunity to observe the intricate web of life that thrives in this oasis, and be open to the possibility of encountering unexpected sights, sounds, and perhaps even encounters with the denizens of this unique multiverse location.
