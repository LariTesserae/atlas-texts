Preparing to visit this alien, underwater realm would require extensive preparation and specialized equipment. Here are some key considerations for an ordinary human multiverse traveler:

Protective Gear:
- A heavily reinforced, pressurized diving suit capable of withstanding the immense water pressure at these depths. The suit must be designed to maintain internal temperature and air supply.
- A specialized breathing apparatus that can filter and recycle air, as the surrounding water is unlikely to be breathable.
- Thermal insulation to guard against the bone-chilling cold of the environment.
- Robust eye protection to shield against the dim, eerie lighting and potential hazards.
- Durable gloves and boots to protect against the jagged, otherworldly terrain.

Mobility and Navigation:
- A powerful, maneuverable underwater propulsion system, such as a specialized submersible vehicle or exosuit, to navigate the complex, three-dimensional environment.
- Advanced sonar and mapping systems to chart the treacherous seafloor and detect any hazards or anomalies.
- Careful monitoring of pressure, depth, and air supply, as the crushing weight of the water would make ascent and decompression a critical concern.

Exploration and Documentation:
- High-intensity lighting systems to illuminate the gloomy depths and capture detailed observations.
- Specialized cameras and sensors to study the bizarre lifeforms and geological formations.
- Sample collection equipment to safely gather specimens for further analysis.
- Communication and telemetry systems to maintain contact with the surface and transmit data in real-time.

Survival and Emergency Preparedness:
- Emergency air and life support systems in case of equipment failure or unexpected events.
- Contingency plans for rapid ascent and decompression in the event of a critical situation.
- Reliable methods of navigation and locating the entry/exit point, as the disorienting environment could easily lead to getting lost.
- Defensive measures, such as deterrent devices, in case of encountering any unknown, potentially hostile entities.

Entering this alien, underwater realm would be an immense challenge, requiring extensive training, specialized equipment, and a deep understanding of the unique challenges and hazards that await. Caution, preparation, and a spirit of scientific exploration would be essential for any ordinary human multiverse traveler embarking on such a journey.
