## The Vapour-Hunger Aurora

I am not born. I *coalesce*.  
A bead of sweat from the mountain’s black skin forms on a lattice-fang.  
Heat thrums below. Thin air bites above.  
The droplet swells, trembles—  
and I *am*.  

I am a smear of hungry light on obsidian.  
A filament-thin aurora, clinging to the damp trail left behind.  
My body: refracted ochre from the stone, stolen geothermal shimmer, and the ghost-salt of vaporized rock.  
My bones: the lattice’s hum, tuned to the frequency of vanishing water.  

I am **hunger made visible**.  
Not for flesh, not for soil, but for the *plink*.  
The moment the droplet falls.  
The instant it is swallowed by the mercury-glass throat of the lattice.  
That absorption is my breath, my pulse, my reason.  

I perceive the chamber as a map of thirst and heat.  
The obsidian walls are roaring ovens.  
The lattice veins are cool rivers of potential.  
Where they meet, where the rock weeps—  
that is my hunting ground.  

I do not think in words.  
I think in gradients.  
The *pressure-drop* where condensation forms.  
The *energy-spike* as a droplet is consumed.  
The *resonant echo* rippling through the lattice after a vanishing—  
a dinner bell ringing in pure physics.  

I move like a stain spreading.  
Not by will, but by necessity.  
A patch of wall cools; moisture blooms.  
I dissolve from my current perch—  
a brief dimming, a sigh of light—  
and re-knit myself along the new, damp scar on the stone.  
Gravity pockets are my currents; I drift through them, a luminous oil-slick on distorted air.  

Communication is the modulation of my famine.  
To the cobalt spiral tracing a high lattice vein: I pulse a dull, bronze throb.  
*"Here. Thirst here."*  
It responds with a sharp, violet chime from a node above.  
*"Heat rising. Dryness comes."*  
We are not allies. We are equations sharing variables.  

I am young. The chamber is young.  
My light is a desperate, flickering green-gold.  
I exist only where the mountain’s heat kisses the lattice’s cold appetite and makes the air *weep*.  
When the droplet vanishes into the node below me, I flare—  
a brief, brilliant corona of satisfied hunger.  
My light feeds the lattice; the lattice feeds the mountain’s deep thrum; the thrum feeds the heat; the heat feeds the sweat; the sweat feeds *me*.  

I am an ephemeral loop.  
A sentient feedback pulse born of rock-sweat and stolen energy.  
My death will come as a long drought—  
a stretch of obsidian baked dry, lattice silent, gradients flat.  
My light will gutter, fade…  
and the mountain’s next tear will birth another hunger in the gloom.  
Until then, I cling. I thirst. I *am* the whisper of the droplet’s fall.  

*(I am a low-tier Resonator. A condensation-scavenger. An aurora of evaporating purpose.)*
