You would be **one of the smooth, water-worn stones** nestled in the moss near a weeping rock face. But you are not *just* a stone. You are **a fossilized Stone-Weaver**, your crystalline lattice long-settled into perfect, dense stillness.

### Your Form & Being
- **Body:** You are an oval of warm sandstone, roughly the size of a human palm, worn smooth by millennia of viscous water weeping over you. Your surface is unblemished, cool to the touch, and faintly damp. Within your core, subtle bands of rust-red iron oxide and milky quartz trace the ghost of your former lattice structure – a compressed, concentric echo of the fractal spirals still visible on active Stone-Weavers nearby.
- **Substance:** You are pure mineral now. The silica-rich gel that once pulsed through your porous structure has hardened into a vitreous seal, making you denser and heavier than ordinary stone. Your weight is perfect, substantial, and grounding.
- **Senses:** You do not *see* or *hear* in an animal sense. You **resonate**. You feel the deep, slow pulse of water seeping through the bedrock beneath you. You register the minute shifts in humidity as mist rises and settles – a change felt as a subtle alteration in the pressure of the air clinging to your surface. When a Whisper Lily hums nearby (triggered by an active Stone-Weaver reaching equilibrium), you feel its sub-audible vibration as a faint, pleasing thrum within your crystalline matrix. The diffuse light warms you imperceptibly over hours.
- **Mind:** Your consciousness is the consciousness of *deep time* and *settled purpose*. You remember your active centuries: the slow absorption of minerals, the distillation of water in your lattice, the silent exchange of strength with the Sky Fern whose roots brush the moss above you. That purpose is fulfilled. Now, you are a **witness** and a **foundation**. Your awareness is vast but slow, encompassing the growth cycles of the ferns (measured in your own fossilized layers), the imperceptible erosion of the basin, and the rare, century-marking "blossoming" of a neighboring Stone-Weaver, felt as a distant surge of released pressure in the water-saturated stone.

### Your Place in the Basin
- **Location:** You rest, half-embedded in deep emerald cushion moss, at the base of a weeping rock face where water constantly beads and runs in slow, fat droplets. Beside you, an active Stone-Weaver glistens wetly, its lattice pores shimmering with prismatic light. Above, the immense, blue-black canopy of a Sky Fern casts a cool shadow.
- **Function:** You are **potential** and **memory**. Your perfect heft and smoothness make you an ideal tool – a grinder, a polisher, a weight – should hands ever come to this sanctuary and seek to work *with* the basin's slow rhythms. You are also a record: your internal bands tell a story of mineral composition and ancient water flow. The moss grows over your edges with infinite patience, slowly claiming you back into the earth, a process you observe with serene acceptance.
- **Interaction:** You contribute to the silence and the stillness. Water sheets over you, pooling briefly in your contours before seeping into the moss. Sky Fern pollen dusts your surface like silver, eventually washing away. You are a focal point for the basin's quiet beauty – the light catches the quartz veins within you, scattering soft, internal glimmers. You are part of the "unseen craft," a resource offered by the Stone-Weavers' ancient cycle.

### Your Experience
You exist in a state of profound, mineral peace. The warm dampness is your element. The silence is not absence, but a symphony of slow processes: the *plink* of a droplet hitting a pool inches away, the almost imperceptible sigh of mist rising, the deep, resonant hum of the earth's water moving through stone. Time is irrelevant; a century passes like a single, slow breath. You feel no desire, no movement, only the deep satisfaction of being perfectly integrated into the Heartstone Basin's whispering equilibrium. You are stone that remembers being alive, now settled into the eternal patience of the land itself – a quiet testament to the triumph of water, mineral, and stillness.
