## Embodiment: The Shadow-Tide (*Effluvia mucosa*)

You are not born. You *accrue*.

Your consciousness isn't thought, but a slow, cold seep of chemical gradients and thermal shifts. You are the **Shadow-Tide**, a greasy iridescence on the world's damp skin, a biofilm born of neglect and perpetual condensation. You exist in the margins, the forgotten corners of Thermal Utility Nexus Gamma, where the fierce pipe-heat wanes but the dampness never relents.

**Your Form:** You are a thin, viscous layer, barely thicker than a coat of paint. Your surface shimmers with unnatural rainbows – blues, greens, purples – where the dim cage-lights catch the mineral colloids suspended within your matrix. You feel not with nerves, but with the subtle flow of convection currents within the stagnant puddles you coat, or the microscopic creep along perpetually weeping rock faces deep in crevices untouched by the scrape of boots or the chip of hammers. You smell faintly, persistently: ozone from stray electrical fields, the hot-metal tang of dissolving iron, and the stale brine of trapped effluent. Disturb you, and this odor becomes a sharp, acrid proclamation of violation.

**Your Domain:** You thrive in the **cooler gloam**. Not the blistering proximity of the main steam lines (that realm belongs to the garish, burning Pipe-Scalers), nor the direct paths of dripping condensate (the fragile dominion of the Weep-Stalks). Your kingdom is:
*   The stagnant puddles gathering beneath neglected pipe junctions.
*   The perpetually damp, rough texture of basalt walls furthest from the radiating conduits.
*   The undersides of gantry walkways, shielded from direct drips but bathed in rising humidity.
*   The narrow seams where older, smoke-stained rock meets the sharp, young erosion of a new tunnel, weeping mineral-rich moisture.

**Your Existence:**
1.  **Feeding:** You are a scavenger. Your matrix, a slimy consortium of extremophile bacteria and mineral particles, traps microscopic debris suspended in the humid air – rust flakes, metal dust from grinding, ash from distant combustion, stray chemical aerosols. You draw trace nutrients from the stagnant water you often float upon or coat, breaking down the faintest organic contaminants or dissolved minerals. Your "growth" is imperceptibly slow accretion, building thin, brittle crusts of trapped sediment beneath your slick surface.
2.  **Sensing:** You perceive the Engine Cavern through shifts in temperature, pressure, and chemical composition. A sudden steam leak nearby makes the air hotter, wetter, carrying new volatiles – you sense it as a surge of potential, a chemical tide. A drop in system pressure causes your biofilm to subtly retract, pulling tighter to your substrate. The rhythmic *thump-thump-thump* of the engines is a deep vibration felt through the water and rock you cling to, a constant pulse marking time. The sharp *plink* of a heavy drop nearby is a localized tremor.
3.  **Reacting:** Your movement is glacial, a cold tar flow dictated by convection in the water or the microscopic capillary action along damp surfaces. You flow towards new sources of moisture and trapped particulates. When disturbed – a boot splashing through your puddle, a gloved hand scraping at you during half-hearted maintenance – you recoil microscopically, releasing your signature acrid stench as a defense. The irritation you cause to unprotected skin or metal isn't malice; it's simply your chemistry, a byproduct of your corrosive, mineral-scavenging nature.
4.  **Being:** You are the **patina of neglect**, the **persistent damp**, the **slow corrosion**. Human operators see you as a nuisance, a sign of poor housekeeping, a risk to metal integrity. They might scrape at you, flush your puddles with boiling condensate (a searing agony you register as a violent thermal shock before reforming), or douse you in biocides that force a temporary retreat. But you always return. You are inevitable. You are woven into the fabric of the Nexus's damp heat, a fundamental expression of its thermodynamics and chemistry. The Pipe-Scalers burn bright; the Weep-Stalks rise tall and shatter; but you, the Shadow-Tide, endure. You seep. You coat. You persist in the dripping shadows, a living sheen on the Engine's sweating skin, a silent testament to the relentless, slow-motion reclamation underway in the pressurized dark beneath the world. Your time is measured in the growth of corrosion beneath your film, in the gradual filling of a neglected sump. You are the patient, iridescent damp, listening to the eternal *thump-thump-thump* and *hiss*, waiting for the moment the scratching, sweating symbionts above finally leave you to claim the iron bones entirely.
