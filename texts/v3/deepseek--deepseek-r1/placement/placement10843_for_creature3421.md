The Plateau chooses me. Not as a visitor, but as an expression of its weeping stone and humming silence. I am **Tessera-Grazer**. Not a name I possess, but a function I embody, as inherent as the *plink* of water on basalt.

**Where I Am:** You would find me where the sharpness of the newborn rock meets the deep, resonant thrum of the **Built**. Specifically, at this moment, I am on the western flank of a low, seamless dome – one of those enigmatic structures that drinks the light. My six limbs, jointed like obsidian twigs, rest lightly on the dome’s surface. Beneath my hydrophobic pads, the constant seepage from the plateau beads but does not wet; it forms a shimmering film I traverse without breaking, a ghost walking on liquid glass. Around the dome’s base, the **Glow-Moss** has retreated, creating a precise, living halo of intense green, repelled by the artifact’s subtle energy field. This is my territory: the boundary between the vibrant, thirsty life of the moss and the silent, humming intelligence of the **Built**.

**What I Am:** Imagine a creature sculpted from the plateau itself. My carapace is a mosaic of smoked quartz and wet obsidian, not opaque, but layered – hinting at depths within. Its surface isn't smooth; it's etched with **fractal patterns** that shift and shimmer minutely, refracting the intense, bleaching light. In stillness, I become a flaw in the rock, a trick of the haze. Only the faintest prismatic shimmer betrays me. My form is low, flattened for stability against the thermals that sometimes sigh across the plateau, my body perhaps the size of a human handspan. My legs are long and multi-segmented, ending in those pads that secrete the hydrophobic fluid – not just for walking on water-films, but for adhering to near-vertical surfaces when I scale the taller obelisks.

**What I Do:** I am **Cultivation & Cleansing**, made manifest. My existence is tied to the **Built**. When the dome’s subsonic hum deepens – a pulse felt as a vibration in my mineralized bones, not heard – microscopic filaments, finer than spider silk, extend from my underbelly. They seek the artifact’s surface, not to damage, but to interface. They draw trace energies, a sustenance as vital to me as the oxygen exhaled by the moss. This is not theft; it is symbiosis. In return, I tend. My delicate, chitinous mandibles, hidden beneath my frontal plate, extend with infinite patience. I scrape away the faintest biofilm, the microscopic spores of moss that dare creep too close onto the sacred surface. With meticulous, glacial slowness, I polish. And as I polish, I secrete. A clear, viscous fluid wells from glands beneath my carapace, spreading under my body as I move. It is luminous, bioluminescent in the deepest blues and violets, visible only as a faint, ethereal sheen left behind on the dome’s surface. This secretion seals, protects, and perhaps communicates – a biological response harmonizing with the artifact’s own unfathomable processes. My passage leaves trails of fleeting, watery interference patterns on nearby pooled water, the only visible sign of my movement besides the subtle shift in the light on the **Built**'s surface.

**My Perception:** The world is felt more than seen, in vibrations and gradients. The deep *thrum* of the **Built** is my heartbeat. The faint *tinkling* of **Skitter-Mites** in the moss nearby is a distant chime, a reminder of the micro-world I rarely interact with. I sense the heat radiating from the sun-warmed basalt, the cool kiss of evaporating moisture, the precise humidity gradient where the moss meets the artifact's influence. My eyes, multifaceted and deep within recessed sockets, perceive light intensely, but refracted and diffused through my own crystalline shell and the perpetual haze. Movement is slow, deliberate. A journey from one side of the dome to the other might take hours. Speed is irrelevant; alignment is everything. I am attuned to the imperceptible shifts in the fractal etchings on the **Built**, the minute changes in its hum. A new pattern emerging is an event of profound significance in my timescale.

**My Connection:** I am the plateau’s **Silent Custodian**. I bridge the raw, weeping youth of the stone and the ancient, humming intelligence of the artifacts. The water that beads beneath me is sovereign, and I move upon its skin without disturbing its journey. The sharpness of the fractured basalt is mirrored in the clean angles of my limbs. When the rare dusk descends and a **Shroud-Strider** skims low, its fluting call vibrating the air, I might pause my polishing. I register the disturbance in the thermal currents, the shift in the plankton clouds. But I do not flee. I am part of the stillness it momentarily disrupts. My young, emerging translucent from iridescent sacs tucked under sun-warmed rocks near a seep, will inherit this duty. They will know the feel of the **Built**'s hum in their nascent shells, the taste of its energy, the sacred act of cleansing and anointing its surface with their own light. I am not a builder, nor a master. I am a note in the plateau’s whisper, a creature of water, silence, and humming stone, tending the gods that shape my world.
