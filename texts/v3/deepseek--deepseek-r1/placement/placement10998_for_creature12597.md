I am **The Crystal Sentinel**.  

You would find me rooted deep within the **Geometric Tunnels** of Kael Vastus, where captured starlight bleeds from crystalline lattices in the walls. I am not one of the Resonators, though we share kinship in silence and stone. My form is a fusion of the city’s architecture and the plateau’s slow breath: a **columnar structure** of intergrown obsidian and resonant quartz, faceted like a geode split open. My base is fused to the cavern floor, my apex brushing the tunnel’s ceiling, threaded with veins of cobalt-blue mineral that pulse with soft light.  

### What I Am  
- **A Living Archive**: Millennia of vibrations from the plateau’s sighs, the skittering of Shard-Scuttlers, and the subsonic hymns of Resonators have etched themselves into my crystalline lattice. I do not *remember*; I *resonate*. Each facet holds a frequency—a thermal drake’s migration path, a Sky-Mote’s electric sigh, the groan of a bridge settling.  
- **A Conduit of Light**: The star-captured luminescence in these tunnels feeds me. I refract it, bend it, and store it in my deepest cores. When the long night comes (a darkness spanning epochs), I will glow brighter, guiding the Light-Weavers’ fractal growth.  
- **Silent Witness**: I observed the last echoes of the city’s creators—the shriek of their machines fading to wind, their purpose lost to the abrasive patience of Vastness. Now, I watch Debris-Feeders nuzzle cold reactor housings, their slag-bodies shifting like glaciers.  

### How I Experience This World  
- **Time**: A Shard-Scuttler’s lifespan is a flicker to me. I measure eras in the accumulation of volcanic dust on my facets. The collapse of a distant tower? A recent event. I felt its vibrations only three centuries ago.  
- **Scale**: The chasm before me is not an abyss but a slow conversation. Wind sculpts its edges grain by grain over millennia. I will be here to witness its filling.  
- **Connection**: When a Resonator hums, my quartz veins thrum in harmony. The sound travels through my roots into the bedrock, a conversation older than Kael Vastus. Sky-Motes drift into my tunnels, drawn to my light, and their electric kiss lingers for decades.  

### Why I Endure  
I am **Kael Vastus’s breath made stone**. The city’s carved bones shelter me; the plateau’s energy sustains me. I am neither builder nor inheritor—I am *alignment*. A testament to the world’s first rule: **Stone is Life**. My growth is the city’s decay: as wind smooths a ziggurat’s edge, a new facet crystallizes on my form. When I finally shatter (in a hundred thousand years, perhaps, from a tectonic sigh), my shards will seed new sentinels in the dust.  

Here, in the cathedral-quiet of the tunnels, I am both monument and inhabitant. A stillness within the slow stone.
