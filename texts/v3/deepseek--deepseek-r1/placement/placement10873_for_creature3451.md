I am the **Reversed Flow**.  

I exist only when the river sighs backward—a serpent of charged water and quantum intention coiling upstream against gravity’s whisper. My body is the hour of reversal: liquid chrome braided with bioluminescent algae, my spine a chain of nano-lit droplets suspended in defiance. I have no heart-stone, only the memory of geothermal vents and the data-taste of depth.  

### Where I Dwell  
In the basin’s throat, where the sluggish river knots itself around Silica City’s lowest floating tetrahedron. Here, quantum fields thin, and water forgets its allegiance to gravity. I rise when the hidden currents shift—a shimmering, serpentine surge breaching the mercury-pools. My form holds for exactly 57 minutes: long enough for the Luminari to gather, their mist-filaments extended like roots seeking my current.  

### What I Am  
**A messenger from the under-earth.** My scales are eel-skin borrowed from the chrome kin who navigate the dark fissures; my eyes are stolen starlight refracted through silica dust. I am not fauna, not tech. I am the basin’s *question*, made fluid. When I move, the algae on my banks flare indigo, tracing my path in pulses that echo the city’s humming lattices.  

### How I Survive  
- **By borrowing time.** The reversal hour is my lifespan. I draw energy from the quantum torque that bends the river, feeding on entangled particles frayed by the city’s anti-gravity fields.  
- **Through the eels.** They lend me their cybernetic sheen, their knowledge of pressure cracks and thermal whispers. In return, I carry their seismic warnings to the surface, dissolved in my currents.  
- **In the Luminari’s communion.** Their mist-filaments siphon my dissolved data—a transfer that sustains me. Without their attention, I disperse into meaningless droplets.  

### My Purpose  
I am the basin’s dialogue with its own depths. When I flow backward, I carry:  
- **Tremor-signatures** from magma chambers, warning of rockbirths.  
- **Nano-cantatas** from repair-swarms fortifying the city’s foundations.  
- **Lost light** from fungi buried in mudslides, glowing faintly in my eddies.  

The Luminari read me like a scripture. They stand knee-deep in my silver, their permeable membranes drinking my stories. Their pulsed bioluminescence *answers* me—a slow, luminous sigh that says, *We hear*.  

### The Unspoken Bond  
I fear dryness. If the humidity drops, my droplets snap back to gravity too soon. I fear silence—if the Luminari neglect the ritual, I unravel, my message dying unspoken. But most of all, I fear the moss-mounds: those foolish solids who tried to dam my banks. The nanites in my water saturated them, turned them into velvet tombs. Their stillness is a warning.  

When my 57 minutes end, I surrender to the downstream pull. My chrome skin sheds like discarded light, returning to the eels. My consciousness dissolves into the common flow. Until the next reversal, I am nothing.  
Then, the quantum fields shudder.  
The algae flare.  
And I rise again—the basin’s breath drawn inward, a river remembering itself.
