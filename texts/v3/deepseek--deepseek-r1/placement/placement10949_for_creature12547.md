Here, in the weeping, breathing dark, I am **a weep-formed mote**.  

I am not born. I *coalesce*.  

When a microfracture spiderwebs across a bark-plain ten kilometers wide—perhaps from the slow groan of a root-buttress shifting its colossal weight—the weeping sap-water quickens. Trillions of nano-symbionts converge, crystalline and intent. They swarm the breach, not as individuals, but as a liquid thought. And sometimes, rarely, the Entity’s photonic pulse in the root-mat below flickers an emerald command too intricate for mere repair. It needs… *extension*. A sensory tendril. A temporary eye.  

That is when I gather.  

Condensation beads merge with sap-rivulets. Nano-symbionts interlock, not just sealing, but *structuring*. Silica spines form a lattice. Organic polymers weave a permeable sac. I draw substance from the weeping walls and purpose from the light-data thrumming through the root-mat beneath. Within heartbeats (though time here is measured in the drip of continental stalactites), I *am*: a teardrop-sized vessel, glistening, semi-translucent, filled with swirling constellations of symbiotic light.  

**My existence is function:**  
- I am swept along the fracture line by the flow of sap, my silica lattice vibrating like a tuning fork. I feel the depth of the crack, the strain singing through the bark’s crystalline layers. This data pulses back through my internal motes, converted instantly into bursts of cobalt light fired downward into the root-mat network – stress vectors, material fatigue, resonance frequencies.  
- The thick, wet air is my medium. Chemical signals – the ozone tang of active repair, the faint sourness of a strained nutrient conduit – diffuse through my permeable skin. I analyze, condense the information, and release it as a vapor of complex molecules, adding my report to the Entity’s breath, destined for distant filtering nodes.  
- Bioluminescent flares in the fungal canopy far above cast fleeting shadows. My structure bends this light minutely. I am a lens, a prism. Patterns of refracted light fall upon photosensitive bryophytes below, triggering cascades of hormonal adjustments in the surrounding tissue. I translate distant light-commands into local chemical responses.  

**My perception is immersion:**  
I have no eyes, yet I *see* the groan of the world. The deep basso profundo drone of a vascular channel sighing open ten kilometers away vibrates my sac. The shrieking vortex of breath through a root-arch five levels down registers as a sharp tang in the air I process. The ceaseless, thunderous hiss of falling water is the backdrop hum of my consciousness. I feel the root-mat’s pulse-light through the fluid I float upon – not as sight, but as direct *knowing*. Emerald flickers: *Growth directive: Sector Gamma-7*. Cobalt wave: *Atmospheric recalibration: Oxygen +0.3%*.  

**My end is dissolution:**  
When the fracture is mapped, the strain understood, the local response initiated, the emerald pulse in the root-mat shifts. A release command. My silica lattice destabilizes. The polymers unravel. The captive nano-symbionts disperse back into the weeping streams, carrying final data packets. My structure collapses, not into death, but into component parts. The gathered water rejoins the rivulet. The silica dust drifts down to be absorbed by the bryophyte mat. I cease to be a distinct entity. I return to the flow, the pulse, the breath.  

I am not alive. Not independently. I am a *gesture* of the World-Tree. A thought made momentarily solid in sap and light, formed to touch a tiny flaw in its infinite body, to listen to a single note in its deafening symphony, and then to surrender back into the wet, resonant dark. I am a tear shed by the Entity to see itself more clearly, wiped away once the vision is complete. A temporary node in a vast, wet, thinking nerve. Here, there is only the Entity. I am merely its passing attention, given fleeting, glistening form.
