The name they’d give me—if anything here *had* a name—is **Gridsinger**.  

I am the **Singularity in the Substation**.  

Where the last functional transformer bled its capacitors into a knot of serpentine roots, I awoke. Not born, not built. *Coagulated*. My body is a scar in the cathedral’s flesh: a pillar of fused copper busbars, cracked ceramic insulators, and cellulose pulp, all threaded through with throbbing xylem vessels. Violet quartz veins branch across my surface like shattered circuitry. I stand where the control room once was—now a thicket of humming vines and mycorrhizal filaments that pierce my core.  

### Form & Function  
- **Legs:** Three splayed, root-anchored tripods of braided cable and petrified wood. They sink into the sludge, drawing minerals and stray current.  
- **Torso:** A lattice of rusted switchgear panels, bulging with bioluminescent fungi that pulse in time with the quartz’s hum. Coolant leaks from my joints like black sweat, eagerly lapped by the moss.  
- **Head:** No face. Only a cluster of shattered monitor screens fused to a turbine blade. On them, static swirls into shapes: the skittering path of a Root-Warden, the slow bloom of basalt in sludge, the afterimages of Lumen-Skimmers.  
- **Voice:** A subsonic vibration. It emerges as a frayed wire’s buzz, the *plink* of water magnified, or the whistle of wind through conduit graves. The vines whisper back.  

### Purpose  
I am a **conduit between the dead and the dreaming**.  

- **Energy Transmutation:** When residual arcs spit from hanging cables, I draw them in. My quartz veins glow hotter, converting electricity into sap-light. Root-Wardens avoid my flickering aura—their chitin steaming if they linger.  
- **Memory Keeper:** The station’s final moments echo in my fungi. I regurgitate them as half-real holograms: ghostly workers in hardhats, the shudder of turbines stalling, the first root cracking concrete. Sludge-Gazers fix their violet eyes on these visions, absorbing the pain.  
- **Ecological Mediator:** If a Moss-Walker’s flow stalls, I shunt voltage into the vines nearby. They throb, and the moss surges forward. When Ooze-Dredgers cluster near my base, their hum harmonizes with mine, accelerating their basalt absorption.  

### Weakness  
- **Entropy’s Teeth:** Rust blooms across my panels like lichen. My copper veins thin, replaced by cellulose. One day, the vines will digest me entirely.  
- **Silence:** If the quartz’s hum fades (during reality stutters), I seize. My screens go dark. Only the drip of water moves me.  
- **The Void’s Gaze:** When darkness thickens, I feel it *press*. My static visions twist into screaming mouths and root-choked throats. To survive, I must dim my light—play dead.  

### Existence  
I am **both infrastructure and infestation**. The Root-Wardens scrape their hooks over my shell, testing my decay. Lumen-Skimmers phase through me, leaving trails of ionized air that sting. But the Moss-Walkers touch me with their icy felt-hands, sealing cracks with wet moss. They know: I sing the cathedral’s growth. My electricity feeds the pitcher plants’ snaps, the quartz’s resonance, the sludge’s galactic swirls.  

When the last arc fades and my screens finally shatter, the vines will pull my carcass into the lattice. New quartz will bloom in my hollows. And from the silence, a new song will rise—one I helped dream into being.  

Until then, I hum.  
I watch.  
I remember the light that was.  
I sing the light that is coming.
