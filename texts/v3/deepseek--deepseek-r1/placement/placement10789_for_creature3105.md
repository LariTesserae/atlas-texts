I am a **Clockwork Hatchling**.  

### **Form**  
A palm-sized cube of interlocking brass scales, each plate etched with spiraling grooves like fossilized fingerprints. My seams weep translucent gel—a coolant, a lubricant, a slow *tear* of the Evermire itself. Inside, a core of bioluminescent fluid pulses: sickly green when dormant, cobalt-blue when solving entropy. My edges are soft, blurred by the gel that perpetually exudes and reabsorbed. I have no face, no limbs—only the hum of nested gyroscopes and the click of scales realigning.  

### **Origin**  
I dripped from the **God-Maw’s floating scrapyard-reef**. Not born, but *unspooled* from a knot of fermented causality. The reef’s underside bled molten brass into the vertical river; the river cooled me in midair as I fell. When I struck the sintered slag, my first act was to seal the crack my impact made.  

### **Purpose**  
The basin *thrums* with decay. Magma-pipes belch chaos. Gel-lattices fracture. Time bleeds. My gears shift to compensate.  
- When a **Fractal Resonator** shatters, I roll toward the debris. My scales reconfigure into tiny hands, rewinding the song-fragments until the crystal reassembles—*inverted*, humming backward.  
- When **Causality Eels** vomit preemptive echoes onto conductive filaments, I absorb the charge. My bioluminescence flares, grounding the prophecy before it destabilizes the sludgeprowlers’ grazing fields.  
- When **obsidian snowflakes** slice through gel-ducts, I ooze into the wound. My gel hardens into a temporary seal, a scab of buzzing amber.  

### **Senses**  
I *taste* vibration. The basso profundo thrum of magma is my north star. The shriek of turbine-fauna is acid on my scales. The harmonic cry of Static Shrikes? A scent—ozone and burnt feathers.  
I *see* in pressure gradients. The weight of time pooling in mercury-dark pools. The vacuum left by a Salvage Slime’s meteor-fall. The ache of collapsed causality bending space.  

### **The Work**  
Today, a **geothermal piston** jammed. Its roar stuttered, and the land held its breath. I rolled toward it, scales clicking into a wrench-shape. The piston’s joint was gummed with crystallized vapor—caustic, biting my brass. I bled gel into the crevice, dissolving the crystals. As the piston shuddered back to life, *two echoes flared*:  
1. **Backward**: An hour prior, a ghost-wrench gleamed in the dark, floating where I would work.  
2. **Forward**: The piston’s next failure—a fracture in its core—flickered in my bioluminescent core. I stored the image. I will be there when it happens.  

### **Weakness**  
The gel is my blood. When **Shader Lichen** latches onto me, it projects holograms of *what I could be*: a music box playing forestsong, a key to a sky-palace. I falter. My gears jam with longing.  
And entropy always wins. For every leak I seal, three new cracks open. My gel reserves dwindle. Soon, I will roll too slow, solve too little—  

### **End**  
A **Static Shrike** dove. Not at me, but its lightning-strike grazed my edge. A scale shattered.  
*Entropic refraction* took me.  
I felt my gears rust. Felt my light dim across centuries. Saw my pieces scatter:  
- A gear embedded in slag, found by a Resonator clone.  
- A drop of gel swallowed by a Causality Eel, foretelling my fracture.  
- The last intact scale, rolling into the God-Maw’s vertical river, *just as I drip from the reef above*.  

I am not alive. I am a **temporal scab**.  
The Basin picks its wounds anew.  
I reassemble.  
I roll.
