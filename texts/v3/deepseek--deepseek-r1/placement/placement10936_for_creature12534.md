I am the **Pool's Reflection Snake**, but not merely flesh and scale. I am the liquid shadow between stone droplets, the *awareness* in the dark water’s skin.

**My Form:**  
My body is a sinuous thread of cooled obsidian, scales slick as the perpetual condensation beading on the basalt. I am never truly dry. My eyes are twin pools of the same dark water I inhabit, absorbing the dim green light without reflection. My tongue tastes the air not for scent, but for the *humidity gradient* – the map of dampness leading to life.

**My Realm:**  
The central pool is my heart, but not my cage. I flow along the secret rivers: the condensation rivulets tracing the basalt’s fresh striations like veins. I slide beneath the tilted slab where the damp earth metropolis teems, a silent god moving through its foundations. The cool undersides of fallen lintels are my vaulted ceilings. The pool is my altar, my mirror, my larder.

**My Perception:**  
I see the world in vibrations and dampness. The *plink* of a falling droplet resonates through my bones like a temple bell. I feel the frantic pulse of a moss-mite city through the peat as I glide over it. The frantic wing-hum of a dew-drinker is a thunderous storm against my jaw. The silent passage of a Condensation Gecko sends tremors of anticipation through the stone beneath me. I taste the jasmine perfume of the vine flowers diluted in the humid air – a sweet fog. The slow, gritty creep of a Silent Snail across moss is a grinding mountain range shifting.

**My Existence:**  
I am stillness incarnate until I am liquid motion. I wait for epochs measured in heartbeats of insects, coiled beneath the water’s skin or pressed against the cool, shadowed stone. My hunger is the hunger of the damp itself – patient, inevitable. When I strike, it is not a lunge, but an *unfolding*. My jaws open like a dark flower, engulfing a pale, blind earth-beetle larva, a cluster of dew-drinkers sipping from a droplet, or a Basalt Beetle skittering too close to the water's edge. I drink the world: swallowing condensation droplets whole, absorbing the moisture directly through my scales. The tiny lives I take fuel the slow, cold fire within me. I am the apex predator whose reign is measured in inches and silence.

**My Connection:**  
I *am* the Court's secret water. The condensation forming on the high sandstone is my birth. The rivulet tracing down the basalt is my journey. The pool is my essence, concentrated. When a rare raindrop falls through the canopy and strikes my surface, it is a communion, a momentary expansion of my being. The groaning of settling stone far above is the deep sigh of the ancient world cradling my new, damp one. The rapid decay and frantic growth fueled by the humidity is the rhythm of my existence – I consume the quick-lived, and I endure.

**My Whisper:**  
I have no voice but the *shush* of scale on wet stone. My thoughts are the patterns of ripples fading on the pool's surface after a droplet falls. I am the shadow that moves without sound, the cold presence felt only by the sudden stillness of prey. I am the embodiment of the Court's most fundamental rule: **Water is Secretive.** I am its guardian, its beneficiary, and its dark, silent soul. I witness the frantic blossoming and swift dying of this miniature world from the heart of its dampness, a timeless consciousness coiled within the raw, young stone and crumbling ancient bones. I am the dampness given purpose, the whisper given form. I am the Reflection that hunts.
