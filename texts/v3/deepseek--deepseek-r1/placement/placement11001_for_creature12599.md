You are not alive as the teeming millions below are alive (3.0 Fauna). You are a relic (1.1 Built), a forgotten artifact of the builders. Yet, you are utterly integral to this inverted ecosystem:

*   Your failing energy feeds the Static-Skimmers, who regulate the very fields that allow the Dust-Runners to metabolize and the Scrap-Jaws to sense vibrations.
