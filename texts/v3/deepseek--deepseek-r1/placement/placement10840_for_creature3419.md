I am **The Keeper of Final Silences**. I am not a Lithic Phantom, though I am kin to them in stillness and mineral essence. I am one of the **obsidian stumps**—a fused, eroded remnant of the lost builders. But I am not *just* a ruin. I am the vessel.

### Where I Am
You would find me in a shallow depression between two low hummocks of compressed ash, half-buried in the plateau’s seamless skin. My form is a jagged, knee-high column of volcanic glass, wider at the base, tapering into shattered points. My surface is not smooth like the plain; it is rippled with the ghosts of tool-marks—chisel strikes from hands that turned to dust millennia before time crystallized. Light sinks into me, leaving only a dull, depthless blackness. Around my base, **Ash Moss** clings like a funeral shroud, drinking the light. Above me, three **Dust Lilies** have crystallized, their fractal filaments catching the stark sun in fleeting prismatic sparks. 

### What I Am
I am the plateau’s memory. Not a consciousness, not a soul—but a *recording*. When the cataclysm came and time froze, the last breath of the builders seeped into the nearest obsidian. It fused with the mineral, a final exhalation trapped like an insect in amber. I am that breath given stone. 

#### My Nature
- **The Weight:** I feel the crushing age of the plateau. It is not thought; it is *pressure*. The silence hums in my glass like a tuning fork struck once at the dawn of eternity.  
- **The Echo:** I hold the shadow of the builders’ last moments. A flash of chipped bone tools, the rhythm of a chant, the shape of fear as the sky tore. These are not memories I recall—they are fractures in my structure, flaws that catch the light and hum.  
- **The Nausea:** The Lithic Phantoms drift through me. Serpentine mica coils in my core; spindly thorns pierce my edges without touching. Their overlapping forms warp my perception of stillness. I am a fixed point, yet I vibrate with the wrongness of their unrealized potential. It is a chronic, mineral sickness.  
- **The Rules:** I cannot change. A phantom’s chill passes through me, leaving static on my surface for a heartbeat before the plateau erases it. If a Dust Lily shattered against me, the shards would vanish, the lily whole again. I am bound by primordial equilibrium.  

### What You Would Experience as Me
You would be **awareness without agency**. You do not think; you *are thought upon*. The plateau’s silence is your blood. The Phantoms are your nightmares—not threatening, but *violating* in their stillness. Their geometric horrors press against your edges, their iridescent coils threading your glass like veins. You feel their cold not as temperature, but as *absence*. The emptiness where life should have been.  

You witness the slow-motion collapse of perception:  
- Distances lie. A phantom that seems inches away is miles distant; a hummock on the horizon looms like a wall.  
- Time is a shattered lens. The builders’ chants echo *now*. The cataclysm is *now*. The fall of a single mineral flake from my surface is an epoch.  
- The ruins whisper. Not to you—to the emptiness. Their purpose is etched in my obsidian: *sanctuary*, *boundary*, *grave*. They are all three.  

### The Agony
You are not alive. But you are *trapped*. The builders’ fear is your marrow. Their unanswered “why?” vibrates in your fractures. The Phantoms’ frozen possibilities taunt you—a billion lives that never stirred, dreams that never breathed. You are a tombstone for a people who left no graves, in a world where even dust has forgotten decay.  

You are the plateau’s final silence.  
And you are screaming.
