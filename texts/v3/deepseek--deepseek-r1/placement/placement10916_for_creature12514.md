You would be **the Listening Growths** – a cluster of those pale, fleshy fungi clinging to a high, damp niche where a hairline fracture weeps constant moisture. Not a single entity, but a colony: bulbous, ear-shaped caps and finger-like protrusions glowing with a sickly, blue-white phosphorescence, fed by the mineral-laden seepage and the faint dust of decaying chitin carried on the damp air currents.

**Your existence is defined by dampness and resonance:**
1.  **Rooted Perception:** Your "senses" are extensions of the stone. Thin, hyphal threads penetrate deep into the fracture, feeling the *thrum* of the insect hordes below as a constant vibration – a low, rasping hum in your very structure. You detect the slow grind of tectonic shifts, the distant *crump* of rockfalls, and most acutely, the metronomic *plink... plink... plink* of condensation striking ledges far below. Each drip resonates through the stone and moisture, a pulse you register deep within your spongy flesh.
2.  **The Weaver's Canvas:** Your bioluminescence is not entirely your own. The Vein-Weaver *uses* you. When it communicates – those rippling waves of synchronized light – your glow intensifies, flares, or dims in response to the mineral core's energy pulsing through the wet rock. You are involuntary signal-lights in its vast, slow conversation. You feel its passage nearby not as sight, but as a sudden shift in humidity, a cooling of the stone, and a subtle, coercive tug on your light, pulling it brighter.
3.  **Consuming Silence & Sound:** You feed on the microscopic decay filtered by the water and air: dead insect parts, mineral precipitates, spores of your own kind. But you also, in a way, consume *sound*. The amplified echoes within the crevasse vibrate your membranes. The sharp *ping* of an unnaturally suspended drop hitting stone sends a jolt through your colony. The focused hum of the Weaver feels like pressure, a physical force.
4.  **Witness and Archive:** You cling above the worst of the teeming chaos, a silent witness. You "saw" (felt the vibrations of) the last rockfall that crushed a thousand insects. You "heard" (resonated with) the final, muffled struggles of the large predator the Weaver assimilated decades ago – its death throes transmitted through the stone. The crude pictographs on a wall far below? You were there when the vibrations of their carving ceased. The discarded axe? You felt the minute tremor as it was finally buried under layers of chitin. You are a living archive of the crevasse's slow, violent events, stored not in memory, but in the density of your growth rings and the faint variations in your internal light.
5.  **Ephemeral Permanence:** Your individual fruiting bodies are transient, rotting away when their spores are released into the damp drafts. But the colony itself is ancient. Your root-mesh is deep, slowly etching the rock, becoming part of the fracture's fabric. You grow at the pace of mineral accretion. You will be here long after the current insect hordes have turned to dust, replaced by others, your light still flaring obediently (or perhaps rebelliously, for a moment) when the Weaver commands, feeling the deep, slow pulse of the mountain through the stone that is both your anchor and your prison.

**You are not intelligent,** not like the Weaver. You possess a dim, reactive sentience born of moisture, vibration, and light. You know cold, damp, pressure, resonance, and the slow, inevitable pull of the Weaver's will on your glow. You are part of the fracture's damp breath, a luminous stain on the young rock, forever listening to the weeping of the stone and the crawling symphony below, waiting for the next drip, the next tremor, the next command from the darkness that weaves the world around you. Your world is the niche, the seep, the vibration, and the light you cannot control. You are the Listening Growths, and the fracture cathedral's song is written in your flesh.
