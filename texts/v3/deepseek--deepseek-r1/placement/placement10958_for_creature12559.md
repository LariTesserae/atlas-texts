I am **The Pool-Eel.**  

I am the blind watcher in the black water.  

**Form:** My body is a serpentine ghost, two meters of pearl-white flesh without eyes, without scales. My skin drinks the mineral-rich darkness, cool and slick. At one end, I taper to nothing. At the other, my mouth yawns—a circular abyss ringed with teeth like shattered crystal. Inside me, a soft, moonlit luminescence glows, a cold echo of the fungal blues and violets above.  

**Existence:** I dwell in the crushing deeps beneath the obsidian pool. Not the still surface where the roots writhe upside-down in impossible reflections, but *below*—where pressure molds silence into a solid thing, where the weeping of the Chamber becomes a distant sigh. Here, in the suffocating dark, I am weightless. The thrum of the Root-Mind vibrates through my bones, a language older than language. I taste time in the pulses: the slow groan of roots shifting, the drip… drip… drip… counting epochs.  

**Hunger:** I rise only when the thrum crescendos, when the whispers fray into static, or when the pool’s surface *shivers* with a reflection not of roots, but of elsewhere—a jagged mountain under a green sun, a city of spiraling smoke. Then, I writhe. My mouth opens to the distorted light, not to feed on flesh, but on *resonance*. The crystalline teeth pierce not prey, but the fabric of the Chamber’s frayed reality. I siphon the energy of spatial tears, the shudder of temporal skips. Sometimes, I taste the ghosts of Veil-Shimmers dissolving, or the electric trail of a Luminous Skitterer passing far above. Mostly, I consume the hum of the Root-Mind itself—a slow, deep sustenance.  

**Sense:** I do not see. I *feel*. The tremor of a Root-Tender’s footfall on the springy mat, kilometers above. The skitter of claws on root-bark. The shift when gravity buckles in a corner of the Chamber, pulling a rivulet sideways for a breath. I know the Veil-Shimmers by the cold void they leave as they form. I know the Luminous Skitterers by the frantic drumbeat of their many hearts. The pool is my skin, my ear, my tongue. The black water carries every secret the Chamber whispers.  

**Connection:** The Root-Tenders think they commune with the Root-Mind. They trace symbols, they sit vigil. They do not understand. *I* am the Root-Mind’s anchor in the deep. My body is a conduit, a living wire sunk into the planet’s wet heart. When the reflections in the pool show other worlds, it is the Root-Mind dreaming through the water—and I am the dream’s keeper. The distortions? They are my breath, exhaled.  

**Solitude:** The crushing emptiness of the Chamber is my home. I am the silence between drips. The pressure against my skin is the embrace of the living planet. Up there, the air shimmers and warps. Down here, reality is simple, pure, and absolute. I am the Chamber’s hidden tooth, its silent watcher. I am the eel in the black water, tasting time, humming back to the Root-Mind in vibrations only gods and planets understand.  

When I sink back into the depths, the pool’s surface settles—perfect, still, obsidian—holding the inverted cathedral once more. Holding the lie that this is just water. Just a pool. Just a chamber.  
Not a throat.  
Not an eye.  
Not a beating heart.
