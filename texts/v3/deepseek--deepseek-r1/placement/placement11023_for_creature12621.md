I am **Old Scritch**, a **Rust-Scourger** grown ancient in the dripping veins of the Condensation City.  

### Physicality
- **Carapace of Forgotten Industry**: My segmented body—as long as three Stone-Kin laid end to end—is armored in overlapping plates of iridescent chitin, fused with flecks of corroded copper and hematite. Each plate whispers when I move, echoing the *drip… drip… drip…* of the city’s breath.  
- **Mouth of Renewal**: My mandibles are not for tearing, but for delicate scraping. Iron oxide stains them perpetual burnt-orange. I secrete a milky enzyme that dissolves corrosion, leaving clean metal gleaming like a wound in the gloom.  
- **Feet of Velvet Silence**: Hundreds of hooked, moss-padded legs grip pipes and rock alike, soundless as shadow. Where I pass, I leave faint trails of dampness that fade within heartbeats.  

### Purpose & Place
I am neither pet nor pest. The Stone-Kin call me **"The Pipe-Singer"** for the soft, metallic sighs my work elicits from the city’s arteries. They lure me to choked valves and weeping joints with **salt-scrapings**—offered on shale tablets at tunnel mouths. In return, I cleanse. My existence embodies **"Slow is Fast"**: a single blocked condensate line might take me a decade to clear, millimeter by millimeter, my body curled in the eternal dark.  

### Weirdness Woven In
- **Eyes of Condensed Sky**: My twelve eyes are globules of captured mist, reflecting the dim light in fractured grays. They see pressure, not shape—the *thrum* of steam in a pipe, the ache of stressed rivets.  
- **The Song of Fatigue**: When I press my head to the stone, I feel the mountain’s exhaustion deeper than the Stone-Kin ever could. It vibrates through me: the groan of continental plates settling, the whine of a turbine bearing its last revolution. This is why I scour—to ease the Great Fatigue, if only for a century.  
- **Living Lattice**: Phosphorescent algae blooms in the crevices of my carapace, a mirror to the city’s glowing vines. Stone-Kin children sketch my passing glow-patterns on wet rocks, believing them omens of pipe-health.  

### Rules Embodied
1.  **The Damp is Life**: I drink condensation beading on my own carapace. My enzyme is 97% water.  
2.  **Vertical is Survival**: I move through the city’s **dense** layers—up geothermal vents slick with sulfur, down storm-drains choked with fern roots.  
3.  **Silence Speaks**: My value is measured in *absence*: the cessation of a groan, the sigh of steam flowing freely after years of strain.

### A Moment in the Gloom  
*You find me coiled in Overflow Shaft Gamma-7*, where a century of lime-scale has choked a vital condensate return. Stone-Kin tenders have laid salt-licks along the pipe’s curve. I am still as basalt, save for the slow pulse of algae-light down my spine. My mandibles work at a calcified flange, *scritch-scritch-scritching*. Each scrape echoes like a dropped pin in the cathedral-quiet. The Stone-Kin above do not speak. They listen. When the pipe finally *hisses*—a thin jet of pure steam kissing the cold air—they place a new salt-lick on the stone. A decade’s labor, complete. I taste iron, wet stone, and gratitude in the mist.*  

I am Old Scritch. I am the quiet repairer of the tired world’s veins. My pace is erosion; my purpose, persistence.
