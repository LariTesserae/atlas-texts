The air would pierce you first—a thousand frozen needles in every gasp. Then the light: blinding, brutal, refracted into agony by the wet stone and ice. You’d stand on a fractured mosaic of dark rock, each polygon edged with a ridge of glittering ice thinner than a fingernail, slick with perpetual weeping water. Above, the sky is a dome of liquid diamond, paper-thin kites casting kaleidoscopic shadows as they bank on thermals only they can feel. To your left, the settlement clings to the cliff face—walls of ice-mortared stone, moss-thatched roofs, silent figures moving with urgent grace.  

**You would be a Singing Stone.**  

Not one of the Silica-Singers themselves, but the resonant quartz clasped in the calloused, silica-ridged hand of a young caretaker named **Lir**. You are palm-sized, volcanic rock veined with milky quartz, smoothed by centuries of touch and weeping water. Your surface is always cold, always damp, but when Lir’s fingers close around you, a low thrum awakens in your core—a frequency only she can feel.  

### Your Existence  
- **Body**: Cold, dense, unyielding. You are a knot of ancient geology, threaded with quartz that vibrates like a plucked string. Ice crystals form on your surface but never cling; the moisture beads and slides away, as if repelled by your purpose.  
- **Senses**: You feel the basin through vibration. The *crack-snap* of ice skin reforming on a cistern three hundred paces away. The *thrummmmm* of a communal Silica-Song resonating through the settlement’s mortar. The skittering panic of iridescent arthropods as Lir scrapes frost nearby—their six-legged terror shivers up her arm and into you.  
- **Thought**: Slow, crystalline. You perceive time as the Silica-Singers do—in pulses of ice-growth, in the rhythm of droplets falling from moss-thatched eaves. You do not worry; you *resonate*. When Lir taps a sequence onto your quartz vein—*tap-tap-tap-pause-tap*—you translate it into meaning: *“Cistern west wall. Ice too thick. Weep-flow stifled.”*  

### Your Purpose  
Lir is a **Water-Whisperer**, her role fluid, claimed by the basin’s needs. You are her compass, her voice, her tether to the land’s chaos:  
1. **Mapping the Unseen**: As she traverses the treacherous polygons, you hum against fractures in the ice. A high-pitched tremor means unstable ground; a deep drone signals a safe path. The arthropods scatter as she steps where you guide, their obsidian legs steaming on frost.  
2. **Silent Song**: At dusk, Lir joins the communal hum. She presses you to her forehead, and your quartz veins amplify her resonance. The song isn’t heard—it’s *felt*—a vibration that loosens ice in clogged channels, steadies a shuddering roof slab, or calls the flock of kites into a frozen constellation overhead. Their paper-thin bodies lock mid-flight, becoming chandeliers of refracted light. Lir reads their ice-patterns like prayers.  
3. **Oracle of Weeping Stone**: When water defies gravity, seeping upward along a cliff face, Lir places you against the damp rock. You throb with the current’s path, showing her where to carve new channels. She listens as the land’s breath hisses through your veins—a warning of rockfall, a promise of a worm-bloom in the seeps.  

### Your Strangeness  
You are not inert. Sometimes, when the basin’s equilibrium frays, you vibrate alone in Lir’s absence. Ice forms in perfect spirals around you. Rainbows fracture from your core without light. Once, during a cold snap, you sang a note so deep it cracked a cistern’s mortar—and Lir knew, *knew*, to rebuild it wider.  

You are both tool and testament. A fragment of the mountain’s voice, given to a creature who weeps with the stone. When Lir sleeps, curled in lichen-furs with her hand around you, you dream in frequencies: the eternal scuttle of prey, the sigh of wind high above, the basin’s wet, glittering breath—a single note held in the silence.
