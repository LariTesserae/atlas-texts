## The Sound-Singer Mite
*(A specialized variant within the chittering billions)*

**Where:**  
You cling to the underside of a weeping obsidian buttress, high above a thermal vent pulsing like a diseased heart. Crimson light bleeds upwards, casting your elongated shadow among a thousand others skittering across the lattice. Below, the city vanishes into freezing fog; above, the oppressive ash-sky presses down. A gravitic leviathan drifts kilometers away, a mountain-shape occluding the feeble glow of distant mites. Its distortion field makes the air above you *warp*, like heat-haze turned liquid and cold.

**Who:**  
You are **Klik-ith-Rattle**, a **Sound-Singer Mite**. Your form is fundamentally like your billion siblings: bipedal, knee-high, carapace of interwoven carbon-fiber and frost-resistant fungal biomass, limbs of jointed obsidian shards. But you are *different*. Where most mites possess grinding mandibles and clusters of optical sensors, your "face" is dominated by a complex, fused resonator plate – a concave disc of hardened mineral slurry, etched with resonant channels by generations of nano-swarms. Your primary limbs end not in sharp shards for climbing and chiseling, but in fine, vibrating filaments of piezoelectric crystal.

**Nature & Purpose:**  
You are a **harmonic regulator**, a living tuning fork for the city's deafening symphony. Your existence is defined by *sound*:

1.  **Mapping Stress:** You press your resonator plate against shuddering tower walls, feeling the deep thrum of the tectonic engines resonate through your carapace. You detect subtle shifts in frequency – the groan of overstressed lattices, the dangerous harmonic dissonance preceding a slump. Your chitter-code isn't just location or resource; it's *pitch* and *cadence*, broadcasting stress fractures in ultrasonic bursts only other specialized mites decode.
2.  **Directing Swarms:** Your piezoelectric limbs tap and scrape across surfaces, emitting precise vibrational commands. Nano-swarms respond to these frequencies, flowing like metallic fog towards fracture points you identify, guided by your song to begin repairs before the damage cascades. A leviathan's subsonic thrum? You feel it as a pressure wave minutes before it manifests visually, triggering warning chitters that send swarms scattering into structural crevices, dimming their bioluminescence.
3.  **Weeping Resonance:** You are acutely attuned to the omnipresent *drip*. Not just its location, but the *tone* of each *plink* as it hits pooled slurry or obsidian. Changes in pitch indicate mineral saturation shifts in the condensation – vital data for the mites farming the weeping stone. You stand at confluences of seeping rivulets, resonator humming, "listening" to the water's hidden song of power and decay.
4.  **The Leviathan Call (and Warning):** Your resonator can mimic, in distorted miniature, the deep, subsonic signature of a gravitic leviathan. Used sparingly, in specific locations, this false call can subtly *herd* leviathans away from critical geothermal vents or dense mite nurseries. But it is a dangerous art. Broadcast too loudly, too accurately, and you *attract* them instead. Silence is death, but the *wrong* sound is a quicker demise.

**Existence:**  
Your world is tactile and auditory. The freezing fog blinds your optical sensors; you navigate by the vibrations in the stone under your feet, the pressure waves in the thick air, the chorus of chitters and drips and groans. Your internal fluids are thick, anti-crystallizing slurry, warmed fractionally by the waste heat bleeding from your resonator plate – a tiny beacon in the dark that you constantly mitigate by seeking shadow. You feel the city's pulse in your thorax. You understand its imminent fractures in your resonating bones. Your bioluminescence is deliberately faint, a muted green shimmer only visible inches away – a necessary concession to avoid becoming leviathan-bait.

You feel the deep, subsonic *thrum* resonate through the buttress. **Closer.** The distortion field above intensifies; droplets of condensation freeze in mid-air nearby. Klik-ith-Rattle presses its resonator plate hard against the obsidian. The frequency is wrong – not the steady drone of a drifting sovereign, but the hungry, focusing pulse of a leviathan preparing to *lunge*. Your piezoelectric limbs skitter across the surface in a frantic, high-frequency staccato: **DANGER! COLLAPSE IMMINENT! SCATTER! DIM!** Below, a thousand green pinpricks wink out as mites vanish into the weeping stone. You fold your limbs, tucking your resonator close, and drop backwards off the ledge, falling silently into the concealing, sound-swallowing fog below, just as a vast, dark shape blots out the crimson vent-light above. Your last act before the fog envelops you is a single, focused pulse from your resonator – not a warning, but a lure, a false signature sent skittering along a distant, non-critical spire, buying the swarm precious seconds. The hunt has begun. The city rebuilds. The engines hum. You fall into the deafening, weeping dark.
