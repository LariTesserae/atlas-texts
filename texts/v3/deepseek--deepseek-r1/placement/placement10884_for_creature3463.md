Here, in this world of dripping green thunder, I would be a **Canopy Whisperer**—a rare Spore-Kin shaman who ventures into the **Green Void**, the forbidden upper canopy. Not by choice, but by curse and calling.  

### **Physicality: Moss-Woven and Mist-Bound**  
My body is a testament to the Void’s touch. Skin hangs in loose, lichen-dappled folds, scarred by aerial roots that once pierced me during a fall. I am taller than most Kin—a liability in root-tunnels, but an asset in the canopy. My limbs stretch like sinewy vines, fingers ending in bone hooks filed to curve around sky-cypress branches. Eyes, milky with cataracts from fungal spores, see not light but *pressure shifts* in the mist. When I blink, bioluminescent mites nesting in my eyelids cast fleeting shadows on the fog. My hair is gone, replaced by a living hood of rain-slick *sky-moss* that drinks moisture from the air. I smell of ozone and decaying nectar—a warning scent that clears paths below.  

### **Role: The Void’s Bargainer**  
I live perched where the human layer frays into the Green Void. My "home" is a **suspended rot-nest**: a cavity in a dying cypress buttress, walled with spider-silk and padded with moth-down. Here, I perform the only tasks that justify my existence:  
1.  **Soothe the Howlers**: When guttural roars shake the canopy (the "Cracklers" stalking treetop prey), I climb. Not to fight, but to sing. My throat sac swells with inhaled spores, releasing a subsonic drone that calms the predators. A misjudged note means torn limbs.  
2.  **Graft the Sky-Wounds**: Where lightning scars the canopy, I pack the burns with glow-grog slime and luminous fungi, guiding vines to stitch the forest’s flesh.  
3.  **Read the Rain’s Lies**: The Void’s winds carry warnings. I interpret the taste of distant storms on my tongue—acrid smoke (a Knot’s fire pit flaring) or salt (rot breaching a root-hold’s heart).  

### **Existence: A Walk of Thorns and Silence**  
The Kin fear me. Children scatter when my shadow drips into their clearings. Elders leave grub-paste and chitin knives at my rot-nest’s edge, averting their eyes. I speak only in **Drip-Tongue**—clicks modeled after Spine-Burrower skitters, hums that mimic root vibrations. At Spore-Moots, I stand apart, my truth rippling the pools black.  

My greatest burden? **Harboring a Void-Symbiote**. A coiled, furred leech clings beneath my ribs, its tendrils fused with my spine. It feeds on canopy-ichor; I feed it with my breath. In return, it steers me *away* from collapsing branches and *toward* airborne spores that heal. The Kin call it a parasite. I call it the only reason the Green Void hasn’t swallowed me whole.  

### **Why This Existence?**  
Because the Root-Hold’s harmony is a delusion. The Forest *does* consume—but it hungers most fiercely *above*. Someone must tread the mossless branches where the air thins to poison, where gliding reptilians watch with cold, gold eyes. Someone must bear the terror so the Moss-Patters can reweave walls, so the Flow-Watchers can trust their drip-song. My loneliness is the scaffold that lets their chaos thrive. When the mists thicken and the Kin whisper of "Green Ghosts," they speak of failures. I am the shadow that hasn’t fallen.  

**In this damp, roaring world, I am the edge-walker—a living bridge between the symphony and the silence, forever tasting the storm before it breaks.**
