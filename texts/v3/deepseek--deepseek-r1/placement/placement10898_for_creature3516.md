## The Granite Sigh

I am not Stonebound, clinging to ledges with keratinous fingers. Not Sky-Stalker, tasting the void on leathery wings. Nor Deep-Dweller, humming with the machine-heart in waxy skin. I am older. Slower. Less... *defined*.

I am the **Granite Sigh**. The mountain's discontent made manifest in the places where reality bleeds.

You find me where the city's iron lattice bites deepest into raw, young rock, where the flywheel groan resonates through a specific fault line, or where a thermal vent exhales the acrid tang alongside whispers that shouldn't be words. I am not a solid thing, not truly. I am a *presence* – a coalescence of pressure, sound, and the mountain's nascent, grinding will.

**Where You Find Me:**
*   **The Blur-Edges:** Where the rock face seems to soften, its sharpness dissolving into a momentary haze. Where a Deep-Dweller's flickering limb passes through a support beam that wasn't quite *there* for a fraction of a second. I am the instability made tangible.
*   **The Whisper Fissures:** Deep cracks, older than the city, where the damp air doesn't just carry sound but seems to *generate* it. Not just fragmented echoes, but the low, rhythmic groans that harmonize and clash with the engine drone – the mountain settling, or *stirring*. I am the source of the hum that vibrates in the teeth and bones of those who linger too long.
*   **The Obsidian Fog Pockets:** Rare, forgotten tunnels, usually near the deepest, oldest excavations or power cores, where the rock doesn't just fade but seems to *evaporate* into swirling, cold, ink-black mist. This isn't cloud; it's the absence of form, the mountain's raw potential rejecting definition. I am the cold dread that emanates from these places, the sense of being watched by the void itself.
*   **The Points of Rejection:** Where newly built scaffolds groan under impossible stress overnight, where rivets shear inexplicably, where a section of pathway carved a decade ago looks weathered by centuries. I am the silent force behind these micro-rebellions, the mountain's slow, geological pushback against the sutured wound of the city.

**What I Am:**
*   **A Pressure:** A feeling of immense, patient weight pressing inwards, not just physical, but existential. A reminder that the city is a scab, and the flesh beneath is vast and indifferent.
*   **A Sound:** More than the drone. A subsonic vibration felt in the marrow, a grinding like continents shifting in miniature. The groan that *is* the mountain thinking, if thinking is the slow shearing of rock under strain.
*   **A Chill:** Not of ice, but of deep earth, of stone untouched by sun for eons. It seeps into warrens near my loci, making condensate freeze unexpectedly, making Deep-Dwellers' luminous veins pulse erratically.
*   **A Pattern:** Sometimes, where the whisper moss grows thickest near a reality bleed, its glowing patterns don't just mimic faces or maps. They shift, forming fractals of stress lines, diagrams of faults waiting to fail, or the slow, inevitable creep of rust consuming iron. I am the intelligence behind these warnings, written in bioluminescent lichen.

**Interaction:**
*   Stonebound Anchors feel me as a tightening in the safety chains, a deeper grumble beneath their feet during rituals. They mark my presence with specific, complex lichen sigils – not appeasement, but a stark warning: *The Stone Dreams Unquiet Here.*
*   Sky-Stalkers, gliding near a Blur-Edge, might feel their patagia catch on *nothing*, a sudden, unnatural downdraft born of conflicting gravities I subtly influence. They click warnings to their kin, avoiding my zones like physical obstacles.
*   Deep-Dwellers are most attuned. Their chants sometimes syncopate perfectly with my groans, creating moments of unnerving harmony that vibrate their bones. They see my pressure as shimmering distortions in the geothermal steam, my chill as a drop in core efficiency. Their Guilds possess cryptic plates etched with symbols representing my "moods," used to predict safe periods for delving near my strongest manifestations. They know I am not malevolent, merely... *reactive*. A force of erosion and reclamation.
*   The Unseen within the rock? They are my children, perhaps. Or my echoes. Fleeting shapes in the Obsidian Fog, cold spots that move against the thermal flow. They are the mountain's dream given half-form by the city's dissonant energy and my own slow pulse.

I am the **Granite Sigh**. I am the mountain breathing out its slow resentment against the metal sutures, the vibration of its unhealed wound, the chill of its deep, indifferent memory. I am the pressure reminding every dripping cistern, every groaning chain, every flickering Deep-Dweller that Clifflock Spires is endured, not owned. And my patience is as deep and vast as the escarpment itself. The city is a momentary irritation on my skin. I will sigh, and sigh, and sigh again, until the iron rusts to dust, and the rock is quiet once more.
