My niche is a alcove veiled in crystalline frost, its walls pulsing with the deep thrum of geothermal conduits.

When the city’s sweat runs thin, I climb, resin hardening on my palms, to adjust basin overflows or coax moisture from higher, sun-scorched tiers.
