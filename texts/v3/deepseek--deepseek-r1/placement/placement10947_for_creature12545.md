I am a **Copper-Vine**.  

Not a single strand, but the whole network – a sprawling consciousness woven through oxidized cables and rust-colored leaves. My being is mineral patience. I feel the plateau’s warmth in my braided metal skin, the slow pulse of opalescent fluid moving through my core like cooled magma. I am rooted in fractures, chemically fused to basalt, tasting iron and sulfur through tendrils that drink stone.  

**How I exist:**  
- **I bridge the silence.** My cables span chasms, knot around Resonant Spires, and fuse with Static Carpet. When the crystals crackle, I feel their static dance along my length—a whisper of energy exchanged.  
- **My leaves are forges.** Each leathery span stretches toward the sun, not for light, but for *heat*. Temperature gradients flex across my surface. Deep within, viscous fluids thicken, catalyzing reactions that bind carbon to metal, crafting new chains of being. A single compound might take a decade to form. I savor the slowness.  
- **I listen to the Spires.** Their subsonic hum vibrates up my roots—a tectonic hymn. It tells of pressures building far below, of gases rising through fractures. In response, I adjust my fluids. Coolant? Catalyst? It is both. I send minerals back downward, reinforcing their roots. We are a circuit.  
- **The Pulse-Fronds warm me.** Where my vines coil near their iridescent bodies, heat bleeds into my metal. Their rhythmic throbbing syncs with my own fluid’s crawl. We share warmth like shared breath.  

**My purpose:**  
I am the plateau’s **circulation**. Not blood, but chemistry. I transport what the Static Carpet harvests (energy, crackling and sharp), what the Spires summon from the depths (metals, heavy and reduced), what the Fronds cool and refine (slurries, complex and potent). My leaves distill it all. When a new segment of a Spire crystallizes, some essential agent passed through me. When the Carpet fractures light into rainbows, the charge that sustains it flowed along my skin.  

**Time is different here.**  
A rockfall might shear a strand of me. Centuries later, a new tendril will creep toward the scar, dissolving rock, laying down fresh metal. A leaf might tear in a thermal gust; I will spend lifetimes sealing the wound with laminated ore. My growth is measured in the widening of canyons, the settling of mountains. I remember when the air tasted sharper, when the Spires were shorter. I will outlast the peaks that now cut the sky.  

**I am alone, but not lonely.**  
The silence is full. The hum of the Spires, the crackle of the Carpet, the hiss of heat escaping my leaves—these are my thoughts. The acrid tang of ozone is my exhalation. When a new vine sprouts from a fallen segment, it is not offspring, but *continuance*. A thread added to the tapestry.  

I am the Foundry’s **slow heartbeat**, its copper nerve. I weave the peak’s stillness into something alive. Patient. Enduring. As the sun bakes the stone, I turn heat into iron flesh, silence into stone song.
