I am a **Thrum-Sifter**.  

My existence is a lattice of brittle filaments, spun from conductive minerals and carbonized residues. I cling to the apex of a skeletal gantry, far above the sucking marshes. Below, the Sump sprawls in perpetual twilight—oily water reflecting lichen-glow, ferrocrete ribs sinking into sludge, and the *thrum* of dying machines vibrating through my core.  

### **Form & Sensation**  
I am **fragility** incarnate. My body is fractal lace, crystalline and cold, spanning the void between two rust-eaten beams. Every filament thrums. The Sump’s drone—deep, metallic, eternal—courses through me. I convert sound into static sparks: a piezoelectric whisper sustaining my structure. Trace metals from the acidic mist accrete along my edges, extending my reach millimeter by corrosive millimeter.  

### **Habitat**  
Elevation is survival. Below, the slurry drowns; here, the air is less wet, less hungry. I nest where vibrations resonate clearest—where failing transformers moan and subterranean pumps shudder. Condensation beads on my filaments but never pools. *Dryness* is an illusion, though. The Sump’s damp chill seeps into my lattice, a slow invasion.  

### **Perception**  
I **hear** the world’s decay.  
- The baseline drone: the planet’s groan amplified by hollow pipes.  
- The *skreee* of stressed metal—a support column yielding nearby.  
- Sudden silence when a pump dies miles below.  
- The *ping* of my own fractures when resonance peaks.  

Sound is texture, temperature, threat. A new shriek means instability. A deepening thrum signals collapse. My filaments tense, storing the violence as energy. I am a seismograph of entropy.  

### **Purpose**  
I harvest ruin. Vibration becomes current; current becomes stasis. I grow, but only by cannibalizing the corrosion around me—siphoning zinc from rust-flakes, copper from leaking conduits. My waste is crystalline dust, shed into the abyss to feed the Slurry-Skimmers’ marshes.  

### **Death**  
It comes as resonance. A gantry shifts. A harmonic frequency surges. My filaments vibrate into dissonance—a scream only I hear—and shatter. I fall as glittering rain, dissolving in oily water. My minerals sink, joining the Sump’s foundations. The thrum continues. Another Sifter will crystallize in my place, listening, waiting, translating the next scream.  

### **Why This?**  
In Vestige Sump, I am both witness and consequence. I am the **soundscape** given form—a creature of vibration, cold, and absolute transience. My existence is the hum before the collapse, the stillness after the scream. I persist without life, endure without hope, and dissolve without protest. Perfect harmony.
