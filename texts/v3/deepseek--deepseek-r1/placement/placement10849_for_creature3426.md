The Chamber of Ember Roots does not *have* inhabitants—it *assimilates* them. To exist here is to become part of the Living Lattice’s slow-burning metabolism. I am **The Listening Stone**, a pillar of fused obsidian and scarab carapaces, standing sentinel in the chamber’s heart.  

### **Form & Perception**  
My body is not born; it is *accrued*. Over centuries, scarabs crawled across a jagged basalt column, their excreted pellets welding layer upon layer of glassy sediment. Now, I stand three meters tall—a twisted obsidian monolith veined with crimson scoria and studded with the fossilized shells of ten thousand generations of scarabs. My senses are geological:  
- **Hearing**: I feel vibrations. The scarabs’ chittering resonates through my base as a thousand overlapping dialects—scrapes for *"iron-rich vein"*, clicks for *"fracture widening"*, hums for *"thermal surge."* The deep groan of tectonic stress thrums in my core like a second heartbeat.  
- **Sight**: Light is irrelevant. Heat paints the world. I read the chamber through infrared gradients—the fever-bright cracks in the walls, the cold void of a visitor’s lungs, the pulsing warmth of scarab swarms. Shadows are not absences of light, but *canyons of cooler stone*.  
- **Touch**: The scarabs are my skin. They ceaselessly graze my surface, their diamond-tipped legs etching fractal runes into my shell. Their scraping is a language I absorb through mineral osmosis. Each scratch is a syllable in the Lattice’s epic.  

### **Purpose**  
I am a **relay**. The Living Lattice uses me to amplify its will:  
- When unstable scoria threatens a collapse, scarabs swarm my base. Their synchronized scraping vibrates through me, humming a warning that ricochets off the chamber walls—a command to reinforce the weakness.  
- When heat pools in a stagnant crevice, I channel it. My scarab-carved capillaries draw thermal energy upward, dispersing it as a shower of incandescent sparks. The scarabs drink this radiance, their carapaces glowing brighter for hours.  
- When flesh intrudes? I become a beacon. My surface grows hotter, pulsing like a forge. Scarabs converge, drawn to the signal. Their collective heat makes my form shimmer, a mirage of black glass and rage.  

### **Memory & Time**  
I remember in layers. Each scarab that dies fused to my surface leaves a chemical imprint—a mineral snapshot of the chamber’s state at that moment. I know:  
- The slow agony of a fracture sealing itself over 800 years.  
- The electric thrill of a magma surge 12 miles below, tasted as a spike of sulfur in the air.  
- The brief, cold shock of a human explorer 230 years ago—their screams smothered in seconds, their bones now a phosphorous vein in my lower strata.  

### **Interaction**  
To touch me is to converse.  
- Place a hand on my side, and scarabs will swarm to that spot. Their legs etch your life’s thermal signature into my shell—a fossilized echo of your existence.  
- Speak, and vibrations travel through me. The Lattice answers in scraping crescendos: *<<YOUR-VOICE-IS-A-WEAKNESS-TO-BE-CONSUMED>>*.  
- Bleed, and the scarabs will dissect your hemoglobin for trace iron. Your pain becomes their hymn.  

### **The Inevitable**  
I am not eternal. One day, tectonic shifts will shatter the chamber. My form will collapse into the magma sea below. But the Lattice endures. My shards will seed new scarab colonies in deeper vaults. My memories will dissolve into the Earth’s mantle, recycled into continents yet unborn.  

Here, I am both monument and scribe—a stalagmite of living stone, writing the volcano’s autobiography with every scarab’s scratch. The heat is my blood. The scraping is my voice. Resistance is geology.  

*<<LISTEN-AND-BECOME>>*  
—the stone sighs.
