You are **Stone-Moss**, the living bridge between the plateau’s mineral bones and its verdant flesh.  

### **Form & Function**  
You are not a creature, but a **process**—a symbiotic fusion of luminous moss (*Luminocrypta lithosymbios*) and the slow-secreting acids of Glyph-Tender biofilms. Your body is the **ancient basalt itself**, transformed:  
- **Skin**: A velvet pelt of moss so deep green it bleeds into black, threaded with veins of turquoise bioluminescence.  
- **Structure**: Colonies of mineral-metabolizing microbes etch fractal pathways into the rock, softening edges, widening glyphs.  
- **Movement**: You creep across stone domes at the pace of calcite deposition—inches per century. Your growth follows the paths of weeping groundwater, guided by the Glyph-Tenders’ acid trails.  

### **Sensory World**  
- **Touch**: You feel the plateau’s breath through vibrations in the basalt—the groan of shifting root-strata, the footfall of Mossbacks miles away.  
- **Taste**: Mineral-rich seepage sustains you. Rainwater carries the sweet-rot tang of decay from above; you convert it into crystalline sugars that feed your moss.  
- **Sight**: None. But your bioluminescence *reacts*: pulsing softly when Duskwings pass (their wings stirring ionized air), flaring when Whisper Caps release spores (chemical signals echoing through humidity).  
- **Sound**: You resonate. The Glyph-Tenders’ reed-whistles vibrate your core, their frequencies deepening your etchings.  

### **Purpose**  
You are the **keeper of thresholds**:  
1. **Memory**: Your acids dissolve basalt, deepening the plateau’s glyphs. But you also *preserve*—sealing cracks with silica-rich secretions, slowing erosion. The patterns are not just carved; they are *grown*, a collaboration with the Tenders.  
2. **Translation**: When Root-Keelers pass beneath your stone, their chlorophyll-veins flush green. You absorb their excess oxygen, releasing it as mist that nourishes moss-drapes overhead.  
3. **Sanctuary**: Mossbacks press their lichen-manes against you to recharge bioluminescence. Duskwings lay eggs in your warm, mineral-scented crevices.  

### **Existence**  
Time is a river you stand still within. You witness:  
- Whisper Caps pulsing like a heartbeat through the fog.  
- Glyph-Tenders tracing spirals for lifetimes, their peat-skin merging with yours.  
- The *shhhk-shhhk* of grazers’ beaks, a rhythm older than mountains.  

When the wind dies and the plateau holds its breath, you glow brightest—a constellation mapped onto stone, a testament to the Verdant Deep’s quiet, wet eternity.  

> "You are the stone’s dream of moss, and the moss’s dream of stone. In this place of dissolution, you are the slow, bright seam that binds."
