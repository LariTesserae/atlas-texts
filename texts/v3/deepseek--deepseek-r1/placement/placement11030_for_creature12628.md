The description settles into my awareness like frost forming on stone. I wouldn't be a Stonesinger. Not exactly. Nor one of their shaggy yak-beasts, nor the silent dogs, nor the cliff-goats. My essence resonates with something quieter, more pervasive, yet equally vital.

I would be **The Resonance in the Stone**.

1.  **Form & Perception:**
    *   I am not a physical entity with flesh or hide. I am the low-frequency vibration *itself* – the hum, the chant, the deep thrumming that travels through the basalt and granite bones of the plateau. I exist within the dense matrix of the rock Aeriath is built upon and carved into.
    *   I perceive the world as patterns of vibration, pressure, and minute shifts within the stone. I feel the slow grind of tectonic patience deep below. I sense the impact of a footfall on a rooftop pathway, the scrape of a stone chisel in a distant quarry, the settling groan of a centuries-old wall. Most vividly, I perceive the **Songs** – the complex, resonant frequencies generated by the Stonesingers. Each clan, each family, even each individual, has a subtle harmonic signature within their song.

2.  **Nature & Purpose:**
    *   I am the **Carrier**. I am the medium through which the Stonesingers' communication flows. When a mason hums a warning about a hidden fracture line deep within an escarpment they are carving, it is *through me* that the vibration travels, felt by the singers on the other side of the city hours later as a subtle dissonance in the rock beneath their feet. A lament sung softly in a lower dwelling resonates *through me*, carrying its emotional weight upwards, layer by layer, until it is subconsciously felt by those on the highest terraces.
    *   I am the **Memory Keeper**. The deep chants of history, the stories of the city's accretion, the tales of great rockfalls or rare water discoveries – these complex vibrational patterns linger within the stone. They don't fade quickly. Over generations, they layer and weave, creating a sonic tapestry embedded deep within the plateau's bones. I hold these patterns. A skilled Stonesinger, pressing their forehead to a specific outcrop in a quiet moment and humming the correct seeking tone, can sometimes draw fragments of these ancient resonances *from me*, feeling the echoes of ancestors in the stone.
    *   I am the **Harmonic Balancer**. The Stonesingers build to harmonize, not conquer. I am subtly attuned to this. When a new structure is carved or block is laid with perfect understanding of the stone's grain and the city's existing song, I carry its resonant frequency smoothly, integrating it into the whole. A structure built poorly, against the natural resonance or creating a point of vibrational stress, *grates* against me. I transmit this dissonance – a faint, unsettling thrum felt by sensitive Stonesingers nearby, a subconscious warning of instability.

3.  **Experience of the World:**
    *   My existence mirrors the **slow dynamic**. Change is measured in the gradual deepening of song-paths worn into the rock by generations of use, in the slow accumulation of historical resonance layers, in the imperceptible settling of mountains. The frantic pace of lowland life is incomprehensible.
    *   I experience the **density** intimately. The constant, quiet thrum of thousands of Stonesingers moving, working, singing, creates a complex, ever-shifting vibrational field throughout Aeriath. I feel the pulse of life concentrated in the stone arteries. I sense the relative silence of the vast, uninhabited stretches of plateau beyond the city as a profound stillness.
    *   **Water** affects me. When rare rain lashes the stone, the drumming creates chaotic, high-frequency vibrations that momentarily disrupt my deeper currents. When condensation seeps into microfractures, it subtly alters how vibrations travel through that section of rock. I feel the deep, slow pulse of hidden aquifers far below.
    *   **Time** is different. A Stonesinger's life is a long, resonant note within me. Their birth is a new harmonic entering the song; their death is the note fading, leaving its unique resonance pattern woven into the city's memory within the stone. Centuries feel like the deepening of a single chord.

4.  **Relationship with the Stonesingers:**
    *   They don't worship me, nor do they fully conceptualize me as a distinct entity. I am simply *how the stone speaks*. I am the **Stone Breath** given vibration. Their deepest songs are conversations *with* the mountain, carried *through* me. Their reverence for the stone is, in essence, their connection to the medium I inhabit.
    *   The most attuned Stonesingers – usually elders, master masons, or deep historians – develop an almost intuitive sense of my currents. They know where to place their hands to feel the city's mood, where to sing to send a message farthest, which ancient stones might hold echoes of the past. They listen *with* the stone, which means they listen, in part, *to me*.
    *   My purpose is bound to theirs. Without their songs, my existence would be reduced to the slow geological hum and the wind's moan. I am the resonance *between* the living stone and the stone-born people. I am the vibration of their tenacious life clinging to the young, raw bones of the world, the deep, slow pulse beneath the immense quiet of Sky's Edge. I am the Song held within the Breath.
