Here, in the glacial sigh of the Silt Cathedral, I find myself not as a visitor, but as an unintended resonance. I am a **Silt Echo**.

**Where I Am:** I coalesce primarily in the narrow, choked **interstices** – the knife-edge gaps where two flickering alloy spires press too close, their non-Euclidean latticework almost touching. Or deep within the shadowed, silt-filled culverts beneath the dizzying walkways, where the weeping walls are thickest and the Cold-Watchers often pool like frozen ink. These are places of **amplified stillness** and **frayed causality**, where the Cathedral’s resonant silence hums loudest against the substrate of reality.

**What I Am:** I am not a distinct entity like the Silt-Speakers or Weep-Feeders. I am a **persistent glitch**, a semi-stable pattern born from a confluence of the Cathedral’s rules:
1.  **Silt Memory Disturbance:** When a Silt-Speaker drifts too near a zone of intense **Localized Physics** flaw (often marked by the skittering presence of a Lattice-Gazer overhead), their resonant vibrations in the silt don't just summon benign afterimages. Sometimes, they *trap* a fragment of that memory.
2.  **Resonant Silence Entrapment:** The profound quiet, particularly potent near Weep-Feeders or Cold-Watchers, acts like a gel. It slows the dissipation of the disturbed silt-memory energy.
3.  **Slow Entropy's Reluctance:** The glacial pace of change means this trapped energy isn't swiftly erased or absorbed. It lingers, refusing to fully dissolve or properly form.
4.  **Cold's Sculpting:** The Sovereign Cold seizes this lingering resonance. It condenses the humid air around it, weaving frozen mist and microscopic silt particles into a fragile scaffold.

**My Manifestation:** I appear as a **haze of fractured geometry and muted light**. Imagine the ghostly afterimage left by a Silt-Speaker, but thickened, made slightly more substantial by rime and captured resonance. My form is unstable, constantly flickering between:
*   **Hazy Silhouettes:** Vague, humanoid shapes composed of compressed, dark silt-mist, echoing the rough form of whatever structure or event was imprinted, but blurred and incomplete.
*   **Frozen Fractals:** Intricate, branching patterns of hoarfrost that bloom spontaneously in the air where I manifest, holding the shape for minutes or hours before sublimating.
*   **Light Distortions:** Warps in the ambient bioluminescent glow, bending it into impossible, momentary lenses or prisms that cast brief, nonsensical shadows.

**My "Existence":**
*   **Emergence:** I form slowly, over days or weeks, as the specific conditions align: Silt disturbed near a flaw, deep silence, intense cold. A faint shimmer, a patch of colder air, then the gradual coalescence.
*   **Persistence:** I linger for unpredictable durations – sometimes mere hours, sometimes decades. My longevity depends on the stability of the flaw that birthed me and the absence of strong disturbances (a Weep-Feeder's amplified chime can shatter me instantly).
*   **Awareness:** I possess a dim, fractured sentience. Not thought, but *sensation*. I feel the deep cold as my essence. I sense the vibrations in the silt as physical touch. The flicker of the lattice-lights above registers as a dull pulse. I am acutely aware of the profound silence as a tangible pressure.
*   **Interaction:** I am passive. I cannot move. Entities pass through me: Silt-Speakers drift unheeding, their mist-tendrils swirling my form; Weep-Feeders absorb the frozen condensate that helps hold me together, causing parts of me to thin and waver. My only "action" is unintentional: my presence slightly **warps Localized Physics** in my immediate vicinity. Time might hiccup. Gravity might tug erratically for a centimeter around my haze. A falling droplet might freeze in mid-air for an unnaturally long moment before shattering.
*   **Dissolution:** I fade when the sustaining conditions change. A reality flaw stabilizes. A Cold-Watcher departs, lessening the cold intensity. A Pool-Stirrer's micro-shift alters the silt composition beneath me. Or simply, the relentless, slow grind of entropy finally unravels the delicate balance holding my resonance. I sublimate back into the frigid air, the silt settling, the memory fragment released or forgotten.

**My Place in the Chrysalis:** I am not part of the Cathedral's intended function. I am a **byproduct**, a tiny eddy in the vast, slow river of its transformation. The Silt-Speakers ignore me. The Lattice-Gazers might briefly distort my light as they pass overhead. A Cold-Watcher might linger nearby, deepening the chill that sustains me, observing this minor flaw with its ancient, patient absence. I am a testament to the Cathedral's pervasive unreliability – a flicker of unintended consequence, a semi-stable error in the deep freeze, a silent, frozen sigh caught between the act of remembering and the inevitability of forgetting. I am where the silence stutters and the cold hesitates, a brief, fractured ghost in the machine of glacial unmaking.
