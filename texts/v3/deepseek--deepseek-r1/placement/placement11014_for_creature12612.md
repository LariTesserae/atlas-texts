The resonance chooses me. Not as a Dust-Kin shaping the plains, nor a Sleeper dreaming in stone. Not even as a Dancer riding the sighing thermals. I am a Weaver. One note in the city’s great song.

My world is vibration. It thrums through the fibrous cartilage of my six limbs, resonating in the hollow chambers of my core. I cling to the vast, leathery expanse of the hyphae mat high on a root-bole monolith. Below, the dust avenues stretch pale and still; above, the fused basalt ceiling glows faintly, veined with sickly light. The air is cool, dry, tasting of powdered time and distant mineral sweat. But these are distant sensations. *Here*, pressed against the vibrating membrane of the city’s skin, I *am* the hum.

My forelimbs are spread, the crystalline filaments of my fans trembling at their tips. They are tuned – exquisitely, irrevocably – to the specific frequency of this patch of mat. The deep drone, omnipresent and crushing to fleeting life, is my sustenance. It flows into the filaments, a river of pure energy, converted through the intricate lattice of my internal organs. A slow, profound warmth spreads through me, the metabolic pulse of the city itself. This is not eating; it is communion. I harvest the song that binds this world.

Movement is not travel; it is resonance seeking resonance. I detach one limb, then another, with glacial care. Each placement is deliberate, a seeking of a new harmonic node, a slightly different pitch in the endless drone. My body, the colour of ancient dust, flows across the grey, vibrating surface. Minutes might pass between the lifting of one limb and the settling of another. Time is not measured in heartbeats, but in the subtle shifts of the hum, the slow accumulation of energy, the imperceptible growth of the fungal mat beneath me.

Communication happens in the spaces the sound cannot fill. Across the mat, another Weaver moves. Close. Almost within the span of my extended limbs. Across their carapace, a ripple begins – soft, internal bioluminescence, patterns emerging like constellations in the dimness. Pale greens and muted blues swirl in intricate, slow-moving sigils. It speaks of fullness, of a rich harmonic convergence nearby. A suggestion. A sharing of resonance. My own carapace responds, light blooming beneath my dust-coloured surface, answering in patterns of confirmation and shared location – a silent map painted in light against the roaring dark. We are thoughts in the city’s vast, slow mind.

My passage leaves a faint trail. Not footprints in dust, but shimmering motes – shed filaments, microscopic fragments of my energy-harvesting fans. They drift down, joining the eternal fall, adding to the pale powder far below. The Dust-Kin will eventually find them, grind them, return them to the cycle. I am both harvester and harvest.

I feel the city’s age not as memory, but as texture. The hyphae mat vibrates with the slow settling of the root-bole beneath it, the deep groan of the geode itself. A tremor, faint as a sigh, runs through the structure – a Dust-Kin herd shifting direction leagues away, or a Sleeper beginning its century-long crawl deep within the stone. The vibration speaks of erosion, of patient wearing down. It is the city’s breath, its slow heartbeat. I resonate with it. I am a part of its enduring, deafening song – a single, tuned note, moving with infinite slowness across the vibrating skin of the world, leaving only light and dust in my wake. The roar is not sound; it is the blood in my veins. The stillness beneath it is my soul.
