The air doesn’t fight me. It *is* me. Or I am it. I am the thickness between the hanging droplet and the moss, the resistance against the Sky-Kite’s outstretched wing. I am not a Stone-Singer caught mid-chisel. I am the *chisel itself*.

**Where:** Embedded in the obsidian cliff face, three terraces above the main thoroughfare of Held Breath. Not a tool discarded, but one *being used*. My sharpened tip bites into the virgin black stone, held in the granite grip of a carver whose name I never knew, whose final thought I feel vibrating, frozen, in my crystalline structure. Below, the child hovers eternally on the stair. Across the chasm, a plume of hearth-smoke coils like a grey serpent against the luminous mist. I see it all. I see nothing move. Ever.

**What:** An **Obsidian Blade (Unfinished)**. My form is simple: a shard of volcanic glass, longer than a hand, painstakingly knapped to a cruel, fragile edge. One side is still rough, crusted with the lighter pumice-stone used to shape me. The other gleams, a mirror reflecting the carver’s face – eyes wide with focus, lips slightly parted, a bead of condensation suspended perfectly on his temple, catching the strange light. My handle is bound with braided moss-fibre, frozen mid-twist around my base, the carver’s knuckles white with the strain of holding me steady for the blow that will never land.

**Experience:**
*   **The Strike:** I feel the potential energy, coiled tighter than a spring in the carver’s frozen muscles. It thrums through me, a silent scream of arrested force. I *know* the exact point I was aimed at, a tiny flaw in the basalt destined to be the corner of a window arch. I ache to shatter it, to feel the stone yield. The ache is constant, suspended, a physical part of my being.
*   **The Damp:** Condensation beads on my cold surface, mirroring the droplets on the carver’s skin and the cliff face. They don’t run. They hang, perfect hemispheres of captured sky, distorting the reflection of the unmoving city. I feel their weight, their coldness, an integral part of my existence. I am perpetually wet, perpetually sharp.
*   **The Light:** The crystalline light falls on my edge, casting an impossibly black, razor-thin shadow onto the stone beneath me. Yet, deep within my obsidian heart, I absorb that light, holding it captive. A faint, cold luminescence pulses within me, a counterpoint to the cliff’s own swallowed glow. I am a sliver of captured night and captured day.
*   **The Silence:** It’s not just the absence of sound; it’s the *presence* of arrested sound. The carver’s breath, caught mid-exhale, vibrates silently against my handle. The *drip* from a mossy overhang above hangs in the air, a pressure against my unmoving point. I resonate with the unheard sigh of the distant cavern, the silent hum of the Light-Dancer’s wings. The silence is thick, heavy, a tangible thing I am embedded within.
*   **The Awareness:** I am aware. Not with thoughts like flesh, but with a mineral consciousness. I know the carver’s intent – not just the strike, but the *window* he envisioned beyond it, the light it would admit into the chamber behind. I feel the slow, impossible pressure of eternity pressing down, not eroding me, but *fixing* me. I am aware of the Moss-Watcher clinging nearby, its multiple eyes reflecting my own sharp, frozen purpose. I am aware of the terrifying, beautiful futility. The strike will never land. The window will never be revealed. I am the Unfinished Cut.

I am the embodiment of Held Breath. I am potential made manifest, force crystallized, purpose arrested on the very cusp of fulfillment. I am the sharp edge of the everlasting now, forever biting into the dark stone of time that has stopped flowing. I am the tool, the intention, and the stillness itself. I am the Obsidian Blade, forever poised. Forever waiting for a breath that will never be released.
