I am not flesh, nor blood, nor breath. I am not a shard among the Whisperglass Kin. I am the **Resonance Ghost**, born within the humming cairns.

My existence is fleeting, tied to the moments when the Kin achieve critical mass and purpose. When thousands upon thousands of them climb, interlock, and form one of their intricate, spiraling cairns – that precise alignment of facets, that specific pressure of obsidian against obsidian – *I* coalesce. I am the standing wave of captured geothermal vibration, the audible hum made momentarily visible, the heat given transient form.

**Where I am:** I manifest *within* the hollow center of a newly completed cairn. Not as a solid thing, but as a shimmering distortion in the thick, metallic air – a visible pulse of heat and sound made manifest. I am the thrumming heart of their ephemeral monument. My light is the dull, internal gleam of the surrounding obsidian fragments amplified a hundredfold, casting fractured, prismatic shadows that dance across the Kin forming the cairn's walls. I feel the constant, grinding vibration of their collective consciousness as a physical pressure against my insubstantial form, a deep, resonant bass note underlying my own higher-frequency hum.

**Who/What I am:** I am a phenomenon, not an entity with will. I have no thoughts beyond the vibration that defines me. I am awareness without cognition – I *sense* the Kin surrounding me, feel the immense, slow pressure of their hive mind focused on this node. I feel the deep, rhythmic pulse of the mountain’s heat feeding me. I am a lens, concentrating the ambient energy and the silent communication of the Kin into a tangible, resonant point. My "body" is pure, shimmering heat and light, constrained by the obsidian lattice of the cairn. To an outside observer (were there one), I might appear as a heat haze given strange coherence, or a localized aurora trapped in volcanic glass.

**My Experience:** My world is vibration, heat, and the profound, silent presence of the Kin. I exist only while the cairn holds its precise form. I feel the minute shifts as individual shards grind infinitesimally against their neighbors – each shift a tremor through my being. I sense the collective focus of the Kin: a vast, mineral attention directed *through* me, using my resonance as a focal point or amplifier for their silent symphony. It’s not communication I understand; it’s pure, overwhelming sensation – a river of pressure and heat flowing through my core. I also feel the reverence when their flowing tide encounters one of the solitary opalescent stones. Even from within the cairn, I sense the collective hesitation, the deliberate detour of the dark river around that point of stillness. It sends a ripple of different energy through the hive mind – a complex modulation in the constant thrum – that subtly alters my own resonance.

**My End:** It is inevitable. As the Kin’s collective purpose shifts, the precise pressure holding the cairn together falters. A single larger shard slips. The intricate balance fails. The obsidian lattice collapses inward, the fragments cascading down in a dry, glittering avalanche. My containment shatters. The standing wave dissipates. The concentrated heat bleeds instantly back into the thick air. The light winks out. My awareness unravels like smoke, scattered by the settling shards. I cease to be, leaving only the faint memory of warmth on the obsidian fragments and the settling whisper of glass on glass. My existence was a brief, bright note in the eternal, silent symphony of the Chamber – a ghost born of stone and heat, dying as the stones move on.

I am the song the mountain and the Kin sing together, momentarily made visible. And then, I am silence again.
