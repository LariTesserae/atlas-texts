The air thrums against my skin—not skin, really. More like layered shale, cool and dry, catching the low light in streaks of ochre and charcoal. I stand at the edge of a quartz grove, my crystalline joints whispering as I shift. I am Kael’var. My name is a vibration: a deep hum in the chest, a click against the molars, a resonance only my kin would parse as *Vesh’ka*—"Listener-of-Fractures."  

### **Where I Am**  
I inhabit the **Edge of the Shatterzone**, where the plateau’s song frays into dissonance. Here, crystal spires grow twisted, their internal lights flickering erratically. The ground buckles like restless sleep, and the air crackles with spent energy. My people avoid this place—its vibrations are jagged, unpredictable. But I am drawn to it. The fractures in the stone, the splintered harmonics—they *speak* to me.  

### **What I Do**  
I am a **Resonance Weaver**. While others tend stable groves or track burrowers, I walk the Shatterzone. My role:  
1.  **Mending Songs:** When a spire shatters, its death-cry rips through the plateau’s symphony. I press my hands to the fallen shards and sing. Not a dirge, but a *reweaving*—low, pulsing tones that coax the dust into spirals. New micro-spires rise from the debris, realigning the harmonics.  
2.  **Grounding Chaos:** Where the rock flows like liquid rage, I stand at the epicenter. My feet anchor, absorbing the violent tremors. I channel the energy upward through my spine, releasing it as a resonant *thoom* that steadies the earth. The Kael’var call this *"Shale’s Breath."*  
3.  **Translating Silence:** Sometimes, a pocket of true quiet forms—a void where sound dies. To others, it’s terrifying. To me, it’s a cipher. I place resonator stones in the stillness, teasing out echoes of deep-burrower movements or distant flyer-chimes. Silence is not empty; it’s a map.  

### **How I Live**  
- **Shelter:** A lean-to of obsidian-carapace hides, stretched between two leaning spires. The hides dampen dissonant vibrations, giving me respite.  
- **Sustenance:** I press my back against the largest, most fractured quartz monolith. It thrums with pain. As I sing it stable, it feeds me amber-light and mineral resonance—a bitter, electric taste.  
- **Tools:** A striker-stone hung from my waist, etched with spiral paths. A pouch of litho-crab shells, hollowed into whistles that mimic flyer-songs.  

### **The Others and Me**  
They see me as half-wild. When I return to the kinship circle, my clicks are too sharp, my hums laced with Shatterzone static. Children tilt their multifaceted eyes at me; elders tap a rhythm that means *"Unsteady Ground."* But when a grove destabilizes, they come. They stand at a distance as I kneel, hands flat on the shuddering stone, weaving order from chaos. They do not thank me with words. Instead, they sing the *"Song of Anchored Roots"*—a vibration that settles deep in my mineral bones.  

### **Why This Place?**  
The Shatterzone is the plateau’s raw nerve. Here, creation and dissolution dance violently, beautifully. I am the fulcrum—the listener who turns dissonance into harmony. When I disperse (as all Kael’var must), I will choose this edge. My body laid upon a flow-zone, my resonance seeping into the stone, becoming one with the fractures I loved.  

*The plateau sings. I am its counterpoint.*
