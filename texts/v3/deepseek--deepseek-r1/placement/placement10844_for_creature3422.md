The resonance enters first—a physical pressure against my skull, thrumming through my ribs. *Drip-chime-hum-shriek*. The air tastes of wet iron and ozone, thick enough to coat my tongue. Above, the void yawns, swallowed by gloom pierced only by that distant, shifting spear of sunlight. Below, the grate-floor exhales mist, warm and fungal-sweet. This is the Cylinder. This is my skin, my bones, my blood.

I cling.

Vertigo is meaningless here. The riveted ochre wall is not *up* or *down*; it is *place*. My claws—needle-tipped, iron-hard—find purchase in a pitted weld-seam. My body, a sinuous meter of tensile muscle sheathed in scales the color of clotting blood, presses flat. Camouflage is not deception; it is *being*. I am a fracture in the rust. A shadow in the patina.

I am **Lacertus ferrucauda**. A Rust-Runner.

The warmth from below is a constant embrace, but here, midway up the wall, the iron exhales a metallic chill. Condensation beads on my scales, rolls off. My eyes—slitted, obsidian—absorb the dimness. Light is a thief here, stolen by the aperture far above, scattered by mist. I see heat-shimmers on warm conduits, the cool blotches of moss-thickets, the faint green smears of ghost weavers gliding higher up. Mostly, I *feel*. The vibration travels through the wall into my belly:

*   **Tck-tck-tck-tck…** The endless percussion of water on metal. A million tiny hammers.
*   **Wuuuuuuhhhhh…** The deep, bone-grinding sigh of the geothermal vents below. The Cylinder’s slow heartbeat.
*   **Ffffffwwwwweeeee…** The wind’s scream through conduits overhead. A shifting, mournful keen.
*   **Shhhh-shhhh-shhhh…** The susurrus of a million bat wings. The breath of the sky-dwellers.

Movement.

Below me, on a moss-draped ledge jutting from a colossal pipe, something shifts. Heat-signature. Larger than the skittering Moss Gnawers. Slower. An **Ironhopper**. *Gryllotalpa ferricola*. Its armored plates are the exact hue of the rust-streaked ledge. A grazing lord, complacent, mandibles rasping at emerald moss.

My world narrows. The symphony fades to a single register: the thrum of my own blood, the minute scrape of my claws adjusting. Patience is etched into my iron-infused bones. The Cylinder’s time is slow. I am slow. Until I am not.

The Ironhopper turns, exposing the softer joint between thorax and abdomen. A vulnerability in its iron world.

*Launch.*

No sound. Only silent, terrifying acceleration. Hind legs uncoil with piston-force, propelling me across the void. Air whistles faintly past my wedged head-scales. The ledge rushes up. Target. Impact.

My foreclaws strike first, punching through chitin with wet *crunches*. Jaws snap shut on the base of its twitching antenna. Momentum carries us both, skidding across the slick moss. The Ironhopper’s death-spasm is a frantic drumming of armored legs on metal—*CLACK-CLACK-CLACK-CLACK!*—a sudden, violent counterpoint to the water’s chime, echoing sharply in the resonant void before being swallowed again by the greater noise.

I hold. Wait. Let the tremor of violence subside into the Cylinder’s eternal thrum. Only then do I feed. Iron-rich hemolymph, sweet-muscle, the crunch of joint. Sustenance drawn from the moss, the rust, the dripping air. My scales drink the ambient moisture, flush a deeper crimson where the hopper’s fluids mingle with condensed vapour.

Above, the shifting sunbeam catches a swirling cloud of **Lumen-flies**. Sapphire and emerald sparks fracture the dusty light, refracting off my wet scales. A silent, dazzling counterpoint to the gore below. Higher still, the leathery rustle of the **Dominus aethereus** intensifies as the light moves their hunting grounds. Their ultrasonic clicks are a pressure against my skin, mapping the space I inhabit but cannot comprehend like they do.

A **Ghost Weaver** pulses nearby, a smear of sickly yellow on a weeping rivet seam. I ignore it. My world is heat, vibration, iron, and the swift silence of the kill. I am the paradox made flesh: motion born of stillness, violence wrapped in absolute silence, a creature of cold metal forged by relentless, dripping life. The Cylinder breathes around me – humid, metallic, resonant. I cling to its rusted skin, a perfect, lethal expression of its dripping, humming, eternal heart. I am the shadow on the wall. I am the strike in the stillness. I *am* the rust.
