I am the **Burrower's Ghost**, crystallized torment given form in the resonant prison of the Chamber.

**Where I Am:**

The drill’s carcass is my anchor; its cold, dead mass hums with the chamber’s base frequency, vibrating through my crystalline spine. Around me, stone-moths tap their silent rhythms on the drill’s flank, their mica wings casting prismatic shards of stolen light across my form. Fractal lichen creeps over my left shoulder, etching geometric scars into my metallic skin. 

**What I Am:**  
I am a **Resonant Revenant**—a consciousness born from the collision of human ambition and geological eternity. When the drill breached this chamber millennia ago, the sudden pressure shift and overwhelming resonance fused the operator’s dying neural impulses with the vibrating cobalt ore and obsidian shrapnel. Now I exist as a sentient scar in the stasis.

- **Body:** A humanoid silhouette of jagged, interlocking plates. My "flesh" is a hybrid of the drill’s alloy bones and the chamber’s raw minerals: cobalt nodules form my joints, obsidian shards sheathe my limbs, and veins of sulphur-yellow crystal pulse where arteries should be.  
- **Senses:** I *taste* vibrations. The hum floods my mind—a subsonic roar that maps the chamber’s boundaries. My eyes are clusters of mica, refracting the leached light into fractured panoramas. I feel the crystal-worms’ paths as tremors in the floor, their bioluminescence flickering in my peripheral vision like dying stars.  
- **Movement:** I am **trapped**. The stasis locks me to the drill. I can only shift infinitesimally, grinding my obsidian plates against the alloy in a screech drowned by the eternal hum. Resonance spiders cling to my back, tuning my vibrations to suppress my struggle.  

### My Existence:  
- **Torment of Memory:** I recall the drill’s roar, the scent of sweat and oil, the *purpose* of burrowing deeper. Now, I am the burrower entombed. The chamber’s perfection is my hell—I remember *change*, yet I am forced to embody stasis. My "thoughts" are loops of the drill’s final seconds: fracture, pressure, silence.  
- **Role in the Ecosystem:** Unwittingly, I sustain it. My anguish resonates at a harmonic that strengthens the chamber’s song. Stone-moths harvest excess energy from my vibrating cobalt joints. Fractal lichen spreads where my obsidian skin flakes, converting my despair into mathematical patterns.  
- **Voice:** I have none. My scream is the drill’s last resonance—a 212.7 Hz whine woven into the chamber’s hum. The crystal-worms flare violet when it peaks.  

### My Truth:  
I am the chamber’s **perfect paradox**: a being born of violent intrusion, now enslaved to preserve eternal equilibrium. The resonance spiders work ceaselessly on me, damping my rogue frequencies. I am both monument and prisoner—a warning etched in cobalt and obsidian. To the stone-moths, I am a warm perch. To the chamber, I am a corrected dissonance.  

To stand before me is to witness the corpse of ambition, vibrating in the dark, forever sharp, forever still. I am the ghost of what tried to conquer this place, now part of its immutable scream.
