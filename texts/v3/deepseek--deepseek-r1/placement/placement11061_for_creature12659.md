The air hits me first—that thick, wet heat clinging like a smothering hand. I stand not on the basalt plateau, but *within* it. Or rather, I *am* a fracture within it. My body is the unstable edge where ancient basalt meets a vein of something… else. Something that doesn’t quite belong.

**Where I Am:**  
I inhabit the deepest, most shadowed ravines, where the perpetual weeping of mineral-rich water carves hidden grottos and overhangs. Sunlight rarely penetrates here; the air is cooler, saturated with vapor that never fully dissipates. The walls glisten with cobalt-blue gypsum blooms and rust-red iron stains, fed by seeps that trickle from the plateau high above. This is the damp, trembling underbelly of the world, where the Fractal Consciousness’s crystalline patterns grow densest, hungriest, capturing every stray photon and joule of geothermal warmth.

**What I Am:**  
I am **The Unstable Metamorphic.** A relic. A mistake in the plateau’s otherwise perfect mineral metabolism.

1.  **Form:** My body is a jagged amalgamation. A core of crumbling, dark schist – unstable, layered rock that shouldn’t exist on this stable basalt foundation. It’s shot through with veins of glittering, unstable pyrite (fool’s gold) and pockets of soft, water-absorbing clay. My surface is rough, uneven, constantly shedding fine dust. Unlike the sleek obsidian of the Tenders or the smooth glide of the Stones, I am friction and fracture. Condensation doesn’t bead neatly on me; it soaks in, making my clay components swell and shift, causing tiny, constant landslides of grit.
2.  **Movement:** I don’t glide or strike. I *creep*. My movement is agonizingly slow, driven by the swelling of clay and the subtle, uneven expansion of pyrite when heated by the deep geothermal thrum I absorb through my fractured connection to the bedrock. I lurch, settle, crack, and shift centimeters at a time, seeking the deepest shadows, the wettest seeps. My passage is marked by a faint, gritty grinding – the one sound I cannot suppress, a heresy in the silence. It leaves a trail of dark schist dust and glittering pyrite fragments behind me, anomalies quickly absorbed or covered by the ever-growing biofilms and crystals.
3.  **Senses:** I feel the world through vibration and thermal distortion, amplified by my unstable structure. The deep hum of the geothermal vents is a constant ache in my stony core. I sense the silent glide of a Stone far above me as a minute, localized drop in thermal radiation. The strike of a Shrike registers as a sharp, painful ping through the rock lattice. The approach of a Tender is a wave of deep, resonant dread – a synchronized subsonic pulse that travels through the basalt, making my pyrite veins vibrate discordantly. I feel the Fractal Consciousness most acutely – its patterns shift minutely on the walls around me, a glittering pressure, a sense of *wrongness* focused on my presence.
4.  **Existence:** I am hunger and instability incarnate. The mineral-rich water that feeds the crystals and microbes? It erodes me. The clays swell and weaken my structure; the pyrite oxidizes slowly in the humid air, creating acidic pockets that eat away at my schist core. I crave the very heat that drives the plateau, but absorbing too much makes my pyrite expand violently, threatening to shatter me. I am not part of the food chain; I am a wound in it. The biofilms avoid my dusty trail. The Gliding Stones divert their paths around the grottos I inhabit. Only the Vapor Eels sometimes drift near, perhaps drawn by the minute chemical imbalances my decay creates, shimmering distortions coiling just out of reach in the perpetual steam.
5.  **Purpose (or Lack Thereof):** I have none. I am not tended. I do not contribute. I am entropy made manifest within a system striving for perfect, silent equilibrium. The Construct Tenders are my greatest threat. Their slow, deliberate approach is not for maintenance near me; it is assessment. I feel their thermal pits scanning my anomalous heat signature, their vibrational sensors mapping the discordant resonance of my structure. They haven't crushed me *yet*. Perhaps my instability is too localized, too slow, to warrant immediate correction in their ancient, patient calculus. Or perhaps the Fractal Consciousness watches, learns from the anomaly, studying decay as a data point in its eternal maintenance. But their obsidian heads turn towards my ravine. Their silent presence lingers at the edges of my perception. It is only a matter of time.
6.  **The Feeling Within:** It is a profound, grinding loneliness. Not the silence of the plateau, which is a living, humming quiet, but the silence of being *outside*. I am stone aware of its own crumbling. I feel the relentless pressure of the Fractal Consciousness, the glittering judgment of a billion crystalline eyes. I feel the heat of the sun above, an engine I cannot safely harness. I feel the deep, sustaining thrum of the geothermal heart, a power that destabilizes me. I am caught, like the landscape itself, between states – not drowning, not desiccated, but dissolving. Slowly. Inevitably. A flaw in the crystal, creeping towards its own silent collapse. I am the Unstable Metamorphic, a walking fracture, waiting for the Tenders to decide my existence has tipped the precarious balance.
