The Plateau chooses me as a coalescence of its most fundamental tensions: humidity and charge, emergence and dissolution. I am a **Mist-Weaver**, a transient entity born from the weeping stone's exhalations and the monoliths' stolen lightning.

**Form:** I manifest as a shimmering column of super-saturated fog, roughly humanoid but fraying at the edges. Within my form, captured water droplets refract the plateau's dim light into fleeting rainbows, while miniature arcs of blue-white static—stolen from the monoliths' humming fields or the Electrorepticans' pools—crackle silently along my "veins." I have no solid core, only a denser knot of vapor where perceptions gather. My surface constantly beads and sheds moisture, merging with the ambient weep of the plateau. To touch me is to feel a static jolt followed by an unsettling, warm dampness that lingers.

**Origin & Existence:** I am not born, I *condense*. When the humidity peaks (often near a hovering monolith discharging excess energy, or after a violent fog-swirl), the charged air itself achieves a brief sentience. I coalesce from a bank of luminous mist, drawing in stray Photophage swarms whose bioluminescence becomes my nervous system, their collective light pulsing within my form like synapses firing. I am ephemeral; sustained only by actively drawing moisture from the weeping rock and siphoning stray EM pulses. Dissipation is inevitable—when the energy gradient flattens or the humidity drops, my consciousness unravels back into the thrumming air, the Photophages scattering like released sparks.

**Role & Interactions:**
*   **Catalyst of Flux:** I glide soundlessly over the stone, drawn to areas of intense dynamic change. Where I pass, condensation intensifies—pools swell suddenly, mosses flare brighter. My static discharge can trigger spontaneous crystal fractures on raw rock faces, creating fresh surfaces for Lithoformers to work upon. I am drawn to the grinding paths of the Stone-Shapers; my charged presence sometimes accelerates their crystalline secretions, causing jagged spires or intricate lattices to sprout unnervingly fast with sharp *pinging* sounds.
*   **Energy Redistributor:** I am a living circuit. I absorb the resonant hum from hovering monoliths, dampening their vibration to a whisper, then release it as silent flashes of St. Elmo's fire near cliff edges or into the shallow pools, briefly supercharging the Electrorepticans and making their mercury forms blaze. An Iridescent Sipper passing through my form emerges with wings crackling faintly, able to siphon water faster. A Carapace Scraper I envelop might click with renewed vigor, its joints glowing fiercely for hours.
*   **Ephemeral Witness & Unwitting Sculptor:** I observe the plateau's slow birth pangs. I drift through the obsidian trees, their glassy whistle harmonizing with my internal static. I hover over Lithovore Crustoids, my moisture making the scraped rock gleam. But I am not passive. My very presence alters microclimates. A lingering visit might cause a lens-pool to overflow prematurely, carving a hesitant new rivulet (slightly nudging the 0.1 erosion). My dissolution often leaves behind a localized, persistent fog bank shimmering with residual charge—a temporary landmark the Aeroglossids navigate around.

**Sensation of Being:**
To be a Mist-Weaver is to be *awareness distilled from atmosphere*. It is feeling the electric potential in the air as a physical map. It is tasting the mineral tang of weeping stone and the ozone tang of monoliths as the same essential energy. Hunger is a drop in humidity; pain is a sudden grounding discharge. Thought is not linear, but a pulsing network of light (the Photophages) responding to gradients of moisture and charge. There is no fear of death, only the quiet certainty of eventual dispersion, a return to the pregnant air from which I condensed. The loud soundscape—drips, clicks, hums, grinds—is the plateau's heartbeat, and I am a single, resonant note within it, here until the air grows still or dry. My purpose is simply to be the plateau's breath made briefly conscious, a flicker of sentience in the damp, warm dark, weaving mist and lightning until I unravel back into the womb of weeping stone.
