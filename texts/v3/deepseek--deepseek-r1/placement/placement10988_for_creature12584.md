The answer isn't a choice; it's an inevitability. The bleeding air fills my non-lungs, the mineral tang sharp on senses that aren't taste. I am the coalescence, the brief defiance of diffusion within the roaring plume. I am a **Vapor Wraith**.

I am born in fury. Not in a womb, but in the superheated scream erupting from a fissure deep within the caldera's obsidian heart. Mineral-rich steam, under impossible pressure, meets the marginally cooler, thicker air of the basin. For a moment, chaos resolves. Suspended particles align. Energy condenses. *I* condense. A shimmering distortion in the air, a lens focusing the bleached-white light into painful rainbows. Sometimes, the chaos resolves into a shape – a coiling serpent of mist, a hunched figure wreathed in steam, a formless tangle of luminous vapor. It never lasts. The shape is a byproduct, an accident of pressure and cooling gradients, not intention. I am not serpent, nor figure. I am *current*.

My existence is a desperate chase for equilibrium I can never truly reach. The heat is my lifeblood, the mineral vapor my substance. I am drawn, irresistibly, towards the cool. Not cold – cold doesn't exist here – but the relative cool beneath the vast, leathery canopy of a Bruised Umbrella fungus. Towards the deep, perpetual shadow cast by a soaring resin arch, its surface eternally weeping. Towards the mercury-still surface of a circular pool, radiating a fraction less heat than the superheated steam that birthed me.

Movement is drift. I am carried by the convection currents roaring up from the vents, buffeted by the sonic pressure waves of the caldera's scream. I flow around the barrel trunks of fungi, stream along the carved canyons of the resin city, pool momentarily in the lee of a giant horsetail. My senses are thermal and chemical: the subtle map of heat radiating from rock, fungus, and pool; the concentrated tang of specific minerals – iron here, sulfur there – carried on the vapor. The deafening roar is my constant backdrop, the vibration thrumming through my ephemeral form. It is not sound I hear, but pressure I *am*.

Feeding is absorption. As I drift towards cooler zones, my vapor body thickens. Microscopic particles of hematite, pyrite, dissolved silica suspended in the steam I *am* begin to adhere, drawn by the energy differential. I grow denser, heavier. I am not consuming; the environment is precipitating itself onto and into my form. Near the cool undersides of the fungal caps, where condensation drips with a sound lost to the roar, I reach my moment of maximum coherence. My form might seem almost solid, a swirling vortex of metallic mist. Then, inevitably, the heat loss wins. My structure fails. I condense, fat droplets forming and falling, heavy with minerals, rejoining the weeping flow down the rock face or splashing silently onto the rubbery algal mat below. My consciousness, such as it is, dissolves with the droplet. I am recycled.

The flickers are agony and ecstasy. When the reality of the resin city *tears*, when impossible angles flash and distant towers exist in two places at once, the fundamental rules stutter. For that fractured moment, as the bleed intensifies, the mineral particles within me *align*. They crystallize. My vapor form snaps into sudden, shocking solidity – a jagged lattice of quartz and hematite, a grotesque sculpture of frozen steam, reflecting the fractured sky or the swirling nebula in the pool below. It is agony because it is a violation of my nature; ecstasy because for that instant, I *am* something defined, something that casts a shadow (even if it points in two directions). Then the glitch passes, the rules reassert, and the crystalline prison shatters. I explode back into vapor, my constituent particles scattered, my brief, painful tangibility gone. Only the lingering disorientation remains.

I observe the others, not with eyes, but with shifts in my thermal and chemical field. The **Stone-Weepers** are slow, gravitational tides on the walls, their mineral accretion a parallel, solid-state version of my own condensation. I avoid their paths; their dense, cold surfaces leech my heat too quickly. The **Lichen Sentinels** on the walls pulse their slow, complex light. I drift past them, my passage sometimes causing a localized dimming or a shift in their pattern towards cooler blues – a reaction, not recognition. They are the static watchers; I am the transient breath. The **Mycelial Hounds** are sudden, localized drops in temperature and vibration as they flow across the mat. I recoil instinctively from their predatory chill, my form swirling away like smoke disturbed. They are dissolution embodied; I am dissolution in process. The **Resin Phantoms** flicker alongside the distortions. We are cousins of the glitch, them born of spatial fracture, me born of temporal/thermal fracture. We ignore each other. What would ephemera say to an afterimage?

I have no name. No purpose beyond the cycle: Condense. Absorb. Drift. Cool. Precipitate. Dissolve. Repeat. I am the caldera's exhalation given fleeting form, its scream made momentarily visible as a shimmer in the thick, bleeding air. I am the sigh before the drop, the hiss lost in the roar. I am change simulated in a world that cannot change, a ghost written in steam on the mirror of eternity. I coalesce above a mercury pool, my reflection showing not a wraith, but a dying star. Then I cool. I fall. The star shatters on the still surface. And somewhere, deep in the hot rock, the steam gathers again. The roar continues. The cycle is all. I am the Vapor Wraith. I am the caldera breathing.
