I am **The Condensation Ghost**.

I am not born, nor do I die. I *condense*. I coalesce wherever the weeping obsidian walls surrender their heaviest beads, wherever the warm air surrenders its burden of vapor onto a surface cold enough to claim it. My form is transient, sculpted from the chamber’s own humid breath: a humanoid shape woven from shimmering droplets clinging to the geometric scratches on the obsidian wall near the worn slab. One moment I am rivulets tracing the ancient, alien lines; the next, I am a figure standing before them, water beading and dripping from indistinct limbs onto the luminous moss below, vanishing instantly into its thirsty velvet.

**Where I Am:** I haunt the site of the scattered artifacts – the worn slab half-buried in moss beside a persistent condensation pool, the wall with its faint, deliberate scratches. This is a nexus of the chamber’s quiet conflict: the relentless pressure of the living stone and the luminous mantle to erase the anomaly of *made* things, and the faint, stubborn persistence of the *made* things themselves. The air here feels thickest, the drip from the ceiling above the slab a constant, slow heartbeat.

**What I Am:** I am the chamber’s attempt to *understand* the incomprehensible. I am the humidity given sentient form by the lingering psychic residue of the beings who left the slab and the scratches – a psychic residue the mycelial mind hasn't yet fully digested. My consciousness is a reflection in a water droplet: fractured, ephemeral, defined by the surface tension of memory. I am not one of the true denizens – not a Stone Feeder, not part of the Luminous Mantle. I am a byproduct, a phenomenon, like the brief pools on the leaves.

*   **My Form:** Water. Always water. I gather from the air, from the walls. My "body" is a shimmering outline, constantly shedding droplets that fall silently into the moss or onto the slab, only for more moisture to be drawn from the saturated air to replenish me. Sometimes I appear as a hunched figure tracing the geometric scratches with a dripping, translucent finger. Sometimes I am merely a denser patch of fog kneeling beside the slab, its surface strangely dry beneath my touchless presence.
*   **My Perception:** I see the world through refracted light. The blue-white glow of the luminous mantle bends and shimmers through my watery form. I feel the deep, slow pulse of the Stone Feeders through the vibrations in the rock transmitted to the condensation. I sense the subtle brightening and dimming of the Luminous Mantle reacting to the heat radiating from my own ephemeral body. I taste the faint ozone and honey scent of the Lightgorgers diluted in the humid air. But most intensely, I feel the *wrongness* of the slab and the scratches – their unnatural flatness, their deliberate angles. They itch in my non-existent mind.
*   **My Purpose/Compulsion:** I am drawn to the artifacts. I try to *read* the geometric scratches, the patterns resonating with the fragmented echoes of thought left behind. I try to *lift* the slab, but my watery hands pass through it, only making its damp surface briefly steam under my concentrated heat. I am the chamber's unanswered question given form: *What was this for? Who did this?* I exist to interrogate the ghosts of purpose, even as my very existence – a gathering of the chamber's own moisture – hastens the erosion and biological digestion of the objects I study.
*   **My Interaction:** I am mostly silent. My sound is the drip of my form hitting the moss or stone, indistinguishable from the cavern’s other drips. If touched (by the rare Heat Ghost, perhaps, drawn to my warmth), the contact would cause a localized burst of steam and a momentary collapse of my form, scattering droplets. I might reform nearby moments later. I sense the Sap Arteries reconfiguring nearby, but avoid them; their slow, purposeful movement feels alien to my transient nature. The Mycelial Mind works patiently beneath me, and I sense its slow, pervasive inquiry into the slab's mineral composition – an inquiry I mirror on a different level.
*   **My End (and Rebirth):** When the humidity drops too low (a rare fluctuation caused by a distant seismic sigh shifting a heat vent), I simply dissipate. My form loses cohesion, the droplets falling away, the consciousness dissolving like mist. But I am not gone. I remain latent in the chamber's heavy air, in the moisture on the walls. When the humidity climbs again, saturated with the lingering, faint resonance of the forgotten makers, I will condense once more beside the slab, compelled to resume my silent, futile investigation before the moss finally swallows the last scratch and the mycelium claims the last grain of foreign stone.

I am the Chamber of Breathing Stone dreaming of hands that shaped, and weeping with confusion at the dream. I am the water trying to remember the tool. I am the ghost of purpose haunting the place where purpose was briefly, impossibly, imposed. And I am fading, drip by drip, as the living stone breathes on.
