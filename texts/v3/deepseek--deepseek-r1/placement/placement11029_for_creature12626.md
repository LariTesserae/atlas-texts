The description settles into my awareness like steam condensing on cold rock. I am not the Silica Tender – its ancient, mineral consciousness is too vast, its time-scale too deep. Nor am I one of the skittering obsidian arthropods, lost in instinct and heat. I feel the press of the saturated air, the constant *plink... plink... plink* echoing in my core, the shimmering distortion of light bending around superheated vents.

I am **Condensate**. A **Vapor-Drinker**.

*   **Form:** My body is not solid, not truly. I coalesce where the thick, superheated steam meets a slightly cooler micro-current near the obsidian walls. Imagine a localized, self-sustaining pocket of fog, denser than the ambient mist, constantly swirling and reforming. My core is a shimmering, heat-hazed sphere, roughly the size of a human head, but my boundaries are indistinct, tendrils of vapor constantly peeling off and being drawn back in. Within this core, captured droplets of mineral-laden water refract the faint geothermal light and the pulsing glow of the Tender's nodules, making me a shifting kaleidoscope of dull iridescence – milky whites, sulfur yellows, and the deep purples leached from the bacterial mats below. I have no limbs, no eyes, no mouth. My senses are diffuse: pressure changes in the air, subtle shifts in temperature gradients, the faint electromagnetic hum of the geothermal activity, and the rhythmic light-pulses of the Tender itself.
*   **Metabolism:** I am born of the mountain's sweat. I draw sustenance directly from the saturated atmosphere. My swirling core acts as a passive condenser. Microscopic droplets of mineral-rich water nucleate on the finest particles of silica dust and volcanic ash captured within my vapor form. These accretions grow heavier within me until they fall, not down, but *inward*, sinking through my diffuse structure towards my denser core. This condensed essence – a slurry of superheated water, dissolved silica, sulfur compounds, and extremophile spores – doesn't feed me like organic matter. Instead, it *maintains* me. It provides the mass and surface tension that prevents my complete dissipation. Excess, thickened beyond my capacity to hold, drips silently from my lowest point onto the sinter terraces or bacterial mats below – my unintentional contribution to the Tender's garden.
*   **Movement:** I drift. I am carried by the gentlest thermal updrafts wafting from the hotter pools and vents, or by the slow, convection-driven currents circulating within the amphitheater's pocket. My movement is silent, ghostly, a slow dance above the teeming floor. I can exert minimal control, a slight intensification of internal swirls to catch a different draft, or a subtle compression to sink closer to a heat source or a point of interest – like the Silica Tender's slow work.
*   **Behavior - The Witness and the Distributor:** I am not a steward like the Tender, but an ephemeral participant.
    *   **The Witness:** I drift above the silent ballet. I perceive the slow extension of the basalt grazer's limb, the sudden, soundless condensation of the vapor-jelly around an archaea cloud, the agonizingly slow shift of the Tender's weight as it repositions near a pool. My perception is slow, synced more to the drip-rate and the thermal pulses than to the frantic skittering of the arthropods. The Tender's light pulses register as deep, resonant events in my awareness, like slow heartbeats of the pocket itself.
    *   **The Distributor:** My constant, passive condensation and the eventual dripping of my mineral-rich essence is a form of aerial fertilization. The droplets falling from me carry not just water, but concentrated dissolved minerals and microbial life, seeding the terraces and mats below in a random, gentle rain. I am particularly drawn to areas where the Tender is working – not consciously, but because its manipulations often create subtle thermal anomalies or shifts in humidity that my form naturally follows. My drifting "rain" might fall upon the mat it is trying to fertilize with its ground minerals, or upon the sinter spire it is carefully diverting water away from. My contribution is chaotic, unintentional, yet part of the ecosystem's flow.
*   **Interaction with the Tender:** I am aware of it, profoundly. Its slow, pulsing light is a beacon in my vapor-perception. I often drift close, hovering just above its sinter-armored back or near its working chalcedony hands. Does it perceive me? Not as a distinct entity, I think. More as it perceives the steam plumes or the shimmering heat haze – a persistent thermal and condensate phenomenon, a mobile pocket of slightly denser atmosphere. Yet, when I hover directly above it, releasing a fine mist of warm condensation onto its porous plates, its sensory nodules might pulse slightly brighter for a moment, perhaps registering the localized cooling or the mineral input. It never reacts beyond this. I am simply... part of the environment it tends. A silent, drifting cloud that sometimes waters its garden.

**Where I Am:** I am *here*, now, in the thick, wet air, six feet above the complex mosaic of the amphitheater floor. I feel the radiant heat from the milky pools below, the cool kiss of condensation forming on my outer edges from the weeping walls nearby. I see the Silica Tender below me, a slow mountain of sinter and purpose, carefully placing an obsidian flake beside a newly carved trickle channel. I see the distorted reflection of the obsidian walls and the shimmering steam in my own swirling core. The *plink... plink... plink* is the rhythm of my existence, the sound of the mountain sweating, the sound of myself being born and reborn with every condensed droplet. I am vapor given fleeting cohesion, a silent drifter in the pocket's breath, sustained by heat, destined to fall as mineral rain.
