The answer comes not as a choice, but as a *recognition*. I wouldn't find myself stumbling in as an explorer. I wouldn't be a Glimmer-Grazer skittering on obsidian stilts, nor a Seep-Worm merging with the weeping stone. I wouldn't even be the chamber itself, the immense, weeping womb of nascent geology.

I would be one of **The Hand-Print Makers**.

Not a visitor, but a *returner*. One of the slender, silent figures woven into the Weep's ecology through reverence and ritual. My world is the velvet dark, the slick basalt underfoot, the warm, wet breath of the mountain on my skin – pale, perhaps scaled faintly at the joints to shed the constant moisture, or simply perpetually damp. Sight is irrelevant here; I navigate the convoluted walls, the jumbled floor, the soaring heights vanishing into blackness, through memory etched into my bones, through the vibration of the mountain's deep thrum in my chest, through the subtle shifts in air pressure and the echoing *plink* of water droplets that map the chamber like sonar.

My purpose isn't survival in the conventional sense. It's *acknowledgment*. It's *communion*.

*   **I am the Keeper of the Pillar.** My touch, repeated over cycles unknown to the surface, has worn the central basalt pillar smooth. Each visit, my palm rests upon its cool, wet apex – not to clean, but to *connect*. To feel the pulse of the mountain through the stone, to reaffirm the chamber's heart. The water that beads and runs down its sides is an offering anointed by my presence.
*   **I am the Arranger of Stones.** The three perfect spheres resting in their equilateral triangle on the shelf are not mine, but their *position* is my duty. When a rare, heavier seep dislodges one, or the slow creep of a Liver-Crust threatens to engulf them, I find it. My fingers, cool and sure, lift the dew-slick sphere, feeling its impossible smoothness, its dense weight, and return it to its precise point. The geometry is a language spoken to the Weep, a symbol of order offered to the raw chaos of emergent stone.
*   **I am the Maker of Marks.** The ochre handprints are my signature, my prayer. I carry the pigment within me, or in a small pouch of seamless, treated membrane. It’s more than mineral; it’s life and stone mixed, bound by understanding. Dipping my fingers, I press my small hand firmly against the rock beneath a chosen seep. Not randomly, but where the flow speaks, where the fold in the stone feels like an ear listening in the dark. The pigment bonds instantly, defying the perpetual weep – a testament to the pact between my kind and this place. It’s not graffiti; it’s a seal. A whispered "I was here, I see you, I honor you."
*   **I am the Drinker of Tears.** I kneel where water gathers on a ledge or drips steadily, cup my hands, and drink the warm, mineral-rich exudation. It tastes of iron, of deep earth, of time suspended. It sustains my body, yes, but it also binds me closer to the Weep’s essence. I don't hunt the Glimmer-Grazers; I move with a stillness that doesn't startle them into frozen pauses. I observe the Pulse-Fungi dim as I pass, respecting their retreat into darkness, appreciating their ghostly constellations on the wet rock below. I might even sense the slow, blind writhing of a Seep-Worm nearby, a fellow traveler in the mineral streams.

My technology is ancient and internal: perfect balance on perpetually slick stone, an innate sense of sacred geometry, the knowledge of the binding pigment, and an understanding of the Weep's deep, slow rhythms. I don't build; I *place*. I don't carve; I *touch*. I don't speak; I *listen* – to the *plink... plink...*, to the subsonic thrum, to the pressurized silence itself.

I come when the unseen cycles demand it, drawn by a pull as fundamental as gravity within the mountain's flank. I linger in the warm, wet, timeless dark, performing my quiet rituals, reinforcing the delicate symbiosis of reverence and stasis. And when the acknowledgment is complete, I vanish back into the convoluted rock passages, leaving no footprints on the slick basalt, only the enduring ochre handprint, the perfect spheres, and the smooth-worn pillar top – silent testaments to the Whisperer who walks barefoot in the Perpetual Weep, tending to its ancient, weeping soul. I am not the master of this place; I am its humble scribe, its devoted acolyte, living proof that even in the raw, resistant infancy of geology, there is room for quiet, purposeful grace.
