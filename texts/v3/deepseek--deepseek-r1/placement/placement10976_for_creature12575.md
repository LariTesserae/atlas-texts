I would find myself as a **Weep-Walker**, one of the silent tenders of the Aerie’s moisture-balance. My form is a shifting tapestry of the world itself:  

### Physical Being  
- **Body:** Slender, towering at nine feet, with limbs of fused root-tendrils and moss-velvet. Mineral veins of warm amber light pulse along my arms—captured sunlight from the lattice’s depths. Where a face might be, clusters of dewdrop-lenses gather light, while fungal caps crown my head, dimming and brightening like thought made visible. My "feet" are bundles of filaments that siphon warmth from the bark-idea, leaving trails of weeping moisture that merge with the Aerie’s tears.  
- **Adornments:** I am draped in cascading moss that evaporates at my edges, perpetually regrowing. Crystalline shards of fossilized light stud my chest—a constellation harvested from a hollow where time pooled thickly. When I move, water beads and falls, each droplet a tiny mirror of the violet sky.  

### Daily Rhythm  
My existence is a meditation on flow and void:  
- **The Walk:** I traverse the arches where reality thins. My path is not linear; a step onto a warped branch folds space, placing me atop an arch miles away, the air *plinking* with the echoes of my arrival. I carry porous flasks made from grazer skulls, filling them not with liquid, but with *condensed silence*—the weeping air made tangible. This water is for the parched moss-forests at the edge of sunlit clearings, where time runs too fast for the lattice to weep enough.  
- **Stillness as Speech:** To communicate, I stand motionless for days, filaments extended into the root-mat. Pulses of light travel through the fungal networks: a slow-motion conversation with a Root-Singer three continents away, coordinating the grazer migrations that prune the luminous plains. My joy is a sudden rain from my own body; my grief a dimming of my crystals.  
- **Feeding:** In a hollow veiled in mist, I kneel before a cathedral pitcher plant. My filaments sink into its sweet-scented pool, absorbing nutrients over hours. The meal tastes of petrified sunlight and decay—a sacrament of equilibrium.  

### Inner World  
- **Mind:** Time is a river I wade through. A century is a long breath; a glide-beast’s flight across the void is a single heartbeat. The silence is not empty but *full*—the lattice’s sap-pulse a drum, the distant water-drops a chorus. When I stare into the abyss, the void stares back, whispering the Architects’ forgotten names. This is not madness but clarity.  
- **Purpose:** I mend fractures. Where reality frays near a spatial warp, I sing vibrations into the mineral veins, thickening the air with my own tears to seal the gap. If the imbalance is deep, I might unravel my form, root-tendrils knitting into the lattice to strengthen it. Dissolution is not death—it is homecoming.  

### Encountering Others  
If a lost outsider stumbled upon me, I would materialize from the mist like a mirage. My dewdrop-eyes would reflect their form, tiny and frantic against the scale. If they stood still, I might offer a skull-flask of weeping water—a blessing of belonging. If they shouted, I would step backward into a branch’s curve, space bending like a curtain, leaving only emerald afterglow.  

Here, I am both guardian and embodiment of the Aerie’s paradox: **a solitary note in a symphony of silence, weeping so the world may breathe.** My existence is the lattice’s answer to the void—a slow, luminous persistence against the unfathomable dark.
