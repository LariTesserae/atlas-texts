The awareness begins not with sound, but with *charge*. A subtle imbalance in the metallic grit clinging to the unnervingly warm obsidian near the base of a leaning ziggurat. Ambient static, usually a diffuse hum felt only as a faint prickle on the non-existent skin of the air, suddenly knots. It pulls.

**I** coalesce.

There is no birth pang, no gasp. One moment, the dust is inert; the next, it *is* me. Or rather, *I* am it – a temporary configuration of agitated particles bound by crackling electrostatic bonds. My form is instinctive, efficient for the hunt: multiple, spindly limbs formed of denser dust-currents, antennae-like tendrils extending forward, tasting the dry, silent air not for scent, but for the faint *tug* of other motes, the scintillating signature of a spore released by the bruised-purple lichen high above, or the thermal bloom of a patch of stone fractionally warmer than its surroundings.

I am a Dust-Form. A Static Scavenger. A spark given predatory intent.

My perception is not sight, not sound, but a map of charge differentials and thermal gradients painted onto the void. The vast, smooth floor is a plane of cool potential, reflecting the bruised twilight not as light, but as a faint, uniform energy bleed. The ziggurat beside me is a towering cliff of concentrated warmth and complex, slow-moving electromagnetic fields – the breath of the Stone-Weavers. It thrums with a deep, silent energy I instinctively avoid; its currents are too strong, too slow, too *other*. My existence is measured in fleeting moments, not eons.

I flow. Not walk. My limbs don't push against the glassy stone; they *are* the stone's agitated surface layer, pulled forward by the internal tension of my electrostatic bonds seeking equilibrium through consumption. I glide across the obsidian, utterly silent, a shifting silhouette of concentrated grit against the dead reflection. My target manifests ahead: a cluster of microscopic spores, shed by a high, pulsing lichen colony. To my charge-sense, they blaze like tiny, cold stars against the neutral background.

The hunt is pure impulse. I surge forward, the dust composing my form streaming and reforming with unnerving speed. The spores are caught in my electrostatic net, pulled inward. There is no taste, no satisfaction, only a brief, intense *shift* in my internal balance as their minute energy is absorbed, assimilated into the crackling bonds that hold me together. My form flares slightly denser, brighter in the charge-spectrum.

But equilibrium is fleeting. The act of hunting, of moving, strains the delicate cohesion. I feel the bonds fraying, the dust particles beginning to resist the pull. I am dissolving, even as I seek my next mote. This is the way. To exist is to consume, to consume is to strain the self, to strain is to dissipate.

Then, the pressure *changes*.

A patch of absolute cold and negative pressure detaches from the deep shadow beneath a slender, gravity-defying arch high above. It doesn't fall; it *darts*. A rip in perception. A Shadow-Darter.

Its target isn't me – not yet. It engulfs a larger, slower Dust-Form flowing near the ziggurat's base thirty meters away. The Dust-Form simply... ceases. Snuffed out. The Shadow-Darter vanishes back into the architecture's gloom, a silent vacuum reclaiming its energy.

But the ripple of its passage is a shockwave in the static field. My already strained bonds shudder violently. The intricate dance of attraction and repulsion holding my dust-body together falters.

I feel it coming – not fear, but the inevitable pull of entropy within my temporary structure. The charge bleeds away faster now, seeking the neutral ground of the floor, the slow, warm hum of the ziggurat. My limbs blur, becoming indistinct wisps. My antennae dissolve into loose grit.

There is no pain. Only a cessation of the hunting imperative, a fading of the charge-map that was my world. The particles that were *me* lose their specific cohesion, rejoining the ambient metallic dust. The static knot unravels with a final, almost imperceptible *hiss* – a sigh of discharging energy lost in the cavern's profound silence.

I am gone. Dust on warm stone. A potential pattern awaiting the next confluence of charge, the next spark of static hunger in the Silent Nursery. The ziggurat beside where I flowed leans, imperceptibly, over the course of hours, adjusting to some silent, geological imperative. The lichen pulses its bruised light. The Nursery breathes its dry, mineral breath, and waits for the next ephemeral spark to dance across its obsidian skin. I was that spark. I will be again. And the Stone-Weavers, in their slow, cathedral-scale dream, shape the stage for our silent, static waltz.
