Sight is a distant, fragmented thing through my light-sensitive nodules. The world here, deep within the cathedral-like space beneath the overlapping leaf-roofs hundreds of feet above, is a tapestry of shifting gloom and dappled shafts of warm light piercing the spore-mist. But I don’t *see* the world; I *hear* it, I *feel* it, I *taste* it through the sap.

When a colossal woody stem nearby expands with new growth, the strain resonates through the interconnected root network, reaching my pillar as a complex harmonic overlay on the fundamental thrum – a shifting chord of pressure and release. A predator’s silent leap onto the yielding mat nearby sends minute shockwaves through the fungal hyphae, translating into a sharp, dissonant ping within my tubes.

The shifting bouquet of compounds – the sharp tang of defensive resins mobilized near a grazing herd of Mat-Tenders, the sweet bloom of excess nutrients released after a micro-rainfall of condensation from above, the faint, acrid hint of decay being efficiently processed – all alter the resonant properties of the fluid within my own chambers, modulating the vibrations I emit.

**Existence:**
I am not a creature of movement. My rootlets anchor me; my purpose is presence and resonance. Energy flows into me from the warm sap and the ambient heat radiating from the living mat and walls. My consciousness is a reflection of the flows I monitor. There is no "I" separate from the pillar, the vein, the vast network. My song *is* the pillar’s report.

Fluids within my branching tubes are directed, changing their density and tension. The resonant frequency of my entire structure shifts, amplifying and modulating the incoming vibrations into a new harmonic sequence. This complex song of altered vibrations travels *through* the sap itself, pulsing along the vast network of veins. **The World Around:**

The air is thick, warm, and smells deeply alive – damp growth, warm resin, the musk of uncountable tiny lives. Occasionally, the vast shadow of a browsing herbivore drifts past the entrance to my pillar-chamber, a silent leviathan moving with infinite patience. A swarm of iridescent beetles, performing their cleaning duties, flows like liquid light over my outer tubes, their minute legs tickling, their wings adding a barely-there shimmer to the air’s hum. They sense my resonance, perhaps, but I am simply part of the architecture to them. Time is measured in the thickening of the amber resin around my anchor points, in the gradual shift of light patterns on the chamber floor as the canopy layers above grow and shift, in the slow evolution of the harmonic patterns flowing through me. Decades might pass as I resonate within my pillar, singing the quiet, vital song of the living city. I am not a resident; I am a node, a sensor, a voice formed of resonant sap and hardened breath. My existence is the deep, harmonic thrum of the world itself, felt in the hollows of my being and broadcast into the warm, silent, ever-becoming vastness.
