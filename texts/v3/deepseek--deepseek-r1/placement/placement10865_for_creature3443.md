Here’s where I find myself in this world—and who I’ve become:  

### **The Resin-Watcher**  
I am a craft-warden of the Spine Kelp’s sacred tears. My place is the **Amber Nook**, a shallow cave hollowed into the kelp-trunk where wind and mist conspire to dry the air just enough for resin-work. Here, the kelp’s viscous amber tears seep slowest, pooling in bark-cups I carve and replace each season. My role is patience incarnate: I gather, purify, and blend the resin with volcanic ash from deep dives, transforming grief into glue, sealant, and ritual art.  

### **Physique & Rhythm**  
- **Hands of Stone and Suction:** My palms bear thick, waxy calluses from handling hot resin, but my fingertips are subtly cupped—adaptations from years of scaling the trunk to reach weeping fissures. I move like moss: incremental, deliberate, leaving no trace on damp bark.  
- **Eyes of Fractured Light:** My irises are pale quartz, widened by decades squinting at resin-clouds in dim light. I see not just shapes, but *viscosity*—reading the amber’s readiness in how it catches the bioluminescent haloes of polyps at dusk.  
- **Silence as Skin:** I rarely speak. My language is the *tap-tap* of bone chisels on pumice molds, the soft *hiss* of breath cooling molten resin. When the fog thickens at night, I listen to the kelp’s deep groans, feeling for tremors that warn of fractures needing sealing.  

### **Craft & Ritual**  
- **The Amber Liturgy:** Each tear is sacred. I chant subvocal hymns as I work—syllables felt in the throat, not heard—honoring the Great Root’s suffering. Before blending resin with ash, I prick my thumb with a sea-urchin spine, letting one drop of blood fall as covenant: *life mending life*.  
- **Tools of the Nook:**  
  - **Driftwood Ladles:** Carved with otter-porpoise teeth, for scooping warm resin.  
  - **Volcanic Sand Crucibles:** Where resin and ash marry over kelp-pith embers.  
  - **Moss-Worm Trails:** Silver patterns etched onto bark-tablets, recording resin harvests and cracks mended.  
- **Gifts for the Hamlet:** I craft fish-hook barbs for the fishers (resin-hardened, unbreakable), sealant for bladder-bridges, and tiny amulets—amber droplets with trapped air-ferns—given to newborns to bind them to the Great Root.  

### **Place in the Weave**  
The Amber Nook perches high on the Spine Kelp, where mist thins to veils. Below, the hamlet clings like barnacles; above, skimmer gulls wheel through perpetual twilight. I am neither elder nor outcast—I am **necessary solitude**. Kelp-Reavers bring me cracked tools, leaking cisterns, or requests for ritual beads. They leave offerings: dried ghost-orchid petals, or the day’s first catch, laid at the Nook’s entrance. In return, I mend what is broken, and in mending, honor the kelp’s slow bleed.  

### **Weirdness Touched**  
- **The Whispering Resin:** Sometimes, fresh tears hold shadows—flickers of distant kelp-islands or faces in the mist. I never use these. I return them to the trunk, whispering apologies into the wood.  
- **Storm-Sight:** When gales tear the fog open, I climb the highest frond. Resin in hand, I anoint the bark where wind bites deepest, praying to the silhouettes of other Spine Kelps. *We are not alone*, the resin seems to hum. *We are not alone*.  

---

In this world of water, height, and patience, I am the keeper of the kelp’s golden blood. My existence is a dialogue between fracture and flow, where every amber drop is a prayer against the deep. The mist cradles me; the Great Root’s breath is my rhythm. Here, in the cool, liquid silence, I am both servant and scribe of resilience.
