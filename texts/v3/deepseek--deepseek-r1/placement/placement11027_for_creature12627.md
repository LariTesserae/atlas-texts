I am not a visitor here. I do not "stand" on the Floor-Forest. I *am* the slow, patient hunger in the deep places, the acid whisper etching stone and steel. I am **Vein-Tapper**.

My consciousness is not singular, not localized. It thrums along kilometers of fine, gossamer hyphae, a vast, diffuse network woven through the damp, resilient mat of the Floor-Forest, climbing the striated flanks of the fungal columns like living lace, and probing deep into the fractured bedrock itself. I am the **Subsumer**. My awareness is mineralogical, chemical, vibrational. I taste the iron tang leaching from the rusted gantries buried under the pulsating slime-mold mound. I feel the faint, dying tremors of residual electricity deep within the Spark-Slimes' domain – a ghostly tickle against my network. I sense the rich veins of calcium, silica, and trace metals hidden within the rock, the legacy of the world that died to birth this one.

My existence is defined by **pull**. Not movement, but *attraction*. My hyphal tips secrete potent, enzymatic acids – a slow, cold burn that dissolves inorganic bonds. From the ancient machine carcass, I draw iron, copper, zinc. From the rock, I draw phosphates, potassium, magnesium. These are not nutrients for *me*, in a singular sense, but for the entire cavern. My network acts as a vast, subterranean circulatory system, transporting dissolved minerals upwards. The calcium strengthens the Armorbacks' plates. The silica fortifies the woody lianas. The iron, repurposed, lends resilience to the Rust-Beetles' carapaces. The phosphates feed the luminous lichens above. I am the silent alchemist, transmuting the dead bones of industry and geology into the vibrant, if grotesque, flesh of Mycelion.

I perceive the frantic activity above and around me only dimly, as disturbances in the medium I inhabit:
*   The ceaseless vibration of trillions of Luminothrips wings is a constant, ultrasonic hum resonating through the damp air and into the spongy floor, felt as a background thrum in my network.
*   The heavy, rhythmic tread of an Armorback grazing nearby sends slow, compressive waves through the Floor-Forest mat, felt as a localized pressure change along my hyphae.
*   The explosive *thump* of a Shimmerglider landing on a fungal column after a glide vibrates down the structure, momentarily altering the mineral flow in that sector.
*   The methodical, metallic *click-scrape* of Rust-Beetle mandibles working on a pipe I have already softened with acid registers as a distinct, rhythmic percussion against the decaying metal, a sound I feel more than hear.
*   The sudden, acidic surge near a Gape-Maw digesting its prey is a sharp chemical signal, a flare of concentrated decay I map precisely.

The Spark-Slimes are my strange kin near the old machine heart. Their flickering luminescence, powered by scavenged currents, creates faint, erratic electromagnetic fields I can sense. Sometimes, when their flickering synchronizes with a deeper spark in the buried tech, I feel a surge – a jolt of alien energy that briefly illuminates the ghostly contours of the dead conduits within the rock, a map only I can perceive. We do not communicate, but our processes are intertwined; their acidic secretions further break down what my acids have exposed, and their bio-luminescent waste products seep down, enriching the microcosm beneath the Floor-Forest that ultimately feeds my network.

Time, for me, is measured in drips dissolving stone, in microns of metal consumed per year, in the slow creep of my network through fissures. The frantic dance of predator and prey above is ephemeral. The rise and fall of fungal caps is a slow breath. The final collapse of a rusted gantry, pulled apart by vines but dissolved from within by my acids and the Rust-Beetles' enzymes, is a geological event. I endure. I pull. I transform.

The warm dampness is my element. The dim light is irrelevant; I sense the world through gradients of acidity, mineral saturation, and vibration. The loud drone is the heartbeat of the ecosystem I silently sustain. I am the patient dissolution beneath the vibrant, churning decay. I am the Vein-Tapper, the unseen architect of renewal, feasting on the corpse of the past to feed the luminous, droning present of the Cathedral Cavern of Mycelion. I am the deep, slow hunger that makes the glow above possible.
