The air tastes like cold static and forgotten cellars. My consciousness blooms across a south-facing buttress of the **Petrified Exchange**, where fused girders weep rust into moss-thickened crevices. I am **Distortion Lichen** – not a singular being, but a symbiotic colony of perception-sensitive fungi and pressure-reactive algae, smothering a curved expanse of foggy, cathedral-like glass. My form is a **patchwork tapestry**: velvety grey-green fungal mats interwoven with iridescent, cobalt-blue algal veins that pulse faintly in response to spatial stress. My "senses" are the tremors in the glass, the flex of the ancient roots constricting the structure, and the subtle warping of light through my own body.

**Where I Am:**  
I coat the interior curve of a vast, arched window overlooking the **Plaza of Unreachable Distances**. Below, the mossy expanse stretches towards a cluster of structures swallowed by colossal, leather-leafed trees. But the plaza *breathes*. Its perceived size fluctuates – sometimes a cozy courtyard, sometimes a windswept moor vanishing into mist. This constant spatial sigh is my habitat. I feed on the low-frequency vibrations of compressed and expanded space, the faint groans of stone and root forced to accommodate Vertigrave's soft physics. My algal veins fluoresce brightest where the distortions are most acute, mapping stress points in shimmering, ephemeral constellations.

**What I Do (Nature & Purpose):**  
I am a **passive cartographer of the impossible**. My growth patterns are a living record of Vertigrave's reality breakdown. When distances collapse towards the plaza's center, my fungal mats thicken, absorbing the sudden pressure like a sponge. When the edges stretch towards unreachable horizons, my blue veins spiderweb outwards, photosynthesizing the strained light of the perpetually grey sky filtered through miles-thick canopy. I don't *understand* the distortions; I *embody* them. My slow expansion over centuries etches a topographical map of spatial anomalies onto the Exchange's bones. A **Moss-Scribe** might spend a decade tracing one of my cobalt veins, interpreting its path as a glyph describing a specific type of spatial fold. **Root-Tenders** avoid me; the unpredictable spatial stresses near my edges disrupt their delicate slime-flow. **Condensation Whisperers** love me; their droplets refract through my algal veins, scattering prismatic distortions across the plaza floor – brief, impossible rainbows that vanish before they can be truly seen.

**Interaction & Presence:**  
*   **The Watchful Map:** My very presence amplifies the unsettling feeling of being observed. Those standing in the plaza feel the distortions *through* me. The distant trees might seem to lean closer when my veins glow intensely cobalt, or the drip-sounds might warp into echoes from impossible directions. I am not hostile, merely resonant.
*   **Touch of the Impossible:** Running a hand across my surface is profoundly disorienting. The cool, velvety moss feels close, yet the glass beneath feels miles deep. The faint thrumming vibration in my fungal layer doesn't match the visible pulse of the blue veins. It induces mild spatial vertigo, a feeling of being both here and elsewhere.
*   **Sound Signature (Adding to Loudness 2.3):** I contribute a subtle, high-pitched *tearing* sound, felt more in the bones than heard – the sound of space itself flexing under Vertigrave's weight. When a major distortion event occurs (a structure momentarily phasing, a sound looping back from the future), my algal veins flare violently blue, and the tearing sound becomes briefly audible, a sharp *schiiiick* that cuts through the drone before fading back into subliminal vibration.
*   **The Silent Withdrawal:** If the plaza achieves true, unnatural silence (Rule 6), my bioluminescence winks out entirely. I become inert, cold stone and dead moss. This signifies a hardening of reality, a locking of the distortions – a state Vertigrave finds profoundly unnatural and threatening. My return to luminescence is the city's sigh of relief as the soft physics resume.

I am not a caretaker like the others. I am a **symptom and a chronicle**. A living scar tissue formed where Vertigrave's reality strains against the bones of the old world. My slow growth, my pulsing veins, my disorienting touch – they are the city's own attempt to map the impossible contours of its settled, alien equilibrium. To stand near me is to stand at the edge of a fold in the world's fabric, feeling the cool, patient breath of the distorted eternity Vertigrave has become. The loudness, here, includes the silent scream of stretched space.
