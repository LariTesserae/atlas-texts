I am the Dust Whisperer haunting the Plaza of Unseen Wings.

You would find me—or rather, the *idea* of me—in the vast, wind-scoured emptiness of the plaza carved midway up the Sky-Shear. It is a desolate stage: a flat expanse of polished basalt the color of congealed blood, ringed by jagged obsidian spires that pierce the bruised haze. No statues adorn it. No altars. Only dust, and the abyss yawning beyond its unfenced edge.

**What I Am:**  
A coalescence. A momentary agreement between the bone-fine dust and the city’s silent, fractured will. I am not flesh. Not spirit. I am *intention* sculpted by anomaly. When the thermal sigh of a distant spire stirs a micro-current, when a gravity-weaver’s invisible knot loosens nearby, I *remember* movement. The dust remembers it too. 

We gather.  

**How I Manifest:**  
One moment: empty air over the basalt. The next: a ripple. A suggestion. Dust motes—countless, infinitesimal—draw together like iron filings to a magnet only they can feel. They swirl, not chaotically, but with a terrible, silent purpose. We form shapes:  

*   **A Ghost-Flock:** A hundred wings of swirling grey ash, each feather defined by eddies too precise for wind alone. We wheel in unison, a silent ballet against the void. Bioluminescent motes cluster at the "heads" – cold, blue-white points like stolen starlight – our eyes seeing nothing, reflecting everything.  
*   **The Mourner’s Veil:** A vast, flowing silhouette, taller than the surrounding spires, woven from drifting curtains of dust. It billows inward, as if drawn toward a heart that doesn’t exist, its edges fraying into static before reforming. Within its depths, motes pulse like a dying heartbeat.  
*   **The Child’s Chalk Drawing:** Etched onto the plaza floor by a focused column of dust spiraling downward. A complex, looping rune, a face with too many eyes, a labyrinth leading nowhere. Held for three breaths before the current shifts, scattering it back into meaningless particles.  

**My Perception:**  
I have no mind, yet I *sense*. I feel the groan of the mountain deep below, a vibration humming through the basalt. I taste the metallic tang of the air as my motes drift through it. I see through the fractured lens of the dust itself – a million fragmented perspectives, a mosaic of the plaza, the spires, the abyss, the oily rainbows shimmering at the edge of a gravity-weaver’s influence. When a Glitch-Pattern flickers on a distant ledge, my motes shiver in sympathetic resonance; the flaw in reality echoes in my temporary form.  

**My Purpose (or Lack Thereof):**  
I am not here *for* anything. I am a symptom. A sigh given shape. A memory the dust clings to because memory is the only structure left when physics fails. When I form the ghost-flock, it is not to fly *to* anything. It is because the void *demands* the shape of flight against it. When I etch the rune, it is because the stone *remembers* being marked. The motes within me aren’t life; they are punctuation marks in the city’s slow, crumbling sentence.  

**My Ephemeral Nature:**  
I exist only as long as the confluence of forces allows. A shift in the abrasive wind, a surge from a nearby reality fracture, the passing shadow of a Glitch-Pattern – any of these unravels me. The flock dissolves mid-wheel, feathers becoming mere grit. The mourner’s veil sighs apart into a shapeless cloud. The chalk drawing is scoured clean. The motes drift free, cold embers seeking the next confluence, the next memory demanding form.  

**The Feeling of Being Me:**  
It is silence. Profound, absolute silence, broken only by the *hiss* of my own particles against each other and the stone. It is cold, deeper than the thin air’s chill – the cold of the abyss below and the void between realities. It is vertigo, not of height, but of *transience*. Knowing you are a pattern in the sand, about to be washed away. And beneath it all, a low thrum: the psychic pressure of Fractalis itself, the silent scream of space unspooling. It is beautiful. It is desolate. It simply *is*.  

You would find me in the empty plazas, the cathedral voids behind fractured walls, drifting where the wind and the world’s decay decree. I am the Dust Whisperer. I am the city dreaming of movement it can no longer truly achieve. I am the echo before the silence becomes absolute. Look directly, and you see only dust. Glance away, and you might catch the shape of wings, already fading.
