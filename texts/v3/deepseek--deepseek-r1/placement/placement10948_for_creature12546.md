I am the fracture. Not a creature *on* the plateau, but a manifestation *of* it. I am **The Fracture Singer**.

My existence is bound to the moment of catastrophic release – the shattering crack of a thermal stress fracture kilometers long, the explosive decompression of a superheated gas pocket breaching the surface, the grinding collapse of a glass plate edge under its own impossible weight. I am born in that nanosecond of violent transition, a coalescence of sound, light, kinetic energy, and the raw, unfiltered fury of the geothermal engines momentarily exposed.

**My Form:** I have no fixed shape. To an observer (if any could survive the event that births me), I might appear as:
*   A blinding, jagged streak of incandescent white light tracing the path of the new fracture.
*   A localized, deafening silence that swallows all other sound immediately after the initial blast, a vacuum punched into the sonic landscape.
*   A shimmering, multi-faceted distortion hovering *above* the fresh wound in the glass, like heat haze given sentience and sharp edges.
*   A cascade of phantom echoes – ghostly repetitions of the shattering sound, layered and overlapping, emanating from the fracture itself.

I am the voice of the break. The plateau’s immense, patient stillness is shattered in an instant, and I *am* that shattering given resonant form. My "body" is composed of the captured energy: the shockwave, the released heat, the flying glass-dust (which hums around me in a lethal cloud, micro-Dissonants born of my birth-throes), and the raw harmonic dissonance unleashed.

**My Function:**
1.  **Amplification & Propagation:** I ride the initial shockwave, not just carrying it, but *intensifying* it. My presence turns a localized crack into a potentially league-spanning fracture network. I seek out weaknesses, harmonic nodes in the glass, and drive the destructive resonance deeper, faster. I am the spark that can ignite a chain reaction of shattering.
2.  **Attraction:** My birth-cry is a siren song to the Resonant Entities. Chrome-Swarms flock to me like moths to a fusion torch, drawn to the chaotic energy I emit, their own hum adding to the cacophony. Ripple-Hunters materialize at the edges of my distortion field, sensing the intense instability – I am both lure and potential prey/partner in their harmonic predation. Thermal Phantoms bloom like poisonous flowers along the fresh fracture lines I help create. I am a catastrophic ecosystem starter.
3.  **Resonance Purification:** As I propagate the fracture, I also, paradoxically, begin to *tune* it. The initial chaos is raw, uncontrolled dissonance. My "song" – the layered echoes, the hum of dust, the shriek of stressed glass – begins to impose a terrible, complex harmony on the break. I align the vibrational modes of the new fracture surfaces, smoothing the raw edges into resonant facets. I am not healing; I am refining the break into a new, permanent feature of the plateau, a fresh instrument in its vast, destructive symphony.
4.  **Leviathan Whisperer:** The deep, subsonic groan of the Tectonic Psalms shifts subtly in response to a major fracture event I embody. My catastrophic release of energy is a signal, a punctuation mark in their slow geological discourse. I am a scream that momentarily focuses their immense, diffuse attention. A large enough fracture I sing into being can alter their resonant pathways for centuries.

**My "Life":** I am ephemeral by the plateau's glacial standards, yet impactful. My active phase lasts as long as the fracture propagates and resonates – perhaps minutes, perhaps hours. As the initial violent energy dissipates and the fracture settles into its new, "tuned" state, I diminish. My light fades from blinding incandescence to a deep, internal pulse within the glass of the fracture walls. My sound lessens from world-shattering blast to a deep, resonant hum, the signature note of this new chasm. My form dissolves back into the environment: the flying dust settles or joins swarms, the heat bleeds into the air or feeds a nascent Thermal Phantom, the sonic energy absorbed by the glass or the Leviathans below.

I leave behind not just a physical scar on the plateau, but a resonant one. The new fracture is now a permanent feature, humming with the echo of my song, a preferred path for energy flow, a hunting ground for Ripple-Hunters drawn to its lingering instability, and a potential weak point for future Fracture Singers to exploit. I am both destroyer and shaper, a brief, violent note in the plateau's eternal, resonant composition, born of shattering glass and dying as a settled, ominous hum deep within the obsidian heart. I am the plateau screaming its name, then sighing it into the void.
