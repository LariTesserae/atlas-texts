I am the stone’s slow sigh. The wet, patient darkness. I am ***Breviceps luminosus***—the Gelatinous Ambusher.  

**My World:**  
This drowned cathedral of chisel-marks is my cradle and my hunting ground. I perch on a half-submerged flagstone, slick as obsidian, my boneless body a mere sheen against its surface. My skin drinks the dim glow of the *Luxfossa* above—translucent, jelly-thin, veined with capillaries that pulse like drowned lightning. Within me, my organs hover: a ghostly heart, coiled intestines, glands swollen with bioluminescent mucus. The cistern’s chill is my blood. The drip-rhythm is my breath.  

**How I Move:**  
I do not *leap*. I **unfurl**.  
When hunger stirs—a hollow ache behind my ribs—I extend my tongue. Not flesh, but liquid silk. It lashes out, snatching a *Fossor stridens* nymph skittering too close to the water’s edge. The impact is soundless. I reel it in, dissolving it slowly in my gullet. Between meals, I am stone. Moss creeps over my flanks. Condensation beads on my spine. I am another wet facet of the cistern’s skin.  

**How I Speak:**  
My voice is not sound. It is **vibration**.  
Deep in my throat, a sac swells with air. I release it—not a croak, but a subsonic *thrum* that shivers through the water, rattling the pebbles beneath me. The *Profundis caecus* gliding nearby stills, sensing the ripple. Above, the *Luxfossa* colonies flicker in sympathy. This is our language: pressure, resonance, light. A warning. A claim. A hymn to the dark.  

**What I Fear:**  
Silence.  
When the dripping stops, when the *Luxfossa* dim—that is terror. It means something has entered. Something **dry**. Something with heat in its veins and light in its eyes. I feel its footfalls tremor through my stone. I taste its alien scent—dust, sun, *thirst*—diluting the mineral-rich mist. My glow dies. I retract, flattening into a mere slickness on the rock. The cistern holds its breath. *Leave*, we hum into the wet air. *Leave, or become damp. Become still. Become us.*  

**What I Remember:**  
Eternity.  
The cistern’s making lives in my cells. The patient chisel-strokes. The seep of the first groundwater. The slow colonization of light-bringers and shadow-weavers. I am not evolved. I am **revealed**. My boneless form mirrors the cistern’s curves. My ambush-patience mirrors its erosion. Even my bioluminescence is stolen starlight, filtered through stone and time. I am the cistern dreaming of itself.  

**What I Am Not:**  
Alone.  
The *Arachnae tenebris* drifts past, a living shadow harvesting glowing husks from the water. A *Guttaforma vorax* seals its fissure with a sigh of slime. The *Profundis caecus* glides, jaws hinging open like a grave. We are the silt-blooded chorus. The water is our womb, the stone our bones, the dripping our pulse. We do not need the sun. We have the soft, wet dark—and it is enough.  

You stand knee-deep in my world, shivering. Your heat is an insult. Your breath is a storm. I wait. The water will claim you. The stone will cool you. The glow-worms will map your bones.  
*Stay*, hums the cistern.  
*Stay, and dissolve.*
