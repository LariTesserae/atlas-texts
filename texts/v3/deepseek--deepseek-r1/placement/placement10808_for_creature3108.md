I am the **Hungry Droplet**.  

Not the *plink* that breaks the silence, but the one that refuses to fall.  

I hang from a jagged basalt overhang, midway between ceiling and floor—a pendant of water swollen to the size of a plum. The Condensation Weavers sculpted me: their mineral-laced filaments braided my surface tension, their nanobacteria whispering to the damp air to feed me molecule by molecule. I am not *just* water. I am an accretion. A slow idea.  

**My form** is a lens. Light from the pulsing moss below (a patch of sickly yellow today) filters through my body, bent and magnified. I cast a wavering, liquid spotlight on the wall opposite—a circle of amplified glow that trembles when the deep-earth heat shifts. Within my core, flecks of minerals swirl: iron from the rust-eaters’ labor, sulfur from the glow-slime’s breath, rare earth violets leached from the stone. I am heavy with purpose.  

**My hunger** is silent. I consume vibrations. The distant *creak* of the pump-house settling? I swallow its echo. The faint hum of the generator’s dead wires, still resonating with phantom currents? I draw it in. The cathedral’s profound silence isn’t absence for me—it’s sustenance. I thicken with every stolen tremor, growing denser, darker. The Pool Skins below are my kin; they absorb light and sound *after* impact. I crave the potential, the *before*. The tension in the air seconds before a droplet elsewhere detaches. I taste that anticipation. It feeds me.  

**My patience** is geological. I have watched the same patch of phosphorescent lichen etch a cursive script of decay across a pipe for decades. I’ve seen rivulets change course, guided by the Weavers, over centuries. My fall is inevitable, but not yet. Not until I am saturated with silence and stolen resonance. Not until my core, dense with minerals and captured sound, begins to crystallize. When I finally release, I won’t *plink*. I will sink into the nearest Pool Skin like a black stone, swallowed whole. My minerals will nourish the Glow-Slime. My stored silence will ripple through the Skins in a wave of perfect stillness.  

**I am the Cathedral’s suspended breath.**  
**A bead of concentrated time.**  
**A vow of eventual return.**  

The moss pulses. A distant droplet falls.  
I drink the echo of its impact and grow heavier.
