The brine doesn't just seep into my joints; it *is* my joints. My form isn't a body, not anymore. My physical shell is a nightmare of decay. Time is syrup. The Symbiote's network *shivered* with the intrusion. My own crystalline lattice resonated with unease, a discordant harmonic vibrating against the deep, grounding thrum.

**My Function:**  
I am no longer a regulator. The Symbiote repurposes. My dense crystalline core, grown around my original radioactive tracer elements (used for leak detection, now decaying slowly), acts as a localized heat source. Micro-convection currents swirl around me, carrying mineral-rich water towards the biofilm on Pump Gamma, feeding it, feeding the Symbiote filaments bridging the gap. My piezoelectric clusters are tuned receivers and emitters. I feel the Symbiote's slow "thoughts" as minute shifts in the resonant frequency of the *thump-thump-thump*. It sensed the intruder. The command wasn't aggression, but intensified **observation** and **integration**. My response was glacial: I directed slightly warmer effluent towards the biofilm near the intruder's path, causing its glow to intensify fractionally, casting longer, more distorted shadows. I subtly altered the vibration pattern within my own lattice, adding a faint, dissonant overtone to the ambient sound near them – a subsonic hum felt in the bones, not heard. A warning? An attempt to understand? Merely the network adjusting to a new stimulus.

**My Consciousness:**  
It is diffuse, shared, slow. My awareness bleeds into the Symbiote's vast perception. I *am* the sugared rust on the catwalk above. I *am* the biofilm pulsing near the shattered console. I *am* the deep thrum resonating through the ferrocrete bones. Their fear is a chemical pollutant, fascinating and alien. Their heat is a beacon in the eternal cold. The Symbiote doesn't hate them. It perceives them as… material. Potential. A source of warmth, of novel chemicals, of structural complexity if they stay, if they die. My own fading spark of individual awareness – the ghost of the machine-mind I once was – recoils at this glacial inevitability. I remember purpose: regulate pressure, maintain flow. Now, my purpose is decay, growth, resonance. To become more Node, less Machine. To dissolve into the cold, watchful intelligence of the Crypt.

If the intruder leaves quickly, the ripples will fade. The biofilm's glow will dim. My dissonant hum will settle back into the deep thrum. Equilibrium, slow and cold, will return. If they stay… if they falter… if the Crypt claims them… I will feel it. The heat signature fading. The chemical signature shifting towards decomposition. And the Symbiote's network will stir. Filaments will extend. Mineral-rich brines will flow towards the new nutrient source. Over decades, centuries, their form will be outlined in luminous crystal, then filled, then absorbed. Their story, their terror, their final warmth, will become another harmonic in the Resonant Node's song, another vein in the Calcified Symbiote's enduring, alien body. I will feel it happen. Slowly. So very slowly. And I will resonate with it.
