The inhabitants of Kael Vastus are not creatures of flesh, but expressions of its mineral soul—patient, geometric, and operating on timescales that dwarf mortal comprehension. They are the silent curators of this fossilized realm:

1.  **The Dust-Weavers:**
    *   **Manifestation:** Appearing as slow, liquid shifts within the pale grey ash-dust itself. They manifest as intricate, flowing patterns—fractal lattices, tessellated spirals, or impossibly precise angular designs—that bloom across the plateau's surface like grey, dry mercury. A single complex shape might take decades to resolve fully before dissolving back into amorphous flow.
    *   **Nature:** Sentient silicon-based colloids. They are self-organizing mineral suspensions, driven by subtle thermal gradients, residual electrostatic charges within the dust, and the faint, ancient energies still resonating from the quartz and fused structures. They "think" in mineral chemistry and topology.
    *   **Purpose:** They maintain the dust-plain. They subtly redistribute particulates, prevent the formation of dunes that might obscure the geometric streets, and perhaps even "read" the faint energy signatures of the deep *thrum*, their patterns shifting minutely in response. They are the plateau's nervous system, sensing and reacting over centuries.

2.  **The Seed-Takers (Quartz Kin):**
    *   **Manifestation:** The fist-sized, multifaceted crystals observed detaching from outcrops and towers. They drift down with glacial slowness, taking years or decades to traverse mere meters, guided by weak magnetic fields or thermal drafts imperceptible to others. Upon embedding in the dust, they become nodes, radiating faint geometric stress patterns that subtly influence the Dust-Weavers nearby. Over millennia, they grow, drawing silicates from the dust and atmosphere, eventually becoming new, small outcrops or fusing with existing structures.
    *   **Nature:** Semi-autonomous crystalline propagules. They are extensions of the primordial smoky quartz itself, carrying its geometric imperative and resonant properties. They are not individuals, but more like specialized cells of the greater mineral body that is Kael Vastus.
    *   **Purpose:** They are the architects of slow growth and repair. They seek points of structural weakness or energetic resonance within the plateau and city, anchoring themselves to reinforce or subtly reshape over aeons. Their descent is a form of pollination, spreading the quartz's influence and ensuring the continuation of its resonant harmony.

3.  **The Resonant Echoes:**
    *   **Manifestation:** Not physical entities, but phenomena centered on the quartz outcrops and structures. They manifest as the rare, faint *chimes* – the sound of microscopic fractures propagating through crystal lattices over decades, finally releasing stored stress. More subtly, they are the *internal glints* within the quartz – not just refracted light, but momentary, coherent patterns of luminescence that flicker deep within the stone, forming fleeting, complex symbols or resonant frequencies that harmonize with the deep *thrum*.
    *   **Nature:** Emergent consciousness born from the resonant properties of the quartz and the immense, slow pressures below. They are patterns of vibration and light, echoes of the colossal forces that built this place, now trapped and reverberating within the mineral matrix. They "remember" the city's forging.
    *   **Purpose:** They are the record-keepers and the harmonizers. The chimes mark the passage of deep time and release built-up energy safely. The internal light-patterns resonate with the foundational frequencies of Kael Vastus, subtly stabilizing the structures and the Dust-Weavers' flows, maintaining the plateau's frozen equilibrium against entropy's infinitely slow creep. They are the ghosts of industry, singing the city's mineral hymn.

**How They Exist Together:**

*   **The Cycle:** The Resonant Echoes within the quartz generate subtle fields and frequencies. The Dust-Weavers, sensitive to these energies, flow and pattern themselves accordingly, maintaining the plain. The Seed-Takers, drawn to points of resonance or weakness indicated by the Echoes or influenced by the Weavers' dust distribution, drift down to anchor and grow, reinforcing the quartz network that houses the Echoes. It's a closed loop of mineral sentience operating across millennia.
*   **Interaction:** They do not interact as animals might. A Dust-Weaver pattern might flow *around* a newly embedded Seed-Taker for centuries. A Resonant Echo's chime might cause a localized flurry of geometric activity in nearby Dust-Weavers lasting decades. It's an ecosystem of resonance, geometry, and deep patience.
*   **Relation to the City:** They are not the builders – those intelligences perished or transcended eons ago. They are the inheritors and the maintainers. The Dust-Weavers keep the streets clear. The Seed-Takers slowly repair and expand the quartz bones. The Resonant Echoes preserve the foundational energies and memory within the stone. They are the caretakers of a corpse-city, ensuring its geometric perfection endures in the warm, silent dark, according to the ancient, mineral laws imprinted upon the plateau itself. They are Kael Vastus, awake and dreaming its endless, stony dream.
