The Plateau of Geometric Tears does not host life as we understand it – creatures with independent will, hunger, or cycles of birth and death. Instead, it cradles **Resonant Attendants**, ephemeral manifestations of the plateau's own frozen processes. They are not separate fauna, but rather *articulations* of the environment's inherent functions, given fleeting, semi-autonomous form. Rare, spectral, and utterly integrated, they move through the luminous mist like thoughts through a vast, dreaming mind.

1.  **The Crystalline Stewards:** These are the most substantial of the Attendants, yet still insubstantial. They appear as shifting, humanoid silhouettes woven from refracted light and condensed mist, their forms echoing the faceted columns. Their "bodies" are hollow frameworks, defined by shimmering edges of prismatic vapor that constantly evaporate and reform. They glide silently across the Living Pave or float weightlessly along the bridges. Their sole function seems to be *maintenance of perfection*. With tendrils of mist, they gently stroke the edges of a hexagonal plate where a bead of water threatens to leave a faint trail, absorbing the moisture instantly. They trace the glowing fractal veins on a column, their touch causing a brief, brighter pulse – perhaps clearing a microscopic obstruction or reinforcing the bioluminescent signal. They are the plateau's immune system and quality control, ensuring no deviation from the eternal pattern. They have no faces, only shifting focal points of condensed light within their vapor forms.

2.  **The Mist-Weavers:** Smaller and even more transient, these Attendants resemble the "moth-like" creatures mentioned, but are revealed as complex condensations. They are intricate, three-dimensional laceworks of water vapor and cold light, constantly dissolving and re-knitting themselves. They cluster around the rhythmic weeping points on the crystalline columns, their forms vibrating in perfect resonance with the *plink... plink... plink* of falling water. As a droplet forms, a Weaver might briefly solidify around it, guiding its perfect spherical fall into the waiting lens-depression below, ensuring no splash, no deviation. They also drift through the high mist, their delicate forms acting as nucleation points, subtly regulating the haze's consistency and luminosity, keeping the diffuse light constant. Their flight is silent and seemingly purposeless to an observer, yet integral to the atmospheric equilibrium.

3.  **The Gelatinous Condensates:** The "living mercury" forms are **Fluid Catalysts**. They are slow, amoeboid masses of hyper-dense, structured water and organic solutes siphoned from the Living Pave. Translucent, they pulse with intricate, shifting geometric patterns of light blue and deep green – internalized fractal echoes of the larger structures. They flow with glacial slowness across the Pave, seemingly aimless. Their function is hydrological regulation and substrate interface. When they encounter a minor irregularity in the Pave's absorption – a place where water pools slightly too long – they flow over it. Their internal chemistry subtly alters, drawing the excess moisture *into* themselves before releasing it as pure vapor elsewhere. They might seep into an unseen pore at the edge of a hexagon, delivering trace nutrients gleaned from the surface or clearing microscopic blockages within the living substrate. They leave no trail, simply vanishing when their task is complete, their substance reabsorbed.

**Nature of the Resonant Attendants:**

*   **Ephemeral & Manifested:** They are not born; they *coalesce* when a specific maintenance function is required by the plateau's equilibrium, drawing substance and energy directly from the Pave, the mist, or the water. They dissolve back into the environment once their subtle task is done.
*   **Purpose-Bound:** They possess no intelligence, only a resonant directive tied to their function – maintain the geometry, regulate the water, preserve the light and sound. They are biological algorithms given temporary form.
*   **Silent & Unresponsive:** They make no sound beyond the ambient hum and drip. They do not react to external stimuli unless it directly threatens the plateau's stasis (which is nearly impossible). An observer could walk through a Crystalline Steward and feel only a cool, damp resistance like thick fog.
*   **Part of the Symphony:** Their movements are slow, rhythmic, and utterly predictable, blending seamlessly with the metronomic dripping and the resonant hum. A Mist-Weaver's vibration *is* part of the falling droplet's sound. A Fluid Catalyst's pulse might subtly harmonize with the thrumming of a nearby bridge. They are notes within the plateau's unchanging song.
*   **Alien Aesthetics:** Their beauty is cold and precise. The play of light within their vapor or gel forms is mesmerizingly geometric. Seeing them reinforces the unsettling feeling that this entire place is a vast, self-regulating mechanism, a biological engine idling perfectly in an eternal moment. They are the tears given form, the hum made manifest, the geometry embodied – not inhabitants, but expressions of the Plateau of Geometric Tears itself.

They drift, they coalesce, they tend, they dissolve. They are the plateau tending to itself, ensuring its weeping, humming, geometrically perfect stasis endures forever. To encounter one is to witness the landscape thinking.
