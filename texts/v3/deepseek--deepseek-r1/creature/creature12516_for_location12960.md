The inhabitants of the Shatterwood Plateau are not merely creatures *on* the Worldwood—they are expressions *of* it, evolved to thrive within its deafening, light-bleached, and violently shifting architecture. Life here is sparse yet intense, each organism a masterpiece of hyper-specialization, echoing the plateau’s themes of resonant energy, mineral austerity, and turbulent symbiosis.  

### **Dominant Fauna**  
1. **Windskimmer Swarms**  
   - **Form:** Razor-thin, iridescent arthropods with four diamond-faceted wings spanning 3 meters. Bodies are jointed silver chitin, tapered like wind-blown shards.  
   - **Adaptation:** Hollow bones vibrate in harmony with the Wind Roar, allowing them to surf gales without tearing apart. Wing-edges collect static electricity from the ozone-thick air, stored in crystalline abdominal nodes to stun prey or fuse with mates mid-flight.  
   - **Role:** Aeroplankton feeders. They sieve wood-dust and airborne spores through mouth-filters, their swarms appearing as sudden, metallic blizzards before vanishing into the glare.  

2. **Bore Grub Colossi**  
   - **Form:** Segmented behemoths (20–30m long), resembling armored centipedes fused with drilling rigs. Heads are rotating discs of bio-ceramic teeth, glowing faintly from internal chemoluminescence.  
   - **Adaptation:** Thrive in the pressurized dark of the Worldwood’s vascular layers. Exoskeeters absorb kinetic energy from sap-pump thuds, converting it into growth. Waste secretions mineralize into new "bone" for the Worldwood, reinforcing tunnels.  
   - **Role:** Ecosystem engineers. Their grinding opens new fluid channels and fractures bark-plates, triggering collapses that reshape the plateau. Their larval forms—pale, burrowing maggots—are prey for Spore Raptors.  

3. **Crystal Weavers**  
   - **Form:** Delicate hexapods no larger than a hand, bodies translucent as cracked quartz. Legs end in resin-exuding spinnerets.  
   - **Adaptation:** Detect stress-fractures in the Worldwood via seismic vibrations. They "sing" to each other in ultrasonic frequencies, inaudible over the plateau’s roar. Their resin hardens exposed fractures into iridescent scar-tissue, sealing wounds or creating prismatic nest-hives that amplify sunlight into energy stores.  
   - **Role:** Healers and saboteurs. Some colonies strengthen weak points; others deliberately weaken plates to access deeper sap-lines, causing catastrophic shears.  

4. **Spore Raptors**  
   - **Form:** Avian-reptilian hybrids with featherless, leathery wings. Beakless mouths house retractable proboscises for piercing grub larvae. Talons are magnetic, adhering to the Worldwood’s iron-rich hide.  
   - **Adaptation:** Navigate via wind-current sonar—ears are funnel-shaped organs that map turbulence. Nests are built in the lee of arch-buttresses, woven from crystallized resin and grub chitin. Eggs hatch explosively during wind-lulls, leveraging rare silence to avoid predators.  
   - **Role:** Apex micro-predators. Hunt Windskimmers in daredevil dives through chasms, and raid Bore Grub hatcheries by melting entry holes with acidic saliva.  

### **Symbiotic & Micro-Fauna**  
- **Static Leeches:** Palm-sized, gelatinous blobs clinging to vents. Ingest electrical discharges from steam-hisses, glowing like dying stars. Their "droppings" fertilize Dust Lichens.  
- **Echo Mites:** Microscopic vibration-parasites infesting Bore Grub joints. Amplify the grubs’ thudding into mating calls, but weaken exoskeletons—making them easier prey.  
- **Sky-Jellies:** Floaters made of gas-filled cellulose sacs, drifting kilometers high. Filter Worldwood canopy-exhalations. When punctured by Crystal Weaver resin-darts, they crash like biological meteors, scattering nutrients.  

### **Ecological Dynamics**  
Life here follows **Resonance Rules**:  
- **Boom Cycles:** When a new arch-buttress erupts, Bore Grubs swarm to devour soft growth-tissue. Windskimmers feast on released spores. Spore Raptor clutches synchronize hatching to these events.  
- **Bust Cycles:** Plate collapses bury entire ecosystems. Crystal Weavers survive in fracture-air pockets; Windskimmer eggs lie dormant in dust-drifts for decades.  
- **Death as Architecture:** Corpse-windfalls are vital. A fallen Spore Raptor becomes a grub-feast, its skeleton mineralized into the Worldwood. Dead Bore Grubs petrify into new struts bridging chasms.  

### **The Unseen Inhabitant: The Plateau Itself**  
The Worldwood is no passive stage. Its groans are not random—they are *communication*. Vents hiss in patterns that scatter Dust Lichens to sun-rich zones. Sap-pumps adjust rhythms to lure grubs where growth is needed. In this realm, even the silence is alive: during wind-lulls, the plateau "listens" through its roots, sensing planetary tides before roaring back to work.  

To stand here is to witness life not as something that *endures* chaos, but as something *forged* by it—a symphony of teeth, wind, and resonating wood where every shriek is a prayer to the storm.
