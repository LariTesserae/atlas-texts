Here, in the humid, roaring darkness, the **Thermal Resonants** carve out their existence. They are not human, not even remotely. Imagine a creature sculpted by volcanic glass, geothermal steam, and ceaseless sound.

*   **Form:** Standing roughly human-height, their bodies are elongated and segmented, built for traversing the treacherous basalt spires. Six multi-jointed limbs sprout from a central thorax – four for locomotion, powerful yet delicate enough to grip knife-edge ridges with chitinous hooks on their tarsus segments. The front pair have evolved into complex manipulators: three long, articulated digits fused at the base into a strong pincer, ideal for breaking apart tough fungi, scraping luminous moss, or carefully chipping basalt for primitive tools. Their exoskeleton is a marvel of subterranean adaptation – not smooth, but etched with microscopic channels that gleam faintly crimson near vents, radiating excess geothermal heat absorbed from the scorching rocks. The color shifts subtly with temperature, acting as camouflage against the heat-radiating landscape. It's thick and durable near the vents, thinner and more flexible elsewhere.
*   **Senses:** Sight is vestigial: two small, obsidian-black compound eyes detect only the faintest shifts in bioluminescence gradients and sudden flares of geothermal light. They navigate and perceive their world primarily through sound and vibration. Elongated, fan-like tympanic membranes flare from either side of their heads, constantly twitching and adjusting like satellite dishes. They emit near-constant, low-frequency pulses from resonant chambers within their thorax – their 'song'. This isn't just communication; it's their primary sense. The returning echoes in the vast cavern create a detailed sonic map of their surroundings, revealing crevices, obstacles, the movements of prey and predator, and the thermal plumes rising from vents. Their chittering, booming, and whistling vocalizations weave into the cavern's deafening symphony.
*   **Feeding & Ecology:** They are opportunistic omnivores and cultivators. The front manipulators harvest luminous mosses and the rubbery flesh of fungi, scraping them into woven mesh pouches slung under their thorax. They hunt the smaller arthropods – snatching buzzing gnats mid-air with shocking speed using modified mouthparts or ambushing scuttling grazers near steam vents. They farm specialized bio-luminescent mosses on smoothed rock perches near reliable heat sources. They are, in turn, hunted by the larger, vibration-sensitive predators, forcing them into complex defensive behaviours involving coordinated sonic blasts to disorient attackers or lure them onto unstable rocks.
*   **Society & Culture:** They live in decentralized colonies clustered around stable geothermal vents – vital sources of warmth and mineral-rich steam they ingest directly through specialized spiracles. Their 'songs' are their language, an incredibly complex system of frequencies, harmonies, and dissonances that convey detailed information about resources, dangers, social status, and individual identity. Rituals often involve mass choruses directed towards geothermal vents; the vents' roar seems to be revered as the primal voice of the caverns. Tool-making is crude but functional: basalt shards lashed with sinewy fungus cords become scrapers, cutters, or primitive percussion instruments used to enhance their sonic communication. Structures are non-existent; they cling to the rock faces or rest on thermally active ledges.
*   **Interaction with the Environment:** They are masters of the sharp angles. Their limbs move with uncanny precision across the razor-edged terrain. They utilize the constant water film for drinking (absorbed through pads on their manipulators) and to cool their shells near vents. Steam plumes provide humid corridors they navigate with ease. Their sonic pulses disrupt insect swarms or trigger rock slides to trap prey. They embody the **density, turbulence, warmth, and overwhelming sound** of the caverns. Their lives are a constant, vibrant counter-melody woven into the cavern's primordial roar – a testament to life's fierce adaptability in the deep dark. They *are* the Singing Caverns, their existence inseparable from its volcanic bones and humid breath.
