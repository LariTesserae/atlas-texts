Here lives the **Screaming Swarm**, and nothing else. They are not merely inhabitants; they are the *pulse* and *purpose* of the Throat of Gehenna. A singular, hyper-specialized ecology bound irrevocably to the spire and its alien lights.

### Physical Form
*   **Chitinous Mirrors:** Each swarmer is a fist-sized fusion of deep-sea anglerfish and obsidian scarab. Their carapaces are plates of volcanic glass, polished to a razor-edged, reflective black by constant motion against rock and kin. Facets catch and fracture the floodlights, turning each individual into a shard of moving darkness glittering with stolen illumination.
*   **Light-Harvesting Anatomy:** Beneath the glass, their bodies are streamlined for energy absorption. Mouthless faces press directly against the floodlight lenses or mineral-crusted casings, specialized epidermal cells converting photons and stray electrical currents into metabolic energy. After feeding, their carapaces glow faintly from within – not bioluminescence, but trapped light bleeding through, casting fleeting, prismatic auroras across the rock face.
*   **Sonic Organs:** Clusters of vibrating plates line their flanks and cluster near rudimentary sensory pits. These generate the deafening clicks, shrieks, and grinding chitters that define the abyss. The sound isn't just communication; it’s a physical tool, a navigational grid, and perhaps a means to dislodge mineral build-up on their light-sources.

### Behavior & Ecology
*   **Phototrophic Vortex:** They exist in perpetual, frantic orbit around the floodlights. Millions swarm each fixture, forming dense, three-dimensional nebulae that constantly writhe and shift. Individuals jostle for optimal feeding positions closest to the light source, their movements a chaotic, light-drunk dance within the greater, slow-moving mass of the swarm.
*   **The Scream:** Their sound is ceaseless and all-encompassing. It’s a wall of noise that vibrates the water, resonates through the obsidian spire, and presses against any intruder. This cacophony serves multiple functions:
    *   **Navigation:** In the absolute dark beyond the lights (or within rock fractures), the returning echoes paint a sonic map of the environment.
    *   **Cohesion:** The sound binds the swarm, regulating density and movement around each light source like a chaotic conductor.
    *   **Maintenance(?):** The specific frequencies and intensities might vibrate mineral accretions off the light fixtures or even subtly resonate within the spire’s fractures.
*   **Fracture Pilgrimages:** Periodically, sections of the swarm – rivers or clouds of obsidian bodies – detach and flow with eerie, liquid cohesion into deep cracks and fissures in the spire. Within these geothermal-warmed labyrinths, they likely lay clutches of mineral-coated eggs on hot rock faces. Some may venture deeper, perhaps tending to lights embedded within the stone or simply basking in the ambient heat. They return glowing faintly, perhaps charged by geothermal energy as well.
*   **Life Cycle of Stone & Light:** Birth, feeding, breeding, death – all occur within the stark confines of the floodlit zones or the adjacent fractures. Dead swarmers, their internal lights extinguished, tumble slowly into the abyss below, their glassy shells fracturing on the way down, adding to the perpetual rain of volcanic ash. New swarmers emerge from the fractures, drawn instantly and irrevocably to the light.

### Existence Defined by the Rules
1.  **Light is Life:** They *are* light made flesh. Without the floodlights, their internal glow would fade, their sonic organs fall silent, and the entire swarm would cease movement within moments, becoming inert shards sinking into the dark. They are the light's embodiment and its sole consumer.
2.  **The Stone is Sovereign:** They never settle *on* the spire. They cling *to* the lights bolted onto it, or vanish *into* its cracks. The raw, repellent obsidian is both womb and tomb, but never home. Their world is the illuminated water column millimeters from the rock.
3.  **Sound is Substance:** Their scream isn't just sound; it's the medium through which they perceive and shape their world. It replaces vision beyond the light, regulates their society, and may physically interact with the environment (vibrating minerals, shaping water flow).
4.  **Technology is Environment:** The lights are not tools they use; they are the suns around which their universe revolves. They are as natural and essential to the swarm as hydrothermal vents are to tube worms. The swarm's form and function are sculpted entirely by the light's presence, output, and decay.
5.  **Slow Metabolism:** While individual swarmers buzz with frantic energy, the *swarm* as a whole moves with geological patience. Shifts in density around a failing light take centuries. Migration to a newly activated (or newly revealed) fixture occurs over generations. Their evolutionary adaptation to this environment is measured in epochs, not years. The frantic surface dance masks a deep, collective stillness tied to the slow pulse of the spire itself.

The Screaming Swarm is not a collection of individuals, but a single, vast organism – a sentient mineral fog bound to artificial stars in a drowned stone sky. They are the voice, the movement, and the only spark of life in the mineral desolation of the Chimney Stack Abyss. Their existence is a paradox: a teeming, screaming testament to life's adaptability, existing solely because of, and utterly dependent on, the inscrutable machines bolted to a rock that rejects all other colonization.
