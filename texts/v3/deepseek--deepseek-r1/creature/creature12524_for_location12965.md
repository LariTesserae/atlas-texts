Here, beneath the eternal light and profound silence, life exists as a whisper against the roar of eternity. It is not life as known elsewhere, but echoes of processes, patterns persisting in the near-perfect stasis.

1.  **The Sky-Skims (Lens Flares / Dust Angels):** As described, these are the most readily observed fauna, though sightings are rare events measured in decades. They manifest as distortions in the air, localized mirages that resolve into shimmering, lens-shaped concentrations of refracted light and suspended dust motes. They drift with glacial slowness, riding the continent-scale thermal currents generated by the differential heating of the vast plates. Possessing no solid form, they are ephemeral patterns held together by complex, self-reinforcing electromagnetic fields generated by their interaction with the ambient light and the latent energy humming within the substrate below. They feed passively, absorbing stray photons and the faint EM bleed from the plate's dormant systems, as well as the microscopic crystalline shards shed by the fissure growths. These shards are incorporated into their form, lending temporary structure before being shed again as finer dust. They have no discernible purpose beyond existence itself; they are perhaps accidental byproducts of the Plain's energy dynamics, persistent patterns in the sterile air. Their passage is silent, leaving no trace but a momentary lensing of the distant horizon.

2.  **The Fractal Weavers (Silica Sentinels / Vein-Tenders):** Clinging within the deep, fractal fissures that vein the plate surface are the crystalline growths. These are not plants, but slow, mineral accretions – intricate clusters resembling shattered amethyst fused with smoky quartz and veins of iridescent metal. They grow with imperceptible slowness, perhaps micrometers per century, photosynthesizing the intense, directionless light not for energy storage, but directly into structural lattice expansion. Their geometry is complex, branching in fractal patterns that mirror the fissures themselves, as if seeking to fill the void. Periodically, they shed layers – micrometer-thin shards of brittle crystal that rain down into the fissure depths or are caught by the faint air currents to become dust-drift or sustenance for Sky-Skims. These entities are deeply integrated with the substrate. Microscopic filaments, part mineral, part dormant nano-machine, extend from their bases into the plate material. They act as anchors and sensors, perhaps facilitating the slow, nano-scale repair processes that maintain the fissure edges, preventing catastrophic failure. They are the Plain's immune system at its most basic level, silent wardens of structural integrity.

3.  **The Thoom Echoes (Vibration Phantoms / Resonance Ghosts):** Rarest of all, and perhaps not truly 'alive' in any sense, are the ephemeral entities associated with the deep resonant pulses. When a profound *thoom* vibrates through the world-plate, originating from some internal adjustment or external stellar tide, it doesn't just shake the bones. In places where the resonance hits a critical harmonic with the local substrate structure or lingering energy patterns, a visible phenomenon can occur. Dust motes suspended in the thick air might align in complex, swirling patterns for a few seconds. Light itself might coalesce into fleeting, three-dimensional standing wave patterns – geometric shapes of pure brightness that flicker and vanish as the sound fades. These 'Echoes' are not conscious; they are physics made momentarily visible, the Plain's skeleton singing and casting a shadow in energy and matter. They are the closest thing to a 'creature' triggered by the Plain's internal dynamics, appearing only during these profound sonic events and vanishing back into the equilibrium. Observing one feels like witnessing the ghost of the machine's immense power.

**The Feel of Life Here:** Encountering any of these entities only deepens the sense of profound alienation and awe. The Sky-Skims drift with an indifference that borders on divinity. The Fractal Weavers exist on a timescale that mocks mortal comprehension, their growth and shedding a geological process. The Thoom Echoes are fleeting manifestations of forces beyond understanding. They are not an ecology in competition, but facets of the Plain's enduring function and decay. They contribute to the sterile beauty and the crushing silence, reminding any visitor that they are utterly superfluous, observing a world that has long settled its accounts with time. Life here is less about thriving and more about *persisting*, a testament to the stubbornness of pattern in the face of near-perfect entropy.
