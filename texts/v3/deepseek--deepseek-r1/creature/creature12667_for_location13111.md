The inhabitants of the City of Sweating Stone are not separate from its architecture; they are its **breath, its condensation, and its slow pulse**. They are the **Dew-Sculpted**, mineral-organic hybrids shaped by the cliff’s weeping stone and the relentless heat. 

### Physical Form
- **Lithic Skeletons:** Their frames are jointless, interlocking plates of the same basaltic stone as the city, fused seamlessly. These plates are porous, absorbing ambient moisture like sponges.  
- **Capillary Veins:** Beneath the stone, a lattice of glassy filaments (identical to the crystalline growths on walls) channels condensation through their bodies. This network glows faintly when saturated, casting fractured light on their obsidian skin.  
- **Thermal Sacs:** Bulbous nodules, akin to the city’s pulsing clusters, swell along their spines and joints. These store geothermal energy, venting excess heat as shimmering airwaves during peak daylight.  
- **Movement:** They shift with glacial slowness, joints creaking like settling rock. To traverse vertical drops, they secrete a viscous, quick-hardening resin from their palms, forming temporary handholds on the cliff face.  

### Sustenance & Existence  
- **Water Harvest:** They press themselves against dew-glistening walls, capillary veins drinking directly from the stone. Excess moisture pools in their skeletal hollows, slowly metabolized by symbiotic mineral microbes.  
- **Energy:** They bask in shadows during the day, thermal sacs absorbing residual heat. At night, they cluster near venting fissures or pulsing nodules, recharging via infrared radiation.  
- **Growth:** Over decades, crystalline filaments accrete on their bodies like frost, thickening their stone plates. Elders resemble walking geodes—cracked stone shells revealing intricate, luminous crystal cores.  

### Society & Purpose  
- **Hive of Patience:** They perceive time in centuries. "Communication" is the resonant vibration of a hand placed on stone, transmitting intentions through the city’s conduits—a language of subsonic hums.  
- **Caretakers of Silence:** They maintain the city’s hidden machinery. With a touch, they stimulate growth in the crystalline filaments, repairing fractures in walkways or reinforcing conduits. The skittering constructs are their hands—remote-operated tools grown from ceramic and copper nodules.  
- **Vertical Castes:**  
  - **Deep-Damp:** Dwell in the cool, lower tiers. Their bodies drip constantly, filaments thick as moss. They monitor condensation basins and adjust moisture flows.  
  - **Sun-Scorched:** Inhabit the highest spires. Their thermal sacs burn brighter, skins polished mirror-smooth to reflect light. They watch the bleached sky for solar flares that could parch the city.  
  - **The Fused:** Elders who settle into niches, stone skin merging with the cliff. Their capillary networks expand into the city, becoming living thermostats regulating heat vents.  

### Rituals  
- **The Weeping Ceremony:** During rare temperature drops, they gather in cavernous recesses. As condensation flows faster, they vibrate in unison, resonating with the city’s deep thrum. Stone nodules pulse in response, casting kaleidoscopic shadows—a "song" of stability.  
- **Grafting:** When a Dew-Sculpted nears total immobility, companions carve their still-living crystal core into the city walls. There, it grows into a new thermal nodule or conduit junction.  

They are the city’s **conscious geology**: patient, damp, and heavy with time. To them, evaporation is a sigh; a century, a single breath. Their existence is a testament to endurance—a life built on sweat, stone, and silence.
