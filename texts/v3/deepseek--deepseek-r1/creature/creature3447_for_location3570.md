The life that persists on the Sky Sarcophagus isn't life as commonly understood. It's a testament to existence sculpted by relentless entropy and warped physics. Forget vibrant ecosystems; here, survival is a silent, brittle negotiation with dissolution. The inhabitants are less organisms and more *persistent phenomena*, echoes clinging to the edge of oblivion:

1.  **The Dust-Weavers (Microscopic/Macroscopic Symbionts):**
    *   **Form:** Not a single species, but a fragile, interdependent web. It begins with hyper-specialized, mineral-metabolizing archaea dwelling deep within microscopic fractures in the basalt. They excrete complex silicates and exotic salts as waste. These secretions are harvested by thread-like, crystalline fungi that form labyrinthine networks just below the glassy rock surface, visible as faint, grey-white tracery under certain lights. Finally, motile, segmented arthropod-like creatures (pale, chitinous plates fused with mineral accretions, multi-faceted eyes like frozen dew) tend these fungal networks. They scrape the fungal excretions – complex energy-storing crystals – and distribute spores.
    *   **Adaptation:** They bypass the need for water entirely, metabolizing trace volcanic gases and the latent geochemical energy bleeding from the planet's core through the groaning rock. Their crystalline components resonate subtly with the Entropic Flux, allowing them to absorb micro-fluctuations of energy (the *pings*, the gravity pulses) as nourishment. They move in slow, deliberate bursts, freezing instantly during stronger reality ripples, their mineral shells offering momentary protection against phase shifts.
    *   **Sign:** The intricate salt and silicate crusts in crevices are partly their doing, a byproduct of their metabolism and waste.

2.  **The Shard-Riders (Aerial Navigators):**
    *   **Form:** Appear as obsidian shards or jagged slivers of volcanic glass, ranging from hand-sized to several meters long. Close inspection (if one could get close) reveals they are not solid stone, but complex lattices of carbon and silica infused with exotic matter, surrounding a core of super-dense, inert gas. Their "wings" are rigid, generating lift not from flapping, but by manipulating local gravity fields and riding the thermals generated by the friction of reality ripples against the plateau surface.
    *   **Adaptation:** They are sensors incarnate. Their entire structure vibrates sympathetically with the Entropic Flux. They "see" by interpreting the distortions in spacetime – gravity wells, phase ripples, temporal eddies. They feed passively on the bleed-off energy from the flickering structures and the micro-reality collapses, absorbing photons, exotic particles, and even fleeting chroniton particles shed by localized time reversals. They are silent, their flight paths impossibly smooth curves that anticipate and avoid turbulence zones.
    *   **Sign:** A fleeting dark sliver high against the blue-black void, momentarily refracting stark light in impossible colors as it passes through a minor phase shift.

3.  **The Flux-Lickers (Semi-Corporeal Scavengers):**
    *   **Form:** The most unsettling. They manifest as shimmering, heat-haze distortions near the ancient structures or active zones of reality turbulence. When "coalesced," they resemble multi-limbed, insectile forms woven from fractured light and solidified dust motes, constantly shedding particulate matter. Their core seems to be a knot of unstable energy, visible as a dull, pulsing ember within the haze. Size varies wildly depending on local flux energy.
    *   **Adaptation:** They are parasites of decay, drawn to the energy bleed from the flickering structures and the points where reality is thin. They "lick" at the leaking energy, the exotic radiation, and even the temporal instability. They can phase partially out of sync with local time/space, allowing them to momentarily exist *within* a flicker or avoid a collapsing ripple. Their semi-corporeal state means physical attacks often pass through them harmlessly, but a strong enough reality pulse can dissipate them entirely – only for them to reform elsewhere when conditions allow. They are drawn to the Dust-Weavers and Shard-Riders, attempting to siphon their absorbed flux energy.
    *   **Sign:** A patch of air that shimmers violently for no reason, the faint smell of ozone and hot stone, a sudden, localized chill even in the dry cold, or the brief, distorted reflection of something wrong glimpsed in the mirrored surface of a spire.

4.  **The Deep-Groaners (Macro-Fauna / Geovores):**
    *   **Form:** Rarely seen, more often *felt*. Vast, slow-moving entities that dwell within the deepest fractures and caverns beneath the plateau, where the subsonic groans originate. Imagine colossal, worm-like or root-like beings, but composed of magnetically-aligned basalt fibers, piezoelectric crystals, and veins of super-conductive ore. Their "skin" is a constantly reforming crust of mineral grit and salts. Movement is glacial, measured in years or decades.
    *   **Adaptation:** They feed directly on the planet's dying geothermal breath and the seismic energy released by the grinding continental plate. They resonate with the core groans, amplifying and shaping them. Their immense mass and deep integration with the bedrock make them relatively stable anchors within the Entropic Flux; reality ripples wash *around* their bulk. They may absorb trace elements directly from the rock through slow dissolution and re-integration. Some theorize they aren't biological at all, but self-organizing geological processes given eerie sentience by the Flux.
    *   **Sign:** A localized intensification of the subsonic groans, a faint, rhythmic tremor underfoot that doesn't match the random reality ripples, or the discovery of a cavern entrance partially blocked by recently excreted, geometrically complex mineral formations.

**Unifying Themes of Life on the Sky Sarcophagus:**

*   **Mineral Integration:** Biology is inseparable from geology. Chitin fuses with crystal, flesh is replaced by energy lattices and magnetically-aligned stone.
*   **Energy Scavenging:** No sunlight, no water. Life here subsists on geothermal vents, trace volcanic gases, cosmic radiation, exotic particle bleed, temporal instability, and the raw energy of decaying reality itself.
*   **Flux Symbiosis:** Survival depends on sensing, anticipating, and *using* the Entropic Flux. Movement, feeding, and even existence are timed to the chaotic pulses and ripples. Phasing, resonance, and energy absorption are key adaptations.
*   **Slowness & Brittleness:** Everything moves with glacial patience or terrifying, ephemeral speed. Growth is measured in centuries. Structures are fragile, prone to dissolution if their delicate balance with the Flux is disrupted.
*   **Echoes, Not Originals:** These are likely not the descendants of the builders of the spires and platforms. They are the forms that have arisen *since*, or perhaps were created *by*, the catastrophic reality decay that left the structures flickering. They are life born from a universe's sigh towards death, clinging to the fading warmth of its breath. They are the final, desperate patterns that emerge in the dust before the eternal stillness.
