The inhabitants of this weeping, groaning foundry-dimension are not creatures born of flesh or instinct, but of the environment's own brutal logic and glitching physics. They are manifestations of the perpetual turbulence, mineral echoes given fleeting form. Forget life; think of them as *environmental processes* that have stumbled into a crude semblance of animation:

1.  **The Weepers:**
    *   **Form:** Amorphous, shifting masses of congealed atmospheric condensate, mineral sludge, and oily runoff. They range from puddle-sized to small pond-like entities. Their surfaces ripple not with muscle, but with the constant influx and evaporation of moisture. Within their semi-translucent bodies, suspended particles of slag, rust, and crystallized minerals glitter dully in the bruised light like diseased constellations.
    *   **Habitat:** They cling to weeping vertical surfaces, ooze across perpetually damp slag flats, or coalesce in the shallow, steaming pools that briefly form.
    *   **Behavior:** They are drawn to points of maximum condensation or active dripping. They move with glacial slowness, flowing like cold tar, driven by the simple pull of gravity and the absorption of moisture. Their only "purpose" is to exist as temporary reservoirs before dissolving back into steam or seeping into the ground. Sometimes, two weepers merge silently; sometimes, a large one fragments under its own unstable weight. They emit no sound beyond the soft *shlick* of their movement. Reality glitches might cause them to momentarily solidify into grotesque, sharp-edged statues before melting again, or flicker translucently like faulty holograms.

2.  **The Slag-Scuttlers:**
    *   **Form:** Jagged assemblages of loose scree, small fractured plates, and half-melted metallic fragments. They resemble crude, spiky crabs or beetles the size of large dogs, but assembled from random debris. No two look alike. Their "joints" are points of unstable friction and magnetic attraction between components.
    *   **Habitat:** Found primarily on slopes of fresh scree near collapsing structures or around the bases of embryonic extrusions where material is still shifting.
    *   **Behavior:** They are catalysts of erosion. Triggered by minor tremors, pressure shifts, or even the intense localized heat of a new extrusion, they skitter and tumble with surprising, jerky speed. Their movement is less locomotion and more a cascading collapse *in miniature*, dislodging other debris, accelerating the breakdown of unstable slopes, or inadvertently clearing paths for new material flows. They move in sudden, unpredictable bursts, then lie utterly inert for long periods, indistinguishable from ordinary rubble. A physics glitch might cause one to briefly hover or vibrate violently before scattering its components.

3.  **The Groaners (Deep Resonators):**
    *   **Form:** Less a distinct body, more a *phenomenon*. They manifest as disturbances within the larger structures or the foundational slag itself. Imagine a localized bulge traveling slowly up a colossal girder, like a peristaltic wave in metal, or a section of fused ground subtly rippling like a slow-motion liquid.
    *   **Habitat:** Within the substance of the massive, settling edifices or deep within the porous, stressed slag layer.
    *   **Behavior:** They are the source of the deep, resonant groans and sharp cracks that echo through the silence. They are stress made manifest – the physical propagation of immense pressures, settling fractures, or the slow warping of titanic components. They travel along paths of structural weakness, often preceding a larger collapse or shift. Observing one feels like witnessing the world's bones grinding together. Reality distortions near a moving Groaner are intense: gravity might pulse erratically, time might stretch or compress seconds around the event, and the affected structure might visibly flicker between solidity and a terrifying, momentary fluidity.

4.  **The Echo-Mimics:**
    *   **Form:** Ephemeral and purely sonic/visual. They appear as fleeting distortions in the mist – a suggestion of a vast, shadowy limb moving high on a tower in sync with a distant groan; the afterimage of a collapsing section flickering *before* the actual collapse occurs; the sound of dripping water perfectly replicated from an empty patch of fog.
    *   **Habitat:** Within the acoustically complex spaces between monoliths and within the dense, condensing mist itself.
    *   **Behavior:** Manifestations of the reality breakdown and the immense acoustic resonance of the environment. They are not intelligent, but rather glitches in perception and physics – sound waves reflecting in impossible ways, light bending through unstable space-time, or pre-echoes of stresses about to release. They amplify the feeling of being watched by the landscape itself. They vanish as quickly as they appear, leaving only unease.

**Collective Nature:**

*   **Mindless:** These entities possess no intelligence, no desire, no awareness. They are environmental processes – condensation, erosion, stress propagation, and reality errors – that have achieved a fleeting, autonomous expression.
*   **Ephemeral:** They are transient. Weepers dissolve, Slag-Scuttlers eventually tumble apart and become inert debris, Groaners dissipate once the stress is released, Echo-Mimics vanish like smoke. None are permanent residents; they are events, not beings.
*   **Mineral:** Composed entirely of the world's substance: water, slag, metal, stone, dust, and fractured light.
*   **Symptomatic:** Their presence and activity are direct symptoms of the world's core state: the immense heat, the crushing pressure, the constant condensation and evaporation, the titanic stresses of construction/destruction, and the unstable fabric of reality. They *are* the turbulence given crude, temporary shape.

To encounter them is not to meet life, but to witness the landscape *hiccup* – a spasm of its mineral metabolism, a fleeting shadow cast by its groaning, glitching reality. They are the closest thing this broken foundry has to inhabitants: environmental phantoms haunting their own creation.
