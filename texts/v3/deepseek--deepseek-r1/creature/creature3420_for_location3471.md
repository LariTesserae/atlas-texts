Here, amidst the screaming birth-pangs of unstable reality, life isn't merely adapted—it is *forged* from the chaos itself. Forget stable ecosystems; this is an ecology of flickering moments, desperate opportunism, and radical metamorphosis. What lives here are less creatures and more *phenomena*, clinging to fleeting coherence:

1.  **The Shriek-Kelp (Vegetation):** More mineral than plant, these are jagged, obsidian-black fronds extruded directly from the steaming rock faces during moments of relative calm. They grow explosively fast, latching onto fissures with glassy root-tendrils. Their surfaces vibrate constantly, amplifying the ambient roar into localized sonic shears that shred passing motes of potential matter, which the fronds absorb through micro-fractures. When turbulence peaks, they shatter explosively, scattering razor-sharp shards that later seed new growth elsewhere. They emit no light, but their vibrating edges fracture the dim illumination into strobing halos.

2.  **Luminarch Jellies (Fungi/Autotrophs):** The fist-sized, pulsing fungi. They aren't rooted; they drift like pale blue, bioluminescent ghosts in the calmer eddies within violent currents. Their semi-gelatinous bodies are complex lattices of solidified water-air and captured energy, forming spontaneously around concentrations of ambient creation-energy. They feed on the "scream" itself, converting sound and spatial distortion into cold light. When reality flickers strongly nearby, they multiply rapidly, blooming like sudden constellations before winking out or collapsing into unstable, pearlescent sludge—the raw material for the precipitated "structures."

3.  **Phase-Eels (Fauna - Predator/Scavenger):** The ghostly eel-like entities. They aren't solid. They exist as coiling patterns of distortion within the turbulent water-air, visible only as afterimages and refractions—a shimmer that resolves into a serpentine form for heartbeats before dissolving. They "swim" by riding shockwaves and temporal echoes. To feed, they briefly solidify *around* their prey—a flickering crab, a cluster of motes, even a destabilizing patch of rock—absorbing it into their distortion field before returning to their fluid state. Their passage leaves brief, localized zones of silence or reversed time-flow.

4.  **Fracture-Crabs (Fauna - Scavenger):** The armored, volcanic-slag colored scavengers. They are startlingly, terrifyingly real compared to the eels. Carapaces resemble freshly cooled lava rock, jagged and incredibly hard, protecting them from the sharp terrain and minor reality shears. They move with unnerving, staccato bursts of speed, seemingly teleporting short distances across stable surfaces (exploiting micro-lapses in spatial continuity). Their primary limbs are obsidian scythes, used for anchoring and shattering unstable mineral formations to access the pulsing energy within. They hunt Luminarch Jellies and devour the dissipating sludge of shattered Shriek-Kelp, but their true prize is newly precipitated matter – they swarm over fresh pearlescent slabs or coral fragments, scraping and ingesting them before they destabilize. They leave trails of rapidly crystallizing brine.

5.  **Vortex-Weavers (Fauna/Environmental Phenomena):** Are they fauna or self-sustaining turbulence? These are semi-sentient knots of current, denser and more organized than the background chaos. They appear as miniature, whirling maelstroms, visible by the silt, gas, and shimmering proto-matter they carry. They actively seek out zones of high instability—pulsing mineral veins, flickering rock faces—and intensify the chaos, feeding on the released energy. Larger Weavers can briefly solidify their cores into temporary, hollow spheres of pressurized water-air, creating fleeting, protected micro-environments where other life might momentarily flourish before the Weaver collapses or moves on, shredding its temporary haven.

6.  **The Stone-Dreamers (Sentient?):** The most enigmatic. Not creatures *on* the landscape, but consciousnesses *of* the landscape. They manifest as localized intensifications of the reality flickers within larger, pulsing mineral formations or the pearlescent "structures." A face might ripple across an obsidian cliff face; a limb of light might stretch from a glowing vein; a chorus of the ambient roar might resolve into fragmented, booming speech for a few seconds. Whether these are emergent intelligences birthed from the chaos, psychic echoes trapped in the rock, or something else entirely is unknown. They don't interact directly with other lifeforms; they simply *are*, dreaming unstable dreams that subtly warp the local physics, sometimes attracting Vortex-Weavers or causing spontaneous mineral blooms. To witness one is to feel the crushing weight of alien thought and deep time.

**How They Survive the Chaos:**
*   **Fluidity Over Form:** Rigid bodies shatter. The eels *are* distortion; the crabs exploit spatial lapses; the Vortex-Weavers *are* the turbulence.
*   **Metabolizing Chaos:** They consume sound, spatial distortion, temporal echoes, unstable matter, and raw creation-energy directly.
*   **Ephemeral Existence:** Nothing lasts. Reproduction is explosive, larval stages are brief and often parasitic/distortional, and death is often reintegration into the chaotic medium.
*   **Exploiting Instability:** The crabs seek freshly precipitated matter; the eels hunt in flickers; the Shriek-Kelp grows during calmer moments before the next upheaval shatters it.
*   **Sensory Overload as Camouflage:** In an environment of constant deafening roar and visual distortion, subtle movement or quiet is far more noticeable (and dangerous) than blending with the chaos.

To encounter any inhabitant of this place is to witness life distilled to its most desperate, opportunistic, and radically unstable essence. They are not visitors; they are expressions of the screaming creation itself, born of pressure, sound, and the refusal of potential to ever fully settle. They live on the knife-edge between existence and dissolution, as much a part of the roaring, flickering storm as the rock and the crushing ocean-womb itself.
