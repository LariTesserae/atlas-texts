Here are the inhabitants of the Sunken City of Therma, shaped by its crushing, warm, silent, and ancient embrace:

1.  **The Groan-Worms (Lithophagus thalassi):** Massive, segmented annelids, thick as ancient pillars and longer than city blocks. They are the ultimate expression of **Stone is Alive** and **Warmth Fuels Slowness**. Their blunt, mineral-crusted heads bore ceaselessly, but slowly, through the softest parts of the ruins – silt-filled foundations, crumbling mortar-analogues. They digest the stone itself via symbiotic microflora, extracting trace minerals and the trapped organic matter of millennia. Their passage is marked by the resonant, low-frequency **groans** of shifting architecture **(Sound 0.8)** – not a vocalization, but a byproduct of their movement. They are ecosystem engineers, creating new tunnels, grottos, and settling silt, unintentionally providing habitats for others. They possess minimal sensory organs, guided by chemoreception towards nutrient-rich strata and the faint thermal gradients **(Temperature 2.3)** of decaying organic matter trapped deep within the stone. Predation on them is rare; only the most desperate or specialized hunters attempt it, targeting the softer segments between their armored plates.

2.  **Silt-Stalkers (Vibrissimordax occultus):** Apex predators embodying **Darkness is Absolute** and **Quiet is Survival**. Eel-like but bulkier, with flabby, pressure-resistant bodies the color of deep silt. They are completely blind. Instead, their entire flanks and oversized, fan-like pectoral fins are covered in hypersensitive vibration receptors (neuromasts), able to detect the heartbeat of a crustacean or the fin-flick of a fish meters away through the dense water **(Density 2.0)**. They drift motionless **(Life is Opportunistic)** near overhangs or within silt drifts **(Erosion 2.0)**, perfectly camouflaged, sometimes for days. When prey triggers their sensors, they strike with a terrifying, hydraulic burst of speed – a short-range lunge powered by specialized muscles, creating a muffled **thrum** **(Dynamic 1.9)**. Their mouths are cavernous, lined with needle-like, backward-pointing teeth. They swallow prey whole, conserving energy **(Warmth Fuels Slowness)**.

3.  **Lumenscuttlers (Photocarcinus symbiotes):** Ubiquitous, crab-like crustaceans, masters of **Life is Opportunistic** and **Darkness is Absolute**. Their size varies, but their defining feature is the cultivation of luminous bacterial mats **(bioluminescence)** on their shells. Different patterns denote species, caste, or even mood – warning signals, mating displays, or lures for smaller prey. They scuttle endlessly **(Dynamic 1.9)** across the stone faces **(Stone is Alive)**, using robust, chitinous claws to scrape off the tough black kelp, luminous mats, and filter-feeder polyps **(sparse 0.8)**. Their vision is attuned specifically to the blue-green spectrum of their own and others' luminescence. They are key cleaners and grazers, and their discarded shells, still faintly glowing, become temporary shelters or lures for other creatures. They communicate through complex patterns of light flashes and subtle vibrations transmitted through the stone.

4.  **Chamber-Fans (Porifera colossus):** Giant, sessile filter-feeders embodying **Stone is Alive** and **The Water is Air**. Resembling colossal, porous, mineralized fans or folded sponges, they grow anchored to vertical walls and overhangs, often where currents **(Dynamic 1.9)** funnel plankton. Composed of a tough, flexible matrix resembling the coral-like amalgam of the ruins **(weirdness 0.9)**, they slowly incorporate silt and minerals, becoming part of the architecture. Their surfaces pulse rhythmically and slowly **(Warmth Fuels Slowness)**, drawing the thick, warm water **(Temperature 2.3)** through their bodies to extract microscopic food. They host diverse communities of symbiotic organisms within their pores – tiny crustaceans, worms, and specialized fish that find shelter and glean nutrients. Their size **(Scale 1.5)** makes them landmarks in the dark canyons.

5.  **Whisper-Jellies (Gelata pulsaris):** Large, gelatinous zooplankton, exemplifying **The Water is Air** and **Quiet is Survival**. Translucent, amorphous blobs, sometimes meters across, drifting passively **(Life is Opportunistic)** on the subtle thermals **(Temperature 2.3)** circulating through the city canyons. They possess minimal self-propulsion, relying on currents. Their bodies are crisscrossed with faint, internal veins of blue-green bioluminescence, pulsing slowly – not for sight, but as a form of passive sonar. They emit low-frequency pressure waves **(Sound 0.8)** constantly, mapping their surroundings and detecting obstacles or the gentle currents carrying their prey (tiny plankton, larvae). Predators disturbing their field trigger a defense: a sudden, intense internal flash that illuminates *them* briefly, potentially startling the attacker and attracting larger predators – a dangerous gamble enforcing the rule of quiet. Their near-perfect buoyancy makes them masters of the three-dimensional space.

6.  **Gravel-Gazers (Oculi profundus):** Sightless, bottom-dwelling fish representing **Darkness is Absolute** and **Life is Opportunistic**. Small, flattened bodies armored in dark, pebble-like chitin, perfectly mimicking patches of rubble on the silt floor **(Density 2.0, Erosion 2.0)**. They possess no eyes. Instead, their heads are dominated by elaborate sensory barbels that taste the water for detritus, decaying matter, and the chemical trails of small crustaceans. They "walk" using modified, sturdy pectoral fins, shifting small stones and sifting through silt with surprising dexterity. Slow-moving and cryptic, they are the vacuum cleaners of the abyssal floor, turning over the detritus that rains down from the city above and the activities of larger creatures. Their only defense is stillness and camouflage; if disturbed, they might emit a small cloud of obscuring ink and burrow frantically into the silt.

**The Interplay:**
*   A **Silt-Stalker** senses the vibrations of a **Lumenscuttler** scraping algae nearby and erupts from its hiding place.
*   **Gravel-Gazers** sift through the silt disturbed by the retreating **Groan-Worm**, finding buried nutrients.
*   Plankton carried on a warm thermal current is funneled past a **Chamber-Fan**, which pulses rhythmically to feed, while tiny symbionts dart within its pores.
*   A **Whisper-Jelly** drifts silently overhead, its pulsing pressure waves mapping the canyon, momentarily disturbed by the muffled **thrum** of the Silt-Stalker's strike.
*   The faint, patterned glow of **Lumenscuttlers** is the only illumination in the perpetual twilight, painting fleeting constellations on the ancient, water-smoothed stone.

Life in Therma is ancient, patient, and exquisitely adapted. It thrives in the crushing dark, turning the ruins of a forgotten world into the foundation of a slow, silent, and perpetually humming ecosystem, governed by the immutable laws of the warm, liquid abyss. Every action is deliberate, every sense attuned to vibration and chemistry, every form shaped by buoyancy and pressure. It is a world of profound quiet, broken only by the sounds of survival: the scrape, the thrum, the drip, the groan.
