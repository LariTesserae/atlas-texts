The Cathedral Cavern of Mycelion thrums with life adapted to its humid, acidic, light-starved world. Here, existence clings, crawls, flies, and dissolves within the living architecture:

1.  **The Swarm-Dancers (Primary Fauna):** The trillions of thumb-sized insects are **Luminothrips**. Their furnace-orange abdomens house symbiotic bioluminescent bacteria fueled by ingested spores and lichen fragments. Their translucent wings beat at ultrasonic frequencies, creating the cavern’s pervasive drone. They are not mere flies; they are the cavern’s circulatory system, distributing spores, pollen, and minute nutrients in their chaotic auroras. Their brief lives end as sustenance for the aerial hunters.

2.  **Aerial Shadows (Predators of the Swarm):**
    *   **Shimmergliders:** Lacertilian predators with iridescent, hydrophobic scales that refract the dim light. They launch silently from fungal columns or vine buttresses, gliding on patagia stretched between elongated ribs. Their primary weapons are sticky, projectile tongues snatching Luminothrips mid-swarm. Their eyes are multi-faceted, seeing the ultraviolet signatures of the lichens and the heat-variance of their prey.
    *   **Needle-Singers:** Furred, bat-like creatures with four membranous wings and elongated, needle-tipped snouts. They echolocate through the drone, targeting individual thrips with horrifying precision. Their fur traps condensation, requiring them to periodically scrape against abrasive fungal stalks to prevent mold growth. Their high-pitched hunting squeals are just above the thrip drone.

3.  **Floor-Forest Grazers & Scavengers:**
    *   **Armorbacks:** Millipedes longer than a human arm, encased in overlapping, chitinous plates infused with calcium leached from the rock. Their countless legs distribute their weight perfectly on the spongy Floor-Forest. They rasp at mosses, algae, and decaying rhizomes with specialized mouthparts, their slow passage leaving temporary, damp trails. Bioluminescent mites often hitch rides on their carapaces.
    *   **Oozewalkers:** Massive, dog-sized gastropods with semi-translucent, gelatinous bodies pulsing with internal blue-green light (symbiotic algae and fungi). They secrete an acidic slime that pre-digests the fungal mat and mosses they glide over, leaving persistent, shimmering trails. Their eyeless heads bear chemosensory stalks that taste the air for decay and moisture gradients. They navigate pools with surprising grace, feeding on blind larvae and detritus.
    *   **Rust-Beetles (Scavengers/Tech Consumers):** Massive, heavily-armored coleopterans dwelling near the subsumed machinery. Their carapace is a dull, corroded-looking brown, resistant to the pervasive acids. Powerful, vice-like mandibles drip a specialized enzyme that breaks down oxidized iron and other metals. They methodically dismantle the remaining gantries and pipes, incorporating trace metals into their exoskeletons. Their clicking communication echoes near the slime-mold mound, sometimes triggering faint, answering sparks from the buried tech.

4.  **Ambush Predators (Static Hunters):**
    *   **Gape-Maws:** The carnivorous pitcher plants are not passive. Their "nectar" is a narcotic cocktail. When insects land, specialized trigger-hairs cause the rubbery lip to contract rapidly, forming a temporary seal. Digestive enzymes, potent enough to dissolve chitin and soft tissue, fill the pitcher within minutes. Their interiors often glow faintly from partially digested Luminothrips.
    *   **Fungal Snappers:** Bulbous, sessile fungi camouflaged amongst the luminous stalks. When a creature (like an Armorback or Oozewalker) brushes against sensitive hyphae, they explosively release a cloud of paralyzing, sticky spores. Mycelial cords then slowly envelop the victim, dissolving it over weeks.

5.  **The Subsumers (The Truly Weird):**
    *   **Vein-Tappers:** Semi-sentient colonies of mycorrhizal fungi forming complex networks within the Floor-Forest and up the living columns. They actively seek mineral veins in the rock and the remnants of metal conduits within the old machinery. They excrete powerful acids to access these nutrients, contributing significantly to the cavern's erosion and the final dissolution of the industrial corpse. Their presence is felt as a subtle, pervasive *pull* on the mineral content of the environment.
    *   **Spark-Slimes:** Colonies dwelling within the pulsating slime-mold mound engulfing the machinery's heart. They metabolize residual electrical currents and trace heavy metals from the decaying tech. Their bioluminescence flickers erratically, sometimes synchronizing with the faint sparks deep within the mound. They slowly convert the inorganic into the organic, their acidic secretions further breaking down circuits and plating into bio-available sludge.

**The Unseen:** Beneath the springy Floor-Forest mat, in the perpetual damp darkness, exists a microcosm: blind nematodes writhing through root tangles, pale, eyeless crustaceans scuttling in capillary spaces, and vast networks of bacteria and archaea processing the constant rain of organic matter and converting rock minerals, forming the fundamental, unseen engine of Mycelion's relentless consumption and growth.

Life here is damp, corrosive, luminous, and overwhelmingly numerous. It thrives on decay, transforms industry into sustenance, and fills the immense, sparse volume with a churning, droning, dripping symphony of survival on the corpse of a forgotten world.
