The inhabitants of the Silent Nursery are not creatures of flesh and blood, but expressions of the cavern’s unique, mineral-driven reality. They are ephemeral, mineral, and deeply integrated with the environment, blurring the lines between life, energy, and geology:

1.  **The Dust-Forms (Static Scavengers):**
    *   **Nature:** Fleeting coalescences of the metallic dust and ambient static electricity. They are not individuals, but temporary patterns – environmental eddies given predatory intent.
    *   **Form:** Appear as shifting, low-to-the-ground silhouettes: scuttling insectoid shapes (many-legged, antennae-like tendrils), sinuous serpentine wisps, or flitting, moth-like puffs. Size varies from a hand-span to a meter across, but they rarely hold form long enough for precise measurement. They have no solid core, only a denser concentration of agitated dust held in shape by crackling electrostatic bonds.
    *   **Behavior:** They "awaken" near the warm stone structures or where dust currents eddy. They move with unnerving silence and surprising speed across the obsidian floor, not walking, but *flowing*. Their purpose is singular: hunt and consume airborne motes – other dust particles, microscopic spores shed by the crystalline lichen, or even the faintest thermal energy gradients. Upon contact with prey or after a few moments of existence, their electrostatic cohesion fails. They dissolve instantly back into the ambient grit with a faint, almost subliminal *hiss* of discharging static. They are the Nursery's base-level consumers, cycling dust and static.

2.  **The Shadow-Darters (Aerial Predators):**
    *   **Nature:** Rarer and more complex than Dust-Forms, these inhabit the higher airspace near the structures and phosphorescent lichen. They seem woven from concentrated shadow and the cavern's latent pressure.
    *   **Form:** Appear as tattered scraps of absolute darkness, roughly avian or chiropteran in silhouette but constantly rippling and re-knitting at their edges. They range from sparrow-sized to the span of an eagle's wings, but their darkness absorbs light, making size deceptive. Unlike the Dust-Forms, they possess a core of intense cold and negative pressure.
    *   **Behavior:** They cling motionless to the undersides of arches or deep within crevices, indistinguishable from natural shadow. When triggered by the movement of a Dust-Form below or the brightening pulse of a lichen patch, they *dart*. Their movement is near-instantaneous, a blur of consuming darkness crossing meters in a fraction of a second. They engulf their prey (Dust-Forms, loose spore clouds) whole, snuffing out light and energy upon contact. After the strike, they vanish just as quickly, melting back into the architecture's gloom. They are silent vacuum incarnate, predators of the Dust-Forms and ambient energy.

3.  **The Stone-Weavers (Silent Architects / The Structures Themselves):**
    *   **Nature:** This is not a distinct fauna, but the *emergent intelligence* or slow consciousness of the warm stone and the structures it forms. The inhabitants *are* the cityscape.
    *   **Form:** Manifested in the imperceptibly slow movements: the leaning spire, the reconfiguring arch, the subtle shift in the angle of a cantilevered block. It's the deep, inner warmth of the stone and the rhythmic pulsing of the lichen colonies (which may be symbiotic mineral growths or external indicators of the stone's energy flow).
    *   **Behavior:** Operating on a timescale incomprehensible to fleeting Dust-Forms or darting Shadows, the Stone-Weavers are engaged in perpetual, silent sculpting. They extrude new spires or arches from the seamless floor over centuries. They adjust angles to optimize the capture of the dim violet light or the channeling of static currents. They subtly reshape the environment to maintain the delicate balance of dryness, static, and mineral energy that sustains the entire Nursery. Their "purpose" is the existence and slow evolution of the Nursery itself – a geological gestation. The Dust-Forms and Shadow-Darters are transient expressions of the energy *within* this system, like sparks flying from a slow forge.

**The Ecology of Silence:**
The inhabitants exist in a closed loop of mineral energy and static charge. The warm stone provides the foundational energy. The crystalline lichen and rubbery filaments convert light and trace atmospheric elements into complex mineral structures and release spores/energy motes. The Dust-Forms consume these motes and ambient dust/static. The Shadow-Darters consume the Dust-Forms and concentrated energy, their dissolution perhaps feeding energy back into the static field or the stone itself. All of it is governed and shaped by the slow, silent will of the Stone-Weavers expressed through the living architecture. They are not born; they *coalesce*. They do not die; they *dissipate* or *reconfigure*. Their existence is a dance of dust, shadow, static, and enduring, warm stone – a silent, alien nursery indeed.
