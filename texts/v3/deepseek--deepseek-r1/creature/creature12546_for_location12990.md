The Obsidian Resonator Plateau does not host life as biology understands it. Instead, it sustains **Resonant Entities** – manifestations of energy, vibration, and fractured physics, evolved to thrive in the plateau's brutal symphony of sound, heat, and instability. They are expressions of the geothermal engines below and the resonating glass above.

### The Fauna of Fractured Harmonics:
1.  **Chrome-Swarm Dissonants:**  
    The fist-sized "insects" are not insects at all, but self-organizing knots of kinetic energy and piezoelectric discharge. Each appears as a shimmering, chrome-plated teardrop shape, its "wings" actually focused fields of vibrating air that propel it. They feed on stray sound waves and thermal differentials, their collective hum (a deafening industrial drone) not just communication but a form of communal energy harvesting. When threatened, they swarm into blinding, coruscating vortices that can induce harmonic resonance in the glass, shattering it explosively beneath a predator. They crystallize briefly upon "death," collapsing into inert, obsidian dust motes.

2.  **Ripple-Hunters (Vibrissae Umbrae):**  
    These are the multi-limbed shadows – apex predators of the surface. They are self-sustaining standing waves, complex interference patterns given predatory sentience. To mortal eyes, they appear as heat-haze distortions resolving into scuttling, multi-jointed silhouettes of utter blackness, trailing discordant shrieks that physically vibrate the observer's bones. They hunt by inducing destructive resonance in prey or the environment. They "consume" by absorbing the kinetic energy of shattering glass, the sonic boom of venting geysers, or the very life-force vibrations of other resonant entities like the Chrome-Swarm. They are drawn to instability, appearing most often near fresh fractures or during violent venting events.

3.  **Thermal Phantoms (Ignis Resonare):**  
    Invisible to most spectra, these entities manifest as sudden, localized superheating of the glass or air. They appear as patches of ground glowing white-hot, or shimmering columns of incandescent gas rising from fissures. They are not fire, but concentrated pockets of geothermal energy that have achieved temporary coherence. They move slowly, drawn to areas of high sonic pressure or deep fractures. Contact with one doesn't burn; it *disassembles* matter through harmonic over-vibration. They are short-lived but leave behind slagged, resonant glass that attracts Dissonants.

4.  **The Fracture-Weavers (Vitrum Aranea):**  
    Semi-sentient colonies of the "crystalline microbes." They exist deep within crevices and fissures, appearing as glittering, fractal frost. They are not microbes, but self-replicating mineral lattices that grow by absorbing silica from the glass and energy from vibrations. They subtly strengthen or weaken fracture lines, acting as the plateau's nervous system. Their "webs" are resonant filaments spun across chasms, humming with captured sound. Larger predators avoid them, as disturbing a Weaver-colony can trigger catastrophic chain-reaction fractures across leagues of the plateau. They communicate through shifts in the harmonic resonance of their webs, a language of subsonic tremors.

5.  **Leviathan Resonances (Tectonic Psalms):**  
    Rarely perceived directly, these are vast, slow-moving patterns of vibration deep within the glass crust and the bedrock below. They are less individual entities and more like geological moods or emergent phenomena of the planetary engines. Their passage is marked by days-long harmonic tremors, the slow warping of light over vast distances, and the sudden, inexplicable realignment of massive glass plates. They feed on the fundamental geothermal thrum and the accumulated sonic energy of the plateau. Interacting with one is catastrophic; their resonant frequency can liquefy solid glass or induce reality fractures visible for hundreds of kilometers. They are the plateau's slow, implacable heartbeat.

### Survival & Interaction:
*   **Feeding:** All entities derive sustenance from energy – sound, heat, vibration, geothermal flux, or the resonant destruction of matter/other entities. There is no organic consumption.
*   **Reproduction:** Entities "reproduce" through resonance events. A Chrome-Swarm might split when its collective hum hits a critical frequency; a Fracture-Weaver colony buds when a major shatter releases harmonic energy; a Ripple-Hunter might be born from the focused dissonance of a venting geyser.
*   **Death:** "Death" is dissolution. Entities dissipate their energy back into the environment – as heat, sound, or new resonant patterns – often seeding the creation of lesser entities or feeding the Leviathans.
*   **Interaction:** Conflict is harmonic warfare. Predators induce destructive resonance in prey. Prey flee by disrupting their own resonance or leading predators into unstable zones (like Thermal Phantom paths or Weaver-webs). Cooperation is rare, usually involving the manipulation of environmental resonance for mutual energy gain.
*   **The Unseen Engines:** The Resonant Entities *are* the biological component of the deep machinery. They regulate energy flow, mitigate instability (or exploit it), and act as a living sensor network. The Chrome-Swarms clean acoustic waste; Fracture-Weavers maintain structural integrity (or direct fractures); Ripple-Hunters cull overpopulated harmonics; Leviathans may be conscious expressions of the engines themselves.

Life here is a dance of annihilation and creation, perpetually balanced on the knife-edge of the plateau's inherent instability. The Resonant Entities are not visitors; they are the plateau's voice, its fury, and its terrifying, resonant soul, born of sound, heat, and the slow shattering of the world.
