Here, in the fractured bones of the world, the inhabitants are not creatures of flesh and bone, but manifestations of the city's dissonant soul. They are ephemera given fleeting form by the fraying edges of reality itself.

1.  **The Resonant Fractals:** In the deepest shadows of sheltered alcoves or within the cavernous voids behind fractured windows, the city's slow, resonant sighs—those subsonic groans and high-pitched thermal pings—occasionally coalesce. They form shimmering, three-dimensional patterns suspended in the dust. These are intricate, shifting mandalas of sound made momentarily visible: spiraling lattices of faint light humming a low C, or angular, crystalline grids vibrating with dissonant harmonics before dissolving back into the ambient noise. They are not intelligent, but they *communicate* – pulses and patterns that resonate sympathetically with the stone, causing distant fractures to glow brighter or veins within the glass to momentarily fluoresce in response.

2.  **The Glitch-Patterns:** These are the inhabitants most often fleetingly perceived at the corners of vision. They are the embodiment of the visual static and spatial unreliability. One moment, a section of dusty ledge appears vacant; the next, a complex, impossible knot of angles and colors writhes in that space – like corrupted digital geometry rendered in bruised twilight hues and shattered glass. They have no solid form. To look directly at them is to see only a faint shimmer over undisturbed dust or the solid rock behind. But in the periphery, they *move*: elongating across impossible distances along a causeway that wasn't there an instant before, flickering between ledges separated by lethal drops, or folding space in upon themselves in silent, geometric screams. They are less beings and more localized reality flaws becoming transiently animate.

3.  **The Dust Whisperers:** The pervasive, bone-fine dust isn't just inert matter here. Driven by impossible micro-currents created by localized gravity anomalies and the city's own subtle thermal pulses, the dust can organize. In the vast, empty plazas or drifting within cathedral-like interior spaces accessed by wall-fractures, the dust coalesces into intricate, flowing sculptures. These can resemble ghostly flocks of impossible birds winging silently across the abyss, or vast, ephemeral faces carved by the wind only to dissolve into formlessness moments later. Sometimes, the motes within them – the cold, bioluminescent embers – cluster densely, forming the "eyes" or "hearts" of these flowing phantoms. They are silent, transient expressions of the void's structure and the dust’s latent, fractured memory of movement.

4.  **The Light-Thieves:** The faint, internal luminescence bleeding from the glass spires and rock fractures is a resource. Slender, filamentous tendrils—like the crystalline frost-glass but darker, almost like solidified soot—creep along surfaces, primarily in the deepest recesses. They seek the light. Where they touch a faintly glowing fracture or vein, they pulse faintly, seemingly absorbing the radiance, growing thicker and more defined for a moment, before retreating back into shadow, leaving the source dimmer. They move with glacial slowness, only perceptible as a subtle darkening along a fracture edge over hours or days. They feel ancient and purposive, perhaps part of the city's slow, mineralogical metabolism or a parasitic echo of its decaying light.

5.  **The Gravity-Weavers:** In places where gravity falters—where dust hangs suspended in defiance or falls upwards—there is sometimes a *presence*. It manifests as an intensification of the distortion. The oily rainbows at the edges of vision thicken into tangible ribbons of warped light. Folds in space deepen briefly, becoming palpable pockets where the air seems denser, colder, and humming with latent tension. Objects or dust within these zones drift with unnerving deliberation, tracing paths that seem planned rather than chaotic. These "Weavers" are not entities one can perceive directly, but their influence is a localized amplification of the fracture. They are knots in the failing fabric of physics, exerting a subtle, invisible pressure. Standing near one feels like standing beside a deep, silent intellect pondering the dissolution of order.

These inhabitants are inseparable from Fractalis itself. They are the city’s slow decay made visible, its silent scream against the void given temporary shape. They inhabit the liminal spaces—between the stone and the dust, the light and the dark, the solid and the glitch. They are not born; they *coalesce* from the city's enduring agony and the relentless friction of unraveling reality. They are as empty and as vast as the metropolis they haunt, transient monuments to a physics that is slowly, silently, forgetting how to hold itself together. Their existence is the city's testament: even in dissolution, complexity and eerie beauty emerge from the void.
