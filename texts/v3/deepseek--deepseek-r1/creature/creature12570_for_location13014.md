The inhabitants of the Resonator Huts are the **Silica Singers**, a species so deeply woven into the basin's mineral symphony that they seem less like creatures and more like conscious extensions of the weeping stone itself. They are small, rarely exceeding a meter in height, but possess a dense, low-slung build that speaks of immense resilience. Their bodies are encased in thick, fibrous integument – not true skin, but layers of mineral-laden felt and accreted silica platelets, forming a natural, sound-dampening armor. This coating ranges in color from the deep charcoal of wet mudstone to the pearly grey of sinter, often streaked with iridescent mineral veins. It absorbs the basin's deafening drone, protecting their delicate internal organs while allowing them to feel the subtlest vibrations through their core.

Their most striking features are their limbs and sensory organs:
*   **Limbs:** Six sturdy, multi-jointed limbs radiate from a compact torso. The lower four end in broad, paddle-like feet with fibrous pads, perfect for gripping slick stone and sensing tremors through the ground. The upper two limbs are more dexterous manipulators, ending in clusters of strong, chitinous digits capable of fine work with stone tools or tuning delicate resonators. All limbs can retract tightly against the body when traversing narrow spaces or weathering a nearby geyser's slurry fall.
*   **Sensory Antennae:** Crowning their flattened heads are two pairs of complex, articulated antennae. The primary pair are thick, whip-like structures covered in ultra-sensitive vibration receptors. They constantly sweep the air and ground, parsing the basin's roar into distinct frequencies – the trickle of a specific seep, the deep thrum of a singing pool, the telltale crackle of a predator stalking nearby. The secondary pair are shorter, stubbier, and used for close-range communication, tapping intricate rhythmic codes directly onto stone or another Singer's armor.
*   **Eyes:** Small, deep-set, and protected by transparent silica lenses. Vision is secondary, useful only for perceiving the dim bioluminescence of Sky Dancers or the faint shimmer of mineral sheens in the mist. They see the world in blurred greys and muted blues, shapes defined more by texture and sound-shadow than light.
*   **Mouth/Feeding:** A simple, lipless orifice beneath the antennae. They consume precipitated minerals and nutrient-rich slurries directly, often using their manipulator limbs to scrape crusts from weeping rocks or gather filtered particulates from catchment traps. Digestion is slow, fueled by geothermal warmth radiating from the stone.

**Life and Culture:**
*   **The Song is Everything:** Silica Singers don't speak; they *resonate*. Communication is a complex language of vibrations – taps, scrapes, hums generated by vibrating plates within their chest cavity or channeled through tools. Their Resonator Huts aren't just shelters; they are instruments and communication hubs. By carefully tuning the stones with levers and chimes, they create localized pockets of sonic clarity ("quiet") for intimate exchange, or project powerful, focused vibrations ("shouts") that carry specific messages for miles through the resonant bedrock, cutting through the ambient noise.
*   **Harmony with the Chorus:** They live in symbiotic tension with the crystalline arthropods. They harvest some for their resonant carapaces (used in tools and hut tuning), but also cultivate clusters near their huts, encouraging specific frequencies that amplify useful natural sounds or deter predators. The deafening chorus isn't just background noise; it's the fabric of their society, a resource to be shaped and utilized.
*   **Stone-Shapers:** Their technology is lithic and acoustic. They shape resonant stones into tools, chisels for carving catchment channels, hammers for striking path markers, and finely tuned plates for focusing vibrations. Their greatest artisans are "Stone-Weavers," who spend lifetimes selecting and placing rocks to build or expand Resonator Huts, creating structures that flawlessly integrate with and modulate the basin's own voice.
*   **Perception of Time:** They experience time as the basin does – slow, deep, and resonant. Geyser eruptions are punctuation marks; the slow erosion of a hummock is a generational saga. Their movements are deliberate, conserving energy, flowing with the rhythms of water and stone. Sudden, violent actions (like a predator's strike or a hut collapsing) are jarring dissonances in their otherwise patient existence.
*   **The Mist Boundary:** They possess a profound, almost religious, acceptance of the mist-shrouded edges of their world. Exploration beyond is conceptually alien; their entire sensory and existential reality is defined by the basin's soundscape and mineral flows. Path markers lead *within*, not out.

**Overall Impression:**
The Silica Singers are less like traditional inhabitants and more like *custodians of resonance*. They are mineralogic golems shaped by eternal damp and deafening sound, their forms hardened against the fury of their world, their minds attuned to its deepest vibrations. They move through the gloom like slow, purposeful ghosts, their thick hides shedding water, their antennae constantly parsing the symphony of dripping stone and amplified life. They build not to dominate, but to harmonize, weaving their own subtle counterpoint into the basin's primordial, waterlogged breath. To see one hunched over a resonator stone, antennae trembling as it tunes the very fabric of the noise, is to witness a profound communion between life and the slow, singing bones of an ancient earth.
