Here dwells **Thermal Grazers** – not creatures as flesh understands them, but vast, slow-tide expressions of the Spires' own mineral logic. They are the dominant life, the visible pulse within the crushing, gelid silence.

*   **Form:** Imagine continental shelves given motive will. Each Grazer is a colony-organism, a single, cohesive entity kilometers across, though appearing amorphous from within the Spires' scale. Its body is a translucent, viscous matrix – less jellyfish, more liquid obsidian infused with glacial milk. Within its depths drift countless mineral nodules, symbiotic micro-fauna, and pulsing veins of cold bioluminescence (fractal patterns of sapphire, viridian, and deep violet). Its "underside" (though direction is fluid here) is a rippling field of millions of cilia: not soft hairs, but filaments of flexible crystal that beat in hypnotic, coordinated waves, propelling it with imperceptible slowness along the spire faces. Its "surface" is studded with sensory nodules like frozen dew, and vast, puckered orifices that rhythmically inhale and exhale the mineral-heavy water.
*   **Function:** They are thermal engines. The cilia aren't just for locomotion; they are exquisitely tuned thermoreceptors. They glide along the Spires, seeking the faintest radiant warmth bleeding from the internal red pulses or rising from hidden geothermal vents far below. Upon finding it, they settle, flattening their massive forms against the obsidian-like surface like a living poultice. Through complex chemosynthesis within their gel matrix, they convert this scant geothermal and mineralogical heat into metabolic energy, siphoning dissolved minerals directly from the water and spire surface. They do not *consume* the Spires; they commune with them thermodynamically.
*   **Light & Shadow:** Their internal bioluminescence is not for communication as we know it. It flickers and pulses in response to thermal gradients, mineral concentrations, and the deep vibrations of the Spires. It casts eerie, shifting constellations within their bodies and throws long, distorted shadows on the spires – the only significant movement of light in the eternal twilight besides the Spires' own sullen glow. This light feels *cold* to perceive, a visual echo of the ambient chill.
*   **Symbionts & Scavengers:** Clinging to the Grazers' vast flanks and trailing in their wake are the **Slick-Eels**. Serpentine forms, no thicker than an arm but stretching meters long, woven from flexible silica strands and refracting light like oil on water. They dart with surprising speed through the viscous medium, cleaning mineral accretions from the Grazer's surface, harvesting shed micro-fauna, or perhaps feeding on the Grazer's waste secretions directly. They are parasites, cleaners, and essential recyclers rolled into one, their movements a constant, glittering counterpoint to the Grazer's glacial pace. Deeper still, sensed as immense, slow-moving pressure waves disturbing the water column, are the **Lithic Leviathans**. Whether predators, rival grazers, or something else entirely is unknown. They are felt, not seen – deep groans echoing through the Spires, vast shadows momentarily dimming the spire-glow far below, immense currents stirred by their passage. They are the Spires given animate, predatory form.
*   **Architects of Flux:** The Grazers are the unwitting keystone of the Spires' **Turbulent Deep**. Their waste products – a complex slurry of catalyzed minerals and metabolic byproducts – seep onto the Spires' surfaces. This slurry acts as both potent acid and rapid-growth catalyst. In some areas, it dissolves ancient spire material, causing silent collapses into the abyss. In others, particularly near geothermal vents or fissures, it reacts violently, triggering the rapid extrusion of new, raw spire growth that hardens almost instantly into the seamless, ancient-looking structures. The Grazers glide on, oblivious, driven by thermal gradients, while their very existence fuels the city's endless cycle of self-destruction and rebirth.
*   **Existence:** To witness a Thermal Grazer is to witness geological time made manifestly mobile. They move with the patience of tectonic plates, their thoughts (if any) as alien as the mineral heart of the world. They are vast, ancient, and seemingly indifferent to anything beyond the next thermal seep. They embody the Spires' paradox: simultaneously primordial life emerging from mineral soup, and the decaying, symbiotic caretakers of a post-biological world-machine. They drift, feed, glow with cold fire, and reshape their world with every exhalation, suspended in the crushing, gelid eternity of the Sky-Drowned Spires.

They are not inhabitants; they are *processes* of Therma given form. To be near one is to feel the deep, slow pulse of the world itself, a pulse felt through the crushing water and the vibrations in the ancient stone – a pulse that speaks only of heat, dissolution, and mineral rebirth in the endless, light-starved deep.
