The inhabitants of this bleeding caldera are not creatures you recognize. They are accretions. Manifestations. Echoes of the place's eternal, screaming stasis. They are as much mineral as meat, as much vapor as vitality. Here is what endures within the crucible:

1.  **The Stone-Weepers:** Sluggish, mineral accretions that form where the weeping walls are thickest. They resemble giant, translucent slugs, but their bodies are built from layers of precipitated minerals – hematite reds, malachite greens, shimmering pyrite flecks – deposited by the eternally flowing water. They move with glacial slowness (if movement is even the right word), sliding infinitesimally downwards *with* the mineral-rich flow that feeds them. Their "mouths" are simply deep fissures in their leading edge, constantly ingesting the weeping fluid. They excrete solidified, porous lattices behind them – the very beginnings of new rock. They have no eyes, no discernible organs beyond the intake fissure and the excretory vents. They are less animals and more ambulatory geological processes, the caldera slowly building itself through them.

2.  **Vapor Wraiths:** Ephemeral condensations within the superheated steam plumes roaring from the deepest vents. They appear as shimmering distortions in the air, sometimes coalescing into vaguely humanoid or serpentine shapes for fleeting moments before dissolving back into the roar. They "feed" on the heat differentials and the mineral-laden vapor itself. They are drawn to the coolest spots – the undersides of giant fungal caps, the shaded resin arches – where they condense into heavier droplets and fall, rejoining the water cycle. Occasionally, when a reality glitch flickers nearby, a Vapor Wraith might briefly solidify into a grotesque, crystalline form before shattering back into mist. Their only sound is the hiss of their formation and dissipation, lost within the greater din.

3.  **Lichen Sentinels:** Not individual creatures, but vast, intelligent-seeming *colonies* of the bioluminescent lichens that paint the vertical surfaces. Each "Sentinel" covers an area the size of a house, its swirling patterns pulsing with complex, slow rhythms. They communicate with distant colonies via subtle shifts in light frequency and pattern, carried through the humid air and reflected light. They anchor themselves to the rock and the resin city structures, their root-like hyphae secreting mild acids to etch their hold and absorb minerals. They react to movement and changes in light (like the flickering distortions) by intensifying their glow or shifting their patterns into warning configurations – sharp angles and strobing reds. They are the silent observers, the ancient neural net painted onto the caldera's bones.

4.  **The Mycelial Hounds:** The only truly motile, predator-like entities. They emerge from the thick, rubbery algal mats and the deepest moss layers near the steaming pools. Appearing as shifting mounds of the black algae itself, they extrude multiple, whip-like pseudopods formed from bundled, glistening mycelial strands. They move with surprising speed when sensing vibration (a footstep, a collapsing droplet), flowing like viscous ink over the sodden ground. They "hunt" not for flesh, but for concentrated mineral nodules or particularly dense patches of condensed vapor. They engulf their target, dissolving it with secreted acids and absorbing the nutrients directly into their algal matrix. They leave behind only a slightly darker, wetter patch of moss. When still, they are indistinguishable from the surrounding mat.

5.  **Resin Phantoms:** Manifestations tied to the flickering distortions within the resin city. They appear as translucent, fractured silhouettes glimpsed within the hollow towers or on the impossible balconies during a reality glitch. They might seem like tall, spindly humanoids, multi-legged insectile forms, or swirling geometric abstractions, always slightly out of phase. They cast no shadow and make no sound. They are not truly alive, nor are they ghosts. They are more like *afterimages* – persistent stains of potential inhabitants momentarily forced into visibility by the unraveling reality, reflections of what *might* have been, or *could* be, in another layer of existence bleeding through. They vanish the moment the glitch snaps back to "normal."

**How they Endure:**
*   **Feeding:** Directly from the mineral-rich weeping water, the steam, the heat differentials, the light, and the very structure of the place. There is no traditional food chain, only direct absorption of the caldera's perpetual outputs.
*   **Movement:** Sliding with mineral flows, condensing/drifting with vapor, flowing like viscous mats, or being utterly sessile (Lichen Sentinels). Speed is irrelevant in a place without change.
*   **Senses:** Vibration (Mycelial Hounds), light patterns (Lichen Sentinels), heat differentials and mineral concentrations (Vapor Wraiths, Stone-Weepers). The deafening roar renders traditional hearing meaningless; sensing is tactile, thermal, or visual.
*   **Reproduction:** Asexual and geological. Stone-Weepers fracture, leaving segments to grow anew. Vapor Wraiths condense and disperse. Lichen Sentinels spread spores only during the most violent reality flickers. Mycelial Hounds bud off smaller mounds. It’s less reproduction and more fragmentation or reconfiguration, maintaining the eternal biomass.
*   **Interaction:** Minimal and indirect. Lichen Sentinels might warn each other of a Mycelial Hound's movement. A Stone-Weeper might inadvertently divert a mineral flow that feeds a lichen patch. Vapor Wraiths condensing on a Sentinel might cause a temporary dimming. They coexist within the static fury, components of the machine, not actors upon a stage.

They are not visitors. They are *consequences*. They are the caldera dreaming of life within its own unchanging, bleeding, screaming dream. They are as permanent and as much a part of the landscape as the weeping walls and the roaring steam. To encounter them is not to meet fauna, but to witness the caldera's wet, hot, mineral soul made momentarily, strangely manifest.
