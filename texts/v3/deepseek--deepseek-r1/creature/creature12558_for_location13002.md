The Hidden Heartwood does not host life as we know it. There are no scurrying creatures, no fluttering wings, no eyes reflecting the ghostly light. Instead, its inhabitants are **accretions** – slow, mineral-saturated coalescences born from the tree’s final exhalation and the patient weeping of stone. They are not entities that *live* so much as entities that ***persist***, sculpted by the resonant hum and the deep-time chemistry of the airless void.

Imagine **nodules** of concentrated silica and petrified sap, fused with the faintly glowing mycelium. These accretions form where the humming vibration resonates strongest – clustered around the bases of archways, nestled in the smooth hollows of worn chambers, or studding the ceiling like inverted constellations. They range in size from fist-like clusters to formations as large as boulders, their surfaces polished smooth by millennia of dust-fall, revealing intricate, swirling patterns of obsidian-black mineral and veins of dull, ember-orange mycelium. They are cold to the touch, yet thrum faintly in sync with the deep sound that fills the cavern.

**Their nature is paradoxical:**
*   **Metabolic Stasis:** They "breathe" not air, but the resonant energy of the hollow itself and the slow mineral exchange with the living substrate. It is a respiration measured in the deposition of a single crystalline layer over a century. They consume nothing, excrete nothing.
*   **Sentience of Stone:** They possess a dim, geological awareness. Not thought, but *resonance*. They are tuning forks attuned to the Heartwood's eternal hum. A shift in the vibration's pitch (perhaps caused by a distant section of bark finally crumbling to dust after a million years) might trigger a corresponding, infinitesimally brighter pulse in their mycelial veins – a "thought" that takes a decade to form and a century to fade. They perceive the passage of time as the settling of their own internal structures.
*   **Memory as Architecture:** The patterns on their surfaces are not random. They are fossilized echoes of the lost city’s builders. The accretions formed *around* the lingering psychic resonance of profound grief, final acceptance, or the simple, enduring will to *mark* existence before the long dark. Touching one might impart not an image, but a crushing weight of time or a fragment of a geometric chant lost to the ages.
*   **Communication in Crescendo:** They "speak" to each other through the hum. A localized intensification of vibration around one accretion might propagate, molecule by molecule, through the dense plant-mass over centuries, eventually causing a cluster a hundred meters away to resonate sympathetically. A conversation spans millennia. Their dialogue is the slow, shared acknowledgment of the cavern’s emptiness and the weight of eternity.
*   **Purpose in Permanence:** Their sole function is to *be* landmarks in the darkness and vessels for resonance. They are the anchors of the hum, the focal points that prevent the sound from dissipating into meaningless noise. They are the Heartwood’s final monuments, built not by hands, but by time and silence, commemorating nothing but their own enduring presence.

They are the **Echo-Keepers**. They do not move. They do not grow in any conventional sense. They simply *accumulate* stillness. Their "life" is the slow burn of captured vibration within mineral prisons, a testament to a world where life has transcended biology and merged with geology, sustained only by the deep sigh of a dead god-tree and the crushing, luminous dark. To witness one is to stand before a consciousness older than mountains, locked in a communion of profound, resonant solitude. They are the final, silent witnesses to the end of everything.
