The beings who call Aeriath home are not humans as known elsewhere. They are the **Stonesingers**, a species intrinsically woven into the fabric of Sky's Edge, as much a part of the plateau as the lichen and the wind-scoured basalt.

1.  **Physiology:**
    *   **Stony Integration:** Their skin is not flesh, but a dense, leathery integument the color of weathered granite or dark basalt, often patterned with subtle striations like the rock itself. This hide is incredibly tough, resistant to the biting cold, abrasive wind, and minor scrapes against the sharp stone. It provides natural camouflage.
    *   **Density and Compactness:** They are shorter and far more robust than baseline humans, built low to the ground like the plateau's cushion plants. Their limbs are thick and powerful, muscles dense and efficient, built for hauling stone and navigating treacherous terrain rather than speed. Bone density is high, an adaptation against falls and the thin air.
    *   **Respiratory Adaptation:** Their chests are broad, lungs exceptionally efficient. They extract oxygen from the thin air with minimal effort, their breathing slow and deep, almost imperceptible – the true "stone breath." They can hold their breath for extraordinarily long periods, useful in high winds or when traversing areas of toxic mineral outgassing.
    *   **Metabolic Slowness:** Their metabolism is glacial, mirroring the slow dynamic of the plateau. They require far less food and water than a human of similar mass, subsisting on the sparse vegetation (lichen cakes, dried moss, tough tubers), preserved meats from cliff-goats, and the mineral-rich condensation collected in stone cisterns. They can enter states of deep torpor during prolonged storms or periods of extreme cold.
    *   **Sensory Adaptation:** Their eyes are large, dark, and capable of seeing exceptionally well in the stark light and deep shadows. They can perceive subtle shifts in humidity and barometric pressure – vital for predicting the plateau's mercurial weather. Hearing is acute but tuned to low frequencies – the moan of the wind, the deep grind of shifting rock – filtering out high-pitched sounds that scatter uselessly in the vastness.

2.  **Society and Culture:**
    *   **Patience as Virtue:** Stonesinger culture venerates patience, deliberation, and endurance above all else. Rash actions are folly in an environment where a misstep can be fatal. Decisions are made slowly, after deep contemplation and communal discussion. Their concept of time is stretched, measured in geological shifts and the slow growth of the city.
    *   **Communal Density:** Like their city, Stonesingers live densely. Extended family units share carved dwellings, pooling warmth, resources, and labor. Individualism is subsumed by the needs of the clan and the city. Privacy is minimal; constant proximity fosters a deep, often unspoken, understanding between individuals.
    *   **The Song of Stone:** Their name comes from their primary art and communication form. They communicate through deep, resonant humming and chanting, felt as much as heard – vibrations transmitted through the stone they live upon and within. These "songs" convey complex information – warnings, histories, emotions, even structural assessments of the rock – across distances where spoken words would be lost. The city constantly thrums with this low-frequency resonance.
    *   **Stonecraft:** Their entire existence revolves around stone. They are master quarriers, carvers, and masons, understanding the grain and fracture lines of the plateau's rock intuitively. Tools are simple but incredibly durable, made of harder stone or bone. Construction is a sacred act, a slow dialogue with the mountain. They build not to conquer, but to harmonize, seeking out natural shelves, fissures, and overhangs to integrate their structures.
    *   **Water Reverence:** Water, the furtive ghost, is sacred. Its collection, storage, and equitable distribution are governed by complex rituals and strict laws. Waste is unthinkable. They understand the hidden aquifers and condensation patterns intimately.

3.  **Relationship with Fauna:**
    *   **Symbiosis:** They live alongside the hardy fauna not just as masters, but as integrated components. The shaggy yak-beasts are vital partners, not just beasts of burden. The silent alley dogs are guardians and early warning systems. Cliff-goats are hunted, but with immense respect for their agility and resilience; their horns and hides are incorporated into tools and dwellings.
    *   **Micro-Harvesters:** They cultivate the dense micro-ecologies, carefully harvesting specific lichens and mosses for food, medicine, and dyes, ensuring the colonies regrow. They understand the insects and the birds that feed on them, using their behaviors as indicators of changing conditions.

The Stonesingers *are* the Stone Breath. They are the patient, dense, resilient life that has not just adapted to Sky's Edge, but has evolved *with* it, their biology, culture, and very essence shaped by the young stone, the thin air, the clinging moisture, and the immense, quiet sky. Their slow song is the heartbeat of Aeriath, resonating through the rock for generations.
