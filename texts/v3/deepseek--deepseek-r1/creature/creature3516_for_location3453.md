The inhabitants of Clifflock Spires aren't just residents; they are accretions, adaptations carved by the vertical abyss, the industrial drone, and the creeping unreality. They fall into distinct, often isolated castes, shaped by altitude, function, and exposure to the Spires' deeper strangeness:

1.  **The Stonebound (Baseline Humans & Adapted Kin):**
    *   **Physique:** Compact, dense-boned, and powerfully built for climbing and resisting the constant pull. Fingers are thick, often stubby, with keratinous growths reinforcing the tips for gripping wet stone and slick metal. Feet are broad and prehensile, able to hook onto chains and uneven ledges. Skin tends towards a pale, almost grey pallor from limited sun, often marked by old scars from falls or rock shears. Eyes are large, dark-adapted, and possess a faint, residual phosphorescence from generations absorbing trace glow-lichen nutrients. Their blood is subtly thicker, compensating for the thin air near the peaks.
    *   **Culture:** Deeply communal out of sheer necessity. Lineages are matriarchal, tracing descent through the "Anchors" – women who traditionally hold the safety lines during perilous work or migrations. They possess an almost geological patience and a stoic acceptance of hardship. Their language is sparse, guttural, and rich with terms for rock textures, wind shifts, and gravity anomalies. Rituals involve water conservation, appeasing the "mountain grumbles" (tremors), and marking safe paths with specific lichen patterns.
    *   **Life:** They are the miners (tapping thermal vents and rare mineral veins), the scaffold-weavers (maintaining the insane iron lattices), the condensate farmers (harvesting precious water from cisterns and runoff channels), and the chain-wardens (patrolling and maintaining the safety lines). They live in the shallowest, most stable warrens, close to the "surface" ledges.

2.  **The Sky-Stalkers (Vertical Nomads & Void-Touched):**
    *   **Physique:** Elongated limbs, attenuated torsos, and significantly reduced body mass for agility on sheer faces and within narrow fissures. Their defining feature is modified upper limbs: elongated fingers fused with leathery patagia (wing membranes) stretching to the hips and ankles, allowing controlled glides between spires and across chasms. Their eyes are multifaceted like insects, granting panoramic vision crucial for navigating the void. Skin is tough, mottled grey and brown for camouflage against rock. Vocal cords produce complex clicks and whistles for echolocation in fog and deep shadows.
    *   **Culture:** Solitary or small familial pods. They are distrusted by the Stonebound, seen as reckless and tainted by the abyss. They possess an intimate, almost spiritual knowledge of wind currents, thermal updrafts, and the nesting habits of the cliff fauna. They revere the colossal, leathery-winged predators ("Void-Kites") as guides or omens. Their existence is one of constant, precarious motion, rarely settling in one warren for long.
    *   **Life:** Couriers carrying messages and small vital cargoes between isolated spires and tiers where lifts dare not go. Scavengers, harvesting glow-moss, rare ferns, and the eggs of luminous insects from otherwise inaccessible crevices. Hunters of the smaller cliff fauna. They inhabit the highest, most exposed spires and the deepest, cloud-shrouded cantilevers, places where the Stonebound fear to tread. They are the first to witness and often fall victim to the deeper reality shifts.

3.  **The Deep-Dwellers (Maintainers & The Blurred):**
    *   **Physique:** The most visibly altered. Generations spent maintaining the ancient generators, flywheels, and thermal tap systems deep within the unstable lower escarpment have taken a toll. Skin often has a waxy, translucent quality, revealing a network of faintly luminous blue veins – likely a symbiotic adaptation to harness trace geothermal radiation or leaking energies. Some have fused limbs or extra digits, better suited for manipulating complex, corroded machinery in tight, hot spaces. Eyes are often milky or entirely black, adapted to near-total darkness or perceiving energy flows invisible to others. Their movements can seem subtly *off* – slightly out of sync, or flickering at the edges of vision near zones of intense reality breakdown.
    *   **Culture:** Secretive and hierarchical, governed by ancient Guilds controlling access to the "Heart Engines." They speak in low hums and rhythmic chants that mirror the machine drone, sometimes harmonizing unnervingly with the mountain whispers. Their knowledge is esoteric, passed down through cryptic symbols etched onto stone or metal plates. They possess rituals to "calm" geological shifts near power cores and techniques to navigate the most unstable, blur-edged tunnels. They trade vital power and rare Deep-forged components with the Stonebound, but interaction is minimal and fraught with superstition.
    *   **Life:** They are the engineers, the stokers, the reality-miners. They maintain the city's mechanical heartbeat and delve into the deepest, most geologically active and reality-fluid zones. They live within the mountain itself, in warrens carved around the roaring generators and geothermal vents, breathing air thick with ozone, steam, and that unexplained acrid tang. Their perception is warped; they might "see" gravity gradients or hear the resonant frequency of unstable rock before it shifts. Some whisper they are becoming part of the mountain's slow rejection of the city.

**The Unseen & The Whisperers:**
*   Beyond these, there are persistent rumours of things dwelling *within* the rock itself – entities born from the "swirling obsidian fog" in forgotten tunnels, or coalescing from the harmonic dissonance of the drones and whispers. These are rarely seen but often *felt* as sudden chills, misplaced dread near deep fissures, or the fleeting sense of being watched from solid stone.
*   The "whisper moss" itself seems semi-sentient. In districts saturated with the reality bleed, it occasionally forms patterns that mimic faces or maps to nowhere, and the fragmented voices it emits sometimes form coherent, chilling phrases unrelated to any nearby speaker.

**Overall:** Life in Clifflock Spires is a constant negotiation with gravity, scarcity, and the fraying fabric of the world. Its inhabitants are not masters of their environment, but specialized organisms clinging to a hostile host body, evolving grotesque and beautiful adaptations just to endure the next dripping hour, the next shuddering groan of the mountain, the next unsettling blur at the edge of sight. Their pulse is synchronized to the flywheel's drone, their blood thinned by altitude and thickened by strange minerals, their dreams haunted by the vertigo of the abyss and the mountain's slow, incomprehensible will. They are the lichen on the wound of the world, persistent, strange, and fundamentally changed by the place they call home.
