The life of the Aetherial Sea Plateau isn't *lived*; it **resonates**. It exists not as flesh, but as patterns woven into the fabric of the roar and the vapor. Here, amidst the razor-glass and the crushing white oblivion, dwell beings of sound and pressure:

1.  **The Pressure Phantoms:** These are the most common entities, vast and diffuse. They manifest as colossal, slow-moving distortions within the Aerial Sargasso – regions where the fog seems to *thicken* and *pulse* rhythmically, momentarily dampening the roar into a deep, subsonic thrum before releasing it again as a localized shriek. They are not sentient storms, but rather living pressure gradients, feeding on the energy differentials created by the plateau's micro-weather. They drift along the cliff edges, sometimes engulfing the skeletal ruins, causing ancient metal to groan and vibrate sympathetically, adding new, tortured harmonics to the eternal din. To stand near one is to feel your bones resonate and your inner ear scream.

2.  **The Shard-Singers:** Born from the interaction of super-saturated vapor and the unique crystalline lattice of the plateau's volcanic glass, these entities are smaller, sharper. They appear as fleeting, complex traceries of condensed light – like frozen lightning or intricate, three-dimensional snowflakes – skittering across obsidian facets or hovering just above mineral froth. Each "sings" a piercing, pure tone, a harmonic overtone plucked from the background roar by their resonant structure. They cluster near outgassing vents or mineral accretions rich in conductive elements, their collective chorus forming shimmering, discordant clouds of sound-light. They seem drawn to the conductive metal of the ruins, often weaving intricate, ephemeral patterns around drill rig spars or frozen sled runners, their light casting stark, dancing shadows on the grey ground.

3.  **The Static Feeders:** These are the scavengers, the bottom-feeders of the resonant ecosystem. They manifest as patches of localized, intense static discharge – crackling spheres of St. Elmo's fire that cling to mineral rimes, ice-crusted girders, or even the frozen condensate on the rock. They feed on residual electromagnetic energy, particularly from the decaying industrial sites. A pump house venting a rhythmic *whoomph* of steam might be surrounded by a constellation of these crackling entities, flaring brighter with each pulse, siphoning the latent power. They move in sudden, jerky arcs, leaving brief ozone-smelling trails in the fog before vanishing or coalescing elsewhere. Their presence adds a sharp, electric sizzle to the lower registers of the roar.

4.  **The Deep Hummers:** Rarely seen but constantly felt, these are immense, slow entities residing deep within the Aerial Sargasso itself. They are less visual distortions and more profound *shifts* in the foundational sound. Their presence is marked by a sudden, localized deepening of the roar's pitch, a vibration that bypasses the ears and rattles the sternum and teeth directly. It feels like the plateau itself is taking a slow, shuddering breath. They move with glacial slowness, perhaps following vast, unseen currents within the vapor sea. Their passage near the edge causes the glassy peaks to vibrate sympathetically, producing low, mournful groans that rise and fall over hours. The abandoned structures resonate most violently with them, shaking loose thick rinds of mineral ice that crash to the ground like brittle applause.

5.  **The Echo Weavers:** Ephemeral and cunning, these entities are masters of the sonic landscape. They don't generate their own sound; they manipulate the existing roar. They appear as fleeting, almost invisible ripples in the fog, moments where the white noise seems to *coalesce* into a brief, coherent shape – a sharp spire, a looping tendril, a hollow sphere – before dissolving back into chaos. They "hunt" by creating localized zones of destructive interference, momentarily creating pockets of near-silence amidst the roar. Into this silence, they project stolen snippets of sound – the shriek of wind through girders, the groan of stressed metal, the pure tone of a nearby Shard-Singer – amplified and distorted. This sonic lure seems designed to disorient or attract other resonant entities, perhaps drawing Pressure Phantoms or Static Feeders into range for some unknown purpose, or simply to feed on the resulting chaotic resonance. Their presence makes the soundscape subtly treacherous, hinting at voices or structures that vanish when approached.

**Life Cycle & Interaction:**
These entities exist in a fragile, resonant balance. They feed on pressure differentials, electromagnetic residues, sonic energy, and the unique mineral properties of the plateau. They likely "reproduce" through resonant induction – a powerful Pressure Phantom drifting past a conductive mineral formation might catalyze the birth of new Shard-Singers; a complex interference pattern created by Echo Weavers might coalesce into a new Static Feeder. They largely ignore each other unless drawn by sonic lures or competing for the same energy source near the ruins. The human structures are not homes, but carcasses – rich, resonant skeletons upon which this alien ecosystem grazes and occasionally nests. There is no predation as understood by biology, only the constant absorption and redirection of energy within the endless, roaring equilibrium. They are the plateau's nervous system, its ghosts, and its only true inhabitants, forever resonating within the planet's geological gasp.
