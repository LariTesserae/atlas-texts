The inhabitants of Sinter's Basin are the **Silica-Singers**, a species of heat-tolerant, amphibious humanoids whose entire biology and culture are woven into the roaring, mineral-saturated fabric of their world. They are not masters of the Basin; they are its expression.

**Physiology:**
*   **Form:** Slightly shorter and stockier than baseline humans, with broad, flat feet evolved for traction on slick sinter and mud. Their skin is thick, rubbery, and porous, ranging from a mottled pale grey to ochre, often stained with mineral deposits. It secretes a thin, silica-rich mucus that protects against the constant moisture and mildly corrosive mineral waters.
*   **Senses:** Eyes are large, dark, and lidless, optimized for the perpetual dimness and mist. They see well in low light but lack sharp focus. Hearing is secondary; the deafening roar of the Basin makes traditional auditory communication impossible. Instead, their primary sense is **vibration**. Highly sensitive pads on their hands, feet, and along their spine detect minute tremors through the sinter and mud – footsteps, water flows, prey movement, and the complex vibrational "language" they use.
*   **Respiration:** Gills are vestigial and inefficient. They primarily breathe air through modified lungs capable of extracting oxygen efficiently from the humid, warm atmosphere, but they can also absorb dissolved oxygen directly through their porous skin when submerged in the warm, mineral-rich water.
*   **Diet:** Omnivorous, but heavily reliant on the Basin's chemosynthetic ecosystem. They scrape microbial mats from sinter surfaces, sieve shrimp-like crustaceans from the rivulets, hunt the larger arthropods and amphibious creatures, and cultivate gelatinous filter-feeders in hollowed-out sinter pools. Their digestive systems handle high-mineral content.

**Society & Culture:**
*   **Structure:** Organized into tight-knit **Resonance Clans**, each associated with a specific, complex network of sinter formations, pathways, and hollows. Clan identity is defined by the unique vibrational "signature" patterns they tap out on the sinter and mud to communicate and mark territory.
*   **Communication:** Primarily through intricate patterns of taps, scrapes, and stomps transmitted through the living stone. These vibrations convey complex information – warnings, locations, status, social bonds – over short distances. Simple gestures and touch are used face-to-face. They possess no written language; knowledge is passed down through vibrational patterns and oral tradition (shouted close to the ear or vibrated through contact).
*   **Technology:** **Primitive** but perfectly adapted. Tools are fashioned from the sinter itself (chipped blades, scrapers), bone, or chitin from larger arthropods. They weave fibers from mineral-reinforced fungal growths (the closest thing to "plants") found in the warmest, wettest crevices for nets, slings, and simple bindings. Fire is unknown and unnecessary in the constant warmth; light comes from bioluminescent cultures they nurture in hollows.
*   **Architecture:** As described, they live *within* the sinter. Pathways are worn, not built. Chambers are painstakingly excavated over generations using sinter tools, creating warrens that amplify and channel vibrations. Bridges are minimal – sinter slabs or woven fiber mats. The largest communal spaces are naturally occurring – stable mud flats or areas around major vents where the steam provides warmth and the roar is slightly less overwhelming. Density is high; privacy is a concept defined by vibration dampening within a hollow, not physical isolation.
*   **Spirituality:** Reverence for **The Constant Roar** and **The Slow Stone Growth** is paramount. Change is alien. Their existence is seen as part of the Basin's perpetual state. Major vents are sacred sites, sources of the life-giving water and sound. The slow, invisible accretion of sinter is a metaphor for their own slow accumulation of knowledge and lineage. Time is measured in generations, not days or years, as the environment itself provides no cyclical markers.

**Adaptation to the Environment:**
*   **The Noise:** They are born into the roar and live with it. Their vibrational sense allows them to function within it. Silence would be terrifying, a sign of the Basin's death.
*   **The Humidity & Warmth:** Their physiology thrives in it. Cold or dry air is debilitating.
*   **The Static Nature:** Their culture reflects the stasis. Innovation is slow, focused on refining existing techniques for navigating and exploiting the *unchanging* labyrinth. History is the present, repeated.
*   **The Mineral World:** They are literally made of it, incorporating silica and other minerals into their bones and skin. They understand the sinter's brittleness and strength intuitively.

The Silica-Singers are not visitors to Sinter's Basin; they are its emergent property. They are the sound given form, the dampness made flesh, living within the slow-growing stone, navigating their world through the tremors in the rock beneath the eternal roar. They are the embodied spirit of perpetual, roaring infancy.
