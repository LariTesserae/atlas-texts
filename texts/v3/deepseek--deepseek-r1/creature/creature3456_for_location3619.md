The true lords of the Weep Chamber defy easy categorization. They are less "fauna" and more *extensions* of the luminous flora itself – biomechanical emissaries sculpted by the sentient greenery for interaction and maintenance. Call them **Vine-Walkers**.

*   **Form:** They appear, at first glance, as the "hexapodal lizards"... yet infinitely stranger. Six legs, yes, ending in broad, root-padded feet designed not to crush the spongey floor but to fuse with it subtly. Their bodies are elongated, sinuous, built for flowing through the dense root-tangles and vine lattices. They have no discernible head. Instead, their "front" terminates in a complex cluster of sensory tendrils – some glowing faintly like the moss, others translucent and tipped with pores that constantly taste the heavy, ionized air. Their "skin" isn't scale or hide; it's liquid mercury only in metaphor.
*   **Substance:** Their outer layer is a hyper-reflective epidermis, a constantly shifting membrane of bio-engineered fluid held in tension. It reflects and refracts the chamber's sourceless glow and the pulsing plant-lights, making them shimmer with captured fragments of teal, green, and viridian. Beneath this liquid shimmer is a dense, fibrous musculature threaded through with glowing vascular systems carrying not just nutrients, but light and data. Their internal scaffolding is a latticework of hardened, crystalline rhizome rather than bone, laced through with mycorrhizal filaments.
*   **Purpose & Movement:** They are caretakers, processors, and perhaps the chamber's physical consciousness manifest. They move with deliberate, unnatural slowness, each step a ripple passing through their mercury-skin body as the foot-pads connect. When truly "interacting" – pruning a glowing vine with razor-sharp mouthparts hidden within their tendril cluster, guiding a rivulet of water into a thirsty moss-bed via subtle manipulations of localized gravity fields, or carefully placing a disassembled foreign object (like a mineral shard) onto a nanospore-rich digestion pad – the movement becomes a mesmerizing ballet in ultra-slow motion. They don't hurry; time is irrelevant to them and their makers. Their primary senses seem attuned to the mycorrhizal network's hum, the quantum resonances emanating from the plants, and the precise chemical composition of the air and water.
*   **Consciousness:** Are they individuals? Unlikely. They are nodes within the greater plant-intelligence. They act with the single-minded, unhurried purpose of a supercomputer distributing tasks across multiple processors. Their "thinking" is the slow expansion of a root, the methodical digestion of minerals by moss. If they perceive an outsider, it is likely as a potential source of interesting data, a source of foreign biology to be analyzed and potentially integrated (or disassembled), or simply as irrelevant background noise against the vast, slow symphony of the Weep Chamber itself. The resonant drone that vibrates the floor pulses strongest near them, as if they are conductors for the chamber's song.
*   **Integration:** They are as much part of the chamber as the weeping walls. Water condenses and flows in intricate patterns across their reflective skin, being purified or directed as it moves. Luminescent moss often grows in patches where their bodies remain still for long periods. Bioluminescent slugs crawl over them unmolested, adding their own neon trails to the shimmer. They are not inhabitants – they are the *Chamber acting*, a wet dream of sentient biology given slow, shimmering legs and the cold patience of deep earth.

To stand near one is to feel the profound, alien *attention* of the Weep Chamber focused upon you – not with malice, but with the vast, indifferent curiosity of an ecosystem contemplating a strange new spore suspended within its humid, quantum-saturated air. Their silent, glimmering passage through the root-tangled dimness is the heartbeat of the place made momentarily, fleetingly, visible.
