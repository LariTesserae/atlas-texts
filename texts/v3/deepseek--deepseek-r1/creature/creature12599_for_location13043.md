The Resonance Chamber teems with life – not life as known on green worlds, but a churning, metallic ecology forged by the complex's waste energy and mineral decay. Its inhabitants are **mechano-fauna**, organisms sculpted by vibration, dust, and the relentless hum of titanic engines:

1.  **The Dust-Runners (Base Population / Prey / Engineers of Erosion):**
    *   **Form:** Minute, segmented arthropods, rarely exceeding 3mm in length. Their carapaces are plates of hardened, mineralized chitin, resembling dull gunmetal flakes or flecks of rust, perfectly camouflaged against the floor. Legs are numerous, short, and spindly, ending in hooked claws for gripping dust and steel. Bodies are low-slung and flattened, built to slide through the dense particulate carpet.
    *   **Behavior:** They exist in ceaseless, intersecting rivers of movement. Millions flow across every surface – floor, walls, even ceiling – in a frantic, hyper-efficient search. They are driven by an insatiable hunger fueled by trace EM fields and ambient heat (1.6 Tech). Their primary diet is the metallic dust, mineral accretions (like the compacted chalk-dust floor), and desiccated lubricant residues within the patina. They also cannibalize their own dead without pause.
    *   **Role:** They are the bedrock consumers and the primary agents of erosion (2.8 Erosion). Their constant scraping and digestion microscopically abrade the durasteel walls and consume the compacted floor, their waste and compacted corpses forming the next layer of substrate for future generations. They are the base of the food chain, perpetually mined.

2.  **The Scrap-Jaws (Predators / Population Control):**
    *   **Form:** Larger, darker, and more robust than the Dust-Runners, reaching up to 7mm. Their carapace is thicker, often a deep hematite black or bruised purple. Most striking are their mandibles: serrated, metallic-looking appendages capable of shearing through Dust-Runner carapace like tin foil. They possess longer, more powerful legs for bursts of speed and ambush.
    *   **Behavior:** Solitary ambush predators. They lie in wait, often half-buried in dust drifts or concealed within crevices in the stratified wall patina. When a dense stream of Dust-Runners flows past, they erupt with shocking speed, snatching victims and retreating to consume them. They are patient, efficient killers, their movements punctuating the constant flow with moments of violent stillness.
    *   **Role:** Crucial population control for the Dust-Runners. Without them, the scavengers would overwhelm the Chamber's limited resources far too quickly. Their predation maintains the terrifying equilibrium. They are the apex predators within this microcosm.

3.  **The Static-Skimmers (Energy Harvesters / Light & Field Regulators):**
    *   **Form:** Beetle-like, roughly 5mm long, possessing a striking iridescent carapace that shifts between oily blues, purples, and greens under the flickering lumen-strip (1.9 Light). Their most notable features are their wings: diaphanous, high-frequency vibrators, usually held folded but blurring into invisibility when active. Tiny, hair-like antennae constantly sample the EM field.
    *   **Behavior:** Primarily found clinging to the ceiling near the flickering lumen-strip housing or directly on the riveted seams of the humming conduits. They deploy their wings periodically, not for flight in the traditional sense, but to generate a resonant frequency that allows them to siphon residual electrical energy and EM bleed from the surrounding tech (1.6 Tech). This process often produces faint, sputtering blue discharges that add chaotic flashes to the strained light.
    *   **Role:** They are the ecosystem's energy specialists and regulators. By harvesting waste EM fields, they prevent dangerous buildups that could disrupt the Chamber or fry the smaller fauna. Their discharges contribute to the chaotic illumination and the pervasive oscillating *hum* layered over the mechanical thrum (2.0 Loud). They represent a metabolic pathway entirely divorced from organic matter, fueled purely by the complex's leakage.

**The Ecosystem as the True Inhabitant:**
The true "inhabitant" of the Resonance Chamber is the horrifically balanced, self-sustaining system itself. It operates on inverted rules:
*   **Metabolism:** Electrochemical and mineral-based. Water is irrelevant (0.2 Water). Energy comes from heat bleed, EM fields, and the chemical breakdown of metals, minerals, and other mechano-fauna.
*   **Reproduction:** Asexual fission, triggered by surges in ambient energy from the distant machinery. Populations can explode or crash rapidly based on the complex's operational rhythms.
*   **Cycle:** Dust-Runners erode structure and consume detritus -> Scrap-Jaws consume Dust-Runners -> All waste and dead bodies compact into new substrate -> Static-Skimmers harvest energy and regulate fields -> New generations of Dust-Runners mine the compacted waste. The Chamber slowly consumes itself and its inhabitants, perpetually recycling every atom in a closed loop sustained solely by the decaying industrial giant's waste (0.8 Unusual). It is a dynamic (2.4), ancient (2.8 Erosion), and overwhelmingly dense (2.5 Density, 3.0 Fauna) pocket of life thriving in a cold, metallic, forgotten crypt.

The scattered tools of the builders (1.1 Built) are mere fossils in the patina, irrelevant relics. The Chamber now belongs entirely to the churning, clicking, humming, metallic life it has spawned – a resonant ecosystem thriving in perpetual shadow, vibration, and dust.
