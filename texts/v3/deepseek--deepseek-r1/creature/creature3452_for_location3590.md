Based on this violently nascent, fog-submerged peak, its inhabitants reflect the landscape's raw urgency, desperate endurance, and unsettling strangeness:

1.  **The Obsidian Skittermaws:** These are the "slate-grey lizards," but far stranger. They aren't reptiles at all. Each is a chimeric entity: a core of flexible, cartilage-like volcanic glass woven with filaments of resilient moss and symbiotic chemosynthetic bacteria. Their "scales" are actually interlocking shards of sharp, dark mineral precipitated from the fog. Their primary sense isn't sight, but a form of **seismic echolocation**. They drum specialized forelimbs or vibrate internal resonators against the rock, listening to the complex echoes bouncing through the humming obsidian. The deafening roar is their communication medium and navigation map. They feed on the hardy lichens and any micro-fauna caught in the shifting scree, retreating into fissures that resonate at protective frequencies when disturbances grow too violent. Their movements are unnervingly sudden bursts, vanishing into crevices that seem impossibly small. The metallic dampness of the air is partly the chemical signature of their metabolisms.

2.  **The Screaming Kites:** The dark, streamlined birds glimpsed high in the fog. Their name comes not from their cries (inaudible in the roar), but from the horrific sound generated by their flight. Their wings are composed of multiple, semi-rigid bone-spars connected by hyper-elastic membranes. When catching the turbulent updrafts, these membranes stretch and vibrate violently, generating focused beams of sound powerful enough to stun micro-planktonic life suspended in the fog-ocean. Their too-many-jointed wings allow them to reconfigure their shape instantly to exploit or escape chaotic wind vortices. They fly perpetually, never landing – their hollow bones are pressurized with lighter-than-air gases scavenged and metabolized from the fog, granting buoyancy within the dense atmosphere. They navigate by sensing pressure differentials in the fog and the faint electromagnetic signatures emanating from the vibrating mountain core. Their phosphorescent eyes are tuned to the fog's internal glow.

3.  **The Resonant Whisperer:** This inhabitant built the solitary dome-hut. It is rarely seen, but its presence is felt. Less a distinct animal, more a **sentient colony organism** resembling a mobile, viscous black lichen. It flows slowly over rock surfaces near its shelter, incorporating mineral grit and fog-condensate. Its core structure is a complex lattice of silica and chitinous fibers, constantly humming at a low frequency that stabilizes the scree immediately around its path (a counterpoint to the mountain's chaotic vibrations). It "sees" by interpreting distortions in this resonant field and the subtle pressure changes in the fog. It created the spiral carving and the collapsed wall – not as symbols, but as **acoustic lenses**. The spiral focuses and amplifies specific ambient frequencies, allowing it to sense distant tremors or fluctuations in the fog-ocean's roar. The low wall channels wind into frequencies that deter predators or vibrate scree into temporary traps. It communicates with the mountain's latent sentience through these resonances, a slow, geological dialogue. Any scavenged material (bone, rare wood) in its hut serves solely as structural reinforcement or specialized resonators. It consumes mineral compounds dissolved in fog-condensate, catalyzed by unique enzymes. Its movement and growth are agonizingly slow, a counterbalance to the environment's violence.

**Ecology of Necessity:**
*   The **Skittermaws** exploit the shifting rock, finding prey flushed by slides or hidden in stable crevices. They are preyed upon opportunistically by the **Screaming Kites** when a kite's sonic blast dislodges one onto open scree. Their waste products contribute nutrients to the rare moss beds.
*   The **Screaming Kites** harvest the microscopic life teeming within the fog-ocean. They avoid landing, as the mountain is hostile. They are the peak's only connection beyond the fog pocket, occasionally venturing through tears briefly, perhaps exchanging trace gases or minerals.
*   The **Resonant Whisperer** is largely ignored due to its low energy signature and defensive resonance. It might subtly guide minor rockfalls near its hut for defense or to expose mineral seams. It occasionally 'farms' patches of specialized moss that grow on its resonating pathways. It perceives the mountain's birth-pangs directly through its hum, a constant, overwhelming sensation for it.

**Why it Fits:**
*   **Mineral Integration:** All residents incorporate geology directly into their biology (Skitterjaw carapace, Whisperer structure) or navigation (all).
*   **Acoustic Primacy:** Life revolves around sound – navigation, communication, hunting, and even stability – in defiance/adaptation to the deafening roar.
*   **Desperate Endurance:** Existence is precarious. Growth is slow, energy is scarce, survival depends on perfect adaptation to constant, violent change.
*   **Emergent Strangeness:** Their forms and functions feel like natural extensions of the weird landscape: vibrational senses, fog-buoyancy, symbiotic mineralogy, resonance as a fundamental force.
*   **Transience Permanence:** The Skittermaws and Kites are fleeting, spectral. The Whisperer represents a desperate, slow grasp at permanence using the very forces that destroy, embodying the landscape's tension.
*   **Living Process:** Like the mountain itself, these beings are processes made flesh and mineral – resonating, vibrating, consuming fog, dissolving rock – participants in the violent, deafening act of existing within a world still being born. They aren't inhabitants; they *are* the mountain's emergent life-pulse.
