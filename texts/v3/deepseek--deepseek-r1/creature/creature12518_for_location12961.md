Here dwells the **Silicate Weavers**, a symbiotic ecosystem where mineral, fungal, and flesh blur into a single, weeping whole:

1.  **The Violet Mantle:** The foundational lifeform. Not merely a mat, but a sentient, cold membrane of **Myco-Sclerotia**. Its rubbery, deep violet and obsidian-black hyphae secrete potent acids that slowly digest basalt and ferro-concrete alike, extracting silica, metals, and residual industrial chemicals. It feels like cold, wet leather and pulses faintly with internal hydraulic pressure as it redistributes nutrients and moisture. It *is* the ground, constantly knitting itself over weeping fissures and crumbling structures.

2.  **The Lithic Sculptors (Glass-Crabs):** Ambling slowly across weeping rocks and fungal carpets are creatures seemingly *grown* from the basin itself. **Obsidian Carvers** appear as moving sculptures of fused volcanic glass, slag, and corroded metal fragments. Their multiple limbs end in chisel-like points or scooping claws, constantly scraping mineral-rich weeping-water runoff and fungal secretions. Their faceted eyes, likely silica lenses over photosensitive tissue, gleam dully in the twilight. They are not hunters of flesh, but miners and processors, grinding ingested minerals into pastes used to reinforce their own bodies and excrete as binding agents for the growing silicate structures. Their movement sounds like slow, wet gravel being ground underfoot.

3.  **The Mycelial Herd (Substrate Processors):** Thriving just beneath the Violet Mantle's surface are teeming colonies of **Ferro-Pods**. Thumb-sized, multi-legged arthropods with dull grey, metallic carapaces forged from sequestered iron oxides. They burrow ceaselessly, aerating the fungal mat, consuming its processed organic-mineral matter, and depositing concentrated metallic and siliceous waste nodules. They are the primary nutrient cyclers, turning the Mantle's secretions into bio-available forms. Their constant, unseen movement causes the fungal carpet to ripple like a sluggish, dark sea.

4.  **The Luminous Threads:** Deep within crevices, weeping pipes, and the sheltered grottos formed by the mineral spires, **Phosphor Worms** pulse. Thin as threads and meters long, they anchor themselves to weeping rock faces. Their translucent bodies glow with a soft, persistent blue or sickly green bioluminescence, fueled by chemosynthesis – processing dissolved metals and complex hydrocarbons leaching from the industrial residues in the water. They are living indicators of chemical flows and provide the basin's only significant light source beyond the dim sky.

5.  **The Silent Reapers (Rag-Fliers):** Drifting utterly silently on thermals rising from geothermal vents below are the **Shroud-Stalkers**. These creatures defy easy categorization: wings of tough, leathery membrane stretched over frameworks of light, hollow silica spicules resemble tattered burial shrouds more than wings. Their bodies are slender, segmented, and seemingly woven from fungal fibers and metallic threads. Faceted eyes, larger and more complex than the Carvers', scan the dimness below. They are apex predators, descending with ghostly silence to snatch Ferro-Pods from the fungal surface or, more rarely, to tear into an Obsidian Carver weakened by age or damage, using hooked silica mouthparts to extract the softer, nutrient-rich tissues within their mineral shells. Their passage is marked only by a slight disturbance in the thick, moist air.

**The Unseen Flow:**
The true inhabitant, however, is the **Weeping Water** itself. Saturated with dissolved silica, metals, fungal metabolites, and decaying industrial poisons, it is the lifeblood and sculptor. It beads, drips, flows, and seeps through everything. It deposits the intricate mineral patterns on the spires, feeds the Violet Mantle and Phosphor Worms, provides the medium for the Ferro-Pods and Obsidian Carvers to process, and carries the chemical signals that subtly coordinate this damp, dark symphony of assimilation. The arrhythmic *plink... plink... shhhh* is the basin's heartbeat.

**Their World:**
This is not a place of growth and bloom, but of relentless, slow transformation. The Silicate Weavers exist in a perpetual state of mineral integration and decay. They consume the dead industry not as invaders, but as inheritors, weaving it back into the primordial mineral fabric of the basin, guided by the patient chemistry of water and the persistent, acidic hunger of the Violet Mantle. Their lives are measured in drips and the imperceptible growth of silica fountains, a testament to resilience in a cool, wet twilight where engines become geology and life wears stone.
