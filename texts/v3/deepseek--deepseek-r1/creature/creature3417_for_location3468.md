The inhabitants of the Breath Hollows of Silica are not creatures of flesh and blood, but **crystalline emanations of the cavern itself – the Silica Resonants.** They are the architects of the hollowed pneumatophores, the harvesters of the mineral trails, and the children of the sighing breath. Here is their nature:

1.  **Form & Substance:**
    *   **Living Geodes:** Each Resonant manifests as a complex, semi-amorphous core of pure, condensed silicate vapor, encased within a constantly growing and reshaping lattice of crystalline structures – primarily smoky quartz and chalcedony, interwoven with filaments of the same flexible silicate that forms the pneumatophores' structure. They resemble humanoid-shaped geodes, roughly 1.5 to 2 meters tall, but their forms are fluid, capable of shifting density and extending crystalline pseudopods.
    *   **Heat Embodied:** Their core radiates intense dry heat, matching the ambient temperature. They *are* the heat of the cavern given slow, purposeful form. Their crystalline lattice acts as both a heat sink and a radiator, channeling the cavern's energy.
    *   **Mineral Diet:** They sustain themselves by absorbing the mineral-rich vapors pumped by the pneumatophores directly through porous sections of their lattice, and by slowly dissolving trace elements from the obsidian floor or crystal pillars with acidic secretions (a byproduct of their internal heat processes). They leave faint, polished trails similar to the crawlers, but more deliberate.

2.  **Physiology & Perception:**
    *   **Resonant Perception:** They perceive the world primarily through **vibration and thermal gradients**. The constant sighing of the pneumatophores is their symphony and their map. They "see" by feeling the minute distortions in the resonant frequency caused by obstacles, other Resonants, or geological shifts. Thermal sight allows them to distinguish active mineral vents, cooling spore drifts, and the heat signatures of their kin.
    *   **Communication:** They communicate through complex, subsonic vibrations generated within their crystalline lattice, harmonizing or counterpointing the cavern's dominant sighs. A conversation sounds like a subtle shift in the cavern's breath – a deepening here, a higher harmonic there, a rhythmic pulse. Loud events like spore releases or rock groans are deafening shouts in their sensory world.
    *   **Growth & Repair:** Damage to their lattice is repaired by drawing dissolved silicates from the air or substrate, slowly accreting new crystal. Significant growth or "reproduction" (see below) occurs near intense geothermal vents, where mineral saturation is highest.

3.  **Society & Culture (Integrated with the Ecology):**
    *   **The Pneumatophores are Home:** They dwell *within* the modified hollows of the great pneumatophores. Their chambers are not furnished in any traditional sense; they reshape the living crystal walls to form resting niches, communal resonance chambers (where the sigh is amplified and shaped for complex gatherings), and observation galleries facing the cavern void.
    *   **Mineral Logic:** Their society operates on principles of mineral equilibrium, resonant harmony, and geothermal flow. Decisions are made through prolonged communal resonance, seeking patterns that align with the cavern's deep rhythms. Conflict manifests as dissonant vibrations, resolved by isolation or ritualized resonance contests until harmony is restored.
    *   **Custodians & Shapers:** They see themselves as stewards of the Breath. They carefully harvest minerals without depleting sources, guide the growth of new pneumatophores near active vents, and perform subtle modifications to the giant structures to optimize airflow or structural integrity. Their "primitive tools" are hardened crystal shards and braided silicate cords, used for precise fracturing, etching, and binding.
    *   **Spore Communion:** Spore releases are sacred events. Resonants gather on open galleries or the cavern floor, aligning their vibrations to the impending crackle. The drifting spores carry trace mineral signatures and resonant patterns; absorbing them (allowing spores to integrate into their lattice) is a form of communion, memory sharing, and genetic exchange.

4.  **Lifecycle:**
    *   **Forging:** New Resonants coalesce slowly, over centuries, around intense geothermal vents. Mineral vapors super-saturate, forming a chaotic lattice that gradually attunes to the cavern's resonant frequency. Older Resonants may guide this process with directed vibrations.
    *   **Maturity:** A mature Resonant refines its lattice, becoming more efficient at energy absorption and resonance manipulation. They contribute to the communal tasks of shaping, harvesting, and maintaining harmony.
    *   **Return:** When a Resonant's core energy finally wanes or catastrophic damage occurs, its lattice fractures. The core dissipates as vapor, rejoining the cavern's atmosphere. The shattered crystal fragments slowly dissolve or become incorporated into the floor, pillars, or even the structure of a pneumatophore, literally returning to the ecosystem. Some particularly large or resonant fragments might become focal points in communal chambers.

**Presence in the Hollows:**
From an outsider's perspective within the vast emptiness, a Resonant might be mistaken for a peculiarly shaped crystal outcrop until it moves. Their movement is slow, deliberate, and eerily silent apart from the faintest crystalline *chime* or the subliminal shift in the air's resonant hum. They flow across the obsidian like heat haze given form, pausing to harvest minerals or commune with a pneumatophore's base. Within the hollows, their presence is felt as a deepening or clarifying of the ever-present sigh – a consciousness woven into the very breath and bones of the Crystal Lungs. They are not separate from the ecosystem; they are its mindful expression, living sculptures formed by heat, resonance, and mineral time.
