Here, in the Basin of Groaning Stone, life is not born—it is *hewn*. The inhabitants are as much a part of the architecture as the cyclopean blocks they shape. They are the **Stone-Kin**, and their existence is a testament to relentless toil and mineral symbiosis.

## Physiology & Form
*   **Silicate Skeletons:** Their frames are not bone, but interlocking plates of dark basalt and volcanic glass, fused by geothermal heat and pressure over generations. Joints grind with a sound like pebbles in a mortar, lubricated by mineral-rich seepage. They stand hunched, rarely exceeding seven feet, but dense and immensely strong.
*   **Skin of Dust and Lichen:** Their outer layer is not flesh, but a thick, granular integument—a mixture of volcanic ash, condensed metallic vapors, and the very same bioluminescent crust that veins the walls. This "skin" glows faintly (often a dull ochre or deep umber, rarely green) where stress or activity concentrates, betraying their movements if not careful. It constantly sheds and reforms, collecting the ever-present ash.
*   **Senses:** Eyes are useless in the eternal gloom. Instead, broad, dish-like auditory organs dominate their flattened heads, constantly twitching to parse the groans, drips, and scrapes. Their primary sense is **vibration**—felt through their stony feet and hands. They "taste" the air through chemoreceptive pits lining their jawless mouths (slits more suited for inhaling vapor than eating), detecting moisture trails, geothermal signatures, and the faint electrical tang of the crust. Touch is paramount; their thick, three-fingered hands (ending in obsidian claws) read the minute shifts and stresses in the stone they work upon.
*   **Sustenance:** They are **chemolithoautotrophs**. Deep within their chest cavities, geothermal heat and catalytic minerals interact with water vapor and dissolved sulfides from the weeping stone. This slow, internal reaction provides their primary energy. Supplemental "feeding" involves pressing themselves against geothermal vents to absorb heat and trace minerals, or scraping the bioluminescent crust for concentrated chemotrophic compounds—a risky endeavor that leaves them brightly lit targets.

## Society & Behavior
*   **The Endless Task:** Their entire culture is built around **The Groaning Work**. They quarry, shape, lift, and stack the titanic stones. This is not mere construction; it is ritual, obligation, and the core of their identity. Why? Perhaps to appease the shifting deeps, to reach some prophesied configuration, or simply because the Work *is*. Generations live and die within the shadow of a single unfinished monolith.
*   **Sound as Language & Map:** Communication is a complex tapestry of **infrasound hums**, resonant **taps** on stone, and modulated **scrapes**. Different groans of the basin have names; specific drip patterns are directions. Silence is a sign of danger, grief, or profound reverence. Their "music" is the rhythmic percussion of obsidian hammers on basalt.
*   **Hierarchy of Fracture & Seam:** Status is determined by skill in manipulating stone. The highest castes are the **Seekers-of-Flaw** who identify the natural fracture lines in bedrock, and the **Joiners** who understand how to fit multi-ton blocks with minimal mortar (a paste of ash, water, and crushed lichen). Lowest are the **Ash-Dredgers** who clear the ever-falling grit from work sites and cisterns.
*   **Relation to the "Fauna":** They view the skittering chitinous things as pests that steal heat from vents, occasionally hunting them with hooked stone spears. The gelid blobs are avoided; their acidic touch etches stone. The leathery fliers are omens, their silent passage often preceding a major structural groan or vent eruption.
*   **Fear of Light:** While dependent on the faint crust-glow for navigation, sustained or bright light is anathema. A Stone-Kin caught in a pulsing patch of lichen will frantically scrape at its own glowing integument or smear itself with ash to dim it. They associate uncontrolled light with the attention of deep predators or the displeasure of the stone itself.

## Tools & Technology
*   **Primitive Leverage:** Their greatest tools are ingenuity and time. They use massive levers of basalt, pivoted on fulcrums of harder obsidian, to shift blocks. Ramps of compacted rubble and ash allow ascent. Pulleys are unknown; movement relies on rollers (logs of petrified wood or shaped stone) and brute force applied by teams harnessed with braided mineral-fiber ropes.
*   **Stone Shaping:** Obsidian cores are flaked into razor-sharp blades, adzes, and chisels. Basalt mauls, bound with sinew-like mineral accretions, deliver crushing blows. Sand (crushed harder stone) and water are used for abrasion polishing on critical seams.
*   **Water Capture:** They are masters of directing the spectral water. Intricate, shallow channels carved into walls guide drips to cisterns. Condensation traps—shallow basins filled with cool, porous stone—dot higher platforms. This water is sacred, used only for mixing mortar, cooling tools during intense shaping, or ritual ablutions before critical Work.

## The Unanswered Groan
The Stone-Kin do not build *for* themselves. Their labyrinthine structures lack dwellings as softer species understand them. They sleep wedged in crevices, propped against the stones they maintain. Their purpose is cryptic, buried deeper than the lowest quarries. Are they maintaining a prison? Building an antenna to resonate with the planet's core? Honoring ancestors turned to stone? The answer echoes only in the deep, tectonic groans they heed with their entire being. They are the hands of the living stone, and the Work is eternal.
