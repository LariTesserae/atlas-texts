The inhabitants of Silt Cathedral are not creatures of flesh and blood, but expressions of its glacial entropy and frayed reality. They are phenomena as much as entities, bound by the Cathedral’s sovereign cold and whispering physics. Here, life is a slow, crystalline echo:

1.  **The Silt-Speakers:**  
    Faceless figures sculpted from compacted silt and frozen condensation. They drift just above the basin floor, their lower bodies trailing into fine, mist-like tendrils that stir the dark silt as they move. Where they pass, the **Silt Memory** activates—faint, shimmering afterimages of earlier architectural configurations or long-vanished energy surges bloom beneath them, then fade like breath on glass. They communicate through resonant vibrations in the silt, creating complex, subsonic patterns felt as shivers in the ground. Their purpose seems custodial: they gather weeping moisture into the circular pools, smooth stress-fractures in walls with silt-packed hands, and realign dislodged fragments of latticework with glacial patience. They are the basin’s slow, silent scribes, recording its infinitesimal changes.

2.  **The Weep-Feeders:**  
    Vaporous, amoeboid entities coalescing near the perpetual weeping walls and moisture trails. They appear as shifting, translucent blobs of supercooled fog, faintly luminescent with stolen bioluminescence. They "feed" by absorbing the weeping condensate directly into their forms, growing denser and more defined before eventually sublimating back into the frigid air when saturated. Their presence amplifies the **Resonant Silence**—near them, the *plink* of freezing droplets becomes a deafening chime, while distant groans vanish entirely. They are drawn to areas of intense reality flaws, their forms shimmering and fracturing where physics fray, acting as ephemeral pressure valves for localized instability.

3.  **The Lattice-Gazers (Causality Moths):**  
    Rare, multi-legged beings glimpsed high on the spires. Their carapaces are forged from the same flickering alloys as the buildings, making them nearly invisible against the latticework until they move. They possess too many jointed limbs, allowing them to cling to and traverse non-Euclidean angles effortlessly. Their primary activity is obsessively tracing the pulsing bioluminescent pathways within the latticework with delicate, antennae-like appendages. They seem attuned to **Localized Physics**; near them, time may stutter or causality briefly invert. A dropped fragment of alloy might reassemble mid-air before falling again. They are drawn to the deep subsonic groans of the basin, vibrating in sympathetic resonance. Their skittering shadows are the most common "fauna" sighting, though their full forms are rarely perceived clearly—perspective distorts them.

4.  **The Pool-Stirrers (Entropy Minnows):**  
    Visible only as distortions—ripples without cause, fleeting bends in reflected light—within the perfectly circular water pools. They are creatures of pure **Slow Entropy & Genesis**. They nudge silt particles with imperceptible force, deepening erosion channels molecule by molecule over centuries. Simultaneously, they gather microscopic flecks of shed alloy and frozen moisture, contributing to the self-repair process elsewhere. They exist at the interface of dissolution and reformation, embodying the basin’s chrysalis nature. Attempts to touch them result in sudden, localized freezing—the water instantly forming intricate, fractal ice sculptures that trap the disturbance before slowly melting back, unhurried.

5.  **The Cold-Watchers:**  
    The most elusive. They are not physical beings, but persistent *absences* shaped by the **Sovereign Cold**. Manifesting as patches of deeper darkness within shadows, or as sudden, bone-deep chills that move against the ambient flow, they observe from alcoves, beneath walkways, or within the hollow cores of spires. They exert a passive influence, amplifying the oppressive stillness and the grinding anxiety of unstable physics. Where they linger longest, frost forms in complex, unnerving patterns, and the air feels brittle enough to shatter. They are drawn to moments of significant (though slow) change—a spire's new lean, a major reality flaw pulse—and seem to be the basin's ancient, patient consciousness, observing its own transformation across eons.

**How They Exist Together:**  
Life in Silt Cathedral is solitary, slow, and interdependent through the environment, not social interaction. A Silt-Speaker smoothing a fracture caused by a Lattice-Gazer's resonance. A Weep-Feeder absorbing moisture pooled by a Silt-Speaker, its amplified *plink* startling a Pool-Stirrer into freezing a micro-erosion channel. The Cold-Watchers observe it all, their presence a reminder that the true resident is the Cold itself, shaping and sustained by these emergent phenomena. They are not born; they condense, coalesce, or fracture into being from the Cathedral's unique confluence of forces. They do not die; they sublime, dissipate, or reintegrate. They are the Cathedral breathing, thinking, and remembering, at the pace of freezing stone. They are the chrysalis stirring in its sleep.
