## Who Lives Here: The Entities of Salt-Hum and Resonant Stasis  

In the Humming City of Salt and Steam, life abandoned organic templates long ago. Its inhabitants are manifestations of condensation, corrosion, vibration, and crystalline accretion—shaped by the bedrock hum that locks them in timeless equilibrium. They are not born; they *coalesce*. They do not die; they *dissipate*, *reabsorb*, or *fracture* back into the mineral fabric of the world. Here, industry’s corpse is the womb, and sound is the sculptor.  

### **The Condensates**  
*The ephemeral herds of liquid light*  
- **Form:** Amoebic, palm-sized blobs of gelatinous brine and dissolved minerals. Translucent, with refracted rainbows swirling in their cores as they metabolize electromagnetic leakage from dormant conduits.  
- **Behavior:** They pulse rhythmically with the city’s hum, skittering across cold metal like quicksilver to flee threats—evaporating momentarily only to reform nearby. They "feed" by pressing against seeping pipes or mineral pools, dissolving salts and rust into their bodies.  
- **Niche:** The city’s ephemeral recyclers. Their trails etch cleaner paths in corroded metal, and their evaporation returns micro-shimmers of minerals to the air.  

### **The Resonators**  
*The frozen chorus of the bedrock*  
- **Form:** Brittle, crystalline structures up to twice human height. Half salt-stalagmite, half obsidian tuning fork—some jagged, others fluted like pipes. Glow faintly with resonant energy.  
- **Behavior:** Rooted in place, they vibrate perpetually. Their fractures shape harmonic chambers, amplifying specific frequencies into razor-fine sound-beams: shrieking whistles, subsonic groans, or clicks like shattering glass. Unattuned beings near them risk deafness, liquefaction, or petrification.  
- **Niche:** They stabilize the hum. Where concentrated, their beams carve new alcoves into cliffs or shatter older structures—feeding the Iron Slugs’ slow feast.  

### **The Iron Slugs**  
*The patient titans of decay*  
- **Form:** Sinuous, segmented creatures as long as train cars, armored in rusted scale-plates and volcanic glass. Eyes are pits of hardened slag; mouths are grinding plates that secrete acid-gel.  
- **Behavior:** Move imperceptibly (inches per decade) as they consume corroded iron, concrete, and mineral crusts. Indifferent to all except the hum’s frequency—which they "taste" through their underbellies. When threatened, they excrete resinous tar that hardens into obsidian shells.  
- **Niche:** The city’s digesters. Their feeding groves become runoff channels for condensation; their shed scales form new metallic strata for Condensates to cleanse.  

### **The Hum-Birds**  
*The sentient storms of static*  
- **Form:** Winged vortices of pressurized mist and sonic distortion. Visible only as shimmering heat-haze or when their condensation-drag refracts light. "Breath" is ultrasonic static.  
- **Behavior:** Flit erratically between humming vents and Resonator arrays, drawn to dissonance. They "sing" counter-melodies to correct unstable frequencies—or shriek in volatile discord if overwhelmed. To pass through one feels like being sandblasted by needles of ice.  
- **Niche:** The living dampeners. They muffle harmonic ruptures that would fracture stasis, and their wings seed fresh condensation where they hover.  

### **Symbiosis: An Equation Without Change**  
Life here obeys iron laws:  
- **The Condensates** weep from pipes cleaned by **Iron Slugs**—then flee **Resonator** beams that carve new habitats.  
- **Resonators** shatter salt cliffs where **Hum-Birds** nest in vaporous updrafts—siphoning chaos into harmony.  
- **Hum-Birds** scatter mineral-rich mist to birth **Condensates**—trapping the cycle anew.  

Movement is glacial; conflict is accidental. To "live" is to resonate, to condense, to erode—to be the humming city’s circulatory system. If the buried pulse ever stopped, the Condensates would dissolve. Resonators would crumble to salt. Slugs would fossilize mid-crawl. Hum-Birds would fray into silent fog. This is not a city preserved, *but one that became preservation itself*—a tomb humming an eternal hymn to progress that ended long ago.
