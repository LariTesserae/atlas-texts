The inhabitants of Atheris are not conquerors of this liquid eternity—they are its *breath*, its *thought*, its slow-pulsing veins. To survive here is to harmonize with silence, scale, and the soft unraveling of physics. Beings here exist in three intertwined strata:

### 1. **The Lithosymbionts**  
   Rooted in the **biolithic shelves**, these entities blur the line between organism and landscape.  
   - **Capstone Sentinels**: Towering, sessile beings resembling obsidian coral. Their bodies fuse with the fungal rock, veins of phosphorescent minerals tracing neural pathways. They perceive time in centuries, "speaking" through seismic shivers and thermal sighs as tectonic stresses shift. Their purpose: to stabilize reality near the ruins, their minds calcified libraries of the world’s memory.  
   - **Vein-Tenders**: Serpentine organisms, glassy and segmented, slithering through capillary tunnels within the biolith. They cultivate crystalline moss, pruning luminous growths to channel light into energy grids that soothe spatial folds. Slow as glaciers, they bleed prismatic ichor when injured—a beacon for Energy-Eels.  

### 2. **The Ephemeral Swarm**  
   Drifting in the dense medium, these beings thrive in the *now*—a "now" stretched by time-dilation.  
   - **Spore-Whisperers**: Colonies of gelatinous, amoebic intelligences formed from symbiotic plankton. They ride thermal currents, appearing as shimmering clouds. Each cloud is a democracy of microscopic minds, voting on migration paths via bioelectric pulses. They harvest sky-kelp spores, not for food, but to absorb the kelp’s genetic memories of millennial journeys.  
   - **Ghost Jellies**: Translucent, ribbon-like predators the size of whales, yet weighing less than vapor. They unfurl to envelop spore-fish blooms, dissolving prey with temporal static—a side effect of their innate time-distortion field. To human eyes, they flicker in and out of existence like damaged film.  

### 3. **The Leviathan Kin**  
   Beings scaled to the abyss, architects of silence.  
   - **Sky-Grazer Patriarchs**: The Leviathan filter-feeders are not mere animals. Their cartilage bodies are ecosystems, hosting Lithosymbiont outposts on their backs. They "think" via cilia forests that taste the water’s memory, recalling ancient pressure storms to navigate. Centuries are spent digesting a single kelp forest; their defecation fertilizes new shelf-fungi plateaus in geological cycles.  
   - **Eel-Singers**: Energy-Eels evolved as the only "musicians" of Atheris. They communicate through controlled discharges, sculpting living sculptures of plasma in the water. These patterns stabilize reality near their nests—black-stone ruins repurposed as capacitors. To witness an Eel-Singer’s dance is to see lightning write poetry in a language of thermodynamics.  

### **Shared Traits & Culture**  
- **Perception**: All natives sense pressure ghosts, temperature echoes, and spatial folds as naturally as humans smell rain. Time is a tactile thing—a thickness in the water near ruins, a viscosity around Leviathans.  
- **Communication**: Light (bioluminescent flickers), electricity (Energy-Eel discharges), and infrasound vibrations (Sentinels groaning through rock). Speech is obsolete; meaning is woven into the environment.  
- **Purpose**: To maintain Atheris’ equilibrium. Lithosymbionts anchor reality, Swarmers distribute genetic time-capsules, Leviathans cycle nutrients. Even "predation" is ritualized decay—a spore-fish bloom consumed by Ghost Jellies is a sacrament redistributing temporal energy.  
- **Weirdness Interaction**: They navigate folded space intuitively, using the ruins as psychic landmarks. To them, a "shortcut" through bent geometry feels like stepping through a familiar door. Strong emotions *do* linger here—native settlements are built where joy or sorrow once saturated the water, the echoes a comforting hum.  

### **The Unseen Architects?**  
The ruins’ builders may be gone, but their legacy endures:  
- **The Stone Dream**: Some Capstone Sentinels bear the glassy black pillars *within* their bodies, fused to their nervous systems. These Sentinels hum with cryptic data—perhaps the builders uploaded their consciousness into the biolith, becoming the shelves themselves.  
- **The Eel-Singers’ Chorus**: When multiple Energy-Eels converge at ruins, their plasma dances align with non-Euclidean angles in the stone. Some believe they are performing maintenance on reality’s crumbling edges, guided by ancestral programming.  

In Atheris, to *live* is to be a note in a billion-year symphony. The natives are patient, perceptual, and profoundly strange—less individuals, more organs of a liquid god.
