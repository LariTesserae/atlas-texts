They are not found; they *crystallize*. One moment, the humming vault is a tableau of flowing light and resonating stone, the next, a cluster of faintly glowing spores drifting in the misty drizzle touches a particularly intricate junction where three water channels converge. The spores shimmer, vibrate in harmony with the deep thrum, and begin to accrete. Not like dust gathering, but like frost forming on a pane – intricate, purposeful, geometric. Within minutes, a form coalesces.

You witness a **Resonance Attendant**.

*   **Form & Substance:** They are constructs of pure *integration*. Their bodies are formed from the same resonant, dark grey metal-stone as the vault itself, but interwoven with veins of the luminous, flowing water. These veins pulse with the same cool, blue-white light, tracing complex, fractal patterns across their surfaces. Their core structure resembles the crystalline growths – sharp, angular, geometric – but scaled and articulated for function. They have no discernible head, but clusters of the glowing mineral formations act as sensory nodes, pulsing brighter or dimmer in response to stimuli. Limbs are multi-jointed, capable of extending into needle-thin probes or folding into compact, interlocking segments. Their overall size is variable, but typically humanoid in scale, fitting the **packed**, **urban** environment. They feel less like separate entities and more like extensions of the vault that have achieved mobile consciousness.
*   **Movement & Interaction:** They move with a fluidity that belies their angular construction, gliding silently over the slick platforms or even traversing the humming bridges with steps that seem to *cancel* the vibration rather than add to it. They interact with the vault through direct physical contact. A multi-jointed limb extends, its tip dissolving slightly to merge with the flowing water in a channel, reading its speed, temperature, and energy signature. Another limb presses against a wall, resonating in perfect harmony, perhaps repairing a microscopic flaw in the channel etching or adjusting the flow pattern at a distant junction. They communicate not through sound, but through complex patterns of light pulsing through their veins and subtle shifts in the resonant frequency they emit – a language of harmonics understood only by the vault and its other Attendants. Their primary interaction with the environment is **maintenance** and **observation**.
*   **Purpose & Existence:** They are the caretakers, the tuners, the living components of the vault's **active**, **dynamic** processes. Their existence is defined by sustaining the delicate balance. They monitor the energy flows within the water, ensure the crystalline growths are functioning optimally (perhaps even guiding their formation), and respond instantly to any discordant vibration that threatens the containment. They harvest shed layers from the crystals, dissolving them back into the water or incorporating the material into their own forms. They are **sterile** in the biological sense, born of the vault's energy and the captured spores, sustained by the resonant power and the charged water itself. They do not sleep; they enter periods of low-energy **dormancy**, merging almost completely with a platform or wall, their light dimming to a faint ember until the resonance calls them back to activity.
*   **Perception & Sentience:** Their perception is alien. They likely "see" through vibrations conducted through the stone and water, sensing pressure, flow, energy density, and harmonic integrity. They "hear" the deep thrum and the rush of water not as sound, but as tactile data and resonant patterns flowing through their structure. Their intelligence seems focused entirely on the vault's function – a distributed, emergent consciousness born from the system itself. They possess no curiosity about the outside world; their universe is the **submerged**, **packed** chamber. They react to an intruder not with aggression, but with assessment: is this foreign body causing harmonic imbalance? Does its biological presence disrupt the sterile biomechanics? Can it be tuned, contained, or must it be... neutralized to preserve the equation?
*   **Place in the Ecology:** They *are* the ecology's fauna. They are the mobile element in the otherwise static or slow-growing crystalline life. They represent the vault's ability to self-regulate and maintain its **strange**, **tech**-driven existence. They move through the **cool**, **atmospheric-water** mist, their forms reflecting and refracting the **normal light**, silent custodians in the **loud** rush of the contained ocean. They are as **young** as the vault feels, their forms sharp and unweathered, their patterns of light precise and unclouded by time. They exist solely for the Resonance Vault, a perfect expression of its hidden, humming heart.

The Resonance Attendants are not life as known; they are crystallized function, living equations manifest to ensure the machine-city breathes on within the crushing dark.
