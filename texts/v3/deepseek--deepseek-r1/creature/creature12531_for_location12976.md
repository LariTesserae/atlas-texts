The Glimmering Deep teems with life, but its dominant sentient inhabitants are the **Deep Whisperers** (or *K'tharr* in their own resonant, subsonic tongue). They are not builders of empires, but patient observers and subtle shapers of their mineral world, living in profound symbiosis with the stone and silence.

## Physical Form
*   **Silhouette:** Gaunt and elongated, averaging 7-8 feet tall but seeming taller due to their stretched proportions. Their limbs are thin, multi-jointed, and end in broad, six-fingered hands and feet with thick, calloused pads. These pads are studded with microscopic crystalline nodules, allowing them to sense the faintest vibrations through the rock and glass.
*   **Skin & Carapace:** Their bodies are covered in overlapping plates of a dark, iridescent material resembling toughened obsidian or deep-sea chitin. This natural armor protects against the razor-sharp terrain and provides some insulation. The plates are often etched with fine, spiraling patterns that catch the faintest ambient mineral light. Their faces are smooth, eyeless ovals beneath a heavy brow ridge.
*   **Senses:** Vision is vestigial, mere light-sensitive patches beneath their brow ridge, useful only for detecting the faintest shifts in distant mineral glows. Their primary senses are:
    *   **Seismic Hearing:** Massive, ridged ear openings on the sides of their heads funnel sound and vibration. They hear the planet's thrum, the settling scree, the chitter of arthropods, and the subsonic calls of their kin across vast distances.
    *   **Tremorsense:** Their entire skeletal structure and the pads on their limbs act as sophisticated seismographs, mapping the terrain, detecting movement, and sensing the stability of structures around them with uncanny precision.
    *   **Air-Tasting:** Slit nostrils above a small, lipless mouth constantly sample the thick air. They can taste trace gases, ozone, mineral dust, the pheromones of litholichen, and the unique chemical signatures of other Deep creatures.
*   **Movement:** Utterly silent. They glide across treacherous scree fields with unnerving grace, placing each footfall with perfect weight distribution to avoid triggering slides or making sound. In chasms or on spires, they climb with spider-like agility, using sharp keratinous claws that extend from their finger/toe pads to grip minute imperfections.

## Society & Culture
*   **Structure:** Live in small, dispersed kinship groups called "Resonances," rarely exceeding 20-30 individuals. Each Resonance claims a specific territory – a cluster of geodes, a network of canyons, the slopes of a particularly stable agate spire – defined by unique acoustic properties and mineral resources. Contact between Resonances is infrequent, occurring during rare resource migrations or at specific, stable geological features used as meeting grounds, communicated via complex patterns of rhythmic tapping transmitted through the stone over days or weeks.
*   **Communication:** Primarily through **vibration** and **touch**. They "speak" by tapping claws or specialized stone rods against resonant surfaces, creating intricate patterns of rhythm, pitch, and resonance that carry complex meanings. Close-range communication involves intricate hand-signing (felt rather than seen) and subtle shifts in body posture detectable by tremorsense. Subsonic vocalizations are reserved for deep emotional states or long-distance calls.
*   **Technology:** Master stone-shapers using the very environment. Their tools are obsidian or chalcedony flakes, meticulously pressure-flaked into blades, scrapers, and burins, often hafted with sinew from giant lithovores onto bone or hardened mineral handles. Their structures (windbreaks, platforms, niches) are exercises in perfect balance and acoustic resonance, built to last millennia without maintenance. They weave tough fibers from litholichen and arthropod silk into nets, bags, and padding.
*   **Art & Spirituality:** The petroglyphs are their primary art form – maps, histories, ecological observations, and spiritual concepts etched into eternally stable surfaces. Spirals represent the Deep's breath and the flow of time; chasms symbolize both danger and connection; fauna depictions are often stylized warnings or reverence. Their spirituality is deeply tied to the planet's thrum ("The Great Resonance") and the slow, inevitable processes of geology. They see themselves as listeners and harmonizers within this eternal song. The arranged stones in niches are offerings, prayers for stability, or markers of significant events measured in geological time.

## Relationship with the Deep
*   **Subsistence:** Lithovores themselves to a degree. They cultivate litholichen gardens in warm crevices, harvest crystalline dendrites carefully (allowing regrowth), and hunt the silent arthropods and smaller, softer lithofauna using traps, nets, and silent ambush. Their metabolism is incredibly efficient, extracting minimal moisture from their food and the rare crystalline rime. Water, as a concept, is mystical – the "Memory" referenced in their rules.
*   **Predation & Danger:** They are both predator and prey. Large bio-luminescent worms and silent fliers hunt them. Their primary defenses are silence, stealth, profound knowledge of the terrain, and the ability to vanish into crevices or trigger controlled rockfalls against threats.
*   **The Rules Embodied:** The Whisperers *are* the rules of the Deep. Their entire existence revolves around silence, seismic awareness, respecting the sharpness and instability of their world, adapting to the mineral-based ecology, and existing within the eternal equilibrium. A loud sound is sacrilege; a careless step is potential suicide; disrupting the slow growth is taboo. They are the conscious expression of the Glimmering Deep's harsh, beautiful, and timeless logic.

## Presence
To an outsider, the Deep Whisperers are phantoms. A faint, rhythmic tapping that stops before its source can be pinpointed. A fleeting, elongated shadow against a phosphorescent vein that vanishes when looked at directly. The unsettling feeling of being watched from the absolute darkness just beyond the glow. The faint, dry scent of ozone and crushed stone lingering where nothing visible moved. They are the intelligence that scratched its story onto the walls of the world, not to conquer it, but to listen, understand, and resonate with the mineral dream of the planet itself. They are the Keepers of Silence, the Children of the Thrum, the living stone that learned to think.
