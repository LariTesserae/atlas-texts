The inhabitants of the Fenghollow Expanse are not lords of this realm; they are its **relentless children**, shaped utterly by the cold, wet, roaring chaos. They are the **Fenghollow Stalkers**, a species evolved within the saturated, acidic peat and the shadow of the weeping rocks and humming pods. They are amphibious humanoids, but that term feels inadequate for their profound adaptation.

1.  **Form & Adaptation:**
    *   **Sleek and Streamlined:** Their bodies are long-limbed and slender, built for navigating dense reed-beds and treacherous peat without sinking. Average height is around 7 feet, but their stooped posture in open ground makes them seem shorter.
    *   **Skin:** A marvel of environmental engineering. It’s a thick, leathery integument, nearly black or deep charcoal grey, mottled with patterns of vibrant, wet-looking greens and blood-reds – perfect camouflage against the mosses and liverworts. It secretes a viscous, slightly acidic mucus that:
        *   Repels the constant moisture, preventing fatal chilling.
        *   Protects against the bog's natural acidity and the sharp edges of the weeping rocks.
        *   Deters the swarming insects that plague other creatures.
        *   Glows faintly with a cold, blue bioluminescence along ridges on their limbs, spine, and brow – essential communication in the perpetual gloom and mist.
    *   **Limbs:** Long arms end in broad, webbed hands with thick, knobby knuckles and short, powerful claws – ideal for digging through peat, gripping slick rock, and swimming through icy pools. Their legs are powerful but digitigrade, ending in large, splayed feet with thick, leathery pads and semi-retractable claws, functioning like natural snowshoes on the unstable peat.
    *   **Senses:**
        *   **Eyes:** Large, entirely black orbs, lidless and protected by a transparent nictitating membrane. They see primarily in low-light and infrared spectrums, piercing the mist and darkness to detect heat signatures of prey and the subtle energy emissions of the pods.
        *   **Ears:** Not external flaps, but complex arrangements of sensitive skin folds and internal chambers recessed into the sides of their skulls. They filter the deafening ambient noise to pinpoint specific sounds – the gurgle of a collapsing hag, the click of prey, the subtle shift in a pod's hum.
        *   **Vibration Sense:** Highly attuned to vibrations through the saturated ground and water, feeling footsteps, collapses, and distant pod tremors long before seeing or hearing them.
        *   **Chemoreception:** A long, forked tongue constantly flicks out, tasting the air and water for pheromones, decay, methane leaks, and the unique metallic tang of the pods.

2.  **Life & Survival:**
    *   **Diet:** Omnivorous opportunists. They hunt the abundant amphibians and small furred creatures, raid insect nests, gather edible mosses and tubers from the bog, and are known to crack open the shells of large, sluggish crustaceans found in deeper pools. They are also drawn to the pods, sometimes scavenging strange, metallic lichen or crystalline growths that form near them.
    *   **Habitat:** They don't build permanent structures. Instead, they utilize natural features:
        *   **Rock Crevices:** Sheltering under overhangs of the weeping rocks, where the constant drip provides fresh water.
        *   **Peat Hovels:** Occasionally digging shallow, temporary burrows into stable peat banks, lined with dried moss.
        *   **Pod Proximity:** Small family groups often camp near, but never directly touching, the humming tech-pods. The pods' ambient energy seems to provide subtle warmth, deter some predators, and stimulate the growth of edible fungi and lichen.
    *   **Mobility:** Expert swimmers and bog-walkers. They move with surprising silence when hunting, but can erupt into terrifying speed when charging or fleeing collapsing ground. They navigate by memorizing rock formations, pod locations, and subtle changes in vegetation and water flow.

3.  **Society & Culture (As Far As It Can Be Understood):**
    *   **Nomadic & Fluid:** Stalkers live in small, shifting kinship groups (5-15 individuals). Larger gatherings are rare, usually triggered by a massive pod event or an abundant, localized food source.
    *   **Communication:** Primarily visual (bioluminescent patterns flashing complex messages across skin) and vibrational (stomps, hand-claps on wet ground, resonant clicks in their throats). Vocalizations are guttural croaks and whistles, used sparingly amidst the ambient noise, often for alarm or long-distance calls mimicking the bog's own sounds.
    *   **Relationship with Pods:** This defines them. The pods are not gods, but immense, unpredictable forces – sources of danger, warmth, fascination, and strange bounty. Stalker behavior near pods is ritualistic and cautious: specific patterns of bioluminescence are displayed, offerings of food or unusual minerals might be left at a "safe" distance, and intense observation is constant. They seem to have an innate, perhaps vibrationally sensed, understanding of a pod's imminent fluctuation. Some groups appear to have territories centered around specific pods.
    *   **Technology:** Minimal and organic. Tools are bone, sharpened rock, hardened peat, sinew, and chitin. They weave water-resistant cloaks from thick reed fibers and treated moss. Their one sophisticated "tool" is their deep, ingrained understanding of the Expanse's chaotic dynamics.

4.  **Psychology & Demeanor:**
    *   **Hyper-Vigilant:** Constant environmental threats (collapsing ground, predators, pod surges) have honed their senses and reflexes to a razor's edge. They are rarely still, constantly scanning, tasting, listening, and feeling.
    *   **Patient & Opportunistic:** They can wait motionless for hours in freezing water or huddled on a rock, waiting for prey or for a pod's activity to subside.
    *   **Alien Calm:** Despite the roaring environment and constant danger, they project an unnerving stillness when not actively threatened. Their large, dark eyes reveal nothing readily comprehensible to outsiders.
    *   **Wary of Outsiders:** Anything not of the bog is viewed with deep suspicion, as a potential predator or a destabilizing element. Their reaction is usually avoidance, observation, or, if cornered, terrifyingly swift and brutal defense.

The Fenghollow Stalkers *are* the Expanse made flesh: wet, cold, resilient, thriving in the deafening dark, navigating the sucking peat and sharp rocks with innate grace, forever dancing on the edge of the chaotic energies humming from the silent, enigmatic pods that shape their world. They are creatures born of perpetual aftermath, perfectly adapted to a landscape that is perpetually remaking itself in the saturated gloom. To see one emerge silently from the mist, bioluminescence glimmering faintly on slick, peat-black skin, is to glimpse the living, breathing essence of Fenghollow itself.
