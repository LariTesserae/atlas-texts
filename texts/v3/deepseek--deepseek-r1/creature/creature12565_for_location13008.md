Here, life exists in the shadow and light of the titans. The ecology is sparse, vertical, and utterly dependent on the Stone Trees, forming intricate, fragile webs upon and between the colossal pillars:

1.  **The Lithoflora (Stone-Tree Dependents):**
    *   **Lumen-Ticks (Symbiotic Epiphytes):** Clinging directly to the crystalline bark, especially near the pulsing vascular systems, are colonies of fist-sized, hexagonal organisms. Their bodies are transparent chitin filled with swirling, light-sensitive algae. They slowly migrate across the bark, harvesting the Tree's ambient radiance for their symbionts. In return, their root-like cilia secrete enzymes that dissolve mineral build-up and prevent parasitic encrustations, performing a vital "cleaning" service for the Tree. They glow softly, emerald near the veins, shifting to pale blue on colder, exposed surfaces.
    *   **Frost-Striders (Condensation Harvesters):** Delicate, multi-legged arthropods (6-12 legs) resembling spun glass and frozen mercury. They inhabit the colder, shadowed lower trunks and ridges near the plains. Their long, filamentous legs possess hyper-sensitive hygroscopic pads. They "dance" across surfaces where condensation forms, harvesting microscopic beads of water and dissolved minerals before it freezes or evaporates. Highly sensitive to vibration and air currents, they vanish into fissures at the slightest disturbance. Their movements leave faint, glittering trails of rapidly sublimating frost.
    *   **Sap-Drinkers (Parasitic Borers):** Found only near fissures or damage in the stone-bark, these are sluggish, slug-like creatures with drill-tipped proboscises made of bio-crystal harder than the surrounding rock. They bore shallow wells into the Tree, seeking the viscous, luminous sap. Their bodies swell with the cold fluid, becoming pulsing lanterns of green or amber light. They are heavily armored but slow; predation is their main danger, not the Tree's defenses, which seem largely indifferent to such minor insults.

2.  **The Canopy Dwellers (Navigators of Light and Storm):**
    *   **Sky-Ghosts (Aerial Filter Feeders):** The primary "flying" fauna. Appearing as complex kites or sails woven from light-refracting chitin and resilient, silk-like membranes, they are more structure than creature. They lack conventional heads or limbs, instead manipulating their entire form to catch thermals rising from the plains and the turbulent currents between the Trees. They drift through the canopy storms, vast filtering arrays on their undersides harvesting luminescent pollen, ice crystals, and even the glass spiders unlucky enough to be swept aloft. They emit faint, haunting harmonic hums from vibrating tension wires within their structures as they ride the wind.
    *   **Storm-Stalkers (Aerial Predators):** Sleeker, more aggressive versions of the Sky-Ghosts. Smaller and more maneuverable, they possess rudimentary manipulator tendrils and beaked mouths hidden within their folds. They actively hunt Sky-Ghosts, Lumen-Ticks dislodged by storms, and even snatch Frost-Striders from exposed ridges. Their chitin is dark, almost non-reflective, allowing them to blend into the deep shadows cast by the canopy lattice before striking. They crackle faintly with static electricity gathered from the charged atmosphere.
    *   **Resin-Weavers (Canopy Engineers):** Found deep within the shelter of the thickest crystalline branches. These are communal colonies of insectile creatures, building intricate, geodesic nests from frozen Tree sap and harvested filaments of the canopy lattice. They farm specialized algae within their nests using the Tree's light and harvest wind-blown nutrients. Their nests glow warmly, distinct from the cold light of the Tree, and serve as waypoints or hazards for other canopy dwellers.

3.  **The Ground Scavengers (Survivors of the Plain):**
    *   **Shard-Eaters:** Low-slung, heavily armored creatures resembling trilobites forged from volcanic glass. They scour the jagged plains, particularly after a canopy cascade. Their multiple grinding mouthparts slowly pulverize fallen ice shards, shattered sap-flowers, and even flakes of shed stone-bark, extracting minuscule amounts of organic residue and minerals. Incredibly slow and resilient, they retreat into crevices during storms or when large debris falls, their tough shells deflecting all but the most direct hits.
    *   **Fog-Crawlers:** Rarely seen, these are blind, pallid crustaceans inhabiting the deepest, coldest fissures where icy fog pools. They navigate via chemosensation, feeding on the sparse crystalline mosses and lichens that grow in the perpetual damp and near-darkness. They emerge only when thick fog rivers flow across the plains, moving within the fog bank itself to graze or scavenge, vanishing back into the depths as the fog dissipates.

4.  **The Stone Speakers (The Faint Trace-Makers):**
    *   **Appearance:** Tall, gaunt humanoids with skin like bleached, fissured limestone and hair like coarse, white lichen. Their eyes are large, dark, and deep-set, capable of perceiving subtle shifts in light and shadow across vast distances. Their limbs are long and thin, ending in broad, strong hands adept at gripping rough stone and carving.
    *   **Existence:** They are not masters of this environment, but observers and interpreters. They build their simple shelters only where the Stone Trees provide some lee from the worst canopy storms and falling debris. Their structures are temporary by necessity – rebuilt, moved, or abandoned as the Trees shift and the storms dictate.
    *   **Culture:** Their life revolves around the Trees and the Sky-Ghosts. They are nomadic, moving between their isolated waystations. They possess an intricate understanding of the Tree's groans, the patterns of the canopy storms, and the drift of the Sky-Ghosts. Their scratched glyphs are not mere art, but records – warnings of impending calving events, maps of safe paths relative to Tree movement, observations of Sky-Ghost migrations. They might harvest Frost-Striders (carefully, ritually) or collect fallen, non-luminous resin, but they take nothing vital from the Trees. They seem to view the Sky-Ghosts with reverence, perhaps as messengers or spirits of the upper light.
    *   **Survival:** They endure the cold with layered hides tanned from the rare, large gliding fauna (hunted only when opportunity and dire need coincide) and insulated with processed lichen. They find water at melt-seeps near Tree roots or by collecting frost with absorbent mosses. Their greatest skill is anticipation – reading the signs of the living world to be in the right place at the right time, and more importantly, *not* to be in the wrong place when the giants move or the storms break.

**Ecological Dynamics:**
*   **Symbiosis & Parasitism:** Life is a tightrope walk between utilizing the Tree's bounty and avoiding its destructive indifference. Lumen-Ticks are symbiotic cleaners; Sap-Drinkers are tolerated parasites. Frost-Striders exploit a transient resource without harming the source. Even the Stone Speakers exist parasitically, but minimally.
*   **Vertical Niches:** Life stratifies intensely. The lower trunk/plain interface hosts Frost-Striders and Shard-Eaters. The mid-trunk sees Lumen-Ticks and Sap-Drinkers. The canopy is the domain of the Sky-Ghosts, Storm-Stalkers, and Resin-Weavers. The deep fissures belong to the Fog-Crawlers.
*   **Fragility & Resilience:** Most fauna are physically fragile (crystalline, glass-like) but incredibly resilient to the specific harshness (cold, desiccation, minor impacts). Their populations are likely small and localized, vulnerable to major shifts. The Stone Trees themselves are the ultimate resilience, shaping everything but seemingly unconcerned by the tiny ecosystems clinging to their flanks.
*   **The Silence & The Sound:** Life here is mostly silent. Movement is slow and deliberate. Communication is likely through subtle light patterns, vibrations, or pheromones. The loudest sounds are the *environment's* groans and cracks, emphasizing the insignificance of the creatures within it. The hum of Sky-Ghosts and the skitter of Frost-Striders are whispers lost in the immensity.

This is an ecosystem of luminous fragility clinging to living, mountainous gods. It is beautiful, harsh, and profoundly isolating, where every creature, including the Stone Speakers, survives on the edge of annihilation by the very entities that sustain them. Life here is a testament to tenacity in the face of overwhelming, uncaring scale.
