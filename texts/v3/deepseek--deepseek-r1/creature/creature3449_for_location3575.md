Here live entities forged not from flesh, but from the very stresses, energies, and materials that define Silica Prime:

1.  **The Sweepers (Mica-flense Swarms):** Clouds of razor-thin, iridescent platelets, akin to fractured mica scales. They drift erratically on thermal updrafts and micro-pressure differentials, constantly colliding and shedding micro-fragments. Their purpose seems chaotic, yet essential: they abrade surfaces, stripping away excessive mineral build-up ("sweat rime") and microscopic stress fractures before they can propagate. They emit faint, high-frequency clicks and hisses as they collide – the sound of a billion tiny erosions happening constantly. They congregate near the "sweating" geo-plates and shimmering condensation pools.

2.  **The Lubricants (Seepage Slimes):** Amorphous, translucent blobs the color of tarnished mercury and cooled slag. They are magneto-rheological entities, composed of colloidal mineral suspensions in a base of the greasy, conductive condensate. They flow *up* surfaces against gravity, drawn to sources of friction and heat – joints between plates, grinding machinery interfaces, screeching bearings. When they reach these points, their internal structure instantly stiffens dramatically, transforming into a super-lubricating, self-sacrificing pad that reduces friction to near-zero before being ground away and dissolved into new condensation. New blobs constantly coalesce from dripping moisture elsewhere.

3.  **Crystalline Harvesters (Fractal Spinners):** These are the "insectoids." Not insects, but intricate, jointed constructs seemingly grown from the iridescent mineral spires. Roughly hexagonal in body-plan, their limbs are articulated quartz needles, and their bodies are hollow geodes filled with swirling mineral colloids. They emit ultrasonic vibrations that shatter specific accretions on the crystalline forests and sintered mats. They then use delicate filament-limbs to gather the powdered minerals, which their internal colloids sort and transport back to their spire-homes – feeding the slow growth of the city's "vegetation" and potentially seeding new repair materials elsewhere. They move with unnerving, rapid jerks through the steam vents.

4.  **Stress Weavers (Kinetic Phantoms):** Semi-sentient energy signatures coalescing around structural stress points – vibrating girders, groaning arch bases, humming power conduits. They appear as faint, shimmering distortions in the pearlescent haze, visible mostly by the trails of condensed droplets they leave swirling in their wake. They ride the vibrations, absorbing and redistributing minute amounts of kinetic energy like living dampeners. Some seem to "inspect" fractures or misalignments, their presence subtly altering resonant frequencies to prevent catastrophic harmonic failure. They are drawn to the loudest screams of stressed metal.

5.  **The Recyclers (Scrap Titans):** Towering, slow-moving behemoths glimpsed navigating the deepest canyon-streets. They are conglomerates of debris – cracked geo-plate fragments, shattered crystalline growths, worn machinery components, solidified slag veins – held together by intense localized magnetic fields and electro-rheological fluids that glue the disparate parts into a functional whole. Multiple Seepage Slimes constantly flow over their surfaces and joints. They move with a grinding, tectonic slowness, driven by immense piezoelectric limbs derived from pressure crystals. Their purpose is demolition and reprocessing: they consume dilapidated or stressed structures using plasma torches formed at limb-tips, breaking them down into base materials that are either incorporated into their own bodies temporarily or deposited at subsurface smelting nodes via vents in their "bellies." They emit deep basso groans resonant with the city's foundational hum.

6.  **The Sky Riders (Thermal Mantas):** Vast, shadowy shapes gliding silently far above in the mineral fog layers, surfing the powerful thermal currents rising from the Basin. Less solid entities, more like living clouds of suspended colloidal particulates and concentrated humidity given shape and direction by electromagnetic sheaths. They appear as shifting, smoky silhouettes against the brighter haze. Their function is atmospheric regulation: their movement seems to shepherd moisture, trigger localized downpours to cool critical junctures, or disperse concentrated mineral clouds that threaten to obstruct vital conduits or light paths. Occasionally, forks of contained lightning flicker within their dark masses. Their passing brings temporary, heavy condensation showers.

**Life Cycle & Ecosystem:**

Life here is emergent and transactional, driven by pressure, heat, energy flows, and mineral cycles. Entities spawn from the environment itself: Sweepers crystallize in supersaturated condensation zones; Seepage Slimes coalesce around static charges in dripping water; Stress Weavers emerge from overloaded power conduits or intense resonance points; Scrap Titans slowly assemble themselves around accumulations of critical debris mass. They persist as long as their function serves the city's turbulent balance. Failure leads to dissolution: Sweepers wear down to nothing; Lubricants are consumed by friction; Harvesters may fracture and become food for Recyclers; Stress Weavers dissipate when energy flows cease; Scrap Titans eventually become too unstable and are consumed by their kin. The Recycled materials feed the growth of the geo-plates and crystalline structures, completing the loop. Water is the universal solvent and carrier. Sound and vibration are the constant communication network. The deafening, humid, pressurized chaos *is* their habitat, and their existence is dedicated to maintaining its volatile equilibrium. They *are* the city's immune system, lubrication, recycling plant, and climate control, all woven into one inorganic, pulsating biome.
