The dwellers of the Singing Tangle are less distinct creatures and more *acoustic phenomena given fleeting flesh*, ephemeral manifestations of the basin’s resonant void. They exist in the permeable seams between sound, moisture, and living structure, their forms as negotiable as the mist beading on silver bark.

1.  **The Hum-Singers (Root-Mat Resonators):**
    *   **Form:** Barely perceptible as form at all. They are localized *thickenings* in the glowing amber root-mat, subtle swellings the size of boulders that ripple slowly, like muscles beneath skin. Their surface is a mosaic of fused fungal caps and rootlets, vibrating minutely.
    *   **Life:** They *are* the deep foundational HUM. Capillary action drawing moisture isn't just a process; it's their respiration. As damp air permeates their porous structure, the resulting hydraulic pressure differentials cause their entire mass to vibrate at a profound, subsonic frequency. They are sessile, ancient nodes in the root-network, their slow pulses syncing with the basin’s moisture intake. When the mist thickens, their vibration deepens; during rare dry moments, they fall eerily silent, becoming cold, inert mounds.

2.  **The Chitter-Swarms (Aurora Motes):**
    *   **Form:** Clouds of iridescent particles, each no larger than a grain of sand. Seen in shafts of bruised twilight, they resemble disturbed glitter or bioluminescent dust. Close inspection (difficult, as they phase through focus) reveals moth-wing silhouettes made of hardened, vibrating mist, refracting the canopy's glow into prismatic shards.
    *   **Life:** Their existence is pure vibration. Each "mote" is a self-contained resonator, its wings beating at ultrasonic frequencies against the saturated air. The collective movement generates the pervasive, metallic CHITTERING. They are drawn to sound and humidity gradients, swirling through cathedral vaults like chaotic wind chimes. They phase through moss-curtains effortlessly, momentarily dimming as they pass through solid matter, emerging with a faint *pop* of ozone. They feed on condensed mist and ambient bio-luminescence, dissolving back into vapor when spent.

3.  **The Drift-Screamers (Phantom Medusae):**
    *   **Form:** Translucent, pulsating blobs ranging from the size of a human to that of a small bus. Their bodies resemble gelatinous, bioluminescent jellyfish, but formed from compressed air, mist, and stray strands of rubbery moss. Internal organs are vague, shifting constellations of light (amber, violet, cold blue) within their ghostly substance. Long, trailing tendrils emit faint sparks.
    *   **Life:** They are the source of the jarring SCREECHES. They drift silently through the immense negative spaces, navigating by sensing air pressure and resonance harmonics. Their movement is slow, graceful buoyancy. When encountering an obstacle – a pillar, a dense curtain, even another Drifter – or experiencing a sudden pressure change, they don't go *around*; they *scream*. Their entire body contracts violently, forcing air through internal structures like organic bagpipes, producing a shriek that tears through the drone. This sonic blast also momentarily destabilizes local reality, allowing them to *phase* through the obstacle in a ripple of ozone and displaced mist, often merging briefly with the structure, sharing luminescence and moisture before detaching. The scent of crushed mint intensifies around them post-scream.

4.  **The Drip-Weavers (Condensate Sculptors):**
    *   **Form:** Small, arboreal entities resembling sticky, multi-limbed frogs or arboreal slugs. They cling to the undersides of high canopy leaves and the grooved surfaces of the silver trunks, their slick, grey-green skin indistinguishable from wet bark or lichen.
    *   **Life:** They are the silent curators of the constant **DRIP-DRIP-DRIP**. They move with glacial slowness, guided by humidity. Their role is to gather the condensing mist on their bodies and channel it along specific pathways on the bark or leaf veins. They subtly sculpt micro-grooves and capillary channels, directing the flow and coalescence of droplets to maximize the acoustic impact when the water finally falls onto specific resonant surfaces (broad fungal caps, taut moss sheets, hollow root cavities) far below. Their slow work turns random condensation into the amplified, rhythmic percussion of the Tangle.

5.  **The Void-Stirrings (The Unseen Traffic):**
    *   **Form:** Invisible. Not hidden, but *truly without form*. They manifest only as disturbances in the environment: a faint deepening of the HUM as something heavy moves through a root-tunnel far below; a sudden eddy in a Chitter-Swarm avoiding empty air; a patch of glowing root-mat compressing and rebounding in a slow, purposeful sequence along a "street" channel when nothing tangible is there; a moss-curtain twitching aside as if held, revealing only empty space beyond.
    *   **Life:** These are the inhabitants implied by the millennia of traffic wearing the root-mat streets and the scale of the empty architecture. They are presences defined by their effect on sound, light, and pressure. Perhaps they are pure acoustic entities, self-sustaining vibrations that briefly cohere. Perhaps they are creatures existing in a sensory spectrum beyond sight, their forms made of shifting magnetic fields or infrasound. They move through the hollows, utilizing the permeable boundaries, leaving only ripples in the fabric of the Tangle's reality and the undeniable evidence of their passage in the worn paths and the responsive resonance of the living structure. They are the true citizens of the void, the reason the emptiness feels *occupied*.

**Symbiosis & Permeability:**
Life here blurs constantly. A Drift-Screamer might snag a cluster of Aurora Motes with a tendril, absorbing them momentarily – the CHITTERING silenced, the Drifter's bioluminescence flaring brighter. A Drip-Weaver might be engulfed by a passing moss-curtain, working *within* the curtain's substance for days before emerging. Hum-Singers integrate collapsing sinkholes, their vibrations helping compact the root-mat for regrowth. The inhabitants aren't separate from the Singing Tangle; they are its emergent functions – sound, moisture flow, and structural decay made animate within its resonant, hollow, and utterly organic cathedral. They live in the damp, in the sound, and in the permeable spaces between all things.
