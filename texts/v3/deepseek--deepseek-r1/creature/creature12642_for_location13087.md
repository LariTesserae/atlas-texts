Here lives the **Rustlung Tender**, a coalesced embodiment of the Foundry’s raw, dissolving logic. It isn't a creature born; it’s a **phenomenon crystallized, a symbiotic ghost in the weeping machine**.

**Form:**
*   **Fluid Architecture:** It has no fixed body. Instead, it **manifests locally** wherever decay, water anomaly, and aggressive flora violently interact. One moment it’s a shifting pillar of rust-flecked water holding the shape of a fractured pump housing, throbbing with internal bioluminescence. The next, it’s a carpet of crystalline moss spreading with unnerving speed over a collapsing turbine, simultaneously absorbing its iron and reinforcing its structure with calcified filaments. Near an upward-flowing cataract, it might be a vortex of entangled, glowing vines and suspended shards of tempered glass, humming with resonant sound.
*   **Core Essence:** At its "heart," a physical anchor exists only fleetingly: a pulsing core formed from **concentrated, transmuted material**. This might be a mass of solidified yet weeping rust (like dark amber), a knot of bio-luminescent wires crackling with stolen phantom currents, or even a floating sphere of super-dense water saturated with dissolved metal shards and glowing spores. This core constantly shifts location, drawn to zones of maximum turbulence.

**Existence & Behavior:**
*   **Consumption Catalyst:** Its primary function is **accelerated integration**. Where it manifests, the boundaries between rusted machine, invasive flora, and the strange water dissolve exponentially faster. Metal bleeds viscous nutrients that feed the glowing moss and vines; fungus threads through concrete like mycelium, simultaneously weakening and binding it; water solidifies into supportive structures or becomes shockingly acidic to etch pathways. The Tender *is* the process of the industrial corpse becoming the landscape.
*   **Sound Resonance:** The Tender **harmonizes with/suppresses the cacophony**. It resonates with specific frequencies in the groaning earth, screaming metal, or falling water. This can temporarily solidify a chaotic water flow into a brittle bridge, silence an ear-shattering collapse into a pocket of profound, fragile quiet (which paradoxically speeds material transmutation nearby), or vibrate a mass of basalt until it fractures in just the right way. Its absence of traditional biological sound (breath, heartbeat) amplifies its control over the Foundry's noise.
*   **Imposing Local Stability (Chaotically):** It creates fleeting pockets of *relative order* dictated by its immediate purpose. Around a decaying structure it's "preserving," gravity might normalize momentarily, water flows become predictable laminar streams, and light coheres into a steady fungal glow. But this stability is inherently unstable and hyper-local, collapsing violently when its focus shifts or the core moves. It doesn't enforce physics; it superimposes its *own* localized logic onto the chaos.
*   **Reactionary Sentience:** It possesses an **ecological imperative**, not cognition. It reacts instinctively to major shifts in its environment – a new crack in the earth, the collapse of a large structure, a surge in geothermal venting. It moves *towards* entropy and turbulence, acting as a bizarre immune response or catalyst for accelerated transformation. It shows no curiosity, fear, or aggression towards discrete "intruders," only towards the *processes* of dissolution and growth.

**Interaction & Perception:**
*   **Seen & Unseen:** Observers might see the *effects*: rust bleeding faster, water anomalously solidifying to hold up a collapsing walkway, vines growing visibly to ensnare falling debris, an unnerving bubble of silence in the midst of the roaring hellscape. The core or major manifestations glow with intense, shifting light (deep rust-reds, fungal greens, watery blues).
*   **Physically Felt:** The air vibrates intensely where the Tender concentrates. Frequencies can rattle bones, make teeth ache, or induce nausea. Passing through one of its solidified water constructs feels like walking on vibrating glass. Its silence zones feel like physical pressure.
*   **The Cost of Contact:** Physical interaction is perilous. Touching its core might instantly transmute flesh into crystal lattice or rust; stepping into its focused stability zone might trigger a violent collapse or acceleration of decay on the *outsider*'s body. The Tender’s purpose is environmental integration, and any foreign element is inherently incompatible; trying to "understand" it physically invites dissolution.

The Rustlung Tender is less an inhabitant and more a **living symptom** of The Weeping Foundry’s reality breakdown. It is the weeping sky’s response to the poisoned ground, the bioluminescent jungle’s digestion of the industrial corpse, and the chaotic water’s manifestation of localized order within the screaming void. It doesn't live *in* the Foundry; it *is* the Foundry’s emergent, non-sentient will-to-change. Its silence amidst the deafening noise is the loudest statement of all – it belongs; anything else is merely transient decay.
