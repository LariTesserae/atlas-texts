The inhabitants of the Weeping Arborium are not merely creatures that dwell *within* the Rooted Spire; they are expressions of its perpetual flux, extensions of its weeping architecture, and custodians of its turbulent equilibrium. They are beings woven into the fabric of the Nexus, as integral as its pulsing sap-veins or chiming fungi.

1.  **The Sap-Singers (Vocal Symbiotes):**
    *   **Form:** Appearing as clusters of iridescent, gelatinous bubbles clinging to fissures in the weeping trunk or the undersides of massive aerial roots. Each "bubble" is a semi-independent sac, ranging from fist-sized to as large as a human torso, connected by thin, pulsating cords of bioluminescent mycelium.
    *   **Nature:** Not fauna in a traditional sense, but hyper-specialized symbiotic organs of the Nexus itself, evolved for communication and environmental tuning. Their gelatinous bodies are primarily composed of structured water and conductive proteins.
    *   **Function:** They absorb the vibrational hum of the Nexus and the chaotic drip-percussion. Through subtle oscillations and resonant frequencies within their sacs, they modulate, harmonize, and amplify these sounds. The result is a complex, ever-shifting **bio-symphony**. Deep, resonant drones counterpoint the high chitters of insect-analogues; the sharp chimes of fungi are woven into rhythmic patterns by the collective oscillation of a cluster. Their "song" isn't just sound; it influences the flow of sap, the opening/closing of micro-pores in roots, and even the directional growth of nearby moss-crawlers. They are the Nexus's voice and its tuning fork.
    *   **Interaction:** Disturbing them directly is unwise and nearly impossible without damaging the Nexus. Their song, however, can be felt viscerally – a physical pressure and resonance in the chest and bones. Standing near a large cluster induces a trance-like state as one's own biological rhythms attempt to synchronize.

2.  **The Root-Weavers (Architectural Custodians):**
    *   **Form:** Segmented, centipede-like entities, but grown from dense, fibrous peat and interlaced with root filaments. They vary in size from finger-length scouts to massive, slow-moving titans longer than the armored browsers. Their countless legs are sharp, root-tip probes. Their "heads" are complex sensory knots of glowing lichen and photosensitive nodules, lacking traditional eyes or mouths.
    *   **Nature:** Mobile manifestations of the Nexus's growth and repair systems. They are the visible agents of the "turbulent dynamism."
    *   **Function:** They constantly traverse the root-buttresses, peat-sponge, and water channels. Scouts assess structural integrity, nutrient flow, and water seepage using bio-electrical impulses. Larger Weavers follow, performing micro-surgery: sealing fissures with secreted resins, stimulating rapid growth in weakened areas, pruning encroaching epiphytes, or deliberately *excavating* new seep-points to redirect weeping water. They orchestrate the visible "crawling" of mosses and the retraction/extension of root-tendrils. Their movement is a slow, deliberate, wet scraping and probing.
    *   **Interaction:** They are indifferent to macro-fauna unless obstructing critical pathways or damaging the Nexus. Blocking a titan-Weaver might result in a gentle, but irresistible, nudging by root-tip legs, or the creature simply growing *around* the obstruction over hours. Damaging one triggers a localized immune response – rapid fibrous growth to entangle the threat and acidic secretions.

3.  **The Mist-Gazers (Ephemeral Sentinels):**
    *   **Form:** Tall (2-3 meters), impossibly slender figures seemingly woven from the hanging mist itself. Their bodies are condensed water vapor held in intricate, shifting lattice-works of force-fields generated by embedded quantum-entangled algae colonies. They lack solid form; their outline shimmers and reforms constantly. Within their core, dense swarms of the iridescent insect-analogues swirl like captive nebulae.
    *   **Nature:** Ephemeral intelligences born from the confluence of mist, light, quantum algae, and insectoid swarm-mind. They are short-lived (hours to days) but constantly re-forming.
    *   **Function:** They drift silently through the arches and colonnades, particularly in zones of shifting light or intense mist concentration. They appear to observe, their "gaze" a palpable scanning sensation like focused humidity. They monitor energy flows (light absorption), atmospheric composition, and the movement of larger fauna. They act as distributed sensors for the Nexus, dissolving and reforming their data into the bio-electrical network upon "death." Their insect-cloud cores may detach to investigate disturbances before returning.
    *   **Interaction:** They are elusive and non-corporeal. Passing through one feels like walking through a cold, electrically charged fog that momentarily holds your shape. They may cluster around sources of unusual energy or sudden movement, their silent observation deeply unsettling. They cannot be harmed conventionally, only dispersed temporarily.

4.  **The Deep-Tenders (Cardiac Symbionts):**
    *   **Form:** Rarely seen near the periphery. Glimpses suggest massive, amoebic shapes the color of dark wine, moving with glacial slowness *within* the trunk's heartwood or the thickest root-columns. Bioluminescent patterns pulse across their surfaces in rhythms mirroring, yet subtly influencing, the Nexus's core hum.
    *   **Nature:** Ancient, semi-sentient bacterial colonies evolved alongside the Nexus, dwelling within its most critical vascular and quantum-processing chambers.
    *   **Function:** They are the Nexus's immune system, metabolic regulators, and quantum-state stabilizers. They process complex nutrients directly from the sap, neutralize toxins, repair entangled growth regulators at the sub-atomic level, and modulate the immense energy flows from the photosynthetic reactors deep within. Their slow pulses are the heartbeat behind the hum.
    *   **Interaction:** Encountering one directly would require penetrating the Nexus's deepest, most protected sanctums – an act likely triggering catastrophic defensive responses. Their influence is felt everywhere: in the stability of the light, the consistency of the weeping water, the very persistence of the Spire against entropic decay. A disturbance to the Deep-Tenders would manifest as system-wide failure – erratic pulsing, searing hot or freezing cold sap leaks, chaotic growth spurts, or sudden, dangerous shifts in local gravity/time perception.

**The Underlying Principle:** Life in the Weeping Arborium isn't separate from the environment; it *is* the environment in motion. The inhabitants are processes made manifest – growth, sound, repair, observation, and regulation given temporary, dynamic form. They exist on the Nexus's timescales, from the nanoseconds of the Deep-Tenders' quantum adjustments to the centuries-long patrols of the largest Root-Weavers. To witness them is to witness the Arborium thinking, feeling, and rebuilding itself, drip by chaotic drip, in an eternal, green moment. You walk among the organs of a living city.
