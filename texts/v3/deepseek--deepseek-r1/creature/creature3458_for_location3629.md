## Resident: The Lech-Tech Colonies

Nothing born of flesh and blood could survive long in the pressurized, dripping inferno of Thermal Utility Nexus Gamma. The true residents aren't creatures; they’re **symbiotic accretions**, a slow-mutation of chemistry and neglect that has become as much a part of the Engine Cavern as the rivets and basalt. They are the **Lichen-Technic Colonies**, or "Lech-Tech."

1.  **The Pipe-Scalers (Thermophilus chroma):** These are the most visible inhabitants. Clinging like irradiated barnacles to the hottest sections of steam conduits and condenser casings, they resemble thick, wet felt. Their colours are warnings: pulsing chartreuse veins in toxic yellow masses, lurid magenta splotches ringed with decaying ochre, bruised indigo patches near cooler vents. They feed on trace volatiles in the escaping steam – sulfur compounds, metal oxides, minute hydrocarbon leaks – and convert the blistering pipe-heat directly into metabolic energy through chemosynthesis. Their fluorescence isn’t mere decoration; it’s waste luminescence from unstable reactions, casting shifting, sickly halos on the wet metal. They grow painfully slow, millimeters per year, but relentlessly, filling crevices, encrusting valve stems, forcing operators to constantly chip them away lest they compromise seals. To touch them bare-handed is to invite severe chemical burns.

2.  **The Weep-Stalks (Precipitas crystallum):** Where mineral-laden condensate drips incessantly onto slightly cooler metal or pooled effluent, the Weep-Stalks form. They are not truly alive, but their growth is an ongoing, quasi-biological process fueled by the cavern's thermodynamics and chemistry. Imagine delicate, branching stalagmites composed not of limestone, but of rapidly-deposited metallic salts and silica, extruded like glassy secretions. Some resemble fragile coral skeletons; others look like clusters of fractured lenses or spilled, solidified solder. They grow vertically towards the drips that nourish them, their composition reflecting the mineral soup of the water they precipitate from – copper salts yield turquoise and malachite-green spires; iron oxides create rust-red, honeycombed lattices; manganese bleeds into jagged black crystals. They shatter easily with a sharp *tink* under a boot or tool, but regrow swiftly, sometimes visibly accruing layers in a single shift cycle where the drip is constant. They choke drains and pit walkways.

3.  **The Shadow-Tides (Effluvia mucosa):** Less distinct but far more pervasive is the Shadow-Tide. This is a thin, viscous biofilm, a greasy iridescent sheen on stagnant puddles or coating perpetually damp rock faces deep in the least-maintained crevices. It thrives in the cooler, lower-light margins where pipe heat diminishes but dampness persists. Composed of extremophile bacteria and mineral colloids, it moves almost imperceptibly, flowing like cold tar on convection currents within the water or creeping microscopically along moist surfaces. Its primary role is mineral scavenger, trapping suspended particulates from the steamy air and effluent, slowly building thin mineral crusts beneath itself. It smells faintly of ozone, hot metal, and stagnant brine. Disturbing it releases this odor intensely, and while not directly toxic, prolonged contact irritates skin and corrodes unprotected metal.

**Existence Within the Drip and Thump:**

*   **Symbiosis & Adversity:** The Lech-Tech Colonies are parasites tolerated grudgingly. The Pipe-Scalers insulate pipes *slightly*, but hinder maintenance. The Weep-Stalks divert water but block flow paths. The Shadow-Tides stabilize sediments but accelerate corrosion. They exploit the Engine's waste heat, moisture, and chemical byproducts.
*   **Tempo:** Their life cycle mirrors the cavern’s geological-industrial rhythm. They grow and spread slowly, measured in months and years, yet their *presence* reacts instantly to shifts: a steam leak accelerates Pipe-Scaler fluorescence; a new drip source sprouts a cluster of Weep-Stalks overnight; a drop in pressure causes Shadow-Tides to visibly retract. They are sensitive barometers of the Nexus's unseen processes.
*   **Dominion:** In the humid, dripping silence broken only by the rhythmic *thump-thump-thump* and the eternal hiss of steam, the Lech-Tech Colonies are the dominant ecology. They glow faintly in the cage-light gloom, crumble softly underfoot as operators pass, and relentlessly reclaim any surface left untended. They are the verdigris and rust given slow intention. They are what thrives amidst the machinery's sweat and exhalations – the true natives of the pressurized, dripping heart. The human crews are mere ephemeral irritants scratching at an ancient, patient skin slowly knitting itself around the iron bones of the Engine.
