The inhabitants of the Mist-Girt Kelpwood Hamlet are the **Kelp-Reavers**, a people sculpted by the Spine Kelp's immense, slow rhythms and the ever-present embrace of the mist and sea. They are not conquerors of this vertical world, but its symbiotic children, woven into its fabric as surely as the air-ferns clinging to the kelp's bark.

1.  **Physique & Adaptation:**
    *   **Lithe and Strong:** Generations of navigating the treacherous, slippery architecture of the Spine Kelp have made them lean and incredibly agile. Their musculature is defined for balance and grip, not brute strength. They possess exceptional core stability and preternatural sure-footedness.
    *   **Damp-Proofed:** Their skin has a slightly waxy, resilient quality, resistant to constant moisture and chafing from damp kelp fibers. Fingers and toes often show subtle webbing or thickened pads for better purchase on wet surfaces. Some lineages might have faint suction-cup adaptations on palms or soles.
    *   **Eyes of the Mist:** Their eyes are large, adapted to the perpetual pearl-gray gloom. Irises are often pale – silvers, grays, washed-out blues or greens – capable of gathering the faintest light. They possess excellent peripheral vision and depth perception crucial for judging distances on swaying bridges and across mist-obscured gaps.
    *   **Sea-Ears:** Ears are often slightly pointed or cupped, attuned to the layered soundscape – the subtle shift in wave resonance below warning of storms, the specific croak of a bull kelp-frog, the distant cry of a skimmer gull over the fog's dampening blanket.

2.  **Appearance & Attire:**
    *   **Kelpcotton & Sinew:** Clothing is minimalist and functional, woven from painstakingly processed kelp fibers ("kelpcotton") or cured fish-skin leather. Layers are loose for ease of movement but cinched at wrists and ankles to avoid snagging. Waterproof cloaks, treated with the kelp's own resinous secretions mixed with fish oil, are essential.
    *   **Subtle Adornment:** Decoration is rare and meaningful. Polished shells, chips of luminous polyp-stone, or beads carved from hardened amber tears might be woven into hair (often kept short or tightly braided) or worn as simple necklaces. Tattoos are intricate but subtle, mimicking silver moss-worm trails or the vein patterns of ghost orchids, applied with bone needles and plant-based inks.
    *   **Weather-Worn:** Complexions are pale from the filtered light, often bearing faint patterns of wind and water marks. Hair tends towards shades of dark seaweed brown, kelp-black, or sun-bleached driftwood blonde.

3.  **Culture & Society:**
    *   **Patience as Virtue:** Their defining characteristic is profound, ingrained patience. Decisions are made slowly, tasks take the time they take (repairing a bladder bridge might be a season's work), and urgency is reserved for true emergencies like sudden structural failure or a rogue wave impact. "Kelp-time" governs all.
    *   **Communal Necessity:** Survival demands cooperation. While families have their hollowed-node dwellings, labor is shared. Fishing, maintaining aquaculture ponds, harvesting epiphytes, processing kelp-fiber, and repairing structures are communal endeavors. Knowledge is passed orally, with elders holding deep understanding of the Spine Kelp's moods and the sea's patterns.
    *   **Silent Communication:** Loud voices are rare outside specific rituals. They communicate extensively through subtle hand signs, body posture, and soft whistles that mimic the fauna, allowing coordination over distances without disturbing the ambient soundscape or alerting predators. Full silence is also deeply respected.
    *   **Craft of the Kelp:** Their technology is elegant in its simplicity and perfect adaptation. Everything is derived from the Spine Kelp or the sea: bone hooks and needles, stone blades knapped from volcanic rock (perhaps scavenged from deep dives or rare flotsam), cordage of incredible strength from kelp fibers, tools and containers carved from driftwood or shaped from the hardened amber-resin. They are masters of knotwork and lashing.

4.  **Spirituality & Belief:**
    *   **The Kelp is Life:** The Spine Kelp is not just home; it is revered as a semi-sentient entity, the Great Root, the Sky-Piercer. Its slow growth, its provision of water and materials, its shelter, are seen as conscious gifts. Rituals involve offerings of the first catch or harvest into the cisterns or onto specific, ancient nodes.
    *   **Ancestors in the Mist:** The all-consuming fog is believed to be the breath of the ancestors and the boundary to the spirit world. Distant glimpses of other Spine Kelps during storms are seen as glimpses of other realms or ancestral islands. The nightly thickening of the fog is a sacred time for quiet reflection and listening for ancestral whispers felt in the deep sea-resonance.
    *   **Amber Tears:** The kelp's viscous secretions are considered sacred tears, gifts of binding and preservation. Working with the amber-resin is a ritualized craft, often accompanied by chants or focused silence.
    *   **Respect for the Small:** The intricate ecosystem is seen as a reflection of the Great Root's health. Over-harvesting is taboo. Moss-worms, polyps, beetles, and even the kelp-frogs are acknowledged as vital kin within the whole.

5.  **Relationship with the World:**
    *   **Vertical Fisherfolk:** Their primary sustenance comes from the sea below and the kelp itself. They are expert fishers using lines, nets, and traps lowered into the abyss or set in the submerged root forests. They cultivate tidal pools, harvest nutrient-rich sea-moss and epiphytes, and occasionally hunt skimmer gulls or otter-porpoises (with deep respect and ritual).
    *   **Mist-Walkers:** They possess an uncanny ability to navigate the fog, reading subtle shifts in wind, moisture, sound, and even the kelp's own minute vibrations. Venturing beyond their immediate terraces requires courage and is usually done for specific, vital reasons, marked by small rituals.
    *   **Guardians of the Height:** They see themselves as stewards, not owners. Their slow, deliberate existence is an act of profound balance, maintaining the hamlet without straining the Great Root that sustains them. They are deeply aware of their fragile perch between the roaring deep and the silent void of the mist.

The Kelp-Reavers are a testament to quiet resilience. They are a people who have learned to breathe with the slow pulse of a giant plant, to find abundance in vertical isolation, and to listen more than they speak, forever suspended in their cool, liquid, mist-girt world. Their lives are a continuous, patient dialogue with the Spine Kelp and the eternal sea.
