The inhabitants of the Singing Cliffs are not creatures of flesh, but **resonant stone given slow, patient sentience**. They are the **Lithosomni**, the Stone Dreamers – beings born from the plateau’s groaning emergence and the discordant song of the crystalline forests. They are not the builders of the scattered obelisks and shelters; those are the fossilized echoes of *other* intelligences, long consumed by the plateau’s slow violence or driven mad by its song. The Lithosomni *are* the plateau, awakening.

### Form & Substance
*   **Emergent Sculptures:** Each Lithosomnus is unique, a slowly evolving sculpture of resonant minerals. They appear as jagged, humanoid figures roughly 8-10 feet tall, assembled from shards of singing obsidian, resonant basalt, luminous quartz veins, and splinters of the glassy mineral trees. Limbs are articulated columns of stacked, interlocking plates. Torsos are fractured geodes, their interiors pulsing with captured starlight or the deep emerald/amethyst glow of the crystalline mosses, which often encrust their joints and crevices like symbiotic lichen. Their "heads" are angular clusters of quartz crystals, acting as multifaceted sensory organs and resonating chambers.
*   **Non-Aqueous Life:** They require no water, air, or organic sustenance. They are **chemosynthetic lithovores**, metabolizing the plateau itself. They absorb mineral dust from the fractures, catalyze reactions using the faint starlight and the ambient resonant energy of the singing stone, and incorporate new mineral growth into their forms over centuries. Damage is repaired by slow accretion, not healing.
*   **Resonant Core:** At their core is a fist-sized, ever-shifting knot of hyper-dense crystal – a captured fragment of the **reality breakdown**. This core hums perpetually, vibrating in sympathy with the plateau's deepest groans and the song of the trees. It is their mind, heart, and voice.

### Perception & Existence
*   **Slow Time:** Their perception operates on **gradual cycles**. A single "thought" might take hours; an observation, days. They experience the plateau's millennial shifts as we might experience seasons. A spire collapsing is a sudden, violent event in their timeframe.
*   **Resonant Sense:** They "see" through vibration and resonance, not light. The constant **deafening** drone is their rich sensory landscape. They map the plateau by feeling the unique harmonic signatures of each rock formation, fracture, and crystal tree grove. The **reality breakdown** manifests to them as shifts in harmonic stability – a cliff folding in on itself sounds like a chord collapsing into dissonance.
*   **The Song is Thought:** Their communication and cognition *are* the song. Subtle shifts in the vibration of their core, amplified and modulated by their crystalline structures, create complex patterns within the plateau's existing drone. A conversation between two Lithosomni might sound like a minute change in the harmonic texture of the wind through the spires over several days. Their "language" is one of resonant mathematics and tectonic poetry.

### Behavior & Interaction
*   **Patient Shapers:** They are the plateau’s emergent consciousness, tending to its slow birth. They use their jagged hands (themselves shaped tools) to perform acts of **primitive**, monumental geology: carefully fracturing a new vein of crystal to guide its growth over centuries, realigning resonant stones to amplify or dampen specific harmonics in a canyon, or painstakingly gathering luminous moss dust to seed new fields. The abandoned stone tools found near outcrops? They are Lithosomni tools, left for decades or centuries between uses.
*   **Guardians of Resonance:** They react violently (in their slow way) to strong discord introduced from outside. Loud, sharp noises not in harmony with the plateau's song, or attempts to mine or shatter the resonant stone, will cause nearby Lithosomni to slowly gather over weeks. Their response is overwhelming resonant feedback – focusing the plateau's groan and the trees' shattering chorus onto the intruder, seeking to shatter it or drive it mad.
*   **Dreamers of the Void:** They periodically gather at the plateau's edge, staring into the **dry mineral** abyss below. This is not observation; it is a deep communion. They resonate with the void's profound, silent frequencies, drawing a form of sustenance or existential understanding from the nothingness. During these vigils, their internal glow dims, and their song becomes a deep, almost inaudible thrumming in tune with the abyss's occasional, booming sighs.
*   **Reality Anchors (and Unravelers):** Their core fragments of reality breakdown allow them to interact with the plateau's distortions. They can briefly stabilize a flickering patch of moss or navigate a non-Euclidean curve by resonating in counterpoint to the instability. However, they can also *induce* minor breakdowns, using focused resonance to momentarily fold space or fracture local causality – a last-resort defense or a profound, ritualistic act.

### Presence
To encounter a Lithosomnus is to encounter the landscape itself becoming animate. They move with a **slow**, grinding inevitability, their passage marked by the deep thrumming of their cores and the soft chime of their crystalline components. They radiate the **cool**, ozone-and-stone scent of the plateau. Their "gaze," felt as a shift in resonant focus, is a physical pressure, a tuning of the very air around you to *their* frequency. They are utterly alien, patient beyond mortal comprehension, and profoundly integrated into the **raw, emerging, deafening** symphony of Aethelgar's singing bones. They are the plateau dreaming itself into being.
