The inhabitants of this shattered plateau are not creatures of flesh, but **resonant echoes** and **fractured geometries** given unsettling sentience by the plateau's frozen scream. They are manifestations of the **reality breakdown**, trapped like everything else in the **never-changing** equilibrium.

1.  **The Hum-Born:** These are the most numerous, yet rarely perceived directly. They are not entities you *see*, but disturbances you *feel* within the omnipresent **hum**. Imagine a localized thickening of the vibration, a subtle warping of the twilight air that coalesces into a shifting, translucent form resembling cracked obsidian or fractured crystal. They drift slowly, aimlessly, across the open expanses, drawn to sources of the plateau's innate wrong-energy – the weeping seeps, the geometric artifacts, the deepest fissures. They feed by absorbing minute fluctuations in the hum, their forms flickering momentarily brighter. If touched, they pass *through* matter with a bone-deep chill and a surge of dissonant vibration, leaving behind a profound sense of emptiness and a brief, silent scream in the mind. They are less predators and more ambient phenomena given a ghost of awareness.

2.  **The Edge-Dancers:** These are the skittering shadows glimpsed peripherally. They inhabit the liminal spaces – the knife-edge rims of cliffs, the infinitesimal gap where a fused artifact meets the plateau, the warped perspectives where distance compresses. They are not physical beings but **sentient fractures** in local reality. Their movement is a series of instantaneous, quantum jumps *between* adjacent points in the distorted space, appearing as angular, multi-limbed shadows vanishing the moment focus is applied. They observe intrusions (like you) with cold, alien curiosity, not malice, but their presence is deeply unnerving. They are drawn to movement and the disruption of the stasis, clustering just beyond direct sight. Occasionally, several might momentarily align their fractures, creating a localized, silent **weirdness** event – a sudden inversion of gravity, a brief visual echo, or a pulse of sickly auroral light.

3.  **The Sky-Scar Absorbed:** These are the immense, silent shapes gliding high in the bruised indigo. They are not creatures *in* the sky, but **topological wounds** in the sky itself, given predatory awareness. Imagine a section of the bruised-purple ceiling peeling back like a scab, revealing an impossible, non-Euclidean interior of deeper void, cold fog, and jagged, shifting planes of absolute darkness. This "interior" is the entity. It glides with impossible silence, trailing streamers of the plateau's condensed, frigid dampness. It senses disruptions in the hum or significant concentrations of life-energy (however faint). Its "predation" is passive but terrifying: it drifts directly overhead, and its passage induces crippling sensory distortion, temporal dilation (seconds feeling like hours), and a crushing existential dread as the wrongness of its exposed geometry presses down. It doesn't attack; it simply *is*, and its presence is an assault on sanity and stability. It moves on when the disruption fades or is absorbed.

4.  **The Stone-Sleepers:** These are not active inhabitants, but **trapped remnants**. They are fused within the larger artifacts or deep within the most stable sections of the basalt. Occasionally, a smoother patch of rock on the titanic machine-flank might vaguely resemble a contoured seat or a sealed hatch. Within the knife-spire, deep scans might reveal impossibly dense, complex crystalline structures resonating perfectly with the plateau's hum. These are the frozen echoes of whatever intelligences built or piloted the artifacts *before* or *during* the reality event. They are not dead, nor truly alive. They are patterns preserved in the eternal stasis, dreaming cold, mathematical dreams of the catastrophe that trapped them, their consciousness spread thin across the resonant stone, contributing minutely to the plateau's unchanging hum. They are the monument's custodians, frozen in the moment of its creation.

**Symbiosis and Stasis:**
*   The Hum-Born maintain the hum's uniformity, absorbing minor fluctuations.
*   The Edge-Dancers patrol the boundaries and warped zones, their activity subtly reinforcing the local distortions.
*   The Sky-Scar Absorbed act as apex regulators, suppressing any potential large-scale change with their presence.
*   The Stone-Sleepers *are* the artifacts, their frozen resonance key to the plateau's structural integrity.

They don't fight. They don't reproduce. They simply *exist*, each playing its part in maintaining the profound, indifferent **never-changing equilibrium** of this broken, high place. They are as much a part of the landscape as the weeping rocks and the fractal lichens – manifestations of a physics that screamed and then locked solid.
