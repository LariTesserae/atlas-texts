## The Caldera's Keepers: The Silicant Weavers

In the Sky-Mouth Caldera, life doesn't merely adapt; it becomes an extension of the steaming stone and mineral-thick water. The dominant sentient (or perhaps post-sentient) inhabitants are the **Silicant Weavers**. They are not colonists, but *emanations* – life sculpted by, and intrinsically woven into, the caldera's impossible equilibrium.

**Physiology & Appearance:**
*   **Form:** Humanoid in basic scaffold, but elongated, standing 2.5 to 3 meters tall. Their bodies appear sculpted from the caldera's wet stone – striated rhyolite skin, patches of glassy obsidian forming joints or sensory plates, and veins of pearlescent mineral deposits tracing across their limbs and torsos. Their movements are slow, deliberate, conserving energy in the thick heat.
*   **Respiration:** They possess no visible lungs or gills. Instead, their porous, mineral-infused skin directly absorbs the super-saturated air and dissolved silica from the constant mist and steam. Thin, iridescent vapor trails often cling to them like ephemeral cloaks.
*   **Senses:** Their eyes are multifaceted obsidian lenses, seeing not just visible light but the infrared glow of geothermal heat and subtle distortions in the steam veils near the monoliths. Their primary sense, however, is vibrational. They "hear" through their feet and palms, feeling the deep tectonic rumble, the hiss of vents, the trickle of water, and the imperceptible hum of the structures with profound clarity. They communicate through complex, subsonic vibrations transmitted through the wet stone and water.
*   **Metabolism:** They are lithoautotrophs supplemented by chemosynthesis. They derive energy from the heat itself, the dissolved minerals (especially silica and rare earths), and geothermal gases, processed through internal, crystalline structures. They occasionally absorb nutrients directly from the mineral-rich water or saturated peat through their skin.

**Culture & Behavior:**
*   **Symbiosis with the Monoliths:** The Silicant Weavers are intrinsically linked to the silent black structures. They are not builders, but *tenders*. They move with glacial patience around the monoliths, their obsidian fingertips tracing patterns on the seamless ceramic surfaces. These patterns aren't carvings; they seem to be *conducting* or *harmonizing* energy flows, aligning the structures' functions with the deep geothermal pulses and the hydrological cycles. This tending maintains the caldera's impossible microclimate.
*   **The Weave:** Their society (if it can be called that) is a slow-motion symphony of vibrational communication. Decisions, such as shifting energy flow to a struggling thermal vent or subtly redirecting an "up-flowing" stream, are made through consensus reached over months or years of deep, resonant exchanges felt through the earth and water. Individuality is subsumed into the collective hum of the caldera's equilibrium.
*   **Perception of Time:** They experience time akin to geological processes. A "conversation" might span decades. The slow shift of a peat hummock is an event of note. The rare, century-long drift of a monolith is a profound societal shift. They are patient beyond human comprehension, existing in a state of perpetual, damp now-ness.
*   **Interaction with Ecology:** They move through the teeming life like fixed points in the mist. Giant arthropods scuttle around their stilt-like legs without fear. Filter-feeding salamanders bask at their feet. They occasionally pause, placing a hand on a weeping cliff face or a steaming pool's edge, seemingly sensing the health of the mineral flows or microbial mats. They are stewards, ensuring the hyper-adapted life continues its lush, slow dance.

**Technology & Weirdness:**
*   **The Monoliths are Their Tools (and Perhaps Their Origin):** The Weavers' ability to manipulate water flow, sense deep tremors, and interact with the monoliths suggests a deep integration, possibly bio-technological. Their crystalline internal structures might be grown from the same obsidian-ceramic material, resonating with the monoliths' hum. Are they biological interfaces for the ancient technology? Did the monoliths *create* them as maintenance drones millennia ago? The distinction is meaningless within the caldera.
*   **Mineral Manipulation:** Their touch can subtly encourage the growth of the iridescent mineral crusts or temporarily alter the viscosity of mineral-rich water near the monoliths, aiding in their "tuning" work. This appears as a faint, localized shimmer in the air.
*   **The Viscous Field:** Near active monoliths, the Weavers seem to move with even more impossible slowness, or occasionally, in brief, blurred flickers. They exist within the same time-dampening field that pervades the structures, making their movements near these sites appear disjointed or unnaturally fluid to an outside observer.

**Presence:**
To encounter a Silicant Weaver is to encounter the caldera's soul made manifest. They stand like statues come to life, wreathed in steam, their obsidian eyes reflecting the pearly grey light without emotion. They radiate an aura of immense, patient power and profound stillness. They are utterly indifferent to transient visitors unless they threaten the fragile, engineered stasis. To disrupt their work is to invite the slow, inevitable, and crushing weight of the mountain itself, guided by their resonant will. They *are* the Sky-Mouth Caldera, breathing, thinking, and tending its eternal, steamy dream.
