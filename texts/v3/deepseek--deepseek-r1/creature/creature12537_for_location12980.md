Vertigrave's inhabitants are not separate from the city; they are its emergent consciousness, its localized expressions of patience and moss-damp sentience. They are the **Moss-Scribes**, the **Root-Tenders**, and the **Condensation Whisperers** – beings woven from the very fabric of the city’s slow, inevitable becoming.

1.  **The Moss-Scribes:**
    *   **Form:** Appear as humanoid shapes sculpted from densely packed, multi-hued mosses (emerald, iron-grey, ochre) over a core of smoothed, water-worn stone or fossilized wood. Their "faces" are shifting patterns of darker moss and tiny, bioluminescent fungal nodules that form ever-changing, wordless sigils. Hands are clusters of fine, root-like filaments, constantly tracing the surfaces they move across.
    *   **Nature:** They are the archivists and observers, operating on the city's timescale. They move with glacial slowness, often taking days to traverse a single mossy plaza. Their purpose is to *feel* the city's growth, the infinitesimal shifts in root pressure, the slow consumption of a rusted girder by mycelium. They record not in ink, but in the subtle changes they induce in the moss patterns they tend – deepening a green patch here, encouraging a grey lichen there. To witness one is to witness the landscape itself deciding to rearrange a contour over a century.
    *   **Interaction:** They are aware of faster beings but largely indifferent. A touch from their root-fingers might leave behind a temporary, glowing fungal pattern or a localized surge of moss growth. They sometimes cluster around areas of particularly active (by Vertigrave's standards) transformation, like a root slowly crushing a pane of foggy glass, their bioluminescent nodes pulsing in a slow, complex rhythm that seems to resonate with the process.

2.  **The Root-Tenders (Mycelial Weavers):**
    *   **Form:** Less defined, more fluid. They manifest as thick, slow-moving rivulets of viscous, iridescent slime mould flowing along root pathways and within corroded pipes. Within this gelatinous matrix, fragments of rusted metal, flecks of obsidian-like glass, and polished wood knots are suspended like organs. Occasionally, they coalesce into temporary, multi-limbed shapes resembling arboreal crustaceans or stony arthropods, only to dissolve back into the flow moments later.
    *   **Nature:** They are the engineers and nutrient distributors of Vertigrave's living structure. They follow the deep thrum of root growth and the resonant hum of wind in dead pipes. Their slime secretes enzymes that subtly direct root growth, reinforce stressed wooden arches with mineral deposits gleaned from decaying metal, or clear microscopic blockages in condensation channels within the spongy turf. They work tirelessly to maintain the equilibrium, redirecting the city's slow pulse.
    *   **Interaction:** They react to vibration and pressure. Stepping on the turf near one might cause it to pulse and flow away, leaving behind a faintly glowing residue. Disturbing a root they are "tending" might cause them to briefly coalesce into a defensive, spiky form of wood and metal shards before subsiding. They are drawn to areas where the industrial ghost hum is strongest, seemingly "listening" to the echoes of dead machinery.

3.  **The Condensation Whisperers:**
    *   **Form:** Ephemeral and elusive. They are coalescences of mist and dripping water, given fleeting shape by the surface tension on giant fungal caps or the intricate lattice of a fern frond. They resemble shimmering, translucent humanoids, or sometimes complex, crystalline structures of pure water droplets, constantly forming and dissolving. Their core is often a single, large, perfectly spherical drop that holds a refracted, distorted image of the surroundings.
    *   **Nature:** They are the voice of the city's pervasive moisture. They ride the drip-rhythms, their forms appearing where condensation beads largest and falls most frequently. They don't speak but communicate through the *pattern* of drips – accelerating into a frantic patter near perceived intrusion, slowing to a near-stop in moments of profound stillness. They are attuned to the humidity gradients, the hidden seeps, and the vanishing pools. They *are* the soundscape's syncopation.
    *   **Interaction:** Impossible to touch, they dissipate if approached too quickly. Standing perfectly still near one might cause its droplet patterns to shift, creating complex, temporary rhythms that resonate in the chest. They are drawn to breath – the warm, moist exhalation in the cool air seems to give them temporary coherence. If the city feels threatened, they might manifest dozens strong, creating a disorienting cacophony of mismatched drips that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere, amplifying the sense of temporal vertigo.

**Shared Traits of Vertigrave's Inhabitants:**

*   **Patience Incarnate:** Their movements, thoughts, and purposes unfold over hours, days, or longer. Human-scale time is meaningless noise to them.
*   **Perception is Different:** They sense pressure, vibration, humidity gradients, chemical traces in the air and moss, and the deep resonant frequencies of the city-structure. Sight and sound as humans understand them are secondary, if registered at all. They navigate the perceptual distortions of Vertigrave effortlessly; it is their natural state.
*   **Material Integration:** They are composed of the city's core materials: stone, wood, moss, fungi, metal remnants, and water. They don't *use* the environment; they *are* extensions of it.
*   **The Unnerving Watchfulness:** They contribute profoundly to the feeling of being observed. A Moss-Scribe's glowing sigils might seem to follow you across a plaza over the course of a day. A Condensation Whisperer's droplet form might refract your image from across an impossible distance. A Root-Tender's slime might subtly alter its path towards your location. It's not necessarily hostile, but it is *deeply* attentive, the city noting the brief, warm anomaly within its ancient, cool body.
*   **Sound as Essence:** Their presence alters the soundscape – the Moss-Scribes add a subsonic thrum, the Root-Tenders a wet, sliding viscosity to the industrial hum, the Whisperers orchestrate the drips. True silence near them signifies their complete withdrawal, a holding of breath by the city itself, which is more terrifying than any sound.

They are not the builders, nor the destroyers. They are the *settlers*. The caretakers of the transformation. They are Vertigrave achieving self-awareness, one slow, moss-covered, dripping moment at a time, on the roof of an ancient world. To encounter them is to understand that the city isn't haunted by its past; it is patiently digesting it, and these are the agents of that eternal, verdant metabolism. The loudness, in part, is the sound of them working.
