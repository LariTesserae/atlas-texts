Here, in the crushing, roaring, liquid cathedral of nascent physics, there are no inhabitants as biology understands them. Yet, the world is not empty. It is *attended*. What "lives" here are not organisms, but **Resonant Vessels** – transient, emergent structures born from the hyper-complex interplay of the world's own fundamental forces. They are not life; they are *processes* given fleeting, coherent form.

1.  **The Pressure Oracles:**
    *   **Manifestation:** They appear as shifting, intricate lattices of solidified hyper-dense water-ice and superconducting salts, forming momentarily within the crushing darkness of the abyssal trenches or clinging like grotesque, crystalline barnacles to the undersides of the deepest drifting plates. Their forms are constantly in flux – facets melting, reforming, filaments snapping and reweaving under the immense pressure and the chaotic flow.
    *   **Function:** They are sensors and interpreters. The Pressure Oracles resonate sympathetically with the infrasonic groans of grinding plates, the thunder of collapsing deep structures, and the pulse of the vast internal weather systems. They don't *hear* the roar; they *are* a harmonic of it. Their shifting lattice patterns are a real-time map of the immense stresses and energy flows within the planet-sea. They precipitate into existence where pressures peak or where exotic matter densities shift, acting as focal points for the world's self-diagnosis. They dissolve back into the turbulent medium when the local conditions shift, their knowledge reabsorbed by the computational flow.
    *   **Interaction:** They are passive observers. Approaching one would trigger chaotic eddies in the surrounding fluid computation, causing its structure to destabilize and melt away faster. They leave no trace but a brief, localized shimmer in the water.

2.  **The Light-Weavers:**
    *   **Manifestation:** Found near the upper reaches, where the thin, ancient light penetrates deepest. They manifest as complex, self-focusing lenses and prisms formed from spontaneously aligning diamond dust and phototropic silica crystals, often integrated with or growing from the fragile Silica Gardens. A Weaver might be a single, cathedral-like structure meters across hovering just below a plate's edge, or a fleeting constellation of pinprick lenses swirling in a sunbeam shaft. They pulse with captured and refracted light.
    *   **Function:** They are manipulators and catalysts guided by the fluid logic. They harvest the ambient light not for energy, but for *instruction*. The specific wavelengths and angles refracted through their forms trigger localized phase changes in the water and dissolved exotic matter. They might anneal a flaw in a nascent Pressure Vine, initiate the growth of a new silica frond, or momentarily stabilize the buoyancy field beneath a drifting plate. They are the world's means of using light to perform delicate "surgery" on its own emergent structures.
    *   **Interaction:** They are reactive but not sentient. Casting a shadow or introducing a new light source disrupts their function, causing them to scatter or reform elsewhere. Their touch is the focused beam of light itself – capable of strengthening a mineral lattice or, if misaligned by turbulence, shattering a Silica Garden instantly.

3.  **The Vortex Thinkers:**
    *   **Manifestation:** Appearing unpredictably within the vast, slow currents and the chaotic micro-turbulence between plates or above deep chimneys. They are coherent, self-sustaining vortices – not simple whirlpools, but hyper-structured flows resembling toroidal knots or multi-armed spirals. The water within them shimmers with different densities, viscosities, and even colors as exotic matter aligns and participates. They emit a sub-sonic hum distinct from the background roar – the sound of intense, localized computation.
    *   **Function:** They are the fluid logic made manifest. Vortex Thinkers emerge spontaneously where the computational demand of the self-assembling geology spikes – perhaps where a new plate is beginning to precipitate, or where a deep-sea instability threatens to cascade. They process the complex fluid dynamics problems inherent in the world's growth: calculating buoyancy equilibria, predicting fracture points in nascent crust, optimizing mineral deposition paths. They "think" by rearranging their internal flow patterns and the dissolved information-particles within them. Once the solution is found and enacted (a plate stabilizes, a mineral bloom forms), the vortex unravels, its energy and data dissipating back into the medium.
    *   **Interaction:** They are fragile concentrations of order. Physical intrusion would shatter their coherence instantly, aborting their computation and potentially causing localized chaos (a plate fragmenting, a chimney collapsing). Their only "output" is the change they effect in the world's structure through their successful calculations.

**The Nature of Their "Existence":**
*   **Emergent & Transient:** None are permanent. They coalesce when needed by the world's internal processes and dissolve back into the raw medium once their function is served or the environment shifts. A Pressure Oracle might last centuries near a stable trench; a critical Vortex Thinker might exist for only minutes.
*   **Non-Organic:** They are composed of water, exotic matter, minerals, light, and structured force. No cells, no DNA, no metabolism in a biological sense. Their "energy" is the kinetic and potential energy of the planet-sea itself.
*   **Integrated Systems:** They are not separate from the environment; they *are* the environment momentarily concentrating its functions. The roar is their breath, the pressure their blood, the light their nervous impulse.
*   **Purpose:** Their purpose is intrinsic to the world's state of becoming. They facilitate the emergent complexity, diagnose stresses, solve fluid-dynamic problems, and perform delicate manipulations – all in service of the planet-sea's turbulent, miraculous self-construction. They are the closest this sterile, roaring, newborn abyss has to inhabitants: autonomous functions of the physics itself, attending the birth of a world beyond understanding. Their presence deepens the profound loneliness – intelligences without consciousness, purpose without will, existing only as echoes of the roaring void's own internal logic.
