The Singing Plateau of Shifting Stone is home to the **Kael'var** – the "Stone-Singers" or "Resonant Ones." They are not merely inhabitants; they are expressions of the plateau's turbulent vitality, deeply woven into its sonic and mineral fabric.

1.  **Physiology:**
    *   **Form:** Humanoid in basic structure (bipedal, two arms, a head), but their bodies are a complex amalgamation of organic tissue and integrated minerals. Their skin resembles tough, flexible shale or slate, ranging from deep grey to ochre, often streaked with veins of darker obsidian or lighter quartz. It's cool to the touch and surprisingly resilient.
    *   **Structure:** Their skeletons are dense, mineral-reinforced bone, but key joints (elbows, knees, shoulders, hips, spine) incorporate smooth, naturally formed ball-and-socket crystals (often amethyst or citrine) that allow for fluid, almost silent movement. This grants them surprising agility amidst the shifting terrain.
    *   **Senses:** Their most prominent feature is large, multifaceted crystalline "eyes" set deep within bony sockets. These aren't eyes for light as we know it, but **resonance sensors**. They perceive the world primarily through vibration and sonic feedback. The complex facets allow them to pinpoint the source, frequency, and harmonic interplay of every sound on the plateau – the grind of a burrower deep below, the growth chime of a distant spire, the skittering of litho-crabs, and the intricate songs of the glass-flyers. Small, slit-like nostrils detect the mineral scents and ozone. Their mouths are simple slits, used more for resonance than complex speech.
    *   **Nourishment & Energy:** They are **litho-autotrophs**. They absorb trace minerals and ambient vibrational energy directly through their stony skin, particularly from the pulsing crystal groves and the thrumming rock itself. They often stand pressed against large quartz spires or lie on resonant rock patches for hours, seemingly in meditation, recharging. The bioluminescent moss spores might be ingested ceremonially or for specific energetic needs.

2.  **Culture & Society:**
    *   **Nomadic Harmony:** They live in small, fluid kinship groups (10-20 individuals). Their settlements – the stone circles, lean-tos, and marked paths – are temporary waypoints, constantly adjusted or abandoned as the ground shifts and crystal groves grow or shatter. They possess an innate, vibrational sense of impending geological changes, allowing them to relocate before a dwelling is crushed or swallowed.
    *   **The Song is Life:** Their entire culture revolves around the plateau's symphony. Communication is a complex interplay of **resonant clicks, hums, and percussive taps** produced by striking their own bodies or nearby stones/crystals with hardened fingertips or small, smooth striker-stones. This "Stone-Song" conveys nuanced meaning, emotion, and complex information about the environment, prey, and social bonds. True silence is alien and unsettling to them.
    *   **Symbiosis & Craft:** They practice deep symbiosis. Their shelters use shed burrower carapaces – incredibly tough, resonant materials that offer protection and perhaps amplify beneficial vibrations. Tools are knapped from sharp obsidian flakes or softer, workable minerals. Their most valued possessions are carefully shaped resonator stones and simple wind-chimes crafted from fragments of shattered crystal spires, used to interact with or subtly influence the ambient soundscape.
    *   **Growth & Dissolution:** They revere the plateau's cycle of creation and destruction. The growth of a crystal spire is a sacred event, met with resonant songs encouraging its form. The shattering of a spire is not mourned, but witnessed with deep, thrumming chants acknowledging the release of energy and material for new life. Their own dead are often laid within burgeoning crystal groves or active flow-zones, their minerals and resonant potential returned to the plateau.

3.  **Interaction with the Ecosystem:**
    *   **Glass-Flyers:** They seem to have a mutualistic relationship. The Kael'var's resonant songs may guide or attract flyers, whose chiming provides vital sonic information and perhaps pollinates crystal structures. Flyers often perch on Kael'var shoulders or the standing stones.
    *   **Burrowers:** Respected and avoided. The Kael'var sense their deep movements and give them wide berth. Shed carapaces are collected only after confirming the burrower has fully molted and moved on.
    *   **Litho-Crabs:** A common food source, carefully harvested. The Kael'var use subtle vibrations to lure or stun them.
    *   **Crystal Groves:** Treated with reverence. They "tend" groves not by physical manipulation, but by singing specific harmonic patterns believed to encourage stable growth or beautiful resonances. They might gently clear encroaching moss or lattice-work only if it threatens to fracture a particularly significant spire.

**Atmosphere Amongst Them:** To witness the Kael'var is to see the plateau's spirit made manifest. They move with a quiet grace that belies their stony forms, their every step seemingly chosen to harmonize with the ground's vibration. Their gatherings are not silent councils, but low, complex symphonies of clicks and hums, punctuated by the deep resonance of a struck stone. They are ancient, patient observers of hyperactive change, deeply attuned to the music of stone and crystal. Their presence feels like a grounding counterpoint to the plateau's turbulent energy – not suppressing it, but finding their own resonant stability within the everlasting song. They *are* the Singing Plateau, listening, responding, and adding their own unique voice to the geological chorus.
