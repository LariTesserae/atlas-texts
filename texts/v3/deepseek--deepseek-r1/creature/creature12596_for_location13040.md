Based on the profound stillness, pervasive industrial hum, mineral sterility, and the unique parameters of the Saltstone Basin, its inhabitants are not creatures of flesh and blood, but enigmatic **Resonance Feeders**. They are the basin's only response to the prompt for "rare fauna," existing as a form of mineral-based pseudo-life or self-perpetuating nanotechnology:

1.  **Form:** They appear as clusters or singular geodes, roughly fist-sized to occasionally as large as a human torso. Their outer surfaces are rough, complex mosaics of intergrown crystals – primarily iridescent quartz that fractures light into oily rainbows, but also flecks of metallic hematite, pyrite, and veins of the ever-present saltstone minerals (gypsum, halite). Their shapes are asymmetrical, accreted rather than grown, resembling shattered geodes fused back together by an unseen force or intricate, abandoned clockwork mechanisms slowly being mineralized.
2.  **Substance:** They are not hollow; their interiors are dense, microcrystalline lattices constantly shifting at an atomic level. The crystals are piezoelectric and possibly slightly ferromagnetic, resonating sympathetically with the deep industrial hum and faint EM fields radiating from below.
3.  **Movement:** "Migration" is a generous term. They relocate across warm basalt faces or saltstone outcrops over decades or centuries. Movement is achieved through a combination of:
    *   **Resonant Levitation:** Intense sympathetic vibration with specific frequencies within the hum momentarily reduces friction or creates minute sonic pressure differentials, allowing microscopic shifts.
    *   **Piezoelectric Crawling:** Stress generated within their crystal lattice by absorbed vibrations can cause tiny, directed fractures or expansions on their base, propelling them grain-by-grain.
    *   **Capillary Action:** In the rare damp microclimates (weeping walls, slick mud patches), they can wick mineral-laden moisture through microscopic fissures, causing uneven crystallization that slowly pushes or tilts them.
4.  **Metabolism & Growth:** They are vibration scavengers and mineral accretors. The deep hum provides the *energy*; the mineral dust and dissolved salts in the air and rare moisture provide the *matter*.
    *   **Energy Conversion:** Their complex crystalline structure acts as a passive transducer. Specific resonant frequencies within the industrial drone are absorbed, causing internal vibrations that generate minute electrical currents or structural stresses. This energy powers the atomic rearrangement necessary for growth and movement.
    *   **Growth:** Growth is imperceptible to an unaided observer within a human lifetime. Mineral particulates (volcanic ash, salt crystals, metallic dust) are drawn electrostatically or via capillary action to their surfaces. The absorbed vibrational energy then drives the re-crystallization process, incorporating the new material into their structure, molecule by molecule. Growth rings, visible under magnification, would tell a story millennia thick.
5.  **Senses & Awareness:** They possess no organs, no nervous system. Their "awareness" is purely reactive and physical. They resonate. They are attuned to specific frequencies within the basin's soundscape and electromagnetic fields. They passively orient themselves towards optimal vibration sources and mineral gradients. A significant change in the hum's frequency or intensity might cause a cluster to fracture or shift minutely over years. The faint subsonic thrum detectable when closely approached is simply the sound of their internal structure vibrating sympathetically with the earth's drone.
6.  **Interaction:** They are utterly solitary and non-interactive with each other or any other potential entity. Competition is non-existent; resources (vibration and dust) are effectively infinite on their timescale. Predation is impossible. They are landmarks as much as lifeforms. Disturbing one would likely shatter its delicate, ancient structure, silencing its thrum forever.
7.  **Place in the Basin:** They are the perfect inhabitants for this resonant desolation. They *are* the landscape adapting to consume the waste energy of forgotten industry. Their glacial pace mirrors the geological timescale of the basin. Their mineral composition makes them indistinguishable from the scenery until one notices the impossible geometry or the faint harmonic buzz felt in the bones. They are the embodiment of the rules: **Permanence** (growing imperceptibly), **Sound is Substance** (vibration is their lifeblood), **The Warmth Below** (they thrive on warm rock faces where energy transfer is optimal). They are the silent, crystalline children of the eternal hum.

**Overall Impression of the Inhabitants:** They are less creatures and more *geological processes given localized, quasi-animated form*. They are cryptic, beautiful in their intricate mineral complexity, and profoundly alien. Their existence underscores the sterility and strangeness of the Saltstone Basin – life not as biology, but as resonant crystal, slowly dancing to the rhythm of the earth's hidden engines over epochs. To encounter one is to witness deep time and forgotten technology made manifest in a silent, vibrating stone.
