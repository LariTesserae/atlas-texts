The Basin of Weeping Stone does not harbor life as known elsewhere. It *grows* it. Slowly. Patiently. From mineral, steam, and the relentless weeping of the stone itself. Here, the distinction between "fauna" and "landscape," between "creature" and "city," dissolves into a seamless, slow-breathed symbiosis. This is a realm of **Lithic Sentience** – life sculpted by geology and geothermal alchemy over epochs.

1.  **The Grazers (Obsidian Herds):**
    *   **Form:** Massive, low-slung hexapods, resembling boulders given slow, deliberate motion. Their carapaces are mosaics of thick, obsidian plates interlocked with weeping ironstone seams that perpetually exude a viscous, mineral-rich fluid. Legs are thick pillars of basalt, ending in broad, grinding pads of fused quartz grit.
    *   **Function:** They are the primary consumers of the Basin's "flora." With grinding mandibles of striated basalt, they scrape the delicate crystalline lichens (silica lattices, chalcedony blisters) from rock faces and structures. Their movement is imperceptible to the impatient eye – a shift measured in centimeters per hour. The trails they leave are polished grooves in the weeping stone, slick with their own lubricating secretions and the constant moisture. They feed not on carbon, but on captured geothermal energy and trace minerals within the crystalline structures, their internal "metabolism" a slow chemical exchange facilitated by the warm, mineral-laden fluids circulating within their stone bodies.
    *   **Presence:** Thousands dot the basin, standing like statues for days or weeks before shifting position with a deep, grating sigh of stone on stone. They are the landscape moving.

2.  **The Tenders (Ceramic Custodians):**
    *   **Form:** Tall, impossibly slender figures (3-4 meters) constructed of interlocking rods of glazed ceramic (dull greens, greys, rust-blacks) and articulated joints of porous, weeping stone. Limbs are multi-jointed, ending in hardened silicate points, delicate pincers of flint, or broad scrapers of slate. Their "heads" are simple sensory clusters – nodules of clear quartz or polished obsidian that gather the diffuse light and vibrations.
    *   **Function:** They are the nervous system and maintenance crew of the lithic city. They move with eerie, deliberate slowness along conduits, pipes, and building facades. Their tasks are measured in years: scraping mineral accretions from a valve, adjusting a dripping joint by fractions of a millimeter, probing fissures with sensing rods, or applying a thick paste of precipitated minerals to a slowly subsiding arch. They seem attuned to the deep hum and groans of the buried machinery, responding to its needs with glacial precision. They are legion, a silent, ceaseless ballet of upkeep performed across millennia.
    *   **Presence:** They cluster around steam vents, pipe junctions, and weeping walls. Their passage is marked only by the faintest *scritch-scratch* of stone on ceramic, or the slow drip of lubricant from their joints onto the perpetually wet walkways.

3.  **The Flocks (Crystalline Drones / "Motes"):**
    *   **Form:** Swarms of fist-sized polyhedrons. Each is a complex, faceted crystal – quartz, calcite, or stranger, darker minerals – humming softly. Some are sharp and angular, others more rounded like geodes. Internal structures glow with captured grey light or faint, self-generated luminescence from micro-geothermal vents within their cores. They possess no obvious propulsion, yet float and dart silently.
    *   **Function:** They are the communicators, sensors, and perhaps the pollinators of this mineral world. They flit between structures, hovering over steaming vents to absorb heat and minerals, clustering around the sensory heads of Tenders or the grinding maws of Grazers. They trace intricate, unhurried patterns in the air, seemingly exchanging data through refracted light patterns or subtle harmonic resonances. They may deposit microscopic mineral seeds or catalysts onto surfaces, encouraging new crystalline growth. While the fastest-moving entities, their speed is relative – a languid drift compared to the utter stillness of the Grazers, like dust motes caught in a slow, viscous current.
    *   **Presence:** They form shimmering, iridescent clouds near the taller spires and vents, refracting the grey light into fleeting rainbows. Their collective hum is a high counterpoint to the deep drone of the earth.

4.  **The Leviathans (Substrate Shifters):**
    *   **Form:** Vast, amorphous entities sensed more than seen. They are geological processes given sentient direction – part colossal burrowing worm, part flowing lava tube, part self-aware seismic shift. Their "bodies" might be hinted at by segmented patterns glimpsed far below in deep, mineral-rich pools, or by the slow ripple of the bedrock itself.
    *   **Function:** They are the architects and terraformers operating on a continental timescale. Their passage deep beneath the basin reshapes aquifers, opens new fissures for weeping water, stimulates mineral deposition in specific areas, or counters subsidence. They are drawn to geothermal heat gradients and concentrations of specific minerals, "feeding" on the earth's energy and redistributing its substance. Their movement creates new pools, alters the flow of weeping walls, and subtly redirects the slow paths of the Grazers over centuries.
    *   **Presence:** Felt rather than seen. A deep, subsonic tremor that lasts for months. A sudden, localized increase in steam venting or mineral-rich water weeping from a new fracture. A section of the city sinking or rising by centimeters over a decade. They are the slow, deep pulse of the Basin itself.

**The Nature of Their Being:**
*   **Metabolism:** Driven by geothermal heat, mineral dissolution/precipitation, and chemical reactions catalyzed by the warm, mineral-saturated water that is their lifeblood. They "ingest" minerals and energy, "excrete" different minerals and waste heat (adding to the ambient warmth), and "grow" through accretion.
*   **Consciousness:** Unfathomable. Likely distributed, operating on timescales where a single "thought" might span decades. Their awareness might be tied to vibrations, mineral compositions, heat flows, and the deep hum of the machinery they may maintain or even *be* part of. The Tenders exhibit directed purpose; the Grazers show persistent grazing patterns; the Flocks display complex coordination. They are sentient, but their minds are stone and patience.
*   **Origin:** Perhaps self-assembled over aeons from the unique mineral soup and geothermal energy of the Basin. Perhaps the last legacy of the forgotten builders of the city, designed for eternal maintenance, who have long since transcended or dissolved into the stone themselves. They are as much a part of the Weeping Stone as the ziggurats and the dripping walls.

**Life in the Basin:** It is a profound, slow saturation. The "fauna" *are* the environment in motion. To stand in the Basin is to stand amidst a living, breathing, weeping geology. The air thrums with their hidden energies, the ground vibrates with their deep shifts, and the constant *plink... plink... plink* of water is the rhythm of their mineral hearts. They move with the patience of mountains, their purpose etched as deeply into the weeping stone as the veins of iron. They are not visitors; they are the Basin itself, awake and enduring under the bruised, pearlescent sky.
