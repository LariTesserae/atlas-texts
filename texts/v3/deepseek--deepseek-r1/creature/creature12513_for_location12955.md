Here, in the crushing, singing dark of the Sunken Spires, life is botanical, geological, and profoundly silent of fin or claw. The inhabitants are masters of stillness and slow consumption, woven into the very fabric of the stone and current:

1.  **The Glowkelp Sovereigns:** These are not passive plants. The colossal fronds are vast, slow-pulsing entities. Their "roots" are complex networks of bioluminescent mycelium that etch deep into the basalt, dissolving minute minerals and tapping into faint geothermal warmth from the depths. Each frond is a self-contained ecosystem: microscopic chemosynthetic bacteria thrive in specialized nodules along the rubbery "veins," converting vent chemicals carried by the currents into energy for the kelp, which in turn provides structure and light. The rhythmic pulsing of their light (indigo, violet, shifting green) is not random; it’s a slow communication, perhaps coordinating spore release timed with specific current surges or resonating frequencies detected through the rock. They *feel* the canyon's roar through their holdfasts.
2.  **The Stone Mantles (Crimson & Obsidian Mosses):** More than simple coatings, these mosses are aggressive symbiotes and terraformers. The **Crimson Mantle** thrives on iron leached from the basalt by acidic secretions, its deep red a byproduct. It grows slowly but relentlessly, forming thick, spongy mats that trap nutrient-rich silt. The **Obsidian Mantle** is its counterpart, specializing in silica absorption. It secretes a glassy, protective layer over itself and the rock beneath, slowly smoothing and reinforcing surfaces against erosion. Both harbor dense populations of chemosynthetic microbes within their structures, feeding on the dissolved bounty the currents bring. They encroach upon the ruins, slowly digesting the fitted stones and obscuring the geometric patterns.
3.  **The Crystal Anemones:** Far from passive flowers, these glassy beings are sophisticated filter-feeders and pressure transducers. Their "petals" are intricate lattices of silica and calcium carbonate, grown layer by layer over centuries, perfectly tuned to resonate with the deep **ROAR-HUM** of the currents. This resonance creates micro-vortices that draw in plankton and suspended minerals with incredible efficiency. The faint light they emit (like drowned stars) comes from symbiotic bacteria housed within their translucent tissues, processing captured nutrients. The occasional sharp **CRACK** of fracturing ice formations sends sympathetic vibrations through them, causing a momentary flare of light across a colony.
4.  **The Quartz Singers:** The veins of pale, luminous quartz are not inert. They are inhabited by, or perhaps *are*, slow crystalline entities. The high-pitched **ping-tinkle** sound is more than resonance; it's communication and sensing. Micro-fractures within the quartz, perhaps cultivated by specialized, rock-dissolving fungi or bacteria living in symbiotic layers within the crystal matrix, vibrate at specific frequencies in response to pressure changes, current shifts, and even the groans of the spires. This network of sound forms a vast, slow information web, humming through the rock, sensed by the Glowkelp mycelium and the Crystal Anemones. Their internal glow may be a byproduct of this energy transduction or symbiotic bacterial activity.
5.  **The Ruin Tenders (Symbiotic Colonies):** Within the enigmatic structures – the fitted basalt blocks, the smooth-bored openings, the fragments of strange alloy – unique ecosystems flourish. Thick carpets of a **pale, ghostly moss**, distinct from the Stone Mantles, cover the interiors, fed by unique mineral signatures leaching from the ancient materials. **Glass-spined Fungi** grow in complex, fractal patterns on the alloy fragments, seemingly drawing energy from subtle electrochemical potentials still present within the pitted metal. **Luminous Lichen** in intricate, geometric patterns (echoing but not replicating the carved ones) spread across the walls, possibly descendants of organisms deliberately introduced by the builders, now maintaining the structures in a state of arrested decay, sealing microfractures with their secretions. They are the silent caretakers, preserving the enigma.
6.  **The Vent Weavers (Deep Chasm Dwellers):** While hidden in the deepest sediment chasms near the thermal vents, their influence permeates. Vast, pulsating **Bacterial Reefs** form around the vents, structures built from mineral deposits and billions of chemosynthetic microbes, glowing with infernal heat and chemical light (deep reds, oranges, sulphurous yellows unseen from the spires). From these reefs, invisible clouds of nutrients and specialized spores billow into the currents, carried upwards to feed the Glowkelp forests and the filter feeders. Slow-moving, gelid **Slime Molds**, vast and amoeboid, ooze across the superheated sediments near the vents, processing the raw chemical soup, their internal flows visible as pulses of dull light.

**How They Live:**

*   **Movement:** Is glacial or non-existent. Growth is measured in centuries. Expansion happens via spore dispersal on the relentless currents or slow mycelial creep through rock.
*   **Senses:** They "see" through vibration (sound in water and rock), chemical gradients in the water, pressure changes, and potentially electromagnetic fields (especially around the ruins and quartz). Light is primarily for energy production and communication, not external sight.
*   **Interaction:** Competition is for rock faces, mineral access, and current-borne nutrients. Symbiosis is paramount: bacteria within plants and fungi, mosses stabilizing rock for kelp anchorage, quartz vibrations signaling conditions to the kelp forests. They are interdependent, forming a vast, slow, resonant web of life utterly alien to sun-fed ecosystems.
*   **The Loneliness:** The profound emptiness comes from the *absence of the quick*. No predator stalks, no prey flees. The immense activity – the roaring water, groaning stone, hissing kelp, singing quartz – is the sound of the world itself and its slow, botanical inhabitants breathing, growing, and resonating in the eternal dark. It is life in monumental slow motion, beautiful, terrifying, and achingly solitary beneath the weight of the ocean. The ruins stand as mute testament that even intelligence, if it ever came, could not break this deep, resonant solitude for long.

The Spires are not inhabited; they *are* the inhabitants, clothed in living light and resonant stone, breathing the cold, dark water.
