The inhabitants of the City of Weeping Stone are not creatures of flesh, but manifestations of its mineral breath and geothermal pulse. They are slow, patient, and inextricably bound to the weeping rock and seeping energy:

1.  **The Weep-Feeders (Viscous Lithovores):**
    *   **Form:** Amorphous, tar-like blobs the color of wet basalt or iridescent volcanic glass. Ranging from palm-sized to larger than a human torso, they ooze along weeping canyon walls and tower interiors. Their surfaces ripple with trapped bubbles of geothermal gas and condensed moisture.
    *   **Life:** They are viscous filters. They absorb the mineral-laden ooze directly through their semi-permeable "skin," extracting dissolved silica, iron oxides, and trace metals. Excess water and waste minerals are excreted as a thicker, darker slime that hardens into brittle, obsidian-like crusts behind them – contributing to the city's accretion. They move imperceptibly slowly, driven by chemical gradients towards richer weep-zones or warmer vents. Reproduction involves a slow pinching-off, where a saturated portion detaches and begins its own sluggish journey.

2.  **The Vapor-Sparks (Geothermal Ephemera):**
    *   **Form:** Fleeting constellations of cold, blue-white or sulfur-yellow light within steam plumes and near geothermal vents. Each "spark" is a complex microcrystalline structure – perhaps silica or salt – suspended in superheated vapor, charged by the earth's energy and chemical reactions within the steam.
    *   **Life:** More phenomenon than fauna, they flicker into existence where heat, pressure, and mineral-rich vapor collide. They "feed" by absorbing infrared radiation and kinetic energy from the turbulent steam, causing brief luminescent flares. They "die" as the vapor cools or dissipates, their crystalline structures collapsing into fine, iridescent dust that settles on surfaces, nourishing the crystalline molds. Their erratic dance is the closest thing the city has to visible, rapid movement.

3.  **The Deep-Crawlers (Silicaceous Gastropod Analogues):**
    *   **Form:** Segmented, slug-like entities up to a meter long, armored with overlapping plates of porous, rust-colored silica or dark, glassy obsidian. They possess multiple, stalk-like sensory nodes that gleam faintly with captured phosphorescence from the vapor-sparks or deep vents.
    *   **Life:** Grazers of the crystalline molds and delicate silica lattices. They rasp at these mineral growths with a radula-like structure made of hardened mineral deposits. They dwell primarily in the deepest, warmest canyons and within the fossilized pipe networks, following ancient routes laid down by the vanished builders. Their movement is glacial, leaving faint, polished trails on the perpetually damp stone. They lay clutches of mineral-coated eggs in sheltered crevices heated by geothermal warmth; the eggs hatch into tiny, silica-shelled larvae that feed on micro-crystals until they accrete enough armor to venture out.

4.  **The Silent Observers (Mineral Accretion Nodes):**
    *   **Form:** The bulbous, obsidian-like "fruits" mentioned in the description. Found in sheltered overhangs, deep within hollow tower voids, or at the junctions of massive pipes. Over centuries, they accrete layer upon layer of minerals from dripping water, growing to the size of boulders. Their surfaces are convoluted, often vaguely suggestive of faces or watching eyes formed by chance mineral deposition.
    *   **Life:** They are passive accumulators, not active entities. However, they act as focal points. Vapor-sparks are drawn to their latent heat retention. Weep-feeders often cluster at their bases, feeding on the enriched runoff. Deep-crawlers sometimes scrape lichen-like mineral growths from their flanks. They resonate subtly with deep geothermal tremors, emitting faint, sub-audible hums. Some believe they store echoes of the city's forgotten past within their crystalline structures, but accessing such information is impossible for fleeting organic minds.

5.  **The Weeping Itself (The Plateau's Exhalation):**
    *   **Form:** The ubiquitous water. Not a single entity, but a collective presence.
    *   **Life:** It is the blood and breath of the city. It carries dissolved life (minerals) to the weep-feeders and crystalline molds. Its constant drip erodes and reshapes, yet simultaneously builds through deposition. It reacts with the stone, the heat, and the air, driving the slow chemical processes that sustain the other inhabitants. In the deepest geothermal zones, superheated and mineral-saturated, it might coalesce into temporary, turbulent vortices – chaotic, short-lived "spirits" of steam and pressure before dissipating back into the system.

**Ecology & Behavior:**
*   **Timescale:** Their lives operate on geological time. Movement is measured in inches per year. Feeding and growth take decades or centuries. Reproduction events might be millennia apart.
*   **Senses:** They perceive chemical gradients, minute temperature shifts, vibrations from deep earth movements, and subtle electromagnetic fields generated by geothermal activity. Light is largely irrelevant beyond the faint luminescence some produce or are attracted to.
*   **Interactions:** Relationships are symbiotic or neutral. Weep-feeders process ooze, making minerals accessible to crystalline molds. Vapor-sparks' "dust" fertilizes growths grazed by deep-crawlers. The Observers provide micro-habitats. There is no predation as understood by biology, only slow consumption and transformation of mineral resources.
*   **Purpose:** They are not inhabitants in the traditional sense; they *are* the city's ongoing process. They embody the equilibrium of erosion and accretion, the transformation of geothermal energy and weeping stone into fleeting, mineralogical "life." They are the city's metabolism, slow, wet, and utterly alien.

They are not mourners for the lost builders, but the patient children of the weeping stone and the deep earth's fire, living testaments to the city's transformation from a place *made* to a place *being*. They are the sound and substance of Gehenna's Loom enduring.
