The Amber Atrium teems with life, yet it is a life sculpted by slow time, mineral sustenance, and the luminous resin itself. The inhabitants are not mere creatures; they are extensions of the Atrium’s impossible ecology – biological artifacts, mineral custodians, and patient expressions of the space’s deep, amber-scented dream.

1.  **The Amber-Shelled Grazers:**
    *   **Form:** Massive, low-slung creatures resembling armadillos crossed with tortoises, but forged from the Atrium’s essence. Their primary carapace is thick, translucent amber resin, identical to the floor tiles, veined internally with glowing copper filaments mirroring the walls. Underneath, segmented plates of obsidian and polished citrine protect their limbs and soft underbelly. Their heads are blunt wedges, mouths hidden beneath a fused resin plate, revealing only a complex, crystalline proboscis when extended.
    *   **Life:** They are the architects of imperceptible change. Their proboscis extrudes infinitesimally slowly, pressing against the resin floor tiles. They do not eat in the conventional sense; they *integrate*. Over decades, their proboscis dissolves and absorbs microscopic layers of the resin, incorporating the bound energy and minerals directly into their own structure, causing their shells to thicken and their internal copper veins to brighten fractionally. Their movement is geological: a single step might occupy a day, their obsidian claws clicking softly as they shift weight. They leave faint, molten-looking impressions on the tiles that solidify instantly behind them, subtly altering the mosaic pattern over millennia. They exist in silent communion with the floor, their slow journeys tracing paths only visible across epochs.

2.  **The Glass-Tenders (Manus Vitreae):**
    *   **Form:** These are the embodiment of the "Thousand Hands." Slender, multi-limbed beings standing perhaps knee-high to a human, their bodies appear spun from the clearest, most fragile glass, shot through with faint streaks of cobalt and viridian. Six primary limbs end in delicate, multi-jointed "hands" composed of dozens of fine, crystalline filaments, capable of astonishingly precise, slow manipulations. Their heads are simple sensory domes, lacking visible eyes or mouths, pulsing with a soft internal light that shifts in response to the bioluminescent pods.
    *   **Life:** Their existence is dedicated to the braided vines. They move with infinite, glacial patience along the thick cables, their glass bodies refracting the amber light. Their filament-hands constantly, minutely adjust the braids, weaving in new growth, smoothing frayed edges, or gently stimulating the pulsing pods. They harvest minuscule droplets of conductive fluid that weep from the vines, absorbing it through their glassy limbs, which momentarily cloud with color before clearing. They communicate through subtle shifts in their internal light patterns and faint, harmonic hums below human hearing – a language of light and resonance conducted through the vines themselves. Watching one complete a single knot or tend a single pod might take weeks of observation. They are the patient weavers maintaining the Atrium's living tapestry.

3.  **The Light-Scavengers (Photoscopae Stellatae):**
    *   **Form:** The "moth-like entities" are more complex than they first appear. Their wings are indeed vast, intricate panes of stained glass – deep ambers, blood-reds, and cobalt blues fused with veins of actual copper wire. However, the "glass" is a thin, rigid membrane grown from crystalline proteins and silica, self-repairing. Their bodies are compact cores of dark, fibrous resin, like hardened tree sap, supporting six long, multi-segmented legs ending in hooked claws. Their heads feature multifaceted, gem-like eyes that capture and refract light intensely, and a short, coiled proboscis.
    *   **Life:** They drift, but their drift is purposeful harvesting. Their primary sustenance is light itself, absorbed through their wing membranes and converted into energy stored within their resinous cores. They also seek the faint static charge generated by the ozone tang in the air and the friction of metallic leaves clicking. Their slow spirals bring them close to the crystalline fronds and bioluminescent pods. With infinite care, they extend their proboscis to lick microscopic mineral deposits from the fronds or collect the faintest electrostatic discharge from the pods' pulsing surfaces. They are drawn to disturbances in the light – a shift in a shadow (though the light is constant, their perception is hyper-sensitive) or a particularly bright pulse. When a pod finally ruptures (a century-scale event), they descend en masse in a slow-motion cloud, their wings casting dazzling, fractured rainbows as they gather the drifting spores, not to eat, but to incorporate their light-refracting properties into their own wing-membrane growth.

**The Unseen Custodian:** While not a distinct "fauna," the **Resin** itself acts as a kind of somatic intelligence. Its slow seepage, its conductive fluid, its integration with the Grazers and the plant roots, and its unchanging, sharp-edged perfection suggest a guiding presence. The Vine-Tenders and the Grazers seem less like independent organisms and more like specialized limbs or processes of this greater, slow-flowing consciousness embodied in the Amber Atrium. The silence is its contemplation; the glacial movement, its breath; the unchanging light, its unwavering focus. The creatures are its manifestations within its own dreamscape.

**The Rule of Stillness:** All inhabitants share the profound time-dilation. Metabolism is unimaginably slow. Perception is stretched across subjective years. A predator-prey dynamic is absent; existence is symbiotic stasis. Aggression would be wasted energy over geological timescales. The only competition is for the optimal position to absorb light, collect static, or integrate resin – a competition played out in movements measured in inches per year. The clicks, hums, and wing-whispers are not signals of alarm or courtship in a fast sense, but rather the deep, resonant notes of a system functioning perfectly within its eternal, amber moment.

They are not visitors to the Amber Atrium; they *are* the Amber Atrium, living its slow, luminous, waterless dream into eternity.
