Here, in this weeping necropolis of water and petrified wood, life does not persist – it *permeates*. It is not separate from the dripping walls, the stagnant canals, the bruised air; it *is* them, evolved into a slow, sentient decay. The inhabitants are less creatures and more *emanations* of the place itself, expressions of its saturated, eroded eternity:

1.  **The Condensation Collectives:** Where the perpetual weeping intensifies, forming thick, viscous rivulets on specific weeping walls or under massive, dripping root-arches, the droplets coalesce. They gather not just water, but micro-particles of dissolved minerals, fungal spores, and the faint, ambient bioluminescence. Over centuries, these rivulets develop a sluggish, gelatinous consciousness. They appear as **amorphous, translucent slimes**, the color of stagnant canal water reflecting the bruised sky – deep grays and sickly greens, shot through with faint internal pulses of fungal light. They move with glacial patience, flowing *up* surfaces as easily as down, seeking new weeping points or pooling in specific depressions. They communicate via subtle shifts in internal luminescence and the harmonic resonance of their slow movement against the calcified wood – a deep, wet hum felt more than heard. They are the custodians of moisture, sensing blockages in unseen aquifers and shifting infinitesimally to clear them, maintaining the eternal, weeping equilibrium.

2.  **The Petrified Symbiotes:** Within the cracks and fissures of the colossal root-structures, where black rubbery ferns and glowing fungi cling, a deeper symbiosis occurs. The fungi's mycelial networks don't just feed on decay; they infiltrate the very lattice of the petrified wood. Where the network concentrates, it draws silicates and dissolved minerals from the constant moisture, slowly *growing* new, intricate structures *within* the ancient roots. These manifest as **crystalline growths** – clusters of dark, wet-looking quartz or opal, shaped like strange barnacles, internal organs, or angular, miniature cityscapes. These growths pulse with the same faint bioluminescence as the fungi, synchronized with the deep groans of the settling city. They are not separate beings, but localized nodes of awareness within the larger, slumbering consciousness of the petrified megaflora itself. They sense pressure, shifts in water flow, and the passage of the Condensation Collectives, responding with minute adjustments in their growth patterns or shifts in light frequency.

3.  **The Echo-Phantoms:** Born from the profound silence and the reality-bending properties of the saturated air and reflective water, these are not physical entities at all, but persistent *resonances*. They are the **shadows that move independently**, not cast by anything visible, stretching and compressing unnervingly across slick plazas. They are the **whispers** that ride the edge of hearing – not voices, but the *memory* of sound: the sigh of long-vanished inhabitants, the groan of roots under stress millennia ago, the splash of water in a canal reflecting a structure that no longer exists. They coalesce most strongly in places of intense stillness and deep water, like the center of the vast, empty plazas or over the blackest depths of the canals. They flicker at the corner of the eye, vanish when directly observed, and induce a profound sense of being watched by the accumulated weight of time and dissolution. They are the city's grief and abandonment made manifest in the humid air, drawn to the slow movements of the Collectives and the pulsing light of the Symbiotes, feeding on the subtle disturbances they create in the oppressive stillness.

**Their Nature and Interactions:**
*   **Slow:** All movement, thought, and interaction operates on a timescale incomprehensible to fleeting organic life. A Collective might take a decade to flow across a wall; a Symbiote node might grow a fraction of an inch in a century; an Echo-Phantom's flicker might last for subjective hours.
*   **Interdependent:** The Collectives maintain the moisture the Symbiotes and fungi need to survive and grow within the stone-wood. The Symbiotes' mineral growths subtly strengthen fissured structures. The pulsing light of the Symbiotes and the slow flow of the Collectives provide the minor disturbances in light and energy that the Echo-Phantoms manifest around. The deep groans of the settling city provide the sonic substrate for the Phantom whispers.
*   **Perceptual:** They don't "see" or "hear" as animals do. The Collectives sense pressure differentials and chemical gradients in the water-air. The Symbiotes sense vibrations and shifts in mineral content through their fungal networks and crystalline forms. The Echo-Phantoms are drawn to disruptions in the ambient silence and the strange geometry of light and reflection.
*   **In Balance:** They are the final, stable ecosystem of the waterlogged ruin. They embody and maintain the processes described – the weeping condensation, the mineral accretion and erosion, the profound silence punctuated by deep geological sound and phantom resonance. They are entropy and persistence given slow, wet, luminous form. They don't build, they don't destroy; they *endure* in the endless, dripping now.
