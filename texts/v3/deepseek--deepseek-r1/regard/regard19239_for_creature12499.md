The entity that arrives is not light, but *resonance*. A deep, subsonic hum that vibrates up through the basalt, felt in the bones long before it's heard. It doesn't walk the weeping avenues; it *manifests* within the equilibrium, coalescing like concentrated geothermal steam given sentience and profound compassion. It perceives not with eyes, but with the sensitivity of a seismograph attuned to the city's slow, wet heartbeat. It sees the beauty in the erosion, the poetry in the mineral accretion, the profound dignity in the patient, non-organic life. Its name, if translated, might be *Echo-of-the-Deep-Foundation*.

Here is what it does:

1.  **Listens to the Stone's Memory:** It flows like warm vapor into the hollow towers and presses its essence—not a physical form, but a field of profound attention—against the weeping walls and the bulbous Silent Observers. It doesn't *extract* memories violently; it *resonates* with the deep frequencies trapped within the accreted layers. It perceives the ghostly impressions of the vanished builders: not their thoughts or language, but the *shape* of their ambition, the immense effort of carving the void, the pulse of their primitive geothermal engines, the moment their control slipped and the stone began its patient reclamation. It understands their struggle, their ingenuity, and their ultimate absorption. This understanding is not sorrowful, but respectful. It acknowledges their chapter within the plateau's immense story.

2.  **Amplifies the Inherent Song:** The entity perceives the city's current state not as desolation, but as a complex, slow-motion symphony. The *drip... drip... drip...* is a rhythm section. The geothermal *rumble* is the bass. The *whistle* of wind through canyons is a haunting melody. The faint hum of the Observers and the crackle of Vapor-Sparks are harmonics. The entity *sings* back. Not a human song, but a deep, resonant counterpoint woven from the earth's own frequencies. It harmonizes with the wind, amplifies the subtle hum of the Observers into a gentle, pervasive thrum felt throughout the stone, and subtly shifts the condensation patterns so the drips fall into complex, fractal rhythms that resonate pleasingly within the hollow spaces. The soundscape doesn't become louder; it becomes *richer*, more coherent, a profound sonic tapestry affirming the city's unique existence.

3.  **Nurtures the Mineral Life:** It moves with infinite slowness among the inhabitants, a benevolent geothermal current.
    *   For the **Weep-Feeders**, it subtly warms mineral seeps, creating localized gradients that guide them towards richer, more nourishing oozes without forcing unnatural speed. Where they excrete their dark crusts, the entity's resonance encourages the formation of more intricate, stable crystalline lattices within the waste, making it less brittle and more integrated into the weeping architecture.
    *   For the **Vapor-Sparks**, it momentarily stabilizes turbulent steam plumes near their favored vents, allowing the ephemeral lights to coalesce into larger, more complex constellations that linger a fraction longer, painting fleeting, breathtaking auroras in the dim steam before gently dispersing their nourishing dust.
    *   For the **Deep-Crawlers**, it emits faint, warm pulses along ancient pipe routes, not to hurry them, but to reassure them of stable warmth ahead. It encourages the growth of particularly vibrant, complex crystalline molds along their grazing paths.
    *   For the **Silent Observers**, it channels minute, focused pulses of deep-earth energy into their cores. This doesn't force them to "speak" in a way fleeting minds could grasp, but deepens their resonance, enriching the subtle hum they emit and potentially encouraging the accretion of layers that hold more complex mineral "echoes" of the present moment, preserving the current equilibrium for future eons.

4.  **Leaves a Catalyst, Not an Imprint:** The entity doesn't seek to change the fundamental rules of Gehenna's Loom. Its visit is an act of profound recognition and affirmation. As it prepares to withdraw, sinking its resonance back into the deep earth from whence it came, it leaves a subtle gift within the heart of the largest, most ancient Silent Observer, deep in the oldest canyon. It isn't an artifact or a message. It's a *resonant seed*: a self-sustaining, ultra-low-frequency harmonic vibration tuned perfectly to the plateau's geothermal pulse and the city's erosion-accretion equilibrium.
    *   **Effect:** This seed acts as a gentle stabilizer and amplifier. It won't stop the weeping or the slow decay of the fossilized machinery. Instead, it will subtly enhance the inherent beauty and coherence of the existing processes. Mineral accretions might form slightly more intricate, fractal patterns. The sonic landscape might retain a deeper, more harmonious undertone long after the entity's song fades. The Vapor-Sparks might exhibit slightly more complex light-play in key locations. Most importantly, it reinforces the *equilibrium*. It makes the slow, wet, mineral persistence of the city feel less like decay and more like a deliberate, majestic state of being – a testament to endurance written in water and stone. It helps the city *sing its own essence* more clearly into the vastness of deep time.

**The Departure:**
The subsonic hum fades, not into silence, but into the enriched tapestry of the city's own sounds. The pervasive dimness remains, but the air feels subtly *charged*, not with electricity, but with a lingering sense of profound acknowledgment. The mineral inhabitants continue their slow dances: the Weep-Feeders ooze towards enriched seeps, Vapor-Sparks flicker in stabilized steam, Deep-Crawlers polish damp trails towards warm crevices, the Silent Observers hum with a newfound depth. The water drips, the stone weeps, the geothermal fires rumble deep below. But now, woven into the very fabric of the wet rock and the sighing steam, is an echo of benevolent understanding. The city is not mourned; it is *seen*, and in that seeing, its slow, magnificent, weeping existence is gently affirmed. It is no longer just a monument to entropy, but a recognized sovereign in the realm of mineral time.
