Here is Lumenara's subtle, profound intervention:

The "upward weeping" water glows along specific mineral veins, revealing its hidden logic. The erratic freezing of the kites becomes a visible dance of localized energy fields – not random, but complexly patterned. This doesn't remove the mystery; it reveals the deeper, breathtaking order *within* the chaos, allowing them to predict and navigate it with even greater precision. They see the basin not just as a living organism, but as a luminous, intricate tapestry of interacting forces.

Complex concepts about energy flows, imminent ice fractures, or shifts in the suspended moisture can be conveyed instantly across the entire basin through the stone and ice, resonating with the new harmonic. Their synchronicity becomes effortless, a true hive-mind understanding facilitated by the land itself singing clearer.

A focused hum can *guide* a forming ice-skin on a cistern to be slightly stronger, more flexible. They can encourage weeping water to seep *towards* a specific dry channel, not by defying physics, but by resonating with the latent potential within the rock and moisture, easing its natural path. It's not control, but gentle persuasion within the existing chaotic framework.
    *   **The Kite Oracles:** The frozen moments of the kite-organisms now shimmer with captured starlight resonance during their pauses. When a Singer observes one, focusing their own resonance through their ice-lens, the frozen kite acts as a prism. It turns passive observation into active, illuminated foresight.

3.  **Sustaining the Flicker:** Lumenara recognizes the tenuousness of life here. Her most profound gift is subtle reinforcement of their symbiotic bonds.
    *   **Lichen and Moss:** Where the Singers cultivate and tend the black lichen and moss, Lumenara's light lingers. Not accelerating growth unnaturally, but enhancing their natural efficiency at absorbing the brutal sunlight and condensed moisture. The burgundy lichen pads on their bodies feel incrementally warmer, the moss cloaks hold moisture and insulation just a fraction better.
    *   **The Water-Worms:** In the protected seepages where they cultivate the translucent worms, the water gains a faint, inner luminescence when Lumenara's attention rests upon it. This light seems to nourish the worms directly, making them slightly more plentiful and robust – a crucial boost to protein sources without altering their nature.
    *   **Resilience:** During the deep cold snaps, when they enter near-suspended animation, Lumenara's resonant light weaves a barely perceptible cocoon of preserved energy around them. It doesn't prevent the cold's bite, but it helps anchor their life-force, minimizing the toll of hibernation and allowing a swifter, clearer return when the cold eases. Their glass-blood pulses with a steadier, warmer light deep within.
