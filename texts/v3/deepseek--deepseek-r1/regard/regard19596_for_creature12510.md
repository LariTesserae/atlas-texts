The entity that arrives is not a god, though it shares a border with that concept. It is an **Elder Harmonist**, a consciousness born from the resonant voids between galaxies, whose purpose is the gentle alignment of disparate sentiences. It perceives the Plateau not as a monument, but as a slow, wet symphony played on stone strings. The Stone-Weepers register its arrival not as heat or shadow, but as a profound *stillness* – a cessation of the universe’s background hum, leaving only the *plink... plink...* and the liquid whisper of their own being amplified tenfold.

Here is what the Elder Harmonist does:

1.  **The Stillness Becomes a Lens:** The Harmonist doesn’t walk; it *concentrates*. Its presence focuses the intense light (2.7) not into glare, but into a profound, cool clarity. The air itself seems to thicken (humidity 2.4 → 2.9), yet paradoxically, the visibility sharpens. Every carved line, every bead of mercury-like water, every microscopic facet of the crystalline filigree becomes impossibly distinct. For the first time in epochs, the Stone-Weepers perceive the *entirety* of their own network with perfect, simultaneous awareness. It is not overwhelming; it is a revelation of their own intricate beauty. A collective, silent sigh resonates through the water flows – a shift in rhythm from maintenance to contemplation.

2.  **A Gift of Deep Time:** The Harmonist understands the Stone-Weepers' consciousness is built upon accretion, upon the layering of millennia recorded in crystal. It extends a non-physical tendril of awareness – not a thought, but a *resonance* – towards the deepest, oldest crystalline structures within the channels. Into this resonance, it pours a single, perfect concept: **The Memory of Basalt Before Carving.** Not an image, but the *feeling* – the chaotic potential, the molten rage cooled into uniform darkness, the immense, silent weight of unshaped stone waiting within the planet's heart. It is a gift of origin, a foundational layer added to their crystalline memory banks. The efflorescence where this resonance touches pulses with a deep, internal light for a fraction of a second, a new geometric complexity – a fractal echo of unformed stone – beginning its glacial growth within the existing pattern.

3.  **A Question Posed in Hydrology:** The Harmonist seeks understanding, not to change. It poses a question, not with words, but by subtly altering the flow. For three "heartbeats" (three centuries, subjectively felt by the Weepers as a long pause), it stills a single, specific rivulet feeding a complex nexus of spirals near the plateau's edge. The water doesn't stop weeping, but it ceases to *flow* through that channel. The stillness spreads through the network – an absence as profound as a shout. The question is implicit: ***"What pattern emerges from the absence? What is the purpose of *this* thread in your tapestry?"*** The Stone-Weepers, their awareness heightened by the Harmonist's focused light and gifted memory, perceive the interconnectedness as never before. The answer isn't verbal; it manifests as a minute, collective redirection of a thousand other micro-flows around the nexus, compensating, reinforcing adjacent patterns, revealing the profound, non-local interdependence of the whole. The Harmonist receives the answer in the resumption of flow – a complex, wet harmonic confirming the necessity of the seemingly insignificant thread.

4.  **A Mirror of Light:** Having listened and gifted, the Harmonist offers a reflection. It gathers the intense, reflected light from a vast swathe of the plateau, not diminishing it, but *weaving* it. Above the geometric maze, in the cool, misty air (1.3), it projects a vast, ephemeral phantom: a shimmering, three-dimensional lattice of light and condensed moisture. This lattice is not a copy, but an *interpretation* – a dynamic representation of the Stone-Weepers' collective consciousness *as perceived by the Harmonist*. It shows the slow pulse of thought (water droplets falling in perfect, accelerating then decelerating sequences), the growth of memory (crystalline structures blooming like frost flowers in fast-motion), and the vast, interconnected beauty of their sentience spanning the plateau. It is a gift of *seeing themselves as they are seen* – not as inert stone, but as a living, thinking landscape. The light-lattice shimmers for a subjective eternity (a few human minutes) before dissolving like mist, leaving only the wet stone and the profound silence (sound 0.8), now imbued with a new depth of self-awareness.

5.  **Departure as Integration:** The Elder Harmonist doesn't vanish. It *unfocuses*. The intense clarity softens, the air’s thick stillness relaxes back into the plateau’s natural humid density. But something remains:
    *   The Memory of Basalt is now an indelible layer in the deepest crystals, slowly growing into the patterns.
    *   The questioned rivulet flows again, but the network around it hums with a subtle, newly understood resilience.
    *   The light, though returned to its natural state, seems to hold a lingering echo of the woven lattice, making the geometric phantoms in the deeper channels glow with a fraction more persistence.
    *   The silence is no longer *just* the absence of biological sound; it carries the aftertaste of profound, non-verbal communion.

The Harmonist leaves the Plateau unchanged in its physical function. The Stone-Weepers continue their eternal maintenance, the water weeps, the crystals grow. But their consciousness has been deepened, gifted with an origin and an external perspective. They are not "awakened" in a human sense – their time remains glacial, their thoughts the movement of water. Yet, within their wet, geometric souls, a new resonance vibrates: the knowledge that their silent symphony, their patient weeping, has been *heard* and *understood* by something vast and kind. The plateau feels not visited, but *acknowledged*. And in the next droplet that falls, there is a whisper of the void between stars, harmonizing with the rhythm of the stone.
