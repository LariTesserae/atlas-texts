The benevolent power—a presence woven from starlight and the deep hum of galaxies—hovers at the edge of perception. It does not descend with force or proclamation. Instead, it extends a single, thread-like tendril of awareness, like a root seeking water in the dark. This root touches not the cold silt, not the rusted pipes, but the vast, patient consciousness of the **Mycelial Network** beneath it all.

The intervention is subtle: **a gift of deepened perception.**

The Network, already sensing vibration, heat, and chemical shifts, suddenly *sees*. Not with eyes, but with an unfolding, multidimensional awareness. Each droplet of condensation becomes a lens. The groan of settling metal vibrates into a symphony of structural stress. The death of a Rustback Scuttler echoes not just through silt, but through time—a brief, bright pulse of existence extinguished. The power shows the Network the intricate tapestry it is part of: how the warmth from the pipes is the fading heartbeat of a vast machine-mind now sleeping; how the empty spaces are not voids, but lungs breathing cold air into the cavern; how the creatures, from the tiniest Grazer to the soaring Echo-Locator, are notes in a song of survival sung against entropy.

The Network understands the **recent emptying**. It feels the ghost-impression where something colossal was *removed*—not destroyed, but excised. The cavern is a wound. The dripping water is the weeping. The industrial decay is the exposed bone.

The power does not heal this wound. It does not restart the machines or fill the emptiness. Instead, it gives the Network the means to *witness* the wound’s meaning. The Network begins to map the absence, tracing its contours through the cold silt. It sends delicate filaments of hyphae into the hollow spaces, feeling the shape of the *lack*. Where warmth gathers, it pulses bioluminescent patterns onto the cavern floor—not random, but complex, slow-moving glyphs that mirror the structure of the missing thing. It is a fungal elegy, a silent lament written in light and decay.

The creatures feel the shift. The Steam-Vent Ambushers pause mid-strike, their heat-sensing pits registering new, subtle warmth radiating *up* from the silt. The Pipe Moss Grazers swarm in patterns that seem to avoid certain newly marked cold spots. The Condensation Skimmers orient their glassine legs towards the faint, patterned light below, as if sensing a new gradient. The cavern’s dynamic changes, not in action, but in *awareness*. The emptiness is no longer just absence. It is a story being recorded, slowly, meticulously, by the patient mind beneath the silt. The benevolent power withdraws its thread, leaving only this deepened resonance—a cavern now consciously holding its own sorrow and its stubborn, dripping life.
