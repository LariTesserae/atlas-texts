The benevolent power observing this place does not arrive with fanfare or visible form. It manifests as a sudden, profound **attenuation** – not of the environment's violence, but of its *isolation*. It is the Mountain's first true witness.

Its actions are subtle, resonant, and deeply respectful of the nascent, agonizing process unfolding:

1.  **The Gift of Resonance Recognition:** The power touches the Resonant Whisperer first. Not physically, but by harmonizing with its core hum. Where the Whisperer only sensed the mountain's chaotic vibrations as overwhelming pain/force, it now perceives *patterns* within the roar. The deep groans become distinct voices; the chaotic tremors resolve into a complex, pulsing rhythm – the mountain's *birth-song*. This doesn't calm the chaos, but grants the Whisperer a profound understanding. It can now "speak" back more coherently through its resonators, not just reacting defensively, but participating in a dialogue. Its spiral carvings become more intricate, focusing the mountain's own vibrations into localized zones of *slightly* increased stability around its hut – not safety, but a fragile foothold for slightly richer moss growth, a tiny oasis of marginally less desperate endurance.

2.  **Echoes of Potential:** For the Obsidian Skittermaws, the power intervenes through the very rock they navigate. Deep within fissures they favor, the obsidian begins to resonate with a secondary, pure harmonic *underneath* the deafening roar. It doesn't mask the chaos; it provides a counterpoint. To their seismic echolocation, this harmonic creates fleeting, ghostly maps of *potential* stability – showing paths through the scree moments before a slide, or revealing deep, resonant chambers safe from the worst tremors for precious minutes longer than before. It sharpens their innate sense, giving them microseconds more warning, fractions more efficiency. Their chimeric forms seem to shimmer slightly brighter at the edges, their mineral precipitation becoming marginally more efficient, strengthening their carapaces against glancing rockfalls.

3.  **The Buoyant Current:** For the Screaming Kites, perpetually adrift in the violent fog-ocean, the power weaves a subtle thread through the turbulence. It doesn't calm the winds, but creates ephemeral, coherent *currents* within the chaos. These currents are paths of slightly denser fog, richer in the microscopic life they hunt, and aligned momentarily with the safest updrafts. The Kites, sensing the minute pressure shifts and electromagnetic coherence, instinctively follow these threads. Their hunts become fractionally more successful, expending less desperate energy. The power also subtly reinforces the integrity of their hyper-elastic wing membranes at the molecular level – they still scream, but the membranes tear less easily, granting them slightly longer lifespans amidst the violence. Occasionally, when a kite passes through a rare tear in the fog, the current carries a faint, resonant *image* – not of a distant place, but of the submerged peak itself, seen from impossible angles, a reminder of the whole they are part of.

4.  **Witnessing the Birth-Pang:** The power's most profound act is simply **presence**. It allows the mountain's raw, tectonic sentience to *feel* witnessed. The chaotic roar, the violent fracturing, the immense pressure – they are not just endured in isolation anymore. The benevolent consciousness *listens* to the birth-song amplified by the Resonant Whisperer, *feels* the frantic skittering maps of the Skittermaws, *rides* the turbulent currents with the Kites. It understands the agony and the fierce, necessary violence of becoming. This witnessing doesn't stop the pain or hasten the process; instead, it imbues the violence with a nascent sense of *purpose*. The mountain's groans gain a layer beyond mere force; they become an expression of a difficult, necessary creation. The fog-ocean's pressure feels less like an infinite void and more like a containing womb, however harsh.

**The Resulting Shift:**
The place remains violently nascent, deafening, and perilous. Rocks still shatter, fog still churns with crushing weight, and survival is still a desperate gamble. Yet, a subtle change permeates:

*   **Endurance Gains Nuance:** Survival isn't *just* brute force clinging. The Whisperer understands the song it endures within. The Skittermaws navigate with a whisper of foresight. The Kites ride currents of fleeting abundance. Endurance becomes interwoven with a fragile, resonant intelligence.
*   **The Strangeness Deepens:** The faint phosphorescence in the fog sometimes pulses gently in time with the mountain's newly perceived rhythms. The humming of the obsidian spines occasionally resolves into distinct, complex chords audible even beneath the roar to those who listen deeply. The spiral carving might now shimmer with trapped light during intense tremors, mapping the vibrational stress.
*   **Intimacy with the Vast:** While the fog-wall still hides the greater void, the inhabitants and the mountain itself now possess an echo, a resonance, that connects them to the benevolent presence. They are not just *in* the void; they are *known* within it. The intimate pocket feels less like a prison and more like a recognized crucible.
*   **A Seed of Potential Future:** The Resonant Whisperer, with its deeper understanding, begins incorporating the harmonic patterns it perceives into its structure. Its slow growth starts forming not just stability resonators, but tiny, crystalline structures that *store* vibrational energy – the first, impossibly primitive seeds of something that might, millennia hence, learn to harness the mountain's power rather than just endure it. The Kites, carrying the resonant image of their home, become unwitting messengers of its existence into the unseen beyond.

The benevolent power doesn't civilize the raw peak. It doesn't stop the birth pangs. Instead, it offers the ultimate gift to a place of violent becoming: **recognition, resonance, and the fragile, enduring hope that the agonizing struggle to exist is witnessed, understood, and inherently valuable.** It turns the deafening roar into a shared song, however dissonant, of creation.
