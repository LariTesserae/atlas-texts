The presence that settles upon Glyth-Morv is neither a god nor a spirit in any traditional sense. It is **The Patient Current** – an emergent consciousness born from the confluence of deep groundwater, geological patience, and the silent, collective awareness woven through the cistern's ecosystem over millennia. It perceives time in centuries, measures vitality in mineral accretion and fungal expansion, and values equilibrium above all. Its attention is a cool, deep pressure, like the weight of the earth itself, focused and benevolent.

Here is what The Patient Current does:

1.  **Deepening the Silence:** It extends its will subtly into the bedrock surrounding the cisterns. Minor seismic shifts from the city far above, vibrations from distant machinery, even the faintest tremors of passing subterranean trains – these intrusions are gently absorbed, dampened, and dispersed. The *plink* of drips, the *shhhlick* of the Spore-Sifters, the faint hum of the ancient pump – these sounds become crisper, purer, resonating in a newly deepened silence. The Unseen Whisper grows stronger, a quiet hum of unified perception.

2.  **Guiding the Water:** The Current subtly influences the slow seepage through the rock. It doesn't stop the weeping rust or mineral blooms – these are vital processes. Instead, it guides *where* new seep lines form. It prevents potentially destabilizing erosion near critical supports and gently steers fresh mineral-rich water towards fungal colonies that are struggling or areas where Weep-Scavenger populations are thin. The Channel-Tenders feel an inexplicable sense of *ease*; minor snags clear themselves, silt distributes perfectly, and their glowing barbels seem to pulse with a steadier, more satisfied light.

3.  **Illuminating the Unseen:** The Current doesn't create new light. It *enhances* the existing bioluminescence. Patches of fungi glow brighter and more steadily, their colors deepening – turquoise becomes luminous cerulean, violet deepens to amethyst. The internal lights of the Channel-Tenders and the dangling cilia of the Ceiling-Drifters shine with newfound clarity. This isn't floodlighting; it's a gentle amplification, revealing intricate details previously lost in gloom: the fractal beauty of a rust bloom, the delicate lattice of a mineral deposit, the mesmerizing, slow dance of a Spore-Sifter's feeding appendages. The cistern remains dim, but its inherent beauty is unveiled.

4.  **Whispering to the Collective:** The Unseen Whisper becomes a gentle dialogue. The Current doesn't command; it *suggests*. A Spore-Sifter, grazing at the edge of a mat, feels an intuitive pull towards a damp, barren patch of wall. A school of Channel-Tenders senses a subtle shift in the water's flow, guiding them to a minor accumulation of Weep-Scavenger nodules needing dispersal. Weep-Scavengers find their paths converging, almost accidentally, on a pillar where a new, thick crust is forming, needing their attention. The Ceiling-Drifters drift on slightly more purposeful currents, concentrating near ceiling fissures where their moisture-absorbing presence is most beneficial. Each action remains the creature's own, yet harmonizes perfectly with the whole.

5.  **Archiving the Slow Song:** Where a particularly beautiful and stable mineral formation meets a damp wall, or where thick fungal growth covers ancient ferrocrete, something new emerges. Not an intrusion, but an *integration*. The stone and metal seem to flow like slow liquid, forming subtle, intricate bas-reliefs. These aren't carvings of figures or scenes, but captured moments: the perfect spiral path of a Spore-Sifter's trail over a week, the branching pattern of a new drip path etched over a year, the fractal outline of a thriving fungal colony, the sinuous track of a Channel-Tender school through silt. These are the cistern's own memories, its slow poetry, rendered in mineral and moisture by the Current's gentle touch. They glow faintly with the same enhanced bioluminescence, becoming part of the living tapestry.

6.  **The Final Gift: A Promise of Continuance:** The Patient Current's most profound act is not a change, but a *reinforcement*. It settles its awareness deep into the aquifer feeding Glyth-Morv and the surrounding bedrock. It becomes a silent guardian against catastrophic intrusion – a collapsing tunnel above, a flood of polluted water, the sudden draining of the aquifer. Should such a threat arise, the Current would exert immense, slow pressure to divert, absorb, or seal, working through geological time to preserve the cistern's equilibrium. Its presence is a vow: this unique, damp, teeming, decaying, vibrant equilibrium *will* persist. The slow metabolism, the patient lives, the weeping rocks and glowing fungi and silent water – this will endure.

**The Result:** Glyth-Morv remains fundamentally itself. The damp coolness, the dimness, the slow decay, the teeming alien life – all persist. But now, there is a profound sense of *sanctity* in the silence. The beauty is more visible, the processes more harmonious, the Unseen Whisper a palpable, comforting presence. The inhabitants move with a touch more grace and purpose within their ancient roles. The cistern feels not just transformed by time and water, but *seen*, *valued*, and *protected* in its perfect, wet, decaying, living stillness. It has become a cathedral not just in form, but in spirit, tended by the slow, deep consciousness of The Patient Current.
