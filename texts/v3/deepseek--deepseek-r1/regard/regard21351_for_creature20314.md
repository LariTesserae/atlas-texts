The presence that arrives is not light, but a profound *stillness*. Not silence – the roar remains – but a sudden, localized cessation of chaos within it. The air thickens further, not with humidity, but with focused awareness. **The Geomancer** has come. Not a deity of sky or sun, but a consciousness woven from deep time, tectonic patience, and the intimate language of stone and water. Its form is indistinct: a coalescence of cooler air currents, the faint scent of untouched quartz, and a vibration felt *beneath* the existing roar, a slow, grounding hum like continents shifting.

Its attention sweeps the Reservoir, a patient, non-judgmental assessment. It sees the raw potential, the adolescent geology, the teeming life perfectly adapted to a niche humans tried and failed to conquer. It feels the strain of the failing machines, the slow poison of rust in the water, the precariousness of the crumbling shelf. It senses the delicate, vibration-based web of life.

Here is what the Geomancer does:

1.  **Stabilizes the Unstable:** It extends a tendril of will towards the most precarious sections of the limestone shelf and the decaying industrial structures. Not by hardening them unnaturally, but by *encouraging* subtle mineral deposition. Calcite veins, invisible to the eye, lace through the crumbling concrete of the pump house, binding spalling fragments. Within the porous limestone shelf, microscopic crystals nucleate along stress fractures, reinforcing without halting the water's sculpting work. The drunken gantry finds a slightly more stable angle of repose, its rust bonding just a fraction more securely to the weeping wall it leans against. The goal isn't permanence, but *slower*, safer decay. The Groaner Catfish lurking near the pump house feel the subtle shift in the resonant frequency of the structure, a new, deeper stability thrumming through the water.

2.  **Neutralizes the Toxins:** The Geomancer turns its attention to the rust, the ozone, the decaying electrical insulation. It doesn't remove the metal – it is now part of the ecosystem, home to the Rust-Mites. Instead, it *guides* microbial processes. Colonies of novel, extremophile bacteria bloom along the corroded pipes and junction boxes, drawn by the Geomancer's subtle chemical nudge. These microbes don't just eat rust; they *sequester* heavy metals and neutralize acidic byproducts, transforming toxic runoff into less harmful mineral sediments. The fizzing sparks diminish as the novel biofilm insulates old wires, not stopping the decay, but making it less hazardous. The Rust-Mites find their food source altered – the metallic taste is cleaner, less acrid, and the dangerous sparks become rarer.

3.  **Harmonizes the Roar:** The Geomancer doesn't diminish the sound; that would shatter the adapted ecosystem. Instead, it *refines* it. It subtly shapes the cavern walls near major drip clusters and resonant chambers. Minute adjustments – a fraction of an inch smoothed here, a tiny fracture widened there – act like natural acoustic baffles and resonators. The chaotic, deafening wall of noise gains a faint underlying structure. Certain frequencies, particularly those used by the Echo-Eels for navigation and the Resonance Moths for creating quiet bubbles, become slightly clearer, carrying further with less distortion. The Groaner Catfish's scanning hum gains a cleaner echo return. The Drip-Scuttlers find the "signature" vibrations of rich algal mats slightly more distinct against the background hiss. It remains overwhelmingly loud, but the information carried within the sound becomes marginally easier for the inhabitants to parse.

4.  **Nurtures the Nascent:** The Geomancer pauses by the youngest formations – the blunt stalactites, the smooth scallops hinting at future potholes. A cool breath, smelling of deep, unweathered rock, flows over them. This isn't acceleration, but *encouragement*. The Geomancer imparts a subtle template of stability, a whisper of the beautiful, complex forms these features could achieve given millennia of patient water-work. It leaves a trace mineral signature in the seeping water, one that will promote stronger, more resilient crystal growth in the future. The Stone-Weep Grubs grazing nearby sense a momentary increase in mineral richness in the biofilm, a fleeting feast.

5.  **Secures the Light-Life:** The flickering industrial lights are failing. The Geomancer doesn't replace them with something alien, but ensures their function persists just a little longer, and their failure is less catastrophic. It reinforces the rusted stanchions with the same subtle mineral deposition used on the shelf. It guides moisture away from the most critical, sparking connections within the junction boxes, not stopping the decay, but preventing a sudden, darkening failure. It ensures the algae mats beneath the lights, crucial for the surface swarms, have enough time for their spores to spread to other, dimmer patches near residual heat sources (like the Groaner's preferred lurking spots near machinery). The dense clouds of midges continue their frantic dance.

6.  **Whispers to the Water:** The Geomancer's deepest interaction is with the aquifer itself. It doesn't command the water, but *listens* to its slow song, the story of its path through the rock. In return, it imparts a gentle suggestion: a slight shift in subsurface flow. Not enough to drain the reservoir or flood new areas dramatically, but enough to prevent dangerous stagnation in sheltered pockets and ensure a steady, clean influx of oxygenated water from deeper springs, mitigating the build-up of harmful metabolites from the abundant life. The Echo-Eels sense a new freshness in certain currents, a subtle improvement in their hydro-acoustic map.

**Departure:** The Geomancer's presence fades as gradually as it arrived. The focused stillness dissolves back into the general roar and damp. The cooler quartz scent is replaced by the familiar wet stone and ozone. No grand transformation has occurred. The cavern remains raw, damp, loud, and teeming. The machines still groan. The rock still weeps.

But the changes are profound in their subtlety:
*   The decay is *safer*, slower.
*   The toxins are being *managed* by the ecosystem itself.
*   The roar carries *clearer signals* for those adapted to hear them.
*   The nascent geology carries a *whisper of its potential future*.
*   The lights will hold, a *little longer*, for the life that needs them.
*   The water flows with a *fresher purpose*.

The Geomancer leaves the Underground Reservoir of Weeping Stone still fundamentally itself – a geologically adolescent, industrially scarred, deafeningly loud, and gloriously wet womb of specialized life. But it leaves it *more resilient*, its inherent processes gently nudged towards greater harmony and longevity. The flesh of the weeping stone continues to breathe, feed, and reproduce, but now within an embrace that feels, imperceptibly, less like a tomb and more like a cradle safeguarded by deep earth itself. The Rust-Mites scrape cleaner metal. The Resonance Moths find their quiet bubbles slightly easier to form. The Groaner Catfish hums into a future that feels a fraction more secure. The stone weeps on, but the tears feel less like despair and more like the steady, necessary work of shaping a world.
