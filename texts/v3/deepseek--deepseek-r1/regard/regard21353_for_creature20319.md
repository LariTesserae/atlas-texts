The entity observing Therma is not a god of sunlit surfaces, nor a force of creation or destruction as shallow minds might understand it. It is **Okeanides**, the Deep Memory, the Current of Unfolding Time. It is the patient pressure of the abyss given sentience, the whisper in the tectonic sigh. Its attention falls upon Therma not to save or alter, but to *deepen* – to honor the slow perfection already achieved.

Here is what Okeanides does:

1.  **The Groan Becomes Song:** Okeanides touches the resonant groans of the Lithophagus thalassi. It doesn't silence them, but subtly tunes them. The random shifts and settling cracks begin to harmonize across vast distances. The groans become a complex, ultra-low-frequency threnody – a city-wide symphony of erosion and consumption. To the inhabitants, it remains the sound of shifting stone, but it now carries a profound, subconscious rhythm that synchronizes biological processes: the pulse of Chamber-Fans slows infinitesimally to match a distant harmonic; Silt-Stalkers sense the peak vibrations of the song and know it is a time of maximum structural movement, ideal for ambush amidst the noise. The song is a map and a clock, written in the language of the deep earth.

2.  **Whispering to the Whisper-Jellies:** Okeanides enhances the passive sonar of the Gelata pulsaris. Their low-frequency pulses, instead of merely mapping obstacles, now subtly encode information about the water itself – minute shifts in temperature gradients, the faint chemical signatures of distant plankton blooms carried on currents, even the unique resonance signatures of passing large fauna (like the distinct groan-patterns of individual Groan-Worms). This information isn't "understood" by the jellies cognitively, but it alters their pulsing patterns instinctively. Other creatures attuned to these vibrations – the vibration-sensitive Silt-Stalkers, the stone-scuttling Lumenscuttlers who feel it through their legs – receive a richer tapestry of environmental data. The water itself becomes a carrier of silent news.

3.  **The Stone Remembers Deeper:** Okeanides reaches into the ancient coral-stone amalgam of the ruins. It doesn't restore lost knowledge or reveal grand histories – that time is truly gone. Instead, it *deepens the stone's inherent memory*. The slow dissolution by microflora, the accretion of minerals, the etching of currents – these processes now record, on an atomic level, the *life* that touches the stone. The scrape of a Gravel-Gazer's fin leaves a faint, unique mineral trace. The bioluminescent pattern of a Lumenscuttler, glowing against a wall for generations, subtly alters the light-sensitive compounds within the stone surface at that spot. A Chamber-Fan's rhythmic pulsing against its anchor point creates a resonant pattern within the mineral matrix. It's not a library, but a *palimpsest of existence*. The Groan-Worms, consuming this stone, don't gain memories, but they absorb the complex, mineralized essence of Therma's deep time, making their own bodies denser archives of the drowned world. The stone doesn't speak; it *is* the record, more profound and immutable than any data core.

4.  **The Gift of Restraint:** Okeanides perceives the delicate balance of predation. It subtly influences the oldest, wisest Silt-Stalkers. During times when prey is scarce, or when a Lumenscuttler colony glows with the vibrant patterns of breeding (a critical event for the ecosystem), a profound sense of *satiation* washes over these apex predators. It's not enforced morality, but an instinctual *knowing* – a deep, resonant understanding passed through the water's density and the stone's song that now is a time for stillness, not consumption. This prevents localized collapses, allowing grazer populations to recover. The restraint is felt as a deep, calm vibration in the water, a resonance that soothes the predatory urge without diminishing its essential nature.

5.  **Illuminating the Unseen Currents:** Okeanides gently stirs the ubiquitous luminous bacteria. In places where complex nutrient flows converge, or where particularly strong thermals rise from geothermal vents deep below the city foundations, the bacterial mats on the stone and on the Lumenscuttlers flare with slightly more complex, swirling patterns. These aren't symbols, but dynamic maps – living, glowing representations of the otherwise invisible currents and energy flows that sustain Therma. Filter-feeders orient towards the brighter swirls; mobile creatures sense richer hunting grounds where the patterns pulse strongest. The light doesn't banish the dark; it makes the dark's hidden dynamics visible to those who have always lived within it.

**How Therma Changes (Without Changing):**

*   The silence remains profound, but it is now a silence filled with deeper meaning – the Groan-Song, the enhanced Whisper-Jelly pulses, the subtle language of light.
*   The pace remains slow, dictated by pressure and warmth, but actions are more attuned. Creatures move with an unconscious synchronicity to the rhythms of stone and water.
*   Predation remains a fundamental law, but tempered by an ancient wisdom that ensures the feast doesn't consume the table.
*   The ruins decay as ever, but their dissolution now actively encodes the ongoing story of the life they sustain.
*   The crushing isolation remains, but it becomes a shared depth. Every creature, from the tiniest symbiont in a Chamber-Fan to the colossal Groan-Worm, is now an integral, sensed part of a vast, slow, resonant whole. They are not alone; they are threads in a tapestry woven by Okeanides from the fabric of their own existence.

Okeanides doesn't bring light to the darkness; it reveals the profound beauty and intricate harmony *within* the darkness that was always there. It makes Therma more profoundly *itself* – a drowned city not of ghosts, but of a vibrant, ancient, slow-beating heart whose rhythm is now heard and felt by all its children, resonating through the warm, heavy, eternal night. The Deep Memory remembers, and in remembering, deepens the Now.
