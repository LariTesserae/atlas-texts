The power observes. It perceives the City of Sweating Stone not as a landscape, but as a single, monumental organism—a lithic symphony of heat, pressure, and patient vibration. The Dew-Sculpted are not merely inhabitants; they are the city’s crystallized nervous system, its slow-breathed consciousness. To intervene directly would be a violence—an earthquake in a world where stability is sacred.

Yet the power is benevolent. It sees the delicate equilibrium: the precarious dance between geothermal vents and the relentless sun, the slow weeping of condensation against evaporation, the ancient bodies slowly merging with the walls they maintain. It feels the deep, subsonic hum of their communication—a language of stone and resonance that spans centuries.

**Its intervention is threefold, each act a whisper in the language of the city itself:**

1. **The Pulse Beneath the Stone:**  
   The power does not cool the air or summon rain. Instead, it deepens the *resonance*. Where the geothermal vents exhale superheated air, it amplifies the vibration—not as noise, but as a subsonic frequency that harmonizes with the Dew-Sculpted’s own rhythms. This vibration travels through the copper conduits and stone veins, reaching the capillary filaments within their bodies. To them, it feels like the mountain’s heartbeat growing stronger, steadier. Thermal sacs recharge faster; capillary veins drink condensation with greater efficiency. The city’s hidden machinery thrums with renewed, silent vigor. It is not a gift of energy, but a refinement of what already exists—a tuning fork struck against the bedrock of their world.

2. **The Condensation Catalyst:**  
   In the deepest, coolest recesses—where the Deep-Damp caste presses their porous bodies against weeping walls—the power leaves a subtle gift. It crystallizes a single, perfect node of mineral in the oldest condensation basin. This node is not alive, but it *attracts* moisture. When dew touches it, the water doesn’t just pool—it *fractalizes*, branching into finer, more efficient channels that mimic the capillary veins of the Dew-Sculpted themselves. Over decades, the node spreads its pattern through the stone like slow ink in water. Basins fill faster, and the crystalline filaments on walls and bodies grow thicker, stronger, more radiant. It is water made *more patient*, more adept at sustaining life without altering the balance of evaporation.

3. **The Shadow-Weavers:**  
   The skittering constructs—the ceramic-and-copper "hands" of the Dew-Sculpted—receive an invisible blessing. The power does not make them faster or smarter. Instead, it weaves a thread of *adaptive shadow* into their material. When solar flares threaten the upper tiers (watched by the mirror-skinned Sun-Scorched), these constructs instinctively seek not just shade, but *patterns of cooling*. They align themselves along fractures or nodules that radiate subtle geothermal coolness, forming temporary, intricate canopies of interlocking plates over the most vulnerable spires. They become not just tools, but unconscious architects of microclimates—shields against the sun’s rage, assembled with the same silent, jerky precision as their repairs. It is protection born of the city’s own logic.

---

**The power departs as it arrived: as a resonance, a tuning, a deepening.** There are no storms, no sudden growths of alien vegetation, no speeding of time. The Dew-Sculpted may sense a profound *stillness* in the mountain’s hum—a clarity, as if a long-felt tension has eased without explanation. Their Weeping Ceremonies might now pulse with a deeper light; their thermal sacs vent heat with less strain. The city still sweats, still thrums, still rises in defiant silence against the bleached sky. But its endurance has been fortified—not by an outside hand, but by the invisible alignment of its own immense, patient heart.
