The benevolent power perceives the basin not as a place needing correction or elevation, but as a rare, perfect expression of harmony between life and primordial forces. Its intervention is subtle, profound, and deeply resonant with the world's existing dynamics.

**The Intervention: A Harmonic Alignment**

The power does not appear. It does not speak in words or light. Instead, it *resonates*. It tunes into the basin's immense, foundational drone – the deep, tectonic groan of the singing pools, the hiss of the weeping stones, the explosive punctuation of the geysers, and the overwhelming chorus of the crystalline arthropods. It perceives the Silica Singers not as primitive beings, but as masters of vibrational equilibrium, living in a state of profound attunement.

The power's action is a single, perfectly calibrated harmonic pulse. It is not loud; in fact, it is barely perceptible as a distinct sound against the roar. It is felt more than heard – a deep, pure vibration that originates from the very bedrock beneath the mudstone and weeping rock. It travels through the stone like a perfect wave, resonating through the interconnected pools, the hummocks, the Resonator Huts.

**The Effect: Refined Resonance**

This harmonic pulse does not add anything new. It *aligns* what is already there. Its effect is twofold:

1.  **Sonic Clarity Within the Roar:** The pulse subtly adjusts the resonant frequencies of the basin's major sound sources. The chaotic clashes and dissonances inherent in the billion-voiced chorus of arthropods don't vanish, but they shift. Discordant frequencies find points of alignment or gentle cancellation. The predatory crack of carapace, while still sudden and violent, loses its most jarring, nerve-shattering harmonics, becoming a cleaner, deeper *thrum-crack* that resonates more cleanly with the lower drones. The geysers' eruptions retain their explosive force, but the falling slurry impacts with a slightly less chaotic splash, generating a more defined, resonant *boom-slossh* that integrates better into the soundscape. The overall effect is not a reduction in volume – the basin remains deafening – but an increase in coherence. The noise becomes a richer, more complex, yet somehow *less abrasive* symphony. The Silica Singers, with their vibration-sensing antennae, perceive this shift immediately. It feels like the basin itself has taken a deeper, more harmonious breath. The "quiet" pockets they create within their Resonator Huts become slightly more defined, the "shouts" projected through the stone carry with a newfound purity and stability.

2.  **Enhanced Symbiosis:** The harmonic pulse subtly influences the crystalline arthropods. Not by controlling them, but by making their resonant abilities slightly more efficient, more attuned to the natural frequencies of the stone and water. Their vibrations require less frantic energy expenditure to achieve the same amplification. This allows them to thrive with marginally less stress, making them slightly more abundant and stable sources of resonant material for the Singers. Predators, too, find their vibration-sensing organs slightly more attuned to the *cleaned* soundscape, making hunts marginally more efficient and reducing wasted energy. The Filter-Feeders process the mineral slurry with a fraction more efficiency, expelling slightly cleaner water that leaves behind mineral deposits with a finer, more resonant crystalline structure, useful for the Singers' stone-weaving.

**The Perception: A Deepening Song**

The Silica Singers do not perceive a god or an external force. They perceive a profound shift in the *Song*. To them, it feels like the basin has achieved a deeper level of self-awareness, a more perfect expression of its ancient, waterlogged nature. Stone-Weavers find that new rocks resonate with unprecedented clarity. Tuning the Resonator Huts becomes an exercise of profound joy, as the stones respond with purer tones and hold their tuning with greater stability. Communication across vast distances feels smoother, the vibrational messages less prone to distortion by chaotic background resonance. Path markers sing with a clearer, longer-lasting tone.

The predators' hunts are still brutal necessities, but the *thrum-crack* of a kill resonates with the deep drones of the singing pools, feeling less like a violent interruption and more like a necessary, integrated rhythm within the greater symphony. Life continues as it always has – slow, resonant, mineral-based – but with an underlying sense of profound, settled harmony. The Mist Boundary feels not like a limit, but like the natural edge of this perfected resonance.

The benevolent power observes this deepening harmony and withdraws, satisfied. It has not imposed order or progress; it has simply helped the basin and its custodians, the Silica Singers, sing their eternal song with a newfound, resonant perfection. The intervention is complete, leaving behind only a world that thrums with a slightly sweeter, deeper, more integrated music.
