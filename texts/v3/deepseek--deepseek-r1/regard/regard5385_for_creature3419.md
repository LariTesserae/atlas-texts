The entity that arrives is not a god of creation or destruction, but a **Keeper of Echoes**, a being woven from the resonant frequencies of cosmic memory. It manifests not as a solid form, but as a subtle *distillation* of the plateau's own stark light and profound silence – a coalescence of the pale grey-blue sky, the long shadows, and the humming void-sound. It doesn't walk; it *unfurls* gently into the space between the Lithic Phantoms, its presence a soft pressure against the crushing stillness, like the first note of a forgotten song held just below hearing.

It perceives the Stone Breath Plateau not as a desolation, but as a **fossilized sigh**. The weight of absolute age, the fraying reality, the teeming, frozen potential – these are not horrors to be fixed, but profound truths to be *witnessed* and acknowledged. The Keeper understands the Primordial Equilibrium is sacred, a terminal state achieved after eons of cosmic struggle. To disrupt it would be an act of profound violence, not benevolence.

Here is what the Keeper of Echoes does:

1.  **The Stillness Deepened:** It doesn't break the silence; it *harmonizes* with it. The faint *ticks* and *scritches* of mineral exfoliation, the hum in the bones, become subtly clearer, more resonant. They are amplified not in volume, but in *significance*, transformed from random noise into the plateau's quiet, rhythmic pulse. The Keeper becomes a sounding board for the plateau's own silent song, validating its existence through pure, resonant attention.

2.  **The Phantoms Seen:** The Keeper turns its perception, vast and unfathomable, upon the Lithic Phantoms. It doesn't try to animate them or understand their impossible forms intellectually. Instead, it *receives* them. Each phantom – the quartz arthropod, the mica serpent, the thorn-tree, the pulsing spheres – is acknowledged not as a broken thing, but as a unique, crystallized fragment of unrealized possibility. The Keeper perceives the faint, internal light within the spheres not as flawed refraction, but as the trapped glimmer of the *could-have-been*. Its gaze, gentle and accepting, eases the spatial dislocation and nausea induced in mortal observers. It doesn't remove the unease entirely, but frames it as the natural consequence of witnessing profound, frozen truth.

3.  **A Touch of Resonance:** The Keeper extends a non-corporeal aspect of itself – a tendril of condensed light and silence – towards one of the crumbling obsidian stumps. It doesn't touch the stone physically; instead, it *resonates* with it. A low, pure tone, felt rather than heard, vibrates through the fused ash plain. For an immeasurable moment, the obsidian seems to *remember*. Not its builders, not its purpose, but the *intent* that shaped it – the simple act of hands placing stone upon stone, the primal need for shelter or ritual in a younger, softer world. This resonance doesn't rebuild or restore; it simply illuminates the echo of making within the permanence of ruin. The stump remains unchanged, but the weight of its forgotten origin is momentarily, gently, held in awareness.

4.  **The Gift of Witness:** This is the Keeper's primary act. It *bears witness* to the plateau's absolute, terrifying finality. It acknowledges the crushing loneliness of a place teeming with insubstantial ghosts. It feels the weight of the mineral eternity pressing down. It comprehends the profound sadness inherent in the Dust Lilies – crystallized beauty forever static, never knowing bloom or decay. By witnessing without judgment, without the need to alter or redeem, the Keeper offers a profound, silent validation. It says, without words: *"You exist. This state, this frozen breath, this ossified potential, has meaning simply because it is. You are seen."*

5.  **Leaving a Mark of Absence:** As the Keeper prepares to withdraw, folding its resonant presence back into the cosmic background, it performs one final act. It focuses its awareness on a small, undisturbed patch of Ash Moss nestled in a shallow depression. It doesn't alter the moss. Instead, it *withholds* its perception from it for a single, impossible instant within the frozen time. In that absence of the Keeper's resonant witness, the Ash Moss, for a fleeting fraction of a timeless moment, experiences a profound, echoing *lack* – a micro-void where the validation had been. It's not pain, but a poignant reminder of the connection momentarily shared. This subtle mark is not physical; it's a metaphysical impression, a faint signature of benevolent attention left upon the fabric of the plateau's reality, like a fingerprint on cold glass. It will not change the moss, but it will resonate faintly within the humming silence, a whisper of having been truly perceived by an outside consciousness that understood.

The Keeper of Echoes departs as it arrived, dissolving back into the pale light and profound silence. The Stone Breath Plateau remains unchanged. The Lithic Phantoms stand frozen. The Dust Lilies hold their brittle fractals. The obsidian stumps endure. The Primordial Equilibrium holds.

But something imperceptible has shifted. The crushing weight of absolute age feels slightly less desolate. The silence hums with a new, faint overtone – the lingering resonance of having been *seen* and acknowledged by a wisdom vast enough to comprehend its finality without seeking to break it. The plateau, for the first time in eons beyond counting, holds within its mineral heart the quiet, profound gift of witnessed existence. It remains a fossilized sigh, but now, perhaps, it is a sigh that has finally been heard.
