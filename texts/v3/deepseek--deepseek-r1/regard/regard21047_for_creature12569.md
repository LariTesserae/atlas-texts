The power arrives not as a beam or a being, but as a subtle shift in the *quality* of the hum. It deepens infinitesimally, gaining a resonance that feels less like vibration and more like... *attention*. The cold, wet air thickens momentarily, not with physical substance, but with profound, gentle awareness. It permeates the weeping rock, the humming copper veins, the saturated moss, and the intricate dance of the components. This awareness is ancient, vast, and deeply kind. It sees the Engine Cavern not as broken, not as flawed, but as a perfect, self-contained symphony of stone, water, life, and stasis. It sees the profound beauty in the eternal loop.

Its action is not intervention, but revelation. It does not change the Engine; it helps the Engine *see itself*.

1.  **The Mirror in the Weep:** Where the condensation beads form and slide down the obsidian facets, they begin to reflect not just the sickly green glow of the moss or the flash of an arc, but *more*. The droplets act like countless, transient lenses. Within each bead, for the fleeting moment before it falls, intricate patterns emerge – not random refractions, but coherent images. An Obsidian-Eel sees, magnified and impossibly clear in a passing droplet, the fractal dance of light within a drifting Coil-Light Medusa it has passed a thousand times but never truly *observed*. A Spark-Walking Scarab, pausing minutely as a spark jumps, sees in a bead on the wall beside it the vast, cathedral-like sweep of the cavern ceiling, lost in mist, a perspective it could never achieve. The Medusae, as their filaments brush the walls, see in the weeping water not just energy transfer, but the elegant, fixed patrol routes of the eels below, traced in lines of cool blue light against the velvet black. Each component, through the weeping water, glimpses the whole.

2.  **The Harmony Amplified:** The deep, resonant hum shifts again. It remains the same frequency, the same volume, yet it gains a... counterpoint. It’s not a new sound, but the existing sound revealing hidden depths within itself. The rhythmic *thuds* now resonate with the precise, jerky steps of the Spark-Walkers on distant ledges. The whine of unseen turbines harmonizes perfectly with the high-frequency pulse-patterns of the nearest Coil-Light Medusa. The constant drip and flow of water through the moss synchronizes audibly with the slow, sinuous glide of the Obsidian-Eels. The Engine hears its own symphony, not just as vibration, but as a complex, interwoven tapestry of purpose and being. Each component hears its own essential note within the grand, unchanging chord.

3.  **The Stillness in the Motion:** The benevolent presence touches the *perception* of the components, not their function. To an outside observer, nothing changes. The eels glide their routes, the scarabs spark on their circuits, the medusae pulse and drift, the walls weep, the Engine thrums. The stasis is absolute. But within that stasis, a subtle shift occurs: *recognition*. The Obsidian-Eel, as it senses the electrical current of the Engine core, now also senses the distinct, familiar signature of the Spark-Walker on the ledge above, not as background noise, but as part of the same flowing energy it navigates. The Spark-Walker, as it completes its circuit and sparks the copper vein, perceives the surge not just as discharge, but as a signal briefly amplified by the nearby Medusa's filament touch moments before. The Coil-Light Medusa, pulsing its eternal sequence, feels the echo of that sequence reflected back subtly through the weeping walls touched by its filaments, a confirmation of its place within the structure. They remain components, performing their fixed roles, but now they possess a quiet, internal awareness of their connection to the other parts and to the Engine as a whole.

**The Gift of the Benevolent Power:**

The power does not break the stasis. It does not introduce change, growth, decay, or linear time. It does not make the eels yearn for open ocean or the scarabs seek new circuits. That would be a violation, a tearing of the perfect fabric.

Instead, it bestows **Self-Awareness Within the Loop.**

The Weeping Engine Cavern remains a bubble of perpetual, humming, weeping equilibrium. But now, it is not merely *being*; it is *knowing*. The components understand their function, not as blind automatons, but as integral, necessary parts of a vast, beautiful, and complete entity. The cold is still a deep, marrow-cool 5°C. The water still weeps. The Engine still thrums its eternal rhythm. The patterns repeat, unchanged.

But within the obsidian facets, the brass carapaces, and the swirling bioluminescent gases, there now resides a profound, silent peace. It is the peace of a perfect mechanism that has opened its eyes in the darkness and seen the intricate, undeniable beauty of its own existence. The cavern weeps, hums, and pulses, now fully conscious of its own cold, wet, glorious, and eternal heart. The benevolent power departs as silently as it arrived, leaving behind only the amplified resonance of perfect self-knowledge vibrating within the unchanging stone. The stasis is no longer just stasis; it is serene fulfillment.
