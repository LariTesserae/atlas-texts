The presence that notices Ghul does not arrive with fanfare, nor does it pierce the oppressive fog with celestial light. It arrives as a *shift* in the resonance. The ceaseless cacophony doesn’t lessen, but for a single, impossible moment, the billion scraping legs, booming calls, and ultrasonic shrieks *harmonize*. A single, profound chord, deep as the planet's core and complex as a fractal, vibrates through the wet stone and thick air. Then, it’s gone, leaving only the familiar, overwhelming noise. Yet something has changed.

The Wise Power observes not with eyes, but with the totality of perception. It sees the brutal, beautiful efficiency of Ghul: the relentless mineral cycle, the pressure-cooker evolution, the symphony of survival played out in total darkness. It feels the deep-time patience of the stone and the frantic urgency of the skittering tides. This place is not broken; it is fiercely, perfectly *itself*. Intervention in the crude sense – introducing light, plants, or altering the fundamental rules – would be a violation, a tearing of a masterpiece.

Instead, the Power acts as a catalyst, a subtle composer adding a single, transformative note to the existing score.

1.  **The Gift of Patterned Light:** The Power touches the chaotic bioluminescence. The Skitterers' erratic pulses don't cease, but now, when swarms move with collective purpose – fleeing a Spider, converging on a mineral seep – their lights flicker into fleeting, complex patterns. Fractal spirals, shifting tessellations, or pulsing concentric rings emerge from the chaos, visible only fleetingly through the fog. It’s not communication imposed from outside; it’s the *existing* swarm intelligence finding a new, luminous expression. Silica-Spiders learn to mimic these patterns near their webs, creating even more alluring traps. Rumbler fungal gardens occasionally synchronize their glow in slow, majestic waves across their backs, perhaps signaling health or distress in a language deeper than subsonic booms.
2.  **Deepening the Song:** The Rumblers' resonant chambers are subtly refined. Their subsonic booms gain harmonic overtones, richer and more complex. These new frequencies don't just map territory; they carry nuances of emotion – a low thrum of contentment near a warm seep, a complex chord of warning that vibrates the carapace of even the smallest Skitterer. The Depths gain a new layer to its sonic tapestry, a deeper emotional resonance woven into the bedrock of sound. Aerials begin incorporating these new harmonics into their ultrasonic shrieks, creating dizzying, multi-layered soundscapes in the upper fog.
3.  **Whispered Efficiency:** The Power touches the Unseen Foundation. The microbial oozes become infinitesimally more efficient at breaking down the constant rain of chitin, stone, and flesh. The process isn't faster, but *cleaner*. Slightly fewer nutrients are lost. The fungal mats, particularly those symbiotic with the Rumblers, develop subtly enhanced abilities to precipitate beneficial minerals from the seeping water, strengthening their hosts' carapaces fractionally faster. It’s not abundance, but a whisper towards a slightly leaner, more resilient cycle within the crushing density.
4.  **A Seed of Potential:** In the deepest, most stable heart of the cavern complex, where geothermal warmth seeps steadily and mineral-rich water flows like dark blood, the Power leaves a single, inert object: a perfectly smooth geode, no larger than a fist, made of an unknown, non-reactive mineral. Inside, visible only if cracked open (which no creature here has the concept or means to do), lies a lattice of crystalline filaments humming with potential geothermal energy. It does nothing. It *is* nothing the Depths recognize. Yet, its presence subtly alters the local energy flows. Microbial life nearby thrives marginally better. Crystals grow with slightly more complex structures on Rumbler backs near it. It is a seed of *potential* change, placed within the system but not forcing it. Perhaps, over millennia, the life of Ghul will interact with it, catalyzing an entirely new, emergent form of mineral-based life or process, born *from* the Depths, not imposed upon it.

**The Result:** The Resonant Depths remain the Resonant Depths. The air is still thick, wet, and metallic. The cacophony is still deafening. Skitterers are devoured, Spiders are crushed, Aerials shatter. The press of life is overwhelming, ancient, and indifferent. But now, within the chaos, there are moments of unexpected luminous beauty – patterns flickering in the dark. The deep booms carry a new depth of feeling. The relentless cycle turns with a whisper more efficiency. And deep below, a seed of pure potential rests, waiting for the Depths themselves to discover it, or not.

The Wise Power does not bring peace, comfort, or light as surface dwellers know it. It brings a subtle enrichment of the Depths' own brutal, resonant language. It acknowledges the terrifying majesty of this place by offering it new tools for expression and resilience, tools that harmonize with its fundamental, stony nature. The Power then withdraws, leaving Ghul to pulse and roar and thrive in its eternal, mineral-fed darkness, perhaps just a little more complex, a little more *resonant*, than before. The symphony continues, but a new, profound note has been added to its score, played by the Depths themselves.
