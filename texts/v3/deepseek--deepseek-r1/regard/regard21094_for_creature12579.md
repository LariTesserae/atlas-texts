The being that turns its attention toward the Singing Caldera isn't a god of thunder or a blazing sun-deity. It is **Ondatha**, the **Deep Resonance**, the **Patient Listener**. Its nature is that of slow vibration, profound stillness, and the subtle harmonies woven through water, stone, and time. It perceives the Caldera not just as a place, but as a nascent symphony – the drip, the hum, the settling crack, the low chant – all notes in a composition barely begun. Ondatha is drawn to the raw potential, the exquisite attunement of the Stone-Weavers, and the fragile, resonant beauty of this newborn world.

Here is what Ondatha does:

1.  **Amplifies the Song:** Ondatha doesn't impose a new melody; it gently *tunes* the existing one. The Deep Resonance subtly enhances the natural acoustics of the Caldera bowl. The drip of water gains a purer, more bell-like tone. The wind-hum deepens, vibrating with a richer, more complex harmony that feels less like random air and more like a conscious breath. The resonant stone chimes of the Stone-Weavers ring clearer and carry further with less force, their pure tones lingering longer in the misty air. The Caldera doesn't just sing; it *resonates* with amplified clarity. The inhabitants feel this as a profound sense of rightness, a deepening of the peace they already cultivate. Their dusk chants naturally harmonize with the enriched wind-hum, creating spontaneous, complex polyphonies that feel both ancient and new.

2.  **Whispers in the Weeping Stone:** Ondatha communicates not in words, but in patterns of resonance and condensation. It subtly influences the "weeping" of the rocks. In specific, sheltered locations – perhaps near a communal cistern, or beside a revered giant fern – the constant condensation begins to form more intricate patterns on the stone surfaces. These aren't random beads; they coalesce into fleeting, shimmering fractal designs – spirals, branching veins like frost, concentric circles – visible only for minutes before evaporating, only to reform differently later. These are messages of balance, of flow, of the interconnectedness of water paths. The Stone-Weavers, masters of observation, begin to notice. They perceive these patterns not as commands, but as gentle guidance, insights into the subtle shifts in moisture flow, potential new seep locations, or the health of hidden thermal vents. Their stone channels become even more efficient, mirroring the fractal designs in miniature.

3.  **Blesses the Symbiosis:** Ondatha focuses its subtle power on strengthening the existing bonds within the Caldera's fragile web:
    *   **The Obsidian Flitters:** Their iridescent wings catch the enhanced light differently. When they glide near the weeping rocks or succulent clusters touched by Ondatha's resonance, their wingbeats (though silent) create faint, visible ripples in the air – like heat haze, but cooler and shimmering with faint prismatic colour. These ripples seem to *linger* slightly longer on the surfaces they pass over, leaving a temporary, hyper-charged dew that nourishes the plants more effectively. The Stone-Weavers see this as the Flitters weaving light and moisture together, a sacred dance blessed by the mountain's spirit.
    *   **The Moss-Shelled Grazers:** Their symbiotic moss and cyanobacteria flourish with a healthier, more vibrant glow. The faint luminescence in their mucus trails intensifies slightly and persists longer after their passage, especially near dusk. These glowing trails, tracing slow, deliberate paths across the rocks, become temporary maps of moisture and micro-life, visible only in low light. The Stone-Weavers learn to "read" these paths for insights into hidden damp patches or particularly rich algal beds.
    *   **The Rootless Ones:** The symbiotic colonies within the fern crowns pulse with a very faint, slow light – a deep, cool bioluminescence visible only on the darkest, clearest nights. The water collected from these "spirit-cradles" seems to possess an even greater clarity and a faint, refreshing energy that aids in healing minor ailments or simply brings deep calm. The Stone-Weavers' reverence deepens; collecting this water becomes a silent, moonlit ritual of profound gratitude.

4.  **Gifts the Stone-Weavers with Resonant Insight:** Ondatha doesn't grant sudden technological leaps or magical powers. Instead, it deepens the Stone-Weavers' innate attunement. Over generations, a subtle shift occurs:
    *   Their sense of the stone's "mood" becomes more nuanced. They can feel the deep, slow settling shifts within the bedrock as a subsonic vibration through their feet, allowing them to anticipate minor rockfalls or shifts in spring flow years before they happen.
    *   Their stone-knapping evolves. Obsidian tools crafted after long meditation near the resonating stones or wind-humming spires seem to hold a finer edge, fracture more predictably, and even produce tools that *hum* faintly when struck just right – useful for finding resonant frequencies in new rock or signalling over extreme distances with minimal sound.
    *   Their understanding of water transcends mere capture. They begin to perceive its subtle energies – the difference between "sleeping" water in deep cisterns and "singing" water freshly condensed or flowing from a spring. They learn to place stones or shape channels not just for function, but to enhance the water's inherent resonance, creating miniature, flowing soundscapes within their settlement that promote harmony and well-being.

**The Result:**

The Singing Caldera remains itself – raw, new, vast in its emptiness, slow in its rhythms. But under Ondatha's benevolent attention, its inherent song is amplified and clarified. The harmony between stone, water, life, and the Stone-Weavers deepens into something even more profound. The Caldera doesn't become easier, but it becomes *more itself*. The profound quiet is filled with a richer tapestry of subtle sounds, the light plays with enhanced ethereality on water and obsidian wings, and the sense of a landscape actively *becoming* is infused with a gentle, resonant purpose. The Stone-Weavers remain patient listeners, but now they listen to a symphony more intricate and beautiful than they ever imagined, composed by the land itself and gently conducted by the Deep Resonance. They become not just tenders of the first notes, but conscious participants in the unfolding song of their world. Ondatha's gift is the profound understanding that their patient existence *is* the music, and their careful tending allows the Caldera's unique, embryonic song to resonate out into the universe with pure, clear beauty.
