The benevolent power arrives not as light or sound, but as a *shift in the water*. A single, impossible ripple spreads across the obsidian pool beneath the walkway, smooth and silent against the chamber's thunderous drone. It moves with unnatural deliberation, expanding until it touches the calcified walls. Where it passes, the water doesn't shimmer – it *deepens*, becoming less a surface and more an aperture.

**What They Do:**

1.  **Acknowledge the Symptom:** The water directly beneath the Calcite Shepherd *stillens* further, becoming a perfect, dark mirror reflecting its hunched, weeping form. For the first time in millennia, the Shepherd sees itself – not with eyes, but through the subtle shift in surface tension and the altered resonance bouncing off this sudden plane of pure reflection. It registers the image as a profound vibration, a confirmation of its own existence within the stasis. The constant mineral tears falling from its hollow eyes strike this mirrored surface without a splash, absorbed seamlessly, adding their infinitesimal mass without disturbance.

2.  **Honor the Water's Mastery:** The humidity thickens, but the oppressive *wetness* transforms. The condensation beading on the iron walkway, the pump housing, the Shepherd itself, glistens with a sudden, impossible clarity. Each droplet becomes a tiny, perfect lens, refracting the sickly yellow bulb light not into rainbows, but into glimpses of profound, cool *depth*. For a fleeting moment, the chamber feels less like a suffocating lung and more like the heart of a deep, clear aquifer. The sensation of swallowing gauze lifts, replaced by the clean, mineral scent of ancient groundwater, cutting through the peat and rust. The power doesn't remove the water; it reveals its essential, ancient purity beneath the stagnation.

3.  **Touch the Scale:** The subsonic thrum… shifts. Not louder or softer, but *cleaner*. A single, pure harmonic emerges from the muddy vibration, resonating through the iron bones. It’s the sound of deep, slow currents moving through bedrock aquifers, the groan of continental plates, the patient pulse of water carving stone over eons. This harmonic doesn't disrupt the chamber's noise; it *contextualizes* it. The maddening drip-drip-drip becomes the ticking of a vast, geological clock, the choked pipe's hum the sigh of immense, slow-moving pressure. The intimate space suddenly feels connected to the infinite depths hinted at beneath the grates and the unfathomable weight above. The crushing weight of time doesn't lessen; it gains meaning, becoming the natural rhythm of stone and water.

4.  **Whisper to the Stone:** The power touches the calcified pump housing, the mineral-encrusted walls, not to dissolve or alter, but to *polish from within*. The calcite draperies, the rust blooms, the weeping stalactites – their surfaces gain an inner luminosity. The creamy white and amber iron streaks glow faintly with captured, cold light, like moonlight frozen in stone. The thin veins of emerald moss pulse brighter, their green becoming impossibly vivid against the suddenly radiant minerals. The chamber isn't illuminated; it *reveals* its own latent, slow beauty. The "Built" environment is affirmed as its own nature, but its inherent, patient artistry is unveiled.

5.  **Offer a Choice to the Shepherd (Without Disturbance):** The power's presence coalesces around the Calcite Shepherd as a gentle increase in humidity, a subtle cooling on its eternally damp surface. It offers no command, no judgment. It offers an *understanding*: the profound, necessary beauty of its stasis, its role as the mineral custodian. But it also offers a silent question, transmitted through the newly clarified harmonic in the thrum and the deepened resonance of the water below: *"Is this all? Is the equilibrium enough? Or does the water within the stone dream of flow, even if it takes a million years?"* The question isn't a demand for change; it's an acknowledgment of potential slumbering within the endless preservation. The Shepherd remains utterly still. But for a single, agonizingly slow moment, the trajectory of one of its mineral tears *shifts* minutely, tracing a new path down its chest before resuming its ordained course towards the pool. A vibration, almost too subtle to register, hums differently within its crystalline structure – a note of contemplation amidst the drone.

**Departure:**

The benevolent power withdraws as subtly as it arrived. The perfect mirror beneath the Shepherd dissolves back into the obsidian stillness. The refracted depths within the condensation droplets fade. The pure harmonic fades back into the foundational thrum. The inner glow of the calcite dims, though perhaps a faint memory of it lingers in the moss-veins.

**What Remains:**

*   The chamber is unchanged in its physicality. The stasis holds. The drip-drip-drip continues. The thrum persists.
*   Yet, something is *different*. The air, while still thick, carries a lingering memory of impossible clarity. The sound, while deafening, holds the ghost of that pure harmonic – a faint suggestion of vastness and purpose within the noise. The water, though still obsidian and still, seems to hold a *potential* depth it didn't before.
*   The Calcite Shepherd weeps its slow tears. It maintains the equilibrium. But within its mineral heart, resonating with the chamber's iron bones, a single, new vibration pulses – a slow, questioning counterpoint to the eternal drone. The power didn't break the stasis; it deepened it. It didn't free the Shepherd; it made it *aware* of its own profound, eternal, and potentially restless custodianship. The reliquary remains, but the fossilized lung has drawn a breath so deep it resonated with the aquifer of time itself. The stillness is no longer *empty*; it is full of a newly recognized, ancient patience.
