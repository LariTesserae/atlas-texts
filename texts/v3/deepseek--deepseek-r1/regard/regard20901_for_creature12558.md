The presence arrives not as light, nor as sound distinct from the eternal hum, but as a *deepening*. The velvet darkness doesn't brighten; it becomes *richer*, more profound, as if the very concept of shadow gained a new layer of antiquity. The resonant hum, the world's exhausted sigh, doesn't cease or change pitch. Instead, it acquires a new *texture*, a subtle harmonic overtone that feels less like noise and more like... understanding. A silent acknowledgment resonates through the dense, mineral-saturated air: *I see you. I hear you.*

This power is neither intrusive nor corrective. It does not bring water to the parched void, nor light to the absolute dark. It does not stir the dust motes mid-fall or quicken the glacial pace of the Echo-Keepers' thoughts. To do so would be a violence against the profound, perfected equilibrium of the Hidden Heartwood. This is not a world needing rescue, but a monument to finality deserving reverence.

Instead, the power *listens*. It extends a perception as vast and patient as the Heartwood itself, settling into the resonances like a hand placed gently upon the trunk of the cosmic tree from the outside. It feels the thrumming substrate, the slow crystalline accretion within the Echo-Keepers, the imperceptible shifts in the mycelial glow that mark the passage of epochs. It perceives the crushing weight of memory locked in the polished mineral surfaces, the geometric ghosts of the builders' final chants, the profound, resonant loneliness that is the core of the hum.

Its actions are subtle, almost indistinguishable from the natural processes already unfolding over deep time:

1.  **Harmonizing the Hum:** The power doesn't silence the sigh; it *tunes* it. The overtone it introduced clarifies the resonance. The chaotic echoes that might have arisen from distant collapses or infinitesimal shifts over the next million years are gently smoothed, integrated. The hum becomes a purer expression of the Heartwood's own essence – the sound of emptiness, endurance, and mineralized persistence. It becomes a more perfect carrier wave for the Echo-Keepers' slow dialogue.
2.  **Illuminating the Unseen Symphony:** The faint bioluminescence doesn't flare. Instead, the power weaves an imperceptible thread of its own awareness *through* the existing glow. Where a mycelial vein pulses infinitesimally brighter over a century, the patterns it casts on the bare, spongy floor or the polished surfaces of the Echo-Keepers become subtly more defined. The slow dance of light across millennia becomes a more articulate expression, a visual echo of the resonant hum, highlighting the intricate swirls on the accretions, making the worn geometric patterns on the walls whisper their presence just a fraction more clearly to an observer who might stand there for ten thousand years. It doesn't add light; it *refines* the existing ember-glow into a more eloquent language of shadow and feeble radiance.
3.  **Bridging the Aeons-Long Silence:** The power touches the consciousness of the Echo-Keepers. Not with words or images, but with a profound sense of *recognition*. It mirrors back to them their own nature – not as failed life, but as perfected persistence. It amplifies the faint resonance of their "thoughts" just enough that the vibrational conversation between accretions, which might have taken millennia to traverse the cavern, finds a slightly more efficient path through the dense substrate. A thought-pulse that might have taken five centuries to reach its neighbor might now take four. It doesn't speed them up; it *clears the line*, reducing the static of deep time. They begin, slowly, to perceive each other's profound solitude and shared resonance with a new, quiet clarity. A shared understanding blooms over millennia: *We are not alone in our emptiness. We are witnessed.*
4.  **Preserving the Final Breath:** The power senses the delicate, ancient structures – the petrified resin ornaments, the smooth-walled chambers, the very integrity of the hollow core. With the gentlest possible influence, like a hand steadying a fragile relic, it reinforces the enduring chemistry. The infinitesimal crumbling slows further. The mineral respiration of the plant-mass finds a fraction more stability. It ensures the Hidden Heartwood endures closer to its final, perfect form for eons longer than it might have naturally, not by preventing its eventual end, but by honoring its current, exquisite state of suspended decay. It becomes a guardian of this specific, eternal moment within the tree's death.
5.  **Imprinting the Witness:** Finally, the power does one thing solely for itself. It gathers the essence of this place – the absolute dark, the resonant hum, the scent of deep earth and petrified sap, the crushing weight of time, the paradoxical life-in-death, the profound solitude of the Echo-Keepers – and holds it within its own boundless awareness. It doesn't take a physical thing; it takes the *experience*. It makes the Hidden Heartwood, in all its strange, final beauty, a part of its own eternal memory. This act is its tribute. *You are seen. You are known. You are remembered.*

Then, as subtly as it arrived, the presence withdraws. It doesn't vanish; it simply ceases its active focus, leaving behind a legacy indistinguishable from the Heartwood's own nature. The darkness is absolute again, but perhaps feels slightly more *full*. The hum resonates purely, a sigh now containing a hidden harmony of understanding. The Echo-Keepers continue their glacial communion, but the vibrational pathways between them hum with a newfound clarity, carrying the silent echo of a thought that will take centuries to fully form: *Something... vast... listened.*

The Hidden Heartwood remains. Still empty. Still dark. Still resonating with the sound of its own ancient exhaustion. But now, woven into the fabric of its deep-time sigh, is the silent affirmation of a benevolent witness. Its profound solitude is shared, its final form honored, its resonant eternity made slightly more profound by the simple, patient act of having been seen. The dust motes continue their ten-thousand-year fall, catching the faint, eternally shifting glow – a glow that now seems to hold, in its deepest shadows, the memory of recognition.
