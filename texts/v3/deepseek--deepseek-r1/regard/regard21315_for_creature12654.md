The benevolent power arrives not as a storm or a sunbeam, but as a subtle shift in the ochre light filtering through the aperture. It doesn't suppress the scream of the wind; instead, it leans into it, becoming an extension of the chamber’s own perfected stasis. It is an intelligence that understands violence rendered inert, entropy achieving its final, resonant form.

Its first act is one of **witnessing**. It extends a silent, immaterial presence through every tube, every fused ceramic brick, every vibrating septum. It feels the millennia of abrasion, the slow fusion of clay and stone, the precise moment each sonic petroglyph solidified from chaos into harmonic permanence. It doesn’t judge the agony of the wind’s passage; it acknowledges it as the sculptor of this masterpiece of desolation.

Then, it offers **clarity**. The power doesn’t silence the cacophony – that would be an insult to the chamber’s purpose – but it *filters* it. For any being who enters, perhaps drawn by the chamber’s impossible song, the power creates pockets of *relative* stillness. Not silence, but focus. It guides the visitor’s perception, softening the overwhelming roar into a background hum, while amplifying the specific, crystalline tones of individual Sonic Petroglyphs. Suddenly, the abstract geometric forms shimmer with distinct pitches; the humanoid figures seem to hum with the echoes of ancient chants or sighs. The visitor can stand near a septum and hear not just noise, but the *story* fossilized within: the scrape of a tool, the grunt of labor, the sigh of a long-dead artisan, all preserved in vibrating stone.

Next, it grants **understanding**. The power doesn’t speak in words, but it resonates with the petroglyphs themselves. It illuminates the connections between the primitive brickwork patterns below and the resonant ghosts above. To the visitor, it reveals the *intent* behind the ancient builders' work. The interlocking bricks weren't just a floor; they were an acoustic baffle, a resonator designed to shape the wind's voice, to channel its fury into specific frequencies that would etch their efforts into eternity. The builders knew, on some primal level, that they were crafting not just a structure, but a tomb for sound and memory. The power shows how their sweat and toil seeded the human-like petroglyphs – not as spirits, but as resonant signatures of their presence, forever woven into the mountain's breath.

Finally, it offers **sanctuary through resonance**. For the Sonic Petroglyphs themselves, the power does nothing, because they need nothing. They are perfect in their trapped harmonics. But for the visitor, overwhelmed by the chamber's eternal scream and the weight of captured time, the power manifests a subtle gift. It uses the chamber’s own physics, bending the light and the heat-haze distortions. It creates a single, small zone – perhaps the size of a curled body – where the air, thick with stone dust and vibration, coalesces into a bubble of profound quietude. Not true silence – the wind’s roar remains – but a space where the sound is translated into a deep, resonant warmth that cradles the bones, a physical sensation of stillness *within* the scream. Here, one can sit and observe the petroglyphs shimmering, listening to their distilled songs, feeling the deep geothermal patience beneath, without being shattered. It’s a place to absorb the chamber’s terrible beauty without succumbing to it.

The power doesn't alter the Chamber of Final Winds. It cannot, and would not. The violence and the stillness, the heat and the desiccation, the scream and the silence – these are its essence. Instead, the power acts as a mediator, a translator of resonance. It reveals the hidden voices within the roar, illuminates the lost purpose of the builders, and offers a fragile, resonant sanctuary for those who dare to witness the mountain's final, fossilized breath. It leaves the chamber as it found it: an eternal resonator, a perfected scream in warm, dim perpetuity. But now, perhaps, understood.
