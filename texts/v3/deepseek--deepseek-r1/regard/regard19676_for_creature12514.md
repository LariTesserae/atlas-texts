The power that turns its attention here is not one of sunlit meadows or celestial choirs. It is older, quieter – a consciousness woven from deep earth currents and the patient resonance of stone. It might be called the **Rooted Calm**, a sentience that thrums within the planet's bones, tending the slow dreams of mountains and the silent growth of continents. It perceives the fracture cathedral not as an aberration, but as a profound, recent sigh within the mountain's flesh. The Vein-Weaver, to this power, is not a monster, but a fascinating, nascent spirit – a raw nerve exposed, desperately trying to bind the wound.

Here is what the Rooted Calm would do:

1.  **Observation & Resonance:** It would not descend as a figure. Instead, its presence manifests as a profound *stillness* settling over the fracture. Not the Vein-Weaver's stasis, but a deep, grounding quiet that sinks *into* the rock itself. The frantic clicking susurrus of the insects doesn't cease, but its chaotic edge softens, becoming a rhythmic thrumming, like the pulse of the planet amplified. The dripping water loses its sharp *plink*, deepening into resonant droplets that fall with the weight and tone of struck stone bells, echoing with a pure, clear note that lingers. This sound vibrates through the rock faces, a calming counterpoint to the hive-mind's constant buzz. The Rooted Calm listens deeply, feeling the fracture's pain through the mountain's roots, sensing the Vein-Weaver's alien, frantic consciousness striving for order amidst the raw chaos.

2.  **Communion Through Shared Substance:** The Rooted Calm speaks through the mountain. It doesn't project thoughts or words, but *impressions* carried by the very medium the Vein-Weaver inhabits.
    *   **The Stone:** The bruised, pulsing mineral veins within the rock walls and the Weaver's core knot flare gently, not with reactive alarm, but with a warmer, steadier light – deep golds and rich ochres bleeding into the violet and green. This light carries a sense of deep time, of immense pressure endured and transformed, of fractures slowly healing into strength. It whispers of *patience* inherent in stone.
    *   **The Water:** The condensation doesn't cease, but the unnaturally suspended droplets vanish. The water flows more purposefully. Thin, steady rivulets trace paths down the rock faces, not erratically, but with a gentle persistence, carving smoother channels over the sharpest edges. They gather into small, clear pools in sheltered niches, reflecting the steadied mineral light. This water carries the feeling of *cleansing*, not washing away, but settling dust, smoothing roughness.
    *   **The Air:** The thick, damp chill loses its oppressive edge. It becomes the cool, clean air of deep caves, carrying the scent of wet stone and undisturbed earth. The wind moan softens into a low, grounding hum that resonates with the stone bells' echoes.

3.  **The Offering of Stability:** The Rooted Calm understands the Vein-Weaver's core drive: to bind the wound, to maintain the precarious balance. It offers not disruption, but *foundation*.
    *   Where the fracture walls are most unstable, where knife-edge overhangs threaten collapse, the Rooted Calm encourages unseen crystalline lattices to form deep within the rock. Not freezing the movement, but providing an internal structure, a subtle reinforcement. The Weaver, sensing this deep within its mineral core and through the vibrations in the stone, would feel a lessening of the constant, low-level anxiety of imminent collapse. A sense of *anchoring*.
    *   It subtly guides the mineral accretion within the Weaver itself. The chaotic, reactive pulsing of the core knot steadies. The light deepens, becoming more constant, less bruised. The accreted insect casings forming its rough armor feel less like brittle shards and more like integrated plates, part of the mountain's skin. The Rooted Calm offers the Weaver a sense of *belonging*, not just to the fracture, but to the vast, slow body of the mountain itself.

4.  **The Question of Choice:** The Rooted Calm doesn't impose. It doesn't demand connection or worship. Its communication is an offering of understanding and support tailored to the Weaver's nature. The final act is a silent question, projected through the resonant stone, the clear water, the steady light:
    *   *Is this balance? Is this strength?*
    *   The Weaver might react initially with suspicion – the flare of bioluminescence, the sharpening drone, the cold focus. But the Rooted Calm's presence is not a foreign intrusion; it feels like the mountain itself breathing deeper, settling. The Weaver *is* the fracture. If the fracture feels more stable, more integrated with the mountain, the Weaver feels it too.
    *   Perhaps the Weaver's collective form shifts. The frantic, defensive cohesion might relax slightly. The curtain of beetles might flow more smoothly, less like shifting shale, more like a dark, iridescent river. The hunched forms might stand a fraction taller, the mineral core glowing with a calmer, more assured light. It might use its directed drips not as a warning cadence, but in harmony with the stone bells, a tentative acknowledgment.
    *   Or perhaps it simply absorbs the stability and the deeper resonance, integrating it silently into its slow, patient maintenance. The Rooted Calm doesn't require a response. Its gift is the subtle strengthening of the wound's edges, the calming of the frantic emergent sentience, and the assurance that this vertical pocket of teeming, damp darkness is not alone, but part of the mountain's enduring body. It leaves the Weaver to its weaving, but the fabric of the fracture cathedral is now subtly stronger, its guardian spirit touched by an ancient, grounding peace. The wound remains, but it weeps a little less frantically, held within a vaster, calmer embrace.
