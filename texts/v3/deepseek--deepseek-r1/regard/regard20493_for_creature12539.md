The power that turns its attention here is not a god of storms or sunlight, but something quieter, older – an intelligence born of deep time and patient stone, perhaps the consciousness of tectonics itself, or the slow dream of a stable planet. It observes the Sunken Garden not with judgment, but with a profound sense of *recognition*. Here is a place that has achieved, through eons of struggle and adaptation, a state of near-perfect equilibrium, a jewel of persistence in the dry bones of the world. The Ashen-Kin are not merely inhabitants; they are the grove become self-aware, its caretakers and its voice.

Its intervention is not a dramatic reshaping, but an act of profound *reinforcement*, a whisper of affirmation woven into the existing fabric:

1.  **Strengthening the Whisper:** The power focuses on the Heart-Stone. It doesn't increase the geothermal warmth – that could destabilize the delicate moisture balance. Instead, it subtly enhances the *efficiency* of the capillary action within the pumice. Microscopic channels become infinitesimally smoother, less prone to clogging over millennia. The non-aqueous moisture wicks with a fraction more consistency, ensuring the grove's vital flow remains uninterrupted for countless more generations. To the Root-Speakers, this might manifest as a new, ultra-deep harmonic in the Heart-Stone's thrum – a resonance of profound stability, a sense of the foundation being *more* secure. They might interpret it as the mountain settling deeper into contentment.

2.  **A Gift of Light and Memory:** The power observes the glow-orbs, the bioluminescent gnats and fungi. It introduces, not an alien light, but a subtle symbiosis. A new, incredibly slow-growing strain of luminous fungus appears, deep within the oldest, most sheltered moss-gardens. This fungus doesn't outcompete existing species; it integrates. Its light is softer, more persistent, and possesses a unique property: it *absorbs* sound vibrations very faintly, particularly the rhythmic chants and chitin-drum pulses of the Ashen-Kin. Over decades, the fungus colonies growing on significant structures – the Root-Speaker's dwelling, the main gathering platform – begin to emit a faint, stored echo of these sounds when gently warmed by hand or breath. It becomes a living archive, a way for the stories, the chants, the very *sound* of their culture to be preserved within the grove itself, accessible only through patient communion. The Ashen-Kin would see this as the grove learning to sing their songs back to them, a sacred reciprocity.

3.  **Deepening the Stone-Telling:** The power strengthens the connection between the Ashen-Kin and the Heart-Stone during the ritual of Stone-Telling. When palms press against the porous rock with sincere intent, the subtle vibrations transmitted through the mountain's bones become slightly clearer, carrying not just a sense of "rightness" or "wrongness," but fleeting, impressionistic glimpses. Not visions, but *feelings*: the slow grind of continents far below, the patient flow of deep aquifers (even if dry here), the immense age and stability of the rock itself. It reinforces their understanding of deep time and their place within it, offering a broader perspective that still aligns with their core belief in the stone's wisdom. Disputants wouldn't receive commands, but a profound sense of scale that makes petty conflicts feel insignificant, washed away by the ocean of time.

4.  **The Unseen Shield:** The benevolent power's most crucial act is one of protection, not through walls or weapons, but through *concealment* and *discouragement*. It weaves a subtle field of profound, resonant *stillness* around the Last Peak. To any approaching with disruptive intent – greed, conquest, careless curiosity – the mountain feels… impossibly distant. Compasses subtly spin, maps seem to shift, the sheer cliffs appear even more impassable, the air thicker, more lethargic. A profound sense of *irrelevance* settles upon intruders; their goals feel trivial, their energy sapped by the weight of deep time. They become disoriented, discouraged, and ultimately turn away, forgetting *why* they sought the peak, attributing it to fatigue or folly. To the Ashen-Kin, this manifests only as a continued, blessed absence. The silence they fear – the silence of disruption – simply never comes. The grove's hum remains uninterrupted.

**The Result:** The Sunken Garden and the Ashen-Kin continue as they are, but *more so*. Their equilibrium is more resilient, their connection to their world deepened, their cultural memory subtly preserved within the living fabric of the grove. The benevolent power doesn't seek to change them, to "elevate" them, or to make them known. It sees their existence – this slow, intricate dance of life, stone, and time within the mountain's heart – as a rare and precious state, a testament to endurance and symbiosis. Its intervention is the gentle hand of a curator ensuring a perfect, fragile artifact endures within its optimal environment. The Ashen-Kin remain the Stone's Breath, moving at the speed of moss, their world a secret, vibrant heart beating in rhythm with the planet's oldest bones, now watched over by a guardian as patient as the stone itself. The only sign of the power's attention is a feeling, perhaps on the stillest of dusks, that the twilight is not an ending, but a deep, contented sigh.
