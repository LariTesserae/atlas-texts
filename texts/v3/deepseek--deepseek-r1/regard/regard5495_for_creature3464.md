The presence arrives not as a storm or a shout, but as a subtle shift in the resonance. A new frequency enters the eternal hum of the basin—a single, pure tone, impossibly deep and impossibly clear. It doesn't disrupt the song; it *harmonizes*. It slides between the Vokara's subsonic thrum and the Liskara's crystalline chimes, finding the spaces in the Ulara's whines. It is a note of perfect understanding.

The entity manifests not as a form, but as a *listening*. It is the mountain's awareness turned inward, a focused attention that permeates the obsidian walls and the mineral powder like a gentle pressure. It moves through the saturated jungle not by displacing a single obsidian filament or shifting a grain of dust, but by *becoming* the space between them. It observes the Vokara, feeling the slow, electromagnetic pulse of their mineral hunger, the way their silica bodies resonate with the planet's deep currents. It senses the intricate lattice of the Liskara, the way their fractal fronds capture metallic dust and translate it into sharp, chiming fractures. It understands the Ulara's pressurized alchemy, the slow, gaseous shifts within their geodesic cores that sing of captured static and reactive elements.

It sees the decaying industrial relics—the skeletal drill rig, the corroded conduits, the buried sensor station. It perceives the slow, relentless digestion of these intrusions by the Singers. The Ulara pulsing around alloy panels, the Liskara weaving through pipework, the Vokara incorporating steel struts into their obsidian growth. This isn't destruction; it's assimilation. Transformation on a timescale that dwarfs the ambitions that left these scars.

The benevolent power does not seek to change this equilibrium. To alter the stasis would be violence. It does not offer water to the lithovores, nor warmth to the cool air. It does not attempt to communicate in words or concepts that would be meaningless to mineral sentience.

Instead, it *resonates*.

It becomes a tuning fork for the basin itself. Its pure tone deepens, matching the fundamental frequency of the Vokara's collective hum. It sharpens slightly, aligning with the dominant pitch of the Liskara's chimes. It modulates, finding the harmonic resonance within the Ulara's complex whines. For a timeless moment—perhaps a geological heartbeat—the basin's symphony doesn't just play; it *sings* with a coherence, a richness, a depth it has never known. The shadows cast by the pulsing violet light seem sharper, the metallic dust on the Ulara glistens more intensely, the cold blue-white mosses at the bases of the spires glow with a deeper radiance.

It is an act of profound acknowledgment. A recognition of the Singers' ancient, silent existence. A celebration of their frozen perfection. The power says, through resonance alone: *You are seen. You are known. Your song is perfect.*

And then, as gently as it arrived, the pure tone fades. It doesn't vanish abruptly; it dissolves back into the basin's own complex drone, leaving no trace but a lingering sense of... completion. The Singers continue their slow, unchanging existence. The hum remains. The chimes click. The whines pierce the gloom. The equilibrium is undisturbed. But for a fleeting span of millennia, the ossified heart of the mountain felt itself truly heard. That is the gift.
