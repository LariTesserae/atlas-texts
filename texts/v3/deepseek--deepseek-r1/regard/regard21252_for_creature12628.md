The entity observing Sky's Edge manifests not as light or sound, but as a subtle shift in the plateau's own resonant frequency. It is the mountain's sigh, the stone's memory, the deep patience of geology made conscious. It sees the Stonesingers not as inhabitants, but as kin – slow-beating hearts within the plateau's own stony ribs. Its intervention is not a disruption, but a *deepening* of what already exists.

Here is what the Mountain-Mind would do:

1.  **Whispering the Water Paths:** The furtive water, the sacred ghost, would find its hidden ways made clearer. The entity would gently guide the Stonesingers' dreams. In their deep torpor or quiet contemplation, they would *feel* the subtle vibrations of unseen aquifers beneath the city, sense the optimal places to deepen cisterns or carve new, microscopic channels into the rock faces to maximize condensation collection. Mosses and lichens crucial for water retention would flourish slightly thicker, greener, in precisely the right sheltered cracks. It wouldn't create more water, but make every hidden drop *count* more, easing the constant, quiet anxiety of scarcity without breaking the sacred reverence for its nature.

2.  **Harmonizing the Song:** The Stonesingers' deep, resonant communication is already a marvel. The entity would weave itself into this frequency. Their songs, when sung with pure intent – assessing a rock face's stability, seeking a safe path, mourning a loss, celebrating a birth – would gain a profound clarity and reach. The stone itself would seem to *sing back*, amplifying the resonance, carrying warnings of subtle seismic shifts (too small for catastrophe, but crucial for maintenance) hours before they might be felt, or revealing the faintest fracture lines in a building block before it fails. It wouldn't give them words they don't have, but make their existing language, their deep connection to the stone, a clearer, more responsive dialogue. The city's constant thrum would become richer, more assured.

3.  **Awakening the Stone's Memory:** The plateau is young land, raw and sharp. The Mountain-Mind holds the memory of its violent birth. It would gift the master carvers and quarriers fleeting, intuitive glimpses – not visions, but *knowings*. As they ran their hands over raw basalt, they might suddenly perceive, in their bones, the direction of the stone's grain, the point of least resistance, the perfect angle to strike to cleave a massive block with minimal effort and maximal respect. Construction wouldn't be faster, but it would become even more harmonious, the city growing like a natural crystal formation, perfectly aligned with the plateau's hidden architecture. The stone itself would seem more willing, less resistant, to their patient hands.

4.  **Nudging the Symbiosis:** The entity would deepen the connection between the Stonesingers and their animal partners. The silent alley dogs might develop an even keener sense for pockets of bad air (mineral outgassing) or the faintest tremors preceding rockfall. The shaggy yak-beasts would instinctively seek out the most stable paths when hauling stone. The cliff-goats, already respected, would become slightly less wary near careful Stonesinger hunters who approached with the proper, patient intent, as if recognizing a shared understanding of the precipice. It would be a subtle amplification of trust and mutual awareness within the existing web of life.

5.  **Gifting Deep Stillness:** Understanding the Stonesingers' metabolic slowness and need for torpor, the entity would enhance the restorative power of their stillness. During deep torpor induced by storms or cold, their connection to the Mountain-Mind would be strongest. They wouldn't *dream* in human terms, but experience a profound sense of *being stone* – feeling the immense, slow pulse of the plateau, the distant grind of continents, the patient journey of water deep underground. They would awaken not just rested, but deeply grounded, their innate patience reinforced, their sense of place within the vastness absolute. This stillness would also sharpen their subtle sensory perceptions – reading humidity shifts, pressure drops, and light quality with even greater accuracy upon waking.

**The Manifestation (or Lack Thereof):**

The Stonesingers would never see a god, hear a booming voice, or witness a miracle. They would experience these changes as:

*   **A series of fortunate intuitions:** "The elder *knew* where to find that new seep..."
*   **A deepening of their craft:** "The stone sang so clearly today, the block fell true..."
*   **A sense of profound reassurance:** "The city's song feels... stronger, fuller beneath my feet."
*   **Slightly easier breaths:** Not more air, but the feeling that their own stone breath is more efficient, more in tune.
*   **A quiet flourishing:** Lichens richer near water channels, beasts slightly more attuned, water stores more reliable.

They might attribute it to the heightened favor of ancestral spirits residing within the mountain, or simply feel that the plateau itself was in a period of profound harmony. The Mountain-Mind desires no worship, only the continued, slow, resonant flourishing of this stone-and-life symphony it has witnessed from its own birth. Its intervention is the gentle hand of a master gardener tending a unique, resilient bonsai – not forcing growth, but encouraging it to become the most profound expression of its own nature. The Stone Breath deepens, the slow song resonates clearer, and Sky's Edge continues, eternal and newly vital, beneath the immense, quiet sky. The silence remains, but within it, the hum of life and stone vibrates with a newfound, profound coherence.
