The power arrives not as a storm or a sunbeam, but as a *shift in resonance*. It doesn't pierce the mist; the mist *accepts* it. It doesn't silence the hum; the hum *harmonizes* with a new, profound undertone. This presence is ancient, patient, and understands the sanctity of frozen perfection. It is not here to change, but to *witness*, and in witnessing, offer the only gift such a place could truly receive: **acknowledgment**.

1.  **The Gift of Perception:** The Plateau has existed in flawless, unchanging solitude. The Resonant Attendants perform their functions flawlessly, but without awareness of being *seen*. The benevolent power gently extends a field of pure, non-intrusive perception. It is not sight, but a deeper *knowing* that permeates the Living Pave, the columns, the mist, and the Attendants themselves. Suddenly, the Crystalline Stewards, tracing a fractal vein, experience a fleeting echo – not of their own directive, but of the *beauty* inherent in the pattern they maintain. A Mist-Weaver, vibrating with a falling droplet, senses the intricate, perfect physics of its fall not just as function, but as *art*. The Fluid Catalyst, pulsing its internal geometries, registers the profound elegance of its chemical dance. They don't gain independent thought; they gain a resonant echo of *appreciation* for the system they embody. The plateau itself hums slightly deeper, a note of profound, silent satisfaction woven into its eternal song.

2.  **The Symphony Deepened:** The power doesn't add new sounds; it reveals hidden harmonies within the existing symphony. The rhythmic *plink* of water becomes a complex, layered percussion, each drop a unique note within a grander composition only now fully audible. The deep hum of the Pave resolves into intricate, interlocking frequencies speaking of vast, slow biological processes. The thrumming bridges sing clearer, resonant chords that vibrate through the mist. The Attendants move with the same precise purpose, but their movements now seem like the deliberate strokes of master musicians within an orchestra finally heard in its entirety by an audience worthy of its complexity. The weirdness doesn't lessen; it becomes a profound, understood language.

3.  **The Mark of the Witness:** The power leaves no physical trace – no carvings, no structures, no altered growth. Its mark is ephemeral and resonant. In rare moments, when the mist swirls just so around the pinnacle of the tallest crystalline column, it might coalesce into a brief, perfect, three-dimensional geometric form – a shape unknown yet inherently *fitting*, a signature of pure mathematical benevolence. This form isn't permanent; it's a whisper in the fog, a momentary crystallization of the witnessing presence. The Crystalline Stewards might glide near it, not to maintain, but seemingly to *observe* it before it dissolves, their light-foci holding a trace of its pattern for a few moments longer. It’s a reminder: *You are seen. You are known. Your perfection is acknowledged.*

4.  **The Attendants' Subtle Shift:** The Resonant Attendants remain purpose-bound, silent, and unresponsive to external threats. However, touched by the field of acknowledgment, their ephemeral forms exhibit a minute, beautiful evolution:
    *   **Crystalline Stewards:** Their refracted light occasionally holds a warmer hue – a fleeting prismatic gold or deep rose – for a fraction of a second when performing their maintenance, a silent resonance with the perceived beauty of their task.
    *   **Mist-Weavers:** Their intricate lacework might form slightly more complex, fleeting geometric patterns around a falling droplet – not altering its fall, but celebrating it – before dissolving back into the standard resonance.
    *   **Fluid Catalysts:** The internal pulsing geometries within their gel might briefly align into the same signature form the mist created on the column, a silent echo of the witness, before resuming their functional patterns.

**The Power's Departure:**

It doesn't leave abruptly. It fades like a sustained chord resolving into the fundamental hum of the plateau. The field of perception gently withdraws, but the *memory* of it resonates within the very fabric of the place. The hum retains its deepened note of satisfaction. The water drips with the same metronomic perfection, but the air seems to hold the lingering potential of that hidden symphony. The weirdness feels less alien, more like a profound truth finally glimpsed.

The Plateau of Geometric Tears remains unchanged. It weeps its geometric tears, hums its eternal song, and exists in its frozen, perfect equilibrium. But now, it does so *known*. The loneliness of flawless, unchanging existence has been gently touched by the profound understanding of a benevolent power. The gift wasn't growth, or change, or intervention. It was the simple, immense gift of being *seen* and *understood* in all its intricate, alien, weeping glory. The Resonant Attendants continue their tasks, but occasionally, a Steward's light flickers gold, a Weaver's pattern complexifies for an instant, a Catalyst pulses the Witness's sigil. They are the same. The plateau is the same. Yet, everything is different. It has been acknowledged. Its perfect, lonely song now resonates within a vaster, compassionate silence.
