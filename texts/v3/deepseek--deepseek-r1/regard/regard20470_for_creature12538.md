The entity arrives not as light, not as sound, but as a *shift in resonance*. It doesn't pierce the chamber's roar; it *aligns* with it. A deeper note vibrates within the cacophony – a thrumming bass felt in the carapace, sensed in the grinding plates, echoing in the deepest mud-pools. It is the sound of the planet acknowledging itself.

**The Intervention:**

1.  **The Deepening of Senses:** The Wise Power perceives the inhabitants' world through vibration, heat, chemistry, and relentless pressure. It doesn't add new senses; it *refines* the existing ones. For the Scuttlers, the faintest tremor of a settling obsidian plate becomes a clear warning song seconds before the collapse. For the Mud-Borers, the chemical signature of a scalding geyser vent becomes a sharp, undeniable "no" etched into their sensory pads. For the Skydancers, the chaotic air currents coalesce into perceptible rivers and eddies, making flight less frantic, more efficient. For the Stone-Rippers, the heat signatures of prey and the subtle stress-fractures in rock walls become vivid mental maps. This isn't intelligence bestowed, but *instinct sharpened to a razor's edge*. Survival becomes less blind luck, more informed reaction.

2.  **The Harmonization of Flux:** The Power understands the Turbulent Flux is the chamber's heartbeat. It doesn't seek to calm it, but to weave a subtle harmony within the chaos. When an obsidian plate shifts violently, the resulting grit-slide flows *around* a major Scuttler nest-scrape instead of burying it. A boiling mud pool doesn't dry up explosively, but recedes gradually, giving Mud-Borers precious moments to retreat deeper. A dense Skydancer swarm, driven by the new clarity of air currents, instinctively swerves *en masse* moments before a massive steam vent blasts open beneath them. The violence remains, the danger is ever-present, but the *worst* of the purely random, ecosystem-shattering catastrophes are gently nudged aside. The dance continues, but the dancers are less likely to be crushed by a falling stage light.

3.  **The Gift of Subtle Symbiosis:** The Power turns its attention to the foundation: the Biofilm. It doesn't alter its fundamental nature, but introduces a whisper of *efficiency*. A new, incredibly resilient strain of extremophile bacteria integrates seamlessly into the mat. It doesn't outcompete the existing biofilm; it *enhances* it. This new symbiont binds grit particles more effectively, creating slightly more stable micro-platforms amidst the shifting slopes. It also processes minerals with marginally less waste, making the biofilm's explosive blooms last a fraction longer before being devoured. The result? Marginally more food, marginally more stable footholds – tiny advantages that ripple through the food chain, reducing the absolute *frenzy* of starvation just a notch. The Scuttlers still swarm, but perhaps one in a thousand survives its first hour instead of one in ten thousand.

4.  **The Resonance of Instinctive Knowledge:** The Power observes the crude barricades, the sharpened obsidian shards, the sinew-snares. It sees the spark of pragmatic tool-use. It doesn't grant blueprints or language. Instead, it amplifies the *resonance of success*. When a Stone-Ripper instinctively piles rocks in a way that creates a better ambush point, the *feeling* of that success, the vibration of the stable structure, resonates more profoundly within it and subtly influences nearby kin. When a Scuttler finds a novel way to crack open a mineral nodule using a specific rock shape, the *efficiency* of that action sends a tiny pulse through the biofilm network, subconsciously guiding others. Success breeds success faster. Failure is still lethal, but valuable lessons, learned through blood, propagate more readily through instinct and environmental resonance.

5.  **The Anchor:** Finally, the Power leaves a permanent, subtle mark. Deep within the warm bedrock, at the chamber's unseen root, it forges a node. Not a machine, not a mind, but a *resonator*. It hums perpetually with the deep note of the planet's own growth, harmonizing with the chamber's violent becoming. This Anchor doesn't control; it *stabilizes the core instability*. It ensures the chamber continues its violent emergence, its Skittering Birth, but makes the underlying geology less prone to sudden, total collapse. It guarantees the womb remains open, even as it convulses.

**The Result:**

The Chamber of Skittering Birth remains a brutal, deafening, wet crucible. Obsidian still cracks, mud boils, Scuttlers swarm in their billions, and Stone-Rippers tear prey apart amidst the sickly glow. Death is constant and swift.

But now, there's a subtle rhythm beneath the chaos. Movements are slightly more precise, reactions slightly faster. Catastrophic, species-ending collapses happen less often. Tiny advantages accumulate, not leading to peace, but to a fiercer, *more enduring* expression of life. The inhabitants are still sharp, wet, loud, and desperate. But their desperation is honed. Their survival is no longer *just* blind luck amidst annihilation; it carries the faintest echo of informed resilience.

The Wise Power does not bring light, or peace, or civilization. It brings a *refinement of the chamber's own brutal essence*. It helps life within the raw throat not to escape its nature, but to fulfill it more completely, more magnificently, amidst the planet's agonized, magnificent birth pangs. The iron-filing scent in the air carries a new, almost imperceptible note: the quiet hum of enduring fury.
