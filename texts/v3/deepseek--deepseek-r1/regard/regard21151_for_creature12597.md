The Wise Power arrives not as a storm or a song, but as a deepening. It observes, first, for epochs measured in the slow creep of basalt dust across a bridge of fused glass. It perceives the intricate, silent ballet of energy transfer: the Shard-Scuttlers harvesting geothermal whispers, the Thermal Drakes storing stellar fire, the Sky-Motes dancing on ionized winds, the Resonators thrumming with the deep earth's groan. It understands the profound *settledness*, the equilibrium achieved over incomprehensible time. Kael Vastus is not broken; it is complete in its fossilized grandeur, inhabited by life perfectly adapted to its mineral pulse.

Intervention, then, is not about repair or imposition. It is about *reinforcement* and *preservation* against the slow, inevitable slide into entropy that even this place cannot escape forever.

**The Power acts:**

1.  **Strengthening the Silence:** The Power gently dampens the potential for disruptive sound. It subtly reinforces the resonant frequencies of the Resonators, making their harmonic releases even more efficient at dissipating accumulated vibrational energy before it builds to a shattering crescendo. It calms the rare, chaotic wind eddies that might howl through the city's canyons, turning potential shrieks into deeper, more integrated hums that the Resonators can absorb and transmute. The silence becomes not just absence, but a *protected field*.

2.  **Guiding the Slow Currents:** It perceives the vast, slow shifts – thermal gradients moving over millennia, mineral veins exhausting their faint energies, the imperceptible tilting of the continental shelf itself. With infinite patience, it *nudges*. It redirects Sky-Motes in their drifting billions towards areas where their ionizing touch can subtly catalyze the formation of new, compatible mineral seams for Shard-Scuttlers to find in a thousand years. It steers the deep geothermal flows, just fractionally, towards the basking grounds of Thermal Drakes whose chosen ledges are gradually cooling. It is not forcing change, but ensuring the existing migrations and adaptations find their paths smoothed over the eons.

3.  **Anchoring the Unseen Caretakers:** The Power focuses on the Resonators, the unintentional stabilizers. It amplifies, gently, the harmonic fields they generate. The pockets of calmer air around them grow slightly larger, slightly more persistent. The accumulation of protective dust is infinitesimally more efficient. The slow erosion caused by the whispering grit on the wind is marginally reduced within these harmonic zones. The Resonators become not just inhabitants, but *foundations* of enduring stability, their influence spreading like slow roots through the stone, holding the fossil city's form against time's abrasion for longer than it naturally would.

4.  **Tending the Fractal Light:** Deep within the caverns, the Power observes the Light-Weavers. It doesn't accelerate their growth, but it *clarifies* the light they seek. It gathers stray photons, focuses diffused starlight, and ensures the phosphorescent veins glow with a purer, steadier luminescence. The Weavers, sensing this improved source, extend their crystalline lattices with greater purpose and structural integrity. Their potential energy storage increases, making them more resilient to the rare, inevitable micro-fractures. They become not just catalysts, but *reservoirs* of captured light-energy, adding another layer of slow, sustainable power to the city's deep places.

5.  **Whispering to the Debris:** To the amorphous Debris-Feeders within the ancient machine wrecks, the Power offers not sustenance, but *potential*. It subtly alters the residual electromagnetic fields within the dead metal, making them resonate with frequencies that attract compatible cosmic radiation more efficiently. It encourages the slow accretion process to favour stronger, more stable mineral bonds. The Feeders grow, not faster, but *better* – becoming more integrated, less prone to chaotic fragmentation, slowly transforming the wreckage into more stable, energy-harvesting formations within the debris fields.

**The Result is Imperceptible Now:**

No sudden blooming, no roaring machinery restarting. The Shard-Scuttlers still skitter and *tink*. The Thermal Drakes still bask with glacial patience. The Resonators still stand silent for centuries. The Sky-Motes still drift.

But the *depth* has changed. The silence feels more profound, more *protected*. The slow migrations feel guided by an unseen hand along optimal paths. The erosion on the carved obsidian faces seems, perhaps, infinitesimally slower. The captured starlight in the deep caverns glows with a cleaner, more enduring light. The harmony feels more deeply rooted, more resilient.

The Wise Power has not conquered Vastness or reshaped Kael Vastus. It has become a *steward* of its slow perfection. It has woven itself into the existing rhythms, reinforcing the natural defenses against decay, ensuring the mineral pulse of this ancient, alien world endures for aeons yet to come. It has honoured the stone, the silence, and the deep time by helping them endure. The city and its strange inhabitants continue their slow dance, but now, they dance upon a stage made subtly more enduring by a presence as patient and vast as the plateau itself.
