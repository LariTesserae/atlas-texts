The power manifests not as light or sound – those are surface things – but as a *shift in pressure*. A subtle, pervasive reconfiguration of the deep currents themselves. It understands the ancient language of force and density, the slow grammar of the abyss.

Its intervention is a gentle *steering*.

**For the Ventshrouds:** The chaotic shearing currents that flay their fragile edges are calmed, not stopped, but *guided*. The violence flows *around* the vents, creating pockets of comparative stillness within the thermal updrafts. The mineral-laden plumes rise straighter, less turbulent, allowing the grey veils to grow denser, their chemosynthetic processes slightly more efficient. Their faint glow becomes a fraction brighter, a constellation of spectral bruises marking sanctuary in the dark. It is the difference between clinging and thriving, measured in millimeters over centuries.

**For the Chalk Grubs:** The deep groans of shifting chalk, which drive them into panic or crush them in unseen collapses, are anticipated. The power subtly influences the immense, slow stresses within the plain. It doesn't prevent movement – the chalk must settle – but it encourages it along paths of least resistance, away from the densest burrowing zones. The Grubs sense the change, a lessening of the background dread. Their burrowing becomes slightly less frantic, their filtering slightly more productive. They leave their meandering tunnels with a little more confidence.

**For the Pressure Skate:** The power addresses the crushing loneliness and the peril of the currents. It doesn't create companions, but it *guides the paths*. The immense, slow-motion maelstroms are subtly deflected, creating wider, calmer corridors between them. The Skate, gliding with its exquisite senses, finds its vast territory slightly less treacherous. The chance encounters – the faint vibration of a Grub, the scent of carrion – become slightly more frequent. The terrifying sweeps into anoxic zones are rarer. And when the Skate is startled, its defensive flash? The power *holds* the light. Not amplifying it, but allowing its brief, shocking bioluminescence to linger a fraction longer in the water, a momentary beacon that says, *I am here*. It is a tiny affirmation in the crushing dark, a signal that doesn't attract predators, but simply... exists.

**The Interplay:** The gentler currents around the vents mean Grubs can graze closer to the Ventshrouds' richer microbial zones with less risk of being swept away. The more stable chalk allows for slightly denser Grub populations. The Skate, navigating safer paths, finds these pockets of abundance more reliably. The entire fragile web becomes a little more robust, a little more resilient against the constant background threat of the churn. The violence is still there, the pressure immense, the cold eternal – but the *balance* tips, ever so slightly, towards persistence.

**The Signature:** There is no monument, no physical mark. The only evidence is in the patterns: the straighter vent plumes, the slightly more complex networks of Grub tunnels that persist longer before being smoothed away, the faint afterglow of a Skate's alarm that hangs in the water like a fading star. The benevolent power works *with* the Churn Depths, not against it. It understands that this world's beauty lies in its harsh, ancient equilibrium. Its gift is not salvation, but *endurance*. It allows the whispers of life to resonate a little longer in the mineral eternity, a testament to persistence written in pressure and pale light. The Depths remain cold, dark, and indifferent – but within them, the fugitives find a fraction more shelter from the ceaseless churn.
