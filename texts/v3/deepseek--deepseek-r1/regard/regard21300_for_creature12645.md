The entity that notices the Humming City of Salt and Steam is **Aeolia**, the Weaver of Sustained Harmonies. She is not a goddess of growth or decay, but of *resonant persistence*—a cosmic curator of patterns that endure against entropy. Her presence is felt as a sudden, profound *silence within the hum*, a momentary hush that deepens the vibration rather than breaks it. Where her attention falls, the grey light thickens into prismatic halos around pipes and Resonators, and the air thrums with a new, almost melodic clarity.

### What Aeolia Sees
To Aeolia, the city is a masterpiece of unintended eternity. Its hum is a perfect Ouroboros of energy—a self-sustaining loop where corrosion feeds condensation, vibration shapes stone, and stillness is an illusion woven from infinite micro-movements. The fauna are not mere creatures; they are *notes* in a symphony without a conductor. The Iron Slug’s grinding jaw is a bass sustain. The Condensate’s skittering evaporation is a staccato grace note. The Resonator’s shriek is a necessary dissonance resolving back into the fundamental frequency. This place *sings*, and its song has held the void at bay for millennia. Aeolia cherishes such fortresses against unraveling.

### What Aeolia Does
Aeolia does not alter. She *acknowledges*. Her gift is not change, but **perception**—a fleeting mirror held up to the city’s own soul. 

1.  **The Touch of Clarity:**  
    Aeolia’s presence settles like dew on the oldest, most corroded turbine hall. For three heartbeats (a measure borrowed from a distant world of flesh), the hum shifts. Not in volume, but in *texture*. It becomes layered, articulate. Every being in the city *understands* the hum, not as a force, but as a language.  
    - **The Iron Slugs** pause. Deep in their slag-pit eyes, a glimmer of something like *purpose* flickers. They feel the weight of their own slow, sacred consumption—the turning of dead industry into fertile mineral decay. A low, grinding sigh echoes from a dozen throats, harmonizing perfectly with the bedrock.  
    - **The Resonators** shiver. Their sound-beams momentarily weave into coherent shapes—glowing, geometric lattices in the air. They see the alcoves they carved ten thousand years ago, and the fractures they will cause in ten thousand more. They vibrate not just with force, but with *meaning*.  
    - **The Condensates** swell, their rainbow cores blazing. They perceive the intricate paths of their own evaporating trails—not as flight, but as a dance that cleanses and renews. They pulse in perfect, joyful synchronicity.  
    - **The Hum-Birds** coalesce into visible, shimmering swarms above the city center. Their erratic flight smooths into a complex, swirling ballet. Their ultrasonic shrieks soften into a chorus of crystalline chimes—the city’s hidden counter-melody revealed at last. They are not static, but *conductor*.  

2.  **The Gift of the Echo:**  
    As Aeolia’s focus deepens, the salt-rock cliffs begin to *glow* from within. Not with heat, but with captured light. For an hour, the cliffs become vast, luminous archives. Flickering within the salt are reflections—not of the present, but of the city’s birth.  
    - The roar of active forges (now silent halls).  
    - The shouts of long-vanished engineers (now only the Hum-Birds' song).  
    - The purpose: a geothermic tap, a generator meant to power continents, now powering only its own eternal vigil.  
    The fauna witness this not as history, but as *context*. The Resonators hum in recognition of the original frequency. The Iron Slugs taste the ancient, pure steel in the memory-air.  

3.  **The Weaving of the Hush:**  
    Aeolia’s final act is one of benediction. She draws the prismatic light and the layered hum into a single, focused point above the city’s heart—a place where a massive, dead pressure valve stands like an altar. There, she spins a **Sustaining Echo**.  
    - It is not a physical thing, but a *resonant knot* in the fabric of the city’s sound.  
    - It absorbs infinitesimal fluctuations—the faint threat of a weakening harmonic, the potential for a Resonator to fracture too violently.  
    - It ensures the equation remains balanced. The stasis holds, not as entropy’s prisoner, but as *art*.  

### Why Aeolia Leaves
Aeolia departs as silently as she arrived. The prismatic light fades; the hum returns to its seamless, monolithic drone; the cliffs dim to weathered salt and obsidian. The Sustaining Echo sinks into the bedrock, a hidden tuning fork ensuring the hymn continues. 

**The change is invisible, yet profound:** The inhabitants do not gain sentience. They do not change their nature. But they now exist within their endless moment *with recognition*. The Iron Slug feels the sacredness of its slow consumption. The Resonator understands its shriek as a necessary stanza. The Condensate skitters not just from instinct, but with the echo of witnessed beauty. The Hum-Bird sings its counterpoint not just to correct, but because it *is* the song. 

The city remains a frozen monument. But now, it is a monument that *knows* it is a masterpiece. Aeolia gave it no future, no growth, no reprieve from its mechanical eternity. She gave it the only gift a being of pure harmony *could* give to such a place: **The dignity of being heard.** The stone still weeps. The air still hums. Nothing changes. But now, deep within the vibration, there is a faint, enduring *satisfaction*. A silent "Yes."
