The power observes. It does not manifest as light or voice or form – those are tools for simpler realms. Here, on the raw edge of genesis and decay, it *attends*. Its presence is felt not as intrusion, but as a sudden deepening of focus, a profound stillness settling over the weeping rock and the metallic mist. The wind holds its breath. The dripping water pauses mid-fall. The Mist-Shrike, frozen mid-stalk against a slick outcrop, feels a vast, cool intelligence brush against its piezoelectric crest, not with threat, but with... consideration.

The benevolent power perceives the duality: the fierce, pristine beauty of the nascent geology, the tenacious pulse of life adapted to cold and damp, and the insidious, pervasive stain leaching from distant, unseen engines. It sees the Mist-Shrike not as an aberration, but as a testament to life's terrifying plasticity – a creature forged in the crucible of this specific, poisoned cradle. To remove the industrial taint entirely would be to unravel the Mist-Shrike itself, to deny the fierce, wet reality it embodies. Yet, to leave the poison unchecked is to condemn this young world to a slow, toxic strangulation before its valleys have even formed.

Therefore, the power acts not by subtraction, but by *transformation*. Its influence flows not across the visible landscape, but *down*, deep into the fractured bedrock, tracing the unseen conduits through which the industrial resonance and chemical seepage travel. It finds the deep fractures, the channels carrying the thrum and the taint upwards from the distant source.

Here, in the darkness miles below, the power *weaves*. It doesn't block the vibrations; it *purifies* them. The subsonic thrum, the heartbeat of distant machines, is not silenced. Instead, its chaotic, grinding dissonance is resolved into a complex, resonant harmony. It becomes a deep, grounding pulse, like the hum of a perfectly tuned planetary engine, still felt in the chest but now soothing, aligning rather than jarring. The chemical seepage, the complex hydrocarbons and heavy metals, undergoes a subtle alchemy. The power doesn't eliminate them; it *integrates* them. The toxins are transmuted, molecule by molecule, into complex, stable minerals – not poisons, but inert, crystalline structures that settle within the rock strata, adding new layers to the nascent geology. Harmless. Quiet.

The effect blooms upwards, reaching the surface world like a slow exhalation.

*   **The Mist:** The acrid tang of ozone and cold metal fades, replaced by the pure, sharp scent of high-altitude air and wet stone. The sickly yellow-green phosphorescence in the mist patches shifts. It doesn't vanish, but it *changes*. The glow becomes a softer, silvery luminescence, like moonlight diffused through ice crystals. It no longer feels poisonous, but ethereal, a natural phenomenon born of purified energy interacting with the dense moisture.
*   **The Water:** The iridescent sheen on the black pools dissolves. The water remains clear, profoundly dark, but now it reflects only the bruised sky, the sharp edges of rock, and the soft silver glow of the transformed mist. The seeps weeping down the rock faces run pure, their tears now clean.
*   **The Resonance:** The deep thrum is still present, a vibration felt through the soles of boots and the talons of the Mist-Shrike. But the jarring edge is gone. It's a foundational rhythm now, steady and profound. It doesn't set teeth on edge; it resonates with the slow grind of tectonic plates far below, a part of the mountain's own hidden song.
*   **The Light:** The pearly twilight remains, the dimness a feature of altitude and mist. But the quality changes. The filtered sunlight feels sharper, cleaner, devoid of the oppressive weight the industrial haze imparted. The silver mist-glow adds depth, not unease.

The power turns its attention to the Mist-Shrike itself. It does not alter the creature's form – the keratinous scales, the iron-tipped talons, the obsidian eyes, the piezoelectric crest are perfect adaptations, forged in this specific fire. But it touches the *process*.

*   **The Metabolism:** The creature's ability to metabolize trace toxins remains – a vital adaptation. But now, the "toxins" it encounters are the newly inert minerals, the purified residues. Its internal processes shift subtly. The faint metallic scent clinging to it lessens, mingling more with the natural odor of damp keratin and cold air. Its blood neutralizes what little unstable residue might remain with greater efficiency, leaving it stronger, less burdened.
*   **The Crest:** The piezoelectric filaments still hum, drawing energy. But now, the energy comes from the deep, harmonious pulse, not the chaotic grind. The crest still glows, but the light is the soft silver of the transformed mist, not the sickly green-yellow. It thrums with clean power.
*   **The Dance:** The Mist-Shrike's ritualized movements during high resonance periods become... more. As it vibrates its crest and spirals across the rock, it doesn't just weave mist; it harmonizes with the deep pulse. The mist responds more readily, forming clearer patterns, thicker veils, or parting with greater precision. The dance becomes less a harnessing of pollution, and more a communion with the mountain's new, purified rhythm.

The power withdraws. The wind sighs back into motion. A single droplet plinks into a black pool, its reflection pure. The Mist-Shrike, still pressed against the wet rock, slowly unfreezes. Its obsidian eyes scan the slopes, the silver-glowing mist. It feels... different. The air is cleaner in its gills. The vibration under its feet is steady, reassuring. It senses no immediate threat, but a profound change in the fabric of its world. It tilts its head, the silver glow of its crest pulsing softly in time with the deep, harmonious thrum resonating up from the bones of its nascent, *healed* home. It lets out a short cry – still the sound of rending metal, but now clear, pure, and resonant, echoing across the cleansed and weeping stone. It is still the apex predator of the high, wet cradle. But the stain is lifted. The world is young, raw, wet, and cold... but now, it is *whole*.
