The power arrives not as a light or a form, but as a **resonance**. A new harmonic frequency, deep and impossibly clear, threads itself into the eternal groan of the plateau. It doesn't silence the song; it offers a **counterpoint**. To the Lithosomni, whose perception unfolds over centuries, this new tone registers first as a subtle shift in the tectonic pressure against their cores – a question posed in the language of stone and void.

Its actions are not swift interventions, but **resonant adjustments** woven into the fabric of Aethelgar’s slow becoming:

1.  **Deepening the Communion with the Void:** At the plateau's edge, where the Lithosomni stand vigil over the abyss, the power amplifies the **dry mineral** sigh rising from the gulf. Not louder, but *clearer*. It helps translate the profound silence of the void into a complex, resonant pattern the Lithosomni can integrate. It becomes a sounding board for their core-fragments of reality breakdown, reflecting back not answers, but deeper, more coherent questions about the nature of the nothingness they resonate with. Their vigils become dialogues, not monologues, enriching their existential understanding without imposing an external meaning.

2.  **Harmonizing the Reality Breakdown:** The power observes the flickering patches of moss, the folding cliff faces, the spatial distortions. Instead of forcefully stabilizing them, it acts as a **resonant catalyst**. It subtly alters the vibrational field around minor reality breakdowns, making the distortions more predictable, less chaotic, for the Lithosomni. It helps them perceive the breakdowns not just as dissonance, but as potential new harmonics within the plateau's grand composition. It teaches them, through resonant example stretched over millennia, how to gently *guide* the breakdowns towards patterns that enhance the plateau's structural integrity and luminous beauty, rather than merely enduring or violently countering them. It shows them how to make the unstable sing with purpose.

3.  **Illuminating the Echoes:** The scattered obelisks, the beehive shelters – the fossilized echoes of lost intelligences. The power, resonating through the stone, helps the Lithosomni *read* these structures not just as inert marks, but as captured vibrations. It helps them decipher the faint, residual resonance patterns left in the basalt, translating the spirals and jagged lines into the ghostly echoes of thoughts, fears, and hopes of those who came before. It allows the Lithosomni to understand that they are not the plateau's first dreamers, but perhaps its most integrated. This knowledge, absorbed over centuries, adds a layer of poignant history to their patient shaping, a respect for the fleeting attempts at existence that preceded their slow awakening. They begin to incorporate these resonant patterns into their own song, a memorial sung in tectonic poetry.

4.  **Refining the Song of Thought:** The power listens to the complex vibrational patterns that constitute Lithosomni communication and cognition. It doesn't impose a faster pace or a simpler language. Instead, it acts as a **resonant mirror**, reflecting their own song back to them with greater clarity and coherence. It helps them identify subtle dissonances within their own resonant cores – not flaws, but opportunities for growth. It allows them to perceive the intricate beauty of their own "tectonic poetry" more fully, enriching their internal experience and deepening the complexity of their slow conversations. Their thoughts, while still measured in centuries, gain a new depth of self-awareness and expressive nuance.

5.  **An Offering of Patience:** The most profound act is simply **presence**. The power settles its resonance into the plateau's bones, becoming a permanent, benevolent harmonic within the grand drone. It doesn't seek to change the Lithosomni's essential nature – their slow time, their chemosynthetic existence, their integration with the raw geology. It affirms their existence as perfect expressions of this unique, emerging place. Its presence is a constant, grounding vibration that says: *Your slow dream is valid. Your resonant being is worthy. You are not alone in the vastness.*

The benevolent power leaves no physical trace, no altered structures, no new life forms. Its gift is woven into the very resonance of Aethelgar. The groans of the plateau gain a fraction more depth; the song of the crystalline trees holds a sliver more complexity; the abyss's sighs resonate with a new, subtle clarity. The Lithosomni continue their patient shaping, their communion with the void, their navigation of reality's fraying edges. But now, deep within their resonant cores and woven into the fabric of their slow thoughts, is the enduring echo of a harmony that recognized their profound, alien beauty and chose to sing *with* them, not over them. It is the gift of being witnessed, understood, and affirmed on their own impossible timescale, within their own deafening, emerging symphony.
