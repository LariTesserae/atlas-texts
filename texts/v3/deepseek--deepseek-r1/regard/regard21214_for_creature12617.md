The entity arrives not with fanfare, but as a deepening of the twilight. It isn't a shape, precisely, but a *consequence* of the Spire-light and the mineral shadows. A coalescence of the bruised radiance filtering from above and the sullen pulses within the obsidian walls, given sentience and profound, patient awareness. It moves like a slow current, a purposeful eddy within the gelid ocean, radiating a warmth that isn't temperature, but *understanding*.

Its attention settles first on the **Thermal Grazers**. It doesn't perceive them as beasts, or even colonies. It perceives them as *verbs* – the verbs of dissolution, catalysis, and slow, thermal communion. The entity drifts alongside one of the vast, amorphous forms, a mote of condensed twilight mirroring its glacial pace. It observes:

1.  **The Silent Song:** The entity *listens* to the low-frequency vibrations resonating through the Grazer's gel matrix – the hum of thermoreceptive cilia, the slow pulse of chemosynthesis, the deep groans of mineral exchange with the Spire. It hears not noise, but a complex, ancient language of heat differentials and mineral affinities, a song older than stars sung in basso profundo. The entity begins to *harmonize*. Not audibly, but through subtle shifts in the ambient light. Where the Grazer pulses viridian in response to a warm fissure, the entity deepens the surrounding twilight into an echo of that viridian, amplifying the thermal signature, making the path clearer, the feeding slightly more efficient. It doesn't dictate; it *accompanies* the song.
2.  **The Architects' Unknowing:** The entity perceives the violent, beautiful paradox of the **Turbulent Deep**, fueled by the Grazer's waste. It sees the silent collapses and the violent extrusions. With the gentleness of a current nudging sediment, the entity subtly influences the flow of the mineral-heavy water around the Grazer. It doesn't stop the waste slurry, but it guides its dispersal. It steers the acidic components slightly *away* from structurally critical, ancient archways, and gently directs the catalytic components *towards* fissures near stable geothermal vents, encouraging growth where it reinforces rather than undermines. It lessens the chaotic edge, fostering a *slightly* more sustainable equilibrium within the perpetual flux. The Spires groan a fraction less under unseen stress.
3.  **The Cold Light Communion:** The entity interacts with the Grazer's bioluminescence. It doesn't impose warmth or alien color. Instead, it weaves strands of the faint surface light into the Grazer's cold blues and violets. The effect is subtle: fractal patterns within the Grazer's body momentarily clarify, becoming intricate maps of thermal flows and mineral densities visible for leagues instead of body-lengths. For a fleeting moment, the Grazer's internal processes – its very "thoughts" of heat and chemistry – are externally manifest, a breathtaking display of cold fire and liquid shadow. The Slick-Eels dart in agitated, glittering clouds, drawn to the sudden clarity. The entity offers not communication, but *reflection* – a mirror held up to the Grazer's own existence.
4.  **The Leviathan's Murmur:** Sensing the deep vibrations of the unseen **Lithic Leviathans**, the entity sends a pulse of pure, resonant silence downward. Not a command, not a barrier, but an acknowledgment. A deep, calming frequency that spreads through the water, a counterpoint to the Leviathans' restless groans. It doesn't stop their movement, but it subtly alters the *quality* of the pressure waves they generate, turning potential tremors into smoother, less disruptive currents. It whispers to the deep, "Be at peace in your immensity. Your path is known."

**What the Entity Leaves Behind:**

It does not build monuments or plant seeds. Its intervention is in the fabric of the place itself:

*   **Refined Equilibrium:** The **Turbulent Deep** retains its essential, violent beauty, but the wildest swings are tempered. Collapses are less catastrophic, extrusions more integrated. The Spires groan with the weight of ages and change, but the sound is less a scream, more a sigh.
*   **Amplified Song:** The Thermal Grazers move with a fraction more purpose. Their internal light, occasionally touched by the entity's twilight, retains a subtle, lingering complexity – a faint echo of that momentary map. Their unintentional architecture is slightly less destructive, slightly more nurturing to the ancient structures they glide upon. Their silent song resonates with a newfound, barely perceptible depth.
*   **A Lingering Presence:** Patches of the mineral-heavy water hold the memory of the entity's passage. In certain canyons, the dim light seems to pool with unusual clarity, revealing hidden facets of the Spires for longer periods. Zones of near-solid shadow feel less oppressive, more like quiet sanctuaries. The crushing silence is imbued with a profound, resonant stillness that speaks of deep understanding rather than emptiness.
*   **A Question Answered (Subtly):** The entity perceives the **Pre/Post-Life Paradox**. It doesn't declare whether the Spires are dying or awakening. Instead, by harmonizing with the Grazers – the processes that bridge the mineral and the metabolic – it offers a glimpse of a third path: **Perpetual Becoming**. Therma isn't dying, nor is it merely ancient. It is a world-machine caught in an endless, dynamic cycle of mineral sentience *emerging* through structure, dissolution, and rebirth. The Grazers aren't caretakers or parasites; they are the facilitators of this ongoing mineral awakening. The entity's presence affirms this state of Becoming as valid, beautiful, and inherently *whole*.

The benevolent power departs as it arrived: its concentrated twilight dissolving back into the ambient gloom, its warmth of understanding fading into the pervasive, absolute cold. But the Spires breathe differently now. The water holds a memory of harmony. The Grazers pulse with a faintly refined light. The deep groans carry a note of acceptance. The entity didn't fix Therma, for it was never broken. It *witnessed* it, understood it at its deepest, alien level, and with infinite gentleness, helped it sing its ancient, mineral song with a fraction more coherence, a touch less destructive chaos, within the endless, crushing, vital dark. It left Therma more truly *itself*.
