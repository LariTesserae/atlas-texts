The water isn't something you enter; it's the world itself. Warm, heavy, and utterly pervasive, it presses in from all sides, a thick, saline atmosphere that fills your lungs with a slow, viscous burn. Visibility extends barely an arm's length, the crushing darkness (0.1) broken only by the faintest, intermittent glimmers – not sunlight, but the cold, blue-green phosphorescence of deep-sea organisms clinging to the ruins.

This is a drowned cityscape (built 2.1), ancient beyond reckoning. Towers, not of steel and glass, but of colossal, water-smoothed stone and a strange, coral-like amalgam (weirdness 0.9), loom like sleeping leviathans out of the gloom. Streets are canyons choked with silt and the skeletons of lesser structures, all worn into soft, organic curves by millennia of patient currents (erosion 2.0, established). The density (2.0) is oppressive; structures crowd close, their bases merging into a continuous, uneven topography of rubble and sculpted rock, overhangs creating shadowed grottos, pillars rising like the trunks of petrified forests. The scale (1.5) feels vast yet contained; you sense the immense, open water beyond the immediate ruins, a pressure against the mind, while navigating the intricate, claustrophobic maze within the skeletal metropolis.

Life here is abundant (fauna 2.2), but it is life adapted to eternal night and crushing pressure. Sightless fish, their bodies translucent or armored in dark chitin, drift with minimal effort in the warm (2.3) currents. Larger shapes, sinuous and silent, glide through the upper canyons, their passage marked only by the sudden vanishing of phosphorescent swarms. Delicate, multi-legged crustaceans scuttle across the stone faces, harvesting the sparse (0.8) growths: tough, black kelp anchored deep in crevices, luminous bacterial mats painting abstract patterns on walls, and strange, slow-growing filter-feeders resembling porous, mineralized fans. This is a world without tools, without fire, without any spark beyond the cold light of biology (tech 0.0). Predation is patient, a slow dance in the dark, ambush over chase.

Sound is a rarity absorbed by the thick water (sound 0.8, mostly quiet). The dominant sensation is the immense, liquid silence, broken only by the occasional, resonant *drip... drip...* from unseen heights, the gritty scrape of a shell against stone, the sudden, muffled *thrum* of a creature's locomotion vibrating through the water, or the low, almost subsonic groan of the ancient structures settling further into the silt. The quiet makes every small noise profound.

Yet, the stillness is deceptive. This ecosystem is intensely active (dynamic 1.9) beneath its quietude. Constant, slow thermals (temperature 2.3) circulate nutrients in the heavy water. Filter-feeders pulse rhythmically. Predators patrol invisible territories. Scavengers endlessly pick at the ruins and the occasional fallen fauna. The water itself is a dynamic element, with subtle currents shifting direction, carrying scents, plankton, and the larvae of countless species through the drowned avenues. It’s a slow-motion frenzy, a ballet of survival played out in perpetual twilight.

**Rules of this World:**

1.  **The Water is Air:** Movement is slow, deliberate, three-dimensional. Buoyancy dictates form. Flight is swimming. Falling is sinking.
2.  **Darkness is Absolute:** Vision is near useless beyond bioluminescence. Perception relies on vibration, pressure changes, chemoreception, and sonar-like pulses used by larger fauna. Light is a rare, localized phenomenon, often a lure or a warning.
3.  **Stone is Alive:** The ruins are the substrate. They provide shelter, anchor points, and are slowly being consumed and reshaped by boring organisms, mineral accretion, and the relentless, patient sculpting of water. They are the bones of the past and the foundation of the present ecology.
4.  **Warmth Fuels Slowness:** The ambient warmth allows metabolic processes, but the pressure and density enforce a languid pace. Energy is conserved. Action is deliberate. Explosive movement is rare and costly.
5.  **Life is Opportunistic:** With sparse vegetation, the food chain is built on detritus, filter-feeding, and predation. Everything is consumed, eventually. The ruins themselves are a nutrient source. Symbiosis is common – luminescent bacteria on hunters, cleaner organisms on grazers.
6.  **Quiet is Survival:** Loud noises attract unwanted attention – often from apex predators whose senses are tuned to vibration. Silence is camouflage, vigilance, and the default state of the deep.

This is the Sunken City of Therma. A place of crushing beauty and profound isolation, where the ghosts of a lost civilization are slowly being digested by a warm, dark, silent, and teeming ocean. It exists in a state of weathered equilibrium, a dense tapestry of stone and life suspended in liquid night, humming with the quiet, relentless activity of survival.
