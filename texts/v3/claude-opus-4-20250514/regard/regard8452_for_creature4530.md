The Lantern Barrens is teaching its inhabitants to become part of a larger organism - not through force or seduction, but through the simple gravitational pull of becoming more real than reality usually allows.

So the Observer does what benevolent powers do when witnessing birth: it midwifes gently, invisibly, with the lightest possible touch.

Thirty-three Struck-off is barely the beginning. The plateau needs more.

It will reach other plateaus, other abandoned places where the margin between human and posthuman has worn thin. *Come,* the frequency says without saying. *There is a place where the light remembers your name in a language you haven't learned yet.*

The Observer withdraws then, satisfied. Not because the work is complete - such transformations take centuries, not seasons - but because the process is now self-sustaining. But it will not speak this word aloud. That privilege belongs to Zero, when she finally understands what comes after zero in a counting system that includes negative infinities and the square roots of regret.

After all, every universe deserves at least one place where the impossible is simply another word for "not yet."
