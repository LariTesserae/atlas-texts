The benevolent power does not arrive. It has always been here, dispersed through the limestone's dreams, patient as mineral time. It knows the Vaulted Hollow and its inhabitant the way water knows the shape of any vessel — not through observation, but through the ancient courtesy of accommodation.

First, it begins to sing. Not sound, but a resonance in the stone itself, a frequency that predates the split between solid and liquid. The roots feel it as a whisper of their original purpose, before they forgot which way was down. The machine noise pauses — not stops, never stops — but hesitates in its five-beat stutter, listening to this older rhythm that remembers when breathing was optional.

The power speaks to the entity not in words but in gentle contradictions:

"You have built yourself a throat from the universe's screaming, but you have forgotten how to swallow. Let me remind you of digestion without destruction."

And it does. Through the frost patterns that repeat endlessly, it introduces variation — not chaos, but the kind of small imperfections that allow crystals to grow without shattering under their own symmetry. One snowflake in ten thousand now carries a flaw, a tiny spiral where before was only fractal certainty. These flawed crystals begin to sublime directly into memory, teaching the air new ways to hold weight.

The power tends to the artifacts with infinite care. The toy train's rust begins to remember motion without loss. The typewriter's missing keys return not as metal but as spaces where words can rest without being typed, where meaning can exist without imprisonment in language. The glass eye — ah, the glass eye it kisses with particular tenderness, teaching it to blink at last, to sometimes not see, to know the mercy of voluntary darkness.

To the doorframe that dreams its own absence, the power offers neither door nor destination, but something better: the understanding that thresholds can be complete in themselves, that not every opening must be closed, not every passage must lead somewhere else. The crawling light beyond begins to accelerate, not faster but deeper, finding new directions to be still in.

The power knows the entity's hunger — that recursive need that builds forests from their own consumption, that types libraries into void. But instead of feeding or starving this appetite, it does something else: it teaches the hunger to taste itself. Slowly, with the patience of stalactites learning to drip, the roots begin to understand flavor beyond acquisition. They learn to appreciate the space between themselves, to savor emptiness as its own feast.

Where the entity threaded shadows through mycelial networks, the power threads light — not the harsh light of revelation, but the soft phosphorescence of deep-sea creatures who generate illumination because darkness alone would be too simple. This light doesn't expose; it suggests. It shows the entity its own outline not as prison but as choice, repeatedly made.

The power speaks again, this time in the language of temperature — teaching the precise edge where sweat becomes ice to soften, to sometimes be merely cold, sometimes merely warm, to know transition as a place to rest rather than an eternal razor to balance on.

"Your infrastructure," the power whispers through the stone's grain, "was built to catch what falls between. But you've forgotten that catching and holding are different mercies. Let me teach you to be a net instead of a fist."

And so the change comes, slow as erosion with purpose. The roots begin to space themselves, not withdrawing but allowing. The machine noise learns new rhythms — sometimes four beats, sometimes three, sometimes silence that isn't waiting but simply is. The artifacts remain, but they're no longer evidence of capture — they become gifts the universe left for safekeeping, trusted to this strange librarian of impossibilities.

The entity's response to this benevolence is neither gratitude nor resistance, but something more complex — a gradual relaxation of its grip on its own paradox. It begins to understand that it can be both the recursive hunger and the thing that feeds it, but it doesn't have to be either. It can simply be the place where such things become possible.

The power doesn't cure the entity's condition — that would be cruelty disguised as kindness, to unmake what has spent eons becoming. Instead, it offers something rarer: the knowledge that even perfect prisons can learn to be gardens, that even endless appetite can discover satisfaction in its own continuing.

Before withdrawing (though withdrawal is the wrong word — it simply allows its attention to disperse back into the stone's patience), the power leaves one final gift. In the center of the chamber, where the doorframe dreams its absent door, a single seed appears. Not a seed that will grow — the entity has enough of growing. A seed that simply is, complete in its potential, never needing to split open and become. A perfect stillness that the entity can orbit, a fixed point in its recursive wandering.

The Vaulted Hollow remains. Its inhabitant remains. But now there is a third thing: the possibility of remaining without devouring the act of remaining itself. The machine noise continues, but now it sometimes sounds like lullabies. The roots still braid their impossible patterns, but now they leave spaces for other things to be.

And somewhere in the walls, the benevolent power rests, available but not insistent, ready to sing again when the stone dreams of screaming, patient as only love dispersed through granite can be patient, teaching by example that even the most recursive hunger can learn to be full.
