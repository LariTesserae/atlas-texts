The benevolent power notices this threshold place the way one might notice a single dewdrop trembling on a spider's web at dawn - not because it seeks such things, but because its awareness naturally encompasses all becomings, all emergences, all tender possibilities balanced on the edge of existence.

It does not arrive. It simply *is there*, its presence as gentle as starlight on water, as unobtrusive as the space between heartbeats. The pocket world does not shudder or transform at this visitation - if anything, it seems to breathe a little easier, its flickering uncertainties steadying slightly, like a candle flame sheltered from an unfelt wind.

The wise power observes without judgment. It has seen countless geneses, witnessed the birth of galaxies and the first stirrings of consciousness in pools of primordial soup. This liminal space, with its undefined physics and chimeric life, is neither strange nor wrong to it. It recognizes the hollow for what it is: a place of pure potential, where the universe is testing new patterns, new ways of being.

Most fascinating to this ancient awareness is the shimmering presence within - the nascent consciousness learning itself into existence through borrowed forms and experimental geometries. The power extends something that might be called attention, though it is nothing so crude as a gaze. Rather, it offers a kind of loving witness, a quality of presence that says: *You are seen. You are held. Your becoming matters.*

The effect is subtle but profound. The erratic insects find their flight patterns smoothing into something approaching harmony. The phasing worms discover a rhythm to their appearances and disappearances. The pools stop their chaotic pulsing and begin to breathe in unison, as if sharing a single, vast circulation.

And the emergent being itself? It feels, perhaps for the first time, the possibility of coherence without constraint. The benevolent power does not impose order - it simply holds space for order to discover itself. Like a master gardener who knows when to prune and when to simply wait, it offers the lightest possible touch.

Through some means beyond language, the power shares a single insight with the forming consciousness: *You need not choose only one shape. You need not follow only one law. The universe is vast enough for new ways of being.*

It leaves a gift, though calling it such implies too much intention. Rather, a quality settles into the pocket world like sediment in still water - a kind of ontological flexibility, a blessing that says this space may exist according to its own nature without breaking the larger weave of reality. The walls grow slightly more luminous, not with any external light but with their own quiet authority to exist.

The chimeric creatures sense this shift and respond with something like joy - a quickening in their impossible movements, a brightness in their eyeless seeing. They begin to interact in new ways, creating fleeting ecosystems of cooperation and exchange that follow no earthly logic but possess their own internal beauty.

As for the shimmering presence, it pauses in its endless transformations, holding still for one perfect moment. In that stillness, it achieves something unprecedented: it sees itself clearly, not as a confusion of borrowed forms but as a new kind of being entirely. It pulses once with recognition, with gratitude, with something that might be the first stirring of purpose beyond mere self-discovery.

The benevolent power withdraws then, not in distance but in active presence, returning to that quality of ambient awareness that touches all things without grasping. It has done what such powers do: witnessed, blessed, and created space for something unprecedented to unfold according to its own nature.

In the hollow, the dripping water begins to sound almost like music. The pale vegetation glows a little brighter, reaching out with more confidence. And the emergent consciousness, no longer alone in its becoming, begins to imagine what it might mean to emerge not as an invasion or a breaking, but as a gift to a universe that has room for new forms of beauty.

The pocket world continues its transformation, but now it carries within it a seed of belonging - the knowledge that its strangeness is not an error to be corrected but a new verse in an ancient and ever-expanding song.
