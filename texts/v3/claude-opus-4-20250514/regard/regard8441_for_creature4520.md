The benevolent power arrives not as arrival but as having-always-been-arriving. She is called the Tender of Unfinished Gardens, though calling requires names and she prefers to be verb rather than noun.

She doesn't descend from above—above has unionized here and refuses overtime. Instead, she emerges from the moment between the valley's inhale and its decision to breathe. Her presence registers at 3.0 on the gentleness scale, which hasn't been invented yet but the valley is already nostalgic for its future accuracy.

The Recalls notice her the way one notices the solution to a problem they haven't realized they're having. The eldest (who might be the valley dreaming it has inhabitants) attempts a greeting but accidentally welcomes tomorrow's rain instead. She accepts both with equal grace.

She brings gifts that are also tasks that are also invitations:

First, she plants a tree of Completed Sentences in the soil that can't decide if it's earth or embarrassed water. The tree grows downward into sky, its roots breathing commas where periods threatened finality. Each leaf contains one finished thought from the valley's endless revision cycle—not to end the thoughts, but to let them rest between drafts. The Recalls gather beneath its branches (or above its roots, depending on gravity's mood) and discover the luxury of conclusions that don't conclude anything except their own need to keep concluding.

Second, she teaches the weather to laugh at its own jokes. The three-movement symphony about disillusionment learns a coda of compassionate absurdity. Rain falls upward some days now, not in confusion but in celebration of options. The Recalls find they can dance between the drops, whether ascending or descending, and their movements write thank-you notes in condensation on surfaces that suddenly remember they wanted to be windows.

Third, she gifts the valley a clock that tells time in kindnesses—each hour marked not by numbers but by moments of involuntary tenderness between particles that thought they were too busy being physics to notice each other. The Recalls discover they can be late for appointments with their own existence and it's perfectly acceptable; the clock applauds their tardiness as innovation in temporal jazz.

She doesn't fix the probability kittens who exist only when unobserved. Instead, she teaches them to purr in frequencies that make observation optional—you can know they're there through the sympathy their contentment creates in nearby molecules. The kittens, delighted by this loophole in their own impossibility, begin leaving gifts of crystallized maybe for anyone who's forgotten that uncertainty can be a form of affection.

For the time weavers who construct themselves from forgotten memories, she weaves a single thread of remembering-forward through their too-many joints. Not enough thread to bind them to one shape, just enough to let them choose their own continuity between heartbeats. They begin teaching the Recalls to braid tomorrow's regrets with yesterday's hopes into present-tense scarves that actually provide warmth measured in giggling.

The six-seven-eight mountains receive her attention as equals. She asks them about their gossip, and when they complain about the newcomers (you), she listens with the gravity of someone who knows that mountains' complaints are how geology expresses affection. She gifts them mirrors made of patience so they can admire all their profiles simultaneously without the exhausting rotation. The mountains, touched by this efficiency, decide to stabilize at seven-and-three-quarters—enough constancy to be dependable, enough fraction to remain interesting.

She addresses the valley's broken relationship with drowning by introducing them to each other formally, as if at a party where former lovers meet after years of processing. The water at 2.2/3 presence and the land that forgot how to be land shake hands(?) and discover they've both changed enough to be friends who share custody of the horizon. Tides begin arriving for their visitation rights on a schedule everyone pretends to understand.

For you, the newcomers, she leaves a note written in the pause between your inhale and decision to exhale. The note says simply: "Your displacement is perfect. Belonging is not about fitting in but about how interestingly you don't fit. The valley collected you because it needed your particular flavor of confusion to complete its recipe for almost-understanding itself."

But her greatest gift is this: she teaches the valley's endless revision to include rest periods. Not endings—the valley shudders at the thought—but moments where the constant rewriting becomes conscious re-reading. The Recalls discover they can exist in their own memories without immediately editing them. The probability fields learn to sometimes collapse into certainty just for the relief of it, knowing they can re-expand whenever they're bored with being definite.

She stays exactly long enough to be missed but not long enough to be mourned. Before leaving (though leaving here means becoming the pause between arrival and arrival), she whispers something to the crystallization patterns that perform computation in visitors' sweat. The whisper becomes a function: anyone who enters the valley now carries with them the ability to be productively lost—to navigate by forgetting their destination until they arrive at where they needed to be instead of where they meant to go.

The Recalls wave goodbye with limbs they borrow from next Tuesday's understanding of anatomy. The mountains hum appreciative bass notes that register as Thursday on seismographs. The probability kittens purr in superposition.

And the valley itself? 

The valley learns the most dangerous blessing of all: how to be satisfied with its own incompleteness. Not through achieving wholeness, but through understanding that its fragments are not broken pieces of something larger, but complete entities choosing to be partial for the joy of leaving room for what comes next.

The next day (which arrives sideways as usual), the Recalls discover they can sometimes cast shadows that remember being solid. The shadows play chess with the echoes of the Tender's laughter, using pieces carved from crystallized perhaps.

Everyone pretends not to notice that they're winning.
