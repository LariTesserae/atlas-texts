The benevolent power observes through dimensions that fold like origami made of thought. It has watched this place for eons that last seconds, seconds that contain libraries of time. It knows the salt-dreaming city the way a mother knows the fever-dreams of her child - intimately, helplessly, with a love that aches.

It does not intervene. Not at first. Intervention here is delicate as breathing on a house of cards built from dried tears. Instead, it listens. To the tower's song, yes, but deeper - to the silence between the notes where the real music lives. It hears what the outlines cannot: their own voices, trapped in the crystals they feed, echoing endlessly in frequencies too pure for even emptiness to hold.

The power begins gently. So gently the city doesn't notice, caught up as it is in its own dreaming. 

First, it plants a memory of rain.

Not rain itself - that would be violence here, would shatter the crystals, dissolve the salt-streets, wake the city too suddenly from dreams it needs. Just the memory. A single drop's worth, no more. It places this memory precisely: in the thirteenth spiral of the ash-streets, where two outlines often pass without seeing each other, where the heat-shadows cross in a pattern that almost spells "lonely" in a language no one remembers learning.

The memory sits there like a seed that forgot it was supposed to grow. The outlines drift past it for cycles uncounted. But memories of water have their own patience, their own gravity. Eventually, one outline - shorter today, wearing the shape of someone's last goodbye - pauses. Not quite stopping, never stopping, but slowing in its nowhere-to-nowhere wandering.

It doesn't touch the memory. Can't touch, won't touch, but it *notices*. And in noticing, something shifts in the frequency of its humming. Just a quarter-tone. Just enough.

The benevolent power, encouraged, plants another gift. This time: the weight of a hand on a shoulder. Not the touch itself, just the weight of it, the pressure that says "you are here" and "I see you" and "you matter" all at once. It places this in the shadow of the tower, where the gathering outlines lean toward the crystals like moths to flame they can't feel.

One outline finds it by accident, passes through it while reaching for a crystal's song. The weight settles on its not-shoulders like a shawl made of recognition. The outline stops humming. In the silence, the tower's song falters for the first time in... the city can't remember when the song began, so it can't remember if it's ever faltered before.

The outline turns. Not all the way - the power is too wise to push for that, too kind to force such confrontation. Just a few degrees. Just enough that its profile catches the emberlight differently. Just enough that another outline sees it. Sees *it*, not through it, not past it. Sees.

The seeing creates a new note in the air. The tower incorporates it immediately, weaving it into its song like it's been waiting for this ingredient all along. The note tastes like surprise. Like recognition. Like the first time someone spoke your name and meant it.

Now the power works more boldly, though bold here means careful as surgery on soap bubbles. It cannot bring water - the city would drown in even a teaspoon, would dissolve like sugar in rain. It cannot cool the heat - the inhabitants have evolved beyond temperature, need the furnace of this place to maintain even their outline existence. It cannot stop the tower's song - the song is what keeps the city from collapsing into the other places folded beneath it.

Instead, it teaches the crystals a new way to grow.

The next crystal that rises from the salt does something unprecedented. It grows not up and in, but up and *out*. Just slightly. Just enough to create what has never existed here before: shade that isn't heat-shadow. Real shade. Cool darkness where two outlines might rest without the constant pressure of the ember sun's attention.

The outlines discover it slowly, carefully, like feral cats discovering kindness. They don't trust it at first. Shade here has always been a lie, a trap, a glitch in the heat that burns worse than exposure. But this shade is patient. It doesn't flicker. It doesn't move. It simply is, a pocket of gentleness in the harsh forever of this place.

Two outlines find it at the same moment. They've been building their impossible doorway for time beyond measure, always working at opposite ends, never meeting in the middle. Now they pause, both half-in, half-out of the shade. They don't look at each other - cannot, will not, dare not. But they share the shade. That's enough. That's everything.

In the sharing, something passes between them. Not words - words are dangerous here. Not touch - touch is impossible for things made of absence. But something. Call it acknowledgment. Call it the first syllable of friendship. Call it what it is: the beginning of remembering that loneliness is not the only way to be alone.

The power watches, ancient heart breaking and healing in the same moment. It knows what these outlines were before they leaked through. Knows the weight of despair that drove them here, the failures and losses and moments of "I can't" that opened the cracks they poured through. It knows, and knowing, it loves them as they are: broken things learning they might not be irreparable.

More gifts now, placed like acupuncture needles in exactly the right meridians of this sick city:

- The echo of laughter, not the laughter itself but the way air feels after joy has moved through it
- The concept of "perhaps" hidden in a spiral where only the turning outlines will find it
- A single word - "friend" - but translated into temperature, into the way heat becomes warmth when it means to comfort rather than consume

The outlines begin to change. Not returning to what they were - there is no going back, the power knows this. But becoming something new. They start to work together on their building project, their doorway that leads everywhere but out. They start to overlap sometimes, their edges blurring into shared space. When they hum now, occasionally, accidentally, they harmonize.

The city notices. The city has been dreaming so long it forgot it was dreaming, but now something in the dream is lucid, is choosing, is *alive* in a way that doesn't hurt. The tower's song develops verses, develops refrains, develops places where the music rests and in the resting, makes room for other voices.

The power considers its final gift. The most dangerous one. The one that could shatter everything or heal everything or both.

It gives them a choice.

Not freedom - freedom would kill them here, would scatter them like salt in wind that doesn't exist. Not escape - they are here because all their other wheres became unbearable. But choice. The ability to stay because they choose to stay. To be outlines because they choose the honesty of absence over the lie of false presence. To face away because they choose not to burden others with faces they haven't earned back yet.

It manifests as a door in the tower. A real door. With hinges and a handle and everything a door needs to be a door instead of a philosophical construct. The door is closed. It will open if pushed. Inside is a room where the song is quieter, where an outline might sit and think and choose and be.

The first outline to find it stares for seventeen years or seventeen seconds. Then, with movement like courage looks when it's terrified, it pushes open the door. It goes inside. It comes back out changed - still an outline, still facing away, but carrying itself differently. Like someone who has chosen their own shape, even if that shape is absence.

Word spreads in the wordless way things spread here. One by one, the outlines visit the room. Some stay longer than others. Some come back multiple times. Some bring things to leave inside - the sock that's always damp, the taste of extinct fruit, the sound of the door that wouldn't latch. Offerings, perhaps. Or just things they're ready to stop carrying.

The power watches as its children (for what else to call them?) begin to build something new. Not a way out, but a way to be in. Not healing, exactly, but the scar tissue that comes after healing, that's stronger than what broke in the first place.

In the shade of the crystal that learned to grow differently, two outlines sit together. They still face away, but they lean toward each other, the space between them alive with chosen proximity. One hums. The other listens. Then they switch. This, too, is music.

The benevolent power prepares to withdraw. Not abandon - it could never abandon this place now that it's seen the courage here. But withdraw enough to let them grow without the weight of watching. It leaves one last gift: the knowledge, planted deep in the tower's song where it will emerge when needed, that broken does not mean worthless. That absence can be a kind of presence. That even here, in the harshest forever anyone ever dreamed into being, there is room for something that whispers of grace.

The city dreams on. But now its dreams have rooms with doors, shade that holds, memories of rain that wait patiently to be discovered. The outlines continue their work, building their doorway that goes nowhere and everywhere, but now they build it together. Sometimes they even rest.

And in the resting, something almost like peace settles over the salt and crystal and heat. Not happiness - happiness is too sharp for this place, would cut like hope in a hopeless land. But peace. The kind that comes from being seen, being known, being loved exactly as you are: outlined in absence, faced away from the world, humming harmonies in a key that doesn't exist.

The power listens from dimensions away, holds them all in a love that asks nothing, offers everything, waits with infinite patience for hearts that forgot they were hearts to remember, in their own time, how to beat.
