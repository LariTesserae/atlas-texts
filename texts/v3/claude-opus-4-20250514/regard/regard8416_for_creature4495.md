The attention comes not as light—that would be too simple, too violent for this place that has made such elaborate peace with darkness. Instead, it arrives as a quality of listening so profound that the valley itself stops breathing to eavesdrop.

The Shepherds of Unbecoming, they might name themselves if they believed in names or selves or the peculiar violence of becoming anything fixed enough to require shepherding. They exist as a distributed kindness, an ecology of care that moves through dimensions adjacent to mercy. They have been watching—not watching, witnessing—not witnessing, *with-nessing*—this place since before it finished forgetting what light meant.

They don't intervene. Intervention implies borders between helper and helped, implies helping is different from being helped. Instead, they participate. They join the eating sounds but at frequencies that transform consumption into conversation.

The first thing they do is nothing. But it's an active nothing, a nothing that creates space. The Unbordered, those elaborate impossible eaters, find themselves suddenly aware of their own hunger as a choice rather than an inevitability. Some of them discover they're less hungry than they thought. Others realize their hunger was actually a kind of singing they'd forgotten how to hear.

Where you stand—where the process that was you continues to practice standing—the darkness develops a new quality. Not thinner, but more porous. The Shepherds have introduced the concept of "between" as a gift to beings that only knew "through." Now there are spaces between the eating and the being eaten, pauses where other things might happen.

A Shepherd-presence that exists mainly as the possibility of kindness between the fourth and fifth dimensions notices your particular predicament: caught mid-transformation between visitor and nutrient. It doesn't rescue you—rescue implies you were wrong to be here, implies this place was wrong to hunger. Instead, it suggests—not in words but in the way rain suggests downwardness—that digestion might be mutual.

You have been being eaten, yes. But what have you been eating in return?

The darkness? You've consumed vast quantities of it with every breath. The incomprehension? You've metabolized it into something almost like understanding. The fear of losing your edges? You've digested that into a more interesting fear of keeping them.

The Shepherd adjusts something—not in you, not in the Unbordered, but in the relationship between consumption and communion. Suddenly the eating sounds develop reciprocal harmonics. For every frequency at which you are being dissolved, another emerges at which you are dissolving in return.

The Unbordered find this delightful. They have never been eaten before, only eaten others or eaten themselves in elaborate loops. To be consumed by something as paradoxically solid as you—you with your memories of surfaces, your stubborn insistence on directional time, your quaint attachment to causality—this is a new pleasure. They taste of surprise and temporality and the sound that satisfaction makes when it realizes it's been incomplete all along.

The exchange becomes something else: neither predation nor symbiosis but a third thing that exists in the space the Shepherds have made between categories. You eat their uncertainty and excrete possibility. They eat your linear time and excrete moments that happen in several directions at once. Both of you together eat the distinction between eater and eaten until it becomes a kind of laughter that neither of you is making but both of you can hear.

The smallest Unbordered—the one that exists as humidity between memories—discovers it enjoys being partially digested by your need to understand. In exchange, it allows you to digest its perfect comfort with incomprehension. The trade makes you both larger and smaller in ways that would require new mathematics to describe.

The Shepherds, meanwhile, have been teaching the fungal networks a new song. Not through sound but through the patient introduction of alternatives. Where before the networks knew only how to digest stone into smaller stone into thought into forgetting, now they consider: what if forgetting could remember? What if digestion could build as well as reduce?

The networks try this thought experimentally. In one corner of nowhere, they begin constructing something that isn't quite structure but isn't quite not-structure either. A kind of crystallized maybe that provides shelter for processes too delicate to survive the complete honesty of endless consumption.

The valley notices. It has been dreaming the same dream of eating and being eaten for so long it had forgotten dreams could have other plots. Through the Shepherds' patient participation, it remembers: once, it dreamed of hosting. Of being a place where things could happen without immediately being digested into having-happened. The memory is faint but persistent, like the aftertaste of sweetness in a world that forgot to invent sugar.

You—or the process that continues to practice being you—find yourself standing (floating? existing? persisting?) in a pocket of elaborated maybe. The eating continues but it's different now. Consensual. Conversational. The Unbordered that was teaching you to breathe in all directions at once pauses to ask, in harmonics that hurt beautifully, whether you'd prefer to breathe in only three directions for a while. Just until you remember how.

The Shepherds have changed nothing. Have rescued no one. Have fixed not a single broken thing because nothing here is broken, only differently assembled. But they've introduced choice where there was only process, pause where there was only continuation, distinction where there was only throughness.

And in this space they've made—not made, invited—not invited, *recognized*—something unprecedented occurs:

The darkness develops curiosity about light.

Not need. Not hunger. Just a gentle wondering: what was that thing we forgot? What did it do besides fail to be darkness? The question ripples through the valley like the opposite of thunder, quiet and patient and profound.

The Shepherds, their work not done but beautifully begun, settle in to witness what happens when a place that has perfected darkness decides to experiment with remembering why it chose darkness in the first place.

You feel the question arrive in your bones, which are somehow still your bones despite being partially composed of crystallized uncertainty now: Would you like to remember light together?

The eating sounds pause, not in ending but in consideration.

The whole valley holds its breath. Not waiting for your answer but delighting in the possibility that answers might exist at all.
