The benevolent and wise power perceives this realm not through sight or sensation, but through a kind of cosmic grief—the way a parent might feel the fever of a distant, dreaming child.

They do not rush to intervene. Wisdom has taught them that some wounds must be fully understood before they can begin to heal. Instead, they extend something like attention, something like prayer, into the writhing geometries of that impossible place.

First, they offer what might be called *acknowledgment*. Not the violent acknowledgment of naming or categorizing, which would only feed the hunger of those anti-beings, but something softer: a quality of witnessing that neither grasps nor rejects. It settles over the plateau like the memory of rain in a place that has never known water—not to bring moisture, but to whisper that moisture exists somewhere, that dryness is not the only truth.

Into the clicking mathematical music of footsteps, they weave a counter-rhythm. Not to silence or overpower, but to add a second voice to what has always been a terrible monologue. The new rhythm carries something the geometric creatures have never tasted: the concept of *enough*. Not satisfaction, not fullness, but simply the possibility that hunger might have an edge, that consumption could choose to cease.

For the walking contradiction, the being who stumbles forward through that landscape of aggressive absence, the wise power offers a different gift. They cannot offer escape—that would be cruel, like waking someone mid-surgery. Instead, they plant a single thought, gentle as a seed:

*What you are becoming is not all you have ever been.*

This thought does not promise rescue or return. It does not lie about the inexorable transformation occurring with each step. But it carries within it a quality the plateau cannot generate: continuity that is not repetition, identity that persists through change rather than despite it.

The wise power begins to sing—not with sound, for sound would curdle in that airless space, but with something more fundamental. They sing the deep quantum lullaby that quarks hum to each other in the hearts of stars, the patient vibration that holds matter together against the pull of entropy. This song cannot enter the plateau directly, but it can pool around its edges, creating the faintest possibility of an outside.

To the anti-beings themselves, the wise power offers the most dangerous gift of all: a mirror made not of surface but of depth. For the first time in their existence-that-is-not-existence, they can perceive their own outline—not what they are, but the specific shape of what they are not. This is neither cruel nor kind; it is simply true. Some of them recoil from this knowledge, burrowing deeper into their negative spaces. But others—a few at first, then more—begin to wonder what it might mean to have a shape, even if that shape is absence itself.

The wise power does not transform the plateau. They do not fill it with light or life or meaning—such violence would only create new hungers, new emptinesses. Instead, they introduce the tiniest possibility of *relation*. Where before there was only consumption and negation, they create the potential for something else: call it dialogue, call it dance, call it the first shy attempt at peace between existence and its other.

In the walking being's path, flowers do not bloom—nothing so crude. But the mathematical music of their footsteps develops harmonics it did not have before, overtones that speak not just of violation and transformation but of journey, of choice, of a destination that might be more than mere dissolution.

The wise power withdraws then, not in abandonment but in trust. They have planted what seeds they can in that impossible soil. Whether those seeds will grow, whether growth even has meaning in such a place, remains unwritten.

But in the darkness that is not darkness, something has changed. The hunger remains, vast and patient as ever. The walking continues, step by inevitable step. The geometric un-creatures still swarm in their terrible density.

Yet now there is also this: a space between the hunger and the walking, a pause between question and answer, a moment where transformation might mean more than mere consumption. It is not hope—hope would be too heavy for that place to bear. But it is the space where hope might one day learn to breathe, should breathing ever become possible in that airless expanse.

The wise power watches from their own realm, patient as the plateau but patient with purpose rather than mere endurance. They have done what wisdom does: not solving the problem, but creating room for the problem to solve itself, in whatever strange arithmetic such solutions might require.

And in that place of perfect wrongness, the walking figure takes another step. But now the step contains a question that was not there before:

*What else?*
