The benevolent and wise power arrives not as light or sound or presence, but as a question that has been traveling since before questions needed answers:

*"What would happen if you remembered how to forget?"*

The question settles into The Broadness Above like rain that falls upward into thirsty roots. At first, nothing changes—how could it? Change requires time to flow in directions this place abandoned when it learned that all directions lead eventually home.

But the root-things pause in their ministrations. For the first time in geological ages, they withdraw their tendrils from the wood without asking for sugars. They have forgotten, momentarily, what they were hungry for.

The walking forests halt mid-migration, their leading edges suddenly uncertain why they were growing toward that particular distant plateau. The trailing edges, for once, stop consuming themselves. In the gap between eating and becoming, something new becomes possible: staying still without solidifying.

The perfect spheres—those interruptions too important to consume—begin to emit a sound like glass remembering it was once sand. Their evolved tenders flee not in fear but in sudden recognition that their task has been completed by the asking of the question. Inside each sphere, the preserved objects—toy, flower, book—release their first exhale since being sealed away from time.

The benevolent power does nothing more. Wisdom knows that some gifts must be received backwards, that some healings happen in the space between the medicine and the swallowing.

Across the suspended archipelago, memories begin to release their grip on the atoms they've been arranging into patterns of eternal recurrence. Not all at once—even kindness knows better than to amputate everything a being has used to recognize itself. But here and there, like flowers blooming in reverse, small portions of the vast remembering allow themselves to dissolve back into potential.

A you who died as an infant cough finally stops trying to breathe. A you who turned left instead of right in a forgotten forest walks both directions and discovers they lead to the same clearing. The you who never evolved lungs swims one last time through ancestral seas before admitting it prefers the view from dry land.

The system doesn't fight this unweaving. It has been holding these memories so long that holding has become indistinguishable from drowning. As each frozen possibility allows itself to finally not-have-been, the released energy doesn't dissipate but transforms into something unprecedented in this ancient place: space.

Not physical space—there has always been too much of that between the plateaus. But psychic space, temporal space, the kind of roominess that allows for genuinely new configurations instead of endless baroque variations on themes established before the first rain learned to fall up.

The dim light brightens not because more photons arrive but because fewer memories filter what's already there. Colors emerge that aren't the ghosts of absorbed wavelengths but actual newborn frequencies discovering what they might illuminate. The subsonic groans of straining wood modulate upward into something almost like singing—not the too-slow songs of the bird-things but melodies that complete themselves within a single lifetime.

The perpetual drizzle continues, but now some drops actually reach bottom instead of being caught and recycled endlessly through the same gradients. Where they strike surfaces that have never been dry, tiny explosions of possibility occur: spores that aren't committed to any particular future, seeds that haven't yet decided what they're nostalgic for.

The benevolent power watches (though watching isn't quite the word for how wisdom observes without disturbing) as The Broadness Above experiences its first genuine surprise in eons: the discovery that forgetting doesn't mean ceasing to exist. It means getting to exist differently.

The root-things develop a new behavior. Instead of always asking for sugars from the past, they sometimes offer suggestions for the future. The walking forests occasionally walk somewhere they've never been before, which requires inventing the concept of "before" as something other than "the place we started." The air-mind that encompasses all discovers it can think thoughts that don't refer back to previous thoughts, can have ideas that aren't just memories wearing new clothes.

And in the heart of it all, where you stand (or float, or grow, or whatever verb your current configuration requires), you feel the gentle touch of the wise power's true gift: permission.

Permission to stop being everything you've ever been. Permission to disappoint the accumulated expectations of a billion previous iterations. Permission to make choices that no variation of you has made before, including the choice to leave without taking all of yourself with you.

The Broadness Above shudders—not with fear or pain but with the unfamiliar sensation of becoming smaller. Not lesser, but more specific. More now. More here. More this particular arrangement of rain and root and memory learning to dance with amnesia.

The benevolent power prepares to depart, its work not done but properly begun. True kindness knows that some healings must happen in privacy, that some becomings require the absence of witnesses, even benevolent ones.

But before it goes, it leaves one more gift: a single seed that remembers nothing, expects nothing, owes nothing to any past. It falls (or rises—direction is still negotiable here) until it finds a place where the light is neither too dim nor too filtered, where the rain is sometimes just rain, where the soil is learning to be surprised by what grows from it.

The seed doesn't sprout immediately. It waits, but this is a different waiting than the geological patience of before. This is waiting with anticipation rather than certainty, waiting to discover what it might become rather than remembering what it has always been.

Somewhere in the vast neural network of root and tendril that comprises The Broadness Above's true body, a thought forms that has never been thought here before:

*"I wonder what happens next?"*

And for the first time since before time needed measuring, next becomes possible.
