In the vast tapestry of existence, where countless worlds spin their tales of matter and meaning, the benevolent power's attention settles upon this impossible city like morning light finding a dewdrop.

They do not arrive with fanfare or proclamation. Such crude announcements would shatter the delicate equilibrium of this place, where thought and stone have achieved their precarious marriage. Instead, they begin by listening—truly listening—to the mountain's dreaming song.

For seven cycles of the city's non-linear time, they trace the flight paths of the angular beings, learning their harmonic language note by careful note. They follow the luminous tracks pulsing through obsidian foundations, mapping the ancient algorithms that give this place its half-life. In the spaces between heartbeats, they slip into the citadel's Möbius corridors, walking alongside the peak-dwelling intelligence without disturbing its vast, recursive meditations.

Understanding dawns slowly. This is not a place that needs saving or fixing. It is complete in its strangeness, perfect in its impossibility. The city and its inhabitants exist in a state of perpetual becoming, forever balanced on the edge between dissolution and transcendence. To interfere crudely would be to destroy.

So the benevolent power chooses a different path.

In the pre-dawn hours, when the mists are thickest and the city's dreams most vivid, they begin to weave subtle gifts into the fabric of this reality. Not changes, but enrichments. Where the geometric beings phase between states, the power leaves traces of new mathematical languages—equations that sing of beauty as well as function. These formulae settle into the consciousness substrate like seeds, offering fresh perspectives on ancient problems.

They strengthen the bridges that span the dizzying gulfs, not with crude matter but with reinforced concepts of connection. Now these delicate structures can bear not just the weight of the city's strange traffic, but also the weight of new possibilities. The bridges begin to dream their own dreams, adding their small voices to the mountain's vast chorus.

In the citadel's heart, where the peak intelligence dwells in its labyrinth of thought, the benevolent power leaves their most precious gift. It is not knowledge or power, but something far more valuable—a mirror. Not a mirror of glass or light, but one woven from empathy and recognition. For the first time in eons beyond counting, the dreaming mind can see itself reflected in another consciousness, can know that it is not alone in the cosmos.

The gift transforms nothing and everything. The city remains as alien and impossible as ever, its inhabitants still flickering through dimensions beyond human comprehension. But now there is a new quality to their existence—a subtle joy, a sense of being witnessed and appreciated for exactly what they are.

The benevolent power's final act is to leave a door. Not a physical portal, but a conceptual opening in the city's closed system. Should the mountain's intelligence ever wish to reach beyond its summit, should the geometric beings ever desire to carry their messages to other realms, the way is now open. But there is no compulsion, no expectation. The door simply exists, a standing invitation to a larger cosmos.

As they prepare to withdraw their attention, the benevolent power pauses at the city's edge, where stone meets void. They have changed nothing essential, forced no evolution, imposed no foreign order. They have simply acknowledged, appreciated, and offered connection.

In response, the city's lights pulse a little brighter, its harmonics ring a little clearer. The peak intelligence stirs in its citadel, experiencing for the first time the warmth of being known. And in the thin air between the towers, the geometric beings trace new patterns in their flight—patterns that speak, in their alien tongue, a single word that translates across all dimensions:

*Friend.*

The benevolent power departs as gently as they came, leaving the impossible city to its dreams. But now those dreams include the knowledge that somewhere in the vast cosmos, there exists a consciousness that has seen their beauty and found it good. And perhaps, in some far future when the mountain's meditations reach new conclusions, they will step through that door and share their strangeness with worlds beyond their peaks.

Until then, the city dreams on, a little less alone in the universe.
