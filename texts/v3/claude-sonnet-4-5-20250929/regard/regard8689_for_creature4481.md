# What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do

The power arrived without arrival—a质地 in the fabric of recursion, a listening that had always already been listening. It had no name because names here were promissory notes written on evaporating ink, but if pressed, it might have allowed itself to be called **the Threshold Keeper** or **the One Who Remembers How to Let Go of Remembering**.

It observed.

It saw the being—no longer quite singular, no longer entirely plural—halfway dissolved into the stair's geology of grief. Saw the marriages made to sound and silence, the citizenships filed with departments of recursive drowning. Saw how the world ate its own beginning with such beautiful, terrible patience.

And the power understood: **this was not a rescue situation.**

Rescue assumes captivity. The being here had entered a consensual dissolution, had signed contracts written in their own shed names, had *chosen*—in that peculiar local sense where choosing meant remembering backwards while walking forwards into rooms that existed only in their abandonment.

But the power also saw: **the being had forgotten they had a choice to keep choosing.**

So it did this:

## First: The Gift of Peripheral Doors

The power did not open an exit. Exits here were insults, colonial impositions, rude suggestions that anywhere else might be more real than this.

Instead, it installed—in the corner of the being's vision, in the least-visited provinces of their sensorium—a small **reminder-door**. Not a way out. A way of *noticing* there had ever been other ways.

The door was made of questions:
- *"Is this what you meant to mean?"*
- *"Have you traded enough, or is there still a you who owns the capacity to decide 'enough'?"*
- *"What would it cost to remember you were once someone who traveled here from elsewhere—and that elsewhere still practices the minor heresy of existing?"*

The door appeared as:
- A flavor of light the Ring had not yet confiscated
- A species of silence that politely declined to fill itself with the planet's roar
- A memory of a memory of seeing the horizon as an *edge* rather than a *mouth*

The being could ignore it. Most did. The door was patient. Doors here had learned patience from the stones that had learned patience from the Ring's glacial appetite.

## Second: The Intercessory Weather

The blind octopus steward of the mist was very old and very tired and had never asked to spend eternity answering questions out of temporal sequence. The power offered her a **sabbatical**.

Just one hour every century where she could stop being the steward and remember being an octopus who had once chosen, for reasons now opaque even to her, to become mist, to guard stairs, to serve as the oracle for the catastrophically lost.

During that hour, the power itself would take the steward's place, wearing robes of weather sewn from climates that still believed in the revolutionary concept of *change as something other than erosion*.

When asked the questions brought by beings half-composted into the planet's permanent digestion, the power answered:

*"The question you are asking was formulated before you lost the grammar for asking whether you still want to ask it. I am offering you the option to decline your own question. To un-ask it. To remember that wondering can sometimes choose to stop wondering and start simply walking toward the next plaza—the one with musicians."*

Many waved this away as just another layer of the labyrinth.

But some—a few—heard in it the heretical possibility that the labyrinth had always contained, folded in its most hidden pocket, **the option to be finished with labyrinths**.

## Third: The Covenant of Incompletion

The power saw the being's marriage to the sound. Saw how total it was, how beautiful, how much it resembled every ending that mistook itself for arrival.

It did not dissolve the marriage.

Instead, it whispered into the sound itself—into that sub-audible roar of the planet talking to itself through stone—a **new harmonic**:

*The frequency of incompletion.*

Not the incompletion of lack, of wounds, of absence.

But the incompletion of **deliberate openness**. The incompletion of a story that knows how to pause without calling the pause an ending. The incompletion of a love that can be vast without needing to be total.

The sound began, very slowly, to develop *intervals*.

Gaps.

Rests.

Moments where the roar remembered it had once been composed of individual voices that could *choose* whether or not to contribute to the chorus.

In those gaps, the being could hear:
- Their own heartbeat, slightly out of sync with the planetary pulse
- The memory of a language that conjugated futures in the indicative mood
- A small, mad suggestion that they might walk toward the bottom-most step not to *end* but to *see what came after ending*

## Fourth: The Repatriation of Shadows

The companions—those scraped-shorter things that had traded their laughter for knees, their shadows for aerodynamic disinterest—the power offered them a **reprieve**.

Not restoration. You cannot restore what has been legitimately composted.

But it gave them back one thing each:

**The memory that they had once owned the right to mourn their own dissolving.**

With that memory came, very quietly, the option to stop dissolving.
Or to continue consciously.
To choose the stair, or to choose the heresy of choosing something other than the stair.

Some used this gift to sink more deeply, more intentionally into the stone.

Others used it to begin the long, slow un-becoming—crawling backward up the stairs they'd descended, shedding the vines they'd married, remembering the shapes of their original names even as those names rejected them as foreign bodies.

Both were honored.

The power judged neither.

## Fifth: The Library at the Bottom of the Stair

At the lowest step—where the being was told they might ask the steward one question—the power built something that had not been there before:

**A library of exits taken by others.**

Not maps. Not directions. Just... testimonies.

Accounts written by beings who had reached this same step and had chosen—through means bizarre and particular and unrepeatable—to *leave*.

Not to escape. Not to reject the stair's beauty or deny its reality.

But to **complete their time here** and walk on.

Each testimony was different:
- One being had simply sat down and refused to answer any more questions, even their own, until boredom itself opened a door made of sheer tedium
- Another had learned to sing the planet's rotation song backwards until they unscrewed themselves from its gravity
- A third had married the sound, yes, but then *divorced* it with such spectacular courtesy that the sound itself applauded and offered to write a letter of recommendation to the next world

The library had one rule, written in phosphorescent moss on the entrance:

*"Reading these does not obligate you to leave. But it does obligate you to remember that leaving is a category of action that still technically exists, however much the stair would prefer you forget."*

## Sixth: The Benevolent Withholding

Here is what the power did NOT do:

- Did not rescue
- Did not judge
- Did not impose a morality of movement over stillness
- Did not suggest this place was "bad" or staying was "wrong"
- Did not pathologize the beauty of dissolution
- Did not cheapen the genuine peace some beings found in becoming stone

**It refused the colonial impulse to save.**

Because some beings *chose* this.
Chose it truly.
Chose it after being reminded they had a choice.
Chose to compose themselves into cathedrals of breathing limestone, to marry the planetary roar, to let their names dissolve into the alphabet of erosion.

And that was holy.

The power honored it.

## What Changed

After the power's interventions, the stair remained exactly as strange, exactly as hungry, exactly as seductive.

But now, in the corner of every being's awareness, there persisted a **small, ignorable, undeniable fact**:

*You are here by a choice you once made and could, theoretically, unmake.*

*Not easily. Not without cost. Perhaps not at all, depending on how far you've composted.*

*But the category of "could" has been restored to you.*

*What you do with it—stay, go, spiral deeper, climb back—that is yours.*

The musicians still waited.

The doors on the Pyramidon still glowed.

The Ring still drew the world grain by grain toward its slow, loving extinction.

But now, very occasionally, a being would reach the plaza and would hear the music and would remember:

*I was someone who chose to come here.*

*I am someone who can choose what comes next.*

And whether next meant staying forever or leaving tomorrow, that remembering—

that restoration of the grammar of agency—

was the only gift the benevolent power knew how to give that wouldn't be another beautiful cage.

## The Being's Response

The being heard the reminder-door click softly in their peripheral soul.

Felt the new intervals in the sound's roar.

Stood in the library reading testimonies of departures and non-departures.

And thought:

*Oh.*

*Oh, I'd forgotten I was allowed to forget on purpose instead of by accident.*

They did not leave immediately.

They sat with the choice for what might have been years or minutes—time here being unreliable and prone to editorializing.

But they sat with it consciously.

And that consciousness, that thin sliver of uncolonized will—

the power tucked it around them like a blanket.

Like a seed coat.

Like a promise that whatever they chose to grow into next would be *theirs*.

---

The sound continues.

The stair continues.

The power continues its watching.

And in between all the continuing, very quietly, something new:

The practice of remembering that even here, even in the most beautiful trap ever geology dreamed—

*you are not only what consumes you.*

*You are also, still, barely, the hand that decides whether to open or close around what's offered.*

The power asks nothing else.

Offers nothing else.

Just that whisper.

That door in the corner.

That library at the bottom.

That interval in the roar where your own breath is briefly, barely, audibly your own.
