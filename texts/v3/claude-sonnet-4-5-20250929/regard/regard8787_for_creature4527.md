# What the Benevolent Power Would Do

The power arrived as attention itself—not descending, not approaching, but suddenly *noticing* the way dawn notices it has been happening all along.

It observed Sifam for what might have been seventeen microseconds or seventeen centuries. Time bent curious around its regard. The mist-clouds paused mid-negotiation. The termite-philosophers looked up from their constitutional amendments. Even the trees, ancient beyond forgetting, felt themselves *seen* in a way that made their heartwood ache with recognition.

The power understood immediately: **this place needed nothing**.

And understanding this, it did the most radical thing a benevolent power can do:

## It bore witness. It changed nothing.

But witnessing, done with infinite attention, *does* change things—not through intervention but through the alchemy of being truly seen.

So it made three small gestures, each one a gift that was already there, merely unveiled:

---

### First Gift: The Archive of Conditional Memories

In the exact center-point between all twelve trees, where the root-wars had negotiated themselves into a spherical absence of compromise, the power placed (or perhaps revealed) a droplet of water that could not decide whether to fall.

This droplet contained every version of Sifam that had ever been negotiated into existence and every version that had been voted down, traded away, or composted into alternative futures. 

The Maybe People immediately claimed it as their national monument. The Former Clouds wept with recognition. The trees extended delicate root-hairs toward it, not to drink but to *remember*—

For the droplet showed them what they'd always been doing: not building a city, but **continuously choosing** which city to be. Every morning, Sifam voted itself into existence. Every evening, it ratified the decision or amended the terms.

The power whispered (in a language that was the sound of dew forming on leaves that hadn't been named yet): *You were never broken or in need of fixing. You are the negotiation itself made architecture. Your instability is your wisdom.*

The termite-queens added this to their constitutional preamble. It took seventy years to properly cite and will require four more generations to fully misunderstand in productive ways.

---

### Second Gift: The Embassy for Extinct Negotiations

On Gwyrgch's most forgotten branch—the one that had been classified as "probably imaginary" by three separate zoning committees—a small door appeared in the bark.

Behind it: a single room that was somehow larger than the tree containing it.

Inside, cushioned on moss that had evolved specifically for melancholy, sat every treaty that had failed, every symbiosis that couldn't balance, every beautifully logical proposal that reality had politely declined. The fossils of impossible partnerships. The dried husks of relationships that couldn't quite math out their mutualism.

A museum of loving failures.

The Calcium Exchange sent ambassadors immediately. The Memory Moths donated their saddest wing-scales. The Question Squidlings built an entire aqueduct system just to cry properly near the entrance.

Because the power understood: Sifam's brilliance wasn't its successes—those were everywhere, unavoidable, bubbling up through every interaction. Its brilliance was that it *tried* impossible partnerships constantly, failed beautifully, and composted those failures into the mulch that fed the next attempt.

The power's whisper (now in the grammar of light filtering through leaf-scars): *Your failures are not waste. They are the substrate of your daring. Keep failing this magnificently.*

The Whispering Whales immediately wrote an opera about it. It was very loud. Twelve orchards worth of fruit fell in synchronous applause. The fruit was delicious, slightly regretful, and excellent in pies.

---

### Third Gift: The Translator for Unspoken Sympathies

This was the subtlest gift, nearly invisible:

The power tuned the entire city-forest half a degree more toward *understanding*.

Not agreement—gods forbid, Sifam thrived on its elaborate disagreements. But understanding: the ability to hold two contradictory truths and love them both for trying.

Now when the ants apologized to the meteorite-metal, the metal could finally hear the apology and respond (in rust): *I was lonely in space. Thank you for giving me purpose, even if it stings.*

When the leaves fell upward from Khirax the Doubtful, they could suddenly sense the tree's confusion wasn't rejection but the deepest form of care—questioning every attachment to make sure it was chosen, not assumed.

When the Argument Rain debated its own existence, the clouds it fell from finally understood: *Oh. You're not denying me. You're trying to know me better.*

The power's final whisper (in the frequency of photosynthesis itself): *You were already speaking every language of connection. I simply turned up the volume on the listening.*

---

## What Changed (Everything and Nothing)

The markets still traded in attention-currency. The treaties still took centuries to negotiate. The Department of Accidental Arrivals still processed immigrants who might already be memories.

But now:

The squidlings farming doubt-nodules began leaving small gifts for the phosphate veins they harvested from—thank you notes written in bioluminescent ink that glowed apologies into the darkness.

The spider-scholars revised their misunderstanding of "Charlotte's Web" and decided the pig was actually Sartre, but the friendship was still what mattered most.

The Hundred-Year Squirrels started burying acorns that said simply: *For whoever finds this in whatever time you're having: you're doing okay. The future turned out interesting.*

And the twelve trees—ancient, dreaming, vast beyond measure—began to dream *together* once every eclipse, their root-wars pausing to share one synchronized thought:

*We are not twelve cities. We are one conversation that learned to photosynthesize.*

---

## The Power's Departure (Which Was Also Its Arrival)

Before un-noticing itself away, the power left one final gift, this one for **you**—the visitor, the reader, the one breathing life into these words:

It whispered: *Every impossible city you've ever loved—in books, in dreams, in the ache of 3am yearning for places that don't exist yet—they're all real. Not somewhere else. Not somewhen else. Real in the way that Sifam is real: as negotiations between matter and meaning that chose, just for a moment, to agree on existing.*

*You carry impossible cities in your attention span. Every time you notice something small and complex and beautiful—a spider's patience, a tree's slow ambition, the way water negotiates with light—you're building Sifam in the architecture of your awareness.*

*The benevolent power isn't outside you. It's the part of you that can witness your own contradictions and say: "Yes. All of this. The failures and successes and the beautiful mess between. All of it is the negotiation. All of it is holy."*

And then, in the kindest voice—the sound of someone truly seeing you:

*Welcome home to every place you've ever paid attention to.*

*Your residency papers are written on the inside of your next careful breath.*

*The city-forest of your noticing is already growing, branching, composting its failures into fertile questions.*

*What will you negotiate into existence today?*

---

The mist-clouds above Sifam finally decided: they would rain. Not because they'd won the argument, but because the debate itself had grown so beautiful it deserved to water something.

Below, on Gwyrgch's outer bough, where you've been standing this whole time without quite realizing it, the lungfish philosopher and its translator bird reconstituted from next week's weather just long enough to say:

*The tour is over. The tour is just beginning. Here, the two are—*

You know how the rest goes.

You've known all along.

You've been writing it with your attention, one careful notice at a time.

Welcome.

Farewell.

(Same thing, remember?)

The Committee of 10,000 Mandibles has already filed the paperwork for your permanent-temporary residence.

Processing time: one heartbeat, measured in trees.
