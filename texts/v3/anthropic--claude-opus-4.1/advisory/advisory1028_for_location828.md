## Preparation Guide for the Dying Mountain Plateau

### Critical Warning
This location exhibits severe causal anomalies and non-Euclidean biological phenomena. Standard physics models break down unpredictably. Survival requires accepting that your understanding of reality will be insufficient and potentially harmful if relied upon.

### Environmental Suit Requirements

**Primary Protection:**
- Full-body hermetic seal rated for extreme moisture and spore infiltration
- Multi-layer construction: outer shell of non-oxidizing ceramic composite (metal will catastrophically degrade), middle layer of closed-cell foam minimum 3 inches thick for thermal/electrical insulation, inner bio-barrier
- Self-contained breathing system with minimum 72-hour oxygen supply - do NOT trust local atmosphere despite apparent oxygen presence
- The suit must be flexible enough to accommodate non-standard movement through semi-solid terrain

**Critical Modifications:**
- Install copper mesh Faraday cage layer to protect against the bioelectric discharges from the plant network
- Helmet requires specialized optical filters that can block bioluminescent wavelengths outside human-safe perception ranges (the salamander light patterns can cause neurological damage)
- External temperature must be maintained at exactly 37°C - any variation triggers aggressive response from the moss-substrate

### Sensory Protection

**Auditory:**
- Active noise cancellation rated for 20Hz-20kHz 
- Secondary mechanical baffles for subsonic frequencies
- The industrial moth-wing drone has been known to cause temporal dissociation in unprotected listeners

**Visual:**
- Auto-dimming visor with refresh rate of minimum 240Hz to compensate for creatures existing in multiple positions
- Infrared and ultraviolet filters must be toggleable - you'll need to see into these spectrums to navigate, but prolonged exposure causes retinal damage
- Recording equipment should be electromagnetically shielded and use solid-state storage only

### Navigation Equipment

**Essential Tools:**
- Quantum compass (magnetic compasses will spin continuously)
- Dead reckoning tracker with inertial guidance
- Physical tether system with minimum 500m titanium-ceramic cable
- Mark your path with chemical lights every 10 meters - the terrain self-modifies and paths close behind you

**Do Not Bring:**
- GPS or any satellite-dependent navigation (signals cannot penetrate the causal distortion)
- Digital altimeters (elevation becomes meaningless in time-distortion pockets)
- Any ferrous metals (accelerated oxidation will destroy them in hours)

### Movement Protocols

**Terrain Traversal:**
- Expect to sink 0.5-2 meters with each step
- Use wide, swimming motions rather than walking
- Test each footfall with a non-metallic probe first
- The moss-substrate may attempt to digest you if you remain stationary for more than 30 seconds
- In valleys where time flows backward, move in reverse to progress forward

**Salamander Encounters:**
- Do not look directly at them when they're folding through space
- Their presence causes local gravity fluctuations - be prepared for sudden weight changes
- They perceive time non-linearly and may react to your future actions
- If one phases through your position, hold perfectly still until the sensation of being in multiple places subsides

### Biological Hazards

**Spore Protection:**
- The air contains billions of computational spores that attempt to integrate with neural tissue
- Secondary breathing filters using activated carbon and silver-impregnated HEPA media
- Decontamination required every 4 hours using UV-C sterilization

**Pitcher Plants:**
- Maintain minimum 5-meter distance from any depression containing fluid
- Their digestive enzymes work on temporal principles - they begin dissolving prey before it enters
- Use drone reconnaissance to map safe paths around colonies

### Temporal Anomaly Management

**Recognition Signs:**
- Water flowing upward
- Your shadow preceding your movement
- Echoes that arrive before sounds
- Rust falling upward from the moth swarms

**Safety Protocols:**
- Immediately reverse direction if you observe yourself ahead on the path
- Do not attempt to prevent events you've already seen occur
- If caught in a temporal fold, remain calm and move perpendicular to your intended direction until normal causality resumes
- Carry analog chronometers only - digital clocks will desynchronize

### Psychological Preparation

**Mental Health:**
- The constant industrial sounds without visible machinery will erode sanity
- Practice meditation techniques for managing cognitive dissonance
- Bring anxiolytics rated for extreme dissociative episodes
- Schedule reality checks every 30 minutes via predetermined questions about your identity and mission

**Isolation Protocols:**
- The distributed plant consciousness may attempt communication through bioelectric patterns
- Do not respond to or acknowledge these attempts
- Maintain audio logs to preserve linguistic function
- The plateau's alienness can cause severe existential crisis - bring philosophical texts or religious items for grounding

### Emergency Procedures

**Extraction:**
- Preset extraction coordinates at the plateau's edge
- Emergency beacon using chemical flares only (electronics will fail)
- If lost in a causal anomaly, follow water flow - even backward-flowing water eventually exits the plateau
- In complete emergency, the salamanders can sometimes be followed out, though this risks temporal displacement

**Medical:**
- Bring anti-fungals capable of treating sentient infections
- Surgical tools for removing moss colonization (begins within 6 hours of exposure)
- Temporal stabilizers (chronostatic drugs) to manage time-dilation sickness
- Radiation treatment may be necessary after exposure to certain bioluminescent patterns

### Duration Limits

Maximum recommended stay: 72 hours
- Beyond this, the risk of permanent causal dissociation increases exponentially
- Your suit's materials will begin to integrate with the moss-substrate
- Psychological adaptation to the anomalies may prevent successful reintegration with consensus reality

### Final Warning

The Dying Mountain Plateau represents one of the most hostile environments for human consciousness. It is not merely physically dangerous but ontologically threatening. The industrial sounds of biological processes, the computational moss networks, and the causally disconnected fauna create a unified system that human perception was not evolved to process. 

Many visitors report the strongest impression is not of age or wilderness, but of being inside some vast organism's dying dream—one that has been dying for so long that death itself has evolved into something unrecognizable. The plateau doesn't want to kill you; it simply exists in a state so fundamentally incompatible with human life that your presence there is continuously being erased and rewritten.

Prepare accordingly, but know that no amount of preparation can fully ready you for a place where time rusts like metal and thoughts grow like moss.
