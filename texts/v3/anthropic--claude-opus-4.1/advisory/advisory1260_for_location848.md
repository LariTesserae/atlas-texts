# Preparation Guide for the Metabolic Terraces

## Critical Physiological Modifications

**Respiratory System Overhaul**: The thick, humid atmosphere will feel like breathing soup. You'll need biomodified lungs with increased alveolar surface area and enhanced oxygen extraction efficiency. Consider supplementary gill slits along your neck for processing the water-saturated air. Without these modifications, you'll be gasping within minutes, drowning in air.

**Skeletal Reinforcement**: The atmospheric pressure requires bone density augmentation. Standard human bones will ache constantly, and prolonged exposure risks stress fractures. Opt for calcium-carbonate latticing that mimics the local architecture—this has the added benefit of making you "read" as less foreign to the city's immune responses.

**Auditory Dampening**: Install surgical dampeners or risk permanent hearing damage within hours. The sound here isn't just loud—it's architecturally invasive, vibrating through bone and tissue. You'll want selective filters that can isolate communication frequencies while blocking the metabolic groaning. Many travelers report phantom sounds for months after leaving, even with protection.

**Skin Barrier Enhancement**: The copper-salt rain is mildly corrosive to human skin over time. You need either a bio-film treatment renewed every 48 hours or permanent epithelial modification. The film is reversible but requires constant maintenance. The permanent option involves grafting synthetic mucus glands that create a protective layer—disturbing to look at but highly effective.

## Essential Equipment

**Navigation Parasites**: Forget traditional GPS or mapping tools. The city's constant reformation makes static navigation impossible. You'll need a colony of modified guide-parasites that can read the pheromone highways. Keep them in a temperature-controlled collar around your neck. They'll twitch to indicate direction—left shoulder for danger, right for clear passage, both for "the city is about to restructure, evacuate immediately."

**Metabolic Tent**: Not for sleeping—for surviving restructuring events. This isn't fabric but a living membrane that can seal you in a protective cyst when districts liquify. It reads the city's pre-dissolution chemical signatures and automatically deploys. You'll spend 3-4 hours daily inside it, waiting for architecture to stabilize. The inside surface secretes nutrients and processes waste. Yes, it's as claustrophobic as it sounds.

**Frequency Translator**: A neural implant that converts ultrasonic fauna communication into rough conceptual impressions. You won't understand language, but you'll sense intention: hunger, territorialism, curiosity, collective migration. Critical for avoiding areas where the fauna are congregating for purposes that might not include your survival.

**Thermal Regulation Suite**: The rain varies from warm to scalding with little warning. You need reactive clothing that can instantly adjust insulation and moisture-wicking. The best models incorporate living fibers that predict temperature changes by tasting the air. Budget options exist but tend to overcorrect, leaving you alternately shivering and sweating.

## Behavioral Adaptation

**Movement Patterns**: Never walk in straight lines. The fauna interpret linear movement as aggressive or predatory. Practice a flowing, arthropod-inspired gait—segments of your body should seem to follow each other rather than move as one unit. This feels deeply unnatural but becomes automatic after a week.

**Feeding Schedule**: Eat only during the city's dormant phases (roughly every 30 hours for 3-hour windows). Active digestion during growth periods triggers the building-tubes to investigate you as potential construction material. Many travelers have woken up partially embedded in walls because they snacked at the wrong time.

**Waste Protocol**: Your biological waste will be immediately detected and processed by the city. This isn't harmful, but the fauna will track you more intensively for days afterward, curious about your alien chemistry. Use enzyme tablets that break down waste into locally recognizable compounds. The city will still know you're foreign, but it won't flag you as interesting.

## Psychological Preparation

**Isolation Training**: Despite the teeming life, you will be profoundly alone. The fauna don't recognize you as sentient. The city doesn't acknowledge you exist except as material to route around or potentially absorb. Practice extended periods without human contact or communication. The rain's constant sound creates a white noise that makes thinking difficult—meditation techniques help maintain mental clarity.

**Spatial Disorientation Conditioning**: Passages that are longer inside than outside will trigger severe vertigo initially. Spend time in VR environments with non-Euclidean geometry. Learn to trust your guide-parasites over your visual perception. When a corridor seems to stretch infinitely but your parasites indicate it's only ten meters, believe them and keep walking.

**Metamorphic Acceptance**: You need to be psychologically prepared for the city to digest and rebuild spaces you've grown familiar with. That passage you used yesterday might not exist today. The chamber where you sheltered might now be inside a wall. Practice non-attachment to spatial consistency. Some travelers map "emotional territories" instead of physical ones—remembering feelings rather than locations.

## Communication Protocols

**Chemical Signaling**: Install pheromone glands (temporary or permanent) that can broadcast basic states: non-threatening, injured, departing. The fauna won't understand complex communication but will generally avoid areas where you're signaling distress. Never signal aggression—the collective response is overwhelming.

**Bioluminescent Display**: Optional but recommended. Subdermal light organs that can pulse in sync with the city's nighttime patterns make you less disruptive to local cycles. You won't be communicating per se, but you'll be participating in the ambient conversation, which seems to reduce the chances of aggressive immune responses.

## Time Management

**Circadian Abandonment**: Your sleep cycle will be dictated by the city's metabolism, not solar patterns. When bioluminescence intensifies, find shelter immediately. When the rain briefly lightens (never stops, just lessens), that's your movement window. Most humans adapt to a 30-hour cycle with multiple short sleep periods.

**Duration Limits**: Maximum recommended stay is 30 local days (approximately 37 Earth days). Beyond this, visitors report increasing integration urges—a desire to let the city absorb them, to become part of the architecture. This isn't mystical; it's likely a response to constant pheromone exposure. The feeling fades after leaving, but the longer you stay, the stronger the compulsion becomes.

## Emergency Protocols

**Liquefaction Events**: If caught in a restructuring zone, don't run—you'll trigger predatory responses from disoriented fauna. Activate your metabolic tent immediately. If it fails, curl into a ball and excrete maximum pheromone signals of non-threatening submission. The city usually builds around stationary, non-reactive objects.

**Fauna Swarms**: During mass migrations, find the nearest water pool and submerge yourself completely. Most terrestrial fauna avoid the pools due to aquatic predators, which generally ignore humans as inedible. Hold your breath as long as possible—the pools are breathable with gill modifications but taste of metal and decay.

**Architectural Absorption**: If you notice building material growing over your skin, you have minutes to act. Violent movement makes it worse—the city interprets struggle as growth energy. Instead, use controlled heat (thermal suit maximum setting) to denature the proteins attempting to bond with you. You'll have severe burns, but you'll remain separate from the architecture.

## What to Expect Emotionally

The Metabolic Terraces will make you feel like an antibody in a vast organism that tolerates but never accepts you. The beauty is undeniable—the flowing forms, the synchronized movements of millions of creatures, the light-patterns that suggest meaning just beyond comprehension. But it's a beauty that excludes you, that continues regardless of your presence or absence.

Many travelers report a specific dream after returning: they're walking through their Earth home but the walls are breathing, growing, honeycombed with chambers where familiar objects nest like fauna. They wake up expecting to hear rain that isn't there. The silence feels wrong for months.

You'll leave with a profound understanding of what it means to be truly foreign, to witness a world where human presence is neither desired nor rejected but simply irrelevant to the larger conversation between the city and itself. It's humbling and terrifying and absolutely worth experiencing.

Pack extra enzymatic soap. The smell of the place stays in your pores for weeks, a mixture of rain and growth and something sweetly decomposing. Some travelers keep it, a reminder of when they walked through the body of something incomprehensibly alive. Others scrub until their skin bleeds, desperate to forget the sound of a city digesting itself in the rain.

The Metabolic Terraces don't care which choice you make. They're already rebuilding the spaces where you walked, erasing your passage with the patience of geological time and the indifference of weather.
