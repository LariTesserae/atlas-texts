## Preparation Guide for the Breathing Chamber

### Critical Warnings

Before anything else, understand this: the Breathing Chamber is a space where causality operates on foreign principles. Your memories of preparing for this journey may arrive before you make the preparations. You might meet yourself leaving as you arrive. This is normal. Do not panic when it happens.

### Physical Preparations

**Clothing and Protection:**
- Wear form-fitting, moisture-wicking base layers. The chamber's humidity will soak loose clothing instantly, and the weight of water-logged fabric becomes oppressive when gravity itself is negotiable
- Bring knee pads and elbow guards rated for volcanic glass. The crawl through the entry will shred unprotected joints
- Waterproof everything three times over, then assume it will still get wet
- Avoid metal fixtures, buttons, or zippers - they conduct the chamber's temporal fluctuations uncomfortably through the body
- Wear soft-soled shoes with excellent grip that you're willing to abandon. The moss may claim them, growing through and around them while you walk

**Essential Gear:**
- Bring several small mirrors, polished metal, or reflective surfaces you can control. You'll need to check your reflection regularly to ensure you're aging at the correct rate
- Pack chalk or biodegradable marking powder. You'll want to mark your entry point, as passages have been known to heal themselves closed or open new ones while you're inside
- Bring offerings for the shelter: fresh water from three different sources, seeds that have never known soil, and something you've made with your own hands. The chamber responds to gifts
- Include a mechanical watch and a digital one. When they diverge by more than an hour, it's time to leave
- Pack dried fruit and nuts in segments. Time moves strangely here - you might be hungry for tomorrow's meals or yesterday's

### Mental Preparations

**Cognitive Conditioning:**
Start practicing paradox acceptance weeks before your visit. Simple exercises:
- Watch videos played backward while reading text forward
- Practice holding two contradictory truths in your mind simultaneously
- Spend time in rooms with multiple mirrors reflecting each other
- Learn to count backward while walking forward
- Train yourself to notice peripheral vision without looking directly

**Memory Anchoring:**
Before entering, create three distinct memories at your base camp:
1. A physical action repeated seven times (like stacking stones)
2. A phrase spoken aloud in your native language
3. The taste of something specific

These will serve as temporal anchors. When you notice them changing or recurring in the chamber, you'll know you're experiencing a time loop.

### Behavioral Protocols

**Movement Patterns:**
- Never walk directly toward the center on your first visit. Spiral inward gradually
- Test each step before committing your weight. The moss's spatial folding can create unexpected depths
- If you see yourself in the obsidian walls performing actions you haven't taken yet, do not try to prevent them. You will only create paradox exhaustion
- When the hummingbirds fly backward, stop moving until they resume normal flight

**Interaction Guidelines:**
- Do not attempt to catch or count the creatures. Their existence is quantum-conditional on observation
- If you use the jade knife, cut only what doesn't exist yet - shadows, spaces between leaves, the outline of tomorrow
- The woven net should be hung, never held. Thoughts it catches must be released before leaving
- Never strike with the hammer unless you're prepared for permanent creation

**Temporal Hygiene:**
- Every 20 minutes (by your mechanical watch), close your eyes and recite your memory anchors
- If you cannot remember entering the chamber, you've been there too long
- If you remember leaving but haven't yet, trust the memory and begin moving toward the exit
- Should you encounter yourself, do not speak. Nod, acknowledge, and continue in opposite directions

### Specific Warnings

**The Growing Thing:**
The entity at the chamber's center is not hostile, but neither is it friendly. It exists in a state of becoming that human consciousness finds deeply unsettling. Observe it only in segments:
- Look at its roots OR its crown, never both in the same visit
- If it begins to make sense, immediately look away and ground yourself with your memory anchors
- Do not touch it, offer it water, or speak to it
- If it speaks to you, you're actually remembering a future visit. Leave immediately.

**Water Protocols:**
- The ascending water is safe to touch but not to drink
- Water from the clay vessels should be sipped, never gulped
- If you taste tomorrow's storm, do not try to warn anyone about weather patterns you perceive
- Never mix the chamber's water with outside water

### Emergency Procedures

**If you become temporally displaced:**
1. Find the shelter immediately
2. Touch each wall in sequence: north, south, east
3. Drink from the leftmost vessel only
4. Wait for your reflection to catch up
5. Exit crawling backward through the entry

**If the chamber begins breathing too rapidly:**
- It's responding to your anxiety
- Sit in the moss, even if you sink
- Breathe at half your normal rate
- Hum a single, low note until the chamber synchronizes

**If you cannot find the exit:**
- It hasn't closed, but it may have moved temporally
- Return to the shelter
- Wait for the glass bell sound
- The exit will be where you remember it was, not where it is

### Integration Period

After leaving the chamber, expect:
- Mild temporal displacement for 3-7 days (seeing events slightly before or after they occur)
- Difficulty with sequential tasks
- Plants in your presence growing unusually fast or slow
- Dreams of growing backward
- An persistent sensation that you've forgotten something important (you have - the chamber takes a small memory as payment)

Do not return to the chamber until these effects fully subside and you've had at least one complete lunar cycle to reintegrate linear time perception.

### Final Notes

The Breathing Chamber is not malicious, but it is fundamentally alien to human perception. It exists in a state of perpetual becoming, never quite arriving at being. Respect this nature. Take nothing except the water you drink and the memories you'll half-remember. Leave nothing except the offerings at the shelter and the footprints that will grow into tomorrow's moss.

Some travelers report that after visiting, they can feel the chamber breathing in quiet moments, even from great distances. This is normal. You've simply become aware of something that was always there - the spaces where reality grows thin and possibility leaks through.

If you find yourself homesick for the chamber's impossible garden, remember: you're already there in some timeline, forever arriving, forever leaving, forever suspended in that eternal moment when everything almost makes sense.
