# Preparation Guide for the Vertical Industrial Anomaly

## Critical Warnings

This location operates on non-Euclidean principles. Your sense of direction, gravity, and linear time will be compromised. The environment is not actively hostile but is fundamentally incompatible with baseline human perception. Mental preparation is as crucial as physical equipment.

## Physical Preparations

### Clothing and Protection

**Primary Layer:** Wear moisture-wicking synthetic base layers. Cotton will never dry here and will accelerate hypothermia. The perpetual dampness combined with industrial surfaces creates a constant heat-sapping effect.

**Outer Shell:** A rubberized industrial raincoat with sealed seams is essential. Not for rain, but for the constant condensation drip. The coating should be chemical-resistant — the water here carries dissolved metals and industrial compounds that will corrode standard fabrics over time.

**Footwear:** Steel-toed boots with aggressive anti-slip soles, treated with hydrophobic coating. Every surface is slick. Every single one. The catwalks have no guardrails designed for human proportions. One slip could send you falling through multiple non-contiguous levels simultaneously.

**Gloves:** Thick rubber industrial gloves with textured grips. You'll need to climb, and the metal is always wet, always cold, and sometimes phases between solid and permeable states without warning.

### Respiratory Protection

Bring multiple P100 respirators with vapor cartridges. The air is thick with moisture that carries industrial particulates and biological spores from the lichen. More concerning: the air sometimes contains colors that lungs weren't meant to process. You'll know when this happens because your breath will taste like copper and mathematics.

### Navigation Equipment

**Do not rely on electronics.** Magnetic compasses spin randomly. GPS signals arrive from satellites that don't exist in this dimension. Digital devices experience temporal inconsistencies — your phone might display times from next week or last year.

Instead, bring:
- Mechanical chalk for marking paths (the moisture means it must be oil-based)
- Several spools of aircraft cable (minimum 500ft each)
- Carabiners to secure yourself when geometry becomes negotiable
- A mechanical watch (it will run incorrectly but consistently, allowing relative time measurement)

## Biological Considerations

### Water and Food

**Never consume the local water.** The condensation farms produce something that looks like water, tests as water, but exists in too many states simultaneously. Witnesses report drinking it and feeling hydrated yesterday, or being thirsty in directions that don't exist.

Bring:
- Sealed water in metal containers (plastic degrades here)
- Calorie-dense preserved foods that don't require heating
- Salt tablets (you'll lose electrolytes to the constant moisture)

### Medical Preparations

Start taking the following two weeks before arrival:
- Industrial-strength antifungals (the lichen is opportunistic)
- Iron supplements (the magnetic fields here deplete hemoglobin)
- Anti-vertigo medication (for when up and down become suggestions)

Pack:
- Surgical sutures (cuts from the metal are invariably deep and geometric)
- Broad-spectrum antibiotics (the flickering fauna carry diseases that exist in probability clouds)
- Antipsychotics (temporary use for when the geometry becomes too insistent)

## Psychological Preparations

### Perceptual Training

Spend time before your visit practicing:
- Peripheral vision awareness (things here exist more solidly when not directly observed)
- Accepting contradictory information (you will see yourself on different levels simultaneously)
- Suppressing your startle reflex (the pigeons phase through your body; reacting makes it worse)

### Mental Anchoring

Create a strong mental image of:
- Your home universe's physics
- The concept of linear time
- The number of dimensions you're supposed to exist in

You'll need these anchors when the industrial logic begins making sense. That's when you're in the most danger — when the city's patterns start seeming reasonable, when you understand why the factories produce components for machines that assemble themselves backwards through time.

## Interaction Protocols

### The Workers

You'll encounter humanoid forms operating machinery. Do not attempt communication. They exist in a different temporal flow and perceive your presence as a smear across several weeks. If you must pass them, move slowly and consistently. Sudden movements cause temporal shear that they experience as painful.

### The Fauna

**The Pigeons:** Ignore them completely. Looking directly at them causes them to multiply across probability states. You'll be surrounded by potential pigeons, all equally real.

**The Cats:** They're aware of you but indifferent. If one phases through you, don't move until it completes its passage. Being inside solid matter when they re-corporealize is unpleasant for both parties.

**The Pool Things:** Stay away from collection pools. The fish-things school in patterns that encode information your brain will try to decode. Success causes temporal displacement. Failure causes migraines that exist in four dimensions.

### The Lichen

Don't touch it directly. It feeds on metal ions but can adapt to calcium if given opportunity. Small patches are harmless, but larger colonies have been observed demonstrating collective intelligence, growing in patterns that spell out warnings in languages that won't exist for decades.

## Navigation Strategy

### Vertical Movement

The city builds upward, but "up" is negotiable. Follow the water channels — they always flow toward local down, even when local down is sideways to universal down. When you encounter impossible stairs:
1. Close your eyes
2. Walk forward twenty steps
3. Open your eyes
4. Accept your new position without trying to understand the transition

### Finding Shelter

Safe zones exist in the gaps between industrial cycles. Look for:
- Factories with dim orange glows (dormant phase, won't suddenly activate)
- Platforms where the lichen grows in perfect circles (indicates stable geometry)
- Areas where the condensation falls upward (temporal null zones, good for rest)

### Duration of Stay

Plan for no more than 72 subjective hours. Longer exposure results in:
- Synchronization with industrial time (moving too slowly to leave)
- Oxidation of iron in your blood
- Developing extra wings (reversible if caught early)

## Emergency Procedures

### If You Become Lost

Find the nearest condensation farm and follow its pipes downward. They always eventually lead to collection pools. From there, look for the flickering workers. Move against their temporal flow (you'll feel resistance, like walking through syrup) until you reach the edge of their shift. Reality is thinner there, easier to punch through back to baseline.

### If Geometry Fails

Should you find yourself existing on multiple levels simultaneously:
1. Choose the self that feels most solid
2. Reject the others (this will hurt)
3. Consume salt immediately (grounds you in single-state existence)
4. Move perpendicular to the direction of impossibility

### If You Begin to Understand

The most dangerous moment is when the city's logic becomes clear, when you understand why the factories must process backwards, why the water must rise before it falls, why the pigeons exist in quantum superposition. This understanding means you're adapting to local physics.

Immediately:
- Recite prime numbers (they're the same in all realities)
- Inflict minor pain (pinch yourself, bite your tongue)
- Remember something impossible from your home universe (true randomness, natural entropy, birds with the correct number of wings)

## Return Protocol

Leaving requires finding a threshold — a place where the industrial logic hasn't fully consumed reality. These appear as:
- Shadows that fall toward light sources
- Rust that grows backwards into clean metal
- Windows that show different exteriors from inside and outside

Mark your entry point immediately upon arrival. The city builds over itself constantly, and your entrance might be three levels up or down by the time you're ready to leave.

## Final Notes

This place operates on industrial efficiency taken to metaphysical extremes. Every process feeds another, every waste becomes input, every cycle exists because it must. Don't try to understand the purpose. The city might be manufacturing reality itself, processing raw existence into structured form, or it might simply be exercising industrial behavior with no goal beyond its own continuation.

Bring a camera if you must, but know that photographs will show different scenes each time you look at them, aging and proceeding through their own industrial cycles. The only reliable record is memory, and even that becomes negotiable after enough exposure to the brightness without source.

The city builds itself from itself, and anything that stays too long becomes material for the next iteration. Respect its patience, don't trust its geometry, and always, always watch for the rain that falls upward — it means you're deeper in the maze than you should be, and finding your way back will require accepting impossible things as true.

Remember: you're visiting a place where industrial logic has replaced physical law. The normal rules are not suspended but overwritten. Navigate carefully, think laterally, and don't stay long enough to make sense.
