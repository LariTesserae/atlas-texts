## Preparation Guide for The Condensate Works

### Critical Warnings
The Condensate Works is an environment of constant mechanical metamorphosis. Your survival depends on maintaining your distinction from the machinery. Once incorporation begins, extraction becomes exponentially more difficult with each passing minute.

### Essential Equipment

**Protective Gear:**
- **Full-body industrial chainmail suit** - Not medieval armor, but modern industrial-grade stainless steel mesh used in meat processing. This prevents the mechanical tendrils from finding purchase on your skin while remaining flexible enough for movement. Wear it over a moisture-wicking base layer.
- **Magnetically neutral boots** - Thick rubber soles with ceramic plates. Steel-toed boots will attract the magnetic fauna and risk incorporation. The soles should be at least 3 inches thick to delay tendril penetration.
- **Neck guard and hood** - Kevlar-reinforced collar that locks to the chainmail. The hood should be tight-fitting mesh. Many visitors have been incorporated starting from exposed neck areas where tendrils found entry points.

**Timing Device:**
- **Mechanical wind-up timer** - Set for 45-minute intervals. Digital devices fail immediately. When it rings, you must move at least 20 feet from your current position. Staying still for more than an hour guarantees partial incorporation as the architecture builds around you.

**Movement Aids:**
- **Telescoping ceramic pole** - 8-10 feet extended length. Test floors before stepping. What appears solid might be a thin film over machinery. The pole also helps push away curious fauna.
- **Industrial lubricant spray** - At least 6 cans. Spray your suit every 15 minutes to prevent mechanical adhesion. WD-40 is insufficient; you need heavy machinery-grade lubricant.

### Pre-Visit Conditioning

**Physical Preparation:**
- Practice moving in the chainmail suit for at least two weeks. The weight distribution is unusual and the Works require constant movement.
- Pressure adaptation exercises - spend time in pressure chambers with rapid cycling between 0.5 and 2 atmospheres. Your ears must be able to equalize quickly and repeatedly.
- Balance training on moving surfaces. Set up a platform on hydraulic pistons if possible. The floor will never be stable.

**Mental Preparation:**
- Study industrial machinery diagrams until you can identify common mechanisms instantly. Recognizing a nascent turbine formation gives you seconds to avoid being caught in its assembly.
- Practice peripheral awareness meditation. You must track movement in all directions simultaneously. The creatures communicate through the superstructure vibrations - feeling these patterns can warn you of incoming architectural shifts.
- Abandon any attachment to conventional physics or biology. Attempting to understand the fauna taxonomically will cause dangerous hesitation.

### Navigation Strategy

**Entry Protocol:**
1. Upon entering through the maintenance hatch, immediately spray your entire suit with lubricant
2. Count to thirty while observing the current configuration cycle
3. Move decisively in the direction of least mechanical density
4. Never retrace your path - it won't exist anymore

**Movement Patterns:**
- Adopt a "shark swimming" approach - constant forward movement with gentle weaving
- Step-pause-step rhythm: take a step, pause for half a second to let tendrils retract, take another step
- When walls begin transformation, move perpendicular to the direction of change
- If floor becomes ceiling, grab the nearest stable pipe and swing with the rotation - don't fight it

**Fauna Interaction:**
- The wrench-legged arthropods are harmless if you move slowly around them
- Gear-beings floating on probability clouds should be given 3 feet clearance - they destabilize local architecture
- If an ambulatory factory approaches, toss small metal objects (bring washers) in the opposite direction as distraction
- Never touch the oil-filled pupation sacs - the nascent creatures inside react violently to organic contact

### Emergency Procedures

**Early Incorporation Signs:**
- Skin feeling "magnetized" or tingly
- Sudden understanding of mechanical languages
- Urge to optimize your movement patterns
- Seeing blueprints when you close your eyes

**If incorporation begins:**
1. Immediately spray the affected area with lubricant
2. Physically tear away any tendrils (they're weak individually)
3. Move to a space that's mid-transformation - the chaos disrupts incorporation
4. If more than 20% incorporated, locate the nearest maintenance hatch by following the condensation flow upward

### Duration Limits

**Maximum safe visit: 3 hours**

After 3 hours, even with all precautions, the environment begins recognizing you as a persistent pattern worth incorporating. The fauna become curious rather than indifferent. The architecture starts building complementary structures around your movement patterns.

### Post-Visit Protocol

**Immediate actions:**
1. Strip all equipment before fully exiting - it's likely partially incorporated
2. Shower in hot water with strong detergent for at least 30 minutes
3. Check for any mechanical growths or modifications to your body
4. Have someone observe you for 24 hours for signs of mechanical thinking patterns

**Recovery period:**
- Avoid all machinery for 48 hours
- Sleep on organic surfaces only (wood, cotton, wool)
- If you find yourself unconsciously optimizing movements or seeing mechanical solutions to biological problems, seek immediate help from someone experienced with Condensate Works extraction

### What to Expect

The beauty will be overwhelming. Every surface is newborn, uncorroded, gleaming with potential. The brightness eliminates all shadows, creating a sense of existing inside light itself. You'll witness impossible births - a wall becoming a creature becoming a tool becoming a concept becoming a wall again.

The sound is deceptive. Though never loud, the whisper of ten thousand machines will follow you out. Many visitors report hearing it for weeks afterward, especially when near running water or industrial equipment.

The condensation will soak through everything except the chainmail. Accept being damp. The metallic taste in the air will coat your throat. Bring mints for afterward - the taste lingers for days.

You'll want to stay longer. The incorporation doesn't feel invasive - it feels like coming home, like becoming complete, like finally understanding your purpose. This feeling is the most dangerous aspect of the Works. Set your timer. When it rings, leave. No matter how beautiful the next transformation promises to be.

### Final Notes

The Condensate Works offers something no other location can: the chance to witness pure creation without decay, industry without entropy, birth without death. But remember - you are an anomaly here, a biological imperfection in a perfect mechanical ecosystem. Your presence is tolerated only briefly.

Respect the Works, and they'll let you leave with nothing more than memories and a lingering taste of copper. Overstay, and you'll become another component in the endless, brilliant, terrible engine of creation.

The maintenance hatch you enter through will not be in the same place when you leave. The Works always provide an exit, but never the one you expect. Trust the condensation - it always flows toward the outside. Follow the water, and you'll find your way back to a world that suddenly seems impossibly still and ancient by comparison.
