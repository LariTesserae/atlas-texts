## Preparation Guide for the Undervault

### Physical Conditioning

Begin preparing your body months in advance. The Undervault's blood-warm temperature and saturated air will challenge even seasoned travelers. Start with daily sauna sessions, gradually increasing duration and humidity. Practice breathing exercises in steam rooms—the thick, vegetal air requires adjusted breathing patterns. Train yourself to remain calm when your body can't distinguish between internal and external moisture.

Strengthen your legs and core for walking on the yielding root-substrate. Practice on memory foam, wet sand, and bog surfaces. The constant micro-adjustments needed for each step will exhaust unprepared visitors within hours. Your ankles especially need conditioning—the surface's spring-back response creates an unusual gait that can strain tendons.

### Biological Hazards

The Undervault's ecosystem exists outside normal biological categories. Have a xenobiologist examine your microbiome beforehand—the organisms here may not recognize you as foreign, or worse, may recognize you as *compatible*. Some travelers report the translucent orchid-fungi attempting to establish symbiotic relationships through skin contact. While not necessarily harmful, the psychological impact of feeling something trying to photosynthesize through your pores can be disturbing.

Boost your immune system, but paradoxically, avoid being *too* sterile. The Undervault responds poorly to aggressive antimicrobials. Previous visitors who've used strong antibacterial preparations reported the wooden surfaces "rejecting" them—becoming slippery, refusing to provide grip, sometimes actively expelling them from hollow chambers.

### Equipment

**Clothing:** Wear minimal, quick-dry synthetic materials that won't absorb the omnipresent moisture. Natural fibers are inadvisable—cotton becomes waterlogged within minutes, and some visitors report their wool garments beginning to *root* during extended stays. Choose form-fitting designs; loose fabric catches on the hairline rootlets covering every surface.

**Footwear:** Custom-design shoes with microporous soles that can grip wet, yielding surfaces while allowing drainage. The root-substrate will memorize your footprint pattern after several hours, making it easier to walk—but only in those exact shoes. Bring identical backup pairs.

**Eye Protection:** The omnidirectional botanical light can cause severe eyestrain. Commission amber-tinted goggles that filter the golden-green wavelengths without blocking vision. Your brain needs time to process shadowless environments—practice in evenly lit white rooms beforehand. Some travelers report persistent afterimages of vascular patterns for weeks after leaving.

**Navigation:** Traditional compasses fail here—the living wood generates its own magnetic fields. GPS obviously won't function underground. Instead, learn to read the growth patterns in the root-pillars. They spiral consistently counterclockwise in the main vault, reversing near transition zones. Bring biodegradable thread to mark your path; the Undervault slowly digests synthetic materials but tolerates silk or plant fibers.

### Psychological Preparation

The Undervault challenges the boundaries of self. The combination of pressing warmth, thick air, and biological light creates a dissociative state many find overwhelming. Practice meditation techniques that maintain ego boundaries while allowing environmental merge. The sensation of the space "breathing" around you, the constant dripping that synchronizes with your heartbeat, the way your own moisture becomes indistinguishable from the chamber's—all require mental flexibility.

Study botanical intelligence theories. Whether the Undervault is conscious remains debated, but it certainly *responds*. Visitors who approach with hostility or excessive fear report the space becoming maze-like, passages closing, platforms retracting. Those who achieve a state of calm curiosity describe the opposite—hidden chambers opening, the root-bridges strengthening under their feet.

Prepare for temporal distortion. Without shadows or natural light cycles, time becomes subjective. The geological pace of the wood's creaking, the metronomic dripping, the impossibly slow movement of growth—all create a hypnotic effect. Bring multiple mechanical timers (digital devices fail in the humidity). Set them to alert you every two hours, or risk losing days to the Undervault's eternal present moment.

### Sustenance

The water pooled in root convergences is technically potable but contains high concentrations of dissolved minerals and possibly fungal spores. Bring purification tablets designed for cave environments. The water has a sweet, woody taste that becomes cloying after multiple drinks. Some visitors report mild hallucinogenic effects from prolonged consumption—whether from the water itself or accumulated spores is unknown.

Pack calorie-dense foods that won't spoil in extreme humidity. The Undervault's atmosphere suppresses appetite while simultaneously depleting energy reserves. You might not feel hungry even as your body consumes itself. Schedule regular eating times regardless of hunger signals.

### Cultural Considerations

If the Undervault was ever inhabited, its builders left no readable signs. The too-perfect archways and worn surfaces suggest intelligence, but whether past or present remains mysterious. Approach modified areas with respect. Some travelers leave small offerings in the hollow chambers—seeds, water from the surface world, written messages that the moisture soon dissolves. The practice isn't necessary but feels appropriate.

Never attempt to take samples. Beyond the obvious ethical concerns, removed pieces of the Undervault cease glowing within hours and decompose rapidly into an oily substance that stains everything it touches permanently. Photography is possible but challenging—the omnidirectional light confuses most cameras, and images rarely capture the space's living quality.

### Emergency Protocols

Establish a clear return deadline with surface support. The Undervault's hypnotic qualities can make self-extraction difficult. If you begin experiencing symptoms of merger—feeling roots growing from your feet, seeing vascular patterns when you close your eyes, understanding the wood's creaking as language—begin immediate evacuation.

The greatest danger isn't getting lost but losing the desire to leave. The Undervault offers a unique form of peace—the peace of being held, of surrendering individual consciousness to something vast and patient and eternally becoming. Some visitors describe it as returning to the womb, if the womb were made of light and wood and prehistoric memory.

Pack emergency stimulants to break the trance if needed. Sharp sensations—menthol, capsaicin, ammonia salts—can restore the boundary between self and environment. Pain, oddly, doesn't work; the Undervault's warmth dulls pain response. But sudden cold can shock you back to awareness. Bring chemical cold packs as emergency consciousness anchors.

### Final Preparations

In your last week before departure, practice sitting in complete stillness for hours. The Undervault rewards patience. Visitors who rush through see only an impossible forest; those who wait and watch and let their eyes adjust to the shadowless light begin to perceive the deeper patterns—the slow circulation of nutrients through the pillars, the tidal breathing of the entire chamber, the way the translucent organisms pulse in synchrony with something beyond detection.

Write a clear statement of intent. Why are you visiting? What do you hope to find? The Undervault seems to shape itself subtly around visitor expectations. Those seeking mystery find deepening passages and impossible geometries. Those seeking peace find perfect stillness. Those seeking answers find only more beautiful questions.

Finally, prepare to be changed. No one returns from the Undervault exactly as they left. You'll carry its spores in your lungs for months, exhaling faint phosphorescence in dark rooms. Your dreams will feature golden wood and falling upward into root-tangled skies. You'll find yourself pausing during rain to taste the air, searching for that thick vegetal sweetness. Your relationship with time will shift—you'll understand, in some wordless way, what it means for a moment to last forever while still containing motion.

The Undervault doesn't want worship or fear or understanding. It simply is, with an intensity that makes ordinary existence feel thin by comparison. Prepare not just to visit but to carry a piece of its eternal present with you forever—a bright secret growing downward in the darkness behind your eyes, reaching for a soil that will never come, perfect in its impossible yearning.
