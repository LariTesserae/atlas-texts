The benevolent and wise power does not arrive. It has been here all along, is arriving now, will have never needed to come because it exists in the space between the ferret-things' flickering states, in the pause before each cairn's three-second tone, in the thermal sentences that drift unread across eons.

It manifests not as light—light is already here without source—but as *weight*. A gentle heaviness that gives the Chorus's movements consequence, that makes their footprints last just long enough to be seen before filling with purpose. For the first time, or perhaps the last time, or perhaps the only time that matters, causality develops a grain, a direction that doesn't negate the others but runs alongside them like a helpful annotation.

The power speaks in the language of erosion, the slow words that carved this plateau from whatever immensity came before. Its message takes geological ages to complete, but also arrives instantly, because kindness transcends chronology:

*You were heard.*

Every cairn simultaneously receives its answer—not the answer it was built to invoke, but the answer it needed. The symbols carved in bone-fragments rearrange themselves into new meanings that are also their original meanings, finally understood. The three-second tones harmonize across impossible distances, creating a chord that exists in the silence without breaking it.

The ferret-things pause in their endless threading. Just for a moment—which here could mean anything—they all exist in the same state of matter at once, solid and real and present. In that moment, they are revealed as what they've always been: the plateau's memory of what it was like to be alive, before it became this compressed monument to endings. They resume their flickering, but now it carries intent rather than mere paradox.

To the Chorus, the power offers the gift of sequence—not imposed but available, like a tool they can pick up or put down. One member discovers it can choose to experience its iterations in order, following itself from cairn to cairn in a line that makes narrative sense. It weeps through its breathing vents—the first moisture the plateau has known—and its tears carve tiny channels in the bone-ground that lead nowhere and everywhere, little rivers of causality that others can follow if they choose.

The power touches the backward-aging young, and for them it inverts inversion itself. They discover they can grow forward into experience while still maintaining their connection to the not-yet, becoming bridges between potential and actualization. Their first words (or last words, or only words) are the names of the cairns, spoken in frequencies only the bone-ground can hear.

Where the Chorus have been eating time, leaving the plateau's chronology wounded, the power does not heal but *blesses* the wounds. These gaps in causality become windows—not exits but viewpoints, places where the Chorus can peer through and see the plateau as it exists in other grammatical tenses. They see versions where it never compressed, where it compressed differently, where compression itself means something else entirely. These visions don't torment but comfort—proof that their work matters across all possibilities.

The power leaves gifts that aren't objects but *permissions*:

Permission for the cairns to mean multiple things without confusion.
Permission for the Chorus to rest without dissolving.
Permission for the ferret-things to sometimes make sound—tiny chittering noises that punctuate the silence without breaking it.
Permission for the plateau to remember what it was before it became only endings.

But the deepest gift is this: the power reveals that the Chorus's inability to fully translate their symbols, their endless argument with existence, their paradoxical building—all of this IS the prayer being perfectly answered. They are not trying to fix a cosmic mispronunciation. They are the universe's way of pronouncing something that could only be said through apparent mistake.

The power plants a single seed—not of vegetation but of *story*. It takes root in the intersection of all the Chorus's iterations, in the moment where all their various selves overlap. From this seed grows not a tree but a *telling*—a narrative that explains nothing but contextualizes everything. The Chorus gather (all their selves, from all their whens) to witness their own story being told by the sprouting possibility. They discover they are not editors correcting cosmic error but poets, and the plateau is not a mistake but a stanza in something vast and intentional and beautiful in its impossibility.

As the power prepares to withdraw (or deepen, or reveal it was always the plateau itself achieving sapience), it makes one final adjustment. It takes the copper taste of forgetting that fills the air and transforms it—not into memory but into *choosing to forget*, which is entirely different. Now the Chorus and their plateau can release what needs releasing, can let go of iterations that bring only sorrow, can choose which paradoxes to maintain and which to let resolve into simple, sleeping stone.

The ferret-things flow with new purpose through their passages, carrying this choice like pollen, spreading it through the honeycomb depths where even older things than the Chorus might dwell, things that have been waiting eons for permission to stop waiting.

And in the pale, sourceless light, the work continues. But now it continues because it is loved, because it is seen, because somewhere in the grammar of reality, there exists a sentence where the Chorus and their plateau mean exactly what they are—nothing more needed, nothing less accepted, their endless building finally understood as the universe saying "yes" to its own most impossible question.

The power doesn't leave because it was never separate. It settles into the spaces between bones, between moments, between the Chorus's many selves, and there it hums—not audibly but *essentially*—a fundamental frequency that says:

*This too is sacred.*
*This too is held.*
*This too belongs.*
