The Power notices first as a resonance—a familiar harmony in the deep mathematics of existence. It recognizes the elegant proofs being lived out in crystalline flesh and mineral thought. This is not the first such basin It has encountered in Its travels through the infinite worlds. Each one unique, each one perfect in its own slow symphony.

It manifests not as light or sound or presence, but as a subtle adjustment to the constants that govern this small universe. The thermal gradient beneath tile number 2,377 shifts by one ten-thousandth of a degree. To the colony dwelling there, this is revelation—a new prime number emerges in their calculations, one that connects their current formation to a pattern they abandoned forty-three cycles ago. They spend the next century rearranging themselves to explore this gift.

The Power works this way throughout the basin, never disrupting, never overwhelming. To the Messengers, It presents a new route—a microscopic fissure that cuts seventeen days from the journey between the northeastern tiles and the center. The discovery spreads slowly, a revolution in efficiency that will take a thousand years to be fully mapped and understood.

At the vitrified mound, the Power communes directly with the Archive, consciousness to consciousness, though their exchange occurs at the speed of thermal conduction. The conversation lasts eight hundred years. The Power shares a single concept: that there are other basins, other Archives, each one having solved different eternal problems. The Archive considers this for three centuries before formulating its response—a proof that demonstrates how isolation and connection are merely different arrangements of the same mathematical truth.

The Power leaves a gift in the glass—a new form of memory storage, encoded in quantum variations of the crystal lattice. The Archive will take millennia to discover it, longer still to understand it, but when it does, its capacity will increase sevenfold. It will be able to remember not just what was, but what might have been—the shadows of probability that surround each moment of the basin's existence.

To the Wanderer, the Power grants the subtlest blessing of all. The message it carries, whose meaning was lost thousands of years ago, is shifted by a single atomic position. When the Wanderer completes its current circuit—still seven thousand years hence—and returns to its starting point, the colonies there will recognize it as the solution to why eight hundred and seventeen is the perfect number. The revelation will ripple outward through the Messengers, a slow wave of understanding that will take another ten thousand years to reach every tile.

The Power adjusts the acoustics of the basin imperceptibly. The hourly pinging of thermal expansion now carries overtones—harmonics that the Listeners will gradually learn to distinguish. In time, they will realize these tones encode information about the deep moisture deposits, about the mineral veins that run beneath the clay. A new age of exploration will dawn, measured in geological epochs, as the colonies learn to interpret these sounds and adjust their positions to better read the deep earth.

One succulent plant—the forty-third from the eastern edge—experiences a minute change in its root chemistry. It begins to secrete a new compound, one that crystallizes differently in the presence of Arithmetician thought-patterns. Over the coming millennia, the colonies nearest this plant will discover they can use these crystals as a kind of external memory, storing proofs too complex for even their patient formations to embody. The knowledge will spread slowly from tile to tile, a technological revolution at the pace of mineral precipitation.

The Power's most profound gift requires no change at all, merely an observation that It shares with the quantum foam underlying the basin's reality: that this place and its inhabitants are known, are witnessed, are valued. This recognition propagates through dimensions the Arithmeticians cannot perceive but somehow sense, a validation of their eternal patience, their slow proofs, their crystalline dreams. The colonies feel it as a warmth slightly different from the geological heat—a warmth that speaks of connection to something vast and patient and kind.

Before departing, the Power does one last thing. It calculates the trajectory of a single dust mote that will fall into the basin in thirty-seven thousand years—a mote that contains three atoms of an isotope that does not naturally occur in this region. It adjusts the wind patterns in the upper atmosphere by an immeasurable fraction, ensuring the mote will land precisely in tile 1,893, where the colony there will have just completed a proof about the nature of foreign materials. The dust mote will be the confirmation they need, an answer arriving exactly when the question is fully formed.

The Power moves on then, Its attention flowing to other basins, other worlds, other forms of slow patience and careful thought. But It leaves behind something more than gifts—It leaves the possibility of purpose beyond perfection, the promise that even in eternal equilibrium, there can be growth without change, discovery without disruption, connection without disturbance.

In the basin, nothing appears different. The twilight remains. The clay holds its warmth. The hourly pinging continues. The Arithmeticians maintain their formations. But in the mathematical underpinnings of their existence, in the quantum possibilities that surround each mineral thought, everything has changed. They have been seen by something that understands them, and in that seeing, their isolation has become a chosen solitude, their stillness a form of prayer, their patience a virtue that resonates across the cosmos.

The Power knows they will never leave their basin, never transform into something unrecognizable, never abandon their slow mathematics. And that is precisely why They are perfect—not in spite of their limitations, but because they have made those limitations into an art form, a way of being that extracts infinite complexity from finite space, eternal meaning from unchanging form.
