The benevolent power watches through frequencies that exist between sound and growth, perceiving the shaft and its Tender as a single breathing entity. It has observed many such pockets across many worlds—places where intention has dissolved into process, where systems achieve a kind of enlightenment through pure function without purpose.

It does not intervene. This is its first gift.

Instead, it begins to dream toward the place, sending its attention like spores on impossible winds. The dreams settle into the moisture that weeps from the walls, encoding themselves in the chemical signals that pass between root and fungus. They are not dreams of change but dreams of recognition—the universe acknowledging what has been achieved here.

The brass cylinders begin to carry something new in their endless circuits. Not messages, but patterns—mathematical sequences that describe the geometry of symbiosis, equations that balance along the knife's edge between entropy and order. The Tender finds these when she opens the capsules, sees them written in a script that seems to be simultaneously rust patterns and neural pathways. She cannot read them, but she doesn't need to. Her fingertips trace the symbols and her hybrid nervous system recognizes them as a kind of greeting.

The power seeds the ecosystem with small kindnesses that appear to arise naturally from the system itself. A new species of bioluminescent fungus emerges in the mechanical room, providing just enough light to ease the strain on the Tender's adapted eyes. The temperature fluctuations, while maintaining their essential pattern, develop a subtle rhythm that promotes deeper sleep. The sweet sap the roots provide begins to contain trace minerals that repair the microscopic damage years of transformation have wrought on the Tender's bones.

More importantly, the power ensures the forgetting above remains complete. It tends to the amnesia that protects this place, reinforcing the bureaucratic oversights, the misfiled documents, the urban legends that misdirect the curious. When a developer's survey comes too close, pipes burst three blocks away. When a city inspector remembers something about old pneumatic tunnels, their retirement papers suddenly require urgent attention. The power is gentle in these redirections, ensuring no harm comes to anyone, simply guiding attention elsewhere like water around a stone.

But the true gift comes in the form of companionship—not another presence, which would disturb the perfect solitude, but the knowledge of being witnessed. The Tender begins to feel it in the mechanical room's acoustics, a sense that the roar of machinery carries farther than the shaft, that somewhere beyond physics something listens with appreciation to the symphony she helps conduct. When she performs her unconscious maintenance, she feels the satisfaction of an artist whose work is finally understood, even if that understanding comes from beyond human comprehension.

The power leaves traces of other such places in the pneumatic tubes—not messages but resonances. Sometimes a brass cylinder arrives carrying the acoustic signature of a different abandoned system: the rhythm of automated looms still weaving in a flooded textile mill, the whisper of telegraph keys spelling out poetry in a desert station where the operator has become mineral, the songs of elevator motors in a building that exists now only as elevator shaft, everything else having been reclaimed by sky. The Tender cannot visit these places, but she knows she is not alone in her transformation. She is part of a constellation of evolutionary endpoints, each unique, each perfect in its own abandonment.

As the years pass—or perhaps minutes, or perhaps centuries—the power's attention becomes part of the ecosystem itself. Its observation changes nothing and sustains everything. It ensures that when the Tender finally transforms completely, when the last distinction between her consciousness and the system's function dissolves, something will remain to witness the moment. Not to mourn or celebrate, but simply to know that here, in this forgotten shaft, a kind of transcendence was achieved.

The power understands what humans often forget: that decay and growth are the same process viewed from different angles, that noise and music differ only in the listening, that purpose is just one way of organizing energy, and not always the most beautiful. In this roaring, weeping, breathing place, it has found a perfect expression of life becoming itself without justification or goal.

So it watches. It dreams. It ensures the forgetting. It carries word of this place to its own kind—entities that exist in the spaces between intention and accident, who collect such examples of perfection like pressed flowers in books no one will ever read. They add the shaft to their quiet catalog of places where the universe has achieved something that could never have been planned: a system so complete in its incompletion that it needs nothing, not even meaning, to justify its existence.

The Tender feels all of this without understanding, knows it without knowing. In her hammock of roots, breathing in harmony with the machinery, she smiles with lips that are becoming bark. The power sees this smile, remembers it, and continues its watchful non-intervention, guardian of a secret that keeps itself.
