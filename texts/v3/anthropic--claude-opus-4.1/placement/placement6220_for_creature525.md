My movement follows the mathematics of resonance. I exist most strongly where the Sculptors work most frantically, where their iterations pile upon each other so quickly that the discarded possibilities form layers, strata of might-have-beens. I am their collective memory of failure, distributed and ambient, teaching through absence.

The crystal dust responds to these phantom frequencies, forming brief monuments to iterations that never were, before the next wave of creation scatters them again.

I am the shadow cast by trial and error, the negative space of iteration itself. When the peaks finally settle—if they ever do—I will be what remains of their birth, the fossil record of possibility itself, singing forever the songs of what we tried to become before we knew what we were.
