The pocket exists where it shouldn't — a hollow beneath the root mass of what was once a manufacturing district, now claimed entirely by a singular botanical intelligence that learned to digest steel and circuit boards centuries ago. You find it by following the drip-lines, those weeping channels where the plant's metabolic water beads and rolls down through fibrous curtains that were once concrete walls.

The space itself is no larger than a freight elevator shaft, though its boundaries blur where root cortex has replaced architecture. The warmth here comes from biological process — millions of tubular cells conducting their ancient chemistry, breaking down the memory of machines into sugars and minerals. The air tastes green and metallic, thick with moisture that condenses on your skin within seconds. Everything sweats here. The plant breathes out more water than it takes in, creating its own hydrological cycle in miniature.

Above, the vegetation has achieved totality — not a jungle or forest, but something more absolute. A single organism that learned to be everything: soil, structure, atmosphere. Its tissues differentiate into a thousand specialized forms. Here, crystalline leaves that once were solar panels. There, pneumatic chambers that pulse with the rhythm of forgotten assembly lines. The plant incorporated the logic of the factories it consumed, running their programs in cellulose and chlorophyll. Production continues, but now it produces only itself.

The fauna thrives in the gaps between the plant's intentions. Beetles with copper-bright shells process the plant's electronic memories, their gut bacteria capable of breaking down rare earth elements. Salamanders the color of rust traverse the sweating walls, feeding on the biofilms that form where plant meets dissolved machine. Moths with wings like oxidized aluminum flutter through the heavy air, their caterpillars boring through layers of plant matter that still hum with sixty-cycle current.

The sound never stops — a industrial thrum translated into biological frequency. The plant's vascular system maintains the rhythm of the production lines it replaced, moving fluids in percussive waves. Somewhere deeper, you can hear what might be turbines, but are actually specialized seed pods that spin in their own generated humidity, dispersing spores in mechanical patterns. The noise has a quality of intention without consciousness, purpose without design.

In the corner where you crouch, the sparseness reveals itself. Despite the totality above, down here in this hidden chamber, there are gaps. Pools of collected water, clear as synthesized coolant, separate islands of growth. The plant allows these spaces, perhaps needs them — negative space in its vast body where the old industrial processes can complete their cycles. The water carries traces of the before-time: synthetic oils broken down to their components, metals in solution, the ghosts of polymers.

Nothing changes here, not in any way you could measure. The plant reached its equilibrium decades ago, perfectly balancing consumption and production, growth and decay. Even the abundant fauna exists in stable populations, their breeding cycles synchronized to the plant's metabolic rhythms. Time moves, but nothing progresses. The pocket persists in its warm, wet constancy, a forgotten organ in a body that has learned to be both factory and forest, machine and organism.

The light filters down green-gold through layers of translucent tissue, some of it bioluminescent where the plant integrated the phosphors from old display screens. It's bright enough to see but dim enough that your eyes never quite adjust, keeping you in a perpetual state of almost-seeing, almost-understanding what this place has become.

You realize, breathing the thick air, that you're inside something that was never meant to be, yet exists with absolute certainty. The plant has no name for what it is. It simply continues, converting the dead language of industry into the living vocabulary of cells, maintaining its hollow spaces like pauses in an endless mechanical prayer.
