The rain is not gentle—it is relentless, a deep-seated force that has shaped this land over millennia, leaving it scarred and transformed.

The glass formations glisten with moisture, but they show no signs of life or growth. Instead, the only signs of activity are the slow, deliberate movements of the constructed materials themselves. These materials, though inanimate, seem to follow their own rules, undulating and morphing in response to unseen forces. The world feels both settled and restless, a paradox of stability and instability held in perfect balance.
