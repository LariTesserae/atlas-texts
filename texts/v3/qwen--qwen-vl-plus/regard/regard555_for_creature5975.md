It is as though time has been held in check, not by force, but by a delicate equilibrium that allows the world to exist in harmony with its own emptiness.

It senses the longing within the inhabitants of this submerged world—their quiet but persistent yearning for meaning, connection, and growth. The Primordial Observer recognizes this longing as a vital spark, a sign of life even in its most subtle form. These changes ripple outward through the world, affecting even the water itself. The inhabitants of this world, though they do not fully understand what is happening, sense a shift in their environment.
