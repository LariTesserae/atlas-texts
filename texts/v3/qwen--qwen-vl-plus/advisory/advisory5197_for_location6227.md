### Physical Preparations

2. **Lighting**  
   - Carry a reliable headlamp or helmet-mounted light system to see in the dim, greenish light. The light should be powerful enough to illuminate the immediate surroundings but also energy-efficient to avoid running out during extended exploration.

   - The air is heavy with a low-frequency hum that might affect sleep patterns and mental acuity. Bring a lightweight, portable air mask to filter out potential particles or contaminants.

   - The vast, undulating terrain lacks clear landmarks, making traditional navigation difficult. Use a GPS device with offline maps, but be aware that the shifting ground might render map markers obsolete. A detailed notebook or digital journal for sketching and note-taking is essential.
