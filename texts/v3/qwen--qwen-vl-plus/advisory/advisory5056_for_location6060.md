### **Physical Preparation**

2. **Gear**:
   - **Waterproof Backpack**: Invest in a sturdy, waterproof backpack to protect your essentials from the constant moisture in the air and light rainfall.
   - **Moisture-Wicking Gear**: Use gear made from materials that wick away moisture rather than absorbing it, so you stay comfortable and dry.
   - **Portable Drying Rack**: Bring a small, lightweight drying rack for items that need to be aired out between rain showers.
   - **Waterproof Bags for Electronics**: If you plan to bring cameras, phones, or other electronic devices, ensure they are protected in waterproof cases or bags.

3. **Personal Hygiene**:
