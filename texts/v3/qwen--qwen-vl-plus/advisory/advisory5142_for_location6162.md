### **Physical Preparation**

   The place is unbearably warm, radiating heat from its crystalline structures. Wear clothing made of materials that can handle high temperatures while still allowing for airflow.    The ground is composed of solidified mineral and salt crystalline structures that shimmer with faint energy. While stable, they may shift under pressure or dissolve in unpredictable ways.    The air here is dense and carries a faint metallic scent, possibly toxic or reactive.
