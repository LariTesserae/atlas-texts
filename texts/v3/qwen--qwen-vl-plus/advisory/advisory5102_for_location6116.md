### **1. Physical Preparation**
The environment of the Verdant Hollow demands that travelers be in excellent physical condition, as the terrain is uneven and requires constant movement over soft, slippery surfaces. - **Clothing**: Wear lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing that can handle heavy sweating. Layer with quick-drying fabrics to adapt to changing temperatures in the Hollow. - **Hydration**: Bring plenty of water, as the humid heat will cause rapid dehydration. Additionally, pack a portable water filter or purification tablets to treat any collected water from the Hollow’s pools and drips.
