### **Physical Preparation**

   - The air is dense and suffocating, with a constant layer of vapor that distorts visibility. A portable air filtration system, such as an N95-rated mask or a full-face respirator, is essential to ensure safe breathing.

3. **Heat Management and Hydration:**

4. **Lighting Equipment:**

   - The lack of sunlight means that traditional energy sources like solar panels are ineffective. Bring a compact backpack stove and lightweight, high-calorie food rations that require minimal preparation.

7. **Medical Supplies:**

8. **Communication Devices:**
