### **Physical Preparation: Embrace the Extreme**

   - A wide-brimmed hat with reflective properties and UV protection is essential to shield your head and face from the relentless dim light of the low-hanging sun.

   - Although there is no liquid water, condensation from the vegetation patches may provide a limited but vital resource. Bring a portable distillation system or hydration pouches that can collect and condense atmospheric moisture.
