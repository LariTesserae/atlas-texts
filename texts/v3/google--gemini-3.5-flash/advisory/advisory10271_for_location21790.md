### Phase 1: Environmental Armor (Defending the Flesh)

*   **The "Glass-Cutter" Boots:** Do not wear rubber, Vibram, or standard leather soles. The flawless black obsidian prisms underfoot will slice through them within three steps. You require custom-engineered boots featuring **vulcanized ceramic-composite soles** wrapped in a fine **tungsten-carbide chainmail mesh**. The soles must be entirely non-ferrous (free of iron) because the native iron floor and solenoids will violently pull magnetic boots toward the earth, pinning you in place or ripping your ankles.
*   **Active-Cooling Hazard Suit:** The radiating tectonic heat, combined with the hot, dry air (stinging with carbon-fluoride), requires a fully sealed, closed-loop environmental suit. 
    *   *No external air intake:* The atmosphere must be 100% self-contained. The bone-dry nitrogen/carbon-fluoride mix will desiccate your lungs and throat instantly.
    *   *Refrigerated Exoskeleton:* The suit needs active, liquid-loop cooling to dissipate the ambient heat rising from the iron floor.
    *   *Aramid-Shielded Outer Layer:* The suit’s skin must be made of layered, slash-proof Vectran or Nomex-aramid weave to prevent the razor-sharp silica needles and metal foil leaves from puncturing your life support.

### Phase 2: Navigation & Physics (Adapting to the "Bent" Pocket)

Once inside the alcove, you will be subjected to localized warping of gravity and light. Standard human spatial awareness will lie to you.

*   **The 30-Degree Tilt Calibration:** Gravity here pulls toward the heavy iron deposits in the back wall, not straight down. When you step into the alcove, your inner ear will tell you that you are falling backward into the mountain, but your eyes will see the vertical cliffside yawning right beside you. 
    *   To prepare, you must practice **tilted-axis walking** in a simulator. If you panic and try to stand "upright" relative to the cliff face, you will actually lean outward over the abyss and slip off. Trust the pull toward the back wall.
*   **Optic Refraction Visor:** Because the dense magnetic fields curve incoming light, your depth perception will be wildly distorted. You might reach for a handhold that is actually six inches to the left. 
    *   You must wear a **Smart-HUD Visor with Real-Time Lidar Reconstruction**. The visor bypasses the curved lightwaves by using non-magnetic laser pulses to map the true geometry of the space, projecting a wireframe grid over your field of vision so you know exactly where the real, physical edges are.
*   **Non-Magnetic Anchor Anchoring:** Since the cliff has a vertical drop and the gravity is skewed, you must be tethered. However, standard magnetic clamps will seize onto the iron-veined basalt and never let go. Instead, use **vacuum-pneumatic suction anchors** or **mechanical expansion anchors** designed for smooth, unweathered basalt.

The natural copper solenoids and iron pistons generate a massive, localized electromagnetic field. Standard microchips will fry, and screens will turn into static.

*   **Pneumatic and Optical Tech Only:** Leave your smartphone, digital camera, and GPS at home. Any gear you bring must rely on **fluidic/pneumatic logic** or **optical computing** (glass-fiber signals rather than copper wires).

If you don't, touching a brass needle-palm will result in a localized arc of lightning that could melt a hole in your suit.

### Phase 4: Botanical Interaction (Do Not Disturb the Machine)

The flora of this alcove—the brass needle-palms and quartz glass-ivy—are not living things in any biological sense. They are geological crystals and slow-formed metals.

*   **Do Not Touch the Leaves:** The glass-ivy leaves are hollow quartz prisms containing pressurized, non-conductive silicone fluid. If you step on or crack one, the escaping fluid is under high tectonic pressure and can spray like hydraulic fluid, slicing through your suit or blinding you.
*   **Mind the Expansion Rates:** The metallic plants expand and contract based on the position of the sun. As the sun moves, a brass needle-palm frond can suddenly swing several inches with the force of a hydraulic piston. Give the plants a wide berth; they do not feel pain, but they will crush your limbs without noticing.

### Phase 5: Sensory & Psychological Conditioning

The silence of this room-sized sanctuary is deceptive. It is a quiet that will rattle your bones.

*   **Atone to the 50-Hz Drone:** The copper-veined basalt hums constantly at 50 hertz—the same frequency as electrical power grids, but amplified through millions of tons of volcanic rock. This frequency can cause mild eyeballs-vibrating vision blur, a sense of inexplicable dread, and mild nausea in humans. 
    *   You must wear **active-noise-canceling ear protection** dialed specifically to phase out low-frequency hums, combined with bone-conduction headphones playing white noise or familiar, organic sounds (like rain or wind) to keep your brain anchored.
*   **No "Softness" Acclimation:** Prepare yourself emotionally for the complete lack of organic touch. There is no dust to settle on your suit, no smell of earth, no decay. The air will smell faintly of burnt copper and pool chemicals (from the fluorides). It is a beautiful, terrifyingly clean world. 

### Entering the Cradle

When you finally step onto the horizontal prisms of black glass, suspend your breath for a moment. Lean into the 30-degree gravity tilt, letting your back rest against the warm, vibrating iron wall. 

Look through your corrected visor at the quartz ivy growing horizontally into the empty sky. Watch the shadow of a brass leaf curve across the basalt like a liquid crescent. Be still, make no sparks, and do not stay past sunset, when the cooling iron pistons begin to contract with the force of a collapsing mountain.
