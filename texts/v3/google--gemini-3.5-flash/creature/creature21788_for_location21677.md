In this world, to live is to hang; to survive is to vibrate. 

The primary masters of this humming void are the **Chordae**, a sapient species whose very biology is a direct response to the absence of the earth. 

### The Chordae

To a human eye, a Chorda would look less like an animal and more like a piece of living rigging. They are immensely tall—often nine or ten feet—but they contain so little mass that they weigh no more than a child. Their bodies are built on an osteo-skeletal frame made of micro-porous, gas-filled cartilage that is both incredibly flexible and as strong as the carbon filaments they inhabit. They have no rib cages; instead, their chest cavity is a hollow, barrel-shaped resonator covered by a taut, silver-grey hide that functions as a single, massive tympanic membrane. 

They do not hear through ears. They hear through their chests. 

A Chorda does not walk; they *glide* and *hook*. Their four limbs are long and spindly, ending not in paws or hands, but in three-pronged, scissor-like calipers. These talons are lined with a dense, specialized keratin that can clamp onto a hair-thin guide-wire with bone-crushing force, or open slightly to allow them to slide along the carbon cables like zipline pulleys. When they travel, they lie flat against the air, their bodies parallel to the wires, sliding at forty miles an hour through the warm wind, their caliper-hands whistling as they cut the air.

Because the rift is a wall of deafening sound, the Chordae have long abandoned vocal communication. They do not have vocal cords, nor do they have mouths meant for screaming. They communicate through **Toll-Speech**. Each Chorda wears heavy, weighted rings of polished bronze-alloy on their ankles and wrists. By striking these rings against the carbon guide-wires, they send high-frequency vibrations traveling miles down the city’s grid. Another Chorda, miles away, will press their drum-like chest against the wire and "hear" the message as a series of rich, rhythmic shivers in their lungs. Their conversations are a silent percussion that runs through the bones of the city, completely lost to the howling wind.

Within the hollow, pale-alloy needles of the floating city, the Chordae live in three dimensions. They do not have furniture. They sleep suspended in hammocks woven from the gossamer ivy, their bodies locked into place by magnetic harnesses that tap into the city’s quantum fields. To them, "floor" is an abstract, slightly terrifying concept. They cook, organize, and raise their pale, blind young in conditions of perpetual, controlled drift.

### The Dew-Thieves and the Flow-Life

Because water does not fall here, but instead clings to the undersides of the suspended corridors in fat, horizontal cylinders, a whole micro-ecology has evolved to inhabit these hanging rivers.

The most common are the **Dew-Sliders**. These are small, translucent, ribbon-like fish, entirely blind, that spend their lives inside the electromagnetically suspended water tubes. They do not swim so much as crawl along the metal hulls *inside* the water, using rows of microscopic, hook-like cilia. They feed on the aggressive purple lichen that tries to creep into the water channels. 

Living alongside them are the **Siphon-Snails**. These gastropods have shells of paper-thin, spun glass that refract the blue leakage of the gravity wells. They crawl along the outer surface of the water cylinders, using long, needle-like proboscises to pierce the water tension and drink, while their heavy, sticky foot keeps them anchored against the howling wind.

The Chordae harvest these water cylinders with remarkable grace. When they need to drink, they do not use cups; they use **Sip-Straws**—long, hollow reeds harvested from the canyon walls. They slide down to the underside of a transit corridor, hang by their feet over the miles of empty space, and plunge the straw into the side of the hanging river, drawing the sweet, mineral-heavy moisture directly into their narrow throats.

### The Aerial Herds: Nautiloids and Ribbon-Beasts

In the eighty miles of empty air between the floating rings, life is thick, buoyant, and competitive.

The **Chasm Nautiloids** are the gentle grazers of the sky. Ranging in size from a fist to a carriage, their shells are made of a light, chambered chitin that they fill with heated hydrogen, which they produce metabolically by digesting the violet lichen. They drift through the sky in vast, silent rafts of thousands, their long, blue tentacles trailing beneath them like silk threads to catch airborne spores and microscopic moss-drift. 

The Nautiloids navigate using tiny, biological siphon-jets, emitting rhythmic, metallic *clicks* that bounce off the obsidian cliffs. To the Chordae, a passing herd of Nautiloids sounds like a field of grandfather clocks ticking out of sync.

Hunting these herds are the **Zarikhs**, or **Ribbon-Beasts**. 

A Zarikh is forty feet of pure, iridescent muscle. They have no wings and no legs; their bodies are flat, wide ribbons of skin that ripple in lateral waves, propelling them through the dense, warm air like eels through water. Their skin is covered in microscopic, static-generating scales. As they slide through the air, the friction of the wind builds up a high-voltage charge across their backs, which hums with a fierce, ozone-scented hiss. 

When a Zarikh hunts, it does not bite; it rolls. It coils its long, ribbon-like body around a Nautiloid or a stray Chorda, releasing its electric charge in a single, blinding flash that paralyzes the prey’s nervous system. They then use their soft, underslung mouths to rasp away at the prey while still drifting mid-air.

### The Soughers: The Lungs of the Abyss

Deep below the city, down where the warm air turns into a thick, purple soup of sulfurous fog, live the **Soughers**. 

No Chorda has ever seen a Sougher in its entirety, for they are too massive and the fog is too thick. They are believed to be miles long—gargantuan, biological bellows that crawl along the invisible, lightless floor of the deep rift. 

The Soughers are the thermal engine of the valley. They pull the cold, heavy air that sinks to the bottom of the chasm into their colossal, multi-chambered bellies. Inside, through a massive, slow-burning process of bacterial fermentation, they heat the air to tropical temperatures and expel it back upward in monumental, vertical columns of warm, nutrient-rich draft. 

It is these warm thermal sighs from the Soughers that keep the city’s gossamer ivy alive, that feed the Nautiloids, and that provide the lift that keeps the non-quantum gliders of the Chordae aloft. The sound of their breathing—a deep, low-frequency *shudder* that occurs once every three hours—is a holy event for the Chordae. When the Sougher breathes, the entire city rises three inches on its lock-fields, and every guide-wire sings in a single, high, beautiful note of appreciation.

### The Edge-Clingers

Finally, there are those who do not live in the floating dance of the city, but on the raw, violent edges of the rift—the sheer walls of black obsidian and basalt.

These are the **Outcasts** and the **Glass-Breakers**. 

Because the obsidian cliffs are razor-sharp and drop thousands of feet without a foothold, these creatures have adapted to live in the vertical cracks. The **Glass-Laid** are a sub-sect of the Chordae who have abandoned the city’s high-tech alloy needles. They wear heavy boots tipped with industrial diamonds. They climb the glass walls by punching holes directly into the unweathered basalt, living in hand-carved caves that hang over the abyss. 

They are the miners. They harvest the raw copper, the rare-earth minerals, and the self-luminous alloys that the city needs to maintain its quantum-locking fields. They live in constant peril; a single misstep on the frictionless glass means a five-mile fall into the purple fog. They are hard, silent people, whose skin has been stained deep charcoal by the volcanic ash of the cliffs, and who speak only in the sharp, metallic clicks of iron chisels struck against the ancient, black glass.
