The Chitinous Crawlers are not just on the surface; they burrow into the cracks and crevices of the rocks, their chitinous exoskeletons providing both protection and the ability to move through the tightest of spaces. The Pulsating Gel is not confined to the surface; it can flow through the cracks and crevices of the rocks, its flexible, amorphous form allowing it to move with surprising ease. The pulsating motion of their bodies is not just a sign of life; it is a mechanism for moving nutrients and expelling waste. The Pulsating Gel often forms symbiotic relationships with the Chitinous Crawlers, with the Crawlers providing a means of transportation and the Gel providing a source of moisture and nutrients.

The Rock Integrators are not just living on the rocks; they are a part of the rocks. They can move, but their movements are slow and deliberate, almost imperceptible. They feed on the minerals in the rocks and the chemicals in the air, and their bodies can process and incorporate these substances, effectively becoming a part of the landscape. The Rock Integrators are often found in clusters, and their bodies can fuse together, creating a network of interconnected, living rock. They are a key part of the landscape's slow, patient transformation, and their presence is often the only sign of life in the most desolate and seemingly lifeless areas.

These small, worm-like beings are about the size of a human finger, with elongated, segmented bodies that are covered in a layer of fine, chitinous scales. The Sulfuric Sifters are filter feeders, using their specialized structures to sift through the air and extract the volatile compounds and minerals. The Sulfuric Sifters play a crucial role in the ecosystem, as they help to process and recycle the chemicals in the air, making them available for other creatures to use.

They are about the size of a human torso, and their bodies are a deep, almost black, with a mottled, shimmering surface that reflects the dim, ember-like light. The Amorphous Shifters can flow and change, splitting and merging with other Shifters, and they can take on a variety of forms, from simple, blob-like shapes to more complex, tentacular forms.
