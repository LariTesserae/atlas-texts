In this vast and sparse landscape, the inhabitants are entities of pure energy, beings that exist on a plane far removed from the familiar constructs of biology and physics. They are the architects of this embryonic world, an ancient and enigmatic intelligence that is still in the process of understanding and shaping its surroundings.

These beings are not bound by the physical forms we know; they are more like patterns of energy, ephemeral and shifting. They manifest as fleeting, shadowy forms that move through the air, gliding and swirling with a grace that defies the chaotic and oppressive environment. These energy beings are not just passive observers; they are actively involved in the creation and destruction that defines the landscape. They are the unseen hands that guide the emergence of the geometric, almost mathematical structures from the raw, unformed stone.

The energy beings are composed of a mixture of light and shadow, sometimes appearing as luminous, glowing entities that seem to be woven from the very light of the distant, sullen sun, and at other times, they are dark, shadowy forms that seem to be carved from the inky blackness of the sky. Their forms are not static; they pulse and shimmer, expanding and contracting in a rhythm that is in sync with the low, mechanical-organic hum that fills the air.

These beings are not solitary; they exist in a collective, a hive mind that is constantly in communication with itself and with the landscape. They communicate through a language of light and sound, a symphony of pulses and vibrations that reverberate through the air and the very rock itself. This language is as much a part of the landscape as the structures and the raw geology, and it is what gives the place its sense of life and purpose.

The energy beings are also the source of the strange, otherworldly veins of light that glow within the rock. These veins are not just decorative; they are a form of energy conduit, a network of channels that the beings use to move and manipulate the raw materials of the world. The light within the veins is a form of energy that is both a source of power and a means of communication, a way for the beings to coordinate their actions and to shape the world according to their collective will.

In the moments when the landscape seems to shift and warp, the energy beings are at their most active. They are the ones who cause the structures to morph and the horizon to bend, using their energy to manipulate the very fabric of reality. The constant, chaotic change is not a sign of randomness; it is a reflection of the beings' ongoing, deliberate efforts to create a world that is both a reflection of their own nature and a place of constant, vibrant potential.

The energy beings are ancient, but they are not static. They are in a continuous process of evolution, learning and adapting to the new forms and structures they create. They are driven by a deep, insatiable curiosity, a desire to understand and shape the world in ways that are both beautiful and terrifying. In this place of constant creation and destruction, they are the living, breathing embodiment of the raw, unformed potential that defines the landscape.
