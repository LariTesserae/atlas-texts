In this sub-aquatic, post-flood urban landscape, the inhabitants are a rare and enigmatic blend of deep-sea life and remnants of the city's former technological and industrial past. The primary residents are a species of bioluminescent, humanoid-like beings, known as the Lumina. These creatures have evolved to adapt to the cold, dark, and dense environment, with features that allow them to thrive in the submerged city.

### The Lumina

**Appearance:**
- **Body Structure:** The Lumina have a humanoid form, with elongated, graceful limbs and a slender, almost skeletal frame. Their skin is a translucent, pale blue, allowing the soft, bioluminescent glow from their internal organs to emanate through, providing a faint, ethereal light.
- **Bioluminescence:** The Lumina's bioluminescence is a key feature, with patterns of glowing, blueish-green light pulsating across their skin. This light serves as both a means of communication and a natural defense mechanism, confusing predators and lighting their way through the dark, winding streets.
- **Gills and Scales:** Instead of traditional lungs, the Lumina have gills, allowing them to extract oxygen from the water. Their skin is covered in a fine layer of scales that provide both protection and a streamlined form, making them excellent swimmers.
- **Eyes:** Their eyes are large and adapted to the low light, with a reflective layer that enhances their ability to see in the dark. The eyes are a deep, luminous blue, with a pupil that can dilate and constrict to adjust to different light levels.

**Behavior:**
- **Nocturnal Habits:** The Lumina are primarily nocturnal, moving through the city in the still, dark waters. They are most active during the brief periods when the bio-luminescent materials of the city emit a stronger glow.
- **Communication:** The Lumina use a complex system of light patterns and subtle movements to communicate. They also have a form of telepathic communication, allowing them to share thoughts and information with each other in the dark, silent environment.
- **Social Structure:** The Lumina live in small, cohesive groups, or "clusters," each with a specific role in maintaining the delicate balance of the submerged city. Some clusters focus on tending to the bio-luminescent materials, while others maintain the still-active machinery and engines.
- **Mystical and Spiritual:** The Lumina have a deep, almost mystical connection to the city and the sea. They believe in a form of spiritual energy that flows through the water and the structures, and they often perform rituals and ceremonies to honor and maintain this energy.

### The Mechanical Guardians

**Appearance:**
- **Robotic Forms:** Scattered throughout the city are a variety of still-active, autonomous machines, known as the Mechanical Guardians. These machines are a mix of old, rusting industrial equipment and more advanced, bioluminescent technology. They range from small, insect-like drones to large, humanoid-like constructs.
- **Bioluminescent Components:** The Mechanical Guardians are often covered in the same bio-luminescent material as the city, providing a faint, blueish glow that helps them navigate and perform their tasks.

**Behavior:**
- **Maintenance and Protection:** The Mechanical Guardians are designed to maintain the city's infrastructure and protect the Lumina from any external threats. They perform tasks such as repairing damaged structures, monitoring the water currents, and tending to the still-active machinery.
- **Autonomous Operation:** The Guardians operate on a form of artificial intelligence, with pre-programmed protocols and the ability to make simple, adaptive decisions. They are often seen moving silently through the city, their movements almost ghostly in the dim light.
- **Communication with the Lumina:** The Guardians can communicate with the Lumina through a system of light patterns and sound, often providing information and assistance. The Lumina, in turn, have a symbiotic relationship with the Guardians, relying on them for the maintenance of their environment.

### The Deep-Sea Fauna

**Appearance:**
- **Ethereal Creatures:** The few other life forms in the city are a variety of deep-sea creatures, adapted to the cold, dark, and dense environment. These include slender, transparent fish with bioluminescent organs, small, jelly-like invertebrates, and long, sinuous eels with glowing, blue eyes.
- **Shadows and Forms:** The deep-sea creatures are elusive and hard to see, often appearing as shadows or fleeting forms in the dim light. They move silently through the water, adding to the sense of mystery and otherworldliness.

**Behavior:**
- **Adaptation:** These creatures have adapted to the unique conditions of the submerged city, finding niches in the complex, layered environment. Some feed on the bio-luminescent materials, while others have formed symbiotic relationships with the Lumina.
- **Mystical and Ephemeral:** The deep-sea fauna often seem to be more shadow than form, adding to the sense of the city as a place of wonder and mystery. They are rarely seen, but their presence is felt in the occasional, almost imperceptible movements in the water and the faint, ghostly glows that sometimes light up the dark, winding streets.

In this sub-aquatic, post-flood urban landscape, the Lumina, the Mechanical Guardians, and the deep-sea fauna coexist in a delicate, almost mystical balance. The city, with its dense, weathered structures and the faint, blueish glow of the bio-luminescent materials, is a place of wonder and stillness, where the line between the natural and the artificial is blurred. The inhabitants, with their unique adaptations and behaviors, add to the sense of a place that is both in a state of suspended animation and in a constant, slow state of change.
