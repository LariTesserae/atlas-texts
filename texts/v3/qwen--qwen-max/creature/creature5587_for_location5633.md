It moves through the grove, tending to the plants, guiding the water, and speaking with the creatures that call the grove home. The Silvian is a solitary being, content in its role as the guardian of the grove. The Silvian is the heart and soul of the grove, a being of beauty, wisdom, and magic. It is a guardian, a caretaker, and a part of the very fabric of the grove.
