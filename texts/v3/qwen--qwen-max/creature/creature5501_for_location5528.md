**Cavernlighters:**

- **Bioluminescence:** The most striking feature of the Cavernlighters is their bioluminescent patches, which are scattered across their bodies in a mesmerizing pattern. These patches can vary in color, from a deep, soothing blue to a gentle, calming green, and can be dimmed or brightened at will, creating a subtle, almost rhythmic pattern of light and shadow. This bioluminescence is not just for illumination; it is also a form of communication, with different patterns and intensities expressing a wide range of emotions and signals.

**Behavior and Ecology:**
- **Habitat:** The Cavernlighters live in harmony with the environment, their homes carved into the walls and ceilings of the cavern. They are highly skilled at blending into the natural and artificial structures, using the intricate patterns and textures of the walls as camouflage. Small, hidden alcoves and tunnels, crafted with a delicate touch, serve as their dwellings, intricately woven into the fabric of the cavern.
- **Diet:** Their diet consists of the bioluminescent mosses and fungi that cover the walls, as well as the small, hardy plants that grow in the shadows. They are also known to consume the tiny, almost invisible insects that live among the vegetation, using their delicate, long fingers to pluck them from the air. The Cavernlighters have a symbiotic relationship with the bioluminescent flora, tending to it and ensuring its health, which in turn provides them with a constant source of food and light.

They have a matriarchal society, with the eldest females, known as "Motherlights," serving as the leaders and keepers of the community's knowledge and traditions. The Motherlights are the guardians of the ancient structure in the center of the cavern, tending to it and preserving the remnants of the long-lost, advanced civilization. They are also the keepers of the oral history, passing down stories and knowledge from one generation to the next.
