**The People:**
The Plateau Keepers are a resilient and hardy group, with a population of around 50-100 individuals. They are a mix of older, experienced elders and younger, energetic members, all of whom play a vital role in the community. Their physical appearance is marked by a rugged, weathered look, with tanned, sun-kissed skin and a lean, muscular build. The Plateau Keepers live in a close-knit, cooperative society, where each member has a specific role and responsibility. Their daily lives are a blend of hard work, simple pleasures, and a deep, almost spiritual connection to the land. They are skilled in a variety of crafts, from stone masonry and metalworking to herbal medicine and the cultivation of the few hardy plants that can survive on the plateau. They have a deep understanding of the natural cycles and the subtle changes in the environment, and they use this knowledge to live in harmony with the land.

The day begins with the soft, early morning light, and the community gathers in a small, central plaza for a simple, shared meal. The elders often lead a brief, reflective moment, where they share stories of the past and offer guidance for the day ahead. The community then disperses to their various tasks, which might include tending to the small, carefully cultivated gardens, repairing and maintaining the stone structures, foraging for wild herbs and plants, or crafting tools and other necessities.

In the afternoon, when the sun is at its highest, the community takes a break from their work. They gather in the shade of the central plaza, where they share stories, teach the younger members, and engage in simple, communal activities like weaving, carving, or preparing meals. The afternoon is a time of rest and reflection, a moment to savor the simple pleasures of life and the beauty of their surroundings.

As the sun begins to set, the community comes together once more for the evening meal. The evening is a time for storytelling, music, and the sharing of knowledge. The elders often pass down their wisdom and the stories of their ancestors, while the younger members share their experiences and the skills they have learned.

The Plateau Keepers are a community that embodies a timeless, harmonious way of living.
