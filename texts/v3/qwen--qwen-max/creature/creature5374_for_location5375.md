In this timeless, ancient landscape, the inhabitants are a reflection of the land itself—otherworldly, enduring, and imbued with a deep, almost sentient presence. The creatures that dwell here have evolved to become one with the environment, their forms and behaviors adapted to the unchanging, silent world around them.

The most numerous and visible inhabitants are the **Drifters**. These ethereal, translucent beings float and drift through the air, their bodies catching the pale, washed-out light of the low-hung sun. Their forms are amorphous, often resembling clouds or wisps of smoke, but with a more structured, almost crystalline interior. The Drifters move with a slow, gentle grace, seemingly unaffected by the lack of wind. They emit a faint, almost imperceptible hum, a sound that adds to the ambient, almost imperceptible vibration of the air. The Drifters are believed to be the embodiment of the ancient, eroded stone, their forms a reflection of the landscape's time-worn essence.

Among the Drifters, there are also the **Shadows**—massive, lumbering forms that move with a deliberate, almost ritualistic gait. These creatures are made of a substance that is both stone and shadow, a material that seems to shift and change in the light, sometimes appearing solid and at other times, almost insubstantial. The Shadows are often found near the ancient structures, moving in a slow, cyclical pattern, as if they are guardians of the long-lost civilization. Their movements are slow and purposeful, and they seem to be in a state of constant, unchanging vigil. The Shadows are believed to be the physical manifestations of the ancient, forgotten memories of the place, embodying the deep, unspoken history of the landscape.

The **Scuttlers** are smaller, more agile creatures that scuttle and slither over the rocky terrain. Their exoskeletons are a mix of muted, earthy tones, blending seamlessly with the surrounding stone. The Scuttlers are fast and nimble, their movements quick and precise. They are often found in the crevices and cracks of the boulders, where they seek shelter from the unrelenting chill. The Scuttlers are scavengers and gatherers, surviving on the scarce, hardy plants that grow in the sheltered nooks. They are the collectors of the landscape, gathering the few, precious resources and distributing them among their kind.

In the shadows of the ancient structures, one might also find the **Whispers**—creatures that are more like living shadows or wisps of mist. The Whispers are elusive and difficult to see, their forms often blending into the background, only visible as a slight, shimmering distortion in the air. They move with a barely perceptible, gliding motion, their presence a faint, almost inaudible whisper. The Whispers are believed to be the guardians of the ancient knowledge, the keepers of the secrets of the long-lost civilization. They are said to possess a deep, almost mystical connection to the place, and those who are attuned to the subtle vibrations of the landscape can sometimes catch a glimpse of their ethereal forms.

The **Stoneforms** are the most enigmatic of all the inhabitants. These creatures are the living embodiment of the landscape itself, their bodies a fusion of stone, shadow, and a strange, otherworldly energy. The Stoneforms are often indistinguishable from the rocks and boulders, and only reveal their true nature when they choose to move. When they do, it is with a slow, deliberate grace, as if they are part of the very fabric of the world. The Stoneforms are both the physical and metaphysical guardians of the place, their presence a reminder of the deep, ancient history and the enduring, unchanging nature of the landscape.

The **Veinwraiths** are the rarest and most mysterious of the inhabitants. These beings are almost invisible, their forms a delicate, web-like structure that seems to be woven from the very air itself. The Veinwraiths are often found in the crevices and fissures of the stone, where they seem to draw their energy from the ancient, eroded rock. They are the conduits of the landscape's hidden, otherworldly energy, and their presence is a sign of the place's deep, unspoken magic.

In this place, the boundary between the physical and the metaphysical is thin, and the creatures that inhabit it are a reflection of this. They are beings that embody the deep, ancient history, the unchanging, timeless nature, and the subtle, yet profound, otherworldly energy of the landscape. They are both familiar and alien, a reminder of the beauty and the terror of a world that is on the edge of reality.
