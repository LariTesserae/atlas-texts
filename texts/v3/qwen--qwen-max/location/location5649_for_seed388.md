The light in the place is dim, with the gray, overcast sky casting a soft, diffused glow over everything. The light seems to seep in from all directions, creating a sense of depth and texture, but also a feeling of being enclosed and contained. The atmosphere is one of a place that is both ancient and enduring, with a sense of having been in this state for a very long time. The slow, gradual cycles of the environment, the gradual weathering of the buildings, and the steady, constant hum of the machinery all contribute to a sense of a place that is in a state of balance, but also in a state of slow, almost imperceptible change.
