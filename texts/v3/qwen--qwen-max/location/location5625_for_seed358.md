The caverns are in a state of constant, slow transformation, with water seeping through the cracks and fissures, creating a network of small, meandering streams and pools. The air is thick with a fine mist, condensing on the walls and dripping steadily, creating a constant, damp environment.

Clumps of pale, fleshy fungi and lichens cling to the walls and ceilings, drawing sustenance from the moisture and the dim, filtered light. Scattered, gnarled roots and tendrils of unknown, underground plants twist and turn, seeking out the few pockets of nutrients in the rocky soil. The air is pungent with the earthy, slightly metallic scent of these hardy, subterranean flora.

### Atmosphere

The occasional, distant clank of machinery and the rumble of heavy equipment add to the industrial, almost alien feel of the environment.

### Sounds

The air is thick with the sounds of industry, a constant, unending symphony of mechanical and organic noise.

The occasional, larger, predatory creatures, adapted to the dim light and industrial environment, can be seen moving through the shadows, their eyes glowing with a faint, eerie light. The sparse vegetation, while limited, provides a crucial source of sustenance for the subterranean fauna, with the fungi and lichens serving as a primary food source.

The rules of this subterranean world are shaped by the slow, gradual processes of erosion and the constant, industrial presence. The environment is in a state of constant, slow transformation, with the water seeping through the cracks and fissures, gradually eroding the rock and creating new, meandering paths. This subterranean expanse is a place of raw, emerging geology, slow, patient processes, and a constant, industrial presence.
