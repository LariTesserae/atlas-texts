The environment is one of gradual cycles and patient processes, a place where change happens over long durations, and the flux is minimal. The ecology of this place is a complex and interconnected web of life and structure. The creatures and the structures are not separate entities but are part of a single, unified system. The environment is one of deep interdependence, where the boundaries between life and non-life are blurred.

The rules of this world are governed by the slow, patient processes that define it. The structures are not static but are in a state of constant, slow change, and the creatures are not independent but are part of a larger, interconnected system. The world is one of deep, ancient wisdom, where the cycles of life and change are etched into the very fabric of the environment.
