Preparing for a visit to this unique and enigmatic high-altitude mountain range requires a blend of physical, mental, and logistical readiness. Here’s a detailed guide for an ordinary human multiverse traveler:

### Physical Preparation
1. **Altitude Acclimatization**:
   - **High-Altitude Training**: Spend a few weeks at progressively higher altitudes to acclimatize your body to the thin air. This will help prevent altitude sickness, which can be debilitating.
   - **Cardiovascular Fitness**: Engage in regular cardiovascular exercises such as running, cycling, and swimming to improve your lung and heart function.
   - **Strength Training**: Focus on building core and leg strength, as you will need to navigate uneven and challenging terrain.

2. **Health and Medical Precautions**:
   - **Medical Check-Up**: Get a thorough medical check-up to ensure you are in good health. Discuss any pre-existing conditions with your doctor.
   - **Vaccinations**: Ensure your vaccinations are up to date, and consider getting a tetanus shot.
   - **Emergency Kit**: Pack a comprehensive first-aid kit, including medications for altitude sickness, pain relief, and any personal prescriptions.

### Gear and Equipment
1. **Clothing**:
   - **Layered Clothing**: Dress in layers to adapt to the fluctuating temperatures. Include a base layer for moisture wicking, an insulating layer, and a waterproof and windproof outer layer.
   - **Insulated Jacket and Pants**: Heavyweight, insulated gear to protect against the cold.
   - **Gloves and Hat**: Insulated gloves and a warm hat to prevent frostbite.
   - **Boots**: High-quality, insulated, and waterproof boots with good traction.

2. **Survival and Navigation**:
   - **Map and Compass**: A detailed map of the area and a reliable compass. Consider a GPS device for additional navigation.
   - **Survival Gear**: A multi-tool, a survival knife, a fire starter, and a lightweight tent or bivvy sack.
   - **Water and Food**:
     - **Hydration System**: A high-capacity water bottle or hydration pack. Consider a water filter or purification tablets.
     - **High-Energy Food**: Non-perishable, high-calorie snacks like nuts, energy bars, and dehydrated meals.
   - **Sleeping Gear**: A high-quality sleeping bag rated for sub-freezing temperatures and a lightweight, insulated sleeping mat.

3. **Communication and Power**:
   - **Satellite Phone or Beacon**: In case of emergencies, a satellite phone or emergency beacon is essential, as cell service is likely.
   - **Power Bank**: A high-capacity power bank to charge your devices, including your phone, GPS, and any other electronic gear.

1. **Mental Resilience**:

   - **Positive Mindset**: Maintain a positive and determined mindset. Visualize success and prepare for potential setbacks.

2. **Research and Knowledge**:
