### 2. **Clothing and Gear**
- **Clothing**:

  - **Climbing Gear**: If you plan to climb, bring a climbing harness, carabiners, and a rope.

### 3. **Technological and Communication Equipment**
- **Communication Devices**: Bring a satellite phone or a two-way radio, as the dense vegetation and rock formations may block traditional communication signals.
