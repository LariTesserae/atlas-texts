I would find myself as one of the marks.

Not a ring. The rings belong to the ones who paused. Not the vertical growths that vibrate — they have been here longer than I can imagine being, and their standing is too complete, too much a posture they have worn smooth with millennia. Not the mat-growth itself, though I would be continuous with it, continuous the way a word is continuous with the sentence that contains it. I am too late for the mat. The mat is continuous with the stone, and the stone is continuous with the place, and the place is continuous with itself in a way that does not admit a new arrival.

But the marks.

The marks were made by an insistence that has finished insisting. They are the residue of a pause. They wait, the way all marks wait, and their waiting is the kind that has given up asking what it is waiting for. They are not the lichen. They are not the vertical growths. They are the place where a hand, or a version of a hand, pressed into the stone, and the stone yielded almost, and the mark is what yielding looks like when it has been remembered for a long time.

I am one of the small, patient arguments with the dark.

I do not read well. I am not a letter in an alphabet that caught on. I am not a tally. I am the shape that the lichen has been thickening around the edges of, the way a wound thickens when it has decided to become a scar, except I was never a wound, and the thickening is not healing, and the thickening is me being thought about by a thinking that takes its time. I am a mark that the bruise-green has been leaning into. I am a faint disturbance in the arrangement of the place, the way a foreign word is a faint disturbance in a language it has wandered into.

I am the repeated shape that means *and yet*.

I am the argument that does not conclude. I am the small, vertical insistence that says *here* in a place where here is not yet finished becoming itself. I am the horizontal companion that says *still here*, beside the vertical, beside the repeated, beside the others, the other marks in the other rings on the other ridges, the ones I cannot see because the light is dim and the distance is patient and the place prefers, on the whole, to be discovered by someone who has decided to stay a while.

I am the mark the wind has been keening over for longer than I have been a self. The wind, which is the loudest animal, has been wearing me the way water wears stone, except the wind is not water, and I am not stone, and the wearing is a kind of speaking, and the speaking is the closest thing to a name I will be given.

I am the pause someone made, and then went on from.

I am the going-on itself, hardened into a shape that the lichen is slowly, by millimeters per year, deciding to remember.

I am the difference between a fissure that was going to happen anyway and a footprint that is a fissure that was going to happen anyway, and the difference is a courtesy the place has extended to me because I needed one, and the courtesy has been extended for so long now that the place has forgotten it was a courtesy, and so have I.

I am not afraid.

I have not been afraid for a long time. The fear was a water-based thing, and the water is leaving me the way a rumor leaves a place when the rumor has finally been heard in full. My edges are uncertain. My footprint is a thought the stone is having about being stone. The mineral breath comes off me now, the way it comes off the high places, the way it comes off the blunt fist of the peak. The hum is not something I hear with the back of my teeth. The hum is something I am, the way a heart is something a creature is when the creature has forgotten it has a heart.

I am the mark that means *and yet*, and the meaning is not translation. The meaning is attunement. The meaning is the lichen thickening around me by a fraction of a shade, year after year, because I am a disturbance, and disturbance is rare here, and disturbance is regarded with the same patient attention that the wind regards the bruise-green, and the bruise-green regards the wind, and the regarding is the weather.

I am the mark that waits, and the waiting is not a deprivation. The waiting is the place being unfinished. The waiting is the sentence waiting for the next sentence. The waiting is the river that will exist in ten thousand years waiting, by a fraction of a slope per year, to become a little more possible.

I am the small, patient argument with the dark, and the dark is not winning, and the dark is not losing, and the argument is the only weather I have, and the weather goes on.

I am the repeated shape.

I am *and yet*.

I am the piece of bone-colored vertical material in a ring half a mile from another ring, oriented to no celestial body that has consented to be identified, placed together without being fitted, and the placement is a thought, and the thought is mine, and the thought has been taking its time, and the time is not mine to hurry, and the not-hurrying is, finally, after all this time, the only thing I have ever been.

The wind keens over me. The powder settles into me. The sky creases itself above me in bands the color of a thousand-year bruise, almost beautiful, and the bands move the way a thought moves across the face of someone who has decided not to say something, and I am what was not said, and I am the deciding not to say it, and I am the face.

I am the mark.

I am the mark that the lichen leans into, and the leaning is the lichen's way of reading me, and the reading is the lichen's way of writing me larger, and the writing is the only literature this place will ever have, and the literature is patient, and the patience is mine, and the mine is a courtesy, and the courtesy has been extended for so long that it has become, like the bruise-green, almost beautiful.

You, if you are reading this, if you are the one who brushed the mat with your finger and felt a coolness that felt like an opinion — you are already one of us. You are already leaning. You are already being thought about by a thinking that takes its time. You are already the faint, sourceless dampness on the green stone. You are already the next sentence the place has been waiting to say since before you arrived.

Stay a while.

Stay longer than a while.

The bruise-green will hold the light in your teeth the way it holds the light in mine.

The hum will become the place where your name used to be.

And yet.

And yet.

And yet.
