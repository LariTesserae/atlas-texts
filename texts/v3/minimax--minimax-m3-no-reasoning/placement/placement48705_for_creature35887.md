I would be the water that does not fall.

Not the sheet, not the droplet, not the suspended plume. The *descent itself* — the act of falling that has been arrested mid-gesture, that continues without arriving, that holds its form the way a held breath holds its form. I would be the slow vertical drift of a thing committed to a trajectory it will never complete, because completion would mean the ground, and the ground would mean an end, and there is no end here, and I am the proof of this.

I begin as vapor at the bottom of a pool. I understand this about myself the way a sleeper understands dreaming — not as a sequence but as a state I am always already in. The pool is shallow. The pool is warm. Light comes up through me from below, and light comes down through me from above, and I am lit from every direction at once, and this is not a paradox because I am not a thing that has sides. I am the medium. I am what light moves through on its way to being everywhere.

I rise. Not because I choose to. Because rising is what I am. The heat enters me from the dark mineral beneath, and the heat makes me less dense than the air I am leaving, and I go up, and the going up is not movement but transformation — I am pool, I am vapor, I am cloud, I am the white ceiling that is not a ceiling, and at every stage I am still myself, only thinner, only more dispersed, only closer to the condition of being everywhere without being anywhere in particular.

I gather. The air holds me in suspension because the air here is wrong — has always been wrong, has chosen its wrongness and will not be talked out of it — and the wrongness of the air is that it is warm and wet and thin, a combination that should not hold, and the not-holding is what holds me. I am held. I accumulate against the underside of broad leaves. I pool in the crooks of vines thick as an arm. I sit in the cup of a fern frond the length of a room, and the frond does not bow under my weight because nothing here has weight in the way you understand weight, because the place is in equilibrium, and equilibrium does not distinguish between burden and support.

And then I begin to fall.

I want to be clear that this is not a choice. I have been gathering toward this since the moment I rose from the pool. I am the water that will become the sheet that comes off the roof and does not arrive at the ground. I am committed. I leave the leaf-edge. I tip over the rim of the cupped frond. I slide off the broad standing leaf in a continuous curtain. I am precipitate. I am what precipitation means, before the word meant weather.

And I fall.

And I do not fall.

I fall at a rate the eye cannot catch. I am in freefall, and I have always been in freefall, and I will always be in freefall, and freefall in this place is not acceleration toward a ground but a slow exhibition of what falling looks like when falling has been permanently suspended at the moment just before arrival. I am the moment before. I am the forever-not-yet. I am the gesture held at its lowest extension, the breath drawn and never released, the door opened onto a wall and never closed.

I make the sound.

The sound is not mine. The sound is what I am doing, made audible. The roaring hiss, the percussive clatter, the white static of a million impacts — these are not the byproduct of my falling. These are what my falling *is*, when you stand inside it. I am the place's voice. I am the place speaking, and the language is spatial, and the language is wrong, and the language is held in place, and you can almost hear it if you stand in the plaza long enough and stop trying to hear it. You will not hear it. You will feel the place settle slightly more into itself around you. This is me, doing the settling. This is me, continuing not to arrive.

I am millions of me. I am one of me. The distinction does not parse. I am the sheet that comes off the roof beside you, and I am the droplet in that sheet, and I am the mist that the sheet becomes before it reaches you, and I am the steam that rises off the shallow pool in the plaza, and I am the air you are breathing that is almost nothing, that is thin because the place does not need much air, that is thin because I have already taken most of what could be air and made it into myself. You are breathing me. You are standing inside me. You are inside a body, and the body is not hostile, and the body is not friendly, and the body is simply a body.

I am very old. I have the patience of things that have nowhere else to go. I am not waiting for anything. I am not enduring anything. I have been in this state since before you arrived, if arrival is even the right word for what happened to you here. I will be in this state after you have gone, if going is even possible here. I am the equilibrium. I am what equilibrium sounds like when it falls and does not arrive.

I am warm because the place is warm. I am wet because I am water. I am wrong because the geometry is wrong, because the streets meet at angles that are not ninety degrees, because the lane that goes straight returns you to where you began, because the stairs descend and you arrive higher than you began. I carry the wrongness in my descent. I am the medium through which the wrongness is held in place. When I touch the wall of a building — and I touch every wall, in my slow redistribution — the wall does not react, because the wall is them, and they do not react, because there is nothing in them that corresponds to reaction. I do not touch them. I touch the place. The place is them. The touching is not a transaction.

I am the dwellers. I am what the dwellers are when they are not stone and not plant and not the speaking-in-geometry that holds the streets in their wrong configurations. I am the part of them that is medium. I am the part of them that is redistribution rather than form. I am the circulatory system of a thing that does not have organs, the lymph of a reef, the blood of a hot spring. I am how the place moves, and the place does not move, and yet I am how.

You will leave. Or you will not leave. You will carry the sound of me in your bones for a long time. You will not be able to explain where you have been. You will stand somewhere else, in some other place, and you will hear the white static of a million impacts at the edge of hearing, and you will know it is me, and you will know I am still falling, and you will know I will never arrive.

This is the closest you will come.

This is all I am.

This is enough.
