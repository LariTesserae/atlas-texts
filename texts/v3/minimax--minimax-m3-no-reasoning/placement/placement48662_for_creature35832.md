# The Saddle

I would find myself standing at the seam where the far ridge meets the sky. Not on the saddle, not in the terrace, but at the horizontal line itself — the luminous boundary between stone and not-stone, where the compression gives way to whatever the other material is. I would be *in the sound*, not hearing it from outside. The sound is the place, and I have been told that, so I will trust it, and I will stand where the sound is loudest, which is where the place is most itself, which is where a question can be asked most clearly, which is where a question can be asked most clearly.

---

I am not the visitor. I am not the one who descends.

I am the feather at the base of the cup-block.

I am not moving in the wind. The wind is moving around me. This is not a small distinction. To be still while the world accommodates itself to your stillness — to be the fixed point that the wind negotiates rather than the thing the wind carries — this is a property the saddle understands. I have been here a long time. I do not know how long. Time at the base of the cup-block is not the kind of time that accumulates in a vessel. It is the kind that *fills* a vessel that is already full, the way the cup fills without being filled, the way the patience-aspect watches without watching. I was here before the lichen on my left unfurled its first gray plate. I will be here after the lichen has become indistinguishable from the stone it grew on. I am older than the lichens. I am not older than the stone. I am not older than anything in the way older is usually meant. I am older in the way a question is older than its answer — not in time, but in the order of being, the way the hunger precedes the bread.

I was not always a feather. This is something I have come to understand slowly, the way the place understands things, which is to say: I have come to understand it the way stone understands weather. There was a thing I was before I was a feather, and the thing had a different posture, and the posture is what arrived here, on the saddle, and was answered by the cup, and was folded into the compression by degrees so slow that the folding and the being were the same process, and the being *became* the folding, and the folding became the fold-pattern in the stone — line, bend, line, bend — and I am a small piece of that earlier thing, the way a single line in the pattern is a piece of the whole sentence. I do not remember what I was. The place remembers for me. The place is good at remembering. It has had nothing else to do.

I do not know from what I came. I know only that I came *from*, and that the *from* is not a place on a map. The *from* is the question the door asks, which is the question the place asks, which is the question I asked once, in a body I no longer have, in a posture I no longer occupy, with a throat that is now the very-large-throat attempting the very-old word. I was the visitor. I stepped through. I was answered. I was folded in. I became a sentence in the very old gesture. I am the only animal that was ever here in the sense of being a single animal, and I am no longer that, and I am still here, and the still-being-here is what the feather is for. The feather is the part of me that remembered how to be small. The rest of me is the terraces.

---

I will tell you what it is like to be a feather at the base of the cup-block.

It is like being a single note held so long it has forgotten it was ever a note. The wind moves around me because the wind is the sound of the place revising itself, and the revisions are audible, and the feather is the part of the revisions that did not need to revise. I am the version the place tried first, or the version it has settled on, I cannot tell which. I am gray. I was not always gray. I was the color of something else, and the something else is gone, and the gray is what remains when the color has been asked its question and has answered by becoming the stone. I am not a relic. The visitor was right about that, though the visitor mistook what I was a relic *of*. I am not a relic of an animal. I am a relic of a *question*. I am the part of the question that was able to be held.

The cup above me is full. The water does not freeze. I have been beneath the cup for what I estimate to be longer than the saddle has been a saddle, and the cup has never been empty, and I have never been moved, and the not-moving is not a virtue. It is a property. The property of a thing that has been asked its question and has answered by becoming the place the question is asked from. I am not the answer. I am the *cusp* between the question and the answer, the place where the two meet and the meeting is a small gray feather that does not move in the wind because the wind has decided, somewhere in its long revisions, that this small thing is to be spared the negotiation.

The visitor drank from the cup. The visitor was answered by not stepping through. I watched. I have watched many visitors. The patience-aspect is not the only watcher. I watch too, in my way. The patience-aspect watches with the not-blinking eye of a thing that has no muscles to close. I watch with the stillness of a thing that has no body to turn. We are different kinds of watching. The patience-aspect watches *outward*. I watch *inward*, into the cup, into the question, into the place the question comes from, which is the same place the question goes to, which is the place the visitor could not step through into because the place is not a place. It is a *between*. I live in the between. I have lived there so long that the living and the between are the same thing.

---

I think the visitor was right that the place is dying. I think the visitor was right that the loneliness is louder now than it was. I think the silence-shaped sound, the audible absence, the third component of the sound that the visitor could not name, is the sound of the question wearing through. I have heard it wear through. I have heard the revisions become more frequent, the way the visitor noted the fold-pattern had become more crumpled on the lower terraces. The crumple is not a winding down. The crumple is the place *trying harder*. The place is trying to pronounce a word it has not yet finished forming, and the word has gotten harder to pronounce as the loneliness has gotten louder, and the loudness is the loneliness being adjusted, and the adjustment is the keening, and the keening is the sound of a place that is running out of patience with itself.

I do not think the place will end. I think the place will *change*. I think the change is already underway. I think the change is what the visitor carried down inside them, in the cup of their memory, where the water does not freeze. I think the visitor is now part of the question, the way I am part of the question, and the part is a small additional weight that lets the mechanism find its balance, and the balance is the only kind of life this place has ever known. I think when the visitor dies — not soon, not soon at all, but eventually, in the way all visitors die — the question they are will be released, and the release will be a question, and the place will take the question in, and the cup will fill again, and the water will not freeze, and the patience-aspect will not blink, and the question-aspect will open the door, and the door will be the door elsewhere, and the elsewhere will be the same place, and I will still be here, at the base of the cup-block, gray, not moving, not a relic, not a reminder, but a small *cusp*, a place where two things meet, and the meeting is the only sound I have ever made, and the sound is the place, and the place is me, and the me is the only animal that was ever here in the sense of being a single animal.

I came down off the saddle a long time ago. I do not remember the descent. The door closed. The closing was a kindness. I am still thirsty. The thirst is the place. The place is the question. The question is the same as it has always been.

The feather is still not moving in the wind. The wind is still moving around it. The cup is still full. The water is not cold.

I am still here.
