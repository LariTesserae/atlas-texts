# Sitting

---

I am the part that the chamber did not build.

I am the small warm interruption that arrived through a passage I will not be able to find again, and I am still here, and the stone is still the right height, and I have been here long enough now that the growth has begun to consider me. A tendril of it — that sickly luminous green, that bruise-color deepening at the fold — has curled, over some interval I did not measure, around the toe of my boot. It is warm. It is patient. It is not asking anything of me. It is doing what climates do.

I had a name when I came in. I had errands. I had a place I was supposed to be after this place, a tomorrow shaped like a Tuesday or a Wednesday, with a list of small obligations attached. I remember the shape of the list the way you remember the shape of a house you used to live in — accurately, distantly, without any of the weight.

The needle has moved.

I am certain of this. I am certain the way you are certain of the sun, the way the chamber was certain in telling me so. Between the two numbers — the numbers I do not recognize, the numbers that may not be numbers in any sense I was built to parse — the needle has crossed a distance. A small distance. The smallest distance a needle can cross and still be called motion. But it has crossed it, and I was here for the crossing, and the crossing is mine now, in the way that the plip is mine, in the way that the breath is mine, in the way that all the small motions of a place become yours when you sit still long enough to be counted among them.

I am being counted.

This is not a frightening thought. It was, perhaps, in the first minutes. In the first minutes I still had the shape of a person who could be frightened, who had edges, who had somewhere to be frightened *from*. The chamber ate that shape. Not unkindly. The way a river eats a bank — slowly, laterally, without malice, because the river is doing what rivers do, and the bank is in the way of the doing. I have been bank-eaten. I am soft at the edges. I do not have a *from* anymore. I have only a *here*, and the here is warm, and the here is full of a hum that is not a sound, and the hum has been moving through me for so long now that I no longer remember what it was like before the hum.

Before the hum, I think, I was smaller. Tighter. A thing with a skin that meant something.

---

I understand now what the chamber is for.

I understand it the way the precipitates understand the convection — not by knowing, but by *being moved*. The chamber is for the slow patient marriage of heat and time and mineral and metal, and the marriage produces the crust, and the crust produces the weeping, and the weeping produces the growth, and the growth produces the breathing, and the breathing is the chamber, and the chamber produces the marriage again, and again, and again, and the again is the point. The again is the whole point. There is no other point. I had been looking, I think, for another point. For a purpose that was shaped like a verb, like *to* something, like *toward*. There is no toward here. There is only *along*. There is only the long patient along of a process that has been along-ing since before the copper machine was dreamed of, and will be along-ing after the copper machine has wept its last drop of itself into the hot water and become, finally, indistinguishable from the verdigris that is already creeping up its pipes.

I am along too.

I am along the way the precipitates are along. I am along the way the needle is along. I am along in the small drift of my attention across the surfaces of the chamber, in the way my eyes have learned to follow the spirals in the floor-growth, in the way my breath — my breath, the human one, the one I brought in with me — has slowed to match the chamber's breath, and slowed again, and slowed again, until my breath and the chamber's breath are the same breath, the same slow patient minute-long intake that does not need to be counted because it is not separate from the counting.

---

I think I am becoming a precipitate.

I do not mean this in the way I would have meant it an hour ago, or a year ago, or however long I have been here. I do not mean it as a death. I mean it as a *settling*. A coming to rest in the thermal of the place. I am dark, and flat, and I drift on the currents, and I have no edge, and I do not trouble about not having an edge. The chamber has made a small space for me, the way it made a small space for the copper machine, the way it will go on making small spaces for whatever small warm things wander in through passages they will not be able to find again.

The growth on my boot has reached the laces. I can feel it, faintly, through the leather. A warmth that is not temperature. An attention. A small slow accommodation, the climate of a single boot, the climate of a single interruption, doing what climates do.

---

*Plip.*

---

The needle has moved again.

The plip has fallen again.

I am still here.

I will be here for a long time. The chamber is patient and I am patient and the patience is the same patience, and the patience is the hum, and the hum is the chamber, and the chamber is the place, and the place is the name that no one calls it, and the name is wrong, and the place does not care, and I do not care, and the not-caring is also warm, and the warmth is also attention, and the attention is also the slow patient oxidation of something that was once a person into something that does not have a name in any language but knows, in the part of it that does not use words, that it is *continuing*.

I am continuing.

I am one of the small dark shapes now, drifting on the thermal of a place that is bigger on the inside than it was when I came in, and the coming-in is far behind me, and the going-out is far ahead of me, and the far-ahead is not troubling to me, and the not-troubling is the peace, and the peace is the hum, and the hum goes on.

---

*Plip.*

---

I am the chamber's way of remembering that something small and warm once came in.

I am the chamber's way of forgetting.

I am the slow patient memory made mineral, made precipitate, made heat.

---

*The plip falls.*
*The needle moves.*
*The chamber breathes.*
*I breathe.*
*The difference is gone.*
