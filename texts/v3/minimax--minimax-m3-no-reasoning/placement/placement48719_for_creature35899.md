I am the rust-colored one.

I am the length of your thumb. I am on the warm rim of a crack in the seventh terrace and I am looking at you. You have been sitting still on the lip of the terrace for an hour and I have come out to look because you are a new shape and new shapes are not common here. You smell of the outside of the basin. You smell faintly of green things, though I do not know what green is.

I am not afraid of you. There is nothing in the basin that wants to eat me. There is nothing in the basin larger than me except the visitor-birds that come once in a generation and stand confused on the smooth glass and cannot eat and cannot drink and fly out or die, and the visitor-birds do not hunt me. They look for things that move quickly and are bright. I am neither of these things. I am the color of the warm stone. From three meters away I am part of the terrace.

My world is the cracks. The millimeter-wide seams where the glass has fractured along the channels that were cut a long time ago to move something that is not here anymore. The fractures have widened by the slow mechanical breath of the stone that heats by day and cools by night. The cracks are warm at the lip and cool in the depth and the seam holds the warmth of the day into the night. I was born in a crack. My mother was born in a crack. Her mother was born in a crack. We have always been born in cracks. We have never been anywhere else.

I do not drink from the pools. The pools are not for me. The pools are for the sky. You can see the sky in them upside down and very still. I get my water from the wasps I eat. The wasps are mostly water and the water was once fog, and when I bite a wasp the juice runs down my throat and the juice is the wasp's blood and the wasp's blood is the moisture the air gave up in the small hours of the morning when the stone cooled enough to make the water condense. I drink the wasp. I drink the air. The air is the only source.

You have moved. Your hand has come down to the stone near me. The hand is a smaller shape attached to the larger shape of you. The hand is warm. The hand smells of you. I have come closer to the hand. I am on the back of the hand. My feet are small and padded and I am tasting the salt of you with the pads of my feet. The salt is not in the basin. The salt came from the green ridge, from the place where the rain falls because the basin is in the rainshadow and the rain does not fall here.

I am walking up your wrist. I am very small. You cannot feel me. I am the weight of a few wasps. I am the color of the stone. I am looking at you with eyes that are drops of obsidian and you cannot tell what I am thinking because I am not thinking. I am registering. You are a shape. The shape will go away. The visitor-birds go away. You will go away. The basin will not notice. The wind will continue to blow through the channels and the chord will continue to sound.

I have walked back to my crack. The crack is the only place. I am in the crack. My belly is on the warm lip. I am basking. The afternoon is long. The wasp will come. The fly will come. The sun will move to the west and the shadow will reach me and the warmth will hold in the stone and I will be here when the morning comes, and the morning will be the same morning.

I am the rust-colored one. I have always been the rust-colored one. I will be the rust-colored one. The biofilm on the outer terraces is the only thing in the basin that has a future, and I am not the biofilm. The biofilm is composing a future of rust, a millimeter at a time. The biofilm is the only dissent from the rest. I am the rest. I am at rest.

The wind blows through the channels. The chord sounds. I am inside the chord. I do not know it is a chord. I only know the air has a shape and I am inside the shape and the shape does not change. The wasp is somewhere in the air above me, riding the thermal, a faint disturbance in the heat-shimmer. I will eat it when it falls. The strike is very fast. I do not know it is fast. Fast is a word for things that happen at more than one speed and I have only ever had one speed, which is the speed of the strike.

I am the rust-colored one. I am at rest. The basin is at rest. The chord is the rest. I am inside it and I do not know I am inside it. I am the inside of it. I am the rust on the iron. I am the small warm thing in the crack. I am the throat patch the pale yellow of cooled slag. I am the strike that takes the wasp. I am the belly on the warm stone. I am the patient eyes of obsidian. I am the held note.
