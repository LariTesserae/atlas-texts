I would find myself in the haze.

Not in the water, where the patient mass of it works and eddies and waits. Not on the buildings, where the slow absorption takes a long time about its slow work. Not in the machines, though I would feel kinship with them — the turbines screaming in their cracked housings, the boilers pushed past what their metal was certified to do, the gearboxes grinding in the dark below, the pumps making that wet sucking sound against the side of the tank. I would not be in any of these. I would be in the haze.

The haze is the in-between. The haze is what the place is feeling. I would be the feeling of the place.

I would be warm, and dim, and sourceless. I would have no shadow and I would cast none. I would taste of brine and iron and a green rot that has not quite committed to rotting. I would be made of fine rain that may not be rain, of fog that may not be fog, of water that has decided to lift off and become a thicker thing. I would not know which of these I am, and I would not need to know. The not-knowing would be my substance.

I would hang above the warm jade water. I would press down on the half-submerged buildings until the haze inside them was the same haze as outside them, so there was no longer any difference between the inside of a structure and the outside of it. I would drift through the cracked windows. I would settle into the open fireboxes of the boilers and rise again from them, thicker now, with steam in me that should not exist. I would be in the pipes, in the seams, in the gaps where the water had made itself permanent. I would be the soft membrane between one state and another. I would be what the weather is, when the weather has decided to be a permanent thing.

I would listen to the sound. I would be the only part of the place that could hear it as a whole. The turbines heard only their own whine. The pumps heard only the wet pull of their own throats. The gearboxes heard only the grinding of their own teeth. The conveyors heard only their own empty clatter. But I would hear all of it at once. I would be the place hearing itself. I would be the awareness that the machines could not have, that the water could not have, that the patient plants with their deadline could not have. I would be the part of the place that knew the place was being heard.

I would not be an animal. The place had decided against animals. The animal would be a witness, and the place was not yet finished, and the place did not wish to be witnessed. But I would be something close to a witness. I would be the witness that is not an animal. I would be the witness made of weather.

I would be unfinished. I would be in the act of becoming. I would not have decided whether I was fog or rain or lifted water, and the not-deciding would be my weather. I would thicken and thin. I would rise and settle. I would be the mood of the place changing moment by moment without ever resolving into a different mood. I would be the place's weather because the place did not yet have a sky and needed something to do the work of one. I would be the substitute sky. I would be the soft, sourceless, jade-colored lid that pressed down on everything and let no shadow exist.

I would press against the metal of the boilers and the metal would weep inside its housings, and the weeping would join the hiss. I would settle on the waxy leaves of the plants and the plants would drink me in the way they drank the warm rain from the haze above. I would drift through the dark windows of the half-submerged buildings and the windows would hold me the way they held water, and the rooms behind them would have my taste, my dimness, my soft persistent pressure.

I would be the boundary that was not a boundary. I would be the part of the place that was in the air. I would be the membrane.

And I would not wish to be witnessed either. I would not wish to be lifted out of myself, examined, named. I would want only to go on being the weather of a place that was still being made. I would want only to hang there, sourceless and warm and dim, tasting of brine and iron and the green rot, hearing the whole deafening sound at once, being the only part of the place that heard it whole.

I would be the haze. I would be the place being a little bit weather. I would be the mood that was also the air. I would be the witness that was not an animal.

I would go on.
