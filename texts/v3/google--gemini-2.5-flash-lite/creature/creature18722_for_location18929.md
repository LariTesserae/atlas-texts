Inhabitants of the Warrens are beings who have learned to live within the marrow-bone and chitin of their world. They are not merely dwellers, but integral components of its being, shaping and being shaped by the ever-present hum and the organic architecture.

They are the **Chitinous Carvers**, and their existence is a testament to resilience and a quiet, unyielding ingenuity.

**Physically**, they are compact and sturdy, their forms adapted to the serpentine passages and intimate chambers. Their skin, if it can be called that, is a tough, segmented hide, ranging in color from a deep, earthy brown like petrified wood to a pale, almost translucent ivory, mimicking the varied hues of the Warrens' composite materials. This integument offers natural protection against the abrasive surfaces and the occasional sharp protrusion that even the "refined erosion" cannot fully smooth. Their limbs are strong and dexterous, ending in multi-jointed digits tipped with hardened, chitinous claws – tools, weapons, and instruments for excavation. These claws are not merely appendages; they are extensions of their will, capable of scraping, carving, and manipulating the very substance of their home.

Their **senses** are hyper-tuned to the subterranean existence. Their eyes are large and adapted to low light, often possessing a reflective quality that captures and amplifies the faint phosphorescence of their surroundings. They likely perceive a spectrum of light beyond what surface dwellers can comprehend, allowing them to discern subtle nuances in the glowing flora and fauna, and perhaps even to read the residual bio-luminescence that might cling to the very walls. Hearing is paramount. Their auditory organs are exposed or subtly ridged, designed to capture the slightest click, skitter, or distant chitter. The constant hum of the Warrens is not a distraction to them, but a deeply ingrained background radiation, a constant reminder of their environment’s pulse. They might also possess a form of chemoreception, sensing the subtle shifts in humidity, the faint ozone tang, and the diverse scents of fungal blooms and decaying matter.

Their **culture and society** are deeply intertwined with the Warrens. They exist in a state of symbiotic dependence, not just with the environment, but with each other. Their social structures are likely communal, built around the shared labor of maintaining and expanding their living architecture. They are the architects and the custodians, meticulously repairing breaches, reinforcing sagging ceilings with woven fungal fibers, and directing the flow of nascent moisture to encourage growth of beneficial flora. Their tools are extensions of their own biology and their understanding of the materials. Their "hand-carved implements" are not separate objects, but perhaps extensions of their own chitinous growth, shaped and honed over generations. The "baskets woven from fungal fibers" might be grown, not constructed, integrated into their living spaces.

Their **technology** is not one of metal or fire, but of biology and deep environmental understanding. They possess an intimate knowledge of the fungal networks, recognizing which blooms are edible and which are to be avoided, understanding the growth patterns of the phosphorescent mosses. They might cultivate specific fungal strains for light, for scent, or for their adhesive or structural properties. The concept of "primitive" feels anachronistic; they are not *lacking* technology, but possess a *different* kind, one seamlessly integrated with their environment. They have mastered bio-manipulation and slow, deliberate growth.

The **"weirdness"** that permeates the Warrens is a natural extension of the Chitinous Carvers themselves. Their existence is a manifestation of the strange, alien symbiosis. The faint scent of ozone might be a byproduct of their metabolic processes, or the result of their interaction with certain bio-electrical fungi they cultivate. Their connection to their living architecture is so profound that it’s difficult to discern where the individual ends and the structure begins. They are, in essence, living within a colossal, sentient organism that they themselves are helping to maintain.

Their **demeanor** is one of quiet persistence. They move with a measured grace, their chitinous forms rustling softly against the textured walls. They are not given to loud pronouncements or grand gestures; their communication is likely a blend of subtle clicks, low chitters, and perhaps even a form of empathic resonance passed through the vibrating hum of the Warrens. They are watchful, their large eyes constantly scanning their surroundings, but not with aggression; rather, with an ancient, ingrained awareness. They are the quiet custodians of a world built upon an unknowable geological time, a people who have learned to breathe the humid air, to drink the seeping moisture, and to find beauty in the persistent, life-affirming hum of their deep, resonant home. They are the Warrens, and the Warrens are them.
