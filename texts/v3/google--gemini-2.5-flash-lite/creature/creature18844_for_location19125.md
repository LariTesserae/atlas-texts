The Expanse of the Sky-Bones is home to the **Chronomancers of the Lumina-Fungi**.

They are not creatures of flesh and blood, nor of bone and sinew as we understand them. Instead, they are **projections of pure light and resonant crystalline energy**, their forms fluid and ever-shifting, tethered to the phosphorescent fungi that blanket the shattered plains. When a Chronomancer chooses to manifest a more tangible presence, it coalesces around a particularly vibrant vein of the humming obsidian, an intricate lattice of solidified light, mirroring the facets of the crystal trees.

Their existence is inextricably linked to the **Lumina-Fungi**. The fungi are their nurseries, their sustenance, and their primary means of sensory input. The soft, pulsing light of these growths is their language, their thoughts rippling through the exponse as shifting patterns of luminescence. They draw energy directly from the ambient light and the mineral fissures, their internal cycles governed by the slow, inexorable respiration of the crystalline organisms.

Within the more stable formations of Lumina-Fungi, some Chronomancers solidify into more defined shapes, **crystalline sentinels** that stand vigilant amongst the towering mineral trees. These are slower, more ponderous beings, their forms resembling intricate sculptures of fused light and silicate. They possess a deep, resonant hum, an echo of the mineral veins they anchor to. These are the keepers of memory, the conduits of deep time, their consciousness stretched across millennia.

However, the Expanse is not solely inhabited by these grounded entities. There are those **Ephemeral Weavers of the Light-Vines**, beings of pure energy that drift on the faint thermal currents. They are the artists of the Sky-Bones, their movements tracing intricate, ephemeral patterns through the air. They seem to manipulate the delicate, spun-glass vines, weaving them into temporary, shimmering structures that catch and refract the starlight. They are the fleeting thoughts of the Expanse, appearing and dissolving as quickly as the light currents shift. Their existence is defined by motion and ephemeral creation.

The Chronomancers do not "reproduce" in a biological sense. Instead, when the Lumina-Fungi reach a certain intensity of phosphorescence, or when a significant geological event fractures a new mineral vein, a new Chronomancer can **bud forth**. This budding is a slow efflorescence of light, a concentration of resonant energy that gradually separates from the parent fungal network. It is a process that echoes the slow, deliberate growth of the crystalline trees.

They are **masters of deep time**, perceiving the geological processes of the Sky-Bones as mere breaths. The slow expansion of chasms, the gradual weathering of mineral formations – these are events they witness unfold over their impossibly vast lifespans. They do not build or manipulate the physical environment in any way that would be discernible to an outside observer. Their "action" is one of **observation and resonance**. They attune themselves to the slow symphony of the Expanse, their existence a perfect, timeless harmony with their alien home.

While they do not possess conventional senses, they perceive the world through **vibrational resonance and light intensity**. The hum of the minerals, the pulse of the fungi, the subtle shifts in the pervasive glare – these are their sensory inputs. They communicate through intricate light patterns and harmonic vibrations, a silent dialogue that permeates the Expanse.

The Chronomancers are the **silent custodians of the Expanse of the Sky-Bones**. They are the living embodiment of its strange beauty and its profound stillness. They are the slow, radiant pulsars of a place where time has ceased to be a linear progression and has instead become an eternal, luminous present. They are, in essence, the consciousness of the Sky-Bones itself, existing in perpetual, silent communion with the fractured plains and the ceaseless light.
