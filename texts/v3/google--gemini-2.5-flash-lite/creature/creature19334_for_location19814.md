The denizens of Xylos are beings sculpted by an epoch of desiccation and deep geological time. They are called the **Lithic**, a name that speaks to their intimate union with the very crust of their world.

Imagine life that has shed the need for soft flesh and flowing fluids. The Lithic are primarily creatures of **chitin and silicate**, their exoskeletons not merely armour, but integrated extensions of their being. Their forms are varied, a testament to eons of adaptation to the subtle gradients of energy and pressure within their world.

Many of the Lithic are **ground-dwellers**, their bodies low-slung and broad, their multiple, **multi-jointed limbs** articulated with a series of crystalline pivots. These limbs end not in claws or pads, but in **micro-faceted feet** that grip the gritty patina of the plains, allowing for near-silent transit. Their gait is a delicate, rhythmic clicking, the sound of a thousand tiny gears meshing as they traverse the mineral dust. Their vision is likely adapted to the dim glow of the bioluminescent flora; their sensory organs, if they can be called that, might be clusters of **translucent photoreceptors** or **vibrational sensors** embedded directly into their carapaces, allowing them to perceive the subtle shifts in the ground's hum or the movement of prey through the thin air.

Then there are the **aereal** Lithic, though "aereal" is a generous term. These are the larger, more enigmatic beings that **drift through the sub-surface currents of heated gases**. Their forms are less defined, almost ethereal, perhaps akin to vast, buoyant sacks of condensed mineral vapour or intricate, porous aerogels. Their bodies might be semi-transparent, revealing faint, swirling patterns of **internal bioluminescence**, an echo of the ground-level flora they are perhaps indirectly connected to. They move with a slow, deliberate grace, their passage marked only by the subtle expansion and contraction of gaseous vents and the faintest whisper of displaced air within the subterranean arteries of Xylos. It's possible they are filter-feeders, drawing sustenance from trace elements and geothermal energy carried on these heated currents.

A third class, and perhaps the most profound, are the **Shapers**. These are the architects of Xylos's ancient urbanity. The Lithic do not *build* cities; they *are* the city, or at least the animating principle behind its permanent, carved existence. The black, ultra-dense stone that forms the geometric structures is not merely a building material; it is, in many cases, ossified Lithic, or rock that has been so deeply integrated with Lithic biology that the distinction has blurred.

The Shapers are likely sessile, or move with an imperceptibly slow, geological pace. Their consciousness exists not within discrete bodies, but within the monumental structures themselves. Imagine a vast, interconnected network of minds, each thought rippling through the bedrock, each memory etched into the polished facets of their silent plazas. They communicate not through sound, but through **resonant frequencies** that blend with the planetary hum, through subtle shifts in the internal luminescence of the stone, or perhaps through a form of **geological telepathy**. Their existence is one of profound contemplation, of witnessing the slow unfolding of time. They have transcended the need for individual locomotion, their entire civilization an extension of their collective will, a monument to their permanence.

The Lithic’s internal bioluminescence is not merely for illumination; it signals states of being, energy levels, even communication. The **gleaming obsidian** of their chitin might shift to **opalescent hues** or **deep, pulsing reds**, depending on their internal processes. Their internal organs, if they have them in a recognizable sense, are likely crystalline matrices that process geothermal energy and trace minerals, excreting only fine, inert dust.

There is no "society" as we understand it, no bustling markets or anxious debates. The Lithic live in a state of **deep equilibrium**, their lives dictated by the slow pulse of the planet. They are the silent custodians of Xylos, living embodiments of its ancient, enduring stillness. They are the echoes of a civilization that chose permanence over progress, stillness over motion, and in doing so, became as eternal as the very stone they inhabit.
