The inhabitants here are the **Gelidians**.

They are beings of the emergent depths, their very forms shaped by the pervasive, warm, and dense waters of their world. Imagine figures that are less solid, more semi-corporeal, adapted to a life of near-total immersion. Their skin, if it can be called that, is a smooth, translucent membrane, faintly bioluminescent, pulsing with a soft, internal glow that mirrors the flora they cultivate. This luminescence shifts in hue, a subtle language reflecting their mood or perhaps their communication, ranging from the soft blues and greens of their ambient environment to warmer, more intense yellows and oranges when agitated or excited.

Their bodies are lithe and elongated, adapted for graceful propulsion through the viscous waters. They possess multiple limbs, perhaps four or six, webbed and dexterous, designed for both navigating the fluid environment and for the intricate, manual labor of their bio-architecture. Their hands are delicate, featuring long, tapering fingers that can manipulate the soft silicates and gels of their emerging world with precision.

Eyes are large, adapted to the perpetual twilight, perhaps multifaceted like an insect’s, allowing them to capture every nuance of the dim light and the subtle shifts in bioluminescence from their surroundings. They might lack distinct noses or ears in the terrestrial sense, instead relying on sensory organs that detect pressure changes, vibrations within the water, and the chemical signatures of their environment.

The Gelidians are creatures of immense patience, embodying the slow dynamic of their world. They are not prone to haste, their lives measured in the gradual shaping of land and the slow cultivation of their living cities. Their understanding of time is geological, their concerns spanning centuries and millennia. They move with an unhurried grace, their actions deliberate and purposeful.

Their "urban" existence as described is not one of individual homes but of communal, bio-engineered living structures. The Gelidians likely live in close proximity, their lives intertwined with the very fabric of their settlements. They are the architects and cultivators of these organic marvels, tending to them like living gardens, coaxing them to grow by adding their own secretions, anchoring them to the shifting substrates, and weaving them into complex, interconnected colonies. Their "technology" is extensions of their biology, or rather, their biology is an extension of their technology. They don't *use* tools as much as they *are* tools, shaping their environment through sustained effort and a deep, instinctual understanding of biological processes.

Their diet is likely derived from the abundant, gelatinous flora, planktonic life suspended in the water, and perhaps symbiotic organisms they cultivate within their own bio-structures. They are likely filter-feeders to some extent, or perhaps they possess specialized digestive systems capable of processing the semi-molten geology and organic gels.

Socially, they are a communal species. The primitive tech and communal living suggest a society focused on shared effort and mutual reliance. They likely communicate through a combination of bioluminescent patterns, subtle sonic pulses within the water, and perhaps even direct, empathetic resonance due to their interconnected existence. Individuality might be less pronounced than a sense of belonging to the collective – the colony, the settlement, the emerging continent.

The "weirdness" of their world is reflected in the Gelidians themselves. They are not simply sentient beings adapted to a strange environment; they *are* the embodiment of that environment's peculiar processes. Their existence is a testament to life’s ability to flourish in the most unexpected conditions, to find form and purpose in the slow, deliberate act of becoming. They are the weavers of this liquid tapestry, the slow-moving artists of a world still in its primordial dawn.
