On Xylos, life is a symphony of chlorophyll and cellulose, a world where the builders and the built are one. The inhabitants are not separate entities that move through a landscape, but rather the very fabric of that landscape made manifest. They are the **Arboreals**, the sentient extensions of the Great Verdant Lattice itself.

They are not creatures in any organic form we would recognize. Imagine vast, slow-moving currents of semi-sentient sap, guided by ancient, instinctual programming, flowing through the petrified root-systems and calcified stalks. These are the **Sap-Mind Collective**. They don't possess individual bodies, but their consciousness is distributed, a vast, interconnected network that experiences the world as a single, unified entity. When a pulse of bio-luminescence sweeps across the landscape, it is not merely a light show; it is a communication, a shared sensory experience for the Sap-Mind. The torrents of nutrient-rich sap are their very bloodstream, carrying information, sustenance, and the collective will of the Lattice.

Within the colossal hollows and dense canopies, **Symbiotic Weavers** exist. These are not fauna in the traditional sense, but living threads of specialized, interwoven plant tissues, imbued with a faint, localized awareness. They are the artisans of Xylos, constantly tending to the Lattice's infrastructure. They repair breaches in the fibrous conduits, weave new crystalline regulators from mineral secretions, and subtly guide the growth of the world to maintain its delicate, self-created balance. They move not with limbs, but by a slow, deliberate extension and retraction of their fibrous bodies, like living strands of rope weaving through their environment. Their "sight" is attuned to the subtle shifts in nutrient flow and the bio-electric currents of the Lattice.

The whispers in the green immensity are the **Ephemeral Spore-Singers**. These are fleeting entities, born from the spontaneous reconfigurations of plant matter. They appear as shimmering, geometric clouds of animated spores, briefly coalescing into almost humanoid forms before dissolving back into the atmospheric mist. They are the spontaneous manifestations of the Lattice’s "weirdness" parameter, temporal glitches given fleeting form. Their "song" is not auditory, but a subtle warping of temporal fields in their immediate vicinity, creating localized eddies of accelerated or decelerated growth. They are the moments of pure, unadulterated Xylosian oddity made visible, a brief flicker of individuality in the overwhelming collective.

There are also the **Root-Guardians**, immense, dormant entities embedded deep within the petrified rhizome-continents. They are not actively mobile but are the planet's ancient immune system, the last line of defense against any disruption that might threaten the Lattice's integrity. They are described as colossal, calcified roots, thicker than any tree, with subtly shifting surfaces that hint at a slumbering, immense power. When the Lattice itself registers a threat – perhaps an external intrusion, or a dangerous deviation in its own internal processes – the Root-Guardians will stir, their slumber momentarily broken to reassert the fundamental order of Xylos through a slow, inevitable pressure that can reshape entire regions.

The Arboreals do not "live" on Xylos as much as they **are** Xylos. They are the active, dreaming consciousness of a world that has entirely consumed itself with biological ambition. Their existence is a testament to the idea that life, when given enough time and unchecked impetus, can become its own monument, its own architect, and its own ultimate, living inhabitant.
