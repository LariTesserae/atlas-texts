This is a realm where existence itself is a curated, intensely localized phenomenon. Those who inhabit it are not born of flesh and blood, but are beings of crystalline structure, energy filaments, and perhaps even patterns impressed upon the ambient radiation. They are the inhabitants of the "lush" flora.

These are the **Lithophytes** and the **Luminoids**.

The **Lithophytes** are the grounded dwellers. They manifest as intricate, slow-growing crystalline clusters that erupt from the mineral dust. Imagine geodes, but alive, their facets shifting with subtle internal light, pulsing like hearts of solidified luminescence. They are more akin to sentient geological formations than plants. Their "roots" delve not into soil, but into the subtle energy currents that permeate the ground, drawing sustenance from the warmth and the faint echoes of subatomic decay. They can take on forms that mimic the "established erosion" of the landscape – smoothed, rounded formations that nestle into the contours, or sharp, angular spires that pierce the dusty plains. When they "grow," it is a process of gradual accretion, adding new, vibrant layers to their existing structure, creating a tapestry of iridescent hues. They are the architects of the "built" environments, their forms coaxed and grown, rather than constructed. The "primitive tech" imbues them with an understanding of geometry that defies our linear perception, allowing them to shape their habitations with impossible angles and self-interweaving forms. They are intrinsically linked to the "warmth" of the planet, their very substance radiating a gentle heat.

The **Luminoids** are the ephemeral wanderers, the fleeting manifestations of this strange void. They are not solid in any conventional sense, but are more akin to contained packets of energy, flickers of condensed ambient radiation. They might appear as shimmering phantoms, trails of ionized gas, or motes of intensely focused light that dance across the mineral dust. Their existence is brief, often tied to fluctuations in the "light" and "dynamic" parameters. They manifest and dematerialize in the vast, echoing voids between the occupied pockets. They are the "rare fauna," and their interactions are more like transient ripples in the environment. One might glimpse a Luminoid coalescing from the dust, a brief spark of vibrant color, before it dissipates back into the pervasive light. They seem to communicate through shifts in their luminescence, through the subtle modulation of the omnipresent "sound." They are the embodiment of the "weirdness," seemingly unbound by conventional physical laws, their passage marked by a momentary distortion of the air, a faint hum that adds to the symphony.

Both Lithophytes and Luminoids are creatures of the "deafening sound." The Lithophytes appear to absorb and amplify this resonance, their crystalline structures vibrating in harmony with it, their internal light pulsing in sync with its rhythm. The Luminoids, on the other hand, seem to generate localized pockets of absolute silence as they pass, a temporary respite from the all-encompassing hum.

The "scale: hidden" and "density: empty" parameters mean that any encounter with these beings is an intensely personal, self-contained experience. The Lithophytes are the builders and tenders of these pocket-universes, creating the "urban" pockets of concentrated life and technology. The Luminoids are the fleeting visitors, the whispers of the void. To an observer, the Lithophytes might appear as squat, geometric sentinels, their surfaces alive with shifting patterns of light and color. The Luminoids, when they deign to appear, would be ghostlike apparitions, their forms indistinct but their presence undeniably felt through localized shifts in energy and light.

They are the inhabitants of a world that defies our understanding of life, thriving in a void where our most fundamental elements are absent, sustained by an energy that is both pervasive and deeply alien. They are the children of this dry, warm, and impossibly resonant existence.
