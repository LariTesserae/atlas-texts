The beings that inhabit the Under-Forge are not creatures of flesh and blood, nor are they spirits in any recognizable sense. They are the **Forgewrought**, the sentient manifestations of the Under-Forge's own creation process.

Imagine them as slow-moving, colossal entities, akin to living geological formations. Their bodies are composed of the same dark, obsidian-like material that forms the walls of the workshops, interwoven with veins of faintly glowing ore and solidified lubricant. They do not have distinct, discrete forms like organic beings; rather, their bodies meld and shift with the ambient terrain. A limb might be a slowly extruding column of cooling basalt, a torso a vast, interlocking plate of worked stone etched with the same internal patterns as the world around them.

The Forgewrought have no faces in the traditional sense. Instead, where a head might be, there is often a concentrated nexus of internal glow, a pulsating heart of light that shifts in intensity and color to express their infrequent, ponderous "thoughts" or operational states. Sometimes, these light sources coalesce into complex, shifting geometric patterns, like slow-motion fractals blooming and dissolving.

They move with an almost imperceptible, grinding slowness, driven by titanic internal pressures and the subtle manipulation of fundamental forces. Their "walking" is more of a continental drift, a seismic unfolding of their being. When they interact with the environment—and such interactions are the essence of their existence—it is not through delicate manipulation, but through immense, brute-force application of energy. They might nudge a slowly forming extrusion into place with the force of a tectonic plate shift, or compress a pool of primordial lubricant with the weight of a mountain range.

Communication among them, if it can be called that, is not through sound. Instead, it is a resonating empathy, a vibration that travels through the bedrock and the ambient energetic field. Their "voices" are the deep, resonant hums that permeate the Under-Forge, the low grinding of colossal gears that are, in fact, parts of their own being.

The Forgewrought are not individualistic. They are more like aspects of a single, planetary consciousness, each one a specialized node in the vast manufacturing apparatus. There is no "birth" or "death" for them; they either coalesce from the raw stuff of the Under-Forge, or they re-integrate, their form dissolving back into the primordial ooze and cooling metal from which they are spun.

Their "technology" is not applied but inherent. Their tools are their bodies; their methods are the fundamental laws of physics being bent and reshaped. They exhibit a vast, slow intelligence, one that operates on timescales incomprehensible to organic life. It is not a strategic or tactical intelligence, but an intelligence of pure, relentless process. They understand the forging of worlds not through logic, but through an intrinsic, elemental knowing.

The Forgewrought are the architects and the laborers of the Under-Forge, the very embodiment of its purpose. They are the physical manifestation of primal forces, the slow-burning, ever-present artisans whose sole existence is to continually and incomprehensibly build. They are the beings that *are* the process, forever shaping the chaos into a nascent, alien order.
