The inhabitants of this liquid cathedral are not merely swimmers, but **Submerged Dwellers**, beings who have adapted to and perhaps even engineered this unique existence. They are **Ceramic-Hearted**, the pressure of the deep having fused their internal structures into a dense, almost mineral composition. Their forms are elongated and graceful, designed to move with minimal disruption through the viscous medium. Imagine figures that are less flesh and blood and more sculpted alabaster, their skin smooth and subtly iridescent, catching the ambient light like polished pearl.

Their appendages are long and prehensile, tapering to delicate, multi-jointed digits that allow them to interact with the crystalline growths and the ancient machinery with exquisite precision. They have no lungs in the human sense; their exchange with the luminescent water is osmotic, drawing sustenance and energy directly from its dense composition. Their eyes, if they can be called that, are large, obsidian pools capable of discerning the faintest flickers of bioluminescence in the perpetual dimness, and perhaps even perceiving the subtle energetic pulses that still thrum through the city's veins.

They are **Guardians of the Deep Equilibrium**, their lives dictated by the extreme slowness of this world. They do not rush, for there is no need. Their movements are deliberate, almost meditative, each action a considered part of the patient enduring state. They might spend centuries tending to the intricate, crystalline gardens that sprout from their submerged monuments, or charting the slow drift of currents that have remained unchanged for millennia.

Communication among them is not through sound, but through **light-song**. They emit subtle, modulated pulses of their internal luminescence, creating complex patterns that dance through the water, conveying emotions, intentions, and shared histories. These light-songs are slow and rich, layered with frequencies that resonate with the very substance of their world.

They are not builders in the human sense, but **Weavers of the Luminescent Tapestry**. They encourage the growth of the engineered kelp, guiding its tendrils to stabilize the ancient structures, and cultivating specific strains of bioluminescent algae to maintain the subtle environmental gradients that sustain them. They are deeply attuned to the symbiotic relationship between the organic and the artifice, understanding that their existence relies on the continued slow operation of the submerged city.

The rare fauna they encounter are not viewed as prey or predators, but as **Transient Compositions**, ephemeral art pieces that drift through their meticulously maintained environment. They observe these creatures with a quiet reverence, acknowledging their unique beauty without seeking to interact or alter their existence.

Their purpose, though perhaps forgotten even by them in its original inception, is now tied to the **Preservation of the Sunken Memory**. They exist to maintain the delicate balance, to ensure that the ancient heart of the city continues its slow, deep pulse. They are the living embodiment of this submerged metropolis, carrying within their ceramic-hearted forms the silent echoes of a civilization that chose eternal submersion over oblivion. They are the patient, glowing custodians of a world that has achieved a profound and alien tranquility.
