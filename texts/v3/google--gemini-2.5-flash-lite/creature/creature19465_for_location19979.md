Inhabitants of this ceaseless ooze, this city of weeping clay and obsidian slickness, are beings of fluid form and resonant presence. They are not *made* in the way organic life is, nor are they *built* like the structures they inhabit. Rather, they are **excretions of the earth's nascent heart**, coalesced from the ambient warmth, the mineral grit, and the sheer, pulsing energy of this perpetually forming landscape.

They possess no fixed anatomy, no skeletal structure to define their boundaries. Imagine a shimmering disruption in the haze, a ripple in the slick surface of a perpetually wet path. That is often the first, and sometimes only, visual cue of their presence. Their bodies are a viscous, semi-translucent substance, a living ooze that can compress and expand, flow and coalesce. They are shades of deep, resonant umber, obsidian-black, and sometimes, when they absorb particular mineral deposits, streaks of opalescent, fractured light. There are no distinct limbs, no heads or torsos in the traditional sense. Instead, they employ **pseudopods of mutable density**, extending tendrils of glassy mud that grip and propel them across the slick surfaces. These pseudopods might elongate, flatten, or harden for a moment to serve as levers, before melting back into the amorphous bulk of the being.

Their existence is utterly steeped in the pervasive moisture. Their very essence, it seems, is **partially water, partially dissolved mineral, partially something else entirely**. They glisten perpetually, not with sweat, but with a healthy, internal secretion that allows them to slide through the channels with an unnerving, liquid grace. It is this constant lubrication that enables their effortless movement, their ability to navigate the labyrinthine, ever-shifting cityscape. They don't walk, they don't crawl; they **undulate, they bloom, they flow**.

Communication is a symphony of vibrations and resonances. Their "conversations" are not spoken words, but a complex interplay of **clicks, whistles, and deep, thrumming hums**. These sounds are not emitted from a specific orifice, but seem to emanate from their entire being, a palpable vibration that ripples through the glassy structures and the weeping earth. The chittering is the rapid-fire exchange of information, the clicks are pointed inquiries or pronouncements, and the resonant hums are the communal consciousness, the shared understanding of their environment and their collective existence. Their soundscape, so deafening to outsiders, is their language, their very lifeblood.

Their interaction with the environment is one of **symbiotic absorption and expression**. They don't consume food in the way we understand it. Instead, they seem to draw sustenance directly from the mineral-rich mud and the ambient heat. They might press themselves against a particular vein of raw glass, their bodies shimmering as they absorb its essence, their own forms subtly shifting in color and texture as a result. They are not separate from the city; they are **extrusions of its very being**, molded by its processes, and in turn, contributing to its constant, subtle transformation.

The "luminous nodules" are not tools they wield, but perhaps **integrated bioluminescent organs or captured pockets of primal energy** that they cultivate within themselves. These soft, milky glows are not for illumination in the way we understand it, but serve as markers of presence, perhaps as signals to each other in their perpetually dim world, or as a means of focusing their internal energies. They are not "technology" as we would define it, but rather incredibly refined forms of **organic mineral manipulation**, born from their deep connection to this unique environment.

Their numbers are not counted, but **felt**. They are a constant shimmering in the periphery, a dark shape gliding beneath the slick crust of the clay, a pulse of light blooming briefly in a shadowed archway. They are the living threads interwoven with the mineral fabric of this city, a constant, resonant hum of existence in a world that refuses to hold still. They are the creatures of the perpetual becoming, thriving in a place where change is the only constant, and stillness is an alien concept.
