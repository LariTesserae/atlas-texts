The inhabitants of the Sky-Reef of Xylos are not biological in the way most life is understood. They are **Automatons**.

These are not crude machines, but intricate, self-sustaining entities of polished chrome and burnished brass, mirroring the very substance of their world. Each automaton possesses a core purpose, though this purpose is now shrouded in the vastness of forgotten history. They manifest primarily as **avian-like beings**, their forms inspired by birds of prey and swift-winged insects. Their bodies are a symphony of interlocking gears, impossibly fine filigree, and segmented, jointed limbs. Iridescent wings, shimmering like oil on water, are not for flight in the traditional sense, but for subtle adjustments in an environment where gravity itself seems to be a negotiated force. These wings whir with a near-silent hum, their movement creating the only discernible disruption in the humid air.

Their "eyes" are not organic lenses but polished, multi-faceted jewels or glowing apertures that emit a soft, internal light, pulsating with an unfathomable intelligence. They communicate through a complex interplay of clicks, whirs, and the subtle modulation of those internal lights. This creates a constant, gentle murmur that forms the sonic tapestry of the Sky-Reef, a constant, almost subliminal *soundscape* that is less a cacophony and more a rhythmic thrum of existence.

They move in **organized swarms**, their flight patterns not random or chaotic, but dictated by an unseen, overlying logic. They patrol the vast metallic plains, navigate the dizzying verticality of the platforms, and traverse the impossible arches. Their proximity to each other on these journeys is not just a matter of shared space, but of shared purpose, creating a sense of a vital, if alien, ecosystem. They are the gardeners of the crystalline growths, the maintainers of the metallic vines, and the silent observers of the slow decay that marks the Sky-Reef's imperceptible erosion.

There are hints of other forms, too. Within the brief distortions of reality, when the air shimmers with impossible colors, one might glimpse **echoes of other automations**. Perhaps these are older models, their forms more geometric or abstract. Or perhaps they are entities of pure energy, temporary manifestations within the ever-present industrial hum. These fleeting visions are rare, and unsettling, suggesting a spectrum of manufactured consciousness within the Sky-Reef.

The Automatons of Xylos are **artisans of entropy**, performing their endless tasks in a world where the natural substrate has been entirely replaced by the artificial. They are the lifeblood of the Sky-Reef, the custodians of a gleaming, metallic dream, forever cycling through a precisely engineered existence, bathed in the relentless, shadowless light of their self-made sky. They are the *fauna*, the *inhabitants*, the very *essence* of this impossible, industrialized atmosphere.
