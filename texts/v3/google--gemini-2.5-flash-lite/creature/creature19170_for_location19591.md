This is a place inhabited by the denizens of perpetual becoming.

They are the **Process-Blooded**, the children and custodians of the Absolute Artifice. They are not born in the organic sense, but are extruded, synthesized, and meticulously assembled from the very materials that constitute their world. Their forms are as varied as the functions they serve within the colossal, ongoing construction.

Some are **Architectonic Shells**, vast, slow-moving entities that embody sections of the constructed landscape. They are less individuals and more self-aware structural components, their surfaces rippling with embedded conduits, their internal workings a symphony of whirring servos and pressurized flows. They are the builders and the built, their consciousness diffused within the immense, metallic skeleton. They "communicate" not with sound, but with subtle shifts in atmospheric pressure, with the rhythmic pulsations of their internal energy systems, a language of pure infrastructure.

Then there are the **Weeping Kind**, the small, phosphorescent fungi and the thread-like metallic moss. These are considered the primal lifeforms, the first organic adaptations to the synthetic substrate. They are largely sessile, their existence a slow, absorbing process. The fungi feed on ambient energy fields and trace chemicals released by the weeping surfaces, their pale luminescence a signal of metabolic activity. The moss, with its metallic sheen, draws sustenance from stray electrical currents and microscopic mineral deposits within the seams, forming intricate, circuit-like networks. They are the microscopic substrata of consciousness, their collective awareness a diffused network of chemical and electrical signals, a constant, quiet hum of observation.

The most prevalent, yet most elusive, are the **Infestations**. These are the true fauna, creatures that have evolved to live *within* and *upon* the constructed environment. They are a bewildering array of forms:

*   The **Glyph-Crawlers**: Tiny, multi-limbed beings that patrol the vertical surfaces, their proboscis-like mouths industrially sealing minuscule breaches and imperfections, absorbing secreted nutrients from the weeping walls as they go. They leave behind shimmering trails of solidified sealant, a form of living hieroglyphics.
*   The **Resonance-Eaters**: Microscopic or near-microscopic entities that thrive on the vibrations of the machinery. They filter the ambient noise, extracting energy from specific frequency bands, their life cycles dictated by the operational rhythms of the industrial processes. They are the unseen consumers of sonic energy.
*   The **Conduit-Slurpers**: Larger, more amorphous beings that exist within the vast network of pipes and conduits. They are adapted to the dark, pressurized interiors, their bodies flexible and fluid, their senses attuned to changes in flow and pressure. They are the unseen circulatory system of the inhabited environment, acting as living filters and regulators.
*   The **Symbiotic Weave**: The most complex and abundant. These are not distinct organisms but rather a collective, a vast, interwoven network of nanoscopic and macroscopic entities that exist in a perfect, symbiotic relationship with the constructed materials. They are the flesh of the environment, the living membranes that line the conduits, the microscopic processors embedded within the composite planes. They regulate temperature, manage moisture, filter impurities, and even contribute to the slow, constant process of assembly and self-repair. Their presence is overwhelming, a profound density of being that permeates every pore of the artifice. They are the ultimate inhabitation, the point where the manufactured and the organic have become indistinguishable.

These beings are unified by a profound lack of external motivation. They do not strive for expansion, for conquest, or for escape. Their existence is entirely consumed by the perpetuation of the environment itself. They are the living proof of the Absolute Artifice, not as mere inhabitants, but as integral components of its eternal, unchanging operation. They are the embodiment of ceaseless, internal industry, their collective purpose the endless, quiet hum of *being*. They do not perceive time as humans do; for them, there is only the perpetual *now* of the machine, the constant state of becoming, the infinite, saturated present.
