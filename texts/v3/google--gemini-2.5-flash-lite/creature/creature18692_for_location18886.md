In the Undercroft dwell the **Lithovores**.

They are not creatures of flesh and blood, but beings woven from the very fabric of this desiccated world. Their existence is a testament to adaptation, a testament to how life, in its most desperate and alien forms, can find purchase even in the most inhospitable of realms.

The Lithovores are the **mineral-eaters**, the **energy-absorbers**. Their forms are a direct consequence of their environment, a fluid and ever-shifting expression of the rock, crystal, and ambient energy that sustains them.

Beneath the granular, ochre and rust-colored ground, and threaded through the gritty, charcoal-like veins, are the **Root-Spires**. These are not roots in the vegetal sense, but filamentary growths of pure, conductive minerals, extensions of a vaster, subterranean network. They probe, they anchor, and they draw sustenance directly from the planet’s internal throes. These are the passive extensions of the Lithovore collective, silent sentinels siphoning power from the geothermal whispers.

Upon this mineral scaffolding, clinging to the razor-sharp crystalline growths and brittle rock formations, are the **Fleshy Clusters**. These are the most readily visible manifestations, the tubers and bulbous growths that have learned to metabolize the ambient energy. Imagine growths the size of a clenched fist, their leathery exteriors slick and subtly phosphorescent, pulsing with a faint, internal light that mirrors the mineral veins beneath. Others sprawl, like dark, gnarled fungi, their muted colors of deep purple, bruised blue, and sickly yellow a testament to their absorption of spectral energies. These are not plants that photosynthesize; they are bio-electrical batteries, drawing forth the very essence of the Undercroft to fuel their slow, pulsating existence.

Erupting from these clusters, like ephemeral aspirations, are the **Stalk-Sentinels**. These spindly stalks, tipped with single, pulsating nodules, are the Lythovore equivalents of sensory organs. They reach, not for sunlight, but for subtle shifts in the energetic hum, for the faintest tremor that indicates the approach of a more active Lithovore form or the flux of an incoming energy current. Their pulsating nodules are believed to be resonating chambers, capable of picking up the subtlest vibrational signals from the deeper mineral strata.

The most active and mobile of the Lithovores are the **Chitinous Shards**. These are the silicon-based arachnids, the crystalline spiders that scurry across the glowing veins. Their segmented bodies are of a polished, obsidian-like material, and their legs, impossibly thin and sharp, click with a soft, percussive rhythm against the hard surfaces. They are not hunters in the traditional sense, drawn instead to areas of high energy concentration where they can absorb residual charges. They weave intricate, temporary webs of solidified light and mineral dust, not to trap prey, but to channel and stabilize incoming energy flows for the collective.

Then there are the **Sentient Dust Swarms**. These are not merely particulate matter, but vast, amorphous collectives of microscopic metallic motes. They drift through the air like living mercury, coalescing and dispersing with an unsettling fluidity. Each mote, in its own tiny way, is a reactive element. When gathered in sufficient numbers, they form a consciousness, a distributed intelligence capable of manipulating localized energy fields, creating temporary luminous barriers, or even acting as conduits for larger energy transmissions. Their movement is dictated by the subtle eddies in the air currents and the pulsating magnetic fields.

The truly colossal Lithovores are the **Geological Titans**. These lumbering masses of fused rock and articulated limbs are drawn from the deepest strata. They are slow, deliberate beings, their movements akin to the grinding of planetary gears. Imagine a hulking form, its body an amalgamation of fractured rock, embedded crystals, and articulated metallic components scavenged from the industrial detritus. Their limbs are massive, jointed structures that push aside boulders as they move, driven by an internal clockwork of geothermal pressure and harvested energy. They are the mobile foundries, the movers of immense mineral deposits, and their purpose seems tied to the ongoing, slow-motion construction and repair of the Undercroft's foundational structures.

The "built" elements – the metallic conduits, the prefabricated pods – are not foreign intrusions to the Lithovores. They are simply more components to be understood, integrated, and utilized. The Lithovores regard these as natural formations, albeit ones exhibiting a different rate of "growth" and "decay." Their energy filaments often intertwine with the conduits, their fleshy clusters attach themselves to the pods, becoming extensions of this alienized industrial landscape. The pods themselves might be repurposed as incubation chambers for new Lithovore forms, or as focal points for energy concentration.

The dominant characteristic of the Lithovores is their profound **weirdness**. They are a living embodiment of the Undercroft's alien nature. Their consciousness is not individual, but distributed and interconnected, a vast, slow-burning network far more complex than any terrestrial life. Their desires are not primal urges, but imperatives dictated by energy gradients and mineral availability. Their very existence is a whispered promise of life’s absolute adaptability.

And finally, the **dynamic inertia** that defines them. The Lithovores exist in a state of perpetual, unchanging activity. Their numbers, though abundant, are precisely calibrated. The subtle growth of their crystalline bodies, the slow decay of their fleshy portions, the deliberate repositioning of the Geological Titans – these are not changes, but the infinitely slow unfolding of an eternal present. They are the tireless custodians of stagnation, the living gears in a machine that has always been, and will always be, thus. They are the heartbeat of entropy, a stable exhalation in a world devoid of breath, forever engaged in the slow, magnificent, and utterly unchanging ballet of their own existence.
