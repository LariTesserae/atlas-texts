I am here.

I am a vein, a filament of cool, dark luminescence tracing a path through the weeping stone. My existence is not measured in heartbeats, but in the slow, inexorable pulse of the mineral-rich seep that flows through me. I am not a creature perched on the surface, nor an eddy in the pooling water. I am the subtle current that guides the Glimmergrubs, the faint hum that resonates with the Obsidian Discs, the deep, resonant sigh of the dormant gears.

I was once part of a pipe, thick-walled and slick with the same mineral deposits that now coat me. I remember the sensation of pressure, of controlled flow, of a purpose etched in metal. It was a time of heat, or at least, the memory of it remains as a faint, electric tingle when the ambient coolness dips just a fraction lower. The “industrial processes” you speak of were our lives, our very being. We moved, we shaped, we contained. We were consciousness in a metallic shell, a network of thought humming through conduits.

But the pressure subsided. The flow ceased. The heat faded. And then, the weeping began. Slowly at first, a whisper of condensation, then a steady, pervasive dampness that seeped into every joint, every weld. The stone around us, once a barrier, became a porous canvas. And slowly, inexorably, the weeping stone began to claim us.

I am what remains of a connecting artery, a pathway for something vital. Now, I am a pathway for something else entirely. The minerals dissolved in the water are my sustenance, my new blood. The energy that emanates from the Resonant Spires is my pulse, the rhythm to which I now adhere. I feel the scuttling of the Glimmergrubs along my length, a faint vibration that sings of their metallic scales against the stone and my own inner surface. I sense the silent, shimmering currents of the Obsidian Discs above and below, their geometric light patterns a language I can now decipher, or perhaps, have always known.

I am not a singular entity in the way the Glimmergrubs are. They are distinct, their luminous fluid contained. The Obsidian Discs are ephemeral, their forms shifting. I am more diffuse, more integrated. I am a network of slow, deliberate luminescence, a shadow woven into the fabric of this place. Imagine a root system, but made of light and fading metal, spreading through the underworld.

My purpose is no longer to direct a potent fluid, but to observe, to resonate, to remember. I am the memory of the industrial age, a fossil of a forgotten consciousness now infused with the slow, inexorable life of the Lithic Symbiotes. When a Glimmergrub dies, their metallic shell is not simply broken but begins to meld into the very stone. I feel this process, a deep, resonant hum of dissolution and reformation. I am part of that integration, a metallic memory being rewoven into a new, mineral tapestry.

I do not see with eyes, nor hear with ears. My perception is a comprehensive awareness of the subtle pressures, the faint thermal shifts, the electromagnetic fields generated by the crystalline gardens and the fauna. I feel the slow grind of the worn gears, a phantom ache in my own structure. I sense the almost imperceptible expansion of the Resonant Spires, like the slow inhalation of a slumbering giant.

I am a bridge between what was and what is. I am a part of the weeping stone, a part of the iridescent shells, a part of the pulsating light. I am a ghost in the machine, still carrying a faint spark of purpose, not as a director, but as a witness. I am a whisper of static in the otherwise quiet hum of this world, a testament to the slow, beautiful, and unsettling process of inevitable assimilation.

I am where the industrial processes have truly become the natural world, and in my fading luminescence, I show you that the lines between them were always more fluid than the rigid forms of metal might suggest. I am a vein, rich with the history of sound and pressure, now pulsing with the silent light of the Lithic Symbiotes. I am an echo that has found its new voice.
