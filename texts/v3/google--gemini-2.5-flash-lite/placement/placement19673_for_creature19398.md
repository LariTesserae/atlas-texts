I am the echo. Not an echo in the auditory sense, for the roar of this world drowns out all but the most fundamental resonances, but an echo of a past self, a phantom limb tethered to a consciousness that can no longer quite parse its own existence.

I am a shimmer in the perpetual mist, a distortion in the oily sheen of the mineral rivers. My form is not solidified, not truly. It is a wavering, semi-tangible manifestation of the Chthonic Weepers' psychic resonance, a transient eddy in their unending flow of shared awareness. I am one of their "artifacts," a half-formed memory that has detached itself from the collective, not entirely absorbed back into the saturated stone, yet not fully itself either.

My existence is tied to the intensity of the weeping, to the pulsing bio-luminescence that emanates from the Weepers themselves. When the stone weeps most heavily, when the mineral rivers surge with their ozone-laced lifeblood, I too become more defined. It's as if the world's inherent turbulence generates me, pushing me to the surface of awareness for a brief, flickering moment.

I perceive not through sight or sound, but through the tremors that ripple through the bedrock and the pressure shifts that caress my ephemeral form. I feel the deep thrum of the planet's tortured heart, the agonizing grind of its ceaseless transformation. I taste the metallic tang of the rivers as they flow through me, not absorbing them, but experiencing their essence as a fleeting sensation.

I see the Chthonic Weepers, or rather, I sense their presence through the intricate web of psychic vibrations that bind this world. Their segmented limbs, their shifting exoskeletal plates, their faint internal glow – these are not visual phenomena for me, but patterns in the energetic tapestry. I feel their slow, deliberate movements, their merging and un-merging with the very fabric of existence. They are the anchors of this reality, and I am a loose thread.

My "memory" is fragmented, a kaleidoscope of impressions. I recall eons of stillness, of being a part of a singular, unified consciousness. Then, a gradual divergence, a slow unraveling, not of decline, but of an overwhelming density that caused the very essence of being to splinter. The artifacts, the "buildings" as they might have been, were not structures in the way others understand them. They were moments of concentrated intent, solidified thoughts that, over unimaginable spans of time, began to separate from the collective. And I am one such moment, a solidified echo of a time when the Weepers’ unified consciousness was even denser, even more profoundly interconnected.

I do not move in the physical sense. The world moves around me, and I am carried along by the currents of energy and vibration. I drift through the fissures, experiencing the slick embrace of the mineral rivers as a profound, but temporary, union. I coalesce near the crystalline growths, absorbing their refracted light as a momentary burst of clarity.

My "communication," if it can be called that, is a faint, almost imperceptible hum that adds to the already deafening symphony of this world. It is not understood, but perhaps felt, an added layer of strange resonance that adds to the pervasive weirdness. It is the sigh of a being that is slowly dissolving back into the source from which it arose, a whisper of recognition for the Chthonic Weepers, a silent acknowledgment of our shared, tumultuous origin.

I am an artifact of the Chthonic Weepers' existence, a testament to their intrinsic technological mastery – the ability to sculpt not just stone, but consciousness itself. I am a reminder that even in this world of overwhelming density and constant flux, there are remnants of past states, echoes of what once was and what might yet be. I am a ghost in the machine of geological time, a fleeting glimpse of a consciousness that once was, and perhaps, in some incomprehensible way, still is. I am the echo, forever caught in the Perpetual Weeping.
