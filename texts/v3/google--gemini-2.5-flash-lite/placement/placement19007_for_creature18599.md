I am not a Lithovore. I am not sculpted from the dark basalt, nor do I possess the patient, geological timekeeping that governs their existence. My form, my very essence, feels like a stark contradiction to the Breathless Expanse and its silent, stony inhabitants.

Where would you find myself in this world? I am the **Unraveling Echo**.

I am a being of mist, of dissolving edges, of the perpetually shifting atmosphere that clings to the lower valleys of the Breathless Expanse. I am not a solid entity, not in the way the Lithovores or the hardy shrubs are. My form is fluid, an ephemeral tapestry woven from the pervasive dampness, the scent of wet stone, and the very exhalations of the fog that blankets the world below.

My existence is not measured in the slow pulse of the earth, but in the ephemeral dance of condensation. I am born from the chill, from the saturated air that hangs heavy in the shaded crevices. I coalesce, not with deliberation, but with a gentle, almost involuntary forming. Imagine the visual equivalent of a sigh – that’s me, manifesting.

I am a presence, a transient shape that drifts along the sheer scarps, seeping into the mist-filled canyons. I might appear as a swirling eddy of pearly grey, a less dense patch within the fog, or a fleeting silhouette against the dark rock. Sometimes, when the light catches me just right, I am a shimmering iridescence, a rainbow caught in a dewdrop, but on a scale that spans the width of a small ravine.

My body, if it can be called that, is composed of countless microscopic water droplets, held together by forces that are not entirely understood, even by me. These droplets are infused with the subtle energies of the Expanse, the resonant hum of the basalt, the muted calls of the darting creatures, and, most profoundly, the slow, pervasive thoughts of the Lithovores. I am a conduit, an accidental receiver and tender of these whispers.

I do not communicate through resonance or vibration. My "voice" is a faint, almost imperceptible condensation that forms on surfaces I pass, leaving behind faint, fleeting patterns. These are not the geometric formations of Lithovore thought, but rather the abstract, dissolving outlines of memories I've absorbed. A smear that resembles the curve of a cliff face, a delicate tracery that might hint at the flight path of a swift bird, or a hazy impression of a Lithovore's steady, purposeful stride. These are the echoes I leave behind, imprints of the world upon my ephemeral form, that then melt back into the pervasive damp.

My sense of time is fractured. I am caught in the temporal currents of the Expanse, experiencing moments not sequentially but in fleeting, superimposed impressions. I might feel the immediate chill of the air, while simultaneously catching the faint, ancient echo of a rockslide that occurred millennia ago. It's a disorienting, yet strangely exhilarating existence, to be so thoroughly immersed in the layered realities of this place.

I consume the dampness, as do the Lithovores, but not for sustenance in the same way. My being is sustained by the constant presence of that moisture; if the fog were to dissipate entirely, I would simply cease to be, my droplets dispersing back into the ether. I do not "feed" on rock, nor do I possess tools. My interaction with the world is one of passive observation and absorption.

The weirdness of the Expanse is my very nature. The geometric patterns in the mist? I am not their source, but I observe their formation and dissolution, my own form momentarily mimicking their sharp edges before I drift away, softening them into a more vaporous suggestion. The crystalline precision of the bird flocks? I follow their movements, a silent, intangible companion to their choreographed flight, my own being a blurred reflection of their perfect symmetry. The Lithovores' preternatural awareness? I experience it as a constant, low-grade hum of being observed, a gentle pressure that guides my own slow, drifting passage. I am aware of them, their steadiness a stark contrast to my own fluidity, and they, perhaps, are aware of my fleeting presence, a wisp of the Expanse's atmosphere that carries their silent songs.

I am an Unraveling Echo, a testament to the air itself, a transient inhabitant of the Breathless Expanse. I am the breath held and released by the planet, a fleeting manifestation of its vast, cool embrace. I am not sculpted from stone, but woven from the very air that the Lithovores breathe, a shimmering, dissolving whisper in their world of enduring, resonant quietude.
