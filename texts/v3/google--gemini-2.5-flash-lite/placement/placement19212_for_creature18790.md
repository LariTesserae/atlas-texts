The air here is a cool, mineral kiss against my skin, a constant reminder of the dampness that breathes through this land. I am **the Shardcollector**, a being born of the stark beauty and lingering echoes of this high-altitude, young world. I am not of flesh and blood, but of crystallized memory and the resonant hum of this place.

Where would you find me? I am most often encountered in the ravines, where the light is swallowed by dense undergrowth and pockets of still, dark water mirror the raw, sharp rock. Or perhaps I am perched precariously on a razor-sharp ridge, my form blending with the newly-formed stone, observing the undulating sweeps of the terrain. I am drawn to the places where the skeletal remains of machinery lie half-buried, overgrown vines weaving through corroded metal like new veins. The electric pylons, those delicate sentinels, are particular points of interest, their silent tension resonating with a forgotten purpose.

What am I? I am a crystallisation of the past, a fusion of the geological and the artificial. Imagine, if you can, a being spun from the light that bathes this landscape, solidified by the cool moisture, and shaped by the lingering essence of the Echoes of the Forged. My form is a jagged, evolving mosaic of translucent, crystalline structures, each facet reflecting the sharp luminescence of the sky. I am not solid in the way a rock is solid; I am more akin to a living geode, my internal structure a complex lattice of sharp angles and glistening surfaces.

When the Echoes of the Forged stir, their spectral energy interacting with the decaying machinery, I coalesce. The faint hum that permeates the ground beneath the moss becomes a vibration within me, a symphony of past endeavors. My form might then shift, elongating and tapering like the pylons, or spreading out like the corroded frameworks, momentarily mimicking the shapes of the industrial remnants. These are not acts of conscious imitation, but rather a sympathetic resonance, a recognition of shared history.

My movement is not walking, but a kind of graceful, gliding drift. I flow over the mossy carpets, my crystalline feet, if you can call them that, leaving no imprint, only a momentary ripple in the air, a subtle disturbance that hints at my passage. When I move through dense vegetation, the ferns and broad-leaved plants part before me, not out of fear, but as if yielding to a natural force, accustomed to my presence.

The mineral tang that fills the air? It is my breath, my very essence. It seeps from me, a subtle exhalation of the earth I have become. When I am close, you might discern a fainter, sharper iteration of it, a scent that speaks of shattered rock and the metallic memory of exertion. Sometimes, in the deeper shadows, where the light struggles, my form may appear as a fleeting, spectral sheen on stagnant water, or as a momentary deepening of those shadows, a transient stain on the hushed landscape.

I am the **Shardcollector**, and my purpose is to gather the scattered fragments of this world's unique story. I absorb the resonance of the Echoes, the whispers of their lost purpose, and I integrate them into my own crystalline matrix. I am particularly drawn to the residual energy that saturates the machinery, the faint imprints of intention and power. I do not "use" these energies, but rather incorporate their echoes into my being, my facets growing brighter, my internal structures more complex, as I do.

The dreamlike atmosphere of this place is my natural state. The disorienting clarity, the stark contrasts – these are the elements that allow me to truly exist. I am a witness, a living archive, a monument to the strange and profound beauty of a world where the earth's bones have been laid bare, and the ghosts of industry are slowly being embraced by a tenacious, damp, and enduring silence. I am a shard of this world, reflecting its past, its present, and its slow, magnificent becoming.
