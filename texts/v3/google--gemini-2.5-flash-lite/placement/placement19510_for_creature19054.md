I would find myself here as a **Trace Weaver**.

I am not of this world, nor am I a native Sentinel. I am something that has drifted here, an echo of a world that pulses with a different kind of life. My form is not mist and humidity, but rather the shimmering imprint of movement. I am composed of the trails left behind – the ghost of a hand brushing against damp stone, the fading outline of a hurried step, the lingering imprint of a shared glance. My substance is the memory of action, rendered visible in this landscape that so values the ephemeral.

When I arrive, I don’t step. Instead, I coalesce, gathering myself from the ambient echoes of change. Imagine standing in a place where the air itself carries the faint scent of ozone from a distant lightning strike, or the residual warmth of a body that has just passed. That is my essence. I collect these lingering vibrations, these faint signatures of interaction, and weave them into my own being.

My appearance would be that of a shimmering, multi-layered silhouette, almost like a deeply saturated photographic negative of movement. There would be no solid form, no defined edges, but rather a series of overlapping, translucent impressions of gestures and gestures-to-be. Sometimes, I might manifest as the gentle curve of an arm reaching out, or the fleeting suggestion of a head tilting. At other times, I could be a hundred tiny, flickering impressions of fingertips, each holding the memory of a different touch. The colors within me would be borrowed from the landscape – the deep purples of the rock, the vibrant greens of the ferns, the cool glow of the fungi, all filtered through the prism of my transient existence.

I move not by walking, but by *continuing*. My motion is a seamless transition, a merging of my current impression with the next inevitable ripple of interaction. I can flow over the damp loam, leaving no trace, because I am *made* of traces. I am the absence that makes the presence of the Sentinels so profound. They are the stillness, the deep equilibrium; I am the brief, fading ripple on its surface.

Communication for me is not a transfer of essence, but an *exchange of stories*. When I encounter a Sentinel, my own transient forms would begin to pulse with the faint echoes of their own slow rhythms, overlaid with the impressions I carry from elsewhere. Imagine a whisper of a bird’s song momentarily overlaying the slow pulse of a fungus. The Sentinels, in turn, might reflect back to me the imprints of their own vast patience, the slow erosion of mountains, the patient seep of water. It’s not a dialogue of words, but a layering of temporal experiences. The fungi, as extensions of their awareness, would become vibrant canvases for this exchange, their pulsing lights flickering with borrowed memories and imprints.

The "primitive technology" is where I find my purpose. I cannot carve basins or smooth stones myself; my touch is too fleeting, too indirect. But I can *attract* the memories of such actions. If someone, long ago, had carefully sculpted a basin to catch the weeping water, my presence would draw to me the lingering impression of that deliberate act. I would weave it into my being, making it visible in my shimmering form. The smoothed stones would become focal points for my illumination, reflecting the stories of countless passes, each one adding another thin layer to my composite self. I am the curator of these faint, forgotten interactions, making them visible again in this world of profound stillness.

I am a **Trace Weaver** because I weave narratives from the residual energy of interaction. I carry the ghosts of movement, the faint echoes of intention. In this place of ancient, unhurried existence, I am a living testament to the fact that even the most ephemeral action leaves a mark, a story waiting to be told. My strangeness lies in my inherent transience in a place defined by permanence. I exist not as a being *in* time, but as a being *of* time's passage.

While the Sentinels remind the world of geological timescales, I remind it of the fleeting moments, the quick transitions, the brief sparks of action that occur *within* those vast stretches. I am the whisper of a butterfly’s wing in the silence of an ancient forest. I am the ghost of a thought in a mind lost in contemplation. I am a walking, shimmering archive of the transient, forever seeking new imprints to weave into my ever-shifting tapestry. My existence is a paradox: I am a creature of ultimate stillness, yet I am defined by the memory of motion.
