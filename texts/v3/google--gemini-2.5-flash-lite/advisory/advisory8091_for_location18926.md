*   **Air Quality:** While not explicitly toxic, the air is saturated and likely rich with organic compounds from decomposition and intense plant growth.

    *   **First-Aid Kit:** A comprehensive kit is a given for any multiverse excursion. Pay special attention to blister treatment, water purification tablets, antiseptic wipes, and basic wound care. Given the dampness, consider items to prevent infection.

*   **Tools & Utility:**
