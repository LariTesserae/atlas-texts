Deep beneath the surface of a vast ocean, a strange underwater city lies, packed dense with towering structures and a web of infrastructure woven between. The waters here are strangely warm, almost tropical, and swirl slowly in gradual currents and eddies. 

The city itself is a marvel of industrial engineering, with factories and generators humming quietly in the dim depths. Lights flicker and glow from windows and along walkways, powered by some unknown subaquatic energy source. Pipes and conduits snake through the water, connecting the various districts in an intricate network.

Despite the density of urban development, lush vegetation grows in every nook and crevice. Strange kelp-like plants with luminescent fronds and flowers sway gently. Coral-esque growths in vivid colors encrust the foundations of buildings. Soft mosses and spongy mats carpet the sea floor between structures. The plants have adapted to thrive in this environment.

Marine life is sparse but what creatures do live here are bizarre - ghostly fish with glowing patterns, floating jellies with trailing tendrils dozens of feet long, giant tubeworms clinging to pipes. They have evolved into strange forms to match this alien metropolis.

The whole city has a sense of great age, like it has existed in this state for centuries. Walls are worn smooth, edges rounded, colors muted. Sediment and mineral deposits have built up. New growth has merged with old. The place feels settled into an ancient balance, a dreamlike eternal twilight. 

Time passes slowly here in long, patient phases. Water currents gradually shift. Bioluminescent lights pulse and phase. The low thrum of engines marks long cycles. Change happens on a geologic timescale. This is a place out of time, where past, present and future blur together into one long, strange, submarine dream.
