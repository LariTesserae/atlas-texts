Wind-scoured peaks thrust into a gray sky heavy with mist, the exposed bones of the world reaching impossibly high into thin, frigid air. The mountainsides are vast planes of cracked, pitted stone, only the barest smattering of lichen and twisted, skeletal shrubs clinging to the rock. Moisture beads on the cold surfaces, running in rivulets down crevices to pool in shadowed hollows before freezing again.

The air shimmers strangely, perspectives bending at unnatural angles as if space itself is stretched thin at this altitude. Distances warp and the horizon wavers, peaks and mist bleeding together in a dizzying haze.

Sudden cracks boom across the slopes like cannon fire, the very stone groaning as forces beyond physics strain and pull at the fundament of reality. Boulders shatter in slow motion, shards drifting on the air before snapping back together in impossible configurations - jagged arches and free-floating spires defying gravity and sense.

Ethereal shapes flicker at the corners of vision, suggestions of structures rising from the mist - a parapet here, a jutting beam there - only to sublimate back into the fog leaving afterimages burned on the eye. Incomprehensible sounds assault the ears, keening wails and rumbling vibrations felt more than heard, the voice of these forsaken peaks.

Life is the barest footnote in this place. The rare flutter of a raggedy raven's wings or skitter of a emaciated rodent in the rubble the only sign this is still a world bound by biology. 

The wind rises to a shriek, tearing at the senses. The peaks shudder and a massive rift splits the mountainside, the very warp and weft of space yawning wide to expose the howling non-void beyond the skin of the world.

This is a place where reality is worn to a tattered shroud, a place abandoned by the rational laws of existence. A place to witness the raw chaos lurking beyond the veil and know the true fragility of the sane world far below.
