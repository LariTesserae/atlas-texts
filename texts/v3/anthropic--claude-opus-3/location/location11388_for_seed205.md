In this vast, plant-dominated world, the landscape stretches endlessly in all directions - a sea of lush, towering vegetation blanketing everything, from low-lying basins to soaring mountain ranges that pierce the shimmering heat haze. The air is thick and humid, heavy with the scents of foliage, sap, and decomposition.

Gargantuan trees, their trunks and boughs swollen to impossible proportions, form the bones of this place, supporting a riotous profusion of epiphytes, vines, and secondary growth. Their canopies, far above, gather into a dense roof that filters the sun into an eerie, cathode-ray twilight, punctuated by occasional golden shafts. At the highest elevations, these forest titans rear above even the carpet of clouds that drifts languidly across the sky.

Within this world-forest, pockets of wetter microclimate gather - mist-shrouded groves where silvery cataracts spill from black cliffs of exposed stone, dripping gardens of moss and lichen. Fungal boles sprout from crevices. Strange, sail-shaped fronds drift on the breeze. But large bodies of open water are scarce, as thirsty root systems quickly drain the sodden soil.

An overwhelming fecundity of animal life teems amidst the undergrowth and in the branching highways overhead: iridescent beetles and lace-winged flyers; scuttling, armored centipedes; gliding mammals that soar between the trees on skin flaps. Everywhere there is motion, pursuit, and watchful stillness; the chatter and buzz and roar of creatures resonates from every quarter. The cacophony is oddly musical, ebbing and swelling in unfathomable rhythms.

Scattered remnants of long-vanished inhabitants surface here and there - mounds of earth and rubble, eroded foundations, toppled pillars carved with inscrutable ideograms. A rudimentary network of rope bridges and bamboo platforms, lashed together with vines, snakes through the lower levels of the trees. Its makers are seldom seen; they appear to possess only the most primitive tools and weapons of wood and bone.

This place seems to pulse with a vital energy that permeates the humid air - a sense of dynamic creation and destruction held in primeval balance. Unknown eons of vigorous growth, decay, and regeneration have sculpted it into an intricate, ever-shifting labyrinth. To enter it is to be swallowed up in a green storm of teeming strangeness, as though one had stumbled into the dream of a sentient forest. A visitor could wander for lifetimes here, never discovering all its verdant mysteries.
