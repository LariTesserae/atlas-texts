In a low-lying basin, ancient beyond reckoning, lies a strange and shimmering wetland. The air is warm and still, heavy with humidity that condenses on every surface. Shallow, crystal-clear pools and slow-moving streams weave between islands of lush, otherworldly vegetation.

The plants are like nothing seen on Earth today - towering ferns with iridescent fronds, luminous mosses that pulse gently, orchid-like flowers that seem to float on the surface of the water. They grow in dense clumps, but with plenty of space between - an airy, open feel to the place despite the riotous growth.  

Scattered throughout are relics of incomprehensible technology. Delicate metal spires rise from the pools, shimmering with patterns of light. Gossamer threads, finer than spider silk, stretch between floating diamond-like pods. On the islands, domes of what looks like glass are shot through with networks of glowing capillaries.

The creatures of this place are astounding in their variety and numbers. Clouds of jewel-colored flying things drift on the breeze. Sleek, eyeless swimmers ripple the waters. Crawling, floating, soaring beasts in every color imaginable forage amidst the lush growth. Their calls and cries should be deafening, but instead are gentle, almost musical.

The light has a strange quality, with no obvious source. It sparkles on the water and infuses the air with a soft radiance. Shafts of ethereal illumination slant between the feathery ferns, sculpting the scene in light and shadow.

There is a sense of deep time here, and of forces and intelligences beyond human ken. This is a place out of time, dreamlike and surreal, where terrestrial biology and far-future technologies blend into a numinous whole. A place of vibrant, burgeoning life, yet also of deep peace and stillness at the heart of the shimmering activity. A final vision at the end of ages, glowing with strange magics.
