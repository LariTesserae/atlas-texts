In the endless grey expanse, a hazy plain stretches to the horizon under a thick dome of oppressive clouds. The ground seethes and writhes, a living carpet of dense biomechanical components packed together, moving against each other with a constant metallic slithering. Their sleek grey surfaces glisten with a sheen of condensation in the warm, humid air.

Towering megastructures rise from the churning mass, jutting into the sky. Layer upon layer of artificial construction, they form impossible organic geometries and fluid architectural forms, as if grown rather than built. Walkways and transport tubes web between them in a dizzying tangle.

Flickering bioluminescent displays ripple across the structures' flanks, data and energy pulsing through them. Swarms of tiny machines flit and dance between the behemoths like glowing insects, weaving in intricate murmurations, their electromagnetic songs adding to the constant background hum.

The air hangs heavy with a metallic haze, and oily rain drizzles from the featureless sky, forming rivulets that run down the buildings' curving flanks and vanish into the seething ecosystem below. 

Strange creatures move among the architecture - darting, winged shapes, segmented tentacular forms hauling themselves up the vertical surfaces, pulsing colonies drifting on the humid breeze. They merge and blend with the machinery, both symbiotic parts of the same vast cyborg organism.

The whole landscape pulses with a slow, deep rhythm, structures rising and falling on the churn of microscopic components, new forms growing and dissolving in slow motion like metal lava. A monstrous, planet-scaled machine, building and unbuilding itself in ceaseless, organic flux, it seethes with the industry of unguessable intelligences, an alien megalopolis at the incomprehensible scale of continental plates.
