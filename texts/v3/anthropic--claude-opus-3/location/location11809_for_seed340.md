You descend through layers of tangled roots and vines into the humid depths of a vast subterranean jungle. The air is thick and warm, pulsing with the breath and movement of countless strange lifeforms densely packed into the twisting caverns and tunnels.

Bioluminescent mosses and fungi cast an eerie blue-green glow, their light rippling and dancing across the writhing surfaces of the plant-encrusted walls. Everywhere vegetation dominates - choking the passages, filtering the soupy air, digesting rock and earth to slowly expand this hidden world.

Skeletal trees with translucent leaves rise hundreds of feet, branches interlocking to form a second canopy beneath the stony ceiling. Rubbery gray-green tendrils snake across every surface, pulsing and shivering as they transport nutrients and chemical signals. Spore-filled puffballs explode underfoot, releasing clouds of shimmering dust.

The creatures here seem more plant than animal. Sluggish photosynthetic lizards lie basking under glowing patches. Swarms of tiny flying octopi float by on hydrogen balloons, dangling venomous tentacles. A trundling beetle-like beast with mushroom-cap carapace gnaws at a fallen log crawling with fuzzy purple millipedes. 

Bellows, shrieks, chitterings and eerie atonal songs echo through the labyrinthine chambers, the communication of things ancient and alien. The whole jungle trembles with tectonic rhythms, roots and vines slithering over each other in slow tectonic dances of growth and decay.

Scattered through the foliage are ruins and remnants of old stone structures, their blocks fused with vegetation as if the jungle is slowly absorbing them. Broken clay pots and eroded bone tools hint at past intelligent inhabitants. A few vine-draped monoliths still stand intact, carved with spirals and unknown hieroglyphs.

The primal creative forces of the green abyss churn on, ancient and inexorable, dreaming in slow eons at the pace of root and stone. Entering here, one becomes just another strange organism, engulfed by the humid press of pulsing, ferocious, eldritch life.
