The Whispering Drifts

Countless ages ago, titanic glaciers carved great swathes through the stony flesh of the mountains, leaving behind towering cirques, plunging crevasses, and secret grottoes where ancient ice still lingers in lightless repose. But the glaciers also birthed stranger children - vast, labyrinthine networks of tunnels, caves, and abyssal shafts plunging to unfathomed depths within the mountain roots, an occult underworld shaped by forces beyond the rational.

In one of these great ice-carved defiles, an immense rift opens in the cliffside, a wound in the living rock forty fathoms deep and half as wide. This is the entrance to the Whispering Drifts, a legendary place shunned by the surface dwellers as a haunted domain, the abode of nameless fears and eldritch secrets. Jagged columns of wind- and water-eroded limestone jut from the rift's throat like rotting fangs, hung with hoary banners of glacial ice that never thaw even under the summer sun. 

Frigid, mist-laden air pours from the rift in a ceaseless exhalation, spilling down the defile in languid, coiling tendrils. The mist has an almost palpable weight, and it muffles sound and sight alike until the rift seems to exist in a bubble of preternatural stillness and silence. Only the moaning of the intermittent wind breaks the quiet, keening forlornly as it skirls and eddies about the stony teeth of the grotesque portal.

The Whispering Drifts earn their name from the strange acoustics within. The network of lightless tunnels and crevices acts as a vast stone trumpet, amplifying and distorting any sounds until they become an atonal cacophony ebbing and flowing through the rift. Voices are rendered down into sibilant whispers, sinister and just at the edge of comprehension. The dripping of water and the groaning of shifting ice becomes a macabre symphony, a music of the deep earth not meant for mortal ears. To listen too long is to risk madness, or so the local tales say.

The few brave souls who have dared to venture within speak of vast caverns whose ceilings are lost in darkness, of tunnels worming away into the utter black of the deeper earth, of rippling sheets of translucent flow stone that crawl down the walls like the frozen flesh of antediluvian titans. They whisper of strange lights bobbing in the darkness, of the skittering of unseen claws on stone, of currents of gelid air that moan mournfully as they wend through the honeycombed mountain roots.

And most unnerving, they tell of the sense that the Whispering Drifts are aware, that some vast and brooding intelligence permeates the place, observing interlopers with cold, passionless interest from the impenetrable dark. They speak of feeling psychically dissected, their minds unraveled and rifled through by an alien presence seeking inscrutable communion. Always the impression is of a numinous abyss gazing back, ancient beyond fathoming and possessed of an eons-deep patience and hunger.

For beyond the ossuary of ice and stone, the Whispering Drifts hold deeper secrets still - forsaken shrines to nameless elder gods carved in the living rock, great vaulted chambers where prehistoric magics still shiver in the æther, hidden plunging gulfs said to reach to the very roots of the world and the primordial chaos that seethes there. Madness and revelation walk hand in hand in that haunted, abyssal realm shunned by beast and man alike.

The disgorging mists that spill from the rift form the only known connection to the hidden grotto nestled within the mountain's flank. Some quirk of the tunnels' Stygian geometry siphons a portion of the vapors through unseen arteries in the stone, each exhalation causing the rusted pipe to clank and shudder as it fills the pool anew. And sometimes, echoing up through that improbable conduit, the creature's eerie ululations resound within the Whispering Drifts, sonar clicks in a vast and lidless dark.

Thus the two places exist in an obscure sympathy, occluded channels conducting the mountain's chill lifeblood from one secret hollow to the other. Above, a cyclopean portal to the abyss, thrumming with the atavistic dread of the unknown. Below, an ancient stone womb where a single uncanny life endures in tenebrous isolation. Each partaking of the other's mystery, each an enigmatic node in a wider web of cryptic meaning, the pattern tantalizingly unclear.

Perhaps the Whispering Drifts are the true source of the grotto-dweller, having birthed or shaped the creature to some unguessed purpose in the undreamt-of reaches of a frozen, stony heart. Or perhaps it is a watchful guardian, set to bar the way to the horrors and wonders of the inner mountain. The truth, as with so much about these nebulous reaches, remains mercifully veiled.

But sometimes, when the moaning wind skirls through the serrated fangs of the portal and sets the mists a-boil... when the distant, atonal piping shivers on the edge of hearing, insinuating itself into the mind... when the shadows within the rift seem to congeal and writhe with intimations of deeper darkness... one cannot help but wonder what other secrets the mountain keeps, and what would happen if the Whispering Drifts ever spoke in more than susurrus.
