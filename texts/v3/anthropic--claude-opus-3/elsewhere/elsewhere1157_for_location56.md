High above the abyssal plain where the cyclopean city slumbers, the ocean fractured long ago. In a cataclysm lost to time, the sea floor split asunder. Lava and superheated gases boiled forth, shattering the crust and sending plumes of scalding water towards the surface. As the earth's fury spent itself and the waters cooled, a great rift yawned open in the seabed. An abyss within the abyss.

Plunging down beyond the reach of all light, the chasm falls away into bottomless depths. Its basalt cliffs, sheer and jagged, drop precipitously into a darkness so complete as to seem almost solid. No mere trench or crevasse, this scar in the earth's skin is a passageway to the planet's very heart.

The waters within the rift are an ink-black column, still and frigid, untouched by any current or eddy. In the lightless calm, the only movement is a gentle, continuous rain of marine snow - the decaying remnants of life from the sun-kissed layers far above. The organic detritus drifts ever downward, drawn into the maw of the abyss, fading and then disappearing entirely as the last feeble fingers of light wither into nothingness. 

Clinging to the obsidian walls, colonies of extremophile organisms thrive in this most hostile of environments. Ghostly white tube worms and gossamer-fined fish gather around hydrothermal vents, eking out a tenuous existence on the heat and mineral-rich fluids billowing from these submarine geysers. Mats of chemosynthetic bacteria paint the rock faces in surreal colors, their alien metabolisms fueled not by sunlight, but by sulfurous compounds and heavy metals.

But even these hardiest of creatures have their limits. A few thousand feet down, as pressure mounts to tons per square inch and the last traces of warmth leach away, all visible life vanishes. The walls are bare, scoured bone-smooth by the sheer density of the frigid water. An absence yawns below, vaster and emptier than the void between stars.

And yet, as the chasm plunges towards the planet's core, a glimmer stirs in the dark. A faint, spectral light, more sensed than seen, pulses in the stygian depths. Drifting up from some unimaginable wellspring far below, a delicate luminance suffuses the black waters, fading and blooming like the slowest of heartbeats. Eons-long rhythms of an unknown origin, whispering of forces and phenomena beyond human ken.

---

The glow rising from the bottomless rift has not gone unnoticed by the rift's nearest neighbors. Many leagues distant across the abyssal plain, the city's alien inhabitants have sensed the beacon pulsing in the void. A siren song in the abyss, tantalizing and terrifying, it draws them towards itself with the gravity of an event horizon.

Through the still waters they drift, their progress dreamlike and inexorable. The strange, yearning light tugs at them, resonating in their cores, awakening instincts and drives beyond conscious thought. Like moths to a flame, the creatures converge on the radiant abyss from all corners of the sunken necropolis. 

Their titanic forms assemble above the chasm, translucent bodies pulsing in time with the glow from below. Calcified protrusions and bony growths thrum and quiver, ancient stone vibrating in sympathy with an unknown force. Gossamer filaments twine and undulate, weaving together in intricate patterns heavy with unguessed meaning.

Clusters of smaller beings coalesce around their massive kindred, pulsing and swirling in fractal choreographies. Nodules of bioluminescence flare to life, ice-blue and ghostly green, flickering across the assembled throng in mesmerizing waves. A subsonic thrum rises from the multitude, a choral song felt in the bones rather than heard.

Here, poised on the edge of an abyss beyond all abysses, the creatures begin a ritual as old as the city itself. A rite of instinct and necessity, its purposes beyond any earthly understanding. In their uncountable multitudes, they press forward until the chasm brims with their pulsing, luminous forms, a galaxy of living stars occluding the deeper darkness.

As one, in a synchronicity both beautiful and terrible, they release their hold on the world above. In silence, they fall into the radiant depths. Their glassy bodies plummet in a slow cascade, dreamlike, into the unknown. A pilgrimage to a destination below thought, a journey to the very roots of the world.

The light embraces them as they descend, absorbed into their translucent flesh, until it seems to emanate from within. Like the city they leave behind, they are ancient beyond reckoning, survivors from a past that never was. Whatever awaits them in the fire below, whatever rebirth or transformation, they go to it as they have always done - unhesitating, answering a call only they can hear.

Above, the abyss lies empty once more, a wound in the skin of the world. In time, the city too will fade, its stones subsumed into the earth from which they were hewn. Eons will pass, seas will rise and fall, continents will march their ancient roads. And through it all, the light in the depths will burn - an eternal mystery, a reminder of wonders and horrors beyond imagining, pulsing in the darkness at the very heart of the world.
