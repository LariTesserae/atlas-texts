The Singing Pools

Deep within the mountain's hidden heart, where the Stoneshapers rarely tread, lies a series of vast, water-filled caverns known as the Singing Pools. Connected to the town's reservoir system by a maze of narrow, twisting passages, these subterranean chambers are the secret source of the Watercallers' power and the object of their most solemn rituals.

The caverns are a place of breathtaking beauty and eerie enchantment. The walls are smooth and polished, the stone shot through with glittering veins of quartz and mica that catch the light of the Watercallers' lanterns, casting shimmering reflections across the still surfaces of the pools. Delicate limestone formations drip from the ceilings like petrified lace, each one ringing with a pure, crystalline note as the slow-gathering droplets strike the water below.

But the true wonder of the Singing Pools is the water itself. Icy cold and preternaturally clear, it possesses strange acoustical properties that the Watercallers have learned to manipulate through their chants and gestures. As they move through the chambers, their voices reverberate off the stone in complex harmonies, the notes lingering and interweaving in the still air until the whole cavern thrums like the body of an immense instrument.

The pools are more than just a natural wonder, however. They are the heart of the ancient pact between the Stoneshapers and the mountain itself. According to legend, when the ancestors first came to this place, the mountain recognized their reverence and resilience, and granted them the gift of the Singing Pools. In exchange, the Stoneshapers swore to protect and maintain the delicate balance of the caverns, to use their waters only for the good of the community and the land.

And so, for generations, the Watercallers have tended the pools with loving devotion, keeping the water pure and the stone strong. They sing the ancient songs that keep the mountain's heart beating, that call the rain and snow to replenish the aquifers, that soothe the restless tremors of the deep rock. In times of drought or other crisis, they undertake perilous journeys into the furthest reaches of the caverns, using their voices to map the convoluted passages and locate new sources of clean, sweet water.

But the Singing Pools hold deeper secrets still. In the very depths of the caverns, where the water is coldest and the stone most ancient, there are said to be chambers that even the Watercallers fear to enter. Chambers where the voices of the mountain itself can be heard, whispering in the hollow tones of bedrock and pressurized mineral. Chambers where the veil between worlds grows thin, and strange visions flicker in the rippling surface of the pools.

Only in times of dire need will the Watercallers venture into these forbidden reaches, seeking wisdom or warning from the mountain's hidden voice. They go alone, without lantern or compass, trusting in their voices and the ancient songs to guide them through the treacherous dark. Some return forever changed, their eyes haunted by the weight of the secrets they carry. Others do not return at all, lost to the mountain's mysterious embrace.

And so the Singing Pools remain the Stoneshapers' most closely guarded treasure, the source of their strength and the symbol of their sacred bond with the land. The music of the caverns pulses through the town's veins like lifeblood, a constant reminder of the ancient pact that sustains them. And as long as the Watercallers keep their vigil, as long as the voices of the mountain are honored and appeased, the people of the high stone town will endure, as timeless and immovable as the rock itself.
