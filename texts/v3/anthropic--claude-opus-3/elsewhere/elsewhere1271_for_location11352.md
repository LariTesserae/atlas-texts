Beneath the abyssal metropolis, the seafloor drops away into an immense circular chasm - a stygian maw plunging to lightless, crushing depths. This abyssal portal yawns beneath the city like a great drain or singularity, its perimeter ringed with seething hydrothermal vents and ragged tectonic fissures belching silt and toxic compounds into the water.

The chasm seems to exert an inexorable pull on the eldritch city above. The unearthly structures are all subtly distorted around it, leaning inward, their twisted mineral growths and flickering bioluminescent sigils converging on the void like monstrous arrows. Subsonic emanations from the city, the great moans and shudders that rattle the abyss, all become strangely amplified and distorted as they echo down into the portal's depths.

Even the entities of the city are inextricably drawn to the chasm. Pulsing rivers of the flickering mote-creatures continuously flow over the precipice and vanish into the black pit. The shambling aqueduct-prowlers migrate to its ragged edge in great numbers and fling themselves in, as though summoned by some irresistible call. On the rarest of occasions, even the colossal shadow-behemoths on the city's periphery slowly, ponderously converge on the chasm and slide their great bulk down into oblivion.

What transpires in the stygian depths is an impenetrable mystery. No trace of the sacrificed entities ever re-emerges from the abyss. But as they vanish into the darkness, eerie sheets of spectral light ripple across the hydrothermal halo in shimmering auroral displays. Tectonic fissures groan and shudder, belching out colossal bubbles of foul ichor that rise through the water like eldritch breath. In the aftermath, the city always seethes with revitalized activity, its structures mutating into new configurations and its entity-organelles multiplying with manic intensity.

It's as though the chasm is the true heart of the eldritch city - a black hole-like organ that siphons the abyssal metropolis' strange denizens and pseudo-life into itself, only to return some unnatural motive force from the crushing depths. An otherworldly engine that devours its own offspring to continuously redefine its own alien anatomy. Peering into the abyss beneath the city is like gazing into the event horizon of an incomprehensible cosmic machine - an eternal, self-consuming maw that existence itself pours into and warps around. In this dark convergence of city, entities, and stygian portal, the very nature of reality itself becomes occult and distorted, an eternally-transforming vortex of eldritch creation and annihilation.
