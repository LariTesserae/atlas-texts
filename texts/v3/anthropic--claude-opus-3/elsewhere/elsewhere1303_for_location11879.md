At the base of the mountains, a vast, sprawling network of caverns and tunnels winds its way deep into the earth. This subterranean realm, known as the Underhalls, is a labyrinthine world of shadows and secrets, inextricably linked to the eerie, abandoned city that looms high above.

The Underhalls are a place of perpetual darkness, their winding passages and immense caverns illuminated only by the same strange, phosphorescent mineral growths that riddle the city's foundations. These glowing veins of crystal pulse with a faint, unsettling rhythm, casting shifting patterns of light and shadow across the rough-hewn walls.

The air in the Underhalls is thick and heavy, saturated with the damp, earthy scent of ancient stone and the faint, metallic tang of the strange minerals that suffuse the caverns. The silence here is broken only by the occasional drip of water echoing in the distance, and the low, pulsing hum of the crystalline growths.

As one descends deeper into the Underhalls, the passages begin to show signs of ancient, long-abandoned construction. Smooth, geometric tunnels branch off from the natural caverns, their walls embellished with intricate, hieroglyphic carvings that seem to glow with the same inner light as the mineral veins. These carvings depict strange, alien scenes and inscrutable symbols, hinting at the vast and ancient knowledge of the Xel'thari.

At the heart of the Underhalls lies a vast, circular chamber, its walls lined with towering, monolithic machines that throb with a deep, resonant energy. These machines, like those in the city above, are an amalgamation of anachronistic technologies, fusing organic and mechanical components in ways that defy comprehension. At the center of the chamber, a great, pulsing mass of the luminescent mineral seems to grow directly out of the stone, its tendrils reaching out to intertwine with the machines in a complex, symbiotic web.

It is here, in the deepest reaches of the Underhalls, that the true nature of the connection between the subterranean realm and the city above becomes apparent. The mineral growths that riddle both the caverns and the city are not separate entities, but rather part of a vast, interconnected network that spans the entire region. This network, it seems, is the true lifeblood of the Xel'thari civilization, the source of their power and the key to their enigmatic purpose.

The Xel'thari themselves are rarely seen in the Underhalls, but their presence can be felt in the faint, echoing whispers that sometimes drift through the tunnels, and in the fleeting shadows that seem to dart just beyond the edge of the crystalline light. When they do appear, it is often as silent, ghostly figures, their luminous eyes glinting in the darkness as they tend to the great machines and mineral growths that fill the central chamber.

It is whispered among the few outsiders who have ventured into the Underhalls that the caverns hold the key to the Xel'thari's true nature and purpose. Some say that the machines and mineral growths are part of a vast, cosmic engine, harnessing the energy of the mountain itself for some inscrutable end. Others believe that the Underhalls are a gateway to other worlds, or even other dimensions, and that the Xel'thari use the power of the mineral network to traverse the boundaries of space and time.

Yet for all the speculation and legend, the true nature of the Underhalls and their connection to the city above remains a mystery. Like the Xel'thari themselves, the subterranean realm is a place of secrets and shadows, its deepest truths hidden from the eyes of the world. And so the Underhalls endure, a haunting, timeless space beneath the mountain, forever bound to the eerie, abandoned city that looms above, and to the enigmatic beings who call both realms their home.
