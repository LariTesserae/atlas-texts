The Salt Flats

Far below the ancient stone city, where the mountain streams gather and flow into a broad, shallow basin, lies a vast expanse of salt flats. Here, the water that so defines the city above finally surrenders to the sun and the arid winds, leaving behind a crystalline residue that stretches to the horizon.

The salt flats are a landscape of extremes, a stark contrast to the lush, moisture-laden environment of the city. The ground is a patchwork of geometric patterns - polygonal shapes formed by the cracking and drying of the mineral-rich earth. The salt crust crunches underfoot, breaking into irregular shards that catch the light like broken glass.

The air here is dry and searingly hot, carrying the faint, acrid scent of minerals. Mirages shimmer in the distance, blurring the line between earth and sky. The horizon is a hazy, wavering line, broken only by the distant silhouettes of mountain ranges that rise like mirages themselves.

Despite the harsh conditions, life persists here. Hardy, specialized plants take root in the cracks and depressions, their leaves coated in a reflective, waxy sheen to ward off the relentless sun. Unique species of birds, adapted to the saline environment, flit among the scattered vegetation, their cries echoing across the flats.

The salt flats are a place of pilgrimage and ritual for the people of the mountain city. At certain times, determined by the alignment of celestial bodies and the whisperings of the Lithic Ones, processions make their way down from the city, following the course of the water. They cross the flats, leaving ephemeral trails of footprints, to gather salt and perform ceremonies at the heart of the basin.

Here, in this place of desiccation and crystallization, the people honor the cycle that sustains their city - the endless journey of water from mountain to sky to earth and back again. They scatter offerings, sing hymns that resonate across the flats, and collect the salt that will flavor their food, preserve their harvests, and maintain the balance of their stone-bound existence.

As the salt flats shimmer under the celestial vault, they serve as a reminder of the delicate equilibrium that underpins this world - the interplay of water and stone, mountain and desert, the ancient city and the timeless expanse below. And though the Lithic Ones do not tread upon this crystalline surface, their presence is felt in the salt-tinged wind, a whispered connection between the realms of stone and sky.
