At the base of the mountains, where the mad spires fade into the mist and the impossible angles become mere jagged silhouettes, there lies a vast, still lake as black and glossy as obsidian. The water is an inky void, never pierced by sunlight through the thick, oily smoke that roils in the valleys, and it reflects the ghostly underbellies of the clouds and the glowing, pulsing lights of the distant city like a warped, funhouse mirror. 

The shores of the lake are a strange, liminal space - a borderland between the twisting unreality of the peaks and the merely haunted gloom of the lowlands. Stunted, twisted pines cling to the stony banks, their branches draped in tatters of fog. Quivering beds of luminous reeds and bulbous pitcher plants drink the reeking moisture. Hulking silhouettes of abandoned machines loom in the twilight - dredges, smelters, and pumping engines left to rust and merge with the skeletal trees, forming eerie industrial topiaries.

The surface of the lake is a near-perfect stillness, a beguiling smoothness that belies the turmoil beneath. For deep below the ebon waters churn with a strange, geothermal life. Boiling currents well up from fumaroles on the lakebed, superheated by the same arcane energies that warp reality in the peaks above. The scalding waters are stained with sulfur, infused with heavy metals and runoff from the mountain factories, forming a bathwater-from-hell that has nurtured a unique extremophilic ecology.

Vast mats of heat and metal-loving bacteria paint the depths in gaudy chemical colors. Schools of eyeless, ghost-pale fish navigate the toxic currents, filter-feeding on the microbes. Long, ropy tendrils of tube worms anchored to the fumaroles wave in the shimmering waters, their blood-red tips glowing like subway tunnel emergency lights through the murk. Giant isopods armored in biomechanical chitin prowl the abyssal lakebed, hunting the fish and each other.

But it's the regular trains of bubbles rising to the surface and popping with reekingly mephitic sighs that hint at the lake's greater secrets. For in the most blistering, lightless depths there are cracks and vents that plunge beyond mere earthly heat into metaphysical fire. These are the direct pipelines to the otherworldly engines that power the impossible city, and their effluent is more than mundane pollution.

Here at the lake's deepest point, the barrier between material water and eldritch energy is at its thinnest, and the two mingle to birth unnatural wonders and horrors. Shoals of many-finned things with hideously human faces flash through the boil. Columns of braided tentacles, each thick as a submarine, rise from the depths to grope blindly at the roiling surface. Orbs of sickly light pulse in the abyss like drowning stars.

And so the lake is both a sink for the impossible city's waste and a font of its aberrant power. In those lightless depths it gestates raw possibility, churning out fresh nightmares to replace those lost to the mad machines above. The ghostly workers never approach its mirrored stillness, though the placid surface sometimes ripples with the suggestion of titanic forms emerging into the twilight. Only the enigmatic overlords of the mountain metropolis, in their smog-swathed spires, dare to dip strange siphons into those sacrosanct waters, returning with liquid catches that glow with unearthly bioluminescence and send oily rainbows skittering across the surface.

Thus the silent lake and the clamorous city exist in symbiotic codependence - one a twisted, smoke-shrouded engine of impossible industry; the other an ink-dark mirror reflecting that madness back as squirming, embryonic potential. Two twinned blasphemies against nature, logic, and reason, forever circling and feeding off each other in their haunted mountain fastness, at the edge of everything sane and right.
