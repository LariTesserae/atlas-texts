The River of Unmaking flows from the hollow, a waterway that seems to erode the very nature of things even as it sustains a strange and shifting ecology along its path. The river's source is a point of quantum indeterminacy at the deepest part of the pocket world, where the shimmering presence of the emergent entity is most concentrated. From this uncanny wellspring, possibility and paradox bleed into flowing water, charging it with the power to unravel the established order.

As it leaves the hollow, the river appears at first to be a normal woodland stream, winding its way through a forest of ancient trees. Yet there is an eerie luminescence to its waters, a pearlescent sheen that catches the eye and then seems to slip away when focused upon. The currents move in unsettling patterns, forming vortices and whorls that defy hydrodynamics. Occassionally, the water's surface will shiver and crack, as though space itself is coming apart, revealing chasms of empty white before sealing up again.

The effects on the surrounding ecosystem are at once subtle and profound. The trees nearest the banks are gnarled into impossible topologies, their branches twisting back upon themselves or dissolving into fractalized mists of leaves. Fungal growths proliferate in psychedelic hues and Escherian forms, as though guided by a non-Euclidean geometry. Everywhere, there is a sense of organic order giving way to something stranger and more permissive.

The creatures that drink from the river are changed most of all. Fish sprout additional fins in asymmetric arrangements, or develop translucent, opalescent scales that refract light into dizzying spectra. Invertebrates unspool into living Möbius strips that pulse with bioluminescent patterns. Amphibians phase between solid and liquid states, blurring the line between individual and environment. It's an accelerated evolution that breaks free from the usual constraints, spilling out into radically new forms.

As the river flows on, its reality-warping influence seems to spread and intensify. The air along its banks shimmers like a heat haze, and pockets of altered physics sprout like strange flowers - zones of reversed gravity, slow-light bubbles, knots in the fabric of spacetime. To walk along the River of Unmaking is to wander through an increasingly alien and oneiric landscape, where the laws of nature become flexible and negotiable.

Eventually, the river brings its weird waters to a terminal point, a liminal zone where it flows out of the known world altogether. This might be a misty borderland at the edge of dreamscape, a hole torn in the map of consensus reality, or an infinite waterfall cascading into the void between dimensions. Here, at the far reach of the river's influence, the unmaking is complete - a place of pure potential, where all rules are suspended.

Perhaps it is in this ultimate unplace that the entities and organisms touched by the river arrive at their final metamorphosis, shedding the last vestiges of their earthly forms and emerging as something wholly new and unfathomable. Or perhaps it is the source of even stranger flows, rivers within rivers, that carry the transformative essence to other worlds and realities, seeding the cosmos with pockets of wild, mutable strangeness.

For the rare wanderer who follows the River of Unmaking to its furthest point, the experience is one of existential vertigo and boundless possibility, a glimpse into the raw, shimmering chaos that underlies the apparent solidity of the world. Whether they return transformed or simply dissolve into the ever-shifting flow likely depends on the strength of their own reality, their ability to hold onto some core of stable selfhood even as all else comes apart. Yet even a glimpse of this ultimate unmaking is enough to change a soul forever, and to plant a seed of wild potential that might blossom in unpredictable ways.

In the end, the River of Unmaking is a reminder that beneath the surface of the known, there are always stranger currents flowing, ready to erode the edges of the possible and carry us to new shores of being. It is a living enigma, a puzzle and a promise, an invitation to embrace the uncanny flux at the heart of all things and see where it might lead. For those brave or foolhardy enough to wade into its luminous waters, no destination is certain - only a journey into the ever-renewing mystery of a world in constant creation.
