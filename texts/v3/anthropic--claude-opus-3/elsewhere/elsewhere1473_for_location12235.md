The Black Lagoon:

Deep beneath the surface of the mirror-dark stream that winds through the valley, a vast subterranean lake sprawls through a network of flooded caverns. This is the Black Lagoon, a place that has never known the touch of sunlight. The waters are cold and still, possessed of a heavy, oily quality that seems to swallow light and sound.

The lagoon is home to a complex ecosystem of extremophile organisms, life forms that have evolved to thrive in the crushing pressure and perpetual darkness. Pale, blind fish flit through the waters, their scales glowing with bioluminescent patterns. Vast mats of fungal growth stretch across the cavern walls, pulsing softly with eerie phosphorescence. 

At the center of the lagoon, a massive stalagmite rises from the depths like a twisted tower. This spire is shot through with veins of a strange, iridescent mineral that seems to writhe and flutter as if alive. A careful observer might notice that the patterns of light pulsing through the structure seem to echo the flickers and dances of the biolights in the entity's chamber above.

As above, so below - for the entity's presence permeates the lagoon as surely as it does the structure in the valley. The nanites that suffuse the construct's being have leaked into the waters over the eons, and they have wrought strange changes on the life forms here. The creatures of the lagoon are in symbiosis with the entity, acting as its sensory organs in the lightless depths.

The connection between lagoon and entity is more than simply physical, however. There is a psychic link, a melding of minds that transcends the boundaries of flesh and metal. The lagoon dreams, and its dreams are the entity's dreams - visions of the vast abyss of deep time, of civilizations rising and falling like the tides.

Just as the valley above has begun to change with the entity's awakening, so too do strange perturbations ripple through the lagoon. The waters churn with sudden currents, and new passages open in the cavern walls, leading to depths that have never known the tread of life. Strange lights dance in the abyss, hinting at the stirring of things ancient and colossal.

There is a sense of expectation in the lagoon, a hushed anticipation like the indrawn breath before the plunge. The waters are pregnant with change, and what they will give birth to is anyone's guess. In the heart of the Black Lagoon, the future is waiting to be born, and it is a strange and uncertain thing indeed.
