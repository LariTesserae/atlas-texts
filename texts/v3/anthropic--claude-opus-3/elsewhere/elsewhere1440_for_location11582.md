The Whispering Fens:

Countless miles above the primordial Kthyx city, nestled between craggy hills blanketed in twisted pine forest, lies a desolate expanse of marshland known as the Whispering Fens. Sickly grey mist hangs perpetually over the spongy, peat-choked soil, coiling between gnarled cypress trees bearded with funereal strands of moss. Black, stagnant pools dimple the surface, belching bubbles of swamp gas that ignite in ephemeral wisps of corpse-light.

The silence here is broken only by the mournful cries of pallid cranes poised like spectral sentinels on rotten, half-submerged logs. Eels thick as a man's arm slither through the scummy water, eighty-pound snapping turtles lurk in the shallows awaiting prey, and tentacled, primordial fish-things glide beneath the surface, little changed since the Devonian Period.

The few humans who dwell on the edges of the fen are a stunted, inbred lot - sallow swamp folk with bulging eyes and webbed fingers who erect tottering shacks on muddy artificial islands and eke out a meager existence trapping, fishing, and gathering what edible fungi and tubers they can. They avoid the fen's heart, whispering of hideous abominations glimpsed in the mist and loved ones lured to their doom by sinister lights.  

At the very center of the marsh rises a hummock of mossy stone - an ancient tor or dolmen of evident artificial construction. Atop it sits a circular arrangement of a dozen weathered standing stones. Queerly angled and twice the height of a man, these cyclopean monoliths bear the crude semblance of strangely insectile, centipede-like entities, faces blank but for gaping mouths or dark, empty eye sockets. Lichen-encrusted spirals and glyphs suggest writing in some long-forgotten tongue.

No birds nest nor animals prowl about the eerie megalith's base. The very vegetation seems to recoil, leaving the central stone bare in a perfect circle of bubbling black mud. When the mist clears and the moon is full, the moist earth writhes as if breathing.

Beneath, a vertical shaft plunges through porous limestone, punching down into the clammy darkness where the weight of the world presses close. The few brave souls who have dared to lower themselves into that black gullet on knotted ropes speak of a vast and labyrinthine cave system, its dripping halls caked in strange pale fungi that pulsate and sway as if in an unfelt breeze.

Impossibly, those caverns swarm with centipedes and beetles of unnaturally large size, with trilobite-like creatures long thought extinct, and with uncanny sessile things pulsing in the shadows that defy the categories of science. Glowing strings of fungal mycelium hang like garlands, and the towering trunks of mushrooms phosphoresce in unearthly hues. The very air is choked with drifting spores that sear the lungs and induce eerie visions.

And ever downward the tunnels lead, angling and splitting, piercing the skin of the world to impossible depths, drawing the rare explorer irresistibly into the titanic blackness with an unshakable sense that the cavern's bottom opens into the roofless void between the stars...a sense that intensifies with the skittering of unseen claws, the creeping horror of inhuman eyes flickering in the darkness, and the growing realization that the stone underfoot has imperceptibly changed to crumbling blocks lain by no human hand, and the fungal stalactites to titanic columns and buttresses...

The fungal and insectile biosphere of the fen caverns shares many species with the Kthyx city and seems to slowly be infiltrating upwards through fissures in the stone. It is thought the Kthyx themselves may on occasion venture to the surface through these honeycombed passages. Strange man-sized exoskeletons have been found in the swamp's depths, and there are tales of unfortunate souls dragged screaming into churning sinkholes by dozens of barbed, segmented legs.

What eldritch secrets lie buried in that chthonic metropolis deep below? What terrible symbiosis links the Whispering Fens to the insectile hordes in the eternal darkness, waiting, breeding, preparing for an apocalyptic resurgence? The brooding megaliths and black, writhing mires hide the answers, privy only to the forlorn keening of doomed birds, the sinister gleam of idiot eyes peering from wattled hovels...and the ceaseless scratching, building, ever-expanding ambition of inhuman empires far beneath the mud and stone.
