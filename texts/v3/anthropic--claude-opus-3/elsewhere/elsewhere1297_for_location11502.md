The Void Whisperers drift silently along the periphery of Zephyrian society, spectral figures cloaked in tattered veils that flutter in the thin wind. Their origin and nature are shrouded in mystery, but they are said to be attuned to the fading psychic energies that linger in the broken world's atmosphere, the residual memories and dreams of a planet that was once alive with thought and emotion.

Void Whisperers are solitary beings, rarely interacting directly with the cities' other inhabitants. They are often seen hovering motionlessly at the edges of the asteroids, their veils billowing around them as they seem to listen intently to unheard whispers carried on solar winds. Some say they are the last remnants of an ancient monastic order that dedicated itself to preserving the world's psychic heritage, while others believe they are a separate species entirely, evolved or engineered to tap into the empathic currents that once flowed through the planet's noosphere.

Despite their elusiveness, the Void Whisperers serve an important role in Zephyrian culture. It is said that they can sense impending catastrophes, perceiving subtle shifts in the psychic aether that presage asteroid collisions or atmospheric disruptions. On occasion, they will drift into the cities bearing cryptic warnings, expressed in trilling, harmonic vocalizations that seem to bypass language and pluck at the subconscious mind.

The Zephyrians regard the Void Whisperers with a mix of reverence and unease. Their presence is believed to be a reminder of the world that was lost, and of the tenuousness of their own existence amidst the ruins. Some even whisper that the Void Whisperers are not entirely of this realm, that their consciousness extends beyond the veil of material reality into dimensions of pure thought and memory.

In the quietest sections of the cities, where the hum of machinery fades and the whisper of wind dominates, meditation chambers are built to commune with the Void Whisperers. These stark, open spaces are adorned with abstract, geometric patterns said to resonate with the residual psychic energies, amplifying the planet's fading memories. Here, Zephyrian Archivists and spiritualists sit in silent contemplation, hoping to glean insights and prophecies from the cryptic utterings of the Void Whisperers.

As the cities drift on the thermal currents, the Void Whisperers maintain their silent vigil, listening to the echoes of a world's lost dreams. Their presence is a haunting reminder of the mysteries that linger in the spaces between life and oblivion, and of the power of memory to endure even in the face of cataclysmic erasure. In a way, they are the living embodiment of the cities themselves - spectral remnants of a once-vibrant world, adrift on the currents of an indifferent cosmos, forever listening to the whispers of a past that refuses to be silenced.
