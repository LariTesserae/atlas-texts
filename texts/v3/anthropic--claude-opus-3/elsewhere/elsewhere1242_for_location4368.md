High above the abyssal plain, where the surreal temple hovers in its improbable bubble of light and warmth, the ocean's surface dances beneath sun and stars. Here, a thousand miles from the nearest shore, the boundary between sea and sky stretches out in an eternal expanse of blue on blue, broken only by the ceaseless rhythm of waves.

And yet, even in this seeming emptiness, life flourishes. Great pods of whales, their songs echoing through the miles-deep water, surface to breathe, attended by flocks of wheeling seabirds. Shoals of silver fish flash and turn in the sun-shot depths, while swarms of krill hang like underwater clouds, staining the water the red of diluted blood.

But strangest and most beautiful of all are the Sun Jellies – vast, diaphanous creatures, shimmering with every color of the rainbow as they drift placidly just beneath the waves. Each one is a city unto itself, its translucent bell spanning fifty feet or more, its trailing tentacles stretching out like gleaming highways of light.

The Sun Jellies are not single organisms, but rather colonies of countless smaller creatures, bound together in symbiotic harmony. Gossamer-thin membranes, shot through with networks of bioluminescent veins, stretch between the individual members, pulsing gently with the collective heartbeat of the whole. Swirling patterns of color and light play across these living sails, a mesmerizing language of motion telling stories of life's journey through the sunlit seas.

Within each Sun Jelly's bell, a rich ecosystem thrives. Tiny crustaceans and luminous fish dart among miniature forests of kelp and coral, while pulsing floor-beds of photosynthetic algae provide oxygen and sustenance. The community is knit together by threads of neural tissue, weaving the disparate creatures into a single, dreaming mind adrift on the currents.

The Sun Jellies are gatherers of light and farmers of energy. During the day, they turn their shimmering sails to the sun, absorbing its radiance and storing it in glowing nodes throughout their bodies. At night, they glow like drifting lanterns, casting soft illumination on the waters around them, attracting the plankton and small fish upon which the colony feeds.

But the link between the Sun Jellies and the hidden temple below goes deeper than these cycles of light and nourishment. For the Sun Jellies are more than mere ocean-dwellers – they are the eyes and ears of the ancient entity in the abyss, its emissaries to the world above.

Through a metaphysical symbiosis as old as life itself, each Sun Jelly is linked to the entity's consciousness, a node in its vast dreaming mind. The patterns of light and color that play across their sails are not merely for show – they are a language, a means of communion with the intelligence in the deep. The Sun Jellies gather the stories and secrets of the sunlit world – the songs of whales, the paths of storms, the stirrings of human souls on distant shores – and relay them downward, weaving them into the entity's endless dreaming.

In turn, the entity shares its own strange wisdom, its aeons-deep knowledge of the workings of the universe. The Sun Jellies become repositories of this eldritch lore, their collective minds illuminated by secrets beyond the ken of mortal creatures. At times, they pass these revelations on to other beings – a piercing burst of insight granted to a pod of dolphins, a whispered truth woven into the song of a whale. And very occasionally, when they sense a special openness and potential, they reach out to the minds of humans, granting visions and dreams of haunting beauty and mystery.

Thus the two realms – the sunlit world above and the luminous temple below – remain forever linked, each one dreaming of the other in an eternal dance of meaning and mystery. The Sun Jellies, children of both realms, drift serenely between them, shimmering threads in the vast web of consciousness that binds all life in the deeps of space and time.
