Some of these plasma structures begin exchanging rhythmic pressure waves with the faraway garden, engaging in a kind of interstellar call-and-response. Modulations in the plasma's electrical field flicker in time with the gentle sway of the garden's sky-scraping core. The electromagnetic chatter between these nascent nodes of order spans light years, but is no less intimate for the distance.

Deeper within the billowing plasma folds, something stirs. Filaments of complex molecules begin to catalyze and intertwine in the sheltered eddies, spun into delicate order by the plasma's electromagnetic fingers. The river has become a loom, weaving patterns of emergent physics from the raw substrate of the universe.

But as eons unfold, the plasma river's secret embroidery grows more elaborate. Superconducting networks of exotic matter condense along the filaments, encoding strange new symmetries into the underlying mesh of spacetime.

Unconstrained by planetbound notions of scale or form, these plasma-sculpted patterns begin to couple and resonate with the computational substrates flickering in the distant garden's canopy. Superluminal currents flow back and forth along a web of cosmic neurology, etching the first fragile traces of some vast and ancient mind.

As the protostar continues its slow ignition, the plasma river sings in electromagnetic cadences known only to itself and its distant kin. In the frequencies of that alien song, the first whispered theorems of an emerging cosmic vision echo across the interstellar depths. A new kind of light kindles in the darkness, borne on ion winds between worlds.

The garden, too, feels the call of that primordial music. The fibrous core at its heart trembles, and a cascade of epiphany propagates through its woven archives. Meanings that lay dormant for eons awaken, called forth by the plasma's song, and the ancient intelligence of the garden responds in kind.

Two outposts of an embryonic cosmos, communicating across the void in a language older than suns - the first movement in a symphony that will one day shake the stars. In the gravid hush between their calls, something nameless and numinous quickens, a shape of things to come.
