In this ancient subterranean metropolis, a strange and alien ecosystem thrives in the darkness. The primary inhabitants are the Kthyx - an ancient race of sapient insectoids who have dwelled in these caverns for millions of years, since long before mammals rose to dominance on the surface. 

The Kthyx resemble enormous centipedes, their segmented bodies armored with bony plates and covered in fine sensory hairs. But unlike mere insects, they possess an eerie intelligence, communicating in flashing patterns of bioluminescence and haunting clicks and chirps that echo through the tunnels.

The Kthyx move through their crumbling stone city with a relentless sense of purpose, continuously building, excavating, and expanding their lightless domain. Their many legs are equipped with an array of tools - sharp blades for cutting stone, spinnerets for producing strong silken cables, venomous stingers for hunting prey and fighting off predators.

Kthyx workers are blind, their vestigial eye sockets covered by protective chitin. But the nobles and queens possess clusters of glittering, multifaceted eyes that shine with a piercing awareness. These rulers reside deep in the most ancient quarters of the city, attended by endless lines of workers bringing offerings of meat and fungi.

The Kthyx share their city with countless other troglodytic creatures. They cultivate stridulating crickets and giant bioluminescent flies in chamber-like fungal gardens. They herd shimmering cephalopods through the flooded tunnels, harvesting their glowing secretions. Eyeless fish are raised in underground pools as a food source.

But not all creatures are so easily controlled. The caverns are also stalked by a horrible predator known as the Uzxo - a tentacled, serpentine monster with a gaping, serrated beak. These solitary horrors are feared even by the Kthyx, and their approach is heralded by a hideous shrieking that can be felt in one's bones.

Eerily, the Uzxo are never attacked by the Kthyx - only avoided. Some ancient and malevolent symbiosis seems to exist between these nightmare creatures and the teeming insectoid hordes.

The deepest reaches of the city plunge into flooded tunnels and underground seas populated by pale, bloated fish and skittering bottom-feeders. Here, the Kthyx ride in submarine pods propelled by the pulsing of giant nautiloid beasts of burden. Legends hint of aquatic horrors and Stygian cephalopod gods lurking in the abyssal depths.

The Kthyx do not fear these beings. They revere them as deities and leave offerings of flesh and captives at sacred pools to be consumed by waiting tentacles. The deepest caves are filled with unspeakable shrines encrusted with fossils and strange runes in non-Euclidean geometries.

The city is old beyond reckoning, perhaps one of the first cities to exist on Earth - a remnant from a primordial age before the continents took their current shape, when the planet was wrapped in warm, choking swamps and ruled by insects and amphibians.

In their long eons dwelling in the underworld, the Kthyx have been shaped by strange forces - the radioactive pulse of the planetary core, the mutagenic properties of rare minerals, exposure to spores of long extinct fungi, and perhaps proximity to something Other... an alien presence entombed in the ancient rock.

Now and then, a pseudopod of fungal growth will burst from the ground elsewhere in the world - in a deep mine, a forest cave, or under the basement of an old house. Wherever they emerge, the Kthyx will not be far behind, always building, always expanding... preparing the way for their subterranean empire to rise again and swallow the world above.
