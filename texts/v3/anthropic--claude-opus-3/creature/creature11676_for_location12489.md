In this strange and ancient forest-city, life has evolved along paths both familiar and utterly alien. The beings that dwell here are the descendants of refugees from realms unknown, survivors adapting to a multiversal wound.

The primary inhabitants resemble a amalgam of Earth creatures - part primate, part insect, part plant. They are the Sap-Weavers. With multiple dexterous arms and fingers, they deftly tend the arboreal infrastructure, encouraging new growth and repairing walkways. Their faces are unsettlingly human, but stretched and flattened, with large liquid eyes that shimmer with bioluminescence. Sap-Weavers communicate through subsonic hums and pheromonal signals, coordinating their activities in a complex hive-like society.

Symbiotic creatures live amongst the Sap-Weavers. The furry, tailed rodents with human visages are known as Whisper-Mice. Highly intelligent and social, they serve as scouts, explorers and playful companions. Their little hands are always busy, weaving fibers and fashioning tools. Sensitive to the subtle energies of the multiverse, Whisper-Mice sing haunting melodies to soothe planar disturbances.

Other beings are more cryptic. The fractalized sloths are solitary philosophers, surfing psychic currents and dreaming the dreams that keep this pocket of life stable. Fungal nodes puff spore-messages through the hazy air, transferring memories between trees. And through it all wander the Amber Ones - not a species, but individuals from many peoples, frozen in sap at the moment of some unfathomable calamity. Locked in eternal stasis, they have become muses and oracles for the forest.

For there are no Sap-Weaver elders. Saplings are born from pods on ghost-tree branches, nurtured by the community but with no parents. The progenitors are long gone, their final fates unknown. Did they ascend to a higher realm? Were they claimed by the outer unreality? None now can say.

And so this eerie eden endures, neither growing nor decaying, held in changeless balance by its inhabitants' collective will. They cultivate a dreamlike existence, tending to the psychic wounds in the space between worlds through humble routine and creative abandon. The Sap-Weavers and their companions may be a dead end, life's last stubborn outpost before the void. Or they may be pioneers, finding paths to rebirth through secret gates of wood and starlight. Regardless, they maintain their patient, wondering stewardship over a world that may one day again bloom with untold marvels.
