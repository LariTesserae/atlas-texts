In this otherworldly city, a race of beings as ancient as the ocean itself dwell in serene solitude. These enigmatic creatures, known as the Lumina, have evolved to thrive in the extreme conditions of the abyssal plain. Their bodies are composed of the same translucent, bioluminescent material as the city itself, allowing them to blend seamlessly with their surroundings.

The Lumina are a highly advanced, telepathic species with a collective consciousness that spans the entirety of their civilization. They communicate through a complex network of light pulses and emotional projections, sharing thoughts and memories instantaneously. Each individual Lumina is but a single facet of a greater, interconnected whole.

Physically, the Lumina are graceful and ethereal, with long, slender limbs and smooth, featureless faces that glow with an inner light. They move through the water with effortless ease, propelled by subtle manipulations of the currents around them. Their bodies are highly adaptable, able to shift and change in response to their environment or emotional state.

The Lumina are not bound by the constraints of biological reproduction. Instead, they create new individuals through a process of energetic manifestation, drawing upon the power of the city's core to form new bodies as needed. This allows them to maintain a stable population and ensures the continuity of their collective knowledge.

As a species, the Lumina are deeply contemplative and philosophical. They spend much of their time in meditation, exploring the vast realms of their shared consciousness and pondering the mysteries of the universe. They have long since transcended the need for physical sustenance, drawing their energy directly from the city's power source.

The Lumina are caretakers of the city and its surrounding environment, working in harmony with the strange, luminescent creatures that swim through its streets. They view these creatures as extensions of themselves, part of the greater ecosystem of the abyss.

While the Lumina have the technology to venture beyond the confines of their city, they choose to remain within its boundaries, content in their self-contained world. They have no desire for conquest or expansion, seeking only to maintain the delicate balance of their existence.

To an outside observer, the Lumina might appear aloof or even indifferent to the wider world. In truth, they are deeply empathetic beings, attuned to the ebb and flow of life in the ocean around them. They are the silent guardians of the deep, watching over the abyssal plain with a wisdom and understanding that surpasses human comprehension.
