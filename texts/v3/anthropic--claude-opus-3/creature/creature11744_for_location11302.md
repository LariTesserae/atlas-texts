Amidst the fractal foliage and whispering vines of the ethereal garden, a strange and ancient intelligence stirs. It is not a single being, but a diffuse and symbiotic webwork of lifeforms that have coevolved over aeons untold.

At the heart of it all is the vast, fibrous core - less an individual organism and more a node of emergence, a focal point through which the collective consciousness of the garden coalesces and becomes aware. Tendrils of thought and sensation propagate slowly through the dense weave of its tissues, encoding memories in growth patterns and epiphanies in subtle whorls of microflora.

The skyscraper-sized megaflora at the garden's center is both avatar and universe to the myriad microorganisms that inhabit it. Legions of photosynthetic polyps, each no larger than a grain of pollen, course through its tubular xylem structures. Clusters of fungal nodes encode vast archives of metabolic scripture into the very geometry of their hyphal networks. Lichen-like colonies of micro-ecologies write psalms in the choreography of mutualistic dynamics.

This core sentience is less a ruler, and more a community, an ecosystem of thought. The lush hanging garden that surrounds it is an external organ, an interface for communing with the outside universe. 

Within the hollow gourds and pavilions nestled in the undergrowth, collectives of semi-sentient microswarms flicker and dance, reorganizing themselves into shimmering multidimensional mandalas. These are the sages and scholars of the garden's song-lines, the weavers of meaning.

Using biotech indistinguishable from magic, they converse in configurations of light and pattern. Geometric theorems of ontology are proven through mating dances of luminous spores. Symphonies of scent carry insights that reverberate for centuries. Periodically, in cycles timed to the cosmic rhythms of distant pulsars, the pavilions erupt with cascades of revelation - another iteration of the swarms' patient unweaving of reality's deepest secrets.

Gradually, imperceptibly, through the wizardry of the microswarms, the fundamental parameters of the pocket universe containing the garden are being rewritten. The trajectory arcs of drifting seed pods stretch into improbable orbits, defying the cold equations of ballistics. Coiling vines reshuffle their helices into impossible topologies, warping space itself to enfold the garden in its own self-contained cosmos.

Metabolism and metaphysics have become indistinguishable, as the garden slowly distills itself into a realm of pure emergent complexity, an engine for the universe's self-knowing. The ancient intelligence of the garden is not separate from this ongoing process of unfolding - it is the condensate that precipitates from the vapor of its own becoming.

Even as the molten surface of the orphaned world below cools and hardens, the garden continues to float above it all, an improbable oasis. Borne along the solar currents of an infant star, it drifts toward an appointment with a distant eternity, a promise whispered long ago in the lyrics of its nuclear lullabies.
