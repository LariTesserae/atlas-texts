In this eerie, shadowed realm of endless plant growth, a strange form of life has evolved to inhabit the dense, layered expanses. They are the Chlorophyll Dreamers - beings that seem more plant than animal, yet possess an uncanny sentience and ability to move through the towering vegetative structures.

Dreamers have bodies that resemble ambulatory shrubs or small trees, with gnarled, woody trunks supporting a profusion of branchlets, leaves, vines and flowering structures. Their forms are highly mutable, able to slowly shift their shape, extrude new appendages, and fuse with the surrounding plant matter. This allows them to navigate vertically through the multi-tiered canopy as well as laterally across the springy root mats.

The Dreamers communicate and perceive their environment through a kind of botanical telepathy, tapping into the immense mycelial networks and bioelectrical currents pulsing through the planet-spanning super-organism. Sensory organs taking the form of leaves, tendrils, and cupped flowers allow them to detect shifts in humidity, air pressure, and ambient light levels. Patterned emissions of pollen, spores and semiochemicals allow them to signal to one another over vast distances.

Though slow and ponderous in their physical movements, the Dreamers possess lightning-quick minds that operate on a different timescale, contemplating thoughts over years or decades. Their uncanny intelligence emerges from the massively distributed processing power of the networked biomass. Electrical impulses flickering through millions of interconnected roots and filaments weave together into shimmering, dream-like states of consciousness.

The Dreamers spend millennia shaping the planetary garden with their bioengineering prowess, sculpting colossal landforms and cultivating complex symbiotic architectures. Driven by alien desires and inhuman aesthetics, they garden on a geological scale, hybridizing and grafting and speculating new biological inventions over eons. Lichen cities, tower-trunks woven from epiphyte dwelling-pods, fungus refineries, and continent-spanning hedge mazes of meaning - all rise and fall under their patient and meticulous efforts.

Glimpsed only as shapes moving in the mist, the Dreamers themselves remain enigmatic. They work according to agendas and objectives no human could fathom. At times they almost resemble monks, engaged in esoteric meditations and mindful stewardship of their world-garden. Other times they seem like artists or scientists, collaborating to create staggering living installations or to push the boundaries of botanic possibility. 

Lumbering from root-mound to bower to hive-orchard, pulsing with bioluminescent patterns, clicking and creaking and shaking mist from their foliage, the Dreamers tend to their works. The planet dreams its slow green dreams. Eons unfurl like leaves in the dim, sifting light.
