In this blasted, nightmarish landscape, a primordial ecosystem of sorts ekes out an existence amidst conditions inimical to most known forms of life. The denizens of this realm are a bizarre menagerie of chitinous horrors and pulsating, gelatinous abominations.

The dominant lifeforms appear to be the colossal centipede-like creatures that scuttle across the obsidian plains on dozens of segmented legs. These armored behemoths, some twenty feet in length or more, are the apex predators of this hostile domain. Their elongated bodies are encased in interlocking plates of glossy black chitin, studded with jagged spines and bristling with twitching, sensory appendages. Saber-like mandibles jut from their eyeless heads, dripping with caustic, paralytic venom. 

These centipedal titans are constantly on the prowl, hunting the lesser creatures that swarm across the glassy badlands. They move with a surprising speed and agility, their segmented bodies undulating hypnotically as their skittering limbs propel them forward. When prey is sighted, they strike with horrifying swiftness, their venomous mouthparts clamping down on struggling victims and injecting them with digestive fluids to liquefy their innards for easier consumption.

The centipedes share their domain with swarms of beetle-like creatures that fly erratically through the stagnant, toxic air. These flying hordes dance in complex, ever-shifting cloud formations, engaging in some indecipherable pattern of areal mating or communication. Up close, they are revolting to behold - their carapaces are mottled and pitted, leaking putrid fluids and sprouting bristling clusters of sensory hairs. Bulbous, compound eyes glisten like oil slicks, and twitching mouthparts constantly taste the fetid air. 

The beetles feed upon the spores and organic detritus that drift through the stale atmosphere and blanket the obsidian plains. Lacking true wings, they fly by expelling noxious gases in controlled bursts from gill-like spores along the sides of their abdomen, jetting through the air in nausea-inducing lurches and spirals. They often alight on the carcasses of dead creatures to feed and lay their eggs, vomiting up corrosive digestive acids to dissolve decaying flesh into a nourishing slurry.

The cyrstalline pillars that jut from the blasted earth play host to an altogether different and more sessile breed of monstrosity. Great oozing, tubular things cling to the columns like nightmare slugs, some stretching over fifteen feet in length. Their translucent hides pulse and twitch, offering hazy glimpses of roiling internal organs and seething fluids in shades of livid purple and sickly bile. These almost plant-like horrors slowly siphon minerals and moisture from the structures they cling to with muscular, suction-tipped tendrils, growing bloated on their crystalline nutrients.

Occasionally, one of these pulsating slugs vomits up a mass of gelatinous, ovum-like spawn, which dangle from the sides of the pillars in bulging, membranous sacks or splatter on the ground in reeking, mutant-embryo-laden puddles. The larval young feed on their mother's nutrient-rich mucus and the organic films that stretch between some pillars before maturing and slowly slithering off to claim their own crystalline feeding grounds.

Other less identifiable organisms seethe in the crevices and hollows of this blasted hellscape. Mats of fungal tendrils sprout from the fissures in the obsidian ground, exuding clouds of choking spores. Lichen-like colonies of crusty grey and sallow green cling to the rocks, pulsing softly and emitting eerie, subsonic murmurs. Oozing, amoebic things crawl in the shadows, and skittering, spider-like creatures with branching crystalline limbs pick their way delicately across the glassy plains.

In this primordial horrorscape, the monstrous inhabitants are engaged in a never-ending dance of predator and prey, parasite and host, frenzied mating and ceaseless consumption. It is a realm where only the most tenacious and ruthlessly adapted creatures can hope to survive, an alien dystopia where Terran biology's worst nightmares have been given form in fang and distended organ and oozing, glistening flesh. The denizens of the obsidian plains stand as a testament to the infinite cruelty and inventiveness of a cosmos with no regard for human sanity.
