In the shattered remains of this ancient world, life persists in myriad strange and tenacious forms. The primary inhabitants of the floating cities are the Zephyrians - a species of slender, insectoid humanoids adapted to the thin atmosphere and low gravity.

Zephyrians stand roughly two meters tall, with elongated limbs and torsos covered in segmented, chitinous plates that range in color from rust-red to deep indigo. Their heads are angular and mantis-like, with large, compound eyes that shimmer iridescently and mandibles that click softly as they communicate. From their backs sprout two pairs of diaphanous, membranous wings that allow them to glide effortlessly between the city spires.

Zephyrian society is organized around the maintenance and expansion of their precarious cities. Different castes perform specialized roles: the Weavers spin and maintain the great cable webs, the Scavengers venture into the derelict factories to salvage resources, the Archivists preserve knowledge in great crystalline data stores, and the Traders barter goods and technologies between cities.

Despite the harsh environment, Zephyrians exhibit a rich culture interwoven with a deep reverence for their lost world. Their architecture seamlessly blends organic curves and geometric precision, with dwellings and public spaces carved into the pitted rock of the asteroids. Artisans craft intricate mosaics and sculptures from scavenged metal and glass, depicting scenes from their mythic past.

In the marketplaces, Zephyrians mingle with other species that have adapted to life among the ruins. Squat, heavyset creatures called Forgemasters salvage and repair ancient machines, their thick, heat-resistant hides branded with intricate patterns. Lithe, swift-moving beings known as Cablers assist the Weavers, scampering along the cables with gecko-like agility. And the mysterious Void Whisperers drift silently at the city fringes, attuned to the fading energies of the broken planet.

As the cities drift through the rust-hued sky, the Zephyrians cling to their fragile existence, driven by an ancestral memory of a world once vibrant and whole. In their rituals and stories, they honor the lost planet and dream of a future where they might restore some semblance of its former glory. But for now, they adapt, salvage, and persevere, their lives a testament to the indomitable persistence of life in the face of even the most cataclysmic entropy.
