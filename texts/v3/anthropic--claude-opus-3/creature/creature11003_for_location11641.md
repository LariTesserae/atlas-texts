In this teeming, ultra-verdant city, the true inhabitants are the plants themselves - if indeed "plants" is an adequate term for the photosynthetic beings that have claimed every inch from soil to sky. These are no ordinary flora, but organisms operating on an entirely different paradigm.

The very substance of the plants seems to shift and flow like a living protoplasm. Leaves meld seamlessly into stems, roots grasp and release like slowly undulating tentacles, flowers unfurl with yawning mouths that drip with strange nectar. The boundary between individual and collective is fluid; vines intertwine until it's impossible to say where one ends and another begins. 

Fungal networks spread in pulsing, bioluminescent webs, transmitting signals across the city in rippling, technicolor waves. Microbes swarm in complex clouds, engaging in elaborate dances as they cycle nutrients and break down matter. Bacteria sheathe every surface in glistening films, whispering secrets in chemical codes.

The plants themselves seem to communicate and coordinate in ways that defy scientific understanding. Towers grow and dissolve, streets seal over and reform, in response to stimuli imperceptible to any observer. Blossoms erupt in perfect synchronicity across disparate regions, then wither just as abruptly. It is a city in constant dialogue with itself.

Whorls of leaves cup pockets of spore-laden mist, spawning miniature garden-storms that swirl through the tangle of growth. Fruits swell to grotesque proportions before splitting open to disgorge seeds on mucus-slick wings. Vegetable tumors bulge from trunks and branches, pulsating softly to unknown rhythms.

Pollen hangs golden and thick in the humming air, clinging to every surface. Bubbles of pure oxygen wobble skyward from sighing leaf pores, vestiges of the city's constant photosynthetic rapture. Scents of growth, decay, sap and chlorophyll mingle in a heady, ever-shifting perfume.

In crevices and pools, new forms are constantly evolving, taking advantage of each novel microclimate. Unique symbioses and ecosystems arise, flourish briefly, then vanish as the city convulses through its ceaseless self-reinvention.

This place is not just a habitat but a vast, decentralized mind in a state of furious cogitation. The plants are neurons in a process of thought unimaginably beyond the human. The city does not sleep, does not pause, because the plants do not distinguish between growth and dreams. To enter here is to be subsumed in that eternally unfurling green vision.
