In the shifting shadows cast by the distant flickering stars and the flash of lightning above, strange figures can occasionally be glimpsed moving amidst the profusion of uncanny creatures swarming across the obsidian plain. 

Tall and gaunt, these beings seem to be composed of the same glistening volcanic glass as the ground beneath their feet. Angular facets make up their elongated limbs and joints, refracting the meager light into brief kaleidoscopic bursts as they stride through the shimmering mucus trails and skittering lesser entities.

Their bodies are sheathed in translucent silica, revealing pulsing networks of dimly bioluminescent fibers within, not unlike the tapestry-creatures that flap through the thick air. In place of faces, their heads are clusters of sensory filaments, waving and tasting the static-charged winds.

These creatures do not walk but rather flow across the plain, their gait an undulating glide as if floating over the glass. As they move, their forms subtly shift, with facets separating and realigning and inner fibers rerouting in mesmerizing fractal patterns. At times they fragment into multiple smaller versions of themselves, communicating in flashing pulses of colored light before merging back together.

They seem to shepherd and shape the profusion of life around them. With sweeping gestures of their long crystal arms, they gather swarms of fluttering moths into rippling curtains of wings. They trace sigils in the mucus trails that send slugs massing into pulsing towers. Beams of refracted light from their palms solidify the silica shells of the great crunching beasts.

The glass entities sing as they work, voices like the pure ringing tones of softly struck crystal chimes, weaving eerily through the background cacophony of clicks, thrums, and skittering. The shattering of obsidian beneath the lumbering colony creatures punctuates their song.

These living prisms seem to conduct the symphony of this nocturnal ecosystem, guiding each disparate voice to contribute to the greater harmonic whole. With each lightning strike, they raise their faceted arms to the roiling clouds, drawing down the electric potential into their fibers, which glow brighter in response.

As quickly as they appear, the entities step sideways into dark crevices in the plain and are gone, leaving only afterimage flashes of refracted color in the eyes of any who glimpsed them. The nature and purpose of these strange beings remains unclear. They may be this world's angels or its shepherds, lovingly tending to the myriad life that dances in the dark. Or perhaps they are gardeners of a sort, carefully maintaining an equilibrium in this primordial glass garden. 

They are a fleeting glimpse of order and intent amidst the chaotic fecundity of the obsidian sea. A reminder that even in the strangest corners of creation, in a realm of broken glass and eternal twilight, there are larger forces at work shaping what might appear to mortal eyes as simple cosmic chance. The truth of their existence is just one more mystery singing out into the depths of an endlessly miraculous universe.
