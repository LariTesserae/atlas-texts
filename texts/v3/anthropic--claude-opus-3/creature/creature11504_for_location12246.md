In the pulsing, churning heart of this monstrous submerged city, strange and unholy things writhe and slither through the twisting conduits and labyrinthine passages of the machine-realm. 

Vast shoals of biomechanical horrors undulate through the turbid waters - nightmare hybrids of meat, metal and unholy energies unleashed by the warping of reality. Their forms defy easy classification; each a unique abomination shaped by the eldritch forces seething at the sunken city's core. Gobbets of pallid, luminous flesh cling to pulsing techno-organic frames. Insectile limbs and twitching tentacles of glistening chrome sprout from puckered, throbbing orifices. Swarms of buzzing, clicking, hovering things with too many eyes and pulsing crystalline protuberances flit and dance around the larger horrors, their forms ever-shifting.

Among the horrors, solitary figures can sometimes be glimpsed scuttling furtively through the corroded wreckage and skeletal remains of the city that was. These are the last descendants of the city's once-human architects, their bodies and minds warped by generations of exposure to the reality-bending energies and mutagenic substances churned out by the machines they once built. Now they are twisted parodies of humanity, their spindly, elongated forms encrusted with chitinous machine growths, clusters of lidless milky eyes peering from misshapen sockets, and spidery metal limbs grafted to suppurating flesh. They gibber and howl through obliterated throats and loll long prehensile tongues as they cavort and squabble over the choicest morsels of scavenged tech. Theirs is an endless scrabbling, feral existence amid the ruins, eking out a meager survival as the cancerous machine-growth slowly subsumes and digests the last traces of their ancestral home.

Deeper still, in the very heart of the machine-hive, titanic monstrosities slumber, fused with the pulsing machinery. Mountain-sized masses of undulating flesh studded with compound eyes, dangling proboscises, and forests of twitching grasping limbs. Great gelatinous oozes of throbbing protoplasm, veined with pulsing cables and suffused with flickering malevolent lights. Immense pulsing brains afloat in bubbling vats of mucous-thick fluid, their glistening gyri and sulci flickering with diseased machine-dreams as reality cracks and splits around them. These unnatural titans are the dread gods of the machine realm - transhuman abominations of ungodly sentience merged with the very fabric of their mechanical domain. Their alien thoughts reshape the impossible city like the fever dreams of a lunatic god, warping flesh, machine, and the very laws of nature to their unfathomable whims.
