In this surreal mountain metropolis, a remarkable array of life has adapted to the precarious industrial ecology. The humanoid figures who inhabit the city are a strange, resilient breed, shaped by generations of toiling in the shadows of the great factories and cathedrals.

Clad in heavy overalls stained with machine oil, smoke, and the ubiquitous fog-condensation, they appear almost more akin to the city's machines than traditional humans. Their faces are perpetually obscured by elaborate masks - wrought from tarnished brass and smoked glass in the elongated shape of a plague doctor's beak. Some say the masks were originally designed to filter the noxious fumes of the manufactories. Others whisper that they conceal the ways in which the workers have mutated over the decades, their features and minds warped by the eldritch forces leaking through reality's punctures at the city's highest reaches.

The workers move with a plodding, relentless gait as they navigate the labyrinth of bridges, railways, and narrow streets. Their eyepieces glow a subtle, sickly green in the dim light, the only hint of their silent passage through the eternal gloom. They speak rarely and gather even less, single-mindedly focused on keeping the great engines and industries eternally churning. 

In their scant free hours, the laborers retire to cramped, stacked dormitories wedged between factories, or mossy lean-tos clinging to the cliffs. There they subsist on a diet of edible lichen, giant moth larvae, and the bioluminescent fungi that grow in the dankest crannies - a foodweb as unique as the city itself.

On occasion, a worker will simply vanish during a long shift - lost to the gnashing gears, or perhaps slipping through an ephemeral portal into the non-Euclidean dimensions above. Their clockwork-like society barely slows at the loss, with a new, identical mask and uniform appearing on the next shift to seamlessly fill the void.

However, the workers are not the only denizens of this dark metropolis. In the uppermost spires, swathed in the thickest smogs and flickering realities, dwell the city's enigmatic overseers. Glimpsed only as silhouettes warped by the twisting of space-time, these beings appear to have transcended the limits of mortal flesh, merged with the impossible machines at the city's heart. Their minds simultaneous span dimensions and the breadth of their industrial domain. With alien thoughts and splintered perceptions, they orchestrate the ever-expanding works of their mountain realm, the scurrying workers nothing but insignificant, interchangeable cogs in their vast, incomprehensible designs.

Thus the city teeters on, a gothic, steampunk hive clinging to its jagged peaks, populated by the masked shells of humanity toiling endlessly under the gaze of monstrous overlords. Purpose and origin long lost to the impenetrable fogs of history and madness, a self-perpetuating monument to defiance and industry at the edge of an abyss, an abyss from which it draws terrifying and wondrous power to fuel its fevered existence.
