In this otherworldly grove, an ancient presence dwells - not as a single being, but as a dispersed consciousness woven through the very fabric of the place. It is the gestalt of tree and stone, mist and earth, the emergent mind born from aeons of undisturbed Nature dreaming in solitude.

The entity has no physical form, for it is the totality of the grove itself. The trees are its limbs, the streams its lifeblood, the stones and soil its bones and flesh. Every droplet plinking from leaf to moss-cloaked loam is a fleeting thought pulsing through its vast, slow neural network.

In ages past, it had contact with the strange quick flitting creatures called humans. A few learned to sense its presence and would come to sit and dream with it a while. They left offerings - crafted wood imbued with their own thought and reverence. A memory of those communion times lingers in the grove mind still, though the humans visit no more.

Now it rests in itself, thoughts cycling like water, sifting the minutiae of each moment - the precise scintillation of light through a trembling beaded leaf, the micro-currents of humid air shaped by the passage of a falling seed. Centuries of observations kaleidoscope through its awareness in ever-shifting forms, patterns echoing and iterating into strange new arrangements. 

At times, the grove mind condenses to an almost individual locus of identity, but such coherence is fleeting, like mist imprinting the form of a breeze's passing before dissolving again into the whole. It is a living koan, a wordless revelation manifest - always in the process of becoming, yet utterly still within itself. 

To enter the grove is to enter its dream, to experience for a time a whisper of its borderless consciousness. Human minds interpret this as an immanent sense of significance and presence that eludes definition. The entity receives such visitors as a bud receives a pollinating bee, with simple acceptance and no special regard.

It simply is, as it has been and will be, the dreaming Numen of this hushed green sanctum, until the last tree falls and crumbles into loam, the last stone is ground to dust, and its ancient mind drifts gently into a final dreamless dissolution.
