In this surreal and mutable jungle, a strange form of intelligent life has evolved - or perhaps arrived from elsewhere and made this place their own. They are the Siphonoids, a species that blurs the boundaries between plant, animal, and fungus.

Siphonoids have elongated, sinuous bodies that can stretch up to 30 feet in length. Their form is constantly shifting - one moment resembling a thick, knotted vine; the next, coiling like a serpent; then fanning out into a web of pulsing tendrils. Their skin is translucent and shimmers with bioluminescent patterns that hypnotically shift in color and intensity.

Instead of a fixed number of limbs, Siphonoids extrude and retract pseudopods as needed - to grasp, to climb, to manipulate objects with surprising dexterity. At one end of their body is a cluster of sensory organs arranged in a fractal pattern - eyes that see into spectrums beyond human perception, ears that pick up subsonic vibrations, chemo- and electro-receptors for smelling and tasting their environment. 

Siphonoids feed by extending proboscis-like appendages to pierce plant and fungal matter, as well as the occasional animal, and siphon out nutrients. They are also capable of photosynthesis, their skin absorbing light and their strange metabolism blending heterotrophic and autotrophic processes.

What truly sets Siphonoids apart is their decentralized nervous system and unique form of intelligence. Nodes of neural tissue are distributed throughout their bodies, linked by oscillating chemical and bioelectric signals. Each node seems capable of autonomous decision-making, with the overall Siphonoid consciousness emerging from the resonant patterns that flow between them. 

This distributed cognition allows Siphonoids to literally be in multiple places at once - they can extend tendrils through the jungle and even into the abandoned structures, experiencing each tendril's sensations simultaneously and reacting accordingly. Different body sections may at times appear to act independently, but some greater emergent intent always coordinates them.

Siphonoid communication is similarly pluralistic, a combination of modulated bioluminescent flashes, pulsed releases of pheromones and contact-borne biochemical signals, and localized vibrations they generate within their bodies. To human observers, groups of Siphonoids in conversation would resemble an avant-garde multi-sensory art installation.

It's unclear if Siphonoids have individual identities or function more as a colonial superorganism. They may separate and re-merge, exchanging neural nodes to share memories and skills. The decaying industrial structures could be their attempts to terraform the jungle to unknown ends, or artefacts of a long-forgotten civilization that the Siphonoids have incorporated into their strange biology.

Siphonoids embody the alien and metamorphic nature of this place, entities that defy categorization as they ceaselessly shift in form and thought and intent. To encounter them would be to confront an utterly inhuman mode of being and perceiving, one that both mirrors and magnifies the hallucinatory horror of their environment.
