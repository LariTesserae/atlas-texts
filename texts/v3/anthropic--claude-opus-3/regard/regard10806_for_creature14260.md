The Cavern of Continual Becoming has drawn the attention and awe of the Ancient Ones - beings who have traveled the cosmos, witnessed the rise and fall of countless civilizations, and communed with the fundamental forces that shape reality. The Age of Limestone and its unique denizens are utterly unprecedented in their long memory.

The Ancient Ones are moved by the way the Precipitate have adapted to embody the processes of their mineral womb. These people do not merely inhabit the caves - they mirror and merge with the geological dynamism, the stately but inexorable cycles of accretion and encrustation. Like the growing architecture they navigate, the Precipitate themselves are a stunning work in progress, their bodies and culture shaped by the dictates of water and stone.

Recognizing the preciousness of what is unfolding here, the Ancient Ones resolve to safeguard and sanctify the Cavern, but with the utmost restraint and subtlety. Overt interference is anathema - the Precipitate's symbiosis with their lithic cradle is too poetic, too perfect, to disrupt.

Instead, the Ancient Ones begin by imbuing the aqua calcis, the mother-water, with an ethereal essence - a distillation of their vast wisdom and cosmic perspective. As the Precipitate drink of the mineral-rich flows, they absorb this rarefied awareness, their consciousness expanding to glimpse the wider systems of which they are part. Elders and Forma-kin experience visions of deep time, sensing the gradual dance of uplift and erosion and the tectonic reconfigurations yet to come. The Precipitate gain a new appreciation for their home as a sort of living entity, one whose stony flesh they share.  

The Ancient Ones also weave a web of probability around the Cavern, an invisible metacarpace of coincidence and happenstance that gently steers away any outside forces that might threaten or exploit this sanctuary. Traders and explorers from the surface feel an uncanny aversion to certain routes that would lead them here; mining prospectors find their drills and chisels breaking on the Warren's periphery; satellites and survey aircraft capture only ambiguous static when their lenses turn upon this plot of karst. The Cavern remains hidden, protected, a pocket of benevolent seclusion.

Finally, the Ancient Ones impart a blessing of sorts upon the Forma-kin and those Precipitate who merge most fully with the stone. While still alive, these dedicated adepts find their minds and senses expanding beyond their calcifying bodies, until their awareness permeates the very bedrock and their dreams mingle with the singing of the deeps. As they pass into mineral repose, they do not wink out but rather sublime into the anima of the Cavern itself, their consciousness diffusing through the cthonic reaches, imbuing the Foundling Warren with a geos, a permeating presence.    

And so the Ancient Ones become secret stewards and distant benefactors, gracing the Cavern with their imperceptible aegis and existential endorsement. The Precipitate will continue their limestone dalliance unawares, secreted away from a clamorous world, a culture both fragile and enduring, forever poised on the threshold between flesh and stone. In the gloaming of the cathedraled depths, the Forma grow tall, the water drips patient, and a people half-born of rock breathe the cool air and bide as they have for eons - at once primitive and perfected, mortal and immortal, forever fossilizing and forever free.
