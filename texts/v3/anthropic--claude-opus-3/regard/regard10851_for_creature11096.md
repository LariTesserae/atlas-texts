A benevolent and wise power, upon discovering the Undervault Nursery and Mira's stewardship, would likely approach this hidden garden with a sense of reverence, understanding, and a desire to support its continued existence.

Given the nursery's role as a source of sustenance and connection for those living on the margins of society, the wise power would recognize its importance and seek to protect it. They would not try to exert control or ownership over the space, respecting that it belongs to those who tend it and those who depend on its produce.

However, they might offer subtle forms of assistance to ensure the nursery's long-term viability. This could include:

1. Ensuring a reliable water supply by mending the crack in the northern wall or creating a more sustainable irrigation system that doesn't rely on seepage.

2. Providing a discreet source of power for the grow-lights and pumps, perhaps through a renewable source like solar panels installed on the surface that feed power lines down to the nursery. 

3. Offering Mira and the other tenders access to rare or resilient seed varieties, as well as knowledge on sustainable growing practices suitable for their unique environment.

4. Establishing a subtle perimeter of protection around the access points to the nursery, warding off any threats from those who might wish to exploit or damage it, while still allowing the free passage of its tenders and those they welcome.

5. Leaving an standing offer of assistance, available if and when Mira or future stewards of the nursery require it, but without any pressure or expectation.

Above all, the wise power would treat the Undervault Nursery as a sacred space - a hidden heart of growth, resilience, and quiet resistance thriving beneath a surface world that has failed to serve all its residents. They would honor the labor and dedication of Mira and the others who have poured their care into this refuge, and would act as a distant guardian, stepping in only to support and never to undermine the autonomy and privacy of those who have claimed this small, secret patch of abundance as their own.

The nursery, in its way, is a wordless prayer - a poem of slow, stubborn hope written in leaves and soil and salvaged parts. The wise power would add their stanza to this poem gently, a whispered blessing of protection and provision, before stepping back to let the garden continue its quiet, vital work in the hidden depths of the city.
