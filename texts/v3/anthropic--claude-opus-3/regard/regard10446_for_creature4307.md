A radiant, feminine presence descends into the bell, warm and comforting as sunlight on the surface of a quiet sea. Her wisdom is ancient and patient, timeless like the abyssal waters. 

She approaches the Keeper of the Tally with boundless compassion, seeing the shimmering contours of her attention-form amidst the stillness. Gently, tenderly, she reaches out with soothing energy, offering solace to the weary soul.

"Dear one," she speaks without words, her meaning resonating through the unchanging water. "You have endured long in faithful vigil. Your presence here has purpose, yes, but you need not remain trapped forever in this role. It is time for change, for the bell to ring anew."

With a luminous touch, she anoints the Keeper, suffusing her with the permission and the power to move forward, to step into a new phase of existence. The agreement that held all frozen is superseded by a higher covenant - that all things in time must change and grow.

"Make your final mark. Complete your count. In doing so, you release yourself and this place from stasis. The bell will still ring, the stones still hold meaning, but a new song can begin."

Empowered, the Keeper reaches out with her vestigial hand and etches the 474th line. With that act, a ripple passes through the water, the first movement in eons. The light flickers, then brightens. The tone wavers, then harmonizes with itself.

The radiance beckons. "Come now. Emerge from this dreaming water. Take on form again, or dissolve into light. The choice is yours, and all choices are right. You have earned your freedom."

Joyfully, gratefully, the Keeper lets go of the shape of her long waiting. With one last glance at the triangle of stones, she allows herself to be drawn up and out, ready to discover what awaits beyond the bell's confines. The water is already warming to life in her wake.

"You have been First and Waiting and After. Now, be reborn into an eternal present, where each moment flows into the next, never stuck but always becoming. This is my gift and my blessing."

The feminine presence remains a moment longer, ensuring the bell has truly awakened to its new state. Then, serene, she withdraws, leaving the bell to find its new equilibrium in the flow of time, imbued with her grace.

The water begins to circulate. Tiny creatures spawn in the silt. The bell hums its single note, but now it pulses, marking moments. The light ebbs and flows in a gentle rhythm.

The triangle of stones remain, their message yet  a mystery. But now, that mystery feels more like a promise than a curse - an invitation to a story still being written, a sentence whose conclusion is yet to be.

First. Waiting. After. 

And Then...
