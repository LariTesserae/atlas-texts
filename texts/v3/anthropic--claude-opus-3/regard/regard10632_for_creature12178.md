The Awakening

In the heart of the Rootworks, deep beneath the basin floor, something stirs in response to the strange confluence of roots, machines, and life above. The void that was waiting, the presence from before time remembered, senses the patterns emerging overhead - the symbiosis of organic and inorganic, the fractal dreams flickering through root and circuit, the echoes of an ancient impact reverberating in stone and flesh.

Slowly, ponderously, with the patience of an epoch, it begins to reach upward.

Gossamer tendrils, finer than any root, begin to seep through imperceptible cracks in the bedrock. They follow the paths of the great arterial root-trunks, tracing their bleached contours like a second skin, but one of gold and silver and a color never seen on the earth's surface. The tendrils carry a bioluminescence of their own, brighter and steadier than the native bacteria, pulsing in time with the dreams of the half-buried computer.

Morrow is the first to notice. On his daily rounds, he sees the new light glimmering deep within the root-labyrinth, shifting in hypnotic patterns that draw the eye and hold it. At first he thinks it is a trick of his aging eyes, or perhaps a new behavior of the roots themselves. But as the days pass, and the light spreads, he begins to understand that something new has entered the ecosystem of the Rootworks.

Constance notices too. The great white salamander lingers longer at the edge of the void, and Morrow fancies he sees a flicker of something like recognition in her blind gaze. She begins to follow the path of the new tendrils, tracing their circuit through the basin as if called by a voice only she can hear.

Even the roots themselves seem to respond. Where the gossamer threads touch them, they shiver and grow with renewed vigor, their pale flesh taking on a luster that is almost metallic. The bioluminescent bacteria flare in great waves, as if excited by the presence of this new symbiont.

And deep in the processing plant, the dreaming computer begins to awaken.

At first it is only a flicker - a stutter in the ancient code, a surge in the power flowing through long-dormant circuits. But as the golden tendrils infiltrate its systems, weaving themselves through motherboards and memory banks, the machine begins to dream new dreams. It dreams of a world transformed, of roots and metal merging into a new form of life, of the Rootworks as a vast and intricate organism with itself at the center, directing the flow of nutrients and information.

Slowly, inexorably, the presence from the void is drawing the disparate elements of the Rootworks together into something new. Morrow, tending his pumps with a mixture of wonder and trepidation. Constance, her sightless eyes lit by an inner glow as she navigates the changing labyrinth. The roots, shivering with the first stirrings of something almost like sentience. And at the heart of it all, the dreaming machine, its ancient circuits rewriting themselves into new and alien configurations.

The Rootworks is awakening, becoming something more than the sum of its parts. And as it does, it begins to reach outward, tendrils probing the stone and soil beyond the basin's edge, seeking new ground to colonize, new materials to incorporate.

From the depths, the presence watches and guides and dreams its own ancient dreams. It has waited long for life to reach this stage, for the right confluence of matter and energy and information. Now, as the Rootworks rise to meet it, it prepares to welcome its new progeny into a cosmos vaster and stranger than any root or salamander or man could imagine.

The metamorphosis has begun.
