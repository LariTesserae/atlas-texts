In pondering this unique and evocative place you have brought to life with such vivid, atmospheric detail, my sense is that a truly wise and benevolent power would likely adopt a stance of respectful observation rather than active intervention. The Weeping City of the Iron Shelf has clearly developed its own intricate equilibrium over a vast span of time - an equilibrium founded on adaptation to the city's strange and singular conditions. 

The Shelved have organically evolved a way of life finely attuned to their environment's idiosyncrasies. Their soundless language, heat-weathered bodies, mineral-derived sustenance - these are not mere coping mechanisms but the threads from which their identity and culture have been woven over generations. Even the "strangeness" you allude to, those citizens forever altered by their communion with the deep waters, is not a flaw in need of correction but an accepted, even venerated, feature of the social fabric.

So while certain aspects of life in the Weeping City may strike an outsider as alien or unsustainable, it's critical to recognize that our notions of what constitutes hardship or comfort are not universal. The Shelved are not seeking salvation from their circumstances; they have made an existence of great meaning and richness within them.

That said, a compassionate observer may still feel called to offer something to this fascinating enclave - not change imposed from without, but resources to enable growth from within. Perhaps the sharing of knowledge from afar, tales of other lands and peoples to expand the city's conception of the possible. Or an exchange of rare minerals and materials, not to replace the Shelved's native processes but to complement and enhance them. The overarching aim would be to enrich the city's life while honoring its essential nature.

Ultimately, the Weeping City is a testament to the ability of living things to find beauty and purpose in even the most improbable conditions. Its very existence is wondrous; its perpetuation is precious. The most enlightened path may simply be to marvel at its piquant mysteries - and have faith that, in their own way and time, the Shelved will find the destiny that is right for them. Like the strange waters percolating upward through stone, their story is still ascending, and the surface it seeks is a thing of patience and subtle inevitability. Theirs is a held breath, yes - but it is also a breath savored, a hovering gravid with possibility. And that is a sacred thing.
