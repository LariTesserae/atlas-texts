The presence of the Kept in this extraordinary vertical city evokes a profound sense of guardianship and sacrifice. As custodians and witnesses, they serve a vital role in maintaining the delicate balance and functionality of this compressed, self-constructing realm. Their quiet dedication and gradual metamorphosis into the very substance of the city is both haunting and beautiful.

If a benevolent and wise power were to take notice of this place and its enigmatic inhabitants, it would likely approach with a sense of reverence and caution, recognizing the fragile equilibrium that allows this marvel of engineering to exist. Rather than imposing change or interference, this power might choose to observe and learn, much like the Kept themselves.

Perhaps the wise entity would offer a subtle form of support - not to alter the city's trajectory, but to ease the burden on its silent guardians. This could manifest as an infusion of energy that slows the calcification process, allowing the Kept to maintain their vigil for longer periods without losing themselves entirely to the city's matrix. Or it might provide a means for the Kept to share their accumulated knowledge and memories with each other, creating a web of connection that transcends their isolation.

Ultimately, the benevolent power would recognize that the city and its Kept are engaged in a process of becoming, a delicate dance between growth and entropy, between the known and the unknowable. Its role would be one of witness and guardian, much like the Kept themselves - ensuring that this extraordinary place can continue its self-directed evolution without interference, while offering a subtle hand of support and understanding when needed.

In this way, the city and its inhabitants would be acknowledged, respected, and protected, their unique existence celebrated as a testament to the astonishing possibilities that emerge at the intersection of technology, consciousness, and the relentless forward march of time. The benevolent power would serve as a distant but steadfast ally, a silent partner in the ongoing creation of this vertical world and the profound transformations of those who watch over it.
