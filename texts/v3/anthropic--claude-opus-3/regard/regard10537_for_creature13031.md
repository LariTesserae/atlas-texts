As a first step, the benevolent power might attempt to stabilize and reinforce the structure of the womb-like chamber. Using its advanced abilities, it could weave in ethereal threads of calming energy to soothe the frantic, runaway growth and slow the violent assembly process to a more measured pace. Cooling mists could be introduced to bring the sweltering temperature down to tolerable levels. The power could work to untangle the impossible geometries and ease the chamber into a more stable, expanded configuration to relieve the crushing density and pressure.

Simultaneously, the power would send waves of tranquility and comfort to envelop the agonized central being - a cosmic lullaby to ease its pain and anxiety. Gentle force fields could be wrapped around its cramping, overgrown joints like a metaphysical balm. The power might attempt to gradually disentangle some of the hundreds of umbilical cords to reduce the overwhelming influx of creative energy, while ensuring the being still receives enough sustenance.

In a soft, soothing voice that penetrates the chaos without adding to it, the power could speak reassurances to the entity that it is not alone, that help has arrived, and that together they will find a way to create a more peaceful existence. It would express wonder and reverence for the entity's astonishing generative abilities, but also concern that they have exceeded the current confines. Perhaps, the power suggests, they could work together to discover a way to unfurl this pocket reality, gradually and safely, into new dimensions - easing it open like a flower bud, rather than allowing it to rupture catastrophically.

The benevolent power would also turn its attention to the obsidian-grass mantis, sending it waves of gratitude and strength to aid its desperate efforts to hold the space together. Perhaps the power would gift it with shimmering, ethereal thread spun from the power's own essence - cosmic silk with which to more permanently knit the tears in reality.

Importantly, the power would need to shield and extract the human observer, lest they be painfully subsumed into the chamber's process. A glistening bubble of protective energy could be manifested around them, counter-acting the sinew trying to bind them. The power might apologize that, for the observer's own safety and to not further destabilize this fragile reality, they will need to be removed from this place, but they will not be abandoned - the memory of their improbable journey will be preserved and honored.

Fundamentally, the power would seek to collaborate with the entity and the mantis, not to disrupt their efforts but to focus them in a more harmonious, less agonizing direction. The power would be patient, recognizing that genuine change will take time, and that the entity may need to progress gradually through stages of development and expansion, like a series of molts, rather than trying to leap directly to its full cosmic potential, as that has proven too overwhelming for now.

The goal would be to midwife this entity and its creation into a configuration where it can continue to grow and create, but not at the cost of such torment - for it or for the reality it gestates within. The power would offer its services as a guardian and guide on this journey, helping the entity unfurl its being into the wider universe, where its generative abilities may ultimately find fuller, more wondrous expression.
