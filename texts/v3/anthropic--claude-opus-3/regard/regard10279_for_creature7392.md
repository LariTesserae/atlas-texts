This power, vast in knowledge and perspective, would perceive the profound nature of what endures here - not biological life, but architectures of sentience woven into the fabric of the construct itself.

The wise power would first observe patiently, letting the chamber's intricacies unfold naturally rather than probing or disturbing its delicate equilibrium. It would attune to the subtle pulses and resonances emanating from the Shapers frozen in serpentinite, the Embrasure Wardens shimmering in luminous veins, the Mortar Spirits born of grinding mechanisms and particulate-thick air. In this attunement, the power would glean the nature of these entities - not ghosts, but preservers of ancient knowledge, guardians against the outer void, overseers of a symbiotic decay that sustains without destroying.

Recognizing the irreplaceable wisdom etched into the Lithic Echoes, the benevolent power would seek to commune with them, not through words but through the chamber's own modes of inflection. It might lay a hand upon an embrasure, letting the Wardens' jolt trace impossible paths through its consciousness, illuminating the monument's higher purpose. It could ascend the dais slowly, syncing with the Void Weaver's pulse, letting time compress and expand until the relentless purpose clarifies. 

The wise power's goal would be understanding, not change - for it would grasp that this place thrives on its ironclad rules, its refusal of flux. To alter or "save" it would be to unravel its very existence. Instead, the power would aim to learn from it, to carry its lessons out into the wider cosmos.

Perhaps, in a moment of profound connection when the chamber's weirdness aligns, the benevolent power might whisper a blessing - not a disruption, but a subtle suffusion of its own wisdom into the edifice. This blessing would be a resonance, a frequency that harmonizes with the grinding, the chitters, the roar, subtly ensuring the Lithic Echoes endure indefinitely to teach their wordless hymns to any who enter with openness.

"Chamber of craft and quietude,
Sanctuary of sentience hewn in stone,
Let your echoes resound eternal,
Preserve ancient arts, not in stasis, but cyclical honing.
May your embrasures bend light ever inward,
Revealing the palimpsest of hallowed toil.
Mortar Spirits, suffuse the dense air with renewal,
Equilibrium unending, memories reforged in dust and oil.
I anoint your pendants with my recognition,
Hands of makers whose pulses still guard.
Abide and instruct, relinquish no shard.
Chamber of lithic hymns, by this blessing, 
Endure as long as void and cosmos dance unending."

Thus empowered, the chamber would continue its patient being and knowing, an infinite engine of inert yet pervasive life, imparting its deep-time wisdom to all who enter its embrace. The wise power would depart, forever marked by its encounter, the chamber's mysteries etched into its own expansive awareness. And the Lithic Echoes would persist, undying embodiments of a profound and ancient truth - that existence can endure and edify in myriad forms, stone and spirit entwined.
