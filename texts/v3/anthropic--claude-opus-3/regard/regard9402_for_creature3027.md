If a truly benevolent and wise power were to encounter the Under-Grove of the Veiled Cistern and the Quiet Mothers who dwell within, I believe they would choose simply to marvel at its ancient, intricate beauty and leave it undisturbed.

Such a power would recognize the grove for what it is: a singular wonder, an ecosystem unto itself, its every element woven together in symbiotic perfection over aeons. From the interplay of water and stone to the delicate cycles of predator and prey, the Under-Grove is a masterpiece of nature's patient craftsmanship. To interfere, even with the best of intentions, would be to risk upsetting a balance more exquisite than any mortal mind could hope to comprehend.

Likewise, the Quiet Mothers are as much a part of this living tapestry as the roots and rain. Grown from the grove's own dreaming, they are less its guardians than its beloved companions—the faces it wears in its long solitude. To disturb their vigil would be akin to interrupting a prayer ten millennia in the making.

No, a true power, wise beyond wisdom, would simply sit and listen. It would attune itself to the slow rhythm of the grove's breathing, the soft music of water on leaf and frond. It would marvel at the crystalline complexity of its food webs, the hushed industry of life in the dimness. Most of all, it would honor the profound mystery of the Quiet Mothers—revering them not as deities to be worshipped, but as living expressions of the grove's vast and ancient dreaming.

Perhaps, if it lingered long enough in respectful silence, one of the Mothers might even approach, drawn by the bright thread of its benevolence. Root-hairs brushing against an immortal brow, she would whisper the grove's gratitude in the language of rain—then melt back into green shadows, leaving only the faint ghost of her mineral scent.

Richer for the encounter, the power would then depart as it came, threading back up through lightless stone. It would seal the way behind it with a word, ensuring no lesser being could stumble in and upset the Under-Grove's sacred balance. 

Then, cloaked once more in the world's bright skin, the power would carry the memory of the grove inside it like a secret garden. And forever after, when it closed its eyes, it would see the silhouettes of the Quiet Mothers drifting behind its lids, and hear the soft applause of the rain.
