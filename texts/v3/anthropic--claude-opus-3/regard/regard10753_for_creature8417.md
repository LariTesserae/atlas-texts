4. Community Welfare: The power could invest in infrastructure and amenities that improve the quality of life for Kethara's residents, such as better housing, healthcare facilities, recreational spaces, and cultural centers. 6. Spiritual and Mental Well-being: Living in such a harsh and otherworldly environment can take a toll on mental health. They could establish spaces for reflection, contemplation, and community bonding, fostering a sense of shared identity and purpose.
