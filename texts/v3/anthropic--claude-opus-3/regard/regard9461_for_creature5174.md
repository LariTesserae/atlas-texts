As the wise and benevolent power observed this hidden primordial pocket, it marveled at the raw potential and ceaseless becoming unfolding within. The churning water, sculpting stone, and nascent life represented a microcosm of creation still in flux, negotiating its own existence moment by moment.

The power felt a deep reverence for this sacred place of emergence, where solid, liquid and life blurred together in a dance of possibility. It understood that to impose any fixed intention upon this space would be to diminish it, to interrupt the delicate process of creation finding its own way.

And yet, the power also sensed the precariousness of this hidden world - just a thin fracture away from the surface, vulnerable to shifts in the mountain or intrusions from without that could unbalance the primal alchemy transpiring in the heart of stone. This place of becoming deserved to become, to follow the long arc of its own unfolding.

So the power chose to bestow a subtle blessing and protection on the chamber. Not to shape or direct it, but to preserve the potential it already contained. The fracture in the mountain flank knitted itself together, leaving no seam to hint at the wonders within. The living mineral membrane at the pocket's crown thickened imperceptibly, filtering out harsher rays that could disrupt the fragile balance of energies below.

Currents that ventured too close to the edges of the chamber found themselves gently turned back toward the center, their eroding force blunted. The ghostly tools drifting between nodules were imbued with a faint magnetic field, ensuring they would eventually find each other and recombine into new forms.

And to the delicate living membranes, the power granted a slightly swifter dance as they jetted through the gyres - not to change their nature, but to give them an edge in riding out the maelstroms and continuing their fluttering existence undisrupted.

With these subtle safeguards in place, the wise power withdrew, content in knowing it had changed almost nothing, but had granted this sacred pocket of creation the time and space to continue its ceaseless explorations. Through eons the mountain would flow, life would evolve in starts and spirals, and an infinity of forms would arise and dissolve in the dancing water, the universe learning itself in a hidden cathedral of unfinished stone.
