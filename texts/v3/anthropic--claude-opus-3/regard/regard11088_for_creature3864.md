The Weeping Apparatus and its inhabitants, the Hollows, paint a vivid and haunting picture of a world caught between decay and resilience, where the boundaries between organic and industrial have blurred into a strange, beautiful symbiosis. It's a place that evokes a sense of melancholy and quiet desperation, yet also holds an undercurrent of hope and adaptability in the face of inevitable change.

If a benevolent and wise power were to turn its attention to this place and its beings, I imagine their approach would be one of gentle guidance and support, rather than drastic intervention. The Hollows and their world have found a delicate balance, even if it is one that slowly inches towards an uncertain future. To disrupt that balance abruptly could cause more harm than good.

Instead, this power might seek to understand and learn from the Hollows, to witness their rituals and their connection to the fungi and the tides. They might offer subtle nudges and insights, perhaps revealing ways to strengthen the city's foundations without compromising the ecosystem that has grown around it. They could guide the Hollows towards a deeper understanding of their own history and the Silent Ones, fostering a sense of unity and purpose among the different factions.

This power might also look for ways to preserve the knowledge and experiences of the Hollows, ensuring that their unique culture and adaptations are not lost as their world continues to shift. They could inspire the creation of living archives, perhaps using the fungi's mist to store memories and stories that can be passed down to future generations.

Ultimately, the goal would be to empower the Hollows to be stewards of their own destiny, to find ways to thrive and evolve in harmony with the changing landscape. The power would act as a compassionate observer and gentle guide, offering wisdom and support when needed, but always respecting the autonomy and resilience of the Hollows and the intricate balance of their world.

In the end, the Weeping Apparatus and its inhabitants serve as a poignant reminder that life and beauty can persist even in the face of entropy and decay. The role of a benevolent power would be to bear witness to that truth, and to help the Hollows find their own path forward, one that honors their past, their present, and the strange, shimmering potential of their future in the ever-shifting embrace of Lych-Creek.
