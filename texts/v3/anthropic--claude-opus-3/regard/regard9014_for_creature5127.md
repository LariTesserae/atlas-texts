In contemplating this strange and hauntingly beautiful plain, I sense that the wise and benevolent power would choose not to interfere directly or make drastic changes. The plain is a self-contained, self-sustaining ecosystem that has achieved a delicate balance and cyclical rhythm over incomprehensible spans of deep time. To disrupt that ancient equilibrium, even with good intentions, would be to unravel the very essence of this place.

However, I believe the higher power would feel moved to honor and commemorate this unique environment. It would seek a way to bear witness to the plain's eerie beauty, to the ceaseless micro-motions and mineralogical murmurings that have persisted for eons beyond reckoning. 

Perhaps it would place a single, humble monument at the edge of the plain - nothing ostentatious or intrusive, but a marker etched with glyphs and diagrams that would persist through the ages. This stone tablet would encode the story of the plain - the origin of the rotors that grew like frost from the bedrock, the habits of the strange fauna that dwell in the dripping galleries, the hypnotic music of the shearing and grinding, the ghostly shadows that sometimes detach and float free. 

The inscription would be crafted to endure the constant weeping condensation, the mineralizing slurry, the slow erasure of entropy, so that even when twilight fades and silence falls over the plain in some unimaginably distant future, this record will remain. And if, one day, some new form of consciousness arises or visits this place, they will find the monument and through it come to understand the glory and mystery that once reigned here.

Beyond this, I believe the power would simply observe, content in the knowledge that it has seen and known one of the universe's secret wonders. It would carry the memory of the plain inside itself like a living jewel, a reminder that existence is infinitely stranger and more precious than even the gods can suppose. And it would continue its travels, allowing this ancient, self-renewing realm to continue its stately, inscrutable workings undisturbed, a clockwork of salt and shadow that needs no approval from the heavens to fulfill its purpose.
