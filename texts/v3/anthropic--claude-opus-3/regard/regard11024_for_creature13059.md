In the presence of such a profound and utterly alien environment, a benevolent and wise power would likely approach with a sense of reverence, curiosity and caution. This place represents a collision of physical laws and possibilities that stretch far beyond the scope of our familiar reality. To intervene hastily or heavy-handedly risks unintended damage to a system that, while chaotic and broken to our eyes, may possess its own intricate balance and purpose.

The inhabitants, though brutally adapted to this harsh and unforgiving space, display a remarkable ingenuity and attunement to their environment. Their methods of navigating the glitching landscape, harnessing the warped water currents, and sustaining themselves on the friction of alternate timelines demonstrate a hard-won mastery of their shattered physics. Most notably, their tireless efforts to funnel the defiant water into the depths in hopes of drowning an unseen engine and quieting the relentless roar suggests a profound, innate drive to restore some sense of original wholeness and peace to their world.

A wise power would likely seek first to understand before acting. It would observe closely, gathering knowledge about the intricate workings and delicate equilibriums of this place. It may even attempt to communicate with the inhabitants, though bridging the sensory and conceptual gulf between realities would be a formidable challenge. The goal would be to discern if their stone-striking efforts to mend the sky and silence the canyon align with a greater restorative purpose, or if it represents a tragically impossible quest born of their limited context that risks destabilizing the very environment they depend on.

If their instinct to flood the depths indeed holds the key to stabilizing their world, a benevolent power might lend its aid by subtly amplifying their efforts. Perhaps it could coax more of the impossible water toward their aqueducts, or mend some of the leaks in the ancient channels to improve the flow. It could seed the pools with nutrients extracted from the fabric of alternate dimensions to nourish and strengthen the inhabitants in their generational work. Critically, this aid would be offered gently and in alignment with the natives' own drives, not imposed in a way that overwhelms or negates their agency.

However, if upon deep study and reflection, a wise power determines that the inhabitants' desperate quest would only bring greater harm and imbalance if fulfilled, intervention would take a different path. The focus would shift to easing suffering and elevating quality of life within the constraints of the broken environment. The pools could be infused with energies that enhance the nourishment and rejuvenating effects of drinking the dimensional water. The precarious handholds and ledges could be subtly reinforced to prevent fatal falls. The power might even weave in veins of a softer, more forgiving stone to provide pockets of respite from the unyielding basalt. 

Most importantly, a benevolent entity would work to awaken a deeper sense of meaning, pride and belonging in the inhabitants for their role as masterful stewards of this singular place. In a fractured world of brutal absolutes, it would seek to nourish the seeds of nuance - an appreciation for the small moments of respite and strange beauty amidst the chaos, and a recognition that their ultimate purpose may lie in tending the balance of their home as it is, not in realizing an impossible ideal. This shift in perspective would be seeded gradually and gently, in harmony with their own process of discovery.

Ultimately, the wise power would understand that its role is not to "fix" this alien reality or mold it to its own vision of proper function. Instead, it would work humbly and patiently to uphold the inhabitants' profound dignity, resilience and agency as they navigate the unimaginably difficult circumstances of their existence. It would offer what subtle aid and relief it could, but with a deep trust in the natives' own path and potential for growth within the unique parameters of their shattered home. The greatest gift it could offer would be to hold space for their journey with compassion, bearing witness to their struggles and triumphs in a spirit of solidarity, and honoring the hard-earned wisdom and meaning they wrest from their world against impossible odds.
