In the dark and lofty realm of the High Rime, a benevolent and wise power takes notice of the harsh conditions and struggle for survival faced by the unique beings who cling to the vast Strangler pillars. Moved by compassion, this power chooses to intervene subtly, not to reshape the fundamental nature of this vertical world, but to provide aid and hope to its inhabitants.

The wise power begins by gently accelerating the growth and regeneration of the Stranglers themselves. The calcified outer bark becomes more resilient, less prone to crumbling under the inhabitants' hooks and claws. The spongy inner layers grow more rapidly, making it easier for the people to carve their warrens and hollows. The resinous sap that serves as their precious fuel source becomes more abundant, allowing them to create fire more often, providing warmth and light in the perpetual gloom.

Next, the power turns its attention to the "Carpet" - the vital layer of mosses, ferns, and fungi that sustains the inhabitants. These plants begin to grow more vigorously, their tubers becoming larger and more nutritious. New species of edible fungi emerge, clinging to the bark in sheltered crevices, providing a reliable food source that requires less dangerous foraging.

To aid in the hunting of the gliding creatures, the power subtly alters the air currents that flow through the vertical archipelago. Gentle updrafts rise between certain pillars, guiding the schools of prey into predictable patterns that make them easier to snare with the inhabitants' gut-rope nets. The banshees, too, find their numbers slowly declining, as their eggs become more susceptible to the cold, thinning their population to a more manageable threat.

The power also turns its wisdom to the emotional and cultural well-being of the inhabitants. In their dreams, they begin to receive visions - not of a different world, but of their own, seen through a lens of resilience and quiet pride. They dream of their ancestors, who first learned to cling to the pillars, and of the generations to come, who will carve their warrens ever deeper into the heart of the Stranglers. These dreams foster a sense of connection and continuity, a reminder that they are not alone in their struggle.

Finally, the wise power leaves a gift - a small, almost imperceptible change in the nature of the High Rime itself. In the deepest hollows of the Stranglers, where the inhabitants retreat to sleep, a new type of luminescent lichen begins to grow. This lichen absorbs the faint, bruised light that filters down from above and re-emits it as a soft, silvery glow. It is not bright enough to attract predators, but it is enough to provide a gentle illumination in the sleeping hollows, a reminder that even in the deepest darkness, there is still light.

These changes are not drastic. The High Rime remains a harsh and unforgiving place, and the inhabitants' lives are still defined by the constant need to cling and survive. But there is now a subtle undercurrent of hope, a sense that they are not entirely alone in their struggle. The wise power watches from afar, knowing that it cannot reshape this world entirely, but content in the knowledge that it has made a difference, however small, in the lives of those who cling to the pillars in the dark.
