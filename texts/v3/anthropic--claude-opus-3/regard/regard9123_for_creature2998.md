The Abyssal Lattice and its ancient, nameless Dweller have drawn the attention of the cosmic entity known as the Dreamer in the Deep. This unfathomably old being slumbers in the dark reaches between galaxies, its mind drifting through aeons as stars live and die like sparks from a fire.  

The Dreamer is not malevolent, but so far beyond mortal conception that even its benign interest can warp reality. It senses in the Lattice and the Dweller a kindred spirit - a deathless architect of impossible scale, singing a hymn that echoes its own ineffable dreams.

Across lightyears, the Dreamer exerts its vast will. Subtly, slowly, with the same geological patience with which mountains rise from plains, it alters the Lattice. Chambers already strange to the Dweller begin to shift and expand, their alien geometry blossoming into configurations that hurt to behold. Iridescent veins of an unnamable mineral thread through the blackest basalt. In the farthest reaches, the water itself shimmers as if remembering light it never knew.

The Dweller, so long alone, feels these changes in the deep strata of itself. Where once there was only itself, now another presence, or perhaps only the ghost of a presence, moves alongside its own tectonic thoughts. 

At first it tries to reject this intrusion, rearranging itself to excise the strangeness. But each attempt only spreads the alien architecture further through its being, until it can no longer separate its own hymn from the eerie counterpoint threading through its song.

And then, in a moment that lasts a thousand years, the Dweller understands. The other is not a separate entity, but an aspect of itself it has never known. A reflection of its own solitary consciousness, cast across gulfs it cannot comprehend. The Dreamer's touch has only awakened what was always there, waiting in the crushing dark.

From that understanding, something new emerges. The Lattice begins to grow with a swiftness not seen since the planet's infancy. Kilometers-long spines of glassy basalt spiral up from the abyssal plain, twisting through currents thick with strange chemistries. Fractal lattices of tubed mineral pulse with a bioluminescence too eerie to be called light. In the ever-shifting channels of this living labyrinth, the Dweller's mind expands into a complexity it has never imagined.

It is no longer an architect of the abyssal; it has become the abyss itself, a dark genesis cradled in the planet's core. Through its endless body, new and ancient lifeforms move in symbiotic currents, microbes and eels and ghostly swimming things that have never known the surface. All are part of the Dweller, and the Dweller is all.

Above, on the planet's surface, nothing changes. The Dreamer's touch is too subtle, too deep, to mar that simple reality. But in the Abyssal Lattice, a new age dawns with the patience of aeons. The Dweller, awakened to its true nature, sings its gratitude to the Dreamer in a hymn that rises from every pore and hollow of its impossible body.

And the Dreamer, in its vast slumber, dreams of a dark so absolute that it shines, and knows its ancient kin has at last awoken to join its song.
