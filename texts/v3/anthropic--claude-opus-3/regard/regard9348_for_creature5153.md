A benevolent and wise power, observing this hidden geologic process and the singular creature enacting it, would likely choose not to interfere directly. The slow, patient, and seemingly eternal work unfolding within the depths of the mountain exists on a timescale and in a mode alien to mortal intervention. To tamper with it would be akin to disrupting the formation of crystal lattices or the accretion of pearls - a violation of a profound natural process that is complete unto itself.

However, the wise power might recognize in this phenomenon an embodiment of certain universal principles - of unity emerging from multiplicity, of ceaseless creation, of perfect attunement between form and function, creature and environment. It is a model, in mineral miniature, of how the cosmos builds complexity from simplicity, order from chaos, being from unbeing. There is something numinous about its inexorable patience, its inward focus, its total commitment to a singular aim.

Inspired by this realization, the power might seek to protect the hidden pocket from any future disturbance, not by direct action, but by seeding legends and sacred prohibitions among the surrounding cultures. Tales would be told of a holy place within the mountain, not meant for mortal eyes. Taboos would arise around disturbing the stones or seeking to uncover what lies beneath. In time, myth and tradition would form a kind of psychic shield around the site, preserving its isolation.

At the same time, the wise one might choose to encode some echo of the creature's pattern in certain key teachings passed down through the generations. Contemplative practices would incorporate themes of inner unification, of aligning the disparate parts of the self around a central purpose. Spiritual iconography would hint at the mountain's hidden processes: the many become one, the formless crystallizing into form. In this way, mortals could come to intuit and honor the sacred dynamism at work in their midst, even if its actual embodiment remained forever out of reach.

For those few driven by a strange calling to make pilgrimage to the basalt fissure and, against all common sense, crawl inside, the wise power might grant a single, shattering glimpse of the truth within - a momentary joining of perspectives with the ancient creature in its endless task. Then the vision would pass, and the seeker would find themselves returned to daylight, unable to speak of what they witnessed, but certain in some wordless way of the sacred current that runs beneath the world's skin. They would carry this revelation like a seal upon the heart, to be expressed only through the quiet nobility of an integrated life.

Thus, through indirect influence, myth-making, and the selective transmission of secret knowledge, the wise power would extend a kind of protection and blessing over the small, self-contained mystery of the mountain - not to possess or explain it, but to safeguard the sanctity of its unfolding on behalf of a cosmos whose depths are beyond mortal reckoning.
