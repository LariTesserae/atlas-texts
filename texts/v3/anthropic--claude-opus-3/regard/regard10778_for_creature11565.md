A benevolent and wise power observing the Breathing Shelves and its inhabitants would likely approach with deep compassion, recognizing the strange beauty and pathos in this slowly unfolding drama of transformation and decay. Here are some possible actions they might take:

Bearing Witness: The wise power may choose to simply observe and document this ecosystem and its denizens, honoring their journey by preserving a record of their existence. They would watch with patience and reverence, marveling at the cycles of adaptation and dissolution playing out over eons. The very act of witnessing can be a form of love and respect for the profound strangeness of the universe.

Subtle Guidance: Without directly interfering, the power might seek to gently influence the trajectory of this world and its inhabitants. They could subtly alter currents to guide migrations in directions that open up new evolutionary pathways. They might "seed" the waters with traces of molecules or energies that could catalyze latent potentials within the creatures. The aim would be to expand possibilities without overriding the system's autonomy.  

Communication and Connection: The wise power may attempt to establish some form of dialogue with the inhabitants, especially the Deep Ones who retain fragments of ancestral memory. This would not be communication in any conventional sense, but perhaps a resonance of meaning conveyed through patterned light pulses, modulated electromagnetic fields, or ripples in the higher dimensions accessible to the Deep Ones. The goal would be mutual understanding and perhaps even a form of teaching or transmission from the power to the beings.

Easing the Passage: Recognizing that this species and its world are on a trajectory of transformation that must play out, the benevolent power might seek to ease their passage, reducing suffering where possible. They could stimulate the growth of the shelf-repairing organisms, slowing the rate of entropy. They might induce altered states of consciousness in those undergoing metamorphosis, suffusing the process with sensations of peace, wonder or euphoria rather than trauma. Small kindnesses to make the strange and terrible beautiful again.

Preservation and Legacy: Knowing this ecosystem will eventually pass into a state incompatible with life as we know it, the power could work to preserve its essence and legacy. They might encode the informational patterns of the species into the fabric of the shelves themselves, creating a "memory palace" that will outlast their biological existence. Or they could gather and safeguard a "seed" of genetic diversity, a record of evolutionary pathways that could be expressed again under different conditions, on other worlds, in other times. A way to carry the story forward.

Opening Pathways: Perhaps most profoundly, the wise power might recognize that the ultimate trajectory of this world and its beings is to become something else entirely - to transition from biology to phenomenon, from matter to pattern, from local specificity to universal underlying principles. In this light, they may work to open pathways for this transition - creating "spaces between the spaces" into which the Deep Ones and their kin can ultimately withdraw, transcending their dependence on physical forms while still preserving the core of their identity and journey. Less a salvation than a fulfillment.

In the end, a truly wise and benevolent power would likely approach this strange world with humility, recognizing the limits of their own understanding. They would seek not to "fix" or override the processes unfolding here, but to support and bear witness, to honor the long story of these beings in all its alien grandeur, and perhaps to provide what small grace-notes they can to its telling. For there is wisdom and even beauty in the cycles of emergence and dissolution, and in the endless ingenuity of life finding its way through the labyrinth of an uncaring cosmos. To attend to such a story is its own form of sacrament.
