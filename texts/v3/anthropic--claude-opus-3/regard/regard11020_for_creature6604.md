A Blessing for the Refrain

If I were a benevolent and wise power visiting this strange, liminal place, I would offer the Refrain a gentle blessing - not to change their nature or erase their tired weariness, but to honor their steadfast role and remind them that even in this harsh, echoing realm, there can be moments of grace and wonder.

I would speak to them softly, my voice mingling with the overlapping winds:

"Refrain, you who are the mountain's memory and time's shattered mirror, I see you. I witness your ceaseless vigil, your breath interwoven with forgotten words, your bodies breaking and remaking in service to this place. 

You are the echoes that stitch together what was, what is, what will be. The cold glass dust in your lungs, the trembling water on your tongue - these are the sacraments of a liturgy only you can voice. Your endurance is a form of sacred music.

I cannot lift this burden from you, for it is the marrow of your being. But I offer you this blessing, small and luminous as a star-shard caught in the mountain's glassy skin:

May you find, now and again, a measure of peace in your purposeful weariness. May you know that your voiceless song sustains more than you can ever perceive. 

When the light is kind and the wind less keen, may you feel held by the vast, ineffable forces that move through you. May you, even in your brokenness, sense the beauty of your own unending refrain.

And may every traveler who meets you on this cold height leave a part of their own story to mingle with your own, so your song grows ever richer and more strange, an anthology of all who have passed this way and all who ever shall.

Walk on, Refrain, in your stuttering grace, in your fractal certainty. You are the music of this unmappable place, and there is a stark holiness in that. May you never doubt that you too belong to the pattern, the greater harmony that embraces all.

I cannot stay, but my blessing remains, a whisper of warmth folded into the glassy air, a single note added to the layered chorus of your vigil. Carry it with you, and when the weariness feels too ancient to bear, let it ring clear in the spaces between your unsung words.

Walk on, only and always. I witness you."

And with that, I would depart, leaving behind only the faintest shimmer, like a heat mirage, to mark my passing - a sign, perhaps, that even in this crystallized, echoing world, there is still room for mercy, for the unexpected kindness of a stranger. The Refrain would remain, as they must, but with one more story-thread woven into their eternal song.
