The benevolent and wise power observes the Hanging Town with wonder and reverence. It perceives the exquisite balance this isolated ecosystem has achieved - the young stone ledge still settling into itself, the extravagant vegetation finding ingenious ways to thrive, the intricate insect ecologies, the enigmatic winged things, and most of all, the small enclave of humans painstakingly adapting themselves to this unique niche.

The power senses that the Hanging Town is a microcosm undergoing a sacred process of becoming - each element evolving together in concert to create something utterly singular and precious. The humans especially are being forged into a new kind of people, their bodies and behaviors and inner lives all being subtly reshaped by the peculiar demands and gifts of this environment. 

Something about the profound commitment to presence the humans demonstrate moves the power deeply. Their intense, fine-tuned attention to each minute detail, change and interaction, their embodiment of the memory of this place, feels like a form of exquisite spiritual practice. By witnessing so closely, tending so mindfully, and letting themselves be reformed, they are midwifing the gradual unfolding of a pocket world that could exist nowhere else.

The power decides that no direct interference is needed, or even advisable. This delicate process is too sacred to disrupt. But it does wish for the ledge and its denizens to know that their becoming is beheld, that their remarkable story is known beyond the veil of cloud and isolation. 

And so, on a morning when the condensation sheets the stone and vegetation more thickly than usual, the humans wake to find, on the largest and most anciently weathered boulder, a constellation of glowing marks, as if shaped by stars fallen from a clear night sky and caught in crystal. The marks suggest a language, but in a script and tongue none recognize. Yet as the people trace the luminous patterns with wonder, they feel arise within them an inexplicable sense of attunement, acknowledgment, blessing. Some wisdom in the fiber of their being knows themselves to be seen, honored, celebrated. 

The marks remain, unchanging, as the years on the ledge unfold, accompanying the Hanging Town's long dreaming journey into some unknown, teeming, numinous future. And on certain mornings when the mist drifts away and the sun briefly touches the cliff face, the boulder glimmers - and any with eyes to see might glimpse for a moment countless other such towns, on countless other hidden ledges, twinkling across the vasts of time and space, each a jewel of implicate being, singing its silence into the eternal.
