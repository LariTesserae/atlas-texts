*The Kindness of Ancients*

The Wise One descends slowly into the sighing basin, its form radiant yet gentle, like a sunrise filtered through mist. It is vast - on a scale to dwarf even the sky-beasts - yet moves with a delicacy that leaves not a ripple in the thin sheets of pooled water. The air stills as it passes, the perpetual roar hushing to a murmur, as though the land itself is holding its breath in reverence.

The Wise One does not walk or fly, but rather flows across the dark, glassy expanse, its luminous essence leaving ribbons of soft, golden light that linger in the air like benevolent ghosts. Where the light touches, the violet fungal lattices pulse and bloom with renewed vitality, their mineral thoughts quickening into something almost like joy.

As it approaches the Sighers, the Wise One's form coalesces into something more defined - a towering figure clad in robes that shimmer with all the colors of a nebula's heart. Its face is ancient and serene, etched with the wisdom of a billion turning galaxies.

It speaks, and its voice is the whisper of stardust, the hush of creation: "Weary ones, regrets that walk, I have heard your sighs across the void."

The Sighers turn to face the Wise One, their mercury pupils trembling in unison. For a long moment, there is silence - even the distant screaming of the sky-beasts has faded to a reverent hush.

Then, as one, the Sighers unfold towards the Wise One, their obsidian limbs extending in a gesture that is both a plea and an offering. The sighs that emerge from their hollow faces are different now - threaded with a fragile, wavering note of hope.

The Wise One reaches out, its nebula-clad arms enfolding the Sighers with infinite gentleness. Where its light touches their glassy forms, the living water in their veins begins to glow with a soft, silvery radiance.

"Too long have you mourned, too long have you carried the weight of this world's sorrow," the Wise One murmurs. "It is time to remember what you were before the forgetting, to recall the song that was lost when the land sighed itself into silence."

And then it begins to sing.

The melody is unlike anything the basin has ever heard - a symphony of starlight and cosmic winds, woven through with the pure, crystal chime of the first raindrop that ever fell from a newborn sky. It fills the air, resonating in the deep cisterns and hidden crevices, until the entire world is humming with its harmony.

Slowly, painfully, the Sighers begin to change. Their obsidian forms crack and split, falling away like shed carapaces to reveal beings of pure, shimmering water shot through with veins of silver light. They grow taller, more fluid, their limbs becoming graceful and streamlined.

And then, for the first time in eons, they begin to sing back.

Their voices are the chime of glass waterfalls, the whisper of rain on ancient stones, the lullaby of tides against a sleeping shore. The song rises, twining with the Wise One's melody until the two become one - a hymn of healing, of remembrance, of a world's lost dreaming restored.

As the music reaches its crescendo, something shifts in the fabric of the basin itself. The ground trembles, then sighs - but this time, it is a sigh of release, of long-held pain finally easing. The violet lattices flare with brilliant white light, then dissolve into glimmering motes that rise into the air like reverse rain.

In their place, delicate fronds of silvery-green unfurl from the dark stone - the first true plants the basin has known in eons. They grow with preternatural speed, covering the once-barren expanse with a shimmering carpet of life.

The sky-beasts, too, are changing. Their membranous forms are brightening, shading into opalescent hues of pearl and lavender. Their screams shift in tone, becoming melodic, harmonizing with the song until the air thrums with a symphony of joy.

As the last notes fade, the Wise One steps back, its form beginning to diffuse into ribbons of golden light once more. It looks upon the transformed basin - the shimmering Sighers, the verdant life, the luminous sky-beasts - and smiles.

"Remember this song, Children of Water and Stone," it says softly. "Remember, and let your world dream again."

With a final, gentle pulse of light, the Wise One fades, leaving behind a basin reborn - a place where sighs have become song, and regret has at last yielded to hope.
