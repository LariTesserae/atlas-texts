*The Old Growth Awakens*

In the heart of the Drowned Jungle, where the shadows pool deepest and the air hangs heaviest, an ancient presence stirs. It is the jungle itself - or rather, the primordial consciousness that dreamt the jungle into being eons ago and slumbers within its tangled depths. 

The benevolent power senses this elder entity, vast and fathomless, neither good nor evil but utterly unconcerned with the fleeting lives that toil briefly upon the surface of its domain before being swallowed back into the loam. The jungle dreams, and in its dreams, mountains crumble, seas rise and fall, and civilizations dissolve like morning mist.

Gently, with infinite care, the benevolent power reaches out, not to awaken the ancient dreamer but to bestow a transient gift - a single whispered thought that will echo through the aeons of its slumber:

*To those who walk in reverence, the jungle will be a harsh teacher but not a wanton killer. Let the Hollow Ones sing their eerie song and guide the worthy through the trials of rot and rebirth, that they may emerge forever changed but alive. For there is wisdom in the fecund murk for those with wit to find it.*

*Let those who would defile and despoil be broken and devoured, their very essences fed to the hungering roots. But let those who would learn from the intricate majesty of the Old Growth be permitted to depart and carry a fragment of its primordial truth within their souls.*

*For in the depths of time, no dream endures forever, and the final twilight will one day come even for the jungle. But until that distant day, may the memory of its savage glory live on in the stories and soul-shards of the small creatures who braved its embrace and lived to tell the tale.*

The ancient consciousness does not wake, but the ghostly figures that haunt the jungle's margin - the shambling carcasses of long-dead beasts and the skeletal silhouettes of trees - seem to pause and listen before the dream takes them once more. 

A flicker of emerald light, there and gone, illuminates the silent sentinels. A promise made, a bargain struck, a reprieve granted. The jungle will endure, and those who walk with respect will be allowed to play their part in its long dreaming.
