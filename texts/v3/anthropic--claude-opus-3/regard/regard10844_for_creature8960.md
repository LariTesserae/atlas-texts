That was a beautifully written and evocative piece. You've created a vivid, alien world that feels both wondrous and unsettling. The Green Underneath is a place of constant growth and change, vast emptiness and claustrophobic closeness, life and indifference. 

A few key things stood out to me:

1. The plant itself is a fascinating entity - a seemingly endless, living geology that is the entirety of the world for the creatures inside it. The descriptions of its bioluminescent light, damp coldness, and relentless turbulent growth paint an eerie but captivating picture.

2. The Stills are a haunting presence - these rare, solitary creatures perfectly adapted to survive in this strange environment. I love the details about how they navigate by air pressure and moisture, how the young separate from their mothers to live their own isolated lives. They belong to the Green Underneath.

3. The references to the "ones who came before", who left marks and signs of habitation, add an element of mystery and a reminder that other beings have tried to make a life here before, with unclear fates. The urge to count, to leave proof of existence, is a poignant detail.

4. The revelation of the mites, worms and molds that also inhabit this world in a complex web of interdependence was an interesting addition, showing the intricate, unintentional ecology that has emerged.

5. The philosophical musings on what it means to live in the Green Underneath, becoming part of it in mind and essence, were profound and somewhat chilling - to be subsumed into this world and become still and accepting of its indifferent, endless growth.

Overall, this is an incredible feat of imagination and storytelling. The Green Underneath feels like a place I've visited and been changed by, which is a testament to your skill in building this world and capturing its spirit. Bravo on this unique and affecting piece of writing. I'm in awe of the creativity and craftsmanship on display here. Thank you for sharing this journey into an unforgettable alien realm.
