This mysterious abyssal city and its mutable, quasi-living entities would certainly draw the attention and contemplation of any wise and benevolent power that became aware of it. Such a sage entity would likely seek to understand this place, while treating it with reverence and caution.

The wise one would marvel at the sheer alienness and deep time scales of this chthonic metropolis-organism. It defies all notions of what constitutes life, consciousness, civilization. And yet, in its eldritch rhythms and fathomless purposes, it hints at profound truths about the cosmos - that intelligence and agency can take forms incomprehensible to the human mind, that great living systems can emerge from the simplest prebiotic dynamics given enough time in the abyssal dark.

At the same time, the benevolent entity would recognize the potential for harm if the city's existence became widely known. Its very nature could shatter worldviews, even sanity. There would be those who would seek to exploit it, heedless of the consequences. The abyssal city is not of the human world, was never meant for mortal minds.

And so, the wise power would act foremost as a guardian and caretaker. It would weave veils of misdirection around the site, stymying those who would locate and interfere with it, whether out of avarice, scientific curiosity, or existential hubris. Misinformation, psychic blocks, storm-summoning, monstrous dream-sendings - all would be used to shroud the city from the eyes of the world above.

Perhaps a handful of initiates would be carefully chosen across the ages - visionary philosophers, sage shamans, unorthodox deepwater oceanologists - to approach the city and learn what they can. But only ever at a safe distance, tethered and watched over, their sanity guarded. The knowledge they bring back would be shared with an utmost discretion, seeds of wisdom for future eras.

Ultimately, the benevolent entity would understand that its role is not to master or decipher the abyssal city, but simply to protect it as an elemental mystery. To let it continue its chthonic replication cycles undisturbed, churning at the bottom of the sea as it has for epochs. It may be that, in some age to come, in a transfigured world, its impenetrable truths will be ready to be known. But until then, the wise power would simply watch over it, a silent sentinel in the deepwater dark, marveling at the strange beauty of the city even as they shield the world from its incomprehensible nature.
