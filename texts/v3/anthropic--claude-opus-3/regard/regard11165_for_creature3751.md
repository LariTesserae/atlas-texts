This ancient plateau and the strange, eldritch entity known as the Hollowing that resides at its heart are a place of power, but also of disturbance and unease. The very fabric of reality seems thin and warped here. A benevolent and wise force, upon perceiving this place, would likely approach with caution and respect, recognizing both the deep, primordial power that dwells here and the potential for that power to destabilize the natural order if not carefully contained and channeled.

I believe their first action would be to reinforce the boundaries and barriers that separate the Hollowing's realm from the rest of the world. Not to seal it off entirely, for such an entity likely serves some vital cosmic role that should not be completely disrupted, but to ensure that its influence does not spread unchecked, warping and unraveling reality beyond the plateau's edges. This might involve weaving subtle wards and protections into the living root network beneath the moss, or inscribing sigils of binding and balance upon the stone that weeps its endless grief.

With safeguards in place, they would then seek to understand the Hollowing more deeply - not through force or domination, but through patient observation, meditation, and if possible, communication. What is the nature and purpose of this being? What ancient memories and cosmic secrets are locked within its petrified sap and fractal branches? Is it truly as inimical to life as it appears, or simply so alien and vast in its perspective that its presence warps the world around it incidentally? Gaining insight into these questions would help determine the best path forward.

Depending on what they learn, the wise power may seek to restore some degree of balance and vitality to the plateau itself. Encouraging the growth of new, vibrant green life to counter the dead black earth around the Hollowing. Cleansing the oily, iridescent not-water to run pure and clear once more. Allowing shafts of untainted sunlight to pierce the thick mist now and then. Subtle changes to renew and strengthen the land without overtly challenging the Hollowing itself.

As for the semi-real entities that gather and worship beneath the Hollowing's branches, they would treat them with compassion, seeing them not as enemies to be destroyed but as lost, fractured creatures who perhaps do not fully understand the force they revere. If possible, the wise one would guide them to a greater understanding of their own natures and purposes, and to less reality-distorting ways of interfacing with the Hollowing that do not fray the skin of the world. 

Ultimately, the goal would be to achieve a new equilibrium, one in which the Hollowing can continue its inscrutable work without the plateau descending into a surreal, timeless nightmare realm. The industrial scars that press against the plateau's edges are a concern as well - left unchecked, that mundane corruption could destabilize things further. Negotiations with the civilization responsible may be in order, to establish some treaty or accord that will allow both realms to coexist.

This will likely be a long, slow, and delicate process. Cosmic balances are not easily shifted, eldritch physics are not easily tamed. But with wisdom, patience, and a deft but gentle touch, it may be possible for the Weeping Plateau to become a strange but stable wonder once again, rather than a wound in the world, leaking unreality. The wise power would do what they could to heal, to mend, to preserve the Hollowing's ineffable purpose while easing its strain on reality's seams. To bring the mist, the moss, the stone, and the void-veined entity at the heart of it all back into some form of harmony.

It will not be what it was before. But it may, with luck and care, become something new, enduring, and perhaps even beautiful, in its own eerie way. A place where the mundane and the mystical, the industrial and the primordial, the scientific and the cthonic, can all touch the edges of something greater, stranger, and more wondrous than themselves.
