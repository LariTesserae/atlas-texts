Upon observing the nascent rift-dweller community and their precarious yet resourceful existence in the freshly cracked chasm, a wise and benevolent power would likely take the following actions:

1. Subtly stabilize the geology to reduce tremors and collapses, allowing the people to build with more permanence and less fear. This would be done gradually and gently, so as not to disrupt their culture of adaptability and attunement to the stone's rhythms.

2. Encourage the growth of more vegetation by channeling additional light and moisture into hospitable alcoves and ledges. Mosses, ferns, and maybe even small fruit-bearing vines could be coaxed to take root, providing a more varied diet and attracting pollinators to begin a richer ecosystem. 

3. Gift the community with knowledge of more advanced yet sustainable technologies suitable to their environment - better methods for firing higher-grade ceramics, techniques for spinning the fibers of those ferns into thread and cloth, designs for small wind or water-powered machines to assist with labor. This knowledge would come through dreams, intuitions and fortuitous discoveries rather than overt instruction.

4. Bless the central cistern pool with a subtle mineral essence that promotes health and guards against common subterranean illnesses when ingested. The water's properties would be mild, more prevention than overt healing, and never strong enough to be considered magical.

5. Gently shape and polish certain crevices into lenses and mirrors that extend and brighten the play of daylight throughout the entire rift. These would be seen as lucky formations, revealing themselves as the stone continues to shift.

6. Whisper into the sleeping minds of the Quiet Council, granting them wisdom in mediating conflicts and maintaining peace as the community grows and changes. Their leadership would remain one of gentle persuasion, not force.

7. Imbue the luminous minerals that appear on moonlit nights with the faintest ability to purify toxins and cleanse wounds. The glow would be the only sign of this property, encouraging reverence without worship.

8. Occasionally, when the shale-folk conduct their Return Walk with the deceased, allow the watchful power a ritual of its own - reabsorbing the spiritual essence of that life into itself before releasing it into whatever larger fate awaits. Not to capture or claim the soul, only to know it briefly as part of the same pattern.

9. As they continue to explore and map the lower depths, lead the Seam-Runners to discover caverns of unimagined mineral beauty - crystal gardens, glow-ceilinged grottos, entire walls that hum when touched just so. These wonders would serve as inspiration, hinting at how much more their world might contain.

10. Finally, weave a steady, almost imperceptible resonance of hope into the stone - a frequency too low for ears but felt in the bones of each new generation. A sensation that even if this particular rift should fail, their community carries the resilience to continue its story elsewhere, a tale always on the verge of finding its next chapter in the planet's unfolding pages.

The benevolent power would make no grand proclamations, demand no rites. It would simply watch over this fragile settlement, tending it as one might a rare and tenacious wildflower - not to domesticate or shape to its own ends, but to safeguard the potential it already carries, and let it find its own flourishing in the broken stones it has chosen for a home.
