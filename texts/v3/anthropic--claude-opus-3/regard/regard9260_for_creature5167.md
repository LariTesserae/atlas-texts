The benevolent and wise power observes this submerged, living place with reverence and understanding. It sees a remnant of a past civilization that has been gently reclaimed by nature, transformed into a thriving ecosystem where plant and animal life have found a symbiotic balance. The power recognizes the beauty and wisdom in this gradual, unhurried process of change.

Rather than interfering directly, the power chooses to bestow a subtle blessing upon this place, enhancing and preserving the delicate equilibrium that has emerged over centuries. It imbues the green-tinged water with a subtle vitality, nurturing the kelp and sea-cane that form the living substrate. This blessing ensures that the plant architecture will continue to grow and adapt, providing shelter and sustenance for the diverse creatures that call it home.

The power also grants a measure of resilience to the inhabitants of this submerged world. It strengthens the slow-moving grazers, the delicate crustaceans, and the enigmatic half-rooted wanderers, allowing them to better withstand any future challenges or changes in their environment. This resilience is not a dramatic transformation, but rather a subtle enhancement of their natural adaptability and hardiness.

Recognizing the importance of the ancient doorways and platforms as waypoints for the larger, seasonal visitors, the power gently reinforces these structures. It ensures that they will endure for centuries to come, serving as both remnants of the past and vital elements of the present ecosystem.

Finally, the power extends its blessing to the very rhythms and cycles of this place. It harmonizes the slow growth of the kelp, the unhurried movements of the fauna, and the gentle currents that flow through the drowned city. This blessing ensures that the delicate balance between past and present, between nature and the remnants of civilization, will persist and evolve at its own measured pace.

As the power withdraws, it leaves behind no obvious sign of its intervention. The submerged plateau continues to exist as it always has, a testament to the resilience and adaptability of life in the face of change. Yet, beneath the surface, the blessing remains, a subtle and enduring gift that will help this unique ecosystem to flourish and persist, a living memory of what once was and a harbinger of what may yet come to be.
