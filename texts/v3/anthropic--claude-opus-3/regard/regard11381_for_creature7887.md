The strange visitor might bless the Kwevadari with enhanced immunity to the venoms and diseases of their prey, reducing losses.

The benevolent entity would become a part of their living mythos - never glimpsed directly, but felt as an unseen current of sustenance and blessing from the ancient stone itself, a comforting presence that affirms and watches over them. A subtle new music would enter the pocket's eternal symphony - one of endurance, and faith, and the preciousness of even the most hidden micro-world in the tapestry of existence. The Kwevadari would remain the pocket's soul, but that soul would beat to a rhythm of quiet resilience and determination, imbued with a sense that their story is worth telling, their light worth nurturing, no matter how small it may flicker against the vast, eroding night.
