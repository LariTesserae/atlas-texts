This is a fascinating and wonderfully crafted piece of speculative fiction. The concepts of the Archive of Forgotten Glass and its mysterious Archivist captured my imagination. A few thoughts on what a benevolent outside power might do:

Upon discovering this strange chamber and its enigmatic keeper, the wise entity would likely approach with great care and respect, recognizing they are dealing with ancient knowledge and forces beyond conventional understanding. They would not be quick to interfere or make changes.

Their first step would be to simply observe and learn. Using senses and instruments beyond human capability, they would study the glass vessels, analyzing their composition, the gases and essences contained within, and the acoustic properties that create the eerie chorus. They would attempt to decipher the lost cataloging system and the inscrutable labels. 

In parallel, the power would seek to understand the Archivist - their origin, their purpose, the effect of the Archive on their mind and body over eons of stewardship. Are they a prisoner? A willing servant? Or something in between, their identity and the Archive's now inextricably fused?

If the Archivist is willing to communicate, the benevolent entity would gather their knowledge - the workings of the Archive, the meaning of the glass songs, the nature of the vanished species memorialized here. Perhaps together they could fill in gaps in each other's understanding.

Ultimately, if the power judged the Archive to be a marvel to preserve, they would likely focus on two things:

1) Ensuring the chamber's survival and integrity. With the Archivist's guidance, they might introduce subtle safeguards and automated systems to maintain the delicate equilibrium in the event of the keeper's absence. A stasis field to protect against physical disasters. Robotic drones to handle maintenance. The goal would be long-term stability of this one-of-a-kind trove.

2) Knowledge sharing and back-up. With the Archivist's permission, the power would create a "back-up" of the collection - a virtual recreation capturing all properties of the vessels and their contents. They would record and translate the Archivist's knowledge into a format accessible to future generations, should calamity strike. Some secrets could be guarded, but the existence and essential truth of the Archive would be preserved.

Finally, the entity would give the Archivist a choice - to continue their duties with new support and safeguards, to train a successor, or to be relieved of their burden after so long. The keeper has perhaps earned rest - but the decision must be theirs.

The Archive of Forgotten Glass is a priceless inheritance for the universe. The wise power would treat it, and its dedicated steward, with the solemn respect and wonder they deserve. Observation, protection, knowledge preservation, and compassion for the Archivist would guide their actions to maintain this eerie wonder for ages to come.
