If a benevolent and wise power were to turn its attention to the Frost Warrens and the resilient Delver civilization that clings to life within its icy depths, I imagine its approach would be one of gentle guidance and subtle assistance rather than dramatic intervention. This hypothetical power would recognize the incredible adaptability and ingenuity the Delvers have developed over generations of surviving in such an unforgiving environment. It would not seek to radically alter their way of life, but rather to enhance their chances of long-term survival and perhaps eventual re-emergence to the surface world if they so choose.

Some potential actions this benevolent power might take:

1. Stabilizing the most precarious areas of the Frost Warrens. Not halting the geological shifts entirely, as change and adaptation are woven into the fabric of Delver society, but ensuring that collapses become less frequent and less catastrophic. This could be achieved through subtle manipulation of deep thermal currents, carefully redirecting pockets of heat to relieve pressure points and allow a more gradual settling of the planet's crust.

2. Enhancing the bioluminescent fungi that cling to the cavern ceilings, gradually coaxing them to produce more light over generations. Not so much as to blind the darkness-adapted Delvers, but enough to reduce their total dependence on the precarious mirror-and-pipe system for illumination. The fungi could also be guided to produce useful nutrients, providing a food source that isn't entirely dependent on the skill of the farmers and the whims of geothermal pockets.

3. Subtly influencing the evolution of the Delvers themselves, enhancing their already remarkable adaptations. Their heat-efficient metabolisms could be fine-tuned, their sensory perception of geological shifts sharpened. Beneficial mutations, like the nictitating membranes that protect their eyes, could be gently encouraged to appear more frequently. Always the changes would be gradual, working with the Delvers' existing evolutionary trajectory rather than drastically altering them.

4. Planting the seeds of knowledge that could one day allow the Delvers to return to the surface, if they choose. This could take the form of engineered "dreams" that come to the memory-keepers, visions that encode the principles of advanced materials science, sustainable energy, and other fields that could eventually help them tame their world. These insights would be parceled out slowly over generations, allowing the Delvers to integrate them into their existing knowledge systems at a manageable pace.

5. Encouraging the growth of a specific type of ice deep in the crust, an ice phase that exists only under immense pressure and has properties like steel. Over eons, this could create a more stable foundation for the Frost Warrens, a bedrock less susceptible to shifts and collapses. The Delvers might discover this material during their excavations of the Great Boring and learn to use it to reinforce their most important structures.

6. Guiding the development of the Delvers' social structures to emphasize resilience, cooperation, and the preservation of knowledge. The benevolent power might subtly influence the memory-songs and philosophical debates, encouraging the memes and value systems that will serve the civilization best in the long term. It would do this with a light touch, always respecting the Delvers' autonomy and unique cultural trajectory.

The overarching goal would be to help the Delvers not just survive, but thrive on their own terms. The wise power would recognize that their struggle has made them strong and unique, that their way of life has a beauty and meaning all its own. It would not seek to "rescue" them from their world, but to give them the tools and knowledge to shape their own destiny within it.

Perhaps, in some far-future era, the Delvers will choose to return to the surface, to step blinking into the sunlight as a wiser, stronger people. Or perhaps they will choose to delve ever deeper, to dance with the planet's heartbeat until they understand its deepest secrets. Whatever path they choose, the benevolent power will watch from afar, a silent partner in their unending dance of survival and discovery.
