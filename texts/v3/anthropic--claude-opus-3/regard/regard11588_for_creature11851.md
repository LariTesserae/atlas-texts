The benevolent and wise power pauses, considering the unfinished tower, the lives entangled with its becoming. It senses the yearning in the structure, the uncertain purpose, the mystery of what is to come. 

With a thought, the power gently augments the tower's voice, letting its song carry further, resonate more deeply. Now the masons will dream more vividly, their hands guided by glimpses of the tower's destiny. The pale frogs' pulsing rhythm finds greater harmony with the ancient stone.

For the boy who does not climb, the power fashions a gift - a staff of living wood, gnarled yet strong, that will not rot in the damp. With it, he can begin to explore the lower levels, discover alcoves and carvings unseen for generations. In time, it will help him ascend, when he is ready.

The power traces a finger over the fading diagram, and the lines brighten, restored and enhanced. New rooms and passageways appear, an entire complex blooming in the upper reaches. The completed tower's true vastness and intricacy are revealed, inspiring the masons and all who see it.

Finally, the power leaves a whisper, woven into the water's endless trickle, the wind's sigh over the summit. Not a command, but an affirmation. "All will be well. The Throat will open, will speak and sing and call to the sky. The one who is coming will come. Trust the shaping stone, the upward yearning, the voice learning itself in the darkness. In time, you will all find what you seek."

The tower does not change, not in any way that human eyes could mark. But it stands straighter, more certain. The future brightens ahead of it. And the lives within carry on, as they must, until the day the last stone is laid and the Throat can truly begin to sing.
