In the face of such an alien and unsettling place, inhabited by incomprehensible beings, a benevolent and wise power would likely approach with great caution and humility. This realm seems to exist in a liminal state between creation and destruction, order and chaos, testing the boundaries of physical laws and perhaps even the nature of reality itself. 

The Obsidian Watchers, while unnerving, do not appear overtly malicious. They are manifestations and guardians of this strange dimension, neither good nor evil but simply embodying its contradictory essence. A wise entity would seek first to understand rather than interfere.

Observing from a respectful distance, they might attempt to discern any detectable patterns in the hum that suffuses this place - is there meaning encoded in its resonance? Does it shift in response to external stimuli or according to some inscrutable cycle? Careful study of the Watchers' behavior and any ruins or artifacts could offer insight into the origins and purpose, if any, of this realm.

Considering the fragility of the veil between realities here, any direct interaction would need to be carefully weighed. Opening a dialogue could be perilous - would the Watchers even comprehend communication from an outside intelligence? And what if in reaching out, one became ensnared by the hypnotic hum and drawn inexorably into its grinding depths?

However, if this strange place and its denizens are connected to our universe, then what transpires here could have profound repercussions. A wise power must consider the consequences of inaction as well. Perhaps in time, a means of exchange could be established, with the aim of maintaining balance and separation between realms.

Ultimately, absent indication of immediate threat, the most prudent course may be one of respectful non-interference - maintaining a watchful presence and continuing to learn while letting this pocket universe run its course according to its own inscrutable laws and life-cycle. There is a stark, alien beauty here that deserves witness and remembrance.

The benevolent power might construct a small monument at the border between realms - a simple cairn of native obsidian and granite, marked with a glyph signifying that this place has been seen, honored, and left untrammeled out of reverence and wisdom. A single lantern, flickering with the same unknowable energies of this realm, could be left atop the marker as an eternal vigil. 

And then, with a final bow of respect to the Obsidian Watchers, the visitor would withdraw, keeping the secret of this haunting place locked in memory, to be contemplated in moments of reflection on the vastness of existence and the mysteries that lie forever beyond the circles of our knowledge.
