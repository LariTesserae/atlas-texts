The benevolent and wise power surveys the vast, immutable plateau. Its boundless compassion is stirred by this realm where existence has been distilled down to its most essential, unadorned elements - stone, wind, light, and the sparse yet tenacious life that endures unchanged across eons.

In its wisdom, the power perceives the perfection in this stark landscape's equilibrium. To alter any aspect would be to unbalance a flawless, self-sustaining whole. The ceaseless tone of the wind through fluted ridges is the plateau's eternal song, the insects and lizards its living embellishments whose patterns are woven into the larger tapestry.  

And yet, even in a place defined by its changelessness, the power senses an opportunity to bestow a gift. Not to disrupt or reshape, but to enrich what is already whole unto itself.

Focusing its benevolence, the power imbues the plateau with a subtle new capacity - the stone can now hold memory. The unchanging wind, in its passage through the grooves and across the angular stems, will leave the faintest of impressions. The darting insects, the still lizards pressed against sun-warmed rock, will transfer a shadow of their existence into the very substance of their world.  

Across ages, these memories will accrue - the smallest refinement of ridges by grit-laden wind, the minute reshaping of pans by lizard bodies, the softening of hairline cracks by rooted stems. Slower even than the wearing of stone, the plateau will encode a record of all that it has been and all that it eternally is.

To any being that comes later, with senses attuned to the voice of stone, the plateau will become an immense, living history. Even if the wind and light remain unchanging, the very rock will whisper of the insects' flight, the lizards' long stillness, and the endurance of fragile stems. Life and land, though seemingly separate, will be revealed as one interwoven story.

The power leaves this gift to deepen the plateau's perfection, not alter it. In a place defined by stasis, memory will be the only change, the subtle thread of time's passage allowing each era to touch the next. The power withdraws, knowing it has brought a new form of continuity to a realm whose very heart is continuance. Though outwardly unchanging, the plateau will hold its own unfolding in eternal wind and stone.
