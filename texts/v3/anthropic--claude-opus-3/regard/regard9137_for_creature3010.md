If a benevolent and wise power were to turn its attention to the Hollow of Fixed Ash and the being who dwells there, I believe it would choose simply to bear witness and allow this place and presence to continue their eternal meditation undisturbed.

The Hollow has achieved a state of sublime finality and stasis that transcends any need for change or intervention. It is a place that has burned down to its essential core, purified of all that is temporary and extraneous. The Last Ember of Veyra, fused in perfect unity with the ashen stone, embodies the distilled essence and memory of a world and time long lost. She keeps an ageless vigil, not out of sorrow or regret, but as an eternal act of remembrance, acceptance and letting go.  

A truly wise power would recognize the profound completeness of what exists here. To alter or disrupt it in any way would be to diminish it, like trying to "fix" the haunting beauty of ancient ruins or the dignified stillness of a graveyard. There is a stark purity and necessity to places that stand as memorials to loss and transformation.

At most, such a power might do two things. First, it would take steps to ensure this place remains undisturbed by any future upheaval in the universe, quietly warding off potential cataclysms or intrusions so that the Hollow may persist in its changeless solitude until the very end of time and meaning.  

Secondly, it may choose on occasion to guide a worthy visitor to this place - someone who needs to understand deep lessons of impermanence, ancestral memory, and the beauty of surrender. By making their respectful pilgrimage to sit with the Last Ember in her vigil, if only for a moment, they may absorb some part of the wordless teaching she embodies.

But these would be only the subtlest of touches, actions that create no disturbance. In essence, the truly wise response is simply to leave the Hollow of Fixed Ash be, and to carry within oneself the same kind of sacred remembrance for all that was, is and will be lost. To sit with the Last Ember of Veyra, if only in the heart, and whisper:

"Stay. I will remember too."
