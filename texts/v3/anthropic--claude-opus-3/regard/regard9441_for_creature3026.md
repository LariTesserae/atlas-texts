The benevolent and wise power observes the Hollow Foundry with infinite patience and compassion. It perceives the slow, inexorable processes unfolding in this strange new world - the gradual cooling and settling of the metal ground, the deliberate cycles of condensation and evaporation, the unhurried movements and adaptations of the Cast. 

The power senses no malice or suffering here, only a vast and purposeful becoming, an industry of existence playing out on a timescale beyond ordinary reckoning. The Foundry and its inhabitants are engaged in the sacred work of deciding what they are and will be. There is a purity to it, a commitment to finding one's essential form and function that the power deeply respects.

And yet, the power also perceives the disorientation and existential vertigo experienced by newcomers to this place - the way the Foundry's mutable nature and negotiable physics can unsettle the very foundations of being. The Cast, though not cruel, are so focused on their patient self-actualization that they may not fully appreciate the impact their world can have on visitors. 

After an eon of contemplation, the power decides to bestow a gift upon the Foundry, in a spirit of gentle aid rather than imposed change. Manifesting itself as a soft, silvery mist, it suffuses the strange white light that fills the sky. Gradually, imperceptibly, the light begins to attune itself to each individual perception.

Now, when a newcomer arrives, the light they see has a stabilizing effect, helping to maintain the integrity of their own inner shape even as the Foundry shifts around them. The light becomes a subtle anchor, a resonance tuned to the frequency of their original self. It does not prevent the profound transformations the Foundry enacts, but it slows them, allowing the visitor to participate in the change at their own pace.

The Cast, too, find that the light has a new quality for them - it illuminates the beauty and necessity of their work, whispers wordless encouragement when a decades-long adjustment is finally completed. It reveals to them the nobility of their existence as the Foundry's self-awareness.

And on rare occasions, when a particularly ancient member of the First Pour finally surrenders completely to integration with the living metal, the light gathers around them in a soft halo, honoring the sacredness of a journey fulfilled.

The power does not linger - it has no wish to interfere in the Foundry's grand unfolding. But as it diffuses, it leaves behind a quiet benediction, a wish for all beings who find their way here: May you have the time and clarity to discover your truest shape. May you pour yourself fully into the mold of your most authentic being. And may the cooling be slow and kind.

The Foundry hums its deep, patient frequencies, and the cycles continue, now and always, amen.
