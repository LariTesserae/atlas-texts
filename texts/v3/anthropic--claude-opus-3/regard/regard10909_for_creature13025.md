Instead, the benevolent power decides to bestow a gift upon the Storm-Weavers, one that will not fundamentally alter their way of life but will provide them with a measure of solace and connection to their fallen kin. The power infuses the tethers with a subtle, resonant energy that allows the vibrations from the abyssal plain to carry not only the physical information about the currents and the anchors but also the essence and memories of the Weavers who sacrificed themselves to secure the tethers.

The wise power knows that this gift will not change the harsh realities of the Storm-Weavers' existence, but it will provide them with a source of comfort, strength, and unity as they continue their endless dance between the sky and the abyss. The Weavers will draw inspiration and courage from the sacrifices of their fallen kin, and they will face the relentless challenges of their world with renewed purpose and resolve.

As the benevolent power withdraws, it does so with a profound sense of admiration for the Storm-Weavers and their extraordinary world.
