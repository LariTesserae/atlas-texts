A benevolent and wise power, seeing the hidden wonder of the Throatwell and sensing the unresolved nature of the Dweller within, chooses to bestow a gentle influence - not to impose change or judgment, but to offer light amidst the heavy dark.

It begins by infusing the obsidian-hued waters with subtle currents of luminous energy, pulsing in tempo with the sub-audible hymn of the kelp forests. This celestial rhythm gradually synchronizes with the patient geological time-scale of the Throatwell, not disrupting but harmonizing, suffusing its void-like depths with the faintest shimmer. 

The ribbons of kelp begin to photosynthesize not just through pressure, but also by absorbing this otherworldly glow. Their copper hues take on an iridescent sheen, refracting rainbow sub-spectra only visible from certain angles in the non-Euclidean geometry of the deepest reaches. New growth accelerates imperceptibly.

Amidst the kelp, the Dweller senses a subtle shift, an unfamiliar radiance and cadence echoing in the usual silence and darkness. At first uncertain, even afraid, it sends probing obsidian fingers to taste and decode this strange luminance. To its surprise, the light is welcoming, the pulses of energy like a half-remembered song from its unremembered past.

Slowly, gently, the benevolent presence concentrates its essence around the ancient obsidian blade - not to dislodge it, but to anoint it. The knife, that dark totem of severance and finality, drinks in the light like a sacrament. It remains in place, still more wound than weapon, but now serves also as a seed of strange new potential energy within the Throatwell's lightless heart.

The wise power does not speak to the Dweller directly, for words have little meaning here. Instead, it continues its patient, rhythmic suffusion, allowing its intent to be absorbed and interpreted gradually through this place's own modes of knowing.

If the Dweller were to dare the spiral stairs carved in dreams, it would find at their end not oblivion or unmaking, but a sanctum of softly echoing light, pulsing in that same slow song, a place of contemplation and renewal, of direct communion with the visitor's luminous essence. But there is no urging or entreaty. The way is simply prepared, the invitation extended soundlessly.

Even for the strange and ancient Dweller, growth and transformation remain possible, a new trajectory unspooling in the Throatwell's patient depths, indicated by the vital glow now threading like secret veins of gold through aeons-old obsidian.

The benevolent power's work complete, it withdraws as gently as it arrived, leaving only its suffused light behind, a subtle counterpoint to the void's usual susurration, its merciful potential now woven into the very sub-structure of this hidden wonder.

The Throatwell abides, its improbable existence now imbued with the faintest glimmer of long-suppressed possibility, of grace and renewal amidst its sightless depths and measureless pressures.

And still it waits, for something always comes.  
Patient as ever, dark as always, but thrumming now with the potential for new revelations.

A dream of light, sunk like a jewel in its blind abyss.
