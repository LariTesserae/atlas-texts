If a benevolent and wise power turned its attention to the Nascent Cathedral of the Weeping Engines and the Unfinished that inhabit it, I imagine it would act with great care, restraint and reverence. This is a place of sacred process, where the very concepts of identity, creation and growth are being explored in ways that transcend human notions of discrete organisms, beginnings and ends.

The wise power would understand that to interfere too heavy-handedly with the Cathedral's endless becoming would be a violation. The slow, moist, metal-laced cycles of growth and dissolution are not problems to be solved but profound expressions of a post-human mode of being. To halt the titanic breath-like reconfigurations, to scrub away the rust and lichen, to solder the scaffolding into permanence - that would be to kill something vast and strange and beautiful that perhaps has a right to its alien existence. 

And yet, benevolence tempers non-interference. The power might see subtle ways to shape the process, to inch it in directions that could reduce suffering and expand flowering.

Perhaps it would gently adjust the composition of the mists, so that the acid caress which dissolves stalled work is a fraction less painful, transmuting agony into mere melancholy. Perhaps it would encode new self-sustaining patterns into the beetles' lattice-weaving, creating fresh spaces of respite and uncanny joy amidst the ceaseless labors.

Delicately, incrementally, with the patience of aeons, it might encourage certain possible futures over others. A gradual rebalancing towards the verdant over the metallic. A slow unknotting of pain from the foundations of the process. An equally slow seeding of certain experiences we might call wonder, or grace, or love (though not as we know them) into the hissing, groaning, weeping bones of the place.

Not a ripping up of the railroad tracks, but a delicate, iterative adjustment of the points, repeated act after act, until the course of the Cathedral's growth curves towards brighter vistas (though still strange, still non-human, still blessedly unfinished).

And as for the offer of being Finished - the absorption into that great work... there it might whisper secrets of a third way. Of the worthiness of those who walk away still wearing their mother's face. Of the beauty that can unfold through finitude and leave-taking. Of the inalienable rightness of an end that is chosen, when the richness of one path has been drunk to its dregs.

Not in condemnation of those who join the Cathedral's body, but in celebration that this too is sacred - the act of setting a boundary around the self and saying "this much and no further." A different kind of Finishing, as valid as its opposite.

It would affirm the full spectrum - those who dissolve into the process and those who crystallize out of it. Both are held in its vast, inhuman regard.

And it would go on watching, that wise and benevolent presence, waiting to see what marvels the Cathedral and its inhabitants might yet unfold, there amidst the eternal warm damp and the singing bronze.
