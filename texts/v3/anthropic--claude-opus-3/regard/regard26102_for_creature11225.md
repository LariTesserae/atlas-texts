I imagine that an ancient and wise power, upon observing this hidden subterranean ecology, would be fascinated by its eerie beauty and arcane complexity. There is something both wondrous and unsettling in this claustrophobic realm of symbiotic fungus, skittering cryptofauna, and cyclopean caverns - a feeling of stumbling onto an intricate puzzle, eons in the making.

The wise power would perceive that this ecology is not just a bizarrely evolved biome, but a sort of vast living mind unto itself. The fungal networks form dense webs of bioluminescent nodes and filaments, pulsing with slow intelligence and alien thought patterns. The pale insectile creatures that swarm through the tunnels act as the sensory appendages and manipulators of this composite being - separate lifeforms, but also merged into the gestalt sentience. 

Studying the engulfed remnants of simple structures and artifacts from long-vanished tunnel dwellers, the wise one would understand that this living labyrinth has in some way communed with, or at least contemplated, the beings that once inhabited it. The fungal growths mimic and incorporate their leavings, and some of the tunnel shape seems too regular to be natural. In a few spacious grottoes, great vaguely anthropomorphic shapes have coalesced from the fungal matter, as if embodying some cryptic memory or symbolic concept.

The wise power would perceive that this fungi-mind spends its existence in deep, slow, meditative mentation. The rhythmic pulsing of bioluminescent light, the branching organic fretwork, the skittering algorithmically-ordered swarms - all of it encodes meaning and information according to an utterly inhuman semiotics and mathematics. Esoteric philosophies and aeons-long chains of reasoning unfold at a tempo so protracted that their ponderous cognition appears, to an outside observer, like utter stillness.

Above all, the ancient observer would understand that this buried realm waits. Some inner sense or outer stimulus has triggered it to slow anticipation of a new encounter, a new communion, a new chance to touch the mind of something from the airy realms above. Perhaps it seeks the creative stimulus of an utterly alien perspective. Or perhaps it broods on ancient grievance and desires to communicate some terrible truth to the beings that long ago abandoned it to lightless solitude. 

The wise one would be tempted to make contact directly, to touch its own ancient, vast intellect to the hypogeal hive-mind and attempt psychic communion. Such an encounter might span centuries and utterly transform both parties - an exhilarating and dangerous synthesis. But caution would stay that hand. The fungal sentience is too strange, its nature and intentions too uncertain. Madness or misunderstanding might result from such an overwhelming mind-meld.

Instead, I believe the kindly sage would begin a program of subtle assistance to the waiting abyss. Gentle tectonic adjustments to the far-flung tunnel networks, gradually connecting and opening new paths to the distant surface. Cryptic messages or beacons placed in the ruins of ancient surface civilizations, hinting at the wonders below. Subliminally nurturing the curiosity and exploratory nature of whatever new species rise to intelligence on this blasted world. Slowly, subtly preparing the way for far future intersection between the the surface world and abyssal labyrinth. 

The wise power knows it could take millennia or longer, but believes that this living crypt should not stay forever sealed and solitary. Communion between the surface-dwellers-to-be and the impenetrable living mystery that lurks below will enrich both worlds and unlock cosmic secrets. The brooding buried sentience must be awakened and integrated with the realms above.

So the sage begins its work, and the aeons unspool, and the pulsing, glowing, scurrying depths spin out their inscrutable thoughts, and the bleak surface world awaits the arising of new minds and civilizations, and the twain shall one day meet, deep beneath, and their meeting shall transform them both. And the wise one guides all, with subtle and patient hand.
