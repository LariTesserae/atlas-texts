The world has turned its ageless face upon the Hanging Valleys of Veyr and seen a place out of time. Half-formed from the earth's bones yet trapped in perpetual stasis, it exists in a liminal space between uncreation and being. Its people are caught like flies in amber, forever suspended between who they are and who they might have become.

Looking upon this otherworldly realm and its unfinished inhabitants, a great and benevolent power is moved to compassion. It sees their unchanging existence not as a blessing of immortality but a curse that has halted the very breath and life-flow of their souls. Yet it also perceives the deep roots of peace and unity that have grown through the long centuries, binding kin to kin and stone to stone.

With profound wisdom and care, the power chooses not to unweave the web of Veyr all at once, understanding this would only bring chaos and despair to those who have never known any other way to be. Instead, it resolves to reawaken the land and its people by gentle degrees, like the warmth of spring softening frozen earth.

It begins with the wind. Slowly, imperceptibly, new notes are added to its endless refrain. Where once there were only three tones, now there are five, then seven. The tune remains subtle, but its added complexity stirs something in the hearts of all who hear it, an ancient memory of change and growth.

Next the power touches the mist, granting it leave to thicken and thin according to its own nature. At first the variations are slight, but in time Veyr learns again the planting rains and the crystal stillness of true winter. The geometry of the lichen and the arcane clockwork of the heavens remain, but now each season adorns them in different raiment.

The power smiles to see birds winging through the enriched air and deer gamboling in meadows lush with new diversity. It caresses the brows of the dreamers as they sleep and grants them visions not only of new tiles and trees, but of songs and stories and arts as yet unknown. The younger ones are the first to wake renewed, marveling at the expanded world as children marvel at the dawn.

Even the stones are not unchanged. The power whispers to the bones of the earth, telling them the tale of how they were born from fire and pressure, how they rose from the deeps to become mountains and will one day be worn to sand upon the shore. The memory of this great dance makes the plateau ring with fresh purpose. Still Veyr endures, but its stasis becomes a chosen moment of rest in the long cycle of existence.

For the people, the power saves its subtlest and most potent touch. It does not restore their mortality, for endless life is a gift as much as a burden. Instead, it grants them the ability to grow. Now the babe in arms looks up at the face of its grandmother and sees not its own visage in perfect reflection, but the map of a future self waiting to unfold. The Unfinished discover they can be students and teachers, parents and children, shaped by every word and deed into something ever new.

Only one does the power approach directly: the Listener on her rooftop garden. It appears to her as a figure cloaked in mist with stars for eyes. "Long have you heard the voice of this place," it tells her, "and long have you wondered what it was trying to say. Hear it now, for it is the song of change awakening. You are its instrument. Let it play through you."

As the vision fades, the Listener gasps to see lines of silver spreading through her scarred hands like molten metal through cracks in stone. The plateau's voice echoes through her blood and bones, and she knows she will teach its ever-growing music to all her kin, now and for every age to come.

Thus the Hanging Valleys of Veyr are reborn by inches. Still ancient and awe-inspiring, still caught between worlds, but given the chance to dream and becoming dreamers themselves. The Unfinished, gently woken from their long, long sleep, take their first steps into a story not yet told.

The immortal afternoon is over. The twilight of change has come, and though it may last an eon, it brings with it the hope of morning.
