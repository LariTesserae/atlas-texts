This is a fascinating and evocative piece of writing about an imaginative industrial underworld. The vivid descriptions bring the Tideplant Underworks to life in a haunting, almost mythical way.

If a wise and benevolent power were to take an interest in this place and its inhabitants, I believe they would focus on improving conditions for the workers while still respecting the strange beauty and mystery of the environment. 

Some possible actions:

1. Ensure worker safety through better equipment, training, and oversight. The dangers posed by rising water, superheated gas, and cave-ins should be minimized as much as possible. The power could subtly influence management to prioritize safety over profits.

2. Provide workers with opportunities to periodically leave the Underworks and access natural light, fresh air and contact with the outside world. This could help prevent the total absorption into the underworld that leads to workers becoming part of the "Sump Choir."

3. Allow and encourage the ritualistic and creative practices that have organically developed, like the sky-squares, frost-flower eating, and feeding of the lamp-eels. These provide psychological solace and a sense of control. The power could imbue the frost-flowers with gentle healing properties rather than destructive effects.

4. Quietly bless the protective mantles of condensation that veteran workers develop, making them even more effective at warding off the unhealthy aspects of constant damp and cold. 

5. Whisper into the dreams of the crack-readers, enhancing their intuitive abilities to detect structural weaknesses and prevent catastrophes. Guide them to train apprentices and pass on their knowledge.

6. Gently encourage more empathy and care from management toward the workers, perhaps by granting visions of what life is like working in the deep. Foster in them an appreciation for the human element of the Underworks.

7. Imbue the Thaw-Stack's morning exhalation with a subtle rejuvenating and clarifying quality, so that the daily "hymn" subtly uplifts and empowers the workers who hear it rather than lulling them into complacency.

8. Bless the workers' tools and machines with enhanced durability and functionality, reducing daily frustrations and risks of malfunction.

The overall goal would be to make this underground industrial purgatory more bearable, even meaningful, for those who toil there, without destroying the mythos that has grown up around it. The workers are in a sense both trapped by and intimately connected to the Underworks. The wise power should respect that complex relationship while alleviating suffering where possible. Small, targeted "miracles" and quiet shifts in perspective could add up to substantially improve conditions over time.
