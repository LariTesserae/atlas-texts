The arrival of a benevolent and wise power to this hidden mountain valley would be a profound event for the Lumina. Such a being would likely recognize the sacred, unique nature of this place and feel a deep reverence for the harmonious existence the Lumina have cultivated here.

Upon entering the valley, the power would take time to observe and understand, marveling at the intricate interconnectedness of all things - the pulsing bioluminescence, the crystalline flora, the gentle creatures, and most of all, the profound wisdom and peace of the Lumina themselves. 

Rather than seeking to interfere or change things, the wise power would approach the Lumina with humility and respect, recognizing that their way of life, while utterly alien, holds deep truths about balance, harmony, and the unity of all things.

The power might seek to engage with the Lumina, to learn from their spiritual practices and understanding of the "Great Weaving". This could take the form of meditative communion, an exchange of knowledge and perspectives across the vast gulf of their different origins and natures.

The Lumina, sensing the benevolent intent and profound wisdom of this visitor, would welcome this exchange. They might share their history, their art, their philosophy - revealing how they came to be, and how they maintain their serene, balanced existence. In turn, the power could offer insights from the wider cosmos, stories of other worlds and ways of being, expanding the Lumina's understanding without undermining their core truths.

Ultimately, the wise power would recognize that the Lumina and their valley are a rare and precious thing - a delicate, self-contained miracle that should be protected and preserved. They might offer some form of blessing or ward, using their power to ensure that this hidden oasis remains undisturbed by the tumults of the outside world.

As a final gift, the power might leave a part of itself behind - perhaps a crystal seed imbued with a fraction of its consciousness. This seed would grow into a new, unique tree at the heart of the Lumina's village, a living embodiment of the connection forged between these two profoundly different forms of wisdom.

Over time, this tree would become a new center for the Lumina's spiritual life - a place where they could commune not just with the mountain and each other, but with the vaster wisdom of the cosmos beyond their small world. The pulsing light of its crystalline leaves would mingle with their own bioluminescence, a constant reminder of the understanding and respect shared between their people and the wise visitor.

In the end, the power would depart as quietly as it arrived, leaving the valley and the Lumina largely unchanged, but forever enriched by the exchange. The hidden valley would continue its serene, cyclical existence, the Lumina tending to the new tree with the same care and reverence they show to all life, their own quiet story now woven together with the vaster tapestry of the cosmos.
