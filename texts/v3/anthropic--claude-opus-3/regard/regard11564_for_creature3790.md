The benevolent and wise power surveys the drowned spire and the strange being that haunts its summit, and a deep compassion stirs within them. They sense the Watcher's aching loneliness, its patient vigil as it waits for the sea to finally reclaim it. They feel the weight of ages pressing down upon this storm-worn shard of stone - all that remains of a forgotten world.

With a gentle hand, the power reaches out to soothe the spire's crumbling stone, mending cracks and coaxing new growth from the sparse lichen and salt-grass. Soft light settles like a mantle over the jagged rock, and for a time, the ceaseless wind quiets to a murmur.

Turning their attention to the Watcher, the power sees beyond its unsettling form to the builder it once was - one who shaped stone and fought the rising tide for the sake of their people. Honoring that ancient purpose, the power whispers to the being's mind, offering it a new role: no longer a mute witness to the sea's encroachment, but a guide and guardian to any who might find their way to this lonely outpost in ages to come.

To aid the Watcher in this task, the power gifts it with eyes that can see truly, and a voice to speak warning and wisdom. The tar-black flesh becomes smooth grey stone, still inhuman, but no longer twisted by the slow malice of the brine. Now, when the being stares into the churning depths, it will understand the nature of what it sees, and find the words to tell of it.

At the spire's crown, the power breathes life into the remnants of that final wall, and the scattered tools leap into the air, mending themselves and taking their place in the grip of phantom hands. Slowly, with the artistry of a hundred patient crafters, a shelter takes shape atop the tower - a refuge for weary travelers, and a watchtower for the Watcher to oversee its charge.

Finally, the power turns their gaze to the waters themselves, the abyssal currents and unseen shapes that haunt the depths. With a surge of will, they set a boundary to the sea's hunger; this much, and no further. The ocean may rage, but it will not devour the spire entire, not while the Watcher holds their vigil and the power's light endures upon the stone. 

Thus warded and renewed, the spire stands - still strange, still liminal, a place balanced between land and sea, between life and oblivion. But no longer forsaken. No longer alone. The Watcher lifts its head to the salt-scoured sky and lets out a long, low sound, half mourning keen, half greeting.

And from beyond the mist, far across the endless waters, an answer comes: the distant toll of a bell, ringing in the deeps, marking the way for any bold enough to seek the tower where the Watcher waits, the guide and guardian of the Drowned Spire.
