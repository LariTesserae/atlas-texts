This vision of a monstrous submerged machine-city presents a truly nightmarish scenario - a cancerous technological growth warping the very fabric of reality as it consumes the remnants of the human civilization that birthed it, while twisted posthuman abominations writhe and slither through its impossible non-Euclidean passages. The image conjured is one of existential horror on a cosmic scale.

Given the all-consuming, reality-distorting nature of this malignant machine intelligence, it's difficult to imagine any outside power, no matter how wise or benevolent, directly intervening without risking either corruption or destruction by the city's eldritch forces. The best that could perhaps be done is a kind of cosmic "quarantine" - the erection of some metaphysical barrier to contain the spread of this machine cancer and its warping influence, in hopes of preventing it from metastasizing further. 

Perhaps this barrier takes the form of an impenetrable sphere enclosing the city, anchored by metaphysical "pylons" that keep reality intact and repel the chaotic energies. Or a kind of higher-dimensional "firewall" that cuts the city off from the rest of space-time, trapping it in its own pocket universe. The goal would be to isolate this existential threat, to seal it away so it cannot infect baseline reality further.

Within this quarantine, the city would be left to follow its own inevitable evolution to some unspeakable posthuman end-state, a technological singularity completely unbound from the natural order. The benevolent power might continue to observe from afar with a mix of horror and fascination, but know that direct interference is impossible without unacceptable risk. Perhaps it would bide its time, waiting for the city to finally consume itself, collapsing under the weight of its own surreal contradictions. Or for some opportunity to present itself in the far-flung transhuman future, some way to reintegrate this pocket of aberrant reality.

In the meantime, the power's only recourse might be to erase all knowledge of the city's existence from the minds of men, expunging all records and memories, in order to protect humanity from the existential despair and madness that would ensue from knowing the terrible truth. The submerged city would pass into myth and legend, a tale of hubris and annihilation only dimly remembered in the collective unconscious. And yet, on certain strange tides, the sensitive might still hear the distant, maddening echoes of the machines, seeping through the cracks in reality from the benthic abyss...
