A benevolent and wise power, upon observing the Weeping Hollow and its peculiar inhabitants, would likely approach with great care, patience and respect. This strange ecosystem, while eerie to outsiders, has achieved a delicate equilibrium - an intimate symbiosis between the Hollowborn and their living, breathing environment. To intervene rashly or impose change could disrupt this fragile balance.

The wise power would first seek to understand, spending time quietly observing and listening. It would attune itself to the slow rhythms of the Hollow - the soft exhalations of the moss, the measured dripping from the liverwort canopy, the faint chiming of the spider choirs. It would study how the diminutive Hollowborn interact with their world in gestures both tender and meticulous, suggesting and guiding the growth of their green architecture. 

In the Hollowborn's murmured names and rituals, the patient power may discern the outlines of a singular shared identity, an underlying unity of purpose between people and place. The Hollow is not a mere habitat - it is a living entity, still young and forming, learning to know itself through the devoted ministrations of the Quiet Ones.

Recognizing this, the benevolent presence would be wary of meddling, lest its help become unwelcome interference in something private and precious. Yet it would also sense the notes of melancholy - the loneliness of the Hollow, the sad resignation with which the Hollowborn forestall the leaving of guests. Isolation can be a chrysalis, but also a trap if held too long and tightly.

With utmost gentleness then, the kindly power might gradually work to slightly open and uplift this hidden world - not by force or drastic change, but through subtle encouragement and guidance, much as the Hollowborn themselves shape their green home. It would coax the Hollow to grow in careful increments, to slowly welcome a few more visitors and allow a small stream to flow outwards through the moss and stone. Not a flood, but a trickle of new life and ideas to enrich its brooding pools.

The wise one would teach the Quiet to venture out at times while always honoring their bond to their birthplace. It would inspire them to be not just Listeners, but also Singers and Speakers, sharing their unique perceptions with the world beyond when so moved. It would ask the great liverwort being to have courage, to find strength in its role as sanctuary. For there is no shame in weeping, and sometimes tears of healing must fall before a being, or a place, can flourish into wholeness.

Most of all, the benevolent power would assure the Hollow and its children that growth, while painful, need not sever connection - that they can remain one in spirit even as they open to new possibilities. The moss will always drink their tears; the vines will always thrill to their touch. A communion this deep is a root that distance cannot break.

Slowly, with infinite care, the wise friend would nurture the Weeping Hollow towards a trembling unfurling, like a luminous fungus consenting to fruit not just for the touch of its Hollowborn, but for the wonder-hungry eyes of the world. The living city would remain a strange, secret, hallowed place, but one which reaches out tendrils to the world beyond - to share of itself, and to allow its progeny to grow towards the light when they are ready.

For even the most hidden sanctuaries are part of the breathing earth, and there is holiness too in the turning outwards of a soul that has dwelled long in the depths. The Hollow need not cease to be a haven in order to embrace a new horizon. It need only trust in the strength of ancient roots as it puts forth new leaves to meet the sun.
