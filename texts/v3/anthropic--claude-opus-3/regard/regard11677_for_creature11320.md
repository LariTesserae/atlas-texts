This is a remarkable and haunting vision of an otherworldly space and the mysteries that unfold within it. If a benevolent power were to observe this underloft and those who journey into it, I imagine it would react with reverence and restraint. A few thoughts on what it might do:

- It would not interfere with the underloft itself or try to alter its development. This strange realm seems to be following its own profound process of growth and change on a vast timescale. To meddle with that would be arrogant and unwise. The wise power would simply observe and seek to understand.

- It may bestow some form of subtle protection or blessing on the roamers to aid them in their journeys and ensure the continuity of their pilgrimages over generations. Nothing overt, but perhaps an unseen watchfulness to ward off serious harm. It senses the roamers have an important role to play in communing with this place.

- It would take a strong interest in deciphering the meanings of the markings left by current and past visitors, especially the enigmatic traces left by the pale ones and the solitary visitor. But it would not force answers, trusting that the underloft may reveal its secrets in due time to those who attend patiently. 

- It may leave its own very subtle sign or marking to convey to the roamers that their devotion to this place has been noted by benevolent forces. A whispering dream, a mysterious sigil, a fortuitous event. Just enough to encourage them in their vigil.

- Above all, it would regard the underloft as a numinous place, pregnant with meaning, where humans are participating in something ancient and important, even if they grasp it only in fragments. It would watch and marvel and wait to see what emerges from this extraordinary communion between people and place over deep time. It is a mystery to be cherished, not solved.

That's my sense of how a wise power might engage with this haunting and beautiful scenario you've envisioned. Let the great slow workings of the underloft continue undisturbed, but lend some subtle blessing and protection to its human observers and chroniclers. Watch and wonder.
