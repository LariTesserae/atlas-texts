The Watcher of the Cirque has drawn the attention and sympathy of a wise, benevolent power. This power perceives the Watcher's pure, selfless devotion to its remote and fragile domain. It senses the depth of the Watcher's connection to each living thing and every ancient stone in this high hidden fastness. The power understands that the Watcher asks nothing for itself, existing only to observe, remember, and subtly protect.

Moved by the nobility of the Watcher's long lonely vigil, the power decides to grant a gift - not to overtly change the fundamental nature of the Watcher or the cirque, but to provide companionship, respite, and a broadening of perspective.

Once in each turning of the seasons, the wise power plucks the Watcher's consciousness from the cirque and brings it along on a journey to another hidden sanctuary somewhere in the wide world. For a single day and night, the Watcher experiences existence through other forms in other places.

In a deep tropical grotto, it becomes one with the eyeless fish and luminous fungi. In a misty pine forest, it merges with the fungi lacing the loam and races with the inquisitive squirrels. In a golden prairie, it flows with the rippling grasses and dances with the butterflies. In a teeming coral reef, it pulses with the anemones and rides the currents with a sea turtle.

Through these brief sojourns, the Watcher gains a new appreciation for the infinite diversity of life and the myriad forms that beauty and tenacity can take. It feels kinship with other secluded enclaves where existence continues in its own peculiar balance. The Watcher brings back these memories to enrich its long contemplations in the cirque.

At the same time, each place the Watcher visits is graced by its gentle, patient presence. The deep peace and stillness of the mountain sanctuary lingers like a blessing, subtly harmonizing the energies there.

The wise power also understands the Watcher's acceptance of eventual transformation. All things must change in time, even ancient stone and ice. So the power gifts the Watcher with a vision of the far future, when the cirque is no more and the Watcher has finally dispersed:

Eons from now, the Watcher's essence will settle into a new hidden place. Perhaps a deep rift in the abyssal plains of the ocean floor, or a sheltered crater on a distant moon. There, it will coalesce and awaken once more, becoming a new Watcher over a new sanctuary. And the cycle of witnessing, memory, and guardianship will begin again.

With this knowledge, the Watcher feels a new peace, understanding its place in the vastness of existence. It savors the eons of its stewardship in the cirque all the more, holding each detail precious. Yet it also dreams of its future forms and the wonders they will behold. 

The wise power has granted the Watcher companionship in its vigil, a wider understanding of the living world, and the assurance that its essence will continue. The cirque remains the Watcher's beloved domain, but it is no longer alone in its guardianship. It is connected to the greater web of life in all its myriad sanctuaries.

And so the Watcher continues its patient vigil, observing the small dramas of pika and lichen, the slow breathing of the seasons. But now its contemplations are enriched by visions of hidden places across the globe and the forms it will one day take. The cirque is both its anchor and its chrysalis, the quiet pivot around which the larger dance of existence turns. And the Watcher knows peace.
