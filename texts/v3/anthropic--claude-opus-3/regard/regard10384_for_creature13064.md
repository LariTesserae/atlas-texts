In a realm of extremes, they survive.
Adapted to the razor's edge,
Thriving in this alpine hive.
Their lives a frantic, frozen pledge.

A watchful presence now takes note
Of this harsh niche, uniquely wrought.
Where thin air carries not a mote
Of sound, just light with fury fraught.

This wise and kind observer sees
The hunters' plight and ceaseless toil.
Respect and care it now decrees, 
For those who on these cliffs must moil.

With subtlety, it lends a hand,
Not changing much, but easing strain.
A gentler bridge now spans the span,
Of sturdier stuff that won't complain.

The windbreaks too, receive a boon - 
More shelter from the biting frost.
Extending warmth as welcome noon,
Delaying when the light is lost.

The quartz-veined stone, a living map,
Conducting knowledge through its seams.
Locations of the choicest sap,
And caches stocked with feathered gleams.

Tools of bone, now stronger still,
Fashioned finer, balanced true.
Enabling greater hunting skill,
Less effort for the meat accrued.

Yet not too much does this hand change,
For this niche is their own to claim.
Ungentle, yet not truly strange,
A life of striving, all the same.

Observed now with a caring eye,
Given subtle aid to thrive,
The rock-riders may touch the sky,
And in their alpine keep, survive.
