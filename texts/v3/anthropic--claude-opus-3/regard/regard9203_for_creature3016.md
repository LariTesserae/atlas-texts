The place called the Hollow Choir and its inhabitants, the Resonants, are a profound mystery - an enclosed world shaped over millennia by the relentless, all-consuming power of sound and vibration. Though alien and unnerving to outsiders, it is a place of deep purpose and strange beauty to those who call it home.

If a wise and benevolent power were to turn its attention to the Hollow Choir, I believe it would first seek to understand before acting. It would listen patiently to the layered harmonies and discords that define this place, allowing the meaning encoded in the music to gradually unfold. It would observe the symbiotic dance between the Resonants and their crystalline environment, seeing how each shapes the other in an endless feedback loop. 

Recognizing the Hollow Choir as a work in progress, an attempt to birth something whole and complete from sheer sonic will, the wise power would likely choose not to dramatically alter or disrupt its trajectory. Instead, it might offer a subtle "tuning" - a targeted influence that respects and enhances the Choir's essential nature.

This tuning could take many forms. Perhaps it introduces a new frequency into the mix, one that soothes and harmonizes the more discordant elements without blunting their vital edge. Maybe it gifts the Resonants with a sudden insight into the deeper mathematics of their music, allowing them to compose with greater intention and potency. Or it slightly alters the acoustic properties of the Glassbriar itself, making its song more complex and adaptive.

The wise power's aim would be to enrich the Hollow Choir's journey toward its final crescendo, not to cut it short or change its destination. It would trust that the ultimate meaning and purpose of the Choir will be revealed in the last measure of the symphony, and that the Resonants' long, strange pilgrimage will prove worthwhile.

At the same time, this power might also offer a strange mercy to any individual Resonants who feel trapped or burdened by their role in the great song. Not an escape, but a momentary respite: a sudden pocket of silence that lets them hear their own heartbeat, a softening of the endless vibration so they can feel the stillness at the core of their being. A reminder that even in the heart of the Choir, there is more to existence than sound alone.

In the end, the wise power would likely withdraw as subtly as it arrived, knowing it had done what it could to support the Hollow Choir without fundamentally altering its nature. It would continue to watch and listen from afar, waiting for the final chord with the same patient anticipation as the Resonants themselves. For it knows that when the song is complete, the universe itself will shiver with a new understanding - one that could only be reached through aeons of bone-deep commitment to a single, impossible music.
