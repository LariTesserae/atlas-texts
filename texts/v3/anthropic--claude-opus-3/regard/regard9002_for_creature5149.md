As the ancient basin lies under perpetual darkness, filled with the layered migrations of crystalline creatures, a gentle light begins to suffuse the dense air. It is sourceless at first, a subtle luminance that seems to emanate from the moisture-beaded stone itself, catching in the translucent flanges of the swarming wafers and refracting into countless shifting points like stars in a living sky. 

Gradually the light coalesces, drawing together into a presence that hovers above the center of the plain. It takes no defined shape, but pulses softly in hues of silver and palest blue, its radiance casting the quartz ridges and feldspar seams into gentle relief. As it pulses, a resonance enters the sound of the swarm - not a new note, but a stabilizing rhythm that seems to slide beneath the relentless abrasion and clicking, gathering the noise into something almost like music.

The creatures do not flee the light, but their migrations subtly alter, the sheets of interlocked bodies flowing around and beneath the shimmering presence like water dividing around a stone. The light pulses again, and now the condensation that has gathered on the basin floor rises in a fine mist, each droplet limned in silver. The mist does not dissipate, but hovers, thickening the air until the swarms move through what seems a shining haze.

Slowly, almost tenderly, the pulsing presence descends until it brushes the damp stone. Where it touches, the mineral surface remains unchanged, but something kindles within the rock - a faint vibrancy, as though some new potential has been awakened in the crystalline structure itself. This vibrancy spreads in slow waves timed to the presence's pulsing, until the entire basin is embraced by its subtle glow. The swarms respond, their clicking and abrasion rising in eerie harmony, the sound itself now a kind of soaring music that fills the dark like the song of an alien choir.

And then, in a moment that feels both sudden and infinitely gradual, everything changes. The vibrancy in the stone reaches a peak and then seems to release, flowing upward into the swarming creatures. Their translucent wafers shiver and then themselves begin to glow, each one filling with the same silver-blue light that emanates from the central presence. The swarms do not break their migrations, but now they leave shining trails behind them, the light caught and held in the vapor and condensation so the air becomes a galaxy of gently drifting stars. The music of their movement takes on a new dimensionality, as though the light itself has become audible, weaving through the abrasion and clicking in shimmering strands.

At the center of the basin, the pulsing presence flares brighter for a moment, and then gently fades, its work complete. But the glow remains, carried now by the living creatures themselves. The swarms continue their endless cycling, wearing the stone as they have for eons, but their passage now leaves luminous patterns in its wake - spirals and whorls of light that linger even as the wafers peel away and re-form. In their combined radiance, the contours of the basin are softly illuminated, the ridges and troughs revealed not as inert mineral, but as something almost sculpted, a silent collaboration between stone and ceaseless motion.

In this strange new harmony of light, sound, and ancient geology, the essence of the place remains unchanged - it is still a world of deep time and slow tectonic shifting, still a domain defined by the almost incomprehensible endurance of its mineral forms and crystalline inhabitants. But it is a world now imbued with a kind of sanctity, its every surface and vibration singing of some inhuman mystery. The wise power has not transformed this place, so much as it has recognized the profundity already latent within it, and drawn that hidden wonder forth into shining expression. Under the benediction of that revealing light, the dark basin continues its patient dreaming, a ceaseless dance of stone and swarm now shot through with ethereal beauty.
