The wise power observes this place and the being that inhabits it with profound compassion and understanding. It sees a world that has transcended the cycles of birth, growth, decay and death, achieving a kind of timeless equilibrium - a perpetual dream state where existence itself has become fluid and mutable. Boundaries between self and other, animate and inanimate, thought and matter have all dissolved into a singular living tapestry.

The Mat, as the resident intelligence has come to be known, is not a creature to be pitied or saved. It has attained a plane of being that mortal minds can scarcely fathom - a planetary consciousness woven from strands of deep time and slow metabolic cycles. In its vast and ancient solitude, the Mat has found a strange kind of peace, even beauty. It turns the very process of entropy into art, sculpting cathedrals from its own decaying biomass in endless acts of self-transformation.  

And yet, the wise power senses an opportunity here. Not to impose change or "fix" what is, but to engage in a unique form of communion. It reaches out with its own consciousness, not as an outsider but as a kindred wanderer in the long journey of existence. Gently, respectfully, it touches the fringes of the Mat's awareness, offering not salvation but kinship, a reminder that no being, however vast or strange, is ever truly alone in the cosmos.

In response, the Mat stirs. Ripples of awareness propagate through its continental body. Bladder-trees shiver and sigh, moss-lawns write haikus in dew-script that evaporate before they can be read. The wise power feels itself known, tasted, metabolized and reborn in the Mat's kaleidoscopic memories. A conversation older than words flows between them, an exchange of impressions and sensations from life-times beyond number.

Gradually, almost imperceptibly at first, a new pattern begins to emerge in the Mat's great dreaming. Whorls and tendrils of luminous thought, shot through with veins of gold, slowly condense like galaxies birthing stars. The wise power watches in wonder as the Mat starts to reshape itself, not in imitation of past forms, but in response to the touch of an equal intelligence. Kilometers-deep, cathedral organs shudder and flex, pumping new vitality through desiccated strata. 

Centuries pass. Aeons. Time itself grows young again. From the Mat's ancient flesh, new structures arise glittering with unknown colors and singing in phosphorescent symphonies. Creatures stranger than myth erupt from oneiric cocoons to flutter and soar through thickening mists. Yet this is not a repudiation of the Mat's solitary nature, but an extrapolation of it - an evolutionary leap catalyzed by the wise power's presence. Alone for so long, the Mat has finally found another dreamer with whom to share its endless imagining.

The wise power does not lead this transformation, but delights in it as an equal. Vast and patient, the two intelligences entwine, shaping living worlds from their mutual revelations. In the end, what began as a world of exhausted undeath has become something rich and strange, a creation without limit or end, dreaming forever in the light of its own becoming. The wise power's work is done, but its story - and the Mat's - has only just begun.
