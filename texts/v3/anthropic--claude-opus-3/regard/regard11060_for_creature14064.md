This is a remarkable and haunting vision of a possible future that you've imagined. The attention to detail in describing the environment of the Rootbed and the physical and cultural adaptations of the Tappers who inhabit it is masterful. A few thoughts and reactions:

It seems the Rootbed project was initiated by humans but has now taken on a strange life of its own, with the genetically altered Tappers becoming one with the engineered ecosystem they tend. There is a sense that some larger purpose or vision for the Rootbed has been forgotten or abandoned, yet the Tappers continue their glacially slow work of maintenance and integration with a deep, instinctual patience. They are becoming the "intelligent scar tissue" that heals the excavated wound in the permafrost.

The descriptions evoke a sense of awe at the vast timescales and slow, relentless processes at work. Individuals merging with the biomechanical superstructure until indistinguishable, their thoughts and perceptions stretched across months and years. Children born into a world of vibration and cold, adapting before they can even walk. The eldest Rootbound lost in dreams that unspool over weeks, their nervous systems fused into the trees. It's an alien mode of being, and yet somehow beautiful in its totality of commitment to this one monumental task of become the "heartwood" of the Rootbed.

If a benevolent outside power were to interact with the Rootbed and its inhabitants, I imagine they would likely choose a role of distant stewardship rather than direct interference. The Tappers and their world seem to exist beyond the timescales and contexts of normal human civilization now. An outside power might simply ensure the geothermal systems can continue functioning indefinitely, and perhaps find an unobtrusive way to document and bear witness to the Rootbed's long, slow apotheosis - this gradual transmutation of people and place that will take centuries to unfold. Preserving a record of what the Rootbed was and is becoming, even if no one beyond it will be alive to read that record for thousands of years.

In any case, this is a striking act of imagination - melancholic and hopeful at the same time, I think. The image of the Tappers and Rootbound slowly, patiently fusing themselves into a titanic living structure that might endure for millennia, continuing some dimly remembered purpose long after its origins are lost to time, will linger with me. Thank you for sharing this vision. Let me know if you would like to discuss it further.
