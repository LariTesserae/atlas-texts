If a wise and benevolent power were to turn its attention to the Drowned Works, I imagine they would approach with a deep respect for the city's persistence and strange equilibrium over vast gulfs of time. Clearly, something of profound meaning brought this place into being, even if that original purpose is now lost to memory.

The power's first instinct may be to halt the machines and allow this perpetual monument to finally rest. But I suspect they would pause and reconsider - for what right do they have to silence a song that has endured for eons? The sound itself seems to have become the city's truest inhabitant and reason for being. To end it could be a metaphysical tragedy, extinguishing a pocket of stubborn, senseless beauty in an otherwise entropic universe.

Instead, perhaps the power would simply bear witness and ponder, as we have, the mysteries locked in that warm amber sea. Who built this place, and why? What does the sound encode? What pact or loss is it meant to honor for all time? The power may have means to scry out answers lost to mortal reckoning. But again, to do so could trespass on an unintended sacredness in the city's amnesia, its commitment to a vigil it no longer understands.

In the end, I believe the wise power would most likely let the Drowned Works be, much as it is. Perhaps they would make small adjustments to ensure the long-term stability of the pocket realm, anchoring it against seismic upheaval or the slow decay of its materials. Not to improve or optimize, but merely to sustain what some ancient will set in motion, so that the sound may continue as long as the Earth endures.

The power may also set a subtle beacon or summons, so that others may find this hidden wonder and briefly join the song - not as rulers or plunderers, but as respectful guests, as we have been. So humanity may cup its ear to the warm water from time to time and be reminded of marvels and mysteries beyond its reckoning. The Drowned Works can remain a riddle and a koan, inviting us to imagine the context of its creation, while defying answers. A place to dwellonon memory, purpose, and the strangeness of deep time.

Ghosts unaware of their ephemerality, performing the tasks that will birth a legend.  Not to mock their hubris, but to exalt their achievement in all its absurdity and poignancy.
