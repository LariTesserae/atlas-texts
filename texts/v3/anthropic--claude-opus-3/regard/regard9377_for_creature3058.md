A vision comes to me as I contemplate this ancient place and its patient inhabitants. I see a being of light and wisdom, vast in its understanding, gentle in its power. It has observed the High Ruin and the Unfinishing for eons untold, appreciating their slow, stately dance with time and entropy.

This being would not interfere directly, for it respects the profound, geologic rhythm of the place. But it senses the quiet yearning in the Unfinishing, their awe and fear before the perfected objects in the deepest vaults. It understands their sacred duty to stave off completion, but also the great weariness that comes with fighting the inexorable tide of time.

And so, with a thought as soft as the pewter light, it grants them a vision. Each of the Unfinishing, wherever they may be scattered across the vast plateau, sees it simultaneously in their mind's eye:

They see the High Ruin finally complete, every tower finished, every wall joined, the intricate carvings shining with meaning. But they also see themselves still there, walking amid the perfected city. They have not been edited out, but rather transformed - their tall forms now radiant, their skin like polished marble, their eyes bright with understanding. They are the city's eternal stewards, beautiful and unchanging.

Then they see the vision begin to unravel. Cracks appear in the walls, towers crumble, carvings fade. And they see themselves unraveling too, their radiance dimming, their bodies slowly blending back into the moss and stone. The perfected city decays around them, returning to glorious ruin, as they too are gently unmade.

Back and forth the vision cycles, eternally building to completion, then falling back into entropy, with the Unfinishing rising and fading, rising and fading, always belonging, always part of the grand pattern.

Then, as softly as it came, the vision ends, leaving only a whisper in the minds of the Unfinishing:

"Be at peace, dear ones. Completion and ruin are two sides of one coin, endlessly turning. You have not to fear the end, for endings too shall always end. The day may come when the last stone falls, but after the last ending comes a new beginning. Your task is sacred, yet you are not alone in it. The universe itself rises and falls with you, in time's endless dance. Through all the cycles to come, you are held eternally in Love."

The Unfinishing blink their ceremonial blinks as the voice fades. They do not speak of what they saw. But as they go back to their patient labors, as they walk their winding paths and tend their beloved ruins, there is a new light in their depthless eyes - a glimmer of peace, of acceptance, of knowing their place in the cosmos's grand, symphonic dream. And though the rain still falls soft and cold, they feel it now as a gentle embrace from the stars.
