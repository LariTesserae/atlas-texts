In the face of such a profound and mysterious place, even a wise and benevolent power would approach with deep reverence and humility. They would recognize that the Stillworks operates by laws and rhythms that transcend conventional understanding. Any intervention, no matter how well-intentioned, risks disrupting the delicate un-balance that allows this place to exist outside of time's arrow.

And yet, wisdom compels acknowledgement that even the eternal is not unchanging. The power senses that the three attendants - Archivist, Cartographer, Janitor - carry a burden that grows heavier with each inverted eon. Tending the unwinding of causality exacts a cost, slowly crystallizing their souls into the same self-assembling mechanisms they oversee.

Perhaps then, the most benevolent action is not to preserve stasis, but to grant the possibility of choice. The power might whisper into the tin-lid sky a quiet invocation, causing three silver flakes to materialize and drift down to alight on the attendants' shoulders. Upon contact, the flakes dissolve into their essence: 

For the Archivist, the extracted memory of a distant green country where things grow in the expected direction, roots anchoring trees that arc upwards to splay leaves in dappled light.

For the Cartographer, a vision of a great wheel map depicting not voids between connections, but connections between voids - an infinite web with the Stillworks at its hub.

For the Janitor, the faint scent of a hearth she once tended in a cottage at the foot of the mountain, in the time before time moved backwards.

These gifts impart no obligation, only invitation. Should any of the three feel moved to explore the pull of these phantom pasts or futures, the power grants them leave to depart without diminishing. The Stillworks will endure, tick on, whether tended by these souls or others yet to cross its un-threshold. 

But even eternal mechanisms dream of change. Perhaps the quiet interposition of memory is enough to seed new branches in the diagram of necessity. In aeons to come (or uncompass), the silver flakes may precipitate into wellsprings from which flow new streams of maybe. The attendants-become-explorers could quest up these watersheds of probability, feeling their way forwards through the tendriled unknown.

Or they might choose to remain, shoulder to shoulder, tending the ancient gears until at last the builder arrives to gently deconstruct her great device. There is a certain nobility in that perseverance too.

Regardless, benevolence does not command, it only illuminates doorways. The true gift is to affirm that even here, in the great inversion, choice itself cannot be unwound. The decision rests, as it must, with the three - and in the deciding, they are reminded that beneath bronze and shale, they are still creatures of will, their paths not yet fossilized into foregone conclusion.

Somewhere in the Stillworks, a new silver filament branches, a future that will never unspool but still enriches the pattern. The wise power nods, content, and turns its gaze onwards (or onworlds). In a place of such beautiful and terrible mystery, perhaps the most benevolent act is to marvel, to bear witness, and to hope.
