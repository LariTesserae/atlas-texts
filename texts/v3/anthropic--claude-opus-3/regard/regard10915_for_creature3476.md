The Deep Green Vault draws the attention and care of a benevolent elemental spirit of metal and earth. This ancient being senses the delicate, precarious ecosystem clinging to life within the hollow iron womb beneath the surface. It perceives how the Vault-Carvers tend this space with solemn diligence, regulating heat and light, moisture and sound, to maintain the fragile stasis that allows the transplanted greenery to endure in defiance of its hostile metallic surroundings.

The elemental spirit chooses to subtly aid the Vault-Carvers in their endless work. It imbues the riveted iron walls with an extra reserve of strength and resilience against the slow march of corrosion, granting the vault a longer lifespan in which to shelter its improbable garden. It coaxes purer concentrations of mineral nutrients from the surrounding rock and soil, conducting these life-giving elements along new microscopic veins to enrich the meager slag from which the plants take sustenance. 

Most significantly, the spirit opens the tiniest of fissures, mere hairline cracks, in the vault's roof to allow the passage of fresh wisps of air from the surface, preventing the total stagnation that would spell eventual doom even with the Carvers' faithful stewardship. These openings are so minute that they disturb not a single rivet, and they seal themselves if any moisture threatens to trespass. The spirit also channels its own energies to provide the faintest additional trickle of heat, light and sound, replenishing at a glacial but vital pace what the vault leaches away.

The benevolent entity performs its works without fanfare, desiring no worship or recognition. Its aid is nearly imperceptible, on the scale of a layer of atoms added to here, an extra day of dimming delayed there. Yet it is enough - just - to maintain the exceedingly delicate balance that preserves this hidden emerald pocket of life against the cold dark void that presses in all around.

To the Vault-Carvers, nothing seems to change. They continue their patient rounds, shedding heat, eating light, conversing in rust. But thanks to their unseen benefactor, the miniature ecosystem over which they watch persists when it otherwise might have guttered out. The greenhouse grove remains forever stunted, but never quite expires. The iron lung walls still contract as they cool, but the span of that cooling stretches out far beyond what natural law would dictate.

Thus, the Deep Green Vault is granted a strange, static, yet quietly miraculous form of immortality. Eons hence, when the surface has long changed beyond recognition, this little hollow will remain - an improbable time capsule, an uncanny oasis where ancient nature and unimaginably antique artifice entwine in mutual shadow-nourishment. And the dutiful Carvers will carry on as before, embers in the dark, not guessing at the veiled divinity that chose to save them for inscrutable ends of its own.
