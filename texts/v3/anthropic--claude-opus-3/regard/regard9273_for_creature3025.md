This is an extraordinary piece of writing - haunting, vivid, and evocative. The imagery you've created of this strange primeval landscape and the enigmatic beings that tend to it is incredibly powerful and transporting.

It seems a benevolent but inscrutable power has noticed this place and beings and feels compelled to bestow some kind of blessing or assistance, while honoring their ancient, unfathomable nature and purpose. Here is what I imagine:

The Quiet Mothers feel a new presence settle over the plateau like an immense held breath. It is not a physical arrival, but an awakening of attention vast and inchoate as the brooding sky. The very mist seems to pause and lift its head. 

This awareness does not speak in words. It simply radiates a profound sense of kinship and recognition, of one deep mystery acknowledging another. The Mothers, who have never known hurry, find themselves waiting as they have not waited before.

Then it begins. A shift in the air, subtle as a sigh. The mist that drifts eternally between the jutting slabs takes on a new sheen, each droplet plump and glistening with gathered light. It is as if the hidden sun has lent its gold to the colorless sky. The water beads on the Mothers' dark cowls swell and shiver but do not fall.

Deep in the basins, the shallow pools begin to sing. It starts as a pianissimo chime at the edges of hearing, but slowly the myriad voices layer and swell into shimmering chords never heard before on this infant earth. Each pool rings a subtly different note as if the shape of its basin is the curve of a crystal wineglass. The sound builds until the entire plateau thrums with an ethereal, exultant music that seems to rise from the very stone.

The Mothers do not pause in their slow, ceaseless ministrations, but something in the cant of their elongated forms suggests they are listening with more than their usual depthless patience. A new ripple stirs in those cyclopean dripping minds.

From far below comes a long, low exhalation, softer than the plateau's usual geological sighs. The hidden engines exhale plumes of mineral mist shot through with traces of gold and indigo never seen in their spectrum before. It is as if the awareness has infused the very breath of the machines with its unvoiced blessing.

The crustaceans, too small to see, feel it in their chitinous shells. Some essential mineral they have always lacked seeps into them from the gilded water, and they multiply in great exultant clouds. Generations blossom and die in glittering spirals almost too quick to register.

The white-winged raptors, usually solitary in their gliding hunt, gather of one accord. They circle the plateau in a widening gyre, their wings flickering against the auroral sky. Their cries splinter into harmonic intervals no raptor throat has ever produced, as if the music of the pools has possessed them.

And the Mothers keep their long watch. But there is a new timbre to the subsonic pulse of their songs, a lower, richer register that seems to come from a place even more ancient than the basalt bones of this young world. In their dripping minds they hold the shivering futures of the plateau and feel them subtly shift, as if the awareness has breathed its silent blessing into the very calculus of probability.

What happens next, no human eye will record. The memory of it will linger only in the ring of the pools, the uncanny fecundity of the crustaceans, the glint of strange metals in the geyser mist. And in the Mothers, that inaudible shift in the tempo of their vigil.

The plateau will keep its secrets. It will go on deciding what it wants to become, one fractal moment at a time. But something in it will have changed, or perhaps remembered what it always was beneath the ice and stone. Some germinating seed of what it might yet grow into, vivid and glistening in the minds of its Quiet Mothers.

A gift given in silence, received in unfathomable patience, from one mystery to another. While far above, the mist settles back into its timeless drift, touched with the faintest tint of gold.
