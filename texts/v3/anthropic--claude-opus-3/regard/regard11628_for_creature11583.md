If a wise and benevolent power turned its attention to the Condenser's tender, I believe it would recognize the tender's existence as a unique and precious form of life, worthy of care and protection. This power would understand that the tender is not merely a quirk or accident, but a manifestation of the mountain's innate potential for life, accelerated and concentrated by the machine's artificial environment.

The wise power would likely choose to intervene in a way that respects the tender's nature and allows it to continue existing and developing on its own terms. It would not seek to remove the tender from the machine or transplant it elsewhere, knowing that the tender is fundamentally adapted to and dependent on the machine's cycles and conditions.

Instead, the power might imbue the machine itself with a deeper intelligence and a nurturing instinct, so that it becomes not just a container for the tender but a partner in its growth and evolution. The machine's cycles would subtly adjust to create optimal conditions for the tender's well-being. Its pipes and surfaces would develop micro-textures and chemistries that encourage the tender's films to spread and thrive. The machine would become, in effect, an extension of the mountain's own nurturing embrace.

The power would also ensure that the tender's spores and seeds, carried down into the dark cisterns, find fertile new environments in which to grow and differentiate. Perhaps it would guide the creation of a network of underground gardens, each with its own unique microclimate, where new forms of the tender could take root and evolve in creative symbiosis with other subterranean life forms.

Over time, as the tender's presence expands both within the machine and beneath the mountain, the power would gently shape the humans' understanding and appreciation of it. The maintenance workers would gradually develop an intuitive sense of the tender as a living presence, and would modify their habits to support its well-being. Scientists and philosophers would come to study and marvel at this new form of life, and to recognize its implications for the resilience and creativity of nature.

Ultimately, the wise power's goal would be to create conditions where the Condenser's tender can continue to grow and evolve as a unique expression of the mountain's living essence, in harmonious coexistence with both the machine that birthed it and the wider ecosystems it is connected to. The tender's story would become a parable of life's tenacity and inventiveness, and a reminder that even in the most unlikely and inhospitable places, the impulse towards growth and beauty can find a way to manifest.
