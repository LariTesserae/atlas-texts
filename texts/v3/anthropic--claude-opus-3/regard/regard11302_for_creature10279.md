A benevolent and wise power, observing the haunting, symbiotic world of the Glimmering Throat and its enigmatic inhabitants, would likely approach with a mixture of curiosity, respect, and gentle concern. This power would recognize the intricate balance and deep history woven into every facet of this place - the slow dance of erosion and growth, the sacred rituals binding the dwellers to their machines, and the ghostly absence of the fauna that once thrived here.

Rather than disrupt the delicate equilibrium, the power would seek first to understand. It would listen to the dwellers' resonant humming, deciphering the layered meanings and ancient knowledge carried in those wordless songs. Through this sonic communion, it would glean insights into the origins of the Rust, the nature of the symbiotic lichens, and the whispered memories of the silver-leafed trees.

Sensing the dwellers' reverence for their corroded world and their fear of the "Great Drowning," the power would offer subtle aid to bolster their efforts. It might imbue the metallic tree-nectar with restorative properties, allowing the ancient pumps to function more efficiently and with less need for constant tending. It could guide the growth of the root-tendrils, ensuring they reinforce rather than destroy the crucial infrastructure. Perhaps it would even coax forth new strains of bioluminescent algae, their pulsing light acting as a soothing balm and guide for the dwellers in their shadowed labors.

Recognizing the poignant absence of animal life, the power might gradually seed the Throat with creatures uniquely adapted to this strange biome - rust-eating beetles with iridescent carapaces, translucent eels that swim through the algae-choked canals, or ghostly birds whose calls harmonize hauntingly with the dwellers' hums. These would not be mere fauna, but protectors and companions, aiding in the endless task of maintaining balance.

To address the dwellers' own slow decay, the power could impart the knowledge of rejuvenating rituals - meditative practices that allow them to absorb the vitalizing essence of the Rust without being consumed by it. It might even reveal hidden alcoves within the Weeping Quarter where restorative spores grow, granting initiates a deeper resilience and prolonged harmony with their environment.

Above all, the power would honor the dwellers' sacred connection to their world, never seeking to transform it into something unrecognizable. The Glimmering Throat's beauty lies in its haunting strangeness, its defiance of natural order, and the dwellers' own metamorphosis in the face of oblivion. To preserve this is to safeguard a pocket of wonder - a testament to life's tenacity and the possibility of finding meaning in the face of entropy.

So the benevolent power would watch, and listen, and offer its quiet blessings. In the rustling of the silver leaves, the dwellers might hear whispers of encouragement. In the patterns of luminescent algae, they could find new inspiration for their woven garments and ritual designs. And in moments of despair, when the weight of their task feels insurmountable, they would feel an unseen presence beside them - a gentle hand guiding their own as they work the ancient machines.

For in the end, the wise power understands that the Glimmering Throat is not a world to be saved, but a song to be sustained. As long as the dwellers continue their patient labors and maintain the sacred hum that echoes through the corroded structures, their realm will endure - a monument to the tenacious beauty that blooms in the heart of decay.
