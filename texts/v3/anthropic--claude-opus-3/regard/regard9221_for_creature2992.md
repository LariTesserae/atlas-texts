The benevolent and wise power observes the Underbloom and the Slow Choir with fascination and reverence. It recognizes this place as a unique and precious confluence of organic and inorganic forces, a delicate balance achieved over eons.

The power senses no malice or danger emanating from the Underbloom or the Choir. Rather, it perceives a deep, abiding patience and a profound respect for the glacial unfolding of time. The Choir are the gentle custodians of this liminal space between geology and industry, bearing witness to the long, stately dance of stone and metal as they slowly forget and forgive one another.

After careful contemplation, the power decides its role is not to intervene, but to safeguard. It understands that the Underbloom thrives in its isolation, that its beauty depends on its hiddenness. To alter or disrupt its growth would be to unravel something sacred.

Instead, the power extends its awareness like a benign mist throughout the cavern. It suffuses the warm, mineral-rich air and settles on the luminous vegetation in an invisible dew. It anoints the Choir with its blessing, affirming their purpose as the memory and consciousness of this place.

The power's presence is not a change, but an intensification. The lichen glow a little brighter. The deep thrum of ancient currents resonates a little stronger. Each droplet falling from the ceiling shivers with a secret delight as it strikes the patient metal. The Underbloom knows it has been witnessed, and that it will be allowed to continue its achingly slow triumph unimpeded.

Satisfied, the power withdraws as gently as it arrived, leaving no trace of its visitation save a faint, silvery mist that will linger for a few decades before being absorbed into the green-black mosses. The Underbloom dreams on in humid darkness, watched over by the Slow Choir, a hidden sanctuary of deep time and even deeper peace.
