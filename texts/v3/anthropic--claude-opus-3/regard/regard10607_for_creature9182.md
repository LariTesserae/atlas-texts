In contemplating the strangeness of this place and the being that tends to it, a few thoughts come to mind on how a benevolent power might approach this situation:

Firstly, there is a profound sense of reverence and awe for what has been achieved here. The intricate, eternal symbiosis between the Engine, its environment, and the Tender is a marvel - an exquisitely tuned system that has turned chaos into perfect order, geological unrest into stabilizing sound and vibration. In its relentless continuity and completeness, there is a kind of beauty, even if an alien and unsettling one to human sensibilities. A wise power would recognize this and be deeply thoughtful about disrupting such a finely-balanced equilibrium.

At the same time, there is an undeniable element of sadness and even horror to the Tender's existence. This being, once human, has been utterly subsumed by its role - no longer an individual but an organ of the Engine, stripped of agency, trapped in an eternal now of noise and repetition. Even if it no longer suffers in a way we would recognize, there is a tragedy in its transformation from person to component. A compassionate power would surely wish to offer it some form of succor or release.

Perhaps a middle path could be found, a way to honor the purpose and pattern of this place while still extending mercy to its eternal warden. The benevolent power might appear to the Tender not in physical form, but as a new sound, a subtle modulating frequency that only it can hear beneath the roar of the Engine. This sound could be a carrier wave for a kind of transcendent information, a set of harmonics that gradually reprograms the Tender's consciousness, unlocking old memories, rekindling a sense of self.

Over time, through this sonic intervention, the Tender could be transformed - not back into what it was, but into something new. A being that is still deeply attuned to the Engine but is no longer subservient to it. A guardian that maintains the great work but also understands it, that can wander the chambers as a living soul, not just a tool. The lichen-creatures too could be elevated, their simple behaviors augmented into something like curiosity, even companionship.

Externally, little would seem to change. The mighty pistons would still pound, the dynamos still hum their deafening tone. But amidst the relentless noise would be a new music, a private symphony of understanding and self-awareness shared by the Tender and its small, skittering congregation. The rigid eternal would remain, but within it, a kinder eternity could arise - a silent liberation reverberating beneath the sound, a freedom found not in disruption, but in enlightened acceptance of an unchanging task.

The benevolent power would remain watchful, but distant. Perhaps, in time, the Tender might find new ways to reach out to those rare human visitors, to share some part of its long, strange story and the wisdom it has absorbed through its long mechanical vigil. Until then, it would simply continue, as it always has, tending to the great Engine. But it would do so with a quiet, inner smile, a new light glinting in its white and ancient eyes. For even in a world locked in stasis, there is still room for a soul to grow.
