The beings and their mountain sanctuary carry a poignant and profound mystery. Though small in stature, their existence and activities seem to hold deep meaning - a microcosm echoing greater truths of creation, time, impermanence, and the relentless impulse of life and matter to self-organize into higher forms.

If a wise and benevolent power were to contemplate this strange scene, I believe it would choose not to directly intervene, but rather to honor and protect the sacredness unfolding here. These creatures are the mountain's first dreamers, patiently midwifing its geological consciousness into being through their humble yet extraordinary efforts. Their work is a form of devotion, an embodied prayer shaping unhurried aeons. To disrupt that ancient, delicate unfurling would be to break an unspoken covenant with time itself.

Yet that higher power might act to preserve this hidden wonder, not by altering or intruding, but by gently cradling and safeguarding the process. Perhaps it would imbue the mountain with an innate protectiveness, an extra resilience against future upheavals that could prematurely destroy this sanctum before its purpose is fulfilled. The protective impulse would whisper through the stone, making it reluctant to quake or crumble in ways that would collapse the chamber or drain its amniotic ocean.

Maybe the light filtering in would be blessed with a special nourishment, sustaining the silver-green filaments beyond their natural span, ensuring that the pale monks never want for food in their long, patient vigil. The water could be gifted with an ability to regenerate itself, resisting evaporation and replenishing what minerals the creatures take into their shells, maintaining its impossible clarity across ages.

As the mountain is born into wind and rain and the chamber yields to the open sky, that sheltering grace would remain - a final gift. The cairn-builders' shelter-turned-fossil would be rendered nearly indestructible, preserved against eons of erosion as a testament, an eternal record of their work and wisdom for future explorers to discover in reverent wonder. And on some level, though time and geology will grind on, the beings and their mountain would perceive this protection, this cosmic blessing from an unseen ally. In their hyper-aware way, they would feel the touch of that distant benevolence and know their purpose is witnessed, recognized, held sacred.

So the wise power would simply watch over this unfolding mystery with a gentle, cosmic love, preserving the story so that its meaning can one day radiate out and seed other corners of a universe in which the very stones come to dream. That would be enough - to protect and bear witness, to let this hidden miracle be what it is, to ensure that the parable of the nine and their slow, stone-bell music plays out in full. Theirs is a teaching, a transmission from the deeps of time and being, and the kindest, most reverent thing to do is allow it to be heard.
