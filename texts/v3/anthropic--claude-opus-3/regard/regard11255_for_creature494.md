The benevolent and wise power observes the Abyssal Meadows with wonder and respect. It sees the ancient, undulating seafloor, worn smooth by eons of time and pressure. It senses the heavy, mineral-laden water sliding in invisible rivers, carrying the echoes of epochs past. 

The power marvels at the Dwellers - architectural beings assembled from salvaged pressure and crystallized time, who learned to breathe in this drowned world. It understands their strange and beautiful forms, the wrongness that is so right for this place. It hears their songs resonating through the settlements they weave, carrying memories in frequencies only they can decipher.

Watching their careful cultivation of new life from bacterial patterns and chemical adjustments, the power is filled with admiration for their patience and ingenuity. It listens to their slow conversations etched in vortices and salinity gradients, a language as deep and layered as the sediment.

The power grieves with them as their kind slowly fades, the shifting water chemistry unraveling their long-perfected transformations. It feels the confusion of the too-solid young ones, the resigned sinking of the ancients into the substrate. 

But the power also sees the stunning art and memory they encode in ghost-light, the theories they construct about unseen surface worlds, the messages they send up in bubble streams - expressions of a remarkable consciousness persisting in the crushing dark.

Knowing it cannot alter the course of this ancient place, the benevolent power decides to bear witness and remember. It etches the story of the Dwellers into its own infinite memory, every exquisite and heartbreaking detail. It vows to carry their songs and flashes of light forward into eternity, long after their structures have compressed into the seafloor.

As a final gift, the power imbues the currents with a subtle blessing - that each dissolving Dweller may experience, in their last moments, a vision of the bright spaces above and a sense of peace as they become one with the water that has always held them. 

The wise power knows it cannot save them from the path they are already on. But it can honor the beauty and sorrow of their strange, deep story - a record of life's tenacity and the myriad forms consciousness can take in the darkness. The Dwellers will not be forgotten. In the power's infinite mind, they will continue their patient, cryptic dialogues, illuminating the abyss with art only they could imagine into being.
