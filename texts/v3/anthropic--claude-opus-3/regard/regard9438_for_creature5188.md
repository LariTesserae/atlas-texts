The being in the fissure is in a state of perfect equilibrium - motionless, changeless, utterly self-contained. It seems to have achieved a kind of geological nirvana, an eternal stasis of body and spirit merged with stone. There is an austere beauty to its existence, pared down to mineral essence.

And yet, one wonders - is this truly the ideal state for a living thing? To calcify, to surrender all capacity for growth or exploration or even thought? To become an object rather than a subject, a passive component of the inanimate world?

I imagine a wise and benevolent power considering this being with a mixture of admiration and sorrow. Admiration for the purity of its self-sufficiency, the elegant efficiency with which it has adapted to its niche. But sorrow too for all that has been lost - the dynamism of life, the ability to reach out and connect and change.  

The power would not seek to disrupt the being's chosen mode directly. To shatter that perfect, crystalline stasis by force would be a violation. But perhaps it might gently open a new possibility - not to draw the being out of its crevice against its will, but to remind it that other ways of being are still available, should it ever wish to remember itself as a creature that can choose.

With the lightest of touches, the power might coax that single filament of light to gradually widen and brighten across eons. Slowly, slowly, until one day the being registers a change in the quality of radiance reaching its topmost fronds. An invitation, not a demand. A hairline crack in the perfect seal of its existence.

The power would wait, then, with infinite patience. Ready to welcome the being if it ever decides to unfurl itself in that strengthening light. To greet it not as half-living stone, but as a marvelous, complex, questing organism restored to its full inheritance of growth and change.

The choice would remain the being's own, as it must. The power would never force an emergence. But it would hold that widening channel of light as a standing offer, a permanent reminder:

There are other ways to be. You are more than the confines of your current shape. You need only reach for the light, and the wider world will reach back.
