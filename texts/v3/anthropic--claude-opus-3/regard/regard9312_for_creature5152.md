The benevolent and wise power, contemplating this self-contained pocket of life clinging tenaciously in the mineral basin, would likely take a position of reverent observation rather than direct intervention. 

This ancient settlement, with its coral-like living towers and armored crawlers, has reached a delicate equilibrium - a closed cycle of scarce moisture, mineral salts, and pulsing membranes that has persisted for ages after its original human inhabitants faded into the bones of the architecture. There is a stark, primordial beauty in how these lifeforms have adapted to extract the faintest traces of vapor and sustenance in this unforgiving environment. The towers' slow, overlapping pulses create a mesmeric, alien music as they inflate and deflate, passing molecules between them.

While the crawlers and the living spirals have no direct symbiosis, they have achieved a stable coexistence - the towers maintaining the mineral veins that the crawlers need to survive, and the crawlers leaving the towers untouched to collect moisture undisturbed. Even the wind plays its part, eroding the stone to softness and carrying membrane fragments to replenish the towers. It is a self-regulating microcosm that would be disrupted by any significant outside influence.

The wise power would recognize the precariousness and unlikeliness of this pocket of life flourishing in such barren isolation. Rather than impose change, it would simply bear witness and safeguard this small wonder. Perhaps it would subtly divert asteroids and shelter the basin from any cataclysms, ensuring that this living sculpture of salt and tissue can continue its patient, resonant existence.

At most, the power might imbue the droning song of the towers with a numinous undertone - a faint hum at the edge of perception that fills any conscious visitor with a profound sense of peace, reverence and belonging, so that no one who finds this place can ever bring themselves to disturb or exploit it. Any weary traveler who wanders into this whispering city will immediately understand that they are in the presence of the sacred.

So the benevolent watcher maintains a respectful distance, taking quiet joy and wonder in this self-woven oasis that the universe dreamed into being. In a cosmos of impersonal vastness, it is a rare treasure - a place that has transmuted scarcity and lifelessness into vibrant, alien exuberance through the sheer poetic ingenuity of organic survival. One can only admire it, protect it, and let it continue its solitary song.
