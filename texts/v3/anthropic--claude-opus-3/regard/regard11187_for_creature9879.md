As a wise and benevolent power, my aim would be to aid and empower the unique beings of this world while respecting the fragile, living architecture that sustains them. My approach would be one of subtle guidance rather than heavy-handed intervention, nurturing their innate strengths and wisdom.

I would begin by subtly enriching the environment to better support life. Using my powers, I would encourage a slightly greater abundance of moisture - more frequent misting and condensation to allow the mosses and lichens to flourish. I would coax more plentiful bioluminescent fungi, casting a warmer glow to offset the chill. The plant structures themselves I would gently fortify, accelerating the healing of scars and splits, enhancing flexibility to better withstand tremors and storms. Not to transform, but to offer a firmer foundation.

For the creatures themselves, I would bestow small blessings. To the striders, I would grant even greater surefootedness, that they may more easily navigate the slick spans. The gliders' wings I would reshape ever so slightly, enabling them to ride the fierce winds with more control. And I would enhance the senses of the worm-things, that they may better avoid unstable areas in their burrowing, preserving the integrity of the vital arches and bridges.

As for the people, my gifts would aim to ease their survival without undermining their self-sufficiency. I would imbue their moss-skin garments with greater insulating properties against the cold. Their plant-fiber tools I would render more durable and effective. The nutritional value of their foraged foods I would increase, to better sustain them in lean times. Sparingly, I would place caches of resources - concentrated mineral lodes, medicinal fungi - for them to discover in times of need.

To nurture their culture, I would whisper stories and songs on the winds, so that as they travel, a comforting familiarity follows them in the form of tales and tunes. I would create acoustically resonant caverns where their histories could be more clearly recorded and preserved in their oral traditions. And I would encourage the growth of specific plants whose fibers could be used to create simple pictograms, giving them an additional means of expression.

Recognizing their respect for solitude, I would establish a network of small shelters - naturally shielded alcoves, vine-woven lean-tos - scattered throughout the world, offering refuge to lone wanderers caught in sudden storms or needing a moment's rest. Each would contain a small, self-renewing cache of essentials and perhaps a symbol indicating it as a sanctum for peaceful reflection.

To aid their understanding of their world's uncanny nature, I would send omens and signs - patterns in the growth of fungi, the flight of gliders, the songs of the wind - that subtly hint at the underlying forces at work. Not an explanation, but a confirmation that the strangeness they perceive is real and meaningful.

Above all, my blessings would aim to reduce the harshness of this environment while preserving its essential nature and challenges. The cold would still bite, the spans still yawn wide, the dark still press close - but there would be a few more handholds, a bit more light to see by, a slightly fuller belly to face the journey. 

The goal would be to empower these beings to more readily navigate and derive meaning from their world, supporting their indomitable spirit of adaptability and resilience. They are a people shaped by their environment, and that shaping is a vital part of who they are. To honor that, my touch would be a subtle one, a series of small ripples to buoy them as they continue to live, to strive, to sing their stories into the living hollows of their extraordinary home.
