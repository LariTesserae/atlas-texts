Preparing for a visit to this planet-spanning underwater world requires careful planning and specialized equipment. Here's what an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider:

1. Breathing apparatus: Given that the environment is entirely submerged, a reliable and long-lasting underwater breathing system is essential. A rebreather or a specialized diving suit with an integrated oxygen supply would allow for extended exploration without the need for frequent resurfacing.

2. Pressure protection: The depths of this ocean may subject visitors to significant water pressure. A pressure-resistant diving suit or a submersible vehicle with a pressurized compartment would be necessary to withstand the conditions comfortably and safely.

3. Temperature regulation: The cool waters may necessitate a thermal insulation layer in the diving suit to maintain body temperature during prolonged exposure. Consider a suit with built-in heating elements for added comfort.

4. Propulsion system: To navigate the vast distances between structures and to keep pace with the underwater traffic, a personal propulsion vehicle, such as a diver propulsion vehicle (DPV) or a jetpack-like device, would be highly advantageous.

5. Navigation and communication tools: A waterproof, high-resolution mapping device with a GPS-like system would help with orientation in the labyrinthine underwater cityscape. A reliable, long-range communication device for contacting local authorities or other travelers is also crucial.

6. Illumination: While bioluminescent organisms and the glowing structures provide some light, a powerful, waterproof flashlight or headlamp would be essential for exploring darker areas and for signaling in case of emergencies.

7. Protective gear: The presence of large, tentacled creatures and other unknown marine life may warrant the use of protective equipment, such as reinforced gloves, fins, and a helmet with a clear, impact-resistant visor.

8. Water quality testing kit: Although the water appears pristine, it's advisable to carry a compact water testing kit to periodically check for any contaminants or unusual chemical compositions that could be harmful to human physiology.

9. Emergency supplies: A waterproof backpack containing emergency rations, a compact first-aid kit, a distress beacon, and a small, inflatable raft or flotation device should be carried at all times.

10. Cultural awareness: Before embarking on the journey, extensively research the local customs, laws, and social norms to ensure respectful and seamless interactions with the inhabitants of this underwater world.

Remember that this is an entirely alien environment with its own unique ecosystems and technologies. Adaptability, caution, and an open mind are key to a successful and enriching visit to this mesmerizing underwater realm.
