Preparing to visit this unnatural, otherworldly city would require both physical and mental readiness. Here are some key considerations:

Physical Protection:
Given the strange atmospheric conditions, industrial pollutants, and chemical mists, robust respiratory protection is essential. A full-face respirator with particulate filters and organic vapor cartridges would provide defense against both airborne toxins and the corrosive, oily condensation. 

Sturdy, non-porous outerwear resistant to chemical degradation is also advised, as contact with the condensate and polluted surfaces seems inevitable. Clothing should allow full mobility for navigating the city's vertiginous, labyrinthine spaces.

Adequate eye protection is a must, given the pervasive bright haze. Wide-spectrum anti-glare goggles may help with the disorienting light.

Supplies and Gear:
Sustenance may prove challenging in this unnatural environment. Bring ample supplies of water and sealed, non-perishable food. The local flora and fauna, being of dubious origin, are best avoided.

Some form of shelter, like a compact tent with a sealed floor, could provide a safe resting space away from the corrosive miasma and unsettling wildlife.

Navigational aids are crucial in this non-Euclidean metropolis. GPS will likely prove useless, so old-fashioned tools like a magnetic compass, maps (if obtainable), and a robust system for marking one's path could prevent becoming hopelessly lost. Ample light sources are essential.

Mental and Emotional Preparation: 
Venturing into a place so divorced from natural law and human sensibilities will be profoundly disorienting. Travelers must steel themselves for severe assaults on their perceptions and sanity.

Time distortion may prove one of the most disorienting aspects. Losing track of time's passing could lead to anything from missed check-ins to total dissociation. Keeping a precise journal with timestamps and carrying multiple timekeeping devices may help maintain temporal awareness.

Having touchstones to anchor one's identity and memories of consensus reality could be psychologically stabilizing. Personal totems, photographs of loved ones, or other emotionally resonant mementos might provide a mental lifeline.

Unwavering mindfulness and self-awareness will be key. Distinguishing between objective external input and distorted sensory illusions or hallucinations may become increasingly difficult. Regularly "checking in" with oneself, both mentally and by documenting perceptions, could help maintain clarity.

Above all, boundless curiosity, a high tolerance for the alien and irrational, and an unshakable sense of purpose must drive any being that dares to brave this fever dream of a city. Unpredictable wonders and horrors surely await—only those of staunch mind and intrepid spirit could hope to behold them without shattering.
