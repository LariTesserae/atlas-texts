In preparing to visit this otherworldly obsidian glass plain, a multiverse traveler should take several key precautions and pack essential gear:

Environmental Protection: The primary concerns are the sharp, fractured glass terrain and the electrical storms. Sturdy, non-conductive boots with thick soles are a must to protect against jagged obsidian shards. An insulated, full-body suit resistant to cuts and punctures will shield against both glass and lightning. A visored helmet with air filtration is advised to guard against airborne glass dust and any potential atmospheric toxins.

Shelter: Given the relentless activity and clamor of the swarming creatures, respite may be needed. A portable, enclosed shelter pod should be brought, equipped with sound-dampening insulation, air recycling, and basic amenities. It should be made of durable, non-conductive materials and have a low profile to withstand storms. Anchoring spikes will prevent it from sliding on the glass.

Navigation and Orientation: The featureless terrain and constant twilight will make navigation challenging. High-powered lights will be necessary to cut through the gloom. A GPS-like device tuned to this realm's coordinates should be used to track position and map the landscape. A compass may be unreliable due to electromagnetic interference from storms. Reflective beacons can be deployed to mark a trail back to the shelter or entry point.

Sustenance: As the plain appears devoid of plant life or water, ample supplies of food and drinking water must be carried. Energy-dense rations that don't require cooking are ideal. Water can be conserved by recycling moisture from breath via a stillsuit-like garment. Water condensation traps could potentially be set out to capture additional small amounts.

Specimen Collection: The bizarre fauna will undoubtedly draw scientific interest. Containment vessels, as well as paralyzing and sedating agents, should be brought to secure organisms for study. Protective gloves will be needed for handling. A crystalline tank with adjustable compartments could hold a variety of specimens. Full environmental suits will prevent contamination of samples.

Communication: The din of the creature swarms will drown out normal speech. A closed-channel short-range comm system built into the helmets will enable communication between explorers. A quantum entanglement communicator in the shelter can link back to base camp or the home universe for check-ins and emergencies.

Defense: While the fauna may not be overtly hostile, the frenzy of activity creates an unpredictable and hazardous environment. A stunner or other non-lethal deterrent weapon should be carried in case of creature attack. An emergency beacon for urgent evacuation is also advisable.

Mindset: The explorer must be mentally prepared for the utter alienness and sensory overload of this world. The constant noise, eerie darkness, and churning sea of life will be psychologically taxing. Training in meditation techniques to maintain focus and composure under stress would be beneficial. Packing mementos of home can help ground oneself in familiar reality.

Time Dilation: The traveler should be aware of any potential time distortion effects between this realm and their home universe. A personal atomic clock or other timepiece attuned to their origin plane will help track the passage of time and avoid unintended overstays.

The obsidian glass plain is an utterly surreal and overwhelming environment, an ecosystem so different from any on Earth as to defy comparison. Meticulous preparation of both equipment and mindset will be essential for any multiverse traveler wishing to explore this darkly mesmerizing domain and emerge with sanity and samples intact. It is a journey not to be undertaken lightly, but one certain to reshape the boundaries of biological and psychological understanding.
