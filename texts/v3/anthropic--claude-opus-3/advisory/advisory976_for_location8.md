For an ordinary human multiverse traveler preparing to visit this hidden, subterranean industrial complex, there are several key considerations to keep in mind:

1. Environmental protection: The oppressive heat, humidity, and potential presence of harmful gases or chemicals necessitate proper protective gear. A durable, lightweight, and breathable environmental suit with a built-in temperature regulation system and air filtration would be essential. The suit should also provide protection against potential electrical hazards and sharp, rusted metal surfaces.

2. Lighting and navigation: Given the dim, flickering light provided by the aging electrical lamps, a reliable, high-powered headlamp or flashlight would be crucial for navigating the complex safely. A compact, handheld device with a digital map and compass would also be invaluable for orienting oneself in the vast, labyrinthine space.

3. Communication and emergency beacon: In the event of an emergency or getting lost, a long-range communication device capable of penetrating the thick layers of rock and metal would be a lifeline. An emergency beacon with a built-in distress signal could also prove essential for rescue efforts.

4. Medical supplies and sustenance: The remote location and potentially hazardous environment underscore the importance of carrying a comprehensive first-aid kit, including supplies for treating cuts, burns, and respiratory issues. A compact water filtration system and a supply of nutrient-dense, non-perishable food would be necessary to sustain oneself during the exploration.

5. Climbing and traversal equipment: The presence of scaffolding, temporary structures, and pipes suggests that some climbing and traversal may be required. A sturdy, lightweight harness, ropes, carabiners, and a compact grappling hook could prove invaluable for navigating vertical spaces and crossing gaps.

6. Scientific instruments and documentation tools: To unravel the mysteries of this place, a variety of scientific instruments would be useful, such as a compact spectrometer for analyzing unknown substances, a multi-tool for collecting samples, and a high-resolution camera for documenting the complex's layout and technology. A durable, waterproof notebook and writing implements would also be essential for recording observations and theories.

7. Mental preparation and adaptability: The eerie atmosphere, strange juxtaposition of nature and technology, and sense of isolation can take a psychological toll. Mental resilience, adaptability, and a strong sense of purpose would be crucial for maintaining focus and motivation in this challenging environment. Regular communication with a support team on the surface, as well as engaging in mindfulness practices, could help maintain emotional equilibrium.

8. Respect and caution: The complex's apparent abandonment and ongoing decay suggest that it may be unstable and potentially dangerous. A respectful, cautious approach to exploration is essential, with a willingness to turn back if conditions become too hazardous. The alien nature of the technology also demands a measured, thoughtful approach to interaction, lest one inadvertently triggers a catastrophic event.

By thoroughly preparing in these key areas, an ordinary human multiverse traveler can increase their chances of safely navigating and unraveling the secrets of this enigmatic, subterranean industrial landscape. However, even with the best preparation, the journey is likely to be unpredictable and challenging, requiring constant vigilance, flexibility, and a spirit of adventure.
