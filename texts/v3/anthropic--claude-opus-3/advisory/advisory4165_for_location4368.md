Preparing to visit this surreal, otherworldly location hidden in the abyssal depths would be a daunting prospect for any traveler, requiring meticulous planning, specialized equipment, and an adventurous spirit tempered with caution and respect.

First and foremost, reaching this extraordinary site would necessitate a private or chartered deep-submergence vehicle capable of withstanding the immense pressures at the bottom of the ocean. A nuclear-powered submarine or bathyscaphe with a reinforced titanium personnel sphere would be ideal. It should be equipped with external floodlights and cameras to navigate the perpetual darkness, and potentially a remote operated vehicle (ROV) for more delicate exploratory maneuvers. 

The traveler would need to assemble an experienced crew, including a pilot, co-pilot/navigator, and at least one technician familiar with the life support and mechanical systems. Depending on the specific nature of the expedition, specialists such as marine geologists, archaeologists, linguists or cryptographers might also be valuable additions to the team.

Extensive safety training and contingency planning would be essential. The crew would need to be well-versed in emergency protocols for system failures, hull breaches, and medical emergencies at depth. Redundant life support, power, and communication systems are a must. Ample food, water, and medical supplies for a potentially prolonged stay should be accounted for.

Upon approaching the luminous underwater grotto, the most crucial precaution would be carefully studying and monitoring it from a distance before attempting direct contact or entry. The peculiar currents, electromagnetic anomalies, and alien chemical composition of the water could pose unpredictable hazards to both personnel and equipment. High-resolution imaging and a full battery of spectroscopic scans would help assess the physical and radiological risks.

Entering the space itself would likely require donning an atmospheric diving suit capable of maintaining internal pressure while allowing for more dexterity and freedom of movement than a submersible alone. A tether should be maintained at all times, and vital signs monitored closely for any psychosomatic effects from proximity to the strange artifacts and energies within.

Protocols for engaging with or handling the unknown technologies would need to be carefully established prior to arrival, erring heavily on the side of caution and non-interference. Disturbing these ancient and mysterious devices could unleash forces beyond human control or understanding. Detailed imagery, readings and samples might be taken, but only with the utmost care not to disrupt their configuration.

Throughout it all, a sense of reverence, humility and open-minded wonder is perhaps the most important thing for the traveler to maintain. This is uncharted territory, beyond the maps and tomes of human knowledge. Even with the most meticulous preparations, a visitor to this liminal space must be willing to embrace the unknown, and accept that there are mysteries in the deep that the human mind may never fully grasp. Expect the unexpected, and be ready to return to the surface with more questions than answers.
