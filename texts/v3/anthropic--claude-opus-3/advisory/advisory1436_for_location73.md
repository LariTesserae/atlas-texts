Preparing to visit this otherworldly subterranean city requires careful consideration and specialized gear to navigate the unique challenges and hazards of the environment. Here's what an ordinary human multiverse traveler should keep in mind:

1. Climate Control: The perpetually hot, humid air will be uncomfortable and potentially dangerous for extended periods. Pack lightweight, moisture-wicking clothing and consider a portable cooling system or ventilated suit to regulate body temperature. Hydration will be critical, so bring ample water and electrolyte supplements.

2. Respiratory Protection: The thick, damp air may be difficult to breathe and could contain harmful particulates or gases from the industrial processes. A high-quality respirator or self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) is essential. 

3. Lighting: With only dim, intermittent electric lights, personal illumination is a must. Bring powerful, long-lasting flashlights or headlamps with extra batteries. Consider also packing flares or chemical light sticks as backup.

4. Navigation: The maze-like tunnels and caverns will be disorienting. Detailed maps would be ideal, but may not exist. Bring a compass, chalk for marking paths, and a spool of guideline to avoid getting hopelessly lost. A handheld GPS unit may malfunction underground.

5. Protective Clothing: The rough, damp surfaces and unknown fauna necessitate durable, waterproof clothing and boots. Long sleeves and pants will protect against scrapes and bites. Gloves and eye protection are also advised.

6. First Aid: Injuries from falls, cuts, or creature encounters are a real possibility. Pack a well-stocked first aid kit including antiseptics, bandages, splints, and antivenom if available. Basic wilderness first aid training could be invaluable.

7. Communication: Standard radios or cell phones may not function deep underground. Consider specialized cave radios or a pre-arranged system of message caches and rendezvous points with your surface support team.

8. Sustenance: Food options will be limited in the depths. Bring compact, non-perishable, high-energy rations sufficient for the planned duration of your stay plus an ample reserve. Water purification tablets could allow use of groundwater in an emergency.

9. Climbing Gear: The crumbling scaffolding and unfinished areas may require scaling walls or traversing chasms. Pack ropes, carabiners, climbing harnesses, and other basic mountaineering equipment, and be proficient in their use.

10. Specimen Collection: The bizarre ecosystem warrants scientific study. Bring sample vials, jars, specimen bags, and preserving agents to collect organisms for later analysis, but exercise caution approaching unknown creatures.

Above all, this seems like an expedition for a well-equipped and experienced team, not a solo traveler. The physical challenges combined with the psychological strain of the claustrophobic, alien environment pose serious risks. Thorough research, contingency planning, and surface support will be essential to emerging unscathed from this strange domain. Fathom the risks and prepare accordingly before descending into the dimly-lit depths of this industrial hive lurking beneath the earth.
