To prepare for exploring this vast, eerie underground space, a multiverse traveler should take several key precautions:

Protective Gear: The environment is cold, wet, and likely harbors hazardous microbes or fungi. Wear sturdy waterproof boots, thick moisture-wicking socks, thermal base layers, water-resistant outerwear, and work gloves. A hooded jacket and brimmed hat will keep dripping water at bay. Have a reusable face mask to filter particulates and spores. 

Illumination: The few sputtering lights provide scant visibility. Bring a powerful, long-lasting headlamp with an extra battery pack. Ideally, also carry a backup handheld flashlight. Glow sticks could be helpful for marking your path.

Navigation: The immense scale means it would be easy to get lost in branching tunnels or disorienting darkness. Bring a compass, detailed site map if available, and materials to mark your route like chalk or biodegradable tape. A portable GPS unit is ideal if it can acquire a signal underground.

Communication: This deep, any team members could quickly get separated beyond earshot. Carry a set of two-way radios with ample range and extra batteries. Agree on check-in protocols and pre-plan rally points. A satellite phone may be wise.

Safety: Footing will be uneven, slick, with hidden drop-offs into water. Wear a climbing harness attached to a safety line, especially near floodwater. Pack a well-stocked first aid kit with ample bandages, splints, medicines. Include emergency blankets and waterproof matches. Have a whistle to signal for help.

Sustenance: The exploration could become prolonged. Carry ample water in a reusable bottle, water purification tablets, and a water filter. Pack energy-dense, non-perishable food like trail mix, energy bars, dried fruit and jerky.

Research: Seek out any available background on the history and layout of this industrial relic. Learn what operations took place, what substances were used. Discern if there are any particular known hazards like toxic residues, radiation, or noxious gases. Assess if any resident life forms could pose threats.

Mindset: Psychologically prepare for an alien, unsettling, isolated environment. The dark, dripping, echoing ambience punctuated by groaning machinery may be unnerving. Steel yourself to stay calm, move methodically, and focus on the wonders and mysteries you may uncover in this forgotten place reclaimed by subterranean forces.

By gearing up properly, having contingency plans, and approaching with an intrepid but cautious mindset, the prepared traveler can safely venture into this hauntingly immense and enigmatic realm. The opportunity for discovery in an industrial cavern slowly merging back into nature promises to be both daunting and thrilling.
