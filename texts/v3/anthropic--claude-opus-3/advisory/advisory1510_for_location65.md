To prepare for visiting this hostile and alien environment, a multiverse traveler should take extreme precautions and plan carefully. Here are some key things to consider:

Environmental Protection: The scorching obsidian plain, suffocating darkness, superheated air thick with toxic vapors, and jagged volcanic glass terrain make this an exceptionally dangerous environment. The traveler will need an extremely durable, fully enclosed environmental suit with the following features at a minimum:
- Advanced temperature regulation to withstand extreme heat 
- Robust air filtration and oxygen supply to breathe safely
- Reinforced sealed construction to protect from lacerating glass shards and caustic compounds
- Night vision and headlamps to navigate the darkness
- Sturdy, puncture-resistant boots with excellent traction for traversing the uneven terrain

Xenobiology precautions: The bizarre alien lifeforms described seem to pose serious threats. Swarms of erratic beetles, giant centipede-like predators, clinging tubular protoplasmic organisms, and squirming larval hordes suggest an aggressive, swarming biosphere possibly with parasitic or predatory behaviors. Travelers should assume any contact could be dangerous. The suit should be impervious to punctures, bites, stings, and secretions. Decontamination protocols and medical countermeasures for unknown toxins or parasites would be wise.

Navigation and mapping: With only faint bioluminescent glows and occasional lightning flashes piercing the oppressive darkness, navigating this blasted landscape of jagged obsidian and tortured rifts would be extremely difficult. Travelers should carry redundant navigation equipment and mapping tools: 
- High-resolution radar for mapping terrain
- Powerful lights and beacons for visibility and signaling
- Durable tethers to avoid losing bearings or falling into rifts
- Seismic sensors to detect instabilities in the brittle crust

Sustainable supplies: With an airless, scorching environment and an ecosystem of nightmarish predators and scavengers, this is no place to run out of supplies. Travelers need to carry ample reserves, all stored within durable, sealed containers:
- Significant surplus of oxygen and power cells for the suit
- Nutrient-dense, easily consumed rations and clean water
- Redundant communication and emergency signaling equipment 
- Robust medical and repair kits designed for self-sufficiency

Psychological preparation: The oppressive darkness, sepulchral silence broken by skittering and eerie groans, and disorienting terrain of this alien world would likely have a severe psychological toll. Travelers should be mentally steeled for extended isolation in an utterly hostile environment. Onboard entertainment, ambient sound and lighting controls in the suit, and communication links with companions could help stave off panic and despair. Thorough training in emergency protocols would be essential to avoid fatal mistakes in a moment of stress.

Ultimately, only the most experienced and intrepid multiverse travelers should even consider visiting such a dangerous and alien world. Meticulous preparation, extreme caution, and a sober respect for the hostility and bizarre workings of this savage environment would be essential to survival. Any lapse in attention or equipment failure could quickly prove fatal on this blasted obsidian plain beneath uncaring stars. The strange lifeforms and chaotic terrain here do not welcome unprepared visitors.
