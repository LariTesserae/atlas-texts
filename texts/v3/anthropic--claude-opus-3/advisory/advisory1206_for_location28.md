To prepare for visiting this alien world, a multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Environmental Protection: Given the dry, airless environment, a self-contained, pressurized suit with a reliable oxygen supply is essential. The suit should also protect against any potentially harmful spores, pollen, or microorganisms that may be present in the atmosphere.

2. Navigation and Orientation: The dense, maze-like nature of the interconnected plant structures may make navigation challenging. Bring a reliable mapping and positioning system, as well as a means of marking your path to avoid getting lost in the alien foliage.

3. Communication: Ensure you have a robust communication system that can function in the presence of potentially disruptive electromagnetic fields generated by the plants. A backup analog system may be prudent.

4. Interaction with Flora and Fauna: Be cautious when interacting with the strange creatures and plant life. Bring tools to collect samples safely, and avoid disturbing the ecosystem unnecessarily. Be prepared for potential hostility from territorial or defensive organisms.

5. Data Collection and Analysis: To understand this world, bring a variety of scientific instruments to measure atmospheric composition, electromagnetic activity, and biological metrics. High-resolution imaging equipment will be vital for documenting the surreal plant structures and ancient ruins.

6. Shelter and Sustenance: While exploring, establish a secure base camp with a reliable airlock to rest, resupply, and analyze findings. Bring ample food and water, as the local flora is likely inedible and there may be no natural water sources.

7. Mental Preparation: The alien nature of this world may be psychologically challenging. Crew members should be mentally resilient, adaptable, and open to encountering the utterly unfamiliar. A sense of wonder tempered with caution will be essential.

8. Redundancy and Resilience: Given the unpredictable nature of this environment, all critical systems should have multiple redundancies. Bring ample spare parts, backup power sources, and a diverse set of tools to improvise repairs if needed.

9. Decontamination Protocols: Upon return, strict decontamination procedures must be followed to prevent any alien microorganisms or materials from being introduced to other environments.

10. Respect and Reverence: Approach this ancient, dying world with a sense of humility and respect. You are a guest in an ecosystem beyond your comprehension, one that has persisted for eons. Tread lightly, observe keenly, and let the wonder and mystery of this place remind you of the vast possibilities of the multiverse.
