To prepare for visiting this world of endless storms and floating cities, a multiverse traveler should take several key precautions:

Protective Gear: Completely waterproof, insulated clothing will be essential to withstand the ceaseless torrential rains and chill sea spray. Opt for sealed, rubberized full-body suits with built-in temperature regulation. Include a full helmet with a rebreather mask and voice amplification to communicate over the deafening roar of the storms. Polarized, heads-up goggles will help with visibility. Magnetized boots will provide traction on the slick metal surfaces of the cities.

Storm Shielding: Powerful electrical discharges crackle through the atmosphere from the immense thunderheads. A portable, ruggedized energy shield generator can provide a bubble of protection from stray lightning strikes and flying debris caught in the gale-force winds. It may also help repel the lashing downpours to some degree.

Flotation/Flight Capability: Travel between the cities will require either flight or nautical capability. An enclosed personal hovercraft with gyroscopic stabilizers could navigate both above and on the turbulent sea surface. Bring backups in case of mechanical failure in the punishing environment. Submersibles would allow exploration of the eerie bioluminescent depths but require more extensive life support.

Redundant Life Support: The traveler should have a fully self-contained life support system with ample reserves, including air recycling, water reclamation, heating/cooling, and waste management. Bring backup power cells and emergency rations. Medical gear including splints and sutures may be needed in case of injury.

Translation and Protective Charms: The city inhabitants may speak an unfamiliar language. Portable machine translation or magic-imbued linguistic aids could facilitate communication. Defensive charms against water, lightning, and any hostile creatures would be prudent as well.

Recording Equipment: To document this remarkable world, bring durable, waterproof audio/visual recording gear and a hardened computer to save data. Photos of the dramatic seascape, storm-swept cities and abyssal creatures would be stunning.

Offering of Goodwill: As a visitor, come bearing gifts to present to the city leaders - perhaps seeds of land crops long lost, or schematics for maintaining their ancient storm-shielding tech. This may gain permission to explore the cities and earn the trust of the inhabitants.

Overall, prepare for a harrowing but awe-inspiring voyage through a world of nature's fury and mankind's tenacity. Respect the dangers of the environment and the local culture. With thorough precautions, experiencing the floating cities' unearthly majesty amid the eternal tempest could be the journey of a lifetime for an intrepid traveler.
