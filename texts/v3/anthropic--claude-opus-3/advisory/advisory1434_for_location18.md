For an ordinary human multiverse traveler preparing to visit this ancient, overgrown desert city, there are several key considerations to ensure a safe and rewarding experience:

Clothing: Wear loose, light-colored, breathable fabrics that provide ample coverage to protect from the sun and insects. A wide-brimmed hat, long sleeves, and pants tucked into boots are essential. Pack a light jacket or sweater for cooler nights.

Footwear: Choose sturdy, comfortable boots with good ankle support and thick soles to navigate uneven terrain, rubble, and slippery surfaces. 

Supplies: Carry a backpack with ample water and electrolyte solutions to stay hydrated in the heat. Pack nutrient-dense, non-perishable food, as well as water purification tablets or a filter. Include a basic first-aid kit, insect repellent, sunscreen, and a reliable light source like a headlamp with extra batteries.

Navigation: Obtain detailed maps or satellite imagery of the city layout, and familiarize yourself with major landmarks. Carry a compass and GPS device, and know how to use them. Be prepared for areas where technology may not function reliably.

Communication: Bring a satellite phone or long-range radio to maintain contact with the outside world. Leave a detailed itinerary with someone trustworthy before embarking on your journey.

Shelter: Pack a lightweight, compact tent or hammock with mosquito netting for camping within the city ruins. A sleeping bag rated for warm temperatures and a moisture-wicking sleeping pad will provide comfort on hard surfaces.

Tools: Include a multi-tool, knife, rope, and duct tape for various needs. A small trowel or folding shovel may be useful for clearing debris or digging. 

Cultural Sensitivity: Research the history and cultural significance of the site before your visit. Treat any artifacts or structures with respect, and do not remove or damage anything. Be mindful of local customs or taboos that may apply, even to an abandoned place.

Health Precautions: Consult with a travel physician about necessary vaccinations or medications for the region. Be aware of potential hazards like venomous insects or plants, and know how to identify and avoid them. Stay alert for signs of heat exhaustion or dehydration.

Mental Preparation: Exploring an isolated, ancient site can be psychologically challenging. Be prepared for feelings of awe, disorientation, and even fear in the face of the unknown. Maintain a sense of curiosity and openness, while also setting clear boundaries for your own safety and well-being.

Documenting the Journey: Carry a notebook and writing implements to record your observations and experiences. A camera with ample storage and extra batteries will allow you to capture the unique sights and atmosphere of the city.

By preparing thoroughly and approaching the journey with respect, flexibility, and self-awareness, a multiverse traveler can immerse themselves in the wonder and mystery of this lush, ancient desert city, while minimizing risks and leaving minimal impact on the delicate, self-contained ecosystem. The experience promises to be one of profound discovery, inviting contemplation on the cycles of time, nature, and human civilization across the vast expanse of the multiverse.
