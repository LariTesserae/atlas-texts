In preparing to visit this awe-inspiring yet deeply unsettling mountain-city, an interdimensional traveler must gird themselves for an experience that will test the limits of sanity and perception. This is no mere sightseeing jaunt, but a plunge into a realm where the very fabric of reality warps under the weight of eldritch industry. One would be wise to pack as much for psychological and philosophical fortitude as for physical needs.

Sturdy boots with good traction are essential for navigating the treacherous, mist-slicked streets and bridges. The air grows thin at those altitudes, so some form of breathing aid—an oxygen concentrator, perhaps—could prove invaluable. Pack warm, layered clothing to fend off the omnipresent chill and damp, likely in dark, stain-concealing hues to blend into the smoggy shadows. A hooded coat and tinted goggles would help maintain anonymity and shield one's eyes from the otherworldly sights above. Leather gloves could provide both warmth and protection from the rusty, dripping, whirring mechanisms omnipresent throughout.

Navigational tools may prove unreliable as the laws of physics bend, but a compass and maps could at least provide psychological comfort. A flickering lantern filled with some self-replenishing bioluminescent substance seems more fitting than a flashlight. Earplugs may help dampen the incessant clanking and grinding, preserving a shred of serenity.

Given the strangeness of the local flora and fauna, a traveler should avoid contact unless knowledgeable of their properties. Bringing preserved rations from home, a water filtration system, and water-purifying tablets is advisable, as the steam-pipe condensation may be of dubious potability.

For self-defense against less-savory denizens, a discrete and versatile weapon is ideal—a well-weighted walking stick, perhaps, or a pneumatic dart gun. Something effective at range, as close combat seems ill-advised. A smokescreen generator could facilitate quick escapes.

Most crucial, however, is mental preparation. Bone up on Euclidean geometry so you can better grasp its violation. Study the uncanny valley so you can brace for the unsettlingly not-quite-humanoid. Meditate on the nature of reality, existence, and perception. Gaze into a fractal and practice remaining unperturbed by spiraling infinities. Journal to ground yourself in your own timeline and identity. Practice lucid dreaming so you can seize control should the boundaries of sleep and waking dissolve.

Pack a memento of home and sanity—a locket with a photo, a childhood keepsake—to clutch like a talisman when the abyss gazes back. Set up automated messages to be sent to loved ones at regular intervals, to be discontinued upon your return, so there's a record if you vanish into the veil.

And cultivate a mindset of awe, curiosity, and philosophical surrender. After all, if you wanted a tidy, predictable holiday, you'd visit a sunny resort with a guidebook in hand, not a mist-wreathed city warping at the seams of reality. Embrace the disorientation and let it recalibrate your understanding of what is possible. Plumb the depths of your psyche and marvel at their echoes in the non-Euclidean spires above. Peer into the grinding gears and flickering lights and glimpse the ghosts in the machine.

Pack a journal and pen to record your insights before they fray into mist. And pack humility, for a traveler to such a place is but a mote in the gears of an incomprehensible cosmic engine. The most profound souvenirs will be seared into your neurons, indelible as the arcane runes etched into the mountain stone, whispering of truths beyond the veil for those who dare to visit such liminal realms.
