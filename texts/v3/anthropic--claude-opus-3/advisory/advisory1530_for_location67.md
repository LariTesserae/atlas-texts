To prepare for visiting this awe-inspiring but inhospitable ancient city, a multiverse traveler should take several key precautions:

Altitude Acclimation: The thin air at this towering plateau's altitude can cause altitude sickness. Ascend gradually if possible, stay hydrated, and consider medication like acetazolamide. Be alert for headaches, nausea, and dizziness. Physical exertion will be more difficult, so take it slow and steady.

Protective Gear: The harsh, bare mineral environment and ancient ruins pose many risks. Wear sturdy boots with good traction for navigating crumbling architecture. A hard hat and leather gloves will protect against falling stone shards. Bring a respirator mask in case of airborne mineral dust. Apply sunscreen, as the thin atmosphere blocks less UV radiation.

Orientation & Navigation: The vast, sprawling ruins stretch to the horizon. Bring detailed maps, a compass, and GPS. Study the layout in advance, including major landmarks like aqueducts and monuments. Mark a clear trail to avoid getting lost in the maze-like skeleton of the city.

Water & Food: With only sparse moisture pooling and no flora, water and food must be carried in. Calculate ample water needs at high altitude and bring purification methods as backup. Pack lightweight, non-perishable, nutrient-dense rations like energy bars, nuts, and dried fruit.

Illumination: Bring multiple light sources - headlamps, flashlights, and glowing markers - to navigate dim interiors and fend off the disorienting shadows that congeal at dusk. The bioluminescent lichen may provide some surreal illumination.

Reverence for Nature: While the city is a petrified relic, it teems with ceaseless bird life. Respect these diverse species by keeping a distance from nests, especially the enormous eagle aeries atop eroded towers. Avoid loud noises or sudden movements that could disturb their activities. Remember, they are the living heart of this place.

Recording Equipment: The stark, surreal beauty of this world is like nothing else. Bring cameras, sketchpads, or a journal to capture the incredible vistas of endless ruins, the evocative interplay of silvery light on ancient stone, and the mesmerizing murmurations of starlings. The singing of the stone from countless bird calls must be heard to be believed.

Mental Preparation: Exploring a place where nothing remains but eternity, water, stone and wings may evoke profound emotions. The vast scale and utter emptiness could trigger awe, insignificance, loneliness, or existential dread. Prepare to confront these feelings. But also anticipate the humbling wonder of bearing witness to the ceaseless unfolding of nature in a place that once teemed with a now-vanished civilization. Embrace the mystical experience of standing at the crossroads of deep time.

With thorough preparation for the physical challenges and mental impact, a trip to this unparalleled location promises to be a transformative journey through layers of history, ecology, and the human spirit. The unfathomable mysteries and stark beauties of the towering bird city will surely leave an indelible impression on all who make the pilgrimage to walk its singing stones.
