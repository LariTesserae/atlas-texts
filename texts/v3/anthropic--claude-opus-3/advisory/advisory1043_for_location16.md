To prepare for visiting this mesmerizing underwater city, an ordinary human multiverse traveler should consider the following:

1. Breathing apparatus: Since the city is submerged, the traveler will need a reliable way to breathe underwater. This could be a advanced rebreather system that efficiently recycles air, or a compact oxygen supply with a long duration. The apparatus should be comfortable, easy to use, and provide a clear field of vision.

2. Pressure protection: The viscous, dense liquid atmosphere suggests the city is located at a significant depth. The traveler will require a pressure-resistant suit or a means of equalizing body pressure to prevent barotrauma. The suit should be flexible enough to allow for easy movement and should provide thermal insulation to protect against the warm water temperature.

3. Propulsion and navigation: To navigate the city's water-filled avenues and graceful arched tunnels, the traveler may benefit from a compact, hands-free propulsion device. This could be a small, self-contained jetpack or a set of fin-like attachments for the feet and hands. A wrist-mounted, waterproof navigation device with a 3D map of the city would help the traveler find their way and avoid getting lost.

4. Communication: To interact with the city's caretakers and understand their hauntingly beautiful language, the traveler might use a sophisticated translation device. This could be an AI-powered earpiece that provides real-time translation or a subvocal microphone that picks up the traveler's thoughts and translates them into the caretakers' language.

5. Microorganism protection: Given the rich abundance of suspended nutrients and microorganisms in the water, the traveler should be prepared for potential exposure to unfamiliar microbes. A full-body, high-tech filtration system integrated into the pressure suit could help prevent any adverse reactions or infections.

6. Light adaptation: The eerie, muted light and bioluminescent glow of the city may be disorienting to human eyes. The traveler's helmet or goggles should have adjustable light filters and a built-in low-light amplification system to help them see clearly in the unique lighting conditions.

7. Respect for the ecosystem: The traveler must understand that the city is a delicate, balanced ecosystem, and their presence could potentially disrupt this harmony. They should take care to minimize their impact, avoid touching the structures, and follow any guidelines provided by the caretakers.

8. Mindset and adaptability: Visiting an environment so alien and unfamiliar will require an open mind, a sense of wonder, and a willingness to embrace the unknown. The traveler should be mentally prepared for the sensory experiences and the feeling of timelessness that pervades the city.

By carefully preparing with the right equipment, protection, and mindset, the multiverse traveler can fully immerse themselves in the beauty and mystery of this underwater city, experiencing its dynamic growth and evolution firsthand while respecting its delicate balance.
