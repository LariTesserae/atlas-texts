The Stoneward's Song

Tckk-tck-tckk... shkk-kss... tckk-tck-tckk...

From the deeps, the long-held tone,
Granite-hewn, through chitin flows.
In crystal eyes, the ages sown,
Where secrets sleep in dark repose.  

Tckk-tck-tckk... shkk-kss... tckk-tck-tckk...

Timeless guardian, lithic-skinned,
Silent-poised 'mid fern and moss. 
Stony wisdom, ancient-ginned,
Watches life's slow gyre and toss.

Tckk-tck-tckk... shkk-kss... tckk-tck-tckk...

In shadowed grottos, water-seeped,
Where mica-dappled lichens creep,
And beetle-jewels ember-heap,
The stoneward's patient aeons keep.

Tckk-tck-tckk... shkk-kss... tckk-tck-tckk...

Through geode-eyes, the realms behold—
Above, the fleeting stars a-turn,
Below, the primal pulse enfolds
In caverned hush and creviced urn.

Tckk-tck-tckk... shkk-kss... tckk-tck-tckk...

The long song echoes, fade and swell, 
Strange notes from deep telluric well,
Where stone and life entwined dwell,
And myths in eon-lock dispel.

Tckk-tck-tckk... shkk-kss... tckk-tck-tckk...

Arcane refrain from dark terrene,
Thrum-hummed from lithic larynx keen,
In mineral dance and muted mien, 
The stoneward sounds the spaces between.

Tckk-tck-tckk... shkk-kss... tckk... tck... tckk...

The signal breaks, the wavelength slips,
To crystal static, grinding skips.
In broken beats and arrhythmic blips,  
The transmission thins to creviced rips...

...and sinks to silence, fathoms-deep,
Where only stone-dreams stir from sleep.
