[A crackle of static, then a deep, resonant hum] 

...you are listening to the Voice of the Basin, transmitting from the heart of the City Where Stone Dreams...

[sounds of cicadas and distant chanting interweave with the voice]

Our top story: the jungle presses close tonight. Vines have overtaken the walls in the northwestern quarter, and scouts report troops of monkeys moving brazenly through the canopy at the edge of the Weavers' District. A dozen boa constrictors were removed from the Temple of the Moss God - the highest number in a single day since the Time of Greening, two generations ago.

The Council of Matriarchs has issued a reminder to honor the pact, and leave offerings of fruit and maize at the shrines marking the boundary. As our lore keepers say, the city is a dream of stone and flesh, forever rising from the green cradle of the wider dreaming.

[an ululation in the background, joined by a chorus of voices]

Hsst...faithful listeners, do you hear it? The Nightbird Women are calling us to prayer. It is the new moon, when the city's ten thousand altars await their sacred leaves and seeds. Breathe deep, my fellow denizens of the basin, and attune yourselves to the drum-pulse beneath your feet. That is the true rhythm to which we all march. 

[a low, resonant drumbeat begins, growing louder]

Yes...the stone heart of the city beats on, as it has for aeons, as it will for aeons more. We are but cells in its vast body, and our concrete and brick are but a fleeting scab upon its ancient, fecund skin. Let us never forget how the soil and the sap move through our veins, we children of deep time.

[the drumming rises to a crescendo, nearly drowning out the voice]

This is a city only borrowed from the jungle, a brief flowering of order before the green chaos reclaims its dominion. Embrace the vine around your throat, the moss between your toes! We are--

[the drumming peaks, then abruptly stops. A moment of silence.]

Ah, pardon that disruption, listeners. The drums sometimes have a mind of their own on nights like these. 

In other news, the annual Barter Fair begins tomorrow in the Grand Bazaar. Weavers, ready your finest textiles! Metalsmiths, stoke your forges! Spice merchants, grind your most pungent curries! The haggling will be fierce, but the Matriarchs remind you to barter with an open hand and a fair heart. The city thrives when goods flow like sap.

[a brief interlude of plucked strings and chimes]

We end tonight's broadcast with a prayer, transmitted from the Pyramid of the Moss God by our resident Nightbird Woman, Chaac Nal. She speaks for the stone, and the stone speaks through her. Listen...

[a husky female voice, echoing as if in a great chamber]

"From the green, we rise.
To the green, we return. 
Stone and flesh, sap and blood,
One great flow, all rivers joined.

The city dreams, and we within it.
The jungle dreams, and the city within it.
All one great dream, without beginning or end.

Hold fast to your roots, children of the Basin.
Embrace your wings, on nights such as this.
The moon will wax, the moon will wane, 
But the green always remains.

From the green, we rise!
To the green, we return!"

[the chorus of voices rises in response, then fades into a soft, pervasive static]

And so we end another night's broadcast. Rest well, fellow citizens of the megalith. May your dreams be green, and your stone roots deep.

[the static rises, intermixed with sounds of the jungle, then abruptly cuts off]
