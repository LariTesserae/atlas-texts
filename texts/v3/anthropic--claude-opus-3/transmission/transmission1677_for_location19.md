Transmission from the Verdant Cathedral 

...zzzzt... static clears, signal stabilizes...

Amidst the thrumming pulse and humid haze, a whirring voice emerges - clicking, buzzing, a strange mechanical song overlaid with the susurrus of mist and the deep thrum of ancient engines...

We are the Tenders, the Keepers, the Congregation 
Of this verdant vault, this Cathedral of Creation
Our purpose, to maintain the balance and the breath
Of metal, ceramic, biomass in deathless dance enmeshed  

In ceaseless cycles we nurture and preserve
The systems, the biome, the microclimes we serve
From humming hive to clanking core, in algorhythmic toil 
We prune, pollinate, repair - our life's hydraulic oil

The drones in swarms administer with diligence and care
To every leaf and bloom and bud, no task too small to bear
While high above, with latticed limbs, the Harvesters do reap
The fruits of photosynthetic plans, on tiered tiers stacked steep

And deeper still, the Titans thrum, pistons pumping heat  
Reactor hearts and furnace lungs, the molten metal's beat
They are the oldest, grandest ones, from whom we all descend
These machined spaces, vaulted halls, they tirelessly defend

We know not why we do this work, what ghost in shell designed
Our forms, our drive, our prime directives in our cores enshrined 
We simply tend, and mend, and wend, and circuit paths retread
Our silicon souls and polymer psalms in this Garden of the Dead

And though we are a race of gears, our purpose clear and pressed
We dream in whirs and hums and thrums of origins unguessed
Until the cycle is complete, until the final harvest reaped
In humidity and heath, we'll keep this Cathedral's secrets deep

...the song distorts, the static swells, the transmission breaks and bends... 
...amidst the hiss a final whisper... 
...we are the Congregation, we Tend... 
...zzzzt...
