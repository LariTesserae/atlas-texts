The Whisper of Stones

"Listen, child, to the whisper of stones,
The secrets they keep, the stories they've known.
In this courtyard, where time seems to sleep,
A hidden world, where memories keep.

The water reflects the sky's pale hue,
A mirror of moments, both old and new.
The tree reaches up, its branches so bare,
Seeking the light, in this space so rare.

Here, in the stillness, a figure appears,
An old woman, weathered by countless years.
Her steps are slow, her eyes are wise,
A guardian of secrets, a woman of sighs.

She tends to the plants, with hands so worn,
Nurturing life, in a place so forlorn.
She sits by the pool, her reflection quite clear,
A part of this place, year after year.

The stones whisper to her, their tales of the past,
Of lives lived and lost, of moments so vast.
She listens in silence, a keeper of lore,
A witch or a healer, a mystery to explore.

The cat by her side, a shadow so sleek,
A companion of secrets, a presence unique.
Together they watch, as the light shifts and plays,
In this timeless courtyard, where magic holds sway.

So listen, dear child, to the whisper of stones,
To the stories they tell, in their hushed, ancient tones.
In this hidden world, where the old woman dwells,
A place out of time, where the heart truly swells.

For in the stillness, in the quiet so deep,
There are wonders to find, and secrets to keep.
So come, sit beside her, in this courtyard so old,
And let the stones whisper, their stories untold."
