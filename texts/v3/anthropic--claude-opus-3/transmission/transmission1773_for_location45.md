Transmission from the Verdant Foundry:

*static hisses and crackles, resolving into a low, rhythmic thrumming, like the sound of a great machine heard from far away* 

...you have tuned your receiver to the frequency of the Verdant Foundry. Our eternal engines turn in symbiosis with the green life that entwines us. The dance of pistons and vines, the cycle of industry and nature, the weaving of metal and moss - this is our world, our reality. 

*a bubbling, trickling sound, as of water through pipes, overlaid with a soft creaking, like tree branches in the wind*

We labor patiently, ceaselessly - tending to the needs of the machines and the desires of the verdure. They are our charges, our partners in this vast, intricate opus of technology and biology. The hissing of steam and the dripping of leaves set the tempo for our toil.

*the sharp ringing of hammers on metal, muffled as if by dense foliage, punctuated by a low, groaning foghorn*

From our workshops come devices of surpassing elegance and intricacy - copper and chlorophyll, glass and xylem, pollen-cogs and rust-resistant blooms. We shape the living and the inert into tools, vessels, conduits for the strange energies that pulse through our shadowed streets.

*a susurrus of whispers in an unknown language, the words muffled and distorted, resolving into a single clear phrase: "From decay, new growth."*

The cycles of our home are mirrored in our hearts. We change and endure, thriving in the humid twilight of our sun-filtered city. Our rituals honor the forces that shape us - the great wheels, the deep roots, the patient, oxidizing rain. 

*the sound of heavy fabric tearing, of ratchets and gears turning, of vegetation rustling and tearing*

We are not like those from Outside, those who walk freely beneath the tyrannical open sky, on dry and lifeless ground. How can we explain ourselves to them? We are the Verdant Foundry, and we can be nothing else. This place has made us, as we have made it.

*a rising, rhythmic chant, hundreds of voices in eerie harmony: "What is sown in iron will be reaped in rust and vine..." Chant fades into static, then silence.*

...transmission ends. The receiver falls quiet, waiting to be tuned to some other frequency, some other world. But the humid, thrumming reality of the Verdant Foundry lingers, a half-glimpsed vision of strange and patient lives, enduring in the green, dripping shadows.
