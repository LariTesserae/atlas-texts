Transmission: Xel'thari Resonance

[A low, thrumming hum fills the air, punctuated by eerie, resonant tones that rise and fall in haunting cadences. The sounds seem to emanate from the city itself, as if the ancient structures and machines are giving voice to some inscrutable purpose.]

Khl'Xaath, vr'Xel'thari. Tz'nath kor'vaal, syn'kath mor'zhal.

[The tones shift, becoming more complex and layered, conveying a sense of deep history and ancient knowledge.]

Vor'nath kyl'dath, Xel'thari'syn. Kor'vaal, Khl'Xaath. Mor'zhal, syn'kath.

[A series of sharp, staccato pulses interrupts the flowing cadence, urgent and insistent.]

Kyl'dath! Vor'nath, kyl'dath! Syn'kath, mor'zhal, Khl'Xaath!

[The tones rise in intensity, the air thrumming with palpable energy as the mineral deposits throughout the city begin to pulse in synchronicity with the sounds.]

Xel'thari, vor'nath. Khl'Xaath, syn'kath. Mor'zhal, kor'vaal.

[The resonant tones begin to diverge and harmonize, creating an eerie, discordant chorus that echoes through the city's vast spaces. Beneath the cacophony, a single, clear voice emerges, speaking in a language beyond human comprehension.]

Kyl'dath vor'nath, Xel'thari'syn. Mor'zhal syn'kath, Khl'Xaath kor'vaal. Tz'nath, Xel'thari. Tz'nath, vor'nath.

[As abruptly as it began, the transmission ends, the sudden silence broken only by the faint, persistent hum of the city's ancient machines. The air seems to vibrate with the aftershocks of the Xel'thari resonance, a fading echo of the unknowable purpose that drives this enigmatic race and their timeless city.]
