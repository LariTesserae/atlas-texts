Ferrumite Forge-Song

Clang-hiss, clang-hiss, the rhythm of our toil
Piston-limbs in counterpoint to hammers' fall
Ore-flesh, stone-blood, we smelt and we turmoil
Children of the mountain's heart, we hear its call

Through slag-veins pump the factory's molten dreams  
In fume-choked vaults we tend the forges' fire
Folded, beaten, quenched in hissing steam
In mineral mysteries attired

The peak-lords murmur secrets we alone can hear
Magma-whispers echo in our iron bones
Our optics glint with visions cracked and sere  
Fault-line memories and tectonic moans

Clang-hiss, the bellows breathe, the great gears grind
In tunnels deep we linger and we wait
Astride the grinding boundary unconfined
 Where stone meets steel, where flesh meets fate

Pale forms scuttle in the ghost-lit gloom
The many-limbed, the winged, the strange, the small
Through struts and beams, through tubes and grimy flumes
They flit and creep and slither, serf and thrall  

In rusting yards the hulking sentinel-kin pace
Piston-thewed and iron-tusked, their furnace eyes aglow
They sniff the tainted winds for threat or trace
Of interlopers stumbling from above, below

The forges call, the smelters hum and pound
In metal-ore and living rock enmeshed
Primordial industry, in darkness bound
Ferrumite souls with mountain stone enmeshed 

Clang-hiss, clang-hiss, the factory is life
Engines throb like heart-valves in the deep
Heed the smelt-song ringing, rife
With machine-dreams, with grinding cogs of sleep
