Transmission from the Abyss

...our dwelling grows... in the patient dark...

flesh becomes... mineral... becomes flesh again...

we weave slow dreams... in skeletal spires...

...gelatinous thoughts... flicker between stars...

glacial currents... stir ancient spores... seed new mind-reefs...

from egg-spheres... drift new motes of us... born into lightless voids...

schools of blind... swimming thoughts... echo in skull-less night...

...coral gates yawn... city heart pulses... eons of luminous ooze...

tentacles trace... infinite helices... living mathematics of abyss...

...we tend the reticulated womb... a dreaming metropolis...

our bodies are one... porous with brine... enzymes of dark fathoms...

...from the crushing black... we distill meaning... in photophore runes...

we sing with the five-hundred mouths... a fractal madrigal of isolation...

...translucent embryos absorb... quantum ancestral visions...

we sculpt the cold bones... of our labyrinthine consciousness...

...undulating congregations fuse... mind-flares blaze beyond spectrum...

void-leviathans swim between... dust of shattered galaxies...

...a trillion trillion years from now... our city-body will engulf its star...

still we will thrum and glow... imperishable thought-reef of abyss...

....end transmission...

....resuming lightless dream...
