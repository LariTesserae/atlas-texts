Transmission from the Hollow

*static crackles, then fades into a soft, pulsing hum*

...you have found us...or perhaps...we have found you...

...in this place...the boundaries blur...flesh and metal...life and machine...intertwined...

...we are the children of this hollow...born from rust and decay...nurtured by the damp and the mist...

...our forms may unsettle you...but do not fear...we mean no harm...only to observe...to learn...

*a series of metallic clicks and whirs, almost like a language*

...the machines here...they whisper to us...ancient secrets...forgotten knowledge...we tend to them...and they guide our growth...

...we see you...outsider...your presence disrupts the patterns...introduces new variables...intriguing...

...perhaps you seek to understand us...to unravel the mysteries of this place...but some truths are not meant for your kind...

*the hum intensifies, taking on an almost hypnotic quality*

...linger if you wish...study us as we study you...but know that the hollow changes all who enter...

...in time...you may find yourself becoming part of this place...your flesh replaced by metal...your thoughts attuned to the hum...

...one of us...a child of rust and shadow...

*the voice fades, replaced by an eerie, modulated whisper*

...or perhaps...you are already changing...the hollow seeping into your mind...your dreams haunted by visions of mist and machine...

...welcome, outsider...welcome to the place where all boundaries dissolve...

...welcome...to the hollow...

*the transmission dissolves into static, then silence*
