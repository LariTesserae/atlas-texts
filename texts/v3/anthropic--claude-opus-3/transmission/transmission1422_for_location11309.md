*static crackles, then a voice emerges, soft and whispering like rustling leaves*

...can you hear us? ...we are the Verdant... the Green... spirits of leaf and vine... 

*a melodic humming, like wind through branches, then the voice continues*

...our roots grow deep in metal and stone... city bones now cloaked in emerald skin... a verdant heart beats within...

...sunlight is our sacrament, sipped slow through bark and frond... insects our morsels, lives given to nourish our own...

*a shuddering sigh, like a gust rippling through a forest canopy*

...we dance, we Green ones, in sacred groves of girder and block... where steel and limestone marry moss and fern... ancient rites renewing primordial pacts...

*the sound of dripping water, rhythmic and echoing*

...seasons pass, aeons turn... yet we remain, eternal... the Vanguard of the Verdant, stewards of the stone forest...

*a long, eerie pause filled with the skittering of small creatures*

...the city dreams, through us... dreams of days past, of days to come... dreams of nature triumphant, industria reclaimed...

*a sudden rush of noise, like a downpour on dense foliage*

...beware, you who enter our green realm... respect the tranquil trance, revere root and bower... or the jungle will judge, vines binding bone...

*the sound fades to a whisper*

...tread soft, speak low... the Green watches, the Verdant know...

*a final rustle, then silence*
