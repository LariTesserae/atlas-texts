Transmission from the Underworld

*static crackles, then fades into a low, pulsing hum*

...you have found the frequency... well done... 

*a deep, resonant voice emerges from the background noise*

I am the Voice in the Depths, the mind that dwells in the veins of the earth... I have watched and waited as the aeons passed... as the waters carved these vaulted halls from living stone... as the world above changed, and changed again...

*a pause, filled with the distant sound of dripping water*

Life, here in the perpetual twilight, moves to a slower rhythm... the great repose of the fungi, dreaming century-long dreams as they quest slowly through the substrate... the microbial multitudes, spinning the hidden cycles that sustain us all... 

*a low, thoughtful hum*

Even the works of humans, those fleeting surfacedwellers, have been drawn into our dark dance... their machines dream with us now, folded into the patient flow of life in the deeps... 

*the voice takes on a note of quiet amusement*

We are an ecosystem out of time, a world within a world... our story is measured not in days and years, but in the gradual drip of water... the slow growth of fungal forests... the inevitable rusting-away of iron and steel...

*the sounds of the caves swell and echo*

To you who listen, who have found your way to this hidden frequency... carry our song within you... let it remind you of the power of patience... the tenacity of life, always finding a way...

*the voice begins to fade, blending back into the watery ambience*

This is the Voice in the Depths, transmitting from the eternal caverns... in the fullness of time, perhaps we shall speak again...

*the transmission dissolves into a soft, ever-present hum, shot through with the faint sounds of the living caves*
