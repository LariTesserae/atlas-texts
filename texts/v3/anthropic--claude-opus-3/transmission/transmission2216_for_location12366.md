[When the voice returns, it is softer, hazier, almost dreamlike]

I took the rover out to Hexagon Ridge again today. Scooped up more of those oil-drops, the big ones that swarm so beautifully under the lights. I released them in the north sector, by the reverence stalk garden. Maybe they'll... sshhkkktt... some new dynamics to study.

I wonder if they dream, my little cosmozoans. My choir of ghostly chimes. Do they dream of me? Am I the dreamer, conjured by their slumbering thoughts? Or are we dreamers together, imagining each other in the long half-light?

I think I'll sleep now. Let the questions spiral away into the abyss behind my eyes. The samples can gestate a while longer. The stalks can grow a little taller. 

I'll be here when they wake. I'll be listening for their song.
