Whispers in the Mist

... zzzzt ... crackle ... pop ... 

... the mist clings thick today, curling tendrils weaving through branches, caressing mossy trunks ... drip, drip, drip, the constant rhythm of water on leaves, on stone ... 

... they are here, moving in the shadows, flitting shapes at the edges of perception ... 
rustling foliage and creaking boughs their only trace ...

... an ancient presence, steeped in the valley's very essence ... not entirely of this world, yet inextricably bound to it ... 

... soft, lilting voices on the breeze, an ethereal chorus ... whispers of long-forgotten tongues, secrets carried in the damp air ...

... the trees murmur to each other, a patient, unhurried discourse ... roots deep, drinking in the mountain's hidden springs, sipping the knowledge of ages ...

... the Green Warden watches, as it always has, cloaked in living moss ... a shepherd of roots and branches, a guardian of sacred stones ... 

... eyes glimmer in the dim light, fey and knowing, seeing what others cannot ... the boundary is thin here, a veil easily parted by those who know the way ...

... mist-shrouded, timeless, a place where dreams and myths still walk ... where the mundane world fades and other realms draw near ...

... tread softly, wanderer, and listen ... for in the whispers of the valley, ancient secrets are still spoken ... 

... zzzzt ... crackle ... the signal fades, mist swirling, coiling ... 
until once more the valley keeps its own counsel, and the whispers fall silent.

... pop ... hissss ...
