*static hisses and crackles, then a voice emerges, raspy and halting, as if the speaker is unused to forming words*

Wet...everything wet. Always wet. The air, the rock, the flesh. We are born from wetness, and in wetness we rot away. 

*a chittering sound, like insectoid laughter*

You marvel at the beasts, yes? But they are kin, not oddities. Spawned from the same primordial moisture as we. Shaped by the same clammy clays, schooled in the same dripping crannies and caves. Their forms are echoes of our forms.

*a pause, filled with a low undulating hum, almost a chant*

The moss is our mantle, the fungus our food. Their fibers clothe us, their flesh fills our bellies and builds our bones. We are remade, day by day, of their seeping substance.

*the hum rises in volume and pitch, taking on a droning, ritualistic quality*

We carve temples from damp stone, only to watch them soften and slump back into their original curves and crevices. We scratch histories on sheets of spore-flesh, knowing they will dissolve and feed new tomes.

*the chanting swells to a brief, ecstatic peak, then cuts off abruptly. The speaker's voice returns, low and insistent*

There is nothing here that does not change, and feed, and change again. Moisture rises as mist, condenses, flows, seeps, drips. Beasts birth and burst and decay into sludge. Sludge feeds fungus. Fungus feeds beast. Beast feeds beast. And we, in our small, damp corner, dance the same dance.

*a long, gurgling exhalation, like the last gasp of a drowning victim*

Your dry eyes see only the surface. Grotesquerie and chaos. But there is pattern beneath the pulsing skin of this place. Ancient, relentless rhythms of rot and regeneration. We are its instrument, and its melody, and its enduring medium.

*the voice trails off into liquid, labored breathing, then is swallowed by another burst of static. The transmission abruptly ends.*
