Transmission: Echoes of the Rust Dream

... static hisses and crackles, then slowly resolves into a low, rhythmic pulsing, like the heartbeat of some vast, slumbering machine ...

Whispers beneath the waves, secrets in the rust,
Echoes of a world unmade, a dream of metal dust.
We walk between the raindrops, dance on oil-slick streams, 
Tending to the ghosts of light, the fungal glowworm gleams.

Fading... fading... the signal breaks up momentarily, lost in a burst of harsh, metallic noise. Then...

Once, great gears spun, belts whirred, the dynamos did sing,
Now corroded gods lie still, while rust-flaked angels wing
Their way between the girders, sending ripples through the marsh,
Reality grows thin here, the veil a tattered scarf.

Do you hear it? The machine choir, the hum behind the world? 
In this place of change and flux, the Real comes unfurled.
We midwife its becoming, with rites of steel and flesh,
The Shaman-song of entropy, the marsh's metal mesh.

The voice takes on an echoing, reverent quality, as if reciting a prayer or incantation.

Rust is sanctified decay, the holy death of form,
By it, we all are changed, reborn, our boundaries deformed.
The marsh, our sacred liminal space, where metal turns to loam,
And flesh takes on a metallic grace, the truth of rust made known.

So come, you seekers of the Real, you nomads of the Dream,
Sift meaning from the silted mists, decipher glyphs of steam.
But tread with care in Shaman realm, where time and space grow strange...
For we corrode all certainty, and rust is more than change.

The last word echoes eerily, then fades into a deep, resonant hum shot through with static. The transmission continues, but it seems to be cycling back through previous snippets in a disjointed, dream-like loop, the order shifting with each repetition. Snatches of prayer, poetry, and metallic sound blur together, hinting at strange and secret histories. The broadcast continues, an endless, hypnotic cipher - inviting interpretation, but never quite yielding to understanding...
