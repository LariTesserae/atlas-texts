The Unending Resonance

...the signal wavers, warps, re-forms. Static hisses, subsides. A low, pervasive hum emerges, felt as much as heard - a deep, droning tone that vibrates in the bones. It rises gradually, taking on the cadence and timbre of a wordless, atonal chant. Layered harmonies drift in and out of focus, suggesting glimpses of arcane pattern. 

The chanting swells, multiplies. Dozens of voices weave together into a seething tapestry of sound, each thread distinct yet inseparable from the whole. Embedded within their interwoven music: the scratch of quill on parchment, the clink and whir of esoteric machinery, the mournful whisper of mountain winds. The chant crests and rolls like a stone ocean, its rhythms precisely controlled yet thrumming with a wild, subliminal energy.

Notes ascend on an austere melodic line, yearning and ethereal. Dissonances creep in at the edges - keening microtones that set the teeth on edge. The voices ululate, fracture into overtones and glossolalia. Meaning dissolves into abstraction, yet the impression of vast, occluded significance remains. It is a song of cryptic rapture, of the ineffable mysteries of cosmos and psyche.

Gnomic utterances surface amid the polyphony, intoned in a dialect at once archaic and unplaceable:

"In the folds of the mountain, the eye that is not an eye dwells..."

"What is uttered in stone shall one day echo in stars..."

"The key and the gate are one..."

The chant spirals outward into anarchy, a maelstrom of ecstatic cacophony. Then, with preternatural abruptness, it ceases. In the shocked silence that follows, the drone persists, sinks to a subaudial murmur. A metallic tang, as of bronze bells shivering in a gale, ghosts across the aural periphery.

All the while, something else has lain beneath the soundscape, never quite rising to audibility - a presence, watchful and ancient, palpable yet utterly ineffable. It defies language or exegesis, this cryptic sentience at the heart of stone and gale.

The transmission dissolves back into the susurrus of empty air, leaving in its wake an unquenchable ache, an unresolved koan that lingers long after the last echoes fade.
