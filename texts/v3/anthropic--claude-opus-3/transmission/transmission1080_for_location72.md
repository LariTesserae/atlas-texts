*static crackles*

...the veil thins...reality flickers...

*a voice emerges, echoing and distorted*

From the ancient stone we call, 
A city on the edge of all,
Where sky shimmers in uncertain light,
And ground beneath is never quite

Solid.
Real.
Known.

We are the Keepers of this place,
Tending garden, ritual, and sacred space, 
Preserving what is, amidst the fray
Of worlds that warp and fray.

*the voice shifts, becomes a chant*

Stone we shape and metal bend,
Water flows to serve and mend.
Patterns woven, symbols sewn,
Meanings kept by us alone.  

*a rumble builds beneath the words*

The Temple whispers, glyphs alight,
Of secret ways and hidden sight.
We dance between the here and there,
The real and un-, the foul and fair.

*static swells then recedes*

...children born to shifting skies...
...elders' knowing, ageless eyes...

*the voice returns, more insistent*

We are the Waking Dreamers here, 
Alive in spite of ceaseless fear
That all we are could end, unstart,
Reality torn apart.

*a rising, rhythmic chorus of voices*

Yet on we press, strange and proud,
Our fragile truth to shout aloud.
This city stands, though realms may fall,
And we, its Heart, endure through all!

*the transmission breaks into static, then goes silent*
