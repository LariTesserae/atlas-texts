Transmission from the Floating Cities of Zephyria 

... static crackles ... signal stabilizing ... 

This is Archivist Zain Tk'li of the Drifting Metropolis Caelinar, broadcasting on all frequencies. If there are other survivors of the shattering out there, know that life endures among the bones of our broken world.

For generations, we Zephyrians have made our homes in the floating cities, stitched together from the remnants of our once vibrant planet. Vast webs of salvaged cables anchor our towers and markets to the hollow asteroids, all that remains of Zephyria's core.

In the thin wind between spires, our winged scouts soar, scanning the fragments for signs of resources and lost technology. The hum of ancient atmospherics and recyclers fills the air, punctuated by the distant calls of metal-feathered avians.  

Within the cities, we Zephyrians go about our duties with solemn purpose - spinning webs, bartering relics, encoding our history into the crystalline archives. The Cablers dart between towers, making repairs. Heavily armored Forgemasters emerge from salvage tunnels, bearing components to repair and repurpose.

In the carved rock halls, Zephyrian artisans shape mosaics of colored glass and burnished steel - shimmering depictions of the world that was, in all its lost majesty and splendor. At the high observatories, the Void Whisperers sing their eerie songs to the stars, divining hints of a future in the cosmic emissions.

We remember, and we persevere.  Though our world is broken, our species endures. We find beauty in the shattered remains, elegance in entropy, hope in adaptation. The cities drift on, aloft on ancient repulsors and the tenacity of life itself.

One cycle, perhaps our scavengers or scouts will uncover some key to restoring the planet - a lost technology or terraforming secret from the days before the shattering. Until then, we maintain. We salvage. We weave our cities and our culture anew with each generation.

To any who hear this message, know that the heart of Zephyria still beats. Our songs still echo between the stars and the floating spires. We are the curators of the ruin, the guardians of memory.

And we will not fade quietly into the void.
