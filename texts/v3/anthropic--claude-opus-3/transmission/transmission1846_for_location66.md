Whispers on the Wind

... krrrrrssshhhh ... krrrrssshhhh ... the endless susurration of the high peaks, where stone meets sky ... krrrssshhh ... only the keenest ears can pluck the threads of meaning from the thin air ... krrrssshhhh ...

... chkkk ... chkkk ... chkkk ... the Dweller of the Heights stirs, preening stormcloud pinions with an obsidian beak ... chkkk ... a king's ransom in diamonds scatters from its wings with each stroke, winking in the merciless sun ... chkkk ...

... skreeeeeee! skreeeeeeee! ... the Roc's cry pierces the marrow, sets teeth on edge and small lives scurrying to bolt-holes ... skreeeeeee! ... it is the sound of fear and fealty, the tithe owed to the Lord of the High Places ... skreeeeeee! ...

... whummmmmmm. whummmmmmm. ... vast wings beat the air, drumming the currents into submission ... whummmmmmm. ... thermal and zephyr yawn wide to grant passage to the undisputed sovereign of these skies ... whummmmmmm. ...

... grooooooooo. grooooooooo. ... a throne of scree and sky rumbles beneath cruelly taloned feet ... grooooooooo. ... lichen and pebble crunch like bones, the sound of centuries compacting in the Roc's claws ... grooooooooo. ...

... eeeeaaaaaaaa. eeeeaaaaaaaa. ... from the nest-aerie, the thin, demanding keen of the ravenous chick ... eeeeaaaaaaaa. ... a prince is born, and the heights ring with its imperious summons ... eeeaaaaaaaa ...

... whoooooooosssssssshhhhhh ... the wind rises, the endless skirling that flenses the peaks to their bare bones ... whoooooooosssssssshhhhhh ... in that elemental voice, in the cries of raptors and rock-fall, the mountain speaks ... whooooooosssssssshhh ...

... krrrrrrrrrrrsssshhhhhhhh ... fading, the transmission hisses into silence once more ... krrrrrssshhhh ... only stone and sky and the great storm-grey wings remain, fading into mist ... krrrrssshhhh ...

... there is no more. only the wind has voice now. the Roc is myth and mirage, lost to all but legend and the deathless stone.

... krrrrrrsssshhhh ...

... end transmission.
