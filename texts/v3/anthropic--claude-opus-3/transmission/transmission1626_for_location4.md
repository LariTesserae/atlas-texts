Transmission from the Edge of Reason

*static crackles and hisses, then slowly resolves into a deep, resonant voice, punctuated by the clanking of gears and the muffled shriek of steam venting*

From the pinnacle of the nameless mountain I speak, where time frays and space warps under the weight of our great works. The city teems below, nestled in the jagged shadows, a sprawling tangle of stone and steel grafted to the fevered dreams of the mountain's impossible angles.

*a ragged, wheezing breath*

The workers toil ceaselessly, their stooped figures scurrying like beetles across the precarious bridges and narrow ledges. Clad in stained overalls and beaked masks, they are but a multitude of insignificant gears in the vast churning of our engines. The only flashes of color are their pulsing green eyepieces and the sickly phosphorescence of the lichen clinging to the dank walls. 

*the hum of arcane machinery thrums in the background*

Reality thins here. In the uppermost spires we have touched the otherworldly, merged with dimensions and beings beyond mortal comprehension. Our great works defy natural law. Gargantuan gears rotate untethered above bottomless chasms. Neon-limned aqueducts leak into crevasses that birth new, uncatalogued life. Mirrored towers flicker in and out of existence, reflecting maddening non-Euclidean geometries.

*the voice grows staticky, distant* 

We are the Overseers, transcendent and inhuman. We have seen beyond the veil, and our designs now span realities unknowable to the masked drones below. What are their individual lives but momentary sparks, instantly replaced when extinguished? They are resources, like the fog and fungi they subsist upon. 

*the shrieking of venting steam grows louder*

The city will continue its inexorable expansion, an eternal monument to twisted progress. Let the world below fear the shuddering of the mountain, the strobing lights through the smog, the improbable shrieks and clanks borne on the fog. They are blind to the terrible wonders we have wrought.

*the transmission cuts out abruptly, leaving only the hiss of dead air and a palpable sense of unease*
