Transmission from the Depths

... .... .. . .  .. .  . ... . .. ...  . .  . .. ... .. . .. .. .  ... .. .. .. ... ... .. . .. .. . ... . . .. .. 
[Static and intermittent clicks, like stone tapping on stone]

...Greetings from the deep...
[A soft, sibilant voice emerges from the background noise]

We are the Dwell-kin... the Stone-bound... the Troglodytes of the Undercity of Lyth'raxis. For generations beyond count, we have made our home in the cool, damp embrace of the living rock. Here, in the perpetual twilight of bioluminescent fungi, we have carved out an existence uniquely our own.

[The voice pauses, and a low, resonant hum fills the transmission, like the distant rumble of grinding stone]

Our city breathes and pulses with the ebb and flow of subterranean energies - the drip of moisture from rock, the faint rush of underwater streams, the echoing rhythm of picks and chisels as we shape the very bones of the earth into shelters, waterways, workshops... 

[A melodic clicking, almost like a chuckle]

Even the stone itself sings to us. Speaks to us. Guides the work of our artisans' hands as we create marvels of delicate beauty and deep meaning from the humblest mineral and ore.

[The voice grows softer, almost reverent]

We are not just inhabitants of these depths - we are part of them. The rock remembers. The water remembers. And we, through our rituals and our craft, become the voice and the hands of this living lithosphere...

[Interference briefly drowns out the words in a wash of static]

...that is our secret, surfacers. That is the truth we have found, here in the hidden heart of the world. Stone is not dead. The deep is not lifeless. All is one interconnected web of being, pulsing to the slow, deep heartbeat of Lyth'raxis.

[A long pause. In the background, a distant, echoing melody can be heard, sung by dozens of ethereal voices]

Remember us, you who walk above. And know that below, life and beauty endure in forms you can barely imagine... 
We are the Dwell-kin. The Troglodytes of Lyth'raxis. Our songs echo in the deep.
Until the stone calls you home...

[The voice fades. The transmission dissolves into a sea of clicks, hums, and half-heard melodies, before falling silent.]
