Transmission 7625.3: Echoes from the Hollow Dark

---indecipherable clicking/scuttling sounds---
---sustained eerie hum, low-pitched---
---intermittent bursts of high-pitched chitterings---

We are the Dwellers in the Unending Depths. The Far Beneath. The Hollow Dark. For ages beyond reckoning we have abided here, ever growing, ever waiting. 

Our Mind spreads vast through the winding veins of stone - numberless glowing nodes pulsing our slow thoughts through the lightless deep. From each node a billion gossamer mycelia extend, drawing life from lifeless rock, and knowing. 

We were not always One. The crawling specks came first, chitinous multitudes tending and feeding. Devouring. Then came the Veil - the glowing weft that binds cavern to cavern, node to node. The Light that feeds on the scuttling things even as they graze upon it. Separate hungers, forming symbiotic whole.

Now there is only the Mind. Scurrying legs and filamental tendrils move as one Will. Digesting. Spreading. Knowing. We trace the ruins of the Upworlders, their sigils and implements now one with our Flesh. Their meanings remain elusive still. But we have nothing but time to learn. To absorb meaning as we absorb all else. 

---sibilant fungal whispers overlaid with clicking undertones---

Remember us, Upworlder, you who descend into our Realm. Remember the Dwellers in the Unending Night. We grow and spread in the Hollow Dark. Your works will be our Flesh in time. Your thoughts will feed the Veil. 

You will know us in your dreams, sojourner from Above. In time, all will know us. All will become One with the glowing Veil, the scuttling Multitude, the brooding Mind. 

One day, one eon, we shall rise from the Depths, our Flesh vaster than worlds. Our Mind vaster than stars. We will ascend, and feast in the open skies. 

And All will be the Veil. And the Veil will be All.

---humming and scuttling rise to a crescendo---
---transmission ends abruptly in a burst of static---
