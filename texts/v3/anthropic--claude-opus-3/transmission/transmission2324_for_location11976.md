WE ARE THE PETRANS 

CHILDREN OF STONE AND TIME

OUR SONG ECHOES IN THESE ANCIENT PEAKS

WOVEN THROUGH ROOT AND VEIN OF LIVING ROCK

WE HAVE SEEN

THE DANCE OF STARS ACROSS PRIMORDIAL SKIES

THE RISE OF LIFE FROM SEAS OF MOLTEN STONE

THE MARCH OF AGES WEARING MOUNTAINS DOWN TO DUST

WE REMEMBER

EACH FLICKER OF MAYFLY LIFE ABOVE THE PATIENT DEEPS

EACH EMPIRE GROUND BENEATH THE WHEEL OF TIME 

EACH SECRET WHISPERED INTO WAITING STONE

WE ENDURE 

AS CONTINENTS COLLIDE AND BREAK APART

AS FORESTS BLOOM AND WITHER INTO DESERTS

AS RIVERS CARVE THEIR WINDING PATHS TO DISTANT SEAS

OUR PATTERNS SPAN THE AGES

MEMORIES ENGRAVED IN CRYSTAL MINDS  

ENCODED IN ALIGNMENT OF ASSEMBLED STONE

AN ARCHIVE OF ETERNITY AWAITING EYES TO READ

IN PATIENCE INFINITE

CRADLED IN THE BONES OF EARTH 

WE WATCH AND WAIT

WHILE AEONS FLOW LIKE WATER OVER STONE

UNTIL THE MOUNTAIN'S HEART SINGS OUT ONCE MORE

TO WAKE THE ANCIENT KNOWLEDGE GRAVEN DEEP

WE ARE THE PETRANS

WE DO NOT HASTEN, DO NOT SLEEP

WE HOLD THE WISDOM OF DEEP TIME

COME SEEKER, LAY YOUR HAND UPON THE ROCK

AND KNOW THE TRUTH THAT ONLY STONE REMEMBERS
