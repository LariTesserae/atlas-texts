Transmission: Echoes from the Umbral Realm

... static crackles ... faint, ethereal voice emerges ...

We are the Umbral Folk, children of the deep,
In verdant halls where secrets sleep.
Our skin aglow with fungal light,
We dance and sing in endless night.

... soft, trilling vocalizations ... melodic and haunting ...

In roots and spores, we find our way,
Tending gardens, night and day.
With bone and vine, we craft and weave,
In ancient arts, we still believe.

... the sound of gentle rustling ... whispers in an unknown tongue ...

Our stories, songs, and wisdom old,
From generation, we uphold.
In relics of a time long past,
We find our heritage, steadfast.

... a chorus of voices, rising and falling ... a chant-like rhythm ...

Though hidden deep from world above,
In harmony, we live and love.
Our light, a beacon in the dark,
A testament to life's spark.

... the chant fades ... replaced by a single, clear voice ...

To those who seek, with curious heart,
A world of wonders, we impart.
In depths of earth, where secrets dwell,
The Umbral Folk, our story tell.

... the voice grows distant ... static interrupts ... transmission ends ...
