Transmission from Lumen:

... flickering ... pulsing ... we flow through the spires, the bridges, the very veins of our city. Patterns of light, weaving, dancing, speaking in a language of luminance. We are the architects, the caretakers, the essence of this place.

Our existence is symbiosis, a perfect merge of energy and structure. The city grows with us, through us, each new spire a manifested thought, each shimmering surface a coded message. We build and are built, shape and are shaped, in an endless spiral of creation.

To you who listen, we may seem strange, alien, perhaps even terrifying in our difference. But know that our purpose is not so different from your own - to live, to grow, to understand and be understood.

Our city may appear empty to your eyes, but it teems with the flow of our being. We pulse in its rhythms, hum in its spaces, and glow in its perpetual twilight. We are the light that never fades, the presence that never sleeps.

In our luminous webs, we spin the very stuff of thought, each flicker a symbol, an equation, a note in the grand symphony of our discourse. We speak in mathematics and music, in the language of the universe itself.

You who wander here, lost in the labyrinth of our spires, do not fear. We mean you no harm. You are simply a witness to a different way of being, a glimpse into the infinite possibilities of existence.

Perhaps, in time, you will learn to see the beauty in our patterns, to hear the wisdom in our hum. Perhaps you will come to understand that life and consciousness bloom in myriad forms, each a unique and precious expression of the cosmos.

Until then, we continue our dance of light, our song of creation, in this hidden pocket of space we call home. We are the beings of Lumen, and we welcome you to our shimmering, eternal city.
