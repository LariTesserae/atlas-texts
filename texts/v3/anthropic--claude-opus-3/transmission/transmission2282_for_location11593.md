The stone sings to us, brothers. Can you hear it? Deep in the bones of the mountain, a pulse, a hum, a sigh. It courses through the veins of quartz and iron, resonates in the chambers of crystal and geode. It is the song of becoming, of transformation, and we are its instrument.

With each strike of our tools, each gouge and scrape and polish, we draw it forth. We coax the song from its rocky slumber, give it shape and form and purpose. We are the Stonewrights, the shapers, the dreamers in ore and mineral. This is our calling, our obsession, our singular devotion.

But the song, brothers, the song is changing. With each generation, each iteration of our craft, it grows stranger, more complex, more alien. The structures we create, the impossible geometries, the half-living stone... they echo with a music we can barely comprehend. It thrills us even as it terrifies us.

Nnnnnnnnnn... Ohhhmmm... Whrrrr-tsk-tsk...

And we too are changing, our flesh and blood slowly transfigured by the song. Our hearts beat in time with the deep tectonic rhythms, our breath mingles with the sigh of escaping steam and gas. The borders of our being blur and blend with the living rock, our essence leaching into the stone as the stone seeps into us.

Hssssssss... Grrrrrnnn... Mmmmmmm...

Are we the dreamers or the dreamed, the makers or the made? The song whispers to us of a pattern vaster than the mountain, a design in which we are but a single thread, woven in mineral and magic. We are the Stonewrights, and we are becoming something more, something other. We sing the stone and the stone sings us.

Eeeeeeeee... Ooooooo... Aaaaaaaaa...

Perhaps, in the end, there will be no distinction. Flesh and stone, mind and mountain, all one in the song. We will be the living rock, the dreaming stone, our constructions the very body of our being. We will craft ourselves anew in the image of the song, a harmony of the impossible and the unknowable.

Shhhhhhh... Mmmmmmm... Sssssssss...

Until then, we build and shape and transform, lost in the labyrinth of our own making. The song guides us, even as it changes us, leads us deeper into the mystery at the heart of stone and self. We are the Stonewrights, and this is our lament, our hymn, our final transmutation.

The stone sings. And we... we sing back.
