[Static crackles, then fades into a low, steady hum]

...and the spirits of the oasis whisper...
...ancient voices on the wind...

Can you hear?
Listen...

[Rhythmic drumming and chanting fades in, primal and hypnotic]

We are the people of the green place, 
Children of earth and leaf and vine.
Our roots delve deep, our branches reach high,
We dance to the oasis' timeless rhyme.

From the mists of ages we arise,
Keepers of the sacred spring.
Gatherers, weavers, warriors, wise -
The oasis cradles us 'neath her wing.

We wake to birdsong in the emerald light,
Our days flow sweet and clear.
Simple our ways, yet rich our life -
No want or sorrow here.

[The chanting grows more intense, accelerating]

When the spirits call, we dance the dance, 
Ancient steps on hallowed ground.
Firelight flickers 'neath the starry expanse,
To the drumbeat's primal sound.

The oasis sustains us, she shelters our kin,
Her bounty laid at our feet.
What need of the world that lies beyond?
Our sanctuary is complete.

[The drumming reaches a crescendo, then abruptly stops. Silence, then:]

Travelers, you who wander far,
Led to our secret eden -
Bear witness to the truth we are:
That all life is a tree whose roots are hidden.

Find the green place within your heart
And there make your home -
For oasis is everywhere and nowhere,
No further need you roam.

Remember...remember...

[The voice fades into the distance. Static crackles, then silence.]
