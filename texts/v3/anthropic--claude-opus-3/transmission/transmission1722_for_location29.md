 An eerie glow from below and beyond 
Bioluminescent tendrils grip
 Strange fruit from an aberrant strip

 Insectile constructs blur and whiz
Weaving webs of occult calculus 
 Foglets coalesce, veer, disperse
Conjuring forms that flicker, then fizzle

 Immense, unknown energies intone
Powering this pocket surreal
 Hyper-accelerated evolution's zeal
Where metamorphic potential is sown

 Laced with circuitry, carrying chemical unknowns  
Spraying cocktails to spur the change
 Sculpting a refuge beautiful and strange
An experiment abandoned to its own

Pad and prowl of patchwork Fauxlves
 Glowing eyes and polymer-bound bones  
Guarding the edges of this altered glen
 Neither fully beast nor truly man
Pacing peripheries as sentinels alone

 Wings shimmering with scales photoelectrified

 Harvesting what faint glimmers smite
