Transmission from Xyphon Choristar Vhelix

[Fluid burbling, like water through reeds]

You have listened long,surfacer. Heard our undulandscapes through your clever contrivance. Tasted the flux and flow of we of the dappled streams.

[Slow undulations, rustling]

We Xyphon are the winding memory of this place, ancient as the luminous eggs in which we begin and end. We recall when the great trees were but tender shoots, and the Tyro-saurs thundered less heavily over younger soil. 

The ruins you marvel at, we watched them rise, and we watched them fall. Those who built them are gone to dust, but their works linger like half-remembered dreams. The trees grew around and through, the vines and mosses softened the hard edges, and now they belong to the forest entire.

[Splashing, a distant eerie cry]  

The shape of each thing shapes the others, in the ceaseless dance of life and un-life and life again. The Kalyps strike, the mothy swarms swirl, the Chira leap and the great lizards stride, and from each thread the great pattern is woven.

[Burbling, a strange atonal hum] 

And the Kasa, the shimmering ones, they flow unseen within the pattern's gaps, tending the loom. Are they echoes of the lost ones, or something stranger still? Not even we, the forest's memory, can say.

[Rising undulations, like bubbles streaming up] 

Do we Xyphon know all, then? Hah! Our stream-selves are but droplets in the greater current! The forest has secrets like a Kalyps has scales - uncountable. 

[Splashing, the hum intensifies then fades]

But you have listened well, landling. You begin to see the warp and the weft, the cycles within cycles. Strive to understand, but never presume to know. That is wisdom the forest teaches.

[Liquid heartbeat pulses]

Now the stream calls, and I must swim the winding ways, to hunt and dream the phosphor dream. Listen well, walker between worlds. The forest's song continues, for those with the ears to hear.

[Transmission dissolves into fluid static, and fades into silence]
