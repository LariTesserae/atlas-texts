*Static crackles and hisses, interspersed with an undercurrent of bubbling, squelching mud.*

*A raspy, gurgling voice breaks through, tremulous and halting:*

The city... the city is gone. Sunk into the muck. The mud, always churning. Thick. Cloying. Preserving our decay.

*Labored breathing, a wet rattling that devolves into a fit of wheezing and retching.*

We wander... shuffle through sludge. Ruined streets. Ruined bodies. Skin stretched. Bones brittle. Souls shackled to the city's corpse. 

*A long, eerie moan rises and falls, echoing as if off the hollowed bones of skyscrapers. The mud gurgles and belches in the background.*

The presence... the presence below. Pulsing. Pulling. Birthing horrors in the mire. Are we its children? Or its sustenance? The agony, the despair - does it feed on us? Trapped. Ensnared. Eternal unlife.

*The voice pitches higher, tinged with hysteria and a yawning hopelessness.*

No escape, no escape! The city is a tomb, a purgatory. And we... we are its ghosts. Shambling, shambling, until the mud finally takes us. Consumes us. Frees us? 

*A cacophony of wails and groans layers over the transmission, rising to a fever pitch. Beneath it all, a deep, rhythmic pulsing grows louder, like the heartbeat of some immense, eldritch horror.*

The presence... the presence comes. Twisting, twisting the world. Devouring, assimilating. We are its harbingers, its heralds. And its victims. The last gasp of a forgotten reality...

*The wails reach a crescendo and then abruptly cut out. For a few seconds, there is only the sound of the squelching mud, the bubbling belches, and the ever-present deep pulsing. Then, the transmission dissolves into static once more.*
