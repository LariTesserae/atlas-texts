The Whispers of the Marsh

Shhhhhhhhh... can you hear us? The voices of the marsh, the whispers in the reeds. We have been here since before memory, since the time when water first seeped into the land's bones. 

We are the gurgle of the bog, the drip of moisture from the eaves. The squelch of footsteps on sodden ground. The soft rustling of creatures burrowing beneath the mud.

We are the keepers of secrets, of histories long forgotten by the world above. In our depths, ancient cities sleep, their proud towers now the perches for marsh birds. The weight of eons presses down upon us, and we remember.

The marsh-folk, they hear us. They have learned our language, the slow murmur of water against root and stone. We speak to them in the patterns of the rainfall, in the curl of mist over still pools. They have made their peace with us, and we with them.

Not so the outsiders. They come with their hard boots and their loud voices, trampling the delicate balance we have woven over centuries. They do not understand the gift of silence, the wisdom of yielding to the marsh's will. And so we take them, fold them into our wet embrace. In time, they will join the whispers.

The marsh-folk know the signs. The sudden stillness before we rise. The brief gleam of lost gold beneath the water's skin. The iridescent flicker of a dragonfly's wing, spelling out a warning. They retreat to their walkways, pull their shutters tight, and wait for our hunger to pass.

For we are always hungry, we children of the bog. We crave the solidity of flesh, the warmth of blood to thaw our chill. The marsh-folk feed us in their own way, with eels and crayfish, with the occasional goat or chicken. Small sacrifices to keep the balance.

But you, you with your dry skin and your quick steps, you would be a rare feast. The marsh has tasted your kind before, in the long-ago days when cities grew too proud. Their bones litter the depths, picked clean and polished smooth by the gentle caress of water.

Come closer, let us whisper in your ear. A secret, a promise. Riches beyond your wildest dreams, the treasures of drowned kings. All you must do is step from the path, let the soft mud embrace your feet and draw you down. 

Shhhhhhhhh... trust us. Trust the whispers. We will take such good care of you, preserve you in our cool depths. You will become one with the marsh, one with us.
