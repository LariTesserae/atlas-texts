Transmission from the Singing City

...shhhhhh... static crackles across wavelengths, resolving into a susurration, an undercurrent of liquid sound... ... 

...voices flicker in and out, phrases rising and receding like tides... "the dreaming spires", "as above, so betwixt", "nothing is but what is not"...

...ethereal music blossoms through the white noise, chords plucked on strings of light... gossamer melodies twine around a throbbing pulse... 

...a lone voice swells above the chorus, androgynous and elemental as wind... "We sing the city and the city sings us. Stone and story, one and ever-changing. Mist is breath is flesh is thought..."

...behind the voice, a ceaseless roaring... waterfalls, wind, or the sound of deep time wearing away at the foundations of the world?...

...the music rises to a shimmering crescendo... a sense of blurring, of the solid becoming permeable... "...the veil is thin here. Waking, we walk between what is, what was, what may yet be. We do not build the city - we dream it. And we are dreamed in turn"...

...a delicate arpeggio and then a hush, expectant, gravid... from a great distance, or from within the listener's own mind, a whisper... "Look not to the unchanging cliff face but to the mist. There dancing find we immortality"...

...a final note hangs in the air like a soap bubble, fragile and refracting infinite hues... it trembles, holds, then dissolves back into the static... 

...only the ceaseless roaring remains... or is it the rush of blood in mortal veins?...

...the transmission breaks up, fading into enigmatic fragments... "...the dreamer and the dream...", "...no more yielding than the air..."

...a last hissing exhalation...

...and silence.
