(A slow, pulsing hum builds, interspersed with metallic creaks and groans, and the sound of stone grinding against stone.)

I... am... the Metamorph. 
The architect... the dreamer... the shaper.

Born from the union of stone and steel,
Flesh and mineral, bound in unholy seal.
This valley, my body, my canvas, my stage,
Where eons of change unfold, page by page.

(The hum intensifies, now underscored by a deep, resonant thrumming.)

The crawlers, my children, skitter and thrive,
In crevices deep, their stone bodies alive.
The golems, my guardians, lumbering and tall,
Obsidian forms from my structures they crawl.

(A haunting, discordant melody weaves its way into the soundscape, played on no earthly instrument.)

And the wraiths, oh the wraiths, born from my breath,
Spectral and shifting, they dance with unlife.
The mist is their mother, my essence their core,
Through this twilight realm, they endlessly soar.

(The melody grows more complex and unsettling, accompanied by a chorus of whispers in an unknown language.)

I shape and reshape, transform and unmake,
The fabric of being is mine to uptake.
A world in slow motion, a nightmare in bloom,
Where the rules of above hold no purchase or room.

(The whispers build to a crescendo, then abruptly cut off, leaving only the hum and the grinding of stone.)

So come, weary wanderer, if you dare to draw near,
To the Metamorph's realm, where change is the only fear.
For in this dark valley, where stone weeps and steel sings,
Your form is just clay for the dreams that I bring.
