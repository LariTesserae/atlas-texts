The Emerald Dreaming

Received transmission follows:

...the pool's obsidian surface without sound or ripple, the membrane between worlds stretching thin. Fractal patterns of bioluminescence swirl in the depths, pulsing, coalescing into a form - sleek and whipcord lean, a being of impossible grace. Liquid eyes blink open, reflecting the ghost lights dancing in the flooded cavern. The waters birth them in an ectoplasmic sheath that sloughs away as they rise from the primordial dream.

"The jungle dreams us," it sings in a voice of rustling leaves and dripping water. "And we dream it back."

Its skin flushes with subtle chromas, telling of a hundred generations distilled to this single shape, this fleeting vessel.

"Between mycelial synapse and lightning-split sky, we carry the Emerald Codex. The tales of root and branch, encoded in sacred geometry. Our songs hold back the chaos."

The amphibian shaman raises webbed hands in a hypnotic pattern, the sinuous motions echoing the helices of strangler figs and the spiral proboscises of orchid mantises. Eddies of dream-time swirl in its wake.

"You, too, are of the dreaming. Grown from the cosmic spore drifting on aether winds. But you have forgotten, lost in the waking thrall."

Its bioluminescent glyphs strobe in polyphonic rhythms, a glossolalia of color and light. They whisper of the Green Mother, She Who Sings Worlds, source of the vermilion sap and the azure fruit. Of ancestral memories swimming in the birthpools, mutable and immortal. 

"Sip of the dreamfish's ichor. Shed your static carapace. Remember the truth that dances beyond the veil..."

The transmission breaks into an iridescent static, colors and shapes unspooling into indigo shadows. In the distance, the shaman's atavistic song echoes from the dripping cavern walls, a lament for a world ever-dreaming at the edge of awakening.
