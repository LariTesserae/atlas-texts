The signal resolves into a susurration, like wind through leaves, punctuated by occasional chirrups and trills. A voice emerges, soft and lilting, with an undercurrent of rustling and creaking, as if the very forest is speaking:

"We are the Verdani, the People of the Green, and this is the song of our hidden world. 

High in the misty boughs, nestled in an embrace of branch and vine, we dance amidst dappled light and flitting wings. The forest is our home, our essence, our all.

We are bark-skinned and leaf-haired, green-blooded and sap-hearted. The Great Trees are our towers, the mosses our carpets, the flowers our jewels. We sip dew from acorn cups and feast on sunbeams flavored with pollen-spice.

Our wings are woven from gossamer and dreams, allowing us to dart and spiral, to alight on petals and pirouette on pine needles. The air hums with our passage, a symphony of buzzing and whirring, in harmony with birdcall and frog-song.

We tend the Green Pulse, the life-force that flows through root and stem, that surges in chlorophyll tides with each dawn. We sing to saplings and soothe sickly shrubs. The forest thrives under our touch, our presence, our love.

Seasons turn in their slow dance, and we turn with them, shedding vibrant leaves in autumn, slumbering through winter's hush, unfurling anew in spring's green surge, rejoicing in summer's verdant abundance. The cycles of leaf and loam are our very breath. 

To be Verdani is to be one with the wild world, to hear its green heart beat, to dance to the rhythms of sap and sun, moon and mist. We are guardians and givers, tenders and talkers, green souls adrift on an emerald sea.

May the branches always embrace you, may your roots drink deep. Tread gently in our domain, walk softly amongst our leafy kin. The forest remembers, the Green Pulse knows. Treat our home with care and kindness, and the Verdani will forever be your friends.

This is but a whisper of our world, a rustling refrain from our viridian realm. The Green Pulse beckons, the dance never ends. In branch and bole, in bud and bloom, the Verdani sing on."

The voice fades back into the susurrus of leaves, and the transmission dissolves once more into the arboreal ambience of creaks, chirrups, and rustling branches, a ceaseless forest lullaby echoing from the emerald aerie high above.
