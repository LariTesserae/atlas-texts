Whispers in the green, voices in the leaves. We are the warp and weft, the patient weavers of this living tapestry. Our limbs dance through the foliage, tending, trimming, nurturing. We are the pulse in the vine, the caretakers of this ancient sanctuary.

The hum of the green, the ancient resonance - it guides us, teaches us. We drink the sap, the lifeblood of our world. It sustains us as we sustain the labyrinth. A symbiosis older than memory, a pact between leaf and limb.

We are small, but we are many. Our dance is slow, but it is ceaseless. In the heart of the desert, in this oasis of green, we persist. We weave, we tend, we remember.
