They skitter, they buzz, they ooze in the darkness... Obsidian plains stretch to black horizons under starless skies. No light but the pulsing nodes, no sound but chitin on glass and subsonic groans from Below.

The centipedes are ravenous, always hunting, devouring with sickening crunches and slurps. Carapaces slick with mucous as they undulate across the jagged terrain. Poison drips from cruel mandibles. The strong consume the weak. 

Beetles swarm in noxious clouds, expelling fetid gases, spiraling in nauseating arcs. They dance inscrutable patterns, a maddening aerial ballet. Vile mouthparts constantly tasting the air. Laying eggs in rotting carcasses.

The slugs cling and shudder, straining against gravity's pull. Organs roil within translucent flesh, fluids pulsing in diseased hues. Feeding... forever feeding on bitter essences. Budding obscene offspring that slither into crevices.

All the crawling, writhing, oozing things in the shadows... a seething ecology of parasites and predators locked in eternal struggle. Only the ruthless persist. Cruelty is the way of this inverted eden. Malice shrieks from every quivering membrane.

The obsidian crackles with each skittering step, a world of blades that drink the blood of the fallen. Above, the dead sky watches in silent disdain. There is only the fever dance of abominations spawn from the darkest cracks of alien minds.

They breed, they feed, they kill, they grow. Generations of insectile and annelid horrors, pulsing and oozing and skittering and swarming. The glistening forest of crystal columns their cathedral. The obsidian plains their parish. The dead sky their uncaring god.

This is where biology reaches with misshapen arms to embrace madness. Where Terran nightmares congeal into carapace and gelatin and squirming larvum. Where no dawn ever rises to banish a darkness that blackens the soul.
