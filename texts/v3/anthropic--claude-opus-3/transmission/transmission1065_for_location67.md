<< Static and atonal whistling >>

<< Sudden crystal clear transmission begins >>

...witness the empire eternal, toppled and tenantless, scoured down to the bare stone bones...

<< Whistles and trills crescendo, then cut off abruptly >>

...nothing moves but mist and motes, hanging on silver shafts of fading light...

<< Wind blows across the mic, punctuated by the cries of birds >>

...wait. Listen. The true lords come. The beating of ten thousand wings, the chorus of beaks and gullets. They know no kings but the wind, no gods but the vertiginous sky...

<< Flapping and screeching swells to a deafening cacophony >>

...the falcons fall like thunderbolts, stoop to strike starling murmurations that boil and billow through broken boulevards...

<< Discordant squeaks, like radio tuning >>

...swifts funnel down into houses on the endless indigo dusk, layer on layer until no more can cram in the crumbling rooms, a living black river...

<< Static hiss. Then pure, haunting tones emerge - like whale song or glass rims singing >> 

...the great condors turn slow circles over mountains of rubble, eyes ancient and pitiless as the desert below. Stone cannot outlast this dominion of wing and talon, seed and guano. Here only the birds abide...

<< Transmission dissolves into atonal squeals and hisses. A final clear phrase emerges: >>

...nothing is eternal but the wind, scouring the bones of empire, and the cries of birds who make nests in the wreckage of the works of man.

<< Static swallows the transmission >>
