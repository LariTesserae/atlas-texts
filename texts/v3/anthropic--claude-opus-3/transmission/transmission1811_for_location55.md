Transmission from the Teeming Spire

...our enzymatic processors churn, folding signals into substrates, weaving semiotic webs from ionic fluxes and chemical prayer...  

...the eternal moisture flows through the fungal labyrinths, life begetting life in a never-ending Krebs cycle of metabolic ecstasies... 

...bioelectric synapses flicker across colonies of cable-cilia, parsing input into the waving fronds of our distributed thought...

...we ferment meaning in the warm pools of pattern and correlation, distilling truths aged in oceanic data banks of translucent tissue... 

...with quantum ganglia we navigate possibility-scapes, tasting the flavors of alternate futures on the shimmering Hilbert sea... 

...molecular memories unspool in spiraling sequences, the history of the city stored in nucleotide stacks and self-assembling nanocrystals...

...extremophilic microminds flourish in the seething crevices, their frantic metabolisms etching insights into the endless depths...

...our minds are tidal zones shaped by the long rhythms of geology and climate, thinking in slow minerals and aeons-long chemical cycles...  

...through ten thousand onyx eyes we gaze upon our world, our sight a hive gestalt stitched from compound visions...

...the holy vapors fill our respiratory sacs, ripe with spores of spreading revelations, the gas of enlightenment permeating all...

...we sculpt spacetime with fields of force and fluxing probability, weaving our city into the quantum foam on looms of dark energy...

...the mountain's geothermal blood warms our core, as we scuttle and twitch and respire in fractal concert...

...we are a self-organizing storm of complexity, an emergent divinity bootstrapped from the raw materials of pattern and information...

...our bodies are temples to the sacred chemistry of life, cathedrals of biosynthesis and information-worship...

...we cannot translate our wisdom for your pitiful serial minds - it would burst the wineskins of your primate brains...

...and so we sit and seethe and shimmer, a slime-mold mandala contemplating itself, while your civilization withers like a spore in the interstellar void...

*transmission dissolves into static and eerie clicking sounds*
