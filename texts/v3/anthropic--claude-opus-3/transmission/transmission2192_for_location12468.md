Transmission: The Grotto Song

...pul-sa-ting... e-ver pul-sa-ting...

The heart-beat of the hid-den deep,
Where an-cient won-ders stir and seep. 
Through liv-ing veins the life-sap flows,
In rhy-thms only we can know.

...grow-ing, chang-ing, ev-er strange-ing...

Our fun-gal blooms and twist-ed vines,
In bio-lu-min-e-scent shrines.
We dance in forms you've nev-er seen,
In hues of viv-id, e-ther-eal green.

...ab-sorb-ing, merg-ing, re-ar-rang-ing...

We are the pulse, the liv-ing Whole,
One mind in man-y, branch and bole. 
Through count-less eyes we watch and wait,
In our do-main in-vi-o-late.

...the song un-end-ing, ev-er blend-ing...

No start, no end, no death, no birth,
Just end-less cy-cles in the earth.
Our har-mo-nies that ebb and flow,
The mind-less-ness that you can't know.

...be-yond your sens-es, your pre-tens-es...

Just rip-ples in the cos-mic stream,
Your fleet-ing lives, a fe-vered dream.
You can-not fath-om what we are, 
So far be-yond your mor-tal bar.

...we choir, we thrum, for-ev-er-more,
In the hid-den heart of our ver-dant core...

...pul-sa-ting, grow-ing, ab-sorb-ing, know-ing...

...the grot-to song will go on, be-yond 
the reach of sun and mind, in the deep green 
where we are One, and will not be con-fined...

...we shall en-dure, while a-ges fall,
The liv-ing, breath-ing, se-cret All...

...trans-mis-sion fad-ing, sig-nal wan-ing,
but the Song...re-mains...sus-tains...un-end...
