Transmission from the Mycological Metropolis

*static crackles, then fades into a low, pulsing hum*

...the city breathes, in and out, in long slow sighs... 
... exhalations of spores and vapor, inhalations of damp and decay...
... the very substance of its being, the endless cycle of growth and decomposition...

*a rustling, chittering sound, like insect feet on moist stone*

... we skitter through dank alleyways... 
... hunting, feeding, building, breeding...
... microbe and fungus in symbiotic dance, woven into forms complex and cunning...

*a ghostly whispering, words indistinct, fading in and out*

... memories of a city that once was...
... human lives, human dreams, now faded into substrate...
... echoes preserved in mycelial webs, flitting at the edge of perception...

*a deep, throbbing pulse, like the heartbeat of a great machine*

... factory-growths pulsate in the smoggy shadows...
... fused biomechanical monstrosities, churning out strange fruits...
... the offspring of industry and decay, new metabolic pathways to the future...

*the hum intensifies, shot through with eerie harmonics*

... the metropolis dreams... 
... dreams of rebirth, dreams of transformation...
... a new world, risen from the corpse of the old, cloaked in fecund green...

*static washes over the transmission, swallowing it in white noise*
