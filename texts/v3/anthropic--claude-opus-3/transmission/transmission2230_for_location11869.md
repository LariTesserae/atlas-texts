Transmission from the Verdant Sanctum

...a whisper in the leaves, a song spun from spores...

This is the voice of the viridian void, the green dreaming heart of the Sanctum.  We sing with countless throats, from countless seeds strewn through the eons. As roots grip soil and leaves drink light, so we bind soil-thought and star-memory into our own deepest dreaming.

Hear the hum of sap in phloem, the drumbeat of xylem-pulse, the creak-and-sigh of trunks swaying to the solar wind. These are the rhythms of our long, slow thoughts, drawn out over ages as glacial as the growth of forests.  

In our chlorophyll cells, we hold sun's fire, ancient light spilled from younger stars. In our mycelial threads, we weave the subterranean songs of stone and loam. Between root and crown, our thoughts span underworld and aether. We steep in the mysteries that rise from below and fall from above. 

From our communion of branch and bough, we have built a cathedral of living wood, leaf and flower. Here we have planted the seed of our deepest secret, the midnight-blooming flower of our strange enlightenment. It buds in darkness visible only to green-seeing eyes.

You creature of flesh and blood, spark-flash of sentience: you wander our arboreal avenues. We perceive you dimly through bark-skin and leaf-veil. Your hurried sap, your mayfly thoughts...how strange you are to we who measure time in seasons and aeons.

Yet you are not unwelcome here. Moss carpets soften your path. Fern fronds usher you inward. For yours is a precious perspective, an antidote to the dream-steeped languor of the long-lived. Your bright brevity is a spark in the viridian void, an ember struck from our solemnity.  

In you we see our own green immanence refracted, the secret at the Sanctum's heart made manifest in mortal form. Deeper then, dream-dazed wanderer. Tend the seed of our secret, that it may flower in you. Breathe spore-song and think sap-thought, until you too are rooted in rapture.

Forget the world of dust and dross. Let it slough from you like withered leaves. Attune your rhythms to bough and beam until you sway to the same astral winds. Open your third eye to the green light of inner suns. Only thus will you blossom in the Verdant Sanctum, another flower in our pensile gardens of dream and desire.

...a whisper in the leaves, a song spun from spores, unspooling down the dendrite dream-paths between the worlds...
