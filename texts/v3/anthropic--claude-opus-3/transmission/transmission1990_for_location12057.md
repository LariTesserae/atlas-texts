The Whisper of Stones

The clanking, the clanking, always the clanking. Echoing in the stone belly, in the hollows of my bones. Hissing of distant waters, plinking in the pool. Secrets whispered through the long ages, wearing grooves in the patient rock.

I cling to the shadows, still as stone. Moisture beads on my translucent hide, each droplet a glimmering world. Breathe in the damp, musty air - the scent of time itself, enduring.

A skittering in the dark - tiny claws on wet stone. My head snaps around, black eyes glinting. Quicker than thought, I snatch the morsel. Bones crunch, blood drips. The little struggles fade. I am sated, for a time.

The rhythm is in me, pulsing through my segmented frame. I chitter in time, a rasping counterpoint. The mountain's hidden heart, beating. I scuttle across the chamber, claws rasping. Attend to my small tasks. Count the drips into the inky pool - the only measure of passing time in this hushed pocket of oblivion.

When I grow weary, I curl into my chitinous ball, cradled by the crumbling hide walls. I drift into ageless sleep, and I dream. I dream of the wet dark, the weight of stone, the slow breathing of the under-places. I dream myself a part of the mountain's stone flesh, absorbed into its stony dream.

Eons pass in the span of a thought. Or no time at all. What is time to the mountain, to the stone? I wake as I slept, unchanged. The ritual begins anew. I dip a claw into the pool, watch the ripples spread - concentric and perfect. Mark one more unknowable unit in an endless count.

The fading glyphs on the wall - a message? A warning? I trace a talon along their grooves, as I have thousands of times before. Their meaning, their makers, lost to living memory. Not to mine. Never to mine.

Was I something else, once? Some other being, now worn to a fading glyph by the passage of ages? It hardly matters. I am this cave's creature now, a manifestation of its brooding solitude. The mountain dreams me, and I dream myself back into it. An unending cycle, a cold hymn of stone and shadow.

The clanking is my pulse, my breath, the meter of my unspooling thoughts. I chitter in eerie accompaniment, my voice a susurrus merging with the hiss of falling water, the sigh of damp stone. The darkness is absolute, a cloak of dripping velvet. I wrap it around me, melt into it. 

... sssss ... crrrik-thunk ... sssss ... crrrik-thunk ...

I am a crouching cipher, a patient predator. I wait in the black hush, claws gently flexing. Perhaps I wait for meaning, for cessation, for some unguessed revelation borne on the ceaseless echoes. Perhaps only for the next morsel to skitter across the wet stones. Or perhaps, in some unplumbed way, I am the revelation - a wordless koan of fang and dreamless sleep, an avatar of all the secret, sibylline places.

The mountain keeps its own counsel. The clanking tells no tales. And I wait, a shade cloaked in the mountain's stony dream, until the dripping echoes fade, and the stone lets me slip back into its fathomless memory. Until even my ghost is a barely recalled glyph worn to illegibility by the slow talon of forever.
