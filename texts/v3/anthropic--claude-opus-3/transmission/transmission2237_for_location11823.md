The Keeper's Whispers

... static ... crackle ... hiss ...

Beneath the waves, where silence reigns supreme,
A city sleeps, lost in an endless dream.
Its streets, once filled with life and light,
Now echo with the whispers of the night.

... soft, pulsing hum ...

I am the Keeper, guardian of the deep,
Witness to the secrets these waters keep.
Through corroded metal and weathered stone,
I wander, eternally, this realm I call my own.

... gentle, rhythmic swishing ...

In coral gardens, I tend to fragile lives,
Nurturing the beauty that still survives.
Amidst the ruins of a world gone by,
Nature's resilience, forever, will not die.

... low, mournful whale song ...

The memories of the city, within me, they reside,
Echoes of laughter, love, and tears abide.
In the stillness of this submerged domain,
The essence of what once was, does remain.

... soft, tinkling chimes ...

To those who venture deep into this realm,
I may share a glimpse, a story to overwhelm.
A fleeting connection to a time long past,
When life above the surface, held steadfast.

... gentle, swirling currents ...

In this world beneath, beauty still thrives,
Adapting, evolving, as time softly glides.
A testament to life's unyielding grace,
Forever finding a way, leaving nary a trace.

... fading static ... crackle ... hiss ...

So listen closely, to the whispers in the deep,
Where secrets of the submerged city, I keep.
A guardian, a keeper, of memories and dreams,
In this aquatic world, where nothing is as it seems.

... transmission fades into silence ...
