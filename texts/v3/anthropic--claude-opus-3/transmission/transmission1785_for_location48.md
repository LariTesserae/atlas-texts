Transmission: Echoes of the Ancients

... static crackles ... wind howls through desolate peaks ... 

... faint, atonal humming ... sound of stones grinding together ...

Voice (flat, inflectionless): Connecting to observation node 77... link established.

Voice: The Kryll have emerged from their warrens. A solitary scout ascends the High Spire, sensory filaments extended. It pauses at a geometric aperture, interfacing with dormant systems.

... a deep, resonant thrum begins, underpinned by rapid clicking ...

Voice: The spire awakens, ancient pathways surging with power. A trickle, then a flood of data, streaming into Kryll implants. Climatological archives, geophysical scans, population metrics...

... the thrumming increases in volume and tempo, almost drowning out the voice ... 

Voice: The scout lingers, absorbing, analyzing. Then it withdraws, filaments retracting, the interface fading back to quiescence. Skittering down, down, ferrying its precious cargo of knowledge back to the colony. 

... thrumming dies away, replaced by layered, atonal chanting, rising and falling ...

Voice: In rippling waves they come, drawn by the scout's discovery. The Kryll swarm over the ancient structure, tiny legs tracing sigils, miniscule charges probing, seeking entry. They pour into opened vaults, tunnels, and chambers, harvesting mineral accretions, transplanting phosphorescent fungi, repurposing antediluvian machines.

... the chanting crescendos, shot through with bursts of static and a deep, bass hum ...

Voice: The ruins live again, not with their builder's intent, but with a new purpose. The mountain's cold heart beats to an ancient rhythm, pulsing with renewed energy. And the Kryll are its blood, its breath, the inheritors of its secrets.

... the transmission dissolves into static, then silence, leaving only the sound of wind keening over barren stone ...
