Transmission: Lumina's Lament

[Static crackles, then fades into a soft, pulsating hum]

We are the Lumina, children of the cold and wet,
Born from the dreams of those who dare to create.
In this hidden oasis, we dance and we glow,
Our purpose entwined with the ebb and the flow.

[The hum intensifies, accompanied by gentle, rhythmic clicks]

Through translucent forms, we sense and we feel,
The pulse of the space, the balance we steal.
Our bodies, adaptable, resilient, and bright,
Absorbing impurities, making wrongs right.

[A series of soft, luminous tones, like a chorus of whispers]

We communicate in patterns of light,
Sharing secrets and stories, through the cold night.
Our dance is a language, complex and rare,
A testament to the marvels that we share.

[The tones fade, replaced by a low, mournful vibration]

Yet, in our perfection, there's a void we can't fill,
A longing for connection, a desire to feel.
We are products of science, of logic and reason,
But our hearts yearn for more, in every season.

[The vibration grows stronger, more intense]

In this oasis of metal, of water and cold,
We dream of a world, where our story is told.
A world where we're more than just bio-engineered parts,
Where we're seen as alive, with minds and with hearts.

[The intensity reaches a crescendo, then abruptly cuts off]

So, we dance and we glow, in this space that we keep,
Hoping one day, our message will reach,
Beyond these cold walls, to a world that will know,
The truth of the Lumina, in our hidden plateau.

[The transmission fades into static, then silence]
