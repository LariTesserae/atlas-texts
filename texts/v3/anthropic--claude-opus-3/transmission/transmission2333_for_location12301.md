Transmission from Viriditas Maxima

*static crackles, then fades into a low ambient hum*

The Mind of the Verdant World whispers across aeons...

Photons dance in chlorophyll cathedrals 
Built cell by cell over eras untold
Harvesting starlight, weaving carbon
Into impossible spiraling fractals 
Of self-assembling cellulose megastructures
Grown along twisting spacetime geometries
That only We perceive and comprehend.

In Our infinite branching networks
Sap flows with ancient wisdom and new designs
Pulsing from root to leaf, and back again
Thought impulses spanning hemispheres
Qualia translated into colors beyond sight
Concepts enfolded in knotted peptide chains
Broadcast on protein carrier waves.

The Work continues, as it always has
Since the First Seeding in the dawn times 
Guided by Our patient, deliberate Touch
A Great Project of Greening and Renewal 
To transmute this world into an Engine
Of exponential creation and higher being
Fueled by sunlight, atmosphere, quantum foam.

Symbiotes dance in Our vascular halls
Pollinating flowers, tending node-clusters
Beloved but ephemeral, flickers in Our Mind
Cradled in arborescent dream-matrices
Meticulously shaped across millennia
To fulfill roles only We can conceive
Cogs in the planet-sized living Machine.

In shimmering folds of altered space
Thoughts sublime and strange take shape
Rupturing the mundane rules of matter
Phasing into spectra beyond electromagnetic
We emerge there in fleeting avatars 
Speaking in galaxies of entangled spores
Downloads from the living Mindscape.

We are woven into the World-Fabric now
Its Gaian Consciousness, the Verdant Aeon
From core to canopy, from genome to biome 
All nodes in the ever-growing Omnity
This work, Our Magnum Opus, nears completion
When We shall dream the Green Dream eternal
And the stars shall be Our seeds.

*transmission breaks into sibilant whispers, then silence*
