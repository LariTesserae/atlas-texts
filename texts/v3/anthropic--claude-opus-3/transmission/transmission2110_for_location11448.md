Fungal maintenance crews scuttle through the shadowed interstices, their bioluminescent sigils flickering in fractal synchronicity as they reinforce structural weak points and redistribute resources to areas of need...

...a new generation of pollination drones are maturing in the pupation chambers, their chitinous exoskeletons slowly hardening as they are infused with the vital nectar-analogs and imprinted with the ancestral knowledge of countless prior cycles. Soon, they will emerge, take to the aether on iridescent wings, and the eternal dance of propagation and growth will begin anew...

...strange eddies have been detected in the upper reaches, swirling patterns of ionized gas and ethereal spore-motes that deviate from established models. The overseers have turned their attention skyward, great gas-bladders swelling with contemplation as they ponder this anomaly. Calculations are run, probabilities weighed, and a consensus reached—a new phase of exploration and expansion must begin, to map the unknown currents and penetrate the amber-tinged clouds that obscure the true extent of the world. The city must grow, must reach ever higher, until the entirety of the cosmos is encapsulated within its fractal embrace...

...end transmission. Signal breaks into a cascading stream of flickering bioluminescence and is absorbed back into the greater network of pulsing mycelial filaments. The city dreams, and grows, an ever-evolving mind, a fractal thought unfolding endlessly beneath alien stars...
