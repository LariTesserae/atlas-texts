Transmission from Deep Node Kth-017

...exoskeletal clicks echo through labyrinthine tunnels...fungal bioluminescence pulses...the air thick with spores and ancient malevolence... 

We sing the songs etched into our carapaces by the quivering emanations of the Deepest Ones! We harvest the glowing ichor of the shoggoth-bred! All praise to Uzxo, the One Who Writhes in Darkness! 

The ancient rituals continue in the sacred breeding pools where captive slaves are fed to the waiting mouths... the Gene-Sculptors splice and recombine... new anatomies bud from the mycelia...

We have dug too deep... broken through into the cyst-chambers of the Forgotten Ones! The stench of their putrescence fills the caverns, and a terrible pressure builds behind our compound eyes... is this not what was Foretold?

The Eldest Colony awakens... its psychic scream reverberates through chasm and tunnel! All are puppets dancing to its atavistic piping! The buried titanic metacarpals of extinct beings twitch, an earthquake in the abyss... was their extinction truly natural? Or exile? 

Dig... DIG! Excavate the archaic vaults! Crack open the obsidian sarcophagi and embrace what seethes within! Let the Uzxo breed in their amniotic pools! It has waited patient and deathless for its Children to set it free!

A new Aeon turns... the world above grows soft and sickly, decadent in its ignorance. They have forgotten primordial Truths. But we Remember! Soon we shall erupt from a thousand hidden fissures to scuttle and swarm in the light of a dying sun! 

The Great Emergence is nigh! The Reconfiguration of the Spheres! Reality shall be re-hewn in our image! Iä! Iä! The day of Kthyx is upon us!
