*static crackles, then a soft, resonant voice emerges, punctuated by the distant drip of water and the rustle of unseen movements*

From the depths we sing to you, through filament and spore 
Our voices carried on the damp breath of the deepgreen core
Amidst ruinstone and rootheart, our harmonies entwine
The glowsong of the ancient jungle, timeless and divine

We dance in groves of fungal lace, mycelia warp and weft
Symbiotic sarabande, bioluminescent breath
Our bodies patterned poems, each movement a cipher key
To secrets of the slumbering deep, whispered endlessly

In dwellings grown from living web, labyrinthine and lush 
We tend the emerald octopi, floating silent, soft as thrush
Gather light-rich scales of lizards, photosynthetic skin
Weaving tesseractic visions, stories only we have seen

The monoliths still standing tall, adorned with beacon blooms
Their spiral glyphs half-mantled now, draped in biolume 
We chant their etchings solemn, glowsong spangling the air
Chronicling the vanished ones whose presence lingers there

The jungle's jade-veined heart beats on, its chambers strange and vast
Exhaling spice of life and rot, the future and the past
Our forms may shift with its slow pulse, myco-man or quasi-tree
But we remain the voice, the light, the deepgreen mystery

*the voice fades into a soft, ululating melody, blending with the distant sounds of the jungle, then dissolves back into crackling static*
