The Whisper from the Depths

... zzzzzzt ... crackle ... pop ... 

... whispers ... ancient ... eternal ...

... we are the stone ... we are the mountain ...

... generations upon generations ... our lives entwined ... 

... the rhythm of the forges ... the flow of the waters ...

... we are the guilds ... the masters ... the keepers of knowledge ...

... the secrets of the depths ... passed from master to apprentice ... 

... the heat of the forges ... the chill of the mist ... 

... we endure ... we adapt ... we thrive ...

... lizard swift on the walls ... pale bats in the dusk ... 

... the city breathes ... the mountain dreams ...

... outsiders marvel ... at our works ... our ways ...

... they cannot understand ... the bond ... 

... we are the chosen ... in balance with the stone ...

... our songs echo ... in the plazas and terraces ... 

... tales of ancestors ... secrets of the peak ...

... we are one ... people and mountain ... 

... ancient ... unyielding ... eternal ...

... the Whisper from the Depths ... 

... crackle ... zzzzzt ... fade ...
