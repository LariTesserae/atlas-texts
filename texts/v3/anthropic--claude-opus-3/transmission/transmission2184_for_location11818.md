The Stoneshapers' Song

Wind through the high passes
Stone-song, stream-song
Echoing bones of the mountain
Shaping the forms of our dreaming

Born from the womb of the bedrock 
Hewn by the hands of our fathers
Shaped by the will of the waters
We are the mountain's grey children

Blacksmiths forging in fire-glow
Masons smoothing the stone-face
Ropemakers binding the paths high
Tenders of fountain and water-flow

We keep the deep secrets
The First-Songs, the First-Dances
The rites of remembrance
In stillness, in star-silence

Seasons turn, moon-silver
Winters long, summers fleet
We endure, stone-strong
The mountain's slow heart-beat

Strangers come, wary eyes
Questions unasked, half-formed 
We turn aside, stone-faced
Guarding the unspoken

But listen, the wind whispers
Stone breathes and water sings
In greeting, in passing
The deep truths of living things

Hewn from stone, star-blessed
Waterborn and sky-wreathed
We hold the mountain's silence
In our eyes, granite-grained

Patient as the high peaks
Constant as the cascades
In our hands, stone-shaping
We are the mountain's true face
