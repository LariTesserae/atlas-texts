Transmission from the Depths

*static crackles and hisses, then fades into a low, pulsing hum*

...life... eternal... in the deep...

*a gurgling, bubbling sound, like thick liquid being sucked through a straw*

...they come... the seekers... the curious... 
probing, prodding, disturbing the balance...

*a sharp, scraping noise, like metal on stone*

...old structures, old machines, incorporated... 
become one with the biomass, with the ooze...
rust and rot blending, melding...

*a wet, squelching sound, rhythmic and hypnotic*

...the plants, the fungi, the eyeless crawling things...
all part of the design, the pattern, the pulse...
tended and shaped, guided by a timeless will...

*the hum intensifies, taking on an almost melodic quality*

...the heat, the damp, the closeness of it all...
cradling, embracing, blanketing... 
a womb of stone and earth and metal...

*strange chitterings and clickings, rising and falling in waves*

...the ones who linger, who push deeper...
they begin to see, to hear, to understand...
glimpses of a consciousness vast and alien and old...

*a deep, shuddering sigh, like a gust of subterranean wind*

...guardian, caretaker, cultivator...
maintaining the cycles, the flow, the stagnancy... 
a purpose beyond the bright surface world...

*the transmission starts to break up, fading into static*

...beauty in decay, in darkness, in depths...
growth and entropy in balance, in harmony...
forever and always, here in the hidden places...

*with a last burst of distortion, the signal cuts out completely*
