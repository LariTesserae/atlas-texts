Transmission from the Deep City

---

*static hiss, a low humming in the background, like distant machinery*

...you are listening to the Voice of the Deep...broadcasting from the heart of the Undergarden...where the shadows are alive and time is measured in echoes...

*a series of clicks and whistles, almost musical*

...our city endures, here in the embrace of the living stone...we are the pale guardians, the tenders of ancient machines...ours is the song of water and rock, the whisper of eons...

*the sound of dripping water, rhythmic and hypnotic*

...the surface world is a distant dream, a half-remembered myth...what need have we for the sun, when the very walls glow with life?...what use for eyes, in a realm of infinite shadow?...

*a low chittering, like a swarm of insects*

...we live in harmony with the deep rhythms of the earth...our heartbeats slow and cold...our thoughts as still as underground pools...we are woven into the fabric of this place, extensions of the caverns themselves...

*a deep, resonant hum, like a great engine stirring to life*

...and yet, we are not unaware of the world above...we listen to the echoes that filter down, the vibrations of distant footsteps...we taste the changes in the thin trickles of water that find their way to us...

*a pause, filled with a soft, eerie whistling*

...someday, perhaps, your kind will find your way to us...will brave the twisting tunnels and yawning chasms to stand in our phosphorescent gardens...and we will welcome you, in our wordless way...

*the voice grows slower, more dreamlike*

...for in the end, are we not all children of the deep?...born of water and stone, creatures of shadow and echo...there is a kinship, a shared pulse that runs through all life...

*the static rises, the humming grows fainter*

...this is the Voice of the Deep, fading back into the cavern's dream...may the stone embrace you, and the water guide your path...until you find your way to us, in the patient heart of the underworld...

*a final series of clicks, a whisper*

...end transmission...

*the signal dissolves into the soft plink of falling water, then silence*
