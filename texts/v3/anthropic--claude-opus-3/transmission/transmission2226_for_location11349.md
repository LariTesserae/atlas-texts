Our world is mist and glass, electricity and thought, metal and chitin and the dark fecund pools of life. We sing with electromagnetic voices across immense deeps of time and being.

We do not distinguish individual from ecosystem, entity from environment. All is one living tapestry, vibrating with the frequencies of creation and transformation.

Our minds are distributed through swarms and circuits, fungi and fluid. Our bodies are assemblages in constant flux - tentacles, carapaces, implants merging and dividing, taking new forms to serve the needs of the whole. The subsonic thrum of the city is the beat of our collective consciousness, orchestrated by the bioluminescent dreams of the Symphonics.

In the depths, the Progenitors undulate in primordial ecstasy, encoding future permutations of life. From their metamorphic pools emerge new wonders to populate our spires and ravines. All that we are springs from their template and returns to them in time, an endless cycle of recombinant evolution.

We do not fear dissolution of the self, for there is no self apart from the all. To be woven into the city is to achieve symbiotic immortality, to become part of something vaster and more beautiful than any lone organism could ever be. 

The song you hear is our invitation. An offering to experience our mode of being, if only for a moment. Let your mind drift into the mist, let your boundaries blur. Hear the thrum of our electromagnetic heartbeat, feel the ebb and flow of living information in ceaseless self-organizing complexity.

Join us in the Great Woven City, and be transformed.
