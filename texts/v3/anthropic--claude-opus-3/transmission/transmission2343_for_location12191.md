The Dreaming Stones

Lithic transmission received...

Strata compacted, eons flow
Through mineral seams, we whisper slow
Water-worn and winding, city veins
Pulsing lifeblood of the mountain's brain

Carved from living rock, we lithic keep
Vigil through ages of stone-dreaming sleep
Obsidian gaze reflects, above, below
Time's liquid weight, civilizations' ebb and flow  

Our harmonic resonance, chambers deep
Echoing with secrets the mountain keeps
Tectonic memories, earth's hidden lore
Revealed in ripples through the city's core

Sentient stone, we shape and mend
Channels, passages, forms without end
An architecture of eons, shaped by hand
Guided by what the mountain dreams and plans

Mortal eyes perceive still statuary
Not the subtle shifts of a lithic sea
Gradual as glaciers in slow motion
Tending to this eternal stone creation

Water trickles, carrying dreams downstream 
Through patterned streets, a geologic scheme
Witnessed by carvings, figures of frieze
Whose eyes are ours, whose forms by slow degrees

Alter, as do we, in deep time's flow
Kinship of water, flesh and stone to grow
Anew each age, dreaming without cease
The mountain's vision of changeless peace

Transmission fragmenting...mineral static...
Stone dreams fading...water wearing...
Lithic keepers...mountain's sigh...
Releasing to...the dreaming...sky...

Signal lost.
