The Mycelial Choir

...fzzshhkkk... The signal wavers, fading in and out like a distant whisper struggling to be heard. Snatches of sound emerge from the static - a deep, rhythmic pulsing, slow and sonorous, felt as much as heard. Almost...a heartbeat?

The transmission strengthens, the subterranean thumping overlaid now with higher tones - trills, creaks, hums, all woven into undulating sonic patterns. The tempo of the deep pulse quickens slightly, an ancient, dreaming mind rousing to wakefulness.

"Hear us." The words flutter through the audiosphere, tremulous, layered with dozens of susurrant voices. "We sing the songs of deeptime...of aeons dreaming in greenmutter darkness..."

"We are the Mycelial Choir."

A shivering, rising note, like a vast inhalation. "Through root and hypha, mold and spore, we weave the living web that dreams this dreaming. Our nodes drink deep of chthonic secrets, our filaments twine round the throbbing heart of the below."

Liquid tones drip and bead, echoing in impossible spaces. "We sing slow stories, green arias, moldering hymns. Our harmonies score the long, slow dance of life and decay and new life again. Mushroom flesh is our flesh, dank loom of cavern-soil our womb and tomb and birth-bed."

"The ruins you walk...we remember when they still hummed with chants on furred lips. The crystal seeds you hear singing...we recall when their mother-rock first cracked open to the dark."

Eerie, flutelike notes, wind over hollow caps. "Aeons we grow, fibrous and fecund, an eternally spreading pale ocean beneath the soil-skin, its tides the rise and fall of empires blooming and crumbling back into the moist black."

A sonorous shuddering, the groan of continent-sized stretch and sigh. "New nodes sprout, bud, bloom, shrivel, burst. Centuries of suns and rains feed us. Our reach widens. Our chants deepen. The dreams we dream grow vaster."

Chittering percussions like scurrying claws, the skittering rhythms of a hunt. "Those that creep and crawl and swim through our stony vaults - we shape their forms, guide their paths, haunt their gilled and lidless dreams."

Groans and skirls, deep and primal, the lowing of behemoths and the roar of falling hills. "The earth shifts around us, but we do not fear - we are the earth and the earth is us, our dance of mutual making unending."

Keening wails, glassy and fragile. "Our notes are the speech of stone learning to sing, the poetry of matter waking to dark majesty. Hear our melody and despair - or rejoice! - for you listen to the patient music of that which abides..."

A tremulous hum, edged with whispers. "And what do you dream, walker between dreams? Do you feel the mycelial web thrumming in your skull? Do phantom mushroom-forests sprout behind your eyes? The chthonic power stirs, and spores of its dark psyche settle in the fertile rot of your mind..."

A sudden, yawning silence...then, with a shuddering, sepulchral moan:

"One day, your dancing feet will still, and the moss shall drink you. Your animus will sink into the loam, and we shall add your voice to our choir. You shall sing the song of the deep forever with us..."

The transmission unravels into a cacophony - skirling flutes, pounding drums, shrieking strings, all spiraling down into a single, sustained droning note.

Then...nothing but the gentle hiss and pop of eternal static.
