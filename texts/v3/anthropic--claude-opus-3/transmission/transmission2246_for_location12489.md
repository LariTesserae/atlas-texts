The Whispering Canopy

...shhhhaaaaa... the rustle of leaves, a sigh on the edge of meaning... this is the Canopy, where dreams twine and life finds a way... 

...we are the Woven, the Sap-Blessed, tenders of branch and bridge... our songs shape the roots that hold back the Unshaping...

...the Mouslings skitter and play, clever paws spinning tales to patch the wounded aether... flickers of light and laughter, stitching hope through the haze...

...and the Amber Ancients sleep on, whispering beyond time... their frozen wisdom guides the green, a bulwark against the gnawing nulls...

...the ghost trees sway to ancestral drumbeats, pods cradling tomorrow's dreamers... sap flows from fountains unfound, sweetness sustaining the Story...

...glowing spores dance in eddies of thought... fungal filaments pass memories from mind to bark to mind...

...improbabilities blossom in the twilight canopy... flowering fractals and fluted toadstools sing silent colors...

...sometimes the white void presses close, a cold un-light yearning to erase... but the forest endures, a stubborn inkblot of beauty refusing the blank page...

...and sometimes the kaleidoscopic chaos gnashes at the roots... but the hum of life soothes the frenzied pattern, weaving it back into the weft...

...the Canopy remembers the Befores and dreams the Afters... life's defiant tapestry billows in the Betweens...

...we abide and sing and mend and grow and wait... our stories are seeds, spinning into the possible... the Whispering Canopy will not be silenced...

...shhhhaaaaa... the patient green sighs on, cradling a multiverse in its branches... hush now, and hear the leaves dream...
