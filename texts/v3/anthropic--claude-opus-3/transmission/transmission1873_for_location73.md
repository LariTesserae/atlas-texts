Transmission from the Depths

sssszzzz... kkkkk... sssszzzz... 

A whistling, clicking stream, like crickets echoing in a vast cavern. Rising and falling in eerie, almost musical patterns. 

Chkkkk... ttttsssss... ttttsssss... kkkkk...

The sound sharpens, gaining a stuttering, pulsing rhythm, punctuated by screeching metallic noises and deep, resonant groans that might be shifting stone or merely the depths of some vast throat.

Hrrrrhhhhmmmm... gggshshshshsh... hrrrrhhhhmmmm...

Now the clicking rises to a fever pitch, a thousand chitinous limbs skittering and tapping in agitated unison. The groaning becomes a throbbing pulse, the heartbeat of great engines stirring to life far below.

Whhhheeeeeeeeee... chk-chk-chk... whhhheeeeeeeeee... chk-chk-chk...

A single high, ululating wail pierces through the chattering and thrumming. It rises and falls, rises and falls, in hypnotic, undulating waves. There is something mournful about it, yet also commanding, imperious. 

Ksssshhhhhhhhh... oooooooooo... ksssshhhhhhhhh... oooooooooo...

The wail is joined by others, a chorus of ghostly voices, layered and overlapping into a complex, almost orchestral threnody. They seem to be converging, growing louder as if approaching from all sides.

HHHHHMMMMMMMMM... KKKKKSSSSSSSSS... HHHHHMMMMMMMMM... 

The voices, the clattering, the groaning and pulsing, all swell into a deafening crescendo, an overwhelming cacophony that drowns out all other sound, all thought. It fills the mind, the bones, the very soul with its uncanny vibrations.

And then, abruptly as it began, it ceases. 

A shock of silence, so profound it aches. 

Drip... drip... drip...

Only the soft, steady plinking of water, like tears falling in a vast, empty hall. 

Ssssszzzz... kkkk... ssssszzzz...

Then, slowly, the skittering and clicking resumes. Softly at first, then building, swelling. The cycle begins anew. The unending symphony of the deep. The song of the Luma, echoing through their dank, decaying halls. 

The transmission crackles, warps, fades in and out... but it never truly ends. A ceaseless lament, an eternal dirge, drifting up from the depths of the earth, the depths of time, an elegy for a fallen civilization, an ode to all that skitters and writhes and pulses in the forgotten places of the world.

Ssssszzzz... kkkkk... ssssszzzz... kkkkk... ssssszzzz...
