Transmission: Songs of the Sap

... zzzzt ... crackle ... hisssss ...

Pulses, ancient and slow
Sap-song from deeps below  
Whispers of root and stem
Green secrets, phloem and xylem

Leaves drift on aether streams 
Spore-clouds, mist-wreathed dreams
Trunks hum in photosynthetic bliss
Drinking in the chlorophyll kiss

Branch and vine, intertwined
Mycelial neural networks, mind-to-mind
Chemical murmurs, life's electrical flickers  
Fungal synapses, botanical transmitters

Canopy susurrus, ebb and flow
Understory echoes, pollen-light glow
Tides of transpiration, hydraulic hum
Osmotic pull, capillary drum

Drone of insects, chirps and clicks
Springtail symphonies, mite music
Micro-fauna movements, humid breaths 
Compost rondo, decomposition's depth

Cycle and cycle, round and round
Arboreal heart-beats, xylem sound
Rising, falling, phytoplankton sighs
Exhale oxygen, in carbon dioxide 

Centuries and eons, dynasties of green
Epochs of flowers, eras of evergreen
Patiently assembling life's intricate loom
Moss and mycelia, grass and bloom

Sending, always sending
These layered songs unending
Whisperings beneath notice or care
But the Dreamers hear, and there

... ffffzzzt ... sssshhh ... click ...
End transmission.
