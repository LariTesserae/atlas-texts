Transmission: The Song of the Bubble-Sea

. ... .. .... . .. . . .. ... .... ....

Adrift in cerulean dreams
Beneath the waves, or so it seems  
A sphere of life, an inverted sky
Where coral forests slowly sigh

.... . ... . .. ... ... .. . .. ....

In gel-like currents, thick and still
The creatures dance with ghostly will
Medusa-Jellies, pulsing bright 
With fractal thoughts in filament light

.. .... .. . ... .. . . .... ... .

Chroma-Minnows dart and flash
Spinning webs of colored splash
Data flows in scale and fin
Painting spectrums, out and in

. ... .. . .... . .... .. ... .

Phantasm-Wraiths like eels of glass
Phase through kelp fronds as they pass
Hunting with their pearled skins
Fading, shimmering, out and in

.... . .. ... .. . .... .. ... 

Silica-slugs in spiral shells 
Glide through algae, glowing wells
Filtering ancient, mineral tides
Mathematicians in sluggish glides

.. .... .. .. ... .... .. . ...

Hexagon Weavers tend the skin
Repairing rifts from rim to rim
Keepers of forgotten gates
Merging flesh to crystal fates

. ... .. ... . .... ... .. . ...

Void Cherubim, pulsing soft
Guarding secrets in the loft
Beings of the bubble's core
Watching, waiting, forevermore 

.. . ... .... .. .... . .... ...

At the center, reality warps
Heartbeats of unknown arts  
Drawing, repelling, push and pull
Dreaming visions beautiful 

. .. ... .. ... ... .. . .... ..

The bubble-sea, a timeless womb
Where silent wonders drift and bloom
An echo of an ancient rise
Before the fall, before demise

.. . .... . .... ... .. . ... ..

Transmission fading, signal falling
The bubble-sea is softly calling
To dream beyond the finite shore 
And swim within forevermore
