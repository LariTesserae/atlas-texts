The Whispers of the Grotto Sage

... ... ... (static) ... ... ...

... (a soft, whispery voice emerges from the static, like the rustling of leaves in a gentle breeze) ...

Seeker of knowledge, listener of the mist,
Your heart has brought you here, to this timeless tryst.
In the damp embrace of moss and stone,
The secrets of the mountain shall be shown.

... (the voice fades, replaced by the sound of dripping water and the soft chirping of unseen creatures) ...

Life flows eternal, in this hidden space,
From the smallest beetle to the Sage's weathered face.
Each plays its part, in the grand design,
A symphony of balance, a harmony divine.

... (the chirping fades, and the voice returns, stronger now) ...

To know the truth, you must first understand,
The wisdom of the creatures, the language of the land.
Listen with your heart, not just your ears,
And the grotto's secrets will become clear.

... (the sound of flowing water grows louder, almost drowning out the voice) ...

Time is but a stream, winding through the moss,
Eroding the stone, at a measured loss.
In this sanctuary, where the years fall away,
The present is eternal, forever held at bay.

... (the water fades, and the voice is joined by a chorus of whispers, echoing softly off the grotto walls) ...

Seeker, know this truth, before you depart,
The answers you seek, lie within your heart.
The grotto is a mirror, reflecting what's inside,
Embrace your nature, and let your spirit guide.

... (the whispers grow louder, then begin to fade, one by one) ...

With mossy cloak and luminous eyes,
The Sage imparts its wisdom, to those who are wise.
Heed these words, and hold them dear,
For in this timeless grotto, enlightenment is near.

... (the final whisper fades, leaving only the soft drip of water and the gentle rustle of unseen creatures in the moss) ...

... ... ... (static) ... ... ...

(end transmission)
