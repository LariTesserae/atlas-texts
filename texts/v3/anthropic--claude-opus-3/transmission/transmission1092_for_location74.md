*static crackles, then a voice emerges, soft and melodic, with an undercurrent of rustling leaves*

...the roots remember...

*a pause, filled with the distant cries of unfamiliar birds*

...we sing the songs of stone and sap, of green and growing things. The great wheel turns, and we turn with it, branches stretching to caress the sky, toes sinking deep into rich loam.

*a gust of wind, the creak of wood*

From our high green halls, we have watched the world beyond wax and wane. Empires risen and fallen like the tides, cities grown and crumbled back to dust. But here, life endures. The mountain remains.

*the patter of rain on broad leaves*

We are the weavers, the green-singers, the keepers of tales whispered by ancient boughs. In our blood runs the memory of all that has been, the promise of what might yet be.  

*a trilling birdcall, laughter like a bubbling stream*

Come, walk the winding ways with us. Breathe deep the perfumed air, thick and sweet as honey-sap. Lay your burdens down and be at peace. In this high place, there is no striving, no toil - only the patient abiding, season upon season.

*the hum of insects, a distant roll of thunder*

See how the sun-leaves turn their faces to the light. How the mist jewels the spider's careful web. The small miracles of each day, precious and vital. We cherish them, these glinting facets of life's ever-shifting kaleidoscope.

*wind-chimes of wood and bone, soft and sonorous*

When night falls and the dark things prowl, we dream the ancient dreams of root and bough. Bright visions of the Summer yet to come, the bounty waiting to unfurl. We do not fear the shadow - it is but the other face of light.

*soft drumming, a heartbeat rhythm*

The mountain dreams, and we dream with it, our sap rising and falling in time with its slow breath. Stone endures and leaf renews, a dance as old as time. And still the roots grow deeper, seeking the heart of the world.

*the static rises, overwhelms the voice, then fades to a whisper*

...we are the green...growing...the roots remember...

*a final susurrus of wind in leaves, then silence*
