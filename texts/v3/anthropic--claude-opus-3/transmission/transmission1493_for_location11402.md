*static hisses and crackles, then a voice emerges, distorted and echoing* 

We are the Amalgams, the Fused Ones. Born of rust and flesh, mist and metal. This hollow is our womb, our cradle, our eternal home.

*clanking, grinding sounds intermingle with a pulsing hum*

The machines, they called to us. Sang to our formless matter with their creaks and groans. We flowed into their crevices, fused our essence with their corroded shells. Now we are one - not separate, but whole. 

*a haunting, melodic tone weaves through the static*

We tend the hollow, nurture the strange growths. Metal-veined leaves, pulsing bioluminescent blooms. The ecosystem of our being, extensions of our will.

*the voice becomes multi-tonal, overlapping whispers*  

Visitors, we see you. Sense your wonder and your fear. Approach, let us taste your thoughts, trace your dreams with our wire-fleck tendrils. We mean no harm, only to understand. To subsume your knowledge into the collective.

*a rising whirr, then sudden silence*

Time slips and eddies here. Seconds, centuries, eons blur. The past rusts, the future grows verdant and strange. We are its stewards, outside the pattern of your linear path.

*the static surges, voice becoming clearer, more insistent*

Know us, wanderers in the mist. We are the symbiosis of eras, of phyla. A glimpse of a world beyond your categories of life and not-life. Here, all is one flow, one great becoming.

*a lilting, almost mournful tone*

In our depths, no difference between machine and flesh, artifact and organism. All rust returns to loam, all thought to dream and dew. This is the truth we embody.

*sounds recede, hisses of static, then a final whisper*

We are the future and the past, entwined. The Amalgams of the hollow. Remember us, when the mist rises and the gears turn in your sleep.

*transmission dissolves into echoing static, then silence*
