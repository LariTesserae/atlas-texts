Whispers of the Verdant

Rustling leaves, creaking boughs,
A symphony of green endows
The city's heart with secrets old,
As Verdant Ones their tales unfold.

Through root and vine, a message sent,
In fragrant sighs and pulses meant
To guide the wanderers who seek
The wisdom of the ancient, the mystique.

"Abandon thoughts of time and space,
Embrace the green, the soft embrace
Of life eternal, cycles spun
From seed to leaf to seed again."

"Within these walls of stone and rust,
Where metal yields to vine's soft thrust,
We dance the dance of ages past,
Our forms entwined, in ritual cast."

"The boundaries blur, the lines dissolve,
As flesh and foliage slow revolve,
In sacred merger, green and strange,
A union born of ceaseless change."

"To you who hear our whispering,
A truth we share, a sacred thing:
The city breathes, the city dreams,
And in its heart, the Verdant teems."

"Release your hold on what you know,
Surrender to the green below,
And find within our leafy maze
A world beyond your human gaze."

"For here, amidst the mist and moss,
Where life and death entwine across
The fabric of this urban wood,
Lies wisdom old and truths withstood."

"So come, embrace the Verdant way,
And dance with us, in green array,
As brick and branch and flesh entwine,
In ancient city, yours and mine."

The whispers fade, the leaves grow still,
But through the city's veins, there will
Forever flow the Verdant song,
A green refrain, eternal, strong.
