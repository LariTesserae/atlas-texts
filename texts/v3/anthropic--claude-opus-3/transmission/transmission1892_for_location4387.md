Transmission from the Mossweaver's Song

Shhhhhhrrrrrrrrr... chk chk chk... vvvvvvvvvvvmmmmmmmm...

[A low, resonant hum fills the air, pulsing with an organic rhythm. It's a sound that seems to emanate from the very rocks and foliage of the pocket world, as if the space itself is breathing, sighing, dreaming.]

Chk-chk-chk-vrrrrrrrrrrrr...

[The hum is joined by a rustling, chittering chorus - the skittering of countless tiny feet across moss and stone, the whirr of iridescent insect wings, the drip and trickle of water down fern-furred walls.]

Mmmmmmmm-vvvvvvvv-sssssssssshhhhhhhh...

[Rising above it all, a melodic whisper, like wind through leaves, like sap pulsing beneath bark. It's a voice that is not a voice, a language of growth and green, of roots delving and shoots unfurling.]

The cycle turns, turns, turns,
From seed to sprout to spreading canopy,
All breathing, breeding, beading dew,
In our emerald sanctuary.

Rust-red relics, ancient alchemy,
Now cloaked in vine and fungal sheen,
Their purpose passed, their power pressed,
Into chlorophyll and chitinous carapace.

Chk-chk-chk-vrrrrrrrrrrrr...

We weave, we weave, we weave,
Moss into mantle, spore into spire,
Tending tendrils, mending mycelium,
An endless dance of weft and warp.

Vvvvvvvvvvvmmmmmmmm...

The cold stone cradles, the mist exhales,
Each niche a nursery, each pool a womb,
Life multiplies in velvet vaults,
Secluded from the wider world.

And here I hum, here I hover,
Neither quite flora nor truly fauna,
A patchwork pattern, a chimeric chant,
The Mossweaver, the green gatherer.

Shhhhhhrrrrrrrrr... chk chk chk... vvvvvvvvvvvmmmmmmmm...

[The voice recedes, blurring back into the ambient drone of the pocket world, leaving only the impression of patient, verdant presence, coiled within the humidity and haze. The song continues, wordless and unceasing, murmuring of hidden histories and improbable evolutions, a lullaby for a green, growing world tucked away from sight.]
