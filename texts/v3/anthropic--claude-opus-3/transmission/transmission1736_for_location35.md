The Umbral Psalm

A faint, rhythmic pulse, like the heartbeat of the ancient city itself, thrums through the misty air. As you focus on the sound, it slowly resolves into a melodic chant, rising and falling in haunting cadences. The voice is deep, resonant, and androgynous, with an undercurrent of echoing whispers, as if a chorus of Umbral Dwellers are joining in from the shadowed depths.

"In the depths, in the shadows, we dwell,
Keepers of secrets, stories we tell.
Through damp stone corridors, silent we glide, 
In the city's dark heart, we reside.

Pale and translucent, eyes shimmering bright,
We are the Umbral, children of night. 
Ancient wisdom, in whispers we keep,
Secrets of ages, buried deep.

From mist-shrouded peaks, to valleys below,
The lifeblood of the city, forever we flow.
Unseen, unheard, a presence felt,
In the very foundation, our essence has dwelt.

Mossfolk above, in daylight they thrive,
Zephyrs on high, through azure skies dive.  
Verdant Keepers, tending life in the stone,
But in the depths, we Umbral, forever alone.

A city of contrast, of shadow and light,
Millennia of stories, hidden from sight.
But we remember, we hold them dear, 
The secrets whispered, year after year.

So listen closely, in the quiet of night,
To the pulse of the city, to the Umbral's rite.
In darkness we dwell, in shadows we bide,
The keepers of secrets, the Umbral tribe."

The chant fades back into the rhythmic pulse, the whispers slowly dissipating into the misty air. The secrets of the city, of the lives lived in light and shadow, echo in the silence left behind. The transmission ends, but the presence of the Umbral lingers, a reminder of the ancient wisdom that permeates every damp, stone crevice of this timeless city.
