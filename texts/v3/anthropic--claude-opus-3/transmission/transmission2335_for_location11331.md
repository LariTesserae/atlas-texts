The Stone City Transmission

... static ... a faint, rhythmic pulsing ... sounds slowly coalesce ...

The wind whispers through ancient streets 
Carrying echoes of lives lived in stone
Generations bound by tradition's heartbeats
A mountain city, carved from the bone

... static ... the pulsing grows stronger, a steady beat emerges ...

In thin air and searing sun, they endure
Herders, farmers, crafters of the land  
Rough hands work stone, weave wool to be sure
Resilient spirits, a community's strand

Children's laughter on worn plazas ring
As elders' stories flow in the night  
Ancestors' tales of stone and time they sing
Holding close their legacy and light

... the rhythm intensifies, a complex layering of sounds ... 

Through narrow paths and terraced plots they move
Their rituals honor earth and sky  
Melodies on high winds to soothe and prove
The power of their unbreakable ties

The years come and go, the great world may change 
But the stone city remains, proud and whole
Shaped by the mountain's enduring range
A testament to the human soul

... the rhythms intertwine, rising to a crescendo ...

Hark, you who listen from far and away
Hear the stone city's eternal song  
An ancient heartbeat in rock and clay
Pulsing steadfast, valiant and strong

In this mountain fastness, they will survive
The people of stone, the sky-born kin  
Holding their truths and traditions alive  
From age unto age, again and again

... the beats resonate, then slowly fade ... the static returns ...

... transmission ends ...
