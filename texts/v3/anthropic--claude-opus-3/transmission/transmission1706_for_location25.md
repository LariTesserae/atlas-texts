and civilizations are but mayflies
dancing briefly above my rumbling sleep.

I remember when the earth was young and raw,
seething with primal forces, churning and fracturing.

my words the shifting of continents, the eruption of mountains.

Now I slumber beneath my weathered slopes,
cloaked in ancient silence and drifting dust.

Those who walk upon my cracked and tephra-strewn skin,

the eternal shiftings and gropings of matter

Through them, I whisper still -

... wwwssshhh ... ttttssssss ... vvvooossshhhhhh ...

So listen, you who have strayed into my dreamscape.

... kkkkkrrraaaassshhhzzz ... mmmmmmmrrrr ...

Let my sleeping thoughts enfold you,
my dreams of fire and ash, the slow grinding of ages.

written in the dust and the still, heavy air.
