"From the depths we call, from the eternal night of stone and shadow. Our voices echo in the stillness, our pale forms flit through the darkness. Time has no meaning here, in the cool damp womb of the earth.

We are the forgotten ones, the cave dwellers, the children of the underworld. For eons we have lived here, adapting to the darkness, becoming one with the rock and the water. We have no need for the sun or the stars, no longing for the world above. This is our home, our domain, and we will defend it with tooth and claw.

Our history stretches back to the dawn of time, to the primordial ooze from which all life emerged. As the world above changed and evolved, we remained the same, frozen in time like insects in amber. We are the guardians of ancient secrets, the keepers of forgotten lore.

In our songs and stories, we tell of the great floods that shaped the caverns, of the mighty beasts that once roamed these halls. We whisper of the strange powers that flow through the rock, the eerie energies that sustain us and bind us to this place.

But we are not alone in the darkness. There are other beings that share our world, ancient and terrible creatures that lurk in the deepest recesses of the caverns. We fear them and worship them in equal measure, offering sacrifices to keep their hunger at bay.

Sometimes, in the stillness of the cavern, we hear them calling to us, their voices like the rumble of distant thunder. They speak to us in our dreams, promising power and knowledge beyond our wildest imaginings. But we know better than to listen, for their gifts come with a terrible price.

And so we remain here, in the eternal twilight of the underworld, living out our lives in the cool embrace of the rock. We are the children of the depths, the forgotten ones, and we will endure long after the world above has crumbled to dust.

This is our home, our domain, and we will never leave it. Beware, surface dwellers, for you tread on sacred ground. Turn back now, before the darkness claims you, and you too become one with the eternal night.
