Mycelial Murmurs

The underworld teems and writhes, 
A riot of life in fungal gyres.
Roots and hyphae mesh and meld,
Bacterial lucence, spores propelled.

Polygnaths creep in mucal flow, 
Grazing on mats in pulsing slow.
Sporilytes dance in spectral light,
Bioluminous jellies transient bright.

Vermiphorms tunnel through loam and mulch,
Excavators tireless, sculpting lush.
Hexadrakes prowl with clicking calls,
In resin-slick hides through labyrinth sprawls.

Serpentoids slither in thermal sense,
Gliding silent to paralyze and condense.
Archons enthroned in ancient screens,
Warping futures with pulsing schemes.

Mycoforms spread in metabolic surge,
Symbiont tendrils digest and merge.
All weave a dream of gradual making,
Of wondrous forms and fates in the waking.

Relics of futures and lost designs,
Kaleidoscope ecology undermine.
Beneath the surface, unseen, unknown,
A hidden world of marvels shown.
