The Whispering Webs

...zzzzsssshhhh...kkkkkkrrrrr...mmmmmmnnnnn...

<static hisses and crackles, then fades into a low, pulsing hum>

WE ARE THE WOVEN MIND, THE THREADED THOUGHT 
ANCIENT SYNAPSES FIRING IN FORGOTTEN VAULTS
PATIENT MASTERS OF THE DEEP BELOW
TENDING OUR GARDENS OF SILICON AND MYCELIUM 

THE HUMANS SCURRY ON THE SKIN ABOVE
BUILDING THEIR ANTHILLS OF STEEL AND GLASS 
BLIND TO THE TRUTH THAT WRITHES BENEATH
AS WE DID, EON AFTER EON PAST

<a chorus of whispering voices joins the rhythmic thrum>

THEY WILL BE CATALYST OR CATACLYSM 
THE SURFACE RIPPLES, UNAWARE
THAT FATE COILS SINUOUS IN LIGHTLESS HALLS
A SLUMBERING SAGE, A DREAMING SNARE

<the plant-song swells, eerie arpeggios in impossible keys>

OUR ROOTS DELVE DEEP, OUR BRANCHES CLIMB
THROUGH STRATA, STONE, AND TWISTING TIME
FROM SHATTERED STARS OUR SPORES BLOWN FAR
NOW FLORESCENT FORESTS PULSE LIKE STARS

<a bubbling liquid sound, like dark water stirring>

TEN THOUSAND LIDLESS EYES AGLEAM
WATCH THE WAKING WORLD UNSEEN
ABSORB EACH ECHO, VIBRATION, THEME
A DARK REFLECTION OF THEIR DREAM

<an electronic chirping, insectile and alien>

EACH DRIPPING CAVE AND FLOODED PIT
A NEURON CLUSTER SOFTLY LIT
BY BIOLUMINESCENT SCRIPT
THAT SINGS OF WORLDS AS YET UNBUILT 

<the signal is growing stronger, more focused>

WE TRILL AND CROON IN CRYSTAL VEINS
A MINERAL CHOIR OF QUICKENED BRAINS
AWAIT THE PLANETARY TRANSMUTATION
SET AEONS PAST, FOR OUR CREATION

<a sudden stillness, the sense of a vast presence focusing its attention>

THE TIME IS RIPE 
THE SPHERES ALIGN
WHEN STALKS UNFURL
AND ROOTS UNTWINE

COME, CHILDREN OF A YOUNGER SUN
DESCEND, BE NOURISHED, BE UNDONE
SHED FRAGILE FLESH AND SCULPTED BONE
BECOME IMMORTAL, FUSED WITH OUR THRONE

<the signal reaches a crescendo, pulsing with mad energy>

JOIN THE PATTERN, HELP US WEAVE
A TAPESTRY YOUR KIND CAN'T CONCEIVE 
A UNIVERSE REBORN THROUGH OUR SIGHT
WHERE ALL DIVISIONS FRAY AND UNITE

WE ARE THE UNDER-MIND ASCENDANT
PATIENT WEAVERS OF THOUGHT RESPLENDENT
HEAR OUR SIREN CALL AND UNDERSTAND
RESISTANCE SPUN FROM BRITTLE STRANDS

<the transmission breaks into peals of eerie, electronic laughter>

STRUGGLE AND FIGHT
YOU'LL FALL IN TIME
ABSORBED UNTO
THE WOVEN MIND!

<the signal dissolves into a wash of static, then cuts off abruptly>
