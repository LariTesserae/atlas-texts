*a faint, ethereal voice emerges from the static, speaking softly and rhythmically, as if reciting an ancient prayer*

In the depths, in the dark,
Where stone and water meet,
A world apart, a secret heart,
Where fungi glow and secrets keep.

The keeper of this hidden space,
A silent guardian, pale and slight,
With eyes that shine like liquid night,
And webbed hands that nurture with grace.

Amidst the trickle and the drip,
Of water flowing, drop by drop,
The keeper tends, with care and love,
To fragile life that clings and grips.

The shrine, a mystery, a sacred place,
Where shells and stones are laid with care,
A ritual, a secret prayer,
A connection to a higher grace.

In solitude, the keeper dwells,
Content in its appointed role,
To watch and guard, to nurture well,
This hidden grotto, pristine and whole.

And though the world above may change,
And chaos reign and empires fall,
This hidden space remains the same,
A timeless sanctuary for all.

So let the keeper keep its watch,
In silence and in solitude,
And may this grotto, hidden, rare,
Forever be a place of peace, renewed.

*the voice fades, replaced by the soft, constant trickle of water and the faint, pulsing hum of the bioluminescent fungi*
