Transmission: Whispers of the Hollow

A low, pulsing hum fills the air, emanating from the depths of the hollow. It is a sound that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere at once, vibrating through the stone and soil, carried on the currents of the moist, heavy atmosphere. The hum rises and falls in a steady rhythm, like the slow beating of an ancient heart.

As the hum continues, whispers begin to emerge from the background noise, their origins unclear. The voices are soft and sibilant, their words indistinct, as if spoken in a language long forgotten by the outside world. They seem to be coming from the weathered stones themselves, the voices of the hollow given form.

"Eaessshhhaaarrreee...sssaaarrreeennnn...vvvooossshhhiiimmmaaa..."

The whispers grow louder, more insistent, as if trying to impart some vital message. The words become more distinct, but their meaning remains elusive, cloaked in layers of ancient mystery.

"Keeper...balance...preserve...endure..."

The voices begin to overlap and intertwine, creating a complex tapestry of sound. The whispers speak of the hollow's history, of the countless ages that have passed within its walls. They tell of the primitive peoples who once sought shelter here, of the Keeper's tireless vigil, and of the delicate balance that must be maintained.

"Cycles...renewal...transformation...eternal..."

As the transmission continues, the whispers begin to take on a more urgent tone. They speak of impending change, of forces gathering beyond the hollow's walls that threaten to upset the equilibrium. The voices call out to the Keeper, urging them to be vigilant, to protect the hollow and its secrets.

"Shadows...gathering...prepare...defend..."

The hum intensifies, the whispers swirling and eddying in the heavy air. The messages become more fragmented, the words breaking apart and reforming in strange new patterns. The transmission seems to be nearing its end, the voices fading back into the background noise.

"Eeessshhhaaarrreee...Keeper...vvvooossshhhiiimmmaaa...endure..."

With a final, shuddering pulse, the hum dissipates, the whispers falling silent. The hollow is once again filled with the soft sounds of dripping water and the skittering of unseen creatures. But the message lingers in the air, a warning and a promise, a reminder of the Keeper's eternal mission and the importance of the hollow's preservation.

The transmission ends, but its echoes remain, reverberating through the timeless depths of the hollow, a testament to the enduring power of the Keeper and the mysteries that lie hidden within these ancient walls.
