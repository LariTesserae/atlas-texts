"... is anyone out there? Can anyone hear me? This is Oria Leafsong, broadcasting from the heart of the Canopy City. I don't know if this signal will make it past the dense foliage, but I have to try...

We've lost contact with the world below. It's been cycles since our last successful transmission, and we fear something has gone terribly wrong. The mist that once nourished our roots now carries a strange, sickly-sweet scent that makes our eyes water and our throats itch. Even the glow beetles seem agitated, their normally steady light pulsing erratically in the night.

But that's not the worst of it. The trees... the trees are changing. Their bark is splitting, oozing a dark, viscous sap that smells of decay. The leaves are withering, curling in on themselves like gnarled fingers. We've tried everything - increasing the humidity, adjusting the nutrient balance in the hydroponics - but nothing seems to help.

Our growers are working around the clock, but they're fighting a losing battle. Each day, more of the gardens turn brown and brittle, the fruits shriveling on the vine. We've rationed our remaining supplies, but I fear it won't be enough.

And then there are the whispers... the ones that come in the dead of night, carried on the sickly breeze. Some say they've seen shadows moving in the depths of the forest, things that shouldn't be there. Others claim to hear voices, strange and distorted, calling out from the darkness. I don't know what to believe anymore.

We're trying to hold on to hope, to the belief that this is just a passing blight, that the forest will heal and our city will thrive once more. But with each passing day, that hope grows dimmer.

If anyone out there can hear this, please... send help. We need supplies, medicine, anything you can spare. And if you have any knowledge of what might be happening to our trees, our very lifeblood... please, share it with us. We are a strong and resourceful people, but even we have our limits.

This is Oria Leafsong, signing off. May the sun shine again upon the Canopy City..."
