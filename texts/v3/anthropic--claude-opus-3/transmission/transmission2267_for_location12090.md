The Song of the Pale Ones

Click-trill-whisper, echo-fade,
Through twilight chambers, a haunting cascade,
Opalescent forms, grace in motion,
Guardians of a realm, timeless and forgotten.

Whisper-click-trill, in shadows they dance,
Obsidian eyes, like liquid night's expanse,
Pale limbs entwined, with vine and root,
Sculpture and painter, musicians of flute.

In crumbling halls, their melodies resound,
A symphony of ages, ancient and profound,
Secrets whispered, in tongues unknown,
Histories woven, in ghostly tone.

Trill-whisper-click, a languid procession,
Through moss-draped streets, a spectral obsession,
Rituals enacted, in phosphorescent glow,
Ethereal beauty, ebbs and flows.

Amid the decay, life's delicate balance,
Preserved by the Pale Ones, in eternal dance,
Click-whisper-trill, their voices entwine,
With the city's heartbeat, a rhythm divine.

Through eons untold, their song echoes still,
In caverns deep, where time stands still,
A testament to life, unyielding and vast,
The Pale Ones endure, in twilight's embrace.

Whisper-trill-click, the chorus fades,
As shadows lengthen, in subterranean glades,
But the song remains, a haunting refrain,
In the timeless depths, where the Pale Ones reign.

[Transmission fades into eerie silence, punctuated by the soft, rhythmic clicking and trilling of the Pale Ones, a ghostly echo of their eternal presence in the ancient, subterranean city.]
