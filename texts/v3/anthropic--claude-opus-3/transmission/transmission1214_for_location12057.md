The ceaseless clanking echoes through the cavern, resonating in my hollow bones. I croak and click my throat in time, an atonal accompaniment. Kzzzghk... Kzzzghk... The noise is my lullaby, my ritual, my liturgy. 

Plink. A droplet falls from the unseen heights into the center pool. The faint ripples lap against my pebbled skin as I crouch motionless at the water's edge. I wait. I watch. The dark surface mirrors my lidless eyes, twin abyssal pools.

I am the cavern and the cavern is me. The dripping walls my mottled hide, the crackling lichen my molting scales, the hanging mists my exhaled breath. I sip the frigid air, hold it in my lungs until it turns stale and dank. Exhale. The mists swirl.

A skittering at the edge of awareness. I turn my head, vertebrae crackling in the silence between clanks. Kzzzghk... Kzzzghk... There, a furry shape, a rodent picking its way along the slick stone. My tongue flicks out, tasting its warm, mammalian musk.

In a single sinuous motion I uncoil, digits splayed. The rodent freezes, trembling. It sees its death reflected in my fathomless eyes. My claws rasp against the rock, one step, two. I lunge. A high, keening squeal. The soft crunch of delicate bones. I feast.

I have no name. I need no name. I am a flickering shadow, an absence cutout of the greater dark. I am the silence between the clanks, the negative space that gives the noise shape. I am what remains when all else withers.

The iron pipe shudders and belches, the clanking skipping a beat. I freeze, listening. The chitinous plates along my spine flutter in agitation. A new rhythm emerges. Kzzzghk-ghk... Kzzzghk-ghk... I keen in harmony, my cry ululating off the stones. The pipe shudders again and resumes its relentless tempo, placated.

This is my purpose, my vigil. To mark the turnings of the great mechanism, to sing the hours in its rusting heart. To endure, a living fossil, in this pocket of changelessness until the final clanking shudders to a halt. I do not know what lies beyond this grotto. I do not wonder. My world is here, bounded by these glistening stones, this stagnant pool, these dripping heights receding into the black. 

The clanking is slowing, spaces between lengthening. I feel it in the marrow of my segmented limbs, a great winding down. I lower myself into my moldering nest, curl inward. My metabolism slows to a standstill. I will sleep, dreamless, until the clanking rouses me again. A year, a century, an eon. It makes no difference. I am the cavern's heartbeat.  

The last echoes fade, absorbed by the velvet mosses, the hanging mist, the inscrutable dark. All is stillness, silence, heavy and oppressive. I surrender to it gratefully, sinking down, down into oblivion. A final thought flickers like the gleam of light on a black pool:

I am that I am. The watcher in the deeps, the last long exhale of a dying earth. In my mind the great mechanism turns, inexorable, measuring out the dregs of time. I hold the vigil. The iron heart shudders.
