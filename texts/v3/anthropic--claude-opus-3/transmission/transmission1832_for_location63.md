Transmission: Echoes from the Deep

... static ... faint clicking sounds ... a rhythmic pulsing ...

... strange, elongated shadow appears on the narrow band ... shadow splits, multiplies, forms intricate lattice ...

They scurry in the black damp
Milk-pale flesh, joints all askew 
Clicking, tapping, feelers fanned
From fungal silk their strange nests grew

No sun, no moon, perpetual night
By luminous growths they cultivate 
Antennae read th' electric paths just right
Sensorium to darkness dedicate

Fiercely they guard those chambers worn  
With venom-fang and unified attack
Generations bred and ancient secrets borne
In blind eyeless bodies, knowledge they pack

Resilience, tenacity here personified
In forms grotesque yet strangely sublime
Life itself 'gainst all odds satisfied
Testament enduring beyond reach of time

They skitter on as they have always done
Beneath crumbling stones of ages past
Of their presence we glimpse only a fraction  
Unseen in the deep realm they hold fast

... lattice of shadows writhes, coalesces, fades into static once more ...

... end transmission.
