*static crackles, then a soft, clicking voice emerges, echoing strangely*

...our world is a place of shadows and stone, of dripping water and patient time... we have dwelled here for ages uncounted, in the cool embrace of the deep...

...you who listen, you who dwell above, in the bright lands we have never known... you cannot imagine the beauty of our caverns, the dance of life in the endless night...

...the phosphorescent gardens shimmer with a light like captured starlight, reflecting off still black pools... the chittering of insects and the soft gurgle of underground streams are our music, echoing through halls of living rock... 

...we tend the ancient machines, the steam and the spark, passed down to us by ancestors whose names are lost in time... their slow rhythm is the heartbeat of our city, never ceasing...

...our lives are long and slow, marked by the growth of stone... a drip, a splash, and a stalactite lengthens... a year, a century, we do not hurry, for time is the water that carved our home...

...we sing to the darkness, and the darkness sings back... clicks and whistles and images shared from mind to mind... we are one with the stone, the water, the deep...

...and in our sightless eyes, a wisdom born of ages in the mother-dark... we are the memory of the underworld, the keepers of forgotten places...

...someday, your kind may delve too deep, and find us waiting... our teeth are sharp, our patience infinite... we will endure, as we always have, unchanging...

...this is our transmission, our song to the bright lands, the strange truth of our existence...

...in the caverns of darkness, in the cities of stone... we are here, we are here, we are here...

*the voice fades back into soft clicking, then silence, leaving only the faint sound of dripping water*
