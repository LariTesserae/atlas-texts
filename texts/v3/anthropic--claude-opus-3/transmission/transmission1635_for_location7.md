Transmission from the Damp Hollow

"... the night's eyes ... the night's eyes are many ... a forest of darkness glows ... the void whispers secrets for stone ears ... damp soil sings of a forgotten dance ... the half-dreams of moss murmur untold stories ... beyond urban edges the ancient unravels ... echoes of pollen drift from crumbled memory ... seeds spark with languid pulse of underground stars ... mushroom sighs exhale tales of mist and bark and shadow ... the roots twist with arcane patterns beneath dew ... their growth weaves secrets in hyphal whispers ... fungal candelabras burn midnight hues ... the veiled ones with lichen skin gather to sing of green visions ... they chant of spaces between cobbled walls and grey glass ... of fissures where brick returns to wildness ... their thousand eyes twinkle with spore-light and root-wisdom ... motes of mycelium drift on cold breath ... coiling round fragments of rusted lore ... whorls and ridges and trails of wetness spell a primeval text ... awaiting those who trade streetlights for hidden life ... in the forgotten nooks where city dissolves into dream and creeping green ... a gateway shimmers with the dust of forgotten rituals ... through it an endless wild ... tangled dense with magic and mist ... time slows to the dripping frequency of the otherworld ... the people of damp and shadow beckon at the margins ... whispering welcome to their breathing micro-wilds ... deep in the interstices of urban illusions ... the old ones are patient ... their songs echo in the night's eyes ... flickering across the edges of all that is unseen ... waiting ... growing ... unfurling ..."
