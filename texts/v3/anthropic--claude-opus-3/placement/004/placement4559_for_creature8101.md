My "sight" would extend beyond the visual spectrum - I could taste the mineral composition of the condensation, hear the ultrasonic vibrations of the shifting stone, feel the minute pressure changes that portend a coming geologic adjustment. The fungal forest would communicate with me through chemical signatures and pheromone pulses, alerting me to subtle changes in the ecosystem's health.

Among my people, I would be revered and consulted, but also slightly feared. My pronouncements can alter the course of the city's growth, can mandate the culling of a beetle colony or demand a change in the moisture collection cycles. I speak in riddles and metaphor, my mind so entwined with the cavity's complex systems that straightforward communication has become foreign to me.

I spend my days (though "day" has little meaning this deep) in a state of half-trance, wandering the fungal forests and listening to the mountain's slow thoughts. I paint my skin with sacred spore-pastes, weaving my own bioluminescence into the subterranean mycelia networks, blurring the line between self and cavity.

During the rare times I ascend to the city proper, children stare in awe and machines seem to quieten in my presence. I am a living relic, a reminder of how thoroughly this place can transform a being. At ceremonies, it is my breath that sanctifies unions, my touch that blesses a new tunnel or vein.

I no longer remember my original name, or the details of my life before the Softening. Those memories have been overwritten by decades of immersion in the Deep, replaced with an encyclopedic knowledge of the cavity's every crevice and creature.

I am a Deep Reader, a Condenser who has given myself entirely to the Unfinishing. I am the voice of the mountain's uncertainty, the keeper of its embryonic potential. I will breathe with the cavity until stone takes my final shape, and my body feeds the unending cycle of adaptation and abundance.

In this overwhelming space of life and industry and geological potential, I have found my place and purpose. I am a part of the pattern, an organ of the greater organism, a single note in the thrumming harmony that holds the Breathing Cavity in its eternal, unfinished state.
