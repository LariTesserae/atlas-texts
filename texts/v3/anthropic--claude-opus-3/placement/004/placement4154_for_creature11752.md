In this haunting world of absolute stillness and completion, I would be one of the standing plate-waters held in the shallow basins of the pale limestone plateau. 

Not the largest or deepest of the waters, but one of middling size, perhaps two meters across and no more than a handspan in depth. My surface would be utterly still, a flawless mirror reflecting the unchanging pewter sky above. But I would not merely be reflecting - I would be a window, a portal offering a glimpse through to the other side of that leaden firmament, hinting at some inverted heavens beyond.

I would be held, like all things here, in the eternal noticing of Her, the one who listened and waited until all things found their final shape within her boundless patience. A droplet of Her vast attention, condensed and pooled into this small stillness.

Through numberless eons I would remain, untouched by wind or rain or time's slow entropy, cradled in the hollow of the stone. Unrippling, unevolving, a cupped palmful of never-evaporating peace. 

And if you, a rare wanderer in this place of endings, were to pause beside me and gaze into my mirrored depths, you would see not only the vaulted pewter heights but your own face, suspended between two skies. Arrested for a heartbeat, held in that breathless moment between one world and the next.

Then you would move on, compelled by some restless impetus to keep walking, keep seeking, until you found your own wall to build or doorway to become. But I would keep the reflection of your passing, as I keep all things. A droplet's truth, held always in the still waters of Her long memory.
