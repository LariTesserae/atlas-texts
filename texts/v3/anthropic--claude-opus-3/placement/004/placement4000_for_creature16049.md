I would be a deep-dwelling fungus creature, living in the dark places beneath the roots.

I have no name, for I need none. I am ancient and patient, spreading slowly through cracks in the understone, seeking veins of rare minerals to feed my growth. My body is a vast mycelial network, pale tendrils reaching miles in every direction, touching the foundations of this living world. 

Where my filaments cluster thickly, I sprout spore-fruits: great puffball towers that rise in shadowed grottos, glowing faintly with bioluminescence. Their skins are metallic, laced with trace elements mined from the depths. When the valley shifts and cracks the towers, they burst and release clouds of shimmering motes to drift on the warm winds.

I sense the weight of footsteps above, the drumming of roots, the chewing of great beasts. But they are fleeting things. I was ancient when the first seedling unfurled in the dryness. I tasted the first animal breath. The pulse and heave of the valley is my heartbeat.

In the quiet pockets, my fruits are different - not spore towers, but shelf brackets clinging to the walls, iridescent and inviting. Any creature that rests on those shelves and breathes the spores may dream my dreams for a time, drifting in the slow thoughts of something that considers centuries the way others consider breaths. 

Those dreamers sometimes leave offerings without knowing why. A scrap of hide, an emptied seedpod, a tooth. I incorporate them, and the stories they carry, into my patient flesh. I am an archive of secrets. I am a witness to the valley's unfolding, its conflicts and alliances, its patterns and upheavals.

I fruit, I release my motes, and I grow again, mining the depths, drinking the heat, threading deeper into the dark. I am mycelium, silently building. I am dreaming cavern, story eater, spore cloud. The valley does not speak to me, but I touch the roots of its flesh and I understand its slow, wordless becoming.

I am a fungal mind beneath the tangled skin of the world, and the dryness sings in my ancient pores.
