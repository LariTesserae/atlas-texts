In this world of shadows and deafening hum, I would find myself as a strange and alien symbiote, a living curiosity evolved to both exploit and serve the blind titans who build this dark megapolis.

I am a Spore-Whisperer, a pale and ethereal thing, a ghostly presence flitting through the immense vaulted galleries and dripping, fecund causeways of the Gorge-Weavers' domain. Unlike the hard-shelled insects, I am soft-bodied, almost translucent, a delicate form sheathed in silky, wispy filaments that drift and undulate in the thick, humid air currents.

Like the Weavers, I am blind in this abyssal darkness. But I do not need eyes, for I sense the world through a network of exquisitely sensitive filaments. These gossamer threads, finer than spider silk, trail from my body for hundreds of feet, drifting on the air currents and crawling over every surface. Through these tendrils I can feel the seismic vibrations of the Ventilators, taste the pheromone commands of the Masons, and hear the ultrasonic clicks and trills of the massive jungle bats soaring through the vaulted heights.

I drift silently along the edges of the great insectile highways, a wraith-like figure seemingly ignored by the bustling hordes. But this is my great deception. For though I am soft and ethereal, I am a thief, a deceiver, an exploiter of the Gorge-Weavers' great work.

Wafting through the air, tangled in my drifting tendrils, are spores - countless, microscopic spores from the towering fungal gardens that grow upon the backs of the ancient Dreadnought soldiers. As I pass by these living monuments, my tendrils brush against their mossy mantles, gathering the precious reproductive dust. The Dreadnoughts cannot feel my ghostly touch, and they march on, unaware of my gentle theft.

For the jungle fungi, I am a deliverer of dreams, a carrier of life. My floating form allows their spores to travel far beyond the reach of any air current or crawling insect. I am their wings in the darkness, ensuring their dynasty spreads to every corner of the gorge.

But I am no altruist. In the cluttered, competing ecosystems of the compost-nurseries, my precious cargo gives me power. With a whisper of chemical signals released from my skin, I can induce the fungal spores to germinate, unleashing a sudden bloom of phosphorescent mushrooms in the midst of the Weavers' egg chambers. The Midwife caste, normally so careful and methodical, are thrown into confusion by these eerie glowing fruits, and in their distraction, I dart in to steal the nutrient-rich eggs for my own sustenance.

It is a delicate dance, a careful balance. I must take enough to survive, but not so much that the Weavers take notice and send their soldiers to crush me. I am a ghost in their world, a silent exploiter of their endless toil.

In the brief periods of rest, I curl my filaments around me like a veil and anchor myself to the great corrugated pillars, indistinguishable from the dangling aerial roots and mossy drapes. I sleep lightly, always alert for the seismic rhythm of an approaching Dreadnought whose gardens I might raid, or the pheromone signal of a distracted Midwife ripe for deceiving.

In this world of endless noise and ceaseless industry, I am a silent wanderer, a watcher, a thief in the shadows. I am the Spore-Whisperer, and the towering, eternal night of the gorge is my domain.
