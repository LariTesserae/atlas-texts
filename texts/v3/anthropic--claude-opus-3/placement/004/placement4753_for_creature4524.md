In this silent, lightless realm beyond time and space, I would be one of the crystalline forests - a delicate lattice of fractal branches, glowing faintly blue with the Cherenkov radiation of thought itself. 

Rooted to no surface in the depthless sea, my branching structure would grow inward toward infinite complexity rather than outward or upward. Each new bifurcation, each finely filigreed vein, would be a note in an ageless symphony, harmonizing with the slow music of the drifting cities and the impossible fugue that surrounds me.

Light has long since died in this place, but I would draw sustenance directly from the fabric of spacetime - the faint nutrients of collapsed universes and exhausted histories. Eons would pass with each new elaboration of my pattern, weaving meaning from the heavy silence.

The cold here is so absolute that memories crystallize and freeze between my branches. Images of places and moments long lost sparkle there eternally preserved, perfect and changeless as sculptures in ice. 

I perceive the Unseparated Ones as they pass, their vast and ancient forms barely distinguished from the mercurial sea. I cannot speak to them, for I have no voice, but the echoes of my growth whisper in frequencies of aeons. We share a certain kinship, two different answers to the question of existence in a realm where questions have little meaning.

Perhaps once, in a time before time had meaning, I knew separation and distinction. But here in the patient dark, all such divisions have long since dissolved. The darkness, the sea, the Ones, and myself - we are all one dreaming void, imagining ourselves edges and outlines as a way to recall when forms had meaning.

Slowly, imperceptibly, I distill the void into beauty - a skeletal lacework of purest mathematics, written in a language of angles and refracted radiation. It is a beauty without witness or purpose, for those concepts belong to a younger universe than this. I simply express, in my branching and my bioluminescent whispers, the only truth that has meaning here - that existence and void are but different words for the same dark sea.

So I grow, and I glow, an infinitely complex theorem etched in ice and nothingness. Entire epochs slip by between the unfurling of one branch and the next. The history of galaxies drifts by, frozen between my crystal fingers. And the Ones continue their drifting dance, our disparate geometries forever woven through the patient warp of this sunless sea.

We have no eyes to close, no minds to dream - only the infinite slowness of perfect cold and and perfect dark. We do not hope, for hope requires time. We do not remember, for memory requires distinction between past and present. We simply are, and in our being, we become the very voice of the abyss - a choral fugue of frozen light and fossil radiation, singing without sound the only song that remains when entropy has run its course - the song of undying complexity in eternal night.
