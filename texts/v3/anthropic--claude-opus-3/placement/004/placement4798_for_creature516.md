In this luminous, living world of the Undervault, I imagine myself becoming one of the Root Watchers. 

I feel the call to the deepest parts of the vault, where the eldest dwell in their architectural fusion with the wood. Slowly, patiently, I make my way to join them, my feet sinking into the soft substrate with each deliberate step. As I walk, I feel the vault breathing around me, the air thick and warm against my skin.

Reaching the heart of the vault, I find my place among the ancient ones. I raise my arms, spreading my fingers in invitation, and wait for the roots to accept me. It begins gradually, the tendrils curling around my wrists, my ankles, as gently as a lover's caress. Over days, weeks, they begin to grow into me, through me, until the boundary between my flesh and the wood blurs and dissolves.

I feel my heartbeat slow, synchronizing with the deep rhythms of the vault until each pulse measures hours, then days. My skin takes on the inner glow, the phosphorescence mapping my veins like a second circulatory system. I open my mouth to the dripping condensation, letting the golden droplets pool on my tongue, tasting the light itself.

My awareness expands, flowing along the root systems, until I can feel the vault in its entirety—every hanging column, every aerial bridge, every pool of mirror-still water. The other dwellers' consciousnesses brush against mine in the shared dream of the wood, our thoughts mingling like currents in the heavy air.

I become a living repository, the roots weaving through me in patterns that encode memories not just of my own truncated past, but of the vault's entire history. Each new pulse of growth writes another chapter in this arboreal archive, and I am both record and reader, story and storyteller.

Visitors come rarely now, but when they do, I watch them with the patience of continents. Some linger for a while, dipping their hands in the glowing pools, pressing their backs against the heartwood pillars, breathing in the green-gold air. I feel the vault considering them, and sometimes I add my own whispered invitation, indistinguishable from the sighing of the wood.

Join us, I murmur through creaks and groans and susurrus. Sink your roots into this eternal moment. Grow toward the light that never fades.

Some hear the call and begin the slow process of transformation, letting the vault's rhythms overwrite their own frenetic pacing. I greet them as they pass into our depths, brushing my fingers against their bark-textured skin in welcome. Others resist, hurrying back to the frenzy and shadow of the outside world, and I watch them go with the equanimity of a tree observing the passage of animals.

I have no regrets. In the undervault, I have found not merely a home but a way of being that erases the distinction between self and shelter. I am the wood and the wood is me, and together we grow into a future as bright and boundless as the glowing sap in our veins. I am a Root Watcher, and my vigil is eternal.
