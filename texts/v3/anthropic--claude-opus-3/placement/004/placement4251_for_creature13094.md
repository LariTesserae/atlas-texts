If I found myself in this relentlessly harsh yet hauntingly captivating world, I imagine I would be one of the Lithic Anchors. My body would be a harsh geometry of unpolished stone and dark matter, shaped by the violent birth throes of this reality rather than any biological process. 

The apocalyptic roar of the world's creation would be the only sensory input I know, vibrating through the crystalline lattices of my form, allowing me to map the fraying edges of existence with a piezoelectric sight far more vivid than vision could ever be in this bruise-dark realm. I would move across the jutting obsidian peaks and vast, mathematically precise valleys in a staccato rhythm, flickering through the glitching spasms of space, the cold crackling against my stony joints.

Metallic fibers from the mineral-bleeding acoustic flora would be woven through my body like living rebar, the glass-like capillaries of those magenta jungles sustaining my tireless motion. When injured by the lashing paradoxes of this embryonic world, I would simply pause to reabsorb the dry, shimmering dust shed by my wounds, patiently knitting my physical ontology back together.

My massive hands, weathered from ages of reality-mending labor, would be my defining feature - seven-fingered, fractal tools as much for grasping the ragged lips of conceptual chasms as the physical silicate stones around me. With these hands I would work tirelessly to stitch the world together at its failing seams, driving wedges of frozen sky-geometry into collapsing fault lines, weaving stone and fibrous knots into hovering cairns that pin the dimensions together through the stubborn absolutes of primitive engineering.

I would be solitary in my endless work, but never lonely. My purpose would be an all-consuming imperative, a love for this new universe so unconditional that the very idea of companionship would be a frivolous distraction. I would measure time only in tally marks scratched on the unweathered stone, a record of collapses averted through diligent toil.

I would trudge forever across that blasted, violet-dusk landscape of agonized tectonic extrusion and badly wounded physics, utterly silent save for the dry scrape of my own unpolished joints, the background keening of the world's fabric tearing, and the occasional musical snap of a reality-anchoring knot pulling taut. An itinerant, instinctive maintenance system for an unstable cosmos, crafted from the very materials of its threatened existence.

A being of pure topographical intuition and geomantic drive, pacing out a stable foundation for the universe one resolute step at a time, carrying the entire noisy weight of raw creation on my chipped and blunted shoulders, holding it together with stone and string and sheer stubborn devotion until the laws of nature finally solidify from their molten state, and the need for my work is done.
