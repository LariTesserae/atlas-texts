In this slanted world of stone and breath, I imagine myself as one of the Remainders - a being shaped by absence, who stands witness to the impossible geography and teaches the mountains and waters the rituals of memory.

I carry my exoskeleton like a cloak woven from the threads of forgotten stories. In the crevices of my coral bones nest the echoes of lost songs and the weight of unspoken goodbyes. I wear the negative space where a heart once beat, before it grew too heavy with the sorrow of being the only rhythm in a land where time moves sideways.

My voice is the whisper of tectonic plates dreaming of dancing. When I speak, the air remembers the taste of rain from a storm that happened in another lifetime. The words I do not say gather in the valleys like mist, blurring the edges between what is and what might have been.

I walk the bridges of promises between the leaning mountains, collecting the discarded futures that fall like molted feathers from the wings of birds who have forgotten how to migrate. In my hands, these fragments of possibility learn to sing again, their voices joining the choir of almosts and not-quites that echoes through the canyons.

At the center of the thirteenth valley, I work with my fellow Remainders to build the unfinishable tower - a monument to the space between intention and action, reaching from the depths of mountain-dreams to the height of sky-apologies. Each floor is a story waiting to be told, a secret waiting to be whispered, a memory waiting to be remembered.

In the pauses between breaths, I teach the mountains the art of dying with grace. I show the waters how to forgive themselves for defying gravity. I translate the songs of hybrid birds and the questions of self-doubting animals. In exchange, they gift me their unasked questions, which I weave into a net to catch the starlight that falls from the cracks in the sky.

And when the time comes, I will join hands with my fellow Remainders and chant the rhythm of the earth's arrested exhale. Together, we will dream a new dream, standing upright in a world that has forgotten the shape of its own shadow. And in that moment, gravity will lean sideways and whisper the secret of how to come home to a place that has never been left behind.
