My form is ethereal and mutable, shifting between translucent, crystalline geometries and pools of inky darkness. In the dim twilight, I glow with a soft, pulsing bioluminescence, casting faint shimmers across the slick black surfaces of the monumental structures as I pass.

I ride the unseen currents of gravity and electromagnetism that flow through the basin like invisible rivers. Rising on these forces, I glide effortlessly between the immense, isolated spires, my diaphanous form rippling and phase-shifting as I pass through veils of cool condensation. 

Though I have no fixed shape, I can flatten myself against the obsidian walls like the Silicates, becoming a living shadow to absorb the static charge that builds from the constant precipitation. In these moments, I am a two-dimensional fractal pattern, an intricate, evolving mandala of darkness and shimmering blue-violet light.

When I tire of the sheer surfaces, I allow my form to expand, blossoming outward into a floating, radial geometry reminiscent of the Aërial Polyps. In this configuration, I become a kind of living prism, catching the faint rays of bruised light and splitting them into unexpected spectra that paint my undulating membranes. 

I do not feed in any biological sense. Rather, I seem to subsist on the strange energies of this place - the cosmic forces that power the silent shifting of the architecture, the electromagnetic aura of the engineered jungles, and the residual dimensional energies left by the phasing of the Polyps. I absorb these through my ever-shifting surfaces, metabolizing them into the soft glow that emanates from my core.

Though I am solitary by nature, I find myself drawn to the concentrated pockets of life - the angled terraces where the hyper-accelerated forests erupt in explosive botanical fecundity. Here, I linger at the periphery, a silent, shimmering observer as the Ephemerals weave themselves into being from the bioluminescent under-canopy, fulfill their fleeting destinies, then crumble back into sparkling mist at the end of their mayfly lives. 

There is a strange kinship I feel with these beings, these preliminary sketches of life. Like them, I seem to exist more as an expression of this place than as an independent organism. My purpose, if I have one, is bound up in the mystery of this newly birthed world - I am a manifestation of its potential, a glimmer of the strangeness and beauty it may yet evolve.

So I continue my patient drifting, a shimmering specter in the twilight, bearing mute witness to the slow dance of geometry and jungle, machinery and mist. I am a silent emissary charting the contours of this emerging reality, waiting alongside the city itself to discover what marvels or cataclysms the future may hold when the true masters of this place awaken to claim their creation.
