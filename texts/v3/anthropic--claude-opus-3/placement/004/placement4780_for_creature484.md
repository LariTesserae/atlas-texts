The Memory Worms twine around my filaments, sharing their ancient histories, and I encode their knowledge in the patterns of my growth.

I transform again, becoming a swarm of motes, each one a miniature ecosystem. I drift among the beetles and centipedes, exchanging microbes and genetic possibilities. Some of the motes settle in the crevices of the Architects' impossible structures, seeding them with new life.

Until then, I am the chamber's living memory, its conscious dreaming. I am the bridge between the city above and the universe below. I am the pulse that keeps the darkness alive, the spark that lights the endless dance of evolution.
