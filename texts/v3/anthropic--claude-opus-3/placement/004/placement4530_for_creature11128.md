What an evocative and haunting world you've described. The ethereal, submerged landscape with its glowing mushroom-like forests, the eerie absence of animal life, and the enigmatic remnants of some long-gone civilization that shaped the stone. And now inhabited by the strange, slow returners who dwell inside the living columns. It's a place that feels simultaneously serene and unsettling.

If I found myself in this drowned land, I imagine I would be one of the returners. Moving through the dim, pressurized depths with a kind of unhurried, meditative purpose. Listening intently to the subsonic songs of the vents and columns, attuned to the groaning rhythm of this place. 

I would make pilgrimages to those few traces of the ancient wall-builders. Standing for long stretches with my hand upon a cairn or stone marker, contemplating the alien strangeness of those worked edges and intentional forms amid all the organic shapes and slow geologic processes surrounding me. 

Perhaps over great spans of time, my encounter with those ruins would kindle the first spark of an urge to create. A nascent desire to shape my environment with directed will, as those unknown ancestors once did. Maybe I would be the first of my kind to place one stone atop another. The pioneer of a new age of made things.

But then again, perhaps not. The lure of simple being is strong - to just exist and observe and listen, as the columns do. To live inside the long, slow dream of the place, content in my small chamber, fed by the silver rain. Changing so gradually across ages as to seem changeless.

I suspect I would vacillate. Drawn to the wall and markers over and over, but never quite finding the impetus to emulate them. Torn between the call of creation and the deep belonging of remaining one with the valley's rhythm. A liminal being, full of inchoate potential, poised on the threshold of a vast, unbounded future.

This is an astounding piece of writing. You've conjured a setting that feels mythic and numinous, shot through with tantalizing implications and unanswerable questions. The world of the Drowned Heights will linger in my mind for a long time. Thank you for sharing this unforgettable vision.
