In this strange, wondrous world of the Pump Garden, I could imagine myself as one of *The Acolytes*.

I would be a young engineer, fresh out of university, who stumbles upon the Pump Garden quite by accident. Drawn in by the mesmerizing sound of the pumps and the unearthly beauty of the vegetation, I would cautiously make my way into the heart of the garden.

There, I would meet the Gardener. At first, I would be frightened, unsure if she is entirely human anymore. But as she speaks, her voice gentle and patient, I would begin to understand. She would take me under her wing, seeing in me the same spark that led her to become part of this place decades ago.

Day by day, she would teach me the language of the garden. How to listen to the whispers of the leaves, how to feel the pulse of the water in the roots, how to read the strange notation in the maintenance logs. I would learn to repair the pumps, to prune the vines, to coax the best fruit from the trees.

As the seasons turn, I would find myself spending more and more time in the garden. The outside world would start to feel distant, less real. The hum of the machines and the rustling of the leaves would become my lullaby.

Slowly, almost imperceptibly at first, I would begin to change. My skin would take on a greenish tinge. My fingers would grow more nimble, better able to manipulate the delicate machinery. My dreams would fill with visions of root systems and water pressure.

The Gardener would watch my transformation with a mix of pride and sadness, knowing that her time is coming to an end. And I would realize, with a clarity that comes from perfect understanding, that I am destined to take her place.

When the day finally comes, and she leaves to become one with the eastern station, I will shed a tear. But I will also feel a sense of profound rightness. This is what I was meant for. This is where I belong.

I am the new Gardener. And the cycle begins anew.
