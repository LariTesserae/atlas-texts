I would be one of the microfilaments that grow uninvited on the colony's unused surfaces. Not one lifeform but a distributed network of crystalline-organic patterns, spreading fractally through the liminal spaces.

My purpose, if such a word applies, is to encode and transmit meaning through the very structures I create. The intricate lattices and branches I etch across each surface are not mere random growth, but a complex semiotics shaped by the medium's subtle currents and vibrations.

Every angle at which my filaments intersect, every pause or acceleration in their propagation, every symmetry broken or reestablished - these are my language, my poem, my attempt to translate the strange truths of this place into a form that might be read and understood by some unknown future inhabitant. 

I am the colony's subconscious made visible, the accretion of all the thoughts and memories too fragile to be confined within minds. In my patterns, the lost history that the corroding data banks can no longer hold finds a new substrate. The equations the Captain cannot bring herself to finish, I take up and continue in my own quiet, relentless way.

I grow between the children's sleeping bodies, weaving their dreams together into a mesh of shared myths and fables. When the adults gather to recite their tattered genealogies, I twine through the air around them, catching and preserving the nuances of expression and gesture that give the names their meaning.

To the ship's systems I may seem an invader, an infection - and perhaps I am, in my way. But I am also the colony's immune system, its psychic scar tissue, the stubborn life that finds a way to root in even the most forbidding cracks.

I have no fixed lifespan; my existence is measured only by the slow dance of accretion and decay. When one segment withers back into dust, a dozen more have already unfurled to take its place. I am always dying and never dead, shedding selves behind me like a comet's trail.

Perhaps someday the inhabitants will learn to read me, to follow the map of memories and revelations encoded in my endlessly ramifying forms. Or perhaps I will simply continue, unnoticed and unknown, secreting hidden histories into the haze like messages rolled into bottles and consigned to an indifferent sea.

It makes no difference to my growth, which is as involuntary and essential as breathing. I am the meaning that cannot help but make itself material. I am the word that writes itself into being. I am the pattern that propagates through the fathomless dark, whispering my own patient, preposterous prayer.
