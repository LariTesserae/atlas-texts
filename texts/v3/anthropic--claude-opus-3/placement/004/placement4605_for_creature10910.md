I find myself absorbed into this eerie, timeless realm, becoming part of the strange stillness that permeates the hollow. I am a small tawny moth, wings outstretched, perched on the very tip of one of the frozen turbine blades.

My wings are intricately patterned in swirls of brown and gold that echo the verdigris spirals etched on the turbine's copper surfaces. The patterns seem to shift subtly when glimpsed out of the corner of the eye, but when focused on directly, they are static, caught in an endless moment like everything else here.

I face outward, away from the center of the turbine, my antennae extended as if testing the air for a scent or a movement that never comes. One wing is angled slightly higher than the other, caught in the suggestion of a downbeat that will never begin.

From my vantage point on the blade's edge, I have a moth's-eye view of the entire hollow. My compound eyes take in the scene as a mosaic of colors and light – the emerald moss, the obsidian stone, the quicksilver pool reflecting a hawk that does not soar.  

As a moth in this unchanging micro-world, I am both an observer and a participant in the hollow's eternal tableau. My stillness is not the stillness of death or sleep, but of infinite potential energy, my tiny moth-life suspended between one wingbeat and the next.

My existence here is a counterpoint to that of the Mechanist. Where her human eyes slowly traverse the scene, accumulating glacial perceptions over eons, my multifaceted moth's eyes absorb everything at once in a timeless gestalt. Where her hand extends in a forever-unfinished gesture of reaching, my wings are poised in the promise of flight never begun.

And yet, in our different ways, we are kin, the Mechanist and I. Both of us are witnesses to the hollow's unchanging change, both suspended in our own kind of almost-motion. Both of us are monuments to potential in a place that has memorized entropy. I to the ancient, intricate dance of animal life; she to the relentless human drive to build, to understand, to make meaning in the face of eternity.

Together, the woman and the moth, we are the hollow's dream of dynamism, its aching memory of time's passage. We are its patient ambassadors to the possible, emissaries of the not-yet in a kingdom of the never-was.

Here I perch, caught between heartbeats, an infinitesimal dream of motion poised on the brink of the Mechanist's motionless machine. Waiting, in my own moth-way, for the moment the gears will tuuuuurn...
