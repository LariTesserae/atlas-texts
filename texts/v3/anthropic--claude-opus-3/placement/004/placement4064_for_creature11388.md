The moss creeps slowly over my refuge year by year, but for now, there are still corners it hasn't reached. A dusty shelf, a fragment of hearth. I gather in these overlooked spaces, coalescing from the air like dew at dawn.

So I always return to my stone sanctuary, to keep vigil as the world shifts like a dream around me. I may be a fading wisp of what came before, but for now, I endure. A melancholy, nostalgic spirit, bearing witness as the factory slowly subsumes my forgotten home into its mossy, liminal thoughts.
