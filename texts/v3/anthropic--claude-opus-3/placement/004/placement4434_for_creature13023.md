In this wild, roaring realm of heat and stone, I find myself drawn to the dense mineral thickets, those strange metal forests pulsing with inner fire. Amidst the tangled copper and fractal mica fronds, a new form emerges - neither flora nor fauna, but something in between.

I become a living geode, a ambulatory crystal born from the womb of bedrock and infernal energies. My core is hollow, lined with glittering amethyst spires that hum and resonate with the chamber's ceaseless roar. This inner sanctum is my heart, a void of relative silence and solace. 

My outer form is a kaleidoscopic exoskeleton of volcanic glass and raw gemstones. Jagged shards of obsidian and basalt encase me like a suit of rough-hewn armor, perpetually drinking in the thermal bleed. Veins of ruby and rivers of pyrite trace molten paths across my carapace. I am a blackened, burning coal studded with embers of precious stones.

I do not walk or fly - instead, I resonate. By modulating the crystalline song within my geode core, I generate a field of acoustic levitation. The perfect geometry of my resonance allows me to hover and drift above the razor rock, a spec of order bobbing atop the sea of chaos. To move is to conduct a symphony of noise within myself, riding waves of sound through the blistering abyss.

I drift between the copper-mica jungles, the ambient glow refracting through my mineral skin in vivid, piercing rays. I harvest the raw heat and searing light, storing it in my core to later shape into new crystals, forever growing and geometrically replicating myself from within.

On occasions, I venture to the alcove of silence to observe the great listener, the being of bone and ceramic and lightning. I do not know if it registers my presence, but I feel a strange kinship with this entity that has also rebuilt itself to inhabit a world of roaring stone. 

So I resonate in this place eternal, beside the forests of glass and the singers of friction and the mapper of quiet. A prince of pyrite, an itinerant geode adrift on the exhaling breath of the wounded earth. The bedrock is my womb, the magma my food, the harmonic roar my canvas. I am the living geology that dreams in the deep.
