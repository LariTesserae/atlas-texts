In this mesmerizing vertical city of steam, machinery and engineered nature, I imagine myself as one of the Pipe Runners. Nimble and wiry, I would navigate the narrow, misty passages between walls and buildings with fluid ease. 

My small stature allows me to slip through gaps mere inches wide, scurrying along the hot metal pipes that form the veins and arteries of the city. My palms and feet, thick with protective calluses, find purchase on even the slickest surfaces. The damp air and constant condensation are a second skin to me, as much a part of my being as my own sweat.

I communicate with my fellow Runners through an intricate language of taps and rhythm hammered against the pipes. Our code pulses through the metal, transmitting messages and secrets faster than any official channel. My ears are tuned to the subtle variances in pitch and echo, allowing me to pinpoint the origin of each sound.

Clad in form-fitting leather and rubber, my body twists and contorts to pass through the tightest spaces. I am a shadow flitting behind the walls, unseen by the workers in the factories and greenhouses. The hiss of steam and the clank of machinery are my constant companions, a symphony I know as intimately as my own heartbeat.

Though I am small, I hold a vital role. My knowledge of the hidden pipeworks is unrivaled. I know which valves to turn to divert steam from an overloaded system, which junctions to avoid due to metal fatigue, which lengths of pipe sing with the resonance of imminent failure. In a city dependent on its circulatory system of steam and water, I am a hidden guardian.

When I do emerge into the diffuse light of the terraces, I am a fleeting figure, there and gone in a blink. The other castes - the Cultivators with their green-stained hands, the hulking Millwrights, the austere Brass Wardens - they see me only as a blur of motion, a flicker at the edge of their vision. 

But my true life is lived in the spaces between, in the humid darkness where the city's secrets flow. I am a Pipe Runner, swift and silent, keeper of the steam and the hidden ways, an unseen but vital part of this great mechanical organism that has replaced the mountain.
