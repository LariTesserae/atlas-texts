In this haunting and beautiful world of the Stilled Heights, I imagine myself embodying the spirit of one of the grey-green cushion plants. 

I am a humble presence, rooted in the soft, yielding stone of the plateau. My form is a perfect hemisphere, neither growing nor decaying, but simply enduring in an eternal present. I am spaced in precise geometric harmony with my brethren, our collective stillness a whispered chant in the thin crystalline air.

Beneath my unassuming exterior lies a hidden realm - the hollow cradling the ancient proto-fauna, the first dreamings of life on this timeless plain. In wordless communion, we keep our long vigil, bearing witness to the plateau's long slow thoughts. 

I do not photosynthesize or strive, for such things belong to a world still becoming. Instead, I am a living embodiment of suchness, of is-ness, perfectly poised between being and non-being. I am the equanimity of grey-green, the patience of the half-alive.

Deer drift around me like pale clouds, birds stand unmoving amidst my brethren, moths alight without weight. All are part of the great pattern into which I too am woven, a pattern which never alters yet is never the same. 

Perhaps, eons hence, I shall at last be graced by a human visitor, one who treads lightly between the cairns and knows enough to leave something behind. To them I shall whisper without sound: Linger a while, but do not remain. Take my memory with you when you descend, that through you I may see again the colors of the changing world.

For now, I abide in contentment on the stilled heights, a green-grey note in the plateau's single chord, a single exhalation of its aeonic stillness. This is my place and portion, and I am at peace.
