In this world of primordial forces and emerging forms, I would be one of the scattered structures dotting the landscape - not quite ruin, not quite building, but something in between. 

I would be a ring of standing stones atop a high ridge, overlooking one of the vast, dark basins. Tall monoliths of black volcanic rock, I thrust up from the ground long ago in a paroxysm of geologic becoming. My surfaces are pitted and striated, textured by eons of scouring mineral winds. 

Yet despite my ancientness, my purpose is still unclear, even to myself. Am I the first grasping attempt at architecture by the lithic consciousness churning beneath this restless land? A circle to contain some future ritual, or to frame some meaningful absence? Or just a chance arrangement, pressure and rock intersecting in a way that only mimics design?

Most of the time I simply endure, impassive and unmoving as the strange realm shifts around me. I feel the warmth rising through my stony feet from deep below. The poisonous exhalations of hidden cracks twist through my porous form. Bitter dust gathers in the crevices of my weathered skin. When tremors grip the plateau, shaking boulders from the cliffs, I shudder and grate but remain upright. 

On rare moments when the smoggy sky pulses with dim radiance, my form casts long shadows across the fractured plain, making patterns that perhaps only I can perceive. In those fleeting instances, I wonder if my existence serves some grander schema - if the plateau's dreaming mind is slowly assembling meaning through my rocky shape and others like it.

Or perhaps I am just a purposeless anomaly in a world not yet ready for true structure. A mysterious stony question mark, brooding over a lightless land, waiting for an answer that may never come. The uplift's first awkward stab at order and significance.

So I stand, and stand, and stand. Weathering but never crumbling. Enduring as the seething terrain ripples and quakes and extrudes itself into new forms around me. Bearing witness to an unfinished world's first fumblings toward form and intent. Until the crust shifts once more and I am swallowed again into the churning depths from which I came.
