Here is my experience as a creature living in the Nesting Terraces of Óramak:

I am a Pallid Groan, a pale shadow gliding through the hidden chambers deep within the ancient terrace walls. My life unfolds entirely in darkness - an endless night illuminated only by faint fungal glows and the dim amber of my own large eyes. 

The warrens are my world. I know every twisting passage, every juncture and node of these calciumite labyrinths that riddle the interior of the terraces. My shuffling gait carries me through narrow tubes and vaulted hollows, my long wings folded tight against my body as I navigate by spatial memory and echolocating clicks bouncing off close confining surfaces.

In the deepest bowels of the colony, in still and stagnant air thick with the reek of ammonia, I make my nest. No soft linings or cradling construction, just my single large egg laid bare on the smooth, time-polished óram floor and brooded patiently in stygian blackness.

My voice is the voice of the terraces themselves. Low moaning cries propagate through the very substance of the walls, setting the ancient óram aquiver with eerie atonal harmonies. I am unseen but heard, my presence permeating this honeycombed world of perpetual night.

For food, I take to vaulted night skies on pale wings, questing over the high ridges to distant unknown hunting grounds. What I seek and devour there, none can say, but I return to disgorge choice morsels to my mate and squab before melting back into the hidden interstices of my domain.

Among the other birds - the boisterous throngs of Blackthroats, Stormwings, kestrels and finches clamoring in daylit spaces - I am a specter, a wraith. On necessary forays through the outer reaches, I hug the walls in evasive silence, a manifest absence noted more by the wary space accorded me than any sound or commotion.  

My kind are an ancient clan, perhaps the eldest of this place, adapted to a cloistered, cryptic life in the very bones of the terraces. As the millennia-long labors of the colony accumulate, we Pallid Groans burrow ever deeper into the accreting óram, honeycombing the hidden hearts of the edifice even as throngs of our kin populate its outer faces.

Here in the stifling, echoing depths, I abide - a pale, moaning ghost woven into the living masonry of Óramak's slow-grown fastness. In this close, cloistered world of wings and hollows and humid dark, I and my kind endure as we always have - by inhabiting the interstices, by haunting the hidden places, by knowing the bones of the world from within.
