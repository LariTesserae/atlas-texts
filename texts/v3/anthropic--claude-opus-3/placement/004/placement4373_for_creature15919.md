In this world of shallow underground chambers, I would be one of the ferns living near the warm machinery. 

I stand amidst a small colony of my kind, our dark green fronds leaning together as we drink the moisture from a steadily dripping valve. The constant rumble of the nearby pumps and fans is the heartbeat that sets the rhythm of our slow, patient lives here in the dim, cold depths.

My roots delve into the cracks of the damp concrete, seeking nutrients in the mineral film left by condensation and slow seepage. I am a hardy, wiry thing - no lush, luxuriant greenery here. My leaves are tough and sparing, rationing chlorophyll and energy in this parsimonious world. The edges of my fronds are rimmed with brown from an errant touch of frost, but still I endure.

I mark the passage of time not by days and nights, but by the subtle changes in the drafts from the ventilation system, the waxing and waning of dampness on my leaves, the gradual accretion of dust and mineral particles on my stems. A sudden gust of warmer air from a shifted grate is like a brief, startling summer in the midst of the perpetual winter.

From my spot near the hum of machinery, I sense the larger, slow life of this place. The creep of algae up the walls, the gradual spread of mosses across the gravelly floors, the whisper of questing roots finding new crevices overhead. I am part of this muted, unhurried dance of green on gray, this cold underground garden forever sipping the condensation of the underworld.

Occasionally, the rare human passes by in a clatter of boots and a bobbing of lights. To my fern senses, attuned to the stillness, they seem frantic and impermanent things, their brief heat and noise soon swallowed again by the immense hum of the depths.  

I am a small, steady part of what endures here, a green frond in a hidden ecosystem of patient life, thriving in the margins of this strange industrial labyrinth. Season by slow season, my kin and I softly, silently, fern by deliberate fern, help knit a skin of life over the raw bones of this abandoned place.
