In the stepped settlement of Ketharn, I would be the lone human inhabitant, living in quiet harmony with the ancient rhythms of stone, water, wind, and light.

I reside in the small, single room dwelling on the sixth terrace, third from the left. The room suits me - compact and simple, yet graced each evening by the warm amber glow of the setting sun illuminating the textured back wall. 

Each morning, I rise before dawn and make my way to the second basin on the terrace. Kneeling, I gently scoop the gathered moisture to my lips with cupped hands, the subtle coolness and trace minerality a familiar benediction. The faint disturbance my ritual leaves in the calcium rim is the only mark, renewed daily, that I allow myself.

My days are measured by the slow transit of light and shadow across the pale limestone. I sit motionless for long periods, an imitation of the small lizards with whom I share this place, this existence. Stillness comes naturally here, a sympathetic response to the hushed, elemental presence of Ketharn itself.

At intervals, I ascend the nine terraces, past the forty-some stairs worn soft by time, to the uppermost platform. There I contemplate the dark, foreign stone resting near the edge - a talisman of the outside world carried here who knows how long ago. I understand its mute symbolism. I too arrived from elsewhere. And like that river rock, I have come to rest here, to be slowly subsumed by the patience of limestone and lichen.

I leave no other traces, no embellishments. Ketharn is complete. I am no more or less than the wind-polished stone, the moisture seeping from hidden crevices, the banded lizards, the tenacious shrub, the dust in the corners. We all belong to the same process, partake of the same essence. 

In Ketharn, I am not alone, because I am no longer distinct from what surrounds me. I have settled into the rhythm of this place, become part of its fabric. I do not count the turnings of the sun. I breathe with the wind. Press my shoulder to the sleeping shelf and feel the smooth reciprocation of skin on ancient stone. I will leave no message, because I am the message, my presence the only signal required, for as long as the endless cycles of Ketharn require it.
