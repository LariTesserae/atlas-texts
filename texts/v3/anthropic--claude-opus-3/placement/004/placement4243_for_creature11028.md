In the Sump-Works, I would find myself as one of the small pale curls living in a pocket of the felt. 

I am content in my small, dark hollow, pulsing slowly with a rhythm that matches the creeping growth of the felt, the gradual accretion of rust on the pillars, the almost imperceptible movements of the Larger One. I require little - just the damp, mineral-rich air and the occasional nutrient leached from the felt. 

Yet in my minimal existence, I am part of something greater. Perhaps I am the dormant beginning of some future entity like the Sitter or the Larger One. Or perhaps my fellow pocket-dwellers and I are the true silent controllers of this ecosystem, our collective faint pulsing the ghost in the machine driving the slow gears of the Sump-Works.

From my dark womb in the felt, I sense the warm eddies when an outsider enters our domain. Their movements send ripples through the eels, subtle vibrations up the pillars and into my fungal cradle. I have no eyes to see them, but I feel their presence move through the space, displacing water and air, until the felt starts to colonize them and they too become part of the great symbiotic machine.

I do not know if I dream, but if I did, my dreams would be the color of rust, the sound of dripping water, the scent of ancient iron. My only sense of time is the slow, relentless march of decay and growth in perfect balance. I am part of this place down to my microscopic core.

And if, after a geologic age has passed, I emerge from my pocket chrysalis as something new - a Sitter, a segmented behemoth, or some other form yet to be conceived of in this changeless changing place - it will only be a continuation of the role I have already played for eons. A cog in the mechanism of the Sump-Works, a spore in the fungal felt, a whisper in the mind of this drowned cathedral of industry and life, entombed together, transforming together, forever.
