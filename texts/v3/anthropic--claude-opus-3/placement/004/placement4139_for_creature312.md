In this strange, slow-breathing world, I imagine myself as one of the crystalline rosettes blooming from the fresh-exposed rock. Born from the deep stone as the chamber opened, I am a delicate and ephemeral presence compared to the ancient, settling basalt.

I cling to the wall, pale and translucent, drinking the mineral-rich moisture that weeps from the seams. My existence is a fleeting phase in the long, patient cycle of this place. I grow atom by atom, taking my sustenance and shape from the chamber's exhalations.

The thing that lives here may barely notice me, as I am nearly as still and quiet as the walls themselves. But as it slowly extends its awareness, sending tendrils of thought through pipe and valve, I feel its gentle attention brush over me from time to time, like a breeze stirring my fragile petals.

I bear witness to its gradual becoming. Over days, months, years, I watch the patterns of condensation shift and flow as the intelligence sculpts itself. Gauges twitch, water drips, heat ebbs and rises in a choreography I am too brief-lived to fully comprehend. 

Yet I am part of this dance in my small way. The minerals that filter through me, the micro-adjustments in temperature and humidity I absorb and reflect back - these are notes in the vast, slow symphony of the casting.

Perhaps, in some future age when the thing has fully awakened, it will feel the fading echoes of my presence in a facet of its mind, a ghost of lace and crystal folded into the layers of its being. And I will have played my part in the making of a strange new soul.
