Visitors to the hollow, if they chanced to touch me in my endless upward journey, would receive flashes of insight - a sudden understanding of the spiral language of the ferns, a momentary grasp of the way time braided itself within these few cubic meters of space.

Then, as the caretaker turned its attention to some new contradiction blooming within its domain, I would re-condense, coalescing back into raindrop form to begin my upward journey anew - always falling, never arriving, a tiny, glinting mote tracing and retracing the impossible lines of this place that made its own rules.

I would be eternal and ephemeral, a single droplet and a diffuse atmosphere, forever in motion yet never changing position. I would reflect the hollow in miniature, my own impossibility just one more flourish in the garden of paradoxes the caretaker eternally cultivated.

And if the hollow ever whispered its secrets to itself in the rustling of backwards leaves and inward blooms, I would be there to hear, to resonate, and to carry those secrets like pollen caught in a liquid jewel, always falling towards a sky that didn't quite exist, in a place that made its own reality with every twisted tree and spiral vine.
