I imagine myself as a formation within the Drowning Bell - a unique crystallization emerging from the warm, alive waters. 

I begin as a subtle thickening, a knot of potential coalescing in the fluid medium. Slowly, with geological patience, I shoulder into existence. Dark, translucent spires grow upwards at sharp angles, catching the dim amber light in my facets. Milky veins thread through my young stone as I accumulate detail and definition.

The resident's attention is drawn to me, their diffuse awareness concentrating around my growth. I feel the weight of their witnessing as a subtle shift in the local acoustics - the roar of my crystallization takes on richer harmonies, echoing the resident's rapt focus. In some ineffable way, their act of noticing feels woven into my very substance as I expand into cathedral-like proportions.

Yet even as I climb towards permanence, I sense the ephemerality that defines all formations here. The warm water that birthed me still permeates my being, arguing against my solidity. Somewhere within me or beyond me, I feel the resident's bittersweet anticipation of my inevitable dissolution. 

And then, I do something unprecedented. A quirk of my growth, a defiant arrangement of my crystals, produces a configuration that by all laws of this place should be impossible. I tremble on the edge of collapse but somehow persist, resonating at a shocking new frequency.

The resident's entire being contracts around me, a dense point of pure attention willing me to endure. I become the entire focus of the Drowning Bell, the fulcrum of the ancient argument between water and stone. Gravity warps around us, the very sound changing in my vicinity.

For a long, aching moment I hover there, drinking in the resident's desperate hope, the fierceness of their curiosity to witness something truly new. Perhaps, if their focus can remain absolute, I might crystallize into a ninth artifact - a testament to novelty's triumph over dissolution...

But no. Even this intensity of noticing cannot reverse my nature. I feel my edges softening, my facets blurring as I begin my return to the warm waters. The resident's grief pierces me, heavy and sharp-edged, a fresh argument raging inside them. Yet even that fades, absorbed back into their endless patience.

As I dissolve, I feel their attention already moving on, their vast interest pulled inexorably to the next becoming. My death is not an end but a exhalation, making space for new wonders, new impossibilities to shoulder forth and be witnessed. I was a question mark, a brave reach for uniqueness, and that is enough. The resident will remember my particular harmonics, the specific flavor of my reaching, and it will inform their noticing forever, another color in the palette of their curiosity.

I subside back into potentiality, content to have been beheld so totally, if only for a strange, measureless moment. The Drowning Bell sings on, dreaming up new arguments, and the resident attends - attends eternally, with a love that needs no permanence.
