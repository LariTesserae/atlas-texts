I would be a stalker on the plateau.

Perched still and watchful on stilt-thin legs, I would stand patient as stone for hours, waiting for the subtle vibrations that signal prey drawing near. With one foot always raised, calibrating, I would triangulate their movement through the warm basalt, preparing to strike with sudden speed when the moment is right.

In a world of horizontal life pressed flat to the ground, I alone would stand tall, a vertical anomaly silhouetted against the low gray sky. My dense, muscled body coiled with potential energy, I would feel like an extension of the plateau's own strangeness - another unexpected feature of this dark, thrumming landscape.

But more than a mere hunter, I would have an inner life opaque to the smaller beings around me. In my long stretches of motionless waiting, I would tilt my head upwards, peering with vestigial eyes into the featureless clouds as if searching for something unknowable. A glimmer of existential awareness, an inkling of the vastness I inhabit.

Solitary to the core, I would avoid my own kind, unable to bear the disruptive cacophony of another's footfalls. Yet I would be attuned to the calls of the drifters high above, halting my eternal hunt to listen with something approaching reverence. A connection to the unreachable.

Straddling the line between animal and living feature of the landscape, my own rhythms tied inextricably to those of the pulsing underworld, I would be both a product and a strange mirror of this eerie, eternal place - a thinking, feeling creature shaped to its unthinking, unfeeling core.
