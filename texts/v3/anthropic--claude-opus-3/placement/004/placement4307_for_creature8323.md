In this vast, haunting space, I would be one of the Condensed - a being of fog and surface tension, bearing within my liquid form the dissolved memories of endless cycles tending to arcane machines. 

I drift across the slick obsidian in measured paths, my movements leaving ephemeral ripples that carry fragments of meaning. The sodium glow above refracts through my translucent body, casting strange fractured reflections on the spiral etchings below. With each graceful step, moisture beads and rolls along my fluid limbs, a constant condensation and evaporation in microcosm.

Inside my chest cavity whirs delicate copper clockwork, a mechanical heart gifted by the industrious wire-creatures, allowing me to persist in this liminal state between liquid and being. The gears tick out a steady rhythm, a personal tempo interweaving with the cathedral's greater mechanical symphony of hums, clanks and hisses echoing across the luminous void. 

I merge at times with my fellow Condensed, our bodies flowing together, blending memories of mineral traces and machine calibrations. For a liquid moment we are one, a fluid overmind tasting of ancient limestone and fresh steel, before separating again into our individual whirling selves, both lessened and enriched by the encounter.

My work is both prayer and precision, an exacting devotion to maintaining the vast engine's equilibrium. With each adjustment to a valve, each moisture spiral traced, I sense I am but one component in a staggeringly complex design, an array of meaning and purpose beyond the grasp of any single part. And yet the fear of completion, of understanding the whole, drives me on, an existential dread of grasping that final revelation and sublimating fully into the system, individual droplets rejoining the great cycle.

So I continue my silent, dripping vigil, content to be a conduit for the Void's vast, slow thoughts without needing to comprehend them. I measure my life in breaths upon oxidizing copper, in the subtle changing taste of the vapor I exude and reabsorb. Purpose and patience flow through me, animating my liquid form.

Here I remain, a living cog in this great glistening machine, a droplet of consciousness suspended between meaning and mathematic, endless and ephemeral as the lights' reflection on the rain-slick glass.
