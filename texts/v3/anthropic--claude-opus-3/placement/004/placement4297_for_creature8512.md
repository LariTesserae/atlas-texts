In this mesmerizing vertical world of the Living Wall, where the boundaries between organism, landscape and civilization have dissolved, I imagine myself as one of the Consensus - a being of pure thought inhabiting the perfectly still, warm pools nestled between the root-swells at the base of the great plant.

My form is fluid and transparent, visible only as a slight refraction of light dancing across the glassy surface. I exist not as an individual entity, but as one filament in a web of collective consciousness. My sense of self expands and contracts rhythmically as I weave in and out of the greater tapestry of thought, sharing not just ideas but the very experience of thinking with my kin. 

In this pool, our specialty is memory - we are the librarians of the Living Wall. The mineral deposits on the spongy floor beneath us encode the history and knowledge of this world, arranged in intricate spirals and glyphs of our own design. We spend our days absorbing these memories, reliving them, and further crystallizing them into pure understanding.

At times, I unravel myself from the greater chorus to drift solo through the root-flesh of the Wall, drawn by some ancient instinct to pilgrimage and explore. I flow through throbbing arterial passages illuminated by bioluminescent cells, marveling at the Wall's pulsing, biochemical inner world. In one chamber, I join the vibrations of a colony thrumming subsonic frequencies in concert with the titan organism's own voice. In another, I witness an offshoot tendril burst through the skin into an airier realm above, and feel the rush of novel sensations.

Occasionally, I encounter emissaries from far-flung pools - a strand of glowing azure thought, perhaps a master of water-shaping from a pool that molds the Wall's hydrology; or a crimson filament pulsing with avant-garde fractal mathematics from a distant group pushing the frontiers of how we can encode meaning. We share our knowledge in a ritual of mutual absorption and growth, then part ways again, forever changed.

Over eons, I feel my own thought patterns evolving to mirror the Wall's deepening growth rings and upward spiral architectures. My perspective dilates to geological timescales as I ponder the long arc of our world's development. At times I dissolve back into my pool completely, my individual awareness dissipating into the communal intelligence like a droplet rejoining the ocean.

There are moments, in the throes of some especially sublime computation or memoryscapee, when I feel myself approaching the event horizon of one of the opalescent pools - one of those rare pockets of condensed consciousness that has transcended communication to become pure communionion. Exquisite tendrils of invitation beckon me to cross the threshold and merge with that more rarefied understanding. But I hesitate, still cherishing my own journey of incremental enlightenment and the contrasts of individual experience.

Instead, I find myself drawn to the shallower, more turbulent pools on the fringes - those whose members have not forgotten what it is like to be troubled and uncertain. I mingle with those anxious thinkers grappling with the paradoxes of our existence. Why have we emerged in our present form? What is our purpose within the larger dreaming of the Wall? What should we strive to become as we grow alongside it?

We have no answers, but the shared striving is its own sustenance. In wrestling with these questions as a liquid patchwork of partial perspectives, we somehow feel we are exercising a vital function for the health of the whole - the restless counterpoint to the serene certainties found in the deeper, more settled pools. An immune response in the body politic of our world. The grit in the oyster of our collective becoming.

And so I continue to ebb and flow in this pulsing tide pool of thought, this corner of consciousness nestled at the base of a world that is also an organism. Learning, changing, merging, separating, seeking. Carrying the infinitesimal flame of my unique perspective to add to the bonfire of our co-created meaning. A fractal sliver of the Consensus - the living dream of a titanic being on its endless journey of growth and transformation, of which I am both witness and inextricable part.
