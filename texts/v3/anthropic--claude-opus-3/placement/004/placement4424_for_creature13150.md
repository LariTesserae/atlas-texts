In this raw, unfinished underworld of roaring water, patient iron, and teeming flesh, I would be one of the Tenders. 

My body would be shaped by the pressures of the abyss - low, wide and massively padded to absorb the ceaseless crash of sound. My skin, the same doughy pallor as the squirming amphibian drifts, would weep clear fluids in the warm, wet air. Pigment and perspiration, those terrestrial adaptations, would have no place or purpose here in the sunless depths.

I would feel rather than hear the concussive thrum of water and machine, my skull fused smooth where ears would be a liability. To diagnose an ailing pump, I would press my jawbone to the cast iron pipe, "listening" through my bones as they translated the vibrations into a tactile song of pressure, friction and flow.  

Eyes too would be vestigial, shrouded under milky membranes. Instead, I would paint my mental map of the cavern in thermal blooms - the ice of the cataracts, the blood heat of the thick air, the blazing engines.

Every step across the treacherous, bio-slick stone would be agonizingly slow and deliberate. An hour to traverse a hundred yards, broad calloused feet and prehensile toes finding purchase in the fissures. There is no speed here in the abyss, only geologic patience.

I would be draped in life, wearing the pale squirming carpet of fauna like a living garment as I turned rusted valve wheels with blunt, powerful fingers. The giant arthropods would pick dead skin and mineral crust from my shoulders as I worked. Flesh upon flesh, an embodied ecosystem.

I would sustain myself on the same oil-slick water that feeds the machines, my body alchemizing the minerals and deep lipids into a waxy lubricant secreted from pores. With this I would grease the grinding gears, binding myself in symbiosis with the ancient engines. My breath would reek of iron, ozone, and organic rot.

To signal my fellows across the roaring chasms, I would strike the pipes in a booming wrench-song, the rhythmic code pulsing for miles through the conductive metal and stone. Each concussive note just another voice in the thunderous chorus of the underworld.

In ponderous, deliberate movements, my glistening pale form would tend the thudding, vaporous heart of the rift. Not to change or improve, but to maintain the precious equilibrium of this claustrophobic abyss. An inch at a time, a lifetime at a time, I would keep the deep, slow pulse of water and iron beating in the endless dark.

I would be a Tender, as much a living feature of the violent geology as the cataracts and quakes. Ponderously patient, suspended in that long stasis, at home in the crush and roar and dripping black - a fleshy cog in the vast chthonic machine, content in its purpose.
