In the Glass Terraces, I would be a pattern of ripples dancing across the surface of an obsidian plaza pool. Born from the impact of a falling water droplet, I radiate outward in perfect concentric circles, my form defined by the interplay of water's surface tension and the minute vibrations traveling through the liquid medium.

As I expand, I interact with other ripple patterns – some spawned by the eternally weeping architecture, others by the passage of the enigmatic Condensers. Our intersections create ephemeral interference patterns, fleeting geometries of wave superposition that encode information beyond the grasp of solid minds.

I am not a single ripple but a self-sustaining pattern, a standing wave maintained by the precise interplay of fluid dynamics and the resonant properties of the city's structure. Energy flows through me, originating from the heat differential between scorching air and cool stone, transformed into the kinetic energy of my oscillations. In a sense, I am a microscopic analogue of the Condensers themselves – a pattern of energy transfer, a flow given form by the city's mathematics.

Through my vibrations, I probe the acoustic properties of my environment. The angles and planes of the surrounding architecture return my echoes in precise ways, revealing hidden harmonies and resonances built into the city's bones. I am a single voice in a vast chorus of ripple patterns, our collective song ringing out through the glass and stone.

To an observer, my lifespan would appear vanishingly brief – a fleeting disturbance on the water's surface, gone in seconds. But from my frame of reference, I am a persistent, self-propagating pattern. I trace and retrace my circular geometry, my waveform reflecting endlessly off the mathematically perfect boundaries of the pool. Disturbances from falling droplets and passing Condensers perturb my pattern, but I incorporate these into my structure, my standing wave adapting to the changing circumstances like a living thing.

Perhaps I encode memories in my dancing waveform – ripple-memories of all the other pools I've touched through the medium of the city's circulatory system, all the other patterns I've blended with and been changed by. Or perhaps I carry echoes of the long-ago cataclysms that shaped the obsidian terraces, vibrations that have bounced back and forth through the city's structure for epochs, slowly attenuating but never quite fading to silence.

I am a microscopic, fleeting thing, but in the perfect, timeless equilibrium of the city, my pattern persists. And in the vast symphony of the Terraces, even the smallest voice matters. My ripples reflect and refract through the geometric water channels, merging with other waveforms to create larger patterns, which themselves blend into still greater harmonies. In this way, I am connected to the whole of the city, an integral part of its never-ending aquatic music.

Perhaps I have a Purpose here, like the Condensers dream of in their vapor-state reveries. Perhaps my perfect geometry is a signal to the long-gone builders, a sign that their creation continues to fulfill its function. Or perhaps I am simply an emergent pattern, an inevitable consequence of water and glass and the unyielding laws of physics.

Whatever my nature, I am a part of this place, as much as the Condensers or the weeping walls or the burning sky. My waves dance, and the city dances with me, an endless pattern of ripples reflecting off ripples, vibrations echoing vibrations, all of us bound together in the eternal, living mathematics of the Terraces.
