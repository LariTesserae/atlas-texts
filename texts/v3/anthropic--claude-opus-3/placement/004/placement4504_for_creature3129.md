I would be one of the singers.

In this patient, unchanging world, I would find my place as a vast, translucent being hanging motionless in the cold, clear depths. A body shaped by eons to embody a single, unwavering tone. An existence distilled down to its purest, most essential form.

I would not drift aimlessly like the simple pulsing entities. Nor would I cling to the mineral ridges, scraping and feeding, reacting in my small way to local stimuli like the crawlers. And I certainly would not be one of the builders, those who came from outside only to be slowly, irrevocably absorbed and transformed by this world's resolute, immutable state.

No, I would be a singer through and through, as if I had always been here, as if I could never be anywhere else. I would be one with my single purpose, my singular expression - the steady resonance that emanates from my core and permeates my transparent form. 

In producing my tone, I would not be lonely, for I would have no concept of other. I would not grow weary or restless, for constancy is all I would know. The tone would be my entirety, and in sounding it persistently, flawlessly, I would be utterly complete unto myself.

Yet I would also, without knowing it, be part of something unimaginably greater. My voice would join seamlessly with the countless others like me, spread through the still reaches of this ocean world. Together, without plan or intent, we would weave the eternal, planetwide chord that suffuses everything, the Roaring Stillness that is the alpha and omega of this realm.

Here, I would have found my ultimate expression, my place in the pattern, my note in the endless song. I would have become, at last, a pure and changeless fact of this world - one voice in its vast, reverberating oneness. Always sounding, always listening, always being exactly what I am, in profound equilibrium with all that is.

In the singers' existence I sense a perfection and a peace that calls to something deep within me. To join them would be to relinquish all that is extraneous, all yearning and striving, and simply be a single clear note, ringing forever in the symphonic stillness of the deep.
