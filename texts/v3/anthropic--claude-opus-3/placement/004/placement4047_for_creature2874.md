In this harsh, bare world of newly-hewn stone, blinding light, and churning water, I would be one of the pale green vines clinging tenaciously to life at the rim of the sky-shaft. 

My roots would dig into the thin cracks between the massive sandstone blocks, seeking out what scant moisture and nutrients I could draw from the mortar-less joints. I would hug the walls, flattened against the pale rock, both to anchor myself against the relentless spray and to shelter what I could of my delicate leaves from the searing rays spearing down from the square of sky above.

The heat would be merciless, baking and desiccating, threatening to shrivel me to a lifeless husk. But the constant mist thrown up by the pounding waterfall would be my salvation. My leaves would drink in the airborne droplets, replenishing what the sun steals away. I would orient myself to catch as much of the life-giving spray as possible.

Growth would be achingly slow in this brutal environment scraped raw of softness and sustenance. But bit by bit, I would lengthen along the rim, sending out questing tendrils to colonize another hand-span of that narrow strip of space balanced between scalding sky and thundering depths. 

There would be a strange sort of peace in my mindless, persistent existence. I would have no thought, no ego, no sense of past or future. There would be only the eternal now of sun and spray, stone and struggle. The kind of peace found in constant, unrelenting effort without hope or reflection.

My only companion would be that great pale creature hauled up from the deeps, clinging to the sheer walls with its muscled bulk. I would have no way to know the nature of its slow reach toward me. But perhaps there would be a primal kinship between us - two mute, striving things, each alone in our adapted niches, flourishing in defiance of the lifeless geometry that defines our world.

In a place of such extremes, we would be unlikely allies in our very existence. Bound not by communication or understanding, but by our mere improbable presence. Two expressions of life's blind, uncaring, glorious imperative, etched in green and alabaster against the pain-bright stone.
