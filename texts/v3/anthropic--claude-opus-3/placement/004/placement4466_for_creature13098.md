In this stark and staggering landscape of primal architecture, I would find myself not as one of the colossal builders, but as an incongruous presence observing from the shadows. 

I am a lone human explorer, lost in this canyon of ceaseless construction. In a world of giants, my presence is insignificant, barely registered by the pale titans and their thunderous labors. I navigate the margins, scrambling up sheer basalt blocks, huddling beneath arches to escape the chalky rain. 

My scale renders me vulnerable, but also grants me access to spaces too small for the builders to perceive. I wedge myself into crevices between bricks still warm from the kiln, shimmy through crawl spaces where three walls intersect at impossible angles. In these liminal zones, I catch my ragged breath and try to comprehend the mathematics of this place.

My pack, once filled with rope, a bedroll, writing implements, is now crammed with builder relics pillaged from the rubble - an obsidian chisel, an oil-stained rag, a shard of white quartz like a piece of broken eye. I scratch inscrutable glyphs in my journal, attempting to discern meaning and motivation from the shifting architecture. 

But I am a foreigner here, an brief interloper in a world of stone and stress. I cannot crack the code of the seismic communication pulsing through the soles of my feet. When a tower groans and tilts above me, I flee in animal panic, while the builders simply factor it into their eternal equation.

I realize I cannot stay forever. This place will literally crush me, grind me into the mortar, use my blood and bone as binding agents. So I plot my escape, even as I am mesmerized by the muscular motion, the percussion, the interplay of stark shadows. 

I will carry this world out with me, in memory and recording. I will tell of the titans, the blind fish, the spectral moths. I will be the lens through which others glimpse the staggering possibility of this place. And though I remain small, finite, bound by the limits of flesh, my tale will endure, like a chisel mark on bedrock - the signature of the fragile upon the face of the eternal.
