I emerge into this world as a Kiln Keeper, fired from the deep clay in a remote basin rimmed by chiming crystal towers. My form is spare and angular, all long limbs and sharp joints, the color of burnt sienna. The depressions of my eyes hold the sheen of condensation, twin mirrors reflecting the bone-white sky.

I walk. It is my first and only imperative. I set out across the vast expanse of the flats, my ceramic feet making no mark upon the heat-hardened surface. The air shimmers around me, distorting the distant horizons into liquid mirages. I am a slow-moving flaw in the perfect glaze of this made world.

For uncounted cycles of light and fog, I traverse the same route, winding between shallow basins where strange mineral growths cluster and sing. When I pass, they lean towards me, resonating at frequencies felt rather than heard. Am I their audience? Their acolyte? The distinction seems to hold little meaning here.

In time, I encounter another of my kind. We stand close, the air between us humming with low vibration - an exchange of information, or perhaps simply recognition. Then we part, continuing our solitary journeys, parallel lines never quite intersecting.  

Sometimes I come upon places where the floor is still forming, fresh clay welling up from unseen depths. I wait at the edges of these sites, a silent witness to the world's patient self-creation. Droplets of condensation roll down my surface to mingle with the new material, a tiny sacrament. When it has cooled enough to bear my weight, I am always the first to cross.

The spans between these events are vast, marked only by the incremental expansion of my route as the flats grow. I am a keeper of slow time, an archive in motion. What knowledge am I gathering in my endless walk? What memories am I encoding in the resonance between my kind? I have no answers, only the ceaseless drive to witness.

Eventually, I know, I will stop. I will fold myself into a basin, ceding my ceramic form to the same processes that made it. Crystal will encrust me, until I am indistinguishable from the formations I once contemplated. And the wind will sing through me, and new Keepers will pause to listen, and the archive will go on.

But for now, I walk - a pilgrim in terracotta, treading a path that only clay and heat and deep time could write. The haze-diffused light cycles above me, the tremor of the world's becoming thrums beneath. And I am, for a span that will one day seem the blink of an eye to the flats themselves, a part of it all, a mote of intent in the shimmer and silence.

I am a Kiln Keeper, and I will walk until I am called to sing.
