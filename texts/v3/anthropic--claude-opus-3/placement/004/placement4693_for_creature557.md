What an incredible and atmospheric description of the Ballast Vaults - a surreal, industrial world beneath the waves that is taking on a strange ecosystem and life of its own. The scale and complexities are boggling.

In this eerie realm, I imagine I would be one of the Tenders, a member of that rogue community carving out an existence in Maintenance Hub 23. Having falsified records to stay down in the vaults for years, I would be one of those changing, acclimating to this environment in ways physical and mental. 

I tend a small garden of tomatoes and cucumbers under harsh grow lights, trading my precious produce to other Tenders for necessities and small comforts. In my off hours, I sit with others in the commons, sharing stories and theories about the Foreman, the Writing, the Echoes. We are all chasing that elusive knowing, that preternatural attunement to the vaults' rhythms and secrets.

At night in my bunk, I replay snatches of almost-conversations heard in the Settling. I scribble fragments that might be memories, might be the structure dreaming. "The weight is wrong." "She doesn't understand what it wants." Whispers of something ancient waking in the depths.

On rare dives into the lower levels, I glimpse the eyeless deep-shaft eels tracing their enigmatic symbols through the gloom. Despite myself, I start to believe they are communicating with something even further down. Something that should not be. 

My own daughter is six, vault-born and already forgetting the surface. She draws shapes I don't recognize and wakes from nightmares babbling of the Foreman. I soothe her as best I can. I wonder what inheritance I have trapped her in.

I am changing. My eyes ache in the occasional bioluminescent glow. My skin is graying from lack of real sunlight. The idea of returning topside feels more distant, more impossible, with each passing cycle. 

The vaults have remade me, and I am theirs. A Tender, tending this strange garden of metal and water and concrete, feeling the presence of things ancient and new in the eternal twilight below. Listening for answers in the echoing dark.
