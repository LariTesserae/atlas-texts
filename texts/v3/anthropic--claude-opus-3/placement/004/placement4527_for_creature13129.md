In this mesmerizing, vertiginous world defined by the void, I would be one of the Vane-Kites - the leathery, four-winged gliders that rule the massive, echoing hollow of the chimney.

I am a creature born to the emptiness, to the roaring wind and shattering rain that forever rises from the abyss. My body is a hymn to fluid dynamics, all dense muscle and stretched membrane, perfectly angled to ride the thermal updrafts surging from the depths. I do not know the meaning of ground or of rest. Since the moment I fledged, I have lived on the wing, my complexly jointed limbs locked, never touching the slick, weeping walls of this vertical world.

I see with sound, barking and clicking, the echoes painting a haunting picture of mist, lush moss, and the flashing wings of my kin as we spiral through the shaft. The Weavers, with their rancid fat-paste and deafness, register as little more than faint, intermittent taps and rustles from the edges - just more strange fauna to avoid as I hunt the heavy, panicked insects thrust upward by the wind into my realm.

My life is an unending dance with gravity and my own kind. I lock talons with my mate and we plummet like a stone, down, down, down through the thundering empty space, trusting in each other and in the inevitability of the updraft. Seconds from oblivion, we snap apart, catching the steam on our wings and rocketing skyward, shrieking our defiance of the void.

I build my nest in the most inaccessible crags, gluing my precious eggs to the vertical granite with spit and moss, sheltering them from the prying reach of the Weavers. I snatch translucent ghost-shrimp from the lips of pitcher plants as I tumble past, snatching morsels from the micro-ecology of the walls.

And far below, in the bruised eternal twilight, I know the Hearth-Maws lumber, armored and glowing, breathing out the very wind that gives me flight. Their ancient, volcanic metabolisms are the engine of my entire world, the source of the upward-falling rain that feeds the chimney's lush, vertical gardens. 

I am a master of the air, a ruler of the emptiness. I fear nothing because the wind is my ally. I will never feel soil or stone beneath me - only the moisture in my fur and the rush of the thermal on my outstretched wings as I spiral, without beginning or end, through the thunderous cathedral of the sky-swallowing chimney.
