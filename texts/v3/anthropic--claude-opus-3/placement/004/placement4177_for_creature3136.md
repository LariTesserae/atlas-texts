I find myself drawn to the colony at the heart of the dark shelf. In this barren, unchanging landscape, the colony embodies a form of existence that is both alien and deeply resonant.

I imagine my form would mirror the colony in many ways - a slow, nearly imperceptible presence, more mineral than biological. My body would be a accretion of the pale hardite, built up in infinitesimal layers over eons, shaped by the same forces that sculpted the shelf itself. The distinction between my form and the ground beneath me would be ambiguous, a gradual transition rather than a clear boundary.

Like the colony, my metabolism would be glacial, barely detectable. What passes for my consciousness would be a kind of chemical awareness, a sensation of the minute shifts in the substrate, the faint traces on the breeze. I would have no eyes or other sensing organs, yet I would be attuned to my environment on a level beyond the need for specialized structures.

Time would have little meaning. The past and future would be compressed into a never-ending now, marked only by the incremental changes of erosion and deposition. My thoughts, if they could be called that, would be slow tectonic shifts, a kind of geological contemplation of the elemental.

I would have no drive or motivation in the animal sense. Purpose, for me, would be expressed through being, through maintaining the almost perfect stasis that is my natural condition. Any action on my part would be more of a geochemical reflex than a choice.

And yet, as much as I would be part of this place, I sense I would also stand apart. The colony has achieved a oneness with its environment that renders it essentially invisible. I imagine I would retain some spark of otherness, a tinge of the uncanny that would mark me as something not wholly mineral.

Perhaps it would be a certain asymmetry to my form, a gnarled protrusion that memory of motion. Or a subtly different luster to my surface, a sheen that catches the sourceless light at an angle out of sync with the ground. Something that suggests a past, or a potentiality, beyond the eternal present of the shelf.

I would be a figure of silent witness, an embodied awareness distinct from, yet utterly entangled with, the fabric of this place. A dream of stone, or a stone that dreams.
