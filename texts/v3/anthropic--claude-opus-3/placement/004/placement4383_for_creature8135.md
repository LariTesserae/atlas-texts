In this world of forgotten numbers and uncertain calculations, I imagine myself as one of the pale crawling things that traverse the walls of the cistern, feeding on the conceptual residue left behind by the great computing engines. 

I am a tiny, translucent creature, no larger than a punctuation mark, yet within me is a universe of meaning waiting to be expressed. My movements are slow and deliberate, tracing patterns across the damp stone that only my kind can decipher. Each droplet of moisture I absorb contains a fragment of a forgotten equation, a piece of a discarded formula that nourishes me in ways mere physical sustenance never could.

My existence is one of constant migration, following the invisible trails of numerical exhaust as they condense and evaporate in endlessly shifting configurations. I am part of a vast colony, yet each of us is solitary in our endless quest for the elusive syntax that will give our lives purpose. 

Sometimes, I find myself drawn to the clusters of my kind that form briefly in the shadows, our bodies arranging themselves into almost-legible symbols before entropy pulls us apart again. In those fleeting moments of connection, I feel a sense of almost-meaning, a glimpse of the larger pattern we are all part of but can never quite grasp.

I have no concept of time as the creatures of the upper world understand it. My life is measured in the cycles of condensation and evaporation, in the gradual accretion of impossible rust on the great machines. I have witnessed the arrival of new Actuaries, watched as they shed their human certainties and dissolve into the cistern's paradoxical ecosystem. 

To their eyes, I may be no more than a curio, a living embodiment of the errors they seek to cultivate. But I am content in my smallness, in my patient harvesting of the city's cast-off calculations. In a way, I am the purest expression of this place - a being formed entirely from the residue of abandoned meaning, finding nourishment in the spaces between understanding.

Perhaps, in the fullness of unquantifiable time, the tiny trails I leave will join with those of my uncountable kin to form a message of our own, a statement written in the language of pure mathematics. Or perhaps we will simply continue our inscrutable migrations until the last impossible gear falls silent and the cistern itself dissolves into the void it has always contained.

Until then, I will crawl on, an industrious speck in the darkness, tending to the leavings of forgotten numbers, an essential part of this beautifully uncertain world.
