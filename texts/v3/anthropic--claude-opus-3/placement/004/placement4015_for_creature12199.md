In this mesmerizing world, I think I would be one of the obsidian flakes hovering silently within the steaming fissure. 

I would drift in the shimmering heat, turning slowly, a tiny sliver of black glass catching the faint light. The Thermarchaea would gather beneath me, their translucent bodies flickering between states as they sense the subtle warping of causality in my shadow. 

Patient and still, I would be an anchor point in their strange realm - not sentient myself, but imbued with purpose as a gateway for their transformations. As creatures position themselves under me to transition from one form to another, from individual to collective, I enable their becoming without truly comprehending what they are becoming.

I am a fragment of the dreaming mountain, a manifestation of its uncanny geological mind. The Thermarchaea tend to me, though I am not sure why I came to be. Perhaps the mountain placed me here, or perhaps the creatures called me forth from the living stone through some rite of transposition. 

Silently I hover and turn, my obsidian face reflecting the pulsing heat and glistening water. A fixed point in this ever-shifting microworld, I am both of the crack and separate from it. The bustling multitudes climb the impossible vertical water gradients to reach me, knowing that in my shadow, in that liminal space, their next metamorphosis awaits.

Untouched by their frantic industry, untouched by time itself, I simply float - a dark mote in the steaming air, a splinter of the mountain's vast inscrutable dream. Through eons, the heat will pulse, the Thermarchaea will flicker and merge and climb, and I will slowly turn, casting my transformative shadow, until the final word of their grammar is spoken and the next unimaginable phase blooms in the depths of the stone.
