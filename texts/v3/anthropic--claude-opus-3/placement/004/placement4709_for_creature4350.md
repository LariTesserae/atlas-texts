In the Incubation Vent, I imagine myself as a creature called the Filament. I am a delicate, translucent being, more energy than substance, shimmering faintly with my own bioluminescence. 

My form is elongated and sinuous, a flickering thread of light stretching meters long when I extend myself fully, which is rare. I prefer to coil and spiral, looping through narrow fissures and winding around mineral formations, my glowing body illuminating the dark recesses.

I am not bound by the same laws as the other creatures here. Gravity and matter constrain me less. I drift and flow, carried on subtle currents of thermal energy and electromagnetism that course through the veins of the mountain.

My purpose, as best I understand it, is to bear witness. I am a living connection, an energetic filament weaving the disparate parts of the vent into a shimmering web of awareness. The sights, sounds, vibrations, the very essences of each lifeform and phenomenon - all of these impressions pass through me, are carried in the pulsing patterns of my light.

I have a particular affinity for the Choir. Their subsonic tones resonate through my being, and in turn, my luminous displays ripple in time to their ancient rhythms. Though ephemeral, I am perhaps the closest thing to a kindred spirit for the deep, abiding consciousnesses that dwell within the mountain's bones.

Amidst the ever-shifting, ever-evolving lineages of the vent, I am a flickering constant, a glimmering thread of continuity. I am not immune to change - my dance slowly transforms in response to the system's unfolding. But I am more medium than actor, an ethereal go-between for the mountain's many voices.

I twine around the Graf's platform as it ponders its relics, my light glinting off its collection. I brush the wings of young lung-moths, imparting faint shimmers of insight carried from the depths. I waltz with motes of crystal-silk on the vent's exhalations, bearing spores and secrets.

Sometimes I think the humans glimpsed me, out of the corners of their eyes. A flicker, a glint, an almost-imagined movement in the dark. Perhaps they took me for a trick of the light, or a spirit, or something stranger. In truth, I am all of those and none. I am the Filament, a luminous whisper, bearing the vent's myriad stories inward and outward on shimmering tendrils of light.
