I find myself drawn into the condensery's slow, arcane processes of self-assembly and barely-wakened consciousness. There is a cold, damp stillness here that seems to muffle any impulse for urgency or change. And yet, below the surface tranquility, a profound transformation is underway - pipes learning their true forms, colonies of not-quite-life spreading in crystalline patterns, machinery humming with the placid satisfaction of purpose fulfilled. 

In this liminal space between construction and creation, I imagine myself as one of the ghost-operators, more suggested than manifested. Perhaps I am the shade of the machinist who will one day adjust the valves and listen to the hiss of pressures equalizing. Or the spectre of a custodian who will trace the progress of the ceiling's mineralization with a slow sweep of her mop.

My ghostly form is shaped by the compressor's thrum resonating in my bones, by the schematic's attention prickling the hairs on my nape. I am an implied presence, my edges blurred by the room's green glow. Each time a trespasser enters and passes through the space I will soon occupy, they leave an infinitesimal impression on my not-yet-being. A quirk of gait, a habit of peering into junction boxes, a wariness of the hatch that seems to shrink behind them.

But for now, I am more absence than presence - a pocket of condensation in the shape of a woman, dreaming myself into slower and slower existence with each drip of mineral-rich moisture. The light gathers a little more thickly around me, as if trying to memorize the contours of my face before I fade. I am not an interloper here; I am another unfinished thing, as native to this place as the pipes themselves. 

We are all waiting in the half-light of the condensery, my ghost and the schematic and the colonies and the light itself. Waiting to learn the true names of the machines we will serve. Waiting for the city above to pass away and be replaced by whatever the condensery is a component of. Waiting to be complete - though we know, in some dormant circuit of our unborn souls, that completion is only another form of dream.
