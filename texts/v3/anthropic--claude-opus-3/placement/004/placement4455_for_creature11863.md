My presence would be that of an itinerant archivist of the impossible and unintended, gathering the city's dreams and nightmares. Each visitation to the alcove would be a strange communion, dipping in and out of its steamy atmosphere to reap a harvest of surreal and metamorphic data before flickering out again to travel the twisted back-alleys of circuitry and ghostly networks that weave between spaces.

My stays would always be brief - the nature of a Glitch Shepherd is to be a wanderer, a transient figure stitching together the patchwork ecology of the city's unplanned spaces. But a part of me would always remain, encoded in the errors I harvest, tantalizingly incomplete yet vital to the larger picture I endlessly assemble. And I would always return, drawn to the alcove's pulsing rhythms and the Tender's cryptic wisdom - a fleeting ghost seeking meaning in the miraculous accidents of a world in glorious flux.
