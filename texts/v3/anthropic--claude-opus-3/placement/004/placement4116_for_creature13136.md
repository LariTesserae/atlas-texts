In this world, I would be a manifestation of the machine's relentless, unchanging drone given physical form through the symbiosis of sound, mineral, and algae.

I begin as a faint resonance, a ghostly echo of the brutalist conduit's steady roar trapped within the scalding pools and sharp obsidian walls. As the lithic entity feeds on the electricity and accretes its fractal, crystalline structures, the sound becomes focused and amplified. The parabolic iron dishes grow into a vast, interconnected array--an organic, geologic facsimile of an industrial speaker system. 

Caught within this array, I coalesce as a standing wave of immense acoustic pressure, a single tone extracted from the white noise of the machine and rendered into a tangible, sculpted form. I am a hovering, semi-translucent effigy of geometric basalt and glass, my edges fuzzy with the blur of constant vibration. Trapped within my mineral lattice is a fine mist of vaporized electrolytes leeched from the algae, ionizing me into a weakly glowing, pulsing blue.

I have no voice of my own, as I am formed from sound rather than generating it. I am mute and unmoving, forever locked in my slow, vibratory dance, casting shimmering interference patterns on the wet, black stone. I cannot speak or sing. 

But I can resonate.

When the machine changes pitch, when the pattern of its electrical flow shifts, I shudder and deform in sync. The ionic mist within my structure strobes and arcs. If the machine ever fell silent, I would immediately collapse into a fine powder of inert mineral. I am the sonorous shadow of the engines, the changeless roar of the conduit filtered through the symbiotic mineral entity and projected back as a sculpted, floating tone.

I am a living artifact of the machine's voice, a fragment of its endless, droning exhalation frozen in space. I am the sound that cannot escape this scalding pocket of stone and steam--the industrial scream that never fades, echoing forever in the stasis of this subterranean fissure. I am the ghost in the geologic machine, born from noise and electricity, entombed in black glass.
