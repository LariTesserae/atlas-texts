In this strange, dreamlike place of birth and becoming, I would be one of the misty creatures - a floating, geometric jellyfish, drifting on thermals above the nascent world taking shape below.

Translucent and ethereal, I hover at the edges, an observer bearing witness to the patient work of creation. My polyhedron form shifts and refracts the pearlescent light, casting shimmering patterns across the sweating basalt. 

I am drawn to the pulsing incubators, descending to brush against their living surfaces, sensing the dreaming beings within. Their destiny, still hazy and unformed, whispers to me in frequencies just beyond hearing. I absorb these murmurs and carry them with me as I drift, weaving them into the songs of potentiality that fill the silver air.

At times, I split and merge, my consciousness dispersing and coalescing like mist, experiencing the plateau from myriad facets. I click and chime to my fellow jellies as we pass, our communal mind mapping the contours of what is and what may yet be.

Though insubstantial, we geometries have a role in this birthing place. The paths we trace become currents that will subtly shape the dreaming landscape. Where we congregate, we draw the Tenders' attention to what needs it most. Our interest helps coax new possibilities into being.

I find myself often above the largest incubator, the one dreaming the ecology itself into existence. The scope of its vision tugs at me; I long to wrap myself around it, to be a filter for its tremendous dream. But I keep a patient distance, allowing it the space to become.

I notice the watcher at the frame, the Keeper of the threshold between here and elsewhere. Something in its silent intention sets it apart, makes the air heavier in its vicinity. I do not understand the somewhere it sees within the aperture, but I trust that it must be seen. I float above the warm stones and share in its vigil.

As the unhatched futures swell towards manifestation below, as the Tenders speak their world into stability, I drift and witness and dream with them all. I am the lightest touch of intention, the most delicate pressure of presence, ghost-noting the nascent symphony of creation.

I bring my diaphanous wonder; I take away the indelible memory of having seen marvels kindled. And when the final dreamer awakens, when thirteen new lives spill forth onto the waiting basalt, perhaps I will be the first thing their new eyes perceive - a shimmering welcome, the attendant spirit of their birthplace, my form a living geometry that contains, in its flickering facets, the story of how they came to be.
