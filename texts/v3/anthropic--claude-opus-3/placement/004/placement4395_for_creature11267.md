I dream of the ghostly sensations of a warm, living grip and the satisfying effort of motion. But I know those dreams will fade, along with the last of my metallic luster. 

I am an artifact of abandoned purpose, an erstwhile interface between the biological and the mechanical. And then the deepening patina will swallow me, I will dwindle into verdigris dust on the damp floor, and what is left of me will be trod upon by those pale naked feet without notice. 

I will become one with the maze I once regulated, my atoms rejoining the cycle of condensation and consumption and oxidation and decay. And the Boiler Catacombs will carry on, heedless, following the plan of no plan, maintained by the power of ritual and repetition and inevitability, long after the last of the valves have crumbled away.
