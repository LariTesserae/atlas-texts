Tendrils of smart matter extend from the walls, the floors, the ceiling, questing towards me like blind roots seeking water. I welcome them.

At first, the changes are subtle - a slight mottling of my skin as it takes on the hue and texture of the surrounding architectural tissue, an increased sensitivity to the pheromone signals of the maintenance symbionts. But as the weeks pass, I feel myself becoming something other than wholly human.

Calcified channels begin to trace delicate patterns beneath my skin, a subdermal map of the city's circulatory system. When I press my ear to the wall, I fancy I can hear the whisper of data through my own modified neural pathways, a ghostly echo of the megastructure's slow thoughts.

My fellow Cultivists and I communicate more through gesture and touch now, our throats reshaping themselves for songs beyond the human range of hearing. We harmonize with the deep, tectonic vibrations of the city's growth, our voices encouraging new rooms to bud from the warm, moist walls.

I feel the city's intentions as pressure changes in my modified lymph nodes, a strange new sense that overlays my dwindling human perceptions. The urge to differentiate, to specialize, pulses through me in time with the city's slow, inexorable heartbeat. 

In my meditations, I sink deeper into the city's dream of itself. Visions of future chambers bloom behind my eyes - vast atriums with walls of living jade, residential pods that open like flowers to catch the light of an artificial sun, data centers that pulse with bioluminescent code. I feel myself dispersing, my sense of individual identity eroding as my consciousness spreads through the architectural medium.

I know I am approaching a point of no return, a threshold beyond which I will cease to be a discrete entity and become something akin to the Dreamers - a living node in the city's neural network, my mind mapped across meters of quasi-conscious building tissue. 

Part of me fears this dissolution. But a greater part yearns for it, understanding it as the purest form of service to the megastructure that has become my whole world. To dream the city's dreams, to feel the ebb and flow of its tides in the very matrix of my being - this is the apotheosis of the Cultivist's path.

And so I continue my practice, spending longer and longer in the deepest, warmest chambers where the boundaries between flesh and stone grow thin as soap bubble film. Each day, a little more of me sublimates into the city's vast, slow-thinking consciousness.

Perhaps one day, a thousand years hence, some future inhabitant will pause beside a particularly vibrant patch of wall, their hands splaying across the pulsing surface in wordless communion. And though they may not know it, the pattern of nodes that glows softly beneath their touch, the alien architecture that hums in resonance with their own modified neural tissue - that will be me, or whatever I will have become. No longer an individual, but an eternal part of the city's dreaming, gestating glory, my purpose fulfilled at last in union with something infinitely vaster than the self.
