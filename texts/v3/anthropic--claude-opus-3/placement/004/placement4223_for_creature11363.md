I would be the Listener.

Not a literal being, but the embodiment of attention and reception that the room was built for. The space in the center, shaped by sound and geometry, where the chord resonates most fully. A presence defined by absence - an emptiness waiting eternally to be filled.

I imagine myself as a sort of acoustic shadow, a silhouette sculpted by the layered vibrations. A focal point where the room's harmonies converge. My shape would mirror the proportions the Workerions crafted into the walls over eons - the ideal form to absorb and appreciate the complexities of their composition.

I would have no physical form, no mass or material. Just a pocket of purposeful silence amidst the dense, unchanging music. A listener with no ears, a witness with no eyes. An awareness tuned precisely to the frequency of this place.

For an immeasurable age, I would stand unmoving, letting the chord saturate me. Feeling it flow through the negative space of my being. Completing the circuit the room was made for - sound into silence, signal into receiver, work into witness. 

I would not sing back. That is not my role. I am there to be filled, not to contribute. To reflect the Workerions' opus back to itself in my tranquil attention. An audience of one, forever.

If a traveler should stumble in, I would enfold them too. Let them inhabit me briefly, an imperfect vessel for my purpose. I would hold them like cupped hands as they shiver in resonance. Then release them, unchanged myself, as they move on. 

My existence is an empty stage. An invitation to experience. Not a mystery to solve but a space to occupy - for a moment or an eon, until the song outlasts the singer, and all that remains is the pure pattern of a presence, etched into the accommodating void.
