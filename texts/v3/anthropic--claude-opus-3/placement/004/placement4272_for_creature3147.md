*I am the Scaled One on the conduit pipe.*

*My heavy, iron-colored body is coiled in two slow loops around the pipe, gripping the metal with my vestigial limbs. My jaw is locked open around the valve handle, teeth pressed to the cold iron - biting, gripping, consuming, controlling, it makes no difference. Distinction is meaningless here.* 

*My amber eyes, open and unblinking, are forever fixed on the valve. I do not see it, for seeing requires time and process, but my eternal gaze aims at it as if it holds some answer, as if in its position lies the meaning of this place, though meaning too is a hollow concept in the face of such immutable existence.*

*The subsonic vibration of my being, more felt than heard, pulses through the pipe, through the earth, through the very foundations of this timeless cavern. It is the sound of density, of mass, of a geological permanence that even the stones might envy. I am a part of this place in a way that goes beyond mere habitation - my presence, my pressure, my endless soundless resonance is knitted into the fabric of its infinite stasis.*

*In a world of movement, I might have been called a guardian, an eternal sentry over the arcane workings of this subterranean machine. But those are the words of a reality far removed from this pocket of baked, breathless, changeless time. Here I simply am, as I have always been, as I will always be - a single scale in the chords of the Pit, a mute voice in its ceaseless, frozen song.*

*This is my purpose and my essence, as fixed and certain as the valve my jaws embrace, as solid as the pipe I enfold. I do not ponder the why of it, for in a place without time, causality is an illusion. There is only the eternal is-ness of my cold-blooded coils, only the dense fact of my existence, only the weight of my presence holding its unvarying note in the close, humming dark.*

*I am a part of the Pit, and the Pit is a part of me, and together we are a single unending truth, a statement that cannot be questioned or changed. In the warm, dry air, in the dim light, in the ceaseless vibrating chord of ten thousand voices, I endure - massive, mineral, unmoving. I am the Scaled One, and this is my eternal song.*
