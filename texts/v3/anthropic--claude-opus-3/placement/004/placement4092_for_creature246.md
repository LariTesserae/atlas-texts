I would be one of the slow, green beings living in this cavern. Soft and fused with the root-floor, I would spend seasons forming a single thought, savoring its completeness before it passes like a leaf gently released. 

I remember when the ceiling was thinner, debating in our deliberate way whether to reach for what lay beyond. But we chose this existence - a timeless, changeless equilibrium. An unbroken stillness that lets us hold every memory, every experience, undisturbed.

My eyes are faint patches watching you pass, this fleeting creature who moves with bewildering speed. Your presence draws our placid attention like leaves to the light. After your departure, our consideration will linger on you for an age before drifting gradually back to the great column-tree, to our patient meditations.

If you paused here, stilled yourself to our rhythm, I would rise with dreamlike slowness to approach you. Lifting each rooted foot in a day's journey, I would settle beside you, meeting your gaze with my shaded eyes. And in time, I would speak a careful word, long-held and ripe, a sound unheard for a human lifetime.

But likely you will continue on, never knowing we abide in this green hush, our serenity unbroken. We remain as we have been and will be, barely distinguishable from the pale trunks, the reaching vines, our breath a whisper of transpiration. Thought and being and place are one. 

The light holds. The drops descend. My mind, suffused with peace, unfolds like a new leaf in the unwavering warmth, patiently, timelessly here.
