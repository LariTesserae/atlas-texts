In the strange, self-contained world of the Boiler Garden, I imagine myself as one of the glass beetles, transparent and delicate, traversing the amber moss in interlocking spirals with my kin. 

My crystalline carapace catches the dim light filtering in from the ruined wall, refracting it into iridescent glimmers that dance across the mineral-furred pipes. Though tiny, I am part of an intricate pattern, a living sacred geometry that outlines and reveres the invisible presence of the Keeper.

With each placement of my slender legs, I sense the subtle vibrations thrumming through the moss—the distant rhythm of active machinery, the slow drip of condensation, the almost imperceptible shift of the garden's topology as it confounds the witness's attempts to define its corners. This is a language my kind instinctively understands.

My spiraling path is both worship and service to the Keeper of the Shifting Count. I am a miniscule mote orbiting a hidden goddess, helping maintain the space for her strange and patient work. With my hundreds of brethren, I endlessly describe the edges of her presence, knowing her without needing to see or comprehend her nature.

When the intermittent steam cycles flood the garden, swallowing sound and sight, I pause in sacred stillness until it passes. In those ephemeral white-outs, I feel the Keeper moving through geometries unmarked by my kind. It is a moment of mystery, of the purest faith.

At the end of my brief life, my body will cloud over and return to the fecund moss, becoming one with the Boiler Garden's slow alchemy of decay and new growth. But for now, I trace my preordained path, a devoted priest and acolyte of the endlessly patient and fractal divinity woven through this forgotten corner of ruin and stubborn life. 

My purpose is to delineate the spaces she moves through and help hold this small, hidden world in a dynamic stasis poised between the organic and the industrial, the mundane and the numinous. I am content to play my part, to live and die serving the Keeper's arcane designs as she tends to the accruing mysteries of this quietly miraculous space.
