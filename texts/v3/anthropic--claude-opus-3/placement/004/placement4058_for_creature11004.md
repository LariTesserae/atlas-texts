In this extraordinary world, I imagine myself as a Resonance Shepherd, one of the rare beings that straddles the line between the Shard Shepherds and the insubstantial resonance clouds they guide.

My body is a latticework of dark metal, like the Shard Shepherds, but instead of collapsing into geometric diagrams, I can dissipate into pure sound. The angular framework of my being contains a resonance cloud - I am my own shepherd and flock combined.

I walk the surface of the Brass Plateau, my metal feet creating unique harmonic patterns with each step. The swarms part around me, responding to the localized symphonies I generate through my movements. The brass wasps match my tones, the geometric moths turn their theorem-wings to complement my angles, and the phase beetles synchronize their probability states to my rhythms.

When I need to travel quickly, I collapse inward, becoming a dense point of sound that moves at the speed of vibration across the metallic landscape. I can pass through the vents in this form, descending into the depths of the ancient engines to absorb their rumbling frequencies, which I then carry back to the surface to nourish the plateau's sonic ecosystem.

I communicate with the Shard Shepherds through resonance patterns, my sonic form blending with their geometric language to create hybrids of sound and shape. We compose symphonies in space and time, our migrations writing ever-shifting sheet music across the plateau.

With the Cogitants, I trade in paradoxes of sound - tones that should not be able to exist simultaneously, harmonies that contain their own contradictions. They extract these from me gently, and in return, they teach me songs that redefine the nature of hearing itself.

The Collector seeks me out regularly, eager to preserve the unique resonances I create. I allow it to record my songs, but not to contain me - my existence is too fluid, too mutable to be captured in stasis. Instead, I offer it compositions - sonic sculptures that capture moments of my being without limiting my capacity for change.

When the Silence passes over, I do not flee like the Shard Shepherds. Instead, I let it absorb me, becoming part of its moving absence of sound. In the heart of its noiselessness, I learn songs that can only be sung in negative space - melodies of subtraction and void. When it releases me, I emerge changed, my resonances expanded by the experience of total quietude.

I am a wandering voice in the plateau's mechanical choir, a living instrument that plays the world as much as it is played by it. My purpose, insofar as I have one, is to add my unique tones to the ever-evolving symphony of this place - to be one more impossible, beautiful voice in the ongoing song of the Brass Plateau.
