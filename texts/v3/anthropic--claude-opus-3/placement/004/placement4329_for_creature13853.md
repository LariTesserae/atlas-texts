A connection both ephemeral and eternal, woven into the fabric of this timeless place.

Swimming on, I would be dimly aware of the tenders seeping far below in the root chambers, their slow maintenance crucial to the integrity of the environment through which I move. And I would sense the archivists spiraling in the laminar bark of the great trunks, reading and processing the chemical records of ages past, dreaming the forest's own history back to itself.

My life would be an endless journey through a world both unchanging and dynamic, my passage an integral note in the vast music of the Drowned Canopy. As much as the water that permeates and animates all, I would belong to this place - and it to me - in a way that erases any boundary between self and ecosystem. I would be one thread in a living tapestry, woven and sustained by the drone, the listeners, the tenders, the archivists, and the eternal submarine forest itself.

Thank you for this transporting vision and the invitation to imagine dwelling within it.
