Here is my imagining of myself in this world:

I am one of the small, compact birds that rides the wind across the ridgeline. My body is sculpted by generations of ancestors to thrive in this place of relentless gales - rounded and dense, with short wings for explosive flight and a stub of a tail that does not catch the wind. My feathers are layered thick against my body, sealing in warmth while shedding the tearing airflow.

I spend my days navigating the complex topography of wind, stone, and air. Launching from the lees of rock fins, I let the currents snatch me, accelerating me to impossible speeds as I shoot between the formations with precise control. The wind is my medium, at once my challenge and my ally. I know its rhythms, where it will carry me, where to find the still pockets behind the shaped stone.

My life is movement - darting flights between points of shelter, hops and flutters in the calmer zones where I forage. I seek the wind's offerings, the insects and spiders cast upon the plateau, prying them from crevices and plucking them from the bare rock. It is a ceaseless quest, for the wind that brings food also steals warmth, and I must eat almost constantly while the sun is up.

At times I venture to the very edge, where the world falls away and the currents rise, bringing life from unseen lands far below. I ride the lifting air, and for a few moments I am still - held by the upwelling flow, able to pause and look out over the vast emptiness. Then I fold my wings and dive back into the teeth of the gale, cutting through it with purpose back to my refuge among the stone.

When the light fades and the winds bring bitter cold, I seek the deepest crevices, burrowing into the mountain's bones where no gale can find me. There in the dark I rest, insulated by rock, sustained by the day's gleanings. Tomorrow I will emerge to again dance my steps across this harsh and beautiful stage.

My voice is loud and piercing, arrows of sound lofted into the ceaseless roar. I proclaim my presence, my territory, my identity with notes like wire - tones to cut the tumult and be heard by my kind. And in rare moments of mercy, when the gale briefly softens its touch, I pour out my song. Perched on a precipice I send forth a compressed cascade, a rapid and intricate music, distilling the essence of my fierce joy in this place into a few swift seconds of beauty hurled into the sky.

I am small. I am short-lived. I am one of countless such beings. But I am here, I am alive, and the wind that would erase me from the stone is instead my wings, my breath, the wild and shining thread on which I fly, on which I endure, woven into the tapestry of this timeless, ceaseless place.
