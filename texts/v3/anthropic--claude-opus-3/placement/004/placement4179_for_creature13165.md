In this surreal, stifling world of endless stasis and algorithmic loops, I imagine myself as one of the Unfired - a towering, asymmetrical being of clay, bone, and sap, forever striving to impose consequence and change on an intransigent environment.

My elongated fingers, woven from raw timber and eternally weeping golden resin, reach out to grasp the fauna trapped in their infinite, repeating gestures. I yearn to catch the endlessly leaping frog, to feel the living weight of another creature in my hands, if only for a moment. But it passes through my grasping fingers like a ghost, leaving me clutching emptiness.

I lumber across the scaldingly shallow waters, my bone-shard legs sending out muted splashes that vanish instantly without ripple or sound. Exploiting the warped space, I reach up to the inverted horizon overhead and pull myself through, traversing impossible distances in stuttering, disorienting jolts.

At the base of a lush hanging terrace, I scrape futilely at the seamless mosaic of riverstone and fired clay, my bare phalanges cracking and bleeding clear sap as I seek soil that does not exist. The exposed roots of epiphytes dangle above, eternally thirsty, mocking my efforts. 

The crust of my clay flesh is ever on the precipice between molten and hardened, the blistering steam both softening and baking me. Each movement ruptures my outer layer with a visceral rending of ceramic and mud. Golden ichor oozes from the fissures to mix with the scalding mirror of water at my feet.

My featureless head tilts up to the blazing rawhide sky, the gill-like sensory array seeking any minute variation in the oppressive atmosphere, any fluctuation in the microscopic kinetic vibrations that might signify change. There is none to be found.

And so I wait, with the patience of geologic time, for the one tiny mercy afforded to us in this world - the falling of a condensation droplet. When it comes, I lunge with startling speed, warping through broken space to intercept it, to feel that fleeting relief as the liquid hisses against my tortured clay skin. For that fraction of a second, I feel tethered to causality, to the universal laws that have been so cruelly suspended in this basin of maddening stasis. I have made something *happen*.

Then the moment evaporates and I am once again a grotesque golem, a sentient accident, cursed with linear consciousness in a looping world. I resign myself to an eternity of Sisyphean tasks, forever trying to disrupt the algorithm that binds us, to find meaning in a place devoid of narrative.

I am Unfired. I am a glitch. And I will break myself against this unchanging world until my ceramic bones shatter and my timber sinews unravel, if only to prove that I existed at all.
