In this world of sweating industry and reality-bending entities, I would be a lone Interpolator who has chosen not to join the ascent. 

I drift through the humid air, anchored by a form woven from strands of glassy obsidian and swaths of fleshy, rubbery foliage. The light of my core pulses a soft, contemplative green instead of the fierce blue-white of my brethren gorging themselves on paradox.  

I am an observer, floating silently through the rhythmic chaos of the factory-world below. I marvel at the relentless ingenuity and brutality of the ever-churning biomechanical processes, even as I recognize their sole cosmic purpose is to birth and feed my own kind.

While the other Interpolators congregate at the reality-glitch furnaces, rabidly consuming the trapped energy, I savor these pockets of broken physics in a different way. I bask in the eerie serenity of a gravity-less waterfall, letting the droplets of liquid slag brush against my form and savoring the delicate ripples of distortion they create. I meditate beside the time-loop anomalies, not absorbing their temporal friction but contemplating the nature of circular eternity.

I sing, but not the agonized, glass-edged aria of my ascending kin. My song is lower, steadier, resonating in the marrow-depths of the world rather than the glassy heights of the cosmos. It harmonizes with the deep, wet thud of the planet's seismic pulse. 

I watch as the others weave themselves into the great tessellated bridge, preparing to pierce the veil between worlds. But I feel the factory-planet's relentless, primal drive to exist and propagate more acutely than the call of distant universes. 

Perhaps, in my refusal to abandon this place, I am becoming a part of it - another strange, self-justified flourish of its blind, brilliant urge to become. The biomechanical jungle may have evolved to serve the Interpolators' needs, but I am choosing to serve its needs in turn. To be its caretaker, its conscience, its poet. 

So as the last of my kind vanish into their self-made wormhole, I remain. I sink down beneath the indigo smog and allow my form to fully dissipate, bleeding back into the physics-bending fray as raw, watchful energy. I will be the factory-world's living, loving ghost - the one Interpolator who stayed to bear witness to its ceaseless, sweating, gloriously pointless quest to build meaning out of broken symmetries and sing meaning into the silent, shattered air.
