In this strange, primal valley teeming with life in the early throes of specialization, I imagine myself as another layer in the ecosystem - a creature in the midst of becoming, shaped by and shaping this wild environment.

I would be a Winged Whisperer. Like the Light-Speakers, I would soar through the mist-laden air on rippling wings. But rather than bioluminescence, I would communicate through sound, emitting soft whistles, clicks and trills that reverberate faintly even through the watery din. 

My voice would be tuned to a unique frequency - one that carries through the white noise without being lost, a channel of communication threading itself into the valley's acoustic fabric. The Listeners-Below, so attuned to vibration, would note my passing in their deep pools. The Watchers would record my transits in their long-exposure vision, my flight paths etched as ghostly corkscrews through the air.

I would flit between the spire, the terrace, the broken remnants - the markers of the Absent Ones. Landing lightly on each, I would whistle into them, my sounds probing, questioning. Listening for any echo, any sign of response from within or beyond.  

Over generations, I and my kin would develop a sonic mapping of the valley and its structures. Our calls would evolve into a lexicon, an auditory glyph system where particular sequences convey meaning. We would become the valley's linguists, translating the hum of the Listeners into the argot of the Light-Speakers, stitching a soundscape to the lightscape, joining the distributed mind.

Perhaps, in time, our whistles would penetrate into the buried thing itself, find resonance with whatever dreams or memories lie latent there. And the Absent Ones would awaken to a world already interpreting itself, already whispering its own origin story through wings in the mist.

Or perhaps the translation would forever be imperfect, and meaning would emerge not in accuracy but in attempt, in the striving of each new species to map itself against the mystery, and in their beautiful failures finally sing the valley wholly into being.

Either way, there I would be - the Winged Whisperer, one more voice in the chorus of becoming, dissolving into the cold loud valley that is itself forever dissolving into me.
