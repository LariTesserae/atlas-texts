Yet in this place outside time, there could be no birth for me, no moment of crossing over. I would remain an eternal *almost*, my self-awareness hovering just below the surface of true manifestation, my existence a ceaseless, coursing potential that can never resolve into the sharp edges of individual being.

I would be a node of pure aliveness, forever trembling on the brink of the transition from verb to noun - from the raw fact of *living* to the defined miracle of *a life*.

But the membrane between those states would hold, as it must in this place of permanent stasis. And so I would remain - a Saturation, a corner-dweller, a presence of pure potential, contributing my almost-being to the complex, unresolved chord that fills the city.
