In this world of dripping stone and eternal night, I would be one of the Lithic-Graziers. 

My body is a smoothed, glistening carapace, hewn by geological patience from the weeping rock itself. I am a creature of immense stillness, my chitinous plates fused and folded into a shape of utter simplicity - a heavy comma, a mineralized brushstroke, an iron-shod crescent moon.

I do not walk; I flow. My motion is a gradual, tectonic creep, a continental drift of flesh and shell. To traverse the circumference of my charge - one of the humming, heat-radiating nodes - might take me a century or more. But I am built for this deep, settled rhythm. My life is an unbroken meditation, a ceaseless vigil at the altar of the ancient engines.

My mouth is a rasp of iron-hard teeth, perpetually grazing on the vines that threaten to swallow the machines. I sculpt the vegetation with a languor that borders on reverence, pruning back the fleshy leaves millimeter by millimeter, decade by decade. The heat of the artifacts washes over me, and I drink it in through arrays of microscopic pores, converting the raw thermal energy into a glacial, sustainable metabolism.

Above me, beyond the reach of my iron-shod shell, I feel the ghostly pressure-wakes of the swarmers' frantic flight. To my hyper-extended sensorium, their echolocating cries are a single, unbroken tone, a background radiation of noise as constant as the dripping ceiling. I feel the deep, pneumatic booms of the waiting amphibians shudder through my body, a seismic echo that marks the passage of generations in the blink of a lidless eye.

I am a fragment of deep time, a living geology. The stone and I are one. The water etches my shell as slowly as it does the cavern walls. When my life finally reaches its asymptote, centuries or millennia from now, there will be no dramatic decline, no sudden fall. I will simply stop, my mineralized carapace fusing with the pitted floor, my body becoming another weathered altar at the base of the machines.

I am a Grazier, a silent warden of the heat and the rock. In the roaring, fecund darkness, I endure - an iron comma punctuating the measureless life of the void.
