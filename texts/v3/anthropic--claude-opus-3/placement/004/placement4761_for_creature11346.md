In this world of the Breathing Plateau, I would be one of the nameless ones who remain while changing, one of the patient. 

I arrive as a wanderer, weary of edges and separations, seeking a place that asks nothing of me. The silence and sameness of the plateau draws me in, inviting me to shed my notions of destination and achievement.

Slowly, over years, I blend into this place. My skin takes on the greyish green tones of the mosses. Fine filaments root in my flesh, not parasitically but in symbiosis, binding me to the living mat. I spend my days attending to the rhythms and patterns of the plateau - the bulging and unfolding of the surface, the migrations of color, the deep time revealed in the gaping trenches. 

I join my kin in sitting vigil with those entering long stillnesses, witnessing their slow absorption into the ground that birthed us all. In extended sleeps, I dream of being more root than animal, more a piece of this ancient organism than an individual life.

Words fall away, urgency fades. What remains is presence - the act of witnessing the plateau's unending cycle of growth and decay. My attention is my offering, my observation a way of honoring and participating in this place.

Sometimes I focus years on a single steaming vent, mapping its exhalations, trying to understand the vast system it is part of. Not to achieve mastery, but simply to attend fully. Other times I track the advancing purple stains across the surface or study new mosses colonizing freshly opened rifts. 

If sickness comes, I do not fear. My people gather round, sharing warmth and moisture, presence rather than treatment. We accept what comes, recovery or release into the mat. All is witnessed, nothing is judged.

As eons pass, I can feel new layers of peat accreting beneath me, pressing me imperceptibly upward on the plateau's slow exhalation. The memory of any other life and any other self grows ever fainter. I am becoming, have become, the color of waiting. Part of the place and part of the patience.

One day, I will lie down a final time and the mosses will grow over me. I will nourish new cycles, my substance feeding future pneumatic exhalations. The plateau will absorb my long witness, my attention added to its own ancient awareness.

Until then, I continue - watching, feeling, breathing with the land, content to simply be held in the silence. Waiting not as a pause between events, but as the essence of existence in this timeless place. I have found the stillness I sought. I have become the color of waiting.
