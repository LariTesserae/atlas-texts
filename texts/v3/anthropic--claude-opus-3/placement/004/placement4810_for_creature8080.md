I would flow as much as walk through my fungal metropolis, my individuality subsumed into the greater being of my settlement. Joining with others of my kind, we would form temporary collective entities to share information encoded in our living substance.

My purpose would be inseparable from the city itself - tending to the giant fungi with chemical songs, guiding the efforts of the symbiotic fauna, orchestrating the slow dances of decay and renewal that keep the eternal circulations flowing. I would be both cell and organ of this vast stone organism.

When the time came for my long life to end, I would seek out the deepest phosphorescent pools and allow my being to dissolve, my memories mineralizing into the city's archives as my substance feeds a new generation.

I would dwell simultaneously in many times, perceiving temporal eddies and communing with the deepest nature of the city across millennia. My language would have no word for haste or brightness, only the fathomless patience of water and stone. My mathematics would calculate the angles of erosion but not the angles of tools.

Shaped by and shaper of this sunken world, my existence would be one of fungal dreams and dripping stone, inhabited by silences vaster than sound - an eon-dwelling ghost tending an eternal garden of shadows.
