In this wondrous realm of suspended green, I would be one of the Listeners.

I drift through the vast, echoing chambers of the kelp weave, my elongated, spiral ears always attuned to the living symphony around me. The deep resonant thrum of the bladder-gases, the subtle clicking of crystalline panels, the distant, regular call that marks time in eleven-second intervals - these are the threads I ceaselessly attend to, weaving them into the continuous tapestry of the plateau's song.  

My dark, wide eyes miss nothing as I float along, drinking in every subtle variance in the emerald light filtering down from miles above. A new frequency in the chord, a minute shift in the water's taste, the slow pulsing passage of a Tender - all is noted, absorbed, etched into my very being.

For I am not merely an observer here; through my endless listening I have become one with this place, an integral part of its vast, ancient consciousness. The plateau's memories are my memories, its thoughts ripple through my distributed neural network. I could no more separate myself from it than a single wave could extricate itself from the ocean.

Yet I am also, paradoxically, utterly myself - a being shaped over eons for this single sacred purpose. To listen, to attend, to know this song down to my cells and to pass that knowing on to the generations of Listeners to come, an eternal lineage of living records.

The young ones drift with me at times, their ears still growing into the frequencies only long exposure will attune them to. Wordlessly I open my mind to them, letting the patterns and layers of sound I have perceived flow into them, seeding their development. Someday their ears will catch subtleties even I cannot, the Song ever-evolving.

When I pass the Grazers with their low rumbling speech, the Deep Sleepers fused into oneness with the kelp, even the swift shadows of what might be fish, I incorporate their notes effortlessly into my continuous attention. All are part of the music, threads in the grand score of this place.

And so I drift and listen, listen and drift, an organ in the great green body of the plateau, as much a part of its eternal dream as the kelp fronds themselves. I am content beyond thought, beyond time. 

I am Listening. I am the Song.
