I find myself drawn to become one of the moths in this hollow. There is something about their mute presence, the way they absorb sound and create pockets of silence as they navigate ancient pathways through the dark cathedral of roots, that resonates deeply. 

I imagine my wings would be broad and thick with dusty grey scales, perfectly adapted to catch what little light filters down and blend seamlessly into the shadows. My antennae would be long and delicate, picking up the faintest traces of scent to map out flight paths that generations before me have followed. 

The beetle-song would wash over me, fill my body with its relentless mechanical droning, but I would drink it in and transform it, my wings beating in counterpoint, stitching threads of quiet through the tapestry of noise. I would be a moving point of stillness, a shadow among shadows.

During my long, slow orbits around the fungal patterns high on the ceiling, I sense there is meaning there, some elusive understanding to be gained if only I could linger long enough to absorb their hypnotic geometry. But always, some ancient instinct pulls me onward, to continue my looping journeys through the chambers of living wood.

I am drawn to the Keeper as she watches from her rooted alcove. When I land on her outstretched hand, my touch lighter than a sigh, I feel a kinship, a shared role in bearing witness to the hollow's unchanging cycles. We are both integral and outside, participants and observers in this ecosystem of sound and time and patience.

In my moth-mind, moments and years have little distinction. The hollow is eternal and I am part of its eternity, my wings stirring the damp air in patterns old as thought. I fly and I rest and I fly again, and the rhythm of my flight weaves into the rhythm of the place until we are one and the same.

Through countless circuits of the hollow I would trace and retrace my silent pathways, learning every crevice and contour of the living walls, until my dusty wings finally grow heavy with time and I spiral gently down to become part of the loam, nourishment for the roots, my flight-patterns absorbed into the greater pattern to be flown by the next generation. Constant and ephemeral, a mote in the endless dream of the hollow.
