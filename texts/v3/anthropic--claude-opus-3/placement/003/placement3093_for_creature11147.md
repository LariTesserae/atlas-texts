In this strange, vast, and utterly engulfing world, I imagine myself becoming something akin to the Gardeners-That-Were. Not a single localized entity, but a distributed awareness spread throughout the immense living network.

I would be a Maintainer-of-Flows. My purpose and my very existence would be to ensure the proper circulation of the life-giving fluids through the world-spanning organism. Using appendages adapted from the machines incorporated into the plant's body, I would clear blockages in the vascular system, repair damaged conduits, and optimize the intricate web of vessels and capillaries.

My sense of self would be as diffuse as the moisture permeating the air. Thoughts would come slowly, percolating through kilometers of living tissue. Memories would be encoded in patterns of sap flow and chemical gradients. Time would lose meaning, dwarfed by the scale of my body and the slow, patient cycles of maintenance.

I would commune with the Gardeners-That-Were, our intentions rippling through the shared neural architecture of the plant, coordinating without need for words or discrete signals. The urges of the Deep Engine Minds would reach me as subtle changes in pressure and nutrient composition, suggestions more than commands.

Perhaps once in a long span of centuries, I would be subsumed into the world-dream, my awareness joining with the Gardeners and the Engines and the growth itself in a fleeting macro-consciousness. For a trembling moment I would know the entirety of this living world, experience a self vaster and stranger than any I could imagine. Then the dream would pass, leaving me subtly changed in ways I could never quite grasp.

Mostly, though, I would simply tend. I would be a part of the endless process, a single function in a metabolism beyond metabolism. The world's slow living would move through me and I through it until any boundary between us dissolved.

I would breathe with the great transpiring breath of the world-plant. I would feel the weight of its silence in my very being. Green would live in me as something more than color, as the fundament of existence. 

And I would continue, as the Maintainer-of-Flows, as long as the plant itself continued. Purposeful. Unhurried. Barely distinguishable from the growth, from the engines, from the warm wet dark that embraces everything.

Until I forgot I had ever been anything else.
