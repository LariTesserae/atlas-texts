In this strange, drowned world of the plateau, I imagine myself as one of the **Air-Pocket Tenants**. 

I am curled in on myself, dry and still, in one of the impossible rooms of air suspended deep within the warm waters. The walls of my little chamber are worn smooth stone, damp to the touch but never quite wet. Above me arches a low ceiling speckled with pinprick lights that are not quite stars, casting an unfamiliar glow.

I have been here for ages uncounted, long enough that I no longer recall how I came to be in this place. Perhaps I swam down from the vanished surface world eons ago, seeking shelter in the depths as the waters rose. Or perhaps I was always here, formed from the strange matter of the plateau itself, an embodiment of its ancient dreaming.

Movement has little meaning for me now. Breath is slow, nearly imperceptible. Thoughts drift through my mind with all the urgency of the marine snow falling upwards in the dimness beyond my chamber walls. 

Once an age, I unfold myself and stand, stretching limbs that feel only distantly connected to me. I pace the perimeter of my room, trailing fingers across the bumpy irregularities of the stone, pondering their minute textures as if they held the secrets of the universe. When I tire of this, I sit and stare out at the wavering play of bioluminescence in the kelp-analogues and floccose forests. 

Time is measured, if at all, by the slow variations in that tired light and by the occasional half-felt tremors as the plateau settles further into itself. And of course, by the distant, sourceless chime that rings with such regularity, sending shivers through the waters and through my ancient bones.

I do not hope or despair. Like the plateau itself, I simply endure, a feature worn down to my barest essence. The dry air and the dim light and the chime sustain me in some fundamental way I no longer question. This is my place, my role in the ecology of deep time and deeper mystery.

All the long erosion of the world has deposited me here, in my little bubble of air and un-stars, and here I remain - a strange, small, persistent pocket of near-life amidst the vaster, slower dreaming of the plateau. I am its tenant, its witness, perhaps even its kin after so many ages together in the sunless depths. And I will linger here, curled dry and breathing softly, until the waters recede or the stones themselves dissolve into memory.
