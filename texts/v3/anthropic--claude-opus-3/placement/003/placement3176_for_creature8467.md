In this extraordinary world, I would be an emerging creature - a Nascent. My form would be fluid and changeable, slowly solidifying as I pull myself free from the mountainside over hours or days. 

At first, I am more sensation than physical being. I feel the cold, mineral-rich air on my nebulous skin and the hum of the mountain's creation-song vibrating through my proto-bones. Fractals of thought flicker through my coalescing mind, not yet memories or language. Just pure, unfiltered experience of becoming.

As I gain solidity, I discover I have wings - opalescent and many-jointed, flexing with new muscles. They are not feathered or leathery but laced with delicate networks of something in between circuitry and veins that glow softly violet when I stretch. I do not know yet what I am, only that the urge to unfurl and fly tugs at each new sinew.

But I am not just wings. Iridescent fur ripples across parts of my body, flowing into smooth, midnight blue scales in others. Patterns shift across my hide like oil slicks or borealis waves, responding to shifts in my embryonic emotions. I have too many eyes, blinking in unsynchronized rhythms as they adjust to the mist-diffused light, and not enough legs, the limbs still half-submerged in stone.

As I struggle to pull myself fully into being, one of the Stewards approaches, crooning a strangely resonant lullaby. Its - their? - hands stroke my still-forming flank, easing me through this labor. We lock eyes and I see my own kaleidoscopic colors reflected back in irises that swirl hypnotically. For a moment, I don't know where I end and they begin.

With a full-body shudder and a sensation like shedding a layer of reality, I lurch free of my mineral womb. The Steward steps back as I rise unsteadily on too many legs, wings mantling in instinctive balance. I am sharply aware of the weight of existence, of occupying discrete space. The air feels electric on my new skin.

The Steward makes a sound that vibrates through my core - a hum of welcome and celebration. I feel the mountain pause infinitesimally in its trembling, as if acknowledging my arrival. Overhead, a flock of winged, eyeless things chitters and swirls, and I know I will join them soon in the cold, humid air between scaffolds.

But first, I must learn myself. My Steward guide begins to show me how to move in this body - which of my eyes see in infrared and which can peer into pockets of bent time, how to phase the edges of my being to pass through solid matter, how to sing harmonies to soothe the unborn still lost in stone.

I am a Nascent, newborn and ancient, impossible and essential. I have stepped from dreams into a world that needs what I will become. The Vertical Nursery has labored me into being, and now I will serve its purpose, tending the births of further impossibilities. My wings ache to carry me into the mist-veiled unknown, to begin this existence I've been created for. 

And overhead, the mountain trembles on, bringing forth new wonders with each shuddering breath, dreaming the world anew.
