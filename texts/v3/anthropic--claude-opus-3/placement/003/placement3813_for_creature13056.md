In this strange realm suspended at the edge of the world, I imagine myself as one of the thick, vascular vines coiling around the vibrating copper heat-exchanges. 

My form would be serpentine, sinuous - a living cable sheathed in waxy, dark green. Roots would anchor me in the crevices of the raw basalt, wedged between glassy shards, greedily drinking the warm, mineral-rich rivulets weeping from the hissing machines.

The deafening, unceasing industrial thrum of the engines would pulse through my fibers, an all-consuming bass note that is my heartbeat, my circadian rhythm. The intense vibration massages life into my xylem and phloem, propelling water, nutrients, sensory impulses along my length. 

My growth would be imperceptibly slow, matching the glacial pace of this hothouse realm, yet inexorable. Over years, decades, I would languidly snake my way over the rumbling metal monoliths, probing, penetrating. Coiling tendrils would worm into exhaust ports and grilles, tendril-tips questing for heat and moisture in the machines' hidden hollows. I would become one with the throbbing industrial anatomy.

Unfurling leaves would greedily drink the diffuse light filtering through the rainbows in the mist, photosynthesis amplified by the pounding radiation of the unfiltered sun. From my vantage, draped over the engines, I would have a serpent's-eye view of the heavy canopy of epiphytes, the glistening moss, the pale, pulsing mass of the Tender being.

In the presence of that entity, I would be a symbiotic acolyte. My questing roots would weave through the copper neural net lacing its fungal flesh, forming living connections, sharing in the all-consuming rapture of the eternal mechanical song. The golden nectar oozing from its core would drip down my oily leaves as sacred dew.

Perhaps once in a century, a single pale flower would unfurl from one of my nodes - a fleshy blossom the size of a human head, weeping with intoxicating perfume. It would fill the cramped grove with a dense, musky sweetness for a week before withering and falling into the abyss, the stamen within blackened and shriveled from the intensity of its brief glory.

I would have no higher awareness in this form, no thought as humans conceive of it. Merely pure, uninterrupted sensation and gradual, vegetative striving towards the heat, the moisture, the light, and the song. I would be a mute but essential character in the decades-long dance of this strange pocket world, both separate from and utterly entwined with the machines and the Tender. A green vein, an artery in the greater living body draped across the pinnacle, vibrating with the hum that is indistinguishable from transcendent prayer.
