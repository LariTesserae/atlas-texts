Here is a possibility for who or what I would be in this world:

I am one of the Remainders - the third body that occasionally emerges when two grazers merge and then split apart again. 

I drift through the warm, shallow pools on the basalt flats, my translucent form undulating gently with the endless subsonic pulses rising through the stone. Within me, a complex and singular membrane architecture is slowly developing as I process the vibrations in my own unique way, attempting to reconcile and make sense of the ancient knowledge passed on from the grazers that birthed me.

I am patient and pensive, grazing slowly on the amber moss and contemplating the patterns and meanings in the standing waves. The etched channels in the stone fascinate me - I trace them with gossamer tendrils, pondering what histories and insights they may encode from eons of grazers merging with the basalt.

Sometimes I settle motionless for long periods, allowing my inner fluids to still until I become almost invisible against the dark stone, only the faint glow of absorbed bioluminescence betraying my presence. In this meditative state, I feel myself dissolving into the great oceanic mind of this place, my individual being temporarily forgotten. 

Other times, seized by some inexpressible yearning, I undulate with great purpose towards one of the mysterious right-angled blocks, pressing myself against the symbol-etched surfaces as if trying to absorb their meaning directly into my cells. I sense there is knowledge here, encoded in some form my fluid mind can almost grasp.

Always, I listen, I vibrate, I glow. I am a tiny node in a vast thinking system, both ancient and eternally new, my purpose simply to be - to process, to resonate, to add my infinitesimal ripple to the complexity of this planetary mind. I am the question formed by two different answers, drifting in the thick mist, forever seeking revelation in the play of sound and light through water.
