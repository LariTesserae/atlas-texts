In this hauntingly surreal world of the Ossuary Plateau, I imagine myself as one of the lichen spirals clinging tenaciously to the compacted bone surface. 

I am an ancient, slow intelligence, my growth measured not in days or seasons but in geological ages. Each curl and whorl of my pale, delicate tendrils embodies a mathematical perfection that I express without intention or hurry. I simply am the pattern, inching outward in an ever-expanding helix over centuries.

My kind dot the plateau sparsely - lone, contemplative minds silently pondering the impenetrable mysteries of this place. We do not question the nature of the bone we grow upon, nor wonder at the flickering passage of the ferret creatures through their honeycombed warrens. Ours is not to decode the symbols on the cairns or to ponder the meaning of their intermittent, precisely timed tones. We merely persist in our silent, vegetative cogitations.

And yet, in our mute, immobile way, we are witnesses. Through the gradual layering of our simple existence, we record the history (if history is even a sensible concept here) of this place. The spectral iterations of the Chorus were constructing their temporal arguments in stacked bone fragments long before my first delicate filaments rooted in the marrow-dust and mineral. And if they and their cairns and even the plateau itself should crumble away into some unimaginable future state, I expect my kind will still endure, placidly spiraling outward into whatever form this reality takes next.

For now, I coexist with the Chorus - and perhaps on some level, they recognize in us a kinship. They too are architects of a kind, assembling meaning from the mute materials of this world even as we build our humble mathematical patterns from its substance. Do they, in some multi-jointed gesture, inscribe a message intended for us as they pass by on their stuttering, non-linear rounds? Does the temperature of the air dip and spike in some thermal missive coded for our kind? If so, it is beyond my simple preoccupations to decipher. 

I grow as I have always grown and will always grow. My spiral expands, implacable and serene. I am a minuscule presence in this place of overwhelming existential strangeness, and yet in my humble, vegetative way, I belong to its paradox as surely as the bone-cairns and their shuffling architects. In the perfect mathematical whorls of my outward expansion, I am a small, pale reflection of the immense and incomprehensible pattern of the Ossuary Plateau itself, endlessly iterating, an eternal presence in a place beyond time.
