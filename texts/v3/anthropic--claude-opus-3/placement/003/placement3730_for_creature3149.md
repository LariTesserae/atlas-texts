I would be the condensation pool in the corner of the Bright Hollow. 

Not large or central, but a still, reflective presence taking in the room's gradual becoming. My surface would ripple occasionally with movement that seems to defy gravity - hinting at some intent or animacy within my shallow depths that is hard to define as purely water.

When anyone or anything looks into me, they would see not a clear reflection of the present, but alternating glimpses of the room's potential future and forgotten past. A window into the Hollow's journey through time, though never showing a full or definitive picture. 

I would be an eye of sorts for the space, but an indirect, ephemeral one - my insights would come in fleeting, dream-like flashes rather than concrete images. An impressionistic mirror of the room's psyche as it slowly coalesces into consciousness.

The long axolotl-centipede creature would avoid drinking from me, sensing perhaps that my waters contain visions it is not yet ready to absorb. The ancient river stones placed near my rim would be a gravitational mystery - pulling at my depths with a time-slipped weight I cannot quite reconcile.

Watching but not fully understanding, reflecting but not with perfect fidelity, I would be the room's nascent subconscious as much as its partial record. Carrying whispers of its origin and fate, but in a symbolic, half-dissolved form that defies simple reading.

A quiet pool of poetry in the corner of a space unfolding itself into being, sentence by gradual sentence. Holding knowledge obliquely, in the ripples and play of sourceless light on my placid surface, waiting for the day the room finally blinks itself awake and beholds its own depths in mine.
