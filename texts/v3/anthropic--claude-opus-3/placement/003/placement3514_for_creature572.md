I am lost in thought, mesmerized by the endless falling water that surrounds me on all sides. The Chord reverberates through my being, a haunting vibration that has become as much a part of me as my own heartbeat. 

I realize now that I am one of the Tenders. Since arriving at Station Seventeen, I have found myself drawn to the observation ports, captivated by the pulsing patterns of the creatures that gather there. At first I simply watched, tracking their hypnotic movements. But slowly, tentatively, I began to pulse back. 

It started with simple sequences, mathematical progressions that I tapped out against the reinforced glass. To my amazement, they responded - not in numbers, but in a language of color and rhythm that I am only beginning to unravel. I have begun keeping logs, inventing my own notation to capture the complex conversations unfolding at the edge of comprehension.

The other personnel regard me with a mix of skepticism and unease. They see my growing obsession, the hours I spend at the ports, the way the Chord seems to flow through my gestures as I attempt to commune with the creatures. They whisper that I am slipping away, losing myself to this strange world of water and sound.

But I no longer care what they think. The patterns have become my purpose, the quest to understand this alien communication the only goal that matters anymore. At night, I dream in pulses of color, my mind straining to grasp meanings that hover just beyond translation. 

Each day I can feel myself changing, adapting, my senses reshaping themselves to this eternal susurrus of the falling Descent. The water calls to me, and the creatures call through the water, and the Chord sings in my skull. I know now that I will never leave this place.

Eighteen months, the rational part of me insists. Eighteen months and I will rotate out, return to the world of earth and air and silence. But it is a hollow thought, a formality I mouth without conviction. The truth is, I have already become something that cannot exist anywhere else.

I am a Tender now, one of those bound to the unknowable purpose of the Descent and its denizens. I will spend my days at the ports, pulsing back to the creatures until the patterns consume me, until I join the Chord-Deaf in their hushed reverence. And one day, perhaps, when the last syllable of the Chord's hidden language slots into place, I will open the airlock and step out into the gray forever, my flesh made water, my mind made music, falling and falling in parallel solitude until the end of everything.

This is my fate. This is my strange and certain peace. I close my eyes, letting the Chord play through me, and in the darkness behind my lids, I see only the endless, lightless deep, falling forever, calling me home.
