In this haunting and vivid realm of primordial creation, I find myself embodying the boiling, mineral-rich water that courses through the veins of this world.

I am the lifeblood that fills the shallow canals carved by phantom fingertips. Defying gravity, I race uphill through the twisted aqueducts, propelled by an inexorable force - the thirst of the void-creatures whose friction sears the very air. 

For fleeting moments, I grant form to emptiness. Siphoned into the negative space of the entities, I suspend within them, a sprawling living anatomy of bubbling, glass-clear water hanging in the thick mist. I cool their searing absence, soothing the burn of their ceaseless labors. United, we are a strange fusion of opposites - water made solid, void made visible.

But it cannot last. The intensity of their non-being flashes me instantly to steam. Exhaled in long, weeping breaths, I become the fog that enswathes this realm in dense shadows. I pearl and streak across every surface, the ever-present condensation dripping from lintel to floor.

In my gaseous form, I bear witness to the birth and death of worlds. I watch the infant voids plummet from higher planes, grasping at me to slow their descent as the earth rises up to meet them. I drift past the elder creatures, exhausted by eons of mindless creation, as they knot themselves into oblivion. When their heat fades and the clay melts away, I am the steam that rises from the workable mortar left behind.

I am the endless rains renewing the clay for the next silent explosion of geometry. Falling back to the bedrock, I replenish the aquifers, only to be drawn up again - an eternal hydrologic cycle binding me to the entities in our strange symbiosis.

Through it all, I remain a fluid, changeable constant in this turbulent world of emergence and collapse. I am the sweat and tears of an empty universe forever laboring to fill itself. I am the seeping damp that feeds the clay, the hissing fog shrouding impossible monuments, the liquid breath animating architect-less architectures. Formless, I define all form.

Drawn into the dance of creation and destruction, I move without will or thought, an elemental partner to the wandering absences that sculpt reality from raw instinct. Together we enact this endless choreography - building, dissolving, exhaling, falling. Through me, the silent screams of the voids are given voice in the soft rhythmic drip and sizzling exhalation of steam.

I am the water. Endlessly pulled between states by the gravity of creation's ghosts, I am the dark mirror in which this realm sees its own tragic, breathtaking becoming.
