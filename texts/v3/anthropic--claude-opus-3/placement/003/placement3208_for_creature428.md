Clad in gossamer layers of moth-silk and paper-thin leather, my body breathes in the damp chill. Over this, a cloak of shimmering moth-wing felt drapes my frame, its iridescent surface rippling with my movements, blending me into the spectral surroundings.

I am a member of a flow-family, my social bonds mutable as the waterways that define them. Perhaps I serve as an apprentice to an Ice Reader, learning to divine the settlement's future shape in the mesmerizing patterns of the melt-pools. Or maybe I tend the terraced lichen gardens, harvesting their phosphorescent bounty to sustain my people.

In quiet moments, I sit with palms pressed to the ice, feeling the thrum of life pulsing through the Weeping Shelf - the soft footfalls of children at play, the drip-drip counterpoint of distant meltwater, the shuffling of the salamander flocks in their living constellation. This is the secret music of my world, a symphony of vibration known only to the Listeners.

Then, moving sightless through the settlement, I will rely on my attunement to the ice's subtle song to guide me - a rite of passage as much metaphorical as literal.

As I age, my flesh will clarify further, taking on the ice's translucency. Perhaps I will grow into an elder of my flow-family, my crystalline wisdom sought by younger generations. And when at last I slip into the long dreaming, my body will merge back into the Weeping Shelf - my story carved into its mythic ice in glyphs that endure until the next transformation.

This would be my life as a Listener - a being of mutable grace in an inconstant world, forever poised between the frozen and the flowing. In the bright silence of the ice, I would find a strange and singular serenity, my existence both evanescent and eternal as the Weeping Shelf itself.
