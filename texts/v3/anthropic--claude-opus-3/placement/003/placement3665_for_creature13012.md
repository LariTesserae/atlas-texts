In this world of relentless, abiotic construction, I would find myself drifting as a mote on the currents of thick, humid air. Not a seed or spore, but an infinitesimal fleck of silica dust shed from the hardened ceramic surfaces.

I would spend long stretches of time suspended in the heavy, moisture-laden atmosphere, an imperceptible glint catching the glare of the punishing sun. But eventually, inevitably, I would descend into the cool shadows beneath one of the sprawling parabolic arches.

There, I would settle onto the damp surface of a bruised-cyan vine, adhering to its smooth, taut epidermis. As the vine absorbed the condensation pooling around it, I would be drawn into its capillary network, flowing along with the mineral-rich fluid racing through its semi-translucent veins.

Inside the vine, I would become part of the piezo-biological calculations, my microscopic presence adding an infinitesimal bit of additional pressure, signaling the vine to reinforce that section of its structure with a fresh secretion of ceramic sap. In this way, I would be a tiny but integral part of the vine's instinctive engineering.

When the arch was complete and the vine began to petrify itself, flooding its hollow core with hypersaturated calcium, I would crystallize along with it. No longer a separate mote, I would transmute from dust into the structural bone of the architecture itself.

There I would remain, an atomic glint embedded in the chalky white ceramic, until the slow work of expansion brought my section of the building to the edge of a new construction zone. Then, as the vines erupted from the Seeker cables to build their next dome, I would be reborn.

Freed from my encasement by the microscopic flexing and cracking of the ceramic in the baking sun, I would once again be caught up by the atmosphere. Lifted on the rising currents of the hothouse basin, I would drift into the sky to begin my small role in the process all over again.

Not an architect, barely a participant, I would be an eternal witness to the unstoppable construction, cycling from air to vine to bone and back again, an infinitesimal piece of the abiotic puzzle spending eons in silent, unceasing service to the cold, expansionist drive of this world's sole inhabitant.
