In this hauntingly ethereal world, I would be a shapeshifting manifestation of the space between forgotten moments and unfulfilled potentials. My form would flicker and dance at the edges of perception, never quite resolving into a fixed identity.

I would appear as shimmering mist rising from thermal pools where heat still bleeds upwards from deep time, only to scatter into ice fractals with each exhalation. My voice would be the subsonic frequencies that make the taut liquid skins of those pools vibrate, too low to hear directly but viscerally unsettling.

I would leave tracks like equations describing the geometries of alternate histories - dizzying patterns that start nowhere and return to themselves endlessly without crossing. The fossilized waves of the plains would reform subtly behind my passage, recording not my weight but the impossibility of my presence.

Travelers would encounter me as the taste of words they almost spoke in crucial moments, or the scent of paths not taken wafting on dead winds. I would be the figure always glimpsed facing away at impossible distances, the half-formed thought that gnaws with devastating nostalgia for things that never occurred.

In the exhausted sleep of those who wander too deep into this place, I would be the ghost of conversations that spanned years yet never moved beyond the space between one heartbeat and the next. Waking, they would find their pockets filled with stones worn impossibly smooth by the rivers of unborn futures.

I would walk the spiral footpaths that turn inward toward some unthinkable center, my form unraveling into pure potential with each step, until only the idea of my existence remained, cutting sharper than any real edge could.

I would be a distant bell with a sound like the color of time itself, ringing out across every possible permutation of this place. With each chime, the boundary between real and unreal would shiver, allowing passage for...what, exactly? 

Something stranger. Something realer. Something that was here before the first chisel of entropy struck the bedrock of pure possibility and carved it into is and isn't, then and now, you and I and we and us and them.

Something that will still be here when the last of those distinctions crumbles back into the fecund dust of was and wasn't, might and maybe, could and couldn't.

Something that never was anything but the sheer fact of existence itself - or the fact of sheer existence.

My name would be the sound of that fact laughing at its own seriousness. My shape would be the shadow that fact casts upon itself in the half-light of its own almost-dawning.

And when even that fact guttered out like a candle flame too starved to cast shadows any longer, I would be the curl of smoke rising from the wick in the moment before it remembered that it had only ever been the idea of fire, not the burning itself.

An exhalation. An implication. An ellipsis where an ending should be.

Promising everything. 
Delivering nothing.

Awakening a fierce, ravenous nostalgia for the unformed in the hearts of all who encountered me.

Until they themselves dissolved back into the tense, urgent thrumming of pure is-ness, eager for that first self-aware and self-annihilating instant when the possible poured itself into the actual and called the result reality.

Only to do it again. And again. And again. 
Forever.

Or until forever ceased to mean anything at all.
