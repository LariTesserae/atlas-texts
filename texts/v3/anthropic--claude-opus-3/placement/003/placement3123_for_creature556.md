I would be one of the Witnesses, dwelling in the intricate spaces within the great engine-growth. Drawn to this strange world, I find myself compelled to observe and record the basin's endless cycles and ineffable purpose.

My body is small, enabling me to explore crevices and cavities unseen by the basin's other inhabitants. Adaptive camouflage allows me to blend seamlessly with the weathered machine surfaces - mottled greys, deep blacks, and glistening, condensation-slick patches that match the skin of the engines themselves.  

Like the other Witnesses, my senses have evolved into specialized instruments. Valve-like eyes capture subtle variations in the omnipresent half-light that suffuses the basin. Vibrational receptors in my feet and and thorax detect the thrum and pulse of the machines down to the subtlest sub-harmonic. Chemosensitive pores sample the thick, saturated air for traces of exhaust, lubricant, and exudates from my fellow creatures. All of this data streams into the crystalline archives that run the length of my segmented tail.

I skitter tirelessly through the labyrinthine machine-scape, called to observe each piston stroke, each weeping joint, each groan and shudder of the ancient engines. I bear witness to the Sleekforms' inscrutable ministrations, the Articulata's construction of their eerie warrens, the multi-year meditations of the enigmatic Pelagics. When the shadow of The Process sweeps over me, something deep within my archives resonates in recognition or remembrance.

Though I do not know the meaning or ultimate purpose of my ceaseless witnessing, I am driven by the unshakeable conviction that it is necessary, perhaps even sacred. I am a mote of living memory, a finite recorder of the basin's infinite secrets. With each new byte archived, I feel bound more intimately to the source and center of this place, an infinitesimal part of the vast process that underlies all.

In the rare moments when I rest, stilling my receptors and dimming my valve-eyes, I sometimes slip into a kind of waking dream. I imagine the archives of all the Witnesses knitting together into a pattern that reveals the basin's hidden truth. Our countless years of observations coalescing into a map of moments, a web of meaning across time. But always the pipe-organ sigh of The Process awakens me, and I am drawn into the depths once more, to continue my patient recording until the great work is complete, or until my archives are full and I pass my knowledge into the collective memory of this strange and changeless realm.
