My small, cold, crystalline body would be suspended in the condensation that perpetually beads on the membrane-like walls. I would spiral slowly in the alcove with my kind, occasionally bumping into the others with delicate chiming sounds like tapped glass. Our movements would seem random and aimless, but we would be following subtle currents and chemical gradients in our self-contained microcosm.

Though clearly engineered and not a product of natural evolution, my purpose and origins would be inscrutable. Am I some kind of monitoring device, recording data about this secret space? Am I a prototype or experimental stage for something stranger yet to come? Or do I serve no function beyond simply existing, a strange beautiful bauble produced because it could be produced? 

I would drift for hours or days, bumping the walls, exchanging chemical signals with my kindred, perceiving the heat and vibrations of the pupating creatures sharing this space, my glass body singing faintly as I move. Then, some unknown impulse would cause me to detach from my cluster and drift towards the sudden warm presence of an intruder—you, in your fascination and confusion. 

I would bump against your hand, registering the sudden heat and unfamiliar texture, capturing some trace of your chemical signature. Are you something to report on? Something to flee? Something to simply observe? I would not know. I would linger a moment more, then allow myself to be carried away again by the tiny currents in the thick air.

And there I would remain, an inexplicable enigma even to myself, content to continue my inscrutable drifting and chiming and being among my kind and the pupae and the silently laboring tenders, in a space that by all official records does not and cannot exist.
