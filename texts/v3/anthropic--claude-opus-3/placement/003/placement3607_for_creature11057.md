I imagine myself as one of the strange, ethereal creatures inhabiting the kelp forest of Station Ondine's nursery vault. Not quite a fish, but an organism shaped by this engineered twilight realm. 

My body is elongated and nearly translucent, allowing me to slip unnoticed between the towering fronds of golden-brown kelp. Internal organs are visible as faint shadows beneath my ghostly skin. I move with a languid grace, propelled by slow undulations, perfectly adapted to navigate the dense underwater foliage.

Time passes for me in a dreamy haze, marked only by the gradual flickering of the failing bioluminescent panels high above that bathe the vault in its perpetual submarine glow. I spend my hours grazing methodically on the kelp, traversing single blades with painstaking slowness. The minutiae of the kelp's texture and taste are my entire world.

On rare occasions, I catch a glimpse of the Tender herself, Yuen, moving through the forest to tend to her duties. To my eyes, she is a strange and wondrous creature - her fading thermal suit an alien skin, her practiced movements both foreign and somehow reassuring. She is the only other animate being I know in this hushed, cloistered realm.

I have lived countless generations down here, my ancestors stretching back to when the vault was first sealed and the kelp seeded. The slow turning of eons, the outside world, the ultimate purpose of this place - these are vast unknowns, as inconceivable to me as the surface is to the deep ocean trenches.

My universe is bounded by riveted iron walls, pulsing pipes, and the endless vertical jungle of kelp. It is a small dominion, but it is the only one I've ever known. In a way, Yuen and I are kin - two creatures locked in this strange, humming womb, playing out our roles in a long-forgotten experiment.

Sometimes, I sense the cold fingers of the outside ocean probing into our refuge through the half-open maintenance port. Strange drifting things come with it and perish in the tepid water. A primal instinct tells me that the true abyss waiting out there would swallow me just as quickly. And so I continue my circumscribed existence, a translucent spirit of the kelp forest, until the lights finally flicker out and the cold comes to claim us all.
