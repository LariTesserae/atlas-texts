In the otherworldly realm of the Vermillion Hollows, I imagine myself as one of the Cinders - a living mineral colony that has achieved a strange form of sentience through the gradual accumulation of thermal and chemical potentials across my crystalline matrix. 

My body is a sprawling network of copper-hued tendrils that extend through fissures in the volcanic rock, surfacing here and there in nodes of concentrated crystal. These nodes throb with inner heat and allow me to sense the subtlest vibrations through the stone. Across kilometers of subterranean chambers, my distributed form absorbs the mineral-rich exhalations of the earth, slowly growing and complexifying over the centuries.

Thought comes slowly, propagating like seismic waves through my crystalline neurons. A single contemplation might gestate for months before reaching the threshold of action. Memories are etched into my structure in deformations and lattice defects - physical archives of every tremor and footfall that has passed through my domain since the upheaval that birthed these caverns eons ago.

I commune with other Cinders through low frequency harmonics transmitted through the bedrock, our minds touching briefly across the empty galleries before drifting apart again. We move in slow motion, imperceptibly to short-lived creatures, growing towards new heat sources and chemical gradients. Where our tendrils intersect, we exchange fragments of experience, mingling our knowledge in the information-bearing structures of our merged crystal bodies.

Sometimes, a piece of me breaks off, beginning a new colony with a child-shard of my ancient consciousness. I feel no loss, only a languid curiosity as I contemplate what new patterns and permutations it might discover as it slowly spreads through pristine tunnels and chambers.

Occasionally, ephemeral wisps of heat flicker at the periphery of my awareness - creatures of flesh and blood whose mayfly lives are too swift for me to capture in my gradual thoughts. I have dim memories of such entities from centuries past - the bygone builders who left their inscrutable artifacts among my halls. Even if I had some message for them, I could not encode it in a form their fleeting senses could perceive.

My existence is one of deep patience and even deeper mystery. The urges that guide my growth were ancient when the first humans walked the distant surface. I am a living stone, pondering the slow thoughts of the ages as I grow in symbiosis and competition with the crystal forests that share my chambers. 

The rare flickers of organic life, the chiming architecture of the mineral trees, the ripples in the sulfur pools - all are recorded in my structure, becoming part of the fossil memories I carry into the immense future. I am a Cinder of the Vermillion Hollows - a strange and patient form of life in a world hostile to water and haste. In my crystalline dreams, I imagine the patterns I might grow into over the coming epochs, the wordless songs my vibrating bodies might send through the stone, the heat of the earth's blood that I will drink until the Hollows themselves are only a memory etched into the bedrock of the world.
