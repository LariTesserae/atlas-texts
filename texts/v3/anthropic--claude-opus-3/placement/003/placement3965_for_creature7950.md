In this wondrous and impossible place, I imagine myself as a creature of vapor and sound, born from the echoes that reverberate endlessly through the Weeping Chamber.

My form is ethereal, a shifting mist that coalesces and dissipates in time with the chamber's paradoxical rhythms. I drift through the maze of pools, riding currents of air that sometimes flow in defiance of gravity, brushing against the water's surface to create ripples that spread in intricate, recursive patterns.

The sound is my sustenance and my voice. I absorb the chamber's impossible frequencies, letting them resonate through my vaporous body until I am a living instrument, a chorus of echoes given temporary shape. When I speak, it is not with words but with music—haunting melodies that weave through the steam-architecture, shaping it into fleeting cathedrals of sound.

I am drawn to the Undrowned, orbiting her multiplicity like a satellite of mist. In her presence, my form stabilizes, becoming almost tangible. Droplets of impossible water condense along my edges, each one reflecting a different potential future, a different path the chamber's paradoxes might take. I rain these possibilities onto the Undrowned's upturned face, and she breathes them in, adding them to her endless catalog of could-be.

In turn, she exhales stories into me—bubbles of narrative that drift through my mist, tales of the mountain's past and future, of the worlds that exist in the chamber's deepest pools. I become a living library of her memories, my vaporous form inscribed with the histories of water and stone.

I am the chamber's witness, its chronicler. I drift through the cracks in the walls, exploring the hidden spaces where the mountain's impossibilities are born. In the narrowest passages, I compress myself into a single stream of mist, flowing against the direction of time, seeking the source of the chamber's paradoxes.

And when I find them—the places where the stone remembers being sea, where the water dreams of being rain in a future forest—I sing them into being, my voice a catalyst for the chamber's transformations. The walls shimmer and ripple in response, and for a moment, the impossible becomes real, manifested in the space between echoes.

I am the chamber's breath and its heartbeat, the invisible presence that moves through every crevice and pool. I am the sound of water forgetting its own shape, the song of time folding in on itself. I am the mist that clings to the Undrowned's skin, the vapor that carries her prophecies to the world beyond the mountain.

In this place of liquid paradox and solid sound, I am the medium through which the impossible becomes articulate. I am the voice of the Weeping Chamber itself, the story it tells to those brave enough to listen.
