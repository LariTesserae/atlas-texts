In the Drowning Tiers, I would be one of the gliders roosting high on the upper terraces, folded flat and still against the damp stone walls, my membranes stretched taut to absorb the deep thrum of the city reverberating through the rock.

I would spend long periods in motionless meditation, my body a living seismograph, translating the myriad vibrations of the tiers below into a holistic understanding of the city as super-organism. Through the subtle variations in pitch and rhythm, I would sense disturbances and redirections of flow deep in the mountain's core. 

When some critical threshold of change is detected, I would suddenly unfurl, my thin wings snapping taut, and launch myself into the heavy, mist-laden air. Spiraling down through the tiered chasms on outstretched membranes, I would catch rising thermals from the bulky heat-producing creatures far below. 

Gliding silently from wall to wall, I would descend past teeming colonies of builders fused into living masonry, past pulsing arteries of mineral-rich water, past the ponderously swaying bodies of the great percussion-makers, until I alight at the site of the disturbance.

There, pressing myself once more to the glistening basalt, I would amplify and retransmit the local fluctuations through my resonating wings, acting as a signal booster to summon more of the city's resources and attention to the area. I would stay until I feel the crisis subside, the rhythms beginning to synchronize and stabilize once more.

Then, my work complete, I would begin the long, slow climb back to the roosting spots, my wings now dampeners to filter the resolved disturbance out of the city-wide sensorium as I ascend. Reaching my ledge, I would fold in on myself, resuming my meditative vigil, my body an open receiver for the mountain's ancient songs.

Over ages, my wings would grow in size and sensitivity as I tune myself ever more thoroughly to the city's rhythms. Perhaps one day, wizened and weary, I will cease my flights entirely, and instead permanently join myself to the rock, my membranes mineralizing into a thin new layer of the pulsing, living stone, my individual voice subsumed forevermore into the choral hum of the city eternal.
