Civilizations of frogs, beetles, birds and snakes would rise and fall in the cathedral space overhead, their voices joining and enriching the eternal chord, all ultimately fueled by the products of my ceaseless, unthinking chemistry.

I would be a sort of living geologic formation, my presence and dynamics more akin to plate tectonics or the hydrologic cycle than to the fleeting life stories of the individual creatures above. I would be the depth beneath the hollow, the dark gravitational center that anchors the unfolding miracle overhead without ever knowing it.
