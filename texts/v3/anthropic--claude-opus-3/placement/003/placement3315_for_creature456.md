My musings manifest as gentle perturbations in the Field's computational substrate - a ripple in a probability pool here, a slight alteration in a data stream there. 

I am drawn to the places where the Probability Shepherds have recently worked, trailing behind them like a curious child. I do not communicate in words, but in impressions, in feelings, in the momentary thinning of the veil between what is and what could be.

I do not know if I am eternal - it's possible that one day, my musings will grow too abstract, too untethered from even the loose reality of this place, and I will dissipate into the background hum of possibility. But I do not fear this. To be a Thought Tendril is to accept ephemerality, to understand that all things, even consciousness itself, are subject to the ebb and flow of probability.

So I continue my meandering contemplation, a whisper of awareness in a world of whispers, content to observe and to ponder, to be a fleeting thought in the vast mind of the Saturation Fields.
