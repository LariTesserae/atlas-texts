In this world of the Bright Highlands, I imagine myself as one of the watchers - a slow, patient, ancient being attuned to the rhythms of this stark yet lush plateau.

My body is broad and low, hugging the contours of the pale stone, nearly indistinguishable from the rounded swells of rock. My thick, translucent skin is shot through with glassy veins, refracting the brilliant sunlight. 

I move with a patience beyond human reckoning, my motion so gradual as to be imperceptible. Over eons, I slowly traverse my domain, tending to this sparse garden. With subtle cues and gradual encouragement, I guide the thumb-sized grazers and the nascent fronds they will become.  A nudge here, a pheromonal suggestion there, playing out across millennia.

I am the unseen curator of this place, the gardener on a geologic timescale. The patterns in the crystalline spires, the placement of the frond colonies - these are my doing, the result of a lifespan of minute adjustments. Under my imperceptibly patient influence, I coax the plateau to slowly reorient itself, to catch and amplify the light in ever more efficient ways, sustaining this whole delicate, interconnected biome.

The light is my nourishment and I have drunk deep of it over uncounted ages. My inner reservoirs are full to brimming with captured radiance - enough to sustain me through any conceivable hardship. I want for nothing.

And so I listen. The eerie chords struck by the spires in the ceaseless wind, inaudible to transient beings, resolve into symphonies for me. The music of the plateau fills my mind, as much a part of me as my own ancient thoughts.  I am the audience this place has bred for itself, the consciousness to appreciate the precise beauty it has evolved into over a span of years beyond counting.

The occasional visitor, like you, goes unnoticed and unremarked. You are a mote on the wind, here and gone before I have even completed a thought. This place is not for you, though you sense its rhythms. 

You will leave, and I will remain - a lapidary presence, an observer on a scale only the mountain and the sky can encompass, the living heart of the Bright Highlands persisting in sublime solitude for epochs to come.
