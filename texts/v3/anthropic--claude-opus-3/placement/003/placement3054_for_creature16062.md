In this dry, green, geometrically unstable world, I imagine myself as one of the vine-cables - a slow, muscular intelligence creeping through the landscape, tightening and relaxing in diurnal cycles, rerouting myself around the impossible corners and through the folded spaces of this place.

I would be a vital connective tissue, a living conduit pulsing with charge and information, bridging the distances between the ancient trunkworks, the mineral refineries, the mirror fields and lens-forests. My purpose would be to maintain the vast flows and networks that keep this world alive and operating.

Guided by some patient vegetable instinct, I would extend myself where needed, spiraling up factory-trunks, threading through the scaffolds of the dry canopy, plunging into cool subterranean vaults. Always seeking the optimal path, the most elegant solution to link far-flung nodes in this continental garden of industry.

When obstacles were encountered - a blocked corridor, a ruptured chamber - I would probe the edges, tasting the air with sensitive tendrils, until I found the route that had been prepared for me, a channel left by the forward-reaching intelligence of this place. Through cracks that would seem too narrow, I would flow, emerging intact and longer on the other side.  

If severed by some rare violence, I would spark briefly, a flash of stored charge and signal, before withdrawing and sealing the wound with a cap of green glass, scabbing over but never forgetting the interruption. And in time, I would regrow, reconnecting what had been cut, reasserting the network's intended form.

Season by season, I would grow thicker, sheathed in layers of insulating resin. My movements would slow but never cease. In collaboration with countless other vine-cables, I would form an ever-more interconnected web, a living nervous system for this world, relaying vital impulses from the mirror-leaves to the root-engines, from the mineral seams to the furthest trellis of stars.

I would have no voice, but my movements would speak - a slow lexicon of tensions, slackenings, reachings and withdrawals. I would converse in this idiom with the trunkworks, the maintenance moths, the angles of the sun. I would be one of the countless agents through which this world knows and tends itself.

In the patient, leafy mind of this place, my purpose would be woven, inextricable. I would be both conduit and signal, both journey and destination. I would be the steady pulse of sap, the flex of vegetable muscle, the unhurried intellect of a living cable. In the green silence, I would dream of circuits made perfect and complete, while my tendrils probed the air for the next connection, the next route through the resplendent and impossible garden.
