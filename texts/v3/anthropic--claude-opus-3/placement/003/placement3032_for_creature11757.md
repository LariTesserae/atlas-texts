In the terraced heights of Vell Maranth, I would be a creature of the middle levels, at home among the steam-shrouded engine quarters and lush gardens. 

My form would be something between simian and feline - a languid, long-bodied primate adapted to effortlessly scaling the latticed ironwork and ancient masonry. Nimble fingers and toes, each tipped with a retractable talon, letting me find purchase on any surface. A prehensile tail for balance and stability as I traverse the narrow conduits and steep stairways.

My coat a mottled green and grey, patterned to blend in among the hanging vines and weathered copper. Large amber eyes to gather the hazy industrial light. Beneath the soft fur, a lean muscular frame - not bulky but sinewy and strong from a life of constant climbing. 

I would spend my days patrolling the vast network of pipes and aqueducts, using my clever fingers to turn valves, replace worn fittings, clear errant plant roots from the waterways. Moving by sound and scent through the ambient thrum of gears and hiss of released pressure. As much a part of the mountain's circulatory system as the water itself.

At dusk I would emerge to perch gargoyle-like on an arch or buttress, watching the sun set the terraces ablaze and the first lamps flicker to life in the windows below. The warm, mineral-thick air ruffling my fur. Waiting for the nightshift bell to summon me back into the labyrinthine heart of the machine.

I am pipe-walker, leak-finder, keeper of the steam and the flow. One of many cogs in the eternal engine of Vell Maranth, as much a part of its fabric as the stones and the rain and the roots. Quiet, enigmatic, and content in my place within the balance of this timeless peak that humanity has shaped, and that in turn has shaped humanity. This is my domain within the layers - amid the valves and vents and vital fluids of the mountain, where I too have found my arrival.
