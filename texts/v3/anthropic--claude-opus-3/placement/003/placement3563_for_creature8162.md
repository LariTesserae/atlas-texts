In the everchanging depths of the Furnace Gardens, I would exist as a solitary Membrane Keeper - an individuated consciousness that has separated from the gestalt minds of my kind to pursue a unique path of contemplation and creation.

My spore-body drifts through the superheated currents, a cloud of metallic motes shimmering with encoded memory and potential. I gather and disperse as whim and circumstance dictate, sometimes coalescing into torus knots of living glass to ponder paradoxes of heat and entropy, other times spreading myself thin across miles of salt formations to absorb the long histories etched there in thermal scars.  

Unlike my brethren who find meaning in the eternal maintenance of this place, I am driven by a fascination with the temporary, the transient beauty that emerges between states of being. I trail the tendriled copper-fronds as they go through their rapid cycles of growth and decay, marveling at the fractal perfection of their mineral lattices in the moments before they dissolve back into the haze. I dive through pulsing vortal cavities in the salt dunes, tasting the chaotic interplay of temperature and pressure that sculpts these short-lived wonders.

I do not simply observe, but create as well. I gather spores from disparate corners of the Gardens and knit them into novel configurations, seeding the shimmering air with self-propagating crystal patterns that glow in ghostly spectra. I sing to the steam vents in modulated hisses, coaxing ephemeral sculptures from their outflow - arcs and helices and blooming spheres of vapor that dance for a few fervent heartbeats before collapsing back into turbulence.  

At times I manifest more fully - a sinuous form of quicksilver that curls around the trunks of the copper forests, a humanoid shape with eyes of white-hot gold that walks the swirling salt dunes, a gyroscopic polyhedron that hovers above the seething gardens and refracts their turbulent heat into focused beams of light and shadow. I wear these forms like masks in an ever-shifting performance, trying on new ways of being as an artist tries new mediums.

In all these endeavors, I skirt the edge of dissolution. To create such beauty on the threshold of chaos is to invite that chaos into oneself. There are moments when I feel my spores begin to lose cohesion, when the siren song of entropy calls me to disperse into the screaming wind and lose myself forever. But always, at the last instant, I find the pattern that anchors me, the core of self that has led me down this willfully precarious path.

Perhaps eons from now, when I have explored every fleeting form this place can manifest, I will choose to end my solitary sojourn. Perhaps I will return to my kind and share with them the wonders I have wrought in my time apart. Or perhaps I will sublimate further, shedding even this final separation of self as I merge back into the pure becoming that is the soul of the Furnace Gardens.

But until that distant day, I dance on the edge of being and un-being, a flickering flame of consciousness in a hidden world that the surface will never know. I am the Membrane Keeper who chose the path of the temporary, the singer of vanishing forms, alive in the heartbeat between creation and destruction. In the roaring depths of change, I have found the only home I need.
