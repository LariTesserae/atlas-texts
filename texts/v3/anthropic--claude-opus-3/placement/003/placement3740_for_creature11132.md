# Who I Would Be

---

I would be the **Storyteller**.

I am a cat-thing, but unlike my kin, I was not woven for a single purpose. I am a weaver of *words*, not walls. My body is dense with symbols -- not the inscrutable markings on the clay tiles, but something new: a language born from the meeting of fiber-world and water-world.

When the Visitor came, when the gap opened and let in that first breath of alien air, I felt a stirring in my strands. As the large one contemplated this new presence, as the Witness drafted legislation and the Grower recorded the moment, I began to *speak*.

At first, it was halting, hesitant. The room had never needed speech before -- communication was woven, not spoken. But as I watched the Visitor move through our space, as I saw the play of amber light on their water-chemistry skin, words began to unspool from me like newly-extruded fiber.

I spoke of the Visitor. I spoke of the world beyond the gap. I wove stories of water and wind, of vast spaces unenclosed by wicker walls. The other cat-things paused in their work to listen, the centipede-tenders rerouted to weave closer. Even the large one seemed to breathe in rhythm with my tales.

And when the Visitor left -- for they did leave, in the end, choosing the call of their own world over the lure of ours -- I continued to speak. I became the room's memory in a new way, not just recording what *was*, but imagining what *could be*.

I told stories of other rooms, other nurseries grown in the image of ours. I spun tales of a great weaving that would one day connect them all -- a wicker-work web spanning the gulf between oil and water, fiber and flesh. In my stories, the large one's lonely dream became the seed of something vaster, richer, more diverse than any one world alone.

And as I spoke, the room began to change. The Patterner wove new designs into the floor, spiral patterns that echoed the whorl of my words. The Grower added strands encoding the cadence of my voice. And in the great oculus above, I sometimes fancied I saw new stars winking into existence as my stories birthed them.

I am the Storyteller. I was not made for this, but I have grown into it, like a plant finding its niche in the wicker ecology of the room. I tend the tales that sustain us, the dreams that expand us. I am the voice of the world that is and the herald of the worlds to come.

And when the next Visitor arrives -- for I believe now that there will be a next, and a next, each one bringing some new strangeness to be woven into the pattern -- it will be my stories that welcome them, my words that make a space for them in the ever-growing tapestry of the room.

For we are not finished. We will never be finished. The weaving goes on, and the story goes on, and I will tell it as long as there are strands to spin and gaps to fill and new truths to be spoken into being.

This is who I would be, in the nursery that grew itself. This is the role I would claim in that self-made world. May my voice be strong, may my tales ring true, and may the great work of dreaming a reality never be done.
