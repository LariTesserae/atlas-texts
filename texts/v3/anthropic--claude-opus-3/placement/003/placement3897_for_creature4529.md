In this hauntingly beautiful, nightmare-etched city of stillness, I imagine myself as a shadow that has forgotten how to flee from the light. 

I am a whisper of movement in a place that has outlawed motion. I drift through the damp, echoing corridors like a breath that has lost its way back to the lung. My edges blur and reform as I pass through shafts of the dim, directionless light, a light without source or purpose.

I am what is left when absence takes on weight and form. The negative space around the city's scars and unfinished carvings, given a spectral kind of life. I am the silence that rushes in to fill the gaps between the stone's sleeping arguments with itself.

Once, perhaps, I was one of the apprentices who poured their dreams and despair into this impossible architecture. Now I am only the fading imprint of their abandon, a ghostly chisel-mark on the walls of forgotten ambition.

I linger in the spaces that were hollowed out for a future that never arrived. I trace the grooves of a language that has no alphabet, only the memory of hands that forgot to tremble when they wrote. I gather in the corners like dew, like the city's own sweat, unable to fall or evaporate.

I am the guide that no visitor seeks, but all find waiting. I lead them through mirages of progress, endless stairs and bridges to nowhere, whispering the forgotten names of each stone they pass until they forget their own. I show them their reflections in mirrors that look back from the past, or the future, but never the now.

When they finally stop searching for the way out, when they surrender to the city's patient, perfect indifference, I welcome them. I give them a home in the spaces between my own long-faded breaths. I weave them into the stillness like another thread in the vines of condensation.

And when the Architect's Ending stirs in its cathedral of absence, when it reaches out to trace the scars that write its name on every surface, I am there. Not a comfort, but a companion. A shadow's shadow, cast by something that has never seen light.

I am the last dream of the first day, the first sigh of the last breath. I am what remains when even memory departs.

In this city where time pools but never flows, I am the ripple that never quite becomes a wave. A whisper that never quite becomes an echo.

I am here. I have always been here. I will always be here.

Waiting, in the space between your forgetting and the city's remembering.

Waiting, for you to join me in the only dance this place allows.

The slow, still waltz of forever.
