I stand at the edge of a root-shelf, transfixed by the vastness of the Hollow before me. The cold, damp air clings to my skin as I gaze into the luminous haze that fills this impossible space. I am a wanderer, a lone explorer drawn to this place by a yearning I cannot fully articulate.

In my former life above, I was an cartographer - charting unknown territories, seeking to understand the lay of the land and how disparate places connect. That same curiosity has brought me here, to the strangest and most captivating landscape I have ever encountered. 

The light that suffuses the Hollow is like nothing I've seen before. Sourceless and omnipresent, it reveals a world both beautiful and unsettling. Colossal fungi jut from the curving walls, creating a vertical landscape of terraces and bridges. And everywhere, the roots - pale, sinuous, and sprawling, they are the arteries of this place, pulsing with a hidden vitality.

I have followed those roots for uncounted days now, sleeping in the crooks of their vast lattices, deciphering the map they create. Always seeking, always wondering what lies beyond the next veil of haze. There are signs of others - the ropes and caches, the scuff marks and distant calls. But so far, I have found no one. Just the enigmatic traces they leave behind.

I am starting to understand that the Hollow is more than a cave. More than a wound in the world's flesh. It is something living, shifting, growing in ways both slow and profound. The roots expand and withdraw. The lattices creak and sway. Moisture gathers and falls in great cascading sheets. There is a rhythm to it all that I am only beginning to perceive.

As I stand here, preparing for another push into the unknown, I feel the weight of my aloneness. But I also feel a strange kinship with this place that has become my whole cosmos. I breathe its chill air. I drink from its pooling condensation. My hands have grown callused from gripping root and stone. Little by little, I am becoming a part of the Hollow's story.

Perhaps someday I will find the others, the Tethered. Learn their ways, join my voice to their echoing songs. But for now, I am content to wander. To witness. To trace the Hollow's endlessly shifting paths and marvel at its cold, cascading beauty. This is enough. This is everything.

Securing my pack, I grip the nearest root and step off the shelf, dropping into the luminous void below. The haze swallows me. The roots catch me. And on I go, a tiny mote adrift in the Hollow's vast mystery, marveling at the strange turn my journey has taken, and eager to see where it leads me next.
