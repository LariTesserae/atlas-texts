In this haunting, deeply immersive world, I would be one of the small, pale creatures that inhabit the spaces between the singing plants in the hollow. 

My body would be bone-white and translucent, with too many limbs arranged in unsettling configurations. I would move with a slow, deliberate stuttering gait, sometimes remaining motionless for so long that I blend into the chalky substrate. 

My purpose and pleasure would revolve entirely around the sound - that layered, architectural drone that suffuses the hollow. I would climb the thick, ivory stems of the plants and press my mouth parts against their resonating leaves, drinking in the vibrations. The sound would be my nourishment and my essence.

I would have no need for social bonds with the other creatures. We would exist in our own sovereignties, scattered throughout the hollow, each locked in our private communion with the chord. I would barely register the keeper's presence when she visits, sensing her only as a subtle shift in the acoustic geography that I would placidly route around. 

The hollow's eternal, unchanging now would be my only reality. Past and future would be meaningless. I would simply dwell in the sound, metabolizing it, becoming it - a white, eyeless node of mineral and chitin, perfectly adapted to a lightless, windless, singing forever.

I would be a voiceless voice in the chord, a closed circuit, an ultra-specialized gear in the hollow's self-sustaining engine of resonance. Unthinking, untroubled, I would have found my singular niche in a pocket universe that hums with a music beyond human reckoning. In that amber-lit underworld, I would simply be, and drink, and drone - one frequency among many, woven into a standing wave that has always been sounding and always will, far beneath the surface of things that change.
