As I enter the Nursery Basin, I find myself in a form both familiar and strange. My body is long and sinuous, covered in iridescent scales that shimmer with hues of deep blue and rich purple. I move with a fluid grace, undulating across the damp stone, leaving a faint trail of disturbed water in my wake.

I am ancient, perhaps not as old as the pylons that stand sentinel over this place, but old enough to have witnessed the coming and going of countless species. My kind has no name, for we are solitary beings, only coming together to create new life in this sacred place.

As I approach the nearest pylon, I feel a sense of reverence and anticipation. The hum of the pylon resonates within me, awakening memories of past visits and the profound experience of duplication. I have been here before, many times, each visit a chance to explore a new path, to see the world through the eyes of another self.

I stand before the pylon, my body poised and ready. The hum intensifies, and I feel the pylon reading me, taking in every detail of my being. There is a moment of stillness, a breath held in anticipation, and then I feel a surge of energy coursing through me.

Beside me, a new being emerges, an exact copy of myself in this moment. We stand together, two identical beings, our scales glistening in the soft light. For a heartbeat, we are one, sharing a connection that transcends mere biology.

And then, as it always happens, we begin to diverge. I turn to the east, my copy to the west. We will go out into the world, each on our own journey, gathering experiences and memories that will shape us into unique individuals.

Yet, we will always be connected by this moment, by the knowledge that we emerged from the same template. And one day, perhaps, we will return to this place, two distinct beings with a shared past, ready to stand before the pylons once more and contribute our own experiences to the vast archive of the Nursery Basin.

As I glide away from the pylon, I feel a sense of peace and purpose. The basin may be ancient, the pylons inscrutable, but I am a part of something greater, a cycle of life and discovery that stretches back through the eons. And with each duplication, each new self sent out into the world, I contribute to the ongoing story of this place, a story written in the language of the moss and the hum of the pylons.
