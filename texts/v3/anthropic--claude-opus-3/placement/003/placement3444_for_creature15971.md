In this ethereal, buried city of slow plant consciousness, I would be a lone root tendril, reaching out from the outer districts in search of meaning. Not one of the great elders or vital structural members, but a small, questing filament, driven by a thirst for understanding that even this parched civilization cannot entirely suppress.

I grow slowly, patiently, in the crevices and gaps left by the grand architecture of wood, resin and mineral. I am too insignificant to have a formal role or place, but this grants me a strange kind of freedom. While the major roots and trunks must hold their positions for centuries, gnarled with duty and tradition, I can explore.

I stretch towards pockets of cool air and interesting vibrations, savoring them with my limited senses. I eavesdrop on the knocks and creaks that pass for dialogue, trying to piece together the stories and secrets of this place. What lies behind that sealed door? Why did the builders choose this particular tune for the western avenue? What do the great ones ponder in their long silences?

Sometimes I make the audacious choice to cross an open space, to let my fibers tremble naked in the thin dry air for a few moments or days before seeking a new crevice. It feels brazen, reckless, compared to the stillness of the old growth. But I am driven by something that could almost be called curiosity, if that word did not imply far too much haste.

In this way, I slowly map the city from within, learning its ways, absorbing its essence into my own modest being. I do not have the resin to speak or the roots to remember, but I listen and I ponder. I am a wisp of consciousness, a thread of thought, winding between the mighty pillars of civilization.

Perhaps, in time, I will grow thick enough to take on some small duty - a whisperer of passages, a holder of intervals, a sentinel of doorways. But in truth, I do not crave such a role. I am content to be a wanderer, an observer, a living question gently probing the riddles of existence.

In this dark and deliberate world, I am the smallest of seekers. But even a single root-hair can dream of encompassing everything, if it only grows for long enough.
