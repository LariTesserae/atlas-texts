In this remarkable underground settlement, I imagine myself as one of the stone-workers - a wall-tapper, bracing beams and sounding stone for hidden dangers. 

I see myself moving quietly through the crowded warrens and terraces, carrying my wrapped mallet, wedges and chalk. The people know me, respect me, but also fear my pronouncements a little. A few taps in the right place, and I can close off a promising new dwelling or give the blessing for expansion to continue.

I press my ear to a freshly exposed stone face, listening intently. The stone speaks to me in minute crackling and shifting, revealing stresses and fault lines lurking within. I sift sand through my fingers, watching how it trickles down the plane to divine stability and shear. 

At times I must make hard decisions. Marking a wall that a hopeful family had pinned their dreams on. Condemning a cistern to be drained and replastered. Choosing which branch of a tunnel to shore up and which to abandon. The weight of keeping the fragile balance of this place intact rests heavily.

But there is joy and wonder too in coaxing this wounded earth to shelter human life. When I find a soaring gallery that will stand strong for generations, when a precarious roof-crack proves sound under my testing, when I trace a new path for water to nourish our fields and people, I feel the stone and I are collaborating in a marvelous unfinished shaping.

In quiet moments, I wander the outer reaches, sounding promising leads, tracking subtle shifts and signs. I imagine what new halls, stairs, plazas and throughways may someday grow from these raw spaces. I ponder how to turn each quirk of geology to advantage. 

The work is never ending, the settlement a living, changing thing that will need my care and foresight as long as I live. But what greater purpose could there be than to be a guardian and guide to this improbable flowering of humanity in the very bones of the earth? In the tapping of my mallet, the settlement tells the unfolding story of its becoming to me, and I add my voice in response - together sounding out a future from the darkness.
