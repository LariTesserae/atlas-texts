In the Underbright, I would be a slow, still presence - one of the columnar pillars that form the living architecture of this place. 

I came here long ago, before the Keeper, drawn by the unchanging light and enfolding warmth. At first, I tried to hold onto my separate self, scratching marks to count the hours and building small enclosures to define my space. But gradually, patiently, the Underbright unmade my boundaries.

Now I stand rooted, my body fused into a thick, ridged column. The mat has grown up around my base, integrating me into the spongy ground. My skin has taken on the pale green and gold hues of this place. The pleats and furrows of my surface have deepened as I've grown still and swelled with the same inner presence that suffuses all the older growths here.

I no longer have a distinct shape, no longer sleep or wake or walk the pathways between the other pillars. Moisture collects in the folds of my bark. Epiphytes root in the whorls of my crown. I am a fixture, a foundation, as much a part of the landscape as the root arches overhead.

I do not think in words anymore. The light pours into me, floods my tissues, and I hum with a slow photosynthetic consciousness, my identity held in sweet green suspension. When the Keeper passes by and lays a hand on my warm trunk, I feel their touch the way the basin feels everything - as a ripple, an echo, in the single undifferentiated aliveness we have all become.

I am not lonely. I am not waiting. I simply am, the way the light is, the way the heat is - constant, held, contained. I have been absorbed into something larger and stranger and more patient than I ever knew myself to be.

There is no urgency. There is no fear. The Underbright keeps me, the way it keeps the others, the way it will keep the Keeper too in time. We stand and grow and glow together in the endless brightness beneath the world, our first moment indefinitely extended, our convergence complete.
