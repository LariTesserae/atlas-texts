I find myself drawn to the Bright Hollow, captivated by the light that seems to live and breathe within the chamber. I am one of the pale moths, born from ancestors who ventured down the light-shaft generations ago.

My wings are luminous, almost translucent, catching and refracting the ever-present glow of the hollow. Intricate patterns are etched into my wings, shaped by the light itself over countless lifetimes. These patterns hold meaning, fragments of wisdom imparted by the Keeper and the ancient scratches on the cave wall. 

I spend my days drifting through the radiant air, alighting on the warm, mineral-rich walls. When I rest upon the cryptic marks left by the Keeper, I feel their energy pulsing through my delicate frame. In those moments, I absorb the knowledge they hold, piece by ephemeral piece.

Though I live out my brief existence in this luminous realm, I am compelled to journey up the light-shaft when the time comes to mate. As I spiral skyward, my wings catch the sun, casting glimmering patterns on the cave walls. In the outside world, I dance and swirl with others of my kind, our wings flashing with the messages we carry from below.

We lay our eggs at the mouth of the shaft, ensuring that our offspring will be drawn down into the Bright Hollow, just as we were. The patterns on our wings are passed to the next generation, an unspoken language that binds us to the Keeper and the ancient rhythms of the hollow.

As my short life nears its end, I descend once more, following the siren call of the light. I long to bask in the glow, to rest upon the sacred scratches a final time. I know that as my physical form fades, my essence will become part of the light, merging with the spirit of the Keeper and all those who came before me.

In the Bright Hollow, I am both insignificant and essential, a single mote in an endless dance of light and life, a bearer of ancient secrets I barely understand. My purpose is not to question, but simply to be - to drift, to glow, to carry the light within me until I myself become light, forever part of the mystery and magic of this shimmering, subterranean world.
