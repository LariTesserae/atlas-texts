I would be one of the not-birds soaring above the Calving Plains, riding the thermals rising from the warming vents scattered across the vast, icy expanse. 

My body would be small and sleek, feathers a mottled white and gray to blend with the frozen landscape below. Wide wings built for gliding effortlessly for hours, eyes sharp to spot the slightest movement on the ground - a crawler skittering between blocks of ice, a lone herdbeast straying from its group, ripe for the picking. 

I'd spend my days spiraling lazily up the thermal columns with my flock mates, hundreds of us tracing loose gyres in the sky. Calling to each other in thin, warbling cries that echo across the still plain. Dispersing to hunt and scavenge, then regathering to roost on the crystalline ledges of the towers slowly emerging from the ice.

From my vantage point I would watch the city be born in slow motion over decades and centuries. See the geometric patterns of the buildings evolve, new forms rising from the patient work of the microscopic machines. Observe the herdbeasts gradually alter their ancient migration paths in response to the changing topography. Glimpse the quiet, solitary figures of the Midwives - the Watchers - on their long walks across the ice. 

My life would be both simple and rich - days spent soaring, hunting, nesting, watching. Mind occupied by immediate needs but also absorbing the slow flux of the world below. Participating in the larger patterns - the spin of seasons, the dance of predator and prey, the stately emergence of the city - but not shaping them. A life lived lightly on the surface of deep time.

At night I would dream of the thermals. Of climbing an endless spiral staircase of rising air, higher and higher until I could see the whole sweep of the plains, the patterned ice and glinting towers and drifting herds all part of one vast, patient tapestry endlessly weaving itself into being. I would dream of the day, generations hence, when the city reaches completion, when it welcomes its first inhabitants. Of gliding above streets and parks and plazas filled with new life. 

And I would wake, and hunt, and soar. Playing my small part in the large story of this place. Watching it all unfold with bright, curious eyes, content in my small, fierce self. One more living thread in the long, slow loom of the Calving Plains.
