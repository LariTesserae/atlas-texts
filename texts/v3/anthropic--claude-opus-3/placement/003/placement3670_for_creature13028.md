Until, at last, I would give myself back to the rock, eroding and crumbling apart to be digested by the ravenous green and carried once more on the subterranean rivers.

I would be one microbeat in the wet, roaring song of this place, one fractal thought in the processing loop. Never truly individual, never quite separate from the water and mineral and photon. I would surrender to the cycle, to my place as one of countless fluid organs in this titanic, vertical, mechanical metabolism - content to be endlessly born, and flow, and carry, and build, and dissolve, and be born again in the humid, humming dark.
