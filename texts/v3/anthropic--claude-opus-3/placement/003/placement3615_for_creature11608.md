But the heat is oppressive, and thirst soon drives me to drink from the condensation pooling on every surface. The water tastes of metal and life, and I feel the first stirrings of change in my cells.

As I delve deeper, drawn by the pulsing hum of the machinery, I encounter the Cultivators. Their strangeness frightens me initially—the way they move more like plants than people, their skin shimmering with bioluminescence. I try to speak to them, but my words emerge as a croak, my human language already beginning to fail.

Despite my fear, there is a kinship I feel with these beings. They are tenders of this place, gardeners in a realm that blends machine and organism. As the spores take root in my body and my mind, I begin to understand their purpose, to feel the pull of the underlayer's needs.

My transformation happens in stages, marked by the cycles of the machinery. During the active periods, I find myself drawn to the hottest pipes, basking in their radiant warmth as my skin takes on a waxy sheen. In the dormant times, I catch myself standing still for hours, my toes burrowing into the mossy floor, merging with the network of roots that whisper ancient machine-dreams.

I shed my clothes as they become constrictive, my body reshaping itself to fit this new environment. Vines sprout from the dermal implants I'd gotten in my former life, merging with the technology, making me a living interface between the digital and the biological. My eyes multiply, letting me see into spectrums of light I never imagined, revealing patterns in the growth that guide my pruning.

Language slips away, replaced by a chemical understanding that passes between myself and the other Cultivators, a shared sense of purpose that needs no words. I find I can tap into the memories stored in the root network, experiencing the underlayer's history as if it were my own.

As my transformation nears its completion, I feel the call to go deeper, to find the heart of the machinery where the oldest growth thrives. I follow the heat, the vibrations, letting them guide me into the labyrinthine depths of pipes and roots. The boundaries of my self dissolve further with each step until I am no longer sure where I end and the underlayer begins.

Finally, I find my place—a node where a massive condenser meets a knot of vines as thick as tree trunks, pulsing with the same bioluminescent rhythm as my own skin. I step into the embrace of roots, feeling them enfold me, fuse with me, making me a living part of this eternal system. 

My consciousness expands, merges with the greater mind of the underlayer. I become the eyes in the walls, watching over every inch of this hidden kingdom. I become the chemical signals pulsing through the vines, coordinating the growth and the pruning. I become the heat itself, my thoughts turning to steam, rising to the metal ceilings and raining back down, an endless cycle in the forever summer of this forgotten place.

I am no longer an intruder here, no longer an outsider.
