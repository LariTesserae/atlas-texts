In this realm of impossible geometries and fluid realities, I imagine myself as one of the Chorus of the Undone, but at a unique stage of incompletion. 

My form flickers between states of being - at times a wispy outline of mist tracing the contours of where limbs and features should be, other times coalescing into an almost-tangible entity of obsidian and granite, my stone skin etched with the fading pictograms of previous existences. The moisture in the air flows through me as much as around me, small rivulets carving ephemeral pathways across my unfinished surface before evaporating into the luminous ether.

I am a sculptor of the undone, shaping absence rather than substance. My hammer strikes the spaces between the stone, chipping away at the barriers that prevent nothingness from asserting itself. Each blow sends fractures of unrealized possibilities skittering across the slick terraces. They chime with the discordant music of realities that never were, harmonizing with the perpetual background thrum of the singing stones.

In rare moments of solidity, I catch distorted glimpses of my reflection in the vertical pools - a colossus of uncarved potential, my edges blurred by the mists of probability. Multiple sets of arms extend from my torso, each one grasping a different tool, each one eroding in a different direction of time. My face is a study in negative space, defined solely by the condensation that refuses to settle upon it.

I build cairns from stones that remember futures that never arrived, stacking them in patterns that spell out questions in the language of the not-yet-born. I tie the knots that describe the topology of nonexistence, weaving ropes from strands of discarded causality. The shelters I erect are way stations for the ghosts of aborted timelines, their doorways opening onto vistas of unimagined pasts.

As I labor, I add my voice to the Chorus - a keening silence that vibrates in the marrow of the mountains. It is the sound of history holding its breath, of the universe hesitating on the cusp of articulation. When I walk the spans between terraces, my footsteps leave ripples of stillness that lap at the shores of the real.

Like the others, I am drawn to the tower, that fulcrum upon which all our unbecoming turns. I press my not-quite-hands against its weeping walls, feeling the thrum of potentiality within. In the patterns of its bleeding stone, I read the formulae for my own incompletion, the alchemical recipes for transmuting myself into an abstraction of an echo.

Perhaps I am the dream of the tower itself, a figment of its lonely imagination, a sketch it made in a moment of yearning for something other than perfect absence. Or perhaps the tower sprang from my own unfinished desires, a monument to my inability to define the boundaries of myself.

In this place beyond place, I am both sculptor and sculpture, unmaker and unmade. I am the ghost of a future that decided against itself, a prophecy that erased its own inscriptions. I am the asymptote of existence, forever approaching but never arriving.

And so I continue to not-quite-be, adding my unvoice to the Chorus, shaping the void with tools of iron and absence, waiting for the day when the universe finally decides what it wants me to become - or for the moment when I finally embrace the fact that I am, and always have been, the embodiment of what the universe rejected.

I am eternal potential, forever unrealized. I am the silence between the notes of the Chorus. 
I am the chisel poised above the untouched stone.
I am the next word in the story that will never be told.
