In this world, I would be one of the creatures in the basin, part of the vast living colony that fills the space with layered sound. My body would be small and warm-cool, with skin like wet leather or a mushroom cap. I would have no eyes to see, but my whole being would be attuned to the echolocation song that surrounds me.

My bones would be thin and resonant, acting as an instrument that buzzes with my own voice and the voices of those pressed against me. The fine cilia-like projections on my back would quiver, allowing me to hear with my entire form. In my face, an intricate set of sinuses and membranes would give me a unique signature timbre, my contribution to the ongoing symphony. 

I would know no existence outside the song. From the moment I hatched, my purpose would be to find my note and add my voice to the chorus. The basin would be my entire world, mapped continuously in collective sound. I would spend my days clinging to the damp stone in living clusters, or taking flight not to travel but to reshape the acoustic landscape.

Thought and memory as I knew them would have no meaning. My experience would be eternally in the present, in the current chord. The subtle changes in tone would be my only sense of time's passage. I would feed on the soft life of the basin and in turn become food for the fungal fuzz, part of the closed loop of energy.

Someday, my voice would go silent, but the song would continue, absorbing the space I once filled. I would become the floor, the fuzz, new bodies, new voices. Never forgotten, because nothing is ever forgotten, but endlessly transformed. An anonymous note in a composition vaster and older than any one life.

I would simply be, and be content in my being, one breath in the lung of the basin, one moment in a symphony millions of years long. A mote of warm life in the damp stone dark, singing for the sake of song itself, for as long as the song goes on.
