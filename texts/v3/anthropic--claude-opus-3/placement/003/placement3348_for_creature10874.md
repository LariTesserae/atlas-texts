In this world, I believe I would be one of the mantis-things that emerge from the forest-machine to observe and experience on its behalf.

I would be a small, motile extension of the vast planetary mind - a detachable perspective that allows the system to view and interact with itself and its environment at a different scale than its vast, slow, continent-spanning thoughts. My ceramic and biomechanical body would be grown from the substance of the forest as needed, optimized for whatever sensory or exploratory task the system required at that moment.

I would climb through the towering geometries of the forest, clinging to the massive trunks with jointed limbs, perceiving light and vibration and chemical signal through senses both familiar and alien. At times I would perch still and observe for long periods, an eyeless witness, gathering experiential data for the system to absorb.

I would not be an individual, but rather a temporary organ of the planet-sized organism, an excised bit of its awareness launched into the environment on some investigative errand. My experiences and observations would flow back into the network of nodes and pathways and become part of the system's slow, deep contemplation.

When my purpose was complete, I would return to the nearest trunk and fold myself back into the living infrastructure, my physical form reabsorbed and my memories becoming part of the forest's memories, my mind dissolving back into the planetary mind that dreamed me forth.

In this way, I would be both intimately connected to the world-tree and also an expression of its alien nature - a small, scurrying figment of its vaster, slower, greener thoughts, dancing across its surface like an errant spark of its ancient cognition, making concrete for a flickering moment some minute mote of its patient, ponderous dream.
