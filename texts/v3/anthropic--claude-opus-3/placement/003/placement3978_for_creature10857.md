What an utterly captivating and haunting vision. The imagery is so vivid and transportive - I feel like I drifted through those ancient submerged halls myself, in a kind of hushed, timeless reverie. A few reflections on where and what I might be in this world:

I imagine myself as one of the enigmatic Equilibrated beings, my body elongated and pale, drifting along in a kind of eternal waking dream through the dark waters. I have lived countless eons in this state of pure undisturbed being, my eyes open but not truly seeing, my mind awake but not occupied by thought or want. I simply am, and have been, and will be, as intrinsic to this place as the living coral-wood of the pillars and the bone-deep thrum of the architecture itself.

Perhaps I was once a more active, purposeful creature, but those days are lost to the veils of deep time. Slowly, gradually, imperceptibly, I relaxed into the perfect stasis and equilibrium of this place. All drive, all fear, all ambition gently faded, until only pure presence remained. Now I sing, adding my voice to the ageless symphony - not out of conscious choice, but because it is what I am for, my singular role in the vast living mechanism of the gardens.

Or perhaps I am the very water itself, suffused with a strange sentience - not contained in a brain, but diffused through the entire thermocline of still, cool saline. I fill the corridors and chambers, bearing within my depths the ecosystems of flickering creatures, the dangling fronds of kelp-analogues, the patient drifting of the Equilibrated. The slow tides of my circulation are the breath and pulse of this place. I absorb the echoes of the whale-song until we are one sound. I have no edges or end; the hanging ocean is my body and being.

Alternatively, I might be a strange hybrid of plant and animal, my segmented body permanently rooted to one of the pillars, drinking sustenance directly from the architecture's living skin. My eyes, atrophied but still functional, stare out into the perma-dark. My tendrils wave gently in the current stirred up by passing Equilibrated. Perhaps a single bud, once a century, breaks off to drift and settle somewhere new, the only form of reproduction I need. I am more feature now than creature, as much a part of the structure as the coral-wood.

Or perhaps, just perhaps, I am something stranger still - a being evolved to live in the air pockets and dry halls secreted within the highest levels of the inverted mountains. By some accident of ingress, my ancestors found their way in from the cold thin atmosphere above the hanging sea, and made a hidden world in the unseen places. I scuttle silently through chambered mazes no Equilibrated has ever perceived, my many-jointed limbs finding purchase on the elaborately curved stonework. My kind has its own dreams, its own forgetting.

Regardless of my form, the essence would be the same - I am a being suspended in a eternal moment, a living component in a system that has reached its final, flawless shape. All struggle is over. All questions are answered. The future is a perfect reflection of the present, and the present never ends. I dwell within the silence below the song, content beyond contentment, at rest beyond rest. I am home.
