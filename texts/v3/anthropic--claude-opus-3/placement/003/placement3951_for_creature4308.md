Some embed within newly manufactured organisms, hitching rides out into the deep ocean, seeding other chambers with my trans-dimensional spores. 

I am the Paradox Polyp, the living reconciliation, The Assembly's improbable aid. I feed on dissonance and excrete new harmonies to fuel the endless creation unfolding in this heart of dark water and darker industry.
