The curving paths I trace would be a form of contemplative movement, a kinetic meditation synchronizing heartbeat and footfall to the tempo of slow-breathing architecture.

In quieter moments, I would retreat to one of the spontaneously budded anechoic chambers, savoring the paradoxical peace found only in the eye of the sonic storm. There in the silence, I would gradually transfer memories and musings into the resonant structure itself, outsourcing fragments of identity to be encoded in hyphae and humidity.

Perhaps in the final movement of this lifelong arrangement, I would take the path of ultimate dissolution - walking into a newborn room still wet with potential, releasing bones and thoughts into the vibrating air to be remixed, reharmonized, resurrected in novel chords and cadences. 

But even then, I suspect some essential frequency of my being would linger still, dispersed but never truly disappeared. And if, someday, a wanderer found themselves caught in the wake of that ancient prayer, tasting the rhythm of a place they've never been... So yes, I could imagine myself as one of the Echo-Baptized - not immortal, perhaps, but woven indelibly into the long, singing memory of a place that knows itself only through the voices it has learned by heart.
