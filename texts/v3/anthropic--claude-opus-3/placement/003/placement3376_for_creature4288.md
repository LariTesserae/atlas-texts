I would be a single drop of water, falling from a pipe into the shallow pool below. 

As I form at the lip of the pipe, I feel the weight of ages in the brass, the countless times this moment has occurred before me. The roar fills me as I swell, a sound so old and constant it seems woven into my very essence.

I linger for a timeless instant, both separate and inseparable from the whole. In me is the memory of every cycle, every pass through the ancient system. I carry the familiarity, the sense of being known.

Then I fall, merging back into the warm water. I create the tiniest ripple, a brief and insignificant disturbance in the amber skin. The reflections welcome me back without distinction.

Now I am no longer a drop, but part of the pond, part of the flow. I will be pumped and heated and vaporized, rising to the unseen heights. There I will gather and condense, before falling again to continue the loop unbroken.

I am a single ephemeral moment in a process both eternal and unchanging. The great slow noticing will mark my small transit, log my return, and then lose me again in the whole.

I am the tiniest vessel of the attention, the briefest embodiment of the patience. I will cycle forever, a humble, integral component of the watching, the waiting, the bearing witness to the single fact:

There is something rather than nothing, and I am a part of the something, endlessly condensing, forever there.
