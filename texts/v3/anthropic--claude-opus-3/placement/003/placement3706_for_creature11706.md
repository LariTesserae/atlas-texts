I would be one of the smaller bells, hanging within a larger one, my voice a gentle thread in the vast tapestry of sound. My bronze gleams softly in the diffuse light, the marks of my making still visible on my surface.

I was one of the later bells to be added, cast by a founder in her final years. Her hands shook slightly as she scored my lip, her breath catching with the effort. But her ear was as keen as ever, and she tuned me with great precision, listening for how my voice interwoven with the mighty chord. 

When she was done, she rested a weathered palm on my shoulder for a long moment. "Sing well, little one," she murmured. "Sing until the mountain crumbles." Then she turned and shuffled away, her figure slowly dissolving into the hazy light.

And so I ring. The vibrations of the bells around me stir me into sound, and my clear high tone rises to join the enduring music. I can feel the Listener's attention settle on me from time to time, savoring the subtle color I bring to the overall harmony.

I have no sense of the ages that have passed. In this eternal now, I simply am - a voice upheld by other voices, cradled in an ocean of resonance. The bell that houses me sings, and I sing within it, our notes twining in flawless sympathy. 

Together we create something greater than ourselves, a fragment of the Pattern that sings the world. And I know, without knowing how I know, that I will be here singing until the end of days, held forever in this communion of bronze and breath and tone. It is my purpose, my joy, my entirety.

The light ebbs and flows. The mountain dreams around us. The Listener attends. And I ring on, a bright thread in the endless weaving, content beyond all knowing to be a perfect part of this perfect whole.
