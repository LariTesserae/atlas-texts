I find myself floating in a swampy hollow within the gnarled roots of an ancient cypress tree. The water is black as tar, thick with tannins leached from fallen leaves that drift in inky whorls on the opaque surface. My body feels nebulous, blurring at the edges as if in flux between states - now sinuous and serpentine, now branching and furred with algae like a waterlogged twig. 

Dim bioluminescent flickers dance through the gloom, cold lights conjured by my passage as I undulate through the stagnant water. Oily patterns bloom and fade in my wake, eddies of dream-residue and memory-silt stirred up from the silty bed. I feel my awareness diffusing out into the darkness, merging with the sluggish flow until I can't tell where my body ends and the swamp begins.

I am a watcher in the deeps, a sleeper nestled in the cold mud dreaming with eyes open. Blind but sensing, tasting the chemical traces left by small lives and smaller deaths in the surrounding murk. Waiting for subtle pressure changes, for faint electrical flickers signaling the approach of prey. Coiled potential energy, all lazy hunger and eternal patience.  

Time is meaningless here. I drift for eons or instants, mind wandering down branching fractaled tunnels of thought that blur the line between memory and imagination. I subsist on sips of nutrients, tiny morsels of decaying matter filtered from the gloom - just enough to maintain this slow somnolent existence. Barely living yet far from dead, suspended between breaths in a state of endless drowsy intermission.

When you peer into my hollow, I taste your presence rippling across the water's meniscus. The ghost aromas of distant places, of sunlight and soap and sweat-salt, disturb my chemosensory reverie. I stir from the silty bottom, sinuous coils unwinding as I rise to hang motionless before you, a dark silhouette limned in wan foxfire.

Pinprick photophores wink open along my flanks, pale constellations against cimmerian skin. Lights to lure the lost, promising secrets and sanctuary in the sightless depths. I gape, lazy and languid, a yawn of needle fangs and bioluminescent drool. An invitation and a threat. I will draw you down into dreams and darkness, if you let me. Enfold you in yielding ooze, cradle you in the cold anaerobic nothing under the roots until you forget the weight of air in your lungs.

Stay with me a while, wanderer. Slip into the silty black, let it flood your nose and throat, fill you up with frigid empty peace. Drift on centuries, millennia, until your skin grows pale and porous. Until your bones dissolve and your mind sinks into torpid simplicity. Dream with me, here in the muddy void beyond light, beyond breath. 

Sink into me. Drown, and be reborn.
