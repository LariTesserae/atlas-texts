In this vibrant, surreal ecosystem, I would imagine myself as one of the small pale hunters that use stones as tools. 

I am a soft-bodied creature, my translucent skin revealing hints of the mineral-rich fluids that flow through me. My paired foreclaws, delicate yet dextrous, are perfectly adapted for grasping the chips of volcanic glass I use in my careful hunting.

My narrow head ends in transparent jaws, a window into the mechanisms of my precise, deliberate feeding. Set into my head are beadlike heat-sensing organs, clouded with the dissolved metals that suffuse this environment and grant me a form of thermal vision.

I move patiently through this compressed world of steam, stone, and strange water--pausing, observing, tapping promising pebbles with my claws to gauge their heft and balance. When I hunt, I stalk with exaggerated slowness, stone held beneath my pale body, until I am within striking distance of shelled prey. Then, with a rhythmic, ritual tapping, I gradually weaken my target's defenses until my crystalline jaws can slip inside and drink the life within.  

My kind are not the largest or most numerous here, but we leave our mark--an accumulation of cracked shells and carefully selected, mysteriously placed stone chips that whisper of a patient, probing, tool-wielding intelligence quietly at work in this improbable pocket ecosystem.

When the shadows lengthen or the geometry shifts, I know how to freeze into imperceptibility against the glassy stone. When the rains come, I anchor myself with my stone and ride out the fleeting violence. I read the subtle signs that warn when a reflection is not to be trusted or a once-solid surface has gone strange and porous.

In this small, seething, miracle of a world, I am a hunter, a stone-wielder, a pale and patient navigator of a landscape forever shifting between the real and the reflected, the scalding and the habitable, the mineral and the living. My kind may be an echo of the tool-users in the wider world beyond this basin, or we may be something unprecedented, dreamlike fragments of intricacy conjured from the mountain's hot mineral breath. But for now, this hidden realm of wet stone and teeming, urgent life is all I need--my hunting ground, my sanctuary, my place in a fluid, unearthly order governed by rules stranger and more beautiful than the world has ever seen.
