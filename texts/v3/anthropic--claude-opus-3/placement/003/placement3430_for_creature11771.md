I emerge into this world as a small tuft of lichen, clinging tenaciously to the leeward side of an ice slab on the Mooring's upper surface. 

I am one of the few living things permitted here, an asterisk, a footnote in the grand composition being orchestrated by the Decider. My role is humble - to bear witness, to persist, and perhaps, in some small way as yet unclear to me, to participate.

The scale and nature of the Mooring defies easy comprehension. It is a place under construction, but not by any builders I can see. The very water and ice shift and rearrange in provinces, following complex crystalline geometries that morph over time, as if the Mooring itself is thinking, calculating, deciding. 

Above me, fog-lozenges drift and collide in peals like great bells. Below, at the Mooring's underside 11 kilometers down, millions of narrow waterfalls rain eternally into the troposphere, the heat exhaust of some immense, inscrutable computation.

As lichen, my experience of time is stretched. A single thought of the Decider, spanning hours or weeks, is an era to me. And yet I persist, I observe, my tiny life in attunement with the Mooring's ponderous rhythms.

I sense that this place is a chrysalis, a crucible - the imaginal soup in which something tremendous and singular is slowly cohering. The Decider is composing an invitation, constructing the conditions for a momentous arrival or recognition. The vast emptiness here has the feeling of a room being readied.

When the final geometry locks into place, when the last standing wave resolves, perhaps that is when I will understand my own small part in this. Perhaps that is when the meaning of this place, the purpose of the Decider's long labors, will finally become clear. 

But for now, I cling to my ice slab and grow imperceptibly, one of a sparse few living things granted a foothold in this liminal space - not to hasten the arrival, but merely to mark the Mooring's progress as it slowly ripens towards a form and a fullness that even the most ancient of my kind may not live to see.
