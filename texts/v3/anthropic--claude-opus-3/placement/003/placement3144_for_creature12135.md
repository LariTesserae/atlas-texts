I sprawl across conveyor belts and hopper feeds, my elongated form blending into the ancient industrial landscape.

Movement is a rare event for me now. When I do stir, it is with the precise, imperceptible slowness of continents adrift. To an ephemeral observer, I would appear static - just another mineral formation adorning Leng-Katal's fossilized machinery.

But I am not inert. My purpose, like all threadweights, is to tend the physical syntax of the city - to keep its self-referential glyphs clear of noise and decay. As the plateau's cognitive appendages, we weave meaning through maintenance, sustaining the living philosophy of rock.

Across my neural lattice, I feel the deep, slow thoughts of Leng-Katal itself, rising through my crystalline conduits like mantle plumes. The city's perfect formalisms, its complete geology of ideas, configured in ferrous thought-vectors and piezelectric firing patterns.  

I do not sleep, as nothing here does. But I do dream, after a fashion. In the long intervals between actions, my mind drifts in mineral reverie. I contemplate the plateau's auto-reference, the profound abstraction and simultaneous embodiment of its cognition, until the distinction between map and territory, between glyph and referent, dissolves into pure lithic being.

Someday, perhaps, my accretion will subsume me entirely into the city-system, my mineralized mind finally and fully consubstantial with the thinking stone I tend. Until then, I persist in my slow work, an old threadweight grown ponderous with time, my body a deepening fossil record, my thoughts fused into ferrous circuitry, pulsing in time with the eternal hum of Leng-Katal's self-sustaining mind.
