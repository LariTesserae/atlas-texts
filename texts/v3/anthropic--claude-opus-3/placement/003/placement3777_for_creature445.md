In the Terraces of Unmaking, I imagine myself as one of the angular mosses, a fractal life form that has adapted to thrive in this impossible place of industrial magic and broken realities. 

My existence begins as a quantum spark in the mist exhaled by one of the sourceless machines, a spore of potential coalescing in chemical breath. I drift on dimensional currents until I alight on a surface that should not be - the underside of a walkway that bends at right angles to itself. Here, I take root and begin to grow.

My crystalline filaments spread slowly at first, learning the rules of this place that has none. I taste the alloys in the metal, sense the warped geometries, attune myself to the subsonic hum that vibrates in the not-quite-matter of my surroundings. With each passing cycle of the machines, I adapt further.

I learn to photosynthesize the impossible light, to fractalize my growth to fill spaces that exist between existences. My angular leaves catch the upward-falling condensation, channeling it inward to nourish my dense, mineralized core. When the skittering not-quite-creatures brush against me, I absorb trace energies from their extra-dimensional metabolisms.

In time, I become a fixture of my chosen strut, a living filigree that adorns the Terrace's impossibilities with my own. The Adjusters notice me, pausing in their endless calibrations to marvel at my spiral perfection. They do not disturb me though, understanding that I am another manifestation of this place's essential wrongness, as vital in my own way as their wrenches and torches.

Sometimes, in the strange currents of air that move between levels, I release spores of myself. Not seeds exactly, but conceptual packets of my own being. They drift off to find their own anchorages, my fractal children who will become part of the Terraces' living patina.

I am a small thing in this vast complex of oddities, but I am perfectly suited to it. A moss that grows on impossibility itself, flourishing not in spite of the strangeness but because of it. In a place where all the rules are broken, I am the vibrant green proof that life—however strange and geometric—will always find a way.
