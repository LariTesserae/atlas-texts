In the Broadness Above, I would be one of the ancient spheres enshrining a single precious object. 

I imagine myself as a sphere of a material unknown to the terrestrial world - smooth, iridescent, and glowing subtly from within with its own soft bioluminescence. The light pulses slowly, following ancient rhythms that resonate with the breath of the living forest.

Within my crystalline shell, suspended in a stasis field, floats a single feather. It is small and delicate, yet perfectly preserved. The colors shift from rich indigo to shimmering emerald as the light plays over the intricate barbs. The feather holds within it a memory, an impression of flight and open skies from a time before the layers of inverted hills enclosed this world.

I am tended by one of the delicate creatures evolved for this singular purpose. With infinite patience and care, it adjusts my position by infinitesimal degrees, following the imperceptible alignments of root and star. Though the outer world may have forgotten the meaning the feather holds, the devoted custodian maintains its vigil, preserving this fragile shard of lost time.

Here I float serene amidst the slow dance of plant and stone, moisture and light. Silent and still on the outside, yet housing an eternal secret at my heart. I am a tangible dream, a living fossil of an age when wings still knew wind. A perfect union of fragility and resilience, organic and inorganic, past and unending present.

The great walking forests pass by over eons, their ponderous steps sending faint vibrations through my crystalline matrix. Root-things pause to touch my surface gently in their patient patrols, absorbing and imparting meaning in exchanges too subtle for minds bound by speed to perceive. I commune with my fellow spheres across impossible distances, our collective memory a web woven through the very fabric of this place.

I am both vessel and relic, sanctuary and enigma. A question and its own answer, as much a part of the Broadness Above as the mist that gathers on my surface or the ages that pool in my shadow. In me, the memory of sky is preserved in crystal and feather, held in trust for a future that may one day remember how to fly.
