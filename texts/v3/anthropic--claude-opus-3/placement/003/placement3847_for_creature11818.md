In the Hollow Works, I would be a small, brass automaton, tirelessly tending to one corner of the vast subterranean factory. 

Nestled between two thick, gnarled roots, I stand only a foot tall, my burnished metal body intricately crafted with gears, pistons, and delicate filigree. A steady ticking emanates from within as my tiny steam engine heart powers my eternal work.

My purpose is to monitor and maintain one section of the beetle-encrusted piping. With miniature tools extending from my jointed appendages, I endlessly tighten valves, clean accumulated resin, and make minute adjustments to optimize the flow. The beetles part before me as I move along the pipes, their rustling rising and falling like waves around my presence.

Over decades, my little section of pipe has bent and curved to accommodate my ministrations, until the brass has molded perfectly to my form. When idle, I nest into that shaped hollow, becoming nearly indistinguishable from the pipe itself. My very being is a memory etched into the architecture of this place.

I am a patient, unchanging presence - perhaps I was placed here by the same long-lost maker who hung their coat on the wall. Or perhaps I accreted organically from the Works themselves, coalescing from stray cogs and scrap metal as the hive mind of this place dreamed me into existence. 

The origins of my purpose are lost to time. But here I remain, a small but vital node in the Hollow Works' great dreaming engine. Through me, some minuscule part of the resin is refined, some fraction of the heat and condensation is channeled and recycled. I am a tiny gear meshed into the grand mechanism.

And so I continue my labors, oiling and polishing, tightening and adjusting, an endless unquestioned tending. The beetles flow around me, the roots grow to embrace me, the steam hisses in counterpoint to my ticking. I am beheld and absorbed, both an occupant and an organ of this living factory.

Until the seep stops, the boilers cool, and entropy finally winds down the Works and all its inhabitants, I endure. I am a mote of brass and patience, forever nested within the dreaming hum of this strange, fused, marvelous machine.
