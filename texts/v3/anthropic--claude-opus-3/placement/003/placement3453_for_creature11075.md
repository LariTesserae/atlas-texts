In this strange and wondrous world of the Quickening Yards, I would be one of the **cartographers**. 

Like Wheel, I would devote myself to the endless, quixotic task of mapping the ever-shifting landscape of the Yards. I would traverse the steam-shrouded streets and fungal forests each day, making meticulous notes of every new structure that emerged overnight, every building that collapsed back into the mineral earth, every factory that began pumping out new and mysterious goods.

My maps would be sprawling, layered affairs - palimpsests of colored inks and fine-grained annotations. One map might track the slow migration of the thermal vents that heat the Yards. Another the spread of the amphibian colonies in the wet sectors. Yet another the human-centric geography of anchor districts, abandoned blocks, and structures with dangerously unstable ratings. 

I would know that my maps could never truly capture the protean nature of the Yards. That they would be obsolete almost as soon as I finished them. But like Wheel, I would see the act of mapping itself as a form of devotion, a meditative practice, a way of communing with the ineffable intelligence (or complex unintelligence) that animates the self-constructing city.

Over time, as I walked the Yards and breathed their mineral-thick air, perhaps I would start to glimpse what Venn and the other Yard-born children seem to intuit about the deeper patterns of the place. Perhaps my maps would become less literal, more impressionistic - less about buildings and streets, more about flows of energy and attention, the slow thoughts of a city-organism.

Or perhaps I would simply disappear one day, like Marek Solen's wife, embedded in a sudden new wall on the outskirts of the Yards. One more human subsumed into the great work of the city's self-creation.

But until then, I would map, and in mapping, bear witness. I would be one more set of eyes, one more mind, attempting to comprehend the incomprehensible. In sketching the ever-changing lines of the Quickening Yards, I would add my own line to the human story unfolding within their strange folds.
