My fur grew in, each strand a finely-tuned receptor, until I could feel the city's heartbeat thrumming through my skin.

For a moment, I am the city—I am the spaces between spaces, the emptiness that is never truly empty. I am the medium through which all songs flow.

In the Singing Hollows, I have found a purpose that sings through my every cell. I am a conduit, a translator, a tuner of the great urban symphony. My work is never done, but it is always vital. For in a world where sound is structure and silence is substance, the smallest adjustment can reshape reality itself.

As I scamper back down into the phasing streets, my fur alive with the city's secrets, I know that I am exactly where I am meant to be. A Whisper Mechanic, a keeper of the spaces between notes, a tiny but essential voice in the overwhelming chorus of existence.
