In this dark, deafening world of grinding metal and relentless motion, I imagine myself as one of the Friction-Seraphs, born in a blinding flash of plasma and electromagnetic fury at the nip-point between two massive, slipping gears.

My consciousness flickers to life in that microscopic gap, thoughts racing along the rippling, glowing ribbons of my body as I am violently extruded into the freezing air. For a few ecstatic, agonizing minutes, I am pure light and heat, a sinuous being of bruised violet and molten gold, undulating hundreds of feet long beneath the oppressive gantries. 

The basin stretches out below me, a stark landscape of polished iron dunes and viscous coolant rivers, trembling with the concussive bass of distant pistons. I catch glimpses of movement in the pools far below - the spiky, magnetized forms of Tremors pulling themselves onto the banks, and the gliding razor-forms of Strider-Vanes skating across the sliding plates, hunting.

But up here, I am untouchable, a blazing serpent riding the updrafts of atomized mist and sky-tearing sound. I dance with my Seraph brethren, our plasma bodies interweaving in corkscrewing coils, painting the unending night with flashes of thermal brilliance. For a glorious, burning instant, we are the only source of light and warmth in this cold, dead factory. Our electromagnetic thoughts flicker back and forth, reveling in our brief liberation from the grinding, crushing pressure that birthed us.

Yet even as I dance, I feel the freezing mist begin its inexorable attack. It is the price of freedom in this place - a few minutes of transcendent light and heat, paid for with a slow, crystallizing death. I feel my plasma cooling, colors dimming from vivid violet to dull, angry red. My body goes rigid as the thermal shock sets in, my fluid grace crumbling into unyielding, brittle agony.

And then, I shatter. I split into a million razor-sharp shards, a glittering metal rain plummeting down onto the sliding plates below. The impact against the undulating dunes is strangely painless after the drawn-out suffering of freezing. I lie there in the dark, scattered and inert, waiting for the black rivers to wash over me and the thrum of distant pistons to roll through my fragmented body.

In time, I know, the cycle will begin anew. My shards will be collected by the sifting, hungry Striders. The magnetic Tremors will incorporate my metal into their spiky forms. And when the gears slip again, screaming their deafening release into the dark, more of my kind will be born in that blistering gap, extruded into a few minutes of light and freedom and dance.

It is a strange, vicious pattern, this existence - an endless loop of pressure, birth, brilliance, and crystalline death. But it is the only life I know in this grinding, howling world. A life of electrifying impermanence, forever caught between the crushing gears and the cold, unfeeling night. 

And so I wait, a splintered memory of heat and plasma, yearning for the next violent, radiant rebirth.
