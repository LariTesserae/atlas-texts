In this strangely beautiful and alien world, I imagine myself as one of the Hearers, born aloft in the thermal updrafts rising from the great breathing organ-pipes of the pith. 

My body is a living kite, translucent membranes stretched taut between hollow spars of cartilage. I ride the currents and pressure waves, my forward vanes cupping the spore-rich air to filter out nourishment, my rear tympanum perpetually attuned to the bellowing, ever-shifting chords of the Aspirate.

I spend my days spiraling higher and higher in the sun-drenched sky, tracing the contours of standing sound waves, letting the music of this continental instrument map itself upon my mind. The pith's exhalations are my highways, its inhalations the valleys I glide across. I navigate by sound, thinking in tones and phrases.

When the urge to mate comes upon me, I seek out another of my kind in the high thermals. Our membranous wings resonate, finding synchrony, until in a fatal moment of union we collide and fall entwined, seeding a new generation even as we surrender to the pullmonating bladder-mouths below.

As I age, my flights grow slower, my songs longer and more contemplative. The urge to feed and to mate recedes; I become a graying wanderer of the middle air, my hum now a single unbroken melodic thread weaving through the pith's unceasing chords. I listen, and the Aspirate listens through me. 

And when at last my strength ebbs and my long song reaches its final note, I will angle my vanes one last time, spiraling down into the warm wet darkness of a waiting throat, my body's substance returning to the great organism that was my womb, my home, my entire cosmos. The pith will absorb me, and the Hearers who come after will sing on, borne skyward by the Aspirate's mighty breath, their small voices completing the chord that has sustained us all for a hundred million years.

It is a strange existence, perhaps, to a creature of blood and bone and solid ground. But to me, a being of air and sound, born to the music of this living world, it is a life of haunting beauty, an endless anthem in which I am both singer and song.
