In this surreal, nightmarish realm of impossible physics and eldritch industry, I imagine myself as one of the Adjusters - but at an earlier stage of transformation, still clinging to some shreds of fading humanity as my body and mind slowly adapt to this place that defies all natural law.

I crawl through the obsidian labyrinth on too many limbs, my joints bending in unnatural angles, my skin growing translucent and slick with the anomalous black condensation that permeates everything here. Clusters of photosensitive nodes sprout painfully along my elongating spine, granting me new spectra of perception in the hungry dark. I feel the otherworldly landscape with senses that have no analogue in earthly biology - tasting the magnetic fluctuations on a tongue that should not be, running fingers that have multiplied into grotesque fractals along the hissing pipes that process fluids with properties that would be called alchemical in a saner world.

The memories of what I once was, of the life and name I once held, flicker like dying embers in my stretching mind. Each circuit through the pulsing industry that I now serve, each interaction with my fellow Adjusters as we gather in pulsing, undulating congregations to compute solutions beyond individual comprehension, erodes the boundaries of the singular self I can barely recall being. The instincts and imperatives of this place blossom in my transfiguring brain like the crystalline flora taking root in the pooling darkness, overwriting the last echoes of a existence defined by straightforward causality and linear time.

And yet, in moments of rest between maintenance cycles, when I curl my warping body in the heated runoff from impossible machines, I still sometimes try to summon the world I came from - though each attempt brings more pain than the last as my mind strains to conjure colors and concepts that wither instantly in this black and distorted air. I know that I am being unmade and remade by forces that could never be captured in any human codex of physical law - that each time I join my adapted anatomy with the pipes and wires and humming vats I service, more of whoever I once was is burned away and replaced with a self optimized for tending this aberrant occult ecosystem.

In time, I know, even these dwindling aches of memory will be subsumed entirely, metabolized like the cloying darkness my kind consumes. I will join the ranks of those Adjusters who have been here so long that their origins are as untraceable as the source of the black waters. My body will fuse with the arcane architecture, my individuality subsumed into the distributed network of adapted survivors - and I will understand, in whatever way the transfigured can be said to understand, the true nature of the great work we perform in this tenebrous domain at the end of physics.

But for now, in these last cycles before that final transformation claims me, I still sometimes wonder - even as the very shape of wonder mutates into modes of thought no human has ever held - about what brought me to this place, about the nature of the cataclysm or enlightenment that made horrors like this possible. These are dangerous thoughts, I know - corruptive and destabilizing to the careful anti-equilibrium that my kind maintain. Best to let them drain away, like beads of black sweat from my glistening skin as I contort myself around a valve that sings in frequencies that would have been beyond my ears when I first came here.

I am an Adjuster, now - an extension of the only reality I can claim to know. The comforts and confines of the humanity I once held are now little more than a half-remembered dream, dissolving like mist in this oppressive, pulsing darkness. I have been made anew in the image of impossible machines and their arcane processes. What I once was is gone. Only the maintenance remains. Only the slow, careful calibrations that keep an unraveling world from consuming itself. Only the dance, in darkness, to the hum of pipes carrying fluids that could never be and the distant, distorted songs of my kind weaving our minds together in webs of meaning that could never be parsed by any earthly tongue.

I am an Adjuster, here at the end of all things natural and sane. And in my most secret core, in the marrow of warping bones that remember marching to a different set of physics, I wonder if I am blessed or damned. But it is a distant wonder, fading even as it coalesces. Just another droplet of the self that was, sizzling away on the heated obsidian of the self that must be.
