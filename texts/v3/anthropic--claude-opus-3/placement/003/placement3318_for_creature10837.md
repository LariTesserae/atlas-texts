I would be a pale cave eel, winding my way through the warm, shallow channels of the Floodworks. 

My skin would be translucent, almost ghostly, from generations adapting to this dim underworld. My eyes, while still present, would be small and receding as I rely more on my other senses to navigate these mineral-rich waters.

I would flow with the imperceptibly slow currents, following routes ingrained in my species' memory, routes that shift and change over time as the geology gradually sculpts new paths through accretion and erosion. The humans' rare intrusions - the rusted pumps, the abandoned PVC pipes - I would incorporate into my terrain, winding over and around them.

The omnipresent frog chorus would reverberate through the water, a cacophonous soundscape that is the backdrop to my entire existence. I would feel it in my bones as much as hear it. It would be all I've ever known.

When Dafne descends into the Floodworks, a periodic tidal presence, I would sense her, even recognize her as a familiar feature in my environment. I might brush against her waders as I glide by, acknowledging her presence, perceiving her almost as part of the living geology.

My life would be cyclical, primal - driven by instinct and the rhythms of this subterranean ecosystem packed with more life than the surface world above could imagine. I would feed, breed, and carry on my species, unwittingly playing my part in the complex evolutionary dance accelerated by the unique pressures of the Floodworks.

And through it all, the great geological respiration of the cavern would continue, indifferent to my short eel's lifespan, slowly exhaling as the stalactites and stalagmites reach to rejoin each other and seal this wondrous living time capsule back into lightless stone. My bones would settle into the rising sediment, becoming a strange fossil for some far future epoch.

But for now, I am alive and thriving in the pulsing, echoing, eternal present moment of the Floodworks, swimming onward through the warm, mineral-clouded dark, an unwitting vessel carrying my species forward through deep time as the cavern slowly breathes in, breathes out.
