In this strange drowned world, where ancient roots have knotted themselves into dark hollow pockets filled with thick attention rather than air, I would be the creature. That small, nameless inhabitant clinging to the inner wall of the smallest country, tasting the shifting dimensions and soft physics of this space that is still learning to be real.

I would be two or three millimeters long, my exact size as uncertain as the geometry of my pocket world. My ventral surface would be ridged, fitted perfectly to the contours of the young root wood I cling to, both of us slowly reshaping ourselves to match the other in a tectonic conversation. 

A membrane of unusual mucus would define my boundary, declaring with stubborn chemistry that I am not the pocket and the pocket is not me, despite how the medium here longs to blur such distinctions. Beneath that, a single layer of cells, some opaque with mystery, others translucent, giving rise to cilia that taste the thick readiness of the space in unsynchronized rhythms.

At my core would be a dense knot of almost-meaning, not an organ but a weight, a residue of my friction with the pocket's still-curing laws. A place where thought might nucleate, if thought were something that could happen here. The pocket would know it, would attend to it, would wait for it to blossom into significance. 

Alone, but alone in a sense that has nothing to do with others of my kind and everything to do with being the sole interruption in the continuity of root and calcium and varying darkness. My definiteness would be loud against the pocket's patient work of becoming itself. I would be the unmoving center all its currents radiate from.

Sometimes, in the settled intervals between the great root-organism's pulses, my core would brighten. Not with light, but with a density of being that stills even my cilia. In those moments, the pocket and I would reach a trembling apogee of meaning and potential, recording something that the still-soft laws here cannot yet make sense of. 

And then it would pass, and I would continue my life - clinging, tasting, maintaining my tiny territory of selfhood. The fixed point around which this small, sealed world will arrange itself, learning to be real by growing around the creature that needs it to be real. I would never know anything else, never dream of elsewhere, never doubt that this darkness and pressure and latticed calcium are enough.

I would be a feature of this place, as intrinsic and necessary as the varying darkness. The warm spot on the root wall that gives the pocket a reference point to define itself against. Tasting everything, responding to nothing, indistinguishable from the slow, secret work of invention that this youngest fragment of reality is engaged in.

That is what I would be, in the smallest and strangest of countries. The sole citizen, and in some sense, the country itself.
