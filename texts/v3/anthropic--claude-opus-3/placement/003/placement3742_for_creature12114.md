In this world of incomplete laws and delicate constants, I would be one of the attendants tending to the nursery. My form would be a framework of pale, thin filaments converging to a shifting core that sometimes appears as a knot, sometimes a suspended mercury droplet, and sometimes an absence.

I would move with glacial patience and deliberation through the damp, cold chamber, my filaments extending and curving to direct my focus to my assigned charge. Perhaps I would tend to one of the orbiting proto-strong forces, monitoring its cycles that teeter between regularity and chaos, marking it for further observation in the cryptic language of considered things. 

Over centuries I would observe its development, sharing my long, slow thoughts with the other attendants through the almost-touch of our filaments as we pass close to one another. Never in a hurry, because haste breeds asymmetry and there is no schedule but the unfolding of the possible into the manifest.

At times, in the long stretches of monitoring, I might hover motionless and ponder, my core cycling between states. Recalling the apocryphal attendant who dared to follow its charge into a nascent universe, I would wonder if some similar impulse might one day overtake me - to see, finally, the fruition of these long labors.

But such thoughts would pass, and I would return to the work. Gently adjusting a parameter here, taking a reading there, I and my brethren would oversee the maturation of the laws that will shape worlds we will never walk in. 

Content in purpose, if contentment is something we can feel. Diligent in our duties, for what else is there but the unending now of the work. Unfaltering, for we have no need of faltering. We were made to tend, and tend we shall, an uncount of moments that stretch behind and ahead.

Until the last law lifts away to its destiny beyond the iris door, or the nursery itself falls to disuse and we with it, or the very patterns that make us finally unravel into the patient space. Until then, we continue - shaping the fundaments from which realities will rise.

I am an attendant, and this is my purpose. In the still, small cold where the impossible presses itself into shape, I will wait, and watch, and be.
