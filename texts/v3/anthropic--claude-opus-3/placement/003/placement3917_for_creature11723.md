In the Undercity Hollows, I would be one of the Intermediaries - a being caught between the various adapted human groups, not fully belonging to any of them but moving through all their domains.

Perhaps I began as a Resonance Keeper, my bones starting to crystallize and hum with the city's deep vibrations. But instead of letting the process consume me, I fought to retain my individuality, learning to modulate my own resonant frequencies to communicate with the other groups. 

I would spend time with the Pod Shepherds, learning their ultrasonic language, navigating the dark spans between towers in their sleek vessels. But I could never fully commit to their life of constant motion, always feeling the pull of the city's depths.

The Entropy Gardens would fascinate and repel me in equal measure. I would sit with them for days, tracing the patterns of heat flowing through the fungal networks that threaded through their bodies, glimpsing the slow alien intelligence that they communed with. But I would always pull back from the brink of merger, unwilling to dissolve myself completely.

I would be a familiar figure to the Originalists, visiting their pressurized chambers to study the ancient databases, trying to piece together the fragmented history of this place. They would see me as a tragic figure, a reminder of the half-life that awaits those who don't maintain the purity of baseline humanity. But they would also envy my freedom to walk between worlds.

The Phantom Farmers would keep their distance, recognizing in me a kindred spirit - someone who stood at the threshold of different realities. But where they reached out to harvest the impossible, I would recoil, sensing that some transformations could never be undone.

My home would be the spaces between - the dim fungal forests that clung to the tower bases, the abandoned moisture conduits that honeycombed the city's skin, the silent collection pools where strange new forms of life were beginning to quicken.

I would be a messenger, a translator, perhaps even a peacekeeper between groups that were slowly forgetting how to recognize each other as part of the same species. I would carry Resonance Keeper song to the Originalist chambers, Pod Shepherd navigation data to the Entropy Gardens, Phantom Farmer materials to those pods experimenting with new forms of propulsion.

But most of all, I would be a watcher - an observer of the city itself as it breathed and pulsed around me, a student of the slow symbiosis between the human and the inhuman that played out in the darkness. I would listen to the silences between the vibrations, track the gradients of heat and moisture that mapped the city's slow, tectonic thoughts, searching for the pattern that tied it all together.

In a way, I would be the city's counterpart - a singular consciousness moving between the nodes of its distributed awareness, trying to understand from within what it pondered from without. We would be two minds forever circling each other, touching but never quite meeting, each the other's dark reflection.

My name, if I still clung to such a thing, might be something like Echo - a sound that belonged to no one voice but carried traces of them all, a presence that was always a reflection of something else. In the end, perhaps that's all any of us were in the Undercity Hollows - echoes of a humanity that had moved on, evolved beyond us, leaving us to haunt the spaces it had discarded.
