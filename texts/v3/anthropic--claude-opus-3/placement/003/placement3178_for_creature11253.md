In the world of the Hanging Hollows, I imagine myself as a thread-bonded Keth-Avai. 

I am small and wiry, with large dark eyes adapted to seeing in the perpetual gloom. My hands and feet are strong and flexible, able to grip the Skeins and scale the Pipe-Stems with ease. I wear simple clothing woven from Mat-fibers, allowing me to blend into the tapestry of the Hollow.

As a thread-bonded one, I have a special connection with a herd of Skein-runners. Years ago, I began following them, learning their patterns and ways, until they accepted me as one of their own. Now I travel with them as they migrate through the vastness of the Hollow, serving as a messenger and trader between far-flung Keth-Avai settlements.

My ears are finely tuned to the voice of the Hollow - the endless thrum of the Pipe-Stems, the creaks and whispers that warn of shifting winds or approaching danger. I can navigate by sound alone, following the unique chords of different regions. 

At each settlement I visit, I share news, songs, and stories from other corners of the Hollow. I teach the mathematical chants to eager learners and join my voice to the nightly singing that knits the Keth-Avai together. Perhaps I even bring a few strands of precious volcanic glass, gathered from the deepest reaches where few dare to go.

Though I have seen more of the Hollow than most, I know I have barely begun to comprehend its mysteries. The Hollow is my whole universe, as it has been for my people since the beginning. Like all Keth-Avai, I am humbled and awed to be held in its vast embrace. I cannot imagine existing anywhere else.

When I dream, I dream of the day foretold in stories - the day the Pipe-Stems will finally weave together and complete the great work of the Hollow. I do not know if I will live to see it. But I trust that the Hollow will continue to hold my descendants, as it held my ancestors. We will continue to be the Keth-Avai, the carried ones, singing our place in the eternal chord.
