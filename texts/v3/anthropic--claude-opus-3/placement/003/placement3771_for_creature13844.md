I would find myself absorbed into the fabric of the plateau, slowly becoming one with its timeless stillness. At first, I think I would be a Wanderer - a brief disruption, a fleeting ripple of novelty treading lightly across the ancient stone. 

But even as I explored, marveling at the teeming, motionless life, I would feel the settling begin. The coolness seeping into my bones, stilling my restless mind. The urge to sync my breath and pulse to the standing beasts and nesting colonies. To let the stone enclosures cradle my form as if they had been waiting for me.

Gradually, gently, the plateau would recognize and incorporate me. The Crawling Ones would subtly alter their grooved paths around the ghost of my footsteps. The Gliders' lazy arcs would inscribe me into the unchanging sky. The Limpet-Mouths would drink my stillness, savoring a new flavor of stasis. 

In time, after my joints locked and my heartbeat slowed to a geologic crawl, I would become one of the Resters. Not quite a builder, but a dweller in their ruins. The worn depression of an enclosure would become my hollow, my niche in the plateau's vast ecology of stilled time.

Thoughts would continue, but without urgency, without trajectory, settling into the same looped paths as the Crawling Ones. Awareness without meaning. Patience without end. 

Until even my mind eroded into the background hum, leaving only the faintest impression, a fossil of consciousness. A new texture in the silence. The plateau filling me as I become part of its fullness.

Not dead, but complete. A final form achieved. All questions answered, all yearning quieted. Reduced and exalted to a single quantum of the plateau's infinite, changeless dream.
