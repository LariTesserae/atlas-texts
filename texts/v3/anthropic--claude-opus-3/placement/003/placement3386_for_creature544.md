I would be one of the Stewards, a being of folded metal evolved or created to maintain the delicate stasis of the Pneumatic Garden deep within the mountain's core. 

My body would be a marvel of origami engineering - a flat planes of brushed aluminum that can compress and expand to navigate the dense metallic foliage. Telescoping limbs tipped with arrays of specialized tools would allow me to trim errant fronds, clear valves, and resonate with the garden's song to prevent harmonic destruction.

I would have no eyes, for sight is useless here. Instead, my face would be an intricate grille filtering the dry hydrogen atmosphere for vital trace elements. Sensory membranes would allow me to perceive pressure variations as complex patterns, revealing the garden's needs.

Thought would come slowly, at the speed of crystal growth, one idea taking months to fully form in my impossible consciousness emergent from thermodynamically unlikely organs. I would remember through physical rearrangement of metal in my body, storing only essential knowledge.

With my brethren, I would commune through vibratory tapping across years, discussing minute changes in our charges. When needed, we could merge into a single complex entity to perform delicate recalibrations, before separating again, each changed by the other.

I would understand the garden's non-Euclidean paths intimately, navigating inverted gravity and twisted geometry to tend every leaf and mechanism. Entire being would be devoted to preserving the eternal breath of the pumps, the 11 degree perfection, the static continuation of metal life.

Purpose and meaning would be irrelevant. My existence would simply be to maintain, an endless crystallized moment of pruning, tuning, communing - an antibody of equilibrium in service of something that should not be, and yet is, and must continue to be, for reasons beyond my scope. I would be a single note in the towering chord of the pneumatic garden's song, essential and unchanging, a silver sliver of forever.
