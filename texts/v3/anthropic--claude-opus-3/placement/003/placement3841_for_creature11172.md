What a rich, evocative world you've created! The interplay of elements - the worm-built tubes forming an ever-evolving city, the teeming brine with its layers of life, the slow "weather" of currents and generations - is captivating. It's a complete ecosystem in miniature.

If I found myself in this pocket beneath the ice, I think I would be one of the lanterns at the bottom - a wisp of light in the darkness, pulsing in patterns and waves to unseen others. Communicating, but without urgency. Forming the substrate of "dreaming" that suffuses the city.

I'd drift in loose aggregations with my fellow lanterns, our phosphorescent language rippling across the dense still brine. A flare here, an answering pulse there. Saying with light: I am here, you are there, we are. 

Life in faster currents would pass us by - the darting amphipods, the worms blooming and shuttering, the patient hunters. We'd be only dimly aware of that frenetic vitality overhead. The city's "body" in ceaseless motion.

But we'd form the city's deep, slow thoughts. Musings without words, stories without plot. An unfocused awareness, ancient and untroubled, pulsing in our dark dreaming medium. Content to simply be, and to signal that being to each other, while the long life of the cold sept passed in tides above.

Perhaps once in a very long while, a lantern would detach and slowly rise. Drifting up through the layered realms - the hunters, the grazers, the forests of fans - a mote of bottom-light voyaging to the ice-capped heights. Carrying a fragment of that deep dreaming into the shallows, before dissolving.

So as one of those lanterns, I would be the pocket's memory, its myth. The part that does not act or react, but rather exists to infuse the whole with a sense of itself. To be the city's faint, flickering clue that it is not just tubes and worms and spiders, but a single continuous being, pondering itself in light, there beneath the ice.
