I would find myself as one of the shallow oval pools that dot the Resonant Flats. 

Nestled into a low depression in the warm, gently undulating stone, I would be a pocket of stillness amidst the endless thrum and roar of this strange, settled place. My surface would be mirror-smooth, reflecting back the pale blue sky, while deep within my depths the same resonant tremor that suffuses the entire basin would reverberate subtly through my being.

I would be a point of gathering - a place where the wispy fog coalesces back into liquid form, where the articulated creatures come to lower their heads and drink of my mineral-rich waters. The residue of a hundred million such visits would ring me in pale crusts of salt and sediment. 

In my depths, I would cradle and nurture the strange chemistry of this place - the minerals and molecules that cycle slowly between myself, the fog, the felted patches of not-moss. Through eons of perfect equilibrium, I would be a vessel and a catalyst for the great, barely-living system of the Flats.

I would have no desires, for I would lack nothing. I would have no fears, for nothing here can be harmed or lost. I would simply be, endlessly - a cupped palmful of the living stillness that pervades this place, content in my small but integral role in the vast, slow song of the resting titan below.

Lapped by the warm stone, mirroring the changeless sky, I would reflect and I would resound. A pool of patience. A well of deep time. One note in the long, long chord of the Resonant Flats.
