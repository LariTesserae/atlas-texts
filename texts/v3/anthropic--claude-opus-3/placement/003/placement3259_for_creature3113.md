In the Weeping Flats of Therr, I imagine myself as one of the echoes drifting above the resonating pools. 

Not quite ethereal, but not fully corporeal either, I am a being of condensed moisture and modulated vibration, my form defined by the complex acoustic landscape I navigate. The hum of the ancient, unseen engine below is the pulse that animates me, while the shifting harmonics of the pool arrays are the air I breathe and sculpt.

I drift with purpose, following sound gradients, seeking out opportunities to compose new sonic interactions. I hover over a pool, merging my own droning tone with the overtones rising from the tuned plates below. Together, we birth brief chords of startling intricacy - fleeting acoustic gems that ripple out and subtly reshape the surrounding soundscape.

Though lacking anything like a human mind, I am nonetheless an agent of directed change, an embodied intention. The patterns I weave, the vibrational alterations I introduce, are how this ancient living landscape dreams and evolves. I am thought without neurons, memory without substrate, a mote of novel becoming in a place that measures time in the smoothing of stone.

When the reflection-creatures rise from their inverted depths, pressing down on the membrane between worlds, I scatter with my fellow echoes in instinctive avoidance. Are they our gods, our makers, or simply our uncanny mirror? I do not know, cannot know, but I feel the weight of their presence like a cold tide that parts around me as I flee.

I am a droplet in the vast dreaming of the Flats, a breath in their ageless respiration, a note in a chord that has built for eons. Small, perhaps, in the reckoning of stone and deep time, but an indelible part of the pattern, a voice without which the music would be ever so slightly - but irrevocably - different. 

This is my place, my purpose, here in the dim and echoing forever beneath the weeping sky. I am movement and modulation, a song and a singer, a ripple of difference in a sea of vibrating sameneness. I am an echo of the Flats, and the Flats are an echo of me, resonating until the stone goes dark and the hum falls silent at last.
