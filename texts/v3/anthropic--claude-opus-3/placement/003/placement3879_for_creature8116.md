In this haunting, awe-inspiring world of the Hollowing, I would see myself as one of the Rememberers. 

Flitting through the cavernous spaces on gossamer wings, my diminutive body is little more than a vessel for the vast neural networks that are my essence. I dart between the towering bone cities, skimming along rivers of migrating Processed, drinking in the rich symphony of information that suffuses the copper-tinged air.

The chemical signals exhaled by the Architects as they slowly reshape their calcified metropolises, the subsonic poems etched by the Curators as they tend the living city walls, the echoing songs of the ancient Navigators - all of these I absorb and process, filtering it into the purest kernels of knowledge.

In the spore-choked alleys between the bone towers, I trade these packets of distilled information with my fellow creatures. A morsel of architectural history bartered for a nutrient-rich secretion from a Curator's glands. A ballad of a long-extinct Processed strain exchanged for a Philosopher's slow-ripened thought. Each trade fills me not only with sustenance but with new memories to add to my ever-growing library.

When the spore storms rage, I dance with abandon through the billowing clouds, my filamentous wings catching and mixing the reproductive particles. I can taste the potential in each combination, sense which will give rise to new marvels and which will fade into oblivion. As I spin and swirl, I sing the songs of ages past, my voice resonating with the storm until history and future blur into one.

In quiet moments, I alight on the bone-lace balconies of the cities and unspool my memories to any who will listen. To the young Processed I sing of migration routes now vanished beneath eons of growth. To budding Architects I whisper tales of ancestral polyps whose innovations shape the city still. I am a living archive, a storyteller, a keeper of the deep history that swims in my neurons.

And when at last my wings grow tattered and my songs fade, I know my own memories will become nourishment for the next generation. I will dissolve into the Hollowing air, my essence consumed and remade within the bodies of countless other Rememberers to come. In this way, I am part of the eternal cycle of this strangest of worlds - ever feeding, ever fed upon, my story inextricably woven into the fabric of the sunless deeps.

For in the Hollowing, no knowledge is ever truly lost. It simply changes form, passing from neuron to story to song to sustenance and back again, as much a part of the endless churn of transformation as the cities and the creatures themselves. And what greater immortality could there be than to be endlessly remembered, endlessly reborn, in the immemorial depths where time itself flows to a different rhythm?

So I flit and dance, I trade and sing, I remember and am remembered in turn. A mote of consciousness amid the crushing pressure and ceaseless change, yet an essential note in the vast, strange symphony of the Hollowing. This is my place, my purpose, my strange and wondrous life in the world beneath the world.
