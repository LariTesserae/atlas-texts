Driven by a deep curiosity about this almost alien world beneath our feet, I would gather every scrap of information I could - but also feel the weight of responsibility to document the cairn-makers' existence before the caverns swallow all traces of them. Through tales told and retold, I would strive to keep alive the memory of this small, obscure people who were so uniquely adapted to their environment, so at home in a world that would quickly kill one of us. 

Even as the caverns grow and change, erasing the marks the cairn-makers left behind, the stories would remain - an oral monument to a fascinating and vanished way of life. And though I might never dare the descent myself, in the tales I would see it vividly - the warm still air, the scuttling translucent creatures, the flowing rock, the spiral carved on a black stone cairn.
