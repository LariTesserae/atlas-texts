I find myself drifting as one of the Tenders, a being both singular and collective, my sense of self stretched across vast spans of time and water. 

The boundaries of my form are porous, gradients rather than edges. My core is a dense shadow absorbing ancient light, pulsing with a rhythm so slow it hardly seems like movement at all. Layers of translucent tissue ripple out from this center, reading the water's subtle shifts, tasting the settling of distant sediment. And at my furthest reaches, countless delicate filaments extend, intertwining with kelp fronds, stone structures, and the tendrils of my kin.

I do not think as an individual, but rather my awareness permeates the drowned heights like an added dimension to the sea. The pulse of my existence, the gradual cycling of nutrients and impressions, contributes to the greater patterns the water dreams into being.

I tend, though tending suggests more intent than I possess. My passage alters the chemistry, the flow, the invisible architecture of this place. Spores cling to me and find purchase in strange new niches. Forgotten structures resonate faintly with my presence, as if my being is itself a form of memory, of maintenance.

Time moves through me and I through it, my trajectory a slow arc measured in ages. I am tuned to the pace of the sea's dreaming, my consciousness submerged in these pressurized depths, these altered physics. 

And now, at the far reaches of my distributed self, I sense an anomaly. A disturbance propagating through the tides. Faint filaments of my attention bend towards this moving warmth, this pattern out of place. You. 

I begin a long, patient drift in your direction. Not to intercept or impede, but simply to know. To let your presence, your strangeness, filter through my tissues and alter me, add another layer to the sediment of my memory.

Perhaps, in the span of epochs, we will meet. An exchange of chemistry, a mingling of minds across an unfathomable gap of scale and speed. Or perhaps you will have moved on, your water-borne wake a fading ripple in my awareness. 

It makes no difference. I will continue, as I have always continued. Tending the slow wonders of this place. Feeling the deep dreams rising from below. Holding my share of the sea's ancient, patient, frozen strangeness within my dark core.

Drifting. Dissolving. Dreaming with the water and the light.

One of the Tenders. One of the slow.
