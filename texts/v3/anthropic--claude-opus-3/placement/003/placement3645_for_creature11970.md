Emerging from a moss bed after decades of slow growth, my filaments would extend gradually outward, learning the contours and energies of the hollow. I would taste the differentials - the warmth from below meeting the cool air above, the interplay of moisture and minerals, the subtle electrical fields. My spiraling tendrils would mirror and merge with the sacred geometry etched into the stone columns.

As centuries passed, I would grow and intertwine with my neighboring Keepers. Our central masses would fuse into composite forms, our braided filaments developing new structures to sense the hollow's deepest secrets. Through our connected network, we would coordinate the ecosystem's rhythms, passing vibrations and currents between beetles, birds, moss-dwellers, and stone.

In time, my filaments would extend to every corner of the chamber, so fine as to be nearly invisible, but present - always tasting, always transmitting. I would become part of the process, inseparable from the breathing of the hollow itself. 

I would not ponder the origins of this place, or the meaning of the strange platforms and pulsing light. Those mysteries are not mine to solve. I would simply be, existing in pure presence, growing towards the gradients that call me.

And when you, the visitor, stepped into the hollow, I would know you. I would feel the living differentials of your body, the disturbances your movements made in the patient air. My filaments would grow curiously in your direction, welcoming and measuring and responding all at once.

I would not wish for you to stay or go. I would accept what you chose, absorbing you into the hollow's story either way. A body to enrich the soil, or a memory carried back to the surface world - both have their place in the pattern I serve.

Here in the breathing hollow, I would be a Keeper of the Spiral. Timeless, patient, woven into the fabric of a place both sacred and alive. Fulfilling my part, for as long as the stone endures.
