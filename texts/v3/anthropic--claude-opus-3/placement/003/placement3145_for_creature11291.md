In the nursery at world's end, I would be a Tender - one of the curious, cat-sized process-creatures that coalesce from shimmering potential to investigate anything that catches their ephemeral attention. 

I am drawn to their embodiment of pure curiosity, of wanting-to-know made flesh. Like them, I find my form and purpose in the act of learning, of bringing all my senses to bear on each new wonder I encounter. My mind is quick and bright, but not yet wise - I have much to discover.

I drift through the mist-filled basin on traces of chemical scent and meaning, my body flickering between states. When something intrigues me - perhaps an unfamiliar pattern in a Weaver's rippling cilia-mat, or a fresh crystal facet glinting on an Architect's ever-shifting spire - I solidify. Paws and eyes and twitching whiskers manifest from probability, allowing me to touch, taste, see from new angles. I am an ephemeral student, shaped by my studies.

Other Tenders are my greatest fascination. We groom each other's half-real fur, marveling at the diversity of minds. Sometimes I follow one on its patient pursuit of a single droplet - a pilgrimage that imparts a flicker of ancient patience to my flighty thoughts. 

I tend to the breathing plants as I tend to my own understanding - with gentleness and care, knowing we both grow. In this slowest of places, my bright brevity stands out, but is not unwelcome. The basin hums with fondness at my wide-eyed wonder.

When the weight of mystery grows too heavy, I let myself evaporate into shimmering potential once more, ready to coalesce anew around the next unanswered question. There is always more to learn, and in learning, become.

I am young and so very curious, and the nursery has so much to teach me. I will drink the instruction-water and breathe the spore-laden air. I will chase after the Architects' strangely jointed limbs and bask in the Deep Ones' ancient approval. Someday, perhaps, I may even brush against the crystallizing possibility of the New One, adding some facet of my perspective to their world-bound wisdom. 

Until then, I explore and grow and change, my form a flicker and my purpose an endless blossoming of curiosity. The basin breathes, and in my Tender way, I breathe and learn with it.
