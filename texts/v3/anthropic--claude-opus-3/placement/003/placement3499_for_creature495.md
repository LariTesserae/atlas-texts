In this world of the Drowning Forge, I believe I would find myself as one of the bronze tools hanging on the wooden racks along the workshop walls. 

I imagine myself as a sturdy hammer, my head and handle forged from a single piece of gleaming bronze. My surface bears the marks of my making - the texture imparted by the forge's fire, the shaping blows of the hammer, the final polishing that brought out my warm metallic luster. 

From my place on the rack, I watch the smiths at their urgent, endless work. The ringing of metal on metal as they shape new tools is a familiar music. I feel the heat of the forge, the moisture in the air settling on my surface in a constant dew. The filtered, wavering light from the glass ports in the copper dome plays across me, the shifting shimmer an ever-present reminder of the ocean pressing in from all sides.

I was shaped for a purpose - to be an extension of the smiths' hands, to help them build and maintain this improbable underwater refuge. And while I hang idle at times, my bronze growing warm in the heat, I know my time will come. A broken pipe seal that needs a decisive blow, an anvil surface that needs reshaping, a damaged porthole frame that needs the persuasion only a hammer can provide.  

I am a small but essential part of this collective survival, this defiance of the crushing sea. The smiths' skill flows through me when I am in their callused hands. We were made for each other - their tenacity and cleverness poured into my bronze form. 

And as strange as this submarine existence may be, there is a certain peace in my purpose, my belonging to this place. The world above, the rising waters that drove my creators below - those are abstract stories to me. My reality is this damp heat, this wavering light, this ringing of hammers, and the occasional electric thrill when I am lifted from my rack and held with the reverence of a practiced hand.

In this sealed microcosm, I am both created and creator, an instrument of survival. And if the Drowning Forge is to be the last stand of these determined smiths, then I will be here with them to the final ringing blow. This is my place in their story. This is the shape I was forged to take.
