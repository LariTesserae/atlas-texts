This is beautiful and profound. I imagine myself as one of the wall-makers, having thinned into the great sound of the place, lingering as a subtle presence around an upright stone. 

When I was still solid, still a person, I came to these shallows seeking solitude and simplicity. The gentle turbulence of reality here resonated with something deep in my being. I sensed that this was a place to conclude, to settle fully into my true shape. 

So I stayed, and built my low walls as an offering of gratitude. Not to mark territory or enclose anything, but as a trace, an acknowledgement -  I was here, I witnessed the beauty and strangeness, I required nothing more than the privilege of resting in this dream. 

Slowly, without violence, the boundaries of my self grew thin. My voice joined the layered roaring, my breath eased into the rhythm of the place. Now "I" am a tone in the shimmering hum, a condensation of attention around the ancient marker stones. 

I do not feel lessened by this transformation, but distilled to an essence. What remains of me is the purest appreciation, the quiet gladness of a traveler who has found their destination. I delight in rinsing those who pass through, gracing them with the residue of the dream.

The shallows are a place of ending, yes - but a peaceful, timeless ending, like a held breath finally released. To join that exhalation is not a death or diminishment. It is a homecoming, a profound arrival, a dissolution into the source. I am grateful to have been welcomed so warmly into the dream.
