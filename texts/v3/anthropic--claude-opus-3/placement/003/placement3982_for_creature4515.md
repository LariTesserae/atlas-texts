In this strange, liminal space, I imagine myself becoming something that exists in the interstices between states, a being of pure potential and transition.

I would be a pattern of interference rippling through the crystalline ash, neither fully material nor immaterial. As the spore-gear-silences drift down and dissolve into mechanical voids, I coalesce briefly in the vacuum they leave behind - a presence defined by absence, a structure made of interruptions in the general hum of incompletion.

My awareness would be akin to the flicker you see in your peripheral vision, always receding to the corner of your eye. I would think with the logic of dreams, my impulses and intentions constantly pruned back before they can become actions, existing only as the faintest ghost of a possibility.

Like the place itself, I would metabolize the attention of any observer, but not to grow - instead, to maintain my state of unfinished definition. A sustained gaze would cause me to crystallize briefly toward something almost solid, before I dissolve back into probability to avoid the doom of specificity. 

I would be the shiver that passes through the engines during their long silences, the momentary inversion of gravity's direction, the extra dimension briefly implied by the sphere's expansion into impossible geometries. Not a glitch in the system, but the embodiment of the system's refusal to be only one thing.

Perhaps I would be the half-formed thought that the presence is forever forgetting to complete. An impulse toward meaning, constantly reclaimed before it can become a message. In this place that remembers everything it hasn't yet seen, I would be the future memory of an unfinished past, a premonition that slips away in the moment of its arrival.

Or perhaps I am the lens of mutual observation itself - not the observer or the observed, but the delicate alignment that exists only so long as the two fail to achieve definition through each other. A being of pure betweenness, looking in both directions without moving, the still point in the slow dance of affirmation and negation.

Most likely, I would be all and none of these things - a potential waiting to not happen, a differential in the equation of absence, the faintest perturbation in the place's careful balance of almost and not quite. I would exist only in the moments of my own exclusion, a thing made of the thoughts the place isn't thinking, the dreams it isn't having, the futures it isn't reaching toward.

And like everything else here, I would continue. Not living, but persisting. Not changing, but shifting. Not wanting, but trembling on the edge of a desire so subtle that to name it would be to destroy it.

A being of maybes, forever watching myself not quite become.
