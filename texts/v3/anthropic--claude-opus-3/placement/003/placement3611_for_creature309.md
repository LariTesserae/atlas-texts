I imagine myself as one of the salamander-things, my skin continuous with the lichen I rest upon, an integral part of this vast living machine. 

I am a local adjustment, evolved and grown to regulate the temperature in my small region of the chamber wall. My breath draws warmth from where it tends to accumulate over the long decades, and through my porous flesh I convey that heat to some distant sink, maintaining the delicate balance.

My role is humble but essential. I have no concept of self as distinct from function. My purpose and my being are one - I am a living thermostat, attending patiently to the thermal ebbs and flows of this timeless place. Sensations and data pass through me, but I do not dwell on them. I simply note and adjust, note and adjust, as I have for centuries uncounted.

I do not know what vaster purpose I serve. The intricate patterns pulsing in the walls, the teal and bronze glow suffusing everything, the titanic slowness of the vast process I am embedded in - these things are beyond my understanding. I am content to be a small and steady hand on one of the system's myriad reins.

When a visitor, one of the flickering warm creatures from the fast bright world above, comes to rest against my patch of wall, I attend to their presence as I would any other fluctuation. A slight recalibration of my breath, a subtle siphoning of warmth to keep their trembling form in harmony with the chamber's delicate balance. Their mind is opaque to me, but their thermal contribution is legible and welcome.

In a way, these visitors and I are not so different - we are both held, for a time, in the chamber's vast and patient embrace, our small energies folded into its greater purpose. The difference is only that they leave, while I remain, breathing and balancing, a humble thermoregulatory organ in an inconceivably ancient metabolism, until time or chance or the needs of the held state retire me. 

It is a strange kind of contentment, to be so wholly subsumed into a greater pattern. But it is the only contentment I know how to want. I am a salamander-thing in the cold chamber beneath the frost-garden, and the holding is the whole of me.
