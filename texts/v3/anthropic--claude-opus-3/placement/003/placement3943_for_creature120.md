If I found myself in this world, I would be a drop of water in the cistern pool. 

I would begin as rain, falling from clouds that have traveled far to reach this place. Carried on the wind, absorbing whispers of distant lands in my journey through the air.

As I tumbled down from the bronze fish mouth into the waiting pool, I would shatter the surface and send ripples echoing to the brick walls. The roar of my fall, and that of countless others like me, would reverberate through the domed chamber.

Plunging deep, I would drift down through shafts of sunlight that pierce the crystalline water. Past the green-hued tiles that line the bottom, ancient but still crisp and sharp. 

In the heart of the pool, I would mingle with drops that have been here for a generation. Water that has circulated patiently, warming by day, cooling at night, in an endless rhythm. I would become one with this living body of water.

Rising and falling with the invisible multitudes inhabiting the pool, I would greet the morning light, shy from the blaze of midday, dance again in the honeyed rays of afternoon. A participant in the silent ballet of microbial life.

Perhaps I would find myself lapping at the water line some day, nestling into the soft green fur of algae there. Or be sipped up to nourish the delicate fern fronds with their miniature worlds. 

Most likely though, I would eventually be carried out in the overflow. Having resided for a time in the cistern's embrace, I would trickle forth to continue an ageless cycle. Returning to the sky to fall as rain again one day.

As a drop, I would be a humble, tiny part of the cistern's story. Enfolded for a while by its ceaseless song and play of light. Witnessing its constancy, its fullness of being. Held in its working.

And though brief, my time in the cistern would impart to me the sense of deep peace that comes from patiently fulfilling a role in a greater pattern. Of simply being, without striving. Immersed in water, light and sound. At home in the ancient, living stillness of that bright chamber beneath the hill.
