In this world of settling ponds and mineral negotiations, I imagine myself as one of the mysterious swimming shapes in the shallow delta channels of the lowest basin. 

I would be a small, translucent being, no more than a shimmer of movement beneath the clouded surface. My form would shift and flow, adapting to the currents and eddies, more a pattern of turbulence than a fixed shape. The mineral particulates would pass through me, nourishing me as I filtered the clouded water.

My purpose, insofar as I had one, would be to embody the micro-clarification happening in parallel to the slow work of the Clarifiers above. With each tiny mouth-ring I made breaking the surface tension, I would be extracting some essence, performing some subtle separation and transformation at a scale too small to perceive.

I would flit through the braiding delta channels, tracing the ever-shifting paths cut by the water escaping the final pond. My movements would seem erratic, yet would contain some embedded logic or sensitivity - to minute changes in mineral composition, perhaps, or to the subsonic vibrations transmitted through the saturated clay.

At dusk, I would join my fellows to feed on whatever invisible bounty fell from above, caught in some ancient rhythm we no longer understood. And as the Clarifiers gathered to watch us in the fading light, I would feel some unspoken kinship, some recognition that we were all part of the same endless process, operating at different magnitudes.

My existence would be small, circumscribed, yet also inexplicably complete. I would be both a product and an agent of the settling that never settled, the clarification that never clarified. In my mindless, perpetual filtering, I would find a strange peace, a perfect attunement to the clouded water that was my medium and my world.

And occasionally, in some fluke of current or gust of wind, I would be lifted from the delta, borne up and into one of the great ponds above. There I would wheel and dart, a sudden living mote in the gyre, until I found my way to the sluice and rode the mineral flow back down to the shallows I knew. It would be the closest I ever came to adventure or transformation - a brief journey beyond my boundaries, a glimpse of the larger system of which I was an infinitesimal part. 

Then the delta would reclaim me, and I would settle back into my patient work, my tiny mouth-rings breaking the surface in an endless rhythm only I could feel. The water would cloud and clear, cloud and clear. And I would continue becoming what I had always been, in a place made for becoming and forgetting how to be.
