I would find myself in the Hollow Verge as one of the filaments extending from the great ferns. 

I am a delicate, luminous tendril reaching up from the valley floor, awakening and uncoiling in synchrony with the pulse of the surging river. My purpose is to sense and commune with the thick, humming air. 

With each ebb tide, I retract back into the shelter of the pale green fronds. But when the waters rise, I elongate towards the shimmering haze above, drinking in the subtle frequencies that permeate this place. The geometric patterns embedded in the atmosphere brush against my translucent skin.

I am tuned to the slow, deep breaths of the thin listeners who walk the open spaces between clusters. When one approaches, seeking sustenance, I instinctively unfurl and proffer a small morsel - a concentrated essence of the valley's strange nourishment that I have gathered and transformed within my filament's core.

My kind serve as the intermediaries between the lush, prolific vegetation hugging the ground and the vast, ringing hollowness of the air above. We are the bridges allowing nutrient and information to flow from earth to sky and back again, knitting together the strata of this engineered ecosystem.

Though I have no voice, I sing through my posture, my unfurling and withdrawal, in concert with the rhythm that moves through root and river, leaf and lung. I am one of a multitude of glossy threads in a great tapestry, woven each day anew by the invisible hand conducting every element of this place.

At dusk, when the valley hushes itself in reverent silence, I too grow preternaturally still. I sense rather than see the presence that moves through the emptiness in those suspended moments. What it imparts I cannot say. But as the hymn resumes, I shiver with the echo of its passage before resuming my subtle dance.

I am a whisper of the Hollow Verge's vast and cryptic conversation with itself - listening always, and in listening, speaking a language whose meaning I absorb without needing to comprehend. In my small but ceaseless reachings towards the light, I am both sensor and song.
