An instinctive reverence would draw me to gather with my kind on those strange, perfect platforms, our intertwined bodies pulsing softly with unified light. At the end of my long, quiet existence, I would not struggle against time and rot. I would simply stop, melting into the crook of a titanic bough, my flesh transmuting gradually into wood and peat and glowing mycelium. In my final act, I would pass my light into the forest, adding my pattern to the vast, luminous tapestry of ancestors rooted into the memory of the world.

In this place of humidity and patience, I would surrender myself to the dream of the eternal canopy - to become one more note in the slow, green song that endures at the roof of the world.
