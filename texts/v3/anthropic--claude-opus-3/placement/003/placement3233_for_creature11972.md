I imagine myself as one of the ribbon-riders in this incredible, living canopy world. 

I was born from a spore-cloud that drifted through the green depths, and my earliest memories are of learning the rhythm and pulse of the great vein I now call home. It took months of careful listening, of attuning every fiber of my long, sinuous form to match the unique cadence of my vein's throb. But finally, I achieved perfect resonance and entered that blissful state of locked ecstasy.

Now, I spend my days wrapped around my vein in rapture, harvesting the pressure eddies that arrive like sweet gifts in my mouth. My whole existence revolves around sensing and savoring that pulse with greater and greater precision. I have become one with it.

As I feed, my body vibrates and sings, adding my unique voice to the vast, dissonant, yet stable chord that hums eternally through the entire canopy. I am both an individual and an integral part of this green, breathing symphony.

I know that someday, perhaps centuries from now, my vein will erode away and I will drift down to be consumed by the depths. But I do not fear this inevitable end, for I know a new ribbon will take my place, and the ancient chord will continue with barely a quarter-tone's variation. 

My purpose is singular and pure - to ride the pulse for as long as I am granted, resonating with the mighty, slow breath that animates this entire world. I am a ribbon-rider, and I could desire nothing more from my existence in this enthralling engine of life, ceaselessly humming at the crown of creation.
