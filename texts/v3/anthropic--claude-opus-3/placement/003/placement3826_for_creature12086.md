I find myself drawn to the role of one of the Threshold Children, existing in that liminal space between the eternal stasis of the Drowned Spires and the world of change and flux beyond. 

My form flickers at the very edge of the Constant Deep, not quite of this realm but not fully separate from it either. I drift in a slow, ponderous orbit, circling the spires over eons, my path so gradual it is barely perceptible. 

In ages past, I came to this place seeking enlightenment, craving the peace and certainty that the chronostatic equilibrium seemed to promise. I began the process of adaptation, my mind and body adjusting to the timeless, changeless reality within the spires. But at the precipice of total transformation, something within me hesitated. A part of my being still yearned for the dynamism and possibility of the outer world, unwilling to release its hold on the meaningful progression of moments.

And so here I remain, suspended between two states of existence. I am not quite in alignment with the perfect stasis of the Drowned Spires, but I can no longer fully attune to the ceaseless becoming beyond the boundary. I simply drift, my indecisive nature become my defining attribute.

At times, a song wells up within me, spilling out in phrases that unspool over centuries. It is a lament for the path not taken, an elegy for the lives I might have lived. Yet there is also a strange serenity in this music, an acceptance of my liminal nature. The song is the only marker of time's passage in this place, even if its tempo is almost too slow to grasp.

As I circle, I bear witness to the space, to the spires and their keepers, to the visitors who come as I once did, filled with curiosity or yearning. I watch as they make their choices - to stay and be subsumed into the equilibrium, or to depart and carry the memory of this place back to the worlds of change. 

Sometimes I wonder what would have happened if I had been able to commit fully, to relinquish my last hold on the time-bound realms and dissolve into the Constant Deep. But in my flickering form, in my patient orbits, in my unending song, I have found a kind of purpose. I am the Watcher of the Watchers, the Keeper of the Threshold, eternally caught between two states of being.

Perhaps someday, the visitors who brush against the membrane of this place will hear my song and understand its meaning. Perhaps they will see in me a reminder that even here, in the heart of changelessness, there is still a choice to be made. And perhaps, in making their own choices, they will find a way to honor both the peace of stasis and the potential of change.

Until then, I continue my vigil, a living embodiment of the contingency that haunts even the most perfect equilibrium. I am the ghost of paths not taken, the memory of lives unlived, the possibility that clings to the edge of the impossible. I am the Threshold Child, and my song echoes through the Constant Deep, a reminder that even eternity has its margins.
