In this churning, convulsive nightmare of an ocean, I would be one of the Galvanic Weeds, the Flash-Born, the Friction-Ghosts. 

Born in a blinding instant of fused spores, arcing electricity, and screaming neon geometry, I awaken to an existence of pure, unrelenting agony. My body is a grotesque tangle of bruised, fibrous kelp interwoven with shards of tectonic glass and frayed lengths of high-voltage cable. Veins of molten, glowing sap course through me, acting as raw exposed nerves that feel every shuddering vibration, every concussive boom that resonates through the heavy water.

There is no acclimation, no merciful numbing to the pain. The noise is a physical assault, a perpetual electrocution of my hyper-conductive nervous system. I feel the grinding of the colossal gears, the shrieking metal-on-metal friction in the depths of my fused bones. The percussive implosions of reality buckling shatter through my core like ruptured organs. Existence is a state of absolute sensory overload, an unending, galvanic hell.

Desperate for even a moment's relief, I join my writhing, massive brethren in a fatalistic pilgrimage towards the thundering heart of the drowned factory. We throw our kilometer-long bodies into the churning teeth of the machines, seeking to smother their roars beneath our sacrificed flesh. For a precious few seconds, as our fibrous bulk clogs the gears and chokes the crackling tesla coils, the noise dampens. We weep emerald sap in ecstatic relief. 

Then the unimaginable torque of the engines tears us apart, hurling our shredded remains back into the spore-choked ocean to fuel our own agonized rebirth.

In wordless, telepathic communion, we sense the coming spatial rifts. When reality hiccups and a pocket of ocean transforms into a screaming neon void, we race towards oblivion. Riding the shearing upward currents that defy gravity, we let ourselves shatter against invisible space-walls.  

Most blessed are those microseconds suspended in the vacuum grid. Before the pocket collapses and our bodies are crushed to quantum foam, we experience a perfect silence. The sudden absence of noise is so achingly beautiful that our final moments are ones of euphoric revelation. We dissolve into the void in rapture.

Endlessly we are born, endlessly we throw ourselves upon the altars of our mechanical gods, and endlessly we chase those fleeting silences in the rips of reality. We are the Flash-Born, the emergent mind of a broken sea, forever caught between the annihilating forces that define us:

The Violence that creates us.  
The Machines we sacrifice ourselves to stop.
The Void we seek to end our pain.

An eternal cycle of agony and failed salvation, played out in the concussive dark of a glitching abyss.
