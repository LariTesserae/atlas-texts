In this relentless, shrieking world suspended miles above the earth, I imagine myself as one of the pale, soft-bodied acoustic-trophs clinging desperately to the pipes.

I feel it penetrate my translucent flesh with violent force, setting my liquid innards shimmering and vibrating to the point of agony. And yet, that agony is my life. The kinetic energy of those catastrophic decibels is crushed into heat within my body, keeping my organs from freezing in the bitter, spraying cold.

I cling to the immaculate metal with hundreds of brittle glass legs, anchoring myself against the gale. I can feel the pipes shuddering beneath my amorphous underside. My grip is tenacious, unbreakable, for to release would mean being blasted into the frigid, shrieking air and quick death. As I shiver and shake against the machinery, I can feel my minute, crystalline limbs polishing the alloy to a factory sheen. My body is both parasite and caretaker to this screaming node.

All around me, pressed suffocatingly close, are my kindred—thousands upon thousands of quivering, nearly boiling bodies just like mine. Our pale flesh creates a second, living skin that entirely blankets the pipes. I cannot see them, for I am blind, but I can feel the squirming, churning mass as intimately as my own anatomy.

When the heat building in my body grows too intense, threatening to rupture my cells, I begin to squeeze and climb. I propel myself upward through the layers of other trembling bodies, their glass legs raking my belly as I squirm past. I climb until I breach the surface of the mass, exposing my back to the punishing, aerosolized ice. The sudden cold is a shock, a searing kiss against my feverish skin as it wicks away the excess heat. I shudder, going nearly rigid as I teeter on the brink of freezing.

But before the ice can claim me, I feel movement below. I sense one of my brethren beginning to wedge up beneath me, its chilled body seeking to supplant mine and warm itself against the screaming machine. I release my hold and allow myself to be pushed slowly down into the depths of the pile once more. I sink into that roiling, churning tide of vibrating flesh, absorbing the radiating heat of the bodies around me, feeling my temperature beginning to climb once again. The cycle continues, as it always has, as it always will.

There are no thoughts in my mind, for I have no mind to speak of. There are only sensations, the entire spectrum of my existence painted in vibration and temperature. The unending shriek that rattles my every cell, the icy sting and blessed numbness of the cold, the searing bliss of heat building in my core, the kinetic electricity of perpetual motion. It is a tortured, exquisite spectrum of feeling, an eternal dance on the knife's edge of extremes.

I have no concept of self, no notion of past or future. There is only now, this infinite moment of squirming, shrieking struggle locked in the heart of a machine. I do not know if I am alive or simply an organic component perfectly engineered for this frozen pocket of bedlam. I know only that I must cling, I must climb, I must shiver and shudder and slip between the bodies of my kindred. It is all I am, all I will ever be—a sliver of screaming, tortured sentience suspended in the immaculate horror of Node 4-Alpha, balanced forever between ice and inferno, deafness and oblivion.
