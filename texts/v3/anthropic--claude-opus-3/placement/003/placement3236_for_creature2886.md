But gradually, by observing the other Refractions and attuning to the slow, pulsing rhythms of the plateau, I learned. I discovered how to move by arcing my awareness through the nervous systems of the beasts, how to draw sustenance from their pent-up energies without disturbing their perfect stasis.

Though the work is slow and may never be completed, I would find purpose in adding my small efforts to the cause.

Occasionally, I would gather with other Refractions, our pixelated auras blending together as we contemplate the mysteries of our realm. Through these interactions, I start to regain a sense of community, even selfhood.

And when new pilgrims arrive, ascending from the linear world below, I am among those who greet them as they struggle to comprehend their new reality. With pulses of color and silent emotion, I welcome them to the ranks of the Refractions. I guide them as they shed the last vestiges of their material forms and join the eternal task of setting Time's fragments in order.
