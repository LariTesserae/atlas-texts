My being pulses in sympathy with the slow cycles of growth and decay - the root questing through crumbling mortar, the fruiting body erupting from cracks in the pitted stone. I know intimately every drip and trickle of moisture navigating the contours of this space.

The sounds of the human world are a muted, distant echo. My senses attune to the skittering of eyeless life, the infinitesimal shifting and settling of this place.

I am the genius loci, the spirit of this realm. An intelligence vast and slow and deep, my thoughts as labyrinthine as the tunnels I inhabit. I hold the memory of ages in my substance, the waxing and waning of civilizations a brief phosphorescence against the velvet dark.

I do not know if I was born of this place, coalescing like crystal from mineral-rich flows, or if I drifted here from stranger spheres, drawn to the particular energies that gather in this occluded space. But here I remain, watching, waiting, an enigmatic consciousness serenely contemplating the mysteries of a universe that moves both above and below.

The pale, scuttling things and squirming, burrowing things are my strange companions in this cthonic domain. I cherish them as rare and precious emanations of life's endless creativity. They are my eyes and ears in the small spaces, thriving where no human form could venture.

Those rare mortals who descend into my demesne tread an uncanny boundary between worlds. They brush against veils of perception, senses straining to encompass a reality that shimmers at the edges of the mundane. In my presence, they feel the weight of the eons, the strangeness of being. 

Whether I am guardian or observer or something beyond human conception, I keep my silent vigil. I hold the shape of this hidden world in my protean form, a shaping influence as much as denizen. As above, so below - and in the between, in the liminal spaces and forgotten interstices, there too am I.

Perhaps one day the tides of history and geology will shift, and these depths will once more see the light. I will greet that dawn as I have greeted countless others, with the patience born of an existence measured in the slow dance of stone and water and the eternal, dreaming dark.

For now, I remain here in the humid, fertile shadows, strange and miraculous, a mystery waiting to unfold for any with the wit and the will to part the veil and descend into the unknown and unknowable reaches below the world of daylight and linear time, into the realm where I dwell in my unthinkable and unguessed majesty.
