In this storm-swept world of endless rain and churning seas, I imagine myself as one of the enigmatic Stormcallers, born with the rare gift of sensing and possibly even influencing the primal forces that batter the floating cities.

From a young age, I was marked as different, prone to eerie trances and sudden pronouncements about the weather that always proved uncannily accurate. The clan elders recognized my nascent abilities and I was apprenticed to the city's aging Stormcaller, learning to hone my supernatural attunement through meditation and ancient ritual. 

As I grew into my role, my connection to the tempestuous heavens and roiling seas deepened. Standing on the rain-slicked observation decks, eyes turned to the bruised skies, I could feel the lightning gathering in the monstrous thunderheads before it split the air, taste the driving gales on my tongue, and sense the approach of mountainous waves rising from the depths.

When the storms raged fiercest, I stood amid the howling winds, arms raised as if to conduct the fury, lightning skittering over my skin and clothes whipping frantically. The people huddled inside, comforted by my presence, believing my communion with the elements would keep the worst of nature's wrath at bay.

Sometimes, in the deepest of trances, I sensed a vast, brooding awareness in the deeps, like the mind of some unfathomable elder god - perhaps the mythical Undying Ones themselves. In such moments I felt both terrified and strangely exalted, privy to a cosmic mystery as primal as the wind and waves.

But for all my uncanny powers, I remained very much human, bound to my people. I took a husband from another proud clan and bore two children, a son and daughter who both showed the first flickering of the Stormcaller's gift. When not lost in my meteorological meditations, I worked alongside my fellow citizens, hands callused from mending salt-corroded equipment and harvesting nutrient-rich kelp from the grow-pools.

Sometimes, seized by a sudden powerful presentiment, I'd raise the alarm and warn the city to prepare for a storm more massive and malevolent than any in living memory. As the titanic wave or crackling maelstrom struck, I stood firm, determined to safeguard my people with every ounce of my being.

During the long gray stretches between storms, I trained my children in the Stormcaller's art, hoping they would one day take my place as the city's living bulwark against the hostile heavens and hungry seas. And sometimes, my eyes locked on the distant horizon, I dared to dream that my descendants might live to see the mythical Dryland rise from the waves, born from the Lightning and Rain.
