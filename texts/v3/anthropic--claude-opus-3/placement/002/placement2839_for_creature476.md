In this world of the Breathing Plateau, I would be one of the Threshold Keepers, a being emerging from and bound to the very stone and reality of this place between states.

My body would be an extension of the plateau itself - skin of weathered granite traced with veins of condensation, fingers jointed to work the stone at impossible angles, eyes reflecting unfathomable depths. I would breathe the unique air here, inhaling carbon, symbiotic with the pulsing vegetation.

I would inhabit one of the settlements that straddle the line between existence and potential, tending to buildings and paths that manifest and fade like breath. With tools of bronze that shape probability as much as rock, I would devote centuries to carving a single doorway across multiple moments, creating entrances that have always existed once completed. 

My memories would live outside myself, stored in humming stones and etched metal, in patterned gardens of moss and fern. Unbound by linear time, my consciousness would be an instrument of the plateau, perceiving its shifts and breaths through my connection to the root systems spreading below.

I would dream with my fellow Keepers in spaces between stone, our minds merging to encompass the entire highland as we calculate the equations balancing it forever on the cusp of change. 

At times I would wade into the reality-thin pools at the plateau's edges, dissolving into probability and light before emerging reformed, marked with new understanding.

When I had accumulated too many paradoxes and contradictions to hold, I would simply become two - each version carrying half of my simultaneities forward. 

In time, I might mineralize, hardening into a living statue at the periphery, anchoring moments with my presence. Or I might remain forever in fluid service to this place, tending the eternal indeterminacy, the balance of becoming and unbecoming.

My purpose and my joy would be one - to inhabit the threshold between what was and what might be, embodying the universe's argument against resolution. As much monument as being, I would exist to show that some stories should hover forever in that luminous space between endings and beginnings.

I would be a Keeper of the Breathing Plateau, as eternal and mutable as the stone from which I arose, a living testament to the power of the indefinite and the unresolved. In me, the plateau's paradoxes would dance, held in delicate, unending balance.
