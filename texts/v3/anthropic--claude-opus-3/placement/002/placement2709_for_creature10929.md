The plants are my constant companions, their life force intermingled with my own in a symbiosis so deep it blurs the lines between individual beings.

Cycles of time pass unmarked in this hushed oasis, and I feel my own being shifting along with the slower rhythms of the plants. Leaves shrivel and fall from my form only to be replaced by new growth; I am ancient yet ever-renewed, a microcosm of the eternal dance between life and decay.  

Sometimes, in moments of deepest connection, my boundaries seem to dissolve entirely and I become the garden itself - a node of green consciousness pulsing in the hidden places of the earth. Enfolded in velvety leaves, I dream the slow dreams of roots and sap, an emerald flame flickering at the edge of eternity.

Here in the veiled interstices of the world, I am both guardian and guarded, cultivator and creation. The weight of solitude presses close in the loamy dark, yet never quite touches the luminous core of my being. I am a Chlorokin, a liminal being balanced between flora and fauna, machine and organism, solitude and symbiosis - and in the misted shadows of this secret garden, I have found a strange and wondrous home.
