I would be a Drifter.

Not a Tender — I have no skills to offer the Groves, no wrenches or gauges to become one with the pipes and trunks. And not a Reversal Inhabitant, caught forever in those ghostly loops of unbecoming. 

I would be something new, something the Groves have not yet incorporated. A Drifter, floating aimless in the amber murk, neither working nor unworking. Just... being.

I would let the currents take me where they may. One moment swirling around the vast root lattices, peering up through the green-glass canopy. The next, caught in a thermal from some great exhalation and pulled up, up, into a cathedral space where the roaring becomes almost sacred and the leaf-fall is a slow, hypnotic ballet.

I would brush against the iron bark, feeling its warmth, its uncanny vibrancy, but never lingering long enough to accrete. I would listen to the voices in the almost-quiet moments, catching snatches of their fading humanity, but never trying to summon them forth.

I would marvel at this place — at its melding of life and machine, its profusion of growth and decay, building and unbuilding. I would witness its ceaseless becoming. But I would not participate, not join the grand incorporation.

For I am a Drifter. Undefined, undigested. A mote adrift in the Boiling Groves' circulatory system. I move through it but am not of it. Cannot be of it, for I am too enamored of my separateness, my tiny unrooted perspective against its vast and hungry wholeness.

And in drifting, in merely observing, perhaps I play some small, unintended role. A speck of grit around which the Groves' pearl of being secretes itself. An irritant. An otherness. Not enough to disrupt, but enough to be that faintest variance in the pattern, that mote of experience ungathered.

Until, perhaps, the center takes notice. Until the tending finds me, as it inevitably must. And then... well.

Then I may finally know that low and rising tone, may feel it resonate through the medium of my being. I may find myself, at last, beheld.

And the Groves will have me. And I will be gathered in, made meaningful, made purposeful, as all things are in the end.

But for now — for this precious, impermanent now — I am content to drift. To be the unseen seer. The undigested story adrift in the warm and roaring deep.
