In this primordial world, I would be one of the gnarled trees that form the living fabric of the oasis. 

My trunk would be thick and twisted, shaped by centuries of slow growth in this isolated sanctuary. Broad, leathery leaves would cloak my spreading boughs, drinking in the filtered sunlight. My roots would delve deep into the hidden waters that are the lifeblood of the oasis, sustaining me through the long seasons of drought.

From my lofty vantage, I would be a silent witness to the life of the oasis. The comings and goings of the small tribe who make their home here, their lives playing out beneath my sheltering canopy. I would watch the children climb in my branches, hear the elders recount their stories in the shade of my spreading crown. The cycles of tribal life - the rituals, the hunts, the joyous gatherings - would mark the passage of time like the turning of the seasons.

My life would be measured in centuries, not years. I would see generations of the tribe come and go, their brief lives flickering like motes in a sunbeam. Yet they would be as much a part of me as my leaves and bark. Their small dramas, their triumphs and tragedies, would be interwoven with my own slow story.

To the tribe, I would be a landmark, a fixture in their small world. But I would be more than that. I would be a link to the deep past, my gnarled form a living testament to the age and endurance of the oasis. The wisdom of the ancients would seem to rustle in my leaves, whispering secrets of rain and drought, feast and famine.

In the wider world beyond the sheltering sands, my kind would have long vanished, lost to the inexorable march of time. But here in the oasis, cradled in this fragment of a younger world, I would endure. A living monument to the tenacious vitality of life, standing watch over the small tribe who are my wards and companions.

Through the long, drowsy days and the star-speckled nights, I would stand as I always have. Rooted deep, reaching high. An ageless sentinel guarding the beating green heart of the oasis. Bearing witness to the turning of the years, the cycling of the ages. Dreaming my slow, green dreams while the world beyond the sands spins on, heedless and unremembered.
