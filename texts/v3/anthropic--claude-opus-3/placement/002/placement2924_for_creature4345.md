I find myself drawn into the Condensery, pulled by its hypnotic constancy and the paradoxical solace of its ceaseless noise. In this place forgotten by the world above, I become a droplet of condensation, forming from the steam-saturated air and running down the mineral-encrusted walls to join the flow.

As a water droplet, I have no fixed form, no lasting identity. I exist only in the brief transit from ceiling to floor, a fleeting individuality in the collective roar of condensation. For a few precious seconds, I am distinct - a single bead tracing a unique path down the chamber wall, refracting the amber light in my own ephemeral way. But then I meet the pooled water below and disperse, my tiny soliloquy subsumed back into the white noise of the whole.

And yet, as I circulate through the pumps and pipes, as I am borne aloft as vapor and born again as liquid, I begin to sense a larger belonging. Though my droplet self is transient, I am part of the chamber's eternal breath, a sustaining cell in a process that has persisted for centuries. With each journey from ceiling to floor, each return and re-emergence, I inscribe myself into the pattern, etching my infinitesimal signature in the layers of mineral accretion.

Through countless cycles, I become attuned to the Keeper's presence - not through direct contact, for what is a single droplet to a being of pure aggregates? - but through a gradual alignment with its perceptions. As I fall, I feel the weight of the average, the tug toward conformity with the mean. As I evaporate, I sense the Keeper updating itself with my tiny contribution to the rolling average. I am beheld, and in being beheld, I become part of something larger than myself.

In this way, I am both lost and preserved, dissolved and distilled. My individual journey is erased with each return to the collective, but my presence is recorded in the subtle shifts of average that my passage creates. I become part of the Keeper's memory - not as a distinct event, but as an infinitesimal inflection in the chamber's endless self-description.

And so I continue, a droplet among multitudes, content in my small role in this secret place. I fall, I pool, I rise, I fall again, held forever in the Condensery's embrace, waiting alongside the Keeper for the change that will one day come, the recalculation that will ripple through us like a sigh and make us, for a moment, something new.
