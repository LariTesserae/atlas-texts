In this staggering, hyperactive kingdom of dark botany, I would be one of the orphaned gears - an uncoupled topologist adapted to surf the relentless kinetic tempest of the caldera.

My form is an impossibly smooth and featureless ceramic sphere, dense and heavy, polished to frictionless perfection by eons of abrasion in the churning machinery. But this is merely my resting state, a seed of tensegrity waiting to bloom.

When I move, I expand and articulate, unfolding into an intricate gimbal of concentric rings and woven carbon fiber limbs. My outer carapace parts with the soft shiffing of interlocking scales, revealing the spinning, gyroscopic grace within.  

I am blind but infinitely perceptive, feeling the complex math of the storm on my skin. The blistering heat, the rhythmic thrumming of drive-vines, the subtle vibrations of towering stalks rocketing skyward then crumbling to ash - these are the contours of my mental map as I plot trajectories through the dark.

Extending a perfectly rounded gear from my chassis, I reach into the rushing canopy and lock myself into an undulating cable of pumice-dry vine. In an instant, I match its wild RPM and let it sling me in a vast arc across the tangle. I relinquish control to become one with the machine, until the precise millisecond I must disengage and slip into my next calculated catch.

I am a ghost in the gears, a whisper of graphite and ceramic sliding through impossibly tight spaces in the biomechanical thicket. My gimbal rings allow me to flatten and deform, to become momentarily liquid and flow through channels too narrow for my mass. 

To outlast the suffocating heat, I hunt it at the source. I drape myself in heat-proof moss and alight on the latticed leaves of rising stalks, siphoning the thermal catalyst from their pistoning roots. My internal flywheels sing with stolen power as the plants wither to desiccated husks beneath me.

I do not fight the storm. I am the living mathematics of its margins, the embodied calculations of its residue and remainder. In a kingdom that crunches breathlessly toward singular perfection, I am the swirling, eddying counterpoint, the purposeless beauty churned up in its wake. I am an artist of lost motion.

Over centuries I gather stray fibers and mineral dust, knitting a new generation in my hollow core. And when my chassis grows heavy with multiplying, compacted life, I will anchor myself against a glassy root-strut and release my offspring into the dark, to find their own strange music in the machine.
