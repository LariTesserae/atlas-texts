Clinging to the dissipating remnants, I would either survive to find myself in a new, nascent lake forming nearby, or perish and rain down to the mossy ground, my body rejoining the rift's primordial cycle of life and un-life.

Like the other inhabitants of this green, dripping, softly-glowing world, my existence would be one of patience, slowness and liminality. Neither quite plant nor animal, a collaboration of multitudes, I would hang in the space between earth and water and air, taking my form from the rift's young, protean physics.
