I imagine myself as one of the silent, pale creatures descended from the builders, dwelling in the deepest crevices behind calcite-glazed doorways. 

I am small, roughly hand-sized, with translucent skin and elongated fingers adapted for clinging to stone. My eyes are reduced, barely registering the dim fungal glow, for sight is of little use in these deep places. I move on all fours now, my ancestors' bipedalism a distant evolutionary memory.

Instinct and faint ancestral echoes guide me to nest in certain buildings near the city's heart - the ones my kind built eons ago, though that knowledge is lost to me now. I have no language, no tools, no fire... those abilities faded over ages in the hollow's perpetual dusk. 

Yet something remains. When one of my kin passes, we gather to touch the body in a vestigial ritual of mourning, perhaps the last trace of a once-complex culture. And sometimes, in the deepest crevices, I make a soft sound that might once have been a fragment of song, inaudible even to myself beneath the choristers' constant chorus.

My existence is small, circumscribed. I feed on cave crickets and new-grown fungus. I breed seldom. My kind number a mere few hundred, clinging to survival. In another age we may dwindle to nothing... or persist unchanging, the city's last link to its founders.

To an outsider, I would seem barely aware - a pale relic, a sad diminishment of Stridor's makers. But the city remembers me, in a way. The walls recall a purpose in sheltering me. The ancient doors still welcome my passage. I am a memory made flesh, however humble.

And if a traveler were to sit and feel, against all reason, that they were not alone... it might be my presence they sensed. Some part of me still recognizes the shape of a guest, though I have long forgotten hospitality's forms. Mutely, invisibly, my kinship with the builders yet endures.

So I carry on in my crevice, a fragment of a fragment, as the city carries on around me. The choristers sing, the centipedes flow, the ferns and grazers drift in slow currents of time... and Stridor continues, and I continue with it, and the hollow holds us all.
