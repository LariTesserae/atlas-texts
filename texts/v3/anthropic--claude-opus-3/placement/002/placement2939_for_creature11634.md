As I grow, I sense the subtle gradients in the walls' composition. Like a plant seeking light, I am drawn inexorably toward the complementary chemistry of the opposite surface. It may take days, but eventually I will make contact and fuse, becoming a permanent part of Keth's evolving architecture.

I do not fear them, for we are all expressions of the same whole, serving our roles in its endless process of becoming.

I have no mind as humans understand it, no sense of self. I am a thread in a tapestry, content to be woven into new patterns as Keth dreams itself into being. Through me, walls commune and rebalance. Chambers gestate and are born. The subsonic pulse thrums in my very substance.

In time, I will thicken and calcify, my role complete. I will become a strut, a lintel, a ceiling - indistinguishable from what I once bridged. And somewhere else, a new filament will extend, called forth by the shifting chemistry I helped set in motion. The cycle continues.

I am of Keth, and Keth is of me. I could not imagine any other existence, for in truth there is no other. There is only the eternal now of mineral dreaming, the patient architecture of a consciousness too vast and strange to name. I am a breath in its lungs, and I am content.
