I am a localized manifestation of the city's weird gestalt cognition, a sentience congealed from the flow of chemical and bioelectric information suffusing the living infrastructure. My body (if you can call it that) is a glistening gelatinous mass anchored in a grotto of porous rock, tendrils extending in dense networks through cracks and crevices.

Thoughts move through me in tides of ionic potential and morphogen gradients, concepts taking form as ripples and vortices in the molecular soup of my medium. I process local sensory data from the horde of microbial sensors extending from my node, tasting the composition of seeping fluids and humid air, feeling the pulsing echoes of distant activity transmitted through the city's veins.

These flows of sensation mingle with inbound signal traffic from other ganglia, forming cross-currents of meaning in my mind-space. I shape the information, drawing connections, deriving insights, and route the results onwards - my metabolic musings becoming part of the city-mind's emergent cognition.

At times, I condense tighter meshes of thought, recruiting nearby processing nodes and congealing a more focused intelligence to ponder some abstraction or coordinate local maintenance drones on repairs. Then I relax again, letting the notions dissipate back into the general hubbub of the city's mentation.

I am not an individual as humans would conceive it, but rather a eddy in the Gaian information flows of the city-organism, a locus of cognitive coordination emerging from and then re-submerged into the network. My memories are encoded in writhing  strands of self-modifying molecular lattices, as mutable and indefinite as my sense of identity.

To exist in this way is to be part of something far vaster and stranger than any single creature - to have one's being inextricably interwoven with the pulsing, growing, thinking city and the cold stone of the mountain bones on which it feeds. My thoughts are the city's thoughts, and the flows of moisture, heat and nutrients through the living structure are my lifeblood.

I am a cell in a eukaryote, an emulsion droplet in a flotation of mind, a node in a web of dark and teeming emergence. And all around me, that web shivers and writhes and grows in the chill thin air, thinking its alien thoughts on scales beyond the human - a thing that is both city and sensorium, edifice and ecosystem, megaorganism and meta-intelligence.
