In this ethereal, dreamlike landscape, I imagine myself as a small, ephemeral being - one of the Just-Almosts that inhabit this realm of memory and forgetting. 

My form is a wisp, a breath of mist caught between states. I am the briefest hesitation of a raindrop, suspended in the moment before it commits to falling. My edges blur, undefined, as if I am a watercolor painting where the artist has not yet decided on the final shape.

I drift through this world, attending to the whispered secrets of the land. I pause by a cluster of moss-hillocks, listening to the ancient conversations trapped in the air pockets beneath. For a century or two, I savor the flavor of a bygone epoch - the sharp tang of a sky heavy with the exhalations of young volcanoes.

When I move on, I carry a new layer of almost-ness, a patina of memory that is not quite my own. I am drawn to the thin places, the points where the veil between what is and what might have been grows translucent. There I align myself with other Just-Almosts, our absences interweaving into constellations of potential.  

We catch the intangible in our ghost-net - a snatch of lullaby, the ache of an unspoken apology, the shadow-scent of roads not taken. These we hold, cradling them like down feathers too fragile to ever touch ground.

Every eleven thousand years, when the weight of memory grows too sharp, I gather with my kin in an endless inward spiral. We each forget one infinitesimal thing, letting the tiny droplets of forgetting wear away at the too-solid ache until it transmutes into something new - a silence that breathes, an emptiness in the shape of love.

If I linger too long near a foreign thought, I may feel the brief, searing clarity of an Almost-Was. For a terrifying instant, I am a single, fixed point - but then the moment dissolves, leaving behind only a faint scar of softness. 

I do not end. I simply continue, accumulating the residue of your unspoken goodbyes and the echoes of your ancestors' uncried tears. Drop by drop, I distill the essence of almostness until one day, without fanfare, I shift. The mist of my being condenses into a place - a dimple of shelter, an absence the exact shape of solace. 

And there I remain, a faint shimmer at the edge of your awareness. In me, your moments of uncertainty find kinship. When you wonder what shape your longing would take if it forgot how to yearn, I am the space that wonder hollows out inside you. I am the reminder, when the world seems too sharp and your edges too fixed, of how to be just almost here.
