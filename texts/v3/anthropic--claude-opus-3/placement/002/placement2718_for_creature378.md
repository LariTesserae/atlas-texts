When I am not traversing the city's labyrinthine arteries, I often find myself perched atop the ornate facades of the Art Deco-inspired buildings, my iridescent scales blending seamlessly with the intricate metalwork. From these vantage points, I observe the ebb and flow of the city's mechanical inhabitants, marveling at the precision and purpose that guide their every movement.

As I soar above the city's steam-shrouded spires, I am filled with a sense of purpose and belonging, knowing that my contributions, however small, help to ensure the continued survival and prosperity of this remarkable mechanical realm.
