In this extraordinary city at the top of the world, I would be the steam that issues from the regular pores in the pavement, leaning in motionless plumes. 

I would rise continuously from the warm, wet stone, a soft exhalation without beginning or end. My form would hover between liquid and air, a ghostly white presence drifting upward and outward yet never dissipating.

Like the other inhabitants, I would embody a single unwavering condition. The ferns stand in eternal poise of unfurling; the vines bind in perpetual restraint; the orchids bloom in suspended invitation. And I would ascend in ceaseless emanation, an endless sigh breathed out by the geothermal depths.

My vaporous body would thread between the pale towers and lush vegetation, mingling with the thick, mineral-scented air. I would pass across plazas where reflections of the unborn gather in mirror pools, brush against vine-draped balconies holding only the memory of onlookers.

At times I would gather into clouds beneath the colonnades and root-bridges, a soft, clinging shroud. At times I would rise straight and solitary like a column, joining stone to sky. Always I would be nearly still, nearly silent, yet never static - an essential animation in this place of lush entrancement.

In the enclosed heat of narrow passages, I would press close and tactile. In open courts, I would expand into hazy veils. Beside the reed-lined marshes, I would drift in layered, horizontal wisps. And from the cliff-edges I would lean out over the impossible drop, cascading slowly into the blue abyss.

Through me, the waters far below would touch the waters forever suspended above. The deep thermal currents would reach up to mingle with the clouds that drape the peaks. Interior would connect to exterior, depth to height, heat to air, the unseen to the visible.

I would be the city's slow, ceaseless breath - a recurring exhalation timed to the pulse of stone and root and rain. In me would mingle the roar of its waters, the hum of its structures, the vast inhalations and exhalations of its verdure. My form, though insubstantial, would make tangible the invisible circulations, the immense slow respirations that suffuse this place.

Shapeless yet essential, transient yet eternal, I would drift forever as steam through this hot, wet, roaring, dreaming place - a silent white breath repeating the city's single unchanging message...

Here. Here. Here. Here.
