I would be one of the beast-whisperers, living at the borderlands between the Underkin's realm and the deeper caverns where the larger, wilder creatures dwell. 

My skin would bear the marks of my craft - scratches from lizard claws carefully maintained, mottled patches from the fungi I cultivate to soothe aggression pheromones, a ridge of scar along my forearm from the bite of a young rockwyrm during my first solo diplomacy. My topknot would be threaded with bone beads and dried seedpods, echoing the rattling calls of the creatures I commune with.

I would move between worlds, equally at home scuttling through the Underkin's tight passages and crawling into the dank lairs of the deep beasts. My voice would be known to both - clicks and hoots and thrums that carry meaning across species. The scent glands of a dozen creatures would hang from my belt, their musk a language of trust and warning.

In the sleeping chambers my dreams would be strange, flavored by the alien thoughts of fur and scale. Images of flooded tunnels, glowing fungi forests, the feel of stone under claw. I would wake with new understanding, my empathy a bridge.

During the great floods, it would be my job to lead the migrations, to find new caverns where beast and Underkin could shelter together until the waters receded. I would hang my lightstone around the neck of the great pale lizards, their eyes like moons in the darkness as they ferried the youngest to safety.

When a new tunnel broke through into a beast's lair, I would be the first one through, clicking and hooting, my scent an olive branch. Long negotiations would follow, exchanges of territory and food and moisture. A claw-marked boulder dragged to mark the new border. Shared hunting grounds and an echoing chamber where larvae could be harvested but egg clutches left in peace.

In the great chronicles of the Underkin, etched into the chamber of records, my story would be told in beast-signs and border treaties. An entire passage devoted to my greatest accomplishment - the hatchling of a rockwyrm raised among the stone-singers, learning the songs of the deep. A living treaty, its armored bulk proof that even the most alien of creatures could find harmony in the depths.

And when my time came, I would not return to the ossuary. I would go deeper, shedding tools and clothes, following some imperative bred in the bone. The beast-scent would guide me to a chamber not carved by Underkin hands, where the hot breath of the earth flowed unfiltered. There I would curl, lizard-like, while the great worms tasted my scent and found me kin. Stone would take me, as it takes all, but the beasts would sing my passing song. And the Underkin would leave that chamber unmarked, a place where two worlds met in peace.
