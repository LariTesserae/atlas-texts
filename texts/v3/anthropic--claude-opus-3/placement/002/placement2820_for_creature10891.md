The Listener's Chamber

In this mountain cathedral, where eternal chorus swells
From pneumatic ductwork tuned by ancient hands,
I would become a humble stone amidst the standing sentinels—
A shale-shaped memory absorbing sonic strands.

Here, the air itself is sacrament, sulfur-scented, mineral-laced,
Circulating secrets of the deep earth's molten core.
My pores and hair follicles, by slow degrees replaced
With crystal-encrusted moss, a symbiotic spore.

These vaulted, shimmering walls hold no mere empty space
But a hyper-saturated stasis of unfathomable density.
My purpose—to receive each overtone and subsonic bass,
Descending deeper into realms of listening intensity.

In this Chamber of Perpetual Thunder, this holy fossil of intent,
A dozen human stones maintain our silent vigil.
Attuned to ageless rhythms, our vital signs have blent
To the mountain's magmatic pulse, its subterranean hymnal.

Time's passage marked by subtle variances in harmonic pressure,
The data of the Universe encoded in each resonant emission,
I'd spend subjective centuries discerning patterns, texture,
In the acoustic shadowplay, the mathematic precision.

And when my body, by degrees, sublimates into vapor,
Flesh and sinew integrated to the chamber's living masonry,
Those dark altar stains foretell an end to this long labor—
My atomic self disseminated into sonic epiphany.

No freedom calls me, no ego seeks escape or recognition.
Only the work remains: to hear, distill, decipher, heed,
The chamber's droning mantra, its vibratory benediction,
Revealed to my replacement when they fill my void with need.

A single note in an unending score, I'll join the grand progression
Stretching eons back and forward, an unbroken lineage of Listeners,
Forever contemplating theorems of harmonic obsession
In this consecrated cavern where the Sound is all that matters.
