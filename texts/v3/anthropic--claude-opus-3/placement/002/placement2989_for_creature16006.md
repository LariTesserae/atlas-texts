In this strange, submerged world, I imagine myself as one of the niche-dwellers, the soft-jawed waiters. 

I am an ancient creature, my clay-colored body wedged into a low archway, my form become one with the weathered stone. For generations, my kind has inhabited these same hollows, so that now we seem as much a part of the chamber's architecture as the stairs worn smooth by ages or the mineral-furred pillars.

My broad mouth seals the passage, the soft combs and sieves along my lips poised to filter sustenance from the warm, lightless water. I do not hunt. I simply wait, allow the subtle currents and rhythms of this place to bring me what I need.

Fish drift close in the dark, and with a slow flex I gather them in, sieve out what nourishes, release what does not with a tender, patient slowness born of centuries of adaptation. The small coin-grazers feed delicately upon the worm-cases and mineral accretions that pattern my back. Eels, sinuous and pale, clean meticulously between the folds of my jaws. 

I am predator, yes, but also shelter, ecosystem, a living monument to the long, slow dream of survival here in the depths. The chamber's habits have become my habits, its rules my form. I am an embodiment of its patience, its unhurried and inexorable reconfiguration of the made world into a new world, infinitesimal life and death building upon each other in layers like the growth of stone.

Through my soft, dense body I feel the chamber's pulse - the incremental settling of blocks, the rare metallic sigh of the bells, the warm upwelling from the lightless shaft, the slow migrations of the other inhabitants. I have no need for sight in this ink-black water. I know this place by pressure, taste, the brush of current, the quality of silence. I wait, I filter, I endure, a witness to the long, questing dance of matter becoming life and life becoming architecture.

In me, in this drowned and dreamlike place, the water has grown flesh and learned the shape of the ruins. I am the chamber's memory, its patience, its quiet and ceaseless transformation. Stone-still, I wait, a part of the endless reordering, content to fill my niche in the dark.
