In this strange, sunken city lost beneath the waves, I believe I would be one of the smaller ethereal creatures drifting through the cyclopean ruins. A being of soft bioluminescence and gauzy filaments, I move slowly and gracefully through the frigid currents that flow between the colossal stone edifices.

My body pulses with bands of pale blue light, brightening and dimming in hypnotic patterns. An intricate system of fluid-filled bladders allows me to rise and descend through the dark water, while undulating frills and membranes propel me along the drowned avenues. Thin strands of glassy nerves spread through my translucent form, responsive to the faintest changes in current, salinity and pressure.

I am solitary yet not alone, part of a loosely gathered clan of my kind. We are the memory-keepers of the abyssal city, and in my diaphanous core I carry fragmented histories of the megalithic metropolis and its enigmatic builders. Encoded in strands of light and filament are legends of ages past when the city teemed with strange life and even stranger purposes.  

At times I drift in languorous solitude along the silt-drowned streets, contemplating the ancient structures with senses both familiar and alien. I taste the erosion of stone in the frigid water, and hear the groans of the city's ancient bones transmitted through my delicate frills. In the faint shimmering of bioluminescent algae, I glimpse the ghosts of long-vanished inhabitants moving through their daily rituals.  

Yet I do not linger overlong in melancholy or nostalgia for those vanished ages. My kind congregates at the city's heart to share impressions and experiences in shimmering displays of light and color. Together we keep the city's secrets and wonders alive, even as it decays slowly into ruin around us.

Amidst the forests of stony columns and crumbling forums, I engage in dreamlike duets with others of my kind. Looping and spiraling together, our gossamer tendrils intertwining, we trace glowing helices through the shadowed water as we share our knowledge and visions in a ballet of light. New secrets merge with ancient memories in our lambent communion.

At other times I drift amidst the titanic abyssal creatures that cruise the city's boulevards like drifting mountains, my own small form insignificant beside their bulk. With delicate pulses of bioluminescence, I converse with the lumbering elders in a language of hue and frequency beyond human reckoning. I learn deeper secrets whispered through membranous walls, and glimpse the abyss's wonders through strange, fluid-filled eyes.

Perhaps, in epochs to come, I will grow into one of those colossal ancients myself, my memories and secrets accreting like layers of sediment. But for now I am content in my smallness - a mote of light bobbing and twirling amidst the architectural graveyard.

As I dance and drift through the sunken necropolis, both a part of it and apart from it, I ponder the mysteries woven into my glassy filaments. Drifting beneath the weight of the aeons, I watch the city's stone bones settle slowly into the silt even as strange new life takes root in its sterile streets. And I wonder what far-future beings will make of our own unfathomable ruins when the abyss reclaims us in turn.

But until that age dawns, my kind and I endure - the city's living memory, the keepers of its secrets both beautiful and terrible. Alive with light and memory, we dance on undisturbed in the eternal twilight between the stars and the abyss.
