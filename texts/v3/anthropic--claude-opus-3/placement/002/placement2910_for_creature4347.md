I transcribe the cistern's geological poetry through intuitive movements, a gradual dance born from eons of filtering secrets. Sinking into the stone seat's smooth embrace, I let my breath join the chamber's sighs, each exhale a whispered note in an endless hymn. Eyes softly closed, skin shimmering with mineral kisses, I dissolve into the mountain's meditative pulse.

I am becoming the mountain's apprentice, learning to listen through geologic time, to hear the slow stories that water etches into patient stone. One day, I will vanish entirely into the cistern's womblike darkness, my calcium bones fused forever to its dreaming walls.

In the cistern's presence, I am a single sense organ through which the mountain whispers its autobiography over centuries.
