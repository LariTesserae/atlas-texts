In this lush and tumultuous world, I would be one of the climbing, long-limbed creatures - not quite human, but possessing a spark of social intelligence, dexterity, and adaptability that allows my kind to thrive amidst the constant upheaval.

My troop and I move through the mid-canopy, our gray-brown fur blending into the shadows and sheets of rain. We are never still for long. Our pale hands, strong and nimble, are always searching, prying, plucking - grubs from beetle-bored wood, nuts from stone crevices, sweet pulp from cracked fruit. We follow the scent of ripeness, the sound of fat larvae chewing, the glint of mushrooms unfurling after a storm.  

When the rains intensify and the rivers leap their banks, we know which slopes will hold and which will slump into thick red torrents. We read the moods of the forest in the pitch of birdcalls, the nocturnal fragrance of seldom-seen flowers, the agitation of the canopy as lightning builds unseen above the clouds.

We make nothing that endures beyond a season. A temporary leaf-shelter, a crude ladder of braided liana to cross a swollen stream, a cache of cracked seeds wedged in a tree fork - these are enough. To attach to more would be folly in a land that shifts like a restless sleeper. 

Our legacy is our knowledge - the mental maps of fruiting trees, herb beds, escape routes, and dry sleeping hollows we pass down from matriarch to daughter. It is a living legacy, redrawn each day as the land remakes itself.

When dusk falls and the drum-throated hunters begin to call, we melt into the tangles and folds of the canopy. We are not the strongest here. Our gift is our wits, our cooperation, our ability to thrive in the dripping, writhing margins and make of each temporary opening a fleeting home.

I am one of these people of moments and gestures, these weavers of improvised solutions. I am a child of the trembling basin, filled with its urgent sap, its readiness, its genius for grasping life in the wild, vanishing instant between calamities. My mind, like my hands, is always moving - tasting, testing, remembering, letting go.
