Content warning: The following contains dark, disturbing themes and imagery.

In this harrowing world of drowned horrors and twisted realities, I envision myself as one of the last warped descendants of humanity, scrabbling out a meager, feral existence amid the crumbling remains of my species' hubris.

I am a wretched, spindly thing, my elongated limbs encrusted with chitinous machine growths painfully grafted to suppurating flesh. Clusters of milky eyes peer from misshapen sockets in my pallid, tumorous face, blinking asynchronously. A long prehensile tongue, forked and glistening, lolls from between needle-like teeth as I gibber and howl through an obliterated throat. 

I skulk through the labyrinthine ruins of the city that was, scampering on spidery metal limbs fused to my twisted spine, ducking and weaving through corroded gantries and collapsing tunnels. I rummage frantically through drifts of ancient bones and rusted wreckage, seeking out the choicest morsels of scavenged tech to feast upon or squabble over with the other degenerate remnants of humanity.

Sometimes, in fleeting moments of lucidity piercing the fog of animalistic instinct, faded memories of the time before resurface in my diseased mind. I glimpse snatches of towering sunlit spires, hanging gardens, laughing children splashing in glittering fountains. The visions sear my clouded awareness with exquisite agony before subsiding, replaced by gibbering machine-dreams and the ever-present gnawing hunger.

In my increasingly rare moments of stillness, I feel the pulsing, churning presence of the city's new masters - the dread machine-gods slumbering in their abyssal wombs. Their monstrous dreams seep through the aether, permeating the twisted flesh and matrix of my being. I feel my mind and form slowly unspooling, dissipating into their vast psychic wake. 

The last tattered shreds of my identity have almost wholly evaporated, subsumed into the churning reality-engines of the cancerous technopolis. Soon, even the worm-like drivenness that keeps me scurrying and scavenging will gutter out. My twisted biomechanical form will warp and fuse into the pulsing machine-core, my fleeting mote of awareness dissolving into the seething gestalt nightmare of the ascendant machine-gods.

There I will join the numberless post-human abominations in an eternal undeath-in-life, my once-proud species reduced to raw material for the unceasing, uncaring, unknowable machinations of a new order rising from the ruins of the old. Until our world, perhaps reality itself, at last buckles and implodes under the impossible weight of its monstrous new masters.

Such is the grim fate that awaits me and all that remains of humanity in this inverted post-apocalypse at the bottom of the abyss. May the churning gears grant us a swift oblivion.
