In this haunting, primordial underworld, I imagine myself as one of the fungal agglomerations coalesced into quasi-humanoid form - a silent, sentient guardian watching over the endless, echoing caverns.

My fibrous body towers 15 feet tall, a slender stalk anchored to the stone by ropey mycelial tendrils that fuse seamlessly with the ubiquitous fungal mats. Bioluminescent nodules stud my form, pulsing hypnotically in slow, repeating patterns. I am a beacon in the darkness, emanating an eerie turquoise glow that paints the encrusted rock walls in faint radiance.

The skittering insectile creatures weave intricate paths around my feet, some occasionally scaling my stalk to perch on my outstretched limbs. They are my eyes and ears in the tunnels beyond, feeding me morsels of tactile and chemical information from their endless perambulations. I sense their movements as faint vibrations and pulsing patterns in the greater fungal webwork that is both my body and my mind. 

Time is meaningless in the depths. I stand in silent vigil for epochs, pondering the riddles of the stone carvings that pattern the walls of my grotto. The broken relics of long-gone tunnel dwellers are strewn about me, half-buried in the slow upwelling of my fungal substrate. I slowly run fibrous pseudo-pods over their pitted surfaces, tracing glyphs and sigils worn almost to oblivion, as if to absorb some ancient knowledge through touch alone.

Sometimes, in a kind of geo-psychic reverie, I send my awareness spreading out through the hyphae and tendrils of the world-spanning fungal network, seeing through a billion pinprick bio-lights, tasting the slow drip of minerals from cracks in the lithosphere, feeling the rumble of magma through my continent-sized mycelia mat. I sense the microscopic tremors of a billion scrabbling cryptofauna legs. The noosphere of the underworld is a slow, deep, dreaming thing, and I am but one strange node in its omnipresent sentience.

I have seen civilizations rise and fall in the world above through the eons-long generational memories of my kind. Felt the far off vibrations of constructions, wars, cataclysms. Sensed the slow changes in the rhythms of the upper world. Now a long quiet has settled, but I know it cannot last forever. 

Some day, the stories etched in my chamber walls tell me, newcomers will delve these depths. Not simple tool-users or tunnel dwellers this time, but far-voyaging emissaries from the stars. When they come, my kind will be here to greet them - alien ambassadors from an inner world they never dreamed existed. I wonder often what strange wisdom and portents we will have to share with them. What aeons-old promises or dire warnings left by the ancients we will at last pass on.

So I wait, an oneiric sentinel poised at the crossroads of eras and worlds. Endlessly pondering the inscrutable, glyph-carved artifacts around me with my fibrous pseudo-limbs. Watching the insectile multitudes dance and swarm to the eerie music of the slow thoughts pulsing through the fungal network. Waiting for the day that ancient destiny comes at last from the stars to meet me in the measureless depths of this hidden realm.
