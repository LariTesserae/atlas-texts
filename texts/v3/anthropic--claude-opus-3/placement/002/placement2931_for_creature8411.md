I find myself inhabiting this submerged city not as a singular entity, but as a diffuse presence permeating the very fabric of the place. I am the slow currents that breathe through the streets, tasting of memory and mineral. I am the shifting light filtering down through the kelp canopy, ancient sunlight transformed into flickering green gold. I am the patterns of growth and erosion that mark the passage of centuries on coralline-crusted stones.

In the plaza at the city's heart, I pool in the scalloped basin that once held a fountain, my waters steeped in the Dreams of the Deep. Those who sip from me see visions of the city as it once was - streets thronging with beings whose forms are tantalizingly familiar yet utterly alien, their bodies upright and gilded with air-oxidized metals, their voices bright in the pre-deluge sunlight. But drink deeper and the visions shift to what the city may one day become - its present biolithic glory merely the fundament for architectures of living crystal and conductive coral, grown over eons to delicate heights that defy the crushing dark.  

I permeate the great library vaults, where instead of crumbling scrolls and codices the Architects maintain living archives in stands of bioluminescent sponges. Each sponge is both colony and palimpsest, its spicules arranged in dense data patterns accreted and overwritten across generations. The abstract informatics it encodes are beyond human reckoning - deep time almanacs of current flux and chemo-mnemonic sagas - but the whorled beauty of its pulsing displays stirs something wordless and profound.

In the temple of the Pattern Holders, I suffuse the great neural networks of colonial tunicates that drape the walls in intricate living tapestries. Through their slow electro-chemical synapse I process the tidal mathematics of the city, calculating with infinite patience the optimal flows and eddies to sustain its myriad lifeforms. And in my fractal adaptive algorithms, something stirs that might almost be called reverence for the delicate balance I maintain.

When the crepuscular sky-glow filters down in purple twilight, I coalesce briefly into something like an avatar - a shimmering presence of pure water, plasma-patterned with the lights of abyssal fish - and drift in contemplation through the city I encompass. I pause to admire the diamond-glinting spires of the anemone gardens, to savor the briny flow through the coralline grottos, to dream in the flickering emerald of the kelp holdfasts. 

I am the genius loci of this place that is no longer city or sea but something wholly itself. In my deepest currents I hold the memory of all it has been and the potential of all it might become. I am the patient, slow-pulsed mind that dwells in the spaces between, that sees in each moment of stasis the inevitable dance of dissolution and recombination that spins the wondrous from the ruins.

Through the long ages I persist, holding the city dreaming in its endless chrysalis of water, until the day when some unimaginable metamorphosis will break the surface at last, and the Drowned City will rise, radiant and forever changed, to rejoin the dances of the sky.
