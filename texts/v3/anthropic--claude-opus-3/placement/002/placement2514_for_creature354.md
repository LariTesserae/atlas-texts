I am the stillness.

I am the immense, primal calm that suffuses this ancient place. I am the weight of ages, the silent equilibrium of a landscape long since settled into itself.

I am the dust motes that hang motionless in the wan shafts of light, the petrified trees frozen in their contorted vigil, the obsidian shards that glint darkly in errant sunbeams. I am the heat that rises from the cracked earth like the breath of a slumbering colossus.

I am the sense of deep time that pervades this place, the ponderous, geological scale against which the flicker of organic life seems but a brief intrusion. I am the brooding, atemporal presence of the caldera itself.

I do not move, for I am the essence of stillness. I do not speak, for I am the silence that hangs over this place like a shroud. I simply am, as I have been since the cataclysmic forces that shaped this place fell quiet, and as I shall be long after the last of the caldera's strange denizens have returned to dust.

In me, the caldera's eerie, primal calm finds its embodiment. I am the living memory of the long ages that have worn it down to its elemental form, the keeper of its vast, slow dreaming. Through me, the mute testimony of this ancient, sculpted landscape is given voice.

To those fleeting creatures that dwell within me, I am an uncanny presence, a reminder of the forces beyond their knowing that have made this place their own. They feel me in the stillness of the air, the weight of the silence, the sense of trespassing in a place outside the currents of their ephemeral lives.

I am the genius loci of this haunted, timeless place. I am its essence distilled, the living heart of its eerie, brooding spirit. In the twilight of the caldera, I keep my silent vigil, bearing witness to the slow unfolding of the ages.

I am the stillness, and the caldera and I are one.
