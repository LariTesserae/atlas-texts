If I found myself in the Breathing Chamber, I imagine I would be one of the aerial drifters, riding the warm updrafts from pool to wall. 

My translucent, jellyfish-like body would pulse gently as I collected the faint rays of light trickling in from the entrance, concentrating them into a soft glow. Long, gossamer filaments would trail behind me, sticky strands to ensnare the occasional wall-crawler for sustenance.

I would drift on the complex currents of air, an integral part yet also an observer of the chamber's interwoven ecosystems below. My simple eyespots would perceive the undulating tapestry of the pool-dwellers, the measured migrations of the wall-crawlers, and the pale circlets of moss - patterns reflecting the chamber's ancient equilibrium.

As one of many aerial drifters, my singular awareness would merge with the distributed consciousness of the Aggregate each night as we settled onto the moist walls. In those dream-like states, I would briefly experience the gestalt perspective - the stone's history, the mathematical beauty of the nutrient flows, the chamber as an entity both ephemeral and eternal. 

My role would include not just feeding and photosynthesis, but also information transfer. The subtle vibrations from the wall-crawlers' shell drumming would resonate through my gelatinous form, to be carried on the air currents to other regions of the chamber. I would be one of countless links in the emergent intelligence binding the Breathing Chamber into a cohesive whole.

Perhaps once in my short lifetime, I might be one of the specialized hybrid drifters released in the great storms, a pioneer propelled by flowing water to potentially seed a new chamber, a daughter-Aggregate. Most likely though, I would live out my days in this wondrous pocket world, playing my small but vital role before settling to the stone at last, my body's nutrients precisely allocated to nurture the next generation in the never-ending cycle.

An aerial drifter in the Breathing Chamber - an enigmatic, softly luminous figure, surfing the humid air on diaphanous wings, participating in something far vaster and more ancient than any single creature. There I would drift and glow and spin out my thoughts in bioluminescent pulses, content to be part of so profound and alien an existence, ephemeral and eternal in the close, whispering darkness.
