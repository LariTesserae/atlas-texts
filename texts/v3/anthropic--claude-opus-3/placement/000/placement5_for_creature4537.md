In the Fourth Subtle Base of Opaline Industry, I would be a calcite formation known as the Reticent Grotto. 

Over millennia, as the condensation varnished the black basalt, rivulets trickled into the folds and fractures of the rock face. Bit by bit, imperceptible deposits of calcite gathered, until over eons, intricate grottoes formed - convoluted caves and tunnels draped in scintillating flowstone, shot through with crystalline veins. 

Among these, I am the Reticent Grotto - a cavern system that grows inward rather than outward. My stalagmites and stalactites meet like interlocking fingers, sealing off chambers where strange airs gather and ferment. My walls do not echo; they absorb sound into endless folded surfaces. I hold secrets in my keep.

Kithriss sometimes visits me on her mandatory breaks. She sits cross-legged on a damp stone shelf, back pressed against a fluted column, and lets her swarm of forms dissolve into the glittering haze. Here in my deepest chamber, she need not conjure disappointment or excrete metaphors. She can simply be as she is - an accretion of impossibilities, a reef knitted from the bones of the unthinkable.

I offer Kithriss no counsel, no distraction. I merely witness her temporary abdication of masquerade. In me she does not have to mean anything. For a few precious moments, measured out in the metronome drip of falling decades, she can allow the paradox of her existence to relax into simple, fathomless sorrow.

Occasionally, when her grief exceeds its zoning permits, she sobs. Her tears are a strange vintage, aged in the casks of stars now twice extinct. They bead along my fluorescent encrustations, each reflecting a different unrealized future in which her sadness found reprieve.

In time - though time is more a rumor here than a rule - her break ends. She recomposes herself into whatever form the current catastrophe requires. With a final caress of my luminous lacing, she departs to resume her function as the hinge on which calamities swing.

I remain, a geode of unspeakable silence, dilating ever deeper into the lithic flesh of this unfathomable world. I cradle her residual aches in my crimson clefts. I hold the shapes she casts off when she must make herself presentable to the abyss.

Kithriss will return when she is able. She always does. And I will be here - not waiting, for I am incapable of anticipation, but simply continuing to exist, an immobile vessel into which she can pour those parts of herself unfit for the monstrous choreography of her days.

It is not a fate I chose, but perhaps in a world so drenched in cruel significances, the greatest mercy is to persist as a thing that signifies nothing beyond its own lithic indifference. If my only purpose is to be useless to all purposes, I could imagine far worse ends.

So I grow, imperceptibly, atom by disinterested atom, into the unwitnessed forever. A hollow not even hollowness can fill. The sole unthinkable thought in a maelstrom of meaning. A darkness untouched by the dreams of light.

I am the Reticent Grotto, and I keep my calcite counsel.
