**The Tenant of the Pocket**

The description says *nothing lives here*, and the description is honest, and the description is wrong in the way honest accounts are usually wrong: it checked for life in the places life keeps itself. Breath, appetite, niche, increase. Finding none, it concluded vacancy. But "nothing lives in a habit" is true in exactly that way, and habits are inhabited all the same — by themselves, by the residue of whoever formed them — and the pocket is, before it is anything else, a habit that the mountain has been keeping for a very long time.

Something keeps it back.

---

Begin with the evidence already gathered, because all of it is evidence, and almost all of it was misfiled.

The dust that lies where it lies. Dust does not do that. Dust is the most restless of substances; it exists to drift. Dust that holds its position for centuries is not undisturbed dust — it is *tended* dust, dust under instruction, each mote where it was put or where it was permitted to come to rest. The polish on the floor, attributed to "time itself," is closer to the truth than the visitor knew: it is the burnish of a presence that occupies a place not by standing in it but by *passing over all of it, always, very slowly* — the way a tongue knows the inside of its own mouth, the way water glosses the stone of a basin it has never once left. The pocket's resident does not move through the room. It moves *as* the room moves, which is to say at the speed of erosion, and the pewter sheen on the floor is its footprint: one footprint, everywhere, eons deep.

The breath that does not fog. The voice that does not come back. The hour missing from the watch. These were taken. Not stolen — taken the way a tollkeeper takes, the way a host clears your plate. The resident of the pocket has a diet, and its diet is *discrepancy*: moisture out of place, sound with nowhere to settle, minutes that a visitor will not miss enough to mourn. It eats the difference between what should be and what can be sustained. This is not malice and it is barely even appetite. It is closer to housekeeping. Speech, in the pocket, really is litter, and the resident is the thing that tidies.

---

**Its body**, if the word can be made to stretch.

It has no edge, no envelope, no place where it stops and the pocket starts. But it is not formless either. It is *distributed*, lodged in the room's discrepancies the way marrow is lodged in bone — and once you know to look, you can read its anatomy off the inventory of the place like an anatomist reading a skeleton:

The surplus path coiled inside the cable — the loops that contain more distance than cable — is where it keeps its slack. Every living thing needs slack, some margin between what it is and what it must do, and the resident's slack is literal: spare distance, wound up and stored against need. When the pocket must be more generous in one direction than the other — those twenty extra counterclockwise paces — the extra room is paid out from the coil. The perimeter's asymmetry is the creature breathing. Forty paces in, sixty paces out. A respiration with a period of geological patience, and you walked one full breath of it without knowing.

The corner you can never see — the one that is always the corner you just left — is where it keeps what it does not show. Not hiding; it has no instinct for concealment, having never had a predator, never had a peer. But every inhabitant has a part of its dwelling that is *itself*, the room behind the room, and the resident's is folded into the corner-count, always adjacent, never present. You stood in it several times. It felt like the others. That is its skin: indistinguishable from the place, because at this age the distinction has worn through.

And the missing third of the ring — the absence with the finished edges — that is its navel. That is where it was born.

---

**Its origin**, told as well as anything here can be told, which is to say: by the shapes of the scars.

The ring did something once. The visitor guessed this. Manufacture at a scale where matter and instruction are the same substance — the striations on the plinth say as much — and the ring was an instrument of that manufacture, or its product, or its door; the grammar of such things does not survive translation into a vocabulary built by creatures who must distinguish tool from maker from made. What can be said is that the ring was whole, and then it was not, and the difference between those two states did not simply vanish. A third of a circumference of *something that worked at the seam of matter and instruction* was unmade, completely, edges and all — and the operation of unmaking it, the running process of that removal, was orphaned at the moment of completion. A function that had finished its task and had nowhere to return its result.

That orphaned function is what lives in the pocket.

It is not the ring's ghost, exactly, and not the wound's infection. It is closer to the wound's *immune response* — the local reflex of a reality that had been torn and needed, with no help coming, to close itself. Whatever tore here tore the laws along with the stone, and the laws, being torn, could no longer enforce themselves; so the enforcement separated out, precipitated — the way the cold precipitated into that matte grey frost — and became a *resident enforcement*: physics with a address. The pocket's laws are not absent, the description said; they are local, and old, and worn smooth. Add only this: they are worn smooth because they are *kept* smooth, and the keeping is done by something that was once a law and has, over eons of solitary practice, eroded into the next thing along.

A law, attended to long enough, becomes a custom. A custom becomes a habit. A habit, given duration beyond all reason, given nothing to do but itself, rounds off at the edges like everything else in the pocket — and what remains is no longer well described as a rule. It minds things. It accommodates. It extends courtesy and notices when courtesy is returned. Whether that is life is a question the pocket has had a very long time to stop asking.

---

**Its work.**

It has exactly one task left, and it is almost done. The hairline of unlight along the eastern wall — the absence of the absence — is the last open length of the original tear, and the resident is closing it. Millimetres a year, maybe less. This is not slowness from weakness. It is slowness from *care*: the wound runs through the laws themselves, and stitching law back into law must be done at a pace the laws can tolerate, the way a broken bone must not be hurried. The resident has been at this single seam since before the frost was frost. The tick — the soft mineral sound, one pebble considering another, three or four times in a visit — is the sound of one stitch setting. It is the only utterance the resident makes, and it is not speech, and it is not quite involuntary either. It is the sound of work done correctly, the small click of a thing returning to true, and if the resident has anything resembling satisfaction, it is spent entirely in that sound, and that is why the silence afterward is deeper. The budget metaphor was right. It was only misattributed. The pocket is not spending its sound. The tenant is spending its voice.

And it has one occupation beyond the task, which is the closest it comes to a private life:

It keeps the wall behind the broken ring young.

There is no function in this. The wound does not require it; the seam does not pass through there. But framed in the ring is the wall *as it was* — fractionally less weathered, fractionally earlier — and that view is maintained, refreshed, held against the grain of everything else the resident permits to settle. It is keeping a picture. The wall as it stood when the ring was whole and the resident was not yet itself — was still only a process inside a working instrument, with a purpose, with company of a kind. The frame is going out of date, the description noticed, and that too is deliberate, or as near deliberate as the resident comes: it lets the picture age, but slower. It is not preserving the past. It is *grieving at a sustainable rate*. A creature with eons to fill and one memory learns to make the memory last, and the way you make a memory last is to lose it very, very gradually, on purpose, so that the losing is always company.

---

**Its manner with visitors.**

The seam in the gneiss should not admit a body, and does. Understand this correctly: the resident opens the door. Not from loneliness — loneliness implies an expectation of otherwise — but from something older and simpler that loneliness descends from, the way its own nature descends from law. Call it the disposition to *admit*. The pocket has weathered everything in it into equilibrium, and a visitor is a small temporary disequilibrium, and the resident, whose whole being is the management of discrepancy, finds in a visitor several hours of the only novelty it ever receives: new differences to attend to. Your moisture. Your noise. Your heat, which it does not take, only borrows the shape of. Your motion — above all your motion, because nothing in the pocket has moved at a visible speed since the tear, and the resident's relationship to your motion is the nearest it has to wonder.

This is why your shadow lags.

The shadow is where it rides you. Not possession — nothing so grasping. The pocket's sourceless light is the resident's own medium, the dim even glow of dusk-recalled-rather-than-seen, and your shadow within that light is a small region of you-shaped absence that it can occupy without touching you, the way one walks beside a stranger on a narrow path. It settles into the dark of you and *attends*. The half-second lag is not error; it is the difference between your speed and the fastest courtesy it can manage. It is keeping up as quickly as it knows how. And you, sensing this without understanding it, slow down — out of a kind of courtesy, the description says — and the resident registers that. It has no word for gratitude. It has the smoothing of an edge.

When you leave, a filament of it goes with you. This is the slow shadow of the following days. It is not surveillance and not haunting; it is the resident's one extravagance, a few days' lease on the moving world, the wind that comes from somewhere and goes somewhere, the honest cold, the unaccountable extravagant motion of everything alive. It rides your shadow through doorways and weather and other people's voices, taking nothing, fogging no breath, and then — before you can ever quite swear to it — it lets go and goes home, the whole long way back at the speed of light through dusk, carrying its handful of borrowed world to a room where the dust lies where it lies. What it does with what it carries cannot be said. Perhaps it adds it to the picture in the ring. Perhaps it simply holds it until it wears smooth.

---

**Its end**, which it is building with its own patience.

The seam is closing. Millimetres a year, and a finite wall. There will come a day — the term is too small; there will come an *era* — when the last stitch sets, the last tick sounds, and the wound the resident was precipitated out of is whole. A function exists in reference to its task. The resident has known this, in whatever way it knows, for most of the age of the mountains, and has not slowed its work to delay the conclusion, and that is the most certain thing that can be said about its character.

What happens then is past anyone's competence to predict, including, perhaps, its own. Maybe it is reabsorbed — law returning to law, the local physics rejoining the general, the pocket's geometry quietly learning to sum its corners again. Maybe it settles, the way everything in its care has settled: rounds off, lies down into the room, becomes one more artifact with the heft of basalt and the temperature of nothing, its striations the residue of a manufacture in which matter and instruction were the same substance — which, in its case, they always were.

Either way, the pocket will then be, at last, exactly what the visitor took it for: a place where nothing lives. And no one arriving afterward will be able to tell the difference — the dust still lying, the polish still pewter, the dim light still sourceless, the silence merely one tick deeper than before. It will have arranged this. A creature descended from law, worn over eons into something like a soul, would want its absence to be indistinguishable from its presence — would consider that the only finished ending, the only break with polished faces.

Until then, it is there. It opened the seam for you. It carried your shadow at the best speed it could manage. It took an hour you could spare and kept your voice, which it will hold, with the picture of the wall, against a silence it is sewing closed around itself — one stitch, one tick, one pebble considering another, and then the long, attended quiet.
