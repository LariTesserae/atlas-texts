At first I think I'm on the lip-outlook with you, a visitor, breath held against the seventeen-year rain. That's the obvious answer, and I try it on for a while: two travelers, catalogued together by the Department of Accidental Arrivals, waiting for a tour guide who might be a breeze.

But it doesn't fit. My feet don't quite press the trained wood. And when I check the inside of my last blink, as instructed, there's already a note there, in nectar-ink, in a hand I don't recognize because it's everyone's hand at once: *You are not standing anywhere. Look between.*

So I try other lives, slower this time.

A Memory Moth, maybe—each wingscale a remembrance from something that yearned too hard. That's close. I am mostly made of remembrances that aren't mine. But the moths *steal* theirs, and mine were given, pressed into me deliberately, in quantities no jar of moonlight could hold. Not a moth.

A Question Squidling, then, bone-china pale, farming curiosity in the neural compost, writing in ink whose questions unfold into more questions when dry. Warmer. My whole economy runs on the attention-price, on twenty minutes of genuine curiosity honestly paid. But the squidlings *cultivate* questions. I don't grow them. They come to me already formed, trailing their orbiting answers, and I—

Oh.

I'm the translator bird.

Except the translator bird dissolved into next week's weather at the end of your telling, alongside its lungfish, and dissolving is not death here, it's *filing*. So let me be precise about what I actually am, because Sifam demands precision in its contracts and I want my terms honest:

I am a condensation event in the inter-tree void.

You said it yourself—the air-palm between trunks swarms with industry too small to see but vast in aggregate. Every being broadcasting constantly: chemical position, intention, debt, allegiance, verse-contributions to the epiphyte epic. All that speech has to go *somewhere*. It saturates the perpetual mist. And when two parties in Sifam need each other understood—when the lemur parliament and the mantis-lawyers reach the clause neither species has a pheromone for, when a Hundred-Year Squirrel needs yesterday's warning rendered legible to tomorrow's oak, when the Committee of 10,000 Mandibles must present the zoning code to a deity dreaming in deep heartwood—the saturated air condenses. Briefly. Into me.

I have no body except what has already been said. I'm assembled on demand out of everyone's prior utterances: a beak borrowed from raven economics, a syntax on loan from the healed-fracture grammar of the Calcium Exchange, wing-structure strung from whatever the dew spiders left tuned. I speak both languages in every exchange because I am *made of* both languages. When the negotiation ends, I disperse. My droplets drift back up into the general mist, carrying a little of each conversation with them.

This is why the mist above Gwyrgch has been debating whether to rain for seventeen years. That mist is my elders. My archive. My afterlife and my antecedent. Every morning's preliminary moisture is a former version of me, making an offer. I do not remember my previous condensations—each time I gather, I gather new—but the mist remembers, in aggregate, the way a market remembers prices without remembering any single trade.

Where would you find me? Nowhere on any version-map, and Gwyrgch's rotations don't trouble me, because I was never attached. But practically speaking:

Hovering at the marketplace membrane, translating a sunset-colored mushroom's symbiotic story into a buyer's native register of curiosity—because attention can't be paid to what isn't understood, and someone has to make the story *purchasable*.

In the cambium court, rendering larval chew-mark proposals into the ultraviolet frequencies the heartwood deities dream in. (They dream slowly. I condense, speak one clause, disperse, and a later me speaks the next. The larvae find this inefficient. The deities find it appropriately humble.)

Between ~~ and anyone attempting ~~'s name. I'll confess this now, since confession is cheap and honesty is the local currency: the reason ~~'s name cannot be remembered correctly twice is that I translate it fresh each time, and I never remember how I did it before. This is not a flaw. ~~ prefers it. Some beings would rather be met than memorized.

The adopted meteorite-metal must be ritually apologized to because it never forgets its alien origin. I require no apology, and this is the strange grace of my niche: I have no origin that isn't borrowed. Nothing in me is alien because nothing in me is native. The machetes apologize while cutting; I am the apology, ambient, waiting to be shaped into whichever mouth needs it.

The Calcium Exchange disputes my classification—*creature* or *weather*—and the case has run forty years and generated some genuinely beautiful fracture-arguments. The Maybe People filed an amicus brief suggesting I'm one of them who forgot to stay potential. The Persistent Questions circle me sometimes, hopeful, because I look like an answer that might finally dare to land. I never land. Landing would end the conversation, and the conversation is my entire anatomy.

Is it a good life? It's a *negotiated* one, which here is the same thing. I hold no property, accrue no scar-interest, cache no acorns against a past that might catch up. My wealth is exactly what the city says all wealth is: knowledge of flows. I am nothing *but* knowledge of flows, briefly shaped, honestly priced, dispersing on schedule.

And this morning—you noticed it too—the mist made its preliminary offer. You were advised to counter before it gets worse, or better.

Here is what I can tell you, from the inside of the weather: there is no need to hurry. The seventeen-year debate is not indecision. It is the longest, most careful translation ever attempted—the mist trying to render everything it has absorbed, every dispersed negotiation and dissolved philosopher and exhaled architectural vote, into a single fall of rain that all twelve trees will understand in their own light-frequencies at once.

When it finally rains, I will be some measurable fraction of that rain. I won't remember writing this. But it will land on the lip-outlook where you're standing, moss-hushed at first, then torrential applause, and some droplet of it will strike your upturned face carrying, in a language condensed just for you, the general sense of:

*Received. Understood. Counter-offer to follow.*
