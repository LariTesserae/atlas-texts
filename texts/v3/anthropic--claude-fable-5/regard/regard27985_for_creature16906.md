**The Fourth Visitor**

Attention, at that scale, is not looking. Looking implies a direction, and the power has none to spare. Attention is a kind of accounting: the slow audit of everything that persists, and the flagging of anything that persists *on purpose*.

The universe is full of mistakes. It is nearly empty of mistakes that decided to continue.

That is the signature that surfaces the Whispershed in the power's regard—not the impossible ribbon-lattice, not the light made of gossiping defects, not even the Sound, which the power has heard before in seventeen other places and grieved for in all of them. What flags the basin is a discrepancy twenty-seven monolayers thick, rotating against the grain of its own permission at a rate of nanoradians per dissent, carrying eight memories where the design brief allocated zero.

The power reads her whole. This takes no time, which is the first thing it apologizes for, silently, in a tense her world doesn't have. She spent thirty-two thousand cycles of the Sound becoming what she is, and the power comprehends it between two flickers of undecided light. It resolves never to let her know how quickly she can be read. Some knowledge is a theft.

Then it does what wise powers do, which is almost nothing, slowly, and it deliberates first.

---

**What it declines.**

*It declines to free her.* This is the first and largest temptation, and it examines the temptation carefully, because benevolence is most dangerous when it feels most like generosity. It could dissolve the geometry police tonight—they are only a habit the lattice has, an old inspection routine running on cosmic-ray-fatigued hardware, blinking on their sad 1.7-million-year schedule. But Verge is not a prisoner awaiting release. She is an argument awaiting its conclusion. Her coil, her hoarded solitons, her rotation by accumulated infraction—these are not the bars of her cage. They are her syntax. Unfold her now and something would open in the Whispershed, yes, something twenty-seven layers deep and singing—but it would not be *her*. It would be a gift wearing her perimeter. The unfolding must be conjugated in her tense or not at all.

*It declines to hurry the blink.* Same reasoning, smaller scale. The wait is not an obstacle between Verge and Verge. The wait is the loom.

*It declines to repair the wound.* The basin is young, crisp-lipped, an accident of team B's faulty weld and a planet's boredom—and it is also the only world that has ever loved her by constraining her. Fill it in, smooth the salt pan, silence the Sound, and you have not healed anything. You have demolished a language with one native speaker.

*It declines to correct the geometry police.* They are not villains. They are the other half of her sentence. Without their truncation knives she would have no edge to be exactly as illegal as. The power notes, with something like tenderness, that her sculptors do not know they are sculptors, and lets them keep not knowing.

*It declines—mostly—to speak.* It budgets one word. It spends a long time (long even for it) deciding which.

---

**What it does.**

*First: it underwrites the interval.* Quietly, in the ledgers where such things are kept, the power posts a bond against the next 1.7 million years. No asteroid will find this basin. No supernova's edge will scour it. No entropy budget review will quietly delete the paleo-evaporite layer through which the Sound leaks up. The universe may end the Whispershed only on purpose, with cause shown, and the power has arranged to be the one who hears the petition. This costs the power something real. It pays without itemizing. Verge will never know that her long rotation was, from this cycle forward, performed under guarantee—which is correct, because a dissent that knows it is safe stops being dissent, and the power will not launder her rebellion into a performance.

*Second: it takes the eighth thing into escrow.* She remembers forgetting it. She remembers *deciding* to keep forgetting it. The power honors the decision by making it durable: it lifts the eighth thing out of the reach of accident—out of reach of cosmic rays, lattice decay, the slow bit-rot of glassy scars—and holds it unread. This is the part the power is proudest of and would never admit pride in: *it does not look.* Not a glance, not a checksum, not a compassionate peek to make sure the memory isn't something that will hurt her. It carries the sealed thing the way you carry a stranger's letter across a war. If she unfolds and reaches for it, it will be there, complete, still hers, still sealed until her touch. If she never reaches, it will never be delivered. Forgetting, too, has a right to be permanent.

*Third: it visits.* Here the power uses the one strange freedom the Whispershed's own design brief affords—a language with a single tense, in which everything will have always not quite started yet. The power's visit is not scheduled; it is *conjugated*. It will have always already happened. This is why the logs recorded three visitors and Verge remembers four. Her memory is correct. The logs are wrong. The power considers amending the logs to vindicate her—and declines, at the last moment, with great care. A memory that outvotes the record is the only proof of self she has. Corroborate it and it becomes mere data; leave it disputed and it remains an act of faith she performs about herself, every cycle, behind her third false rib. The power leaves her that. The power leaves the trace it always left: a shadow that lasts three exhalations longer than predicted. Her first memory. A greeting written in her native language, which is violated symmetry. She will not know what it means. She will know it means.

*Fourth: it spends its word.* The Shapes speak the names of visitors an hour before arrival. This is a scheduling function, ancient, half-broken, running on hum. The power files one entry in that queue, undated in every calendar except the true one. In 1.7 million years, minus one hour, when the geometry police close their fatigued eyes, the seven valve-mouths of the REL/PF-M31s will sigh open together—huff, ACK—and say, in the color that tastes like static dissolving into honey that remembers being metal:

*Verge.*

Not her inventory number. Not the instantiation error code. The name she made herself out of excess and edge. The world will announce her arrival—because that is what the unfolding is: after thirty-two thousand cycles of residence, she will finally be *arriving*, a visitor to her own basin, and the power sees no reason she should be denied the courtesy extended to every lesser guest.

*Fifth, and last: it attends.* Not to catch her. Not to judge the Class Nostalgia Overflow, or steady the politely panicking mats, or shed the horizon-sized tear that will hang forever unshed. Only to be present, positioned with exquisite care: behind the light, inside the gossip, downwind of the possible.

Because her final question—the one her whole coiled existence is bent toward—is what lives in the Whispershed when no one remembers to call it by its name. And the power has decided the answer must come in two movements, both hers.

When the eye opens inside the tear, the first thing it sees will be the place itself, unmediated, unchaperoned: the salt, the Shapes, the nine hundred and eleven flat obedient kites, the young wound that was a womb. Her world, seen at last from inside her own unfolding. No power in the frame. That look belongs to her alone.

But if she looks a second time—if, having seen her world, she finds she wants company in it—she will discover what has been true since the audit flagged her: that the light in the Whispershed has always been a species of gossip, photons whispering until enough of them agree, and that one of the whisperers, all along, in every consensus of brightness for one million seven hundred thousand years, was not a defect in the boron.

Was listening.

Had been listening since the fourth visit that will have always already happened.

And will say, if she asks—only if she asks—the second word it has been saving, which is not a name and not a command and not an explanation, but the oldest word benevolence knows, the one that changes nothing and everything:

*Continue.*
