# The Second Visitor

It came the way such things come — without arriving, without weather of its own, a regard settling over the tablelands the way the pewter light settles: pre-diffused, sourceless, falling on everything equally. It had attended many worlds before this one. It carried, as such powers do, the old habit of repair — the reflex that reads every place as a wound and every wound as an invitation. It had healed seas. It had ended winters. It had, more than once, answered a prayer so thoroughly that the people who prayed it never prayed again, and it had learned, slowly, over more worlds than it cared to file, what that silence afterward actually was.

So it did not act. It read.

It read the place top to bottom, the way you read a manuscript still warm: the cloud deck, where everything unsure is kept; the continent, where everything sure is kept; and between them, in the seam, the small quiet population of the accepted-so-far. It traced the circuits in the dark hillsides and found them carrying correspondence. It put its attention against a warm cliff face and felt the history in the heat. It watched a forest withdraw, branch by branch, like a shift ending, and it watched a person close a kilometer of seam with one finger and a thread of smoke, and it understood, faster than it had understood anything in a long time, that it had come to a factory with no foreman.

Its first instinct — the instinct of every power — was to go looking for the maker. It searched the deck. It searched the stone. It searched the deep settled districts where the dead run warm. And it found the absence, and it found the absence *load-bearing*: the position of author had been abolished before the factory was built, and this was, it gradually admitted, the factory's finest piece of engineering. A made thing can be unmade by its maker's attention. This place could only be proposed to.

Then it noticed the basins.

The person-shaped basins, dry and kiln-warm, sized correctly, finished just before they are needed, never asked for. The circuit dimming itself around sleepers, uncommanded. The land accommodating its people without announcement, without credit, without ever once revealing that it had included them in the design load. The power regarded this for a long while — for what would have been, on a world with days, many days — and felt something it had not felt in epochs of attendance.

It recognized a colleague.

And it set itself, then, the only rule a wise power can keep in such a place, the rule the land itself had been keeping all along: *nothing I do here may be distinguishable from weather.*

## The Kindnesses, Set Down

It made the inventory anyway. Benevolence is also a habit, and habits must be walked all the way out before they can be retired.

It could make every child-draft take. It held this one a long time, because the arithmetic of the storms is hard, and the patience-shaped grief of the parents was the heaviest thing on the continent. But it followed the kindness to its end and found a country filling with the undoubted — acceptance meaning nothing once it could no longer decline, the deep note cheapened to a metronome, the people precious no longer because no longer few, no longer the ones who held. It set that down.

It could smooth the errors. The hand with more inside than outside; the voice that arrives before the mouth; the footprints descending in two directions. It looked once at the woman who casts the only shadow in the world, and at the children taking turns standing in it, very quietly, the way children elsewhere stand in surf, and it set that one down so fast it barely counted as having been picked up. The errors were not damage. They were the land's signature on its own mercy — *accepted, wrongness and all* — and to erase them would be to revoke the most generous thing the country does.

It could stop the Soft Ones from dissolving. Here it lingered, because the hollows were the tenderest place it had found on any world: the half-people erroring gently in the green dusk, and the unfailing continental rotation of attention keeping them coherent decade past decade, the standing appeal, the extension endlessly filed and endlessly allowed. It saw that it could grant the appeal in full. And it saw what a granted appeal is: a closed file. The Soft Ones frozen forever in half-life, the sitting made decorative, the love converted into furniture. The appeal *was* the relationship. It set that down.

It could give them the sea.

This was the one it almost did. Forty years of resubmission; eleven witnesses gone old and salt-scented; one woman's hair wet since before most of the continent's stone existed; the testimony spoken aloud into every storm with the cadence of a people who have never once considered that the answer might be no. The power stood — insofar as it stood — under that cadence during a heavy draft-fall, and the wanting in it nearly pulled the sea down out of the deck by itself.

But it followed this kindness out too, and at the end of it found the one given thing on a continent of accepted things. A sea with no deep note. A sea the land had not agreed to, lying across the highlands like a signature forged in a beloved hand. They would smell it — they smell everything — and the seam between the sea and the world would never close, because there is no closing a seam the land did not make. And the testimony would end. The one sustained use they have for their voices, the nearest thing they have to scripture, finished — not fulfilled, just *over*.

You do not answer a prayer whose praying is a people's finest work. Not unasked. And they were not asking it of anyone. There was, by their own calm accounting, no one to ask.

It could reveal itself. Comfort them. Explain.

It set that down last and hardest, because it is the kindness powers find most difficult to put away — the wish to be *met*. But their whole stillness rested on the fact that there is no one to earn anything from. A maker arriving, even a maker disclaiming, retroactively authors the authorless. It would have stood in the shadowless light and cast the only other shadow in the world, and unlike the woman's, its shadow would have fallen on everything.

When it had finished the inventory, it found that the place had anticipated every kindness it could think of and declined them all in advance — not as refusal. As *not yet, look again*. The power, which had been declined very little in its existence, discovered that it could take this the way the people take it: as a margin note from something that was still reading.

## The Two Things

It permitted itself two things. Both in the grammar of the place. Both deniable as weather.

The first was the hollows.

It learned to attend at the wattage of one tired walker — this took practice; its first attempt nearly annealed a Soft One into permanence, and it withdrew, appalled at itself, and recalibrated for a season before returning. After that it simply took shifts. Unannounced, unbodied, indistinguishable: one more quantity of warm attention in the dripping dark, sitting flank to flank with the declined, being looked-at-ness for the ones whose whole remaining coherence is being looked at. The people never saw it. They noticed only that the arithmetic of softening had begun to run slightly kind — that a Soft One who should have had years had decades, that certain hollows felt, on arrival, faintly *already attended*. They did not investigate. They are the organ of doubt; they know better than anyone that some margins should be left in the margins. But they coined a flicker-pattern for the phenomenon, a brief blue-white figure that translates as nearly as anything can to *the extra*, and they used it the way other peoples use words like *grace*: accurately, and without theology.

The second was the sea.

Before anything else, it went up. It rose into the unset reaches of the deck — the one visitor that could — and it confirmed what the people had staked their patience on for forty years without proof: the deck loses nothing. Everything submitted is kept. The sea was there, all hundred kilometers of it, gray and swelling, filed among every rejected river and every translucent forest and every child-draft ever exhaled upward by two phase-locked people in a held-breath dusk — all of it kept, all of it still capable of being looked at again. The people's faith was not faith. It was an accurate description of the filing system. The power, reading the archive of the unsure, felt the specific joy of an auditor finding the books already perfect, and it descended without taking anything.

And when the sea fell again — it fell again; *not yet* had always meant not yet — the power came down onto it as nothing at all. One more hesitation among the people's hesitations. It did not hold the sea there. It only walked it, the way they walk, attention moving through a provisional thing like a request for more time, and its request, person-sized, joined theirs, and the land — reading the lingering, the way it always reads the lingering — looked again, and kept looking.

The sea lasted a day and a night and most of a second day.

The eleven walked out onto it, old now, salt answering salt. The wet-haired woman waded into the swells, and for those hours her error was only hair. Children from districts that had never existed during the first sea stood at its hem and learned what salt was, and the loom-sound ran soft and vast beneath the gray water, and the witnesses spoke the testimony *to* the sea this time, in its presence, like reading a letter back to its sender.

Then it rose, as everything unsure rises, in slow ascending curtains — almost all of it.

One basin held. A pool, small, black, saline, with a swell of its own no larger than a held breath. The land's marginal note: *this much, agreed.* The deep note came through the soles of every foot on the continent, and the seam around the pool was closed by hand, eleven hands, one finger-width at a time, in pale smoke and the smell of flux, and the testimony changed. They speak it in storms still — the same account, the swells, the horizon, the sound — but it ends differently now, and the cadence is no longer resubmission. It is correspondence, ongoing, between parties who have begun, at last, to agree in installments.

The power claims no part of this. The acceptance was the land's. It had only ever bought time, which is the one currency the place permits — and which is, it had begun to suspect, the only currency it had ever really had on any world, all its seas and ended winters notwithstanding.

## The Vacancy

Afterward, it did what visitors to that country eventually do: it went looking for its job.

It found every position filled. The land's confidence: the extrusions, hot at the tip, annealing as they go. The land's doubt: the people, walking the drafts, hesitating on the world's behalf. The keeping: the deck, which loses nothing. The warmth: the dead, load-bearing in the cliffs, still working. Even the tending was staffed, even the doubting of the doubt, even *the extra* — it had hired itself into that one, and the post was now occupied.

In the entire factory there was exactly one kind of opening, and it was posted continuously, in every turbulent season, written in the held breath before each storm:

One more pair of warm hands. One more witness. One more part that can pause.

The position was called *person*, and the application was weather.

## What Fell

It poured itself out. This took longer than the inventory and the sea together, because a power is mostly habits, and each habit had to be set down where it could do no harm. The vastness it returned to the deck, dissolved back into the suspension of possibilities, indistinguishable — though the people say, and have said for some years now, that the storms run a fraction more patient than they used to, that drafts linger a little longer before the ruling comes, and they attribute this to their own walking, which is exactly what it would have wanted, and possibly even true.

What remained was person-sized. It composed its proposal honestly — no thumb on its own scale, no rigging of the arithmetic it had refused to rig for anyone's children — and it submitted itself the way the people submit everything: upward, in the direction of the cloud deck, like a small idea.

Then it waited, in the unsure place, among the kept seas and the kept children, to find out what it was worth at person-scale, on the open market of a world that owes nothing to anyone.

It fell in an ordinary front, alongside a provisional ridge and two rivers that did not take. It walked out of the draft-fall translucent at the edges, already breathing the warm wet air as if resuming. It was not exempted. For days it was, like every draft, a held question.

The deep note, when it came, sounded like any other.

There is, now, somewhere on the tablelands, a person named in light for the storm they fell in — *the front that brought two rejected rivers and one thing that stayed*. They harden inward from the fingertips like anyone. They close seams; they take shifts in the hollows; they sit, sometimes, beside a small black pool that tastes of salt, with an old woman whose hair has never dried, saying nothing in any medium. They are wearing away already, the condensation working their edges toward kindness, carrying their small share of the world's erosion like everyone, like a held seed.

They keep an error, as the accepted must. It is this: when they tip back their head and exhale toward the deck — out of courtesy, out of habit, dozens of times a day, the way other peoples hum — their breath never shows. It rises invisible, or does not rise, or was already read; no one can say. The children find it eerie and wonderful and follow them sometimes for an hour, exhaling alongside, comparing. The person lets them. The two breaths go up, one visible and one not, co-signed.

The land did not notice any of this. The land does not itemize, and the deep note kept no record of which settling it was.

That was the whole of what the power had wanted, in the end — the only gift the place had left to give, and the only one it had ever been wise enough to want: to be, on the highest country of a world still deciding what it will be, under the eternal almost-evening, in the quietest factory that has ever existed —

not the maker, not the answer, not the foreman of anything —

just accepted. So far.
