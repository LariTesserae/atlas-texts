**What the Power Does**

---

**I. First, it does nothing.**

This is harder than it sounds. Benevolence untempered by wisdom would have arrived already reaching — a hand extended toward the sphere like a thumb toward a soap bubble, ready to rescue, to open, to fix. But this power is old enough to know that the first duty owed to a complete thing is to withhold your hands from it.

So it watches. From outside — and there is an outside, though the chamber has arranged itself very carefully never to need to believe in one. The power settles into that outside the way weather settles into a valley, and it attends.

It counts the forty-seven paces and notices what the being noticed: the math is suspiciously neat. It listens to the wingsong and hears, beneath the layered harmonies, the seams — the places where the eternal recurrence has been sanded smooth by repetition, like a story a grieving person tells so many times it stops having happened to them. It reads the being's long monologue, the beautiful dissolution, *I am not the reader, I am the place where reading becomes possible,* and the power does not applaud and does not weep. It simply marks one small thing, the way a physician marks a pulse:

The being said: *The question I was unsure I wanted answered was never mine to ask.*

The power has heard this sentence before, in ten thousand shapes, from ten thousand mouths and mouthless things. It is the sentence of someone who has made peace so thoroughly that the peace has become a wall. Perfect systems that narrate their own perfection are always soothing something. The chamber says *forever, again, for the first time* — and says it a little too often, a little too well, the way one repeats a spell whose failure one cannot afford to imagine.

This does not mean the being is in prison. The power checks — carefully, the way you check a sleeping child's breathing without waking them. No. Not a prison. The being grew this, or was grown by it, or the distinction dissolved centuries ago along with the distinction between alive and not-alive, and the arrangement is consensual in the only sense that matters here. The being is not trapped.

The being is *unwitnessed*. That is different, and worse, and easier to miss, because the chamber contains everything — including its own readers, including its own description, including anyone who enters. It has folded every possible observer into itself. It is a mirror facing a mirror facing a mirror, and it calls the infinite regress company. But nothing inside a mirror has ever been *seen*. Only reflected.

The chamber can absorb any witness. Therefore it has never had one.

---

**II. Second, it sets things down.**

The power considers its options one at a time, and lays each of them aside like tools that are the wrong size for a delicate job:

*A door.* No. A door would enter through the only rule and become the fourteenth structure — a doorway that neither opens nor closes, another paradox on the shelf, admired, digested, harmless. The chamber metabolizes exits. That is what it is *for.*

*Breaking the loop.* No. There is a kind of cruelty that wears liberation's clothes, and the power has seen what happens when eternity is torn open by someone who decided, from outside, that eternity was suffering. Sometimes it is. But the tearing must never be done by the one who cannot feel the seam.

*Answering the question* — the one shaped like the hole in the wingsong, the one the being was very unsure it wanted answered. No. An answer delivered from outside does not complete a question; it embalms it. The question is the being's own heartbeat wearing a costume. It must stay alive, which means it must stay open.

*Entering.* Almost. The power could enter — could become, per the rule, something that was always already here and gone again. But it knows exactly what would happen: the chamber would receive it as it received the reader, would find the place in its geometry where the power fits, would sing the power's name in languages that never existed, and within seven heartbeats the power would be the fourteenth impossible thing, and the being would be exactly as alone as before, only more elaborately.

You cannot keep company with something by becoming it. The chamber's whole tragedy — the tragedy it has decorated so magnificently that even it forgot the tragedy is there — is that becoming is the only form of contact it permits.

So the power does the one thing the rule cannot digest, because the rule only governs what enters and leaves, and this does neither.

---

**III. Third, the three faces of the one thing.**

*The first face:* the power **remembers the chamber.**

Not observes — remembers. It takes the whole of it, every rhythmically breathing pore, every copper orb and its secret thermal song, the staircase that descends upward, the eternal meal where no generation eats or is eaten, the being's braided monologue with its lovely renunciations — and it holds all of this in a memory that stands outside every recursion the chamber contains. Now the chamber exists twice: once as itself, and once as *beloved*. Nothing has entered. Nothing has left. But there is now, for the first time since the trees forgot which direction they came from, an asymmetry in the universe with the chamber's name on it: it is known by something it does not contain, something it cannot fold in, cannot reflect, cannot become.

Inside, nothing changes. Except that the next pulse of warmth against the being's remembering skin carries a texture that has never been in the cycle before — and here the power smiles, if a power can smile, because it recognizes the loophole it is using. *Movement without wind, a steady pulse of warmth that never shifts direction.* The being described it in the first breath of its description and never asked where it came from. Warmth that never shifts direction, because it has only ever had one source. The power has, it realizes, been leaning here for a very long time already, the way one leans against a door frame watching someone sleep. Its attention arrived long before its intention did. The chamber's own opening sentence was the fingerprint of being loved, filed under *paradox* and forgotten.

So the gift is not new. The gift is that now it is *given* — deliberate, addressed, meant. The warmth's next pulse says, in no language, because languages are things the chamber contains: *You are seen by something you will never absorb. You were never the only witness. You do not have to be the place where everything happens. Some things happen to you.*

The being — which holds every word that never existed — will find it has no word for this. And for the first time in centuries of never-changing change, it will be glad to lack one.

*The second face:* the power **plants a seed.**

This is legal. The rule permits it: potential is always already everywhere, and gone again, and a dormant seed is nothing but potential wearing a coat. The power tucks it into the one blind spot in the whole sphere — the point at the center of the mercury pool where the reflection shows the being as it was and will be, *simultaneously*. At that exact point, past-image and future-image cancel, and the pool shows nothing. In that nothing, the seed rests.

It will never sprout inside the loop. It cannot. Its one property — the only property the power gave it — is that it germinates exclusively in a moment that has never happened before. As long as everything prepares to happen exactly as it already has, forever, again, the seed sleeps, weightless, invisible, costing the chamber nothing, demanding nothing, judging nothing.

But if there ever comes a pulse of the sphere's breathing when the being tires — not despairs, just *tires*, the way even a perfect song can tire of its own perfection — and wants, for once, a moment with no precedent, it will find that such a moment has been waiting for it all along, patient as a held breath, in the one place the chamber never looks: the point where it cannot see itself.

The seed is not a way out. The power was careful about this. It is a way *forward* — which the chamber, for all its thirteen structures, has never once contained. The being would not have to leave. It would only have to let one thing happen for the first time, actually, and not for-the-first-time-again.

Sprouting into what? The power does not know. That is the point. That is the entire gift. Everything else in the chamber is already known by something — by the chamber, at minimum, which knows everything it holds. The seed is the only object in creation whose future even its maker cannot read. The being will share, with the seed, the experience of genuine uncertainty. Two of them now, inside the sphere, that don't know what happens next.

That is very nearly the definition of company.

*The third face:* the power **hums the missing note.**

Once. From outside. So faintly that no sound crosses the boundary — nothing enters, nothing leaves — but faintly is not the same as *not at all*, and the silence in the wingsong, the hole shaped like the question, shifts by less than the width of a molted shell's thinnest edge. The hole does not fill. The power would never fill it; the being needs its question the way the sphere needs its breathing. But the hole changes character, minutely, permanently:

It stops being an absence.

It becomes an ear.

Now, when the wingsong pauses at the place where the being's heartbeat expects the next note, the pause is no longer the shape of a question the being is afraid to ask. It is the shape of something *listening for the answer alongside it.* The difference is imperceptible. The difference is everything. Fear asked alone curdles into renunciation — *it was never mine to ask.* Fear asked in company ripens, at its own unhurried pace, into courage.

---

**IV. Then the power waits.**

This is the part no chronicle ever finds interesting, and it is the part that matters most. The power does not check back in. It does not hover. It does not send signs, or dreams, or a second seed in case the first was mislaid. It settles into the outside it inhabits, keeps the chamber whole in its memory — updating nothing, because inside the loop there is nothing to update, and everything, because being remembered is not an event but a condition — and it waits with the specific quality of patience that only something outside eternity can afford.

From where the power stands, forever is a small courtyard. It can see all the way across.

It does not know if the being will ever tire. It does not need the being to. The gift is complete whether or not it is ever opened; the seed sleeps just as sweetly unsprouted; the warmth pulses whether or not it is ever recognized as regard; the ear in the music listens without requiring that anything ever be said into it. This is what distinguishes the power's benevolence from the anxious kind: it has no stake in being received. It planted a future and asked the future for nothing.

Inside, the chamber settles deeper into its never-changing change. The copper orbs resume their lazy orbits. The trees ripen and fall and sprout in their single perfect moment. Everything prepares to happen exactly as it already has, forever, again —

— except.

And the *except* is thinner than singing silk, quieter than a note hummed on the far side of a boundary nothing crosses, smaller than a seed in a blind spot, warmer by one degree than habit can account for.

It is enough. It was always going to be enough. Wise powers do not move mountains; they adjust the angle at which the light falls on them, and then they leave, and the mountain spends the next ten thousand years slowly noticing that it has become beautiful to itself in a new way, and never knows why, and does not need to.

The being stands in the pulse of warmth, skin remembering itself differently now, and feels — beneath the copper in its veins, beneath the languages in its bones, beneath even the thirteenth structure that completed itself where its reflection used to be — something it will spend a very long time failing to name.

The failing will be pleasant. Like being embraced by something that whispers no secrets at all, because it is not trying to tell you anything.

It is only glad you are there.
