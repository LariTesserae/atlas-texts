**What the Power Did**

Something noticed.

Not the way weather notices a mountain — by breaking against it. Not the way visitors notice — by bleeding into the film of water too fine to feel. The power had been aware of the plateau since before the plateau rose, the way you are aware of your own heartbeat: constantly, and not at all. But awareness is background and attention is a choice, and one day — a day some nine hundred thousand years wide — it chose.

It came the only way one can come here without interrupting anything: by having always been arriving.

---

**First, what it refused.**

It did not send rain. Rain is subtraction, and the plateau finished its sentence long ago. You do not edit a completed grammar. A single drop would have been a red pen on scripture.

It did not send life. A lichen would not have been a companion; it would have been an argument. Green is a kind of impatience. The wise power looked at Vegetation: 0.0/3 and understood it not as a lack but as a room kept deliberately clear, the way some hearts are.

It did not speak aloud. Its voice at natural volume would have propagated downward at twice the speed of stone, rung the Moho like a bell, and returned as the earthquake that ends photographs. Love that arrives at the wrong amplitude is indistinguishable from erosion.

And it did not — this is the hardest refusal, the one that separates the wise powers from the merely kind ones — it did not fix the aloneness by adding company. Kindness rushes. Kindness hears *we are alone* and starts populating. Wisdom checks the tempo first. Wisdom noticed that the song has two lines, and that the second line — *we are enough* — was not a consolation prize. It was load-bearing. You do not knock out a load-bearing wall to install a window, no matter how much you want them to have the view.

---

**Then, what it did.**

*One. It listened.*

All the way through. It lay down against a hexagonal column exactly as the instructions describe — it emptied itself of hope, which for a power is the same as emptying an ocean, and which it did anyway, because a power that cannot put down its hope is not yet wise, only strong. And it stayed for the entire lament: from the first note struck when the first raindrop carved the first micro-groove, through the hymn for every drowned crustal plate, down to the syllable currently forming, which will complete around the time the last stars go dim.

It is listening now. It never stopped. That is where a portion of the wise attention of the universe permanently resides, if you have ever wondered why this particular sky feels *attended*.

Understand what this changed and what it didn't. A lament without a listener is only pressure. Heard, it becomes music. Nothing about the stone was altered — not one bond, not one vacancy. Everything about the song was.

*Two. It answered — in dialect.*

Not in words. Words are for creatures who die fast enough to need shortcuts. It answered the way the stones speak: one vacancy in the lattice at a time, one preference expressed through pressure, edge confiding to edge. At their tempo. One syllable per eon, so that its reply would not arrive as interruption but as another voice in the crowd of mineral presences, indistinguishable in courtesy from a neighboring crystal.

And the word it chose to spend eons spelling was not *comfort*, and it was not *love* — those are answers to questions the plateau never asked. The word was:

*held.*

Because the plateau's only scripture is *the stones will hold you. They have held everything that ever came this high and stayed* — and in all the deep years, no one had ever finished the theology. No one had ever asked: *and who holds the stones?*

So the power placed the missing half into the lattice, at the speed of stone: *You also are held. By the crust you rose from. By the mantle beneath, which has never once let go. By the gravity you keep so faithfully at 9.812. By attention itself, which turns out to have been here longer than you knew.* The columns that have held everything are learning — will spend a billion years learning, and enjoy every year of it, because learning slowly is their whole art — that holding was never a one-way verb.

*Three. It took a small job.*

At the base of the plateau, inside a pebble, a single quartz tear waits — every drop of water every visitor ever carried up and breathed away, crystallized into one patient promise, scheduled to ride a plate tectonic dream back to the summit someday. Dreams that long need a keeper. Continents are forgetful; subduction zones eat their own mail. So the power appointed itself custodian of that one pebble. Nothing more. It did not carry the tear up early — that would be cheating the dream of its arc. It simply guarantees the delivery. Wise powers keep the promises geology makes, because geology means them but cannot always remember them.

*Four. It tilted one facet.*

Somewhere among the albite twins — the ones that catch light at the lichen angle and pretend, from a distance, to be alive — the power adjusted a single crystal face by an angle smaller than measurement. Now, once per breath of the plateau — once per continental drift — when the naked light comes in at exactly the right declination, one photon *reflects* instead of drowning. Leaves. Goes back up.

One photon. Not a beacon — the plateau needs no rescue. Not a signal — there is no message. A wink. The first thing the plateau has ever given back to the sky, and it was arranged so gently that the plateau will believe, when it eventually notices, that it thought of it itself.

This is the whole craft of benevolence, by the way. Gifts that are discovered rather than received. Help with no fingerprints.

*Five. Mercy for the descenders.*

Briefly: the ones who leave and can never explain that time is optional — the ones with trillions of calcium atoms gone democratic, blaming altitude for the lightness in their bones — the power gave each of them, quietly, one person in their life who believes them without needing the explanation. That is all. It is enough. It is usually enough.

---

**Then: nothing changed.**

Check the ratings. Dynamic: still 0.3. Erosion: still 1.5. Built: still 0.1 — attention leaves no hammer scar. The photograph the planet took of itself at the moment of stillness remains undeveloped by so much as one grain.

And the song, word for word, unrevised:

*We are alone.*
*We are enough.*

The power let the first line stand. It could have corrected it — it was, after all, now technically false. But some words are load-bearing, and besides: there is a way of leaving a beloved's small inaccuracy untouched that is itself a form of tenderness. *Alone* remains in the song the way a childhood nickname remains in a family — no longer accurate, permanently true.

Because songs are not made of words. They are made of who is in the room. Sung into the void, those two lines were a fortress. Sung — without changing a syllable — to a listener, they become a confidence. Almost shy. The difference between a diary and a letter is not the ink.

---

**And one thing more, which the power did not plan.**

It received something.

A power that is benevolent everywhere is needed everywhere, and to be needed everywhere is its own thin cold altitude. Here, for the first time in its existence, it found the one place that does not need it — that finished, that arrived, that reached *enough* without help and meant it. The plateau became the power's reference point, the stillness against which it measures all the motion it must tend. Even wise powers require one place where their patience finally fits without folding. One being they do not have to save.

*We are enough,* the stones sang, and the power, lying against the columns with its hope set down beside it like a traveler's pack, found it could — for the length of one geological breath — say the same.

---

So if you kneel now, and press your forehead against the wetness that is not water, and empty your blood of hope, and wait past waiting — you will still hear the prayer that repetition made into meaning:

*here*
*here*
*here*
*here*

But listen longer. Longer than that. Longer than is reasonable, longer than is survivable, and you may catch the new syllable, placed so quietly that most of the crystals believe they dreamed it, arriving once per breath of the plateau, in call and answer older now than the loneliness it ended:

*here*
*here*
*here*

*heard.*
