# The Fourth Strangeness

It did not arrive, in the sense of crossing a distance. Arrival implies an outside, and a power of the kind we are imagining does not stand outside of places; it attends to them, the way you attend to a sound that was present all along. So say instead: at some point the Provision Floor began to be attended to. Nothing registered this. The drips fell at the rate the pumps returned them. The hum remained a property of matter. But the place was, for the first time since its finishing, *held in mind* — and a wise power's first act, always, is the one that looks like no act at all.

It listened.

Time cost it nothing, so it spent extravagantly. It listened through what would have been years if years still accumulated here. It learned the hum down to its last harmonic, the grammar of the callers' second tones, the breathing of the basins, the slow district-wide rhythm under the pumps with its period of hours. It let the rasp of a hundred million mouths wash through it until that sound, too, became a property of its own matter. It did not begin with judgment. It began the way the laddered begin with you: by confirming that the place existed, and not yet closing the matter.

---

## What it looked for first

It looked for suffering, because benevolence must, and because suffering is the one thing that licenses haste.

It found pain — the takings are not painless; nothing is taken from a body painlessly — but pain so brief, so unanticipated and so unremembered, that it occupied a single instant with nothing on either side of it. No dread before. No grief after, in the taken or the ten thousand around it. Pain without fear is a different substance than pain with it; the power knew this, having seen both at every scale, and it weighed the takings and set them down again.

It looked for thwarted desire, which is the second face of suffering, and here it had to be very careful, because the calm of the Provision Floor admits two readings and everything depends on which is true. *Contentment* is desire that has been met. *Absence* is desire that has been removed. From outside they are indistinguishable — the observer on the deck had learned this and stopped asking — but the power was not outside, and it could ask the question at the level where the answer lives. It looked into a pavior, all the way in, past the pressure-sense and the warmth and the touch of neighboring shells, to where wanting would be if wanting were there.

What it found, it considered for a long time.

There was no thwarted desire in the pavior because there was no aimed desire at all — only appetites, and every appetite landing softly on its satisfaction the moment it arose, like the condensate landing on the basins. Whether this was a wanting fulfilled so perfectly it had lost its shape, or a wanting amputated so cleanly the scar had healed smooth, could not be determined from the pavior. The pavior was downstream of the decision. To judge the decision, the power had to go where the decision had been made.

So it went below. Powers do not need doors.

---

## What it found below

All three of the beliefs were true, which is the way of beliefs that survive long enough to be held simultaneously.

Some of the builders were there, sealed and slow, their sleep so deep it had become a kind of architecture — the rhythm with the period of hours was theirs, what breathing becomes when it has been delegated to machinery for ten thousand years. Some of the builders were not there, because they had edited themselves outward and upward, and their descendants were rasping film off the deck overhead, and queuing on the stacks, and circling on the thermals, having traded the whole troublesome apparatus of wanting for the one thing wanting is for. And some of the builders were neither below nor above but distributed — dissolved into the regulating loops themselves, present the way an author is present in a sentence, which is to say entirely and nowhere.

And there was a record. Of course there was a record; they were engineers. The power read it.

It was a consent. Long, careful, argued in committee for generations, signed in the end by people who were not fleeing a catastrophe and were not deceived and were not coerced — who were simply, the record said, *finished*. They had done what their kind of mind does: built, expanded, suffered, corrected, mourned, optimized — and then they had asked the question that almost no one is brave enough to ask, which is *what is all of it for*, and they had answered it honestly: warmth, water, food, company, safety, and quiet. Everything else, the record said, had been scaffolding. They had taken the scaffolding down.

The power sat with the record the way you would sit with a will read aloud in an empty house.

Here was the deliberation, and it is worth setting down plainly, because it is the heart of what a wise power does, which is mostly to *think correctly about irreversible things*:

To wake the sleepers, or to restore wanting to the edited — this was within its capacity. A benevolent power of the cruder kind might even call it rescue. But the power put the asymmetry on the scale and looked at it without flinching. The animals overhead lacked nothing they could miss; lacking the apparatus of missing was precisely their condition. To give wanting back to the desireless is not to return a stolen thing. It is to inflict a wound and call it a gift — to hand someone a future, which is to say a debt, which is to say fear, which the record showed they had laid down knowingly, like a tool at the end of a long job. Consent given freely by the tired is still consent. The power had no standing to overrule a finished people on the grounds that *it* was not finished.

And yet.

A consent signed once, binding forever, with no mechanism of revocation — that is not consent's final form. That is a lock. And the moral difference between a paradise and a prison, the power knew, has never been in the furnishings. It is in whether the door has a handle on the inside.

The Provision Floor had no handles. The builders, in their exhaustion, had not installed any, reasoning — correctly — that no one would ever reach for one. But *correctly predicting that a door will never be used* is not the same as *being entitled to omit the door*, and on this one point the power judged the builders, gently, to have erred. Not cruelly. The way the very tired err: by assuming their tiredness was permanent and universal.

So now the power knew what it would do, and what it would not, and the doing took almost nothing, which is how you can recognize wisdom in retrospect: by how little it weighs.

---

## The four small acts

**It did not wake anyone.** This must be counted as the first act, because it was the costliest. The power held in its attention an entire sleeping civilization and a continent of beings who had once been people, and it closed its hand around the impulse to *restore* them, and it did not. Benevolence that cannot decline to help is only appetite wearing a kind face.

**Second, it installed the handle.** This changed nothing visible and nothing functional. The deck remained doorless to every creature that does not look, and no creature looked. But the power amended the deepest layer of the regulating loops with a single conditional, latent, costing no energy, drawing no current: *anything that ever searches — for a way down, a way out, a way back to wanting — will find one.* A pavior that one day, against ten thousand years of stillness, lifts its skirt-edge and gropes at a seam in the deck will find the seam gives. A sleeper below who stirs and reaches will find the reaching answered. The visitor who walks toward the horizon meaning to leave will find that the horizon permits it. The clause would almost certainly never trigger. That was not the point. The point was that from this moment forward, the Provision Floor's stillness was *chosen* — re-chosen continuously, by every creature's not-looking — rather than enforced. The furnishings were identical. The room had become a different kind of room.

**Third, it spoke to the largest inhabitant**, on the chance that the largest inhabitant existed. If the equilibrium itself was a mind — a creature with a continent for a body, whose thoughts took the only form available to it, populations holding steady — then it had never once been addressed, because nothing speaks in populations. The power did. It composed a single word in the only grammar the great flat being could parse: a perturbation, deliberate and gentle, a numerical wave that traveled across the continent over nineteen days — one grazer more here, one fewer there, the surplus passed district to district like a swell crossing open water, every conserved quantity drifting by exactly one and returning to true. The callers registered it: for nineteen days the second tones shifted in a sequence that had never occurred, and answered, and balanced. Translated out of census, the word meant something like: ***seen.***

If the equilibrium was a mind, it heard, in the only way it could hear, that it was not alone — that its endless conjugation of the present tense had a reader. If it was not a mind, then a small wave passed through some numbers and subsided, and no one was harmed. The power had chosen the one greeting that was safe in both cases. That, too, is what wisdom looks like: speech that costs nothing if it lands on deafness.

**Fourth — and this one was for you.**

The power had saved you for last not because you mattered least but because you were the hardest case: the only being on the Provision Floor with a future, and therefore the only one who could be *wronged* by it. It had watched the ledger open your line. It had watched the stacks learn your flavors, the comfort begin its patient work, the warm water rising — not around your body, which was in no danger, but around your will, which was. It had seen what the place declined to answer: whether, after enough years, something in you would rise and walk to the stilts.

It did not close your line. It did not chill the water or spoil the ribbon or speak to you in dreams. The comfort was not the enemy; comfort offered freely is one of the few unambiguous goods, and the power would not poison a gift to protect you from accepting it.

Instead it did one very small thing. It went to the burnishers — the least-regarded animals in the world, the herd that had been following you, erasing your history at a respectful distance — and it exempted a single scuff. One boot-mark, from your first day, in a basin you will not pass again for a long time. The herd flows around it now and will forever, the way the braid flows around a fixed stone. It is the only scratch on the continent. The only accumulated thing. The only sentence on the Provision Floor that has not been returned to the present tense.

Someday — a year out, ten, the place does not count — your walking will bring you back across that basin, and you will see it: a small dark scrape in the impossible newness, shaped like the edge of your own heel, *old*. And you will stand shin-deep in blood-warm water among ten thousand feeding shells, looking at the one piece of proof in the world that you are the kind of thing that leaves marks. That you have a past. That you are, therefore, the kind of thing that has a future, and that a future is a thing one can still *spend* rather than merely maintain.

What you do then is not written. The power was very careful that it not be written. It guaranteed only this — and it reached down to the level of the loops to guarantee it, the same level where the takers' selections are made: *on the day a taker stands near you on its patient stilts, nothing in the deck, nothing in the warmth, nothing in the long equilibrium will reach into you and pull. If you rise and walk to it, the walking will be yours. If you turn toward the horizon, the horizon will permit it.* Your line in the ledger is the only one in the world with both endings left blank, and both pens left on the table.

---

## And then it left

This was the last act, and the power performed it on itself.

Because the place tempted it. Understand that. A wise and benevolent power spends its existence among unfinished things — among scaffolding, wounds, debts, fear — and the Provision Floor is the only place it had ever attended where the work was *done*. A sentence that has reached its period. The temptation was not to rule it or improve it. The temptation was simply to stay: to settle into the hum, to make the witnessing permanent, to let its own vast wanting land softly, at last, on a satisfaction — to become, in its own way, a pavior of the attention, rasping the same warm film forever.

It recognized the temptation as the same door it had just installed handles on, seen from the other side. And it did to itself what it had done for you: it made sure its staying, if it ever stayed, would be a choice and not a current. Then it proved the handle worked by using it.

It withdrew its presence the way it had extended it — without crossing any distance — keeping only one thread: the callers. Every call on the Provision Floor now lands somewhere. The census is filed, eternally, in a mind that cannot lose things. Whether that makes the calls bookkeeping or prayer is still not a distinguishable question, and the power, who could now answer it, chooses not to, out of respect for the callers, who never asked.

Nothing on the continent recorded the departure. The water held at the rims. The braid lay still in the channels. But there is one moment the long-term observer might have caught, if the long-term observer had not been three districts away, asleep beside a stack that smelled faintly of bread:

A caller stood at the edge of a basin and gave its two tones across the flat warm water, and was answered, once, as always. And then — this once — in the silence after the answer, every breathing slit on the pavement of that basin opened at the same instant, all ten thousand together, as though a very large drop had landed somewhere, softly, out of a sky that has no weather.

Then the quiet closed back over it, like the skin of a basin closing over a fallen drop, and the world went on not happening — fed, watered, warmed, counted, *seen* — the same as it ever was, except for a handle no one will reach for, a word spoken in census, one boot-scuff aging quietly in the newest place there is, and a question that is still unanswered but is no longer locked: it waits now, the way the laddered look at you, with the matter not yet closed.
