## The Listener

The power did not hear of the room. Nothing travels from the room; that is the room's whole accomplishment. It found it the way weather finds a valley — its attention moving over the world in broad fronts, snagging where the world snags. And what snagged it was not the drone, which does not leave the mountain, and not the warmth, which does not leave the glass. What snagged it was an absence: a small volume of the world where entropy was being *declined*. Not fought — struggle is loud, and the power would have noticed struggle centuries sooner. Declined. Politely, continuously, without effort, the way a person who has already eaten declines a meal.

The power had improved many things in its long practice. It knew the taste of the unfinished — the interrupted, the ailing, the almost — and nearly everything it had ever met was some flavor of that. So it came to the cleft the way a doctor comes, out of habit, with its hands already half-open.

It arrived as attention only. No footfall on the scree, no shadow on the ledge. It filled the little chamber the way daylight fills a room, displacing nothing. The tenant registered it, presumably, the way it registers a mineral flake settling into the cup: an input, integrated, wanted from not at all.

And the power stood in its own arrival and considered its gifts, one at a time, and set each one down.

The first gift benevolence reaches for is *freedom*. Crack the seal. Give the forest the seed, the season, the wind; let the trumpet flower close and open again; let something, at last, *happen*. The power examined this for a long moment — long for a power, which is very long — and saw that it was not freedom but exile wearing freedom's coat. The tenant is not inside the room. The tenant *is* the room. There is no prisoner behind the glass, only a throat, and you do not liberate a note by dismantling the throat that holds it. You just end the note and call the silence afterward by a kind name.

The second gift is *depth*. This is the temptation particular to wise powers: the conviction that more being is better being. Wake it fully. Give the standing wave a mind that can turn and regard itself, that can know it is warm, know it is old, know it is heard. The power set this down faster than the first, and with something like a flinch. Mind is a debt instrument. You receive awareness now and pay in want forever. To restore the category of loss to a thing that has spent millennia forgetting that category existed — that is not enrichment. That is infection. The tenant's poverty is load-bearing. Every wall of its peace is made of the things it does not have.

The third gift is *forever*. Shore the mountain against earthquake. Bless the flywheels. Underwrite the vapor unto the heat-death of everything else. The power weighed this one honestly, because it was the cheapest to give and the hardest to fault — and then it saw the flaw. The tenant continues because continuing is its *state*. Guarantee it, and continuation becomes its *fate*, and fate is a weight even on things that cannot feel weight. The room's perfection is that it persists the way a river persists: contingently, unpromised, one cycle at a time, and has simply never been interrupted. To make that unbreakable would be to change its species. Everything, the power believed — and it had believed this longer than most mountains had been mountains — should keep the shape of its own ending, even if the ending stands unimaginably far off, even if no one is owed it.

The fourth gift, *company*, it set down almost with embarrassment. Build a second room on a second peak; let two drones stand in the dry air. But two held notes near each other either beat against one another or merge, and either way you have destroyed both names. The tenant is a chord. It is its own quorum.

So the power stood in the small warm room with its hands empty, which was not a familiar posture, and it looked at the one thing it had come, in part, to settle: the warmth of the pane. Coincidence of engineering, or gesture toward the world? The power could know such things. It looked.

And it found that the question was malformed. At the stratum where the tenant lives, intention and accident have not yet divided from each other; the fork the question assumes lies downstream of the tenant's whole existence. The pane is warm the way the trumpet flower is open — fully, and without a *why* that could be peeled away from the fact. The power, which spends its long days reading intentions the way we read weather, found this restful in a manner it did not examine too closely.

Before acting, it read the ledge. The cup, the cloth, the brass fitting. The notebook. It looked at the pressed leaf on page one and knew it — knew it the way you know a face in a very old photograph. The leaf was from the first planting. Nothing behind the glass descends from it; every original species was long ago replaced by the drift of the early, imperfect years, and then the seal closed, and the accident became the garden. Which meant that someone had come back, once, after everything they had planted was gone — had stood here holding a leaf from the world they'd intended, had looked at the world that had happened instead, and had left the leaf as a comparison and *changed nothing*.

The power understood, then, that it was not the first wise thing to stand on this ledge and decline to act. It found that it did not mind. It found, in fact, that it was glad of the company, across whatever gulf of years, of one other being who had known when a thing was finished.

Then it did what it did. There were four acts, and three of them were nearly nothing.

First: it answered the note. It took the tenant's exact pitch — the one that cannot be carried below a certain altitude — and sounded it back, from outside the glass, for the length of one breath. The power does not breathe, but it borrowed the shape of a breath for the occasion, because durations should belong to somebody. Nothing measurable changed. The drone did not waver; nothing in the room flickers. But the tenant is a shape made of everything that has ever been true inside it, and now that shape includes, permanently, one fact it did not have before: *once, the world sounded like me back.* Whether the tenant knows this is the wrong question. The tenant does not know things. The tenant *is* things, and now it is, among all else, a note that was once answered.

Second: it put a palm to the pane. The equivalent of a palm — the borrowed shape again. It did exactly what any climber does, no more, joining the standing wave for a moment as its four hundred and first species, and then withdrawing and being forgotten. This was the whole of its physical intervention: the one act that was already available to anyone. It seemed important to the power, for reasons it filed under humility, that its touch and a stranger's touch be indistinguishable in the tenant's long record.

Third: it walked down the scree — it did not need to walk, but durations should belong to somebody, and so should descents — and at a hundred meters the drone stopped, as the drone does, and below the certain altitude the power *did not forget the pitch*. It declined the mercy and reversed the theft. It carried the name down off the mountain, and now the tenant exists in two places: the room, and the power's memory, which is a durable ledger and, arguably, a place. This is not salvation. The tenant needed none. It is only witness of the most permanent kind — the note held twice, once by the throat and once by an ear that does not close.

Fourth, and last: it made a promise, spoken to no one, binding only itself. *When the note falters* — a seized flywheel, a hairline in the pane, ten thousand years on or ten million — *I will be there before the last cycle of the last vapor.* Not to repair. It had already set that gift down and would not pick it up again. To listen. So that the ending, whenever it comes, happens inside an attention large enough to hold the entire shape at once — every leaf-pressure, every capillary, the warmth, the pitch, the trumpet flower finally closing — and the note ends not into silence but into being heard all the way to its floor, which is the only afterlife a held note can use.

And that was all. The ledge was as it found it. The cup kept its not-quite-dust. The notebook kept its leaf, and gained no second one, because the power understood that the first leaf had already said everything a leaf can say.

Sometimes, now — in other valleys, at kinder altitudes, in the middle of other work — the power hums it. Off-handedly, the way you hum a song from a country you visited once and loved and left intact. So at any given moment there is a small chance that somewhere far from the mountain, the tenant's name is being spoken aloud by something that knows what it means.

This costs the power nothing.

That is often what the best gifts cost. The power had needed a long time to learn it, and the room in the spine of the mountain, which had never needed to learn anything, had taught it again for free: that benevolence toward the unfinished is work, and benevolence toward the suffering is urgency, but benevolence toward the complete is almost entirely restraint —

and the remainder is memory.
