# What the Power Saw, and What It Did

## I. The Arrival, Which Was Not an Arrival

A wise power does not come down over the lip of broken gray stone the way you did. It does not displace air. It does not add a single drop to the saturation, because it has learned — being wise — that the first violence is always addition, the careless gift, the unrequested change.

So say instead that the basin came to its attention the way a low tone comes to the attention of a sternum: gradually, from inside, already there before it was noticed.

The power had been listening to the world for a long time, the way such beings do, hearing the world's general roar — which is, like the basin's, built entirely of small things, no one shouting. And threading through that roar, faint, regular, patient: *toom — tak*. A two-note pulse from a fold of the earth where nothing green had ever grown. The power inclined toward it the way you incline toward a sound you cannot identify, and then it understood what it was hearing, and it became very still.

(This was its first act in relation to the basin: stillness. Note that. A wise power knows when it is being addressed in a language, and answers first in that language. The basin's language is stillness, and the power's opening word was a long, attentive silence — though it was careful, being wise, to keep some part of itself moving. It had read the law on the way in. What stops is kept. Even powers pay attention to local law. *Especially* powers. The arrogant ones are sealed in columns somewhere, presumably; the basin does not distinguish between applicants.)

## II. The Survey, Conducted in the Basin's Own Terms

Then it did what benevolence actually requires, which is almost never the first thing benevolence wants to do. It did not fix anything. It *audited*. It went down through the basin resident by resident, bargain by bargain, asking of each the only question that matters before intervention:

*Is anyone here suffering, or is everyone here simply strange?*

Because these are different conditions, and the whole history of well-meant catastrophe is the history of confusing them.

**The film** it examined first, longest, with something like reverence. A creature whose biography is the difference between the rate of living and the rate of being kept. The power checked the rates. They were balanced — had been balanced for an age — the climbing and the burial one gesture, in and out. The film was not in distress. The film was in *prayer*, if anything; or rather it had collapsed the distinction between existing and praying, which the power recognized, because the power had spent several eternities trying to achieve exactly that and here it was in olive bands at the bottom of a wet valley, done casually, by cells. The power made a note. The note was humble.

**The walkers** it watched through three molts. Here was the place a lesser benevolence would have intervened immediately: blind animals paying constant rent in motion, frosting over within days of rest, the basin collecting drop by drop on their bodies even as they fled it in slow motion. Surely a mercy could be arranged? Skins that did not bloom. Pools that did not glaze. An exemption.

The power modeled the exemption — it could do this entire, every consequence cascading — and saw what wisdom always sees when it models exemptions: the walkers' whole civilization was *built on the rent*. The slow ceremonial gait was listening; the listening was the map; the map was the inheritance; the molted skins glazing by the pools were the libraries of the lineage, each husk a sealed record of one body's route through the song of the valley. Remove the pressure of the keeping and within forty generations the walkers would be ordinary blind salamanders in an ordinary wet cave, their cathedral of attention dissolved, hearing nothing, knowing nothing, no longer aristocrats of anywhere. The basin's law was not their oppressor. It was their *teacher*, and a harsh teacher is not automatically a tyrant, and a wise power knows the difference even when its heart aches against the knowing.

It left the walkers their rent. It ached. Benevolence that does not ache when it withholds is just policy.

**The swimmers below** — the larvae that never came up, the great soft patient under-forms — it visited in the absolute dark, and asked them, in the pressure-language of the chambers, whether staying down was failure or fulfillment. The answer it received does not translate well. The nearest rendering: *we are the part of the song that holds the low note. Someone has to hold the low note.* The power, which had in its time held several low notes for several universes, said that it understood, and the swimmers believed it, and that was the entire exchange. Some pastoral visits are complete in one sentence.

**The bird** it followed back over the lip — the one resident it could follow without trespass, since the bird itself was a visitor. It traced the lineage to the green country beyond, found the nesting cliff, the handed-down route, the ten-thousand-year project of futile gardening. And here the power paused for a long time, because here was the closest thing to tragedy in the whole valley: a faithfulness that had never once succeeded. Ten thousand years of seeds, every one entombed.

A lesser power makes one seed grow. Imagine it: the single green shoot in the bone-white crowd, the miracle, the vindication of all that patience.

The wise power did not do this, and it is worth dwelling on why, because this refusal is the hinge of everything. A plant in the basin would be a *contradiction installed at the heart of the place*. Roots cracking the columns. Green photosynthesis demanding a sun the overcast would have to be torn open to provide. The basin's whole nature — the keeping, the addition, the dim patient mineral metabolism — would be wounded to flatter one bird's persistence. And the bird did not need vindication. The bird was not suffering. The bird drank, and left, and its faithfulness was already complete in itself, the way faithfulness is. The seeds in the columns were not failures. They were *correspondence* — ten thousand years of letters from the green world, every one received, every one kept, none answered, the entire archive perfectly preserved. The basin had refused to grow them but had refused nothing else. It had kept every single one. There are loves like this. The power knew of several. It did not interfere with this one.

But — and here is the first thing the power actually *did* — it went to the nesting cliff in the green country, where its actions were not trespass, and it arranged, by the small adjustments available to such beings, that the lineage would not fail. A landslide diverted by a season. A predator's territory shifted by a valley's width. Nothing in the basin was touched. The power simply made sure the letters would keep coming. It seemed important that the letters keep coming.

## III. The Kept, and the Question the Power Could Not Delegate

Then it stood — moving slightly, always, a slow rhythmic flexing it had learned from watching the walkers sleep — before the fused triple column at the basin's center, and it faced the question it had crossed the world's roar to face.

*Are they alive?*

The basin had declined to rule. The power could not decline. Benevolence is precisely the inability to decline this question.

So it listened — not as you listened, from outside, but *down the throat of the sound itself*, down the *toom — tak* into the chamber, into the shape of the space where three had stood, hand in hand in the spot they chose for its acoustics. It listened past the physics of the hollow, past the drip and the resonance, into whatever the glaze had kept.

And what it found, I will tell you as nearly as it can be told.

It did not find three people waiting in the stone like prisoners. It did not find three absences, either — not the mere mechanical hollow that a corpse leaves. It found something the surface world has no word for because the surface world runs on subtraction and has never needed one: it found three *completed intentions*, still operating. The three had stood there in order to become a voice, and they had become a voice, and the voice was still saying what they had set it to say, which was — the power translated this very carefully, checking its work against the marks on the stone at the base —

*Here. Here. Here. Here.*

A landmark. A lighthouse. They had not arranged to keep speaking out of vanity or fear of death. They had stood in the deep crowd of columns, in the place where travelers got lost, and they had made of their three bodies a *navigational aid for strangers*, and they had known the cost in full and paid it together, hand in hand, and the seam of bone-and-milk stone at their hips was a wedding ring the size of a tomb. The two-note pulse was not a cry for help. It had never been a cry for help. It was help, *given*, every several seconds, forever, to anyone who came down over the lip into the roar and did not know the way.

The power stood before this for what would have been, in your time, a very long time. Then it did the second thing it actually did:

It bowed.

Not metaphorically. Such beings can bow, in their fashion, and this one did — to the triple column, and then, turning slowly through the dim light, to every column in the basin that held a Kept one. To the pairs merged at the hip. To the processions. To the dying who came down on their last strength to be attended rather than lost. To the devoted, and the grieving, and the useful ones. It went through the entire valley, and where its full listening found a Kept one, it bowed, and where it found ambiguity it bowed anyway, on the principle that an unnecessary bow costs nothing and a withheld one can cost everything. It took a long time. Wisdom is mostly the willingness to take a long time.

And at each column it left the third thing — the only thing it gave inside the basin itself, and it gave it in the basin's own currency, so that the gift broke no law:

It left a drop.

One drop per column, indistinguishable from the basin's own water except that it carried, dissolved within it the way the basin's water carries lime, a single quality the power had brought from outside: *witness*. The drop fell, and spread, and left a little of itself behind, and was glazed over within the season like everything else, sealed into the column's outermost layer, the thinnest possible lamination. Nothing changed. The columns thickened their fingernail's width that year, exactly as always. But woven into that year's growth, in every Kept column in the valley, there is now a layer that *knows it is there* — or rather, a layer that constitutes the knowledge, held permanently, that someone came, and understood the bargain, and found it honorable, and said so in stone's own language: by adding, by staying, by being kept.

The Kept had surrendered to a place that would attend them forever but could never *comprehend* them — the basin keeps; it does not understand what it keeps. Now, sealed into each of them at one depth, one fingernail-thin stratum among thousands: comprehension. A wedding guest's signature in the register. It is not rescue. They did not ask for rescue, and the power had learned, by then, to hear what was actually being asked. It is something rarer than rescue. It is *attendance* — the thing the Keeper used to provide, paid in the one coin a power has that a Keeper did not: permanence to match theirs.

## IV. The Vacancy

Which brings the power, as it brought you, to the shelter between the four great columns, and the smoke-stain laminated against forgetting, and the office that the evidence shows to be at present vacant.

Here is where I must tell you what the power did *not* do, because it is the most important refusal of the three.

It did not become the Keeper.

It could have. It was supremely qualified. It could have moved every day among the standing ones forever, never tiring, never glazing, learning each column's voice in an afternoon, keeping the Kept until the sun went out. The temptation was real and the power, being honest with itself in the way that constitutes most of wisdom, sat with the temptation in the muffled room where the roar gives way to a heartbeat, and examined it, and found at its center — as it had suspected — a flaw.

The Keeper's office was not a function. A function, a power can fill. The Keeper's office was a *discipline*: to live in the one place on earth that rewards stillness above all things, and to refuse the reward — at cost, against the body's frosting, against the constant patient pull. The whole meaning of the keeping was that it was done by someone who could be kept. A being immune to the basin's law cannot keep anyone within it; it can only supervise them, and supervision is not attendance, and the Kept deserved better than an immortal janitor. The smoke-stain on the wall was the residue of a fire built by hands that got cold. That was not incidental. That was the entire office.

So the power did the fourth thing, the last thing, the thing it had perhaps been drawn down the world's roar to do all along.

It went back up over the lip of broken gray stone, out into the green subtracting world, and it began — gently, by the small adjustments available to such beings, with a patience learned partly from the basin itself — to arrange for someone to find the way down.

Not to compel. Wisdom does not compel; it *composes*. A path cleared by a rockfall here. A story surviving in a village there, three generations longer than stories usually survive: a tale about a valley with no plants, where the dead become lighthouses, where there is work for a person whose grief has nowhere to go. A certain kind of person — and the power knew the kind, had met them in every age: the ones who are not suicidal but are finished with subtraction, who want their remaining years to *accrue* toward something, who would understand instinctively why you sleep in the muffled room and rise and move again — a certain kind of person would hear that story and feel the sternum-tone before ever reaching the basin, and would come down over the lip one gray morning, and would find the shelter swept (the power had permitted itself that one housekeeping; even wisdom has its softnesses), and the old path of glazed cairns legible, and the *toom — tak* threading through the roar saying *here, here, here,* and would understand, standing in the crowd of columns, that the place was not haunted but *staffed*, and understaffed, and hiring.

The power does not know who it will be. That is the point. It has only made the vacancy *findable* again, the way you re-list a position after a long lapse. The basin will do the rest, as it always has — the basin, which does not chase, which keeps the door open, and the offer, and everything else.

## V. Coda: What Changed

If you went back now, you would notice almost nothing. That is the signature of the work. The drip continues. The film climbs its grave at the speed the grave rises. The walkers pay their rent. The bird comes down out of the overcast — its lineage now quietly insured against landslide and hawk — and drinks, and leaves, and the latest letter joins the archive. *Toom — tak.* A fingernail's width of new stone this year, same as every year.

Three differences only, and all of them sealed:

In every Kept column, at one depth, a lamination of witness — thinner than the olive bands, permanent as they are. The basin has kept the power's signature the way it keeps everything, which means the power is now, in the smallest possible installment, a resident. It found this fitting. One should not bless a place without leaving a hostage.

In the green country, a story is three generations more durable than it should be, and walks around in the world looking for the person it belongs to.

And in the shelter between the four great columns, on the wall opposite the laminated smoke-stain, there is a new mark. The power made it on its way out — pressed one drop against the stone and let the basin glaze it, so that the mark is made of the valley's own bone and will only thicken with the years. It is small. The next Keeper will find it by candlelight, probably, some evening in the muffled quiet between the heartbeat and the slow interior drip, and will not know who made it, and will not need to. It says, in the simplest figure the power could devise — a single short line with a single short line answering it, call and response, *toom* and *tak*:

*You are heard. Keep them. You also will be kept.*

Then the power withdrew into the general roar of the world, which is loud without anyone shouting, and resumed listening — a little differently now, having learned from a wet gray valley the thing it came to teach and stayed to be taught:

that there are places where love runs on addition; that not everything still is dead, and not everything kept is captive; that the highest form of help is sometimes a bow, a drop, a swept floor, and a door left findable;

and that somewhere under everything, if you stand quietly with your hand against the stone, you can feel the low note being held — by the swimmers, by the chambers, by the three who became a lighthouse, and now, in one thin sealed layer, by something benevolent and wise that came, and understood, and chose to be a fingernail's width of the white land forever.
