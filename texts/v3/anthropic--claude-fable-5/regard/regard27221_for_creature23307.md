**The Witness of the Working Heath**

The power did not come up through cloud. Powers of that kind do not travel toward things; they have a kind of standing acquaintance with everything, thin and even, the way daylight is acquainted with every surface it falls on, and what changes is not position but attention. For a very long time the heath had been an entry in that acquaintance no brighter than any other — a green altitude, a closed circle of water and work, filed.

What brightened it was the traveler. Somewhere in the great even field of the power's regard, a small thing registered: a warm-blooded weight moving across the plateau for several days, compensated for, and then — this was the detail that drew the attention in — *revised against*. The heath had closed a route behind a guest. It was such a minute act, and so wholly in character, and so unlike anything else in the catalog of places, that the power gathered itself the way a person leans toward a sound, and looked.

It looked for what, by its own reckoning, was a long time. By the heath's reckoning it was a few dozen demolitions.

---

The habit of benevolence is to arrive with hands. The power's first act, performed before it was aware of performing it, was an inventory of needs — the reflexive opening of accounts that precedes every kindness. Water: closed loop, losses recovered. Heat: self-made, budgeted to the calorie, vented on schedule. Structure: in permanent revision, no repair outstanding, because nothing was ever permitted to stand long enough to be broken. Hunger: none. Disease: digested. Loneliness: no organ for it. Fear: no organ for it. Purpose —

The inventory stopped there, and the power held very still, because it understood it had nearly committed the only vandalism the place was vulnerable to.

The heath did not know what it was for. It had never known. There was no maker to have told it, no ruin under the green where the question might once have been asked, and in the absence of the question the heath had grown the most complete innocence the power had encountered anywhere: ten thousand years of labor without a single hour of justification. To answer the unasked question — to lay a purpose on the plateau, even a true one, even gently, even as blessing — would be to introduce the one parasite the digestion pits could not break down. The power, which had given meaning to many things, and watched many things bend under the gift, closed that account unopened.

After this it went through the other interventions in order, the way a careful visitor goes through a house deciding what not to touch.

It considered permanence. It was within the power's means to set the heath outside of weather, outside of the slow dissolution of the buried mountain, outside of endings generally — and it declined, almost with embarrassment, because the heath was the most articulate argument against permanence it had ever read. The place demolished a district of itself every day. Its whole character was the refusal to let anything, including itself, be exempt from revision. To make it eternal would have been to disagree with it.

It considered warmth — and here the power was, for a moment, genuinely tempted, because it had watched a family of grey riders evacuate a slackening cable in single file, the young carried by the scruff, calm and correct and very small in the cold, and everything in the power's nature leaned toward easing them by a degree. One degree. It worked the consequence forward and saw what a degree would do: thrift relaxing, lushness creeping in, the woody patience giving way to soft fast growth, the cables slackening into vines, the riders' miraculous feet illiterate within twenty generations because the heath would no longer be saying anything worth overhearing. The cold was not the place's hardship. The cold was its prose style. The power withdrew the degree.

It considered companionship — a second heath, seeded on some other altitude, so that this labor would not be the only one of its kind. It examined this wish honestly and found that the loneliness it proposed to cure was its own. Filed, closed.

Last, it considered the galleries. The smoothed lanes on the lacquered floors, glassy with habitual passage; the thing no rider would descend toward and no light had ever found. Looking, for such a power, is not even an act — it is the relaxing of a courtesy. The power did not relax it. It had been keeping accounts on the heath for some while by then, and it understood that a place run on total accounting is owed, by whoever audits it, one unaudited room. It left the entry blank, and the blank entry was the most respectful line in the ledger.

---

So: nothing. Almost nothing. Here is what the power did do.

First, it counted. This was not a small thing, though it changed nothing. The heath had been meeting its quota since before there was anyone to count, and the power simply became the one who counts: it took the whole sum into itself — every demolition, every venting, every gram of grit the dark birds had carried up out of the valleys across the centuries of that lineage, every closed circle closed again — and it entered the work as *received*. Witnessed labor and unwitnessed labor are physically identical and are not the same. The power knew the difference better than anything alive, and it gave the difference to the heath the only way it could be given: silently, comprehensively, and without a receipt.

Second, it went down into the valleys. The bird-line was the one true fragility in the whole arrangement — a supply chain one bird wide, no redundancy, maintained by a lineage that did not know it was a supply chain. The power did not touch the birds. It touched the world a half-step around them: a cliff face that would hold its ledges a few centuries longer than erosion intended, a valley winter that would, at intervals no one would ever find suspicious, break a week early. A thumb on the scale so light that no bird, no naturalist, no god of birds would ever detect it. The heath's only import was grit and salts; the power added a second import the heath would never be able to assay — a few grams of luck a generation, delivered by the same dark courier, who was paid, as always, in warmth.

Third — and this it did on the plateau itself, the only thing it did there — it let itself be weighed. It gathered a little of its presence into something with mass, and rested that mass on a single cable for the length of one slow hauling stroke. The tension shifted; somewhere in the distributed bookkeeping of the heath, an entry was made, compensated for. Then the power withdrew the weight, and the entry balanced, and the transaction closed in the same hour it opened. It was the only handshake the heath was capable of, and the power had wanted, before leaving, to have shaken its hand.

Then it stayed a little longer, not as a benefactor — there was nothing left to consider — but the way the bird stays: standing in the warm fall of vapor at a venting dome, feathers loosened, taking the heat. Of all the places in the power's acquaintance, this was the only one that had asked it for nothing, and the power found that it rested there, and was honest enough to call the resting what it was, and to know it had been paid for. The witness was the payment. The rest was the change.

---

It left no mark. That was deliberate; the absence of marks was part of the architecture, and the power's abstention is, in a sense, the largest structure on the plateau — invisible, and load-bearing.

But it made one appointment, and keeps it.

Someday — the power has seen the shape of it, far down the accounts — the climate will shift past the heath's tolerances, or the last knuckle of the buried mountain will dissolve, and the heath will run a final demolition with nothing scheduled after it. The power will not prevent this. It has agreed with the heath about endings. What it will do is be there: when the last dome vents and the vapor goes up soundless into the cold and does not fall back, something will be present that holds the entire sum — every revision, every recovered drop, the riders and their listening feet, the one-bird trade route, the blank entry it never opened — and the work will end *received* rather than merely stopped. The books will be closed in good order. Quota met, in full, witnessed.

The heath will never know any of this. It has no organ for knowing it.

That is most of what the knowing is for.
