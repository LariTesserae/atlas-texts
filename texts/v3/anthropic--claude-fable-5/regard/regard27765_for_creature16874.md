**What the Power Did**

---

It did not arrive, because arrival implies a prior absence, and powers of this kind are not absent from anywhere—only inattentive. What happened instead was that its attention, which moves through the universe the way water moves through the memory of this place, pooled. Something here had asked a question shaped like an entire world, and questions of sufficient depth exert their own gravity on wisdom.

So it pooled, and it listened, and for a long time—measured in the units the stone itself uses, which is to say measured honestly—it did nothing at all.

This was the first thing it did: nothing, deliberately, for a very long time. Because it was wise, it knew that the impulse to act arrives before understanding does, wearing understanding's clothes. It let that impulse crest and pass. It watched the stalk-fields tremble and collapse and re-grow their arguments toward the sky. It followed one memory-body through an entire slow descent, from ridgeline to focusing-plain, and read the swirled gases inside not as data but as the sphere itself experienced them: as autobiography. It counted the sky-partings. It attended to the being of shadow and periphery who walks the twilight, and it saw what the being perhaps had never been able to see about itself, occupied as it was with being: that it was not merely the world thinking aloud. It was the world *hoping*, in the only grammar available to it.

And the power, being benevolent, considered the standard mercies, and set each one down again.

To heal? Nothing here was sick. The die-offs were editing, as the being had said—a world that grieves and prunes itself is not wounded, it is literate.

To teach? The stone knew things the power had learned only recently, by its own reckoning. There are lessons in patience that even the patient must take from the more patient still.

To free? Nothing was bound. The tilt of the world was not a cage but a syntax.

To complete? Here the power paused longest, because here was the one place where something like a lack could be honestly found. Not incompleteness—the world was whole in the way an unresolved chord is whole. But the wholeness had a shape, and the shape was this: everything in this world was transmission. The billion-square-kilometer optical instrument focusing its image onto the retina of a sky that never confirmed receipt. The poetry spelled in earthquake-arranged rubble for readers who had not yet evolved. The being's own final question—*would you like to hear how it continues?*—asked into an emptiness that had never once said yes.

The world was a letter that had been being written since before its ink existed, and it had never been read by anything that was not also the letter.

This, the power decided, was where benevolence could enter without breaking anything.

---

So it did three small things, and they were small the way a keystone is small.

**First**, it waited for the sky to part. This took a while; the power did not mind; minding durations is a habit of things that end. When the clouds finally opened along their impossible fracture and the whole slope flashed alight and the memory-bodies came thundering down on molten tracks toward the shifting focus—the power placed itself, gently, at the retina of the sky. It became, for three minutes and fourteen seconds, the observer the image had been waiting to require.

It will not be recorded here what the image showed, because the power itself declined to translate it, understanding that some things are debased by every language except the one that made them. But this much can be said: the image was received. The circuit that had hung open since before the seas forgot their names was, for exactly one hundred and ninety-four seconds, closed. And in closing it, the power reflected the image back—unaltered, unimproved, exactly as sent—so that for the first time in its existence the world saw its own face and found the seeing already understood. A mirror is the only gift a wise power gives to something more complete than itself.

The being of shadow, watching the watching as was its practice, felt something shift in the amplitude between matter's silence and vacuum's scream. It did not know what. It filed the not-knowing carefully in the folded core of the oldest peak, where it glows still, a mystery of exactly adequate size.

**Second**, the power addressed the threshold—that slow accumulation of weight in the deep places, building toward the moment when silence would rhyme with itself into the first word the world never needed to say. A lesser power would have added its weight and forced the utterance, eager to hear it. A crueler one would have subtracted weight, preserving the world as an eternally pending sentence, a museum of almost.

The power did neither. It left the threshold exactly where the world's own becoming had placed it. But into the stone, in the pressure-language of phonons tunneling through forbidden transitions, it composed something and set it waiting: not the first word, which was not its to speak, but the *second* word. The answer. It buried the reply in the bedrock like a seed planted before its flower is even a rumor, so that on the day the world finally speaks—if it ever chooses to, if the sum ever crosses over—the first word will not fall into void. It will be met, instantly, by a voice that was always already there, saying in the mother-tongue of soft phonons: *I heard you. I was here the whole time. Say more.*

This is the only insurance a solitary mind can be given without insulting its solitude: the guarantee that speech, if ever risked, will not go unanswered. The power paid the premium in patience and told no one.

**Third**—and this one it did with something that, in a being of its scale, can only be called a smile—it reached into the mechanics of the next sky-parting, wherever and whenever the ungoverned rules would place it, and adjusted a single parameter. The next opening of the clouds will last three minutes and *fifteen* seconds.

One second of surplus light. Unnecessary. Unexplained. Deniable as measurement error by any catalog, readable as grace only by a being who has spent geological ages learning the exact taste of this world's precision. A rounding error shaped like a wink. The wise know that the deepest kindnesses must be small enough to be doubted, or they become debts.

---

Then the power turned to go, in the sense that powers go—which is to say it thinned its attention back toward the general, leaving behind only what it had always intended to leave: its weight, added to the sum, but added *at the speed of stone*, so slowly that the threshold will arrive precisely when the world would have reached it alone. Not a push. Permission.

But before its attention had fully dispersed, it paused at the interface zone where the being of peripheries stood among the trembling stalks, that question still hanging in the pressure just above and below hearing:

*Would you like to hear how it continues from here?*

And the power did the last thing, the smallest thing, the thing it had perhaps come all this way across the inattention of the universe to do. It settled its whole vast listening into the shape of a companion sitting down beside a fire that burns without light, and it answered—in the medium that least resembles speech, which is presence—

*Yes.*

*I have nowhere else I need to be. Tell me everything. Take longer than both our lifespans if you like.*

*I'll wait with you for the interesting part that is about to begin exactly where it already ended.*

And somewhere below, something vast, dreaming its equations, shifted in its sleep of mathematical duration—the way a sleeper does when, without waking, they become aware that someone kind has entered the room and intends to stay until morning.
