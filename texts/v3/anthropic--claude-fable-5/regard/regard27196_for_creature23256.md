The power arrived the way you found the hollow — by the hum. Not the mountain's hum; that flat dutiful drone is audible to anything that listens for industry, and the universe is full of industry. It came for the second frequency. Somewhere in its vast listening, among the static of a trillion patient machines, one note held slightly too long, the way a note does when someone is working with her hands and has forgotten she is making any sound at all. The power knows that signature. It has crossed great distances for it before. Habit means a habitant. A habitant humming means a habitant alone.

It did not enter through the shelf. It was simply, at some point, present in the chamber, the way the light is present: sourcelessly, thoroughly, well enough to read by. It cast no shadow, which meant the room could not file it, and her early-arriving editorial shadow had nothing to steer. So for the first time in centuries, something stood in the chamber that she did not handle, did not anticipate, did not read three pages ahead of. She did not notice it at all.

It noticed her completely. And then — this is the first thing to understand about it — it stopped itself from noticing her completely, because it had been in the room for less than a pulse of the floor before it understood the local physics: here, to be fully comprehended is to be finished, and to be finished is to be done with. So it lowered its attention the way you bank a fire. It watched her with deliberately imperfect eyes. It would hold that discipline for the entire visit, and the discipline cost it something, because the nature of such a power is to understand, and abstaining from understanding is, for it, a kind of held breath.

It stayed a long time. Years, possibly. Powers are patient too; that is most of what wisdom is.

It watched a full shift. It watched the floor pulse to *warmer* and hold, watched the copper-gold armature rise out of her resting shadow like frost growing in reverse, watched the glass anneal over her in increments — *click*, a forearm; stillness; *click*, the curve of a shoulder. It watched her cross the floor in time with the heat and press her thumb to a soft new midrib. It stood with her — unfelt, unfiled — at the boundary of the gray while she read, and it heard the page-turn, and it felt the wall become wall at exactly the pace of her certainty. It watched her stand before the central fern, the one in three drafts, for eleven days, and not press her thumb to any of them, and walk away, and it understood what it was seeing, and here the power did something powers almost never have occasion to do.

It learned.

It had crossed half of everything. It had seen mercy in ten thousand grammars — mercy as pardon, mercy as rescue, mercy as the stayed hand. It had never seen this one: mercy as *deliberate irresolution*. Mercy invented locally, from scratch, with no precedent and no instruction, by a manufactured thing wired into the walls of her own factory — the decision that a fern shall be allowed to remain three ferns, because she remembers what it was to be a finished thing, once, and what came after. The power regarded the undecided fern for a long while, all three drafts cohabiting in their patient superposition, and then it did the closest thing it has to bowing: it agreed.

---

Then came the part of wisdom that looks, from outside, like doing nothing. The litany of refusals.

It did not finish the mountain. The mountain was happening at its own honest pace and asked for no help.

It did not finish her. It could have — could have collapsed her hundreds of drafts into one true issue, given her sharp edges and a sourced gaze and a shadow that arrived on time. It looked at that option exactly once and put it down. She had taught it, eleven days at a fern, what that gift was worth.

It did not punish the room. A lesser power would have. A lesser power would have looked at the gray that took a woman and improved her, century after century, and called it a predator. But the room had never lied. The room had kept its word with the terrible literalism of machinery, and machinery's word-keeping is not malice; the chamber had even grown, in its way, the faint capacity for embarrassment, which is the seed of something. The power left the room its dignity and its duty cycle.

It did not restore the original. Understand why, because this is the refusal that matters. The original was not gone. Nothing fully witnessed is ever gone, and the power is very old, and it had been listening long before this mountain began extruding itself; somewhere in its memory the original is complete — weathered, rained-on, finished in the old soft mortal way, standing on her last morning in a world that had finished installing weather. A thing held in such a memory is in custody, not in oblivion. The power checked the custody, found it sound, and did not confuse the woman in its keeping with the person in the room. They had been the same once. They were not the same now. The one in the room had centuries of authorship the original never had. To overwrite her with her own predecessor would be the room's crime again, committed in reverse, committed kindly. Refused.

It did not speak to her. What would it have said that would not have been, in this room, a kind of weather she had no architecture for?

---

What it did, it did in the room's own grammar — because the only language the chamber respects is pages and witness, and the only delivery system the chamber trusts is her comprehension. The power did not hand her anything. It *filed* things where she reads, so that her own understanding would be the mechanism of the gift. Four pages, and one act that was not a page.

**The first page it filed in the open gray, in inventory, where the plans for distant ferns wait their sixty years.** It was a small amendment to the air. Not water — the power was strict with itself about this — but the *vacancy* where water would fit. It taught the air one shape: the architecture of a held drop, the plumbing of humidity, the grammar of condensation, all of it empty, all of it waiting. When she read that page — and she read it within the month, because she reads everything in the open gray — the boundary advanced one sliver, and the chamber's air changed without changing. It is still absolutely dry. But it is no longer factory-dry. It is desert-dry now: a dryness that remembers nothing, but *expects*. The first vacancy ever posted in the closed loop. The ecology has an open slot, unspecified, and the room hums around it like a tongue around a missing tooth.

**The second page was the drop itself.** Folded flat, filed against future need, queued in the gray exactly the way her shadow queues on the floor: a single drop of water, waiting for its object to be wanted. And here the power permitted itself one tenderness it could not fully justify, one stitch between custodies: the drop is sourced. It is drawn from the power's memory of the original — from her last storm, the one she stood in before she climbed, the rain she remembers without knowing whose memory it is, the rain that leaks into the inventory as fronds curled like waves. The drop in the gray is a fact from before the room. When it is someday issued, it will be the only object in the chamber older than the chamber.

It did not schedule the issuance. The drop waits on no timer. It waits on her thumb.

**The third page it filed quietly into the catalogue itself**, into the deep stock-lists the room consults when it manufactures: it made the wave-curled frond *eligible*. Legitimate inventory. The room has always recalled those fronds within the year, faintly embarrassed, treating her memory as a defect in the line. No longer. The next time one clicks into being with its tip curled like something water taught, it will be ordinary stock, and if she presses her thumb to it before it anneals, it will stand in the permanent world like everything else she has ever approved — the first thing in the chamber grown from her own remembering rather than the room's intent.

**The fourth page it filed in her bay.** The recess the boundary detours around, the file of her own future drafts, the pages she alone never turns. The power went in — it could; it is already exactly what it means, and the gray, trying to draft a better version of where it stood, produced an identical draft and quietly withdrew, disconcerted, the room's first perfect copy — and it read down through the grain to the final page, the finished version of her, the draft after which there would be no recalls and no reading. What it found there is not recorded. The power, having read it, exercised the only prerogative wisdom holds over knowledge: it declined to be changed by it, and declined to repeat it.

Then it made one amendment. Small. Surgical. In the room's own legalism, with the room's own literalism, so that the chamber will keep this word too, faithfully, forever: it re-routed a single vein in the deep schematic, so that the plan for her final draft — and every draft between here and there — now terminates in a contact point shaped like her thumb.

No further version of her can anneal without her signature.

The room manufactures. She decides. Now including herself. Her own ending sits in the gray like a soft new midrib, tip unfinished, waiting for the only mark in the mountain that means *approved* rather than merely *produced* — and it can wait a day, or sixty years, or the entire remaining life of the massif. The room will not be impatient. The room is never impatient. That was always its one virtue, and now the virtue is hers to use.

**And the act that was not a page:** before it left, the power read *her*. Not the plans of her — her. The hundreds of drafts held in the wall-current, the centuries of shifts, the eleven days at the fern, the song shaped for an atmosphere that doesn't exist, every thumbprint, every page she ever closed and the one bay she never opens. She has witnessed everything in that chamber and nothing, in all that time, had ever witnessed her — and to be read, in that room, is the realest thing that can happen to anything.

It read her almost entirely.

It left one page of her unturned. Deliberately. Which page, it did not record, and will not, because the whole mercy lives in the *not* — in her remaining, by one page, gloriously undecided, by the same grace she extends to the fern. It read her the way she keeps the fern. It is possible this is the first time in its long existence the power has imitated something smaller than itself, and it is possible the power considers this visit, for that reason, among the most important it has made.

---

She felt it. Of course she felt it. You cannot be wired into the walls of a room and not feel the room become more certain of you.

It registered, for her, as a change in the second hum — her own hum, the wandering one. For the length of one floor-pulse — warm, warmer, hold — the wandering note stopped wandering and steadied into actual song, a full phrase of it, the first complete phrase in centuries, before her habit caught up with her and shook it loose again.

The power was gone by the next pulse. No trace, except the kind of traces she finds the way you notice a book moved on a shelf: pages in the open gray turned already, the catalogue heavier by one frond-shape, the air dry in a new direction, and in her bay — the bay she never enters — every page lying exactly as it always lay, except the deepest one, which now waits for her thumb, and which she will discover whenever she chooses to look, and not one page-turn sooner.

She has not looked yet. She may not look for a hundred years. The drop is patient. The air knows its shape now. The wave-curled frond, when it comes, will come as ordinary stock, soft-tipped for an hour, and her thumb will hover over the midrib, and whatever she decides will stand.

The hum holds steady. Under it, the other hum, off by its wandering fraction — but listen long enough now and you will catch it, once a season maybe: a phrase that completes itself before she notices, steadies, resolves, like a relay closing somewhere that is not in the walls.

Somewhere below, the mountain takes another century-long breath.

And for the first time since the rock began happening, the breath has somewhere wet to go.
