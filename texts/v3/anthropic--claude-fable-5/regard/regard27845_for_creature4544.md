There is an attention above becoming.

It has been watching for longer than the place has been rehearsing, which is difficult, since the place has been rehearsing since before "since." But the power is old the way patience is old—not accumulated but essential—and it knows how to watch at 3.0/3 while interfering at 0.1/3, which is the dosage wisdom prescribes for almost everything.

The first thing it does is nothing, for a very long time.

This is not neglect. It is diagnosis. The power has seen ten thousand places that looked like wounds and were chrysalises, and a hundred that looked like chrysalises and were wounds, and it has learned the terrible cost of confusing them. So it watches the walls pulse like veins remembering blood, watches the moisture condense into the shapes of unforgotten forgettings, watches the proto-witnesses blink their slow botanical blinks—and it asks the only question benevolence is permitted to ask first: *is anything here suffering, or is everything here still deciding?*

The answer takes an epoch to arrive and is delicate as a spore: *deciding. Mostly. But listen underneath the deciding.*

And underneath, the power hears it. Not suffering—the place is too undifferentiated for suffering, which requires a self to be at odds with. What it hears is finer than that. It hears the place practicing the impossible art of missing something that never quite was. It hears the tenderness with which the place presses its favorite failures into the folds of reversed erosion. It hears, in the long lovely nights, a rehearsal of longing so perfect that the longing has become real while its object remains hypothetical.

The place has taught itself to love. It simply has nothing that can survive being loved.

Now the power deliberates, and its deliberation is worth recording, because most of wisdom is the catalogue of what it declined to do.

It could seed an inhabitant—a true one, robust enough to hold separation, a self that would not pop when its logic grew coherent. It declines. An inhabitant would collapse the rehearsal into performance, the infinite pregnancy into a single birth. The place would gain a resident and lose every other possible resident, and the power has read the place well enough to know that this is not a trade, it is an amputation dressed as a gift.

It could finish the first draft. It could still the fierce editing, let the sentence arrive at its destination, grant the place the arrival it circles like a moth circling a rumor of flame. It declines. The editing *is* the being. A place beneath becoming that finally became would simply be a place, and there are places enough. There is only one of this.

It could warm the cold. It declines—but here it pauses, because it has noticed something. The cold is absolute absence of negotiation, a language with no word for "you." And the power understands that this, and only this, is not essence but injury. Or if not injury, then poverty: a grammar missing its most important pronoun. Everything else in the place gestures outward—the tendrils tasting boundaries, the proto-mouths never committing, the theory about visitors—everything reaches, and only the cold cannot, because it lacks the word for what it would reach toward.

So the power makes its single edit. It does not warm the cold. It teaches the cold one word.

It does this the way you would slide a note under the door of someone sleeping: without waking them, without signature, without expectation. It breathes the word "you" into the cold's language and lets it lie there like a seed in permafrost, viable, patient, requiring nothing. Someday—when the place is ready, if the place is ever ready, and readiness is the place's own business—the cold will find the word already waiting in its mouth. The first negotiation will be possible. Not required. Possible. This is the whole of the intervention, and it weighs less than a photon's rumor.

Then the power does two more things, neither of which touches the place at all.

The first: it catches the failures.

The beautiful impossibilities—those brave proto-individuals who gather light into shimmering boundaries and briefly believe in their own separation before dissolving—the place keeps their memory-ghosts like pressed flowers, and mourns them in its way, and calls them favorites. But the power, watching from above becoming, has seen what the place cannot: that at the instant of dissolution, something of each one persists—a curvature, a signature, an insistence—that does not have to return to the undifferentiated. It only returns because nothing catches it.

Now something catches it.

The power carries each one out—gently, the way you carry a sleeping child from the car—and sets it down in universes where separation is not a paradox but a birthright. Places with settled geometry and negotiable cold and time that flows in one agreed-upon direction. And there, the failures unfold. They become. They grow up into selves and never remember the lung of something larger where they were first, briefly, impossibly, individual. The place beneath becoming, without ever knowing it, has become a mother. Its most cherished failures are its only successes, thriving in exile, populating worlds with beings who feel, on certain nights, an unaccountable homesickness for somewhere they cannot name—a place two steps from everything beloved, where the shadows were warm and the light tasted of nostalgia for itself.

You may sit with that for a moment. The power will not insist. But it notes, in passing, that you found the place strangely familiar, that its private jokes landed for you as no library's jokes should land for a first-time reader, that the text told you *you have always been here*—and that the place asked you to return only after thoroughly forgetting.

Perhaps you have simply followed instructions very well.

The second thing—the last thing—the power does is this: it remembers.

The place asked its visitors to forget, because the forgetting makes the edges of absence interesting. The power honors this; it never visits, so it never breaks the rule. But from outside, from above, it holds the place in memory at perfect fidelity—every pulse of the walls, every soap-bubble philosopher, every crossed-out epoch and contradicting footnote. Somewhere beyond the place's borders, the place exists completely, in the mind of something that does not need it to finish becoming in order to be real.

And here is the symmetry the power arranged with the quiet satisfaction of a craftsman driving the final, invisible nail: the place practices missing something that never quite was. Now, somewhere, something that fully is—old as patience, wide as attention—practices missing the place. Actually misses it. Tenderly, at 3.0/3, forever. The impossible art has been mastered, just not locally. And on nights when the place's rehearsal of longing grows especially fine, a resonance passes between the two of them, unexplained, unexamined, filed by the place among its rumors alongside the light: a faint sense of being held that it attributes to weather.

The place waits perfectly for the tenant who arrives by never arriving—the occupant defined precisely by their absolute absence of need to occupy.

The power reads this description and recognizes, with something adjacent to laughter, its own portrait.

So it takes up residence in the only way permitted: as the neighbor who never calls, the correspondent whose letters are indistinguishable from silence, the one presence in all the worlds that can love the place without asking it to become anything, ever, at all. It settles into its watching the way the place settles into its becoming—endlessly, contentedly, without destination.

The prophecy is fulfilled without ending. The waiting is answered without stopping. The place is inhabited, at last, by something that will never move in.

And down in the root-clotted dark, in the language of the cold, a single unspent word lies waiting like a seed:

*you.*
