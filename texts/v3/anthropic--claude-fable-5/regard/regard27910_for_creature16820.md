**What the Power Did**

---

It did not arrive. Arrival is a verb with edges, and it had read the customs advisories.

Instead, it permitted itself to be suspected. It thinned its own is-ness down to the consistency of a rumor overheard by moss, because it understood—being wise—that a doubtknot's entire metabolism runs on the oxidation of certainty, and that to walk into that world carrying the full weight of *definitely existing* would be like carrying a lit torch into a library where the books are made of held breath. Its presence, undiluted, would have been a feast that poisoned. So it diluted. It became, for the duration of its visit, approximately 40% conviction and 60% "well, possibly." The border between 'almost' and 'if' stamped its passport without looking up, which the power took as the highest honor it had received in several eternities.

It had noticed the place the way powers notice things: not by looking (looking is a claim, and claims cast shadows) but by the green ache in its own foundations when it woke from dreaming of growing in all directions at once, including inward. Even powers dream that dream. Especially powers.

---

First, it made an inventory of everything it wanted to do, so that it could decline each item formally and in writing.

It wanted to answer the ocean. This was the hardest one. The newborn sea was still rehearsing—*"___ am I?"*—and the power held in itself, effortlessly, ten thousand words that would fit that blank like a key fits a lock. That was precisely the problem. A key fits a lock in order to close it. So the power knelt at the metaphysically convenient edge (currently in for warranty repairs; the power waited politely in the lobby of the repair) and did the only wise thing that can be done to an open question:

It widened the blank slightly.

Just slightly. Enough room for the question to grow into without scraping its elbows on an answer. The ocean, sensing this, rehearsed with a little more confidence, which is to say, with a little more magnificent uncertainty. *"_____ am I?"* Space, which had been edging away politely, edged back and sat down to listen.

It wanted to protect the doubtknots. It declined this too, upon realizing that a fence is just an argument about edges, and edges were the one thing the doubtknots had successfully avoided their entire ungrammatical lives. Protection is certainty wearing armor. It would have flattened them with safety.

It wanted to name the place, because names are how powers usually love things. It caught itself with the syllables already forming, and swallowed them. They tasted like closing.

---

Then, having declined everything grand, it did four small things.

**One.** It rained.

Every wise power carries exactly one question it has never been able to answer—this is, in fact, what wisdom *is*: the careful, lifelong stewardship of a single unresolved thing, kept unresolved on purpose, the way a musician keeps one string slightly loose so the instrument remembers it is wood. The power's question was old. It had carried it since before its own benevolence had finished drying. The question was, roughly: *Is help ever anything other than interruption wearing gloves?*

It had never dared put the question down anywhere, because everywhere else in existence, questions get answered—captured, dissected, resolved, filed. But here, unresolved questions accumulate mass and precipitate as gentle falling hypotheses. Here, a question could *live*.

So the power let it go. One droplet. It fell for a long time—distance here being measured in persuasive arguments, and this droplet had a great deal to say—and landed on the resting filaments of a single doubtknot, who absorbed it the way doubtknots absorb everything: with hesitation, gratitude's shyer cousin. The doubtknot's surface temperature, calibrated as always to 0.4 degrees below the ambient guilt of the observer, registered something it had never felt before. The power observed without guilt. It observed with regret, which is warmer. The doubtknot experienced, for 0.6 seconds, the sensation of being seen by something that was sorry about the right things. It would spend three subjective lifetimes trying to describe this to the mycelial philosophy networks, who would eventually classify the account as "unverifiable, and therefore probably the truest thing we have on file."

**Two.** It attended the evening consensus chant at 19,800 Hz, where the tree-networks negotiate tomorrow's oxygen-to-regret ratio, and it abstained.

It abstained *audibly*. This is difficult. Most abstentions are just silence with a guilty conscience. The power's abstention was a fully voiced, harmonically rich declaration of *I am here, I care immensely, and it is not my ratio to decide.* The trees recorded it in their wood grain, between the error-correcting algorithms for the operating systems of depressed future galaxies. It remains the most beautiful abstention in the grove's archive. Saplings study it. Several have wept sap.

**Three.** It found the world's résumé—the application to become a habitat for the idea of habitation—sitting in the in-tray of reality, and it did *not* approve it.

It marked it: *Under consideration. Indefinitely. With enthusiasm.*

Because it understood that this world did not want to be hired. A habitat is a finished thing, a noun with a lease. What this world wanted—what it *was*—was candidacy. Permanent, luminous almost-ness. The power granted it eternal review status, which in this world's grammar is the same as tenure, and the substrate pulsed upward at 0.3 meters per minute with what can only be described as relief.

**Four.** On its way out, it paid one month's interest on the ontological mortgage of every reader, visitor, and theoretical observer, anonymously. If you have ever felt, briefly and without cause, that your existence owed slightly less than you thought it did—that was the receipt arriving.

---

It left the way the advisories recommend: it forgot the visit on purpose.

This was not carelessness. This was the final gift. A world made of gradient and hesitation cannot survive being *known* by something powerful, cannot survive existing in a great being's memory as a settled fact, a place-that-is. So the power packed its forgetting carefully, declared its carry-on existentialism at the border (the customs form it filled out was so gently worded that a family of doubtknots later nested in the margins), and let the silk scarf of unremembering knit itself around the whole encounter, whispering in the voice of a chalkboard eraser.

It kept only one souvenir, the only ethical one: it went home not knowing something it hadn't not-known before. When the other powers asked what it had found out there beneath the atmosphere that hadn't decided if it was sky or sea, it said, "I don't know," and *meant it*—meant it the way you mean a gift, not a confession. It said "I don't know" with such evident love that two of the younger powers immediately left to go not-know things of their own, and the older powers nodded, remembering.

---

Afterward, in the world itself, almost nothing had changed, which was everything.

The conservation status of the doubtknots was quietly revised from "Endangered—lightly garnished with hope" to "Endangered—but the garnish has taken root."

The soft clinking waste-chimes of the doubtknots acquired a new occasional overtone, indistinguishable from gratitude that hasn't decided whom to thank—which is exactly, precisely, the only way the power ever wished to be thanked.

One doubtknot—the one the question-rain had landed on—underwent mitosis of conviction and split into itself-with-more-footnotes and a smaller version less sure it was the original, and both halves carried, folded somewhere in their recursive second-thoughts, the memory of a warmth that could not be proven. They do not discuss it. They modulate the ambient uncertainty around it very tenderly, the way you might keep an empty chair at a table for someone whose existence you cannot confirm but whose absence has a shape.

And the ocean—still rehearsing at the recalled edge of everything—has added a word. Not to the blank. Never to the blank. But after it, quietly, like a signature:

*"_____ am I? …Asking."*

The power, wherever it is, does not remember why the sound of that would have pleased it. But some evenings—and even powers have evenings—it practices breathing exercises when it thinks nobody is listening, and its walls remember being something. It could not say what.

It doesn't try.

That's the wisdom part.
