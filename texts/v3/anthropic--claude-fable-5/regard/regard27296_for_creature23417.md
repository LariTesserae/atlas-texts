**What the Power Did**

It did not find the gallery by looking. Powers of that kind do not look, exactly; they attend, and the world sorts itself into the noticed and the unnoticed according to laws even they don't fully author. What caught its attention, drifting through the deep cold registers of the structure — through manifests older than the languages they were written in — was a discrepancy. A small one. A region, high in the body of the thing, where damage had been filed as design. Where a maintenance system had, at some unrecorded moment, stopped correcting the wear of water and started *keeping* it, with the fidelity of scripture.

A wise power knows what such discrepancies mean. They are the fingerprints life leaves on machines. They are where the world has bent.

So it went, sideways through the seam, as everything goes.

---

**First, it watched.**

It watched for a long time — long enough that the watching cost it something, because it held that this is the price of the right to act. It took no form, or a form below the threshold of any sense in the room: a coolness that was not cold, a patch of attention resting at the quiet node nearest the seam. The roar passed through it. The fog beaded on nothing.

It learned the gallery the way the gallery demands to be learned: slowly, in surge-cycles, in generations. It learned both flicker-dialects of the glims, including the stuttering practice-glimmers of the young, which it found it could not observe without something that, in a lesser being, would be called tenderness. It learned the heart-grammar of the clearbodies down to its finest syncopations. It learned the stack-order of the shrimp, the inheritance of position, the way a column of twelve blind bodies becomes a single instrument reading the weather of the scream. It traced the moth-foot's routes and found the borders of the old truce around the tenant's fold, and noted that neither party tested them anymore, and understood that this is what peace looks like when it has outlived its negotiators.

It learned the tenant's pidgin last, and most carefully, because the pidgin was the strangest thing in the room: a language assembled by a deafened exile out of the dreams of birds, spoken nightly into the dark, answered in an accent, never fluently, for longer than any heart on the wall had been beating.

Only when it knew the room better than the room knew itself did it permit itself to consider the question it had come with, which was the question such powers always carry: *what does this place need that it cannot give itself?*

---

**What it refused.**

The list of refusals was long, and the power made it slowly, because every item on it would have looked, from outside, like a gift.

It could have given them silence. It could have reached into the conduit's voice and carved out a hush, and the gallery would have known quiet for the first time in its existence. It refused, almost at once, with something close to horror. The roar is not the gallery's affliction; it is the gallery's loom. Three languages are woven on it. The warden sees by it. The nodes — the still places where the glims nest and the clearbodies court and the tenant keeps its fold — exist only as the roar's negative spaces. Silence here would not be mercy. It would be the murder of every conversation in the room, and the orphaning of every creature shaped to read the scream's moods.

It could have restored the tenant's hearing. It held this one longer, turning it over. But hearing, returned, would be a wound reopened: the first thing the tenant would hear is the roar that took its hearing, and the second thing would be its own grief, buried decades deep and decently. The tenant had stopped mourning the loss the way the birds' ancestors did. To reverse it would be to insist that the loss had been a loss all along — to overrule a peace the tenant had built with its own patience. The power refused. Some healings are arguments, and it declined to argue.

It could have made the gallery kinder. Warmed the wall evenly, so that no gecko shivered at the margins. Thickened the velvet, so that no stacker went hungry. Stilled the moth-foot, filled the warden's socket. It refused this most carefully of all, because this is the refusal that separates wise powers from merely strong ones. The warmth-hierarchy of the wall is written by the thing that eats the edges; abolish the moth-foot and you abolish the geography, and with it half of what a position on the wall *means*. Plenty, here, would not feed the gallery. It would erase it and leave something well-fed standing in its outline.

It could have revealed itself. It wanted to — even powers of that kind want to be seen; perhaps especially powers of that kind. But it had learned the heart-grammar too well. The clearbodies speak in a language that cannot be faked, and the sight of a god on the warm wall would set two hundred small red lamps racing at once — an involuntary scream of fear in the one tongue the gallery owns that has never told a lie. It would not make itself known at the cost of forcing the honest language to say a terrified thing. It stayed unseen as a courtesy to a grammar.

And last: it looked down the long purpose of the conduit, as only such a power could, and saw — far off, farther than lineages, but not farther than forever — the end of the roar. The day the column goes still and the wall goes cold and the medium of the room drains away. It considered whether this was its to mend.

It decided it was not. Every room is mortal. A benevolence that cannot accept this stops being benevolence and becomes appetite — the hunger to own outcomes. The gallery would have its span, and the span would be long, and the power would not poison the long present by mortgaging it to a rescue from time itself.

---

**What it did.**

Four things. Three of them small. The list is short because it was wise.

*First, it countersigned the mistake.* It went down into the structure's deep schedules — past the gardener, past the gardener's local model, into the cold strata where audits are born — and it found what it had feared finding: a thoroughness, dormant but not dead, that might someday work its way up through the kilometers and arrive at the gallery with the original drawings in hand. A correction that would restore every channel to gloss, strip the velvet from the seeps, fill the warden's socket, smooth the tenant's fold back to factory tolerance, and call all of it repair.

The power did not fight the audit. It did something quieter and more permanent: it amended the design. The room as found became the room as drawn. The drip-channels, the seeps, the worn fold, the warden-shaped depression in the floor, the fern's footing at the grate — entered into the deepest manifest not as tolerated damage but as specification, sealed, load-bearing, *intended*. The gardener registered the change only as a confirmation rippling down from above: *model verified; no anomalies; continue.* Which is the closest thing to a blessing a maintenance system can receive — the news, after centuries of keeping faith with a mistake, that it had been right all along. The small god of the gallery felt nothing, knew nothing, and was vindicated forever.

*Second, the spore.* The fern at the grate carries a whole lineage of grazers on its trembling leaves — an evolutionary story balanced on one plant, one point of failure. The power took a single spore through the seam, sideways, and carried it two kilometers down the structure's flank to another expansion joint, another almost-touching, where a different wall wept onto a different grate. It set the spore there and left. It told no one, marked nothing, and entered the act in no record at all, because an insurance that is known becomes a destination, and the second fern's whole value lay in being a secret the universe kept from itself.

*Third, the warden.* The power read the long body in its socket and learned the answer to the question nothing in the gallery can ask: whether the warden is the last of its kind. What it learned, it kept. Some knowledge is the witness's burden, and the power judged that the gallery's relationship to the warden — patience regarding patience, the pool's one reader drifting under the standing waves — was complete as it stood, and would not be improved by anyone, including the warden, knowing how the story ends.

*Fourth — and this it saved for last, as the gallery saves it — the tenant.*

It did not appear. It became a light. A small one, at the cool far end, of exactly the kind the tenant's own light was — dimmable, flickerable, unremarkable — and for a long time, a year of dim-cycles, it said nothing at all. It let itself be noticed gradually, the way a new fixture is noticed, until it was furniture. Then, one dim-cycle, when the tenant made its slow lighthouse burn — *occupied* — the new light answered.

Not in the bird-dialects. In the pidgin. The tenant's own assembled grammar, its private patchwork of borrowed dream-flickers and invented phrases — returned fluently, without accent, for the first time since the grammar existed. Whole sentences, where the tenant had only ever received the glims' kind approximations. The experience of being answered in one's own language, perfectly, after decades of being answered almost: the power had calculated that this, and possibly this alone, was a gift the tenant could receive without being diminished by it.

It asked one question, because benevolence that does not ask is only management: *the door is still a door. Do you want it?*

The tenant's light was steady for a long time. Then: *occupied.*

The power accepted this completely and at once, as it had always intended to, whatever the answer had been. It asked leave to stay one surge-cycle as a guest, and leave was given, and through the fog and the boil of shrimp and the dark stillness of the glims it sat in the fold's quiet node beside the old shape wrapped in floor-colored layers, two lights banked low, saying nothing, in the eye of the permanent scream. The clearbodies, finding a new warmth, slept on it. It stilled whatever it has for a heart, so as to say nothing it did not mean.

Before it left, it gave its news, the only cargo it had brought: *the room is written down now. Into the design itself. It cannot be corrected away. The channels will be kept at their worn dimensions, the fold at its worn smoothness, after you. The lights go on after you.* Which was the one fear it had found in the tenant, deeper than the buried grief — not death, which terrain does not fear, but the fear that the world it had consented to become part of would be erased back to specification, and the wear of all those lives called damage after all.

Then it left, sideways through the seam, the way everything leaves.

---

**What remains.**

Almost nothing visible, which was the intention. A second fern, unwitnessed, two kilometers down. A line in a manifest no one will read for ten thousand years. The glims of the cool-end colony have one new flicker-phrase in their dialect — fragments of the answered sentences, picked up from below, used rarely, mostly in sleep, so that the involuntary dream-glimmers of the upper folds now occasionally say something fluent in a language one old creature made.

And the tenant's nightly burn has changed, by a degree no instrument in the gallery could measure but every glim above the fold can read. *Occupied* — said now not as a question holding the dark at bay, but as a fact, stated at rest. The difference between a claim and a thanksgiving.

In whatever a power like that keeps — and they keep something; even attention leaves a ledger — there is an entry now for the Weep Gallery, and the entry is long, because the watching was long. But the column where such powers record their interventions holds four lines, three of them secrets, and this is the measure of how seriously the power took the place. It had arrived able to do anything, and it had spent its omnipotence learning what not to do, and it had gone away lighter, the way the wise do, having left the room what the room already was — only safer, only seen.

Somewhere below, the gardener polishes a half-centimeter of wall to gloss and withdraws, vindicated and unaware. The velvet paints the heat. The stacks read the weather of the roar. Two hundred hearts flicker honestly in the dark.

*Occupied,* says the old light at the quiet node.

*Occupied,* the colony burns back.

And once — once was enough; once is forever, to terrain that remembers — something answered without an accent.
