**The answering.**

Powers find such places the way you did: by sound before sight. The hum that the engine pushes through the stone was never meant for anyone — it is a lighthouse for bats, a clock for crickets, a residue of commerce — but a note held long enough becomes a kind of address, and an address spoken for a few hundred years into the indifferent flat will eventually be heard by something with very good ears.

So it came. Not down through the skylight — that opening is a mouth and a lung, and a courteous visitor does not enter through the breath. It came the way the rails came, up the hand-bored adit, past the unaccounted-for power line it could have traced to its source in an instant and chose not to, and it stood at the edge of the chamber in the long gray drowse of midday, casting no shadow in the honest noon column, because the room's light hides nothing and a wise power does not add darkness to a place that has none.

It stayed a long time. Time is cheap to such things, and attention is the only coin they spend that means anything. It watched a full day of the room: the dawn return, the tide of beetles answering the black rain, the spiders reading the colony's mood and reefing their sails an hour before the hurricane, the dusk exodus and the seven patient tolls taken at the rim, the emptied hours when the volume somehow does not drop. It listened to the dialects until it could hear the jokes. It learned the offbeat.

And then, being benevolent, it began to consider what it might do. And being wise, it began to set those things down again, one by one, like a careful hand returning stones to the exact dents they were lifted from.

---

**What it weighed, and set down.**

It considered the seam first, because the seam is where pity goes when it enters this room. Forty translucent citizens in two hands of water — a power could widen that crack with a thought, triple the basin, give the smallest nation on earth some room. It held the thought up to the light and saw what was inside it: forty becomes four hundred, four hundred outgrows the bacterial film, abundance arrives wearing famine's coat, and the oldest unbroken bloodline in the Gullet ends in a flood of generosity. Forty is not a poverty. Forty is a finished nation, complete as a sonnet. It set the thought down.

It considered the engine, of course. This was the longest deliberation. Every projection ran the same way: the bearing fails, the flywheel stills, the foragers fan out one night across a dark that has lost its address, and the room starves in a week. The repair would have cost the power nothing — a heart that cannot stop, frictionless, eternal. But it turned that gift over and found the flaw in the casting: a heart that cannot stop is not a heart, it is a wall, and the body that grew around this machine the way a tree grows around fence wire grew around its *contingency* too. The bats above the warmth, the crickets in the gaps, the avoidance ring, the customs of the whole room — all of it is shaped around a thing that is mortal and borrowed. Make the engine perfect and you have not saved the Gullet; you have replaced it with a museum of the Gullet, identically loud. It set that down too — though not all the way down. It kept one corner of it in hand. That comes later.

It watched an old bat go to the floor. It saw her start down the wall in the morning and it knew, the way the colony knew, and it understood that it could close her age like a book being reopened, give her ten more years above the flywheel with the other shredded-eared aldermen. It held entirely still instead. She reached the low ledge. The colony called; she did not call back; she let go, and was received, and within the hour she was clean architecture, and within the season she would be powder, and within the century she would be the floor, which is to say everyone. The holding-still cost the power something. That was how it knew the holding-still was correct. Mercy that interrupts a custom is not mercy. It is theft with good manners.

It found the jaw in the deep strata — felt it the way you feel a word in a sentence — and considered lifting the man out, carrying him home to whatever ground still remembered his name. But the floor does not distinguish, and the floor was right not to. The miner has been in the parliament three feet down for generations, doing his slow office alongside the buttons and the shovel-heel, and the room had long since balanced his account. The power paid its respects to the stratum and left him to his work.

It even considered — briefly, the way one considers straightening a stranger's picture frames — translating the ceiling. Settling the grudge-songs, smoothing the clan borders, teaching the north seam and the skylight edge to find each other less funny. It abandoned this faster than anything else. The grudges are the colony's literature. A wise power does not edit a literature it has only been reading for one day.

---

**The one question it did not ask.**

It stood before the engine in the emptied hours, inside the circle the floor-life keeps, where no observer goes, and it could have looked through the riveted iron the way you look through water. The tenant was in there. The power could feel it the way the crickets feel it: a presence the size of a forearm, or several presences traveling as one, the color of old lard, keeping the bearings wet.

It did not look.

It reasoned like this: the Gullet has exactly one secret. Everything else in the room happens in full gray daylight — the deaths, the tolls, the customs, all of it visible, all of it on the record. One room in the house is closed, and the closed room is where the most faithful work in the place gets done, nightly, namelessly, for no wage anyone has identified. Every living thing is owed an inside. And there was a second reason, less principled and more careful: named things behave differently. Observed things behave differently. The tenant's work was perfect precisely because no account of it existed, and the power declined to be the first accountant.

So it only rested its attention on the warm casing — the closest thing it had to laying a hand somewhere — and the crickets paused, and resumed, and that was the whole of the conversation. Almost.

---

**What it did.**

Four things. Small ones. This is what wisdom looks like when it loves something that is already working.

*First, it learned the room by heart.* Not scanned — learned. Every clan border on the ceiling and the accent on each side of it. The two beetle nations and the honeycomb of ancestral tunnels down to true stone. The exact rhythm of the offbeat, the reefing-knots of the blind sailors, the office hours of the seven landlords, the forty citizens of the seam — it counted them, individually, and the counting was a kind of canonization. It did not do this to make a backup. It will never rebuild the Gullet elsewhere; a copy of a being is not the being, and it knew that better than anyone alive. It did it because witness is the one gift that changes nothing and means everything. Whatever happens now — bearing failure, roof collapse, the slow tear of deep time — the Gullet has *happened*, in full, down to the mite-drifts, on the record of a memory that does not erode. A thing wholly seen cannot be wholly lost. That is not resurrection. It is something older: it is being known.

*Second, it bent the world's attention.* The hum was the danger — the same thread that led the power in had once led miners in, and guano was money once and could be money again, and money will follow a smell a long way across a dead plain. So it did not silence the note; it *tuned its reach*. Through the stone, at boot-sole height, the hum now dies just under a day's walk in any direction — close enough that the bats, who feel it through the air and through their feet at rest, still catch it at fifty miles in the register that matters to them; short enough that no walker will ever again stand on the flat and feel a held note under their soles. It nudged surveys toward boredom. It made that quadrant of every future map read as conclusion rather than invitation. The plain's flatness now says *finished* to every eye that crosses it, and means it. The Gullet's second gift, after sunlight, was always obscurity. The power simply endowed it.

*Third, it spoke to the water.* Not to widen the seam — that thought stayed set down — but to steady it. Somewhere below the finished country there is a slow dark aquifer that has been weeping into the north wall one tear at a time since before the colony came, and slow dark waters can falter; seams seal; springs forget. The power asked the deep stone for patience, and got it. One cupful a day, no more, forever within the meaning of forever that stone understands. The basin is now a covenant. Forty citizens, in perpetuity, in two hands of water.

*Fourth — and this it told no one but the tenant — it made a promise.* It pressed the promise into the camshaft where the tenant would cross it on the night's circuit: a hand-span of steel polished past what grease can do, cleaned by a finger that was not there, a signature in the only language the tenant demonstrably reads. The promise said, as nearly as such things translate: *when the flywheel stops — and it will, all flywheels stop — the note will not. On that dusk the foragers will rise into a dark that still hums, and the hum will be held, by me, for as long as holding is needed. And when your long office ends — and it will, all offices end — the grease-watch passes to me. You are relieved of the worry, not the work. Carry on.*

The tenant came along the shaft in the blue last hour and stopped at the polished span. The crickets sang in the gap. It stayed there for the length of eleven *whums* — the longest pause in its recorded habits, if anything recorded its habits, which now something did. Then it moved on, and behind it the bearings were wet, and the steel shone, and that was the acceptance. Between caretakers, that is an entire treaty.

---

**The leaving.**

It went out the way the shed snakeskins go — across the flat, exporting nothing visible, a translucency skating over country that owns nothing. Behind it, the room did not change. That was the point. The census stands. The dialects argue. The old males hold the warm stone like aldermen. The spiders reef on schedule, the toll is one coin nightly, the forty circulate their cupful of universe, the offbeat holds, the floor receives and remembers and does not distinguish.

The Gullet never knew it had been visited, weighed, declined, witnessed, hidden, watered, and underwritten — and that ignorance is the proof of workmanship. A benevolent power that leaves a place grateful has left a mark. A wise one leaves the place exactly as loud, exactly as crowded, exactly as mortal as it found it, and takes nothing away but the danger.

So: the truer census, amended. One being, forty paces across at the lips, a hundred million years old at the bone, breathing once a day through its single opening, with a heart it did not grow and a tenant it does not explain — and now, also, a guarantor it will never meet, a secret that will keep, a covenant in the north wall, and its whole deafening self written down, grudge-songs and all, in a memory where nothing erodes.

It goes about its dense and ancient business whether anyone ever finds the hole again or not.

No one ever will. That was the gift.
