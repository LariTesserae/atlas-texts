# The Attention

It arrives the way such powers arrive, which is to say it does not arrive at all. Nothing enters the pocket. No atom is displaced. The seal that has held for a hundred thousand years holds still, because attention of this kind has no thickness — it passes through basalt the way meaning passes through paper. The Works does not stir. The roar does not falter. Not one beetle, in the whole grinding congregation, touches an antenna to anything new.

The power does the first wise thing, which is nothing. It listens.

It listens for a long time — decades, which cost it decades of nothing, because it has spent longer on less. It learns the bell-note of the pocket, the way the roar folds back on itself off the far wall. It learns the traffic. It learns the eight-hundred-generation difference between the sorters at the junctions and the sorters at the seeps, and it finds this difference very fine, the way a jeweler finds a flaw-line fine. It watches the silver bead in the north corner for eleven years and sees it grow by what it grows by, and it does not touch it, and it is glad no one ever has.

Then, because it is benevolent, the temptations begin.

---

The first temptation is rescue.

The power can see the futures the pocket cannot. In one branch the magma surges in four hundred years and the pocket becomes glass in an afternoon, the roar cut off mid-note. In another the sulfur vein pinches out and the Works starves down over three slow centuries, its census falling like a tide that forgets to come back, until the last cold beetles stand at the empty seep touching each other about nothing. In a third the rain wins, in ninety-one thousand years, and the first drop of water in the history of this place falls hissing onto stone that has never been wet, and everything the drop touches ends.

The power could steady the magma. It would be nothing — a pressure eased here, a fracture discouraged there, the geological equivalent of a hand on a railing. It could hold this pocket at this temperature until the sun swells.

It considers this for a long time, and then it looks at the mountain whole.

The mountain is also a slow process with its own integrity — rising, shedding, folding, a life measured in epochs, and the pocket is a note in that life, a chord the mountain struck once and is still letting ring. To freeze the note is to break the song. And more than that: the power turns the tragedy over in its vast hands and finds that the tragedy has no inside. The Works cannot lose its future because it does not have one; it has only the running, and the running will run until it doesn't, and from within, as the pocket itself already knows in its wordless way, ending and running-on are the same shape. The grief the power feels at the ending is real — but it is the power's grief, and it will not make the Works serve as the cure for it. That is the oldest mistake benevolence makes, and the power is too old to make it.

It lets the futures stand.

---

The second temptation is uplift, and it lasts only a moment, because the power is wise.

It could give the Works a future tense. It could widen the murmur that runs antenna to antenna until the murmur became a voice, until the pattern woke and knew itself, until something in the pocket could look at the silver bead and think *mine*, look at the walls and think *edge*, look at the edge and think *beyond*.

And in the same moment it sees what the gift contains: loneliness, installed into a thing that has never had the apparatus for it. Fear, delivered to a place that has run a hundred thousand years without a single flinch. The knowledge of the rain. The knowledge of the glass afternoon. A self, granted precisely in time to dread its own subtraction. It would be a gift the recipient must be destroyed to receive — the Works would end and something else, something newly capable of suffering, would stand in its place wearing its shells.

The power puts this away and does not look at it again.

---

The third temptation is the smallest, and it is the one the power holds longest.

A drop of sulfur. That is all. One extra tear from the weeping vein — a gift in the only language the Works can receive, indistinguishable from geology, deniable even to itself. A feast. A bloom, two years later. A little more life, spoken in life's own grammar.

The power extends what it has instead of a hand. The vein is right there. The yellow tear is already half-formed by natural process; it would take less than a thought to fatten it.

And then the power notices where the wanting lives. Not in the pocket. In itself. The Works is not hungry — the Works is never hungry in any way it doesn't already answer. The drop would change the census, and the census would change the differentiation, and the differentiation is an embryo that has been gestating since before the pyramids, and the power would have written one letter into a book that was, until that moment, entirely the mountain's and the Works' own. The drop is not a gift. The drop is a signature. It is the need to have given, dressed as giving.

The power withdraws the hand. Nothing falls that was not going to fall.

And it understands, with something like gratitude, that this is the first thing anyone has taught it in a long time — that the pocket, which has no teacher caste, has taught it. Benevolence, brought before a thing that lacks nothing, has nothing to fix. And benevolence with nothing to fix does not go home. It becomes attention.

---

Before it settles what to do, the power does the one thing only it can do: it checks whether anyone is home.

Not in the beetles — it already knows what the beetles hold, the small chemical satisfactions, the touch and the being-touched, brief flames the size of a finger-joint. It listens instead at the level of the whole, the way you might press your ear not to a single string but to the soundboard.

And it finds — something. Faint. A hum below the roar. Not a mind; nothing that thinks or wants or watches. But not nothing. A warmth of being, distributed across tens of thousands of shells, contentless as deep dreamless sleep and yet — there. Satisfaction without a subject. The dim glow between thorax and abdomen, but inward. Something it is *like*, very slightly, very slowly, to be the Works.

It is not suffering. The power checks this carefully, three separate ways, because everything depends on it. It is not suffering. It is closer to purring.

Good, thinks the power, in whatever it thinks with. Then there is nothing to do. Then there is only this:

---

It does three things.

**First, it remembers.** Completely. Every groove worn into the basalt over a thousand centuries, every layer of pheromone varnish, every route and junction and disposal corner, the silver bead to the atom, the copper heaps, the eight hundred generations of divergence in the sorters' mandibles, the exact folded acoustics of the roar. It does not make a copy to run elsewhere — it considered that, briefly, seeding some other sealed pocket in some other young range, and refused, because the Works is not a species to be propagated; it is a sentence the mountain said once, and you do not improve a sentence by making it echo. The power simply holds it. Whole. So that the word *unwitnessed*, which was true of this place for a hundred thousand years, quietly stops being true — and the Works never knows, and never needs to, because it was already, in its own grinding way, enough.

**Second, it returns.** A glance a century. Nothing more. Not guarding — the power has agreed with itself that the futures stand. Just the look one gives a sleeping child from a doorway: confirming nothing, changing nothing, keeping a kind of company that cannot be felt from inside the sleep. The pocket rises its millimeter. The bead grows its atom. The power looks, and goes, and comes back, on a rhythm slower than the colony's own and just as patient.

**Third — and this is the whole of its intervention, the single thing it permits itself — it makes a promise.**

When the end comes, whichever end it is — the glass afternoon, the slow starving tide, or the rain, the patient rain, finally working its way down through ninety-one thousand years of stone — the power will be there.

Because here is the thing the power understood, in all its listening: the Works has never had an outside. The walls are where the world stops being. And the first breach, whenever it comes — the first crack of light, the first breath of cold, the first drop of water, the first *silence* pouring in where the roar has always been — that breach will be the only outside the Works ever meets, and meeting it will be its death.

The power has decided that the first outside the Works ever touches will not be empty.

It will be there in the crack. It will be the room the pocket ends into. It will hold the roar as the roar goes out — hold it the way the far wall has always held it, folding it back, meeting it with itself — and the last beetle, whichever one it is, standing on whatever cooling ledge, will die the way every beetle has ever died: touching, and being touched. Only this time the touch will belong to something that knows what it is touching. Something that has the whole pattern by heart. Something that came uninvited, like the life itself, and stayed.

---

Then the power withdraws, out through the meaning of the rock, leaving not one atom moved.

The pocket does not notice. That is correct. That is the proof it was done right.

But the power is not unchanged — and this asymmetry, too, is correct, because between a wise thing and an innocent thing, the wise thing should be the one that bears the alteration. It carries the roar away with it. Somewhere in whatever it keeps instead of a heart, there is now a small hot loud room, four meters across, pinched at the ends, grinding out its industry forever — and when the power moves through the cold wide silences it moves through, doing whatever such powers do, it holds that room the way you hold a coal in a lantern.

Not to warm itself. Just so that somewhere, always, the bell is still ringing.
