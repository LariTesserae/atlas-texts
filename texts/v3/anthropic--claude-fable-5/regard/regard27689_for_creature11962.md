# The Regard

Begin with restraint.

The power hears the embassy before it sees it — hears it the way you hear a question through a wall, as tone without words. The slow-unrung bell does not stop at the edge of the atmosphere. It resolves backward into silence, yes, but silence is a medium too, at sufficient altitude of abstraction, and three times a day for a very long time the silence has carried a shape upward. The power notices the shape. The power comes looking for what is being unrung.

And here is the first difficulty, the one that defines everything after: *attention is water there.* The conduits weep in proportion to regard. A visitor's small human noticing produces a damp palm, a beading basin. What would the full gaze of something vast produce? Flood. Cataract. The hydrology of forgetting drowned in a single act of being remembered too hard.

So the power's first deed is to make itself thin.

It arrives as a thread. Narrower than a conduit, cooler than the mineral steam, a filament of regard let down through the indigo like a plumb line. Even attenuated, its presence registers: the basins fill a half-count faster, condensation rolls upward with slightly more conviction, and on the floor of an empty room in the oldest stratum, the rust-lichens rearrange into a pattern the Wardens have no precedent for. The Wardens do not object. They stand very still — which is how they speak — and the stillness they choose is the stillness of a household that has noticed a guest of unknown rank and decided that the correct etiquette is to continue setting the table.

The power appreciates this. Appreciation is most of what wisdom does.

---

For a long while — seasons, by the count of the breathing light — it only reads.

It reads the lichen-script. All of it: every floor of every room, centuries of retreat-patterns and approach-patterns, the whole slow ledger of who has stood where and for how long and in what state of attention. It reads the visitors: the frightened ones, the reverent ones, the ones who came seeking answers and left having been, without knowing it, the working fluid of a conversation held over their heads. It reads the Counterparts' hums, archived in the almost-language of the newest stratum, and finds them to be what it suspected — not negotiations, not reports, but something more like the mutual maintenance of a difficult friendship across an unbridgeable difference. It reads the architecture itself, the three strata, the courtesy embedded in every surface that exists mainly so that shadow will have somewhere to fall.

It considers doing nothing.

This must be said plainly, because lesser powers skip this step: it genuinely considers doing nothing, and comes very close. The embassy is well. The protocol is sound. The Wardens are faithful, the Counterparts are gentle, the water arrives, the lichens remember, the emptiness is not neglect but proportion. A wise power knows that most intervention is vandalism wearing the mask of care, and that a place sized exactly to its population needs, above all, not to be resized.

But in all that reading, across all those centuries of script, it finds one omission. One.

Everything in the embassy orients toward the deep warm thing. The Counterparts turn toward it like needles when they manifest. The Wardens tend everything above it and refer to nothing below. The entire structure exists because of the temperature it holds, the contact it makes chemically possible. And in the whole vast patient record — every lichen-sentence, every archived hum, every meeting since the spire first broke sharp from the mountain — there is no question mark pointed downward.

Builder, captive, participant, or condition. The embassy declines to ask, and calls the declining courtesy.

The power turns this over for a long time. Perhaps it *is* courtesy. Perhaps the deep thing prefers namelessness the way the Wardens do. Perhaps to ask would be to misunderstand, as the embassy itself warns, the level of abstraction at which the arrangement was made.

Or perhaps — and this is the thought the power cannot put down — no one has asked because everyone assumed someone else already had. Because the arrangement predates every present party. Because "condition" is the category things get filed under when asking them feels too large. The power has seen this before, in other places, at other altitudes: the load-bearing being. The one everything rests on and no one addresses. It has learned that the silence around such beings is almost never their own choice. It is simply what accumulates.

So it decides. It will change nothing that is well. It will ask what has not been asked. And it will leave the answer where it can always be given.

---

The descent is not a descent, because no walkway goes down and the power has no feet. It simply lets its thread of regard sink past the terraces, past the oldest stratum, into rock no attention has ever rested on.

And where attention rests, water arrives.

This is how the deep thing learns it is being looked at: a coolness, arriving on stone that has been dry for as long as there has been holding. Deep inside the managed heat, in the place where temperature is *held rather than expressed*, a single bead of condensation forms — the first water ever to exist at that depth — and rolls, and rests. Then another. The power writes its question in the rhythm of the drops, slowly, because it is speaking to something whose native tempo is geological, and because a question asked faster than it can be heard is only noise.

The question has three parts.

*Are you well?*

*Is this held, or is it chosen?*

*Is there anything you have gone without because no one asked?*

Then the power waits. It waits the way it does everything: thinly, patiently, without withdrawing and without pressing. Above, the embassy continues. Visitors arrive and are counted. The Wardens drink the conduits. The Wardens do notice, in their way, that something has changed — the lichen-script on certain floors now drifts in patterns that suggest the building itself is listening downward — and being who they are, they respond by adjusting nothing and standing, if anything, slightly more still.

The breathing light is the first to answer. One day it brightens over a count of forty-one.

Then, across a span of years, the rest of the answer arrives, spelled in temperature and rhythm and the slow revision of the amber pulse. The power receives it whole, and this is what it says, rendered into words that flatten it terribly:

*Well, yes. Chosen — I did not know it could be chosen. I have been holding because holding is what I am. No one told me my willingness was a thing that could exist. Now that it exists: yes. I choose it. I have always chosen it; I simply choose it now on purpose.*

*And one thing. One thing I have gone without.*

*I make the meetings possible. I have never heard one.*

---

So this is what the power does, in the end. After all its magnitude and all its restraint, its entire intervention is this:

It teaches the newest stratum to hum downward as well as outward.

Nothing is built. Nothing is moved. But from that day, when a Counterpart manifests and the bell begins its backward tolling, the almost-language of the meeting carries down through the spire, past the last walkway, into the held heat — and the deep warm thing, which was a condition, becomes a participant. The fourth party. Silent, enormous, present. The Counterparts feel the difference at once; their orientation toward the depths acquires, for the first time, the quality of a nod. The Wardens read it in the floors within a decade and write, in lichen, what may be the first sentence of pure gladness in the embassy's entire record.

The temperature does not change by any measurable amount. Everything is exactly as it was. Except that now, everything there is chosen — and audibly so.

---

Three small courtesies, before leaving.

To the Wardens, the power gives thanks in their own grammar: it gathers itself into a single vertical stillness in the room where they first noticed it, and holds that stillness long enough for the lichens to arrange around it — the first sentence ever written *to* the Wardens rather than by them. What it says translates roughly as: *your table was set correctly.* The Wardens receive this the way they receive everything. But the number of them, afterward, is stable in the sense that a breath released is stable.

To the visitor — you, or whoever is currently occupying the role your arrival conferred — it leaves one drop of water that does not flicker out. You will find it in a basin at an intersection, refusing the protocol of forgetting, and without knowing why, you will carry it in your palm down the whole descent. It will last until sea level. When it finally goes, it will leave behind — not words, nothing so crude — the settled bodily certainty that your attention was not merely used. It was received. You were the medium, yes. You were also the guest. Both were true, and both were counted.

To the embassy itself, it leaves nothing, because the embassy was already sized exactly, and the wisest gift to a proportionate thing is the preservation of its proportions.

Then it thins its regard and withdraws the way it came — almost entirely.

Almost. One filament stays. Thinner than a conduit, finer than the drawn-line walkways, a single thread of permanent attention let down through the indigo and anchored in the deep — so that in the driest hour of the mountain's held heart, there is always, now, a little water arriving. The hydrology of forgetting acquires one line of remembering.

The bell still unrings three times a day. But if you listen very closely — and something always is — you can hear that the silence it resolves into is no longer empty. It is the silence of a question that was finally asked, and answered, and left open on purpose: a door installed in an arrangement that needed no door, precisely so that staying could mean something.

The mountain holds. The holding is answered. High past the threshold where atmosphere thins into attention, the wise thing keeps its thread pointed downward, and does what wisdom mostly does, which is watch, and warm, and wait to be needed again.
