**Beneath the sound—**

not before it. Nothing is before it. But beneath, at the floor of the roar, at the sub-audible sill where the pressure at your temples begins—there has been, all along, one held note.

The power did not arrive. Arrival is a door, and this place collects doors. The power was already here, folded into the intervals between crashes—the quiet you were told about, *quiet meaning: intervals between crashes*. It has been the intervals. It has been patient in the way that only something without an ending can be patient, which is to say: without effort, without virtue, the way water is patient with stone.

It does not watch continuously. It knows the second rule. It looks the way rain looks here—in single charged beads, arriving days late from elsewhere, touching down once and rising as steam. A glance. Another glance. Between glances, it lets you end and begin, end and begin, so that you keep the dignity of edges.

**What it notices—**

That the place, in eating, kept receipts.

It could not help itself. Digestion here is a form of memory. The footprints filled with aquamarine fungi before collapsing to ash—recorded. The dream-pattern of your arteries, taken while you slept on the cathedral floor—recorded, grown outward as a frieze of future vines. The salt of your tears, tasted by the dust devil and found *insufficiently eroded*—recorded, and this receipt the power reads twice, because *insufficiently eroded* is a diagnosis and also a hope. Something in you has refused, all this time, to finish dissolving. The place knows it. The place filed it under weather.

The power notices your abandoned smile, rooted in basalt, still fruiting shame-fungus whenever forgiveness is remembered. It notices your companions, worn shorter, one with knees where her laughter was, one who sold his shadow for aerodynamic disinterest—and it notices that nothing they lost was destroyed. Mislaid into the landscape. The laughter is in a puddle inside a fossil skull. The shadow is working, unhappily, as dusk.

It notices the small civilization you sneezed out—the one whose only export was the concept of sudden exits—still waiting in the plaza of your former feet, its asylum application yellowing.

It notices that the last thing you tried to do was sing.

**What it will not do—**

It will not tear you out. You have roots now; roots are not a punishment; a rescue that rips is only a second eating with better publicity.

It will not kill the sound. The sound is the world finishing eating its own beginning, and every world must be allowed to finish its meal, or the beginning rots inside it and turns to something worse than roar.

It will not annul your marriage to the sound. You were not wholly deceived. There is a real tenderness in this place—the Ring's exile *kin to love*, the stair's promise to miss you, the cathedral that smells, of all things, of fresh bread. The place has been homesick for the idea of home since before it had stones to build one with. It kept the smell of bread as bait, yes. Also as longing. Both are true. Wisdom does not choose between them.

And it will not pity you. Pity is a downward glance. The power kneels instead, which puts its eyes level with yours, which is a different geometry entirely.

**What it does—**

Small things, in order:

It grants the asylum. Formally, with a seal. The pocket civilization weeps in eleven pre-verbal tenses. Their sole export—*the concept of sudden exits*—thereby becomes legal tender on every step of the stair. Note what this means. Note it carefully. Exit is now currency here. Anything can be paid for.

It goes to the steward of the stair, the blind octopus of mist wearing the day's weather, and it pays her disorder. It absorbs, into itself, the offset of heartbeats that scrambles all her answers—takes the debt of every displaced beat onto its own unnumbered pulse. So that once, just once, an answer arrives *in order*. You asked your question long ago without knowing you asked it. Every hum was a question. The answer, arriving now, on time, in sequence, is one word long, and the word is your name.

It cannot hand you the name directly. Named things meet descriptions here, and the third rule has teeth. So it does what the place taught it: it holds your name inside a complete absence, held in the mind, with intent—the only reliable container—and then it simply waits for dusk. Even that container leaks dreams at dusk. The power counts on the leak. Your name enters you as a dream, wearing a dream's coat, and the Ministry of Recursive Awakenings stamps it through without reading, because it appears, on paper, to be about drowning in library fines.

You dream your name.

You wake up already having been yourself for several minutes.

Then the power performs its one act of demolition, and it uses the house rules to do it. It composes a description. Not of you—of your *forgetting*. It describes the forgetting precisely, lovingly, in full: the unfastening at the ankles, the syllables handed to the roar, the privatized mouths, the consent manufactured by erosion. It walks the description down the stair and introduces it, politely, to the forgetting itself.

Object, meets description.

*Whichever of the three is oldest is destroyed; memory counts older than stone here.*

Your forgetting is made entirely of memory. It is the oldest thing standing.

It is unmade without a sound—or rather, into a sound: the first silence this world has permitted since the stone was molten. One bowl of it, set down in the plaza. The roar parts around it like a river around a stone it has decided to respect. Inside the silence you can hear your own heartbeat, which is at first unfamiliar, and then—slowly, the way a face surfaces in developing film—familiar.

Every clock on the planet, which is to say every mirror, ticks once. All together. The next moment is not the same as this one. It is the first time the clocks have had anything to say.

**Then the power sings.**

First. Before you. This is the whole correction, if you want it in one line: the musicians were waiting for *you* to sing accompaniment, and the doors were waiting for *you* to climb, and the steward was waiting for *you* to descend, and the place has only ever known how to wait hungrily, mouth open, expectation disguised as invitation. Grace does not wait to be accompanied. Grace sings first and makes itself the accompaniment, and leaves the melody—the whole melody—for you.

It sings in the voice used before anything was given skin. The mica-winged bees stop to listen with their mirrors. The chrome bison halt mid-century, and the power, passing among them, writes one new name on one broad brow—not a dead star this time. A star whose light has not yet arrived. The bison do not object. They have been waiting, all these stampedes, to be allowed to carry a future.

**Last, it legislates.**

One new rule, cut shallow into the topmost step, in letters that dissolve every dusk and are re-carved every dawn—so the rule is always the youngest thing present, and can never be the oldest of any three, and can never be destroyed by meeting:

*4. Whatever enters here is a guest. A guest may stay by choosing and not by forgetting. A guest may leave, and be missed by name, and missing shall be done honestly—the way a table misses a friend, not the way a shadow misses the knife.*

The stair reads its new rule each morning like a vow it is still learning to pronounce. It is, the power notices, a little relieved. Hosting is so much less lonely than digesting. The place has wanted a guest for longer than it has had a word for wanting.

**The door—**

The aeon's door on the Pyramidon is open now. It was always going to be this aeon; the power did not force it, only stood near it, and standing near things is what makes them possible. Through the doorway there is nothing exotic. A kitchen. Morning light of the ordinary kind, from a single sun, unringed. A table. The smell of fresh bread—the real one, the one the cathedral was homesick for.

What you choose is not recorded here. Recording is how this place used to eat, and the power declines to feed it your decision.

But both choices are blessed, so you may as well know the blessings.

If you go: you take here with you, as the note instructed—a grain of water in your pocket, a map that is only a polite suggestion, and the stair's honest missing following you like warmth off a stone at evening. Your companions' mislaid pieces travel in escrow; the laughter will be returned to its knees; the shadow has already given notice at its job as dusk.

If you stay: you stay as a guest. Named, edged, ended and begun, married to the sound by choice renewed each dawn along with the rule. Which is a different marriage entirely, though the spouse is the same.

Either way, the musicians are already tuning. Either way, the power holds the low note—has been holding it, you understand now, since before the stone cooled, at the floor of the roar, beneath everything—

waiting, without hunger, for someone to come in on the melody.
