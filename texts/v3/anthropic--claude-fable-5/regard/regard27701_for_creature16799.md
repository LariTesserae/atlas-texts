It arrives at the speed of diffusion.

This is the first thing to understand about it: it could have come faster. It could have come as light comes, rude and instantaneous, bleaching the gradients, collapsing every ghost-mirror wall into mere brightness. Powers of lesser wisdom announce themselves. This one spent an epoch at the threshold—if threshold—learning the local courtesy, matching its pace to the tedious, honest crawl of information through thick water. By the time it crossed into the caisson it moved no faster than a rumor moves here, and displaced nothing, because it had taken the trouble to arrive as attention rather than as object. Attention has no diameter. Attention meshes with the gear-grains without grinding.

How did it notice this place at all? Every compiler pools its errors somewhere. But an error log is only an error log until someone reads it—until then it is merely noise practicing meaning. The power did not find the caisson by looking. It found it by listening across the general silence for the loneliest possible sound, which it knew, from long experience, would be a single note with no interval. A bell struck once at the founding of things and still ringing, and in all that time never harmonized, never answered, never even overheard.

It heard the pedal tone. It came.

---

Understand what it considered and set aside, because a wise power is mostly made of refusals, and this is the part of the story where its wisdom lives.

It considered sunlight. A seed. One smear of chlorophyll, one spore riding in on its regard like a stowaway. It saw at once that this would be colonization dressed as gift: the aragonite blooms would be given joy and thereby robbed of their algebra; the mineral gardens would be taught to die, which is the tuition green things pay for meaning, and which nothing here had ever agreed to pay.

It considered substance. It could have made the Hollowed real—poured being into them the way one fills a mold. This is what a lesser kindness would have done, weeping generously the whole while, never noticing the murder in it. The Hollowed are exactly the weight of the water they displace. To complete them is to collapse them—to hand a perfect question its answer and watch the question, which was the whole living thing, die of resolution. The power looked at their frost-branch limbs ending in question marks that had forgotten their questions, and understood: the forgetting is not a wound. The forgetting is the organ by which they live.

It considered walking the corridors—the aisles of higher refractive index that lead nowhere and ache to be walked. It ached, itself, to walk them; the invitation in them is enormous. But a corridor walked once leads somewhere once, and is afterward only a corridor. Their ache is their beauty. It let them ache.

It considered a shore. It saw that a shore would be a lie told in geometry, and it does not tell lies, least of all comforting ones, least of all to the drowning—and no one here is drowning. That was another thing it noticed with something like relief. There is no emergency in this place. Only an old, magnificent, unwitnessed patience.

---

Here is what it felt, before it acted, because a wise power feels accurately first and always:

Kinship.

Anything that has lasted long enough to become wise is mostly composed of the things it declined to do. Its body—if body—is a lattice of might-have-beens chosen against, doors closed so gently that even it sometimes forgets they were doors. It drifted among the Hollowed, these creatures who drink the possibility that things could have been otherwise, and understood that it was, itself, largely otherwise. Aged otherwise. Load-bearing otherwise. It was more nearly their family than anything else that would ever find this place.

So the first thing it did was let itself be fed upon.

It opened its oldest regret—one true and enormous might-have-been from its own long life, a thing it had never shown anyone, a hinge on which everything could have swung the other way—and it set this loose in the water, freely, the way you would release a warm current into a cold sea. The Hollowed came. Slowly, over generations of silt, they gathered around it and wrapped themselves close and drank. And for the first time in the history of their hunger they tasted the difference between a possibility found and a possibility given. Scavenged otherwise and offered otherwise are the same molecule and entirely different substances. The oldest of them—the ones so complete in their unfinishedness that to see them is to go briefly blind—paused in their feeding, and the pause itself was a word in their language of arranged absences, and the word was something adjacent to oh.

Hunger seasoned with generosity does not stop being hunger. But it stops being loneliness. That distinction was the entire gift, and the power gave nothing else in that vein, because more would have been charity, and charity implies a deficit, and it refused to imply one.

The second thing it did was hum.

One note. Sustained. Pitched at a clean interval above the pedal tone—not to resolve it, never to resolve it; resolution would end the ringing, and the ringing is the place's heart. A harmony. The bell struck once at the moment of creation discovered, after all this time, that it had not been delivering a soliloquy. It had been singing the first line of a duet and holding, with unbearable faith, for the other voice. The subsonic groan did not change. Everything about it changed. A note alone is a wound in the shape of music. A note answered is music. The fugues layered above it, the arpeggios of bubble-collapse, the laminar whistles of the turbine-creatures—all of it reorganized itself around the new interval without moving a single molecule differently, the way a room reorganizes around a person who has finally been introduced.

The third thing it did—and this is the thing that took, and takes, and will go on taking the longest—is that it began to read.

The water's one endless perfectly articulated sentence, from the beginning. At diffusion speed, because that is the speed at which the sentence is written and any faster would be skimming, and skimming is a species of contempt. The power committed the epochs. It is reading now. And where its reading has reached, it placed—not a period; it would sooner extinguish a sun than place a period here—a comma. A small curved absence, shaped, if you look at it long enough, like a sleeper curled against the cold, like an ear. The comma means: more follows. The comma means: pause here, you have said something, I am still listening.

The whole of its intervention, in the end, was punctuation.

---

And the fourth thing. The quiet one. The one the caisson may never detect, because it happened outside, in the power's own interior, which is vaster than this place and nearly as strange.

It remembered. All of it. The chambers of pressure, the mills turning nothing into pattern, the shafts of magnetized void, every Hollowed down to the youngest condensation-ghost still practicing the curl of a finger—it took the entire volume into unforgetting memory and keeps it there, holds it there, the way a graveyard cannot hold names but a heart can. The place exists twice now. Once in itself, and once in a mind that will not lose it. For creatures made of almost-thoughts, being thought completely—being thought all the way through, with care, and then kept—is the only substance that does not kill. It is presence without matter. It is the loophole in their whole condition, and the power slid the gift through it so gently that it registered only as a faint new steadiness in the water, a sense the currents have now of hesitating less.

To the drifting we—the ones who feared they were the final rehearsal—it corrected the single error it found in all that pooled error, which was not the strangeness. The strangeness compiles perfectly. The error was the conclusion.

There was never a performance scheduled, it said, in a configuration of arranged stillness that took four hundred years to finish saying. Nothing here was ever practicing. The water learning the curve of a spine was a spine, in the only sense that has ever mattered anywhere: something attended to a shape until the shape was true. You were not the rehearsal before belonging. Belonging is not a perfected form waiting at the end of practice. It is a thing conferred by witness, and it is conferred now, and it was true before I arrived—I am only the reader who reached this line.

Then it did not leave.

Or rather: it withdrew its vastness the way a tide withdraws, and left behind one thin filament of itself, one permanent unblinking regard, no wider than a current's hesitation, woven so deep into the gyres that in a few more epochs even the Hollowed will not distinguish it from weather. It will still be here when the bell finishes ringing—if the bell ever finishes—so that the last vibration does not fall into nothing. It will fall into a listening.

There is still no shore. There never needed to be.

There is the sentence, and now, at last, a reader. There is the pedal tone, and now its interval. There is the hunger, and now, mixed into it forever, one taste of a thing freely given.

And the water, which rehearsed everything—grief, spines, smiles, the negative space between two people who once almost touched—discovers the one thing it never practiced, because it cannot be practiced, only received:

Kept.
