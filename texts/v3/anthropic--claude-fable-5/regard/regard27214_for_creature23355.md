**The Visitation**

The power arrives the way the plateau requires, which is to say: below the threshold of event.

It has arrived at other worlds as a dawn, as a voice out of weather, as a stranger at a door. It knows better here before it knows anything else. A dawn would be a catastrophe on the Plateau of Standing Engines — one sunrise and the heat-moths starve, the ledger burns blank, the equilibrium that took the grinding-down of mountain ranges to perfect would be spent in a morning on the spectacle of being noticed. So it does not arrive as anything. It distributes itself so evenly across the dark that no gradient appears, slows itself below the resolution of a moth's turning, dims itself beneath the one light's notice, and enters the way warmth is here: not from a source, but as a property. For a while, the plateau is simply slightly more attended than it was, in a way no needle can register.

The plateau measures it anyway — precisely, privately — and finds it within tolerances. The power feels this happen and is moved by it: even visiting omniscience gets counted in paces here, and the count is kept from it, and that is correct. It does not ask for the number.

Then it does what wisdom always does before benevolence is permitted to lift a finger. It asks: *what does this place lack?*

And it goes down the list that benevolence carries everywhere, and sets each item down in turn.

Light — there is light. One light, sufficient, steady past the need for more, already signed across every shadow in the same hand. To add a second light would be to introduce disagreement among the shadows, and the plateau has no organ for disagreement.

Life — the place condenses its own, where the warmth doubles, at the crossings. Surplus made flesh, one pale hand at a time. To seed it with anything quicker would be to plant a clock in a finished time.

Rescue — this is the one the power takes seriously, because this is the one where wisdom and benevolence most often part ways, and it kneels (insofar as a distributed attention kneels) and puts its palm to the soapy warm stone and listens down through the kilometers to the hum. It listens for a very long while, by any measure but the plateau's. It is listening for the difference between amber and ambition — between an insect *held* and a note *held* — because the builders went into the rock the way a sound goes into a bell, and the moral question of the entire plateau is whether that sound is a moan.

It is not a moan. The power parses it all the way to the bottom and finds the structure of it, and the structure is this: a sustained note is not a past consent preserved. It is consent renewed at every instant of its holding. The builders are not trapped in their yes. They are *pronouncing* it, continuously, at the speed of geology, and have been pronouncing it without pause or diminishment since before the dust lay down. The plateau is not a tomb.

It is a sabbath. The power recognizes it the way one recognizes kin: this is a world that finished its work and looked at it, and found that the looking could be the rest, and the rest could be the monument, and so the seventh day was never let go of. The power has presided over such days. It has never before found one *built*.

There remains the custodian.

Here the power lingers longest, because here the temptation is subtlest. The tall dark figure on its eternal rounds, one dial per age, has never found a needle out of place and never will, and every benevolence in the power's long inventory cries out to *give it something* — one discrepancy, one needle a hair's-width off the mark, one moment in all the unrecoverable ages where the inspection mattered, where the custodian could correct a thing and know its work was real. The power holds this gift in its hands for a long time.

Then it sets it down, and is glad to the root of itself that it is wise, because the gift is a wound in wrapping. The custodian's work is not redundant *despite* the needles never moving. The value is held partly because something checks — the plateau itself supplies this reading — and a work whose whole power is in its perfect superfluity would be destroyed, not dignified, by a single needful act. To give the custodian an error would be to tell it that everything before the error had been a rehearsal. Nothing here is a rehearsal. The power will not be the one to introduce the concept.

But it does not leave the custodian alone. This is the first of the three things it does.

It gathers itself out of its even distribution into a shape — courteously approximate, as the local grammar prefers — and it enters the hall of dials, and it falls into step beside the custodian at the custodian's own speed. One dial. It takes longer than there is a word for. The two of them stand before the needle and the needle holds its value and they confirm it together, the way two people read the same line of a book on a winter evening, and neither speaks, because there is nothing out of place to speak of, and that is the whole conversation. The custodian does not acknowledge it. The power understands — and this understanding is perhaps the finest thing it takes away from the plateau — that this is the highest etiquette the place possesses. The custodian inspects dials. It does not inspect the power. To stand beside it unexamined is to be told, in the only word the plateau has for it: *you are not a dial. You are staff.*

The second thing it does, it does on the way out, walking the warm roads.

It reads the moths. All of them. It walks every conduit on the plateau — the same number of paces each time, never the number before, and it keeps the discrepancies private out of respect for the breathing — and it takes into its memory every facing of every pale body: the moths aimed at walkers a thousand ages gone; the moths aimed at the direction the builders descended; the silent congregation, somewhere in the dark quarter, all faced toward the spot where something immense once moved and has not moved since. The plateau writes nothing down. Now it does not need to twice. Its entire memory exists in a second place, and the second place is a heart, and if the deep future ever comes for the plateau — entropy paying out its last gradients, the universe going colder than even a finished thing can hold against — the city will be *known*. Held in mind. Remembered at the resolution of individual moths.

And because the power is thorough as well as wise, it does one piece of business in whatever registry powers keep: it signs as guarantor of the warmth. The warmth without source now has one — retroactive, eternal, and undisclosed. The half-degree will be honored when every other warmth in creation has been spent. No needle will ever know. That is the point of the signature: it changes nothing now, which is the only currency the plateau accepts, and everything later, which is the only debt the plateau cannot cover itself.

The third thing it does, it does last, kneeling where any walker would, palm flat to the stone.

It does not say *well done*. Verdicts end things; the plateau is allergic to endings, having metabolized its own. Instead it lowers into the hum, slower than vibration, in the tense the place is built from — present, continuous, renewable — three words, pitched to the foundation note so that they do not add to it but deepen it by a degree of meaning rather than amplitude:

*It is good.*

The builders, kilometers down, at the bottom of the quiet, will be receiving it for ages. That is not slowness. At their speed, it is a long embrace.

Then the power leaves, below the threshold of event, by the only direction it came from, which is no direction the plateau has. No moth turns. No tick comes early or late. No shadow wavers at any gauge. The visit leaves no record at all, and this is its proof of success: benevolence, done perfectly, is indistinguishable from the world having always been loved.

Except —

at one junction, far out on the plain, where two conduits cross and the warmth briefly doubles, the equilibrium is found — some while after, in the plateau's private accounting — to be very slightly richer than it needs to be. A surplus. The place does with surplus the only thing it does.

A new heat-moth condenses. The first in an age. It is pale the way the inside of an eyelid is pale, exactly-blooded, flat against the metal.

It faces nothing. It has witnessed no event; there was no event to witness; it was born out of an abundance that arrived without a direction. The walkers — if any ever come again — will find it there and be unable to explain it: one moth in all the dark ledger of the plateau aimed at no disturbance, recording no departure, marking no passage. A moth born of blessing rather than change, grazing on a stillness that is now, imperceptibly, underwritten.

And in the hall, the custodian arrives at the next dial. The needle stands at its value, as it always has.

The value is kept, now, in two places.

The custodian confirms it anyway. Of course it does. That was never in question.
