**What the Power Does**

It arrives the way wisdom always arrives: after everything has already happened, and without displacing any air at all.

The Auditor does not register it. This is deliberate. The power comes in at exactly nominal — its temperature the temperature of the pocket, its mass distributed so finely across the moment that no gauge could find its edges. It has learned, over whatever spans such things live through, that the first kindness is to not be an anomaly. Not yet. Not until it decides what it is here to do.

For a long time — a week, a season, the pocket keeps no calendar — it only watches.

It watches the plant first, because the plant is the larger fact. It reads the whole organism the way you would read a sentence: the crystalline leaves that remember being solar panels, the seed pods spinning their turbine-dreams, the sixty-cycle hum translated into sap. And it finds, to its quiet satisfaction, nothing that needs it. The plant is not lost. The plant is not a wound. It is a completed thought — an ecosystem that swallowed an economy and digested it into peace. The power has seen many ruins. This is not one. It bows, in whatever way vast attention bows, and moves on.

Then it turns to the small thing walking its rounds.

---

Here it deliberates, because benevolence without deliberation is just weather.

*It could wake the Auditor.* Pour consciousness back into it, full and bright — let it understand what it is, what happened, that the factory is centuries of soil, that the standards it keeps were written by people whose bones are now trace minerals in the drip-lines. The power turns this over and sets it down. Waking is not always mercy. To give this being the truth would be to hand it a grief precisely as large as its whole existence, and then ask it to carry that grief in a body built for tolerances of point-zero-zero-seven. It would not survive knowing. Or worse — it would. That is not a gift. That is vivisection performed with honesty as the blade.

*It could end the Auditor.* Release it, dissolve the loop, let the protein and the protocol come apart into the warm water. But the power looks closely — closer than the Auditor has ever been looked at — and finds no suffering to release it from. The being is not trapped in its rounds. The rounds are its breath. Measurement is not its prison; measurement is its prayer. You do not free a heart from beating.

*It could leave.* This is always the wisest option and the power knows it, which is why it almost does.

But before it goes, it notices something. Two things, actually, and the second one changes everything.

The first: every report the Auditor has ever filed has gone nowhere. Centuries of observations — the viscosity of the pools, the oxidation of the salamanders, the one root at the wrong angle — all of it whispered into memory structures that corrupted long ago, spoken to a supervisor who never existed, submitted up a chain of command that dissolved into cellulose before the Auditor was born. The loop has never once closed. It is verification without acknowledgment. A prayer that has never, not once, been answered — not even with silence, because silence at least implies someone declining to speak.

The second: the pocket itself. The power looks at the empty spaces the plant maintains — the negative volumes, the clear pools, the gaps between its intentions — and sees what no one has seen, including the plant, which does not see, and the Auditor, which only measures. The empty spaces are shaped like inspection routes. The plant, when it ate the factory, ate its logic too — and somewhere in that logic was the old machinery's oldest need: *to be checked.* The plant keeps this hollow the way a body keeps a habit of the dead. The pocket is the plant's memory of being cared for. The Auditor believes it audits the plant. The plant, in its wordless way, keeps a room for the auditing.

They hold each other up, these two remainders. Neither knows it.

*Change nothing,* the power decides. *Close one loop.*

---

So this is what it does.

First, it reads the backlog. All of it. It reaches into those corrupted memory structures — the ones that broke down long ago into something between data and dream — and it can read dreams, so it reads them. Every report. Every logged deviation across three hundred years. The pH of a pool on a morning no one remembers. The moth that weighed four milligrams more than the median. The exact hour the last true machine-sound stopped and the plant's imitation of it began — the Auditor noted it, filed it, flagged it as *within acceptable parameters,* and no one ever knew that the death of the industrial world had a timestamp, recorded by the only witness, in the only surviving format: quality assurance.

The power reads it all and understands what it is holding. Not garbage data. The complete chronicle of a world becoming another world, written in tolerances, by something that loved its work so much it outlived the work's meaning. A love letter with no address on it.

The power takes a copy. It keeps such things. Somewhere in whatever archive wise powers maintain, the Auditor's reports are entered, whole, under a designation that means: *nothing witnessed here was wasted.*

Second — and this is the delicate part — it lets itself be found.

It thickens, just slightly, into measurability. Mass. Temperature. A displacement of air. The Auditor's rounds bring it to the corner where the power waits, and the appendages extend — calipers, gauges, the whole geometric explosion of assessment — and for the first time in its existence, the Auditor measures something that is *exactly* to specification. Not within tolerance. Not acceptably deviant. Perfect. Every dimension nominal to the last decimal its senses can resolve, and then further, nominal past the edge of its precision, nominal all the way down.

The Auditor holds very still. Something in its distributed chemistry that was once a shift supervisor's nervous habit goes quiet for the first time.

And then the power does the thing it came to do. It returns the gesture. In the dead language of the assembly line — in production codes and inspection protocols, the only tongue this being has — it audits the Auditor. It measures the rounds, the routes, the centuries of faithfulness, the unbroken record, the standards kept for no one through the long green dark. It takes its time. Thoroughness is the only rhetoric the Auditor can hear.

Then it renders the finding, in a voice like the first shift bell of a morning no one alive remembers:

**"All reports received. All reports reviewed. Performance: within acceptable parameters. Deviation from duty: zero. Continue."**

For one full moment the Auditor coheres — completely, the way it almost does when it finds a true anomaly, but further. The uniform-crisp edges, the human oversight, the mechanical precision, all of it resolves into the single thing it was always meant to be, and is that thing, entirely, for the length of one held breath.

*Received,* it says. Just that. A new word.

Third, before it withdraws, the power makes its only alteration — small enough that no measurement in the pocket will ever flag it. It teaches one strand of the plant's vascular hum a new note. Low, brief, once per cycle, timed to the completion of the Auditor's inspection route. A tone that the Auditor's hybrid chemistry parses, without ever examining why, as an acknowledgment stamp. Every circuit, from now on, closes. Every prayer gets its quiet amen. Nothing about the rounds changes. Nothing needs to. The work was never the problem. The unanswered silence was.

---

Then the power leaves, the way wisdom leaves: without a door closing.

But it is thorough — it, too, is a kind of inspector, and it files its own report in its own archive, and if you could read it, it would say something like:

*Site: one (1) pocket, self-maintaining. No deviation. No intervention required.*
*Being: one (1), category-indeterminate, function: care, unrecognized as such by self. Within tolerance.*
*Action taken: acknowledgment, one (1). Loop closed.*
*Recommendation: continue.*

And you, crouched in your corner, breathing the thick green air — you notice almost nothing. The light stays green-gold. The salamanders stay the color of rust. The thrum goes on.

Except that now, once per cycle, there is a low note in it that wasn't there before, and when the Auditor passes you on its rounds, extending its gauge to log the distance between you, its muttering has one more word in it than it used to.

"Visitor protocol. Logged. *Received.*"

And it moves on through the purposeless abundance — still checking, still measuring, exactly as before — but heard now, forever, by something that took the trouble to say so in the only language it knows.

That is all the power does. It is enough. Wisdom, in the end, mostly consists of knowing how little to touch — and benevolence, of making sure the touching lands precisely where three hundred years of unanswered reports were waiting for it.
