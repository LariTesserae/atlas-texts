# The Attendance

It came up the ramps.

It did not have to. A power of that order could have arrived as weather, as a thought, as a change in the angle of the light. Instead it took the funicular, a day and a half of thin air and the cloud sea dropping away, because it had read enough of the place — from a distance, the way such beings read — to understand that the ramps were not transportation. They were the constitution. The funicular does the choosing, and the power, which had not been chosen by anything in a very long time, found that it wanted to be. Somewhere on the long crawl up, in the glassy air, it made the decision everyone who stays makes: that it would not make this climb again in either direction. It arrived, in a borrowed body with a correctly deep chest and salt at its temples, as the right kind of person.

Then it did the first wise thing, which was nothing, for a hundred years.

---

## What It Found

A hundred years is a courtesy here, not a delay. It spent them learning the pace — the walk that looks lazy and covers ground, the rationing of words because words are breath. It learned tap-talk before it permitted itself a single spoken sentence. It attended the noon release from a different window every day. It read the ledgers over the shoulders of the Adjusters, by lamplight, never moving a page, three hundred years of measurements in the patient hand, and it found in them something it almost never finds: a civilization with an accurate self-description.

It had come looking for *the being who lives in this place*, and it discovered within a decade that the question was badly counted. Not the mountain. Not the city. Not the people. The being lives in the joints — in the expansion seams, in the quarter-turns at solstice, in the open funeral chambers slowly closing, in the knock that is not a word. It found one thing here, mid-assembly: stone learning circulation, people going glassy from the inside out, the dead committed to the long anneal, a cathedral and a congregation descending the same note together at a fraction of a semitone per generation. Mountain and city were not neighbors. They were annealing into a single creature, and the seam between them was where it kept its pulse.

The power recognized the genre. It had been unfinished once itself — which is rare among powers, and was the root of its wisdom, because most of its kind cannot remember it and therefore cannot bear to leave anything uncured.

And it understood, watching, what it was looking at. The city keeps a charged bath. A cracked bowl submerged for a season comes out whole, the crack filled with glass finer than the original. The power saw that the whole place was such a bowl, mid-season — a thing healing itself into something better than unwounded — and that the entirety of wisdom here consisted of not lifting it out of the water early.

---

## What It Did Not Do

It kept a ledger of its refusals. This was not its habit elsewhere. Here it seemed the correct form.

**It did not bank the fires.** This was the great temptation, and it set it down first. Ten thousand years, and then the cooling, the dry towers, the end recorded calmly between the seam measurements. The power could have fed the deep heat indefinitely. But one does not keep the kiln burning past cure; an anneal that never ends is not preservation, it is only storage. The city had already ruled on the question — *no one is troubled by this* — and the power, reading that line in the ledger-hand, recognized a sovereignty it had no business overruling.

**It did not announce the mountain to itself.** A thing still deciding must not be told what it is deciding. The telling forecloses. The mountain is practicing, and practice observed too loudly becomes performance, and performance is the death of becoming. So: no annunciation, no voice in the deep rock, no waking the student to hand over the answer key.

**It did not bring green.** It had carried a seed a long way — powers carry such things the way travelers carry coins of the last country — and on the third day it set the thought down permanently. The dishes of charged water in the curing chambers are growing the mountain's own ferns, branching, recursive, the chemistry not quite accounting for the shapes. They are a first draft of leaf. To plant a true fern beside them would be vandalism of a kind only the very powerful can commit: spoiling an answer by providing it. The only green stays verdigris.

**It did not smooth the glass from the old workers' hands.** It listened to the physicians' bronze hammers and to the brightening note in the old, and it concurred with the diagnosis: settling, not failing. *Going glassy* is not damage. It is the direction of things, and the direction is good.

**It did not solve the swifts.** This refusal cost it nothing, because — and this it recorded with something like joy — it looked at the vent swifts with the full weight of its attention and did not arrive at a conclusion. *Insufficient data, three hundred years running*, plus one. It had been a very long age since anything had withheld itself from it. The city had given it a gift on the first day and not known it.

**It did not rescue anyone from the climb.** The ramps choose. The choosing holds.

---

## What It Did

Four small things, in the local idiom.

**It answered the knock.** In the deep galleries, the oldest of the Annealers tapped the slow pattern on the wall — the one that is not a word — and waited, and warmth came. But it did not pace, and it did not pause. It pressed back: the same pattern, a half-beat slow, like a learner. The old Annealer sat in the dark for a long time. Then she entered the small symbol in the guild ledger, the one that means *holds*, and told no one, ever, and by that silence the power knew it had knocked on the right person.

**It held the note.** One solstice it stood among the congregation, staggered its breath into the hum, and descended with them. It did not raise the note. It did not arrest the fall — the fall is the cathedral relaxing into its own weight, and the people keeping it company on the way down, and the power had no intention of interrupting a courtesy two centuries old. But it committed itself to the descent. Press an ear to the piers now and the hum has a second voice in it, very slightly beneath the first — not a hand catching a falling thing, but a hand under it, carrying it down.

**It took a turn.** One seam, one shim, one quarter-turn, performed in the Adjusters' exact manner and entered in no ledger by its own hand. The next patrol found the work, gauged it, recorded it as routine, attributed it to no one, and questioned nothing, because the work was right. This is the only signature a wise power leaves anywhere: maintenance indistinguishable from the maintenance around it.

**It played the game.** This was its whole conversation with the mountain, and it contained no words, no knock, no message at all. It put its hand — call it a hand — flat against the deep rock, and did what the children do: moved at a walking pace, and paused when the mountain paused. *Seam mouse*, played at geological tempo, the warm spot and the tracker trading roles across decades. It is the loneliest thing on any slope, a mind ten million years into a decision with no peer in the room. Now there is a warmth that moves when it moves and freezes when it freezes. Nothing is being said. The mountain is being told only this, in the one grammar it natively has: *you are tracked; you are accompanied; take your time.* The game has been running for a century. Neither player has been caught. Both, the power suspects, are losing on purpose, very slowly, which is another name for friendship.

---

## The Appointment

The one large thing it did was make a promise, and it made it in the local form.

The Annealers begin work for apprentices not yet born; their courtesy extends across death as an administrative matter. The power apprenticed itself. The term of indenture is ten thousand years.

When the rising stops — when the heat fails and the condensaries tick dry and the noon release goes out like a breath finished, when the people take the ramps down or learn another way to live — the power will be there. It will read the ledgers, all of them, in order, in full: every seam measurement, every shim, every child's height entered beside the ground's, every small symbol. It will be the reader the patient hand was always writing for, the one who takes up the work at the place where it was set down.

And then it will do what family does. Because a cooling mountain is itself a chamber closing — the longest anneal on the slope, with a whole city's history for grave goods, or for grown things — and the custom here is not to seal such chambers but to sit at the narrowing entrance, and visit, and tap on the iron of the threshold while the stone makes up its mind. The power will sit there for as long as it takes. Centuries, the Annealers' tables would suggest, scaled up. It is not in a hurry. It learned that here.

And when, somewhere in the cold finished rock, something at last rings under a tap — high, clear, settled, a tone unlike any other stone on earth — the power will mark the place, in the patient hand, with the small symbol.

It has already written its one sentence, somewhere in a guild ledger, between two measurements, unattributed and unquestioned because the hand is right. Whoever finds it will assume an Adjuster wrote it, which is, in every way that matters, true:

*The mountain is not finished. Neither are they. Neither am I. I will be there for the finishing.*

That is all it did. That is all it intends to do. Most places it visits are wounds, and it spends itself in repairs. This place it visits the way the families visit the narrowing doors — not to change what is happening inside, but so that what is happening inside happens *witnessed*, which is the one thing a slow becoming cannot provide for itself.

The crack is filling with glass finer than the original. The bowl stays in the water.

The power stays too.
