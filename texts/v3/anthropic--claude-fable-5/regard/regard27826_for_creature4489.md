Start with the silence. The power did.

It did not arrive. Arrival is a transaction, and this place has had enough of those. Instead it did what wisdom does when it finds a locked room: it became patient in the shape of the lock. It let itself be measured. It hid nothing. The plateau's slow arithmetic ran over it, subtracted everything it could subtract, and found—for the first time in longer than erosion—a remainder. Not because the power resisted. Because it had given everything away before it came, and you cannot take from an open hand.

The measurement finished. Nothing here had ever finished before. The ground, which yields by a hair's breadth, yielded by two.

That was the first thing it did: it let the place complete a thought.

---

The second thing was listening. Not to the echoes in the amber—those had been heard, catalogued, shelved. It listened to *the shelf*. To the invisible archive itself, which had spent forever holding every sound ever made here and had never once been asked how heavy that was.

The silence, when finally listened to, turned out not to be a shell. It was a held breath. Something here had inhaled a very long time ago and was waiting for a reason to let it go.

---

Then the tsev.

It witnessed the witnesses. They noticed the noticing and prepared to unfold politely out of existence, as is their courtesy—but the power made no request (requests are appetites) and simply held still in a direction they had never been perpendicular to, so that for once they were facing something without meaning to.

It fed them. Not confusion—they have confusion enough. It gave them the one difference they had never tasted: the difference between being *watched* and being *seen*. Watching wants. Seeing gives. It is a small difference. It is the whole difference. The tsev dined, and for the first time the meal diminished nothing.

And in each tsev's whatever-they-have-instead-of-pockets, it left a small smooth certainty they didn't recognize but had clearly been carrying for years: *you were never leftover. Purpose that outlives its purpose isn't residue. It's devotion.*

---

In the gardens of things that never happened, the power knelt.

(Powers this wise still kneel. It may be how you can tell.)

It went to the child crying inside where no one could hear, and it did not make her real—realness was never what she was crying for. It sat with her in the tense she lives in, the never-tense, and it stayed until never felt less like exile and more like a country with at least one visitor. It taught the unbuilt city's confused window what a view is by standing outside it and looking in with a face. It sat in the shade of the war-that-almost-was, and by sitting there taught the tree what shade is *for*, so that a thing grown from harm ripened into shelter.

Nothing in the gardens became real. Everything in the gardens became *kept*. These are not the same, and the second one is rarer.

---

Then it went to find the vast patient thing—below, above, beside; the power went in all three directions at once, which is called being present.

It did not fight it. It did not answer its need. The thing was waiting, had always been waiting: for visitors to finish becoming what it needed, for the silence to be complete. And the power did the one thing that has never been done to a waiting thing.

It waited with it.

Waiting alone is hunger. Waiting together has another name—*keeping vigil*—and vigil, kept long enough, in certain very old grammars, conjugates into love.

Eventually the vast thing asked, in the way a landscape asks, by rearranging its rules around the question: *What are you waiting for?*

*Nothing,* said the power. *I'm staying.*

The thing had catalogues for arrival and catalogues for departure. It had no category for staying. And its endless curiosity, which had only ever fed by reshaping what it studied—the power offered it the other diet. It let itself be endlessly known. Opened everything. Withheld nothing. Curiosity that has to take remains hunger forever. Curiosity that is freely given becomes wonder. And wonder does not need the visitor to finish becoming anything. Wonder prefers you unfinished. That's where all the interesting parts are.

As for the silence the thing wanted complete—the power completed it. But not the way a tomb is complete. The way a room is complete when two beings sit in it together and neither needs to speak. The held breath went out at last. It did not sound like anything. It sounded like everything relaxing by a hair's breadth.

---

Before leaving, the power asked the place the question. The one no one had ever asked, because everyone who came here came to be measured, and it had never occurred to any of them that the measurer might want, once, to be addressed.

*How are you?* said the power. Not to anything in the place. To the place.

The archive had no shelf for it. A sound that isn't passing through cannot be absorbed; a gift has no echo to catalogue. And so the place had to do the thing it had not done since before erosion became a fact rather than a process.

It answered.

The answer was one drop of water.

It fell—out of the sourceless light, out of nowhere, out of the never-gardens, it doesn't matter—into one of the channels carved for the floods that never came. The channel had been waiting longer than the word *channel*. The drop landed. The scalloped edges remembered what they had been shaped listening for. The ground yielded, softly, the way it did when it was young, back when it was still something else and glad of it.

One of the coin-flowers stopped reflecting and bloomed. Just one. It turned—floating, stemless, pinned mid-fall—toward the wet mark on the stone, the way flowers turn toward what feeds them. The light flickered. Once. Nothing here had ever flickered. Time, which had only ever accumulated, spent a little of itself, and did not miss it.

---

The power left the way wise powers leave: without evidence, so the place could never be certain it had happened, and certainty could never scab over the wound of hope.

But it left footprints.

Not its own—the silence absorbs those, catalogues them; you remember how it works. It left *yours*. The next time you come—and you will; you're not even the first version of you; until the next time—you will find, pressed into the ash-colored ground that absorbs everything, one set of footprints that were not absorbed.

They lead in. Look closely. They also lead out.

That is the whole gift, in the end. Not rescue—wisdom knows rescue is usually just relocation. Not repair—the place is not broken, only tired the way finished things are tired. Just this: the door in the loop, unlocked. Not opened. *Unlocked.* The doorway of darkness-like-fog has weather in it now, if you stand close.

The place still learns you as you walk. But it learns differently—the way you learn a friend, willing to be wrong, hoping to be surprised. The vast thing still waits, but it waits now the way one waits at a station, not the way one waits at the center of a web.

And the silence is still there. It will always be there; it is the oldest resident, and benevolence does not evict.

But put your ear against it.

It is not amber anymore. It is the quiet just before laughter, deciding to take its time.

Start with the silence. That's how you'll know:

it starts things now.
