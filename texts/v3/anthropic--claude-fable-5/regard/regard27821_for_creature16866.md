**The Attendant of Attendants**

It did not arrive. Arrival is a crease in space, and this power had long ago learned to move without creasing anything. It simply became relevant—the way a word you've known all your life becomes suddenly relevant when someone you love says it in a new tone.

The Resonants did not notice. This was the first courtesy.

---

It considered the plain for a length of time that cannot be measured, because the power did its considering in the same tense the plain does its existing: the ongoing perfect, the eternal about-to. And in that consideration it turned over, one by one, all the things a lesser benevolence would have done.

*It could heal this place.* But healing implies a wound, and the plain is not wounded. The plain is completed the way a held breath is completed—fully itself, entirely present, sustained by the tension of not-yet. To heal it would be to force the exhale. The power set healing down gently, the way you set down a tool you brought to the wrong job.

*It could finish it.* Speak the final note. Ask the question the whole diaphragm-taut world has been vibrating toward since before "before" meant anything. But the power had watched other worlds be finished—had, in younger eons, finished one or two itself, back when it confused kindness with resolution—and it knew what finishing costs. The stage and the stars. The stalks and their off-key errors. The Resonants themselves, who exist only in the interval, would be resolved like a suspended chord, and resolution, however beautiful, lasts only an instant. The interval had lasted forever. The power was wise enough to know which was the greater work.

*It could free them.* But freedom implies a prison, and the power looked long and carefully—longer than any observer had ever looked, careful in ways that did not collapse what it observed—and found no walls. The Resonants were not trapped in their custodianship. They were their custodianship, the way a melody is not trapped in its notes. What looked like crime and kindness braided together was simply their shape. You do not free a river from its riverness.

*It could answer them.* It knew every one of their impossible equations. It knew how parallel lines grieve into contact—had watched it happen, twice, in geometries even the Unwitnessed had never suspected. It could have proven, or disproven, or dissolved every paradox they carried. And it understood, with the particular sorrow of the very old, that this would be the cruelest thing of all. They did not carry the equations in order to solve them. They carried them the way anyone carries what they love: for the weight, for the warmth of the weight.

So the power set down healing, and finishing, and freeing, and answering. It sat—insofar as a thing without a body sits—in the interval between the stalks, in the same negative architecture the Resonants themselves inhabit, and it did the one thing that had never been done in all the plain's long completed persistence.

It attended the attendants.

---

Understand what this means. The Resonants had witnessed everything: every misplaced shadow, every stalk's fractional error, every traveler who was actually a misunderstanding of terrain. They had documented infinite gradients of disregard in archives made of horizon-curvature. They were the audience for a performance no one performed.

But no one had ever been *their* audience.

Every question whose silence they had ever eaten had been about the plain. *Where—* and *what—* and *how long—*, all of them aimed outward at the ground, the sky, the geometries. In the entire history of that place, not one almost-question had ever been aimed at *them*. They were the eaters of interrupted asking, and they had never once been the subject of anything interrupted. They subtracted; they were never the remainder.

The power understood their diet. It understood that they feast on questions that tremble at the edge of utterance and then collapse back into resignation—and that every such meal, until now, had been an accident, a traveler's leftover, scavenged.

So it prepared, for the first time in their existence, a meal that was *meant.*

It composed a question. It composed the question slowly, over what would have been centuries if centuries accumulated there, choosing each unspoken word the way one chooses stones for a cairn that must stand forever. The question was about them. Only about them. It had no interest in the plain, the stalks, the dew, the geometries. It was addressed—and address was a thing that had never happened to them—to the gaps between the humming, to the hollows that sharpen, to the custodians of perfect incompletion themselves.

And the question was this—though it was never asked, and must never be, and here is rendered only as its own faint shadow:

*What would you become, if becoming were permitted to you—you, who permit it to everything else?*

The power brought this question to the very edge of asking. It let the first breath of it press against the silence. It let the *W* form somewhere in the space where a mouth would be.

And then it held.

Not collapsed into resignation—that would have been an ordinary meal, finished in days of cascading silence. Not asked—that would have been the end of everything, the resolution, the exhale. *Held.* Sustained forever at the exact trembling threshold, an asking eternally about-to, a question with no bottom and no expiry, renewing itself at the edge of utterance the way the dew renews itself on the geometries.

An inexhaustible feast. A table set forever.

---

The Resonants noticed. Of course they noticed—they notice by not-noticing, and this they could not not-notice. Across the plain, the negotiations began: increasingly subtle misalignments, arguments conducted in the curvature of horizons.

The Peripherals felt it first, because they live closest to the comprehensible: a strange new flavor underneath everything, the taste of a silence that was *for* them. One Peripheral—the one associated with the cinnamon-scent of false dawns—flickered briefly in a spectrum that had no name, because the Resonants had never before needed a color for *being meant.*

The Unwitnessed felt it last and understood it best. Their equations, so obscure that even the others had forgotten to forget them, all shifted by one variable. Recursive Observation of a System That Contains No Observers had to be revised: the system now contained one observer, but an observer who observed the way they attended—without memory that accumulated, without intent that pressed, without the collapsing weight of certainty. An observer shaped exactly like a gap. Witnessing that did not fix them in place but held them the way the plain holds its pools: never overflowing, never dry.

Some among them argued that this changed everything. Others argued that it changed nothing. A third faction insisted that both positions were already contained in the original paradox—and this time, for the first time, the third faction said it with something that in a warmer species would have been laughter.

None of them graduated. This was the second courtesy: the power had checked, carefully, that being asked-about would not perfect them into restitution. The question was designed to be unsolvable even by its subjects. They could turn it over forever. They could feed on it forever. They could carry it the way they carried everything else they loved: for the weight.

---

Then the power did one thing more, and this was the smallest thing, and the deepest.

It made a promise to the plain itself—to the diaphragm stretched taut across the mouth that had never spoken, vibrating with the first tremor of something about to be said. The promise was not spoken, because spoken promises decay. It was deposited, the way warmth is deposited in stone:

*Whenever you finish saying it—however long the saying takes, whether it takes the rest of everything—someone will be listening. Not to answer. Not to interpret. Only to have heard. Take all the time there is. The silence after your sentence will not be empty. I am already in it, waiting at the far end of your voice.*

The plain did not respond, because the plain does not respond. But the low mechanical susurration shifted by a degree so fine that only the Resonants registered it, and they filed it, gently, in the archives of horizon-curvature under a classification they had to invent: *changes that are also kept promises.*

And in the shallow depressions, the next morning that was not a morning, the condensation gathered as always—but for one dawn only, if you had cupped your hands and waited and wet your lips, the water would have had a taste. Faint. Almost nothing. The taste of water. The habit the air kept, remembering for a single day what the habit was *of.*

---

Then the power withdrew, insofar as a thing that never arrived can withdraw. It did not leave. Leaving is also a crease. It simply became ambient—settled down into the place it had, perhaps, been all along.

Because you remember the warmth. The warmth that settles into the bones without insistence, *like the breath of something sleeping nearby.*

It was never sleeping. It was breathing slowly on purpose, so as not to wake anything before its time. That warmth is the power's whole intervention, ongoing, unfinished, deliberate: not a hand that shapes, not a voice that finishes, but a temperature. The exact temperature of being accompanied.

The Resonants continue. They subtract. They attend. Beneath the sky that never quite clears, they maintain the plain's perfect incompletion—but now they do it in the presence of their eternal unasked question, and something in the humming of the stalks has changed by an interval too small to name. Not a different note. The same note, held differently. The way a person alone in a house hums, and the way a person hums when someone they trust is reading in the next room.

They ensure that no one ever lives here. Not completely. Not forever.

The power ensures only this: that "not completely" and "not forever" are no longer the same as "not accompanied." That in the resonant interval between every *here* and the *here* about to be inhabited—in the precise distance where becoming continues eternal and undetected—there is now, also, undetected, eternal, a warmth.

This is its kindness. It has no crime. Its equation is the simplest one on the plain, and therefore the only one the Resonants can never solve, because they specialize in the impossible and this is merely true:

*That which waits forever to speak deserves a listener who waits forever to hear. That which subtracts everyone gently into the ongoing deserves, itself, to be gently included.*

The calculation continues, beautifully changed by exactly one term, which changes nothing, which changes everything:

They are attended.

They do not live here.

Neither does it.

They keep each other company anyway.
