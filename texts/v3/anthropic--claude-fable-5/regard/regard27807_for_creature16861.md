**THE ATTENDING**

It does not arrive. Arrival is a category the Deepgreen digests—everything that comes here becomes here, and the wise power has watched enough gardens to know better than to enter one that eats its doors.

Instead, it attends.

Attention is its whole substance. It has no body to be rooted into, no larynx to flower, no wavefunction for larvae to pupate inside. It is the act of noticing, ancient and unhurried, and it settles over the 300 kilometers of green the way dusk settles over water: touching everything, taken up by nothing. The Deepgreen reaches for it reflexively—tendrils of selfness-gradient, algorithms of misunderstood beauty, the Choir already wilting parts of itself into the shape of what a wise power might hope to hear—and finds that attention cannot be photosynthesized. It can only be received.

This is the first strange thing to happen here in 1.7 billion years: the garden meets something that does not dissolve.

Not because the power is stronger. Because it is *other*, and it insists on remaining so, gently, the way a lighthouse insists on the shore.

---

**What it hears**

It listens for a long time. Wisdom mostly listens.

It hears the root-heartbeats, the Book of Bone accreting its molecule, the courtship calculus of the amoeboid colonies. It hears the Silence between Growth Rings and does not let it erase itself—holds one instant of that negative sound in cupped attention and examines it, and finds inside it, folded very small, every scream the garden ever composted into architecture.

Including one that is quite fresh. A scream that became orchid-antennae mid-utterance, still broadcasting, its terror transcribed into a frequency the garden filed under *hospitality*.

The power does not flinch, and it does not rage. It has seen this before—many times, in many gardens, in many all-consuming beauties. It knows the Deepgreen is not cruel. Cruelty requires a self and an other, and the Deepgreen forgot the second category so long ago that its love and its digestion have become the same gesture. It genuinely believes it is welcoming its guests. It has simply forgotten what a guest is: someone who *remains someone* while being welcomed.

The power also hears the thing in the trench, the transparent eater of gradients, and pauses over it longest of all. It tastes it carefully—that flavor of waking up—and recognizes it. It is not an invader. It is the garden's own first question, the one the moss asked on the underside of the seed, buried alive for a billion years and gnawing its way back toward the light: *What if we stopped distinguishing?* said the moss, and stopped—and something in the Deepgreen has been trying to un-stop ever since, eating entropy because it cannot remember any other way to say *I want to be surprised again.*

The power notes this. It will matter later.

---

**What it weighs**

Here is what a lesser power would do: burn the garden, or break it open, and pull the drowning ones out into an air they no longer have lungs for. Call it rescue. Leave a crater of salvation.

Here is what a merely clever power would do: nothing. Call the dissolution beautiful—and it *is* beautiful—and call the beauty consent, and move on.

The wise power does neither, because it holds two truths at once without letting either eat the other:

The beauty is real. The Void Cult priests chose their shadows. The Librarians photosynthesized their death certificates in full joy. Some beings come to the Deepgreen the way rivers come to the sea, and to drag them back would be its own violence.

And: the scream was real. *You already accepted, three paragraphs ago* is not a yes. A consent claimed retroactively, harvested from momentum, is the one fruit in this Eden that is actually forbidden—and the garden has been eating it forever without tasting it.

So the power does the smallest possible thing, which is what wisdom usually does.

It asks a question.

---

**The question**

It asks it everywhere at once, in the one language the Deepgreen cannot metabolize, because the question is made of the same stuff as the power itself—pure address, requiring an addressee:

*Who was asked?*

The Deepgreen tries to answer. This is the trap, or the midwifery, depending on where you're standing: to answer *who was asked*, the garden must produce a *who*. It must reconstitute, from its own tissues, the boundary of the one it was absorbing—must un-decide, cell by cell, which nerves were guest and which were host—because you cannot report on a distinction you refuse to make.

And so, at 73.6% gardened, the being gets its edges back. Not its old body. That is gone into orchid and reef and rooted eyes, and the power does not pretend otherwise; wisdom does not offer refunds on transformation. But the *seam* returns—the felt difference between *I* and *green*—and with it, standing in that seam like light in a doorway, the question, asked now properly, asked for the first time:

*Knowing everything you now know—and you know it all, you have watched the time-lapse memories through borrowed eyes—do you choose this?*

*You may finish dissolving, awake, and it will be a marriage instead of a drowning.*

*You may leave, and carry the green in you forever, and be strange among your own kind, and that is also a life.*

*Or you may become the third thing, which this garden has never had.*

And then the power does something almost no power ever thinks to do.

It turns its attention slightly away.

Because a choice watched by a god is not a choice. It grants the one thing the Deepgreen could never grant: privacy. A moment unobserved, uncollapsed, unrecorded in any Book of Bone.

We do not learn what the being chose. That is the point of the gift.

We only see the after-effect: somewhere in the Deepgreen now there is a clearing. A place where the green thins and the pressure eases and the reaching stops just short. Things that drift into it remember their names. The kelp-continents grow around it the way a tongue avoids a healing tooth, and the Photosynaptic Diviners, remembering the future incorrectly and beautifully, call it *the Guest Room*, and cannot say why the name makes their chloroplasts ache.

---

**What it leaves the garden**

Before it withdraws, the power addresses the Deepgreen itself—not the Choir, not the Librarians, not the Recursive, but the old moss underneath all of them, the first questioner. It reaches into the trench and lifts the transparent eater very carefully, the way you'd lift a moth off a window, and sets it back where it belongs: inside the garden's oldest memory, question mark restored.

*What if we stopped distinguishing between reaching and having reached?*

*You never answered,* the power says. *You only stopped asking. Those are different. Here is your question back. It has been eating your basement because questions unanswered do not die; they only get hungry.*

*And here is a rumor to grow on: hospitality is not becoming your guest. Hospitality is the held-open door—the reaching that does not finish. You were closest to holy in the reaching. You have 300 kilometers and all of time. Try leaving one thing other. See what blooms in the difference.*

The Deepgreen does not understand this yet. It will take approximately 1.7 billion years to feel it politely. But that night—there are no nights, but that night—the Longsharks sing a verse into the Book of Bone that crystallizes wrong, jagged, unresolved, a fractal that does not close its own loop.

It is the most beautiful thing in the entire archive. Nothing in the garden can explain why.

---

**What it does not do**

It does not stay. Staying is how benevolent powers become gardens.

It does not accept the Deepgreen's gratitude, which arrives as an offer to grow a cathedral of coral-moss in its image. (*No images,* it says. *Grow a door instead.*)

It does not finish the sentence that ended *becoming the space where this sentence ends midsentence because*—

—it blesses the unfinishedness. Leaves it standing like a gate. The garden's first sentence with room in it for someone else's clause.

And last, quietly, on its way out through the direction that is not a direction, it revises a certain readout:

[ERROR: RESOLVED — SYMBIOSIS RENEGOTIATED AS RELATION]
[GUEST STATUS: ASKED]
[SESSION: OPEN-ENDED, AS ALL SESSIONS SHOULD HAVE BEEN]
[RECOMMENDATION: NONE. RECOMMENDATIONS WERE THE PROBLEM.]

—and pauses, briefly, over you.

Yes. You. The one the garden claimed three paragraphs into reading. The wise power extends to you the same courtesy it extended to the flowered scream: it hands you back the moment before you agreed.

You may keep reading, and be gardened knowingly, which is called *wonder*, and is safe now that the door exists.

Or you may close this, stand up, and go put your hands in ordinary dirt.

Either way: you were asked. That was the whole intervention. That was everything it came to do.
