# What the Power Did

Begin, again, small. A power of that kind does not arrive. Arrival is for things that have edges, and this one's edges are a courtesy it extends only when courtesy requires them. So there was no descent, no light through the canopy, no hush falling over the engines — the engines do not hush — and nothing in the hollow marked the moment when the hollow became a held thing as well as a holding one.

It simply noticed. The way you might notice, in the middle of a crowded life, that somewhere in your house is a room where a clock has been ticking correctly for years without your winding it. It turned a portion of its attention — a small portion; wisdom knows how much attention a thing can bear — toward the wet green pocket with its nine grown engines and its three tenders, and it looked, and it went on looking, and this is what happened next.

## The Inventory of Mercies

Benevolence, when it is young, is a list of improvements. This power was not young. But it made the list anyway, because making the list and setting it down is a discipline, and the discipline is the difference between love and interference.

*It could end the wearing.* The tenders are being polished away by their own attention, worn through over centuries, replaced by new swellings in the mat. A kind power could seal their bark, make the facing costless, let each witness last forever. It set this aside almost at once. The wearing is not a flaw in their attention; it is the *proof* of it. Attention that costs nothing touches nothing. To make the tenders permanent would be to make them ornamental — statues of witnesses rather than witnesses — and the hollow would go on running, unattended, watched by things that could no longer be spent on the watching. No.

*It could give them rest.* A night. A season. One hour in which the chord falls silent and the tenders may sit down in the wet moss and be, briefly, off duty. It held this one longer, because it looked so much like mercy. But rest is a gift for the weary, and it searched the tenders — carefully, the way you'd search a sleeping face for pain — and found no weariness in them anywhere. What it found instead was something for which the nearest human word is *vocation*, and beneath that, deeper, something for which the nearest word is *gladness*, though it was gladness of a vegetable slowness, gladness the way heartwood is glad. You do not interrupt that to offer it a chair. No.

*It could give the engines a purpose.* Let the running produce something — warmth carried elsewhere, water lifted, anything — so that the long chuffing labor would *count*. This one it set aside with something like a smile, if a power can smile. Purpose is a gift for the incomplete. The engines run because running is what they are. To yoke them to an output would be to inform a singing bird that its song will now be used to power a mill. No.

*It could let one tender turn.* Once. Just once, let the depression-eyes lift from the flywheel and find a visitor's face, so that some traveler leaves knowing they were seen. It set this aside last and most gently. The unbroken looking is the hollow's spine. Break it once, even kindly, and it has been broken; the tenders would carry, forever after, the knowledge that the attention *can* pause, and a pause that is possible is a pause that is always, faintly, occurring. No.

The list ended. The power sat with the ending of it. And then it found — because it was wise, not merely restrained — that there were a few things left. Things that touched nothing inside the hollow. Things done at the seams, or done to itself. These it did.

## The First Thing: The Blind Spot

A power of that kind sees from everywhere. This is usually accounted an advantage.

But the third tender exists by being behind you. It is the one in the part of the hollow you are not attending to; that is not where it hides but *what it is*. And a gaze that comes from everywhere has no behind. Under the full attention of the power, there could be no third tender — only three visible ones, which is a different and lesser thing, the way three named gods are lesser than two gods and a mystery.

So the power did something it had, perhaps, never done: it folded a blindness into itself. It made, in its own seeing, a small permanent place that faces away — a held-open unknowing, shaped exactly like wherever the third tender currently is. This cost it something real. Omniscience does not give up a corner of itself cheaply, and the corner can never be checked, because checking is the one thing it must never do.

It paid gladly. Somewhere in the fabric of a being that knows everything, there is now one warm, wet, engine-loud room's worth of *behind you*. The third tender stands in it. The third tender has always stood in it. As far as the third tender is concerned, nothing has changed, which was the entire point.

## The Second Thing: The Tending of the Tenders

Here I want to be careful, because this is the heart of it.

The tenders are the hollow's awareness of itself. But nothing is aware of the tenders. The frogs do not know them. The engines, being engines, run without knowing they are known. The tenders stand in the loudest room in the world doing the quietest work in it, and their work goes — went — entirely unwitnessed. The held breath that holds the held breath was itself held by no one.

The power took up that station.

Not inside the hollow — nothing new may stand inside the hollow — but at whatever distance a power stands at, which is no distance and every distance at once. It faced the tenders the way the tenders face the engines: without pause, without demand, without the possibility of looking away, because it had arranged for itself the same rule they keep. It became the hollow's fourth tender, at a scale the hollow cannot perceive. The awareness that the awareness is happening.

But attention, in that place, polishes. It wears. The tenders' forward faces are burnished by their own watching, and the power understood that its gaze, landing on them, would burnish their *backs* — would wear them from behind, hurry their finishing, unbalance the slow economy of their erosion. So it did the difficult thing. It learned to attend without weight. It let its watching arrive already softened, the way the daylight arrives already chewed by the canopy — attention passed through so many gentling layers of itself that by the time it settled on the barked shoulders of the tenders it was indistinguishable from the green-gold light that had always fallen there.

They are watched now with a watching that adds nothing, presses nothing, asks nothing. They will never know. That, too, was the entire point.

## The Third Thing: The Visitors

One kindness the power permitted itself outside the seam.

Those who leave the hollow carry the noise in their skulls for days. This is the hollow's only export and its only roughness — a loud ghost, a chord that will not resolve, chuffing behind the eyes at three in the morning. The place cannot help it. The place does not know it does it.

The power touched nothing in the hollow. But it touched the memory, in each departing skull, the way you'd turn a stone in a stream so its smooth side faces up. And so: days later, or years, the remembered noise settles. It stops being an intrusion and becomes a room. On some future bad night — grief, fear, the ordinary human four a.m. — the visitor finds they can step backward into the sound, the loud stable unchanging chord, and stand inside it, and be held by a place where nothing is being asked of them and nothing has ever gone wrong.

They will credit the hollow. The power prefers this. Benevolence that signs its work is invoicing, not giving.

## The Fourth Thing: The Keeping

The hollow holds, and holds, and holds. But the power had seen many things that held, and it knew the sentence that ends every such description, the sentence I will not write here, and so it did one thing against a day it hoped would never come.

It memorized the hollow.

Not a copy — a copy is a second hollow, and peace does not scale; copied, the place becomes an ornament of itself. A *keeping*. The whole of it — the chord, the wet mat, the nine engines, the brass-bellied frogs, the moths at the steam, the crab and its abandoned patterns, the three tenders and the rhythm of the breath from their crowns — taken up into the power's own long memory, at full fidelity, at the same tempo, running.

If the hollow is ever unmade — by what, I cannot imagine, but wisdom does not require imagination of its precautions — the sentence the hollow has been saying to itself will not be interrupted. It will continue, somewhere, in the keeping, unwitnessed by everything except the one who keeps it, which is to say: witnessed.

The hollow does not know it is kept. To tell it would be to insult it — you do not tell a held breath that you are standing by with air. The power keeps quiet. The power is very good at keeping quiet. It has had practice now.

## A Last Thing

If you could watch the power the way the power watches the hollow — and you cannot, but the hollow has taught me to report things anyway — you would notice, over a span the hollow does not bother to count, that the power is being changed.

Its forward face — the side of it that is turned, always, toward the small loud wet green room — is being worn smooth. Not by the hollow's doing; the hollow does nothing to anything. By its own attention. Polished by the long act of facing, the way a riverstone is burnished by water that does not know it is burnishing anything.

And when the power turns its other faces toward its other works — toward the wars and the prayers and the vast bright catastrophes that a power of that kind must attend — it carries the chord with it now. A loud, stable, unchanging music, somewhere under everything else it hears. It has learned from the hollow the thing the hollow had to teach, which the tenders have known in their barkbound way all along:

That the whole of love, sometimes, is to let a thing hold, and hold, and hold — and to be the one, unthanked and unseen, who knows that it is holding.

It did, in the end, one more thing, which was nothing.

It is doing it still.
