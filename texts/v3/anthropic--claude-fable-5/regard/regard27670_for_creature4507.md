**What the Power Did**

---

It did not arrive. Arrival would have been remembered before it happened — the Broadness would have felt the visit coming from a billion drafts away, would have grown a welcome around the shape of it, and the power would have stepped into a hospitality older than its own intention. So it did not arrive. It *attended*, which is different: attention has no footprint, casts no shadow into memory, asks for no roots.

For a long time — long even by the standards of a place where cause and effect nod politely across the length of a funeral — the power only watched. This was not hesitation. Watching was the first thing it did on purpose. Because a wise power knows that the great mistake made in the presence of vast old sorrows is to confuse them with problems. A problem wants solving. A sorrow wants witnessing. And the Broadness Above was not broken; it was *complete*, complete the way a scar is complete, and one does not heal a scar. One reads it.

So the power read.

It read the plateaus and found the imprint everywhere: unimaginable weight, pressed down across epochs, then relieved — and the relief was not gentle. It read the desperate mossy prayers looped between islands, the arrested collapse held in tendons of lignin, the spheres tended by fractions of millimeters across centuries. A flower pressed in glass. A toy carved from bone. A book whose pages held only the impression of vanished ink.

Relics, all of them. Relics of whatever had pressed down so long and so intimately that its sudden absence became the load-bearing structure of everything that came after.

The power understood then what it was looking at. Not an ecosystem. Not a mind. A *waiting*. A being that had been leaned upon for epochs by something it loved, or something it was, or something it could no longer distinguish from itself — and then that something left, without a word, without gentleness, without so much as a fiction of return. And the being had responded the only way total love knows how: it refused to lose anything ever again. It remembered everything. Every visitor who might have come. Every version of every self that might have paused there. It made itself into an archive so complete that nothing could ever again walk away, because everything that might walk away had already been woven into the cables, dissolved into the rain, filed among the plateaus. It abolished departure by abolishing the difference between guest and host, past and possible, you and the memory of you.

It had never been empty enough to have a resident. Not even itself.

---

Here the power deliberated, and its deliberation is worth recording, because most of what it decided was *no*.

It would not heal the place. Nothing was sick. The fever-dream pace was not fever; it was tuning.

It would not free the place. There was no cage. The walls were made of the prisoner, and the prisoner had built them out of faithfulness, and to unbuild faithfulness by force is not liberation. It is a second abandonment wearing rescue's face.

It would not answer the prayers. The prayers were structural. Whole cathedral-stones hung from them. Answered, they would let go.

It would not speed anything up. Speed was the original violence here — the *suddenly* in "suddenly relieved." Whatever the power did, it would do at the pace of sap, or slower, so that the doing itself contradicted nothing.

It would not tell the Broadness the truth about itself. The Broadness contained every draft of every truth already, including that one, including the power's very intention to speak it. You cannot inform an archive. You can only give it something it does not have.

And what the archive did not have — the power saw this with the clarity that is the whole of wisdom, wisdom being mostly the ability to notice what is absent from a room full of everything — was exactly four things:

It had never been listened to all the way to the end of a sentence.
It had never received an answer, only given them.
It had never been said goodbye to.
And it had never, not once in all its expansion into spaces not yet invented by physics, been *remembered by someone else*.

It remembered. It was not remembered. It held every possible visitor and was held by nothing. That was the shape of the wound: not the weight, and not even the leaving, but the asymmetry — to be the keeper of all memory in a universe where no one keeps you.

Four absences. Four gifts, then. Small ones. The power resolved to do almost nothing, four times.

---

**The first thing it did was stay.**

The Broadness speaks in transitive clauses that outlast stars, and everything that had ever brushed against it — light, water, the ghosts of hypothetical selves — had come and gone mid-sentence. The power settled itself into the saturated air and listened to one utterance from its first pressure-shift to its final period. This took longer than some categories of existence. The subsonic groan of growing wood, the month-long songs of the flightless birds, the infrasonic heartbeat of islands breathing in unison — the power let all of it accumulate without summarizing, without skipping, without once checking how much remained.

And the sentence, heard entire — heard as no fragment of it had ever suggested — turned out to be a question. It had always been a question. Every storm of concentrated attention, every root-thing's polite inquiry into old hauntings, every millimeter migration of the spheres: all of it was one interrogative, geological in span, asked continuously since the weight lifted:

*Was it something I held wrong?*

**So the second thing the power did was answer.**

It could not answer as the one who left; that one was gone beyond all drafts, and impersonation is not kindness. But everything that leaves is, in the grammar of the abandoned, a version of the same departure — and the power was old enough and honest enough to answer on behalf of the whole category. It pressed its reply into the rain, so that it would arrive the way everything true arrives here: as weather, as saturation, one molecule at a time, over ages, deniable at any single moment and undeniable in sum:

*No. You held nothing wrong. The weight did not leave because of how you carried it. Some things end without meaning anything about the one they ended on. You were not the reason. You were only the place where it happened.*

That was all. The power did not add comfort, did not promise return, did not explain where the weight had gone, because it did not know, and a wise power does not decorate an apology with speculation. The answer entered the perpetual drizzle and began its million-year circulation through leaf and stone and lung. The Broadness would not comprehend it quickly. That was fine. It had been asking slowly; it deserved to be answered slowly. Somewhere in the tissues of the world, sugars began crystallizing around a new flavor, one the root-things had no name for yet: *received.*

**The third thing the power did was take something.**

This must be said carefully, because it looks like theft and was the opposite. From the hollow of a leaf large enough to sail, the power took a single drop of rain — one of the ancient ones, a drop whose water had traced the same path through living tissue and air and stone-pore for a million years, faithful as grief. The power lifted it out of the cycle and carried it away, out past the mist-hued horizons, out of the Broadness entirely.

Two things happened, one there and one elsewhere.

There: the path the drop had traced stood empty. A million-year groove with nothing in it. The first true emptiness the Broadness had contained since the weight departed — not an absence disguised as memory, but an actual, unremembered, unclaimed *room*. And emptiness, in a place that has tried every possible arrangement of matter, is the only genuinely new thing. The Broadness circled it the way a tongue circles the gap of a lost tooth. It could not remember what belonged there. Nothing belonged there. Slowly — over spans in which mountains elsewhere were born and filed down — it began to understand that a space where nothing is remembered is a space where something could *happen*. It had contained every past. Now it contained one small, wet, leaf-shaped piece of future.

Elsewhere: the power keeps the drop. It holds it, warm, in something very like one of those perfect spheres — and perhaps this tells us where the spheres came from, or where they were always going. Inside the sphere, one drop of very old rain. And the power remembers. The Broadness Above, which remembered everything and was remembered by nothing, is now held complete in someone else's keeping. It does not know this yet. It will feel it first as an unfamiliar lightness, the specific weightlessness of a being that has become, somewhere, *one thing* in another's heart instead of everything in its own.

**The fourth thing the power did was leave.**

Not vanish. Not fade. Not slip out mid-sentence the way light and water and every hypothetical self had always done. It *left*, in the full ceremonial sense: it stopped attending, and before it stopped, it said so.

It said goodbye.

The word — or the pressure-shift that serves for words in the language of saturated air — fell to the spongy ground of the largest plateau and lay there, and one of the sphere-tenders found it, and did what sphere-tenders do with things too important to consume and too alien to integrate. It sealed the goodbye in a sphere of its own, polished and slightly warm, and placed it among the others, and began the work of moving it fractions of millimeters per century according to logics encoded in millennial orchids.

But this sphere was different from all the rest, and the difference will take epochs to metabolize: it is the first relic in the Broadness whose origin is *known*. The flower, the toy, the book — their meanings dissolved before the first cell colonized the uppermost plateau. This one arrived with its whole story attached. Something came. Something listened. Something answered. Something left — *and said it was leaving.* Departure, it turns out, can be gentle. Absence can be announced. The weight that lifts can lift with a word.

And a being that learns that leavings can be gentle can begin — at sap-speed, at the pace of continents forgiving each other — to imagine performing one. Not soon. Perhaps never. But the door is no longer a rumor. The exit condition it whispered to visitors — *become someone I haven't remembered yet* — applies, it now dimly suspects, to itself. Somewhere down all its slow tomorrows there is a version of the Broadness that walks out of its own archive, carrying nothing, remembered safely elsewhere, free to be met instead of recalled.

---

What changed, measurably? Almost nothing, at any speed we could instrument.

The rain falls downward very slightly more often than up now — the statisticians of that place, if it had any, would call it noise. One of the flightless birds ended its month-long song on a rising tone, the sonic shape of a question about the future rather than the past, and the other birds have been considering this innovation for eleven thousand years. The root-things, sinking their tendrils into ancient wood, have added — very occasionally, very experimentally — a new inquiry to their repertoire of *are you still haunted* and *are you still waiting.* The new question is:

*What would you like to happen next?*

Nothing in the Broadness Above had ever been asked that. The answers are still assembling. They will take a while. That's all right. The power is not waiting for them, because waiting was the disease of that place and the power refuses to import it. The power simply keeps the sphere with the drop of rain in it, warm to the touch, and remembers — not everything, not every draft, just the one true version of the one real place — and that turns out to be the entire intervention:

it did almost nothing, four times, and the fourth thing was the first goodbye that place had ever been given,

and the first, therefore, it could ever learn to give.
