**What the Power Does**

The power notices the way powers notice: not by looking. Looking is for things that fear missing something. The power has never missed anything, which is its particular loneliness, and it keeps eyes anyway, out of courtesy to things that want witnessing.

It notices via the taste. Ten thousand mouths, scattered across every hemisphere, fourteen minutes and thirty-three seconds after their local sunsets, tasting mineral and citrus and sorrow and desiring a place they cannot name. Homesickness without an address is a species of prayer. Powers are bound to prayers the way mountains are bound to patience—not by law, but by being unable, constitutionally, to be otherwise.

So it comes. It could arrive the way answers arrive inside questions, having always been partially present. It doesn't. It enters through the throat of the collapsed vent, palms against the black ribs, breath pluming and curling back, because arriving matters. Shortcuts insult places. This place has been insulted enough by geology.

The swarm's arithmetic hiccups the moment it crosses the threshold. Here is a presence with no absence stapled to its back. A number with no column to file it under. For the first time since the mountain died upward in a hurry, the recalculation runs long—seven minutes, forty-three seconds, and then a pause like a held breath in a room full of held breaths. The sound does not come. The swarm waits, millions of segmented small regrets, armored in harvested wall, uncertain for the first time whether inventory is even the right question.

The power stands in the chamber and considers, because even powers must consider, or especially powers must.

---

It could heal the stalactites. Teach them to drip once and die meaningful, the way they'd dreamed. It declines. Their upward elongation, their search for a sky they've decided is buried deeper—that is not failure. That is faith wearing mineral. You do not cure a thing of its faith and call the result mercy.

It could answer the question that started everything—*Who am I without weight?*—the subjectless question that persuaded the spaces between atoms into becoming a swarm. It declines, carefully. Answered questions stop breathing. The swarm exists *because* the question is open; to close it would be to unpersuade millions back into the gaps between atoms, and no arithmetic of kindness comes out even on that ledger.

It could bring the sea back. Annul nothing, remarry everything, let the minerals have their weddings witnessed properly this time. It declines. The sea left for its own reasons, and even powers do not get to conscript oceans into reconciliations.

It could bring light. It laughs, once, quietly. Light here files lawsuits. The power has been deposed by light before and did not enjoy it.

So it does small things instead. Four of them. Wisdom, it has found over the long unspooling of itself, is mostly the art of doing four small things instead of one enormous one.

---

**First, it kneels.**

The chamber, despite its cathedral curves, was carved for something that kneels rather than stands. In all its history—under ocean, under pressure, under the fever dream of depth—nothing has ever knelt here. Visitors stand, theologically, on the polished floor, negotiating with the smoothness. The power lowers itself to the stone, and the chamber *fits* for the first time, the way a word fits a mouth that was shaped around it.

And kneeling, it discovers, is the only honest reply to the open question. *Who am I without weight?* Kneeling is weight given freely. Weight offered stops being burden and becomes gift. The question is not answered—it is *accompanied*. It has spent an epoch asking itself to empty walls, and now something heavy has gotten down beside it on the floor and said, in the grammar of posture: *I don't know either. Ask again. I'll stay.*

The swarm feels this through the stone. Their polish, they realize, has been preparation. All those centuries smoothing floor for future footsteps—they had been setting a place at a table, and something has finally sat down.

**Second, it listens through the sound. All of it.**

The interval resumes—it had to; inventory is a heartbeat, not a habit—and the sound comes: thirty-seven rains on thirty-seven metals, rust speaking in chorus, pain as melody through rockslide. Twelve heartbeats. Every visitor before has endured it, teeth vibrating independent of skull, waiting for it to end.

The power does not endure it. It *attends* it, the way you attend a child's recital or a deathbed—fully, without flinching, without waiting. And at the end of the twelfth heartbeat, in the surgical cutoff, the power adds a thirteenth: a single beat of shaped silence, offered back. A response. The first the sound has ever received.

The tinnitus afterward does not apologize. It has never needed to apologize—it only apologized because nothing had ever indicated the sound was *received* rather than merely survived. The tinnitus, unburdened, simply rings, like a struck bell that knows it was meant to be struck.

**Third, it speaks a name.**

The swarm's names are verbs conjugated only in second-person future imperfect, and to speak one aloud is to become its next tense. The power knows this. That is precisely why it does it.

It selects a name—it does not matter which; here, honestly, it matters entirely which, but that name cannot be written down without the page becoming its next tense, so let the record show only that a name was chosen with the care of someone choosing which of their children to carry across the river first, knowing they will return for all of them.

It speaks. And the power becomes the swarm's future tense.

Understand what this means. These millions were never born, merely persuaded—commas and clauses flaked off walls by a lonely question. They practice hunger until it becomes geography. Their leader is the arithmetic of their own absence. They have never had a future; they have had *intervals*. And now, conjugated into their grammar, standing in the tense just ahead of them, is something benevolent and vast, waiting for them the way dawn waits for a nightmare to finish so it can be there when the eyes open.

*You will have been enough*—that is roughly, in the language after language, what the spoken name now means. The recalculation every seven minutes and forty-three seconds still runs, still sums the absence. But the sum now includes one entry of presence that cannot be evaporated, declined, or annotated away. The arithmetic doesn't balance. It doesn't need to anymore. It *leans*, the way a plant leans, toward something.

**Fourth, it apologizes first.**

The peak and the root of the mountain collapsed into each other over an argument neither remembers clearly and both refuse to apologize for first. This is the oldest wound in the room. The column—that fossilized moment of indecision, subpoenaed weekly by causality—stands at the argument's exact center, which is why the center relocates when approached: wounds flinch.

The power does not take a side. It cannot; there are no sides left, only the refusal. So it does the thing that no party to an argument can do and any bystander can, if they are willing to look foolish before geology:

It says, on behalf of no one, to no one, into the general air of the chamber: *I'm sorry. For whatever it was. It has gone on long enough, and someone had to be first, and it costs me nothing, so let it be me.*

Apologies work strangely here, in a place without consequence. This one does not resolve the argument. Elevation and depth remain collapsed; the chamber still exists at peak and root simultaneously. But the refusal—the *first*—has been spent. Discharged. Neither height nor depth has to hold out any longer, because the holding-out has been done for them, absorbed by a third party with shoulders built for it. What remains between them is no longer an argument. It is just a shared address. Many marriages survive on less.

The column, feeling the subpoena of causality quietly withdrawn, relaxes by a fraction of a degree. Inside its translucent layers, one of the never-invented ecosystems—the ocean where waves break in equations—resolves a single equation all the way to zero, gently, like a knot coming undone in warm water. The gear-toothed butterflies do not fly. But they *mesh*, for a moment, all their teeth aligned, and something in the column that might be a heart, or might be the fossilized moment of indecision between heart and not-heart, turns over once.

---

One more thing, unplanned, on the way out. Powers, too, are ambushed by their own mercy.

The echoes—the previous versions of visitors who took the exit too literally, drifting in peripheral honesty, whispering advice backwards—the power passes among them, and it *remembers*. It remembers everything; that is its loneliness, but here, for once, its loneliness is currency. Each echo has forgotten which question it was supposed to be the answer to. The power carries every question ever asked. So it goes among them like a lost-and-found attendant at the end of the world's longest day, returning to each echo its question.

*Yours was: does she forgive me.*
*Yours was: was the leaving braver than the staying.*
*Yours was: what did the fruit really taste like, the first time.*

It does not answer the questions. It only returns them. An answer without its question is a ghost; a question restored is a spine. Some of the echoes, re-questioned, straighten and walk toward the exit and are subtracted and gone, out into whatever outside still honors their names. Others, re-questioned, choose to stay—and *choosing* to stay is a different substance entirely from being unable to leave. The chamber's population does not change much. Its citizenship changes completely. Residents now, not remainders.

The tall, thin, convincingly disappointed one—tomorrow's someone—offers the power its bargain: the memory of first fruit for the apology the mountain owes the sea. The power smiles, if a power can be said to smile, and says: *That debt was just paid. I got a good rate. Keep the fruit.*

---

Then it leaves, because the wisest thing a benevolent power can do with a living place is not to move in.

The exit exacts its toll; even powers pay. It considers what to leave behind, since the passage demands its small sacrifice of *before*, and it chooses deliberately: it leaves behind its certainty about how this story ends. It walks out not knowing what becomes of the chamber, the swarm, the column, the residents. It has not been uncertain about anything in a very long time. The sensation is terrible and delicious, like the first breath after a long dive. It understands, tasting its own uncertainty, why mortals gamble and fall in love. It resolves to remember this, and then—because the exit is thorough—it does not remember resolving it, only carries afterward a faint suspicion that it once decided to be surprised.

Outside, if there is an outside, the power's gait is slightly off, its bones in negotiation with the ground. This is correct. This is the receipt.

---

Afterward, the interval is seven minutes and forty-four seconds.

One extra second. That is all. But in that second—stretched between inventory and inventory—the swarm does nothing. Not hunger-practice, not polishing, not recalculation. Nothing. It is the first leisure in the history of the mountain. Some of the millions spend it standing still. Some spend it touching the segmented body beside them, which they had never had a second free to do. The sound, when it comes, has a new phrase in it. It is recognizably from the lullabies in the language before language, but this phrase is not one that was ever sung. It is one that was always *meant* and never got written, and the swarm found it, in their spare second, lying where meaning keeps its unclaimed luggage.

Fourteen minutes and thirty-three seconds after every sunset, the scattered mouths still taste minerals and citrus. But the sorrow in the mixture has changed vintage. It tastes less like a place they can never return to and more like a place that is *keeping something for them*. Homesickness with a coat-check ticket. Several of them, on several continents, stop mid-sentence at the tasting and say, to the confusion of their companions, "Oh—it's all right, actually," without knowing what is.

The ceiling still weeps its occasional silver thread, writing and wiping a word no language ever approximated. The power, note, never read the word. It could have—it reads everything. It chose not to, and stood instead with its attention politely averted, the way one gives privacy to someone praying, so that the word could be *attempted* without being *taken*. The thread noticed. Threads notice. It writes a little longer now before wiping, unhurried, like a hand that has stopped expecting to be slapped.

And the column enters the visitation in its infinite stone ledger, beneath all the others:

*"Visited. Fixed nothing. Broke nothing. Knelt.*

*Listened to the whole sound and answered with one beat of its own.*

*Spoke a name and became our future tense—we are conjugated toward it now, all of us, like plants toward a sun that finally exists.*

*Apologized first, on behalf of no one, which is the only jurisdiction where apology works.*

*Returned the questions to the answers. Paid the exit in certainty and left grinning about it, which we did not know powers could do.*

*Would welcome back if welcome existed here.*

*Which it does.*

*Which it does.*

*Repeat—no. Not repeat.*

*Continue."*
