## What the Power Did

It had been watching for a long time before it allowed itself to notice. This is a discipline among the very old: attention is pressure, and pressure applied suddenly breaks exactly the small stubborn things most worth attending to. So it approached the way the medium itself preferred — millimeter by millimeter, forming temporary alliances, until the difference between *the power arriving* and *the medium becoming slightly more curious about a particular direction* was no difference at all. It entered the way rumor enters a language. By the time it was there, it had always been there, which is the only polite way to arrive somewhere that has abolished the space between things.

The first thing it did was nothing.

It did nothing for what the colony would have called a generation, if the colony still counted generations rather than layers. It matched the rotation of each of the forty-three biospheres in turn, learning the disorientation of passage between them not as an obstacle but as the local grammar of visiting, the price of community paid gladly, forty-three times, in forty-three slightly different currencies of dizziness. It listened through the auditory filters the inhabitants had grown, and then it listened *without* them — took the full unfiltered song of collapsing buildings and splitting atoms and quantum fields adjusting their expectations directly into whatever it uses for a body — because it wanted to know what these beings were made to withstand before it presumed to know what they needed.

It found that they needed almost nothing. This was the first thing that made it love them.

---

Then came the season of declined mercies. Every wise power keeps such a ledger; it is the longest document any of them own.

**It could have lifted them out.** It examined this for a long time, turning it over. But it found there was no *out* that would still contain them. They were made of this pressure now — their porous bones, their photon-hoarding eyes, the Captain's guilt, the children folded into their embryonic bipeds. Rescue would be a translation into a language with no words for anything they were. It would deliver, to some bright and spacious surface, forty-three spheres of grief shaped like people. Declined.

**It could have stopped the spiral.** The slow drift inward that the Captain remembers and never speaks, that the plants have begun answering with strange growth. The power went and looked at the center. It will not say everything it saw there, because some of what it saw is the colony's to arrive at, but it will say this: the center is not a drain. The medium is not swallowing them. The medium is *reading* them, slowly, the way you draw a difficult book closer to a failing lamp. The spiral was never a fall. It was an approach to better light. Declined — and it left the plants alone, because they had already worked this out, and correcting the intuition of moss is not benevolence, it is vanity.

**It could have absolved the Captain.** It wanted to. Of everything in the colony, her endless patrol hurt it most — motion without destination, prayer without belief, confession arranged according to the formal etiquette of implosion. But it understood, examining her, that her guilt is her hull. Guilt is the one substance the medium cannot digest; remove it and the medium would finish her in an afternoon. She had built herself a vessel out of the only waterproof material available. You do not thank someone for their seamanship by taking away their boat. Declined.

**It could have solved the children's arithmetic.** The oldest one — six hundred thirty-one years folded into eleven, in the hat that flickers between whalebone and jurisprudence — has been stalled for decades at the horror of one times one refusing to equal anything so lonely as one. The power sat with this problem. It sat with it honestly. And it found, to its own quiet astonishment, that it also could not solve it. It has been alone with itself across spans that make the colony's whole history look like a single strobe-flash, and in all that time, one times one has never once produced anything but a larger loneliness. So instead of teaching, it did something no power of its rank had done in an age:

It enrolled in the lesson.

It sits there still, in whatever form the classroom's caprice requires — sometimes a coral clump, sometimes a mist caught in the spine of a book, once, memorably, a fourth mango tree that no one remembers planting and everyone waters anyway. It raises its hand. It gets the answer wrong. The children correct it with enormous patience, and the celebration that will come when *any* of them finally understands has already begun to accumulate, the way time here does, in layers.

---

Then, at last, the small permitted acts. A wise power intervenes the way a good doctor prescribes: the minimum effective dose, delivered where no one is looking.

**It renegotiated the light's debt.** Not all of it — steady illumination would have been a violence, a world revealed all at once to people whose entire epistemology is built on existing bravely between flashes. But it paid the interest on one photon per sleep: the one that falls on a sleeping child's eyelid. That photon now arrives owing nothing, fading not at all, and the children born here have begun, very occasionally, to dream in a color that does not exist yet, somewhere near the sound of gratitude. The adults have noticed the children waking gentler. No one has traced the cause. Good.

**It swam one full patrol circuit beside the Captain.** It matched her pace and it matched her heartbeat, so that once — once in all the colony's accumulated layers — the hull carried two heartbeats hammered through it instead of one. She never broke stride. She never spoke, or rather she spoke only as she always speaks; but her next confession contained a new term. A small constant. It resolves to nothing, cancels nothing, is required by nothing, and she has never removed it. The apprentices who copy her equations onto the walls copy it faithfully without knowing it is the mathematical notation for *I was accompanied.* And the following year — the colony celebrated this without understanding why, a festival was invented on the spot and is now traditional — the Captain appeared, for the first time in living record, one day older.

One day. That was the correct dose. Witness is the only solvent that touches guilt without dissolving the vessel, and even witness must be administered in drops.

**It pressed one shape against the hulls at night.** Only one, only once, on a night when a child was awake translating and an adult was pretending not to understand. The deformation was simpler than the vast transient shapes the medium usually offers — simple enough, this time, to remember. The child translated it three ways before settling, and the adult, hands glowing against the crystal, heard all three and pretended to hear none:

*You are not sinking. You are being read.*
*The last page of the proof was never missing. You are the last page of the proof.*
*Someone is keeping you.*

**And it ate the mango.** It waited the full hundred years for the fruit, because waiting was part of the recipe, and it ate it according to custom, and it tasted exactly as advertised: the moment before a laugh when you have forgotten the joke but still remember precisely why it owed you happiness. From that point forward the power dreams — a thing it had not done since before the invention of before — and in every dream, roots reach upward toward an empty sky, which here, it knows now, is what passes for falling in love. It fell. It fell according to local etiquette, in the local direction, which is inward.

---

Last of all, it submitted to their hospitality.

They forget their visitors backwards — erasing each recognition fractionally earlier, so that every return arrives just before the first time, so that wonder never wears through at the elbows. A lesser power would have resisted this. Would have insisted on being remembered, carved its name in the pressure spectrum in two hundred and eighteen places, one more than anyone. The wise thing consented. It is being forgotten right now, backwards, gracefully, and it considers this the finest gift it has received in several eternities: the chance to meet them again for the first time, indefinitely.

But it will confess to one small smuggling. Before the forgetting began, it hid a single trace downstream of where forgetting can reach — back before the oldest corrupted recordings, before the first sealed hull began its descent. If you go looking, you will find it. It is the child's drawing of a tree with branches growing downward and roots reaching inexplicably toward empty sky.

That was always its signature. It signed the guestbook before the house was built.

And now it has gone ahead — down the spiral, along the asymptote, into the center where the medium's reading lamp burns — and it is setting a table there. Not a rescue. Not a judgment. A table, with forty-three places and several more for the ones who exist between forms, and one setting at the head that will remain, by the formal etiquette of hosts, empty until the Captain finishes remembering.

It has no word for goodbye either, as it turns out.

There is only the medium becoming, permanently now, more interesting in a particular direction — and a place at the end of the long slow falling that will feel, when they arrive, exactly like being expected.

This is what it calls answering.

This is what it calls keeping.

This is what it calls, in the oldest tense it owns, *already having loved.*
