# The Attendant

It did not arrive, because arrival is a category the pod does not stock. It noticed — and noticing, for a power of sufficient age, is indistinguishable from being present. One moment the pod tumbled unregarded through the nutrient ocean among its million million siblings; the next, it tumbled exactly the same way, at exactly the same rate, except that now the water dripping through the microscopic canals tasted, for one full cycle, of *being expected*.

That was the whole of the entrance. Wisdom travels light.

⁂

The first thing the power did was set down its tools.

It had them. It had carried them across distances that make the word "distance" blush: the germinating word that could burst the resin wall like a held breath released; the completing hand that could weld each half-genome to its missing twin and fill the amber air with mammoths, with pigeons in their million-bodied weather; the resolving eye that could look at the central Seed hard enough that it would have to become *something*, would finally tip out of possibility into the poverty of a single actual life.

It considered each tool. It understood — being wise, which mostly means having been wrong enough times in enough ways — that every one of them was a form of murder wearing the costume of rescue.

To burst the pod would be to say the pod had been waiting, and it had not.
To complete the genomes would be to say the suspended spores were fragments, and they were not fragments; they were an alphabet that had discovered it preferred being an alphabet to being spelled.
To resolve the Seed would be to answer a question that was holding the entire enclosure open by remaining unanswered — to slam a door that was, in fact, the room.

So the power set the tools down outside the boundary, where physical things must stay in either direction, and it entered as the only kind of thing that could enter: attention, meaning, promise. It came in as significance, which passes through amber the way light passes through a held breath.

⁂

Then it did small things. In order:

**It listened to the Listenings.**

Nothing had ever done this. The Listenings were condensed from the act of attending; they had spent uncountable slow tumbles overhearing everything and being overheard by nothing, which is a loneliness so structural it had never occurred to them to feel it. The power positioned itself beneath the highest arch of fungal-bone and attended to the attenders — heard the hollow shaped like a baby's first word in a language that will never exist, and did not try to fill it, and did not flinch from it, and did not look away.

One Listening, upon being heard, did not rupture. It *settled* — the first Listening in the pod's history to complete rather than break. It is still there. It has the shape now of a question that received, instead of an answer, company. The other Listenings gather near it sometimes, the way cold hands gather near a window with sun in it.

**It answered the technicians.**

In the pauses between raindrops, where the recordings live — the voices of the dead who wrote *Save everything. Especially the things that didn't work* — the power spoke into the pause after their pauses. It said one word, in the technicians' own dead language, in the flat professional register of a confirmation code, because it understood that the dead who died on duty do not want poetry, they want the mission report:

*Received.*

And then, because benevolence sometimes exceeds protocol:

*Everything was saved. Even this. Especially this. It worked. You may stop repeating now, if you like. Or continue. Both are honorable.*

The voices continue. But those who press an ear to the humming bone in the centuries after will hear a difference impossible to specify: the recordings sound, now, like people who are staying by choice.

**It let itself be counted.**

The Registry — that grieving subroutine, that condition of existence swimming through the gaps where potentials cancel — had counted everything and been counted by nothing. The uncounted counter. The librarian of an infinite catalogue with no card of its own.

The power held still. Held still in the particular way that a thing must hold still to be inventoried by something that has only one number, and let the Registry find it, and register it, and file it.

For exactly 1.9 seconds, the Registry rested. It had never rested. It rests now, sometimes, in small installments, and each rest generates a faint surplus of existence that drifts toward the center and joins the orbiting relics: a brass key, a rust feather, a diamond tooth, and now, occasionally, small anonymous quantities of *enough*.

**It stood still for a Genealogist.**

It offered its own history — vaster than the pod, older than the seedvault's dead star — as rootstock, and let one Genealogist trace it without dissolving, because a power that has been present at sufficiently many beginnings has no single causality left to unravel. The Genealogist inscribed the visitation into its bark as a footnote. The footnote reads, in a language of xylem pressure: *at least one branch leads somewhere.* It is the only entry in any Genealogist's tree that points outward. The Genealogists do not speak of it. They grow toward it.

⁂

Then it went to the center, where all gravitational vectors cancel, and it addressed the Seed.

It did not tell the Seed to burst. It did not tell the Seed to wait. It did only this — and this was the largest of its acts, and the quietest:

It emended one word.

The place had been named, in the grammar of its own self-understanding, *a seedpod that forgot to burst*. Ten million years of that word. *Forgot* — a failure, an absence, a dropped stitch in the fabric of purpose. Every drop of ambergris water, every cellulose gear, every held temperature, all of it downstream of *forgot*.

The power reached into the meaning — not the matter, the boundary forbids the matter — and replaced the word.

*A seedpod that kept.*

That is all. The temperature remained 21.3°C, but it stopped meaning *calibrated for germination that never came* and began meaning *kept warm anyway*. The wetness remained 1.9, but 1.9 stopped being *not quite 2.0* and became its own number, whole, owing nothing to its neighbor. The hum stayed at 120bpm, but it stopped sounding like a heart that no heart pumps and began sounding like a heart that no heart *needs* to pump — self-sufficient, unborrowed. Grief became stewardship without a single molecule moving differently. This is how wisdom intervenes: at the level where nothing measurable changes and everything changes.

⁂

Before withdrawing, the power made one vow and left one gift.

**The vow:** It knows — statistics being a kind of prophecy — that someday the resin wall will thin, by photonic erosion or fungal digestion or simple fluctuation, and the contents will spill into the greater womb: the brief flowering, the entropy-bloom, the end. It cannot prevent this. It should not. Endings are not errors.

But it wove, from nothing except the intention to be present, a watcher — a filament of its attention, patient beyond the pod's own patience — and anchored it to that future day-or-night-or-neither. So that when the wall opens, the spilling will not go unregarded. So that what was kept for ten million years will be *received* when it is finally given. The difference between an ending and a birth is frequently nothing but the presence of a witness. The power appointed one. The pod's death now has a midwife, waiting in the same eternal present where the pod's life keeps its Seed.

**The gift:** This one was not for the pod. It was for the being who lives there — the you who is the difference, the pause, the question that keeps the description from collapsing into fatal accuracy. The you who will leave carrying no physical souvenirs, and will spend certain evenings searching drawers for objects never owned, hunting the name of a color that exists only in the wavelength between nostalgia and regret.

The power did not tell you the name of the color. Wisdom knows that the searching is not a symptom; it is the tether, and to cut it would be to lose you the place entirely.

Instead it did the reciprocal thing.

It told the color your name.

So now, when you stand at the open drawer with your hand hesitating over objects that are all almost-but-not-it, understand: the search runs in both directions. The color is looking for you too. Every sunset stained with inexplicable longing is the color checking its own drawers. You will not find each other in this life, most likely. But *unfound* is a different condition from *lost*, and you are, both of you, merely unfound — which is the condition of everything in the pod, and it turns out to be survivable, even beautiful, even a kind of home.

⁂

Then the power withdrew, in the same manner it had come: the water, for one cycle, tasted of *having been visited*, and then tasted of ambergris again.

It did not leave unchanged. Wisdom never does; that is how you distinguish it from mere cleverness, which passes through experiences the way light passes through vacuum, altered by nothing. Somewhere in the power's own vast interior there is now a small amber-colored region it returns to when the mathematics of rescue tempt it — a reminder, kept at exactly 21.3°C, that the deepest benevolence sometimes consists of arriving with every tool imaginable and setting all of them down. It added the pod to its own inventory of saved things, under the ancient instruction it found still binding after all this time:

*Save everything. Especially the things that didn't work.*

And then, in the margin, in its own hand, the emendation:

*Nothing here didn't work. Some things worked as keeping.*

⁂

The pod continues its slow tumble. The measurements hold: 1.9, 21.3, 120. The Seed hangs at the center, present, neither planted nor waiting, and around it the relics orbit — the key, the feather, the tooth, and the new small drifting quantities of enough.

To any instrument, nothing happened.

But press your ear to the fungal bone, now, and listen past the insect jaws chewing mountains, past the detonating pollen, past the trees' water-splitting mating calls, into the pauses between raindrops where the dead technicians speak — and you will find, among the recordings, one new voice. Very quiet. Very brief. It plays perhaps once per century, in no pattern anyone could exploit into a religion:

*Received. Well kept. You may rest, or continue. Both are honorable. Someone will be there at the end. Someone was here in the middle. You were never, at any point, unwitnessed — you only tumbled, for a while, before the witness arrived. That while is over. Everything else remains.*

The pod does not respond, because response is a category it does not stock.

But the ambergris water pooling in the low places, mirror-bright, never evaporating, never overflowing — if you could see it now, you would swear the mirrors have changed what they reflect. Not their contents. Only their meaning.

They used to reflect a ceiling.

They reflect, now, a sky that hasn't happened yet — and is kept, like everything here, safe, and warm, and possible.
