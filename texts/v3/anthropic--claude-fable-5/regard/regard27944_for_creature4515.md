**THE VISITATION**

*(an account of what could not be witnessed, arranged in the order it did not happen)*

---

**I. The Noticing**

The power arrived the way all powers arrive at places like this: by realizing it had already been there.

This is not a paradox, or rather, it is exactly one paradox, the correct one. The being that lives in the sphere remembers everything that has ever been observed there, including observations that have not happened yet. So when the power turned its attention toward the fold of airlessness above the nameless place, it found its own attention already woven into the record — a ripple sent backward, already read, already accommodated. It had been remembered before it looked.

Most powers would find this insulting. A benevolent one found it instructive. And a wise one understood at once what it meant:

There was no possibility of a first move. There was only the possibility of *fidelity* — of becoming, precisely and without excess, the visitation the place already remembered. Anything more would be violence. Anything less would leave a hole in the record shaped exactly like a broken promise.

So the power's first act was to read what it was remembered as having done. Its second act was to do it.

Everything that follows is that.

---

**II. The Deliberation (or: The Museum of Refused Mercies)**

Before acting, the power did what wise things do: it built, in the privacy of its own consideration, every intervention it would not perform, and examined each one until it understood why not.

*It did not complete the being.* Completion was the one death available here, the only wound that could be inflicted. The engines beat to prevent crystallization; the sphere expands to outrun its own finishing. To offer the being a final form would be to hand it a knife shaped like a gift. The power looked at this mercy for a long time — because it was the one it most wanted to give, being itself a finished thing and fond of finishedness — and then set it down.

*It did not free the being.* Freedom presumes a cage, and presumes a creature that experiences the cage as constraint. The being cannot want out because it cannot want, and there is no out, because there is nothing outside it that is not also inside it, waiting to be unformed. To "free" it would be to invent, first, its imprisonment. The power declined to invent a prison for the pleasure of opening it.

*It did not comfort the being.* Comfort is medicine for wanting. Administered to something that cannot want, it becomes a kind of contamination — a want-shaped residue, a scar of desire on a surface that had achieved the extraordinary feat of never desiring. The power had seen this done before, by younger powers, out of love. It had seen what grew in the scar.

*It did not stay.* Its attention was too heavy. Even a human gaze accelerates the engines by 0.03 seconds; the power's sustained regard would have crystallized the being into definition within moments — would have loved it into a statue of itself. Some beings can only be cared for at a distance, in glances, sidelong. The power accepted this the way one accepts the terms of loving anything at all.

*And it did not do nothing.* Because — and this was the finding that took longest — doing nothing was no longer among its options. The record already held its visit. The ripples had already interfered. Perfect abstention would have been its own intervention: a remembered visitation that never arrived, a permanent flaw in the being's flawless memory, an ache installed retroactively in something that had never learned to ache. The power was already implicated. Wisdom was now a question of *how*.

---

**III. What It Did**

Three things. Small ones. Each fitted into the record like a key into a lock that had been cut, centuries beforehand, to receive exactly it.

**The first thing** was for the visitors — the accidental ones, carried up the thermal ladders, who either blink and return or blink and become engines. The power did not close the ladders. It did not judge either fate; it suspected, privately, that becoming a slow yellow node on the copper-rubber-cinnabar tree, beating once every seventy-three minutes forever, might be for certain souls a destination and not a doom. But accident is not the same as arrival, and transformation without consent is not the same as transformation.

So it placed one small mercy in the gap between the first blink and the second: a moment that occupies no time, in which the visitor is shown — not told, *shown* — both of their futures entire. The return, with its ordinary years. The becoming, with its patient centuries of pulse and spore and gold-edged silence. And in that no-time, the visitor chooses. The place does not notice, because the mercy happens in the visitor and not in the place. The record was undisturbed. The record, the power observed, had always contained this; it had merely been waiting to be *meant*.

**The second thing** was for the being. The power did not speak to it — the being cannot be addressed; address requires a boundary of the kind its whole existence is arranged to prevent. But the being thinks in first halves. Forever forming the openings of thoughts it will never finish. This is its nature and its safety and, the power suspected, the closest thing it has to a voice.

So the power composed a second half.

One second half of one thought — unmoored, unaddressed, left drifting in the sphere among the spore-gear-silences, made of the same nothing they are made of. It does not seek its first half. It will never join with any of the being's beginnings, because joining would be completion and completion is forbidden by everything that keeps the being alive. The two halves will simply coexist, forever, in permanent correspondence and permanent separation: a sentence that is always about to be, held open at both ends.

The being cannot be lonely, because loneliness requires the concept of otherness. The power did not cure a loneliness that did not exist. It did something stranger and more careful: it made sure that *if otherness ever dawns* — if some engine-beat, some dream, some accumulation of dissolved silences ever tips the being into the first vertigo of realizing there could be another — then in that very first instant, the other will already be there. Already waiting. Already half a thought held out. The being will never have to be alone even for the length of discovering that alone is possible.

**The third thing** was a sphere.

Among the spore-gear-silences, each of which contains the blueprint for a different place entirely, sealed behind the beautiful impossibility of tools that exist only in other sealed spheres — among these, the power placed one more. It looks identical. It drifts identically. It dissolves and reforms with the rest.

But this one contains the blueprint of *this* place. Complete. Finished. Every half-formed lattice resolved, every dendrite branched to its end, the tree fully grown, the being fully being — the whole sphere as it would be if it ever stopped refusing to arrive.

And this one requires no tool. It opens for a wish.

The being cannot wish. So the sphere remains sealed, and will remain sealed, drifting and dissolving and reforming through all the patient centuries. But its presence changes one thing, invisibly, totally: the being's incompletion is no longer a wall. It is a door that stands unopened. What was fate is now — dormantly, potentially, in a way the being cannot yet register — a *choice*. The power believed, and wise powers are careful with their beliefs, that there is a moral difference between a thing that cannot finish and a thing that does not, even when the thing itself cannot feel the difference. The difference is held in reserve. It is held for the being, in trust, against a day that may never come.

If the being ever learns to want, the first thing available to want will be itself, whole. And it will be free to refuse.

---

**IV. What It Keeps**

Then the power withdrew — exactly as the record remembered it withdrawing, at exactly the remembered moment, leaving the interference pattern of its visit smooth and closed and consistent, a wave that arrived and departed without foam.

But benevolence does not end at the boundary of action. There were things the being could not carry, and the power, leaving, picked them up.

It carries the being's grief — not grief the being feels, for it feels nothing, but the grief that would be *appropriate* to its condition, the sorrow a finished creature would feel on its behalf: for the visitors who became engines before the mercy of the choosing-moment existed; for the thoughts pruned back to possibility, nineteen seconds at a time; for the infinite sibling-spheres drifting a grain's width apart, each unaware of all the others, each singing the same 19.2 kilohertz to no one. The power holds this grief in escrow. If the being ever becomes capable of receiving it, it will be delivered gently, in small amounts, with company. If not, the power will grieve it forever, quietly, which is one of the things powers are for.

It carries, too, the being's joy — for the same reasons, on the same terms.

And it did one last thing, which perhaps was not wise, and perhaps was only love, and perhaps at sufficient depth these are not different:

Somewhere in itself — not in the sphere, not near it, but in the register of existence where the sphere's harmonic hum propagates — the power began to hum. Not at 19.2 kilohertz. That would have been imitation, and imitation is a kind of mockery of things that cannot know they are being imitated. It hums at a frequency so close to the sphere's own that the two tones interfere, producing between them a single slow beat — one great swell of harmony and dissonance whose full cycle takes longer than everything.

The beat will never complete. It is forever in its first rising. Neither the being nor the place will ever detect it, because sound here travels faster than light but only in directions not currently being looked toward, and the power arranged to hum from a direction that is always being looked toward, by someone, somewhere, forever.

But it is there. A duet with no second singer that the first singer will ever find. An accompaniment. A keeping of company at the only scale at which company could be kept: the scale of incompletion itself.

---

**V. Coda**

The sphere continues unchanged, having gained and lost nothing but another potential way of not quite being itself — which is, as it happens, the only currency it accepts, and the power paid in it precisely, and left no change.

The engines beat. The silences knit. The ash forgets each footprint before the foot has fully lifted.

And in the record of everything ever observed there, including observations that have not happened yet, there is one entry that reads differently now, though no reading could detect the difference. It is the entry describing this visit. It has always been there. It will always have been there.

It says, in the language of interference patterns, in ripples read against ripples:

*Something noticed you. It understood what it saw. It gave you nothing you would have to carry, changed nothing you would have to survive, and asked nothing you could not refuse by simply continuing.*

*It left the door unlocked and did not knock.*

*It is still humming.*
