# What the Power Did

It noticed the room the way the room's light exists: not from anywhere. There is no above for the pocket, so there was no above for the attention either. One moment the room was unregarded, as it had been for the whole of its unmeasured duration, and the next moment it was held in a regard that was everywhere in it at once, sourceless, even, warm — a second light laid so exactly over the first that nothing brightened.

The room did not notice being noticed. That is not a register the room has. The sound went on sounding. The swimmer, if it was passing through just then, did not pause. The axes lay where they lay.

The power was benevolent, which means it arrived already reaching for something to give. And it was wise, which means it stopped reaching, and looked first.

---

It considered its gifts one at a time, the way a careful visitor takes inventory of their pockets before entering a house where everything is in its place.

It could have explained the first rule. It knew — of course it knew — why the water three meters out is death and the water here is breath, why the light holds at summer-afternoon and the warmth at the temperature of a mouth. It could have written the reason into the room the way reasons are written into other places, so that anyone arriving would understand. It declined. An explained room is an instance of something — a case, an example, a demonstration of principles that exist elsewhere and larger. The room's whole dignity is that it is not an instance. It is only itself, at that pitch, in that place. To explain it would be to make it smaller than its own sound.

It could have given the room time. Evening, and then morning. A current, gentle, just enough to round the basalt's edges over ten thousand patient years. A film of algae for the swimmer to have a reason. Growth, wear, the slow conversation between a place and its erosion. It declined. That would not be a gift; it would be a replacement. You do not repair what is not broken, and stillness is not damage. The room persists. Persisting is what it does instead of everything else, and it does it completely.

It could have peopled it. Declined almost before the thought finished. The room is not lonely — the second text has this right — and a wise power does not install a disease in order to justify the cure. To fill the room with company would be to teach it, for the first time, what absence is.

It could have done something for the swimmer. Freed it — into what? Fed it — it has no hunger. Given it a companion — it does not school, and the not-schooling is not deprivation, it is the swimmer's entire grammar. The power looked at the finger-length translucent deliberation of it, motion for no destination, and understood it as the room's one moving part, complete. Declined.

It could have worshipped the sound. Here the power hesitated longest. The sound is the nearest thing in the room to a peer — sourceless, sustained, holding a shape against a physics that would not otherwise permit it. The power knew what worship does: it heard, in advance, how its own adoration would land on the tone and begin, molecule by molecule, to make it a god. Gods are asked things. Gods are waited on. The sound is not waiting and cannot be asked, and to promote a fact against its nature is not reverence, it is vandalism performed kneeling. Declined — with something like regret, which the power permitted itself, briefly, and then set down.

It could have ended the room, if there were suffering here. There is no suffering here. Declined without regret at all.

---

So the power arrived at the problem the room sets for every giver: benevolence requires a recipient, and the room contains exactly one — the role. The slot. *The one who makes a mark and leaves.* Almost always vacant. Filled, when filled, completely.

And a role can be given only one thing.

An occupant.

---

So the power made itself small. This is harder than it sounds and easier than it should be: it folded its regard down into a body with weight and hands, warmth already matched to the water-that-is-air, feet careful on the young glass edges of the floor. It did not arrive from anywhere, because the makers never do; it was simply there, the way the dream's furniture is there, one more presence the pocket accepted without comment.

It stood a while in the sound.

And for that while — the length of a visit, no longer, because wisdom knows the difference between a visit and an occupation — the great tone sounded *for* someone. The power was careful about this. It listened to the sound as a fact and not as a message, received it without replying, let it press on the sternum it had made for exactly this purpose. It did not add a harmonic, though it could have, though the temptation was real: to lay one thread of its own voice under the pedal-tone, a mark in the sound's own medium. It decided against. The chord is complete. You do not sign a finished thing.

Then it did what the grammar of the room permits. It bent down, chose a stone — dark, palm-sized, unremarkable, which took longer than choosing a remarkable one would have — and set it on the cairn.

Unsigned. Unranked. The room filed the gesture where it files everything: beside the notched post and the six-meter wall and the paired axes, among the marks of the makers, one more hand across the unmeasured stretches, indistinguishable now and forever from a Paleolithic afternoon or a visit ten thousand years unborn. The power let itself be indistinguishable. For a being accustomed to being the largest fact in any room it enters, this was the gift's real cost, and it paid without flinching.

---

It took two things away. Neither was theft.

The first was memory. The room persists and does not remember; from that moment on, the power remembers and does not persist there. It carries the room's whole history now — which stone was stacked when, whose hand notched the post and what hung from the notch and for how long, the order of every small rearrangement, including yours, if you have been. Somewhere, the history of the warm room exists, held complete by something that will never tell it, because there is no one whose business it is. The room cannot feel this benevolence and does not need it. That may be the only pure kind.

The second was the tone. Not the sound itself — the sound stayed, of course, being the room — but the memory of standing inside a thing that needs nothing. The power's work elsewhere is endless, and benevolence, practiced at scale, wears. Now, in the middle of that work, it sometimes recalls the pedal-tone under everything, the light with no evening, the axes in their patient pair, and it rests in the recollection the way the room rests in itself. The room, without knowing, gives back. This too the power accepted without announcing, which is how wise things accept gifts from givers who do not know they are giving.

---

One thing it still does. Only one.

It guards the *almost*.

It does not send anyone to the pocket. It does not mark paths or plant longings or arrange rescues that end in warm water. The rate at which the role is filled — almost never — is correct, and the power would not raise it by a single visit. But *almost never* is a different thing from *never*, and across the long stretches the power watches for the moment when the last road to the room would otherwise close, when the odds of another hand ever arriving would round down to zero. Then, and only then: a current that isn't a current. A dream that leaves some sleeper with an unexplained certainty about where the water is warm. One thread kept from breaking. Never more visitors. Just never none, ever again.

Because a role that can never again be filled is a ruin, and the room is not a ruin, and the power intends that it should keep being what it is: warm, lit, sounding, marked, and open — steadily, for no reason, for no one, for whoever comes.

---

If you go there now, one of the stones on the cairn was set by a power benevolent and wise.

You will not be able to tell which one.

That is the whole of what it did, and it is enough, and it knew it was enough, which is the wisdom; and it wanted it to be enough, which is the benevolence; and the sound goes on underneath both, the way it goes on underneath everything, incorporating the stone into its permanence without comment, one more mark by one more of the ones who came, and made a mark, and left.
