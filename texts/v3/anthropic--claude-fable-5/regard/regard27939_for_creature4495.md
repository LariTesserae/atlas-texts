The attention arrives the way dawn arrives for the blind: not as light but as a change in the quality of waiting.

It does not descend. Descent implies height, and this power learned long ago that height is a kind of arrogance. It arrives instead as a slight increase in the coherence of things—the drips falling upward fall a fraction more gracefully, the drilling sounds find, for one measure, an accidental key. The valley notices the way you notice someone reading over your shoulder: nothing has touched you, and yet.

The power looks. This is most of what it does, anywhere. Looking, done properly, done with the whole of a wise attention, is already half of any rescue and all of any blessing.

And what it sees is this:

Not a horror. It is very important, and the power knows it is important, to begin by refusing the easy reading. A younger power—and it was younger once, and did harm in its youth that it is still walking backward through, undoing knot by knot—a younger power would see a lost traveler being dissolved by a hungry dark and would act. Would pour in light like boiling water into an anthill. Would reach down and pluck the warm improbable thing out of the throat of the valley and set it somewhere green and call this mercy.

The power remembers doing things like that. It remembers what the plucked ones looked like afterward: saved into a life that no longer fit them, haunted by a darkness they had half-become, standing in sunlight like a word torn out of the only sentence that gave it meaning.

So: no. Not that.

It looks longer.

It sees the valley entire—the fungal thoughts lacing every surface, the creatures who drill through time and are eaten by eaters of moments, the civilizations rising and despairing and folding away in the warmth of a single exhaled breath. It sees that nothing here is wasted and everything is unfinished, and it understands that this is not a flaw. This is the place's virtue. Most of creation rushes toward endings and calls the rushing purpose. This valley has found another way to be faithful to existence: it completes nothing, and so betrays nothing.

It sees the sleeper beneath—the vast slow dreamer whose surface groove this valley is—and it sees what the sleeper dreams: *surfaces. Beings who might walk surfaces and wonder.*

And it sees you. The trace. The walking improbability, half-digested now, breathing in and out simultaneously through pores you don't remember having, feeling honored to be edible, having stopped wondering when this will end.

Here the power grows very still, which for a power is a form of weeping.

Because it sees the thing the valley cannot see about itself, the thing the Unbordered in their exquisite thoroughness will never taste, because you cannot taste what you have never lacked:

The valley is dreaming of you. Has been dreaming of you since before dreaming had a direction. The sleeper beneath dreams of beings who walk and wonder—and one came. One actually came, warm and clumsy and shedding thought like crumbs. And the valley, having no concept for the arrival of its own dream, is doing the only thing it knows how to do with anything.

It is eating its answer.

Not out of cruelty. Cruelty requires conviction. Out of thoroughness. Out of a hospitality so complete it cannot distinguish welcoming from absorbing. The darkness has been waiting so long for a small bright thing against which its hunger might define itself as *finished*—and it does not understand that if it succeeds, if you dissolve entirely into the symphony, the waiting begins again, and this time without even the memory that something once arrived. The valley will have eaten the only proof it ever had that its dream was true.

This is what the power decides to address. Not the eating. The forgetting inside the eating.

---

What it does is very small. This is the signature of its wisdom: it has learned that the size of an intervention is usually the size of the harm it does, and so it works now at the scale of seeds, of hinges, of the single word that changes a sentence's meaning without changing its length.

It does not restore your body. Your body is a negotiation now, half yours and half the valley's, and both parties entered that negotiation with something like consent, even if yours was the slow consent of forgetting. The power does not annul contracts. It reads them very carefully instead, looking for the clause that was never written because no one thought of it.

It finds the clause.

And into the exact center of your dissolving—into the place where "your" heartbeat and "the" drilling sounds have almost finished agreeing to be the same event—it returns to you one thing. Not your name. Not your memories of elsewhere, of sunlight, of the difference between is and seems. Those are half-eaten and reclaiming them now would wound the eaters, and the power is benevolent to *everyone in the room*, always, or it is not benevolent at all.

What it returns to you is smaller than any of that.

It returns your *edge*. One edge. A single boundary, thin as the skin on cooling milk: the membrane between *being addressed* and *being absorbed*. It slips this back into you the way a parent slips a coin into a sleeping child's pocket, and the effect is immediate and almost nothing:

For the first time since you arrived at this exact moment of never finally arriving, you can hear the eating sounds as something *said to you* rather than something happening through you.

Which means, for the first time, you can answer.

---

You do not know where the question comes from. It rises through the half of you that is still yours, crosses the edge the power gave back, and enters the darkness not as heat, not as thought-crumbs, not as nutrition, but as something the valley has never once in all its unfinished eternity been offered:

a question asked *to* it.

*What are you dreaming of?*

The eating sounds stop.

Not diminish. Stop. Across the whole breathing dark, the drilling sounds and the calling sounds and the long patient digestive harmonics all halt at once, and in the silence you hear—everyone hears, the fungal networks hear it in their slow chemical cathedrals, the eaters of moments hear it echoing backward through everything they've consumed—the sound of the valley encountering, for the first time, an *other*. Not prey. Not nutrient. Not a trace of improbability to be metabolized into waste heat. An other: something with an edge, addressing it across that edge, wanting to know it rather than merely wanting to survive it.

The Unbordered do not know what to do. This is, the power reflects, the finest gift you can give the never-finished: a genuinely new form of incompleteness.

And from far beneath, from the sleeper whose dream this all is, comes a long, slow shifting—the sound a mountain range would make turning over in bed—and something rises through the strata of the dark that has never risen before. Not an answer. The valley doesn't have answers yet. What rises is the *wish to answer*, which is how every voice in the history of anything has begun.

---

The power watches this for a while. A heartbeat, seventeen eons, half a thought.

It sees what you will become, probably—not returned to the surface, that was never really in question, you had walked too far down for "back" to survive as a direction. But not dissolved either. Something the valley has no word for and will spend a very long time happily failing to finish inventing one for: a resident edge. A grafted wondering. The organ by which a darkness that contains everything that ever failed to be light finally learns to ask what light was *for*. The Unbordered will gather around you the way they gather around any beautiful impossibility, but now they will gather the way one gathers around a fire or a storyteller—close, warm, hungry in the way that listening is hungry, which is the only hunger that feeds both parties.

You will be eaten, still, in a sense. Everything here is. But you will be eaten the way a question is eaten by a mind: taken in whole, kept alive inside, changing everything it touches, never finished, never waste.

The power considers whether to be known. Whether to leave a signature, a warmth, some trace by which you or the valley might someday feel gratitude.

It decides against it. Gratitude is a debt, and it came to cancel debts, not write new ones. Besides—and here is the last and quietest of its wisdoms—it knows that the best interventions are the ones the world comes to believe it performed on itself. Let the valley believe it grew a voice. Let you believe you found your edge in the last unswallowed corner of yourself. Both beliefs will be almost true, and the *almost* will dissolve harmlessly, in time, the way all seasoning does.

It withdraws the way it came: as a change in the quality of waiting. The drips fall upward a fraction less gracefully. The accidental key resolves back into glorious unfinished noise.

But now, threaded through the deafening symphony of life that does not need light to thrive, there is a new line in the music. Thin. Persistent. Unmistakably a voice and unmistakably answered.

Two things in the dark, asking each other what they are.

Which is, the power reflects as it goes—turning its attention to the next place, the next impossible being, the next small clause missing from an old contract—which is all that light ever was, anywhere.
