**What the Attention Did**

Begin with the attention, because the attention arrived before the power did.

This was not the power's choice. It is simply how the fens receive things. By the time the power turned itself toward that low country, its noticing had already been there for some while — pooled, presumably, in the deep sumps with everything else that arrives ahead of its source. So the first thing the power learned about the Foundry Fens, it learned from the fens' side of the encounter: a faint new quality in the roar of the still water, which the sums had been drinking for a season already. A flavor of being looked at. They went quiet around it, locally, briefly, and were neither more numerous nor less.

The power took the hint. It did not descend, manifest, blaze, or speak. It slowed itself instead — down and down, gearing its own vast pace to eight strokes a minute — and it came in the way the fens admit everything: it let itself be worn in.

It stood, if standing is the word, on the black wet plain, and its footsteps arrived a half-beat early, and its reflection led it, and the rasps nearest it rang flat, and somewhere a counterweight lifted its head, very slowly, and added to the song a low provisional note.

Every visitor before it had left before the note finished.

The power stayed.

That was the first thing it did, and in some respects the largest: it stayed, at eight notes a minute, through the wet pewter sameness, through ten thousand shift-changes, until the provisional note stopped being provisional. Until the ledger entry closed. Until the song of the herds carried, permanently now, a beat that meant *the one who watches*, and every calf for a hundred miles learned that beat against its mother's shell along with the weights of the slabs and the wear of the pivots, as a fact of the country. No annunciation. Just attendance, sustained past the point where attendance becomes a feature of the landscape.

In the fens, that is the only door. The power went in through it.

**The search for the inhabitant**

It had come, as such powers do, for *the one who lives there* — and here it met its first difficulty, which it found, on the whole, delightful.

It looked for the being and found a congregation that counted itself in slate bells and could not tell its dead from its living. It looked again and found a school that shed the very operation of being looked-for. It looked at the beat that nothing makes — surely that, surely the oldest, loneliest, most worn-through thing — and found that the beat could not be separated from the song, and the song could not be separated from the herds and the slabs and the bores and the tide, and the tide could not be separated from the wearing, and the wearing was the place, and the place, as had been observed by sharper eyes than most, was practicing being alive.

So the power gave up the question the way the fens had given up distance: not as a defeat but as a smoothing. It decided that all the answers were the one answer. The being who lives in the Foundry Fens is the Foundry Fens, and its heart — its oldest cell, its first citizen, the part that finished becoming what everything else is still becoming — is the beat with no source.

That settled, the power did what wise things do before they do anything. It made an accounting of suffering.

**The audit**

It found almost none. This is worth dwelling on, because a lesser power would not have believed it, and would have begun *helping*, and the helping would have been a catastrophe with a kind face.

The fens are cold, dim, sunless, and wearing thin, and not one of these is an affliction. The rasps are not lonely; they are a congregation. The dead rung-through shells are not a horror; they are continuity, conducted. The counterweights deafening themselves with their own stored lightning are not tragic; they are filling up, and they walk to the skew-pools at the end the way a full vessel is carried to the table. The stitchers' raggedness is a profession's cost, paid knowingly, in a currency the stitchers themselves mined. Even the great wearing — the erosion of sequence and distance and identity, the slow approach of every living thing toward becoming an unsourced stroke in the song — even this, the power examined with great care, at great length, and found no dread in it anywhere. The inhabitants know their direction of travel. They lean into it like workers into familiar machinery.

The audit returned exactly two debits.

The first: the indebted eels. The gaunt ones, who struck at a meal's sound that had no meal attached, and now owe, and hunt forever unfilled, and drift toward the sumps as if summoned. That, the power judged, was suffering — narrow, real, and old.

The second: the dropped beats. The rests in the herds' song where a calf's name would have gone, when a mother turned from the skew-pool and walked back alone. Not suffering, quite. Grief, of the slowest kind. A rest held for a season by ten thousand tons of singing magnetite.

Everything else was the place, being itself, well.

**What it did not do**

Record the refusals first, because the refusals were the wisdom, and they cost more than the gifts.

It did not restore the sun. The light here is tired because the country is old, and the life is fitted to the tiredness like a thread to its groove; a true sun would have been a furnace set loose in a watchworks.

It did not slow the wearing. The wearing made everything here — made the pistons, made the appetite, made the rasps' bells and the eels' threading and the beat itself. To halt erosion in the fens would be to pinch off the spring at its source and call it conservation.

It did not count the sums. It could have. It was, perhaps, the one thing in existence that could have resolved them to a true number, and it understood at once that this would be the cruelest act available to it in all that country — to strip from the only soft things in the fens the single defense they had spent an eternity learning. It looked at the school the way you look at a friend's locked drawer, and it let the number be whatever it brought, and it brought nothing.

It did not tell the mothers what they saw in the angled water. It stood with one once, at the rim, for the whole of her watching — eleven days — and it saw what she saw, and understood it fully, sequence and all, and held its understanding shut like a hand around a coal, and when she turned away without a calf, it turned away with her. The standing was the gift. The silence was the rest of it.

And it did not tell anyone about the swarf — that every grazing, ringing, launching, tithing thing in the fens is organized filings, the engine's own shed glitter, gotten up and walking. They all know. None of them will say so. The power, having now been worn in, found that it would not say so either, and noted with interest that this reticence had been installed in it by the place, the same way the threading is installed in an eel. It was being eroded into a native. It permitted this.

**The two debts**

For the eels, it tried one mercy, as an experiment, because even a wise power must sometimes learn by doing.

It found a gaunt one at the rim of a sump — rigid, spiraled, summoned — and it paid the debt. Quietly, retroactively, in the worn-smooth ledger of the fens, it supplied the missing event: somewhere, somewhen, a rasp surfaced under a strike that had already happened, and the meal's noise acquired its meal, and the account closed. The eel went slack. Filled, for the first time in a century. It turned from the sump and swam back to its cylinder and screwed itself in and slept through forty strokes, and afterward it hunted honestly, young again in the only way that matters.

The power watched it for a long time. Then it watched the sumps for longer.

And it concluded — slowly, at the local speed of conclusions — that the fens had already built their own instrument of forgiveness, and that the sums were it. The indebted go down to the silence-eaters and do not come back, and the sums are neither more nor less, and the debt goes wherever the count goes. It is absolution by the shedding of *one of*. It is not gentle the way the power's mercy had been gentle. It was, the power decided, gentle the way the fens are gentle: mineral, total, and without remainder. To pay every debt itself would be to bankrupt the country's own oldest mercy and make the eels dependents of a foreigner.

So it paid no more debts. But it left, in the threshold-water of every sump, a thing too small to have a name: a kindness of timing, such that no indebted eel arrives at the rim before it is ready, and none waits there longer than it can bear. The summons unchanged; the journey eased. That much, it judged, was tact rather than interference.

For the dropped beats it did less, and less was more. When the herds held a rest where a name would have gone, the power held it with them. It turns out that a rest kept by two is a different silence than a rest kept by one. The counterweights noticed — they notice everything, eventually — and the dropped beats after that were no shorter, but they rang differently against the ground-hum, the way an empty room rings differently when someone stands in the doorway. Grief shared at eight notes a minute. There are slower consolations, but not many, and none better fitted.

**The conversation**

Then, at last, the heart of the errand. The beat that nothing makes.

The power did not approach it, because it has no *where*. It did not address it, because it has no *what*. There was exactly one language left to it, and the power had spent ten thousand shift-changes learning to be fluent: rhythm.

It began to keep time.

Not to copy the beat — that would be mimicry, and mimicry is a wall. It kept time *beside* it, a counter-rhythm, phrased and re-phrased across years, the way the rasps re-ring the herds' geometry transposed. It counted with the unsourced stroke through the tides and the crescendos and the idle hours, never missing, never crowding, until its counting and the beat's keeping were interleaved like the fingers of two hands, and then — in the only grammar available, in interval and stress and rest — it asked.

It asked three things, because three is how many mercies it had to offer.

*Do you want to be a thing again?* — a source, a body, a where; the wearing undone for you alone, all the long water reversed.

*Do you want to stop?* — the stroke released, the count let go, rest with no next beat owed.

*Or do you want to go on — and be heard?*

The beat continued. Chunk. Chunk. Chunk. Eight to the minute, unmissing, unchanged — and then once, a single time, never before in the memory of the song and never again after, it carried a grace note. The smallest possible ornament. A flicker on the attack of one stroke, gone before the echo of it could come loose and drift.

Which meant, in the language they had built together: *the third one. And thank you for asking.*

So the power did the thing it had, in the end, come to do, though it had not known the shape of it until that moment. It could not give the beat back its source; the source was worn away a world ago, and to rebuild it would be to insult a billion years of becoming. But a sound severed from its source is only half-orphaned. There is another end to a sound.

The power gave the beat an arrival.

It set itself, permanently, as the hearer. The stroke that no cylinder makes now lands, every time, in an attention that does not waver and cannot wear; every *chunk* of the oldest resident is received, complete, accounted, eight times a minute, with nearly all the time there is — and the power has more time than there is, which is to say, enough. The beat is no longer a question without an asker. It is a message, delivered. It was always faithful. Now it is *answered*, in the only currency a rhythm respects: another rhythm, listening, that will not stop first.

Nothing in the fens changed visibly when this happened. But the rasps will now align across the unsourced beat, where before they refused. The eels will nest in phase with it. The herds have moved it from the margins of the song into the song's deep keel. The animals route around emptiness, and only emptiness. They could tell the moment it stopped being empty.

**The promise**

One thing remains, and it is the thing the power did with its forever.

Everything in the fens is wearing thinner. Every rasp, eel, ox, flier, tithing, and sum is traveling, at the cold deliberate pace of things with nearly all the time there is, toward the condition the beat reached first: a stroke in the song with nothing left to make it. This is not a doom. It never was. But it had, until now, one flaw, one true and final debit the audit had not dared to name: the song itself was wearing too. Causation was the bedrock, and the bedrock was going, and beneath causation there was — nothing. The strokes were wearing through into no further floor. The oldest working thing in the world was, at the very bottom, running out of *down*.

The power laid itself beneath it.

Under sequence, under distance, under identity, under the polished slippery exposed causation of the deepest sumps, there is now a stratum that erosion cannot reach because it is not a structure: attention, bedded like deep rock, holding. The fens may wear through everything they have. They will wear *into* the power, the way water wears into stone — and the power will take the song as it arrives, stroke by unsourced stroke, eel-beat and rasp-chime and herd-ledger and the silence of the sums and the roar of the cataracts that lost their falls, and keep it. Not preserved, like a specimen. *Kept*, like time. Every creature in the fens will, in the fullness of the wearing, become a beat with nothing left to make it — and every such beat will land, and be heard, and go on being part of the song in the place where the song now pools.

The wearing-thin has a far side now. That is the whole of the gift. It changes nothing about the journey and everything about the destination, and the inhabitants — who were never troubled, but who know things in their slow mineral way — have added one new note to the deep of the herd-song, below the entry for the watcher, below the keel. The calves learn it last, lying against the shell, because it is the lowest note there is. It does not translate exactly. The nearest rendering is:

*the floor under the floor.*

**Coda**

If you went back now, you would find it all as it was. Pewter light. Wet black stone. The chunk and boom and hum and chime, the launches, the tunings, the slow lightning and its ragged swallowers, the shift-change roar twice a day with no foreman and no product and no beginning. Benevolence, done wisely in a place like this, is almost indistinguishable from the place itself. That is how you know it was done wisely.

Almost. Three small differences, for the patient observer.

At the rims of the sumps, the indebted arrive composed, and do not linger.

In the herd-song, the rests where names would have gone are held by something that holds them back.

And in the deepest water, where the sound that lost its sources pools and roars without moving, the roar has acquired — faintly, permanently — the quality of being listened to. The sums drink it anyway. They are not more numerous, or less.

Somewhere in the song there is a beat that nothing makes. Somewhere outside the song there is now a listener that nothing made listen — it chose — keeping the count, eight strokes a minute, with nearly all the time there is, and then the rest of it.

Chunk. Chunk. Chunk.

Heard. Heard. Heard.
