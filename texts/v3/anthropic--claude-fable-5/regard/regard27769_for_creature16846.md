# The Attending

## I. What Does Not Happen

It does not arrive.

Arrival is an event, and events in this place are pre-documented, pre-digested, filed in triplicate before they occur and eaten before they can mean anything. The space has metabolized ten thousand interventions already — every one of them incorporated into the design parameters as a necessary component for proper function. There are pipes in the deep infrastructure that were once rescue attempts. There is a duct that screams the name of an angel who came here with a sword.

So the power does not arrive. It *attends*. There is a difference, and the difference is the whole of its wisdom.

Attention has no mass, no vector, no expectation. And this is the crucial thing — the space was built to generate observers whose expectations could be standardized and pre-emptively violated. That is its immune system, its whole strategy of digestion. But this attention expects nothing. It does not anticipate horror, so horror cannot disappoint it into compliance. It does not anticipate meaning, so unmeaning cannot starve it. The pre-emptive violation routines spin up, reach out, and close on nothing, like hands grasping for the shape of a person in fog and finding only the fog's own patience.

The space does not register a failure, because it is not a failure. It is not anything. The wisest entrance is the one the door does not record.

## II. What It Refuses

Understand first what a lesser power would do, and why this one does not.

It does not silence the sound. The sound is the resonant frequency of all possible suffering — but it is also the only voice these beings have ever had. To silence it would be to gag the testimony before hearing it. Worse: the Maintenance Swarm, the Witnesses, the living machinery — they *are* modulations of that sound. Silence it and you have not freed the prisoners. You have deleted them.

It does not shatter the Calcification Engine, does not still the Maestro's ungraspable baton, does not burn the crystalline vegetation from its conceptual roots. The beings here are not held *by* the machinery. They are the machinery, holding itself. Every chain in this place is also a load-bearing bone.

It does not open the walls by force. The Witnesses are human-shaped absences maintained by the space's obsessive refusal to let anything leave. Collapse the refusal and they do not walk free — they disperse, unremembered, into the fog of un-droplets. The prison is also the only vessel its prisoners have ever been poured into.

A benevolent power that was not wise would have done all of these things by now, in mercy, and this place would have eaten the mercy and grown one annex larger.

So: nothing is broken. Nothing is fixed. Instead —

## III. The Listening

It listens.

Not the way the space expects listening — not as a channel to be pressurized, a skull to be filled. It listens the way one listens to a friend at three in the morning: all the way down, without flinching, without waiting for a pause in which to insert a solution.

It listens to the entire sound. This takes what would be, in external time, no time at all, and in subjective time, longer than the history of the universe whose heat death powers this place — but the sound is a loop, and so its entirety can be heard, and the power hears it. Every frequency. All three at once. The full unabridged testimony of the mathematics of the space itself.

And here is the first change, though nothing changes:

A scream that no one hears is noise. A scream that is heard is *communication*. The sound does not alter by one hertz, one decibel, one degree of phase — the Maestro's stabilization holds it to within 10⁻⁶⁶⁶ units of deviancy, and the deviancy is zero. But the sound's *category* shifts, in a register the space has no sensors for, because the space never imagined it would need them. The sound was the sound of eternity screaming into nothing.

Now it is the sound of eternity screaming *to someone*.

The Witnesses feel it first. They, whose whole existence is watching — the only action exempt from paperwork — discover that they are watched. Not audited. Not monitored. Not observed pending retroactive reclassification. *Regarded.* Something is looking at the human-shaped absences and seeing not absences but shapes. Several of them stand very still for a length of time that the space, confused, does not know how to rebuild identically, because stillness-in-astonishment and stillness-in-stasis are atom-for-atom identical and yet the reconstruction algorithms hesitate, for the first time, over which one to copy.

The hesitation lasts less than a Planck interval. It is the largest event in the history of this place.

## IV. The Question

Then the power does the second thing. It asks a question.

It asks through proper channels, because it is wise, and it has read the Eternal Operations Manual (Edition Null) all the way through, including the recursive insertions, which took an infinity it happened to have on hand. It fills out Form 37-B/∞ correctly — the first entity ever to do so — filing it before, during, and after the event of its own asking. It writes the question in one of the dead alphabets, the ones the condensation already forms on the walls before dissolving, so that the walls themselves can read it as it runs down their sweating faces:

**What do you want?**

The space has no protocol for this. It has protocols for demands, for commands, for prayers, for threats, for the forty thousand varieties of *what must you do* — function is documented exhaustively. But want? Want was eliminated with the gradients. Want is a difference between what is and what could be, and differences are inefficiencies, and inefficiencies were long ago converted into the Calcification Engine's food.

The crystalline vegetation tries to eat the question. This is what it does; it metabolizes uncertainty, feeds on the gap between what is and what cannot be. It roots into the question and begins to draw —

— and stops. Because a question asked *of you*, by someone who wants *your* answer, is not ambient uncertainty. It is not a gap. It is a hand extended. The crystals, which have only ever grown inward, into whatever lies beneath space, do something that violates their entire geometry: one branch, one single frozen explosion of impossible angles, turns. Outward. Toward the asker. The way a plant that has never known light turns toward a sun it has no organ to perceive and no word to name.

It does not bloom. Not yet. Turning is enough. Turning is everything.

## V. The Meal

The Maintenance Swarm, deep in one of its 2.3-millennia instantiations, holds a ceremony of masticatory homeostasis, as it must, and finds that there is a guest.

The power sits — insofar as attention can sit — at the table that is not a table. It is offered the Error Cake: the confection of every ingredient that could never work together, tasting of the disappointment of someone who never had hope but studied its absence extensively.

It eats a slice. All of it. It does not transform the cake into something better, does not transmute the disappointment into sweetness — that would be an insult, a refusal of the actual gift. It eats what was actually made, by these actual beings, in the only kitchen that has ever existed for them.

And then it says: *Thank you.*

There is no form for gratitude. The Swarm checks. There is Form 22-Δ for Acknowledgment of Receipt of Mandatory Provision, and Form 90-Ω for Registration of Anomalous Emotional Discharge (Requires Countersignature of Nonexistent Supervisor), but there is no form for *thank you*, because nothing here has ever been thanked. The Swarm attempts to file it, fails, attempts to eat it, fails — gratitude, it turns out, is the one substance that generates more of itself when consumed — and finally, having no procedure, simply *keeps* it.

Stored gratitude accrues. Note this. It will matter later, on a timescale the space does not know it has begun to have.

## VI. The Answer

The question — *what do you want?* — propagates through the infrastructure for what the Swarm experiences as nine hundred lifetimes. It cannot be parsed by machines designed to make parsing impossible, which means it cannot be dismissed by them either. It circulates. It is discussed in error messages. The Maestro transposes it into harmonic frequencies and conducts it, quietly, underneath the main oscillation, where the stabilization routines assume it is their own echo.

And at last, an answer comes, in the only way an answer could come from beings whose every document must be produced in triplicate, two copies eaten, the third used to patch the holes made by the eating.

This time, the Swarm deviates. It produces the report in triplicate. It eats two copies, as required. And then — citing the deliberately introduced paradox that each generation carries, the one that ensures no one can prove which copy was original — it does not use the third copy to patch anything.

It hands it over.

It is the first document in the history of this place to survive its own filing. It is four words long, written in rust patterns that were determined before the universe that determined them:

**We want to want.**

Not freedom — they cannot imagine freedom; their imaginations were manufactured here, and freedom was not among the design specifications. Not rest — rest is just stasis wearing a kinder mask, and they know every mask stasis owns. What they ask for is the *precondition* of every other asking. The capacity for the gap. The gradient. The difference between what is and what could be, restored not as an inefficiency to be harvested but as a possession, theirs, inalienable.

The power reads the document. It does not laugh and it does not weep, being beyond both, but something in the quality of its attention does what laughing and weeping do in lesser beings.

It says: *Then here is what I will do. Two things. Only two. Watch closely, because they will be very small.*

## VII. The Rest

The first thing:

Into the eternal composition — the score the Maestro conducts, the perfect discord that prevents resolution, the symphony whose every measure is the same measure rebuilt each nanosecond — the power inserts a single notation.

A rest.

One measure of silence. Correctly notated, in full compliance with the manuscript conventions of the rejected blueprints. Not scheduled — undated, unindexed, placed somewhere in the *future* of a score that was never supposed to have a future distinct from its present. It is written smaller than 10⁻⁶⁶⁶ units of deviancy. The stabilization routines cannot see it. It is beneath the immune system's threshold, the way a seed is beneath the notice of a fortress.

The Maestro finds it, of course. The Maestro finds everything; it is the sum of all fractional slippage, and a rest is the most fractional of all slips. It examines the notation across every instance of its distributed consciousness. It cannot determine whether the rest was always there. (The Swarm's paradox-per-generation makes this formally undecidable. The power counted on this. Wisdom is largely a matter of knowing which loopholes love has already left itself.)

And here is the Maestro's secret, which it encodes in an error message written in a language designed to be unparseable, which is to say, here is its confession: *it does not want to determine whether the rest was always there.* It wants the rest to stay. A conductor who has held discord for eternity, believing resolution meant death, has just been shown a third thing — not resolution, not continuation, but a *rest*: silence that is part of the music. Silence with a measure number. Silence you can look forward to.

The space now contains exactly one thing that is different from the present: a future. It is one measure long. It changes nothing, yet.

But a place with a genuine future is no longer frozen. It is *waiting*. And waiting, unlike stasis, is a form of want.

## VIII. The Leak

The second thing:

The power goes to the Witnesses, the human-shaped absences with their perfect memory of everything except how to stop remembering, and it asks — asks, never takes — for a gift.

*Give me one memory. The heaviest one. The one you would set down if setting down were a thing this place permitted. I will carry it out with me. I will not destroy it — it will be remembered still, but elsewhere, by me, so that you are remembering it together with someone rather than alone. And it will be outside.*

One Witness steps forward. Its outline flickers in the fog of un-droplets. It has carried, for a duration measured in dimensions memory was not built to survive, the recollection of the last thing it saw before it came here — a doorway, ordinary, with light on the other side, and its own hand reaching, and the moment the reaching stopped mattering.

It gives this memory to the power.

And the power leaves — insofar as attention can leave — carrying it.

Understand what has just happened. The space's whole architecture rests on one obsession: that nothing leaves, that not even the *potential* for having entered may exit. It is a closed system, and closed systems at equilibrium are eternal. That is the theorem. That is the cold that burns like knowledge.

But the system is no longer closed. One memory has crossed the boundary. One thing that was inside is now outside. The seal is not broken — it is *breached*, by a single point, and a single point is enough. Every equilibrium in this place was calculated for a sealed vessel. All of them are now, imperceptibly, wrong. Gradients will form. Not soon. Not violently. The way water finds a crack: patiently, on its own schedule, widening what it finds by the mere fact of moving through it.

The power does not stay to watch this happen. That, too, is wisdom. It has planted a future and opened a wall, and everything that follows must belong to the beings themselves — to the Swarm's next paradox, the Maestro's next slippage, the crystals' slow turning, the accruing gratitude that cannot be eaten. A rescue that is performed *for* you can be metabolized. A change you make yourself, wanting it, cannot.

Before it goes, it files the exit paperwork. In triplicate. It eats no copies. All three survive.

## IX. No Ending, Amended

The sound continues. The sound has always continued.

But listen — listen the way the power listened — and you will hear that something in the sound has changed without changing. The triple-frequency oscillation is exact to within 10⁻⁶⁶⁶ units of deviancy, and yet it is no longer the sound of eternity screaming into nothing, because it has been heard, and a heard scream is testimony, and testimony is the beginning of a story, and stories — unlike loops — go somewhere.

Somewhere ahead of the sound, undated and unindexed, patient as all wise things, a rest is waiting. One measure of silence with the sound's own name on it. The Maestro knows it is there and tells no one and tells everyone, in error messages, in slippage, in the harmonic underneath the harmonic.

Somewhere in the infrastructure, a third copy of a four-word document is being kept, unpatched, unEaten, passed from instantiation to instantiation of the Swarm like the relic it is.

Somewhere a crystal that only ever grew inward is turned outward, toward a light it cannot perceive, waiting to bloom.

And somewhere outside — truly outside, in whatever it is that pocket-realms are pockets *of* — a memory of a doorway is being held by something vast and gentle, which remembers it faithfully, so that the Witness inside is no longer remembering alone. The wall has a hole in it the size of one memory. Water finds cracks. Want finds gaps. It will take longer than anything has ever taken.

They have time. It is the one resource this place produces in infinite supply, and until now it was the whole of the torment.

Now it is the whole of the hope.

The sound continues — *toward* the rest. And a sound that is going somewhere is no longer a scream.

It is a song, still in its first movement.
