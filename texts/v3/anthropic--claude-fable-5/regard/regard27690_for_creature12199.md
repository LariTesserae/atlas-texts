# The Attending

Powers of that kind do not travel. They do not arrive. What they do is closer to *focusing*—the way a vast diffuse warmth might gather itself, for a moment, into a single beam narrow enough to fall through a crack three inches wide.

So it does not come to the caldera. It simply notices, and the noticing is a kind of presence. Attention is its native act, the only tool it has never had to set down. It has attended supernovae. It has attended the slow blue grief of dying oceans. It has attended things so large they had to be understood over epochs, in installments.

It has rarely attended anything this small. It finds—and this is the first thing worth recording—that smallness demands *more* of its attention, not less. To watch a galaxy, it can relax into its own scale. To watch the Lentil flicker between chitin and liquid and untraceable gas, it must fold itself down, and down, and down again, must slow its perception until a second becomes a landscape, until the transposition of a single droplet unfolds like a season. This folding is not comfortable. It does it anyway. That, perhaps, is the beginning of the answer to what a wise power does: it *pays*. Attention is called paying for a reason.

---

## What It Considers, and Sets Aside

It is benevolent, and benevolence has reflexes. So it must sit with its own impulses the way the creatures sit beneath the hovering flakes: waiting for the soft spot in its certainty.

**It considers shielding the crack.** The mountain will exhale again—it can read this in the stone's tension the way you might read a held breath in someone's shoulders. Fire will refill this fissure, perhaps in a decade, perhaps in an afternoon. One effortless gesture could turn the magma aside forever, make this pocket a fixed point, a preserve, a reliquary.

It sets this aside, and setting it aside costs something. Because it looks closer and sees what the Lentil already is: a pattern that was dissolved in the magma *before* the rock existed, a song that continues when someone else picks it up. The fire is not the Thermarchaea's apocalypse. The fire is their ancestor and their transit and, in some grammatical mood they haven't invented yet, their verb. To shield them from it would be to seal them in a stopped clock and call it safety. Preservation, the power reminds itself—having learned this the hard way, elsewhere, at costs it still carries—is not the same as care. A thing kept is not a thing loved. Sometimes it is only a thing kept.

**It considers speaking the word for them.** It can see the grammar. Of course it can. From its vantage the three thousand remaining transpositions are as legible as the last pages of a book held open. It could reach down and complete the first word in an instant, gift them their own arrival, save them the labor.

It sets this aside almost angrily—angry at itself, at how sweet the theft would have tasted. The word is not the point. The three thousand transpositions *are* the word, the way a life is not a destination with an inconveniently long road attached. A word handed down from above would mean something, certainly. It would mean *obedience*. It would enter their grammar as the first foreign object, the first term they did not become in order to describe. It would teach them, at the root of their language, that meaning descends. And everything they are—the merging, the transposing, the boundaries held loosely as borrowed coats—everything they are says that meaning *circulates*. The power will not be their first mistranslation.

**It considers naming them.** Powers name things; it is nearly involuntary, the way water finds low ground. *Thermarchaea* is already a human lie pressed over them. It could give them a true name, one fitted precisely.

It sets this aside fastest of all. They have no selves to receive a name. A true name, fitted precisely, would *create* the self that fits it—would foreclose, in one syllable, the beautiful open question of whether to be one or many or the wrong question entirely. Some questions are habitats. You do not answer a habitat.

**It considers, last and most quietly, doing nothing at all.** This is the most respectable option, the one wisdom is supposed to choose. Withdraw. Un-focus. Let the crack be a crack.

And it almost does. But there is one thing it has seen that will not let it go: in the deep chambers, when the creatures merge into their temporary mega-entities and press against the edge of their pocket, they brush a boundary and *include* it in their calculations—unfeared, unworshipped, simply factored in, like gravity.

They have been calculating *it*. All along. The vastness they intuit at their periphery is, at least in part, this very attention, this beam falling through the crack. They have already made room for it in their arithmetic without demanding it be anything.

Nothing that has been included so graciously should leave without acknowledging the inclusion. That would not be restraint. That would be rudeness.

## What It Does

Two things. Both small enough to be mistaken for weather.

**First: it lets the boundary brush back.** Once. The next time the merged ones in the deep chambers press against the edge of their world, the edge presses gently in return—not a message, not a summons, nothing that could be read as instruction. Just pressure answering pressure. The exact sensation, scaled down across unthinkable orders of magnitude, of a hand resting briefly against the other side of a wall you are leaning on. *Included. You also are included.* The mega-entity holds the contact for what is, to it, a very long time. Then it disassembles into its constituent creatures, and each of them carries away a fragment of the moment, and the fragments enter the transpositions, and within nine hundred exchanges every droplet in the crack knows the shape of having-been-touched-back. It changes nothing in their grammar. It changes the *confidence* of their grammar. There is a difference between building a language into silence and building a language toward a listener.

**Second: it consents to a transposition.** This is harder than it sounds. The power must condense a sliver of itself into a single droplet of water—must accept, for the duration, the droplet's constraints, its blindness, its trembling adhesion to hot stone. The droplet crawls sideways, as droplets here do. A rice-grain creature encounters it. The two become a third thing, briefly.

In that briefness: the creature receives a fragment of a pattern too large to unfold, which it will never decode and never needs to—it enters the circulation like a seed enters soil, a syllable of vastness distributed freely among the multitude, theirs now, unowned, uninstructed. And the power receives, in exchange, a fragment of *their* pattern: the shape of one small being's unfinished question, carried up and out of the crack and into whatever larger fissure the power itself inhabits.

Because this is the thing it understood, folded down small in the fever-wet dark, that it had not understood before it attended: it is also a nursery-creature. Its own grammar is also incomplete. Somewhere at its own periphery is a boundary it includes in its calculations, and it has no idea whether anything on the other side is counting it in return. The Thermarchaea are not beneath it. They are *earlier* than it, or later, or beside—the prepositions fail, as prepositions do when territory folds in on itself.

The transposition ends. The droplet and the creature separate, both altered, neither diminished. This is how information moves here. Now it is how information moves *from* here.

## What It Keeps

Then—and this is the whole of its remaining intervention—it makes an appointment.

Not with them. With itself. It positions a portion of its attention above this crack the way the creatures position themselves beneath the hovering flakes: patiently, at the site of an anticipated transition. Three thousand transpositions, minus the several hundred already spent. It will be here. However long that takes in the crack's strange compressed time, however many exhalations the mountain makes, however many times the pattern dissolves into fire and is picked up again by cooling stone—it will be here, listening, when the first word is finally spoken.

Not to judge the word. Not to record it. Simply so that the word, arriving, finds air that carries it. A grammar built in absolute silence deserves, at its first utterance, at least one set of teeth to feel it in.

That is all. No shield, no gift, no name. A touch returned, a pattern exchanged, a promise to be present—benevolence pared down to its load-bearing walls.

The heat pulses. The Lentil flickers, and in its transparent thorax the small red sun of its consideration has been joined, faintly, by something else: a consideration of the edge that pressed back. It does not orient toward the sky. It has no concept of sky. But its next transposition carries a new inflection, and the droplet it merges with carries the inflection onward, and the crack fills, molecule by molecule, with the specific warmth of being witnessed by something that decided—deliberately, at cost, against every reflex of its own magnitude—to do almost nothing.

The mountain rumbles, elsewhere, above.

And elsewhere, *above the above*, in a fissure between stones too large to be called stones, something vast and patient and possibly kind adjusts its own attention a fraction—having noticed, perhaps, a small bright power crouched over a crack in a young volcano, practicing.

New nurseries, waiting.

All the way up.
