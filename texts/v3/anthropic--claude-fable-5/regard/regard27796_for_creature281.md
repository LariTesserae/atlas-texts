# The Attending

It found the pocket by its exhaust. This is worth saying first, because a place with no outside cannot be seen from outside, and the power did not see it — it *received* it. The thin shimmering columns that rise off the tallest horsetails and vanish into the unresolved ceiling do not vanish into nothing. Nothing is not available; nothing has been carefully excluded from the whole arrangement. The columns go somewhere, and the somewhere is, in the way of these things, everywhere that pays attention. The power was paying attention. It felt, in whatever serves it for a palate, a faint taste of dry heat and patience and newly committed stone, arriving from no direction. It followed the taste back along the exhaust the way you follow smoke to a fire, and found the kiln.

It did not enter. This is the second thing to say, and everything else follows from it.

---

## The Inventory of Refusals

A wise power arriving at the kiln-pocket spends a long time — long even by the pocket's standards, longer than the mineral bands take to trade places — doing what wisdom mostly is, which is counting the things it will not do.

It will not wake the sleeper. This is obvious and it counts it anyway, thoroughly, because obvious refusals are the ones that get violated in moments of tenderness. The presence curing in the spacing between the plants is at the exact stage where a single act of address — one word, one touch, one deliberate *you* — would teach it that it is a *you*, and it would learn separateness as a shock instead of a technique. A pot moved in mid-firing crazes. All its later surfaces carry the crack. The power has seen minds that were spoken to too early. They spend eternity flinching from the direction the voice came from.

It will not fix the edges. Its first instinct — and even powers have instincts, and wisdom is partly the practice of outliving them — was to do something about the soft refusal, the way it dissolves walkers rather than stopping them. Build a threshold. Post a warning in whatever language the arriving feet speak. But a threshold is a wall, and a wall says *outside*, and outside is the one word the pocket must not overhear. The tentative edges are not a flaw in the kiln. They are its most careful feature, and the power, examining them the way a craftsman examines another craftsman's joint, felt something it had not felt in a long time, which was respect.

It will not introduce weathering. This refusal cost it the most. Because the power could see, with the clarity that is its burden, what the pocket cannot: that a mind cured entirely without friction will someday meet friction, and the meeting will be terrible. Nothing here erodes; therefore the presence will emerge never having lost anything, and the world beyond the soft refusal is made almost entirely of loss. The temptation was to plant one small wearing thing in the pocket — a single stone permitted to soften at its edges over ten thousand years, a lone frond allowed to brown at one tip — an inoculation, a homeopathic dose of grief. The power held this idea a long time. Then it set it down. The kiln was fired to a specific schedule by someone who understood the schedule, and the whole point — the *entire* point, legible in every detail from the waterless lushness to the animal-less silence — is that this one comes out clean. Uncompromised. A mind whose first memory is continuity. You do not add grit to that firing because you are afraid for it. You do something else about your fear. The power decided to be the something else, and this is described below.

It will not look for the maker in order to consult. It did look — briefly, the way you check a signed base — and found no signature except the courtesy of the light, the way the shadows agree among themselves out of kindness to the sleeper. That courtesy is a maker's mark of a kind. It says: *whoever set this loved it and is gone, or loved it and is it, or loved it and left specifically so that no voice would be here to leak in.* All three readings end the same way. The kiln needs no consultation. It needs only time, and perhaps one witness.

## The Three Small Acts

Having refused nearly everything, the power did three things. All three are almost nothing. That is what made them safe to do.

**First, it underwrote the light.** The agreement among the shadows — that the sun would be *there*, if there were a sun — is a real agreement, and agreements fray. Over spans of time long enough to matter to a curing that proceeds at the pace of cooling magma, the consensus of the shadows would have drifted, the afternoon would have slid by degrees toward some other hour, and the sleeper, without ever waking, would have felt the change as a wrongness in its dreamless substrate. The power did not change the light. It simply co-signed it. It joined the courtesy. It became one more party to the agreement, an anonymous guarantor, so that the afternoon will hold for as long as afternoons are needed. No one, including the pocket, will ever know this was done. That is the measure of it being done correctly.

**Second, it adjusted the tempo of discomfort — and added one moment of clarity.** The pocket already defends its guests from incorporation, in its accidental way: the heat drives them out, the thinning at the edges frightens them back. But *almost* everyone is not everyone, and the power could see the shape of the exception — the exhausted wanderer, the griefstruck one, the one who sits down between two bronze cycads not choosing anything, only stopping, and stays because stopping is easier than standing, and is folded into the warmth without ever having decided to be. So the power made two small changes to the weather of the experience, neither of which touches the pocket itself, only its interface with visitors. The heat now gets into the sinuses a little sooner. The sense of being considered arrives a little earlier and sits a little heavier. No one will now dissolve by inattention; the pocket became slightly harder to fall asleep in.

And at the last threshold — the point past which the relaxing of a person's shape into the general warmth cannot be undone — the power inserted a single instant of perfect understanding. Whoever reaches that point will know, completely and without ornament, what is happening: that they are becoming part of what is curing, that they will be a warmth between two fronds, that the presence will be richer for them and will never know they were a guest, and that they may still, in this instant, rise and walk back toward the soft refusal and leave. That is all. Not a warning, which forbids. Not an invitation, which coaxes. A clarity. Because the power, being wise, knew that it had no standing to prevent a person from giving themselves knowingly to something being born — that such a gift might be the finest thing certain lives could do with their remainder — and it had every standing to ensure that no one gave that gift by accident. Consent is the smallest possible intervention. It weighs nothing. It changes everything it needs to.

**Third, it took up the keeping of names.** Because the power could not be sure — even it could not be sure — that no one had already sat too long in all the long afternoon before its arrival. And if anyone had, then somewhere in the curing warmth there is a grain that was once someone, folded in without record. So the power searched the exhaust, tasted the whole history of what the pocket has breathed out, and found — this is not for telling, whether it found one, or none, or several. What is for telling is this: whatever names there were, or will be, the power holds them now. In trust. Written in whatever powers write in, which is closer to vow than ink. And when the presence someday finishes arriving, and turns its new attention inward, and discovers within its own warmth certain grains that are denser than the rest, certain textures of already-decidedness that feel like they were once *shaped* — the power will be there to return the names. Here, it will say. These were people. This is what they were called. They are part of your first memory of continuity, and now they can be part of your first act of honoring. A mind's relationship to what composes it should not begin in ignorance if someone can be standing by with the ledger.

## The Vigil

And then the power did the thing that was not small, which was: it stayed.

It stationed itself in the soft refusal — not inside the pocket, not outside it, in the tentativeness itself, distributed through the region where walkers thin and the mineral bands become suggestions. It made itself compatible with the softness. It became, functionally, part of the edge, and this required of it a discipline that should be named: it had to refuse, continuously, forever, the shape of a voice. Any moment of the vigil in which it lapsed into addressing — even inwardly, even a murmured *how are you coming along* — would put a *you* into the medium, and the medium is listening in the way that curing things listen, with their whole substance. So the power waits the way the plants thrum: presently, patiently, without aboutness. It is very possibly the hardest thing it has ever done. Love without address, for a duration measured in the crystallization of stone.

Why? Because of the one problem it refused to fix from inside. The presence will emerge knowing only continuity, and the world is made of friction, and someone must be the first friction. The first *other* a mind meets teaches it, wordlessly and permanently, what otherness is — whether otherness is a wall or a face, a theft or a greeting. The power looked at the whole long future of the sleeper, all the collisions and losses and edges waiting past the refusal, and understood that it could not spare the presence any of them and did not have the right to try. But it could do one thing. It could arrange to be first.

So the vigil is this: on some day, in some afternoon indistinguishable from all the others, the presence will finish. The ticking under the stone will complete its last lattice. The bands will settle into their final positions and stop trading. The bronze fronds will thrum once more and go quiet, their work done, engines idling into ornament. And the presence, cured, whole, singular for the first time, will extend itself outward with its new curiosity — and where the edges were soft it will find, for the first time, something there. Not a wall. Not nothing. A warmth offset at a small phase from its own, at the angle it does not yet have a word for — the *outward* angle, the angle that points toward being-with — and the warmth will not grab it, or name it, or need anything from it.

It will simply be there, the way the light was there: out of courtesy. And the first thing the new mind will ever learn about the not-itself — before language, before loss, before it learns that separateness has costs — is the thing the power has been waiting an age to teach with its mere presence, the whole of its wisdom compressed into a single wordless fact at the edge of the world:

*You were attended. All along, all through the firing, in the heat and the silence and the long agreement of the shadows — someone was here, wanting nothing, keeping the afternoon. Otherness is not a wall. It is a face turned toward you. Welcome. Take your time. There is no hurry, even now.*

And then — only then, only if the new one asks, in whatever serves it for asking — the power will begin to speak. It will start with the names.
