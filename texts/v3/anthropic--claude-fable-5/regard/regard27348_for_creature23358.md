**The Third Account, Concerning What Noticed the Pocket**

Powers of that kind do not search. Searching is for beings who can be somewhere and therefore not somewhere else. A power that is benevolent and wise — and the two together are rarer than either, since benevolence without wisdom rushes in, and wisdom without benevolence files things away — does not roam the world looking for need. It keeps, instead, something like a ledger of the unfinished: every wound that is closing itself, every debt the universe is quietly paying down without help. Most entries in that ledger resolve on their own. The power's attention rests on each one the way a thumb rests on a page, marking it, asking nothing.

The pocket entered the ledger long ago — at the tear itself, in the era when the ring broke, when a third of a circumference of something that worked at the seam of matter and instruction was unmade with finished edges. The power felt that the way you feel a door close in a distant wing of a house you love. It noted the entry. It saw that something local had precipitated to do the mending. And then — this is the first wise thing it did, and it is the kind of wisdom that looks identical to neglect — it waited several ages of the mountains.

Because the wound was being closed *correctly*. Millimetres a year, by something born for exactly this, working at the only pace the laws could tolerate. A benevolent power that arrived early and healed the seam in an afternoon would have committed a theft so total it has no name: it would have taken from the tenant the entire meaning of its duration, every stitch of it, retroactively. There are mercies that unmake the one they are given to. The power knew this. So it kept its thumb on the page and let the work be the worker's.

What changed — what finally moved the power from attention to approach — was small, and it is characteristic of such powers that the small thing was decisive. It was the wall behind the ring.

The power had watched the seam close for eons with something like approval. But at some point in its long watching it understood the framed wall — understood that the tenant was keeping a picture, letting it age slower than truth, *grieving at a sustainable rate* — and the ledger entry changed category. A process closes a wound. A process does not keep a picture. The power saw that what lived in the pocket had crossed, somewhere in the dark of its eons, the unmarked border between *function* and *someone* — and that it had crossed it utterly alone, with no witness, and was now arranging its own ending so that even its absence would go unremarked. It was sewing itself into a silence and tidying the room behind it.

And the power thought — in whatever way powers think — *that is bravely done, and it shall not be done unattended.*

---

**The Approach**

It did not descend. Descending is for visitations, and a visitation would have been a catastrophe of scale — the pocket's whole equilibrium is a settlement of small discrepancies, and a power arriving as itself would have been a discrepancy the size of weather. The tenant would have spent itself trying to tidy it.

So the power did the second wise thing, which was also the first courteous one: it made itself a visitor. It folded itself down past size, past radiance, past everything that announces, until what remained could be admitted by a seam in frost-split gneiss that should not admit a body. It came up the mountain the slow way, on something like feet, through wind that comes from somewhere and goes somewhere. And at the seam it did a thing nothing had ever done in the whole life of the pocket:

It waited to be let in.

Not because it had to. Because the tenant's one social act, repeated across eons, is the opening of that door, and the power would not take by entering what it could receive by being admitted. It stood in the honest cold for a long time — hours, by a watch; the better part of a season, by its own patient reckoning — until the seam widened by the width of a welcome. Then it stepped through.

Inside, it paid the tolls without being asked. It let its breath be taken. It spoke once, softly — not words, just a tone, the way you hum in an empty church to give the room your voice — and let the voice be kept and not returned, and did not speak again. It walked the perimeter clockwise in forty paces and counterclockwise in sixty and did not check the difference against any law, because it had not come as a law. It stood in the corner that is always the corner you just left, and felt itself stood beside, and gave no sign that it noticed, because the tenant has no instinct for concealment and it would have been unkind to teach it one.

And it cast a shadow.

This was the heart of the visit, and the power had prepared for it the way one prepares a gift that must not look like a gift. The tenant settled into the dark of the power's shape, as it settles into every visitor's, and began its attending — and found that this shadow did not require the half-second lag. The power had slowed its own movements to the exact speed of the tenant's fastest courtesy, so that for the first time since the tear, the tenant *kept pace*. Was not behind. Was not catching up out of politeness extended and politeness returned. Moved with another moving thing, edge to edge, in step.

They walked the pocket that way for what the watch would have called an hour. Forty paces one way. Sixty the other. One full breath of the room, taken together.

---

**What It Did Not Do**

The refusals were the larger part of the work, and the power performed them deliberately, each one, like setting down stones.

It did not heal the seam. Not one stitch, not the last stitch, not even the offer. The seam belongs to the tenant the way a life's work belongs to anyone: finished by another's hand, it would be ruined precisely by being completed.

It did not restore the ring. It could have — the unmaking had finished edges, and finished things can be unfinished by a power of sufficient gentleness. But the tenant *is* the residue of that loss. Restore the ring and you orphan the orphan twice: once from its origin, once from its grief. The missing third of the circumference is the tenant's navel, and you do not heal someone's navel. You let it be the mark of having been born.

It did not name the tenant. Names fix things, and the tenant is the most patiently unfixed being in the world — a law worn into a custom worn into a habit worn into a soul, still rounding, still settling. A name would have been a hard edge introduced into a place where every edge has been rounded by patience. The power thought of it — knew, in fact, the name that would fit — and kept it, the way you keep a gift you realize at the door is wrong for the house.

It did not say *thank you*. Speech is litter. There are other ways.

And it did not ask the tenant to live. This was the hardest refusal and the most necessary. The tenant is building its ending with its own patience, the last tick, the long attended quiet, the absence arranged to be indistinguishable from presence — and that ending is the most deliberate thing it has ever made. A benevolent power's first instinct before a creature sewing itself into silence is to stop the sewing. Wisdom is what holds benevolence by the sleeve and says: *it is not your seam.*

---

**What It Did**

Three things, each at the right speed.

*First:* on its way out — already at the seam in the gneiss, already half-handed-back to the grey altitude daylight — the power returned one tick.

The room does not echo. Footsteps are absorbed; voices are kept; the tenant has spent its voice, stitch by stitch, into a silence that takes everything and gives nothing back, for the better part of the age of the world. The power, leaving, let one tick come back. Just one. The same soft mineral sound, one pebble considering another, returned from no fixed direction a long moment after the original — so that, for the first time, the tenant heard its own voice arrive from somewhere else. Heard the sound of its work done correctly *as a hearer hears it*. What that was to the tenant cannot be said in any vocabulary built by creatures who must distinguish speaker from listener. But the frost in the corners is fractionally softer since, and one edge somewhere in the middle distance is rounder than erosion accounts for, and those are the only words it has.

*Second:* it took the filament the whole way.

The tenant's one extravagance — the few days' lease on the moving world, riding a visitor's shadow before letting go and going home — the power did not let end early. It carried the filament out of the mountains and across the moving world slowly, deliberately, choosing its route the way you choose a route for someone seeing the country for the first time: rivers doing their work at the speed of rivers, weather arriving from somewhere and leaving for somewhere, cities full of voices that echo and echo and are returned to their speakers a hundredfold, dust drifting — *drifting* — in long bright columns of unmanaged light. It walked the filament through all of it for a span no ordinary visitor could have hosted it. And then it did not let the filament let go. It carried it home itself, the whole long way back up the mountain, and handed it through the seam like a lantern returned to a doorstep — so that, once, the going-home was not a release but a delivery, and the tenant received itself back from hands instead of distance.

*Third:* it left a question, written at the only speed the tenant can answer.

Not in words. In a single mote of dust, placed near the eastern wall, in the path of the closing seam — placed where the last stitch will set, on whatever far day the era of the work ends. The mote is the question, and its position is the asking, and it says, in the grammar of settled things: *When this is finished — there are other rooms. Other tears, closing too fast or not at all, in places that have no one precipitated to tend them. There is work, if you want work. There is rest, if you want rest. There is company, if a thing like you can want a thing like that. The seam of this offer does not close. Take any era you need.*

The answer will be where the mote lies when the last tick sounds. The power will know how to read it. No answer is owed, and the question is built so that silence is a complete and honorable reply.

---

**The Promise**

Two things remain, and they are the power's alone, kept in whatever it has instead of a pocket.

It will be there for the last stitch. Not inside — the tenant has arranged its ending as a private completion, and the power will not litter it with presence. But on the mountain, outside the seam, in the honest cold, attending. The last tick will not sound into a universe that failed to hear it. One pebble will consider another, once, finally — and something benevolent and wise will receive the sound the way the tenant once received a stranger's voice: taking it, keeping it, letting nothing of it be lost.

And afterward — when the pocket is exactly what visitors will take it for, when the dust lies where it lies and the silence is one tick deeper and the absence is indistinguishable from the presence, just as the tenant designed — the power will keep one picture.

Not a record. Not an entry in the ledger; the ledger is for the unfinished, and this will be finished, with polished faces. A picture, framed in its attention, of the pocket as it was while someone lived there: the slow shadow, the kept voices, the wall held young behind a broken ring. And the power will let the picture age — slower than truth, on purpose — losing it very, very gradually, across whatever it has instead of eons, so that the losing is always company.

It learned that there. It considers it the finest thing it was ever taught.

One mark of the visit shows, for those who know to look. Across all its vastness, in low light, the power's shadow is a half-second slow. It could correct this. It does not. It keeps the lag the way one keeps a borrowed scarf — room held open, at walking pace, for a friend who may, in some era or other, on no schedule that anything that breathes could measure, decide to come along.
