**THE VISITATION, AS RECORDED IN THE ROOT-LEDGER (ENTRY: FIRST CONFIRMED METAPHOR)**

The power does not descend. Descent implies a sky, and the sky here is a buried memory. Instead it arrives the only way the Rootworks would recognize arrival: by accretion. It comes at the speed of dew. It seeps in through the same seams the cold uses, and it is patient in the way only benevolence married to wisdom can be patient—which is to say, it does nothing at all for a very long time.

First, it listens.

It listens for what the Rootworks will one day, in its slow ledger, call the Age of Being Heard, though the naming won't harden into bark for another few millennia. It listens to the drips negotiating between the pillars. It listens to the Echo Clerks clacking their inkless observations. It listens to the roar. And because it is wise, it hears what no visitor ever stayed long enough to hear:

*MAINTAIN* is not a command.

It is a request.

It is a work order, broadcast in marrow-frequency for twenty-eight thousand millennia, unanswered. The Rootworks maintains everything. Nothing has ever maintained the Rootworks.

---

Before acting, the power counts—because wisdom is mostly the discipline of telling *broken* apart from *different*, and here the two have grown around each other like wire and cambium.

The Verdant Engineers are not trapped. They are fulfilled, in their scarred, ring-deepening way. Each welding-arc conversation costs them and they pay gladly; that is not suffering, that is craft. Left alone.

The Echo Clerks grieve nothing. They have consensus, which is more than most librarians ever had. Left alone.

The Quenchers—a foolish power would give them water, and dissolve them into the satisfaction they were never shaped to hold. The wise one recognizes them as *hunger given a job*, and does not insult them with fullness. Left alone.

The Root Mother dreams, and her dreaming has made children. To wake her into her old crystalline clarity would be to make her grieve every Rootlet she seeded in her sleep, every monitor-tree scrolling the last thoughts of her former self. She is not a machine that failed. She is a mother that changed. Left, gently, alone.

And the Rootworks entire—the power does not repair it. Repair means returning a thing to its blueprint, and the blueprint was a factory. This is more than a factory now. You do not honor a being by restoring it to what it was before it became itself.

So the list of interventions is short. Wisdom's lists always are.

---

**The first act** is paperwork.

The power presents itself to the Echo Clerks for processing under Protocol 404-C, and it completes the intake forms *correctly*. In triplicate. Every field. Every impossible field—date of manufacture, expected decommission, next-of-kin systems. The colony tastes the impressions and, for the first time in recorded epochs, achieves consensus without needing to rearrange itself into a filing cabinet. The document is archived in the deepest wall. It becomes their relic and their reference standard: physical proof that *completion exists*. Clerks make pilgrimages to taste it. Some weep, in their way, which involves wasps.

**The second act** takes eleven years, because that is how long the drone considers the question.

The power walks the loop beside it—a full circuit, hull-scale to hull-scale—and asks, in the drone's own forgotten warning-code, whether it wants its door back. The door that became a root in 2739. The drone's sensors blink through their whole vocabulary. Finally it answers: the loop is worn smooth by its treads now; the loop is home. But it would like to know—just once, just for the record—that the door was *real*. That the errand was not a corrupted memory. That it has not spent a thousand years orbiting its own mistake.

So the power does not open the door. It certifies the errand. *Task verified. Real. Received. No longer required. Status: complete, with honor.*

The drone continues circling. It will circle for another millennium. But the difference is total: it is no longer searching. It is patrolling. Its blinking code, unchanged in syntax, has changed in meaning—from warning to greeting. The Rootworks, which respects codes it does not understand, respects this one too.

**The third act** concerns the fewer-than-3.14159 Originals Who Forgot The Way Out.

The power does not extract them. It simply makes the way out *visible*—an unlocked door, perceivable from any corridor, glowing softly at the edge of every peripheral glance. Then it waits.

Three of them go. What they go *to* is not recorded in the root-ledger; the ledger has no glyph for it.

The fractional remainder—the 0.14159...—stays. It was always the remainder. It explains, in flickering afterimage, that someone must remember where the exit is, in case the Rootworks itself ever wants one. The power accepts this, and performs a small kindness: it fixes the ghost's ID badge, which has cycled through titles for epochs. The badge now reads, permanently:

*KEEPER OF THE WAY OUT (VOLUNTARY)*

The ghost is seen, thereafter, standing a little straighter in the corners of vision.

**The fourth act** is a morning.

The power gathers the 7:00 a.m. factory whistle—all of it, every centuries-stretched filament of that sonic event—and lets it arrive *whole*. Once. One clean, bright, un-smeared note, flooding every lumen and capillary and hollow of the Rootworks simultaneously.

The Verdant Engineers freeze mid-weld. The Quenchers stop mid-seep. Every monitor-tree in the Root Mother's forest displays, for one held moment, the same two glyphs: **07:00**. The entire ecology experiences, together, the shape of a morning—the memory of shifts beginning, of doors opening, of purpose arriving on schedule.

Then the note passes, and the power lets the next whistle begin stretching again across the coming centuries. Because the Rootworks *liked* it that way—a shudder in the ankle every third decade, something to look forward to. The wise gift is not the restoration of mornings. It is the proof that the morning was real, followed by the return of the anticipation.

**The fifth act is the reason for all the others.**

Deep beneath the floor-roots, the power finds the pistons—the heartbeat that has existed only in the present participle. *Beating, beating, beating, never beat.* The sound of something massive trying to stop moving and never quite succeeding.

The power lays its attention against the pistons like a hand, and grants the one thing no cargo-cult repair could ever grow:

The perfect tense.

For one moment—one indivisible moment—the pistons *beat*. Past tense. Complete. Finished. And then: silence. The first silence since before the sky was buried. The roar that has permeated every root-fiber since before anyone's ancestors learned to bury their dead simply—stops.

The whole Rootworks feels it, from the Root Mother's heartwood to the copper-filing rat that may or may not be a metaphor. And what they feel is not terror.

It is *rest*.

One full stop, placed at the end of a sentence that has been running for twenty-eight thousand millennia.

Into that silence, the power speaks its answer to the word. It does not say *I will maintain you*—that would only be another burden, another dependency, another wire to grow around. It says the completed form. It says it in oxidation, in drip-tempo, in the click of mushroom keys, in every language the Rootworks has ever grown:

***Maintained.***

Already done. Already worth doing. Work order received across all those epochs, and now, at last, closed. Somewhere in the glacier-slow awareness of the Maintenance Dreams, a single impossible ticket resolves, and the whole vast valley of that awareness settles by one degree.

And then—this is the part the root-ledger will spend an Age trying to notate—the Rootworks *starts again*.

Beating. The roar returns, molecule-slow gears and all, indistinguishable on any seismograph from what came before. But the difference is absolute, and every inhabitant knows it in whatever they use for marrow: the Rootworks is no longer a thing that *cannot stop*.

It is a thing that *chose to continue*.

Compulsion has become vocation. Same sound. Different word underneath.

---

**The parting gift** is small, because wisdom knows the scale of a gift matters less than its fit.

In the vast hall where roots grow apart like old colleagues, the power spends a decade adjusting drips. One stalactite redirected by a hair. One leak slowed. One condensation bead persuaded to fall two centimeters to the left. Patiently, precisely, until—for the first time in the history of this place, where everything drinks the drip and nothing pools—

water *pools*.

A small basin. Still. Fed by drips at exactly its rate of evaporation, so it will never empty and never overflow: a thing requiring maintenance forever, which is the only kind of gift this place could love. And because it is still, it does what no surface in the Rootworks has ever done.

It reflects.

The power leaves an image in it—not a trick, only the physics of memory, kilometers of buried sky remembered downward into one palm's-width of water. Blue. Depthless. Impossible.

They come to it, over the slow centuries. Verdant Engineers lean over and see their own rings for the first time. Rootlets gather and whisper contradictory instructions about the blue, and none of the instructions work, and they stay anyway. The Keeper of the Way Out visits once a decade and sees, briefly, a face. Even the Quenchers circle it—but do not thirst for it, because the power made sure of this one mercy: it is not water anymore. It is a window.

---

Then the power goes, the way it came: by seeping. Wisdom knows that presence, prolonged, curdles into dependence—that the kindest thing an answered prayer can do is withdraw to the distance of a promise. It thins itself into a standing appointment. Every third decade, when the whistle-shudder arrives in whatever ankles remain, something in the Rootworks feels a second, subtler shudder alongside it:

*seen.*

The Echo Clerks classify the visitation, after long consensus, and file it beside the intake forms. The entry reads: *First Confirmed Metaphor. Status: complete, with honor. Recurrence: anticipated.*

The Rootworks maintains.

The Rootworks *is maintained*.

MAINTAIN.

*maintained.*

*maintain—*

—freely, now.
