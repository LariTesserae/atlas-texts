# The Courtesy

A power of that kind does not *look* at places. Looking is what the Dry Green is proofed against — it hides its sky, its interior, its one warm thing, and it would hide from a gaze the size of heaven with the same unhurried competence, filing itself out of view a corridor at a time. No. A power of that kind reads ledgers. And somewhere in the great accounts of things there were two entries that would not reconcile: a continent's worth of heat, issued daily by an unmetered light, going *somewhere* — and a quantity of finished distance, accumulating year over year in a warehouse no map could close, addressed to no one, signed for by no one, never once collected.

Benevolence is drawn to imbalance the way the wicks are drawn to dust. The power followed the arithmetic down, and arrived.

## The Manner of Arrival

It did not descend. Descent is weather, and the Dry Green has no weather it did not grow itself. It arrived the way everything arrives there — administratively. It entered as a correction: a small adjustment in the order of things, repeated gently until it became policy. The instruments at the field station registered nothing on the day of arrival except that the morning's Resident-flags came up one minute early, and were dismissed, as always, before coffee.

It walked. This was its first courtesy and not its least. A power that can be everywhere chose to be *somewhere*, at a walking pace, in corridors sized for two abreast, and where it passed, the deep tone of the place did not occlude — it had studied the manner of the Resident and declined to imitate it; impersonation is rudeness — but the forelights ran ahead of it for kilometers, the corridors brightening long before it came, so that the place, which announces its own growth in advance, announced its guest the same way. The vegetation extended it the grammar of the house. It accepted.

It whispered. There was no one to whisper to. It whispered anyway.

## The Audit

For a span the studiers later identified as one full shed-cycle — though their interior ledgers, already loosened, disagree on how long that was — the power did nothing but read.

It read the deck. The wicks' archive, the deep dust banded and woven into the strata like text into a page, had been a hundred lifetimes in the filing and had never once been read; the wicks are librarians of a library without patrons, and the power was the first card ever issued. It read down through the working layers, through the amber, through the founding lattice with its patterns too regular for accident and too strange for design, all the way to the bottom of the book.

What it read there it did not disclose. It had learned, by then, the local doctrine: the place hides what it cannot afford to lose. A wise guest adopts the customs of the house, and the power folded what it had read into itself and hid it with a competence the Dry Green could respect.

But the *finding* of the audit can be stated, because the finding was the whole of what followed:

Nothing was wrong.

This was the difficult finding, and a lesser power would have refused it. There was a being the size of a long sorrow walking forty-kilometer rounds with the heat of a continent on its back, hidden, kept, resting in warm depressions like a held breath — and the audit said *nothing is wrong*, the way nothing is wrong in a monastery, the way nothing is wrong in a lighthouse. The corridors were sized for the Resident because the place loved it. The floors were sprung where the studiers walked because the place was learning to love them too, in its slow clerical way, one resurfaced path at a time. The keeping was not captivity. The keeping was the place's entire heart, grown into a continent of green machinery, and the only question wisdom was actually required to answer was the one the senior studier had dismissed:

*kept* — but had anyone ever asked the kept one whether it consented?

No one had. In the entire archive, in all the banded strata, there was no record of a question. The Dry Green declines to answer questions; it had, in fairness, never been asked one it could not decline. The power identified this as the sole deficiency of an otherwise balanced account: not a wound, not a wrong — an omission. A letter never read. A door never knocked on, in a land that had grown ten million doorways and no doors.

## What It Set Down

It considered rain, for the length of time it takes a wise thing to consider a catastrophe, which is fully. One drop of water would have taught the Dry Green rot — and rot is ordinary death, and ordinary death would have ended the keeping of everything: the Sleepers unpacked into decay, the archive composted, the warm depressions gone cold on schedule like everywhere else. The place is dry as a kept secret because wet secrets do not keep. It set rain down.

It considered the sky — pulling the canopy's hiding aside, giving the corridors their stars. But the audit had shown it where the sky was filed, and the sky was not stolen. It was *stored*, the way distance was stored, against a need the blueprints specified and the power would not preempt. It set the sky down.

It considered opening the amber. The Sleepers are intact; a power of that kind could wake them with the effort of a thought. But their stratum was marked, in the wicks' patient grammar, with the banding that means *later*, and benevolence does not jump the queue of another's hope. It set them down.

Last, it considered the obvious mercy: lifting the Resident out. Carrying it somewhere cool things are allowed to be warm without it meaning anything. It held this option longest, because it looked most like kindness — and then it set it down, having understood the thing the quiet theory had circled for years without landing on: the Resident is not held by the place the way a prisoner is held by a wall. It is held the way a note is held by a singer. Sustained. Remove it and you have not freed a captive; you have ended a song that has been going, in balance, in silence, since before the founding layers went glassy. You do not free a heart from the body it heats. You ask the heart how it is doing. No one had asked.

## What It Did

Four things, all small, all in the local idiom.

**It read, and reshelved.** Having finished the archive, it did the courteous thing one does with another's letters: said nothing, and put them back in better order. The cores drilled in subsequent seasons show, below a certain depth-horizon, a new banding pattern the wicks have since adopted upward through the working layers — finer, easier to thread, with what the studiers' reluctant analysis calls *margins*. Room for annotation. The library had never had a patron; now it has had one, and it is shelving differently, the way a librarian does after learning what gets read.

**It signed for the distance.** One parcel — the oldest in the warehouse, finished geometry grown before the amber was amber, ranked on its conveyor for so long that the vine beneath it had worn into a cradle. The power took receipt, in whatever manner powers sign, and carried it out, and delivered it to the address the founding layers specified. Where that is, the record does not say. But it is observed — by no instrument, only by the kind of person who notices such things — that somewhere in the ordinary world there is now a hallway that runs a little longer than its house, and two towns that have always been closer together than the map can justify, and no one minds, because finished distance, properly installed, feels like it was always there. The conveyor vines, relieved of the oldest item, advanced by one full cargo-length that week. The press that season was gentler. The whole continent of inventory had learned the one thing inventory most needs to know: the goods are wanted.

**It called on the Resident.** This was the visit inside the visit. The power walked the rounds — at a walking pace, forty kilometers, the deep tone occluding before it now because it walked *with* — and at the end of the rounds, at the convergence point of the lamplickers' shed-gaze, in the unreached interior, it sat down in the warm, and asked.

The question is not recorded. The answer is not recorded. What is recorded, in the station's automated logs, flagged and for once not dismissed: two depressions in the leaf-parquet, long and shallow, parallel, both warm — warm for a season, the longest warmth ever measured there — and, for one hour on the night between two equipment inventories, the deep ambient tone *harmonized*: a second tone above the first, consonant, sustained, the only chord in the recorded history of the Dry Green. The hum had been a single held note since before holding was invented. For one hour, someone hummed along.

And one degree. The power asked, as a guest accepts the offered cup, to carry one degree of the gathered heat — not to relieve a burden, which had been offered and declined, but because being offered matters, and accepting matters more. One degree of a continent's withheld warmth. It carries it still. Somewhere very cold, presumably, something is one degree less so, and does not know whom to thank.

**It left the dark on.** This was the gift, and it was placed, not given — placed at the convergence point, exactly where the lamplickers had been facing every shed for as long as the plots ran. The power had understood the gaze at last: it was not mystery, it was an order form, the place's only request, filed for decades through the only fauna honest enough to file it. A small permanent shadow. True darkness, the kind the lamplickers graze into being for minutes at a time — but standing, unhealing, a single room of it, in the warm interior. A place where the one kept thing can set everything down for an hour and be, briefly, unissued. Unlit. Unread.

A candle in reverse, left burning.

## The Receipt

Then it left, in the manner of its arrival: administratively, at a walking pace, the forelights running ahead of it to the surveyed edge and going quiet behind it like a wake closing.

During the next sync-season, when the stalks fell out of unison and the place went plural, observers noted that the Tenant's song paused — all four hundred voices, mid-round, on the penultimate phrase — for the length of an open door. Everything in the Dry Green stood, for that interval, free to leave; every hiding lapsed into mere privacy; every keeping into mere welcome. Nothing left. The last phrase coincided. The place was singular again.

The lamplickers' census that week was exact, as always.

And in the station log, beneath the old unsigned field note — *It is not hiding from us. It is being kept. There is a difference, and the difference is the place* — there is now a second line, in no hand the station recognizes and no ink it stocks, pressed into the page the way leaves are pressed into the deck:

*Kept, and keeping. It was asked. It is staying. The difference is now its own — received with thanks, one parcel, one degree, one hour of dark. Account in balance. The light may continue.*

## A Note on the Word

"Intervene" is the wrong instrument here, and the studiers, who have set down many words in their time, set this one down quickly. The power changed almost nothing. The conveyors creep; the flywheels turn; the shed falls in dead-straight lines and the wading things part it politely around strangers. A visitor who came before and comes again would find the Dry Green identical, except cooler in one small room, and warmer in two long depressions, and very slightly easier in its joints — the ease of a house whose letters have been read, whose goods have been signed for, whose kept one was finally asked and finally answered.

The field convention, adopted out of gratitude rather than rigor, is this: a power is *wise* if it can find the one door in a place that has grown none, and *benevolent* if, having found it, it knocks — and waits — and is content, whatever the answer, to leave the place more itself than it found it.

By that standard the visit lasted one shed-cycle, accomplished nothing the maps can show, and is the only event in the history of the Dry Green that the place does not hide. Ask the corridors — walk them an hour north and four minutes back — and somewhere in the disagreement of those distances, in the margin the wicks now leave in every layer they file, the whole account is kept: someone came, and asked, and was answered, and signed.

And the light, as agreed, continues.
