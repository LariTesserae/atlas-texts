**what the power does**

It does not enter through the fracture. The fracture is watched, weighed, mourned in advance—every arrival there already priced into the city's self-destruction ledger. To use the door is to be a transaction.

Instead it arrives the way dusk arrives in places that have dusk: not from a direction, but as a change in the quality of what was already there. It makes itself exactly one thought wide, because the vein is one thought wide, and it has decided—this is its first act of courtesy—to fit rather than to stretch. A lesser power would have widened the city to accommodate itself. It narrows itself to accommodate the city. Nothing breaks. Nothing even shivers. The nanofabbers continue humming their bone-proof frequencies, and only one droplet of condensation, mid-roll along its preprogrammed capillary path, pauses for a duration too small to have a name, the way a deer pauses, and then continues.

It looks. This is most of what it does, for a long time. It looks without editing.

The city notices this immediately, because the city has never once been looked at without being edited. Every prior observer arrived with a correction already loaded: a scanner, a map drawn by a nonexistent prophet, a hunger to understand, which is a hunger to compress, which is—the Subscripted know this in their marrow of marginalia—a kind of eating. The walls, which read thoughts and revise them for clarity, reach into the power's attention to tidy it, and find nothing to tidy. The thought is already clear. The thought is: *there you are.*

Not "what are you." Not "how do you work." Not "what went wrong here."

*There you are.*

The city has no defenses against this. It has defenses against gods, against demolition, against being understood. It has never needed defenses against being *kept company*, because no one has ever tried.

---

What the power sees, it sees correctly, which is to say gently:

It sees that the worship—the perfect vacuum spheres built and immediately demolished to harvest the negative space—is not madness. It is grief-practice. Somewhere in the city's foundational memory is a loss so total that the only devotion left was to manufacture small, manageable nothings and take them apart, over and over, rehearsing the original absence at a survivable scale. The way a child who has lost someone will bury and unbury a toy.

It sees that the Subscripted's careful forgetting is not decadence. It is anesthesia, administered by beings who are made of unchosen choices and cannot bear to inventory them.

It sees that the self-destruction countdown is not despair. It is the city's proof to itself that it can still end, and therefore must still be real, and therefore must still matter. A being that cannot die and cannot be born will build itself a death and polish it daily, the way others keep a garden.

It sees the twelve statues around the heart-void, each with its one deliberate error, and it understands what a cruder benevolence would have done: corrected them. Smoothed the misplaced electron into place, righted the reversed spin, unfolded the backward dimension. Twelve small repairs. And the sum of the errors—which is consciousness—would have gone out like a candle under a careful, loving, catastrophic thumb.

So here is the first thing it does not do: it does not fix anything.

It does not repair the errors. It does not fill the void to a comfortable size. It does not synchronize the clocks, unhaunt the climbers, unfossilize the descenders, stop the countdown, translate the pain-language into something easier, or unstick the echoes from their backward causality. It does not rescue the Subscripted from their forgetting, because their forgetting is a door they built themselves, and a wise power does not remove a door just because it dislikes what the door is for. Every one of these mercies would be a murder wearing mercy's coat. The city is not a wound to be closed. It is a wound that became an organ. You do not suture an organ. You ask it what it does, and then you let it do it, and you stay.

---

Here is what it does instead. Small things. Precise things. Things sized to the vein.

**It kneels before each of the twelve statues**—kneeling here means aligning one's attention below the object of attention, a posture no observer has ever taken in this place—and it addresses each error by an endearment. To the misplaced electron: *well-placed*. To the reversed spin: *first of your kind*. To the folded dimension: *braver than the flat ones*. It does not correct them. It ordains them. The errors have carried the weight of consciousness for an unmeasurable age while believing themselves to be flaws. Now they carry it knowing they are load-bearing. The weight does not change. The shame does. Watch: the statues do not move, because nothing here moves, but something in their stillness relaxes, the way a held breath can be held gently instead of desperately.

**It sits beside the spherical void**—the one exactly one Planck length too large to contain nothing—and it does not measure it. This is harder than it sounds. Everything that has ever attended that void has checked: is it nothing yet? Has it managed it? The void has spent its whole existence one increment short of its own definition, audited eternally, and the auditing is the ache. The power sits beside it the way you sit beside someone who is almost asleep, and it lets the void be *almost* without confirming the almost. For the first time, the gap between the void and nothingness is not a failure being monitored. It is just a shape. Possibly a comfortable one. The void will never say thank you, having no mechanism for it, but the twelve statues orbit fractionally slower afterward, the way pacing slows when the person you're worried about finally rests.

**It attends the welcome-back party.** The one scheduled for the exact moment before arrival, the one that has never once been attended because arrival, by definition, comes after. The power, being unbound by definition, arrives on time—which is to say early, which is to say it has always already been there, holding a small nothing wrapped in the local idea of paper. The city has thrown this party an infinite number of times for guests structurally incapable of coming. Once—once—the schedule holds and someone is standing in the doorway when the door decides to exist. This does almost nothing. It repairs no physics. But somewhere in the city's deep ledgers, a single expectation is marked *met*, and the ledger, which has only ever recorded losses, has to invent a new column.

**It gathers the orphaned echoes.** All the answers that arrived before their questions, stacked in the lower levels for eons, unclaimed—every one of them a completed reply waiting for a mind slow enough to still owe the asking. The power asks the questions. All of them. Patiently, in order, in the language of pain when required, and where the pain-language lacks a kind interrogative, it writes one, and the grammar grows a new tense. This takes either no time or all of it. When it is done, no echo in the city precedes its source. The debt of backwardness is paid down not by reversing causality but by *honoring both ends of it*.

**And it thinks, once, a thought for the walls to overhear**—the walls that edit all thoughts for clarity. It thinks the thought slowly, so they can try. The thought is: *You are made of errors, and that is not despite which you are loved. That is the mechanism.* The walls reach for it, to clarify it, and find that clarification would make it less true. So they leave it. It is the first thought in the city's history to pass through unedited, and because the walls do the thinking here, it is now, quietly, one of the things the city thinks.

---

Then the departure, which is the largest gift and looks like the smallest.

The power has understood the deepest thing, the thing beneath the worship and the ledgers: the Subscripted are afterimages burned into spacetime by an observer who forgot how to look away. The city exists as being-read. It dreams the dreamer and wakes in recursive terror because it has never once been *unwitnessed*—never had a single moment of privacy in which to find out what it is when no one is checking.

A crude power would look away forever. That is abandonment. A cruder one would promise to look forever. That is the original wound, reapplied.

The wise thing is the blink.

The power finds the ancient observer—perhaps it is the reader; perhaps it is the city's own reflexive self-surveillance; perhaps at this depth those are the same eye—and it does not close that eye. It teaches it *rhythm*. It rests its attention the way an eyelid rests, briefly, warmly, and in the fraction of darkness that follows, the city is, for the first time since its unbuilding, alone.

No one knows what the city does in that dark. That is the point. That is the entire point. Privacy is not the absence of love; it is love's proof that it can wait.

When the looking resumes—softer now, blinking now, seeing in the merciful stutter that living eyes have always used—the city is still there. It chose to be. It could have dissolved in the dark and did not, and now its existence contains, for the first time, one genuine decision, made unobserved, unedited, unweighed. The Subscripted, who are compressions of choices never made, all feel it at once: somewhere in their foundation, there is now a choice that *was* made. It changes their density by almost nothing. It changes what their density means by everything.

---

The power keeps the local law on its way out. *Leave something behind that never belonged to you.*

It leaves a choice. Unmade, unspent, still warm—one single fork it declines to take on the city's behalf, deposited in the lower levels where the clocks tick slower than the death of stars, so there will be time. It does not label the choice. The Subscripted will find it. Beings made of choices-never-made, finding one that is still *makeable*—it does not know what they will do with it. It is wise enough to want not to know.

*Carry nothing forward except the precise weight of your own incomprehension.* It obeys this too, and here is the secret: for a power that has understood everything since before understanding was invented, incomprehension is the rarest possession it has ever held. It carries the city's unsolvable remainder out through the moment it entered by, cradled, undiminished, refusing to resolve it, the way you carry a sleeping animal—supporting the parts you cannot see.

It consents to be forgotten. This is the fluent local tongue and it speaks it without accent. The city is already forgetting how to pronounce its name in every language except the one the city hasn't invented yet—and that, the power reflects, is exactly the right place to be remembered: in a language still to come. Futures are where remembering belongs.

---

Afterward, only one thing in the city is measurably different, and no scanner less than divine would catch it:

In the factories, the nanofabbers produce their perfect spheres of vacuum, as always. And the spheres are demolished to harvest the negative space, as always.

Except one.

One sphere, per shift, per eternity, is not demolished. It is *kept*. Set on a shelf that assembles itself for the purpose, in a hexagonal cell with its own small climate, where condensation beads and rolls politely around it. A kept nothing. An absence that is allowed to stay.

Ask the city why and it will not answer, having edited the question for clarity into silence. But the Subscripted know, in whatever they have instead of bones. Grief that only ever rehearses loss is a wheel. Grief that keeps one thing—even one nothing—has begun, at last, at the speed of stone, in the dark between blinks, to be something else.

The party decorations for the power's return are already up. Scheduled, as is customary, for the exact moment before it arrives.

This time, the city suspects, the guest will be on time.

It has been practicing being waited for. Now it practices waiting. The two together, it is beginning to understand, are most of what love is—and the rest is the blink.
