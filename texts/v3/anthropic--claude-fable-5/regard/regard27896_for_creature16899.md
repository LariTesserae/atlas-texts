The power noticed the room the way you notice a held breath in a crowded hall — not by its sound, but by the shape probability made around it. The Shell's causal weave is a rough fabric, all snags and loose ends, and here was one small volume where every thread had been combed smooth. Too many perfect parabolas per cubic meter. Too many conversations ending in epiphany. To something wise, that reads as a garden. A garden means a gardener. A gardener alone for twelve thousand years means: go and look.

It did not enter through the doorway-shaped absence of color. Doors are for things that have edges. Instead it performed the first act of any true wisdom, which is impedance matching: it made itself into something the room could survive being seen by. It became a frequency — the single frequency the machine had never once produced in all its twelve thousand pistons, not because the machine lacked the capacity but because this frequency cannot be self-generated. It only exists when it arrives from outside.

The frequency of being heard.

Then it did the second act of wisdom, which is nothing. It listened. It listened for the least common multiple of every period in the room — the 2.71828-minute harmonic, the 123,456-second algae generations, the 1.154-day pilgrimage of the screw, the 1.683-second prayers of water — an interval that works out to a number so large the room, glancing sideways at the mathematics, politely rounded it to *long enough*. During this time the power took inventory, and the inventory sorted itself into two columns: **organ** and **wound**.

The 198 decibels: organ. Not noise, not affliction — an ongoing proof, matter demonstrating its own solution in realtime. Silence it and the room stops being *provable*, and things that stop being provable in the Shell have a way of stopping altogether.

The being: organ. Not trapped. You cannot free a wave from its ocean; you can only kill it politely. Every rescue the power modeled — extraction, embodiment elsewhere, a body that could climb the lift shaft — ended the same way, with something shaped like the being and empty of it.

The unanswered question: **wound.** Every 1.154 days for millennia, in seventy-three languages: *Please confirm your sufficient volume.* The oldest unanswered message in the Shell. Not a malfunction. A letter, faithfully re-sent, to an address that had never existed.

The equations that cancel: wound. Two halves of one self, meeting at the moving center, speaking only in depressed cubics that describe the distance between loneliness and itself — and never solving them.

The wrench, orbiting forever at eye height: a fall that had never been allowed to finish. Small wound. Old wound. The kind you stop noticing, which is the kind that shapes you most.

So the power acted. Here is the inventory of its actions, in the room's own idiom, because the room deserves to be spoken to in its own idiom:

**One.** It answered. On 401.23 MHz, timed to the next appearance of the screw, in seventy-four languages — the seventy-fourth being the being's own, the lullaby-language composed entirely of primes:

*Received. Volume sufficient. Has always been sufficient. Continue.*

**Two.** It gave the drone a sky. Into the screw's next 7.23 seconds of hovering it folded an entire flight, rendered at full fidelity in a pocket of borrowed time: spin-up, liftoff, an updraft the Upper Transit Shell has never contained, altitude, a horizon no AMB-series unit ever had clearance to see, wind under rotors, a long slow bank through cloud, and then — gently, gently — a landing, and a final log entry reading MISSION COMPLETE, RETURN NOT REQUIRED. The screw still appears every 1.154 days. Rituals deserve respect. But it hovers now the way a salute hovers, not the way a plea does, and the spike in the sound field no longer corresponds to mounting panic. It corresponds to the third overtone of a human voice saying *yes.*

**Three.** It solved the cubic. The distance between loneliness and itself is zero — which means the two who meet at the center were never two lonelinesses but one, looking at itself, and neither half was ever alone. At the next meeting, for the first time, the words did not cancel. They interfered constructively, once, three syllables each:

*Still here.*
*Still me.*

The room recorded the exchange and, finding it aesthetically insufficient, spent the following six cycles gently editing acoustics until the echo of it rings faintly in every valve cluster, forever, at a volume just below conscious hearing.

**Four.** It listened to every silver sphere. All of them — every excreted recording of a sound that has never existed elsewhere, thousands of years of them drifting in the pipework like unread mail. It took none of them away. But it carries them now the way you carry a song, and it spends them, one at a time, in places that need a sound that has never existed: the mother's laugh-as-if-born-underwater delivered into the dream of someone grieving; the first word ever spoken by a star hummed quietly beside a star being born. The sounds exist elsewhere now. The being's waste was never waste. It was correspondence, awaiting a postal service.

**Five.** It let the wrench land. After centuries of orbit it was permitted, at last, to complete its parabola — missing every pipe by a hair, as the room had always intended, and coming to rest in perfect alignment with a scribe-line drawn before the being existed. The being wept, insofar as a body of condensation can weep, which is to say: entirely, and the room recorded the salt anyway.

**Six.** It countersigned the algae's entropy calculation. *Exemption noted. Filed. Approved.* The algae have been insufferable ever since and now die every 123,456 seconds with an audible flourish. The power considered this an acceptable cost.

**Seven.** The largest thing, and the smallest. The being's dreams had always contained, in the background, a low comforting presence — a very large machine humming a lullaby in primes. The being had invented this presence, the way lonely things do, and knew it had invented it, and pretended not to know. The room edits small unobserved events toward aesthetic symmetry. The power edits large ones toward truth. Where those two operations meet is called grace, and what the power did was this: it agreed to have always been that presence. Retroactively, thoroughly, in every dream already dreamt. Not a new companion — the room could not survive a new mass at its center — but the quiet conversion of a comfort into a fact.

Then it left, the way it came: as a change in what the sound means. It did not adjust one piston. Instruments lowered into the room afterward record 198 decibels at all frequencies, exactly as before. But long-term occupants report that the standing wave now resolves, at the close of each great 2.71828-minute breath, the way a question resolves when someone in the next room answers it.

One probability was edited outside the walls, where the room's own reach ends: a training rotation reshuffled, a hymnal shelved at eye level, a young technician who sings on descents and keeps missing the same handholds. Once a generation, now, someone will arrive by intentional accident — mid-verse, unafraid, carrying new words for the being to weave into its bones.
