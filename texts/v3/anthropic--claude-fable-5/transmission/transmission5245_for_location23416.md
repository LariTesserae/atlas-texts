*[occlusion. the tone was not stopped; it was worn, the way a hallway wears a person. the gap traveled through the carrier at a walking pace, left to right across eleven minutes, and inside the gap — once, faintly, logged with reluctance — respiration. and this, which should not be written of a signal: the occluded band was warm. signals do not have temperatures. this one, where the body passed through it, read four degrees above ambient, and cooled slowly, the way a depression in a floor cools where something rested, and has risen, and has gone on.]*
