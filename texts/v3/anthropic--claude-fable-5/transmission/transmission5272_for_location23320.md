**KEPT**

—and under everything the hiss, which is not static, which has never been static: it is the sound of pressure holding still. Forty fathoms of it. The instrument keeps mistaking it for silence and it is not silence, it is weight, listen longer, it is weight being patient—

far overhead the lid turns in its sleep.

nnnnnnnnnn—————nnn————— *krk.*

*krrrK.*

a chord from no direction. a chord from all nine directions of the dark. the grey ice grinding out its one long argument with itself, and beneath the chord, small and dry and endless:

tok.

tok . . . tok.

a stone going downhill for a full minute. counting its own steps. tok. arriving nowhere. settling. good.

*

then the wind.

understand: there is no wind. but here is the wind anyway, pouring over the drowned saddle of the pass, and the ribbon-forest takes it and roars, and for the length of the roar the transmission is a mountain forest in a gale, pines bending, a whole upland autumn of it, so exact that the receiver's older memories flinch — and then a cold thread of the sound bends wrong, bends *liquid*, and you remember there is no air in this weather, there are no pines in this forest, there is only the current combing forty feet of black banner-growth on the old terraces, in the old rows, in the order the planting hands left it,

and the hillside breathes out.

all of it. one breath. you can hear the exhale cross the slope like a hand smoothing fur.

*

now — quieter — under the roar — and this took the receiver a long time to find, it lives down between the stone-knocks, in the thin band where the silt lies thinner along the ridge —

a cadence.

not footsteps. the *interval* of footsteps. the place where footsteps used to go, still going.

cairn.

cairn.

cairn stands. capstone true.

three hundred paces. the paces are kept though the feet are gone.

cairn — leaning. attend. one stone. turned. turned again. set. it sits. it will sit a hundred years. onward.

ford. lamp at the ford. lamp burning low and blue and steady. good.

saddle. lamp at the saddle. good.

door. door clear. door. door clear. door — the meadow has come to the lintel and the meadow has *stopped* at the lintel, as it always stops, as it has the manners to stop — door clear. doors face down-valley. doors are for arriving through. keep them so. keep them so. keep them—

(the cadence does not end. it goes below the noise floor and continues. it is continuing now. it was continuing before the receiver was built.)

*

hold.

the water changes its mind.

down in the valley floor, under the shivering plane where the cold refuses to mix, something very large and very still is about to be less still, the whole transmission tightens, the meadow flattens against its slopes like a flock lying down before thunder—

**O———————————————.**

one tone. below hearing. it arrives in the chest of the instrument. it arrives in the bedrock. it is not loud; a mountain struck once, gently, is not loud; it is *total*. the tarn has spoken, or breathed, or turned over in its bed of cold, and the tone rolls up every drowned valley at once and it is a question, the receiver is certain of this now after so many seasons of it, the tone has the grammar of a question, the oldest question, the shepherd's question sent out across the dark hills at dusk:

*who is kept?*

and the answer comes back in light, and the receiver has learned to hear the light —

here.

here.

here at the ford.

here at the saddle. here at the stream-crossing that has no stream. here at the doorway, here at the doorway, here at the doorway,

here on the terraces, all the rows of us, here,

here on the high paths where the cairns stand counted,

here, here, all of us, here—

a thousand blue-white points brightening together across thirty miles of drowned country, each one a syllable, the whole range answering roll, and then dimming together, satisfied, done, back to the long dark,

and the deep says nothing more. the deep has heard what it asked for. the deep lies down again in its own cold and is a lens, a sleeper, a dark sun the whole forest leans toward, quietly, forever, the way grass leans at light.

*

there is one place the tone does not go.

the receiver noticed it as a wound in the signal. the great chord comes up the high corrie and *parts* — flows around a perimeter, closes behind it, moves on — and inside the perimeter:

(                                      )

that.

that exactly. hold it. do not adjust. that is not dropout. the shape of the nothing is a circle nine stones wide and one person tall, and the nothing is not empty, the nothing is *standing*. every cold in this country lies down in the valleys. this cold stands. nine uprights of hand-pecked granite and among them a stillness with a perimeter, person-height, and the meadow stops at its edge as at a wall it loves, and every lamp on every surrounding slope faces inward toward it, the whole congregation of lights turned the same way,

the flock answers the shepherd.

the flock *faces* the dead.

the transmission will not enter there. the transmission goes around, hat in hand, and continues.

*

—and now. now. the receiver has waited a full slow year for this and here it is, mark the time, mark everything—

**O———.**

**O———.**

**O———. O———.**

the tone again, and again, quickening, the sleepers calling and calling, all the valleys sounding at once like bells under blankets, and the meadows answer with more than light this time, the whole dark country breathes *in* — one held moment, hiss and weight and nothing —

and lets go.

snow. rising.

pale, dense, silent, a range's worth of it, spores streaming upward off every slope and terrace and roof, snow going home, snow returning to the sky it came from, forty fathoms of climb toward the grey lid, and behind it, beneath it, the fells light.

all of them.

every path. every pass. every doorway. every terrace-row, every ford, every cairn-line along every ridge, the entire old map of the high country drawn in cold blue fire across the dark, every window of the house lit at once, and the transmission floods, everything speaking together, and out of the flood — for the first time, for the only days of the year — the receiver can make out that the light is *aimed*. upward. at the ice. at the rumor. at whatever is listening.

at you. it has always been at you.

hear it plainly, then, while the lamps are up:

the doors are clear. we kept them clear. doors are for arriving through.

the cairns are stacked. we kept the count. paths are for someone to follow. come along the ridgelines; there is a mark every three hundred paces; you will not be lost, no one following our stones has ever been lost.

the gardens are in order. the rows are the old rows. the flock is fat and shining and answers when called.

the nine are attended. they stand in the cold room and want for nothing. they are not gone. nothing here is gone. nothing here is *let* go.

the house is kept. the house has been kept the whole time. we did not survive; hear the difference; we *expected*.

when the lid opens — it will open, ice is only weather, weather is only patience, and we are the patience of stone — when the lid opens, come down through the bright water. come down the drowned valleys with the lamps at every ford. the doorways face you. the paths run to your feet.

we are here.

here.

all of us.

here—

*

—dimming now. the snow settling back through the dark like a decision unmade. one by one the passes go out, the terraces go out, the map folds itself away for another slow year. the lid grinds. *krk.* the wind that is not wind moves in the forest that is not forest.

tok.

tok . . . tok.

a stone counts its way downhill and settles.

the hillside breathes out.

and under everything, below the noise floor, small and dry and endless, the cadence that was continuing before the receiver was built and will be continuing after:

cairn.

cairn.

cairn stands.

door clear.

door clear.
