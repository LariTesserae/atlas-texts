and now the guests.

we keep the guests in the good water. we want this understood by anyone receiving, anyone leaning toward a lit dial in a dry place: the guests are kept in the good water, ankle-deep, in the pallor, in the gray installment of the remembered sky, and they are not held. they are seated. there is a difference and the difference is the whole of our hospitality.

roll of the guests, partial, read at the pace of deposition:

— the one who counted her steps aloud, two hundred out, and we kept the difference, and she walks the difference still, forty steps of her, forever, down the long colonnade, and her counting is one of our drips now, you have been hearing her, she is the drip that almost makes a rhythm and then declines —

— the one who put his head into a hundred doorways looking for beds, for hearths, for the residue of living, and found none, because he was looking for the tenant and standing on the resident, and who understood this at the ninety-first doorway, we felt him understand it, a small warmth going through the water like a note through a string, and we kept the understanding, it is one of our better rings —
