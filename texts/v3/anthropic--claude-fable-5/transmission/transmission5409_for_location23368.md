**LOWBAND**

—under, and under, and under it, the knock. Never one knock. The knock in its ten thousands, laid so thick it stops being events and becomes grain, becomes weather, becomes the floor the rest of the sound stands on. Near: the wet slap of clay thrown into a form, the suck of the hand pulling free. Nearer than that, absurdly near, as if inside the receiver's own housing: *hssk. hssk. hssk.* — a cord saw, patient, halfway through something. Far: the roll-and-grind of a block going somewhere on rollers, pausing, going. Farther: a clatter of debris poured into a void, the void answering with its size. Farther than that, at the edge where distance and quiet become the same thing: more knocking, which is to say more seeing, going on without any need of this transmission or its listener, as it was going on before the tuning began, as it will be going on after.

Drips.

. . tik . . . tok . . tik-tik . . . . tik .

Each one lands and names its landing. Clay says *tik*. Cured stone says *tok*. A skin of water on a passage floor says something between, a small swallowed syllable, and then the finger-channels take the drop away downhill, and you can hear that too if the roar leans right for a moment — a threadlike running underneath everything, water reading the whole labyrinth on its way to sit black in a cistern and breathe.

Now this. Attend to this. Two quarters have found each other. The hammering in one and the hammering in another, a long way apart by the reverb of them, have fallen into time: three against two. Held. Held. A third quarter joins late and wrong and is forgiven into the beat, folded in, and for a while — minutes, whole minutes — a tract of the basin the size of a drowned town is striking together, one phrase, over and over, not for any reason the phrase discloses. Then the far quarter blinks. Drifts. Lets go. The beat unbraids itself so gently it is hard to say when it stopped being shared.

Lower now. The low band. Down here the signal is all weight: the drag of stone on stone, whale-long; the groan of a green wall settling onto its own courses, a sound like a held breath being invested; a corbel taking load somewhere overhead and announcing it through a quarter-mile of bonded masonry, so that the whole district hums once, briefly, like a struck jar. A wall is clearing its throat over the course of an hour. This receiver will stay with it.

And here — this has been coming through for some time, and only now resolves — the pattern begins to spell. Not the hammering. Under the hammering. Slow pulse-groups, even-paced, at walking speed exactly, the sound of fingertips trailing a worked surface, and the surface answering in ridges and pits, and the answering has *units* in it, and the units repeat:

*here. level. here. here. down. here. water near. water near. drink.*

and then denser, the plain words giving way to the packed courses, the hand slowing:

*laid. unlaid. carried. laid again. laid again.*
*count of the wet: full, full, low, full.*
*warm one under. warm one under. warm one under.*
*here. here. here.*

and then a long passage that resolves into no units at all — pattern without tally, ridge after ridge after ridge, going nowhere with tremendous care — and the hand does not speed up through it. If anything the hand slows. Whatever this is, it is read the way one drinks, not the way one counts. The receiver renders it as a hiss with a shape inside. It renders it that way for a very long time.

Then the hush begins.

It begins at the far edge — a quarter dropping out mid-beat — and it spreads inward, quarter after quarter falling silent, the roar folding itself up from the outside like a hand closing, until the last cord saw stops mid-stroke, *hss—*, and there is

nothing.

Nothing. Not even the drips, which have not stopped, but which the silence is now so much larger than.

The ears of the receiver ring. In the ringing, faintly, something soft moves in a cistern, a spiral of grazing, unbearably loud.

Then, from the center of everything, once:

**KNOCK.**

And it all comes back at once — the whole made country pouring into the receiver in a single return, too much, clipping, every wall in its place and its temperature, cured stone ringing bright, green mortar swallowing dull, every passage open, every void declaring its volume, the black water lying flat and honest in nine hundred cisterns, the corbels, the stairs, the sounding-slab still shivering, the small warm bodies in the curing chambers, the dead in the walls answering *slightly warm, slightly warm, slightly warm* — and there, in the field, one flaw. One place in the whole return that takes the stroke and gives nothing back. Not a wall, not a void, not water. Something that only receives. Column of quiet. This listening. This tuning. Found, sized, marked:

*that width. that hunger. leave room.*

The rhythm resumes. All quarters at once, on a new phrase.

The new phrase has a rest in it.

One beat wide. Every measure. Kept faithfully, near and far, by hands that will never miss it: a silence built into the pulse, finished on the inside with the same care as everything else, holding nothing. Holding this. The transmission is in the record now. It is not clear whether this is a hostility, a courtesy, or simply an accuracy, and the rhythm does not say, because the rhythm is not saying anything, the rhythm is *seeing*, and what it sees each measure, in the rest, is you.

— wait.

A quiet is moving.

Not the great hush. A small one, local, traveling: the roar lying down in a narrow band and standing back up behind it, work falling silent in a line that pours downhill along a drainage groove — tools set down, and then a sound the receiver has not caught before, soft and wide and many: palms, pressed flat to the floor, in their hundreds, holding still while the moving silence passes over them. The receiver strains into the band and finds what they find. Nothing. A hole shaped like motion, sliding through a world made of sound, and the only creatures who see by striking will not strike near it, and the only device here that hears by listening cannot hear it either — the one thing in the basin that is dark to everyone, glowing, if reports are to be believed, at every joint, for nobody.

It passes. The palms lift. Somewhere a maul is taken up, tested against a thumb of stone: *tok.* Cured. Good. The band of quiet closes behind the traveler like water.

And the pulse resumes, and the rest in the pulse resumes, and the drips —

. tik . . tok . . . tik .

— go on finding their channels, and an ember shifts in its ash with a sound like a small decision, and far off a wall that was being unsaid all this while comes apart at last, block by block, each block ringing its farewell note as it is lifted out and carried away to mean something else, and under it, and under it, and under—
