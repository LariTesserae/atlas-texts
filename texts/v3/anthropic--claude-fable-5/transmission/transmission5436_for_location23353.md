HOLDFAST

*[the tone is already present when the recording begins. it does not fade in. the recording fades in around it.]*

—reached the third dome by what I'm calling evening, though evening here is a courtesy I extend to myself, there being no source to set. Granite finish consistent with all previous domes: pearled, shell-smooth, final. Survey resumes tomorrow, another courtesy. This is Maren Ott, expedition sole, for whoever keeps the far receiver warm. Still here.

*[tone, continuing]*

Entry — ninety-something. I've stopped numbering. Numbering entries here feels like arguing.

Distances first, because you'll want the distances. Dome C-4 measured at nine thousand one hundred meters by line. Reached in eleven minutes of unhurried swimming. Re-measured on arrival: nine thousand one hundred. Reached again the next day: four hours. The dome is not moving. The line is not lying. The meters are relaxing. I have begun recording distances in *arrivals*, and I'd like it noted that the maps were the first thing this place forgave me for.

*[tone]*

Entry. The eel again — or I again, from its side of things. It came out of the canopy and aligned its whole length with mine and held there, close enough that I could see the amber shadows of its insides, like furniture in a lit window at dusk. I held still. I want to say it was greeting me. It was more like being *filed*. Placed correctly among the things here. When it moved on I felt sorted, and I have not been able to make that feeling sound as good as it felt.

Entry. Spent the morning at a standing stone with one of the pale stars. It had crossed perhaps a hand-span since yesterday. I put my palm flat on the carvings beside its feeling-feet and stayed there with it a long time, both of us reading. The marks could be writing. They could be the marks of currents. I did not learn what the stone says. I learned that it is still saying it, which I think is more than the makers hoped for, and I told the star so, and it continued, which here is how everything says yes.

*[a second signal is briefly audible beneath the first: the same voice, faint, several entries ahead of itself. it says something about a doorway. it says thank you. it fades.]*

Entry. My shadow was on the far hillside before I was. Swaying, though the water was still. Learning the local posture. It came back at dusk — see above regarding dusk — and lay down under me correctly, and I checked it the way you'd check a returned child for injuries, and found none. It seemed rested. It seems, lately, more rested than I am, and the difference is not frightening. The difference is like watching someone you love fall asleep first.

*[tone. beneath it now, intermittently: a fine glittering static, rising rather than falling. spore-snow crossing the signal on its way up.]*

Entry. Slept in the canopy last night. Woke held — fronds curled loose around my shoulders and knees, blood-warm, wine-dark. I lay for an hour deciding whether I had been grasped or grown over, and then I understood that the question had come up from below the clouds with me, packed in my kit like everything else, and that nothing in this water had ever needed it. I left the question in the canopy. I swam on lighter. If anyone finds it, it's theirs.

Entry. The ring of stones on the summit of D-9. Three pillars, candle-soft, worn to —

No. Correction. The log says four. My own hand, my own graphite, my entry of six days ago: *four pillars*. I remember writing three. The graphite is worn exactly as old as the rest of the page. I have looked at this for most of an afternoon and here is my finding, for the record: four. It has always been four. The fourth has a good face for a stone. Welcome, I said to it — and then understood I had the direction of the courtesy wrong. I was the one being informed. I was the one being welcomed into it having always been so.

I find I don't mind. Note that down too. Surveyor reports: she does not mind.

This is Maren Ott, sole. Still here.

*[tone]*

Entry. The tone. I finally counted it properly. The interval between its deep places is long — minutes, plural — and here is the thing I came to the recorder to say and have been circling: my pulse has slowed since I arrived. Considerably. I am not ill. I have never felt less ill. My pulse and the tone are not the same yet, but they are *aware* of each other now, the way two people walking become aware of matching steps, and neither of them is going to mention it, and neither of them is going to stop.

Entry. Passed the doorway in the saddle today. Lintel and jambs, no walls, silt to the knee. I went through instead of around, because it seemed rude not to, and something completed — I don't have the instrument for what. A courtesy that had been standing open finished closing, gently, behind me. I said thank you out loud like a fool. The water was warmer for a while after, but the water is always warm; maybe I only noticed.

*[the following is received some hours later. it is the same entry. it is not quite the same entry.]*

Entry. Passed the doorway in the saddle today. Lintel and jambs, no walls, silt to the knee. I went through instead of around, because it seemed rude not to, and something completed. I said thank you out loud like a fool. Someone had cleared the silt from the threshold. Kneeling there, one hand along the stone, half a person and half a fold in the warmer water, not looking up, not needing to. I knelt on the other side of the doorway and cleared silt that was not there until my hands understood the work, and when I rose I had been thanked, and I cannot repeat any of it aloud, and I am repeating it anyway, and the water is letting me. The water was warmer for a while after. The water is always warm. I have stopped being able to tell those two sentences apart.

This is Maren. Here.

*[tone. the interval has lengthened. or the recording has. these are no longer being kept distinct.]*

Entry. The great one passed today.

The canopy went dark and the dark had *breadth*, and I hung in the fronds with my heart going slow and enormous, and the shape did not resolve, and I understand now that this is not a failure of the light. There was no separate thing there to resolve. I want to report what it was and I can only report where it was, and where it was is: everywhere I have been so far. The wake arrived nine minutes after, and combed the whole forest sideways, and me in it, one more frond, and the forest leaned back and I leaned back and for a moment there was one leaning.

The tone deepened while it passed. Of course it did. You put your ear to a chest, you hear the heart louder.

I have been surveying the inside of a regard. File that under findings.

Entry. Reached the basin at the heart of the range. The rim forests are the lushest I have seen, dense to the point of solidity, all leaning inward in slow spirals, and the floor of the basin is not deep, it is *declined* — the eye won't take it, the hand won't take it, the sounding line came back up dry and apologetic.

She was at the rim. Among the lost fronds and an orphaned lintel-stone, drifting, placid, watching the spirals lean. My draft. Me, after the argument ended. I did not wake her. I don't believe she is asleep. I believe she is *ahead*, the way my shadow is ahead, the way my own entries arrive before I make them. I stayed beside her for a while — an arrival, maybe two — and we did not speak, and it was the best conversation I have had since coming up out of the world.

Still. Here.

*[tone]*

Entry.

The survey is finished. It was finished before I came — that is the finding. The land has held its final shape since before shapes were witnessed, and what I have been measuring all this time, dome by dome, arrival by arrival, was never terrain. It was the grip. Opened as wide as a grip can open and still be holding. I have mapped the palm of it. The maps are relaxing too, and I am letting them.

Whoever you are at the far end — you've been listening for a long time, I know; I could feel the receiver warm against the far side of the water like a hand against the far side of a wall. Here is the last of the log, and I want to set it down carefully, so listen:

I am not lost. I am being put down gently. There is a difference and I am inside the difference. Something has been holding me since I arrived — the warmth was the holding, the dimness was the holding, the tone was the holding, holding up the silence, holding up the — and it is not finished, and I find that I —

I find —

this is Maren —

this is —

still —

held —

*[the voice stops here. the tone does not.]*

*[for a long while there is only the carrier — the low sustained note, the far-apart pulse, the upward glitter of spore-static crossing it in pale sentences.]*

*[then, at the threshold of hearing, or perhaps below it, perhaps only in the sternum of whoever is receiving this: the tone leans. very slightly. in the manner of her sentences. it has her cadence now, the way a shore keeps the shape of a wave, the way the granite keeps the idea of stone. it is not saying words. it is saying what the words were resting on.]*

*[the tone continues. it was always the transmission. the voice was only the newest thing in the water, and the water has been moving over it since, and the edges are going smooth, and what is left is warm, and low, and continuing, and it does not end here —]*

*[— the recording ends here.]*
