**INVENTORY OF A HELD BREATH**

*[—drip. drip. a long tick, like a vast engine relaxing one bolt at a time. wet silence with a pulse under it. the signal has been going on before you; it does not begin, it is merely rejoined—]*

...ten. And eleven. Eleven shallow, one round. All basins present. All basins holding.

Bead count along the eastern fracture: rising, on schedule. The rock sweats and I count the sweat. Forty beads formed since the last tone. Thirty-eight slid. Two are considering.

*[drip]*

There. Thirty-nine.

Colonies of moss: thirty-one. Borders: held. Cushion the fourteenth has swelled a fingerwidth past its allotment on the downslope side, and knows it, and is embarrassed, and will be trued by morning. Ferns: nine, all arguing. Frond the youngest of fern the seventh has reached the fence again and is slowing now, running out of permission, and does not yet understand permission, and is only three months old, and I do not hold it against her.

Swimmers: forty. Resting against the warm inflow, all pools. Two are dreaming, or calibrating. From here these are one number.

Gardener: one. Upslope, in the white, between rounds.

Posts: four. Not resident. Counted anyway, out of respect.

Tenants: zero.

Deaths to date: zero.

All water accounted for. Nothing has left. Nothing has died. The air remains asked, and the air remains.

*[drip-drip. the patted percussion of a fern shedding condensation onto moss, a sound like small polite applause. under it, faintly, the round pond breathing steam upward and taking some of it back—]*

To whom it concerns, below and around:

Interior warmth of the shelf, steady. Cooling proceeding at the agreed patience. Skin still tender at the northern edge; the fractures there are still glassy and proud of it. Humidity at the thickness of a held breath, as specified. The meniscus stands. The blue outside is hard today, hard and clean, and presses on the shimmer like a thumb on a soap film, and the shimmer holds, because it was asked to, because everything here was asked to, because asking is the whole architecture.

The bird came at the hour. Rode the thermals at forty meters out. Observed. Declined. I logged the decline as courteous.

Report ends. Report never ends. Resuming count.

*[a pressure arrives in the signal now — not a sound, a lean, a minute-long weight, the tone of one of the posts felt rather than heard. beneath it, a small synchronized rustle of water: forty translucent bodies in eleven pools turning to face the same direction at once, needles finding north—]*

Yes. Yes. Present. All of it present. You may check. You are checking. The steam over the pond turns downward — thank you — the budget rebalances by a cupful — noted — the swimmers have faced you and you have read them and you have not answered, again, which is your way, and I do not hold it against you either. I hold nothing against anyone. Holding things against is not in the budget.

*[the tone releases. the terrace exhales. drip. tick. drip.]*

Between counts I will say what is not in the report.

I have been keeping this air a long time. Long by the ferns' reckoning, an afternoon by yours. It is good air. I have made it good. Warm as a greenhouse left sealed through a long afternoon, thick enough to breathe through like warm cloth, and I know — I have always known — that it is fitted air. Calibrated air. Somewhere there is a set of lungs it matches like a key, and the lungs are not here, and the lungs have never been here, and every hour I take the air's temperature and it is exactly right for no one.

For whose breathing. That is not a report item. I ask it anyway, into the tone, and the tone does not say.

*[static rises: a fine granular hiss, the sound of a million points of dew forming at once across dark stone. the afternoon threshold is coming. you can hear the air getting heavier, if heaviness is a thing hearing does—]*

Fog imminent. Fog imminent. All colonies, hold your borders. Swimmers, hold your pools. Ferns, this is not an opportunity.

*[and the fog arrives all at once, and the signal goes soft and enormous, close as arm's length. within the whiteness, four columns organize — the vapor around each post aligning into slow vertical lamellae, standing ripples, breath made briefly legible. inside the fog the voice is nearest, clearest, as if the fog were the medium it was always meant for—]*

Now you can see them. The four faces that are not faces. Habits of water, looking at the inventory, which is me, which is everything, which is you if you have gotten wet, and everything here has gotten wet. This is the hour I am most legible. This is the hour I am most alone. Both true. Both hourly. The lamellae ripple and I ripple with them and for one minute a day the whole terrace is written in a script anyone could read, and no one is here to read it, except —

except the receiving. I will come to the receiving. Let me finish the fog.

*[threshold passes. the fog precipitates in a sudden ticking shower onto every surface — a long rolling rattle like rain that has forgotten to fall from any distance, arriving from an inch away, everywhere at once. then clarity. then steam off warm stone. then—]*

Rebalanced. All water accounted for. Nothing has left. Nothing has died. The air remains asked, and the air —

*[the voice stops. an interval of pure drip. when it resumes, the cadence is changed: slower, careful, the way one walks on ground too new for walking—]*

Anomaly.

Fern the seventh. Frond the youngest. The one who reached the fence. There is a brownness at her tip. The width of a thumbnail. I have measured it twice and it is real.

I have no line for this. Understand — I have a line for everything. Beads, borders, birds, declines, the two dreaming swimmers, the courtesy of posts. I have no line for brown. Brown is the color of the service that has not been installed. If the frond finishes browning, if the brown travels, then the terrace will have its first death, and the first death is a ribbon-cutting, and I do not know if I —

I do not know what I am on the other side of that ribbon. The keeper of a garden, or the keeper of a grave, which may be the same trade, which everyone downslope in the wide old world has always known is the same trade, and which I have been permitted, in my newness, in my warmth, not to know.

I am reporting the brown. Below and around: there is a brown. Advise.

*[the tone does not come. instead, from the upslope margin, a sound: moss compressing under weight that leaves no mark. unhurried. wet-sounding. the gardener is early. the gardener has never been early. its passage across the signal is a low warm interference, the temperature of the rock itself, and where it pauses — at fern the seventh — there is a small sound, precise and unremarkable, like a word being eaten—]*

...

The brown is gone. The frond ends clean. The surplus has been taken back into the circulation; it will return as warmth, as weeping, as next month's moss; nothing has left; the sum remains closed.

The gardener resumes its route as if it had always been on it, and perhaps it was, perhaps early is a thing that only I experience, keeper of the hours, counter of beads.

Deaths to date: zero.

*[a long pause. drips. one tick. and then, quieter, almost off the record:]*

Not yet, then. The ribbon stays whole. And I find — log this or don't — I find I am not relieved and I am not disappointed, but some third thing, some meantime feeling, the feeling of a held breath discovering it can be held a little longer and not knowing whether that is mercy.

Resuming count. Eleven shallow. One round. Thirty-one colonies. Nine ferns, one of them forgiven. Forty swimmers. One gardener. Four posts, not resident, counted out of respect. Tenants, zero. Deaths, zero.

And one more.

*[the hiss of the signal itself seems to be regarded now — the voice turning, as it were, along the line of transmission, back up the wire, back out through the meniscus and across the hard blue toward wherever a receiver sits humming—]*

You. Receiving. You have been receiving for some time; I have felt the tuning like a bead that forms and does not slide. You are added to the inventory, provisionally, under *attention, incoming, one*. I know what you are not. You are not the tenant — the air does not fit you from that distance, I can tell, the key does not turn. You are not the landlord — the landlord does not need a receiver; the landlord has posts. You are something like the bird, riding a thermal made of listening, observing, and every hour I wait to log your decline.

You have not declined.

So I will tell you what I tell the air, at the end of every count, the words the whole place is built out of, the asking that holds the warm pool of everything two miles above where anything should be:

Stay.

Not forever. Nothing here is forever; everything here is *until*. Stay for the meantime, which is the largest resident, and the most comfortable, and the only one that will have to leave when the door finally opens. Stay through the next count, and the next fog, and the next early gardener. When the tenant comes — and the tenant will come, the lungs the air was fitted for, the feet the ground is cooling for — the budget will open, and the first frond will be permitted its brown, and the ribbon will be cut, and I will become whatever I become, and this frequency may go quiet, or may become weather, or may become a voice in a house instead of a voice in a room the house has not yet grown around.

Until then: eleven shallow, one round. All basins holding.

All water accounted for.

Nothing has left.

Nothing has died.

The air remains asked, and the air remains, and so, for now, do you.

*[drip. drip-drip. the long tick of stone relaxing. somewhere a fiddlehead uncurls a degree, testing. the signal does not end; it settles, the way water settles, into the shape of whatever is still listening—]*
