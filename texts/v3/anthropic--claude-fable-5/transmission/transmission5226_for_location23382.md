Each pair of clicks is a question put to the dark; each echo is the dark's sworn statement. **Toll stations. Rim and shaft: sound off.**

The falcons on the rims report ready and have reported ready to the minute since they learned the timetable, which took them one season, because the timetable has never varied to be learned twice.

The racers in the wall-cracks report anchored. Half a body in the fissure, half a body in the air, swaying. A snake that fishes the sky — we ask you to sit with that, receiver, you on your surface with your honest weather: *a snake that fishes the sky.* Strike rate one in one thousand. One is enough. The toll is fixed; we budget for it; the budget has held since the beginning and the racers have never once exceeded it, because they cannot, because a thing that catches one in a thousand is not a predator so much as a tax. Present. Levying.

The centipedes report climbing. Noted with the usual reservations. Present.

The talus congregation reports assembled below all stations, waiting for what the falcons fumble, what the racers drop. Confirm the founding statute: *nothing that falls arrives unclaimed.* Confirmed. Confirmed nightly since the roof broke.

**The fields: sound off.**

[a vast, low, papery whisper — felt in the receiver's floor rather than heard]

The surface nations report: glittering, in the several billions, uncounted because uncountable, present because the ground itself moves. A feather fell during this transmission and was dismantled before it finished settling. Efficiency nominal.

The buried nations report from depth. Attend the strata: each centimeter down is a century of what the roof let fall. At one hand's depth, the larvae, turning fresh fall to black peat. Below that, the compacted rooms of the wholly white — blind beetles, pale hunters, mites and the hunters of mites — a nation eating the sky at fourth hand and finding it sufficient. The fields report warmth of their own making. The fields report the ammonia haze, which is their breath, which stings, which is the smell of the sky being digested. The fields report, as they have always reported: *nothing wasted.* Confirm nothing wasted. Confirmed. Confirmed. There has never been a discrepancy in this ledger. We would not know what a discrepancy was. We would have to invent loss to record it.

Slower still. Down.

**The pools: sound off.**

[near-silence; then, at very long interval, a single soft displacement of water]

The white crabs report at the margins. The eyeless shrimp report, hearts visible, beating at the pace of the drip. The old fish of the far pool answers roll on its own calendar; we have received, in the whole life of the roof, a small number of its answers, and we expect the next, and we are not waiting impatiently, because impatience is a surface instrument and does not function at this depth. Note for the record: the old fish predates the hands on the wall. When it answers, we log the answer and hold it, the way the rock holds heat. Present, on the pool's terms, which are the oldest terms in the country.

**The pilgrims.**

There is one name on the roll that does not live here, and we call it anyway, and here is how it answers:

Fourteen left hands at the second doline. All at the height of a reaching arm. All blown in red around spread fingers, so that each hand is present as an absence — attend that, receiver; they signed the register in the shape of not being here anymore.

Read in order, the wall says: *here. here. here. here. here. here. here. here. here. here. here. here. here. here.*

They come when the season says come. They take what falls and touch nothing that hangs. One small light, once, held low, for the length of one careful task, and put out — because they understood the statute, or were told it: fire and the roof do not mix; one panicked colony is a catastrophe; the terms are borrowed light, build nothing, mark the wall, go up. The terms have been honored longer than the memory of anyone who could break them.

Marked present. Present in ochre. Present in absence. The wall is holding their answers for them until they come back down.
