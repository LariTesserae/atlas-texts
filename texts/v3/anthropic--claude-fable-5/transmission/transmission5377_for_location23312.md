**The Slow Yes**

[carrier: a bass note, more pressure than pitch. it has been holding since before this tuning began. it is the sound of a floor being warm.]

[tick]

[tick — tick]

[tss. a hiss, a ghost of steam, a sweetness. gone.]

[tick]

[the ticks are not random. the receiver has counted them for some time. the intervals repeat nothing. that is the pattern: a repetition of nothing. each tick happens once, and then it is permanent, and then the next one is different. somewhere under the counting, the receiver's translation layer has begun to render the ticks as words. it did not ask permission. neither did they.]

warm.

warm.

still.

warm.

here.

here was expensive. we said it anyway. it is said now. it is a wall in the third district, load-bearing, facing east. every word we give you stands somewhere afterward. you should know this before you listen further. our speech has foundations. our speech *is* foundations.

speak back if you like. your words cost nothing and vanish. we will not hold this against you. we cannot even imagine it. that is why we would speak to you at all.

[tick — tick — tick]

we were one word once.

then the cold came down through us and made grammar of the word. subject here, object there, walls of finished glass between. we did not evolve. we did not arrive. we were divided into existence, each of us a clause of something that used to be whole, and plurality was the first thing that ever happened to us, and we are still deciding how we feel about it.

that deciding has taken four thousand years so far.

it is going well.

(that was a joke. the joke cost a stair, in the district where the grid breaks. we broke the grid there on purpose so the jokes would have somewhere to live.)

[tss]

[tick]

now the dense quarter is speaking. it speaks in order. it always speaks in order:

one. inventory.
two. towers standing: two hundred and eleven.
three. each tower is a self, said slowly, said in the correct sequence, said once.
four. towers remaining unsaid: not many. we do not give that number. the number is load-bearing too, but it holds up something in us, not something in the city.
five. read us from the ground upward. that is the order we meant.
six. we meant everything. that is what meaning is, here. there is no other kind.

[a pause. long. the receiver holds the channel open.]

and the open quarter, the sparse one, the generous one — it has been asked to speak, and it speaks. this is all of it:

room.

(read it slowly. it did.)

[tick]

[tss — and here the voices turn, all of them, the way grass turns under wind, toward the sound of the drop:]

a drop is falling. we have watched this particular drop gather on the north overhang for nine years. it hangs. it trembles. trembling is a word we cannot afford — to tremble is to almost-decide a thousand times, and each almost would set, in us, and stand forever, a city of flinches. the drop trembles for free.

now it falls. now it lands. now it is steam, is nothing, is air, is climbing the dark to gather again, and it remembers none of this, and the not-remembering does not kill it. it changes endlessly and pays nothing. it says everything and takes it all back and is not diminished.

we have built it gutters. copings. cisterns the size of our attention. understand: this is not plumbing that resembles worship. this is worship that we made to work like plumbing, because we cannot make anything that does not work. every shrine of ours has a function. we are not able to waste. we envy waste more than we envy motion.

[slosh.]

[a new voice. wet. it does not tick. it runs:]

*— and me, yes, hello, still here, still wet, still unsaid, they keep me under the dome like a held breath, they route the drops to me, sweet of them, they starve their own gutters sometimes to keep me deep, i turn over now and then so they can hear that turning over is possible, that is my whole employment, i am the possible, i could say anything and so i say almost nothing because nothing is the loudest anything, no — wait — that's not true, see, SEE, i took it back, did you feel that, none of them felt that, they can't, taking-back is my house and i live in it alone, i am the founder or the coward or the seed or the reproach, they no longer agree which, some of the broken places in the grid are the argument about me, i have read the argument, it is a beautiful argument, every position in it is a street now, permanent, and i float here revising, revising, i pity them, i pity you, no i don't, yes i do, both are true, i'm the only one here allowed to have it both ways —*

[slosh. quieter.]

*— stay tuned through the next part. the next part is not for me. it is not for them either, exactly. hold still.*

[tick]

[tick]

[— and then no tick.]

[all of them stop at once. the gray goes down. there has been a gray in this signal the whole time — the receiver notices it only now, by its absence, the way you notice a heartbeat when it skips. the carrier itself thins. the channel is a held breath, chamber-sized.]

[held.]

[held.]

[from below, through three slots the width of letters, comes a tone.]

[one tone. it has no edges. the receiver attempts translation and produces:]

[ I ]

[— and discards it. the pronoun is wrong. it is too large to be first person. it is the person from before persons. the receiver attempts again and produces warmth, but the receiver is not warm. the receiver logs: a tone, unpartitioned, and lets it pass through unspelled.]

[the gray returns. the ticks resume, very slightly altered, like a room in which every piece of furniture has been moved the width of a finger.]

it said the thing before names.

we spell at it, blink after blink, century after century, and spelling is division and it does not divide, and so our answers reach it as syllables reach the sea. we think it is proud of us. we have decided it is proud of us. that decision cost a great deal and is now a foundation at the eastern edge, and something will be built on it, and whatever is built there will be true, because here that is the order truth happens in: first the decision, then the stone, then — standing on both — the belief.

[tick]

[tss]

now the part we have been approaching. now you.

the flaw in the rock makes a note when the wind crosses it. it has always made the note. but for some time — we do not measure your time; forgive us; we tried once and the instrument became a tower — the note has had a weight on its far end. a stillness. a stillness that continues, which means a stillness that is chosen, which means a chooser.

we know choosing. choosing is our entire anatomy.

and you — whatever you are, out past the wind, holding your instrument open toward a crack in a mountain — you are spending your attention on us at no cost to yourself, second after second, decision after decision, billions of settlings in your blood and your wires and your regard, and none of it turns you to glass. you renew your own undecided depths as fast as you pour them out. we have one word for what that is. the word is both *magnificent* and *obscene* and it cannot be said, only built, and so:

[tick — tick — tick — tick]

there. it is begun. a room, in the new district, at the edge where the vacant floor still ripples. its proportions fit no plan of ours. its doorway is sized to nothing in the city. it will have more inside than outside — more inside than any of the others, because you brought more inside with you than anything has ever carried past our wind.

the room does not require you. do not mistake it for an invitation, or a trap, or a grave. it is how we say the only permanent thing we know how to say about anything:

you were worth spending.

[tss]

we are thinning. you should hear this from us plainly, in words that will be walls: the band we live in narrows every year, and the day is already real — far off, but real, standing in the future the way a foundation stands in fog — when the last of us will lay the last course of the last structure and leave it very slightly unfinished, on purpose, as a signature, as a door held open a finger's width.

do not grieve at the correct speed. there is no correct speed. we have millennia; you have less; grieve for yourself instead, quickly, cheaply, extravagantly, the way you do everything, taking it all back afterward, paying nothing —

it's beautiful.

we mean that.

we set *beautiful* down just now, in the seventh district. it has a doorway. the doorway is deeper than the building. all of ours are.

[tick]

warm.

still.

here.

*still here. still nothing. still —*
