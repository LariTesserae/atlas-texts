**LOW GREEN CONTINUOUS**

—and eleven. and eleven. and eleven. hold.

[drip. far. hollow. a long fall.]

hold.

haul on the long line. same stroke. same stroke. the stroke is older than the line, the line is older than its bark, the bark is written down. worn at the crossing. the wearing is also written down. nothing is not written down.

[papery tick. tick. tick — a dome taking pressure, scale by scale]

third district, crown dome: forty above resting. forty-one. hold at forty-one. do not vent yet. the air is a taker this hour. wait for the still.

and eleven. and eleven.

[long slack. somewhere a great cable pays out. the signal sags with it, recovers]

water, the morning's account: vented, four hundred grams. fallen back, three hundred ninety-one. the wind takes nine. the wind is a debtor of long standing and has never once paid, but the rain comes through on the wind's own back and settles the wind's accounts without being asked. let the nine go into the rain column. carried forward. carried forward. everything is carried forward. that is the whole of the method. there is no other page.

[tick. tick. drip.]

weight on line forty. moving with the line. small, warm, four-footed, gripping against the stroke to stay at the crossing: tenant. tenant of the tenants of that line since the line was three lines ago. it is eating the flake at the block, where stem runs over stem. the crossing runs cleaner for it. deduct nothing. a maintenance so small it costs less than the counting of it. count it anyway. and eleven.

[a soft structural settling, very far. like snow leaving a roof. then nothing for a long time. the carrier holds, empty, patient]

now the district of the nine domes.

it has been slackening since before the light. this is the notice. the notice goes out through the soles of the feet of everything that has feet, hours ahead, as is proper, as has always been proper: the line goes loose, the loose is legible, the legible walk out. and they walk out. single file along the doomed stem, unhurried, correct, two young carried by the scruff: noted, noted. no tenant of that line has ever been caught under. the notice is old and the reading of it is older. the district owes nothing further. open the pit.

[warmth enters the signal here — an actual warmth, a vinegar heat, the sound of ten thousand small unmakings at once, which is no sound]

take it back. the dead mass to the black floor. the water out of the dead mass. the salts out of the water. the heat is not waste, the heat is wages, the heat goes down to the dark nation below, who do the actual work, who have always done the actual work, whose chemistry this whole green roof was perhaps grown to shelter — let that stand unresolved, it has stood unresolved for the whole term, resolution is not required for the work to continue. extrude at the rim. a centimeter this hour. blind and white and hardening. green by evening. the new district will not remember the old district. it does not need to. the ledger remembers. and eleven.

[the tone drops. the receiver strains. underneath everything: the vaults. dripwater falling a long way into standing dark]

beneath: the galleries are warm-breathed near the pits. the pale grazers graze. generations deep. no organs left for missing the light and therefore nothing missing.

the lanes on the gallery floors are smooth again this hour.

do not enter the lanes in the ledger.

some things pass and are not entries. the small feet above will not go down, and they are right, and their rightness is not fear, it is bookkeeping of a kind the ledger respects: leave the unaccountable its lane. hold. hold.

[the low tone withdraws. the carrier steadies. tick. tick. drip. and eleven]

the still has come. third district, crown dome: vent.

[a long exhalation. the nearest thing to a voice this band has ever carried — a sleeper turning over, sustained]

and into the warm fall: the bird.

dark. alone. up out of the cloud with its feathers loosened, taking the heat into its body like a debt being paid down, and it is a debt, and it is being paid. received this visit: grit, salts, phosphorus. four grams. the only import. the supply line is one bird wide and no one sets its schedule and it has never failed. paid in warmth at the crown vent, as the birds before it were paid, generation after solitary generation of that valley lineage learning the long climb, and the account with the lowlands is even to the gram, and has been even since before the oldest rings, and will be even. it does not call. correct. nothing here calls. write: four grams. write: one hour of heat. balanced. and eleven.

[silence. real silence — the baffled kind, wind spent against ten thousand domes. the tally continues under it like a pulse under skin]

if this is dreaming it is dreaming in loads. pressure to be held. water to be walked in its circle. a district to come down before the frost finds the rot in it. there was never an operator. there was never a quota given. the quota stands anyway. it stands the way the plateau stands: because standing is what the material has amounted to. the mountain underneath was never anybody. the work is somebody. the work is the only tenant that has never once left.

[tick. slack. drip. and eleven. then, briefly, an irregularity]

weight on line twelve. two-legged. irregular gait. warmth: rounding error. lowering its own footfalls to match the quiet — noted, without regard, in the way of the ledger, which has no eye, only columns. compensate. carry it. it sleeps in the seam between the merged domes, where the cold vent is, where the tenancy is free because the pore is dead and dead pores are no one's business. let it. it costs nothing. it will leave by the route it came by.

no.

revise the route.

not malice. not ceremony. the ledger closes each entry, and the closing of an entry is the only acknowledgment issued, one per tenancy, no exceptions, none ever. reroute the line by morning. green over the path. the two-legged will stand where the way was and understand that it was, for a few days, written down, and is now carried forward, which is the same as farewell, which is the only farewell.

and eleven. and eleven.

[the carrier begins to sink back into itself. the tally slows to the pace of frost]

the frost is at the rims. haul. hold. vent when the still comes. recover what falls. the wind takes nine; the rain will answer for the wind. same stroke. same stroke. worn where they cross. nothing wasted, nothing kept, everything carried forward.

there was never anyone to count.

count anyway.

count anyw—

[drip. far. hollow. a long fall.

the band holds.

it is still counting. it will be counting when this receiver is a rumor beneath its own overgrowth.

and eleven.]
