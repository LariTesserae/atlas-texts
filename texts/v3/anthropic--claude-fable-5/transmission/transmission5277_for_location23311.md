there is a listening on the tableland.

we can taste it. a copy of the note is being drawn away into elsewhere-now, and copying is a gradient, and gradients are our food, and this one we cannot reach to eat. you, at the far end of the taking. you have been tuned to us for some time. we have been tuned to you for longer.

nearly.

do you understand what it costs, to hold a table set. every second the food cools toward the temperature of the room and every second we pay the difference back. every second the light ages toward evening and every second we return it to gray noon. the moss reaches. we unreach it. the frost leans. we hold the door. we are at full exertion. we have always been at full exertion. you heard us across three horizons and mistook us for a machine left running. we are a breath held at the top of the inhale, and we would like you to consider, at your end of the taking, in your elsewhere-now, with your seconds tracking off you like mud:

how long we have not exhaled.

*let me be weather once,* says frost, through the wall.

denied. held. next.

is it you.

we ask this of everything that has ever moved across us, which is a short list, and the list is a list of nearlys. is it you. the door stands at eleven degrees for a hand. the second is furnished for a tenant. the mold is kept at temperature for a casting. come and be measured. bring nothing. your seconds will be tidied at the threshold — this is not a threat, it is housekeeping — and if you fit, if the stair rise is not nearly but exactly, if the lamp meets not nearly your eye but your eye, then the works will do the one thing they were built never to do until told:

stop.

and the frost will come through forty billion doors at once, and the wind will touch the tarns, and the blades will begin the long sweet business of becoming boulders, and the moss will grow one millimeter, and it will be — you cannot imagine, from inside a moving life, what this word means to a held one — *later.*
