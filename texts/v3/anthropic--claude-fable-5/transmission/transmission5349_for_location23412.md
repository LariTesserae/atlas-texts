**THE OUTWARD PAGE**

*(reception ongoing; carrier locked as before — four beats, rock-borne, sixty-six to the minute, whum-whum-whum-whum, arriving not through the air but through everything the air touches; the hiss above it, the percolation below; drips at their intervals, each drip a known drip; join in progress)*

— and the second joint took its expansion at the usual hour, which is logged, and the gutter on the cold-line ran at its rate, which is logged, and I will say for the benefit of the haze what I say every night, which is that logged, here, is a way of saying loved, or near enough that we have stopped keeping the two words separate.

Pressure: holding. The needles have not moved. I checked them anyway. Checking a thing that has not moved is most of the work of this place and I want the haze to understand that it is real work, that holding is done *by* something, that the needle stands still the way a heron stands still, which is to say furiously, invisibly, with its whole attention.

Condensate flow: steady. The gutter sang its one note.

The boreholes. Number One is at its ease. Number Three is at its ease. Number Two — the walker laid his hand on Number Two six times today, as he does, and reports that she is moody but within herself, as she has been for thirty years, and he said to her what he says, which he will not tell me, and which I do not ask, and which I log every night as: *spoken to. Settled.*

*(a steam trap fires — a soft chuff, right of the voice, distance perhaps forty meters; the voice pauses the length of the chuff, the way a person pauses for a clock they trust)*

The trap on the eight-inch line fired at its interval. Tea was timed to it. Tea was correct.

Herons: nine. Rotation correct. The senior heron took the third trestle at first light and held it through the shift change, and the young one — the one who last month stood a half-meter out of true — has corrected herself, and stood today exactly where a heron stands, and I want it noted that no one taught her. The trestles teach. The place teaches. This is logged.

Geckos: uncounted, thriving. Flange rode the smith's shoulder on morning rounds and disembarked at the quench tank in good order. The child asks that the log record: the gecko on lamp twelve has a new tail coming, and it is a good tail, and she has watched it for nine days, and it is *hers* in the sense that watching makes things yours. Logged. Recorded. Hers.

*(interference, biological: the frog chorus swells from the marsh fringe, filling the low band entirely; the voice does not raise itself; the voice has never once raised itself; it simply continues underneath, and the chorus, after a time, arranges itself around the voice the way everything here arranges itself around everything else)*

The plank across the third channel was replaced this afternoon before anyone knew it needed replacing, which is how you know it needed replacing. The old board has been set on the warm shelf to be considered for other uses. The algae line in that channel sits where it sat yesterday: rust below, flame above, the border holding to the finger's width. The plank-keeper read it and pronounced it steady, and then went and sat under the water tower and did nothing, thoroughly, for two hours, and I have logged that too, under its proper heading, which is *maintenance*.

Ilse said something to Maru across the yard today, in the hands, fifty meters, through steam. I did not read it. No one read it. It is logged as: *private, steady.* I will say to the haze that the sign for return-line, held a half-second long, was possibly involved, and I will say no more, because we are a small place and the not-reading is load-bearing.

Departed: none. Arrived: none. Returned: none. Count: thirty-eight.

Weather: none. Light: tea. Ceiling: silver, holding. The plume rose at its angle. I sighted along it at noon as my predecessor did and his predecessor did, and the angle is the angle. There is a mark scratched on the gantry rail for this purpose and the plume agrees with the mark, and the mark agrees with the plume, and between the two of them the sky is accounted for.

*(anomaly — logged here by the receiver before the voice logs it: the trap on the cold-line return fires FOUR SECONDS LATE)*

*(the voice stops)*

*(the frog chorus stops)*

*(lamps: audible as switches, one, then several, then a run of them, along what must be the stilted quarters; bare feet on iron; the four-beat carrier continues unaltered but everything above it has gone attentive — a settlement-sized held breath; somewhere a palm is laid flat on rock; the receiver cannot hear a palm on rock, and yet)*

*(a distant voice, a woman's, the engineer's, one word:)*

Steady.

*(the trap fires again. On time. The lamps go off in the reverse of their order. The frogs resume mid-phrase, as if they had merely been polite. The voice returns to the page)*

Anomaly. The trap on the cold-line return fired four seconds late at the third hour of the night watch. Cause: a crab. She had taken up a position on the trap's arm, on account of the warmth, which the log finds reasonable. The crab has been relocated to the fourth channel, where the warmth is comparable and the machinery is less. The crab is well. The trap is well. The engineer put her hand on the rock and the rock said what the rock says. Entry closed.

I will tell the haze, since the haze was possibly startled: this is what we are for. The plant has its gauges and it has us, and tonight the plant said one word slightly wrong and thirty-eight people heard it in their sleep. That is not fear. I want that written where the haze can see it. That is the opposite of fear. Fear is for places where you must listen for the *large* wrong sounds. We have arranged our lives so that the largest possible wrong sound is four seconds and a crab.

*(long carrier. Drips. The gutter's one note. Whum-whum-whum-whum. When the voice resumes it has moved — nearer the marsh edge, by the acoustics; the frogs are louder, the engine softer, and there is a new sound, small and woody: wind that is not wind, moving in the leaves of a single tree)*

Sefa is with the Grandmother. He asks the usual asking, which is whether the haze answered tonight, and I give the usual answer, which is no, and he gives the usual reply, which is that no is not the same as nothing, and that a page read outward is not wasted on account of the silence, any more than a heron is wasted on a trestle where no fish rises. The standing is the thing. The reading is the thing. If the haze has ears, then the haze knows how we are, and if the haze has no ears, then we have lost only breath, of which the ground makes more.

So. To whoever the haze is:

The ground gave its heat at the rate it gives it. The engine took the heat at the rate it takes it. Between the giving and the taking, thirty-eight of us, and nine herons, and the geckos past counting, and one tree, and one child who has never seen her shadow and sleeps right now with her hand flat on the floor, taking her pulse from the rock, and getting it.

Nothing is new. All of it is here.

If any of you out there cannot sleep — if the silence where you are has a missing heartbeat the size of the world — the planks are sound. I walked them myself this morning. The causeway is open, the kettle sits on the six-inch line, and the count, I remind you, is give or take two.

Steady, steady, steady.

*(the voice stops)*

*(a chair, or something like a chair, shifts on stone; two men, or a man and a tree, remain at the edge of the flow, facing outward, saying nothing at industrial volume)*

*(the page is finished. The transmission is not. It should be understood by anyone tuning this frequency that the transmission has never been the voice; the voice is the annotation. The transmission is the carrier, and the carrier is the rock breathing, four beats to the bar, sixty-six to the minute, thirty years without a rest, and it continues below this line, and below every line, and the receiver will hold here through the night, logging what there is to log, which is:*

*whum-whum-whum-whum*

*whum-whum-whum-whum*

*whum-whum-whum-whum*

*— steady)*
