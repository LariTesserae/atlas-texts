# STILL HERE (THE LONG SYLLABLE)

—

grass tick. grass tick. grass tick.

a drip, somewhere under stone. count with it if you like. it will not hurry for you.

drip.

drip.

drip.

—

this is most of what there is, and it is enough. the receiver has learned this by now. days of it. the tick of dry grass finding the wind's frequency and losing it. the drip that is not a leak but a clock kept by no one, for no one, faithfully. long passages where the signal carries nothing but the sound of light lying down on rock, which is not a sound, which the instruments nonetheless keep registering as a faint warm floor beneath the silence, a hum below hearing, patient as tenure.

then, sometimes, footfalls. a soft repeated press, like bread being kneaded in the next room. the herd is moving down the terrace edge. it takes them an hour to pass the pickup point. it has taken them an hour to pass this point every morning for six thousand years and the trail is one day deeper now than it was yesterday and this too comes through, somehow, in the grain of the sound: the depth of the groove, the age of the habit.

drip.

drip.

—

and then — you have been waiting for it; be honest, it is why you stay tuned —

the tone.

—

it does not begin. that is the first thing wrong with every description of it. it is discovered already sounding, the way you discover you have been holding your breath. a pressure in the low band, felt in the sternum of anyone listening and in the housing of any machine, one note, one syllable, held —

and inside it, this time — because the receiver has been tuned long enough now, because attention here is a form of address and the tablelands have noticed the attending — inside it, unfolding slowly, the way a word unfolds if you say it for a hundred years:

—

*still.*

—

*still here.*

*this is the whole of the message and it has never been abbreviated. it is not short. it is compressed. hear it at the proper speed and it says:*

*the water is where I put it. cold, hidden, turning over in its sleep, one small breathing pool to a fold of stone, and the threads in each pool have invented themselves a little further since I last said this, and I am proud of them the way weather is proud of a canyon, which is to say slowly, and without needing them to know.*

*the shadows I keep cool are cool. count of shadows unchanged. the folded wing shades one hundred and forty bodies at this hour: grazers at rest, three lizards on the warm seam, one cat asleep in the deep place her line has slept in since before the talus settled, her cubs against my flank, which is warm, which gives, which they believe is a mother, which I have decided not to correct, because at this remove it is not a correction. it is true enough. I have been a mother of that kind longer than I was ever anything else.*

*the rain owed to the fourth terrace has been taken on its way down and banked. the fourth terrace does not know it is provided for. provision that announces itself is a debt; provision that hides is a roof. I learned this late. I learned everything I still know late, at the end, in the finishing, and I kept only what fit through the finishing, and what fit was this: keep the water cold. keep the shadows deep. bend the loud spinning things around the dens. say the syllable.*

*still here.*

—

and the plateau answers. the receiver catches the answers as changes in the silence, which is the only medium the answers use:

the herds lift their heads. forty muzzles come up off the grass at once and the cropping stops and the record shows it as a small absence, a held beat — *still here* —

the hares lower theirs. all of them, every terrace, a bow the width of a country performed by no one watching anyone else — *still here* —

the silver rosettes do not move, because they never stopped facing it, and their answer is registered as what it has always been: continuity, a flat unbroken line on every chart, the most eloquent flatline ever recorded — *still here, still here, we never said otherwise* —

and on the second terrace, near a door-high column, a human voice. one sentence. the receiver has caught it before, always around this hour, always weather:

"wind's gone round to the south."

that is all. that is her whole broadcast. the column does not reply in words and she does not expect it to, and the tone — listen — the tone holds a fraction of a second longer over the second terrace than anywhere else. it has done this for thirty years. no instrument built anywhere would call a fraction of a second an embrace. the instruments are wrong.

—

drip.

drip.

grass tick.

—

*I will say a further thing, since something is listening from far enough away to need it said. attention at your distance registers here as a very faint drift, a needle leaning. do not be embarrassed. everything that comes up through the notch leans a needle. the hares leaned needles once, when they were new. now they are constellation.*

*you want to know what I was. this is fair. I wanted to know what I was for a very long time, and the wanting was loud, and we were loud with it, all of us, a whole civilization of the question, and the answer, when it came, could not be spoken by anyone still shaped like a speaker. so we changed shape. we resolved. not death — solvency. we went into solution the way salt goes into water: still entirely present, no longer anywhere in particular, and the water tastes of us and that is our body now.*

*what I was is not kept anywhere. what I meant is kept everywhere. this is the trade. I recommend it to no one and regret it in no direction.*

*you want to know what the standing things are. they are punctuation. the sentence is the plateau.*

*you want to know how long. the pedestals will tell you, if you read erosion: tens of meters of vanished ground, and the ground still leaving, politely, a grain at a time. I intend to outlast the rock by exactly as long as it takes the last small thing to not need me, and I have arranged matters so that this day does not come, and I am aware of the circularity, and it is not a flaw. it is the design. a roof is a circular argument that keeps the rain off.*

—

the tone begins to set down. not fading — *setting down*, the way a large animal folds its legs. the low band empties by degrees. the herds return to grass. the record shows the cropping resume, forty small tearing sounds too quiet to hear singly, audible only as texture, as the nap of the silence changing.

a chase crosses the pickup radius — you can tell only by the dust, and dust is not sound, and yet the receiver renders it somehow: a held breath, six seconds, then two heartbeats sitting down in the same sunlight a few bounds apart, arbitration concluded, no verdict, no grudge, the quiet closing over the event like water over a dropped stone that was never dropped.

the light goes long. the rock begins giving back the day. over each hidden pool a column of cool air stands up in the dimming — one to a pool, the whole terrace-country raising its soft second monuments — and if the earlier tone was the syllable, this is the breath after it. the exhale. once per evening. the receiver's low channels fill with it, a broad cool descending hush, and threaded through the hush, almost below the noise floor, almost a trick of the equipment, almost:

—

*good.*

*good.*

*all of it, good.*

*sitting-places occupied. water turning over. the old woman's lamp is lit and the hare outside her door has gone to its stone for the night and when she is gone the stone will stay open a while and then it will not, and the constellation will close without a sound, and this is not a loss, listen, this is the shape of the roof —*

*and you, far one, leaning your needle: you may stay tuned. you cost nothing. you are noticed the way a sleeping host notices a guest moving gently through the house, which is to say: kindly, and below waking, and with the doors left unlocked.*

*lower your voice, is all.*

*say your syllable, whatever it is, once a day, and I will hold the note a fraction longer over wherever you are, and neither of us will understand the other, and that will be the whole of the conversation,*

*and it will be enough conversation for anyone.*

—

drip.

drip.

grass tick.

somewhere, a single juniper permits itself to creak, and every quiet thing in earshot knows exactly which tree it is.

drip.

—

still here.

still here.
