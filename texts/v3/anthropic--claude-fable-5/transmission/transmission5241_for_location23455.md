north, along the ridgeline, the telegraph with strong feelings:

    [threshers working a windrow. old slag. generations old. they are answering each other about it, or they are only counting their own feet. after this long listening the difference has stopped presenting itself.]

close pass now. gain up. one of the great ones grazing within a few hundred yards, and under the mill-grind of the jaws — cart-wheel stones turning without hurry, HRRUNNhh, HRRUNNhh — under the rope-creak of the plate-beds, under the vent-wave traveling stern to bow, there is the small sound, the sound you only get this near:

    tk. tk-tk. tk. tk. tk-tk-tk. tk.

    thousandfold. finer than rain. each tick placed.

    [the tenders. each one holding one small when. the whole fine lattice of the animal's honesty, audible for as long as it takes the beast to walk past, and then gone back under the drone like a watch dropped in a forge.]

---

the heat goes horizontal. you can hear it happen — the shimmer-hiss off the flats changes register, the rock begins giving back its day.

and then, from the east, first voice:

    HOM. — HOM. — hom-hom. — HOM.

    long, long, short-short, long. she always opens. she has opened every dusk this receiver has been tuned.

    [still.]

answered, southwest, nine miles:

    HOM-hom. — HOM. — HOM.

answered, answered, answered — they come up like the furnace hearts come up, one orange point of rhythm at a time, until the whole band is signatures overlapping, two hundred miles of finished stone taking its own attendance:

    hom — HOM — HOM — hom
    HOM. HOM. HOM.
    (each different. each the same word.)

anomaly.

    HOM-hom — hom — HOM-HOM

received clean at bearing 340, and then, forty seconds later, the *intake* that should have preceded it — the long hiss arriving after its own roar, the breath coming home behind the cry, unhurried, like a dog trailing a cart.

    [worn seam between here and there. logged. the herd routes around it. the sound does not get to.]

    [do not resequence. receive it in the order the sky offers it. that is the discipline now.]

and here — flagged, because it has been coming for many dusks and tonight it can no longer be unflagged —

the drifter.

her signature used to fall like this: HOM — HOM-hom — HOM, square on the herd's stroke, on the shared beat, mated to it. tonight:

    HOM — — HOM-hom........HOM

late on the second figure. long on the rest. the answers that come back to her come back *guarded* — you can hear guardedness at nine miles, it is a thing this world taught the receiver to hear — the herd repeats her name-rhythm back to her corrected, gently, the way you'd say a word properly to someone losing the word.

she takes the correction.

she takes it a fraction late.

    [her ticking is thinning. somewhere on her flanks the small citizens are fewer than they were. she is drifting toward the middle of the country and everyone singing tonight knows toward what basin the middle of the country descends, and no one stops singing, because the singing is the only rope anyone here has ever had to throw.]

---

now the bad thing. received as it came. the transcript does not flinch and neither will this.

the west bearing thinned.

not quieted — *thinned*, the drone-floor going threadbare across an arc of maybe four degrees, and the thin patch moving, smooth, no stroke in it, no beat in it, nothing reciprocating, a place in the soundscape where sound was being *unasked*—

and then — received out of order, and entered here out of order, because the discipline is the discipline —

    WHUMP.

just that. flat, sudden, small at forty miles, unmistakable at any distance because nothing else on this world begins:

    WHUMP.

the sound of catching. a calf-shaped silence in a slag basin somewhere south becoming a calf-shaped *noise*. and only afterward — a full minute afterward, dragged backward through some worn seam between there and here — the cause arrives trailing its effect: the thin thread-hiss of a mother's smallest vent, the lending breath, the fire being divided one further time, the loan that is never called in.

    [the breath got here after the burning it lit. the plateau declines to apologize. receive it in the order the sky offers it.]

and the band —

the band goes up like nothing this receiver has held before or will pretend it can render. every voice. horizon to horizon, the drifter's late voice too, the flyers ratcheting down out of the high thermals to skim the news, the threshers hammering their telegraph flat out along every ridgeline, all of it at once, the whole province answering the whump with the only sentence this world has:

    STILL — STILL — STILL — STILL —
    STILL — STILL — STILL —
    [ .... ] —
    STILL —

they kept his gap in it. even in this. especially in this. the doorway stands open in the middle of the welcome and the new small fire is carried in past it, so that the first thing the calf ever hears, in its first minute of having a when, is both facts at once: *we are counted, and we count what is missing.*

    [the count tonight is the count of last night, minus one, plus one. it is not the same number. it will never be presented as the same number. the gap is held and the whump is held and the ledger does not simplify. nothing here simplifies. that is what the drone is for.]

---

the chorus is settling now. voice by voice, coal by coal, back down into the floor of the sound, the ka-ta-ka-ta, the mill-grind, the rope-creak, the fine rain of the tenders ticking their small honest whens in the hot dark under the plates.

somewhere near the center of the country, a spine that closes on itself is exhaling into air that no longer minds. last week's plume is still rising beside it. from the rim of hearing, faint, out of all order, comes something that might be its old signature, played backward, at leisure, meaning — if it still means — *still,* in whatever tense the basin keeps.

the drone continues. the drone is the census and the census is the country and the country is finished and continuing to be itself, loudly, hotly, at scale.
