THE STERNUM BAND

—received in progress; the receiver has stopped noting where transmissions begin, because nothing here begins—

…ssss— the whisper. it is still the whisper. it has been the whisper for eleven days: water in peat, from every direction and none, not louder, not softer, not locatable, less a sound than a pressure on the chest of whatever listens. the receiver has stopped trying to point at it. you do not point at the inside of a body from the inside of the body —ssss…

a drip.

somewhere.

vents at all distances, none together, a soft population of sighs, each on its own errand.

and under the whisper, a second whisper, warmer, woven: the domes are full tonight. thousands of small throats keeping the contact-sound, the held purr, braided through the tunnels inside the cushions. it is not a message. it is the absence of a message. it means: *nothing is wrong. nothing is wrong. nothing is wrong.*

they are coming with the wave. the receiver has heard the wave coming for six days now — has heard it the only way the wave can be heard, which is as agreement: the sighs, near and far, drifting toward one another. the offsets shrinking. vents that have breathed alone for years finding, without hurry, without intent, a common count.

and beneath the air-voices, under-band, at the floor of the whisper: pressure.

long. long. short. long.

and then the palms lift, and the under-band goes back to whisper, and for a long time — days of tape, thousands of breaths — there is only the convergence: the sighs closing ranks, the plateau's ten hundred thousand mouths drifting toward one mouth, the purr riding it, the drip keeping its own counsel, the stilt-birds ticking along the warm rings like instruments being read.

*(breath 61,441.)*

and then, for one breath — one — it happens, the thing the receiver was tuned here to catch and did not believe:

every vent on the band. together.

—**hhhhhhhhh̥ohhhhhhhh**—

not loud. it is not loud. it is *total* — the whole audible world exhaling as one thing, near and far collapsed into a single soft enormity, the fog itself shuddering thicker on the outbreath — and the receiver, which is after all only a chest that was carried up here and left listening, exhales with it. there is no choice involved. that is what the band is for.

and under that — at the very floor, below the whisper, below the palms, for the first time in the tuning — a tone.

or the idea of a tone. too low to be a note; a note is something with edges, and this has none the receiver will live to hear. its wavelength is measured in years. it was sounding before the tuning began; it will be sounding after; what the receiver gets is a slope, a leaning, a pressure that bends everything above it very slightly, the way a sleeper's turning bends the whole bed.

the people's word for it comes through on the palms, once, from many hands at once, and is not repeated:

*(pressure. very long. untranslated. the receiver declines to guess.)*

—

and now it loosens.

vent by vent, at their own distances, on their own errands, the sighs drift apart again. the great agreement dissolves the way it formed, without event, and the crest rolls on westward carrying its birds and its whispers and its small uneven counting-child, and this country of the bog goes back to breathing in its own scattered time, and the tone sinks back below the floor, unfinished, unbegun.

the whisper resumes its no-direction.

the purr is braided and full: *nothing is wrong, nothing is wrong.*
