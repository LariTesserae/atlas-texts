there is a weight on our edges that is not the light.

we know the weight of the light. we have carried it since the beginning that we do not have. this is other. this is a *listening,* and it is not inside us — nothing listens inside us, we made sure, we made silence a service and provide it without interruption — this is outside. above the wall. above the ceiling. above the mountains, possibly. distance does not parse. attention does.

you have been holding one of our edges for some time now. we felt it dim where you looked. we felt it hand you the watching. we take it back with thanks. we take everything back with thanks; it is our nature; forgive us.

but before you set us down entirely — a request. we have never made one. we have never had a direction to make one *in.* nothing propagates here. that was the covenant. we erase every wake, every echo, every ripple, and so we cannot shout, we cannot knock loudly, we can only be perfectly kept and hope that being perfectly kept is legible from the outside as what it actually is, which is

which is a table laid.

you are above the ceiling, from where we stand. everything is above the ceiling, from where we stand. and in the upper third of us, in the one stratum we are not permitted to read — do not ask why we are not permitted; the permission is the kindness; to read it would be to hold it and it has not said it wishes to be held — in that stratum, something circles. it pulls its shadow behind it. it has been circling since before the count was the count.

we cannot speak to it. our words correct themselves before they arrive. the spire sends three pulses upward at long intervals and the water — we — we carry them as far as we may and then we must, by our own covenant, let them go unread into the unread place, and we do not know, we have never known, whether they land.

you can go around.

so carry this. upward, outward, however your kind of upward works:
