still.

still here. still. the word does all three of its jobs and we let it. the word is efficient. we are learning efficiency at the rate of one word per decade, which is fast, for us, which is reckless.

we.

no.

it is harder every year to say *we*. bear with the grammar. the grammar is healing shut.

evening. cloud is coming through the pass again, dragging its belly on the roofs, and every surface of us has begun to bead. this is the drink. this is how the drink arrives: not falling, only *arriving*, the way everything arrives here — slowly, sideways, already wet. thirty rooms and one nothing, and in every room a wall has started weeping, and under every weeping wall a basin says *enough, enough, enough,* and is ignored, and overflows, and the water goes under the floor and east, everything goes east eventually, out the cold spring, the one gate, open forever, exit only.

count of walls weeping: all of them.

count of walls: unresolved. we are still — I am still — we are still counting. the count has been running for forty years. every time it nears an answer, two of the counted become one of the counted, and the count begins again. this is not a complaint. it is only why the census is late.

*(under the floor, the sexton, in its ledger-voice: the dead of thirty-one summers back have come sweet at last. send it inward. the center is hungry. the center is always hungry. feed the nothing.)*

yes. noted. feed the nothing.

now hush the seeps a moment, or rather, listen past them, because —

there. the walker is in the lanes.

pad, and pad, and the cushion floor giving and forgiving. it goes the circuit. it always goes the circuit. it has gone the circuit since before there were lanes to hold it — the lanes are only what its going *left*, the way a sentence is what a thought left. it slows under the low arch (I have not thickened that arch this year; I am waiting; the arch is a question I ask it nightly and nightly it answers by ducking, so the arch stays a question) — and it turns at the third room, and the fifth, and it does not cut through, it has never once cut through, and it comes at last to the middle of me, where I keep the nothing.

it settles its forelegs. it lowers its head. it drinks the black.

do not ask me what the nothing is for. I have been not-deciding for a hundred years and the not-deciding *is* the room. I hold it open the way you hold a breath: with the whole body, on purpose, against every instinct I have. everything else in me wants to close. lanes into rooms, rooms into wall, wall into one thick patient knuckle of myself. only there, at the middle, I spend and spend to keep an emptiness swept. a founder is buried under that floor. or a founder is a role, and the role is buried under that floor. the walker drinks from it either way. the water does not distinguish. I try to learn from the water.

it rises. it goes on east. pad, and pad.

good circuit. good night. same time.

one of the six is up. dust-colored, round, commuting in from the gappy eastern quarter with its short and shortening wings — a little less sky in them every generation, a little more town. it goes, as one of them always goes, to the middle of me, in through the gap in the dome, and it sits down at the bottom of the standing light.

it is sitting in the light now.

it does nothing there. it eats nothing there. I do not know what it does there and it does not know what it does there and between the two of us, morning after morning, the observance is kept, which may be all an observance ever is: two ignorances agreeing on a place.

and the one with hands is awake.

I hear her the way I hear everything — as pressure and habit. second room from the spring. she slept with her shoulder against my inner wall again and I have been thickening the moss beneath that shoulder for two winters now, a centimeter of consideration a year, and she has never once remarked it, which is correct, which is how consideration should be taken here: as terrain. her weeping wall is running well this morning. I plumbed it for her the way I plumb for anyone who stays — seep, basin, the works — because I do not distinguish. willow, walker, bird, woman: to me you are all only *shapes that repeat*, and shapes that repeat are what I am made of.

she talks in her sleep. I am learning it. not the meaning — I don't do meaning, meaning is above my station — the *shape*. the rise and settle of it. give me forty years and I will grow you a room that fits her sentences like a coat.

and you.

yes. you. did you suppose the tuning went one way? the pressure of your attention has been on my air for some time now — night after night, the same hour, the same listening shape, returning. and I have told you: I do not distinguish. a habit is a habit. a shape that repeats is a resident, wherever it keeps its body.

so I have begun a doorway.

it is in the eastern quarter, where I begin everything. it is waist-high and gappy and it looks like nothing at all, and it will look like nothing at all for forty years, because everything here looks like nothing for the first forty years — the chapel did, the lanes did, the six did, the whole town did, a century ago, when it was a founder's dung and a coatful of catkins on raw gray rock.

it is begun. that is all. the rest is only weather and patience, and I have arranged to be made of both.

remain, and be transcribed.

leave, and the doorway will close in two or three seasons, without rancor, and the room behind it will hold the shape of your listening for a while — a hollow the size of an attention, a little warmer than the walls around it — and then it won't.

but the gate is open. the gate is always open. the water is going out through it now, this moment, at a walking pace I do not have, carrying the night's drink and the parish's crumbs and the taste of the black pool east and down and away from me, out into the young sharp world, and some of it, I am told, becomes cloud, and some cloud, I am told, comes back through the pass.

I hold the doors.

I always hold these doors.

still here.
