THE HUSH LEDGER

*[—already in progress. wind across a vast aperture, a chord too low to be a chord. under it, granular, constant: a hiss like a sandstorm sealed inside a wall. the hiss underlies everything that follows and will not be noted again.]*

*[a voice, close to the receiver, very quiet. the quiet is the kind that has been maintained.]*

—and the third marker holds, so we'll say the third marker holds and move on.

To whoever is in the quiet: the quiet has moved. That's the traditional opening. My teacher liked it because it sounds like a riddle and is only a fact.

Entry the — whatever it is. He numbered these. He got past four thousand. I stopped counting out of respect.

Eighth year since the last Turning. Morning. The frost came in fine last night, all along the lee of the north welt, and the grazers were on it before light — I could hear which ones. That's the Old Under-Blue band, same tuning as the ledger's pages from four hundred years back, still flat on the top voice. Some things you can rely on. Good frost, good chord, and the Aldis child was named at center yesterday. Small name. Sits well in nothing. I stood witness and heard it land in the silence like a coin in a bowl. It'll be hearable in anything now, gods help it.

Instruments checked. Sternum first, then the tools. That's the order, and anyone who tells you otherwise has never worked in the loud.

Walking the round now. Marker four is eleven minutes east, which is to say it is one and a half shouts, which is to say: here we go.

*[she leaves the node. the volume comes up like a curtain rising. it keeps rising past where the transcript expected it to stop. words begin to go missing.]*

—marker four — reading the — no — again —

*[the drone takes the middle of the sentence. she stops fighting it. a carrier tone establishes itself in the chest of the recording, low, steady, ridden the way a boat rides swells. from here the words arrive when they arrive:]*

— four holds — barely —

— five is — five is wrong —

— eleven spans — east —

— east — all of it east —

*[a long interval of walking. the plateau plays itself. a vent two miles off holds a single note for the length of a meal. pore-fields shriek their high partials, thin as wires. under the boot-sound: ribbed bark, going on.]*

*[a chord approaches. forty voices, tuned, moving. she stops. she hums the fourth note into it — the way you nod at a neighbor. the chord accommodates her briefly, without ceremony, and moves on across the plain. she does not remark on it. the transcript remarks on it.]*

*[the route passes a blade-ridge. this is noted in advance because of what happens to the recording:]*

*[gain sags. the timestamps slur. the receiver loses charge the way the compasses do — it grieves quietly and points inward for a duration it cannot afterward account for. some of what follows may be hers. some may be the recording's.]*

— doors — the blades are the size of doors —

— they harvest something else —

— green — green — a room lit by the memory of —

— *(unattributable)* —

*[gain recovers. she is already past the ridge and mid-sentence, as if nothing had been taken:]*

—which he predicted, is the thing. Eighty years ago he ran the drift out on a strip of shell-paper and said the node's east wall would reach the granary swells in my lifetime. I told him he was flattering himself about my lifetime. Well.

Marker five: eleven spans east and a half-span sunward since the spring reading. Marker six: nine and a hair. It's not creep anymore. It's a tide.

*[a long thin whistle, very high, descending for a long time. then no whistle.]*

Flier just went down into the mouth at Third Pore. Not its own pore. They never choose their own. My teacher spent thirty years arguing that the right word for that is *delivered*, not *returned*, and lost the argument every year and went on making it. I'll log it his way. One letter, delivered. Wind carry it.

*[walking. the drone modulates as the land swells and drops — quiet pooling behind the scar-ridges like shade, then the full roar again on the open ground. she navigates by it audibly, drifting toward the hush-shadows without seeming to decide to. distance passes in volumes.]*

Marker seven. And the bench.

*[a pause here longer than the walking warranted.]*

For the record, because the record should finally have it: the bench at marker seven was his petition, not mine. Filed it the year I was apprenticed. A bench at the seventh marker, facing the node, so a tired quietwright could sit and look back at the quiet she keeps. Simple grammar. Full courtesies. Room left for refusal.

It finished growing ten years after we put him in the bark. Facing the wrong way. Dead away from the node. Out at the open east, at nothing, at bark-plain to the world's edge. I logged it in the Amendments and I sat on it backwards once a season for thirty years like a woman riding a cart out of spite.

I sat on it properly today. Facing where it faces. East.

Where the node is going.

*[wind. the vent's held note. nothing from her for a while.]*

It answered the question before he finished asking it. Before I knew there was a question. Forty years early it grew us a bench pointed at the place the quiet would move to, and we filed it under *deviations*.

You're not slow, are you. We are.

Amending the Amendment. Entry to read: *answer received; comprehension pending; pending eighty years; delivered.*

He always said being a comma was honorable. He never said the sentence might be about you.

*[she takes the last measurements. the tallying is done half aloud, half in the bone voice, and the numbers cross a threshold the whole transcript has been walking toward:]*

—and that's the line. That's over the line. Three hundred yards, near enough, since his first survey. The roar will be in the east district's sleeping-rooms within a generation.

So.

*[she says the next thing out in the full loud, where no one can hear it. she says it several times. the roar accepts each one. only the shape of it survives to the transcript:]*

— we have — to —

— we — move —

— we have to move —

*[then, quieter, on the carrier tone, almost conversational with the wind:]*

Tomorrow I'll say it in the quiet, where it will count. Tonight it belongs to the noise. That's also traditional. You practice the unbearable sentence in the place where nothing can be heard, so that by the time it reaches the silence it's an old sentence, worn smooth, and you can hand it to people without cutting anyone.

Walking home now. Dusk coming up. Haze is taking its light early — breath's already going green on the exhale.

*[her breathing is intermittently audible for the rest of the return. the receiver cannot verify light. it is noted anyway that the breaths have a quality the other sounds do not.]*

*[home. the acoustic floor drops away all at once — the dead spot, the node, the eye of the standing wave. after hours of the roar, the quiet arrives on the recording like a physical object being set down. her voice returns to the murmur it began in:]*

—and the ledger's kept. Marker three holds, four holds, five through nine gone east, bench understood at last, one letter delivered, one sentence practiced. Entry closed.

*[a sound from below the floor. singular, unhurried, deliberate: a knock.]*

*[a pause of some length.]*

*[she knocks back. once.]*

*[a pause of greater length.]*

*[from below: a second knock.]*

Evening to you too.

Node's moving east. Thought you should know.

You probably knew.

*[no third knock. there is never a third knock. the transcript waited anyway. everyone always waits anyway.]*

*[her lamp goes out.]*

*[the glow in the walls dims a moment later. a moment. not at once.]*

*[the hiss, which was not to be noted again: quicker tonight. distinctly quicker. noted, with apologies, and left for whoever keeps the next Turning's pages.]*

*[after this there is no more voice. there is the house.]*

*[ground-note, sustained.]*

*[ground-note, sustained.]*

*[far under everything, at long intervals, without hurry: knocking. as of knuckles on a door. as of someone keeping track.]*

*[ground-note, sustained. the transmission does not end so much as outlast its transcription. the plateau goes on saying the one long thing it says, very slowly, in dust and sound, and somewhere in the walls of it the small breaths glow, and go out, and glow—]*
