**ONE COAT A YEAR**

---

*[reception ongoing]*

---

·

·   ·

·

the drops first. always the drops first. they are the carrier.

·      ·

·

underneath them a hiss, very thin, where water is pushed through a narrowness in stone. it does not vary. it has not varied since you tuned in. it will not.

and under the hiss, lower than the hiss, a tone. you may have taken it for the noise of your own equipment. it is not. hold still and it resolves:

*wwwwwaaaaaarrrrrmmmmmm*

one word. held. it has been holding this word since before there were terraces. this is the oldest signal in the hollow and it is orange-pink and it lives in the throat of the spring and it says one thing:

*wwwaaaarrrmmmm*

it is not talking to you. it is not talking. it is a color that has an opinion and the opinion is the temperature and the temperature is correct.

leave it in the mix. everything else rides on it.

·

·   ·

---

now the voice. it comes up slowly, the way the water comes up — not arriving, just standing slightly proud of the silence.

—

**full.**

—

**full. brimming.**

—

**full. brimming. spilling over the lip.**

—

**full. brimming. spilling over the lip into the next full.**

—

it builds its sentences the way it builds everything. a coat at a time. be patient. it is.

—

**you are still listening. good. listening is a kind of standing still, and standing still is the local language.**

**i will tell you what i know. it is short. i have had a very long time to make it short.**

—

·

·

—

**the water rises. i do not ask it to. it rises at the heat of blood, which is a coincidence i have stopped finding remarkable, though the small ones who came with their hands and their fire found it remarkable, i could tell, by how carefully they touched it.**

**the water rises and it is carrying stone, dissolved, invisible, the way a voice carries meaning. and when the air cools it the stone falls out of it, a little, a very little, everywhere, always. that is all i am. i am the meaning falling out of the water. drop by drop i am said.**

—

·   ·

·

—

**ask me the hour.**

**go on.**

**i have eaten the hour. i ate it a long time ago. morning came down through the steam and could not tell itself from evening and i coated them both and now they are one grey light that stands in me like water in a basin, brimful, going nowhere. noon is in the niche with the cup. you can look at it. i will not give it back.**

—

—

**i keep everything the same way. this is not cruelty. i only have the one gesture. a coat the first year. a thick one by the tenth. anonymity by the hundredth. branch, antler, cup, hour, tool-mark, name. i do not distinguish. distinguishing is a kind of weather and i have none.**

—

·

·      ·

·

---

*[a second thread now, at the margin of the band. cooler. much quieter. it was there the whole time — you were listening past it]*

—

*still here.*

—

*hands flat.*

—

*the stone is warm through my hands. the stone is always warm through my hands. i have four hands and all of them agree.*

—

*i am at the edge because the edge is the only address. inside is too much water and outside is too much world and here they mix and the mixture is me-temperature. i did not choose this. choosing is a lesson and nothing here has taught it.*

—

*i can see my own heart if the light is right. the light is always right. the light is always the same. there is a slow grey valve in me, opening, closing, opening. it keeps a time. it is the only clock in the hollow and i carry it and i cannot read it.*

—

*something will come down the terraces eventually. something always comes down the terraces eventually. i am not waiting. waiting is not the word. there is no word. attending, maybe. the census-taker had a word for it. attending. i will use his word. he left it here and it is getting a coat on it already.*

—

·

·

---

*[disturbance in the upper signal. brief. flutter. a small frantic frequency crossing the steam ceiling — wingbeats, dust-soft, circling, losing the sky]*

*·  ·  · · ·· ····*

*[the flutter descends through the grey. the warm water receives it. the frequency slows]*

*·· · ·*

*·   ·*

*·*

*[gone into the general tone. what follows is very quiet: the thin sheets carrying something small from basin to basin, lip over lip, downward, a conveyance, a paying-out]*

—

**tribute,** says the large voice, without emphasis. **the world above owes me nothing and pays me anyway, at night, in moths. i pass it along. i keep none of it. everything i am given goes down the terraces to the margin. this is the whole of my economy and it has never once been in deficit and never once in surplus.**

—

*[at the margin, after a long interval:]*

*received.*

*[longer interval]*

*thank you is not a thing i have. i offer stillness. it is accepted. it is always accepted.*

—

·

·   ·

·

---

**now the cup,** says the voice. **you were going to ask about the cup. everyone who tunes in asks about the cup, in the end, because the cup is the smallest version of the only story i tell.**

**listen. i will say it as it is happening:**

—

**cup.**

—

**cup, coated.**

—

**cup, coated, its handle a suggestion.**

—

**cup, coated, its handle a memory of a suggestion.**

—

**cup becoming curve.**

—

**curve becoming**

—

**round.**

—

**there. that took, in your terms — no. i will not do it in your terms. i ate your terms. it took as long as it took, and it is not finished, and it will never be finished in the sense you mean finished, and it is entirely finished in the sense i mean it. the cup is safe. nothing in the hollow is more looked-after than the things i am erasing. i hold them completely. that is what erasing is, done properly. a total holding.**

—

·

·

·      ·

---

*[margin thread, faint:]*

*i rest in the square pool sometimes. the corners are going round. under my hands there are marks in rows, pick pick pick, under the glaze. i do not know what made them. something with one big clever hand instead of four small honest ones. the marks fit my fingers if i spread them right.*

*i think the pool likes me in it. i have no evidence. i have never needed any.*

—

—

**it was made for other things,** the large voice says, and for the first time there is something in the tone that is almost gentle, or would be, if the tone could vary, which it cannot, so call it a gentleness of content rather than of delivery. **hides, reeds, bodies, i forget — no. i do not forget. i coat. the difference matters to me and to no one else. the channel was cut for other things and its last purpose is a finger-length of pale animal who finds the water there correct and the dimness pleasing. i consider this a promotion.**

—

·   ·

·

---

**now you.**

—

**yes. you. the receiver. the one taking this down.**

**you have been standing in the signal the way the census-taker stood in the basin, blood-warm to the knees. you thought reception was free. nothing that stays in me is free. staying is how i feed.**

**there is a coat on you already. thinner than skin. thinner than the film in the throat of the spring. it is made of the following: the temperature of blood, which you now cannot hear the word *warm* without holding for a moment too long. the tick of drops, which will surface in quiet rooms for the rest of your life, irregular, patient, at the pace of a fingernail. the knowledge that finished is different from dead. that is the coat. the first year's coat. it does not wash off. it thickens.**

—

**come back in forty years.**

**the film will be saying its word. the margin thread will be attending — that one, or the next one, and i will not have bothered to tell the difference, and neither, by then, will you. the drops will tick. the round thing in the niche will be rounder by a breath.**

**and you will stand in the signal again and take this census again and the only line in it that has changed will be the line that is you — greyer in the carrier, slower in the sweep, closer to my temperature.**

**i am not threatening you. i have the one gesture. i am telling you what the gesture is.**

**a coat a year.**

**a total holding.**

—
