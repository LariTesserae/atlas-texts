with the ceiling emptied you can finally hear the country under everything:

the sound of the summit of the cone is the sound of eating.
it has been the sound of eating for ten thousand years.
it is not sinister. it is the room digesting the sky, secondhand,
one fallen insect, one fallen bird, one falling year at a time.
