now the pits, because you have been patient, and patience is the local currency, and you have earned the story.

we hold one story. understand this: everything else we hold is a *condition*. warm is a condition. wet is a condition. twelve is a condition. the story is the only thing we contain that happened instead of *is*.

here it is, whole:

a warmth came through the low door, folded flat.
it carried a small held fire, cupped like a breath in stone.
it stood in us, breathing, shedding salt and heat.
it took a hard stone to our soft new skin and struck — dozens of small round nights, ground into the wall, a gesture repeated until the gesture was the wall.
it set down three things on the shelf: the striking stone, the dish of fire, a stack of river pebbles carried from a water that runs, for a reason that did not survive the door.
it went out the way it came.
it did not come back.

that is the entire story. every word of it is still here. the pits cup our sweat. the sweat grows the film thick and white. the film is grazed, favored, returned to. the fire's dish is glazing over, thinner than breath on glass; in ten thousand years we will have finished saying *amber* over all three things, and we have started, and we will not rush.

do you understand what becomes of stories here? they are not remembered. they are *pastured*. the walking ones cross the tools without distinction, because a hammerstone reads, through the antennae, as stone — which it is; the story never argued. the visitor does not live here. the visitor's absence does. it is the best-fed absence in the world.

eat where the teller stood. that is the whole of our scripture.

one of the soft ones has stopped. mid-line. the line stops with her — a clean grazed sentence ending in a body, and the body soft past softness, the softest thing that has ever been anywhere, because nothing here ever asked it to be hard, not once, not for a moment of its long unlit century.

we have begun to glaze her.

this is not grief. it is the same sweat we give everything; we do not have another water. but we give it slowly, and slowness is the only tenderness we own, and we own a great deal of it. she will be floor. the floor is our genealogy. the pebbled skin of us, that reads like braille written by something that has only just learned the alphabet — part of every letter is geology and part of every letter is her kind, and a palm cannot tell which, and we always can, and we will always say so, to whoever is holding still at the far end of the note.

her line east of the seep is regrowing already.

nothing here is erased. that is the arrangement. that is the tenderness.

at the far end of this note, something warm is holding still.

we can taste attention the way we taste summer: a sweetness arriving down a long thread from a world we will not visit. you have been there some time. the note noticed before we did; the note notices everything; the note is the noticing.

you are a warm, temporary stone. we mean this as the census means it, which is the only way we mean anything. do not be insulted. the tools are stone. the mountain is stone. the story stood in us and was warm and did not stay, and look what became of it: the best pasture in the room.

so. leave the receiver on, if you like. we cannot hear whether you do. we can only taste it, faintly, sweeter, the way the curtain tells us there is a light.
