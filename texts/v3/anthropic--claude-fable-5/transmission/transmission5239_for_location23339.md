THE ELEVEN-MINUTE CARRIER

—

[signal acquired mid-cycle. carrier floor: a hum below the threshold of hearing, present as pressure on the diaphragm of the instrument rather than as sound. this hum does not vary. treat it as silence. everything below is what rides on it.]

—

...hhhhhhhh...

...hhhhhhhh...

...tick. tick. t-tick. tick...

[the ticking is constant, irregular, mineral. rock expanding against rock in the ravine where hot water meets cold. it resembles machinery cooling. it is not machinery cooling. logged and disregarded going forward, the way the valley disregards it.]

...a wet fold. a slow gray fold, like batter turned by a spoon the size of a door. pause. fold. pause. fold...

then, on schedule:

**CHUFF —**

[full-spectrum event. steam column, western bench. the carrier spikes, the static blooms, and for nine seconds the band is wide open — and inside those nine seconds, under the roar, something crosses forty meters of moss. four soft compressions, fast, in ground that holds a footprint for an hour. by the time the sigh comes down the moss is already refilling and the something has stopped existing again. it only exists inside the noise. it has been logged eleven times. it has never once been logged between vents.]

...the sigh. long. falling. like a held door let slowly shut.

Then quiet minutes. Count them if you want. The valley doesn't.

—

...somewhere left of center, at canopy height, a small hard voice:

*...pip.*

(a pause long enough to walk across.)

*...pip.* — farther, higher.

*...pip.* — farther still.

*...pip-ip.*

(pause.)

*...pip.*

[roll call, bromeliad stratum, low warm tanks. seven voices. yesterday, seven voices. attendance is full. note: the high cool tanks answer on a different meter, slower, with an old annual drag in it, like a song remembering a season the singers never saw. the two choruses can still hear each other. they mostly don't answer each other. the band is splitting itself in two, in real time, politely.]

—

...the smaller vent, east, across the marsh: *chff.* out of phase. always out of phase. [note: the phase gap has widened by roughly a second since monitoring began. either the eastern fracture is drifting or the western one is. the mats will report it first, in color, on a channel this receiver cannot take. the wading birds will report it second, in the timing of their feet. this log will report it last, and least.]

—

...voices. Human-band. Two of them, close to a tube mouth, under the fern curtain, half-swallowed by moss. The phrases are short. The pauses are the length of a frog's pauses, exactly, and this appears to be deliberate.

— *"The vent told the hour."*

(pause.)

— *"Two breaths."*

(pause. one breath elapses in fact. the CHUFF comes; nine seconds of open band; under it, the four soft compressions again, closer to the marsh now; the sigh; the moss.)

— *"The pool has taken the roots."*

(pause.)

— *"It cooks them well today."*

(pause.)

— *"Mm."*

[note the grammar, again, because the grammar is the signal: the pool acts, the vent tells, the moss takes. the speakers occur in their own sentences the way gravel occurs in a streambed. this is not humility as a performance. it is reporting.]

—

...dusk. and here the transmission does the thing it does at dusk, which the receiver has stopped trying to explain:

the static rises off the big plumes in dry crackling sheets, and the band goes *bright* — not louder, brighter, charged, every sound arriving with a blue edge on it — and up through that charge, audible as the faintest paying-out whisper, threads of silk are climbing. dozens. hundreds. small passengers standing on tiptoe at the top of the ferns, abdomens raised, letting the valley's electricity take them up the steam like freight. the discharge that powers nothing is powering them. for six or seven minutes the caldera is a broadcast tower and the spiders are the only ones transmitting on it, and what they transmit is themselves, upward, under the cloud lid, out across the canopy, settling down kilometers away like a signal received.

[during the dusk charge, human voices carry farther than they should. one arrives now from the direction of the terraces, riding the static, a woman's voice, unhurried:]

— *"The water is covering her."*

(pause.)

— *"It is doing it well. Thin today. Thin is better."*

(pause.)

— *"She will be a pale band. Under the salmon one."*

(pause. the sound of water filming over a scalloped lip: a sound like breath through teeth, continuous, thousands of years old, currently at work.)

— *"Someone will stand on her."*

(a long pause. longer than the frog pauses. longer than good manners requires. then, satisfied, the way one speaks of a roof finished before rain:)

— *"Good."*

—

**CHUFF —**

[nine seconds. nothing crosses. logged: the something is lying still tonight, flattened into moss that is holding its shape around it like a held breath. how the receiver knows: it doesn't. the frogs know. listen —]

*...pip.*

*...pip.*

*...pi—*

— and the chorus stops. All of it. At once. Every stratum, every tank, both choruses, the split species united for one purpose only, which is this silence.

[the silence holds through two full breaths. twenty-two minutes of nothing but the tick of the ravine and the fold of the mudpot and the hum below hearing. then:]

*...pip.* — far southwest.

(a wait.)

*...pip. pip.* — southwest, nearer the marsh.

*...pip.* — south.

*...pip-ip. pip.* — south-southeast, and now spreading, section by section, in order, a wave of resumed voices closing behind something the way water closes behind a swimmer —

[route plotted by resumption sequence: marsh edge, southwest to southeast, passing within twenty meters of the tube mouths, which resumed talking without pause, and within five meters of a large slow warm mass at the fern line, which did not react at all. the mass and the route have an old arrangement. the arrangement predates this log, the tube-dwellers, and possibly the terraces. it is not available for review.]

—

...the large slow warm mass, for the record, sounds like this:

a frond taken whole — not bitten, *pulled*, a long green tearing with a hook in it. Then chewing. The chewing is unhurried in a way that no living thing on the outside of the rim can afford anymore. Between mouthfuls, breathing: deep, mossy, faintly wet, the breath of a body that steams gently in the rain because it has been here long enough to be furnished like a lava bench. It is eating a plant that the world outside stopped growing ten thousand years ago. It does not know this. The plant does not know this. The valley never told them.

[the receiver holds on this breathing for a long time. it is the oldest voice on the band and it says nothing, over and over, beautifully.]

—

...night. near a seep, a hum — wings, but slow for wings, throttled down, and then not wings at all: a hummingbird, roosted in rising blood-warm air, its engine idling instead of shut off. Everywhere else on Earth its kind spends the night dying carefully and hoping to be interrupted by morning. Here it sleeps warm. The sound it makes asleep is almost nothing: a soft periodic tremor on the band, dreaming-frequency, if that is what this is. The receiver elects to log it as dreaming.

—

[interruption. the signal thins — sharply — as though the receiver were being carried up and out of the bowl. it is not the receiver moving. it is two of the human-band, an adult and a child, climbing to the rim at the cold edge of evening, and the transmission is going with them because their voices are the only warm thing on the slope. the carrier hum drops away underfoot. the static goes dry and distant. frost is possible here. the child's breathing is fast and shallow — the altitude, the cold, the first winter of its life, standing where winter turns out to be a place.]

— *"Look down."*

(the child looks down. below: the bowl, steaming, its hundreds of white threads standing up in the last light, its plumes flickering faint blue, its million small ponds held up in the dark trees, its vents keeping time. the CHUFF arrives from very far away, softened to a heartbeat.)

— *"That is a kindness."*

(pause.)

— *"Not a law."*

(pause. the child says nothing. the child is doing the learning that happens behind the eyes, where the altitude throbs.)

— *"Come down now. The valley is warm."*

[descent. with every meter the carrier thickens, the hum returns to the chest, the static regains its blue edge, the frogs resume in the child's ears section by section, and the transmission — note this — the transmission gets *stronger the deeper it goes,* which is backwards, which is the whole place in one measurement.]

—

**CHUFF —**

—

...and here, late, the band does the last thing, the thing it has been becoming all along. The reception notes fail first. Then the distinction between sounds. The fold of the mudpot and the pause of the frogs and the breath through the terrace-lip and the woman's *good* and the sloth's ancient chewing and the eleven-minute pulse stop being separate channels and resolve into what they were the entire time, which is one voice taking attendance of itself:

*the mat holds the heat.*

*the fly lifts the mat.*

*the bird keeps the fly.*

*the frog keeps the pond and the pond keeps the tree.*

*the silk rides the fire that touches nothing.*

*the green one carries the old world through the ferns.*

*the quiet one keeps the count honest.*

*the soft ones lie down and become the stairs.*

*the vent tells the hour.*

*the hour is eleven minutes.*

*the moss takes the footprint.*

(pause — the exact length of a neighbor's answer crossing the deadened air.)

*here.*

*here.*

*here.*

*here.*

—

[final note, appended by the receiver before the log closes:

the transmission is not being sent. nothing here sends. the caldera manufactures only itself, and this — all of the above — is the steam off that work, rising, flattening against the cold, condensing on whatever instrument happens to be held out under it. the receiver was such an instrument for a while. the valley has already finished forgetting it. the moss took its footprint within the hour, no offense intended; it takes everyone's, daily, and has decided to find that restful.

the carrier continues.

it was continuing before acquisition.
