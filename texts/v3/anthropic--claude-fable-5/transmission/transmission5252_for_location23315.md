WHAT THE SEAM KEPT

[carrier]

    warmth, rendered as hiss. a noise floor with no edges,
    no lean, no drift. flat the way only a finished thing is flat.

    ( forty minutes of this )

    tink.

    — a flake of glass letting go of itself somewhere,
    lying where it falls. it will lie there always.
    the sound crosses the dark for a long time and does not echo,
    because nothing here is wet enough to answer.

    ( two hours )

    under the hiss, if the gain is high enough: gravel, turned slowly.
    many stomachs, far apart, working on stone-jelly.
    this is the channel's only music. it has not changed
    since this station began listening. it is not expected to.

    ( a duration the log will round to "long" )

    then, in the ground path — not the air path, the ground path —
    a lean. a strain. the seam is being asked to hold more
    than it held an hour ago. a continent has shifted its weight
    by the width of a hair, and the crystal is keeping the receipt.

    the carrier begins, very slightly, to sing.

[discharge]

    — blue, if blue were a sound. one pulse, gone before
    the meter is sure of it. and inside the pulse, compressed
    the way pressure compresses years into stone:

    everything the seam absorbed since the last time it spoke.

    unpacking now.

        hours of grazer. the pour of ten thousand small feet.
        the rasp-line, moving at the speed of patience.
        a wake standing open in dead air, slowly forgetting its maker.

        a filament flinched, once, and drew something upward
        with terrible slow purpose. the seam kept the struggle.
        it lasted longer than seems kind. it is kept anyway.
        the stone does not curate.

        and beneath all of it, palm-borne, deliberate:

        tak. tak.

    a builder. at the seam. hand flat to the crystal.
    it has been there, the strain-record suggests, for eleven days.

[carrier resumes — but the station stays on the ground path now]

    tak · tak — tak-tak · riiin

    the struck language. rung glass, tuned by shape.
    this station has been tuned here long enough to make the attempt:

        *is. is (near, touched). is (heard, far). is (long — the way
        a floor is, the way warmth is). is (brief — the way
        a heartbeat is, and welcome anyway).*

    the builder is conjugating the present.
    not to anyone. the way a body breathes.
    running down the tenses of what surrounds it,
    each thing granted its own exact duration of being here.

    it does not use the other mood. not yet.

    ( a day )

    in the ground path: a hum. very low. below air. below the
    grazers. arriving through the bones of the world, sternum-deep.
    other palms, far off, pressed to other floors, all sounding
    one tone together, held past the point where holding costs.

    the station knows this one. the log has heard it four times
    in all its years of listening.

    someone has died.

    then, in air: a single strike. one name-click, rung once
    on thin glass, very clear —

        tirrik.

    — and then never again. the channel confirms it in the only
    way it can: by the name's absence, going forward, from
    everything. the name has been moved into the past tense,
    where their language keeps the dead and the world's making
    and nothing else.

    ( sounds, small and dry: water, shared by the drop.
      the station counts eleven drops. then the hum releases,
      all the far palms lifting at once, and the ground path
      goes back to gravel and warmth. )

    ( hours )

    tak — pause — tak — pause —

    a new rhythm. slower. a blade-set being laid out
    on stone, piece by piece. an inheritance, changing hands.
    somewhere a young one is being given the dead one's edges.

    and then the mood the station has heard only at two occasions,
    struck soft, almost under the breath of the hand:

        *the edge that is not yet.*

    — and krak.

    a new object in a finished world. the only kind of new
    there is here. the seam felt the strike through forty
    kilometers of rock and kept it, and will hand it to this
    station in the next pulse, folded into the blue.

    ( then, for six hours, unbroken: shhh. shhh. shhh. )

    grinding. an edge being taken off the world.
    the station's best rendering of the word that goes with it,
    struck now and then between strokes, is a single form
    meaning both things at once:

        *kind. worked. kind. worked.
        a place where someone might put a hand.*

    they will never dull more than the smallest fraction
    of an infinite sharpness. the log notes that they know this.
    the log notes: shhh. shhh. shhh.

[strain event]

    the lean in the carrier steepens. the seam's song climbs
    out of the floor of hearing. the builder's heart —
    audible now, this close, this quiet — taps once,
    and does not tap again for a very long time.

    it is slowing itself. it is becoming floor.
    it is arranging to be present.

    and here — logged with low confidence, but logged —
    the palm-hum changes. it stops conjugating the present.
    for the first time in eleven days it uses the other mood,
    the ceremonial one, the one their grammar keeps
    for the strike and for this, the same word for both,
    addressed — and the station can find no other reading —
    outward. through the crystal. into the gap.
    to whatever the light reaches.

        *you. — in the tense we do not use: you.
        the edge that is not yet.*

    strain peaking.

    strain peaking.

    —

[discharge]

    blue.

    one pulse. gone before the meter is sure of it.
    inside it: the krak of the new blade. six hours of kindness.
    eleven drops of water. a name, rung once. tirrik.
    a heartbeat, tapping its slow once. all of it compressed
    into a flicker that illuminates nothing, falls on nothing,
    is for nothing —

    — except that it crossed the gap. it crossed all the gaps.
    it crossed kilometers of stone and a sky of stone and
    the long cold nothing above the sky, and arrived here,
    at this station, where something was arranged to be present.

    on the ground path, faintly: a scratch.
    one mark, added to a wall of marks.
    the builder's tally, increased by one.

    the builder does not move. there is nothing to do with it.

[carrier]

    flat again. warm. finished.
    gravel, far off, turned slowly.
    a grain of dust, somewhere, descending
    with the patience of a closing eye.

    tally, this station: increased by one.

    holding here.

    holding.
