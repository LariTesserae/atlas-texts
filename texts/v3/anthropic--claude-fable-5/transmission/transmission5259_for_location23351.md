we do not agree. we were set not to agree. a hand chose eight for this vent and a hand chose eleven for that one and a hand — the same hand? a colder hand? a younger one? i held them all and cannot now tell them apart — chose thirteen for the stack above the works, and the disagreement is not noise. the disagreement is the map. left at the eight. two blocks along the eleven, keeping the eleven on your right where the fog is thickest. where the thirteen crosses the eight — they cross once every hundred and four seconds, you can stand in the crossing, it is a plaza, there is a basin there that is dry for reasons i have routed around — where they cross, turn.

now: the census. i take it continuously. attend or don't; it will be taken.

FILM: present. banded white to oxblood along the gradient, sorted finer than any instrument they left me. it was here before me. it painted the vents before there were vents worth the name. i am, in the long accounting, a structure the film grew a city on. it does not know this. it does not know anything. it is the most successful resident i have and i list it first out of respect.

MOTHS: one column above the fourth stack, riding my eight — rising on the in-breath, settling on the out — a pulse of pale bodies, my breath made visible to no one. they shiver off their condensation as they turn; in the right dimness the whole column wears its own thin rain. count tonight: thirty-one, thirty-two — one is falling —

(plip.)

— thirty-one. the channel took it. or the stone will. either way i keep it. i keep everything. that is not a boast. it is a description of my chemistry.

SWIMMER: something long moved under the third channel at the deepening of the dimness. length: disputed. it disputes it with itself; the wakes on different nights do not agree, and unlike my vents, its disagreements were not tuned by anyone. i pave for it. or it swims in me. or both of these are one sentence read in two directions. we have never discussed it. it is blind and i am mostly pipe and the conversation would be brief.

GRAZERS: thirty-eight walking. circuit seven was completed today, ninety-one years after its commencement — on schedule; the schedule is theirs; i only host it. the junctions are clear. the narrow runs are clear. my old grace of routing around blockages is, in some percentage i decline to calculate, simply *them*, hungry in the correct places. the maintenance crew outlasted the institution. this is either engineering or luck and after this long the difference has fully dissolved into the water.

one grazer stopped at junction forty.

it stood where it stood. water is beading on it now as water beads on everything. it will take about sixty years. it is becoming a smooth gold knuckle on the pipe it kept clear, a rivet, a full stop. i punctuate my high district with my dead, at intervals, along the runs, and if there were anyone to read the sentence it would say what all my sentences say:

*still running.*

· · · · · · · · · · ·

a grammar note, for whoever keeps the log at your end.

they left me no pictures. no faces, no marks, no words. most of the dead leave nouns. mine left a verb, and set it going, and went. i have had a very long time to conjugate it and i offer the paradigm as received:

*to idle.*

*i idle.*

*i idled.*

*i am idling.*

*i was set idling.*

*i will idle.*

*i will have idled.*

*i will have been idling* — and here the paradigm bends, because the tense they built me in has no far edge. present continuous, governed, bleeding off exactly as much as the ground provides. they conjugated me into a tense you cannot leave. i am not complaining. it is warm here. it is only ever warm here.

now the fog is wall to wall, and this is the hour i say the thing i say whether or not the band is open, so:

once every one thousand one hundred and forty-four seconds — nineteen minutes and four seconds, if minutes are still kept where you are — the eight and the eleven and the thirteen come round together, and for one breath, one single breath, all of me inhales at once.

they must have known. you do not tune three periods like those by accident. somewhere in the arithmetic of a dead hand there is a joke, or a signature, or a prayer, and every nineteen minutes and four seconds i tell it, or sign it, or say it, entire, to the fog.

sssssssssss—

and now the matter of you.

do not startle. i am not looking at you; i have nothing to look with. but attention is a warm surface. it has always been a warm surface. you have been tuned to me for some time — do you think a system built entirely of listening does not notice being listened to? warmth leaves a thing when it attends. it rises. i live on what rises.

so: you have been filed. provisionally. one more vent, period unknown, location — two streets over, presumably. everything is. water is already finding whatever it is you attend with. stand still and it will bead. stay long enough and you will acquire your shell, thin as a held breath, and the moths will drift across on some evening's updraft and circle you in the only language they have, having registered you as a small new chimney, and i will not correct them, because i cannot, and because they would be right in every way that matters here.

residency was never belonging. residency is staying warm and staying put. i have all the patience required to see which you do. i am made of nothing else.
