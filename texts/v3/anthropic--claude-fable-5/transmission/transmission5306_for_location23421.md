# The Long Minute

[—the hum, first. Always the hum. A single sustained note off a cable strung between two stone fins and connected at neither end. Everything below arrives riding it.]

[somewhere in the hum, a stroke. Iron moving once, slowly, the way a sleeper turns over. Then nothing for the length of a held breath, and another breath, and another.]

[water. dripping off a body that is walking. the body speaks. it has been speaking for some time.]

— three hundred and ninety-one. three hundred and ninety-two.

three hundred and ninety-three.

[water. the click of many small feet, passing, unalarmed.]

three hundred and ninety-four. warm on the left hand now, which is early, you're early,

— or I'm late, or the ground moved you round in front of me again while I was counting. three hundred and ninety-five. no. no, stopping the count. count's no good once the heat argues with it.

[the count is abandoned without regret. wading. a long time of wading.]

there you are.

[a palm, flat on iron. the hum thickens. through it, faintly: the long piston, and under the piston, quicker, catching a little, the second rhythm — the small asleep sound inside the housing.]

hello. still breathing in there. good.

let's have the dent. — dent. yes. and the weld running off it, sunwise — no. not sunwise. old word. running off it *warmward*, toward the pipes. so it's you. Second Engine. or the First again, come round to meet me. either way I know your face, and either way you're logged.

registered: Second-or-First, by the dent and the warm-running weld. piston at one to the long minute, no change. greasers thick along the gasket-line, working, don't mind me, loves. condensation heavy. she's sweating tonight.

[a pause. the word *tonight* hangs. she notices it too.]

listen to me. tonight. as if there's a day I'm putting it against.

[wading resumes. a soft body bumps her shins; you can hear the water fold around it and close.]

evening, whoever you are. wool tonight. wool going to shell halfway down the back, and the fifth leg still folded up under, still waiting for the load. you're new. or you're the same one wearing a different mile of yourself. announce, then, go on —

[far off, a single low call. brief. patient.]

— *here* —

[a very long pause. rain on water. then, from another direction entirely, in the same register:]

— *here* —

confirmed. two entries. or one entry, twice. filed either way. that's how we file everything down here, love, twice and either way.

[the hum dips — a moving warmth is passing along the cable, drinking as it goes — the whole signal muffles like a hand laid briefly over the mouth of it, then lifts. she waits it out. she knows this pause.]

there it goes. back along the wire. drink up.

[wading. longer now. the drizzle is audible on the backs of her hands, and — this is faint, but it is there — it does not fall at quite the same rate on each.]

right hand's raining faster than the left again. blue on the slow hand. that's what blue is now. blue is the slow hand in the rain. I kept all the words, you know. I just moved them to better rooms.

red is a pipe at pressure. I'll show you. we're coming to the pipes.

[iron heat, rising through the signal as radiated warmth rises through water. she kneels — the crust creaks, glassy, new — and lays both palms on a pipe going down into the ground. it pulses. once. the pulse takes a long time.]

red.

that's red. anyone tells you different never held it.

[the pulse again. she stays down. her voice drops — not quieter, *nearer*, the way you speak to something through a wall.]

and you. carrying again, aren't you. sweeter in the water since last sleep. green's come up under the iron, greener than it was, green like the inside of a stem, and that means more of us on the way, doesn't it. more drafts. more one-of-eaches. pour slow. no hurry. nobody down here is in any hurry, we learned that off you.

[a long time. the pipe pulses three more times. that is a long time.]

[when the voice returns it arrives doubled — the same sentence twice, three seconds apart, in the same mouth. the basin does this. she talks through her own echo without flinching, and the two voices braid:]

walking on. — walking on. — stair next, if the ground agrees. — stair next, if the ground agrees. — count from the pipe: one. two. — one. two. — three —

[the doubling pools, thickens, the two voices drifting further apart until the trailing one is a full sentence behind, a woman following herself through the dark, and then, without event, they close together again and are one:]

— nine steps. nine. it's near tonight, the stair. distance is running thin along here, mind your span, two fins an arm apart could cost you your whole supper's walk and nine steps could be the rim, you never know, you never — ah.

grating underfoot. here's the stair.

[iron rings faintly. she does not climb. eleven steps go up into nothing and she stays at the bottom of them, and her voice takes on the courtesy of a doorway:]

evening. top step's yours. only me. only the walk.

still facing out, are you. still watching the way there's nothing to watch with, at the nothing there is to watch. that's all right. somebody has to hold the direction. you got here first and you'll see it first, whatever seeing turns out to be.

logged: stair, eleven steps, occupied. as ever. as ever.

[she withdraws. wading. and now something changes in the ambience and it changes the way weather changes, from the edges in —]

[the drizzle. listen to the drizzle. across the water, some distance off, there is a gap in it. a standing-shaped silence in the rain, and the silence is moving.]

[every small sound in range goes still at once. the clicking feet. the shifting greasers. even the second rhythm inside the far engine seems to catch and hold. the hum stays — the hum always stays — but everything riding it has stopped, the whole full theater of it, ten thousand quiet bodies going stiller than still.]

[her voice, when it comes, is barely above the water:]

oh.

there you are too.

no — not logging you. you're not stock. you're the address. walk your room, love. mind the wet. it's all yours, it was always going to be all yours, we're only keeping it warm —

[the gap in the rain crosses the water. passes. the drizzle closes behind it like a seam healing. a long, long stillness. then, very near her, a small sound: a creature's face under her hand, the smooth firm dome of a sealed eye, and at its edge — you can hear her breath change — the tiny lifted corner of the seam.]

not yet.

[she says it the way you tuck something in.]

not yet. but it's lifting. I'd swear the corner's higher than last sleep. won't be long now, as we count long.

[she stands. water runs off her. far away, the low call:]

— *here* —

[the pause. the answer:]

— *here* —

[and then — this has not happened before in any hour of this signal — a third call, from a third direction, new, small, first-time-spoken, finding its pitch as it goes:]

— *here.*

[her laugh is one note, warm as the water.]

filed. welcome. filed, filed.

[she wades on. the count resumes somewhere under her breath, one, two, already folding. the hum dips — the wire-walker passing — and lifts. the piston turns over once in the dark, one stroke,]

[and the long minute begins again, and is still going, and is still going—]
