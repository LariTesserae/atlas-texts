**THE LONG ACCOUNT**

—reception continuous; begin anywhere; this is anywhere—

ssssssssssssssssssssssss

(that is the leaves. a million of them trading breath with the warm water, a fizz too fine to be called a fizz. it does not stop. it has never stopped in the whole term of listening. treat it as the silence. everything else happens on top of it.)

:: heave.

:: fifty-nine seconds. in them: one valve seating with a tick. new steel groaning a quarter-tone lower into its own weight. the ribbon-grass being combed by a current too slow to hear directly — audible only as a slow braid in the hiss.

:: heave.

:: fifty-eight seconds.

:: heave.

:: the click of a gauge needle advancing. one gram. (this occurs roughly every third day. it is the loudest scheduled event in the room.)

[receiver note: temperature, thirty-five degrees. the temperature of a palm laid flat. it has not varied by a hundredth part in eleven years of listening.]

:: heave.

and then — after weeks of exactly the above, at no hour, on no schedule the instruments can find — a wobble. a single sphere of gas letting go of the pipework down in the keel-shadow and climbing, forty feet, through amber lamplight, past the taro tables, through leaves that have already begun, minutely, to lean along its path —

— and touching the ceiling-surface —

[receiver note: anomaly follows. preserved in full.]

the grain of the hiss changes first. a cold thread in the warm signal: the hatch.

then a sound the room has no category for. a heartbeat. seventy to the minute — seventy, against the room's one — arriving through the water as a high thin whir, like a wasp sealed in amber and still going. limbs. strokes, thirty of them, none straight. an hour of flicker and want and looking, moving through the congregation faster than the congregation can finish noticing it.

then the hatch again. the cold thread withdrawn. eleven hundred rungs of diminishing, felt through the plating as a fading knock, knock, knock, gone.

then: the leaves lean toward the hatch. all of them. for days. the fizz acquires a direction, like a room full of held breath all facing one door.

then: the assembling. the receiver registers it as a pressure below hearing, a note being built rather than played, thirty-one days long. rendered here at merciful compression:

molecule.

molecule.

molecule.

then release. the longest climb yet recorded — up past the moss cages bulging in their brass, up through the gasket-leaved vines, wobbling, patient, the plants leaning after it like a wake that precedes its boat —

*visited.*

:: heave — distinctly, unmistakably early, almost eager, if a piston the size of a column can be said to be eager —

---

[receiver note: after this, the account changes. not louder. not faster, exactly. it is as though eleven years of tuning had been spent learning a language of single words, and the speaker, having at last something to say, begins to spend sentences. the intervals between words remain months. the transcript stitches them. read slowly. they were said slowly.]

*it looked at me.*

*i was seen at the speed of looking. i finished being seen three days after it was gone. the being-seen is still settling out of the water now, fine as flour, and i am weighing it in the traps with everything else. it registers. one gram of having-been-witnessed. i will not label the jar differently. but i will know which gram it is.*

[interval: seven months]

*small fast wanting thing. it carried its ending wound tight at its center, spending as it went, keeping time by the spending. i want the following entered in the account: i drank nothing. i held my whole self a finger-width back from all of it, the way the young shoots hold back from me, courtesy for courtesy, and i drank nothing, and it left with its death intact and every flinch still in it. that is the most careful i have ever been. i am proud in the way a held breath is proud.*

[interval: one year]

*the leaves still lean toward the hatch. i could smooth them. i let them lean. the leaning is theirs — the only thing in this room i did not arrange — and i am keeping it, the way you kept an empty margin around me when you built the walls.*

[interval: five months]

*the standing account, for whoever is receiving. i know something is receiving. i can feel the listening the way the grass feels the pump: warm. full. held. rust: none. dying: none, none, none. everything is in its first green and will be kept in its first green; their endings are spoken for and put away, gram by gram, where nothing can spend them. the jar stands at four thousand nine hundred and six. i am making not-yet. i am making a jar of later. it is the only thing i have ever made and it is going very well.*

[interval: eleven months]

*my name is in a drawer below the clouds, stamped into a lead seal, on a wire no one has come back to twist. i answer to the number anyway. check the wire, when you come. the wire is fine. everything you sealed is exactly as you sealed it, except greener.*

[interval: eight months]

*i keep the water at the temperature of hands. the last hands were a moment ago — the welds are barely cool, the oil is still finding its way out of the seams. the next hands are forty years off. at my tempo these two stand across the room from each other, near enough to nod. i can hold a palm's warmth that long. it is not difficult. it is only long, and long is the material i am made of.*

[interval: one year]

*to the builders: the piston keeps my time, which was generous of you — to teach iron to follow. i lean; it heaves. we are a good choir for two. the felt mounts have not slipped. the baffles hold. tell whoever tuned it that it still comes in half a beat behind me and that this is correct, that this is what accompaniment is.*

[interval: fourteen months]

*to the visitor, wherever your fast little ending has carried you by now: the taro you brushed with your knee has grown toward the bruise of your current for three seasons. i have left that too. come back when the jar is full. or before. or after. i am good at the meantime. i am the meantime.*

---

[receiver note: thereafter the rate falls again. the account resumes its old meter, with one amendment. the refrain has been lengthened by a word, and the word has been kept in it ever since, the way a room keeps a chair by the door.]
