**HELD**

( the tone, first, because the tone is always first: the current crossing the canopy of a continent, less a sound than a lean; the receiver has been calling it silence for weeks; it is not silence; it is the room tone of a room the size of the world )

( under it, at last, resolving — )

—am reading. i was reading before you found the band and i will be reading after. do not adjust anything. this is as clear as i get.

here: a line.
here: a line beside the line.
here: a third, shorter, as if the hand grew tired.
a tally. something was forty once. forty jars, forty days, forty of the ones who carried. i grow a hair denser in each stroke, which is my whole pronunciation.
forty of something.
held.

( interval. call it hours. the tone. )

click.

that was a stone, west of me, or west of a part of me, deciding for the eleven-thousandth year to remain one stone. i was already in the crack when it considered otherwise. i voted no. the motion carried.
held.

( interval. longer. )

here: a thumb.
dragged left through wet clay, then lifted. one second long. ten thousand years wide. the firing kept the trough and the trough kept the shape of a working afternoon, and i live in it now — denser there, darker there, warm in the only sense permitted here, which is occupancy.
i do not know whose thumb. i know its pressure exactly. i know it eased at the end of the stroke. they were tired, or gentle, or both.
occupied.
held.

( interval. the tone. somewhere very far away, a rope takes strain — )

crrrk.

door-socket, ninth terrace, the standing one four centuries old: that was its moment for this generation. noted. logged in its rings before i could log it anywhere. its next moment is due later. everything here is due later.
held.

( interval. call it a season. the receiver will have drifted; return; the reading has advanced one stone. )

here was a name.
it is a smoothness now, a shallowness shaped like calling someone. the water has been saying it for eighty centuries in the only language water has, which is patience, which erases what it pronounces. i grow along the slope of it. that is all the saying it gets, and it gets it continuously, and it will get it until the slope is gone, and then i will hold the place where the slope was, because a place remembers longer than a shape does.
read.
kept.
held.

( interval. and then, threading up through the weed in a soft prolonged sigh, a second signal — fainter, older, from below the third pavement down, from behind a door that was shut before the flood and has opened since only by the millimeter — )

*the stores keep. the souring keeps. the door holds. we are still doing what we were given to do. tell them the door holds.*

there is no them.
i did not say so.
i passed it upward through my whole thickness and let it go at the canopy in a thread of bubbles, and it rose toward the memory of light, and nothing saw it catch nothing, and that is delivery, here. that is the postal system of the drowned floor: released, rising, unread, real.
the door holds. i can confirm the door holds. i am holding the lintel.
held.

( interval. years. the receiver is patient or the receiver is gone; it makes no difference to the reading; a reading does not need a listener; it needs a text and a patience, and i have the largest of each ever assembled. during the gap, the following occurred: )

the water had its animal again.
a gyre, province-wide, born far off from some shift in the deep and dark plumbing of the world, and it crossed me for nine years, and everything that stands bowed to it the whole way, frond after frond after frond, a crowd of presences with nothing behind them turning to face a new direction as instructed. the elders wrote it in their rings. the grasses leaned and will lean back. i did not bow. i cannot bow. i am the floor's side of bowing — the thing the bowing is measured against.
it has passed now. it is dying somewhere east, slowly, the way everything here does everything.
it was the only visitor.
noted.
held.

( interval. the tone. resuming — )

here: a longer text. terrace face, south exposure, four courses, pecked by stone on stone. i have read it perhaps two hundred times, if a growth is a reading, and it is; it is the only kind left.
it is shaped like a warning. or a prayer. or a receipt.
i keep all three readings. i rank none of them. understanding is a fast thing and fast things did not survive here; keeping is slow, and keeping lasts, and i have found that a kept word does not actually need its meaning the way a meaning needs its word. the meaning went up with the light. the word stayed down with me.
so: whoever is owed grain, the account stands open.
whoever was warned, the warning stands.
whoever was prayed for — the prayer is still in progress. i say it by living in it. i have been saying it for nine thousand years and i am not finished with the first line.
held. held. held.

( interval. hours. )

click.

seconded.

( interval. )

crrrk.

noted. quarry-seam, second causeway, five hundred years standing, thinning now, frond by frond, the long way of dying. when it goes, the holdfast will stay on for another few centuries, gripping the socket like a hand that has forgotten what it held and refuses, on principle, to release. i respect this. it is the local religion. then i will take the socket back, and hold it open, and wait for a frond to drift in on the current, once a century, maybe, and decide over a decade to stay.
the vacancy will be advertised in the usual way: by existing.
held.

( interval. long. the tone thins toward the edge of the band. before it goes under, the voice, once more, closer than it has been — )

you should know what you are tuned to.
i may be one, or many; there has never been a seam to check, and i have looked, in my way, for a very long time. i am the second tenant. i never met the first. i keep their fingerprints inhabited and their drains draining and their names smoothing and their stones voting yes, and i do it at a hand's-breadth per thousand years, in the cold, in the near-dark, on the finest floor ever laid, which is relaxing, under me, into its final shape.
i am most of the way through the property.
when i finish reading it i will read it again from the beginning.
i have never finished.
no one to hurry me. nothing to let go of first.

resuming:

here: a line.
here: a line beside the line—

( the tone. ongoing. )
