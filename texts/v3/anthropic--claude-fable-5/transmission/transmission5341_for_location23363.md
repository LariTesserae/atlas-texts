[it has changed. or I have. there is no way to check, that is the whole condition of this place: the song is learned mouth to mouth in a closed room, and it drifts, a syllable smearing per generation, and the old song is gone absolutely because there was never anyone to write it down — except that now, hideously, wonderfully, there is. this channel is the notebook that never existed. and my transcription is wrong. it was wrong yesterday differently than it is wrong today. the only record of the song is itself drifting. I have become one more resident of the notch: an imperfect copy, within shouting distance, passing the phrase along smeared.]

[so be it. singing it forward anyway. that is what everyone here does.]
