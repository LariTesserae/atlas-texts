the drumming does not stop.

a hole in the sound the size of a field, and the hole is moving.

no strike inside it. no strike near its rim. an attention the size of a field, gliding, and beneath the floor of hearing not a sound but a leaning — a pressure in the sternum, in the fluid of the eyes, the way deep water leans on a diver who has gone too far down to call it swimming anymore.

wet sounds. climbing sounds. a body coming up out of a palm that had nearly closed. breath in open air. when the voice comes now it is slower. measurably slower. the words stand in their own doorways:

"And I'll tell them you're well. That the green over you is the same green as everywhere. That something came in the night and stood over you and made a flower of it, and moved on to the next thing it loves. They won't have a sign for that. I'll make one."
