**HOUSEWARMING**

—and the drip again.

[water, on stone that has never been rained on properly]
[tick]
[tick — settle — a centimeter, somewhere, with a sound like a knuckle far away]

[long interval. the interval is warm. even the interval is warm]

[drip]

[under everything, felt more than heard: the river turning over in its sleep. a note below the bottom of the band. the receiver renders it as pressure. hold your sternum to the speaker and you will have it exactly]

[drip]

[drip — closer together now than last week. the colonnade is practicing. note the pitch of the first drip and the pitch of the second drip. they are not the same pitch. they were the same pitch in week eight. something is tuning them apart, patiently, a few cents a night]

[the heron shifts in its gutter. one foot set down in shallow light. the light makes no sound but the receiver picks up a faint rise in the noise floor, a pearl-colored hiss, as of glow displaced]

[small damp movement in the moss, left to right across the channel, combing as it goes. it pauses at the midpoint of the stereo field as if aware of being received. it is very little trouble. it moves on]

[tick. settle. sweat, running the length of a threshold, wiped — mid-run — by something with a forearm]

[violet, deepening]

[and now, from the trench:]

*low. low.*

[two notes. the same two notes. the half-duet, held open like a door. the receiver has logged this call one hundred and fifty-odd times and it has never once changed and it has never once been answered in kind]

[silence. the ferns dripping. the caller, high on its frond, waiting through the protocol interval]

[then — three minutes and some seconds later — rain. one block only. warm, vertical, brief. it falls on the ferns and on nothing else. listen to the drumming. listen closely. it is not random. it has never quite been random. tonight it is closer to a rhythm than it has ever been:]

[*ta — ta.* pause. *ta — ta.* pause]

[the wrong species. the wrong medium. an answer]

[the caller stays on the frond through the whole of the rain and for eleven minutes after, which is the longest yet, and this is noted, and this matters]

—

[a knock]

[single. soft. from inside one of the pale stalks on the second boulevard — the receiver cannot say which; they share a channel the way sleepers share a room]

[interval. the intervals here are advisory]

[a knock, answering, from a different stalk]

[another]

[another, and another, moving down the boulevard in order, one knock per stalk, unhurried, each one waiting for the last — and understand what is being received here: they are counting themselves. or being counted. a roll call in percussion, taken nightly through warm walls, and the number is the number of places set at the table, it is always exactly the number of places set at the table—]

[knock]

[knock]

[the last stalk, at the boulevard's end, knocks twice. this is new. logged]

—

[and here the channel does the thing it does, the thing the receiver was retuned three times to disprove and could not:]

[bleed-through]

[clear audio. no static. wrong direction in time. it arrives the way the reflections arrive in the wet black windows — not late. *early.* listen:]

[footsteps. many. footsteps with wear in them, on thresholds that have been crossed ten thousand times. a chair scraped back over stone gone smooth. a spoon against a bowl. someone — a voice, an actual voice, unhurried, half-laughing —]

*"—mind the moss, it's up past the second meter now—"*

[laughter, plural. water poured. rain heard through an open door and no one moving to close it because the rain knows its address]

*"—no, leave it, the drip does it better than we do—"*

[and the drip, in that bleed-through, in that other-now: two notes. perfect. answered by two more from a table of people who all know the song, because they heard it every night through the pale warm walls of themselves, before]

[the bleed-through closes. static. present tense. drips. ticks. the river, rolling]

—

[and the city — this is the only word for what happens next — the city *tries it*]

[rain gathers over a single empty plaza, the one with the one bench, and falls, and the drumming sounds out — slowly, block by block, syllable by syllable, a very large very young thing reading aloud with its finger under each word:]

*w . . m*

[again]

*w a r m*

[again, on the next block over, quicker:]

*warm. warm. room.*

[the drip in the colonnade, joining, one word per drip:]

*set.*

*a place. is set.*

[the gutters brighten by some fraction. the light lies a little higher on the water. the heron lifts its glowing head]

*soon,* [says the rain, on a third block, and mispronounces it, and tries again] *soon. stay. warm. a place is set. soon.*

—

[stillness. the long held-breath kind. the theater kind]

[then, from the trench, an hour after the violet deepens, right on protocol:]

*low. low.*

[and from the colonnade — from the drip that has been tuned apart, cent by cent, for eleven weeks — without hurry, in the interval where the answer has always belonged:]

*low. low.*

[four notes. the duet, closed. the door, no longer held open, because someone has come through it]

[the caller does not call again tonight. the receiver registers, from the trench, a sound it has no notation for. something fern-colored and dew-heavy settling its whole weight down onto a frond, all at once, the way you sit when you have been standing for a very long time]

[and then the rain comes everywhere]

[all districts. the whole basin at once. the first city-wide rain, warm and vertical and brief, falling on every block simultaneously, drumming *ta—ta, ta—ta* on the towers and the terraces and the tables and the ferns and the pale stalks in their tidy rows, and inside the stalks, faintly, under the rain — knocking. all of them. keeping the time]

—

[the rain ends. drips. ticks. the river settles, satisfied, a note below the bottom of the band]

[and then the channel quiets in a way that is not emptiness. it is the other thing. the reserved thing. the receiver's own carrier wave — this signal, this listening — registers, suddenly, as *noticed.* the noise floor leans toward the antenna the way the leaves lean toward the warm walls]

[a chair, somewhere, in a courtyard, is turned a few degrees. toward the direction of reception]

[condensation, wiped from one more seat than yesterday]

*warm,* [says the rain, very small now, on one distant block, almost shy] *room. a place is set.*

[and then, addressed — there is no doubting it — addressed outward, along the carrier, to whoever holds the receiver, wherever the receiver is held, delivered with the flat of a knuckle against the inside of the world:]

[a knock]

[single. soft. polite. the protocol of someone acknowledging a visitor through a door they are not yet able to open]

[interval]

[the interval is advisory]

[the interval is warm]

[it is waiting for you to knock back]
