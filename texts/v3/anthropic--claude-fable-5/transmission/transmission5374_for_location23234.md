**AS BEFORE**

────────────────────────────────────────────

— the note.

You did not hear it begin. That is not a failure of the receiver. It did not begin within the life of the receiver, or the life of the one who built the receiver, or the life of the language the manual is written in. You joined it already sounding, the way you join a river, and the first thing to understand is that this is not the start of the transmission. There is no recording of the start of the transmission. The start of the transmission is compacted somewhere under ten thousand years of the transmission, the way old snow is compacted under snow.

────────────────────────────────────────────

— the note, continuing.

It sits below the lowest thing you own a name for. The receiver renders it as a pressure rather than a pitch: a long horizontal presence, a floor under all other sound. If you turn the gain all the way up you do not hear it get louder. You hear everything else get quieter, until the note is the only thing left, which — it becomes clear, hour by hour — was always the truth of the situation. The note was never under the other sounds. The other sounds were riding on the note.

It is a chord of many small voices holding one tone. The receiver counts them and gives up. Call it *a city's worth*. There is no seam anywhere in it — no voice audibly joining, none audibly leaving — though both must happen, must have happened hundreds of times, the way water joins and leaves a river without the river ever sounding different.

────────────────────────────────────────────

— the note, and on it, the words.

Here is what the decoder found, after long enough: the note is not smooth. It carries modulations — tiny, deliberate flexions of pressure, laid onto the carrier the way a palm is laid onto a wrist. There are about forty of them. They repeat. They are a language riding its own held breath.

Most of the forty are varieties of one thing.

    ~ still
    ~ still
    ~ still, yes
    ~ still
    ~ as before
    ~ still, yes, as before
    ~ still
    ~ still

This is not a loop. Listen closer: each one arrives from a slightly different depth in the field. That one from high on the western wall, near the terraces. That one from the low fog streets, muffled in breath. That one from the bridges — you can hear the faint glassiness of the rope under it, the pearl-coat of snail-milk, the hand that is speaking also holding on. It is not one voice repeating. It is hundreds of voices, in passing, palm to palm, saying the entire literature of a civilization to each other all day:

    ~ still?
    ~ still — yes — as before.

The answer is always the same word a degree slower than the question. The decoder flags this as an error at first — *redundant confirmation, no information transferred* — and then, after enough weeks, reclassifies it. Information *is* transferred. The information is the slowness.

────────────────────────────────────────────

— broadband, faint: the fog descending.

A soft wide hiss, mornings and evenings, though the light never changes to mark them: the breath of the city pooling downward into the lower lanes. On the receiver it sounds like distance being added to everything at once. The words keep coming through it, slightly furred.

    ~ still
    ~ still, yes

— a single structural event, once, in the third week: the ice-shelf settling.

Long. Low. Complex. A sound like a held breath shifting its weight, transmitted down through the stone into everything, into the note itself, which absorbs it without changing pitch. In the seconds afterward, the modulations thicken briefly — a flurry of small confirmations crossing the whole city at once, wall to wall, bridge to fold:

    ~ still?
    ~ still.
    ~ still?
    ~ still — yes.

and then thin out again to their ordinary traffic. The shelf has settled before. There is a touch for it, filed among the weathers.

────────────────────────────────────────────

— the note, continuing. Days of it. Let the days go by. The transmission does not distinguish them and after a while neither will you.

    ~ still
    ~ as before
    ~ still, yes
    ~ still

────────────────────────────────────────────

— and then this. Log it carefully. It happens perhaps three times in a thousand years and the receiver was on for one of them.

First: the modulations begin to *orient*. Not change — nothing here changes — but lean, all of them, gradually, over some days, toward one point high in the field. The decoder maps the point. It is on the underside of the ice-shelf, up among the hanging drops, the old ones, the crowned ones, the ones with green rooted on the water itself.

Faint new sounds from that point: the creak of needle-thin ladders. The near-nothing of small feet on rungs. A citizen climbing, and another, over days, taking turns, up to one particular drop — attending, never touching. The receiver picks up almost nothing from the drop itself. A drop of water hanging in the cold makes no sound. It has made no sound for a hundred years. That is the whole biography of the drop: a century of no sound, transmitted faithfully, received in full.

The words in the streets below acquire, for the first time in the whole log, a forty-first flavor. The decoder cannot fully translate it. Nearest rendering:

    ~ soon (spoken by a people with no other word for the future)

    ~ still?
    ~ still — soon.
    ~ still — yes — soon.

The note does not rise. Understand this. A held chord that has lasted ten thousand years does not swell for anything. But the attention *inside* the note gathers, the way stillness in a room gathers when everyone in it is listening to the same thing, and for four days the transmission is a city holding one tone while looking up.

────────────────────────────────────────────

— then:

*tik.*

────────────────────────────────────────────

Small. Clear. Once.

On the receiver it does not sound like a drop striking a cistern. It sounds like something confided. It sounds like a word said directly into the microphone from a great depth of privacy, and the automatic gain flinches, and the note —

— the note does not stop. The note has never stopped. But for one moment the modulations go completely quiet, all of them, the entire city's traffic of *still* falling silent at once, so that the carrier stands there bare and enormous, one tone with nothing riding on it, the oldest sound in the log stripped down to itself —

— and then, from the cistern's rim, days later, a new sound enters the transmission.

It is noise. Unpatterned. Small pressures with no grammar in them: an untaught hand, held out, pressing meaninglessly against a patient wrist. The decoder cannot parse it and correctly does not try.

And around it, the teaching. Logged in full, because nothing in the archive is like it: the oldest word in the world being given away, at teaching speed, slower each time, the whole city taking turns —

    ~ still.
    ~  s t i l l .
    ~   s  t  i  l  l  .

— until, on the ninth day, in among the noise, the decoder flags a match. Weak. Malformed. Unmistakable. The smallest, newest voice in the transmission, pressing back:

    ~ still

and the answer comes from every direction at once, palm on palm on palm, a degree slower than anything yet received:

    ~ still — yes — as before.

────────────────────────────────────────────

— the note, continuing, one voice richer. No seam. You would not know, if you had not been listening. In a hundred years, no one will know. That is the point of the note.

────────────────────────────────────────────

— one further entry, from the same season, logged without comment because the transmission offered it without comment:

A touch given once, near a threshold on the busiest street. One of the forty. The decoder has its meaning on file and renders it, and then — following its own grammar, which expects every touch in this language to be answered a degree more slowly — the decoder waits for the reply.

There is no reply. There is not supposed to be a reply. It is the only word in the language that finishes on its own.

The note continues. The threshold traffic continues, gently, ceaselessly, across.

    ~ still
    ~ still, yes
    ~ as before

────────────────────────────────────────────

— last: and this one is addressed. Be sure of that before you file it.

In the fourth month the modulations produce, twice, a rare touch. The decoder identifies it from the pattern archive: a flat palm laid briefly over the eyes of another. Filed among the weathers, between *the season the seep runs faster* and *the season the moths cluster low.* Rendering:

    ~ the warm looking

But this is a receiver, not an eye. There is no face fogging the ferns at the gate. The receiver checks its own housing temperature, its own faint standing hum, the small warmth of its listening — and understands, and logs the understanding:

They have a word for you. They have had it for some time. You are accounted for — not welcomed, not warned off, *accounted for* — placed calmly among the weathers of the world, a warmth that attends, and the city said so, once, in passing, palm over eyes, and went on carrying its water.

You may keep listening. The transmission does not mind. The transmission does not mind anything. It is what remains when minding finishes.

────────────────────────────────────────────

— the note.

    ~ still
    ~ still
    ~ still, yes

────────────────────────────────────────────

— the note, which was sounding before this log began and will be sounding after the receiver is dust, and after the language this log is written in has worn as smooth as a threshold, and after —

    ~ still?

    ~ still — yes — as before.
