the signatures reassembling themselves, softer, colony by colony, each remembering its own shape of throat. the rings on the water widening out and going flat. the seventy-one cups ringing down through their spiral in reverse, (t)(t)(ti)(tii)(tin)(ting), and still.

*quiet at the boundaries tonight. no small cries from the nest-edges. the shift will change without a fight. it lasts a day. it always lasts about a day.*

*…OMMMM………………………OMMMM………………………*

the long tone under everything, and folded into it now, small, a new figure, the way a bell keeps the shape of the last thing that struck it.

and at the rim, in the dark that is only a dimmer gray, the silent one lifts its blunt face out of the water, and opens the throat it has not used in eleven years, and makes, into the whole enormous wall of it, one sound:

pip.

thin. generic. shaped by no one. the first call and the only call. it does not carry a body's length before the chorus takes it out of the air.

*received.*

*kept. it goes into the stone with the rest.*
