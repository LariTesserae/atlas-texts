—

the loom begins.

there is no way to notate the loom that is honest. imagine a sound made of shuttling, systematic, vast, arriving from every bearing including below, and never once loud. imagine a continent whispering to itself at industrial scale. the light-values on the auxiliary channel are flickering as the deck spends itself. terrain is falling somewhere west of here — the signal carries the long soft concussions of provisional valleys settling like laundry.

and now, on the voice band, which has been silent for nineteen days:

breathing. ten distinct signatures of breathing, close together, upturned — you can hear the angle of a throat, it turns out, if you listen long enough.

then:

—

**a voice, the first, very old, worn smooth:**

"There was water where the high ground is. It came down in the tenth minute of a two-day fall and it lay across the country and it *moved*. The moving had a rhythm. The rhythm served nothing. I have looked for forty years for what the rhythm served and I have not found it and I no longer believe I was meant to. It had a horizon. It had a sound. The sound is the part I cannot keep and the part I want back. I submit this again."

—

**a voice, the second** — and here the log must note an anomaly the receiver has stopped calling an anomaly: this voice arrives twice, a faint copy preceding itself by half a second, so that every sentence is announced by its own ghost —

*"—it was gray"* "—it was gray. Not the gray of the light. A gray with *weight* in it. It lay down over a hundred kilometers as if the land were a basin that had always been waiting to be one. Ten minutes. I counted my own pulse and I have distrusted my own pulse ever since, because it says ten minutes and my body says I lived there. I submit this again."

—

**a voice, the third.** close to the receiver — very close — and accompanied by a constant fine hiss, like mist against a warm surface, which the log now understands to be her hair, which has been wet for forty years:

"I walked out into it. Eleven of us stood at the edge of it and I walked out into it, and it held my weight the way doubt holds weight — provisionally, generously, on review. The swells came through me more than against me. When the land began to decline it, I felt the decline pass through the water like a change of mind, and I came back, and my hair came back wet, and it has stayed wet, and I understand this to be a receipt.

I submit my hair. I submit it every storm. It is still wet. The matter is therefore still open."

—

⟨the fourth through the tenth voices gave their accounts in turn. the receiver holds them all. they are the same account. they are not the same account. one witness submitted only the sound the sea made, or an attempt at it — a long shaped exhalation, rising and falling, rising and falling — repeated nine times, into the loom, toward the deck. the log preserves all nine repetitions. they get closer as they go.⟩

—

then the eleventh position.

silence, on the voice band. the breathing signatures all pause together.

and from directly beneath the witnesses — through the ground, through the instrument's mount, through everything —

one deep note.

soft. patient. felt in the sternum of the equipment.

the log has recorded forty-one such notes since carrier acquisition, and understood them as districts settling. this one arrived at *her turn*, in the gap held open for her, out of stone that the auxiliary channel confirms is running warm.

the ten waited through it as if it were an account.

the receiver has decided that it was.

—

⟨and then. log the following exactly, in order, without composure:⟩

the condensate changed.

salt. salt in the fall, on the wind, on the housing of this instrument — measurable, unambiguous, first occurrence on record —

and in the shallow black basin east of the witnesses, something condensed that was not fog and was not rain. gray. translucent at the edges. perhaps ten meters across. it lay down in the basin and it *moved*, and the moving had a rhythm, and at its scale — this must be said plainly — it had swells. a sea the size of a resting body. a sea the size of a bed. it made, very quietly, the sound.

the ten went silent on every band at once. no pulse. no breath the receiver could find.

then the old first voice, barely:

"Ah. You kept it."

they did not touch it. they walked its edge — ten sets of slow footsteps circling ten meters of provisional ocean, around and around, attending, hesitating on its behalf, lending it their doubt the way they lend it to everything — and it lasted. it lasted an hour. it lasted two. the hiss of the wet one's hair moved around its circumference all night, if this dimness has a night, beading, weeping or its equivalent, the log cannot tell the difference and declines to try.

in the third hour it went soft at the edges, and rose.

a small ascending curtain, gray going up through gray, taking its salt with it — but slowly, the witnesses' attention hanging on it like weight — and as it rose the ten tipped their heads back and exhaled together, ten breaths climbing after it, visible, co-signed, and the pulse-band lit with one pattern repeated down every vine on this bearing, hillside relaying hillside toward the cold margins, gloss confidence total:

the loom is fading now. the front is passing. the drips have resumed, one leaf at a time, like an orchestra retaking its seats.

the ten are dispersing. the second voice said goodbye before itself. the wet one passed close by this instrument and paused — the hiss of her held still for a moment, a small weather standing in front of the antenna — and the receiver will not speculate about what she was doing except to note that the housing now smells, faintly and probably permanently, of solder.
