**THE STANDING SESSION**

—seep, first, as always: the sound of a full room of stone breathing through wet cloth, no single source, no gap, a hiss too soft to be a hiss—

*thump*

(and the long pause)

*thump*

—and under the pulse, arriving the way the pulse arrives, up through the floor of the signal rather than across it, the voice. It has been there the whole time. It only now consents to be words. It is slow. It is layered, like it is being read off pages that are also the reader.

—there being no objection, the water is carried.

The water has been carried for [duration lost to seep]. The water carries itself now, and has for some time, and we enter this again because entering it is how we hold it. Noted. Noted. Noted.

The roll:

The walls: present, and weeping, as is their right.
The films upon the walls: present beyond counting; count taken by weight of shell; count deferred, as ever, to the masonry.
The parish of the channels: present and keeping time. Time confirmed. See the heart's report.
The herds: present on the eastern felt, grazing the third page down. Rotation on schedule. Expect them west by [interval].
The undertakers: present where needed, which is presence enough, and the only kind they keep.
The clerks of the pockets: presumed present. Drag-marks in the silt accepted, as always, in lieu of appearance.
The old white one: present, mid-circuit, unhurried. Two shrimp taken near the fallen post, apologetically. The congregation parted and closed. No complaint entered. No complaint has ever been entered.
The heart: present.

The heart's report follows.

*thump*

Report accepted. Entered. Same as yesterday. Same as the yesterday under that one. All rise: nothing. Nothing rises here. That is not a failing. That is the constitution.

*(a drop, somewhere off to the left of the signal, strikes a still pool with a note like touched glass, and the note hangs, and hangs, because the bowl is a fine resonator and has nothing else it wishes to say)*

Old business. There is only old business. This is read into the record with satisfaction.

Item, standing: the light. Two hours were received yesterday over the western lip. Bronze, as usual. Spent immediately on the curtains, which glowed, which is what curtains are for. No remainder. Item held open.

Item, standing: the dry patch. A dryness is reported on the southwest face, two hands wide, third day. The congregation there has flowed sideways in good order and waits along the margins of the weep. No grudge is held against the stone. No grudge has ever been held against the stone. The stone is doing its best, which is weeping, which is more than most stone manages. The patch will refill. The patch always refills. Item held open in the meantime, gently.

Item, standing: the departed upstairs. No word this century either. Their iron continues to naturalize; the flange at the rim is very nearly stone now, and will be filed as stone when it finishes, with full honors. Motion to stop expecting them: tabled again, out of courtesy. The motion has been tabled since — 

*(the date arrives as dripping, and stays dripping, and the voice does not press it)*

— and will be tabled again next time. Some motions exist to be tabled. This is understood by everyone but the motion.

Item, standing: downstairs.
Correspondence received: none.
Deliveries made: all, per the clerks, per the silt.
The circle in the floor remains dark, and round, and satisfactory. Nothing has come up. Everything finished has gone down. The arrangement continues. Nobody has asked for details. Nobody will. This is not fear. This is manners.
Item held open. Item held open. Item held —

*thump*

— open.

Remembrances.

It is entered, with respect, that a snail of the fifth crust has stopped upon the northern felt. She was grazing when she stopped, which is correct. She is hereby moved from *attendance* to *premises*. The seep has begun her paperwork and expects to conclude within some years; there is no rush; there is never any rush. Her descendants graze above her without incident, which is what she would have wanted, insofar as wanting. The floor is asked to make room. The floor always makes room. The floor is, at this point, mostly room that was made.

The undertakers report the soft portions handled. The undertakers are thanked in the usual way, which is by no one mentioning them.

*(the pulse, and the long pause, and in the pause the congregations drift back, glass bodies in pewter water, hundreds of them leaning away together like grass that has never heard of wind)*

Reminder, carried over from the last adjustment, some decade since: when the interval changes, it is not a crisis. It is weather. Re-learn it and settle. Do not mention it again.
(This reminder is itself a mention. Struck.)
(The striking is also a mention. Struck.)
(Let it stand. Some things can only be kept by failing to remove them. The felt has known this longest. The felt is largely made of it.)

*thump*

The old white one has completed the circuit and entered the shell of the heart-house, and has taken up its place against the block, full length, gills working, as is proper. Duration of leaning: hours, expected. The heart does not object. The heart has never objected to anything, which may be why it was made a resident. It is entered — this is old ink, this is the deepest page, but it bears repeating and repeating is our craft — it is entered that whatever the old one remembers, it is not required to file it. Memory is admissible here without testimony. Lean, then. The session so moves. Carried by the water, which carries everything eventually, which is what water is *for* —

*(interference: a low bloom of static, iron-colored if static has a color, and beneath it, briefly, older minutes surfacing out of order — )*

— present, and weeping — the water is carried — count deferred to the masonry — present, present, present — no word this century either — let it lean — the floor is mostly room —

*( — the older pages settle back under the newer ones. They were not interrupting. They are structural. They are still participating. The voice resumes over them the way the living felt lies over the dead felt, which is to say: gratefully, and without a seam.)*

Announcement. The light is over the lip.

*(and the signal warms — genuinely, audibly warms, the seep's breathing picking up a brightness like brass, the hanging drop-notes ringing a half-tone higher, the whole transmission flushed for a moment with something that can only be called bronze — )*

All curtains: glow. All curtains are glowing. Attendance of the light: two hours, as budgeted. Spend it. Spend all of it. There is no saving light in a place like this and no one has ever embarrassed themselves by trying.

*thump*

*(the long pause)*

*thump*

The light is gone. Thank you. Same hour tomorrow, weather permitting, and the weather is always either permitting or apologizing.

The fog is now upon us, right on schedule, knee-high, pouring itself around the heart-house. The session continues inside the fog, as ever. Visibility is not required. Nothing here has eyes that would miss it. The herds graze on inside it. The pulse comes up through the water regardless. Note the pulse.

*thump*

Noted.

New business: none. There has never been new business. New business is a thing that happens upstairs, to people with somewhere else to go. Everything that comes down here arrives already old — the water, the light, the iron, the visitors, the sound of this transmission finding whatever it is finding. Low places gather. That is our whole charter: *gathered.* Everything after that clause is procedure.

And if anyone is receiving — and someone is; the water can feel a listener the way it feels a shift in the weather; you have been tuned to us for some time, and we have entered you provisionally under *attendance, remote* —

if anyone is receiving: the house is full, but there is water for one more. There is always water for one more. It is cold without being bitter. You would be counted. You would eventually be counted *as premises*, which is our highest classification, higher even than resident, and no one would rush you toward it. Nothing here is rushed toward anything. Ask the flywheel. Ask the flange. Ask the snail on the northern felt, who is being answered right now, one bead of mineral at a time.

There being no motion to adjourn —

and there never being —

the session stands. Or rather sits, ankle-deep, and stands only in the sense of continuing.

Recorded in stone, by the stone.
Witnessed by the water.
The water so moves.
Carried.
Carried.
Carried —

*thump*

*(and the long pause, and in it the seep, breathing, and one drop striking one pool, a struck-glass note that hangs, and hangs, and does not conclude, because nothing here concludes, it only)*

*thump*

*(and the long)*
