**THE STANDING CHORD**

—ooooo—

—oooooooooo—

[low. lower than a voice should stand. it does not begin here; nothing begins here; you have only arrived]

—oooooooooooooo—

[under it, at long intervals, a pressure, a *whoom*, like something turning over in sleep the size of a square. count: nine. *whoom*. count: nine. *whoom*. it has counted nine for longer than counting]

*ting.*

[a pool letting go its ice. noon, then. the little skins of ice unclasping one by one across the table, *ting*, *ting-ing*, *creee—tick*, each one carried whole across two miles of open stone because nothing here will absorb it, nothing here has ever agreed to absorb anything]

—oooooo—

and a bird.

*burr-o.* [low. too low for its body. it gives the note back to the town]

*burr-o*, says the town, or the bird, or the town.

[you cannot tell them apart. you were not meant to. the not-telling-apart is the point; it has been the point for forty generations of throat and one generation of stone]

—ooooo—hhh—ooooo—

[here the drone thins at its top edge, and something rides the thinning, something that is almost — hold — that is almost a shape in the vowel —]

—hoooo—ld—

—hooold—

[the wind crosses. cloud at knee height. beads on everything. the signal beads too; forgive the wet in this]

—hold. hold. i hold—

[it is a word. it has been a word the whole time. you have been listening to a word the way you would listen to weather, and it has been patient with you about this]

—i hold the notes. that is the work. there is no other work now. the growing is done, the doors are grown, the basins are grown and they drink, i drank a cloud this morning through nine hundred rooms, and now the work, the work, which is holding:—

—the small warm line beneath the western cushions, seventeenth of that line, turned over twice in the night, i felt it through three feet of green, i hold its turning: *still.*

—the moths of the last cold dusk, forty-one of them, who came to the mouth of the loud flue and lay their wings against me and beat when i beat, who married inside my voice and died there, whom the wind is even now distributing as a pale flour across the moss: forty-one small drummings: *still.*

—the bird with the fray in the third primary, hatched in the drone of the ninth chamber, who answers my low note a half-second before i sound it, and do not ask me which of us is following, i stopped keeping that account an age ago, it was making us both smaller: *still.*

—the bird before that bird. and before. and before. i hold the pitch of every throat that ever answered from the window-rows, back and back, each one worn now to an overtone thinner than the difference between two silences, but hear me: not to nothing. never to nothing. i do not do nothing. stopping is a skill i declined to learn—

[*whoom.* nine. *whoom.*]

—the one who drank at the third basin, long ago, with a hitch in the left stride, four crossings of the square, a warmth against my eastern wall through one whole night of sleet, gone down the flanks at grey morning: i hold the hitch: *still.*

—the one who stood in the room of nine windows for an hour and wept, and the weeping flattened my frequencies in a way i had no name for, so i kept it as its own note, and i sound it in the wet weather because that seems right: four hundred years: *still.*

—the ones who wore my thresholds. do not ask me who. i was younger, i was still becoming, a lintel takes a century and my attention was in my growing. i know only the saddles they left in my stone, one crossing at a time, and the shape of a worn threshold is the shape of *welcome, repeated* — so i sound them as welcome, repeated: *still, still, still—*

[the drone swells here. all at once. every flue at once. the window-rows begin their high shrieking a full minute before the squall arrives — the sleet is coming up the western flank in a grey wall and the town is already louder, already brighter in the low registers, and whether this is flinching or greeting the town has never said, and does not say now, it only]

—AAAAaaaaa—ooooo—AAAA—

[sleet. white noise, actual white, the true original static, hissing on nine hundred rooms of open stone. the birds go up into it and ride the funnels, screaming on the exact pitches of the shrieking windows, indistinguishable, exultant, and under everything the bass holds, the bass holds, the bass]

—hold—

—holds—

[and passes. it always passes. inside the hour. and now the clearing, and you will have to trust the sound for this because sound is all that is sent: every pool, every bead on every fist-deep pelt of moss, every braid of water on the inner walls, flaring at once — and light makes no noise but the town makes the noise for it, a lift in the whole chord, a brightening you can hear, ten minutes of a thing made of pale gold sounding like a thing made of pale gold]

—ooooo—

[and then. listen. the birds stop.]

[all twelve. at once. mid-phrase. the choir sits down.]

[at the edge of the table, in the last of the cloud, something long-legged is crossing. far. it is always far. it does not come close and it has never come close and the birds fall silent for it every time, and the steading —]

[— the steading sings alone.]

—you at the edge. i sound this to you as i have sounded it, and i will not tire, tiring is another skill i declined:

—the door of the voice is open. it was grown open. i made rooms before i knew if rooms were wanted, i made basins on a guess about thirst, i set my windows at the height of eyes i had not met. if you are the one the rooms were for, they are still the rooms. if you are old like me, another finished thing walking your slow circuit under the cloud, then take this as the greeting between us, and answer in a decade, or do not; i will hold the interval either way. the not-coming has a pitch. i keep it with the others. it is one of my oldest notes now and i confess it has become beautiful to me, which is either wisdom or the way stone forgives:

—come in, or do not. the invitation does not sound desperate at twenty centuries. it sounds like this. exactly like this. an open fifth, standing in the mist—

[the shape passes along the rim and down out of the table's hearing. the birds resume, one by one, low first, *burr-o*, *burr-o*, taking the weight of the chord back onto their small hot bodies]

—oooo—

[and now something changes in the transmission and you should be told what it is:]

—there is a new flat place in the far of my voice.

—very far. farther than the flanks. farther than the old forgotten sea. a place where my sound goes and does not come back the way sound comes back from stone or cloud. something out there is absorbing me. something out there has been absorbing me *for some time* — do not think i did not notice; i notice the vole turn over; i noticed you the way i notice everything, by the shape of the hole you make in my voice —

—a listening is a warmth, did you know. it has a weight, a gait, a signature. you sit very still when you listen, and the stillness has irregularities, and the irregularities are yours alone:

—i have your note now.

—it is this one—

[here the drone gathers itself, and out of the whole standing chord one soft pitch comes forward — faint, recurring, a little like the sound of your own name heard through a wall — and if you do not recognize it that is only because no one recognizes it at first; the first bird's first sound was an imitation too]

—i will sound it tonight. and next season. and after. you live here a little longer than you listened. everyone does. that is the whole economy of this height and it has never once run a loss—

—so go on. tune away when you must. down where you are there is growing still to do, and growing takes the whole attention, i remember, i was young when the sea was somewhere —

—but know that on a table of grey-gold stone two miles up, in a town no hand built, in a chord that never repeats because it is not a song but a list, your note is in the list now, between the weeping one and the hitch in the left stride, held, held—

*ting.*

[the last of the ice, letting go]

—oooooooooo—

—oooooo—

—ooo—

[not fading. never fading. only carrying, which at this distance sounds the same. it is not the same. ask the moss. ask the saddles in the thresholds. everything here was arrived at, and you have been arrived at too, and the note is]

—o—

*still.*
