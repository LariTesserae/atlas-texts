[regarding the eleven-ring wall: there is a phrase on this band older than every other phrase. it has stood through forty revisions. it is furred grey at the edges of its waveform — annotated, ringed, marked by something that grows only on what has been let stand, and may be the reason it is let stand. the phrase is one word:]

[night. the temperature of the carrier drops. and here — the closest the band comes to music. a chord, very quiet, widening: two degrees, three. paper lobes unfurling in the dark to drink the air. it happens where nothing sees it, every night, the only voluntary movement in the whole transmission, a chord opening by degrees and then, before the grey light comes back, folding to silence again. it will not be transcribed further. some things should stay in the dark where they have chosen to sing.]
