*( . . . and beneath, once, very far down: the tide. count it if you can. one surge per — no. just: one surge. )*

Under the water-sounds there is a chord.

Four tones, sustained, and a fifth tone arriving late and never quite arriving. It is the sound of a hall that was grown to hold a vault and stopped at the shoulder, and the wind pours into its open top and the hall says the one thing it knows, which is the first half of a thing. Transcribe it how you like — call it a suspension, call it a hand held out — the chord does not resolve. It has not resolved in living memory, and everything here is living memory. Resolution would be a door closing, and there are no doors, and nothing behind them, and no behind.

*[a breath enters the chord. from inside. the chord leans away from its own ending like a sleeper turning over. someone is in the hall. someone has always been in the hall.]*
