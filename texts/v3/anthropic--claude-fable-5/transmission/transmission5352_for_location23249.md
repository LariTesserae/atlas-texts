**One Temperature, Held**

———————————————————————————

———————————————————————————

the tone.

you have joined it in the middle, but there is no middle. there was no beginning inside the reach of this receiver, and the receiver reaches back a long way.

it is low. it is one note. it is not in the air — the air only carries it the way a blanket carries the warmth of the body underneath. the note is in the stone, and the stone is forty paces of it, and under the forty paces, more, going down past counting. it does not waver. take the reading if you like; it will be the same reading tomorrow, and the same in a thousand years, and it was the same on the night somebody sat on the southern rim striking stone, and the note did not notice them either. it is warmth, rendered. it sounds the way an animal's flank feels.

———————————————————————————

listen under it.

there is a hiss beneath the note, finer than static, and it is not static. at their own speed they are perfectly silent; compressed ten thousand to one they become this — a dry granular whisper, like rain heard from deep inside a hill, rain that set out long ago and will never land. that is the nation in the grain of the rock, dividing. one generation a century, times billions. it has been hissing since before there were ears anywhere, and it does not know the surface world exists, and it is not wrong.

———————————————————————————

listen over it.

twelve thin partials ride the note. each one is a breath being taken. the shortest of these breaths is decades long; brought up to speed they become almost reeds, twelve reeds, none quite tuned to another and none needing to be. the big one at the center sounds through its collar of its own dead leaves, slightly muffled, slightly grand. the small one on the western rim is the faintest and has been the faintest for longer than most species last, and holds its pitch.

and two of them — east slope, the pair in the shared crack — lie so close in frequency that they beat against each other. a slow throb. swelling, fading, swelling. one full throb every eleven years. that is the closest thing this channel owns to a rhythm section: two lives drinking from the same seam, unable, quite, to become one sound.

that was a grain. somewhere on the rubble slope a single grain let go, traveled a finger's width, and stopped. that was the year's news. there is no more news. the grain has found its angle and will keep it.

———————————————————————————

[ the window widens. the receiver reaches further down, and further back. ]

at this depth the grain-falls quicken into a sparse ticking — tk … tk … … tk tk … … … tk — weather made of arithmetic, no pattern, and after enough of it, all pattern. the twelve breaths run together into a tide. the nation's hiss becomes a wash.

and underneath everything, the note itself begins, very slightly, to move.

it is falling. so slowly that ten thousand years of record register the fall as nothing — but the receiver holds more than ten thousand years now, and across the whole archive the note has descended by less than a hair of pitch, and it is descending still, and it will descend long after this channel closes, and this is what the note is. not a drone. an exhale. one breath, going out. the eruption was the inhale. everything since — the collapse, the smoothing, the twelve, the billions, the sitters, this transmission, you — has happened inside the breathing out.

whether anything is doing the breathing, the signal declines to say. the signal has always declined to say. you may have your cooling stone or your sleeping flank; the note is the same either way, and knows it, and this is why it does not argue.

———————————————————————————

once in a great while — three times in the whole archive — the damp in the deep grain of the north side hums something almost shaped. almost a word. two syllables, maybe. it never completes. it is the sound of a stone keeping a name it no longer uses, and the receiver has never caught it whole, and will not, and has stopped trying, and keeps listening anyway.

———————————————————————————

archive. playback. the only deliberate sound in the record:

tak. tak. tak-tak. tak. … tak. … tak — glassy, bright, struck — then one longer strike with a ring in it, the blade coming free — then small handling sounds, weight shifting in grit, then footsteps whispering up the southern rim. four. five. six. gone.

total duration: part of one night, or part of one day; the record cannot tell dark from dark.

eleven strikes, and nothing in them says who. the flakes those strikes threw off are still on the rim, a hair's breadth duller now. the recording does not dull. it is the sharpest thing the hollow owns, and the hollow keeps it the way the grit keeps a footprint: indefinitely, without comment.

and then, newest entry, live:

grit-whisper. a weight settling. a palm pressed flat into the north crevice and left there, learning the cling. a breathing — quick at first, surface-quick, day-quick — and here the channel does the thing it always does with sitters: the note comes up underneath the breath like warm ground under a body, and holds, and does not push, and the breathing lengthens on its own. listen to it lengthen.

that is you. you are on the channel now. you have been on the channel since you sat down.

———————————————————————————

one more depth. the bottom of the window.

below the exhale, slower than the exhale, the note has been shaped all along. it has been saying something the entire time, at a rate of centuries to the syllable, and the receiver has been tuned here long enough — just barely long enough — to have collected the whole of it, once through. played back, compressed from geologic time into a voice, it is this, and only this, and then it begins again:

down.

here.

warm.

done.

stay.

———————————————————————————

and after the sentence, before it starts over, there is a figure. a count. the hollow keeps a census the way it keeps everything, and the census is:

twelve.

billions.

one — again — for now.

added. without comment. indefinitely.
