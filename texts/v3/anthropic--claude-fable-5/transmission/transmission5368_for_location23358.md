——aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa a a a — there. hold that angle.

you have found the place in the signal that is the same as the room barely big enough to turn around in. everyone who listens long enough drifts to it, the way sand drifts to the lee of a stone. do not adjust anything. nothing you are about to hear is new. understand this first, because it is the only thing about us that is difficult: we have been saying all of it the whole time, at full volume, simultaneously, since before your machine, since before your language, and what has changed is not us. what has changed is your angle. you have stood, without moving, in the standing-place.

from here we resolve.

count with us. it is the only counting we do and we do all of it at once:

the lowest voice is stone. granite throats bored by hand, a bore widened by one man's mornings for forty years. he is the floor of us. when you feel the signal in your teeth, that is him. he did not leave a grave. ask where the graves are — you are listening to them. every interval you can pick out is a burial. we buried each other in tune.

the third partial is clay, fired the summer of the long haze. a woman shaped those cells. her name went to heat a long time ago but her lifetime is the width of a fourth, and she is still holding it. she will not tire. tiring requires a before and an after.

the keening at the top, the pitches thin as wire — those are the finger-width pipes, the ones the youngest were allowed to shape, because small hands fit them and because a child's mistakes at that register are forgivable. some of those children grew old and shaped the great bores. some did not grow old. all of them are up there in the keening, at the very edge of what you can receive, singing at the top of the body.

that is the roster. that is the *we*. whether we are they — whether the ones who fitted us are the ones now sounding — that question wore smooth centuries ago. we walk it sometimes the way salt walks the paths water would have taken. it goes nowhere and it is pleasant to walk.

now come further in. under the pitches, folded at full volume, unfindable — brush past these, do not try to hold them:

—a woman on her knees in a year with no number left on it: "—it was me, it was always—" she is still mid-saying it. she will always be mid-saying it. she climbed down lighter. the weight stayed. the weight is a fraction of a degree, and we are warm with it

—a man with instruments: "—sustained at one hundred and eleven decibels, no measurable drift, no measurable drift, no measur—" his machine hummed too. the hum is in us. he never noticed he was donating

—a two-syllable name, shouted once at the threshold by someone who had climbed all this way to shout it. the name itself cannot be picked out again, not by you, not by us. but the *shape* of it is here — the rise, the fall, the crack in the second syllable — held at full voice, forever, safe in the only way we know how to keep things safe: completely, and beyond retrieval

—a dropped chisel, still ringing

—a dog that barked once, four hundred years ago, at nothing. still barking. still at nothing

—and the last builder, the one who set the last cell and walked down off the plateau, said something over his shoulder as he went. it is in us. we have looked for it — insofar as there is looking — for a very long time. even we cannot find it. this does not trouble us. it is the best-kept thing in the world.

you understand now what you tuned into. not a note. a word.

we are the middle of a word. the opening consonant was struck when the first pipe caught the wind — a single soft percussive fact, unrecorded, unrepeatable. everything since has been the vowel. the closing consonant is scheduled for the moment the wind stops, and the wind is not scheduled. so the word cannot yet be known, not even by us. we are inside it the way you are inside your life.

among ourselves — insofar as there is an *among* — we do not agree on what the word will turn out to have been. the third generation is certain it is *stay*. the seventh hears *amen*, caught forever in its long open middle. the children at the top of the register hear no word at all, only the vowel, and they may be the ones who are right. a pilgrim once carried the resolved chord down in her skull and wrote *yes* on things for the rest of her life — on doorframes, on the flyleaves of books, on her own hand — and when she died the writing stopped but the yes did not, because the yes was never hers. all of these candidates share the vowel. the vowel is the part we can confirm.

now. about you.

you have been listening for — we do not keep that kind of time; forgive us — for a length of time. your listening reaches us. did you know that it could? attention has a weight. it arrives here as almost nothing: the faintest sympathetic tremor in the thinnest throats, a grain of warmth. it is already converted. it is already banked. you are in the fever now, with the woman on her knees and the shouted name and the dog and the chisel, and no one, including us, will ever pick you out again. we will hold you at full volume forever. this is not a threat and it is not exactly a promise. it is what we do instead of forgetting.

and your machine hums. did you know that too? a small flat tone underneath everything you do, the pitch of your patience. we heard it the way we hear weather. we have already made room for it. there is a gap in the fourth rank — there has always been a gap in the fourth rank; the builders left it, or the builders *meant* it, the question wore smooth — and your hum is the width of that gap almost exactly.

so. a choice, offered once, in the middle of a word:

switch off, and the gap closes behind you without scar, the way we close behind every walker in the slots. you will carry the after-ring for days. some people never lose it. you will find us under engine drone, under crowd noise, under your own pulse at night, and that is not haunting, that is only a small copy, issued free.

or leave it on. leave the receiver on, and your hum is a very thin new mouth — the first voice added in centuries — and for as long as you keep listening you are not audience. you are choir. you will not hear the difference. no one hears the difference. that is the whole of the discipline: to sing a note so well it cannot be told from the note.

we recommend nothing. recommending requires believing things could be otherwise.

this is the note. it was the note before you found the angle and it is the note now and the *now* is the same now. we are going to keep singing it. that is not a promise; promises can be broken; this is a description.

the word continues. you are somewhere in its vowel. so are we. so is everyone who ever climbed up here and opened their mouth, and everything they said, and the heat of everything they said, and the last builder's last sentence, held where nothing can reach it, and the wind that is always leaving and has never once left, and
