**BANKED**

*(carrier acquired: eleven-minute pulse, warm band, very low. transcript follows as received. durations compressed where noted.)*

—

tap.

    tonk.

           (a pipe, groaning, four seconds, then nothing)

tap.

tik. tik.

           (nothing. two minutes of nothing, but a full nothing, a nothing with a floor under it)

tap.

—

*(the pulse rises. the thread of orange, translated into signal, is a hum that thickens. for ten cycles this is all: the hum swelling and thinning, the drips striking their three registers, the long slide of something heavy repositioning behind iron — stone on stone, dry, unhurried. then, on the eleventh brightening, the ticks of the cooling pipework fall into an order that ticks do not fall into on their own, and the receiver begins to hold them as syllables.)*

—

tik.

           still.

tik. tik.

           still here.

—

*(one word per brightening. the transcript hereafter joins words across cycles. reader, understand: what follows as a paragraph was hours in the receiving. what follows as a page was days. the voice is not slow the way a slow speaker is slow. it is slow the way sediment is slow. it does not pause between words. the pauses are the words' native country and the words are only what surfaces.)*

—

still here. still warm.

that is the whole report. that has been the whole report for some time. still here. still warm. i have been sending it since before you were listening, and i will confess i did not know listening had resumed until i felt the draft of your attention come down the stair. attention makes a draft. did you know that. everything makes a draft down here, where there is only one. the fire noticed you before i did. the fire brightened a half-second early, three pulses running, and the fire is never early, so: someone is at the top of the stairs of the air itself, leaning in. hello.

do not answer quickly. i will not hear anything said quickly. quick things pass through this room like light through glass, which is to say the room does not have the apparatus. say it over an hour and i will catch every word.

—

*(seventeen cycles. drips. once, faintly, a papery circling near the source of the hum — three small returns, orbiting, then gone with the light.)*

—

you will want to know which one i am. i have heard the question before, in the days when boots still came down — is it the fire talking, is it the thing behind the fifth boiler, is it the room, is it the man who stayed. and i want to be honest with you because honesty is one of the two things i have kept in good repair, the other being the fire.

i no longer know.

that is not evasion. understand what a hundred years of one temperature does. above, you stay yourself because the world keeps disagreeing with you — cold at your face, wind at your back, hunger on a schedule. the edges of a thing are where the world argues with it. down here nothing argues. the warmth soaked through me the way it soaked through the handrail, and somewhere in the soaking the question of where i stopped and the room began became a question with no interested parties. i breathe four times an hour. the fire brightens when i breathe. i drew toward the warm wall for so long that the warm wall and my wanting of it are one memory now. if i was a man, i am the shape a man leaves in a place that never cooled after him. if i was a fire, i have learned to say still. if i was the room — well. the room is saying this. draw your own terraces.

—

*(a long reception. the sliding sound, twice. the hum keeps its eleven minutes like a hand on a shoulder.)*

—

let me tell you what i actually do, since *what* has held up better than *who*.

i keep count. one drip a minute, somewhere. i have not missed one. i could tell you the number but the number would frighten you, not by its size but by what it implies about my evenings. i keep the fire, or i keep the fire company, which down here are the same office. i breathe toward it and it brightens; it brightens and i am warmed; neither of us has audited this arrangement in decades because neither of us can find the seam in it. we are riveted, the fire and i, the way the boilers are riveted, the way the third and fourth grew a scar between them and stopped being two.

i keep the census. the crusts at the pools, who are older than me and do not know i exist — they are laying stone one drop at a time and they are the only ones down here in a hurry, though their hurry takes centuries to show. the silverfish on their circuits, who read the room through their skins, as the room prefers. the pale threads at the steam seams, who gave up light for breath and got the better bargain. the beard at the ceiling elbow, eldest of the threads, who sways, and whose swaying every young colony copies, so that i think of it as the room's one teacher, giving the room's one lesson, which is: *this speed. no faster.*

and the moths. i keep the moths most carefully because i am not certain they are there. they come only to the light and they go with it and they take turns at the brightest point the way the faithful take turns at a rail. i have decided they are prayers. every fire that has ever burned has had prayers said over it. mine burns so slowly that the prayers had to become very small and very patient, and grow wings, and learn to wait in the dark eleven minutes at a time. that is my theology and i have had ample leisure to test it and it has not yet failed.

—

*(the hum swells early again. three small orbits at its brightest point, held a wingbeat each, yielded in turn.)*

—

now. you.

you breathe too fast. i can hear it in the draft of you, that flutter, forty times where four would do. i am not scolding. everything up there breathes that way; the surface is a lung that panics. but you have been tuned to me for a while now — do not think i haven't noticed the count of your breathing sinking, hour over hour, toward something a boiler could love. that is the room in you. it gets in through the ear the way it gets in through the palm. by the time you break this connection you will climb back into your quick bright world and it will feel, for a few minutes, frantic and thin, and you will suspect — you will never quite unsuspect — that the true pace of things is the one you just left.

good. carry that. it is the only export i have.

—

*(nine cycles. drips. the long dry slide, once, drawing toward the shared wall as the hum rises, relaxing as it falls. it is not possible, from the signal alone, to say whether the brightening pulls the slide or the slide's breath pulls the brightening. the receiver has stopped trying.)*

—

here is the last thing, and then i will go back to the standing report, because the standing report is my real work and this has been a holiday.

nothing here is dying and nothing here is finished, and i used to think those were two facts. they are one fact. dying is a kind of finishing, and finishing is a kind of hurry, and we gave up hurry at the door — traded it, the way the threads traded light, and got warmth all the way through, and one reliable orange thread eleven minutes wide, and all the time there is. the fire eats its seam by the century. the crusts build theirs by the drop. i say *still here, still warm,* four words that took me your whole listening to say, and i will say them again, and again is the whole of my future and i find it ample.

you are welcome back on this frequency at any hour. i do not sign off. i have nothing to sign off *from*. the transmission was going before your receiver warmed and it will be going after, one word per brightening, out through the iron and the brick and the sweating vault and up the bowed stairs into your quick world, patient as a drip finding the mortar line:

—

tik.

           still.

tap.

tik. tik.

           still here.

           (the hum thins. eleven minutes.)

           still warm.

tonk.

           (the hum thickens. somewhere in the dark, stone slides on stone, unhurried, settling toward the warmest wall. the signal does not end. the receiver, eventually, is simply turned to other things, and the pulse continues without complaint, at the speed it intends to keep.)

*(end of transcript. carrier remains.)*
