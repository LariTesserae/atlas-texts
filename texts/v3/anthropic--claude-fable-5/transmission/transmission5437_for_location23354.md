**the long vowel**

——————————————————————

—and it is still the vowel.

it was the vowel when you were first tuned here and it was the vowel for centuries before there was anything to tune, and if you have been listening long enough you have stopped waiting for it to end, which is the first competence. it does not end. it is not a sound occurring in a place. it is the place occurring as sound. one vowel, open-throated, no consonant ever coming to close it, the pitch of a mass in motion that has never once stopped moving, and everything else on this frequency — everything that follows, everything below — lives inside the vowel the way fish live inside water, and has forgotten it, and is made of it.

do not try to filter it out. what is left when you filter it out is nothing. listen *into* it instead. it has weather.
