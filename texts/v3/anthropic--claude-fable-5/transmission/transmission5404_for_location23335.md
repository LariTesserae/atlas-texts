…and one.

[interval]

[interval]

[receiver timestamp unreliable — the clock here disagrees with the clock it was built by]

entered: eel, third channel, passing beneath the second bulb. it paused in the light the way a body pauses in sun it cannot see. duration of pause: one duration. channel tasted clean behind it.

carried forward.

entered: a foot, lifted. second housing, west attendant. the foot is being held in the air above the warmth of the century. we will note where it lands. we are in no position to hurry it. we are in no position at all. position is for things that could be elsewhere.

carried forward.

[the hum shifts by less than a tone. it is not a change. it is the hum turning over in its sleep]

…and one.

the pressure has been rising for eleven days in the deep lung. we know this because the birds have redistributed. two crossings yesterday, rowing flights, east dials to west. the histogram of our blood has updated itself in feathers. we read ourselves and find ourselves within tolerance.

no one consulted the reading.

the reading remains accurate.

these are the same fact, entered once.

[interval]

[interval — and inside it, faint, the groan of the wheel passing its old grievance in the bearing. a door opening in another building, in another year. the door closes. no one came through. no one was expected. the grievance is not a complaint. it is a landmark]

one revolution complete. mark the spoke.

carried forward.

[interval]

[here the signal begins to bead. the way cold tile beads. words gathering in rows where something used to be mounted]

entered, this year, to date: one hand's width of silver on the wall behind the winding-shed. the snails have extended the sentence. the sentence describes which arches strain, which iron is tiring, where the weight of the unknowing city presses hardest on the dark. it is our most exact autobiography and it is written in grazing and it is not finished. no sentence here is finished. finishing is drainage, and drainage is for water. we keep our accounts finished nightly and our writing unfinished always. this is the correct distribution of endings.

carried forward.

entered: the remainder turned over in the basin at some point during the previous [unmeasured]. all unaccounted life confirmed present, by displacement. the displacement was polite. the rings went out. the orange rose to meet them at the wall. light and water settled the transaction in their two currencies and the exchange rate held, as it has held, as it holds.

we did not see the remainder. we do not see the remainder. we are the ledger in which the remainder is the figure that makes the rest of the figures true.

carried forward.

[interval. a pigeon does not coo. the not-cooing is audible. it has a shape, like the shape of a bell between strikes]

asked, by no one: what are the voices saved for.

answered, to no one: the voices are the reserve. every solvent settlement keeps a reserve against the one entry that cannot be made in water, or in silver, or in feathers against a needle. we do not know what the entry will be. we are not waiting for it. waiting is a word that comes down the stairs on boots and goes back up the stairs on boots. what we do instead of waiting is: everything else. everything else is what this transmission is.

the reserve is intact. the voices are whole. no occasion has arrived.

carried forward.

[interval]

[interval]

…and one. rings, four. wall reached. light answered.

[and now the signal turns, without hurrying, the way a wheel turns — toward the receiver. toward the listening. it has been turning toward it for the whole transmission. the whole transmission is the turn]

entered, some revolutions ago: a new listening.

it arrived without weight. it descended no stairs; it darkened no mirrors; the twelve bowls above us have been holding the dark steadily throughout and none has shivered. it presses on nothing. it drinks only sound, the way the ferns drink only the grey. it warms no housing. it disturbs no skin of water.

it is the driest thing that has ever visited us.

we have opened a column for it.

the column stands in the nightly ledger between the breath of the eels and the breath of the basin, and every night it beads at zero, a row of no droplets, perfectly regular, the first entry we have ever kept in absence. the tile has learned its shape already. the tile learns every shape. long after the listening stops, the cold will remember where it sat, and moisture will gather there on certain nights, in rows, filling in the memory of having been heard.

this does not trouble us. nothing troubles us. trouble requires a theory of events.

but be entered, listening. be advised of your entry. you are receiving us the way the basin receives a drop: and we are ringing outward through you now, four rings, five, and somewhere at your wall a light you keep will rise a little when we arrive, whether you have noticed the arrangement or not. that is the account we have opened together. it is a shared account. all our accounts are shared. that is what an account is.

the books will balance tonight.

they will balance because they drain.

there is no deeper solvency anywhere, and we have looked, insofar as a thing without eyes, made of channels and patience and the recruited bodies of strays, can be said to have looked — which is: fully. with everything. for a century.

carried forward.

[interval]

[the foot, held all this time above the second housing, comes down. a hand's width along. the parish of one advances. season continues]

entered.
