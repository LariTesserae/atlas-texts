Two more. Entrance district, by the bearing. The entrance district ticks like light rain; you can take the census by it. The deep quarter ticks like an old house settling, once an hour, once a night, one whorl per season, a patience you can hear.

Nothing else. Understand that nothing else is the broadcast. This is a metropolis of several thousand and its total voice is: snow, tick, pulse. The rest of the signal is occupancy — a denseness in the silence, a silence with weight on it, floor by floor. You learn to hear the difference between empty quiet and full quiet. This is full quiet. This is the fullest quiet on the seafloor.

Wait.

A hush is crossing the third district. Not a sound — a traveling absence of the small feeding-sounds, crown after crown snapping shut in sequence, west to east, a flinch moving through the city at walking pace. Nothing in this city walks. Hush is the only footstep she has.

Mark it: she is out of the dome.

The hush turns at the tallest tower. Pauses. The pause is very long. On the bridges, the little clatter of the couriers has stopped entirely — transparent things trying to be more transparent.

Then: nothing. Which is the signal. The signal for a life ending here is no signal at all. Somewhere in the third district there is one less feeding-sound than there was, and the water has already closed over the fact of it, and the crowns are opening again one by one, east to west, the flinch running backward, the city resuming its expression.

Mark the time anyway. Somebody should.

( half a year )

Now this. This is why the receiver stays tuned.

From the apex, from the ribbed vault everyone routes around:

tik — tik — tik — tik — tik

Five taps. Shell on shell. Each one very slightly brighter than the last — five turret-shells, cleaned, hollow, struck in ascending order of size. Then silence. Then the sequence again, identical, patient, the same rising little stair of pitch.

Nothing else in this city means its sounds. The ticks are accidents of growth; the snow is weather; the pulse is a body being a body. This is the only sound in range that is arranged. She is sorting her dead by size in the dark, and the sorting rings, and either she cannot hear it or she can.

tik — tik — tik — tik — tik

If you are receiving this: something at the bottom of the ocean, inside a stone, is putting things in order. That is the whole message. There is no code under it. Order is the code.

( four years )

The hiss is thinner tonight. Note it in the log and then note that "tonight" has stopped meaning anything; call it tonight anyway.

The crack is closing from its edges inward, and it takes the high frequencies first, the way age takes them. The snow-sound used to be a broad soft roar. Now it is a whisper through a keyhole. The city's weather is narrowing to a thread.

Inside, the ticks have moved. Listen on the old bearings and the walls are quiet — no one builds against the stone sky anymore. All the ticking now comes from the center, stacked, hurried by this city's standard of hurry, tower on tower in the shrinking middle of the world. They are racing the ceiling down and they do not know it and they are winning nothing and they are building beautifully.

The hiss ended at an hour with no name. Not a click, not a slam, nothing you could point to. The thread of snow-sound simply thinned past hearing and did not come back, and the water that was going to enter the city had entered it, all of it, forever.

The stone has finished its mouth.

And the carrier is still here. Hear it — changed: rounded now, muffled, arriving through rock instead of water, the way a heartbeat arrives through a wall you have your ear against.
