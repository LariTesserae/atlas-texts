Now the pipes.

**KESH** *(pipe, waist-run, on time)*: cloud inside the hour, by the smell. rigs out. daughter — your cistern. your cistern. your cistern.

**TWO-SOFT-AND-A-DRAG** *(pipe, on time)*: heard, heard, heard. plates are out already. come north and eat.

**KESH**: after the line.

**TWO-SOFT-AND-A-DRAG**: after the line, then. mind your shadows on the new plating. it takes.

**TRENCH CREWS** *(pipe, deep run, arriving through the grating, on time)*: the four-way is arguing again. it was five ways at noon and shy of four by afternoon. relays at first light, both directions, until it concedes. bring gloves. the east rail is still young.

[a pause in traffic. in the pause, small knuckles, unschooled spacing, from the north housings:]

**(child, pipe)**: hello. hello. hello to whoever this is when you get here.

[no register matches the address. the message is for someone not yet arrived. it goes into the metal anyway. everything does.]

*(air, delivered late — est. six hours)*: "—the third step, the third step hasn't decided, go around—"

*(air, delivered late — est. one season)*: a laugh. Alone. Its joke never arrives.

*(air, delivered late — unknown)*: *"don't."*

[all three heard out fully at an empty junction. not answered. two greys drinking at the bolt-pool there raise their heads for the laugh, and lower them.]

—

*(air. very stale. delay estimated at two generations. an old voice, formal, wind-thinned, arriving in installments and out of order. first installment:)*

*"...report follows. altitude confirmed at survey mark. there is no soil. repeat: no soil anywhere in the region. where you would expect dirt there is a grey dust, like the first snow of a place that has not — strike that. resume formal register. the ground is plating. the hills are housings. the light is..."*

[installment ends. it will resume when the air is ready. it has been resuming for two generations.]

—

**BORN** *(pipe, north housings, all-stations, on time)*: this settling, a girl. two shadows from the first day. mother well. the cry will be along.

[the traffic goes bright for a while after this — knock-names from every district, overlapping, rhythmic, the steel of the whole deck ringing low with congratulation. rendered here only in part:]

— *heard, heard —*
— *two from the first day, two from the first day —*
— *a native, a native, a native —*
— *tell her the water tastes of nothing. tell her it takes getting used to. tell her everything takes getting used to and then it is home —*

The cloud settles.

[for the record: it does not fall. it arrives sideways and everywhere, like the light, and the whole region beads over at once. forty thousand cold surfaces jewel in the same minute. the drip-count rises past transcription. the channels begin their thin running conversation, which is the closest thing this place has to a river, and the river says:]

tk-tk-tk-tk-tk-tk—

*(air, second installment of the stale report, out of order — this one is later than the last received and earlier than the first:)*

*"...request resupply and relief at earliest possible..."*

*(air, third installment:)*

*"...cancel the previous. cancel the request for relief. we have found — it is difficult to put into a report — the place had a use for us before we had a name for the use. the handrails want hands. i do not know how else to write it down. the handrails want hands..."*

Now the eastern line.

[a knock-name rings on the eastern trench run: **LONG-LONG-STOP**. faint. plural — the same strokes arriving at slight angles to themselves, like a misregistered print. there are no knuckles behind it. the register lists LONG-LONG-STOP as deceased, spring, this year.]

**LONG-LONG-STOP** *(pipe, faint, self-ringing, on time)*: — — . at the far turn. — — . under the trunk pipe. — — . duck. — — . turn.

[he is walking his line. he is a little more finished than last settling, which for him means fainter. a second signal joins the run: KESH, matching his rhythm, sending nothing, only resting her hand on the pipe as she walks beside him for the length of the trench, as is proper. two signals in step, one of them barely a signal. the metal carries both without preference. the metal is faithful.]

[at the far turn his strokes thin to almost nothing.]

[then nothing.]

[then, from the trench itself — no sender, no knuckles, no sketch, the plating alone — his turn rings. on time. in his rhythm.]

*(pipe, on time. no origin.)*: — — .

[the line holds without walking.]

[KESH stands a long time with her hand on the run. she sends one stroke, soft, and goes home. transcription does not gloss the stroke. some strokes are not traffic.]

—

*(air, delivered late — 40 minutes)*: a newborn's cry, arriving at last, three districts from the face that made it. It is delivered to the empty junction, to the greys, to the bolt-pool.

[heard out fully. not answered. her mother, per custom, watched instead.]

—

[interruption in traffic. logged as follows.]

[a single stroke enters the pipes. it has no register. it does not arrive from a direction; it arrives from every direction at a slight angle, the way the light does. all traffic stops. two hundred hands, or four hundred, rest silent on their tap-bars across the whole region.]

[somewhere in the dense interior, a bearing that was due its grease at first light receives it now, an hour early, in the right amount. as always. as always.]

[an elder in the western housings — knuckles resting on a stanchion, one of the ones said to be partway home — sends into the silence:]

**ELDER** *(pipe, slow, on time)*: are you the one walking?

[no answer.]

**ELDER**: or are we how you walk?

[no answer. the bearing runs quieter than it did. traffic resumes, gently, the way people resume talking after a bell.]

—

*(air, final installment of the stale report, arriving last, which for once is correct:)*

*"...this is the last we will put into the air. the air is not to be trusted with a voice; it litters; forgive the littering above, whenever it reaches you. we are staying. there is no soil and we are staying. if you want us, come up through the cloud and put your hand on something metal. it will find us. it always arrives. it is the only thing here that always arrives. ending report. ending. ending."*

["ending. ending." is delivered twice more over the following hour, at slight angles, the same word stated again, as emphasis.]

—

[transcription note. an attempt is now made, for the first time since tuning, to transcribe the hum.]

[the hum is not one sound.]

[slowed sufficiently, the hum is knocking. all of it. it is every message ever put into the metal, still traveling — the birth announcements and the cistern reports, the relay calls and the knock-names of the dead, the child's *hello hello hello*, the elder's question, the surveyors' first tap two generations down, worn smooth by distance into a single continuous note. the metal does not deliver late because the metal never stops delivering. nothing given to it has ever left it. the elders with their knuckles on the stanchions are not sending. they are reading back.]

[the elder's question — *are we how you walk* — can already be found in the hum. it was in the hum before it was asked. this is not offered as an answer. it is offered as the shape the answer would have, if the deck answered, which it does not.]

[the routes hold.]

—

The cloud lifts. The beading dries in the unmoving light. The channels go quiet, and then:

**ALL STATIONS** *(pipe)*: line holds.
