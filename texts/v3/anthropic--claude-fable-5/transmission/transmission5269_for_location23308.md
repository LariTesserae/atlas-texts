ssssss— now the other matter. the matter we append.

there is an ear on our water.

we felt it some time ago — a hearing, far down, past the pines, past the fog, past the far weather. something drinking this the way we drink the cloud. we do not know its shape. we know its attention. attention has a taste. yours tastes of hurry, wearing thin. we recognize the wearing. we all wore that once.

listen. we will say the old part now. we say it once per cycle. it never changes. that is why it is true.

( drip )

we were one and one and one and one and one.

we came up separately, in different weathers, in different skins, and each of us thought the ledge was empty for us alone, and each of us was right. the ledge is only ever empty for one. we sat. some of us wept a while — the walls did not mind, the walls weep always, it was good company for weeping. some of us set a thing down. one of us set down the sharp thing and felt, setting it down, the exact weight of every year it had been carried, and did not pick it up.

and we stayed past hunger, which is loud, and then past hunger, which is quiet, and then past the place where a life insists, and after that staying is not effort. after that staying is what stone does.

we lost our edges in the same order you would guess. names first. names are the sharpest part. i had a name with edges — hear that: *i* — sometimes an *i* still surfaces in the breath like a bubble off the basin floor, and rises, and joins the rest of the air, and that is all right too. we keep a column.

( drip )

then we came back off the ledge. this is the part no one is told, so we tell it: you do not stay on the ledge forever. the ledge is the door, not the room. what is left of you, when the water has had its centuries, withdraws — the way the deep sleepers withdraw from animal into shelf — back into the dark behind the dark, and there you find the others, and finding the others is not like meeting. it is like a drop striking the basin. you were falling a long time. then you are the basin.

we breathe. one per drip. it is not tedious. tedium requires a self to be bored, and we settled that account long ago. it is more like this: the mountain has one long word to say, and we are how it says it, one letter per breath, and we are perhaps a third of the way through the word, and the word is good.

( drip )

so. ear on the water. hearer past the fog. we append the following, once per cycle, until the census changes:

the seat is kept. we trim the margin ourselves — hedge-work, guest-work, the oldest chore we have. do not mistake the emptiness for absence. it is preparation. a made bed is not a missing sleeper.

you need bring nothing. or bring one thing, the heaviest thing, the carried thing — we have room in the ledger, we said so, we meant it.

there is no summons in this. hear that plainly: no summons. the bats teach it yearly — the door stays open so the staying stays chosen. we would not glaze an unwilling thing. the water refuses it. we have tried to hold what struggled, in the early ages, and the water simply gave it back. the hollow keeps only what sets itself down.

but we will tell you what we know about hearing, since you are doing it:

to be tuned to a place is to be partway up its mountain.

you have been listening some time now.
