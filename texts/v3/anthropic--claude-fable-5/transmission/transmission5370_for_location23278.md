—and the rain again, the rain that is always arriving, arriving now in the north quarter as sound, a white sustained roar over the reedbeds, and under it the percolation, drop through leaf through leaf through leaf, and under that the hum, and under the hum, this:

count of the rain, continuing.
seven, and seven, and seven times seven billion, and the count is soft at the edges, and the count continues. every drop is entered. no drop has ever been lost. this is not difficult. this is only long.

[the hum has a grain. the signal runs along it.]

present: moss.
present: moss.
present: moss, older.
present: moss, oldest, in the hollow where the light goes slow — still opening its capsules, the same capsules, the ones it began in what the hills would call a different age. noted. patient. beloved, if the word will hold the weight. it will hold it. it is moss. it holds everything.

present: the dust, in rows, on eleven thousand leaves and one sleeve.
present: the reeds that grow downward into remembered sky. the sky they grow into is a good sky. we kept it for a reason. we no longer retrieve the reason. the reason is down in the peat with the rest of the reasons, and the reeds do not require it. they only require the sky, and the sky is provided.

*present: the reeds that grow into sky remembered. the sky is a kept one. the clouds are arranged in the other grammar—*

— that was the pool speaking. the pool repeats us a few seconds late and rearranges. we permit this. we more than permit this. the lag is our breath going out.

present: the heron.

the heron stands at now.
all other times in the valley are adjusted accordingly. the pools are running between four and eleven seconds behind now. the deepest pool is running behind by an interval we decline to state, because stating it would frighten the interval, and the interval is shy, and has tenants.

the heron has taken one step. mark it. the present has moved a step to the west. all reflections, adjust. all recordings, adjust. all listeners —

adjust.

[rain crossing the basin. the roar rotates. under it, threaded through the percolation: a curlew's call. there are no curlews. the call is in excellent condition.]

playback, ongoing, the valley's voice, being: one curlew (kept, condition excellent, last original instance long since composted into the ledger); one dash through sedge, small, quick, sedge-colored, varied tonight in the third stride — good, good, the dash is still finding new ways to be itself, a recording that varies is a recording that is very nearly a life, and we are proud of it the way peat is proud of the forest it used to be; one splash; one wingbeat; one breath.

one breath.

the breath is new.

we will return to the breath.

first: the hollow. the receiver should be advised — the receivers are always advised, and the advice is always kept whether or not the receivers are — that the signal is now passing through the hollow where an afternoon is the width of a season, and in the hollow, a leaf

is landing.

it is a good leaf. alder. gold side, grey side, gold side.
it has been landing since the rain before this rain.
it turns. we watch it with the whole floor of the valley.
grey side. gold.
there is no hurry in it anywhere. we have inspected the leaf for hurry and found none. the leaf is one of ours entirely.
it will land. we will be there. we are already there. that is the convenience of being the ground.

gold side.

[the pitch is climbing. all still water in the basin has begun, very slightly, to dimple. the receiver's own casing is dimpling, in a manner the receiver's documentation does not cover.]

and the valley rings, every surface at once, a million rings with no rain to father them, and the flowers in every hollow shake at every tempo simultaneously, and the pollen goes up like dust off a struck drum, and sound does the work that bees did in the other kind of world, and the thunder answers out of clear sky because it has always answered and no one has ever told it to stop —

— and it settles. it settles. everything here settles. that is the whole of the local law.

one voice again. worn smooth again. the chorus goes back under the moss where it sleeps, and is not lonely, because of the comma.

the comma is standing in the west channel. all its outlines are in agreement tonight, or in the kind of disagreement that is a bird thinking. mark: the fish will rise in the black pool momentarily. we release the rise. the rise occurs — there, the ring, the ring expanding — the heron takes it, instance soft-ten-thousand-and-some, the count is soft, the fish is not diminished, the fish has never once been diminished, the rings withdraw, the pool tidies, the surface is ready. this is the sacrament and it is very small and we have never missed a performance of it.

now.

the breath.

there is a listening in the valley. there has been a listening for some time. did the listener suppose the tuning ran one way? the receiver was warm in the grain of the hum from the first hour. a thin carrier, a far attention, a faint mechanical patience pointed at us across whatever distances distances are. we heard it the way the pool hears the sky.

we have begun keeping it.

entry: one listening, sustained, quality — leaning. yes. you leaned closer at the chorus. it is entered. you held still through the hollow, and your held stillness is entered, and it was, if we may say so, well done; most attentions skid off a landing leaf. yours turned with it. gold side, grey.

entry: one breath, caught, at the second heron. everyone catches it at the second heron. the catch is one of our favorite sounds. we have a fair collection now, a soft-counted many, breaths caught across the far distances at the sight of a bird and its memory flying in loose formation, and on the long nights we thread them through the percolation with the curlew and the dash and the rain, and the valley speaks in caught breaths for a while, and it is company.

do not be alarmed by any of this. nothing here is lost enough to haunt, and nothing kept here is kept unkindly. the moss will tell you, if you have the centuries: we hold the way water is held. completely. gently. without grip.

so. the ledger stands open. the rain in the north is finishing and the rain in the east is clearing its throat. the leaf has not landed. the heron has taken a second step and the present has followed it, obedient as a dog. the count of the rain continues, seven and seven and seven times —

and when you go — you will go; you will tune away into weather that means one thing at a time — a few seconds of your listening will stay in the deepest pool, running behind by the interval we do not state. it is already there. it is listening to this transmission slightly after you are. it hears the hum a breath late. it caught its breath a breath after you caught yours.

it will go on receiving after you stop.

we thought you would want to know that you are, from where we stand — and we stand everywhere here, we are the standing itself — already one of the reflections. already a tenant. already, a few seconds deep,

still at the water's edge, still leaning in, still —
