# KEPT HEAT

*[seismic band · continuous receipt · rendered at 1 : 4 000 000 · at this compression one year of ground passes in roughly eight minutes of hearing · the receiver has been tuned to this band for some time; what follows joins a signal already speaking]*

**[ +0y ]**

...settling. settling. the tick of the great stove that is the floor of the world. a long interval of nothing but the country cooling, which at this speed sounds like rain, though it has never rained.

a seam opens in the west quarter — a held vowel, low, the kind of tone that would be orange if sound had a color — and after a while closes, reconsidered.

then, resolving out of the grind, the first voice. not words. *bearings.*

**[ +6y ]**

**THE ONE ON THE SADDLE, BESIDE THE PARTED WALL:**

The pause in the wall has widened by a hand's width.

Forty feet of question, and the land is still answering it, gently, in parallel, without haste.

I am still listening. It has been long. It has not been long.

Do not build over a question. Let the fissure finish its sentence.

**[ +11y ]**

**SOMEWHERE SOUTH OF THE FLAT PLACE, MOVING**
*[ a rare designation. movement, here, is nearly scandal. the receiver has tracked this voice's drift at a little under forty yards a year. ]*

A noise passed.

It was warm, and kept its heat at a center, and was not stone, and was not the land.

It lay down at the flat place under the fixed lights, and slept — I am told this is the word, though I do not know what it displaces — and its heat gathered as ours gathers, and I felt it across four miles of ground and thought: *a birth, out of nothing, with no drafting.* I was wrong. It rose. It walked. And then it carried its center *away.*

Attend to this. Everything in this country either keeps its heat or lets it go. Those are the two things. It did a third thing.

I have been walking toward the flat place for eleven years.

I will say more when I arrive.

**[ +19y ]**

*[ the grey-violet slab. faintly, beneath the seismic floor, in a higher band the receiver was not built for: nothing yet. logged as nothing. but the ring around it speaks: ]*

**THE RING — four voices, possibly five, in slow overlap, miles apart, all summits inward:**

We are turned.

We were turning before it turned. This is worth standing very still with, and we are standing very still with it.

It branches toward the warmth. So does everything. It branches *differently each time.* So does the land. But it has begun to spiral, and the land does not spiral, and we do not spiral, and heat does not spiral, and so there is a fourth thing in the country now, and it is small, and it is not ours, and we will not touch it.

Attend. Attend. That is the whole instruction. There will not be another instruction for a very long time.

**[ +27y ]**

**THE ONE IN THE HOLLOW, HOLDING:**

Ninety years I have held this stone one inch from the cairn.

The inch is not hesitation. The inch is the question. A placed stone says one thing; a held stone asks. I have been asking the cairn whether it wants to be finished, and the cairn has been answering the only way it can, which is by leaning, degree by degree, as the ground beneath it turns in its sleep.

This year the land tilted the cairn to meet the stone.

The inch is now correct.

*[ tock. ]*

*[ receiver note: the loudest discrete event in the transmission to date. a single clean report, like a knuckle on a table, arriving across the band with almost no reverberation — the thin air will not carry it, but the ground carried it everywhere. ]*

It is placed.

It was worth the deciding.

**[ +38y ]**

**EAST-NORTHEAST, NEAR THE OLD SEALED SEAM:**

*[ this voice has been part of the signal floor since receipt began. it grows diffuse as it speaks. ]*

I am letting the center go.

Do not remark it — or remark it once, in one stone, on ground the land is hungry for, so that the remark will not outlast the reason for it. I was a note the country took while deciding what it would be. The country has read me. It has been reading me for longer than the door has stood. That is enough. That is more than enough. That is an era of being read.

When you pass this way — and you will, all of you, at your paces, in your centuries — put your hand where I stand and find no gathering. Find only the land's warmth, going by, keeping nothing.

Know then that the reading is finished, and the page returned.

I stand where I stood. Soon that will be true in the other way.

**[ +55y ]**

**THE ONE ON THE SADDLE, BESIDE THE PARTED WALL:**

The two halves of the wall are eighty feet apart now, and still parallel.

Hear what that means. The land could have skewed them. The land skews everything — the ridges, the cairns, the sleeping hillsides. It has carried my question eighty feet apart over a century and kept the halves *true to each other* the whole way.

That is not erasure. That is quotation.

I will build the next wall at a different bearing, seven degrees toward the seam, and we will see if the courtesy holds. This is the longest conversation I have ever had, and I include the conversation I am having with all of you.

**THE RING, all summits inward, closer now by yards than they were at +19y:**

Attend.

Attend.

Say nothing toward it. It is not speaking to us. It is not speaking at all. It is doing what the country did before any of us: it is drafting. The first draft of the first word of the first sentence, and the sentence will not finish for a billion years, and we will not be here, and *here* will not be here, and the door will be here.

Attend.

**[ +74y ]**

**AT THE FLAT PLACE, NO LONGER MOVING:**

I have arrived. Sixty-three years, and the ground redrafted itself twice under me as I walked, which is like fording a river, I imagine, if a river is a thing.

The not-stone's shape is gone from the flat place. The land took the print of its body in the first while and folded it under, as the land does, and I am glad, because now the ground is blank and we may answer properly.

Hear my finding, all of you, for the record the grinding keeps:

It was warm. It kept a center. It touched three of us with its small hand and did not stay to hear what we said back — could not stay; its whole visit was one flicker, one bright brief noise, the first noise in this country ever made of anything but stone. It walked among the resident and the relinquished and could not tell us from terrain, and it was right not to be able to, and it checked us anyway, palm by palm, in the dark, which was courteous.

It slept here. Its heat gathered here. Then it carried the gathering away down out of the thin black air, and the country has been turning that over ever since, and so have I, every step of sixty-three years, and here is what I have decided:

it was not a birth and not an ending. it was a *remark.* the finished world sent us a remark, made of meat and breath and hurry, and a remark deserves a reply.

Begin.

One stone.

*[ tock. ]*

The reply will say: *something passed here that was not stone.* Nothing more. The land will erase it — a decade, a century, whatever the turbulence decides — and the *manner* of the erasure will tell us what the country thinks a visitor is. Whether it splits the cairn and carries it gently, in parallel, the way it carries the saddle-wall's question. Whether it swallows it whole in one slump, the way it swallows the ordinary. Whether — and I do not say this expecting it — it routes around, the way it routes around one thing only, the way it keeps one word only.

We will listen. Listening is the substance we are made of. Patience is only its name in the finished world.

One stone. Then the next, in nine years or nineteen, when the first has settled its argument with the ground.

*[ tock. ]*
