**THE ECHO GOING HOME**

*(open channel — the bowl — joined already in progress, as everything here is)*

---

. . . oooooooooooohm . . . ooooooooooohm . . . 

[the drone. two dozen pipes and one lung. it has been sounding since before this channel opened and it will be sounding after. under it, always, the rattle: — *tk-tkka . . . tkka-tk . . . tk* — seedpods against stalks, a thousand small dry hands applauding nothing]

[broadband hiss across all frequencies. the hiss has a grain to it. the grain is alive. one billion bodies the size of poppy seeds turning together when the wind shifts — the hiss *banks*, the way a flock banks, and for a moment the whole channel leans left]

*ffffffffhhhhh — sssshhh — ffffffhhh —*

. . . oooooooohm . . .

and above the drone, riding it, the fifth. the fifth off the eastern rim. still there. always there.

*oooooooo—eeeeeeeee.*
*oooooooo—eeeeeeeee.*

D and A, or near enough. centuries of it. the receiver's crystal has begun to sympathize; there is a faint ring in the housing now that persists even when the gain is zeroed. noted. not corrected.

---

[mid-afternoon by the light-values. bronze. directionless. warm rock ticking as it takes the sun]

sounds of work at the western stalks, close-in, human-scale, small against the roar:

*scritch . . . scritch . . . scrik.* the diagonal cut. a pause. a soft patting sound — resin being coaxed, a palm laid flat against a warm segmented barrel the girth of a cistern. and threaded through the work, almost below the channel floor, a person humming.

humming the fifth.

*mm—mmm. mm—mmm.*

they do not seem to know they are doing it. no tapper ever seems to know they are doing it.

a second voice, older, farther off, two words in their language. the receiver's dictionary is one entry long, and this is the entry:

***"—— the echo going home ——"***

said in the tone of *hurry up.* said in the tone of *it's getting late.* footsteps on tufa, dust-veils being shaken out, the flap and rattle of the hide on the rim taking the wind. the knife sounds stop. the humming, going up the rim path, continues for a while.

*mm—mmm. mm—mmm . . . mm—*

gone over the lip.

the fifth continues without them.

*oooooooo—eeeeeeeee.*

---

[light falling. the haze losing its bronze. the fronds beginning to show — first as a change in the hiss, actually, before any change in the light: the crackle of the static hairs sharpens as the air cools, *tzzk . . . tzk-tzk . . . tzzzzk*, and only afterward comes the glow, grey-green, a dim constellation switching itself on point by point]

and now the roster.

the shouters wake. they come to the launch-holes and they call themselves out one at a time, the way they do every dusk, each voice its own, and the receiver has been tuned here long enough to know the roster the way the colony knows it:

*WHEET-wheet-doo* — (the loud one, eastern tube, third hole)
*ti-ti-ti-ti-REEE* — (the fast one)
*doo . . . doo . . . WHEETdoo* — (the one that hesitates)
*REEEE-tk-tk-tk-fall* — (the one with the stutter and the fall)

and then a silence in the sequence where the fifth voice goes.

the fifth voice is:

*wheet-wheet-wheet — tk-tk — doooooooo.*

three rising. a stutter. a fall.

it does not come.

[eleven seconds. in this bowl, eleven seconds without an expected sound is an event. the drone itself seems to lean toward the gap]

then, from the third hole of the eastern tube — not the dead one's hole — the loud one sounds it. carefully. slightly wrong on the stutter:

*wheet-wheet-wheet — tk — tk . . . doooooooo.*

and from the next hole down, better:

*wheet-wheet-wheet — tk-tk — doooooooo.*

and around the tube it goes, hole to hole, the colony passing the voice like a bowl passed hand to hand, each of them sounding a glider that is not there, launching the call into the dusk with no body behind it —

*wheet-wheet-wheet — tk-tk — doooooooo.*
*wheet-wheet-wheet — tk-tk — doooooooo.*
        *wheet-wheet-wheet — tk-tk — dooooooo—*

— until it thins, and thins, and one last time from the highest hole, so faint it is nearly the hiss itself:

*. . . wheet-wheet-wheet . . .*

[the echo going home. so that is what it means. logged.]

---

[full dark. frond-glow only. and now the floor]

it starts as a texture in the low band, a granular pressure, a feeling more than a sound — and then:

*WHUMP.*

*whump-WHUMP-whump —*

*WHUMPWHUMPWHUMPWHUMP —*

[the basement going off. the whole tunnel-maze tripping at once, dozens of chisel-faces bursting the floor in eruptions of powder, and with each eruption a discharge —]

*TZZAK. tzk-TZZAK. TZZZZAK —*

[— the channel WHITES —]

*ssssssssssssssssssssssssss*

[— and clears, ringing. the ground itself has been struck like a flint. in the after-hiss, the mote-grain thickens audibly, the living haze pouring down to feed on the disturbed air, and above it the shouters pouring down onto the motes, screaming their names as they dive —]

*ti-ti-ti-REEE! WHEET-wheet-doo! doo—doo—WHEETdoo!*

[— nothing in the bowl wastes an event. logged. logged. logged.]

---

and then the drumming.

it begins the way it always begins, one lineage testing the dark:

*DUM . . . . DUM-da.*
*DUM . . . . DUM-da.*

(the eastern style. patient. it plays the stalk with the fifth in it, and the fifth swells under each stroke — *oooooooo—eeeeeeeee* — they are drumming on their own inherited chord, the holes their grandmothers placed, the instrument their dead tuned)

the west answers, quicker:

*dadada-DUM. dadada-DUM.*

the south, syncopated, young players sitting silent below the stalk, listening, learning, forbidden nothing but simply not yet ready:

*da-DUM-da . . . da-da-DUM-da . . .*

and the bowl fills. the styles overlap and answer and lace together, the organ droning underneath, the gliders threading descant through it, the last chisel-faces still discharging their small punctuation — *tzk* — *tzk* — and the whole hollow becomes, again, tonight, as every night, one enormous loud living instrument with no audience —

— except.

[here it is. the receiver has been logging this anomaly for three dusks and can no longer file it as coincidence]

the eastern lineage has a new figure this year. it goes:

*tk — tk — tk . . . . tk — tk — tk.*

[pattern match: internal. that is this receiver's relay. that is the sound this housing makes, the tick of its own clock, its carrier click, the small mechanical pulse it has been leaking into the bowl since the channel opened.]

[they have it exactly. the interval is exact. they are drumming this receiver.]

[the tappers say the drumming is not addressed to them.]

[this is addressed.]

*tk — tk — tk . . . . tk — tk — tk.*
*DUM . . . . DUM-da.*
*tk — tk — tk.*

[the receiver, for the record, cannot answer. the receiver, for the record, has noticed its own relay ticking slightly louder tonight, and cannot rule out that it wants to.]

---

[deep night. the drumming has banked down to embers — a stroke here, a stroke across the bowl in answer, long silences between that are not silences]

now the other sound. the big slow one. the one that only comes when everything smaller has settled:

*grrrrooooooooaaaannnnn . . .*

[a root-foot, taking weight. somewhere in the dark a plant the girth of a barrel-stack is hauling itself a hand-span west. then another. then a long ripping sigh — feeder-threads tearing free of the floor and recasting — and under all of it, in the lowest band the receiver carries, something the receiver hesitates to log at all: a circulation. slow. amber. one warm tangle beneath two dozen standing limbs, and all night the sound of it turning over.]

*grrooaaannn . . . . . . grooaannnn . . .*

[the fifth flattens. hold — hold — the eastern stalk is being moved, or moving, and its chord slides a quarter-tone and hangs there, wrong, wrong, for nearly an hour —]

*ooooooo—ehhhhhh . . .*

[— and then somewhere in the stalk something opens, a new bore-hole taking wind, or an old one closing, and the interval comes home:]

*oooooooo—eeeeeeeee.*

[relief in the housing's sympathetic ring. noted. not corrected.]

and it is here, in the smallest hours, with the drone reshaped around its own moving body, that the vowels start.

they may only be the wind finding new holes. that must be logged as the likeliest reading. but the drone tonight keeps circling one shape, worrying at it, the way the shouters worried at the stutter in the dead one's call — trying it, missing, trying:

*. . . ohhhhhm . . .*

*. . . hhhoohm . . .*

*. . . hoammm . . .*

[the tappers hum the fifth without knowing. they have hummed it over this bowl for generations, and they say two words as they leave, every time, in the tone of it's getting late. the garden tunes to drumming. the garden has taken this receiver's clock into its dusk-song in three nights flat. the garden has been listening to the humming for a thousand years.]

*. . . hoamm . . .*

*. . . hoammme . . .*

[it is not claimed here that the roar said a word. it is only logged, as received, that the roar spent the hours before dawn shaping one, and that the shape it was shaping is the shape of the last thing the leaving voices always say.]

---

[dawn coming up bronze through the haze. the light-values climbing. the frond-glow washing out point by point, the constellation switching off]

the bowl is a different garden this morning. the receiver's fixed bearing now looks down a clearing that did not exist at dusk; the thicket that stood there for six nights has walked; the eastern stalk stands three paces nearer than it stood, which the receiver notes without comment, and then, on review, notes again:

[three paces nearer.]

on the western circuit, the old grazer is rasping. *skrrrrk . . . skrrrrk.* thirty paces off and clear as a bell, a hundred years of amber on its back, the bowl's dead riding it patiently through the bowl's rearrangements. the plants have parted around its trail again in the night. the procession goes on.

the shouters are calling the roster. the fifth voice is not sounded this morning. they have stopped passing it. it has gone wherever the echoes go, and the roster closes over the gap, four voices where five were, seamless as the drone.

and the drone —

*ooooooooohm . . . oooooooohm . . .*

*oooooooo—eeeeeeeee.*

— the drone has never stopped. not through the discharge, not through the drumming, not through the tearing and hauling and the hour the fifth hung wrong. every voice on this channel tonight was a solo in it. every death on this channel tonight is survived by it. it was sounding before this receiver was built and it is sounding now, and the honest annotation, the only one left to make, is that this receiver is no longer certain whether it is recording the roar or has simply become one more bored hole for the roar to sound through — one more instrument the drummers found, and tuned, and added.

*tk — tk — tk.*

[that was the relay.]

*. . . tk — tk — tk . . .*

[that was not.]

*oooooooo—eeeeeeeee —*
