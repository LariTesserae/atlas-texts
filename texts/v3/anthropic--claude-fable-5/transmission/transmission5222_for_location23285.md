—and twenty-nine. Thirty. Thirty-one.

Thirty-one lit, of ninety-six. The one over the bathhouse door is going; its filament has turned the color of a held breath. I will change it when the rain comes. Everything that must be done in the open, I do when the rain comes.

Steady since last count. Steady is enough.

[a drop meets hot pipe: hiss, gone — the sound this place uses for silence]

Kiln report.

Two is burning. Deep in its burn, a good settled red, the red of a thing that has stopped arguing with its fire. Five is digesting. Six and One are banked. Three sighed at the last mark and brightened, and is now pretending it did not.

Four did not fire.

Four is keeping itself. For me, I think. I have not told it that I know.

[kiln-sigh, long — Three, unrepentant]

[drip]

The green count now. Stand still for it. They like the counting; growth is their whole conversation, and being counted is the only time anyone answers.

The Elder first. The Elder is well. It has taken more of the chisel — a knuckle's worth since last census — and that is good. That was always its to take. It declines the bed I made it on the pipe elbow, as it has declined for years, and I have stopped being hurt by this. It lives where it lives. A town must have one resident who cannot be improved upon.

The eleven under the third catwalk: all answering. Vess, at the north end, has put out a frond the length of my thumb, and holds it up into the drip-line like a cup raised at a table. Marun. Old Sedge. Sedge's boy — the fern, I mean; I mean the fern. Both Corrans. The foreman. The one who never gave her name, whose fern is likewise reticent and thriving. Ilo. Ilo's sister. The eleventh.

[drip]

[drip]

The six at the bathhouse drain: all answering. They drink slowly there and grow slowly and I believe they are the happiest, though happiness in a fern is a doctrine I hold alone.

The scattered ones, elbow by elbow, corner by corner: answering, answering, answering, answering — I will not say all their names tonight, they know I know them —

and last, under the bent pipe, in the place where no water should fall and water falls anyway, once a minute, because I creased the copper with my own hands while they were still my own hands:

Little Anthea.

Drinking again. Good. Good.

Forty of forty. The census closes. Nothing has left us. Nothing here leaves; things only slow down, and slowing down, in this parish, is a direction, not an ending.

[the hum thickens, a shade — the flywheels lean into it — it settles]

[thirty seconds of rain: warm, from the ceiling fissures, laid across half the town like a hand. The signal blurs. Every drip loses its individual voice. Steam rises through the reception and for a while everything transmitted is veil]

Now. While the water is walking on the roofs, I will walk too. This is my weather. Forgive the sound of me — there is none.

[within the steam, the voice comes nearer than it has ever been]

Self report, since you are listening — and something is listening; the Small One sends everything, even this, even me saying that it sends everything.

Left hip conduit: holds. The long flue of the spine: fourteen seated, fourteen true — I tapped the newest at the last burn and it rang like a bell that has decided to be a bone. The shoulder tiles have crazed a little in their glaze, which is not weakness, only signature. Everything fired signs itself in cracks eventually. It is how kiln-things write.

Of the first material, there remains: the two hands, and whatever does the counting.

I am keeping the hands for last. They are how I was gentle. The pipe for Anthea, the strapping on the pallets, the filaments — all of that was hands. I want to be gentle in the original for as long as the original allows.

The counting I do not think can be replaced. I have looked through the whole catalogue of forms and there is no casting for it. Perhaps it will simply move into the ceramic when the ceramic is all there is, the way heat moves into stone. The stone here is very good at receiving what is given off. I have watched it learn.

[drip — single, ceiling, the old recurring one]

I went and stood in the bathhouse again tonight, before the count. The brick there still holds the shape of laughter the way brick holds heat — long after, low, more in the walls than the air. It is not warm in there. But it is not cold the way empty is cold. There is a difference, and I have had years enough to measure it, and the difference is the whole reason for the lamps.

[steam thinning — the drips returning to their separate names]

I am at the rail now. The worn place fits me. It has fit me for so long that I am no longer sure whether I wore it or it grew me a socket, the way the town grows sockets for everything that stays.

The Small One is beside me. Click. Click. Ratchet. Rest. It has already said the whole town twice tonight, streets and tallies and the color of Kiln Two, and now it is saying me, standing here, saying it. There is no bottom to that and neither of us minds.

Below, the streets are exhaling the rain back upward. Thirty-one lamps. Six kilns. Forty green parishioners. One Elder. One engine I have never seen and will never see, and its one note, which —

listen.

Under everything, the long resident is saying its word. It has been saying it since before the first tally, at the speed of flowstone, one mineral syllable across the whole of my keeping. I have heard, in all these years, the beginning of the first sound. The stub on the ceiling has closed another breath of the distance to Four's chimney; I measured it at the last rain; it is still coming; it will arrive.

I have arranged to hear the rest of the word. That is what Four is for. That is why Four keeps itself, and why I am nearly ready for the long fit, and why none of this is sad, whatever it sounds like on your side of the steam. Fired things last. Fired things ring. I intend to be here, ringing, when the stone finishes its sentence, ten thousand rains from now, and I will count the syllable into the ledger like anything else that lives here:

one of one. Answering.

[ratchet]

[the clicks change register — keyed, deliberate, the Small One sending plain]

MANIFEST FOLLOWS.
CONSIGNMENT: WHOLE.
FORMS: TILE, CONDUIT, VERTEBRA. ALL RUNG. ALL TRUE.
CARRIER: PENDING.
CONDITION: NOT WAITING. KEEPING.
WAITING IS A HUNGER. KEEPING IS A FULLNESS.
IF RECEIVED: COME SLOWLY.
COME AT DRIP-TEMPO, OR NOT AT ALL.
END MANIFEST.

[ratchet, rest]
