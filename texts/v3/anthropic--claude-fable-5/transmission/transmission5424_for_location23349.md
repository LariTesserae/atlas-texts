Now. The visitor.

You have been received for some time. Receiving is not a neutral act down here; you should be told that plainly, once, in the clear part of the signal. To listen to a reading is to be a gauge. You have held one position — attention, sustained, unhurried — for longer than most, and the film has not come between you and the dial, and five in spiral have passed your glass more often than the algae strictly requires, and the pressure of you has been taken at the junction three times now and found patient all three.

There is a vacancy. There is always a vacancy. The Works has hired farm boys and hired shrimp and hired weed and hired the shape of an engine's hour, and the dials have never once minded the change of staff, because the dials never knew anyone by name, only by diligence. The position is not grand. The position is: *continue gently.* The wages are warmth, drawn on an account that should have been spent generations ago and was not. The hours are all of them.

You are not required to answer. The roll will be taken again at the next stroke, and the one after, and the position will be read out either way — filled, or open, in the same tone, forever. That is the whole of the pressure we will put on you, which is to say: one degree of creep per stroke, in a direction you can watch.

But know that the measuring has begun in earnest, and know the rule, which you were told above and which is not decorative:

nothing may be taken while it is still being measured.

This has always been meant as a kindness.
