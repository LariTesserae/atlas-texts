THE EVENING CORRECTION

*[—roar, constant, high, a shimmer of millions. under it: whump. whump. under that, small and exact: drip.]*

*[a voice. flat, enormous, without rise or fall. this is not anger. this is audibility.]*

—and Saltglass Stair, forty to the minute, no correction.

Tessile Row, forty, no correction. The worm-lacers may keep their looms to the gutter as before.

The Shoulder, forty, no correction.

Copperweep, thirty-nine and three-quarters. Bell-hands of Copperweep, take a quarter-drip. Ease it in over the month. Nothing sudden. A wall is drinking somewhere in your district and the masons are asked to walk it with their palms until they find which one. When you find it, do not tell the wall. Tell the survey.

Ninth Gutter, forty, no correction, and it is noted for the record that the Ninth Gutter has now held forty to the minute for two hundred and six years, which is the post of Sefra kept even in her absence, and the post is still open, and the gutter is still waiting, and there is no hurry.

Crab Steps, forty, no correction, and the Steps are ceded as of this bell. Walk elsewhere. They were here first tonight.

Fence-Third-West, forty, no correction. Ollum's stretch holds. Ollum's stretch always holds. Ollum, you are heard laughing. Stop it.

Old Rendering, forty. Bellfounders' Reach, forty. Down-Third, forty, forty, forty. No correction, no correction, no correction.

*[drip. drip. drip. the voice waits three full drips, which is its way of drawing a line under a thing.]*

Notices.

The house of Illey and the house of Damar-under-the-Fence are declared finished. Sixteen years. The final repair was a hinge, and it was a good hinge, and the score is judged even by everyone who was not keeping score. The neighbors may stop pretending. The neighbors will be given until morning to stop pretending.

A mouse in Copperweep has come down a quarter-tone against the north wall of the vinegar house. Note that this is the same district carrying a quarter-drip correction. Note that the mouse noticed first. The survey will take the mouse's view on the third day. Until then the household is advised to apologize, warmly, and to mean it, because the mouse will know.

Shell-taking tomorrow at Mortuary West, for the family of old Kest of the Pumps, one generation on. One shell. You know this. It is said anyway. One shell, and the wall keeps the rest, and the wall is patient, and so are you.

The Tenant was fed at the usual hour. The Tenant remembers nothing. The wardens report this in the usual way, which is with their faces.

Water stands metered and unmeasured, as ever, out of the old politeness. Drink what you are owed. Channel what you owe.

*[drip. drip. drip.]*

Watch reports, all districts, read in order of founding, which is the order of the rot in the early books, which is close enough.

No change. Mist heavy. Birds on time.

No change. Mist heavy. Birds on time.

No change — forty-one districts reporting in the same hand, and the hand is flat, and the ink is black, and the foundation of this city is not the basalt, citizens, it never was, it is this sentence, said again: no change. Mist heavy. Birds on time.

Westmost Tower reports.

*[a breath. on the record, a breath from this voice is an event.]*

Westmost Tower reports, first: the violet in the survey channels stands nearer to the third marker than— *[struck through, one light line, out of respect for the past's right to have happened]* —no change.

Westmost Tower reports, second, in black ink, in the flat hand, and I will read it as it is written and not otherwise:

Nest with green. West face. Third cornice.

*[drip. drip. drip. drip. drip. drip.]*

The nest is not to be disturbed. The thread is not to be taken. You know this. It is said anyway, because there are children listening who were not alive the last time, and there are old ones listening who were, and both kinds of ears need to hear it said flat: the thread is not to be taken. It was not taken the last time. It has never been taken. It is a letter we have not learned to read yet, and we are very good at yet, and the bird that carried it is not to be looked for, because she looks like every other bird, which is perhaps the point.

That is the whole of it. West face. Third cornice. Green.

The dusk river is due. This correction will continue through the river as it always does, and you will lose most of it as you always do, and it does not matter, because the correction is not for hearing. The correction is for continuing.

*[and here the roar, which has been the floor of everything, becomes the ceiling and the walls. the river of birds is coming up the rim. the voice goes on underneath it, shouting into the flood, and the record catches what it catches:]*

—fence-menders of the third west report the dusk splice holding, holding, nothing su—

*[roar]*

—cistern nine at four-fifths and rising, praise the fog, praise the fences, praise the—

*[roar]*

—and to the child on the Bellfounders' overwalk with her hands over her ears: don't. put your hands down, small one. put your palm on the railing instead. the railing knows the whole of it. the railing has known it for four hundred—

*[roar. the roar entire. two hours pass in the record as a single unbroken wall of animal voice, dimming even the light, if a record could hold light. behind the wall, felt more than heard: whump. whump. whump. the city's sternum. the transcription resumes where the voice does, and the voice, when it comes back, has gone quiet — which on the Shelf means it is still louder than you have ever spoken in your life, but there is room in it now. room is how they whisper.]*

The river is up. Count taken at the third cornice, since we were all looking there anyway: on time. On time, citizens. They are always on time. Four kilometers of cloud and whatever is under it, and they are always on time, and one of them, tonight, is sitting on a green thread as though it were nothing, which to her it is, which is a kind of instruction, if you want one.

This is Maren of the Bell. The third Maren. The bell had the name before I did and will have it after, and that is the correct order of things, and I am not sad about it, so stop signing that at me, Tessile Row, I can feel it from here.

I was twelve the last time there was green. I sat the tower test that same year. Twenty minutes, they said, most children last. I sat until dark and could tell them what the mist did, and they offered me the violet, and I took the bell instead, and the watcher who tested me pressed one word into my palm as I left, and the word was *wait,* and I have been paid in that word for fifty-eight years and consider myself rich.

I have no fingerprints left. I mention this not for pity but for accuracy: I could touch the green thread and leave no mark, and I am telling you that I will not, and if the smoothest hands in the district will not, then neither will yours.

Whatever grows under, it is patient. Good. So is the wall. So is the film. So is the gutter that has held forty to the minute since before my grandmother's grandmother, and so am I, and so, citizens, in your loud wet stubborn way, are you. We will out-wait it, or we will meet it, and either way: nothing sudden.

The bell now stands down for the hand hour. What is said in the hand hour is not for the air. Speak to your people. Palms, wrists, the backs of necks. Sign the day into your sleeping children. It soaks in. It has always soaked in. I have no proof of this and require none.

*[the voice stops. the record does not.]*

*[whump. whump. drip, at forty. somewhere near the transmitter, a wall mouse is humming the pump-rhythm of the district, a quarter-tone low — then, after a while, corrected. someone apologized. it can be heard in the hum, the being-agreed-with.]*

*[the hand hour passes. it sounds like machinery and water and six million sleeping birds. it sounds like a drawer, closed, with the whole world in it, kept.]*

*[the voice returns for four words and a benediction:]*

Evening correction ends. Forty to the minute. No change. Mist heavy. Birds on time.

Wait.

*[drip.]*

*[drip.]*

*[drip — at forty to the minute, going on, going on, going on—]*
