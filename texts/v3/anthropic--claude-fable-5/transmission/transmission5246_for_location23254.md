*[the chord frays. band by band the upper voices thin and gutter as the wind dies on the plain. by full dark there is almost nothing on the carrier — the felt-hiss down to a whisper, the ticking silent, the flues at a murmur — almost nothing]*

*[the fundamental. 11 Hz. it has not moved all day. it does not move now. the wind that made everything else is gone and the low note remains, even, corrected, held — and a note held in the absence of its cause is no longer weather. log it plainly: the pit is quiet and something in the pit is not]*
