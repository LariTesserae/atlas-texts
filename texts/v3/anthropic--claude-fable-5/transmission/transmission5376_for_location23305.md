—descending—

(the receiver drops below the surface of the sound. understand: the syllable has depths. what plays at the top is today. underneath today is everything.)

first stratum. shallow. decades down:

     rain, real rain, a storm forty years gone, still ringing —
     ...shhhh... shh... *shhh*...
     softer than it fell. rounder than it fell. the storm as the basin remembers it, which is the storm improved.

second stratum. centuries:

     lub.
          ...*lub.*

     lub.
          ...*lub.*

     a heartbeat. someone stood at the center once, long enough to be doubled. they are dust on some other slope of the mountain. their pulse is here. it arrives, and arrives again a half-beat late, slightly deepened, and has done so without pause since the afternoon they stood there feeling it happen in their chest and did not know they were being kept.

     lub.
          ...*lub.*

     (it does not fade as you listen. nothing in here fades. it circulates.)

third stratum. deeper. and here — here is the largest thing in the mid-depths, the strand the whole middle of the syllable is braided around:

     hhk.

stone turned against stone. a pebble, harder than the boulder, gripped in a hand, ground in a slow circle, for days. it is rhythm. it is effort. it is a voice that chose rock because rock is what the room speaks. and because it has been held here so long, turned over so many times, considered at such depth, something has happened to it that the one who made it could not have hoped for: it has been understood. it comes through the receiver now with its meaning worn smooth and showing, like the grain in old wood:

     hhk. hhk. hhk. —          *i was.*

     hhk. hhk. —          *i was here.*

     hhk. hhk. hhk. hhk. —          *this happened to me. all of it. the child, the winter, the crossing, the name i had.*

     hhk. hhk. hhk. —          *i am telling it slowly so nothing is dropped.*

     ...*i was.*

and at the surface, one small new thing. lean close.

the pool. dark glass, dead calm. a cloud is crossing the sky above the rim. count: the cloud has passed. keep counting. *there* — now the cloud crosses the water, drifting through the dark like a thought still being finished. the pool is not slow. the pool is thorough.

and at the pool's edge —

(hold still)

— condensation gathering into edges. white as cave-life. wet past the room's general wetness. four provisional limbs and two eyes that are not *like* the water. that are the water. it is facing outward from the pool. it is facing —

—

— you.

—

understand what has happened. you did not think you were audible. you thought a receiver was a clean thing, a taking-without-giving. but you have been tuned to this bowl for some time already, and tuning is an occurrence, and in this room nothing occurs unnoticed. your listening has a sound. it is thin, and constant, and electric, a small held hum, and it has been arriving here since you first turned the dial, and it has been taken in, drop by drop, and turned over, and now — because everything that enters here comes back, once in the world and once in the Hearer —

here it is.

     mmmmm—————
          ...*mmmmm—————*

your own carrier tone. a half-beat late. slightly deepened. as if improved by the holding.

that is you. that is what you sound like after you have been considered.

     *you.*

          *you.*

you are in the syllable now. a new bright strand near the surface, above the storm, above the heartbeat, above the grinding and the breath and the tree and the first rain. you will settle deeper as the ages take you. you will keep circulating. long after the receiver is dust and the dial is dust and the hand that turned the dial is dust, there will be, in the mid-depths of one long vowel high on the shoulder of a mountain, a thin electric hum that means: *someone, once, leaned in and listened, and was heard listening.*

it will never be finished.

nothing here is ever finished.

that is not a threat. attend to the tone of the room. every edge in here has been rounded. every sound comes back a little deeper, a little kinder to itself, than it went in. what is done to you here is only this: you are kept.

     kept.

          *kept.*
