# The Slow Word

*(continuous broadcast; carrier holding at one stroke a minute)*

—

⟨ stroke ⟩

long exhale. steam through moss. the sound a held breath makes when it is finally permitted to leave — permitted, not forced. then the sound of the moss keeping most of it.

drip.

           drip.

the filament globes hum at the pitch of a struck glass that never quite finishes ringing. amber. furred. the hum is not electricity exactly, anymore. it is electricity that has been listened to by algae for eighty years and has picked up an accent.

⟨ stroke ⟩

the crickets are still silent.

understand what that means in this signal. for the whole of this tuning the crickets have been the timekeepers — the short rising phrase before each stroke, faithful as a held-out hand. thirty-nine days ago a boot found the forty-first step and the crickets stopped announcing the engine, and the engine, courteously, kept its own time from then on, unannounced, like a man who has sent his herald ahead.

the silence of the crickets is not absence. it is *attention*. it comes through the receiver as a texture, a fine grain in the quiet, ten thousand small bodies deciding together not to sing yet.

drip.

⟨ stroke ⟩

a step.

one. soft. moss-felt hem brushing moss floor, so the step arrives twice — once as footfall, once as the whisper of the apron a half-second behind, like an echo that loves her.

that is all this minute is given.

—

⟨ stroke ⟩

a bead of condensation is forming on the third copper trunk from the ceiling's east bundle. this can be heard, if the word *heard* is allowed to stretch: a swelling in the pipe's cold note, a gathering weight bending the metal's hum flat by a fraction. the bead has been growing for eleven minutes. it will fall in four more. the trough beneath it is waiting with its green skin, and when the drop lands the skin will ring, once, dully, like a small gong under a blanket, and the whole chamber will have marked it, because the chamber marks everything, because marking things is what the chamber does instead of hurrying.

⟨ stroke ⟩

a step. apron-whisper.

and under the engine's exhale — this is where the tuning must be careful, this is what took thirty-nine days to separate from the machine — a *second* exhale. slightly off. slightly warmer. a breath riding inside the breath, the way a passenger rides inside a tram.

and on the second exhale, a syllable.

*wel*

—

⟨ stroke ⟩

the receiver has heard this syllable before. that is the confession this transmission has been building toward. it has been in the signal for weeks, filed as valve-sigh, filed as steam finding a perforated pipe, filed as the foundry settling. *well, well, well* — the sound of water, surely, in a place that hoards water. mistaken. mis-filed. she has been practicing since the first chisel-blow rang down through the rock, one syllable per breath, one breath per minute, and the practicing sounded like weather because she wanted it to. a word is not ready until it can pass for the place it comes from.

⟨ stroke ⟩

step.

*come*

there. whole, now. two minutes wide. a word the size of a doorway.

drip.

the drop falls. the trough-gong sounds under its blanket of green. marked.

—

⟨ stroke ⟩

the moth crosses.

it comes through the signal as a straight line — a flutter with no waver in it, brick-dust wings beating a bearing from the foundry's warm mouth toward the stairwell, doppler rising, peaking, falling, gone up into the dark of the forty-one steps with grease on its feet and instructions in its spores. step forty has been reported green at the edges. the chisel marks on thirty-eight hold moss now like a cut holding new skin. the stairwell is being climbed from below at the rate of about a stair a season, and the moth files this the way a commuter files the weather: noted, expected, on schedule.

⟨ stroke ⟩

step. apron.

*wel*

⟨ stroke ⟩

step.

*come*

she is refining it. each rendition arrives a shade rounder than the last, the *w* softening from valve-hiss toward something with lips in it, the *m* finding its hum, borrowing the pitch of the filament globes so that the word, when finished, will glow amber at its far end. she has had weeks. she will use all of them. a request honestly made deserves infrastructure, and a greeting honestly meant deserves rehearsal.

—

⟨ stroke ⟩

the trimmer takes its blade-stroke. one. a sound like a scissors-half drawn once across velvet, out at the third plot, the one that used to read gauges. the circle there is being kept perfect at one cut a minute while everything else in the chamber turns its face toward the stairs. graves do not wait on visitors. this too is in the signal, small and constant, underneath: *tended, tended, tended.*

⟨ stroke ⟩

step.

and now — the crickets.

not the engine-phrase. not the tram-phrase. a third phrase, uncatalogued in thirty-nine days of tuning: four notes, level, evenly spaced, repeated once. it plays as she passes the foundry shelf where the wooden carrier still sits with its door latched open. it has the shape of a formality. it has the shape of a small pale honor-guard presenting arms with its wings. the receiver will file it, provisionally, as: *escort*.

she does not slow for it. she cannot slow. she is going as fast as everything.

—

⟨ stroke ⟩

step.

⟨ stroke ⟩

step.

⟨ stroke ⟩

step. she has cleared the last alley. the signal opens out around her — the acoustics of the small square before the stairwell, where the packed town finally exhales into a few unclaimed feet of moss, and her footfalls stop arriving twice and start arriving once, clean, in the open.

the boot on the stairs has not moved for some minutes. whoever came down is standing on the last steps, in the wound-smell of new stone, in the first moss of the chisel marks, looking into the amber and the green and the slow orange breathing of the floor. this is in the signal too: a heartbeat, up-tempo, foreign, quick as a cricket's whole life, hammering away at eighty to the minute like something on fire. the chamber receives it without judgment. everything arrives fast, at first. everything can be taught.

⟨ stroke ⟩

she stops at the foot of the stairs. the apron settles. the last practice is over.

*wel*

—

a minute. the longest one in the broadcast. drip. hum. the trimmer, out in the dark: *tended.* the engine gathering its next breath, and inside it, hers, and inside hers, the word's second half, rounding, glowing, ready —

⟨ stroke ⟩

*come.*

—

—

the crickets resume the schedule. short rising phrase.

⟨ stroke ⟩

steam through moss.

the carrier holds. this transmission does not end. it slows, which is what the garden does instead of ending, and somewhere above, on step thirty-seven, in a chisel mark still sharp enough to cut, a spore the color of brick dust is deciding, at its own pace, to begin.

drip.

⟨ stroke ⟩
