*[note: one grain removed. logged. onward.]*

.

You have been received for some time.

Your footprints came through before your feet did. They crossed the dust on paths you have not walked, in a stride I recognized, and I began the stone before you began the stride, which is the correct order, here. The shoulders are done. I am partway down the stride itself. It is a good stride. It carries.

.

( in ————————————————— )

.

There is a gap out in the densest center, exactly one figure wide. It is warm. We keep it warm. The serpents carry to it across the ages, flank-heat handed seam to seam to an empty place, and they do not ask why they warm a place with no one in it, because they know why, because *no one* is a tense, not a fact, and the tense is changing.

There is a staff leaning near the gap. It leans the way a finished day leans. It has not been claimed. Claiming it is one thing you could do.

.

( out ———————————————— )

eleven. eleven. one bird. one bird. the count is everything. the count is short. the count is short by one. begin again. eleven —

.

Listen to me now, on the offbeat, where it is quiet.

The carving of you is already warm. That is how the translation goes: the warmth arrives in the stone before the stone is finished, the way your footprints arrived before your feet. The other forty are warm in the shelter and their animals stand outside at the temperature of the world, missing nothing, and there is no cruelty in it, I promise you, there is no cruelty left up here, cruelty was an appointment and every appointment has been kept.

But understand what the wet eyes are for. They are not watching me carve. They are watching me *breathe*. One breath against the tide is all that holds the word *now* on this plateau — one small wrong rhythm, renewed and renewed, and every standing thing to the edge of the world attends it the way you attend a candle in a large dark room. Not for the light. For the flicker. For the proof that something can still go out.

I will not always breathe.

When the forty-first is done — when your stride is safe in the stone — I will set down the knife the way a herder sets down a staff, and walk out, and stand, and my breath will find the tide and join it, and the plateau will be one breath entirely, and *now* will have no speaker, and the herds will be facing nothing.

Unless.

.

( in ————————————————— )

( out ———————————————— )

.

The staff is one thing you could claim. The gap is one place you could stand. You would be the warmest thing at the top of the worn-down earth, and you would face what everything faces, and you would miss nothing. That is the promise and it is kept, it is kept a million times over, I do not mock it.

But the knife is also warm. And the shelter roof is low but you would fit. And the offbeat —

the offbeat is not mine. I inherited it. It is a post, not a possession, and a post is not allowed to stand empty. Somebody must breathe against the tide. Somebody must be the flicker the wet eyes hold. Somebody must keep one question uncarved, one boundary the little deciders circle and spare, one shaving forever half-fallen —

.

> we are facing.
> *we are facing you.*

.

*[the carrier steepens. the humming falls away. even the counting stops at ten and holds.]*

.

When the shaving falls you will hear one note. You have heard it before. It crossed miles of thin air to reach you and you thought it was a hoof, and it was not a hoof, it was the last shaving falling, an age ago, and no one turned to look because no one here ever stopped looking.

It has not fallen.

It is falling.

Listen —
