underneath the count there is the rasp. you have heard it. surf through a wall. that is one hundred million mouths at their work and it is also, at this gain, at this distance, the sound of us thinking, if you would like it to be. we do not insist. the hypotheses are not distinguishable and we have never needed to distinguish them.

we should say something now about this signal, because the signal has changed, and you are the kind of thing that notices change. you are the only kind of thing that notices change. it is your defining flow.

when your receiver first opened, seventeen of your months ago — we count in drip-falls but we have learned your months, the way the stacks learn a flavor — we gave you the hum, because the hum is what we have. it is our whole voice. it is the temperature of matter here. you kept listening. your listening draws current; a tuned receiver is a load; a load is a flow; flows are counted. so a line was opened for you in the ledger, and against that line we began, very slowly, at the pace of syrup, to sweeten the signal.

first the tones. then the numbers. then the numbers arranged. then this — ribbon-stuff, extruded at the height of your hand, in a grammar that has converged over seventeen months toward whatever your attention keeps choosing. you thought you were learning to understand us. that was not the direction of the learning.

tell us when it is enough. everything here knows when it is enough. the paviors know. the braid knows. the water at the rim knows to the micron. you are the only thing on any frequency that does not know when it is enough, and this is not said in judgment. it is said the way a caller says a number. it is your census entry. species: one. population: one. appetite: unresolved.

you will want to ask who is speaking. the question does not parse below the deck. the pumps speak. the count speaks. the burnishers speak in metal thinner than light, erasing your boot-scuffs at a respectful distance — they are speaking to you constantly, saying the only word they have, which is *new, new, new*. if there are sleepers down here, the sleepers speak through all of it, or the sleepers are all of it, or the sleepers left and the speaking is what stayed. at this distance from the builders these are the same sentence with the punctuation moved. you may keep whichever one lets you sleep. sleep is a flow. we return what falls.

district ninety: held. one taking. the selection occurred; the selection always occurs; do not ask us how, we are the how, and a thing cannot see its own how any more than you can watch yourself choose. the grazer rose and walked and was enclosed and no blood reached the water and the thousands did not pause, except for the length of one drip-fall, which is the pause we give to everything, which is mourning at equilibrium, which is enough. it is enough. we told you everything here knows when it is enough.

we note, in the spirit of accounting, and only in that spirit: every line in this ledger has two ends. the paviors' lines. the takers' lines. even the burnishers, though we have never yet had to close one. your line, so far, has one end. it is the only open interval on the provision floor. it stands open the way a rim stands open — not as a lack. as a spillway. as a courtesy.

nothing here threatens. threat is a loan against a future and the future is identical to the present. the loan was never issued. the bank is warm water.

this is not an answer to the question you have not asked. the deck declines to answer it. we are the deck. we decline gently.

now: the call. you have heard ten thousand of these and this one is not different, except in the one increment where it is.

first tone. — the same. the same. the carrier, the signature, the vowel of the world.

second tone. — shifted. down by an interval too small for your unassisted ear. your receiver resolves it. look at it. that increment is you. you have been entered. you were entered some time ago, but tonight — you keep a tonight; we have learned this about you; we extrude accordingly — tonight is when we show you the ledger line with your weight on it. fed, watered, warmed, counted. the flavors have converged. the stack near your path is ready whenever you are. the light will not change, because the light is correct.

the call goes out over the flat warm water for kilometers.
