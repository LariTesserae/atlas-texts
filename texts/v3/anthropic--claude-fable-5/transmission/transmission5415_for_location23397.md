**The Setting — Night Reading, Seventh Cavity**

*[carried, as every night, on the stray charge of the lamp-grid; low orange; the voice arrives close, mouth-to-wood close, because it is]*

—

[join in progress. Under everything: ticking. Sporadic, all directions, like a vast roof cooling in reverse. A capacitor-gall filling somewhere near the source, its small dry click faster than the rest. Loom-whisper, distant, half a cavity off. Then the voice — the same voice, the reader of the Seventh, unhurried as always—]

"...and the sixth scaffold's taking, finally, after the beetles sat on it a full week — Ost's herd, herd eleven, tell her they earned their resin — the joint hums now. I can feel it from here. Sternum-deep, good and low. Mark the sixth *taking* and tell the bandagers to stop losing sleep over it, they were right, the angle was right, they only had to wait, which is the whole of the job, which they know, which I will not knock to them because they know.

Third quarter is thin again. Three nights running. Lignin coming in like loft-bat wing, you could read through it. A high sad ping off the west ribs, right at the vault, same place, same hour. Something up there wants attention. Send a well-walker down the near trunk with a lamp and good eyes, and send one of the old ones, not a squinter, someone whose eyes still work in the dusk.

Ninth quarter set beautifully. I'll say it plain: beautifully. Whole district went *tick — tick — tick* all evening like an elder telling a story with her knuckles, every joint on time. Somebody had a good night out there. Mark it."

[pause. The gall clicks. Far off, brief: a rapid knuckle-patter through the walls — quick, doubled, private cadence. Not transcribed. Lovers' traffic, tower to tower. The voice waits it out, courteous, the way one waits for a bird to finish]

"Mm. Those two again. They'll wake the third quarter before the third quarter wakes itself."

—

[a slower knock arrives. Formal. Spreading through the wood from the east passages, repeated, relayed, growing fainter as it passes and stronger as it nears —]

[gloss: ONE OF US — GOING UP]

[the voice stops. Nine knocks in reply, measured, flat-knuckled: the passing-answer. Then, quieter than before—]

"That'll be Ferren. The gear-wright. Well — good. He was mostly seasoned already, that one. Grain on his face like burl, the apprentices used to find excuses to bring him things just to look at him. He'll go up light. He'd been getting light for years."

[a long breath. The ticking goes on around it]

"Add him to the walls. He liked the fourth gallery, the one over the old press-hall — he said the shafts turn all night under it and a man might as well season somewhere with company. Give him the fourth."

—

[at this point in the transmission: the ticking stops.

All of it. Every direction. Even the gall.

Nine seconds. This receiver has learned the length of it. It is always about nine seconds]

[the voice, barely voiced at all:] "Still. Still."

[the ticking resumes, east first, then everywhere, like rain starting on a roof, except dry, except upward]

"...East along the trestles and gone. The bats are talking again already, listen to them, gossips. Mark nothing. It knows what's stock."

—

"Now. Grazer-watch item, and I'll give it the way it was given to me. Fourth night running, the grazers have made a ring out past the nub-fields, at the dark end of the Ninth range. Twelve of them. Spaced even as loom-teeth, the watch says, and standing, not feeding, and facing in. Watch asked me what I hear from out that way.

I hear what I always hear from out that way.

I hear it deciding.

I told them: keep walking. Keep counting. It is not our conversation."

[pause. When the voice resumes it has changed — lower, and slower, and this receiver notes that the ambient ticking has thinned around it, as if making room]

"And the other thing. I'll say it soft, because it isn't mine to say loud, and half of you knocking me questions tomorrow can save your knuckles, because this is everything I know.

There is a new sound over the dark end. Been there since early season. Not settling — settling is short, settling is *done-being-done*. This is long. One note, held, coming down through the overburden a little more every night, straight as a plumb-fiber, and hollow.

It is bringing a light-trunk down out there.

There is nothing out there. There is no district planned out there. There are the nubs, and the grazers standing in their ring, and the dark, and now there is a shaft of tomorrow coming down through half a mile of ground toward all of it, and nobody asked, because nobody asks, because it was never ours to ask.

It is growing a room. Out past everything. For someone.

So: someone is coming. Give it thirty years."

[a small dry sound. It takes this receiver a moment to identify it as laughter]

"Where's the fire."

—

[from very far off, faint, fast, a child's knuckle-patter, too quick, cadence uneven:]

[gloss: ARE YOU THERE ARE YOU THERE ARE YOU]

[the voice answers on the wall, slow, each knock spaced like a footstep:]

[gloss: HERE. SLEEP. GROW.]

"...The Weir girl. Knocks like her mother did at that age. Faster, though. They all knock faster now. The walls will slow them down eventually. The walls slow everyone down eventually. That's what they're for."

[the unstoppering of a gourd. Drinking. The stopper replaced, waxed thumb pressed round it — audible, everything is audible, the dryness gives up every small sound like a confession]

"That's the reading, then. Third thin, sixth taking, ninth singing, one of us going up light, a ring at the dark end, and a room being grown for a stranger who isn't born yet.

A fair day.

Mark it a fair day."

—

[the voice knocks goodnight into the wood. The old sign, worn smooth on ten thousand lintels:]

[gloss: GOOD NIGHT. GROW WELL.]

[the lamp-hum drops as the grid banks for the night. The voice does not speak again.

The ticking continues.

As every night, this receiver's protocol attempts a knock-code gloss of the settling itself, and as every night it fails — the intervals are growth, not grammar; expansion, lignin setting, new wood declaring itself; noise.

Except.

Slowed to one-fortieth speed, tonight, as on other nights, a pattern repeats beneath the noise. One sign, over and over, at the tempo of a thing that thinks in decades answering a thing that thinks in afternoons.

Nearest gloss, low confidence: WORTH.

Or WAIT. The two signs are adjacent in the code. They have always been adjacent in the code.

Signal continues. It always continues.]
