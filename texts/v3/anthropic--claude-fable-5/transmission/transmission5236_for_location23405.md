# THE NOON WORD

[wind. thin, without weight, arriving from a long way off with nothing in its hands. a tone rides it:]

aa—o—aan

[and under that a low fifth, held, older, tuned by no one living. the tone is a name. it is said hourly whether or not she is home.]

[she is home.]

[stone, ticking as the sun crosses it: tk . . . tk . . . . . . tk. no rhythm. a cooling engine the size of a country.]

·

·

[somewhere in the third limb a leaf lets go. seventy years of service ending in one clear note:]

*tink.*

[it chimes once on the stone and is finished moving forever.]

[grinding, close to the receiver. skkr. skkr. dust register — a mark meant to stop being true by spring. she murmurs as she draws it, the way a hand murmurs while it writes:]

— Leaf down. Third limb, ninth joint. Heard before seen. No wind to blame. It was time.

·

[nine days]

·

[noon. the sun stands high enough now to lend to everyone, and the traffic begins — brightness stuttering off the rises, hopping ridge to ridge, arriving crisp across distances that should have softened it:]

⟪DUST FRONT CROSSING THE LOW BASINS. WIDE AS THREE DAYS. LET YOUR LEAVES GO SLACK.⟫
— the south, relayed four times, a day and a half old

⟪WALKER THROUGH MY EAST HALO IN THE NIGHT. SIXTY-ONE PRINTS. UNHURRIED. LOGGED.⟫

⟪TO THE ARGUMENT: THE SEATED ONES DO NOT SPEAK. LIGHT PASSES THROUGH THEM AS IT PASSES THROUGH LEAVES. THE WORD IS OURS. WE READ IT INTO THEM AND CALL IT HEARING.⟫
— the north rim. the argument is nineteen years old.

[her mirror stays covered. she is thinking about her answer. she may think for a season. she may think for ten.]

·

[a glider crosses, once, high and slow, more kite than bird. she stops grinding for the whole hour of its passage. then: skkr, skkr — logged.]

[laughter, once, alone, carrying three meters into the thin air and no farther. reason not received.]

·

[a season]

·

[the dust front arrives. the signal goes to grain:]

hhhhhhhh———sssssss———

[the world is a white disc and a hiss. the trees ground the sky — threads of violet climb off the high limbs with a sound like a struck wire drawn upward:]

ffffff—eeeeeeee— [gone.] ffff—eeeee— [gone.]

⟪—REAK— DUST BETW—— US. SAY AGAIN AFTER.⟫

[three days of hiss. under it, faithful, the chord — flattened by the fine fog, a name spoken through cloth:]

aa—o—aahn

[after. the air pops clean. everything smells of the dry world's after-rain, which does not transmit, but the silence that follows has that shape.]

·

·

⟪A CHILD IS ASKED FOR AT THE GROVE THAT HUMS ee—ie—ee. THE PARENTS ASK FOR SEVEN LENDERS. WHO WILL SHORTEN?⟫

[her mirror uncovers. one flash, brief, weighed for a full day before sending:]

⟪THREE YEARS. I PLEDGE THREE YEARS. I WILL WALK IN SPRING.⟫

[through the afternoon, other answers arrive over the horizon like slow rain:]

⟪TWO YEARS.⟫
⟪TWO.⟫
⟪FOUR. I HAVE MORE THAN I WILL USE.⟫
⟪ONE YEAR, AND SORRY IT IS NOT MORE.⟫

·

[spring. the seeds go up through the light, glass beads on filament wings, snow falling in the wrong direction. she walks northeast and out of range of everything but her name.]

[for forty days the grove hums aa—o—aan to no one. the low fifth holds it steady, the way it has held six keepers' names before hers.]

[she returns lighter by three years, with a small star sealed on her left forearm. that night the silence has a grain to it: a pulse, close, even, and — if a pulse can be — proud.]

[weeks later, at noon:]

⟪THE CHILD IS QUICKENED. SHE HOLDS NINE NAMES. YOURS IS THE SEVENTH NOTE IN HER.⟫

[grinding — but slow this time, deep. stone register. forever:]

— THREE YEARS FOR A SEVENTH NOTE. FAIR. MORE THAN FAIR.

·

[dusk. footsteps to the west edge of the grove, where the second keeper sits — four hundred years annealed, facing the high center, green glass going amber as the light leaves it. close speech, the intimate register, spent on the dead without hesitation:]

— There's a child now with three of my years walking around inside her. I thought you'd want that kept somewhere warmer than the floor.

[the seated figure holds its one posture. warmth departs it slowly, the way heat leaves stone.]

— Goodnight, then.

·

·

[a fleck on the northwest sightline. forty miles out.]

[a day of watching.]

[another day of watching.]

[by the time there are two shadows under one tree, every word has been finished for hours:]

— Your teacher has chosen her place.
— Where does she face?
— The high center. She asks you to come read the floor with her once more. And to take the low notes.
— When?
— She is in no hurry. She said: tell aa—o—aan there is time. There is exactly enough time.

[a long quiet. two people breathing, in air too thin to carry it between them.]

— Stay tonight. The cushion by the east limb is the coolest.
— I know. I slept there forty years ago.
— I have it in the floor.

·

[a season. the receiver drifts west with her, or the traffic carries word — at this distance there is no difference.]

[another grove, deeper-voiced: mm—mm—ooo. weeks of two voices under it, reading the grooves aloud, oldest ring outward:]

— "Glider, two days over the south sightline, turning."
— "First hard light of the year on the far rim. It rang."
— "The child who will keep this circle arrived today. She reads slowly. Good."

[the teacher's voice, gone half to glass already, even and warm:]

— That one. Grind that one deeper before you go.

[then the weeks of annealing. the hum of the teacher's grove sinking, note by note, as she takes her hands out of it. her fingers pass translucent first. she arranges her palms with great care, checking them against the noon sun four times, adjusting by degrees, the way one tunes an instrument that will only ever play a single word.]

[one dawn, a new sound. small. a single drop striking stone.]

[once.]

[it does not dry for three days.]

[stone register, ground slowly, each stroke taking most of a day:]

— ONE TEAR. FOR mm—mm—ooo. SPENT KNOWINGLY. ACCOUNTED. NOT REGRETTED.

·

·

[home. noon. her mirror uncovers. her first and only flash to the argument, twenty-odd years in the composing:]

⟪TO THE ARGUMENT. MY NAME IS SAID BY WIND THROUGH LEAVES I TUNED. IT IS STILL MY NAME. HER WORD IS SAID BY SUN THROUGH HANDS SHE SHAPED. IT IS STILL HER WORD. IF THE WIND SPEAKS ME, THE LIGHT SPEAKS HER. I WILL HEAR NO DIFFERENCE, BECAUSE THERE IS NONE.⟫

[the continent thinks. a season of ordinary traffic — dust fronts, walker prints, leaves down.]

[then, from ridge after ridge, relayed inward from farther than sight, the same short flash:]

⟪CARRIED.⟫
⟪CARRIED.⟫
⟪GRIND IT.⟫
⟪CARRIED.⟫

·

⟪NEW SEATED ONE ON THE HIGH CENTER APPROACH. HANDS RAISED. FIRST PRINTING TODAY. THE WORD IS: HERE.⟫

·

·

[years, or the shape of years. the traffic goes on. once:]

⟪A CHILD RAN TODAY. WEST TO EAST ACROSS MY WHOLE CIRCLE. A FULL MILE. NO REASON.⟫
⟪SEEN FROM THIS RIDGE AS A MOVING FLECK. THANK YOU.⟫
⟪WHICH CHILD?⟫
⟪THE ONE WITH NINE NAMES.⟫

[under the grove, close-voiced, to the seated second keeper, who is facing the right direction to have seen it:]

— Three of those years were mine. Look at her spend them.

[laughter. three meters. no farther. enough.]

·

·

[noon.]

⟪HERE.⟫

[*tink.*]

[skkr. skkr.]

[the chord: aa—o—aan, the old fifth under it — and now, when the wind comes out of the northwest, a new low note beneath them both, untuned by anyone living: mm, held, the bass of a name she carried home.]

[night. the silence with its one grain in it.]

[noon.]

⟪HERE.⟫

[noon.]

⟪HERE.⟫

[the receiver stays as long as it can. the word does not change. it is not going to change. that is the whole of what it says, and it says it entire, once a day, in light, and somewhere a mirror always answers, and somewhere a groove is always deepened, and the stone ticks, and the leaf falls, and chimes once, and is witnessed —]

[noon.]

⟪HERE.⟫

[still receiving]
