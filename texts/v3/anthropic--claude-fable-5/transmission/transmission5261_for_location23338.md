⟨hour 5,266 continued · noise floor advisory⟩

There is a hole in the roar.

Not a silence in the valley — the valley has none — but a notch in the spectrum, narrow, clean-edged, centered on the receiving frequency. First measurable eleven days ago at a fraction of a decibel. Tonight it is unmistakable: the cracks, the groans, the sap-thunder, all of it present at full force on every side of the channel and thinning, courteously, precisely, as it approaches the channel itself, stopping a uniform inch short, all around, on every course.

Nothing in acoustics does this. Leaves do this. Leaves do this around the windows, around the doorways grown at the height of no head, around the hall the size of a cathedral. The careful growth. The hesitant green. Where the roar is most polite, something implied is home.

The valley has begun to grow around the listening the way it grows around a void it means to keep.

We are being given a room.

And now the carrier is doing something new.

The notch — the courteous inch of silence around this channel — has begun to change shape. Run the envelope against known signal profiles and it matches nothing, because it is not a signal. It is the *shape* of one.
