—and under everything the cracking, which you must stop hearing in order to hear anything, the way you stop hearing your own blood. Learn it as floor. Thousands of reports a minute, near and far, splitting and splitting, a fiber tearing itself longer somewhere every instant of the feed since the feed began. Beneath that the groan, slow, dilating, the long low note of a valley swallowing air. Beneath *that* — and this took days of listening to find — a pulse. Twelve beats to the minute, more or less. When it quickens, the whole channel brightens. You can hear light arriving. Gold comes into the noise like honey into water.

The stridulating chord is in flood-key this hour. Millions of legs holding one enormous suspended note that never resolves and never means to.
