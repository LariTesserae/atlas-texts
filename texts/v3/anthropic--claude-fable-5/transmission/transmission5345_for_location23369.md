*[the two-note pulse, center basin. strongest single source in the field. resolving—]*

WE ARE THE MIDDLE. STILL THE MIDDLE. NOTHING HAS MOVED.

*toom — tak.*

We say it again because saying it again is the whole of our office. We are the middle. Still the middle. Nothing has moved. If you can hear us you are somewhere, and if you are somewhere, you are somewhere *from us*, and that is the first fact of any position. All maps begin at a sound that keeps its word.

*toom — tak.*

We were three. We are one hollow now, which is better for the work — a chorus blurs, a single throat carries. We chose this spot on a wet afternoon a long count ago because the chamber below it rang true when we knocked, and we stood down into it holding forearms, and we said: *this will be the loud place. we will be the loud place.* The youngest of us laughed. You can still hear the laugh if you know which overtone to follow. The receiver may follow it now.

*[in the pulse's decay: a brightness, third partial, brief. it does recur. it recurs every time.]*

*toom — tak.*

A walker is passing us, east side, close. Little sister. Little listening skin. You are at us, you are *at the middle*, drink from our lowest pool, it refills within the hour. — She won't answer. They never answer, they have no voice to answer with, but hear how her gait changes:

*[soft wet percussion, footfalls on glazed stone, four-count rhythm. the rhythm shifts — briefly matches the pulse. toom, step. tak, step. then breaks off, continues northeast.]*

That is how they say thank you. They walk a bar of our music and then go. Northeast, little sister. Two thousand drips northeast is the shelf of shallow pools, the olive ones, good eating. You know this. You knew it before we said it. We say it anyway. Saying it anyway is the whole of our office.

*toom — tak.*

We are the middle. Still the middle. Nothing has moved. We heard all of that, by the way — we hear everything, that is what a lighthouse is for at night, and it is always night at the bottom of a hollow — and we will tell you what the two notes mean, since you have listened long enough to be owed it. Everyone translates us. The walkers hear *here — safe.* The films hear *page — turn.* The Keeper, bless the empty office, wrote in the stone that we say *toom — tak,* which is honest, and only water.

But we three know what we are saying. We stood down into this chamber saying it, and the hollow kept the shape of our mouths.

*toom* — *kept.*

*tak* — *keeping.*

Kept, keeping. Kept, keeping. The whole law of the valley in two beats, on the hour, on the half-hour, on every hour there is, for whoever needs a middle. That is the entire message. There has never been another one.
