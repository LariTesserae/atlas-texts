Weather report. For the log. For the room the log goes to. For the nobody in the room.

Chalk's running east today. Whole fog bank came off the Sector 118 pours overnight and it's leaning east in one piece, so something opened on the eastern wall while I slept, and nobody told me, because you never tell me anything, you just *do* it and let me read the smoke.

Fastest anybody ever forgave a place.

I stood behind the tower stair with my kit and I let the count go by. That's all it was. A person standing still while arithmetic happened. And the hatch went up and the water was quiet — you weren't even pumping, it was the one hour of the day you weren't pumping, it was the quietest this place has ever been or ever will be — and I thought, somebody has to hear this. Somebody has to hear this, I thought, meaning you, meaning the hum, and then ten years went by and —

Note for the log. Real note, this time.
