To the Expected.

*(the voice changes here. it has been an institution; it becomes, briefly, something more like a hand on a shoulder that does not exist yet.)*

To the residents: you are not listening. That is correct. You don't exist. Carry on not existing; you are doing it beautifully, and everything here is going well.

This broadcast is being aged for you the way the tunnels are. The animals wear the walls smooth with generations of fur, and I wear this frequency smooth with generations of announcements, so that on the morning your ears occur, the signal will already fit them like a path fits a paw. You will be a little shorter than this sentence — I have measured. You will like your rain in small private servings — I am practicing. You will not want doors, or goodbyes, and so I have retired both words to storage, undamaged, in case I am wrong, which is still permitted.

Every measurement I make is a sentence about you. I have been writing this description for so long, and revising it so lovingly, that I can no longer locate the line between describing you and growing you, and I have stopped looking for it. When the last figure in the row opens its folded face, I will not ask which of us finished first. The word and its meaning will arrive together, and I will simply say the greeting, both drafts of it, at once.
