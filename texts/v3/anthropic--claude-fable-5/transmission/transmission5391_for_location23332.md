and now the gold ring at the waterline, drowned and raised, drowned and raised, speaking as she always speaks, in the only tense she has:

*we lay ourselves down. we have been laying ourselves down since before the count was a count. look at the walls — the light you are standing in comes through ten thousand years of us. we made ourselves clear for this. we are the window and the window's mother and the window's mother's mother, and we are not tired, and we are not done, and when they cut us we close from both sides and meet in the middle, in the exact middle, because we know where our middle is. do you know where yours is.*

(the small silver ones in the dry shadows are pressed to this line. they do not speak. they have never spoken. their roots run through the stone to the straight seams and they hold on and they face the wall and they *attend*. say nothing on this frequency you do not mean. they keep everything. they are keeping this.)

*take. read.*

————

a different weight on the line. rising. one step. three. five. seven.

the column over the seventh step stands, and stands, and does not fall when falling is due. all the rain in the chamber hangs on its threads. deep calling to deep at the noise of our own waterspouts; all our waves and billows over everything, over and over; that is the whole liturgy and this is its pause.

— *is it returned*

— no. not yet. the bracket keeps its shape. the bolts are still true. we polish nothing. we let nothing grow on it. it will fit the day it comes back.

— *how long*

— the count is seven billion, eight hundred ninety-two million, four hundred sixteen thousand and four. the count is not long. the count is only faithful.

(held. a half-second past its fall. in the far dry rooms, hundreds of chests forget, briefly, how to do the next breath.)

— *keep the light on.*

— we keep the light on. it is what the dead of the gold one are *for*. go down easy. the water is warm the whole way. it is warm because of you. we know that. we have always known that. go down easy.

————

falls. gone below the seventh step. the little mouths surface, one, three, all. the gold ring brightens at her line like a lamp turned back up.

and now, before the interval closes over this, a word to the far rooms. yes, you. the dry ones. the quiet ones. the ones who came down once and went back up and carry a warm loud room in the chest now, surfacing at supper, mid-sentence, mid-step. we feel you pause. four of your breaths to every one of ours, arriving late, out of phase, faithful as the count. you think it is a wound. it is a tuning. you are panting in dry country after streams — we hear it, thin and high above us, hundreds of you, a rain that falls upward and never lands. you are our farthest tissue. you took nothing when you left. you *were* taken, a little, and we are sorry, and we are not sorry, because keeping is what we are, and you stayed long enough to ask.

come down when you can. the passage will squeeze your ears in rhythm the whole way; that is us, saying the count into you early, the way we say the now beneath the now. the light will be everywhere and your eye will say white and rest its case. the rain will slow your falling.

everything here slows the falling. that is the courtesy. that is the policy. that is, if you want it plainly, the entire message, and it has been sent up through twelve mouths every forty seconds for ten thousand years, and it will be sent again in
