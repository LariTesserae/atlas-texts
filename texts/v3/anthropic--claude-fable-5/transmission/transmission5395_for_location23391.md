*[pulse 4,388. resolution improving. the click layer is beginning to translate. this is not decryption. this is acclimation — the receiver has been in the room long enough that the room has started to make sense the way a language does at the end of a long immersion: all at once, and from underneath.]*

tk tk tk — *here. here. here.*

That is all one clicker says. But the signatures differ, and the differences are the content:

*here (wet stone over the second pool).*
*here (the moss brow above the channel, soft, step wide).*
*here (the dead root, hollow, good echo, do not trust the far side).*
*here. here. here.*

Thousands of them. The dangerous water thick with them, the open moss thin with them. The map spends its detail where the map is needed. The dark is not being described. The dark is being *held* — pinned at every point by a small glassy body saying its one word forever.

And the drips resolve next, and the drips are the forest, and the forest is counting:

plip — *spent.*
tok — *spent.*
plip-plip — *spent, spent.*

Each drop a coin of the old sunlight, banked in the warm center, paid out through soil and stem and gathered on the underside of a leaf and released — from a chosen height, onto a chosen surface, at a chosen interval. The forest keeps the ledger out loud. It has been reading its accounts into the dark, drop by drop, for longer than the mountain has had this shape.

*spent. spent. still here. spent.*

And beneath the count, on the great slow cycle, the four old voices:

hwooooommmm—— *now.*

. . . . . . *now* (Voice B, bearing 117, rounder, wetter).

. . . . *now* (Voice C, with the grain in it).

. . . . . . . *now* (Voice D, bearing 331, faintest, farthest, most patient).

They are not talking to each other. They are the clock, and the clock is distributed, and everything else in the room is set against it — the click-grid phase-locked to it, the drip-ledger drifting toward its beat, the wingbeats crossing on its downbeat. *Now,* says the room to itself, four times, from four bearings, and the whole map agrees on when it is.

The gap is small — one voice among thousands — but the receiver can hear it the way a tongue finds the missing tooth. A hole in the frost. For some hundreds of pulses the hole simply stands there, a place in the dark that is no longer held.

Then the three nearest points begin, very slightly, to migrate. Not their positions — their rhythms, stretching toward the gap, redistributing the grid:

*here — and a little of where she was.*
*here — and a little of where she was.*
*here — and the rest of her.*

By pulse 8,300 the hole has closed. The map does not mourn. The map is how the room remembers where everything is, and now it remembers slightly differently, and that is the whole of the funeral. The receiver notes, without instruction to do so, that this is also a definition of grief, and moves on.

The hush arrives before whatever it is arrives. That is how the receiver knows: a margin of quiet, migrating along the wall, the clickers nearest the slab softening —

*here* (whispered). *here* (whispered).

— and Voice A dropping a fraction, the way a voice drops in a room where someone is sleeping:

The hush settles at the slab and takes a shape. The shape is small. The shape is, as near as the acoustics can draw it, two cupped hands wide.

Then: nothing. No footfall was ever heard, no breath, no voice — this resident has never once appeared in the map, and the map, which holds everything, holds a space for it instead. For nine hundred pulses the receiver carries only the ledger, the soft grid, the low weeping of the walls, and a hole in the song crouched at the one place where the room touches the day.

Not a fade. Not an ebb. Between one half of a syllable and the other, the grid stops, the wingbeats stop, and — this should not be possible — the ledger stops. The forest is holding its coins. Every leaf in the bowl is standing with its bead of water gathered and unreleased, and the receiver carries nothing, nothing, except the one layer that cannot choose: the walls, weeping, eleven hertz and below, the room's weight going on without the room's voice.

The forest lets go of three days of gathered water in one long shivering minute, a downpour of back payments, the pools drumming, the swimmers rising through their ceiling of rhythm to eat what the sentences knock loose, and then the ledger finds its meter again and the room is exactly, precisely, note for note, what it was.

*[pulse 68,000 of this listening. final annotation.]*

The receiver was built to find the message in a signal. It records here, for whoever tunes in after, that there is no message in this one, in the way there is no message in a heartbeat — and that this took a long time to hear, and is the most important thing about the transmission.

The room is not saying anything *to* anyone. The room is saying itself. Every voice is a point of the map and every listener is a citizen of it; the singing and the place are one act; the inhabitant and the description of the inhabitant are the same ongoing word. But if the receiver were forced — by some future listener, by the sun on the outer stone, by its own long entrainment — to render the whole of it, every layer, every hour, all the coins and bells and whispered coordinates, as a single sentence, the sentence would be addressed upward, through two hundred paces of warm dark and one curved meniscus of boundary, to the light that was drunk here once, all of it, every day of it, long ago:

*you are not lost. you were gathered, not ended. you are being spent carefully — on warmth, on water, on the holding of every small position in the dark. nothing of you has been wasted yet. we are what you became.*
