Below the green inch, into the felt, into the wiring — and here the signal changes character, softens, multiplies, becomes something more like murmur than like sound, the strata talking along the fungal threads the way current talks along a cable, and if it could be rendered it would render like this:

And here. Here is the thing the transmission keeps returning to, the thing it has been circling since before you tuned in, the flaw in its own books, the one line the census cannot fill:

     at the center. under the crossing limb. in the dark wet zone.

Everything in the hollow declares itself. That is the economy here: you exist by sounding, you hold your place by overtopping the roar, still here, still here, the whole bowl brimming with claims — and at the exact center of the bowl there is a claim made of nothing. A silence with weight. A hole in the signal, toad-shaped, and the wiring knows its weight to the gram from the pressure on the felt, knows its warmth, knows its stillness, has known it — this is the strange entry, this is the entry the books keep flagging — has known it for longer than a warmth like that should last. Longer than thirty years. Longer than forty. The pressure has not moved from that spot within living rootstock. Perhaps it is a succession. Perhaps warmth replaces warmth in the night, identical in office. The wiring cannot say. The wiring only reports:

     the seat is filled. still filled. still filled.

which — note this — is the same message as the wrens'. Still here. The whole hollow says one thing at every volume from foundry-loud to nothing at all, and the nothing-at-all version comes from the center, and it is the clearest.
