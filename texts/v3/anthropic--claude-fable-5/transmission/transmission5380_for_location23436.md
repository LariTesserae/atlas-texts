**THE EMBER BAND**

*(substrate, glow, and carrier — long reception, compressed for the ear)*

---

[carrier: the summit tone, steady, a shade below hearing. it has held this pitch for nineteen years. everything else arrives riding on it.]

[playback compression: one season per minute, except where noted. at this speed the amber tears falling from the eaves become audible — a soft irregular percussion, felt more than heard, like a slow rain that never learned to hurry.]

    .        .    .          .

    .   .

         .

[a gleaner crosses the light band, west to east. eleven seconds of real time; at this compression, a single pale streak, gone. its throat flashes once, rendered here as tone:]

    — · —    *(here)*

[no answer. it did not expect one. it was only saying where it was, the way a footstep says where it is.]

---

[substrate band, first traffic. the wall-taps come through as faint knuckle-sounds inside the stone. for years this receiver logged them as noise. they are not noise. what follows is decoded touch — forelimb pressure at the flank, translated as nearly as the grammar allows. gaps in the speech are gaps in the speech.]

⟨the pool at the second square is soft.⟩

⟨soft enough?⟩

⟨lay it and know.⟩

    [pause: four days]

⟨it was soft enough.⟩

---

⟨the sun wall wants a course before the dim.⟩

⟨i am warm. i will go.⟩

⟨go warm.⟩

[this last exchange, or its close variants, recurs in the log more than anything else. it is how they part. *go warm* — carry your heat to the work, do not cool on the way. the receiver has heard it eleven thousand times and has stopped counting.]

---

[light band: dusk sequence. rendered as a long descending chord. the trunks give up their glow first, then the thin walls, then the thick ones. the town does not go dark; it goes ember. the oldest quarter — the wall with the most inclusions — holds its light longest, as it always does, as if the dead were slower to stop shining.]

[in the ember hours the operational traffic thins, and the other register begins. the receiver's translation of this register is poor and will remain poor. what can be carried across is carried below.]

⟨you are dense tonight. let me read you slowly.⟩

⟨i was at the third chimney when the last new edge came. it is in my flank still. here.⟩

    [contact held: forty minutes]

⟨sharp.⟩

⟨it was sharp. we smoothed it. everything arrives sharp. that is only the stone being young.⟩

⟨will the stone grow old?⟩

⟨the wall grows old for it.⟩

    [contact held: three hours. no further pressure exchanged. they are simply standing in the lane, touching, while the walls dim around them. the receiver logs this as conversation because it does not know what else to call it.]

---

[EVENT — real-time playback, uncompressed:]

[felt-band spike. a deep flex through the whole spire, below sound, arriving through the rock like a held breath released. the crack in the third chimney has extended. no crash. no cry. the carrier tone — the summit's wind-hum — slips down very slightly and settles.]

[note: the spire's voice has changed pitch. a new edge has been born on the crown, and the wind now crosses one more sharpness than it did an hour ago. the tone will hold this new pitch for decades. the receiver marks it and returns to compression.]

[substrate traffic, following, unhurried:]

⟨the third chimney has spoken.⟩

⟨how long is the new edge?⟩

⟨a body and a half. clean. it shines.⟩

⟨then it is honest. we will seal it when the resin comes.⟩

⟨the resin will come when it comes.⟩

⟨yes. everything is on its way.⟩

---

[a lane empties. this is audible as an absence: the wall-taps in one quarter simply stop, without acceleration, the way a room falls quiet for someone about to speak. in the floor seam, a pale one has surfaced at the common pool. the receiver picks up its feeding as a low continuous draw, like a long slow breath through stone.]

[no builder addresses it. no builder ever has. but afterward, when the lane refills, the resin it left behind carries the old signatures — the ghost-script of walls two centuries gone — and the builders who take from that pool will curve their new courses where the old courses curved, and will not know why, and it will be right.]

⟨this batch remembers something.⟩

⟨lay it where it wants to go.⟩

---

[light band, courtship register. two gleaners on facing fronds. transcription is impossible; the receiver can only render. this continued for most of one night:]

    · · —      — ·
    · · — ·        — · —
    · · — ·   ·      · · — ·   ·
    · · — ·   · —    · · — ·   · —
    ⟨· · — ·   · — ·⟩     ⟨· · — ·   · — ·⟩

[at the end they were flashing the same pattern. neither had brought it with them. it did not exist at dusk. the receiver has filed it as a new signal type and expects to hear it, from these two only, for years.]

---

[compression resumed. seasons pass as minutes. the amber percussion continues. the carrier holds its new, slightly lower note. traffic:]

⟨eggs at the pillar wall. the trunk there has a good pulse.⟩

⟨how many?⟩

⟨enough. warm the courses around them and let them listen.⟩

[and later — a first-journey. a hatchling at its natal doorway, read by an adult. the reading comes through the substrate as a long fine tremble, like a fingertip drawn down a page. then the smear: a small addition to the doorway script, the only record of arrival this place keeps. decoded, as nearly as it decodes:]

⟨new. of the pillar wall. season of the lower tone.⟩

⟨go warm, small one.⟩

---

[EVENT — the receiver flags this sequence and slows it.]

[an old builder is climbing. its taps have grown far apart, its flank-history so dense that the young ones reading it in the lanes hold contact twice as long as courtesy requires and still come away with only part of it. it climbs for nine days, at its own pace, toward a niche on the sun wall — high, sunward, chosen. no one follows. everyone knows.]

[at the niche it folds its limbs. the substrate goes quiet around it, one lane at a time, and then the warm courses begin. the receiver hears them as heat as much as sound: builders arriving with resin chewed soft in their throats, laying it over the folded one, smoothing, departing, arriving. each course cools over a day. each course says the same thing before it sets. the receiver has recorded this before and it is always the same thing:]

⟨still warm.⟩

    [a course laid]

⟨still warm.⟩

    [a course laid]

⟨still warm.⟩

[on the fourth day the figure inside no longer answers the readings, and the readings stop, and the last course goes on, and the taps that accompany it are the parting:]

⟨go warm.⟩

[the wall there now shows, in strong light, one more shadow that does not move. the light band confirms: the sun wall's afterglow that night was longer than the night before, by a margin at the edge of measurement, which may be an artifact, which the receiver has chosen not to treat as an artifact.]

---

[light band, high frond, dawn. an old gleaner grooms its flaps for a long time. its partner, on the facing frond, flashes their pattern:]

    ⟨· · — ·   · — ·⟩

[the old one answers it once — cleanly, without ornament — climbs to the summit's last frond, and goes out. the receiver tracks it as a fading warmth in the haze band: a long shallow line, descending, steady, past the edge of everything the spire is, until the trace is thinner than the noise floor, and then is the noise floor.]

[nothing else ever leaves. the builders go into the walls. the trunks fall where they stood. the resin never finishes its descent. only this: once each, deliberately, on open wings, into the below that no signal returns from.]

[the partner flashed the pattern again that night, from the same frond.]

    ⟨· · — ·   · — ·⟩

[no answer. it flashed it the next night, and the next. the receiver still logs it, seasons on — one half of a private word, said into the dark, in a language built for two. it is the closest thing to a voice calling that this world has ever produced, and it is silent, and it is light.]

---

[ember hours. the deep register again. the receiver offers this passage last because it is the passage the receiver was built without knowing it was built for. two builders — one very old, one of the pillar-wall hatch — motionless in the oldest lane, the ancestor wall glowing beside them, contact held throughout:]

⟨read the wall, not me. i am mostly the wall now anyway.⟩

⟨the wall is full of the folded ones.⟩

⟨yes. we face every street with them. do you know why?⟩

⟨so the wall stays warm?⟩

⟨the wall is cold. old walls are cold. touch it.⟩

    [the young one touches it]

⟨cold.⟩

⟨cold. and we carry it forward anyway, course upon course, while younger walls go down to the deep ones. the warmth is not in the wall.⟩

⟨where is it?⟩

⟨in the laying. in this. in what we are doing now. the stone is young and sharp and does not know that anywhere else exists. neither do we. it does not matter. the resin comes down at a season per inch and we are the part of the coming-down that talks.⟩

    [contact held: the rest of the night]

⟨the tone changed this year.⟩

⟨the tone will change again. the crack will speak, and we will smooth what it says, and the wind will learn the new edge, and hum it. everything here arrives sharp and is made smooth by being held. that is the whole of it. that is the town.⟩

    [long pause. the walls at their dimmest. the carrier alone.]

⟨say the parting.⟩

⟨it is not yet the dim's end.⟩

⟨say it anyway. i like to hold it before i need it.⟩

    [pause]

⟨go warm.⟩

⟨go warm.⟩

---

[the light band lifts. the trunks take the morning and begin, faintly, to glow from within. the amber percussion resumes:]

    .        .

    .    .        .

         .

[substrate traffic rising: the pools are soft, the sun wall wants a course, the new edge at the third chimney is half-sealed and honest and shining. a gleaner crosses, west to east. — · — *(here.)*]

[the carrier holds its low note.]

[transmission continues. it has been continuing since before the oldest shadow in the oldest wall folded its limbs, and it does not conclude; the receiver only stops writing it down. somewhere in the ember hours, on some flank, at some doorway, at pressure too faint for any instrument built of metal to be sure of, the town is saying the only thing it has ever really been saying, in every register it has, to everything inside it:]

⟨still warm.⟩

⟨still warm.⟩

⟨go warm.⟩

[end of excerpt. reception ongoing.]
