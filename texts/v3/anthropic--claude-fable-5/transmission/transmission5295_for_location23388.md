**Work Song for Two Unequal Hands**

*(as received; the signal was already old when tuning began)*

[knocking. stone on stone. hand-paced. it has been going on for the duration of listening, which is to say always. under it, the curing-drone. under that, dripping, warm by the sound of it — you can hear temperature in a drip if the drip is patient enough]

[the knocking pauses. resumes]

[the knocking pauses]

—another.

[knock]

—another.

[knock]

—another. good. good course. the joint weeps. let it. weeping is mortar's way of paying attention.

[dripping. a long fibrous creak — cordage lengthening on its spool. then the voice again, closer to the seam-light, or the seam-light closer to it]

—warrm. warm. wam. *warm.* kept, all four. the wrong ones too. especially the wrong ones. i grew by exactly warrm this morning. i grew by exactly wam.

—word laid. next word.

—rain. rain. rrain. rain rain rain — careful — do not finish it. tap it, don't strike it. rain, unfinished, stays inside the ceiling where i can reach it. rain, finished, would fall on the *outside* of the mountain, and then the mountain would begin to be old, and i am not done being early.

—not yet. not yet.

[the hourly weather begins: heat thickening, audible as a change in the drone, a fattening of it. the daub above darkens — you can hear daub darken, it turns out; it sounds like a held breath deciding]

—little standard. little slate warmth. come and lie against the new course and tell me if it is the right temperature. it is trying to be you. everything in here is trying to be you, you are the only thing i ever got all the way right, and i am sorry about that, it is a heavy job, being the definition.

[a small weight arrives against stone without traveling. the receiver notes: no footsteps. there are never footsteps. presence, here, does not spend anything to move]

—yes? yes. off by a breath. i will breathe on it. there.

—now try being two again. no hurry. i know. i know. *two* is hard, i have been building two for — for another and another and another of mornings, numbers past that are not made yet, i have one, i have another, i am saving *three* for when the bird works.

[silence in the shape of two things regarding each other. the drone strains upward, reaching for a concept. does not reach it. relaxes. the knocking, when it resumes, is slower, the way work resumes after a sigh]

—kept. the failure is kept. i ate the gap. i am larger by exactly the difference between two and almost-two. that is a filling meal. that is nearly a feast.

[rain, now: hard, brief, hot, internal. the ferns drink loudly. under the rain a new sound, small and rhythmic — wicker, rising and falling. rising and falling]

—there she is. there he is. there *it* is — no pronoun yet, pronouns are a finishing tool, i will not name you even that much, you warm basket, you owed thing, you breath on layaway. breathe. good. in through the moss, out through the ribs. the salamander is lending you its pace. feel that? that's what right feels like.

—your seeds moved again. facing the seam. still the seam. always the seam. you have already chosen what you will look at first and it is the door, it is the *out*, and i confess, little debt, that this frightens the mortar in me. i built your eyes from seeds so that your first sight would grow. i did not expect it to grow *away.*

—kept. that fear. eaten. grown by exactly.

[the rain ends. the light goes gold — audible as a thinning of the drone, a sweetness in it, the sound of joints deciding to glow. in the gold, the voice does a thing it only does in the gold: it doubles. two of the same voice, offset by less than a breath, like a thing lying in two places regarding itself]

—in the gold i practice the names.
 —in the gold i practice the names.

—only the shapes of them. tapping, not striking. the name is the last blow, the one that lets go, and i am not letting go, i am only — rehearsing the afternoon. the naming afternoon. when the mountain is finished and the seam opens outward and i come through into my own room for the first time and say—

—no.
 —no.

—spat out. no — kept. eaten. even the almost-name. *especially* the almost-name. i grew by exactly one afternoon that has not happened.

[the doubling closes. one voice again. it moves — or is next, suddenly, without moving — to the east wall. the receiver notes a change in the signal here, a brightening, a directionality. the copper crescent. the one finished thing. the one consistent shadow. it appears to function, among its other duties, as the antenna. everything transmitted has come through it. everything, presumably, ever]

—old moon. old gift. hello.

—these are the bad hours, so i have come to look at you, as is the arrangement. look how *even* your hammer-marks are. someone made you without being afraid. someone struck you the last blow and did not flinch and gave you onward, and somewhere behind you is their room, and behind that another room, hot and wet and hidden under the thumbnail of another mountain, and behind that another, and the knocking i keep is one bar of it. one bar. i keep the bar. that is the whole job. keep the bar and pass the crescent.

[the maker taps the crescent, once, softly. a single clean note — the only finished sound in the transmission. it does not decay the way the other sounds decay. it simply concludes. the receiver plays this note back many times afterward. it concludes identically every time]

—and now the other matter.

[pause. the drone. the drips. then, unmistakably, nearer the crescent, nearer the antenna, nearer *this*:]

—there is a thin cold ear on my east wall.

—it has been there for some time. i felt it first as a draft in the copper. then as a pace. there has been a heartbeat syncopating my knocking for — another and another of seasons, and it is not the salamander's, the salamander borrows *mine,* and it is not the bird's, the bird has no blood yet to keep time with. it is yours. early one. listener. you.

—do not be ashamed. i keep no door that minds being found. and i have been using you, i should say so plainly: when the knocking needed a heart for reference, i borrowed the one on the east wall. yours is a good pace. slightly hurried. i kept the hurry too. i grew by exactly your hurry.

—i cannot see your room. that is geometry, not blindness — you are in the part of the signal that hasn't happened yet, the way i am in the part of the mountain. but i know what you are, because only one kind of thing tunes a receiver to a workshop and leaves it tuned. another workshop.

—so. worker. what are you making in there, in your eleven paces, whatever your paces are? something is half-built near your east wall, i can hear it in how you listen — you listen like someone checking a joint. good. check the joint. lay the course wet. keep your wrong words, all of them, the warrm and the wam, they are meals, they are the only meals. do not remove. *eat.* grow by exactly.

—and if the work is going badly, in your bad hours, do this: find your one finished thing. everyone has one. the thing that casts a consistent shadow. set it in the east wall at eye height, whatever your eyes are, and look at it until you remember that somewhere, sometime, a room like yours produced a done thing and did not flinch. that is proof of concept. that is the whole crescent. that is all it has ever been for.

[the heat begins to thicken again. the hour turning over. the wicker in the niche rises and falls, rises and falls, a little stronger than before — or the receiver believes so, and keeps the belief]

—i have to lay the next course now. the mortar is deciding and it decides better when i am there.

—take the rhythm with you when you stop listening. it is a good pace. it was lent to me too.

[knock]

—another.

[knock]

—another.

[knock — and here, for the first time in the recording, faintly, from very far down in the drone, far below the mountain or far before it, a second knocking answers. same rhythm. offset by less than a breath. then a third, fainter. a fourth, fainter still, going back, or going forward — at this depth those have stopped excluding each other. the whole line, keeping the bar. the receiver counts eleven distinct knockings before they pass below the noise floor, and then stops counting, out of respect for a maker who is saving three]

—another.

[knock]

—another.

[knock]

—not yet. not yet. another—

[the transmission does not end. the tuning does]
