*[h+0000 — lock steady. ground-coupled. the line is warm.]*

—drone at the floor of everything, eleven cycles to the second, less a sound than a place to stand. It has not varied in the whole of the lock. On top of it, rain: not falling so much as leaking, ten thousand drip-points each with its own small pitch, and beneath the drip-points the terraces sorting the rainfall into music as it descends — pool lip at a true note, holding; the pool below it a shade flat; and somewhere on that flat pool a patient knocking, shell against stone, grinding at the lip. The flat note is being corrected. It will take days. The corrector does not appear to be in a hurry, and knocks in a rhythm that suggests it has never been in a hurry, and comes from a line of things that were never in a hurry, all the way down.

Under the terrace-music, off to one side of the field, a low cluster of notes drifting very slowly — twenty, thirty sustained purrs, close-voiced, moving together across the swale at the speed of grazing. The chord loses a note at its edge, gains it back. One purr at the rim is held a little louder than the rest, deliberately, like a hand keeping a tent-rope taut. Inside the cluster, briefly, a smaller purr, higher, doubling its mother's note a fraction sharp and then finding it. Finding it. Holding it.

Above all of this, traffic. Click-trains running the upper register in bursts too fast to count, crossing, answering, overlapping — the dark being read aloud to itself, continuously, at a resolution the receiver cannot follow, only admire. When one of the readers moves, a taut line hums somewhere and publishes the movement, and the movement is never denied. Nothing up there is hidden. Nothing up there has ever been hidden. The whole canopy is a signed confession, ongoing.

A long note crosses the field of the signal, left to right, swooping — a single sustained voice bending under its own speed, pitch falling as it comes on, correcting, correcting, correcting, and then a soft chord of struck rigging and the note stops mid-syllable, satisfied. Something has landed exactly where it sang itself. There is no other way to describe it and the receiver has stopped trying: the glide is the song and the song is the instrument landing itself, and the log will hereafter mark these events as *arrivals* and leave it at that. There have been forty-one arrivals since lock. Every one ended safely. The dark up there is crossed on pure voice, all night, except there is no night, except it is always night, and either way they keep flying.

*[h+0240 — second fundamental detected, bearing NE, 11 cycles and a fraction]*

A neighbor. Another drone, kilometers off, a hair sharp of the local one, and where the two notes lie over each other the air begins to pulse — a slow swelling and fading, twenty-two seconds to the cycle, older than anything that hears it. And in the null of each pulse, in the two or three seconds where the great notes cancel and the floor of the world drops out, a small voice calls. Only there. Only then. It has fitted its entire song into the gap between two machines, and it waits out the swell in silence, and sings in the hole, and has evidently been doing so for as long as the hole has existed. Its call is answered from the next null over. They are courting in the seams of the interference. The receiver logs this and then, for some time, does nothing but listen to it.

*[h+0413 — new tone. onset gradual. struck-metal quality, sustained.]*

Out of the floor: a ring. Not from the air — from the line itself, from the buried warm line the lock rides on, a bronze tone rising as something settles its weight onto the conduit and begins to draw. The ring is sour. It wavers; it beats against itself; whatever is making it is young and rings the way the young ring, approximately, hopefully, wrong. The local drone does not respond.

Forty minutes later a second ring, lower, from further up the line, and this one comes on like a poured thing — pure, wide, steady, a note with decades inside it. The sour ring falters, listens, and begins, audibly, to *copy*. Reaching for the elder's pitch, missing, reaching. And now — logged over the following three hours, compressed here to a sentence — the drone leans. The eleven-cycle floor of the world shifts by an amount at the edge of measurement, in the direction that steadies the old one's ring, and the old one's ring settles into the new note like a hand into a hand, and the young one, still wrong, is a little less wrong than before.

And then nothing.

Not silence. The rain continues; rain is not afraid. The drone continues; engines are not afraid. But everything that can choose has chosen, all at once, across the whole audible ground — the click-traffic gone, the terrace-knocking gone, the ox-chord thinned to something below carrying, the null-singer of the interference gaps letting its null pass empty. For the first time in the log, the receiver can hear its own noise floor. The absence has a shape. The shape is moving. It crosses the signal slowly, south to west, a hole with edges, and everything around the hole holds still and gives it nothing to be a hole *in*.

Once — placed, deliberate, exquisite — a single tick. One drop's worth of sound, set down in the middle of the quiet like bait.

Nothing answers it. Nothing has answered that trick in a long, long time.

The hold lasts fifty-one minutes. Then a vine hums, far at the western edge, then another nearer, then the knocking resumes mid-phrase as if between one knock and the next nothing had occurred, and the chorus closes back over the kilometer from the edges inward, the way water closes, and the log cannot find the moment the hole stopped being there. It is simply not there. Until it is again, somewhere, it is not there.

*[h+0902]*

The canopy is doing the engine.

Dead on pitch. Thirty, forty voices holding eleven cycles' worth of overtones in chorus, so exactly that the receiver's discriminator fails — for a full minute the log shows two engines where there is one engine and one troop of liars. Then the imitation collapses, all at once, into a tumbling downward cascade of clicks that the receiver declines to classify as anything other than what it obviously is.

Then they do the rain. Then the old ox at the tent-rope, her exact note, so that far below the real ox falls silent, listening to herself. More laughter.

Then they do something else.

— and under all of it, the whole time, the drone: dragging, laboring, pulled flat under the load, but *held*. Never lost. The floor of the world flexed and did not go.

Then passing. Then the long unclenching: rain thinning back to its usual indecision, the terraces overfull and every pool a half-step sharp until the lips carve themselves true again, which they are already doing, which the knocking — resumed, patient, everywhere — is already helping them do. And one by one, out of the settling, the engines come back to pitch. The local one first, then the northeastern neighbor a hair sharp of it as ever, each a little out of true with the others, by design or by wear or by something the distinction stopped covering long ago, and the slow beating starts again, twenty-two seconds to the cycle, the pulse the whole dark calls home. In the first null after the storm, small and undamaged and exactly on time, the gap-singer sings.
