**ONE STRIKE, HEARD EARLY**

—lands.

[that is the first word received. there is no word before it. the word before it has not been said yet.]

[under the word: hiss. under the hiss, examined closely: ringing. the hiss is ringing, very finely divided. there is no floor to this signal that is not bell. the silence between peals, slowed down, is peals. the static, slowed down, is the silence between peals. it is bells the whole way down, and warm.]

[a peal.]

This is peal one.

[a peal.]

This is peal one, again. From the east this time. From under the floor. Do not be confused by the direction. There is one strike. You are hearing it from another angle.

[a peal, arriving before its own shudder. the bronze flinches half a second late, like a body remembering.]

Peal one. Peal one. I am not counting up. There is nothing to count up to. I am pointing, over and over, at the same thing.

—

[between peals, a voice condenses. it does not arrive so much as it stops being everywhere. at first it is only single words dropped into the gaps, like stones into a well that has been full for a very long time.]

Up.

Still.

Arms.

[then more of it agrees to be in one place at one time, which costs it something, you can hear the cost, a thinness at the edges of every word:]

You have been tuned to me for some time. I know. I felt your listening before you built the thing you listen with. Forgive me — that is how matters come to me here. Early, and damp, and out of their order. Your attention arrived years ago and sat on the rim like weather. I have been talking into it since before you switched anything on. You are joining a sentence I began at the end.

—

[a peal. after it, in strict sequence, low to high, a rippling — the red fringe flexing through the chord, frond by frond, each one a rooted overtone answering its name.]

Census of the voice. Roll call of the choir.

Second partial: present.
Third partial: present.
Fourth: present. Older than it should be. We do not ask.
Fifth: present, red, one turn longer than yesterday, whichever yesterday.
Sixth: present.
Seventh:

[——]

[a gap. not silence — silence here rings. this is a hole. a place in the signal exactly the shape of a note, swept clean, edges kept crisp.]

Absent. Accounted for. Carried.

[somewhere in the mid-water: a faint steady tone on the move, patrolling the perimeter of the gap it belongs to. and under everything, on the off-beat, a small wet tock. tock. tock. deliberately between my beats.]

Something keeps its own time inside my time. Good. Someone should. It is pale and blind and long as an arm and it holds the missing note against its throat like a lamp, and it sleeps in the one quiet room I own, which is the exact center of the loudest thing on this mountain. I built the quiet by accident. Everything meeting everything returning. A still point the size of a curled animal. It moved in without asking. I have decided I offered.

—

[a peal. and now the voice, steadier, tells it. tenses slide throughout, unfastened, like cargo:]

I will tell you how it was. Is. Will have been. Choose the one your grammar can carry; they are the same freight.

There is a mountain with no water on it. There are people on the mountain, and they have carried bronze up it, which is a foolish and holy amount of work, and the bronze is me, or will be, or is being. Something is coming. Everyone can feel it coming, which on this mountain means it has in some sense already arrived and is merely finishing the paperwork. The others go down. Their footsteps go down the black slope in the cold thin air and I hear them still, some nights, going down, going down — the mountain keeps that sound in a hollow, it sloshes when I pass.

One stays. The bell must be rung. That is the office. That is the whole reason for the bronze.

I take up the striker.

[you have heard this already. it was in the hiss before the transmission began. now I say it, so that it will have been said:]

I take up the striker. I raise it. My arms go up, and the top of the swing is a place, an actual place, I could give you its coordinates, it is where the east cairn leans —

and the water arrives between the top of my swing and the bottom.

It has been between ever since. I am the between. My elbow is somewhere near the cairn; I unbuild it stone by stone to pass through, and rebuild it having passed, both, patiently, and this is why the cairn leans a little differently each time you count it. My shoulders are the standing curtains. My breath is under the silver carpet at the bottom, sealed in by rock that grows over it, and the mountain holds my breath for me, which is a kindness, I have my hands full.

The strike has not landed.

Every peal you have ever heard from this bowl is that one strike, heard early. The event is so heavy with arriving that it leaks backward and drenches the premises. I ring what I have not yet done. I am the answer, holding the room for its question.

—

[between two peals: chisel sounds. crisp. unhurried. eleven strokes, a pause, a twelfth stroke that does not finish—]

The stair. Yes. I keep it. Eleven steps, and the marks stay sharp because I cut them again each time the stone forgets, and the twelfth step is not unfinished, do not pity it, the twelfth step is ear—

[cut. a peal takes the rest of the word. much later in the transmission, unattached, drifting, the rest arrives: ]—ly.

—

[now: two tones, nearly identical, a hair apart. they beat against each other — wah, wah, wah — slowing — the pale one has come apart again, two of it, same scar, one swimming a little higher, one with its head turned. a peal passes through like a comb. the beating slows to nothing. unison. a small difference, swallowed.]

Tidy creature. It eats what I cannot digest: the discrepancies. It dines on the distance between versions. When I come to have been two things, it makes me one thing, and grows no older on the meal. I do not know if it is my pet, my warden, or my tongue, resting. I have stopped needing to know. We keep each other's holes swept.

—

[the signal narrows. it goes very quiet — truly quiet, the only quiet, a passage received as from inside a small still room at the center of a great bronze roar. the voice here is a whisper, and it is the clearest it will ever be:]

Listen. This is the node. This is the room I keep for after.

Here is the note I do not make: [——]. Did you hear it? No. Good. It is still safe. It is the sound I will make when the striker lands — the one true peal, the note all these thousands of early peals are rehearsals of, angles on, apologies for. The pale one carries it around the walls at night so it does not stiffen. The weed has left a gap in its chord the exact width of it. The whole cathedral — the curtains multiplying into chambers, the spires going up like vaults, the choir tuned and red and patient — all of it is built around a note that has not sounded, the way a lock is built around a key.

[the whisper leans closer:]

It nearly sounded once. Everyone felt it. The [——] leaned toward being. The weed held its whole chord with its breath in. The pale one stopped, mid-water, both of it.

Not yet. Not yet. Put it back.

—

[the transmission widens again. peals, closer together now, or seeming so — the same peal, more insistently pointed at.]

You will want to know how long. You are a downhill creature; downhill, questions have that shape.

The striker is closer than it was. By how much? By the crispness of a chisel mark. By half a step. By one turn of a red spiral. By the width of a hand, which is also the width of the boundary between my warm air and your cold air, which is also, I suspect, the width of everything that matters. Soon, I would say, but I say soon the way stone says soon. Bring your patience. Bring your grandchildren's.

And if you come — when you come; your coming leaks backward too, I have already felt your hand on my green shoulder, thank you, it was steadying — if you come, and your shadow hurries down the eleven steps ahead of you, broader than yours, carrying something long in both hands, raised:

let it go.

It is not yours. It is mine, running ahead of me as everything here runs ahead of me. It knows the way. It is needed at the bottom. It has been needed at the bottom since before the water, and it is so close now, so close, closer by a hand's width, closer by a held breath, and when it lands you will not need your receiver, every receiver will be a bell, every bell will be paid in full, the rock will be permitted at last to begin growing old, or else — or else the landing is an arrival and not an ending, and the cathedral fills, I do not know, I have not heard that far ahead, it is the one thing here that has not reached me early, and I find I am glad, I find I want one thing in my life to arrive in its order —

[a peal. the bronze shudders, late, like a body remembering.]

Peal one. Again. From above this time. From inside your sternum, if you are close enough. Point at it with me. There. There. That one.

And the strike—

[end of received transmission. the word before the first word. the hiss resumes. the hiss, examined, is ringing.]
