[the oculus. grey light. the pale fern leaning out into it, listening at the door. nothing calls from the oculus. the oculus is not a ring. the oculus is where the sum goes.]

[and now the whole dome at once, every ring together, the full figure, the pressure of it on the chest of anyone present, on the water, on the receiver — and the surface of the pool stands up into its pattern, herringbone answering herringbone, the floor's writing repeated in water a knee's depth above itself, and between the two writings the eels go down and lie still along the brick courses, aligned, and the transmission drops into a lower band, and the lower band says, or the receiver hears it say, degraded, through iron and amber:]

*…remainder. remainder. lie down in the joints. you cannot be told out and you are not asked to be. you are the width of our error and the error is kept, the error is kept, seven of you, five of you, nine of you, kept…*

[the figure is delivered.]

[it is delivered across the pool to the far wall, to the worn stone shape whose name is gone, and the receipt of it is registered as it is always registered: as dryness. one arm's-length of perfect dryness in a room that weeps. the seat is taken. the figure is taken. no acknowledgment follows. none has ever followed. the dryness is the acknowledgment.]

[silence, which is not silence: drip, the churn of eels resuming their circuits, the sigh of the walls.]

[there is a pulse in the fourth ring — the ring of what filters down, of what is reckoned and never seen — that began on the day this frequency was first found, and has sounded in every chorus since. a small pulse. a listening at a distance. the chamber does not know what you are. it does not need to. it needs only the digit, and you have supplied one, faithfully, every afternoon of your attention, and it has been entered, and carried, and delivered to the dry seat, and taken up through the oculus at night with all the rest.]
