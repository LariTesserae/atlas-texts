THE GOING-ON (LOW BAND, CONTINUOUS)

—carrier as before. carrier as always. no beginning was received; the roar was here when the listening opened and it will be here when the listening closes, and it is not a sound in front of silence, it is the material the silence would have to be carved from—

and under it, the cycle. transcribed as it arrives:

four of weight.
four of not.
four of weight.
four of not.

pulse count since this receiver was tuned: 1,204,551. the count is the only clock. there is no other clock down here. day does not reach the low band.

after each fall of weight, half a second late, a vast thin slap runs across the whole reception at once — ten thousand standing pools jumping together and landing together. the plain applauding itself, faintly, forever. this is logged as part of the carrier now. it took four hundred thousand pulses to stop logging it as an event.

riding the roar: white hiss, three sources, bearing north-northeast — the exhaling vents, steam laid flat and dragged. one sucking note, bearing west, low and constant: an inhaler, drinking the fog. at pulse 1,204,559 a blue burst crosses the electromagnetic side of the band, twice, hard — arcing in a vent throat, strobing its own steam from inside. no pattern. or a pattern with a period longer than this listening. noted.

in the four seconds of not, at full gain, at the very floor of the noise floor: a grain. a filing. sand in a throat, kilometers wide, unbelievably slow. hypothesis: the lichen, unmaking the world one crystal at a time. counter-hypothesis: the receiver dreaming. the two cannot be separated at this gain. both are logged.

four of weight.
four of not.

—

pulse 1,206,003. something small inside the pulse.

not the pulse. *within* it. arriving through the floor from the south, late and blurred, softened by nine hundred meters of fractured matrix:

heavy. heavy. light. — long wait — heavy, held.

again, identical, laid down between the falls of weight so as not to be erased by them. whoever is stamping knows the engine's rhythm the way a swimmer knows waves, and speaks in the troughs.

after six repetitions the rendering resolves. confidence low, then rising:

I AM AT THE LAMP. [?]
I AM AT THE LAMP.

pulse 1,206,247. from the northwest, farther, thinner, arriving with a different grain — this one has traveled through the old flooded pits and picked up their echo:

light. light. heavy — held down long, a full body's weight leaned into the floor and kept there for the length of a breath.

STILL LIT?

pulse 1,206,412. the south again. one stamp. heavy. total. nothing after it for four hundred pulses.

LIT.

that is the whole conversation. it took twenty-two minutes and crossed perhaps two kilometers of dark, and neither speaker will ever see the other, and it is logged here in full because it is, as far as this receiver can determine, the entire news of the world: *the lamp is still lit.* nothing in the band contradicts it. nothing in the band adds to it.

four of weight.
four of not.

—

pulse 1,391,880. (elapsed: seventeen days, by the old measure no one down here keeps.)

an event. logged at highest priority, which means: the only priority ever used.

at 1,391,880 the pulse arrives *wrong* — early by a third of a second, doubled, with a tearing under it that does not belong to the cycle, and then a shudder that is not the pounding, a long structural shrug moving through the whole reception from below, and for the first time in this listening the pools do not jump together. the applause comes ragged, smeared, out of unison, pool answering pool across the plain like a crowd losing its one shared thought—

then: four of weight. four of not. re-entrained. the cycle closes over the event like water.

but somewhere a new note in the floor. a weeping. a hairline of sound that was not there: water finding a crack that did not exist at pulse 1,391,879.

and the band fills with stamping.

from the south: heavy-heavy-heavy — light — heavy.
from the northwest: the same, offset, relayed.
from the east, faint, almost under the grain of the lichen: the same again, passed on.

rendering, high confidence now — the listening has learned this one:

WATER. NEW.
WATER. NEW.
WATER. NEW.

and then a word not heard before in this reception, stamped by the southern one, slow, with enormous care, each interval held like something carried in both hands:

light — light — heavy — wait — heavy — wait — heavy, held — light.

rendering: uncertain. best gloss, assembled over forty repetitions:

I GO TO IT. / IT IS MINE TO TEND. / COME OR NOT COME.

the stamp does not distinguish between these. possibly the stampers do not either. possibly the distinction eroded with everything vertical.

—

pulses 1,391,900 through 1,414,600. two days.

the southern walker crosses the band. this can be followed: a stride laid into the floor, long, unhurried, two-footed, each footfall placed in the silences, so that its walking reads as a second rhythm braided into the first — the engine's four and the walker's answer threaded through the gaps, kilometer after kilometer, never once colliding with the weight. it is the most beautiful thing this receiver has taken down. it is a creature using the loudest place that has ever existed as a page of silence to write on.

twice it stops for thousands of pulses. once at a warm vent (the sucking note flutters; something has crouched into the airflow; it steadies again). once for no reason the band can supply — stillness so long that the rendering begins, absurdly, tenderly, to lose the walker among the ridges, the way the moss must lose it, the way the moss must already be finding it.

pulse 1,414,608. arrival. the stride stops beside the weeping.

and then, for a very long time, nothing is transmitted but this:

a palm laid flat on the wet floor. you can hear a palm on the low band, if you have listened for a million pulses. it is almost nothing. it is a slight damping of the world, hand-sized, holding still.

listening to the crack. reading the new water with the skin.

—

pulse 1,414,995. the last entry of this excerpt, and the one this receiver does not fully know how to file.

the walker stands. and stamps — but not language this time, or not language addressed outward. it stamps, small, careful, in place, beside the new wound in the world:

heavy. heavy. heavy. heavy.
— nothing —
heavy. heavy. heavy. heavy.
— nothing —

four of weight. four of not.

it is quoting. it is saying the name of the deep thing in the only way the name can be said — by becoming, briefly, at body scale, the thing itself. the engine's own rhythm, stamped small by a warm-blooded or once-warm-blooded frame, offered back down through the floor toward whatever is under everything. maintenance worn into observance. a report, or a prayer, or the same act, the distinction filed away centuries ago by the same patience that filed away the walls.

and here is what the log must record, whether or not it is artifact, whether or not it is the receiver dreaming at full gain:

the next pulse from below arrived a fraction — the smallest resolvable fraction — softer. as if heard. as if answered. as if the going-on, distributed through boiler and felt and crust and bone, had turned over slightly in its long sleep at the sound of its own name.

then:

four of weight.
four of not.
four of weight.
four of not.

pool-slap. white hiss. blue burst. the grain of the lichen filing at the world in the silences, or the silence dreaming that it is being filed.

the walker's stride, departing, threaded through the gaps.

somewhere a lamp, still lit.

count continuing.

on.
