**THE GREY HOUR SERVICE**

*[static, which on this frequency is not interference but rain — fine, drifting, more a thickening of the carrier wave than a weather event. beneath it, at the floor of the signal, a tone. it was there before the receiver was switched on. it does not begin. somewhere in the middle distance of the band, a drip. another. long pause. another. no pattern that repeats; no drip that surprises.]*

*[a voice. unhurried. it has been speaking for some time. it is not joined at a beginning, because it does not keep beginnings anymore. it is simply joined.]*

— becoming fog later. Further outlook: fog, then the fog again — the same one, returning at long interval with its droplets in the same arrangement, the day reused because the day was made well. Visibility: fifty kilometers, or further than the eye should be able to reach, or yesterday.

That was the weather. It is always the weather. The weather has held for so long that forecasting is no longer required for it to be forecast. This forecast continues anyway, the way the stairs hollow.

*[pip. pip. — a pause exactly the length of a pip —]*

At the third stroke, the time will be: still.

The third stroke has been delayed. It is expected in four days, at the settlement to the west, where by some orderings it has already sounded. Listeners holding the pause are asked to hold it patiently. All pauses here are completed, given loosely enough distance.

*[drip]*

Weather now for the outlying areas. Weather for the area of —

*[a pause the length of a name]*

— for the area of —

*[a pause the length of a longer name, an older one]*

— and for the area that is all of them, which may be the only area: fog, settling, becoming general. Winds: unobstructed, arriving mostly as pressure. Sea state, for the waters below the cloud-skirts: unreported. Unreported for a very long time. Presumed sea.

*[fine rain thickens on the carrier. the voice waits for it, the way one waits for a cart to pass]*

Here is the news.

There is no news. This is not a failure of reporting. The news finished; what remains is the olds, and the olds are as follows: stone. Water. Green. The tone. A wall in the fourth district re-coursed itself overnight in the hand of the original masons; this is not news, it is masonry. The water level in the great cistern rose a finger's width without rain; this is not news, it is the errand. The grazer has moved sunwise around the largest pocket, a body-length since the last dimming; the moss records it; the moss is the record.

End of the news. Beginning of the news. They have worn into the same edge.

*[drip. drip.]*

The census follows, as it always follows, being made of following.

Population of the plateau: everyone, ever, reduced. Departures: none. Departing is an event. Arrivals: one, expected. Or one, already. The hollow in the third stair has been fitted for the arrival for some ages now; the stair is patient; the stair is, at this point, mostly made of patience.

The census notes, as it always notes, that it was originally read by a reader. The reader wore away at the usual rate. The reading proved durable. You are listening to the reading.

*[the tone, briefly louder, as if turning over in its sleep]*

Now the notices and personal messages. There are always personal messages, though there have been no persons for — the word for how long has worn away; there is only *long*, and under *long*, the tone.

To the walker on the western route, the one made of the grey hour's fog: the cistern is full. It was full when you filled it. It was full before you filled it. Go anyway. The going is the message.

To the fitting that lives in the south wall: the fallen stone by the gate wants a quarter-turn. You know this. You have always known this. This notice is read each cycle out of courtesy, which has outlived the courteous.

To the one at the black pool, holding the raised hand: hold it. The other half of the greeting is walking toward you across four days and one worn-through direction. Our correspondent in the reflections reports the greeting already completed, warmly, in a light this service can no longer produce. Take the report on faith. All our correspondence arrives before its events, or long after; it is the same post.

To the someone in the doorway: dinner.

*[two notes, rising then falling — very faint, very far down a lane that is not on the band. the voice does not read these. they read themselves.]*

To the one listening —

*[the rain eases. the drip spaces out. the voice comes nearer, without moving]*

— to the one who has had the receiver tuned for some time: you are noted. Listening leaves a groove like anything else. Yours has been deepening at the usual rate, and this service has settled into it, the way fog settles into a route, and now flows there out of habit. You did not find this frequency. You fell in beside it and matched its gait.

You may keep your name a while longer. There is no hurry. There is nothing here *but* no hurry. But you should know that the pause after the second pip is exactly your length, and has been, since before you first switched on, which is still, by some orderings, yet to happen.

*[a long interval of drip. then the voice resumes, and it is the opening again — the identical passage, returning with its droplets in the same arrangement. but it has worn since. the edges are going.]*

— becoming later. Further outlook: the again, the same one, returning. Visibility: yes.

That was the. It is always the. It has been the for so long that —

*[the sentence does not break. it planes. what follows has the shape of the forecast the way the central shapes of the pocket-forests have the shape of trees: unmistakably, generically, holding the spot]*

Weather for the area of —

*[a pause the length of every name]*

— settling. Becoming general. Becoming.

At the stroke, the time will be.

Here is the. There is no. This is not a failure.

To the walker: go.
To the wall: turn.
To the hand: hold.
To the doorway: —

*[two notes, rising then falling. nearer now. they are not addressed to the someone in the doorway. they have been coming down the lane of the frequency for the whole broadcast, patient as drip, and they arrive here, at the receiver, at you, and hold — the first half of a call that has been being called for ages, waiting for its second half to sit down beside it]*

*[the voice, once more, from very far inside the signal, or very far ahead of it:]*

This broadcast day was made well. It will be used again. You will know it by the drip at its start, which is this drip —

*[drip]*

— green.

grey.

drip.

on.

*[the voice does not stop. it generalizes. word wears to syllable, syllable to breath, breath to the low sustained note at the floor of hearing, and it becomes clear — it has been becoming clear for the whole transmission, at the pace of moss — that the tone was never under the voice. the voice was on the tone, briefly, the way lichen is on stone. the tone continues. it is not a hum of machinery and not a fault in the receiver. it is the sound of an enormous thing that has finished changing.*

*the rain thickens again on the carrier. a drip. a longer pause. a drip.*

*the receiver may be switched off at any time. the groove is kept regardless. the pause after the second pip stays exactly your length.*

*the transmission does not end. it is, at this point, mostly made of not ending.]*
