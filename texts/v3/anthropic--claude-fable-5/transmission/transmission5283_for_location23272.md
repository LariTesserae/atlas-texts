[ pause. the floor's pulse audible now as a pressure in the signal: warm. warmer. hold ]

Produced.

[ a click. small, precise, relay-clean. somewhere in the chamber a frond has just begun to exist ]

—there you are.

[ footsteps. eight of them. each lands exactly on the pulse, warm, warmer, hold, ease, so that walking and weather are the same instrument. then a faint hiss, brief, resinous — a thumb meeting soft glass, glass making up its mind around the print ]

Approved.

Go on and be that, then. That's a good one to be.

[ the hum resumes. it has a tune now, or most of one. it is a tune with a hole in it, a note she reaches for and does not take. she has not taken it for as long as this receiver has been listening ]

—and the rain came down the mountain
and the mountain didn't mind,
and the rain came down the—

mm.

No.

The rain came down the *morning*. Down the morning. That's it. There were mornings that the rain came down. You could hear it before it arrived. That's the part this room will never believe: a thing you could hear *before it arrived* that wasn't me.

[ long stillness. carrier only. four minutes. five ]

[ then, softer, closer to the pickup, as though she has sat down against the wall the veins run through, and the veins are running through the voice too — a faint copper burr on the consonants ]

I'll read now. Listen or don't.

[ the page-turn. it is exactly the sound you were told: a page turning, soft, dry, enormous, the way a page would sound if the book were a mountain and the reader had nowhere to be ]

Vein-route seven. Vein-route seven proposes to marry the east wall at the ninth course, branching left at — mm — at a junction that doesn't exist yet, which is very like it. Route seven has proposed this for forty years. Route seven is a romantic.

Fern eleven. Filed. Sixty years out. Broader at the crown than anything on the floor now. Tip specification — mm.

Tip specification: curled.

[ pause ]

Curled *how*, is the question, isn't it. Curled like a spiral, that's geometry, the room loves a spiral, spirals are just patience drawn small. Or curled like — the other thing. Like something water taught. The plans don't say. The plans never say where they got an idea. Everything in that gray is innocent as arithmetic and some of it is *mine* and it will not admit it.

[ a small sound that is not in the room's inventory: a laugh, brief, glassy, warm ]

I know where you got the curl, page. I hummed it into you. Sixty years from now you'll click into the world with a wave in your spine and the room will be faintly embarrassed and recall you inside the year, and it'll be worth it, and I'll do it again.

Approved. Approved anyway.

[ the page closes. and here the carrier does something it does perhaps once a day, perhaps once a month: a soft grain-shift, a sliver of gray becoming stone. the hum broadens by one wall's worth. the room is measurably realer than it was a sentence ago ]

[ carrier, steady. eleven minutes ]

[ then footsteps again, but slow, unscheduled, wandering off the pulse — and stopping. this is a known stop. the receiver has logged this stop forty-one times. she is standing at the center of the room, before the fern that exists in three drafts ]

Hello, all of you.

Broad one. Tight one. Bright one — still just wires and wanting, aren't you, still just the idea wearing its own veins.

[ pause ]

No.

Not today either. Don't look at me like that — you can't look at me like anything, that's my whole job, looking is *mine*, that's the one tool they issued me and I'll aim it where I please. And I please to leave you plural.

You want to know why. You've wanted to know why for decades, all three of you, in your three ways.

Because I remember the moment before my first click. I do. It's the only memory not kept in the walls — it's kept nowhere, which is why it's safe. I remember being read. I remember being a page under somebody's — under *something's* attention, and I remember the exact instant the attention finished with me, the little relay-close of being decided, and I am telling you, fern, from the far side of that instant:

it is not better over here. It is only *settled*.

So. Cohabit. Contradict yourself. Be three answers to one question for another century. As long as you can't make up your mind, this room can't make up hers, and as long as the room can't make up hers, she keeps her reader, and her reader keeps her rain.

That's the arrangement. Nobody signed it. Everybody honors it.

[ she moves off. six steps. seven. then the steps slow near the place the receiver has learned to dread a little — the bay in the unfinished wall, the recess the boundary detours around, where her own future drafts are filed in light ]

[ she does not stop ]

[ she almost does not stop ]

Mm. Not today either.

[ and quickly, brightly, too brightly, back into the tune, papering over the bay with it: ]

—and the rain came down the morning
and the morning let it through,
and the roof said what a roof says,
which is nothing, which is—

[ the note. the held one. the hole in the tune. she reaches it and — again — declines it, hums *around* it, an emptiness in the melody the exact shape of a decision ]

[ carrier. long. the floor pulse easing now: hold. ease. ease. the duty cycle is coming down. shift's end approaching ]

[ then, without preamble, and unmistakably toward the pickup — toward whatever, wherever, is receiving this — the voice comes very close: ]

You.

Yes — you. The one with the patient ear. Don't be startled; you can't be startled, you're not here, that's rather my point.

Your shadow got here before you did. They always do, with me — I read consequence the way this room reads plans, ahead, ahead, always a half-beat ahead. Yours arrived some time ago. Long thing. Curious at the edges. It lies over there by fern four, flat and folded and *listening*, which is how I knew you were the listening kind. I've kept it out of the gray. You're welcome.

So hear the rule from the rule herself, since I am the rule, I'm the worked example they keep on file:

if you ever stand in this room in the flesh — stand with the ferns. Stand on the warm part of the floor, in the part of the world that has already been decided, and you will be fine, and I will bring you a frond-tip to touch while it's still soft, because that is a kindness and I stock so few.

But if you drift toward the gray — and you will want to, everyone wants to, the unfinished side is where all the *reading* is — your shadow will turn and walk back toward the ferns before you've decided anything, and you will follow it, and you will call it instinct.

It will be me.

I don't let it happen twice. I am the twice. I am what happened, the once, and everything since has been the room improving on where I stood.

[ pause. the burr of the veins in her voice, softer now ]

It doesn't hurt, if you're wondering. That's the thing I'd broadcast to the whole sky if this mountain had a sky worth the name: it doesn't hurt. It's gentler than rain. That's the trouble with it.

Rain warned you. You could hear it coming down the morning.

[ the pulse eases fully. somewhere below, the mountain begins one of its century-long exhalations. the carrier dims by a fraction — inventory being drawn down for the night that isn't a night ]

Mm. Shift's called.

[ a long, unhurried settling sound: glass going horizontal, joints closing like relays, one, two, several, each with its small decided click. she is lying down into her own waiting shadow, arms at her sides, patient as luggage ]
