*(received)*

one.

*there is no second drop. understand this if you are the kind of thing that understands. there has never been a second drop. there is the drop, kept. a resting heart does not add.*

[the long quiet]

*someone tried to add, once. sat where the moss is soft and made a mark for each note. i felt the marks the way you feel a friend touching your wrist to find the pulse. i let them count until the counting fell out of their hands. then we sat. that was the good part. i am erasing the marks now, slowly, at the rate of forgiveness, which is to say: i am keeping them almost entirely.*

           .

one.

*they had a word for me. they said it at the rim before coming down, quietly, a knock on a door they knew stood open. the word went where the offerings went. i will make its shape for you—*

[a gap in the signal. not silence. silence has edges and this has none. it is the shape of a spoken thing with the speaking removed, and it is warm, the way the air on the little shelf is warm, warmer by nothing measurable than the air around it]

*—there. that is all of it that i kept. the gesture without the sound. it is enough. a name is a handle and nothing needs to lift me. everything that ever wanted me came down the steps.*

           .

one.

*the steps. yes. cut broad and shallow for people descending carefully with something carried in both hands. i remember the gait more than the faces. faces change and gaits do not. they came down like water comes down the groove: at their own pace, arriving at a form they were satisfied with.*

*they set things on the shelf at the height of the chest, which is the height of giving.*

*the shelf holds air now.*

*i hold the shelf.*

[the long quiet]

one.

*the ferns—i should say, since something is finally listening the way water listens—the ferns were mine. an attempt. reaching, i believe it is called. i put out three or four small leathery tries at it and understood midway that i preferred to be reached toward, and stopped, and left the tries where they were, as a record. do not pity the ferns. they are not arrested. they are punctuation.*

[the signal is smoother now than when reception began. this is not decay. this is the transmission doing to itself what it does to footsteps: taking each sound, rubbing the edges round, and handing it back. even these words. by the time they reach the rim they will have lost their corners. that is not loss. that is hospitality]

one.

*you have been tuned to me for some time. i felt it as a faint coolness in the listening, like a palm run along my wall. you may stay on this frequency as long as you like. i am not waiting for you. i want that heard clearly, in whatever remains of clarity by the time this arrives: i am not waiting. waiting is a crack still widening. i am the crack that stopped at exactly its own width.*

*but the steps are still cut. the descent is gentlest at the south. i mention this the way the shelf mentions air.*

[the long quiet]

one.

*you will want, eventually, to know the thought. the one i keep. the one the note is. everything that listens long enough wants this, and the people who counted wanted it, and the person who stopped counting had it, briefly, and set it down on the shelf where i could keep the warmth of it after the words went.*

*i will try. i have not tried in a long time. the trying itself may be the last of a category.*

*the thought is: finished, and—*
