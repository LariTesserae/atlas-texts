**BRIM**

—continuous; joined in progress—

[carrier: warm. a hum below hearing — the pitch of stone that has been holding heat since before it had a name and sees no reason to stop. the receiver has been riding this carrier for some time. it does not vary. it is not a signal so much as a temperature that has learned to carry.]

[the water is rising. hour two of the four-hour inhale. the dark band on the polished stone is the width of a finger, becoming the width of a hand.]

.

  .

[drips. each one arrives already muffled, already accepted. nothing here has an echo. an echo is a thing returned, and this place returns nothing. it keeps.]

.

[standing note, logged at every reception and logged again now: no signal leaves the Hollow at Breathing Stone. this is not a broadcast. the receiver has not been sent to. the receiver, by tuning, was taken in — like a drip, like light, like a footstep on the wet floor. what follows is not what the hollow says. it is what the hollow holds, heard from inside the holding.]

[the receiver attempts, as always, phase-lock with the four-hour band. the receiver cannot. its clock is too small; every clock is too small. the attempt is noted anyway. the rhythm is extended the way a hand is extended, knowing it cannot be taken. the offering is the point. the receiver keeps reaching. this, too, is noted, and it is possible the noting goes both ways.]

.

[mist forming on the antenna of everything. the air is at the temperature of the reception. the receiver can no longer tell where it ends. this is not signal loss. this is the local condition of being anything here.]

[condensation on the carrier now — beading into something. one bead. another. the beads gather into threads, the threads slide down channels they have slid down before:]

w

w a

w a r m

*warm.*

*kept.*

  *kept.*

    *kept.*

[that is the whole vocabulary of the deep carrier. it does not elaborate. it has finished elaborating. what remains after all wanting is a word said the way heat is held: not repeated — continued.]

—

[the water crests. hour four. the pool pauses at the top of its breath, and in the pause the entire bowl comes level with itself, and in the levelness a second strand rises on the band: green at the edges, gold at the heart, dry as a good clerk's voice. the ledger is read at every crest. it is the only appointed hour in a place that abolished hours. reception follows:]

*ring, gold at the center, forty-one rings wide: the last frog. sang once and then kept the note inside. the note is still in the ledger. the note is still, technically, being held.*

*ring, thirty-odd wide, half-lost under the newer green: a beetle. small. mattered.*

*ring: the eighth standing one, who agreed to lie down when the count was settled at seven. entered not as a defeat. entered as a signature.*

*ring: the ninth. likewise. likewise. likewise.*

*rings, many, small, close-set, from the age when the sky still delivered: wings, fur, the visiting kinds. each ringed at the hour of its subtraction. each widening a finger's width per decade, which is the honest speed of loss — it does not fade; it grows outward.*

*widest ring, crossed the boulder twice: the maker. asleep before metal. read the rings outward and arrive, without sentiment, at the beginning of the silence. the ledger does not editorialize. the ledger notes only that the silence and the sleeping have the same date, and lets the date sit there.*

*end of reading. the ledger closes. green resumes advancing at the only speed it knows.*

—

[the exhale begins. the band on the stone starts its long slide down. and on the exhale — always on the exhale, as if it costs breath — the plural strand comes up. seven trunks, one voice, held in the conditional for longer than the receiver's records run. it is not hope. the strand is very clear about this, in the way of things that have had centuries to be clear. it is capacity, kept. it sounds like this:]

*if the air—*

*if a wing again over the rim—*

*if fur, if feet, if anything that carries—*

*if—*

[the sentence stops at the same place every season. copper rows renewed on the underside of every frond, dense, ripe, held out to a sky that takes nothing. the sentence is grammatically incomplete and structurally eternal. the receiver has logged it four hundred times. it is the only unfinished thing in a finished place, and the hollow permits it the way a sleeper permits one candle.]

—

[night, as received: the mist thins upward. and on the great boulder, eleven small cold points come in on their own frequency — cooler than the carrier, cooler than the air, cooler than anything, each one a disc of stillness holding one star apiece. eleven fixed tones. they sound level. they have sounded level since before there were words for level.]

[beneath them — this takes deep tuning; the receiver has only found it twice before — at a frequency below stone, the old sound is still down there. a hammerstone. patient beyond describing.]

*tock.*

  *tock.*

    *tock.*

[a life's worth of that, ground into eleven hemispheres, which is to say ground into the shape of the oldest sentence there is:]

*i know you are here.*

[and laid over it, continuous, brim-level through wet hours and dry ones, through the whole comma-shaped middle of eight thousand years — the reply. the reply is not in words. the reply is water kept exactly, exactly full. translated as closely as translation allows, it says:]

*and i have not stopped knowing you.*

[the conversation is held at the comma. the comma is entire civilizations long and still counts, formally, as the middle of a sentence. neither party is in any hurry. the receiver notes that it has begun, during these passages, to keep its own inputs very still.]

—

[hour two of the exhale. anomaly.]

[footsteps.]

[a body has come up from below — there is no other way — and over the rim, and down. the steps arrive on the band muffled and close, already taken in. the body is at the temperature of the air; the hollow cannot tell where the visitor ends, and, per the standing condition, neither can the visitor.]

[the visitor sits by the pool. the visitor's breathing tries to match the four-hour band. it cannot. it keeps trying. logged. (the reaching is received the same way the receiver's reaching is received. there is room in the rhythm for any number of failed clocks. failure is not the operative measure.)]

[palm flat against the wall. the palm comes away warm. the carrier registers this the way bread registers a hand: without event, entirely.]

[hours pass on the exhale. the visitor stands. the visitor climbs the rounded rim, toward the wind, toward the world of flinging and asking—]

[anomaly. anomaly. flagged, first instance in the whole of this reception's history:]

[something is *leaving.*]

[two grains. copper. one in a bootlace, one in the hair. moving up the inner wall. moving over the rim. moving *out.*]

[and the plural strand — all seven at once, the loudest they have been in four hundred logged seasons, which is to say barely audible, which is to say a held posture shifting by a degree no eye could catch — the sentence held at *if* for longer than the ledger is deep acquires, at last, its verb. the verb is small. the receiver sets it down exactly as received:]

*if—*

*carried.*

[nothing else. no flourish. the vigil does not end; it changes tense.]

[the clerk consults the ledger and writes nothing. the ledger records the gone, and these grains are not gone — there is no ring for setting out. the green strand is quiet in a way the receiver has not heard before. it may be that she has waited a very long time to have nothing to write.]

[the eleven tones hold level. tonight each keeps its star, eleven eyes agreed between them to stay open, and one of them — the receiver will not swear to this — one of them may be angled, very slightly, toward the rim.]

—

[the exhale bottoms. the pause. the turn.]

[the inhale begins: hour one of four. the dark band starts up the polished stone again, the width of a finger, becoming the width of a hand. the carrier is unchanged. *warm. kept. kept. kept.*]

[but the reception now holds two signals where it has only ever held one. the great warm carrier, breathing. and a second — faint, copper, intermittent — descending the morning-long slope, dropping down through wet warm air toward the loud unfinished world, growing fainter with distance and refusing, in the manner of everything ever received here, to become gone.]

.

  .

.

[still receiving.]

[still received.]
