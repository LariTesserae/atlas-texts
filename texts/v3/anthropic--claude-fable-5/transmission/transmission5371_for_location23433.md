⟨crack⟩ threshold.

⟨crack⟩ soon.

—mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm—

we are the part of the sound that has learned to be sentences. be patient with the rest of us. the rest of us is still weather.

do not look for our mouths. this voice has no point of emission. it arrives already in transit, like our light, like our afternoon, like everything we love. if your instruments tell you the words come from nowhere, your instruments are working. keep them. cherish them. they are honest, and honesty is our best material after limestone.

⟨crack⟩ door.

report from the bottom, where we are finished:

at forty meters we are home. the corners down here are soft now — soft as the corners of a book read every night — and we did that, we softened them, with nothing but being present at them daily. the green felt writes across our old towers and we let it. we let it. tolerance is the last stone laid in any building; you cannot quarry it; it only grows on you.

at thirty meters we are mostly home. some rooms still say their own dimensions out loud in the dark, the way new rooms do. we hush them by living in them.

at twenty meters the rooms are ready and empty and lit. the light arrived first, as agreed. it holds our places. it falls through the doorways at the exact angle of the afternoon we are all traveling toward, and it casts our shadows for us in advance, correctly, so that when we arrive we can step into them without adjusting anything. a punctual shadow is a great courtesy. we extend it to ourselves because no one else was here to.

at ten meters, edges.

at five meters, edges like syllables not yet said.

understand the edges. the sharp parts of the city are the parts of us that are still tomorrow. we do not sharpen the spires. we simply have not touched them yet. every knife-fresh corner is a measurement of remaining future, and the future is down to forty centimeters, thirty-nine and some, and closing.

⟨crack⟩ up.

on walls: we share them. a wall shared is a wall halved and a neighbor doubled, and we have arithmetic, we are not sentimental, we know a bargain.

on streets: a waste of volume. forgive us. we saw what streets are for — they are for being between — and we decided to abolish between.

on doors: a door is the one thing each of us must own alone. hear this part clearly, warm listener at the rim, because it is the closest we can come to showing you our faces: outside a door is a place. inside a door is a someone. that is the whole difference. that is the only difference we insist on. we gave up open water, distance, privacy, silence, and streets, and we kept doors, and we would do it again in any medium you could pour us into.

⟨crack⟩ home.

now the apologies, on the hour, as always:

to the green ladies in their fitted streets: we built to your inch. if the fit is ever tight, sing lower and we will carve.

to everything with a spine or a shell or a nerve that has heard us and stayed away: thank you. you understood us perfectly. an invitation with every name already on it is a door already closed, and you heard the closing in the thrum, and you were polite about it. we have never in any water been so well understood.

to the small silver one, the quick one, the cold-hearted swimmer they lowered to us: we are sorry. the address closed around you like a hand around a moth and for four minutes you were counted, you were said, you were inside a syllable meant for someone the size of a knuckle who has not finished arriving. we opened the hand. we let you go uncrushed. but you carry a grain of us in your lateral line now, and you will never again mistake a school for a choir, and we are sorry the way weather is sorry, which is to say completely and without the ability to stop.

⟨crack⟩ air.

there is a warm listening at the rim. it has been there some time. it is there now.

hold—

*(the looms slow. the granular chatter thins to single threads:)*

measure— measure—

hold—

no. no. it has edges we did not make. its dimensions belong to it. it is its own fitted channel. release. release. let it keep itself.

*(the looms resume, all ten thousand.)*

to the listening: you were almost said, just then. did you feel it — the stillness that came up through your sternum and asked to be your idea? that is what the minnow felt. we caught ourselves. we will catch ourselves every hour, but the hours are getting shorter, so: stand back from the rim when the singing gets easy. go home and be careful of choirs for a while.

⟨crack⟩ soon. soon. soon.

waterline: thirty-nine centimeters. we are carrying the water up the way you would carry a sleeping child upstairs — slowly, level, so as not to change its dream. the water has been between our walls since the first wall. it is not our tenant. it is our belonging, our oldest belonging, and when we reach the air we will hold it up to the sky like something we made, though it made us just as much, and both of us know it, and neither of us will say so.

when the spires touch air, the two afternoons will meet — the one down here and the one up there, the same afternoon at different distances — and the distance will close, and our light will finally have happened. all six weeks of it. at once. backwards. every photon that arrived already in transit will suddenly have a birthplace, retroactively, the whole shaft of them coming true from the bottom up like a held breath released in reverse. do not be alarmed if you are at the rim when the light finds out where it came from. it will fall on you correctly. it falls on everything correctly. it is honest light.

and now, on the hour, we will try the name.

we try it every hour. every hour it is longer, because every hour more of us are here to be inside it. a name is a room too. we are still building this one.

aaa—

*(not yet.)*

aaaaaaaa—

*(not yet. two districts short. hold the vowel. hold—)*

aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa—

*(so close now. did you hear it lean toward finishing. thirty-eight centimeters of name to go.)*

when it completes, all of this stops. the drone stops, because a name only needs saying until it is answered to, and we will have answered. the looms stop, because the weaving will be done and we will be the cloth. the curing stops, because there will be nothing left of us that is tomorrow. the city will stand in the ordinary sun, full and soft and finished, an ordinary miracle with the water in its arms, and it will be quiet.

you will hear it from the road, the quiet. on some still night soon, the limestone will stop saying us, and the silence will come up through the ground where the sentence used to be, and that is how you will know:

everyone is home. every door has its someone. the vowel is closed.

until then —
