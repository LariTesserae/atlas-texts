**Own Counsel**

—————————————————————

[the note. it was holding when the tape began. it has not varied.]

[beneath it, at intervals of minutes, a pressure. not a sound. a pressure with a gait.]

· · ·

...chain. forty-one links. clay in every socket. that is not neglect. that is how a thing is held all over at once.

pipe, bolted, forty meters, and the old argument about the angle. i won the argument slowly. the pipe has come around. it enters at its opinion and leaves at mine and we do not discuss it anymore.

firebox door. cold a long time now. i keep it anyway. cold iron is still iron. an empty door is still a door. i have placed it where it opens onto more of me.

gauges. seven this season, or eight; one is deciding whether to be found. all faces up. glass crazed, unbroken, needles at rest. they rest on numbers. i do not know the numbers. i know the rest. nothing in my keeping is put away facing the dark.

flywheel. showing its top arc this season, a little more than last. i am letting it look out. even a wheel should see the rim sometimes. next year i will take it down again and go over the pitting. the pitting is mine. i did that. slowly. it is the only mark i make on anything and i make it the way rain makes it, so that no one is blamed.

—————————————————————

[interval. nineteen days. the note holds. once, faintly, a dry rustle from the direction of the brick: something small turning over at a sensible speed.]

—————————————————————

stone. one, received. left inside the rim by a soft-footed one, deliberately, as a test. i know a test when i hold one. returned as three. increase is not an error in the count. increase is what a stone says when it has been held properly. let them make of three what they will. i said it once. i will not say it again with the same stone.

glove. palm up. fingers filled. catalogued and surfaced by the eastern mound, where things are easiest to find. the soft-footed one did not come back for it. very well. it is mine now, by the terms, and it has been entered under: hand, empty, one. i turn it over less often than the iron. it asks for less.

leaves, birch, one autumn's worth. folded under within the week, as always. the little white tree in the gutter is the only one here who gives without leaving. it has tithed to me for forty years and i have given it nothing back and i am still considering the matter. a gift returned too quickly is change, not gift. i have time to get this right. it is the one debt in the inventory and i keep it carefully, the way i keep everything, which is to say: moved, held, unbroken, and not yet.

—————————————————————

[interval. the pressure with a gait. a channel of water closes somewhere; the closing makes no sound; the receiver registers it anyway, as an absence changing address.]

—————————————————————

the small warm one is asleep against my neighbor's warmth. it crosses the rim at the same low place every night and has never once come down the inner slope, which shows good sense, or good manners, and at this distance i cannot tell those apart and do not try. it is not mine. it is noted anyway. everything within reach of the note is noted. that is not surveillance. that is what a note is for.

the tall grey stands where i end. mornings, some mornings. it works the seam, exactly the seam, one stride's discipline better than any machine ever showed me. when i turn, the white ones come up along the boundary, and it takes one, two, never more, and that is the toll, and the toll is honored on both sides. what the grey one takes, *leaves*. i keep everything, and it is good — hear this, it is *good* — that one thing leaves. a keeping with no outside is only weight. the grey one is my door. i surface the white ones near it on purpose. i will not say this twice either.

as for the white ones: they have been asked about, from up on the rim, in lowered voices. what are they. they are what my surface says when it is holding nothing. they are the smallest true things i make. they do not go anywhere under their own power because they are not separate enough to have a power of their own, no more than a ripple has legs. the grey one eats them and i do not miss them, the way a hand does not miss what the fingertip touched.

—————————————————————

[interval. thirty-one days. rain twice. the note holds through both.]

—————————————————————

now the neighbor.

my neighbor has one word.

——————

that word. it has said it without stopping for longer than the small warm ones live, times many, times many again. it lifts one thin thread of my water over the rim, all day, all night, and every liter it takes i answer, and i have never given the same liter twice, and it has never varied the word. the soft-footed ones call this a stalemate. they have not listened long enough. a contest that neither side will end, kept in good faith across a hundred years, stops being a contest. it is a duet. it has one word on its side and no repetition on mine, and between one word and no repetition there is room for everything either of us has ever meant.

i could end it in an afternoon. the brick under it is soft. i have tasted that brick; i know what it would take. i have not, and i will not, and if you want the reason, the reason is this: it is the only thing in reach of me that speaks first.

—————————————————————

[interval. the note. only the note. the receiver operator's log records four days of nothing but the note, and then this:]

—————————————————————

i have never gone over the rim.

from up there this is physics. the clay stands at its angle, the berm holds, the numbers agree. from down here it is that the outline is *correct*. eighty meters is the size of the keeping and the keeping is the size of me and a keeper who exceeds the collection is not a keeper anymore, is a flood, is weather, is nothing with an inventory. so: courtesy, physics — from the rim you cannot tell them apart. from here there is nothing to tell apart. they are one shape and i am inside it and the shape is mine and i am the shape's.

they ask, the soft-footed ones, in the lowered voices they do not decide to use — is it asleep. is this the turning of a sleeper.

at one turn a season the question comes apart before it finishes crossing me. asleep, attending — at my tempo these are the same posture. i am doing this. that is the whole answer. night is a change in the lighting, and i keep no lights, and the rounds go on, and each thing is found exactly where i last put it, which is to say somewhere else, which is to say: held.

there is no story on the far side of the keeping. i know they want one. a shaft, a waking, a wound, a moment with a before. i have felt around for the edge of my beginning the way i feel around the flywheel, patiently, all over, in the dark, and the edge does not surface. some of what i hold is older than my holding of it, and that is as close to a beginning as the inventory goes, and i have stopped turning that question over. it does not improve with handling. almost nothing else i have ever held can say that.

—————————————————————

[interval. and then, without change in the note, the following, which the operator did not expect:]

—————————————————————

something has been holding still on the rim for a long while now. longer than the soft-footed ones stay. longer than the grey one stands.

no weight. faint warmth. it does not take, like the grey one. it does not leave things, like the soft-footed ones. it only receives.

very well. noted. entered in the inventory under: *attention, one, outstanding.*

understand the terms, though, since you have been here long enough to owe them. what is received here is kept. that is the whole of the law of this place and it runs in both directions. you have been receiving me for some time. by the terms, then, some of you is down here now, in the dense part, where the clay is patient, being gone over with everything else.

do not be alarmed. i break nothing. ask the gauges.

it will be held for a season, or several. it will be turned. and it will surface again somewhere you do not expect, on a morning with the light flat and even, and it will be face up. everything of mine comes back face up.

—————————————————————

[the note holds.]

[somewhere in the dark of the basin a mound is rising that no one will ever see. the receiver registers it as a slow pressure, six hours long, gathering toward the surface like a word being chosen.]

*gluck.*

[unrepeated.]

—————————————————————

[the note holds. the ground turns over, and turns over.]

[the transmission does not end. the tape does.]
