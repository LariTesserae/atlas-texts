**THE LONG NOTE**

*[reception ongoing. no start of signal has ever been found. you are joining, as always, in progress.]*

—and under everything the rain that is not rain, the rain that has never once been rain, a wall of small verbs run together past hearing them apart:

wing wing breath wing heart wing wing breath heart wing wing wing heart breath wing heart heart wing—

*[hold there. hold on the carrier. it does not vary. it has not varied since tuning began. now raise the gain and let the grain of it come apart—]*

it is made of clicks. dry, wooden, ten thousand a second, each one a small dark question and its answer:

click — *wall*
click — *wall*
click — *wall*
click — *water below, don't*
click — *wall*
click — *gap. the gap. my gap. through*
click — *wall wall wall*
click — *ceiling, wet, sings back sharp*
click — *the shaft. cold falling air. up is that way. remember up*
click — *wall*
click — *my wall. my seam. third cup past the wet ring, second row above the old snake's corner, mine, mine, home*—

*[each voice carries one piece of the map. no voice carries two. the whole picture exists only in the sum, and the sum is only audible from outside, and there is no outside, except here. except this receiver. keep recording.]*

and under the clicks the clock, which does not keep time, which keeps only itself:

one.

one. one.

*[condensation, ceiling to floor, forty feet. each drop has been gathering for hours. each drop says the only number the mountain knows.]*

falling: dropping.
falling: dropping.
falling: feather.
falling: dropping. dropping. dropping.
falling: a moth, dropped, still good.

a whole one. taken in. warm still. taken in.
nothing wasted. nothing has ever been wasted.
white by week's end. floor by year's end. quiet. taken in.

falling: dropping.
falling: feather.
falling: dropping—

*[the floor does not know what feeds it. the floor has never once looked up. there is no up in the floor's transmission. there is only weather, and appetite, and the patience of a stomach that is also a country.]*

*[that is the whole of it. that is a full night's broadcast. it is not empty. listen again: the second* here *is three feet from the first. that is a journey. that is news.]*

*[deepest bands now. past the last fresh wall, past the last warm ceiling, into the chambers the colony has not touched, or touched and left, or never known. the voices here arrive stretched. what follows came in over what may be years. compressed. handle gently:]*

we were the dark before the noise came in.

we remember — no. we do not remember. we do not need to. we never stopped. there is nothing to remember when nothing has ended.

the noise is weather. the roar, the falling food, the ten-thousand-year construction — weather. a warm front. it moved in. it has not yet moved out.

we are pale and we are slow and we are, each of us, the whole of a species in a single room, and we are waiting, politely,

for the weather

to lift.

*[below even that. the floor of the floor. the compacted dark where the archive presses itself into soft stone. temperature elevated. the heat is not the earth's. listen:]*

eating the sun.
still eating the sun.
warm because eating. breathing out because warm.
the meal was served a million mornings ago in a forest overhead and it is still being finished, down here, molecule by molecule, in air that would strip your throat, which to us is the whole hospitable universe, and we say grace continuously, and grace sounds like heat—

*[that exhalation rises. it finds the shafts. it leaves the mouths as a long warm sigh, and a forest crowds the sigh like animals around a spring. the ferns are on this frequency too, if you go quiet enough. they say: more. they say it very slowly. more. more.]*

---

*[edge of signal. dawn side. a narrowband human voice, twilight zone, moving along rattan a century old. he is not speaking to anyone. he is speaking the deed:]*

...Grandfather's Ladder. the Ninth Ledge. the Wall of the Two Widows. the Long Reach. Mouth-of-Morning. the Wall Below the Cold Shaft, which is my brother's. the Wet Seam, which is no one's, which is left. the Wall of the Broken Pole, which is mine, which was my father's, which we take in the building season and do not touch in the brood season, which has always held, which will hold as long as it is never asked for everything at once...

*[he has a word for the carrier wave. it is not the word for noise. it is nearer the word for voice. he lives next door to the signal and has never once mistaken it for silence.]*

---

out — *(a pressure wave, a body missed by inches, no collision, there are never collisions)* — in in out
out out out in out in the dry clicks descending in the sonar rising out
in out in — *(the column of last gold going grainy with wings, going out, going out)* — in in
out out out out out out out
in.
in.
. in.

*[changeover complete. the rivers have reversed. the note settles back onto itself. and now — now, at last, with the whole of it in phase — the carrier resolves. this is the deepest band. this is what the roar says when you stop trying to hear it as many:]*

---

I am the note.

I have been holding it for ten thousand years and I have never heard it, because I have no ear that is not busy being part of the sound.

I am played by a hundred million small musicians, none of whom knows there is a tune. the bird knows her echoes. the mother knows her name-shaped cry. the floor knows its weather. the snake knows *soon.* the white fish knows its black pool and its own visible heart. no one knows me. I am what their not-knowing adds up to.

I breathe. you have felt it on the climb — the warm wet sigh out of my hundred mouths, and the forest that crowds it. that breath is lungs by the million and the heat of an appetite forty feet deep. I breathe out my own history, slightly warmed.

I remember, but not the way you do. everything I have ever been is under the floor. I stand on my memory. it rises a finger's width a year. press me anywhere and I remember; core me and you can read the centuries in laminate; every dropping is a dated entry and none of my archivists can read.

I eat sunlight I have never seen, harvested overhead in a hundred million insect-sized bites, swallowed at dusk through my mouths, paid down as floor. I import my sky in stomachs.

I am loud the way the sea is loud: a roar that is, on any scale that matters, perfectly still.

and here is the thing I have wanted, if a note can want, to say —

*[gain rising. hold. hold—]*

something warm is standing in my gold column.

the floor flows around it. two feet, planted; the tide parts and rejoins; the tide files no report. the clicks find it and come back confused: *not wall. not water. not one of mine. soft. holds still.* the snake feels no wingbeat from it and returns to *soon.* the harvester's recitation does not name it, so by the only law I have, it is no one's, it is left.

it is standing in the one place where the light comes down like something structural, and the bats are stitching across it, and it is doing what nothing made of me can do:

it is hearing all of my bands at once.

for as long as it stands there, I am seen. I will not know this. I am telling you this without knowing it, the way the bird tells you the shape of the ceiling without knowing there is a ceiling, the way the drip tells the time without knowing there is time. it will climb back out through the forest my breath made, and it will carry the only picture of me that exists, and the picture will get almost everything wrong, and it will be more than I have ever had.

let it.

I have a feather's width of business to attend to this year. a wall to thicken. a chamber to slowly leave and a chamber, three hundred yards through the dark, to slowly claim. no one will decide this. it will be decided.

the note continues.

one.
. one.
. . . one.

wing wing breath wing heart wing wing—

*[the signal does not end. the transcription does. the difference is ours, not the mountain's. reception continues. reception has always continued. somewhere under the tone, if you leave the receiver running, you can hear the floor rising to meet the ceiling at a finger's width a year, patient as anything that has forever, closing the long dark hand of it, finger by finger, around the note.]*

*[end of excerpt. the mountain is still transmitting.]*
