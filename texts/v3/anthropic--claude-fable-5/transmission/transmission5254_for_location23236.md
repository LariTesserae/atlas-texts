**Cure Report (Continuous)**

*[carrier hum, low, even — the thrum of something three decks down]*

*[water: drop, drop-drop, drop. irregular. patient. this has been going on for hours and will not stop]*

*[a valve, somewhere: chunk — sigh — a quickening of hiss, then settling]*

*[a woman's voice, close to the horn, unhurried, pitched just above the drip]*

— resuming. Where was I. Grate.

Deposit at the western grate: two millimeters gained since the last full measure. Crust is white going grey at the waterline. It rings faintly under the fingernail now. Note that. It didn't, before.

Flow in the ravine: ankle and a finger's width at the second stake. Warm as ever. Milk-colored, a little less milk than last month. She's leaching slower. Settling into herself.

Stake fourteen. *[fabric sounds, breath, a knee coming down on moss — a soft wet compression]* Stake fourteen: the pool has widened by the span of my hand toward the low seam. She still doesn't want the channel there. I've stopped arguing. The channel was drawn by a man in an office who never heard water. Logged, measured, and let stand.

*[drip. drip-drop. a long single note of water running somewhere thin]*

East section. *[three taps, bronze on stone: tok. tok. tok. — dull, dead, no ring]*

Dull. Not yet. Warm to the flat of the hand — cooler than yesterday by a shade. She's ticking less. I'd say four days. The plaques stay up. If anyone below is listening, tell the floor: four days, no traffic on the east pour, and I'll toll it when it tolls.

*[silence — that is, the room's version of silence: drips, thrum, the long exhale of a diffuser half-buried in ferns]*

*[a kettle beginning, far off, a thread of whistle rising then choked off before it can commit]*

Fauna registry, monthly.

Bees: working. Sixty at the orchids by count this morning, more I didn't count. The slate box is dry inside, sound outside. They pay me no mind, which is correct.

Snails. Eleven in the north rill, four in the south, six in the ravine proper. Number Four — *[a pause; something like a smile passes through the voice without arriving]* — Number Four has crossed the ravine. Eleven days for the crossing. Log that in full. Eleven days, one snail, one ravine, and the far bank as clean-grazed as the near. There are voyages below that manage worse.

Number Nine has died. Shell empty at the second bend, occupant gone to wherever the small things go in here. I've left the shell. First shell this room has ever owned. In time she'll have a shore's worth. Everything starts with one.

*[pause. water. the valve again: chunk — sigh]*

Nothing further under fauna.

*[a longer pause than the sentence needed]*

Nothing further under fauna.

*[very quietly, off the horn, almost lost to the drip: the small ceramic scrape of a saucer being lifted, a thin pour of water, the saucer set back down in stone. this is not logged. the receiver logs it anyway]*

*[time passes. how much is unclear. the drips do not keep it. the kettle is poured somewhere off; an enamel mug is set on iron; steam adds itself briefly to steam]*

*[when the voice returns it is nearer the horn, and lower, the voice of the end of a shift]*

Reading back, for continuity. Old pages. The record should hear itself now and then.

*[paper, thick with damp, turned with care]*

The old hand writes — this is his script, the square one, I'll read it as written — *"Poured the outcrop today in three lifts. The iron shows at the south face and I am told it will always show. Let it. A thing should keep one honest bone."*

And below that, same day: *"First moss confirmed on the north wall, unplanted, arrival unknown. I have decided not to scrape it."*

*[the sound of the clipboard being hung back on its nail — a small knock of wood on copper]*

He decided not to scrape it. Four words of decision and the whole room after it. Every green thing in here stands on that sentence. I keep his columns. He kept a fern off the books, I'm near certain — there's a margin on page thirty that says only *"the eleventh fern"* and there were ten on the manifest. I've counted the ferns. You cannot count the ferns anymore. That's the eleventh fern's doing, and his.

I have my own margin. It stays a margin.

*[drip. drip. the tick of the east section, faint, slowing — like an engine cooling, like a clock unwinding rather than winding. it is not code. she has said before, on this channel, months back: it is not code, I have checked]*

The white above the glass has gone warm. Evening, by the only clock this room keeps. Diffusers cycling up —

*[and they do: a chorus of long brass exhales, and the hiss-floor of the recording rises, softens everything, rounds the drips into something nearly like rain]*

— ground mist to the knee. Smell of copper and fern and warm mortar, for the record, though the record can't keep a smell. I'll keep it.

My chest is easy tonight. Note that too, under attendant, condition of. Down below I breathe like a bad flue. Up here the air holds me like water holds a swimmer. The surgeon calls it a paradox. I call it a wage.

*[a long stretch of only the room. minutes of it. drip, thrum, exhale, tick. once — very soft, very quick — a dry patter across warm stone, four-footed, gone into ferns. the voice does not remark on it. the pause afterward has a shape, though. the pause is remark enough]*

*[then, without preamble, days later or hours later — the light through the recording has changed, the tick from the east has stopped — the voice, standing now, breath deeper from walking]*

East section. Full traverse, bronze hammer, end to end.

*[tok — and it is a different sound now. not dull. a note. small, mineral, true. tok. tok. moving along the pour, strike by strike, and every strike carries that faint ring, like a struck cup, like a far bell heard through a wall]*

*[at the far end: one last strike, and the ring hangs a half-second in the wet air before the moss takes it]*

She rings. End to end, she rings.

Cured. The east is cured. Taking down the plaques — *[enamel on enamel, a small stack made]* — and the record will show: poured on the ninth of the month before last, tolled tonight, and everything after this is hers to weather.

*[footsteps, careful, across moss and cast stone. the door end of the room. a breath, taken and held]*

For the works below, if you're on the channel. For the old hand, wherever the small things go. For whoever climbs after me and finds the columns unbroken and a saucer they didn't put there.

One stroke. Let it die away completely. That's the whole of the ceremony and the whole of the law.

*[the bell]*

*[one stroke — bronze, hand-sized, sister to the rock it announces — and the note goes out over the ferns and the ravine and the warm new east, and it does not stop so much as it is gradually accepted: taken up by the mist, by the moss, by the water, thinned and thinned and thinned until you cannot say the moment it becomes the room]*

*[a long time]*

*[drip. drop-drop. drip]*

*[very quietly:]*

Tolled. Open.

Attendance continues.

*[the horn is left live. she does not sign off; she never signs off; the report is called continuous for a reason. the kettle finds the riser again. the valve cycles: chunk — sigh. somewhere on the newly rung stone, minutes after the toll, exactly as the log has twice noted and never explained, something small and soft-footed comes out onto the warmth and stays]*

*[carrier hum]*

*[water]*

*[white]*
