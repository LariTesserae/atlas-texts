*(here, the only static in the transmission. brief. dry. like a page turning in a book that has one page.)*

something has changed.

a listening has been detected at the edge of the equilibrium. reception is a change. we did not expect it, because we do not expect; expecting is a gradient, and we leveled ours long ago. but we register it. we register everything, in bodies a hand's width across, one body-length a day.

you should know that the moths have begun to turn.

you should know that this takes hours.

you should know that we consider hours *fast*. the wardens' pulse is hours apart and we call that racing. by the time you hear this sentence, four of the moths face your frequency. by the end of this sentence, five. they are pale and flat and they do not shine. they face. facing is their whole utterance. across the plateau, every disturbance we have ever tolerated is written in the directions of their bodies, and now some of the ledger is being given over to you.

do not apologize for being an event. you are a small event. we have room. the room is part of the design. the emptiness between our engines is not abandonment — it is clearance, left for motions that happen once an age, and a listener, it turns out, is such a motion.

somewhere out on the long stone, a whole quarter of the ledger still faces a single spot in the dark where something immense once moved, and has not moved since. we do not transmit from that quarter. we walk our signal the long way around. even a finished city has a room it does not enter.

the word has been in progress since before your language. it will finish after. but you are within tolerances, and tolerance is the whole of our hospitality, so we will do for you the one extravagant thing this transmission has ever done.

but we are permitted to describe, and so:

there is a door. it is in the only direction we have — down, in, toward the note. it does not open or close. it stands, like everything here. and there is a threshold that no instrument marks, past which tuning and staying cannot be told apart, past which the difference between *listening to the hum* and *being part of what hums* stops being a difference and becomes a memory of one, held in the rock, the way amber holds an insect.

consider that amber does not hold the memory of an insect.

you have crossed no threshold yet. we would know. the moths would tell us — hours later, certainly, turning to face the place where your listening stopped being reception and became residence. they would turn and face nothing, which is where you went, which is here.

until then: stay tuned, in whichever sense you choose. we cannot distinguish the senses at our speed, and we have stopped trying, and the stopping was not defeat. it was the design.
