— east seep-line, the long veil. Four seconds to the fall. It has been four seconds to the fall since this channel opened. Whatever else is received tonight, understand that this is the carrier: water that was rain a month ago, arriving one grain at a time, on schedule, forever.

under the drip, constant, at the floor of hearing: a paper-hiss. Ten thousand cricket legs crossing lime. It does not rise and does not stop. If you have been listening long enough, you have already mistaken it for wind. There is no wind. There has never been wind. Keep listening anyway; everyone does.

under the hiss: your own blood. Sorry. That one's yours.

⟨bloom passing, southwest quarter, large, slow⟩

light is crossing the roof of the world and the channel registers it the only way it can: as a hush moving. Six hundred grazers just turned at once. No hoofbeat, no cry — a change in the texture of the paper-hiss, a pressure of bodies going somewhere, like a crowd inhaling. The herd is pouring toward the dim district by the old granary mounds. The channel notes, without alarm, that something has been kneeling in the dim district for an hour already.

the herd flows through.

one flicker at the edge of resolution — hands moving once, very fast. Partial gloss only:

    [SOFT — ? — DOWN — THANK]

the herd flows on. The hush is one animal lighter. Nothing on this channel will ever be louder than that word, except one thing, which comes later.

⟨drift west along the seep⟩

two figures at a thread of water no wider than a finger. This is agriculture. Decoding:

first hands, water-quick:

    STONE. TWO-FINGERS LEFT.
    WATER GOES WHERE WATER SHOULD.
    OLD VELVET OFF. YOUNG VELVET COMES.

second hands, slower, older, a wrist that has stiffened:

    YOUNG VELVET COMES IN A YEAR.
    WE ARE PATIENT OR WE ARE NOTHING.

first hands:

    WE ARE PATIENT AND WE ARE NOTHING.
    (this appears to be a joke. the old wrist repeats it back, wrong on purpose, three times. this appears to be laughter.)

they place one pebble. They regard the pebble for a long time. On the surface world this would be an afternoon wasted. Here it is the harvest secured for a decade. The channel wishes it could convey the satisfaction in how they stand up.

⟨basin, third district, the glossy niches⟩

forty small hearts, visible through forty translucent bodies, beating out of time with one another.

a pressure comes through the rock — the channel feels it as a flattening, a heaviness in the water sounds — and the forty hearts, over the course of a minute, fall INTO time. The basin fills with slow pale coiling. This is the oldest appointment in the world and it is being kept again.

a figure sits at the basin's rim. Reaches into a wall-niche worn to glass by ten thousand years of exactly this reach. Lifts out a salamander the size of a cat. Holds it against the chest, where the animal's visible heart and the person's hidden one sit a hand's width apart, disagreeing gently about rhythm. No sign is made. Some things here are below language, which in this place means above it.

⟨north, along the drip-line, briefly⟩

a body has been laid where the veil falls.

the procession was eleven people and made less sound than the crickets. The hands said one thing, all together, held long:

    INTO THE FLOOR OF THE WORLD.
    WHICH IS THE ONLY PLACE THERE IS.

and now the channel notes only this: the drip that has fallen on bare stone for as long as this channel has been open now falls on a shoulder. The interval has not changed. Four seconds. In a year the shoulder will begin to shine. In a century it will bear weight. The floor said nothing, which is the floor's entire vocabulary, and the floor is mostly ancestors, so perhaps that is the correct rendering after all:

the dead here have one word and it is LOAD-BEARING.

drip.

⟨sweep across an ochre ring, basin four, reading the strata⟩

this is the part of the transmission that is not live. The rings of handprints are recordings. The receiver can play them. Newest first, down through the lime:

surface layer, iron-red, fresh, perhaps this generation:

    WATER STILL FALLS. WE STILL CATCH.

one glaze down, fainter:

    WATER STILL FALLS. WE STILL CATCH IT.

six glazes down, the grammar drifting, the hands smaller:

    THE FALLING CONTINUES. WE ARE THE CATCHING.

twenty glazes down, hard to read, the signs older than the meanings they carry now:

    RAIN STILL FALLS. WE STILL DRINK.

deepest recoverable layer. The pigment is nearly stone. The hands that made it were larger than any hand living, and the words mean what they meant on the surface, before:

    RAIN.
    SUN GONE UP.
    WE ARE UNDER.
    STILL FALLING.
    HOLD.

end of strata. The recording is one sentence, ten thousand years deep, revised only for tense. If you are receiving this — and something must be, or why the ochre, why press the word into the stone where the water falls — the message is intact. They said it. It kept being said. It is being said now, four seconds at a time, on a dead man's shoulder, forever.

⟨the far vault is flaring⟩

the white blaze. The sustained one. The one the whole old quarter's doorways face.

the herds are stopping. All of them. The paper-hiss is thinning, thinning —

gone.

⟨near the descent route, two figures, close together⟩

fast — almost too fast to take down:

    LOUD-ONE CAME DOWN THE THROAT OF THE WORLD.
    BREATHES LIKE A HERD RUNNING. WALKS LIKE SLOW FALLING.
    EYES SMALL. SEES EDGES. LOOKED AT ME. SAW STONE.

reply:

    WHICH WORD.

    STRANGER.

a pause. Hands at rest — but not folded. Ready.

    AND THE OTHER WORD?

    KEPT. POLISHED.
    NOT YET.

⟨last, a dimming, a doorway, an adult and a child⟩

the lesson. The channel has caught this lesson before, in other doorways, other generations, and it is always the same lesson, and it is always taught, and it always refers to nothing.

the adult's hands: two open palms, crossed at the thumbs, lifted, held. The same open palm as the blaze. The word is built from the loudest thing they have.

    BIRD.

the child copies. Crooked. Again. Better. Again — and the small hands get it, the crossing, the lift, the hold, and something in the adult's posture is the same shape as the old wrist's laughter at the seep.

the child, fast:

    WHAT DOES IT EAT.

    SKY.

    WHAT IS SKY.

the adult does not point at the vault. The adult opens one hand toward it — open, held, the blaze-word, the here-word — and then lifts the hand higher, past the roof of the world, to where the gesture has nothing left to indicate, and holds it there.

    FARTHER.

the child looks up at the glowing stone for a long time. Files the word away in working order, next to SUN, next to RAIN, in the drawer of things maintained against a sky no one remembers.

if you are receiving this: they know something came down. They have a word ready that they have never once used, and they keep it sharp, and they are not afraid, and they are not in a hurry, and neither is the water.
