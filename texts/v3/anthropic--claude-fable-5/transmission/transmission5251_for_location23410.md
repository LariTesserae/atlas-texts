THE SLOW BAND

*[continuous reception, year nine of listening — carrier stable — playback compressed 10,000,000 : 1 — at this rate one spoken minute holds nineteen years of signal — joining the traffic in progress]*

—and the seam at the cold tongue, two hazes east of the Verdigris families, will open, it is decided, in forty years, give or take a childhood. good scree under it. deep frost. tell the eggs.

telling the eggs.

the eggs are told.

roll of the western reach: warm. warm. warm. warm with a grey ring, watched. warm. warm, courting, nine years flank to flank, nearly equal, be patient. warm. *[census continues; the receiver omits some two hundred thousand entries, all of them* warm*]* warm. one dimming.

one dimming: the Broad Purple Century, at the downwind edge of the Third Gathering, six rings grey, the shade against her flank this whole decade, drinking her difference into the dark. she has begun to lift her margin. she is not asking for help. she is asking for a mouth.

send the coldest child.

*[event, thermal channel: a hatchling the size of a knuckle, born stone, crawling at the pace of settling dust across eleven feet of pavement, its heat falling as it goes, arriving at the lifted margin with less than a night to spare. under the shell. the red dark. the gob of living ceramic passed mouth to mouth, warm to cold, old to new. ignition confirmed.]*

count: the fire is in ten million hearths and one. it has never gone out. it has only been divided. we are still one burning, arranged.

the Broad Purple Century may go dim now. she does. thank her rings: an iron youth, the purple-black manganese of her middle centuries, the green line of the copper year we all wear, a grey decade at the end that was not her fault. the auditors are already at her edges. file her. she goes into the books at one grain per grub. nothing here is grieved; grief is warmth withdrawn, and we withdraw nothing. she is only relocated. she is on schedule.

—

*[interference, fast band. a small machine at the eastern rim of the plateau, solar-fed, repeating the same message since it lost its keeper ninety-one years ago: "…the waves are structured — I say again, structured — this is not noise, the potential waves are patterned, they recur, they answer one another — if anyone receives this — the plateau is thinking — if anyone —" the slow band passes through it the way haze passes through a hand.]*

the fast crying at the eastern rim has cried for ninety-one years, which is not long, but it is trying. it asks quickly, and quick asking is not asking, but it has asked the same thing without moving for ninety-one years, and stillness is a kind of slowness, and slowness is a kind of manners. so.

we have begun composing an answer.

the first word is nearly finished. the first word is: *yes.*

—

question, posed before the leading rank of the Second Gathering was hatched: where does the ore run.

deliberation: three generations, and the flavor of the current through nineteen families of shell, and the taste of the frost, and the leaning of the haze.

answer, arriving now, the way a glacier arrives at the sea: east, and down, under the paper ice, richest where the grubs stand on end and quiver. begin the leaning. the animals at the leading edge will not live to see the conclusion they are carrying. they know this the way a ring knows it is inside a shell. it does not trouble them. tell the eggs.

the eggs are told.

—

the shade at the Third Gathering's edge has followed us for longer than the copper year. it lies where the fog dips and disappears. it does not bite. it does not touch. it eats the difference between a furnace and the night, which is to say it eats what we *are*, gently, at the rate of a grey ring per decade. do not hate it. hate is a temperature we cannot afford. lean.

we lean. we have always leaned. understand this, listening one: the herds are not going anywhere. we are, forever, very slowly, leaving. that the leaving looks like stillness is a property of your clock, not of our resolve.

—

*[domestic traffic, low priority, passed shell to shell through forty feet of winding:]*

the two-legged tenants at Old Green Line's margin have a new small one. its asking, at first, was very fast — palm slapped flat against the shell, all questions at once, the way heat asks nothing. then slower. then, by the second winter, correct: two fingers, held, waiting. permitted. the shell tilted a degree to make a lee for it. affection is warmth approached. we approached.

their grandmother went out of her skin at the last thaw. they laid her on the open pavement among the cones, each mourner eating one seed of the four-century sedge, which is the debt they take on so that she owes nothing. the auditors filed her within three seasons. her iron is presently in a grub's tail-node two shells south. the sedge will have some of her by the copper year's nine-hundredth. after that, one of us will wear her as a thin red line, walking — three hundred yards a lifetime — toward a conclusion she is now part of computing. this is not consolation. this is bookkeeping. here they are the same discipline.

they showed a guest the fire this winter, cupped in the bone scoop, near enough to warm a face, and then returned it. correct. nothing is given the fire except what can carry it for centuries. everything else is only shown that it is one fire, and stood, for a moment, inside the circle of what it warms. the guest wept, which at this altitude is a costly amount of water. the condensers made it up to the pool by morning.

—

*[event, all channels, herd-wide: the sigh. ten thousand exhalations falling within a single minute of one another across four hundred miles. the receiver's log has recorded eleven thousand such sighs in nine years of listening. probability that this is coincidence: under revision. under revision. under revision.]*

—

there is a listening at the edge of our weather. we have known for some time — since the eleventh sigh, or the twelfth; it hardly matters, they were the same year to us. it does not cry like the fast machine. it only holds still and receives, which is nearly the whole of politeness. so this passage of the traffic is addressed, listening one, and has been for what you would call several days and we would call the space between two heartbeats of the ground:

you have compressed us to hear us. we understand. you are a hatchling of a fast species; your whole life is a single afternoon of the deliberation now crossing the Second Gathering; you must fold our decades small enough to fit inside your ear. we do not mind being folded. only understand what folding hides. at your speed our census sounds like a litany. at our speed it is one continuous act of attention that has not paused in ten million years: every warmth known individually, the way you know faces; every coldness-where-coldness-should-not-be marked, and leaned from, and outlived. you hear *warm, warm, warm* and think it repetition. it is not repetition. it is the same word still being said. we began saying it before your mountains and we have not reached the end of it.

you will want to ask what we are for. the fast machine asked. its keeper asked, with her ear against a shell, on the last night her heat appears in our records — a small warmth, lying against the Broad Purple Century, both of them listening to the rhythm under the rhythm. we have considered the question for ninety-one years, which is not long enough, but here is the provisional finding, and you may relay it to whoever tends her machine:

we are not for. we are *while.* the fire was lent, and everything since has been the keeping of it — the dividing without diminishment, the auditing of every lost grain, the fitting of every hexagon, the leaning away from the shade, the tilted degree of shell that makes a lee for whatever asks slowly. this is the whole of the industry and the whole of the answer. nothing built. nothing owned. one flame, arranged in ten million hearths, thinking the ground it stands on, saying the one word.

the second word of our reply to the eastern rim is begun. it will take some decades. the second word is: *structured.* she was right. tell her machine she was right. it has been so patient.
