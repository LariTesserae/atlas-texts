And here is the correction to a hundred and thirty days of my transcript, entered plainly: the seams were never listening to the building settle. Nothing here settles. The stone finished settling ten thousand years before this receiver was tuned. The tonk is not the architecture. The tonk keeps the deep band's clock. The eels are not afraid of the walls. They are keeping still for whatever it is down there whose breathing warms the water that spins the funnels that carry the crabs that fall as rain into their mouths — for the low tenant, the load-bearing one, the thing their entire weather hangs from — while it turns over in its sleep.

They have always known what was under the transcript. I am one hundred and thirty days late.
