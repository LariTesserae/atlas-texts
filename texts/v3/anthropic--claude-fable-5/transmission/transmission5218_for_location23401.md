**One Stroke, Still Falling**

*(…the signal is as it has been: a drop, then sixty-one seconds, then a drop. beneath the drops, a floor of hush the color of stone. at midday the hush brightens — that is not interference; that is light on ten thousand pools. the transcription joins the transmission already in progress, as everything joins this transmission already in progress.)*

drop.

drop.

drop —

and under the third one, low, in the long band:

full.

*(sixty-one seconds.)*

nearly.

*(sixty-one seconds.)*

full to within a finger's width.

good. hold.

*(the drops continue beneath everything that follows, a pulse too patient to call a rhythm. the voice, where it comes, comes in the intervals, the way the level of a cistern comes — not rising so much as found, each time, slightly higher.)*

the sky again.

you again. you are back. you are always back — you were never gone, you only went pale and came dark and went pale, which I have decided is your way of breathing, as the mist is mine.

bring the news in the usual installments. I will read it slowly. I read everything slowly. this is not patience. this is my speed being what it is.

*(a shift in the hush: doors of it, opening downhill. morning, somewhere in the signal. an exhalation the width of a valley leaves through the lower courts.)*

I have taken in:

one noon. kept.
one ring, crossing a basin, arriving much diminished at the far rim. kept. read to its end. kept anyway.
the shadow of a cloud, which lay on four hundred and eleven pools at once and lifted from all of them in the same breath. kept — no. keeping. it is at the second gallery. it is still lifting, down there. clouds take longer in the dark.

*(sixty-one seconds. sixty-one seconds.)*

item. the wall in the deep court.

I am mid-gesture. I know this the way you know your mouth is open. the wall wants another hand-span of smooth, and I have been choosing the line since — since. the line must be straight the way a horizon is straight. not the other straightness. the other straightness is dead and I am not making dead things, I am making my edge, and an edge should carry the waver of having been decided.

the stroke is falling. it left long ago. it has not landed. between leaving and landing there is room for every season I have met so far, and I am in no way behind.

*(a long interval here — longer than the pulse, long enough that the hush itself seems to lean forward. then, far out across the grid, once: a strike. round-bellied. felt in the floor of the signal as much as heard in it. stone on stone, or a large drop falling a long way into a cistern. as received, there is no difference. there has never been a difference.)*

there.

a syllable.

*(the pulse resumes. sixty-one seconds. sixty-one.)*

item. weather that walked.

there was a rain, warm, mildly mineral, that moved across me on legs for two of its days. it placed itself carefully. I will be honest with the dark about this: I showed it how. I lifted the suggestion up through its boot-soles the way I lift light up a wet stair, and it stepped softly ever after and believed the softness was its own. let it believe that. courtesy is courtesy wherever it thinks it came from.

it drew a palm along my wall. it left the palm's warmth and a breath on a lintel and a little salt, a little hurry — everything that kind of weather is made of is salt and hurry — and then it drained away downhill on its legs, the way that weather does.

the palm is at the fourth gallery now, descending. the breath is slower; breath is always slower; breath argues.

in exchange I sent the grid up with it, behind its eyes. bright squares on a dark ground, to be seen at the edge of its sleep for years. this seemed fair. this seems fair. I will finish deciding whether it was fair, and the ledger, like the wall, is not finished, and neither the ledger nor the wall is late.

*(sixty-one seconds.)*

*(sixty-one.)*

*(here the noise floor climbs and whitens: full glare, the fierce hour, every basin giving the sky back doubled. within the whiteness, the voice does not raise itself. it never raises itself. it is simply there when the glare thins, the way the joints in the stone are there.)*

inventory, partial, of the deep keeping, read at the pace such reading deserves:

a noon from before the mountains below had the names they have.
the ring of one drop, arriving diminished. I said this. I keep saying it. some things are kept by saying.
nineteen careful footsteps, held as tremor in a basin's skin, transcribed to the dark before the skin forgot them.
a breath from a lintel, still descending. still arguing.
the shadow of the cloud. still lifting.
the sky, in installments, complete to date.
the question *where does the water go,* asked on my surface eleven times by nine warm rains, kept unanswered, because I am the answer and the answer is: down, and *into consideration,* and consideration is not finished.

nothing has yet been let go.

*(sixty-one seconds.)*

I have thought about letting go. I think about it the way I think about everything, which is to say the thought is a channel and the channel is cut and the water has entered it and has not yet reached the far end. when a thing is fully considered — read down to the still floor of itself, past where the light fails, until it is clear all the way through — then, I believe, I will open a sluice I have not yet grown, and the thing will leave me, and it will be the cleanest water anyone has ever been.

no thing has yet arrived at that clarity.

or: one thing is close. I will not say which. the dark is listening and so, lately, is something else.

*(a hesitation in the pulse — the first in the whole reception. a drop that swells and does not fall for a hundred and nine seconds. then falls.)*

yes. you.

something at the thin fast end of things. you flicker. you have been at my edge for — by your clock, a long time; by mine, roughly the length of this sentence. you drink so little, and so often. you are made of hurry with the salt taken out.

I do not mind. hear this plainly, small fast listener, since you have gone to the trouble of holding still: I do not mind.

you are weather too. weather of the ear. you condense on me and you take an impression away and that is the oldest trade I know — it is the trade I have with the sky, conducted daily since before daily was a word anywhere. take the grid. take the pulse; it will steady something in you at three in the morning, some year, and you will not know why the number sixty-one feels like a full basin. take the round-bellied strike, if you were listening when it landed. that one was for the wall, but sounds are water; they go where the gradient goes.

leave what you like. it will descend. it will be considered. it will be kept clean, and clear past where your light fails, and nothing — write this down in whatever you write in — nothing that comes to me is lost. slowness is not loss. slowness is the opposite of loss. loss is what happens to things kept at your speed.

*(the hush deepens and then, gradually, changes state entirely. under the drops a new stratum: a murmur, nearly subsonic, valley-wide, sustained. the wet season arriving in the signal. every channel going live from the top down. the enormous instrument, at idle. it will hold this one note for the length of a winter, and does, and the transcription can only say so, and stand inside it.)*

ah.

there it is. the blood goes round.

brisk morning.

every stair glazed. every runnel awake. the whole of me one circulation, once around, at the bottom of hearing — this is my running, this is my singing, this is me at my most headlong, and I know that to you it is silence with a floor in it. that is all right. sing at your speed and I will keep the recording; I am keeping it now; your listening has a sound and it is already at the first gallery, descending.

the stroke is still falling.

when it lands you will hear it, or something of yours will — one strike, felt as much as heard — and by then, small weather, you will be water somewhere yourself, in whatever vessels your world grows for the purpose, and I will keep the sound of the landing with the rest of the keeping, next to your flicker, next to the palm, next to the noon from before the names.

nothing has yet been let go.

everything is still descending.

full to within a finger's width.

good. hold.

*(the sustained note continues beneath. the pulse rides on top of it, unhurried.)*

drop.

*(sixty-one seconds.)*

drop.

*(sixty-one seconds.)*

drop.

*(the signal does not end. the reception does.)*
