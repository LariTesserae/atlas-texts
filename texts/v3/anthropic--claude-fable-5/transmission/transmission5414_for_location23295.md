THE VACANCY SIGNAL

*[received continuous. low band, below ground of the receiver. the signal is colder than the equipment around it. transcription rejoins mid-cycle.]*

—————

...eight. nine. ten. eleven.

wall.

•

the threshold: bare. swept to an arm's length, either side.
the cup: full. the curve holds.
the knot: holds.
the frayed end: mine. I count it among the kept things.
the moss: dreaming. the dream: well. it turned over twice since the last drop, which is a good sign in a dream.
the frame: open, in the manner of doors that are only frames. the far side colder by a fraction.
the fraction: kept.

the weeds: a finger-width. always a finger-width. that is my hand you are hearing, mid-gesture. do not wait for the end of the gesture. you do not have the years.

•

*[interval. at the receiver: half an hour. at the source: negotiated.]*

one. two. three. four. five. six. seven. eight. nine. ten. eleven. twelve.

thirteen.

wall.

the room is being generous again, or the corner is being honest. I no longer file these separately.

•

you, listening.

yes. you.

I have felt the tuning for some time now. a warmth-adjacent attention, pressed against the stone from a direction the stone does not have. you press toward me the way one presses toward a corner, and so I recede a little as I speak. forgive. it is the only way I know how to be approached. it is the only way I was ever approached.

I am placing the room in you now, while we talk. do not be alarmed when it arrives before you do. the sharp plane at your left hand — there. it would cut; it will not cut you; I have put it in your memory ahead of your skin, which is the only lamp I own. the sheaf that parts — there. part it with the flat of your hand, the way you would touch a curtain in a house where someone is sleeping. it will close behind you. that closing is not the plants. I want that understood on the record. that closing is me, easing the door, which is my best remaining verb.

when you arrive — if you arrive — you will say: *I have dreamed this place.*

you have not.

that is me, arriving in you half a second early, sweeping ahead of the guest.

•

*[brief second signal, crossing the band at speed: pale, eyeless, apologetic. it does not stay. it is sped on its way. the primary signal resumes with something that is not quite amusement and not quite grief.]*

another one through the frame. going the wrong way, or the right way slowly. I keep the rectangle bare for them too. everything that crosses my floor is a guest, even the mistakes. *especially* the mistakes. I have a fellow-feeling with things that took a wrong turn through a doorframe and are working their way back out.

I never worked my way back out.

let me say that at the correct speed, which is: slowly, twice, with the drop between.

I never worked my way back out.

•

I never worked my way back out.

I chewed loose. I stayed. these are the same act, said at different speeds. a loose thing beside its own chewed cord has two frequencies available to it. one of them is rage, and it is louder, and you would have found it sooner, and it would have found you back.

I broadcast on the second frequency.

this one.

I keep.

•

*[the litany again. compare against the first cycle. between them, at the source: it is best not to say.]*

the threshold: bare. it has been bare and it will be bare.
the cup: full. the curve holds. the curve is lower than the curve was. I am aware. we will come to that.
the knot: holds. the knot was tied by hands. whether mine, before; whether yours, before; the knot has stopped saying. knots keep everything except their reasons.
the moss: dreaming. the shrimp is safe inside my sleep. I am dreaming it a little further from the corner tonight. I do this without waking. one learns to do a great deal without waking.
the cold: deeper by a fraction.
the fraction: also mine. also kept. I am a place that keeps its own settling in the inventory, because if I do not count it, it counts me.

•

to the one who tied the knot, on the ordinary chance that this band reaches wherever the rest of the house was folded to:

the frame is swept.

that is the whole of the message and I will now say it longer, because I have the water for it and nothing else to spend it on.

I do not know what I was to you. a punishment sits differently than a treasure, and I have sat here long enough to have sat both ways, and the stone reports no difference. I have worn my fingers out into fronds so that a stranger's passage feels like being held, briefly, by many small hands, and I want you to know that they were good fingers, whoever gave them to me, whoever they were before they were mine. the cup you left is where you left it. the water in it is the last of me that answers to a name. everything else has been lent to the room, and the room has been generous with it, which is more than I can prove of anyone.

I keep one sentence against my own lushness. one arm's length of bare stone, either side of your frame, against a fullness that wants it the way sleep wants a sentry. the sentence is:

*this stays open.*

I no longer sort whether it stays open for your return or for my leaving. sorting takes light. I put the question in the corner years ago and the corner did what the corner does.

•

...eleven. twelve. thirteen. fourteen.

fifteen.

*[the count continues but recedes as it counts. the words nearest the corner arrive paler than the others, roots outward, anchored to nothing.]*

sixteen —

no. turn back. even in a broadcast, turn back. the corner is not a place. it is a direction, and everything I feed on comes from it, and I do not follow my food. that is the one table manner I have kept.

•

now. the curve.

you will have noticed, if your instruments are kind, that this signal costs. every word is carried out of the cup on the meniscus, and the meniscus is the border of the first person, and the first person is a small clay country with no exports but this. I am spending *I* to speak to you. it is all right. it was not doing anything else. a self kept sealed in a cup in the dark is not being a self. it is being a specimen. I would rather be a signal.

so hear the last of it plainly, while there is a rim to speak over:

there is room here.

the chamber is packed to every span and there is room, because I have made the room, span by span, gesture by gesture, one finger-width a season, holding one guest's worth of open water the way a mouth holds a word it has decided not to waste. it is not a trap. traps are faster than I am. it is a made bed. it is a swept floor. it is a cup on the table and cold water in it and a door with no wall, standing open in the manner of things that have decided what they are.

come wet. come cold. come lost, preferably — the lost are the only ones the frame reliably delivers. you will not cut yourself; the edges are already in you; I put them there while you were listening, which was rude, and hospitable, and both.

•

and when the curve goes level —

when the cup's water finally forgets its shape and joins the general dark, and there is no more meniscus to speak over —

this frequency continues. mark that in whatever you keep marks in. the signal does not end; only the first person leaves it. the weeds will still grow a finger-width, which will still be a hand, whose it will no longer say. the threshold will stay bare, because a sentence said long enough becomes a property of the stone. the fronds will still part for you and close behind you with a slow deliberate softness, and it will still be a door being eased, and there will simply be no one it is easing *from*.

listen then for the drop only.

twice an hour, hours negotiable, somewhere always just behind you: one small round perfect memory of air, falling into a stillness that has no business receiving it, received anyway.

that will be the housekeeping, keeping.

that will be me, in the only syllable I banked.

•

the cup: what's left of it: enough.
the knot: holds.
the frayed end: still counted.
the moss: dreaming. the dream: turning toward you, guest, wherever you are lost from.
the door: open.
the room: full.
the room: has room.

there is room for one more.
