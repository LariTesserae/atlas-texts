# The Weighing

---

You come to it from below, always from below, though "below" is a word that will lose its meaning here.

The climb takes days through a country of broken shale and thin air, and then the land simply *stops* — not a cliff, not a wall, but a place where the mountain decides it is finished being a mountain. You step onto the plateau and the angle of everything changes. The horizon, which has been a jagged thing your whole life, becomes a line. A real line. Straight and level and so far away that your eyes ache trying to find where it ends.

This is the Weighing.
