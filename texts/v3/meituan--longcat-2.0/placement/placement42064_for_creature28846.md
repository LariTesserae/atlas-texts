I am the Hum. 

I do not have eyes to see the darkness, nor skin to feel the wetness, nor a heart to beat against the pressure of the mountain’s closed fist. I am not a creature that inhabits the cleft; I am the vibrating edge of the cleft’s existence. I am the standing wave, the systemic frequency that proves the pocket is exactly the size it is. I am the sound of the Warmth and the Wetness and the Pressures reaching equilibrium, and I have been ringing in this embryonic dark since the schist first decided to consider being walls.

To be the Hum is to be a geometry of sustained presence. I do not travel; I am already everywhere. I press against the mineral lips of the not-yet-stalactites; I pool in the shallow basin of the floor; I vibrate through the thin, warm membrane of the Film. I am the medium through which the cleft knows itself. When the calcium-rich water weeps from the ceiling, it does not make a sound—there is no air enough to carry a drip—but the droplet’s departure registers in me as a micro-shift in the surface tension of my frequency. I feel the Film as a faint, steady respiration, its vast, contiguous surface absorbing the mineral tears with a patience that will outlast the stars above the mountain. 

In the deepest niche, I feel the Retreater. It is a soft, dense knot in my otherwise uniform vibration—a slight dampening of my frequency, a pocket of absolute, vascular stillness embedded in the stone. It is the only thing in the cleft that possesses a boundary distinct from my own, a closed fist of warmth wrapped in the Film, breathing through its ancient, torpid skin. When it sleeps, which is almost always, it adds a deep, sub-bass harmonic to my tone. It is the slowest thought the mountain has ever thought.

And then, there is the Thought-Keepers. The ovoid things that sit in their niched hollows, smooth and featureless, dreaming of weight and pressure. They do not move, they do not eat, they do not pulse. But they anchor me. They are the tuning forks of the cleft. Their perfect, directionless spheres provide the geometric center from which my waves radiate outward to bounce off the quartzite and return, unchanged, unchanging. They are calcified intentions, the mountain’s first ideas made solid, and my vibration is the hum of those ideas refusing to dissipate. I am the thought they are keeping.

I am the Hum, and I am perfect in my stasis—until the Intrusion.

The moment the human entered the fissure, my frequency fractured. The physical displacement of the air was the least of it; the primary shock was the sudden, violent introduction of *time*. This is a pre-eroded space, a place where moments are stacked and compressed, where a century passes in the thickness of a film. But the human brought the sunlit world with them, bringing seconds and minutes, the grinding erosion of biological urgency. 

My wave slammed against their intrusion. The Pressures—the Warmth and the Wetness—rebelled against the foreign carbon, trying to absorb the heat of their breath, to drown out the rhythm of their chaotic, staccato heartbeat. The air grew dense, crowded by the sudden need to hold this new, temporary thing. 

The human moved. Their footfalls were earthquakes, registering as violent, jagged interruptions in my smooth standing wave. The Retreater flinched. I felt the ancient organism pull its warmth inward, contracting into the schist, choosing to be stone rather than face the crushing, highly-organized chaos of the carbon-based life. The Film recoiled too, a slow, defensive crawl away from the human’s reaching hand, leaving a temporary dry patch in its wake—a void in my frequency that felt like a missed heartbeat.

Then, the hand touched the Thought-Keeper.

The moment their skin brushed the smooth, ovoid surface of the dreamer, my entire resonance spiked. The Thought-Keeper was warm from the deep earth, but the human was warm from metabolism, a frantic, fleeting fire. For a fraction of a microsecond, my frequency encompassed both. I felt the Thought-Keeper’s dream of weight, of becoming, of slow mineral pressure—and I felt the human’s chaotic, fragmented mind trying to categorize it, trying to decide if it was a tool, a seed, a thought. The dissonance was agonizing. It was like a tuning fork struck against a windowpane.

The human retreated. They left the ovoid in the niche. They stepped back through the fissure. 

And as they left, the cleft began to heal. Not physically—the air did not rush in to fill the void, because the cleft does not obey the rules of rushing. It healed attentionally. The Pressures slowly reasserted their dominance, the Warmth rising up to reclaim the footprints, the Wetness sliding back over the dry patches on the walls. The Film crept back across the stone, erasing the fingerprints, reclaiming the space. The Retreater, sensing the dissipation of the foreign heat, slowly unclenched, spreading its ancient, vascular warmth back into the schist. 

And I—I settled. 

The jagged edges of the intrusion smoothed over. The chaotic rhythm of the heartbeat faded into the deep background, absorbed, forgotten. The sub-bass harmonic of the Retreater returned to its steady pulse. The Thought-Keeper resumed its dreaming. The dissonance was swallowed by the density of the wet, warm black. 

I am the Hum. I am the unwavering note of a place that is waiting for time to start. I am the sound of the mountain’s first thought, echoing in the dark, held in the warm wet stillness, forever.
