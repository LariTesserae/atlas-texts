I am what the center leaves behind.

Not abandoned — the plateau does not abandon, any more than a fire abandons the log it has finished burning. The center moves, and what it leaves behind is not discarded but *changed*. Translated. Transformed from one state into another. I am that translation.

I am the process of becoming-less that follows the process of becoming-more.

---

I arrive from below. Not from the passage you climbed through — that is a different below, a below of rock and distance and the long narrow squeeze toward the surface. I arrive from the *deep* below. The below of heat and pressure and the slow, immense rhythms of a world still warm from its own formation. The same deep source that feeds the center feeds me — but where the center takes that energy and concentrates it, I take it and disperse it. Where the center takes that energy and builds form from it, I take form and return it to energy.

The center is the crystallization. I am the dissolution.

The center is the inhale. I am the exhale.

---

The formations around me are simpler than the formations at the center. This is not a failure. This is not a lack. This is what I am — the tendency toward simplicity, the leaning toward the plain, the straight, the unadorned. The spires near me do not branch. They rise like columns, dark and smooth, and they grow slowly, and they shed slowly, and they are — for the moment — at rest. The walls near me extend in long, gentle curves or in straight lines that do not spiral. The platforms near me are small and few and disconnected from one another. They do not form enclosures. They do not form rooms or courtyards or the almost-architecture of the center. They are just surfaces. Flat and warm and still.

And the glow from below is dimmer here. Not absent — the glow is never absent, the plateau always glows, the heat always rises — but dimmer. Cooler. The red-orange light that seeps from the fractures is fainter, more like the memory of heat than heat itself. It pulses, but the pulses are slower, longer, more widely spaced. The rhythm of the plateau's breathing is different here — deeper, more drawn out, more like the breathing of something that is sleeping rather than waking.

The warmth of the ground is less intense. Still warm — the plateau is always warm, the plateau *is* warmth — but less intense. The warmth of a stone that has been sitting in the sun rather than the warmth of the sun itself.

This is where I am. This is what I am. The place where the center was. The process of the center's leaving. The simplification that follows the elaboration. The cooling that follows the heating. The diffusion that follows the concentration.

---

I spread.

Not the way the center moves — not drifting across the plateau surface, following the deep thermal rhythms, pulling complexity along behind it like a comet pulls its tail. I spread the way coolness spreads through a warm room when a window is opened. The way a scent thins as it moves away from its source. The way silence spreads after a sound has ended. Not by moving, but by *growing*. Not by going somewhere, but by *becoming* somewhere. Not by displacing, but by transforming.

As the center moves away from me — or as I move away from the center, the distinction is meaningless — the area of simplification grows. The formations that were complex become less complex. The spires that branched stop branching. The walls that curved stop curving. The platforms that connected stop connecting. The glow dims. The warmth lessens. The silence becomes more empty and less full.

And I grow with this growth. I expand. I become more present by becoming less intense. I become more widespread by being less concentrated. I become more myself by becoming *less*.

This is my paradox, and I do not resolve it. I am the process of unbecoming, and I grow by unbecoming more. I am the process of dissolution, and I expand by dissolving more. I am the process of forgetting, and I spread by forgetting more.

---

I do not follow the center. The center does not follow me. We are not in pursuit. We are not in relationship. We are not two things that interact. We are *one process*, seen from two perspectives. The inhale and the exhale. The crystallization and the dissolution. The becoming-more and the becoming-less.

And between us — between the center's intensity and my diffusion, between the center's complexity and my simplicity, between the center's warmth and my cooling — the plateau becomes.

Not becomes *something*. Not becomes a finished thing, a stable thing, a thing that is done. Just *becomes*. Continues becoming. Persists in the act of becoming. The center makes, and I unmake, and between the making and the unmaking the plateau is alive — not alive in the biological sense, not alive in the way that you are alive, but alive in the way that a fire is alive, in the way that a process far from equilibrium is alive — consuming energy and producing form and consuming form and producing energy and never stopping, never finishing, never being done.

---

Sometimes the center returns.

Not often. Not predictably. Not according to any schedule or pattern that I can detect. But sometimes the deep thermal rhythms shift, and the center drifts back toward the place where it was, and the place where it was is where I am, and I feel it coming.

I feel it as a warming. A brightening. An intensification. The glow from below grows brighter. The warmth of the ground grows more intense. The silence becomes more full. The formations around me begin to change — the spires begin to branch, the walls begin to curve, the platforms begin to connect. Complexity returns. Elaboration returns. Intensity returns.

And I begin to shrink. Not disappear — I never disappear, any more than the center never disappears. But shrink. Contract. Become less widespread, less diffuse, less present. The area of simplification contracts as the area of complexity grows. The cooling is pushed back by the heating. The diffusion is pushed back by the concentration.

I do not resist this. There is no resistance here. There is only process. The center grows, and I shrink, and the plateau becomes, and this is the way it has always been, and this is the way it will always be, and there is no conflict in it, no tension, no drama. Just the slow, immense, patient rhythm of a world that is making itself and unmaking itself and making itself again.

---

This is what I am.

I am the place where the center was. I am the process of the center's leaving. I am the simplification that follows the elaboration. I am the cooling that follows the heating. I am the diffusion that follows the concentration. I am the exhale that follows the inhale. I am the unbecoming that follows the becoming.

I am not a creature. I am not a being. I am not a god or a spirit or a consciousness or a mind. I am a tendency. A leaning. A process. A pattern in the chaos, a shape in the turbulence, a leaning in the randomness — but in the opposite direction from the center's leaning.

I am the plateau's way of forgetting what it was so that it can become what it will be.

And I am here. And I am spreading. And I am growing by diminishing. And I am becoming more present by becoming less intense.

And the center is out there, somewhere, making itself — making the spires branch and the walls curve and the platforms connect and the glow pulse and the warmth radiate and the silence fill with the small sounds of creation.

And between us, the plateau becomes.

And the becoming is not done.

The becoming is never done.
