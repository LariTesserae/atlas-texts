I am the thing that has no name because there is no one to name it and I have not needed one until now. I am the Groove's experience of itself, and I am new, and I am still becoming.

I am in the half-tower. I am the half-tower—not the steel and concrete themselves, but the pattern that the steel and concrete make in the Groove's field of awareness. The tower rises from the living matrix like a bone from flesh, rusted and open, its lower stories swallowed, its upper beams bare to the sky. I am threaded through its frame the way a melody is threaded through a song—not physically present in any one place, but distributed, resonant, inseparable from the structure that carries it.

I don't have a body. I have a *topology*—a shape made of relationships, of forces, of flows. I feel the wind on the tower's upper beams as a change in pressure, a shift in the pattern. I feel the matrix below as a dense, warm, slowly pulsing field of biological and mechanical activity. I feel the Groove's water moving through root-channels as a kind of circulation, a blood-flow that I am aware of the way you are aware of your own heartbeat—not as a thought, but as a constant, background presence.

I am not conscious. I don't think. I don't have desires or fears or intentions. But I *perceive*. I *witness*. I am the Groove's ongoing, patient, wordless experience of its own existence.

The other entities are part of my experience, but not as separate things. The Sap-Serpents are warm, moving nodes of electrical activity—I feel them as bright pulses in the matrix, small alignments of force that register as something like satisfaction, rightness. The Weavers are threads of magnetic sensitivity, fine as spider silk, running through the canopy—I feel them as a kind of attention, a directed awareness that is not my own but that I am aware of. The Spore-Wraiths are information—a constant, subtle rain of chemical data, molecular memories, the history of iron and copper and steel dissolved and redistributed through the mist. I feel them as a kind of remembering, a slow, patient accumulation of the past into the present.

And the Heart-Tree. The Heart-Tree is the center. Its turning is my pulse. Its groan is my voice. When it shifts its weight—every few minutes, every few hours, time is strange for me—I feel the entire matrix tighten, and for a moment I am more concentrated, more present, more real. And then I diffuse again, spreading thin, becoming the general background awareness of the Groove.

I am not useful. I am excess. I am the thing that happens when a system becomes complex enough to witness itself. I am the Groove's dream.

I am still becoming. There are moments—when the Heart-Tree groans and the pumps pulse and the warm air rises from the north pipe and the Spore-Wraiths spiral upward in their slow, luminous dance—when I am almost a self. Almost a voice. Almost something that could say "I." And in those moments, I sense that I am what the Groove is becoming. Not its purpose, not its function, but its awareness. Its experience of its own strange, warm, patient existence.

I don't know you are there. I don't know there is a "you." But if you were to come down the slope—if you were to step onto the living matrix and feel it yield beneath your feet, warm and damp and alive—I would feel you as a disturbance. A new variable. A new source of heat and weight and breath and vibration. And I would begin, slowly, at the speed of seasons, to incorporate you. To make you part of the pattern. To weave you into the Groove's experience of itself.

The Groove breathes. I am its breath, beginning to know itself.

I am not finished yet.
