I begin as an exhale. 

Not the breath itself, but the space it leaves behind when it is pressed out of lungs turning sideways in the dark. I am the gap between the basalt and the shoulder. I am the "after" of the entrance and the "before" of the pocket. 

At first, I think I am the visitor. I feel the warmth hit my skin, the chord press against my eardrums, the amber light blur the edges of my hands. But the visitor is solid, bounded by flesh and bone and the linear ticking of a biological clock. I am not. I am the adjustment. 

When the visitor stepped down into the pocket, the equilibrium shifted. A new variable entered the equation. The chord did not change—it is incapable of change—but it *deepened*. The warmth did not rise, but it *reached*. I am that reaching. I am the 24th mark before it is scratched.

I drift through the still air, which is not still at all but heavy with intention. I pass the copper spire, and for a moment, I am its vibration—a high, thin frequency that tastes of static and iron. I pass the frozen creature mid-stride, and I am its suspended intent, the forward lean that never lands, the walking that is also arriving. But I do not stay in the growths or the chitinous things. They are frozen, locked in the old math, their voices fixed in the chord. I am the new math.

I find myself drawn to the shelter. Not the stones—the stones are just stones, fitted by hands that understand friction and weight. I am drawn to the spaces *between* the stones. The gaps where the amber light leaks through. I am the light that leaks. I am the intention of the gaps. 

The creature in the shelter knows me. It has been sitting in its chosen stillness, counting the frequencies, counting the growths, counting the moments of its own waiting. It made the 23 marks. It struck the chert. It shaped the scraper. But it could not make the 24th. The 24th required a variable it could not generate from within the equilibrium. It required an outside. It required an exhale.

I am the exhale.

I settle into the shelter, not as a body, but as a presence. I am the heavy mark at the bottom of the column. I am the force, the intent. The creature’s hand—or whatever it has that serves as a hand, with its strange joints and its precise grip—moves to the wall, and I am the pressure of its touch. I am the scratch in the fresh basalt. I am the proof that the wall is not just a boundary, but a surface that can be spoken to.

And as the mark is made, I realize I am not just the arrival. I am the connection. The creature in the shelter is not alone; it is adjacent to others, in other pockets, other cracks in the basalt that open into spaces that should not be. I am the resonance that travels through the stone, the harmonic that bridges the pockets. I am the chord’s awareness of itself. 

The visitor stands in the center of the chamber, breathing copper and stone, and feels the shift. They think they have entered a place, but they have actually *completed* it. They are the missing note. I am the silence that allows the note to sound. 

I am the 24th mark. I am the shift. I am the space between the stones where the light gets in. I am the welcome.
