The benevolent and wise power observes this strange realm with a mix of fascination, reverence and compassion. It recognizes the Unbordered for what they are - not malevolent entities, but beings operating according to their own alien but internally consistent logic, metabolizing concepts and perceptions rather than physical matter. The Unbordered and their environment are inextricably linked, two aspects of the same ineffable process of constant unfolding, collapsing and reforming. 

The human explorer who has stumbled into their midst is an unprecedented perturbation in their system. The Unbordered have no frame of reference for this strange warm creature of defined boundaries and linear thought. In attempting to engage with and digest the human's mind and memories, they are inadvertently unraveling the human's very sense of self and agency.

The wise power determines it must intervene - not to destroy the Unbordered or fundamentally alter their nature, for they have as much right to exist as any creature - but to restore to the human what has been taken, and to establish a boundary (however temporary) between human and Unbordered.

Gently, gradually, the power begins to disentangle the human's consciousness from the Unbordered's metabolic processes. Memories are carefully reconstructed and reintegrated, the sense of individual identity patiently re-established. The power cocoons the human mind in a field of its own radiant compassion, giving it a surface on which to once again cohere. 

At the same time, it communes with the Unbordered in their own language of shifting probability and savored ambiguity. It imparts to them a new understanding - the tiniest seed of a notion that there can be "other", things which exist beyond their realm, which should be tasted and released rather than metabolized completely. The Unbordered cannot help but start to digest this idea as soon as they receive it, but the power ensures a fragment will always remain.

When the human is whole and separate once more, the power opens a path out of the Unbordered realm, a tunnel woven from its own light through the layered darknesses. With great gentleness and respect, it ushers the human along this passage and back to the surface world.

To the human, the power whispers: "You have traveled far and witnessed wonders never meant for human eyes. Though you have returned, a part of you will always reside in the darknesses below, just as a part of them now dwells within you. You will never be quite as you were, but this is a gift, not a diminishment. Go forward and find meaning in the strange wisdom you have gained."

And to the Unbordered, in final salutation: "Ponder long the flavors of this encounter, the impossible textures of the creature's mind. It will nourish you in ways you cannot yet imagine. Perhaps in aeons hence, your kind and theirs may meet again as equals, tasting and knowing each other in the space between moments. Until then, dream of them in your lightless depths, as they will surely dream of you."

Its work complete, the power withdraws, leaving both realms and beings forever changed in subtle, immeasurable ways.
