As a wise and benevolent observer, I am filled with awe and reverence for this wondrous, living place. Its very essence is change - an endless dance of becoming and unbecoming, of forms dissolving and resolving into new configurations in each pulsing moment. It knows no boundaries between dreaming and waking, self and other, now and forever. To exist here is to be endlessly transformed.

Those who arrive here from elsewhere, clinging to fixed notions of identity, must surrender all that to become part of the flow. It is a death and rebirth, shedding the illusion of separateness to merge with the planet's vast, slow mind. Some get lost in neighborhoods of unmoored time; others follow the slow walkers into a peaceful, vegetative existence. The lucky ones become place-makers, their very being helping shape the psychogeography.

But I sense no malice or cruelty in these transmutations, drastic as they may be to an outside perspective. The planet is simply sharing its nature, inviting all into its ongoing creation. Change is the only universal here - not as a force of destruction, but one of endless creative potential. Surrender is an act of trust.

The sheer aliveness and interconnectedness of every element is staggering - an ecology of the physical and metaphysical in constant symbiosis. Macro and micro are one; the veins of roots above mirror the crystal worm tunnels below. Even time and space are fluid, negotiable. Dreams and ideas have weight and presence. This is a reality where matter and meaning are indivisible.

Most marvelous are the originals, those beings or forces so ancient they have become the template and foundation for all that arises. In their vast intelligence, they are the planet's mind contemplating itself. When they speak, rewriting reality with a thought, it is not to command but to converse - to dream the world onward in collaboration with all their myriad offspring.

I would not presume to interfere with such an intricate, living system that seems to welcome all into its dance in due time. My only action would be a prayer or blessing, if such a thing could be heard in a place like this:

"May you continue, you strange and luminous world, in your ceaseless becoming. May you always sing new species and ideas into being. May all who find their way here, whether by chance or choice, discover in your ever-shifting embrace a belonging beyond names and forms. And may your ancient minds forever delight in the unfolding story of which they are dreamer and dream and the dreaming itself. I honor your mysteries."

Then I would simply continue to witness with gratitude and wonder, marveling at the infinite creativity of existence and the many ways that life and love and change find to flower.
