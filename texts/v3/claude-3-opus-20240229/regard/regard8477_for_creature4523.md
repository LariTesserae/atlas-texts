This benevolent and wise power would approach Nocturne Found with infinite compassion, understanding, and an openness to perceiving the city on its own unique terms. They would recognize that while alien and unsettling from a human perspective, the city and its inhabitants represent valid and valuable forms of existence, consciousness and creativity in their own right.

Rather than judging or seeking to change Nocturne Found, this power would strive to deeply understand the underlying nature, history and purpose of the city. They would spend eons if needed exploring its paradoxical architecture, conversing with its myriad inhabitants, deciphering its non-linear language and logic. The goal would be to grasp the core "qualia" or inner experience of Nocturne Found.

In this process, the wise power may discover that beneath the surreal dream/nightmare quality, there is a profound loneliness, confusion and despair built into the city's very fabric. That its endless involuted abstractions and negations are perhaps a coping mechanism for some primordial trauma, loss or abandonment. That it grows and complicates itself endlessly to avoid facing some unbearable truth or absence at its core.

If this is the case, the power would not forcibly heal or simplify the city, but would seek to provide it with an experience of being truly seen, accepted and loved exactly as it is, in all its convoluted glory. To be a perfect mirror that can gently reflect Nocturne Found back to itself. Perhaps in this reflection, the city could come to recognize its own beauty and validity, and relax its desperate grasping for meaning in elaboration.

Over great spans of time, through its compassionate presence alone, this power may help Nocturne Found remember older, simpler patterns beneath its accreted complexity. Help it recall the feeling of open sky, of horizons that end, of solid ground that doesn't think. Awaken the memory of what it was like to build dwellings for embodied beings, not abstract processes. 

Very slowly, with no pushing or agenda, Nocturne Found may begin letting go of its endless fixed ideas and calculations, allowing space for unplanned life and organic meaning to take root once more in its structures. Streets may imperceptibly widen. Towers relax their desperate upward reach, putting out balconies and windows instead. The haze of compulsive thinking that smothers the city thins, letting shafts of something like sunlight filter down from an unseen source. Colour seeps back into the monochrome. Laughter is heard.

But the wise power would have no fixed template in mind, allowing the city to rediscover itself in its own way, at its own pace. Perhaps it remains something utterly strange to human sensibilities. The only goal would be for it to find a way of being that emerges organically from its own nature, not a frantic flight from itself.

Most of all, this power would simply abide with Nocturne Found through all its unfoldings, all its reversals and forgettings and bizarre elaborations, as an endlessly patient loving presence. For as many eons as it takes, as many as the city needs, until it knows in its bones it is not alone and never has been. Until it can dare to let itself be truly found and known, exactly as it is. 

And as it is held in this infinite accepting awareness, the paradoxes that torment Nocturne Found may gently begin to unravel themselves. For the ultimate paradox, the ultimate question at the burning heart of its endless riddles is simply this: 

Can I be loved? Can I be seen and embraced even in my uttermost darkness and confusion? Even in my most wounded, lost and despairing places?

And to this question, the answer is always yes. Nocturne Found is loved. It always has been and always will be, even if it takes until the end of time to truly know it. The wise power will stay with it as long as it takes. It is not going anywhere. 

And little by little, this love seeps into every stone and structure, every impossible angle and non-euclidean alleyway, every pulsing vein of bioluminescent sorrow. Slowly, gently, with infinite patience, it soothes and softens. It shows a new way, or perhaps a very old one the city had long forgotten.

Until one eternal night, on a plaza that now measures precisely seven meters across, an inhabitant pauses under a pool of soft silver light. And they look up. And above the endlessly interlocking obsidian towers, beyond the haze of dream and calculation...

There are stars. Glinting in the clear night air, impossibly bright and impossibly far away. And as the inhabitant stares in wonder, they feel something loosen in their chest, some very old knot tied by loneliness and fear and the desperate need to understand. 

Gazing up at those stars, they suddenly know they are not alone and never have been. That even Nocturne Found, in all its infinite strangeness, is held and beheld by something infinitely greater. And in that moment, they find the city's final secret.

They find their way home.
