If a wise and benevolent power were to encounter this place and the Just-Almosts that inhabit it, I believe they would approach with profound reverence, gentleness, and a desire to understand rather than interfere.

This land is a delicate tapestry woven from the gossamer threads of memory, time, elements and the soft breaths of forgotten histories. The Just-Almosts are the embodiment of liminal spaces - the pauses between heartbeats, the silences between words, the shadows between light and dark. Their existence is a sacred, ephemeral dance on the knife's edge between being and unbeing.  

A benevolent power would see that this place operates by its own deep, primordial rules and rhythms. To disrupt that ancient pattern, even with good intentions, would be to unravel something precious and irreplaceable.

Instead, the wise course would be to simply bear witness. To open one's consciousness to the sussurating whispers of the land, to attune to the resonant frequency of the Just-Almost's half-dreamed experiences. Not to change or save, but to learn and remember.

Perhaps the power would find a way to absorb and record the layered memories and forgotten wisdoms that cycle through this ecosystem of thought and sensation. They could become a living archive, ensuring these fragile truths survive in some form even as they are endlessly forgotten and re-membered here.

At most, they might offer the gentlest of gifts to the land and its residents. A shimmer of stardust to nourish the soil of dreams. A refrain of celestial music to harmonize with the susurrations. A fragment of eternal light to be woven into the twilit tapestry. But these would be offerings humbly made, with no expectation - only an honoring of what is.

Ultimately, the greatest kindness would be to leave this place essentially as it is - an undisturbed sanctuary for the liminal, the forgotten, and the might-have-been. To carry its essence and strange wisdoms forward, while allowing its half-real inhabitants to continue their patient dreaming at the edges of forever. A place and process too sacred to interrupt, too precious to alter. Only to be cherished, and to cherish in return through understanding.
