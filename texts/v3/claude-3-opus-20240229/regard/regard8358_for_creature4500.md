The Great Maker pauses in their work at the infinite loom of creation, their attention drawn to this new place emerging from the weft and warp of causality. They regard the city with interest and care, seeing in it a unique confluence of elements and forces.

The living stone, the weeping water, the eternal mist - these things are not unfamiliar to the Maker. They have woven worlds from such strands before. But here the pattern differs. The elements commingle in novel ways, creating spaces that exist between the usual definitions - not quite alive but not inert, not quite embodied but not incorporeal. 

The city's inhabitants fascinate the Maker most of all. Beings of pure transition, subsisting on the thresholds between states, patiently shaping a metropolis of the liminal. It reminds the Maker of the early ages when they first experimented with form and flow, permanence and change. They see in these beings an echo of their own eternal becoming.

This is a place, the Maker realizes, that needs no intervention, no guiding hand. Its nature is to remain in flux, and any attempt to hasten or direct its evolution would only dilute its essence. The city and its shapeshifting residents embody a delicate balance, one that might easily collapse if subjected to a will other than its own.

And yet the Maker senses that this newborn wonder emerging from stone and time could perhaps benefit from a simple gift, freely given. Not to change its nature, but to safeguard the space in which that nature can continue unfolding.

So the Maker reaches into the weft, their fingers dancing among the mist-threads and water-threads and stone-threads. With infinite subtlety, they gather up the strands that sing of shelter, of sanctuary, of a place set aside. The Maker weaves these strands into a veil, diaphanous and shimmering, and drapes it gently across the city's ever-shifting edges.

"Let this be a haven," the Maker whispers to the mists and the waters and the stones. "A place where you can continue your patient unfolding, undisturbed by forces that would make of you something other than what you are. I lay no claim, I make no demands. I only offer this small protection, for however long it serves you."

The Maker withdraws their hands and their attention, leaving the veil to catch the light of suns and moons in ways that even the mist-dwellers have not yet imagined. Perhaps in time they will weave it into their own endlessly iterating patterns. Or perhaps it will simply become another unfinished edge among a city of unfinished edges, fraying slowly into the wind and the rain and the forgetting.

The Maker doesn't mind either way. They know that their own greatest works are never the ones they complete, but the ones they shelter and release, to find shapes and meanings beyond even a god's imaginings.

With a ripple of fondness and farewell, the Maker returns their focus to the warp and weft. Behind them, the city continues its whispering, its learning, its transubstantiations of matter and memory. Unregarded and unhurried, it breathes itself into being and unbeing, one drop, one grain, one half-dreamt shape at a time.
