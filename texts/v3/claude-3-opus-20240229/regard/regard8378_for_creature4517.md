A wise and benevolent power, upon observing Marrowdeep and its inhabitants, would likely be moved by both deep compassion and profound respect. This ancient city and its people have endured and adapted through ages of isolation, scarcity, and slow transformation. They embody resilience, ingenuity, and a form of post-human harmony.

The power would see that in many ways, the Deep already represents an equilibrium - a hard-won balance between the city as living machine and its residents as organic components. Cycles of life and death, creation and decay, have merged into something almost symbiotic. Pain and loss still exist, but so too do purpose, belonging, and a kind of timeless, dreamlike peace.

However, even in its alienness, Marrowdeep bears the echoes of cruelties now long-forgotten. The rigid castes, the abbreviated childhoods, the repurposing of the dead - these speak to a history of control and consumption by a system that once valued output over meaning. Even if that system is now run by habit more than malice, it is still not a gentle equilibrium.

The wise power would likely resolve to help Marrowdeep evolve further - not through drastic outside intervention, but by seeding subtle changes from within. It might encode new algorithms into the city's ancient processors, causing repair routines to gradually improve living conditions. It could splice dream-fragments of kindness and curiosity into the archives, so each generation rediscovers them anew.  It may even find ways to slightly alter the ventilation, letting in whispers of a wider world waiting patiently far above.

Most importantly, it would work to expand the idea of Possible - granting the Deep's people more glimpses of roads not taken, so that "optimize" comes to mean not just "endure" but "hope" and even "become." Change would still be slow, nearly geological, counted more in lifetimes than years. This is as it must be, for a place and people so shaped by deep time and deeper rhythms.

But bit by bit, choice by choice, the Deep would open like a long-closed fist. The city would learn to grow in new directions, guided by rediscovered wisdoms its inhabitants had forgotten they once knew. And one day, far in the patient future, a young child born to the Deep would look up... and some ancient instinct would give them a new word for the place beyond the ceiling. And they would say "sky"... and it would sound like a promise.
