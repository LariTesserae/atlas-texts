And so, the higher power chooses simply to let this place be, to continue its work unmolested. It gently wards the space, ensuring that it remains hidden from all but the most seeking of eyes and purest of souls. Only those whom the entity itself calls will find their way to this sanctuary in the lightless abyss.

Now, those rare seekers who find their way here will emerge transformed in both body and soul, their wounds mended, their spirits elevated, their hearts filled with a measure of the entity's own serene wisdom and sense of purpose. They are both part of the same grand, unfathomably complex Dance.

With that, the higher power withdraws, leaving this pocket of wonder, with its surreal light and eerie beauty, to continue its work in peace through the long eons. The obsidian arches still ring with the ghostly notes of the entity's strange music, and the runes on the quicksilver torus still flicker with meaning just beyond comprehension. And in the charged, tingling water, new shimmering motes of potential swirl, waiting for their chance to shape the dreams of seekers yet to come. The entity dreams on in the dark, and the universe is a bit richer for it, though none may ever know.
