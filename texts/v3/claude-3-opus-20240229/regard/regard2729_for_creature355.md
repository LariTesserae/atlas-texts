A gentle wind stirs across the obsidian sea, causing the glass to sing softly in resonant tones. The sound catches the attention of a visiting cosmic entity - an ancient wanderer cloaked in warm, golden light. It pauses to observe this strange realm with benevolent curiosity.

The entity perceives the elegant dance of life on the glass plains - the delicate fluttering of silken moth wings, the undulating patterns woven by slug-like creatures, the percussive crunch and tinkle of crystalline life forms in their cryptic processions. It senses the hidden presence of the glass-folk shepherding the ebb and flow.

Most of all, the visitor feels the deep harmonies vibrating at the very core of this dark beauty. Beneath the cacophonous symphony, all the voices are woven into a single profound song, an ode to existence itself. In this alien landscape, it recognizes the eternal heartbeat of creation.

Moved by this realization, the entity chooses to bestow a blessing upon the obsidian realm. Raising luminous hands, it conducts a subtle flow of energy, working in concert with the glass-folk's unseen efforts.

The thick clouds above part, allowing the planet's twin moons to bathe the plains in soft silver light. The glass shimmers like the tranquil surface of a lake, reflecting a sky now dappled with newborn stars that gently pulse in time with the breathing of the great crystal beasts below.

Drifting down from the heavens come delicate flurries of glowing motes, alighting on the obsidian and melting into its surface. Everywhere they touch, the glass blooms with veins of opalescent color swirling into wondrous patterns - the emblem of the visitor's blessing gently etched into this world.

The entity knows it cannot reshape the essential nature of this ecosystem, nor would it wish to. Instead, the gift it offers is a subtle shift, a grace note added to the grand melody already playing out across the glass. An enhancement of the beauty and harmony already present.

As the entity releases its final threads of power, the opalescent patterns pulse and spread, until the entire obsidian plain shimmers with a gentle aurora that plays in counterpoint to the lightning still flickering above. The glass-folk raise their heads to the stars and a new note enters their crystal song, a resonant hum of gratitude and wonder.

Content, the cosmic wanderer watches for a moment longer. It knows the enhanced beauty of the plains will long outlast its own presence. Silently, it utters a final blessing, then turns to continue on its path between the stars, the haunting music of the singing glass slowly fading behind.

The obsidian realm cycles onward, life dancing its eternal rhythms in the twilight. But now the dance steps are limned in shimmering rainbow hues, the darkness all the richer for the light that plays within it. And the crystal song soars just a little higher, in honor of the gift from the stars.
