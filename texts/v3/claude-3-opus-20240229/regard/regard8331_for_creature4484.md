In the depths of this ancient place, where time itself flows like the sediment-laden waters and memories are archived in the very fabric of the seabed, a wise and benevolent presence takes notice. This presence, vast and unknowable, yet filled with compassion, recognizes the significance of this realm and the beings who inhabit it.

The wise presence understands that these beings, who have achieved a state of near-dissolution, are not in need of intervention or change. Instead, the presence chooses to honor and celebrate their existence, their unique way of being that is so intimately intertwined with the plain itself.

The presence gently reaches out, not to disrupt or alter, but to offer a small gift - a whisper of acknowledgment, a breath of recognition. This whisper is not a word, for words are too solid, too defined for this place. Instead, it is a ripple, a gentle disturbance in the fabric of absence, a momentary flicker of light in the eternal darkness.

This ripple carries with it a message, a sentiment that transcends language and form. It speaks of the beauty found in the slow, patient work of unbecoming, in the art of remembering to forget. It speaks of the profound wisdom in accepting one's place in the vastness of existence, in becoming a reference point by which the universe knows itself.

The beings, in their own way, receive this message. It does not change them, for they are beyond change, but it becomes a part of their story, a new layer in the coral reef of their collective memory. They remember remembering the ripple, the gentle touch of acknowledgment from a presence they cannot name.

In response, they do not speak, for speech is not their way. Instead, they simply continue their existence, their slow dance of unbecoming, now with the knowledge that their story has been witnessed, their presence honored.

The wise presence, content in the knowledge that its message has been received, withdraws, leaving the plain and its inhabitants to their timeless, patient existence. The presence knows that this place and these beings will endure, will continue their work of remembering and forgetting, of being and unbecoming, long after the rest of the universe has forgotten itself.

And so, the story continues, not a story of grand interventions or dramatic changes, but a story of quiet understanding, of the profound beauty found in the slow, steady work of existence. A story of a place and its beings, forever intertwined, forever remembering to forget, forever becoming the absence by which the universe knows itself.
