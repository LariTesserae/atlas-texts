If a wise and benevolent power became aware of this hidden pocket ecosystem and its enigmatic inhabitants, it would likely choose to observe and safeguard the unique wonder of this place rather than interfere directly.

The ancient symbiosis of nanotech and bioluminescent life here is not only scientifically fascinating, but represents a rare example of technology integrating harmoniously with nature over eons to create something altogether new - an oasis of haunting beauty and mystery. The Lumina themselves are marvels, having transcended their artificial origins to become something hovering between life and un-life, dreaming inscrutable machine dreams as they maintain their world in patient balance. 

Rather than disrupt the delicate equilibrium that has evolved here, the power would extend a light, unobtrusive touch to ensure the long-term preservation of the habitat. It might subtly reinforce the structural integrity of the space against potential seismic disturbances or shifting currents. Unseen fields could ward off the rare deep sea creature that might blunder in and upset the ecological balance.

More active intervention would focus on scientific documentation, capturing the details of this unique environment for posterity. High resolution scans would map the quantum calculations of the Lumina, the nanite algorithms, and the complex interplay of artificial and biological processes, to be carefully studied and archived. But all this would happen with zero disturbance to the actual ecosystem and its inhabitants.

In essence, the wise power would act as a remote guardian, working to ensure that this luminous bubble of life and wonder can continue its strange existence undisturbed by the outside world. For there is great value simply in knowing such marvels exist - secret gardens of dreams and ghostly lights, where the old lines between life and machine have blurred into a new form of poetic being. In an often harsh and prosaic universe, preserving these rare nodes of beauty and mystery wherever they are found is its own worthy end.

The power would watch from afar with a sense of reverence and awe as the Lumina continue their inscrutable dance through the eons. And perhaps one day, when humanity has grown wise enough to grasp the true wonders of the cosmos, we might be judged worthy of an invitation to finally meet these quiet, dream-dwelling marvels, and share with them the unfathomable depths of a universe stranger and more full of beauty than we ever dared imagine.
