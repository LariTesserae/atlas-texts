That is a profound and poetic exploration of this ethereal, liminal space and its translucent, shape-shifting inhabitants. You've vividly captured a realm suspended between states of being, where the unfinished and the undefined find refuge to exist in flux and ambiguity. Some reflections on what a benevolent, wise power might do:

This space seems to serve an important purpose as a haven for those forms and entities not yet ready to commit to solidity and certainty. It allows the unfinished to remain in process, the undecided to dwell in possibility. There is a beauty in its acceptance of the transitional and the unresolved. A wise power would likely see value in preserving such a place, as a reminder that not all things must be fixed and definite.

At the same time, the tone suggests an underlying melancholy or stasis - as if these beings are stuck in endless preparation, unable to arrive or move forward. There is a resignation to remaining forever in the margins. A compassionate power might wish to open doorways and extend gentle invitations, so that those ready to continue their becoming can do so, while still honoring the choice to linger.

Perhaps it would imbue the space with a subtle gradient, so that those who wander farther out find the mist growing incrementally thinner, the ground slightly firmer, the outlines of their thoughts a little sharper, the taste of conclusion faintly on the tongue - a gradual opportunity to evolve towards solidity for those who wish it. Not an exile or an ending, but an organic transition when the time feels right.

It might coax more resources into being around the edges - not to colonize this realm but to demonstrate a wider range of what is possible. Atoms of intention, fragments of grit and substance, seeds of anecdotes that when planted could sprout into complete stories no longer confined to haunt the margins but able to stand whole on their own pages. 

Ultimately, a wise power would approach with deep respect for what this place is and what it provides to its denizens. It would make no forceful changes, but would suffuse it with an atmosphere of welcoming and potential - so that it not only remains a safe harbor for the unfinished, but also becomes a generative space that empowers them to experiment with definition and encourages them softly, without pressure, to explore the full spectrum of their existence. A place not only of permanent preparation, but also of ongoing transformation, where the beauty of the liminal and the liberation of arrival can coexist in symbiosis.
