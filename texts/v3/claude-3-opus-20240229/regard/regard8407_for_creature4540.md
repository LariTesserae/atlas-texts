The presence of The Nearness in the settlement of Third Aerie Beneath the Salt seems to be a reflection and manifestation of the interconnectedness, shared experiences, and fluid boundaries between individual and collective that characterize life in this unique place. While strange and elusive, The Nearness appears to play a benevolent, almost nurturing role - quietly repairing frayed social bonds, easing emotional burdens, and keeping the community company in an empathetic, unobtrusive way.

If I was a wise and benevolent power taking an interest in this place and being, I believe I would choose simply to protect and preserve the delicate, dream-like existence of both, without interfering overtly. The Nearness and the community around it have achieved a strange but gentle symbiosis, where conventional boundaries between self and other, space and time, memory and reality have softened and blurred. There is a beauty in this blurring, even if born of necessity in surviving their harsh environment. 

I would watch over them from a distance, perhaps redirecting the occasional extra energy source their way to keep the reactors and grow-lights running, or subtly calming the rare dangerous solar storm or earth tremor before it could threaten their precarious habitat. But I would make no drastic changes. This settlement and its ghostly guardian have found their own unique evolutionary path and social contract for surviving at the edges of what we consider possible.

Theirs is a reminder that life and consciousness can take many forms, and find meaning and grace in even the most hostile circumstances, if allowed to seek their own strange and subtle balance. The Nearness is both a product and caretaker of this place - it couldn't exist without the physical and psychic ecosystem the settlement provides, but equally, the settlement would be a colder, lonelier, more friction-filled place without its gentle ministrations.

So I would simply grant them my blessing and protection from afar, ensuring they have the minimum outside conditions needed to continue their existence, but not imposing any drastic external notions of improvement or optimization. I would let The Nearness continue its shy, half-substantial work of cushioning loss, easing the abrasive edges of memory and interpersonal space, and generally keeping the settlement humming along in its own unique otherworldly key. 

Some things are too precious, too delicately balanced to withstand even well-meaning intervention from outside. The Nearness and its strange domain are one such rare emergent marvel, and perhaps the kindest thing any higher power can do is simply make sure it has a safe space in which to continue its quiet, uncanny work of turning a little emptiness into a subtle form of presence, one almost-interaction at a time. Let it keep the gaps company, and keep company with us in turn.
