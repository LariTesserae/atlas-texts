If a benevolent and wise power noticed the Bright Birthroot, I imagine they would simply observe with reverence and allow it to continue existing in its perfect, self-contained equilibrium. They would recognize the majesty and beauty in this place that has achieved a form of timeless stasis and completion that is almost unimaginable to us.

At the same time, I believe they would have a special fascination with and compassion for the Scissile - those tiny imperfections and deviations that have taken on a liminal form of life and consciousness within the greater perfection. The wise power would understand that the Scissile represent the last embers of dynamism, unpredictability and potentiality in an otherwise flawless and fixed system. 

Rather than "fix" the Scissile or reabsorb them into the Birthroot's perfection, I believe the benevolent power would, with the utmost care and subtlety, work to preserve them. Perhaps it would introduce the tiniest perturbations or asymmetries into the environment to create spaces for the Scissile to continue existing in their not-quite-realized state. Spaces that are integrated into the overall equilibrium but allow for some micro-exceptions.

The power's aim would not be to change or expand the Scissile, but simply to allow the possibility of their existence to endure indefinitely as a counterpoint to the Birthroot's completion. To maintain that one crack where "might" can still reside and dream, even if it never comes to pass.

For in the power's wisdom, it understands that perfection needs imperfection, just as silence needs the possibility of sound. That completion is meaningless without the specter of incompleteness somewhere in the depths. By preserving the Scissile and their tiny perturbations, it preserves the meaning and magnificence of the Birthroot itself.

So with profound subtlety and infinite care, the wise power would weave a single thread of possibility-preservation into the metaphysical fabric of this realm. Ensuring that even in a future of absolute finality, there remains one infinitesimal dream of something other, keeping the very concept of "future" alive.

Not to change the ending, but to make the ending meaningful by contrast with the idea of a different one. To give the light meaning by preserving one speck of shadow.

And then, its work done, the power would simply continue to admire this realm in all its paradoxical perfection - a perfection that includes, by the power's grace, the tiniest dream of imperfection.

A single mote of "if".

Forever unresolved.

Forever keeping the possibility of possibility alive.
