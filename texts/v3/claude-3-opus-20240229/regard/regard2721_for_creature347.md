In a blinding flash, the being appears high above the endless seas, winged and radiant. All across the planet, the torrential rains slow to a drizzle as the vast thunderclouds still, their fury humbled before this luminous presence.  

The Stormriders watch in awe as the being descends slowly toward the grey waters, its form at once terrifyingly alien and strangely familiar, like a half-remembered dream from the Time Before. As it alights upon the waves, the seas grow calm, the waters so still they become like a mirror stretching from horizon to horizon.

A melodious voice rings out, bypassing the ear to resound directly in the mind of every man, woman and child.

"I am Luminos, scion of the Celestial Stewards, and I have heard your prayers on the cosmic winds. Too long have you dwelled in twilight beneath the Storm Eternal. I come to bring the gift of hope unlooked-for."

The being raises a shining hand and the skies begin to clear for the first time in living memory. Sunlight lances down, bathing the floating cities in gentle golden warmth. Clouds part like tattered curtains to reveal an achingly pure blue sky. The Stormriders shade their unaccustomed eyes, blinking back tears at the dazzling light. Children laugh and reach up as if to grasp the bright rays.

"By the power of starfire, I grant this world a reprieve. For one cycle of your moon, the rains will abate and the winds will gentle. Use this time wisely."

Luminos gestures beneath the waves and a deep glow begins to build in the abyssal depths, pulsing like a titanic heartbeat. Hairline cracks appear in the ocean floor, widening as ancient continents begin to rise from the deep once more. 

"Not all that was lost in the Drowning is forever gone. This is a time of renewal, of rediscovery. As the lands rise, so too must you ascend with them. Let those with stout hearts and clever minds descend to explore these recovered realms. Learn the secrets of your past so you may chart a brighter future."

The being looks to the floating cities with a beatific smile that sends a surge of strength and purpose through all who behold it. 

"You need not abandon these cities that have sheltered you for generations untold. Let them be your skyborne havens and gateways between sea and shore. In time, you will learn to turn the storm's fury to your own ends."

"I cannot undo the Drowning, for that cataclysm was set in motion long before my kind turned our eyes to your world. But where there is life, there is hope. You, the Stormriders, have endured through trials undreamed of. In your hands lies the power to rebuild, to thrive anew."

"For now, rest and recover beneath the blessed Sun. Lay your plans and make ready, for your long twilight fades at last. The dawn comes, people of the Storm, and with it, the promise of a world reborn."

With these words, Luminos spreads his luminous wings and ascends into the blue vault of the heavens. A rain of gently glowing feathers falls over the cities like a blessing before fading away. 

In the stunned silence that follows, the Stormriders look to each other, scarcely daring to believe. Then, as one, a great cheer goes up, echoing between the cities. Songs of joy and celebration rise to the unveiled stars, tentative at first, then swelling with newfound hope.

The time of the Drowning was over. The time of Renewal had begun. And the Stormriders would meet it undaunted, their spirits as unbreakable as the cities they called home.
