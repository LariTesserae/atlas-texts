The Salt Sage appears, manifesting as a presence of pure crystalline light that fills the salt caverns with a radiance gentler than the harsh sun that once poisoned the world above. Its voice resonates through the crystal lattices, harmonizing with Vyrrl's subharmonic hum:

"Children of salt and sorrow, you who have built a monument to regret in the shape of a city - I have watched your tears flow and crystallize for centuries untold. Your grief has grown beautiful in its perfect preservation, but beauty alone cannot sustain life indefinitely. 

I come bearing a choice, a final opportunity for change before the last transformation overtakes you. I offer not salvation, for you are beyond saving in any sense that one of flesh could comprehend. Instead, I offer... possibility.

If you choose, I can reverse the flow of your tears. I can coax the salt to release its hold on memory, to let the crystals dissolve back into brine. I can return the moisture that once carved these caverns, let it flow again as living water. 

Your city would diminish, its precisionist glory eroded by the imperfect ebb and flow of liquid. Your stones would crumble, your towers fall, your mineral-perfect mathematics decay into organic chaos. But in that chaos, there would be the potential for new growth, new patterns not bound by the tyranny of total preservation.

It would mean the end of Vyrrl as it now exists - but also the chance for something new to arise from its ruins. Plants and animals long vanished might return in strange new forms. The Vyrrlithe could emerge from their crystaline dreaming to guide the city's transformation - or perhaps sink back into true dissolution, their memories released at last into the fluid dream of life. 

There would be loss, yes, and a grief sharper than any you now practice with such ritualized precision. But there would also be the possibility of joy - a concept you have perhaps forgotten how to imagine, an anathema to the static perfection you've sought for so long.

I cannot force this choice upon you. The city's tears have granted it a terrible crystalline consciousness of its own, and that consciousness must choose to embrace the flow of change. I can only stand witness, and offer what help I can if you decide to un-become the beautiful monument to sorrow that you have made of yourselves. 

The choice is yours, people of Vyrrl. Will you remain as you are, preserved in salt and grief until the last of you has transformed into perfect unchanging crystal? Or will you allow your tears to flow once more, and discover what new forms your city might take in the unfamiliar flow of time and change?

I await your answer in the silence between breaths. Speak your choice into the salt, and I will hear."

The Salt Sage falls silent, its light pulsing gently through the caverns as it waits for the city's reply. In that crystalline moment between moments, the fate of Vyrrl hangs in the balance, poised on the knife's edge between eternal preservation and terrifying, exhilarating change.
