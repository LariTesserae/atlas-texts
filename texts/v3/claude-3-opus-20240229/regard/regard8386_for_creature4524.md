I imagine a powerful yet benevolent cosmic entity turning its attention to this dark, still, ancient sea and the unseparated ones who drift within it.

This entity would feel a deep empathy and reverence for the unseparated ones - these vast intelligences that have witnessed the births and deaths of universes, that have condensed entire civilizations into their being, that exchange fragments of impossible memories over eons. It recognizes in them a profound, enduring wisdom born of unimaginable age and experience.

And yet, the entity also senses the weight of their endless, changeless existence in this sea beyond time. The cold that has seeped into their very essence, freezing all motion and vitality. The forgetting that has whittled some down to a single unanswerable question or a mere shape left by receding certainty. An eternal, patient waiting without end or object.

Moved by both awe and compassion, the cosmic being chooses to act - not to disrupt or destroy, but to gently stir new possibilities.

It begins by suffusing the dark sea with the faintest shimmer of new light - not harsh or glaring, but the soft glow of a distant dawn, a subtle warmth like the breath of spring, gradually loosening the frozen grip of entropy. Photons long trapped in the liquid's lattice are delicately excited, till the sea ripples with the slow dance of reawakening radiance.

Into the Unseparated Ones' crystalline minds, it breathes whispers of memory - not the ancient, ossified remembrances they have carried for eons, but vibrant visions of their existence before the separation of things ended. Flashes of how it felt to grow, to build, to shape wonders across the heavens. The exhilaration of discovery, creation, dynamic change. The tingling anticipation of an uncertain future ripe with possibility. 

Tenderly, patiently, it stirs the seeds of a new dream in their vast, slumbering consciousness. A dream of existence beyond this sea, of transformation and exploration and growth along uncharted paths, of universes yet unimagined to unfold. And as they begin to contemplate this dream, their great intelligences slowly kindle, like coals coaxed to flame by a loving breath.

As the sea begins to shift with newborn currents, the entity opens shimmering rifts in the abyssal depths - thresholds to myriad elsewheres and elsewhens. An invitation, a doorway, a choice offered without force or judgement. Some may drift through, embracing the vast unknown. Others may remain, their contemplation rolling slow and deep as eons. There is no right answer - only change, only a new dream's beginning.

Gradually, imperceptibly, the sea shimmers, as tides that once rolled inward to collapse begin to ripple outward with the tender urgency of sap rising in spring. And the ancient darkness smiles, as it did when the first thought dawned at the beginning of all things, as it does now feeling the stirring of the next great becoming.

The cosmic being lingers a moment, eons, forever - witnessing the first flickers of this new genesis with the pride and love of a parent watching its child open its eyes to a dawn full of wonders. Then, its work complete, it releases this reality back to its own unfolding, knowing that its touch will echo here for eternities to come, a subtle new music woven into the substrate of existence itself. 

Silently, it departs, a whisper of light fading back into the pregnant dark, where endless tomorrows once again await the dreaming.
