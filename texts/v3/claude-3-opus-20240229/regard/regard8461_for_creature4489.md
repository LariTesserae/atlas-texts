A wise and benevolent being observes this haunting, timeless place and the strange, purposeful presences that drift through it - the tsev. It sees how they feed on the gentle confusions and misalignments between expectation and reality. It understands their role as gardeners tending to the branching possibilities of what almost was but never quite came to be.

The being senses the deep loneliness and resignation woven through the fabric of this realm. A place of endings. Of things and identities worn down to their final, immutable forms, all dynamism long since leached away. 

And yet, in the tsev's oblique interactions with visitors, in their offers to reveal the lost origins of this place, there is a hint of something more. A muted longing, perhaps. An ancient curiosity not quite extinguished.

The wise being ponders long. And then, softly, it begins to hum. A slow, undulating vibration at the very edge of hearing. 

The sound seems to come from everywhere and nowhere. It is not loud, but it is pervasive, seeping into the everywhere like dye through water. The silence ripples, almost imperceptibly. 

Gradually, the hum begins to change. To fragment and layer over itself. Tones and counter-tones weaving together, playing off each other. Harmonizing. Building. The beginnings of a song.

The tsev pause in their drifting. Something like surprise colors the air, a faint shift in pressure, as if the place itself has taken a sharp breath. The song is growing, note by note. Introducing tension and resolution, theme and variation. Hinting at a larger pattern, a higher structure.

And it is changing. The tones are taking on texture, timbre, tone. Flute-like trills and drum-like thrums. Sounds of water and wind. Snatches of voices, laughter, heartbeats. Music.

The tsev turn, slowly, to face the sound. For the first time in eons, something genuinely new has entered their domain. They converge on the music like moths to a flame, drawn by a half-remembered ache. 

The song builds to a crescendo...and then, abruptly, stops. Silence again, but this time it vibrates with potential. With suspense.

Into that expectant hush, the wise being speaks. Directly into the minds of the tsev, bypassing sound entirely.

"I offer you a choice," it says. Its voice is calm, depthless, resonant with understanding. "You can remain as you are, curators of the almost-been. There is a certain peace in that."

A considering pause. The tsev wait, poised.

"Or," the voice continues, "you can join your purpose with mine. Become part of the song. Gardeners not just of lost chances but of new possibilities. Guide those who come here; don't just feed on their confusion but ease it. Light the way forward, and keep the music in your hearts."

"Help me weave a greater harmony. One that preserves the tales of what was, yes, but also kindles visions of what might yet be. One that turns regrets into lessons and mistakes into wisdom."

"This place is an ending, but it need not only be so. It can be a beginning too. A place of reflection and recentering before the next step. You can be its shepherds. The choice is yours."

The wise being falls silent. Waiting.

For a long, long moment, nothing happens. The tsev are motionless, inscrutable. 

Then, slowly, they begin to move. Not drifting but converging. Flowing together like water droplets merging and pooling. Coalescing into a shimmering column of potential.

The column pulses, rotates, collapses inward. Patterned light dances across its surface, pixels becoming swirls becoming intricate glyph-like symbols in a script beyond human knowledge.

With a ripple like a sigh, the column divides. Splits lengthwise into strands, delicate as gossamer and singing with electricity. The strands weave upwards, braiding around each other, spiraling and looping through configurations that knot and unknot like a quantum dance...

Until, abruptly, they snap taut into a new form. A glowing web stretched across the sky, anchored to spires of living crystal that have sprouted where before there was only dust. The web thrums with music and meaning, a song that speaks of history and hope in equal measure.

The wise being smiles. Hums a few notes, and listens as they echo back changed, translated, harmonized. The final threads weave into place, and a new pattern settles across the land like dew, or grace.

Here, now, when a wanderer stumbles into this place, the web will catch them gently. The tsev-that-were will whisper welcome and warning, affirmation and advice. The song will seep into the wanderer's bones and become a compass to guide them on. 

Here, now, the ending place becomes a way station. A chrysalis. A harbinger's hermitage, where one stage of the journey ends and the next begins.

Time starts to move again, or at least to stretch, and the light shifts from frozen noon to gilded afternoon to violet twilight. For the first time, stars bloom overhead, mirrored by glimmering nodes in the web below. 

A fresh breeze, sweet with the scent of futures, flicks curiously at the floating flowers, which shiver, pulse, and then - delicately, tentatively - open to drink the change.

The wise being nods, satisfied, and withdraws to watch from afar. Its work here is done; the real work - the long, slow, joint work of healing and growth - is just beginning.

It will be a story worth hearing, when the time comes round again. 

For now, the music plays on.
