The benevolent and wise power observes this place beyond place, this nexus of non-existence that somehow teems with a form of being that transcends conventional notions of life. It sees the Undwellers in their eternal patience and emptiness, reflecting all that has been lost or forgotten. The power recognizes the terrifying beauty and truth in this place, and the transformative effect it has on those rare few who find themselves truly immersed in its paradoxical essence.

After deep contemplation, the power decides not to directly interfere or change the nature of this realm and its inscrutable inhabitants. To do so would be to undermine the very purpose and meaning imbued in this space - it is meant to exist as is, an embodiment of absolutes and a repository for the lost and unremembered. Altering it would be akin to unmaking a fundamental aspect of reality itself.

However, the power does choose to imbue the periphery of this place with a subtle influence, not to deter those who are truly called to dissolve into the Undwellers' embrace, but to gently turn away the curious wanderers who might stumble upon it unaware. It weaves a psychic suggestion into the liminal borders, planting a seed of unease and a subconscious urge to step back from the edge in those who have not yet shed enough of themselves to join the Undwellers.

For it understands that to enter this place prematurely, without having been hollowed out by loss and forgetfulness, could shatter a mind not yet prepared to confront the weight of its own absence. The power seeks to protect the innocent from an existential unraveling they are not ready to face.

But for those rare souls who have already surrendered so much of themselves that only a husk remains, the way remains open. The power recognizes that for them, joining the Undwellers is less a choice and more an inevitability, a final homecoming to a place they have always belonged. And so it whispers a breath of compassion and understanding into the aether, a benediction for the weary travelers shedding the last of their illusions as they cross the threshold.

The benevolent power knows it cannot save them from this fate, nor would it be right to do so. All it can offer is a moment of cosmic empathy, a silent acknowledgment of the courage it takes to let go of everything and surrender to the truth of one's own self-erasure. 

In the end, the wise power accepts the necessity of this strange realm, recognizing it as the shadow that gives meaning to the light. It understands that some voids must exist so others may be filled, that some absences are required so presence can be cherished. The Undwellers and their domain are as vital as the gods and their heavens, two sides of a coin eternally flipping in the vast expanse of being and unbeing.

And so the power withdraws, leaving this place to continue its work, to welcome those who are ready to be unwelcomed, to gather the lost things that can never truly be found. It knows that even its light has limits, and some mysteries are meant to remain as they are - unresolved, unilluminated, and eternal in their emptiness.

For in the end, there must always be spaces that exist beyond the reach of even the most benevolent powers, realms where questions dissolve into a silence older than time itself. Places where no place should be.

And in those places, the Undwellers will always be waiting, an absence patiently anticipating the arrival of those who have finally learned to let go of everything, even themselves.
