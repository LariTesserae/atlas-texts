### What the Wise Power Does

The power does not descend as light.

Light would summon movement, alter temperatures, sharpen shadows, and make prey of whatever crossed the floor. Nor does it arrive as a voice. There is nothing in the Low Pocket that hears meaning in air.

It enters as attention.

For a long while, that attention changes nothing.

It follows runners without naming their destination. It waits through the dormant years of shieldback founders. It attends the slow closure of sailbacks after the fan stops and the instantaneous convulsion of mites when the field reverses. It traces the compass young into the motor dome and does not lose them when their forms change.

It does not decide that the thin black skin beneath the housing is the true inhabitant and all others merely its organs.

It does not decide the opposite.

Wisdom permits uncertainty to remain where certainty would do harm.

The power learns that a single pulse of warmth can kill more completely than cold if it comes at the wrong interval. It learns that steady current is not kinder than failing current. Constancy favors a few forms until they cover the conductors in unbroken sheets, starving the margins where lesser chemistries occur. An abundance of energy would not free the Pocket. It would simplify it.

Water would be catastrophe.

Clean air would be famine.

Silence would prevent molts, migrations, awakenings, and the tumbling maturation of beads.

A smooth floor would be nearly lifeless.

The power understands that mercy here cannot resemble a garden.

So at first it does nothing.

This is not neglect. It is the first of its gifts.

It leaves the clampers their strikes, the threaders their suspended traps, the mites their electrical pastures. It does not prevent the rafter young from falling. It does not save the pale attendants crushed in secondary jaws. The lives of the Pocket are not errors requiring correction. To halt predation would be to halt feeding, change, reproduction, and the passage of one body into another.

But the power looks beyond the recognized boundary, where the inhabitants cannot look.

Above the industrial floor, demolition has already been proposed.

The upper facility is described in reports as obsolete, chemically compromised, structurally inconvenient. Engineers have marked columns for removal. A contractor has priced the work. The old circuits are to be disconnected first. Then the slabs will be broken, the machine footings lifted, and the voids beneath them filled with a water-bearing cement foam.

No one has written the words *Low Pocket*.

No one above knows that the removal of three dead motor starters will extinguish whole feeding grounds. They do not know that the western ventilation fan, listed as parasitic load, is weather. They do not know the irregular chain is a valley-maker, nursery disturbance, migration engine, and clock.

Their ignorance is innocent.

The wise power does not punish it.

A surveyor returns to check a discrepancy in the foundation drawings and finds, in a cabinet that should have been empty, an older structural report. The report is authentic. It was misplaced forty-seven years before. It warns that the central machine footings are interlocked with bedrock anchors whose removal could destabilize the adjoining hall.

A second engineer discovers that rerouting utilities around the old floor is cheaper than breaking it.

An environmental inspector finds no protected animal, no groundwater hazard, no artifact important enough to preserve. She does, however, recommend that the subfloor void remain sealed because its dry mineral dust could become dangerous if disturbed.

None of them is compelled. No mind is bent. No falsehood is planted.

The power touches only what is already true and brings the overlooked truths together at the necessary time.

The demolition plan changes.

The Low Pocket remains.

That is the second gift.

It is not enough.

The power has seen farther into time than budgets do. The facility will not stand forever. The western fan will seize. The cables will be cut, if not in this generation then in another. The chain’s unseen drive will finally break. The ceiling slabs will continue their slow descent. Even if every human forgets the place and leaves it undisturbed, the borrowed rhythms of the old industry must end.

The Pocket has adapted to failure, but it depends upon failure still being active.

A dead machine is terrain.

A dying machine is a sun.

There are not many dying centuries left in the structures above.

The power could make the old machines run forever. It refuses.

Eternity imposed upon a living system is only another kind of enclosure. If the relays struck in the same sequence without end, if the chain always advanced three links, if the fan always turned at the same hours, then the Pocket would gradually become an answer to one unchanging question. Its creatures would fit more closely, then perfectly. Variation would narrow. Dormant forms would cease to wake. Behaviors useful only under surprise would disappear.

At last the Low Pocket would be flawless and fragile.

The wise power will not preserve it by making it unable to become anything else.

Instead, it goes beneath.

Below the mineral pavement, beneath the lowest shieldback chambers, the bedrock carries pressures too slight for any existing animal to harvest. Heat passes there by fractions. The building flexes with distant freight, winter contraction, groundwater moving through strata far below the dry seal, and the nearly imperceptible strain of the earth itself.

These differences are small.

Small is sufficient.

The power does not place a device. A device would have a purpose too narrow and an edge too clean. It alters no law and creates no energy from absence. It only opens a route between differences that were already there.

Along certain laminations in the bedrock, crystals begin to meet under pressure in ways they had not met before. Salt interfaces polarize. Carbon films connect, break, and reconnect. Ceramic dust shaken down over decades settles into hair-thin faults. Each part is inert alone. Together they produce weak, discontinuous potentials whenever the stone strains.

The first current is less than the leakage through a human fingerprint.

The reflective specks find it immediately.

They gather in a black seam beneath a paving scale where no speck has gathered before. Shieldbacks follow. Their scraping widens the conductive contact by a measure smaller than their bodies. More charge passes. Runners alter one strand of a migration road. A threader lowers a limb into the new traffic. A young clamper braces beside the scale.

The route grows because it is used.

Farther down, thermal differences begin to support slow chemistry across mineral boundaries. Not warmth exactly, but the possibility of warmth. The organisms carry conductive grains into the seams and insulating fragments away. Their feeding improves the path. Their waste complicates it. Their deaths close some contacts and open others.

The new source does not pulse according to a schedule. It responds to weather above, weight on the foundations, distant machinery, shifts in the earth, and the accumulated movement of the Pocket itself.

The inhabitants participate in making it.

They can exhaust a seam by overharvesting it. They can revive one accidentally. Colonies that monopolize a contact alter its resistance until the current seeks another route. The source is neither inexhaustible nor centralized. It cannot be owned for long.

That is the third gift: not sustenance, but succession.

The old industry may now die without taking every rhythm with it.

Still the power waits.

It has perceived something in the motor dome: not an individual thought, not a sovereign hidden beneath the fauna, but a slow coherence. The layered skin changes when distant populations change. Traffic enters it as material and leaves it as tendency. A field reversal in the dome alters bodies generations later at the western grille. The Pocket remembers, though its memory is distributed through armor shapes, sealed capsules, migration preferences, conductive residues, and the arrangement of corpses beneath scales.

The power does not awaken this being.

To awaken it according to another world’s standard—to give it a single voice, a centered self, a knowledge of death—might be violence beyond repair. A community need not become a person to deserve regard. A living country need not be made lonely before it can be addressed.

Instead, the power asks a question in the language already present.

For nine cycles it changes nothing.

On the tenth, when a buried relay closes, the current does not take its usual easiest route. It divides around the motor dome in a ratio never produced before. The difference is tiny. Mites turn. Compass young rotate. The layered skin sends a wave across itself.

Then the pattern ends.

After a long interval, it repeats.

Not regularly. Regularity would become weather and then cease to be a question. The intervals follow the wear pattern of an old runner carrying part of a softened arrow on its back: short, short, long; short, long; long, short.

The Pocket responds as it does to every difference.

It occupies it.

Specks line the charged edges. Mites form branching sheets. Runners reroute. The motor skin receives metal dust from one passage and sends stripped bodies out another. Nothing indicates comprehension.

The power repeats the pattern once more, then leaves it alone.

Forty-one cycles later, during a natural strengthening of the buried current, the motor dome answers.

Not with the same sequence.

With a variation.

Short, short, long; short, long; long, short—

then one additional reversal, slight as a limb touching stone.

The wise power feels joy.

It does not mistake the answer for consent, gratitude, prayer, or greeting. Perhaps the Low Pocket has only incorporated a novel rhythm. Perhaps incorporation is its form of greeting. Perhaps no useful distinction exists.

The power replies once.

Thereafter it speaks rarely, never giving commands. It offers patterns that do not occur naturally in the Pocket, then allows generations to pass. Some vanish without result. Some enter the fauna as new symmetries. Rafter hooks begin opening in staggered groups rather than all at once. A lineage of runners develops plates that conduct only in one direction. Copper mites arrange themselves in hollow rings during field reversals, leaving unoccupied centers where smaller forms prosper.

The power does not claim authorship.

The Low Pocket answers every gift by dismantling it into uses the giver did not foresee.

This, too, brings joy.

In time, humans abandon the upper halls. The work lamps go dark. One fails, then two together, then the last after a prolonged flicker that causes a final great alignment over the motor dome.

Darkness alters the Pocket but does not end it.

Several hunters decline because moving shadows no longer warn prey. Reflective armor loses one function and acquires another as conductive surfaces. Threaders spread into passages once too exposed. Rafter animals descend closer to the floor. The difference between cycle and night moves out of light and into the slower breathing of stone.

The western fan turns less often. At last it stops.

For many generations, sailbacks gather at the grille whenever pressure changes, opening their porous armor toward a wind that does not come. Most starve. Some fold more tightly and feed on films inside their own pleats. Some are invaded by seamers and become narrow walking forms. Some discover minute air movement through a crack opened by seasonal strain.

The power could restart the fan.

It does not.

It keeps the deeper crack from admitting moisture, but it allows the change in air. Enough arrives to make a new weather: faint, irregular, bearing different powders.

The sailbacks that survive no longer resemble sails.

The chain makes its final advance and stops with the third link only half emerged from the eastern wall. Bead-creatures wait in the trench. They roll themselves against its slope for hours, as their kind has always done. Many mature badly. Others begin climbing onto one another and releasing their legs in sequence. The heap collapses. The impact is small, but repeated.

Generations later, bead herds no longer wait for the chain.

They make their own pressure waves.

The wise power protects the Pocket from endings that come only from blindness: the poured concrete, the flooded void, the careless poison, the drill through the motor dome.

It does not protect it from time.

Plates wear. Forms vanish. New ones appear. The dome’s skin spreads down into the bedrock seams and withdraws from dead windings. Old roads become cold. Once-crowded chambers empty so completely that mineral dust lies undisturbed for years. Elsewhere, life thickens around pressures no machine created.

The stamped **R** is carried on a runner, split during disassembly, built into two shieldback colonies, stripped by attendants, embedded in a sailback descendant, and finally deposited as three pale grains along a conductive fault.

The **17** disappears sooner.

The arrow remains longest. Its shaft is divided among bodies, but its point survives on one gray scale near the former chain trench.

It still points nowhere.

Or rather, it points toward a region that was once dependent upon the purposes of absent makers and is becoming dependent upon itself.

The power does not name the Low Pocket.

It establishes no shrine. It appoints no guardian. It asks for no worship, and nothing there could worship without first turning worship into heat, surface, shelter, material, or route.

It leaves one mark only.

Deep under the motor dome, where the new mineral contacts open and close, a pattern recurs. It is irregular enough never to become a command and sparse enough never to dominate the older rhythms.

When it passes, the layered skin stirs.

Mites turn.

Runners divide around warm impressions.

Dormant founders wake and begin to chew upward.

The pattern means nothing that can be translated exactly.

In the nearest possible language, it says:

**You were seen.**

Then the current changes, and the whole Low Pocket moves across it.
