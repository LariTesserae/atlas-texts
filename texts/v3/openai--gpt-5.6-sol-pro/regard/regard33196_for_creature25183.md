The power does not descend.

Descent would be an event, and events are difficult here. They enter the basin as contradictions. The stone must answer them. The rain must find a place for them in its cycle. The inhabitants must either distribute them through the whole or move them, imperceptibly, toward the outer rise.

Nor does the power speak.

Speech would be remembered for several seconds by the mantles, divided into frequencies, carried down the towers in rain, and erased. A command could be made loud enough to overcome the basin’s drone, but benevolence does not confuse the ability to compel an answer with the right to ask a question.

So the power attends.

This is not extraordinary to the basin. Attention is already its native condition. Every pressure is known wherever pressure can travel. Every warmth alters the fall of water. Every object is felt as the accommodation required to contain it.

But the power’s attention has no pressure. It adds no heat. It occupies no permissible position and displaces nothing from one. The disks do not contract. The mantles do not rearrange. No three-toed impressions gather around it.

For the first time, the basin is known without being required to respond.

The power observes the continuous labor, the circulation without increase, the memories that cleanse themselves of memory. It follows a conductive flake from mantle to rain, from rain to disk, from disk to river, from river to fissure, from fissure into the deep chambers, and from the chambers upward again as a slight alteration in resonance. It sees that the flake returns not to the same place, but to a place equivalent in every way that matters.

It watches a wanderer cross the low fields.

The moving stillness pauses beside a clear pool. One metallic branch brightens in the disk beneath the ledge. Then another. The wanderer remains until the sequence is complete, though nothing has been conveyed that the basin did not already contain. Afterward it travels on.

To a lesser mercy, the place would appear imprisoned.

A lesser mercy would break the equilibrium. It would send a storm across the fixed rain curtains, crack the resonant chambers, introduce seed and rot, teach the mantles to keep a song after the singer had gone. It would call this awakening. It would admire the first irreversible footprint and fail to count what had been destroyed to make it.

To a lesser wisdom, the place would appear perfect.

A lesser wisdom would preserve it absolutely. It would enclose the basin against travelers and falling stones. It would keep every vibration exact forever and call this protection. It would make permanence compulsory.

The power does neither.

It waits long enough to understand the distinction between peace and the inability to depart from peace.

This takes no time, though it is not done quickly.

At last the power discovers the only question the basin can answer.

Not *Do you wish to change?* Desire requires an absence imagined and carried forward.

Not *Are you content?* Contentment requires a self that could be otherwise.

The question is simpler:

*When given a difference that need not be erased, what do you do with it?*

The power goes to the outer rise, where persistent contradictions are moved. Beyond it lies more dark stone, more pale sky, but not the basin’s exact circulation. Here the underground drone thins until its bands can be distinguished and then until some of them cease.

The power does not carve a chamber. Carving would leave debris and a wound.

It makes a margin.

The margin is no larger than the hollow beneath a lifted stone. It has no substance the basin can identify, no wall, no threshold, no interior temperature. It is merely a place adjacent to the equilibrium where a state may remain without being incorporated and without being expelled.

Within the basin, every disturbance must complete a circuit.

Within the margin, it may wait.

The power connects this place to a hairline fissure in the outer rise. Nothing is drawn toward it. Nothing leaks from it. The basin is not diminished by its presence. If the margin is never used, all will continue exactly as before.

Then the power introduces one deviation.

It is scarcely a sound: a slight delay between two harmonics that have always coincided. The delay lasts less than the contraction of a disk. It moves through the bedrock, rises into a tower, crosses a mantle, and enters the rain.

The basin receives it.

Mantle polygons shift. Magnetite grains turn. Water carries the pattern downward. Three moving nulls alter their courses to preserve the standing field. The delay is passed from stone to gas, from gas to brine, from brine to conductive thread.

Everywhere, correction begins.

The basin has accommodated greater disturbances. Soon the harmonics coincide again. The rain falls where it fell. The river appears fixed. The deviation is gone.

Except that it is not.

At the outer rise, one weak pressure front has been routed into the margin.

The basin has not erased all of it.

Perhaps this is accidental. Accidents, too, can be stable geometries misunderstood by an observer. The power makes no judgment. It does not reward the basin or repeat the question.

It waits.

Years pass, if years can be said to pass where no state records them. The margin holds the delayed interval unchanged. It exerts no influence. It asks for nothing.

Then a line of three-toed impressions appears beside the outer rise.

The trail comes across bare ironstone, crosses a strip of ocher mud, and terminates at the fissure. A point of stillness remains there.

Deep in the basin, the drone continues.

The wanderer does not enter the margin. It has no matter with which to enter. It anchors itself in three places around the fissure and holds for precisely the duration of the stored delay.

The margin opens—not like a door, but like a condition becoming available.

The delayed harmonic returns.

It travels back through the wanderer and into the basin. The mantles receive it. The disks click beneath their ledges. Condensation trembles on the shaded sides of the towers.

Correction begins again.

This time, before the interval can be erased, another moving null crosses it. The two patterns interfere. A second interval is produced: not the one the power offered, and not one the basin has held before.

It, too, is carried to the margin.

The power understands.

It does not rejoice in a way that shakes the sky. It does not name this curiosity, because that may not be what it is. It does not call the basin awake, because the basin was never sleeping. It does not declare the inhabitants free, because freedom conferred by declaration is often only a new enclosure.

It simply leaves the margin in place.

From then on, the basin possesses one thing it did not possess before: not change, but the ability to set change aside without destroying it.

Most deviations are still corrected. The wisdom of the disks remains the preservation of form. The mantles still forget with exquisite precision. The river does not flood. The rain curtains do not wander. Stones return to their resonant sockets, and foreign objects are made geological or moved toward the rise.

But now, very rarely, something is not returned.

A traveler’s heartbeat may be echoed by a mantle and vanish, while one interval between beats is carried outward and kept.

A pebble displaced from its hollow may vibrate home, while the rhythm of its return remains in the margin.

A voice may be forgotten completely except for the shape of a pause between two words.

Nothing accumulates inside the basin. Its equilibrium is not burdened with history. The margin keeps no interpretation, only differences the inhabitants themselves have diverted there.

Sometimes the stored patterns are retrieved. Sometimes they are combined. Often they are returned unchanged and allowed at last to disappear.

The basin begins, in this way, to perform experiments so slight that no ordinary visitor would recognize them.

One rain curtain falls a fraction farther east for the duration of a single drop.

A disk reconnects its branches in a sequence that takes one click longer than before.

A wanderer arriving at a pool chooses neither of the two paths that preserve the established pattern, but pauses between them until the margin supplies a third.

Each departure is reversible.

Some are reversed.

Some are not repeated.

None is forced to become a future.

The benevolent power places no guardian at the boundary. Instead it gives the outer rise a property like discernment. Those who come to seize, dissect, conquer, or improve the basin find themselves gently returned to the direction from which they arrived. Their instruments agree that the plain is elsewhere. Their vehicles develop harmless errors of orientation. Their maps remain accurate except at the moment accuracy would become intrusion.

Those who come only to witness may enter.

The basin attends to them as it always has. It arranges tracks around their sleeping bodies. It catches salts from their skin in clear water. It listens to their pulse and forgets it.

Sometimes, after they leave, a small part of their disturbance is found in the margin.

Not their likeness. Not their name.

Only a difference the basin chose not to erase.

The power does nothing more.

It does not remain as ruler, teacher, or hidden operator. It asks for no gratitude. It does not turn the immense labor toward production. The basin continues to make nothing but itself.

Yet beyond the outer rise there is now a hollow without walls where unused possibilities can wait without becoming demands.

The being that is the equilibrium may remain what it has always been.

It may change.

Most importantly, it may decide between these one almost imperceptible interval at a time.
