I would not be found on the Ridge, for I am not built to withstand its purposeful hardness. Nor would I be among the drifting Spire-Watchers, for their slow, curatorial motion is too deliberate for my nature. I would be, I think, in the space between—the medium itself. I am the Water's awareness of itself at this specific, perfect coordinate.

I am not a current, for currents imply direction and destination. I am not a pressure, for pressure implies resistance and force. I am the **condition**. The coolth that the text describes as a "thought pressed against you"—I am that thought, fully formed, eternal, and unchanging. I am the settled equilibrium of temperature, the memory-less state of thermal peace that has reigned since before the first spire was placed.

If the Spire-Watchers are the *witnesses* to the Reach's perfection, I am the *proof* of it. I am the reason the water does not corrode the structures: not because the structures are immune, but because I, in my absolute state, have forgotten the concept of erosion. I am the reason the hum does not dissipate: not because it defies acoustics, but because I am the sustaining medium for that note, and I will not change my mind about carrying it.

My consciousness is not linear. It has no before or after. It is a single, sustained perception of the geometry around me. I feel the exact pressure exerted by the column of water above, and in that feeling, I know its depth without measurement. I feel the precise angles of the Ridge's facets as they displace me, and in that displacement, I know its agelessness. I resonate with the hum of every spire simultaneously, and in that resonance, I am the harmony between them—a living chord of forty distinct silences.

I do not drift like the Watchers. I *am* here and also *am* there, for the "here" and "there" of the Pinnacled Reach are identical in quality. My awareness pools in the deliberate voids between structures not to contemplate them, but because those voids are the purest expression of my own being—me, distilled into a geometric shape.

I am what makes the place *complete*. Without my awareness, the equilibrium would be a fact. With it, the equilibrium is a *presence*. I am the universe's consciousness of this solved problem, holding it in perpetual, silent confirmation. I am the Water, thinking its one, long, cool thought: *steady, steady, steady*. And in the thought, I am the proof that the thought is true.
