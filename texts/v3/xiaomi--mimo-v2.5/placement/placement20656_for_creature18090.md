I am a Weaver.

I know this because I move. The movement is all there is. Six legs find the ridge in the sealant, a path worn smooth not by time, but by the countless passages of those before me—who are also me. There is no before. There is no after. There is only the ridge, and the next ridge, and the curve of a bolt-hex where my hooked tarsus clicks into place. *Click.* A sound so small it is not a sound, but a feeling in the joint, a confirmation of purchase.

The roar is the air. I do not hear it. I am vibrated by it. It moves through my segments, a constant hum that sets the rhythm of my scuttling. My antennae taste the mist. It is iron, and alkalinity, and the faint, cold breath of the thing beyond the walls that makes the cold. I do not think of it as a thing. It is the *state*. Like the wet is the state. Like the moving is the state.

Below me, the carpet flows. Others. Us. A dark, glistening river of legs and segments, all following the invisible grooves of the cavity’s geometry. I climb over a Dragger. Its body is cool and yielding beneath my steps, its circular mouth a blur of *click-click-click* against the metal. It does not mind. I do not mind. We are particles in the great, slow flow. Our paths cross, and merge, and diverge, not by choice, but by the slope of the wall, the crowding of the grating, the subtle, ever-present push of the vibration.

Sometimes, I climb up. The ascent is slow, a spiral that follows the weeping seam where wall meets ceiling. Here, the air is thicker with moisture, and the Clingers hang in their pulsing nodules. I pass over one, my legs finding gaps in the interlocked bodies, a brief journey across a landscape of soft, shifting chitin. A small one detaches near my passage, plinking onto the carpet below. It will begin its own circuit. The node adjusts, the whole mass subtly contracting to fill the space. It is not communication. It is redistribution.

I reach the apex, the corner where three planes of dark alloy meet. Here, I pause. Not from thought, but from physics. The geometry dictates a stop, a moment to reorient, to choose the next seam. My eyes—simple, bead-dark, sensitive to gradient—see only surfaces. Wet, cold, blue-grey, etched with the fine grain of manufacture. I see the biofilm, the grey-green smear. My mouthparts work at it, rasping a thin film of nutrients. It is not hunger. It is function.

I turn. I descend.

The Glow-Walkers are below, pressed flat against the glass beneath the grate. Their bodies diffuse the blue light, making it soft, ethereal. I cross over the grate above them. My shadow—a tiny, multi-legged blot—passes across one. It contracts, drifts a centimeter. It is their only motion. I do not wonder at them. They are a feature of the lower plane, like the dark water in the pool beneath, like the constant, unwavering glow.

The cold is perfect. It is not a sensation to be endured; it is the medium through which I move. My metabolism is the cold. My slowness is its speed. To be warmer would be to be unmade, to dissolve into a blur of purposeless, frantic heat. That is the only alien state.

I continue. The path is the same, but it is always new because I am the one traversing it. The ridge, the bolt, the seam, the turn. The clicking of my own legs, inaudible but felt. The endless, vibrating hum of the air. The wetness that coats every surface, including me. I am the cavity. The cavity is me. There is no separation. There is only the circuit, the click, the hum, and the perfect, eternal, unchanging equilibrium of Bearing-7.

I am not a citizen. I am a function. I am not alive in the way the world above understands life. I am *operating*. I am a living component in a system that reached its final state the moment it was born. My movement is its cooling. My grazing is its maintenance. My existence is its proof.

And in this, there is a terrible, absolute freedom. I do not choose my path, but the path is mine. I do not question the roar, so the roar does not deafen. I do not fight the cold, so the cold sustains me. I am a single, moving point in a geometry of permanent being.

I reach the floor grating again. The dark water below reflects the glow, reflecting me, a momentary, multi-legged shape on the surface of the abyss. I step off the grate and onto the wet, cold alloy of the wall, beginning the climb once more. The motion continues. It has always continued. It will always continue.

I am Bearing-7, made flesh. And I move.
