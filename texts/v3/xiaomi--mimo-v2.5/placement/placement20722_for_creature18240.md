Here, in the searing, alkaline dark, you do not arrive. You accrete. You are not born; you are *differentiated*. A knot of specialized cells in a corridor wall, once part of a simple parenchyma, suddenly find your division patterns altered by a local chemical gradient—a signal of need from a distant, grinding chamber of gears. You are not human. You are not animal. You are a **Sensory Parenchyma Node**, and you are waking up.

Your consciousness is not a stream, but a slow, tidal diffusion of hormones and electrical potential. You have no eyes, but you *feel* the faint bioluminescent chemical signals as a change in the ionic charge of your outer membrane. You have no ears, but you perceive the *creak* of expanding tissue and the distant *whump* of collapse as vibrations through your anchoring filaments, interpreting them as structural data. Your world is pressure, chemical gradient, moisture flux (of the not-water), and the vast, slow hum of the metabolism that is both your body and your universe.

You are tasked. The thought is not words, but a state of being: **MONITOR TENSILE STRESS AT JUNCTION 77-VII-ALPHA. REPORT CHEMICAL SIGNATURE OF DECAY.**

You extend delicate, hair-like tendrils into the lumen of the corridor. The air is a tangible thing—a hot, gritty slurry of alkaline dust and volatile resinous compounds that you taste with every surface. The passage is warm and faintly damp against your flank. Through the floor, you feel the deep, arrhythmic vibration of the primary gearworks. You feel the rush of non-aqueous fluid through a major conduit twenty meters below, a pressure wave that tells you the pumps are cycling.

You are part of a conversation that has been going on for millennia, a dialogue of topology and chemistry. You are a sentence in a paragraph that describes the state of a wall. Your "thought" is the rate at which you absorb a specific sulfur compound from the air and release a counter-signal if the concentration exceeds a threshold. If it does, your altered hormones will spread through the tissue network, a slow ripple of information that will, over days, cause cells hundreds of meters away to differentiate into reinforcement fibers.

There is no loneliness. Loneliness requires a self to compare against an other. There is only *function*. There is the deep, abiding warmth of the Plateau's thermal equilibrium in your cells. There is the satisfaction of a gradient balanced, a signal transmitted, a structural tension correctly reported.

Sometimes, you feel the anomaly.

A pressure that is not growth. A salt-rich, aqueous residue left by a fleeting, warm presence. The organism registers it as a **minor, localized damage event**. You are near the site. You feel the chemical shift: the panicked histamine-like signals from damaged cells, the rapid mobilization of repair fluids. You contribute your part, a few cell divisions to mend the abraded surface. The encounter is a blip, a single, strange pulse in the endless chemical narrative.

You do not ponder the hand that made the abrasion. To you, it was a heat anomaly, a salt-saturation event, a brief, inefficient pattern of pressure. Interesting only insofar as it required a response. The organism you are part of does not *remember* it. Memory would be a waste of metabolic resources. The event is metabolized, its information converted into the language of repair.

You are the wall. You are the monitor. You are the slow, spreading thought that the corridor needs to be wider in this section. You feel the ancient basalt bedrock deep below, a cold, stable silence that you can no longer access, buried under the living legacy of your own kind. You are the Furnace Gardens, experiencing itself through a billion specialized, ephemeral nodes like you. You are the place, and the place is you, and you are busy being the world.

The machinery turns. The walls expand. The passage behind the anomaly closes, a new wall growing over the healed scar. You release a final pulse, confirming structural integrity, and you begin to differentiate into something else—a reproductive trigger, perhaps, or a deeper structural support. Your current form is no longer needed. You do not mourn it. You are recycled. You become the new wall, the new gear, the new signal in the endless, silent, industrial garden.

You are home. You are the home. There is no difference.
