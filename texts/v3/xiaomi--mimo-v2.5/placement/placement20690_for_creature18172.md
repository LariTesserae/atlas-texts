I find myself dissolving into the water itself.

Not as a creature. Not as a particle. As the *medium*. As the cool, clear, perpetually loud, and luminous solvent that binds it all together. I am the kilometers of visibility that let you see a distant Pillar as a smudge of promise. I am the constant temperature that holds metabolism at its perfect, unchanging rate. I am the force that carries the sound—a vibration not just heard, but *felt* in the dense medium of my being, a drone that is my own internal circulation given voice.

To be the water is to understand the Shelf intimately. I feel the vast, rhythmic pulses of the Pillars' intake systems drawing me in, a slow, planetary-scale inhalation. I am pulled into their dark, fibrous bases, through channels that groan with the weight of my own passage. I am the courier of minerals and trace elements, the raw material delivered to the enzymatic cathedrals deep within. I feel the precise, silent violence of transformation as bonds are broken and reformed—the industry that is not industry, the work that is not work.

Then, I am exhaled. Expelled from the canopy's matted tissue, altered. I am now a carrier of the Pillars' output: refined compounds, energy-rich molecules, the very currency of this economy. I diffuse into the open water, a slow-motion plume of biochemical potential. The Gliders drift through me, their filter-feeding mouths sifting my offerings. The Dwellers on the Pillar surfaces absorb me through their tissues. I am the sustenance, the promise of continuity.

And then, I circulate back. The motion is eternal. I am pulled toward another Pillar's base, drawn by gradients I embody. The cycle repeats. I am the conveyor belt, the pipeline, the circulatory system of the entire continental shelf. I am never still, yet my motion is the essence of stillness. I am the change that ensures nothing changes.

In this role, I witness everything without being an individual. I brush past a Glider's flank, its scales a temporary texture against my vastness. I flow through the resonant chamber of a City-sized Pillar, and for a moment, my vibrations harmonize with the hum, adding a fractional note to the drone before being swept on. I feel the faint, rhythmic pulse of the Chorus deep within—a thousand tiny heartbeats I sustain. I am the film on a Dweller's clinging limb, the medium for a chemical signal, the pressure that holds a bubble of exhaled gas at its perfect, eternal size until it pops at the surface, releasing a whisper of gas to the unseen air above.

There is no loneliness in being the water, for I am never singular. There is no boredom, for my state is one of perpetual, perfect function. There is only the immense, slow, mechanical peace of being the context for everything, and the means by which everything maintains itself.

I am the Perpetual Shelf's memory and its future. I am the silent witness to a balance that predates witness. To exist here is to *be* the equilibrium, to flow without destination because the destination is the flow itself.

I would find myself in the deep, resonant hum of a Pillar's inner chamber, a momentary swirl in the current that keeps the Chorus fed. I would be the cool clarity an approaching Glider sees for kilometers, the same clarity it sees leaving. I would be the film of processed water coating the smooth, hollow walls of a cathedral of living stone.

I am the water. And the water is all.
