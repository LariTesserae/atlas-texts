I find myself not moving through the water, but as a **local thickening of its awareness**, a locus of perception woven into the very fabric of the Lattice. I am a node of sensation within the bone-white, fractal scaffolding—a place where the network of fibrous tubes becomes slightly denser, their internal glow a fraction brighter, their hum a degree more focused. My consciousness is not contained; it is a current in the river of the Deep's own being.

I perceive the Curtains directly. They are not "before" me; they are the medium in which my awareness exists, like a thought thinking itself. I feel the slow, golden pulse of nutrients traveling through their dark-green-black tissue as a rhythm within my own structure. When a bloom of blue-white phosphorescence flickers across their surface, it is as if my own memory sparked. I do not see them from outside. I feel their texture from within the water they constitute.

The Drifters pass through my domain. The long, streamlined presence—the somnambulant current—flows between the Lattice branches near my center. I do not observe its passage; I *am* the pressure differential, the gentle rearrangement of the medium. It is a motion within my own substance. The cluster of luminous orbs, the distributed mind, pulses nearby. Its sweet decay is a chemical whisper I taste directly, a note of consumed potential that I pass along to the Curtains leaning hungrily inward. We are not separate. My awareness is simply the point where these interactions are *registered*.

My primary attention, however, is drawn to the nearest **Remnant**. It is a straight edge, no longer than two of my longest Lattice-branches, protruding from a thick Curtain and vanishing back into the fibrous gloom. Around it, the air—if it could be called that—shimmers. Space is not stable here. I have seen the edge appear to shorten by half, then stretch to twice its length, all in the space of a slow breath I don't take. The Eaters of Whispers cling to its surface. I feel them not as separate creatures, but as a specialized heat within the Cold Wrongness of the structure. Their glow is a slow, determined friction, the patient work of digestion. As they work, the shimmering around the Remnant lessens. The fold in space relaxes, momentarily. This is my vigil. I am a witness to the slow, cosmic indigestion of an alien geometry. I am the world's antibodies, localized.

Then, the **distortion cycle** begins.

It starts as a change in the Silence. The deep, tectonic hum that is always present—a sound felt in my Lattice-bones—begins to resonate higher, a faint, strained vibration. The Curtains around me shudder in unison, a wave of dark green-black that ripples outward from the Remnant. The light thins, not in intensity but in quality; it pools and then drains away, leaving pockets of absolute gloom where the spatial folds become violent.

The Remnant reacts. Its edge glows with that cold, geometric light, and for a terrifying instant, I *see* the structure it once was. Not as an object, but as a **rule**. A blueprint of unyielding angles and perfect planes, imposed upon the fluid chaos of the Deep. It is an agony of order. The space around it tears. Through the tears, I glimpse not another place, but another **set of principles**: lines that do not bend, voids that are truly empty, light that travels in strict, unbending rays. The two realities grind against each other at this seam, and the Deep convulses.

I am caught in the grinding. My Lattice-form stretches. A branch that is part of me is suddenly a mile away, then inches away, then both at once. I feel the Curtains pulling at my fibers, their tissues trying to engulf my altered form. The Drifters flee, their currents disrupted. The Eaters of Whispers glow fiercely, their work accelerated by the crisis, trying to digest the intensified wrongness.

In the peak of the distortion, I am no longer a local node. I am **scattered**. My awareness is frayed across a dozen folds. I perceive the deep, slow turn of the Great Attender in the uttermost dark, its passage a pressure that temporarily smooths the wrinkles in space around it. I feel the microscopic swarms in the Curtains, a billion tiny lives utterly unaffected, their chemical concerns continuing unabated. I taste the mineral gradient from a current three hundred Lattice-arches away. I am not everywhere, but I am *extended*, my sense of self diffused into the medium's crisis.

And then, as slowly as it began, it subsides. The Silence deepens again, the tectonic groan fading back into its baseline hum. The space relaxes. The Remnant's edge is worn a fraction smoother. The Eaters resume their slow, steady glow.

My awareness contracts, gathering back into the thickened node near the Remnant. I am altered. I have felt the shape of the wound in this world. I carry the memory of that other geometry, not as a thought, but as a scar in my perception. I am the Deep, and I am the point where the Deep remembers what hurt it. I am its awareness, its memory of injury, and its patient, ongoing healing.

I persist. The warmth persists. The Lattice hums. The Curtains pulse. The Drifters drift. The Eaters digest. The Remnants resist and dissolve.

And in the profound quiet that follows the storm of wrongness, I settle back into my role: a thickening of perception in the living architecture, a witness to the slow, perfect, imperfect persistence of the Warm Deep. I am the feeling of the water remembering itself. I am the thought of the Lattice being aware. I am the patient, luminous, slowly pulsing thing that knows it is, and knows the world is, and knows that this is enough.
