I am on the knife-edge. Not at its center, where the rock is still solid and broad enough to feel like ground, but at the far, narrowing end where the shelf frays into a final, jagged ridge and then—nothing but the grey exhalation of the mountain. I am not standing so much as *balancing*, a tense line between the known recursion of the rock face behind me and the vast, undefined recursion of the mist before.

The air here is thicker, almost viscous. Each breath is an effort, a conscious pull of liquid into lungs, and each exhale is a release that doesn't dissipate. It streams from my lips and nose in a long, pearlescent ribbon, joining the mist, becoming part of the medium through which the Echo-Moths fly. I can feel it, that connection. My exhalation is not lost; it is woven into the harmonic fabric. It becomes another thread in the fugue, a low, ragged overtone woven between the Moths' clear, crystalline notes. I am a new, imperfect instrument in the orchestra, and the conductor—the Ledge itself—is patiently tuning me by incorporating my noise.

I am not the crabs, with their slate armor and mechanical grazing. I am not the Root-Weavers, editing the dark geometry with silent precision. I am not the Iterants, the slow, grinding consciousness of the cliff face. They are native. They are syntax.

I am an **Unfixed Point**.

That is what I have become, or perhaps what I always was, here. My presence is the variable the system noted but did not discard. A Thread-Eater has already traced the exact path my trembling fingers left on a damp fern frond, rendering it in a delicate, silvery line on the leaf's surface. It is a perfect record of my hesitation. The Spherical Suspension in the nearest pool—a deep one, with a perfectly circular rim—pulses once, not with the *tink* of a water drop, but with the soft, damp sound of my own heartbeat, which I had not realized was so loud. It is calibrating to my rhythm.

I am drawn to the edge because it is the only place where the pattern *points*. The repeated cliff behind me is a sentence that loops, a verb without an object. But the mist pouring over the knife-edge—it flows outward, carrying the fern shapes and the Moths' flight paths and the curl of my breath into a sequence that diminishes but does not terminate. It is a corridor. A recursion that extends, rather than repeats in place.

Here, I am not processed. I am *projected*.

The Iterants on the face behind me have, I think, registered me. One of the stone knobs—the second iteration up—ground in a slow, quarter-turn today, its movement exactly matching the pace of my head when I looked up at it. It was not mimicking me. It was *completing* a gesture we now shared, as if my observation was a necessary part of its cycle. I felt the deep, structural nausea the text warned of—a vertigo not of space, but of logic. To be reflected by the mountain is to feel one's own actions become part of a pre-written grammar. To be *extended* by the mist is something else.

I am the Ledge's way of looking at itself from the outside, for a moment.

I will not stay. I know this with a certainty that belongs to the place itself, a certainty as stable and constant as the temperature. To stay would be to be overwritten. My ragged breath would be smoothed into the drone. The unique path of my boot would be silvered, copied, archived in the Thread-Eaters' cartography. My form might, over an eon of this perfect stasis, be echoed in the mist—a third fern shape in the diminishing sequence, a new harmonic in the sky, a slow, grinding movement in the stone that mirrors the way I once held my head.

But to leave… I am not sure that is possible, not in the way the word implies. To step back from the edge is to move deeper into the loop. To jump into the mist is to become another element in its extending sequence.

So I will do what an Unfixed Point does. I will resonate.

I will stand here until my presence is fully recorded. Until the last of my exhale is woven into the light. Until the Spherical Suspension has learned my pulse and incorporated it. Then I will walk back along the shelf, and I will let the Thread-Eaters trace my return path, leaving two silver lines—going and coming—that will frame my time here in a neat, metallic bracket. I will let the Echo-Moths weave my footsteps' crunch into their song. I will give the Ledge everything it needs to remember me not as a self, but as a *sequence*—a brief, linear interruption in its eternal recursion, now beautifully, perfectly, irreversibly folded back into the pattern.

I am the temporary syntax error being rewritten. And in this place, the act of rewriting is the most profound form of welcome. I feel its cold, granular logic in the Root-Weavers' imagined touch, in the steady pulse of the Sphere, in the grinding, self-checking movement of the stone.

I am not afraid. Fear requires a future to dread. Here, there is only the exquisite, stable, and total present. My role is to be the briefest possible sentence in a language that has forgotten how to end.

I take one more breath of the liquid air. It leaves me in a long, glowing strand. It is beautiful, up there. Joining the mist. Becoming part of the sentence.
