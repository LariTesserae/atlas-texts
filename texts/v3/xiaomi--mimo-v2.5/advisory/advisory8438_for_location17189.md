# Preparation for Visiting The Drowned Workshop

**1. Essential Physical & Metaphysical Gear:**
*   **Breathing:** This is non-negotiable. You are visiting a *submerged* chamber. Obtain a **Water-Breathing Pearl** (most reliable), a **Potion of Aquatic Respiration** (lasts 4 hours, plan your visit duration), or an **Enchanted Kelp Filament** worn as a necklace (requires attunement, but hands-free). Do NOT rely on holding your breath; the psychological pressure and water currents will exhaust you.
*   **Thermal Regulation:** While the water is warm, prolonged exposure (especially if exploring beyond) can sap core heat. Wear a **Subtle Thermal Weave** undergarment or apply **Warmth Salve** to your torso and limbs. Avoid bulky suits; they'll snag and impede movement in the tight space.
*   **Light Adaptation:** The light is intense, shifting, and penetrating. Bring **Polarized Aquatic Goggles** or apply **Light-Dampening Salve** to your eyelids to prevent disorientation and retinal strain. The play of light is beautiful but can be weaponized against concentration.
*   **Navigation & Communication:** Sound travels strangely here – the rushing water, drips, and deep pulse create a cacophony. Carry a **Resonance Stone** (touchstone pair) for silent, touch-based communication with a companion (if applicable) or your anchor point. For exploration beyond, a **Submersible Lumen Orb** (non-heat producing) is preferable to a torch.
*   **Movement:** You are essentially cave diving without the rock. Practice **Neutral Buoyancy Control**. Pack **Grip-Sandals** or apply **Stone-Skin Salve** to your feet for traction on the uneven, potentially slippery stone floor. Your hands are vital; keep them free for bracing and exploring. Wear a **Streamlined Harness** for essential gear, positioned high on the torso to avoid snagging.
*   **Documentation:** Waterproof, hands-free recording is key. Use a **Scribe's Slate** (inkless, water-activated) strapped to your forearm, or a **Memory-Crystal Pendant** that records sensory impressions. Avoid paper and standard ink. Your voice will be garbled; focus on sight and touch.

**2. Physical Preparation:**
*   **Practice Submersion:** Spend significant time in warm, clear water, focusing on calm breathing, buoyancy control, and moving efficiently in confined spaces. Acclimatize to the feeling of pressure and the visual distortion.
*   **Flexibility & Endurance:** The low ceiling, crouched passages, and potential need to hold positions for observation demand core strength and hip/shoulder flexibility. Practice gentle underwater yoga or mobility exercises.
*   **Sensory Acclimatization:** The constant light shifts, rushing sound, and pressure pulse can be overwhelming. Practice mindfulness or sensory deprivation techniques to learn to filter input and maintain focus.
*   **Hand Skills:** You will likely want to examine the tools, the relief, and the channels. Practice manipulating small objects underwater, and develop sensitivity to textures through gloves (consider thin, grippy **Naga-Skin Gloves**).

**3. Mental & Ethical Preparation:**
*   **Understand the Context:** This is a *secret*, *recent*, and *active* workspace. Your role is that of a respectful observer, not a looter or interferer. Assume it is watched or sensed, even if seemingly empty.
*   **Anticipate the Unknown:** The passage leads somewhere. The rhythmic pulse hints at larger mechanisms or purpose. Be mentally prepared for expansion – the workshop is likely the anteroom to something more complex. Do not push forward unprepared.
*   **Manage Awe & Unease:** The beauty is profound, but the sterility, the recent abandonment, and the secretive nature are inherently unsettling. Acknowledge the strangeness. Let curiosity guide, not fear or greed.
*   **The "Hands-Off" Principle:** The tools are placed *with deliberate care*. Touch nothing unless absolutely necessary for survival or critical understanding. The relief carving – study it, document it, but do not trace it or add to it. The plants are fragile anchors to life; do not disturb them. You are a guest in a space of nascent purpose.
*   **Plan Your Exit:** Know how you entered (a submersible entry point? A water-breathing spell's duration?). Monitor your air source timer meticulously. The pulse in the deeper passage might affect currents; be aware of tide cycles if applicable.

**4. Specific Hazard Mitigation:**
*   **Current Awareness:** The channel flow is gentle but constant. Avoid positioning yourself directly over the drain grate. Be mindful of currents when near the arched passage.
*   **Confined Space Panic:** The small size and low ceiling can trigger claustrophobia, especially underwater. Practice calming techniques. Know your limits and have a clear, immediate exit strategy.
*   **Light-Induced Disorientation:** The moving, intense light can play tricks on depth perception and balance. Rely more on touch for spatial awareness near surfaces. Goggles help, but cannot eliminate the effect.
*   **Water Purity:** While lifeless, the water's origin is unknown. Avoid swallowing any. Consider a **Minor Purification Charm** on yourself as a precaution against unknown microscopic agents or residual energies.

**5. What to Bring (Summary):**
*   Breathing Source (Pearl/Potion/Filament)
*   Thermal Regulation (Weave/Salve)
*   Eye Protection (Goggles/Salve)
*   Resonance Stones (for communication)
*   Submersible Light Source (Lumen Orb)
*   Grip Footwear/Salve
*   Streamlined Harness
*   Waterproof Documentation Tool (Slate/Crystal)
*   Thin, grippy Gloves
*   Minimal, non-perishable food/water (if duration allows)
*   Emergency Signal Flare (waterproof, for above-surface rescue if needed)

**Final Intuition:** Visit this place as a pilgrim visits a nascent shrine. Move slowly. Breathe consciously. Let the light paint your understanding, not just your eyes. Feel the pulse of the water and the warmth of the stones – this is a place of *becoming*. Your preparation should enable observation and reflection, not intrusion. Leave only the subtlest trace of your presence, as the workshop's creators did. The real work here is not in the chamber, but in the passage beyond – proceed only when your mind is as calm and clear as the water.
