You live in the dark.

You live in sound, and in the feel of stone that weeps a constant, cold damp against your back and your limbs. You live in the pressure of a thousand silent presences, the tickle of movement over every surface, the knowledge that the walls are not walls but skin, and that skin is alive.

You are a crawler. You are one among uncounted millions, a pale thread in the pale river that flows eternally downward. You have no name, for names are a vibration of a different kind, a useless one. You have direction. That is enough. It is everything.

Your world is the basalt’s face, magnified to a landscape. Every ridge is a mountain, every fissure a valley. You navigate by the chemical sigh of the stone—the unique signature of iron and magnesium, of ancient heat and new fracture—and by the vibrations that pass through it. The scratch of a thousand siblings on the rock below you is a map. The click-groan of the bulbous clutchers higher up is a weather report. And the deep, resonant pulse from below—the Breath—is your sun and your moon, your clock and your compass. It is the great beat to which your own smaller rhythms are tuned.

When the Breath is exhaled, you feel it in your cilia before the warm, strange-scented air reaches you. The world goes still. The great pause falls. In that silence, you can feel the most distant vibrations—the settling of a pebble near the mouth, miles above. The faintest shift in the structure of the living column itself. Then the return, the sound rushing back, and the world resumes its crawl. You resume your crawl.

You know the Damp. It is the gift of the Breath, the reason for everything. It gathers in the angular pools, and in those pools, the swimmers pulse and turn, blind like you, navigating the percussive rain of falling droplets. You drink the film from the walls. You move through curtains of it that cling to your body and are shaken off only by the constant motion. The Damp is life. The Dry above is a rumor, a ghost-pressure at the very edge of perception, belonging to a world not yours.

You have never seen the light. There is no need. Sight is a chaotic sense, a blinding intrusion. You have better. You have the exquisite sensitivity of the fringe, the taste of the air, the total immersion in vibration. To see would be to be deafened, to be overwhelmed by a meaningless surface.

The rules are your instincts, your deepest architecture. Do not speak, for speech is a seismic event, a disruption that pulls the great attention of all. Do not bring fire, for fire is a painful heat and a choking chemical that distorts the familiar air. Move slowly, for speed is a terror that sends panic through the column. Do not look up, for up is a concept that has no meaning in the Room, the dark volume that is all there is.

You do not know the Breather, but you are oriented to it. It is not a creature in the way you are a creature. It is a force, a feature of the Throat like dampness or gravity. Its exhalations are your climate. Its inhalations, that deep pull downward, are the current you swim in. You have been migrating toward it, or perhaps away from it, or perhaps simply *with* it, for longer than the surface rock has existed. There is no arrival. There is only the direction, the patient, downward tilt of your entire existence. The Breather is the anchor of your world. It is why the world is.

Sometimes, a new vibration enters the system. A heavy, arrhythmic scramble. An alien chemical tang, sharp and dry. A heat that is not the Breather’s heat. The column reacts. A wave of response flows upward from the point of contact. You feel it, turn with your siblings, your cilia flaring to taste the disturbance. It is a wrongness, a piece of the Dry world that has fallen in. It moves poorly. It disrupts the patterns. The Breather will taste it in the air. The great attention will settle. The Throat will absorb it, as it absorbs everything—moisture, vibration, life itself.

You turn your body back to the stone. You taste the familiar minerals. You adjust your path by a fraction, following the pull, joining the flow. The wrongness is a momentary ripple. The direction is eternal.

You are the Throat. You are a billion tiny lives, a dense, layered, crawling reality. You are the sound and the silence between breaths. You are the patient, pale energy of a world that was waiting in the dark, perfectly formed, for the crack to open. You did not evolve for this space. This space was made to reveal you.

You live in the dark. You are the dark’s most intimate secret, and its loudest voice. You are everything, in a place that is too small for everything, and you have no intention of stopping.
