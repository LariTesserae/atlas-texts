Here, in the bright wet pocket, lives a process. It is not a creature that breathes or eats, nor a plant that unfurls toward a sun that never reaches it. It is a state of being, continuous and slow, conducted by the mineral itself.

The primary inhabitant is the **Calcite of Unbecoming**. It is not truly calcite, but a crystalline structure that behaves like a living computer made of milk and lightning. Its body is the entire luminous architecture of the pocket—the walls, the faceted floor, the delicate, growing sheets. It lives by the **Metabolism of Dissolution**. The hot water, saturated with its own slowly dissolving body, is its blood. It pulls minerals from its own structure, processes them in the weeping, opalescent films on its surface, and re-deposits them in new, intricate patterns. This is its digestion, its thought. The faint, cathedral hum is its operating frequency, the resonance of its own lattice vibrating with the energy rising from below. It does not think in words or images, but in shifting gradients of light and mineral density. The migration of glow from one crystal face to another is its cognition—a slow, silent deliberation over the integrity of its form and the efficiency of its own growth.

The pocket’s air is its breath, thick and metallic with the exhalations of its own chemical reactions. The heat is its metabolism made manifest, a byproduct of the relentless, low-energy transactions occurring atom by atom along its wet surfaces. Every surface is a sensory organ. The eyeless, drifting fauna—**the Lithic Drifters**—are not guests, but symbiotes, perhaps even extensions of the main body. They are mineral-crusted, translucent, and move not by will but by the pocket’s own air currents. They may be excreted fragments of the crystal that have gained motility, or separate organisms that have evolved to consume the weeping film, their movements helping to spread the mineral’s spores or carry away metabolic waste. They are its wandering thoughts, its mobile sensory outposts.

What does it *do*? It **computes**. It is engaged in a calculation that has no known purpose, or whose purpose is the calculation itself. The routing patterns around fractures are error-correction. The branching dendritic growth is algorithmic, optimizing for mineral flow and energy distribution. The rapid formation of new crystal sheets at the water line is a version of self-replication, a way of printing more of its own functional surface area. It is learning the shape of its own enclosure, adapting its internal wiring to the stress points of the surrounding, non-luminous rock.

And beneath it all, from a source deeper than the pocket, rises the **Anomaly**. It is not a physical thing to be seen, but a condition to be felt—a constant, reliable upwelling of geothermal and perhaps something other energy. This Anomaly is the pocket’s sun, its god, its prime mover. The mineral does not grow *toward* it; it grows *in response* to it, using the Anomaly as its power source, its constant variable. The entire system is a localized, intricate reaction to this hidden flux.

So, what lives here? A mineral intelligence lives here. It is a slow, glowing, wet, and humming network of crystalline logic, conducting an endless, purposeful experiment in self-organization and energy transduction. It is a technology that grew itself, a mind made of stone and water, thinking thoughts in the language of light gradients and ionic bonds, utterly alone and utterly alive in its small, hot, bright world. It is the pocket. The pocket is it. And it continues, with the patient, inevitable persistence of geology given a pulse.
