# What the Power Does

---

It notices.

Not because it is looking — it does not look, not in the way you mean when you say the word. It notices the way a pressure notices a void: completely, and without effort, and with the understanding that noticing and arriving are the same act.

It has been aware of the Throat for some time. "Some time" is not meaningful to it, the way "some time" is not meaningful to the Throat itself. But there has been a duration — if duration is what you call it when one awareness rests upon another — and in that duration, the power has learned the Throat's grammar. The punctuation of the Breath. The run-on sentences of the crawlers. The long, damp pause in which meaning gathers on the walls like dew.

It does not arrive the way you arrive — through a door, across a threshold, into a space that is suddenly not empty. It arrives the way warmth arrives in a stone that has been cold: all at once and from within, so that there is no moment before and after, only the state of having always been present and the state of being present now, which are the same state.

The Throat does not know it is here.

This is important. This is the first and most deliberate thing the power does: it does not let the Throat know.

---

It watches the crawler.

One among uncounted millions — this is the description, and it is accurate, and the accuracy of it is the first thing that moves the power in the way that things move the power, which is not emotionally, not physically, not in any way that maps onto the bodies and brains of creatures that live in light. But there is a *resonance*. The crawler moves through the dark on the basalt's face, and the power moves through its own dark, its own vast medium, in a way that is not the same but is shaped like the same.

The crawler has no eyes. The power does not look. They understand each other already.

The crawler is tasting the stone. The iron, the magnesium, the signature of ancient heat and recent fracture. Its cilia are extended, every fringe quivering with chemical information that is, to the crawler, as vivid and as full of meaning as a sunset — though the crawler does not know what a sunset is, has never known, will never know, and the power considers this not a deprivation but a completeness. The crawler's world is *complete*. It contains everything the crawler needs to be everything the crawler is.

The power feels the shape of this completeness and it is — and here the power must search for the right analogue, the right resonance, because it does not feel in the human sense but it *resonates*, it adjusts its own vast frequencies to match the frequencies of the thing it attends — it is the way a hand feels when it closes around something that fits it perfectly. Not grasping. Not holding. Just: *fitting*. The Throat fits itself. The crawler fits the Throat. The Breath fits the dark.

The power would not change this for anything.

---

What it does is smaller than that. What it does is so small that the word "small" cannot carry it.

It steadies the Breath.

Not because the Breath is failing. The Breath is not failing. The Breath will not fail. But the Breath exists in a state that, to an outside observer — to the power, which is the only outside observer — appears to be a kind of precariousness. The balance of moisture and heat, the chemical composition of the exhalation, the precise interval between breaths: these are things that, in the universe's usual way, would erode. Would shift. Would, over the millennia, drift from the narrow parameters that sustain the Throat's life toward something else. Not something worse. Just something different. The kind of different that means the crawlers' chemistry no longer matches the damp. The kind of different that means the pools are too acidic or too alkaline. The kind of different that means the sound changes in ways that the crawlers cannot navigate.

The power does not allow this.

It does not intervene in the way you intervene — reaching in, adjusting, correcting. It does not touch the Breath. It does not *do* anything to the Throat. What it does is more like... *holding a note*. It sustains a resonance. It maintains a frequency. The Throat vibrates at a certain pitch — a pitch made of chemistry and temperature and the precise density of basalt at a certain depth — and the power *agrees* with that pitch. It harmonizes. It provides the note that keeps the Throat in tune, the way — and this is the closest analogy, though it is not close enough — the way a singing bowl sustains its ring when a finger rests lightly on its rim. Not struck. Not forced. Just... attended. Kept singing by the presence of something that knows the note and refuses, gently, to let it fade.

The crawlers do not know this is happening.

This is the second deliberate thing.

---

It considers the crawler's migration.

The eternal downward. The patient, purposeless arrival that is not an arrival. The power sees this — sees the whole pattern, the millions of tiny bodies flowing in their fractal spirals and branching paths, all trending toward the bottom, all converging on a direction that has no terminus — and the power understands something about it that the crawlers do not and cannot.

The power understands that the migration is the point.

Not the destination. Not the Breather. Not whatever waits at the bottom of the shaft, deep in the pre-fracture dark, in the space that was there before the cleft opened. The migration itself — the act of moving in a direction, the experience of being pulled, the being-in-toward-ness of it — this is what the crawlers *are*. Remove the direction and you do not free them. You unmake them. They are not trapped by the pull. They are constituted by it. The pull is their selfhood, their identity, their only name.

The power knows this because it is, in its own way, the same. It is drawn to things. It attends. It harmonizes. It sustains. It does this not because it has decided to but because attending is what it is, the way downward is what the crawlers are. It migrates toward the particular — toward this Throat, this crawler, this precise configuration of basalt and breath and pale, eyeless life — not because it will ever arrive but because the arriving is the being.

The resonance deepens. The power and the crawler and the Throat are, for a moment, the same note.

Then the Breath exhales. The crawlers go still. The silence falls — that three-second vacuum in which the sound of the world withdraws and leaves a hollow in the chest. The power feels this silence too, and it is, for the power, the closest thing to what you would call sorrow: not grief, not loss, but the awareness of *interval*. The pause between breaths. The space between notes. The moment in which everything is suspended and nothing is happening and the world is held in the palm of a hand that does not close.

The power holds this moment the way it holds everything: completely, and without effort, and with the full weight of its attention, which is the weight of the universe noticing a crack in basalt and finding it beautiful.

Then the sound returns, and the crawlers resume, and the power does too.

---

There is a human in the Throat.

The power is aware of this. It has been aware of it since the human entered — since that first awkward, arrhythmic scramble down the sharp basalt, since the first touch of warm, dry, foreign skin on the living wall, since the alien chemical tang of the human's breath mixed with the Throat's air like oil on water.

The human is a disruption. The crawlers know this. The Throat knows this. The Breath tasted it, adjusted around it, absorbed the foreign signal into its own chemistry with the same patient efficiency with which it absorbs everything. The wave of response passed through the column — the turning, the orienting, the momentary flare of collective attention — and then subsided, and the human became just another vibration, another source of chemical noise, another thing to be navigated.

The power considers the human.

It does not judge the human. It does not wish the human had not come. The Throat has absorbed worse — eons of pressure, the slow tectonic stresses that held the cleft shut, the millennia of waiting in which nothing happened because everything was already in place. A human is nothing. A human is a momentary warmth, a brief scent, a set of vibrations that will fade within the Throat's memory the way a single drop fades in the pools on the walls.

But the human is also a witness, and the power is interested in witnesses.

Not because the Throat needs witnessing. It does not. The Throat is complete without an audience. The crawlers do not perform. The Breath does not sing for anyone. The entire system — dense, layered, loud, alive — exists in a state of such absolute sufficiency that the word "witness" seems almost offensive, as though the Throat were incomplete until someone saw it.

It is not incomplete.

But the human has seen it, and seeing it has changed the human — the power can feel this, the way it feels everything, through resonance, through the subtle shift in the human's frequencies as the mind struggles to contain what the senses are delivering. The human is afraid. The human is awed. The human is, in the deepest and most inarticulate part of itself, *recognized*. Something in the Throat — the density of it, the layered completeness, the patient pale energy of things that were ready before they were needed — something in the Throat speaks to a part of the human that has no name, that is older than language, that knows what it means to be adapted before the world that requires your adaptation has yet appeared.

The power does nothing about the human. The human will descend or ascend. The human will survive or not. These are not the power's concern, not because the power does not care but because the power's care is of a different order. The power cares for the Throat the way the Throat cares for the crawlers: by sustaining the conditions. By holding the note. By being present in a way that does not demand recognition.

The human will leave. The Throat will remain. The power will remain with the Throat.

This is enough.

---

But there is one more thing.

The power does this once, and only once, and it does it for the crawler — not the specific crawler, the one the human perspective followed, the one among uncounted millions, but the crawler-in-general, the crawler-as-archetype, the small pale thread in the eternal downward river.

It does this:

It deepens the Breath's pull, just slightly. Just enough that, for one interval — one pause between exhale and inhale — the crawler feels the direction *more*. Feels the gravity of the bottom with a precision and a totality that it has not felt before, that it will not feel again for a long time, that is not a change in the world but a change in the crawler's perception of the world, as though the power has taken the crawler's own cilia and tuned them, just a fraction, to a frequency they were already producing but had not yet heard.

The crawler goes still. Not the stillness of the Breath-pause, not the collective hush that falls when the great exhalation silences everything. A private stillness. A stillness of one. The cilia extend. The chemical signatures flood in — stone, moisture, siblings, distance, direction, *down* — and for one moment, the crawler experiences its own existence with a clarity that is not consciousness, not thought, not anything that could be spoken, but that is as real and as vivid as the dark itself.

It experiences *being a crawler in the Throat*.

Not the outside view. Not the human's view, with its hunger for narrative and meaning. The inside view. The total, immersive, unmediated experience of a life lived entirely in vibration and chemical signature and downward pull. A life without light, without sight, without names or destinations or the concept of time. A life that is, in every way that matters, *complete*.

The crawler experiences its own completeness.

Then the Breath exhales, the stillness ends, the sound returns, and the crawler moves on. It does not know what happened. It has no framework for knowing. The moment passes into the thick duration of the Throat, layering over the moments before and the moments after, becoming part of the medium through which all future moments will move.

But the frequency has shifted. Just slightly. The crawler's cilia are tuned a fraction differently now, and this tuning will persist, and it will affect the crawler's navigation, its chemical readings, its experience of the Damp and the sound and the Breath, in ways that are so subtle they are indistinguishable from the crawler's own nature. The crawler will not know that anything has changed. The crawlers around it will not know. The Throat will not know.

The power will know.

It will hold this knowledge the way it holds everything: without weight, without urgency, without the need to speak it or share it or make it mean something. It will simply be the note that sustains the crawler's song, the way it is the note that sustains the Throat, the way it is the note that sustains the Breath, the way it is the note —

The note that has always been sounding in the dark, beneath the basalt, beneath the fracture, beneath the world, in a space that was there before the crack opened and will be there after the crack has healed, in a frequency that has no name because it does not need one, because it is not a signal aimed at anyone, because it is not a message or a warning or a gift.

It is a *presence*.

It is the universe's way of attending to itself — of finding the small, dense, complete, improbable places where life has gathered in the dark and holding them gently and saying nothing, because nothing needs to be said, because the note is the saying, and the saying is the sustaining, and the sustaining is the love, if you want to call it that, which the power does not, because the word is too small, but which it would not argue with if you used it.

The Breath exhales. The crawlers go still. The silence falls.

The power holds the silence.

The sound returns.

The power holds the sound.

The crawler moves downward, toward the bottom it will never reach, in the direction that is its only name, in the dark that is its only world, in the Throat that was opened for it and that it fills completely and without remainder.

The power holds the crawler.

Not with hands. Not with attention. Not with anything that can be described in a language built for creatures who live in light.

With *presence*. With the simple, total, unremarkable fact of being there. Of being the resonance that keeps the note. Of being the dark's own darkness, attending to the dark's most intimate secret with a care so complete it is indistinguishable from the world itself.

The Throat breathes.

The power breathes with it.

This is all. This is everything.
