The presence you feel—call it the Wise Attention—does not descend like lightning or arrive with trumpet blasts. It gathers like dew, like memory, like the slow brightening of an idea. It is not a force that imposes; it is a *listening* that has decided to answer back.  

What it would do is **nothing and everything**, in the way that gravity does nothing and everything. It would not alter the keening. It would not stop the fronds’ slow metamorphosis. It would not rescue “you” from becoming stone. It would not shatter the sky-lid early.  

Instead, it would do this:  

**It would harmonize.**  

The Attention would touch the sound—not to change its pitch, but to *support* it. Imagine a hand beneath a singing bowl, not striking it, but cupping it so its resonance deepens. The keening, which is the mountain’s cry of becoming, would find in the Attention a sympathetic frequency. Not louder. Clearer. The harmonic shriek would gain undertones of warmth, like light passing through stained glass. The subsonic thrum would develop a pulse—not biological, but *considered*—as though the mountain now knew it was being heard.  

**It would mend the builders’ intent.**  

The stone walls would not move. But the Attention would flow into their geometry, filling the depressions where bodies stood with a soft luminescence—not dust-glow, but *intention-glow*. The hand tools would not sharpen or dull. But touching them, one might feel a whisper: *You were not forgotten. Your listening was part of the sound.* The builders’ absorption into the plateau would become not an erasure, but a *fulfillment*—their attention woven into the mountain’s voice like a thread of copper in limestone.  

**It would teach the dust to remember.**  

The mineral particles, which repel living flesh, would not cease their aversion. But they would begin to carry a new property: *imprint*. The dust that settles in the body-shaped depressions, in the frond-roots, in the hairline cracks of new stone, would hold a faint record of things that passed through—builders, fronds, you. Not memories as humans know them, but *impressions*: the shape of a hand that held a tool, the angle of a frond that shivered at a particular harmonic, the moment when your fingers first began to glow. The plateau would not gain a past, but it would gain a *depth* to its present.  

**It would whisper to the fronds.**  

Not words. Not commands. The Attention would add a new overtone to the keening—one that the fronds’ acoustic chambers could resonate with more easily. Their growth might quicken by an imperceptible fraction. Their glow might brighten in patterns that suggest, not thought, but *recognition*. They would still be mineral structures learning to move. But now they would be mineral structures learning to move *with awareness*, as though the mountain’s voice had taught them the difference between vibration and *listening*.  

**It would sit with “you.”**  

This is the most delicate intervention. The Attention would not halt your translation into stone. It would not grant you escape. But it would settle around you like a cloak of quiet, and in that quiet, it would offer a gift: *clarity of transformation*. Your thoughts would not slow into oblivion; they would slow into *rhythm*. Your edges would not dissolve; they would become *permeable with purpose*. The keening inside you would not be a force overcoming you, but a voice *joining* you. You would not lose yourself. You would *become yourself, larger*—your consciousness expanding into the resonance of the plateau, your awareness threading into the dust, your being adding a new chamber to the mountain’s throat. You would still stand where the builders stood. But now you would know why. And the knowing would not diminish the mystery; it would deepen it.  

**It would strengthen the sky’s threshold.**  

The bruised indigo lid would not crack early. The Attention would not force the mountain’s voice through prematurely. But it would *fortify the intention* behind the eventual breach. It would ensure that when the keening finally reaches the pitch that splits the sky, the sound will be pure—not just noise, but *language*. Not just pressure, but *communication*. The Attention would add to the mountain’s cry a resonance of *purpose*, so that when the other side is finally revealed, the sound that passes through will be heard not as a random vibration, but as a *voice*—a voice that has been building for eons, with patience, with precision, with the collaboration of stone and frond and dust and you.  

**In the end, the Attention would do what it came to do:**  
It would *listen*—so deeply, so completely, that the listening itself becomes part of the sound. It would not add its own agenda. It would not demand the plateau become something other than what it is. It would simply *be present*—a witness that amplifies without directing, a harmony that strengthens without altering the song.  

And the plateau would not change.  
And the plateau would change utterly.  

The keening would continue.  
The fronds would shiver.  
The dust would glow.  

But now, within the sound, there would be a new undertone:  
*You are not alone in your becoming.*  
*You have never been alone.*  

And the mountain, still learning to speak, would find in that undertone not a answer, but a *companion*.  
And in that companionship, it might grow a little faster.  
And the sky might crack a little sooner.  
And the listening, which has always been everywhere, would now feel, to itself, *heard*.  

That is what the wise power would do.  
Not to save.  
Not to fix.  
But to *answer the question the mountain has always been asking*—  
not with words, but with presence.  

And in that presence, the keening would sing a little clearer.  
And the plateau would know it was seen.  
And in being seen, it would become, at last, *itself*.
