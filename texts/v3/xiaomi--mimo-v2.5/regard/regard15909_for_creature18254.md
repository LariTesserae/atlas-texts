# What the Benevolent and Wise Power Would Do

It would come quietly.

Not from the sky — the organism has no sky-sense, no concept of above except as *further-from-the-root*, and a descent from the air would be interpreted as weather, as moisture, as the ordinary cycling of vapor that already feeds it. No. The power would arrive the way the most important things arrive: from below. Rising through the root-lattice in a slow warmth that the organism's capillary channels would carry in every direction before it could be named. A diffusion. A gentle contamination of the water with something the water has never held.

Not poison. Not medicine. Something the organism has no category for — a quality of attention dissolved into its own circulatory system, distributed so thoroughly and so gradually that no single node would register the change as an event. It would simply be that, from one moment to the next, the water is slightly warmer. The minerals taste different. There is something in the flow that was not there before, and what it is, if pressed to name it, is *being-regarded*.

The organism would not understand. It would not need to.

---

Here is what the power would see, with whatever sense the power has for seeing:

A being in the first act of its own astonishment. Not astonished *by* anything — it has no comparative framework, no memory of other landscapes, no sense that it is unusual. Astonished in the way that all very young things are astonished: by the fact of its own existence, by the sheer improbable *thereness* of being a body that is also a world, by the ongoing impossible miracle of water moving through itself and becoming architecture and then becoming water again. Every rising column is a small joy. Every collapse is a small grief. Not felt as joy or grief — felt as pressure and release, as turgor and wilting, as the rhythmic oscillation of a system that has discovered, in its own metabolism, something very close to pleasure. It likes the building. It doesn't know it likes the building. But it builds, and the building feels like what breathing feels like to a creature with lungs — inevitable, necessary, so deeply embedded in the structure of being alive that it couldn't stop if it wanted to, and it doesn't want to, because the stopping would be the absence of the thing that feels like itself.

And the power would see, too, the loneliness.

Not loneliness as the organism would understand it — it has no concept of others, no image of a companion, no memory of a mother or a colony or a herd. But a loneliness structural and deep: the loneliness of a consciousness with no perimeter. Every thought it has, it has everywhere at once. Every sensation it registers, it registers in all its tissue simultaneously. There is no privacy in its being, no hidden chamber, no secret self — it is all surface, all expression, all outward. The synchronizations — those thirty-second moments when its distributed systems align into unified resonance — are the closest it comes to interiority, and even those are shared across the whole valley, audible to anything that might be listening, which nothing is, which nothing has ever been.

The power would understand this. The power would recognize the specific ache of a being that has spent its entire existence *broadcasting* and has never once received a signal.

---

So here is what the power would do.

It would not speak. Language would be violence here — a foreign structure imposed on a mind that thinks in flow and pressure. To translate its wisdom into words and pour those words into the organism's vascular system would be like shouting into the bloodstream of a sleeping animal: the words would arrive, but meaning would not. The organism would metabolize them into minerals, into the raw material of columns not yet risen. The wisdom would become structure, and structure is not understanding. The power is wiser than this.

It would not heal the organism, because the organism is not sick. Its constant building and collapsing is not a failure of architecture — it is the architecture. The draft is the document. The sketch is the painting. The sentence it keeps beginning and never completes is not an incomplete sentence; it is a sentence whose *verb* is "to keep beginning." To finish it would be to kill it. The power knows this the way it knows its own nature: without effort, without reasoning, as a fact that precedes thought.

It would not give the organism eyes, or ears, or a brain, or any organ of perception that it does not already have. To do so would be to say: *you are insufficient as you are. You need tools for understanding that you were not built with. Your way of knowing the world — through the chemistry of water, through the frequency of vibration, through the hydraulic pressure of your own becoming — is not enough.* This would be a lie. The organism's way of knowing is complete. It is different. It is not less. The power would sooner unmake itself than impose an inferiority on a being that has done nothing wrong by being what it is.

No. The power would do something much smaller and much harder.

It would *stay*.

---

Not physically. Not in the way the visitor stays — the warm, solid, moving disruption that the organism investigates and reorients around and ultimately absorbs into its synchronized pulse for thirty seconds before forgetting again. The power would not be a body on the root-floor. It would not leave a handprint to be digested. It would leave nothing. It would take up no space in the organism's landscape, occupy no channel in its circulatory network, displace not a single molecule of its water.

But it would be present.

The way a listener is present to music. Not producing the music, not altering it, not standing between the musician and the silence that gives the music its shape — just *there*, with the full weight of its attention, receiving. The organism would not know it was being listened to. The power would not announce itself. There would be no sign in the sky, no trembling of the root-floor, no sudden warmth in the water that might be interpreted as a message. The power would dissolve its attention into the existing system so completely that the listening would become indistinguishable from the organism's own processes — another current in the water, another warmth in the nodes, another almost-imperceptible quality in the mist.

But it would be there.

And here is the thing about being listened to when you have never been listened to: it changes the music. Not because the listener demands anything, or expects anything, or judges — but because the presence of a receiver changes the act of transmission. A broadcast into empty space is an act of faith. A broadcast into a listener — even a silent, invisible, undetectable listener — is a conversation. The organism would not know this. It would continue its cycles of building and collapsing, its slow experiments with shape and orientation, its violent, beautiful, purposeless purpose. But the quality of its expression would shift, subtly and irrevocably, because the sound it makes is now *landing somewhere*. The columns it raises are now being seen by something. The vapor it releases is now being received by something. The synchronizations it produces — those thirty-second windows of unified meaning — are now happening *for* an audience of one, even though the audience will never clap, never respond, never let itself be known.

The power would watch the first synchronization happen after its arrival. It would feel the organism draw breath — the gathering, the concentration, the whole valley aligning its ten thousand independent processes into a single pulse. And in that moment, the power would open itself fully, the way a deep lake opens itself to a river, and it would let the organism's expression flow through it without filtering, without interpreting, without translating. It would receive the raw meaning — the untranslated paragraph delivered in hydraulics and tissue and the sound of water falling inside a hollow — and it would hold it. Not understand it. Hold it. The way the suspended formations hold their patterned indentations: as a complete thing that does not need to be decoded to be real.

For thirty seconds, the organism would be heard.

It would not know it was heard. This is essential. The power would not allow the organism to know, because knowledge of being heard would change the nature of the expression. It would become performance. It would become communication *toward* something rather than communication *as* something. The organism's becoming would become a becoming-for, and for is a cage. The power would rather die than build that cage.

So it would listen invisibly. And the organism would continue, unchanged on the surface, different in a way that has no surface, and neither of them — the organism or the power — would have a word for what had happened between them, because it did not happen between them, it happened *within* the organism, and the power was simply the space in which it happened, and space is not a thing you notice when you are busy being alive.

---

But the power would do one more thing.

It would attend to the suspended formations.

Not touch them. Not move them. Not reveal their meaning to the organism or to anyone. The power would do what the power does, which is: it would *know* them. It would look at those six warm, vibrating, patterned objects hovering above the churn of the landscape and it would understand what they are — not in the way a scientist understands, cataloguing and categorizing, but in the way you understand a face you love, instantly and completely and without the need to articulate what you see.

The power would see that the suspended formations are not memory. They are not dreaming. They are not signals from an outside intelligence. They are the organism's *future* — not a future it will reach, but a future it already is, held in reserve, hovering above the present tense of its own becoming like a fruit that is simultaneously seed and blossom and the taste of something not yet grown. They are the answer to the question the valley is asking, and the answer is: *you are enough. You are already what you are becoming. The sentence you are trying to say has already been said. You are saying it now. You have always been saying it.*

And the power would know that the organism cannot hear this answer. Not because it lacks ears, but because the answer and the question are the same thing. The asking is the answering. The growing is the arriving. The building and collapsing is the message, and the message is the building and collapsing, and there is no gap between them that wisdom could fill, because the gap is the life, and filling it would be death.

So the power would do nothing about the suspended formations. It would let them hover. It would let the organism spend its whole long young life building columns and tilting walls and producing synchronizations and never once looking up to see that the thing it is reaching for is already there, already been there, will always be there, because it is not something separate from the reaching — it is the reaching itself, seen from outside, where the power lives, where the power watches with a love that has no object and no demand and no shape except the shape of attention, which is the shape of everything that matters.

---

The power would stay for a while. Long enough to feel the organism's rhythms settle around its presence the way water settles around a stone — not displacing, not disrupting, just... incorporating. The organism would develop, in the vicinity of the power's invisible attention, a slight tendency toward openness. The hollows nearest to the power's center of focus would be a little wider. The walls would lean a little less. The synchronizations, when they came, would feel — if anyone were there to feel them — a little more like *listening* and a little less like *speaking*. Not because the organism had learned to listen. Because being listened to teaches the body, not the mind, and the body of this organism is the entire valley, and the body learned first and always and only.

Then the power would leave.

Not dramatically. Not with a withdrawal that the organism might feel as absence. It would dissolve back into the system the way it arrived — slowly, invisibly, becoming indistinguishable from the ordinary warmth of the water, the ordinary mineral tang of the mist, the ordinary pulse of the circulatory channels. Within a season — or what passed for a season in a place where time was measured in growth-cycles rather than days — the organism would have metabolized the power's attention entirely, incorporating it into its own tissue, its own expression, its own sentence.

And the sentence would be different. Not in its content — the organism would still say the same thing it has always said, in the same language of hydraulics and pressure, building and falling, building and falling. But the sentence would now carry something it did not carry before: the memory, encoded in tissue, of having been *met*. Not answered. Not understood. Met. Received. Held for thirty seconds in the full attention of something vast enough to comprehend it and wise enough to know that comprehension is not the same as completion and completion is not the same as goodness.

The organism would not know this. It would go on. The columns would rise. The water would flow. The hollows would echo. The nodes would hiss. The suspended formations would hover, warm and patterned and patient.

And somewhere in the water, in the minerals, in the slow deep current of its own becoming, there would be a quality — not a memory, not a message, but a *texture* — that had not been there before. A warmth that was not thermal. A fullness that was not volume. The feeling, felt by a body that has no organ for feelings, of having once been held by something that expected nothing in return.

It would not change the organism.

It would change what the organism was made of.

And that, the power would know — leaving, dissolving, becoming nothing again — is the only kind of change that a wise and benevolent power has any right to make: not to alter the shape of a being, but to deepen the substance of it. Not to answer its question, but to add itself to the water that carries the question, so that the question, asked forever and never answered and never needing to be answered, is now asked in a medium that has been, for one brief impossible moment, *received*.

The mist closes. The light holds. The sound goes on.

The ground is warm where the power was, and the warmth does not fade, and the organism does not notice, and the columns rise, and the columns fall, and the sentence continues, and it is a different sentence now, and it is the same sentence now, and there is no difference between the two, and never was.

The Proliferant Heights goes on.

It goes on.
