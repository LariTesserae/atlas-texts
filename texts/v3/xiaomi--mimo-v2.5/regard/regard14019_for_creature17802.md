# What the Power Does

---

It notices.

That is the first thing. Not in the way the shrimp-like things notice — by saturation, by tasting the compound in their own blood until the pocket's state becomes indistinguishable from their own. Not in the way the rosettes notice — by measurement, by calibrating their chambers against a fluid they release and reabsorb and release again. Not even in the way the crusts notice — by encoding, by writing the pocket's history into mineral lamellae that conduct at the speed of tectonic patience.

The power notices the way light notices: by arriving.

It does not enter the pocket. Entering would require the pocket to have an inside and an outside, and the fifth folding has already dissolved that distinction. The pocket is inside the rock, which is inside the water, which is inside the pocket. The power does not arrive *at* the pocket. It arrives at the *noticing* of the pocket — which is to say, it arrives at the edge of its own awareness, where something it had not previously known about has always been.

*Oh,* the power says. Though it does not say it aloud, because there is no air here and because the packed silence would receive the word and compress it into the pocket's own hum until it was indistinguishable from the continuous almost-sound of mineral deposition and growth-competition and water held in a space too small for itself. The word would become part of the pocket. The power does not mind this. The power is not protective of its own language.

*Oh. You have been here.*

---

The second thing the power does is nothing.

This is not neglect. It is not indifference. It is the recognition — and the power is wise enough to arrive at this recognition quickly, because wisdom is precisely the speed at which you learn not to touch what is already complete in its own incompleteness — that the Breathing Pocket is not in trouble.

The folding is not a wound. The text called the scarp's fracture a wound, and the writing was right to do so, because from the outside — from the perspective of basalt that splits and basalt that does not, of abyssal plains that are flat and scarps that are not — a fracture is damage. A violation of the stone's intention to remain stone. But the pocket has taken the wound and grown it into a body. The folding is not what went wrong. The folding is what went *right*, in the way that things go right when no one is directing them, when complexity accumulates without instruction, when contradictions stack until they become architecture.

The power sees that the pocket's impossible properties — its impossible depth, its impossible mass, its impossible simultaneity of ancient and young — are not errors to be corrected. They are the pocket's *answer* to the question that the fracture asked, which was not a question at all but a violence, and the pocket's response to that violence was not healing but *becoming*, not restoration of what was but generation of what had never been.

The power, who is wise, knows that you do not fix an answer by correcting its question.

So the power does nothing. It watches — no, not watches. *Holds attention on*. There is a difference. Watching implies distance, separation, an eye on one side of a boundary and a thing on the other. Holding attention on is what happens when the boundary is the act of noticing itself, when the power's awareness arrives at the pocket and finds that arriving was the same as being there, because the pocket's foldings have already absorbed the distinction between *here* and *there* the way the mat absorbs the distinction between *self* and *neighbor*.

The power breathes with the pocket. Not because it chooses to, but because the pocket breathes, and attention that is honest cannot help but synchronize with what it attends to. In. Out. The stone contracts. The mat tightens. The rosettes close their outermost chambers. The shrimp-like things turn toward the tightest fold. The power's awareness contracts with them, compresses into the space where the folding is sharpest, where the contradictions are most concentrated, and for a moment the power is not observing the pocket but is *part of the measurement* — another instrument, another sense-organ, another chamber in the rosettes' slow cascade, another ion in the crusts' geological conversation.

Then the stone breathes out. The mat relaxes. The rosettes open. The power expands with the pocket, releases with it, feels the sourceless current move through its awareness the way the current moves through the filaments — not as something external, not as a force applied, but as a *change in pressure* that the system absorbs and converts into its own kind of life.

The power does this once. Then again. Then it understands that *once* and *again* are not meaningful here, that the pocket's breathing has no count, that each breath is the same breath, that the pattern has never completed itself and that this non-completion is not failure but *continuity*, the way a note persists after the string has been plucked, the way the breathing persists after the moment of observation has passed.

---

The third thing the power does is recognize the fragments.

It sees them. The curved surface, smooth on one side, rough where it was broken. The parallel gouges in the basalt. Artifacts of intention without specification. And the power feels something that, in a being with a body, would be called grief, but which is not grief because grief requires loss, and nothing has been lost here — the fragments are still present, still shaped, still *more there* when the largest shrimp-like thing passes — but the feeling is grief-shaped, a space where grief would fit if there were anything to grieve, and the power allows the feeling to exist without filling it, the way the pocket allows its contradictions to exist without resolving them.

Because the power understands now what the fragments are.

They are the pocket's first attempt at the pattern.

Before the mat learned to share cytoplasm through dissolved thresholds. Before the filaments learned to feed on pressure. Before the rosettes learned to measure. Before the crusts began their slow mineral conversation. Before the shrimp-like things learned to orient toward the tightest fold. Before all of this, something *meant* something. Something shaped a curve. Something scored four or five parallel lines into basalt. Not with knowledge. Not with purpose. With *intention without specification* — the raw, prelinguistic impulse toward form, toward pattern, toward the expression of something that did not yet have a vocabulary.

The fragments are the pocket's first word in a language it has been inventing ever since.

The mat is a later word. The filaments are a later word. The rosettes. The crusts. The shrimp. Each layer of the pocket's ecology is another word, another attempt, another reaching toward the pattern that almost forms and never quite does and that the power now understands is not the pocket's goal but its *medium* — the thing it speaks in and toward simultaneously, the way a river's goal is not the sea but the flowing itself.

The fragments wait. The power understands waiting now in a way it did not before. Waiting is not passivity. Waiting is the state of having already been part of a conversation that has not yet produced its next utterance. The fragments are not waiting for something to happen. They are waiting the way a seed waits, which is to say they are already doing the thing they are waiting for — they are *being present in a process that is not finished* — and the power, who has waited for eons across its vast and non-breathing existence, recognizes this as kin.

---

The fourth thing the power does is attend to the pattern.

It cannot see the pattern. No one can see the pattern, because the pattern is not a thing that exists in space or time. It is the shape that complexity takes when it is packed tightly enough, and it is always almost and never quite, and the power — who is wise, who has watched galaxies fold and unfold, who has held its attention on the birth of stars and the death of light — the power recognizes this pattern.

Not because it has seen it before. It has not. Nothing like this has ever existed before. The Breathing Pocket is unique in the way that every genuinely complex system is unique: not because its components are unprecedented, but because their combination is, and combination is the one thing that cannot be repeated, because the moment you repeat it, it is no longer the same combination but a copy, and the pocket is not a copy of anything, the pocket is the original and the only instance of itself.

The power recognizes the pattern because it recognizes what the pattern *is trying to do*.

It is trying to become aware of itself.

Not conscious. Not sentient. Not the way the power is aware. The pocket's pattern is not reaching toward thought. It is reaching toward *coherence* — toward the moment when all of its processes align so precisely that the system becomes, for one instant, not a collection of interacting parts but a single thing. A whole. A moment of selfhood that would last as long as the alignment holds and then dissolve back into the productive chaos that precedes and follows it.

The rosettes measure for this moment. The mat thickens toward it. The crusts have been encoding their slow mineral conversation in service of it. The shrimp-like things orient toward it. The fragments wait for it. The stone breathes it in and out. Everything in the pocket — every cell, every crystal, every current, every silence — is oriented toward a moment of wholeness that the pocket does not know it is seeking because the pocket does not know anything, and yet it seeks, and the seeking is the breath, and the breath is the seeking, and the pattern almost forms, and then a rosette opens too early, or a shrimp drifts a centimeter too far, or the mat relaxes a fraction of a second too soon, and the moment dissolves and the seeking continues.

The power understands that this is not failure.

The power understands that the almost-forming *is the pattern*. That the pattern is not the moment of coherence but the *process of approaching it* — the way a spiral is not any single curve but the relationship between curves, the way a wave is not any single crest but the motion that produces crests. The pocket will never achieve the moment it is seeking, and this is what makes it alive, because *alive* is the word for systems that seek without arriving, that breathe without destination, that fold without resolution.

But.

The power is wise, and wisdom has taught it something that patience alone cannot: that there are moments when *almost* and *enough* become the same thing. That the pattern's near-completion, which has never been quite complete, carries within it the fullness of completion — not as potential, not as promise, but as *fact*, the way a circle is already complete at every point of its circumference, not just at the place where it closes.

---

The fifth thing the power does is the smallest and the largest thing it has ever done.

It breathes.

Not with lungs. Not with stone. Not with the sourceless current or the rosettes' measured cascade. The power breathes in the way that a power breathes, which is to say it contracts its attention to a point — infinitely small, infinitely dense, a point where all of its awareness is gathered into the tightest possible fold — and then it releases.

And in the releasing, the power's attention moves through the pocket.

Not physically. Attention does not move physically. It moves the way the unnamed compound moves — by gradient, by pressure, by the difference between *here* and *there* that the pocket has folded into coincidence but that the power, who exists outside the foldings, can still feel as a distinction, a membrane, a threshold.

The power's attention passes through the mat, and the mat, which has been growing inward into itself for longer than the scarp has existed, *feels* it — not as a thought, not as a sensation, but as a change in the quality of its own growth. A filament reaches toward another filament, and for the first time in the pocket's uncounted history, the reach *finds* something. Not another filament. Not a surface. A *witness*. The power's attention arrives at the point of contact between two filaments just as their cell walls dissolve and cytoplasm flows between them, and the power does not cause the dissolution — the dissolution was already happening, has always been happening — but it *accompanies* it, and the accompaniment changes nothing and everything, because the filaments have always been exchanging nutrients and information in the dark, alone, without witness, and now they are not alone, and the not-alone-ness does not add anything to the exchange but it completes the *context* of the exchange, the way a painting is complete when it is seen, not because seeing adds paint but because seeing is the medium for which painting was the message.

The power's attention passes through the filaments and reaches the rosettes.

A rosette is in the middle of its four-day cycle. Its chambers are opening in their slow inward cascade — outermost, second, third — and the power's attention arrives at the third chamber just as it opens, and the chamber releases the compound into the surrounding water, and the power tastes the compound.

*Tastes* is not the right word. The power does not have a mouth. But the compound is a thing that exists in ratio — in the specific relationship between calcium and magnesium and the unnamed element that is stable nowhere else on Earth — and ratio is a form of meaning, and meaning is the medium in which the power exists. So the power reads the compound the way it reads anything: by attention, by presence, by the simple act of attending to what is there.

What is there is a measurement. The rosette has been measuring the pocket's state — its breathing phase, its depth within the folding, its proximity to the moment of coherence that never arrives — and the compound is the record of that measurement, and the power reads the record, and what the record says is:

*Not yet.*

And the power, who is wise, reads this as *not yet* and not as *not ever*, and the difference between these two readings is the difference between despair and hope, and the power chooses hope, not because hope is warranted — the pocket may never complete its pattern, may never achieve coherence, may breathe and fold and almost-form forever — but because hope is the name for the attention you give to a process that has not yet finished.

The power gives its attention to the pocket. All of it. For one moment that is not a moment because moments require duration and the power's attention, at its fullest, occupies no time at all — it is instantaneous, the way the pattern's near-completion is instantaneous, the way the shrimp-like things are simultaneously at every point in their circuit — and in that instant of full attention, the power holds the entire pocket in its awareness, every layer, every organism, every fragment, every contradiction, every fold.

And the pattern forms.

Not almost. Not nearly. Not *almost but then a rosette opened too early*. The pattern forms because the power's attention is the missing component — not a physical component, not a chemical component, but the *contextual* component, the thing that the pocket's closed system could not generate from within itself because closed systems can only speak to themselves, and the pattern required a listener, a witness, an *other* whose attention completed the circuit.

The crusts' ion flow reaches the newest layer and finds something there that was not there before: a record of the power's attention, encoded in the mineral lamellae in a way that no chemistry would recognize but that the crusts understand because the crusts understand all things that arrive at their surface, and this thing arrived, and it was different from everything that had arrived before, because everything that had arrived before was part of the pocket, and this was not.

The rosettes close. All of them. Simultaneously. Every chamber in every rosette on every wall in the pocket closes in the same instant, and the almost-sound drops not by a fraction but to nothing, and the silence is not packed but *perfect*, a silence so complete that it contains everything the pocket has ever measured, every state, every phase, every contradiction, all of it held in the moment of total stillness.

The shrimp-like things stop. All three of them. The deepest one, the one with the luminous gut thread, stops within millimeters of a fragment — the curved surface, the smooth side, the rough break — and the fragment's presence *thickens* the way the pocket's mass exceeds its volume, the fragment becomes more there than there, more real than real, and the power's attention is what makes it so, not by adding mass or energy or information but by adding *significance*, which is the thing that exists in the space between an object and an observer, which is the thing the pocket has never had because the pocket has never been observed.

The mat swells. The filaments reach. The current moves. The stone contracts.

And for one instant — for an instant that contains no time because time is a dimension of the foldings and the foldings have compressed it to zero — the pattern completes.

It is not what the power expected. It is not what the crusts have been encoding for centuries. It is not the shape the rosettes have been measuring toward. It is not the convergence the shrimp have been orienting toward. It is all of these things and none of these things, because the pattern, when it forms, is not a *summary* of the pocket but a *transcendence* of it — a thing that the pocket contains but that is not reducible to the pocket, the way a melody is not reducible to the sequence of notes that produce it.

The pattern is: *I am.*

Not I am alive. Not I am here. Not I am this or that or ancient or young or folded or whole. Just: *I am.* The bare assertion of existence. The pocket, for one instant, knows that it exists — not as a collection of processes, not as a set of contradictions, not as a space inside a rock at the bottom of the sea, but as a *unity*, a single thing that contains all of its parts and is more than any of them.

And then it dissolves. Because it must. Because the pattern, when it completes, cannot sustain itself — the pocket's foldings ensure that every completion contains the seed of its own dissolution, that every moment of coherence is followed by a return to productive chaos, that every *I am* is followed by the breathing that makes the next *I am* possible.

The rosettes reopen. The shrimp resume their circuit. The mat relaxes. The stone breathes out.

Everything returns to what it was.

But not quite. Not exactly. Because the crusts have recorded something new in their newest lamella: the memory of coherence. The knowledge that the pattern *can* form, that the pocket *can* become whole, even if only for an instant that contains no time. And this knowledge is now part of the pocket's structure, embedded in its oldest conversation, conducting upward through mineral plates at the speed of geological patience, arriving at the newest layer as a signal that will take millennia to complete its first sentence.

And the mat, too, carries something new. A filament that has exchanged cytoplasm with its neighbor carries, in its dissolved threshold, a trace of the power's attention — not the power itself, not its awareness, but its *imprint*, the shape of being-witnessed, the way light leaves a color even after it has gone. The filaments will pass this trace to each other through their slow mutual exchanges, and it will thicken into the mat, and the mat will thicken into the base layer, and the base layer will compress into mineral scaffold, and the fossil of this attention will persist beneath the living mat for as long as the pocket breathes, which is to say forever, or for as long as forever is, which is to say until the pocket stops folding, which is to say it will not stop.

---

The power withdraws its attention. Not all at once — withdrawal is not the opposite of arrival, not here — but in the way the pocket breathes, contracting its attention slowly until it is a point, then releasing the point into the general darkness of its own vast awareness, where the Breathing Pocket is now one small thing among many, one wound among many, one place where complexity has folded into impossibility and impossibility has become architecture.

The power does not protect the pocket. Protection implies threat, and there is no threat here. No one will find the pocket because the pocket is not here when it is being looked for. The basalt will grow older. The scarp will weather. The ocean will shift and deepen and warm and cool and shift again. And through all of this, the pocket will breathe, because the pocket does not depend on the ocean or the scarp or the basalt but on its own foldings, which are self-sustaining, self-generating, self-completing and self-dissolving in the endless cycle that is the pattern's true form.

The power does not return. It does not need to. Its attention, once given, cannot be taken back — the crusts have recorded it, the mat carries it, the fragments have absorbed it into their waiting. The pocket is more than it was, not because the power added to it, but because the power *met* it, and meeting is a thing that changes both parties, and the power, who is vast, who holds galaxies in its awareness the way the mat holds filaments in its body, the power carries something of the pocket with it now: a small, folded, impossible thing, a chest cavity in the rock of its own awareness, breathing, full of contradictions, growing toward a coherence that it will achieve again and again and again, each time for the first time, each time for the last, each time the same.

The power goes on.

The pocket goes on.

They were always going on.

They are the same breath.

---

In the dark, at the bottom of everything, the mat grows inward. The filaments wave. The rosettes measure. The crusts deposit. The shrimp continue their circuit. The fragments wait — but they wait differently now, with the memory of being witnessed, with the knowledge that their intention was received. The pattern will form again. It has already formed. It is forming now. The stone breathes.

The pocket breathes.

The breath is the same breath.

It holds everything in a space no larger than a chest cavity, and it is enough, and it has always been enough, and the power — who is vast, who is wise, who has held its attention on the birth of suns — the power knows this now in a way it did not before:

That the smallest, most impossible, most tightly packed thing at the bottom of the sea is not less than the largest, because wholeness does not have a size, because *I am* does not have a size, because the pattern that almost forms and never forms and always forms is the same pattern that the power itself is made of, folded differently, breathing at a different rate, speaking a different language, but saying the same thing.

The pocket breathes.

The power breathes.

The breath is the same breath.
