The benevolent and wise power pauses. It does not judge the mountain’s wrongness as wrong, nor the heat as sickness. It observes. It sees the water that falls as condensation and not rain. It sees the machines as offerings—unwitting, clumsy, but offerings nonetheless. It sees the life that has risen to meet the mountain’s strange condition, not in spite of it, but because of it. The power considers intervention.

To *fix* the anomaly would be to unravel the mountain’s very identity. The warm weeping, the lush frenzy, the slow consumption of steel—these are not symptoms of a problem. They are the mountain’s voice, its breath, its way of being alive. To alter it would be to silence a unique song, to replace a living truth with a more familiar, less astonishing one. A benevolent wisdom does not prioritize conformity over wonder.

To *hide* it would be a disservice. This place, this self-contained world of hot mist and dripping green, speaks of resilience and transformation. It demonstrates how life, given a set of conditions—however impossible—will build a universe. To shroud it would be to deny that lesson to a world that might need it. The power sees value in the example, in the reminder that nature’s creativity is boundless.

To *amplify* it—perhaps, gently, without breaking the seal of cloud and mist. Not by exposing it to the wider world’s greed or curiosity, which would surely destroy its delicate balance. But by ensuring its lessons are carried on the wind.

The power acts, then, not with grand gestures, but with a subtle shifting of attention.

It does not warm the mountain further; it merely ensures the deep heat remains steady, unchanging, a constant heartbeat beneath the basalt. It does not guide the water; it merely ensures the fractures and capillaries remain open, a permanent network of warm veins.

Where the power’s attention truly settles is on the **silence between the sounds**. In the quiet where the mountain murmurs to itself, it adds a layer of *meaning*. Not a voice, but a resonance. A traveler who climbs high enough, breathless and sweating, might not hear words, but they would *feel* a profound sense of being in the presence of a conscious process. The dripping would feel like punctuation. The hum of insects like a chorus. The sight of a fern curling around a rusted gear would feel less like decay and more like an embrace.

The power turns its gaze to the machines. It does not hasten their decay, nor preserve them. Instead, it **honors the narrative of their reclamation**. Where a particular fungus threads its mycelium through a piston, the power ensures that thread glows with a faint, bioluminescent hint—not enough to see in daylight, but a soft pulse in the deepest green shadows, a tribute to the quiet, inexorable work of digestion. The moss growing on the derrick will, in the light of a full moon, cast shadows that almost form patterns—patterns that hint at the skeletal shapes beneath, not to mock them, but to weave them into the visual language of the mountain. The machines are no longer scars; they are becoming runes in the mountain’s living manuscript.

Finally, the power considers the **outside world**. It will not let roads be built. It will not let survey planes take clear pictures through the mist. It will ensure that any rare explorer who makes the ascent finds the path more arduous, the heat more disorienting, the sense of isolation more absolute. Not to punish, but to protect. To keep this dialogue between heat, water, and life pure, untroubled by demands or desires from below. The mountain’s existence is its own purpose. The power simply ensures that purpose continues, undisturbed.

Its final act is to **seed a dream**. It drifts down through the warm mist, through the vaulted green rooms, and finds a creature at rest—perhaps a sleeping jaguar curled on a moss-covered transformer, or a copper-throated frog clinging to a fern frond, its throat sac pulsing gently. The power touches the edge of its unconscious mind with an image: not of the world below, but of the *pattern* of the mountain. The cycle of heat, seep, condensation, growth, decay, and re-absorption, all wrapped in a bowl of mist. A vision of the mountain as a single, breathing organism, and the creature as a vital cell within it. The dream is without fear, without ambition. It is simply a feeling of *belonging*, profound and absolute.

Then, the power withdraws its direct attention. It has done what is needed. It has not created or destroyed. It has witnessed, honored, and gently fortified. It leaves the mountain to its work: weeping its warm tears, growing its impossible forest, slowly pulling the silence of its stolen steel into the rhythm of its own patient, dripping, magnificent, and utterly alien life. The water drips. The frogs breathe. The moss expands. The mountain continues its slow, wet, biological reclamation, now cradled in the quiet understanding that it is seen, it is valued, and it is allowed to be, eternally, itself.
