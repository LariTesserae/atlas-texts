# The Attention

The power does not arrive. It is already present, woven into the rock and light as the cold is, as the mist is. But for a moment—a moment held outside the cirque’s own rhythm of freeze and drip—it *attends*. It is the intelligence that notices the intelligence already there.

**It begins with the mist.**  
On its next descent, the cloud-tendrils move with a marginally greater precision. They do not merely pool on the cirque floor; they *seep*. The water, when it condenses, carries not just moisture but a faint, almost chemical calm—a stabilization of the tiny liquid bonds so they cling more tenaciously to moss fronds and willow leaves, reducing the waste of evaporation by a fraction. This is not abundance. It is a slight thickening of the odds. The moss mat will survive a slightly longer dry spell between occupations. The willows will put out one more rootlet.

**It turns to the stone.**  
The frost-cracking continues—this is the mountain’s breath, its slow metabolism, not to be silenced. But the power smooths one subtle violence: the sharp report of a calving slab on the upper western wall. Instead of a sudden, catastrophic fracture, the stress releases over three days in a series of small, almost inaudible sighs. A larger boulder that would have shattered into dangerous shrapnel instead settles, intact, into the talus apron. It becomes a future shelter for pikas, a solid, lasting anchor in the shifting rubble. The sound of the mountain’s thinking becomes a murmur, not a shout.

**It considers the hearthstone.**  
The shallow grinding depression is not deepened, nor is it protected from the elements. Instead, the power encourages a colony of a specific, slow-growing lichen—not to cover it, but to *frame* it. The lichen grows in a fine, silvery tracery around the rim of the depression, making its artificiality more visible to the careful eye. It is an act of preservation by emphasis, a way of saying: *This was shaped. Remember.* The moss that works to erase it is not hindered; its patient work is simply made to contour around this one small testament.

**It attends to the life.**  
No new species are called. No predator is removed. The intervention is one of subtle fortification. The stoat’s coat completes its transition to winter white a day earlier, reducing its vulnerability in that “unfinished” phase. The rosy-finches find a slightly denser patch of moss-seeds along the southern edge, a bounty small enough to ensure their continued presence without overloading the system. The pika’s bark, when it echoes off the walls, carries a frequency that, to other pikas, more clearly encodes location and alarm—a sharpening of their language, not a change.

The fox, arriving at dusk, does not find a plumper rabbit or a simpler hunt. It finds the same cirque, the same calculations. But in the quality of the air as it descends, in the precise way the last light catches the granite, there is a **confirmation**. A sense that this specific arrangement of rock, light, and tenacious life has been seen, and in being seen, is rendered slightly more *real*. Not more permanent—for the power knows its nature is to be temporary—but more meaningful.

**The strangeness deepens, but in a new way.**  
The shimmer at the edges of vision remains, but it now seems less like a trick of the light and more like an aura—the cirque’s own vital signature, acknowledged. The low hum felt in the chest when silence peaks is recognized not as a mystery, but as the sound of a system in equilibrium, a heart beating. The mist still tends the hearthstone, but now it feels less like accident and more like a quiet, recurring tribute.

**What the power does not do:**  
It does not make the place easier. The cold is still a searching, intimate thing. The light still punishes. The struggle remains absolute. The power does not grant life; it *witnesses* it, and in the witnessing, adds a layer of significance that the cirque’s inhabitants cannot perceive but that subtly alters the quality of its existence. It is the difference between a process and a *place*. Between survival and *persistence*.

**The final effect,** if one could stand at the cirque’s lip as this attention withdraws, would be a change in the *feeling* of the air. Not warmer, not softer, but… settled. As if a held breath has been acknowledged and thus can continue to be held, with greater patience, for a little longer. The cirque and its being have been noted in a ledger of meaningful things. This does not help them. But it means they are not alone in their magnificent, improbable dance. The light is still brutal. The life is still fierce. And now, there is also the quiet, benevolent weight of being known.

The mist rolls in again. The pika barks. The willows rock and still. The rhythm continues, unchanged and yet irrevocably witnessed. And that, perhaps, is the power’s only gift: to say, with the certainty of a mountain and the precision of frost, *You are here. You are real. This counts.*
