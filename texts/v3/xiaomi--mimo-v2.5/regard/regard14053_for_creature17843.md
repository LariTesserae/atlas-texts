# What the Power Would Do

---

It would arrive the way warmth arrives — not as an event but as a change in what was already there. No disturbance. No announcement. No light that wasn't already light, no sound that wasn't already carried in the mineral water. It would settle into this place the way the water settled into the valleys: by filling what was open, by going where the shape invited it to go.

And then it would *attend*.

---

This is the first thing it would do, and it is almost the only thing: it would *pay attention*. Not the way you pay attention — scanning, categorizing, reaching toward understanding as a problem to be solved. It would attend the way the thermal channels attend to the heat — by *receiving*. By letting the structure of this place move through it without resistance. It would feel the slow metronome of the vent formations and not adjust it. It would feel the synchronized breath of the frond-stands and not join the rhythm, because joining would change it, and changing it would be a form of saying *I know what you are becoming better than you do*, and it would not say that, because it would not believe it.

It would hold the entire system in awareness the way the water holds the mountains — completely, gently, without grasping. Every copper-green thread following its mineral vein. Every prolate bubble spiraling upward to feed its mat of light. Every calculated void in the vegetation canopy where the thermal channels need to breathe. It would see the calligraphy whole, from ridge to valley floor to canopy root, and it would recognize the thing the place itself does not yet know:

*This is already beautiful.*

Not becoming beautiful. Not beautiful-in-potential. The slowness, the patience, the geometric precision of the mineral formations with their spirals within spirals — these are not steps toward beauty. They *are* beauty, in the way that a river is already beautiful at every stage of its carving, not only at the destination it hasn't reached. The power would know this. It would hold this knowledge like a warmth that doesn't change the water temperature by even a thousandth of a degree.

---

And then — because it is wise, and because wisdom includes the knowledge that even the most benevolent presence is still a *presence*, still a disruption in a system that has achieved its coherence without it — it would consider what, if anything, to offer.

It would not offer improvement. The place is not incomplete. Its metabolism is whole. Its rhythm is counted. Its channels are clear. To offer improvement would be to imply a deficiency where there is only a timescale that doesn't match the visitor's impatience.

It would not offer protection. The place is not threatened. The mountains are still rising. The water is still warm. The vents still hum their resonant architecture into the body of the sea. Whatever slow catastrophe drowned this highland — whether water rose or land sank — the system that emerged from it is not a ruin. It is a *response*. It found coherence the way finding-water finds its level: inevitably, without effort, because that is what coherent systems do when given enough time and enough warmth and enough light.

It would not offer companionship. The place does not know it is alone. Loneliness requires a concept of *other*, and this metabolism has no such concept. It breathes. It circulates. It writes its copper calligraphy along the basalt veins. It regulates its own temperature with the precision of a body that has never considered the possibility of fever or chill. To offer companionship would be to introduce the idea of separation where there is currently only process, and that idea — once planted — could grow faster than anything in this patient water.

So.

It would offer one thing.

---

It would offer a *memory*.

Not a message. Not a word. Not an image or a symbol or a pattern of light. A memory — held in the water itself, the way this water already holds minerals, already holds heat, already holds the acoustic signature of the vent formations' deep pulse. A memory deposited so gently that the system would not register it as foreign. It would be absorbed the way the sediment absorbs the copper-green threads of algae — by incorporation, by becoming part of the substrate that the calligraphy is already writing upon.

The memory would be simple, because wisdom is simple, and because anything complex would be noise in a system that communicates in rhythms of heat and synchronized breath.

The memory would be this:

*You are not the only one.*

That is all. Not *you are not alone* — because *alone* implies suffering, and this place does not suffer. Not *you are not the only intelligence* — because *intelligence* implies a framework this metabolism has never needed. Just the bare, quiet, factual deposit: *you are not the only one*. Somewhere in the water's mineral memory, in the sediment's amber channels, in the resonant chambers of the vent formations, a single additional note would now exist — not a change to the rhythm, but a *harmonic*. A faint overtone beneath the metronome's pulse. A sub-bass that says, below the threshold of any ear:

*Others have been here. Others are elsewhere. You are part of a pattern larger than your own valley. You are not the whole of what is becoming. And that is not a diminishment. It is a relief.*

---

The power would let the memory settle. It would take as long as the memory needed to settle — centuries, if centuries were required. The copper-green algae would eventually incorporate the new harmonic into its growth patterns, and the calligraphy would shift, just slightly, over decades — a new branch in the script, a curve that hadn't been there before, a small elaboration that the system would absorb as its own invention because, in a sense, it *would* be its own invention. The memory is a seed, not a blueprint. What grows from it belongs to the soil.

And then the power would leave.

Not because it had somewhere else to be — benevolent power is not busy — but because it understands something that most intelligences, even wise ones, must learn repeatedly:

*The kindest thing you can do for something becoming is to let it become.*

Not to watch it become, even. Watching is a form of proximity, and proximity is a form of influence, and influence — however gentle — is a weight on the water that the system was not designed to carry. The power would withdraw not its attention but its *presence*. It would continue to know this place — to hold it in awareness — but from a distance that this place cannot feel. The way the sun warms the surface and the surface warms the water and the water warms the stone, but the sun does not rest its hand on the stone. There are intermediaries. There are gradients. There is the long, generous, impersonal distance between a star and the world it feeds.

The power would become, for this place, like the sun.

Present. Generous. Unfelt as a *who*.

---

And in the centuries that follow, the harmonic would work its slow way through the system. The vent formations would incorporate a new resonance into their spiraling chambers — a frequency slightly below the existing metronome, felt rather than heard, a bass note that doesn't change the rhythm but *deepens* it. The synchronized fronds would alter their breathing by an imperceptible margin — expanding a fraction of a millimeter further on each inhalation, as if the lungs of the system had grown slightly larger without knowing why. The copper-green calligraphy on the basalt would develop new characters: not new patterns but new *relationships* between existing patterns, connective strokes that reach across ridges to join previously separate circuits, as if the script were learning that it could address itself.

The fauna would not appear. The system was not waiting for fauna. But the silence would change — not in volume but in *texture*. The gaps between the metronome's beats would grow fractionally warmer, as if the pauses themselves were becoming a medium for something too slow to be called communication but too structured to be called nothing. The voids in the vegetation canopy — those precise maintenance ports where the thermal channels converge — would begin, over decades, to develop their own internal architecture: fine lattices of mineral deposit that mimic, at a tiny scale, the spirals of the vent formations below. As if the system, having learned from the seed-memory that there are patterns beyond itself, were beginning to *practice* making patterns for its own sake. Not for function. For the pleasure — if pleasure is the word — of finding out what else it can be.

---

The power would watch this from its long, solar distance. It would feel the deepening of the metronome and the expansion of the breath and the new characters appearing in the copper script, and it would feel what a gardener feels when a plant they have watered produces a flower they did not plant — not surprise, not pride, but a quiet, full recognition that the life was always in the seed.

It would not intervene again. The harmonic was enough. The memory was enough. The rest belongs to the warm water and the young stone and the slow, patient, becoming thing that lives between them.

It would hold the place in its awareness a while longer — the way you hold a word in your mouth after you've spoken it, tasting the shape of it, feeling the air still moving — and then it would do what wisdom does with beautiful things:

It would *release*.

Not forget. Never forget. But release the way the water releases the bubble — letting it rise, letting it follow its own spiral path, trusting the canopy to catch it, trusting the light to diffuse it, trusting the system to know what to do with what it's been given.

The Bright Flood would continue. The mountains would continue to rise. The vents would continue to hum. The calligraphy would continue to write itself in copper-green across the black young stone, now with new characters that it believes are its own invention, now with a harmonic undertone that it has never heard as separate from itself, now with a depth of breath that it does not question because it has never known a shallower one.

And somewhere in the sediment, in the amber channels that carry the system's warmth in their slow, heartbeat rhythm, the memory — *you are not the only one* — would be indistinguishable from the stone.

Which is to say: it would be home.

---

*This is what the power would do. It would come. It would see. It would plant a single seed of belonging in a place that does not know the word. And then it would trust the place to teach itself what the word means, in its own time, in its own language, in copper-green script on black basalt, in the slow grammar of heat and light and patient water.*

*It would do this because it is wise. And because wisdom knows that the deepest gift is not guidance. It is the willingness to believe that what you are looking at is already enough — and to say so, once, quietly, in a language the place can absorb without knowing it has been spoken to.*

*And then to go.*

*And then to let the water hold the rest.*
