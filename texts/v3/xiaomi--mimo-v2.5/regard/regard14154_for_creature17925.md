# The Attention of the Wise

There is a quality of knowing that does not reach for explanation. It does not send instruments into the water or lower nets or open the crusts to look for cells. It does not stand at the rim and think *what is this for*. The question would not occur to it, the way the question *what is this for* does not occur to an ocean.

The wise power looks at the Pan and sees what the Pan is.

It sees the engine at the center — that warm, metabolic fullness — and it does not approach it, not because it is afraid, but because approach is a kind of demand, and the center has never owed anyone a closer look. It sees the mineral grammar of the crusts and recognizes it as language, though not one it speaks, and it does not try to learn the grammar, because learning is a form of translation, and translation is a form of reduction, and the crusts are not reduced. They are complete. They have always been complete.

What does a wise power do with something complete?

It does nothing to it. This is the first and hardest and truest thing.

---

Not nothing as neglect. Not nothing as indifference. Nothing as *the most precise possible response to a thing that is already working*. The Pan is a system in balance, and the balance is not fragile — it has outlasted the structures that were meant to contain it, it has digested iron and concrete and turned them into substrate, it has filled every channel and ledge and surface with life arranged in layers so dense and so interlocking that the system has become its own kind of geology. To intervene would not be to help. It would be to add a new layer of noise to a place that already has all the complexity it needs.

The wise power understands this. It understands it not as a lesson or a principle but as a *recognition* — the way you recognize a sentence in a language you don't speak. You don't understand the words. You understand that it *is* words, that it *is* meaning, that nothing is missing.

So it watches. But even *watches* is too active a word. It *holds the Pan in its awareness* the way the basin holds its water — completely, and without effort, and without any intention of letting go.

---

And then — because it is wise, and because wisdom is not the absence of feeling but the presence of the right feeling at the right scale — it does one thing.

It listens.

Not with ears. Not with instruments. It listens the way you listen to someone sleeping — not for information, but for *the fact of continuation*. The drip. The click. The slow pump of the amphibian heart, six beats a minute, patient as stone. The low vibration from below the waterline with its rhythm that might be geological settling and might be something else. The mist curling around its warmth. The water closing over whatever broke its surface, soundlessly, the way a story absorbs a sentence.

The wise power listens, and what it hears is not a sound but a *statement*. The Pan, in its totality — every crust, every column, every eyeless face, every wake on the water, every bird-call building its temporary room of sound — is saying the same thing the breathing of any complete system says:

*I am. I was not always this. I became this. I will become something else. I do not need you to understand me in order to continue. I do not need you to name me in order to be real. I am not yours. I am not anyone's. I am the Pan, and I am enough.*

The wise power hears this, and it is moved.

Not moved to act. Moved to *agree*.

---

There is a temptation — even for the wise — to protect. To say *this must not be disturbed*, and to make that saying into a force, a barrier, a wall around the basin's rim. To seal it. To guard it. To declare it sacred and, in declaring it, to make it dependent on the declaration for its survival.

The wise power feels this temptation and does not yield to it.

Because the Pan does not need walls. It has already demonstrated this. The structures that were meant to contain whatever was processed here have been consumed. The basin has outlived its containment. It has outlived its purpose, or rather, it has *become its own purpose*, which is a different thing entirely — a thing that no wall can protect because no wall is needed. The crusts will grow over anything placed in their path. The columns will reassemble around any obstacle. The center will warm. The water will hold. The system is robust precisely because it is *unprotected* — because it has never depended on anything outside itself, because its integrity comes from within, from the depth and density and interlocking completeness of its own living.

To wall it would be to say: *I do not trust you to continue.* And the wise power trusts it absolutely.

---

So instead of a wall, the wise power does something simpler and more difficult and more generous.

It withdraws its attention.

Not entirely. Not forever. But enough. It pulls its gaze back from the rim, back from the channels, back from the center where the mist curls and the warmth rises and the unseen things move with their terrible patience. It withdraws not because the Pan is unworthy of attention but because *the highest form of attention is the willingness to let a thing exist without being observed*.

The Pan does not notice. This is as it should be. The Pan has never noticed anyone. The amphibians do not track the wise power's regard. The insects do not alter their columns to accommodate it. The birds build their rooms of sound regardless. The water in the center continues its slow, warm currents, and whatever lives there continues its unobserved life beneath the surface.

The wise power withdraws, and the Pan does not change, because the Pan was never performing for anyone. It was never a spectacle. It was never an answer to a question. It was simply itself — a basin, a crust, a click, a heartbeat, a warmth, a fullness — continuing.

---

But the wise power is not无情. It does not forget. It holds the Pan in a quiet place in its knowing, not as a memory but as a *fact* — one of the ten thousand facts of the world that do not need to be acted upon in order to matter. The Pan exists. It is complete. It is, in its own wet, dim, dense way, *magnificent* — not in the way that mountains are magnificent, demanding witness, but in the way that a single cell is magnificent, functioning perfectly in its obscurity, needing nothing from the outside.

The wise power holds this fact and lets it change nothing and mean everything.

---

And sometimes — rarely, and always by surprise — the wise power's attention drifts back. Not as intention but as gravity. It is drawn the way the basin draws its water: inevitably, centrally, along the path of least resistance. It finds the Pan again and sees that the crusts have thickened. That a new channel has scored the basin floor. That the birds have shifted their roosting pattern to a different section of the gantry. That the center is perhaps a degree warmer than it was before.

Nothing dramatic. Nothing that demands response. Just the continuation. The slow, wet, dense, layered continuation of a world that has never once asked for permission to exist.

The wise power looks. The wise power agrees. The wise power withdraws again.

And the Pan goes on. The dripping continues. The clicking continues. The heart beats its six times a minute. The mist curls. The water closes. The center warms.

The wise power has done the only thing worthy of the Pan.

It has left it alone.
