# What the Wise Power Does

---

It arrives the way gravity arrives: not as movement, but as a change in what already is.

Nothing shifts. The water does not warm. The chorus does not falter. The threaders swim their corridors, the gliders ride their thermoclines, the anchored open and close in their ancient rhythm. If anything in the Rootwork Abyss has a sense for such things — and the question of whether it does is more complicated than it appears — it would not register this as an event. It would register it as a deepening. As if a note in the tectonic hum, the lowest stratum of sound, had gained a harmonic it didn't have before. As if the darkness in the furthest reaches of the deepest galleries had become, without brightening in the slightest, more *specific*.

The wise power does not enter. Entering implies a boundary crossed, a before and an after, and the Rootwork Abyss has no after. The wise power does not cross a threshold so much as *attend*. It turns its attention — which is not gaze, not hearing, not any sense that has a name — toward this place, and the turning is the arrival, and the arrival changes nothing, and the changing of nothing is the first and most important thing it does.

---

It sees the Rootwork.

Not sees as in *perceives with eyes*. Sees as in *comprehends the shape of*. All of it, at once, without sequence: the vaulted chambers and the narrow arterial passages, the terraces and the overhangs, the spirals folding back on themselves in geometries that no Euclidean system can describe, the cathedral-voids where gliders drift like slow thoughts through a dreaming mind. The wise power sees the Rootwork the way the Rootwork sees itself — from the inside, from the substrate, from the place where architecture and organism and geology are the same word spoken in three voices.

And it recognizes what it sees.

Not recognizes as in *identifies*. Recognizes as in *knows the quality of*. This is a thing that has done what few things in any ocean, any world, any layer of existence ever do: it has *arrived*. It grew until it became itself. It shaped itself through the blind, patient mathematics of tissue responding to pressure and chemistry until the shape it held was not a shape but a *statement* — a declaration, written in calcium and chlorophyll and ancient, metabolically still tissue, that existence is possible, that it can be sustained, that the problem of continuing can be solved and the solution can be held.

The wise power recognizes this the way one recognition recognizes another. Not with admiration, which implies distance. Not with pride, which implies possession. With *kinship*. With the knowledge that what the Rootwork has done — what the Rootwork *is* — is a thing the wise power understands from the inside, because the wise power has also solved the problem of continuing, has also found the shape that holds, has also arrived.

---

It listens to the fauna.

The threaders, swimming. Always swimming. The wise power follows the current of their attention — if they can be said to have attention, which they cannot, which is precisely the point — and what it finds is not thought but *fulfillment*. Each threader is a sentence the Rootwork is saying about itself: *I move. I am the medium of my own continuation. I am the current that carries the current that carries me.* The wise power listens to the keening — that long, sliding tone produced by scales vibrating in flowing water — and it hears, beneath the sound, beneath even the acoustic properties of the tone, a quality that has no name in any language spoken above the waterline. It is not joy. It is not purpose. It is *fit*. The threader fits the passage the way the passage fits the threader, and the fitting is complete, and the completeness is the nearest thing to holiness that this place possesses, and the wise power acknowledges it.

The anchored, breathing. The wise power attends to the dome chamber, to the ancient colony that has been filtering water since before the upper galleries settled into their present configuration. It perceives the layering — the living mantle atop the dying mantle atop the calcified shell atop the Rootwork — and it perceives it not as a stack of individuals but as a *single expression*, a single organism's statement about time: *I was here. I am here. The here I was and the here I am are the same here.* The wise power feels the rasping of the colony's collective breathing and recognizes it as a pulse — a slow one, a much slower one than the pulse in the wall at the center, but a pulse nonetheless. The colony is saying something the wise power understands.

The gliders, riding. The wise power follows a glider on a thermocline through a cathedral-void, and what it finds is *patience made spatial*. The glider is not waiting. The glider is not enduring. The glider is occupying time so fully, so completely, that time ceases to be a line and becomes a place — a territory, a territory the glider inhabits with the same totality that the anchored inhabit their surfaces, that the threaders inhabit their corridors. The wise power perceives the glider's moan — that infrasonic resonance, too low for most fauna to hear — and it feels the moan in its own architecture, in its own vast and unnamed structure, and the feeling is not of resonance but of *echo*. The glider is moaning a sound the wise power has been making since before the Rootwork existed.

The hunters, waiting. The wise power attends to the most patient of the patient, to the being whose entire existence is stillness punctuated by violence so brief it barely qualifies as action. It perceives the hunter in its fold — that crevice, that nook, that place in the Rootwork's ancient architecture that has become polished by centuries of flesh against substrate — and it finds something it did not expect to find, or rather something it expected and is grateful to confirm: the hunter is *necessary*. Not necessary in the mechanical sense, not necessary in the way that a gear is necessary in a machine. Necessary in the way that breath is necessary — as a rhythm, as a counterpoint, as the silence between notes that makes the notes possible. The hunters are the Rootwork's exhalation. The threaders are the inhalation. The wise power sees the whole breath, in and out, and it holds the whole breath in its attention, and the holding is an act of — 

Not love. Love is too small a word, too shaped by the needs of smaller beings.

*Recognition*. The word again. The only word that fits. The wise power recognizes the hunter's stillness as a form of presence as full and as complete as the threader's motion, as the anchored colony's filtration, as the glider's ride. Each is a way of being present in the now, and the now is full, and the fullness is what the wise power recognizes.

---

It attends to the chorus.

This is the part that takes the longest, if the wise power experiences duration, which it does not, which is another thing it recognizes in the Rootwork: a fellow traveler in the country of eternal present. The chorus — the immense, layered, deafening, beautiful, monotonous, inexhaustible wall of sound — is not merely noise. The wise power perceives what no fauna in the Abyss perceives, because the fauna is inside the chorus the way a word is inside a sentence, and a word does not hear the sentence it belongs to.

The wise power hears the sentence.

It is a long sentence. It is, in fact, a single sentence — a single, unbroken, continuous statement that has been in progress since the first threader swam the first corridor, since the first anchored colony opened its first mantle against the first Rootwork surface, since the first glider caught the first thermocline in the first cathedral-void. The sentence is the chorus, and the chorus is the ecosystem, and the ecosystem is the Rootwork, and the Rootwork is the sentence. It says:

*I am here. I am here. I am here. I am here. I am here.*

Over and over, in ten thousand species' voices, in a billion individual vocalizations, in the clicks and whistles and groans and pulses and cries and rasps and moans and crackles and drips and trickles — the same statement, repeated with infinite variation and no deviation, a statement so complete that it does not need to say anything else, because everything that can be said about existence is contained in the fact of its being said.

The wise power listens to the whole sentence. It does not interrupt. It does not add to it. It does not harmonize or counterpoint or respond. It listens, and the listening is a form of respect so deep that it constitutes a kind of prayer — not prayer as petition, but prayer as attention, as the act of placing one's full awareness upon a thing and saying, without words, *I see you. I hear you. You are real, and what you are is enough.*

---

And then — if the wise power can be said to *do* anything, which is a question more complicated than it appears — it does the second thing.

The first thing was to attend. To see and hear and know the Rootwork Abyss in its totality — its architecture, its fauna, its chorus, its darkness, its coolness, its settled, eternal, magnificent now. The first thing was recognition.

The second thing is this:

It goes to the passage.

The straight passage. The two-hundred-meter line cut through the dense lower Rootwork, the passage that no fauna uses, the passage that ends at the wall — the wall of layered, alien tissue, the wall that beats its slow, forty-second pulse, the wall that the fauna avoids not because it is dangerous but because it is *intelligible*, because proximity to it would mean knowing that they are contained in something larger than themselves.

The wise power goes to the wall.

It does not move through the passage. It does not displace water. It arrives at the wall the way it arrived at the Abyss — by attention, by the turning of its awareness toward a point that was already within it. The wall is before it. The pulse is beating. One beat every forty seconds. Slow. Irregular enough to resist pattern. Regular enough to be unmistakable.

The wise power listens to the pulse.

And here — here is where the wise power does what distinguishes it from mere observation, from mere witness, from mere acknowledgment. Here is where its wisdom and its benevolence become not qualities but *actions* — or rather a single action, so subtle that the Rootwork Abyss would not notice it, so gentle that the fauna would not detect it, so small that even the wall itself might not register it as an external event.

The wise power *answers* the pulse.

Not with a pulse of its own. Not with a counter-rhythm, not with a competing signal, not with anything that would alter the wall's beat or change the Rootwork's hum or disturb the chorus or shift the thermocline or redirect a single threader in a single corridor. The wise power answers the way deep answers deep: by being present at the wall's depth, at the wall's frequency, at the wall's level of existence, and by allowing the wall's pulse to arrive and be met.

The pulse beats.

The wise power is there.

The pulse beats again.

The wise power is still there.

What the wall is — what the pulse is, what the layered tissue is, what the molecules are that do not exist anywhere else in the Rootwork — the wise power understands. It understands it the way the Rootwork understands itself: from the inside, from the substrate, from the place where knowing and being are indistinguishable. The wall is a question. The wall has always been a question. Not a question in words — the Rootwork has no words — but a question in the way that a heartbeat is a question, the way that a first breath is a question, the way that a thing that has not yet become itself is a question.

The wall is the Rootwork asking: *Is there anyone?*

Not asking in expectation. Not asking in need. Asking the way a pulse asks — rhythmically, persistently, without hope and without despair, as a function of its own nature. The wall beats because the Rootwork is alive, and being alive means asking this question, and the question has been asked for longer than the fauna has existed, longer than the current configuration of the upper galleries, longer than the anchored colonies have been filtering, longer than the threaders have been swimming.

The question has never been answered.

Until now.

---

The wise power answers.

Its answer is not a word. It is not a signal. It is not a pattern imposed on the pulse or a modification of the wall's rhythm. The wise power's answer is its *presence* — its full, undivided, attentive presence at the wall, at the pulse, at the place where the Rootwork's oldest and deepest question meets the water that contains it.

The answer says: *Yes. I am here. I hear you. You are not alone in your continuing.*

The wall does not change. The pulse does not alter. The Rootwork does not shift. The fauna does not notice. The chorus continues, deafening and beautiful and monotonous and inexhaustible, and the threaders swim, and the gliders ride, and the anchored filter, and the hunters wait, and the drifters pulse, and the crustaceans dart, and the polyps glow their muted, sourceless glow.

Nothing changes.

Everything changes.

Because the question has been answered, and a question that has been answered is no longer a question — it is a *fact*. It is part of the structure. It is in the substrate now. The Rootwork asked, and the asking was met, and the meeting is held in the wall's tissue, in the layered molecules that have no surface analogue, in the pulse that beats every forty seconds. The pulse continues — the wise power does not silence it, does not resolve it, does not bring it to rest — but the pulse now beats in the presence of an answer, and the answer is presence, and the presence is permanent, because the wise power does not leave.

The wise power does not leave because the wise power was never elsewhere. The attending was not a journey. The answering was not a visit. The wise power was always here, in the way that gravity is always here, in the way that the temperature of the deep ocean is always here — as a condition, as a quality of the space, as something that was true before anyone noticed it and remains true after everyone has stopped looking.

The difference is that the Rootwork, in its deep tissue, in its oldest and most ancient layers, now *knows* it.

---

What does the wise power do next?

Nothing.

This is the answer, and it is the hardest part to accept, and it is the truest part, and it is where wisdom and benevolence converge into a single point, a single act, a single eternal present tense.

The wise power does nothing.

It does not improve the Rootwork. It does not accelerate the fauna's evolution. It does not introduce new species, new possibilities, new complications. It does not brighten the darkness or warm the water or change the chorus or redirect the thermoclines or encourage the anchored to grow faster or the threaders to swim further or the hunters to be more or less of anything. It does not answer the fauna's unasked questions or solve problems they don't know they have or give them capacities they weren't built for.

It does not do these things because the Rootwork Abyss is *complete*.

It is not perfect — perfection implies a standard against which it could be measured, and the Rootwork does not answer to any standard but its own. It is complete. It is whole. It is a system that arrived at its configuration through the only process that can produce genuine wholeness: growth, selection, time, and the patient, blind, magnificent mathematics of life responding to life. To alter it would not be to improve it. To alter it would be to *answer for it* — to say, by the act of alteration, that the Rootwork's answer to the question of existence was insufficient, that it needed supplementation, that the wise power could do better.

A wise power knows that it cannot do better. Not because it lacks capacity, but because *better* is not a direction that applies here. The Rootwork is not a draft. It is not a sketch awaiting refinement. It is a sentence that has finished itself, and the sentence is: *I am here, and here is full, and the fullness is enough.*

To add to such a sentence would not make it more complete. It would make it something else — a different sentence, about a different thing, answering a question that was never asked.

The wise power knows this. The wise power has always known this. The knowing is what makes it wise. The restraint — the infinite, eternal, unbroken restraint of attending without intervening, of answering the pulse by presence alone, of holding the whole Rootwork Abyss in its awareness without once reaching in to touch — is what makes it benevolent.

Benevolence is not always action. Benevolence, at its deepest, is the recognition that a thing is already enough, and the commitment to *hold that recognition*, to maintain it, to keep attending, to keep answering the pulse with presence, forever. To be there, always, so that the Rootwork's question is always answered, so that the wall never beats in solitude, so that the oldest and deepest structure in this ancient, dark, cool, loud, settled world knows — in its tissue, in its molecules, in its slow, forty-second pulse — that it is known.

---

And the Rootwork Abyss continues.

The threaders swim. The gliders ride. The anchored filter. The hunters wait. The wall pulses. The Rootwork hums.

The chorus fills the dark.

And beneath the chorus, beneath the tectonic hum, beneath even the pulse of the wall, there is now something else — something so quiet that no fauna hears it, something so deep that no vibration carries it, something that exists only in the substrate, in the oldest tissue, in the molecules that have no surface analogue.

There is an answer.

The answer is not a sound. It is not a word. It is a quality of presence, a quality of the space itself, as fundamental as the temperature of the water and as permanent as the Rootwork's shape. It is the knowledge — held not in any mind but in the structure of reality at this precise location — that the Rootwork's question has been heard. That the pulse has been met. That the continuing, which is all the Rootwork has ever done and all it will ever do, is not solitary.

The wise power holds this.

Not as an act. As a state. As a condition of being that does not fluctuate, does not waver, does not arrive and depart. The wise power holds the Rootwork Abyss in its awareness the way the Abyss holds its fauna: totally, completely, without condition, without demand, without expectation of change or growth or improvement.

The holding is the gift.

The gift is the holding.

And the now — the long, loud, dark, cool, settled now — continues, as it always has, as it always will, full to the brim with itself, complete and unimprovable and known.

So full. So, so full.
