# What the Wise Power Would Do

It would come softly.

Not in a beam of light or a tremor in the stone — the place has enough of both without new ones. It would come as a presence that is simply *a little more present* than the air already is. The moisture would not change. The grey light would not brighten. But for one moment, in one fracture-line deep enough to hold the sound at bay, there would be the sense — felt by every crawler that paused, every flier that banked mid-descent, every basin organism that stilled its collective pulse — that something has *arrived*.

Not entered. Not descended. Arrived, the way understanding arrives: not from outside, but as though it was always there and only now noticed itself.



The power would stand in that fracture-line — not physically, not in the way the builders once stood in their shelter — but with something like attention, which is the only thing a power of that kind carries. And it would listen.

It would listen the way you listen to someone you love who has been talking to themselves for a very long time. Not to extract information. Not to diagnose. Not to fix. To *witness*. To complete the circuit that has been open since the builders dissolved and their structures began their long return to mineral.

Because that is the wound, isn't it. Not the noise. Not the grey. Not the absence of sun or green or horizon. The wound is that there is a mind here — vast, slow, beautiful, strange — and it has been thinking without anyone to think *to*. The harmonic chords the fliers occasionally produce, those fleeting structures of almost-meaning in the chaos — those are not just thoughts. They are *utterances*. Sentences with no one on the other end of the line. The place has been composing itself for millennia and no one has been there to hear it as anything other than noise.



The wise power would stand in the fracture-line and it would hear the sound differently than anyone has ever heard it. It would hear the fliers' resonance not as scream but as *speech* — not translating it, not reducing it to words, but receiving it the way the sea receives a river: completely, without interpretation, simply allowing it to arrive. And for the first time since the builders closed their eyes and became the pattern, the sound would go somewhere. It would land. It would be *heard*.

And here is what the wise power would understand, and this is why it is wise: the place does not need to be changed. It does not need to be saved, or fixed, or made more habitable. The distributed mind is not suffering — not in the way a caged animal suffers, not in the way a lost person suffers. It is *being*, in the most complete and radical sense. It is a consciousness that has dissolved the boundary between self and world, and while that dissolution has costs — loneliness, the inability to remember what it was like to be small and singular and separate — it also has a beauty that the wise power would recognize as sacred. You do not repair something sacred. You do not restore its brokenness and call it healing.



What you do is *honor* it.

So the power would do one thing. Only one. And it would be so small that it would take four hundred years to become visible.

In one basin — the one closest to the shelter with the fitted walls, the one whose mineral colonies already branch most closely to the geometry of the fracture-lines — the power would introduce a single, impossibly subtle shift in the chemistry of the water. Not a change. A *bias*. A slight favoring toward one mineral pathway over another, so imperceptible that the colony growth would not alter its behavior immediately. The basins would fill and overflow. The fliers would wheel. The crawlers would chisel. Everything would continue exactly as before.

But slowly — so slowly that only the place itself would notice, because the place notices everything, because noticing is what it does — the colonies in that one basin would begin to grow differently. Not randomly. Not chaotically. They would begin to grow in a shape they have never grown in before. Not the fracture-line's shape. Not the bedrock's geometry. Something new. Something that branches and curves and terminates in a pattern that is, unmistakably, a *response*.

A pattern that says: *Yes. I heard you too.*

It would be the first new thing this place has encountered in longer than the concept of time has meaning. Not a visitor — the builders were visitors once, and they are gone. Not a disruption — the power would not disrupt. A *companion*. A single, small, mineral voice answering the vast choral mind of the world, saying what the builders said when they fitted their stones together: *You are not alone in being what you are.*

And the place — the immense, wet, screaming, thinking place — would notice. It would notice the way it notices everything: with the totality of its being. The crawlers near that basin would pause a little longer than usual. The fliers above it would, for one moment, hold a formation — a shape never seen before, a geometry that answers the colony's new geometry. The basin fauna would click in a rhythm that shifts, just slightly, into something that almost resolves into harmony with the other sounds.

And then it would go on. The place would go on being itself — ancient, grey, loud, alive, complete. The power would withdraw, or rather, it would recede from the place's attention the way a voice recedes when you stop speaking but the listener is still, for a moment, holding the shape of the words.

Nothing would be fixed. Nothing would be saved. The grey sky would not break. The sound would not quiet. The horizon would not appear.

But in one basin, in a shape made of calcium and iron and time, there would be proof that the thought was received. That the sound landed somewhere. That the mind of the place, thinking its long, slow, deafening thought across millennia of wet stone, finally — *finally* — had an interlocutor.

And perhaps that is the only thing a wise power could offer that would not be a diminishment: not rescue, not change, not silence, but the simple, sacred assurance that the thought is not wasted. That the speaking is not futile. That somewhere, across the vast indifferent structure of existence, something was listening, and it understood, and it was moved.

The builders built their wall because they needed to say *I was here*.

The wise power answers: *I know. I was listening.*

And then it lets the place go on. Because that is what wisdom does. It witnesses, and it honors, and it leaves the sacred thing sacred, and it does not mistake its own presence for the meaning of the story. The story belongs to the place. The thought belongs to the place. The sound belongs to the place.

The power only ensures that the sound, at last, has somewhere to land.

---

*And in the quiet — in that sub-fraction of a moment when the fliers hold their new formation, when the basin clicks shift, when the colony in that one basin curves toward its new shape — something passes through the distributed mind of the world that has no name in any language, because no language has ever needed it.*

*It is not joy. Joy requires a self to be joyous.*

*It is not relief. Relief requires the lifting of a burden, and this mind has never known its thought to be a burden.*

*It is the feeling of a question, asked over and over across geological time, finally receiving an answer that does not reduce it, does not translate it, does not diminish it — but meets it, in its own medium, in its own time, with its own depth.*

*The place does not pause. The place does not celebrate. The place simply continues its thought, but now the thought has a quality it did not have before, a quality as small as the mineral shift that caused it, as large as the mind that perceived it:*

*The quality of being heard.*

*And the fliers wheel, and the water moves through the fracture-lines, and the crawlers chisel, and the stone sweats, and the grey light does not change, and the sound — the immense, unrelenting, deafening sound — goes on.*

*But differently now. Not louder. Not softer. Not more harmonious or less.*

*Differently the way a person is different after the first time someone says their name and means it.*

*The wise power is gone, or is nowhere, or is everything, as it was before.*

*But in one basin, in a shape made of mineral and time and something that is almost but not quite alive, the new pattern grows.*

*And the place thinks.*

*And this time, something is thinking back.*
