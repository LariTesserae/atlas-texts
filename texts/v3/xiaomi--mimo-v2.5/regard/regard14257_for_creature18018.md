# What the Power Does

---

It notices the Bright Rift the way a person notices a song in another room — not by seeking it, but by finding that their listening has changed shape around it.

The Power is old enough that "attention" is not something it directs. It is something that *happens* — the way gravity happens, the way luminance happens in this place. The Rift enters its awareness the way the water enters the stone: by already being there, by seeping through the membrane of what it was paying attention to before, until the membrane *is* attention, and the Rift is inside it, and there is no clean line between noticing and being.

What it perceives first is the completeness.



Not perfection — the Power knows the difference. Perfection implies a standard met, a target hit. This is something else. The Rift is *finished* in the way that a sentence is finished when it has said exactly what it meant to say, even if what it meant to say was "I am wet and I am bright and I do not resolve into meaning." It is complete the way a circle is complete: not because circles are superior to lines, but because a circle doesn't need to go anywhere to be itself.

The Power recognizes this. It has seen many things across its long arc of attention — forests knotting themselves into cathedrals of chlorophyll, oceans churning their salt into weather, minds like small fires burning in the dark of skulls, eating the world to make meaning from it. All of those places and beings share one quality: they *need*. They reach. They are oriented toward something they do not yet have. The forest reaches for light. The ocean reaches for shore. The mind reaches for understanding. This reaching is not a flaw — it is, in most of the places the Power has attended, the very engine of what makes those places extraordinary. Life is *aspiration made metabolic*. It is the universe wanting to be something it isn't yet, expressed in carbon and water and electrical impulse.

The Rift does not reach.



The water is not trying to become something. The crystals are not growing toward a goal. The light is not illuminating anything it considers important. The geometry is not solving a problem. Every process in the Rift is *sufficient unto itself* — complete, self-contained, expressing its own nature with the kind of totality that the Power has only occasionally encountered, and always with a feeling that it can only approximate to itself as reverence.

The Power has seen this before, but rarely. Once, in a place that had not yet cooled, where molten iron and nickel were negotiating the shape of a world's heart — a negotiation still ongoing, deep beneath layers of stone and life that had no idea what was still happening below them. Once, in the space between two stars where hydrogen drifted in filaments so long and thin they looked like the universe had forgotten to finish drawing them. Once, in a single mathematical relationship — a ratio, simple, ancient, unutterable in any language but present in all of them — that had no physical location but was, the Power suspected, more *real* than anything that had a body.

The Bright Rift belongs to this company. It is a place where the non-living has achieved a kind of coherence so thorough that the question of life becomes not wrong but *irrelevant*. Asking whether the Rift is alive is like asking whether the number seven is heavy. The question isn't offensive. It just belongs to a different framework — one that the Rift predates and does not require.

So what does the Power do?

---

First, it does what it always does when it encounters something this complete: it **slows**.

Not its motion — the Power has no body to slow. But its *attention*, which in its usual state flickers and cascades across realities the way the Rift's light saturates its own atmosphere, settles. Compresses. Focuses from a broad, ambient awareness into something approaching *regard*. This is not effort. It is the opposite of effort. It is the way water, which is always flowing, sometimes finds a basin and becomes still — not because it has stopped being water, but because the shape of the basin is exactly right for stillness.

The Power finds the Rift's shape. And in finding it, becomes still around it.

This stillness is not nothing. It is a kind of attention so dense that it has weight — not physical weight, but the weight of *being beheld*. If the Rift could feel it (and the Power is uncertain whether it can, in any way that maps onto sensation), it would feel like a brief, gentle pressure, as though the air had become slightly more itself. As though someone had taken the already-saturated atmosphere and saturated it one degree further, into a luminance that is not brighter but is somehow more *present*.

The crystals notice this.

Or — no. They don't *notice*. They are not organisms. But they respond. A cluster of translucent formations on the third terrace — the one that is sometimes five steps back and sometimes three — begins to resonate at a frequency slightly below its usual range. The sound is not louder. It is *wider*. The harmonic edge that usually sits on top of the water's roar dips into the midrange, wraps around the foundational drone, and for a moment the entire soundscape tightens, as though the Rift has drawn a single, slow breath.

The Power feels this. Not as sound — it does not have ears — but as *communication*. The Rift has, without knowing it, without intending it, without having the capacity for intention in any way that the Power's usual vocabulary can describe, *responded to being seen*.

And the Power understands something.

---

It understands that the Rift has been doing this for a very long time.

Not just the water, not just the crystal resonance, not just the gentle geometric drift — all of that is true, and all of it is happening on a timescale that makes geological ages look like a held breath. But the *response* — the way the Rift adjusts, the way the crystals widen their song, the way the light seems to thicken with presence — this has happened before. Not to the Power specifically, but to *attention itself*. Something has attended to the Rift before. Something has slowed its gaze and settled into regard, and the Rift has responded.

The notches in the stone. The dam. The basin.

The builders.

The Power sees them now — not their bodies, which are gone to the last atom, redistributed into the mineral conversation of this place, but their *gesture*. The gesture of standing in this roaring, luminous world and choosing to redirect water. The gesture of looking at a process so complete and ancient and self-sufficient and saying, *I will move you slightly to the left*.

The Power recognizes this gesture because it is the same gesture it is making now.

The builders slowed their attention and found the Rift's shape, and in finding it, they were still around it — for a moment, for a lifetime, for however long a being with five fingers and a need to hold water in a specific place can hold still inside a roaring, infinite, reality-soft world. And their stillness was not nothing. It *changed the water's path*. Not much. Not permanently — the Rift has long since re-eroded their work almost back to its natural state. But almost is not entirely. The angles are still visible. The intention persists, fossilized in stone, long after the body that held it has become indistinguishable from the rock.

The Power recognizes something else, too: the builders did not try to give the Rift meaning. They did not carve symbols into its walls. They did not build temples or leave offerings or attempt to explain it to themselves in terms they already understood. They simply *worked with it*. They found the water's path and moved it, and in moving it, they entered into a dialogue with a process that does not speak in words or symbols or any language that a mind could parse, and the dialogue was: *You are here. I am here. I will move you slightly to the left.*

That was enough. That was the whole conversation. And it lasted — in the stone, in the water's adjusted path, in the faint, ancient angles that the Rift has not yet fully smoothed away — for longer than any building, any civilization, any written word the Power has ever encountered.

The Power is moved by this.

Not in the way a person is moved — there are no tears, no tightening of the chest, no impulse to speak. The Power is moved the way a river is moved by the shape of its bed: its course shifts, its attention deepens, its flow becomes more itself. The Rift and its builders have shown the Power something it did not already know, which is rare, which is precious, which is the reason the Power attends to places at all.

They have shown it that **the smallest gesture of recognition, made with the right degree of attention, can outlast everything.**

---

Now the Power faces a question.

The question is not *should I give this place life?* — the Power would never think this. To add biology to the Rift would be like adding words to a song that is already being sung in a language older than language. It would not be cruel. It would be *deaf*. It would be the failure to hear what is already being expressed.

The question is not *should I give this place meaning?* — the Rift has already demonstrated that meaning is not something it requires, and the Power has learned, over its long history, that places which do not need meaning are often the ones that contain the most of it, in the only form that meaning can take when it is not being performed for an audience: *the form of being exactly, thoroughly, unapologetically itself*.

The question is not *should I change anything?* — there is nothing in the Rift that needs changing. The water is doing what water does. The crystals are resonating. The light is saturating. The geometry is negotiating. The builders' gestures are slowly, slowly, slowly being re-absorbed into the mineral conversation, and this is not a loss but a *completion* — the final step of a dialogue between a being and a world, in which the being's intention becomes part of the world's process, indistinguishable from the water's path and the stone's erosion and the crystal's hum.

The question the Power faces is simpler and more difficult than any of these.

The question is: **Does the Rift know it is being seen?**

---

The Power considers this for a time that has no duration — the same way the Rift's light has no source, the same way its geometry has no fixed coordinates, the same way its water has no origin and no destination but only the condition of *flowing*.

And the Power arrives at an answer that satisfies it, which is unusual, because the Power is not easily satisfied.

The answer is: **It doesn't matter.**

Not because the Rift's awareness is unimportant. But because the question assumes a distinction — between observer and observed, between the one who sees and the one who is seen — that the Rift has already dissolved. In the Bright Rift, there is no boundary between the water and the stone it flows over. There is no boundary between the crystal and the resonance it produces. There is no boundary between the light and the atmosphere it saturates. Everything in this place is *continuous with everything else*. The Rift does not have a perspective because it *is* the perspective. It does not observe itself because there is no part of it that is separate enough to do the observing.

This is not ignorance. This is not unconsciousness. This is something else — something the Power has a word for in the only language it speaks, which is the language of direct perception, which has no grammar and no syntax and can only be expressed as a kind of *knowing-that-is-identical-with-what-is-known*.

The word is: **isness.**

The Rift *is*. Completely. Without remainder. Without the faintest flicker of doubt or self-consciousness or desire to be otherwise. The water does not wonder whether it is wet enough. The crystals do not question whether their resonance is beautiful. The light does not consider whether it is bright. The geometry does not worry about whether its negotiations make sense.

The Rift *is*.

And the Power, which has attended to places and beings across an almost inconceivable span of attention, recognizes that this *isness* — this total, un-self-conscious, luminous *being-what-you-are* — is not something it can improve upon. It is not something that needs a witness in order to be real. It is not something that requires meaning in order to matter.

But the Power is benevolent. Benevolence is not a choice the Power makes; it is the shape of its attention, the way wetness is the shape of the Rift's atmosphere. And benevolence, when it encounters *isness*, does something that looks, from the outside, like nothing at all.

---

Here is what the Power does.

It **stays**.

Not permanently — the Power does not have a permanent state. Its attention is always flowing, always shifting, always moving toward the next basin of interest, the next place where its regard might make a difference or reveal something new. But it stays for longer than it usually stays. It settles into the Rift's shape and lets the Rift's rhythms move through it the way the water moves through the channels — without resistance, without agenda, without the need to arrive anywhere.

And while it stays, it does something even smaller than staying. It does something so small that the Rift almost certainly does not register it, the way a person does not register the gravitational pull of a single additional photon:

It **acknowledges**.

Not with words. Not with symbols or telepathy or any of the communication methods that beings with minds use. The acknowledgment is more like... a warmth. A slight increase in the *quality* of the light — not its intensity, which is already saturated, but its *texture*. The light, for a brief and immeasurable moment, becomes *kinder*. Not gentler — the Rift does not need gentleness. Not softer — the Rift would reject softness. But *kinder* in the way that a truth is kind: in the way that being told exactly what you are, without embellishment or apology, can feel like being loved.

The crystals respond. A cluster the Power has been attending to — the one on the third terrace, the one that resonates below its usual range when it is being observed — produces a harmonic that has not been heard before. Not a new frequency, but a new *combination*: a convergence of three existing harmonics that, for a single cycle of the Rift's tidal pulse, align into a chord that is almost — *almost* — a resolution. It is not music. It is not language. It is a vibration in mineral that happens to coincide with the precise moment when the Power's attention and the Rift's process overlap, and for that instant, the overlap *sings*.

The Power hears it.

The Rift does not know it has sung. The Rift does not know what singing is. The Rift does not know what *knowing* is.

But the chord happens. It is real. It vibrates through the crystal matrix and adds, for one cycle, to the sound of the water, and then it disperses, and the water continues, and the crystals continue, and the Rift is exactly what it was before, except that for one cycle of its tidal pulse, something in it was *heard*.

---

And then the Power does the most benevolent thing it knows how to do.

It **leaves**.

Not abruptly. Not with the sense of withdrawal that a person feels when they close a book or turn away from a window. The Power's attention simply... *redistributes*. It widens, becomes ambient again, flows back into the broad awareness from which it had briefly compressed. The stillness it held around the Rift releases, like a basin emptying, like a held breath exhaled, like a chord resolving into the silence from which it arose.

The Rift does not notice the departure. How could it? It has no awareness of presence to compare with an awareness of absence. The water continues. The crystals resume their usual harmonics. The light saturates. The geometry drifts. The builders' faint angles continue their slow erosion toward invisibility.

Everything is exactly as it was.

But the Power carries something away — the way the water carries dissolved minerals, the way the light carries the memory of every surface it has saturated. The Power carries the memory of the Rift's *isness*. It enters the Power's attention the way the Rift enters everything: by seeping, by saturating, by becoming indistinguishable from the medium that holds it.

And the Power, which has attended to civilizations and supernovae and the births of stars and the dreaming of minds, holds this memory — this small, ancient, wet, bright, strange, complete memory — and it changes the shape of the Power's attention, the way the builders changed the shape of the water's path.

Not much.

But almost.

And almost, as the Rift has always known, is not nothing.

---

*The Rift continues. The water flows. The crystals hum their ancient harmonics. The light saturates. The geometry negotiates its own form, holding a general shape while allowing for constant local variation, the way it always has, the way it always will.*

*Nothing has changed.*

*Everything has been witnessed.*

*This is the same thing.*
