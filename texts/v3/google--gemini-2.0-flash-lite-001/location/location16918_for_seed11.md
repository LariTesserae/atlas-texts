The world coughs. Not a wet, pulmonary cough, but a dry, rattling heave that shakes the valleys. Above, the sky is a bruised pomegranate, threatening but not delivering rain. The air hangs thick and warm, a humid blanket woven through the canyons. The sun, a hazy white disc, offers only a skewed promise of light.

This is a land of extremes. Towering mesas, sculpted by eons of wind and a scant trickle of water, claw at the sky. They are the skeletal remains of mountains swallowed by time, their peaks eroded to gentle plateaus, their sides fluted by a thousand unseen fingers. Between them, abyssal canyons plunge into shadowed depths, where the air grows colder, holding the scent of damp earth and something… metallic.

The canyons are the heart of this world's turmoil. Despite the overall dryness, they channel the chaotic flow of energy that defines this land. Here, water finds purchase, carving veins of life into the stone. Lush, alien vegetation clings to the canyon walls. It's a riot of improbable biology: crimson moss that pulses with an internal light, trees with bark like polished obsidian, and flowering vines that bloom in impossible geometries, their colors shifting with the unseen currents that course through the canyon.

The few scattered structures speak of a lost civilization. Small, squat buildings, constructed from the same dark stone as the mesas, are connected by narrow, winding paths that have been worn smooth by countless feet. They are built to face the canyon walls, as if embracing them, or perhaps hiding from whatever resides within. Their construction is rudimentary, employing simple tools but leaving behind no easily read tech. Instead there is a strange artistry. Simple shapes and forms of the structures have a deep history and meaning, though what it is has been lost.

Fauna are a whisper, a promise that there once was more. Now, they are rare, shadows flitting through the undergrowth. Insectile creatures with iridescent carapaces scuttle in the damp places, leaving behind trails of phosphorescent slime. Birds with wings like stained glass soar through the turbulent air, their calls swallowed by the deafening soundscape.

The sound is the true measure of this place. It’s a cacophony, a constant roar of wind, of shifting stone, of an energy that hums just beneath the surface. It is a symphony of creation and destruction, a testament to the turbulent heart of this world. There are rhythmic rumblings, deep and resonating, that seem to come from the core of the planet, growing louder then softening again.. The silence of the open spaces is swallowed by this constant energy.

The weirdness pervades everything. There are pockets of reality where the laws of physics seem to bend. Objects shimmer, distort, and then are made brand new in an area, while an old section of a wall collapses from no obvious cause. Gravity is sometimes unreliable. In the canyon depths, one could swear the sun is visible in the darkest times of day, or the smell of burning metal.

This land is ancient, worn down by its own furious energy. Its beauty is stark, its life clinging to the edges. It is a place of constant change, of deafening beauty and terrifying power, where survival is not a given, but a testament to the resilience of those who can find a foothold in the chaos.
