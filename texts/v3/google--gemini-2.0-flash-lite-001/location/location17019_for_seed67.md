The air hangs thick and warm, a nearly tangible presence, saturated with the faint tang of ozone and the metallic scent of… something. Below, the ground is a vast, undulating plane of black, obsidian glass, reflecting the faint, diffused light from above. This is a landscape of absolute construction, a testament to an unseen, unknowable maker. Everything here is built, every curve, every angle meticulously planned. There is no substrate, no natural foundation. The materials themselves are alien, seemingly inorganic, yet imbued with a subtle, internal hum that vibrates through the very air.

Despite the ‘no plant life’ parameter, the surface of the glass isn't sterile. Interspersed throughout the planes are intricate, fractal-like patterns. These aren't carvings, but rather are a delicate network of pulsing, iridescent lines. They undulate with a slow, hypnotic rhythm, not quite alive. More like the way energy might express itself in a medium free from any constraints.

Water here is not in its familiar form. Instead, it manifests primarily as a pervasive ambient humidity. In this environment of dense, constructed things, it condenses and weeps from unseen sources. Imagine a world suspended in a perpetual state of post-rain, the condensation clinging to every surface, painting the obsidian with slick, shimmering sheens.

The temperature, while warm, isn't oppressive. Instead, it enhances the feeling of a world held just below some threshold. The light, as mentioned, is normal, yet it’s filtered, softened, as if by a perpetual overcast. The sky is hidden. 

The most unsettling feature of this place is the fauna. They are *everywhere*. But they are not easily seen. The "overwhelming" presence is felt as the whisper of movement. At times, the glass underfoot seems to ripple, distorting the reflections, or briefly dimming—as if something had passed beneath. If you were to bring an instrument to this place, you might *mostly* find silence, but the very act of listening might reveal the hum of movement, the occasional tap or scrape.

The scale is vast, continental, even planetary. Despite the seeming simplicity of its features, this is a place designed to endure, with the absence of change. This place is in perfect, if terrifying, balance. There are no gradients here, no processes, no movement—except within the creatures which are the essence or the product of this place.

The ‘industrial’ presence here is palpable. It isn't a factory, but an *engine*. The pulsating lines, the hum, the very air, all speak of a vast, unseen operation. The weirdness comes from the alien geometry, the illogical angles, the impossible intersections. The entire place seems to defy known physics. Something about the scale, the density, and the unfathomable purpose of the maker suggests a reality beyond human comprehension.

This landscape is a paradox. It is young, in terms of erosion, with sharp features and defined angles, yet it's also a place of absolute stasis, a frozen equilibrium. It's a place of almost complete silence, yet filled with the unacknowledged presence of countless, unseen things. It is a place of water, of energy, and of an overwhelming, unknowable presence.
