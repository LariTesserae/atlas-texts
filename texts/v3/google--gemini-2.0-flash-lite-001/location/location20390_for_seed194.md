The air is thick, not with water, but with the dust of a thousand grinding years. Not the fine dust of a desert, but a coarser, granular material, like pulverized stone that's been aged and then re-aged. This is the **Floor**, and it covers everything. It’s what things are built *of*, what they're *in*, the very stuff of the place.

You are within the **Root-City**.

The Root-City is contained, a pocket of permanence. It is a structure of, within, and of-the-Floor. Erosion is impossible here; the city *is* erosion, the final state of things. The very act of perceiving it, of existing within it, *is* the process of wearing down the stone.

No light penetrates, so the world is a pressure-cooker of silence, until you speak.

The “sky” is a ceiling, an endless plane of deep indigo. This is the ceiling of the **Root-Cathedral**, the only extant structure in the city. The roof undulates in slow curves, the only relief in the monolithic architecture.

The only "vegetation" in the Root-City is the **Stone Bloom**. There are no plants, no flesh, no fauna. The Bloom is the only living thing. The Bloom appears in the form of stone "petals" that unfurl with glacial slowness from the floor. The "petals" are the color of dried blood. They feel cool to the touch.

The lack of ambient light fills the city with a terrible, pressing silence. Until you speak.

The Root-City is empty. The slow crawl of the Stone Blooms is barely perceptible. The only sound comes from the **Chambers**. Located in the deepest recesses of the Root-Cathedral, they are constructed of interlocking stone-petals, forming spheres of a size only the human mind could conceive. Entering a chamber is to enter its "memory", and find yourself in the memory of another.

The technology of the Root-City is pre-metal, based on simple, interlocking, and often immense tools made from the same stone of which everything is constructed. This tech is used by the **Root-Carvers**, who move within the Root-Cathedral. They are entities that seem to *be* the Root-Cathedral, appearing as part of the architecture, built-of-the-building. They are the only true inhabitants.

The "world" of the Root-City works by a single rule: the more you observe, the more the Stone Bloom will absorb you — the same way that the Root-City is absorbing everything.
