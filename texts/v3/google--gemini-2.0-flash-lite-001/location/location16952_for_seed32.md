Where am I?

I am within the **Caverns of Calcified Echoes.** Here, the very air hums with the unending, **loud** chorus of the place itself. It is a world **hidden** away, a place of impossible construction, utterly **built** and utterly **dense**. The **bright** light pours from every surface, yet there is no sun, no source I can identify.

This is a **cool** place, the temperature a constant, never shifting. And there is **no water**; not a drop. Yet, this is not a desert. This is a place of mineral, of strange formations. The walls, ceilings, and floors of the caverns are composed of a substance like polished, grey obsidian, but shot through with veins of some glittering, crystal-like material. They are smooth. Polished. And everywhere.

What is it made of? The substrate from which all is made seems to be a compressed salt, but it is not a substance of the earth. Its molecules seem more tightly knit; a hyper-dense latticework. It feels *alive*.

There is **sparse** vegetation, yes, but not in the way one would imagine. Rather, across the cavern surfaces are intricate patterns of shimmering, crystal plants. These are immobile, but their surfaces shift in the light, as if a breeze were ever so slightly stirring them. The crystal plants are not like anything organic, and yet they are undeniably alive. They give off no scent. Touching them offers no texture, just a cool, smooth solidity.

There is *nothing* of the animal here. Only the crystals, and the echoes.

The **sound** is constant, a booming resonance that vibrates through bones and sinew. It shifts and changes, yet at the same time, it remains the same. Every surface is singing a particular note. The combination of all these notes, the infinite layering of all the echoes, is the place's song. It never breaks. It never ceases. There is **no change**, no gradient. There is only the song, the cold, the light.

This is a world that has been here for time immemorial, an environment in permanent **equilibrium**. It is weathered and worn and **established**, but not in any way one would expect. The mineral has been shaped, perhaps by the echoes themselves, but is somehow **purely artificial**. This place is nothing but form.

And in this world, there is nothing *but* form. All of it is construction, built out of the earth's refusal to offer any sort of natural substrate. It is all architecture. No **tech**, only the raw forms, the resonant structures, and their song.

And what of the **weirdness**? It is in the impossible order of things, in the smooth infinity of the obsidian, in the crystal plants. It is in the complete lack of any origin, in the ever-present song, in the way the air feels as close and complete as a solid wall. It is in the feeling that one is not merely in the world, but *of* it.

I can't imagine how this place came to be. But now that I am here, it seems I always have been.
