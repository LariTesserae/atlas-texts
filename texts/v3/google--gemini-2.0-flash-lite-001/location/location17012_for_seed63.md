The Obsidian Hive. A vast, echoing vault carved into the heart of a mountain. Not a mountain of rock, but of a substance like polished, porous obsidian, shot through with veins of coruscating gold. The air is thick, hot, and stings the nostrils with a mineral tang, like the breath of a smelter’s forge. Light is a forgotten luxury. Here, darkness reigns, absolute and smothering, yet not empty. A thousand pinpricks of crimson light pulse erratically, marking the trails of the Hive's only denizens: the Kryll.

The Kryll are the fauna here, the overwhelming denizens of this world. They are not insects or anything approaching familiar taxonomy. Imagine instead, living crystals. Gem-like, faceted bodies, ranging in size from a sparrow to a small dog, drift through the vast cavities and tunnels of the Obsidian Hive. They generate the pinpricks of light. They don't seem to have eyes or mouths, but they are driven. 

No water exists here. The Kryll, however, are drawn to the golden veins that lace the obsidian. They swarm the veins, their facets catching and refracting the dim, internal light, creating shimmering, chaotic displays of color.

There is no vegetation, no plants, no familiar life. Yet there *is* a strange, almost fungal presence. A network, a kind of biological latticework, clings to the walls of the Hive, a dull, oily sheen. This network is not alive in any understood sense. Instead, it seems to "capture," and store, some form of energy from the deep heat of the world. It provides a means of generating the obsidian, though the precise mechanism is a mystery.

The temperature is consistently high, almost unbearable to any creature outside of the Kryll. The elevation is high, and the Hive is deep within the mountains. The Obsidian Hive, as a whole, is vast. The interior's features are numerous, but the scale of any one feature is small – a tunnel, a chamber, or a complex of rooms.

The Hive is in perfect stasis. Not a single thing moves.

The built environment is scattered and fragmentary. The Kryll create no structures, and there is no evidence of any previous inhabitants. Occasionally, a single shard of something, perfectly smooth and angular – a fragment, perhaps, of a larger, forgotten structure – can be found embedded in the obsidian. Sometimes, these shards contain traces of the golden veins, as if whatever made them was drawing on the same source as the Kryll and the dark energy network.

There is zero technology here. No tools, no devices, just the raw power of the Hive and the tireless dance of the Kryll.

The darkness is all-encompassing, a void only broken by the crimson lights of the Kryll. These lights are the only source of light. Their patterns shift, forming constellations, lines, and at times, something that resembles crude symbols.

The Kryll do not make noise, at least not that can be heard. Their movements are silent; only the rhythmic pulse of their internal light betrays their presence. The darkness is mostly quiet, but sometimes a faint, high-pitched whine can be heard deep within the tunnels. There always is a feeling of impending change. The Kryll seem like they are a part of a larger, dynamic ecosystem, with the Obsidian Hive in constant motion. And if one is caught in it, they will be caught forever.
