The air smells of ozone and something metallic, like blood that hasn’t yet dried. Above, there is no sky. Or perhaps it would be more accurate to say that the sky is everywhere, pressing in from all sides. It is a shifting, inky black, shot through with veins of electric blue, and riven by the incessant crackle and pop of raw energy. Light, in this place, is a dangerous commodity, a corrosive poison that burns as it illuminates. Patches of sickly, phosphorescent fungi cling to the rock faces that make up the walls of the world, offering the only weak, cold glow.

This is a place of *making*. It is a wound in the earth, a scar carved by the unrelenting churning of some unseen, unknowable process. The landscape is dominated by impossibly tall, jagged peaks of obsidian, obsidian so fresh that it gleams like fresh ink. Between these towers, valleys of slick, black mud twist and gurgle. A network of rivulets, some flowing, some merely dampening the stone, trace the courses of the valleys. These are not rivers of water, but something else, something vital. The air hangs heavy with their scent.

The sound is *intense*; a cacophony. The hiss of steam. The grinding of gears. The whine of unseen engines driving… well, driving something. It is a symphony of Industry, a churning, ceaseless rhythm of work. You get the sense that something is being *made*, constantly, endlessly, within the belly of this wound. But what is made here? Is it stone and mud? Is it *more*?

The extreme elevation is evident in the scale. You are at the bottom of something, and its walls stretch upwards into the darkness. But the scale is also small. A single, contained space. A single, echoing foundry. The density makes it claustrophobic, the dark hides many things, but you are not alone. And the dynamic is terrifying.

The cool temperature fights the latent heat that bleeds from the machinery’s innards, from the mud, from the very stone itself. Young mountains, young rivers… This is a place of *creation*, but it is a violent one. The landscape is constantly changing, shifting, and reshaping itself. New peaks claw their way from the ground, old ones crumble into the morass, and the valleys deepen and twist. The lack of animal life is obvious; there is no flesh or blood here, only stone, mud, and the all-consuming processes that seek to remake them both. No mercy.

As you stand, the ground trembles. From the peaks above, droplets of condensation form, and then, as they lose their hold, they plummet into the canyons below in a shower of icy death, each drop a tiny, killing star. The only moisture is that from the dark, which collects in thin, oily sheets on the rocks and the metallic detritus. This isn't a place of gentle rainfall, there is only intermittent, deathly condensation.

The lack of vegetation is what is most odd. There are no plants here, save for the sickly fungi, and yet, the lushness of it is a sensation, a feeling of fertile creation, a womb of stone. It’s hard to breathe with the metallic tang in the air. This place is not a ruin; this place *is*. It is a thing in the act of becoming. And within it, the silence has just begun to speak.
