The ground is not of earth, nor of any familiar stone or mineral. It is a slow, endless churning. A dust, but a dust of pure, dark energy. Swirling, coalescing, and then dissolving again in an endless dance. This process is the only sound, a subtle susurrus, a hiss and whisper like an immense, unseen pressure. The air itself seems made of this dust, a dim, inky soup that obscures visibility beyond a few dozen meters.

What little *is* visible shifts with agonizing slowness. Immense crystalline structures, like fractured teeth of a forgotten god, rise and fall. These formations are not fixed. They undulate, stretching impossibly, growing from the dark energy-dust, only to crumble back into it again. Occasionally, faint glimmers of light, like dying embers, appear within the crystals. They are not light as understood by any terrestrial technology, but something closer to solidified intention, holding a pale memory of structure.

There is no sun, no moon, no stars. The only "light" comes from within this roiling, dark sea. It is a cold, internal illumination that does not alleviate the shadows, but deepens them. The temperature is not as it seems. It feels warm where the light glimmers, and even warmer near the towering, shifting crystalline structures, but a deep, numbing cold pervades the spaces between them.

Here and there, scattered across the churning landscape, are the faintest hints of structure. Worn, broken lines that only exist in the abstract. They are built of… something. Almost like vast, half-formed, structures, too massive to comprehend, yet they seem familiar. They are hints of paths, of shelters, or vast temples, but with nothing for scale, it’s impossible to draw a real conclusion about the nature or size of these structures. They suggest a purpose or intention, as if something, somewhere, was attempting to build and inhabit this impossible place.

There is no plant life. No fauna. And the dust that makes up the ground, also appears to be the only available sustenance. But the place is not entirely dead; it is a chaotic, vibrant form of un-life. The air is thick with a strange energy, an entropy-soup of all possible shapes, all possible formations. It is raw creation, constantly attempting to lock into place or manifest only to be immediately subsumed back into the dark dust.

The place is *alive*, if “alive” is the right word. A nascent consciousness feels as if it were struggling to be born. It is an impossible place, a void trying to become a world. A world that might, one day, hold life, but not yet. Not here. Maybe not ever. Its very essence suggests a form of non-being, a potentiality that has yet to resolve itself.

The 'rules' here are the unfettered laws of physics without any governing hand. Gravity warps and shifts, the dust flowing like a liquid with every unseen motion in this realm. Time itself seems uneven, both stretched thin and compressed, like a cosmic bellows. The scale is impossible to measure. The shifting crystals could be mountains, or single grains of sand; the structures could be entire cities, or simply the traces of some forgotten dream.
