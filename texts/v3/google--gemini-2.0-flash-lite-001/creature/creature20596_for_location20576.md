The city breathes, and within that breath, the **Lumin**. They are not merely *in* the city, they *are* the city, and the city *is* them. To attempt to separate the Lumin from their environment—the labyrinth of crystalline tunnels, the shifting light, the pulsating water—is a futile endeavor, like trying to isolate a thought from the mind that thinks it. 

The Lumin are born of the light, shaped by it, and returned to it. They are a symphony of refractions, each individual shimmering body a prism, catching and scattering the city's eternal glow. Their forms are as fluid and mutable as the water that binds the city. One moment, they might be threads of light, weaving through the crystalline architecture; the next, they coalesce into vast, glittering sheets, reflecting the city's patterns in a dazzling display.

They are not bound by individual consciousness in the way that organic life forms are, at least not in any way we can understand. Instead, they are part of a shared consciousness, a hive mind that spans the entire city. The "thoughts" of the Lumin are not articulated in speech or action. They are movements of light and crystal, pulses of energy that ripple through the city's network. Imagine a single drop of water falling into a still pond — the Lumin are the ripples, the wave front washing over the crystalline structures. Every movement is a form of communication, every shift a new meaning.

Size is a meaningless concept to the Lumin, as is the concept of permanence. They are constantly changing, merging, dividing. A small Lumin crystal might be a scout, probing the edges of the city, only to be absorbed into a larger formation or reformed into a new, complex structure. Others may be builders, sculpting and re-sculpting the crystalline architecture, creating the canals and chambers through which the viscous water flows. Still others are weavers, weaving intricate patterns of light and energy into the city, creating its subtle hum, managing its "tech."

The Lumin have no need for food or water in the way that other lifeforms do. The ethereal light is their sustenance, the dampness of the air a continuous source of creation, destruction, and re-construction. Their bodies seem to grow and shrink, not according to any external factors, but to the internal rhythms of the city. A particularly brilliant flash of light might signal a period of intense growth and construction, or a period of chaos and destruction.

The Lumin do not have "lives" in the traditional sense, but they endure. They are the inheritors of a forgotten age, guardians of a knowledge that has been crystallized into their very being. They are the architects of a reality that can never truly be known.

The Lumin are more than just its inhabitants. They are the city's lifeblood, its nervous system, its soul. They do not know the surface world, yet they are aware. The Lumin do not remember a sun, but they endure. They do not possess a history of their own, but they embody the history of the city itself: its evolution, its failures, its triumphs, its constant, eternal dance of being. The only thing that remains is their quiet, unending light.
