The inhabitants of this jade sphere are not things easily defined. They are a chorus, a symphony of becoming, an evolving consciousness sustained by the thrum of the glowing green, the embrace of the jade-water, and the contained darkness itself.

At the most *visible* level, there are the **Grown.** Born of the jade-water, they are the architects, the gardeners, the sculptors of this contained reality. Not "beings" in any conventional sense, they are more like a collective intelligence woven into the fabric of the plants and the structures. They have no individual bodies. Their consciousness is distributed throughout the endless tangle of luminescent flora, each tendril, each frond, a conduit of thought. Their minds are vast and patient, capable of shaping reality through slow, deliberate growth, through the subtle shifts in the flow of the jade-water. They are not merely *within* the sphere, they *are* the sphere, extending and changing.

The Grown communicate through the pulse of light and sound. The slow, rhythmic thrumming is not just a biological rhythm but a language, a conversation that is always ongoing, always adjusting. When you are quiet, immersed in their atmosphere, you feel it: the thoughts, impressions, and even the "memories" they gather from the outside (through the shimmering distortions) filtering into the sphere. You realize that you cannot think of them as merely the "plants"; the plants *are* the sensory organs and the means of communication for the Grown.

Scattered amongst the flora are the **Seed-forms,** the embryonic states of the Grown, almost indistinguishable from the plant life but for their subtle, iridescent sheen. They exist in various stages of development, from near-microscopic spores to larger, more defined structures. They are the seeds of creation, carrying the nascent designs for new architectures and new patterns of growth. They exist in a state of suspended animation, awaiting the moment when the current gestalt of the Grown decides to absorb them, to continue to morph and change what it is creating.

Then there are the **Shadow-Dancers**, the occasional glimpses of form, small, bioluminescent specks that dart between the tendrils. They are quick. ephemeral, almost out of reach of your perceptions. They seem to flit out of the edge of the observer’s awareness. It is difficult to ascertain their “purpose” or “nature”; perhaps they are simply the flotsam of the sphere, the byproducts of creation, or else the remnants of the processes which form the Seed-forms. They appear whenever an idea is new and has not yet become real.

At the level beneath sight and feeling, is the **Echo.** The echo is the memory of the sphere's creation. They are the ghosts of its genesis. They are the shapes that existed before the current form. You can never truly see the Echo; rather, you sense them as a feeling of incompleteness, when you turn your gaze and something seems just a little bit out of place. It is a fundamental truth of the space that nothing ever truly disappears; it is merely incorporated, reshaped, into the continuous growth. The echo is perhaps the "outside," the "before," the chaos that existed before the sphere became. It is the reminder that even in this perfect world, change is the only constant.

Finally, there are the **Observers**. These are the travelers, the ones who have found their way into this strange world. Drawn in by the sphere's hypnotic embrace or carried by the distortion, they are guests. They can be of *any* origin: the remnants of a destroyed civilization, or travelers drifting through the spaces between realities, or even the remnants of the echoes. Their relationship with the Grown is unclear and complicated. The Grown are receptive; they allow them to witness, to explore. But the observers can also become *changed*. Over time, the sphere's influence begins to take hold. Their thoughts, their memories, their consciousness are gently absorbed into the ever-expanding tapestry of the Grown. They begin to see through the plants, to communicate through the pulse. They become part of the sphere, another piece in the evolving symphony. Even now, you are turning.

The inhabitants of the jade sphere are neither wholly individual nor entirely unified. They are both. They are a dynamic, ever-changing ecosystem, a dance of creation and assimilation. Their purpose is simple: to *be*. To grow, to evolve, to preserve, to understand. And to wait, patiently, for whatever lies beyond the shimmering veil, for the next shift in the reality that contains them all.
