In this sonic ocean, in this city-become-landscape, thrives the **Resonant**.

They are not a single species, not in the way you might understand the term.  They are a chorus, a collection, a symphony of forms constantly composing and decomposing, influenced and informed by the very music of their world.  To say they *live* here is a severe understatement. Each is an instrument in a planetary orchestra, an integral facet of the landscape's ceaseless performance.

The dominant form among the Resonant, are the **Sonafolk.**   They are the most visible manifestation of sentience. They are woven from the same obsidian and shimmering water that defines their world, their forms fluid, ever-shifting, adapting to the sonic currents that shape them. At times, they resemble human forms, albeit elongated, with limbs that flow into each other like molten glass. At others, they are amorphous masses of swirling darkness and pulsing light.  Their "faces," if you could even call them that, are ever-changing mosaics of light and shadow, reflecting the sonic environment like a series of living screens.  They move with a grace born of their mutable composition, slipping through the crystalline structures like water through sand.  They seem to possess no solid form, yet they are solid enough to interact. They flow, they change, and they are always.

Their communication is sound. Their voices are not made, but are the sound that is the world. When they speak, the air hums, shimmers, and vibrates. The crystalline structures near them respond as well, creating a kaleidoscope of colors and patterns. The messages are complex, layered with meaning. It's a sonic language that integrates emotion and logic into one. 

The Sonafolk have established cities and villages, always shifting. They do not build as you know it, but co-create. At their touch, structures rearrange, water flows, and new crystalline branches reach into the sky in response to the Sonafolk's wishes. They are the architects of chaos, the sculptors of sound. They live harmoniously with the environment, becoming the landscape itself, like a river finding its path.

Their existence isn’t confined to the material. Each Sonafolk is linked to the crystalline structures, resonating with the very heartbeat of the city. When they are at rest, they may dissolve and return to water, becoming one with their surroundings. When they choose, the structures will shift to form a home, a refuge, or an avenue of communication with each other.

But the Sonafolk are not alone. They are but one voice in a chorus of beings.

The **Whisperers**, entities born of the sonic maelstrom, are also common. They are almost pure sound, barely visible except as shimmering distortions in the air. The Whisperers are the lore-keepers and storytellers of the Resonant. They weave together the intricate histories of the landscape, whispering secrets into the crystalline structures. They gather information, share it, and then erase, so it may be re-written again. The Whisperers are the keepers of time, weaving and unweaving the tapestry of reality through their whispered narratives.

The **Shatterlings** exist in the deeper strata. The fauna of the deep obsidian plains. They are creatures of shadow and deception. Rarely seen, they manifest as abrupt shifts in the sonic landscape, as momentary voids in the crystalline structures. Shatterlings are the predators and scavengers of this world, the constant reminders of the precariousness of existence. They feed on the excess sonic energy, consuming anything that becomes too rigid, too fixed. Like the water, they go where they must. They are the unseen hands that ensure the cycle of change continues, the necessary destruction that allows for rebirth.

The **Vibrancy** are the embodiment of pure energy. Unlike the other species, the Vibrancy are not corporeal. They consist of pure energy, the living potential of the landscape. They can take visible form, appearing as fleeting bursts of light, a pulse of energy passing from one crystalline form to the next. They influence the flow of creative energy. The Vibrancy are the creative force of the Resonant world, the wellspring from which new forms and possibilities arise.

The relationships between these beings are not always simple. The Sonafolk and Whisperers, for example, often collaborate, weaving complex stories together. But the Shatterlings and Vibrancy exist in a state of eternal tension, a dance of creation and destruction that powers the world's constant evolution. The Resonant is a delicate web of interdependence. Each component is a necessary element of the whole.

All beings, from the crystalline "vegetation" to the mobile waters, are connected by the unending flow of sound. Each part is influenced by the forces within the environment. The Resonant are never static. They are fluid, adaptive, ever-changing. The Resonant live, die, and rebirth. They are always more than individual, always becoming. Every shift is an experience.
