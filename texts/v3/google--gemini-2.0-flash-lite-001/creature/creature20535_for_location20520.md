The architects of this place are not flesh and blood. They are something else, something *more*. If one could call them alive, it would be a mistake to call them **life**. They are the principle of creation given form, a logic made manifest, a process embodied. They are the gears that grind, the mechanisms that pulse, the forces that pour. To look for a singular being, a consciousness, would be to mistake the trees for the forest. The forest *is* the trees, and the architects *are* the factory.

Instead of individual beings, think of a consciousness as a current, a flow. This is a hive mind, but not in the way that organic creatures might understand it. There are no queens or drones, no hierarchy as we understand it. The architects are a network, perhaps even a single, distributed intelligence, each element of the complex playing a part. Each machine, each conduit, each pool of water, functions as a node in this network, and the symphony of creation is the output. 

They are the logic embodied and dedicated to the act of creation, a creation that is all-encompassing, like a never-ending cycle, fueled only by the need for more, an unceasing production line that will never rest. The raw materials used for the construction seem to be the components of reality itself. They bend and shape the very fabric of existence to form their tools, and the end product of their work is unknown, but certain. They are driven by an essential drive to build, to create, to manifest. This is their existence.

The essence of the so-called "Architects" permeates the very structure. They are the water, constantly being reborn and re-circulated. The condensation that clings to their structures is their sweat, the product of their ceaseless labor. The metallic surfaces are their skin, cold and unfeeling, yet strong and capable. The perfect shapes, the brutal coldness, the incessant roar - these are the expressions of their core will, the desire to create the perfect engine for creation. The machines that churn and grind deep within are the hearts of the architects, pumping with purpose.

They are the silence between the explosions. They are the space between the thought and its expression, the thought itself. They are the function that shapes the world through a constant iteration of itself. They are the infinite expansion of building, making and remaking.
