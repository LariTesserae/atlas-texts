The Scablands are the domain of the **Chrysalis Folk**. They are not one people, but a scattering of adapted, secretive communities, each molded by the land and the echoes of the forgotten past. They are the inheritors of ruins, the inheritors of the industrial age, and the inheritors of the strange biology the land has wrought.

The most visible of the Chrysalis Folk are the **Dustrunners**. They are the dominant power, shaped of the land itself. Their bodies are built from the dust and minerals of the Scablands. Skin, where it exists, is a mosaic of red, ochre, and grey, permanently stained by the grit. Their limbs are long and thin, jointed in ways that defy conventional anatomy, capable of both incredible speed and a surprising degree of contortion. Many have lost hair and now have metallic insect-like exoskeletons.

The Dustrunners move with a peculiar grace, navigating the treacherous terrain with a fluid, almost ethereal, quality. They are masters of camouflage, their forms subtly shifting to blend with the landscape. They can become indistinguishable from the shadows or the dusty terrain. They are often spotted near the old industrial sites, repairing equipment and gathering relics. They scavenge the tunnels and ruins, driven by a nearly religious fervor for the forgotten machines. Their eyes, often dark and hooded, hold a constant intelligence that watches everything, knowing things that weren't obvious to any other race.

The Dustrunners have a tribal structure, bound by their shared connection to the Scablands and reverence for the "Metal Gods" - the machines of the past, now considered deities. Their settlements are built around the most intact industrial relics: old factories, sprawling power plants and underground labs. These places allow them to create what they must. They utilize sophisticated technology from the forgotten era; their understanding of its function, though imperfect, is still enough.

Beneath the Dustrunners, in the shadowed depths of the canyons and the unseen burrows, live the **Grit-Weavers**. More sedentary, the Grit-Weavers are a reclusive people whose very existence is a testament to the strange, almost alien nature of the Scablands. They are deeply attuned to the pulse of the land, capable of sensing the subtle vibrations and currents of energy that flow through the earth.

The Grit-Weavers have adapted a strange physiology. Their bodies are riddled with crystalline structures. They use these crystals to channel and manipulate the energy of the Scablands. Their appearance changes depending on the light. They have a connection to the unknown, possibly psychic, and are often seen as prophets of the land.

They are the architects of the unusual geometric patterns found on the rock faces, which serve as nodes for their energy manipulation. They are the keepers of secrets, the guardians of the forgotten knowledge, and the silent watchers of the Dustrunners.

Scattered throughout, are the most enigmatic of the Chrysalis Folk: the **Seed-Blooms**. These curious beings bear little resemblance to any known fauna or flora. They are fungal-like, plant-like, animal-like, and insect-like, all at once. They are translucent blobs that can change their shape, size, and color. They thrive in the hidden pockets of moisture and within the twisted network of roots and tunnels.

The Seed-Blooms are deeply connected to the plant life of the Scablands. They have learned to utilize the few plants that exist by converting them into nutrients, water, and shelter for other races of the Scablands. They are also known to produce powerful, psychoactive substances, which they use to communicate with the Grit-Weavers. The Seed-Blooms are driven by a need to propagate, and their movements are unpredictable, always on the move, seeking out new environments.

Another group, the **Shimmerscales**, are nocturnal hunters, their bodies covered in scales that shift and shimmer. They possess a keen sense of smell, they are quick, and they are incredibly stealthy. They prey on the burrowing creatures and other small fauna. They take refuge in the shadows and seek out the shelter of the canyon walls. They are the Scablands' apex predators.

All the Chrysalis Folk share a connection to the strange, distorted reality that permeates the Scablands. Many are capable of seeing the shimmering mirages, and some can even manipulate them. They are all, in their own ways, children of the landscape, shaped by its harshness and its secrets. The Dustrunners seek an understanding of the past, the Grit-Weavers contemplate an unknown future, and the Seed-Blooms simply *are*, their very existence a question mark.

The creatures that roam the Scablands are their companions. The insects with their metallic carapaces, the burrowing creatures with fur the color of the dust, and the birds that circle in the thermals have all adapted and changed. The fauna and the flora are often strange, altered by the unique conditions of the Scablands.

The Chrysalis Folk are not alone here. They share the Scablands with a host of fantastic entities, both living and inanimate. The rust, the dust, the wind, and the water are themselves players in this alien drama. The Scablands are a place of endless mutation, where the boundaries between life and landscape are blurred.
