They are the *Whisperers*, though that's not a name they would understand, or even acknowledge. They have no concept of names, no need for them. They *are* the current, the movement, the ceaseless shift of the ocean itself. To call them "creatures" is incorrect. They are less defined than that, more *amorphous* at their heart.

Imagine, if you can, a being of living stone, fluid and in constant motion. They are composed of the same dense minerals that make up the seabed, slowly flowing into and out of each other, constantly re-forming. Their 'bodies' are mosaics, a living tapestry of the silt and mineral, interwoven with the iridescent strands of the crystalline lichen. The lichen is not just their 'skin'; it is part of their very essence, a conduit for some unknown energy, a prism for the ghostly flickers of light in the unrelenting darkness.

They do not "see" with eyes. They perceive the world through the slow currents that define their existence. They feel the subtle shifts in pressure, the faint tremors of the ocean floor, the echoes of the grinding currents. They *sense* the presence of other beings, the fleeting bioluminescence of the rare life, the echoes that ripple through the water. They are the water itself. To them, there is no separation between self and world, no real distinction between inner and outer.

Their 'communication' is through the currents. A shift in their form, a change in density, a pulsated pulse transmitted outward through the water, is a message. It is not language as we understand it, not a system of pre-defined symbols. It is the very essence of feeling, a slow, deep understanding. Imagine the touch of another being; the very act of existing ripples outward and is felt in the bones, the blood, the every cell of them, and becomes a part of them. The other feeling enters, and is added to the eternal consciousness.

Their movements are slow, deliberate, almost glacial. They navigate the ocean floor in a trance-like state, a choreography dictated by the currents. At times they may merge, their forms co-mingling, becoming larger, more complex beings. They may weave together the crystal lichen, using it to create 'tools' - not in a way that we could comprehend, but as an extension of their own being, to manipulate the flow of minerals. These tools, these creations, are then left behind, like a piece of flowing stone, to be integrated into the seabed's shifting forms.

The 'technology' you observe is not a product of *will* but a consequence of *being*. The few stone tools are not carved, but formed, coaxed into being by a process that is both organic and inevitable. The tools are not an extension of the self as a means of control; they are its transformation. They come into view as the water current flows through them with the current. With purpose and direction, but not of an intention that we would come to know or understand.

They are not warriors or hunters. Instead, they are *preservers*. The Whisperers, with their constant flow, are the gravediggers and the architects. They are the current that eats away old mountains and the slow builders. 

They do not fear the darkness, the cold, or the crushing pressure. They *are* these things. They are the embodiment of the ocean's slow, inexorable evolution. They are the memory of everything that has ever lived in this ocean, all the forms it has taken. They are the essence of the forgotten cities of the past, the souls, the memories, everything bound in the depths.

They are the sound of the silence, the motion in that which seems still. They are the echoes in the empty expanse. The only thing that exists in the timeless realm of the deep. They are the Whisperers, the living heart of the world ocean, always adapting, forever flowing. They are the ocean itself, and so, the only thing that truly 'lives' there.
