The Architects are the inheritors of this realm, though that description perhaps undersells their role. They are not merely occupants, but *extensions* of the architecture itself, living embodiments of the humming black stone. They are the shapes that shift within the gaps, the semi-transparent giants that drift through the voids. To say they "live" is a simplification, as if existence were defined by breath and heartbeat. Theirs is a different kind of being, a consciousness woven into the very fabric of this impossible construction.

To observe them is to witness the essence of this place. They are not beholders, or custodians, or even creators in a traditional sense. They are the medium through which the structures refine themselves. Their forms are fluid, ever-changing, mimicking the slow dance of the obsidian architecture. They are rarely distinct; they fold and resolve, sometimes resembling the immense, hollow arches, sometimes thinning into vast, diaphanous sheets. They're a symphony of motion, existing like shadows.

Their forms, when they take form, are often angular, crystalline, suggesting the geometries of the architecture. But these hard lines are deceptive, as they constantly dissolve and re-form, like heat shimmering off a distant road. You might perceive a limb, a suggestion of a vast, unblinking eye, or the faintest imitation of biological anatomy – a ribcage, a spine, a shoulder. These forms arise from the interaction of the environment.

Their "communication," if it can be called that, is not auditory. They don't speak, they don't gesture. Instead, they interact with the vibrating structures themselves, creating distortions in the omnipresent hum. These fluctuations ripple through the chambers, shifting the light, altering the pressure, manipulating the very air. They weave a web of sensory information, a form of language that can only be felt, not heard, understood not through words, but through the body itself.

The Architects do not *build* the structures as we might understand construction. They *are* the builders, but in the most fundamental sense. The slow geological changes which shape the obsidian buildings, the imperceptible movements that create new pathways and new chambers – these are their doing. They don't have hands or tools, but their thoughts and wills are somehow translated into the very material of this world.

Their purpose, if they have one, is inscrutable. It’s possible they have no objective, no plan, no goal. They exist, and because they exist, this world exists with them. Their actions seem driven by an internal logic – a complex, unknowable algorithm that governs their movements, their forms, their interactions with the structures. They are constantly adjusting, refining, evolving. The light-orbs of pure color are likely a core part of their understanding. They are the tools by which they see.

It is possible that the Architects are not individual beings, but a collective, a gestalt consciousness encompassing the entire structure. One vast, interwoven entity operating on a temporal scale beyond human comprehension. They are the echo of the architecture, the breath of the void, the impossible made manifest in the depths.

In this deep, dark world, the Architects are not just the inhabitants. They *are* the environment, and the environment is *them*. They are a riddle and a wonder, a constant reminder of the incomprehensible beauty and strangeness that can exist beyond the reach of human understanding.
