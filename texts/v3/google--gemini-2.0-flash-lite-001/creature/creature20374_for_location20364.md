The inhabitants of this city are not easily defined. They are not creatures in the traditional sense, with hard edges and distinct forms. Instead, they are more like patterns, echoes of thought made manifest in the fluid embrace of their world. They are the architects and the architecture, the currents and the constellations of light that bloom within the liquid deep.

They are the children of the twilight, bathed in the bioluminescent glow that blooms from within. Their forms are fluid, ever-changing, mimicking the currents that shape the city. Imagine figures woven from pure light, their bodies shifting, morphing, and reforming to navigate the intricate labyrinth of obsidian towers. Their "skin" shimmers with the violet and crimson hues of the kelp forests, the colors reflecting the light of their internal bioluminescence. There are no fixed faces, no discernible limbs in the human sense. Instead, they are composed of nested forms, a series of concentric, constantly shifting halos that subtly radiate energy.

These beings are not driven by need, as we understand it. They do not eat, they do not sleep, they do not reproduce in a way that suggests a biological imperative. They exist and they create, they are the living expression of the city's complex geometry. Their primary mode of communication is not sound. Instead, they interact through the manipulation of the bioluminescent light within and around them. They orchestrate patterns, weaving intricate messages visible across the vast expanse of the city. A dance is a conversation, a subtle shift in the city's architecture is a spoken word, a pulse of light a question or affirmation. Their thoughts, emotions, and memories are imprinted on the flowing currents, absorbed by the stone, and reflected in the light.

Some might call them "weavers," for they shape the very fabric of their reality. They guide the currents, sculpt the obsidian towers, and nurture the strange, bioluminescent flora. They are intimately connected to the city, feeling its heartbeat, sensing its subtle shifts, and responding to its every tremor. They are capable of travel. The inhabitants of this city exist within the currents, using their light bodies to navigate the ever-changing city, the spiraling pattern that expands toward the unseen surface. They travel using their ability to shift their shape inside the patterns of currents, allowing one being to become part of the flow.

Others might see them as "dreamers." Their existence is one of constant contemplation, an eternal meditation on the patterns of existence and the mysteries of the deep. They possess a deep understanding of geometry and a profound connection to the complex ecosystem they reside within. The erosion provides an example: The beings have learned to work *with* the erosion, and have used it to shape and refine their city. These are not buildings as we know them, but something greater, a complex sculpture that encompasses everything that exists within it.

There are whispers of older forms, beings of immense power that predate the city itself. They are the entities that built the foundations, the ones who understand all the secrets of life and nature. These elder beings exist as the foundation, constantly shifting and changing, and yet are always present in the city. Their voices resonate in the subtle clicks and groans and distant rhythmic pulses. They're connected to the city, but never can or will be separated from it.

Perhaps the most accurate way to define these beings is as a collective consciousness. Each individual is a fragment of a greater whole. They are the city, and the city is them. Their sense of self is not tied to a physical body , but to a network of interconnected energy, a shared understanding of their reality. They are bound together by the liquid darkness, the bioluminescent light, and the eternal dance of life and decay.
