The inhabitants of this watery Eden are not merely creatures; they are emanations, echoes of the world's deep dream. They are shaped by the constant, caressing rain and the dim, luminescent light, existing within the endless, submerged jungle like thoughts within a vast consciousness.

Firstly, are the **Dream Weavers** - not physical beings, but the psychic imprints of individuals who once witnessed the dawn of the world's existence. They linger, unbodied, like after-images in the very fabric of the dream. They are the guardians of a forgotten language, communicating primarily through the shifting colors of the giant flora, causing the jungle to bloom in responses to their thoughts. Their forms are fluid; at times they manifest as constellations of bioluminescent motes, drifting through the watery canopy, and at others as the patterns woven into the submerged vegetation. They can manipulate the environment at will, often subtly, shaping currents, guiding light, and subtly guiding the dream’s perception. Any who dare seek their counsel would find only silence, or whispers from the jungle, a lesson in oneness.

Next, within the jungle’s depth are the **Waterborne**, a kaleidoscope of ethereal life. Their forms are rarely solid, often shifting and ephemeral. Some resemble colossal jellyfish, pulsating with an internal, bioluminescent light, their delicate tendrils trailing through the water, leaving ephemeral trails of sparkling glitter. Others are like schools of miniature comets, glittering and darting, and still others are massive and amorphous—living shadows that absorb light and become darker, nearly vanishing altogether. They are drawn to the Dream Weavers and their whispers, moving through the water as if carried on invisible currents of thought.

Another prominent type are known as the **Gloomwings**. These are massive, bat-like creatures, whose wings are not made of flesh and bone, but of the darkness itself, woven with veins of luminescent fungi. They are mostly blind, navigating the maze-like jungle by sensing the subtle shifts in the water’s pressure; as they move, they create wakes of swirling bioluminescence, lighting up the underwater canyons and passageways. If disturbed, they emit a deep, mournful hum that resonates for miles through the watery world, reminding the inhabitants of their shared dream.

Then there are the **Seed-Keepers**, rare and precious entities that closely resemble plants with an animal spirit. Growing upon the largest of the plants, they are living, breathing repositories of memory and potential. They are immobile for most of their lives, and appear as a congeries of interconnected, crystalline structures. Within these structures are the seeds, which are not physical, but rather concepts, emotions, and possibilities. When disturbed, they release these seeds into the water, where they can take root in the minds of the other dream inhabitants, offering visions and potential new forms or experiences.

There are also the **Architects of Echoes**, shadowy entities who linger near the impossibly-placed, obsidian-like objects. They are obsessed with structure, with the logic of the real, even though they exist within a place where logic is more suggestion than iron law. They attempt to give shape to the formless, and, in doing so, they leave behind strange structures, that are more echo than imprint, and cause ripples of distortion in the dream. They exist in defiance of the dream, unable to relinquish their grip on the world that once was.

All these beings are intimately connected, weaving an intricate tapestry of existence. They are the dream's components, forever interacting, in a cycle that is both grand and slow. They are the essence of this world, bound together by the neverending rain, the dim light, and the shared dream. They are not conquerors or travelers, not creatures bound by instinct. They are the dream itself.
