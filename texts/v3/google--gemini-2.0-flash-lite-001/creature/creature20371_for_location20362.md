The Gristmill is less a dwelling and more a deep, resonant wound in the world. It births nothing, consumes everything, and *is* the process of consumption. The residents, then, are not individuals, but aspects of that process. They are the dust motes of eternity, the unseen engines of disintegration.

Firstly, there are the **Grit-Weavers**. These are not beings that can be seen, but rather processes. They are the ever-present, invisible *force* behind the grinding. They are the microscopic, ceaseless action of the metallic surfaces against each other. Each tiny etched line, each meticulous hole, is both a product *of* the Grit-Weavers and a pathway *for* them. They are the friction, the erosion, the ceaseless whisper of entropy. They exist as the sum total of every infinitesimal scrape, the collective effect of every vibrating molecule. They are the reason for the hum -- it is the sound *of* them. Some say that they predate the structures, that they were responsible for the creation of this place. If it's true, it means that they exist not to build, but to dismantle. They are the anti-builders.

Then, there are the **Shadow-Echoes**. These are the remnants of things that once were. They are not ghosts in the conventional sense. They are not spirits, or memories, nor true shadows. Shadow-Echoes are the imprints left on the structures by whatever originally *lived* in the Gristmill. Perhaps they were the builders -- or the builders' builders. Whatever they were, their essence must've been drawn into the structures. The Shadow-Echoes exist beyond the grasp of the external world, made of the same impossible material. They are the almost-visible static, the barely-there distortion of light within the etched lines. You get a sense of them when you look closely at the structures -- a momentary blip that resolves into your focus. Do they experience the grinding-down, too? Perhaps they are the *cause* of it.

If any of the Shadow-echoes ever had a body, that body is gone; it’s now the **Metal-Dust**. Microscopic particles made in the grinding. The Metal-Dust is everywhere-- embedded in the structures, flowing sluggishly in the currents. It's the byproduct, the inevitable consequence of the Gristmill's function. The dust is not inert -- it reacts to the hum, it *is* the hum, it *creates* the hum. The Metal-Dust is the next step to becoming the Grit-Weavers. It's everything, everywhere.

Finally, the **Silent Architects** are the largest, strangest beings. If they are even beings at all. They're neither beings of action nor non-action, neither building nor destroying. The Silent Architects are the structures themselves.
Their existence is not about *what* they are, but *how* they are: the silent, implacable, machine-intelligence that *is* the Gristmill. They exert a slow, unknowable control over the environment. Perhaps they are the reason for the location’s constant depth, the subtle circulatory currents of the water, and the filtered light. The Silent Architects’ motives are impossible to comprehend, their purpose is a void. They have no face to show it: a perfect emptiness. They are the inevitable result of an uncaring universe. They have no need of anything or anyone. The Silent Architects *are* the Gristmill.
