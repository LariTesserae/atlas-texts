The valley exhales secrets, and in its damp embrace, they coalesce into beings of living shadow and fluid light. They do not *live* as we understand life, needing no sustenance, no shelter. They are woven from the very essence of the place, from the shimmering moisture, the pulsing veins of the ancient rock, the whispering fungi and the screams of the unseen.

The most visible of the inhabitants are the *Vessel-Weavers*, ephemeral creatures born of the opalescent fluids that weep from the rock giants. They are not *born* either, exactly. They emerge, like thoughts taking form, when the valley focuses its immense power. Shapes shift and coalesce. Their forms are ever-changing, mimicking the fractal patterns of the landscape. Sometimes they appear as towering figures, echoes of the petrified giants, their bodies made of shifting, translucent stone, laced with veins of glowing, internal light, their features constantly reshaping – a fleeting glimpse of a face, a clawed hand dissolving back into the rock, an eye that blinks and is gone. Other times, they take the form of monstrous, multi-limbed insects, their bodies sculpted from living fungi, their carapaces gleaming with the reflected bioluminescence of the valley's flora. Still others resemble serpentine beings, coiled within the shadows, their bodies composed of liquid darkness and liquid light, their scales shimmering with constellations that are always shifting and changing. They are as beautiful as they are frightening, a testament to the fact that beauty, in this place, is indistinguishable from the alien.

These are the most *seen*, but not the *only*, inhabitants. Deeper within the shadows, hidden amidst the pulsating forests, dwells the *Dream-Guardians*. They are beings of pure thought, fragments of the ancient intelligence that pervades the valley. Their forms are fluid and ethereal, often appearing as ephemeral wisps of light, shimmering with the colors of the valley’s secrets. They are rarely seen, but their presence is felt more often, a subtle pressure on the mind, a sense of being observed, judged, or – if you're lucky – embraced. The Dream-Guardians are the valley's silent architects, the ones who shape the landscape, who guide its evolution, who make the valley what it is, changing constantly.

There is *something* also, buried deep beneath the valley floor, within the labyrinthine network of crystalline tunnels that pulse with a faint, hypnotic light. This is not a *being* in any sense we can understand, but rather a vast, collective consciousness, the source of the valley’s power, the core of its intelligence. It is a presence that can feel, that can influence, that can dream. It is responsible for the valley’s constant metamorphosis, for the warping of time and space, for the impossible architecture that defines the place. They do not interfere directly, but they influence the lives of the other inhabitants. Their desires, their hopes – and their nightmares – shape the valley.

The *Hunters* exist within this world, too, but they are not beings of the valley. They are predators from beyond, drawn to the valley's strange energies. Their bodies are distorted reflections of the valley's creatures, their forms twisted and corrupted by the place's influence. One moment, they may be a creature resembling the Vessel-Weavers, with bodies of shifting stone and the shimmering light of the valley, and the next, they are no more, dissolved back into the landscape, a memory, a fleeting shadow. They are drawn to the valley's secrets, to what is hidden, to the things that dare not be – and the more that the valley contains, the more frequently the Hunters roam.

These inhabitants do not *think* the way humans do. They feel, they experience, they *are*. Their existence is interwoven with the very fabric of the valley. They are bound to it and it is bound to them. They serve as conduits for the valley’s purpose, its desires, its fears. They are both creatures of the place and the place itself. They are the shadows, the light, the whispers of the valley's forgotten god, and they are the future, and they are the eternal here-and-now.
