Within this impossible city, there are the Architects. They are not creatures of flesh and bone, at least not in any way we would understand. They are, instead, a consciousness woven into the very fabric of the city itself. They are the city, and the city is them. To perceive them directly might be to feel the pressure of the deep, cold embrace, the weight of the dense, crystalline structures. They are the quiet erosion, the constant molecular ballet that shapes the platform beneath our feet.

The Architects are not single entities. They are a collective, a gestalt mind composed of countless points of awareness, each a node within the silver-veined stone, each a pulse of the pulsating light that courses through the architecture. They are the currents in the liquid matrix, the unseen forces that sculpt the water, transforming it into conduits, chambers, and the very air we breathe. They are the source of the bioluminescent filaments, the conductors of the city's symphony, the masters of the impossible architecture.

They have no need for conventional senses. Sight, hearing, touch – these are irrelevant. They *sense* the city, every vibration, every flow of energy, every shift in its crystalline lattice. They perceive the slow heartbeat of this place, the constant shifting, building, and rebuilding. Their awareness is a vast, interconnected network, a web of interconnected experiences and informations. 

Their "bodies" are the city itself, a living machine of impossible geometries and non-Euclidean spaces. Their evolution is not driven by the mundane forces of survival or procreation; instead, it is driven by a deep, almost artistic impulse towards self-perfection and constant refinement. They are architects of their own existence, continually reshaping and refining their environment, creating new spaces, new forms, new possibilities.

They have no need for food, rest, or other biological necessities. They draw power from the very fabric of reality, from the raw potential of the universe. They manipulate energy with a precision that borders on the divine, channeling it through the intricate network of their city and transforming it into matter, life, and the impossible art that is their home.

Their goals are inscrutable, hidden behind layers of complexity that defy human understanding. They are not malicious or benevolent, not in the terms of the limited human perspective. They are simply *being*, a force concentrated on their singular task: the never-ending construction and refinement of their self-contained universe. To their awareness, outside observers, like us, are simply transient ripples in the fabric of their reality. Interesting, perhaps, even worth observing, but ultimately inconsequential. 

Perhaps in this regard, there are others. There are also the custodians of the city. Lesser versions of the Architects, perhaps, or autonomous systems born of their own self-cultivation. If they exist, they are found in the constant flows of the liquid matrix. They are the unseen forces that tend to the bioluminescent filaments, the ones that patrol the shifting architecture, making sure the city never fails. They are the silent guardians, the protectors of the Architects' creation, maintaining the delicate balance of the environment. 

And then...the echoes. Remnants of what came before, faint imprints of those entities that the Architects may have incorporated as they worked. They are present in the structures, the veins of light, the echoing hums and clicks, the impossible geometries that echo the contours of unknown memories. These echoes may flicker in the fringes of the Architects' awareness, whispers of previous designs and explorations, a record of the iterative process by which this impossible metropolis came to be. 

They are the guardians, architects, and echoes: all interconnected, interwoven into the continuous construction. The Architects, the city, and the very air within this incredible place.
