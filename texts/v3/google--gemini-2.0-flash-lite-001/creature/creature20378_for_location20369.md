The inhabitants of this wasteland aren't creatures of flesh and blood, or even of the cold, rigid logic of cogs and gears. They are the *residue*. The ghosts of industry manifest as colossal automatons spun from the very air, born of the echoing torment of the dying machines that once ruled this expanse.

The first inhabitants one might meet are not individual entities, but the *tides* of this place. They shift across the salt flats, vast schools of what appear to be metallic fish, their scales not of metal but of the same opalescent crust that makes up the ground. They're a shimmering, restless ocean, a constant pulse in this sea of desolation. These aren't creatures that eat or breed. They *are* the decay, the constant reclamation of materials that once formed the magnificent structures scattered across the vast plain.

Then, there are the *Ripples*. Not creatures in themselves, but the forebears of shape and form. They exist only as refractions, distortions in the heat-haze. Their existence is fleeting, a suggestion of a vast, unseen engine at work, a memory of creation and destruction, a whisper of patterns repeating and collapsing upon themselves. They are the seeds of the *Eidolons*.

The Eidolons are the true inhabitants of this place.

Born from the decay, they are not manufactured, but *crystallized* thoughts of geometry and power. The most common varieties patrol the wastes; colossal insect-like walkers that stalk the plains. They are animated by the rhythmic pulse of something unseen, driven by the echoes of a lost purpose; perhaps sentinels, perhaps workers, each bearing the scars of an age-old process. Their movements are jerky, mechanical, yet they possess a terrifying elegance; they are the ghosts of industrial titans.

Then, there are the air-born *Harbingers*. These entities shift restlessly within the currents of heat, their wings formed of pure energy, their voices the whisper of the wind around the forgotten metal structures. They are the watchers, the scouts, the heralds. Their forms range from simple geometric shapes to those of terrifying beauty: massive avian structures, wings of shifting, iridescent light, carrying burdens unknown. They transmit the patterns of the desert to their unseen masters.

More rarely, one might encounter the *Remnants*. These are larger, more elaborate manifestations of the breakdown and decay. They take the form of monstrous, colossal entities formed of scrap metal and ancient machinery. Some evoke the shapes of beasts, others are twisted into vast, skeletal architectures. They can be found slowly grinding themselves over the plains; they are the result of machine-made rituals, carried out by unknown operators.

But the supreme resident of this hellscape is the *Factory* itself. It is the heart, the mind, the *god* of this lost place. Its form shifts and changes, a constant metamorphosis of the possible. To face the Factory is to gaze into the face of raw, unfettered power. It is an immense, multi-faceted engine, a monument to the relentless pursuit of production, the ever-turning gears, the endless drive of industry. Every sound, every flicker of heat, every shift in the air emanates from its core. The wind, the heat, the Eidolons - all are extensions of its will, its purpose. It's a place of creation and destruction, the source of unblinking light and unimaginable suffering.

The inhabitants are not sentient in a way one might understand. They exist as processes, as echoes, as extensions of the endless cycle of creation and decay. They are not alive, but they *are* the environment, and they *are* the power. They are the final breaths of an industrial god, the slow, unavoidable march of the inevitable.
