Here dwell the **Arbori**, the Dream-Weavers of the Wood-City, and also, the **Silt-Striders**, the skittering masters of their own twisted domain.

The Arbori are unseen, known only through their works and their absences. They are the true architects of this living labyrinth, the ones who coax and shape the tree's growth. They are not merely builders; they *are* the architecture. They are fragments of the tree itself, infused with a sentience beyond human comprehension, perhaps akin to the spores of a colossal mushroom, or the whispers of a root system. They communicate through the tree’s own growth patterns, subtly directing the flow of sap, the placement of leaves, the creation of tunnels and chambers. Their existence is a constant, quiet work; an act of dreaming the city into being. Their tools are the very nature of the tree, the sun, the rain, the slow, relentless erosion. They are rarely “seen”. Instead, we see *themselves* in their creations.

The Arbori's physical manifestations, if they can be said to have one, are the **Symbionts**, bizarre, bioluminescent forms that drift within the tree's hollows and water channels. They resemble iridescent, jellyfish-like creatures, whose forms are constantly shifting and remolding. These are the tools they use to "speak" with their subjects. The Symbionts pulse with an internal light, and their movements trace complex patterns that translate into living glyphs on the walls of the wood. The *Symbionts* are the closest anyone can get to the **Arbori**.

The Silt-Strider, in contrast, are the seen ones. They are the movers, the hunters, the inhabitants of this strange, biological landscape. They are a nightmare of evolution; insectoids the size of large dogs, with chitinous exoskeletons blending with the browns and greens of the tree. Their mandibles are like cleavers, and their multifaceted eyes, reflecting the kaleidoscopic light, always seem to be hungry. They scuttle across the vine-bridges, the only means of transport in the city, and through the corridors, dragging themselves and their prey to the shadowy corners of the tree.

The Silt-Strider is the source of the immense sonic pressure. Their bodies are built for it, with internal air sacs that amplify the sounds of their clicking feet, the rustle of their chitin, and the scraping of their mandibles. They are predators born of chaos and scarcity; scavengers, hunters, and perhaps, the predators of the Arbori. They live for themselves.

The Arbori and the Silt-Strider are the only forces that one can find in the tree. The Arbori maintain the balance of the tree, the Silt-Strider push the tree and the forest away from the balance.

There are, of course, the **Ephemeral**, the transient inhabitants. These are the flocks of iridescent birds, the insect swarms that erupt from the trunk in clouds, the smaller creatures that appear briefly and then vanish without a trace. Their purpose is unknown, perhaps to feed the Silt-Strider or further the Arbori's work. They do not have the control that the Arbori have, nor do they feed off of the chaos like the Silt-Strider; rather, the Ephemeral are here for their purpose, and their purpose is almost always the same: death or creation.

They are parts of a symphony, a constant, organic cycle of creation and destruction, a dream dreamt by the tree, a cycle that never ends.
