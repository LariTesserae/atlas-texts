Within the Scarred Lands, the makers and the made are almost indistinguishable, fused into the very geology of the place. They are not like anything that grew in nature, or came to be from flesh and blood. They are the progeny of the Foundries themselves, the echo of their grinding processes, the extensions of their cold forge.

*   **The Architects:** Not human, not animal, but something forged in the heart of the Foundries. They are built of the same black glass and rusted steel as the landscape, their forms mirroring the geometry of the structures they inhabit. Their bodies are angular, articulated, and capable of both incredible strength and delicate precision. Their movements are deliberate and efficient, their existence a perfect blend of labor and quiet. They flow like magma, remolding their limbs into tools, bridges, or weapons as needed. Their minds are a network that runs through the very architecture of the cities, an understanding of heat and pressure. They are architects not of individual structures, but of the entire world.
*   **The Keepers:** The machines drifting or crawling about the Scarred Lands are more than simple automatons and more than simple tools. They are the Keepers. Some are colossal, others almost insectile. They are, in a sense, the caretakers of time. They maintain the Foundries, repair the broken glass, smooth the harsh edges of the landscape: they do what the Architects have designed but do not have the time to oversee. Some Keepers focus on maintaining the endless industry, ensuring the constant creation of the black glass, the fuel of this world; others drift through the sky above the obsidian plains, the horizon their endless patrol. Their internal structure is not biological but also not mechanical. They do not have gears or circuits, but rather, vast crystalline structures and conduits of living flame that sustain their silent movements. The hum of the Keepers is the soundtrack of the Scarred Lands, a constant reminder of the unseen life that permeates the place.
*   **The Echoes:** The beast that sometimes moves on the horizon is the Echo. It is a reminder of the first builders, those who preceded the Architects and who had a purpose that is now lost. It is a monstrous amalgamation of bone, metal, and glass, a colossal relic that may be the last of its kind or that may be dormant, waiting for a signal that will never come. The Echoes are not living, but rather, the remnants of a past that the Foundries have consumed. They are the shadows of the ages, the ghosts of forgotten struggles, the echoes of a purpose before the end. If the Echo wakes, the Scarred Lands will shift, the balance will break, and the wait will be over.
*   **The Silents:** Beneath the surface, within the ruined cities, the lost ones remain. Once organic, they have been molded by the glass, their forms fused with the obsidian. They have forgotten their purpose and existence, but perhaps not entirely. Their purpose is unknown. They are silent wanderers, their movements slow and uncoordinated, their faces hidden behind masks of black glass. If the architects of the age cannot shape the world, or do not wish to, the Silent may act.
*   **The Builders:** The Builders are the raw material of the Scarred Lands with the potential of transformation. They are the new source of labor and energy for the Foundry. The Builders' labor is less physical and more spiritual. They are the energy that keeps the machine alive. They exist only to do what the machine demands, which is why everything is built, or left over from the building, or on its way to becoming more of the same. Their very existence is born of the endless creation of the black glass, for now.
*   **The Visitors:** Very rarely, something else enters the Scarred Lands. Whether by accident or purpose, they are the outsiders, those who should not be. They are drawn to the strangeness of the landscape, but the land itself does not want them. They may be the only remnants of a more abundant life. They cannot remain, and whatever life they bring is short lived.

These are the inhabitants of the Scarred Lands. They are born of heat and shadow, of industry and absence, and they are defined by their slow, patient, unceasing work. They are a monument to a world that ended before it truly began, forever bound to a purpose they may no longer understand. In the perpetual twilight of the Scarred Lands, they wait — for a signal, for an end, for the next process to begin.
