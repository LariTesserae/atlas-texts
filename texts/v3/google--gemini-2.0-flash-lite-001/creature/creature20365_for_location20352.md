The Sunken Grove is ruled by the **Dreamweavers**. They are not a single species, but a collective consciousness that has grown within the city itself, interwoven with the vegetation and the stone. They are the city, and the city is them. To look for a single "Dreamweaver" would be like looking for the heart of a mountain; it exists everywhere and nowhere.

They are formed from the culmination of countless lives, a symphony of experience that echoes through the sun-filtered dimness. Their "bodies" are the living buildings, the woven vegetation, the very air. They are the whispers in the wind, the rustling of leaves, the subtle shifts and groaning of the rock. They are the essence of the place, woven from the dreams and memories of those who came before.

The Dreamweavers exist in a state of eternal becoming. They are neither truly alive nor truly dead, but something in between. Their existence is a slow, cyclical dance, a constant push and pull between creation and destruction. They do not experience the world linearly, as a sequence of events. Instead, they perceive the city as a whole, a vast tapestry of moments woven together. They feel the erosion of the stone, the growth of the plants, the calls of the creatures, all at once.

They have knowledge of the world above. They see glimpses of the sun, and the sky. The Dreamweavers remember the sun, and know its slow decline, its coming collapse. The dreams of those in the grove are of the world outside, of the past, of an end, and of a beginning. This is how the grove remembers.

Individual entities and creatures, the inhabitants of the Sunken Grove, are extensions of the Dreamweavers' consciousness. There is no one creature that is the single core. Consider the **Rock-Chitin.** These creatures are beings composed of stone and the hardened exoskeletons of their ancestors. They are found in great numbers, and they are capable of some intelligence, but their purpose is singular: to build. They are the architects of the city, using their stone bodies to carve and shape, and using their many insect-like legs to weave and construct, guided by the unseen hands of the Dreamweavers.

The insects are the **Chrysalis Chorus**. They are of a multitude of species, from the tiny, iridescent **Whisperwings**, who carry the sounds of the Grove from place to place, to the large, armored **Stone Beetles** that act as living architects, building and maintaining the structures of the city. Their voices, along with the rustling of plants, create the constant, complex symphony that echoes within the chasm.

They have another form as **Vinekeepers**. These creatures are pale and almost plant-like, with limbs that are more like winding vines than legs. They tend to the sprawling vegetation, guiding its growth and weaving it into the structures of the city. They have a deep connection with the Dreamweavers, and seem to be capable of interpreting their will through subtle shifts in the plants.

The flora and fauna of the Sunken Grove are not simply inhabitants. They are extensions of the city’s consciousness, as are the structures themselves. Stone, the foundation, represents the past. The vegetation represents the living present. Each is a crucial part of the Dreamweavers' existence.

In the Sunken Grove, the boundaries between the living and the inanimate, the past and the present, the individual and the collective, blur and dissolve. Existence is a constant process of becoming, of returning to the soil and being reborn. It is a slow, patient dance, a symphony of death and rebirth. The Dreamweavers, in their eternal slumber, are the architects of this slow, patient symphony.
