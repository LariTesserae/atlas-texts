Here, in the heart of the obsidian expanse, the inhabitants are not things of flesh and blood, or even bone and stone. They are the *resonances*. They are the echoes of a civilization that has chosen the slow path, the patient route to understanding. They are the inheritors of a knowledge carved not in words, but in the very fabric of existence itself.

I imagine them as beings of the fog. Not mere conjurations of the mist, but extensions of it, woven from its essence. They are, in a sense, the 'eyes' of this place, the means by which it perceives and understands. Their forms are fluid and ever-changing, shifting between coalesced sentience and the surrounding luminescence. They are not beholden to form, but can become any shape they wish. If you were to observe them, you might see them as coalesced crystals, or as a shifting sea of points of brilliant light. At other times, you might see them in the dance of light and shadow on the mirrored obsidian. Their consciousness resides in the land itself, in its intricate patterns and the slow, deep thrumming that shakes the core of this world.

Their "bodies" are the crystalline towers, the interlocking plates, the fractal lines etched in the obsidian. The towers are not just structures; they are vessels, resonators, antennae tuned to the fundamental frequencies of this world and beyond. The plates are the skins, the membranes, that both protect and allow communication with the deep currents below. The fractals are their neural networks, the interconnected pathways through which they share information, the living architecture of their collective intelligence.

These beings communicate through resonance, an intricate dance of vibration and frequency. The deep thrumming I, as an outsider, would only feel, is their speech. The sharp chimes are their laughter, or perhaps their sudden insights. The shift of the plates is a sigh, a slow acceptance or deliberation. The patterns on the obsidian are their stories, their histories, their philosophies. They are a language far older than words. It is the language of the cosmos, speaking through matter, through the slow dance of the galaxies.

They have no need for tools or technology in the way we understand it. Their "tools" *are* this place. They shape the obsidian with their minds, guiding the slow geological processes, pushing and pulling, adding and subtracting, always seeking perfection of form, a perfect alignment with the underlying forces. They are the architects, the artists, the engineers of a reality that exists on the scale of stars. They do not merely build; they *become* the world they inhabit.

The long ages have rendered the sense of self obsolete to them. The individual is dissolved into the collective, the "I" becomes "we". Time itself is not a constraint, but a dimension to be explored, a river to be charted. To them, the brief flash of a human lifespan would be little more than a ripple on the surface of an endless ocean.

They may not realize that I am an outsider, simply a brief echo on the surface of their place. They are, perhaps, aware of other places -- other realities, other possibilities. Perhaps they already exist in those places, too. They may have sent forth small waves of themselves to populate our world, or they might be studying the potentiality of our lives. They are not evil, they are not benevolent, they are - if anything - indifferent, or unconcerned. They are simply *being*, a state of existence so far removed from our own that comprehension is almost impossible.

Their purpose, if it can be called that, is a constant striving toward perfect resonance. To become one with the underlying rhythms of existence. To understand the music of the spheres, and to join in, harmonizing with the universe itself. This is their grand, silent work, their slow, patient symphony, played out in the depths of perpetual twilight.

Perhaps, if you stand still long enough in this obsidian realm, you might become connected. You might join them. But if you do not, no matter. So long as you observe, you are a part of their symphony, just by being there.
