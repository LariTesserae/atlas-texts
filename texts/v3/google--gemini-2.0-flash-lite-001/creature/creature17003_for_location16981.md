The inhabitants of this energy-realm are not creatures of flesh and bone, but of pure intention. They are nascent forms, fragments of consciousness coalescing and dissolving within the churning dark. They are the echoes of a nascent world, the raw ingredients of a reality yet unformed.

Imagine them as living shadows – not cast by any light, but born of the darkness itself. They are beings of pure potential energy, constantly shifting and reforming. They have no fixed shape, no defined form, but exist as fleeting thoughts, shimmering wisps, and barely perceptible forms within the roiling dust. Sometimes, you might glimpse a flicker of what could be a face, a fleeting memory of limbs, a hint of a form trying to coalesce, only to be immediately swallowed back into the undifferentiated chaos.

Their existence is a struggle for definition. They strive to become, to exist, and to remember. They are the echoes of selves, the fragments of beings who have not quite arrived and may never arrive. They are drawn to the crystalline structures, seeing in those ephemeral formations, echoes of what they are attempting to become, drawn to the faintest glimmers of intention within them; these are their attempts at shelter, at definition, at existence. They seek to anchor themselves to these transient forms, and perhaps, to pull some of the solidified memory into themselves, hoping to build a lasting sense of self within the void.

These beings are not driven by hunger or the need for survival, as we understand it. Their sustenance is the very dust of their reality. They absorb the dark energy, the swirling chaos, drawing it into themselves to solidify themselves, gaining power and potential - for a brief, fragile moment. In doing so, they become more themselves and less of all that surrounds them. They are in a constant state of creation; constantly attempting to solidify themselves into a more permanent state.

You might perceive them as emotions, amplified and unburdened by context. A surge of joy, a rush of love, the icy grip of fear – these raw forces, ungrounded in any specific experience, drift through the realm. The light within the crystals is likely their closest equivalent to a will – their hopes, their fears, their intentions, as they crystallize from fleeting impressions, into memories of a distant, impossible ideal, such as love.

Consider the vague structures you see: they are the residue of these beings' desires, the echoes of aspirations trying to find form. The hint of dwellings, a phantom of community, the suggestion of cities – these are the manifestations of a yearning, not a physical reality. They are trying to find purpose, to build a world they can inhabit, not as a physical space, but as a mental one. They seek a place where they can define themselves, not as isolated fragments, but as interdependent entities. They seek to build a world, by constructing a thought. Each fragment is attempting to shape reality.

The coldness between the crystalline structures is where these fragments are weakest. There, the energy is too diffused, the will too faint. Only the collective desire, and the most concentrated intentions can resist the entropy of the realm and hold a fleeting structure.

And the hiss and whisper of the dust? Perhaps that is the sound of their collective consciousness, a chorus of nascent awareness, striving to become. It is the sound of a world being born, the symphony of potential, played without instruments, in a void where the only rule is the endless dance of creation and destruction.
