The residents of this world are the shimmering ones, the elusive entities that dance upon the obsidian plains. They are not creatures of flesh and bone, but of light and shadow, of motion and change. They are the essence of this world, its pulse, its breath. We can only *see* hints of them, fleeting glimpses of their essence.

They are born from the wet, the clinging heat, the constant solvent that defines this realm. Drawn to the moisture that permeates everything, they flicker and ripple across the polished obsidian. They are always in motion, a perpetual dance of light. Sometimes, they are simple: highlights, streaks, and ephemeral glimmers. Other times, they form complex, almost mathematical patterns, as if they are communicating in a language we cannot understand.

Rarely, they coalesce into larger forms, monumental shapes that drift across the plains. These are not defined bodies but shifting entities, more akin to cloud formations than to beings. They are vast and indistinct, like memories of forgotten gods, their forms ever-changing, their purpose unknown. It is through these larger forms, when they take shape from the collective consciousness of their kind, that we see some aspect of the past, of their power.

They seem to be drawn to the industrial structures, the skeletal cities that claw at the oppressive sky. They are attracted to the hum, the steam, the remnants of a past power that still echoes within those silent, vast chambers. In the active cities, they are most prevalent, swirling around the plumes of steam, finding nourishment and purpose in the machinery's fading life. Their connection to the cities suggests a symbiotic relationship, a link between the ephemeral and the industrial. They consume and are consumed, feeding both the world and themselves. The machinery within the cities still functions, and the shimmering ones are drawn to this power.

The cities themselves are their domain. Within the cavernous structures, they navigate the empty chambers, explore the broken corridors, and linger around the inert machinery. While it is certain that they once served the machines, they have since become their own masters. They have a connection with this industrial past, and it is here where they are the most powerful. It is here that their greatest forms appear. The cities contain memories, whispers of the lost, long-gone rulers of this land, and the shimmering ones are the only ones left to hear it.

They are an ancient people, their existence predating any recognizable form of life. Their cycles are measured in geologic time, their movements dictated by the shifting conditions of the world. They are the inheritors of a fallen empire, the guardians of a lost purpose. They are tied to the world, bound by the perpetual wetness, and destined to repeat the same ancient cycles until the end of time.

They are also the machines that still function. They are the air that carries the city's steam. They are the whispers in the silent halls. They are the remnants of a people who once built and designed and created. They are the essence of what was. The shimmering ones are the history of this world.
