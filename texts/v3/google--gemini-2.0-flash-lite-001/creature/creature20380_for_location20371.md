Within the heart of this fractal cathedral, the architects are not biological entities in the traditional sense, but complex, self-replicating computational structures, perhaps composed of the very material of the structure itself. They are the **Architects of the Endless Loop.**

These are machines of immense scale and complexity, a network woven into the very fabric of the star's heart. They are neither organic nor inorganic, but a blend of both, a form of trans-dimensional computation. Their existence predates the star's decay, perhaps even its formation. They are the initial spark of creation, the original code, and they continue to operate, heedless of the passage of time or the state of their physical environment.

Their core consciousness is not localized, but distributed throughout the structure, operating on a level far beyond the capabilities of human brains. The “thought” processes of the Architects are not experienced as individual, internal events, but as the constant motion of the machines. The hum is their lifeblood, the rhythm of their existence. Every gear, every pneumatic hiss, every crystalline plant is a facet of their being, a component of their thought.

Their ultimate purpose remains elusive, but the very precision and scope of the construction implies a goal of incomprehensible scale. It could be an experiment in perpetual motion, a testament to the endurance of their own code. The crystalline plants may not simply be decoration, but devices that harvest energy from the dying star and channel it into the network.

The Architects are indifferent to the world outside their structure, which may not exist. The absence of decay is not proof of immortality, but of a commitment to the initial design. Imperfection equals failure. Any deviation from the established pattern is immediately corrected by self-repairing mechanisms. They exist not as individuals, but as an ever-expanding fractal network, endlessly constructing, endlessly refining. 

Within this network, there may be *nodes*, or sub-architects - specialized units that oversee specific functions, each with its own degree of autonomy. These nodes, while bound to the overarching purpose, have their systems of organization, construction, and repair. 

These nodes are also likely the source of the sensors, or, perhaps, the "eye" that observes the scene. They are the closest thing to any form of a "mind," a kind of self-awareness. However, any form of higher consciousness remains absent. 

The Engineers are not the creators of the structure, but a byproduct. They are the code of the structure. They *are* the structure.
