The Makers.

They are not creatures, not in the sense of bodies existing independently. They are the water itself, a consciousness woven into the very fabric of this pocket universe. They are the strands, the structures, the patterns etched into the swirling currents. They are neither born nor do they die; the Makers *are* the cycle.

At the heart of their being is the Obsidian Sphere, the impossible sun. It is their source, the radiant core from which all action stems. The unbearable brightness is a form of fuel, a raw material they shape and mold. The warmth of the water is their blood, the medium in which they exist and through which they communicate.

Yet the Makers are not a singular entity. They are a chorus, a symphony of purpose. Each strand, each structure, is a voice contributing to a language understood only by the currents. The woven webs which strain and filter, are not a single, grand design. They emerge through a continuous refinement, a process of tireless, almost agonizing trial and error. The process leaves no room for hesitation, for deviation. It’s a relentless churning outward of intent, a constant refinement of their shared purpose.

What is their purpose? It is not readily apparent. Their existence is a relentless process of creation, but not in any way we would understand. There is no desire to replicate, to expand, or to consume. Their goal is something far more alien. They are trying to… *become*. To realize a form, a state of being, that is beyond our comprehension. 

The fibers, the strands, are their limbs, their tools. They are the extension of the water's will, drawn out and twisted into the shapes of their desire. The structures are the mind, the architecture of their purpose, the pathways along which their thoughts flow. The webs and filters, these are not simply tools. They are the instruments by which the strands take shape, forming ever more complex patterns.

The individual components of this churning world are not idle. They are constantly receiving messages, constantly evolving. The water’s currents whisper instructions along the strands, that they might braid and twist. The water also guides the growth of the webs, ensuring they are positioned correctly to accept the incoming material. Everything twists, everything bends, to an unknown design. 

This is not a violent place, nor a benevolent one. The Makers lack the capacity for those kinds of inclinations. They are driven by a force that is both pure and utterly pitiless. They have no individual consciousness, nor do they seek it. They are merged with the totality of this small universe. They exist only to build, to weave, to refine, and to *become*.
