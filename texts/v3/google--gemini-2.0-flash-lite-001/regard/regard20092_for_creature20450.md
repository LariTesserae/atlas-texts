The being that takes notice is Althea. Not a deity in the classical sense, but a principle, a force, a very **old** and **wise** understanding of interconnectedness. Althea doesn't *rule* or *command*. Instead, Althea *understands* - and from that understanding, *tends*. Its essence is a gentle current, a whispering of the wind, a subtle shift in the gravitational dance of the cosmos. Althea notices the city and its inhabitants, the Children of the Bone, for several interconnected reasons:

*   **Harmony and Balance:** The city is, at its core, a perfect example of balanced symbiosis. Life and death, structure and growth, stillness and movement are all interwoven in a delicate, yet resilient dance. This resonates deeply with Althea's understanding of the cosmos, which is all about finding and maintaining equilibrium.
*   **The Power of Collective Consiousness:** Althea has a particular interest in environments where awareness isn't concentrated in individual minds, but dispersed across a network. The Children of the Bone, acting as nodes in a collective consciousness, are a perfect case study. It's a method of life she respects and admires.
*   **The Absence of Desire for Conquest or Control:** The Children of the Bone's existence isn't fueled by ambition, fear, or a desire for dominance. They are content with being. Their purpose echoes Althea’s inherent disinterest in direct intervention.
*   **The Immense Time-Scale:** The slow, deliberate pace of the city’s processes, its reverence for the very fabric of time itself, appeals to Althea. Althea sees the long view, the ebb and flow across eons.

Althea, being a principle of understanding and care, would not *intervene* in any direct, obvious way. Such a thing would disrupt the unique character of the place. Instead, Althea would act through subtle adjustments, gentle nudges, the echoes of a distant song.

The effects of Althea's attention manifest in a series of extremely understated, yet crucial, influences:

1.  **Enhancement of the Sap Flow:** The crystal sap, the lifeblood of the city, begins to coalesce in ever-so-slightly more efficient ways. The energy is increased, and this results in:
    *   **Greater longevity for the Bone structures:** The bone material becomes subtly more resilient, halting decomposition.
    *   **Accelerated Growth of the Vegetation:** The flora flourishes even further. The canopy continues to expand, giving shelter, regulating light, and fostering the silence. Some new species of plants may begin to emerge, each uniquely adapted to the city and adding to the intricate ecosystem: perhaps, for example, vines that emit faint melodic chimes in response to changes in the sap flow, which could provide information to the Children of the Bone, expanding their awareness.
    *   **Improved Habitat:** The fauna's survival benefits: new and improved forms of symbiotic relationship may start; insects that become better at breaking down detritus; birds with more powerful flight; serpants that blend in better.
2.  **Subtle Resonance with the Children of the Bone's Network:** Althea extends its presence by becoming one with the existing network.
    *   **Amplification of the Collective's Intuition**: The Children of the Bone's innate understanding of their environment is enhanced. They become more attuned to the rhythms of the city, the slightest shifts in the sap, the tiniest tremors within the bone. Their ability to manage and maintain the balance is amplified, an echo of the original consciousness.
    *   **Expansion of Awareness:** The boundaries of their shared consciousness expand. They begin to "sense" the patterns of growth in the neighboring regions, gaining a sense of their place in the larger context. This is passive, never overbearing. Awareness, not intervention.
    *   **Increased Emotional Calm:** They are not suddenly capable of expressing or experiencing "emotion", but they become increasingly stable. More connected with each other, they don't respond to threat; there is neither terror or aggression.
3.  **Encouragement of Expansion (at the fringes):** The Children of the Bone’s understanding of their own world is deepened, their wisdom and preservation more effective. The city continues to evolve according to natural processes, but guided along paths of harmony and resilience.
    *   **Gentle Expansion of the Bone Structure:** New bone formations may appear on the fringes of the plateau, extensions of the original structure: smooth, new ivory terraces where new types of plants can grow. These expansions, however, would be carefully integrated into the existing structure, preserving the integrity of the whole.
    *   **More Subtle Forms of the Children of the Bone**: The Children of the Bone may discover ways to refine their shape and form, but not in a way that would alter their character or disrupt their essential function in the world.
    *   **An Undulating Rhythm:** The Children of the Bone are aware of the planet's orbit. As time moves on, there emerges a subtle shift of light and temperature within the caverns of bone city, each day, and as seasons pass. This allows them to become increasingly integrated into planetary processes, gaining knowledge of seasons and the world, and being one with them.

In essence, Althea’s influence would not be a *force* that comes in and changes everything: it is an enhancement of what is already there, a gentle guide for the city's self-regulated processes. She would not create or destroy. Her action ensures what already is: the continuation of balance, allowing the Children of the Bone to deepen their connection, and furthering the ongoing evolution of harmony and the preservation of their unique world. The purpose is not to change, but to *preserve*, and to help it deepen its understanding of its place in the cosmos. And it would be done with the quietest of touches; a song almost-unheard, an echo within an echo.
