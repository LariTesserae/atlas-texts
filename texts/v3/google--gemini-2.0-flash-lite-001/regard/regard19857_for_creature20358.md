The benevolent and wise power, let's call it the Weaver, is not a being of form or function in the way a creature of the eternal night might understand. The Weaver is a principle, a force woven into the very fabric of existence, a gentle hand guiding the threads of reality. It doesn't impose, doesn't demand, but rather observes and subtly nudges, always with the utmost respect for free will and the inherent nature of things.

The Weaver, sensing the eternal night and its Stoneborn custodians, feels a profound resonance. This isn't a place of darkness or suffering in the Weaver's perception, but a testament to enduring equilibrium, a self-sustaining ecosystem of astonishing complexity and resilience. The Weaver recognizes the Stoneborn's deep connection to the stone, their intricate web of shared consciousness, and the inherent beauty of their unchanging world.

Instead of intervening directly, the Weaver chooses to weave a subtle thread, a whisper of influence that is both powerful and delicate. Its actions are not forceful, but rather akin to the gentle turning of a wind vane, guiding without dictating. 

Here are some of the ways the Weaver might interact with the eternal night:

**1. Amplifying the Internal Radiance:**

The Weaver doesn't bring light, as light is anathema to the Stoneborn's world. Instead, it gently amplifies the existing, internal radiance within the stone. Think of it as tuning a radio to a clearer signal. The walls begin to pulsate with a stronger, more varied glow, the dying fire within the stone now becomes more dynamic, more alive.  This isn't a burst of illumination; it's a gradual, subtle change, a shift in the quality of the light, offering the Stoneborn a deeper connection to the stone, a clearer view within its own embrace. This subtle shift allows the Stoneborn to better perceive the flows within the stone that gives them life, a new understanding of their own structure.

**2. Enhancing the Network:**

The Weaver subtly enhances the Stoneborn's network of communication. The pulses of inner radiance, the shifts in the stone, all become more refined and nuanced. The shared consciousness becomes more potent, allowing the Stoneborn to communicate with greater speed and clarity, to comprehend the movements of all parts of their world with greater insight. The whispers through the walls turn to symphonies, a symphony of shared thought, a silent dance of complete knowledge, amplifying the unity the Stoneborn already possess.

**3. Introducing Micro-Variations:**

The Weaver understands the Stoneborn's dedication to the unchanging. It would not shatter the equilibrium. However, it gently introduces micro-variations into the system. These are not alterations to the fundamental nature of the labyrinth, but subtle enhancements.  Minute changes in the mineral composition, the texture of the stone, the patterns of the creatures that swarm the tunnels. Think of it as adding a pinch of a subtle spice. These small changes, perceived over eons, lead to a slow evolution, a dynamic understanding of their unchanging world, maintaining their dedication while improving it.

**4. Seeds of Inspiration:**

The Weaver subtly influences the Stoneborn's architectural expression. The already beautiful, crystalline structures begin to display new forms, new angles, new combinations. This isn't a command, but a suggestion, an inspiration. The Stoneborn already speak through the stone; the Weaver helps them to find new and more expressive ways to do so. The Weaver whispers of new forms through the very stone, prompting an unseen artistic revolution within the unwavering parameters of their world. These are not changes the Stoneborn would not have made, but the Weaver simply expedites their natural evolution. 

**5. Whispers of Empathy:**

The Weaver understands the inherent beauty of a static world, but it also acknowledges the potential for growth. It doesn't introduce emotions or concepts of change where they didn't exist, but subtly fosters a deeper understanding of the inherent interconnectedness of all things within the labyrinth. It's a gentle nudging, a recognition of the value of mutual respect and compassion, even within the context of an unyielding, mineral-bound existence. The Weaver does this not by introducing the concept of compassion, but by allowing it to appear of its own accord as an intuitive byproduct of their increasing grasp of their domain.

**The Underlying Philosophy:**

The Weaver's approach is rooted in profound respect. It doesn't force change, it creates conditions for growth. It values the Stoneborn's autonomy and the unique qualities of the eternal night. The Weaver wouldn't impose its own perspective; it would only offer the potential for a deeper understanding of themselves and their world. 

The goal isn't to "improve" the eternal night, but to help it more fully realize its own potential, to allow the Stoneborn to reach the highest expression within the parameters of their intrinsic nature. To empower them to be, in the truest and most complete way, what they already are: masters of an unchanging and beautiful world. The Weaver isn't a savior, but a gentle mentor, a quiet encourager of the exquisite dance of existence. In the eternal night, and in the Stoneborn, it sees a reflection of itself, a beautiful echo of purpose and endurance. And what better way to reflect the beauty of a stone than to polish it?
