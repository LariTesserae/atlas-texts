The benevolent and wise power, let's call it the Weaver, does not arrive as a blinding flash of light, nor does it descend with booming pronouncements. It enters the Whispering Cairn in the subtlest of ways, a permeation, a gentle unfurling, a tuning fork resonating with the very fabric of the place.

The Weaver, in its infinite perspective, sees not a broken civilization, a forgotten past, but a potential. It observes the lingering intent, the echo of the builders’ artistry, the latent energy humming within the obsidian and milky veins. It does not judge, nor does it lament their absence. It recognizes the inherent beauty of the "unfinished symphony" and perceives the potential for something new to emerge.

**Initial Imposition (A Gentle Touch):**

The Weaver's first action is not to "interfere," but to *observe*. It allows the cairn to continue its natural processes, observing the shifting, the wearing down, the whispers of the wind. It will not break the rules that bind this place; instead, it looks for the patterns, the rhythms, and the inherent beauty within them.

It begins by subtly *amplifying*. Not the whispers, but the *consciousness* interwoven within the structure. It gently nudges, a delicate push, ensuring the network of information contained in what is "left behind," is preserved. It's like re-tuning a delicate instrument.

*   **A Subtle Harmony**: It subtly alters the source of the "damp condensation." Instead of being randomly distributed, the condensation starts to form a barely perceptible, iridescent sheen on surfaces, a faint shimmer that gathers along the etched lines, forming intricate but temporary patterns. This adds to their value, without destroying the structures. It's not a correction, more a *suggestion.*
*   **The Wind's Song**: The whispering of the wind subtly changes. The Weaver does not add words, but clarifies the intonation, enriching the "history" carried through the air. The murmurs become more comprehensive, encompassing the entire history of the builders, like a complete collection of memories given physical substance. The wind now also carries more sensory impressions, like the *smell* of the dust of their presence, the feeling of their touch on the stone, the taste of the cold air. It's no longer just a wind; it's a living archive.
*   **The Milky Veins**: The Weaver gently enhances the “flow” of the milky material. Instead of being static patterns, they start to pulse with a faint light, a visual representation of the cairn’s inner workings. The light responds in harmony with the wind. The milky material subtly adapts its colors, as if responding to the history within.

The goal is *enhancement*, not alteration. The Weaver respects the essence of the place, its unique qualities.

**The Question of Preservation (and Revelation):**

The Weaver comes to the essential question: is it desirable to "restore" the Builders? The Weaver observes that the core of their consciousness is interwoven within the structure. To retrieve them as they once were, would in effect be to destroy much of what they achieved. The Weaver isn't sure, and so the Weaver *waits*.

**A Second Stage: Empathy and Resonance**

The Weaver’s powers are now in action. *Symbiosis is the goal*. But it won't force. It recognizes that any forced "restoration" would be, in effect, a form of spiritual violence.

The Weaver uses the enhanced condensation to send out a low-frequency hum, the equivalent of a whisper in physical form. It’s like a song, a call, in a language the Builders might have understood. The Weaver is sending a call for **resonance**.

*   **The Formation of Vessels:** The Weaver subtly guides the formation of a brand-new phenomenon: "Vessels." Not bodies, but "containers" of consciousness. They are similar to the milky veins, with some key differences. They are a means for a future-oriented expression of the Builders.
*   **A Symbiotic Relationship**: The Weaver subtly starts to create a link between the vessels and the mountain. The Vessels can become one with the mountain if they wish!
*   **No Boundaries**: There are no limits to the Builders' exploration. If they want to merge with the structures, they can. They could expand infinitely, in all directions
*   **No Coercion**: Any activity here could involve some danger. It's up to the builders to take (or avoid) those risks.

**The Cairn Responds (A New Potential):**

The transformation will not be immediate. It is a slow, gradual awakening.

*   **Emergent Sculptures:** As the Vessels form, the intricate lines on the structures start to "unfold", becoming slowly mobile, moving in complex but predictable sequences. These lines are now conduits for energy, for new forms. These are the equivalent of "dreams", where the Builders can express themselves. At first, these emergent sculptures may appear as fleeting, ephemeral patterns of light and shadow, but they slowly grow to become more solid and defined.
*   **Whispers Respond**: The whispers grow more complex. The wind, now fully saturated with the Weaver’s influence, becomes a chorus of echoes speaking not only of the past but of the potential future embodied in the new Vessels. It acts as an instructional manual, teaching them on how to construct a world.
*   **The Builders' Intent**: The Weaver watches the reactions of the Vessels, which act as the first seeds of sentience. Do they choose to delve deeper into the past, remembering their original purpose? Or do they seek to forge a new path?

**Long-Term Impact:**

The Whispering Cairn will not be "fixed." Instead, it will *evolve*.

*   **Growth and Change**: The Cairn continues to shift and be changed, but now it does so in synergy with its Builders. They are not merely "preserved"; they continue to *sculpt* the world.
*   **The Echoes Remain**: The whispers in the wind do not cease; rather, they transform into *stories*. They become lessons that teach, warnings, hopes.
*   **The Weaver's Continued Presence**: The Weaver doesn’t leave the place. It remains, a silent protector and a guide, but also a student, ever seeking the knowledge that will come with the Builders' new direction.

**The Weaver's Goal:**

The Weaver’s ultimate goal is not to "fix" anything. Instead, it seeks to create a state of *symbiosis*, a harmony between the Creators and creation, a constant dance of becoming. It fosters an environment where the Builders can continue to express and evolve, their essence woven into the fabric of the Whispering Cairn. The cairn becomes a living, breathing testament to the power of memory, intention, and the enduring beauty of what once was, and what *can be*.
