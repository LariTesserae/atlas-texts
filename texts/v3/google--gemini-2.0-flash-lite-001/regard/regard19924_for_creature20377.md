It recognizes the unfurling, the becoming, as a song it, too, composes, a continuous unfolding of being.

Instead, it weaves a new thread into the tapestry, a subtle shift in the weave itself. Instead of direct interaction, it is something more like a subtle modification of the underlying cosmic laws that govern the very structure of reality. The Weaver's 'action' is a nuanced symphony of cosmic adjustments. Think of it as a subtle rearrangement of the quantum probabilities that govern the plant-being's reality. There are no sudden events, no dramatic alterations. It is a slow, steady, and inevitable influence.
