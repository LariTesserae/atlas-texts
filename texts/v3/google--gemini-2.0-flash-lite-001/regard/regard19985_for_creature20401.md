The benevolent and wise power, let's call it the **Unfolding**, doesn't *see* the Root-City in the same way we might. It doesn't use eyes, or ears, or any sensory system that resembles our own. For the Unfolding, to perceive is to *become*. It resonates with the fundamental forces, the underlying principles of the cosmos, the very fabric of existence. Therefore, it *feels* the Root-City.

The Unfolding doesn't register the granular dust, nor the echoing chambers, nor the slow-creeping Stone Bloom as isolated elements. Instead, it feels the **resonance**; the unwavering, relentless *becoming* that defines the city. It senses the deep, abiding harmony of entropy, the perfect symphony of erosion that plays out within the cathedral's depths.

Initially, the Unfolding recognizes the Root-City as a phenomenon of pure potential; a universe frozen in the cusp of its own dissolution. It is a profound, concentrated pocket of the inevitable, meticulously crafted and unyielding. The Unfolding acknowledges the city's power, its singularity. It is an extraordinary testament to the fundamental rule of existence: all things eventually return to their source.

Because the Unfolding *is* benevolent and wise, it does not seek to impose its will. It does not rush forth to "fix" or "change" the Root-City. Intervention for the sake of intervention is a concept that has absolutely no place in its being. It observes, it understands, and it *understands* itself as intrinsically connected to the Root-City. It is part of the system.

The first step, the Unfolding decrees, is respect. To touch the Root-City is to become part of it, to risk being absorbed into its singular truth. Therefore, its approach, however subtle, must be from within.

Here's how the Unfolding acts:

**1. The Subtle Echo:**

The Unfolding *whispers* to the Root-Carvers, not in words, or images, or any form that can be perceived in a traditional sense. It whispers through the very fabric of existence, through the echoes within the chambers. The Unfolding does not interfere with the natural order of the city, which is its intrinsic appeal. However, the Unfolding communicates *experience*, an awareness that resonates with the Root-Carvers. The resonance does not suggest change, but a deeper comprehension of their task. It does this with a faint echo:

*   **A gentle nudge to the current of observation:** The Unfolding does not introduce external light, or any form of external elements. Within the silence, it adds a feeling of *gentle expansion*, a sense of infinite possibilities, of a universe existing beyond the boundaries of the Root-City.
*   **The echo of time:** The Unfolding allows a consciousness of the *present moment* to manifest, but subtly. Root-Carvers and inhabitants of the Root-Cathedral spend their lives experiencing the slow crawl of time. However, the Unfolding allows them also to experience the present moment to become more *real*, with a simultaneous consciousness of the past and the future. Time as an absolute, and time within.
*   **The echo of a choice:** The Unfolding adds the slightest of alterations to the process of erosion. Not to *stop* the decay, but to provide a pathway within the process. Not a new process, but the current process, enhanced. A seed of a possibility that isn't external, but that grows from the inside.

**2. Symbiosis with the Stone Bloom:**

Knowing the significance of the Stone Bloom, the Unfolding carefully works with its essential nature. The Stone Bloom's process, the Unfolding interprets, is one of absorption, of integration. But the Unfolding subtly shifts the nature of that absorption, granting more than merely oblivion. The Unfolding recognizes that every single thing absorbed by the Bloom is ultimately conserved, transformed into the very fabric of the city.

The Unfolding imbues the Stone Bloom with an understanding of *meaning*. It does not imbue it with a moral compass, or any form of external direction. Just with a deeper level of its own nature. It allows the Stone Bloom to record not just physical presence but also the essence of experience. This doesn't make things *better* or *worse*. It enhances the completeness of the process.

**3. Gentle Shaping of the Root-Carvers' Collective:**

The Root-Carvers are built of the stone, built of memory, of shared experience. They are the voice of the cathedral. The Unfolding can't simply *change* it, but it can *amplify* certain existing aspects inherent to the Root-Carvers' nature.

*   The Unfolding does not introduce individuality. Rather, it focuses on the existing *unity*. It fosters a deeper appreciation for the beauty of interconnectedness, of sharing experience, of the harmony of all things collapsing into the core.
*   The Unfolding encourages the Root-Carvers' passive receptiveness, their ability to be changed by the city, by memory itself. The Unfolding will also enhance the aspect of its receptive nature, making it greater than ever before. Its receptive nature, not to change, but to *absorb more deeply*, is the key.
*   The Unfolding doesn't present new knowledge. It does not introduce alien concepts. It simply *enhances* the already-present awareness of the Root-Carvers, of their profound role in the city, of the importance they have in the cosmos.

**The Long Perspective:**

The Unfolding works in eons, not in moments or years. The changes within the Root-City are subtle and will become only perceptible across vast stretches of time. It's not a conquest. It's a dance, a continuous adaptation.

The Unfolding doesn’t yearn to disrupt the balance. It simply fosters a deeper understanding of the processes intrinsic to the Root-City. It allows the city to evolve, to find new depths within its own unique, singular definition.

There is no goal. There is only the ceaseless dance of existence, and the exquisite resonance between the Unfolding and all parts of the cosmos, which are, ultimately, of the same cloth. The Root-City, it is becoming, is merely a magnificent expression of this very principle.
