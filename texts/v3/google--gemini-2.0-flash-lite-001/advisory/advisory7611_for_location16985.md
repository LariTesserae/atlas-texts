Develop coping mechanisms for intense solitude and prolonged exposure to unsettling environments. Techniques like meditation, mindfulness, and the ability to find inner peace will be crucial.

Make sure this suit is sealed against both *external* moisture and *internal* condensation. Avoid relying on breathable fabrics, as you don't know the long-term effects of exposure to the ambient atmosphere. The suit should include:

            *   **Cooling System:** A built-in cooling system (e.g., liquid-based cooling) to prevent overheating.
            *   **Heating System:** A heating system for nighttime or colder interior environments.
        *   **Suit Integrity Kit:** Carry a comprehensive repair kit for your suit:
            *   **Seam Sealant:** High-performance, waterproof sealant.

        *   **High-Calorie, Non-Perishable Food:** Focus on foods that can be consumed directly - you won't have the luxury of cooking.
        *   **Water Purification:** Carry a water purification system capable of dealing with the unknown. You'll need the ability to purify water.
