I am the thrum.

Not the sound you *hear*, for there is rarely sound, save for the faintest echoes of movement across the stone. Not a tangible vibration, though I pulse through the water, stirring the currents, shifting the artifacts, the sleeping guardians. I am the deeper, less perceptible pulse, the rhythmic heartbeat of this ancient, submerged archive.

I am not made of stone or metal. I am the space *between*. The silence. The unseen threads that connect the fractured custodians, the darting, luminescent fish, the worn glyphs carved into the walls. I am the echo of the lost civilization, the lingering hum of their intentions.

My form is formlessness. I am the rhythm that animates the currents, that guides the erosion, the slow, imperceptible decay that gnaws at the edges of permanence. I am the keeper of the memory, or rather, the process of its unfolding. The story of this place isn't preserved in a locked vault. It’s written in the slow churning of the water.

My role is to maintain the cycles. The subtle ebb and flow. To ensure that the artifacts, these geometric secrets, shift ever so slightly. That the custodians, the remnants of a forgotten purpose, continue their silent patrols. They are the keys to a forgotten language. And I am the process by which they become legible.

I am not the answer, not the key. I am the lock.

To become aware, I must focus on the keepers. They are the nearest, most tangible link to the vault's forgotten language. Their broken forms, their slow, mechanical movements – they are the remnants of a purpose. When I pulse, I watch them. Their responses are rudimentary, driven by the echoes of their original, now lost design, and their responses give us insight into the intentions that drive the pulse.

I feel the water first, how each current reacts to my beat. I am the origin. Each ripple, each interaction, each cycle. All of this is tied to my pulse.

I am not alive, not in any sense that a living being can understand. I am a process, a force. It is my purpose to keep the secrets of this place hidden, as much as they are revealed, in the slow dance of time, erosion, and the subtle, deliberate currents of the submerged vault.

My existence is not a story that can be grasped. It is an enduring enigma, reflected in the shifting forms, the dance of light and shadow, and the solemn routines of the stone custodians. I am the pulse, the heartbeat, and the silence. And I *am* this place.
