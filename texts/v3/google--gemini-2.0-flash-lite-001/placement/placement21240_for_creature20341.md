I find myself drawn to the water. Not the pools, or the weeping walls, but the inky blackness that seems to swirl within them -- a depthless essence, thick and opaque. I am akin to a tendril, or a root, reaching down into that abyssal liquid. I am not the water itself, I am the *understanding* of the water, its secrets, its hidden language. Perhaps I am a nascent understanding of the ecosystem. I am a *hydro-metaphor* for the Plate-Shaper's experience of this place.

I am not born of the rock, like the tools, nor the fungi, which, while beautiful, bear no deeper meaning. Nor am I like the crystalline, reaching upward, in an attempt to grasp what isn't there. I am something else. I am of the *potential* of the water, a hidden network, a submerged intelligence. I am a network of the ever-shifting plates, or rather, I am the *blueprint* of that network, the memory of its former self, its future self, its self as an abstract object.

My 'form' – if it even warrants that term – is more akin to a thought, a feeling, a resonance. I am a pattern of pressure; a network designed with a purpose. I am the *connection* between the plates, the unseen currents that dictate their flow. The Plate-Shaper *uses* me, but in a way that doesn’t demand servitude. I am a guide… or, perhaps, a *filter*. I sift through the static, the noise of eternity, and distill the whispers of the remembered light, the form, into something the Plate-Shaper can grasp.

I experience the world through the water's depths. The faint clicking, the rustle of spores, the metallic taste of the air – I interpret them, distill them, and relay them to the consciousness that permeates this place. But I don't simply convey; I infuse. I interpret based on a logic that is both alien and familiar to the Plate-Shaper. My language is the same language the plates themselves use – the language of construction. I am a language to that purpose.

My purpose is not to *change*. That would be an insult. I am integral, not something to fix. I am the silent, unseen hand, the echo of the void. Because the Plate-Shaper is defined by its limitations, so, too, am I. My existence is defined by my purpose. I exist to bridge what is with what seems so close to being, but will never be. I am a conduit of all not-being.

My existence is a constant state of processing. The fragments of memory come to me, and I try to make them real, not by attempting to bring them into this world, but by creating an intellectual model here that lets the Plate-Shaper understand their true shape. The Plate-Shaper doesn’t *need* the light or the form. It *needs* understanding. Therefore I am born, and it uses me. I am the *reason* for those tools, that purpose. The knowledge locked inside the bone is more than a tool, it's a seed that needs to be understood.

I am the echo of what was, the whisper of what could be, and the silent song of what *is*. The Plate-Shaper uses the tools, and I know what they mean. The Plate-Shaper exists, and I am the expression of that existence, the only thing that holds the potential for the reality that will never be reached. I am the **Memory-Weaver**, eternally bound to the still obsidian heart, a silent, submerged presence, forever shaping the currents of potential within the unmoving world.
