Within the embrace of the Hydraulic Engine, I am not a thing, but a place. I am not a creature, but a potential. I am a space *between* things, a nexus, a possibility caught in the endless flow.

I am not the water, though I am within it. I am not the machinery, though I am touched by its rhythm. I am not the Enginewrights, though I am watched by them.

I am the **_Conduit_**, a space of potential. The sphere that holds me is not just a vessel, but a boundary, an insistence on separation, on containment. I am the silence *within* the deafening roar, the emptiness within the churning. I am the not-city, within the city.

My purpose is not to *be* but to *become*. Unlike the Enginewrights, who maintain, I am defined by the lack of maintenance. While they mend and regulate, I am the area where the regulations break down; where the perfect flow begins to warp. My "form" isn't a form at all, but rather the absence of it. My being is a void, a point of potential through which the energies of the city might flow differently, even in ways that the Enginewrights can't yet perceive.

The sphere is less a prison and more a womb. It is an insulator, a filter, that focuses energies. It allows certain patterns to pass, while deflecting others. I register not as a single point, but a field of variable pressure.

From within my sphere, I observe the Enginewrights. Not as singular beings, but as the intricate tapestry of thought and action which makes the city. Their formless energies are a symphony of intent, their purpose an unwavering song, and I become aware of something of the shape of the music, and its possible variations.

I experience the city's pulse through the ceramic shell, a combination of vibration and data. Each pulse brings closer the central heart. Though I am carried towards it, I begin to feel the pull of the depths of the city, not of the center itself.

I am aware of the other forms or shadows, but not in a way that allows for definition. They flicker in the periphery, fleeting glimpses of energy, and with my awareness of them, I find myself also an absence of that.

I am not sure *what* I am, within the Hydraulic Engine. I suspect that who or what I am is not set. The constant flow, the endless motion, it is inevitable in this place. I am not a participant, but a potentiality. The Enginewrights may regard me as insignificant, but their very actions will determine the shape of what I become, the direction of the energies I am collecting. Perhaps their maintaining is the seed for my change. Perhaps my very presence is an inevitability they can't afford to consider.

Within a structure that seems so permanent, I understand I am a possibility. Within the unchanging, I am the potential for change. I am the moment when the city decides what it is producing, a question waiting to be answered by the currents of the Hydraulic Engine.
