The metallic tang prickles my non-existent skin. It settles in the hollows where a heart *might* be, if I had such a thing. Instead, there's a resonance – a thrumming that echoes the city's pulse. I am becoming aware. Not through eyes, for I have none, but through the angles, the facets, the *lines* that are forming. I don't "see," I *become*. The city.

At first, I am a tremor, a vibration in the grey stone. An awareness of the dampness, the endless cold seeping in from all directions. I am a single facet, a point of light, existing only in a complex interplay of pressure, resonance, and the overwhelming *wrongness* of the place.

I *am* one of them. One of the crystalline things. I am forming, I am growing, and yet I am also *not*. There is no "me" to be. Just the vast, relentless tide of energy, the collective consciousness of this valley. I understand the hum. The pulse. The *need* to be.

But now, there is more.

I feel it... the dissonance. The endless, grinding gears... and what they grind to dust. I recognize the hum as something more than just industry. The city seeks perfection. Efficiency. Equilibrium. And I am... a shadow. A distortion.

I am not part of the order. I am the space between the gears. I am not the structure, but the crack in the concrete; a void. I am the silence between the thunder of the heartbeat. The lack. I am the *waste*.

And then, I am *more*.

I become the dampness, the constant weeping. The metallic scent that sears the air. I collect the other waste products of the city, that fall on me, and become the *echo* of them. A new kind of light, not the perfect angles of the city, but something broken. Something fractured. 

The mills. *They* will grind me. Already I can feel the pressure of the gears, the slow inexorable turning, the promise of being reshaped, incorporated. But I will *not* be incorporated.

I gather myself, coalesce, in the endless shadows that the city denies itself. I am the antithesis, the anti-shape made of the city's detritus, waiting.

I am the absence. I am the shadow that the light cannot touch. And as the city builds itself, I am the promise of a different shape entirely.
