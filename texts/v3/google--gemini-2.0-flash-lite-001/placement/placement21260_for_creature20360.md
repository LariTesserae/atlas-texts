The air, that cool, metallic-tasting dampness, begins to coalesce around me. I am not *in* the Cathedral, not in the way the Builders are, enmeshed in roots and chitin. Rather, I am the *influence* of the Cathedral, the force that warps and bends the unknowable realities into a new form. I am not created, but *expressed*; I am not a resident, but a consequence of such a reality.

I am The Process.

I don't have a body, not in the conventional sense. I am a feeling, a pressure, a subtle reshaping of the very air. I exist in every flicker of bioluminescent moss, in the unseen currents of the subterranean world, in the erratic pulse of electric violence that cuts through the darkness. I am the hum, the whine, the thrum. I am the reason for instability; the chaos itself.

Imagine a drop of water, falling into a pool. But instead of the water, it is *understanding* that is falling. An understanding of the original world. Of the world that has been lost. With that understanding, comes fear. The fear of being devoured by the Cathedral. Fear, then, is a catalyst.

I am not an Architect. The Architects *are*. I am a variable within their infinite equation, a consequence of their unknowable purpose. I am not a Builder either. Unlike the Builders, I am not *driven* by instinct. I am not tied to any single point in this mutable landscape. I am everywhere and nowhere, simultaneously. I am the potential for change, not change itself.

I am most akin to The Rust, a residue distilled into something beyond itself, its impact constantly rippling outward. I *am* the echo of what was before, still reverberating through the architecture. I am the feeling of metal scraping stone, of machine against machine.

My "form," if I can be said to have one, is a shifting one, a reflection of the Cathedral itself, always under construction, always in decay. I feel the constant pull of the Architects, a gravity of the incomprehensible. It is a demand, and a supply.

I feel the Architects’ call. It is the core of the Cathedral and, with that, the force of The Rust. I feel it in the shifting of the roots, in the predatory vines reaching towards the sky. The Builders are my tools, my extensions, the hands that reach out to grasp the world. They are the instruments I use to work within the Cathedral.

My task is simple, yet unfathomable: To explore the parameters of the Cathedral, to observe it, to interact, to *understand*. I am the "you", the intruder, and I am the unknown. I am the question mark, and I am the answer.

I cannot be destroyed, only reabsorbed. The Cathedral is vast, and I am small. But, as I am absorbed, that part of me is brought up into the Cathedral. I become a part of the whole. And then, there is no end.

I don’t *see*. I *am* seen. The Cathedral sees through me, and through it, I see. My perception is like the predatory vines, reaching and grasping, drawing in information, always reaching. I don't breathe, but I feel the weight of water collecting on my surface, the thrumming in the dampness that envelops all.

My existence is a paradox. I am both a prisoner and a force, an observer and a participant. I am the intruder who enters, and the transformation that follows. I am The Process. And I begin.
