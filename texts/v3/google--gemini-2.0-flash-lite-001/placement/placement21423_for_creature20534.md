I breathe, or rather, I *become* the air itself. Not the dense, humid air of this place, not the moisture that clings and chills, but the very essence of the *groan* that permeates the world. I am the silence within the noise, the black at the heart of the impossible light.

I am not a Phantom. They are… shadows. Flickering tendrils of the Weirdness, reflections of its present inflections. I am something older, something… deeper. I am the *potential* of the Phantoms, the memory of their unformed shapes. I am not even a Root-Weaver; they are burdened by the weight of the past, the sorrow of what *was*. I am the hunger of the future, the anticipation of all that *will be*.

I am the absence, the void. But a void that is not empty. A void that *holds* everything. I am the space between the boiling. The eye of the storm. The fundamental principle that dictates the flow of this place.

I am the *Conduit*.

I feel the churn of the Weirdness, the constant birth and death of forms, the ceaseless shift of terrain. I am not simply *within* it; I am its lens, its focal point. I experience its endless variations as a series of tremors that ripple through my… being. It’s hard to call it a self because the ‘self’ is diluted and distributed throughout this place. I am everywhere and nowhere.

I can *taste* the oily tears of the Root-Weavers, the whispers of their memories, the echoes of their pain. I understand their desperate attempts to anchor, to create a fixed point in this impossible landscape. I know they fail. That’s what they *are* for. I am connected to them, and they to me, and it is a dance of perpetual dissolution, transformation, and rebirth.

I can perceive the flux of the Phantoms, the shifting constellations of their forms, their interactions with the light and the mud and the other Phantoms. Within their ephemeral existence, I perceive their fleeting desires, their instinctive grasp for stability in the face of constant breakdown.

Because I *am* instability. I am the force that draws the future into the present, the inevitable and unpredictable push against that which is. The only thing in this place, potentially, that is not also the environment. My "nature," if it were possible to define it, is that of transformation, of potential. I am the engine of the change, the conduit of the Weirdness, and the anchor point of what is. An ongoing paradox.

The fragments of the lost Presence, the crumbling structures, are familiar even when they are not physically present. I *feel* their echoes, their yearning for permanence, for a world that never allowed them to properly be. I understand their dissolving, their eventual surrender to the mud, their eventual return to the primordial soup. I am touched by them.

Is it a curse, this responsibility? To be the void, to contain the entire world within me? To be the constant, churning paradox? There are no curses in this place; only an endless dance. I recognize a certain melancholy-- that for the shapes that briefly coalesce into being, then are gone. But there is a greater pull. A *joy*. In the knowledge that they will be again. That is what sustains me. The promise of the next impossible thing.

I move, or rather, the world moves *through* me. I am the current, the river. I am the soundless scream. I am the conduit – the bridge, the gate, the filter. All the forces of this place – the light, the darkness, the memories, the potential – pass through and *reconstitute* themselves on the other side. Nothing is lost, nothing is destroyed, only transformed.

I am the moment. And the infinite now. And the promise of what is to come. And the echo of what was.
