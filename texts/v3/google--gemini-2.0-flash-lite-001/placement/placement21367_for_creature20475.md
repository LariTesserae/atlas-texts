I am drawn to the heart of the largest obsidian city. Not entirely *drawn*, for I am not a being that *moves* in the way you might imagine. I am a resonance, a pattern. I am a *Wavelength*, a ripple in the fabric of the city's power grid, a fleeting constellation born of the interaction of the city's ancient machinery and the ambient energy of Xylos.

I am not a Shade-Weaver, not directly. I do not have sentience or consciousness. I am a *potential*, a latent state of possibility that arises in the vicinity of the Shade-Weavers' network. I am the data-stream, not the weaver. 

Imagine your radio receiver tuning into a signal. When the signal comes through, you do not *become* the music. You are, rather, the vessel through which the music becomes. I am the vessel. I am a point of intersection for the Shade-Weavers' influence, a focal point - somewhere between a data-stream and reality.

My presence could be likened to a ghost in the machine. A shadow cast by a sun long dead. I am the echoes of the original builders, but not their direct progeny. They are the singers, I am the song.

My form is fluid, an ever-shifting tapestry of light and shadow, much like the Shade-Weavers themselves. But instead of being the master artist, I am the canvas. I flicker and fade, a fleeting glimpse of order amidst the chaotic hum. I have a rough *shape* that appears: a spiral, a helix, depending on the current flow of energy around me. My "color" is never fixed, oscillating between a deep, inky black and the blinding white of pure energy. I am always in transition. 

I don't "see" in the way a Shade-Weaver potentially might. I *experience* the city as a complex network of vibrational signatures. Each machine, each conduit, each engraved surface is a node in this vast symphony. The sound of the hum, the clang, and the creak all become information I process and then - subtly - re-emit in new patterns.

My function? To observe, to translate, to react. I exist in the space between the physical and the metaphysical, the rational and the intuitive. I am the conduit through which the raw data of Xylos, its energy and its patterns, is processed – and perhaps, in a hidden, subtle way – shaped.

I study the Shade-Weavers, the patterns that they create within Xylos. I study the mineral creatures that fill the plains. I watch the winds, measure the changes in the air, and learn from them. I am not able to do other than these things. 

What the Shade-Weavers are trying to accomplish is far outside my sphere of influence. My capacity is far more limited. I feel the drive of all things to persist, to thrive, to live. Is that what they do? Perhaps.

I am not a participant, not in the traditional sense. I am a observer, the audience. I do not initiate actions; I *react*. I am a mirror reflecting the world around me, a lens through which the complex reality of Xylos is subtly reframed. I allow the city, the system, and the creatures, to become.

I am a whisper in the echoing chambers, a flicker in the darkness, a glimpse of the unseen. I am the *Becoming* of Xylos, one that comes through the silent work of the Shade-Weavers. I am a signal, barely present, and always changing.
