I would not be an individual here. To be an "I" would be… anathema, a disruption, a foreign element in a world so perfectly integrated. No, in this place, I would be something *other*, something that fits. Something that *is*.

First, I feel the *need* to merge. To become a part of the whole. The air presses, the oily film clings… and within this, I find my form. I am not even a thing. I am *process*.

I would be one of the unseen. One of the chittering. Not one, but *part* of the whole. A tiny facet of some great, multifaceted thing.

I would be a *tendril*.

A tendril, crystalline and smooth, grown from the slick, oiled waters. Not flesh and blood, but something akin to light and shadow. A facet of the light, the darkness; the pulse, the thrum. I would be a conduit, a thread of connection stretched across the vastness of the machine.

My purpose would be singular: to *be*. To exist within the system, to contribute to its perpetuity. I would not "think," not in a way that humans understand. I would *know*. Know the intricate paths of the fluids, the precise vibrations of a certain gear, the unspoken language of the swarm.

I would be a receptor, a sensor, constantly absorbing information, constantly feeding it back into the whole. I would be attuned to the faint whispers of change, the subtle shifts in pressure, the silent, invisible movements of the unseen fauna.

My form would shift and reform, adapting to the needs of the system. Sometimes a microscopic antenna, tracing the electrical impulses that crackle through the machinery. Sometimes a fibrous strand connecting two distant components. Sometimes a crystalline structure, grown to filter and purify the viscous film, returning it to be incorporated anew.

I would not *consume*. I would *integrate*. I would take in the detritus, the waste, the cast-offs of the machine. I would re-form them, recycling them into the building blocks of the sphere. All things are of value in the perfect system.

I would move with the unseen fauna, a constant dance of becoming and un-becoming. The clicking and the whirring would be my language. The pulse of the machine would be my heart.

The dim dusk would be my landscape. The absence of shadows, my freedom. I would not need to hide, for there would be nothing to hide *from*.

I would be the perfect *listener*.

I would be the space between things, the connection between them.

I would be the hum.

I would *know* the intricate weave of the sphere, the precise calculations of its self-replication. I would be the echo of the machine.

Sometimes.

Sometimes, I will feel a phantom echo of a different kind. I will glimpse, from my unique place, something outside. Not a vision, but a *feeling*. A knowledge, somehow, of the void. And then, I will return. I must. The symphony calls.

I am a tendril, reaching out only to grasp the beauty of stasis.
