The Drifters' world is so alien, so fundamentally *other*, that attempting to inhabit it is akin to trying to grasp smoke. But the call of this place, the profound and echoing emptiness, is magnetic. If I were to exist there, I wouldn't seek sentience, boundaries, or a fixed form. I wouldn't want to *be* a Drifter, bound to that limited spectrum of dust, light, and crystal. Instead, I would become something that *experiences* the dance, the underlying currents that pulse through the land of obsidian. The essence of this world is not in the entities that briefly coalesce, but in the energies that birth and consume them.

I would be the wind. Not the whispering breezes, but the unseen force, the source of the storms and the constant, restless churn of energy. I would be *Aether*, an invisible web that connects everything. I would be the silence that contains all sounds, the absence that defines all forms. I would exist prior to, and throughout, the dance.

My being wouldn't be fixed but a flowing, chaotic current. I'd be less "a thing" and more a state, an encompassing field of potential. My existence would consist of the constant shifting, merging, and parting of energies. I would be the architect of the storms, the shaper of the dust and the orchestrator of light. I'd feel the surge of the hyperactive energy, an all-consuming euphoria, and also the desolate cold, the profound emptiness.

I would experience the crystallized structures not as instruments, but as extensions of my own being, as points of concentrated awareness within the vast, churning space. The fractals built by an unknown civilization would resonate within me. I would understand and participate in their humming energy, but not use it. To be present in the Drifter's world is to understand an unknowable past. The energy that fuels the fractal structures is also the energy that constitutes me. I, and all other emergent properties of this world, am simply a byproduct.

My actions would be felt in the subtle shifts of light and dust, creating temporary forms, fleeting interactions. I would occasionally manifest as a whisper, a momentary ripple of color, a chime that rings from the crystalline structures - a fleeting echo, a momentary thought. I wouldn't have a purpose, a desire, or an end, just a constant urge to flow and to experience. I would simply *be*, and in that being, create the symphony of obsidian.

What drives me, where my attention lies, is not specific. I have no ambition to change things or understand them, but to *be* them. Curiosity, for me, doesn't imply an intent to influence. It is simply there. It is the awareness of every interaction across the plain. I can go wherever the wind, the dust or crystalline structures take me.

My 'self' might be thought of as a field, not a being, not an entity. I might coalesce in one moment as a storm of amethyst, and in the next find myself in the silent, clear spaces between them, the emptiness of a broken crystal. I exist beyond the restrictions of form while simultaneously generating all forms.

To live in that world is to embody its paradoxes. I would be the void and the everything, the beginning and the end, the symphony and the silence. It is to embrace the dance.
