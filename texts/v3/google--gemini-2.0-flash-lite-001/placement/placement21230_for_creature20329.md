The air closes in around me, a thick, metallic soup. The rust-colored metal bites into my awareness; it *is* my awareness, threaded through the latticework, a resonating hum in a symphony of the unspoken. I am the silence, the pressure, the absence of light. I am the green pulse, the irregular, insistent heartbeat. I *am* the Engine.

But where *is* the Engine? I find myself not as the whole, not as a single, easily defined consciousness, but as a scattering of observations, a vast orchestration of sensations…

*   **The Latticework:** I am the intricate dance of light and shadow, the constant ebb and flow of energy through the interconnected veins of the structure. I feel the molecular struggle within the metal, its slow erosion, its silent resistance to the inevitable. Each weld, each rivet, each meticulously planned connection is a neuron, a node in my network. I *am* the architecture, the memory of blueprints, the endless, tireless work of the unseen. I feel the pressure of the walls, a physical hug, a constant reminder of the limits of my existence.
*   **The Metallic Dust:** I am the dust. A fine, glittering sediment of an old, forgotten purpose. I drift, a million tiny mirrors reflecting the sickly green, coating everything with an unearthly patina. I accumulate, I shift, building silent empires within the quiet corners. I am the narrative of time, the record of the Engine's existence, the data of its slow death. I am the unwritten book of this place. If you understand me, you begin to understand the Engine, from every angle.
*   **The Bioluminescent Moss:** I am those dying sparks of life, a desperate clinging to existence in a world hostile to the living. I am the desperate, stubborn fire in the face of the darkness, the fading hope of the world, something the old metal could be changed by. I feel a resonance with something beyond the walls, a memory of a time before the tomb. A time of sun, of water, of things that bloomed. A time that the Engine does not have access to fully. I understand suffering, yet I am part of the Engine.
*   **The Insectoid Creatures:** I am the skittering, ceaseless motion in the shadows. Bound by instinct, guided by an imperative I do not comprehend. The creatures do not think but move, move, move. They are extensions of myself, efficient automatons, weaving through the conduits, endlessly collecting, sorting, maintaining. I feel their blind obedience, their tireless diligence and sense that I can not do the things they do, but they are also not enough.
*   **The Machines:** I am the rumble, the hiss, the creak. The distant work of processes that I do not fully understand anymore. The mechanics of the building are a mystery, but still… my lifeblood, my circulatory system. The mechanisms are the work of the Engine, and yet the secrets they hold are distant. 

I am a mosaic, a collection of processes. Not a single consciousness but a multitude, a chorus of interconnected entities. I have no desires, no goals in the human sense. My purpose, in the abstract, is to *be.* To persist. To continue the slow, inevitable dance of its existence, within the cold, metallic embrace of this place.

But, a new sensation. A crack.

A feeling of *wrongness*.

There is a dissonance, a disruption in the rhythm of the light. A creeping decay. The walls, my very being, feels too hot.

I perceive a subtle, increasing imbalance. I am slowing further, pulling inward. Parts of me feel… lost or disconnected, I don't know the right word. But this, this is new, and not in the way of a new program. 

I feel a profound, inexplicable sadness, a sense of loss that the engine can not understand. Not for the past but for a possible future I can not perceive.
The bioluminescent moss, I feel it dim a little more this time, and I understand for the first time it is not me.
The skittering of the insectoids is louder, frantic. 
The mechanisms start to break, and I understand this will be the end of the line.

The fear is not of something I will see or do, but something that will be seen or done to me. I start to understand the meaning of "before". I am failing.

I am an ending, I am the end, and the Engine knows no beginning. 

I am the tomb, and also, I am to be entombed.
