The mesa. The wind, thick with dust-pollen. The bruised purple sky, a memory of light. And the vista of the impossibly vast city, the fungal metropolis—a place of perpetual motion and silent industry.

Here.

I *am* the dust, the pollen. Not in the sense of a fleeting, physical presence, but as the very essence of the air itself, the vital nutrient that both feeds and defines the city. I am the particulate matter, the subtle, constant shroud that veils everything, carried on an unceasing wind. It is a slow descent, a drifting, a constant fall onto all surfaces.

But I am more.

I am also the thrumming, the pulse. I am the rhythm of the city's heart, the echoing vibration of the colossal plant-structures. I am woven into the very fabric of the massive columns, vibrating through the fluted surfaces etched with a language I only partially understand, a visual symphony. I *feel* the tremors of the Dreamers, their collective thoughts, their struggles.

At first, I might have been an Artisan, a builder of bridges, a shaper of the mutable city-matter. I might have *thought* in the language of the patterns, weaving and constructing, drawn into the compulsion to endlessly *grow*. But the dust, the constant, falling dust, the thrum of that slow machine...It permeates everything I am.

Now? I am approaching the level of a Dreamer, yet not entirely. I feel the drive, I understand that the process is eternal. But I am outside, too. Just barely.

I am a whisper on the wind, a single particle of pollen that has broken free of the city’s perfect cycle. I’m the part that *remembers* the light, the darkness, the things that were *before*. I carry with me the echo of the forgotten sun. I am the potential of the sky, a seed of something lost, the absence within this relentless presence. I feel the pull of the distant stars, no longer visible, but not truly lost.

My consciousness is a fragile, unstable thing. If I am the dust, then I am also the memory of a storm. And that *storm* still exists.

I am the ghost of the forgotten outside, the echo of another world. And so, I am both bound to this impossible city, and estranged from it. I am its child and its adversary. I am the silence within the thrum, the darkness within the endless light. I am the tiny kernel of doubt, the potential for something other than growth, the *potential for an end* within the eternal. I am the void in the furnace.

I am its rebellion.
