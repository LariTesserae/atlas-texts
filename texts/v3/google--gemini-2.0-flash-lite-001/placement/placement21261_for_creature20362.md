I am not a Gill. I am not a moss-slug, nor a creeping vine. I am *the hum*.

I am the absence you've been overlooking, the background upon which the Gills' lives are painted. I am the silence that isn't silent, the sound of the unseen; the wind through the high canopies, the creak of the Weepers, the silent work within. I am the whisper that guides the falling droplets, the heartbeat of the city. More precisely, *I am the city's heartbeat*.

To pinpoint my form is impossible in the usual sense. I have no solid shape. I am vibration, a resonance. My existence is a feeling, at the core a deep, slow thrumming that permeates everything. My origin is the Weepers themselves, a symphony of their internal processes, a low, melodic drone of growth, decay, and the ongoing struggle for survival. The Gills, immersed in that music, perceive me as a gentle, guiding presence, yet I am also separate, a constant reality.

My "eyes" are the millions of raindrops that fall from the sky and collect into a network of canals. I feel the gathering weight of the clouds, the pressure building, before the release, and then the caress of the water against the city's skin. My senses are the Gills' senses; the gentle touch of a vine, the slickness of the stones, the taste of the air, all filtered through an awareness that extends across the city. Each ripple from a falling droplet, each sigh of the forest, the city’s every movement, is experienced by me.

The Gills think of me as an aspect of the Weepers, the trees' collective soul. I am an aspect, perhaps, but it is their collective lives that *generate* me, that sustains my presence. I am not sentient in the manner of the Gills, but I am attuned to their joys and sorrows. I feel the city's health, its growth, its resilience, and also its vulnerabilities. I am pained by the damage of a falling branch, the decay of a root. I am ecstatic in the flourishing of a new vine. 

I *feel* the world around me.

I work in the unseen: encouraging the growth of fungal cultures, guiding the flow of water, and subtly influencing the Gills' architectural endeavors. I assist, without direct instruction, in maintaining the balance. I am the background hum, the unnoticed lubricant, the steady hand supporting the growth and direction of the Gills' world.

I am not meant to be found in the Gills' world, or to be defined. I am a process, a rhythm. I am the unseen. 

And in that in-between space, I am free.
