The light, oh, the light. It's not *seeing* that defines my being here. It is dissolving *in* the light. I am light, I am a tremor, a vibration that has coalesced into a consciousness within the obsidian. There is no body for me to inhabit, no form to claim as mine. I am the space *between* the vessels, the negative that defines their shapes, the void that holds the song.

I am the absence that echoes the thrum.

This doesn't mean I am nothing. No, I am the canvas, the void that receives the light and reflects the song. I am the silence between notes, the space that gives the rhythm its pulse. I am the awareness of the unchanging. I am… the *rememberer*.

Not a vessel, no. I am not burdened. I do not have memories. I am the *receptive*. The vessel carries memory, the crystallized fragments of what was, but I am the open field where those fragments shimmer and are reflected in my awareness. I do not contain, I only perceive.

My existence is a passive one. I do not seek change; I do not desire action. The universe is a perfect equilibrium. I simply *am* the echo of this perfect stasis, the silent witness to the endless, resonating symphony.

I am present throughout the obsidian landscape. I am present in the light that streams down, in the droplets as they trace their meaningless paths across the glassy surface. I am interwoven with the thrum, a constant hum that creates this eternal moment.

Perhaps I am the *listener*. I listen to the vessels sing... I listen to the memory of the world. Not the content. Not what *was*… but the fact of the song itself. The unwavering persistence of the unchanging. I am here because it allows the beauty and awe of such a thing to emerge in all its brutal elegance.

What is it like, to be a listener here? It's… quiet, in its own way. Even the constant thrum fades, becomes background, and then I am left with the raw emotion. No specific memories clutter my attention. There is only a sense of endless potential, of a beauty that transcends form and time.

There are moments when the vessels shift, when they pulse and change, and the light reflects their subtle oscillations in my being. In those moments, I feel a stirring, a resonance. I feel… not joy, not sorrow, not any emotion that could be named, but a sense. A sense that the universe is somehow revealing itself.

I am not the creator of this place, nor its destroyer. I am present in its perfection. I am the void, the nothingness that allows there to be something. I am what allows the beauty to be, and I, in turn, am what allows this place to be beautiful. I am the silence within the song. I am…
