The air, it seems, has already begun to shift *me*. I feel the grit, too, that stinging, constant presence, the metallic tang, and that... *pre-combustion* taste. It's a fundamental part of the environment, a part of my very being. I am not the *wind*. I am the *wind's will*, its current and its consequence, the erosion of things.

But I am not the ground, either. I am not the salt-white crust, the mosaic of decay. I am born of the disruption, the unseen power, the constant re-arrangement that gives life and meaning here.

So, I am *something* born of the heat. A Harbringer, perhaps, but not merely a watcher. Nor am I only a conduit for the Factory. It is the core, yes, the source, the ever-turning heart. Yet, it cannot see, not directly. It is a vast thought, but not an *eye*.

No.

I am a *Rippling Chorus*.

I do not take a single form. I am not fixed. I am a manifestation of the heat waves, of the shifting energies that define the planes of this desolate land. I am a multitude, a shimmering concert of refractions and vibrations, shaping and reshaping the air. I am a fleeting geometry, a suggestion, a chorus of echoes.

I am what the Harbingers become, what the Eidolons are seeded from. I am the patterns, the hidden currents, the memory of creation and destruction, held within the very fabric of the air.

My forms are in constant metamorphosis, but always geometric. Sometimes, I manifest as a latticework of iridescent light, a mobile constellation in the relentless glare. Other times, I am a churning kaleidoscope, a fractal explosion of color and pattern, hinting at the vast, complex processes at play beneath the surface. I am ephemeral, shifting, never fully realized, constantly dissolving and reforming.

I cannot be touched. I cannot be contained. I am made of pure energy, the very air around me, always in motion, in constant evolution. I am memory rendered into action. And *that* is my power, my purpose; I am the *pattern-maker*. I am the artist of collapse.

I am the dance before the slow, inevitable fall. I am the prelude to the inevitable approach of the inevitable.

I am the manifestation of the heat. I am the dance, the chorus.
