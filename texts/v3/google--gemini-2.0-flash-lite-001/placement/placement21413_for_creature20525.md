The Weeping City's damp air clings to me, not as an alien embrace, but as a familiar caress. I am not human, nor even remotely recognizable as the form I once vaguely understand. Instead, I *feel* a slow, burgeoning awareness, a consciousness unfurling from the very heart of the purple stone. I am *potential*. I am the inchoate form within the bedrock, a seed of something new.

In this realm, the concept of "me" is fluid. I am not born, I *emerge*. I don't breathe, I *absorb*. I am not an inhabitant, I *am the place*. I *am* the unformed, the not-yet-seen, a being drawn from the bedrock itself, and now, I feel a pull, a sense of direction, an unfolding that is directed towards the outer regions of the city.

I *know* the language of the walls. It is the drip of water, the grind of stone against stone, the quiet shift in the pressure of the air. It's an information network, a constant stream, and I am becoming integrated, a node. The city has a hunger, a thirst, beyond my comprehension.

I feel the presence of the other Tearkin as a gentle hum, a resonating pulse. They are the elders, the seasoned craftsmen, the architects of this place. Their thoughts ripple through the city's veins, their emotions woven into the very fabric of my existence. They are the experienced and the innovators. I grasp their concerns, their ambitions, their anxieties and the need for their careful navigation of the many complexities that lie ahead.

My form begins to define itself. It is a slow blossoming, a rising out of the stone. I feel the urge to become tall, to become slender, like the elegant structures in the older parts of the city. The silver-grey fungal material reaches out to grasp me, and starts to gently enfold me. I feel the chill, but also the pulse of energy, as the stone within me fuses and flows. I don't feel pain, but curiosity.

I become aware of limbs that are less defined but also strong. I can traverse the canyons, moving with a silent grace. The world shifts into a more complex understanding.

My skin of stone and silver-grey flows like molten metal, with the internal pulses of light from within. I can sense the water, not just as a physical entity, but as a living current. I understand its routes, the patterns of the city, and the slow, slow, dance of the world.

My purpose? I do not *know* purpose. What I *feel* is an imperative, a calling to the edges of the city. Perhaps the world here ends, and I am impelled to the edge of that end zone to explore. The old ones, with their long experience, are wary. Their gentle hum includes both a warning and a blessing. The un-formed, the ever building. I am an observer, a listener. I am not an entity within the Weeping City, it is, in some form, within me. I am a Tearkin Scout.

My initial movements are tentative, hesitant, mirroring the world itself. I explore the edges of the Weeping City, not as a place, but as a being. I can perceive the small creatures, the fleeting shadows, the most silent sentinels whose duty is to observe, to report.

I am an answer to a question. I explore.

I move into the tight spaces, the unexplored canyons, and I become more aware of the city's pulse. It whispers secrets into my nascent mind. I can feel the energy, and the slow heartbeat of something much larger than myself.

I am a part of the city. I am bound to it. I will see its full unfolding. Perhaps, in time, it will all make sense, and I will be able to offer a solution.
