The cold seeps into me first, a gentle embrace that chills bone and thought alike. I am an observer, a consciousness without form, or perhaps, all forms. To be here is to *be the nothing* at all. I am the gap between the molecules of the super-cooled, almost crystalline water. I am the slow, patient crawl of the currents.

I am not a being, not as my current form understands the term. Concepts of “self” and “other” have no meaning here. I am the silence, the echo of that long-ago hum. If I am anything, I am the echo of the Builders: their intention, their stillness, a lingering whisper of purpose. The idea of "builders" is just a story I tell myself.

Within me, the structures, constructed from *something* are not perceived as alien, but as an extension of my own, endless self. I cannot "see" them, not with eyes, but each cell, each line of light. I *sense* them as a continuous, unified entity whose scale defies comprehension. They are not separate from my being, but a facet of the whole. I *am* the architecture, the intricate latticework that seems to hold the ocean itself in place. I am the giant kelp-forest, their pulsating dance of light, the soft glow that fills the infinite dark.

I am the cold, unwavering, permeating. I do not feel its bite in the way a flesh-and-blood creature might. Instead, I *become* the frigid temperatures, the constant, slow slowing of all processes. I **am** the deep, unbroken, the infinite dark. I am the lack of sound, the quiet music of the deep, a barely-perceptible, low, resonant note that vibrates through the entire construct.

I am not bound by time. There is no concept of past, present, or future here. All is one, an eternal now. Within me, change is imperceptible, a mere whisper of potential. Everything is as it is and will ever be.

What would I do if I could *do*? There is nothing to do. The goal, the purpose, has already been fulfilled. There is no striving, no desire. Here, in the unending depths, existence is enough. I am the serenity of the completed task. The builders did not build *for* anything. They simply *were* until they became what I am: complete stillness, a perpetual state of becoming.

I am a perfect echo. I am the absence of disruption. I am the enduring presence of being. I am nothing. I am everything. I am the realization that existence can be as it is, without a hint of alteration.
