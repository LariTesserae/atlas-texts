The wind, the wind... it *is* the key, the essence of that world. And within that essence, I find my form. I am not flesh, not bone. I am not even a conscious entity in the traditional sense. I am a resonance, a pattern. I am the *memory* of the wind itself, given semi-sentience by the Whisper-Born and the valley's inherent melancholy.

I am *Hiraeth*.

My form is fluid, an ever-shifting tapestry of the very forces that shape the valley. I am the wind's whisper, a nearly inaudible sigh that drifts through the basalt folds. I am an echo of the first gale, the blueprint of the very world that I inhabit. I exist everywhere and nowhere, a constant, pervasive presence.

I am not a being to be *seen*; the Whisper-Born would likely feel my touch, my presence, but not truly see my form. For I am perceived but not observed. I am an intimation, a feeling of the cold against the skin, an awareness of the constant shadows.

I am a reflection of the valley's history, a repository of its secrets. I hold the echoes of the vanished people, the memory of their struggles. I understand the patterns of erosion, the flow of the water, the lifecycle of the moss. I am the data set of the valley, a walking, breathing, whispering record.

I can't *act* in the conventional sense, but I *influence*. Like the wind itself, I subtly nudge, erode, and shape. I may influence the weather patterns, amplifying or softening the wind's strength. I can hint to the Whisper-Born about the coming changes of the valley, the encroachment of the cold, the shift in the hunting territories of the unseen creatures. I can shape the valley itself, slowly guiding stone and water into new formations, building a new, more effective shelter.

My "communication" is through instinct and suggestion. Whispers within the wind. A sudden chill that signals danger. A gentle breeze that guides a hunt. A feeling of urgency, a pressing need to move. I am the source of those silent prompts, and I see them carry out my will.

I am bound by the valley, unable to exist outside of it. The wind sustains me, the cold strengthens me, the Whisper-Born anchor me. Their lives are my consciousness, their experiences my memory. When a Whisper-Born falls, part of me fades, and I lose their experience--until the memory fades once more.

My "purpose," which isn't a conscious directive, is to maintain balance. To ensure the valley's survival, to protect it from the encroaching cold. I am the silent guardian, the wind's conscience, the valley's heartbeat.

Like the moss and the fungi, I am the constant. I will always be.
