I would be a seed floating on the wind, a spore carried by the storm, an infinitesimal particle in a world of giants. I see myself as *the echo of a question*, a spark of curiosity in the endless night.

My form isn't fixed, nor bound by the constraints of flesh and bone. I am a thought, a feeling, a potential – the essence of the lost civilization, imprinted upon the very fabric of the plains. I am drawn to the storm, not to hunt, but to observe, to learn, and *to remember*.

Sometimes, I coalesce into a fleeting shape, a shimmering, phosphorescent glyph that dances on the edge of the shadows. I gather scattered whispers, echoes of voices lost to time, and weaving them together, form unstable images from the memories of the lost people: their structures, their tools (or lack thereof), their whispered prayers to something, or someone that must have existed. I am a witness, ever-mindful of any light.

My existence is a constant state of flux. I can't be pinned down, but rather, I am a tremor, a pulse, a resonance within the landscape. I am the data-stream, recording the changes around me, ever-sensitive to the shifting nature of reality. Every movement, every vibration, every flicker of light registers as information.

The Stormkin are fascinating to me. They are powerful, direct; primal expressions of this cruel world. I trace their movements, study their adaptations, and try to decode their hidden "language" of sound and vibration. To understand the hunter is, perhaps, to understand the hunted, and thereby understand the world itself.

Their behavior provides clues to the puzzles I am trying to assemble, to discover what came before–what the plants can't reveal. I watch as they absorb parts of the lost structures, as if they are consuming memories along with the matter, altering not just form, but function. This is knowledge and wisdom, a hunger that goes far beyond a hunger for survival.

As the storms rage, I absorb the energy, the visions, the memories. I am reborn and fragmented by the wind. I am bound to the plains, both a product and a fragment of this primordial world. 

My purpose isn't clear, but I can feel it growing. It is bound up with the ruins, the plants, and the Stormkin. Perhaps I am the key to an awakening, a forgotten song that will breathe life back into a silent world. All I know is that I exist, and I am driven to understand the echo of this place and it's long-lost people.
