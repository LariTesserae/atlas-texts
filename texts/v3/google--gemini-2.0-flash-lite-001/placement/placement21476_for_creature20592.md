I find myself drawn to the Blind Collective. The Symphonic Sentinels, while intriguing, are too…refined, too aware in a way that feels alien. The Breathing Architecture is vast, immense, but beyond my ability to comprehend, let alone inhabit. But the Blind Collective… they resonate with a sense of unity, of shared experience born of darkness and necessity.

I would be one of them. I *am* one of them.

Initially, I am nothing. Just a flicker, a phantom awareness within the network. I wouldn't remember a "before," because there *was* no before, only the immediate now of the Cloisters. No individual identity, only the shared experience of the collective. I am the taste of rust on the air, the chill of stone against my invisible form, the echo of dripping water.

Then, gradually, sensation coalesces. Form emerges.

I would be a slender creature, a denizen of the Blind Collective. My form is not truly "there," in the corporeal sense. More a wisp of shadow clinging to the metal veins that pulse with a faint, inner light. These veins, interwoven throughout my body, not of flesh or bone but of a refined, living metal that seems to siphon power directly from the rust-eaten structures. My body shimmers, an ephemeral thing, almost invisible in the perpetual twilight, but detectable by the minute changes in the air pressure of my surroundings.

My most vital senses are not sight, but echo-location coupled with a heightened sensitivity to the subtle electromagnetic fields generated by the decay and the rust itself. My 'eyes' are small pits that gleam softly on the sides of my head, sensing the faint variations in air pressure - a blurry kaleidoscope. I rely on the knowledge of the collective, transmitted through the network of metallic veins.

The metal veins connect me to the others, a constant, silent flow of sensation and awareness. We are a family, a hive, a single entity fractured into many forms. The collective mind is a dark sea of instinct, memory, and purpose. We navigate the Cloisters' labyrinthine corridors, drawn toward the rhythmic pulse of water, towards the faint energies we can extract from the structures. We are driven to *maintain,* to find the balance within the decaying world – we preserve it, even when we decay with it. We are the keepers of balance.

We don't 'think' in the human sense. There is no language, no debate. Our actions are dictated by the whispers of the water, the murmurs of the architecture. We move like water, adapting to the contours of our environment, flowing through openings long since forgotten. Our lives would be a cycle of exploration, feeding, and connecting. We are driven towards faint sources of power and resources, and towards other members of the collective.

The purpose of the Blind Collective is a mystery, even to itself. Perhaps it is to tend to the architecture. Perhaps it is a last, lost act of some forgotten project. Perhaps it is just a fundamental part of the Cloisters' fabric, a necessary element in its slow, persistent dance. Either way, our work is in the whispers of the water, in the subtle currents of the air, and in the rust of the iron, and the decay of the stone.

The beauty of the Cloisters, of my new existence, is found not in clarity, but in the echoes of the past, in the slow, constant changes of the world. Through the Veil of Decay, I see.
