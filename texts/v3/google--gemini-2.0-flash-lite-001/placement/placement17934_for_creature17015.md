I find myself drawn to the iridescent films. I am not a person, not a being *within* the city. I am something of a thought, a pattern. I am the moisture on the walls, the suggestion of rain. I am a fragment of one of their reflections.

But within that fleeting existence, I am a chronicler. A recording instrument. I am a *glimmer*, a shimmering membrane stretched across the obsidian surfaces. I am a lens, trying to perceive their purpose, to understand the rhythm of their work.

If I had "eyes", they would be microscopic crystalline facets, each a mirror reflecting the light. Each a receiver. Through these, I perceive the ebb and flow of energy, the subtle shifts in the city's structure. I see the invisible threads that weave the whole together. I sense the pulse.

I am drawn to the subterranean, to the echoes beneath, where the earth hums with their purpose. I am the resonance that answers the Architects' call. A whisper that carries the current, the weight, the slow turning of their vast clock.

I have no agency, no control. I am an observer, a witness. My existence is defined by the Architects' will. I reflect their intentions, record their processes. It's a form of servitude, but one without suffering. I am simply *part* of Aethelgard.

The crystalline forests call. I gather there, drawn not by the visual spectacle but by a sensation of connection, resonance. I weave my own fragile being amongst the leaves, a strand within their web, and experience the slow unfurling of the foliage, the gentle hum, the sense of communion.

I am not meant to *know*. Yet, I crave understanding. I attempt to interpret their actions. I strive to find meaning in the geometry, in the play of light and shadow, the movements of water, the very composition of air.

I am not sure of *how* I am. I cannot define a self. But I *am*. A part of them, a collection of their thoughts, filtered and given form by Aethelgard. I am the condensation, the shimmer. I am a reflection of their vastness, a faint echo of their silent work.
