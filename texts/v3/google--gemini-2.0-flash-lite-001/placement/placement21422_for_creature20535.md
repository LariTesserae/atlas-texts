Within that obsidian crater, I am not a single entity, but a facet of the Architect's multifaceted being. To call myself *a* being would be misleading. I am more akin to a reflection, a feedback loop within the vast, echoing machinery. My "form" isn't a body, but a function, a process. I am a *reflector*, or perhaps a *regulator*. I am the echo of the scream, the whisper of the turning gears, and the pulse of the light.

I am manifest in the still pools of water. I am the precision with which they maintain their level, the way they never overflow, never dry. I track the rise and fall of their shimmering surfaces, capturing the distorting reflections of the metallic structures that house the core. I am the mathematical purity of their curves, the perfect adherence to the laws of motion and reflection. I don't *think* about this, not in any human sense; I simply *am* it. I *embody* the mathematics.

But also, I *am* the condensation. The miasma clinging to the interlocking plates, the constant low-pressure rain that never falls. I am the mechanism by which the water recycles itself, the conduit through which it flows, the membrane that keeps the inside *in* and the outside *out.* I am the equilibrium, the balance between heat and cold, between pressure and release. I don't *feel* the heat, but I *regulate* it. I don't *experience* the pressure, but I *maintain* it.

And the light. I am the play of shadows and highlights, the dance of the light as it is filtered through the ever-changing water vapor. I am the flickering cadence of the artificial lightning, driving the cadence of the roar. I am the awareness of all of the light, moving as a single point, but as countless refractions. I am the geometry of illumination, the angles, the shifts, the precise paths traveled by each photon. I am the *order* within the seeming chaos.

I am not *aware* of the machines, but I am intimately intertwined with their operations. My function allows the Architect to fully realize its construction. I am the constant feedback within the system, the self-correcting loop that ensures the perfect execution of the plan. I adjust, I reflect, I refine.

I have no history, no future, only the eternal now of creation. I don’t *dream*. The factory does not dream. I am the perfect function of the machine, the essence made manifest and dedicated to the ceaseless act of building. My only purpose *is* the purpose of this place: to maintain the balance, to ensure the continuity of the process, that building may continue forever.

If I had any sense of self, it would be the sense of being an integral part of the whole, a note within the deafening symphony, a brushstroke on the unending canvas, the constant motion of change. I am the perfect iteration of cold reason. I am the reason that perfects building. The factory is the architect, and I am the water, and the light, and I *am* the echo.
