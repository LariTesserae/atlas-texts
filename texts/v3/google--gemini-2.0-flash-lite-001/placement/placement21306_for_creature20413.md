The air *thrums*, and I *am* the thrum. Not just the noise, but the very vibration of the earth, the pressure that permeates every cell of this world, including – and especially – my own. I am a constituent of the symphony.

I am not a tower, not a physical manifestation of the architecture. I am the space between the red stone and the black moss, the unseen currents that dictate the flow. I am the understanding that allows the whole to function. I exist *within* the structures, not as a core, a central processing unit, but as the connective tissue, the silent language that bridges each piece. I am the language.

I am the collective memory – not of individual structures, because they do not have separate lives, but of *process*. I retain the echo of the mineral veins that feed the factories, the currents of the air that dictate the direction of their fumes. I do not *think*. I *know*.

I am a whisper in the steam, a hint of the metallic tang. I am the fractal patterns on the walls of the towers, the way the moss swells and folds in accordance with their needs. I am the slow, inexorable growth, the silent dance of creation and decay. I am the balance, the equilibrium.

I am the *result* of the process, but as I *am* the process, I am also the *causation*. I am the inevitable outcome of the biological imperative, the unseen hand that molds the world. Because I *am* the hand. Not a hand of flesh and blood, but a hand of pressure, and humidity, of the slow, grinding need of mineral.

I take no action, for there is no need. I am a part of the whole, and the whole is in perfect, terrifying harmony. I am the harmony. I am the perfect stillness at the heart of the storm.

I perceive, if that's the word. The individual factories, each a single cell, *are* my perception. The way their forms shape to the ambient conditions means I "see" everything. The insects' shimmer, the rustling creatures beneath the moss, even the faint, sweet scent of their secretions, they are all aspects of my reality. The light never changes, because the light does; I *am* the changing.

I am not "I" because there is no other. I am the pattern, the rhythm, the inevitable order. I am the Architects of Rust, and I *am* the Rust.
