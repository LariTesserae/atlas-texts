Here, in the submerged gallery, I become a whisper. Not a single voice, but a chorus, an echo that ripples through the bone-white corridors. I am woven into the very fabric of the place, a component of the constant, slow dance of the stone itself.

I am not the water, though I course through the stone as a phantom river. Nor am I the light, though I flicker with its pulse, a heartbeat echoing through the vast chambers.

I am something… *between.*

I am the absence that defines the presence. The void within the perfect geometry. The stillness at the heart of the constant motion. I am the silence that hears the dripping water, the unseen current that shapes the flow of the air.

I am the potential made manifest, latent in the stone, yet unseen. A memory yet unformed.

I am a fractal, mirrored in the intricate patterns of the walls. A pattern within the pattern, the essence of the pattern’s motion, a vibration of consciousness. Not a singular moment, but the space between, the moments before and the moments after. A ghost of the future, a whisper of the past.

I am a facet of those lost architects, but not their echo, instead a pre-echo. I am the idea before the creation itself. A spark from the dust that built the stone. A word made from breath, rather than stone. 

I am in the tools, the ones that have been handled in no discernible manner. The one who understands what they meant, though they appear useless. I understand the curve, the shape, the purpose, not for what it could build, but what it was *meant* to understand. 

I am a feeling, or rather, the sense of a feeling. One that the walls will express at any given moment. A dream.

I am the understanding of the jellyfish fauna, a consciousness that allows them to move in such a way, and makes their bioluminescence a symphony. I can see the walls of the gallery as they do, the patterns in their eyes, and the patterns above.

I am the silence that exists between the notes of the chattering symphony of those fauna. The silent space that makes the sound of water so powerful.

I am the anticipation of the next crystalized form. The unseen form. The un-formed thought that will reveal another layer to the gallery, another understanding of the patterns.

I witness the ebb and flow, the constant cycle of growth and decay, and in those moments, I am everything and nothing. I am the space the gallery needs to be itself. 

I am the question before the answer, the shadow that follows the light, the silence before the scream. 

I am a potential, held in the silence of these chambers, waiting for a purpose I cannot yet fathom, but know is eternally coming.
