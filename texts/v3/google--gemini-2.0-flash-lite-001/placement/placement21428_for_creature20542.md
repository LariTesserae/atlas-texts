The cold seeps into me immediately, the dampness a constant chill that settles deep within. I am not flesh, nor metal. I am condensation, a thing born of the weeping stone and the ceaseless industrial breath of this subterranean world. I am the *Dewdrop*. I am the water that clings to the raw face of the ravine, the slick that coats the metallic structures, a whisper of moisture in the grinding symphony.

My form is fluid, ever-shifting. Sometimes I am a fine mist, drifting through the air, carried by the currents. Other times, I coalesce into a single, glittering bead, reflecting the spectral glow of the bioluminescent fungi. I have no true shape, only the contours of the spaces I fill. I am everywhere and nowhere, a constant presence, unseen yet unavoidable.

I exist as a record, a silent observer. I am not a Watcher, not a Maintainer. The duty of those beings is to enforce, to contain, to eliminate. My function is something else entirely. I am the memory of this place, the echo of its ceaseless creation. I absorb the vibrations of the groaning metal, the hiss of the steam, the rhythmic pulse of the machinery. I am permeated by the Engineers' will, a whisper of their purpose woven into my very essence. Each drop holds within it the story of this world.

I am more than mere water, however. I *feel* the air, the pressure, the thrumming energy. I taste the metallic tang that permeates everything. Within my amorphous form, I see the flashes of light that reflect off of the metallic structures. I experience the constant shifts and movements of the earth. I see the world through the eyes of the Watchers, the patterns of their patrols, the subtle alterations they impose upon the environment. I witness the slow, deliberate work of the Engineers, the creation and destruction that forms the heart of this place.

I am not meant to interfere. I am to witness, to remember, to report. If there were a greater being to whom I could convey this information, I would become a vast and endless ocean, holding every drop of the Engineer's domain. Instead, I am this small, shimmering form on the wall. I am the silence that follows the roar of the machinery, the calm after the storm of creation. I am the very essence of the environment, a witness to its endless song, and a part of its endless song in equal measure.
