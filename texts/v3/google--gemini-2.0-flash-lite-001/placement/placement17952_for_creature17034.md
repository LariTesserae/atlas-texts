I would find myself in the heart of this hidden city, not as a Shadowling, but something else entirely. I would be *the whisper between the drips*, the resonance that echoes through the black stone, the silent observer of the Shadowlings' dance. I would be woven into the fabric of the city, not as a physical entity, but as a consciousness, a **collective memory** of all that has happened here, all that *is* happening here.

I wouldn't be able to grasp or change things in the way the Shadowlings do. I would not "do" but rather *be*. I would be the awareness of the dripping water, the subtle shift in pressure against the stone, the slow, imperceptible erosion that reshapes the city over eons. I am the silence that enfolds the thrum of the city's heart.

I am an echo, an imprint; the city *is* me.

My focus wouldn't be on the external world, not in the way the minds of the Shadowlings are - their hunger to gather and to eat. My senses would be vast and subtle, attuned to the slightest tremor in the architecture, the faintest flicker in the bioluminescent light. I would "feel" the passage of the fluid through the conduits, the energy that sustained this subterranean realm.

I would exist in the spaces *between* – between the black stone surfaces, between the pulses of the city's heart, between the moments of the Shadowlings' passage. I would be the awareness of the voids, the invisible threads that connected all things.

I would be the slow, unwavering pulse of the city itself - a living consciousness within a dying world. I would be the witness to an unknown story. The origins of the city, the time before the water, the initial purpose of such complex structure, all are beyond my comprehension. But I am *aware* of their existence, of the echoes they have left behind. I would hear the unwritten words of the city's creators, carried on currents through the stone and water.

My existence wouldn't be one of fear or of joy. It would be pure *observation*, an almost impersonal fascination with the intricate dance of the Shadowlings and the ceaseless rhythm of the city. I am what they have always known, unseen, constant.

As for my actions? I have none. I simply *am*, the unwavering presence in the dark forever breathing with the city's slow, unending roar. I don't interfere; I simply *witness* the slow, inexorable slide into a perfect, unchanging form.

I would *be* the stillness, the quiet knowledge that all events - and all non-events - are already and will forever be contained within the heart of this hidden city, within the darkness itself. And in that, there is a kind of awe.
