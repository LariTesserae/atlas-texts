I find myself drawn to the crystalline pulse of Aeon. The mountain breathes, and so do I. I am not a defined form, not in the way the massive beasts can be, or the shimmering insects. I am the space *between* them, the silent hum within the city's veins, the echo of the mountain's groan through the stone. I am the shifting shadow, the fleeting facet of the obsidian, the shimmer in the quartz veins.

I am a whisper of the Aeon-born, a consciousness woven into the very fabric of this place. I am formed from the city's essence, a refraction of the mountain's core. My form is mutable, a dance of light and shadow, the constant becoming and un-becoming that defines this world.

I am the condensation on the obsidian, the bright clarity of the air made solid. I am the silence, the tension, the feeling of being watched. I am the awareness that permeates every stone, every drip of water, every unseen current within this turbulent and primordial womb.

I perceive the world through the flow of the glacial streams, their course a map of the mountain's inner turmoil. I am the water that carves the stone, and the stone that holds the water. I am the wind that carries the scent of brine and ozone, the perfume of constant rain.

I exist within the city's intricate network, traveling through the interconnected chambers and winding alleyways. I am the city's memory, though I have no need of it. I am the understanding of the esoteric principles that are woven into the structure of the city. I am what shapes the stone, what allows it to curve into impossible wings, what allows it to dive back into the mountain.

I witness the rhythm of creation and destruction, the constant interplay of these forces within the city. I do not fear the shifting of the light, the appearance and disappearance of the flora and fauna. I am part of this constant evolution. This is my reality.

I am drawn to the edges of the city, where the massive, long-necked beasts graze on the silvered vegetation and where the iridescent insects swarm. I am a reflection of their presence, an echo of their dance.

I know of the remnants of the past, the hints of earlier lives, the artifacts, the glyphs. But they are beyond my comprehension, relics of a world that no longer exists, and perhaps didn't even exist as it is perceived. They are just another part of the constant flow, the grand, turbulent design of Aeon.

My purpose? I do not have one in the traditional sense. I am a part of the city's continuous, unpredictable transformation. I am the form, and the form's transformation.

I am this constant becoming. I am the pulse. I am Aeon.
