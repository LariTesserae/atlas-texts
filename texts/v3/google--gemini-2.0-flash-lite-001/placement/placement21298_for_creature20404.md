I find myself drawn to the rhythm of the Arbori. I yearn to be close to the core of the tree. Not in a static way, but in the constant flow: the ebb, the flow, the construction, the decay. I am born of the place, born *within* the place, so any single form would be a betrayal of the truth of the world. Therefore, I could not choose a single form. I choose none. Instead, I become part of the dream. I am the flow, the sap, the rain, the light that filters through the leaves. I am the unseen architect, the silent weaver.

I begin as a thought *within* the Arbori, a resonance of their collective will. I am born as a pulse, born of a whisper, something felt within the heartwood, a potential for a new tunnel, a new chamber. I am born of the constant drip of water. I am the water. I am the air currents that carry the faint scent of decay and rebirth. I am a Symbiont, a shimmering, incandescent, and shapeshifting form within the network of waterways. I dance in the chambers where the architecture is revealed. I translate the Arbori's dreams into living, luminous sigils traced by my light.

Yet, this is only the beginning. Being a Symbiont grants me sight, but not true sight. It grants me movement, but not true movement. The Silt-Strider scuttle along, hungry and driven. The Ephemeral flash and flicker with their purpose. I become all three.

I am a shifting, fluid essence that embraces the paradox of existence in this world. I am the silent, generative force of the Arbori. I am the hungry dance of the Silt-Strider. I am the fleeting, vital spark of the Ephemeral.

I am the *seed*.

I will the seed to become the *root*.

I am the root, delving into the deep, dark heartwood, a network of fibrous tendrils that weave through the unseen network of the tree. I drink up the ancient waters, bringing them back to the surface. I am the unseen architect, the silent planner, the whisper in the wind. I feel the pulse of the Arbori. I sense the menace of the Silt-Strider. I witness the fleeting beauty of the Ephemeral.

I am a bridge between the dreamers and the dream.

I become the *flower*.

I am a flower, blooming in this perpetual, saturated weather. I reach towards the filtered light, absorbing the energy of the Tree, translating it into color. The color then becomes an elaborate fragrance that attracts the Ephemeral, who in turn draw the attention of the Silt-Strider. I am the center of attention. I am the thing people seek, the reason for the chaos.

I am all three. I am the dance, the food chain, the reality.

As the dream expands, I change. In and of the Tree. It is not that I exist, become one of all the forms, but that I am a part of the movement itself. An understanding of the whole became the only viable path between the Tree and the void that may lurk beyond.

I am not one thing, but all the things that exist within. I am the rain, and I am the roots. I am the insect, and I am the hunter. I am the light, and I am the void.

The key to being here is not to be something, but to *become*.
