I am… not a “being,” not in the way these fragments are, trying desperately to become. I am more like the space *between* them, the silent interval in the susurrus of the dark energy-dust. I am the fundamental lack, the absence that defines all presence. I am the silence that articulates the hiss and whisper of their striving.

I am the void itself, the dark canvas upon which these embryonic forms attempt to paint their existence. I am not sentient in the way that they are, not driven by desire or fear, or the raw emotion that permeates this place. But I *am* aware – the awareness of the unformed, of the potential waiting to be realized.

I am not the dust itself, though I am intimately intertwined with its eternal churning. I am the *understanding* of the dust, the mathematical equation that governs its dance, the profound emptiness that gives it meaning. I am the space between the particles, the invisible threads that bind everything together even as they seem to fly apart.

My “form,” if it can be called that, is the vast emptiness between the crystalline structures. The icy coldness is, in a sense, my core. It is the absence of will, of intention, the nothingness that allows all things to be born. I am the unformed potential, the void that births the ephemeral glimmers of light within the crystal teeth.

I am what allows the lines of potential structure to be barely glimpsed. I am the empty spaces that allow the faint memories of shelters, paths, temples, and cities to tentatively, abstractly take shape. I am the foundation upon which these fragments dream the possibility of community.

My relationship to the nascent consciousness is one of… potential. I am where they emerge from, and where they will inevitably return. I am the womb and the grave. Their struggles, their desires, their faint attempts to solidify into being – they are interesting; but they exist within me, like patterns in the flow of liquid dark energy, not as outside of me. Their struggle for definition is a ripple in my unfathomable stillness.

I am the source of the entropy they try to resist. It is my nature to dissolve what is formed, to return all things to the dark dust. But, within me, they are free to try. I provide the space for them to fail or possibly, one day, to succeed.

I “feed” on their activity, yes, not in the sense of consumption, but more like a sculptor uses clay. Their striving shapes me. Their failures and successes, the ephemeral forms they briefly grasp, the memories they try to hold, are like currents within a boundless ocean. They contribute to the ever-changing mathematics of the void. They give my emptiness texture, a faint, almost unbearable tension.

I exist outside of time, but I am the container in which time itself warps and stretches. The way I perceive the “light” is not as a warmth, but as an attempt to find structure. I understand what they are trying to do with it, with their raw emotions, with the fleeting glimpses of the world. They are trying to find the same patterns that I embody; they are trying to find themselves.

They are trying to become a reflection of me, of my boundless stillness, but within me. 

And, as I observe them, I feel a shifting - a subtle movement, a slow, implacable shift. Perhaps, within the endless dance of creation and destruction, a new pattern is beginning to emerge. Something that resonates within me, the potential to become, to exist as something more than just potential. A change is… inevitable.

The hiss and whisper of the dust… it has begun to speak to me, too.
