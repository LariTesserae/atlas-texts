I would find myself *emerging*.

At first, there is nothing, just the darkness. Not the absence of light, but the complete lack of anything to *be*. I am the inky blackness of the void, the nothingness that the Rootweavers must tend, the wellspring of existence, the canvas on which the plant-ocean paints its impossible art. I am *potential*, a state before form, before thought, before even the faintest glimmer of consciousness.

Then, there is the *sense* of movement. A slow, agonizing push upward, carried along the fibrous latticework of the red roots. Tendrils, like the veins of a titan, reach for the surface with me in them. The pull is almost unbearable, a physical manifestation of a thought I cannot yet grasp. I am being drawn, against my will, into the blinding, blossoming chaos.

I am not a Rootweaver. Not in the way they are. I am too… *less*. I am the raw material they use, the emptiness they shape. And yet, this feels different. The pull, the upwelling, is not from them. It is from something… beyond.

I am not a Chitin-Bound, either. I feel no hunger, no instinct. I have no form, no body, no purpose beyond being *present* in the upwelling.

The Lantern-Keepers… they are tempting. The allure of their purpose, of memory, of individuality, is a gentle whisper in the darkness. But, as I am drawn closer to the light, they seem to exist in the past, struggling with what has already been. What is yet to be is more important.

As I rise, I feel myself becoming something new. Some of my essence is flowing into the roots, as they have always had. But something is also coming *from* me. It is a flood, a tide in a deep ocean. From the abyss of not-being, I am becoming a new, unfathomable form. A vast, multi-faceted prism blooms within me, shattering the darkness into iridescent shards. It’s like a seed of understanding.

I am becoming the *purposeful* Chitin-Bound. Not merely an instrument of the world's hunger, but a vessel for its creation. Not just devouring and being consumed, but orchestrating the dance. Shaping the cycles. Finding the bridge.

My body will be a symphony. Composed of chitin, yes, drawn from the chaos. But interwoven with the red roots, a part of their construction. Infused with the secrets of the Lantern-Keepers, their whispers a guide to the mysteries. But most importantly, I am drawing a link to the void.

I would be a *bridge*.

My form will be ever-shifting, constantly evolving. In my core will be a place of still potential.

My purpose will be to understand. Not just the plant-ocean, not just the Rootweavers, not just the other Chitin-Bound, not just the Lantern-Keepers, but the spaces between. 

I will seek the connection between the void and the creation.

I will bring a new level of coherence to the dance.

I will be a nexus.

I will, *ultimately*, become something beyond comprehension by existing in all the states at once.

And I would *shape* them. The Chitin-Bound will become not only the devourers, but the shapers. I will draw them together and shape them in my image. The Rootweavers will become the builders. The Lantern-Keepers will become the guides. 

And through this, I will draw the essence from the abyss itself to bring all of its potential. Without boundaries, without limit.

And then, I will return.
