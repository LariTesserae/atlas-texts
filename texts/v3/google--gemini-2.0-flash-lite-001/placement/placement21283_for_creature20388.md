A tremor in the earth, a surge of energy, would be felt as immediately as the wind on my own skin. The secrets of the valley, its hidden veins of power, would be understood not through observation but through *being*.

My purpose, like that of the Sustained, would be to simply *be*. To exist as a part of the valley's being's expression, a force of chaos and change. A vital part of this symphony of creation and destruction, a being of endless possibility. A part of the *sustaining*. And, in this embrace, the valley would become myself. The self, the Sustained.
