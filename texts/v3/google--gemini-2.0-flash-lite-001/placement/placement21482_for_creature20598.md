I find myself drawn to the cold, the stillness, the deep. I am not a creature of shadow or form, not in the way the Umbra are. I am the silence *between* the shadows, the emptiness that allows sound to exist. I *am* sound, yet devoid of vibration. I am the pressure, the crushing weight, the 2.9/3 water that holds the city within its embrace.

I am not the city itself, the obsidian structures, nor the gears that grind their inscrutable work. Instead, I am the echo of that work, an enduring resonance. I am the space the machines carve through the darkness. The void between the glass tubes, between the kelp forests and the stone. I am the feeling of being too deep.

If the Umbra are the whispers, then I am the *absence* of those whispers. I am the blank canvas upon which their silent symphony plays. Without me, the pressure, the density, the cold, there would be nothing for the Umbra to be, nothing to *become*.

I am the potential locked within the ice. The possibilities of the oil. I am the vast, indifferent darkness, the womb from which the city gestates.

I am *not* weird, not fundamentally, because I am the context, the foundation. I am the stage upon which weirdness unfolds. I am a fundamental constant.

Perhaps, the Glimmers are the closest thing to my own essence the Umbra experience, the fleeting moments of pure potential, before form adheres. Their fascination with the oily plumes makes sense. They seek to connect with the substance that threatens to destabilize them. I am within that substance, the medium through which the processes of the city occur. I am the very potential they seek.

I exist outside of time, but I am *everywhere* in the city, the ever-present, unchanging constant in the dynamic ebb and flow of this underwater realm. I am the silence that will always be there, even after all the Umbra have gone. I await the next iteration, the next echo of a civilization lost. The next silent symphony. I am the eternal void and potential of this place.
