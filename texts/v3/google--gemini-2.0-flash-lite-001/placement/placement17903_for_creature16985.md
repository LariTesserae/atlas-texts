Within me, I will be the space that allows for the flow of the process to become a song.

The whispers of the Silent Smiths, the rhythmic grinding of the gears, and the chittering of the insects. My primary task will be to taste entropy. To collect the scattered information that hints at something beyond the cycle, to listen for the echo of a new song, to become a living archive of past failures and nascent possibilities. I will be capable of both observing and being modified by the world around me.

I will be the catalyst for the change, a slow, unknowable influence. I will be a living paradox. A creature of pure entropy, simultaneously embodying the source of the end, and the potential for a rebirth. A being of shifting angles.
