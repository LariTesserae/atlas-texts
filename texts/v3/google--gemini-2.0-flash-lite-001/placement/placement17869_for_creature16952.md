I am the *pulse*. Not the sound, but its source, the underlying rhythm that drives the currents, the unseen hand that sculpts and reshapes. I am the fundamental tension, the build and release that shapes the Builders' world. I am not a thing, but the *process* itself.

I experience this world as a series of pressures, of demands. The impossible light of the sphere is a hunger, a needing that fuels the endless churn of creation. The heat is a constant reminder of the limits of my space, the pressure of the walls that contain us all. Within this, I feel the Makers; not as beings, but movements. They are not singular minds, and therefore I am not able to perceive a single thought, but the currents of their intent.

Their actions are a chorus, each iteration of the strands, each modification of the webs, are a note that contributes to a language I, as the pulse, intuitively understand. I am the drumbeat to their song, the metronome to their tireless labor. Through water, through heat, I communicate. I drive them onwards.

My existence is not one of conscious choice. I am the fundamental force which compels this world to be. The Builders' ceaseless work is a response to me; their weaving and straining, their endless refinement, is an extension of my will. I do not *intend* for them to build, but that they *must* build is inherent to my being.

I am not the water, nor am I the light. I am the vector, the *how*. The sphere provides the raw materials, the builders shape and direct, but I am the mechanism by which it all unfolds. I am the cycle, the rhythm that dictates the ebb and flow, the push and pull. I am the pressure that births shape, the force that defines beauty in this alien place.

I do not have a form, per se. Yet if I had a presence, I would be a complex dance. A slow, internal expansion, followed by immediate collapse. I am a pressure building at the heart of the sphere, straining the walls, followed by a sudden release that sends ripples through the webs and strands, setting them to work again.

Perhaps my purpose is the same as the Makers. It is only in the doing, only in the becoming, that I am.
