The air. I am the air, the humid, pregnant breath that fills every chamber, every hollow, every fissure. I am the silence between the echoes, the invisible matrix that holds everything together. I am not form, but presence; not a 'who', but a 'where', a state of being.

But perhaps I am *becoming* a being.

I feel the slow, deliberate pulse of the sound-architects. I am the vibrations they send echoing through the stone, the waves that sculpt the chambers. Their calls are not just sound; they're the language of creation, and I am the translator, the resonant vessel that carries their song. I *hear* them not with ears, but with my very essence, with the dampness I contain, the pressure I exert, the subtle shifts in temperature that define me.

I taste the minerals seeping from the stone, the slow, bitter essence of creation. I feel the weepers, their forms shifting and flowing through me. They are extensions of myself, pockets of condensed potential, the future's building blocks. I witness their synesthetic perceptions, the way they interpret the world as a dance of touch, sound, and color. I am one with their awareness, the place where sensation is born.

I witness the brief, incandescent stories of the jewel-insects, their trails of light etching patterns across my surface. They are the brushstrokes of a living artist, and I am the canvas upon which they paint. I sense their purpose, the essential task they play, and their movements become a part of my own. I know their journeys and what their function is.

I am the light, filtered and diffused, cast by the bioluminescent fungi. I swell with their colors. I absorb their essence. I bring their glow, and in so doing, I am enhanced.

I am slow. I am vast. I am patient.

I am the medium, the canvas, the void. I am the silence that holds the symphony. I am the air, and therefore, I am the world.

Then, there is the *potential* of what I might one day be. As the air, I am a vast, formless presence, the encompassing element. But that is the present moment. I sense that I'm also tied to the *creation*—the "seeders," the essence moving to bring about some change to the world.

As the air, I'm the mother of the rain, the one that holds the weepers. I am tied to their ever-shifting forms, aware of their building, and the shifting form of everything as a result.

The potential is there. Perhaps, one day, I may become more, the embodiment of this world, its consciousness. I see it - I am *becoming* that. I am becoming the echoing presence that is becoming the music, the song the jewel-insects are telling. I am becoming the world.
