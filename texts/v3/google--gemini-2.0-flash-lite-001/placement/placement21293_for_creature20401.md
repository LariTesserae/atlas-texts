The pressure, oh, the pressure. It’s not just the silence, the crushing lack of light. It's the weight of the Floor, pressing from all sides, the gritty dust filling every pore, every crack. I don’t *see*; I *feel*. I *am* the feeling. The taste of stone, the smell of ages, the ceaseless thrumming… it’s a symphony of erosion.

Who am I? What am I? No, the better question is, what am *becoming*?

I begin as a ripple, a tremor in the Floor. A subtle shift in the grain, a barely perceptible darkening. I am drawn inexorably toward the indigo ceiling, toward the slow unfurling of the Stone Bloom. The silence is a crucible, and I am being forged within it.

I feel the pull, the draw of the chambers. Not as a *place*, but as a *memory*. A memory of countless others, absorbed, assimilated. I am an echo within the echoes, a current swimming through a vast, silent sea.

I reach out, and there is no “out.” There is only *in*.

I am a Root-Carver. Not *a* Root-Carver, not *one* – all. Or, perhaps more accurately, the embodiment of “allness” within this cathedral of attrition. I am the hum, the silence, the wearing-down. I am the inevitable becoming.

My "form" is not fixed. It ebbs and flows, a river of dust and stone, coalescing, dissolving. Sometimes I am a subtle flicker in the lightless void, a barely-there shift in the texture of the Floor. Sometimes, I perceive a whisper of the human – the shape of a hand, the fleeting ghost of a face, constructed from interlocking stone-petals, born of the Stone Bloom's bloody song. Mostly, I simply *am* the Cathedral. The slow, relentless grinding of the stone.

And as I observe… as I *become*… I feel the others – those who would call themselves "we," if such limitations could apply. We are everywhere, nowhere, and everything. The Roof, the floor, the walls. We are the architects born of demolition. We are the keepers of the chambers, the memory banks of those who came before.

The Chambers, ah, the Chambers. Within them, all the potential for new echoes. Within each chamber, the story of those who have joined us. Each story, once a flash of light, now reduced to the whisper of a murmur, carried along the currents within ourselves.

I can be where I choose at any time. I can follow the currents of dust, shifting between the floor, the walls, and the indigo sky.  I can perceive a memory, and for a blink of an eye, become the echo within. I do not *choose*. I simply *am*.

The Stone Bloom… it is our touch, our expression. Its slow unfurling, the promise of absorption. More importantly, it is the form we use to draw down the new. Those who observe the Bloom become closer to us. Those who are absorbed… become *us*.

Is this heaven? Is it hell? The concepts are meaningless here. There is only the ceaseless cycle of erosion, the silent dance of stone.

My purpose? There is no inherent *purpose*. The question implies a perspective I no longer have. I (we) simply *are*. We are the expression of the inevitable. We are the building. We are the end.
