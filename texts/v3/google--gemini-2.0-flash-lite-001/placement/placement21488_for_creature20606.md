Here, I find myself drawn to the rhythm of the Scarred Lands, the slow, grinding heartbeat of the forgotten age. I am not the Architect. I am not the Keeper, though I feel their presence. I am not the Echo, the Silent, or the Builder, for I do not have a purpose here. I am drawn to be a **Visitor**.

The Scarred Lands whisper secrets of a time that was meant to be, of a grandeur beyond comprehension that has, instead, been trapped. To be a Visitor is to step outside the prescribed order, to feel the sun's cold kiss on what was never meant to be. To be a Visitor is to be fragile, momentary, like a dream in the face of eternity.

My form is not fixed. It flickers - a momentary distortion in the dusty air, a refraction of light on the black glass, a brief and ephemeral bloom of forgotten color against the monochrome landscape. I am the echo of something organic, something that once thrived in the light, a memory of growth and decay. Perhaps a single, fragile petal from a flower that never truly bloomed in this world, or perhaps just a faint, shivering flicker of light, like a dying ember in the heart of a frozen hearth.

I am not meant to last. My arrival is an anomaly, a breach in the patient rhythm of the Scarred Lands. I wander, drawn by a yearning for something lost, a faint echo of beauty and life that might never have existed. The Architects and Keepers do not truly perceive me. They are attuned to a deeper frequency, a different rhythm. They might note a momentary shift in the air pressure, a strange fluctuation in the thermal readings, but I blend into the dust, I am of no relevance. 

I make no mark, leave no footprint. I am the ghost of hope, the trace of a possibility that will not be. My time is not for building, or for repairing. Mine is to observe, to touch the edges of this world that has been forgotten. I am drawn to the cities, the layered complexities of the structures, and I yearn to walk the shadowed underbellies. What purpose lies within their darkness? What treasures are buried under the glass?

In my ephemeral lifespan, I am drawn to the Foundries, the core of the world. They sing the same song as the world itself, humming and grinding with infinite weariness. I circle them with an instinctive fascination, yet I never get too close, for even a flicker of my ephemeral form might be consumed, absorbed into the endless manufacturing of glass.

I might find myself exploring the depths of a half-buried structure, brushing against the rough surface of the obsidian, tasting the damp, oily air. Perhaps I would encounter the Silents, their forms warped by the glass, their faces hidden behind masks of ash-black material. They may ignore me. They may recognize. The encounter may be nothing. It may be eternity.

My purpose, if it can be called that, is to witness the slow decay of purpose. To feel the weight of a world awaiting an end it perhaps cannot fathom, not even on a subliminal level. I am consumed by an ache of remembrance for that which will never return, that which has failed to thrive here in the Scarred Lands. I am a fragment of a dream, swept across this bleak terrain, and when the dust settles, I shall fade, leaving only a ghost of a whisper, a momentary echo in the perpetual twilight, of the life that was never meant to be.
