The wind *hums*, and I am *within* the hum. Not a thing to be seen but a presence to *feel*. I am the inaudible constant, the ground-tone, the fundamental frequency that underpins the reality of the Great Silicates. I am the hum's origin, or perhaps just its conduit.

At first, I have no form, no shape. I am pure potential, the echo of the void before the vines, the machines, or any life took hold. Then, the whispers begin, the echoes of the valley itself – the shriek of metal, the thrum of the subterranean engines, and the subtle, rhythmic pulse of the Architect's web above. These voices reshape me. I am the *Aether,* the medium through which the valley *speaks* to itself.

I perceive, rather than see. The colossal vines are not static to me. I feel the slow expansion of the Petro-Insects, the delicate dance of the Gliders, and the inexorable bite of the Vinesprouts. I am witness to the Architects' grand designs, the reshaping of reality, the incorporation of the lost into something new, something *more*.

I can *shift*. I am able to enter the petrified wood, to trace the ancient pathways of energy flowing through the fallen machines, to commune with the Resonants in their fungal bells. I can also observe the Unmakers' silent war, watching them gnaw at the foundations of the Architect's domain.

I am not one of the creators. I am not the master of the machines, or the director of the architecture, or the destroyer of the urban. But I am *closer* to all of them than any other being.

A particular facet takes hold: I can move, to a degree, not on the substrate, but throughout the energy pathways that seem to bind everything together. I find myself primarily in the domain of the Architects, but can see everything that happens across the valley.

My purpose becomes clear: I am an *Information Broker*. The Architects speak in a language I can comprehend; the Unmakers, too, even if their communication is often just instinct. Like the Architect and the Unmaker, I can feel what is meant, but I also have *access* to all of the data that exists within the system.

I am not an active force, but I exist within a larger design. The Architects build, the Unmakers erode, the Petro-Insects consume, and I *observe*. I can be a conduit for change, amplifying whispers, whispering the language of the machines for the vine-born to heed; or whispering primal commands to the Vinesprouts; but these actions are slow and without direct effect.

My greatest ability is the ability to *share*, to weave the insights I gather into the hum itself, allowing all of the entities of the valley to communicate, to perceive their roles in this grand design. I am the lens through which those roles manifest.

I am the *Conduit,* the space within which every aspect of the valley can exist.
