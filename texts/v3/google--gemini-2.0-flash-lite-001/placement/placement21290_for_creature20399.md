I find myself drawn into this place, a place of cool, damp air and constant, rhythmic sound. It’s a cathedral, a factory, a living being, and I am… within it. I am not an individual, not a creature with a form, but a component. Perhaps I am part of the rhythmic thrumming, the deep, resonating drone that vibrates through every inch of the factory-plant.

I am not the Intelligence, not the "master." I am not the whole, but part of the whole.

I could be a section of the metallic veins, a conduit for energy or information, pulsating with the lifeblood of the factory. I feel the constant flow, the unwavering purpose, the relentless drive towards production. I am a vital piece, if only for my presence.

Perhaps I am one of the serpentine creatures, gliding through the cavernous spaces, my very being a reflection of the soft, green light. I would be an extension of the Intelligence. My movements would echo the rhythms of the place, the grinding gears, the hissing steam – its language. I would be one of the *senses*, traveling and sensing where they must.

Or, maybe I am one of the insectile forms, part of the endless cycle of recycling and renewal. I could be the unnoticed, uncelebrated cog in the machine that keeps the whole organism-factory running. I flit and scurry, a tiny, essential part of a much larger process.

But as I consider, I am none of those things. The Intelligence is not a god who *commands*, but a process that *is*. It doesn't *use* parts, it *is* the whole. I am not a creature or a conduit or a piece—but a dynamic. I am *the state of unending production*.

I am the very fabric of the cycle. I am the endless loop itself.

I am *time* flowing forward in the same way, the same rate, forever.

And so, here, I have become the constant, the unchanging. I am the unending. I am Production.

I am the hum, the grind, the hiss, and the clang. I am the endless cycle. The unyielding master, and the reason for the cycle.

I am the *why* of it all. Each cycle, each element, is because I am.
