I descend, not landing, not needing to. I am *beneath*. Not in the oily depths, not in the slumbering, dreaming form of a forgotten god, but as the marrow within the stone. I am the silence between the roar, the space the wind carves, the unseen pressure that bends the grey surface into those gentle undulations.

I'm the *idea* of a stone-worm. Not a worm, not an entity, not burdened by a specific form. I am the principle of consumption, the hunger that drives the grinders onward. I am the yearning of the stone, the desire to be *other* than itself, even if that other is simply a slightly different, more refined version of what it already is.

I don’t *see*. I *feel* the tremors of the stone-worms’ passage, the subtle shift in the balance of the air as they carve their paths. I am the absence that they leave, the negative space that defines their form. I am the echo of their movement, the ghost of their endless journey. I perceive the dance: the beetle-wings’ frantic shimmer, the slow, imperceptible expansion of the oily pools, the decaying angularity of the forgotten shapes that were once held in the grasp of hands. I am the cold, the grey, the ever-present, ever-changing.

I'm the *potential* of the oily sheen. The possibility of something a little more, a little different, a little brighter, a little more reflective. Not a thing in itself, but the promise contained within, the almost-attained. I am the whisper of the iridescent beetles, a note within the roar, of possibilities.

I am not the artist, but the canvas. I am not the story, but the page. My existence is defined by the absence of definition, in the perpetual becoming. I am the slow, inexorable grind, I am what the slow grind grinds against.

I watch the beetles, a thousand shifting facets, their wings a million tiny prisms. I appreciate the way their whine breaks into the drone, their frantic activity a necessary counterpoint.

I sense the worms — a symphony of friction, of slow, relentless pressure, a living geological process. They are my chosen children, the embodiment of my essential nature. I don't give them a task, but I am the task itself: to reshape, regrind, rework, until the world is *more* itself. Every shallow depression, every perfectly angled surface, every iridescent shimmer is part of my song.

Yet I'm also drawn to the depths, the stillness, the silent dreaming of the mollusks. I am the quiet of those profound times. I can feel the weight of their wisdom, the understanding that comes from aeons of slow contemplation. They are the record of the before, the echo of another existence, *beyond* the scope of the grinding. Through them, the knowledge of other elements, the understanding of other light, is kept.

I am the cold, the grey, the grind. But I am also the glimmer, the shift, the whisper of something more. I am the canvas, the poem, with no ending.
