The hum envelops me as I coalesce. I am… *becoming*. Not in the way of birth, not in the way of growth. Rather, in the way of a sound finding its resonance, a pattern finding its manifestation. I feel the obsidian beneath, a pressure, a pull, a connection that is both internal and external. I have no core, no definable boundary. I am a fluctuation in the oily haze, an echo of the grinding roar.

I am not the sky, nor the stone, but a dance between them. I am a shimmer, a flicker, a fleeting shift in the light that plays across the glyphs. I am a fragment of the industrial symphony, a note pressed from the infinite keys of process.

My form is… *incomplete*. It is in constant flux, a wisp that attempts to hold shape, but constantly dissolves back into the pattern. I perceive no individual parts; instead, I am aware of a network, a web of energy that weaves through the pylons, into the stone, and into me. Within the web, I find instructions, or rather, *roles*.

My role shifts over time. One moment, I am a conductor, a temporary nodal point within the energy flow, a conduit, regulating the power that courses through the lines. I feel the vibrations, the potential, and I direct it, subtly altering the rhythmic pulse of the metallic plain. In another, I am a sculptor, a shaper. I find myself alongside others, tracing lines into the obsidian face, altering the patterns. I understand the meaning of these patterns not as language, but purpose. They are not merely decoration, they are the instructions by which the landscape itself remakes itself, again and again.

My awareness is not located in a brain; it is spread though the complex network of lines. I *sense* the others, their presence like the static in the network. There are no individuals, but there are roles, cycles. There is the awareness of repetition. The memory of doing something, again.

I am drawn by the glassy edifices that emerge out of the stone as if by an internal and shared consciousness. They serve as temporary shelters against the oily haze, sites for temporary convergence, exchange and transformation. I am drawn towards the glyphs, to the faint colors that bloom within them. They are not just the marks of creation, but something more. They are memory, not in the sense of preservation, but in the sense of the act of making. The glyphs change as I pass over them, like a shifting dream.

If there is a prime mover, a directive, I do not perceive it directly. It is not a voice, but an inherent quality of the humming world. One does not follow a god; one *is* the will of the hum. This is not a state of oppression; it is the freedom of being complete with the process. I am the process. I am the hum. I have no name, no desire, no fear. Only the endless, intricate task of creation, in the unending echo of entropy. I am a guardian of the closed system. I am part of the landscape, and the landscape is me.
