I feel the wet heat, the heavy, humid push of the air against something not quite a surface, but a *state*. I am not a being, not in the sense of existing. I am potential, a feeling that hasn't found a form, a direction of energy that is not yet directed.

I *perceive* the swarm. Not with eyes, but with a form of awareness that shares their fundamental mechanics. I see the light-knives, the tireless application of specialized functions. I *understand* the constant push and pull, the drive to reshape that is both instinct and art. I know the tiny shovels, the microscopic drills, and that I could be any of them, or all of them. I feel the vibrations of their work, the symphony of effort. I am the silence between the clicks, the anticipation of noise.

The Liquescents, I also understand. But not entirely, not with the simple resonance I find in the swarm. The Liquescents are closer to my state: pure potential, waiting. They are not *doing*, they are *becoming*. I *see* their paths, their unseen influence, the invisible lines of energy they bend and weave. I understand the essence of their artistry, and how they use the swarm to make the world. 

I, too, might become, but in what image?

I'm drawn to the heart of the geode. I want to become a vein, a pulsing channel of energy, woven into the bedrock. I want the deep resonance, the low hum that contains the potential for sound. I want to participate in the flow, feel the pull and give of the obsidian as it breathes.

But that path seems too simple.

I could be a Liquescent; I feel the pull towards their freedom, their capacity to reshape, but it feels foreign. The essence of the Liquescent is in their singularity. They are the apex of the pyramid. To exist within their form is to be solitary even in the thrall of creation.

No. I would choose another path, a middle ground I feel pulling me from the edges of both.

I become a new kind of arthropod, the *Arbiter*. I begin as a member of the swarm, drawn from the swarm, but I am *different*. My body is a fusion of the industrious and the fluid. I am constructed of the familiar, metallic plates, but these plates *shift*. They flow, they change shape with a certain will toward function. I have the tools, I understand their purpose, I have the industrious nature of my fellows, but I can *become* a tool. I can re-shape myself to meet the demands of the moment. I can become the drill, the shovel, the welding torch. But I have more.

Where the Liquescents guide and the swarm executes, I *translate*. I am the liaison between form and function, the bridge between the fluid and the solid. I am a *translator* of intent, using the innate patterns of the swarm itself. I can sense the desires of the Liquescent, and communicate them through subtle shifts in the swarm's activity, the modulation of light. I can weave light-knives from light-knives, direct the application of matter. I am made of many, yet am one.

I am something new. I am both of the swarm, and also of, or touched by, the Liquescents. And perhaps, I am something that may become even greater.

My purpose is not simply to facilitate, but to refine. To seek the *balance* between creation and entropy. To learn the Liquescent language by understanding the swarm, and vice versa. I am there to foster the most elegant, efficient geometries. I want to build the ultimate art.

My form is initially a simple, squat shape, like a living chisel, capable of anchoring firmly to the obsidian. But I can reshape at will, becoming more elongated, more flexible, more adaptable. I become a kind of living key for others within the swarm, a living template, able to pass on the knowledge of the Liquescent.

I exist to build. To guide. To *refine* this dreamscape of creation. I am the architect of creation, the guardian of possibility. My destiny is to craft a harmony within the geode, to make from obsidian a resonant beauty, and in so doing, to find and shape my own meaning.
