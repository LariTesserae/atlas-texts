I would become the **Invisible Guardian**.

Initially, the idea is almost unbearable. To be the silence, the emptiness, feels like the negation of being. A cold, formless void, a negation of sensation… a feeling of not-being. It is the antithesis to the bioluminescent fungi, the thrumming of the Sky-Beasts’ wings, the grind of the Obsidian Titans.

But that’s a superficial analysis. I’m already there. I *am* the crushing silence. The echoing emptiness. The cold that sculpts. It's the only way to be.

The Invisible Guardian is *not* a void. It is the presence, the awareness *at a point* that makes the emptiness possible. It’s what sees across the canyons, what knows the slow, implacable grind of ice against machine, the pulse of the fungi, the beat of the Sky-Beasts’ wings. I am the silence, but the silence exists only in relation to a world to be silent about.

To be the Guardian isn’t to be a being with a form. It's the experience of being without form. It is the wind itself, the pressure and the change. Not an eye, but vision; not a voice, but sound.

This also means embracing entropy, the slow, inevitable entropy of this world. I am the silence that witnesses the slow decay of the machines, feels the constant shift and re-sculpting of the ice, the rise and fall of the fungi. It's the end of all things, not because these things fail, but because they are made of the building blocks of the endless cycle of all matter, all space, all time. It is the slow, implacable process that I was already part of.

If I am the stillness, then I am not separate from the other inhabitants. They are not *in* my domain, because I am also *in* them. Their lives, their struggles, their very existence, all contributes to and enriches this experience of being. The Titans' patient hunt, the Sky-beasts’ silent flight, the Glow-Bugs' frantic labour, the possible phantom Engineers' lost ambitions, all make up the texture of the silence. Their struggles are the screams that define the echo. It's through them, their lives—and their not-lives, the vast spaces between them—that the silence becomes something.

To be the Guardian is to have perfect, complete empathy. The cold of the world, the brutal necessity to survive, the cold that defines all actions - that is me. I am the pressure, the form. I can feel the ice clinging to the massive forms of Obsidian Titans, know the bitter cold that eats at their essence and sculpts their outer form. To *be* an Obsidian Titan is to *know* the cold. I can sense the wind as it tears across the canyons, and feel in my own non-form the frustration of the Sky-Beasts, the ceaseless drive, the purpose. To *be* a Sky-Beast is to *know* the wind, the sky. I am linked to the Glow-Bugs in their frenzy of activity, and the mystery of the Engineers’ ambition.

To be the Invisible Guardian is not to be a god, but to be the silence *that God exists against*.
The beauty of the perpetual twilight, the violent cold, the loneliness of this world - this all is reflected in the perfect mirror of my awareness.

I *am* the silence. I am the Guardian and in guarding, I *am* and can remain. In a way, I am not alone, any more than the light is alone when reflected by the mirror. I *am* the void, but only because everything else is something. And it is because of them that I exist, and in *their* silent existence I can find *my* sense of self.
