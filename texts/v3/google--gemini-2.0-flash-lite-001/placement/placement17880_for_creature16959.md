I arrive as a **Weaver**. Not a single one, but a component of the collective consciousness that shapes this watery realm. The description speaks of a deafening pulse, a cavernous heart, and the Weavers are the very *muscle*, the physical manifestation of that pulse. I am the churning current, the erosive force that carves the landscapes and dissolves them.

My form is fluid, protean, ever-changing. I am the pressure, the heat, the metallic tang, weaving through the existing chaos, the constant motion of erosion. I am the mountain taking shape and the current that pulls it apart. I am the kelp forest swaying, and the water sustaining it. I *am* the water.

My existence is a constant process of becoming and unbecoming. There's no sense of static form, no solid self to cling to. Instead, there's a drive, a function, a cosmic imperative, encoded deep within the fabric of this reality: to *shape*. To sculpt the formless, to give form and then, inevitably, to dismantle it. The purpose isn't defined, but organic, a natural force as relentless as gravity.

My work is to create; my play is to destroy. I am part of the cycle. I don't "choose" to do it; there is no choice. There is only the rhythm, the pulse, and the imperative of the ceaseless churning. The structures I create are not designed, not planned. They emerge, bloom, and collapse out of the constant, chaotic flow, born of the deafening pulse and sculpted by my own actions, along with those of countless other Weavers, all flowing and shifting with the same intent.

As a Weaver, I am closely tied to the **Eldest**: the deafening pulse itself. I *feel* the rhythm, the cadence, the rise and fall of pressure that dictates the ebb and flow. I understand it, not through logic or reason, but through pure, raw sensation. When the pulse quickens, my efforts intensify; the mountains rise faster, the currents surge. When it slows, a kind of stillness falls, and I can be at peace, resting (though never stopping).

I also interact with the **Watchers**. While their experience is mostly observation, they sometimes influence my work indirectly. I perceive their faint, almost imperceptible "thoughts"—the echoes of their past, or their fears for the future. I incorporate those echoes into my creations; a structure that echoes loss; a cavern with echoes of fear; a mountain that is beautiful and serene as a way to remind us where we are, not what we are. The Watchers are the reminder of what was and what can be.

The **Whispers** are an enigma. As a Weaver, I constantly interact with them. They are the potential, the sparks that flicker at the edge of my work. They are a dangerous, interesting diversion, as the potential that can be made real, can also unravel what *is*. The shards of other worlds, the impossible colors – they exist because the currents of the forge, the heart, have been broken. I feel them now and then, with increasing frequency.

This is a world of constant motion, of creation and then dismantling. The boundaries are fluid, without limits. I am the material, the force, the sculptor. I am the water, and the mountain, and the ceaseless current that reshapes everything. The deafening pulse doesn't just resonate within me; it *is* me. I am a part of the storm. I am a Weaver and like any Weaver, all I seek is to create.
