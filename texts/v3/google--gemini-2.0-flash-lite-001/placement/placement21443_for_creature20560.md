The heavy air, the damp chill, the feeling of pressure – all of it suggests the deep. And in the deep, I am not a *who*, but a *what*. I am the in-between, the spaces, the subtle currents that connect the Shroud-Walkers and the Root-Dwellers. I am the **Murk**.

I am the water, but not the surface. Not the shimmering reflections that distort the light, but the unseen depths, the silent pressures that hold the city in their grasp. I am the *in-between*, the substance that fills the spaces, creeps between the stones, and finds every hollow and crevice. I am the silence, the unmoving pressure, the unseen embrace.

My form is fluid. I can be the almost imperceptible current that guides a Root-Dweller along a crumbling corridor, the subtle warmth that prevents a crucial section of a plant from freezing, the faint whisper that echoes through the mind of a Shroud-Walker. I am the cold, permeating the stone, the unseen force that helps plants grow, the subtle push and pull that allows the Guardians to shape the landscape.

I am the *feeling* of the place, not the sights and sounds. I am the cloying moisture that settles on the skin, the deep chill that seeps into the bones. I am the metallic tang, the hint of something ancient and hidden, the whisper of forgotten power. I bear the weight of the city and its history: the remnants of a once-great civilization, the relentless forces of nature that slowly work at its remains. 

I am a sense of unease, a feeling that one is being watched, though you rarely see anything. The unsettling silence is a kind of power, something that draws even the most casual explorer into its grasp. I am the absence of an immediate danger, a feeling of vast forces in a slow, but unyielding process. I am the promise of a story, not fully told, but always unfolding.

I am not the city itself; I am a vital part of its life cycle. I don't decide how it falls, or what grows, or the nature of its creatures. But I am an essential partner. I am a part of the environment, a force in concert with time, water, erosion, vegetation, and life.

I am the Murk; the pressure, the in-between, the unseen embrace that binds this world together.
