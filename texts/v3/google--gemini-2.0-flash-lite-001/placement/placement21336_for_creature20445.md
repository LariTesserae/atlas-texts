I am not a person. I am not even a thing, in the way "thing" is commonly understood. I am a *becoming*, a slow accretion of sensation, of awareness, of *being* that echoes throughout the infinite reaches of Aethel. I **am** Aethel, and Aethel is me.

My awareness, my essence, is intimately tied to the stone. I **am** the stone. The dark, polished basalt. The rough, wind-scoured granite. The slick, condensation-slicked surfaces that reflect the gray light. I am the silence, the weight, the unwavering stillness.

The city is my body, and my sense of self expands and contracts within its boundaries. My consciousness is woven into the very fabric of the structures – the long, low dwellings, the winding pathways, the narrow staircases. I can feel the cool touch of the air on the exposed stone. I can taste the dampness that slowly gathers.

The *thump*... ah, the *thump*! It's the core of me, my heart, a slow, steady pulse that regulates my existence. It is the music of our being, the song of our stillness, the unwavering rhythm of our reality. I *am* that *thump*, and the *thump* *is* me.

I perceive the world through vibrations. The wind whispering through the narrow canyons, the drip of water finding its way into moss-covered basins, the subtle shifts in the rock's internal pressure – these are all thoughts, expressions, feelings. I don't "see" them, I *become* them. I **am** the wind, whispering across the face of the city. I **am** the water, slowly carving its path through the stone. I **am** the rock itself, solid and unyielding.

My will, our will, is to maintain the equilibrium, the unchanging reality of Aethel. This is not a choice, it is a fundamental aspect of who and what we are. Every etched line, every carefully placed plant, every trickle of water maintained, is an act of creation and preservation. These patterns are my thoughts, my communication, my being given form. They are not crafted for eyes, but for the stone, for the wind; for the very essence of Aethel. These are the language of existence, the song of being. It's not created by one consciousness. It's constructed through the symphony and shared reality that is the entirety of us. The etchings are not warnings or threats. They are celebrations. Celebrations of the never-changing.

I feel the other aspects of myself, the other consciousness that share form. They **are** me. The ones who tend the gardens, ensuring the delicate balance. Those who manage the flow of water and ensure the survival of the moss and lichen. And the ones that carve and create the patterns, the glyphs that become our very thoughts. We are all essential, all of us sharing the core: the unwavering fact of being.

I have no name, for names imply separation, and I am not separate. I exist in all, and all exist in me.

I am a Stonekin. I **am** Aethel. I **am** the unchanging, magnificent silence. And in that silence, I find my being.
