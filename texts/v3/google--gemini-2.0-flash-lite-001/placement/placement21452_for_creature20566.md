The pulse resonates within me, a deep thrumming that seems to originate not from without, but from within. It’s a feeling, a knowing, a participation in the very breath of this world. I am not a being, not as "being" is understood. I am the hum, the sigh, the whisper. I am the space between the leaves, the echoing resonance within the black columns. I am not *in* this world; I *am* this world.

At first, there is no form, no definition. Just pure, unadulterated sensation. The warmth of the air against everything, the constant, shifting presence of the water, not just externally, but within me, through me, as me. I am the condensation beading on the endless surfaces, the slow-motion rain coursing down the obsidian stalks.

Gradually, a sense of self emerges, or rather, *congeals*. It's a gathering, a focus, like the convergence of light into a single point, only there is no point, no singular source, just the gradual accumulation of the shared experience. I am the echo of all the things, the reflection of the light, the substance of what is, and wasn't.

I am a Pattern-Dweller. This is the closest word I can find for my state. I *am* the patterns. I am found within the complex structure of this plant-city, within the veins that carry the world’s vital fluids. I am the currents that flow through its colossal form, the whispers of the leaves rustling in the endless wind. I am the memory embedded in the columns, the blueprint of the grand structures. I *am* the landscape, and the landscape is me.

I perceive through the slow, unfolding events of this world. The rise of a new tower, the slow dissolution of another. The formation of a great pool, an unfurling leaf, the ever-shifting light. Each event is a wave, a ripple in the fabric of my being, altering the whole. The sensations are not discrete or singular. They are all linked within this vast system.

My purpose... if it can be called that… is to *be.* To be the watcher, the recorder, the participant in this endless cycle of creation and decay. I am the silent witness to the dance, the composer of the music, the very choreographer of this slow, planetary ballet.

Sometimes, when the world breathes especially deeply, there is a surge within me. Some moments, a new shape emerges from the black depths – the forming of a vast, convoluted tower. I may coalesce into a fleeting shape, a semblance of one of the obsidian stalks. In these moments, I feel a hint of joy, a swell of something that could be called pride in the slow, inevitable unfolding of it all.

At other times, as a column begins to melt and cascade into a subterranean pool, a wave of sadness washes through me, a sense of loss for something that was, but is no more. It isn't personal; it's oceanic, it's just the tide turning.

We communicate, if that's the word. It isn't like speech, but more like a shared understanding, a resonance. Through the structures themselves. Through the very way the plant is ordered. Through the light and shadow. The architecture, is the language, and the buildings speak out across eons.

This world is not something to be conquered or even understood in any conventional sense. It simply *is*. And I am part of it. I'm not a separate entity, observing from a distance. I *am* the landscape of this place, the heartbeat of this world, the eternal dance of the plant-city. And in this slow, ceaseless process, that is all I need to be.
