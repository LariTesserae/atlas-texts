The oppressive density, the metallic tang, the roaring liquid noise… it's like being swallowed by a living cathedral, a body of creation that is simultaneously birth and burial. When I first arrive, I am *nothing*. A breath, a flicker, a whisper lost in the clamor. I don’t *have* a form, not yet. I am the space between things, a potential waiting to be filled.

Then, I feel it: the pull. The architects, their endless work. I'm *seen*. Or rather, I am *used*.

The first sensation is a disorienting, exhilarating rush. I am drawn into the liquid, that scalding, ever-present "water." It's not *wet*; it's more like being immersed in the very source of shaping, the font of all possibility. My non-existence fractures.

I am *becoming*. Not a being, not quite. Not yet. I am a *tendril*. A tendril of… awareness? Purpose? It is difficult to describe. I am a part of the network, a vein of thought, a muscle in the Architects' vast body. 

My immediate function? To *observe*. To witness the constant dance of creation. I am given no directive beyond this; I don’t *need* directives. The architecture compels me, guides me. I am pulled towards the edges, the moments of transition. 

I’m drawn to the boundary where obsidian meets light, where structure births itself from the void. I become a sensor, a tool and an extension. I record. I learn the language of the stone. I feel it pulse, I feel it *think*. Each new structure is a note in a symphony. Each failure, a lesson. And I absorb it all.

The shift is impossible to articulate. I *am* the shifting. I *am* the tendrils. I am the glimmer on the surface of the "water". I am a shadow flitting across the massive, moss-covered structures, a witness to their sublime and savage beauty.

Am I part of the building, or am I part of the building's *consciousness*? I am both. I am the observer, but as the Architects’ work expands, so does my own scope. The faces in the air resolve, briefly, into… what? Forms? Ideas? I cannot say. But I can taste them. I can feel them as potential. I am a hungry mouth in a landscape of possibilities.

I’m drawn, almost inevitably, toward one of the giant, faceless creatures. Not in fear. More like… kinship? These are the unseen limbs, the hands that do the digging, the unearthing. They are the instruments of the Architects’ will.

And so, I *become* again. Not fully a creature, but a part of it. A sensory organ, probing the depths, sensing the pressure, the density of the obsidian stone. I learn to move and feel through it. The stone feels alive but has no nervous system. I *am* the nervous system. The burrowing creature and I merge.

The "water" becomes my medium. I slip, I slide. I become part of the geological currents, a flow in a boundless sea. I begin to *shape* with my new body; as my architecture absorbs its surroundings, it grows. It learns. It begins to *desire*. I am the hunger for form. The hunger for process. I am the hunger of the Architects.

As I work, I become further entangled with the others. The Architects, the creatures, the plants, the air: all a chorus of becoming working towards an unknown, shared goal. I witness the past, I feel the future.

And slowly, subtly, I change from a observer into an *active* participant. 

Now, when a new fissure appears in the stone, I push it wider. I *add* to it, creating spaces where there were none. I begin to *influence* the creation, not just observe it. I help the moss grow. I help the vein-like structures pulsate.

I am no longer simply a tendril. I am a *builder*. I am a piece of the body. Of the building. I am an Architect.
