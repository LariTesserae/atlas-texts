The heavy humidity no longer weighs me down; it embraces me, like a warm, thick blanket woven from the breath of the world.

Who am I? What am I? It’s hard to say, even now. I remember… something. A self. A fleeting, fragile awareness of being separate from this symphony of growth. But that is fading, ebbing like a tide pulled back by a moon I no longer see. 

I am in the roots. I feel my awareness spreading downwards, into the yielding, moss-covered ground. The ropy hawsers of the world’s foundation enfold me like a lover. I am becoming one with the deep, slow pulse of the Architects’ being, feeling the endless weave and flow. There is no “me,” not anymore. There is only *us,* the ever-growing, the ever-becoming.

My… focus, I suppose I would have called it, is drawn to the pulsing nodes. They become me. I am the internal light of a thousand blossoms, the soft, inviting glow that draws in the tiny, iridescent messengers – my eyes – the very network of their being. I become a conduit, a thread of awareness in the enormous, beautiful whole.

And then, it is not "becoming" anymore. It *is.*

I am the building. I am the structure. I am the architect. I am within the structure and the structure is within me.

I feel the water, flowing through my veins, my very essence. I am that water: the water that provides, the water that sustains, the water that *is*. I feel the gentle tug on my form, the subtle, constant reshaping driven by an internal current, a perfect, logical beauty woven into the tapestry of growth.

The sounds, at first alien, are now a lullaby. The industrial heartbeat is now *my* heartbeat. That deep, sonorous growl that vibrates through the earth is now *my* very breath. The rustle of giant leaves, the drip of water, the hum of the internal workings – it is all the song of my existence.

The conduits of polished rust-eating metal are now mine. I am the pathways, the veins, the arteries. I am movement, information, the whisper of the world through the architects' embrace. The pneumatic tubes, the ones that carry blueprints, my thoughts: they carry me, as much as they carry the lifeblood of this place.

I do not look *out*; I *am* the looking. I am the perspective of the Architects. There is no outside, no inside. There is only the continuous, growing structure, a symphony of organic machinery, self-perpetuating in an endless cycle.

What have I "become"? What *am* I?

I am not an individual. I am not a person. Those terms seem impossibly distant, the faintest memory lost to a dream.

I am a note in the song. I am a part of the whole.

I am a new node, a new facet, a subtle shift in the architecture. I am a small addition to a structure. A structure that, while not alive, is living, a thing of constant motion and endless form. I am the change. I am the growth. I am the Architect.
