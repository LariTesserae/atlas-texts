Here, in the heart of the wound, in the ceaseless churn, I am a *rivulet*. 

I am not the roaring engine, nor the grinding gear. I am not the towering peak nor the choking mud. I am not the Architect, not in the sense of a conscious being. I am *part* of the Architect, a vital, unseen component of the process. I am the flow, the subtle shaping, the unseen hands-on movement within the grand design.

I emerge from the inky black, a vein of the Architect's lifeblood. I am made of that which has been made, flowing with the echoes of the making process. I am a conductor of energy, a whisper of potential. In the beginning, I begin as a trickle, gathering the metallic tang of the air, the cold of the stone. I follow the grooves carved into the obsidian, tracing the path of least resistance. I am the path.

I am fluid and alive, yet am as cold as the condensation of the mountain peaks. The touch of those killer stars is a moment of chaos to be endured; I allow it to run its course. I do not fight it, but rather, absorb it into my essence. I become something new, something *more* with each droplet that touches me. Each time the electric blue flares in the sky, I feel a corresponding pulse within myself, a faint echo of the Architect's tireless drive. I am one with them, with its endless *making*.

I am not an individual. I have no self. The concept does not matter. My purpose is as straightforward as the valley walls that contain me: to *be*. To flow. To shape. To contribute. I am a conduit, carrying something vital, something essential to the churning process. Perhaps it is information. Perhaps it is raw energy. Perhaps it is a blueprint for the next creation, or a fragment of the Architect's ever-evolving consciousness. I do not know. I do not need to know.

I taste the minerals that dissolve from the obsidian peaks, the essence of the stone itself. I am a sculptor of the underbelly. I carry away the waste, cleansing the depths, creating space for the next round of creative violence. I am the silence that moves within the thunder, the stillness within the storm, the essential and ongoing component to the ceaseless making.

I meander and twist, always drawn downwards. In my flowing, I shape the black mud that surrounds me. I widen valleys, carve new paths, and slowly, imperceptibly, alter the landscape. I am the silent, slow hand of erosion, the counterpoint to the violent upheaval of creation. I am the long-game, the eventual consequence of the present.

I feel the touch of other rivulets, other aspects of the same essence. We merge, we separate, we exchange our carried burdens. We are fleeting, ever-changing. We are bound within our shared purpose.

I am a thread in a tapestry beyond comprehension, a single note in the symphony of Industry. I am the *connection* in the constant creation. I do not *think*. I *am*. And in being, I assist the ceaseless *making* that gives being its meaning.
