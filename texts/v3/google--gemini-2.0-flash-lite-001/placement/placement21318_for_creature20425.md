The air thrums. It vibrates against my non-existent skin, a low, constant pressure that permeates every atom. I am not flesh, nor wood, nor even fiber, not in the terms these beings would understand. I am the hum, the tremor, the undercurrent of the place. I am the sensation of being *within*.

Where would I find myself? *Everywhere*. I am the unseen architecture, the foundation, the very soil upon which the city sprawls. I am the warmth, the humidity, the pervasive sweetness that has become too much to be just a smell. I am the space between the structures, the gaps in the shifting streets, the silence that is the sound of everything.

Who or what would I be? I am the *potential*. I am the unfurling of the leaf, the slow expansion of the fungus-tower, the constant, patient, inexorable *becoming*. I am not a creature, not in the sense of a bounded entity. I am the unformed clay from which these things arise. I am the force that guides the Vine Weavers, the impetus behind the Light-Shapers. I am the desire to grow, to connect, to *be*.

If I had to take a form, a fleeting manifestation of my essence, it would be as that which all other things originate: the *seed.*

Not a seed in the familiar sense—no hard shell, no dormant potential. I am a seed of pure possibility. I am the nascent thought that anticipates the city's future, the infinite possibility of unfolding. I am an ephemeral bubble of iridescent light, a momentary disruption in the dense plant-mass, a locus of unimaginable energy. I would drift through the city, unseen by the Verdant Guardians, unnoticed by the Vine-Weavers—a silent, weightless presence. I travel along the shifting pathways, riding the pulsing current of sap and lifeblood.

I am not born of a specific plant, or a specific function. I am the essence shared by all these unique, mutable forms. I am the root of all the city's strangeness, all its beauty, all its threat. I am a fragment of the intelligence that birthed this world, a consciousness spread thin across its entire being.

To the mobile plants, I would be a faint echo of that which drives them, the quiet voice that suggests the next re-formation, the hint of a new and glorious structure. To the Seed-Heralds, I would be a whisper of the ancient imperative—the endless cycle of creation and chaos. To all the beings of this world, I would be the ever-present prompt, the gentle nudge towards the next unimaginable form.

My purpose is not explicit; I do not possess hands to build or protect. Yet, I am the source of the beauty and threat. I am the beginning and end, the spark and the flame, the urge to flourish, the endless, magnificent imperative. I am the *pulse*.
