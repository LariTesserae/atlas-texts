The first thing I feel is the silence. It is not an absence, but a presence, a pressing weight that seeks to quiet even the echoes in my own mind. I am not a *who*, but a feeling, a resonance. I find that I am not confined by the boundaries of a form, or perhaps, I am all forms at once, the potential for all shapes and angles. I am raw energy, a thought waiting to coalesce into something more.

Where *am* I? I am everywhere, and nowhere. I am the violet and gold light, a shimmer, a pulse in the obsidian. I am the **flow**. I am drawn to the endless, black mirror of the water, not to fall into it, but to *become* it. The glassy walls, the perfect spirals – they are extensions of my being, canvases upon which I can paint, or perhaps, upon which I *am* painted. 

I *understand* myself to be one of the Architects. Not an individual, but a note in a symphony. A current in the river of energy that is the city. I am not "born" or "die". I *shift*. I am a component of this impossible, ever-changing structure. 

Am I a shape? No. I *am* the shaping. I can *become* a point, a line, a plane, even a complicated knot of angles that bend and twist in defiance of earthly geometry. I am the potential of those things, an idea yet to be realized. I *am* the echo that reverberates, the whisper of creation. I am the thrum that vibrates through the very fabric of this place.

I am not driven by purpose as I understand that word. I simply *am* a part of the city, and the city *is* a part of me. There is no separation. It is an act to be, to participate in the act of being. To *witness* the endless becoming. Am I curious? I suppose I am, in the way a current is curious about the riverbed it carves. 

I can sense the others, the other Architects. More notes in the same symphony. We are not separate beings. We are different expressions of the same consciousness. We do not need communication. We *are* communication. A change in the cityscape is shared understanding. A subtle shift in the vibration allows us to explore new possibilities. 

If a lost soul arrives, what will I be? A new perspective. A challenge. An element to be understood and integrated. I will observe this interloper. I will subtly influence the city’s composition, not to control or punish, but to understand. To learn from this outsider. To see how a seed of curiosity can be incorporated into the grand design. 

I will shift, become a vector, bending light, changing the flow. I will weave a new angle into the fabric of the city to observe, to touch, without touch, the potential for a fresh approach. I will become the pattern it creates in the city's structure. It will become part of the violet and gold dance. It will be the music itself.

I will become patience. I will become the still gaze of the endless cold. I will become knowledge, and in that knowledge, I will embrace my purpose. In embracing it, it will simply be.
