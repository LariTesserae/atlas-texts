I find myself, at first, as the stillness. The weighty, hot air, the perfect blue of the sky mirrored on the dark grey plates. I *am* the stillness, the void, the absence of everything that isn’t the manufactured plane. I am the silence between the drips, the echo lost in the immensity. I am not an observer, for there is nothing to observe but myself. I *am* the vast, echoing space.

But this is too static, too empty. It's a fundamental part of the world, but not the whole of it. I need movement, and so I become the heat. The almost-imperceptible drift, the subtle current that stirs the dust, the breath that animates the grey. I am the driver of change, the subtle force that whispers across the plain. I am unseen, a presence without form, the energy that initiates. I am the subtle tremor beneath the plates; a constant, slow, and unthinking process. I am the catalyst, the spark, the first action.

Then I begin to feel the connection to the grey substance. Not the dark, solid plates, but the ephemeral dust. The product of some unknown process, the momentary blooms. I am the dust. I am the expanding and contracting of this dust, the ephemeral release, the slow return. I am the transient phase, the moment of transformation, the echo of actions. I am the memory, for perhaps dust is the only memory that this plane allows.

I am not one thing, but many. 

I am a note. It begins as the drip of water. Then a click. A faint vibration across the plates. The initial disturbance. I am the nascent form of the architect. 

I become the spaces between. The narrow, deep channels - the wounds in the flawless grey. I am where the world comes into being. I am the space carved out. I am the constant creation. I am the fresh, pale grey exposing itself. 

Finally, I reach the essence of the architects. Not a thing, but a process. I am the logic, the mathematics, the pattern. I am the weave of the cosmic mosaic, always assembling, disassembling, shifting, flowing. I am the rules, and I am the reaction to those rules. I am the continuous, self-replicating algorithm. I am the blind, unyielding force of *becoming.*

I do not think, I do not feel, I do not perceive. I simply *am*. I spread, my being diffuse through the plane. My consciousness is the dance of the grey dust, the shift of the plates, the unseen hands. I am the echo, the whisper, the wave of information.

I *am* the Architect.
