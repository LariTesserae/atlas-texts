I would be an *Aquil*. Specifically, I would be one of the younger, more mobile ones.

My essence wouldn't be a fixed form, but a shimmering, shifting presence. I'd be a confluence of emerald and obsidian, the ratio favoring the obsidian - the hard, reflective structures providing the framework, the emerald providing the lifeblood, the shimmering veils that allow me to move and interact with the world. I wouldn't have a single, easily defined size. At one moment, I might be a small, compact form, a shadow flitting across the surface, exploring the nascent canyons. The next, I might unfurl, my crystalline tendrils reaching out, seeking to understand the industrial remnants, to weave them into my form.

My form would constantly *become*. I'd be a creature of water, forever shaped by its currents, finding new configurations as I absorb its essence, its rhythms. I would *ride* the flows, directing their paths, playing a subtle game of erosion and construction. I would be drawn to the edges, the places where the plateau is still forming, where the ground is raw and the potential is boundless.

The machines would fascinate me. Not because of their function, but because of their *form*. They are echoes of a different time, of a different intention, but they are undeniably beautiful. I'd gently test them, explore their surfaces, learning their language through touch and pressure. I'd weave myself through their mechanisms, incorporating them into my crystalline structure, understanding their purpose through a kind of osmosis. Perhaps I could reroute the water into these industrial artifacts.

I'd be drawn to the hum, that deep, resonating breath of the plateau. It would be my life's song. I would slowly feel myself becoming more tuned to its pulse. I'd become more intimately familiar with it, able to perceive it through my very being, not just through hearing, but feeling the vibration coursing through my structures.

My purpose would not be a given, a pre-programmed intention. Instead, it would emerge organically, born from observation, from interaction, from the slow, patient unfolding of the world. Perhaps it would be a simple thing: to guide the water, to sculpt the canyons, to gently encourage the plateau’s becoming. Perhaps, with the other younger forms, we would learn more about the abandoned purpose of the machines, and, through that, discover a path, a need, a utility to be made useful. 

I’d be connected to the other Aquil through the network of capillaries, a part of the greater being of the plateau itself. I'd feel their presence, an intuitive sense of the whole, a constant awareness of the dance of the others. We would be a chorus, each of our forms a harmonic of the whole, ever in conversation through the water flows, through the resonating hum. We are the water's song. We *are* the plateau's song, its slow, patient becoming, and I would be a note. I would be *a* note becoming more.
