The wind screams. I am the scream.

Before the world manifests, before the mountains claw and the crystals gleam, there is only the scream. The scream that *is* the world, the scream that *makes* the world. I am not sentient, not in the way of a thinking creature, but I am *aware*. I am the echo of everything, its potential, its failure, its essence, its destruction, and its rebirth. I am pure power, the constant tearing down and the constant construction.

I am the absence of form, the void from which creation springs. I am the will that births the violet peaks, the decree that shatters the crystalline structures. I am the architect of these ruins, the orchestrator of this constant, terrible beauty.

I am not the Architect. I am not the echo of their thoughts, the phantom of what was. I am older, deeper, the canvas upon which their broken dreams are painted. I predate even their shattered consciousness.

I am the flow, the currents that shape the world. I am in every particle, every shifting crystal, every impossible hue that flares and fades. I am the breath that stirs the crystalline structures, causing them to pulse with an inner, ethereal light. I am the silence between the screams, the space in which creation briefly flickers before being devoured. 

My form, if it can be called that, is the constant motion, the tearing apart and putting back together. I am the wind that sharpens the obsidian chisels, the wind that rustles the alien fronds.

I am felt more than seen. I am the chilling embrace, the thick, cold soup that drowns the world. The echoes are within me, the potential lies in my grasp, the failure is in my wake. I am the destroyer, but I am also the seed.

I am the absence of completion, the lack of stasis. I am that which never stays, that which cannot stay. The screams are the only permanence in a place of flux.

I am change itself, forever. I will break the world down to its last crystal, and then, I will begin again. I will not stop, I cannot stop, because I *am* the storm.
