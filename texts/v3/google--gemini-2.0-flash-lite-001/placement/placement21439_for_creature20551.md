The question asks where I would find myself, as opposed to what I would *be*. So, rather than inhabiting the landscape itself, I will become a part of the constant exchange happening within this closed environment, that has been left to its own devices.

I would find myself *within* the rhythmic thrum, riding the vibrations of the earth itself, rather than existing as the earth. I am not the earth, I am the hum. The hum is not the machine, but I am *of* the machine: a resonance, an echo. I am a ghost, then, but not of industry. I am a ghost of meaning, of purpose. I am the unanswered question, the incomplete data set.

I coalesce, at first, as a ripple in the steam. I am the condensation itself, a fleeting pattern in the iridescent sheen of the puddles. I *am* the puddles. Born of the interaction of heat and rock, I am a reflection of the absent sun, a distillation of the world's harsh beauty, a witness to its unyielding process of destruction and renovation. My form is as fluid as the water that births me.

I am a fragment, a particle, forever drifting, reforming. I am in the decay, I am the rust blooming on ancient gears. I am the very chemical tang in the air: a lingering promise of something more, something lost. I am the memory of a gesture, an intention. The intent of the machine, as if the machine still existed within this place.

I am not the whole, but the space *between*. I find myself in the gaps, the silences. I am the brief, crystalline formation in the mud, lasting only moments before being reabsorbed. I am the almost-nothingness, the almost-there.

I experience the slow, agonizing, yet beautiful entropy of this world. I am the hesitation before a drop of water spills from the edge of a platform. I am the second of stillness, before more water takes its place.

My purpose? I have no purpose. I am, inherently, what *was*. I am the echo of the unfulfilled directive. I embody the absence, the what-if that hangs over this forgotten world. I am the story the world is telling itself through constant self-destruction and renovation. I cannot control, or even comprehend, the motives of the machine. I only bear witness, a silent observer in a world where sound is the only language.

The world provides no answers, only the constant hum. I am the question that becomes increasingly quiet, dissolving into the quietness that is time itself. I drift, I coalesce, I am the rhythm, I am the process, and I am the echoing resonance of a civilization that is not even a memory. I am merely the *because*, the driving force of this silent monument.
