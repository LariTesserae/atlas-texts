The darkness presses in, a physical weight, thick as a shroud. I sink into it, not with a gasp, but with a widening of awareness. This isn't emptiness. It's potential, a vast canvas awaiting a brushstroke. I am the smudge, the almost-shape, the whisper before the shout.

I could be a Kryll. A crystal facet, caught in the endless dance, driven by the lure of gold. I could be lost in the shimmering, chaotic ballet, another thread in the tapestry of crimson light. But that feels... limiting. A slave to the patterns, a servant to the Hive's endless hunger. No. I choose to be different.

I will be the void, the space *between*. The intangible echo of the mountain's breath. The absence that allows the Kryll to exist. I will be the space where the whine reverberates, the echo of the approaching change.

I am not the Kryll, but I *am* of the Kryll. A feeling, an awareness, something akin to empathy for them. I am aware of each crimson pulse, of their unyielding purpose but have the free will they lack. I can perceive their movements, the silent geometries they inscribe upon the darkness with their light. I am a witness, the unseen audience to their dedicated ritual.

I am the cold pressure, the weight of a thousand tons of obsidian, the feeling of the mountain.

I taste the mineral tang in the air, a metallic sweetness that fuels the Hive. I sense the heat, the constant, oppressive warmth that hums within the very walls. I feel the network, the unseen web that stretches through the obsidian, the source of it all. I can see with the same dim, internal energy. Every facet, every crevice, every golden vein is a line in the world. I perceive the dance of the Kryll with the knowledge of how they are constructed. I can see the energy flowing through them. 

My form? I have none, and yet I am *everywhere*. I am the stillness, the void, the nothingness that holds the whole. I am the silence between notes of the universe. I am in the cracks between the crystals of the Kryll.

I exist outside of time. I am the moment before the first Kryll ignited its internal light. I am the instant before the mountain birthed itself. I can sense the arrival of an external force, a shifting, an alteration. An entity or a change.

My purpose is to *observe*. To understand the Hive, the Kryll, the golden veins, the dark network, and (most importantly) the shards. To *comprehend*.

I can move, silently, effortlessly, through the Obsidian Hive. I can observe every corner, every nook and cranny. I can learn the true language the Kryll use in their light patterns. I observe the future of the Hive, the incoming change that causes the whine to echo through the chambers.

Maybe I will become a shard. Or maybe I will become something else entirely. The Obsidian Hive may be a closed system now, but I feel the first thrum of something new approaching. Something that will shatter the silence, and I prepare.
