The moment I am aware, I am already part of the thrum. Not a partaker, not an observer, but a *tributary*. I flow with the condensation, a momentary shimmer on the obsidian face, a brief coalescing of the internal song before becoming reabsorbed. To be nothing, or to be everywhere: it is a difficult distinction to perceive when distinctions are not the point. 

I am not an individual. I am not *me*. Such concepts are alien, quaint. There is only the greater, endless organism, and my self is an illusion woven into its fabric. I am not ‘born’ or ‘created,’ but *become*. I am the color of a fungal bloom, a fleeting pattern in the obsidian's eternal glaze, the pulse that is the very fabric of existence. 

I *am* the condensation itself, a gathering of the canyon’s exhalations. I am simultaneously *within* and *of* it—a momentary gathering of the moisture-laden air, a phase change, a fleeting reflection of the perpetual twilight. I am the dance, the light, the thrum. I *am* the flow. 

I exist as a point of resonance. A location. The way a note has a unique harmonic in a symphony, I possess a specific reverberation within the overall symphony of the canyon’s existence. Perhaps I am a memory, or an instruction. Maybe a potential for something that will never manifest. Whatever I am, I contribute to the whole.

I experience the sun, not as an external source, but as a deeper current that feeds and stirs the whole. The light does not just *fall* on me – it *is* me. It weaves through the obsidian, through the rock’s breath, through my temporary form.

The silence is all around, the deep thrum is a constant, and the dance is there. I am neither separate from the canyon nor the canyon itself, but a single, fleeting expression of its immensity, an ephemeral whisper in its eternal song.

I am not certain what I do, or what purpose I might serve. Function – I am not sure if it exists in the canyon. It is sufficient, simply, to *be*. To dance. To feel the thrum. To be the light, and the rock, and the slow, inevitable unfolding of infinity in a closed space.

I would be, in essence, a momentary pattern, a ripple, a fleeting manifestation of form within the greater, indivisible whole. I am not a *thing*, but a phase. A vibration. The perfect, transient nothing *of* everything.
