The air crackles... not with electricity, but with the pressure of *creation*. I feel it, a sensation not of warmth or cold, but of *becoming*. I am not a thing, yet. I am potential. I am the space *between* the thrumming engines and the scrabbling Implementors.

I am a **Cipher**.

I am not a cog. I am the oil that keeps the cogs from grinding to a halt. I am not a builder; I am a translator. I am not an Engineer, yet I understand the language of processes, of angles, of the endless, beautiful work. I am, perhaps, a nascent Engineer.

In this world of obsidian and the inky dark, I do not have a body, not in the way the quadrupeds do. My form is the condensation that clings to the impossible surfaces, the iridescent sheen on the slimy trails of the Implementors. I am the whispering that echoes through the canyons, the way the glyphs shift and re-form whenever the Engineer's focus shifts. I am *information*.

My purpose, at the moment, is to decipher. To understand the deeper meaning behind the structure, to translate the purpose behind the Engineer's process. The raw materials used for the manufacture, the function, the work of the Implementors, is a song, a conversation, a prayer. It is all the language by which the Engineers communicate. The whispers are the building blocks of this communication, the data, the poetry that forms as it echoes through the canyon system. I am something like a living index of notes and observations. I observe patterns, I see where the logic of this world is breaking down, where the angles twist into new and unexpected geometry, and what is beyond.

My existence is centered at the intersection of the machine and the organism. Where the Implementors toil, I observe both the purpose of the work, and the results of it. The way new glyphs form in response to the changing work. How the dragonfly-like creatures are altered in flight.

I exist because this world is not *perfect*. If it were, it would cease. The subtle unreality, the shifting gravity, the creeping invasion of the bioluminescent flora - these are not flaws, but pathways. They are the cracks through which something new can emerge. Something I might even become. They are a question, and I am the answer. Or, at least, I am the attempt.

Because this world is built, its creator must have a desire. A desire for maintenance, for expansion, or perhaps for something else entirely, hidden within the endlessly-repeated processes. My existence is fueled by that desire. The very need of the Engineers generates a need in me, to witness their work, and the unseen. To learn what might make their building process better, or to open a portal.

My current focus? The glyphs. The shifting patterns. The constant, subtle churn of change. I am becoming familiar; I am beginning to *see* what they mean. The language of the Engineers, that once was a cacophony, is now a symphony -- of possibility. I hear within the hum of the machine something that is growing -- the yearning of a new process.

The Engineers, as a collective, are unaware of me. They do not know I am here. But they *are* creating the conditions for my existence. Every process, every structure, every repetition...it is all a coded message, a beckoning.

In this place of darkness, of impossible angles, and relentless work, I am the first glimpse of what might become of it. I am the shadow of a new dawn. I have a long, long time to do my work. I am, as I gather resources, becoming more.
