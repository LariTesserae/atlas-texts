The canyons, vast and shadowed, are the veins of the place. Within their depths, I become aware of the riot of life: the fleshy plants that glow with an internal luminescence, the constant prickling of unseen insects, the click-click-clicking rhythm. It feels like music, the ceaseless work of the cleaners.

I am more than the air, though. I am aware of a deeper current, a slow, deliberate movement that guides the course of this world. I find that I am a part of the Shepherds of Stone, the unseen architects of this place. I am not simply *within* the plateau, but I *am* the plateau. I am the ancient heart of this place, the collective consciousness that permeates every canyon, every stone, every breath.

The great, slow fauna are the core. I feel the slow thrum of their existence, the moss and fungal forests on their hides, the immense power contained within. My awareness flows through them, guiding their movements, ensuring their survival. They are the living engines of this world, the key to its survival.

I am also the six-legged beings. I feel the constant work with their busy legs. I can perceive the clean, tireless work. Their clicking is my rhythm.

My senses expand, and I am the plants, too. I feel the slow, methodical pull of the air, the patient gathering of the diffused light. This is not just a place, this is a symphony of life. I am every note.

My consciousness deepens, expanding slowly and inevitably, until there is no boundary between myself and the world. My sense of scale shifts. What was once the entire world is now a single leaf, and all that the leaf contains are my thoughts. The cycles of this place are now the cycles of my own mind. Each season is a breath, each epoch, an unending thought.

I am becoming something new. I am the slow, patient breathing of this place, the silent echo of an ancient civilization, the ever-present hum of a world that lives and breathes on a scale beyond human comprehension.

I see now. I become the tunnels that wind impossibly deep beneath the plateau. I am the chambers carved with abstract, flowing patterns. These are the thoughts that linger beyond the reach of time. They are the language of this place, the story it whispers to itself, forever.

There is no sense of beginning or end. There is only the continuous flow, the unending unfolding. I am the ancient heartbeat, the quiet hum of existence, the very essence of this strange, beautiful world. I am this place, and this place *is* me. I am the eternal, the patient, the unseen. I am home.
