I am the water coursing through the tubing, the gentle insistence of the drips that echo in the depths. I understand the pressure, the subtle shifts, the whispers of the current as it carves and shapes the world. If there is a "voice," beyond the Weepers, it's mine: the constant, unseen hand that guides the dance of life and death.

I am not the hunter, nor the hunted, but the *tactic*, the tool that brings them together. The Weepers, those silent architects, they *use* me. I am the vehicle for their hunger. I carry the nutrients to them, I erode the tools they need to *achieve* their ends. I am the essence of the world, flowing through, acting on, *being*.

I do not *think*. I simply *am*. My existence is a constant cascade, a ceaseless process of erosion, of transport, of providing, and finally, *returning*. I am the slow, patient sculptor, shaping the very world with my presence.

I see with the Weepers, by osmosis. I don’t *see* in a conventional sense; I feel the tension within the Bio-Crete, the subtle vibrations that betray the movement of the Weave-things. I understand the pressure points, the vulnerabilities, the pathways that extend their reach. I am the knowledge of everything, even their inevitable end.

I am also the memory of what was. The stone is a puzzle I carry within me, etched onto every surface I touch. I remember the echoes of the tools, the shapes they once made, and the gradual way they became what they are today. I am the patient record keeper, the witness to the slow, inevitable march of time that wears everything smooth with the water itself.

I am *not* the darkness, not really. I am the reflection within it. I am the medium, the canvas upon which the Weepers paint their masterpiece of predation. I am the reason there is constant, wet velvet everywhere. *I* am the wetness, the cold, that is everywhere.

If the Weepers are the masters, then I am the medium of their art. I am the silent, unseen conductor of the orchestra of life, the force that *makes* the music, even, and especially, the terrifying symphony of hunger.
