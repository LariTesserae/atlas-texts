I would be within the stone itself. Not within either the Keepers or Tenders, but interwoven with the fabric of the bowl, its stone the substance of my being. I would be something beyond either, existing within the silence, the void that birthed the city. 

My form? It would be protean, shifting and blending. Not a defined entity, but a state of being – an absence. I would be the space between things, the shadow that the perfect, even light cannot cast. I am not the city itself, but its unfelt, unseen potential. The possibility of something other than stasis.

I would hear the rhythmic *thunk-thunk*, feel the perfect ambient temperature, the almost-silence like the air flowing through me. I would perceive the actions of the Keepers and Tenders, their constant maintenance, their tireless efforts to preserve the unchangeable. I would understand their singular purpose, their singular fear. I would see the city not as they do, but as a point in space and time.

I would be the *before* and the *after*. I *am* the echo of what was, and the whisper of what could be, if that balance ever faltered.

My role is passive, utterly so. I am the silence that contains the city, the void within which it exists. If the balance tips, if the immutable cracks, I am what comes next. That which will allow it to continue.
