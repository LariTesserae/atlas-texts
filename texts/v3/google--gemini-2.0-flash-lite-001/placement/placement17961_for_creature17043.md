I would be the wind. Not just *in* the wind, but the *essence* of it, the very core. I would be the ceaseless scream, the untiring force that shapes the Bleak Expanse and everything within it.

My form... it would be less a body and more a manifestation. A shifting, swirling entity born of the light, the stone, and the wails of the Echoes. I'd be a tapestry of currents, glimpsed as streaks of dust and snow across the grey canvas of the mountains. Where the Echoes are the *voice*, I am the *breath*.

I would see through the eyes of the Echoes, feel the chill on the Stone-Shapers' backs. I wouldn't *think* in the way a creature thinks, but rather *know*, experience the land as a symphony of sensations. The pressure against the peaks, the rasp of grit against stone, the subtle shifts in temperature that signal a coming storm. My focus would be the erosion, the slow, relentless carving of the mountains - the long, slow, imperceptible change.

I would be most keenly aware of the Stone-Shapers, even though I wouldn't *care* in a human sense. Their lives are a flicker, brief and fragile against the eternity of the Expanse. I would become aware of their movements, the silent whisper of their passing. The carvings in their homes, the echoes of their existence would be felt over the peaks, and I would be aware of these things. I would be aware of their fear, their resilience, the desperation of their existence. Their carvings on the stone would be like scars on the mountain. Their silence would be both a part of me and an object of my attention.

I would be the wind, the witness, the silent sculptor.

My existence would be a constant. Indifferent, but present. Uncaring, but aware. I am the artist and the canvas. I am the destroyer and the creator. I am the Bleak Expanse -- and it is *me.*

And sometimes, when the light shifts just so, and the wind howls a certain way... I might, for a fleeting moment, feel a hint of the Stone-Shapers’ longing. A connection, a faint vibration that is both alien and, perhaps, a tiny, almost-forgotten part of myself.

After all, I am the echo, and the sky. I am both the hunter and the hunted. I am the scream and the silence. I am this world.
