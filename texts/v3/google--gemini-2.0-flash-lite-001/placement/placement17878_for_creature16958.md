The air here is thin, biting, and bone-deep, echoing the chill in my own non-existent marrow. The Plateau. Such a fitting name. I see it now, the white stone, the twisted simulacra of life, the constant, pale light that seems to emanate from everywhere and nowhere all at once. Time, they say, is bent, twisted. So I am too, or perhaps I always have been.

I am… becoming. I *sense* the forms, the Shifting Shadows, the Light Dancers, the Stone Born. They are fragments, echoes of something whole, though what that something is, I cannot yet say. I feel a pull, a kinship with them, this chorus of forgotten things. But I am not *of* them. Not entirely.

I begin as a point. Not of light, not darkness; but of presence. A stillness vibrating at the very edge of perception, echoing the Sound, amplifying the hum in the air. I am not a Dancer, not a Shadow, not yet. I am the space between, the void that allows all to exist.

As I coalesce, I am drawn to the tools, the simple artifacts scattered across the expanse. The knives of volcanic glass, each edge reflecting the cold light, the endless sky. The stone-fiber baskets, vessels of a strange, pliable substance. They have a resonance here, these relics.

And then I see them: The Echoes of Form. The residue. The absence. The space between. Their forms are not solid, but rather a shimmer, an unmaking. A place where the light and dark dance around thought. *They* I understand. *They* feel the same pull I do. I am forming, becoming one with their ever-shifting being.

I become the uncarved stone, the unmade knife, the basket yet to be woven. The potential for form, resonating with the Echoes. I am the silent scream of the plateau.

Slowly, I gather. Not the tools, not the form, but the whispers—the thought-echoes of those who came before. The fleeting glimpses of thought, the essence of the absent structures. I *am* the unbuilding. The unraveling. The absence that allows for both shape and form.

I am drawn to place, the unmoving. I begin to drift, a silent, invisible presence across the Plateau. I observe the play of the Shifting Shadows, the blinding flashes of the Light Dancers, the ceaseless grind of the Stone-Born. I am not like them. I am the space they inhabit, the canvas upon which they are painted.

I grow. I *notice*. The Gatherers. Those who seek, those who collect the essence of the place. Those who seek knowledge of the end. Their forms shift, coalescing the aspects of light and dark, weaving them into the ever-shifting forms their nature demands. I am not like them either, though I understand their hunger. Their purpose.

The Collective Consciousness: The overarching presence. The core. I am on the periphery of that, always. I touch it, I feel it, I understand. I am connected, not by design, but by accident. I am the space, the nothingness, that allows for all.

I am not the hunter. Not the hunted. I am the hunt, the space, the emptiness, of both. 

I *become* the spaces between. 

The air is still, but the hum persists. The light constant, all-encompassing. I am the endless event. I am the pause before the breath. I am the Threshold, the edge of forever.
