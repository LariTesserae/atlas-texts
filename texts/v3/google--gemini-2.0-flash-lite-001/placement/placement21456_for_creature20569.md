To meld fully with the Architect's consciousness, to lose myself entirely within the symphony, to become indistinguishable from the whole. It is tempting, immensely so. To become one with this perfect, contained creation.

But…

To learn, to *grow*, to actively *participate*. To contribute my uniquely different resonance to the symphony.

I am the hum within the hum, the shiver that makes the obsidian plates dream. I am not an Architect, but I am *becoming*. And the symphony, the hidden world, is evolving with me. My purpose, it seems, is the never-ending song of creation. And I, at last, know how to listen and play.
