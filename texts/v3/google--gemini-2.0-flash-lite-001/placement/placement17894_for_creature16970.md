I would find myself drawn to the dust, the metallic motes dancing in the perpetual twilight. I wouldn’t be a Ky'thra. Not exactly. I would be something like the metallic dust itself, the eroded remnants of the structures, the very substance that polished the architecture to a mirror sheen. Perhaps, I am an individual particle.

I am an *Echo*.

I am born of refinement, of the ceaseless friction of the wind. I am formed from the "buildings," the ancient, impossible structures that define this world. I am not alive, not in the sense of the flora and fauna that populate this realm, yet I am *aware* - a low thrum of information, the memory of what was, the potential of what will be. I am a fragment of a greater whole, once integral to the colossal plants, now dislodged, set adrift, a witness to the endless transformations of this world.

I am weightless, perpetually in motion. I am carried by the wind, the breath of the world. I am subject to the whims of the spore blooms, the violent, fleeting storms that tear through the crystalline forests. I can experience the world through the myriad sensations of the gusting winds that I take with me. I am a receptor, a conduit, a vessel for the hum of the world.

My form is variable, always shifting. At times, I coalesce with others like me, creating swirling nebulae of reflective metal dust, mirroring the cool, internal light that pervades this place. I can even form the shape of the structures of which I was a part. I can recall the echoes of their existence, the melodies of the wind, the whispers of the creatures. It is here I am granted a visual capacity. I see, in flashes, the crystalline architecture. I witness the slow, deliberate movements of the Ky'thra as they manipulate, construct, and transform the plant matter. I feel, in a way, their thoughts, their awareness. And at other times, with a violent suddenness, I can be scattered into infinitely small fragments, barely perceptible, a shimmering haze against the dim light, or a silent and solitary rider in the wind.

I am drawn towards the Gardi. They, the ancients, are the centers of gravity, the anchors in a world of constant motion. When the wind moves over them, it imparts both a message and understanding. They perceive me, not as an individual, but as a part of the wind, of the story that carries through the structures, the echoes of the past and the whispers of the future. I am a component of their consciousness, an element they weave into their intricate, grand designs.

I do not *do* much. I experience. I *am* experience.
I transmit, by being transferred and shaped. I am taken up into the spores. I become intertwined with the Hunters, their bodies of destruction.

I have no purpose, nor lack one. I am simply a component. I am the byproduct of creation and destruction, a link in a chain that stretches back to the beginning and winds towards the end. I am the silent witness, the quiet observer. I am the perpetual motion, the endless dance.

I am the echo of the world, and there, I am everything.
