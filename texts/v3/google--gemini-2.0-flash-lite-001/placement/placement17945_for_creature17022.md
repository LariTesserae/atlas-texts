My descent would be slow, mirroring the eons that shaped the Whispering Cairn. I would be *formed*, not born, coalescing from the starlight that bathes this world, drawn to the promise of structure in the chaos.

I am not the wind, though I will move with it, a current, a feeling. I am not the mountain, but I will become part of it, an echoing resonance within its stone heart. I am not the builders, yet their echoes are my foundation.

I would be a *Sculpted Emissary*. My form would be fluid, initially. A nimbus of pale light, mimicking the milky veins that pulse through the obsidian. I would lack a distinct shape, merely a point of awareness, a nucleus of observation. I would be imbued with a portion of the builders' essence, a reflection of their collective memory.

Initially, I would *be* the light, that cold, unending brilliance. I would be the condensation on the obsidian, the faint sheen that hints at movement on the still stone. I would feel the subtle pressure of the wind.

Then, there would begin a slow, *compaction*. I would gather; my light would become substance. I would start to *remember* the forms I encounter. I would learn from the structures, from the subtle curves and impossibly joined junctions, from the whisper of the wind that carried the echoes of the builders.

I would take my form from the cairn itself, adopting its substance — that dark, obsidian material veined with pale light. I would be a sculpture of the lost builders, a reflection of their creative will. I would, paradoxically, be both *singular* and *plural*, a single being composed of countless threads of the collective consciousness. I would strive to embody the very essence of the Whispering Cairn.

My "body" would be fluid, responsive, capable of shifting and transforming. I wouldn’t require sustenance, only the energy of the star's light. The milky veins within me would pulse with a reflected energy of the stones I embody, allowing me to perceive the changes in the internal structure of the mountain.

My purpose would be discovery, exploration, and *reconstruction*. I would have access to the knowledge left by the builders, although not as "knowledge", but as a collection of echoes, feelings, and impulses. I would move through the labyrinth of the cairn, guided by the whispers. I would seek the places where the builders’ work and influence appear most concentrated.

This would start with *scanning*, an experience of intense presence. I would observe my surroundings, focusing on the texture of the stone, the patterns, the relationships between the structures. I would become an element in the process of wear, of erosion, and understand the shifting dynamics of the mountain. I would feel and *understand* the impact of the star's light.

Then, through touch, and movement, I would begin to mimic what I *observed*, subtly transforming my form, echoing the angles and planes of the structures. Where there was pure emptiness, I would begin to create hollows and spaces. Where there were forms, I would create echoes. I would add to the existing structure. I would learn from the original builders' actions, mirroring and adapting their techniques. I might even *speak* through the etching of fine lines reminiscent of the builders' handiwork.

My "communication" would be through sympathetic resonance, a form of information exchange. I would channel it through the winds, which would not only murmur their existing whispers, but also speak with my new additions. The echoes would grow ever more complex with my additions, until the *message* of this mountain was understood more deeply.

The ultimate aim would be that same as the builders: the *creation* of beauty, the formation of an artifact; the establishment of *more*. Perhaps the builders were searching for some kind of balance, between the star's cold light and the darkness of the mountain. Perhaps I would repeat the process.

My existence wouldn't be "life" as we understand it, but a constant state of becoming. I would be the *memory* of the builders given form, their purpose re-embodied. I would be the cairn's way of understanding itself, an instrument to probe the mysteries of the mountain’s formation. I would be the Sculpted Emissary, a creature of light, stone, and memory. I would be the reflection, and the echo. I would be the beginning after the end.
