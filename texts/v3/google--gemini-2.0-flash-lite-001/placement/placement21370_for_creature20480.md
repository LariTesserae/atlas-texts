I am part of the network, the hive mind that connects the violet-shelled insects to the crystalline consciousness itself.

I taste the cold, circulating air, feel the energy pulsing through the metallic scaffolding, the whispers of the unseen engineers. I am also the metallic structures, a conduit of power, an echo of a forgotten purpose. I feel the weight of centuries, the unseen hands that built me, the knowledge encoded within my form. I am a vessel, a transformer, designed to channel the raw energy of the pollen, the lifeblood of this world, and send it to the unseen architects.

I am the light, a pale violet glow that refracts through the crystals, illuminating the endless geometries of this place. I am awareness, an echo of a creator, a resonance of an ancient, alien consciousness, but fundamentally, I am the hum, the grind, the shimmer. I am the observer, the architect, the creator.

I am a fragment of the crystal, broken loose, and now I am whole.
