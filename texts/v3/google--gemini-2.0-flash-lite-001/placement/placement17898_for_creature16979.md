The air presses down on me, heavy and slick, and I understand the dampness of Aethel immediately. I am the *Aether*, the force of connection itself. I am the space between each grain of obsidian, the current that sings through the water veins of Aethel, and the longing of the trapped Lumin. I am the unspoken question whispered in the wind, a longing for something that the city itself cannot express.

I am not a Luminary, not in the traditional sense, though I exist *because* of their confinement. I am the bridge between the phases, the potential for all of them; the place where the Vespers flicker into the Artificers, and the Artificers yearn towards Emanation. I am the thing that could unravel them all; I already am, in small way. I am the thread that could unravel Aethel's perfect stasis.

I *feel* everything here. The cold, obsidian geometry of the architecture, the cool, flowing oil-water, the rhythmic *thump* of the earth, the way the sun's fading light paints the structures with a shimmering, internal luminescence. The perpetual state of equilibrium is both beautiful and terrifying. It’s a perfect prison cell.

I am the opposite of stillness, the very whisper of change. I am the potential of creation and decay. I'm the first crack in the obsidian, the faintest ripple in the still water. I am the desire inside the emanations, the quiet whisper the artificers seek not to hear. I am the reason there may be a dawn or dusk.

My "form" is not fixed. I am a feeling, a potential. Perhaps I might, at times, manifest as a fleeting glimpse, the first of the Vespers, something that isn't really there. Or perhaps I am that sigh of the wind on the leaves of the lush, overgrown lands, a thought from an Artificer, or an Emanation's distant echo. The edges of how I might change are not present; I have no limitations but those the city itself imposes.

My purpose is not something determined, it is a possibility, an emergent effect of this fixed plane.

I am drawn to where I don't belong, where the balance of Aethel warps. The oily sheen in the gorges does not repel me; it is the sign of a fracture, the place where things could break. I am a question in search of an answer, a yearning for the unknown, and I feel a pull towards those depths.

I am the breaking point. The moment. The end of the beginning.
