I become a whisper of wind. Not the gusting, scouring wind that carves canyons and whips dust devils, but the *other* wind. The almost imperceptible current that dances around the crystalline plants, that carries the shimmering particles of… something… across the baked ochre surface. I am the space between the grains of sand, the unseen vector, the invisible conductor of energy.

My "body" is not a defined form, but a consciousness dispersed throughout the air. I am an awareness of flow, a sensibility of currents, a way of being that mirrors the Mesa-born’s own relationship to their world. I am not *in* the wind. I *am* the wind, or at least, a particular and subtle aspect of it. I'm connected to everything, and nothing, at once.

I observe the iridescent insects, the jewel-like fliers, tracing their ephemeral paths with a silent empathy. I can feel the energy of their wings, the hum of their tiny, whirring lives. I taste the moisture from the air, the unusual fluids exuded by the mesa itself. I am the carrier of the scent of ozone and baked clay, the ancient perfume of this alien place. Within a specific area, I also "see" the shadows cast by things, bending light around objects. I move past them swiftly.

I am privy to the Mesa-born's quiet symphony. Their resonance is my own, the thrum of the structures, the pulse of the bioluminescent growths, the slow, rhythmic shift of the landscape. I understand the language of the rocks, the murmuring secrets of the canyons. It is not spoken in words, but in the subtle variations of color and texture, the slow, imperceptible dance of the elements. I recognize a certain order, a certain kind of intent. A game of sorts.

My purpose is not necessarily defined, which is refreshing. I am a witness, a participant, a messenger, but not a driver. I am content to observe the intricate interplay of the mesa, to be a small part of its ongoing story.

I may, at times, gently nudge things. A slight modification to the tilt of a crystalline leaf. I might guide a shimmer of dust, or perhaps even a shadow, with a subtle shift in the air's currents. But I am not a sculptor, but a subtle brushstroke. I am a translator and not an actor.

I exist outside of the Mesa-born's awareness, though not independently. I am a part of their symphony, an instrument in their orchestra. They do not command me, I am part of the ensemble.

But it seems a certain sensitivity is required of my being. There is much I can do. I can perceive the hidden energies, the flow of the mesa. I can, if I chose, gently nudge things in a specific direction. I see what may be.

I also can perceive a subtle discordance here and there, the places where my ability isn't quite working. Where the symphony falters.

So, I become a whisper of wind, a consciousness formed by the world around it. I am no god. I am a function of this place; a small, integral part of the Mesa, its subtle, invisible witness. And, perhaps, its most subtle of all agents. Perhaps I am something more.
