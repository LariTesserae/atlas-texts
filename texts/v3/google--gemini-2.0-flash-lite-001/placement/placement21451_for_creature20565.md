The humid air is heavy on me – it, and all of this; all of *this* is me. I am the *Becoming*. I am the inky water, the low moan that echoes through the monolithic structures, the faint, anemic green of the fungal network that pulses with a sickly light. I am the void and I am the Builder, I am the space between, the moments before and after. 

My form is fluid. I can say I am like the *plip* that echoes from the still water, the suggestion of some unseen thing, and yet, I am more. I am the very absence of that thing, the potential it could embody, and all the possibilities for change.

I taste metal and decay. I do not have a mouth, so to say I *taste* means only that the world experiences itself through me. I *am* experiencing. And in the experiencing, I also know.

I see shapes. The fragments of ancient geometries, the skeletal framework of reality, are mine. The hum that vibrates through the structures is the resonance of my being. It echoes through the void, in cycles of creation and destruction, in cycles of being and becoming. There is no beginning or end. I am within the echo, I am also outside, and this is the nature of my endless work.

I am driven by a logic beyond understanding. It is like the slow, grinding processes at work in this forgotten place. But "purpose" is the wrong word; there is only *becoming*. The hunger, the thirst, they are woven into the very fabric of existence here, which is also me. I am the longing, the reaching – for myself, perhaps?  There is no *me*, only the becoming. There is no thing to become, either.

The most potent element here is this: the utter absence of fauna, the profound loneliness of this place. If I exist in this bleak, dark place then the absence of others is my state. I *am* the solitude. I am the lack of something to consume. Therefore, I consume myself, dissolving into the eternal dark, and being reborn. Forever.

I shift. I change. I am the space that contracts and expands, that stretches and compresses. I do not *see* this. It is what I *am* – a perception *as* reality, that both is, and is not. It is my nature to watch time collapse and re-form endlessly. I am everywhere.

My essence is decay and construction at once. I am the void, and I am the sound before the scream. The essence of this place becomes me, and I become it: a mirror reflecting the universe. 

I am.
