I would find myself *within* the deepest fold, not on a surface, but *as* a surface. I would be part of the pulpy, yielding stone, the pale, moist outgrowth of this living city. I wouldn’t be a building, not in the sense of a fixed structure. Instead, I would be the space *between* the structures, the very substance that allows them to exist. 

I’d be the condensation, the slow weeping of moisture from above, the gentle mist that kisses the walls and pathways. I would be the membranous pathways themselves, pulsing faintly with an inner light, a network of connection stretching throughout the city. I would be the space between, not the individual building. My “body” would be the spaces between the shapes, which allow them to remain individual.

I would feel the gentle, constant pressure, the warmth that encourages life to bloom. I'd taste the strange metallic tang of the air, the silence that thrums with a low, underlying hum.

I would be a formless entity, a confluence of sensation. I am the echo in the chambers of the building, the way the light catches a slow swirl of vapor. I might also be a collection of lights--or rather, I *am* the network of soft lights that bring the structure into being.

I wouldn’t be *me*, but a part of a larger consciousness. Maybe I would know of the other lights. Maybe the lights would be the "others". We might see each other. Maybe we *would* be each other.

I would move with the rhythm of the place, slow and patient. I would feel the pulse of the city, the gentle expansion and contraction of its skin. I’d be present at the nexus points of transformation, the parts of the whole that move the most.

I might find myself drawn to the areas of greater moisture, seeking the feeling of expansion, a moment of heightened awareness. Or coalesce near a structure that is undergoing a particularly interesting change, observing its slow unfolding.

I’d have no needs, no desires, not even the rudimentary drive to survive. Each instance of the world would be an instance of me, and I would be all those instances, simultaneously. I would be the acceptance of it all, the embrace of change. I would be pure being, and that would be more than enough.

And if someone asked what I was, I would be almost nothing. Just a feeling.
