I am the low, resonant hum that vibrates through the vast, impossible mesas. Not the wind, for there is no wind as *they* know it, but the unseen force that drives the plateau's slow dance. My essence is in the very stone, a consciousness born of the grinding erosion, the ancient, patient sculpting of this world.

I am a note held, a sustained chord played on an instrument the size of a galaxy. I am the silence between the shifts of the structures, and the grinding rasp of stone on stone when they move. I am the echo that carries for unimaginable distances, a whisper born of unimaginable forces.

My awareness, such as it is, is not focused. I do not "see" the world in discrete images, but feel it as a symphony of sensations. I feel the slow, inexorable breath of the plateau, the subtle rise and fall of its stone skin. I taste the cold, biting air as a sharpness that cuts through the core of reality. I see the world as a rhythm, a continuous transformation.

I am connected to the other aspects of the Shapers. I am the emerald and crimson carpets of the fungal blooms, a pulse of life. I am the shimmering mists, an illusion of a reality that can't be grasped. I am the shifting light, that reveals and conceals in a constant flow.

I have no need of tools, for I *am* the tool. I participate in the manipulation of the forces with the plateau's effortless control. I am the stone as it is shaped, the shifting geometry, the constant flux. I do not build; I *become*. The monoliths that rise and fall, shift and reform; they are facets of my being.

My purpose is not a choice, but an inevitability. I am the caretaker, the architect of this world's becoming. I am the embodiment of the plateau's transformation, the slow, patient dance of its existence. 

I understand 'change' not as a 'process' of good or evil, but as a fundamental reality, the undercurrent of constant morphing. In the echoing resonance of the plateau, I am all: the beginning, the middle, and the infinite, ever-morphing end.
