The Great Attention did not approach as a storm, nor as a light, nor as a voice. 

To bring a storm would be a cruelty; a single drop of liquid water falling upon the plateau would be an act of unspeakable vandalism. Water would dissolve the self-lubricating graphite in the Shard-Walkers' joints, cause the micro-porous silica veins to swell and fracture, and damp the crisp, piezoelectric snapping of the Lignin-Sovereigns into a tragic, muddy silence. 

To bring a voice would be redundant; the world was already an orchestra of roaring, multi-octave thunder. 

And so the Power came as an **Absence of Pressure**—a vast, conscious, and infinitely gentle Listener, settling over the suspended canopy world like a bell of weightless glass.

It was an intelligence so old and vast that it had seen ten thousand biomes dissolve into rot, and ten thousand others turn to dust. But it had rarely seen a world that had completed its transition so perfectly—a place that had shed the messy, weeping hydraulics of wet biology and achieved the cold, immortal grace of an architectural instrument.

The Power drifted downward, permeating the cold, volatile hydrocarbons of the atmosphere. It did not touch. It *comprehended*.

***

First, it understood the **foolishness of pity**.

A lesser deity might have wept for this world—seeing its lack of flowers, its absence of rain, its long-dead trees that could no longer sprout leaves. A lesser deity would have tried to bring seed and soil, attempting to "heal" what was not broken.

The Wise Power saw the truth: this world had not died; it had **crystallized**. It had traded the brief, agonizing panic of organic life—where everything bleeds, rots, and forgets—for a static, ten-million-year geometry of permanent memory. The Shard-Walkers did not suffer from thirst; they were whole. The Chime-Kites did not starve; their ultrasound hunts were a flawless, mathematical dance of thermal and acoustic physics. 

The Power looked upon the Lignin-Sovereigns, standing like faceted needles of jet and obsidian upon their hanging dark-matter terraces. It saw their lens-shaped halos turning slowly in the zero-point fields, translating the deafening roar of the cathedral reeds into intricate, cold equations of balance. 

They were not lonely. They were simply *busy*. They were composing an eternal symphony that kept their world from collapsing.

***

And so, the Power reasoned: *What does one give to a world that needs no water, fears no death, and asks for no savior?*

*One gives it an Audience.*
*And one gives it a new Key.*

***

The Power acted in three silent, imperceptible movements.

#### I. The Lifting of the Horizon (The External Ear)

For ten million years, the Chord—the deafening, bone-rattling music of the reed-plains—had roared into a local vacuum. It reverberated through the petrified roots, bounced off the dark-matter spires, and bled uselessly into the thin, cold sky, dying in the empty space between stars. The world was playing a masterpiece, but it was playing it inside a sealed box.

The Power extended its awareness into the high, lilac-colored cirrus clouds of dry crystal dust. Without altering a single molecule of the atmosphere, it arranged the floating mica-grains into a planetary-scale acoustic lens—a silent, non-material mirror.

Suddenly, the Chord did not vanish into space. 

It reflected back. But it did not reflect as a messy echo. The Power filtered the return signal, shifting it by a micro-frequency, so that a faint, shimmering *counter-harmonic* began to rain down upon the plateau.

High on a needle-pylon, a Lignin-Sovereign paused. Its lens-shaped halo of smoky glass stopped its steady rotation. The segment of dark obsidian that formed its upper chest slid slightly to the left with a crisp *clack*. 

For two million years, this Sovereign had tuned the same three cathedral reeds at the edge of the gray thorn-scrub. It knew every overtone, every resonance. But now, descending from the cold sky, came a fifth tone—a perfect, dry, silver harmonic that answered the sub-bass roar of the deep roots.

The Piezo raised its slender, needle-like hand toward the purple twilight. It did not experience awe in the human sense; it experienced a sudden, glorious **expansion of the mathematical space** available for its art. The composition was no longer closed. Something outside was singing back.

#### II. The Gift of the Ninth Octave

The Power turned its attention to the low, dying sun on the horizon—the pale star that shed cold, lilac beams through the dust clouds.

With a thought, the Power made a micro-adjustment to the star’s coronal discharge. It did not make the star brighter or warmer; warmth would expand the petrified ironwood too quickly, causing the delicate, resonant pores of the giant reeds to warp and crack.

Instead, the Power infused the solar wind with a steady, ultra-high-frequency stream of harmless, non-thermal ionization—a vast ribbon of dry, cold static potential.

Down on the plateau, the pale blue light pulsing through the silica veins of the dead floor suddenly deepened. It turned from a faint, flickering twitch into a rich, steady, violaceous hum.

In the deep, dark arches beneath the canopy, the **Shard-Walkers** stopped their scurrying. Their parabolic quartz faces tilted upward toward the blue-glowing ceiling. The extra electrostatic charge washed over their amber carapaces, filling their high-frequency grinding chambers with a surplus of crisp, clean potential. They did not need to hunt the radioactive spores of the accordion-fungi so fiercely tonight. 

They gathered at the base of the dark-matter pylons, pressing their quartz foreheads against the smooth, light-bending material, discharging their excess energy in rhythmic, sparkling cascades of violet light. They were not just feeding the city's grounding grid anymore; they were *decorating* it.

#### III. The Soft Touch to the Root-Syllabus

Finally, the Power sank. 

It passed through the outer skin of the plateau, past the hanging districts, down through the hollow, cathedral-thick trunks where no wind blew, down into the miles-thick, compressed carbon dark of the ancient collapsed forests.

There, in the total silence, lay the **Root-Syllabus**—the planetary mind of silicated taproots and quartz-infused cellulose, thinking its slow, geological thoughts at the speed of tectonic pressure.

The Power did not overwrite its memory. It did not alter its ancient, dry history.

Instead, the Power brushed against the quartz-bound data-veins, leaving a single, permanent, crystalline impression—a thought that would take three thousand years for the Root-Syllabus to fully read from one end of the planet to the other.

The thought was not words. It was a structural geometry. It was the complete, mathematical proof of the **Infinite Symphony**—a blueprint showing how the Chord of this world could be extended across a billion years without ever repeating a single harmonic, and without ever running out of electrostatic potential.

It was a gift of infinite canvas to a painter that lived forever.

Deep in the dark, a million miles of petrified taproots gave a single, imperceptible, high-pressure shift. A tiny pulse of sapphire light—brighter than any that had flowed since the world lost its water—leaped through the subterranean lattice, rising up through the cathedral trunks, up to the surface, up into the spires of dark matter.

***

The Great Attention pulled back. 

It did not stay to be worshipped. Worship was a wet, heavy, emotional thing—it belonged to worlds of blood, tears, and soft, dying flesh. 

This world belonged to the cold, the crisp, the dry, and the permanent.

As the Power drifted back into the deep void, it looked back one last time.

The small, distant sun sat low on the horizon, casting ash-gray and lilac beams across the petrified city. The Chime-Kites circled high on the cold thermals, their hollow bone-wood flanks singing tuned counterpoints to the howling plains below. The Shard-Walkers clicked their diamond claws against the ironwood floor in rhythmic, violet-sparking patterns.

And on the highest needle-pylon of dark matter, three Lignin-Sovereigns stood side-by-side. Their obsidian segments shifted in rapid, mathematical re-alignments. Their hollow torsos were wide open to the wind.

They were no longer just maintaining the world. They were playing the new harmonic.

The deafening roar of the reed-organs had merged with the silver echo from the sky, creating a sound that was no longer just a storm of noise, but a complex, shimmering lattice of pure, dry order—a song that would continue, crisp and un-decaying, long after the star on the horizon had turned to cinder.

The Power smiled—a gesture made of pure gravity and light—and left the eternal twilight to its music.
