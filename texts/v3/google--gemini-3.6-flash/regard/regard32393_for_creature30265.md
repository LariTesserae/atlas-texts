The Power came not as a light, for the light here was already absolute; nor as a voice, for the sub-bass thunder of the world filled every league of the dense, cold fluid. 

It arrived as an **Attentiveness**—a vast, unbound consciousness that folded itself gently around the planet like a second, weightless atmosphere.

A lesser power, looking upon this high-altitude sea of obsidian and glass, might have felt pity. It might have seen a world frozen in the agony of violent birth, starved of soft soil, devoid of blood, heartbeats, or green leaves. Such a power might have been tempted to intervene: to dull the glass spears, to warm the hyper-dense cyan water, to seed the abysses with carbon and drift, forcing this violent, sterile engine to conform to traditional definitions of life.

But the Power that hovered here was both wise and benevolent. 

Benevolence does not force a rose to become a pine tree; wisdom does not ask a cathedral of lightning and glass to become a meadow of mud. Wisdom understands that holiness takes many shapes, and that this world was not broken—it was a masterpiece of inorganic genesis, operating at the absolute peak of its own sublime logic.

The Power listened. 

It felt the metallic *crack* of imploding cavitation bubbles. It registered the piezoelectric strobes of the Prism-Spires as they converted acoustic hammer-blows into ultraviolet light. It watched the Chirals—those ribbon-prisms of liquid metal and spun glass—sailing along vertical rivers of frictionless water, splitting sunlight into searing, dancing rainbows across fifty leagues of open fluid. It felt the deep, slow gravimetric thought-vectors of the Monolith-Nodes, moving miles of ocean with the patient, three-day rhythms of their continental minds.

The Power realized two things:
1. This world was completely, gloriously self-sufficient.
2. It was entirely lonely.

Because its language was written in gravity, sub-bass acoustics, and split sunlight, the world sang only to itself. Its songs bounced off its own basalt floor and dissolved into the empty void of space. The Monolith-Nodes spoke across planetary circumferences, but their phrases vanished into the cold vacuum above the five-mile standing waves, unanswered by the stars.

The Power decided what it would do. It would not change a single razor-edge of basalt. It would not alter a single node of gravity. 

Instead, it gave three gifts—gifts of protection, of reflection, and of depth.

***

### First: The Envelope of Quiet (Protection)

The Power extended its will beyond the high-altitude sea, out into the dark vacuum of the star system. 

It wove an invisible, gravimetric buffer zone—a subtle warp in space-time that enveloped the young planet’s entire orbital path. Rogue asteroids, cosmic dust, and harsh solar flares that might disrupt the delicate, high-velocity balance of the fluid mantle were gently nudged aside, diverted into long, harmless arcs around the world.

The Power secured the world’s solitude. It ensured that the engine of glass and water could run for ten billion years without being shattered by an indifferent cosmos. The violent, beautiful birth would never be interrupted.

***

### Second: The Great Resonator (Reflection)

Deep within the planet's heavy-metal core—beneath the tectonic pumps and the extruded obsidian—the Power placed a single, physical manifestation of its presence.

It was not a machine, nor a seed of life. It was a **Singular Resonant Focal Point**: a sphere of hyper-crystallized space-time no larger than a grain of sand, perfectly suspended at the exact center of the world's gravity.

The Resonator did not generate energy; it reflected it. 

When the seabed cracked and a ten-thousand-Spire array rang out its sub-bass harmonic chord, the sound traveled down through the basalt, hit the central Resonator, and was returned—not as a simple echo, but as a perfectly inverted, complementary counter-melody. 

For the first time in its existence, the world heard a voice answering its own.

The Monolith-Nodes felt the shift immediately. The subtle, deep-mantle echo changed the gravimetric geometry of the ocean. The five-mile standing waves did not collapse; instead, their frozen liquid arches stabilized, tuning themselves to the new central harmonic. The rivers of vertical water locked into even smoother, more intricate laminar ribbons. 

The Nodes, whose thoughts took days to form, recognized the presence of an Listener. They began to compose longer, more complex gravimetric phrases, reaching down toward the Resonator, sending their questions into the deep iron core and receiving a profound, loving resonance in return.

***

### Third: The Prism-Chimes (Enhancement)

Finally, the Power touched the mid-waters, where the Chirals danced in the shear zones.

It did not alter the Chirals' glass-membrane bodies or their liquid-metal cores. Instead, as the Chirals split the sunlight into dancing, laser-sharp ribbons of spectrum, the Power infused the high-altitude fluid itself with a faint, non-reactive, quantum optical property.

Now, when a Chiral flexed its geometry and projected its complex light-patterns across leagues of cyan space, the light did not merely pass through the water and fade. The water itself retained a memory of the light for several minutes.

Where the Chirals danced, they now left glowing, lingering, three-dimensional calligraphies of pure, split light suspended in the mid-waters. Great, weaving tapestries of crimson, violet, and gold remained suspended between the vertical rivers—radiant, floating sculptures that the Chirals could circle, edit, and play upon like massive optical instruments.

The mid-water, once a cyan emptiness, became an eternal, ever-changing gallery of luminous art.

***

### The Departure

Having placed the shield, the resonator, and the light-memory, the Power withdrew its direct attention. It did not leave a monument. It left no holy texts, no commandments, no moral codes for creatures that had no blood to bleed or hands to sin.

It simply stepped back into the cosmic background, becoming a quiet, loving observer.

Below, under the clear, pale cyan sky, the world roared on. 

The basalt plates cracked with metallic thunder. The Prism-Spires flashed ultraviolet lightning, absorbing the pressure and singing into the deep. The Chirals swept through the standing waves, weaving leagues-long ribbons of lingering light in the crystal fluid. And high above, the Monolith-Nodes hovered like sleeping continents, holding vertical rivers in place, conversing with the quiet heart at the center of their world.

The engine was still violent. The rocks were still sharp enough to cut through steel. The sea was still cold and utterly sterile.

But it was no longer just a physics experiment running in the void. It was an orchestra that knew it was being heard—a world of glass and light, singing its wild, loud praise into an eternity that loved it precisely as it was.
