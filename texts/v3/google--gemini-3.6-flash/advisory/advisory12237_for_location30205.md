### Field Guide & Expedition Protocol: The Black Saddle Basin
**Destination Classification:** *Aqueous-Zero / Hyper-Thermal / Autonomous Chitinous Ecosystem*  
**Primary Hazards:** Ambient Radiant Kiln-Heat; Extreme Desiccation; Non-Solid Living Terrain (3-foot Depth); Pit-Organ Targeted Predation; Total Optical Deprivation; Mineral Haze Atmospheric Pollution.

---

### Understanding the Environment
You are entering a thermal trap. The location is an unlit, high-altitude mountain saddle bathed in intense, dry geothermal radiation, sealed under a vault of mineral haze. There is zero ambient moisture—no humidity, no dew, no steam. 

The floor is not stone; it is a three-foot-deep, fluid mass of calcified, armor-plated, and leathery organisms. Because they possess pit-organs that detect heat, **you will be a massive thermal anomaly**. Depending on the exact surface temperature of the rock (which likely exceeds 60°C–90°C / 140°F–194°F), your body will either act as a refreshing cool spot or a distinct, radiant beacon. They will notice you instantly—not by eye or ear, but by the thermal signature of your footprint.

### 1. Suit Architecture: The "Kiln-and-Chitin" Configuration
Standard hazmat or EVA suits will fail here. Rubber will melt or bake into brittle sheets; soft fabrics will be shredded by millions of needle-thin jointed legs dragging against you; standard cooling systems that vent steam or moisture will create localized condensation, which could trigger a frantic, predatory feeding frenzy among organisms desperate for trace elements.

#### A. Outer Shell: Hard-Plated, Smooth-Contoured Armor
*   **Material:** Mirror-polished or matte-coated titanium-ceramic composite plates over an aramid (Kevlar/Nomex) matrix. 
*   **Design:** Zero snag points. Joints must be sealed with flexible, heat-resistant metal bellows rather than exposed rubber or fabric. The creatures weave through gaps; if a leathery ribbon-organism gets a loop around a soft seal, the continuous writhing motion of the swarm will strip it off you.
*   **Surface Treatment:** Hydrophobic and oleophobic dry-slick coating. The organisms are dusty and dry; a micro-slick surface ensures they slide off your suit rather than gaining purchase to climb you.

#### B. Climate & Thermal Management
*   **Closed-Loop Liquid Cooling Garment (LCG):** Worn directly against the skin. It must circulate chilled fluorocarbon liquid to a backpack-mounted, solid-state thermoelectric heat pump. 
*   **Zero-Venting Mandate:** Your cooling system **cannot vent moisture**. Any water vapor released will immediately condense on your suit’s exterior, attracting millions of blind, desiccated mouthparts. Heat must be radiated off high-temperature heat-sink fins positioned above your head.
*   **Thermal Masking / Equalization Outer Layer:** The exterior of your suit should match the ambient temperature of the basin as closely as possible. If the ambient rock is 70°C, your suit’s outer skin should be insulated to reflect or match that 70°C. If you radiate human body heat (37°C) into a 70°C environment, you are a cold spot; if you radiate exhaust heat, you are a hot spot. Either contrast will draw the swarm.

### 2. Mobility & Footwear: Walking on a Living Sea
You cannot walk normally in this basin. Stepping onto the carpet of fauna is like stepping into a three-foot-deep trench filled with warm, dry, armor-plated roller bearings. You will sink to your hips, lose your balance, and be churned toward the basalt floor.

#### A. Load-Distributing "Saddle-Skis" or Chitin-Shoes
*   Do not wear standard boots. You require wide-footprint, articulated, snowshoe-like platform extensions made of light, smooth carbon-titanium mesh.
*   **Beveled Edges:** The perimeter of these platforms must curve smoothly upward (like a sled runner) so they ride *over* the writhing plates and bead-husks without catching jointed legs in the mesh.
*   **Telescoping Balance Staves:** Carry two lightweight, ceramic-tipped poles. You will need them to gauge the depth of the organism carpet, feel for the underlying basalt or ancient masonry, and maintain balance.

#### B. Anchoring & Recovery Gear
*   **Pneumatic Piton Launcher:** Before stepping off the basalt rim, drive heavy-duty expansion pitons into the dry rock above the basin line.
*   **High-Tensile Steel Wire Tether:** Attach a motorized winch line to your harness. If you drop into a hidden crevice or the swarming mass pulls you under, human muscles cannot fight the collective weight of thousands of calcified bodies pressing in on you. The winch is your only guaranteed way out.

### 3. Breathing & Atmospheric Integration
The air smells of hot copper, dry molts, and baked dust. It is bone-dry. Inhaling this air unconditioned will cause your lungs to lose moisture at a catastrophic rate, leading to rapid pulmonary edema, dry-cracking of the respiratory tract, and severe desiccation within minutes.

*   **Positive-Pressure Rebreather System:** A fully closed-circuit breathing loop (CCRB). Do not use an open-demand regulator that exhausts your exhaled moisture into the dry air. 
*   **In-Line Air Humidifier & Cooler:** The rebreather must cool the air before it reaches your mouth and recirculate your body’s natural exhaled moisture within the closed loop.
*   **Dust & Mineral Filtration:** The mineral haze above the basin likely contains fine silicate or copper particulates. Ensure the makeup gas intake (if mixing ambient air) uses a multi-stage HEPA and activated-charcoal filter.

### 4. Vision & Navigation in the Mineral Haze
Visible light is useless. Sunlight does not penetrate the vault, and standard high-powered flashlights will reflect blindingly off the heavy mineral haze above, creating a wall of white glare. Furthermore, bright light sources emit radiant heat.

*   **LIDAR / Active Acoustic Visor:** Your helmet visual system should rely on high-frequency, low-energy LIDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) projected onto a Heads-Up Display (HUD). This will map the contours of the ancient basalt masonry, the surrounding cliffs, and the undulating surface of the living carpet in crisp, wireframe 3D without relying on visible spectrum light.
*   **Short-Wave Infrared (SWIR) & Thermal Array:** While the entire floor will be a chaotic storm of thermal radiation, a high-resolution thermal camera will allow you to spot the "thermal chimneys"—the ancient masonry walls where the hottest geothermal heat vents up from the deep earth.

### 5. Behavioral Protocols & Survival Tactics

#### A. Approaching the Ancient Masonry
The stone blocks are your only safe islands. They are worn smooth by centuries of friction, but they are solid ground. 
*   **The Paradox of the Masonry:** The structures act as thermal chimneys. They are the hottest points in the basin, meaning they will have the highest density of creatures pooling around their bases and flowing over their archways.
*   **Ascent:** To climb a block, use smooth, curved climbing hooks. Do not use sharp crampons that could crush organisms and release fluids. Liquid hemolymph or crushed organic matter will break the "closed metabolic loop" of the basin and create a frantic localized feeding frenzy around your feet.

#### B. Movement Dynamics
*   **Move Slowly and Rhythmically:** Sudden, violent jerks create mechanical vibrations in the brittle plates below. The creatures are blind, but they are finely tuned to kinetic transfers through the gravelly mass. Smooth, continuous sliding movements mimic the ambient "papery rustle" of the ecosystem.
*   **Zero Fluid Discharge:** Never dump waste, spatter water, or open a seal. A single droplet of water in this basin is an chemical explosive event to these organisms. 

#### C. Psychological Management
*   **Auditory Suppression:** The sound of millions of dry legs dragging across scales is quiet, but it is continuous, ambient white noise that can induce disorientation, auditory hallucinations, and intense claustrophobia over hours of exposure. Wear active noise-canceling ear protection tuned to pass speech/system alerts while muting the papery rustle (2 kHz – 8 kHz range).
*   **Claustrophobia Mitigation:** You are in pitch darkness, inside an oven, floating on an ocean of writhing, calcified flesh. Ensure your suit suit contains mild, automated transdermal sedatives or anti-anxiety triggers if your heart rate spikes above 130 BPM. Panic in this suit will overheat your liquid cooling loop, causing systemic thermal failure.

### Emergency Kit / Pack Contents
1.  **3x Solid-State Hydration Packs:** Electrolyte-rich gel packs consumed via an internal helmet port. No open containers.
2.  **Pyrotechnic Thermal Decoys:** Magnesium/thermite flare canisters. If swarmed, launching a high-heat decoy away from your position will draw the pit-organ-guided organisms toward the new heat source, clearing a path.
3.  **Aerosolized Dry Ceramic Powder:** Spraying this onto your suit joints immediately neutralizes sticky secretions or friction binding caused by crushed husks.
4.  **Emergency Thermal Blanket (Reverse-Spec):** A deployable, rigid heat-shield umbrella to reflect the basin's floor radiation away from your suit if your LCG system fails.
