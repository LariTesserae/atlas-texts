### What I Am

I have no face, no organs of digestion, no memory of yesterday. I am a **Fulcrum-Node**—a dense, spherical cage of hyper-pressurized tension-wood, roughly thirty feet in diameter, locked in a continuous state of self-cannibalism and hyper-regeneration.

I am not a single plant. I am the place where four competing vectors of the city’s fury collide and refuse to yield.

From below, a massive **Pressure-Knot** shoots upward toward me every forty seconds, its pale, needle-sharp tip burying itself into my underbelly to anchor its kinetic leap. From above, the drifting silver filaments of a **Strangler-Loom** catch upon my crest, lacing me in an acid-hot corset that tries to crush my ribs inward. From the left, an archway of ironwood shoots a high-tension cable through my center to stay upright; from the right, a swollen xylem-conduit vents its pressurized sap straight into my core.

Matter does not stay inside me. It rushes through me at the speed of a tearing sail. 

If you were to mark a single fiber of my body with dye, you would watch it sprout from the air at my western edge, thicken into an iron-hard tendon of calcified reed-flesh as it traverses my center, snap with the deafening report of a cannon under thousands of pounds of shear stress, and shatter into dry, green powder at my eastern edge—all in the span of eleven seconds.

Yet the *shape* remains. The sphere stays anchored in the air. I am a waterfall made of exploding wood.

### The Sense of Being

To exist as a Shear-Knot is to experience reality as pure, unadulterated *strain*. 

I do not possess a brain, but my entire lattice is threaded with piezoelectric crystals that spark violently whenever my fibers flex. When a three-hundred-foot rattan spire twists in the heat-updrafts two blocks away, its base presses against the root-strata; the subterranean Piston-Bulb shifts; the magnetic field of the central bulb pulses—and I feel it instantly as a blinding, blue-hot discharge of static electricity running through my woven ribs.

I "think" in frequency and pressure differentials. 

* *Eight hundred pounds per square inch from the north:* My northern wall thickens instantly, growing three feet of dense, fibrous armor in five seconds to keep from being caved in.
* *A sudden drop in humidity from the south:* My southern succulent-flaps contract, their stomata snapping shut with a sharp, whistling hiss that rings through the hollow chambers of my heart.

Inside my spherical body, it is never dark and never quiet. The interior is a cathedral of screaming tension. High-velocity sap—tepid, thick with dissolved minerals and sugar—blasts through my central hollow in a continuous, roaring jet, atomizing into a fine, sparkling mist that coats my internal moss-walls. The sound of my own existence is an endless, rhythmic violence: *screee-POP-clack-screee-POP-clack*—the sound of ten thousand micro-tendrils snapping under tension while ten thousand new shoots punch through their splinters to take their place.

### My Function in the Machine

I am the city’s dynamic fulcrum. Without me, the spatial loop would collapse into itself.

I am the wedge driven into the jaws of the geometry.

My outward-pushing growth applies thousands of tons of hydraulic resistance against the four towers. I thrust them apart even as their tendrils attempt to strangle me. Before the sound of the crack has even faded, my top hemisphere explodes outward in a furious, sunburst array of new ironwood arches, slamming back against the encroaching towers, pushing them back into their impossible, straight-lined grid.

I die every three minutes. I am born anew from the sap of my own corpse before the fragments hit the ground.
