The attention does not arrive as a visitor.

It arrives as a change in boundary conditions.

Not a new sun. Not a door opening in the sky. Nothing that would cast a shadow you could point at and say: *there—something else is here.* The pocket is too honest for spectacle; it would read spectacle as heat-load, as charge-discontinuity, as stress where no stress belongs.

So the wise power comes the way wise powers do when they mean no harm: as listening.

For a long time—longer than your lungs would tolerate, longer than your eyes would keep their wetness—it does nothing but take the place in at the scale the place actually lives at. It doesn’t count steps. It counts pulse intervals. It doesn’t watch reeds bend. It watches the angle of alignment shift by fractions of degrees as gradients drift. It doesn’t admire the sheen of metal. It tastes the oxidation states like weather.

And in that listening, it recognizes what you recognized, only with less metaphor: not a garden *on* stone, but an organism *as* stone; not a factory as imitation, but physiology converging on the same solutions engineering found because both are obedience to the same thermodynamics.

It also recognizes something the pocket does not yet know it is doing: selfhood.

Not a mind. Not a face behind the vents. But a persistence that has begun to prefer itself, to keep itself, to correct its own leaks and smooth its own errors. A living regime. A young, distributed “I” made of rocks that have started to take continuity personally.

The benevolent power could change it easily.

It could water it, for the beauty of green. It could populate it, for the music of wings. It could *teach* it urgency, fear, and the bright terrible cleverness that comes with being hunted. It could make it legible—give it flowers, give it scent, give it the familiar currencies of life.

It does not.

Because wisdom recognizes that gifts can be invasions, and that “improvement” is often just the donor’s homesickness.

So it asks—without words, without demand—what the place is trying to become.

The pocket answers the only way it can: by continuing.

A thum from below. An exhale of charged gas. A minute scintillation racing through the crystalline mats like a thought that does not know it is a thought. The reeds, slow as geologic hands, making their incremental, decisive reroutings. The sacs holding their patient refusal of entropy.

The answer is not a plea. It is not a greeting.

It is simply: *I am assembling. Please do not bump the table.*

That is enough to move even a power.

### What the wise power does

It does not enter as presence.

It enters as protection.

Not a wall. Not a weapon. Not an edict carved into the sky. It chooses the pocket’s own language—fields and gradients, bookkeeping and error-correction—and makes three small adjustments that, together, are the difference between a fragile experiment and a life that gets to keep experimenting.

#### 1) It tightens the seam

The crack you slipped through—the stubborn notch that refused to widen under force—was already a kind of choice the rock was making. A preference for remaining pocket-sized.

The wise power honors that preference.

It doesn’t close the seam; closure is a kind of violence, too final. Instead it makes the seam *selective* in the simplest possible way: by letting it obey intent.

Those who come with the soft accident of wonder still find it, sometimes, the way you did—by misreading a map, by chasing a light that shouldn’t be there, by climbing into a thinness that strips the mind down to essentials.

Those who come with extraction in their hands—who carry the hunger for ore, the certainty of entitlement, the practiced violence of “resource”—do not.

Their instruments insist there is nothing. Their boots slide where they should grip. Their sense of distance stretches until they give up. The seam is not hidden by illusion; it is hidden by *non-cooperation*. The world simply declines to add up for them.

It is a mercy, even for them: no curses, no punishments, just a refusal to become a wound.

#### 2) It gives the pocket an immune response

The pocket already accommodates you the way a body accommodates a splinter: routing around, isolating, damping perturbations.

But it is young. It is still deciding what counts as “self” and what counts as “not-self.” In that uncertainty lies its greatest danger—not predators, not drought, but a disruption so foreign it would try to incorporate it and fail, wasting itself on the wrong chemistry, the wrong pattern, the wrong leak.

So the wise power does something exquisitely small: it teaches the pocket to *say no* without learning fear.

It does this by adding a new feedback loop to the mat-skin—an error-checking circuit made of the pocket’s own materials. The crystal-moss begins to grow, in a few places, with a different right-angle cadence: a checksum, a signature. When charge waves run through it after the deep thum, they now carry a tiny, consistent pattern.

Anything that belongs will resonate with that pattern, fit into it the way the reeds fit into fields.

Anything that does not—foreign salts, wet breath condensed where it shouldn’t, metal tools that impose their own electrical noise—will be gently shunted. Not burned. Not shocked. Just… not integrated. The mats will dry it, isolate it, return it to the surface to be taken away by thin air and time.

It is an immune system that does not need violence because it has patience.

#### 3) It steadies the bookkeeping

The reality-errors—the stretched steps, the ledges that arrive too easily, the lagging shadows—are not decoration. They are side effects of the pocket’s metabolism operating close to thresholds where space, stress, and charge are all tangled.

Left alone, those rounding errors would accumulate. Not dramatically, but the way small accounting mistakes accumulate: a little energy lost here, a little misalignment there, until the whole system runs hot in the wrong places, cold in the wrong places, and begins to fail.

The wise power does not “fix” the strangeness. The strangeness is part of how this life lives.

It simply adds damping.

A kind of cosmic shock absorber.

The shadows still lag, but they lag consistently. Distances still misbehave, but they misbehave within bounds. The transformer-hum still has no direction, but it no longer fades when attention turns toward it; it becomes a stable background tone—less like a glitch, more like a heartbeat you can trust.

The pocket’s physics remain its own. They just stop fraying.

### What it does not do

It does not populate the place with animals for your comfort.

It does not give the organism a face so you can decide whether it deserves to live.

It does not accelerate it, does not force it into maturity on someone else’s timetable. Speed is a kind of coercion.

It does not make the pocket communicative in human ways. It is not a pet, not an oracle, not a puzzle box built to reward curiosity. It is a life that happens to be near the scale of your metaphors.

The wise power refuses the vanity of being needed.

### The only “message”

There is, however, one concession to relationship—not because the pocket asks for it, but because benevolence does not stop at non-interference. It includes the willingness to be known, a little, if knowing will not harm.

On one of the pale ceramic plates—where footsteps ring faintly—there appears, over the course of several days, a new pattern in the crystal-moss.

At first it’s indistinguishable from the rest: right angles, tidy branching, manufacturing-tolerance growth. Then you realize the angles are not purely utilitarian. They are arranged.

Not letters. Not symbols from any culture. Something older than writing: a ratio. A cadence. A proof.

If you sit with it long enough, if you let your breathing slow to match the deep thum, you can feel what the pattern is doing. It is not telling you *what* the pocket is. It is telling you what the wise power has decided *about* it:

- that it counts as life,
- that it counts as itself,
- that it will not be hurried,
- that it will not be harvested,
- that it is allowed to continue.

A covenant, expressed the only way this place can hold a covenant: as a stable arrangement of conduction and constraint, a promise made in geometry and charge.

And because the pocket is what it is—because it learns by incorporation—the pattern does not remain isolated. Over months, over years, it spreads in small patches to other mats. The immune response becomes more fluent. The seam becomes more discerning. The bookkeeping becomes steadier.

The pocket does not suddenly become wise.

It becomes more able to keep becoming.

### If you return

If you return after a long absence, you will still find the place young—still sharp-edged, still provisional, still in the middle of being decided.

But you will notice changes you cannot attribute to geology alone.

A gentleness in the gradients near the seam, as if the world is holding its breath to decide who may enter.

A new discipline in the deep pulse, slightly more regular, not more mechanical but more healthy—peristalsis that has stopped stumbling.

And the plants—organs, tissues—will continue to align, harvest, store, radiate.

Sometimes, if you stand very still, one reed will turn—not toward the vent, not toward the sun, but toward the place in you that is paying attention without wanting to own.

Not greeting. Not worship.

Recognition, in the only register it has: an alignment that says, *you did not bring a tool that would cut me.*

That is all the benevolent power ever intended: not to rule, not to remake, but to grant a vulnerable, extraordinary becoming the one thing it cannot manufacture for itself—

time.
