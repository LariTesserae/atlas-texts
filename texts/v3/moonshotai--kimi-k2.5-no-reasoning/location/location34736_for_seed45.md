The Upheaval Fields

There is a sound like holding your breath so long your ears begin to sing. That is the baseline here. The Upheaval Fields exist in a darkness so complete it seems to have *depth*—you could fall upward into it, you feel, if you stood too long at certain angles. But the angles are wrong here. The ground has not finished deciding what geometry it means to occupy.

The terrain: imagine a world in the molten moment before first rain, before water learned to gather. Vast plains of something between cooling slag and half-remembered geology, stretching farther than any eye could measure in this lightless vault. The surface breathes heat. Temperature rides at that threshold where bare flesh would redden, then blister, then char—and yet there is nothing living here to blister, nothing to char. The warmth rises from below in pulses, irregular, arrhythmic. Something underneath is still forming. Something underneath has not yet solidified its intentions.

And the elevation—*extreme* does not approach it. The Fields begin where any reasonable topography would end. Plateaus the size of nation-states rear at angles physics should not permit, their cliff-faces not eroded (nothing here has been weathered; nothing here has *had time*) but *born* that way, sheared into existence by forces still active, still visible if you stand very still and watch the dark. Because the darkness is not empty. It *moves*. Not with light—there is scarcely any, a faint phosphorescence bleeding from certain mineral seams, enough to suggest the outline of what towers above you, never enough to reveal it. The darkness moves like fabric in wind, like the shimmer of heat you can almost see, like the visual echo of your own straining eyes.

The turbulence lives in the making and unmaking. You might stand on ground that has held its shape for what passes as hours here, feel its warmth steady through your boots, and then feel it *shift*. Not earthquake—*gestation*. The plain beside you might begin to dome, might crack along a seam that glows briefly with interior heat, might birth a new prominence, a new ravine, a new impossible geometry while the surface you stand on remains, by comparison, still. Or the stillness might not hold. The making is local, capricious, almost *curious*. As if the world is trying forms, discarding them, building towers and collapsing them in the same continuous exhale. You cannot walk with purpose here. You can only navigate the present moment, and even that poorly.

The materials: everything is mineral, but mineral in states I have no names for. Basalt would be a luxury, a *finished* thing. Here there are outcroppings like half-cooled syrup, like stone caught in the act of becoming stone. Smooth in places where the heat has worked longest, then suddenly rough, suddenly *spined*, suddenly *folded* with layers that have no stratigraphy because nothing has been deposited twice. Nothing here is old enough to have history. Everything is first-generation. When I find something that resembles a pebble, I cannot trust it. It may be cooling from liquid in my hand. It may be warm for reasons of chemistry I do not understand, continuing some reaction begun in the dark when I was not looking.

The sparse presences: there are other landforms at distances that suggest scale without confirming it. A needle of something darker than the dark, visible only when the phosphorescent seams behind it pulse. A depression that holds heat differently, that I avoid because the air above it shimmers with warmth and something else, some *frequency* that makes my chest hum in sympathetic vibration. These presences are scattered across a vastness I feel more than map. Between them, the emptiness is not dead. It is *expectant*. The balance with emptiness is precise, almost calculated, as if the Upheaval Fields know how much nothing to hold between each something.

The built things: scattered, yes, faint marks. I found one. A structure, if structure is the word. It would not shelter anything. It rises perhaps twice my height, a hollow roughness of the same material as everything else, but *positioned*. Deliberate. There is a cavity within it that holds the darkness differently, that seems to *collect* the faint ambient phosphorescence and dim it further, an absence of light more than a presence of anything else. I cannot say who made it. I cannot say if it was made, or if the turbulence of formation simply produced something this close to intentionality, this *suggestive* of purpose. There are others, I am told—or I have inferred—from similar hollows on distant elevations, similar arrangements of raw material that seem to look at each other across the void. Single buildings, partial remains, occasional artifacts of a process that may not be building at all. They have no technology. They have no tools. If they are objects, they are objects the way a crystal is an object, the way a geode is an object—formed by conditions, not by hands.

No fauna. No flora. No water to make them possible, even in this warmth, even in this turbulence that might have driven evolution given time. Time has not been given. The Upheaval Fields are pre-life in the most absolute sense—not dead, not hostile to life, but *earlier*. Life has not been invented here yet. The chemistry may not permit it. Or the chemistry may be inventing itself still, may be moments or millennia from the conditions that would allow a cell to persist. I walk through an embryonic world, and my own biology feels *transplanted*, feels like an anachronism I carry with me, a wet warm bag of processes that do not belong in this mineral exhalation.

The sound: mostly quiet, the score says, and this is true in the way that a held note is quiet. There is the baseline hum of heat moving through stone. There is the almost-infrasonic pulse of new formations rising, old formations adjusting to the changed stresses around them. There is the sound of my own body, which I hear more clearly here than anywhere—my heartbeat, my swallowing, the creak of my attention straining toward the dark. And occasionally, the turbulence makes *more*. A crack that is not thunder because there is no atmosphere to carry thunder, but something like it, a percussive assertion of new matter arriving. A whispering that might be gas escaping through cooling fractures, or might be the sound of the darkness itself, moving like fabric, remembering wind.

The weirdness: unusual, only 0.6, but the weirdness here is *structural*. I expected with so much turbulence, so much active making, that there would be chaos. There is not. There is *pattern that has not finished completing itself*. The scattered structures face each other at distances that suggest communication, or preparation for it. The phosphorescent seams follow lines I cannot map but *sense*, geometries that might be mathematical if I had the mathematics. The warmth pulses in sequences that almost establish rhythm, that my animal brain keeps trying to predict, to *music-pattern*, and that keep escaping just at the edge of recognition. This is not a weirdness of violation. It is a weirdness of *prematurity*—the sense that you have arrived before the invitation specified, that the host is still dressing, that everything you see is *about to be* something, and you cannot know what, and your presence may or may not be disrupting the process.

I do not know how long I have been here. Time moves strangely in darkness and warmth and the continuous low-level emergency of formation. I have watched a ridge *unfold* from a plain, the material not flowing but *declaring* itself into a new configuration, over what felt like hours, and when I looked away and looked back, it had stopped, held, become *feature* rather than *process*. Until I disturbed it by approaching, and felt it shiver, warming further, as if my attention—or my mass, or my biotic chemistry—had triggered some continuation. I backed away. It settled. We have this understanding now, the ridge and I. I do not know what it thinks it is becoming. I do not want to interrupt.

The rules of this world: nothing is finished. Nothing has been weathered into patience. The turbulence is not destruction but *incompleteness*, the energy of a system that has not yet found its resting state and may never, may continue this active becoming forever, or until it suddenly *snaps* into something else. The darkness is not absence but *unrealized potential light*. The warmth is not climate but *symptom of ongoing creation*. You do not walk here. You *negotiate*. Every step is a conversation with ground that may answer, may shift, may begin to dome or crack or cool into finality beneath your weight. The structures that might be architecture, might be Crystal formations of scale outside their usual occurrence—they do not instruct you. They simply *hold position* in the darkness, patient, premature, waiting for the world to finish around them.

I will leave, eventually. I am not native to pre-life, to this mineral urgency. My water-bag biology complains, though it does not suffer—the warmth sustains something in me, some ancient cellular memory of thermal vents, of first origin. But I am not at home here. I am witness to a world that has not yet decided what to be, and my witnessing may itself be one of the conditions it must incorporate, one of the perturbations that will shift its eventual form toward something that can account for eyes in the darkness, for the weight of attention, for the strange persistence of a creature from a much later chapter of possibility.

When I go, I will leave no mark. There is no erosion to preserve footprints, no water to carry trace, no life to notice my passing. But the world will have noted me, I think. It notes everything. It is still learning what noting means, what response to make, what shape to take in answer to the fact of my temporary presence. The ridge still shivers when I approach. We are teaching each other, in our limited way, what it means to exist alongside something becoming.

The Upheaval Fields continue. Dark, warm, elevated beyond reason, sparse in their vast presences, scattered with structures that may or may not intend, turbulent with the energy of everything still possible, quiet in their held-breath waiting, empty of all life including mine soon, soon. The phosphorescence pulses. The darkness moves like fabric. Something underneath has not yet solidified its intentions.

Something underneath is still forming.
