I need to absorb these parameters, let them settle and find their contradictions first. Water near-absent, yet vegetation lush. Beyond-modern technology, yet nothing built. Reality broken, yet never changing. Warm, low, dense, open, quiet, young, rare fauna. These are not compatible in any ordinary world. They demand something stranger.

Let me follow where they pull.

---

The Thicket of Unbecoming

There is no ground, properly speaking. Only a low horizon line where dense vegetation meets denser vegetation, the whole world pressed down to chest-height, spread open in every direction until vision loses itself in green haze. What grows here does not root in soil—there is none, or what exists functions otherwise. The plants draw their sustenance from something else entirely, some substrate that pools and concentrates rather than flows, that accumulates in the interstices of being like frost on invisible glass.

The vegetation: lush beyond any temperate meaning of the word. Not jungle, not forest, not meadow. Something more intimate and more oppressive. Each plant grows pressed against others, layered, interpenetrating, stems threading through leaves that are themselves hollow chambers containing smaller stems. The density is not merely spatial but ontological—multiple presences occupying what should be single locations, the same cubic meter hosting a fern and its shadow and the shadow's fern, distinct yet coincident. You could reach out and touch six surfaces in the space your hand expects one. The plants do not compete because competition requires gradient, requires change, and here nothing changes. The young-looking leaves, their edges still carrying the tremor of first emergence, have held that tremor for—duration is wrong here, but let us say, for the permanence that substitutes for time. They are fresh and newborn and eternal.

The temperature holds at warm, not the warmth of sunlight but something internal, metabolic, generated by the dense packed life in its own respirations, though no breathing can be heard. The sound parameter demands quiet, and quiet you receive: the hush of a thing that has never moved and thus has nothing to say about motion. No wind. No insect hum. No creak of growth. Only, occasionally, a soft settling, like fabric adjusting to a body that will never stand up—a dimensional readjustment, plants shifting their weight across layers of unreality.

Water at 0.2 is almost absent. There is no rain. No rivers cut this low terrain. No morning dew beads the eternal newborn leaves. Yet the plants do not dry. They operate on anhydrous chemistry, or perhaps on chemistry that has forgotten it ever needed aqueous mediation. Some leaves are crystalline, silicate-lattices mimicking vascular structure. Others are more like plasmas held in organic shapes, warm to touch, humming faintly at frequencies below hearing. The rare moisture that exists—some captured atmospheric trace, some exhalation of the plants themselves—collects in pocket-dimensions, in the folded spaces between overlapping presences. You might find water by reaching into what appears to be solid stem and finding your fingers wet, withdrawn with the shock of intrusion into somewhere else.

What is this place? The technology parameter insists: beyond modern, engineering that current physics cannot describe. Yet nothing is built. No structures, no artifacts, no maker's mark. The technology has dissolved into process, into the ongoing function of the place itself. This is the thicket as machine, as computational substrate, as engine whose purpose is not to produce anything but to maintain the conditions of its own impossibility. Nanoscale operations visible only in their macro effects: the exact right placement of each leaf to maintain the density without collapse, the precise ontological indexing that allows coexistence without interference. Or cosmic-scale structures folded small, the thicket a single node in some larger array, its warm stability purchased by transactions across distances that don't map to space as we measure it.

The reality breakdown: this is not subtle. Walk through the thicket—though walking is difficult, the density presses, you must move through rather than past—and you will find places where your own coherence frays. Your shadow may continue without you, negotiating a path through denser growth you cannot access. Your hand, extended to part leaves, may part instead into two hands, each authentic, each continuing its own exploration, rejoining only when you withdraw and choose which memory to keep. The young erosion speaks here: these features are not worn because nothing wears, but they are sharp, newly broken, the edges of reality shearing where incompatible states abut. You find a clearing, perfectly circular, where vegetation has never grown and never will, the ground there not ground but a hole in existence showing—what? Not blackness. The color of a direction you cannot point. The sound of a question you haven't learned to formulate.

Fauna rare at 1.1. Life that moves, life that eats, life that dies: almost absent. Yet not none. Occasionally, something passes through the corner of vision, something that might be animal if animals were topologies instead of organisms. The Wandering Knot, some call it, though naming implies more stable knowledge than exists—a configuration in the dense growth that rearranges, that has goals or instincts or programming impossible to distinguish, that drinks from the folded water-pockets and is drunk in return by the same mechanism, predator and prey collapsed to identity. It makes no sound. It changes nothing in its passage, the leaves resealing behind it, the density unaltered, the eternal equilibrium undisturbed.

And dynamic at 0.5: never changing. This is the binding constraint from which everything else derives its strangeness. The thicket exists in frozen equilibrium, no gradients to drive process, no time for sequence to unfold. The warmth does not dissipate. The plants do not grow toward light because there is no growing-toward, only the eternal state of having-grown. The beyond-modern technology is perhaps simply this: the engineering of a pocket outside change, outside entropy's long mandate. Yet it is young, freshly emerged, its features sharp with recent formation. The paradox is not resolved but inhabited. Something made this recently, something beyond our physics, and made it *to not change*, capturing the moment of emergence itself in permanent display, the universe's snapshot of itself before it learned to run.

Light at 2.5, normal, and this is perhaps most disturbing. The warm green receives illumination without source—no sun in any sky you could locate, the sky itself being merely more thicket rising to meet the eye, the horizon low and everywhere. Light permeates, sufficient for vision, for the lushness to manifest its colors, for the shadows to exist and sometimes to walk independently. It is the light of a completed equation, balanced, no entry point for mystery, no mystery needed.

I have described what I can. The place resists narrative because narrative requires change, and change is what the Thicket of Unbecoming has subtracted from itself. It exists, dense and open, warm and newborn-permanent, technological and untouched, broken and perfectly stable. You could enter it—if you found it, if it permitted your coherence to persist across its threshold—and wander, and find no destination because destination requires temporal sequence, because arrival requires departure. You would become part of its density, another presence in the layered space, your own ontological index added to the computation, your warmth contributing to the whole, your silence finally absolute.

Perhaps there are others there now, among the rare fauna. Perhaps they are you, in configurations the thicket has separated and maintained, each version walking paths the others cannot see, each holding memory of entry without memory of exit, each as eternal and unchanged as the newborn leaves they brush aside, brush through, become.
