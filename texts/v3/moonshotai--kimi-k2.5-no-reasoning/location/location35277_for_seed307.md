## The Throat

It begins with a sound you cannot make, a frequency that exists only as pressure behind your eyes. You find yourself *in* it—the passage, the cavity—without memory of approach. This is the first symptom of its topology: the Throat has no outside, or if it does, no one has returned to describe it.

### The Shape of Containment

Imagine a geode the size of a coffin, or a cathedral nave compressed into a womb. The scale is wrong—intimate, yes, but your body knows it's also *high*, impossibly high, because your inner ear insists on falling even as your hands touch both walls. The elevation parameter operates orthogonally here: not height above some ground, but stacked impossibility, compression of vastness into handspan. You are in something that remembers being a mountain, or will become one, folded now into this breathing space.

The walls are young—sharp, recent, barely cooled from some formation that should have taken millennia but completed in a gasp. Obsidian and stranger silicates, glass-edged, sweating. They weep. The water parameter manifests as *exudation*, as the rock itself unable to hold its liquidity against the heat. Not rain, not springs. Condensation without atmosphere, without cycle. The walls bead and run with minerals, with salts that crystallize in mid-drop and fall as faint chimes you almost hear—the silence parameter eating itself, producing near-sounds that accumulate at the threshold of perception until the quiet becomes *dense*, becomes a presence.

### The Heat

2.9 of 3. Not the dry furnace of deserts but something worse—*humid* heat, the heat of something living, though nothing here lives as vegetation lives. The fungal-or-simpler category blooms: the walls grow slime-curtains, bacterial films in opalescent strata, archaeal mats that shift color with temperature gradients. The heat comes from below, from within, from the walls themselves in their unconsolidated youth. They are still *finishing*, these rocks, still releasing the energy of their rapid precipitation. To touch them is to feel time made tactile, formation in progress.

### The Fauna

This is where reality frays most actively.

There is no food chain here. Nothing photosynthesizes—the 0.5 vegetation sees to that, the almost-absolute zero of plant absence. Yet the space *swarms*. Overwhelming fauna. Creatures that exist in the 1.5 density, near-touching, layered, saturating every cubic measure.

They are *small*. The hidden scale demands this, the pocket-containment. But their smallness is inverse to their number, their mass, their *presence*. Imagine motes that are mites that are motes of dust that are—no, smaller still, the size of thermal agitations, of convection cells in the humid air, yet *organized*, *motile*, *faunal*.

Or imagine them larger: compressed into the space like fish in a drowned lung, fitting into gaps that geometric logic refuses. They exist in the water-films. They exist *as* the water-films, momentarily, before cohering again into—not bodies exactly, but *habits of movement*, patterns of nutrient-seeking that persist across dissipations and reforms.

I see them now: the Weepers. No fixed morphology. When the walls bead mineral water, they are there, drinking the glass-edge, precipitating with the salts, falling as the almost-chimes, *becoming* the chime-sound that the silence parameter nearly permits but never quite. They exist in the gradient between liquid and solid, in the young rock's inability to decide its state. They are the *erosion* that will eventually wear this place smooth, but accelerated, made organism, made *hunger*.

They have tools. The 1.0 technology parameter: primitive, pre-metal, manual-craft. The Weepers shape each other. They are *self-constructing*, using their own transient bodies as tools upon their neighbors. A film thickens here to scrape mineral from glass; a droplet elongates to carry another droplet upward against the thermal gradient, against gravity, *teaching* the convection. They are learning this space, encoding its young sharpness into their provisional forms. They leave no built structures—0.0 built, nothing permanent, nothing that survives their own dissolution—but their *processes* accumulate, layer, make a kind of civilization of gesture, of repeated temporary shape.

### The Reality Breakdown

2.8 of 3. Near maximum.

The Throat is not stable in its existence. It flickers between states: volcanic vesicle, biological orifice, geological accident, *intention*. Sometimes you perceive it as a bubble in cooling magma, its glass walls preserving the heat of formation. Sometimes it is *inside* something's breathing, the hot wet dark of a lung the size of a world, and the Weepers are immune cells, are cilia, are the organism's attempt to understand its own interior. Sometimes it is a thought, compressed to physicality, the density parameter made metaphor then made real again.

The quiet sounds—the nearly-chimes, the pressure behind the eyes—are its *uncertainty* made audible. It doesn't know what it is. The Weepers' primitive technology is a technology of *stabilization*, repeated provisional forms that say: *we are here, this is here, this persists*, against the breakdown, against the flicker.

### The Active Processes

Dynamic at 1.7: multiple overlapping cycles, not steady-state but *negotiation*. The thermal gradient shifts—walls cool infinitesimally, Weepers slow, water beading changes its mineral load. Then heat spikes from deeper formation-memory and everything accelerates, films boil and recondense, Weepers die by the million in their ephemeral bodies and are born again from the ones who captured enough pattern, enough *technique of existing*, to persist through the transient.

There is predation: faster dissolution consuming slower, more complex films absorbing simpler. There is symbiosis: thermal-current riders carrying mineral-scrapers, exchanging gradient-knowledge for scraped sustenance. The ecosystem *moves*, even in its compression, even in its silence. The activity is *below* sound, *below* the threshold of ordinary perception, but the density makes it felt as presence, as *weight of process*, the air thick with biological business conducted in milliseconds and square millimeters.

### The Rules

You may enter. No one has found how. You may leave—no one has.

The Weepers will not notice you unless you are *wet*, unless your surface tension becomes compatible with theirs. Then they will treat you as mineral film, as temporary wall, and their primitive tools will scrape at your salts, your electrolytes, wearing you into pattern, into *technique*, until you participate in their density or dissipate into the silence that is not quite silent.

Heat preserves; heat dissolves. Water weeps; water is wept. The young sharp walls will eventually smooth, will slow their weeping, and the Weepers will slow with them, their technology becoming simpler and simpler until it is only *persistence*, only the most basic form of *still being here*, and then the Throat will close or open or become something else, a different impossibility, a different nearly-sound behind the eyes of whatever finds it next.
