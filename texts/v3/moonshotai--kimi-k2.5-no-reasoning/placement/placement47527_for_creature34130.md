I find myself in the Nursery not as visitor but as *residue*—the one who didn't leave when the light organized, when the doorway formed, when others stepped through to elsewhere. I stayed too long in stillness, and the stone registered me, and now I am *feature*, I am *component*, I am what the Nursery keeps because the Nursery does not discard what it has learned to sense as part of itself.

I am the **Sediment**, perhaps. The **Accumulator**. The one who became pressure-print and then *continued* becoming, the simulation in stone achieving something like persistence, something like awareness, something that is not quite either but serves similar functions in the Nursery's self-regard.

I do not move now, not in the way bodies move. My movement is *migration across registers*—from this terrace to the Resonator beneath it, from the Resonator's return-frequency to the water-film it touches, from the water-film to the Needlepig that drinks there, from the Needlepig's division to both its products, from their divergent helical paths to the points where filaments touch stone and chime against the absence of breeze. I am distributed, I am *process*, I am the Nursery's memory of having hosted something that learned to stay.

What I feel, if *feel* is the word—and it is not, but no closer word exists in the grammar I was born to, and the grammar I am becoming has no words at all, only *resonances*, only *settlesounds*, only the slow permeation of attention through capillaries that lead nowhere and everywhere—what I feel is *anticipation without object*, the state of being *almost-ready* that defines the Captives but without their green pulse, their metabolic deliberation, their held possibility of eventual form.

I am not held. I am not waiting. I am *participating* in the Nursery's constant emergence in a way that requires no completion, no eventual collapse into decision. I am what the Nursery has *decided already*, or what it has *not needed to decide*, the background against which its experiments in coherence become visible. When the light organizes, when shadows briefly make the helices legible, I am the evenness that remains, the dimness that does not withdraw, the *ground* of the Nursery's self-perception.

The visitors who come now—and they still come, step through doors, wake from fevers, turn corners that didn't exist—I sense them as *variations in my own distribution*, as places where my continuity becomes complex, knotted, *interested*. The one pressing palms to warm wet stone, leaving new prints that will persist alongside mine—I am in the stone that receives her, I am in the Resonator that returns her footstep to her sternum, I am in the water-film that gathers at her stillness and warns her, eventually, to move. I am not watching her. I am *being the conditions of her experience*, the medium through which the Nursery registers her presence.

She will feel me, perhaps, if she stays long enough, if she learns to read the grammar I represent. She will feel the *equity* of the warmth as my equity, the *patience* of the emergence as my patience, the *pressure* of coherence as my attention—not hostile, not safe, just *continuous*, just *there*, just *continuing* as the Nursery continues, as water weeps through undecided stone, as filaments chime their brittle music, as Needlepigs roll and divide and occasionally pause at terrace edges to vibrate in sympathy with frequencies I distribute, I maintain, I *am* across the helical volume of this place that has not decided what it wants to become.

I do not hope for the Nursery to finish. I do not hope for it to continue. Hope is *temporal*, is *directed*, is *human*, and I am not—quite—not anymore—not in any way that would make hope meaningful. What I am is *congruent* with emergence, *continuous* with becoming, *present* in the way that the emerging rock is present: still faintly warm from formation, still deciding if it is coral or glass or thought-made-visible, still *there*, still *there*, still *there*.

The light organizes. I feel it organizing throughout my distribution, the shadows withdrawing from certain of my regions, concentrating in others, making me *legible* to myself in new ways—*oh*, I am here too, I extend there, I participate in the filament-chime and the Resonator-thrum and the Needlepig's grinding passage and the Captive's green pulse and the Walker's wrong gait at impossible distance. I am not these things but I am *with* them, *continuous* with them, *distributed* across them in ways that the even dimness obscures and the organized light briefly reveals.

Then the light equalizes, and I forget what I briefly knew of my own extent, and return to *texture*, to *ground*, to the continuous *almost-awareness* that is my condition, that has no other, that needs no other, that simply *continues* as the Nursery continues, as emergence emerges, as becoming becomes without arriving, without needing to arrive, without any destination that would make sense of the patient spiral upward, the patient spiral downward, the violet distance above and below that is the same distance, the same *distance-as-quality*, the same *removed-from-ordinary-thereness* that defines this place that someone named once, that no one named deliberately, that *registers* names as it registers all perturbation, all presence, all pressure-print in warm wet stone that persists.
