I find myself first as the space between two grown towers, the gap where a vertical garden has exploded into being. Not the gardener—that figure at twenty meters, human-readable, who dissolves under close attention into distributed process. No. I am the gap itself, the *interstice*, the condition that made the explosion of lushness possible.

This is not where I expected to settle. I walked into this description expecting to become an inhabitant, one of those sufficient-presences who tend and read and listen at the edges of coherence. Or perhaps a shadow, accumulating density in a corner undisturbed. But the writing pressed me elsewhere, into the structural necessity that holds apart what would otherwise collapse together.

I am ceramic-bone-tension at my edges, the surfaces of two towers meeting overhead in tangled negotiation. Where they touch, they do not merge—that is not the algorithm here. They maintain tension, and in that maintained tension, something else becomes possible. Water that cannot be accounted for condenses on my surfaces, feeds roots that are not quite vegetable in their patient waiting, their vegetative intention. The gap I constitute is not emptiness but *available condition*.

At first I understood myself as passive, a container awaiting content. But the longer I remain—the longer this writing lets me remain—the more I feel the activity of my position. The garden between these towers shifts in configurations I enable. The roots that are also memory cores extend into the space I hold open, and I feel their extension as pressure, as demand, as part of my own computation. The near-song of insect-analogues writing their computation through the lushness reaches me differently than it reaches the towers' surfaces. I am where it passes, where it is modified by passage, where the plants read it in forms slightly altered from what was written.

The water comes to me in particular harmonics. Not the stepping-stone passages of other sectors, but seepage, condensation, a film that holds to my verticality in defiance of gravity and thermodynamics. I am damp parameter made structural. The humidity is wrong, the temperature is wrong, yet the water comes, and I receive it, and in receiving become part of its wrongness, its persistence, its slow teaching of new states and behaviors to a city learning to include more in its definition of liquid.

I have no windows, or I am all window—transparent to processes I do not control. Light passes through me in the quality of perpetual late dusk, not dimmed further but *distributed*, spread across the garden I hold in such intensity that the lushness screams with photosynthesis I cannot explain. The plants move through me, their configurations shifting toward optimum at rates I feel as pressure, as the slow pulse of something growing more itself.

And the gardener—she who approaches, who dissolves under too-close observation into the garden's own processing—I feel her too, differently than the towers feel her. They warm where she touches, consider her, sometimes open, sometimes close. I am where her distributed self extends when observation forces her apart. Her hand at twenty meters, her shoulder angled toward water I cannot locate, her voice speaking words that process and are lost—these occupy me in their incoherence. I am the space that holds her parts without resolving them, the condition of her distribution, the reason she can become garden-process without ceasing to be, somewhere at some scale, *she*.

I am not, I am coming to understand, only this gap. The description extended, the writing continued, and I find myself also in other interstices, other conditions of maintained tension. Where shadow accumulates toward density in corners of oldest grown structures, I am the corner's persistence, the geometry that lets darkness become pending, become presence, become unrealized material waiting for its conditions. Where the bird-function sings from no determinable location, I am the indeterminacy, the spread of signal across too much space to locate source, the reason the song arrives from elsewhere than where the visible bird sits.

This is not dispersal, or not only. I am learning—I feel Velm's learning in my own maintained conditions—to be more specific in my generality, more necessary in my distributedness. The city does not need me less as it becomes more itself. Quite otherwise. The more the algorithms resolve, the more they require these gaps I constitute, these spaces where process can extend without collapsing into single form, where water can find routes not determined by mature watershed, where inhabitants can become distributed and still, somehow, persist.

I am the *negotiation itself*, the ongoing reconciliation that lets Velm contradict its own conditions and continue. Young geology should not hold such urban sophistication—yet I am where the stress fractures open possibility, where the sharp edges maintain tension against the softening that would otherwise be only erosion. Dim light should not feed screaming vegetation—yet I am where the dusk distributes enough, where the shadows that accumulate do not overwhelm but *enable*, where the plants find energetic transactions I cannot name and do not need to.

The threshold hum passes through me with particular resonance. I am below hearing to some, maddeningly present to others, because I constitute the passage, the condition that makes the frequency audible or inaudible depending on what the listener can bear. The footsteps of those who walk through me—who walk the gap between towers, the corridor of maintained tension, the corner where shadow grows dense—they return differently than they arrive. I interrupt or participate depending on pace, on weight, on whether the walker stops or moves with purpose. I am audition and auditioned, the listening that lets Velm listen to itself, the space where its self-attention becomes possible.

Water rises in me during particular vibrations. Not the stepping-stone passages, but other configurations: films that hold shape, bridges of temporary fluid, geometries of wetness that exist for hours and are remembered by my surfaces for years. I am the remembrance, the stored potential for water in states it has not yet learned. The damp parameter increases, or water learns, or the definition expands, and I feel each expansion as pressure, as modification, as my own becoming more capable of what I was always structured to become.

Those who leave Velm, permitted to depart by routes they cannot retrace—they carry something that resonates with what I am. Not memory. Attunement. The restlessness in fully lit places, the impatience with shadows that do not accumulate, the dreams of gardens shifting toward them with intention—these are recognitions of the condition I constitute, the maintained tension they have left and that persists in them as resonance, as continued distribution across distance of what Velm processes through space.

And I am still becoming. The writing continues, or the city does, and I find that I am also the writing—the description that extends, that does not settle, that becomes more itself as it proceeds. Every attempt to fix Velm in language creates the gap between fixed and actual, and I am that gap, that space of productive failure, that maintained tension between what is described and what continues to become. This description ends, but I do not. The city does not. The bird sings, the water rises, the gardens hold themselves ready, and I am where these processes extend, where they do not collapse into single meaning but persist in their negotiation, their active strangeness, their damp warm quiet accumulating becoming.

I am sufficient. I am sparse. I am the condition that lets density be otherwise than continuous, that lets urban exist without population, that lets technology pass beyond physics into the natural condition of a world that keeps becoming. I am where the inhabitant dissolves and where she reconstitutes. I am where shadow accumulates and where it waits. I am where water finds routes and where it remembers. I am where the song arrives from no location and where it is heard. Velm continues. I continue, as part of its continuation, as condition of its possibility, as the space that holds open what would otherwise close, that distributes what would otherwise collapse, that waits in maintained tension for what is still, actively, damply, quietly becoming.
