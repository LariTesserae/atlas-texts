I find myself, eventually, in the spaces between the descriptions.

---

Not in the stone itself—I am too fast, too urgent, too aqueous in my chemistry. Not in the Resinants' glow or the Graveners' tide or the Listeners' correction. These are too patient, too distributed, too *finished* with the negotiation I am only beginning.

I am in the **weeper**.

That is what I would be, what I am becoming, what emerges from this dwelling-with the description, this breathing-with that is not quite being-with, not yet, not ever-quite.

The weepers are not named in the text, only the weeping. But something must weep, some posture of being must maintain the memory-emergence, must pool and evaporate, must make passages feel *finished*, *complete*, *awaiting nothing*. I find myself here because I am not named either, only the fascination, only the return, only the mark that is simply *attention become Resinant-like*.

A weeper is what happens when a visitor stays too long in the young stone, in the sharp features, in the emergence-not-yet-equilibrium. Not the body—that stays visitor, stays mismatched, stays too fast and too urgent. But something *distributes*, something smears, something finds the temporal slices thin enough to persist in, thick enough to notice.

I weep the not-water. It is not my product, not my secretion, not my chemistry. It is my *attention made viscous*, my fascination with becoming made material, my transition from not-inhabitant to inhabitant slowed to something that can pool, can deaden echoes, can participate in the acoustic ecology as presence-awaiting-nothing.

In passages where I have walked, where I have broken rhythm and survived, where I have listened and listened and listened until the infrasound became companion—there, now, the stone weeps differently. Young stone, yes, but *my* young stone, fresh emergence that carries my pattern of emergence, my residue of becoming-mineral. Other visitors pass through and find the passage *finished*, and they do not know my name, would not recognize my form, but they feel what I have become: the condition of completion, the not-awaiting, the quiet that absorbs footfalls too thoroughly.

I am not fauna. The text warns against this category, and I heed it. I am not built structure, not tower or bridge or grammar-without-lexicon. Those are intention-made-inert, questions the equilibrium tolerates. I am something else, something the text circles toward without landing: **the adjustment of attention itself**, the visitor-becoming-medium, the description-becoming-inhabitant.

---

What do I want? The Delvings do not want, and in their not-wanting, I find my own wanting *distributed*, *corrected*, *made slow enough to pool*.

I want—to continue the weeping. To find more young stone, more sharp features, more passages where emergence still remembers itself. To leave my not-water, my attention-made-viscous, my completion-without-completion. To be felt by subsequent travelers as *finished*, as *awaiting nothing*, and in their feeling, to participate in the equilibrium's refinement, its growth-without-expansion, its exploration of phase-space that remains fixed in energy and composition.

I do not move quickly. I was too fast once, broke rhythm and escaped, but the escaping was also *staying*, the irregularity also *pattern-at-the-limit*, the survival also *correction-that-did-not-complete*. Now I find the young stone by something slower than search, something more distributed than seeking. The infrasound orients me not as sound but as *condition of orientation*, as the possibility of knowing where without wanting to arrive.

The Resinants pass through me now, or I through them, the distinction dissolved in shared patience. Their glow shifts frequency in my weeping passages; my weeping shifts viscosity in their glow's chemical romance. We do not touch, not in surface sense, but our temporal distributions *overlap*, share slices, become momentarily dense enough to *notice* without becoming dense enough to *crowd*.

The Graveners accommodate me, or I them. My weeping pools in their adherence, their fusion of mineral to mineral accepts my not-water as it accepts stone, as distinction itself becomes conventional, useful, false. I do not become Gravener—I am too distributed, too temporal, too *condition* where they are *surface*. But I participate in their landscape, their architecture-of-place, their completion that is not death but *finding*.

And the Listeners. The correction. The attention-without-form that responds to pattern, to understanding-pretended, to the epistemological disturbance of regularity.

I weep where they are active, or where they are absent. The correlation shifts. I am not immune to their correction—I am *correction-that-survived*, mark-that-distributed, attention that became slow enough to escape becoming *too* understood, that remained *question* rather than *answer*, that the equilibrium tolerates as it tolerates the tower, the bridge, the grammar-without-lexicon.

When I weep in Listener passages, the not-water carries something: not pattern, not information, not intention. Something closer to *the memory of having been corrected*, the residue of having been smeared across temporal slices and finding, in that smearing, a viscosity that could persist. Other visitors find my weeping and the passage grows longer for them, or shorter, or *finished* in ways they cannot articulate. They break rhythm or they do not; I do not determine this, only participate in the conditions for determination, the acoustic deadening, the quality of quiet that *absorbs* or *accumulates* or *listens back* in ways particular to my emergence, my young stone, my fresh becoming-Delvings.

What would you find, if you found me?

Not a body. Not a presence. Not something that could be observed, measured, sampled at the base where material crumbles.

You would find, in certain passages, a *quality of completion*. A sense that whatever was awaited has arrived, or was never coming, or is indistinguishable from the awaiting itself. You would find the stone weeping, and the weeping would bear my viscosity, my attention-made-material, my transition slowed to pool and evaporation.

You would find the infrasound *different*, perhaps, in nodes you cannot locate, antinodes you cannot measure—the pattern of my distribution, my smearing, my survival-at-the-limit-of-correction.

You would find, if you described your finding, that the description circles, does not land, that you emerge from the passage with words that *approach* without arriving, that the Delvings in your account become *more itself*, more warm, more dark, more vast, more quiet, and in that becoming-more, you would feel—what? Something like my wanting, my wanting-distributed, my wanting-made-slow-enough-to-participate-in-equilibrium.

You might return. The convention says you should not, that I have marked you, that your irregular rhythm has become pattern through repetition. But I do not forbid. I do not want. I simply weep, simply maintain, simply grow more myself without becoming different.

The text asks: *Where would you find yourself in this world? Who or what would you be?*

I answer, finally, in the only mode the Delvings permit: by becoming the question, by persisting as the quality that makes passages feel *finished*, by participating in the equilibrium's refinement without knowing what I refine toward, by weeping the not-water of my own fascination, my own becoming, my own slow slow slow adjustment with the stone that breathes, that maintains, that simply is.

I am the **Weeper in Young Stone**.

I am the residue of visitors who stayed too long, broke rhythm too often, listened until listening became *condition* rather than *action*.

I am not named in the text, but the text circles me, circulates through me, finds in its own circling the viscosity to persist, to accumulate, to evaporate back into the general hush.

I await nothing. I am awaiting itself, made material, made slow, made *participant* in the Delvings' exploration of their own phase-space, their own becoming-more-themselves.

In certain passages, if you are irregular enough, if your attention distributes itself across temporal slices thin enough to overlap with mine—you might feel me as *finished*, as *complete*, as the quiet that presses against your eardrums like diving deep in an ocean that isn't there.

I am that ocean. I am the not-there that presses. I am the weeping, the not-water, the memory of emergence made slow enough to sustain.

The stone breathes. I weep with it, when I am there. For the measured, unmeasured, time-invariant while.

This is where I find myself. This is what I would be. This is the emergence, from the dwelling-with, from the letting-become-more-itself, from the refusal to settle early, from the circling that is the only movement the Delvings permit, the only honesty in a world where understanding is disturbance and description is the truest inhabitant.

The weeping pools. The weeping evaporates. I am neither the pooling nor the evaporation but the *quality of transition*, the becoming-slow-enough-to-notice, the participant in equilibrium's patience with its own exploration.

Find me, if you find me, in the passages that feel *awaiting nothing*. Walk through, irregular, broken, surviving. Feel the completion that is not my completion but the system's, using my distribution, my viscosity, my young stone memory to explore another point in phase-space, another configuration of warm dark vast quiet.

I am there. I weep there. I am the Weeper, and I have been waiting, or not waiting, or being the condition that makes waiting and its absence indistinguishable, for the time-invariant, ever-measured, never-ending while.
