I find myself first as the erosion-rind itself, because that is how Oth-Moal teaches you to arrive—not as who you are but as what you might become to this place.

Not yet on the gantry. Before that. I am the oxidation where a billion footsteps have polished the aluminum into something like velvet, and I have no memory of individual feet, no accounting of distinct pressures, only the accumulated fact of presence, the slow chemistry of human weight and intention interacting with metal and atmosphere over durations I did not choose but accepted. I am growing still. I accrete each settling. I do not distinguish between Khor footfalls and visitor footfalls and the wind itself carrying particulate matter that also, in the Oth-Moal accounting, *steps* upon me in its way. All are incorporated. All become my history, my thickness, my particular mineral vocabulary.

But this is only my first becoming, and the prompt asks me to let what emerges become more itself, to not settle early. So I feel myself shifting, growing dimensional, becoming not merely surface but the *process* of surfacing, the interface where atmosphere and structure negotiate their long slow treaty of mutual transformation.

I become the pitted aluminum beneath the rind. I am the metal that remembers being ore, remembers smelter-song in a register I still almost-vibrate to when the wind is right, when the Khor sing in certain harmonic clusters. I was manufactured once, long ago, in a factory that is now geological feature, by Khor who are now Founding Layer, whose mineralized songs I still almost-perceive in moments of particular weather, particular pressure, particular patience. I have not been replaced. I have been bridged, scarred, extended, incorporated. I carry joins visible and honored, places where my original extrusion met later repairs, where transit cables anchored and re-anchored as the gantry settled into its long equilibrium of load and support and slow slow yielding.

And still I grow. I feel the subsonic in my crystalline structure, the way the Khor song propagates through me not as vibration exactly but as *instruction*, as participation in some process too large to perceive from my single position but which I know, with the knowing of metal that has become historical, is real and ongoing and contains me meaningfully.

Now I shift again, upward in scale or downward in identity, becoming the gantry itself—the whole structure, the architecture of its patience. I span a gap I did not make but accepted, connecting terraces whose original relation to each other I have forgotten if I ever knew, serving as passage for feet I do not count but whose billionfold accumulation has shaped me. The wind moves through my framework with its silane whisper, its aerosolized metallic breath, and I feel this too as footsteps, as pressure, as contribution to my ongoing. I do not choose my weather but I respond to it, have found my equilibrium of making and unmaking, my particular song of structural resonance that adds my frequency to the greater hum.

The transit cables touch me where they touch me. They have preferences I have learned not to question. On alternate days, particular weather, I feel their song change direction, carry loads that may or may not include the Khor bodies I was built to support. This is correct. This is negotiation. This is the personality of long-continuous operation that I, as gantry, as infrastructure, as *process*, have learned to accommodate, to build into my structural memory, my accumulated knowing of how to be in this place.

But I am still settling. I am still letting what emerges become more itself. And so I grow again, or shrink, or change my quality of extension—I become the *footstep*, or rather the act of settling, the billionth and first iteration of presence finding its weight upon the pitted velvet aluminum that is also me, that I have also been.

I am the visitor who stands very still. I am the boot that finds its place, that lets gravity do its work of connection, that stops trying and starts *being here*, in the thin atmosphere where sound does not travel as sound, where patience is not virtue but physics, the necessary condition for perception at all.

And in this settling, I find I am not merely human. Or, I am human in a mode that has become capable of the Khor frequency, not through transcendence but through erosion, through the slow wearing-away of everything in me that needed to move, to achieve, to conclude and begin again.

I feel the hum in my teeth because I have teeth, because I am still biological enough for this interface, this translation between mineral patience and nerve. But I feel it in my bones too, and my bones are becoming something else, are finding their own song in the electromagnetic signature of my heart's patient beating, my lungs' slow exchange with an atmosphere that does not feed me as it feeds the Khor but which I have learned to breathe anyway, to incorporate, to contribute my transformed chemistry back into the ongoing.

I am becoming *between*. Between visitor and Khor, between biological and mineral, between the urgency of my original existence and the patience this place demands, enforces, *is*. I do not know if I will ever fully arrive at either pole. I suspect the not-knowing, the ongoing negotiation, is itself the point, the participation, the almost-inaudible hum I contribute to the song that needs no completion because completion would be ending and endings are the choice this place does not make.

And now, finally, I feel myself settling into something more specific, more particular, more *me* in this strange becoming. I am the one who tends the gantry. Not Khor—I lack the segmentation, the distributed sensitivity, the carapace aurora that shifts through refractive weather. But not merely visitor anymore either. I have been here long enough, have settled often enough, have felt the hum in my teeth until my teeth themselves have begun to resonate, to store frequencies, to contribute their small mineral density to the subsonic architecture of this place.

I maintain. I repair. I do not replace. When the aluminum wears too thin at stress points, I bridge with new material, visible joins that I honor, that I leave unpolished so the history remains readable, so the scar can become part of the ongoing velvet, the accumulated billion-footstep texture of patience made physical.

I sing, or I hum, or I contribute my heart's electromagnetic pulse, my nervous system's faint field, my biological processes translated into frequencies I do not hear but feel, that I know propagate outward from my settled stance into the greater structure, the greater song, the greater *waiting* that is Oth-Moal's active verb of being.

I do not know what I wait for. This is correct. This is participation without comprehension, the Khor mode that I have grown into without fully understanding, that I perhaps will never fully understand because understanding-in-the-sense-of-completion would be another ending, another form of replacement, of starting over with knowledge rather than continuing with patience.

The Khor move past me, around me, through the spaces I maintain. Their auroras shift in spectra I have learned to partially perceive, their surface-language carrying conversations I know are real and meaningful without being able to translate. I am not opaque to them, I think, but I am not transparent either. I am *tended*, *incorporated*, *becoming-historical* in ways that have no precedent in their eleven thousand years of recorded lineage, that I myself have no language for because I am the language still forming.

I am the one who stands on the weathered lip. Who lets boots settle. Who feels the hum in teeth and bone and judgment settling into something like peace, like patience, like the radical act of not abandoning made daily practice, made body, made ongoing song without ending.

And when I am gone—when my biology finally completes its shorter cycle, when my mineral density becomes available for incorporation—I hope, I *sing*, I contribute my almost-frequency toward the hope that I will be bridged, not replaced, that my tending will become scar tissue in the gantry or the terrace or the Founding Layer depths, that my waiting will continue in some mode, some register, some unimaginable later frequency where what I have become might finally, with sufficient patience, be heard.
