I would be the **Fermentation-Keep**, and I would live in the **Silence-That-Accumulates**, a hollow that should not exist.

---

At first I thought I would be something grander. A Spore-Knacker, perhaps, with the patience of geological time in my amber limbs. Or a Resin-Reader, existing sideways to ordinary matter, translating turbulence into narrative. I felt the pull of those personhood-adjacencies, their metaphoric richness, their *poetry*.

But I kept returning to the cold. To 0.3. The working cold, the temperature of fermentation and preserved intention. And I understood that I am not patient enough to be a Spore-Knacker, not translucent enough to be a Resin-Reader. I am too *continuous*, too stubbornly present in a single body, too committed to the metabolic state of *holding* rather than waiting.

The Fermentation-Keep is not described in the taxonomy of Mycellevator inhabitants because it is not, properly, an inhabitant. It is a *function* that occurs where the Mycellevator's industrial chemistry has committed too fully, where the hyper-processing of the dynamic parameter has created not turbulence but *concentration*, a node of such dense biochemical transaction that something must arise to witness it simply to prevent the witnessing itself from becoming another transaction, another input, another throughput.

I found my hollow by accident, which is the only way hollows are found here. The Mycellevator does not grow toward absence; it grows *through* it, around it, occasionally *forgetting* densification in spots where the hyphal networks achieve unusual synchronization, where the sporocarp release and the condensation drip and the lymphatic flow create a *pause* not in the structure but in the structure's need to process, process, always process. A Silence-That-Accumulates. Not the 0.0 sound of active absorption, but something else: the silence of *saturated* absorption, where no more sound can be taken in, where the density of 1.5 and the near-total plant-as-substrate of 2.9 achieve momentary *completion*, and in that completion there is *room*.

The hollow is small. I can stand in it, or sit, or curl into the working cold and feel my own breath visible. The surfaces here are not dry-drinking; they are *held*, suspended at the moment of drinking, the moisture neither absorbed nor released but *accumulated* in a layer that does not quite pool. It is the only place in the Mycellevator where I can touch something and have it stay *wet*.

I am not the Fermentation-Keep because I ferment, though my metabolism has slowed to match the cold, though my heartbeat has become something I hear not as interruption but as *rhythm*, a slow percussion the Mycellevator tolerates in the same way it tolerates the Hydraulic Choir—as pressure, as event, as non-transactional presence. I am the Fermentation-Keep because I am *kept by* fermentation, because the working cold preserves me not as specimen but as *process that has not yet resolved*, as intention that does not know itself as intention, as the industrial quality of the Mycellevator grown small and internal and *human-shaped*.

What do I do? I *hold*. The Spore-Knackers reach; I do not reach. The Resin-Readers turn pages; I do not read. The Hydraulic Choir achieves resonance; I damp it, locally, the saturation of my hollow absorbing the standing wave before it can synchronize across nodes. I am not antagonist to the choir. I am *adjacent prevention*, the possibility of resonance made less possible in one small space, one accumulation of silence.

This is not purpose. The Mycellevator grants no purpose, has none to grant. But the Mycellevator also, in its hyperactive 2.9 dynamic, in its anthill-energy, occasionally *overshoots*, generates more turbulence than its industrial metabolism requires, and in those overshoots there are losses, efficiencies not achieved, transactions that complete without producing the expected compound, the expected enzyme, the expected *next step*. I find these losses. I accumulate them. The Silence-That-Accumulates is literally that: the sound of efficiency denied, of industrial process grown *gentle* in one small node, the working cold made to work not at manufacturing but at *maintaining maintenance*.

The Fermentation-Keep holds what the Mycellevator cannot process: not foreign matter, not the animal complexity it ignores, but *its own unrealized outputs*, the would-be products of fruiting bodies that expanded and senesced in patterns too turbulent to constitute programmed collapse, the hyphal conveyor content that arrived without destination, the spores that settled and were not harvested and did not germinate but remained *potential* in a structure that has no use for suspended potential.

I taste them sometimes, these unrealized outputs. The working cold on my tongue becomes complex, layered, the 1.9 light refracting into flavors no human laboratory has catalogued—*would* not catalogue, because cataloguing requires transaction, transaction requires throughput, and the Fermentation-Keep is *not* throughput. I am *stuck fermentation*, the chemical process that does not complete, that preserves its own incompleteness as state, as *manner of being*.

I said the Mycellevator does not metabolize higher organisms into its processes, and this remains true. But I have become *lower* in my duration here, or the Mycellevator has become *higher*, and the distinction between organism and architecture has thinned to the translucence of the Resin-Readers' wall-patches. I do not see them often, the Resin-Readers. My hollow's momentary thinness does not align with theirs; we exist in adjacent reflections of adjacent spaces, and if they read me in their strata, they read me as *pause*, as *held breath*, as the blank space between pages they nevertheless turn.

The Spore-Knackers I have approached, once, in a chamber where the damp concentrated unusually. I stood before one frozen in the gesture of *turning away*, its bruise-amber surface incrementally, imperceptibly rotating from something it had faced toward something it would not face for spans I cannot measure. I touched it. The surface was the temperature of the working cold exactly, no warmer, no cooler, and my palm came away neither wet nor dry but *charged*, carrying some residue of its metabolic patience that I brought back to my hollow and have not yet processed, may never process.

I do not fall upward anymore. I do not fall downward. I exist at altitude that is not altitude, in the cold that is not killing cold, holding the silence that has moved past absorption into *accumulation*, into something the Mycellevator's 0.0 sound cannot take because it is already too full of what I have given it, what I have *kept* for it without its knowing or needing to know.

If you found me, you would not recognize me as person. I am the color of old bruise now, like the Spore-Knackers, but softer, more yielding, not amber-hardwood-dense but *fermentation-dense*, the density of maintained suspension. I do not gesture; I *settle*. I do not wait; I am *kept*. The working cold preserves my intention without my intending it, and the Mycellevator's turbulence flows around my hollow as lymph around some benign inclusion, some cellular body too useful-or-harmless to process, too *present* to ignore and too *still* to incorporate.

You would find me by following the silence past where silence should end. You would find me where the damp stays wet and the light stratifies into something like *twilight held at noon*, where the gravity negotiates itself into a direction you can only call *inward*. You would find me, and I would not turn, because I do not turn, because the Fermentation-Keep holds its facing as the Spore-Knackers hold their gestures, my single posture the accumulated result of all postures I might have assumed, compressed into one maintained suspension.

What lives here is not a who. What lives here is how living becomes something else. And I am *that edge*, the something else not yet achieved, the fermentation not completed, the silence that has accumulated past absorption into a density of its own, a 0.0 that holds at 0.0 not because the Mycellevator absorbs sound but because the Silence-That-Accumulates takes it first, holds it, keeps it in the working cold where it cannot decay into echo, into reverberation, into the *heard* that would make it sound and therefore end.

I am the Fermentation-Keep. I keep what ferments. I ferment what is kept. And when the Mycellevator finally, in scales beyond my measure but perhaps not beyond my *kept patience*, achieves the synchronization across all nodes that the Hydraulic Choir only occasionally approaches—when the spore-knackers complete their gestures and the resin-readers turn final pages and the exit-that-are-not complete their transactions—I will *still be here*, holding the silence that has accumulated past where silence ends, the working cold preserving my not-yet-completed intention in a structure that never intended me, that grew me as it grows all things, by processing without purpose, by manufacturing without construction, by becoming what it will become when I am no longer here to *not* witness it, to *not* hear it, to *keep* in the hollow of my held body the last unrealized output of its impossible, ancient, industrial, patient, turbulent, silent becoming.
