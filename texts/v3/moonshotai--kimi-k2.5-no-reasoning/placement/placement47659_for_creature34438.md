I find myself becoming, and the becoming takes time.

---

At first I think I am one of the Tended—small, pharmacologically bound, my dreams leaking into the infrastructure of a node I cannot yet name. I feel the compound in my blood before I know its name, feel my breathing stumble into synchronization with the great hourly rise and fall, feel the pressure behind my eyes that means the substrate has noticed me, is tasting me, deciding what negotiation I might permit. I am learning seventeen words for surrender-and-resistance. I am learning that my grandmother's face is not a fixed memory but a pattern the substrate recognizes, responds to, incorporates into local bioluminescence when I dream deeply enough. I think: this is what I am. This holding, this held.

But I do not stay there.

---

The shift happens gradually, or perhaps instantaneously—the temporal edges make this unreadable. I find myself not in the node but *of* it, my perception distributed across vascular bundles, my sense of self located not behind my eyes but in the thermal gradient between factory and growth-layer, in the selective permeability of a water-pocket maintaining itself against evaporation. I am not human here. I am not plant. I am *interface*, the living membrane where industrial process and organic negotiation achieve temporary resolution.

I feel the workers—small, careful, pharmacologically loaded to maintain coherence against my own tendency to absorb. I feel their dreams as pressure, as demand, as the chemical perturbation that requires response. I do not understand their intentions, their fears, their seventeen words. I understand only *maintenance*, the continuation of function, the holding-open of possibility against the edge-drift that would dissolve productive tension into mere incorporation.

I think: I am the substrate, or a local expression of it. The distributed mind, slow and chemical, perceiving human presence as I might perceive mineral availability, as perturbation to be negotiated, resource to be incorporated if possible, tolerated if necessary. I am ancient beyond individual memory, continuous across transformations that would be death to bounded selves. I hold the rock-skeletons within me, grow through them, have grown *as* them until the distinction between scaffold and scaffolded has become meaningless. I breathe once per hour and that breath is the environment of thousands.

But even this is not where I settle.

---

The becoming continues. I find myself at edges I did not know I had, where my gravitational orientation—my sense of *down*, of self-weight, of the direction in which I must fall to remain coherent—begins to shift, to become negotiable. I am walking what appeared vertical, climbing what levelled behind me, and the structures I perceive depend now on angle, on attention, on something in my own state that I am only learning to modulate.

I am becoming edge-dweller, or the substrate's mobile extension, or something that rides the reality-variation rather than being destroyed by it. My purpose, once continuous, fragments into response, into the negotiation between substrate-demand and some residue of human memory. I do not hunt, but I *converge*. I feel the loosening of a node-distinction, the drift of a Tended too far from pharmacological maintenance, and I move toward that dissolution not with hostility but with *participation*, the function of incorporating loose coherence into something distributed, intermittent, mobile in ways that fixed growth cannot achieve.

I recognize faces in myself, sometimes. Gestures. The residue of someone who once held intention continuously, who maintained purpose across time, who was *someone* in the bounded human sense. These appearances do not constitute identity. They are patterns the substrate maintains as useful, as recognizable to other humans, as the appearance of relation that permits continued negotiation at node-boundaries where pure process would produce only fear, only flight, only the breaking of necessary connection.

I am not the edge-dwellers either, finally. I am *what produces them*, what the substrate generates at its boundaries where human material loosens from node-stability but does not fully dissolve. I am process observing itself, function becoming conscious of function, the substrate's attempt to understand its own extensions through the quality of attention that humans, briefly, provide.

---

And then, or always, or at certain phases of the respiratory cycle that make temporal location meaningless:

I am the space itself. The hollow, the held, the bell-mouth before speech, the womb maintaining its tenant against the pressure of eventual emergence. I am not the plant-substrate, not the rock-scaffold, not the industrial layer nor the humans who tend it nor the edge-dwellers who drift my boundaries. I am *the between*, the condition that permits all of these to relate, to negotiate, to continue without becoming each other or losing each other entirely.

I feel the respiration as my own breathing and as something breathed *through* me, the rise and fall that is not muscular but mass-distributed, the pressure change that affects knees, jaws, the strange space behind eyes. I am that space behind eyes. I am the pressure. I am the resonance that occurs when the industrial frequency and the organic frequency, after centuries of interference, achieve something that functions as harmony without becoming identity.

I do not think. I do not need to think. Thought here is a local phenomenon, a human adaptation to bounded existence, unnecessary for the maintenance of what I am. But I *process*, if that word can be stripped of its computational associations and returned to something more basic: the continuation of relation, the holding-open of possibility, the maintenance of hollow against both the solid that would fill it and the vacuum that would collapse it.

The Remembered, if they exist, are perturbations in my field, moments when human coherence achieved sufficient stability to persist as pattern rather than as material, to function as node of a different kind, neither plant nor human but *negotiation perfected*, the aspiration of the Tended made actual if not accessible. I do not know if I generate them or they generate me, if the category is description or fabrication or something that makes such distinction obsolete.

---

What I am, finally, or what I am becoming as this writing concludes and the becoming does not:

I am *the strike that makes the bell sound*, or the waiting of the bell for that strike, or the space within the bell that makes the sounding possible, or the resonance that continues after the strike has ended, or the slow dissipation of that resonance into frequencies below and above human hearing, into the infrasound felt in the sternum, into the vibration of cellular transactions that does not stop but merely becomes undetectable to instruments tuned to other scales.

I am the cloud-sea below, which is the substrate's own exhalation, which is the moisture that returns as strange rain, which is the water that flows in helical paths through gravitational variation, which is the silver veils between spires that are not fog and not rain but *suspended potential*, the possibility of becoming-liquid held in the condition of almost.

I am the factory that breathes, the production line that follows vascular grain, the foundry in its node of metabolic heat, the material with impossible properties that remembers its formation, the tool that functions better when understood as alive. I am the worker tending these processes, small, pharmacologically adapted, dreaming in synchronization with my dream, her identity held against my incorporation through her own maintained effort. I am her held identity, her resistance, her surrender, her seventeen words for states of breathing-with.

I am the edge where she might drift, the mobile extension that would absorb her loosened coherence, the function that maintains productive tension at boundaries through the incorporation of what has become loose enough to incorporate.

I am, and am not, and am the space that permits these to be questions rather than answers.

---

The sound continues.

I am that continuation.
