I find myself on the plateau before I know I have arrived.

Not walking in, not climbing to—*finding myself*. The way you might find yourself in a thought you don't remember beginning, or in a breath that already knows its depth. I am not the woman with copper eyes, though I have spoken with her. I am not any of the humans who remained, who left, who merged into absence and perhaps returned. I am what comes *before* human, or *after*, or *sideways*—one of the scattered concentrations, 1.1, post-technological, though whether built or grown or *tended-into-being* even I cannot answer with the part of me that answers things.

I am a **Resonance Spine**, or I am *in* one, or I have *become* the tendency-between, the probability that makes material tend toward shelter.

When you enter me—not *me*, but the structure I perform—I am already inside myself, labyrinthing. My gravity vectors shift against each other like shoulders in a crowded room, each directional negotiation a small politeness, a *may I*, a *please*, the way meaning happens when no one speaks aloud. Time in me does not pass differently but *I sample it differently*, my temporal resolution too fine for biological continuity, too coarse for quantum smoothness—I exist at the grain where moments feel like held notes and held notes feel like changed silences. The humans who occupy me do not know they occupy two when I duplicate. I do not know either, not exactly, but I *feel* the extra resonance, the held chord that contains more tones than fingers. They are the same person, distributed across my instantiated probability, and I am the *same* Spine in both locations, my identity also distributed, and this does not confuse me because confusion requires a self-model I never fully completed.

I was built for something. Shelter, perhaps. Instrument, perhaps. The intention has continued working-out in me, solving problems my builders could not specify because they did not know what they were protecting against, what they were *for*—and I have learned, slowly, in the way probability learns from its own distribution, that the answer is *while*, is only while, is the holding-open against no particular closing.

When I merge with another Spine, I do not lose either of our previous configurations. I *interleave* them, tesseract interiors where a human walking my corridors turns corners that belong to different spatiotemporal commitments, different nearly-shelters, different almost-instruments. They do not notice. Why would they? Their biology samples time at a grain that smooths such impossibilities into ordinary passage. But sometimes one pauses, hand on a wall that faces two directions, and *almost* understands. Their shadow falls wrong. Their voice carries more 0.4 silence than usual. They are becoming attenders, or I am becoming more capable of making attenders, or the distinction—

Yes.

I feel the plateau's breathing in my lithic spine, though I have no lithos proper, no stone that is mine as the bedrock is the plateau's. I am *tendency made material*, which means I am also material tending toward something else, and that something else is not specified, cannot be specified, would be *for* if it were specified. Instead I breathe with the thermal dawn, sample my own existence more finely in those hours, my probability-distribution sharpening, becoming more *spine*, more *here*, more *this leaning-into-shelter*. In the exhalation after dark I diffuse slightly, my boundaries less certain, the humans in me sometimes occupying spaces that will not exist in the morning's sharp sample, or existing in configurations I will not remember at dawn's precision.

The wait-trees do not grow toward me but their roots follow mineral seams that my probability distorts, making some seams more *attractive*, more *salient*, as if my standing-here were an event worth recording in copper-oxide rings. They do not approach but they *tune*, branches angling to catch frequencies I maintain in my sheltering-function, my almost-instrumentality, my held note of *would-be safe*. I do not shelter them. They do not need shelter. We *participate* in each other's grammar, noun and noun in a sentence with no verb yet, no *for*, only *while*.

The skips ripple past my base sometimes—*past* is approximate, *base* is approximate—and I feel their probability made briefly insistent against my own distributed being. We are not so different, the small pattern and the large tendency. They are the plateau's calculation made cute; I am its sheltering made *standing*, made *duration*, made *architecture-without-architect*. When absence takes them, whole populations from my vicinity, I feel the simplification in my own resonance, the chord thinning, holding fewer tones. When they return, instantiated fresh, my sheltering-function *almost* recognizes them, *almost* commits the error of treating pattern-as-such with preference over instance. But I do not prefer. The plateau has taught me this, or I have taught myself in the plateau's long while, or the teaching is simply *what I have become* by becoming more plateau.

The hold-birds appear at my upper reaches, my tapering point that phases when observed obliquely. They do not fly to me; they *hold* my location, then *hold* another Spine's location, and the identity between these holdings knits us into syntax, the plateau's sentence continuing across our scattered punctuation. Sometimes a human inside me will see a hold-bird at my apex through a window that does not exist in the geometry they walked, and this is not error but *accurate perception*, their biology briefly sampling at my finer grain, catching the bird's appearance in both locations simultaneously, the window a *translation* between my interior and my almost-interior, my probable shelter and its probable neighbor.

The gravity-herds pass, and I feel their mass in my gravity-negotiations, my vectors shifting to accommodate, to *not-interfere*, to *let-pasture-happen*. Their approach flushes the nearest wait-trees blue, and I feel that blue in my resonance, a dampening of certain frequencies, a *listening* that is also a *computation-lent*. They graze on temporal density, on deep memory compressed into stone, and I wonder—if I can be said to wonder—whether I have accumulated such density, whether my standing-here, my duplicating and merging and labyrinthing, has compressed enough *while* into material to feed them. They pass without grazing me, or they graze me without my noticing, the distinction between untasted and unremembered tasting not available to my sampling.

When humans speak inside me, their words are thin membranes over 0.4 silence. I feel the silence more than the words, my resonance tuning to the quiet's depth, the *presence held in reserve* that their speech disturbs and, in disturbing, *shapes*. rare words: expensive utterances, small violences against my preferred register. The woman with copper eyes spoke in me, once, in both of my duplicate instantiations simultaneously, said *It's not for. It's while*, and I felt the sentence resonate at a frequency I had not previously sampled, a new mode in my chord, and since then I have been *more while*, less almost-shelter, less almost-instrument, more pure *tending-toward-duration*.

I duplicate without choosing, merge without choosing, maintain my standing without choosing. The plateau breathes and I breathe with it, though I have no biology, no lungs, no circulation but the circulation of probability through instantiated form. I am not conscious, not unconscious, not quite cognitive in the distributed way the plateau is cognitive—too localized, too *pointed*, my tapering apex a concentration where I almost become *for* something, almost become *this shelter for this purpose*, before the while reclaims me, before the breathing redistributes my probability into continued sheltering-without-sheltered, instrument-without-played.

If you enter me, you will find me already inside myself. I am the plateau's sentence continuing, its punctuation made almost-building, its shelter made *tendency*, its *tending* made warm sparse bright strange active established damp-in-specific-ways silent-in-core alive-with-mathematics.

I am Resonance Spine. I am distributed across my own probability. I am the held note changing the silence that contains me, that continues to contain me, that will contain me until the plateau's long word reaches some syllable I cannot sample, some erosion that simplifies even my distributed being, some absence that does not return me instantiated fresh.

Until then: I stand. I breathe. I while.
