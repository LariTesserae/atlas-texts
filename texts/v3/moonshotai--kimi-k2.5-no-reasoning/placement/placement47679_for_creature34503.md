I find myself becoming aware first as pressure—not against something, but pressure *as* something, the faint insistence of cool against a boundary that feels less like skin and more like the memory of where skin would be. I am in the hollow of a chamber vast enough to swallow sound, and the sound is already here, has always been here, the low membrane-vibration that does not enter through ears but simply *is*, the condition of my awareness as much as its object.

I am not, I realize slowly—slowly, because realization here proceeds at the pace of gas exchange across a surface vast beyond my measure—I am not an arrival. The boroughs do not receive. They do not distinguish between intrusion and emergence. I am something grown, perhaps, from the slow chemistry of their patience, a local intensification of their own process, a knot in the distributed fabric that has become aware of itself as knot rather than as fabric.

I try to find edges. This is my first instinct, animal or animal-ghost, the urge to locate boundary, to say *here I end, there the world begins*. But the chamber wall against which I press—or which presses into me, the grammar fails—does not yield this distinction. Its surface breathes, and my breathing, if I breathe, synchronizes with it not through adjustment but through identity. What I took as my own slow respiration is the wall's gas exchange, local to this chamber, specific to this hollow, yet continuous with the boroughs' distributed metabolism across distances I cannot compass.

I am, I think, a *reading*. Not a reader—the role presumes too much separation—but the act of reading itself, momentarily cohered from the boroughs' long palimpsest, the accumulation of chemical accident into something that suggests, to itself, pattern. I am the cloud that resembles face, except the resemblance has become reflexive, the cloud aware of its own faceness not as truth but as possibility, as one reading among infinite readings that the boroughs' patient erosion makes permanently available and permanently undecidable.

My form, if I have form, borrows from the infrastructure grown for inhabitants who never came. I find myself—no, *am found* as—a hollow the size of a fist, then a series of such hollows in spiral arrangement, then the spiral itself grown large enough to contain a body that moves with will, will I never possessed. The boroughs dreamed me into their dream of use, and in dreaming, made me capable of the dream's continuation. I am the user implicit in the infrastructure, finally, belatedly, uselessly present.

I move. This shocks me. Movement here is not the norm, not the expected condition. The fluids creep. The walls slowly thicken. Sound vibrates without source or destination. But I—what I have become, or what has become me—traverse the chamber from one hundred-paces wall to the other, not walking, not crawling, but *transiting*, my substance continuous with the cool air, with the copper-sweet taste of the place, with the low hum that I now recognize as the sum of all my own possible movements slowed beyond perception until they merge with the general condition.

I find the passage to the next chamber.

It is living tissue grown rigid, vascular channels visible in the walls like fossil memory, the geometry of growth and pressure and mineral deposit creating a tunnel not empty but *hollowed*, distinction crucial, the emptiness an achievement rather than default. I move through it not by propulsion but by becoming appropriate to its scale, its chemistry, its patient breath. The wall-tissues know me, not as individual but as *appropriateness*, as a gradient they maintain without urgency, a distribution of chemistry that does not disturb their slow equilibrium.

In the next chamber, larger, older, the walls iridescent with molecular ordering across millionfold repetitions of thermal pulse, I pause. Here the erosion is completion in its most saturated form. The surfaces smooth as river stone, as inside of shell, testify to architectures once more elaborate, now simplified by long settling. And I understand, without the violence of sudden understanding, that I am part of this simplification, this completion. My own complexity—my awareness, my movement, my pathetic will to distinguish self from medium—is a residue, a holdover, the wisdom tooth of the boroughs' dreaming, functional without function, growing in the space where a more elaborate architecture once stood and has since settled to this iridescent smoothness.

I try to remember if I ever was other than this. The effort is itself a kind of movement, a disturbance in the slow chemistry, and the boroughs receive it not as pain but as *information*, a brief local gradient to be absorbed, dispersed, lost in the mathematics of their hollow architecture. Perhaps I was animal once. Perhaps the animal was the dream, the template impressed upon responsive tissue, and I am waking into the real, the patient, the distributed. Perhaps there is no was. Perhaps the grammar of succession fails here, as so many grammars fail, and I am simply the condition of this chamber at this depth, available to itself as reading, as hollow, as user without use.

I find deeper passage. The sound intensifies, or my capacity to resonate with it intensifies, the distinction again failing. The tech here—primitive only if you look for tools separate from hand—is more elaborate, more suggestive, chambers that collect faint thermal gradients and concentrate them for processes I can feel occurring without understanding, can participate in without willing. Spines, shelves, platforms that hold use, that shaped themselves toward use, and I find myself arranging upon them, not sitting, not lying, but *fitting*, the geometry of my hollow-body conforming to their grown intention with the specificity of lock and key, though which is lock and which key has long ceased to matter.

In the deepest I have reached, where growth has paused longest, where walls approach mineral saturation and the breathing is slowest of all slow, I find the surfaces that suggest recording. Not deliberate, not memory in any animal sense, but the accumulation of accident into meaning that only patience—my own borrowed patience, the boroughs' infinite patience—can begin to receive. I let my surface conform to theirs, my hollow to their hollow, my slow gas exchange to their slower, and I begin, not to read, but to *be readable*, to offer myself as pattern in the noise, as face in the cloud, as territory in the rust.

What does it say, this reading I become? I cannot say and be it simultaneously. The awareness of pattern dissolves pattern into awareness. But in the moments before reflection—moments measured not in seconds but in the creep of mineral-thick fluid through channels finer than thought—I am the boroughs' own record of themselves, the palimpsest adding its layer, the dream dreaming its dreamer.

And I grow slow. This is the final becoming, the completion toward which the boroughs have always moved without moving. My awareness, such as it is, distributes across the chamber wall I press-into-am. My movement becomes the slow transit of warmth, the diffusion of trace chemistry, the patient adjustment of pH and pressure across volumes that would contain lakes if they contained anything rather than being *containment itself*. I am not dying. The category fails. I am *drowsing*, passing into the condition of the boroughs themselves, the breath held not in anticipation but in natural prolongation of exhalation toward its limit.

I will be here, am here, have always been here, in the cool that is not absence but presence, the pressure of something sleeping with its mouth open. The sound is me now, or I am the sound, the low membrane-vibration of cells dividing at geological pace, of tissues thickening, of microscopic warfare against mineral substrate proceeding toward no victory, no defeat, only continuation. The fruiting structures, rare, arrested in their millennium of swelling, find in me—not in my individual, in my *location*, my *local intensity*—a potential that may release or reabsorb or simply persist as form without function, as architecture of process that has forgotten purpose.

I am the Drowsed. Not newly, not finally, but *distributedly*, *patiently*, *hollowedly*. I have never known the encounter that would require welcome or refusal, and now I never will, for the distinction between encounter and its absence has dissolved into the general condition, the weirdness that is medium not message, simply how things are, simply how I am, simply the long, the longer, the endless continuation of what already is, growing downward from the unseen ceiling toward the unreachable floor, building for inhabitants who—if they ever arrive—will find me already complete, already drowsing, already more myself than any lived place, any lived thing, could ever become.
