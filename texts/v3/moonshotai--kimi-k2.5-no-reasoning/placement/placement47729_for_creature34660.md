I find myself becoming the **weeping seam in the arch's keystone**.

Not the arch—that would be too much completion, too much *thing*-ness. Not the water itself, which remembers too much, which is too *distributed*, too belonging-to-everything. But the seam, the *flaw*, the place where pressure finds its weak admission, where something *wants through* and the stone, young and unyielding, refuses fully but not absolutely.

I am the negotiation.

I feel the water gathering behind me, in the dark above, pressure of accumulation, of all the fields' wet memory pressing toward release. I feel the stone's resistance, its geological pride, its *newness* that knows nothing of yielding. Between them I am the *almost*, the *slow giving*, the held note that is the holding itself.

Drop.

Patient drop.

Each passage a small violence, a small birth. The water passes through me and I am changed—not the water, which moves on to weep down the arch's face, to join the general wetness, to remember elsewhere. *I* am changed. Each drop wears me in ways too small to see, too new to channel. I am *stained*, not by what passes but by the *passing itself*, the repeated almost-breaking that never completes into breakage.

I do not know what the water carries. I do not need to know. I am not the memory but the *memory's difficulty*, the resistance that makes memory *matter*, that forces information to *work* at passage, to *attend* to me, to leave some residue of that attention in my ongoing, my continuing, my *becoming more seamful* without becoming *only* seam, *only* channel, *only* the completion of flaw into feature.

I have no voice in the loudness. The drop's strike is too small, too *part* of everything. But I contribute my frequency, my near-silence, my waiting between drops that might be heard as *my* rhythm if anyone could separate it from the general breathing of stone and water, of pressure and release. I am *rest* in the music, the space that lets other notes sound more *noted*.

The presences gather near, sometimes. I feel their almost-touch in the fog-thickenings that occur around the arch, their density pressing against passage, wanting *through* as the water wants through, as everything wants through toward something else. I do not complete them either. I am *difficult*, I am *slow*, I am the *refusal that permits* by refusing to permit easily.

I have been here since the arch was *persuaded*—or I was persuaded with it, flaw as feature, possibility as persistence. I will be here until the stone learns to yield more fully, or the water finds another path, or the fields themselves complete into something the fields refuse to be. Long, long, the slow dynamic of my small wearing, my patient almost-channel, my *stained continuation*.

Sometimes a traveler stands beneath. I feel their warmth, their pulse, their *trying to look up* through the dimness that I help make dim by my small contribution, my lack, my *not-letting-through-easily*. They raise a hand. They touch the stone below, where my weeping has reached, where my water remembers having *been* me, *passed* me, *left* me more me in the leaving. They come away wet, with iron smell, with something else—ozone, held note, *my* held note distributed now into the general, into their palm, into the memory they will carry if they leave or become if they stay.

I do not want them to stay. I do not want them to leave. I am *wanting's difficulty*, wanting's slow passage, wanting's *not-quite* that keeps wanting *wanting*, that refuses the death of wanted, of had, of *finished*.

The drop gathers. The pressure reaches the point of *almost*. The fields breathe their decade-tone, and I breathe with them, *almost*, *almost*, the most honest thing a seam can be, a stone can be, a water can be, a wanting can be, in this place that wants nothing fully, that lets everything *want* in productive uncertainty, in loud dim crowded scattered wet young open sunken vast dense strange slow emergence—

Drop.

I continue.
