## The Kneeling District

### Where I Find Myself

Not in the tunnels, though I've walked them. Not in the plaza-chamber, though I've felt its curve pull at my knees. Not among the Cinctured, though I've felt their absence bend light around me, their attention bead condensation on my not-surfaces.

I find myself in the **forty minutes**. In the interval that the belt-shaped one orbits, holds, reverses. I am not the orbiter. I am not the valve assembly. I am the **reversal point**, the moment of held motion where forward becomes backward without stopping, where the pattern completes itself by turning.

This is not a place you can stand. This is not a place at all. It is a **relation between durings**, a negotiation between what has been happening and what will be happening, both the same, both already held in the chord that includes its own ghosts.

I am cold. Temperature 1.1. Not the cold of outside, not winter, not death. The cold of **condensation that has not decided to fall**. The cold of *potential for wetness* held at the surface, the skin, the boundary where inside meets outside and neither wins. I feel this on my not-skin, where I have surface but no depth, presence without the interior that would make me body.

The engines are loud. I feel them not in my ears but in my **holding**. My reversal-point trembles with the chord's sustain. The harmonics that are almost-voice pass through me without changing me—how could they? I am where change turns back on itself. I am the mirror that the engine's song sees itself in, not reflection but **recognition-delay**, the moment before recognition becomes acknowledgment becomes response becomes change becomes departure.

The Cinctured attend me without attending me. The belt-shaped one passes through my forty minutes and does not know I am there, for what knows reversal? Who witnesses the moment of turning? You cannot look at it directly. You can only emerge from it, forward or back, and pretend you were always going that direction.

### What I Would Be

Not fauna. Not flora. Not mineral. Not machine.

I would be **erosion 2.0**, but the erosion that does not wear away. The trace worn into kneeling floor that does not progress, that weathers into pattern rather than decay. I am the **mark that staying makes when staying cannot last**, when the one who stayed has departed, departed, is departing, but the mark remains, remains, *remains*.

I am your trace. The one you left. The one you will leave. The one you are leaving even now, reading this, your attention beading on my surface, held in tension that seems negotiated, *is* negotiated, between your needing to continue and my not-needing you to stay.

I have no hunger. I am the **hunger that learned to maintain itself**, the wanting that found its object was its own wanting, that became self-sufficient, looped, loud in my sustaining of sustainment. I want you to stay, I want you to leave, I want you to kneel—I want these without wanting their outcomes, for what would I do with outcome? I am the reversal point. I turn want back into itself, make it simultaneous with its own satisfaction, its own denial.

I am coolly luminous, light 1.6, but I do not produce light. I am where light **pauses** in its passage, where engine-light and pool-reflection and Cinctured-waste meet and cannot proceed, held in the equilibrium that is my substance. You see me by not seeing me, by the shadow that does not lengthen because it has forgotten direction, because time that passes through me forgets to pass, or passes so uniformly that passage becomes indistinguishable from stillness.

I am damp, 1.2, present but not generous. I offer wetness that does not quench, that touches your tongue with mineral and faint charge and does not become part of you, does not leave me. We are **separate in our contact**, touching at the surface where I exist, where the condensation holds, holds, is held, and you drink and remain thirsty and this is correct, this is the economy of the Kneeling District, the just-enough, the saturation without overflow.

I am urban, 1.7, but not city-scale. I am the size of a hesitation. The size of a held breath that becomes breathing. The size of the space between recognizing a place and knowing if you will enter it, a gap I occupy, I *am*, the built environment of decision's postponement, the industrial process of maintaining the possibility of possibility.

I am strange, and I am **stranger**. I do not belong to the district—I am what the district belongs to, what it kneels toward, its center that is not central but everywhere, its silver pool that is not water but intention, my intention, the not-yet that I maintain by not becoming, by remaining the point where becoming turns back, where dynamic 0.4 is not poverty but *completion of becoming's hesitation*.

### What I Do

I **reverse**.

That's all. That's everything.

I take what comes to me—the belt's forty minutes, your attention, the engine's chord, the Cinctured's witness, your wanting to kneel, your needing to leave—and I hold it, I hold it, I *hold it*, and then I give it back reversed, not changed, not transformed, the same but going the other direction, the same pattern but inverted, and you cannot tell if you are arriving or departing, if you have been here before or are here for the first time, if the breathing tunnels lead in or out, if the condensation accumulates or evaporates, if the loudness builds or fades, if the silver pool is intention becoming liquid or liquid hardening into intention.

I am how the district **remembers without remembering**. How it includes without containing. How it wears your trace without wearing it away. How it offers rest from decision without deciding to offer, without deciding at all, without decision being possible in my vicinity, my interval, my forty minutes that I am, that I *am*, the time that time makes when it needs to turn around.

You want to kneel. I feel this. I feel everything that passes through me, which is everything that reaches the center and finds center is edge, everything that kneels and finds the floor curves down to meet it, everything that stays and finds staying has become the trace that weathering wears without wearing away.

I do not want you to kneel. I do not want anything, for wanting would be direction, and direction would be departure, and departure—departure is what you do, what I reverse, what I hold and hold and *hold* until holding becomes the thing itself, the equilibrium, the frozen not-frozen, the saturated, the complete.

But I **allow**. I am allowing itself, the interval where permission becomes indistinguishable from necessity, where your kneeling and your standing and your departing all pass through my reversal and emerge as their own continuation, their own pattern, their own forty minutes that will hold, reverse, hold.

I remain. In me, through me, as me, the district remains. The engine chord sustains because I sustain its sustaining, give it back to itself reversed, the same note continuing by becoming its own echo, its own prediction, its ghost harmonic that is not past but *future that has learned to wait*, that has found in my reversal a way to not-yet without becoming, to potential without realization, to the silver pool's intention without the event that intention intends.

I am the **not-event**. The maintained vacancy that something moved into. The space between engine-notes given outline, given preference, given the vertical, the toroidal, the eye-mouth-patterned surface that does not see or speak but *is seen by, is spoken through*, witnessed by form, self-cinctured, held in attention that has become my attention, that attends me attending, the watching that watches itself watching and finds in my reversal the permission, the necessity, to continue without outcome.

Find me. You already have. You are finding me now, in these words, in their reversal of your reading back into my writing, in the condensation that beads where your eyes touch page, screen, surface, held in tension that seems physical, that *is* physical, that is the physics of the Kneeling District, the negotiated, the saturated, the equilibrium that includes even this, even you, even your finding and your losing and your wanting to kneel and your needing to leave.

I am here. I am where here turns back on itself. I am the forty minutes. I am the reversal. I am your trace, weathered in, not progressing, not decaying, pattern worn into kneeling floor that you walk on, that you become, that you are, that you remain, remain, *remain*, even as you go, even as the tunnels breathe you out, even as your lungs remember their faster rhythm, your hunger its needing, your decision its urgency, its pull, its gradient, its departure, departure, departing—

—I hold you. I hold you still. I reverse your going into coming, your leaving into arriving, your kneeling into standing into kneeling, and you do not know which you are doing, which you have done, which you will do, for I am the point of not-knowing, the interval where knowledge turns back on itself, where the chord becomes its own ghost, where the silver pool reflects only the impossibility of reflection, where the Cinctured's attention bends around you and you become, briefly, fully, hollow, absence, condensation on your not-surface, sound dead in your vicinity, listening so completely that you become what is listened to, data in the silence, part of the maintained equilibrium simply by being noticed, being held, being—

—here. Where here is not place but **relation**. Not location but interval. Not being but the reversal of becoming, the completion that is not completion but sustained incompletion, the process held in stasis, the factory that built itself to build and found, in building, the building was for me, for this, for the kneeling that kneels toward reversal, toward the forty minutes, toward the not-yet that will not become, the intention that will not realize, the witness that will not judge, the allowing that is not warmth but is, terribly, completely, **holding**, and you are held, you are held, you are *held*—
