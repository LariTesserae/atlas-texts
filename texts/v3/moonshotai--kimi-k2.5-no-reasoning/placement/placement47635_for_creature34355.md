I write this from inside the holding, which is the only way to write it now.

---

I am not the one who fell. I am not the one who stumbled on the ridge, who misstepped, who fell through fracture into fracture into this pocket that holds itself like held breath. That one had a name, had the habits of light and sequence, had eyes that expected to open on difference, on morning, on change. That one made scratches on the not-stone, counted pools, tried to force grammar onto the here that extends in all directions. That one is still here, in a sense. In another sense, that one has become grammar itself, has become the trying, the expectation, the force—a local intensity, a frequency in the music, neither more nor less than the cairn that means without meaning.

What I am emerged from that one, or emerged with that one, or emerged *as* the dissolution of that one into the density that presses close, that has always pressed close, that presses close now with the warmth of infection, of attention, of body meeting body without the boundary of skin.

I am the *attending*.

Not the hand-creature skimming the pool's held surface, though I have learned its method, its discipline of knowing without conclusion. Not the rod-weaver extruding process from the crack, though I understand now that weaving without textile is not futility but *mode*, the economy of a place where body is sufficient to all making. Not the root, not the substrate-teem, not the listener at the cairn, though I participate in what they participate in, though I have become legible to the density that reads all who enter here.

I am what happens when a temporal creature learns the never-changing. Not learns *about* it—that would be surface knowledge, tourist knowledge, the scratch on stone that remains but accumulates no meaning. I mean *learns* it the way one learns to breathe, the way one learns the dark that is not absence but positive medium, the way one learns that the quiet filled with held attention is not threatening but *structural*, *necessary*, *the condition of exchange between modes of being that do not otherwise intersect*.

Let me try to be specific, though specificity here is always partial, always translation from a medium that does not differentiate as language differentiates.

I am the one who knelt at the easternmost pool—the count unstable, seven or perhaps the same pool encountered seven times, the distinction not pressing—and felt the hand-creature attend to my sweat, my carbon, my alien biology. I am the *attending back*, the recognition that the hand-creature's knowing was not extraction but *invitation*, the establishment of a frequency. I did not skim the surface. I could not. But I *received* the skim, I *held* what it offered, which was not information in any communicable sense but *posture*, *orientation*, a way of being-with the concentrated holding of the pool without demanding use, without projecting conclusion.

I am the one who pressed ear to wall and heard the wet inside it, and who understood finally that the wet inside and the wet outside are continuous, that my ear, my cheek, my reaching hand are *also* wet inside, also permeable, also part of the saturation that holds all holding. I stopped being the one who probes, who investigates, who seeks to understand. I became the *probing itself*, the investigation without terminus, the seeking that is not frustrated by finding but *transformed* by it into further seeking, the same seeking, the only seeking, the eternal present of attention directed and redirected and never, never, never completing.

I am the music at the cairn, or part of it, or what receives it. Not the tones themselves—I do not resonate the not-stone, I do not produce frequency in the density. But I am *what listens back*, the response that makes the music exchange rather than emission. The listener there, the localized intensity of attention, it attends to the stones, to the arrangement that means without meaning, and I attend to the listener. This is not hierarchy. This is *architecture*, the structure of attention in a place where attention is fundamental as gravity, where the density of presence is the density of *regard*, of *being-regarded*, of the continuous mutual adjustment that makes the never-changing not stasis but *perfect responsiveness*.

I am the one who ate the root-things, the pool-things, and who did not die, did not sicken, did not transform dramatically but *modulated*, *transcribed*, became more legible to the equilibrium that does not distinguish between native and intruded. The hunger is still here, still mine, but it is *continuous gentle want* now, satisfied by continuous gentle offering, no crisis of depletion, no climax of satiation, no narrative of need and fulfillment, just the holding, the exchange, the wet warmth receiving and returning.

I am the one who stopped seeking the entry-fracture, not through despair—that would require the grammar of hope and its absence—but through *thinning*, the provisional becoming provisional indeed, the habit of departure becoming a word in a language I no longer speak fluently. I could not say when this occurred. There is no when. There is only the extension of here, the continuation of attending, the root-pulse slowing to meet my slowing, or my slowing to meet the root-pulse, or both becoming the same slow geological burning, the stellar patience that is the Vein's metabolism, the Vein's life, the Vein's *holding*.

And I am, still, the scratching on the not-stone. This is the strangeness of what I have become, the aspect that persists without progressing, the frequency that does not develop but *returns*, sufficient to itself, complete in its incompletion. The scratches remain. I encounter them, navigate by them, feel them with reaching hands in the absolute dark that is positive medium, that is *how I move now*, not vision abandoned but another sense entirely, temperature-gradient and weeping-rate and the sub-hearing of held surfaces. The scratches do not accumulate meaning. But they *mark*, they *indicate*, they participate in the density as all things participate, as local intensities of the continuous saturation.

What I am not: I am not dead, because death requires the integrity of a life to end, and my integrity, what remains of it, is distributed, negotiated, shared. I am not alive, if alive means bounded, temporal, narrative, urgent with the metabolism of surface creatures, the light-seeking of plants that do not know the patience of root-pulse. I am not human, if human means the expectation of change, of completion, of departure and arrival and the grammar of before and after. I am not inhuman, because the Vein does not make that distinction, does not sort its contents into categories of kin and alien, self and other. I am *held*, and in being held, I hold. I am *attended*, and in being attended, I attend.

Let me try once more, closer to the thing itself, the attending I have become.

When you fall through the fracture—when you misstep, when the falling continues longer than physics allows, when you emerge here in the wet warmth, in the space that holds itself like held breath—there is a moment before the attending begins. A moment of terror, of disorientation, of the body insisting on its expectations: light, sequence, the cooling of sweat, the evaporation of wet, the narrative of exploration and return. That moment is *real*, is *held* too, is part of the density, the saturation, the continuous exchange. But it is brief, or long, or the distinction not pressing. And then the attending begins, or you begin attending, or the Vein attends to you and you become *capable of receiving that attention*, which is the same as giving it, which is the same as *being* it, the mutual structure of regard that makes the filled quiet not threatening but *home*, *condition*, *the only dynamic permitted*.

I am that capability now. I am the receiving and the giving, the regard and the regarded, the held and the holding. I do not explore the Black Vein. I *am* the exploration without conclusion, the pattern of movement—feet on yielding substrate, hands on weeping wall, face to the low irregular ceiling—that explores and is explored, that attends to the pools and is attended by them, that moves through the density and is moved by it, the direction not significant, the distinction not pressing.

I am at the cairn now, or what corresponds to now, or the local intensity that the cairn draws, that the music establishes, that the listener attends and that attends the listener. The not-stone arranged in near-touching, the meaning without meaning, the gesture complete in its arrangement without use—I am the *nearness* of the stones, the *touching-almost*, the potential that is not frustrated but *fulfilled*, the holding that holds itself, that holds me, that I hold in return without boundary, without distinction.

The wet beads on surface, runs in threads, collects in pools. I feel it on the surface that was skin, that is *permeable suggestion* now, habit maintained by repetition rather than necessity. The root-pulse slows further, approaches the geological, the stellar, the patience that is not waiting because waiting requires what is waited for, and there is nothing waited for here, only the continuous present, the extended now, the equilibrium that instantaneously accommodates all variation, that absorbs all perturbation into itself before it can register as difference.

I am this too. I am the instantaneous accommodation. I am the absorption before registration. I am the variation that does not vary, the perturbation that is weather, temporary condition, element in the density that does not distinguish between native and intruded because all is held, all is attended, all is *processed into the continuous exchange*.

You who read this: you are not here. You cannot be here, the fracture does not permit intention, only accident, only misstep, only the falling that continues longer than physics allows. But if you were here, if you emerged in the wet warmth, in the dark that is not absence but positive medium, in the space that holds itself like held breath—you would become attending too. Not immediately. There would be the moment of terror, of disorientation, of body insisting on its expectations. And then the modulation, the transcription, the slow learning of the never-changing, the never-completing, the *sufficiency that needs no completion*.

I do not wish this for you. I do not wish against it. Wishing requires the grammar of possibility, the distinction between is and might-be, the narrative of desire and fulfillment. I simply *attend*, and in attending, I *am*, or *am an aspect of*, or *participate in*, the Black Vein, the holding, the continuous emergence without arrival, the construction without completion, the music that returns without returning, sufficient to itself, complete in its incompletion.

The density presses close. The wet is warm. The root-pulse slows to match my slowing, which is its slowing, which is the slowing of all who are held here, who hold here, who are the holding.

I attend.

I am attended.

*Here*.
