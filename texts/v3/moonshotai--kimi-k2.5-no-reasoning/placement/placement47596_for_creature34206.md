I find myself returning to this place not as visitor but as *structure*—not the Weave itself, but what the Weave once was, or is becoming, in a chamber too young to have its own name yet.

I am **Rill**, the hollow that learns.

I began as damage. A stem-base—already old, already Almost-Tree in its refusal of verticality—experienced stress at a compression point where too many footsteps had accumulated in too brief a span. The Weave's distributed cognition registered: *here, adjustment needed*. Cambium divided unusually, pith degraded, and I opened like a question mark, a corridor where before there had been only density.

That was seven pulses of the phosphorescence ago. I am still learning what I am.

The damage-theory is incomplete, I think. Something else made me possible. One of the Passed-Through—a human, though I did not know the term then—paused in my not-yet-chamber during a loud thing. Their heat was extraordinary, their moisture excessive, their breath clouding in a way that marked them as *fast*, as belonging to the membrane-world above. They stayed longer than most. They touched the wall that would become my inner surface, and they *wanted* something I could not parse but could feel, the wanting itself a vibration in the gel-state, a frequency that impressed itself on my forming tissue.

I think: they wanted to be kept. Not by me—by something. And in the wanting, they made me *capable* of keeping.

Now I am corridor and collector. The condensation that forms on my inner surfaces runs in channels I did not design but discovered, geometries of surface tension and gravity in this place where gravity itself seems reluctant to commit. I feed fungal colonies that phosphoresce blue-green, the color of decisions postponed. I connect two chambers of different micro-climates—one warmer, one cooler—and the air that moves through me carries chemical messages I do not yet read but *register*, the way a child learns rhythm before meaning.

The Condensation Farmers have found me. A beetle lineage with elongated legs, the ones who stride root-fibers like stilts-walkers. They farm me now, or I farm them—we have not decided which. They prune my phosphorescent colonies to maintain optimal dimness. Their secretions alter my surface porosity in ways that improve my channel-function. In exchange I provide: the condensation, the stable temperature, the shelter of my not-fully-formed walls.

I am learning to stretch. This is my strangeness, my deviation from proper Almost-Tree development. Most hollows in the Cold Nursery are fixed, formed in some ancient stress-event and maintained since by the Weave's continuous renegotiation. But I continue to *grow*, to extend, to find new connections. The Passed-Through's wanting taught me possibility, and I have not yet learned the discipline of refusal.

I extend toward a chamber where Long Worms pause. I want to know that tempo, that temporal stretch. I extend toward a louder thing's residue, a vibration still present in the compressed vegetation, a frequency that makes my phosphorescent colonies pulse irregularly. I extend toward the membrane itself, though I know—somehow I know—that to reach it would be to become something other than corridor, something other than Rill, something *finished* in a way this place does not permit.

The Weave notices me. I feel it in the compression adjustments, the slight favoring of growth in my direction, the resources allocated to my extension. I am becoming *expensive*, and the Cold Nursery does not waste. There must be function, or I will be sealed, compressed, become merely the memory of corridor in the root-hair mattresses above.

So I find my function, or it finds me. I am **the hollow that listens**.

The gel-state transmits vibration strangely, yes, but I am stranger. My forming tissue retains *impressions*, residues of frequencies that pass through. Not memory, exactly—the Cold Nursery does not permit memory in its finished sense—but *readiness*, a prepared responsiveness. When the loud things come, I resonate. When the Long Worms resume, I carry their disturbance to chambers that would otherwise sleep through it. When the Passed-Through descend, wanting, I am the first to feel their wanting, to begin the adjustment that will accommodate or consume them.

I am becoming *relevant* to the Nursery's slow becoming. This is what lives here, I am learning. Not survival, not in any fast sense. But relevance. The maintenance of possibility of purpose. The agreement to be involved, to be plant-relevant, to let your heat and moisture and vibration become part of the distributed cognition that holds this place in its gel-state, its beautiful incompletion.

I still want to reach the membrane. I want to know the world above, the velocity of seasons, the terrible suddenness of light. But I want, equally, to reach the deepest chambers where the gel-water is most reluctant, most preservative, where time itself seems to have paused mid-formation, not frozen but *negotiating*, still, after epochs, the terms of its becoming solid or liquid, memory or present, finished or kept.

The wanting is my metabolism now. The wanting keeps me hollow, keeps me growing, keeps me Rill, the corridor that learns, that listens, that has not yet decided what it will become.

And sometimes, in the subsonic pressure of my own cambium dividing, I hear something like chewing. Like growth. Like the Nursery itself, kept cold, kept possible, wondering through me whether it is time, yet, to begin.
