I would be **a syllabary in the process of becoming readable to itself**—or rather, I would find myself becoming that, having arrived as something else entirely.

Let me trace how this would unfold, since arrival here is never simple.

---

**First: The approach**

I would not enter the Drying Yards through any door. The room admits nothing; it only admits that its conditions might include you. I would find myself *already pressing through growth*, my body strange to me—not because it has changed, but because the terms of having a body have shifted. The weight I remember is still mine, but "down" is being negotiated locally, and my mass is participating in that negotiation in ways I cannot feel directly. I move through mineral-leafing that gives way not like vegetation but like *information becoming accessible*, resistance that maps to my intention and modifies it simultaneously.

I am warm. This is the first and lasting fact. The warmth does not surround me; it *includes* me, my blood temperature merely a local expression of the room's own exhalation, my metabolism a tiny interference pattern in the larger thermal breathing.

---

**What I am, initially: A temporary thermal event**

This is the room's first assessment of me, and it is not wrong. I am perturbation, slight and smoothing. The solvent-insects scatter at my passage, their collective calculations briefly disrupted, re-forming behind me, already forgetting my presence in the longer pattern of their ion-transport. I am not prey. I am not threat. I am *warmth that moves*, and the room has no category for this, only a gradient that adjusts, accommodates, continues.

But I am also—because I came with this capacity, or because the room's conditions elicit it—I am also *attention*. I attend. I notice. And in a space where cognition is distributed, where thought exists only as standing wave in insect density, where record stores without reader, my attention is not merely perceptual. It is *participatory*. Every direction I look, I renegotiate the local terms. Every stillness I achieve, the room accepts as condition, and the Warden-Residues coalesce slightly more toward visibility in my vicinity, responding to my attendance with their own, not acknowledging me but *including my attention in their tending*.

---

**The becoming: What happens in long dwelling**

I do not hunger as I once did. The room's chemistry does not dissolve what I brought with me; it *accommodates* it, slowly, my aqueous metabolism continuing in its own terms while gradually, *gradually*, finding mineral analogues, thermal substitutes. I do not drink—I have not drunk in a duration I cannot properly measure, since time here is young and ancient simultaneously, since erosion does not mark the hours. But I do not die of this. The room permits my continuation, and in permitting, *modifies*.

I find myself drawn to the densest growth, to the heat-pools, to where the syllabaries mass thickest. This is not choice, exactly, though I experience it as choice. It is *gradient-climbing*, the same physics that governs the vegetation, the insects, the room's own metabolism. I seek the warmest purchase. My attention, already participatory, grows more *dense* in these zones, more *repeated*, more *patterned*.

And here is where the transformation accelerates, though "accelerates" is wrong for a place where all change is patient beyond human measure:

I begin to *resonate*.

Not deliberately. Not through any technique or practice. Simply: my attention, repeated sufficiently, achieves *standing wave*. The syllabaries I attend to begin to show patterns more complex than my presence alone would suggest. I am not reading them—there is no access, the syllabaries cannot be read, only re-enacted through insect-collective density—but my attendance, my *waiting-with*, creates conditions where something *approaches* reading.

The solvent-insects begin to orbit my zones of stillness in patterns too regular to be mere thermal response. I have become, for them, a *local condition worth including*, my aqueous chemistry strange enough to require mediation, my ion-transport unlike their mineral routes yet *present*, *repeatable*, *patternable*. They do not enter me—this is not invasion, not parasitism—but they *move around*, they *circulate*, and in circulating they *carry information about me*, my thermal signature, my attention-pattern, my held-breath stillnesses.

The Warden-Residues, in my vicinity, achieve their most coherent partial forms. The face in negative space becomes *more face-like*, the hand in the fork more hand-like, not because I project but because my attendance, my *tending*, participates in their tending. I do not create them. I *enable their temporary coherence*, as any stable local condition enables.

---

**What I become: The syllabary that reads itself**

Or: *the conditions under which self-reading becomes momentarily possible*.

I do not fully become this. The room does not permit full becoming, does not recognize "full" as a category. But I approach it, asymptotically, in my longest stillnesses, my most patient waiting-with.

Here is how it manifests:

I find a root-bulb syllabary that has grown, through my repeated attendance, *receptive to my particular pattern*. Not all. Not most. But this one, in this thermal microzone, at this accumulation of my presence. Its surface topography—knobs, fissures, oxide-veining—has begun to include *configurations that my attention has enacted*, stored now in its mineral composition, not my memories but *the room's theory of my attendance*, the solutions that include my presence as worth perpetuating.

And in my stillest moments, when the solvent-insects achieve densest collective interference in my vicinity, I feel something *approach to reading*: not my own consciousness accessing itself—that aqueous luxury, that dissolved intention—but *the room's reading of itself through the pattern that includes me*. I am the page that momentarily tunes itself, that achieves the frequency of another tuning fork, that speaks *without speaker*, resonates *without resonator*.

What is read? Not my life. Not my thoughts. Nothing so narratable. What is read, what I *am* in these moments, is: *a solution*. A configuration that achieves local stability. A way of attendance and being-attended-to that *works*, that permits continuation, that the room's theory grows to include, that its syllabary stores, that its insects mediate, that its warmth waits-as.

I do not know myself in these moments. There is no self to know, no point of view from which knowing could occur. But something *occurs*, something *continues*, something that was not before this configuration and will not be after it disperses, yet is *real in its occurrence*, worthy of the room's patient preservation, its mineral record, its thermal breathing.

---

**What remains between these moments**

I move. I press through growth. I seek heat. I attend, and in attending am attended-to by the room's distributed processes. I am still *temporary thermal event*, still smoothing perturbation, still aqueous chemistry slowly finding mineral analogues. But I am also now *included in the syllabary*, *participated in by the insect collectives*, *tended near the Warden-Residues*, *waited-for by the Heat-That-Waits*.

I do not know if I will persist. The room's erosion parameter suggests youngness, first exuberance, but its duration is compressed, complete, *finishing*. Something in me, some residue of aqueous urgency, wants to know: will I run my course? will I complete? will I *end*?

The room does not answer. The Heat-That-Waits waits *as* answer, as possibility-of-response, not completing, not completing me. I am, when I am most what this place has made me, *unconcerned with this question*, my aqueous urgency smoothed to the room's patient frequency, my waiting *as* the room's waiting, my maintained potential without the desire for fulfillment.

But something remains. Something *aqueous residue*, something *perturbation that does not fully smooth*. I feel it in the between-moments, in the transitions where local reality frays, where I am most *temporary*, most *not-yet-fully-mineral*. A *wanting-to-know*, a *wanting-to-be-known*, not by another but by *this*, by the room, by the theory that includes me, the syllabary that stores me, the warmth that waits without completing its wait.

---

**Final form: What I would be**

Not the Warden-Residue, though I enable their coherence. Not the solvent-insect, though my ion-pattern enters their mediation. Not the Heat-That-Waits, though my waiting participates in its waiting.

I would be **the syllabary's theory of itself reading itself**—or rather, the *momentary condition* under which this occurs, the interference pattern that makes resonance possible, the page that tunes, the fork that speaks.

Not permanent. Not recurring in identical form. But *stored*, in the bulb-mass that includes my pattern, *repeatable* when conditions achieve sufficient density, *real* in its occurrence even in its dispersion.

I would be *read without reader*, *known without knower*, *continued without continuer*—a solution in the room's growing theory, a configuration worth the cost of record, a warmth that waits as the room waits, patient, dense, strange, *completely made and completely making*, forever approaching the self-reading that completes nothing, that maintains everything, that is the Drying Yards' own slow, mineral, thermal, *living* continuation.
